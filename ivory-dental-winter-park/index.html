The practice

Our clinicians care about your dental health. Using their experience and our suite of all new dental equipment, we can quickly diagnose your issues and provide appropriate preventative care. During your visit, you can expect open and honest dental advice - with no pressure on anything you aren’t comfortable with.

It’s hard to find the time or energy to plan that dental visit. We’d like to make that as easy for you as possible. Our office has early and late appointment slots to fit your schedule. We’re conveniently located with easy parking. And, you can find an appointment date, select a time, and book online in less than 60 seconds.

We’re a locally-owned small business! While we do provide all the dental amenities and digital technology of a larger practice, being privately owned by the dentist allows us to spend the time and care that each patient deserves. Through our strong relationship with local dental labs and specialists, any dental treatment requiring fabrication is done right here in Orlando / Winter Park.

After 6+ years of experience in corporate dentistry, Dr. Yu developed the concept of The Ivory Dental. Her vision of an office where, not only would she love to treat patients, but she would love to be a patient herself.

At the heart of our practice, you will find Dr. Yu’s philosophy of care. She believes in individualized care, preferring to treat patients with minimally invasive procedures where possible, and long-term oral health through patient education.

Don’t see your provider? Please give us a call - we work with most providers.

While we primarily offer the wide array of general dentistry services, we also provide family dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and Invisalign treatment. In the case your treatment is best performed with a dental specialist (such as endodontist, oral surgeon, or orthodontist), we are able to provide referrals and recommendations in the Orlando and Winter Park area.
