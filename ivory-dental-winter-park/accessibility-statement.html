Notice of Privacy Practices

If you have specific accessibility needs or would like to request a particular accommodation for your visit, please call us at 407-813-1788 so we can ensure your appointment is seamless.

  • Clear Routes: Level entryways and curb cuts from the parking lot to our front door (shared with our other tenant neighbors).
  • Treatment Rooms: Spacious operatory rooms designed to accommodate wheelchairs and mobility devices comfortably.
  • Restrooms: Fully ADA-compliant restrooms equipped with grab bars and accessible sinks.
  • Adjustable Panoramic X-ray: Our digital panoramic imaging system is height-adjustable and features an open-entry design.
  • Accessible Dental Chairs: Our treatment rooms are equipped with specialized dental chairs designed for easy entry and exit utilizing fold-out armrests, a low-entry height, and pivoting bases.

Conformance Status

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide array of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

Current Efforts & Known Limitations

We are actively working to maintain and improve the accessibility of https://theivory.dental . While we strive for perfection, we recognize that some parts of our website, specifically third-party integrations, may occasionally pose challenges.

Third-Party Tools: Our website may utilize limited third-party plugins to help patients learn about treatment options. Because this is a dynamic tool provided by an outside vendor, it may not fully meet all WCAG standards at all times.

Ongoing Review: We regularly audit our images to ensure meaningful alt text is present and monitor color contrast to ensure readability.

Alternative Ways to Access Information

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on our site, or if you require assistance with any part of our website, please contact us during normal business hours. We are happy to provide any information or service offered on the website over the phone or in person.
