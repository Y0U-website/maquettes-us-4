Decades of Expertise. A Passion for People. A Commitment to Excellence.

Get to know Dr. Reid Chaney

Dr. Reid Chaney didn&#x27;t just end up in Harrisburg by chance — he came home. Originally from Kannapolis, Dr. Chaney knew early on that he wanted a career in medicine, and by high school, dentistry had become his focus. After graduating from A.L. Brown High School in 1998, he went on to earn his Bachelor of Arts in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from UNC-Chapel Hill, then completed his dental training at Boston University School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 2007. From there, the path led right back to Cabarrus County, exactly where he wanted to be.

Dr. Chaney brings nearly two decades of clinical experience to every patient interaction, with particular expertise in dental implants, Invisalign, and in-house same-day crowns. He is Invisalign certified, All-on-Four implant certified, and a member of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI). He has also completed a Dental Sleep Medicine Mini-Residency, expanding his ability to serve patients with sleep-related concerns. His commitment to continued learning means you&#x27;re always benefiting from some of the most current techniques and technology available.

Outside the office, Dr. Chaney is a husband and father of two. He and his wife Ashelea have been married since 2006, and together they cheer on their son Colson and daughter Baylor at soccer games and dance competitions. When he&#x27;s not with his family, you can usually find him on the golf course.

First Visits

What to expect

From the moment you walk in, you&#x27;ll notice that Smile Cabarrus isn&#x27;t your typical dental office. We&#x27;ve worked hard to create an environment that&#x27;s comfortable, welcoming, and built around you, because we know that showing up for a dental appointment takes trust, and we don&#x27;t take that lightly.

Patient Experience Your first visit will give us the chance to get to know you, your dental history, your goals, and what matters most to you when it comes to your oral health. We&#x27;ll conduct a thorough exam, take any necessary imaging, and talk through our findings together so you leave with a clear picture of where your dental health stands and what comes next. No pressure, no confusion, just honest, patient-centered care.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

Your dental insurance card (if applicable)

Any relevant dental records or X-rays from a previous provider (if available)

Completed new patient forms (available digitally ahead of your visit)

Comfort Meets Innovation

At Smile Cabarrus, we&#x27;ve invested in both the technology that improves your care and the little comforts that make you feel at ease while you&#x27;re here. From state-of-the-art imaging to a lobby that actually feels welcoming, we&#x27;ve thought through every detail, because providing you the best possible experience matters.

Same-Day Crowns with CEREC Gone are the days of temporary crowns and return visits. Using our CEREC Primescan and in-house milling unit, we can design, fabricate, and place a permanent crown in a single appointment. That means less time in the chair, no waiting weeks for a lab, and a result that&#x27;s precise and custom-made just for you.

Digital scanners for impressions Say goodbye to the goopy impression trays of the past. Using our iTero, Trios, and Primescan digital scanners, we capture highly accurate digital impressions that are more comfortable for you, and more precise for us. Whether we&#x27;re planning a restoration or monitoring your clear aligner progress, digital impressions help make the process faster, more accurate, and more comfortable.

CBCT for 3D visuals and implant planning Our cone beam CT (CBCT) unit allows us to see your anatomy in full three-dimensional detail, something traditional X-rays simply can&#x27;t offer. This technology is especially valuable for implant planning, helping us map out the most precise and predictable treatment possible before a single step is taken.

A Lobby That Feels Like a Living Room Our waiting area is stocked with complimentary coffee, water, and a TV to help you settle in before your appointment. For our younger patients, we have coloring pages and crayons ready to go, because the little things make a big difference.

Comfort in the chair We know some patients feel anxious about dental visits, and we take that seriously. Blankets, pillows, and water are available in every operatory. Televisions are mounted in each room so you can watch a show or movie during your procedure, or catch up on what&#x27;s happening with our team via our office slideshow featuring specials, upcoming events, and staff highlights.
