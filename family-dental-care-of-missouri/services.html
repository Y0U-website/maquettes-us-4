Keeping small problems small

Preventive care is most of what a healthy mouth needs, and it is the cheapest dentistry you will ever buy. A dental exam and cleaning every six months lets us catch decay while a small filling still fixes it, screen for oral cancer, and keep gums healthy before gum disease treatment becomes necessary. Children’s visits build the habit early, with fluoride and sealants when they help.

Repairing and replacing teeth

When a tooth is damaged, decayed or missing, restorative dentistry brings it back into use. Tooth-colored fillings handle everyday decay. Crowns and bridges protect broken-down teeth and span gaps. Root canal treatment saves a tooth whose nerve is infected, and when a tooth cannot be saved, extraction followed by an implant , bridge or denture restores the space.

Changing how your smile looks

Cosmetic treatment changes the color, shape or position of the teeth that show. Professional whitening is the simplest starting point, veneers and bonding reshape chipped or uneven front teeth, and clear aligners straighten crowded or spaced teeth without brackets and wires. Dr. Berkvam holds her Invisalign certification and plans aligner cases in the office.

When it cannot wait

Severe pain, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling around a tooth: these get same-day attention where possible. Our emergency dentistry page explains what to do in the first hour, what we can treat on the day, and when a hospital emergency department is the right call instead. Wisdom teeth that flare up repeatedly are assessed the same way.
