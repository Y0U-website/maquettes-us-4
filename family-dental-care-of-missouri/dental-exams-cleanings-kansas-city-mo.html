The economics of the six-month visit

Preventive care is the cheapest dentistry there is, and the six-month interval is where the economics work. A cavity caught at a checkup is a small filling . The same cavity left for two years can mean root canal treatment and a crown , at many times the cost and none of the comfort. Early gum inflammation reverses with a cleaning and better home care; established gum disease needs treatment for life. The checkup exists to keep you on the cheap side of that line.
