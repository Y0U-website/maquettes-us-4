Brief Biography

Dr. Babak Noohi is proud to offer his patients the skills and experience of a top-tier education, which has included: a “Doctor of Stomatology” in 1998 in Europe, a “Doctor of Dental Surgery” in 2005 from New York University College of Dentistry , a-3-year specialty training in “ Advanced Education in Prosthodontics ” in 2008, and a one-year fellowship in “ Surgical Implant & Periodontal Prosthetics ” in 2009 from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, and a “Master of Science” degree in 2008 from the University of Maryland Graduate School .

Beside many restorative and periodontal treatments, Dr. Noohi is offering his patients a “one stop dental implant treatment” consisting of treatment planning for implant supported prosthetics, surgical placement of dental implants, fabrication of the prosthetics and long term post-delivery care and maintenance. Rigorous training in prosthodontics & periodontics and experience provide Dr. Noohi with an in-depth understanding of the dynamics of a smile and the preservation of a healthy mouth.

He is renowned for his special attention to detail, and devoted to providing his patients with the highest level of comprehensive care. Dr. Noohi lives with his wife in Potomac, Maryland; they are enjoying their children Ethan and Chloe, and the new addition to the family, a cute Pomeranian called Alice . While a great portion of his professional life is devoted to his patients, outside of dentistry Dr. Noohi pursues his passion for photography, literature, and hiking. He deeply appreciates time spent in nature, where he reflects and meditates.

DETAILED BIOGRAPHY

My passion for learning continued and motivated me to pursue further training at an advanced level. Shortly after graduation from NYU, I was accepted into the University of Maryland, School of Dentistry for Advanced Education in Prosthodontics, a full time three year residency program. Beside the specialty education in Prosthodontics , I earned a Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences with honors from the University of Maryland Graduate School in 2008. My mentors persuaded me to enroll in a post-doctoral surgical fellowship right after completing my residency in Prosthodontics.

I am extremely proud and privileged to say that for the four years postgraduate training I had the opportunity to be trained and mentored by Board certified specialists in both Prosthodontics and Periodontics, to whom I will always be in debt.

Extreme emphasis on combination of science and advanced new technologies and continuous supervision by dedicated master clinicians is the highlight of my four years post-graduate training at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry.
