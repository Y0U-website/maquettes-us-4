In Office Hygiene

This comprehensive evaluation and cleaning process ensures your teeth and gums stay healthy while laying the groundwork for long-term oral health.

  • CBCT/3D Imaging (As Needed) If necessary, we’ll use advanced 3D imaging to get a complete picture of your teeth, gums, and jaw. This allows us to spot hidden concerns like bone loss or infections before they become more serious or you feel the symptoms. In cases of dental emergencies or unexplained pain, CBCT imaging provides invaluable insights, helping us identify the root cause quickly and develop a precise treatment plan. This ensures that you receive the most accurate and effective care possible.
  • X-Rays (As Needed) We typically take traditional X-rays once a year or as required by your dental needs and insurance guidelines. These focused images complement our 3D imaging by giving us detailed views of specific areas, allowing us to detect cavities, monitor bone health, and identify issues like infections or fractures before symptoms appear. Regular X-rays are a crucial part of preventive care, helping us ensure your smile stays healthy and problems are addressed early.
  • Oral Cancer Screening (Optional) Your visit may includes a oral cancer screening using our advanced VELscope® system. This quick, painless screening helps us detect subtle changes in your soft tissues early, when treatment is most effective.
  • Comprehensive Oral Examination and Gum Care At The Dental Retreat, we take a thorough and proactive approach to your oral health during every visit. Here’s what you can expect: Gum Health Assessment: Healthy gums are the foundation of a strong smile. We’ll measure the depth of the pockets between your teeth and gums to check for any early signs of gum disease.
  • Oral Examination: Our team will carefully examine your teeth, gums, and soft tissues for signs of decay, gum disease, or other potential issues. Early detection allows us to address problems before they become more serious, ensuring simpler and more effective treatments.
  • Plaque and Tartar Removal: Using specialized tools, our hygienist will gently remove plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing can’t reach. This step is critical for preventing cavities and gum disease.
  • Teeth Polishing: Finally, we’ll polish your teeth to remove surface stains and leave the enamel smooth. Polished teeth not only brighten your smile but also make it harder for plaque to accumulate.
  • Laser Gum Therapy (As Needed) If we detect early signs of gum disease, laser therapy may be included as part of a mutually agreed treatment plan to address the affected areas. This advanced approach is minimally invasive, reduces discomfort, and promotes faster healing while effectively targeting problem areas. We’ll always discuss the need for laser therapy with you beforehand, explaining its benefits and answering any questions to ensure you feel informed and confident about your care. Your treatment plan is designed with your health, comfort, and preferences in mind.
  • Personalized Care and Guidance After your cleaning, we’ll provide tips and recommendations tailored to your specific needs. Whether it’s advice on brushing technique or product suggestions, we’re here to help you maintain your smile at home.
