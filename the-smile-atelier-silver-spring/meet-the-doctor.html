Dr. Antoinette Ramdath

Dr. Antoinette Ramdath decided to become a dentist because she liked her own dentist growing up! She graduated magna cum laude with a Bachelor of Science from the Honors College at Adelphi University, NY. She earned her D.M.D. at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. In 2009, she moved to Maryland with her husband and realized her dream of creating her own unique practice.

Dr. Ramdath is genuinely concerned for your health and general well-being and makes a special effort to create personal connections with each of her patients. Her capable hands and compassionate, down-to-earth approach easily put our patients of all ages at ease. At the same time, she is meticulous and professional in her work. She consistently builds and fine-tunes her skills and knowledge through continuing education courses.

Dr. Ramdath is a member of the American Dental Association , the Maryland State Dental Association , the Southern Maryland Dental Association , and several study clubs.

She is passionate about her community, both in Silver Spring and around the world. She has participated in several dental mission trips, locally and abroad, to help people in need get dental care . She is also committed to helping people in poverty have good dental health and volunteers with the youth group at her church.

She believes in treating the whole family. She feels it’s never too early to start on good dental habits and can care for your children as young as one year of age.

Dr. Ramdath is originally from Trinidad and Tobago in the Caribbean (she met her husband there too!) and frequently returns home to celebrate “A Trini Christmas.” Outside of the office, Dr. Ramdath enjoys good food, the beach, travelling, and spending quality time with her family and friends.
