Meet the Team at Skyway Dental Clinic

Opened in 1982, Skyway Dental Clinic is a comprehensive and caring dental home for families and working professionals in and around the Downtown and North Loop neighborhoods of Minneapolis.

The practice was established by Dr. Frederick Schilling, who served this community for over 40 years. In 2017, Dr. Maggie Thompson joined the practice and took over ownership in 2021.

We are proud to be part of the large and bustling downtown community but our approach to dentistry features a more personal touch. Our dentists believe spending focused, one-on-one time with our patients is important for fostering strong patient-provider relationships. We are committed to patient education and our goal is to empower each person who comes through the door to be active, informed participants in decisions regarding their dental health care.

From routine dental cleanings and regular oral health care visits to advanced dental procedures, oral cancer screenings, and reconstructive dental surgery, the team at Skyway Dental can help detect and treat all your dental concerns. Call or book an appointment online today to learn more. We look forward to meeting you!

Cindy

Cindy graduated from Concorde Career Institute as a licensed dental assistant in 1994 and joined the Skyway Dental Clinic team in August 2008. In 2015, Cindy joined our administrative team, and she is the first friendly face you see when you walk into our office. She keeps our ship afloat! Patients are quickly comforted by Cindy’s friendly demeanor and extensive dental experience. Cindy maintains her dental assistant licensure, and you may occasionally catch her assisting one of our doctors.

Cindy married her husband, Dan in 2020. She is a loving mother and also proud grandmother to five beautiful granddaughters.

In her spare time, Cindy enjoys spending time at the beach, motorcycling, and vacationing in Florida.

Eileen

Eileen earned her degree from Northwestern University in Chicago, Illinois, and has worked in dentistry since 1989. She and her family moved to Minnesota in 1997, and she joined Skyway Dental in 2014.

The most rewarding part of the job for Eileen is building relationships with patients and supporting them through their highs and lows. By remembering patients names and recalling details from previous visits, she hopes to help them feel valued and cared for.

Eileen believes in the power of teamwork and enjoys colleagues who share a commitment to helping one another to ensure the office runs smoothly and efficiently. Her coworkers are like a second family, and she cherishes their time together outside of work.

Drawn to healthcare from an early age, Eileen chose the dental field after speaking with professionals in the industry. Outside work, she loves to travel, with past adventures including cruises to the Caribbean, trips to Europe and Australia, and an upcoming trip to Japan. When not traveling, she enjoys working on home projects or spending weekends at the family s cabin. A unique hobby of hers is restoring a 1958 Corvette. A fact people might not know about Eileen is that she is the youngest of nine children and grew up in Connecticut.
