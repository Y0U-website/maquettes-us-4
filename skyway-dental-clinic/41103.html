Dr. Maggie Thompson, DDS

Dr. Maggie Craven Thompson was born and raised in Austin, MN a.k.a. Spamtown, USA. She moved to Minneapolis in 2009 to attend the University of Minnesota for her undergraduate career, receiving a BS in Biology. After falling in love with the metro area, she continued her academic career at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2017. Go, Gophers!

Dr. Maggie is excited to have found a home at Skyway Dental Clinic and grateful for Dr. Schilling s mentorship at the beginning of her career. In 2021, she became the new owner and operator of Skyway Dental Clinic. As a proponent of lifelong learning, she is active in various professional organizations, such as the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, the City of Lakes Chapter of the Seattle Study Club, and the Spear Education Study Club.

Dr. Maggie is passionate about developing lasting patient relationships through patient education and quality, personalized care. She resides in St. Paul with her husband, Matt, two young daughters, and their rescue pup, Lena. She spends her free time with family and friends, traveling, crafting, cooking, and exploring the Twin Cities through long walks with Lena and frequenting local restaurants.
