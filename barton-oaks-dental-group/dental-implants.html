Dental Implants That Look Natural and Feel Comfortable

Dental implants offer a long-term, modern solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike tooth-supported restorations, implants are placed directly into the jawbone, giving you a stable, natural-feeling foundation for your new smile.

Here’s what makes them such a popular choice:

Natural Look and Feel

Implants are designed to function and appear just like real teeth. Once they’re in place, most patients forget they even have them. You can eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Strong, Secure Fit (Osseointegration)

Implants are made from titanium, which naturally bonds with your jawbone over time. This creates a strong, secure base—essentially acting as an artificial tooth root.

Customized for You

With the help of our in-house dental lab, every crown, bridge, or denture is custom-made for an ideal fit and natural appearance. Your restoration is crafted to match your bite, shape, and smile.

Built to Last

With good care, implants can last a lifetime. Restorations like crowns may eventually need replacement, but the implant itself stays firmly anchored for years to come.

If you’re considering dental implants in Austin, we’re here to walk with you through every step. Our goal is to help you feel comfortable, supported, and confident in your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Austin, TX

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution. With good brushing, flossing, and routine maintenance , implants can last a lifetime. The restoration (like a crown or denture) may eventually need replacement, but the implant itself stays securely anchored in your jawbone.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients in Austin are surprised by how comfortable the process feels. Dental implants are placed using precise planning technology, and we offer gentle anesthesia options to keep you relaxed. Mild soreness afterward is normal and usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

How much do dental implants cost in Austin, TX?

Every patient’s needs are different, so cost can vary based on how many implants you need, your restoration type, or whether bone grafting is required . After your consultation, our team will provide a personalized treatment plan and review financing and insurance options.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Many patients with missing or damaged teeth qualify for implants. During your implant consultation, we evaluate bone support, oral health, and overall goals. Even if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate elsewhere, options like bone grafting or sinus lifts may make implants possible.

Can I replace all my teeth with implants?

Yes. Full-arch implant solutions can replace an entire upper or lower set of teeth with natural-looking results. Our Austin implant dentists will help determine whether individual implants or implant -supported bridge or denture is best for you.

How long does it take to heal?

Healing time varies, but most patients can resume daily activities within a day or two. The implant takes several months to fully bond with the jawbone, but temporary tooth replacements are often available while you heal.

Will my dental implants look natural?

Absolutely. Our restorations are custom-made through our in-house dental lab to match your smile’s color, shape, and bite. Most patients can’t tell the difference between their natural teeth and their implants.

Do you offer dental implants near Barton Creek, Westlake, or South Austin?

Yes. Barton Oaks Dental Group proudly serves the entire Austin area, including Barton Creek, Westlake, Circle C, Southwest Austin, and surrounding communities . We even have patients who regularly travel to see us from other states and countries.

How do I schedule an implant consultation?

You can request an appointment online or call our Austin office, and our team will help you find a time that works best. We’re here to answer questions, explain your options, and help you feel confident about your care.
