Services

Prevention is the foundation of lifelong oral health. With both the VIP Basic and Premium Plans, you gain access to a range of preventive services designed to keep your teeth and gums healthy and to catch any potential issues early.

Dental implants can even be used to stabilize dentures that snap onto the implants, so they don’t dislodge. Learn more about our denture services here.

Dental implants offer a long-term, modern solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike tooth-supported restorations, implants are placed directly into the jawbone, giving you a stable, natural-feeling foundation for your new smile.
