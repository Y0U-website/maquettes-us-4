Meet Our Austin Dentists

Your oral health is our priority, and our diverse range of services ensures that we can address any dental concern you might have.

  • Pediatric Dentistry : Tailored specifically for our younger patients , we provide children with a fun and comfortable environment, ensuring they start their dental journey on the right foot.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry : Enhance the beauty of your smile with our cosmetic treatments . From teeth whitening to veneers, we have solutions to elevate the aesthetics of your teeth.
  • Preventive Dentistry : Prevention is better than cure. Our preventative services, including cleanings and dental sealants, aim to prevent dental issues before they arise.
  • Neuromuscular Dentistry (TMJ) : Our specialized TMJ treatments offer relief and improved function for patients experiencing jaw pain or issues with their temporomandibular joint.
  • Oral Surgery : Our skilled team is equipped to handle a range of surgical procedures , ensuring safety and precision at every step.
  • Restorative (Reconstructive) Dentistry : Whether it’s dental implants, crowns, or bridges, our restorative services aim to bring back the function and aesthetics of your smile.
  • Implant Dentistry : Replace missing teeth and restore your smile’s functionality with our state-of-the-art dental implants .
  • Sedation Dentistry : Dental anxiety is natural, and we understand. Our sedation options ensure that every patient can have a comfortable and stress-free dental experience.
  • Root Canal Therapy : Save your damaged or infected tooth with our efficient root canal treatments , ensuring minimal discomfort and optimal results.
  • Gum Treatment : Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. Our periodontal treatments ensure your gums are free from disease and inflammation.
