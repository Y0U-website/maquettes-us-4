Dr. Thomas Schweitzer

Dr. Thomas Schweitzer earned his Doctorate Degree from Northwestern University Dental School in 1997 and completed an advanced training residency at the West Side Veterans Medical Center in Chicago.

Dr. Schweitzer takes pride in cosmetic smile makeovers, but also provides full service dentistry. His advanced training allows him to handle almost all your needs right here in our office. His dedication to providing quality care is evident in his participation in advanced continued education in the areas of general and aesthetic “cosmetic” dentistry and TMJ therapy. Memberships include American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.
