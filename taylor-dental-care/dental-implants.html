What are Dental Implants?

The implant itself is made of titanium, which is biocompatible. This means that it has the ability to fuse with the jawbone that it will be implanted into. The jawbone grows around the implant over the course of three to six months through a process referred to as osseointegration. Once the implant combines with the bone, the abutment’s upper portion can be restored with a crown, bridge, or denture.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Consult: The first step of every dental implant treatment involves a consultation with one of our implant dentists in Sandy. We will take X-rays and complete an examination of your mouth, allowing us to determine whether you’re a good candidate for treatment. If so, we will walk you through what your treatment process can look like.
  • Surgery: The next step following your consultation is surgery. During this step, your dental implants will be surgically placed below your gumline and within your jawbone to restore the missing root structure of your teeth.
  • Healing: Osseointegration, which is when your implants will fuse with your bone, typically takes between three and six months. Your gums will also heal around the implants within a couple of weeks. In many cases, patients are able to return to work within a day or two after their procedure.
  • Restoration: After your abutments have been secured to the top of your dental implants and our team has received your restoration from our dental laboratory, it’s time for the last step! We’ll have you come into the office to secure your dental crown, bridge, or denture to your implants to complete your smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are a versatile solution that can restore any number of missing teeth. That’s one of the many reasons they are considered the gold standard in tooth-replacement! They are also widely loved by patients and dentists alike because:

Day-to-Day Benefits

Once you’ve recovered from the procedure, you’ll be able to enjoy the following benefits:

  • Restored Bite Force – Dental implants can restore up to 80 % of your original bite force. So, you won’t have to eat applesauce, plain yogurt, and other soft foods on repeat. Instead, you’ll be able to enjoy a satisfying, well-balanced, and nutrient-dense diet.
  • Lifelike Appearance – Unlike other tooth-replacement solutions, dental implants won’t slip or shift unexpectedly. Plus, the restorations secured on top are custom-made for each patient to ensure they look as natural as they feel.
  • Clearer Communication – Oftentimes, patients experience a bit of a learning curve when it comes to speaking with dentures . That’s not the case with dental implants since they are anchored to your jawbone, can replace any number of missing teeth, and feel natural.

Health Benefits

The benefits of dental implants in Sandy also extend to your oral and overall health! More specifically, they:

  • Don’t Require Your Existing Teeth to be Altered – With dental bridges, the teeth surrounding the gap need to be altered to support your prosthetic, even if they are healthy. Since dental implants are self-supporting, we can preserve more of your natural smile.
  • Prevents Dental Drift – Following tooth loss, it doesn’t take long for the teeth surrounding the gap to begin to shift. Even relatively minor changes to your bite can lead to several issues, including premature wear. Fortunately, dental implants can help prevent that from happening.
  • Easy Maintenance – Oftentimes, patients with dentures end up buying special products, like cleaning tablets. Conversely, dental implants can be maintained with the same best practices you use to care for your natural teeth, like brushing your teeth each morning and evening.

Long-Term Benefits

Finally, dental implants come with a few long-term benefits that make them well worth the investment of your time and money, including:

  • High Success Rate – Dental implant failure is quite rare. In fact, the success rate of dental implants is over 95 % for non-smoking patients.
  • Long Lifespan – With a commitment to healthy habits (i.e., flossing daily, wearing a mouthguard during sports, scheduling a cleaning every six months), dental implants can last for 30 years or more.
  • Prevents Bone Loss – Dental implants are unique because they are secured to your jawbone. The stimulation that this provides helps prevent it from deteriorating, which, in turn, helps keep your bite strong and your facial shape youthful.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

There are a few qualities that we look for in a good dental implant candidate, including a strong jawbone, healthy gums, and a willingness to break harmful habits, like smoking. However, if you don’t meet these criteria initially, you shouldn’t get discouraged. After all, we provide several restorative dentistry services at our office, including gum disease treatment, so there’s a chance that we will be able to provide the preliminary care that you need right here.

Missing One Tooth

Are you missing one tooth? If so, we will likely recommend a single dental implant. If we decide together that this is the best option for you, then we will place the implant post below the gumline and within the jawbone. Then, once osseointegration has taken place, we can place a custom dental crown on top, filling the space within your arch of teeth and preventing dental drift in the process.

Missing Multiple Teeth

Previously, replacing multiple missing teeth was accomplished with a dental bridge. Although this is still an option, there’s a notable downside: your natural dental structure needs to be altered in the process. Fortunately, that’s not the case with implant bridges! Instead, the restoration is secured in place with dental implants, which provide unmatched stability and preserve more of your natural smile.

Missing All Teeth

Do you want to completely rejuvenate your smile without having to worry about slipping or shifting dentures? If so, you should consider implant dentures! This involves us securing a full custom denture to 4-8 dental implants. There are several benefits that come with this innovative tooth-replacement solution, including a lifelike appearance, unmatched stability, and a long lifespan.

Ultimately, if you are interested in replacing one, a few, or an entire arch of teeth with dental implants in Sandy, then you shouldn’t hesitate to schedule a consultation at our office. From there, we can determine if you’re a good candidate and, if you are, walk you through your treatment plan and let you know what path you can take to get the whole smile you’ve been wanting.
