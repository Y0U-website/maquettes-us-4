Full Arch Dental Implants

  • What is the difference between fixed teeth and snap-in teeth? Fixed teeth stay in place and are not removed at home, which many patients prefer for a more natural feel. Snap-in teeth are removable for cleaning, but they attach to implants for a stronger, more secure fit than conventional dentures. The best choice depends on comfort preferences, cleaning routine, and anatomy.
  • How many implants are used for a full arch? Many full-arch plans use four to eight implants per arch, depending on bone quality, bite forces, and the type of restoration selected. During planning, we determine the number and positioning that provide stable support for your specific case.
  • What should i expect during recovery? Most patients feel comfortable returning to normal routines within a few days, while deeper healing continues for several months. Integration often takes around four to six months as the implants fuse with bone. We provide clear aftercare steps and schedule follow-ups to monitor healing and comfort.
  • Will i need bone grafting or other preparatory procedures? Not everyone needs additional procedures. If bone volume or density is limited, grafting or other supportive steps may be recommended to improve stability and protect long-term results. Your imaging and exam determine whether anything additional would be helpful.
