Vida Smiles Uses

Cutting Edge Technology

Digital Impressions (also known as 3-D intraoral scanning) are the latest technology in capturing a replica of your mouth. By using a wand-like tool connected to a computer and advanced software, VIDA SMILES will create a virtual model of your teeth. These replicas are more accurate than a mold, and can be used for a variety of products. After taking a scan of your mouth, we will then immediately begin the process of making your custom-made device.

1. Accurate Dental Impressions

With an intraoral scanner, the accuracy of the impression is impressive. The scanning removes the risk of human error and ensures the image is faultless. This simplifies the procedure, reducing the need to fix inaccuracies.

2. Reduced Chair Time

The traditional method of taking impressions is a long and uncomfortable process, requiring an extended amount of time. Using an intraoral scanner means reduced time and less hassle.

3. Comfort

In the past, dental impressions have been taken using a metal tray that is quite uncomfortable, especially those with a sensitive gag reflex. With an intraoral scanner, the digital impression is taken without any discomfort.

4. Faster Results

Because an intraoral scanner produces a digital impression, the result is immediate so that Vida Smiles can process any appliance more quickly and efficiently. With this innovative technology, you can start enjoying a more beautiful pleasing and functional smile in just a few weeks.

5. Better Experience

Using intraoral scanners is less invasive than traditional dental impressions, creating a better experience. Say goodbye to uncomfortable metal impression trays and hello to fast, accurate images.
