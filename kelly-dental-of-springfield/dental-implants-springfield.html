Dental Implants have one of the highest upfront investments compared to other options (around the cost of a 3-tooth bridge if replacing1 tooth with an implant), but are worth every penny in the long run. Let us explain. They usually last 2-3 times longer than a bridge, and are easier to clean, more natural to eat with, preserve bone, are individualized allowing us to preserve precious enamel from adjacent teeth, and are easily flossed between.

Basic Implants require 3 components: the man-made root, the abutment, and a crown. The crown can be retained by either cement or a small screw.

On some cases additional techniques are used for a more positive outcome such as building up of bone and gums, sinus treatments, and healing caps prior to the final crown. We will make sure to pick just the right design for you so you can get back to looking and feeling how you were naturally intended to.
