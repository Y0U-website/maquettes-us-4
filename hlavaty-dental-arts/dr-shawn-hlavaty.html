About Dr. Hlavaty.

A few things patients often ask about our lead dentist, his background, and his care. Don t see your question? We d love to hear from you.

Dr. Shawn Hlavaty Santa Barbara earned his DDS from NYU College of Dentistry, completed advanced implant training at NYU/Bellevue Hospital, and pursued advanced cosmetic training at the Rosenthal Institute for Aesthetic Dentistry. Before pursuing dentistry, he worked as a landscape architect, which gives him a distinctive design perspective on smile aesthetics. He has more than 19 years of experience — 17 of them in Santa Barbara — and is a member of the ADA, AACD, AAID, and Global Dental Implant Academy.

Dr. Hlavaty specializes in porcelain veneers, smile makeovers, teeth whitening, cosmetic bonding, and clear aligners on the cosmetic side. For tooth replacement, he provides single-tooth implants, All-on-4 full-arch restorations, full mouth reconstruction, and bone grafting. Advanced technology such as 3D CT scanning and computer-guided planning supports precise, natural-looking outcomes.
