Tooth Replacement vs. Cosmetic Dentistry — Which Do You Need?

It is common to wonder whether your concern calls for tooth replacement or cosmetic dentistry. The distinction is straightforward: tooth replacement addresses teeth that are missing or need to be extracted, while cosmetic dentistry enhances teeth that are still present but may be chipped, discolored, or misaligned.

In some cases, patients benefit from both — for example, replacing a missing tooth with an implant while improving surrounding teeth with veneers or bonding. During your consultation, Dr. Hlavaty will help you understand which approach is right for your smile.
