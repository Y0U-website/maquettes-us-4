Services

  • 0 Preventative Care Includes comprehensive oral examination, professional cleaning, and oral cancer screening.
  • Value: Regular preventive care helps maintain optimal oral health and prevents future dental problems.
  • Length of Service: Approximately 1 hour.
  • Unique Offering: Our thorough examination helps detect issues at an early stage, saving patients from costly treatments in the future.

Restorative Treatments

  • Fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures to restore damaged or missing teeth.
  • Value: Restoring the function and aesthetics of teeth for improved chewing and confidence.
  • Length of Service: Varies based on the complexity of the treatment.
  • Unique Offering: We offer personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s specific needs for optimal results.

Periodontal Therapy

  • Deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) and periodontal maintenance for gum disease treatment.
  • Value: Improving gum health and preventing tooth loss due to gum disease.
  • Length of Service: Multiple sessions may be required based on the severity of the condition.
  • Unique Offering: Our experienced hygienists ensure thorough and gentle care for effective treatment of gum disease.

Cosmetic Dentistry

  • Teeth whitening, veneers, and composite bonding for enhancing smile aesthetics.
  • Value: Boosting confidence and improving the overall appearance of teeth.
  • Length of Service: Typically completed in 1-2 visits.
  • Unique Offering: Our cosmetic treatments are designed to provide natural-looking results that complement each patient’s features.

Geriatric Dentistry

  • Customized dental care tailored to the unique needs of older adults, including denture care, gum disease management, and oral health education.
  • Value: Addressing age-related oral health challenges and promoting overall well-being in seniors.
  • Length of Service: Varies based on individual treatment plans.
  • Unique Offering: Our team understands the specific concerns of geriatric patients and provides compassionate and gentle care to ensure their comfort and oral health.
