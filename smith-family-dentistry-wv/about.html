Learn More About Our Practice We are Smith Family Dentistry WV and we’d like to personally invite you to explore what makes our practice unique and special.

We offer comprehensive dentistry for the entire family, including routine silver and composite fillings, extractions, pediatric dental care, endodontics and treatment for gum disease. Whether you have chipped, discolored or missing teeth, we offer the most current cosmetic dental procedures to dramatically enhance your smile, such as dental implants, teeth whitening, bonding and porcelain veneers.

We take great pride in providing high-quality dental care by using the most advanced technology. We utilize digital X-rays to gather invaluable information about your dental and oral health. Digital X-rays reduce radiation exposure by 90 percent. Using an intra-oral camera, our experienced dentists can detect problems that cannot be identified through a general exam. We believe our precise combination of knowledge, skill and technology enables us to deliver optimum results for each of our patients.

Part of our commitment to serving our patients includes providing information that helps them make an informed decision about their oral health needs and treatment in a friendly environment.
