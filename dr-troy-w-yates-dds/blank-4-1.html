Dental services

Teeth whitening

At-home teeth whitening services offer a convenient, effective way to brighten your smile. These services provide custom whitening kits with personalized trays and professional-grade gels, tailored to your needs. You receive detailed instructions, and flexible scheduling for a comfortable experience. Most users see significant results within days to weeks. These services are cost-effective compared to in-office treatments and are overseen by dental professionals to ensure safety and quality. Enjoy a brighter smile with professional guidance from the comfort of your home.

Implants

Dental implants are a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. They involve surgically placing a titanium post into the jawbone, which acts as a root for a replacement tooth or bridge. Implants provide a natural look and feel, improve chewing and speaking, and help maintain jawbone structure. This durable and long-lasting option restores functionality and aesthetics to your smile.

Preventative Care

Preventative dental cleanings are routine procedures aimed at maintaining oral health and preventing dental issues. These cleanings involve removing plaque and tartar buildup, polishing teeth, and conducting an oral health checkup. Regular cleanings help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems, ensuring a healthy, bright smile. It s recommended to have these cleanings every six months for optimal oral hygiene.

Fillings

Dental fillings are used to restore teeth damaged by decay. During the procedure, the dentist removes the decayed portion of the tooth and fills the cavity with desensitizing medication and various types of composite resin. Fillings restore the tooth s structure and function, prevent further decay, and alleviate pain and sensitivity. They are a common and effective treatment to maintain oral health and preserve natural teeth.

Botox

Botox is a cosmetic treatment that reduces fine lines and wrinkles by temporarily relaxing facial muscles through small injections of botulinum toxin. It s effective for smoothing out lines on the forehead, around the eyes (crow s feet), and between the eyebrows (frown lines). The procedure is quick, minimally invasive, with results lasting 3-6 months. Additionally, Botox is used medically for conditions like TMJ, migraines and excessive sweating, providing both aesthetic enhancement and therapeutic relief.

Extractions/bone grafting

Tooth extraction involves removing a tooth due to severe decay, infection, or other reasons. It s done under local anesthesia by loosening and gently pulling the tooth. After extraction, the area may be packed with gauze to aid healing.

For cases where the extraction leaves a gap that needs support for future dental work or to maintain facial structure, bone grafting may be performed. This procedure involves placing bone graft material into the extraction site to promote new bone growth and stability. Both procedures aim to ensure patient comfort and long-term dental health.

Crowns/inlays/veneers/bridge

Dental crowns are custom-made coverings placed over damaged teeth to restore their shape, size, strength, and appearance. Made from materials like porcelain and zirconia or a combination, crowns encase the entire visible portion of the tooth above the gum line. They are used to protect weak or broken teeth, cover large fillings, improve the appearance of misshapen or discolored teeth, and support dental bridges. Crowns are durable, natural-looking, and help restore both function and aesthetics to the smile, providing long-term protection and enhancement for treated teeth.

Gum contouring

Gum contouring, or gingivoplasty, is a cosmetic dental procedure that reshapes the gum line to enhance your smile. It corrects uneven or excessive gum tissue, treats gummy smiles, and exposes more of your teeth. The procedure involves removing and sculpting gum tissue under local anesthesia, with minimal downtime and lasting results. This safe, effective treatment provides a balanced, attractive smile.
