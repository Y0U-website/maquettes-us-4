Dr. Yates has been helping people put their best smile forward for the past 24 years. As an active community dentist, Dr. Yates specializes in comprehensive dental services including preventive care, restorative treatments, cosmetic dentistry, and advanced procedures like dental implants and Comprehensive Botox treatments. After graduating from Virgina Commonwealth University School of Dentistry in 2000, Dr. Yates joined Yates Family & Cosmetic Dentistry and worked alongside Dr. Yates Sr. for 10 years before taking over the family practice solo.

In his free time, Dr. Yates enjoys camping in the Uintah’s, fishing, golfing, traveling to Alaska, and all outdoor activities with his family. Dr. Yates listens to the patients concerns and enjoys helping people feel confident in their smile and is committed to a positive and comfortable experience while in the dental chair.
