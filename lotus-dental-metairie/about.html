ABOUT US

Founded upon the Lotus Dental philosophy of total patient care. Our office is the result of 23 years of success in providing excellent dentistry to generations of Louisiana families.

Experience in surgery, implants, orthodontics, cosmetics, children’s dentistry, infection care, gum disease, TMJ, and even Botox, ensures that you will have a home for all our your Dental needs.

Growing into more than one location with Doctors, Hygienists, and staff mentored personally by Dr. Verges. Our office continues to uphold our core values of Patient first-Teamwork-integrity and compassion. More providers and locations give our patients greater access to world-class care.

MEET THE DENTIST

DR. TROY MICHAEL VERGES, DDS

Dr. Verges lives in Covington, LA. He loves spending time with his 2 sons, Owen and Spencer. He runs in the mornings with his Goldendoodle, Shep. His hobbies include being outdoors, running, watersports, skiing, camping, and most recently learning the guitar. Among his many personal interests, health and wellness are a top priority, which overflow into his interactions with his staff and patients. He is always concerned with the health of the whole person.

DR. WYNN RUSSO

We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Wynn Russo to our dental team! With over 39 years of experience in private practice, Dr. Russo brings a wealth of knowledge, skill, and compassion to our office. He is passionate about providing high-quality dental care and building lasting relationships with his patients.

Dr. Russo completed his undergraduate studies at Tulane University and earned his dental degree from LSU School of Dentistry in 1985. His commitment to excellence and genuine care for his patients are just a few of the many reasons we’re excited to have him on board.

When he’s not in the office, you can find Dr. Russo out on the water fishing or cheering on his grandchildren at their sports games.

Please join us in giving Dr. Russo a warm welcome to the practice!

DR. CELINE SNOW, DDS

Dr. Celine Snow was born and raised in New Orleans. She went to LSU for undergrad, where she received her bachelor’s of science in 2017. Dr. Snow is also a recent graduate from LSU school of dentistry. Her love for dentistry came from her own dental journey, her parents, and through community service. Growing up, she was constantly in the dental chair because of misaligned teeth. She says she basically had metal in her mouth for 11 years!
