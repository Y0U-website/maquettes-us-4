What Can Be Expected While Undergoing Dental Emergency Treatment?

When you visit our office for a dental emergency, we prioritize your comfort and swift relief from pain. Here’s what you can expect during your treatment:

  • Initial Assessment: We start by discussing the problem you’re experiencing. This helps us understand the severity and nature of your emergency.
  • Diagnostic Imaging: If necessary, we will take x-rays to get a clear view of the issue. X-rays help us identify problems not visible to the naked eye and determine the best course of treatment.
  • Examination: We thoroughly examine your teeth and gums to pinpoint the root cause of the problem. This comprehensive assessment ensures that we address all aspects of the emergency.
  • Immediate Treatment: Based on our findings, we provide immediate treatment to alleviate your pain and prevent the problem from worsening. This may include procedures such as filling a cavity, extracting a damaged tooth, or repairing a broken appliance.
  • Infection Management: If there is an active infection, we will prescribe antibiotics to help eliminate the infection and prevent it from spreading.
  • Follow-Up Care: We provide guidance on follow-up care and any additional treatments that may be necessary to ensure complete recovery.
