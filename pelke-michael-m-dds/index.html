Welcome to Woodbury Dental Care, your award-winning, nationally recognized, and locally owned dental office. Here at WDC, Drs. Michael Pelke, Dr. Chip Shimota, Emily Wernberg, Stephanie Wenzel, and David Augustson have a combined total of over 50 years of service to the community at large.

Staying true to the practice philosophy established by Dr. Kyle Edlund in 1989, Woodbury Dental Care started with a simply stated rule: Treat your patients how you would want to be treated essentially, The Golden Rule.

In dentistry, that translates to providing the same high-quality dentistry to our patients that we expect for ourselves and our families. This belief and empathy for your health are what we believe in and what sets us apart from other providers.
