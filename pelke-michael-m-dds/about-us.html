Meet the Team at Woodbury Dental Care

At Woodbury Dental Care, Drs. Michael Pelke, , Emily Wernberg, Stephanie Wenzel, David Augustson, and Chip Shimota carry on the proud tradition started at this office by Dr. Kyle Edlund in 1989. We are committed to practicing the golden rule and treating patients as we want to be treated. In other words, we provide each patient with the same respect, kindness, and high-quality dentistry that we expect for ourselves and our families.

Our amazing team is dedicated to providing a comfortable and enjoyable experience from the moment our patients walk through our doors. Many of us have been working here for decades and still bring energy and enthusiasm to our jobs because we love serving the patients who are like family to us.

We have seen many of the same faces for years and love catching up with them to see what’s been happening in their lives. Our new patients soon learn that they are also valued members of our extended dental family.

Through high-quality dentistry and individualized treatment, we would love to show you how improving your oral health can enhance your entire outlook on life. Our dentists have over 50 years of combined experience, and their interest in the latest dental techniques and technology ensures that you receive world-class modern dentistry.

Our amazing team of dentists, hygienists, assistants, and front office staff look forward to welcoming you soon. Until then, please take a few minutes to get to know us.

Dr. Michael Pelke

Dr. Michael Pelke is the most senior dentist and partner at Woodbury Dental Care. He started in private practice in 2002 and teamed up with Dr. Kyle Edlund in 2004. Together, they grew the office from seven bays to the seventeen we have today! Dr. Pelke strives to be a caring and compassionate provider and enjoys spending time with his patients and listening to their stories.

Born and raised in Minnesota, Dr. Pelke grew up in Rochester, where he attended John Marshall High School. He received a bachelor s degree from Drake University and earned his doctorate in dentistry from the University of Minnesota. With a particular interest in Sports Dentistry and dental trauma, he currently serves as a board member of the Academy For Sports Dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Pelke is a committed family guy, and he and his wife, Jill have three adult children, a cat (Ginger) and a dog (Basil). Dr. Pelke and his wife enjoy spending time with friends and family, trying new restaurants and an occasional trip to Las Vegas.

Dr. Emily Wernberg

Dr. Emily Wernberg was born and raised in Eagan, Minnesota. She attended the University of Wisconsin-Madison, earning her Bachelor of Science degree in kinesiology and biology. She decided to continue her education at a rival school, where she became a Minnesota Gopher and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Dr. Wernberg joined Woodbury Dental Care in 2016 and is now proud to be part owner of the practice.

Dr. Wernberg is passionate about dentistry because it allows her to alleviate pain, prevent disease, and improve smiles. She strives to get to know her patients and their unique situations to develop an individualized treatment plan with their concerns in mind.

Dr. Wernberg lives in Woodbury with her husband, Luke, and their two boys. They love exploring Woodbury and all that the surrounding areas have to offer. When not in the office, Dr. Wernberg enjoys being with family and friends, traveling, spending time outdoors, and being active.

Dr. Stephanie Wenzel

Dr. Stephanie Wenzel is a third-generation dentist who, along with her father and grandfather, attended the University of Minnesota, where she earned her Bachelor of Science degree in biology and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. While at the School of Dentistry, she first realized the enjoyment of building friendly and professional relationships with patients based on trust and honesty. Her goal is to provide patients with the best care possible and put them at ease, especially those traditionally fearful of coming to the dentist.

Dr. Wenzel grew up in Brainerd, Minnesota, where she developed an appreciation for the outdoors. In her free time, she enjoys fishing, biking, and boating. Dr. Wenzel has called Woodbury home for the past 12 years and has raised her identical twin girls, Abby and Ella, here.

Dr. David Augustson

Before attending dental school, Dr. Augustson worked as a research assistant at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, investigating enamel erosion. He continued on at the University of Minnesota to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree. After graduation, Dr. Augustson completed a General Practice Residency at the University of Washington in Seattle before moving to Ann Arbor, Michigan, where he practiced for three years while his wife trained at the University of Michigan.

Dr. Augustson moved back to Minnesota in 2018 when he and his family settled in Woodbury. After practicing in Burnsville for over two years, he joined Woodbury Dental care in 2021. He is thrilled to serve the same community that he calls home.

Dr. Augustson strives to be an empathetic and caring provider while always maintaining a positive attitude, strong attention to detail, and commitment to lifelong learning. He loves the variety of ways that general dentistry can profoundly impact his patients lives.

When not in the office, Dr. Augustson enjoys spending time with his wife, Neli, and their three kids, William, Eloise and Adeline. His favorite hobbies include cooking, watching sports, and long-distance running.

Dr. Charles Shimota

Dr. Chip Shimota was born and raised in Woodbury, Minnesota. Just as any other lifelong WDC patient would do, he chose to spend the next eight years of his life pursuing his passion for dentistry. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree in biology and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota.

Dr. Shimota strives to make sure all his patients feel informed, respected, and comfortable. His goal is to build trusting and caring relationships with his patients in order to make sure their dental experiences are as positive as possible.

When not in the office, Dr. Shimota loves to spend time with family and friends, be on a lake, watch Minnesota sports, and suffer from a love-hate relationship with golf.
