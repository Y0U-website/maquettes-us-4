Dental Implants in Brookline, MA

Dental implants are a reliable, long-term solution for missing teeth, using small titanium posts to support custom restorations that look and function like natural teeth. As a form of permanent tooth replacement, implants help preserve bone health, restore full function, and enhance the overall appearance of your smile.

As an experienced implant dentist, Dr. Laura Galiazzo takes a precise, personalized approach to care—using advanced technology to plan and deliver predictable, natural-looking results. In our comfortable, spa-like environment, we focus on restoring not just your smile, but your confidence and long-term oral health.

Next Steps

Healing and integration: Over the next few months, the implant integrates with your jawbone to provide long-term stability and support. Follow-up visits: We’ll monitor your healing and ensure everything is progressing smoothly. Long-term maintenance: With proper care, including good oral hygiene and regular checkups, your dental implants can provide lasting results for years to come.

FAQ

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last many years—often decades. They are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth.
  • How many dental implants can be done at once? The number of implants placed at one time depends on your individual needs and oral health. In many cases, multiple implants can be placed during a single appointment.
  • How often do dental implants fail? Dental implant success rates are very high, typically around 95 % or higher. Failures are rare and often related to factors like poor healing, smoking, or underlying health conditions.
