What Are Dental Implants?

Brookside Dental Care - Dental Implants - FAQs Replacing Missing Teeth with Dental Implants with Allentown, PA Dentist Dr John Gilliland A dental implant is a small metal post that replaces a missing tooth. Placed into the jawbone during a minor procedure, it acts like a natural root and is topped with a crown for a lifelike look and feel.

Considering dental implants but not sure what to expect? We break it all down so you can make a confident, informed choice about your care.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Losing a tooth can impact more than just your smile—it can affect your confidence, comfort, and overall oral health. A missing tooth may cause discomfort while eating, lead to shifting teeth, and even contribute to bone loss over time.

  • Restore your natural look Dental implants are custom-designed to match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth. Once placed, they look and feel just like the real thing—blending seamlessly into your smile and restoring your appearance with confidence.
  • Enjoy lasting comfort and stability Dental implants are securely anchored in your jawbone, where the titanium post fuses with the bone during healing. Whether supporting a crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture, implants offer unmatched stability. You can laugh, smile, and eat your favorite foods without worrying about shifting or discomfort.
  • Support jawbone health When a tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it can begin to weaken due to lack of stimulation. Dental implants help prevent bone loss by encouraging new bone growth around the implant, keeping your jaw strong and healthy.

Dental implants are a durable, long-term solution that can last for decades with proper care—restoring your smile, confidence, and quality of life.

Dental Implant Restorations

The restoration is the visible part of your dental implant—it’s the “false tooth” that sits on top and completes your smile. While the implant itself is hidden beneath the gums, the restoration is what gives you the look and function of a natural tooth.

  • Dental Crown This is the most common type of implant restoration. A crown is used to replace a single missing tooth and is attached directly to the implant.
  • Dental Bridge A bridge is used to replace multiple missing teeth in a row. With just one or two implants, a dental bridge can span the gap—restoring your bite and smile. Full implant-supported bridges can replace an entire upper or lower arch, supported by four or more implants.
  • Implant Denture For those who wear dentures, implants can add extra stability. A removable denture can securely snap onto two or more implants, offering a better fit and greater confidence when eating or speaking.

How It Feels

Getting a dental implant may sound intimidating, but the experience is usually much more comfortable than you’d expect.

We’ll start by numbing your mouth with a local anesthetic, so you won’t feel any pain during the procedure. Thanks to modern dental technology, implants can be placed quickly and precisely—often in just 30 minutes—minimizing discomfort and speeding up recovery.

Afterward, it’s normal to have some mild swelling, bruising, or tenderness for a few days. Don’t worry—we’ll give you clear aftercare instructions to help manage discomfort before the numbness wears off. Most people start feeling better within a week.

With a success rate of nearly 95 %, dental implants are one of the safest and most predictable treatments in modern dentistry.

What to Expect During Your Dental Implant Journey

Getting a dental implant is often more comfortable and straightforward than most people think. Here’s what your treatment process will typically look like:

Consultation and Assessment

Your journey starts with a thorough consultation. Your dentist will take X-rays and a 3D cone-beam CT scan to evaluate your jawbone and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants. We’ll also take digital scans or impressions of your teeth to plan the exact placement and angle of the implant.

Placing the Implant

Once you’re ready, we’ll numb the area with a local anesthetic to make sure you’re completely comfortable. A small opening is made in the gums and jawbone, and the implant post is carefully placed. In some cases, stitches may be needed to close the gums.

Healing and Restoration

After the implant is placed, you’ll go home to allow the area to heal and the implant to fuse with your jawbone—a process that takes a few months. Once healing is complete, you’ll return for your final restoration. We’ll securely attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture, giving you a fully functional and natural-looking new tooth.With each step, we’re here to guide you and ensure your comfort. The result? A long-lasting smile you can feel confident in.

Cost of Dental Implants in Allentown

If you’re considering tooth replacement options, it’s helpful to understand what to expect when it comes to pricing. A single dental implant typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000+. For full-mouth implants, costs can vary from $6,000 to $60,000 or more, depending on your individual needs. A full set of implant-supported dentures generally averages around $30,000.

Dental implants in Allentown are a highly personalized treatment, which is why it’s difficult to provide an accurate quote over the phone. Your final cost will depend on several unique factors.

Are There Alternatives to Dental Implants?

Yes. For one to three missing teeth, a dental bridge may be recommended. For more extensive tooth loss, removable dentures or dental prosthetics might be suitable alternatives.

While these options may cost less initially, they typically require more maintenance and may not last as long. Implants, on the other hand, offer a stable, long-term solution that feels and functions like natural teeth—and with proper care, they can last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Will anyone notice that I have dental implants?

Not at all—dental implants are designed to look and feel just like natural teeth. While the implant itself is a small metal post placed beneath the gums, it’s completely hidden. What people see is the custom-made crown or restoration on top, which is carefully crafted to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth. No one will be able to tell the difference—your smile will look completely natural.

Is it better to get dentures or implants?

Dentures and implants serve different purposes and function in different ways. Dentures rest on the gums and can sometimes shift, while dental implants are anchored into the jawbone for greater stability and bite strength. Each option has its own pros and cons. The best way to determine what’s right for you is to schedule an in-person consultation so we can evaluate your needs and recommend the best solution.

Are there alternatives to dental implants?

Yes. If dental implants aren’t the right fit for you, options like dental bridges or full/partial dentures may be ideal. These solutions can restore your smile and function depending on your situation. We’d be happy to discuss all available options during a consultation.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most healthy adults are good candidates for dental implants, but a strong jawbone and healthy gums are essential for success. If you’re unsure whether you qualify, we encourage you to schedule a consultation. In many cases, treatments can be done to restore your oral health and prepare you for implants—or help you explore the best alternative.

Will my dental implants match my natural teeth?

Absolutely. Your dental implant will be topped with a custom-made crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture designed to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. Most patients can’t tell the difference between their implant and their real teeth—it blends in seamlessly with your smile.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

When can I return to work after dental implant surgery?

Most patients can return to work the very next day. Depending on your specific treatment, we’ll let you know ahead of time if extra recovery is recommended. We’ll walk you through everything you need to know before and after surgery.

How often do I need checkups after getting dental implants?

Just like natural teeth, implants require regular checkups and cleanings . Most patients benefit from seeing us every six months, though we may suggest more or fewer visits based on your individual needs.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for implants is simple—treat them like natural teeth. Brush and floss daily, come in for routine cleanings , and let us know if you experience any issues. Good oral hygiene is key to keeping your implants healthy for years to come.

Do dental implants need to be replaced?

In most cases, no. Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution and can last for decades—or even a lifetime—with proper care.
