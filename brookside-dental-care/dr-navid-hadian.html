Dr. Navid Hadian

Partner Dentist

Navid Hadian, DMD, is a graduate of the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. He completed a year-long general practice residency at Allentown Sacred Heart Hospital and has trained under world-renowned dentists in the fields of sedation dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and implant dentistry. A focal point of Dr. Hadian’s professional career has been to provide patients with the most modern dental advancements, which allows him to provide more complete care to his patients under one roof.

He has undergone training for cosmetic dentistry in the Pacific Aesthetic Continuum and completed an NYU Implant Maxicourse. His professional memberships include the American Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation, and Pennsylvania Dental Association.

Dr. Hadian and his wife Leah have three beautiful children, and in his spare time, he enjoys working out, golfing, and travelling.
