Dental Implant

Throughout our lives, our teeth experience wear and tear. Although we maintain our teeth with regular exams and cleanings, we may have a tooth taken out. Implants are a great way to restore your smile and function. A dental implant is a dental procedure that helps to replace a missing tooth. This is an ideal treatment for replacing a single tooth or multiple missing teeth. Many patients are great candidates for implants. Your dentist can help determine if you are an ideal fit during your consultation appointment! There are many benefits to implants. Here is a list of some advantages:

  • Longevity: With proper maintenance and follow ups, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Maintenance: Dental implants are easier to maintain compared to traditional dentures.
  • Cosmetic appearance: Implant crowns are custom made and can mimic your surrounding teeth or give you a more ideal smile.
  • Preservation of bone: Dental implants help preserve the jawbone structure.
  • What is the process for implant placement? At the first appointment, a metal post is placed in the jawbone to replace the tooth root. Following the first appointment is a 3-month waiting period. After adequate healing time, a crown, bridge, or denture is placed on top.
  • Am I a good candidate for implants? Determining factors include a patient’s health history, bone density, and oral health. Your dentist can help determine if you are an ideal fit during your consultation appointment!
  • Can I get an implant the same day that I get a tooth taken out? Yes! An immediate implant takes place on the same day that a tooth is extracted
