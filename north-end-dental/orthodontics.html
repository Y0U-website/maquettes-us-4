What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear braces are an excellent alternative to traditional metal braces. These braces are made of smooth, clear ceramic and are barely visible. Many patients appreciate clear braces because they are less noticeable than traditional ones. Since these braces are the same color as your teeth, they don’t stand out. Another advantage is that clear braces can be worn by patients who are allergic to nickel.
