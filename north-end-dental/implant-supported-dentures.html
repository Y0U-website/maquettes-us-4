Why Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures offer a significant improvement over traditional dentures. Unlike conventional dentures that rest on the gums, these are anchored in the jawbone, offering a level of stability that mimics natural teeth. This not only facilitates efficient chewing but also contributes to better speech clarity.

Furthermore, because they stimulate the jawbone similar to natural tooth roots, they help in maintaining bone structure, thereby preventing facial sagging that often occurs with the loss of teeth.

Call Us Now What Are the Advantages of Implant-Supported Dentures? One of the biggest benefits of implant-supported dentures is their improved ability to eat. A traditional removable denture provides only 10 % to 15 % of normal chewing function. You can chew soft foods, but anything with crunch or texture like apples or steak should be avoided. With implant-supported dentures, you will be able to eat a greater variety of foods. Also, the dentures would stay in place without getting dislodged, unlike traditional dentures.

Call Us Now What Materials Are Used to Make Implant-Supported Dentures? An implant-supported denture has two parts. The custom-made denture has an outer layer of acrylic or porcelain and an inner layer that looks like gum tissue. The dental implant, which is a screw made of titanium, is surgically placed in your jaw. The dental implant acts as an anchor for the denture, snapping into place on the implant. The implant-supported denture is designed so that it will not shift or move when the patient speaks or eats.
