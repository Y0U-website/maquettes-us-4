Benefits of Austin scaling & root planing

Stops gum disease in its tracks

By removing harmful bacteria and tartar, this treatment halts the progression of gum disease, preventing it from advancing to more severe stages.

Protects your teeth and bone

Gum disease can destroy the bone supporting your teeth. Periodontal cleaning helps preserve your natural teeth and the foundation that holds them in place.

Fresher breath

Bacteria buildup is a leading cause of bad breath. A deep cleaning eliminates these odor-causing culprits, leaving your mouth feeling fresh and clean.

Improves overall health

Research shows a strong link between gum disease and systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes. Treating gum disease can positively impact your overall well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is scaling and root planing?

It’s a deep-cleaning procedure that involves removing tartar and plaque from the tooth surfaces and beneath the gums, and smoothing the root surfaces.

How often is scaling and root planing needed?

It depends on your oral health. Some patients may need it once, while others might require regular treatments due to ongoing gum disease.

What can I expect during the process?

The process involves thorough cleaning of the root surfaces to remove plaque and tartar from deep periodontal pockets and to smooth the tooth root.

How can I maintain my oral health post-treatment?

Besides regular brushing and flossing, we recommend avoiding smoking, following a balanced diet, and attending regular dental check-ups.

How long does it take to see results after the procedure?

Many patients notice improvements within a few weeks, such as reduced gum swelling and bleeding. Full healing can take a bit longer, depending on the severity of your gum disease.
