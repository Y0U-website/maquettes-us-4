Meet Vivid’s Dentist

Dr. Megan Ross

DR. MEGAN ROSS- DENTIST & PRACTICE OWNER

Committed to Excellence, Compassion, and Community

Dr. Megan Ross was born and raised in Fox Chapel, where she now resides with her husband and daughter. She earned her undergraduate degree from Northwestern University before attending the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. While at Pitt, she received numerous awards for clinical excellence and was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, an honor reserved for the top 10 % of her class.

Following dental school, Dr. Ross completed a General Practice Residency (GPR) at the Washington DC Veterans Affairs Medical Center, where she gained advanced experience in implants, root canals, surgical extractions, complex treatment planning, and restorative dentistry. She then spent over three years in private practice in Butler, PA, honing her skills and deepening her commitment to patient care before stepping into ownership at Vivid Dental.

Dr. Ross has spent many hours on advanced clinical education

Dr. Ross is thrilled to serve her hometown community, combining her clinical expertise with a warm, patient-focused approach. She looks forward to building lasting relationships and helping every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.

When not in the office, you can find her pool-side cheering on her daughter, Brooke, in competitive swimming, or sampling new restaurants with her husband, James.
