About Lubbock, TX

Known as the "Hub City," Lubbock, TX, reflects a charming mix of rich history and modern culture. From its lively arts scene to its nationally renowned universities, this West Texas city offers something for everyone.

Notable Local Landmarks

  • Buddy Holly Center: Explore the life and legacy of Lubbock’s own rock-and-roll pioneer, Buddy Holly, in this interactive museum.
  • National Ranching Heritage Center: Step back in time and experience authentic ranching history through beautifully preserved artifacts and structures.
  • Prairie Dog Town: A quirky yet beloved spot to marvel at prairie dogs thriving in their natural habitat.
