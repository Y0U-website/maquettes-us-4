Financial Policy

For your convenience, we accept payments made with cash, debit cards, Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and American Express. We also offer financing through CareCredit®.

CareCredit®

CareCredit® is available to help you pay for dental procedures your insurance doesn’t cover. CareCredit® offers both No Interest* financing or low minimum monthly payment options so you can take care of your dental health. With three simple steps, including an instant approval process, it’s easy to apply for CareCredit. Click the Logo below or Scan the QR Code for more information or to apply for financing.
