Types of Orthodontic Treatment

Traditional Metal Braces

The tried-and-true solution for addressing complex alignment issues, modern metal braces are more comfortable and less noticeable than ever before. These reliable appliances gradually guide teeth into their optimal positions through gentle, consistent pressure.

Clear Aligner Therapy

Using advanced Invisalign ® and ClearCorrect® technology, we offer nearly invisible aligners that straighten teeth without metal brackets or wires. These removable aligners let you eat, drink, and maintain oral hygiene with ease.

Six Month Smiles

Perfect for adults seeking quick cosmetic improvements, this innovative system focuses on the teeth that show when you smile, delivering noticeable results in just six months for many patients.

Space Maintainers

Essential for growing smiles, these custom devices preserve proper spacing for permanent teeth after premature loss of baby teeth, preventing future alignment issues and reducing the need for extensive orthodontic treatment later.
