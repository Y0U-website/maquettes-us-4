Why Would I Need a Dental Implant?

Enjoy a Bright, Lasting Smile

Damaged teeth or tooth loss can make you feel self-conscious about your smile. Dental implants can restore that confidence by enhancing your functionality and overall oral health. With the longest-lasting, secure tooth replacement option like dental implants in Alexandria, you can get back to comfortably eating, speaking, and smiling. Call (571) 222-5072 today!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Unparalleled Comfort

Because dental implants act like your tooth’s natural root, they feel just like normal teeth and sit comfortably.

Natural Look

Dental implants, which are usually made from porcelain, are the most naturally-appearing restorative option.

Preserve Your Oral Health

Dental implants are beneficial in preventing cavities and gum disease and preserve a healthy jawbone.

Treatment Timeline

What to Expect at Every Step

Placement

Step 1

During your placement appointment, a small opening in the gums and jaw will be made to place the implant. Then, the area will be cleaned and sutured shut to encourage proper healing.

Healing & Osseointegration

Step 2

After the implant surgery, you will need up to two weeks to heal. However, it may take up to six months for the implant to bond to your bone structure in a process called osseointegration.

Applying the Restoration

Step 3

A dental implant restoration will be attached to your implant to complete the restoration. While your implant heals and bonds with your jaw tissue, Alexandria dentist Dr. Han will take images and impressions of your teeth during your follow-ups, and begin building your dental implant restoration during this time.
