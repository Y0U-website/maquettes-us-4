What Are Dental Implants?

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

While getting dental implants may be a much more involved process than other options like dentures or dental bridges, the results are well worth the effort! During your consultation, we’ll go over each step of your treatment plan in detail so that you’ll know exactly what to expect. For now, here’s a simple outline of the four-step dental implant process.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

If you’re interested in rebuilding your smile with dental implants, your first step will be meeting with Dr. Philipps. She’ll review what your goals are, go over your medical history, and inspect your mouth to ensure you’re a good candidate for dental implants. If we detect any signs of issues that could cause your dental implants to fail, such as gum disease, low jawbone density, or nightly teeth grinding, we’ll plan to address them before starting your treatment.

Dental Implant Surgery

Once your oral health is on track, we can move onto the surgical placement of your dental implant. This relatively straightforward surgery starts with a small incision in the gum tissue. Then, your Midland implant dentist will insert a small titanium post into your jawbone to mimic the missing tooth’s roots. The post is then covered with a cap to protect it during the healing process and maintain the shape of your gum tissue.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

As soon as your final restoration has been created, you’ll return to our office for the final step in your dental implant treatment. Using the abutment piece, we’ll securely attach your new teeth to the dental implant post. We’ll check that your bite feels comfortable and make any necessary minor adjustments, and then you’ll be ready to start enjoying your newly rebuilt smile!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Hundreds of thousands of dental implants are placed each year in the United States, as more people learn about the superior benefits these prosthetics provide. Their unique placement and long lifespan make them an attractive option for tooth replacement, and their ability to improve your health, confidence, and quality of life makes them a worthwhile investment. Discover the many benefits of dental implants by checking out the list below to see what so many others are already experiencing.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • You Get to Eat Your Favorite Foods: Not only do dental implants last longer, but they make basic tasks like chewing your favorite foods easy. Whether you’re chewing your favorite cut of steak or you’re biting into an apple, there’s never any worry of your replacement tooth becoming loose or falling out.
  • They’re Easy to Maintain: Dental implants do not require special tools for cleaning. Instead, you can use a soft-bristled toothbrush, nonabrasive toothpaste, regular dental floss, and an antimicrobial mouthwash to keep your real and artificial teeth healthy.
  • You’ll Feel More Confident: When looking in the mirror to see your complete smile, you’ll feel more confident moving forward with your day because of how natural your dental implants look.

Health Benefits

  • They Offer Jawbone Stimulation: The placement of your dental implants means that they fuse with the existing bone over time. As a result, they will continue to stimulate your jawbone so that the chances of bone loss and facial sagging are significantly reduced.
  • They Keep Natural Teeth in Place: Tooth loss often leads to the misalignment of natural teeth because they tend to shift over time. With dental implants, though, everything stays in place.
  • You Enjoy Better Overall Health: Tooth loss is commonly linked to an array of diseases, including diabetes, heart disease, rheumatoid arthritis, and even osteoporosis. By choosing dental implants to replace missing teeth, you can lower your risk of these systemic issues and enjoy better overall health.

Long-Term Benefits

You’ll Save More Money: Traditional dentures and dental bridges require regular replacement over time; however, dental implants, although they cost more upfront, will save you money in the long run. You won’t need to buy denture adhesives or see your implant dentist in Midland for replacements as long as you commit to taking proper care of your new smile.

  • They Have a High Success Rate: Dental implants maintain success rates as high as 98 percent, even after 10 years.
  • They Have Greater Longevity: Since dental implants integrate with your existing bone and gum tissue, you can expect your tooth to last significantly longer than other tooth replacement methods (30+ years)!

Who Dental Implants Can Help

One of the reasons dental implants are considered so versatile is because they can be used to replace teeth in a variety of situations. Whether you’re missing one tooth, multiple teeth, or all of the teeth in either of your arches, you can get them replaced with dental implants.

Missing One Tooth

To replace just one tooth, a single implant is placed inside of the jaw, then closely monitored to ensure the healing process is successful. Afterward, an abutment is placed to both stabilize the eventual restoration and keep it attached in the long-term. A ceramic crown is placed on top, which keeps your smile looking natural.

Missing Multiple Teeth

To replace multiple consecutive teeth at a time, a pair of dental implants can be placed to hold a dental bridge. Instead of modifying existing tooth enamel so it can effectively hold the restoration, the implants themselves do all of the heavy lifting. This is ideal if you don’t want to affect your current tooth enamel.

Missing All Teeth

To replace all teeth in a given arch or multiple teeth on opposite sides of the mouth, an implant denture can be held with a series of dental implants. One of the benefits of this method is that you can often replace an entire arch of teeth with just four to six dental implants.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants in Midland are built to last, but their long-term success largely depends on how well you take care of them. With the right routine, implants can look, feel, and function like natural teeth for many, many years. Dr. Laura Philipps and her team guide patients every step of the way, from placement to maintenance, ensuring you know how to protect your new smile and enjoy the full benefits of your investment.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Dental implants can’t decay themselves, but the surrounding gum tissue and bone still need protection from bacteria. Brush at least twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss carefully around your implant, and consider using an alcohol-free, antimicrobial rinse.

Good oral hygiene helps prevent peri-implantitis, an infection similar to gum disease that threatens implant security. Keeping your mouth clean is the simplest, most effective way to safeguard your dental implants and preserve your smile.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Your implants are strong, but they still rely on a healthy body for long-term support. A diet full of calcium, vitamin D, protein, and fiber will improve your gum health and keep the tissues around your implant well-nourished.

Fresh fruits and vegetables are also great natural toothbrushes that help clean your teeth as you chew. So, don’t forget to include them with every meal you can. In addition, try to limit sugary snacks and acidic drinks like soda.

Break Bad Habits

Some habits can shorten the lifespan of your dental implants without you even realizing it. For example, smoking, vaping, or chewing tobacco slows healing, increases the risk of infection, and weakens gum and bone support.

Nail-biting, chewing on pens, or opening packages with your teeth are also good examples of implant-chipping behaviors that can put your restorations at risk. Breaking these habits is often tough, but if you’re serious about stopping, our team is happy to provide resources.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Like natural teeth, dental implants need extra protection during physical activities. If you play sports, getting a custom athletic mouthguard to cushion your implants against injury is a must.

For those who grind their teeth at night (bruxism), a nightguard prevents chips, fractures, and loosening of your implant restorations. Taking these simple protective steps ensures your implants stay secure, so you can enjoy them for a lifetime.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Even with excellent at-home care, routine visits to Dr. Philipps and Dr. Ramos are essential. Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar in hard-to-reach areas, and exams allow your dentist to check the health of your implants, gums, and surrounding teeth.

Regular checkups also ensure that potential issues get caught early when they’re easiest to treat. By staying proactive and seeing us every six months, you can extend the life of your implants and stay confident for decades to come.

Dental Implant FAQs

Does getting dental implants hurt?

If you feel nervous about the procedure, you can always speak to Dr. Philipps about sedation options to help calm your nerves before the surgery takes place.

What is the recovery process like after dental implant surgery?

For the days following your dental implant surgery, you may experience some tenderness and discomfort as your gums heal. However, this pain should be manageable with over-the-counter medications such as ibuprofen. You may also experience some bruising of your skin and gums or minor bleeding, but this is a typical effect and isn’t anything to be concerned about. After your surgery, you can use a cold compress on your face to help reduce any swelling. Be sure to place it on the affected area for 10 minutes on/10 minutes off.

It’s important to keep your mouth clean and regularly rinse with warm salt water throughout the following days. This will provide you with relief, soothe your sore oral tissues, and help clean the area. To help with tenderness, it’s also important to maintain a soft diet as your gums heal.

How long will it be before I can use my new teeth?

Every dental implant treatment timeline differs depending on factors such as how many teeth you need replaced and what type of restoration you need to have crafted. Based on your needs, we’ll let you know how long your individual dental implant procedure will take. Typically, the process occurs over the course of several months because it requires at least two healing periods. These are essential for the long-term success of your implants, and patients agree that the wait is well worth the benefits.

How successful are dental implants?

The modern dental implant procedure has been around for several decades and boasts an impressive 98 percent success rate. With the proper maintenance and care following your procedure, you can make a fast and problem-free recovery.
