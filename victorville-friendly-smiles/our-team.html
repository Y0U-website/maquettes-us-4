Our Team

From our front office team members and back office dental assistants to our dentists, we show that the best customer experience comes from genuine care and concern. Our goal is to ensure every appointment is comfortable, relieves any discomfort you may have and gives you the smile you’ve always wanted!

Dr. Dan Rusen DDS Dr. Dan Rusen has been a valued member of Victorville Friendly Smiles for 8 years as our general dentist. Dr. Dan was born in the city of Constanta, Romania, and moved to the US when he was 13 years old with his parents. He pursued a genetics degree at UC Davis, and completed his DDS degree at University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni. Dr. Dan met his wife, Dr. Tang, who is also our other general dentist, in 2011 in dental school, and married in 2016. He enjoys cooking, camping and hiking, and spending time with his wife and daughter, who is almost two.

Ruby Green Ruby has been a valued member of Victorville Friendly Smiles since the office was first established as our regional manager. Ruby takes care of all front office matters for the office. She is highly proficient in her position, having worked in the dental field over 21 years. Ruby is proud to have been born and raised in Barstow. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with family and taking care of her grandbabies, of which she is blessed to have eight.

David Trochez David has been a valued member of Barstow Friendly Smiles since the office was first established as our regional manager. David takes care of all back office assisting matters for the office. He is highly proficient in back office dentistry, having worked in the dental field for over 23 years. He has trained most of our assistants. David was born in Honduras and moved to Barstow when he was 12. He enjoys spending time with his family, grilling, traveling and riding his Harley.
