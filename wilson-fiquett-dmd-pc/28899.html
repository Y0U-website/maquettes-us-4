Dr. Timothy Fiquett

Dr. Timothy L. Fiquett, a native of Phenix City, AL., moved to Fort Payne in the summer of 1995. Dr. Tim graduated from Glenwood High School in 1987 as Valedictorian of his senior class. He is a graduate of the University of Alabama, receiving a B.S. in Biology. He earned his D.M.D. degree from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry.

Dr. Tim has served on various community service boards over the years that benefit the people of our community. Recently, through organizations like Dentistry from the Heart, Dr. Tim provides dental services to those in our community who are less fortunate at no cost. Dr. Tim also partners with other local organizations to provide needed dental services.

Dr. Tim attends extensive continuing education courses covering all aspects of general and family dentistry. As a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry, Dr. Tim strives to improve the quality and delivery of care to his patients.

Dr. Tim and his wife, Sherri, have three children, Forrest, Noah, and Olivia. When away from the office, Dr. Tim enjoys hiking, cycling, skiing, and worshipping at the Church of the Highlands.
