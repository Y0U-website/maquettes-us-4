Dentist

Dr. Mark C. Wilson

Dr. Mark C. Wilson was born in Fort Payne, Alabama, and graduated from Fort Payne High School in 1976. He graduated with honors from Auburn University in 1980 with a B.S. Degree in Chemistry. He received his dental degree from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry in 1984 and was awarded the International College of Dentists Outstanding Achievement Award. Following dental school, Dr. Wilson completed a one-year general practice residency at the VA Hospital in Birmingham, Alabama. Dr. Wilson returned to Fort Payne in 1985 to start his dental practice.

Dr. Wilson is married to Donna Estes Wilson, who also is a native of Fort Payne. They have two daughters. Dr. Katherine (Katie) Wilson Teel is a pharmacist, and she, her husband, Jim, and son, Tripp, live in Atlanta, Georgia. Meg Wilson Massey teaches first grade at Wills Valley Elementary School, and she and her husband, John Massey, PT, DPT, and son, John Wilson, reside in Fort Payne, Alabama. The Wilsons are active members of Fort Payne First Methodist Church.

Continuing education is important to Dr. Wilson, and he attends numerous comprehensive classes each year. He is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. His hobbies include hunting, fishing, skeet shooting, and water sports.
