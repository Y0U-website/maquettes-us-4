The practice

Are you looking for patient-centered and personalized dental care in Fort Payne, AL? Would you like to work with a dental team that you can trust to have your best interests at heart? Would you love to go to a dental office that feels like home?

Welcome to Wilson & Fiquett, DMD, PC! Our team provides you with the skills and expertise of three dentists with over 60 years of combined experience. Our dental practice has proudly been serving our neighbors in Fort Payne for over 38 years, and we look forward to being a part of your family for many years to come.

We welcome new patients to Wilson & Fiquett, DMD, PC, and you can expect a warm and friendly experience when you first visit our dental office. You’ll receive a tour of our dental office to make you feel right at home. You will also have plenty of time to visit with your hygienist and your dentist to discuss your goals or concerns for your smile during your teeth cleaning and examination.

Children can have their first visit to our Fort Payne dental office, starting around the age of two years old. We love working with children, and we aim to make every experience your little one has at the dentist is a positive one.

Whether your son or daughter is ready for their first dental visit, or you have a family member who is considering implant-retained or conventional dentures, our team is here to help.

We do not use a one-size-fits-all approach when it comes to dentistry. Instead, our dentists spend time getting to know you so we can provide personalized dental care that is focused on the things that are most important to you.

Our patients appreciate that our Fort Payne dental office is well equipped with modern technology including a Cone Beam CT Scanning Machine for 3-D views. Our team is committed to providing you with the best experience possible, and using the latest technology and techniques helps us do that.

If you or a loved one feels anxious or fearful about dental appointments, we understand, and we want to help. We offer nitrous oxide, or laughing gas, to help our patients feel relaxed during dental procedures. Our dentists are also happy to discuss other forms of anxiety relief at the time of consultation if you feel that it would benefit you.
