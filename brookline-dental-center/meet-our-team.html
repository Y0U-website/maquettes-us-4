Our Hygienists

Katie Duggan

RDH

Katie grew up in Ft. Lauderdale, Florida and moved to Boston in 2016. She has a bachelors in Psychology and Sociology from the University of Central Florida and received her Dental Hygiene degree from the Forsyth School of Dental Hygiene. She is passionate about continuing education and actively participates in a study group looking at the relationship of oral and overall health. Her goal is to make every patient feel comfortable while also educating them about their health.

Outside of work, Katie enjoys spending time with family and friends, weekend trips to the cape, baking, and trying new restaurants!

Kathy Tran

RDH

Outside of work, Kathy enjoys spending time with family and friends, traveling, and trying new foods and experiences.
