Our Dental Implant Process

When considering a dental implant, a comprehensive exam is done on your teeth, gums, and jawbones, and digital images are taken. Our implant dentists discuss your oral health history, smile goals, and expected outcomes from the procedure. After your evaluation, we present your options and help you decide the appropriate treatment for your case.

Once you&#x27;re ready to move forward with your dental implant, we can schedule your surgical appointment. Your implant posts are then carefully and precisely placed in our office. Afterward, you&#x27;re provided with detailed care instructions to help promote healing and comfort. While your implant post becomes integrated into your jawbone, your custom restoration is made, and it&#x27;s placed after a period of healing.

Benefits of Having an Implant Placed

Choosing to replace a missing tooth with a dental implant offers more than just cosmetic improvement. Implants restore strength and function to your bite while also protecting your long-term oral health. Unlike other options, they&#x27;re designed to integrate with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation that feels natural. Some of the benefits you can experience from having an implant placed include:

  • Durability : Dental implant posts are designed to last a lifetime, and the restoration can last for over a decade with proper maintenance.
  • Appearance : Implant-supported restorations look natural and lifelike in your smile.
  • Preservation : Dental implants work to help preserve your jawbone from deterioration and keep teeth from shifting out of place.
  • Functionality : Restoring your chewing ability and speech are possible with dental implants.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

When making decisions about your care, it&#x27;s common to have many questions or concerns. We want you to always feel confident throughout your dental implant journey. Our team is happy to answer any questions or concerns you have about them so you can feel informed about your options. Some questions previously asked included:

What if my restoration gets damaged? If your dental implant restoration becomes damaged or worn, our dentists will be happy to work with you to repair or replace it. Accessing the implant restoration is simple, and you often won&#x27;t need any additional surgery.

Can I get a dental implant if I have gum disease? It&#x27;s important to treat gum disease as soon as symptoms begin to appear and before you have a dental implant placed. Our team will work with you to maximize the success of your implant procedure by restoring your optimal oral health beforehand.

Are my other teeth affected by a dental implant? No. Your restoration is designed to seamlessly integrate into your existing dental structure, allowing us to repair your smile without negatively impacting any of your surrounding teeth.
