At Mark Streitz Dental, we believe everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care—even without insurance. That’s why we offer an in-office Dental Savings Plan to make preventive and restorative care more affordable for individuals and families.

Dr. Mark was born and raised in Joliet. His father, Richard Streitz, opened the dental office in 1951. Dr. Mark completed his undergraduate degree at the College of St. Francis before graduating Loyola’s school of dentistry in 1990. In his free time he enjoys aviation, tennis, fishing and, of course, spending time with his family.
