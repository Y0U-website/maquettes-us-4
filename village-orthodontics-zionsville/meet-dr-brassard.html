Meet Dr. Brassard

Dr. Nick knew he wanted to be an orthodontist from an early age. To him, the profession provides the perfect combination of personal interaction, community involvement, health sciences, and use of his natural attention to detail. He feels fortunate to be part of such an esteemed field.

Dr. Nick graduated from Indiana University and received his dental training at Indiana University School of Dentistry. He attended the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine where he received a Certificate of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics and a Master of Science in Dentistry degree.

Along with Dr. Nicholson, Dr. Nick is a member of the American Dental Association, American Association of Orthodontics, Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists, and the Indiana Association of Orthodontists. His training provided him with comprehensive knowledge using the most current, respected philosophies in orthodontics.

Dr. Nick believes a coordinated team approach amongst dental professionals is essential in properly treating patients and maintaining their oral health. Dr. Nick takes great joy in getting to know the patients, families, and colleagues that make this such a wonderful place to practice.
