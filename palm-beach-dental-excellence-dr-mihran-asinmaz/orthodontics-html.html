How Do Clear Aligners Work?

We will take impressions of your teeth and create a virtual model that will be used to plan your tooth movement. We can show you what your teeth look like before we start and what they will look like once your treatment is complete!

Every two weeks you will wear a new set of aligners until the treatment is finished and your teeth are firmly in their new location.

For most patients, our team will need to see you every 6 weeks to make sure the teeth are moving as planned. A minor case can take as little as 10 weeks to complete, and more complex cases can span over a year. The majority of patients using clear aligners are done after approximately 5-9 months of treatment time.

Who Can Clear Aligners Help?

If you are thinking of straightening your teeth, it is most likely because you are not happy with the way they look. If that’s the case, then clear aligners may be the solution you’ve been looking for! The only way to know for certain is by scheduling a consultation, but feel free to read on if you are interested in learning about the different orthodontic problems that they can correct.

Crowded Teeth

In addition to negatively impacting your self-esteem, crowded teeth make it hard to practice good home hygiene. That’s because the unique crevices between your teeth can trap plaque, increasing the incidence of tooth decay and/or periodontal disease in the process. Fortunately, many patients with mild to moderate cases are candidates for clear aligners, which allow us to eliminate crowding without metal brackets and wires.

Gaps Between Teeth

Like crowded teeth, gapped teeth can get worse with time. So, if you are not happy with the way your teeth look now, it’s likely that you will not be happy with the way they look in the future. That’s why it’s best not to take the “wait and see” approach. Instead, schedule a consultation with us so we can determine if you’re a good candidate for clear aligners. If you are, then we can gradually close the spaces (often in just 12-18 months, too).

Overbite

Do your upper teeth protrude beyond your lower ones more than they should, negatively impacting your self-esteem and speaking patterns in the process? If so, we have good news: clear aligners can fix overbites. So, don’t just assume metal braces are the only path to the straight, healthy, and beautiful smile that you deserve. You may be surprised to find out that your orthodontic treatment won’t involve any metal at all!

Underbite

Do you have an underbite (when the lower teeth sit in front of the upper ones)? If so, it’s important to schedule a consultation with our dedicated cosmetic dentist , Dr. Asinmaz. This is important because the sooner you intervene, the shorter the treatment time will be. Not only that, but we can save you from chronic jaw pain, premature wear, and other common issues that stem from this orthodontic problem.

Crossbite

What if only a few of your upper teeth sit behind your lower ones? In that case, you may have a crossbite, which is an orthodontic problem that can lead to several serious problems, including TMJ dysfunction. For that reason, we recommend exploring your treatment options sooner rather than later. If we decide together that clear aligners are the ideal solution, then we will have a custom series made to gently align your teeth and your bite.

Open Bite

When you bite down, is there a space between your upper and lower teeth that prevents them from meeting evenly? If you answered “yes,” then you may have an open bite. Fortunately, like the other bite problems listed here, clear aligner treatment is often a solution. So, if you’re less than enthused about the idea of wearing metal brackets and wires, don’t worry – you may not have to!

Benefits of Clear Aligners

Have you ever wondered why clear aligners are so popular? If so, this next section is for you! In it, we’re covering a few of the many benefits that come with this discreet orthodontic treatment, starting with comfort.

Improved Comfort

Traditional braces continue to be a reliable and effective orthodontic treatment. However, they aren’t the most comfortable because the metal brackets and wires are sharp. Plus, they need to be tightened regularly to keep you on track with your treatment plan. Fortunately, clear aligners are a much more comfortable alternative. In fact, many patients don’t experience any soft tissue irritation or mouth sores at all!

Better Dental Hygiene

Clear aligner patients also don’t have to worry about all the food that typically gets stuck in traditional braces or the constant cleaning braces require to maintain healthy teeth and gums. In fact, since they are removable, all you need to do is take them off and store them properly first. Then, you can brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash like usual.

Freedom to Eat Your Favorite Foods

Another perk of this smile-enhancing solution : you can eat whatever you want. Simply remove the aligner, eat and drink what you desire, pop in the tray, and away you go (after brushing your teeth, of course!).

Important reminder: Orthodontic emergencies aren’t limited to broken attachments and misplaced aligners. Tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health problems will negatively affect your treatment, so it’s best to eat mostly smile-friendly foods instead of sugary ones.

Shorter Average Treatment Timeline

One benefit of clear aligners that often isn’t talked about is the fact that the average treatment timeline is shorter. While traditional braces usually take 2-3 years, clear aligners often deliver results in just 12-18 months. With that said, there are a lot of factors that need to be considered, including the complexity of your case and how well you follow the guidelines provided. So, if you want an estimate of your teeth-straightening journey, the best thing to do is schedule a consultation.

Virtually Undetectable Aligners

Of course, the list of perks would not be complete without touching on how discreet the aligners are. There are a few reasons for this, including that they are see-through and custom-made to fit the contours of your teeth perfectly. As a result, they blend in, and no unwanted attention will be drawn to your smile during your orthodontic treatment.

If you are thinking of straightening your teeth, it is most likely because you are not happy with the way they look. They are either too crowded or there are spaces that you wish were not there. While correcting these issues will no doubt result in a nice smile, there are other reasons to consider clear aligners.

Living with Clear Aligners

Since clear aligners are removable, the transition into life with braces is considerably more seamless. After all, there aren’t dietary restrictions, cumbersome oral hygiene regimens, or monthly adjustment appointments! With all of that said, there are a few things you should know upfront, like how to meet the 20+ hour-a-day wear-time. With this in mind, read on to learn some helpful information about living with clear aligners.

Wearing Your Trays

As we mentioned above, you need to wear your aligners for 20+ hours a day in order to stay on-track with your treatment plan. Don’t let this overwhelm you though – it’s easier than you think with the right best practices in place. The first one is only taking them out when absolutely necessary, like before eating. The second is multi-tasking where you can, including drinking coffee alongside your breakfast or lunch instead of separately.

Cleaning Your Aligners

Cleaning your aligners is easy! Simply rinse them with clean, cool water before using a soft-bristled toothbrush and some clear, mild dishwashing liquid to gently scrub the surface. It’s also a good idea to soak them for about 15 minutes each day in a dentist-recommended cleaner to remove saliva, plaque, and other debris. Last, but certainly not least, make sure to rinse your trays with clean, cool water each time you take them off.

Eating & Drinking

Good news: there aren’t any dietary restrictions with Invisalign! In fact, there’s basically only one rule: you need to remove your aligners beforehand (with the exception of water). While you’re enjoying your meal or beverage, keep your trays in their designated case instead of wrapping them in a napkin or leaving them on the table. It may seem like a good option in the moment, but your aligners can easily get damaged or misplaced, resulting in unwanted treatment plan delays.

Losing or Damaging a Tray

Whether you accidentally lost your aligners or there is a serious crack down one side of them, the next step is the same: contact our West Palm Beach dental office. The sooner you do, the sooner we can determine the next best steps. Oftentimes, it’s moving onto the next set in the series a bit earlier than anticipated. However, there are some cases where we need to have a new set made, and we’ll ask you to wear your previous pair in the meantime.

Routine Check-Ins

In order to ensure your teeth are moving as anticipated, we will schedule check-in visits every 6-8 weeks. During these appointments, we will make sure that you’re on-track with your treatment plan and, if you’re not, make the necessary changes. We will also give you your next batch of aligners, answer any questions you have, and schedule your next appointment!

Factors That Affect the Cost of Clear Aligners

Because each patient’s smile is unique, and each treatment is so intricate, there is no fixed cost of clear aligners in West Palm Beach. We can’t even give you an accurate cost estimate until Dr. Asinmaz has had the opportunity to review your specific case and plan out your treatment. The most influential factor that affects how much this form of treatment costs is the number of aligners you’ll need to wear, which varies based on:

  • The complexity of your case – Naturally, it takes more time (and more aligners) to straighten extensively crooked teeth than it does to fix minor orthodontic flaws.
  • Whether you’re straightening one or both arches – Most patients need to wear aligners on both their upper and lower teeth throughout their treatment, but that’s not always the case. If you only need to align one arch, your overall costs will be lower.
  • Your compliance with our treatment instructions – Your treatment could end up taking longer (and being more expensive) if you don’t wear the aligners as directed, lose an aligner, or don’t attend regular progress visits.

Clear Aligner FAQs

Am I Too Old for Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are perfectly suited for adults, as the aligners are nearly invisible and will minimally interfere with your everyday life. Clear aligners will enhance your smile and self-confidence, and you will have teeth that are in ideal positions for better at-home care that will help you keep them for a lifetime.

How Long Do Clear Aligners Take?

While there is an average timeframe of 12-18 months with clear aligners, the ultimate amount of time you can expect to wear these trays depends on a few unique factors:

No two patient cases are the same, so while you might only need to wear clear aligners for 14 months, your friend might need to wear them for a few additional months. One thing is certain, though. You must commit to the process if you want to see finalized results in a shorter amount of time than you would with traditional braces.

  • The severity of your overcrowding, gaps, or bite misalignment
  • The number of trays you will need to complete treatment
  • How committed you are to the process of wearing your trays 20-22 hours each day
  • Whether you lose one of your aligners or accidentally damage one

Do I Need to Wear a Retainer After Clear Aligners?

Wearing a retainer after completing clear aligner treatment is necessary. Your teeth are not yet integrated into their new location, which means that without some form of retention, they can move back into their original spot. This can undo countless hours of hard work and mean thousands of dollars were spent for nothing. Once finished with aligners, you must commit to wearing your customized retainer according to the timeframe provided by our team.

Initially, you can expect to wear your retainer all of the time. But after several months, you may be able to switch and wear it only at night while you sleep.

Can I Be Treated with Clear Aligners If I’ve Worn Braces in the Past?

This is a question that many patients have, especially those who wore traditional braces as a teenager. Time passes, retainers are worn less and less, and teeth begin to slowly shift out of alignment. Knowing whether you can pursue clear aligners requires meeting with your dentist to discuss your options. In most cases, if the problem is only minor, you will likely be able to move forward with clear aligners. However, if your bite is too far off, the only viable solution might be traditional braces once again.
