Accessibility Statement

  • Design Considerations: We aim for clear and consistent navigation, readable text, and appropriate color contrast.
  • Alternative Text for Images: We strive to provide descriptive alternative text for all meaningful images.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Our website is designed to be navigable using a keyboard.
  • Compatibility: We aim for compatibility with commonly used assistive technologies and web browsers.
