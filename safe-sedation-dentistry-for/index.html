The practice

**There are 2 & 3 year olds with baby bottle caries- mouths full of deep dental caries who need hours of extensive dental work. Such a young child is incapable of being compliant with the treatment they need.

**There are older children, some with hyperactivity or autism who also can not physically stay still without physical restraints.

** The other category of children who can not endure their necessary dentistry are those children who have been previously hurt and traumatized. These children were harmed physically and emotionally, they no longer can trust adults and be vulnerable.

Based on data available; more than one thousand times safer than sedation in a dental office

A routine practice in pediatric dental offices employs a hard board with straps to “papoose” a young child. This immobilization is useful for emergency rooms or in other dire circumstances but not repeatedly on the same child. Often children are papoosed while fighting, crying, gagging and even vomiting. No child should be physically coerced by adults while trying to fight back with all their strength.
