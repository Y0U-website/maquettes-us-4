Your First Appointment

Your initial appointment is the first step in your smile journey! We will introduce you to our team and show you around. Then, we provide you with an oral exam and consultation. You’ll receive a cleaning and digital x-rays to diagnose any developing issues such as decay. Then, Dr. Friedman will recommend a treatment plan for you based on your unique smile. This appointment is also a great time to discuss any cosmetic treatments you might be considering.

We take the time to explain your dental condition to you and why any recommended treatment will be of benefit. We are happy to address any questions and concerns you may have. Our treatment coordinator will discuss with you a written plan itemizing each procedure, fees, and what we estimate your insurance will cover. She can also talk to you about your financial options. Then, it’s up to you whether or not you decide to undergo treatment. We provide you with the information necessary to make a choice that is right for you!
