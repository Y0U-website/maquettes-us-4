A comprehensive dental exam is performed at the initial visit, and subsequent regular check-up exams involve the examination of diagnostic X-rays, oral cancer screening, gum disease evaluation, and the examination of tooth decay and existing restorations.

Dental implants are titanium artificial roots surgically placed into the jawbone by a dentist or periodontist, serving as a natural-looking and fixed solution to replace missing teeth and stabilize dentures.

Periodontal disease, which involves bacteria and plaque filling the space between the tooth and gums, is treated based on its severity, ranging from regular cleanings for early gingivitis to specialized deep cleaning or surgery for advanced stages.
