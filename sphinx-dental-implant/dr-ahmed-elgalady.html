Dr. Ahmed Elgalady

Dr. Ahmed Elgalady brings over 14 years of experience in dentistry to the Bunnell community. He began his dental journey at Mansoura University in Egypt, earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree in 2009. After completing a one-year residency in general dentistry, he pursued advanced training in the United States, completing a two-year residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at the University of Florida in 2021.

Dr. Elgalady is an active member of several professional organizations, including the American Dental Association (ADA) , the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI), and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) . His dedication to continued education and professional growth ensures his patients receive the most effective and up-to-date treatments available.

Dr. Elgalady believes in treating people, not just teeth. With a comprehensive approach to dental care, Dr. Elgalady provides services ranging from root canals and cosmetic dentistry to dental implants and full-mouth rehabilitation. He is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles and is especially skilled in making those with dental anxiety feel at ease.

If you’re looking for a dentist who combines experience, compassion, and advanced technology, Dr. Elgalady is here to help.
