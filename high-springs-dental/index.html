The practice

When you turn to us to take care of your oral health, you can trust that we always strive for the best patient experience. Your comfort is our priority, and we are always available to answer any questions or concerns you might have about your visit.

Dr. Curtis Von Gunten believes in the power of preventative care. He doesn’t want to have to react to a painful problem – he wants to help you prevent that dental issue from happening in the first place.

Dr. Curtis Von Gunten wants every patient to feel like family when they step into our warm and inviting office. He is a product of the local community and loves to be involved. Even before attending dental school at the University of Florida (Go Gators!), Dr. Von Gunten started in the field by working as a dental assistant.

In his free time, he enjoys spending time outdoors with his wife, daughter, and dog – the perfect place for him to practice his photography hobby.
