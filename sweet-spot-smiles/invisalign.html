Invisalign Clear Aligners

  • Removable trays let you eat, brush, and floss with less disruption to your routine.
  • Smooth aligners minimize mouth irritation, a common issue with brackets and wires.
  • Digital treatment planning offers a precise, phased approach to tooth movement.
  • Fewer food restrictions simplify mealtimes compared to traditional braces.
  • Office visits are often efficient due to the absence of wire adjustments.
  • Maintaining oral hygiene is more straightforward, supporting gum health during treatment.

How Invisalign Works

  • Consultation and 3D Scan: Digital images and scans map your teeth and bite.
  • Custom Plan: A comprehensive plan outlines how each aligner set will progress the teeth.
  • Attachments and Refinements: Small, tooth-colored attachments or minor enamel reshaping may be employed to aid movement.
  • Daily Wear: Aligners must be worn 20–22 hours per day , removing them only for eating, drinking anything but water, and oral hygiene.
  • Aligner Changes: Transition to the next set on a schedule, typically every one to two weeks.
  • Check-ins: Regular visits monitor progress and provide subsequent aligner sets.
  • Retention: Post-treatment retainers maintain your new smile positions.

What to Expect With Invisalign

You may experience gentle pressure for a day or two with each new aligner set, which is a normal sign of active tooth movement. Initially, speech might be slightly affected, but your tongue will quickly adjust.

To ensure optimal results, wear your aligners as directed, and be sure to brush and floss after meals. Clean your aligners before reinserting them. To avoid warping, remove your aligners when consuming hot liquids like coffee or tea, and always store them in a protective case.

Treatment duration varies but often ranges from several months to about a year, contingent upon the complexity of your case and adherence to guidelines. Some plans may involve mid-course "refinements," which entail additional aligners for precise adjustments. After completing treatment, retainers are crucial to prevent any tooth relapse, and your provider will guide you on the correct retainer usage and care.

Understanding Your Invisalign Options

Invisalign offers flexibility for adults and many teens by incorporating features such as compliance indicators if necessary. However, not all bites are identical, and specific tooth movements might mandate attachments or elastics. If Invisalign isn&#x27;t your best option, alternatives like ceramic braces or alternative clear aligner systems may be discussed to meet your goals.
