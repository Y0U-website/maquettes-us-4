Dental Implants Locust Grove, GA

Patients at Sweet Spot Smiles frequently express how dental implants have positively transformed their lives as implants feel just like real teeth, allowing them to eat anything worry-free. Such feedback highlights the life-changing effects this treatment can offer.

  • Jawbone stimulation that helps mitigate bone loss after a tooth extraction.
  • No impact on neighboring teeth, unlike traditional bridges that require altering adjacent teeth.
  • Secure fit without adhesives, slipping, or clicking, often associated with dentures.
  • Long-term solution with proper home care and regular dental checkups.
  • Simple daily care, akin to brushing and flossing your natural teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation and Planning

Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive consultation—a review of your medical and dental history, a detailed examination, and 3D imaging if necessary. During this visit, we evaluate your eligibility, discuss the implant options, and determine the number of implants you may need. We will also assess if bone grafting is essential.

Surgical Placement

Under local anesthesia, with optional sedation for comfort, implants are strategically placed in the jaw. Thanks to our modern techniques and advanced equipment, patients often describe experiencing pressure rather than pain during the procedure.

Healing and Osseointegration

Your bone will bond with the implant over several weeks to months, forming a solid base for the final restoration. To maintain your aesthetic appearance, a temporary tooth can be placed in visible areas during healing.

Abutment and Final Tooth

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached, and impressions are taken to create your custom crown, bridge, or denture. Designed to match your bite and tooth shade, this personalized approach ensures seamless restoration.

Follow-Up and Maintenance

Regular check-ups are crucial to monitor the health of your implant and surrounding tissues. Maintaining good oral hygiene practices, such as brushing, flossing, and using interdental brushes or water flossers, aids in preserving your implant for years.

What to Expect

A typical single implant procedure takes about an hour, with possible mild swelling or soreness for a few days. It&#x27;s common to recommend soft foods, gentle brushing, and prescribed rinses during initial recovery. Depending on bone quality and grafting needs, healing can take from three to six months before securing the final crown.

With diligent aftercare, dental implants offer high success rates. However, habits like smoking, unmanaged diabetes, and active gum disease can increase risks of complications. Many patients find the process similar to a routine extraction and are often able to return to work shortly after with basic precautions.

Implant vs. Bridge: Key Differences

An implant replaces both the root and crown of a tooth without altering neighboring structures. Unlike traditional bridges that depend on adjacent teeth for support and may require enamel removal, implants stimulate bone growth, preventing bone loss at the site of the missing tooth.
