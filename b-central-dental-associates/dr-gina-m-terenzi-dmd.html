* Reminder Before Booking: All 1st time patients must select New Patient Dental Exam as an appointment. This includes Cleaning and X-rays. I t is very imperative that you select this as the reason for your visit when booking your new patient appointment. If this is not done it will adversely effect your requested appointment not being accepted and rescheduled with a system selected next available new appointment date.

Also Please Note, when booking, if the system is not accepting your insurance, please skip and click on "Paying Myself" and put your insurance information with subscriber/member number in the comment section. If you are having problems with the online booking, please don’t hesitate to give us a call (617-722-0035) and we will be more than happy to assist you.

FYI:If you are not the guarantor of the insurance plan, we will need the full name, date of birth and subscriber/member number of the guarantor in order to verify your coverage.
