B. Central Dental Associates is located in the Beacon Hill area, conveniently serving the Boston metro. Our practice’s top priority is to provide you the highest quality dentistry care in a friendly, comfortable environment. Our friendly staff will attend to you as we use state of the art equipment (digital x-ray; paperless charts, etc.) to ensure that you receive the most effective care provided.

At B. Central Dental Associates, we recognize that every patient has different needs, and we will provide courteous service to each person who walks through our doors .

The safety of our patients and staff remains the priority and mission of the team at B. Central Dental Associates. As we reopen, We want to make sure that, during this time of uncertainty, our patients can feel confident that they are safe in our Clinics. Out of an abundance of caution with respect to the coronavirus (COVID-19).

In order to mitigate community spread, we are requiring all patients to answer some screening questions before their scheduled appointment.

Please Note if you arrive earlier than your scheduled appointment, please wait in the lobby or your car (if possible). For the safety of you and others we are limiting patients in the waiting area. Please let us know you have arrived and we will respond when your chair is ready.

Also please remember to bring/wear your face mask to your appointment. This is an unprecedented time. We wish you and your family continued health and safety.
