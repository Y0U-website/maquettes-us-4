Dr. Kollesar

Dr. Kollesar was born and raised in upstate New York in the Hudson Valley. Her pursuit of a career in dentistry began early in high school despite being a part of a family of computer engineers. Her career goals brought her to Buffalo, NY, where she completed her undergraduate education at The University of Buffalo studying biomedical science. She remained in Buffalo to complete her dental education and her pediatric specialty residency at The University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Kollesar is excited and passionate about providing quality dental care to the pediatric population of the Mat-Su Valley. She enjoys learning about every child and their families and strives to give children the best possible experience at the dentist.

She is moving to the area with her dog, Smokey, who is excited for Alaskan winters! Dr. Kollesar has always enjoyed the outdoors. She grew up hiking, skiing, kayaking, rock climbing, and traveling all around the US and internationally. She is so excited to bring these hobbies to Alaska and hopes to pick up more hobbies while here alongside her boyfriend, who is a pilot in the US Air Force.

Dr. Kollesar thanks you for welcoming her to your beautiful state! Make sure to let her know what your favorite things to do in Alaska are!
