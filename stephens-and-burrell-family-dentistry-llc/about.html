At TranscenDental Smiles of Georgia, we’re all about transcending fear, transcending self-consciousness, and transcending the ordinary Doctor-Patient relationship!

TranscenDental Smiles of Georgia is especially interested in making all our patients feel right at home. We are a team of pleasant and courteous professionals who strive to keep you informed and satisfied with your services.

Our administrative staff brings more than 40 years of customer service experience to serve you. Our clinical staff is composed of experienced dental assistants and hygienists who are dedicated to steadily upgrading their skills via continuing education and knowledge of the newest technology in dentistry.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Andrea Burrell

Dr. Andrea Burrell has been practicing attentive, personalized family dentistry for more than 29 years. She is a graduate of Howard University and the University of Florida, where she received her Doctorate of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Burrell maintains active membership in several professional organizations, including the American Orthodontic Society, the Georgia Dental Association, and the North Georgia Dental Society. She is very active in her community and shares her love of dentistry at career days with local schools. Dr. Burrell has also been involved with missionary work in Peru. She is married with three children.

Dr. Burrell is committed to educating the community on oral health. In her own words, “With proper education, nurturing, and guidance, we can help them attain and maintain good oral hygiene habits, thus making a lifelong healthier and happier smile.”

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Diane Stephens

I have been practicing dentistry for 39 years, and feel truly blessed to have found the profession I was meant to serve in early in life. I knew after my personal smile transformation that I wanted to do that for others as a dental professional.

Taking care of the needs of my patients is a privilege I don’t take for granted. Meeting so many wonderful people and being a small part of their lives is the best part by far. Helping people smile with confidence, educating them to be able to enjoy their teeth for a lifetime, and protecting their overall health by achieving optimum oral health is my goal!

I love providing comprehensive care for my patients. Guiding them through the progression toward complete oral health is exciting. Participating in a partnership with them to achieve the smile they want and deserve makes my job fun and rewarding. I love using my mind, my hands, and my heart every day on each patient.

Dr. Ahna Kelley

Dr. Ahna Kelley is overjoyed to be re-joining the team at Transcendental Smiles of Georgia. She prides herself on having a caring heart and gentle hands, which makes her a perfect fit for the care team. She says her life’s purpose is to heal those who need healing by developing and sharing the gifts and talents she’s been given. Dentistry has allowed Dr. Kelley to use her hands to remove sickness and disease from the oral cavity of her patients, thereby healing her patients, bringing them closer to complete oral health.

Dr. Kelley, an Atlanta native, is happy to return home to the office where her career in dentistry began. She believes the best doctors listen to their patients and learn from their patients to better serve their patients. She is dedicated to understanding the concerns and goals of each patient in order to provide the best comprehensive care possible. Schedule your appointment with Dr. Kelley without delay — she is ready to take great care of you!
