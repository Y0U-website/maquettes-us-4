Dental Implants in Denver, CO

Unlike traditional dental bridges or dentures, implants stimulate jawbone growth, restore full chewing ability, and prevent bone loss, making them one of the best long-term solutions for missing teeth.

  • Abutment – Connects the implant post to the final restoration.
  • Crown, Bridge, or Denture – A custom-made prosthetic that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants over other tooth replacement options offers several advantages:

  • Long-Term Durability – With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Improved Chewing and Speech – Functions like a natural tooth, allowing you to eat and speak comfortably.
  • Prevents Jawbone Loss – Stimulates bone growth and preserves facial structure.
  • Natural Look and Feel – Custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your smile.
  • No Impact on Nearby Teeth – Unlike traditional bridges, implants don’t require modifying adjacent teeth.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

If you have bone loss, we may recommend bone grafting to strengthen the jawbone before implant placement.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density
  • Are in good overall health for oral surgery
  • Are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene

Our Dental Implant Process

At TranscenDental Smiles of Georgia , we follow a comprehensive approach to ensure optimal results:

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging: We use 3D CT scanning to assess your jawbone and plan precise implant placement.
  • Implant Surgery: A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone through osseointegration.
  • Abutment & Restoration Placement: Once healed, we attach an abutment and a custom-made dental crown, bridge, or denture for a seamless finish.
  • Aftercare & Maintenance: Routine dental cleanings and check-ups help maintain implant longevity.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long do dental implants last?: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear.
  • Is the dental implant procedure painful?: We offer sedation dentistry and local anesthesia to ensure a comfortable experience. Most patients report only mild soreness after surgery.
  • Can I get dental implants if I have bone loss?: Yes! If your jawbone isn’t strong enough, we may recommend bone grafting to improve implant success.
  • How do I care for my dental implants?: Maintain good oral hygiene with regular brushing, flossing, and professional dental cleanings to ensure long-term success.
