Meet Our Team

Mark S. Tardugno, DDS

Dr. Mark S. Tardugno is a native of Rome, New York, and graduated from the Loyola University School of Dentistry in 1985.

Prior to dental school, he attended Fairleigh Dickinson University. Dr. Tardugno has been practicing dentistry for over 30 years and stays current on his education by completing many continuing education courses throughout the year.

In his free time, he enjoys golf, cooking, and spending time with his friends and family.

Mark A. Tardugno, DDS

Dr. Mark A. Tardugno is a native of Rome, New York, and graduated from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in 2019. Dr. Tardugno completed his post-graduate training at Upstate Medical University’s General Practice Residency in 2020.

Prior to dental school, he graduated from Syracuse University. Dr. Tardugno has also pursued continuing education in Implant Dentistry, completing an American Academy of Implant Dentistry “MaxiCourse” in Boston, MA.

In his free time, he enjoys playing music, golfing, watching SU sports, and spending time playing cards with his friends and family.
