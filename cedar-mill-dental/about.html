0 Our Team Ngan Hoang, DMD Dr. Hoang is a compassionate and experienced dentist serving the Portland area, with an impressive educational background and years of clinical experience. She completed her Bachelor of Sciences degree at Portland State University, where she graduated with honors majoring in Organismal Biology and minoring in Business Administration.

After completing her undergraduate studies, Dr. Hoang attended Kornberg University School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, where she received training in all aspects of dentistry. After graduating from dental school in 2015, Dr. Hoang returned to Oregon where she has been practicing. Dr. Hoang is a strong believer in lifetime learning, and when she’s not in the office, Dr. Hoang is taking courses to continually learn about the newest changes in technology and products. Additionally, Dr. Hoang had spent time shadowing Dr. Samuel Higdon on Temporomandibular joint disorders.

Dr. Hoang has been serving the community for over eight years, and her reputation as a skilled and caring practitioner has earned her a loyal following of patients. She believes that every patient deserves the highest quality dental care in a warm and welcoming environment, and she strives to make that a reality for everyone who walks through her doors.
