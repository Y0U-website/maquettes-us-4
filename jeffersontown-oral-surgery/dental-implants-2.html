The Implant Process at Jeffersontown Oral Surgery

Every dental implant case at our Jeffersontown office begins with a thorough consultation and 3D cone-beam CT (CBCT) imaging. This advanced technology — unavailable at most general dental offices — lets our surgeons evaluate bone volume, nerve anatomy, and sinus positioning with sub-millimeter precision before a single incision is made. For Fern Creek patients, this means a treatment plan that is customized to your exact anatomy, minimizing surprises and maximizing long-term success.

Step-by-Step Process

  • Consultation & Imaging: We review your dental and medical history, take CBCT images, and discuss your goals. You leave with a clear treatment plan and cost estimate.
  • Bone Grafting (if needed): Some patients who have experienced significant bone loss after tooth extraction may need a bone graft to build a stable foundation. We handle this in-office under comfortable sedation.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is placed in a brief outpatient surgical appointment, typically 30–90 minutes depending on the number of implants. Most patients return to desk work the next day.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: Over the following 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone. During this time, you may wear a temporary restoration.
  • Crown Placement: Your referring dentist or our team places the final custom crown. At this stage, your new tooth looks and works just like the real thing.

Frequently Asked Questions — Dental Implants for Fern Creek, KY Patients

How far is Jeffersontown Oral Surgery from Fern Creek?

Our office at 3841 Ruckriegel Parkway is approximately 10–12 minutes from central Fern Creek via Bardstown Road or I-265 (Gene Snyder Freeway). Free parking is available on-site.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults in good general health with adequate jawbone volume are excellent candidates. Patients who have experienced bone loss after a long-term missing tooth may need a bone graft first — a procedure our surgeons perform in-office. The best way to determine candidacy is a consultation with 3D imaging.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

Coverage varies by plan. Many dental insurance policies cover a portion of the implant crown or the surgical placement fee. Our team will verify your benefits before your procedure so you know exactly what to expect financially. We also offer third-party financing options for qualified patients.

How long does the entire implant process take for a Fern Creek patient?

From consultation to final crown, the process typically takes 4–8 months, depending on whether bone grafting is needed and how quickly osseointegration occurs. We work around your schedule and offer early morning appointments to minimize disruption to your Fern Creek or Louisville workday.

What is recovery like after implant surgery?

Most patients experience mild swelling and soreness for 3–5 days after placement, manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. The majority of Fern Creek patients return to work within 1–2 days. Our team provides detailed post-op instructions and is available for any questions during healing.

Can I get multiple implants at once?

Yes. Many patients choose to replace several missing teeth in a single surgical appointment, which is both efficient and cost-effective. Our surgeons will recommend the optimal treatment sequence based on your specific anatomy and goals.
