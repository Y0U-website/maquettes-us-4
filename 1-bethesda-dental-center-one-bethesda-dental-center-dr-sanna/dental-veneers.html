Types of Dental Veneers

We offer two primary types of veneers, each with its own unique benefits:

  • Porcelain Veneers : The most popular choice for cosmetic dentistry, porcelain veneers are known for their strength, durability, and natural appearance. They are also highly stain-resistant, making them ideal for patients who frequently drink coffee, tea, or wine. These veneers are custom-made to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, providing a seamless, beautiful result.
  • Composite Veneers : Made from a durable composite resin, these veneers are bonded directly to the front of your teeth. While composite veneers are more affordable than porcelain options, they may not be as long-lasting or resistant to stains. They are a great option for patients looking for a more budget-friendly solution.

The Dental Veneer Procedure

Getting dental veneers usually takes two visits to 1 Bethesda Dental Center. Here’s what you can expect during the process:

  • Consultation and Preparation : During your first visit, your dentist will evaluate your smile and discuss your goals. If you’re a good candidate for veneers, the dentist will prepare your teeth by removing a small amount of enamel from the front surface to ensure a perfect fit for your new veneers.
  • Impression and Fabrication : Next, we’ll take an impression of your teeth, which will be sent to a dental lab where your custom veneers will be created. While your permanent veneers are being fabricated, you may wear temporary veneers for comfort and appearance.
  • Placement and Bonding : During your second visit, we’ll place the veneers on your teeth and make any necessary adjustments to ensure a proper fit and bite. Once everything looks perfect, the veneers will be permanently bonded to your teeth using a strong adhesive.
