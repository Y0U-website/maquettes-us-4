Dr. Akram Sannaa

Dr. Akram Sannaa embarked on his dentistry journey at Boston University School of Dental Medicine. His passion for enhancing oral health was deeply rooted in his prior involvement with public health research at Harvard School of Public Health.

As the leader of both Chevy Chase Dental Center and 1 Bethesda Dental Center, Dr. Sannaa’s goal is to provide high-end dentistry while preserving the family dentistry values that have defined his practices. His commitment to excellence ensures both locations deliver patient-centered care of the highest standard.

Dr. Sannaa is also recognized as a celebrity dentist in Washington, D.C., treating congressmen, ambassadors, and TV personalities. His reputation extends to his exceptional skills in senior dentistry, and he has initiated the Senior Dentistry Program of 2025, demonstrating his dedication to improving oral health for seniors.

The name "Sannaa" means "The Makers," a fitting reflection of Dr. Sannaa’s heritage and his family’s legacy in olive oil production. This heritage symbolizes generations of craftsmanship and dedication, values that Dr. Sannaa brings into his dental practices.

Beyond the realm of dentistry, Dr. Sannaa treasures moments spent with family and friends. His interests range from exploring hiking trails and camping to indulging in culinary adventures. Building upon the foundation of his first practice, Dr. Sannaa’s leadership and dedication have driven the success of both practices, making a significant impact on the field of high-end dentistry.

Dr. Destiny Bean

Dr. Destiny Bean brings a fresh, holistic approach to modern dentistry, blending her expertise in general and cosmetic dentistry with professional training in culinary arts and certification in functional nutrition. She believes the mouth is more than just the start of the digestive system—it’s a gateway to full-body wellness. Her multidisciplinary background allows her to craft treatment plans that go beyond the surface, merging esthetic excellence with biologic insight for truly personalized, long-lasting results.

Dr. Bean began her journey with a deep passion for health and healing. Her education spans both coasts, and her work has been shaped by rigorous training in clinical dentistry and culinary science. She has been named among the Northeast’s Top Doctors and received formal accolades in culinary excellence—honors that reflect her unique combination of precision, creativity, and care.

Patients often describe Dr. Bean as calm, thoughtful, and deeply intuitive. She’s known for creating a safe, welcoming environment where individuals feel seen, heard, and empowered. Whether she’s enhancing a smile or guiding someone through a broader wellness journey, Dr. Bean approaches every case with intention and empathy. Her holistic philosophy means every filling, crown, or cosmetic enhancement is designed with the whole person in mind.

Outside the dental chair, Dr. Bean leads an adventurous and grounded life. She loves exploring new cultures through food and travel, seeking out hiking trails, and occasionally taking to the skies for a skydiving thrill. When it’s time to slow down, she enjoys dance, music, and literature—passions that nourish the same mindfulness she brings to her work.

With her rare fusion of clinical skill and whole-body insight, Dr. Bean is redefining what it means to deliver patient-centered dental care. Ready to discover the connection between a healthy smile and a healthier you? Dr. Bean is here to guide the way.

Dr. Alejandro Uribe

Dr. Alejandro Uribe’s journey in dentistry started young—rooted in memories of helping out at his father’s dental office in Michigan. Those early experiences taught him that dentistry isn’t just about teeth—it’s about people, relationships, and trust.

Today, Dr. Uribe (pronounced Your E-Bay ) brings that same philosophy to our practice. Working side by side with Dr. Sannaa, he shares a comprehensive, proactive approach to dentistry—always prioritizing prevention before treatment and building long-term relationships with his patients. Known around the office for his charming personality and calming presence, Dr. Uribe combines precision, artistry, and warmth in everything he does.

After earning his dental degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry , Dr. Uribe made it his mission to redefine the dental experience. He works to eliminate the stigma around dental anxiety by creating an environment where patients feel genuinely comfortable and at ease.

He’s passionate about oral health as a foundation of total wellness and takes time to educate each patient about the powerful connection between the mouth and the rest of the body. His ultimate goal? Empowering every person in his chair with a confident, healthy smile.

When he’s not crafting beautiful smiles, Dr. Uribe is a devoted foodie, always cooking at home or exploring D.C.’s best restaurants. A die-hard soccer fan (especially the English Premier League), he also enjoys staying active with outdoor hobbies like biking, golfing, and pickleball.

Proud of his Colombian heritage , fluent in both English and Spanish , and fueled by a passion for people, Dr. Uribe is here to help you smile bigger, brighter, and healthier—one visit at a time.
