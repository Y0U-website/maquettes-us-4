How Orthodontics Impacts Oral Health and Appearance

Proper alignment of teeth is essential for both their function and appearance. When teeth are well-positioned, they not only look more attractive but are also easier to clean, significantly bringing down the risk of plaque buildup and related gum issues.

Furthermore, orthodontic treatment in Westminster, CA , considers how bite alignment affects critical functions like chewing, speaking, and overall comfort. By addressing any alignment problems, individuals can improve their facial aesthetics, enhance functional capabilities, and support long-term oral health.
