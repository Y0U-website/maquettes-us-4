The practice

My name is Dr. Dang. I want to take this opportunity to welcome you to our dental practice in the city of Westminster. I have made every effort to provide our patients with a friendly, comfortable, and caring environment. Our staff is courteous and truly cares about the well-being of every patient we see.

Dentistry is an extension of my love for life for the past 20 years since I attended Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. Part of the graduation oath from dental school was to use my privilege as a dentist to “make man whole.”

To fulfill this commitment, I have made myself a life-time student of dentistry to learn the latest procedures and materials on a regular basis so that our patients receive the best treatments dentistry can offer. My staff and I are blessed to have the opportunity to serve our community.

We share the same values of good work ethics and commitment to excellence. Our office welcomes patients from all walks of life. We would love to meet you for a tour of our facility and introduce you to the staff and doctors. We thank you for opportunity to serve you.

Our multilingual staff, fluent in English, Spanish, and Vietnamese, ensures effective communication and personalized care for a diverse patient community.

As a Gold+ Invisalign Provider, we provide quick orthodontic treatments, utilizing advanced techniques and technology to straighten teeth efficiently and improve smiles in record time.

We provide free health scans for all patients, providing valuable insights into their oral health and ensuring early detection of potential issues.

Our dentist brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to the practice, with a distinguished working experience in dentistry since 1998.
