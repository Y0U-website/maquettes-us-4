Our Services

New Patients

At Primary Teeth, we welcome patients aged 0 - 18. Our new patient consultation includes a thorough examination, cleaning, x-rays, and fluoride treatment, all tailored to the child’s age and insurance. Schedule your child’s first visit with us today!

Hygiene Appointments

We offer recall schedules for 3, 6, or 12-month intervals. These visits provide excellent opportunities to discuss any concerns, with treatment options provided if needed. Talk with your doctor to see which recall interval is best for your child.

Emergency Visits

No child should endure tooth pain. We’re here to help with emergencies from painful teeth to infections, trauma, and more. Call us ASAP, and we’ll do everything we can to get your child into the schedule and out of pain that day.

Operative Appointments

From sealants to fillings, crowns, extractions, and more, our state-of-the-art equipment and compassionate staff ensure your child will be in excellent hands.

Sedation

We offer various sedation options to ensure a positive dental experience for every child, including Laughing Gas, Oral Conscious Sedation, and General Anesthesia. Your doctor will discuss the best option for your child.

Insurance

We participate with a wide variety of insurances to ensure that every child receives quality care. Contact our insurance coordinator if you have any questions about your coverage.
