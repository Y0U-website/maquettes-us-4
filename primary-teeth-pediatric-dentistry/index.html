The practice

Our team of oral health professionals are highly experienced and dedicated to providing the best care for your child’s oral health. Our receptionists, dental assistants, hygienists, and doctors all strive to give you a pleasant appointment for you and your child.

We provide a wide range of dental services tailored for children, from routine check-ups and cleanings to more specialized treatments. Operative appointments including laughing gas and oral conscious sedations can be done in office.

Contact us today to schedule an appointment. We look forward to welcoming you and your child to our dental family.
