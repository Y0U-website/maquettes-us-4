Meet Dr. Da Hee Park

Experience. Integrity. Excellence.

Dr. Da Hee Park is originally from Canada and came to the U.S. after receiving her undergraduate degree from Dalhouse University in Halifax in Microbiology & Immunology.

She received her dental training and education from Case Western Reserve University, followed by a General Practice Residency (GPR) at Summa Health Hospital in Ohio.

Dr. Da Hee Park was in private practice in California for almost five years, before relocating to the Northern Virginia area, where she continued private practice.

Dr. Park truly enjoys getting to know each of her patients, understanding their dental goals and then helping them to meet those goals in a highly professionals, artistic, and gentle way. Dr. Park enjoys dentistry and takes great pride in her expertise and contiuous training.

In her time outside the office, Dr. Da Hee Park enjoys trying out the many new restaurants and dessert offerings in the Northern Virginia area, including the many neighborhoods of Arlington.
