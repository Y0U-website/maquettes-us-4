Frequently Asked Questions About Solea Laser Dentistry

What is Solea Laser Dentistry?

Solea Laser Dentistry is an advanced dental technology that allows dentists to perform many procedures without needles, drills, or pain. It uses a specialized CO2 laser for precise and comfortable treatment.

Does Solea laser treatment hurt?

Most patients experience little to no pain during Solea laser treatment. The laser numbs the tooth and soft tissue naturally, often eliminating the need for anesthesia.

What dental procedures can be performed with the Solea laser?

The Solea laser can be used for cavity treatment, gum reshaping, frenectomies, decay removal, and other soft- and hard-tissue procedures. It is safe for both adults and children.

Is the Solea laser safe for children?

Yes, the Solea laser is extremely safe for children and is often preferred because it eliminates the fear and discomfort associated with needles and drills.

How long does a Solea laser appointment take?

Most appointments are shorter than traditional dental visits because there is no need for numbing injections or waiting for anesthesia to take effect.
