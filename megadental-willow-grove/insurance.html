Dental Insurance We Accept

0 Insurance Dealing with insurance companies can be frustrating, and we want to make things as easy as possible for you. We accept an assignment of benefits from your insurance provider — this means that we will take care of the claim filing process with them on your behalf to ensure that you receive the coverage you deserve.

To ensure transparency, we’ll review your entire treatment plan and provide a detailed cost breakdown before proceeding with any services. This will help you understand the costs involved and make informed decisions about your treatment.

If you’re responsible for a co-payment, we’ll collect it during your visit. We believe in providing upfront and honest information about the costs involved so that you can plan accordingly and avoid any surprises.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most PPO plans at the moment and are happy to welcome fee-for-service patients at any time.

Unfortunately, there are some dental insurance plans we don’t accept, including Medicaid, Medicare Advantage, Healthplex, HMOs, DMOs, and others. If we don’t take yours, we’ll contact you ahead of your visit.
