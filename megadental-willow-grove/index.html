Dr. Igor Plotnik, DMD Dr. Igor Plotnik, DMD, graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine and has practiced for over 30 years. Dr. Plotnik also has various degrees from esteemed international dental schools and can speak multiple languages in addition to English— Russian & Ukrainian.

Dr. Plotnik is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry. Locally, he participates in a chapter of LeTip, alongside other business owners based in Bucks and Montgomery counties.

He is committed to professional growth and keeps himself up-to-date with the latest advancements in his field by attending continuing education courses on a regular basis. These courses specifically focus on cutting-edge research and emerging trends that help him enhance his skills and patients’ care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Plotnik spends time with his wife, 2 children, and his Goldendoodle, Watson.
