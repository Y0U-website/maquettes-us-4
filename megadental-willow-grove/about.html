About Us

General Dentist and Founder

Dr. Igor Plotnik is a highly experienced dentist with over three decades of experience in the field. During his career, he has established himself as one of the most trusted and respected dental practitioners in the greater Philadelphia area. His dedication to providing exceptional care to his patients has earned him a loyal following, and he is widely regarded as a leader in the dental community.

Dr. Plotnik founded MegaDental Willow Grove with the goal of creating a dental practice that would provide his patients with the highest level of care possible. He and his team of dental professionals are committed to delivering personalized treatment plans that meet each patient’s unique needs. From routine check-ups and cleanings to advanced restorative procedures, MegaDental Willow Grove offers a full range of dental services to help patients achieve and maintain optimal oral health.

Dr. Plotnik’s patients appreciate his gentle touch, compassionate care, and attention to detail. He takes the time to listen to their concerns and answer their questions, ensuring that they feel informed and empowered throughout their treatment. With his extensive experience, advanced training, and commitment to excellence, Dr. Plotnik is a true asset to the dental community.

Dr. Plotnik’s education in Dentistry is truly impressive. He holds multiple international degrees in the field, including one from the prestigious University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. With this level of education, it’s no surprise that Dr. Plotnik has been a longstanding member of the Academy of General Dentistry. This esteemed organization is dedicated to promoting excellence and high standards in the field of dentistry.

Dr. Plotnik is a member of LeTip, a networking group that connects business owners and sole proprietors in Bucks and Montgomery counties. He participates in the local chapter, LeTip of Buxmont, which provides a platform for members to exchange leads and referrals. Being a part of LeTip has allowed Dr. Plotnik to establish meaningful connections with fellow business owners in the community, in addition to his professional affiliations.

When Dr. Plotnik is not working at MegaDental, he loves to spend quality time with his family. He is happily married to MegaDental’s incredible hygienist, who shares his passion for promoting dental health. Together, they have two lovely children with whom they adore spending time. In addition, their family is completed by a goofy and playful Goldendoodle named Watson, who always adds an extra dose of joy and laughter to their lives. Whether they go on adventures, play games, or relax at home, Dr. Plotnik cherishes every moment he spends with his loved ones.

Our team at MegaDental Willow Grove provides full-service dentistry, making their office your one-stop solution for any dental need.

We believe in a holistic approach to dentistry that considers our patients’ overall health and well-being. During your visit, we will listen to your concerns, answer any questions you may have, and work with you to develop a personalized treatment plan that meets your unique needs.

Our preventive services include regular checkups, cleanings, and fluoride treatments to help you maintain optimal oral health. Our restorative services include fillings, crowns, and bridges to repair damaged or missing teeth, while our cosmetic treatments can help enhance your smile and boost your confidence.

When you visit us, you can expect a comfortable, relaxing environment to help you feel at ease. We aim to ensure that every patient has a positive and enjoyable experience with us. So, don’t hesitate to schedule your first visit with us today and experience the difference our expert dental staff can make!
