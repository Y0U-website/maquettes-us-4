Payment Financing Options

We accept cash, American Express, Mastercard, Discover, and Visa for all treatments. For patients without dental insurance, or whose plan doesn’t cover the full cost of treatment, we offer interest-free financing* through CareCredit.

Payments can be spread equally over 6 or 12 months with no prepayment penalty, or extended up to 5 years at a low interest rate. If needed, our team can also break a larger treatment plan into stages to fit your budget. Contact us to go over financing options before your visit.

*Subject to credit approval. Minimum monthly payments required. Contact our office for details.
