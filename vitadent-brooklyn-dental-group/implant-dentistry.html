What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small screw or posts inserted surgically into the jawbone. Most are made from medical-grade titanium, a lightweight alloy that is highly biocompatible.

Some are made from zirconia, a strong ceramic material that can be a suitable choice in specific situations, such as when gum tissue is thinner or an individual has concerns about metal sensitivities.

Dental implants have three different components, which are:

Dental Implant Screw

An implant screw is several millimeters in length, providing essential contact with the surrounding bone. It is inserted into the jawbone during a short procedure. It can take as little as an hour to place a single dental implant.

Once an implant post is inserted into the jawbone, it becomes firmly fused in place over several months, a process known as osseointegration. During osseointegration, the body deposits new bone on and around the post. Sometimes the implant is specially treated or coated to encourage this process.

It typically takes anywhere from 2 to 6 months for osseointegration to complete.

Implant Abutment

The abutment is the crucial connector that fits between the implant and your final crown. We custom-design each abutment for a precise fit and the correct gum contour, usually utilizing materials like titanium or zirconia for optimal strength and aesthetics.

A perfectly fitted abutment is essential for ensuring the long-term stability of the implant and guaranteeing the final crown looks natural.

Implant Restoration

The implant restoration, which is the final crown or bridge, is meticulously designed and fabricated to perfectly match the shape, shade, and function of your natural teeth. To ensure both durability and superior aesthetics, we utilize high-quality materials such as all-ceramic zirconia or porcelain fused to metal. Once permanently attached to the abutment, your new tooth or teeth will fully restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with renewed confidence.

Multiple Teeth Replacement

Replacing several missing teeth doesn’t require an implant for every single gap. Instead, we provide multiple teeth replacement in Sheepshead Bay with nonremovable implant-supported bridges that restore your smile efficiently. A dental implant bridge is nonremovable.

Full Arch Restoration

A full arch restoration could be a dental implant bridge that is firmly fixed in place. Alternatively, an implant restoration could be an implant-supported denture , which is a removable appliance. It clips onto the implants but can be snapped out for cleaning.

Our Dental Implant Solutions in Sheepshead Bay

Whether you need to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth, there is likely to be an option that is right for you. Our Sheepshead Bay dental implants solutions include:

Single Tooth Implants

If you only need a single tooth, a single dental implant can hold an implant crown securely. An implant crown is nonremovable and is normally screwed onto the dental implant post. A single tooth implant looks and feels very natural, just like a real tooth.

Implant Retained Bridges

If you have lost several teeth that were originally side-by-side, a dental implant bridge can replace them.

With this solution, you don’t need an implant for every single missing tooth. Instead, two or more implants that are covered with implant crowns will support replacement teeth between them. Each replacement tooth supported by these implant crowns is referred to as a pontic.

Dental implant bridges are nonremovable and are suitable for securing multiple missing teeth. They can even replace an entire arch.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are removable and are sometimes referred to as implant overdentures. Denture implants are affordable. As few as two or four dental implants can hold them securely in place.

Implant-supported dentures snap onto special connectors or abutments. Once in the mouth, they provide a biting and chewing strength that is significantly better than that of an ordinary denture. When you wish to clean the denture, you snap it out of your mouth.

All-on-Four or All-on-Six Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implants are a comprehensive solution that supports an entire arch of teeth with just four implants. Sometimes six implants are recommended for additional support, especially when restoring an upper arch of teeth where the jawbone is naturally thinner and weaker.

These implants are inserted differently compared to ordinary implants, as while two are inserted vertically, the remaining two are angled. The implant screws used for these angled implants are longer, which increases contact with the jawbone and enhances the strength and stability of the treatment.

All-on-Four is an advanced implant solution that requires a technique-sensitive approach. It can provide excellent results. It’s worth understanding the potential complications of All-on-Four implants before committing to treatment.

These implants can support either a removable implant denture or a non-removable dental implant bridge. If you have lost an entire arch of teeth and are looking for low-cost dental implants in Sheepshead Bay, All-on-Four might be one solution.

Mini Dental Implants

These implants are used less frequently and are narrower than ordinary implants , although the length of the implant post remains the same.

You may need a mini dental implant when replacing a small tooth in a situation where space is limited. For example, a mini dental implant may be recommended if you need a dental implant for a small lower front tooth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implant treatment offers several benefits, as outlined below.

A Long-Term and Secure Solution

Dental implant treatment is a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. Treatment has a success rate of 95 % or higher, and implants can last for many years or even a lifetime. This does depend on good ongoing aftercare, both at home and through professional preventive dentistry .

Implants Look like Natural Teeth

Dental implants provide a natural-looking restoration for tooth loss , allowing you to enjoy teeth that look and function well. At VitaDent, we custom-design every implant restoration to ensure that each tooth provides the right support for your cheeks and lips, thereby restoring and protecting your facial dimensions. We also make sure your implant teeth are strong and durable.

Protecting and Preserving Your Jawbone

One of the major problems associated with tooth loss is its impact on the jawbone and the remaining teeth. When teeth are removed , the entire tooth, including the tooth root, is taken out.

Your jawbone relies on the tooth root for stimulation, ensuring the bone surrounding it remains strong and healthy. Without stimulation, jawbone resorption occurs quickly. Significant jawbone loss can occur in just the first year after tooth loss.

Jawbone loss can destabilize any remaining teeth, increasing the risk that they will become loose or fall out. It reduces support and retention for traditional dentures that rest on the gums, which is why these dentures can become challenging to wear after just a few years.

In contrast, dental implants replicate real tooth roots, providing a similar level of stimulation to the surrounding bone and preventing jawbone loss. Dental implants are the only solution that can provide this significant benefit.

Protecting Your Existing Teeth

Dental implants help protect your natural teeth. Implant teeth prevent your own teeth from shifting out of place, thereby maintaining your bite.

We don’t need to modify or reshape potentially healthy teeth when fitting dental implants. In contrast, if you have a traditional dental bridge, then it’s necessary to significantly reshape the teeth adjacent to the gap, removing some healthy tooth structure to make room for the supporting or abutment crowns.

Improving Your Speech, Comfort, and Ability to Eat

Dental implant teeth help ensure you can speak clearly and confidently. They should feel entirely comfortable and natural. Your implants will make it easier to eat a wider choice of foods by improving your biting and chewing strength.

If you have a permanent implant crown or bridge, your biting and chewing strength is similar to that of natural teeth. If you have an implant-supported denture, your biting and chewing strength is greatly improved compared with a traditional denture.

Protecting Overall Health

The ability to eat comfortably and to enjoy a wide range of foods is important. It makes it much easier for you to obtain all the vitamins and minerals you need.
