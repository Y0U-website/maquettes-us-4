How Do Dental Implants Work

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed in the jaw to serve as artificial tooth roots, securing a new tooth, bridge, or denture in place permanently. Each implant is fitted with a dental crown to provide a chewing surface and restore the look of a missing tooth. Experience the best in modern tooth restoration!
