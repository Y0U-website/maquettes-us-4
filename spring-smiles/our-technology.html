Safe, Trusted Care

SMART Amalgam Filling Removal

If you are interested in the health and aesthetic benefits of removing old metal amalgam fillings, we offer safe and comfortable treatments with SMART. This is an acronym that stands for the Safe Mercury Amalgam Removal Technique. The technique ensures that all patients are protected from mercury during the removal of an amalgam filling. Since amalgam fillings contain mercury which can be harmful, it’s important to see a dentist certified in SMART for your treatment.
