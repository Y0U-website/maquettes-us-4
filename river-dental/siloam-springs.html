Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Logan Putnam, DMD

Dr. Putnam is a cosmetic dentist who grew up in the Wasatch Front Mountains of Ogden, Utah. For his undergraduate studies, Dr. Putnam attended Weber State University where he played college football and graduated with a BS in Athletic Therapy. He then attended Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Glendale, Arizona where he received advanced training in cosmetic dentistry, denture fabrication, and dental implants.

Dr. Putnam is a member of the American Dental Association, the Arkansas State Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. His favorite part about being a Dentist is helping his patients feel comfortable and relaxed in the office, and helping them achieve confidence in their smiles! He enjoys attending continuing education conferences and training particularly in the topics of dental implants, Invisalign, and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Putnam and his wife love biking with their 2 boys and spending time on the water in NWA. Dr. Putnam’s brother works for the US Military fabricating dentures for veterans and he has 6 uncles who are also dentists!

Dr. Kale Garner, DDS

Dr. Kale Garner is a skilled general dentist at River Dental in Siloam Springs, Arkansas, dedicated to improving his patients’ lives through exceptional dental care. Originally from Jonesboro, Arkansas, Dr. Garner’s passion for dentistry began with his desire to make a meaningful impact on others. He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biology from the University of Arkansas before continuing his education at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center, where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree.

What excites Dr. Garner the most about being a dentist is the ability to change lives. He believes that helping patients regain their confidence and smile without worry is incredibly rewarding. “Being able to give someone the ability to smile without worry again is the best feeling ever,” he shares. His top priority is always patient comfort, ensuring they are not only at ease during procedures but also well-informed about the treatment process and its benefits. For him, dentistry is about more than just teeth—it’s about enhancing lives and fostering lasting relationships.

Outside of his professional life, Dr. Garner enjoys spending time with his lovely girlfriend of over three years. While they don’t have children yet, they are the proud pet parents of several beloved animals: Momma Matcha, a cherished feline; her son, Casper, a playful kitten born in 2024; and Teddy, a Chesapeake Bay Retriever known for his impressive frisbee-catching skills. Dr. Garner is also passionate about sports, often playing and watching games, hiking with Teddy, attending concerts, and indulging in his love of cooking and savoring delicious food.

Dr. Garner’s combination of clinical expertise, patient-centered approach, and compassionate nature makes him a trusted and valued member of the River Dental team. He looks forward to continuing to make a positive impact on the lives of his patients, one smile at a time.

Dr. Anthony DelCorral, DDS is a general dentist who is passionate about improving patients’ health, confidence, and overall quality of life. He believes dentistry is about more than treating teeth. It is about relieving pain, building trust, and forming long-term relationships through education and positive dental experiences.

Dr. DelCorral earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Kansas State University and completed his dental degree at the UMKC School of Dentistry. He further advanced his clinical training through an AEGD-1 residency with the United States Air Force at Offutt Air Force Base in Omaha, Nebraska. His broad training allows him to provide comprehensive, patient-centered care while prioritizing comfort and understanding.

A strong believer in patient education, Dr. DelCorral takes time to explain both what treatment is recommended and why it is needed. His goal is for every patient to feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their dental care decisions.

Dr. DelCorral is committed to serving the community. He currently donates his time at the Fayetteville Community Center and previously volunteered with Donated Dental Services in South Dakota, providing care to underserved populations and participating in community outreach events. He also proudly serves in the South Dakota Army National Guard.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. DelCorral enjoys spending time outdoors with his wife and their two dogs, Elsie and Bruno. His hobbies include hiking, camping, boating, golfing, gardening, and winter sports such as skiing and snowboarding. Spending time outdoors helps him reset and recharge. One of his bucket list goals is to visit every national park in the United States. He and his wife are also excitedly expecting their first child and look forward to this new chapter in their lives.
