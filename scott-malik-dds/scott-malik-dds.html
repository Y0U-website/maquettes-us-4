0 About Us Learn More About Us We pride ourselves on being the most honest, warm, and family-oriented Glenview dentist. We strive to make your visit as comfortable as possible. Our standards are set high because we believe each of our patients deserves to be cared for as we would our own families.

A variety of services are offered in our Glenview office to ensure that you and your family receive the best possible dental care from one doctor, in one location. Since our practice opened in 1959, we’ve been building lifetime relationships with our patients, helping them maintain and improve the health of their teeth and gums.

Dr. Scott Malik

Dr. Scott Malik received his DDS from Loyola University in Maywood, Illinois in 1989. He has since been practicing in Glenview, providing and improving upon the compassionate and professional care this office has been known for since 1959.

In 2010, Dr. Malik was named a Top Doctor in Glenview, recognizing his talent and many years of outstanding dental care. Dr. Malik is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Chicago Dental Society (CDS) .

Outside the office, Dr Malik can frequently be found at hockey rinks around the North Shore. A skilled defenseman, Dr Malik and his team have won the last six and eight of the last ten National Pond Hockey Championships (40+ Tier 1). He also loves spending time with his family and friends.
