Robert Perry, Jr, DMD

Dr. Jennifer Lozada

Dr. Jennifer Lozada is a compassionate and highly skilled dentist dedicated to providing exceptional care to her patients. A proud native of Dorchester, MA, and the daughter of Puerto Rican parents, Dr. Lozada earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Howard University College of Dentistry in 2023. She also holds a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene from MCPHS University-Forsyth School of Dental Hygiene (2015) and a Bachelor of Science in Human Physiology from Boston University (2014).

Fluent in Spanish, Dr. Lozada is known for her strong communication skills and empathetic approach, ensuring that all patients feel comfortable, informed, and supported throughout their treatment. Before becoming a dentist, she gained valuable clinical experience as a dental hygienist at Robert R. Perry Jr. PC, where she developed expertise in preventive care, patient education, and a wide range of dental procedures.
