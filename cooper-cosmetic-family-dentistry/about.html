About Us

Having been interested in health careers in high school, Dr. Jessica Peele’s love for dentistry began with a volunteer trip to the Dominican Republic where she gained her first experience in the field.

After obtaining a B.S. in Biology from UNC Chapel Hill, she took a gap period in Europe before heading back to Chapel Hill to attend dental school, where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Dr. Peele was able to expand the reach of her education by organizing a dental service trip to Nepal. She was also involved in local community service throughout her career at UNC.

Dr. Peele completed an extra year of training through a General Practice Residency at MAHEC Clinic in Asheville, NC. Here, she treated a variety of patients with complex dental needs, ranging from Oral Surgery, Denture and Partial, Endodontic, and Crown and Bridge cases. She is also a member of the American Dental Association, South Carolina Dental Association, and Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Peele transitioned into ownership of Cooper Cosmetic & Family Dentistry in 2019.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Peele enjoys spending time with her wonderful wife, Ashley, and her children, Emmeline, Archer, and Leighton.

Dr. Sabrina Von Bargen was born and raised in Pottsville, Pennsylvania. She later moved to “The Southern Slice of Heaven” to complete her undergraduate degree in Business Administration at the University of North Carolina. She loved Chapel Hill (and Carolina basketball) so much, she pursued her dental education at the UNC School of Dentistry, making her a Double Tar Heel.

Dr. Von Bargen knew she wanted to be a dentist since high school, and her passion for her career continues to grow every day. She loves learning along with the advances in dentistry, but her favorite part is fostering meaningful relationships with her patients and helping them achieve the smile of their dreams.

One of Dr. Von Bargen’s favorite experiences in school was traveling to Uganda to provide free dental care to people with little to no access to healthcare at all. She continues to provide care for underserved individuals closer to home at the North Charleston Outreach Clinic.

When she is not working, you can find Dr. Von Bargen enjoying the outdoors, exploring different restaurants, cooking new recipes, or spending quality time with her husband, C.J., her son, Briggs, and dog, Nellie.

Dr. Jessica Tabb, a Virginia native, graduated from Virginia Tech with a degree in Finance and a minor in Business Leadership Management. Soon after graduation, she married her college sweetheart and moved to Charleston.

After working in sales and marketing for several years, Dr. Tabb decided to return to school to pursue a career in dentistry and earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical University of South Carolina.

Dr. Tabb gained extra training after dental school by completing a one year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Hospital in Charleston. She is an experienced dental practitioner who loves helping people achieve their best oral health.

She is a member of the American Dental Association, South Carolina Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and Delta Sigma Delta Professional Dental Fraternity. She also serves as an alumni class agent for her dental school class.

Dr. Tabb enjoys the beach, spending time on the water, and exploring all the Lowcountry has to offer. Most importantly, she loves spending quality time with her husband, John, and their two young children.
