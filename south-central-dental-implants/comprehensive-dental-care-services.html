  • Full-Arch Implant Dentures (All-on-4 / All-on-6) – Replace a full arch of teeth with a fixed denture supported by just 4–6 implants.

PREVENTATIVE CARE

Routine care is designed to stop dental issues before they start.

  • Routine Dental Exams & Cleanings – Professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar, while exams catch problems early.
  • Digital X-Rays & Imaging – Safe, low-radiation imaging helps us detect decay, bone loss, and abnormalities.
  • Oral Cancer Screenings – A quick, painless check for early signs of cancer in the mouth, tongue, and throat.
  • Fluoride Treatments – A protective mineral application that helps strengthen enamel and prevent cavities.
  • Dental Sealants – Thin coatings applied to molars to prevent decay in the deep grooves of teeth.
  • Personalized Home Care Instructions – Guidance on brushing, flossing, and habits to maintain oral health between visits.

SMILE MAKEOVER

Cosmetic procedures that improve the appearance of your teeth and boost your confidence.

  • Professional Teeth Whitening – Brightens your smile by removing stains caused by food, drinks, and aging.
  • Porcelain Veneers – Thin, custom shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, gaps, or discoloration.
  • Tooth-Colored Bonding – A tooth-colored resin is applied and shaped to fix chips, cracks, or small gaps.
  • Invisalign® Clear Aligners – Nearly invisible trays that straighten teeth without the look or feel of traditional braces.
  • Gum Contouring – Reshapes the gum line to improve symmetry and eliminate a gummy smile.
  • Smile Design Consultations – We analyze your goals and create a customized plan for your ideal smile.

EMERGENCY CARE

Urgent dental treatments to relieve pain, repair damage, and protect your oral health.

  • Severe Toothaches – Diagnosing and treating the source of pain, often caused by decay or infection.
  • Chipped, Cracked, or Broken Teeth – Immediate care to restore damaged teeth and prevent further injury.
  • Knocked-Out Teeth – Replantation and stabilization to save a tooth if addressed quickly.
  • Lost Crowns or Fillings – Quick replacement or repair to protect exposed teeth.
  • Dental Abscesses or Infections – Drainage and antibiotics to treat infections and prevent serious complications.
  • Emergency Extractions – Safe removal of teeth that are damaged beyond repair or causing severe pain.

ORAL SURGERY

Advanced surgical care to address complex dental needs with precision.

  • Tooth Extractions (Including Wisdom Teeth) – Removal of teeth due to decay, crowding, or impaction.
  • Bone Grafting & Ridge Preservation – Rebuilds bone structure to support future implants or maintain facial shape.
  • Biopsies for Oral Lesions – Safe tissue sampling for diagnosis of suspicious growths or lesions.
  • Surgical Exposure for Orthodontics – Helps guide impacted teeth into the proper position during orthodontic treatment.

FAMILY SERVICES

Care for every generation, from your child’s first tooth to senior dental support.

  • Pediatric Dentistry – Gentle cleanings, fluoride, and sealants to protect young teeth.
  • Space Maintainers & Orthodontic Evaluations – Preserves space for adult teeth and checks for alignment issues early.
  • Adult Cleanings & Periodontal Maintenance – Prevents gum disease and keeps adult smiles healthy.
  • TMJ/TMD Evaluations – Diagnosing jaw joint disorders that cause pain or difficulty chewing.
  • Custom Night Guards & Sports Mouthguards – Protect teeth from grinding or athletic injuries.
  • Senior Dental Care – Denture fittings, gum care, and screenings tailored to aging smiles.
