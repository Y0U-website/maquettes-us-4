Les soins

Orthodontics (also referred to as dentofacial orthopedics ) is a specialized form of dentistry focusing on the diagnosis, prevention, and treatment of dental and facial abnormalities.

The Invisalign system is the virtually invisible way to straighten your teeth and achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. Invisalign and Invisalign Teen use a series of clear aligners that are custom-made for your teeth to move them over time.
