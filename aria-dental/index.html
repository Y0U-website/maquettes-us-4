Dental Implants are the gold standard in replacing extracted teeth. A single tooth implant closely mimics the natural dentition, resulting in a natural appearance in anterior teeth and complete masticatory function in the posterior teeth.

Implants are placed in three parts: the implant screw, the abutment, and the crown. The implant screw is placed in the bone and acts as the foundation of the implant. The abutment is placed on top of the implant screw and acts as the bridge between the screw and the crown.
