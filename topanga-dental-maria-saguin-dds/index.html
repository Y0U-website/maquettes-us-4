The practice

Topanga Dental serves the community with a strong commitment to assisting you and your family with maintaining healthy gums and teeth. Our General & Family practice is designed to provide thorough dentistry for every member of your family. Topanga Dental offers full preventive, protective and restorative dental services. You and your family will be amazed at what we can do!

Topanga Dental is located in Chatsworth but serves the entire Los Angeles community. Dr. Maria Saguin is professionally trained in the latest dental techniques and procedures providing you a state-of-the-art experience for your dental treatments.

View our Smile Galley *Your treatment results may vary and are not a guarantee of other past results.

Dr Saguin completed her post graduate program training in cosmetic dentistry from the leading educators of UCLA School of Dentistry. She earned her dental degree in 1999 at Centro Escolar University. In 2002, She also gained her dental hygiene license in Florida. She also completed Advanced training in the field of Orthodontics.

Dr Saguin is very passionate about life and love her job tremendously. She is looking forward meeting you and helping you with all your dental needs. Dr. Maria Saguin is an active member of American Dental Association, California Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and San Fernando Valley Dental Society.
