Our Appleton Orthodontics Services

If you are familiar with braces, you know orthodontics! Having a correct bite not only helps your teeth look their best, but also helps you to properly bite, chew and speak. Your jaw alignment and bite will thank you! Contact our team for an orthodontics service.

Orthodontics and braces are traditionally associated with children and teens, but not anymore. Our Appleton and Fox Valley dentist not only provides youth screenings, but also advancements in treatments like clear aligner therapy. As such, there are now more opportunities for many adults to seek out treatments to help guide their teeth back into the proper positions!

Services Overview:

Clear Aligners

Step into the future of orthodontic treatment with our clear aligners services! This innovative orthodontics solution offers a clear, comfortable, and convenient way to straighten your teeth without the appearance of metal braces. Each aligner is custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth, gently guiding them into the desired position. Clear aligners are suited for adults and teens alike.

Retainers

Our Appleton orthodontics team provides a variety of retainer options, including clear, removable ones and fixed styles that work around the clock. Dr. Van Hoof will guide you in selecting the best type for your lifestyle and will provide personalized instructions for care and maintenance. Trust us to support you in preserving that perfect smile for a lifetime with custom retainers!

Orthodontics Surgery

For some, achieving the perfect smile requires the precision of orthodontic surgery. Our orthodontics surgery service will meet the needs of those requiring more complex dental issues. Dr. Van Hoof will ensure that the foundation of your smile is as strong as it is beautiful. Pre-surgical orthodontic treatment prepares your teeth for surgery, and post-surgical care ensures a swift recovery and optimal results. At Van Hoof Dental, we coordinate every step with compassion and expertise, ensuring your comfort throughout the process.

Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Van Hoof Dental’s orthodontic treatments offer numerous benefits that can greatly enhance the quality of life and overall health for individuals seeking a straighter, healthier smile. Beyond improving cosmetic appearance, orthodontics can also boost self-confidence and contribute to better oral health.

Orthodontic treatment also plays a vital role in correcting bite issues and ensuring that teeth function properly. By aligning the teeth and jaw, it helps prevent future dental problems and promotes long-term oral health.

Key Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

  • Improved Chewing Function: Properly aligned teeth make chewing more efficient and comfortable, which can improve digestion and overall nutrition.
  • Enhanced Cosmetic Appearance: Orthodontic treatment straightens teeth, resulting in a more attractive and natural-looking smile that enhances your overall appearance.
  • Correction of Bite Issues: Orthodontics can correct bite problems such as overbite, underbite, and crossbite, reducing the risk of jaw pain, uneven wear on teeth, and other dental issues.
  • Prevention of Future Dental Problems: By aligning teeth properly, orthodontics can help prevent issues such as tooth decay, gum disease, and the need for more extensive dental work in the future.
  • Improved Oral Health: Straighter teeth are easier to clean and maintain, reducing the likelihood of plaque buildup and promoting healthier gums and teeth.
  • Boost in Self-Confidence: With a straighter smile, many patients feel more confident in their appearance, leading to an improved quality of life both personally and professionally.
