Restorative Driven Implants

Over the past year, Dr. Van Hoof has found deep professional fulfillment in his role as a mentor with Restorative Driven Implants (RDI). Through this partnership, he has had the opportunity to guide dentists as they build confidence and mastery in the surgical placement of dental implants—an area that can be both transformative and intimidating for clinicians.

Most importantly, Dr. Van Hoof values the relationships built through RDI. Collaborating with motivated dentists who are committed to elevating their skills has reaffirmed his passion for teaching and lifelong learning. As he reflects on the past year, he is grateful for the opportunity to contribute to the growth of fellow clinicians and to support RDI’s mission of advancing excellence in implant dentistry.
