Dental Implants FAQs Reno, NV

If you’re considering dental implants , it’s normal to have questions. Replacing missing teeth is an important decision, and understanding the process can help you feel more confident about your treatment. At Great Basin Dental , our team works closely with trusted oral surgeons to provide custom implant restorations that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Below are answers to some of the most common questions we hear from patients.

What are dental implants?

At Great Basin Dental, our dentists focus on creating custom implant restorations that restore both function and appearance. We work closely with respected local oral surgeons throughout the implant process to ensure seamless communication and outstanding results. From precise digital impressions to beautifully crafted crowns, bridges, and implant-supported dentures, every restoration is designed to help you eat, smile, and speak with confidence.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Many healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are excellent candidates for dental implants. Your dentist will evaluate your oral health, jawbone density, gums, and overall medical history before recommending treatment. Even if you’ve experienced some bone loss, procedures like bone grafting may make implants possible. The best way to determine if implants are right for you is through a personalized consultation.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and routine dental visits, the implant itself can often last a lifetime. The custom restoration attached to the implant may eventually need replacement due to normal wear, but many crowns and bridges last for decades. Maintaining healthy gums, brushing twice daily, flossing, and attending regular professional cleanings all contribute to long-term success.

Does getting dental implants hurt?

Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the implant process is. During implant placement, your oral surgeon uses local anesthesia or sedation to keep you comfortable. After surgery, mild soreness and swelling are common for a few days but are usually manageable with medication and home care instructions. By the time your final restoration is placed at Great Basin Dental, the surgical area has already healed.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Every patient is different, but the complete process usually takes several months. After the implant is placed, the jawbone typically needs three to six months to heal and integrate with the titanium post. Once healing is complete, your dentist designs and places your custom restoration. Although the timeline requires patience, the result is a stable, long-lasting tooth replacement.

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants can replace a single missing tooth, several missing teeth, or even an entire arch of teeth. Depending on your needs, your dentist may recommend a single implant crown, an implant-supported bridge, or implant-supported dentures. Each solution is custom-designed to restore comfort, function, and a natural appearance.

What if I don’t have enough jawbone for implants?

Bone loss doesn’t automatically prevent you from receiving dental implants. Many patients qualify after procedures such as bone grafting or other preparatory treatments that rebuild the jawbone. Advanced imaging allows your dental team to evaluate your bone structure and determine the best treatment plan for your specific situation.

Can dental implants get cavities?

The implant itself cannot develop cavities because it is made from titanium and porcelain or other durable restorative materials. However, the surrounding gum tissue and supporting bone can still develop problems if plaque accumulates. Maintaining excellent oral hygiene helps prevent gum disease and peri-implantitis, which are among the most common threats to implant health.

How natural do dental implants look?

Modern dental implants are designed to blend seamlessly with your smile. At Great Basin Dental, every restoration is custom crafted to match the size, shape, and shade of your surrounding teeth. The result is a replacement tooth that feels comfortable and appears so natural that most people won’t be able to tell it apart from your natural teeth.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Smoking doesn’t automatically rule out dental implants, but it can increase the risk of healing complications and implant failure. Tobacco use affects blood flow and slows the body’s natural healing process. If you smoke, your dentist may recommend reducing or quitting before treatment to improve your chances of long-term success.
