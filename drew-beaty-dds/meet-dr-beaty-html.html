Get to Know Dr. Drew Beaty

With over two decades of experience in the dental field so far, Dr. Beaty has plenty of firsthand exposure of the ways quality oral healthcare can positively transform a person’s life. He believes that patients with excellent dental health will be happier, healthier, and even live longer! He particularly enjoys being able to help those in need finally overcome their fear of the dentist here in Federal Way.

To learn more about his background, continue reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

In 1994, Dr. Beaty had to undergo extensive upper and lower jaw surgery, as well as in-depth orthodontic treatment. He was in engineering school at the time, but the experience was eye-opening for him. Dentistry seemed to click into place as a career option out of the blue and decades later, he hasn’t looked back!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

After completing his undergraduate degree in biomedical engineering from Tulane University in New Orleans, Dr. Beaty attended dental school at Northwestern University in Chicago. He’s also extensively trained in sedation dentistry and proudly completed all 9 levels of postgraduate education at the prestigious Kois Center for Dental Excellence in Seattle. Today, he still takes over 100 hours of continuing education per year, which is four times the amount required by the Washington State Department of Health.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Beaty is married to his beautiful wife Kristin and has two children. In his free time, he enjoys running, hiking, and staying busy with kid-related activities. Soccer is also a big passion for him both playing it and cheering for the Seattle Sounders! Community work plays a role in his life as well; for the past eight years, he has participated in a dental outreach program in Jamaica, helping to care for underserved populations.
