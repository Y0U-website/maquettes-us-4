Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 treatment offers some truly remarkable benefits:

  • A strong bite. You can expect to be able to eat virtually any food without worries.
  • A beautiful smile. Our team carefully designs All-on-4 dentures in order to make them as comfortable, natural-looking, and attractive as possible.
  • Jawbone preservation. Because dental implants stimulate the jawbone, they can help to keep it strong and whole.
  • An All-on-4 denture is permanently anchored in the mouth, so in many ways, you can treat it in the same way you would natural teeth.
  • You will never have to worry about your denture slipping out of place.

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dental Implants

There are a few key differences between All-on-4 treatment and traditional dental implants:

During your consultation, our team can help you decide which dental implant treatment is the best fit for your unique circumstances.

  • All-on-4 is often faster and less invasive than full-arch replacement with traditional implants. That is because it usually requires just one surgery, rather than two or more. Plus, All-on-4 often uses fewer implant posts.
  • Traditional dental implants are more versatile. They can replace any number of lost teeth. Plus, using more implants to support a denture may provide it with additional strength and stability.
