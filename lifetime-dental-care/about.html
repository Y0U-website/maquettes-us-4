If you’re looking for a new family or general dentistry provider, you’ll love Lifetime Dental Care®. We believe that maintaining quality dental health involves more than just general cleaning such as brushing and flossing your teeth — it involves caring for your smile for a lifetime. We understand that most people have at least some degree of uncertainty regarding a trip to the dentist’s office. That’s why our goal is to keep you informed of your condition and treatment at all times, and to explain each dental procedure in a clear, straight-forward manner.

Everyone deserves a beautiful smile. At Lifetime Dental Care®, Bel Air dentists Drs. Bivin Phillips and William Nagle will give you the smile you’ve always wished for through cosmetic and general dentistry techniques.

Our new guests are amazed by the pampering they experience in our office, while our regulars look forward to their visits. From your first call to book your visit, you begin to notice the extras we offer our guests. You’ll receive unrushed, one-on-one care customized for you. We always take the time to listen to your concerns, to explain our procedures and to answer your questions. We use the latest, most gentle equipment, appropriate for your needs. We offer massage cushions, warm towels and comfortable pillows to put you at ease.
