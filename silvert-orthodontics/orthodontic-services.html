Orthodontic Treatment for Adults

Adult teeth and jaws are not as easily manipulated as children. Even so, adults can receive enormous benefits from orthodontic treatment.

Common problems with adults that orthodontia may correct include tooth loss and degradation of the structures (jaw bones) that support the teeth.

Orthodontic Treatment for Children

Orthodontic appliances can do miracles as your child develops. For example, a device may stave off problems with an upper or lower jaw that isn’t growing at the same rate as the other or correct problems that create difficulties with chewing or swallowing.

Step 3: Get Your Braces Placed

Once you decide to begin, we schedule your braces appointment. Getting dental braces in Crown Point, IN, usually takes about one to two hours.

First, we clean and dry your teeth. Then we place brackets and attach wires that gently guide your teeth into position.

Step 4: Adjustments and Progress Checks

Braces work through steady, gentle pressure. With Dental Braces in Crown Point, IN, you will visit our office every few weeks for adjustments.

At each visit, Dr. Michael E. Silvert, orthodontist, checks your progress. We may tighten wires or replace elastic bands.

These appointments are short and help keep your treatment on track.

Step 5: Caring for Your Braces

Good care is important during treatment with dental braces in Crown Point, IN. Proper brushing and flossing protect your teeth and gums.

Our team will show you how to clean around brackets and wires. We are always here to answer questions.

  • Brush after every meal using a soft toothbrush
  • Floss daily with tools made for braces
  • Avoid sticky or hard foods
  • Wear rubber bands as directed

Step 6: Completing Treatment and Retainers

When your teeth have moved into the right position, your dental braces in Crown Point, IN, will be removed. This is an exciting day for every patient.

After braces, you will receive a retainer. Retainers keep your teeth from shifting back.

Wearing your retainer as instructed helps protect your new smile for years to come.
