The practice

Phase One orthodontic treatment is a guiding treatment for children having both baby and adult teeth present. Phase One may include space maintainers, rapid palatal expanders, removable appliances and/or fixed orthodontic appliances.

Phase Two orthodontic treatment is used for children and adult patients having both late mixed dentition (adult and baby teeth) or only adult teeth.

Dr. Silvert collaborates closely with several oral and plastic surgeons to offer combined orthodontic and surgical jaw correction (orthognathic treatment) for the severest of skeletal-dental problems, including cleft palate
