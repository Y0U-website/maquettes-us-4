Get To Know Your Dentist

Dr. Hengameh Jannati

While growing up, Dr. Jannati was always interested in biological sciences, human physiology, and healthcare. These interests drew her toward dentistry at a young age. And as she grew up and had positive experiences with her childhood dentists, her curiosity only continued to grow.

Finally, she decided that dentistry was the right field for her. As a dentist, she could combine her love for people, her passion for science, and her artistic side, too. Dr. Jannati went to UCLA for her undergraduate degree in Psychobiology. Then, she attended the UCSF School of Dentistry, graduating with her dental degree in 2017.

After working as an associate dentist for several years, Dr. Jannati jumped at the opportunity to open her own practice. As the owner and operator of Dental Suite of Manhattan Beach, she uses her past experiences, dental training, and unique vision of dentistry to provide a truly top-class patient experience.

Dr. Jannati knows that getting dental care can be intimidating. That’s why she focuses on educating patients, building trust, and creating long-lasting relationships. And as a conservative dentist dedicated to transparency, she’ll only recommend dental procedures when she truly believes they&#x27;re necessary to protect your oral health.

Outside of the office, Dr. Jannati loves spending time with family and friends. She also enjoys indulging her artistic side by painting, visiting art galleries, and attending classical music performances. She also loves to stay active and enjoy the gorgeous California weather by exploring the great outdoors, hiking, and practicing yoga.

Putting Smiles On Patients’ Faces

From the moment you walk into Dental Suite of Manhattan Beach, we want you and your family to feel safe, comfortable, and welcome. Want to meet our friendly front office staff and experienced dental team before your first visit? Read on!

Preparing For Your Appointment What To Expect From Your Visit If you are visiting us for the first time, we ask that you fill out a few forms before your visit. You can do so online, or in our relaxing waiting area with a complimentary beverage.

One of our dental team members will then bring you back to an operating room for your appointment. Dr. Jannati will then perform a comprehensive oral evaluation. She will then discuss all her findings, go over any recommendations she has, will answer all your questions and guide you in making a decision if a treatment is needed.

Lastly, you’ll stop by our front desk to schedule your next checkup or any other followups. That’s all there is to it!
