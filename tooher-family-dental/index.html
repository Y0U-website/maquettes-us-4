The practice

Dr. Alex Tooher began Tooher Family Dental in February of 2025, continuing the legacy of care that Dr. Stephen Palmer and Dr. Laura Krause provided for over 40 years to the South Portland community. With a deep respect for the practice’s history and the patients it has served, Dr. Tooher is proud to carry that legacy forward.

As part of this transition, we’re pleased to share that the friendly team you’ve come to know and trust has stayed the same with a few wonderful additions. We look forward to seeing you soon!
