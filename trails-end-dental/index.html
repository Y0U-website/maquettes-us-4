In October 2018, Kelsey transferred from Dr. Retzlaff’s chairside Dental Assistant to our front desk Scheduling Coordinator position. Now, she serves as Office Manager.

Her dental knowledge is invaluable in scheduling appointments, billing insurance, and responding to many phone calls. After her work day, Kelsey loves spending time outdoors with her family camping, snowboarding, four-wheeling, and horseback riding.

Robyn started her dental career 25 years ago as a chairside assistant. Her work experience includes a periodontal office, orthodontic offices and general dentistry. Although she started as an assistant, she found herself enjoying the front office and has worked as a patient care coordinator as well as a scheduling coordinator.

Robyn is a mother of 2 children, her son is studying to become a marine biologist and daughter is in high school. Her main interests are riding and showing horses with her daughter, game nights at home and traveling with her husband and kids, especially to Hawaii and Disneyland.

We welcomed Cindy to our team in May 2019. She has twenty-seven years of experience as a Dental Hygienist and truly loves this profession. As a hygienist, Cindy is motivated, professional, and committed to helping patients achieve optimum oral health.

Cindy and her husband are long time Oregon City residents. Her hobbies are antiques and quilting.
