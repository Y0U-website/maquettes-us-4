Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Hishmeh received his undergraduate degree in Biological Sciences and Chemistry from the University of Illinois at Chicago in 1997. He graduated with full membership in the Honors College. Dr. Hishmeh attended the University of Illinois College of Dentistry in 1999 and received a Bachelor in Dental Science. He later obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2002.

Dr. Hishmeh strives to help patients conquer their fear of visiting the dentist. He also enjoys working with patients and developing relationships that would last a lifetime.

The world of Dentistry is constantly evolving and new technology is making the profession easier and more comfortable to patients. Dr Hishmeh attends many continuing educational classes to keep up with the changes. Dr. Hishmeh is a member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Tri- County Dental Society and DOCS oral conscious sedation.
