Payment Options & Insurance

Treatment coverage varies from one dental insurance provider to another. Lanier Family & Cosmetic Dentistry in Buford, GA is not responsible for and can make no guarantees as to the coverage your dental insurance will provide.

Lanier Family & Cosmetic Dentistry accepts many insurance plans and will work with you to provide your estimated cost for treatment and maximize your benefits. As a service to you, our staff will happily electronically file your insurance claim to your insurance company so that you will know what your benefits will cover.

Most dental insurance companies will cover preventive services such as dental cleanings, routine examinations, and dental x-rays but most do not cover advanced restorative dentistry procedures like dental implants.

Many of our patients have dental insurance and we will help our patients maximize their dental insurance benefits. If you have specific questions about your dental insurance, please contact our patient coordinator.

We accept a range of payment options and understand the financial concerns of our patients. We will try our best to work with you to provide the dentistry you need or desire within your budget. As a courtesy, we do offer a 5 % senior citizens discount.
