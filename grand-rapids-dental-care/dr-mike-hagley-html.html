Grand Rapids’ Hometown Dentists

Drs. Rachel and Spencer Buchert met in dental school and bonded over their passion for dentistry and the outdoors before they got married in 2014. Today, they’re proud to be considered a trusted and go-to dental team in Grand Rapids. Following in the footsteps of Grand Rapids Dental Care founder, Dr. Mike Hagley, they are focused on patient centered care and continuous education for themselves and their patients. They collaborate closely on treatment plans because they each bring a unique skill set and knowledge to the table, and two heads are better than one!

Featured Dental Services

We’re committed to our patients’ health and wellbeing, which is why we make sure that no stone goes left unturned during examinations and treatments. We provide a wide array of services to meet all of your family’s needs, making accessing dentistry easier than ever.

I Have a Cavity or Broken Tooth

We use the latest technology, including CEREC & CAD/CAM software to craft and place customized dental crowns in a single two-hour appointment.

I am Missing One or More Teeth

Missing teeth can have a drastic impact on your daily life. We create customized, durable prosthetics to restore the function and appearance of smiles.

I Need a Dentist for My Child

Children deserve attentive, mindful, and educational dental care from a team that doesn’t make them feel on-edge, and we provide just that!

I Want a Straighter Smile

If you’d like to straighten your smile or address bite problems, but don’t want to commit to metal braces, Invisalign could be the perfect option for you!

I am in Pain & Need Help

We provide same-day appointments and first-aid guidance to make sure you don’t have to sit in discomfort.
