Complaint Resolution & Grievances

Valley View Health Center is accredited by the Accreditation Association for Ambulatory Health Care. Accreditation means Valley View Health Center has demonstrated compliance with organizational, patient care, and safety standards. Our set standards are to continuously improve performance, provide the highest quality of patient care, and ensure our patients are treated in a safe environment.

If you have a concern or complaint about patient care, privacy, safety, or any other concern, please contact our Compliance Hotline at 855.252.7606 or submit your concern by clicking here . (Please use either Valley View or VVHC as the organization to ensure your concern makes it to our organization.)

If you would like our staff to respond to you, please provide your contact information in your report.
