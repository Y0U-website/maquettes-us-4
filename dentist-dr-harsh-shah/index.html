Welcome to Family & Implant Dental, the general dentistry practice of Harsh Shah, DMD, and his talented dental team.

Everyone is different. Some of our patients want nothing more than to eliminate pain when they eat. Others want a celebrity-perfect smile that dazzles everyone in the room. We communicate with our patients to discover their unique goals so that we can provide exactly the type of dentistry that helps them meet their goals for their smiles.

We never pressure patients into a decision, but answer questions, provide the pros and cons for various treatments, and help you make a choice that makes the most sense to you. We want you to feel confident in the treatment you receive while in our care!

We understand that families today are busier than ever and that fitting your dentistry into your schedule can pose a real challenge. That is why we offer evening and weekend dental appointments to make it easier to get the care you need to stay healthy.
