What Is Teeth Cleaning & Hygiene?

Teeth cleaning and hygiene in Elma is a fundamental aspect of maintaining a healthy and radiant smile. It refers to the routine process of removing plaque and tartar that builds up on the teeth over time. This procedure is performed by a skilled dental hygienist using specialized tools to ensure precision and comfort. Regular teeth cleanings prevent dental problems such as cavities, gum disease, and tooth loss. They also contribute to overall oral health, which studies have linked to general health conditions, including heart disease and stroke.

The Teeth Cleaning & Hygiene Treatment Process

Dental Examination

The teeth cleaning and hygiene process begins with a comprehensive dental examination. This involves a thorough assessment of your oral health by Dr. Finney. He will inspect your teeth, gums, and mouth for any signs of dental issues such as cavities, gum disease, or oral cancer. This step is crucial as it helps to identify any underlying problems that need to be addressed before the cleaning process.

Plaque and Tartar Removal

Once your teeth have been examined and deemed healthy, the next step is the removal of plaque and tartar. Plaque is a sticky layer of bacteria that builds up on your teeth, while tartar is hardened plaque that is more difficult to remove. At Springbrook Family Dentistry , our dental hygienists use a process called scaling to gently remove these substances from the surface of your teeth and along the gum line.

Polishing and Fluoride Treatment

The final step in the teeth cleaning process involves polishing your teeth to remove any remaining plaque or stains. This is followed by a fluoride treatment, which strengthens your teeth and helps prevent cavities. This process leaves your teeth feeling smooth, clean, and refreshed.

The Benefits Of Teeth Cleaning & Hygiene

Prevents Oral Diseases

Regular teeth cleanings can help prevent various oral diseases, such as cavities, gum disease, and tooth loss. By removing the buildup of plaque and tartar, they reduce the risk of these problems and promote overall oral health. Additionally, professional cleanings can reach areas that are difficult to clean at home, ensuring a more thorough removal of harmful bacteria. This comprehensive approach significantly decreases the likelihood of developing painful and costly dental issues in the future.

Enhances Your Smile

Cleaning your teeth not only ensures your oral health but also enhances the appearance of your smile. It removes stains caused by coffee, tea, wine, or tobacco, giving you a brighter and more confident smile. Furthermore, regular cleanings can help maintain the natural shape and contours of your teeth, preventing wear and erosion that can alter your smile&#x27;s aesthetics. This preservation of your dental structure contributes to a more youthful and attractive appearance over time.

Improves Overall Health

If gum recession has exposed some of your tooth roots, dental bonding can be used to protect these areas. This not only improves the appearance of your smile but also helps prevent sensitivity to hot and cold.
