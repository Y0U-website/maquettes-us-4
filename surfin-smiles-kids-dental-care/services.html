Preventative Care

General Anesthesia

For those children that have a tough time with treatment our office offers an extremely safe and humane alternative. We have the ability to preform the treatment in the hospital or surgery center while your child sleeps under a dual provider model where our Doctors take care of the teeth and a Board Certified Anesthesiologist monitors your child. This is an extremely valuable alternative that allows us to provide the treatment your child needs while ensuring we do not traumatize your child.
