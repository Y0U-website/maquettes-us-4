Get To Know Dr. Fallon Theis

Dr. Fallon Theis was born and raised in Hutchinson, Minnesota, with deep ties to the community she now serves. Her journey began with a high school job shadowing experience that ignited her passion for oral healthcare. Dr. Theis earned her Bachelor&#x27;s of Science degree from the University of St. Thomas in 2014 and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2018. She is a member of the American Dental Association and the Minnesota Dental Association.

Dr. Theis has proudly volunteered her services for Minnesota Mission of Mercy as well as Donated Dental Services. She shares three young sons with her husband Spencer, also a Hutchinson native. In her spare time, she enjoys her family, reading, journaling, cooking, gardening, watching sports, and participating in community events.

Dr. Theis takes pride in creating a comforting, nurturing, safe, environment for all of her patients. She values the importance of working together with her patients to achieve the optimal care they deserve. She is honored and proud to come home and serve the community that raised her, and she looks forward to building lasting relationships with her patients for many years to come.
