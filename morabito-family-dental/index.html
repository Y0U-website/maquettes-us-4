Starting at about $1/day, our low-cost in-house dental plan can cover your entire family. For a small annual fee, you’ll receive free regular cleaning & general care. Crowns, fillings & bridges, as well as cosmetic care, are available at a lower out-of-pocket cost. Call us today for details!

Dr. Morabito recognizes what it takes to be the best: “You must dedicate your mind, heart & soul to your profession.” He spends more than 100 hours annually on continuing education & invests heavily in the latest technology to ensure optimal dental care for all his patients.

Dr. Morabito is a second generation dentist, having picked up the torch from his father Peter A. Morabito: “I watched my father help so many people, not just with their teeth, but with their lives. He was an incredible humanitarian. When I was a child, people knew him & would come over to say hello. As a teenager, while working in his office as a dental assistant, patients spoke incredibly highly…

“Dentistry, for me, is just as I remember with my father. Every day is like a party with your friends & family coming to see you. You get to watch the little children grow up, & the younger generation gets married & has children. Our practice feels like a family.”
