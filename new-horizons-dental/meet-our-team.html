Our Team

LAURA BLAKEMAN - STELLA

Laura is our Hygienist Team Lead, with nearly 30 years of Hygiene experience. Laura graduated from the Clark College Dental Hygiene Program and received her Bachelors of Science from Eastern Washington University. She is a Pacific Northwest native. She is a blended-family mother of eight children and a grandmother of a few more. Laura prides herself on providing an amazing experience for both new patients, and those who have been seeing her for years.

MONIKA ALCORN

Monika graduated from the Dental Hygiene Program at Pacific University in 2008 and went on to attain her Masters in Rural and Community Oral Health at Idaho State University. She has been with New Horizons Dental since June of 2015. Monika has two sons. She practices sustainable living by growing food in her garden and fruit orchards. She enjoys travel and is a voracious reader of all kinds of books.

MALLARY VAN KLEY
