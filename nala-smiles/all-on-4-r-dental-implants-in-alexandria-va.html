Caring for Your Fixed Full-Arch Restorations

Maintaining your new All-on-4® smile is remarkably straightforward and closely resembles caring for a natural set of teeth.

Daily brushing with a soft-bristled toothbrush, flossing beneath the prosthetic arch using a water flosser or specialized threaders, and visiting our office for routine dental checkups will keep your oral tissues in pristine condition.

Your dentist may recommend specialized cleaning tools designed specifically to glide under the prosthetic arch to sweep away hidden food particles and plaque. Consistent hygiene routines protect the surrounding gum tissue from inflammation, ensuring the lifetime stability of your implant foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4® Dental Implants

What is the average cost of an All-on-4® dental implant in Alexandria?

The total cost of an All-on-4® full-arch restoration varies based on the current health of your jaw, whether extractions are required, and the specific materials selected for your final custom bridge (such as high-strength zirconia or acrylic). During your initial consultation at NaLa Smiles, we provide a completely transparent, itemized fee breakdown alongside available financing options so there are never any financial surprises.

Is the All-on-4® dental implant procedure painful?

The surgical placement is performed under local anesthesia or comfortable dental sedation, meaning you will feel absolutely no pain during the actual treatment. Over the first few days of recovery, it is completely normal to experience minor swelling or mild discomfort. This is easily managed using prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication as directed by our surgical team.

How long does it take to recover from All-on-4® surgery?

Most patients comfortably return to their normal work and light activities within 2 to 3 days after surgery. While your surface gum tissues heal over a few weeks, the underlying jawbone takes roughly 3 to 6 months to fully fuse with the titanium implants through a process called osseointegration.

Am I a good candidate for All-on-4® dental implants?

If you are missing most or all of your teeth, currently wear standard dentures, or are facing widespread extractions due to advanced periodontal disease or decay, you are likely an excellent candidate. Because the implants are tilted to exploit the maximum bone available, even patients who have been told they lack the bone depth for traditional implants are often candidates for All-on-4® without needing a bone graft.

What is the difference between traditional dentures and All-on-4® implants?

Traditional dentures are removable plates that rest loosely on top of the gums, often requiring sticky adhesives, altering your speech, and restoring only a small fraction of your natural chewing power. All-on-4® implants are surgically locked directly into your jawbone, meaning they never move, require no adhesives, look entirely natural, and restore up to 90 % of your original bite force.

How long do All-on-4® dental implants last?

The underlying titanium dental implants are designed to be a permanent, lifetime solution. The custom-fabricated prosthetic bridge attached to them is incredibly durable and typically lasts 10 to 20 years or more, depending on materials, individual wear, and how consistently you follow your home hygiene and professional dental cleaning schedules.
