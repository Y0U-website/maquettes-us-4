The Dental Implant Process: What to Expect

Receiving dental implants begins with a thorough evaluation. The dental team examines the teeth, gums, and jawbone structure using advanced digital diagnostic imaging to determine whether implants are appropriate for you. Each stage is carefully planned to promote long-term comfort and stability:

  • Consultation and Treatment Planning: A comprehensive diagnostic evaluation of your oral health to map exact implant placement locations.
  • Implant Placement: A precise surgical procedure where the titanium post is positioned securely within the jawbone.
  • Healing and Integration: A crucial period during which the implant naturally fuses with the bone via osseointegration.
  • Restoration Placement: The final step, where your custom crown, bridge, or permanent denture is attached to complete the replacement tooth structure.

Caring for Dental Implants

Dental implants are maintained much like natural teeth. Daily brushing, diligent flossing, and routine professional dental visits help keep the surrounding gums completely healthy and protect the long-term integrity of the implant restoration. Regular checkups allow our Alexandria dental team to closely monitor the condition of the implant and surrounding tissues to support ongoing success.

All-on-4 Full Arch Implant Procedure With the innovative All-on-4® implant procedure , we can literally restore an entire arch of teeth in just one appointment at our Alexandria, VA, office. Bone Grafts & Sinus Lifts Bone grafting helps preserve your smile and the natural contour of your jaw by adding biocompatible grafting material around areas affected by gum disease or where long-term tooth loss has occurred. Placing grafted material in areas with missing teeth strengthens the sites and prepares them for dental implants. Rebuilding your bone in areas of deterioration provides a firm foundation for your implants to safely fuse during healing.

When you lose an upper tooth—especially due to long-term gum disease or shifting—there may be minimal natural bone structure left to work with. A specialized sinus lift can resolve that. During a sinus lift, a small opening is safely made in the sinus area above the roots of the affected missing tooth to form a precise space where your dentist will place the composite material to safely secure the implant post later.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are a popular and effective long-term solution for people who have missing or failing teeth or chronic dental problems. They are artificial tooth roots, shaped like screws. When dental implants are placed in your jawbone, they bond with your natural bone. They serve as a sturdy base for one or more artificial teeth, called crowns.

At NaLa Smiles, our team has extensive experience in dental implant care, helping patients from all over Alexandria regain their confident smiles.

Why should I consider dental implants?

Dental implants are widely regarded as the closest thing to natural teeth. They restore not only the appearance of your smile but also your ability to eat and speak with ease. Unlike dentures, which can slip and cause discomfort, dental implants are firmly anchored in your jawbone, providing stability and preventing bone loss.

Plus, they&#x27;re designed to last a lifetime with proper care and regular check-ups.

What does the procedure for dental implants in Alexandria involve?

The dental implant procedure involves several stages. First, the implant, made of titanium, is surgically placed into the jawbone. Once the implant is in place, the bone around it needs time to heal through a process called osseointegration, which can take several months. After healing, an abutment is attached to the implant post.

Finally, the new artificial tooth (or teeth), which is custom-made to match your natural teeth, is attached to the abutment. Our dentists at NaLa Smiles in Alexandria, VA, are highly skilled in performing this procedure, ensuring minimal discomfort and optimal results for our patients.

Are dental implants right for me?

Most people with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates should have good overall health, healthy gums, and sufficient jawbone to support the implants. However, even if you have jawbone loss, you may still be a candidate for dental implants, as modern techniques such as bone grafting can provide the necessary support.

It’s important to discuss your medical history and all possible treatment options with a qualified professional. Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The surgical placement of a dental implant is typically performed under local anesthesia, meaning you shouldn&#x27;t feel pain during the procedure. Most patients report that the recovery process feels very similar to a standard tooth extraction. Any mild post-operative discomfort can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers or with care instructions from our dental team.

How long do dental implants last?

When properly integrated into the jawbone and maintained with excellent oral hygiene (brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings twice a year), dental implants are built to be a permanent, lifetime solution to tooth loss. While the custom ceramic crowns attached to the implants might experience normal wear over decades, the underlying titanium posts are designed to remain securely fused for life.
