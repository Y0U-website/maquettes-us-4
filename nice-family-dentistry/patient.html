Insurance & Co-Pays

Nice Family Dentistry is happy to work with patients covered by dental insurance. We believe insurance is a great incentive to maintain a vital level of dental health. However, It is rare--very rare that a dental plan covers 100 % of all fees. Here’s why:

The fees we charge for dental services are the same for every patient, insured or not. A given insurance policy, however, is based on a fixed fee schedule--"usual and customary"--that may have nothing to do with the real world. Dentistry has changed very quickly, insurance fee schedules have not. After all, insurance companies are profitable businesses, not dental benefactors.

We work with most dental insurers. Our office accepts nearly all PPO insurance plans for dental services. Sorry, we do not accept HMO’s or Medicaid.

If you have an insurance plan that we participate with, that means you will receive a Fee Schedule fee for some procedures. Your insurance may still pay a percentage of our regular fees, even if you have a different plan than what we participate with. It is likely that you’ll discover our regular fees are more competitive than many other offices. To ensure coverage by your provider, please call our office to confirm your insurance plan with Nice Family Dentistry.

We’ll fill out your claim forms with you and answer any questions we can. Please keep in mind you are responsible for your total obligation should your insurance benefits result in less coverage than anticipated. We do ask that you pay your estimated portion at each visit. Please note that we can only give you an estimate, after the dental insurance payment is received, if an additional balance occurs for the insured, a statement will be sent explaining the patient balance.

We’re happy to help you with any insurance questions you have. We’ll go over your policy with you, try to maximize your benefits, and if needed we will request a predetermination of benefits to let you know what your insurance will pay. Please remember your employer purchased your plan and your insurance company dictates your coverage--we don’t.

Electronic Claim Processing

Nice Family Dentistry uses electronic claims processing. This means that rather than sending your dental claim via snail mail, most claims will be sent electronically to your insurance company via the internet. By filing your claim electronically, information is submitted more efficiently and with fewer errors.

This benefits our patients for the following reasons:

As a courtesy, our team is happy to submit your dental claims to your insurance company for you.

  • The turnaround time on claims is faster
  • Fewer claims are returned or denied.
