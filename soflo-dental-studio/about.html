Carmine Scarfone, DMD

Born and raised in South Florida, Dr. Carmine Scarfone has always believed that exceptional dentistry begins with genuine relationships.

Before pursuing dentistry, he competed as a Division I football player at Colgate University , where he developed the discipline, work ethic, and attention to detail that continue to shape his approach to patient care. He later earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology , graduating Magna Cum Laude from Florida Atlantic University , before receiving his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Nova Southeastern University.

These experiences laid the foundation for a career dedicated to combining clinical excellence with compassionate, patient-centered care. Dr. Scarfone founded SoFlo Dental Studio with a simple vision: to create a dental practice where patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in every step of their care. By combining advanced technology, modern techniques, and a gentle approach, he strives to make every visit as comfortable as it is rewarding.
