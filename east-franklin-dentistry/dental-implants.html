Dental Implants

  • What are the different types of dental implants? Common types of dental implants include single-tooth implants, implant-supported bridges, and implant-supported dentures. Some patients may also be candidates for mini implants, which are smaller and used in specific situations.
  • What are the benefits of dental implants? Dental implants look and function like natural teeth, help preserve bone, don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support, and provide long-lasting stability for a confident, comfortable smile.
