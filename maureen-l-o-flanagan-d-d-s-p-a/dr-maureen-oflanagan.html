Dr. Maureen O’Flanagan

Dr. Maureen O’Flanagan’s passion for dentistry began when she was in high school, working part-time for her father’s dental practice in Deerfield Beach. She attended college in New Orleans. She then attended the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco, where she received her DDS degree.

Fellowship in Geriatric Dentistry, The American Dental Association, one of 10 given nationally, 1986.

Dr. O’Flanagan started her practice in Deerfield Beach in 1988; she and her highly skilled staff have the knowledge, experience, and skills to care for you. She is a consummate professional who is committed to continuing education and using the latest technology. Please review a list of a few of her most recent accomplishments among her peers in the dental field.

Florida Academy of Dental Practice Administration - 1993 to present

Broward County Dental Association - 1989 to present
