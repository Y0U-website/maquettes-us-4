Education & Prevention

Preventative care and education are the keys to optimal dental health.

Good “dental health care” can preempt “disease care,” and we focus on thorough exams – checking the overall health of your teeth and gums, performing oral cancer exams, and taking x-rays when necessary.

These routine cleanings, flossing, sealants, and fluoride are all helpful in preventing dental disease.
