Why it is important to visit a dentist regularly?

Should I visit the dentist even if my teeth look fine?

Yes, it is important to visit your dentist regularly even if your teeth seem fine. There might exist several dental problems without your knowledge. Also, if you are not happy with the alignment, structure, or color of your teeth, then you can contact us. Our cosmetic dentistry services will help you get that smile which you have always wanted.

At what age should I start taking my child to a dentist?

Your child should start seeing a dentist at an early age of six months. This has been recommended by the American Dental Association. This is so because, at this age, your child’s baby teeth would start coming up. And it is important to take proper care of them from the very beginning.

What is a cavity?

Tooth decay leads to the formation of a small hole in the tooth known as the tooth cavity. These are mainly formed due to plaque buildup on the tooth. If the cavity is left untreated, then it can cause severe oral problems. Cavities can be prevented by maintaining regular and proper oral hygiene routine.

When should I change my toothbrush?

When you brush your teeth twice a day, it will eventually lead to your toothbrush getting worn out. Hence, it is recommended that you change your toothbrush every three months. If you have gum diseases, then it is better to change your toothbrush every six weeks. Keep the bristles clean with hot water and if you have been sick, change the brush as soon as possible.
