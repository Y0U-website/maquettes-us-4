How do you help with airway health?

Our approach isn’t just about straightening teeth after the fact. We use early intervention appliance systems, designed to correct harmful habits and guide jaw development to optimize your airway health.

Our airway-focused dentistry is built around the principle of prevention. Early guidance of jaw growth and correction of oral habits can prevent more invasive orthodontic or airway treatment later.

  • Myobrace®: This preventive pre-orthodontic treatment addresses poor oral habits like mouth breathing, tongue thrusting, and incorrect swallowing patterns, often reducing or eliminating the need for braces later.
  • HealthyStart®: HealthyStart uses a series of orthodontic appliances to address early signs of sleep-disordered breathing.
  • Vivos Therapeutics®: A non-surgical approach that supports optimal airway health and jaw development using a customized retainer-like appliance.

What is interceptive orthodontics?

Waiting until all permanent teeth come in can be too late for orthodontic care. Unlike adults, children’s jaws are still developing. That means there is a window of opportunity to guide the jaw into the proper direction. With interceptive orthodontics, Dr. Del Valle can intervene and correct problems before they advance.

For children with narrowed dental arches, early crowding, or crossbites, your dentist recommends palatal expansion . Palatal expanders gradually widen your child’s palate, creating space in the mouth as the jaw develops and more teeth erupt.

Think about when you pinch a straw. Not so much water or soda can pass through. The same is true for your child’s airway. When it’s restricted or too small, the tongue has nowhere to go, often restricting airflow. However, palatal expansion creates space in the upper airway, which means better sleep, better breathing, and a better life.

  • Reduces future orthodontic complexity
  • Creates space for incoming adult teeth
  • Prevents the need for extractions
  • Supports optimal jaw and facial development
  • Optimizes airway health

Do you offer metal braces?

When metal braces are the best option, we offer Fastbraces®. This groundbreaking innovation in orthodontics allows dentists to move teeth quickly and safely in as little as three months.

Conventional braces move teeth in two phases. Fastbraces uses a triangular bracket system that moves the tooth’s root and crown simultaneously. The system bypasses the slower two-step process used in traditional braces.

Should I wear a retainer after treatment?

Finishing your orthodontic treatment is a milestone. After treatment, Dr. Del Valle will recommend a retainer to protect your investment. Otherwise, your teeth may relapse to their old positions.
