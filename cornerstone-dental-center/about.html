Dr. Don Tatalovich

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Don Tatalovich is proud to carry forward the trusted legacy of Cornerstone Dental Center. Originally from rural Wisconsin, he holds degrees in biology and public health and served in the U.S. Navy as a rescue swimmer before earning his DDS from Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. His diverse background shapes his well-rounded, whole-health approach to dental care—one that emphasizes prevention, patient education, and personalized treatment plans tailored to each individual.

Dr. Tatalovich has a special interest in surgical and prosthetic procedures, including dental implants, same-day crowns, and comprehensive restorative dentistry. With a deep belief in transparency and patient autonomy, he focuses on helping patients make informed decisions about their oral health. Whether he’s restoring a smile or guiding a family through long-term dental care, Dr. Tatalovich is committed to maintaining a compassionate experience while achieving outstanding outcomes for each person.

Dr. Hann

Get to Know

With more than four decades of experience serving the Hayden and Coeur d’Alene communities, Dr. Hann built Cornerstone Dental Center on relationships, trust, and the belief that patients should always feel like family. Known for his calm demeanor and commitment to service, he has participated in both local and international outreach efforts and remains a valued part of the practice today.

Now practicing part-time, Dr. Hann continues to support patients who have known him for years. His presence in the office helps maintain the friendly, familiar atmosphere that long-time patients love—while also welcoming a new generation of care under Dr. Tatalovich’s leadership.

Meet the Cornerstone Team

Always Here for You

From the front desk to the operatory, our close-knit team is here to make your visit feel calm, clear, and completely personalized. You won’t be rushed or passed between providers—you’ll be greeted by familiar faces, listened to, and cared for with excellence and empathy.

What to Expect at Your First Visit

Know What’s Ahead

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll notice the difference. Our practice isn’t focused on speed—we’re focused on connection. Your first visit will include a comprehensive exam, time to ask questions, and a one-on-one conversation about your goals, concerns, and treatment options. We believe dental care should be collaborative, not confusing.

Your First Visit Experience You’ll enjoy personalized, educational care in a peaceful environment. Our bird feeders outside each window offer a calming view, while our team focuses on getting to know you and your smile. We’ll walk you through each step and make sure you feel supported and informed from the start.

Technology and Comfort That Put You First

We’ve designed every aspect of our office with you in mind—offering advanced tools for efficient, high-quality treatment and simple comforts to help you feel at ease. From in-house crowns to peaceful views, our goal is to make dental care less stressful and more personal.

In-House Same-Day Crowns

Using CEREC and digital design software, we can craft and place crowns in a single visit—no temporary, no second appointment. It’s faster, easier, and more precise.

3D Printing & Digital Design

We use in-house 3D printing and scanning technology to deliver accurate, comfortable restorations with fewer appointments and greater customization.

Bird Feeders at Every Operatory

Instead of a TV screen, you’ll find a tranquil view of nature just outside your window. Our operatories feature bird feeders that attract local wildlife, offering a quiet, calming experience unlike any other dental visit.
