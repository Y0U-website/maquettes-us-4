Schedule your checkup Visit our practice Call (208) 772-5141 What’s the Difference Between Traditional and Immediate Implants? Traditional dental implants are typically placed in a two-step process. After a damaged tooth is removed, the area is left to heal—usually for several months—before the implant post is placed. Once the implant has integrated with the bone, a final crown is added.

Immediate implants, on the other hand, are placed at the same time the tooth is extracted. This approach can shorten your overall treatment timeline and reduce the number of surgical visits. However, not every patient is a candidate for immediate implants. Factors like bone health, infection, and location of the tooth can impact whether this option is right for you.

During your consultation, we’ll help you determine which approach best fits your needs, health, and goals.

Tooth Removal & Implant PlacementI

If you’re a candidate for immediate implants, the implant post is placed on the same day as your tooth extraction—minimizing treatment time and helping preserve bone. If traditional implants are needed, we’ll first allow the area to heal after extraction, then place the implant post once the bone is ready.

Healing & Final Restoration

With both approaches, the implant needs time to fuse with your jawbone—a process called osseointegration. Once healed, we’ll place a custom crown designed to match your natural teeth in shape, shade, and strength, completing your confident new smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

What is a single tooth implant?

A single tooth implant is a modern solution for replacing a missing tooth. It consists of a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone and a custom-made crown attached to it. Unlike removable options like partial dentures , implants are permanently fixed, offering stability and comfort that feels just like your natural teeth.

How are dental implants made?

Dental implants combine precision engineering with customization. The implant post is made from medical-grade titanium and comes in various standard sizes. The crown, however, is custom-made specifically for you. We either create it in-house using advanced technology or work with a specialized lab. The crown is typically made from porcelain for its natural appearance and strength. Once ready, we carefully attach it to your implant, completing your restoration.

How long will my dental implant last?

With proper placement and care, dental implants can last for decades. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement over time due to normal wear, but the implant itself often lasts a lifetime. Regular check-ups allow us to monitor everything and address any issues early.

Can my dental implant become infected?

While uncommon, dental implants can develop an infection called peri-implantitis. This typically happens if proper oral hygiene isn&#x27;t maintained. To minimize this risk, it&#x27;s important to follow post-operative care instructions and maintain good oral hygiene practices.

Do dental implants stop bone loss?

Yes! When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area starts to deteriorate from lack of stimulation. An implant acts like an artificial tooth root, transmitting chewing forces to the jawbone. This stimulation maintains bone density and strength, much like a natural tooth would.

Can I get an implant in one day?

In some cases, you can receive a dental implant and temporary restoration in a single day. This depends on your specific situation and the type of implant best suited for you. The temporary restoration allows for normal function while your implant heals. We&#x27;ll schedule follow-ups to monitor healing and create your permanent crown.
