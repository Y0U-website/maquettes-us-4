About Southwest Family Dental in…

At Southwest Family Dental, we are 100 % committed to providing the best possible dental experience. Our goal is to provide patients a comfortable atmosphere with scheduling options that fit busy schedules. Southwest Family Dental provides a wide range of dental services for the entire family.

DR. BRENDAN MCGRAW

Brendan McGraw, DMD grew up in New Lenox and attended St. Jude School and Lincoln-Way Central High School. He is the seventh of eight children and enjoys spending time with his big family. Dr. McGraw and his family live locally in the area.

Dr. McGraw received his undergraduate degree in Biology from Augustana College and his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine, where he served as president of his dental school class.

In his free time, Dr. McGraw volunteers at the Will-Grundy Medical Clinic in Joliet.

DR. David stoneburner

David Stoneburner, DMD grew up in Northeastern Minnesota. He attended The College of St. Scholastica in Duluth, MN and studied both Biology and Chemistry. He continued his education at Midwestern University, where he obtained his Doctor of Dental Medicine.

He currently resides in the Western suburbs of Chicago. Outside of practicing dentistry, Dr. Stoneburner enjoys golf, any outdoor activity, and hunting and fishing in Minnesota and Canada.
