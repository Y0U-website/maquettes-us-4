Dental Implants

  • Dental Implants - Perry Dental and TMJ Center Restore Your Smile with Strength, Function Confidence at Perry Dental & TMJ Center Our Dental Implant Services A dental implant is a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a custom-made crown is attached, creating a secure and natural-looking replacement for missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants fuse with the bone, offering unmatched stability and longevity. Benefits of Dental Implants Permanent & Durable – Implants can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Natural Look & Feel – Designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Restores Full Function – Eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
  • Prevents Bone Loss – Stimulates the jawbone to maintain facial structure.
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth – Unlike bridges, implants do not require support from neighboring teeth.

Our Dental Implant Services

Consultation & Evaluation

We begin with a thorough examination, including digital imaging, to determine if dental implants are the right option for you.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it naturally integrates over a few months.

Healing & Restoration

Once healed, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing your restored smile.
