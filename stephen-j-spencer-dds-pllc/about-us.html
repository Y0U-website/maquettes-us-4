Meet the Team

Kelly

Dental Hygienist Kelly is a registered dental hygienist who started working at the practice in 2014. She is a Montana native who grew up in Valier and now lives in Choteau. Kelly enjoys building trusting relationships with our patients and providing exceptional care. In her free time, Kelly enjoys the great outdoors, cooking, and ranching with her husband and two kiddos.

Sabrina

Dental Hygienist Sabrina joined our office in 2020. She grew up in the “middle of nowhere” in Glasgow, Montana. After graduating from Great Falls College MSU, Sabrina studied dental hygiene. Always striving to provide a positive experience to all, she enjoys creating long-lasting relationships with patients. Sabrina is part of an Air Force family. She cherishes all the time she can get making memories and experiencing new adventures with her husband and three children.

Theresa

Dental Assistant Theresa has worked in the dental field in Great Falls for more than 30 years and joined Dr. Spencer’s office in 2017. Theresa loves how far the dental field has come and enjoys helping patients to get the best care possible. On weekends, Theresa likes jet boating and spending time with her family. She is married with two daughters. She also loves music and concerts—so much so that she has a part-time job at a local concert venue in Great Falls!

Bev

Financial Coordinator Bev brings 20 years of front desk dental office experience to our practice. She began working with us in 2020. Outside of work, Bev loves to entertain and spend time with her friends and family. She enjoys the outdoors and relaxing in her backyard. In the summer, you can find her camping, biking, walking, or enjoying evening cruises with her husband. She’s also a passionate home decorator, gardening enthusiast, and NFL fan.

Lauren

Dental Assistant/Treatment Coordinator Lauren grew up in the Cascade area and has always enjoyed caring for others. Her compassion is what fueled her career choice as a dental assistant and treatment coordinator. Lauren feels like she has found her niche, and she enjoys advocating for patients and making them as comfortable as possible. Outside of work, Lauren loves listening to music, hanging out with her son Harley, camping, and other outdoor activities.

Breanna

Bre loves meeting new patients and helping to create positive dental experiences. In her spare time, Bre loves camping, hiking, and spending time outdoors.

Maddie
