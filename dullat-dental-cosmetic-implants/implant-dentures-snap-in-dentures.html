Who Is a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You may be a great candidate if you: Currently wear dentures but want more stability

  • Have multiple missing teeth or no teeth at all
  • Have enough healthy bone to support implants (or are open to bone grafting if needed)
  • Want a secure, comfortable, and natural-looking solution for tooth loss

The Implant Denture Process

We take pride in delivering precise, patient-centered care for lasting results. If you’re seeking a comfortable and secure tooth replacement, our snap-in dentures in San Jose offer the perfect blend of aesthetics, function, and convenience.

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging: We evaluate your gums, bone, and bite using advanced imaging to design a custom plan.
  • Implant Placement: Typically 2–6 implants are placed in the jaw for each arch.
  • Healing & Integration: Over the next 3–6 months, the implants fuse with the bone for maximum stability.
  • Attachment of Denture: Your custom denture is fitted with special connectors that “snap” securely onto the implants.

Benefits of Implant Dentures

Implant dentures provide greater comfort, confidence, and stability for everyday life.

  • Secure fit with no slipping
  • Better chewing and speaking ability
  • Helps preserve jawbone health
  • More comfortable than traditional dentures
  • Natural-looking appearance
  • Easy to clean and maintain

How Many Implants Will I Need?

Most lower implant dentures require 2–4 implants, while upper dentures typically need 4 or more for optimal support. Your treatment plan will be customized to your needs.

Does It Hurt?

Implant placement is usually very comfortable with local anesthesia and sedation options. Most patients experience only mild soreness afterward.

Caring for Implant Dentures

Following a simple cleaning routine and regular dental visits can extend the life of your restoration. Experience reliable and lasting results with implant dentures in San Jose, tailored to your needs by our skilled implant team.

  • Remove your dentures daily for cleaning
  • Clean the attachments on your implants
  • Brush and floss any remaining natural teeth
  • Visit us regularly for exams and professional cleanings
