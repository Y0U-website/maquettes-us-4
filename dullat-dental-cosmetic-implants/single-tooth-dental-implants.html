How Long Does the Process Take?

Most single tooth implants take about 3–6 months from placement to the final crown, depending on healing and whether procedures like bone grafting are needed. Some patients may qualify for same-day implants.

Does Getting an Implant Hurt?

The procedure is typically very comfortable with local anesthesia and sedation options. Mild soreness afterward is normal and usually manageable with over-the-counter medication.

Why Single Tooth Implants Are Worth the Investment?

Dental implants are a long-lasting solution that can prevent bone loss, protect surrounding teeth, and restore natural function and appearance.

What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant replaces a missing tooth with a titanium post and a custom dental crown designed to look and function like a natural tooth.
