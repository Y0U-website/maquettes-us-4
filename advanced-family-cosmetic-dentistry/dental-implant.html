Dental Implants in Middletown, CT

Dental implants are a reliable investment in your oral health and quality of life.

  • Improved Function: Eat, speak, and smile with confidence
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Implants stimulate the jawbone, helping to prevent bone deterioration that occurs after tooth loss
  • Long-Lasting: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime research published in the Journal of Clinical Periodontology has documented survival rates above 95 % at 10 years
  • No Dietary Restrictions: Enjoy all your favorite foods without worry

Are Dental Implants Safe?

Yes, dental implants are considered a safe and reliable option for replacing missing teeth, with documented success rates above 95 % when placed by a trained professional. Since implants fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, they provide a stable and secure solution. While risks like infection or implant failure are rare, they can be minimized through proper care and by working with a qualified dental provider .

Why choose dental implants over other options?

Dental implants offer several advantages over dentures or bridges . They look and feel like natural teeth, provide unmatched stability, and preserve jawbone health by preventing bone loss. Unlike other options, implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support, reducing wear on neighboring teeth. They are a permanent solution that does not require adhesives or adjustments.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance. The implant itself, made from durable titanium, integrates with your jawbone to create a long-lasting foundation. The crown (the visible part) typically lasts 10 to 15 years before needing replacement due to natural wear. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene play a crucial role in extending their longevity.
