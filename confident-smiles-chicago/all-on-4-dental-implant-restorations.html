What is a Full Mouth Dental Implant Restoration?

A full mouth dental implant restoration involves replacing all of the teeth in one or both arches of your mouth with dental implants. Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into your jawbone and act as a replacement for the root of your tooth. A dental crown is then attached to the implant, creating a natural-looking and functional replacement tooth.

What are the Benefits of Full Mouth Dental Implant Restorations?

There are numerous benefits to choosing full mouth dental implant restorations over other tooth replacement options, such as dentures or bridges. Dental implants are designed to look and function like natural teeth, providing you with a more natural-looking smile. They also allow you to eat and speak normally, without the discomfort or embarrassment that can come with other tooth replacement options.

How Much Does a Full Mouth Dental Implant Restoration Cost?

The cost of a full mouth dental implant restoration varies depending on a number of factors, such as the number of implants needed, the type of implants used, and the complexity of the procedure.
