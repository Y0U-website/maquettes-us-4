About Us

We are a state of the art Oregon City dental facility that focuses on care for the entire family. Our doctors provide excellent dental care with state of the art technology and a gentle touch.

Our Approach

Oregon City Family Dentistry, 602 Monroe Street, has been dedicated to quality dentistry for decades! Many of our staff members have been with the practice for more than 10 years, and we work diligently to implement the latest technology and safest techniques. We use all digital technology and offer the latest in dental procedures.

We’re a guilt-free office that works to ensure all patients are healthy and comfortable during their visits. For our more fearful patients, we offer sedation dentistry (nitrous oxide and oral conscious sedation).

Our office is warm and inviting, and we welcome patients of all ages, whether you’re 2 or 102. Our doctors have a passion for treating children, and our office even has a dedicated section called the kid’s corner.

Affordable care to families and loved ones

It’s our mission to provide affordable care to families and their loved ones. We accept insurance and all major credit cards, and we carry CareCredit, which allows patients to finance treatments.

When you become a patient at our office, you’ll be treated like a member of the family.
