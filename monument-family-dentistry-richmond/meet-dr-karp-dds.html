Meet Dr. Rebecca Karp

Dr. Rebecca Karp DDS

I believe great dentistry starts with listening. My goal is to make every patient feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their care.

Education and Continuing Education

– Doctorate of Dental Surgery – Virginia Commonwealth University

– Bachelor of Science in Biology and German Literature – University of Virginia

Professional Affiliations

Dr. Karp is an active member of the American Dental Association, Virginia Dental Association, and Richmond Association of Women Dentists. Staying current in clinical practices is important to Dr. Karp , and she is a member of various study clubs that focus on continuing education and collaboration within the dental community. In addition, Dr. Karp enjoys volunteering with Mission of Mercy projects and has travelled state-wide to provide clinical care to patients of all ages.

Outside the Office

Outside of the office, Dr. Karp enjoys a competitive family tennis or ping–pong match (although her sisters won’t keep score anymore to keep it fun ). She can be found running on the roads and trails around Richmond, often with her dog, “Lady”. She enjoys baking and making sweet treats, and loves sharing them with friends and family. Dr. Karp is delighted to now call Richmond home and is proud to have joined Monument Family Dentistry in 2023.
