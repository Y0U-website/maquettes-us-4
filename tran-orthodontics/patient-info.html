Patient Info

Get Started First Visit Your smile transformation starts with a complimentary initial consultation that lasts about an hour. During this time, you will have the opportunity to meet Dr. Tran and our friendly team, who will walk you through a comprehensive examination, discuss treatment options tailored to you, and address any questions or concerns you may have. Our priority is to ensure you feel comfortable and informed, ready to embark on your smile journey with confidence.

Our commitment to your comfort extends to our coffee bar, and waiting room massage chairs, where you can relax and refresh while taking in the serene atmosphere. We value your trust in us, and your information is secure as we work together to craft a stunning and confident smile that reflects your unique personality.

Finance and Insurance

We accept various payment methods, including cash, credit cards, and personal checks. For your convenience, we also offer flexible financing plans that can be tailored to fit your budget. Our financial coordinators are here to help you explore all available options and find the one that best suits your needs. You can have peace of mind knowing that our fees are all-inclusive, enabling you to focus solely on your smile transformation journey.

We understand the importance of insurance coverage, and we gladly work with most major insurance providers. Our experienced team will assist you in maximizing your benefits and handling the insurance paperwork, ensuring a smooth and hassle-free process.

Patient Forms

Save time and streamline your first visit by completing our secure online patient forms. Submit your health history and new patient information from the comfort of your home, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience when you arrive at our office.
