Treatments

Treatments Braces Ceramic braces are a more discreet alternative to traditional metal braces. Made from translucent or tooth-colored materials, ceramic braces blend in with your teeth, making them less noticeable.

This option is particularly popular among teens & adult patients who desire a subtler orthodontic solution while achieving outstanding results. At Tran Orthodontics, we offer ceramic braces to align your smile with precision and finesse.

Clear Aligners

Explore our clear aligner options for a discreet way to straighten your smile.

Step into the future of orthodontics with Invisalign, a revolutionary system that redefines how you achieve a straighter smile. Utilizing a series of clear, removable aligners, Invisalign aligners gently guide your teeth into their desired position, offering an innovative alternative to traditional braces.

The best part? These aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to showcase your smile even during treatment confidently. Experience the comfort, flexibility, and effectiveness of Invisalign, and embrace the journey towards your dream smile.

Accelerate your smile journey with the efficiency and convenience of our in-office aligners. Our advanced technology and expertise allow us to craft custom aligners right here in our office, reducing the waiting time for your smile transformation.

By choosing in-office aligners, you can take advantage of a streamlined process that offers precision, speed, and tailored results. Say goodbye to the anticipation and begin your journey to a confident smile sooner with our in-office aligners.

Technology

At Tran Orthodontics, we embrace cutting-edge technology to provide you with the most advanced and efficient orthodontic experience.

Our state-of-the-art iTero scanner eliminates the need for messy impressions by capturing a precise 3D digital image of your teeth. This advanced technology allows us to create accurate treatment plans and visualize the outcome of your orthodontic journey. With the iTero scanner, you can enjoy a comfortable and streamlined process, ensuring a seamless transition to your dream smile.

Utilizing 3D printing technology, we bring innovation to orthodontic treatments. Our 3D printer enables us to create custom aligners, retainers, and appliances with exceptional precision and efficiency. This means shorter turnaround times and a more tailored approach to addressing your orthodontic needs, enhancing the overall quality of your treatment.

Our soft tissue laser enhances the precision of various orthodontic procedures, ensuring minimal discomfort and faster healing. The laser technology allows for gentle gum reshaping, reducing the need for traditional surgical techniques. This advanced tool enables us to provide a more comfortable and efficient orthodontic experience while achieving remarkable results.
