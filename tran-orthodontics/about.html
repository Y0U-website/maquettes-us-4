About Us Dr. Paul Tran Meet Dr. Tran, your smile specialist and passionate orthodontist.

With a steadfast commitment to excellence, Dr. Paul Tran approaches orthodontics with a personalized touch. He believes in providing conservative and patient-centric treatments, tailoring each plan to meet the unique needs of every individual. Dr. Tran’s warm and caring approach fosters a welcoming atmosphere, ensuring patients feel at ease throughout their orthodontic journey.

As a specialist in the field, he established his practice in Louisville in 1995, expanding to a second location in Prospect, Kentucky. Dr. Tran continuously enriches his expertise through numerous continuing education courses, remaining well-versed in the latest orthodontic techniques and technologies.

He earned a bachelor’s degree from the University of Houston before pursuing his passion for dentistry, achieving a doctorate in dental surgery from Baylor University. Driven by his desire to specialize in orthodontics, he furthered his studies at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry, obtaining a certificate and master’s in orthodontics.

Committed to staying at the cutting edge, he continuously pursues advanced training and is an alumnus of the prestigious L. D. Pankey Institute and the McLaughlin 2-year postgraduate orthodontic course.

Dr. Tran’s commitment to the orthodontic community is evident through his active involvement in various local and national orthodontic associations. Holding esteemed leadership roles, he served as the president of the Kentucky Association of Orthodontists and represented Kentucky as a Delegate to the Southern Association of Orthodontics.

Making a Difference Together.

At Tran Orthodontics, we believe that being an active and positive force in the community is essential. We are dedicated to making a difference by engaging in various community involvement initiatives. From sponsoring activities at local schools like North Oldham Middle School and Harmony Elementary School to supporting Bridgepointe Swim and Tennis Club and the Great BalloonFest, we take pride in contributing to the vibrancy and well-being of our neighborhoods.

Our commitment to community involvement extends beyond sponsorship. We are honored to be a part of the Special Olympics, where we champion the spirit of determination and inclusivity. Through these efforts, we aim to create a warm, balanced, and friendly environment that fosters positive relationships with our patients and their families. Together, we can bring smiles not only within our practice but also throughout our wonderful community.
