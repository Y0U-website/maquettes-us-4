How the Invisalign Process Works

Your journey with Invisalign therapy in Scottsdale, AZ, follows a clear, step-by-step plan designed around your goals.

  • Consultation and evaluation: dentists reviews your smile goals, oral health, and suitability for aligners.
  • 3D scans and planning: Digital scanning replaces messy impressions and creates a precise model of your teeth.
  • Preview of results: A virtual simulation shows how your teeth can move across treatment stages.
  • Custom aligner series: You receive a sequence of trays to wear in order, usually changing every 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Attachments if needed: Small tooth-colored supports may be placed to help guide specific movements.
  • Progress check-ins: Periodic visits confirm tracking and make any needed refinements.
  • Retention: After active treatment, retainers help keep your new alignment stable.
