What To Expect From The Implant Restoration Process

The process for implant restorations in Scottsdale, AZ, is tailored to your smile and follows a clear, patient-friendly sequence. Expect a collaborative approach that prioritizes precision and comfort.

  • Comprehensive evaluation: dentists reviews your goals, assesses your bite and gums, and evaluates implant placement to guide the design of your restoration.
  • Digital design and impressions: High-accuracy scans and impressions help create a customized crown, bridge, or denture that fits your implant and bite.
  • Custom abutment selection: The abutment connects the implant to the final restoration and is chosen for ideal gum contour and strength.
  • Shade matching and esthetics: The color, shape, and texture of your restoration are refined to blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth.
  • Precise placement: The restoration is carefully fitted, and bite adjustments are made to ensure even contact and comfortable function.
  • Home care guidance: You receive clear instructions for brushing, flossing, and maintenance to protect the restoration and surrounding tissues.
