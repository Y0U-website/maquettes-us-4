Orthodontic Retainers

  • Hawley removable retainers that use an acrylic base with a thin wire across the front teeth.
  • Fixed or bonded retainers that are a small wire attached to the back of the front teeth.

How Retainers Help Your Smile

  • Maintain tooth alignment by keeping teeth stable while bone and ligaments remodel.
  • Reduce the risk of gaps reopening or crowding returning after braces.
  • Support a comfortable bite as teeth “settle” into ideal contact.
  • Provide a simple, non-invasive way to protect orthodontic results for years.
  • Offer flexible options, including fixed vs. removable retainers, to fit daily routines.

The Retainer Process

The process is straightforward for patients in Scottsdale, Arizona:

  • Assessment and planning to choose the best design for your teeth, bite, and lifestyle.
  • Impressions or digital scans to capture an accurate model of your smile.
  • Fabrication by a lab to create a precise fit for effective retention.
  • Fitting and instructions to review wear time, cleaning, and storage.
  • Follow-up checks to confirm comfort, fit, and long-term stability.

Your Wear Schedule and Care

Wearing your retainer as directed is essential. Many patients start with full-time wear (except for meals and brushing) for several weeks to a few months, then transition to nightly wear. Long-term nighttime use is often recommended to keep teeth straight for years after braces or aligners.

  • Initial pressure is common and usually fades within a few days.
  • Clear speech returns quickly; reading aloud can help speed adaptation.
  • Keep a protective case handy to prevent loss or damage when the retainer is out.
  • Rinse after removal and brush gently with a soft brush and mild soap.
  • Avoid hot water, which can warp clear retainers.
  • Do not soak in alcohol-based mouthwash that may damage plastic.
  • For fixed retainers, use floss threaders or interdental brushes to clean under the wire.
  • Bring your retainer to checkups so fit can be reviewed and adjusted if needed.

Before and After Orthodontic Treatment

Retainers work the same way whether your teeth were aligned with braces or clear aligners. The timing and style may vary, but the goal is identical: keep teeth in their corrected positions as tissues stabilize. If a retainer feels tight after skipping wear, it is a sign that teeth are moving. Resuming directed wear promptly can help. If tightness does not improve, schedule an evaluation.
