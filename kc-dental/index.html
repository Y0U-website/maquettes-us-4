About Us KC Dental was founded in July 2006 by Dr. Kyleen Chen. Since then we have grown and now have an additional three General Dentists at our office as well.

The practice

Dr. Chen graduated with a Bachelor’s degree from The University of Texas at Austin. She earned her D.D.S. from Baylor College of Dentistry in 1998. Dr. Chen moved to California from 2000-2005, where she worked as an associate dentist for family and children’s dentistry. During this time, Dr. Chen volunteered in underprivileged communities promoting dental hygiene education. She returned to Austin…

Dr. Chen is currently a member of American Orthodontic Society, American Dental Association, and Texas Dental Association.
