Implant Treatment Options

Whether you re missing one tooth or several, dental implants restore function and aesthetics naturally. Dr. Griffin offers single tooth implants, implant-supported bridges, and implant dentures. Each treatment is customized using advanced 3D imaging and surgical guides for precision.

Why Choose Dental Implants

Dental implants can provide a long-term tooth-replacement option designed to support function and a natural appearance. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants preserve jawbone health and don t affect adjacent teeth. Dr. Griffin s Advanced Implant Training is intended to support expert care.

Care moves forward one clear step at a time.

What to expect from treatment

Your implant journey begins with comprehensive CBCT imaging and consultation. Dr. Griffin evaluates your bone density and develops a personalized treatment plan. The titanium implant is precisely placed using surgical guides, ensuring optimal positioning for long-term success and aesthetics.

Follow through with confidence

After healing, we attach your custom crown, bridge, or denture to the implant. The result is a restoration that functions and feels completely natural. Dr. Griffin provides detailed aftercare instructions and ongoing support to help support lasting success.
