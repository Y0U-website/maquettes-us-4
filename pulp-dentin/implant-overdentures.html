The Implant Overdenture Process

Every step of the process is designed for comfort and long-term success.

  • Consultation: Dr. Renieris evaluates your oral health and reviews your restorative options.
  • Implant Placement: A surgical specialist places 2–6 implants in your jawbone for support.
  • Healing & Integration: The implants fuse naturally with your bone over a few months.
  • Denture Design: Once healed, we design a custom overdenture to attach securely to your implants.
  • Attachment & Fitting: Your new smile is fitted, adjusted, and ready to go — stable, functional, and beautiful.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many implants are needed for overdentures?

Most patients need 2–6 implants per arch for optimal stability, depending on bone quality and treatment goals.

Are implant overdentures removable?

Yes — they securely snap into place but can be removed for easy cleaning and maintenance.

How long do implant overdentures last?

With proper care, implants can last a lifetime. The overdenture component typically lasts 7–10 years before needing replacement.

Will I need bone grafting before getting implants?

If you’ve experienced bone loss, bone grafting may be recommended to ensure a strong foundation.

Can I convert my current dentures into implant overdentures?

In many cases, yes. We’ll evaluate your existing dentures and discuss conversion options during your consultation.
