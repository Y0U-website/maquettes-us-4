The Dental Implant Process

We also offer bone grafting for patients who need additional support before implant placement.

  • Consultation & Planning: Dr. Renieris evaluates your oral health, bone structure, and smile goals.
  • Implant Placement: A specialist surgically places the implant into your jawbone.
  • Healing & Integration: Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone for lasting stability.
  • Abutment & Restoration: Once healed, we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture designed for a perfect fit.
  • Follow-Up Care: We monitor your implant and provide maintenance guidance for long-term success.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are an investment in both your smile and your overall well-being — offering strength, stability, and confidence for years to come.

  • Permanent and secure — no shifting or slipping
  • Restores natural chewing and speaking ability
  • Prevents jawbone loss and preserves facial structure
  • Long-lasting and easy to maintain
  • Looks and feels like a natural tooth

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, implants can last decades — often a lifetime.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. The procedure is performed under anesthesia, and recovery is typically mild.

How long does the implant process take?

It can take several months from start to finish, allowing time for the implant to fuse securely with your jawbone.

Can implants replace dentures?

Yes. Many patients upgrade to implant overdentures for improved comfort and stability.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Brush and floss daily, attend regular cleanings and exams , and avoid smoking to ensure longevity.
