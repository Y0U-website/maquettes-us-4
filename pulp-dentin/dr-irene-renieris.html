A Thoughtful Approach to Modern Dentistry

Dr. Renieris blends the precision of advanced dental technology with the warmth of compassionate, individualized care. She takes time to listen, explain, and ensure that each visit feels comfortable and empowering. Her goal is to help every patient — regardless of their background, age, or experience — feel confident in their oral health and proud of their smile.

Education & Training

Through years of education and hands-on experience, Dr. Renieris developed a patient-first approach grounded in both clinical excellence and empathy. Her hospital residency strengthened her expertise in treating complex cases and working closely with patients in need of compassionate, comprehensive care.
