Looking for a Dental Home? Schedule a visit with our team today and experience specialized, kid-friendly care!

A Positive Start for Lifelong Oral Health From the moment you walk in, we aim to make your child feel relaxed, supported, and confident. Our sensory-friendly waiting room, thoughtful team, and personalized care approach help ensure even the most hesitant child feels at ease during their first appointment.

Complete all necessary forms and provide insurance prior to arriving for a smooth and fast check-in

Let us know if your child has specific sensory or behavioral needs—we’re here to help

Arrive early to explore the kid’s game area or request your child’s favorite movie for their visit

Parents are always welcome to accompany their children during their appointment.

Making a Difference By Keeping Everyone Comfortable Every element of our new Madisonville office was created to make pediatric dental care feel easier, calmer, and more enjoyable for families. From the playroom to the parent coffee bar, we’re here to make everyone feel right at home.

Sensory-Friendly Spaces Quiet rooms and ceiling TVs help children with anxiety or special needs stay relaxed and focused.

Coffee Bar & Beverage Station While your child gets their care, enjoy a cozy space just for parents, complete with coffee, refreshments, and calm.

Kid-Approved Entertainment Touchscreen game stations, streaming services, and ceiling TVs keep kids engaged and happy before and during treatment.

Modern Technology That’s Built for Pediatric Dentistry We’ve designed every part of our experience to help kids feel comfortable, build trust with parents (you&#x27;re always welcome to accompany your child throughout the visit!), and deliver the highest level of pediatric dental care under one roof.

Nomad Portable X-Ray Our handheld imaging device allows for quick, comfortable, and low-radiation X-rays—perfect for younger patients.

Digital Pano & Bite Wing Intraoral X-ray These diagnostic tools help us evaluate your child’s full smile and deliver the most precise, personalized care possible.

SOAN Numbing Technology We can target just the tooth needing care instead of numbing an entire quadrant, making recovery easier for kids.
