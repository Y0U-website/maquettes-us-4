The Fixed Implants Procedure

The fixed implant process involves multiple steps to ensure long-term success:

Initial Consultation

Dr. Jonathan Saliby evaluates your oral health and discusses your goals.

Bone Assessment

If needed, Bone Grafting may be performed to strengthen the jawbone.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone.

Healing Period

The implant integrates with the bone over time.

Abutment Placement

A connector is attached to support the final restoration.

Final Restoration

A custom crown or bridge completes your Dental Implants treatment.
