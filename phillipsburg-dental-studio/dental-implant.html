The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process is carefully planned and completed in multiple steps to ensure long-term success:

Initial Consultation

Dr. Jonathan Saliby evaluates your oral health and determines if you are a good candidate for dental implants.

Treatment Planning

A customized plan is created based on your needs, including imaging and digital scans.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone.

Healing & Osseointegration

The implant fuses with the bone over a few months, creating a strong foundation.

Abutment Placement

A connector is placed on top of the implant.

Final Restoration

A custom crown is attached, completing your new smile.

The Benefits of a Dental Implant

Dental implants offer several advantages compared to traditional tooth replacement options:

Natural Appearance and Feel

Dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They blend seamlessly with your smile, giving you confidence in your appearance.

Improved Chewing and Speech

Unlike dentures, implants are securely fixed in place, allowing you to eat your favorite foods comfortably and speak clearly without worry.

Prevention of Bone Loss

Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone deterioration that usually occurs after tooth loss. This helps maintain your facial structure.

Long-Lasting and Durable Solution

With proper care, dental implants can last for many years, making them a reliable and cost-effective option in the long run.

No Impact on Surrounding Teeth

Unlike dental bridges, implants do not require support from adjacent teeth, preserving the health of your natural teeth.

Enhanced Confidence and Smile Aesthetics

A complete and healthy smile improves your self-esteem and overall quality of life, helping you feel more confident in social and professional settings.
