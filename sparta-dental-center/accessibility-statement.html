Accessibility Statement - Sparta Dental Center

Accessibility Policy

Our Commitment to Accessibility At Sparta Dental Center, we are committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA compliance wherever feasible.

Our goal is to provide a seamless and inclusive experience for all visitors—whether you’re using assistive technology such as a screen reader, magnifier, voice recognition software, or keyboard navigation.

Ongoing Efforts

  • Regularly review and test our website against WCAG 2.2 criteria.
  • Collaborate with web developers to remediate issues and ensure continued compliance.
  • Train our staff to recognize accessibility barriers and respond promptly when reported.
  • Use third-party tools and automated scans to identify potential accessibility concerns.

ADA Widget and Accessibility Tools

  • Adjust text size, color contrast, and spacing.
  • Enable keyboard navigation and focus indicators.
  • Pause animations and enhance content readability.
