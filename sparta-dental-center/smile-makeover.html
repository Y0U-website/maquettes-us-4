How Do I Choose? Are there several things you do not like about your teeth and you do not know where to begin? Do you avoid smiling in pictures? Are your teeth broken, discolored, or missing? Being self-conscious about your smile can haunt you every day, and it is a needless distraction from enjoying your daily life. Your Cosmetic Dentists in Sparta

As your smile makeover dentists, our doctors know the importance of having a smile you can be proud of. A smile makeover is designed to restore all of the teeth in your smile line with beautiful porcelain restorations. Dr. Morgan or Dr. Steien will discuss what you want your new smile to look like. You can choose the size, shape, and shade of your teeth for a completely custom-made smile.

Our doctors work closely with a Wisconsin lab to ensure your new smile is exactly what you deserve: beautiful and confident. With a smile makeover you can get a perfect smile and possibly even smooth out some fine lines around your lip area to look and feel great in a fraction of the time!

Why should you consider a smile makeover?

We would love to be your smile makeover dentist and give you the smile of your dreams. Call our office today to schedule your consultation. We look forward to meeting you.

  • The end result is a custom-made smile.
  • You will have a beautifully uniform smile line.
  • In some cases, fine lines and wrinkles around your lips may be smoothed.
  • Restorations are stain-resistant.
  • In many cases, you will get a new smile that is stronger than your natural teeth.
  • With proper home care, your smile makeover can last a lifetime!
