Single and Multiple Tooth Implants

Missing one tooth or several? Individual implants with custom crowns replace each missing tooth without affecting healthy neighboring teeth. Multiple implants can support bridges to replace several teeth in a row.

All-on-4 and All-on-6 Full Mouth Solutions

Replace an entire arch of teeth with just four or six strategically placed implants. These full-mouth solutions provide immediate improvement in both function and appearance, often allowing you to leave with temporary teeth the same day.

Implant-Retained Bridges, Partials, and Dentures

Traditional dentures rest on your gums and can slip or slide. Implant-supported options snap securely into place, giving you the chewing power and confidence of natural teeth. No more worrying about loose dentures during meals or conversations.

Immediate Load / Same-Day Implants

In the right cases, we can place your implants and attach temporary teeth on the same day. You’ll walk out with a functional smile while your implants heal and integrate with your jawbone.

Bone Grafting and Ridge Augmentation

Strong bone is essential for implant success. If your jawbone needs reinforcement, we offer bone grafting and ridge augmentation to create the solid foundation your implants need to last decades.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults make good implant candidates, but the best results come from patients with adequate bone density, healthy gums, and good overall health.

During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your specific situation and verify insurance benefits so you know exactly what to expect financially.

  • Have realistic expectations about treatment
  • Practice good oral hygiene
  • Don’t smoke (or are willing to quit)
  • Have sufficient bone to support implants
  • Are committed to regular dental visits

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last 20+ years with proper care. Many patients keep their implants for life with good oral hygiene and regular checkups.

Are dental implants painful?

Implant surgery is performed with local anesthesia for patient comfort. Most patients report less discomfort than they expected during the healing process.

How much do dental implants cost in Kennesaw, GA?

Dental implant costs vary based on the number of implants and the type of restoration. We provide detailed estimates and verify insurance benefits during consultation.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Smoking increases the risk of implant failure, but some patients may still qualify with precautions. Quitting smoking dramatically improves success rates.

Do dental implants look natural?

Dental implants are designed to match surrounding teeth in shape and color. Most people won’t be able to tell which teeth are implants.
