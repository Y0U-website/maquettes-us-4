DDS

Dr. Bonanno is a New Jersey board-certified general dentist dedicated to high-quality, compassionate, and comprehensive care for patients of all ages.

He earned a bachelor’s degree in psychology and neuroscience from the University of Connecticut before completing his DDS at Touro College of Dental Medicine in Westchester, New York.

After dental school, he completed a General Practice Residency at JFK Hospital in Edison. Whether you are visiting for a routine checkup or a complete smile makeover, his goal is to make the process comfortable and understandable.

Outside the dental chair, Dr. Bonanno enjoys running and playing soccer on weekends.
