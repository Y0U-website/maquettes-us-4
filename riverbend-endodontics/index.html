The practice

An Endodontist is a specialist in the area of soft tissue inside the tooth.

Endodontics is a branch of dentistry recognized by the American Dental Association involving treatment of the pulp (root canal) and surrounding tissues of the tooth.

Located in Bettendorf, Iowa, Dr. Duane Van Nieuwenhuyzen and his staff offer exceptional endodontic care using the most advanced technology, ensuring excellence in diagnostics and treatment. We understand the importance of from the heart customer service and will always strive to exceed your expectations.

A successful practice doesn’t just happen. It is the result of a strong commitment to excellence in our treatment and in our relationships with patients and other doctors.
