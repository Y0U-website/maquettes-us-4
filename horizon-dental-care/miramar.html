During your first visit at Horizon Dental Care, you can expect the following:

Upon arrival, you will be asked to fill out and sign new patient forms if you haven’t done so already.

You will then be called in to sit in the dental chair when the doctor is ready.

The dentist will ask questions about your current oral health and your goals moving forward.

Your new patient visit will include a comprehensive dental exam, professional cleaning, digital X-rays, and use of an intraoral camera.

You will have the opportunity to ask any questions about procedures, costs, or oral care habits to help you feel confident and informed.

The team will review your dental benefits and discuss a customized treatment plan based on your specific needs.

If you have dental anxiety, the staff will work to make your visit comfortable and stress-free, offering sedation options if needed.

At Horizon Dental Care, your safety is a top priority. We follow strict sterilization and infection control protocols to ensure a clean and safe environment for every patient.

Horizon Dental Care offers a comprehensive range of dental services to meet the diverse needs of patients, including:

General Dentistry: Routine dental exams, cleanings, dental imaging, dental X-rays, and guidance on home care.

Restorative Dentistry: Dentures and partial dentures, inlay/onlay restorations, dental crowns, porcelain crowns, and dental implants.

Cosmetic Dentistry: Dental bonding, porcelain veneers, and teeth whitening to enhance your smile.

Periodontics: Diagnosis, periodontal maintenance, periodontal treatment, and root canal therapy.

Orthodontics: Invisalign treatment for teeth alignment.

Oral Surgery: Simple extractions, wisdom teeth extractions, and oral sedation.
