Our experienced team guides you through every step of the implant process:

Dental Implant Advantages at Horizon Dental Care:

  • Improved Speech & Eating: Enjoy your favorite foods again and speak confidently without worrying about dentures slipping.
  • Enhanced Confidence: Smile brightly with confidence, knowing your teeth look and function naturally.
  • Preserves Facial Structure: Implants prevent bone loss that occurs with missing teeth, maintaining a youthful facial appearance.
  • Consultation: We evaluate your oral health and discuss treatment options. X-rays and a CT scan may be needed.
  • Implant Placement: A skilled surgeon inserts the implant into your jawbone using advanced techniques.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: The implant bonds with the jawbone over several months. Temporary dentures may be used.
  • Abutment Placement: A small connector piece is attached to the implant to support the artificial tooth.
  • Crown or Denture Placement: Our restorative dentist creates and fits your permanent crown or denture for a natural-looking smile.
  • Experienced Team: Our team includes skilled oral surgeons and restorative dentists for seamless implant care.
  • Advanced Technology: We use the latest dental implant technology for precise placement and optimal results.
  • Minimally Invasive Techniques: Whenever possible, we utilize minimally invasive procedures for faster healing and patient comfort.
