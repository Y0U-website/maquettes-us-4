The practice

Dr. Porcello is a native of Allegany, NY and graduated from Allegany-Limestone in 1996. Dr. Porcello then attended St. Bonaventure University from 1996 2000. While at St. Bonaventure, Dr. Porcello majored in Biology and attended the Franics E Kelley Oxford Program where he studied for a summer in Oxford, England. He graduated Cum Laud in 2000.

After St. Bonaventure, Dr. Porcello attended dental school at SUNY Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, which is one of the top dental schools in the country, graduating in 2004.

After dental school Dr. Porcello worked at an Indian Health Service site in Salamanca, NY, and employed in private practice in Allegany. During this time Dr. Porcello was exposed to an exceptionally wide range of dental procedures which enables him to provide an extensive array of general dentistry.
