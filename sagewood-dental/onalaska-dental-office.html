About Us

What Sets Our Onalaska Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our practice, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Ask us anything, and we will happily provide expert advice and guidance.

Sagewood Dental is a general dental practice located in Onalaska, Wisconsin. Our clinic specializes in comprehensive preventative dentistry, restorative services, surgical procedures, and cosmetic dentistry. At Sagewood Dental, we make it our priority to deliver gentle, compassionate dental care to the Onalaska area. We strive to develop lifelong relationships by combining the latest dental technology with a professional and compassionate staff. The result is a beautiful, healthy smile that will last a lifetime.

Our Mission: Provide comprehensive and gentle dentistry, with an on-going commitment to combining the latest advances in dentistry with compassionate care and a comfortable, stress-free environment. Book an appointment today!
