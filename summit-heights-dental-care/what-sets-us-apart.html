What Sets Us Apart

Summit Heights Dental Care is a practice that delivers personal care, based on a patient’s unique needs. Starting with your first visit, Dr. LaGow will talk with you about your dental goals and smile dreams. Then he’ll work with you to find the treatments that are right for your situation.

We’ve created a warm, safe environment in which you’ll receive a dental experience you’ll never forget. From a welcoming reception area to cable TV in our treatment areas, everything is focused on your comfort. Our staff members are friendly and personable. We enjoy getting to know and building long-term relationships with our patients.

We understand that insurance is a big deciding factor when choosing a dental home. We are a premier provider with Delta Dental and in network with BCBS. We can also bill most other insurances. So many of the smaller private practice offices in our area have been bought out by corporate offices or have decide to go non-par with insurance. We have a special niche of being a small private practice that offers exceptional personalized care while still billing all insurance plans.

Our dental team is dedicated to helping you maintain your health and your beautiful smile for many years to come. We look forward to getting to know you and your family. Please contact our Jackson dental office to learn more about the care we provide and to schedule an appointment.
