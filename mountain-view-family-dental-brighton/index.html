The practice

Our mission is simple: give every patient the best possible experience using the most advanced dental technology available. We’re not just your dentist in Brighton we’re your neighbors who happen to fix teeth really well.

We stay current with the latest dental techniques and technology because your mouth deserves the best care possible. From digital x-rays to same-day crowns , we invest in equipment that makes your visits faster, more comfortable, and more effective.

You’ll find us at 191 Telluride Street, Suite 3, right in the heart of Brighton. If you’re familiar with the area, we’re easily accessible from Highway 85 and close to the Brighton Crossing neighborhood.

Our office sits in a convenient location near other local businesses, making it easy to combine your dental visit with other errands. Whether you’re coming from the Prairie Center shopping area, the Brighton business district, or any of the residential neighborhoods like Bromley Park or Brighton East Farms, we’re just a short drive away.
