What Are Dental Implants?

Missing teeth can change the way you eat, smile, speak, and feel in photos. At Sunnyside Smiles, Dr. Kingra plans implant treatment around your health, bone support, bite, smile line, timeline, and budget.

Dental implants are medical-grade titanium posts placed in the jaw to replace tooth roots. They can support custom crowns, bridges, or dentures so your replacement teeth feel stable and look natural.

Implants are designed to restore more than the missing tooth.

Stable implant restorations help you chew more comfortably and enjoy more foods.

Your restoration is shaped and shaded to blend with your face, lips, and remaining teeth.

Patients often want replacement teeth that feel secure when talking, laughing, and smiling.

Implants help stimulate the jawbone and support natural facial structure over time.

Implant-supported teeth can reduce slipping, movement, and food limitations.

Single implants can replace teeth without reshaping healthy neighboring teeth.

Options for one tooth, several teeth, or a full smile.
