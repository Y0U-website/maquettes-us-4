Invisalign In Oakland, CA

At Downtown Oakland Dental, we focus on more than just straight teeth. We use Invisalign to improve how your bite functions, prevent long-term issues like tooth wear or gum recession, and make everyday things like eating and speaking feel more natural. Your smile will look better—but more importantly, it will work better too.

The benefits of Invisalign Nearly invisible treatment Clear aligners are virtually undetectable—so you can straighten your smile without anyone noticing.

Comfortable and removable Unlike braces, aligners can be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing. Plus, they’re smooth and custom-fitted, so there’s no poking or irritation.

Results designed to last With consistent wear and regular check-ins, Invisalign delivers predictable, lasting results—and we’re here to support you every step of the way.

The Invisalign process

Step 1 Digital scans and consultation We’ll start with a digital scan of your teeth using our iTero scanner—no messy impressions! Then we’ll chat about your goals and confirm if Invisalign is the right fit for your smile. While we can treat many cases in-house, some complex orthodontic needs are best managed by a specialist—and if that’s the case, we’ll connect you with a trusted orthodontist we know and collaborate with.

Step 2

Custom treatment planning

Using Invisalign’s advanced software, we’ll map out every step of your tooth movement. You’ll even get a sneak peek of your future smile before treatment begins.

Step 3 Start wearing your aligners Once your aligners arrive, you’ll start wearing them for 20–22 hours a day, switching to a new set every 1–2 weeks. We’ll check in regularly to track your progress and make adjustments if needed.

Step 4

Maintain your new smile

After treatment, we’ll provide custom retainers to help keep your teeth in place—so you can enjoy your new smile for years to come.
