Our unique approach

Your 90-minute first visit

We believe better care starts with deeper understanding. That’s why we set aside 90 full minutes to truly get to know you, your smile, and what matters most to you.

Step 1 Get comfortable We’ll welcome you into our cozy, loft-style office with your choice of Nespresso, tea, or water. Take a breath, settle in, and know you’re in the right place—we’ve designed every part of your experience to feel easy, warm, and personal.

Step 2

Understand your oral health

Your visit begins with a thorough set of digital X-rays, intraoral photos, and a personalized iTero Wellness Scan. This scan helps us track your bite, recession, and tooth wear over time—so we can prevent small issues from becoming big ones.

Step 3 Comprehensive exam You&#x27;ll receive a full exam and charting with your dentist, who takes the time to understand your health history, concerns, and goals. If appropriate, we’ll also complete a cleaning during this visit.

Step 4

Personalized care planning

Before you leave, you and your dentist will sit down to walk through your results together. We’ll answer your questions, explain any findings, and work with you to build a clear plan that fits your goals, lifestyle, and budget.

The Downtown Oakland Dental Difference Comprehensive new patient visits Every new patient visit is 90 minutes and includes everything you need to get started. You&#x27;ll receive a comprehensive exam, full-mouth charting, X-rays, iTero scans, and a cleaning when appropriate. Most of your visit will be spent with your dentist, and if a hygiene slot is available, your cleaning may be completed by one of our gentle, comforting hygienists. If a deep cleaning is needed, we’ll discuss it together and schedule a follow-up that works for you.
