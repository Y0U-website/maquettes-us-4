Our goal is to offer the best and most up to date treatment for our patients in a friendly, comfortable, and clean environment. We treat patients of all ages and have been a part of this community for over 55 years. Dr. Edmond and Joseph Stout founded the practice in 1962 .While they have since retired, their tradition of excellence continues through with our amazing doctors.

Dr. Jeffery Stout​

Born and raised in Newark, Dr. Joseph Stout’s son Jeffrey joined Newark Dental Associates in 1993. Dr. Jeffrey is a 1981 graduate of St. Mark’s High School and a 1986 graduate of the University of Delaware. He received his doctorate from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 1992. Dr. Jeff completed a one year General Practice Residency at Yale University affiliate, St. Mary’s Hospital in Waterbury, Connecticut in 1993. Dr. Stout is a member of the Delaware State Dental Society and the American Dental Association. Dr. Jeff and his wife Rachelle have two children, Grace and Joseph.

Dr. Madeleine Taylor

Dr. Madeleine Taylor was born and raised in Newark, Delaware. She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Delaware before completing her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry. Wanting to give back to the community she grew up in, Dr. Taylor returned home to complete her General Practice Residency at ChristianaCare Health System.

Dr. Maddy is excited to join Newark Dental Associates, where she looks forward to building lasting relationships with patients and providing individualized, comfortable care in a practice that has been part of the Newark community for decades.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Maddy enjoys spending time outdoors hiking, walking, and relaxing in nature. She is a devoted local sports fan—especially of the Eagles and Phillies—and loves sharing time with friends and family, exploring new cultures, and trying different foods.
