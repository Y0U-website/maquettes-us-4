Your Trusted Partner in Dental Care

Our entire professional team loves what they do. We are committed to providing comfortable, compassionate, and consistent care.

We do our best to provide quality dentistry in an atmosphere built on trust and comfort. Our mission is to motivate patients to prioritize their dental health and be proud of their smile!

Why Choose Us

We deeply care for our patients and patient comfort is always a top priority for us. From the moment you enter the building, you will be greeted and treated with respect and care from our team at the front desk to the exam room. We understand how stressful and anxious it can be to visit the dentist. Our team is trained to alleviate that fear and make your experience as stress free as possible during your treatments. We excel in personalized gentle, comprehensive dental care that focuses on minimizing anxiety.

At Newark Dental we understand in today’s world, time is valuable and that is why we have quick turnaround time on calls and appointment requests. Our dedicated team is always available and happy to answer questions you may have from treatment to insurance. Our job is to make this as easy as possible. Life is busy, so let our professionals help you with your dental care.

We use the latest and most advanced dental technology available to ensure that our patients receive the best possible care. From digital X-rays and intraoral scanners and cameras to laser dentistry we strive to stay up-to-date on the newest technology so that we can provide our patients with the best possible experience.

Our Team of Gentle and Caring Professionals

Our team consists of dedicated individuals who are kind, friendly, and caring. Our staff is here to ensure a pleasant experience during your visit to our office.
