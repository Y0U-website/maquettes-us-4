Mini Dental Implants

Our approach is all about matching the right person with the right procedure. Many find the minimally invasive nature of mini dental implants advantageous for their specific conditions.

  • Individuals with Missing Teeth : Whether you have one or multiple missing teeth, mini dental implants provide an effective replacement.
  • Those with Thin Bone Tissue : If you’ve been informed that you have thin bone, mini dental implants are often recommended due to their less invasive requirement.
  • Patients Preferring Minimal Invasiveness : For people seeking a dental solution with minimal discomfort, this is an attractive choice.
  • Those with Limited Bone Structure or Smaller Mouths : Mini implants are a fitting choice for smaller jaw structures that might not accommodate traditional implants.
  • Individuals Seeking a Faster Recovery : If extensive bone grafting and lengthy recovery times aren’t appealing, this alternative can offer a simple and fast solution.

What Are Options for Mini Dental Implants?

Mini Dental Implant Options at Southern Smiles

At Southern Smiles of Sylacauga, we offer a range of solutions for Mini Dental Implants in Sylacauga to meet your specific needs. Let’s explore the options:

Single Implant

A single mini dental implant involves placing a small titanium screw into the jawbone to substitute a missing tooth. This approach provides a stable and functional replacement, allowing you to chew and speak naturally. One patient said, I couldn’t tell the difference between my natural teeth and the implant, it felt so genuine.

Implant-Supported Denture

For those missing multiple teeth, an implant-supported denture offers exceptional stability and comfort. This removable prosthesis attaches securely to dental implants, ensuring you won’t encounter the slippage that often comes with traditional dentures. Patients appreciate the significant improvement in their quality of life; another patient noted that these dentures felt like having a full set of my own teeth again.

Roundhouse or Implant-Supported Bridge

A roundhouse or implant-supported bridge is perfect for replacing several adjacent missing teeth. Not only does it enhance chewing ability and aesthetics, but it also prevents bone loss, ensuring that your facial structure remains intact. It’s a long-term solution that provides both durability and a natural look.

Choosing an option that fits your dental needs can significantly improve your daily life. Because smiling with ease matters, we’re here to help guide you to the best choice.
