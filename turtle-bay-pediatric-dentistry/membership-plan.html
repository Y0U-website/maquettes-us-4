Choose the Plan for Your Child

Infant Plan

Perfect for little ones just starting dental visits.

Includes

  • 2 comprehensive exams
  • 2 age-appropriate cleanings
  • All necessary X-rays related to preventive visits
  • 2 fluoride treatments
  • 1 emergency exam per year
  • Cavity risk assessment and oral hygiene counseling
  • Growth and development monitoring

Child Plan

Our most popular plan for growing smiles.

Includes

  • 2 routine exams
  • 2 professional cleanings
  • All necessary X-rays related to preventive visits
  • 2 fluoride treatments
  • 1 emergency exam per year
  • Preventive sealant evaluation
  • Cavity risk and gingival health assessment
  • Oral hygiene and dietary counseling

Teen Plan

Designed for teens transitioning between pediatric and general dental care.

Includes

  • 2 exams
  • 2 cleanings (teen prophy)
  • All necessary X-rays related to preventive visits
  • 2 fluoride treatments
  • 1 emergency exam per year
  • Monitoring of eruption, spacing, and orthodontic needs
  • Oral hygiene coaching tailored to teens
