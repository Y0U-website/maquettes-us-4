Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance for orthodontic treatment?

Yes. We accept Medi-Cal, including Denti-Cal and other state-supported plans, along with most major PPO insurance plans. Our front office team is happy to verify your benefits before treatment begins.

What should I do if I have an orthodontic emergency?

Call our office right away. We prioritize emergency evaluations and will get you in as quickly as possible to address discomfort or appliance issues.

At what age should my child be evaluated for interceptive orthodontics?

Early evaluation is best, often around age 7, when a mix of baby and permanent teeth makes it easier to identify developing issues.

Are orthodontic mouthguards covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. Our team can review your benefits and discuss financing options like CareCredit or Cherry if needed.
