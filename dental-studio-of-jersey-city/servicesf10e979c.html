In-House Technology Advanced tools for precise, comfortable care Oral Scanner Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable impressions. Our digital oral scanner captures a precise, 3D image of your teeth and gums in just minutes, making the process faster and more comfortable for you. This detailed digital model helps our doctors plan treatments with greater accuracy, whether you’re getting a crown, aligners, or another restorative solution. It’s modern technology that makes your visit smoother from start to finish.

Regular x-rays at recare appointments are an essential part of a truly comprehensive exam. They help us catch issues like decay, bone loss, or infection that aren’t visible to the eye alone, so problems can be treated early, before they become bigger concerns.

Periodontal Treatment Treat gum disease and stop it in its tracks Scaling & Root Planing If you’re dealing with periodontal disease, scaling and root planing offers a deeper, more targeted approach than a routine cleaning. Dr. Chung and her team use specialized techniques to carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup from below the gumline, where a standard cleaning can’t reach.

This process helps eliminate harmful bacteria, reduce inflammation, and support your gums as they heal, so you can get back to a healthier, more comfortable smile.

Restorative We restore smiles for better health Restorative Crowns Teeth can become damaged for a variety of reasons, including cavities, heavy bite forces, or trauma. If a root canal is needed to save a compromised nerve, the tooth is often left fragile afterward and needs extra support. Dental crowns, or caps, allow these teeth to be rebuilt and covered with materials that mimic the look and function of natural teeth. Crowns can be crafted to repair a single tooth or multiple teeth, restoring both strength and a natural appearance to your smile.

  • Fluoride Fluoride is a natural mineral that is often referred to as nature’s cavity fighter. This amazing mineral strengthens the tooth’s enamel, making it more resistant to tooth decay. Using fluoride toothpaste and drinking fluoridated water are great practices to ensure you intake a sufficient amount of fluoride daily. Professional fluoride treatments are also an effective method to fight against tooth decay, strengthen enamel, and help children develop healthy teeth.
  • Oral Cancer Screening Early detection and treatment of oral cancer can significantly increase your chances of a quick and complete recovery. At every hygiene visit, we examine all the tissues of your mouth, including gums, cheeks, lips, and tongue. If we suspect any unusual changes in your mouth tissue, we may suggest a biopsy and microscopic analysis by a qualified lab.
  • Near-Infrared Imaging Cavities can hide in places traditional X-rays miss, especially in their earliest stages. Near-infrared imaging uses light instead of radiation to detect decay between teeth and beneath the surface, often before it’s visible to the eye. This technology allows our team to catch problems early, so we can treat them while they’re small and simple, saving you time, discomfort, and cost down the road.
  • Pearl AI We use Pearl AI, an advanced artificial intelligence tool that analyzes your dental X-rays with an added layer of precision. Pearl AI helps our team detect cavities, bone loss, and other oral health concerns with greater accuracy and consistency. It’s another way we bring the latest technology into your care, giving you and your provider a clearer picture of your oral health so you can make informed decisions together.
  • Digital X-Rays Digital sensors capture detailed X-ray images using a fraction of the radiation exposure found in traditional film X-rays. These advancements provide multiple benefits, from safety to improved treatment outcomes. Benefits: Safety: While traditional dental film already requires minimal radiation, our digital x-ray system takes safety even further, reducing radiation exposure by up to 90 % compared to older film-based systems.
  • Time Savings: Images captured instantly with a digital sensor mean less time spent in the dental chair.
  • Better Outcomes: Improved image clarity gives Dr. Erickson greater ability to accurately diagnose and treat conditions.
  • Implant Crowns When a tooth is missing, a dental implant replaces the root, but a crown is what completes the restoration. Custom-crafted to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth, implant crowns are placed on top of the implant to restore a fully functional, natural-looking tooth. Our doctors design each crown to blend seamlessly with your smile, so your implant looks and feels just like the rest of your teeth.
  • Fillings Dental fillings are one of the most common procedures in dentistry. In this routine treatment, we may inject an anesthetic near the tooth to numb the area. Then, we will use a dental drill to remove any decay present in the area and fill in the carved-out space with a dental restoration material. Fillings can be made of gold, porcelain, amalgam, or tooth-colored composite resin, depending on your budget and aesthetic desires.
  • Bridges Whenever you drive across a bridge, you’re being supported by the same principles that support a dental bridge. Dental Bridges are a non-removeable way to replace a missing tooth or teeth. It’s called a bridge because it spans the space and is attached at both ends to other teeth. An artificial tooth is connected to the supporting teeth and replaces the missing tooth. Many patients choose bridges because it is an affordable treatment to replace multiple teeth.
  • Full & Partial Dentures Dentures are an affordable way to replace any number of missing teeth. They have a long history of success, and over 40 million Americans rely on dentures to maintain oral function. Dentures are removable substitutes for missing teeth. Full dentures are used when a person is missing all their natural teeth, while partial dentures replace only a few missing teeth to eliminate gaps and improve oral functions. Denture treatment is customized to each patient’s specific needs. Our team can help you decide which option works best for your smile.
