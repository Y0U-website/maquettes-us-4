What are my options for orthodontic treatment?

Keller Smiles offers a full range of advanced orthodontic solutions for children, teens, and adults. Depending on your needs, our team may recommend one or more of the following treatments:

Braces are the most common type of orthodontic treatment. Using metal brackets and wires, braces apply gentle pressure to gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment, creating a healthier, more attractive smile.

Clear aligners are a modern, discreet alternative to braces. Custom-made to fit your teeth, aligners are transparent and removable, making them a convenient option for both adults and teens. Over time, a series of aligners guide your teeth into their ideal position.

For younger patients, a palatal expander can help widen the upper jaw before the bones fully develop. This treatment creates the space needed for proper alignment and reduces the risk of crowding.

In more severe cases of jaw misalignment, our team may recommend orthognathic (jaw) surgery. This procedure, combined with orthodontics, helps correct functional issues and improve facial balance.

At Keller Smiles, we’re committed to creating customized orthodontic plans that combine the latest technology with compassionate care—helping you and your family achieve healthy, confident smiles.

What goes into the planning for orthodontic treatments?

At Keller Smiles, we know that every smile is unique. That’s why planning orthodontic treatment is a careful and personalized process. Our team works closely with you to design a treatment plan tailored to the type and severity of your malocclusion.

We begin by reviewing your (or your child’s) medical and dental history. From there, we use advanced imaging technology—such as digital X-rays and 3D scans—to get a precise look at your teeth and jaw.

Our goal is to give you a clear path toward a healthy, confident smile.
