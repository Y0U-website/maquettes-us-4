Invisalign

While Invisalign is an excellent option for adults and teens, it is not typically encouraged for anyone under the age of 16. Because Invisalign requires a lot of self-disciple to remove and replace aligners at appropriate times and the overall cleaning and care required, many parents and orthodontists agree that a maturity level needs to be reached before this can be a good option.
