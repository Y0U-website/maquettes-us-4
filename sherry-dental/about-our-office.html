Questions, answered .

Is Sherry Dental accepting new patients in Grand Rapids?

Where is Sherry Dental located in Grand Rapids, MI?

You can find us at 50 Crahen Ave NE, Suite 202, Grand Rapids, MI 49525. We are easy to reach from the Forest Hills, Ada, and Cascade areas, with convenient parking right at the office.

What makes Sherry Dental different from other Grand Rapids dental offices?

Comfort comes first here. From a calm, low-pressure environment to a team that listens before recommending anything, we focus on the details most offices overlook, so even a routine visit feels easy and unrushed.

It has been years since my last dental visit. Is that okay?

Completely okay, and you are in good company. There is no judgment at Sherry Dental, ever. We meet you where you are, take things at your pace, and build a plan that helps you feel comfortable getting back on track.

What should I bring to my first appointment at Sherry Dental?

Just your ID, any dental insurance details if you have them, and a list of any medications you take. From there we handle the rest, so your first visit to our Grand Rapids office starts relaxed and unrushed.

How do I schedule an appointment with Sherry Dental?

Whatever is easiest for you. Schedule online any time through our booking page, fill out the appointment form on our site, or call (616) 330-1008 and our team will find a time that works. New patients are always welcome.
