Questions, answered .

A few things patients often ask before booking with Sherry Dental. Still curious about something? We’re always happy to talk it through.

Is Sherry Dental accepting new patients in Grand Rapids?

Yes. Sherry Dental welcomes new patients of every age across Grand Rapids and the Forest Hills area. You can book online or call (616) 330-1008 , and same-day visits are often available.

Do I need insurance to see a dentist in Grand Rapids at Sherry Dental?

Not at all. We believe your care should be guided by what’s best for you, not by insurance limitations. And for patients without coverage, our Radiant Smile Membership covers routine care and saves you on everything else.

What can I expect at my first visit to Sherry Dental?

A calm, unhurried appointment. We start by listening, then walk you through a thorough exam and any findings in plain language. You will never be rushed or pressured, just clear options and a plan that fits you.

I have dental anxiety. How does Sherry Dental help nervous patients?

Comfort comes first. Every visit includes our comfort menu, noise-canceling headphones, weighted blankets, heated neck pillows, and nitrous (laughing gas) if you’d like it, so anxious patients can settle in and truly relax.

What dental services does Sherry Dental offer?

As a full-service dentist in Grand Rapids, Sherry Dental provides general and preventive care, restorative treatment like crowns and implants, cosmetic dentistry, and clear aligners, comprehensive care for the whole family in one place.

Can I get a same-day or emergency appointment at Sherry Dental?

Often, yes. If you’re in pain or have a dental emergency in Grand Rapids, call (616) 330-1008 as early in the day as you can. We do our best to see urgent cases the same day they call.
