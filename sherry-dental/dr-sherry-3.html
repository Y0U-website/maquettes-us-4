Questions, answered .

Find answers to common questions about appointments, our dental office, and what you can expect during your visit. Have something else in mind? Our team is always happy to help.

Are you accepting new dental patients?

Yes, we welcome new patients and families looking for comfortable, personalized dental care. Scheduling is simple — you can book online or call (616) 330-1008 and our team will help find a convenient appointment time.

Where is your dental office located?

Our office is located at 50 Crahen Ave NE, Suite 202, Grand Rapids, MI 49525. We proudly serve patients throughout the surrounding communities and offer convenient parking for your visit.

What can I expect during my first visit?

Your first appointment is focused on understanding your needs, checking your oral health, and creating a care plan that works for you. We take the time to answer questions and make sure you feel comfortable every step of the way.

I have not been to the dentist in a long time. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many patients return after taking time away from dental care. Our team provides a welcoming, judgment-free experience and will help you create a plan based on your current needs and goals.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring your identification, insurance information if applicable, and a list of any medications you are currently taking. Our team will take care of the rest and help make your visit as smooth as possible.

How can I schedule my appointment?

You can schedule online through our website, complete our appointment request form, or call (616) 330-1008 . Our team will help you find a time that fits your schedule.

Have another question? Contact our team and we’ll be happy to assist you.
