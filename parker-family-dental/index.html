CAD/CAM dentistry uses advanced computer-aided design and computer-aided manufacturing technology to create precise, custom dental restorations such as crowns, veneers, and bridges. This innovative approach allows dentists to design, fabricate, and place restorations in a single visit, improving accuracy, comfort, and convenience for patients.

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and smile through treatments like whitening, veneers, bonding, and reshaping. These procedures can enhance confidence by creating a more attractive and balanced smile.

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental treatment that lightens the color of your teeth and removes stains or discoloration. It can be done in-office for immediate results or with take-home kits for gradual whitening.
