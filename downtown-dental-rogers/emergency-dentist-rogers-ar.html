When to call us right away

If you’re not sure it can wait, call us — we’ll help you figure out the next step.

  • Severe or throbbing tooth pain
  • A knocked-out, loose, or broken tooth
  • A lost or broken filling or crown
  • Swelling in the gums, jaw, or face (possible abscess)
  • Bleeding that won’t stop after an injury
  • Something stuck between your teeth you can’t remove

What to do until you get here

Rinse with warm salt water and take over-the-counter pain relief as directed. Don’t place aspirin directly on the gum.

Handle it by the crown (not the root), keep it moist — tucked in milk or against your cheek — and get to us fast. Time matters most here.

Save any pieces, rinse gently, and use a cold compress on the outside to reduce swelling.

Keep the piece if you can, and avoid chewing on that side until we see you.
