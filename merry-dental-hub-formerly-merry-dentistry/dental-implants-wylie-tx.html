Why Dental Implants Are the Gold Standard

Unlike bridges or dentures, a dental implant is the single replacement option that both maintains jawbone and replicates the function of a natural tooth.

Prevents Bone Loss

Within the first year after losing a tooth, the underlying jawbone can lose as much as 25 % of its volume. An implant transmits chewing forces into the bone like a natural root would — the only replacement that prevents this collapse.

Full Bite Force

Implants return 90–100 % of your natural chewing power, versus just 20–25 % for dentures — so corn on the cob, steak, and apples are all back on the table.

Lasts a Lifetime

With good care the titanium implant body generally lasts a lifetime, and the crown 15–25 years — well beyond bridges (10–15 years) or dentures (5–7 years before relining).

Natural Appearance

Each porcelain or zirconia crown is shade-matched precisely to your adjacent teeth, making the result undetectable — even to you.

Protects Adjacent Teeth

A conventional bridge requires reshaping the two healthy teeth flanking the gap. An implant is self-supporting and leaves those neighboring teeth completely untouched.

Easy to Clean

An implant is brushed and flossed just like a natural tooth — no soaking, no adhesives, no special equipment. It is the lowest-maintenance replacement available.
