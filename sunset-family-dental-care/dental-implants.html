Our Dental Implant Process at Sunset Family Dental Care

Our Vacaville implant dentists use a personalized, phased approach to ensure every implant is placed safely and comfortably.

From start to finish, our team provides expert guidance and gentle care for a stress-free experience.

  • Initial Consultation: We perform a comprehensive exam and digital imaging to assess bone structure and determine eligibility.
  • Surgical Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone. Healing typically takes several weeks to allow the implant to integrate (osseointegration).
  • Abutment and Crown Placement: After healing, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant for a secure and lifelike restoration. We may use ceramic crowns for a more natural appearance.
  • Aftercare and Maintenance: Regular checkups and cleanings will help maintain your implant and surrounding teeth. Our dental cleanings and exams are available throughout the year.

FAQs About Dental Implants

  • How long do dental implants last?: With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or longer. They are considered a permanent solution for tooth loss.
  • Are dental implants painful?: The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery.
  • How do dental implants compare to dentures?: Unlike dentures, implants are fixed in place, offer greater chewing power, and prevent bone loss.
  • What is the cost of dental implants in Vacaville?: Costs vary based on the number of implants and the complexity of your case. We accept PPO plans and offer consultations to discuss pricing and coverage.
  • Can I get dental implants if I have bone loss?: In some cases, a bone graft may be recommended. We’ll evaluate your jawbone and provide personalized recommendations during your consultation.
