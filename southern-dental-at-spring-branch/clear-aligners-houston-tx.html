Clear Aligners in Houston, TX

Clear teeth aligners are a great solution for mild to moderate misalignment cases.

  • Discreet – Most people won’t notice you’re wearing them
  • Comfortable – No wires or brackets to irritate your gums
  • Convenient – Fewer office visits and easier maintenance

Are Clear Aligners Right for You?

You might benefit from clear aligners if you have:

Our experienced team will assess your bite and develop a custom treatment plan tailored to your goals.

  • Crowded or crooked teeth
  • Gaps or spacing issue
  • Mild bite misalignment
  • Shifting teeth after previous orthodontic work

How the Clear Aligner Process Works

At Southern Dental Associates, getting started is simple:

  • Digital Scan & Consultation: We begin with a 3D scan of your teeth to evaluate alignment and build your treatment plan.
  • Custom Aligner Creation: You’ll receive a series of aligner trays to wear in sequence, changing every 1–2 weeks. In some cases, we use discreet resin attachments to enhance effectiveness.
  • Progress Monitoring: Routine check-ins ensure your teeth are tracking as expected.
  • Retainers for Long-Term Results: After treatment, we’ll provide removable or permanent retainers to preserve your smile.

Clear Aligners vs. Braces

Both aligners and traditional braces are effective, but they serve different needs. Clear aligners are more convenient and less noticeable—ideal for adults and teens with mild to moderate alignment issues. Braces may be recommended for more complex corrections.
