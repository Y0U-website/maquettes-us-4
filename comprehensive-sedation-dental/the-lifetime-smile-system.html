Your Bite Is Built Around Comfort and Balance

A healthy smile depends on a relaxed and stable bite.

Instead of forcing your bite into a position, we allow your body to guide us.

By gently relaxing the muscles first, your jaw settles into a position that feels naturally comfortable. From there, we design your bite around balance and long-term stability.

When the bite is built this way, patients often experience:

This step is about harmony and comfort, not force.
