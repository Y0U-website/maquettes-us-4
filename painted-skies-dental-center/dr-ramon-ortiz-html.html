Get to Know Ramon M. Ortiz, DDS

For nearly 15 years, Dr. Ray has been blessed to work alongside his amazing wife, Dr. Natalie, and serve patients in a profession that he truly loves! He is also a firm believer that dentists need to grow with the field over time, which is why he regularly furthers his education through state-of-the-art training and takes the time to ensure that his patients are receiving the most advanced and thorough care possible. Interested in learning more about him? Just keep reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Some of Dr. Ray’s earliest memories in life are of wanting to become a doctor just like his father. His parents have always been a guiding force in his life, and he went through every stage of school simply KNOWING that he was going to be a physician someday. However, after completing his pre-requisite courses in college and starting his medical school application, he finally had to admit to himself that the career didn’t feel like his true calling in life.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Ray completed both his undergraduate degree and his dental doctorate at Creighton University. Following graduation, he took additional courses at the prestigious Spear Center in Scottsdale, AZ. He also trained through Dental Implant Seminars and with CEREC Doctors. Today, he is a designated trainer for Patterson Dental and Sirona regarding CEREC technology and procedures! He teaches other dentists how to properly use the chairside software, hardware, and clinical instruction to create amazing restorative solutions for patients in need.
