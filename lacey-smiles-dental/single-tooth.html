Am I a Good Candidate?

Single tooth implants may be ideal when:

If bone is insufficient or the site is complex, Dr. Hodges and our team will present grafting, sinus augmentation, or alternative restorative options. Do you want to learn more? See answers to common implant care and healing questions.

  • You have one or a few missing teeth
  • You have good bone or are willing to have a graft
  • Your gums are healthy
  • You have good general health (or controlled conditions)
  • You commit to excellent oral hygiene
