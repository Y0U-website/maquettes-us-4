Treatment Process

Evaluation & Planning

CBCT imaging helps us measure bone, determine implant locations, and choose the best attachment style.

Implant Placement

Typically 2–4 implants per arch are placed in key positions.

Healing / Integration Phase

Implants integrate over several months; in many cases, a temporary denture is modified to fit the implants.

Attachment & Final Prosthesis

Once healed, you’ll receive the final overdenture that snaps, clips, or locks into place.

Benefits of Implant Dentures

  • Superior stability — no slipping or adhesive
  • Improved chewing ability — enjoy more foods
  • Better bone preservation than traditional dentures
  • Easier speech and digestion
  • Can upgrade to fixed alternatives later
