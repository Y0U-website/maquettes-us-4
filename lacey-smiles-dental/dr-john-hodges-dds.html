Experience You Can Trust

Dr. John Hodges brings more than 30 years of experience to the Lacey Smiles team. He’s placed and restored over 7,500 dental implants—including 750 full-arch cases—and is internationally recognized for his expertise in implant dentistry and full-arch prosthetics. But Dr. Hodges offers more than implant care. He provides a full range of general dental services, from routine checkups and cleanings to crowns, fillings, and cosmetic dentistry. His goal is always the same: to deliver personalized, high-quality care in a way that helps each patient feel comfortable and understood.
