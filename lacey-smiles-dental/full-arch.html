Full Arch Implant FAQs

How long does full arch treatment take?

Often 3–6 months from start to final prosthesis (temporary arch may be placed earlier).

Does it hurt?

Discomfort is minimized with local anesthesia, sedation, and pain management.

What if I don’t have enough bone?

Bone grafting or sinus augmentation may be needed (see our FAQ page).

How do I care for my new arch?

With proper brushing, flossing, and regular check-ups, full arch implants can last a lifetime.
