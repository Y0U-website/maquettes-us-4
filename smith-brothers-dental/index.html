The practice

Our dad opened his doors in Klamath Falls in 1981. Andrew took over in 2008, and Nick joined him in 2020. We grew up here. Our patients are our neighbors, the families and hard-working people who make this basin what it is.

That’s who we built this practice for, and it’s why we do dentistry the way we do: honestly and carefully.

Cleanings, exams, fillings, and more for patients at every age and stage. We’ve been caring for Klamath Falls families for over four decades, and most of what you need, we handle right here.

Your first visit is a comprehensive exam: a look at your teeth, gums, and overall oral health. We take our time, explain what we find, and build a plan around you.

If a tooth needs a crown, our in-office CEREC system lets us design, mill, and place it in a single appointment. Yes, just one visit and you’re done.

Les soins

Routine visits help prevent small issues from becoming bigger problems while keeping your teeth, gums, and bite healthy.

Scaling and root planing help calm inflammation, treat gum disease, and protect the foundation of your smile.

Missing a tooth and wondering if an implant is right for you? We’ll give you an honest answer after a comprehensive exam, because the right plan depends on your bone, your bite, and your goals. Dr. Andrew Smith handles implant cases individually and carefully.

Clear retainers help maintain your smile after orthodontic treatment or minor tooth movement with a comfortable custom fit.

Lab-made veneers can improve the shape, color, and balance of your smile, with a digital preview available before treatment begins.
