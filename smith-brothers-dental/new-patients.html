Kids are welcome here at any age A child’s first visit is designed to be easy: they sit in the chair, get familiar with the office, get some light polishing and fluoride varnish, and leave with a prize.

Early positive experiences make a difference; the goal is for going to the dentist to feel like something they’re used to, not something to dread.

Haven’t been to the dentist in a while?

We hear this often. People skip the dentist for a year or two, sometimes longer, and then feel embarrassed about coming back.

There’s nothing to be embarrassed about here. We’ll tell you what we find, explain what matters most right now, and let you set the pace from there.
