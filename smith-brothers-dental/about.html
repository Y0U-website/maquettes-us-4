About Smith Brothers Dental in Klamath Falls

Not just family dentists. A family of dentists.

Since 1981 The apple didn’t fall far from the dental chair Smith Brothers Dental started with their father, who opened the practice in Klamath Falls in 1981 and cared for local families for more than 30 years. Dr. Andrew Smith and Dr. Nick Smith grew up around the practice, learned the craft, and came home to carry it forward.

Today, the brothers care for patients in a modern, custom-designed office built for the next generation of Klamath Falls dentistry.

Same family name, honest approach, and two dentists who still treat patients like neighbors.

The two of us Dr. Andrew Smith Dr. Andrew Smith grew up in Klamath Falls and came back home in 2008 to practice alongside his father, Dr. T. Alexander Smith. For him, dentistry is a way to keep caring for the same community that raised him, with honest treatment, steady improvement, and long-term relationships at the center of every visit.

Same-day crowns, because your time matters

We invest in technology when it makes the experience better for patients. CEREC same-day crowns are one of the best examples.

Instead of taking goopy impressions, placing a temporary crown, and asking you to come back weeks later, our CEREC system lets us design, mill, and place many crowns in one visit.
