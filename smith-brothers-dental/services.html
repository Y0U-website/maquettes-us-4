Custom mouthguards help protect your teeth from grinding, clenching, and sports-related injuries.

Cosmetic Dentistry Cosmetic dentistry should still look like you. Whether you want a brighter smile, a repaired tooth, or a more complete smile refresh, we’ll help you understand your options without pushing treatment you don’t need.

Veneers

Lab-made veneers can improve the shape, color, and balance of your smile, with a digital preview available before treatment begins.

Bonding and Cosmetic Tooth Repair

Bonding repairs small chips, worn edges, gaps, or uneven tooth shape with natural-looking, tooth-colored material.

Take-home Teeth Whitening

Professional take-home whitening helps brighten your smile safely and gradually with guidance from our team.

Clear Retainers

Clear retainers help maintain your smile after orthodontic treatment or minor tooth movement with a comfortable custom fit.

Restorative Dentistry When your smile needs repair, restorative dentistry helps rebuild strength, function, and confidence. We’ll explain what’s happening, what your options are, and what makes the most sense for your long-term health.

Same-day CEREC Crowns

CEREC crowns restore damaged or weakened teeth in one visit for many patients, with each crown designed and milled in-office.

Bridges

Bridges replace one or more missing teeth, restore chewing function, and help keep nearby teeth from shifting.

Inlays and Onlays

Inlays and onlays repair damaged teeth when you need more support than a filling but less coverage than a full crown.

Oral Surgery

Sometimes surgical care is the best way to protect your oral health. When that happens, we focus on clear communication, careful planning, and helping you feel comfortable before, during, and after treatment.

Extractions
