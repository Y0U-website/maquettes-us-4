Beautiful Porcelain Veneers

At each step in our process, from design to crafting, we emphasize precision and quality to create beautiful porcelain veneers and natural-looking smile makeovers. When it comes to design, we utilize cutting-edge technology and our partnership with the professionals at Digital Smile Design to identify the smile that best complements your unique aesthetics. Our veneers are crafted from beautiful porcelain by a master ceramist certified by the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

To ensure that you achieve your dream smile, we collaborate with you throughout the design process, allow you to try on your smile before any work is done, and provide a Beautiful Smile Guarantee.

Digital Smile Design

At Naples General & Cosmetic Dentistry, we embrace modern technology to facilitate the creation of a design that is not only precisely aesthetic but 100 % aligned with your aspirations. We begin by capturing detailed images of your face, smile, and expressions. Then, we partner with the Digital Smile Design Planning Center to assess these images and develop a design that promotes ideal symmetry, ratios, and proportions of your teeth and face.

Next, a digital version of the design is made, superimposing your new smile over an image of your current one. We’ll look it over together and collaborate to make any necessary changes until it completely reflects your aesthetic goals. The final step in our Digital Smile Design process is the opportunity for you to try on a 3D-printed version of the finished design so you can tangibly interact with your new smile before moving toward making it permanent.

Conservative Smile Makeovers

We value preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible while giving you the smile that you want. In some cases, optimum aesthetics can be achieved with little to no tooth preparation required. At most, our tooth preparation involves removing a margin of enamel of only about 0.3-0.5 mm, no more than the thickness of a fingernail. Beautiful Smile Guarantee

At Naples General & Cosmetic Dentistry, we guarantee that you’ll love your new smile with porcelain veneers, meaning that we won’t bond it on unless you love it. Our Digital Smile Design process invites you to take an active role in the development of your design and to know how it will look on you before approving it. As a result, by the time your veneers are finished, we’ll both be confident that you’ll love your new smile. In the rare case that any adjustments need to be made when you try your veneers on, we’ll take care of them and even have them re-made before bonding them on.

Our Porcelain Veneers Process

Step 1: Smile Design

To begin, we’ll gather all of the information needed to design a beautiful smile for you. This will include high-resolution photos and/or videos as well as digital oral scans. You’ll be invited to share your smile goals and any photos or pictures that encapsulate them.

Dr. Bracho or Dr. Keller will then submit all visual data (scans, photos, videos) to the Digital Smile Design Planning Center so it can be used to create a design customized to your unique facial aesthetics. When a design is developed, he’ll discuss it with you and tweak it as needed until your full vision is represented.

You’ll then have the option of purchasing a 3D-printed model of your newly designed smile so that you can even more fully experience its look and feel before committing to it. Once the design is finalized, we will have a set of provisional veneers made for you to wear following your tooth preparation at your next appointment.

Step 2: Tooth Preparation

Step 3: Try-In & Bonding

When your veneers are finished, it will be time to make your smile vision a reality! Dr. Bracho or Dr. Keller will first give you a chance to try on your new smile by applying a try-in paste to the veneers and placing them on your teeth. And once you experience the beauty of your new smile, you’ll be excited for him to permanently bond them. We’re confident you’ll love your porcelain veneers exactly as they are and committed to making any minor adjustments needed to ensure your complete satisfaction before bonding.
