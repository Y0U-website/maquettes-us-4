About Dr. Alkesh C. Sura

Dr. Alkesh C. Sura is a North Carolina native, having attended the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill for his undergraduate and Doctor of Dental Surgery degrees. With his background in public health, he chose dentistry because of the immediate and positive impact he can have on people. He has always had a passion for helping others. Dr. Sura has volunteered on numerous mission trips, both nationally and globally, and looks forward to continuing those efforts in the near future.

When not in the office, Dr. Sura enjoys spending time with his wife Kruti (a native Texan and the reason he moved to Austin) and their children Saya and Sahin. Dr. Sura loves sports, reading, outdoor activities, and traveling the world.

Education

Dr. Sura is dedicated to learning the latest techniques through continuing education in all aspects of dentistry, and he is active in a study group of local Austin dentists and dental specialists (orthodontists, oral surgeons, periodontists, prosthodontists, etc).
