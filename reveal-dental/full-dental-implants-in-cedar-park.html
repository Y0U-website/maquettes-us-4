Frequently Asked Questions

We have the answers you are looking for.

What is the lifespan of complete dental implants?

Full dental implants can last for many years—often decades—with proper maintenance and regular dental checkups. Over time, the artificial teeth may require periodic maintenance or replacement.

Do complete dental implants cause pain?

The majority of patients report very little discomfort. An oral surgeon performs the surgical part under appropriate anesthesia, and any discomfort following the treatment is usually tolerable.

How long does it take to recover?

While full implant integration may take several months, initial healing may just take a few weeks. You will be guided through each step by your dentist.

How should complete dental implants be maintained?

Just as with real teeth, you should brush twice a day, floss frequently, and go to the dentist for regular cleanings and examinations.

Are dentures inferior to implants?

Compared with traditional dentures , implants offer greater stability, comfort, and functionality for many people. The optimal choice, however, is contingent upon personal requirements and dental health.
