Your Top Questions About Dental Implants Answered

These answers address common questions about dental implant treatment, including evaluation, timing, comfort, alternatives, and maintenance. They provide general information only; an examination is necessary to determine what is appropriate for an individual patient.

Dental implants can last 25-30 years or even a lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular dental maintenance. Success depends on factors including bone quality, oral health habits, and following post-surgical care instructions. Dr. Diamond monitors implant health during routine appointments to ensure long-term success.

Dental implants offer superior stability, prevent bone loss, and don’t require removal for cleaning like traditional dentures. They restore full chewing capacity and natural speech while eliminating concerns about slipping or adhesives. Implants also preserve surrounding healthy teeth by not requiring support structures.

Most adults with adequate bone density and healthy gums are excellent implant candidates. Dr. Diamond evaluates factors including medical history, bone quality, and oral health status during comprehensive examination. Even patients with bone loss may qualify through additional procedures like bone grafting.

Initial healing typically takes 7-10 days with manageable discomfort controlled by prescribed medications. Complete osseointegration occurs over 3-6 months as the implant fuses with jawbone tissue. Dr. Diamond provides detailed aftercare instructions and monitors healing progress throughout recovery.

Implant costs vary based on individual needs, number of implants, and restoration type required. Diamond Dental accepts major insurance plans and offers financing options to make treatment accessible. We provide transparent cost estimates during consultation and work with your budget for optimal care.
