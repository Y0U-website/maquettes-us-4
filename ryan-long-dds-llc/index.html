The practice

When you come in, we take the time to explain your treatment options, answer your questions, and work with you to build a plan that fits your life. We want you to walk out feeling heard, comfortable, and confident in your care.

Dr. Long founded Ryan Long Dental with one goal in mind: to create a practice where patients feel genuinely heard and cared for. With over 13 years serving the Dayton area, he brings deep experience in restorative dentistry–including crowns, bridges, implants, and dentures–and a hands-on approach where every procedure is performed by the doctor, start to finish.

Dr. Nathaniel Soto brings a two-year General Practice Residency, advanced training in implants, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry, and a dental sleep medicine residency from the University of Michigan to Ryan Long Dental. With hundreds of hours of continuing education, he is committed to comprehensive, compassionate care for every patient he sees.

From routine cleanings to dental implants and sleep apnea appliances, Ryan Long Dental offers a full range of services under one roof. Dr. Long and his associate bring decades of combined experience and advanced training.

We’re currently accepting new patients and do our best to make scheduling as seamless as possible — whether you’re new to the area, switching providers, or dealing with something urgent.
