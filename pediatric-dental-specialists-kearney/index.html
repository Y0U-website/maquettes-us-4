The practice

Pediatric Dental Specialists–Kearney is a trusted partner in your child’s dental care. Maintaining good oral health is important for long-term health and well-being. Our caring team enjoys creating positive dental experiences for children so they can go on to have a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Your kids will feel right at home at Pediatric Dental Specialists–Kearney. We offer a relaxed and friendly environment where dental visits are fun. Your child’s comfort is our top priority.

The Pediatric Dental Specialists–Kearney team is specially trained in accommodating kids with special needs. You can be confident your child will receive the highest quality dental care in a comfortable, friendly environment.

As pediatric dental specialists, our team members are specifically trained to provide top-notch, compassionate dental care to children of all ages. You can trust that your children will be in excellent hands. We make dental visits fun and exciting and have a knack for teaching your kiddos how to keep their teeth and gums healthy at home.

Cosmetic dentistry can totally transform an imperfect smile into one that your child is proud of. Damaged, decayed, or discolored teeth can make children feel self-conscious. Cosmetic treatments, such as tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and teeth whitening can boost their confidence and encourage them to smile brightly again.

She is very excited to provide high-quality pediatric dentistry services to the Kearney community.
