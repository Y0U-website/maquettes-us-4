Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants offers numerous benefits:

  • Durability: Implants are designed to be a long-term solution and, with proper care, can last a lifetime.
  • Improved Oral Health: Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants do not require altering adjacent teeth, which preserves more of your natural tooth structure. They also help prevent bone loss and maintain jawbone integrity.
  • Enhanced Comfort and Ease : Implants eliminate the discomfort of removable dentures and improve your ability to chew and speak.
  • Natural Appearance : Dental implants look and feel like your own teeth, enhancing both your smile and your confidence.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Getting dental implants is a multi-step process, typically involving several visits over a few months:

  • Consultation and Planning: During your first visit, we conduct a thorough examination, including X-rays and possibly 3D images, to assess your jawbone and gums for suitability. We discuss your treatment options and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: Under local, sedative, or general anesthetic, the implant is surgically implanted into the jawbone; the choice is based on your demands and the intricacy of the process.
  • Healing and Bone Integration: The healing process can take several months as the implant integrates with the bone. During this time, you may have temporary dentures and should eat a soft diet.
  • Abutment and Artificial Tooth Placement: Once the implant has bonded with the jawbone, an additional minor surgery might be needed to place the abutment, which will hold the new tooth. After your gums heal, the artificial teeth are made and fitted.

Caring for Your Implants

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential to maintain the health of your implants.
