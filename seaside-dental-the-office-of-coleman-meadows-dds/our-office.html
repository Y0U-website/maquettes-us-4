Our Technology

At Seaside Dental, our office is equipped with modern technology including a CEREC machine, digital scanners, and implant-planning software. With this state-of-the-art equipment, we offer stress-free, comfortable, and efficient dental care for every treatment, no matter how simple or complex. Schedule a visit today and say hello to modern dentistry!

Same-Day Crowns with CEREC

If you have a broken tooth or a severe cavity, we can restore your dental health in just one appointment. With our CEREC machine, it takes just under two hours to create a custom crown that fits perfectly in your smile and looks just like a natural tooth.

Digital Impressions for Invisalign

Say goodbye to uncomfortable, messy impression moldings! Digital impressions make it possible to plan your Invisalign treatment with just one scan. The information from these digital impressions is much more accurate, providing superior results along with enhanced comfort.

3D Cone Beam Technology

Traditional x-rays require patients to stay perfectly still and can take up a lot of your appointment time. 3D Cone Beam Technology makes it much easier and more efficient to diagnose small oral health issues and ensure that you get treatment as soon as possible.

Digital Implant Planning

If you come to Seaside Dental for implant treatment, we can handle every step of the process. We can plan your treatment, handle your implant placement with skillful ease, and create a masterful restoration that will look natural when you smile.
