Who may be a candidate for dental implants?

Dental implants are worth considering if you want to replace one or more missing teeth with a stable, long-term solution. A consultation is the best way to determine if you are a good candidate.

Common reasons people consider implants

You may want to ask about implants if you:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Want a long-lasting tooth replacement option
  • Want to restore daily function as well as appearance
  • Are comparing restorative dental treatments
  • Are already receiving comprehensive dental care and want to understand your long-term plan

Questions to discuss at your visit

  • Am I likely to be a candidate for dental implants?
  • What would the process look like in my case?
  • What may affect timing?
  • What may affect cost?
  • How would implant treatment fit into my overall oral health plan?

How do you care for dental implants long term?

Like natural teeth, dental implants still need consistent care. Good home habits and regular dental visits are part of protecting your investment over time.

Daily Home Care

  • • Brushing thoroughly every day
  • • Cleaning carefully between teeth
  • • Reporting any changes or concerns promptly

Professional Care

Regular professional dental checkups and cleanings are essential. While excellent home care is critical, routine visits allow your dentist to monitor your implants and catch any issues early.
