Services

Most patients should plan on two routine dental cleanings per year , or about every six months. That schedule helps keep plaque and tartar buildup under control and gives your dentist a regular chance to monitor changes in your oral health.

Regular plaque and tartar removal lowers bacterial buildup that contributes to tooth decay and inflamed gums.

Dental implants are worth considering if you want to replace one or more missing teeth with a stable, long-term solution. A consultation is the best way to determine if you are a good candidate.

Address alignment concerns discreetly with ClearCorrect invisible braces for a straighter smile.

Cosmetic dental services are a good fit when your teeth feel healthy enough, but you want to improve how your smile looks. At Ormsby Dental, cosmetic care is practical, personalized, and focused on natural-looking improvement.
