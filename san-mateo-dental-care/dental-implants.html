Initial Consultation

Your implant treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, bone density, and overall medical history. We use advanced imaging technology to assess your jaw structure and determine the optimal placement for your implants. During this visit, we’ll discuss your goals, explain your treatment options, and address any concerns you might have about the implant process.

Customized Treatment Plan

Based on your consultation findings, we develop a detailed treatment strategy tailored to your specific needs. This plan outlines the type of implants recommended, whether traditional implants, All-on-X, or implant-supported dentures, along with the expected timeline and recovery process. We ensure you understand all aspects of your treatment before proceeding.

Implementation of Treatment

The implant placement procedure is performed with precise attention to detail and patient comfort. Using modern surgical techniques, we carefully position the implants to ensure optimal integration with your jawbone. For immediate-load procedures like All-on-X, we can often provide temporary restorations the same day.

Aftercare & Maintenance Tips

Post-implant care is crucial for successful healing and long-term results. We provide detailed instructions for maintaining oral hygiene around your implants and managing any temporary discomfort. Regular cleaning and proper care ensure your implants remain healthy and functional for years to come.

Follow-Up

We schedule regular check-ups to monitor your healing progress and ensure proper integration of the implants. Once healing is complete, we’ll place your permanent restorations and provide guidance on long-term maintenance. Our team remains available to address any concerns that arise during your recovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is implant dentistry?

Implant dentistry is a specialized field of dental care focused on replacing missing teeth with permanent artificial tooth roots and restorations. This advanced dental specialty combines surgical placement of titanium implants with prosthetic dentistry to create natural-looking and functioning replacement teeth. The field encompasses various techniques and technologies designed to restore both the appearance and function of your natural teeth, while also preserving bone structure and oral health.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made primarily from biocompatible titanium that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. The implant post integrates with your natural bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Modern dental implants are designed to match your natural tooth structure in both function and appearance, providing a long-term solution for tooth replacement.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime, though the crown or restoration on top may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear. The success rate of dental implants is remarkably high, typically over 95 % when placed by experienced professionals. Regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene, and avoiding tobacco use significantly contribute to the longevity of your dental implants.

What are denture implants?

Denture implants, also known as implant-supported dentures, combine traditional denture technology with dental implants to create a more stable and comfortable tooth replacement solution. This system uses several strategically placed implants to secure a full denture, eliminating the need for adhesives and preventing the slipping commonly associated with traditional dentures. The result is improved comfort, better chewing ability, and increased confidence in social situations.

What is all on x dental implants?

All-on-X is an innovative full-arch replacement solution that uses strategically placed dental implants to support an entire arch of replacement teeth. This advanced technique can often be completed in a single day, allowing patients to leave with temporary teeth while their permanent restoration is crafted. The exact number of implants used depends on individual factors like bone density and arch size, but typically ranges from four to six implants per arch.
