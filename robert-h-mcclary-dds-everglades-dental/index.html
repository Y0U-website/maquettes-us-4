The practice

Welcome to Everglades Dental Clinic Our dental clinic has been serving the community for over 20 years. We pride ourselves on providing high-quality, affordable dental care to our patients. Dr. Robert H. Mcclary along with his team of experienced hygienists is dedicated to making your visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

We diagnose & treat all aspects of dentistry, including everything from the preventive education & routine teeth cleanings that help to reduce dental problems to expert cosmetic & restorative solutions for the dental issues our patients face.

Combining the right mix of personal care, technology & continuing education, we maintain a commitment to our Western Palm Beach County patients in every facet of our practice.
