Say Goodbye to Goo: The iTero® Digital Scanner

For many patients, the thought of traditional dental impressions – with their messy, gooey materials and potential for discomfort – can be a source of anxiety. At Swenson Family Orthodontics, we’ve eliminated this outdated process thanks to our iTero® Scanner.

The iTero® Scanner is a revolutionary intraoral scanner that creates a highly accurate, three-dimensional digital model of your teeth and gums in just minutes. Here’s why this technology is a game-changer for your orthodontic experience:

At Swenson Family Orthodontics, we are proud to invest in technology that makes your orthodontic journey more precise, comfortable, and efficient. Our 3D X-rays and iTero® Scanner are just two examples of how we blend advanced science with compassionate care to help you achieve the healthy, confident smile you deserve. Come experience the difference that modern orthodontics can make!

  • Unrivaled Comfort: No more gagging or messy impression material! The iTero® scanner uses a small, handheld wand that glides comfortably over your teeth, capturing thousands of images per second.
  • Superior Accuracy: The digital scans are incredibly precise, eliminating the potential for errors or distortions often associated with traditional molds. This leads to better-fitting appliances, whether it’s for braces, retainers, or Invisalign® clear aligners.
  • Efficiency and Speed: The scanning process is quick, and the digital models are instantly available for review. This means less time in the chair for you and a faster start to your treatment.
  • Real-Time Visualization: You can watch your teeth appear on a screen in 3D as the scan progresses. For Invisalign® patients, we can even simulate your potential treatment outcome, allowing you to visualize your new smile before you even begin!
  • Digital Record Keeping: The digital scans are securely stored on file, providing a permanent and easily accessible record of your dental anatomy. This is invaluable for tracking progress, creating new appliances if needed in the future, and ensuring continuity of care.
  • Eco-Friendly: By going digital, we reduce waste associated with traditional impression materials, contributing to a more sustainable practice.
