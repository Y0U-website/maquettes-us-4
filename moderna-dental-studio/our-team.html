Noah Cruz, DDS FAGD

Dr. Noah Cruz is a dedicated and compassionate dentist committed to providing exceptional oral health care to patients of all ages. With a genuine passion for dentistry, Dr. Noah Cruz combines extensive clinical expertise with a patient-centered approach to create a comfortable and positive dental experience.

Dr. Cruz earned a Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from Texas A&M College of Dentistry (formerly Baylor College of Dentistry), where a rigorous curriculum and hands-on training equipped him with the skills necessary to excel in the field of dentistry. His commitment to staying current with the latest advancements in dental science is reflected in his ongoing pursuit of continuing education and professional development.

Dr. Cruz understands the importance of building strong doctor-patient relationships. Known for his friendly demeanor and excellent communication skills, he takes the time to educate patients about their oral health, discuss treatment options, and address any concerns. Creating a comfortable and welcoming environment is a top priority for Dr. Cruz, fostering a positive dental experience for every individual under their care.

Dr. Cruz maintains active membership in professional dental associations, such as the American Dental Association (ADA), Dallas Academy of General Dentistry (DAGD), Dallas County Dental Society (DCDS) and stays connected with peers and staying abreast of industry developments.

When not in the office, Dr. Cruz enjoys playing basketball, tennis, and is a consistent runner. Dr. Cruz has 2 miniature Australian Shepherds and is married to a dentist. All to say, he has a well-rounded personality dedicated to both professional excellence and a fulfilling personal life.

For exceptional dental care rooted in expertise, compassion, and a commitment to patient well-being, look no further than Dr. Noah Cruz.

Mayra Estrada, DDS FAGD

Meet Dr. Estrada, a beacon of excellence in the field of dentistry. With a passion for transforming smiles and a commitment to patient-centered care, Dr. Estrada stands as an exceptional dentist with a stellar reputation.

Boasting an impressive educational background, Dr. Estrada earned her dental degree from Texas A&M College of Dentistry (formerly known as Baylor College of Dentistry) and has since accumulated years of hands-on experience, honing her skills to perfection. Known for her meticulous attention to detail and commitment to staying abreast of the latest advancements in dental science and technology, Dr. Estrada ensures that each patient receives the highest standard of care.

What sets Dr. Estrada apart is not just her clinical expertise, but also her genuine compassion for patients. She takes the time to listen to individual concerns, tailoring treatment plans that address unique needs and aspirations. Patients consistently praise Dr. Estrada for her warm demeanor, creating a comfortable and reassuring atmosphere within the dental chair.

From routine check-ups to complex restorative procedures, Dr. Estrada approaches dentistry with a holistic perspective, understanding the profound impact a healthy smile can have on overall well-being. Beyond the clinic, she is actively involved in community outreach programs, striving to promote oral health education and awareness.

Step into the care of Dr. Estrada and experience dentistry at its finest—a harmonious blend of skill, compassion, and a genuine commitment to crafting smiles that radiate confidence and joy.

Dr. Estrada maintains her membership with the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry and teaches implant courses some weekends to ensure other dentists provide excellent care to their patients.

Jay Patel, DDS, MS

Jay Patel, DDS, MS is a board certified orthodontist who specializes in the diagnosis, prevention, and correction of mal-positioned teeth and jaws. Dr. Patel provides expert care with a comfortable environment.

Dr. Patel was born and raised in the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex and is excited to serve the community he grew up in. He attended the University of North Texas (Go Mean Green!) graduating summa cum laude. Following undergrad, Dr. Patel graduated from the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio with the Highest Honors and Distinctions in Dental Education and Research. Upon completion of Dental School, Dr. Patel was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

In his free time, Dr. Patel enjoys playing the drums, spending time with family and friends, traveling and playing or watching sports. Through and through a Dallas native, Dr. Patel always roots for his hometown teams; Cowboys, Mavs, Rangers, and Stars!

Aracely Nieto, RDH

I am a passionate dental professional committed to bringing smiles to life through comprehensive oral care. With a background rooted in both clinical expertise and a genuine love for promoting dental wellness, my journey as a dental hygienist has been a fulfilling adventure.

From the moment I embarked on my career, I recognized the profound impact that oral health has on one’s overall well-being. Armed with a degree in Dental Hygiene from Wichita State University, I have diligently pursued opportunities to stay at the forefront of the ever-evolving dental field.

My commitment to continuous learning has led me to attend numerous workshops and conferences, ensuring that I stay abreast of the latest advancements in dental hygiene practices.

What truly sets my professional journey apart is the genuine connection I establish with each patient. I believe in fostering a welcoming and comfortable environment, where open communication and personalized care take center stage. Whether guiding patients through the intricacies of oral hygiene at home or providing gentle and thorough cleanings, I am dedicated to creating positive and lasting impressions.

As your dental hygienist, my mission is to not only enhance your oral health but also to make your dental experiences enjoyable and stress-free. I look forward to the opportunity to meet you, understand your unique dental needs, and embark on a collaborative journey toward achieving and maintaining a radiant, confident smile. Together, let’s make your oral health a priority and keep those smiles shining bright!
