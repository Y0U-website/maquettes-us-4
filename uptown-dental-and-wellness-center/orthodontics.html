FAQs About Orthodontics in Gig Harbor, WA

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time depends on your needs. Most patients in Gig Harbor, WA, complete treatment in 12-24 months. Your personalized treatment plan will give you a clear timeline.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Yes, clear aligners like Invisalign® can be very effective for many cases. Our orthodontist will recommend the best treatment options based on your teeth and goals.

Is orthodontic care only for kids?

No. Patients of all ages can benefit. Many adults in Gig Harbor choose orthodontics to improve their smile and oral health.

Will orthodontic treatment hurt?

You may feel mild pressure after adjustments. This is normal and shows your treatment is working. Our team helps make every visit comfortable.

How do I start my orthodontic journey?

The first step is to schedule an appointment at our orthodontic office in Gig Harbor. We will guide you through every step and help you achieve your best smile.
