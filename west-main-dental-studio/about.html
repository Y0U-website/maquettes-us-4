What to Expect

You deserve a dental office that you actually look forward to visiting. At West Main Dental Studio we strive to provide the best patient experience each and every time, knowing that doing so starts with listening to your unique needs and helping you achieve your dental goals. Our friendly, hardworking team can answer any questions you may have, and Dr. Maylor will provide a thorough exam and keep you informed of the best options for your care at every visit.

Comfortable & Convenient From comfortable dental chairs and a fresh, clean environment, to paperless forms for an easier check-in, our office is equipped with modern amenities designed to make your life simpler. And with our top-of-the-line technology—including AI-assisted 3D CBCT imaging, intraoral cameras, low-dose Carbon Nano handheld x-rays, and mirrorless cameras for better imagery—we’re able quickly and comfortably get the most accurate sense of your total oral health.

K9s for Warriors We love our Louisville community, which is why we’re passionate about giving back whenever we can. As a veteran himself, Dr. Maylor partners with K9s for Warriors , an organization that pairs veterans who suffer from PTSD with highly-trained service dogs to help them heal, recover, and rediscover the joy in life. Dr. Maylor also provides free dental services to local veterans in need.
