Meet Dr. Anjana Gupta

Dr. Anjana Gupta has aspired to be a dentist since childhood and feels fortunate to share her passion for dentistry with her dedicated team and loyal patients. For her, Smiles on Niles is the realization of a lifelong dream, and she strives to make every patient’s experience positive, comfortable, and rewarding.

Dr. Gupta joined the practice of Dr. Lisa McShane in June 2011 and became a co-owner in 2017, assuming full ownership in 2020. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Indiana University and her Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) from the Indiana University School of Dentistry in 2007. She is an active member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, Chicago Dental Society, Lakeland Valley Dental Society, and Mommy Dentists in Business.

Originally from Elkhart, Indiana, Dr. Gupta now lives in St. Joseph with her husband, Seth Brown, and their two children, Alice and Andre. In her free time, she enjoys the beauty of Lake Michigan, traveling, exploring new cuisines, and, most importantly, spending time with family and friends.

Dr. Soo Sim, DDS

Dr. Soo Sim joined Dr. McShane and Dr. Gupta as a part-time associate dentist in March 2019. She was born and raised in Northwest Indiana and attended Indiana University, where she received her degree in biology with a minor in chemistry. Dr. Sim then attended Indiana University School of Dentistry and graduated alongside Dr. Gupta in 2007. Dr. Sim and Dr. Gupta have been best friends since meeting their very first day of dental school, and working together is a bit of a dream come true.

She currently resides in Chicago and travels to St. Joe to practice. She enjoys trying different restaurants, running along the lake, and going to concerts. Dr. Sim enjoys spending her free time with her family and friends (especially Alice!) and she is very excited about bringing her passion for dentistry to St. Joe!

Our Hygiene Team

Registered dental hygienists are professionals, licensed by the state, who promote optimal oral health through preventive and educational services. Our team of registered dental hygienists includes:

Kindra, RDH

Kindra graduated from the Kalamazoo Valley Community College Dental Hygiene Program in 1996 and is certified in local anesthesia administration in the State of Michigan. Kindra has worked with Dr. McShane since 2000 and Dr. Gupta since 2011. Dr. Gupta says one of Kindra’s best qualities is her ability to make people laugh. Kindra has a laugh that is contagious, and we all love hearing it throughout the day!

Kindra and her husband, Kevin, have two beautiful children. Kindra enjoys camping with her family in the summertime, going to concerts, and watching ND football.

Leanie Staniszewski, RDH

Leanie graduated from the Dental Hygiene Program at Indiana University South Bend in 1994. She joined Dr. Gupta and her team in January 2023, bringing more than 20 years of experience as a dental hygienist. The team enjoys learning new things from Leanie’s experience, and she also contributes a calming presence to the office.

Leanie lives in Berrien Center with her husband and three beautiful children. Outside of her role at Smiles on Niles, she enjoys traveling, being in nature, and gardening.

Michele, RDH

Michele brings 32 years of experience in dental hygiene and a true passion for connecting with her patients. She says her favorite part of being a hygienist is building lasting relationships and getting to know the people she cares for each day.

After taking a short break from hygiene, Michele is excited to return to the profession and join our team. Having known Dr. Gupta for many years, this felt like the perfect place to continue doing what she loves.

Michele and her husband, John, have three children, Ali, Jack and Justin. In her free time, she enjoys spending quality time with her family, working with glass, and reading.

Amanda (Mandy) Williams
