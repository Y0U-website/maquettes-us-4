FAQs Find answers to frequently asked questions from our patients.

What should I expect during my first visit?

During your initial visit, you will undergo a thorough examination, receive x-rays if necessary, and engage in a conversation about your dental health objectives. We will develop a tailored treatment plan and address any questions you might have.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend visits every six months for most patients, but some may need more frequent care. We&#x27;ll create a custom care plan based on your individual needs.

How long does a typical appointment last?

The length of your appointment varies based on the treatment you will undergo. Typically, a standard check-up and cleaning lasts around an hour, whereas more involved procedures might require additional time. We will always give you an estimated duration when you schedule your appointment.

What makes Winn Smiles Cleveland unique?

If you experience a dental emergency, call our office immediately. We reserve time in our daily schedules for emergency patients. If the emergency occurs after hours, call our main number for instructions on how to reach the on-call dentist.
