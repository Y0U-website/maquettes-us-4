Dr. Blake Quigley

Dr. Blake Quigley graduated from the University Of Washington School Of Dentistry where he was recognized by his peers and the faculty for restorative and prosthodontic excellence. After dental school, he commissioned in the United States Navy and was stationed at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune in North Carolina where he was awarded the Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal for his contributions to restorative and comprehensive care and leading the integration of Digital Dentistry and CEREC technologies.

While in dental school and serving in the military he has participated in numerous volunteer medical mission trips providing emergency and restorative dental treatment to underserved populations. He has traveled to Jamaica, El Salvador, and rural communities in Eastern Carolina and he hopes to add more to that list in the years to come. Traveling has been very rewarding for him and has helped him to gain perspective.

Dr. Quigley was raised in Richland, Washington and he’s grateful and excited that his life path led him back home to the Pacific Northwest. He loves being a dentist and he feels that his experiences, skills, and life lessons have prepared him his entire life to provide dental care for the community. He would love the opportunity to be your dental provider! As he said,

Dr. Quigley is a member of the following organizations and educational communities: American Dental Association, American Dental Association Advisory Circle, Kois Center, Wellness Dentistry Network, and Implant Pathways. He has completed over 200 hours of Continuing Education and was recently named a fellow by Implant Pathways for the practice of implant dentistry.

He is happily married to Megan since 2007 and together, they have 3 children: Samuel, Jack, and Summer. When not in the office, he loves to spend time with his family and outdoors.
