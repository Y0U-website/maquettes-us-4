Following these guidelines will help you enjoy your bright smile for as long as possible between whitening treatments.

  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene by brushing twice daily with a whitening toothpaste (but not more than recommended, as overuse can damage enamel) and flossing daily to prevent plaque buildup that can lead to discoloration.
  • Schedule regular professional cleanings at our office every six months to remove surface stains and polish your teeth.
  • Use periodic touch-up treatments as recommended by Dr. Polito, either with professional products or through brief at-home applications using your custom trays. Some patients benefit from a maintenance protocol using lower-concentration whitening products for occasional use.
