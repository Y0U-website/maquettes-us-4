We take a holistic approach to dentistry, which means dental care that promotes whole-body balance and wellness.

WELLNESS dentistry focuses on the whole body, using non-toxic materials and addressing root causes of dental issues. Conventional dentistry often focuses solely on treating symptoms.

" Health and wellness has been a big topic for me for a while now and with my own aging and with loved ones aging, it has become an even bigger deal for me. "- says DR. Mathew.

The three pillars that we believe are the foundation of health are: Gut health, inflammation, and airway health. As dentists, we can positively influence each of these, especially when collaborating with a medical colleague looking at your systemic health.

It’s about more than just your teeth - your overall well-being. Let’s prioritize your health together!

At TLC DENTAL WELLNESS we don’t see patients, we see people with real lives, real struggles and real stories. getting to know you is on the top of our list. We want to know what brought you to us, concerns for your oral health, wishes for the smile you’d love to have.

We believe teeth don’t walk in here! We have the privilege to care for people who happen to have/had teeth.
