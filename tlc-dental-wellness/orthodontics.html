ARE YOU LOOKING FOR A STRAIGHT SMILE WITHOUT THE BRACES ?

Whether you are looking to fix teeth that are unevenly spaced or a bite that is just not right, clear aligners is a great option

INVISALIGN is a popular alternative to braces because the aligners work quickly and are easy to wear. The smooth plastic aligners are clear, so most people won’t notice you’re wearing them

We know you’ll love the fact that clear aligner braces are removable. You can avoid worrying about food getting caught in wires and brackets; just remove the aligners before you eat! Since you can remove them, effective oral hygiene is also much easier than with traditional braces.

HOW CLEAR ALIGNERS- INVISALIGN WORKS?

After you and your doctor decide that I NVISALIGN is the right treatment option to fit your unique goals and needs, we will take your records, X-rays, and map out a customized treatment plan of gradual adjustments that take your teeth from where they are currently to where you want them to be.

You’ll wear each set of aligners for a specific amount of time as your teeth gently adjust and align to your ideal smile. You’ll visit our office periodically for checkups to monitor your progress until you eventually have the straight smile you’ve always wanted.

What about Online Clear Aligner Treatments?

Choosing to have orthodontics without the supervision of a qualified dentist can not only give you less-than-stellar results, but it could also be dangerous to your health. Incorrect movement of teeth can lead to jaw and bite issues as well as bone loss, which can ultimately lead to tooth loss.
