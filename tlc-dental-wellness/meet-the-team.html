Meet Dr. Mathew

Dr. Mathew graduated with honors from NYU. But, she had this strong desire to further her skill set to help patients with a variety of complex needs. Once again, her husband as her biggest cheerleader, she was inspired to take an even bigger step of pursuing her dream of becoming a Prosthodontist. As one of only two residents chosen for this competitive program, she received 3 years of advanced speciality training at the University of Pittsburgh. There she served as the chief resident and also trained dental students to treat patients with complicated dental conditions.

As a Prosthodontist, Dr.Mathew is an expert in total mouth reconstruction and has gained special understanding of the perfect balance between art and technique in the dynamics of a smile. She offers many treatment options including but not limited to crowns, bridges, veneers, dentures, surgical placement and restoration of dental implants, metal free fillings and correction of esthetic imbalances of a smile.

Dr. Mathew is known for her relentless dedication to remaining on the cutting edge of dental care practices, and we use the latest advances in dentistry, latest techniques, and finest materials because that’s what we would expect, as well. Dentists in GA are required to take 40 hours of continuing education every two YEARS for license renewal. Dr. Mathew invests hundreds and hundreds of hours into ongoing training each year.

She is one of the few Dentists in Georgia certified in conscious sedation to help her patients with dental anxiety. Does snoozing thru the procedure sound like music to your ears. Then you have come to the right place. She has completed multiple continuums, specializing in implant dentistry, cosmetic dentistry and biological dentistry. This has further elevated her cosmetic craft in full mouth rehabilitation for the more complex aesthetic cases.

While the practice specializes in biological dentistry, Dr. Mathew’s team places equal importance on proper nutrition, posture, sleep, mindset, and emotional wellness. Through an endless pursuit of knowledge, Dr. Mathew and her team are equipped to walk alongside and support their community with modern and innovative techniques that inspire holistic wellness.

Beyond TLC Dental Wellness, she is a wife to her wonderful husband Liju and a doting mother to their twin daughters, Abigail and Isabella. She and her family are members of Cornerstone Church Athens where they are growing spiritually, loves volunteering and making great friendships along the way. She loves to share stories with her family of patients, of her latest cooking experiments with her daughters, travel experiences and #momlife struggles and invites you to do the same!

WHY CHOOSE A PROSTHODONTIST FOR YOUR GENERAL & COSMETIC DENTAL NEEDS

The official description of a prosthodontist with the American Dental Association is a practitioner whose focus is the cosmetic and functional repair or reconstruction of broken, discolored, or missing teeth. This type of dentist has mastered the art of complete oral rehabilitation and can maintain the highest standard of care.

As a Prosthodontist, Dr. Mathew is an expert in total mouth reconstruction. She offers many treatment options including but not limited to crowns, bridges, veneers, dentures, surgical placement and restoration of dental implants, metal free fillings and correction of esthetic imbalances of a smile.

Dr. Mathew strongly believes in continuing education and strives to keep updating herself with the latest innovations in the dental field to provide her patients the best treatment available.
