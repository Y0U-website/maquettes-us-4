Dr. Rami Nazarian

Dr. Nazarian is a highly accomplished dentist with a wealth of experience, particularly in the area of dental implant therapy. As a Fellow of both the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and the International Team of Oral Implantologists , he has undergone extensive training in oral implantology, ensuring that he stays up to date with the latest advancements in the field.

Supporting Oral Health for Veterans

Beyond his professional achievements, Dr. Nazarian is deeply involved in giving back to the community. He believes in the importance of oral health education and hygiene for everyone. To support this cause, he donates toothbrushes, toothpaste, and floss to area Veterans on an annual basis. Dr. Nazarian’s dedication to serving those who have served our country highlights his compassion and commitment to improving oral health in the community.

Outside Office

When Dr. Nazarian isn’t brightening smiles, you can find him pursuing his diverse range of interests. He is an avid sports enthusiast and enjoys playing soccer, which not only keeps him active but also helps him foster teamwork and camaraderie. Additionally, he stays fit through activities like CrossFit and swimming.

Dr. Nazarian is also passionate about cooking. He believes that a healthy diet plays a crucial role in maintaining good oral health. His culinary skills allow him to explore different flavors and experiment with nutritious recipes that promote dental wellness. Dr. Nazarian is always happy to share his cooking recommendations with his patients, ensuring that they have the knowledge to make informed choices about the relationship between their diet and oral health!
