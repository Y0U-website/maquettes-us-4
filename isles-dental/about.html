About Us

A Modern Dental Experience with a Personal Touch

Isles Dental is more than just a dental office—we’re a part of the Uptown Minneapolis community. Our practice is designed to feel inviting, featuring a subtle nautical theme inspired by the nearby lake. This reflects our approach to dentistry: modern, clean, and effortlessly comforting.

We invest in advanced dental technology to provide precise, efficient, and long-lasting treatments. Whether it’s digital X-rays for safer imaging, clear aligners for discreet orthodontic care, or dental implants for a complete smile, we use the latest advancements to deliver top-tier results.

Meet Dr. Springer

Dr. Robert Springer leads Isles Dental with a passion for gentle, patient-focused care. His philosophy is simple: everyone deserves exceptional dentistry in a welcoming, judgment-free environment. He took over Isles Dental to continue its tradition of compassionate care while introducing modern techniques and cutting-edge treatments.
