Signs You May Need an Implant Restoration

If you notice any of the following issues, it may be time to restore your dental implant:

Our goal is to ensure your implant remains functional, secure, and aesthetically pleasing for years to come.

  • Cracked, chipped, or fractured implant crown
  • Loose or shifting implant-supported bridge or denture
  • Gum irritation or discomfort around the implant
  • Discoloration that no longer matches your natural teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking

How Long Do Implant Restorations Last?

While dental implants themselves can last a lifetime, the implant restorations may need replacement after 10–15 years due to normal wear.

To extend the lifespan of your restoration, we recommend:

With proper care, your implant restoration will remain strong and functional for years.

  • Brushing and flossing daily to prevent plaque buildup
  • Avoiding hard foods that may damage the restoration
  • Scheduling regular dental checkups for implant maintenance
  • Wearing a nightguard if you grind your teeth
