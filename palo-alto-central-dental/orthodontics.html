Retainers and Long-Term Results

Retainers are custom appliances worn after active orthodontic treatment. They help hold teeth in their new positions while the surrounding bone and tissues adjust.

Depending on your needs, a retainer may be removable or fixed behind the teeth. Your dentist will provide instructions for when to wear it, how to clean it, and when it should be checked or replaced.

To care for a removable retainer, clean it regularly, store it in its case when not in use, and keep it away from heat. Contact our team if your retainer feels uncomfortable, becomes damaged, or no longer fits properly. Consistent retainer wear is important because teeth can shift over time.
