Attaching the final crown, bridge, or denture usually does not involve the same surgical procedure used to place the implant. Your dentist will explain the process based on the implant system and your individual treatment needs.

Your treatment begins with an examination of the implant and surrounding tissues. Precise impressions or a digital scan may be taken to plan a restoration based on the size, shape, shade, and position of your surrounding teeth.
