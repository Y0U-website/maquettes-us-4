Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and we continue to monitor and improve our website.

In-Office Accessibility Reynoldsburg Family Dental, located in Reynoldsburg, Ohio, is also committed to providing accessible care at our office location.

If you require specific accommodations, we encourage you to contact us in advance so we can best prepare for your visit.

Third-Party Services Some features of our website, such as appointment scheduling or maps, may be provided by third-party vendors. While we strive to work with accessible providers, we cannot guarantee full accessibility of third-party content.

Need Assistance? If you experience difficulty accessing our website or need assistance during your visit, we are here to help.

When reaching out, please let us know how we can assist you, and we will do our best to provide the information or accommodations you need.

Feedback We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website and office. Your input helps us improve and better serve all patients.

  • Ensuring forms are accessible for appointment requests
  • Providing descriptive text for images and links
  • Supporting keyboard navigation and assistive technologies
  • Accessible parking and building entry
  • Wheelchair-accessible treatment areas
  • Assistance from our team upon request
  • Accommodations for patients with mobility, hearing, or other needs
