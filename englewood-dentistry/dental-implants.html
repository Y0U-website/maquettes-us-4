How implant treatment works, step by step

Implant treatment is planned carefully. We evaluate your bone, place the implant, allow healing time, then restore it with a custom tooth or prosthetic.

Consultation & imaging

We evaluate your gums, bone, bite, and medical history to confirm whether implants are a good fit.

Implant placement

The implant post is placed with local anesthesia and a plan designed for stable healing.

Healing & integration

Healing is monitored as the implant integrates with the jawbone. The timing varies by case.

Final restoration

A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached and adjusted for comfort and appearance.

Are dental implants right for you?

Dental implants may be a good fit if you are missing one tooth, several teeth, or want a more stable alternative to a removable denture. The best candidates usually have healthy gums, enough jawbone support, and a commitment to regular brushing, flossing, and dental visits. If bone loss, gum disease, smoking, or medical conditions are part of your history, we will talk through those factors before recommending a plan.

Patients visit us from Englewood, Teaneck, Fort Lee, Tenafly, Leonia, Hackensack, and nearby Bergen County communities for implant consultations because they want a clear explanation of their options before making a decision.

Technology, comfort, and planning

Implant care starts with diagnosis and planning. Digital imaging helps us evaluate the missing-tooth area, bite, bone support, and nearby teeth. When appropriate, we also review restorative options such as an implant crown, implant bridge, or implant-supported denture so the final result supports chewing, speech, and smile appearance.

Comfort matters throughout the process. We explain each step in advance, numb the area for treatment, and give clear home-care instructions so you know what to expect after your visit.

Healing, maintenance, and implant risks

Implant treatment involves surgery and time for healing. Infection, healing problems, and injury to nearby structures are possible; the risks depend on the implant site and your medical and dental history. Tell Dr. Ahmed about medicines, tobacco use, and health conditions before treatment so these can be considered in your plan.

Follow your procedure-specific aftercare and keep review appointments. Contact the office about worsening pain, swelling, or a loose restoration. Daily cleaning and ongoing professional maintenance remain necessary after the final tooth is fitted.
