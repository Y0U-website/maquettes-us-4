What to Expect: Your Invisalign Process

Your path to a straighter smile begins with an initial Invisalign consultation. During this session, we will evaluate your dental structure, discusses your goals, and assesses your suitability for Invisalign treatment.

Following your consultation, we create a custom treatment plan tailored to your dental needs. This plan is crafted using advanced 3D imaging technology so you can visualize the step-by-step transformation of your smile. Dr. Weitz, will provide a clear timeline and expectations for the treatment.

Once your first set of Invisalign aligners is ready, you’ll return to Pearly Whites Dental Care for the fitting. Our team will guarantee a perfect fit and instruct you on how to wear and care for your aligners.

Throughout your Invisalign treatment, you’ll have regular appointments with our team for monitoring and adjustments. These check-ups are essential to track your progress and make any necessary adjustments to your treatment plan.

Maintaining your new smile is important after completing your Invisalign treatment. We provide post-treatment retainers to ensure your teeth stay in their new positions. Our dental professionals will guide you on how to use and care for your retainers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can anyone choose Invisalign braces over traditional braces?

Invisalign is suitable for a wide range of dental misalignments, including crowded teeth, gaps, and certain bite issues, but some complex conditions may require traditional braces for better results. During your Invisalign consultation at Pearly Whites Dental Care, we will evaluate your teeth, gums, and overall oral health to determine if Invisalign is the right choice for you.

Insurance coverage for Invisalign treatment varies depending on your dental insurance plan. Many insurance plans that cover orthodontic treatments also provide coverage for Invisalign to a similar extent as traditional braces. We recommend checking with your insurance provider to understand your coverage details. Our office staff is happy to assist you in exploring financing options to make your Invisalign treatment possible.

How long does Invisalign take to get results?

On average, Invisalign treatment can take 6 to 18 months to achieve desired results. During your initial Invisalign consultation, Dr. Weitz will provide you with an estimated timeline based on the complexity of your case and how often you wear your aligners.
