What to Expect: Your Porcelain Veneer Process

This critical first step assesses whether porcelain veneers are right for your needs. Our expert team will check for any underlying issues such as gum disease or tooth decay to make sure your teeth are healthy and ready for the application of dental veneers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Porcelain veneers last anywhere from 10 to 15 years. To maximize the longevity of your porcelain veneers, we recommend regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene practices, and avoiding damaging habits like biting on hard objects.

Is the dental veneer procedure comfortable?

Our process of applying porcelain veneers is designed to be as comfortable as possible. Most patients report feeling little to no sensation during the procedure. Some might experience slight sensitivity in the days following the procedure as their mouth adjusts to the new veneers, but this usually subsides quickly.

What are the risks or potential side effects of getting dental veneers?

While dental veneers are a safe and effective way to enhance your smile, there are some risks and potential side effects to be aware of. These include sensitivity to hot and cold temperatures due to the enamel removal process. There’s also a small risk of chipping or cracking your veneers, especially if you grind your teeth, bite your nails, or chew on hard objects. In rare cases, veneers can become dislodged. However, these risks can be minimized with proper application by an experienced dentist like Dr. Weitz.
