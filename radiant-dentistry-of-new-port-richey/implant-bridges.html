What Are Implant Bridges?

Missing multiple teeth in a row can be solved with implant bridges. This means that instead of natural teeth to support them, they are secured with implants in the jaw. So, it creates a firm, strong base for the bridge.

Bridges supported by implants do not affect adjacent teeth and preserve bone density, unlike traditional bridges. Many patients can trust them for a long time.

Why You May Need Implant Bridges

Tooth loss affects more than just your appearance. It can lead to bone loss, shifting teeth, and difficulty chewing. Implant bridges help prevent these problems while restoring full function.

If you are dealing with missing teeth and looking for implant bridges near you, this treatment offers a fixed, non-removable solution that feels natural and secure.
