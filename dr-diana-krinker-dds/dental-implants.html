Dental implants offer a modern solution for those missing one or several teeth, providing a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Unlike other Tooth Replacement Options , dental implants offer unparalleled stability and longevity, making them the preferred choice for restoring your smile at Omni Dental Care.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Choosing dental implants means opting for a lifetime of smiles and confidence. The Benefits of Dental Implants extend beyond aesthetics; they prevent bone loss, maintain facial structure, and eliminate the discomfort associated with removable dentures. With dental implants, you can enjoy your favorite foods and smile without hesitation, knowing your teeth are secure.

The Dental Implant Procedure Explained

Embarking on your dental implant journey involves several key steps, meticulously carried out in our Brooklyn office. The Dental Implant Procedure begins with a thorough evaluation, followed by the precise placement of the implant post directly into the jawbone. For patients requiring Bone Grafting for Implants, we offer comprehensive pre-treatment to ensure optimal outcomes. After a healing period, during which the implant fuses with the jawbone, a custom-made crown or bridge is securely attached, completing your smile restoration.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Maintaining your dental implants is straightforward and crucial for their longevity. Dental Implant Care involves regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups, much like your natural teeth. We&#x27;ll provide you with all the tips and tools you need to keep your new smile shining bright.
