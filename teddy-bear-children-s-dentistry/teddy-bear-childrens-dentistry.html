Mission Statement

Our mission at Teddy Bear Pediatric Dentistry is to provide comprehensive and compassionate dental care to children in our West Hills community, while promoting oral health education and prevention to families. We strive to create a positive and welcoming environment for our patients and their families, and to foster long-term relationships built on trust and exceptional service.

Our practice has a reputation for offering comprehensive pediatric dental care in a friendly, inviting, and professional environment. When you visit our practice, you’re not just another smile – you are family! We practice with compassion, treating all of our patients with respect and individuality as each patient and family has different needs.
