Accessibility Statement

  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Browser zoom up to 200 %
  • High-contrast display modes
  • Reduced-motion settings

Specific features

  • Skip-to-content links for keyboard users to bypass navigation
  • Descriptive alt text on all informational images
  • Semantic HTML with proper headings and landmarks
  • Visible focus indicators so keyboard users can see where they are
  • Color contrast that meets or exceeds WCAG AA minimums
  • ARIA labels on icon-only buttons and links

Known limitations

We’re honest about what we’re still working on:

  • Our embedded Google Map has limited keyboard accessibility. Alternate directions and address are provided as text on the Contact page.
  • Our online booking is handled by a third-party provider (NexHealth). If that tool presents accessibility barriers, call us and we’ll book your appointment by phone.

Office accessibility

Our West Chester office is designed to welcome patients with a range of needs:

  • Wheelchair accessible — ground-floor entry, accessible parking, and treatment rooms sized to accommodate mobility devices
  • Sensory considerations — we try to keep the waiting area calm, and we can dim lights or reduce noise on request
  • Communication accommodations — if you need extra time, written materials, or specific communication support, just tell us when you book and we’ll prepare
  • Sedation options — for patients with significant dental anxiety or specific needs, we can discuss sedation approaches

Requesting accommodations

If you need any accommodation for your visit — mobility, sensory, communication, or anything else — please let us know when you schedule. We’d rather know in advance so we can prepare, but we’ll do our best to accommodate walk-in needs too.
