Six hygienists who actually know your mouth.

Most of our hygienists have been with the practice long-term. They are real professionals — skilled, calm, patient. Many of our patients see the same hygienist visit after visit, year after year. That continuity matters more than people realize.

A hygienist who has cleaned your teeth for ten years knows where the trouble spots are. She knows which areas you tend to miss with floss. She remembers that you grind at night and watches for signs of progression. She knows when something looks different than last visit, even subtly. That is preventive care done at a level you do not get when the hygienist is always new.

Low staff turnover is one of the quiet signals of a well-run practice. It is also one of the things that makes long-term care actually work.

Preventive questions.

  • Do you see kids? Yes — we see patients of all ages. Some patients assume we are adults-only because of how the practice looks, but we are a family practice. Pediatric cleanings, exams, fluoride treatments, and sealants are part of what we do. For complex pediatric cases (significant behavioral needs, very young children with extensive treatment plans), we coordinate with a trusted pediatric dentist.
  • How often should I come in for a cleaning? For most adults, every 6 months is right. For higher-risk patients (gum disease history, dry mouth from medications, history of decay), we recommend every 3-4 months. The right interval is the one that keeps your mouth healthy — we tell you what is right for you specifically rather than apply the same answer to everyone.
  • Do I really need X-rays every year? Bitewing X-rays are typically every 1-2 years for healthy adults, more frequently for higher-risk patients. Panoramic X-rays are typically every 5 years. We do not take X-rays routinely — we take them when they will change what we recommend. Our digital X-rays use significantly less radiation than traditional film.
  • What happens at a new patient visit? We combine the exam and cleaning into a single visit (most other practices split this into two). You meet your hygienist, do a comprehensive exam including oral cancer screening, sleep apnea and TMJ screening, take any needed X-rays, get a thorough cleaning, and review findings with the doctor. About 60-90 minutes.
  • What does it cost? Most preventive care is covered at 100 % by PPO dental insurance plans — including 2 cleanings, exams, and X-rays per year, plus sealants for kids. We are in-network with most major PPOs and submit claims on your behalf. For patients without insurance, our in-house benefit plan starts at $229/year and includes preventive care plus discounts on other treatment.
  • What if I have anxiety about dental visits? Anxiety is common and we equip the office for it. Wireless headphones with your choice of music. AR-style movie glasses (similar to XReal glasses) for watching a show during the visit. iPads for visualization so we can show you what we are seeing. Our hygienists are patient and never rushed. If you have not been to a dentist in years and are nervous about coming back, tell us when you call — we can structure the first visit to make it easier.
  • Do you do deep cleanings (scaling and root planing)? Yes. For patients with active gum disease, we perform scaling and root planing in-house. This is a deeper cleaning that goes below the gumline. Often split across multiple visits with local anesthesia. After active treatment, we put patients on a 3-4 month periodontal maintenance schedule.
  • Do you screen for oral cancer? Yes — every routine exam includes an oral cancer screening. We examine the soft tissues of the mouth, throat, and neck for any unusual changes. Early detection dramatically improves outcomes. It is a quick exam that has saved patient lives over the years.
  • Do you make night guards or sports mouthguards? Yes to both. Custom night guards for grinding or clenching (a common cause of sensitivity and worn teeth). Custom sports mouthguards for kids and adults playing contact sports — far more protective than boil-and-bite versions. Both fabricated from a digital iTero scan.
