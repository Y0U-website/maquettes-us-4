Try not to eat hard or crunchy foods like:

If you want to eat these foods, cut them into very small pieces and only chew the pieces with your back teeth:

  • Candy bars with caramel
  • Fruit Roll-Ups
  • Candy or caramel apples
  • Skittles
  • Starbursts
  • Toffee
  • Gummy Bears
  • Pizza Crust
  • Nuts
  • Hard Candy
  • Corn Chips
  • Ice Cubes
  • Bagels
  • Popcorn Kernels
  • Apples
  • Carrots
  • Corn on the Cob
  • Pizza
  • Pears
  • Celery
  • Chicken Wings
  • Spare Ribs
