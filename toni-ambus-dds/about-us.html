dr. toni ambus

Dr. Toni Ambus graduated from San Francisco State University in 1991 with a Bachelor of Arts in Biology. In 1995, she earned a Bachelor of Science in Dental Sciences and a Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of California San Francisco (UCSF). She continues to change and update her offices with the newest dental procedures and equipment, always assuring patients and providing the best service possible.

ashleigh (Front desk)

Ashleigh was born in San Francisco and raised in Pacifica. She played soccer from age six through high school (CYSA, AYSO, FutSal, indoor). Ashleigh attended Nova High School then went on to Skyline Community College to get an AA in Liberal Studies. She is currently a candidate through the University of Phoenix for a Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration with an emphasis in HR. Ashleigh’s hobbies include crafting, cooking hiking and travelling. She is also a loyal SF Giants and 49ers fan. Ashleigh has been with the dental practice since 2011.

christine (registered dental hygienist)

Christine earned her Bachelor of Sciences degree in Biological Sciences with an emphasis in Neurology, Physiology and Behavior at UC Davis. She later earned her Associate Degree in Dental Hygiene at Western College. Christine grew up in San Francisco and loves city life. When she isn’t cleaning teeth, she enjoys taking walks and spending quality time with her family and friends. Christine has been with the dental practice since 2008.

joy (registered dental hygienist)

Joy is a first-generation San Franciscan. She attended SFSU and then got her Dental Hygiene degree from UCSF in 1999. Joy enjoys traveling, baking and staying active by playing tennis, bike riding and taking fitness classes. She has been with the dental practice since 2011.
