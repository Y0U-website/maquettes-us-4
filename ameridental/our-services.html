🏥 Restorative Dentistry

What is involved in tooth restorations?

Tooth restorations include fillings, inlays/onlays, and bonding to repair damage or decay, restoring both function and appearance.

What are crowns and bridges, and how long do they take?

  • Crowns : Caps that cover and strengthen damaged teeth.
  • Bridges : Appliances that replace missing teeth, anchored to adjacent teeth. Most crown and bridge procedures can be completed in one appointment using ceramic restorations.

😁 Cosmetic Dentistry

How can cosmetic treatments improve my smile?

We offer porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, and aesthetic bonding to enhance the color, shape, and alignment of your teeth—giving your smile a natural and beautiful upgrade.v

What are porcelain veneers?

  • Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to improve appearance and mask imperfections like discoloration, chips, or minor gaps.

🦷 Periodontal & Surgical Services

What is "parodontosis" treatment?

Also known as periodontal disease care—early intervention includes deep cleanings (scaling and root planing) and possibly regenerative therapies to prevent tooth loss.

📸 Diagnostic & Imaging Services

What is dental radiography?

We use digital X-rays to diagnose cavities, gum disease, bone loss, and other issues effectively, with minimal radiation exposure.
