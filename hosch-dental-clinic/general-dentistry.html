General dental care that helps you stay ahead of problems

General dentistry focuses on preventing, finding, and treating common oral health problems.

At Hosch Dental Clinic, that means helping you protect healthy teeth, repair damage when it happens, and understand what your smile needs before agreeing to treatment.

Cleanings & Exams

Routine dental cleanings and exams help remove plaque and tartar and give Dr. Hosch an opportunity to check your teeth, gums, and bite for developing problems.

Catching concerns early can often mean simpler treatment.

Dental Fillings

A cavity does not get better on its own. A dental filling removes and repairs the damaged area before decay can spread further into the tooth.

Hosch Dental Clinic offers tooth-colored composite fillings designed to restore function while blending with your natural smile.

Dental Crowns

A dental crown can protect a tooth that has been weakened by decay, a crack, a large filling, or other damage. The crown covers the affected tooth to restore its shape, strength, and everyday function.

Dental Bridges

Missing a tooth can affect how you chew and how the surrounding teeth fit together. A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth with a fixed restoration supported by neighboring teeth.

Not sure whether a bridge or another tooth replacement option makes sense? Dr. Hosch can review your options with you before you decide.

Gum & Bone Disease Treatment

Bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, swollen gums, or loose teeth can be signs of gum disease. Hosch Dental Clinic provides gum and bone disease treatment to help control infection and protect the tissues and bone that support your teeth.

Emergency Dentistry

Sudden tooth pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or a lost restoration may need attention sooner than your next routine visit. Hosch Dental Clinic offers same-day emergency dental appointments in Camp Springs when availability allows.
