About Dr. Hayley Botwin

Board-Certified Pediatric Dentists 9+ Years Serving Culver City Most PPOs Accepted Se Habla Español Dr. Hayley Botwin Dr. Hayley Botwin brings a unique background to our team, and if you’ve been coming to our office for a few years, you might remember her working as a hygienist dating back to 2019.

Dr. Hayley Botwin was born and raised in Southern California. She jetted off to the east coast to earn her first Bachelor’s degree at Boston University. She spent years working as a Camp Director at a residential summer camp and enjoying her time working with children. Wanting to continue her education, Dr. Hayley Botwin returned home to earn a second Bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene at USC before pursuing her dental degree and later completing her Pediatric dental residency.

Dr. Hayley’s Credentials

Your child’s first visit is really about you as much as it is about them. We’ll show you what to look for at home, answer all those questions you’ve been Googling at midnight, and help your toddler get comfortable with our team.

After graduating from USC Dental School in 2023, Dr. Hayley Botwin completed the prestigious USC Pediatric residency program, focusing exclusively on children’s dental needs.

What Makes Her Different

Dr. Hayley Botwin worked here before becoming a dentist. She already knows our families and understands the culture we’ve built. She shares our philosophy of prevention over treatment and takes the same minimally invasive approach we’re known for.

When treatment is needed, she explains everything clearly and helps you make decisions based on what’s best for your child. Recommendations she makes focus on your child’s long-term oral health.

Her USC pediatric residency training equipped her with advanced techniques, and she uses them thoughtfully. She understands subtle approaches often work better with young children, and patience typically leads to better outcomes than rushing.

Building Trust With Families

Dr. Hayley Botwin knows you probably have questions about bottles, thumb-sucking, or when to start flossing. She provides clear answers based on your child’s age and development.

Dr. Hayley Botwin values parent education because confident parents can help their children adopt healthy oral health habits.
