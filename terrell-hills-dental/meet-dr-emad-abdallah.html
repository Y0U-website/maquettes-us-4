Meet Dr. Emad Abdallah, D.M.D.

Boston University DMD &#x26; Five Years of Practice in Detroit

Dr. Abdallah earned his DMD from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2017. He then spent five years practicing in Detroit, managing a wide range of restorative and implant cases. That experience prepared him to handle complex situations and deliver consistent results.

Areas of Clinical Focus

Dr. Abdallah places and restores dental implants, from single tooth replacements to full mouth reconstructions. Using an in-house lab and 3D CT scanning, he plans each case precisely.
