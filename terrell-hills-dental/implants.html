What Happens When a Missing Tooth Goes Untreated

Leaving a gap in your smile may seem like a minor concern, but the effects extend beyond appearance.

The Consequences of Waiting

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to resorb because it no longer receives stimulation from the root. Within the first year, up to 25 % of bone volume can be lost. Nearby teeth start to shift into the empty space, leading to bite problems, crooked teeth, and increased risk of decay and gum disease. Over time, the bite can collapse, changing your facial shape and making chewing difficult.

As bone loss progresses, it becomes harder to place implants later without additional grafting. That is why addressing a missing tooth sooner rather than later gives you the best chance for a straightforward implant procedure.

If you have already waited, don’t worry. Our bone grafting and ridge augmentation procedures can rebuild what has been lost, restoring your candidacy for dental implants.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in San Antonio

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with healthy gums and enough bone density to support an implant are candidates. If bone loss is present, procedures like bone grafting or sinus lift can often create a solid foundation. We evaluate each patient individually using 3D CT imaging to confirm candidacy.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The timeline varies by case. A single tooth implant typically takes three to six months from placement to final crown, including healing time. Same-day teeth options allow placement of a temporary restoration on the same visit. Dr. Abdallah will give you a personalized timeline based on your treatment plan.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage depends on your plan. Many dental insurance policies offer partial coverage for implant procedures, especially for the restoration. We accept major insurers including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, and others, and our team will help verify your benefits before treatment.

Does getting dental implants hurt?

The procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during placement. Most patients report mild soreness for a few days afterward, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. We also offer sedation options through our Comfort Menu for patients who feel anxious.
