A dental bone graft is a procedure that restores lost bone volume in the jaw, creating a solid foundation for dental implant placement. When a tooth is lost or extracted, the surrounding bone begins to resorb because it no longer has the tooth root to stimulate it. Over time, this bone loss can make implant placement difficult or impossible without grafting.

Common Causes of Jaw Bone Loss That Affect Implant Candidacy

Jawbone loss can happen for several reasons. Periodontal (gum) disease is one of the most common causes, as it destroys the bone that supports your teeth. Tooth extraction or injury that results in tooth loss also triggers bone resorption. Other causes include untreated cavities that lead to infection, dentures that accelerate bone loss over years of wear, and natural aging. If any of these apply to you, a dental bone graft in San Antonio may help restore your implant candidacy .

What Happens If You Skip the Bone Graft?

Choosing not to have a bone graft when one is needed carries real risks. Without enough bone, an implant may lack the stability required to integrate properly. This can lead to implant failure and continued bone loss at the site. The graft is not an added hurdle. It is the step that makes your implant investment possible and lasting.

Why Bone Grafting Matters for Long-Term Implant Success

Bone grafting is not just about making room for an implant. It is about creating the biological conditions that allow an implant to fuse with your jawbone and stay stable for decades. Patients considering a bone graft for dental implants San Antonio deserve a team that combines skill with precision technology. Those who receive a graft when needed have a stronger foundation, better aesthetic outcomes, and a lower risk of long-term complications.

What to Expect During Your Bone Graft Procedure in San Antonio

If you are scheduled for a bone graft, knowing what to expect can help you feel prepared and confident.

Step-by-Step: From Consultation Through Healing

Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam and 3D CT scan to evaluate your jawbone. Dr. Abdallah reviews the images with you, explaining whether a bone graft is needed and what type of graft material will be used. On the day of the procedure, the area is numbed with local anesthesia. The graft material, which may come from a donor source, synthetic material, or your own bone, is placed at the implant site. The site is then closed with sutures, and you receive aftercare instructions. Most patients heal over 3 to 6 months before the implant can be placed.

Sedation and Comfort Options for Your Procedure

We offer several sedation options to keep you comfortable during your bone graft procedure. Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) helps take the edge off while you remain awake. Oral conscious sedation allows you to relax more deeply. IV sedation is available for patients who prefer to be fully relaxed throughout the procedure. Our Comfort Menu also includes pillows, blankets, and headphones to help you feel at ease.

Bone Grafting vs. Ridge Augmentation — Which Do You Need?

Both bone grafting and ridge augmentation restore bone in the jaw, but they address different patterns of bone loss. Understanding the difference helps you know what to expect from your treatment plan.

Your dentist will determine which procedure fits your needs based on your 3D CT scan. Both are designed to restore your jaw to a state that supports successful implant placement. If you have been recommended a ridge augmentation , we can explain how it compares to bone grafting for your specific case. For patients needing additional bone height in the upper jaw, we also offer sinus lift procedures.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Bone Grafts in San Antonio

How long does a dental bone graft take to heal?

Most patients need 3 to 6 months of healing before the bone graft is stable enough to support a dental implant. During this time, new bone grows and fuses with the graft material. Your doctor will monitor your progress with imaging to confirm the site is ready.

Is a bone graft always required before dental implants?

Not everyone needs a bone graft. If you have sufficient healthy jawbone, implants can often be placed directly. A comprehensive exam and 3D CT scan at Terrell Hills Dental will determine whether grafting is needed.

Does insurance cover bone grafting for dental implants?

Coverage varies by plan and the reason for the procedure. Many insurance plans cover at least part of the bone graft when it is needed for restorative dental treatment. We accept most major insurance plans and invite you to check your insurance and financing options with us.

How much does a dental bone graft cost in San Antonio?

The cost depends on the type and amount of graft material needed and the complexity of the procedure. We provide a detailed cost estimate during your consultation and offer flexible financing options.

Will the bone graft procedure be painful?

The procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia, so you will not feel pain during the surgery. Most patients report only mild discomfort afterward, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. If you have anxiety about the procedure, we offer sedation options to help you feel calm and comfortable.
