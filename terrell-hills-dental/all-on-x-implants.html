Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Implants in San Antonio

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, the implants themselves are designed to last 20 years or more. The restoration (the bridge that attaches to the implants) may need replacement after 10 to 15 years of normal wear. Good oral hygiene and routine checkups help protect your investment.

Can I get All-on-4 implants if I have bone loss?

Yes. All-on-4 was developed specifically for patients with moderate bone loss. By angling the two rear implants, we can often avoid the need for bone grafting. During your consultation, 3D CT imaging will confirm whether you have enough bone for placement. In more severe cases, bone grafting may be recommended first.

Is the All-on-4 procedure painful?

The procedure itself is performed under sedation, so you will not feel discomfort. We offer oral conscious sedation, IV sedation, and nitrous oxide to keep you relaxed. After surgery, any soreness is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most patients return to normal activities within a few days.

How soon will I have teeth after surgery?

In most cases, you receive a provisional set of teeth the same day as implant placement. These temporary teeth allow you to eat soft foods and smile while your implants heal and integrate with the bone. Your final, custom-crafted restoration is placed once healing is complete, usually within three to six months.
