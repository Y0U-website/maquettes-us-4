Dental Services

CEREC Crowns

With CEREC technology, we no longer take physical impressions for crowns and send them to a lab. Instead, we use a 3-D camera to create a virtual model. Once the images are taken, they are transmitted to CEREC milling unit which is like a 3-D printer. With a few minor adjustments, your crown is seated, dry, and ready for your convenience.

Sleep Apnea Devices

The Elastic Mandibular Advancement (EMA) appliance is a custom-fit, removable oral device FDA-cleared to treat snoring and mild-to-moderate obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). It works by gently pulling the lower jaw forward and opening the bite, which tightens airway tissues, prevents throat collapse, and allows unobstructed breathing

Implants

For many people, implants are a great option to replace lost teeth which offers a smile that looks and feels natural. Dr. Jenny Filter has been restoring implants for more than 20 years and has completed more than 300 hours of additional education in both the surgical and restorative phases of implants. Implants are an amazing alternative and can be life changing.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers can correct imperfections with teeth that are worn or chipped, severely discolored, misshapen, crooked or unevenly spaced. They are very thin pieces of durable porcelain that are bonded to the front of the teeth. We work with you to create custom-made veneers that are shaped and colored to your satisfaction to create a uniform, white, and beautiful smile.

Whitening

Whitening is an ideal way to enhance the natural beauty of your smile and can be achieved with our in-home procedure. We have available whitening kits to take home with you that whiten teeth in just 2-3 days.
