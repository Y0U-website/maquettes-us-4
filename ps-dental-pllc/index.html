The practice

Vision: To be a forever dental home where hope replaces fear, compassion replaces judgment, and a trip to our office is one of the best parts of your day.

Preventive dentistry is dental care that helps maintain oral health throughout your life. Good oral health can also impact your general health. Preventative dental care combines regular dental checkups and good habits like brushing and flossing. Taking care of your teeth starts at birth and extends throughout your life.

Focuses on eliminating bacteria to lower infection of the gingiva (gums) to stop periodontal disease from advancing and destroying bone.

Restorative dentistry requires your dentist to keep your mouth healthy and functioning. The most common procedures are filling cavities (caries), protecting teeth with crowns (caps), and replacing teeth with implants, bridges, partials, or dentures.

Focuses on improving the appearance of your mouth with veneers and whitening.
