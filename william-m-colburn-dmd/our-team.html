Dr. Colburn, a native of Tuscaloosa, graduated from Holt High School in 1981. He received his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Alabama in 1985. During his collegiate career, he was a member of the Million Dollar Band 1981-83 and a cheerleader 1983-85.

Terri was born and raised in Tuscaloosa and lives in Northport with her husband of over 40 years, Bubba. They have two children and six grandchildren. Terri has been in dentistry for over 35 years, as a hygienist and assistant, and has been with our practice for over 30 years. Her expertise and skills are unmatched. In her free time, Terri enjoys spending time with her family.

Andrea will be the first smiling face you will see when you enter our office. She has been in dentistry for over 30 years and with our practice for over 10 years. In her spare time, she enjoys watching the Tide, traveling, and shopping.

She also has her special pug, Peaches!
