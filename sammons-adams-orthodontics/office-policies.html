Appointments

We make every effort to schedule our patients so they are not kept waiting and to allow sufficient time and attention necessary for the highest quality of care. To accomplish this, it is unrealistic to assume that appointments will always avoid conflict with an individual’s school or work schedule. Be assured that we will make every effort to be equitable in sharing "prime time" appointments.

Please try to give us 24 hours notice if you are unable to make your appointment, and we will try to reschedule it as soon as possible.
