Dental Implants

Dental implants are a great way to replace lost or missing teeth to provide form and function.

A dental implant is essentially a screw that is placed into bone and then integrated to feel and act like the root of the tooth. A crown is then placed over the screw once the implant is stable within the mouth.

Dental implants are a great alternative to partial or removable dentures , giving patients the confidence to smile again.
