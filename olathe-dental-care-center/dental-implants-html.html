What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small posts placed into your jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Unlike traditional methods that rely on support from your gums or other healthy teeth, implants become a part of your mouth. Once healed, implant fixtures can support a variety of restorations, including crowns, bridges, or dentures that look and function just like natural teeth. With proper care, these prosthetics can stand the test of time, often lasting decades.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implant treatment seems complex, but we break it down into a simple four-step process:

  • Consultation and Planning: We begin with a consultation and digital imaging session to evaluate your jawbone health and plan out your placement.
  • Dental Implant Surgery: Next, one of our dentists will oversee your implant surgery. Here, your fixtures are surgically placed into your jawbone.
  • Osseointegration and Healing: Over the course of a few months, your implants fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Delivery of Restorations: Once healed, an abutment and final restoration such as a crown, bridge, or denture are attached, restoring your smile to like-new.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are considered the gold standard of tooth replacement for a reason. Here are some of the great benefits you can expect:

  • Strong, reliable stability that helps eating and speaking feel natural.
  • They support bone health and help prevent nearby teeth from shifting.
  • A lifelike appearance that lets them blend seamlessly with your smile.
  • With proper care, implants can last for many years, often decades or even a lifetime.
  • Restore confidence, allowing you to feel good about smiling in a variety of social situations.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

Dental implants can help patients missing one tooth, several teeth, or even an entire dental arch. They’re a great option for those looking for stability, comfort, and a natural feel. During your consultation, we’ll take our time and figure out if implants fit your oral health needs before moving forward.

Missing One Tooth

A single dental implant can replace one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth at all. This technique fills the small gap and restores your appearance while helping preserve your jawbone underneath.

Missing Multiple Teeth

Implants can support bridges to replace multiple missing teeth in a row, offering more stability than traditional options. Implant bridges also don’t rely on shaping healthy teeth to accommodate the prosthetic, so you keep more of your natural smile while gaining the rewards of dental implants in Olathe.

Missing All Teeth

Implant dentures and All-on-X solutions provide a secure, full-arch tooth replacement for patients missing all teeth. This is a fantastic alternative to traditional options that rely on muscle control and suction for their stability. With implant dentures, you’ll be able to enjoy all your favorite foods without worrying about slippage.
