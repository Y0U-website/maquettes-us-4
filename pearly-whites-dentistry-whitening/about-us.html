About Pearly Whites

OUR STORY

Pearly Whites was created in Myrtle Beach, SC to provide affordable, modern dental care in a welcoming and patient-focused environment. From preventive care and hygiene services to fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, and whitening treatments, our mission is to help every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Myrtle Beach s Modern Approach to Dental Care

The Pearly Whites Difference

At Pearly Whites, we believe quality dental care should be accessible, comfortable, and affordable. Our practice combines modern technology, experienced professionals, and personalized treatment plans to help patients maintain healthy smiles for life.

Whether you re visiting for a routine cleaning, dental exam, filling, crown, bridge, denture, or professional whitening treatment, our team is committed to providing exceptional care in a relaxed environment.

Our Team

Our Pearly Whites team is here to make dental care feel simple, comfortable, and affordable. From routine cleanings and whitening to restorative treatments, we help patients in Myrtle Beach and Carolina Forest feel confident about their smiles.

Our Mission

At Pearly Whites, our mission is to make quality dental care more accessible, affordable, and convenient for our community.

We believe everyone deserves a healthy smile, which is why we provide comprehensive dental services including preventive care, dental cleanings, exams, fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, whitening treatments, and oral health education.

Our goal is to help patients achieve lasting oral health through personalized care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Our Commitment

Pearly Whites Dental Studio proudly serves patients throughout Carolina Forest, Myrtle Beach, Conway, Forestbrook, Socastee, and surrounding South Carolina communities.

Our services include dental cleanings, exams, digital X-rays, fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, gum therapy, preventive dentistry, and professional teeth whitening.

We accept most major insurance plans and are committed to making quality dental care affordable and accessible for every patient.
