Our office is outfitted with the most current dental technology. We proudly offer all digital x-rays to best serve your needs and keeping you and your family safe.

X-rays are an important tool for diagnosing and treating dental health problems. The NOMAD Pro 2 hand-held x-ray system allows us to capture intraoral images with ease right from your chair, which make it ideal for children. Advanced safety features include minimal exposure to radiation.

This complete maxillofacial x-ray unit allows us to capture a wide variety of 2D images, including panoramic x-rays, bitewing images, and sinus images. With horizontal and vertical segmentation, your radiation dose is reduced by up to 90 % compared to full panoramic exposure.

Dr. Ives is passionate about providing dental care to the less fortunate. He has participated in Free Dentistry Days every year since graduating dental school. This program provides one dental service at no cost to everyone who attends.

Since 2008, he has been a volunteer with Donated Dental Services . This service provides comprehensive dental care persons who lack adequate income to pay for dental care and either have a permanent disability, are elderly (age 65 or older) or qualify as medically fragile.
