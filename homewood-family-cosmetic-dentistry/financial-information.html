Multiple Payment Options

We offer you several financial options in order to provide you with impeccable service. Aside from our standard credit card payments, we also offer you Cherry. This card is designed to assist in paying for procedures your current insurance provider may not cover. Click here for more information .

Pay Ahead of Time

All of our patients at Homewood Family & Cosmetic Dentistry are required to make financial arrangements prior to their dental procedure. We will not perform any treatments until your method of payment has been determined. We also require that all of your dental services be paid for at the time of service.

We’re Happy to Help You

If you carry dental insurance, we’re more than happy to assist with preparing insurance forms or collecting from insurance companies. Please understand that we will not perform services simply by assuming payments will be made later.

How to Avoid Fees

You will be subject to a fee if you change your appointment with less than a 24-hour notice or if you’re a no-show. Appointments are considered confirmed when they are made, however, we will attempt to remind you of your appointment the day before as a courtesy.
