Dental Procedures

Porcelain Veneers - In the event, your tooth is discolored or you have a general problem with the appearance of your teeth, we offer ceramic veneers.

Power Bleaching - If your teeth are discolored, we also offer the option of power bleaching. Power bleaching is a technique done in-office using a laser bleaching gel. The process is similar to the traditional bleaching process except the results will, typically, come much quicker.

Porcelain Crowns and Bridges - Crowns and bridges are ideal choices for a number of different tooth issues. One of those occasions is when a tooth has broken and is, then, inflicted by decay. Another occasion is when you have a missing tooth that is surrounded by two teeth. You also have a few different options when it comes to the creation of your crown or bridge.

Dental Implant Restoration - Implants are ideal in the event you have lost a tooth, whether it is due to an accident, disease or decay. Our implants are made to blend perfectly with the rest of your teeth by both color and shape. We also offer implant-supported bridges in the event you are missing several teeth in the same area.

Partials - If you are missing several teeth in various areas of the mouth or you have a large area of the mouth without teeth, we offer partial dentures. A metal or plastic piece is custom made to fit your mouth, replacing those gaps with plastic teeth. This procedure will not only restore your mouth to full functionality, it will also give you a beautiful smile you can be proud of.
