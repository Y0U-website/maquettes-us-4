Urgent Dental Procedure Pricing

Payment Options

Payment is expected at the time services are performed.

For your convenience, we accept cash and most major credit cards.

We add a 3 % Surcharge for Credit Cards, and no Surcharge for Debit Cards.

We also offer financing through Cherry Payments & CareCredit .

Insurance

Now Accepting the Essence Spending Flex Card

We accept insurance from Delta Dental Premier , Guardian PPO, Aetna PPO, Metlife PPO, and Cigna PPO . Scroll down for details.

Patients with insurances other than above are welcome to be seen at our office as a non-insurance "Self Pay" patient with the fees below.

Insurance

We accept insurance from Delta Dental Premier , Guardian, Aetna, Metlife PPO and Cigna PPO .

Essence Spending Flex Card

We are NOW ACCEPTING the Essence Spending Flex Card, which is provided by Medicare plans.

We remain UNABLE to accept state-sponsored Medicaid or Medicare plans; this is strictly a card loaded by t hese plan s to use as cash wherever insurance coverage is not available.

Delta Dental Premier

We ARE IN NETWORK with Delta Dental Premier. Delta Dental Premier patient’s fees are determined by their specific plan.

Cigna PPO

We are OUT OF NETWORK with Cigna PPO. But will accept and submit claims for patients with Cigna PPO Insurance. At the time of service, we will look up your insurance coverage and you will only be responsible for your estimated deductible and co-insurance. We will then submit a claim on your behalf to Cigna PPO.

However, it is always the responsibility of the patient to fully understand their coverage. Any remaining balances after insurance processes the claim are the patient’s responsibility.
