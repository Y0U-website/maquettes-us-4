What is Dental Implants?

Dental implant is a procedure that replaces tooth roots with metal, screwlike posts and replaces damaged or missing teeth with artificial teeth that look and function much like real ones.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants do not simply fill in the gaps in missing teeth. This will give you more benefits than you might think.

  • Worry-free from Cavities - Materials made from dental implants do not cause cavities. You will never worry about getting cavities in your dental implants!
  • Easy Care - Dental implants do not require special care. Just take care of your teeth as you usually would.
  • Protect your healthy teeth - If there is a gap between your teeth, the position of your healthy teeth is not stable. This will protect your healthy teeth.
  • Permanent Solution - Dental implants are designed to last for the rest of your entire life.
  • Beatiful Smile - This is exactly what your natural teeth look like. Dental implants will bring back your perfect smile.
