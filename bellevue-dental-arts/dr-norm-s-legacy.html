Dr. Norman Chiang’s Legacy at Bellevue Dental Arts

For nearly 50 years, Dr. Norman Chiang had the privilege of caring for patients throughout the Bellevue community. Known for his gentle touch, compassionate approach, and commitment to clinical excellence, he built lasting relationships with generations of patients and helped shape Bellevue Dental Arts into the practice it is today.

After earning his degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 1974, Dr. Chiang dedicated his career to providing exceptional patient care while continually expanding his knowledge through advanced education and active involvement in professional organizations. He also generously volunteered his time and expertise with Northwest Medical Teams, helping provide dental care to underserved members of the community.

Although Dr. Chiang has retired from practicing, his influence continues to be reflected in the values and patient-centered philosophy that remain at the heart of Bellevue Dental Arts. Our team is proud to carry forward the standard of compassionate, personalized care that he helped establish, ensuring every patient continues to receive the exceptional experience they have come to expect.

Thank you, Dr. Chiang, for your decades of dedication, your leadership, and the countless lives you’ve touched along the way. Your compassion, professionalism, and commitment to excellence have left a lasting impression on our patients, our team, and our community. We are grateful for everything you’ve contributed to Bellevue Dental Arts and wish you many years of happiness enjoying retirement, spending time with your family, and pursuing the passions that have always brought you joy.
