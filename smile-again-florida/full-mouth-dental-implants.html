Full-Mouth Dental Implant Aftercare

  • Rest for the remainder of the day following surgery.
  • Apply ice packs intermittently for the first 24–48 hours to help reduce swelling.
  • Take all prescribed medications as directed.
  • Avoid vigorous rinsing, spitting, or drinking through a straw for the first 24 hours.
  • Expect mild swelling, soreness, and slight bleeding or oozing during the first few days.
  • Stay hydrated and eat soft foods such as yogurt, eggs, mashed potatoes, smoothies, and soup. Avoid hard, crunchy, spicy, or sticky foods while healing.
  • Maintain excellent oral hygiene to support healing and long-term implant success.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for several days after surgery.
  • Refrain from smoking, vaping, and using tobacco products during healing.
  • Limit alcohol consumption while healing and taking medications.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How many implants are needed for a full arch? Most full-arch restorations are supported by 4 to 6 dental implants per arch, depending on your bone structure, oral health, and treatment goals. Solutions such as All-on-4 and All-on-X use strategically placed implants to securely support a full set of replacement teeth while maximizing available bone. During your consultation, our team will evaluate your unique needs and determine the ideal number of implants for a stable, long-lasting smile.
  • Will I have a new smile in 24 hours? In many cases, yes. Patients receiving full-arch dental implant treatment, such as All-on-X, can often leave with a beautiful temporary restoration within 24 hours of implant placement. While this transition smile allows you to enjoy improved appearance and function right away, your final restoration will be placed after your implants have fully healed and integrated with the jawbone.
  • Are full-mouth implants removable? No, full-mouth dental implants are designed to be a fixed, permanent solution for missing teeth. The restoration is securely attached to dental implants in the jawbone and can only be removed by a dental professional.
