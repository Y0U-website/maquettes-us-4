Cons of Full-Mouth Dental Implants

  • Higher initial investment than traditional dentures
  • Require a surgical procedure for implant placement
  • Treatment can take several months to complete
  • Some patients may need bone grafting or preparatory procedures
  • Not all patients qualify immediately due to bone loss or certain health conditions
  • Require ongoing maintenance and routine dental visits for long-term success

Traditional Dentures

Pros of Traditional Dentures

  • More affordable upfront cost than dental implants
  • Can replace multiple teeth or a full arch
  • Non-surgical tooth-replacement option
  • Treatment is typically completed more quickly than implants
  • Improve appearance, speech, and chewing ability
  • Custom-made to fit your smile and facial features
  • Easy to adjust or replace if needed

Cons of Traditional Dentures

  • May slip or shift while eating or speaking
  • Can require adhesives for added stability
  • Do not prevent jawbone loss over time
  • May need periodic relining or replacement due to changes in the jawbone
  • Can limit the ability to eat certain foods
  • May take time to adjust to wearing them comfortably
  • Less natural feel and function compared to dental implants
