Dental Implants

  • What aftercare is needed for dental implants? After getting dental implants, proper aftercare helps ensure healing and long-term success. Patients should keep the area clean by gently brushing and using recommended rinses, avoid hard or sticky foods during recovery, and attend follow-up appointments. Mild swelling or discomfort is normal, but persistent pain should be reported. Maintaining good oral hygiene and routine dental visits will help protect your implant and keep your smile healthy for years to come.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants often last 15 to 25 years or even a lifetime with proper care. Their durability depends on good oral hygiene, regular dental visits, and overall health. While the crown may need replacement over time, the implant itself can remain stable for decades, making it a reliable and cost-effective choice for many patients.
