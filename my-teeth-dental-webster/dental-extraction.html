Tooth Extraction

  • Surgical extractions: For broken or severely decayed teeth that require sectioning or minor gum/bone access.
  • Wisdom teeth: We assess crowding, infection risk, and root position for wisdom tooth removal in Webster, TX . When appropriate, we plan impacted wisdom tooth removal or coordinate a trusted referral for complex cases.
  • Infected tooth extraction: If an abscess or failed treatment is present, we’ll discuss infection control and timing to relieve pain safely.

What to expect (step by step)

Concerned about dental anxiety or a fear of painful extraction ? Please tell us ahead of time. We build in extra time, explain each step, and use a calming, patient-paced approach.

  • Emergency-focused exam: We identify the source of pain, review your medical history, and discuss whether emergency tooth extraction is the right next step or if a same-day palliative treatment makes more sense.
  • Gentle tooth removal: Using precise, controlled movements (not force), we free the tooth and protect surrounding tissues.
  • Site care & clot protection: You’ll leave with written instructions to minimize bleeding, support the blood clot, and reduce the chance of dry socket .
  • Pain control & healing plan: Expect guidance on medications, ice, diet, and activity—plus a follow-up to ensure you’re healing as expected.
