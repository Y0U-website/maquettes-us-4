What to Do Before You Reach the Dentist

Knowing what to do before you arrive can help protect your teeth and reduce pain:

Always call our office first—our team will guide you step by step until you arrive for care.

  • Toothache: Rinse with warm salt water and use a cold compress. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gum.
  • Knocked-out tooth: Keep it in milk or saline solution. Do not scrub it.
  • Broken tooth: Save any pieces and bring them to your appointment.
  • Bleeding: Apply gentle pressure with clean gauze.

Tips to Prevent Future Dental Emergencies

While not all emergencies can be avoided, you can reduce your risk by following these tips:

  • Wear a mouthguard during sports or physical activity.
  • Avoid chewing on ice, hard candy, or pens.
  • Maintain good oral hygiene with brushing and flossing.
  • Keep up with your routine checkups every six months.
  • Don’t ignore minor tooth pain—it often signals a problem that could worsen.
