Types of Dental Implants Available

Different patients need different solutions. Your dentist will recommend the most suitable implant option based on oral health, bone structure, and the number of missing teeth.

01.

Single Tooth Implant

A single implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting nearby teeth.

A crown is attached to the implant to restore the appearance and function of the missing tooth.

  • Lost one tooth due to injury or decay
  • Want a fixed, natural-looking replacement
  • Prefer a long-lasting alternative to bridges

02.

Implant Bridge

When several teeth are missing in a row, an implant bridge can replace them without using individual implants for each tooth.

This solution often provides more stability than traditional bridges.

  • Fewer implants required
  • Strong support for multiple teeth
  • Improved chewing function

03.

Implant Supported Dentures (Snap-On Dentures / Overdentures)

Implant supported dentures attach securely to implants placed in the jaw.

Unlike traditional dentures, they do not rely only on suction or adhesives.

Patients who previously struggled with loose dentures often find this option much more comfortable.

  • Improved denture stability
  • Reduced slipping during eating or speaking
  • Greater comfort throughout the day

04.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 implants replace a full arch of teeth using four strategically placed implants.

This treatment provides a stable fixed solution for people missing most or all teeth.

All-on-4 can often reduce treatment time compared to placing individual implants.

  • Full smile restoration
  • Fewer implants required
  • Stable and natural-looking results

05.

All-on-6 Implants (Teeth in a Day)

Some patients qualify for teeth in a day , also called All-on-6 implants.

In this procedure, a full set of temporary teeth can sometimes be placed on the same day as implant surgery.

Your dentist will determine if teeth in a day treatment is suitable based on bone health and overall oral condition.

  • Faster smile restoration
  • Immediate improvement in appearance
  • Reduced waiting time during healing
