0 WHO WE ARE The team of Luxe Rx is founded by a team of diversely educated health practitioners:

Dr. Pole Dr. Apeksha Pole began her career in health care as a pharmacist at the University of British Columbia. She worked as a hospital pharmacist briefly before pursuing a career in cosmetic dentistry. She attended Dental School at the Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University from which she graduated Summa Cum Laude in 2010. She did a one-year residency at St Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx, NY and an additional chief year of residency at Wyckoff Hospital in Brooklyn, NY.

Dr. Pole loves to keep on learning about new advancements in aesthetic dentistry and makes excellence her passion. She is always eager to help her patients on their path to a healthier more beautiful smile. She furthered her love to help patients by doing mission trips which started with a trip to Uganda. Since then she has always looked for ways to be involved in humanitarian acts and giving back to the community.

With over 10 years of continuing education, Dr Pole has furthered her expertise in the cosmetic and aesthetic sector as well as broadened their knowledge of cutting-edge dental technology and surgical procedures.

Dr Pole is very passionate about health and wellbeing which led her and Dr Wong to start the wellness studio aspect of Luxe Rx. Here she is working with hematologists and mental health practitioners to introduce wellness and nutrition as a part of overall dental health. In accordance with this alliance, she recently received her nutrition degree from Harvard University.

She is also a dedicated wife and mother. She lives with her husband and 3 year old son. She stays active with family activities and is always finding ways to balance work and home life. From planning seasonal events with mothers in the community to celebrating and creating cultural awareness for kids in the area, she keeps very busy. She has a mission to try to do the most she can in all her roles: as a mother, business owner and health practitioner.

Dr Chan Dr. Christopher Chan hails from Burnaby, British Columbia, canada. He graduated with a BSc from St. Michael’s College in Colchester, Vermont and then received his dental degree cum laude from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Since then, he has been working for a decade in New York City. He has always had a passion for detail and art and loves to make people smile. From his gentle bedside manner to his warm personality, he is a caring and talented clinician.

He has recently joined the Luxe Team as we expand and bring more smiles to more patients! he is an active member of the Spear Study Club and loves to continue to learn and grow.

When he’s not in the office, he enjoys playing ice hockey, tennis and golf, and has recently been getting into pickleball! He loves food and is always interested in trying new restaurants and bars around the city, so feel free to let him know where you’ve been!
