Welcome to Pradera Dental! Led by Dr. Carly Schrade, our Sterling, CO, dental office is where clinical excellence and compassion converge to provide exceptional dental care for all ages.

Embracing the unique dental needs of each life stage, Pradera Dental offers personalized dental care tailored to patients of all ages, ensuring optimal oral health from childhood through the golden years. We offer a wide range of dental services, including general and family dentistry, crowns and bridges, composite fillings, teeth whitening, dentures and partials, extractions, and emergency…

Innovation is key to our approach, as we constantly evaluate and enhance our methods to deliver precise and personalized care. Our dental office uses advanced technology like digital x-rays and impressions, intraoral cameras, and CEREC same-day restorations. Also, our team constantly pursues continuing education and training to stay abreast of all the latest advances in dentistry and patient care.

At Pradera Dental, we strive to create a tranquil and welcoming atmosphere where patients feel at ease. Alongside our warm and friendly staff, we offer comfortable amenities such as pillows and music to make your visit as relaxing as possible.

At Pradera Dental, we aim to extend our commitment to oral health beyond our office doors. Our engagement in initiatives such as volunteering at the Colorado Mission of Mercy, a free annual dental care event, reflects our unwavering dedication to fostering dental health within our community.
