Meet the Team at Pradera Dental

Meet Dr. Carly Schrade

Our lead dentist, Dr. Carly Schrade, carries a wealth of knowledge and an unwavering commitment to excellent dental care. With a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and years of training, she brings comprehensive dental solutions to our patients.

Dr. Schrade believes in keeping her knowledge up-to-date, regularly participating in continued education programs. She is dedicated to understanding the latest advancements in dental technology and integrating them into our practice to ensure our patients receive the highest level of care.

Join us at Pradera Dental, where we create healthy smiles for a lifetime.

Carly Schrade, DDS

Dr. Schrade was born and raised in Sterling, Colorado. She studied Human Biology at the University of Colorado at Colorado Springs and graduated in 2013. She went on to dental school at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus and graduated in 2017. She also takes continuing education classes as often as possible each year.

She enjoys all aspects of dentistry and loves working with special needs patients, children, and elderly patients. For the past five+ years, she has been the lead dentist for a non-profit dental clinic in Louisville, CO, where her husband is a firefighter-paramedic.

Her goal in starting Pradera Dental is to provide high-quality dental care and education for your entire family. She hopes to promote a lifelong relationship built on trust, confidence, and exceptional patient care. She is excited to be able to give back to the community that has given so much to her.

Outside of the dental office, you can find her waterskiing, snow-skiing, or enjoying a good campfire. She loves taking care of her vegetable and flower gardens, but right now, most of her free time is spent working on home renovation projects with her husband, Mack. They have a rambunctious golden retriever named Rocco, who is already loving becoming a farm dog, and an equally energetic two-year-old son Brady, as well as chickens and farm cats.

Justine

As an Expanded Duties Dental Assistant (EDDA) with a decade of experience in dentistry, Justine brings exceptional expertise and a genuine passion for patient care to our practice. Joining our team in May 2023, Justine has quickly become an integral part of our close-knit group.

Justine received her dental education and training from the Dental Assisting School in Broomfield, CO, in 2013. Her commitment to professional growth led her to pursue an EDDA course in Longmont, CO in 2020, further expanding her skills and knowledge.

Working with patients is the highlight of Justine s day. She enjoys meeting new faces and building lasting relationships with familiar ones. Taking and studying x-rays is a particular favorite of hers, as it allows her to assist patients in getting the answers they need when facing dental concerns.

A fascination with dentistry from a young age and a genuine love for dental visits spurred Justine s decision to join the dental field. Her positive childhood experiences at the dentist s office drive her dedication to delivering excellent care and ensuring patients feel at ease during their visits.

Beyond her dental career, Justine enjoys spending quality time with friends and family. She finds solace in reading, fishing, camping, and cooking, with a particular passion for spending long hours fishing, finding the activity incredibly relaxing.

An interesting fact that many may not know about Justine is that she holds dual citizenship as a Canadian and American citizen.

Arika is a skilled dental hygienist with vast experience spanning 17 years in dentistry. Arika s main responsibilities involve providing a range of preventive treatments, from thorough teeth cleanings to performing x-rays and educating patients on maintaining optimal oral hygiene. One of Arika s favorite aspects of her job is building personal connections with her patients. She takes great pleasure in getting to know each individual on a personal level and ensuring their best interests are always at heart. Her passion for oral health began during her high school years when she participated in a work-study program at a local dentist s office, shadowing dental hygienists. This experience fueled her love for the dental field, and she has dedicated her career to dentistry ever since.

Outside of work, Arika enjoys attending UW football games and embarking on annual "girl s trip" vacations with her childhood friends. She also has a unique talent for canning tomatoes, cucumbers, and jalape os from her garden, adding a personal touch to her culinary skills.
