Preventative Services

Dental Check-Ups are necessary for maintaining a healthy mouth. Exams, x-rays, and cleanings help strengthen teeth and gums but also monitor for potential future health risks.

Dental Restorations

From fillings, root canals, crowns, bridges, and dentures. The team at Quartz Creek Dental has you covered and will help restore your smile.

Dental Implants

Our state-of-the-art dental implants provide a permanent, natural-looking solution to replace missing teeth and restore full function. Designed for comfort, durability, and aesthetics.

Dentures and Partials

Custom-made dentures and partials are designed to restore your smile, improve your bite, and boost your confidence. We create dentures that look natural, feel comfortable, and fit securely.

Sedation Dentistry

We understand that dental visits can be intimidating, especially for patients with anxiety or special needs. Our sedation dentistry options provide a calm and relaxed experience, allowing you to receive the care you need without stress or discomfort. Whether you’re feeling nervous or need extra assistance, our compassionate team and anesthesia partners are dedicated to ensuring your comfort.

Clear Aligner Orthodontic Therapy

Orthodontic care has come along way! Using the newest and advanced technologies we are able to give you the wire-less option for obtaining a healthier bite and a straighter smile.

Same Day Crowns

Using the latest technology, same day crowns are an option for most patient needs. Scanned and milled in house, these crowns offer aesthetic appeal as well as strength needed for a long lasting restoration option.

Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal disease can lead to bad breath, unstable teeth, and possible bone loss. Often more frequent and deeper cleanings are needed to protect from these health issues.

Extractions

Dental extractions are sometimes necessary. It is our goal to provide our patients with the best experience possible with options for healthy restorations.

Dental Emergency Services

Dental emergencies can happen when you least expect it and often can’t wait for days or weeks for treatment. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, please call, don’t wait.

Dental Appliances

Dental Appliances such as Night Guards/Occlusal Guards and Snore Guards are custom made to each patient. Appliances can help with a more restful sleep, grinding prevention, head and jaw ache reduction.
