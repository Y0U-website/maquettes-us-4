Orthodontics For Adults

Life with Braces as An Adult Adults tend to adapt quickly to braces. Eating and oral hygiene are typically require the most adjustments avoiding sticky or hard foods, and diligently following cleaning protocols for braces or aligners. Sometimes adults can experience more distinct initial soreness, as their bones are denser and no longer growing. For more detailed information, please visit our Life with Braces page.

Orthodontics For Adults FAQ

Am I Too Old for Braces?

The answer is almost certainly no. Teeth can be moved at any age as long as gums and bone are healthy enough. The main factor is that adult bones are no longer growing, which can introduce certain limitations, or necessitate different approaches.

How Long Does Treatment Take for Adults?

Most adult cases take 18- 24 months, though more minor cases can be shorter, and more complex cases can be longer. Treatment time depends on the starting position, the end goals, and (for aligners) wear consistency. Dr. Hagan will provide a treatment timeline for each patient’s case.

Can I do orthodontics if I have crowns, fillings, or missing teeth?

Usually, yes. Brackets and aligners can work around restorations, and orthodontics is often used to set up spacing and alignment before replacing missing teeth (like implants). Each patient’s candidacy will be reviewed in detail to determine the right treatment for their situation.
