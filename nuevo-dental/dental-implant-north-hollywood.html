What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

Here’s what to expect when starting your dental implant journey at Nuevo Dental:

1. Consultation and Assessment

Your first appointment involves X-rays and 3D imaging to assess your jawbone health and create a tailored treatment plan.

2. Implant Placement

Titanium implants are surgically placed in your jawbone under local anesthesia, using advanced technology to ensure accuracy and comfort.

3. Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, your implants will naturally fuse with your jawbone. This creates a stable base for your new teeth.

4. Final Restoration

Once the healing process is complete, custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures are attached to complete your smile.

How do implants compare to other options like dentures?

Dental implants offer significant advantages over dentures and bridges. Unlike removable dentures, implants don’t slip or require adhesives. They preserve jawbone health and don’t affect adjacent teeth like bridges do. While the initial investment is higher, implants often prove more cost-effective long-term due to their durability.

What should I expect during a dental implant procedure?

The implant procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, with sedation options available for anxious patients. Most patients report minimal discomfort during placement. We use gentle surgical techniques and provide detailed post-operative care instructions.
