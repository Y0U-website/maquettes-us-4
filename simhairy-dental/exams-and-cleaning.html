What Is A Dental Check-Up?

A dental check-up is a routine dental examination of the teeth and gums. It aims to maintain and improve the dental health of a patient. It allows the dentist to check if a patient has any major or minor dental issues. It enables the dentist to determine if the patient needs to undergo any dental procedures to treat or prevent dental problems.

What Does A Dental Check-Up Include?

A Dental Check-Up in Rancho, San Diego, commonly involves a standard procedure. Expect to experience the following steps when you visit Simhairy Dental for a dental examination:

Step 1: Welcome

Our friendly receptionist will welcome you when you arrive at our dental clinic.

Step 2: Patient Questionnaire

You will be requested to complete a patient questionnaire before you proceed to the exam room. Fill up the form with your basic information and other requested information.

Step 3: Teeth Cleaning

The dental hygienist or assistant will clean your teeth. He or she will utilize a variety of dental equipment and instruments to eliminate plaque and tartar from the surface of your teeth and gums.

Step 4: Initial Examination

The dental hygienist or assistant will perform a preliminary examination of your teeth and gums. The assistant or hygienist may also take an X-ray of your teeth and mouth if necessary. He or she will report the initial findings to the dentist.

Step 5: Comprehensive Examination

The dentist will perform a comprehensive check-up on your teeth and gums. He will review the X-ray results. The dentist will share his evaluation of the current condition of your dental health.

Step 6: Dental Recommendations

The dentist will make recommendations based on the results of your dental examination. He will make suggestions regarding any dental treatments or procedures that you may need. The dentist will discuss your oral health and habits. Finally, he will determine when you need to go back for a follow-up check-up.

2. Digital X-Rays

3. Oral Cancer Screening

An oral cancer screening is a regular part of your dental check-up. This screening enables the early detection of cancer to protect your oral health. It allows the dentist to pinpoint any signs of cancerous and precancerous conditions like lumps, sores, or white patches.

How Often Do I Need To Visit My Dentist For Teeth Check-Up?

Dentists generally recommend that you visit your dentist for an oral check-up every six months. Nevertheless, the American Dental Association explains that the recommended intervals between examinations may vary because there is no one-size-fits-all dental treatment.

Based on their oral health and needs, some patients will need to visit the dentist once or twice a year, while other patients may require more frequent checkups. According to the National Health Service, the lower the risk of dental problems, the longer patients can wait before their next examination. On the other hand, patients with more severe oral health issues will need to visit the dentist more often.

You may ask the dentist during your dental check-up to find out how often you need to visit the dental clinic for an examination.
