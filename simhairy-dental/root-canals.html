How Long Does A Root Canal Take?

The average time of root canal treatment ranges from one hour to 90 minutes. However, the treatment can take a long time if it is a complicated procedure. The exact length of your treatment will depend on the current condition of your tooth and root canal. You may request an estimate from our dental practice.

Does A Root Canal Hurt?

The pain involved in a root canal procedure is reasonably bearable because of modern dental technology. The dentist will use local anesthesia to numb your tooth and gums to make you comfortable during the treatment. As a result, the pain level is only comparable to that of regular dental procedures. Nevertheless, your root canal may feel sore after the procedure. You can experience mild discomfort for a few days.

How Do You Know If You Need A Root Canal?

You need a root canal treatment if the pulp of your tooth has been damaged because of a bacterial infection. These are some of the symptoms of an infected pulp:

  • Severe toothache
  • Prolonged sensitivity
  • Gum swelling
  • Pain while eating
  • Loose tooth
  • Facial swelling
  • Discolored tooth
  • Pus discharge
