Kendall Miami Gardens Family Dentistry in Kendall & Miami Gardens

With over 35 years of experience in the dental field, Dr. Luisa Corrales-Iglesias is a truly inspiring and integral part of the Pro Dental Centers family, bringing a wealth of clinical expertise and a heartfelt dedication to every patient she treats.

Her journey in dentistry began in Cuba, where she earned her initial dental degree and practiced for six years before immigrating to the United States in 1997. Driven by a deep passion for oral health and an unwavering determination to continue changing lives, she made tremendous sacrifices to rebuild her career in a new country. Overcoming the immense challenge of mastering English as a second…

Today, Dr. Corrales combines her remarkable life experience, advanced clinical training, and genuine empathy to deliver gentle, comprehensive care. Fluent in both English and Spanish, she takes immense pride in connecting with her patients and creating beautiful, lasting smiles for families of all ages.

Since joining Pro Dental Centers upon her graduation in 2023, Dr. Lilibeth Gómez Flores has been a dedicated and passionate member of our clinical team at Kendallwood Park Dental Center.

A dual-trained dentist with degrees from both the University of Medical Sciences of Havana (DDS, 2010, with honors) and the School of Dental Medicine of Puerto Rico (DMD, 2023, with honors), she brings an exceptional depth of knowledge and over a decade of hands-on clinical experience to every patient she treats.
