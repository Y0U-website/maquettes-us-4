Caring for Dental Implants:

Dental implants are essentially cared for the same ways as your natural teeth. Your Green Bay dentists at the Oral Health Associates will recommend that you brush your implants twice per day and floss between each tooth daily. You will also need to visit your dental hygienist twice per year for regular cleanings (possibly more often if your dentist recommends it).
