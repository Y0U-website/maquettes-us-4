Services Provided by Your Green Bay Family Dentist

One of the things that makes our practice stand out is our diverse and highly qualified staff like Dr. Glime, as well as, a vast array of beneficial dental services. Some of the treatments that your Green Bay family dentist will be able to provide for you are listed below.

Dental Treatments

  • Fillings – Our fillings can repair teeth that are decayed or damaged.
  • Root Canals – If a tooth is dying, we can perform root canal treatment to save it.
  • Crowns – Crowns will help restore a tooth’s structure, function, and appearance.
  • Veneers – These are thin shells that are placed over the fronts of teeth, masking any flaws.
  • Dentures – For those who have missing teeth, dentures can be used to help restore a smile.
  • Dental Implants – Dental implants offer a more permanent solution for those who have missing teeth.
  • Periodontics – This branch of dentistry is used to help stave off gum disease.
  • Teeth Whitening – This cosmetic dentistry treatment can give a patient a whiter and brighter smile.
