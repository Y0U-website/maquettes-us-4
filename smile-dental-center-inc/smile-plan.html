Smile Plan

Affordable Dental Care, Even Without Insurance

Smile Plan is our in-house membership that makes preventive dentistry easy and additional treatment more affordable. No deductibles. No claim forms. Just smarter care.

Affordable Care, the Aloha Way

No insurance? No problem. Smile Plan keeps your cleanings, exams, and X-rays simple and your other care more affordable.

What’s Included with Every Plan

  • 2 routine cleanings per year (the standard kind, not deep cleanings)
  • 2 checkups per year (1 full first checkup, 1 follow-up)
  • 1 full-mouth X-ray per year
  • Member discount on any other treatment you need

Who Is Smile Plan For?

  • Patients without dental insurance
  • Patients whose insurance doesn’t cover preventive care well
  • Families who want predictable, all-in-one dental care
  • Anyone tired of confusing dental bills

What’s Not Included

  • Cosmetic treatment like whitening and veneers is not discounted under Smile Plan. (Implants and Invisalign do get a 5 % member discount.)
  • Deep cleanings and ongoing gum care are billed at our standard rates.
  • Emergency visits (a quick exam for tooth pain or other urgent issues) are billed separately.
  • Treatment by outside specialists is not covered.
  • This is not insurance and cannot be combined with insurance.

How Enrollment Works

  • Enroll in person at the office or by phone.
  • Pay your annual fee.
  • Use your benefits for one full year from your enrollment date.
  • Renew each year to continue.
