Dental Implants in Waipahu, HI

How we make dental implants

This is how we make dental implants. First, we place the titanium implant into the bone. You can think of it almost like placing a screw into wood. Then, over the next three to four months, your bone heals around the implant and helps stabilize it. After that, we connect what we call the abutment, and then we place the crown on top of it. The goal is for this crown to look, feel, and function much like your natural tooth.

Are dental implants easy to maintain?

Dental implants are generally easy to maintain. You need to keep the area clean with good brushing and flossing and, of course, come in for your regular dental visits so we can continue to monitor the implant and the surrounding tissues.

What if a missing tooth is in the back and doesn’t show?

Over time, these changes can create additional dental problems. I’ve seen patients eventually lose more teeth and need additional treatment. That’s why it’s important to evaluate a missing tooth and talk about your replacement options before it gets to that point.

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that takes the place of a missing tooth root. It’s placed in your jawbone, where it integrates with the bone over a few months. Once it’s solid, we attach a custom-made crown on top that looks and feels just like a natural tooth.

Implants are the closest thing we have to a real tooth. They don’t slip, they don’t decay, they don’t need to be removed at night, and with good care they can last a lifetime.

Who dental implants are right for

Implants need healthy gums and enough jawbone to support them. At your consultation we’ll look at both. If you’re low on bone, there are options to build it back up before placing the implant.

  • Replacing a single missing tooth
  • Replacing several missing teeth in a row
  • Anchoring a bridge so neighboring teeth don’t need to be ground down
  • Stabilizing dentures so they don’t slip while you eat or talk
  • Patients who want a permanent solution, not something to take out at night

What to expect

  • Consultation and 3D imaging. We map your bone, your bite, and your existing teeth. You’ll know up front whether implants are right for you and how long the whole process will take.
  • Plan and quote. We give you a clear, complete price before anything starts. No surprises.
  • Implant placement. One of our dentists trained in implant placement places the implant, with local numbing. Most patients are surprised at how quick and comfortable it is.
  • Healing. The implant bonds with your jawbone over the next 3 to 6 months. You’ll wear a temporary tooth in the meantime so your smile stays full.
  • Crown placement. Once the implant is fully integrated, we attach the custom crown. You leave with a tooth that looks and works like your natural teeth.
  • Care and check-ins. You brush and floss your implant just like a real tooth. We see you for normal cleanings and keep an eye on everything.
