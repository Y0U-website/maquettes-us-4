Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Wyoming Cosmetic & Family Dental offer?

We provide a wide range of comprehensive services including general, cosmetic, preventive, and restorative dentistry. Our offerings include teeth cleaning, extractions, root canals, teeth whitening, veneers, orthodontics, digital x-rays, dental implants, bridges, dentures, fillings, and crowns.

Does the practice offer state-of-the-art procedures and technologies?

Yes, we utilize technological advancements to bolster the effectiveness, efficiency, and comfort of our dental care. For instance, we use digital x-rays, 3D scanning, computer models and laser technology ensure accuracy and comfort in both diagnosis and treatment.

What cosmetic dental services do you offer?

Our cosmetic dental services include in-office teeth whitening treatments and veneers. We also provide orthodontics to cater to the aesthetic needs of our patients.

What is your approach to preventative dental care?

We proactively work to prevent dental issues before they arise in order to save our patients time, stress, and discomfort. We believe in educating our patients about at-home dental hygiene, proper nutrition, and we offer routine cleanings, fluoride treatments, mouthguards and other preventative treatments.

What are Digital X-Rays and why do you use them?

Digital X-rays are a type of imaging technology that exposes patients to significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays. They produce images automatically and are an important component of preventive care, revealing any issues developing beneath the surface, in areas of the smile not visible during a visual exam.

Can you provide restorative dental care?

Yes, we offer a variety of restorative dental services. Whether your smile has been affected by trauma, decay, or infection, we can restore your smile to health and beauty. We offer services like dental implants, bridges, dentures, fillings, and crowns.

Does Wyoming Cosmetic & Family Dental offer affordable dental care?

Yes, we believe all families should have access to high-quality comprehensive dental care. We offer a variety of payment plans and financing options to help make your dental care more affordable.

What payment plans and financing options are offered?

We offer convenient payment plans, 0 % financing, and in-office dental plans. Whether you have dental insurance or not, we will work with you to determine a suitable option that fits your needs and budget.

What are the patient forms I need to fill out?

There are specific patient forms required, which may vary based on the treatment. You can download the required forms directly from our Patient Resources page.

How can I make a payment?

You can make a payment right from our website’s Patient Resources page.

Is my dental insurance accepted?

Yes, we accept most dental insurances and will work to ensure you pay as little as possible for all services.

Does the clinic offer preventative dentistry?

Yes, we firmly believe in keeping teeth and gums healthy through preventative measures like regular cleanings and educating patients regarding oral hygiene. The clinic has provisions for insured patients to receive our preventive services for free. For uninsured patients, we offer different payment options.

Do you offer free dental examinations?

Yes, we offer free dental exams for adults, brighter whiter cleaning, cleaning & exams for children, and second opinion evaluations with most insurance. Some restrictions may apply.

Who is responsible for co-pays or deductibles required by my insurance?

The responsibility for co-pays or deductibles rests with the patient, as this is a part of your agreement with your insurance provider. However, we try to ensure you pay the lowest possible price for your dental care and are happy to bill your insurance directly.
