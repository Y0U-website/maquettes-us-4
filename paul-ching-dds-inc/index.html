The practice

We love meeting new patients. Part of easing your concerns is explaining what you can expect from our practice.

Here at the Practice of Paul G. Ching, D.D.S., we believe that a smile tells a thousand words, and we are dedicated to giving our patients high-quality dental care. We’re here to give you reliable diagnoses, comfortable procedures, and great advice on your oral and overall health.

Our goal is for you to leave our office with a memorable and enjoyable dental experience, which is why our welcoming and compassionate staff will do everything they can to make you feel right at home.

We are proud to be the only GBT certified provider in Hawaii. You can find more information about GBT under the Hygiene Services tab.

We focus on providing quality dental care, excellent customer service, and a relaxing environment to maintain a lasting patient relationship. We consider your first visit the start of a long-term relationship with you.

During your initial appointment, we will perform various tests to assess the condition of your oral health and to create a dental plan based on your unique, individual needs. We look forward to meeting you!

You and your family can take advantage of a full range of dental services here at our practice. During each visit, you will be treated by trained professionals who will provide top quality care to prevent dental problems and treat current conditions.
