Membership Program Rules & Guidelines

The Klassen Care membership cannot be used for:

  • Membership fees are due up front and are non‐refundable.
  • Klassen Care membership expires 12 months after signing up. Benefits can be used at any time during the 12-month period.
  • Patient’s portion of bill is due one week before service (for dental fillings, crowns, etc.)
  • The Klassen Care membership cannot be suspended or “frozen.”
  • The membership is non‐transferable.
  • Klassen Care cannot be used in conjunction with another payment plan (such as Care Credit) or dental insurance.
  • Membership is only for services rendered at W Dental, the office of Carly Klassen, DDS, PA.
  • Any payment due to a specialist or hospital
  • Treatment which, in the sole opinion of Dr. Klassen , lies outside the realm of her capability
  • Services of injuries covered under worker’s compensation
