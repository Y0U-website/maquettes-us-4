Single Dental Implant

A single tooth implant is designed to replace the missing tooth, as well as its root. Yours will be sealed with a custom-shaded dental crown that perfectly matches the rest of your smile.

All-on-4® Dental Implants

All-On-4 implant dentures are a long-lasting solution for patients who are tired of having to deal with traditional removable dentures. The best part? This system is designed to give patients a new, usable full set of teeth on the same day as their procedure!

They work by implanting four titanium implants into the patient’s upper or lower arch, securing stronger than traditional implants since they are integrated into the jawbone and attached to custom dentures. All-On-4 dental implants appear natural and realistic because they are crafted for each individual’s comfort and fit!

Implant-Retained Dentures

Implant-retained dentures are partial or full dentures that sit atop dental implants for a healthier, more stable fit. Implant-retained dentures are proven to be a healthier alternative to traditional dentures as their ultra-snug fit promotes healthy bone retention. Similar to traditional dentures , they are removable and require daily care.
