Meet Our Team

Carly Klassen, DDS

Originally born in Canada, Dr. Carly Klassen was raised down in the Rio Grande Valley. Although she wasn’t born in Texas, she got here as soon as she could!

After graduating from high school, she attended Baylor University (sic ’em) and later, The University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio, where she received her dental degree. Since finishing dental school, Dr. Klassen has acquired hundreds of hours of continuing education, constantly striving to provide her patients with the most current technology and excellence in dental care.

She truly loves people and has gone on several dental mission trips. She hopes to go on many more! In her spare time, she enjoys running, traveling, and spending time with her husband and two daughters. You might spot her out in the community since she’s an active member of Christ Chapel and frequently attends local school events.

Rebecca Doolittle, DDS

Dr. Doolittle is a daughter of a Marine and has lived coast to coast. She came to Texas in 2013 after spending many years in Minnesota pursuing her education and working. She is a graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry where she also received her undergraduate degree in Dental Hygiene in 2005 before becoming a dentist in 2012.

When Dr. Doolittle isn’t in the office, her three boys keep her busy. They enjoy hiking, camping, swimming and creating with Legos. She and her husband travel frequently and enjoy planning adventures for their family. She loves gardening and playing the piano. She serves as a dentist in the Air National Guard where she works to improve the oral health of her fellow soldiers. Building relationships and helping people love their smile brings the most satisfaction to Dr. Doolittle.
