Symptoms of a Failed Dental Implant

Issues with dental implants may occur soon after they are placed, but they can also arise months or years down the road. That is why it is always wise to pay attention to any changes in your mouth and schedule an exam promptly if you believe something may be wrong.

Signs that an implant may be failing include:

  • Ongoing or intense discomfort: After you have recovered from implant surgery, your smile should be pain-free. Pain that lingers or becomes worse should be professionally evaluated.
  • Signs of infection: Redness, swelling, sensitivity, bleeding, or pus around the implant could point to peri-implantitis.
  • Movement: Either the implant itself or the attached restoration may start to feel loose. This is a reason to call us right away.
  • Difficulty chewing: Implants are designed to help you eat with confidence. New problems biting or chewing may signal a significant issue.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

If you are concerned that one of your implants is failing, schedule a visit with our team as soon as you can. The earlier we are able to take a look, the better the chances are that we can manage the problem with conservative treatment.

At your appointment, our team will thoroughly examine your implant, gum tissue, bite, and nearby oral structures. We may also take diagnostic images so we can see what is occurring below the gumline. After we identify the cause and extent of the issue, we will explain the treatment options that best fit your situation.

Sometimes, the solution may involve antibiotics, a thorough dental cleaning, or replacing the restoration attached to the implant. If the implant is no longer stable enough to remain in place, it may have to be removed so the tissues can recover. After you heal and complete any needed follow-up care, you might be able to get a new implant.
