Meet the Doctors

Dr. Divya was born and raised in Erie, Pennsylvania, a quaint town where she enjoyed Lake Erie sunsets and Wegmans grocery shopping. She received her Bachelor of Science degree from Duke University with a major in psychology and minors in neuroscience and chemistry. Wanting to expand her horizons, Dr. Divya moved to the big city and received her dental degree from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and then stayed on to pursue her love for children at Columbia University-New York Presbyterian Hospital’s Pediatric Dentistry program.

She maintains membership in the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, Women to Women Dental Network, and the New York State Dental Association. She is also certified in Basic Cardiac Life Support and Pediatric Advanced Life Support. Inspired by the wonderful mentors in her own life, Dr. Divya pursued a faculty position and serves as an assistant clinical professor in the division of Pediatric Dentistry at Columbia University.

New York City has really taken Dr. Divya in, and she would not have it any other way. When she is not cheering on the Duke Blue Devils during March Madness, Dr. Divya enjoys running at Central Park’s reservoir, spending time on the beach, and traveling with her family – especially her adorable 1-year-old niece!

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Kelly was raised in Rockland County, New York. She attended New York University where she completed her undergraduate and dental school training, earning a Bachelors of Arts degree in Psychology with honors, as well as her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

During her dental training, Dr. Kelly participated in various volunteer opportunities to provide dental care to children around the NYC area and was awarded the Dr. Edward G. Kaufman Award for Academic Excellence, for achieving the highest-grade point average in the class for the academic year.

Following dental school, Dr. Kelly received her training and certificate of specialty in pediatric dentistry at Columbia University/New York-Presbyterian Kids.

Dr. Kelly grew up working with children as a babysitter and summer swim instructor, and is so grateful for the opportunity to continue to do so as a pediatric dentist. She is committed to making each dental visit a fun, educational, and nurturing experience for every child, and to building meaningful relationships with each family.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kelly enjoys exploring New York City restaurants, reading, playing soccer, and spending time with family, friends, and her dog.
