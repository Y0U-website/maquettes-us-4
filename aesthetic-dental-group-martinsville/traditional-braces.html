Metal Braces

When it comes to effective and reliable orthodontic treatment, metal braces have stood the test of time. These classic braces consist of metal brackets and wires meticulously crafted to align your teeth with precision and efficiency.

  • Durable Construction : Metal braces Bridgewater are renowned for their durability, ensuring that they can withstand the demands of orthodontic treatment throughout the process. They are among the most recommended solutions for moderate to severe malocclusions.
  • Bracket-and-Wire System : These braces employ a time-proven mechanism featuring small metal brackets affixed to the front of your teeth and wires that connect them. This setup exerts gradual pressure to shift your teeth into their optimal positions.
  • Customized Treatment: Your orthodontist will personalize your treatment plan by adjusting the tension on the wires and placing rubber bands to correct specific dental issues such as overbites, underbites, or misaligned teeth.
  • Hassle-Free Attachment : Brackets are securely glued to your teeth during a straightforward and painless procedure. This means that aside from showing up to your orthodontist’s adjustments, you can confidently go about your daily activities without needing to actively manage your braces.
  • Affordable Treatment : While treatment costs vary depending on various factors, metal braces can still be considered the most affordable option to correct tooth alignment.

The Metal Braces Journey

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we assess your dental needs, take images of your teeth, and discuss treatment options.

Once you’ve chosen metal braces, we carefully attach the brackets to your teeth, securing them with special adhesive.

Throughout your treatment, you’ll attend periodic appointments for adjustments , during which your orthodontist fine-tunes the braces to guide your teeth into their desired positions.

Proper dental hygiene is crucial during treatment. Regular brushing, flossing, and attending dental check-ups help prevent any potential issues.

After achieving the desired alignment, your braces will be removed, and you’ll transition to a retainer to maintain your beautiful new smile.

  • Treatment Duration : Metal braces typically require a treatment duration of up to two years, so be prepared for a commitment to achieve your desired results.
  • Oral Hygiene : Diligent oral hygiene practices are essential to prevent plaque buildup and maintain the health of your teeth and gums during treatment.
  • Diet Adjustments : Some dietary modifications may be necessary to avoid damaging brackets or wires, especially during the initial adjustment period.
  • Discomfort and Speech Changes : It’s normal to experience some discomfort or slight changes in your speech, but this usually subsides within a few days.

Clear Ceramic Braces

Clear ceramic braces offer an exceptional blend of orthodontic precision and discreet aesthetics. These braces are specially designed for those seeking effective tooth alignment without the conspicuous appearance of traditional metal braces.

  • Subtle Appearance : Clear ceramic braces feature translucent brackets and tooth-colored wires that blend harmoniously with your natural tooth color, making them less noticeable.
  • Durable and Effective : Despite their subtle appearance, clear ceramic braces are robust and highly effective in treating a wide range of orthodontic issues.
  • Hassle-Free Attachment : Brackets are securely affixed to your teeth using a straightforward, minimally invasive procedure, ensuring that you can continue your daily routines without excessive interference.

The Clear Ceramic Braces Journey

Your journey with clear ceramic braces is typically the same as its metal counterpart. There may be some slight changes in how your orthodontist instructs you to take care of your appliance , but the general process is similar.

The best way to tell if clear ceramic braces Martinsville NJ are a great fit is through a consultation with Aesthetic Dental Group. We will conduct a thorough examination and give you the best recommendation based on our findings.

  • Treatment Duration : Clear ceramic braces generally require a treatment duration similar to traditional metal braces, ranging from one to two years.
  • Dietary Considerations : Some dietary adjustments may be advisable to minimize the risk of damage to the brackets or wires.
  • Minimal Discoloration : While the brackets themselves are resistant to staining, the ligatures (rubber bands) may need periodic replacement to prevent discoloration.
  • Investment in Dental Care : To maintain their discreet appearance, investing in dental care supplies such as toothpaste and mouthwash designed for ceramic braces is crucial.

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces redefine discreet orthodontics, offering a hidden solution for those seeking effective treatment without visible hardware. These innovative braces are placed behind your teeth, ensuring a treatment that remains entirely concealed. For this reason, many choose them as the best braces for adults Martinsville NJ.

  • Invisible Alignment : Lingual braces are hidden from view as they are affixed to the back surface of your teeth, making them completely inconspicuous.
  • Customized Precision : Your orthodontist tailors your treatment by creating customized brackets and wires that fit the unique contours of your teeth, optimizing comfort and efficiency.
  • Effective Orthodontic Correction : Despite their concealed placement, lingual braces are highly effective in addressing various dental issues, from mild misalignment to complex cases.

The Lingual Braces Journey

Your journey with lingual braces begins with an extensive consultation . While lingual braces are effective, they are not for all cases. We will assess your dental requirements and discuss the most suitable treatment options for your specific needs.

Upon selecting lingual braces, your orthodontist will meticulously craft custom brackets and wires to fit the back surfaces of your teeth, ensuring precise alignment .

After achieving your desired dental alignment, your lingual braces will be carefully removed, and you’ll transition to a retainer to preserve your newly aligned smile.

  • Speech Adjustments : Initially, you may notice minor speech adjustments due to the braces’ placement, but these typically resolve as your tongue adapts.
  • Dietary Considerations : Some dietary adjustments may be necessary to avoid damaging the lingual brackets.
  • Oral Hygiene Challenges : Cleaning behind your teeth can be more challenging with lingual braces, requiring extra diligence to maintain optimal oral hygiene.
  • Potentially Higher Cost : Lingual braces may come with a higher price tag compared to traditional braces due to their customization and unique placement.

Self-Ligating Braces

Self-ligating braces represent a significant advancement in orthodontic technology, operating on a unique mechanism that eliminates the need for traditional elastic or metal ties. This brace type offers a more streamlined and effective approach to orthodontic alignment.

  • No Ligatures or Ties : Self-ligating braces utilize a specialized clip system that replaces the conventional elastic bands or metal ties used in traditional braces. This mechanism allows for reduced friction and more controlled tooth movement.
  • Efficiency : The absence of ligatures and ties means that adjustments can be made with greater precision, potentially reducing the number of orthodontic appointments required.
  • Comfort : Self-ligating braces are designed for enhanced patient comfort, as they exert less pressure on teeth and gums. This can lead to a more comfortable treatment journey.

The Self-Ligating Braces Journey

Once you opt for self-ligating braces, your orthodontist will carefully attach the brackets to your teeth . The absence of ligatures or ties makes this process simpler and less cumbersome.

Periodic appointments for adjustments are a key part of your treatment journey. Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is also crucial.

As your teeth align efficiently, you may experience a reduced treatment duration compared to traditional braces.

  • Treatment Duration : Self-ligating braces may lead to a potentially shorter treatment duration compared to traditional braces, but the exact timeline will vary depending on individual factors.
  • Cost Considerations : The cost of self-ligating braces may differ from traditional braces, so it’s essential to discuss the financial aspects with your orthodontist.
  • Oral Hygiene : While maintaining oral hygiene is generally more straightforward with self-ligating braces, it is still essential to be diligent in your dental care routine.
