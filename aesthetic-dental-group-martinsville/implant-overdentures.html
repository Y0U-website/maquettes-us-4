What Are They?

It’s not what you’d assume; dental implants are not replacement teeth, but replacement tooth roots. Implants provide the proper foundation for permanent or removable replacement teeth that are designed to match your natural teeth. Implant restoration Bridgewater involves creating a foundation to fill in those missing gaps in your teeth.

Do They Work Well?

The success of dental implants can vary, dependent on the shape of your jaw or where they are placed, but most patients experience a 95 percent success rate. With proper care, which includes flossing, mouth rinses, check-ups, and cleanings, your dental implants should last a lifetime.

Why Get Them at All

Get ready for an improved appearance, more confidence, less embarrassment (not having to remove dentures in front of others), and more enjoyment chewing and eating food. As you can see, there’s many advantages to getting dental implants. The biggest one is the comfort; dental implants are infused with your own teeth, so they feel natural and normal. Dental implants also help prevent further collapse of teeth and improve the health of the jaw. It’s a win-win situation.

Your Speech Improves

It’s something we don’t associate with teeth, but speech can be impacted with ill-fitting dentures. The dentures can slip inside the mouth causing you to slur or mumble your words. Dental implants vastly improve your speech patterns and you don’t have to worry about them slipping as you talk. Less embarrassment all around.
