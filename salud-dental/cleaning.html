Periodontal (Gum Disease) Therapy

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults — but it doesn’t have to cost you your smile. If you have signs of periodontitis, we offer scaling and root planing (deep cleaning) to remove bacteria below the gumline and stop the disease from progressing.

  • ✓ Deep cleaning: scaling and root planing
  • ✓ Targeted cleaning schedules for chronic gum disease
  • ✓ Antibiotic therapy as needed
  • ✓ Maintenance visits every 3–4 months for gum disease patients
