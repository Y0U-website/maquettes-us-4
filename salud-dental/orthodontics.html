Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Invisalign uses custom clear plastic aligners to gradually move your teeth — no brackets, no wires, no dietary restrictions. Virtually invisible and removable.

  • ✓ Virtually invisible — almost no one notices
  • ✓ Removable for eating and oral hygiene
  • ✓ No metal irritation — smooth plastic
  • ✓ Change aligners every 1–2 weeks at home
  • ✓ Fewer office visits than braces
  • ✓ Available for teens and adults

Retainers

After braces or Invisalign, retainers are essential to maintain your results and prevent teeth from shifting back.

  • ✓ Fixed retainers bonded to back of teeth
  • ✓ Removable Hawley and Essix retainers
  • ✓ Replacement retainers available
  • ✓ Critical for maintaining your straight smile
