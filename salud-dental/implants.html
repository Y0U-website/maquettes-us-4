All-On-4® Dental Implants

All-On-4® is a revolutionary full-arch restoration that replaces an entire row of teeth using just 4 strategically placed implants. Many patients can receive a complete new smile in a single day — dramatically less time and cost than traditional implant methods.

  • ✓ Full upper or lower arch replacement with 4 implants
  • ✓ Often completed in a single procedure — same-day teeth
  • ✓ More cost-effective than individual implants for full arches
  • ✓ Eliminates need for traditional removable dentures
  • ✓ Stimulates jawbone to prevent bone loss

Porcelain Bridges

A dental bridge fills the gap left by a missing tooth by anchoring a porcelain replacement tooth to the adjacent natural teeth. Bridges are a fixed (non-removable) solution that restore chewing function and smile appearance.

  • ✓ Fixed restoration — permanently cemented in place
  • ✓ Restores chewing ability and bite alignment
  • ✓ Prevents remaining teeth from shifting into the gap
  • ✓ Matched to your natural tooth color and shape
  • ✓ Typically completed in 2–3 appointments

Full & Partial Dentures

Modern dentures are more comfortable and natural-looking than ever. Full dentures replace an entire arch of missing teeth; partial dentures fill gaps when some natural teeth remain. Implant-supported dentures offer even greater stability.

  • ✓ Full dentures for complete tooth loss in upper or lower arch
  • ✓ Partial dentures when some natural teeth remain
  • ✓ Implant-retained dentures for improved stability
  • ✓ Custom-fabricated to fit your mouth comfortably
  • ✓ Improved chewing, speaking, and confidence
