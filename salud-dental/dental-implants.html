Single Tooth Implants

The most common implant procedure — one implant post topped with one porcelain crown. Ideal for replacing a single missing tooth anywhere in the mouth. The result is indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

  • ✓ Replaces one missing tooth root-to-crown
  • ✓ No impact on neighboring healthy teeth
  • ✓ Natural-looking porcelain crown perfectly color-matched
  • ✓ Average treatment time: 3–6 months (including healing)
  • ✓ Can last 20+ years with proper hygiene

Multiple Tooth Implants

When several teeth are missing, individual implants or implant-supported bridges can restore function and aesthetics far more effectively than removable partial dentures.

  • ✓ Individual implants for multiple missing teeth
  • ✓ Implant-supported bridge (fewer implants needed)
  • ✓ Preserves bone at every implant site
  • ✓ Fixed — does not remove like dentures

All-On-4® Full Arch Implants

All-On-4® is a breakthrough technique that replaces an entire upper or lower arch of teeth using just 4 strategically angled implants. Many patients receive a complete new smile in a single day.

  • ✓ Full arch of teeth supported by just 4 implants
  • ✓ Often completed in one day — immediate temporary teeth
  • ✓ No more removable dentures — fixed and permanent
  • ✓ More affordable than individual implants for full arches
  • ✓ Stimulates jawbone to prevent facial structure changes

Implant-Supported Dentures

Traditional dentures slip, restrict what you can eat, and accelerate bone loss. Implant-supported dentures are anchored by 4–6 implants — eliminating slipping and dramatically improving comfort, chewing power, and confidence.

  • ✓ Snaps onto implants for superior stability
  • ✓ No denture adhesive needed
  • ✓ Removable for easy cleaning
  • ✓ Better chewing force than traditional dentures
  • ✓ Prevents continued bone loss unlike traditional dentures
