Meet the Team

Doctors

Dr. Cynthia Belgum

Dr. Belgum established her Davis dental practice in 1983. Over the years she considers herself very fortunate to get to know wonderful patients who were interested in her focus on health. Together with some excellent hygienists and support staff, she grew the practice by improving dental health. The team’s goal was to develop and maintain long term relationships. She has loved her years working with everyone but was also happy after 39 years to pass the mouth mirror to Dr. Shannon, who is bringing a more technology-based approach to the same problems.

Dr. Shannon Chris

Dr. Shannon Chris is a dedicated and compassionate dentist. She has a bachelors of science in biomedical sciences from California State University Sacramento and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Unversity of Southern California.

Originally from the Fiji Islands, Dr. Shannon has brought her warmth and dedication to her practice. She takes great pleasure and learning of new dental technologies and researches how these can benefit her patient. Her unique background enriches her approach to patient care, blending her island heritage hospitality with top-notch dental expertise.

Dr. Shannon Chris is an active member of the California dental association (CDA) and American dental association (ADA). In her spare time, you will find her attending quilting classes, during various crafting, spending time with her family and especially her two dogs Rexcel and Rosco (who join her occasionally at the office as therapy pups) and hiking. It is her goal to visit all 63 national parks in the USA.

Dr. Shannon is passionate about providing high-quality dental care to her patients and looks forward to welcoming you in her practice.
