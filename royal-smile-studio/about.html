Outside Office

My approach to dentistry is to be someone that each and every parent/guardian can rely on for honest and compassionate care for their children. I know that the dentist office can be an awkward and sometimes even scary place in the eyes of a child, but I hope that with each visit parents and patients will feel at home and comfortable in our office.

I graduated from the prestigious Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry at University of Southern California in 2015 and have been practicing dentistry in the private sector for the last ten years. While I began my career working as a general dentist mainly treating adults, for the last 6 years I have worked in an amazing pediatric dental office in Acworth, Georgia. During that time I have grown to see the need for a more relational and personal dental office for kids, teens, and young adults here in Cartersville and I hope you’ll check us out.

In my free time I enjoy being outdoors (mostly playing golf or hiking with my amazing girlfriend Casey), relaxing at home, and trying to stay involved in the community. I’m an avid sports enthusiast and I’m always cheering on my two favorite teams -- the USC Trojans and Tottenham Hotspur.

Caring for Our Cartersville Community

Cartersville is home, and caring for our neighbors is part of our mission. We partner with local schools, support student programs, and volunteer weekly with the Help A Child Smile program in Northwest Georgia to bring essential dental care and education to children.
