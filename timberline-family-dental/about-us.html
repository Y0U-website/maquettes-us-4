Meet The Timberline Family Dental Team

Our Team

We believe comfort is a result of trust, combined with a natural chair-side manner and gentle touch. We earn your trust by constantly having your best interests at heart.

Dr. Brown and the staff pride themselves in offering the highest quality care available. Our fulfilment is a result of your happiness. We recognize that our practice wouldn’t exist without our patients and we strive to build meaningful relationships based on a true understanding of your needs.

Dr. Jason Brown, DDS Founder, Lead Dentist Dr. Jason Brown was raised in West Des Moines, IA and graduated from Valley High School.

Morgan Brown, ARNP Morgan Brown, ARNP grew up in Urbandale and graduated from Dowling Catholic High School.

She started nursing school at Mercy College of Health Sciences and began her passion of caring for others by working as a nurse’s aide at Methodist Hospital.

After becoming a registered nurse, she started her professional career in Pediatric Oncology at Blank Children’s Hospital, where she cared for children with cancer for eight years. During employment there, Morgan continued her education and earned her Bachelors of Science in Nursing degree and eventually her Advanced Registered Nurse Practitioner (ARNP) license through the graduate program at Chamberlain University.

She has always lived and worked in the Des Moines area and is currently practicing as an ARNP at Broadlawns Medical Center.

Morgan then became certified and currently practices as a licensed Botox injector. She offers Botox to patients through her own LLC, Injectable Solutions, for aesthetic purposes and is currently taking patients at Timberline Family Dental. Morgan is also fulfilling a managerial role at Timberline Family Dental as well.

Morgan and Dr. Jason Brown married in 2013 and enjoy making memories with their family of five. They have a son and identical twin girls, Chael, Raegan, and Remington. She enjoys exercising and leading a healthy lifestyle, traveling, reading, and spending time with family.

Tamara Doria Dental Hygienist Tamara was born in Morgantown, West Virginia and raised in Ames, Iowa. She attended Ames High School, Iowa State University, and went on to graduate from the Dental Hygiene Program at DMACC in 2001. Tamara’s mom was a hygienist in Ames, which sparked her interest in the field. She began dental assisting at the age of 15. Tamara has a continued passion for providing the best care for her patients.

Tamara has two dogs, Stella, and Ollie. She enjoys golfing, going on bike rides with her husband Andrew, and spending time with friends. Tamara has also been a season ticket holder to the Broadway Series at the Civic Center in Des Moines for years.

Denise Dental Hygienist Denise is originally from Stevens Point, Wisconsin and currently resides in Ames.

She attended the University of Wisconsin- Stevens Point where she obtained an Associates Degree in Liberal Arts in 1997. She then went on to complete her dental hygiene degree from NorthCental Technical College in Wausau, Wisconsin in 2000. She has taken many classes since then to include orofacial myology, sleep dentistry and craniosacral fascial therapy. She is fascinated by natural healing and functional medicine and likes to incorporate that knowledge when she is working with her patients.

In Denise’s spare time, she likes to go camping, hiking, biking, cross country skiing, traveling, reading and spending time with her husband and three children.

Amanda Dental Assistant Amanda is originally from Cedar Rapids and currently resides in Waukee, IA.

She attended Kirkwood Community College in 2021 and completed the dental assisting program and graduated with her associates degree in applied science in 2022. She went on and got her Expanded Functions Dental Assistant license in 2024. Since then she has been working as a dental assistant in the Ames area for 3 years before joining the team in January of 2026. She is passionate about helping patients feel more confident in their smiles.

In her free time she enjoys reading, cooking new recipes and exploring different coffee shops and restaurants.

Shirley Odegaard Patient Care Coordinator Shirley was born in Bloomfield, Iowa, and later moved with her family to Tipton, Iowa where then went on to graduate.
