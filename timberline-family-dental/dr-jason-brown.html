Dr. Jason Brown, DDS

Meet Dr. Jason Brown, DDS Founder, Lead Dentist Dr. Jason Brown was raised in West Des Moines, IA and graduated from Valley High School.

Following graduation, he attended Iowa State University, where he played four years of varsity ice hockey. Playing as a forward in 2007, he helped the Cyclones win the CSCHL tournament in his senior year.

Dr. Brown then went on to the University of Iowa College of Dentistry, where he graduated in 2011 as a Doctor of Dental Surgery.

Dr. Brown has since been practicing general dentistry throughout the Des Moines area including Pleasant Hill, Bondurant, Urbandale, and Ames.

Dr. Brown is passionate about all aspects of dentistry with emphasis in cosmetic dentistry. He has completed various continuing education programs in his own time to continue to excel in aesthetic dentistry, complex surgical extraction, endodontics, and implant placement with the most update research and practice techniques.

He has obtained both Invisalign certification as well as Dental Laser certification.

Dr. Brown is currently a member with the American Dental Association, Iowa Dental Association, and the Des Moines Dental Society.

Dr. Brown currently lives in Waukee with his wife and three beautiful children, son, Chael, and identical twin daughters, Raegan and Remington. They also have two family dogs, Ruby and Jazz. He enjoys physical fitness, being outside, playing hockey, arts and crafts, and spending time with family and friends.
