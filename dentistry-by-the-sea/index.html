We understand how important a healthy, confident smile is. When a tooth becomes damaged, weakened, or worn down, a dental crown can restore its strength, function, and appearance — giving you back your smile with confidence.

At Dentistry-by-the-Sea, we believe in dental care that’s not only effective but also esthetically pleasing. That’s why we use composite restorations — also known as tooth-colored fillings — to repair cavities and restore your teeth without compromising your smile.

Missing teeth can affect more than just your smile — they can impact your confidence, your ability to eat and speak comfortably, and even your long-term oral health. We offer dental implants as a permanent, natural-looking solution to restore both function and beauty to your smile.

Services

Unlike traditional silver (amalgam) fillings, composite materials bond directly to your tooth, providing both strength and a flawless finish.

A dental crown is a custom-made cap that fits over a damaged or decayed tooth. It protects and strengthens the remaining tooth structure while blending naturally with the rest of your smile.

A dental implant is a small titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root. It’s surgically placed into the jawbone, where it integrates with the bone over time to create a strong foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.
