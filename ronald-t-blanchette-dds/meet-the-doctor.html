Meet The Doctor

Ronald T. Blanchette, D.D.S.

General Dentist

Dr. Blanchette was born in Selma, Alabama. He received his undergraduate degree at Knoxville College. In 1982, he earned his DDS at UCLA. Dr. Blanchette has provided dental care in the Sacramento area for over 28 years. He is a respected member of the American Dental Association, the Sacramento Chapter of the National Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the Sacramento District Dental Association. He completes several hours of continuing education courses each year to ensure that his patients are receiving the most up-to-date dental care available.

Dr. Blanchette is the proud father of two daughters. He enjoys playing golf. In his spare time, he is very active in his community. He spends time mentoring young people and coaching youth sports.
