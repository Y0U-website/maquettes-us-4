We offer IV sedation, or “sleep” dentistry, for children who need a calmer path to healthy teeth, working alongside Blue Sky Anesthesia Associates (opens in a new tab) . Their nurse anesthesiologist brings hospital-level sedation into our office and monitors every breath and heartbeat while Dr. Natalie takes care of the rest.
