Orthodontics for All Ages

While many associate braces with teens, adult orthodontics is more popular than ever. We offer discreet options like Invisalign and custom retainers for adults looking to refine or realign their smile later in life.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign is a discreet, convenient solution for mild to moderate misalignment. These clear, removable trays gently shift your teeth into place without metal brackets or wires.

Benefits of Invisalign

Great for adults and teens alike, Invisalign is an excellent choice for busy lifestyles.

  • Virtually invisible and comfortable
  • No dietary restrictions
  • Easy brushing and flossing
  • Fewer in-office visits

Retainers & Post-Orthodontic Care

After orthodontic treatment, retainers are essential to maintaining your new smile. We offer both removable and fixed retainer options, customized for comfort and durability.

We provide

  • Clear removable retainers
  • Bonded retainers
  • Replacement retainers
