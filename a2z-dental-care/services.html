Services

If you have experienced an emergency dental situation, we have treatment options to help you. Contact our dental office to book your emergency exam today.

  • How long will I spend with my dentist? It depends on your appointment type. Routine visits like cleanings may take 45–60 minutes, while more involved treatments may take longer. We’ll always give you an estimate before your visit.
  • Is teeth whitening safe? Yes. Our whitening treatments are performed by trained staff following strict safety protocols, using products designed to protect your teeth and gums while delivering noticeable results.
  • Do you take insurance, HSA or FSA? We accept most major insurance providers, including Delta, Cigna, and Moda. We also gladly accept HSA and FSA payments. If a service isn’t covered, we’ll provide the paperwork you need to request reimbursement. Full insurance list
  • What’s your late/cancellation policy? Cancellations or reschedules within 48 hours of your appointment will incur the full cost of your appointment to your card on file. However, if you reschedule and complete a new appointment within 7 days, the rescheduled appointment cost will be discounted by 50 %. We have a 5-minute grace period for late appointments - please be sure to fill out your forms before heading in so check-in can be a breeze.
  • When should I come in for an emergency? If you have pain, swelling, bleeding, a chipped or broken tooth, or signs of infection (such as pus), call us right away for a same-day dental urgent care appointment.
  • What is considered a dental emergency? Any uncomfortable issue you are experiencing in your mouth is serious. And if it is painful, it may be considered an emergency. This could include: Severe toothache,
  • Uncontrollable bleeding from the gums or teeth,
  • A chipped or broken tooth,
  • A loose or accidentally removed filling,
  • And more.
  • If I need a procedure, how much is it going to cost? If you require a procedure, our team will work with your insurance to tell you the estimated cost. We prioritize transparency at A2Z Dental and we want you to be in the know before any procedure is completed! Don’t see what you need on the list? Give us a call at (908) 526-2224 and we can help you.
  • I have dental anxiety. What should I do? Let us know before your visit so we can make you comfortable. From gentle techniques to explaining each step, we’ll help you feel relaxed and in control throughout your appointment.
