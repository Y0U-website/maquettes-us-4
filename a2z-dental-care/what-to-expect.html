Skip the Wait Fill Out Your Forms Early Want to avoid arriving early for your first visit? Completing your patient forms ahead of time saves you time at check-in and gives you peace of mind knowing your insurance coverage and payment options are confirmed. The forms are provided to you before your appointment so you can walk in confident and ready for care.

FAQ’S Do I need to bring anything with me for my visit?

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card (if you have one), and a list of any medications you take. If you’ve had recent dental X-rays, bring those along too.

  • How early should I arrive for my appointment? We recommend arriving 15-30 minutes early to allow time for check-in, completing any necessary forms (if you haven’t done so already), and settling in.
  • What can I expect at my first appointment? Our friendly front desk team’ll welcome you, complete any needed forms, and have a thorough exam with one of our dentists. We’ll review your dental and medical history, take any necessary X-rays, and discuss your goals before creating a personalized treatment plan.
  • Will you review my dental and medical history? Yes, understanding your full health picture helps us provide the safest, most effective care for you.
  • How do you handle treatment planning for new patients? After your exam, we’ll go over any findings with you, explain your options, and work with you to choose the plan that best fits your needs, goals, and budget.
  • How long will my first visit take? Most first appointments take about 45–60 minutes, depending on whether you’re getting a cleaning the same day.
  • Can you help me understand my insurance coverage before treatment? Absolutely. Our team will check your benefits, explain what’s covered, and give you an estimate before we begin any treatment.
  • What if I don’t have insurance? We’ll review all your options, including payment plans, so you can still get the care you need without surprises.
  • How often should I schedule check-ups after my first visit? Most patients benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months, but we’ll recommend a schedule that’s right for you.
  • What if I have dental anxiety? Let us know! We have experience working with anxious patients and can take extra steps to make your visit more comfortable.
  • Do you offer same-day treatment for urgent needs? If we discover an urgent issue during your exam, we’ll do our best to treat it the same day whenever possible.
