Implant-Supported Dentures

The stability of implants. All the coverage of dentures. Just better.

Two options to fit your lifestyle and comfort: Fixed stays in place Removable gives you flexibility. Fixed Implant Dentures

For patients seeking a permanent, slip-resistant solution.

-‎ Screwed into implants—only removable by your dentist -‎ Feels and functions most like natural teeth -‎ Stays in 24/7: eat, sleep, and live without worry -‎ Best for those wanting maximum strength and simplicity -‎ Requires slightly more implants (often 4–6 per arch)

For patients looking for a secure but removable option.

-‎ Snaps onto implants with special attachments -‎ Easy to clean and remove at home -‎ Smaller number of implants needed (as few as 2) ‎-‎ More budget-friendly than fixed options -‎ Can be converted into a fixed implant in the future

Which is right for you? Lifestyle: Want to “set it and forget it”? Go fixed. Prefer flexibility? Try removable. Budget: Removable is more affordable upfront. Fixed is a long-term investment. Care Routine: Fixed requires brushing like natural teeth. Removable means daily cleaning out-of-mouth. Bone Health: Our team will assess if you need bone grafting to support your choice.

Taking care of your dentures

Take them out at night + soak them in water or cleaner.

Brush them gently every day—just like natural teeth.

Keep your gums and mouth clean with regular checkups.

  • 1. Tooth Extraction If the damaged tooth is still present, it will be gently removed. You’ll be fully numbed—no pain, just light pressure. Healing starts right away.
  • 2. Implant Placement A small titanium implant is placed precisely into the jawbone. This acts as a new root. You’ll be numb throughout, and healing takes about 3–4 months while the bone fuses to the implant. We’ll check in monthly with a quick X-ray.
  • 3. Healing Cap Placement Once the implant is stable, we’ll place a small healing cap to shape the gum and prep for your new tooth. This step may happen during implant placement or at a brief follow-up visit.
  • 4. Crown Placement You’ll come in for two final visits: first for impressions, then to place your custom-made crown. It’s color-matched and shaped to look and feel like a natural tooth—so you can smile, chew, and speak with ease.

  • How long do dental implants last? With good hygiene and regular dental checkups, implants can last 20 years or more—many even last a lifetime.
  • Do implant dentures feel different from regular ones? Yes—in a good way. They’re more secure, comfortable, and natural-feeling, especially when eating or speaking.
  • How many implants do I need for a full denture? It depends on your jaw and needs, but most full-arch dentures are supported by 4 to 6 implants.
  • Is bone grafting safe, and where does the bone come from? Yes, it’s a safe and common procedure. Grafts can come from your own body, a donor source, or a synthetic material—all biocompatible and carefully selected.
  • How long is the full treatment process—from bone graft to final implant? If bone grafting is needed, healing can take 3–6 months. After that, the implant is placed, followed by several more months of healing before the final restoration. We’ll guide you through each phase with care.
  • Will I be left without teeth during the implant process? No—temporary teeth or dentures are typically provided so you never go without a smile while your implant heals.
