Is this right for you?

  • Children and teens ready for comprehensive treatment
  • Adults who never had braces or whose teeth have shifted over time
  • Patients with crowding, spacing, or rotated teeth
  • Bite problems such as overbite, underbite, crossbite, or open bite

What to Expect

  • 1 Complimentary consultation We review your goals, take digital records and a 3D scan, and outline your options and estimated timeline, all at no cost and with no pressure.
  • 2 Placing your braces At your bonding appointment we attach the brackets and guide wire. It is painless, and most patients are in and out in about an hour.
  • 3 Adjustment visits You will see us roughly every 6 to 10 weeks so we can progress your wires and track movement. Self-ligating braces keep these visits short.
  • 4 Debond and retention When your smile is where we want it, we remove the braces and fit a custom retainer to protect your results for the long term.
