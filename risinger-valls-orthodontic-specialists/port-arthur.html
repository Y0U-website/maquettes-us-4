Personalized Care for Every Age

Orthodontic needs change as you grow. At our Port Arthur office we meet patients at the right time with the right plan, from early growth guidance to adult smiles.

Early Treatment (Phase 1)

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child&#x27;s first orthodontic screening by age 7. Evaluating early lets us recognize potential problems and correct them before they become more serious.

The Benefits of Early Treatment

By evaluating for treatment early, we can recognize potential problems and correct them before they become more serious. Early interceptive treatment has proven beneficial for the overall desired result. Because a child&#x27;s permanent teeth have not finished erupting, we can thoroughly evaluate and address impending problems at the earliest stage.

Careful Monitoring

Often, treatment is not necessary right away and we will simply monitor your child&#x27;s growth until they are ready. Our philosophy is to recommend treatment only when it provides a meaningful advantage.

Teen Treatment

It&#x27;s time to start thinking about braces once all the baby teeth are on their way out, typically around age 10–12. While it&#x27;s never too late for orthodontic treatment, the best results are achieved during the peak growth spurt.

Treatment Options for Teens

Treatment at this age consists of braces, appliances, and/or clear aligners depending on the custom treatment plan we present to you. We help teens choose an option that fits their lifestyle, activities, and confidence.

Adult Treatment

More and more adults are seeking orthodontic treatment to improve their smiles. We provide a full range of treatments designed specifically for adults that leave you with a dramatically different smile and a healthier bite.

Options Built for Adults

We offer a variety of clear aligners, braces, and appliances to meet the individual needs and desires of each adult patient. We&#x27;re confident we can help you achieve and maintain the beautiful, healthy smile you&#x27;ve always dreamed of.

Appliances & Technology

The right tools make treatment faster, more comfortable and more precise. These are some of the approaches our Port Arthur specialists use alongside braces and clear aligners.

Carriere Motion System

The Carriere Motion System corrects bite problems early in treatment with a sleek, comfortable, and non-invasive design, often before braces or aligners are placed.

Class II Correction

With its sleek, aesthetic, non-invasive design, the Motion 3D Class II Correction Appliance provides greater comfort and shortens treatment time by up to four months, delivering a natural, gentle, uniform force for distal molar movement before brackets or other appliances are placed.

Class III Correction

The Motion 3D Class III Appliance is a minimally invasive way to treat Class III malocclusions without extractions, painful jaw surgery, or obtrusive face masks. Used at the beginning of treatment, it delivers rapid correction before braces or aligners.

Advanced Technology

In our office, we use the latest technology available to provide superior treatment options, which means getting you out of braces or clear aligners faster.

CBCT Digital Scanning

Intraoral Scanner

The intraoral scanner is a wand-like device that captures 3D digital dental impressions, creating a detailed image of your mouth. These images are available instantly and used to plan your treatment, no messy molds required.

3D Printing

Using the scans we capture, we 3D print a variety of orthodontic appliances and diagnostic tools, allowing us to deliver even more precision to your care.
