Meet Our Team

Shilpa Dhawan, DMD

Dr. Dhawan earned her DMD from the University of Pittsburgh, School of Dental Medicine. She has been practicing dentistry for over 10 years and provides quality care to her patients keeping their goals in mind. Dr. Dhawan is known for her caring and compassionate approach in treating her patients.

She is passionate about educating her patients and the community about the importance of good oral health. She enjoys all aspects of dentistry and stays up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry with several continuing education courses over the years.

Apart from being a dentist, Dr. Dhawan enjoys reading, yoga and dancing. She lives with her husband and two lovely children.
