Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How do dental implants compare to bridges or dentures?

Unlike bridges and dentures, dental implants replace both the tooth and the root, preventing bone loss and offering a more natural look and feel. They also don’t require support from neighboring teeth, making them a healthier long-term solution for tooth replacement.

How long does it take to recover after getting dental implants?

Most patients experience mild soreness for a few days after implant placement. However, complete healing and integration with the bone (called osseointegration) typically take several months. During this time, patients can continue normal activities with minor dietary adjustments.

What kind of maintenance do dental implants require?

Dental implants should be cared for just like natural teeth—brush twice a day, floss daily, and visit your dentist for regular cleanings and exams. While implants cannot get cavities, keeping the gums and surrounding tissue healthy is essential for long-term success.

Can I get a dental implant right after tooth extraction?

In many cases, yes. If the bone and gums are healthy, an implant can sometimes be placed the same day as a tooth extraction. However, a healing period or bone graft may be needed first if there’s infection or bone loss. Your dentist in Sachse, TX, will determine the best timing for your situation.

Are dental implants safe for older adults?

Absolutely. Age alone isn’t a limiting factor. As long as you’re in good general health and have sufficient bone structure, you can successfully receive dental implants. Many older patients find implants life-changing—restoring comfort, confidence, and full chewing ability.

Will my dental implant look natural?

Yes. At Polaris Dentistry, each implant restoration is custom-designed to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth. The result is a seamless, natural appearance that blends perfectly with your smile.
