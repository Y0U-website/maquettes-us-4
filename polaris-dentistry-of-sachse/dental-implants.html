Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with healthy gums and enough jawbone are good candidates for implants. If you’ve lost one or more teeth due to injury, decay, or disease, we’ll evaluate your mouth and medical history to see if implants are right for you.

Is getting implants painful?

Most patients are surprised at how comfortable the process is. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, and any post-op discomfort is usually mild and manageable with over-the-counter medication. We’ll guide you every step of the way.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of a dental implant varies depending on how many teeth you’re replacing and if additional procedures (like bone grafting) are needed. At Polaris Dentistry , we offer transparent pricing and flexible payment options to help fit your budget.
