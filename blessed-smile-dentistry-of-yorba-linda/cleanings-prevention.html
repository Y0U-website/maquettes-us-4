Protect Your Smile with Preventive Dental Care

For most patients, visiting the dentist every six months provides the best protection against developing dental disease. Who Is a Good Candidate for Preventive Dental Care Preventive dentistry is recommended for virtually everyone. Whether you’re a child visiting the dentist for the first time or an adult maintaining your smile, routine care is essential.

Our dentists customize your preventive care schedule based on your individual oral health needs and risk factors. What to Expect During Your Appointment Your preventive dental visit is designed to be thorough, comfortable, and educational. Comprehensive Dental Examination

Our dentists examine your teeth, gums, bite, existing dental work, and overall oral health to identify any developing concerns before they worsen.

Professional Dental Cleaning

Our skilled dental hygienists remove plaque and hardened tartar from above and below the gumline using specialized instruments. Your teeth are then polished to remove minor stains and leave your smile feeling fresh and clean.

Gum Disease Evaluation

Healthy gums are essential for a healthy smile. We measure the health of your gums and monitor for early signs of gingivitis or periodontal disease.

Oral Cancer Screening

We examine your lips, tongue, cheeks, throat, neck, and surrounding tissues for any abnormalities. Early detection greatly improves treatment outcomes.

Personalized Recommendations

Before you leave, we’ll discuss our findings and recommend any necessary treatment or adjustments to your home care routine to keep your smile healthy.

Digital Dental X-Rays

Digital dental X-rays provide valuable diagnostic information while using significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays.
