Available Options For Dental Implants In Houston

Dental implants blend seamlessly into the smile to replace missing teeth with screw-like implants that adhere with the jaw bone. As you bite and chew with the attached dental crown , the implant provides the same bone stimulation as a natural tooth root, which keeps your bone levels healthy and protects the integrity of your smile for years to come.

Single dental implant: If you are missing even one tooth, your oral health is at risk. Without a tooth to stimulate the jaw bone, bone atrophy begins to occur which can lead to shifting of other teeth or increased risk of infection . We can replace your missing tooth with a natural-looking implant and crown the same day a tooth is removed .

Multiple Dental Implants: Missing more than one tooth is common and should be addressed immediately. Unlike a tooth supported bridge which relies on surrounding teeth to support the restoration , implants provide a healthy, more natural alternative to bridges , which will never shift, decay or require root canal.

Full arch dental implants: If you have dentures and are looking for a more stable, healthier alternative, we can complete your smile in one visit. This option uses a number of dental implants (as few as four) surgically placed in the jaw bone to support a custom, natural-looking prosthesis. There are no pastes or need to worry about slipping dentures ; this option is designed to stay in place.

What a Dental Implants is?

How Are Implants Placed?

The Dental Implants are placed under local anesthesia . Therefore, you will not have to experience any pain while you are going through the treatments. The surgical procedures followed for Dental Implants can take anywhere in between 30 to 60 minutes. It depends on the location of the implant in your mouth and the number of implants that are placed.

This is a surgical procedure, where the tooth is being placed with another one. It is done with the assistance of screw-like posts, which are made out of metal. The new tooth can be made to look and function in a similar way to the real tooth.

Why Dental Implants Matter?

If you leave an empty space in the mouth after losing a tooth, you will have to experience numerous health issues in the future. For example, it can lead you towards deterioration of jawbone. That’s where you must go ahead with dental implants. The implant procedure will help you to replace the missing tooth and stimulate bone growth. Along with that, you can refrain from bone loss and negative consequences associated with it.

Benefits of Dental Implants

The dental bridge you get will be able to deliver numerous benefits to you. For example, it can enhance your overall appearance and provide you with confidence to simile. Moreover, it can help you to enhance comfort, consume foods with ease and improve overall oral health. You can also receive added convenience with dental implants that you are getting. It is one of the best methods available for you to replace natural teeth in your mouth.

Do Dental Implants Require Special Care?

No, you don’t need to worry too much about taking special care of dental implants. You just need to maintain good oral hygiene habits and you will be able to take good care of the abutment and crown that you have inside the mouth. If you don’t pay attention to basic oral hygiene procedures, your dental implant may fail.

Do Dental Implants Need To Be Replaced?

Dental implants can last for a duration of around 30 years. In some of the instances, the implants you get will last forever as well. This is one of the most durable methods available for you to get missing teeth in your mouth replaced. If you can properly care for them, you will be able to enhance the durability effectively. Your dentist will provide you with basic information on how to take care of them.

An all on four dental implant treatment in Houston will cost around $12,000 to $25,000 per jaw. This is the average price and it can slightly vary. Are Dental Implants Safe?

Dental implants have been there for quite some time. They are proven to be safe and effective. On the other hand, it can be considered as one of the most predictable dental procedures that you can find within the industry as well.

What If My Implant Falls Off?

If your implant falls off, you will need to get in touch with your dentist immediately. Then he will check and see whether there is any possibility to re-insert the implant.

What If My Crown Falls Off?

If the crown falls off, you need to understand that you are dealing with a serious problem. That’s because it has to be screwed down again. You can call your dentist and schedule an immediate appointment. Then you will be provided with the assistance you need.

What is The Rate of Dental Implant Failures?

Dental implants are associated with a very high success rate. According to recent studies, it has been identified that the probability associated with dental implants to fail is around 5 % to 10 %. Most of them will fail soon after the procedure. However, the implants can fail after few months of few years as well.

Reasons for Dental Implant Failure

Dental implants tend to fail for many different reasons. The most common reason out of them is bone loss. Failing to adhere to proper oral hygiene habits can lead you towards this. In most of the instances, failed dental implants can be replaced. However, you must maintain proper oral hygiene and make sure that a failure doesn’t happen.
