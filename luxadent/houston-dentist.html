Our Orthodontic Services

At Luxadent, we offer a full range of orthodontic solutions to help you achieve a perfectly aligned smile .

Traditional Metal Braces

Braces remain the most effective and affordable option for straightening teeth and correcting bite issues. Today’s metal braces are smaller, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever before.

  • Severe misalignment
  • Overbites, underbites, and crossbites
  • Crowded or widely spaced teeth

Ceramic Braces (Clear Braces)

For patients looking for a more discreet option, ceramic braces provide the same effectiveness as metal braces but use tooth-colored brackets to blend in with your smile.

  • Patients who need braces but prefer a less noticeable option
  • Adults and teens concerned about aesthetics

Invisalign & Clear Aligners

If you want to straighten your teeth without metal brackets, Invisalign offers a comfortable, nearly invisible solution. These clear aligners are removable, making it easier to eat, brush, and floss compared to traditional braces.

  • Mild to moderate misalignment
  • Professionals and adults looking for a discreet option
  • Patients who want a flexible, removable treatment

Retainers & Post-Orthodontic Care

After completing your orthodontic treatment, retainers prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original position. Luxadent offers:

  • Fixed retainers (bonded to the back of teeth)
  • Removable retainers (clear plastic or traditional wire)

Early Orthodontic Treatment for Kids

For young patients, early orthodontic intervention can prevent serious alignment issues later in life. We provide:

  • Palate expanders to correct narrow jaws
  • Space maintainers for missing baby teeth
  • Phase 1 braces for significant alignment concerns

Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic care is not just about having a beautiful smile—it’s also about improving your overall oral health. Some benefits of straightening your teeth include:

  • Easier cleaning – Straight teeth are easier to brush and floss, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Improved bite alignment – Correcting overbites and underbites prevents jaw pain and wear on your teeth.
  • Better speech and chewing – A proper bite allows for clearer speech and more comfortable chewing.
  • Boosted confidence – A straight, attractive smile can enhance self-esteem and overall well-being.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the cheapest way to straighten teeth? A: Traditional metal braces are typically the most cost-effective option, but we also offer affordable Invisalign payment plans to make clear aligners accessible.

Q: How long does orthodontic treatment take? A: Most patients wear braces or Invisalign for 12 to 24 months , depending on the severity of misalignment.

Q: Does insurance cover braces or Invisalign? A: Many dental insurance plans cover part of the cost. Our team will help you maximize your benefits and find the best financing option.

Q: What’s the difference between Invisalign and braces? A: Invisalign is removable and nearly invisible , while traditional braces are fixed and suitable for more complex cases.
