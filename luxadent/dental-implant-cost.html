Are dental implants covered by my insurance?

In most of the instances, the health insurance plan you have will not cover the cost of dental implants. It will not even cover the cost associated with tooth implants. That’s because the insurance companies tend to classify that as a cosmetic dental procedure. But if a dental implant is needed due to an oral health related issue, you will have to go for it and your cost will be covered by the dental insurance policy. It is better to discuss this with the insurance company and confirm the facts, before you move forward with the treatment.

Do you offer payment plans for dental implants?

Since dental implants are not covered by the insurance plans, flexible payment plans are offered to the people who wish to go ahead with them. After moving forward with such a payment plan, patients will have to make a convenient payment on a monthly basis. You can discuss with the dentist and get to know about the payment plan options available. Some of the credit card companies are also providing extended payment plans to assist people without any interest.
