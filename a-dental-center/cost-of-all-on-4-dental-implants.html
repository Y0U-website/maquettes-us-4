What Affects the Cost of All-on-4 Dental Implants?

The implant brand matters more than most patients realize. At A-Dental Center, Dr. Elzayat uses premium FDA-cleared implant systems including Nobel Biocare, Straumann, and BioHorizons. These brands have decades of published clinical data and significantly higher long-term success rates than generic or unverified alternatives often used by discount providers. When you compare All-on-4 prices across offices, always ask which implant brand is being used. It is one of the biggest factors separating a $9,000 quote from a $20,000 one.
