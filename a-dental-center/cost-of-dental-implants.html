Dental Implant Cost by Tooth Location

Implant cost is generally consistent regardless of tooth location. Bone condition, not tooth position, is the primary cost variable. These are the most common location-specific questions patients ask.

How much does a front tooth implant cost?

Front tooth implants at A-Dental Center start from $2,799, the same as other single-tooth implants. Front tooth cases require precise aesthetic planning to match the color, shape, and contour of surrounding teeth. Dr. Elzayat uses digital planning to design the final crown before lab work begins.

Molar implants start from $2,799 at A-Dental Center. Molar placement requires careful bone assessment due to higher bite forces and proximity to the sinus in the upper jaw. A sinus lift may be needed for upper molar implants with limited bone height.

Does tooth location affect implant cost?

Generally no. Single implant pricing is consistent regardless of tooth position. Cost differences arise from bone condition, whether preparatory procedures like bone grafting are needed, and restoration complexity, not the tooth’s location in the mouth.
