Meet the dentist

Anthony ‘Tony’ DiPasquale, DMD has been delivering expert dental care to the Melbourne Area Community for more than 20 years. Dr. Tony’s Dentistry expertise is coupled with genuine concern for his patients. He continually strives to be ahead of the curve in his profession and is always on the cutting edge of dentistry. His staff is dedicated to your comfort and prompt attention. Their goal is to keep your teeth healthy and your smile bright.

Dr. Tony is a member of the American Dental Association, Central Florida District Dental Association and Brevard County Dental Association. He is a member of the fraternity of Delta Sigma Delta.

Dr. Tony is married. They are the proud parents of a daughter. During Dr. Tony’s free time he loves to cheer for his favorite football teams, collect coins and vacationing with his family at national parks throughout Florida and the United States.
