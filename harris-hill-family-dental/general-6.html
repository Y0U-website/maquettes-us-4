Teeth Whitening

It has never been easier to whiten teeth! There are multiple effective and safe options to choose from for any busy lifestyle.

Pre-filled trays

This is our most cost-effective solution, and it doesn t require an appointment or impressions! Recommended for patients looking to whiten on their own time without over-the-counter strips that tend to slide around. Wear these trays for 20-30 minutes at a time over 5-10 days. Make it your routine on your commute to work!

Custom fit trays

With this option we make custom-fit trays that perfectly adapt to your teeth for ultimate comfort and results. A gel is applied to the trays, which are worn for 30-60 minutes per day for 1-2 weeks. This option is highly effective in whitening teeth up to 5 shades brighter, even if you have struggled to see results from over-the-counter options!

In-office treatment

Have you tried to whiten at home but you can never stick to a schedule? This option allows you to achieve results in about an hour, right in our office! Perfect for special occasions and more dramatic whitening.
