Fluoride Treatment

A fluoride treatment is a simple yet powerful way to protect your dental health. Fluoride is a natural mineral that strengthens tooth enamel, making the surfaces of your teeth more resistant to tooth decay. After your cleaning, we may apply a professional-grade fluoride treatment to give your teeth an extra layer of defense against acids and bacteria.

We will carefully examine several areas, looking for unusual sores or lumps, red or white patches, or changes in texture in your mouth, lips, and tongue.

The length of your visit can vary, but most regular dental hygiene appointments are completed within an hour. How Often Should I Schedule a Dental Hygiene Appointment?

Most Westerville patients benefit from regular dental hygiene appointments every six months. These routine cleaning visits help maintain healthy teeth and prevent dental problems before they become serious problems.

However, some patients may need more frequent visits depending on their oral health needs. Individuals with a history of periodontal disease or gum health concerns may require more frequent cleanings to maintain their oral health. Our dental team will recommend the best schedule for your individual needs.

Patients of all ages can benefit from regular dental hygiene visits, from children developing healthy oral care habits to adults maintaining long-term dental health.

What Happens If I Skip Dental Hygiene Appointments?

Skipping regular dental cleanings can allow plaque and tartar to accumulate on your teeth. Over time, this buildup may increase your risk of cavities, gum disease, and other oral health problems.

Untreated gum disease can eventually lead to bone loss around the teeth, which may contribute to tooth loss if not addressed. Additionally, some dental issues develop gradually and may not cause symptoms right away.

Routine dental exams allow dental professionals to detect early warning signs before they develop into more serious problems requiring extensive dental work.

Maintaining regular dental hygiene appointments is one of the most effective ways to protect your healthy smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need to update my medical history at every appointment?

Your overall health affects your dental health. Updating your medical history ensures we are aware of new medications, conditions, or allergies that could affect your treatment or oral health care plan.

Do I have to get fluoride?

Fluoride treatments are optional but recommended because they strengthen tooth enamel and help prevent cavities, especially for patients at higher risk. We’ll discuss whether it’s right for you.

Will a dental hygiene appointment help my bad breath?

Yes—thorough cleaning removes the bacteria and buildup that often contribute to persistent bad breath. We’ll also give you tips for freshening your breath between visits.
