Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are widely considered one of the most effective ways to replace missing teeth. Patients throughout Westerville often choose implants because they provide several long-term benefits.

A Natural Look and Feel

Implants support a replacement tooth that closely resembles natural teeth. Because the implant is anchored in the jawbone, the artificial tooth feels stable and comfortable when eating or speaking.

Protection Against Bone Loss

When a missing tooth is not replaced, the surrounding jawbone can begin to shrink. Implant placement helps stimulate the bone and maintain the structure of your jaw.

Improved Oral Health

Unlike some other tooth replacement options, implants do not require altering nearby teeth. Your remaining natural teeth remain intact, which supports the long-term health of your smile.

Better Function

Implants provide strong chewing ability. Many patients find they can enjoy a wider variety of foods compared to wearing removable dentures.

Long-Term Durability

With proper care, tooth implants have excellent success rates and can last many years.

Bone Grafting for Lack of Bone

If there is not enough bone at the implant site, a bone graft may be recommended. Bone grafting helps rebuild the quantity of bone needed to support the implant body.

Over time, the graft encourages new bone cells to grow, strengthening the implant site. Once the graft heals, the dental implant process can move forward.

  • Your own bone
  • A donor source
  • Synthetic materials
  • In some cases, bone from an animal donor

What Is the Dental Implant Surgery Process?

The dental implant procedure is typically completed in stages over the course of several months. Each step allows time for proper healing and ensures the implant is stable.

Comprehensive Consultation

One of our Westerville dentists will examine your teeth, gums, and jawbone using digital imaging. This allows the team to determine the best type of implant and placement location.

Tooth Extraction (If Needed)

If a damaged or failing tooth is present, a tooth extraction may be performed before implant placement.

Implant Surgery

Dental implant surgery involves placing the implant body into the jawbone. The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to keep you comfortable.

After the implant is placed, we may attach a cover screw or healing cap to protect the top of the implant during healing.

Healing and Bone Integration

The implant integrates with the bone over a course of several months. This allows the implant to become securely anchored.

Are There Risks or Complications Involved?

Dental implants have very high success rates when performed by experienced dental professionals. However, like any surgical procedure, there are some potential risks to be aware of. In rare cases, complications may include infection at the implant site, implant failure if the implant does not properly integrate with the surrounding bone, or irritation of nearby nerves or sinus structures. Fortunately, these issues are uncommon and can often be prevented with careful planning and proper aftercare.

Maintaining good oral hygiene and attending regular dental visits are important for protecting the long-term health of your implant.
