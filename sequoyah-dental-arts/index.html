Dr. Pablo Foncea graduated with honors from Boston University in 1997. He began practicing dentistry in South Carolina with a well-respected cosmetic practice, but a desire to be closer to the mountains, prompted a move to Knoxville in 2001. He has practiced comprehensive, restorative and cosmetic dentistry in Tennessee ever since.

One of the things that make Dr. Foncea’s dentistry unique is his artistic skill and ability. He sees his dental work as an extension of his love of art and delights in the artistic challenges he encounters while making custom dentures and smile designs.
