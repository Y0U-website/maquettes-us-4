Meet Dr. Tarek Safadi.

Welcome to Angel Smiles Dental.

Dr. Tarek Safadi and his friendly, knowledgeable team are here to cater to the dental needs of families in Crown Point, Merrillville, and Valparaiso. The same courteous, caring, modern dentistry you’d expect from larger offices, in the cozy setting of a hometown clinic.

All of our patients are treated to the same courtesy, caring, and current dentistry, with the benefit of a small, hometown dental clinic. While our practice may pride itself on the hometown feel, it nonetheless has all of the modern equipment and treatment options found in larger offices.

Dr. Tarek Safadi, DDS

As a native of Valparaiso, IN, Dr. Safadi has his roots deeply entrenched in the community and understands the needs of his patients. He attended Loyola University in Chicago for his undergraduate degree and the University of Illinois College of Dentistry to complete his education. He continues his education to stay current with the latest dental procedures and treatments.
