If you ever wonder, "Do I qualify for dental implants?" you’re not alone. Many patients come to Burbank Valley Dental wondering if they’re eligible. Below are answers to some of the common questions our patients ask us regarding our dental implant treatments:

Once you’re approved for treatment, we’ll take an impression of your mouth to create a new tooth in the dental lab. Each synthetic tooth will match the size and shape of the surrounding teeth. Your implants will also have a perfect color match so your smile is as natural as possible. Additional preparation includes discussing medical history, x-rays, CT scans, oral exams, and dental impressions.

The next step is to implant a metal post under the gum line. Afterward, we’ll secure the new tooth to the metal post at the surface of the gums. The new tooth will attach so securely that you’ll be able to eat your favorite foods, maintain your active lifestyle, and more without worrying that your implant will fall out.
