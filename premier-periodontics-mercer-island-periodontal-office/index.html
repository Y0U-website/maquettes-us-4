The practice

Our modern practice is best accessed through the parking lot in the rear of the building, on the south end of the complex, where easy wheelchair access and ample free parking are available.

Dr. Samira Toloue and Dr. Edith Lin utilize the most advanced technology available today to deliver the highest standard of periodontal and implant care. They are supported by a skilled and compassionate team who help ensure each patient receives exceptional care and service.

We are proud to offer best-in-class services to patients throughout the community, including modern treatments like precision laser therapy, digital imaging technology, and more.

If you are seeking an experienced periodontist in Mercer Island, WA we invite you to contact Premier Periodontics to schedule your treatment consultation.

During a detailed consultation, we’ll help determine the right solution for your needs, whether it’s All-on-4® implants, immediate placement, or implant-supported crowns and bridges.

If additional support is needed, we also perform sinus lifts, ridge augmentation, and bone grafting to strengthen the jawbone for implant placement.

Every treatment is delivered with precision and care, using the latest techniques and technology to ensure long-lasting, natural-looking results.

Dental implants are titanium posts that replace missing tooth roots and provide a strong foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. They restore both the appearance and function of natural teeth while helping preserve jawbone health and facial structure.
