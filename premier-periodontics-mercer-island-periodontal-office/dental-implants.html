Implant Dentistry Services

Dental implants are titanium posts that replace missing tooth roots and provide a strong foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. They restore both the appearance and function of natural teeth while helping preserve jawbone health and facial structure.

We prioritize immediate implant placement whenever possible, allowing patients to complete extractions and implant surgery in one efficient appointment, cutting treatment time by up to 50 %.

Bone grafting strengthens and rebuilds areas of bone loss in the jaw. By using advanced techniques and biocompatible materials, we can create the solid support needed for successful implant placement and restore the natural contours of your facial structure.

A sinus lift increases the amount of bone in the upper jaw near the molars and premolars. This procedure makes it possible to place dental implants in areas where natural bone height is limited, improving stability and long-term success.

If a dental implant becomes loose or painful, our specialists can often restore it using techniques like the LAPIP™ Protocol. These advanced procedures are designed to treat infection, stabilize the implant, and preserve the surrounding bone and gum tissue.

All-on-4® implants offer a secure, streamlined solution for patients with full-arch tooth loss. This method uses four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, improving comfort, chewing ability, and smile aesthetics with minimal downtime.
