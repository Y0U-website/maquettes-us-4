Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Dental implants are convenient, permanent solutions compared to other cosmetic and restorative dentistry treatments. Because the titanium rod affixes to your mouth, the implant slides over naturally and rests comfortably.

Should you opt for other dental treatments, like dentures or partial dentures , you will have to take them out at night and clean them regularly. Permanent implants have less upkeep because you clean the false tooth while brushing the rest of your teeth.

If you think you’re a candidate for dental implants, schedule an initial appointment with Dr. Christine Han, DMD .

Our team will provide you with a thorough examination to determine the best treatment plan. Should your situation make implants difficult, we may recommend initial bone grafting to make more room.

What Implant Procedures Look Like

Implant treatment varies depending on the patient and how many teeth need replacing. We specialize in a few different tooth implant options. Dr. Han will numb the area with local anesthesia before removing the tooth and setting the titanium rods to hold the false tooth or teeth in place.

Single-Tooth Replacement

The initial implant will mirror the roots of the natural tooth it’s replacing. Once set into the bone, the implant merges with your bone to create a solid foundation. At this point, it can support a ceramic, porcelain, or metal crown. The new restoration will look, feel, and act as a natural tooth.

Multiple-Tooth Replacement

Often, we utilize pairs of implants to hold teeth in place. If you have a row of teeth that need replacement, we will fit the implants at each end where the base of the dental crowns and bridges can fit snugly. These cosmetic bridges will restore all functions, filling the space of several missing teeth.

Full-Mouth Reconstruction

Full-arch implants work well in patients who need a secured denture that they never need to remove. We develop a hybrid restoration that creates a cosmetically-pleasing result, giving you the full functionality of natural teeth.
