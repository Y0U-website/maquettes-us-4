A neighborhood practice, built on care.

Blacklick Creek Dental is a family-led, modern dental practice serving Reynoldsburg and the surrounding Blacklick communities — with the kind of one-on-one attention you rarely find anymore.

Care that feels personal — because it is.

We opened Blacklick Creek Dental because Reynoldsburg deserved a dental practice that treated patients like neighbors, not numbers. That shows up in small ways: longer appointment slots, clear treatment plans, and a team that remembers what’s going on in your life between visits.

Whether you’re here for a six-month cleaning, a brand-new crown, or your child’s very first dental visit, you’ll get the same calm, attentive care.

Ankit Patel, DDS

Dr. Patel earned his doctorate at the NYU College of Dentistry, then completed a general practice residency at the VA Hospital in Brooklyn — where he treated veterans with complex medical histories and learned how to keep care calm under pressure. He continued his training at Mount Sinai, sharpening the surgical and restorative skills he uses every day.

What patients notice first is how he listens. He won’t recommend a treatment unless he’d recommend it for his own family. And he’ll always walk you through the options — including the option of waiting.
