Insurance & Payment

We accept Medicaid and most major PPO dental plans and we’ll verify your specific coverage before any treatment begins.

Expanded Medicaid coverage.

We’re proud to accept Delta Dental Triadvantage Medicaid (formerly CareSource) and Molina Medicaid , in addition to most major PPO insurance plans. Walk-ins are welcome, and we’ll help you understand your benefits before any treatment begins.

Not sure whether your plan is in-network? Call (614) 860-0065 with your member ID and we’ll verify before your visit.

In-network, out-of-network, or none.
