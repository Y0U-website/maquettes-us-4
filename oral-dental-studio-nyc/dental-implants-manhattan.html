After implant surgery, a crucial healing period begins during which the implants integrate with the jawbone through osseointegration. Our team closely monitors this healing phase to ensure optimal implant integration and patient comfort, laying the groundwork for the final restoration.

Dental implants provide unmatched comfort and stability when compared to removable dentures. By securely attaching to the jawbone, they eliminate the hassle of slipping or shifting, seamlessly fitting into your everyday routine.
