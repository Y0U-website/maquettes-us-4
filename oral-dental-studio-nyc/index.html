Experience Comfortable Dental Care with Nitrous Oxide at ORAL Dental Studio

At ORAL Dental Studio NYC, located on the 2nd floor at 131 Essex Street in Manhattan’s Lower East Side, we prioritize your comfort and well-being during every dental visit. We understand that dental anxiety is a common concern, which is why we offer nitrous oxide sedation dentistry—a safe and effective way to help you relax during treatments.

Nitrous oxide, commonly known as "laughing gas," is a mild sedative administered through a comfortable nasal mask. It induces a state of relaxation while keeping you fully conscious and able to communicate. The effects wear off quickly after the procedure, allowing you to resume your daily activities without downtime.
