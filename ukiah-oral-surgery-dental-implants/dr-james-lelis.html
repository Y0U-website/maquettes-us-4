Dr. James Lelis in Ukiah, CA

Contact Us James Lelis, DDS, MD A Salt Lake City, Utah native, Dr. Lelis made his way to California after graduating with a Bachelor’s Degree in Biology from the University of Utah. He attended Dental School at the University of California, San Francisco where he served his classmates as a member of the student government as well as various oral surgery related positions and received the Horace Wells award for outstanding achievement in the field of dental anesthesiology from the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology.

In addition to training in the full scope of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery during his residency, Dr. Lelis received training in General Surgery, Otolaryngology (ENT), Adult and Pediatric Anesthesiology, Internal Medicine, Pediatric Emergency Medicine and more. He is passionate about utilizing the skills and knowledge he received during his training to provide the highest quality whole person care to all of his patients in a kind and compassionate manner.

More about Dr. Lelis

An Eagle Scout, Dr. Lelis finds joy in the outdoors and spending time with his wife Nichole and two boys, Henry and Harrison.
