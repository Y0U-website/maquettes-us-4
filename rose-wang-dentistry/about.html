About Rose Wang Dentistry

Rose Wang Dentistry conveniently provides excellence in comprehensive dental care right in your community. We specialize in modern techniques and cutting-edge technology, while remaining anchored in delivering personalized care specific to your needs and goals. Under the leadership of our highly skilled mother-daughter team, Rose Wang Dental is the modern professional dental practice you need with the warm and caring experience you desire.

About Rose Wang

D.M.D. & Owner/Practioner

Dr. Rose Wang is a General Dentist practicing in Lexington, Massachusetts.

She graduated with a D.M.D. degree from the University of Pennsylvania, School of Dental Medicine in 1996. She also holds an M.S. degree in Oral Science from the State University of New York at Buffalo.

About Jessica Cheng

D.M.D. & Associate Dentist

Dr. Cheng is a General Dentist practicing in Lexington, Massachusetts.

She graduated from University of Pennsylvania, School of Dental Medicine with a D.M.D. degree in 2017. She then completed a 1-year General Practice Residency program at New York-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell.

Dr. Cheng was first exposed to dentistry at an early age through her mother (Dr. Wang!), who instilled in her a passion for helping patients improve not only their smiles but also their dental health. She is excited to learn more and is constantly trying to improve her knowledge to better help her patients.

About Haohao Wang

Associate Dentist

Dr. H. Wang graduated from the New York University College of Dentistry in May 2025 and holds a Bachelor of Arts in Biology from Oregon State University. He joined our team in December 2025 and has quickly become a valued part of our practice.

Dr. H. Wang is highly skilled in radiographic interpretation, diagnosis, and comprehensive treatment planning. He provides a wide range of dental services, including emergency care, preventive cleanings, fillings, root canal therapy, crowns, extractions, and dentures. His clinical approach focuses on delivering thorough, personalized care tailored to each patient’s needs.

Known for his calm and compassionate demeanor, Dr. H. Wang is dedicated to building strong relationships with patients from all backgrounds. He takes time to clearly explain treatment options, helping patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their care. Dr. H. Wang also has a special focus on supporting patients with dental anxiety, ensuring a gentle and reassuring experience throughout their visit.

Dr. H. Wang values teamwork and collaboration within the clinic and works closely with dental assistants and staff to maintain a supportive, respectful, and patient-centered environment.
