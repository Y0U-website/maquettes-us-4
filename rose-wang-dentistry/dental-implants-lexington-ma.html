Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants in Lexington are a remarkable solution for individuals seeking to restore missing teeth and rejuvenate their smiles. Here’s what you need to know about this transformative dental procedure:

Dental implants in Lexington have revolutionized the field of dentistry by offering a permanent, natural-looking, and functional solution for missing teeth. Consult with a skilled dentist to explore if dental implants are the right choice to restore your smile and improve your overall quality of life.

  • Strong Foundation Dental implants provide a sturdy foundation for replacement teeth. They consist of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, mimicking the natural root of a tooth. This provides unparalleled stability and support.
  • Natural Appearance The crown or prosthetic tooth placed on top of the implant is carefully designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. This results in a seamless and natural-looking smile.
  • Improved Function Dental implants restore more than just aesthetics. They offer enhanced functionality, allowing you to eat, speak, and chew with confidence. Unlike removable dentures, implants won’t slip or shift.
  • Bone Preservation When a tooth is lost, the jawbone can start to deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. Dental implants stimulate the bone, preventing further loss and preserving facial structure.
  • Longevity With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. This makes them a cost-effective and durable solution in the long run.
  • Versatility Dental implants can replace a single tooth, or multiple teeth, or even provide support for full dentures. Your dentist will tailor the treatment plan to your specific needs.
  • Comfort Implants become a permanent part of your mouth, eliminating the discomfort and inconvenience associated with removable dentures.
  • Health Benefits By restoring your ability to chew and enjoy a wide range of foods, dental implants can contribute to better nutrition and overall health.
  • Preserving Adjacent Teeth Unlike traditional bridges that require adjacent teeth to be modified, dental implants don’t impact neighboring teeth. They stand independently, ensuring the health of your remaining natural teeth.
  • Success Rates Dental implant procedures have a high success rate. Proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits are essential to maintaining their longevity.
  • Consultation If you’re considering dental implants, start with a consultation with a qualified dentist. They will assess your oral health, review your medical history, and determine if you’re a suitable candidate for the procedure.
  • Treatment Process The process typically involves several stages, including implant placement, a healing period where the bone fuses with the implant (osseointegration), and finally, the attachment of the crown or prosthetic tooth.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults are candidates for implants. However, certain conditions, such as gum disease or insufficient bone mass, may require additional treatment before surgery. Our specialized training allows us to handle complex cases with care and precision. During your consultation, we will let you know if bone grafting or periodontal therapy is necessary before moving forward.

If you have been told in the past that you are not a candidate, advances in implant dentistry may change that. Our Lexington team will work with you to explore all available options.

Types of Dental Implant Restorations

At Rose Wang Dentistry, we customize implant treatments to meet each patient’s needs:

Single Tooth Implants Ideal for replacing one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown. Ideal for patients seeking a seamless, permanent alternative to a bridge.

Implant-Supported Bridges Used when two or more teeth in a row are missing. The bridge is anchored securely in place with implants for added strength and stability, making it easier to chew without stressing adjacent teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures For patients missing an entire upper or lower dental arch, implants can securely hold dentures in place. This option gives patients the confidence to speak and eat without worry, often transforming their quality of life.

Regardless of which option you choose, our dedicated approach to patient care ensures that treatment is tailored to your specific needs and focused on restoring both your health and aesthetics.

The Dental Implant Process

Every patient’s treatment plan is unique, but most follow a step-by-step process that ensures success:

Throughout the process, our dedicated team makes sure you are informed, comfortable, and supported at every visit.

Patients who stay proactive with their care often enjoy decades of use from their implants. At every follow-up, we will monitor your restoration and ensure your smile continues to function optimally.

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Wang evaluates your oral health, bone density, and medical history to confirm you are a candidate.
  • Surgical Placement: The titanium post is gently placed into the jawbone. Advanced technology enables this step to be precise and efficient.
  • Healing and Integration: Over several weeks, the bone bonds to the implant, creating a solid foundation.
  • Final Restoration: A custom crown, bridge, or denture is secured to the implant, completing your smile.
  • Read More: Caring for Your Dental Implants Maintaining implants is simple: brush and floss as you would with natural teeth, and schedule regular dental checkups. While implants cannot decay, the gum tissue and bone supporting them still need care. Professional cleanings and at-home oral hygiene will help your implants last a lifetime. For best results, patients should: Use a soft-bristled toothbrush to avoid scratching the crown surface.
  • Floss daily, especially around the gumline, with implant-specific floss or water flossers.
  • Avoid chewing on hard items, such as ice or pens, that can damage both natural teeth and restorations.
  • Quit smoking, as it can slow healing and reduce implant success rates.
