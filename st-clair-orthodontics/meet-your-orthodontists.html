Meet Dr. Nicole Kim

Associate Orthodontist

Dr. Nicole Kim has been a practicing orthodontist for nine years. She also has extensive experience working with cleft lip and palate patients through the Washington University cleft lip and palate fellowship and is currently a diplomat of the American Board of Orthodontics.

But more importantly, Dr. Kim aligns with our practice’s mission and core values. We exist to love on people and help them flourish, and Dr. Kim is committed to that mission and will help us fulfill it both for our patients and for our team.

Dr. Kim grew up in sunny California and attended dental school in Arizona. The day after they graduated from dental school, she married Dr. Charles Roberts. She then went on to complete her orthodontic residency in St. Louis, Missouri. After Dr. Kim and her husband settled in west Texas, they adopted their fur baby, Zorro. Dr. Kim loves to travel and experience new cuisines and cultures. She enjoys learning new things from books, people, YouTube, and the Bible.
