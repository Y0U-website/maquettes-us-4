The practice

We offer same-day appointments and flexible hours for emergency situations.

During your complimentary consultation, we will take photos and x-rays, complete a smile analysis, and customize your treatment plan. You will meet your doctor, and we will review and discuss your financing options.

Early treatment starting at age 7 allows your orthodontist to monitor your child’s growth patterns, focusing on the proper time to employ braces or orthodontics.

We offer our patients the latest cutting-edge orthodontic care with the Damon System. This system uses gentle force, protecting the health of your teeth and gums and working on a beautiful and natural smile.

As Lubbock’s Top Invisalign Provider, we offer this clear alternative to traditional braces.

At-home whitening syringes are provided at the end of orthodontic treatment to put the finishing touches on your smile. We also offer Zoom Teeth Whitening, an easy procedure that restores the natural color of the teeth in just a few visits.

Your airway is the path for air to reach your lungs. Improper function of your airway can hurt your overall health. Orthodontics can help by correcting unhealthy behaviors and patterns and promoting healthy oral and facial development in children.

In 2012, St. Clair launched Live Thankfully, a community-wide food donation drive to serve food-insecure families in West Texas. Since its launch in 2012, more than 3,000 families have received a Thanksgiving meal through Live Thankfully. The goal is to foster a sense of hope and love during the holiday season.
