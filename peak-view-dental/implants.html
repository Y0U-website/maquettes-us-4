Our Dental Implant Process

Starting from your very first visit, we’re here to make your dental implant experience as smooth and stress-free as possible. Whether you’re replacing one tooth or many, here’s what the dental implant journey typically looks like at Peak View: Comprehensive Consultation

We begin with a thorough dental exam, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess your jawbone health and customize your treatment plan.

If a damaged tooth or inadequate bone is present, we’ll extract the tooth and perform bone grafting to ensure your jaw is ready for implant placement.

We surgically place the titanium implant into your jawbone. Over 3 6 months, it fuses with the bone to create a stable, permanent foundation.

Once healed, we attach a connector (abutment) and custom-designed dental crown or prosthetic that matches your natural teeth.

We’ll provide follow-up visits and hygiene tips to help your implants last for decades.
