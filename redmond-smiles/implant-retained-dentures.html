Implant Supported Dentures…

  • Comfortable fit . Because implant-supported dentures do not rely on suction or adhesives to stay in place, they tend to be more comfortable and less irritating to the gums.
  • Better chewing ability . The stability provided by the dental implants allows for better chewing function, making eating a wider variety of foods easier.
  • Better speech . Traditional dentures can sometimes slip or cause difficulty with speech, but implant-supported dentures provide a more natural feeling and allow for clearer speech.
  • Long-lasting solution . With proper care and maintenance, implant-supported dentures will last patients for many years, making them a durable and cost-effective option.

Can anyone get implant-supported dentures, or am I not a candidate?

Implant-supported dentures are not suitable for everyone. Candidates for snap-in dentures must have enough bone in their jaw to support the implants and be in good overall health. Dr. Isabella Amar thoroughly examines your medical history before determining if you are a good candidate for this treatment.

What is the process for getting implant-supported dentures?

Dr. Isabella Amar and her staff will examine your general oral health and discuss your objectives and expectations at your initial evaluation and consultation session at Redmond Smiles. This session marks the beginning of your road to a new smile. If implant-supported dentures are the best choice for you, a personalized treatment plan will be developed.

Two procedures are usually required: one to insert the dental implants into the jawbone and another to fasten the abutments, or metal posts, to the implants. Interim dentures may be used until the final denture can be attached to the implants.

Once fully healed, impressions of your mouth are taken to create a custom-made denture that fits comfortably and securely onto the implants. Dr. Isabella Amar makes adjustments as needed to ensure proper fit and function.
