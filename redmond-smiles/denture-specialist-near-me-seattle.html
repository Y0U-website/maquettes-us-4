What are dentures, and how exactly do they work?

Dentures are quality, custom-made dental appliances that are designed to replace missing teeth and restore both appearance and functionality of your smile. Dentures are specifically crafted to look natural and provide a secure fit, allowing you to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. Depending on your needs, dentures can replace some or all of your teeth. Think of them as not only a solution for missing teeth but also a gateway to better oral health and improved quality of life.

What are immediate dentures?

Immediate dentures are a wonderful option for individuals who need to have teeth extracted and want to leave the office with a new smile the same day. These dentures are made in advance and immediately placed after teeth extractions. Immediate dentures allow you to avoid the inconvenience of living without teeth during the healing process. While they may require adjustments as your gums heal and change shape, immediate dentures provide an effective temporary solution for maintaining both aesthetics and function.

How are partial dentures different?

Partial dentures are uniquely designed for patients missing only a few teeth. They use a combination of a sturdy base and clasps that attach to your natural teeth, ensuring a comfortable and secure fit. Partial dentures can fill in gaps while keeping your remaining natural teeth in their appropriate positions. They are an affordable and efficient way to enhance the smile and prevent further dental issues caused by missing teeth.

What about using complete dentures for my smile restoration?

Complete dentures are best for patients who are missing all their teeth on either the upper or lower jaw—or both. These dentures restore the complete arch and are customized for your unique gum shape, ensuring they fit snugly and function smoothly. Advanced materials allow complete dentures to look incredibly natural, blending seamlessly to create a beautiful smile.

What are implant-supported dentures, and what makes them popular?

Implant-supported dentures provide the ultimate in stability. Unlike more traditional types of dentures that rest on the gums, implant-supported dentures are anchored by titanium implants placed in the jawbone. These implants act almost as artificial tooth roots, offering a secure, long-lasting foundation for the dentures. Implant-supported dentures prevent slipping, enable better chewing, and even help maintain natural bone structure in the jaw, making them an advanced choice for long-term oral health.
