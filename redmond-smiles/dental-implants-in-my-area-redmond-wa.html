Dental Implants: What Are They and How Do They Work?

Dental implants are titanium restorations that act like replacement roots for a missing tooth. Dr. Amar describes them as anchors for your new teeth. Once placed into the jawbone, they fuse with the bone over time through osseointegration. In the end, you have a stable base for a crown, bridge, or a set of implant-supported dentures.

What Is the Process for Getting Dental Implants at Your Office?

It all starts with a consultation with Dr. Amar. After he reviews your dental history, the team will take X-rays or scans to give him a map of your jaw. The implant surgery comes next. Once the titanium posts are placed, healing will take a few months as the post fuses with your bone. After healing, an abutment and a porcelain crown are added to complete your tooth.

How Do Dental Implants Compare to Other Tooth Replacement Options?

Implants are secured in the bone. Dentures rest on the gums, and dental bridges rely on nearby teeth or implants. Implants last a lifetime, don’t damage surrounding teeth, and help prevent bone loss in the jaw. Over time, dental implants outlast the alternatives, saving both time and future dental work.

What Are the Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants for Missing Teeth?

Dental implants do more than replace teeth. They also restore your bite, along with improving your speech. But equally important, they keep your facial structure intact. Plus, they stay put. No slipping. No glue. You can:

  • Eat the foods you enjoy.
  • Smile without worrying about slippage.
  • Speak with clarity.
  • Maintain healthy bone and gum structure.

Can Anyone Get Dental Implants?

You’ll need enough healthy bone and decent oral health to qualify for dental implants. If you don’t have enough bone mass, Dr. Amar may recommend bone grafting. Also, if you have gum disease or tooth decay, he will want to address these issues first.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last, and What Is the Success Rate?

With good care, your dental implants will last a lifetime. It’s important to continue practicing good oral hygiene at home. Regular checkups at our office are important for continued oral health.

Success rates for implants are high, usually hovering around 95 percent. Avoiding tobacco and sticking to follow-up visits helps keep those numbers strong and stable.
