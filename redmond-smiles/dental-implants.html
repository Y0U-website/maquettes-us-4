Dental Implants Redmond, WA

  • Implant surgery. Once the initial evaluation is complete, the actual implant surgery can begin. The first step is to make an incision in the gum tissue and drill a hole into the jawbone where the implant will be inserted.
  • Osseointegration. After the implant is placed, it needs time to heal and fuse with the surrounding bone tissue. Osteointegration, as this process is called, typically takes several months.
  • Abutment placement. Once the dental implant is fully integrated into the jawbone, a small connector, an abutment, is placed on top of the implant. This serves as a base for attaching the replacement tooth or bridge.
  • Final restoration. The final step involves placing and securing the replacement tooth or bridge onto the abutment. Your dental team will ensure it fits properly and feels comfortable before completing the process.
