Dr. Michelle Swift Corcoran, DDS

She’s a girl boss, dentist, wife, Christian & mom to five! With her passion for happy healthy smiles and genuine concern for the comfort and overall health and wellness of her patients, Dr. Michelle Corcoran, owner of Peppermint Sage Dental Wellness in Lake Charles, Louisiana, is going above and beyond to learn all about biological dentistry and how oral health plays a big role in overall health and wellness.

A graduate of Barbe High School in Lake Charles, LA, Dr. Corcoran received her undergraduate degree from Louisiana State University (LSU) in Baton Rouge, and continued on to LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery. But her education has not stopped there. She continues to seek out continuing education opportunities in order to stay up-to-date on the latest and safest in dental treatment best practices and advances in dental equipment and technology.

Dr. Corcoran is a devoted Christian, loving wife to Sean, and proud mother of their five beautiful children - John Francis, Zizi, Henry, Elijah and Sophia. She is currently on the Board of Directors for the Southwest Louisiana Dental Association and the Board of Directors for Krewe of Mistique.

Whether it is providing exceptional dental care, wrapping her family and friends in warmth and love, or giving back to the community in meaningful ways, Dr. Corcoran is on a mission to change her world and the world of her family, friends and patients, with hopes to help you change your world too!
