What Dental Implants Are and How They Work

Here s what most people don t realize about dental implants: they re not just fake teeth. They replace the entire tooth crown and root which is a big deal. Dentures sit on top of your gums. Bridges lean on neighboring teeth. Implants? They go straight into the bone, and your body actually accepts them like they belong there.

That s what makes them the gold standard. If you ve been dealing with temporary fixes that crack, loosen, or just feel wrong every few years, implants are a completely different animal. We re talking about a restoration built to last the rest of your life.

Think of an implant as three pieces working together:

So the magic behind all of this? It s a biological process called osseointegration. Basically, your living bone cells grow into the titanium surface and fuse with it. The implant literally becomes part of your jaw. Pretty incredible when you think about it.

1. Initial Consultation and Imaging We start with 3D CBCT scans same advanced imaging technology we use right here at our Brooklyn office. This lets us check your bone density, map out where nerves run, an1d plan placement down to the millimeter. No guessing.

3. Healing and Osseointegration Now comes the waiting game three to six months while your bone heals around the implant. During this phase, you re not walking around with a gap. We ll set you up with a temporary tooth so your smile stays intact.

4. Attaching the Abutment Once that implant is rock-solid in your bone, we pop on the abutment the little connector piece. Your gums need a short time to heal around it. Quick and straightforward.

5. Final Restoration Last step. Your custom crown goes on, and that s it. Eat whatever you want. Smile without thinking twice. You re done.

  • The Implant Post: A tiny titanium screw that gets placed into your jawbone. It basically becomes your new tooth root. Your body treats it like real bone grows right around it and locks it in.
  • The Abutment: This is the connector. It sits on top of the post and gives your new tooth something solid to grab onto.
  • The Restoration (Crown): The part everyone sees. We custom-make it from porcelain or zirconia so it matches your other teeth shape, shade, everything. Nobody should be able to tell the difference.
  • Bone Preservation: Lose a tooth, and the jawbone underneath starts shrinking. It just does. Implants stimulate that bone and keep it from collapsing which is what changes your whole facial structure over time if left alone.
  • No Slipping: Dentures shift around. They click. You need adhesives. Implants don t move. Period.
  • Longevity: A bridge might last you seven, maybe ten years before it needs replacing. Dentures? Same story, plus constant relining. A well-care16d-for implant can genuinely last a lifetime and that s exactly what our patients expect when they sit in our chair.

  • Single Tooth Implant: Usually $3,000 to $6,000. That includes the implant post, abutment, and crown. All of it. We don t break things out separately to make the starting number look artificially low.
  • Implant-Supported Bridge: Somewhere between $6,000 and $12,000, depending on how many implants and teeth are involved.
  • All-on-4 Dental Implants: Full arch restoration runs $20,000 to $35,000 per arch. The range in All-on-4 pricing across dental implants NYC providers comes down to which implant brand they re using, what the final restoration is made of (acrylic vs. zirconia), and whether you need bone grafting or extractions.
  • Zirconia Full Arch: This premium option lands at the higher end because the material costs significantly more but it s also significantly tougher and more lifelike than acrylic.
