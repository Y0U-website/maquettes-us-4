Ian R. McDonald DDS Dentist in Hickory, NC

Dr. Ian R. McDonald moved to Hickory, NC , as a child in 1988. He graduated from Saint Stephens High School. He received a BS in psychology from UNC-Chapel Hill. After shadowing several local dentists and volunteering with NC Missions of Mercy, he decided that dentistry was his calling. He became operations manager for NC Missions of Mercy, where he developed a strong interest in providing dental care to those in need. He attended UNC dental school, where he served as a class officer, received the 2016 Spurgeon Dental Class Award and the Robert Tormey Award.

After graduating from UNC dental school, he and his wife, Janelle (also a UNC dental school graduate), moved back to Hickory, where Ian’s parents and siblings all live. They are looking forward to becoming involved in the community and volunteering with local dental outreach programs. Ian is a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the American Dental Association, the North Carolina Dental Society, the Seattle Study Club, the Western Piedmont Dental Society, and serves on the CVCC School of Hygiene Advisory Committee.

When not involved with their dentistry, Ian and Janelle enjoy birding, boating on Lake Hickory, local sightseeing, and frequenting local restaurants. They live with their two cats, Abby and Pete, who always keep the household interesting.

Wanda, Registered Dental Hygienist

Wanda has a bachelor of science from UNC-Chapel Hill. A member of our team since February 2004. Wanda’s credentials are exceptional, and her professionalism impressive. Her goal as a hygienist is to create a long and lasting relationship with her patients and work together with the patient as part of the team to achieve optimal oral health.

Education, Wanda feels, is one the most crucial roles of a dental hygienist. "It’s very important for me that patients never feel they have been lectured or nagged. Rather, patients should be well informed about their oral health and how it can affect their overall physical health and how to maintain healthy gums and teeth." Wanda’s gentle, caring approach quickly wins the confidence of even the most dental- phobic patients. It’s not uncommon for her patients to say, "I just love her."

As a busy wife, mother of two daughters, and career woman, Wanda maintains a great spirit and sense of humor in all things.
