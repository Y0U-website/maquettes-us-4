Meet Your Dentist Dr. Devon Kutanovski Dr. Devon Kutanovski has been serving the Crown Point community with expert dental care built on integrity and patient-first values. Her diverse experiences and genuine passion for her work make Dr. Kutanovski not just a skilled dentist, but a caring, relatable professional committed to your oral health.

Her journey in dentistry is as diverse as her background. Born in St. Petersburg, FL, and raised in California&#x27;s Bay Area, Dr. Kutanovski found her way to Chicago during her high school years. It was at the University of Connecticut where her passion for dentistry first took root.

"Dentistry allows me to combine my love for science with my desire to help others," Dr. Kutanovski explains. "I&#x27;m fascinated by the complexity of oral health and find great satisfaction in working with patients to improve both their dental well-being and their confidence in their smiles."

After earning her B.S. in Biology with minors in Molecular and Cellular Biology and Philosophy from UConn, Dr. Kutanovski went on to achieve her DMD from the UIC College of Dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Indiana Dental Association, the Northwest Indiana Dental Society, the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics, and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

When she&#x27;s not at the office, Dr. Kutanovski cherishes time with her family—her two sons Graham and Reese, husband Andrew, and their two dogs, Boone and Bjorn. The Kutanovskis are an active bunch, often found outdoors skiing, hiking, or boating. In those rare quiet moments, you might find Dr. Kutanovski unwinding with a good book.
