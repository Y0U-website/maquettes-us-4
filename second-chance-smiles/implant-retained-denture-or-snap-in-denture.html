What to Expect During Your Denture Treatment (Implant-Retained Denture / Snap-In Denture)

Your denture process begins with a detailed exam, digital imaging, and, when necessary, impressions to create dentures that fit precisely. If implants are part of your plan, small titanium posts are placed in your jawbone and used as anchors for your denture. For qualifying patients, we may provide a temporary denture on the same day — giving you a Same Day Smile so you don’t have to go without teeth.

After healing, your final denture is fitted and snaps onto the implants for a secure, stable fit. We make any necessary adjustments to ensure optimal comfort, bite function, and appearance. Because the denture snaps into place, you can easily remove it for cleaning while enjoying the confidence and stability of implant support.
