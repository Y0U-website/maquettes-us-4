Single Tooth Implant Replacement

A single implant is used to replace one missing tooth. After the implant fuses with the jawbone, a custom-crafted crown is attached to restore full function and appearance. This option:

  • Looks and feels like a natural tooth
  • Restores chewing and speaking ability
  • Protects surrounding teeth from shifting
  • Helps preserve jawbone density

Bridge Implant Replacement

When multiple adjacent teeth are missing, implant-supported bridges offer a strong, long-lasting solution. Instead of relying on natural teeth for support, implants are placed to hold the bridge securely in place. This approach:

Many patients qualify for a Same Day Smile , meaning temporary or even permanent restorations can be placed immediately so you leave with a functional, complete smile the same day as your procedure.

  • Fills larger gaps with durable, natural-looking restorations
  • Provides better stability than traditional bridges or partials
  • Helps maintain proper bite function and jawbone health
