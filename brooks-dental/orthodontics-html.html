Invisalign Clear Braces

Many men, women, and teens shy away from the notion of spending years in metal braces. During your orthodontic consultation with Dr. Bruno, don’t hesitate to ask about Invisalign clear braces. These custom aligners are virtually invisible and effectively guide your teeth into their proper positions without metal brackets or wires. Since Invisalign aligners are removable, you won’t have to alter your eating habits or oral hygiene routine. You’ll love the versatility and freedom you can enjoy throughout your treatment as well as the incredible results that follow.

Traditional Orthodontics

There’s a reason why bracket-and-wire braces are still popular, even with Invisalign available traditional braces work! This treatment is effective for children, teens, and adults and often comes with a smaller price tag. Affixed to the surfaces of your teeth, this tried-and-true system shifts them over time to close gaps, create space, and realign bites for a more functional and fluid smile.

If you or your child have more severe alignment issues, or if you’re simply looking for a more affordable option to straighten teeth, Dr. Bruno can provide the treatment you need here in our comfortable, familiar office.

Interceptive Orthodontics

Dr. Bruno’s expertise doesn’t just include traditional braces and Invisalign clear aligners but also interceptive orthodontics. Children who require treatment to fix existing dental problems and/or need help to reduce the risk of future ones can receive headgear, functional appliances, limited braces, specialized retainers, expansion appliances, and space maintainers. Our in-house orthodontist will evaluate your child’s needs during a scheduled consultation to determine which method will be best to improve their smile.
