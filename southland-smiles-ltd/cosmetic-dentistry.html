What are the benefits of Invisalign®?

Invisalign treatment uses a series of customized plastic aligners that move teeth into the proper position over time. Every two weeks, we’ll replace your aligners that will shift your teeth where they need to be. Invisalign can treat many of the same orthodontic issues as braces, including crooked, gapped, or crowded teeth. It can also correct minor bite problems.
