About Southland Smiles, Ltd.

At Southland Smiles, we’re so much more than a traditional dental clinic.

As a team of passionate and dedicated dentists and dental professionals, we work together to empower our community ‒ one smile at a time.

Located in Flossmoor, IL, our practice is committed to providing a comfortable, enjoyable, and rewarding experience for patients of all ages.

Utilizing state-of-the-art techniques and technology , we offer a diverse range of general , cosmetic , and restorative dental services for your utmost comfort and satisfaction.

Our Dentists

Dr. KHADIJA SULEMANJEE

Dr. Sulemanjee is a recent addition to Southland Smiles, bringing with her several years of experience in the dental field.

Her love for dentistry comes from her desire to help her patients feel comfortable and confident in their smiles while improving their overall oral health.

She has a passion for interacting with people and enjoys learning about their unique stories, which she uses to provide the most personalized care possible.

Dr. Sulemanjee earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Illinois at Chicago in 2019.

She is a member of professional organizations including the American Dental Association (ADA) and the American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA).

In addition to her formal education and professional affiliations, Dr. Sulemanjee is committed to continuing her education.

She has attended CE courses on Dental Implants, Invisalign and Oral Conscious Sedation.

Dr. Juana Ryckowski

We are excited to add Dr. Juana Ryckowski to our team of Dentists.

She started in the dental field over 10 years ago as a Dental Assistant. Her passion for Dentistry and caring for others inspired her to become a Dentist.

Dr. Ryckowski graduated from the University of Illinois at Chicago in 2020.

She is bilingual, speaking both English and Spanish. She wants to ensure the Hispanic community gets the dental care they need without any language barriers.

Dr. Ryckowski is Invisalign certified and is very skilled in all aspects of General Dentistry.

She loves Cosmetic Dentistry and helping patients achieve the smile they always dreamed of.

She places an emphasis on providing tailored Dentistry by listening to each patient, answering their questions, and helping them make informed decisions on their treatment options.
