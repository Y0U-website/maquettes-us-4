Hi, I’m Dr. Lisa Lam. I earned my Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University College of Dentistry in 2008 and completed my residency at Lincoln Medical Hospital. As a dedicated dentist, I’m known for my commitment to providing personalized dental care to my patients.

Driven by my desire to make a positive impact in my community and craft healthy smiles, I established my dental practice with a focus on building strong relationships and delivering high-quality care. I strongly believe in the importance of patient education and take the time to explain treatment options in a clear and compassionate manner.

Throughout my career, I’ve remained committed to staying at the forefront of advancements in dentistry. I regularly attend continuing education courses and incorporate the latest techniques and technologies into my practice to ensure optimal outcomes for my patients. I’m proud to be a member of Spear, CDOCs, and the International Academy of Biological Dentists.
