TMJ Disorder Services

TMJ is the temporomandibular joints that connect your jaw to your skull. This joint allow you to open and close your mouth, speak, and chew food. If you have jaw clicking, headaches, earaches, pain when your mouth is opened widely, and tenderness of jaw muscles, you may be suffering from TMJ Disorder.

Pronghorn Family Dentistry can diagnose you and work to alleviate your pain. We uncover the underlying cause of your discomfort and take the best path towards a solution. This may be recommending an orthodontist or creating a custom mouthguard depending on your needs! Speak with us today to learn more!
