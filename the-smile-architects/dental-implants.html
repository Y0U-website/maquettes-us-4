Are You a Good Candidate for a Dental Implant?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for implants, though a few factors matter most. Adequate jawbone density, healthy gum tissue, and good overall oral health all play a role in how well an implant holds up over time.

Enough jawbone to anchor the implant post securely. Bone grafting can address deficiencies in many cases.
