About Us

Menu Dr. Anna Simon Dr. Simon has many years of experience and skills providing excellent orthodontic care. She earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine and post-graduate Certificate in Orthodontics from the well-renowned Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. Prior to that, she received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the Central University School of Dentistry in Venezuela. She began practicing orthodontics in the Boston area in 2001.

Dr. Simon maintains her skills and continues her education with training in the latest orthodontics techniques. She is also a member of numerous professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, American Association of Orthodontics, and Hispanic Dental Association. Dr. Simon is also fluent in Spanish.
