The Dental Implant Process

First, the dentist evaluates you. If you’re a candidate for implant dentistry, we then plan your treatment, which includes placing the implant (or implants) into the jawbone using image-guided technology.

After placement, the implant integrates with the jawbone, and we add a crown or prosthetic to complete the work.

Dental implants may be single (one implant and one tooth crown), or the dentist can use up to six implants to secure an arch of replacement teeth to the gum.

How long does the dental implant restoration process take?

Dental implant restorations can vary from patient to patient, depending on your oral health, the number of implants needed, and how well your jawbone heals and fuses with the implants.

Each Step In the Process

The full dental implant process can take anywhere from 3 to 6 months, depending on healing and any additional procedures required. We are here to make the process go as smoothly as possible.

  • You and your doctor will have a consultation to map out the plan and expected timeframe for dental implants. This could vary between 1 and 2 visits, so that your doctor can take x-rays and CT scans, and we can tell exactly where you need your implants.
  • If needed, you can have a tooth extraction done. Now, this isn t a step that everyone needs in their implant timeline, but if you have weak or damaged bone in your mouth, we can extract it to make sure you have a good foundation for your implants, and this healing process can take up to 4 months.
  • Surgery for your implant should take about an hour, during which we place the implant post into your jawbone. You will be fully numb for this part, and you shouldn t feel pain, likely just a slight pressure.
  • The healing process should take around 3 to 4 months, and in that time, osseointegration happens, which is your jawbone fusing to the implants. This is the critical time to listen to your doctor about healing habits after surgery.
  • After surgery and your mouth has healed, we go back in to place an abutment and restoration. This is where we go in and attach everything, then we test your bite to make sure everything is correct.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

Here’s a quick rundown of the dental implant process:

  • Initial Consultation First, we’ll take a close look at your oral health and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants. We’ll work with an oral surgeon who will perform the surgical placement of the implant.
  • Dental Implant Placement The oral surgeon places the titanium post into your jawbone. Over the next few months, your jawbone and the implant will fuse together in a process called osseointegration . This gives your new tooth a solid foundation.
  • Restoration Once the implant is fully healed, we step in to design and place the restoration. This can be a single crown, a bridge, or even a full set of dentures, depending on your needs. Our goal is to make sure your restoration blends seamlessly with your natural teeth, so you can smile with pride!
  • Final Touch After the restoration is in place, we’ll make sure everything fits comfortably and looks amazing. You’re now ready to show off your new, fully functional smile!

How to Care for Your Dental Implants

Taking care of your dental implants is just like caring for your natural teeth. With proper routine care, you’ll enjoy your new smile for years to come! Here’s what you need to do:

  • Brush and floss daily. Keep your dental implants sparkling clean by brushing at least twice a day and flossing daily. It’s especially important to clean around the gumline to prevent plaque buildup and gum disease.
  • Schedule regular checkups. Come in for your regular dental visits so we can monitor your implant restoration and make sure everything is functioning as it should.
  • Protect your smile against teeth grinding. Do you grind your teeth at night? We can provide you with a custom mouthguard or nightguard to protect your smile.
