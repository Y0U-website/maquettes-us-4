Dental implants are restorations designed to restore the entire tooth, including the root. They offer a strong, sturdy restoration that you can bite and chew with and use just like a natural tooth. You can even brush and floss it right alongside your other teeth.

The implant consists of several parts. The implant itself fits into the jawbone, and after dental implant surgery, the bone tissue grows into and around the implant, essentially fusing it to the jawbone. An abutment is attached to the implant, and it is to this abutment that a dental crown or other restoration is secured.
