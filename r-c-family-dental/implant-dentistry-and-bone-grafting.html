What Are the Best Dental Implants Benefits?

Our Expert dentist Dr. Fallah in Royse City, Texas can help you learn more about the many benefits of dental implants.

  • Longevity: With good care, dental implants can last a lifetime, and they have a success rate of over 95 %.
  • Comfort: Dental implants fit, function, and feel just like your own teeth. You’ll be able to smile confidently, speak clearly, and eat everything you love without worrying about your teeth.
  • Convenience: Unlike dentures, you won’t have to remove your implants at the end of the day. They’re just like your own teeth!
  • Protection: There’s no need to alter natural teeth for support, like bridges require, and you don’t need to take them out at night for cleaning or to let your oral tissues rest. Because your jawbone is stimulated each time you bite and chew, it will stay stronger, too.

Answers for your doubts

Adequate jawbone density is also essential to the success of your implant surgery, but if your jawbone has lost bone density, you may be a candidate for bone grafts, which can ensure you’ve got ample bone tissue to support the implant and your new tooth.

Are There Multiple Kinds of Dental Implants?

The most common types of dental implants are endosteal implants, which are screw or rod-like devices that fit into the jawbone, and subperiosteal implants, which are attached to a metal framework that sits below the surface of the gums on top of the jawbone.

How Do I Take Care of My Dental Implants?

Dental implants don’t just function like natural teeth. They also require the same care that natural teeth do. While the porcelain restorations are impervious to decay, your gums can still be damaged by oral bacteria. Good dental hygiene that includes twice daily brushing and daily flossing will reduce the risks of any post-implant health issues.
