What to Expect: The Single Tooth Implant Process

Every implant case at Berry Road Dental starts with a consultation with Dr. Croom, who personally evaluates your smile, reviews your dental and health history, and takes the imaging needed to plan your treatment. If the bone in the implant area is too thin or soft to support a post, she may recommend bone grafting first to build a solid foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I don’t replace a missing tooth?

Over time, the teeth next to the gap can shift or tilt, and your bite may change as a result. The jawbone under the missing tooth can also shrink since it’s no longer being used, which can affect the shape of your face and make future treatment more involved. Replacing the tooth sooner rather than later helps avoid these added problems.

How much does a single tooth implant cost in Norman, OK?

Cost depends on factors like whether bone grafting is needed, the type of crown used, and your insurance coverage. During your consultation, we’ll review your case and give you a clear, personalized estimate before any treatment begins, along with your options for insurance and financing.

How long does the implant crown process take?

The full process usually spans a few months from start to finish, mainly because the implant post needs time to bond securely with the jawbone before the crown can be placed. Your exact timeline depends on your healing and whether any preparatory treatment, like bone grafting, is needed.

Am I a candidate for a single tooth implant?

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone to support an implant are good candidates. Factors like gum health, bone density, and certain medical conditions can affect candidacy, which is why Dr. Croom personally reviews your history and imaging at your consultation to confirm the best plan for you.
