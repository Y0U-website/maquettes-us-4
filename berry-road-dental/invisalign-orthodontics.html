What to Expect: Your Invisalign Treatment Journey

Getting started with Invisalign at Berry Road Dental is simple. During your first visit, we ll take digital scans of your teeth and talk with you about what you d like to change about your smile. From there, we build a custom treatment plan and show you how your teeth are expected to move over time.

Once your aligners arrive, you ll wear each tray about 20 to 22 hours a day, only taking them out to eat and brush. You ll switch to a new tray every one to two weeks and come in every six to eight weeks so we can check your progress. Most patients finish treatment with a straighter smile and a retainer to help keep it that way.

What Happens If You Wait to Straighten Your Smile

It s tempting to put off orthodontic treatment, but crowded or misaligned teeth tend to get harder to clean over time. That can lead to more plaque buildup, cavities, and gum irritation. Bite problems left untreated can also add extra strain on your jaw, which sometimes leads to discomfort down the road.

Starting treatment sooner, while crowding or spacing is still mild, often means a shorter, simpler path to a straighter smile. Waiting doesn t always make treatment easier, and in many cases, it makes it more involved.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign treatment plans take about 12 to 18 months, though this varies based on how much your teeth need to move. Some minor cases finish in as little as six months. We ll give you a personalized estimate after your initial exam.

Is Invisalign right for teens?

Yes, Invisalign can be a great option for teens once their adult teeth have come in. It s discreet, removable for sports and photos, and doesn t require the diet restrictions that come with metal braces. We ll check your teen s bite and tooth development to confirm they re a good fit.

Does insurance cover Invisalign?

Many dental insurance plans include some coverage for orthodontic treatment, including Invisalign. Berry Road Dental accepts most major PPO plans, and our team will help verify your benefits and explain your coverage before you start treatment.
