About Dr. Amy Croom

Amy Croom is a trusted dentist in Norman, OK, and a second-generation Oklahoma dentist who has spent her whole career close to home. She earned her dental degree from the OU College of Dentistry in 1998 and has been in private practice for more than 23 years.

Patients appreciate that Dr. Croom accepts most major PPO insurance plans and always makes room for new patients who want a dentist they can stick with for years to come. To learn more about the full team behind her care, visit our About Us page.

Ready to get started? Call us at (405) 329-7950 or schedule your visit online whenever it’s convenient for you.

Military Service & Community Roots

Before settling into full-time private practice, Dr. Croom served as a Major in the Army National Guard, where she held the role of State Dental Officer. That background shaped the way she practices today: steady under pressure, careful with planning, and focused on the whole patient, not just the tooth in front of her. Her family’s roots in Oklahoma run deep, and that local, personal connection is a big part of why so many patients think of her as their long-term, forever dentist.

Areas of Focus: General, Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry

Dr. Croom’s day-to-day work covers a wide range of care, from routine checkups, cleanings, and preventive care to more advanced treatment. On the cosmetic side, she helps patients brighten and reshape their smiles through options on our Cosmetic & Orthodontic Dentistry page, including veneers, whitening, and bonding. She also provides restorative dentistry , such as crowns, bridges, and root canals, for patients who need to repair damaged or worn teeth.
