Cost depends on how many implants you need, whether bone grafting is required, and the type of restoration you choose. We review your full treatment plan and go over costs with you in detail before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

What s the difference between All-on-4 and single tooth implants?

A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth with its own implant post and crown. All-on-4 uses just four implants per arch to support a full set of replacement teeth, making it a good option for patients missing most or all of their teeth in one arch.

What happens if I don t replace a missing tooth?

Leaving a gap can cause nearby teeth to shift, change your bite, and lead to bone loss in that area over time. Replacing a missing tooth helps protect your remaining teeth and keep your jawbone healthy.
