Your Clear Braces Consultation

During your consultation, our dentists will carefully examine your teeth and jaw using digital x-rays and imaging technologies to determine a correct diagnosis.

If you decide that Invisalign is right for you, they will take digital impressions of your teeth and use those impressions to develop your treatment plan.

Generally speaking, most patients with mild-to-moderate misalignment are candidates for Invisalign. That being said, if we discover a more severe orthodontic issue, we may provide you with a referral to seek traditional orthodontic treatment instead.
