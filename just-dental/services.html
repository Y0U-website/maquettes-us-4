Just Dental Services

Cleanings

Extractions

Any simple extractions, we can do! If any extraction we deem as difficult or might have complications, we will refer you to a specialist.

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Patients missing multiple or need to replace all of their teeth in their mouth may undergo full mouth reconstruction. Procedure is customized to every patient’s needs to help restore and enhance their smile.

Implant

Dental implants are medical devices implanted into the jaw to replaces the tooth and root of the tooth to provide support for the artificial tooth. Benefits to implants include restoring the ability to chew and cosmetic appearance, preserving your gum health from diseases, and keeping the nearby teeth structure stable.

Bone Graft

Surgical procedure to replace missing bone to restore and strengthen the bone structure. Bone grafts may be necessary if patient is getting a tooth extracted or getting ready for an implant or denture.

Root Canal Therapy

An endodontic treatment when the pulp inside your tooth has become infected or inflamed. During a root canal, the pulp is removed and the tooth is cleaned and filled to restore it back to its full function.

Fillings

Dental fillings are used to treat a decaying part of a tooth and stop it from decaying any further.

Crowns/ Veneers

Crowns and Veneers are caps, usually made of porcelain, made to match and enhance your smile. There are multiple reasons why you would get crowns/ veneers such as having a heavily decaying toot, broken or fractured tooth or filling, or just cosmetic enhancement. If your current crown or bridge is loose or pops off, we offer services to re-cement.

Dentures/ Partials

If you have missing teeth and surrounding tissue, you may need dentures. If some of your teeth are still remaining, you may need partials. While waiting for the partial or denture to be delivered, we also offer a Stay Plate in the mean time so your gums and supporting bones can heal and you can eat and drink as you normally would.

Invisalign

Invisalign® are an alternative teeth strengthening treatment to braces using clear aligners.

Night Guard

For those who grind their teeth, a Night Guard helps protect your teeth from touching. It’ll also alleviate the stress on your jaw when you sleep to improve a good night’s rest.

Retainer

Retainers help hold your teeth in place so they don’t shift. If you’re looking for a replacement retainer, we can make one for you.
