Q: Is Invisalign painful?

A: Mild pressure or soreness during each aligner change is normal, as you wear them throughout the treatment, but it’s typically less intense than traditional braces—no wires poking or sharp brackets.

Q: What happens after I finish Invisalign treatment?

A: To maintain your new smile, you’ll wear retainers as directed—usually nightly. This helps ensure the results are long-lasting and include proper care of your new smile.

Q: What issues can Invisalign correct?

A: Invisalign can address a wide range of orthodontic problems: spaced or crowded teeth, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and more—many cases that other providers might not treat, all with clear aligners.

Q: How long does Invisalign treatment take?

A: Dr. Galkin will give you an estimate during your consultation, making Invisalign a fixed option compared to other traditional treatments.

Q: Can I eat normally during Invisalign treatment?

A: Absolutely, Invisalign allows you to experience an unobstricted diet. Your aligners are removed before eating or drinking (except water), and you brush before putting them back in. This means no food restrictions compared to braces.

Q: How often do I need appointments with Invisalign?

A: Check‑ups are spaced out—typically every 10-14 weeks. This is less frequent than braces and supported by our advanced digital tracking.
