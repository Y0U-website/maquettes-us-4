Orthodontic Evaluations Starting at the age of seven, we recommend children regularly receive their first orthodontic evaluation. An exam is the best way to determine if you have an orthodontic issue, or if an underlying issue may be forming.

These exams help identify existing issues and determine the possibility for future concerns. We invite patients of all ages to come in for an evaluation whenever they may be considering orthodontic treatment.
