FAQs About Implant-Supported Dentures

Are implant dentures permanent?

They can be either permanent (fixed) or removable. Fixed implant dentures stay in place and are only removed by your dentist, while removable options snap in and out for cleaning.

How many implants will I need?

Most full-arch dentures require 2 to 4 implants per jaw. We offer All-on-4 and customized plans depending on your bone quality and goals.

What’s the cost of implant-supported dentures?

Costs vary based on your treatment plan, number of implants, and materials used. We provide detailed estimates and financing options at your consultation.

Do implant dentures hurt?

Implant placement is a surgical procedure, but we offer sedation options to keep you comfortable. Most patients report minimal discomfort during healing.

How long do implant dentures last?

With proper care, implants can last decades. The denture portion may need replacing after 10–15 years.
