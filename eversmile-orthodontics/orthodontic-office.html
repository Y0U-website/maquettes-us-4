About Us

Orthodontics With Intention

At Eversmile Orthodontics, we’re not just straightening teeth. We’re elevating lives.

Founded by Dr. Ryan Davis, a board-certified orthodontist with a passion for science, art, and patient-centered care, our practice blends advanced techniques with a human touch to deliver beautiful, healthy smiles that last a lifetime.

Dr. Davis’s journey into orthodontics began when he experienced firsthand the life-changing confidence of his own braces. That moment led to a fulfilling career, shaped by rigorous research, top-tier education, and a love for helping others. His deep roots in Utah and Ohio, adventurous spirit, and artistic background infuse our office with purpose and heart.

Our Mission

To construct occlusions that last a lifetime and create smiles that foster confidence and joy. We focus on crafting customized smiles that are not only beautiful but also healthy, functional, and resilient. Our goal is to enhance our patients’ lives well beyond the end of treatment.
