Why Straight Teeth Are About More Than Appearance

Misaligned teeth create friction in more places than the mirror. When your bite does not close properly, the load is distributed unevenly across your jaw joints, which can quietly drive chronic tension, clicking, and discomfort over time. We approach orthodontics the same way we approach everything else in our practice: we look at the basement up, with a structured forward plan that connects what we see in your alignment to how your whole system functions.

The link between alignment and TMJ and bite alignment is something many patients do not realize until we explain it in their first appointment. One patient came to us having already completed orthodontic treatment elsewhere. Her teeth looked straighter, but her jaw pain and sleep disruption were getting worse, not better. We placed her back in Invisalign® specifically to address the bite mechanics behind her TMJ pain and sleep apnea, and her first question was: Why was this not done before? That story says more about our approach than any list of services could.

Alignment is not just a cosmetic checkbox. It is a foundational part of oral health, and treating it well now prevents a lot from going wrong later.

Five Reasons Patients Choose Clear Aligners at Icon Dental

Before we get into how the system works, here is what our patients consistently tell us matters most when they choose clear aligners over other options.

Subtle and Aesthetic

The trays are clear and close-fitting, so most people around you’ll not notice you’re in treatment. For adults who want effective orthodontic correction without the look of metal brackets, this matters.

Comfort You Can Feel

There are no wires to poke and no brackets to irritate your cheeks. Each tray applies gentle, controlled pressure, and most patients adjust within a day or two of switching to a new set.

Freedom to eat and clean normally

You remove the aligners to eat, drink anything besides water, and brush and floss. Your diet does not change, and keeping your teeth clean during treatment is straightforward.

A faster path for eligible patients

For patients who qualify, we offer acceleration treatments.This stimulates bone response to aligner forces and can reduce overall treatment time for the right cases.

Healthier teeth and gums over time

Straighter teeth are easier to clean thoroughly. Crowded or overlapping teeth create spaces where plaque accumulates and brushing cannot reach well, which raises the risk of cavities and gum disease. Correcting alignment reduces that risk over the long term.

Denver Clear Aligners at Icon Dental: Invisalign® Clear Braces

Invisalign®: Proven Results with Platinum-Level Expertise

Invisalign® is the flagship clear aligner system. The trays are removable and clear, with no metal brackets or wires, and they are worn for 20 to 22 hours per day to move teeth gradually and predictably. We use 3D digital scanning instead of traditional impressions, so the fit is more accurate and the process is faster and more comfortable from the start.

Before treatment begins, you’ll see a digital preview of your projected smile, which lets you understand the plan before committing to it. Invisalign® works well for teens and adults across a range of case complexities, and our Platinum Provider status means we bring hands-on experience with cases that go well beyond minor crowding. Treatment typically runs 12 to 18 months depending on the scope of movement needed, though your actual timeline will be confirmed at consultation.

We evaluate every patient’s alignment needs individually before recommending Invisalign® clear aligners, so the decision is clinical, not arbitrary.

Orthodontics: Accelerate Your Treatment Timeline

Invisalign® acceleration treatment pairs clear aligners with supplementary devices or minor in-office procedures that stimulate bone remodeling around the teeth. When bone responds more readily to aligner forces, teeth can move on a faster schedule, which is what allows some patients to change trays every 3 to 7 days rather than the standard 10 to 14. For eligible patients, this can reduce overall treatment time by up to 50 percent compared to a conventional aligner timeline.

For busy adults who want effective results without stretching treatment longer than necessary, this is worth asking about during your consultation. We identify eligible candidates during the initial assessment. Not every patient needs an accelerated treatment, but for the right case, it makes a real difference in how long you’re in treatment.

3. Custom Aligner Fabrication

Your aligners are made to fit your specific scan, not a generic mold. Each tray in the sequence applies gentle, controlled pressure to move your teeth toward the planned position.

4. Wearing Your Aligners

For best results, aligners are worn 20 to 22 hours per day. You remove them to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush and floss. This makes hygiene during treatment much simpler than with fixed braces.

5. Progress Checkups

You’ll visit us every few weeks so we can confirm your teeth are tracking as planned and make any adjustments needed. These appointments are short, but they keep your treatment on schedule.

6. Retainer and Long-Term Results

Once your active aligner treatment is done, we’ll recommend a retainer plan to keep your teeth in their new positions. Retention is what turns a finished treatment into a lasting result.

Beyond Straight Teeth: Alignment, Bite, and Your Long-Term Health

We don’t fix a broken tooth only. We look at how to fix it but also why it got there so it doesn’t happen again. That same thinking guides how we approach orthodontics. When we evaluate a patient for clear aligners, we look at bite function and jaw mechanics alongside tooth position, not instead of them.

For some patients, this evaluation also touches on sleep and airway health . The relationship between jaw position, airway space, and sleep quality is real, and we screen for it when it is relevant. Frequently Asked Questions About Denver Clear Aligners

  • How long does Invisalign® treatment take? Treatment typically runs between 12 and 18 months, depending on how much movement is needed and how consistently you wear your aligners. Some less complex cases finish sooner, and patients who are eligible for acceleration treatment may see their timeline shortened. We’ll give you a realistic projection based on your specific scan at the consultation.
  • Do clear aligners hurt? Most patients notice pressure when they switch to a new tray in the sequence, which is normal and usually fades within a day or two. Because there are no brackets or wires, the kind of irritation common with traditional braces does not apply here. If discomfort persists beyond the first day or two after a new tray, that is worth mentioning at your next checkup.
  • Can I remove my aligners? Yes, and that is one of the practical advantages of clear braces over fixed braces. You remove them to eat, drink anything besides water, and brush and floss your teeth. For treatment to stay on track, you’ll need to wear them for 20 to 22 hours per day, so the removal is intentional, not optional.
  • Are clear aligners right for adults? Adult patients are actually a significant portion of the people we treat with clear aligners. Many adults delayed treatment earlier in life or had previous orthodontic work that has since shifted. Clear aligners suit adult lifestyles well because they are discreet, removable, and do not require the dietary restrictions that come with brackets and wires. We’ll confirm your candidacy during your exam.
  • How is Icon Dental different from other Denver clear aligners providers? We hold Platinum Invisalign® Provider status, which reflects a higher volume and range of completed cases than most general dental offices. Every new patient gets a 90-minute appointment, and we evaluate alignment in the context of your bite and jaw health, not just cosmetics.   • What if I have TMJ or bite issues: can I still get clear aligners? Bite and jaw issues do not automatically disqualify you from clear aligner treatment. In some cases, addressing alignment is part of how we approach those problems. We evaluate TMJ and bite alignment alongside orthodontic planning because the two are connected, and treating them in coordination produces more predictable outcomes than treating one while ignoring the other. If your situation is complex, we’ll explain your options clearly before any treatment begins.
