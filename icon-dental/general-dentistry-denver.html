The Icon Dental Difference: Dentistry That Looks Further

Most dental practices are built around a straightforward model: identify what’s broken, fix it, and schedule the next cleaning. We respect that model, but we’ve found that this approach often leaves patients with recurring problems, unanswered questions, and a sense that something deeper is being overlooked.

At Icon Dental, a comprehensive dental evaluation goes far beyond teeth and gums. We assess bite alignment, jaw function, TMJ health, airway, and sleep patterns alongside standard oral health indicators, because we know that the health of your mouth is directly connected to the health of your whole body.

Chronic inflammation from untreated gum disease has been linked to systemic health concerns. Jaw tension and bite misalignment can drive headaches and disrupted sleep that patients are often told are simply stress-related. Sleep apnea frequently goes undetected for years because no one has screened for it in a dental setting. We screen for all of it, routinely, as part of how we practice dentistry.

If you’ve accepted pain, poor sleep, or recurring dental problems as simply part of life, it may be time for a second opinion from a team that looks further.

Comprehensive Check Ups / Dental Exams

A dental exam at Icon Dental is not a visual sweep and a handshake. Our comprehensive exams evaluate the full health of your mouth, including your teeth, gums, bite alignment, jaw function, soft tissue, and oral cancer risk. Digital X-rays are taken to identify concerns that are not visible during a visual examination, including cavities between teeth, bone loss, and structural issues beneath the surface.

We also assess TMJ function and bite alignment as a standard part of every exam, not as an add-on. Early detection is the foundation of preventive care. When we catch an issue early, treatment is almost always less invasive, less costly, and far easier on you.

Professional Dental Cleanings

Even the most diligent brushing and flossing routine cannot remove all of the plaque and tartar that accumulates over time. Professional dental cleanings performed by our experienced hygiene team remove that buildup thoroughly, protecting your gum health and reducing the low-grade systemic inflammation that untreated oral bacteria can contribute to.

For most patients, we recommend a professional cleaning every six months. Patients who are managing gum disease or have a higher risk of buildup may benefit from more frequent visits, and we will work with you to find the cadence that supports your long-term health most effectively. Our hygienists are known for being thorough, gentle, and genuinely invested in your care.

Digital X-Rays and Imaging

Our digital X-ray system captures clear, detailed diagnostic images with significantly lower radiation exposure than traditional film X-rays. These images allow us to detect cavities between teeth, monitor bone density, identify root concerns, and evaluate structural health with a level of accuracy that a visual exam alone cannot provide.

Because our imaging is digital, we can review your X-rays with you in real time during your appointment. That means you see what we see, and we can explain exactly what we are looking at. Patient education is not an afterthought in our practice. It is built into every step of the diagnostic process.

Cavity Prevention

When a cavity is detected early, treatment is straightforward and conservative. We use tooth-preserving fillings that restore the structure and function of the affected tooth without removing more healthy tissue than necessary.

For patients who want an added layer of protection, dental sealants provide a thin, durable coating applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, where decay most commonly begins. Sealants are available for both children and adults and can be a simple, effective way to prevent cavities from forming in the first place. Fluoride treatments further strengthen enamel and help teeth resist the acid attacks that lead to decay over time. Our philosophy here, as with everything we do, is to intervene early and thoughtfully rather than wait for problems to deepen.

Gum Disease Screening Treatment

Your gum health is the foundation of your oral health, and it is connected to your whole-body health in ways that are often underestimated. Gum disease has been linked to chronic systemic inflammation, and research continues to explore its relationship with cardiovascular health, blood sugar regulation, and other long-term health outcomes.

At every exam, we screen carefully for early signs of periodontal disease, including gum recession, pocket depth changes, and bone loss. Catching gum disease in its earliest stage, known as gingivitis, gives us the opportunity to address it before it progresses to more advanced periodontitis.

Scaling and Root Planing

When gum disease has moved beyond the early stage and standard cleaning is no longer sufficient, scaling and root planing is the most effective non-surgical intervention available. This deep-cleaning procedure goes below the gumline to remove the hardened plaque and tartar deposits that have accumulated on root surfaces, deposits that a routine cleaning simply cannot reach.

By clearing those bacterial deposits and smoothing the root surfaces, we give your gum tissue the environment it needs to heal and reattach properly. Most patients complete treatment over one or two appointments, typically with local anesthetic to keep the process comfortable throughout. Consistent with our root-cause philosophy, scaling and root planing is not simply a reactive procedure. It is a targeted intervention that addresses the underlying bacterial environment driving gum disease, giving your oral health a genuine reset rather than a temporary fix.

Laser Dentistry

Our laser dentistry technology allows us to perform a range of soft tissue procedures with greater precision and significantly less discomfort than traditional methods. Laser treatment supports bacterial reduction, helps treat gum tissue concerns, and promotes faster healing with minimal post-procedure sensitivity.

For patients who experience anxiety around dental procedures or have had difficult experiences in the past, laser dentistry is one of the tools that makes care at Icon Dental feel genuinely different. Less discomfort, faster recovery, and a gentler overall experience are outcomes we pursue with every technological investment we make. Learn more on our laser dentistry page .

Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth is damaged, decayed, or structurally compromised, timely restoration is essential to prevent the problem from spreading and to preserve the function of surrounding teeth. At Icon Dental, we offer porcelain crowns, bridges, and our signature same-day crown service using CEREC technology.

With CEREC, we design, mill, and place a custom porcelain crown in a single appointment. There is no temporary crown to manage, no second visit to schedule, and no prolonged period of sensitivity while you wait. We take a digital scan of your tooth, generate a precise 3D model, and deliver a finished restoration that fits beautifully and functions immediately. For patients with busy schedules, same-day crown technology is one of the most practical conveniences modern dentistry offers. Learn more on our same-day crowns page .

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy has a reputation that no longer reflects the reality of modern dental care. When infection reaches the root of a tooth, treatment is focused on two goals: relieving your pain and saving your natural tooth. With today’s techniques and our commitment to patient comfort, root canal therapy is a manageable, targeted procedure that most patients find far less difficult than they anticipated.

Preserving your natural tooth is always our first priority. Extraction is a last resort, and we will always explore every option to keep your smile intact before recommending removal.

Dental Implants

For patients who have experienced tooth loss, dental implants are the most durable, natural-looking, and functionally effective solution available. Unlike bridges or traditional dentures, implants integrate directly with the jawbone, preserving bone density and supporting the long-term health of surrounding teeth.

An implant is a permanent solution designed to last a lifetime with proper care. We will walk you through exactly what the process involves, what to expect at each stage, and how implants compare to other options so you can make a fully informed decision. Learn more on our dental implants page .

Oral Surgery and Extractions

When a tooth cannot be saved, extraction is performed with precision, care, and a genuine focus on your comfort. Our team takes the time to ensure you are fully at ease before, during, and after the procedure, and we provide clear aftercare guidance so your recovery is as smooth as possible.

Wisdom tooth removal is one of the most common preventive procedures we perform, often recommended before problems develop rather than in response to pain or infection. As with all of our care, the goal is to stay ahead of issues rather than react to them.

Sleep Apnea Screening Treatment

Nearly 54 million American adults live with obstructive sleep apnea, and a significant number of them do not know it. Symptoms like chronic fatigue, morning headaches, difficulty concentrating, and disrupted sleep are often attributed to stress or lifestyle rather than an airway issue that is both identifiable and treatable.
