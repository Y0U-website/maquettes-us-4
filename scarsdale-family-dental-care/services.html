Phase 2 treatment usually begins once most permanent teeth have erupted. This stage focuses on fully aligning teeth and correcting bite issues to achieve optimal function and aesthetics.

Traditional braces gradually straighten teeth and correct bite problems using metal brackets and wires that gently move teeth into their proper position.

Ceramic braces function similarly to traditional braces but use tooth-colored or clear brackets that blend with natural teeth, making them a more discreet orthodontic option.

Clear aligners provide a discreet way to straighten teeth using removable transparent trays that gradually shift teeth into alignment. They are a popular option for teens and adults seeking a more subtle orthodontic treatment.

Brava braces are a completely new approach to invisibly and gently straightening teeth. They’re not like traditional braces or aligners because they’re personalized, programmed and placed behind the teeth. Using Independent Movers® technology, Brava is programmed to move each tooth based on a customized smile design. Not only is Brava hidden behind the teeth, but it works fast to quickly improve smiles. That means easier care and fewer office visits because Brava is always working.

Various orthodontic appliances may be used to support treatment and improve bite alignment. These appliances help guide jaw development, correct bite issues, and support tooth movement during treatment.
