Dr. Kim Batterson

Dr. Batterson is an active member of the American Board of Orthodontics, the American Association of Orthodontists, the Rocky Mountain Society of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Colorado Dental Association. She is involved in several study clubs, including Spear and ITI. In addition, she is actively involved in a nationwide study club called the Angle Society of Orthodontists. She regularly meets with orthodontists from all over the country but more specifically the southwest part of the United States.

Dr. Batterson and her husband, David, have two children, Abigail and Jack. They love living in Colorado with all the outdoor opportunities it offers. They enjoy backcountry skiing, mountain biking and climbing fourteeners as a family. Now that her children are in college, she is enjoying traveling any free time she gets.
