The practice

1. Personal ownership: We take responsibility for our actions and proactively work towards shaping the culture around us. While it may be easier to blame an outside circumstance or another person, we have the humility to look within ourselves to see how personal change can influence the outcome. We welcome accountability.

2. Servant’s heart: We strive to serve those around us joyfully because we recognize we have been blessed beyond what we deserve. We serve one another through a lens of appreciation and gratitude rather than from a self-centered posture.

3. Better than yesterday: We aim for self-improvement daily, both small and large. Whether personal or professional, we are pursuing growth and continue challenging our previous methodologies, thinking, and habits. We understand that change can happen unnoticed in small ways, so we actively search for daily growth and celebrate the wins.

4. Perseverance: We exhibit grit and welcome challenges as a pathway toward growth. Adversity is often mistaken as bad, however, we see it as an opportunity to improve and develop character.

5. Mindfully driven: We are intentional in what we do and thoughtful in how we carry ourselves. We aim for meaningful and deliberate actions that are active and not passive. We have conviction in our actions and exhibit the highest level of integrity.
