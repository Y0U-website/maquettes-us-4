The Dental Implant Process

At Good Day Dentistry, we work closely with a trusted local oral surgeon to ensure the best possible outcome for your implant procedure.

  • Surgical Placement: The oral surgeon places a titanium implant post in the missing tooth’s space.
  • Healing Period: The bone integrates around the implant over 4-6 months, creating a secure foundation.
  • Final Restoration: Once the implant is fully healed, Dr. Elmirra custom-designs and attaches a beautiful, natural-looking crown to complete your smile.

Caring for Your Dental Implant

Dental implants require proper care to maintain their longevity. While they don’t decay like natural teeth, they still need routine hygiene and professional cleanings to prevent gum disease.

Home Care Routine

  • Brush at least twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Floss once a day using traditional floss or a water flosser to clean around the implant.
  • Schedule regular dental checkups every six months for professional cleanings and evaluations.
