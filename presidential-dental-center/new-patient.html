Your Comprehensive New Patient Examination Includes:

After gathering all information, Dr. Melisande Wolff will review her findings and photos with you, discuss diagnoses for your teeth and gums, and provide personalized recommendations for your treatment options. She understands that every patient has unique needs and will outline which treatments are crucial to address first and which can be deferred. She will also discuss any risks associated with delaying or not completing treatment, empowering you to make an informed decision about your dental health.

If time permits, dental treatment may begin on the very same day. Otherwise, Dr. Melisande Wolff will personally escort you to the reception counter to schedule your next appointment. Additionally, if you’re interested in enhancing your smile with facial rejuvenation, Dr. Melisande Wolff gladly offers a complimentary consultation for Botox and Dermal Fillers during your new patient visit.

  • A personal meeting with Dr. Melisande Wolff.
  • Detailed medical history review.
  • Blood pressure reading (if necessary).
  • Full mouth series of digital radiographs (90 % less radiation than traditional X-rays).
