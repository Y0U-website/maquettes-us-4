General Checkups

Pediatric

Help your children build a foundation of calm and friendly dental experiences, and give them the gift of anxiety-free or reduced-anxiety future visits.

Cleaning

Enjoy the feeling of sparkling clean teeth and the benefits of early intervention by scheduling and attending regular visits with one of our experienced and friendly hygenists.

X-Rays

Routine x-rays make it possible for us to indentify and treat problems before they become painful. Diagnostic x-rays help us determine how extensive a problem has become. Please know that we will always tell you the cost before we move forward with treatment, and we will never recommend treatment if treatment isn’t necessary.

Restoration

Bridges

Missing teeth are more than just an aesthetic disadvantage. They can cause structural changes to your mouth that could make eating and/or speaking difficult. Bridges use your existing teeth to literally bridge the gaps left by missing teeth and keep your mouth looking and functioning as it should.

Crowns

Our natural color crowns restore both structure and function to teeth that have grown too damaged to be fixed with a regular filling. They also save you money over same-day crowns from other dental practices. We think you’ll find them worth the wait.

Dentures

Releive pain, improve your health and appearance, and make eating a joyful experience again. If your teeth have become too damaged to restore through other means, dentures are an important part of restoring your overall health and well-being.

Bonding

Keep your smile white and bright instead of full of metal fillings. Bonding is a safe, affordable, and attractive way to cover up stains, correct cracks or gaps, or restore teeth after cavities have been removed.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers

Discoloration, cracks, gaps, and chips can leave you feeling self-conscious about your smile, but it doesn’t have to stay that way. We can redesign and reshape your mouth with veneers so that your confident smile can shine!

Whitening

A bright white smile is a great way to improve both your appearance and your self-confidence, but some whitening options leave your teeth painfully sensitive. Our Opalescence whitening system is different! A unique formulation actually strengthens your teeth and prevents dehydration and shade relapse. Restore the natural color of your teeth or make them whiter and brighter than ever with either our in-office proceedure or custom-made take home trays.

Invisalign

You can experience the confidence of a straighter smile and the comfort of a better-aligned bite without the expense of traditional braces.

Other Services

Root Canals

A root canal can make it possible for you to enjoy the foods you love without pain or sensitivity to heat, cold, or sugars. Advances in dental technology have made this infamous dental proceedure much less scary. Proper pain medication makes it possible for us to remove infected nerves from a tooth with little to no pain for the patient.

Extractions

We can help you avoid the pain, infection, and overcrowding problems that are often caused by wisdom teeth. We can remove them in our office, even if they’re impacted.

Bone Grafts

If you need to have your jawbone built up before receiving dental implants or other restorative devices, our bone graft proccedure is safe and can be done in our office.
