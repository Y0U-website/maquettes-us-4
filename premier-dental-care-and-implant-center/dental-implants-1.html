How Implants Replace Missing Teeth

dental implant benefits

Dental implants have countless benefits beyond restoring the health and beauty of your natural smile. Some of which include:

• Restoring missing teeth • Improving speech and digestion • Renewing confidence • Maintaining the integrity of the jawbone • Ongoing care is identical to caring for natural teeth

Eligibility for Dental Implants

Because dental implants are anchored into the jawbone, patients seeking this beneficial treatment must meet specific eligibility requirements in order to be considered a candidate. In the event that there is not enough bone to support the restoration, or if gingival health is compromised, we can work with a team of specialists that can perform a bone-grafting procedure to prepare you for dental implants. Additionally, most patients don’t realize that dental implants can provide a fixed solution for patients with missing one tooth or multiple teeth.
