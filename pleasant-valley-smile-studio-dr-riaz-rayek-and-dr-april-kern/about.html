Find helpful information below addressing common concerns and inquiries from our patients about treatments and procedures.

For most people without serious oral health issues, a visit to the dentist every 6 months for a cleaning and evaluation is sufficient. Those who have special circumstances may need to see a dentist more often.

Gum disease and cavities are by far the most common oral health issues that you need to watch out for. Brushing, flossing, and visiting the dentist regularly is the best way to guard against both of these problems.
