Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Dental implants may be an option if you:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Want a permanent alternative to dentures or bridges
  • Have sufficient bone density for implant placement
  • Maintain good oral health and hygiene

The Dental Implant Process at Great Northwest Dental

  • Consultation and Evaluation – We assess your oral health and determine if dental implants are the right choice.
  • Implant Placement – A titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone.
  • Healing and Bone Integration – Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone.
  • Abutment and Restoration – A custom-made dental crown, bridge, or denture is secured to the implant.

Restorative Dental FAQ

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The attached crown, bridge, or denture may need replacement after 10-15 years.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Local anesthesia is used during implant placement to minimize discomfort. Some mild soreness is normal but can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How long does it take to get dental implants?

The full process, including healing time, usually takes several months. In some cases, immediate implant placement may be an option.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Dental implants provide greater stability, function, and bone preservation compared to removable dentures. They feel and function more like natural teeth.

How do I care for dental implants?

Brush and floss daily, visit your dentist for regular checkups, and avoid habits like smoking that can affect implant longevity.
