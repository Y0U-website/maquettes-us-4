  • Implant Placement : We place four dental implants: two vertical implants in the front of the jaw and two angled implants in the back of the jaw.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a bone grafting procedure to prepare my jaw bone for All-on-Four dental implants?

If bone grafts are required, the grafting procedure will take place a few months before implant placement, giving the grafts time to generate new bone material. We’ll discuss all options during your personalized consultation.

What are axial and tilted implants? What is the difference between these dental implants in the All-on-Four dental implant surgery?

When placing All-on-Four dental implants, we use both axial and tilted implants to maximize support for your new smile. An axial dental implant is placed vertically in the front of the jaw. A tilted dental implant is angled in the back.

Using both tilted and axial implants in the All-on-Four surgery is essential. Axial implants in the front provide strong vertical support, while tilted implants in the back offer angled support. This combination creates a stable foundation for your implanted teeth across the entire arch.

Additionally, combining the two types of implants spreads biting and chewing forces more evenly across your teeth and jaw, promoting long-term implant success and bone health. At Yeo Family Dental Group, our experienced implant team carefully plans each implant’s position and angle to maximize these benefits for your specific case. We ensure the best possible outcome for your All-on-Four treatment.

How long does the All-on-Four procedure take?

The initial implant surgery and temporary teeth placement typically take 2-3 hours per arch.

Is the All-on-Four procedure painful?

We use advanced anesthesia techniques to ensure your comfort during the procedure. We also specialize in sleep dentistry, offering different levels of sedation for patients with dental anxiety. We will provide comprehensive aftercare instructions and medication for managing swelling and discomfort in the first few days after the procedure. Most patients find the recovery from All-on-Four dental implants very manageable.

How do I clean my All-on-Four implants?

You’ll brush and floss your new teeth like natural teeth. We’ll also provide special tools for cleaning under the prosthesis.

Can All-on-Four dental implants break?

While rare, damage to dental implants can occur. Our durable materials and precise fitting minimize this risk. To protect your teeth from damage, avoid chewing on hard candies or other hard foods.

How soon can I eat normal food after getting All-on-Four dental implants?

You’ll need to stick to soft foods for a few weeks. We’ll guide you on gradually reintroducing harder foods.

Will my speech be affected by All-on-Four dental implants?

You may notice minor changes initially, but most patients adapt quickly and experience improved speech clarity.

How much do All-on-Four dental implants cost?

Costs for your all-on-4 dental implants vary based on individual needs. We will provide a detailed estimate during your consultation.

Can I get All-on-Four implants if I have gum disease?

We’ll need to treat any existing gum disease before proceeding with implants. Our team can help you achieve the necessary oral health with advanced periodontal care for a successful dental implant treatment.
