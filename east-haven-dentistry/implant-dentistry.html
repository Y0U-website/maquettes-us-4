Implant Dentistry

  • Finishing the Restoration Once your implant has healed and bonded with the bone, it becomes a strong foundation for a new tooth. We then place a custom crown that’s designed to match your natural teeth in both look and feel. This process involves uncovering the implant, taking a precise impression, and creating a crown that blends seamlessly with your smile.

Options for Replacing Several Teeth

Implant-Supported Bridges

A bridge anchored by two or more implants can replace several missing teeth in a row, giving you strength and durability without relying on your natural teeth for support.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Removable dentures can slip or feel unstable. By securing them with a few implants, you’ll gain comfort, confidence, and the ability to chew without worry or concern.

Fixed Implant Dentures

For those with significant tooth loss, a full arch of teeth can be anchored permanently with 4-6 implants. This option provides the most hassle-free solution.
