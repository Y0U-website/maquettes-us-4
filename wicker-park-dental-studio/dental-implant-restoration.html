FAQs About Dental Implant Restoration

Can you repair a broken dental implant crown? Yes. Most of the time, we can restore or replace the crown without disturbing the implant post.

How long do implant restorations last? With proper care, a new implant crown can last 10–15 years or more.

Is the implant post removed during restoration? No, unless it’s damaged. We aim to keep your original implant post intact.

What causes implants to fail or loosen? Over time, wear, bite misalignment, or even teeth grinding can affect your restoration.

Do you offer implant repairs if I got my implant elsewhere? Absolutely. We treat many patients from other offices who need a second opinion or expert restoration.
