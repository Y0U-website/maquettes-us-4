Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

When is the best age to start orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic evaluations are recommended for children by age 7, but treatment is effective at any age. We offer solutions for kids, teens, and adults.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time varies by case, typically lasting between 12-24 months. Dr. Sanders will provide a clear timeline during your consultation.

Will orthodontic treatment hurt?

While some discomfort is normal as your teeth adjust, today’s orthodontic solutions are designed for maximum comfort.

Can I eat normally with braces or aligners?

With braces, you’ll need to avoid sticky or hard foods. Invisalign and clear aligners allow you to remove them while eating, so you can enjoy your favorite meals.

How much does orthodontic treatment cost?

Costs vary depending on the treatment. We offer flexible financing options and work with many insurance plans. Learn more on our finance and insurance page .
