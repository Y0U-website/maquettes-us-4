The practice

As a locally owned and independent dental practice, we are deeply rooted in the Johnson City community and committed to providing care that reflects the values and needs of our neighbors.

Located on Peoples St. in Johnson City, dentists Dr. Michael Mefford, Dr. Tyler Davis, Dr. Chantel Keller and our dedicated team are of the firm belief that both the aesthetics and health of your smile demand an exceptional standard of dedication and precision. At Blue Ridge Dental Group, we merge profound experience with state-of-the-art techniques to craft the smile of your dreams.

Dr. Michael Mefford is a 2002 graduate of the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry, where he graduated as President of his class.

Dr. Tyler Davis is a native of southwest Virginia. He obtained his B.S. in Biology from the University of Virginia at Wise. After his undergraduate education, he obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry in Richmond, Virginia.

Dr. Chantel Keller began her journey in dentistry with an undergraduate degree from Texas Tech University before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Texas A&M Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas.

Services

Dental porcelain can be sculpted to closely replicate the look of your natural tooth enamel, while providing you with desirable strength and resilience you can trust.

Dental implants can be used for a variety of dental problems. Whether you are missing one, several, or all of your teeth, dental implants may be the answer for your restorative needs.

Blue Ridge Dental Group is proud to be certified Invisalign providers. The virtually invisible aligners gradually reposition your teeth into a smile you’ll be proud of.

Porcelain Veneers are precisely fabricated thin coverings that are placed on the front part of the teeth. Veneers are effective in correcting a variety of dental concerns.
