A root canal removes infected pulp from inside a tooth to prevent further damage and relieve pain, allowing you to keep your natural tooth.

Signs include severe tooth pain, sensitivity to heat or cold, swelling, or discomfort when chewing or touching the tooth.

Most root canal treatments take about 60 to 90 minutes, but the length may vary depending on the complexity of the case.
