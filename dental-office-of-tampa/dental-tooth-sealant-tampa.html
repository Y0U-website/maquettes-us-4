Dental Sealants

  • Preparation: A mild acid solution is applied to gently roughen the tooth surface, which helps the sealant bond more effectively.
  • Application: The sealant material is painted onto the chewing surfaces and flows into the grooves and pits.
  • Hardening: A special curing light is used to harden the sealant quickly so you are ready to go in no time.
  • There is no drilling, no numbing, and no recovery time. You walk in, get protected, and walk out with a stronger smile.

Top Reasons Patients Choose Dental Sealants

  • Fast and painless with no anesthesia required
  • Long-lasting protection that holds up under normal chewing for several years
  • Reduces cavity risk by up to 80 % in the years following application
  • Tooth-colored material that blends naturally with your smile
  • Effective for both kids and adults at risk for back tooth decay
  • Affordable preventive care that saves money by avoiding future restorations

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental sealants last?

Sealants hold up well under normal chewing and typically last several years before a reapplication is needed. Your dentist will check them at your regular visits to make sure they are still intact.

Are dental sealants only for children?

Not at all. Adults whose back teeth are free of decay but prone to cavities can benefit just as much. Your dentist will evaluate whether sealants are a good fit during your exam.

Do sealants replace brushing and flossing?

No. Sealants are a supplement to good oral hygiene, not a replacement. You should still brush twice a day, floss daily, and keep up with regular cleanings and checkups.
