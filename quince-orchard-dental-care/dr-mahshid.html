Dr. Mahshid - Endodontist

Some people spend years searching for their calling. Dr. Mahshid Majlessi found hers at age 12. After a root canal that could have left most kids dreading dental visits, it instead sparked a lifelong passion. The endodontist who eased her fears and treated her tooth inspired her to do the same for others and she’s doing exactly that.

Dr. Majlessi brings over 20 years of experience in dentistry to our team, with a specialty in endodontics the branch of dental care focused on protecting and preserving the inner health of your teeth. She completed six years of dental school at Azad University in Tehran, Iran, earned her degree at the University of Pennsylvania and finished her three-year endodontic specialty at the University of Michigan. Her road wasn’t always easy, but every step shaped the compassionate doctor she is today.

What sets her apart isn’t just her credentials it’s her philosophy. She treats every patient like a member of her own family, takes the time to get the diagnosis right and never recommends treatment that isn’t truly necessary. Her goal is always the same: relieve your pain, save your natural teeth and send you home feeling better than when you walked in.

Apart from Dentistry, she enjoys hiking, biking, painting watercolors, gardening and spending time with friends. She describes herself as calm, quiet and organized qualities that translate directly into the care she delivers every single day.
