Preventive Dentistry in Coppell

No one wants to undergo extensive restorative or prosthetic dental treatments, so preventing oral health problems or detecting them at their very earliest stages should be every patient’s goal. Dr. Lee can help with checkups and cleanings every six months, as recommended by the American Dental Association, and extra protection for patients who are more at risk for cavities or have tooth sensitivity.
