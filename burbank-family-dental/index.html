The practice

Burbank Family Dental provides dental care with a strong emphasis on cosmetics. Dr. Burbank and staff are passionate about dentistry and strive to provide dental care with the highest standard of quality in a friendly, spa-like atmosphere.

We are conveniently located in Youngsville off Chemin Metairie Parkway at East Milton Avenue behind Twin’s Burgers. We offer comprehensive dental care utilizing sedation options to eliminate fear and anxiety. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Burbank and his wife, Fran, moved to Lafayette to pursue his professional career.
