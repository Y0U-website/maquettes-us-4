The All-on-4 Process Explained

The journey to a renewed smile with all-on-4 dental implants involves several key steps:

  • Consultation and Planning: Your journey begins with a detailed consultation with Dr. Lee, who will assess your oral health and discuss your dental goals. Using 3D imaging, we plan the implant placement meticulously to ensure optimal results.
  • Implant Placement: On the day of surgery, the four implants are placed in your jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation to ensure your comfort.
  • Temporary Teeth Placement: Once the implants are in place, a set of temporary teeth is attached, providing function and aesthetics while your mouth heals.
  • Healing Period: Over the next few months, your implants will integrate with the jawbone in a process known as osseointegration.
  • Final Prosthesis: Once healing is complete, you’ll return to our office to have your permanent teeth fitted, leaving you with a durable, stable, and aesthetically pleasing smile.
