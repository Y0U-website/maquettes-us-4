Jonathan Fan, DMD

One of the most exciting aspects of dentistry is the ongoing development of new techniques and technology. I love learning and staying current on the latest information in order to determine what’s best to bring into the practice.

I have completed postgraduate courses in Invisalign clear aligner therapy. In addition, I’ve taken the comprehensive implantology continuum through the Columbia University College of Dental Medicine.

As an active member of the American Dental Association , New Jersey Dental Association , and Monmouth Dental Society , I have access to a range of continuing education, as well. Taking courses locally, regionally, and nationally, and attending meetings and lectures, allows me to learn from the leaders in the field.
