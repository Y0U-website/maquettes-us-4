The practice

Dr. Jonathan Fan is committed to delivering high-quality dentistry and customer service that will have you smiling. He will always listen to you, learn your desires and concerns, and craft a personal treatment plan that’s tailored to your unique needs.

Everyone at Advanced Family Dentistry looks forward to meeting you. We’re excited to be your partners as you achieve optimal oral health and with it, a smile you love to share. Call today!

It is an honor to partner with my patients to achieve their dental goals. Whether it involves alleviating pain, restoring oral health and function, making a smile a little more pleasing, or improving home dental hygiene care, we achieve our patients ’ goals by working together.

I value the relationship that develops between each patient and myself. Starting with our first conversation, I foster that rapport through non-judgmental communication and actively listening to the person’s desires and concerns. This creates a bond of trust: I genuinely care about the person, beyond his or her dental needs, and want to help as best I can.

Not everyone likes to see the dentist, and some people come in with a fear of dentistry. It’s my responsibility to understand this apprehension and help the person overcome it. I don’t judge, and will never pressure someone into a certain treatment path.

Instead, I thoroughly explain their clinical situation and walk them through the treatment options. We discuss the pros and cons of each, so the individual is able to make informed decisions about their smile’s health.

Being a dentist is about caring for the whole person, and I am continually humbled by the trust people place in me. Together, we achieve beautiful smiles and optimal oral health and wellness.

One of the most exciting aspects of dentistry is the ongoing development of new techniques and technology. I love learning and staying current on the latest information in order to determine what’s best to bring into the practice.
