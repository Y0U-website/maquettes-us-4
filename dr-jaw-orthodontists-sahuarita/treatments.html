Braces for kids, teens, and adults

Braces use brackets and wires to guide teeth into alignment. Our team will explain the available styles and help you choose an approach for your smile, bite, and preferences.

  • Silver, gold, and clear braces
  • Self-ligating braces

Invisalign® at every stage

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners to gradually move teeth. Dr. JAW offers options for children, teens, and adults, with a plan tailored to your smile and bite.

  • Clear, removable aligners
  • Remove for meals, drinks other than water, brushing, and flossing
  • Typically worn 20–22 hours a day, as directed by your orthodontist
  • Regular check-ins to monitor progress
