Meet Your Orthodontists

A whole crew in your corner.

Skilled hands, familiar faces, and a shared love of helping Southern Arizona smile.

Kyle Rabe

A small-town Ohio native, Dr. Rabe values individualized care, innovation, and continuing education. As a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, he brings a thoughtful approach to treatment and a relaxed, friendly feel to every visit.

Laura Robison-Rabe

Dr. Robison-Rabe first imagined becoming an orthodontist during her own treatment in Farmington, New Mexico. She earned her DMD at Oregon Health Sciences University and became a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics in 2008. She loves helping the Dr. JAW community grow, one smile at a time.

Colby J. Weaver

A Tucson native, Dr. Weaver studied Ecology and Evolutionary Biology before earning his DDS at Virginia Commonwealth University and pursuing orthodontics in Atlanta. He takes a keen interest in facial aesthetics. Outside of work, you may find him golfing, spending time with his dogs, or cheering for the Arizona Wildcats.
