THE 4-STEP DENTAL IMPLANT PROCESS

There is a four-step process that all patients must complete to ensure successful dental implant placement:

  • Initial Dental Implant Consultation – You will meet with Dr. Walker to determine your candidacy as well as if you require any preliminary treatments such as bone grafting, periodontal therapy, or tooth extraction.
  • Dental Implant Surgery – Depending on the severity of your case, you may remain in-house for your dental implant surgery, or Dr. Walker may refer you to an outside oral surgeon.
  • Dental Implant Osseointegration/Abutment – Once your implants are in place, osseointegration will occur, which means the posts will fuse with the jawbone. After several months, you will return to have your abutments put into place.
  • Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s) – The final step is to receive your customized restoration, which may include a crown, bridge, or denture depending on how many teeth are missing.

UNDERSTANDING THE COST OF DENTAL IMPLANTS

The cost of dental implants cannot be determined until you schedule a consultation with our team. It is during this visit that Dr. Walker will go over your medical history, take digital images of your smile, and recommend whether you require preliminary treatments or not. Once we have a clearer understanding of what is expected, we can draft an estimate for you to review. We’ll then go over your available payment options as well as help you get the maximum reimbursement from your insurance company should you decide to file a claim.
