What is dental hygiene: Dental hygiene is a service that includes thoroughly cleaning the teeth to remove tartar, stains, and plaque, taking x-rays, checking for decay and other problems in the mouth, gums, and teeth, and educating patients about proper oral hygiene techniques.

Benefits of good dental hygiene: Even if you have no cavities, dental hygiene is excellent preventative dental care as it identifies problems before they become major issues. Your teeth cleaning will help to prevent the buildup of plaque and tartar that can lead to tooth decay and gum disease. Ideal candidates: Beginning at age 3, you should see a dentist for a cleaning every six months. However, if your teeth build up plaque or you develop frequent cavities, are diabetic, have current gum disease, have a weak immune response, bacterial infection, are a smoker or are pregnant, you may need to see the dentist more often.
