Sharon R. Hatcher, DDS. Home town Dentist

Dr. Sharon Hatcher is a dedicated member of the Hopkins County community, having deep roots as a 5th generation resident. A proud graduate of Sulphur Springs High School in 1986, she went on to earn a Bachelor of Arts in Biology from the University of Saint Thomas in Houston, TX, in 1990. Her passion for dentistry led her to the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

In 1996, Dr. Hatcher returned to her hometown of Sulphur Springs, where she established her dental practice in 1998. She is committed to providing quality dental care to her fellow residents. In 1999, she married Sam Baxley from Reilly Springs, and together they have raised two children, Katarina Bell and Joseph Baxley, both of whom also graduated from Sulphur Springs High School. Dr. Hatcher’s long-standing ties to the community reflect her dedication to both her profession and her roots.
