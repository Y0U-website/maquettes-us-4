Dental Implants at Pendleton Smiles

At Pendleton Smiles, we offer a variety of dental implant systems to meet our patients ’ needs, from replacing a single tooth to a full arch. Our top implant systems include:

Single Dental Implants

If you are missing one tooth, a single dental implant is the ideal choice. We place one implant to act as the root, then attach a custom crown to restore the tooth completely. This method doesn’t require us to alter your adjacent healthy teeth, unlike a traditional bridge.

All-on-4® Implants

For patients who are missing an entire arch of teeth, the All-on-4® treatment can provide a full, fixed set of teeth in a single day. This innovative technique uses just four strategically placed implants to support a full-arch prosthesis, often eliminating the need for bone grafting .

Implant-Retained Overdentures

For a more stable alternative to traditional dentures, we offer implant-retained overdentures (also known as implant-supported dentures). These dentures are secured with a few dental implants, providing a much more secure fit that prevents slipping, improves chewing ability, and boosts confidence. The dentures are still removable for easy cleaning.

Dental Implants FAQ

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed in the jawbone to act like an artificial tooth root. Once restored with a crown, bridge, or denture, it can replace missing teeth with a stable, natural-looking result.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants can help restore chewing function, support a natural-looking replacement tooth, and help preserve jawbone health after tooth loss. They are designed to provide a secure foundation for long-term tooth replacement.

Can dental implants replace more than one tooth?

Yes. Dental implants can be used to replace a single missing tooth, support a bridge, or stabilize a denture. Pendleton Smiles offers implant options for patients missing one tooth, multiple teeth, or a full arch of teeth.

What are implant-retained overdentures?

Implant-retained overdentures are dentures that attach to dental implants for added stability. They are more secure than traditional removable dentures and can still be removed for cleaning.

How do I know if I am a candidate for dental implants?

A dental implant consultation is the best way to find out. Your dentist will evaluate your oral health, jawbone support, missing teeth, and treatment goals before recommending an implant option.
