Prevention and Hygiene

Custom mouth guards made by a dentist offer better comfort, durability, and protection than store-bought options.

  • Night guards are recommended for patients who suffer from bruxism (teeth grinding) or TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorders, helping to reduce jaw pain, protect enamel, and improve sleep quality.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride is a natural mineral that strengthens tooth enamel and helps prevent decay. We offer professional fluoride treatments for both children and adults as part of our preventive care services. These treatments are especially beneficial for patients at higher risk of cavities.

Our office provides two types of fluoride:

  • Fluoride varnish, which is painted onto the teeth and absorbs quickly for long-lasting protection.
  • Fluoride gel, typically used in a tray, which is effective for patients needing broader coverage or enhanced enamel strengthening.
