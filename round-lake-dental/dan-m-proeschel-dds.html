Meet Dentist Dan M. Proeschel, DDS in Andover & South Minneapolis, MN

Dr. Dan Proeschel has spent 48 years dedicating himself to helping his patients achieve optimum dental health. He is passionate about the role that dental health plays in each individual’s total well-being.

Dr. Proeschel is a graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. A lifelong resident of Minnesota, Dr. Proeschel has practiced in the Twin Cities metropolitan area since 1973. Having spent hundreds of hours continuing his dental education, he takes great satisfaction in creating the smile of their dreams for his patients.

Dr. Proeschel resides in Bloomington. He enjoys being active in various sports, including golf, biking, and swimming. He also enjoys reading, boating, fishing and loves to travel when his schedule permits.
