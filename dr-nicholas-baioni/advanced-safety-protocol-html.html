What do I do if I can’t afford a dentist?

At the end of the day, dentistry makes prevention a priority. This is because preventive services are generally more affordable than restorative or emergency services designed to treat dental problems after they have already developed. Start by double checking your dental insurance plan’s benefits if you have them to see if they provide coverage for your needs. If you do not have a dental plan, we also offer third-party options like CareCredit that enable you to make monthly installments on your care over a longer period of time, often with little to no interest attached.

How can I make a same-day appointment with the dentist?

We recommend calling our office directly, rather than sending an email and waiting for us to get back to you. Calling us increases the chances of you getting a response sooner and allow our staff to provide guidance over the phone, especially in cases where your needs are urgent. We’ll do our best to get you seen the same day that you call and provide you with immediate relief.

How much does a checkup & cleaning cost in Pea Ridge?

Costs can vary based on your location and your current oral health, but on average checkups and cleanings fall between $200 and $350. If you have dental insurance, you can offset most of the costs associated with your care as these services are largely preventive in nature. During your checkup and cleaning, we’ll go over your patient health history and discuss any concerns you may have before completing an exam, cleaning, X-rays, and oral cancer screening.

How much does it cost to get a tooth pulled without insurance?

The average fee for a tooth extraction without dental insurance usually falls between $150 and $400, but this can vary depending on your unique circumstances. For example, if pulling the tooth is extremely difficult or if you need other related procedures to complete your extraction, your costs can increase.
