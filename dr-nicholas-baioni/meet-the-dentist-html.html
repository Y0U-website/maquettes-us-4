Get to Know Dr. Nicholas Baioni

As our dedicated dentist, Dr. Baioni really enjoys being able to help patients escape dental pain and finally achieve a smile they’re happy with! For each appointment, he focuses on prioritizing efficiency and convenience while still maintaining a trustworthy level of quality that Pea Ridge residents can rely on. If you’d like to learn more about him, just keep reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

As he embarked on a college career, Dr. Baioni knew that he wanted to one day do something in the field of medicine he just wasn’t sure what yet. Thankfully, he had one uncle and two aunts who worked as dentists of varying specialties. After shadowing his uncle for the first time, he realized that oral healthcare was the right fit for him. Seeing what was possible in this line of work and how it could make such a positive difference in someone’s life was truly inspiring and he’s never looked back!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

After completing his undergraduate degree at the University of Arkansas, Dr. Baioni pursued his dental doctorate at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center. Following graduation, he worked for one year at a community health clinic, where he performed a variety of procedures for the underserved population, before venturing into private practice. Today, he is a member of the American Dental Association and the Arkansas Dental Association.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Baioni and his wife met during their freshman year at the University of Arkansas, and they married in 2018. Their first child, Brooks, was born in January of 2021. Outside of dentistry, his two biggest hobbies are anything related to Arkansas Razorback sports and duck hunting. As alumni, he and his wife try to make it to as many sporting events as possible, especially baseball games at Baum Stadium. Ever since middle school, he’s loved going out on chilly mornings to hunt ducks with his dad and uncle as well. He can’t wait to bring Brooks along with him one day!
