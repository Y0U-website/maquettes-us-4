Partial Denture Fittings

At Renee Sheeler Denture Clinic, we believe that every smile is unique, and so are your dental needs. Our skilled denturists will work closely with you to design and create custom partial dentures that fit comfortably, look natural, and function flawlessly.

We understand the importance of affordability when it comes to dental care. That’s why we offer a range of budget-friendly partial denture options without compromising quality. We’ll help you find the right solution to suit both your oral health and your budget.

  • Metal Framework Partial Dentures: They are strong and made to aid in chewing when back teeth are missing. They anchor to your existing teeth and most of the time, stay in place without an adhesive. We make everything but the metal framework in our lab using all ADA-approved materials.
  • Acrylic Partial Dentures: These are the least expensive but do not have a long life span and only provide limited functionality. Can be made with or without wires. Most common for those missing one or two front teeth or as a temporary solution while a permanent partial or bridge is made. Mostly used for aesthetic purposes, not for function or as a long-term solution.
