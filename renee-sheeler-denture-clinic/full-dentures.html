Premium Dentures to Help You Rediscover Your Smile

Are you in search of a reliable solution to replace missing teeth? Since 1999, Renee Sheeler Denture Clinic has provided exemplary denture fitting services to those in need. As a proud family-owned establishment, we ensure our clients receive individualized attention and care when looking for the right dental solution.

When considering partial dentures, our range of DuraFlex Partials stands out due to their exceptional comfort, lightweight design, and flexibility. These partials look natural, making them virtually indistinguishable from real teeth. Moreover, with their virtually unbreakable and stain-resistant characteristics, DuraFlex Partials guarantee lasting beauty and durability.

On the other hand, our Metal Frame Work Partials are designed explicitly for those missing back teeth, ensuring enhanced strength to aid in chewing. What sets these apart is the anchoring mechanism. These partials anchor securely to your existing teeth, usually eliminating the need for adhesives.

At Renee Sheeler Denture Clinic, we prioritize your health and safety. That’s why we manufacture everything, barring the metal framework, in our in-house lab using materials approved by the American Dental Association (ADA).

Comprehensive Denture Solutions Tailored to You

Whether you’re considering a complete denture fitting or exploring options for partial dentures, our team at Renee Sheeler Denture Clinic has the expertise to guide you through every step. We take immense pride in our affiliations with the National Denture Association and the WA Denture Association, which serve as a testament to our dedication to quality and professionalism.

Emergencies can strike unexpectedly, but rest assured, we have got you covered. Our clinic offers emergency repair services to address any immediate issues with your dentures. And if you’re pressed for time or need urgent assistance, same-day appointments are often available.

Navigating the financial aspects of denture services can sometimes be daunting. To make the process smoother, we collaborate with most major insurance companies. If you have queries regarding insurance coverage or need clarification on specific details, our team is readily available to assist.

Conclusively, your journey to a confident and radiant smile is just a call or click away. At Renee Sheeler Denture Clinic, we believe everyone deserves the best, so we’re pleased to offer free consultations for potential clients.
