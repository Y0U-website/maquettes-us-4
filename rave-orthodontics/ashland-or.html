Orthodontist Ashland OR

  • Comfort dental misalignment doesn’t just look bad, it can feel bad. From how we speak to how we eat, if our teeth are not in proper alignment, it can make everyday activities really uncomfortable. Orthodontics can fix these issues and give you a better quality of life.
  • Prevention you may feel like now is not the right time’, but earlier is always the better choice when it comes to orthodontics. The longer you wait, the more likely it will be that treatment will take longer and be more extensive and expensive. In addition, early orthodontic intervention as early as age 7 can help prevent tooth wear, jaw deterioration, and other dental diseases.
  • Overall Health opting for orthodontic treatment is a commitment to your health. Not only does this help you establish healthy dental habits, but orthodontics can promote overall health by decreasing headaches, neck pain, and sleep disorders.
