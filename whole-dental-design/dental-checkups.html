DENTAL CHECK UPs

Why a Regular Dental Check Up is Important?

A regular dental check up is important because they help keep your teeth and gums healthy. You should have a regular dental visit at least every 6 months or as recommended by your dental professional. Based on this initial exam, your dentist will create your customized dental treatment plan, which will be a guide for you and your dental team going forward in order to give you a healthy mouth. While the services you’ll receive may vary, here’s what you can expect at your first dental checkup:

Your dentist at Whole Dental Design will tailor your comprehensive treatment plan, including your hygiene appointments, based on your initial exam. Treatment could begin as soon as the day following your initial exam. For more information about on-going care, click here.

  • A review of your medical history. Tell your dentist about your diet or medications as these may impact your treatment and oral health.
  • A conversation with your dentist about any concerns you have regarding your teeth, mouth, or overall oral health—we encourage you to ask questions.
  • An oral exam, including a visual gum tissue check and visual oral cancer scan.
  • X-rays, if necessary, to see your teeth below the gum line.
  • A comprehensive treatment plan, with recommended treatment and ongoing care from your dentist.
