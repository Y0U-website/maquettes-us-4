How Invisalign Works

Invisalign is a clear aligner system that gently shifts teeth over time to correct misalignment, overcrowding, and gaps. Instead of using metal brackets and wires, Invisalign’s discreet trays fit snugly against teeth and provide continuous pressure when worn 20-22 hours a day. After 1-2 weeks of wear, patients swap out their existing set for a new one so that their teeth will continue to move in the right direction.

The Benefits of Invisalign

  • Clear aligners are discreet, so others will never know they’re there
  • Greater flexibility because the trays are removable, so patients can eat what they want
  • Easy-to-clean solution that only requires lukewarm water, clear soap, and a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Faster results, on average, than traditional metal braces
  • Improved oral health by addressing any gaps, crowding, and misalignment

Candid Clear Aligners

An alternative to Invisalign is Candid Clear Aligners. Candid uses a thinner material and can be more comfortable for patients. Depending on the individual’s case, these aligners can often be a more cost-effective solution. With it, patients can achieve the same healthy, beautiful smile, but our dentists will be happy to go over each clear aligner option to determine which one is right for you.
