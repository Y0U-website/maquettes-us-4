What are Dental Implants?

Traditional tooth roots deliver stimulation to the jawbone each time a person bites down or chews. When teeth go missing, though, the bone remains untouched, causing it to shrink over time. With dental implants, the titanium, screw-like posts fit into the jawbone and deliver the same level of stimulation as their natural counterparts.

Dental implants are the only root-to-crown tooth replacement available, making them superior to more traditional solutions that only rest on top of the gums and mimic the look of a natural smile.

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Every dental implant placement begins with a consultation with one of our talented dentists. They will then go through a thorough exam focusing on your oral and physical health, your goals, lifestyle habits, and medical history.

This may seem like a lot of information, but it’s all necessary to get a clear picture of your eligibility for implants. It also helps our team plan your treatment with precision and care, reducing the risk of complications and making recovery a smoother experience.

Even if you’re not a good candidate right now, don’t worry. Our team can use the information gathered at your consultation to create a plan that prepares you for them later.

Dental Implant Surgery

The next step in the dental implant process is the surgery itself. At some practices, you’d need to be referred to a specialist for this. However, at Boulder Rock Dental, we do everything in-house. Not only does this make your treatment more convenient, but it also saves you money and ensures that you get care from a professional you trust.

During your surgery, our team will open your gums to access the bone underneath. Then, with special instruments, a pilot hole is made, and a titanium fixture is inserted into it. This is then capped with a healing abutment and sutured closed to begin the healing process.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

Once your implant is placed, it will need ample time to heal before moving to the final stage. During your recovery, a process called osseointegration takes place, fusing the implant permanently into your jawbone. This is a critical step to the success of your smile, as the post serves as an anchor for your future restoration.

You’ll need to continue your oral care routine and follow all of our team’s aftercare instructions. This reduces the risk of implant failure and ensures that your implant will be as strong as possible for the future.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Finally, the most exciting part of your treatment takes place the delivery of your restoration! This is when your custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to your implant, completing your smile.

Our team will ensure it fits well, your bite feels natural, and the aesthetics match your surrounding teeth. Once everything has undergone a final quality check, you’ll walk out with a fully restored smile that looks, feels, and functions just like the real thing.

Benefits of Dental Implants

More than half a million dental implants are placed annually, and it’s not hard to see why these astounding prosthetic devices are so popular when you consider the incredible oral and overall health benefits they provide. Here’s a quick guide to a few of the many perks of replacing your missing teeth with dental implants from the friendly team at Boulder Rock Dental in Palm Coast.

Day-to-Day Benefits

Enhanced Appearance The placement of the dental implants allows for a fuller, more vigorous facial appearance, which can give patients a more confident and youthful look.

No Food Restrictions Because dental implants are positioned within the jawbone for a stable hold and maximum leverage, patients can eat all their favorite foods with virtually no restrictions.

Superior Security Dental implants allow lifelike restorations to enjoy a secure hold, which prevents embarrassing situations such as dentures falling out of the mouth during meals or conversations.

Health Benefits

Jawbone Stimulation Once the implant posts are in the jawbone, they will fuse with it to create the foundation artificial teeth need to function just like the real thing. This also allows them to stimulate the jaw so it remains strong, full, and stable.

Supreme Stability Restorations supported by dental implants will remain firmly anchored in position, which can prevent gum irritation and enamel damage.

Dental Protection Dental implants will help the mouth distribute the pressure of chewing more evenly, which can spare the remaining natural teeth from unnecessary strain and enamel erosion.

Easy Oral Hygiene Dental implants require only the same oral hygiene routine as natural teeth, so you’ll only need to treat your smile to daily sessions of brushing, flossing, and antibacterial mouthwash to avoid cavities and gum disease. This means you can forgo the time-consuming cleaning rituals required by traditional dentures.

Long-Term Benefits

High Success Rate Dental implants have a success rate of around 95 % even after ten years!

Long Lifespan Dental implants that receive excellent care can last for several decades or potentially for life, which makes them a smart investment in your appearance and your oral and overall health.

Preserve Facial Structure Dental implants can keep the jaws stimulated and support the cheeks, which will help the patient maintain a youthful and robust appearance for years.

Fantastic First Impressions Dental implants can give patients the confidence they need to leave a positive first impression in business and social situations. This may help someone make friends, close sales, or even land a lucrative new job or promotion.
