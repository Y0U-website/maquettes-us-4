Meet the Doctors

Dr. Zeinab Abbas

She was drawn to dentistry because it brings together science, artistry, and service in a way few professions do. For Dr. Abbas, restoring a tooth is never just a technical exercise — it’s an opportunity to relieve discomfort, rebuild function, and give a patient back the confidence to eat, speak, and smile without thinking twice about it.

She also understands that for many people, the hardest part of a root canal is walking through the door. Dental anxiety is something Dr. Abbas takes seriously rather than brushes past. She explains every step before it happens, works at a pace her patients set, and checks in throughout treatment so no one is left guessing about what’s coming next. Patients who have avoided the dentist for years are often the ones she’s most glad to see — and many of them tell her it was nothing like what they braced for. Her goal is for every person in her chair to feel informed and in control, never rushed.

Dr. Abbas is a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside the office, Dr. Abbas’s time belongs to her family. She and her husband have three children, and together they love to travel and explore new places — she’s a firm believer that the best part of any trip is the people you meet along the way. Hiking has become a family favorite, and weekends on the trail are some of their best time spent together.

Dr. Camila Mallard

Dr. Camila Mallad, DDS, is passionate about providing thoughtful, personalized dental care that helps patients achieve healthier smiles and improved quality of life. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2024 and is a member of the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Mallad was drawn to dentistry because it allows her to combine science, artistry, and service while building meaningful relationships with patients. Through her experiences volunteering in communities with limited access to dental care, she developed a passion for connecting oral health with overall wellness through prevention, education, and compassionate care.

Her approach to dentistry centers around treating each patient as an individual and providing personalized treatment plans focused on comfort, function, and long-term health. She especially enjoys restorative dentistry, where she can combine precision and creativity to restore damaged teeth, relieve discomfort, and help patients feel confident in their smiles again. She provides a variety of treatments, including fillings, crowns, root canals, veneers, and dentures.

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Mallad continues to expand her skills through continuing education focused on advanced restorative techniques and endodontic care. She is also interested in advancements in dental laser technology and exploring areas such as airway dentistry and TMJ therapy to better serve patients with complex needs.

Outside of the office, Dr. Mallad enjoys spending time with family, traveling, taking walks, exploring new coffee shops, and baking. She loves creating desserts and documenting her baking projects, often designing dessert tables for special events.

Dr. Mallad appreciates being part of a supportive, collaborative team that is committed to creating a welcoming experience for every patient. She values the opportunity to care for people from diverse backgrounds and believes every interaction is an opportunity to learn, connect, and make a positive impact.

Dr. Muhammad Atif

Dr. Muhammad Atif, DDS, FICOI, is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized, compassionate care. With more than 10 years of dental experience, he combines clinical expertise, advanced technology, and a genuine commitment to building lasting relationships with his patients.

Dr. Atif earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2020 and is a licensed Michigan dentist. He is also a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Michigan Dental Association (MDA), as well as a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI).

Drawn to dentistry because of its unique blend of science, artistry, and patient connection, Dr. Atif believes in treating every patient like family. He focuses on honest recommendations, gentle care, and customized treatment plans designed to support lifelong oral health.

His areas of interest include comprehensive general dentistry, preventive care, restorative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and clear aligner therapy. He is committed to staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry, including digital technology, 3D imaging, and modern treatment techniques that improve patient comfort and outcomes.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Atif enjoys spending time with family and friends, traveling, staying active, exploring new restaurants, and enjoying the outdoors. He values getting to know his patients beyond their dental needs and believes strong relationships are at the heart of exceptional care.
