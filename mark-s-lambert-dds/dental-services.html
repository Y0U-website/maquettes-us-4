Menu Facebook-f Dental Services Services Include Cleanings Regular dental cleanings are essential for maintaining healthy teeth and gums. Our professional hygienists will remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains, leaving your smile refreshed and your oral health in top condition.

Gum Disease Treatment We provide effective treatments for gum disease (gingivitis and periodontitis) to prevent further damage. Our services include scaling, root planing, and guidance on maintaining healthy gums to protect your smile.

Comprehensive Exams A thorough examination of your oral health, including teeth, gums, and jaw structure. Our dentist will assess your overall dental health, check for signs of decay or disease, and recommend any necessary treatments.

Preventative Care Our preventative services focus on keeping your teeth and gums healthy. This includes regular check-ups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and advice on brushing, flossing, and diet to prevent dental issues before they start.

Relines Relining is a process used to adjust the fit of your dentures. It helps restore comfort and stability by reshaping the inside surface of the denture to fit your gums better.

Consultations Schedule a consultation to discuss your dental concerns, treatment options, and goals. Our team will guide you through your options and help you make an informed decision about your oral health.

Oral Cancer Screening Regular oral cancer screenings are crucial for detecting early signs of oral cancer. Our dentist checks for abnormalities, such as sores, lumps, or lesions, and provides early detection for better outcomes.

Patient Education We believe in empowering our patients with the knowledge they need to maintain good oral health. Our team provides personalized advice and education on dental care, hygiene, and preventive treatments. Crowns Crowns are custom-made caps that restore the shape, size, and strength of damaged or decayed teeth. Our crowns blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing durability and aesthetic appeal.

Repairs to Partials & Dentures We offer repair services for partials and dentures to ensure they remain comfortable and functional. Whether it’s fixing a crack or adjusting fit, we help restore your dentures to optimal condition.

Bridges Dental bridges are an excellent solution for replacing missing teeth. They span the gap between teeth, restoring your smile and improving both function and appearance.

Low Radiation Digital X-Rays We use state-of-the-art digital X-rays that require less radiation and provide instant, high-quality images. This advanced technology helps us diagnose and treat dental issues with greater accuracy.

Tooth-colored Fillings We use composite resin fillings to repair cavities in a way that blends naturally with your teeth. These fillings are durable, aesthetic, and offer a more discreet option than traditional metal fillings.

Implant Restoration We provide restoration services for dental implants, including custom crowns and bridges, to restore the function and appearance of your teeth after implant placement.

Dentures Dentures are removable appliances designed to replace missing teeth. We offer full and partial dentures tailored to fit comfortably, restore your smile, and improve your ability to eat and speak.

Preventative Dentistry

Recent research suggests there may be a link between gum disease and other health problems. We strive to provide you with excellent preventative care to remove the bacteria plaque which can build up and cause inflammation of the gums, tooth decay, and even loss of teeth. Preventing gum disease is the best way to keep your smile healthy. With our staff, we will keep you compliant and always on schedule for that next preventative appointment.
