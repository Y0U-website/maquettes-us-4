Meet Collingswood, NJ, Dentist Charles Wahl, D.M.D.

Meet your Dentist in Collingswood, NJ: Charles Wahl, D.M.D.

Dr. Charlie Wahl was born and raised on the island of Guam. After attending Glassboro State College, he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Temple University in Philadelphia. Subsequently, Dr. moved back to Guam to practice dentistry. After four years he joined the practice of Dr. Thomas Honhold and Peter Samson in Collingswood, NJ. He is an active member of the American Equilibration Society, Advanced Dental Implant Study Club, Faculty Club Elite member at Spear, and is a Scholar from the Dawson Academy for advanced dental education.

What to Expect on a Visit to Dr. Wahl

Dr. Wahl’s work centers on the in-depth attention and care he gives his patients. He believes in treating the masticatory system of his patients to help create a more harmonious relationship between the joints, muscles, and teeth. Each patient has unique needs to obtain optimal oral health, and he will work closely with you to achieve your desired results. Your first visit will consist of a 90-minute appointment where you will receive a comprehensive exam, a full set of radiographs, joint analysis, oral tissue evaluation, oral cancer screening, and smile analysis.
