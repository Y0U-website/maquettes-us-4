Dental Implants Collingswood, NJ

Dental implants are a revolutionary solution for replacing one or more missing teeth. They are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials like titanium. Dr. Charles Wahl works with a local specialist to place all types of dental implants, from single teeth to implant-supported dentures. Dr.Wahl provides experienced dental implant services for patients with missing teeth. Understanding the importance and benefits of dental implants can help you make informed decisions about your oral health.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

For many patients, dental implants are a great choice because of their natural look and permanent results. A dental implant replaces both the missing tooth root and the crown of the tooth which is visible above the gum line. They can also be placed to support a dental bridge or a denture, securing your new smile and restoring natural function. Stable and secure, dental implants minimize irritation of soft tissues and allow normal biting force for enjoying a varied diet.

Improved Appearance

One of the most significant benefits of dental implants is improving your appearance. Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth. They are designed to fuse with your jawbone, making them permanent. This fusion helps maintain the natural shape of your face and prevents the sunken look that often occurs with missing teeth.

Enhanced Chewing Ability

Dental implants function like natural teeth so that you can enjoy your favorite foods without worry. Unlike dentures, which can slip or cause discomfort, implants remain secure. This stability makes it easier to chew and speak without any difficulties.

Durability

Dental implants are known for their strength and durability. With proper care, they can last a lifetime. Regular dental check-ups with Dr. Wahl ensure your implants remain in excellent condition. This long-term solution sets dental implants apart from other tooth replacement options.

Dental Implant Procedure

Consultation and Evaluation: The first step in the dental implant process is a consultation with Dr. Charles Wahl. During this visit, he will evaluate your oral health and determine if you are a good candidate for implants. This evaluation includes X-rays and a thorough examination of your jawbone structure. If implants are right for you, he can recommend an oral surgeon for your procedure.

Implant Placement Process: Once you are cleared for the procedure, the implant placement begins. An oral surgeon will surgically place the implant into your jawbone. This procedure is typically done under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. The implant is then allowed to heal and integrate with the bone.

Healing and Osseointegration: After the implant is placed, a process called osseointegration begins. This is when the implant fuses with the jawbone, creating a strong foundation. This healing period can take several months, during which time you may receive a temporary crown to maintain your appearance.

Placement of the Crown: Once osseointegration is complete, Dr. Wahl can place the permanent crown on the implant. This crown is custom-made to match the color and shape of your natural teeth. The result is a functional and aesthetically pleasing tooth replacement. At our practice, we mostly restore dental implants and crowns.

Aftercare for Dental Implants

Compared to other restorative treatments, dental implants are relatively low-maintenance. However, you still need to develop good oral hygiene habits.

Oral Hygiene Practices

Maintaining good oral hygiene is crucial for the longevity of your dental implants. Brush your teeth at least twice a day and floss regularly. Using an antibacterial mouthwash can also help keep your mouth clean and free from infection.

Regular Dental Check-ups

Regular visits to Dr. Wahl are essential for monitoring the health of your implants. During these check-ups, he will examine your gums and the implant site to ensure everything is in good condition. Any issues can be addressed promptly to prevent complications.

Potential Complications and Solutions

While dental implants have a high success rate, complications can occur. Common issues include infection and implant failure. Dr. Wahl will provide you with detailed aftercare instructions to minimize these risks. If complications do arise, prompt treatment can help ensure the long-term success of your implants.
