Meet Your Dentist in Collingswood, NJ: Dr. Gregory Czachor

Dr. Gregory Czachor is a dedicated dentist known for his commitment to patient care and oral health. Born and raised in New Jersey, Dr. Czachor developed a passion for dentistry at a young age, inspired by his childhood dentist who made every visit a positive experience.

He earned his Bachelor’s Degree in Biology from the Rutgers University where he was admitted to the highly competitive BA/DMD program affiliated with Rutgers University Dental School. During his studies at Rutgers Dental School, Dr. Czachor excelled in both clinical skills and patient communication, earning several awards for his academic achievements such as the Gamma Pi Prosthodontic Honor Society.

After completing his education, Dr. Czachor further advanced his training by completing a dental residency at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital in New Brunswick, NJ. At the Level 1 trauma center he treated and gained more valuable experience across all facets of dentistry.

Dr. Czachor takes pride for his gentle approach and treatment of patients. He is committed to staying up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry, regularly attending workshops and courses to enhance his skills and knowledge. Dr. Czachor always remains committed to improving the lives of his patients through exceptional dental care.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Czachor enjoys going to the gym, watching movies, and spending time with his family.
