We are seeking a compassionate individual who is committed to growing both personally and professionally. If this sounds like you, come join our supportive team. We are seeking an enthusiastic and personable dental assistant with a minimum of 2 years’ experience, but we are willing to train if you are a perfect fit.

The dental assistant works under a dentist’s supervision to prepare equipment that will be used in dentistry procedures, to ready patients for these procedures, and to help the dentist during these procedures by passing instruments, holding the suction catheter in place, and generally anticipating the dentist’s needs. Some Other responsibilities may include sophisticated functions such as removing sutures, processing x-rays and taking the oral impressions used to fit crowns.

Summary: Completes treatment of patient’s oral and periodontal care including patient education by performing the following duties.
