What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials, such as titanium, that are surgically placed into your jawbone to support replacement teeth. They serve as a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures, mimicking the natural structure and function of real teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, dental implants are a permanent solution that fuses with the jawbone, providing unmatched stability and durability.

At SoCal Modern Dentistry, we use state-of-the-art techniques and equipment to ensure your dental implant treatment is as comfortable and efficient as possible.

Our Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process at SoCal Modern Dentistry is designed to be straightforward and comfortable. We’ll work closely with you to customize a treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. Here’s an overview of what to expect:

1. Consultation and Evaluation

Your dental implant journey begins with a consultation at our Torrance office. During this visit, our dentist will discuss your goals, assess your oral health, and determine if dental implants are right for you. We’ll take digital X-rays and 3D images to evaluate your jawbone density and ensure there is sufficient bone structure to support the implant.

If you need additional procedures, such as a bone graft, we’ll explain these options and create a plan that prepares you for successful implant placement.

2. Implant Placement Surgery

Once your treatment plan is set, the next step is the implant placement surgery. Our team will place the titanium implant post into the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. This minor surgical procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. After placement, the implant will undergo a healing process called osseointegration, during which the implant fuses with the bone, creating a stable foundation for the artificial tooth.

3. Healing and Temporary Restoration

The osseointegration process typically takes a few months, during which time the implant will become securely anchored in the bone. In some cases, we may provide a temporary restoration to maintain the appearance of your smile while you heal.

4. Abutment Placement

After the implant has successfully integrated with the jawbone, an abutment (connector) is placed on top of the implant. This small piece extends slightly above the gum line and serves as the attachment point for your custom crown.

5. Custom Crown Placement

The final step involves placing the custom crown, bridge, or denture on the abutment. Our team will ensure the restoration matches the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, giving you a fully restored smile that looks and feels completely natural.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

One of the advantages of dental implants is that they’re easy to care for. However, regular dental hygiene practices are essential to maintain their longevity and appearance. Here are some care tips to keep your implants in excellent condition:

By following these simple care guidelines, you can enjoy the benefits of your dental implants for many years.

  • Brush and Floss Daily : Just like natural teeth, implants require regular brushing and flossing to prevent plaque buildup.
  • Regular Dental Checkups : Visit us every six months for routine checkups to ensure the health of your implants and gums.
  • Avoid Hard Foods : Be mindful of chewing very hard foods, which could damage the restoration or put stress on the implant.
  • Wear a Mouthguard : If you grind your teeth or play sports, consider wearing a mouthguard to protect your implants and natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Implants

Are dental implants painful? The implant placement procedure is usually performed under local anesthesia, minimizing discomfort. After the procedure, some mild soreness is expected but can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How long does the dental implant process take? The entire process, from implant placement to the final crown, typically takes several months. This allows for proper healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone.

Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most people are candidates for dental implants, though sufficient bone density is required. Our team can assess your eligibility during your consultation and discuss alternatives if necessary.

How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants varies depending on factors like the number of implants and any additional procedures needed. We offer payment plans and financing options to make implants affordable for our patients.
