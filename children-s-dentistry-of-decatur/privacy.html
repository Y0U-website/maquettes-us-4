3. Website Technologies Disclosure

Like many website operators, Children&#x27;s Dentistry of Decatur and its analytics vendors use server logs and automated data collection tools, such as browser cookies, pixel tags, scripts and web beacons.

What is a cookie?

  • Session cookies provide information about how a website is used during a single browser session. They usually expire after the browser is closed.
  • Persistent cookies remain on your device between browser sessions to remember user preferences, settings, or actions.
  • First-party cookies are set by the operator of the website you are visiting.
  • Third-party cookies are set by third parties different from the website operator.
