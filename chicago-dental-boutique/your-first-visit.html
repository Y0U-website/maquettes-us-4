Our office staff provides personalized insurance support, helping to maximize your coverage. Choose from a variety of payment options that match your financial budget.

Relax in the a Spa-like Environment

Our treatment rooms are designed for your comfort. You can relax in a spa-like environment with beautiful views of Lakeshore East Park from our treatment rooms, and sit back in our massage chairs and watch TV on the overhead screen while wearing our noise canceling headphones.

Receive a Comprehensive Oral Exam

After having a personal visit with the dentist, it is time to start a thorough examination of your teeth. The dentist will go over your medical and dental history with you, and ask you a series of questions about your teeth related to function and cosmetic needs and concerns. Then, he will examine digital x-rays and provide an expert oral health exam, followed by a personalized treatment plan (if applicable). Our treatment plan coordinator will then provide all of the information you need to start treatment including insurance coverage, patient financial duties and scheduling recommendations.

High Quality Treatment

Our focus in dentistry is in providing high quality treatment. We don’t rush our treatments or cut any corners. We only use the highest end products and technologies to ensure the highest quality results. We do this because we are committed to perfection, and we love it.

Before You Leave

Your oral health is important to us and we’ll do everything we can to help you stay on track with your smile goals. If all you need are your 6-month cleanings, we’ll take care of that. If you want to get Invisalign but are not ready to start now, we’ll help you to plan for the future. Our team is here for you, whatever your dental needs may be.
