FAQs About Our Pine Lawn Location

FAQs About Our Pine Lawn Location

We want to ensure you have all the necessary information before your first visit to our Tower Grove clinic. Here are some of the most common questions we receive from patients regarding our services and office policies.

When Should I Book My First Appointment?

We recommend scheduling a routine exam and cleaning every six months to maintain the health of your smile. If you have not seen a dentist in a while, our Tower Grove team is here to help you get back on track with a judgment-free visit.

Do You Accept Medicaid At This Location?

Yes, our Tower Grove office proudly accepts Missouri Medicaid for both adults and children. We are committed to ensuring that quality dental care remains accessible and affordable for all our neighbors.

Are X-Rays Required During A Checkup?

We use digital X-rays to identify issues that are not visible during a standard exam, such as decay between teeth or bone loss. We only recommend X-rays when they are necessary to provide an accurate diagnosis or to plan your treatment.

Is This Office A Good Fit For Children?

We enjoy seeing patients of all ages and focus on making dental visits a positive experience for children. Our team provides gentle care to help your little ones develop healthy habits that will last a lifetime.
