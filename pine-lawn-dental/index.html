The practice

At our new office, we strive to create a stress-free environment where patients of all ages feel welcome. We have built our practice around the needs of the local community to ensure you receive dental care that fits your lifestyle and your budget.

Whether you are looking for a routine cleaning or need to restore your smile, our Tower Grove team provides a wide range of services. We emphasize personalized treatment plans that prioritize your long-term oral health, giving you the confidence to share your smile.

We provide thorough cleanings and exams to help you maintain a healthy smile and catch potential issues early.

We offer emergency services on a first-come, first-served basis. You can walk in Monday through Friday at 8:30 AM for urgent care, although please note that we do not accept walk-ins on Tuesdays. If you are experiencing pain, please contact our office right away.

Les soins

We use digital X-rays to identify issues that are not visible during a standard exam, such as decay between teeth or bone loss. We only recommend X-rays when they are necessary to provide an accurate diagnosis or to plan your treatment.

Implants help protect the jawbone and do not rely on neighboring teeth for support. Many patients find they speak and chew more comfortably compared to removable dentures.

If you are living with missing teeth, we offer durable solutions to help you regain full function and comfort.

Our team provides alignment treatments to help straighten your teeth and improve your bite.

We focus on treatments that improve the appearance of your teeth, from professional whitening to reshaping.
