Dental Implants FAQs

Interested in dental implants? Read our dental implant FAQs to learn more about your options, treatment process, and what to expect.

Am I a candidate for implants?

Most healthy adults are candidates. Adequate bone and healthy gums are important. If needed, additional procedures (such as bone grafting) may be recommended to support long-term success.

How long does the process take?

Timing varies by case. Some patients receive a temporary tooth the same day, while others benefit from a healing period before the final restoration. We’ll review your timeline at the consultation.

Do implants hurt?

Placement is performed with local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort afterward. We’ll provide clear instructions for a smooth recovery.

How do implants compare to dentures or bridges?

Implants help protect the jawbone and do not rely on neighboring teeth for support. Many patients find they speak and chew more comfortably compared to removable dentures.

Are implants covered by insurance or Medicaid?

Coverage varies by plan ; many policies consider implants elective. We’ll check your benefits and provide a transparent estimate. CareCredit financing is available to make treatment more affordable.
