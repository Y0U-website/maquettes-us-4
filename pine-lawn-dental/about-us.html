Dr. Mario Franco-Di Tore

Dr. Mario Franco-Di Tore, DMD is an associate dentist at Pine Lawn Dental, where he provides patient-centered, comprehensive general dentistry with a strong focus on cosmetic dentistry and restorative care. With two years of clinical experience, Dr. Mario is committed to delivering high-quality treatment that supports both long-term oral health and confident smiles.

Dr. Mario earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. He offers a wide range of services, including cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, root canal therapy, crown and bridge work, dentures, and removable partial dentures. He actively pursues continuing education in fixed and removable dentistry, restorative dentistry, and implant dentistry to ensure his patients receive evidence-based, modern care.

Dr. Mario believes in delivering patient-centered care through clear communication, thoughtful treatment planning, and a calm, compassionate approach. He prioritizes long-term oral health while ensuring patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their care.

Committed to professional growth, Dr. Mario regularly attends continuing education courses to stay current with advancements in dental materials, techniques, and technology. At Pine Lawn Dental, he utilizes modern tools such as digital imaging and advanced diagnostic technology to provide efficient, precise, and comfortable care, including same-day treatment when appropriate.

Dr. Mario values a collaborative, team-based practice environment where patients are treated with respect and care is delivered with consistency and attention to detail. He is proud to work alongside a supportive team dedicated to creating a welcoming experience for every patient.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Mario enjoys staying active through tennis and golf, pursuits that reflect his appreciation for precision, focus, and continuous improvement. He is also an avid sports fan and proudly supports the Cincinnati Bengals and Ohio State football.

Dr. Mario looks forward to welcoming new patients to Pine Lawn Dental and helping each individual achieve a healthy, confident smile.
