Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Aligner FAQs

Clear Aligner FAQs

Ready to start your aligner journey? We’ll design a plan that fits your goals, lifestyle, and budget.

Am I a candidate for clear aligners?

Many mild to moderate spacing, crowding, and bite concerns respond well to aligners. We’ll evaluate your goals and recommend the best options for your smile.

How long will treatment take?

The average treatment duration is approximately 12 months , although timing varies by case. We’ll provide a personalized estimate after your exam and digital scan.

How many hours per day do I need to wear them?

Plan on 20–22 hours daily . Remove only to eat, drink anything besides water, and brush/floss.

Can I whiten while in treatment?

Yes— whitening is often included with SureSmile® systems. We’ll review when to whiten and how to maintain your results.

Will insurance or Medicaid cover aligners?

Orthodontic coverage varies by plan and age. We accept most PPO plans and Missouri Medicaid for covered services. Our team will verify benefits and provide a clear estimate. CareCredit financing is available.
