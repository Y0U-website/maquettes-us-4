Advanced Technology

We understand the value that advanced dental technology plays in providing you with exceptional, high-quality care. Dr. Pagel, Dr. Sutherland, and Dr. Albers are all passionate about maintaining a cutting-edge facility and invest in the most up-to-date equipment to better serve you. We use dental technologies like digital x-rays, CEREC same day crowns, and SureSmile to help us deliver a more accurate diagnosis, more comfortable treatment and lasting results. We will continue to invest with you in mind.

Personal Relationships

We work in the dental field because we have a strong passion for helping others. We enjoy interacting with our patients and look forward to getting to know you personally. Our personalized relationships with our patients allow us to better meet your needs and deliver a customized dental experience. We are here to help you achieve your goal of creating a beautiful, healthy smile, and we know that is best achieved through personalized care.

Quality Dentistry

Our team never settles for less than the best when it comes to your oral health. We consistently deliver thorough, high-quality treatments, we pay attention to detail, and we utilize advanced technologies to ensure lasting health solutions and beautiful results for you. Beyond that, we make patient education a priority. We will answer your questions and explain all the details of your care. At Downtown Dentists, we aim for excellence in everything we do.

Trusted Care

We strive to be known as the practice that can be counted on for integrity. With our constant commitment to excellence, we can proudly stand behind the work we do for you and your family. We provide honest recommendations and clear explanations, never pushing you to do anything you don’t want or need. We will always keep your best interest in mind. We’re a team you can trust.

In-House, Comprehensive Services

At Downtown Dentists, we offer a comprehensive list of services so we can meet the majority of your dental needs all in one place. From general dentistry and cosmetic procedures to implants, SureSmile, root canal treatment and more, there’s little we don’t do. We aim to meet your needs under one roof so that you can feel at ease and secure in every aspect of your dental care.

Familiar Team

We have had the privilege of serving the community for more than 20 years now. You’ll get to see familiar faces every time you visit, and our patients love that about us. Our caring team works together to make sure each visit to our office is exceptional. At Downtown Dentists, you will always know you are receiving personalized care.
