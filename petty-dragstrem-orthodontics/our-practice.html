Dr. Kristie

I grew up in Orland Park and currently live in Hinsdale with my husband Ryan and children Vivian, Svea, & Karl. We love the small town feel of the community and surrounding areas so please make sure to say hello if you see me around town! In our free time we enjoy trying new restaurants, snow skiing, neighborhood walks with our dog Stella, and spending quality time with family.

I feel considerably blessed to be in a profession where I have the opportunity to help people gain confident, beautiful smiles. Orthodontics is my passion and I truly love what I do everyday. Having children has given me an entirely different view of my profession. I now see the importance of orthodontics through a mother’s eyes.

My favorite perk of orthodontics is getting to know my patients and their families. Making them a part of my extended family is very important to me. I want to hear about their latest achievement, trips, jobs; basically, I want to know their stories! When you are in the office I want it to feel like the comforts of HOME!

Continuing education is very important to me so that I can stay on the forefront of orthodontic research and technological developments. In turn, this allows me to provide treatment with less appointments, improved comfort, and better esthetics for my patients. I am currently an active member of the following affiliations:

  • American Association of Orthodontists
  • American Dental Association
  • Illinois Society of Orthodontists
  • Illinois State Dental Society
  • Bachelors of Science (BS) in Molecular and Cellular Biology: University of Illinois at Champaign-Urbana
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS): University of Illinois at Chicago
  • Specialty residency in orthodontics and Masters of Science (MS) in Oral Science: University of Illinois at Chicago
