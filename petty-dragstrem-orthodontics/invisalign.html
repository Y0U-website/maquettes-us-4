Invisalign

  • Removable: no food or drink limitations and brushing and flossing can be done as normally.
  • Comfortable: flexible plastic trays mean no wires or metal which means no emergencies.
  • Customized: made specifically for your teeth leading to less adjustment appointments.

What is it?

Invisalign® is a series of clear, removable aligners that progressively straighten your teeth and bite. Invisalign clear aligners are made specifically for you with 3D computer imaging technology and our doctor’s input. Aligners are worn 1-2 weeks full time (except eating, drinking, or brushing) and then switched out for the next set. When we see you for your 8-10 week appointment we ensure your teeth are moving as planned and then give you your next couple of sets.

Invisalign ® for teens

Invisalign® is not just for adults. We offer Invisalign® Teen for those looking for a less obvious option to straighten teeth. We understand how socially busy our teenagers are and giving them a more comfortable, removable, and clear solution may fit in their lifestyle better.

Invisalign Teen can treat a wide variety of dental problems that in the past were only treated with traditional braces. These conditions include:

  • Overbites
  • Underbites
  • Crowded teeth
  • Spaced teeth
