How Does the Process Go?

Here’s what you can expect with dental implants at Nashville Dentistry Co.

A small titanium post is surgically placed within your jaw, which serves as an artificial tooth root. We’ll use digital imaging and advanced technology to plan and place your implant for long-term success.

Once your post is healed, which takes an average of three months, an abutment is placed. This connects your post and crown, serving as a solid foundation. Often, we place your abutment and crown at the same appointment.

  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? There a few factors that go into being a dental implant candidate. These include: Age: Your mouth will need to be fully developed.
  • Oral health: To have successful implantation, you’ll need to be free of oral diseases, like periodontitis.
  • Overall health: We’ll examine your health history to make sure this procedure is safe for you and the post will heal properly. Factors like smoking and uncontrolled chronic diseases may jeopardize your eligibility.
  • Bone structure: You’ll need sufficient bone to support an implant. If you don’t have enough, a bone graft may be needed.
  • How long do dental implants last? Well-placed and cared-for dental implants should last for decades. Luckily, dental implants don’t require much additional maintenance than your natural teeth: Brush your teeth twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss your teeth once a day (This is important for implants! Make sure you floss around the post)
