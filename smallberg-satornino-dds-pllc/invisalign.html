Reasons to Choose Invisalign® in Westbury, NY

Invisalign® removable braces have become a popular orthodontic treatment because of their many benefits, including the following:

  • Discreet Clear aligners cover your teeth and make adjustments without anyone noticing.
  • Removable Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign® aligners are completely removable. Removing them beforehand lets you eat and drink anything you want and keep your aligners clean.
  • Comfortable These clear aligners are smooth and made from soft plastic, which will not irritate your lips, cheeks, or gums.
  • Convenient You will require fewer trips to the dental office because the aligner trays are already created at the start of your treatment. You just need to swap trays at home after two weeks, or as advised by Dr. Smallberg or Dr. Satornino .
  • Custom-made Trays are crafted specifically for your teeth, fitting them comfortably and precisely while realigning them.
  • Effective Clear aligners have proven to be successful in treating mild to moderate orthodontic problems.
  • Predictable With sophisticated digital treatment planning, Invisalign® treatment results can be anticipated and seen in advance.

FAQ About Invisalign in Westbury, NY

Smallberg & Satornino, DDS, PLLC, located in Westbury, NY, is home to two certified Invisalign providers, Dr. Smallberg and Dr. Satornino. Both doctors are trained to use Invisalign to correct cosmetic and functional dental issues, including overcrowding, gaps, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and misaligned teeth. You can contact the office to schedule a consultation and find out if Invisalign is right for you.

Whether Invisalign is better than traditional braces depends on your specific case and personal preferences, as each has its own advantages. Invisalign offers benefits such as being removable, virtually invisible, and more comfortable, since there are no metal brackets or wires, making it a popular choice for mild to moderate alignment issues. However, traditional braces may be recommended for more complex orthodontic cases. Dr. Smallberg or Dr. Satornino can assess your teeth during a consultation and recommend which option will give you the best results.

The length of Invisalign treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case and the amount of movement your teeth need. Patients typically switch to a new set of aligner trays approximately every two weeks until treatment is complete, though the timeline varies from person to person. Dr. Smallberg or Dr. Satornino can give you a personalized treatment timeline based on an evaluation of your teeth.

The cost of Invisalign depends on several factors, including the complexity of your case, the number of aligner trays required, and your insurance coverage. During a consultation, Dr. Smallberg or Dr. Satornino can evaluate your specific alignment needs and provide an accurate cost estimate along with available financing options .

Invisalign aligners should be cleaned regularly to keep them clear and free of bacteria. This generally involves rinsing them when you take them out, gently brushing them with a soft toothbrush, and avoiding hot water, which can warp the plastic. Since the aligners are removable, you can continue brushing and flossing your teeth as usual without obstruction. Dr. Smallberg or Dr. Satornino can provide specific care instructions tailored to your aligners during treatment.

Because Invisalign aligners are removed before eating and drinking, the trays themselves are not typically exposed to food and stains the way teeth or fixed braces are. However, if aligners aren’t properly cleaned, residue from dark-colored foods or drinks, such as coffee, tea, or red wine, could still affect their clarity over time. Removing your aligners before eating and cleaning them as directed helps keep them clear and discreet.

Most patients need to wear a retainer after completing Invisalign treatment to help keep their teeth from shifting back to their original position. The frequency and duration of retainer wear vary by individual and are typically tailored to your specific case. Dr. Smallberg or Dr. Satornino will provide personalized guidance on your retainer schedule once your treatment is complete.
