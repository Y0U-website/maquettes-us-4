Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are an increasingly popular substitute for traditional braces . Instead of using brackets and wires to straighten teeth, these are a series of translucent, customized aligner trays. You wear each set of trays for one to two weeks before switching them out for the next set in the series. The clear aligners gradually move your teeth into their proper locations. Treatment time varies based on your specific alignment needs.

We are happy to provide our patients with Invisalign®, the most popular brand of clear aligners available today.
