Invisalign Clear Braces

At the beginning of your Invisalign treatment, you’ll be given a series of aligners. Each one is designed to move the teeth slightly closer to their eventual positions; once you’ve completed the series, you’ll have a straight, properly aligned smile to admire. Unlike braces, your aligners will be made out of clear plastic, making them much more discreet when worn. You can also take them out of your mouth to enjoy your favorite foods.
