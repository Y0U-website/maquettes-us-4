Meet Dr. Azhari H. Elamin, DDS

Dr. Azhari H. Elamin - DDS

Dr. Azhari Elamin is proud to come from a family of healthcare providers — his parents are both optometrists, and his older sister is a dentist. Having spent part of his life living in Sudan, Dr. Elamin developed a deep appreciation for culture, language, and the value of human connection. He speaks Arabic and brings with him the warmth and hospitality of the communities that helped shape him.

Dr. Elamin chose dentistry because it beautifully blends science, art, and service. With a lifelong passion for drawing, critical thinking, and understanding how things work, he finds deep purpose in helping others feel healthier, more confident, and genuinely cared for. Outside the office, Dr. Elamin enjoys staying active — whether it’s basketball, hiking, yoga, or exploring new foods. He also has a strong interest in philosophy and history and believes that true health goes beyond the physical. For him, wellness means balance — mind, body, and spirit.
