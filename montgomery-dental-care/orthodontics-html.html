Traditional Braces

Traditional braces are what most patients think of when they hear the word orthodontics. Using metal brackets and wires, braces place a gentle amount of pressure on your teeth and entire arch to help gradually shift them into alignment. They can address all types of concerns, ranging from bite misalignment to crowding.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign offers an excellent alternative to noticeable traditional braces for patients experiencing minor to moderate orthodontic problems, including bite misalignment. Throughout your journey, you will receive a new pair of custom-crafted trays every two weeks, which will place a different amount of pressure on your teeth to shift them.

Phase I Orthodontic Treatment

We can help assess the need for orthodontic care for your children starting at around seven years old. In some cases, we may recommend intervention or special developmental appliances to help them avoid serious orthodontic concerns down the road. We partner with several local specialists to provide collaborative, comprehensive phase 1 orthodontic care for children.
