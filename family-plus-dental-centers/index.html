At Family Plus Dental Centers, our oral surgeons perform dental implant procedures, expertly placing implants in empty spaces.

Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth and are commonly used to replace teeth that have been removed or lost.

Dental implant procedures are an effective way to restore you natural smile with little to no hassle. A dental implant does not require other teeth and can stand on its own. With dental implants, a titanium post is used to fuse together the artificial tooth with surrounding bone.

Following dental implant procedures, the bone heals around the implant and holds it in place just like the roots of the original tooth. There is nothing temporary about dental implants as they can last a lifetime.

Dental implants are also used to support other integral dental prosthetics such as removable dentures, permanent bridges and crowns.
