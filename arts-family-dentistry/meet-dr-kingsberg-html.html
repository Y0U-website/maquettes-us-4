How Do I Get Emergency Dental Care?

First, you’ll want to call our dental practice about getting emergency dental treatment, that way someone on our team can schedule you in. Not only do we offer same-day appointments, but we’re also open on Saturdays, providing more convenient hours for those who need our help. Should you experience a dental emergency, don’t hesitate to give us a call.

However, if your situation is life-threatening, be sure to call 911 first and/or head straight to your local ER for help. Though the ER staff may not be equipped to treat dental emergencies, they can at least alleviate your symptoms until you can seek care from your dentist. In most cases, a dental emergency is an indication of an underlying dental issue that could have been avoided with good oral hygiene and biannual dental visits. Remember to see your dentist regularly to save yourself time and money with a healthy smile.

How Much Does it Cost to Get a Tooth Pulled Without Insurance?

The exact price of a tooth extraction without help from dental insurance will depend on factors like geography and a patient’s oral health. In many instances across the United States, the average cost for this treatment can be anywhere between $150 and $400. Extra expenses may also apply based on the difficulty of the extraction and if sedation or any other procedures were required. For wisdom teeth extractions, you might expect a higher price. If you do have insurance, the cost of the procedure will be negotiated between your provider and the dentist.
