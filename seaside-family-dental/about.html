DR. BARRIENTOS

EXPERIENCED, COMPASSIONATE, PROFESSIONAL

Dr. Barrientos is a highly accomplished dental professional and decorated U.S. Naval Officer dedicated to providing exceptional care for patients of all ages in Rhode Island. A graduate of the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey (1994), she completed her residency at Oakland Naval Hospital and served over six years on active duty, followed by 19 years in various Navy Reserve roles. Her military career includes leadership positions such as Officer in Charge and Detachment Director, supporting operations like Iraqi Freedom and Expeditionary Medical Facilities.

Currently, she continues her service as a dental reservist officer supporting the Marine 4th Dental Battalion. Her unwavering commitment to excellence ensures patients receive top-quality care in a safe and welcoming environment.
