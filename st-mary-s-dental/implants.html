Health Effects Of Missing Teeth

Most patients focus on how missing teeth affect their appearance and their confidence levels. Your emotional health is an important factor to consider when thinking about the benefits of dental implants. However, you should also know about the negative impact that missing teeth have on your oral health. Missing teeth can mean:

  • Higher risk for gum disease.
  • Loosening, shifting, or loss of remaining teeth.
  • Resorption of jawbone.
  • Loss of facial structure.
  • Suffering improper bites and temporomandibular joint issues.

The Dental Implant Process

A dental implant is comprised of a titanium post that’s surgically embedded into your jawbone. Some implant placements will require a referral to an oral surgeon; however, most implants can be placed in our office.

Implant Evaluation

At St. Mary’s Dental in Southern Maryland, Dr. McCray and Dr. Demer will evaluate if you are a candidate for dental implants. Our 3D CAT scanner will give us a much more complete view of your jaw structure than typical x-rays can provide. We will be able to determine any prep work that might be needed, such as bone grafts, as well as the optimal placement of the implant to make your implant placement successful.

Tooth Implant Placement

Once the tooth implant is placed, we evaluate your healing and uncover the implant in order to begin the process of making your tooth/teeth that will sit on top of the dental implant(s). Once fabricated, the prosthetic tooth/teeth are precisely seated onto the tooth implant(s) to restore the beauty and function of your mouth.

To further improve precision and patient comfort, all dental implants at St. Mary’s Dental are placed using advanced guided technology.

Guided Dental Implant Placement

Both Dr. Demer and Dr. Brandon use a completely guided approach for dental implant placement. This technology allows us to plan your procedure with a high level of accuracy before treatment even begins.

Using detailed 3D imaging and digital planning, we map out the ideal position of your implant based on your unique anatomy. A custom surgical guide is then used during the procedure to ensure precise placement, supporting long-term success while helping to minimize discomfort and healing time.

Whether you need to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth, guided implant placement allows our team to deliver a streamlined, predictable experience from start to finish.

If you are considering dental implants, our team can help you determine the best approach for your needs.

  • Precise, digitally planned implant placement
  • Use of a custom surgical guide for accuracy
  • Minimally invasive approach when possible
  • Improved comfort and healing
  • Predictable, long-lasting results
