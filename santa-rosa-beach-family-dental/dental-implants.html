Dental Implants in Santa Rosa Beach, FL

Dental implants replace missing teeth using posts placed within the jawbone to support custom restorations. As the surrounding bone heals, it stabilizes the implant and creates a strong foundation for chewing and speaking. Implants can replace individual teeth while helping preserve jawbone support and neighboring tooth structure.

Santa Rosa Beach Family Dental provides dental implant treatment using CBCT imaging, digital scans, and personalized planning. If you’re searching for a dental implant dentist in Santa Rosa Beach, FL, our team will evaluate your bone, gums, bite, and goals before recommending care. Bone grafting, sedation, or other preparatory services may be included when needed.

Process

We’ll discuss your medical history, missing teeth, symptoms, and treatment goals. Your dentist will examine your teeth, gums, bite, and available jawbone. CBCT imaging and digital records will guide implant position, size, and treatment planning. The area will be numbed, and sedation may be provided based on your needs. A titanium implant post will be carefully placed within the jawbone and allowed to heal. After integration, an abutment and custom restoration will complete your replacement tooth.

Next Steps

Follow all written instructions for medications, bleeding control, eating, and oral hygiene. Choose softer foods and avoid chewing directly over the implant site during early healing. Clean the surrounding teeth carefully without disturbing the surgical area as instructed. Avoid smoking, vaping, and other habits that can interfere with healing. Attend scheduled follow-up visits so we can monitor the implant and the surrounding bone. Maintain daily brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups after treatment is complete.

FAQ

  • How long does a dental implant last? A well-maintained dental implant can last for decades and may remain functional for a lifetime. Longevity depends on oral hygiene, gum health, bite forces, smoking, medical conditions, and regular dental care. The attached crown or restoration may wear sooner than the implant post and eventually require repair or replacement.
  • How long does it take for a dental implant to completely heal? Dental implants commonly need three to six months to integrate with the surrounding jawbone, although healing varies by implant location, bone quality, overall health, and whether grafting was required. The gums may feel better much sooner, but your dentist will confirm stability before placing or fully loading the final restoration.
  • How many dental implants can be done at once? Multiple dental implants can often be placed during one appointment, including several implants used to support a bridge or full-arch restoration. The appropriate number depends on your missing teeth, bone volume, health, procedure complexity, and ability to heal. Digital imaging and treatment planning help determine a safe, predictable approach.
