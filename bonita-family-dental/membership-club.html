No insurance? You Don’t Need it With Our Bonita Membership Club

When you put your smile in our hands, we take that level of trust very seriously and want to show you how much we appreciate it. That’s why we offer a convenient Bonita Membership Club for our patients.

You can save on the cost of preventive care when you join our Membership Club. For a low monthly payment, you’ll get your exams, cleanings, and other preventive services in the most affordable way possible. You’ll save money on other services too, including cosmetic and restorative treatments.

You’ll find options for adults, children, and patients vulnerable to gum disease. No matter which you choose, you could save hundreds of dollars a year on dental care!
