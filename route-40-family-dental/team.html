modern dentistry in a comfortable environment

Providing compassionate, gentle dentistry is the heart of our practice. By offering accessible insurance and financing options, we are able to lower barriers to superior dental care, which allows us to make real differences in our patients’ lives. Everything we do at Route 40 Family Dental is with you in mind. We love to have fun with our patients and hope that we will be able to form lasting friendships as we help maintain your smile. We always provide honest, straightforward advice and strive to keep you informed every step of the way.

Dr. David D. Lee, DDS

Dr. Lee is a lifelong Maryland native and grew up in Ellicott City, attending nearby Centennial High School. Dr. Lee completed and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in Baltimore. He also has advanced residency training in general dentistry from Bellevue Hospital (NY).

Dr. Lee is committed to providing exceptional care to our patients in a caring and compassionate manner. He always strives to put the patient’s needs and concerns first. In order to provide the very best care for our patients, Dr. Lee stays up to date with the latest technology and best practices by taking hundreds of hours of additional dental training each year, including dental implants , oral surgery, cosmetics , and Invisalign clear aligners.

When he is not in the dental office, Dr. Lee enjoys hiking and family bike rides with his wife and two young children, William and Madelyn. Dr. Lee is thankful and excited for the opportunity to continue the care of Dr. Navai’s patients and looks forward to meeting you and your family!
