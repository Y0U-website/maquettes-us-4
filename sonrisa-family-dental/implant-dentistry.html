Dental Implants in Chicago, IL

Traditional dental bridges require grinding down healthy teeth adjacent to the gap. Full dentures may slip or cause sore spots, making eating and speaking challenging. Dental implants eliminate these concerns while maintaining your natural facial structure.

  • They function independently without requiring alteration to healthy neighboring teeth
  • Dental implants allow you to eat all your favorite foods without dietary restrictions
  • They eliminate the embarrassment of slipping dentures during conversation or meals
  • With proper care, implants can last decades or even a lifetime

How Can You Prevent Bone Loss?

When you lose a tooth, the surrounding jawbone begins to deteriorate without the stimulation from chewing forces. This bone loss can change your facial appearance, causing a sunken look around your mouth and lips. Dental implants continue to stimulate the bone just like natural tooth roots, preserving both bone density and facial contours for years to come.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth can receive dental implants. During your consultation at our Chicago practice, we evaluate several factors to determine your candidacy:

If you have experienced bone loss due to periodontal disease or long-term tooth loss, you may need a bone graft before implant placement. This additional procedure builds up the jawbone to provide adequate support for the implant.

  • Overall health and any medical conditions affecting healing
  • Jawbone density and volume in the area needing implants
  • Oral health status, including gum disease treatment needs
  • Lifestyle habits, such as smoking, may affect healing

What Should You Expect During the Dental Implant Process?

The implant placement procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, and healing time for the implant body may take several months as your bone integrates with the titanium post. During this healing period, you may wear a temporary restoration to maintain appearance and function. Once osseointegration is complete, we attach your permanent crown, bridge, or denture.

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during recovery. We provide detailed post-operative care instructions and remain available to address any concerns. Following proper oral hygiene practices and attending regular dental visits are essential for long-term implant success.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or longer, often for a lifetime. Success depends on several factors, including your overall health, bone quality, and oral hygiene habits. Unlike natural teeth, implants cannot develop cavities, but the surrounding gum tissue still requires cleaning to prevent infection.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

What is the best method of replacing missing teeth? Are dental implants worth it?

If you’ve lost a tooth due to injury, decay, gum disease, or any other reason, we recommend dental implants to replace missing teeth. Dental implants come the closest to replicating the look, feel, and function of your natural teeth.

Are dental implants painful?

Dental implants are placed into the jawbone and mirror the same function as the root of a tooth. The procedure for dental implants is usually performed while a patient is sedated. Patients who undergo IV sedation must have an empty stomach and transportation home following the procedure. Most sedation patients will have little to no memory of the procedure occurring.

What are dental implants made of?

Generally, dental implants are made out of a biocompatible metal such as titanium. Biocompatible metals are also used for other common bone implants (such as shoulder, hip, and knee replacements). The visible portion of the implant is usually made out of porcelain and is custom-made to match your existing teeth.

Are dental implants permanent?

Dental implants are designed to fuse to the bone, which makes them become permanent fixtures. Typically speaking, the success rate is nearly 100 %. There are few cases in which the implant will not fuse as intended and must be removed. If this happens to occur, the procedure can be attempted again a few months later.

Are dental implants expensive?

Dental implants are not usually covered by dental insurance, but may be covered under a patient’s medical insurance. Our office and your insurance company can discuss coverage options with you based on your individual case and treatment plan.

How do I care for my dental implants?

It’s easy just take care of an implant as if it’s a natural tooth! This involves regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. If you have any concerns about your implant, contact us immediately.
