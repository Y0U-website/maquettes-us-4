services book online contact us Meet Our Dentists in Levittown, PA Dr. Zhafar Badalov, DMD

Dr. Zhafar Badalov is one of the University of Pennsylvania–trained dentists serving Levittown and Lower Bucks County. He earned his undergraduate degree in Biology with honors from Pennsylvania State University — completing his studies a full year ahead of schedule — before continuing to the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, an Ivy League institution recognized for clinical excellence and advanced dental training.

At Penn, Dr. Badalov built a reputation for precision and strong clinical skill, mastering complex procedures and comprehensive treatment plans across restorative, cosmetic, surgical, and aesthetic dentistry. He brings that same meticulous, patient-first approach to every visit at Pro Dental Group.

Beyond general dentistry, Dr. Badalov has expanded his expertise into modern medical aesthetics, including Apex RF by InMode — reflecting his belief that looking and feeling your best starts with a healthy, confident smile.

Dr. Khamida Badalova is one of Pro Dental Group’s Levittown dentists, with more than eight years of clinical experience. She completed her undergraduate studies at Pennsylvania State University and earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania, with a focus in general dentistry.

Dr. Badalova has built a strong reputation in cosmetic dentistry, with a refined artistic eye and a deep understanding of dental anatomy. She focuses on smile-enhancement treatments — including implants, custom crowns, and veneers — always prioritizing natural-looking, lasting results.

Her interests also extend into medical aesthetics and women’s wellness, where she applies the same commitment to safety, precision, and patient-centered care.
