The Dental Implant Process at TruCare Dentistry

Step 1: Comprehensive Consultation & 3D Imaging

Dr. Toral evaluates your oral health, reviews your medical history, and captures high-resolution 3D CT scans to assess bone volume and map out the exact placement angle for your implant.

Step 2: Gentle Implant Placement

Using local anesthesia and optional sedation dentistry for maximum comfort, the titanium post is precisely positioned in the jawbone. If additional bone support is needed, gentle bone grafting can often be performed during the same visit.

Step 3: Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next 3 to 6 months, the implant integrates securely with your jawbone. You will be fitted with a comfortable temporary restoration so you never have to go without a tooth.

Step 4: Custom Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, an abutment connector and a handcrafted porcelain crown are attached. We carefully match the shade, shape, and translucency to ensure your new tooth looks completely natural.
