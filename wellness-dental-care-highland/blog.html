As a dentist with 18 years of experience, I’ve found immense joy in my profession. Since the beginning of my career, I’ve been placing dental implants, and I’ve successfully completed over 5,000 implant cases. Recently, I had a personal experience that highlighted the importance of dental health and the impact of missing teeth.

A few days ago, my husband experienced sharp pain while eating tostadas. He described the sensation as a shooting pain radiating into his head. Upon examination, I discovered that he had broken his tooth in half through the root, leaving him with no option but to get a dental implant. I performed the procedure and placed a temporary crown while we waited for the final crown, which would be ready in two months.

During this time, my husband shared several challenges he faced with his temporary crown. First, he felt that his lips were sinking into his mouth, which made him look older than he is. Second, he noticed that air leaked out when he spoke, affecting the clarity of his speech. Third, he experienced discomfort while eating, as food would escape between his teeth and lips. Finally, he realized that his other teeth began shifting to close the gap left by the missing tooth.

Out of all the meals I prepared for him during this period, he expressed the most happiness when he finally received his dental implant and regained his complete smile. This experience reinforced my belief that a missing tooth can lead to significant physical, emotional, and mental challenges, as my husband vividly articulated.

Restoring a smile goes beyond aesthetics; it enhances overall well-being and confidence. As a dentist, I take pride in helping my patients achieve not just a functional smile but a renewed sense of self.
