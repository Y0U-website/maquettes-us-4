  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the procedure. We offer anesthesia and sedation options to ensure your comfort throughout the process. Any post-procedure pain is typically mild and can be effectively managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups are essential to maintaining the health and longevity of your implants.
