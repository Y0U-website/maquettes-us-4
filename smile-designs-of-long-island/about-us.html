Meet the Team at Smile Designs of Long Island

At Smile Designs of Long Island, we are your neighborhood dentist. Dr. Edward Sloyer and Dr. Brian J. Davis, as well as our entire team of professionals, enjoy doing what they do. We do our best to give our patients the very best in modern dentistry .

We work hard to make sure that we earn the trust and respect of patients as well as others in this profession and in the rest of the community. We are proud to have been serving patients in this neighborhood for generations and providing them with exceptional customer service and dental care!

Our friendly staff provides a welcoming touch when you walk through our doors, and as you look around, you ll see that we have stayed current with the latest in technology. High-tech dentistry means a more comfortable, convenient visit for you as well as less time in the dental chair!

The two tenets upon which our practice was built are prevention and education. We want you and your family to enjoy good dental health for life. We look forward to serving you!

Edward Sloyer, DDS

Dr. Edward Sloyer received both his undergraduate and dental degrees from New York University with a post-doctoral program at Brookdale University Hospital Medical Center. With a special emphasis on cosmetic and implant dentistry, he has maintained a quality-oriented private practice in the Hicksville-Plainview area for more than 40 years.

Dr. Sloyer is an active member of the community. He conducts elementary school programs and participates in the Give Kids a Smile Program, providing free oral screening to underserved children in the Long Island area.. He has also partnered with Catholic Charities, providing dental care to Long Island families in need.

A world traveler, Dr. Sloyer has completed a humanitarian trip to Cuba and recently visited townships in South Africa, where he promoted dental care to preschoolers.

Brian Davis, DDS, FICOI

Dr. Brian Davis received his Bachelor of Science degree from St. Lawrence University and completed his dental degree at the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine. Upon graduation, he received several awards for his accomplishments in dental esthetic and prosthodontic treatment.

He also completed a dental residency at St. Charles Hospital in Port Jefferson, New York, where he received additional training in all phases of dentistry. He is certified by Invisalign as a preferred provider.

Thanks to his additional training and experience in placing and restoring dental implants, Dr. Davis was awarded a fellowship from the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. He is currently an active member of the Suffolk County Dental Society, American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and International Congress of Oral Implantologists.
