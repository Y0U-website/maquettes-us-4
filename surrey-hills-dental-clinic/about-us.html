Meet the Team at Surrey Hills Dental

Welcome to Surrey Hills Dental, where our doors have been open to friends and families in Yukon and Piedmont for over 30 years. We develop relationships that go beyond doctor-patient because when you’re here, you’re family. We love learning about what matters to you your stories, your laughs, and, of course, your smiles. Every time you visit, you ll see a friendly, familiar face with Dr. Onley or another member of our long-term team.

Through the decades, our approach to patient care has remained the same: we listen, we understand, and we work with you to get the care you need. No pushy services or one-size-fits-all solutions here. We re proud to offer top-notch dental care that s as unique as you are, delivered by a team who works seamlessly to ensure your visits are comfortable and enjoyable.

Ready to feel good about going to the dentist? Give us a call, and let s chat about how we can help keep your family’s smiles bright and healthy. Until then, please take a moment to get to know the dedicated people serving you. We can t wait to meet you!

Dr. Brandon Onley

With six years of experience in dentistry, all of which have been at Surrey Hills Dental, Dr. Onley has cultivated a deep commitment to his patients and the exceptional team he works with daily. He dedicates himself to forming lasting relationships with his patients and is extremely grateful for the trust they place in him.

Dr. Onley s inspiration to pursue dentistry began during his orthodontic treatment in junior high. His family dentist, Dr. Walter Coffey, suggested he consider a career in dentistry, planting a seed that has grown into a fulfilling vocation. Dr. Onley is thankful for Dr. Coffey s guidance and encouragement.

Outside the office, Dr. Onley enjoys spending time with his family and fishing, which he finds to be a meditative activity. He also has a unique passion for maintaining his lawn, dedicating significant time to keeping it in top condition. A fun fact about Dr. Onley is his appreciation for 90 s country music, which you will probably hear playing throughout our office!

Dr. Onley and the Surrey Hills Dental team are committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care tailored to each patient s needs. They look forward to welcoming you and helping you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile!

Dr. Scott Wyatt

Dr. Scott Wyatt has been in the Piedmont/Surrey area for 30 years. A graduate of the University of Oklahoma, College of Dentistry, he is a fan of all things "Big Red."

For the many wonderful patients we have, most will attest that Dr. Wyatt is indeed a gentle giant, always ensuring that patient comfort is paramount. He loves providing individual care to each patient and thinking out of the box if the circumstances present unique challenges. Dr. Wyatt loves being "just a dentist" and abhors the business part!

As he tells his patients, no one will ever accuse him of being a workaholic. He loves to spend time away from the office tinkering...building a house, landscaping, figuring out electronics, and reading anything non-fiction. He has been married to his wonderful wife, Teri, for 36 years. The Wyatts and their son, Daniel, love to ride ATVs in Colorado, eat seafood on the coast, golf, and escape the summer heat in the mountains.

The family is active in their local church, and Dr. Scott and his wife have served on the worship team.
