The Dental Implant Process

Getting a dental implant is a process requiring several appointments over several months. Our dental team will provide all the details you need, but the process typically involves the following steps:

  • Initial consultation to determine if you re a good implant candidate.
  • Planning implant placement using leading-edge technology
  • Placing the implant post under local anesthesia
  • A healing period of several months takes place while your implant fully integrates
  • Placement of an abutment to connect the restoration to the implant
  • Fabricating and placing a natural-looking restoration for a seamless appearance

How Do Dental Implants Work?

An implant is a small titanium screw that Dr. Onley expertly places in your jawbone under local anesthesia. The implant acts as a replacement tooth root, and over the next several months, bone and tissue grow around it, making it as stable as a natural tooth root. The implant s stability after healing allows us to attach a crown, bridge, or denture to restore your smile’s appearance and chewing ability.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer numerous benefits over other tooth replacement options:

  • Can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth.
  • Stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.
  • Allows you to eat, speak, and smile confidently and won’t slip or shift like removable dentures.

Dental Implants Technology

3-D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT)

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is a leading-edge technology that provides a single 3-D scan of all your teeth, soft tissue, bone, and nerve pathways. This comprehensive view is crucial for complex procedures like dental implant planning, orthodontics, and evaluating jaw issues. CBCT technology is indispensable for ensuring precise diagnoses and crafting tailored treatment strategies, ultimately leading to superior patient outcomes and enhanced safety.
