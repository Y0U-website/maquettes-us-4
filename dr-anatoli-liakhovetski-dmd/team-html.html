Our Dentist

Dr. Anatoli Liakhovetski

Right after graduation from University of Pennsylvania, he moved to Florida where he has been practicing dentistry by providing all aspects of dental treatments, including but not limited to, restorative and cosmetic dentistry, endodontics and periodontics, oral surgery, implants, and pedodontics. He considers himself very fortunate to be in a profession he loves and passionately enjoys.

Doctor Anatoli Liakhovetski is an active member of Continuum Study Club and Triple Win Study Club, and a past member of South Broward Dental Society along with Florida Dental Association.

When Doctor Anatoli Liakhovetski is not in the office, he enjoys spending time with his wife and children.
