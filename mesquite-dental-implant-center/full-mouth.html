Candidacy for Full Mouth Implants

Candidates for full mouth implants often have significant tooth loss, deteriorating dental health, or are dissatisfied with their current dentures. Comprehensive assessments by Dr. Varghese ensure that patients have adequate bone structure and health for the implants, paving the way for a successful full mouth restoration.

Detailed Procedure of Full Mouth Implants

Dr. Varghese performs a meticulous evaluation, including 3D imaging, to plan the precise placement of the implants. The procedure is typically completed in stages:

  • Implant Placement: Surgical insertion of the implants into the jawbone.
  • Healing Phase: A period of healing and osseointegration, where the implants bond with the jawbone.
  • Final Restoration: Attaching a custom-made, high-quality prosthetic set of teeth to the implants, fully restoring function and aesthetics.

Aftercare and Longevity

Post-procedure care is critical for the longevity of full mouth implants. Dr. Varghese advises on proper oral hygiene practices and scheduling regular check-ups to ensure the health and durability of the implant restoration.

FAQs about Full Mouth Implants

How do I maintain my full mouth implants?

Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential to maintain the implants and overall oral health.

Can full mouth implants fail, and what are the risks?

While the success rate is high, potential risks include infection, implant loosening, and gum recession, necessitating proper care and monitoring.

What is the longevity of All on 4 and All on 6 implants?

With proper care and maintenance, both All on 4 and All on 6 implants can last many years. Regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene, and avoiding harmful habits can extend the life of your implants significantly.
