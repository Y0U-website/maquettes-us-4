Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, implants replace both the visible tooth and the underlying root structure, helping preserve jawbone health while restoring function and appearance.

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for replacing missing teeth because they closely mimic the look, feel, and performance of natural teeth.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Patients throughout Mesquite and the surrounding Metroplex choose dental implants because they offer benefits that other tooth replacement options cannot match.

Natural Appearance

Dental implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth. Most people won&#x27;t be able to tell the difference between an implant restoration and a natural tooth.

Improved Chewing Function

Because implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, they provide exceptional stability when eating. Patients can enjoy many of their favorite foods without worrying about slipping or discomfort.

Jawbone Preservation

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it begins to deteriorate. Dental implants stimulate the bone similarly to natural tooth roots, helping preserve facial structure and prevent premature aging.

Long-Term Durability

With proper care and routine dental visits, dental implants can last for decades and often a lifetime.

Enhanced Confidence

A complete smile can improve self-esteem and allow you to speak, laugh, and socialize comfortably.

The Dental Implant Process

Comprehensive Consultation

Your treatment begins with a detailed examination, digital imaging, and Cone Beam CT scan.

For first-time patients without insurance, our implant exam and CBCT package is available for $299.

Personalized Treatment Planning

Using advanced imaging technology, Dr. George develops a customized treatment plan based on your anatomy, oral health, and goals.

Implant Placement

The implant post is carefully placed into the jawbone using precise surgical techniques and guided technology.

Patients who experience dental anxiety may benefit from our IV Sedation or Oral Sedation options.

Healing and Integration

Over several months, the implant integrates with the jawbone, creating a strong foundation for the final restoration.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, restoring function and appearance.
