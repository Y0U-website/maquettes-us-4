All-on-4 is a life-changing procedure. It replaces an entire arch of failing or missing teeth using four to six strategically placed dental implants to support a fixed bridge. In many cases, patients leave with temporary teeth the same day or the next day.

But success in full-arch implant treatment isn’t just about placing four implants. It’s about planning, surgical precision, prosthetic design, and long-term maintenance.

That’s where private dentistry makes the difference.

What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is a full-arch dental implant solution that:

● Replaces all teeth in one arch ● Uses 4–6 implants to support a fixed bridge ● Often avoids extensive bone grafting ● Provides same-day temporary teeth in many cases

Patients often refer to it as “Teeth in a Day,” “Same day denture.”

But true long-term success depends on detailed 3D planning, surgical accuracy, and restorative expertise — not volume.

Do You Really Leave With Teeth the Same Day?

In many cases, yes if not within 24–48 hours.

Patients often receive a fixed temporary prosthesis the same day. These allow you to smile confidently while your implants heal.

Final teeth are delivered after healing to ensure strength and longevity.

The difference is not whether you get teeth the same day. It’s how carefully those teeth are designed and how precisely the implants are positioned underneath them.
