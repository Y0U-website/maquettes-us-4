Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontic Evaluations

At what age should my child have their first orthodontic evaluation?

The American Association of Orthodontics recommends an evaluation by age seven, or sooner if your dentist notices a potential bite or spacing issue.

Does Pearson Pediatric Dentistry provide braces?

We perform orthodontic evaluations and monitor your child’s development. If treatment such as braces is needed, we will provide a referral to a trusted orthodontist.

Why does age seven matter for orthodontic evaluations?

By age seven, the first adult molars have usually come in, allowing us to evaluate your child’s bite from front-to-back and side-to-side more accurately.

What are the benefits of early orthodontic evaluation?

Early evaluation can help make room for permanent teeth, support facial symmetry, reduce the risk of trauma from protruding teeth, and often shortens overall treatment time.

What happens if my child doesn’t need treatment yet?

If treatment isn’t needed right away, we will simply continue to monitor your child’s bite and jaw development at future exams and start treatment exactly when it’s most beneficial.

What problems can an orthodontic evaluation catch?

An evaluation can identify issues like crossbites, open bites, overcrowding, and other bite irregularities before they become more difficult and costly to correct.

How do I schedule an orthodontic evaluation for my child?

Orthodontic evaluations are typically included as part of a routine exam. You can request an appointment through our appointments page or call us at (202) 889-5200 .
