Caring For Your Dental Implant:

After your tooth implant procedure , the next step is to take care of it to prevent further complications. The main aspect to keep in mind is to always keep your implants clean, especially during the first week after the dental implant procedure. Brushing, flossing, and maintaining regular cleanings from your dentist are essential.
