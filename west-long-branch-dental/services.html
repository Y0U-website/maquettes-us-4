Our services

Dentistry for the whole family Dr. Bastos loves seeing patients of all age groups.

Periodontics and Gum Disease Gum disease is extremely common and not always easy to diagnose without the help of your dentist. If you are concerned about your gums, let us know.

Botox Dr. Bastos is trained and certified by the American Association of Facial Esthetics to provide Botulinum Toxin. Botox can be cosmetic and therapeutic improving headaches and TMJ problems.

Emergency Care All teeth are different and accidents happen. Whether your teeth are crooked, chipped, damaged or if you’ve had mouth trauma, Dr. Bastos can treat you quickly using the latest techniques and the finest materials.

Invisalign Straighten your smile with Invisalign. Improve your oral health since misaligned teeth can increase the risk of gum disease and cavities. Gain confidence to smile!

Restorative Dentistry Crowns, bridges, veneers, white fillings (bondings). We strive to protect and restore your teeth function and appearance. Looking natural is very important to us.

Teeth Whitening We offer one hour whitening in office and take home whitening kits.

Porcelain Veneers Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made ceramic shells bonded to the front surface of teeth to improve their color, shape, size, or alignment.
