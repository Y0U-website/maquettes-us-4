Meet our Team

Dr. Bastos worked for 8 years at Monmouth Medical Family Health Center where she comprehensively treated a diverse patient population using a multi-disciplinary approach. She is currently an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), New Jersey Dental Association (NJDA), and Monmouth and Ocean County Dental Society (MOCDS).

Dr. Bastos has been teaching the dental residents during their General Practice Residency (GPR) at the Monmouth Medical Center, in Long Branch, NJ since 2015. She is passionate about dentistry and loves to improve patients’ smiles.

In her spare time she likes to spend time with her husband and 3 children.

She is fluent in Spanish and Portuguese.

Geovanna - Dental Hygienist Geovanna Braga Oliveira, BSDH, RDH, was born in Brazil and moved to the U.S. at 12. A Long Branch High School graduate, her journey with us began in 2022 when she joined West Long Branch Dental at the front desk. While working with our team, she discovered her passion for dental hygiene and went on to earn her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene.

She loves helping patients improve their oral health and feel more confident along the way.

She is fluent in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

Outside the office, you’ll find her working out or running!

Liz - Dental Hygienist Lizeth Rios, RDH, is originally from Long Branch and earned her degree in Dental Hygiene from Rutgers School of Health Professions in 2018. Raised by Mexican and Puerto Rican parents, she is fluent in Spanish and enjoys connecting with patients from diverse backgrounds. With a gentle touch and friendly approach, she helps patients feel at ease while supporting lifelong oral health.

Outside of work, she loves going to concerts and spending time with her dog, Batman.

Victoria - Patient Care Coordinator Victoria was born in Goiânia, Brazil, and moved to the United States at the age of 12. She grew up in New Jersey and graduated from Long Branch High School. Being fluent in English, Portuguese, and Spanish, she is able to communicate with patients from a variety of backgrounds and help make their experience in the office as comfortable as possible.

Outside of work, Victoria enjoys spending time with her friends, watching movies, listening to music, and following Formula 1. She especially enjoys going to concerts and discovering new music. She is excited to be part of the team and looks forward to creating a welcoming and comfortable environment for our patients.

Astrid is our compassionate Patient Care Coordinator, dedicated to making every visit a seamless and positive experience. With her exceptional organizational skills and in-depth knowledge of dental insurance, she ensures that patients maximize their benefits and receive the care they deserve.

Astrid’s passion for helping others shines through as she works tirelessly to build confidence in her patients’ smiles, supporting them on their journey to optimal dental health.

Danie - Registered Dental Assistant Danie, RDA is very efficient and does everything with passion. She ensures our rooms are clean and ready, while keeping patients comfortable and at ease.

Danie enjoys seeing people’s smiles transform and gain confidence.

You would never be able to tell but she is a thrill seeker. Always willing to go Skydiving or zip-lining

Laysa was born in Brasília, Brazil, and moved to the United States at the age of 13. Since then, she has proudly called the U.S. home. Her love for connecting with others shines through both in her personal life and in her career in dentistry.
