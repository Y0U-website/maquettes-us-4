  • Single-unit dental implants look and feel like your other teeth. Designed and shaped just like your natural tooth, no other tooth replacement option provides such realistic results. No one will ever notice that you have an implant, and you will not be limited by having to avoid certain foods.
  • Dental implants are convenient because you can brush and floss them just like your other teeth. Once we surgically place them in your bone and they heal completely, they are permanent and non-removable. When cared for properly, dental implants can last a long time, even decades!

Implant-Retained Dentures and Bridges

If you are looking for more stability when it comes to your dentures, we can design implant-supported dentures to give you additional comfort and confidence. By using a few strategically placed dental implants, you can eliminate the likelihood that your dentures will come loose, which can cause sores and make eating difficult.

When you have multiple missing teeth and a traditional dental bridge is not an option, we can use implants to create a permanent dental bridge to replace your missing teeth. With a combination of dental implants and bridgework, we can replace multiple missing teeth without the need for a partial denture. Implant-supported bridges offer convenience and comfort.
