  • What are dental implants? Dental implants replace missing teeth with natural-looking restorations. Implants are titanium posts that are permanently placed in your jawbone. At Family Tree Dental Care, Dr. Hong creates custom restorations such as crowns, bridges, and implant dentures to attach to the implants and replace one or more missing teeth.
  • What is the process of dental implant restoration? An in-house oral surgeon places your dental implant after carefully planning the procedure with digital scanning, X-rays, and other technology. After osseointegration, you can visit Family Tree Dental Care for your restoration.
  • What are the advantages of dental implants? If you’re missing one or more teeth, you should consider the advantages of dental implants over other treatment options like bridges or dentures without implant support. Compared to other options, dental implants can offer: Zero dietary restrictions
  • A natural feel and appearance
  • Permanent stability
  • Durability
  • Easy maintenance
  • Comfort
