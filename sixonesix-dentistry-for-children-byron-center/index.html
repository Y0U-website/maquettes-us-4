Child-Focused Communication Partnering With Parents REQUEST APPOINTMENT Aimee C. Valleau, DDS Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Valleau believes children deserve dental visits that feel calm, respectful and easy to understand. She speaks with patients in age-appropriate ways, helping them feel included rather than overwhelmed. Her goal is to build trust early, so every child can feel more confident about dental care.

Dr. VanDeven focuses on making pediatric dentistry approachable for children and practical for parents. He understands that every child responds differently, so he takes time to explain care, answer questions and create a steady, reassuring environment from the first visit through ongoing treatment.

Dr. VanDeven earned his dental degree from the University of Michigan and completed pediatric residency training at the University of Maryland. His background also includes public health dentistry, where he provided care for children without insurance, strengthening his commitment to accessible, compassionate pediatric dental care.

Dr. Feuerstein is passionate about helping children feel safe, understood and proud of their smiles. She values gentle communication, positive reinforcement and family-centered care, especially as children grow through different stages of development, dental needs and comfort levels.

Dr. Feuerstein earned her dental degree from Indiana University and completed pediatric dentistry residency training at Riley Hospital for Children. She brings a compassionate, detail-focused approach to every appointment, helping families feel informed while supporting healthy habits that can last well beyond childhood.
