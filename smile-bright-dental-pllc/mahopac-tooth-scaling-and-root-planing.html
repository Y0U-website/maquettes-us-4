Frequently Asked Questions

The primary goal is to remove plaque, tartar, and bacteria from below the gum line to promote healthier gums. This procedure helps prevent further periodontal disease.

A standard appointment usually lasts between one to two hours, depending on the extent of cleaning needed. Complex cases may require multiple visits.

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the process, especially with local anesthesia. Any post-treatment sensitivity can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain relief.

Frequency depends on individual oral health needs; generally, it’s recommended every six months to a year. Your dentist will provide personalized advice based on your condition.

While complications are rare, some patients may experience temporary swelling or bleeding in the gums. Following aftercare instructions can help minimize these issues.
