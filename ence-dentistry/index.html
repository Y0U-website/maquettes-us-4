The practice

Dr. Mark Ence was born in Las Vegas and grew up in Santa Clara, Utah, where he attended Snow Canyon High school and met his wife, Tami. They were married after Mark served a mission in Chile. They have 3 wonderful (and crazy) boys.

Mark has enjoyed helping patients restore their smiles to full health while working inthe office with his father. Dr. Mark Ence is a member of the American Dental Assocation and Utah Dental Association.

We welcome all new patients and treat you as family in our practice. With services ranging from general dentistry to cosmetic smile makeovers, we work with children, teenagers, and adults.

Your first visit to Ence Dentistry will enable you to have a greater understanding of your own dental health. You will also get to know the Ence Dentistry Team who will be serving you. You will be given a comprehensive oral examination that includes X-rays and photographs of your teeth. The Dentist will then discuss the findings of the exam with you.
