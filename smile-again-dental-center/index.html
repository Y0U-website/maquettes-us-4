The practice

At Smile Again Dental Center, we utilize digital imaging, dental X-rays and 3D CT scan to capture the entire mouth and establish an original or baseline oral health and history for individualized risk assessment. Our dentists provide the most in-depth clinical evaluation and consultation to address all your concerns about your oral health or your smile in general.

Following your full mouth examination, you can get a dental scaling and polishing from our dentists and hygienists. Our preventive treatments prevent bacteria from turning into decay, gum disease and many other dental issues.

Our team understands that every patient has individual needs and concerns from financial arrangements to anxiety towards any potential treatments. We are here to help providing you with customized treatment recommendations and options available for your dental care.

As part of our mission to offer our patients with highest standard of dental care, our dentists use laser technologies to effectively treat for tooth decay, gum disease, bacterial infection and other oral health concerns while minimizing bleeding and damage to the surrounding healthy tissues.

We choose laser dentistry because of the distinct benefits including reduced discomfort and a smoother post-operative experience for our patients.

Here at Smile Again Dental Center, we offer dental implant treatment, the best long-term option for tooth-replacement with the highest success rate.

Our implant treatment starts with the 3D CT scan to capture the anatomy of your jaw, nerve and bone to ensure that the implants are placed in the most ideal position. Dental implants can become integrated into the bone itself and replace the tooth roots while preventing inevitably bone loss.

Properly cared for implants can function like natural teeth and last for the rest of your life. The implant crowns are designed in our in-house lab with customized shape, color and length to enhance your smile.
