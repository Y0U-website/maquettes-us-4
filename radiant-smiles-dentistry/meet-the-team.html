Meet The Team

Meet Dr. Sonam Patel and the team behind Radiant Smiles Dentistry; a locally owned, boutique-style practice, built on nearly 10 years of clinical experience and a genuine commitment to patient-first care.

About Radiant Smiles Dentistry

At Radiant Smiles Dentistry, we believe that a healthy smile is the foundation of overall well-being. Our mission is to provide high-quality, compassionate dental care in a welcoming and comfortable environment. Whether you are here for a routine checkup or a more involved treatment, our experienced team is committed to delivering personalized care tailored to your unique needs.

With modern digital imaging and diagnostic tools, including a complimentary wellness scan for every new patient, we offer comprehensive dental services including preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, restorative treatments, and emergency dentistry. Our goal is to make every visit as stress-free and comfortable as possible, helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile for life.

Why Choose Radiant Smiles Dentistry

Radiant Smiles Dentistry is a locally owned, practice built around a single commitment: doing dentistry the right way, with time, intention, and genuine care. Dr. Patel has spent nearly 10 years treating patients in the Pooler and Savannah communities, and every aspect of this practice reflects the experience she always wanted her patients to have.

Modern Technology Our office is equipped with modern digital imaging and diagnostic tools, including a complimentary wellness scan for every new patient. We use technology to show you exactly what is happening in your mouth so you can see your oral health clearly and make informed decisions about your care.

Education-First Approach Every member of our team is here to make sure you leave each visit more informed, more confident, and more in control of your oral health than when you walked in. We believe that when patients truly understand their care, they make better decisions — and that makes all the difference in the long run.

Our Comfort Menu From the moment you walk in, your comfort, time, and trust are our top priorities. From our comfort menu featuring heated blankets, eye masks, and ceiling-mounted TVs to the way Dr. Patel takes a moment to get to know you before any treatment begins, everything is designed to feel personal, not transactional.

Meet Dr. Sonam Patel, Founder of Radiant Smiles Dentistry

Dr. Sonam Patel did not set out to open just another dental practice — she set out to build the one she always wished existed. A graduate of Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry and a General Practice Residency alumna of Albert Einstein Medical Center in Philadelphia, Dr. Patel brought nearly a decade of hands-on experience in the Savannah and Pooler communities with her when she founded Radiant Smiles Dentistry. Her vision was simple but rare: a practice where patients are genuinely involved in their own care, not just talked at from across the room.

Dr. Patel is also an Invisalign Certified Provider and SureSmile clear aligner provider, offering a comprehensive range of services from preventive cleanings and restorative care to cosmetic treatments and Botox for TMJ. Every treatment plan is built around your individual goals, your oral health, and your long-term well-being.

Experience Radiant Dental Care

From routine checkups to advanced treatments, we provide care designed for your health, comfort, and confidence. Whatever your needs, our team is dedicated to making every visit gentle, positive, and stress-free.

General Dentistry General dentistry includes a wide array of preventive and wellness services that help maintain the long-term health and function of your teeth and gums for patients of all ages. Whether it’s a routine checkup or cavity treatment, we’ve got your whole family’s smile covered every step of the way.

Restorative Dentistry When a tooth is damaged, decayed, or missing, the right restorative care can mean the difference between preserving your smile and losing it. Dr. Patel approaches every restorative case with precision, honesty, and a commitment to results that look, feel, and function completely natural.

Cosmetic Dentistry A smile you feel confident in changes how you look, how you carry yourself, and how you connect with the people around you. From Invisalign and professional teeth whitening to Botox for TMJ, Dr. Patel offers personalized cosmetic care designed around your goals and delivered with nearly 10 years of clinical expertise.

Invisalign and Clear Aligner Therapy Straightening your teeth has never been more discreet or more convenient than it is with Invisalign and SureSmile clear aligner therapy at Radiant Smiles Dentistry. As a certified provider, Dr. Patel builds every treatment plan around your goals, your timeline, and your life, so you can work toward a straighter smile without interrupting it.
