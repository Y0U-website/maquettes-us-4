The practice

Everyone should feel comfortable in their own skin. Unfortunately, crooked teeth can result in insecurities and wanting to hide their smile from the world.

When a bite is off or a tooth is out of place, it can quickly lead to almost unbearable pain, trouble eating, and even digestive issues.

As adult teeth start coming in, it’s crucial they develop correctly. Improper growth can result in unnecessary wear, joint disorders, and even tooth loss.

Every patient deserves our very best, which is why we only recommend what we would recommend to our own family.

We pride ourselves on doing difficult things well. We are committed to you and will adjust to fit your needs, no matter how long it takes.

Every smile is unique, meaning every treatment plan should and will be custom to you, down to the tooth!

We begin by listening to your goals and concerns, then we create a complete diagnosis using 3D imaging to get the full picture.

We will put together a plan for appliances (braces, retainer, etc.) moving forward that is tailored to your specific needs.
