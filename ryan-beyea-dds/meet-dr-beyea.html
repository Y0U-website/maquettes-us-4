Meet Dr. Beyea

Dr. Ryan Beyea grew up in nearby Haslett, MI, returning to practice near his hometown after earning his DDS degree from the University of Michigan.

Prior to his time at U of M, he attended Michigan State University earning a degree in Physiology with a minor in Healthcare Ethics. While at Michigan State, he participated in multiple healthcare research projects while competing for the Spartans on the Cross Country and Track teams.

He continues to pursue advanced education in his areas of interest such as implant surgery, oral-systemic health, and dental technology.

Dr. Beyea and his wife, Ellen, live in Williamston, where they spend their time getting outdoors with their energetic labrador retriever, Layla.

In his free time, Dr. Beyea can be found running and working out, golfing, camping, and spending time with his family.
