Why Dental Implants Are a Great Choice

If you want a solution that mimics the natural structure and appearance of your teeth, dental implants offer an exceptional option.

  • Permanent solution: The dentist designs dental implants to last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Improved functionality: Implants restore your ability to chew and speak without discomfort.
  • Natural look and feel: Implants look and feel like real teeth, providing aesthetic benefits.
  • Preservation of jawbone health: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.

The Step-by-Step Process of Dental Implants in Okemos

The dentist follows several key steps to place your dental implants in Okemos, ensuring that your new implants are secure and function properly. Here’s an overview of the typical dental implant procedure:

Throughout the process, the dentist uses cutting-edge dental technology to ensure the best possible results. Our 3D scanner, TRIOS, provides detailed images of your teeth and gums, which helps the dentist plan your implant placement with precision. The dentist also uses intraoral cameras and digital X-rays to monitor your progress and ensure everything heals as it should.

  • Consultation and planning : During your initial consultation, Dr. Ryan Beyea or Dr. Gregory S. Brya will evaluate your oral health, including the condition of your gums and jawbone. Advanced technology like 3D scanners and digital X-rays help our staff create a customized treatment plan for your implants.
  • Sedation : We understand that some patients may feel anxious about getting dental implants. That’s why we offer various dental sedation options to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. Whether you prefer local anesthesia or a deeper level of sedation, Dr. Beyea and Dr. Brya will work with you to choose the best option for a relaxed and stress-free experience.
  • Implant placement : The dentist places a small titanium post into your jawbone, where the tooth root used to be. This post will serve as the foundation for your new tooth.
  • Healing period : After the implant placement, your body requires several months to heal. During this period, your jawbone fuses with the titanium implant in a process called osseointegration. This step is critical for ensuring that the implant remains stable.
  • Placement of the abutment : Once the implant integrates into the bone, Dr. Beyea or Dr. Brya will place an abutment on top of the implant. This small connector will hold your permanent crown or prosthetic in place.
  • Final restoration : After the abutment placement, the dentist attaches your custom-made crown to complete the process. Our staff designs the crown to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth.
