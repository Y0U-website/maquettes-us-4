Dental Impants

A dental implant is a surgical component used to replace missing teeth. It’s a modern, long-term solution that mimics the look, feel, and function of natural teeth. If you have a missing tooth that’s impacting your comfort or confidence, a dental implant could be your solution. The new replacement tooth will function as seamlessly as your regular tooth.

Dental implants have had a considerable impact on modern dentistry – offering a highly viable solution to replacing missing teeth.

Dental implants can replace:

  • A single tooth
  • Multiple teeth
  • All of your teeth (implant dentures)

Advantages of Dental implants:

  • Permanent and durable
  • Looks and feels like natural teeth
  • Prevents boneloss in the jaw
  • Allows normal eating and speaking
  • Preventing teeth from shifting to close the gap caused by a missing tooth
  • Enhancing the appearance of your smile
