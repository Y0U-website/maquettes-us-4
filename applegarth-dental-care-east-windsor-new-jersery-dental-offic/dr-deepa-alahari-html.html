Dr. Deepa Alahari (DDS)

Family and General Dentist at Applegarth Dental, East Windsor, NJ

Dr. Deepa Alahari is a compassionate highly skilled and experienced dentist dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for patients of all ages. With a focus on creating a welcoming and comfortable environment, Dr. Deepa Alahari ensures that every patient feels at ease during their visit.

After earning a Doctor of Dental (DDS) degree from University of Detroit Mercy, Dr. Deepa Alahari started and is consistently acclaimed as one of the Top 100 Dentists in the state of Texas. Dr. Deepa Alahari with her extensive 20 years of dentistry experience is proficient in complex and advanced dental treatments like Root Canals, Crowns, Veneers, pediatric dentistry, cosmetic procedures and Dental Implants. This advanced education and experience allows for a comprehensive approach to oral health, combining the latest techniques with personalized treatment plans.

Beyond the clinic, Dr. Deepa Alahari is actively involved in the community, participating in local health fairs and educational programs to promote oral hygiene awareness. In their free time, Dr. Deepa Alahari enjoys hiking, cooking, or spending time with family during her free time reflecting a well-rounded and approachable personality.

Dr. Deepa Alahari is committed to building lasting relationships with patients, emphasizing preventive care and patient education to achieve optimal oral health outcomes.
