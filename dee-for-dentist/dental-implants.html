Who Is an Ideal Candidate for Dental Implants?

Determining candidacy for dental implants involves evaluating multiple factors.

Ideal candidates typically have good general health for healing after surgery, adequate bone density or willingness to undergo bone grafting, healthy gums free from active periodontal disease, commitment to excellent oral hygiene and regular cleanings, non-smoking status or willingness to quit, and realistic expectations about the treatment process.

  • Most patients can become suitable candidates with proper preparation. Even those with medical conditions or bone loss often qualify after preliminary treatments. Our team has successfully treated thousands of complex cases.
