Enhanced diagnostic capability

CariVU is a diagnostic aid for the detection of open or incipient carious lesions above the gingiva and for monitoring the progress of such lesions. Proximal dentin lesions represented in CariVu images correlate to the actual condition in the tooth with 99 % accuracy. Indications for the use for CariVu are detection of: smooth surface caries, occlusal caries, interproximal caries, cracks and fractured teeth.

Digital radiology

Digital radiography is a form of X-ray imaging, where digital X-ray sensors are used instead of traditional photographic film. Advantages include time efficiency through bypassing chemical processing and the ability to digitally transfer and enhance images. Also, less radiation can be used to produce an image compared to conventional radiography.
