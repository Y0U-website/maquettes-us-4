Planning and Preparation

Before embarking on our dental implant journey, we take great care to conduct a detailed consultation to gain a full understanding of your desires, needs, and aesthetic aspirations. Through this process we assess oral health using advanced technology like cone beam CT scanners; additionally we may perform extractions as necessary to address failing teeth.

Individuals who have suffered tooth loss over time may notice a gradual reduction in jaw bone volume, as this provides essential support for dental implant placement. To address bone loss, we offer bone graft procedures: by adding new bone material directly into affected areas and fostering healing and fusion with existing bones, bone graft procedures help restore proper levels of support needed to support new dental implants successfully.

Surgery And Restoration

Dr. Pokrovskaya can use Halcion during dental implant surgeries for those needing multiple implants or with severe dental anxiety to help ensure a comfortable experience. Following Halcion, biocompatible posts will be placed into predetermined areas in their jaw to act as replacements for missing teeth.

Implant posts serve as artificial tooth roots, stimulating jawbone growth while simultaneously anchoring new teeth securely into place. We may even provide temporary crown attachment on the same day to make sure you never go without one!

After giving your implant time to heal for several months, we complete its treatment with a permanent, natural-looking crown that restores natural mouth function and provides you with a healthy smile again.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the dental implant procedure take?

Most dental implants can be placed in minutes, but they need to solidify in the bone before putting a tooth on it. This usually require 3-6 months of healing between the initial installation and the final permanent crown.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

Dental implants cost thousands of dollars for each implant.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

Dental implants should never be painful. If the area is fully numbed, and the dentist is trained and skilled in dental implants, the procedure will not be painful.

Are there financing options available for dental implants?

Can dental implants be placed on the same day as tooth extraction?

Yes, in many cases an implant can be placed at the time of extraction.

In most cases, the patient is back to normal within one day of the surgery.
