What is Orthodontics?

In the world of dental care procedures, orthodontics hold a unique place. It’s a specialized field that primarily focuses on correcting crooked teeth and misaligned jaws to achieve an enhanced smile.

Orthodontic treatment often involves using devices such as traditional braces, which comprise metal brackets tied together by wires and bands, to alter tooth alignment gradually.

It goes beyond merely improving the aesthetic appeal of your smile. The right orthodontic solutions can boost oral health by making it easier for you to clean your teeth effectively.

Moreover, these treatments play a vital role in addressing potential speech or biting issues that arise due to poorly aligned teeth or jaw problems. Whether it’s with traditional braces or convenient treatment methods like custom-made aligners and detachable retainers, orthodontics paves the way for a bright and confident smile.

Braces

Braces remain a time-tested and effective method for straightening crooked teeth. Traditional braces use metal brackets and wires to apply gentle, constant pressure on the teeth over time, improving tooth alignment.

At Auburn Dental Center, our dedicated orthodontists tailor each treatment plan with braces to suit your unique needs and desired results. From traditional metal braces to more modern options such as clear or ceramic brackets, we offer a range of orthodontic solutions geared towards giving you that confident smile you’ve always wanted.

Enjoy enhanced smile aesthetics and improved oral health with our convenient treatment methods!

Invisalign

Invisalign presents an innovative approach to correct misaligned teeth without the need for metal brackets or wires. As one of our dental care services, we offer these tailor-made aligners that are both clear and removable.

They fit perfectly over your teeth, offering a comfortable orthodontic solution and the added benefit of being able to take them out when eating or brushing. Unlike conventional braces, Invisalign offers a discreet treatment plan that won’t disrupt your normal daily activities.

Through this convenient method of treatment, we aim to deliver enhanced smile makeovers while always prioritizing your oral health at each stage of the process.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a popular alternative to traditional braces for orthodontic treatment. At Auburn Dental Center, we offer clear aligners as one of our convenient treatment methods.

These custom-made aligners are designed to gradually shift your teeth into the desired position, providing an effective solution for tooth alignment. With clear aligners, you can enjoy a bright and confident smile without the use of metal brackets or wires.

The aligners are removable, allowing you to easily maintain proper oral hygiene during your treatment period. Our dedicated orthodontist will work closely with you to create a personalized treatment plan using clear aligners that suit your specific needs and goals.

Retainers

Retainers are an essential part of orthodontic treatment at Auburn Dental Center. These custom-made, detachable aligners play a crucial role in maintaining your beautiful smile after braces or clear aligner treatment.

Our dedicated orthodontist will provide you with removable retainers that help ensure the alignment of your teeth remains stable over time. By wearing these retainers as instructed, you can enjoy a confident and enhanced smile for years to come.

Choose our convenient treatment methods and trust us to take care of your oral health both during and after your orthodontic journey.

Jaw Surgery

Jaw surgery, also known as orthognathic surgery, is a procedure that aims to correct issues with the alignment and structure of the jaw. It can be done to address problems like overbite, underbite, or TMJ disorders.

This type of surgery is often recommended when braces or other orthodontic treatments alone cannot fully resolve these issues.

During jaw surgery, the bones in the upper and lower jaws are repositioned to improve facial balance and bite function. The procedure is usually performed by an experienced oral surgeon who works closely with our dedicated orthodontist at Auburn Dental Center.

By combining the expertise of both professionals, we ensure you receive comprehensive care and achieve optimal results.
