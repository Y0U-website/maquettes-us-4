What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth or dental bridges. They serve as a strong foundation for fixed or removable dentures and help maintain proper oral health.

Purpose and benefits

Dental implants serve a two-fold function – they fill in for lost teeth and help maintain the shape of your face. Their many advantages include bringing back a confident smile, improving oral health, and offering an enduring solution for tooth loss.

In contrast to dentures or bridges, dental implants resemble natural tooth roots. They support jawbone health and so help prevent bone attrition. Moreover, when cared for with appropriate oral hygiene habits and regular dentist visits, they demonstrate impressive durability and long-term use.

Dental implants not only renew your radiant smile but also lay the groundwork for complete oral wellness!

Implant surgery

Our expert team guides you through each step of the dental implant surgery to ensure a smooth and stress-free experience. The procedure involves placing an implant post into your jawbone, which acts as a sturdy foundation for your new tooth.
