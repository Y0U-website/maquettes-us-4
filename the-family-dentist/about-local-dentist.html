Meet the Dentist | Fairless Hills, PA

General Dentistry & Oral Surgery in Fairless Hills, Bucks County, PA Dr. Mathur B.D.S., L.D.S., D.M.D., Ph.D (Oral Surgery) Dr. Mathur has been a dentist for over 30 years. He has a D.M.D. from the University of Pennsylvania, and a Ph.D. in oral cancer from the Royal Dental School, Malmo, Sweden. He is a member of the American Dental Association, and a member of the Pennsylvania Dental Association. He has several scientific publications in oral cancer and has lectured extensively in the field of dentistry.

Dr. Mathur had oral surgery training in Sweden and also practiced dentistry there. Dr Mathur performs difficult surgical extractions, impactions, minor surgical procedures, implants, and prosthetic replacements.

We also have an associate General Dentist and a Periodontist on staff.
