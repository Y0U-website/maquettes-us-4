The practice

In these days of HMOs, retail dental clinics and high-volume practices seem like assembly lines. We think you will welcome our relaxed, people-oriented approach. We have grown successful because we still believe in personal care and one-on-one communication.

During your first visit, you will meet with us to discuss your needs with the doctor. We will thoroughly examine your soft tissues, teeth, and gums and decide if any X-rays are necessary. You may also discuss cost, insurance, and treatment.

We provide various emergency dental procedures, including oral surgery, implant extractions, and periodontal procedures. We also offer safe IV sedation to ensure your comfort and relaxation.

We specialize in bondings, porcelain, composite tooth veneerings, repairing chips, and more! Our Esthetic department can produce lifelike restorations in ceramics, ceromers, and composites.

The Family Dentist offers dental implants to replace missing teeth with durable, natural-looking solutions. Our team ensures a comfortable, precise procedure to restore function and improve your smile’s appearance.

We accept Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, and debit cards for your convenience. If you have insurance, you will pay your deductible and co-pays (if any) on the same day as treatment.

We are a participating provider for many major insurance companies. (Sorry, no HMOs, no DMOs.)
