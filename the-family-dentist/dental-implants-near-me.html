Understanding Dental Implants

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth, offering a natural look and feel. The implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone, which acts as an artificial root. Once the implant heals, a crown is attached to restore the appearance and function of the missing tooth. This durable option allows you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer several key benefits, making them one of the best solutions for missing teeth:

With proper care, implants are designed to last many years, often a lifetime.

Implants look and function like natural teeth, blending seamlessly with your smile.

Implants help preserve jawbone structure and prevent bone loss, which often occurs after tooth loss.

Dental implants can be brushed and flossed like natural teeth without adhesives or unique cleaning products.
