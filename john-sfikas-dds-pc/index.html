Bleaching is a state-of-the-art method for whitening your teeth. We take a mold of your mouth and teeth and create custom trays to fit your teeth. You can then take home syringes of whitening gel, place the gel in the trays, and place the trays in your mouth. At least 7 to 10 applications are usually needed

Porcelain veneers are thin, beautiful, and durable porcelain covers bonded to the front of our incisors and canines to enchance the shape and color of the teeth with minimal preperation!

Cosmetic bonding is a more affordable alternative to porcelain veneers and can be done with little or no tooth preperation. Cosmetic bonding may not last as long as a procelain veneer restoration, but offers a quicker and more affordable option to enhancing ones smile!
