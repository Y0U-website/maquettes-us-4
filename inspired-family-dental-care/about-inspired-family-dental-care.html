Meet Samantha Registered Dental Hygienist

Samantha has been a dedicated Registered Dental Hygienist for over 12 years, bringing a gentle touch and a genuine passion for patient care to every appointment. Her favorite part of the job is building meaningful connections with patients and helping them feel at ease while improving their oral health.

Originally from Richmond, Michigan, Samantha is a proud mom of one and shares her home with two beloved cats. In her free time, she enjoys traveling and exploring new places. Her kind nature and experience make her a valued member of the Inspired Family Dental Care team.
