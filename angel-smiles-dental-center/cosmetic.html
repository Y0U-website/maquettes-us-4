Cosmetic Dentistry

We offer a few different options to teeth whitening.

ZOOM Whitening Zoom Teeth Whitening is one of the quickest, safest, and most effective ways to improve your smile. This is offered in the office and the process takes approximately 1 ½ hours. This involves isolating your gum tissues in order to protect them from the whitening process. A whitening solution is applied and a laser light activates the process. After a maximum of 3 sessions of 20 minutes you will see desired results immediately. You will be sent home with written instructions on how to care for your whitening experience.

Take Home Teeth Whitening We offer two types of at home whitening to match your lifestyle

Custom Trays -We can scan your mouth with our iTero scanner and create custom whitening trays for you to take home. You will receive syringes of whitening gel to use in the trays to whiten your teeth. You can purchase refill syringes and the tray is reusable. Opalescence Go - Prefilled whitening trays ready to use right out of the box. The tray conforms and molds slightly to fit your smile. The tray is one time use and the box comes with a 10 day supply of disposable trays.

It is important to note that fillings, crowns, and veneers can’t be whitened during any whitening process, but they can be replaced to match the new shade of your teeth.
