What’s New Silver diamine fluoride (SDF) is an emerging nonsurgical treatment option for early caries management. This topical antimicrobial and remineralizing agent is relatively inexpensive and simple to apply, providing an important addition to the caries management care plan.

While SDF is safe and effective, it is not a magical cure-all for caries. When the application is placed at the correct site and at the right time, it can be beneficial.

Dr. Turner will let you know if your child would benefit with at treatment plan including SDF.
