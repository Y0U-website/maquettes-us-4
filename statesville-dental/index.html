We offer professional teeth whitening treatment in our office that uses a powerful whitening gel applied directly to the surface of your teeth. This gel can dramatically whiten your teeth and reduce stains, discoloration and yellowing in your smile. Teeth whitening is often available in both in-office and take-home treatments.
