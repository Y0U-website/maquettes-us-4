Dr. Zachary Joel Harrison grew up in the small town of Belhaven, North Carolina. He graduated Magna Cum Laude from East Carolina University where he triple Majored and received the prestigious Robert H. Wright award for Exceptional Academic and leadership performance. He attended UNC School of Dentistry at Chapel Hill, earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery with honors in 2009.

In 2010 Dr. Harrison joined Dr. David Marshburn and later bought the practice in 2013. He, his son Eli and their three dogs Mance, Chica and Peanut reside in Stokes. In his free time you will find him chasing behind his son, reading or playing guitar.

Dr. Harrison is dedicated to serving the dental needs of the Martin County community along with surrounding areas. He uses a conservative, patient-centered, evidence-based approach.
