What should I consider when looking for a new dentist?

Convenient location to work or home, referrals from family and friends and the dental office reviews, then meet the dentist and staff to ask questions about dental procedures, fees and insurance taken.

Do some general dentists offer dental crown procedures?

Some dental practices offer dental crowns while some refer patients to a prosthodontist to make and fit your crown.
