THE benefits OF DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental implants offer advantages that traditional bridges and dentures simply cannot match. At South Bay Dental Loft in Torrance, CA, Dr. Gloria Luong provides advanced implant dentistry designed for long-term function, health, and aesthetics.

Look and Feel Completely Natural

Each dental implant is custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your smile. The crown is carefully shaped and color-matched to your surrounding teeth, so it looks natural and feels secure. Most patients quickly forget which tooth is the implant because it functions just like a real one.

Preserve Your Jawbone and Facial Structure

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone underneath can begin to shrink. Dental implants act like natural tooth roots, stimulating the bone and helping prevent bone loss. This support helps maintain your facial structure and long-term oral health.

A Long-Term, Reliable Solution

THE DENTAL IMPLANT PROCESS

Getting dental implants is a step-by-step process designed for long-term success. While treatment can take several months from start to finish, the result is a strong, natural-looking tooth replacement that can last for decades. Here is what to expect at South Bay Dental Loft.

Step 1: Consultation and Digital Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive implant consultation with Dr. Gloria Luong in Torrance. We take digital X-rays and 3D cone beam imaging to evaluate your jawbone, gum health, and overall oral condition. Using advanced treatment planning software, Dr. Luong maps out the precise placement of your dental implant to ensure optimal stability, function, and aesthetics.

Step 2: Bone Grafting (If Necessary)

If there is not enough healthy bone to support the implant, a bone graft may be recommended. This procedure rebuilds and strengthens the area to create a solid foundation. Healing typically takes a few months, but it significantly improves the long-term success of your dental implant.

Step 3: Implant Placement

Once the area is ready, the titanium implant post is carefully placed into the jawbone. The procedure is performed comfortably in our Torrance dental office. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return to normal activities within a day.

Step 4: Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next three to six months, the implant naturally fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a stable, root-like foundation. During this time, a temporary restoration may be provided so you can maintain a complete smile.

Step 5: Abutment Placement

After the implant has fully integrated, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached. This sits above the gumline and serves as the anchor for your final crown.

Step 6: Custom Crown Placement

Impressions are taken to create a custom dental crown designed to match your natural teeth in shape and color. Once placed, your new implant restoration looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth - restoring both confidence and comfort.
