THE benefits oF ALL-ON-X IMPLANTS

All-on-X dental implants offer advantages that traditional dentures and individual implants simply cannot match. At South Bay Dental Loft in Torrance, CA, Dr. Gloria Luong provides full-arch implant solutions designed for long-term strength, stability, and natural aesthetics.

Permanent, Fixed Solution

All-on-X implants replace an entire arch of teeth with a secure, non-removable restoration. Unlike traditional dentures, they do not shift, slip, or need to be removed overnight. You get a stable, confident smile that feels natural and functions like real teeth.

Fewer Implants, Full-Arch Support

This advanced technique uses just 4 to 6 strategically placed dental implants to support a complete upper or lower arch. By maximizing bone support and minimizing the number of implants needed, All-on-X offers a streamlined and efficient approach to full-mouth restoration.

Preserves Jawbone and Facial Structure

Unlike removable dentures that rest on the gums and allow bone loss to continue, All-on-X implants stimulate the jawbone like natural tooth roots. This helps prevent bone deterioration and maintains facial structure over time.

THE ALL-ON-X DENTAL IMPLANTS PROCESS

The All-on-X dental implant process is carefully planned to ensure long-term stability, function, and aesthetics. While treatment spans several months, each phase is designed to deliver a secure, natural-looking full-arch restoration.

Step 1: Consultation and Digital Planning

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation at South Bay Dental Loft. Dr. Gloria Luong evaluates your teeth, gums, and jawbone using digital X-rays and 3D cone beam imaging. Together, you&#x27;ll discuss your goals, and a personalized treatment plan will be created. If extractions or bone grafting are needed, they will be included in your plan.

Step 2: Implant Placement Surgery

During the surgical appointment, any remaining failing teeth in the arch are removed, and 4 to 6 dental implants are strategically placed into the jawbone. These implants are positioned at precise angles to maximize stability and bone support. The procedure is performed comfortably in our Torrance dental office.

Step 3: Temporary Teeth Placement

In most cases, a temporary set of fixed teeth is attached to the implants the same day. This allows you to leave with a complete smile while your implants integrate with the bone. You&#x27;ll be able to eat soft foods and smile confidently during the healing phase.

Step 4: Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next 3 to 6 months, the implants fuse naturally with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong, permanent foundation for your final restoration. Follow-up visits ensure proper healing and progress.

Step 5: Final Full-Arch Restoration

Once healing is complete, your permanent prosthesis is custom-designed for strength, comfort, and aesthetics. Crafted from durable, high-quality materials, your final set of teeth is securely attached to the implants. Dr. Gloria Luong carefully checks the fit, bite, and appearance to ensure optimal function and a natural smile.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

All-on-X is a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses four or more strategically placed dental implants to support a fixed, non-removable set of teeth. It restores function, appearance, and stability for patients missing most or all teeth.

Unlike removable dentures, All-on-X implants are fixed in place and anchored to the jawbone. They provide greater stability, improved chewing power, and help prevent bone loss while offering a more natural feel and appearance.

In many cases, patients receive temporary teeth on the same day as implant placement. This allows you to leave the office with a functional smile while your implants heal and integrate before the final restoration is placed.

The surgical placement is typically completed in one day. Full healing and final restoration placement usually take three to six months, allowing the implants to properly fuse with the jawbone for long-term stability.

The cost of All-on-X in Torrance, CA depends on factors such as bone condition, number of implants, and materials used. While it is a significant investment, it offers long-term durability compared to removable dentures. Our team at South Bay Dental Loft will review pricing with you during your consultation.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and may include sedation for added comfort. Most patients report manageable soreness after surgery, which typically improves within a few days.

Candidates typically include patients missing most or all teeth, those with failing dentition, or individuals struggling with loose dentures. A comprehensive evaluation and 3D imaging determine eligibility and whether bone grafting is needed.

With proper care and regular dental visits, the implants can last decades. The final prosthetic may need maintenance or replacement over time, but the implant foundation is designed for long-term stability.

All-on-X restores full chewing ability, improves speech, enhances facial structure, and eliminates the need for removable dentures. It provides a secure, permanent solution that looks and feels like natural teeth.
