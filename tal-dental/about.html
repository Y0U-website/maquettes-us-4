About Tal Dental

0 Who we are At Tal Dental , we believe that a healthy smile starts with compassionate care and a personal touch. Our practice has proudly served the Alameda community for over 30 years, offering exceptional dental care for patients of all ages — from children getting their first checkup to seniors maintaining lifelong oral health.

Led by Dr. Sean Tal and his father, Dr. Robert “Sonny” Tal , our family-owned practice combines years of trusted experience with the latest advancements in modern dentistry. Whether you’ve been with us for years or you’re visiting for the first time, you’ll always be treated like part of our family.

We take time to get to know every patient — their goals, their comfort level, and their unique dental needs. Our warm, friendly team is dedicated to creating a relaxing environment where you can feel confident about your care.

Our philosophy is simple: honesty, comfort, and quality care for every smile that walks through our door.
