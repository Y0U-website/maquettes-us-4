Signs You May Need Restorative Dentistry You may benefit from restorative treatment if you have:

Early treatment can prevent more complex and costly procedures later.

  • Dental Crowns Custom dental crowns protect and strengthen damaged teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics.
  • Dental Bridges Bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent teeth, restoring your bite and smile.
  • Dental implant crowns Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth—offering a permanent, stable, and natural-feeling solution. At Fox Point Dental Studio, we will ensure your implant is crowned once it’s stable in your jaw.
  • Tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Cavities or visible decay
  • Cracked, chipped, or broken teeth
  • Missing teeth
  • Difficulty chewing
  • Old or failing dental work
