The Dental Implant Process Step 1: Consultation & Planning We evaluate your oral health and take digital images to determine if implants are right for you.

  • Natural appearance and feel
  • Restores full chewing function
  • Prevents bone loss in the jaw
  • Maintains facial structure
  • Does not affect neighboring teeth

Step 2: Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed into the jawbone with precision.

Step 3: Healing & Integration

The implant fuses with the bone (osseointegration), creating a strong foundation.

Step 4: Final Restoration

A custom crown is placed to complete your smile.
