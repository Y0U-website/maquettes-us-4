Now is the fun part! We will take our time getting to know what your goals and vision are for your dental health, ensuring that we personalize our recommendations to your specific needs. There are a lot of people who are anxious about going to the dentist. We completely understand and it’s at this initial visit that we will discuss all of the ways that we successfully manage dental anxiety, including oral sedation and laughing gas if it is something you feel you will need. These can even be made available for your first visit.

Dr. Osundeko loves new patients because each of you has a story to tell and it is an honor to be a part of your journey!
