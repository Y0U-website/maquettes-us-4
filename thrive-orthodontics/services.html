Braces

Traditional braces have been trusted for generations—and for good reason. They’re a reliable, effective way to achieve a beautifully aligned smile. At our office, we offer more than just the basics! Choose from classic silver, sleek champagne gold, clear ceramic braces, or even fun-shaped brackets like hearts, stars, or Mickey Mouse to make your treatment as unique as you are.

Straightening your teeth can be both effective and fun!

Clear Aligners (invisalign)

Clear aligners are a nearly invisible, removable option to straighten your teeth without brackets or wires. Ideal for teens and adults who want a more discreet treatment, aligners fit comfortably and can be taken out for eating, brushing, and special occasions. They’re custom-made to gently shift your teeth into place with precision and ease.

retainers/ appliances

After orthodontic treatment, retainers keep your teeth in their new, aligned positions. Whether you choose a removable or fixed retainer, wearing it as directed is key to maintaining your smile for the long run. It’s the final, essential step in keeping your results lasting and stable.
