DENTAL EMERGENCIES

If you are experiencing a dental emergency, it is important to seek treatment as soon as possible. Some common dental emergencies include: toothache, broken tooth, and lost crown. If you are experiencing any of these emergencies, do not wait to seek treatment. Delaying treatment can lead to further damage and more pain. Call us as soon as possible, and we’ll try our best to accommodate a same-day appointment.

FAMILY DENTISTRY

Family dentistry is a branch of dentistry that focuses on the oral health of the entire family. This type of dentistry typically offers a variety of services, from preventive care to restorative procedures. Family dentists typically have a lot of experience treating patients of all ages, which makes them a good choice for those who need routine dental care.

RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

Restorative dentistry is a branch of dentistry that deals with the reconstruction of teeth that have been damaged by cavities, tooth decay, or trauma. It can involve the use of dental implants, crowns, bridges, and veneers to restore the appearance and function of the teeth. Restorative dentistry is often used to improve the patient’s smile and oral health.

COSMETIC DENTISTRY

Cosmetic dentistry covers a range of services designed to improve the way you feel about your smile. Services include teeth whitening, bonding, veneers, tooth-colored fillings, Invisalign orthodontics, and more. We are able to give you a digital image of the smile you have always wanted. With payment plans for any budget, the smile you dreamed of is truly achievable.

SLEEP APNEA

Sleep apnea, a condition in which breathing stops for a period of time during sleeping, is increasingly being treated by dentists. We can evaluate your case and, if appropriate, create a dental device to keep your jaw and soft tissues in a better position to stop apneic episodes.
