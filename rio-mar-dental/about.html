Who We Are

Rio Mar Dental was our first business collaboration as a husband and wife dentist team. This incredible journey started from scratch in a remodeled 1920s Fullerton home turned private practice in July of 2014. Our doors opened for business in January 2015 with the goal of creating a practice that could truly serve and support their community while fulfilling a family life.

The vision was to expand on a unique practice where spending quality time with patients and fostering trust through honest work would result in a new dental experience centered around genuine care and personalized attention.

Our dedication and commitment to providing exceptional dental care resulted in a steadily growing patient base where we outgrew our "little blue house" on Euclid St. and expanded into a new location. This has allowed us to continue our mission of serving even more individuals and continue delivering top-notch dental care in our local Orange County community.
