Get to know our expert orthodontists & staff in Merrick.

Dr. Paladino, DDS

Orthodontist, Star Smiles (Merrick, NY)

Approach Dr. Paladino takes pleasure not just in delivering stellar orthodontic treatment but also in actively engaging with each patient, forging meaningful connections and nurturing relationships. He considers these interactions and the established bonds to be extremely fulfilling.

Credentials Armed with a Bachelor of Science degree, a Doctorate of Dental Surgery, and an orthodontic certificate, Dr. Paladino understands the importance of staying ahead of cutting-edge developments in oral healthcare and orthodontics.

Personal A native of Massapequa, Dr. Paladino and his wife have two sons, CJ and Bryce. They love to give back to the community and are very involved with Team in Training, which raises money for leukemia and lymphoma research. He also walks annually with MADD.

Dr. Fernandez, DDS

Orthodontist, Star Smiles (Merrick, NY)

Credentials Dr. Fernandez received her DDS from Columbia University. Following this, she completed a residency at the Columbia University College of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Fernandez is board-certified with the American Board of Orthodontics and is committed to continuously learning the latest developments in orthodontics so she can continue delivering high quality patient care and crafting healthy smiles.
