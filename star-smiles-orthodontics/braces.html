Braces

Metal braces are still the most common orthodontic treatment and use a combination of stainless steel brackets, arch wires, and elastic ties or bands to gently move teeth as prescribed by your orthodontist.

Metal Braces

The most common type of braces? The traditional metal ones we all know! These braces consist of brackets and wires that are attached to the front of your teeth. Metal braces have evolved over time and are more comfortable, smaller, and more customizable than ever before. Metal braces are ideal for children, teens and adults who want a beautiful, healthy smile in the shortest time possible. Throughout your braces treatment, you’ll visit us to check-in every 6 to 8 weeks (depending on your personalized treatment plan) for us to review your progress and adjust your braces if necessary!

Questions about metal braces? Give us a call!

Clear Ceramic Braces

Crafted from transparent or tooth-colored materials, clear braces seamlessly merge with the natural shade of teeth, rendering them discreet and inconspicuous. This makes them an outstanding choice for individuals who prefer a more subtle method to address misalignments.

Ceramic clear braces work similarly to traditional metal braces, except ceramic braces have clear brackets and wires. These features help the braces blend in with the natural color of the teeth for a less noticeable appearance.

Braces for Kids

Our doctors have years of experience creating personalized orthodontic treatment plans for young children in Merrick, Long Island.

The AAO (American Association of Orthodontists) recommends that children receive their first orthodontic evaluation at age 7. While treatment might not begin immediately, an early assessment can give you the peace of mind that your child’s smile is developing well!

Orthodontic treatment isn’t just about ‘straightening teeth’, it’s about establishing a healthy foundation for a beautiful set of permanent teeth.

Braces for Adults

It’s never too late to get braces. As a confident smile can significantly improve your quality of life – any age is the perfect age to begin orthodontic treatment.

There’s also more to a smile than just aesthetics and perfect alignment. Adult orthodontics can improve not only your smile and confidence, but it can also help with several health issues.
