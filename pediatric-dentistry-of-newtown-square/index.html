The practice

At Pediatric Dentistry of Newtown Square, we love partnering with families to help little smiles grow up healthy and bright. Our compassionate, kid-friendly team is here to make dental care easy, educational, and fun! With our experience, skills, and a whole lot of heart, we’re here to be a dental home, where your little one can grow!

Welcoming families from: • Broomall • Havertown • Devon • Wayne • Radnor • Springfield • Media • Drexel Hill • Upper Darby • Lansdowne • Malvern • Berwyn • Ardmore • Bryn Mawr • And beyond

We know that going to the dentist can be intimidating for little ones. That’s why we take time to build trust, explain things in kid-friendly ways, and treat every child with patience and kindness.

Your child’s smile is in great hands with our board-certified pediatric dentist, specially trained in caring for infants, children, and teens—including those with special healthcare needs.

Our bright and cheerful office is designed with kids in mind, from our fun waiting area to our gentle techniques and advanced technology that help make every visit smooth and stress-free.

We focus on gentle, conservative care that preserves as much of your child’s natural tooth structure as possible. Using the latest techniques and materials, we provide effective treatment with less discomfort and faster recovery—keeping little smiles healthier and happier.

We welcome children of all abilities and tailor care to meet each child’s unique needs. Our team has extensive training and experience working with patients who have special healthcare needs, creating a safe, supportive, and positive dental experience for every child.

Dr. Joanna Mangar is a board certified pediatric dentist and proud diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry
