Your Child’s First Visit

We’re so excited to be part of your child’s journey to a bright, healthy smile!

At your child’s first visit, you’re invited to stay right by their side—we know it helps them feel safe, gives you a chance to ask questions, and lets you see how we make dental visits fun and positive. We’ll spend time getting to know you both, go over health and dental history, and chat about anything that could impact your child’s smile, and how to keep things on track.

If your little one is small, you might even hold them in your lap during the exam—it’s cozy for them and gives you a front-row seat to everything we’re seeing. We’ll also cover things like how teeth grow in, brushing tips, healthy snacks, thumb-sucking, and how to avoid dental injuries.

Our biggest goal? To make sure you feel confident and supported every step of the way—because when we team up, your child’s smile shines even brighter!

When should I schedule my child’s first visit?

The recommendation by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry is for children to have their first dental visit within 6 months of their first tooth coming in, or by their first birthday!

How do I prepare for their visit?

A great dental visit starts at home! Talk to your child about what to expect and let them know our team will explain everything and answer their questions along the way. Skip words like “needle” or “drill”—we use kid-friendly language to keep things positive and stress-free!
