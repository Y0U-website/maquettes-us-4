Meet Dr. Jo!

Why did you want to be a pediatric dentist?

I chose to become a pediatric dentist because of the unrivaled opportunity to shape children’s attitudes toward oral health from an early age. Helping little ones build positive habits in a fun and nurturing environment can produce a lifelong impact- and that is what it’s all about.

What are some of your favorite aspects of working with children?

One of my favorite parts of caring for children is the honesty, purity, and unexpected humor that comes with their unfiltered conversations! I am also amazed everyday by children’s resilience. It is incredibly rewarding to meet each child where they are at and watch them grow more comfortable and confident with each visit.

What is something that you’d like families to know?

I am deeply grateful for the chance to be part of your child’s healthcare journey. It’s a joy to partner with you in nurturing their bright, beautiful smiles and overall well being. Thank you for trusting me with such a special role in your family’s life.

When you’re not busy creating smiles, what’s your favorite thing to do?

I cherish spending time with my husband and daughter. We’re always out exploring new parks and trying fun restaurants! If you have any favorite spots, we’d love to hear your recommendations!
