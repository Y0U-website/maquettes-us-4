Unicorn Dental Group provides comprehensive dental care for the whole family. From dental hygiene cleanings and professional exams to cosmetic dental services and restorative treatments, we have all the tools to help you maintain optimal oral health and a beautiful smile.

Born in Varanasi, India, Dr. Rupesh Pandey graduated with a degree in dentistry from MAHE University. In 2006, he attended the University of Alabama at Birmingham to earn his master’s degree in epidemiology. After graduation, Dr. Pandey worked for a CDC program collecting data for numerous research studies.

Returning to his clinical roots, Dr. Pandey went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree through the New York University College of Dentistry Advanced Standing Program. In 2015, he joined the United States Army and is a proud member of the United States Army Reserve 455 Dental Company in Fort Devens, MA.
