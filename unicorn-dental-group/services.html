What dental services does Unicorn Dental Group offer?

We offer a full range of services including cleaning and prevention, family and children’s dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, tooth replacement, extractions, oral appliances, orthodontics, conscious sedation, and advanced dental technology.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist for these services?

No, most of our services are provided in-house by our own team, so you typically don’t need an outside referral.

Can I combine multiple services in one visit?

In many cases, yes. Our team will work with you to plan appointments efficiently based on your treatment needs.

Which services are best for children?

Our children’s dentistry services, including infant oral exams, fluoride treatments, and sealants, are specifically designed for young patients.

How do I know which service is right for me?

Schedule a consultation with our team, and we’ll evaluate your oral health and recommend the services that best fit your goals.
