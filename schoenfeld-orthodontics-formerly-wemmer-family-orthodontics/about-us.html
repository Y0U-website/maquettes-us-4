About Us

ABOUT US Our office is the place people will come to obtain a beautiful,

healthy smile in a fun, happy and safe environment.

A Warm Welcome and Exceptional Care for All Ages

From the moment our patients walk through the door they will be greeted by a warm atmosphere, smiling, courteous staff, and the welcoming smell of cookies (not dental acrylic!)

We cater to anyone age 7-77 (give or take a few years) who wants to improve their bite, gain the self-confidence of straight teeth and a beautiful smile, or who just needs minor tooth movements before a specific dental procedure.

Comfort, Care, and Cutting-Edge Orthodontics

The office décor, upbeat music, & complimentary fresh-baked cookies & coffee bar will help patients relax and feel comfortable. The energetic, friendly, and well-trained team will provide the highest quality orthodontic treatment with the most up-to-date techniques and equipment. The staff and doctor will take the time to explain treatment recommendations, go over step-by-step details and answer all questions. Our team is well-trained, knowledgeable, friendly, and compassionate.

When ready, our patients will leave our practice confident and happy that they made the right choice for their orthodontic needs. Not only will they have a healthy bite and beautiful new smile, they will feel important knowing that they have become part of our family .

MEET DR. SCHOENFELD

Dr. Jennifer Schoenfeld has been a proud resident of Vero for 24 years and has dedicated her career to creating beautiful smiles alongside her father at Wemmer Family Orthodontics. With 24 years of experience, she’s now thrilled to continue her journey in orthodontics at her own practice.

​ ​ When she’s not perfecting smiles, Dr. Schoenfeld enjoys spending time with her husband of 20 years and their 17-year-old twin boys. You might find her cheering on the Florida Gators or Baltimore Orioles, hiking in the mountains, or simply relaxing on the beach with her two beloved dogs, Cooper and Bailey. Dr. Schoenfeld also loves to unwind with a good book or a new recipe in the kitchen.
