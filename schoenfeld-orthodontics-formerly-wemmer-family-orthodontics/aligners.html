Aligners

ALIGNERS Aligners are extremely effective and can straighten your teeth faster than with traditional braces!

Clear aligners offer a modern, effective alternative to traditional braces by using a system of clear, removable trays to straighten teeth. Each aligner is custom-made to fit comfortably over your teeth, gently guiding them into the correct position over time.

Aligners are not just for teens and adults they can also be an excellent early treatment option for children as young as 8 or 9, helping to correct alignment issues as their teeth and jaws develop. Clear aligners provide a discreet, convenient way to achieve a beautiful, healthy smile for all ages.

Schoenfeld Orthodontics is a Certified Invisalign Provider Invisalign clear aligners have become one of the leading methods of orthodontic treatment for kids, teens, and adults! Invisalign consists of clear removable trays that provide tooth movement. Also, there are no metal attachments or wires, thus, brushing and flossing is easy!

Each aligner is custom-made to fit over your teeth and designed to gradually and painlessly move teeth into their proper position. You will be given several aligners (the number depending on your customized treatment plan) and each aligner will be worn for 1-2 weeks.
