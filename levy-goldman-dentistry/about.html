Dr. Naomi Goldman

WITH LEVY GOLDMAN DENTISTRY FOR 14 YEARS

Dr. Naomi Levy Goldman has a passion for cosmetic dentistry but practices a wide range of restorative dentistry as well. She grew up in Peabody, earned a Bachelor of Science degree in biology from the University of Connecticut, and then attended the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, where she received her Doctorate in Dental Medicine in 2013. She then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the Togus VA Medical Center in Augusta, ME, where she furthered her dental education by treating veterans with complex medical and dental needs.

Dr. Naomi is a published author on the topic of the dental needs of the elderly patient. She participated in a mission trip to Honduras to provide dental care to remote under-served villages. She is an active member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Clear Aligners, and the Massachusetts Dental Society. She recently earned her Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry as well.

In her spare time (if she has any) she loves doing yoga, hiking, and spending time with her husband and three kids Ben, Bodhi, and Bella.

Dr. Danny Levy

WITH LEVY GOLDMAN DENTISTRY FOR 38 YEARS

Dr. Danny Levy is a longtime Peabody resident. He graduated with a Doctorate in Dental Surgery from New York University dental school in 1986. Dr. Levy first practiced in a multidisciplinary group practice in Worcester, and subsequently in Peabody. In 1991, he opened his own practice in Peabody where he established himself as a general dentist, serving the dental needs of the community, from the very young to the elderly. His practice grew rapidly primarily through word-of-mouth referrals from patients who appreciated his caring approach and a light touch.
