Dr. Jonathan Feller grew up in a small town in Wyoming and later moved to Utah , where he discovered his passion for dentistry while helping his orthodontist father during high school.

After a service mission in Australia, Dr. Feller explored a career in music production. However, his direction changed while working as a dental assistant for his brother-in-law. Delivering a denture to a patient and seeing the immediate boost in confidence and happiness solidified his decision to pursue dentistry.

In 2019, Dr. Feller established Columbia River Dental Care , which has now become Pine Ridge Dental. Located in The Dalles, OR , Pine Ridge Dental is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care to the community. Dr. Feller focuses on functional aesthetics and implant dentistry, constantly seeking ways to improve patient care and outcomes.

At Pine Ridge Dental, we believe in creating life-changing smiles. Dr. Feller and his team are here to serve The Dalles community with compassionate and expert dental care.

Dr. Feller and his wife, Ashley, have four young kids they try to keep up with. The family enjoys snowboarding and skiing together. Dr. Feller loves his college football team, the University of Utah Utes. He also loves music and can be found playing guitar if he can spare some time.
