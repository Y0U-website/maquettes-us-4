Full-Arch Dental Implants…

A Complete Smile on Four Posts Full-Arch Dental Implants:

Four implants. One fixed arch. No denture adhesive.

Traditional dentures sit on the gums and rely on suction or adhesive. Full-arch implants anchor a complete set of teeth directly to your jawbone using four titanium posts. Two are placed vertically in the front of the jaw; two are angled in the back to maximize bone contact without needing grafting in most cases.

The result is a fixed prosthesis that never comes out. You brush it, floss it, and eat whatever you want. Patients who have lived with loose dentures or failing teeth often describe it as life-changing.

From consultation to your new smile.

3D Planning

Cone-beam CT scan maps bone volume and density. A surgical guide is created to position each implant with sub-millimeter precision.

Implant Surgery

Four titanium implants are placed under IV sedation or local anesthesia. Any remaining teeth are extracted at the same appointment.

Same-Day Teeth

A temporary fixed prosthesis is attached to the implants the same day. You leave the office with a full set of teeth.

Final Prosthesis

After 3 to 6 months of healing, your permanent zirconia or porcelain prosthesis is fabricated and attached. Custom-designed for your face and bite.
