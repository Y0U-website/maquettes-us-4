When to Contact a Family Dentist

Recognizing the signs that it’s time to visit a family dentist can help maintain optimal oral health. Consider reaching out to a professional if you experience any of the following:

Get in touch with Springboro Family Dentistry if you notice any of these symptoms.

  • Sensitivity to hot or cold
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Bleeding gums
  • Pain while chewing
  • Discolored teeth
  • Dry mouth
