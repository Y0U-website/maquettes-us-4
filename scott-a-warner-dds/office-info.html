Meet The Team

Dr. Scott A. Warner

Dr. Scott Warner believes in building relationships based on trust. He takes time to get to know you, even talking about things outside of dentistry, so that he has a full understanding of who you are and what you want. This way he can work with you, helping you make informed decisions about your dental care, and creating a smile that is literally made just for you.

With a personal touch and calm demeanor, Dr. Warner and his team provide a relaxing atmosphere in their office that puts you at ease. Their dentistry is tailored to the needs of each individual person.

He begins with a consultation during which he explains what is going on with your mouth, why it’s happening and how it can be fixed. However, he never wants you to feel overwhelmed at any point. He lets you know what is most important to do now, and what can wait.

Once he has laid out all of the options, he respects any decisions that you make regarding your treatment. He and his staff will not recommend any procedure unless it is in your best interest, and they never pressure or try to sell any treatment at any time.

Dr. Warner wants what’s best for you. His practice uses minimally invasive procedures that get better results giving you a more natural looking smile that will last.

Dr. Warner also focuses on preventative dentistry—he wants to stop disease and restore teeth to health. If the situation calls for it, he will perform general restorative dentistry, such as fillings, crowns and orthodontics. His goal is to preserve your natural tooth structure and create both long lasting dental health and a naturally beautiful smile.

With attention to detail and a calm manner, Dr. Warner provides the best for you and your dental health.

Dr. Scott A. Warner has been in practice since 1988. He earned his doctorate from U.C.L.A and has taken over 1500 hours of continuing education courses, including attending the prestigious Pankey Institute. In order to stay up-to-date on all the latest advancements in the field of dentistry, Dr. Warner maintains active memberships with the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association and the Western Los Angeles Dental Association. Outside the office, Dr. Warner enjoys technology, sports, music and traveling.

Dr. Dylan J. Warner

Dr. Dylan J. Warner DDS is a third-generation dentist. He graduated with Honors in 2017 from the University of Southern California Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, one of the most prestigious dental schools in the country.

Dylan attended local schools, starting at Palisades Elementary, followed by Paul Revere Middle School and Palisades Charter High School. Before attending dental school, Dylan earned his BA in Economics from the University of California, Santa Barbara. When he’s not in the office, Dylan enjoys cooking, playing basketball and watching the Clippers.

Dr. Dylan J. Warner works alongside his father Dr. Scott A. Warner and his wife Dr. Arielle Jones, teaming up to make the first family practice in the Palisades. Dylan’s addition has allowed the practice to expand and we always welcome the referrals of your friends and family.

Dr. Arielle E. Jones

Dr. Arielle E. Jones earned her Bachelors of Science degree in Natural Science from LMU and continued on to the Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry of USC, where she met Dr. Dylan Warner. She has known she wanted to work in dentistry ever since she was 12 years old- after having braces. Having a beautiful smile left such an impact on her at a young age and she wanted to help give that joy and confidence to others.

She believes in patient centric, conservative care and understands the importance of broadening the scope of her practice through continuing education. She recently received accreditation for the use and administration of Botulinum Toxin to help patients achieve functional results, reduce facial pain, & attain full facial esthetics.

Insurance & Financing

We are long standing partners and “in network” with Delta Dental Premier, Aetna , and United Concordia insurance plans. We are also happy to provide care for patients with most PPO and indemnity dental plans, however, your benefits will vary depending on the plan type.

We may be classified as “out of network” if you have another insurance plan, but you can check with your plan on their policy for out of network benefits. Typically, you will still have coverage for your treatment, but there often will be a slightly higher out of pocket for treatment done “out of network”.

As a service to our patients, we will bill insurance companies for services. If you have any questions, our courteous staff is always available to answer them.

We do not want our patients to feel like their dentistry is a financial burden, so we offer financing options through Care Credit. Care Credit offers several options that include 0 % interest plans. Please ask us for further information.
