Dr. Nazareth Bedoya

Dr. Nazareth Bedoya is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through compassionate, personalized care. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Universidad La Salle in Mexico with advanced training in General Dentistry. Dedicated to staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry, she is an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and San Diego Dental Society.

For Dr. Bedoya, dentistry is about much more than treating teeth. It’s about building meaningful relationships and caring for people with empathy, integrity, and respect. She strives to create a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, valued, and empowered to take an active role in their oral health. By prioritizing trust, open communication, and patient education, she helps individuals and families make informed decisions about their dental care.

Dr. Bedoya enjoys treating patients of all ages and has a special interest in endodontics. As a fluent Spanish speaker, she takes pride in connecting with and caring for a diverse patient community, helping every patient feel understood and at ease.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Bedoya enjoys exploring new destinations, spending time at the beach, and creating lasting memories with her family.
