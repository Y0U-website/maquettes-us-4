Upon arrival, check-in at the front and relax in our beautiful spa-like waiting room while you wait to be called back.

Once called back, our friendly team will greet you and guide you through our state-of-the-art facility to the exam room.

We’ll take initial X-rays, conduct a thorough examination of your oral health, and identify any potential concerns.

Our dentist will sit down with you one-on-one to discuss your dental concerns and answer any questions you may have.

Based on your consultation, we’ll create a personalized comprehensive dental plan that outlines the recommended treatments and procedures needed.

With your treatment plan in hand, our team will welcome you to the My Care Dental family and help you get started on your journey to a healthier, happier smile.
