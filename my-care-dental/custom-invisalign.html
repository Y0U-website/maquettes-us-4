Invisalign vs. Traditional Braces

Invisalign

Invisalign aligners are a popular alternative to traditional braces for a number of reasons:

Invisibility: The most notable difference and main selling point of Invisalign aligners is that they are virtually invisible. Most people won’t be able to tell that you’re wearing aligners.

Removability: Invisalign aligners are removable, which makes brushing and eating more convenient for patients. While they should be worn for up to 22 hours a day in the early stages, the fact that they can be taken out whenever necessary is a true game-changer.

Customization: Invisalign aligners are custom-made for each patient, ensuring a perfect fit and maximum comfort.

Safety: Invisalign aligners are designed to move teeth gradually and safely without the need for tightening or adjustments.

Traditional Braces

While braces have been a tried-and-true method for straightening teeth for decades, they do have some drawbacks:

Visibility: Braces are clearly visible to everyone. While it’s a matter of taste whether you or anyone else finds braces unsightly, the metallic appearance of braces will always be obvious to onlookers.

Maintenance: Braces require expertise and tools to adjust, making at-home removal non-existent. Patients may also experience discomfort and irritation from the brackets and wires.

Dietary restrictions: Patients with braces are typically advised to avoid certain foods that may damage or get stuck in the braces, such as sticky candy or popcorn.

Length of treatment: Braces may require a longer treatment time than Invisalign, depending on the severity of the case.
