Orthodontic Retention

You ve worked hard for your beautiful smile; let s keep it that way!

Retainer care is simple. Brush them with your toothbrush and water (no toothpaste) every time you take them out of your mouth. If you cannot brush them, rinse them with water. Do not eat or drink anything other than water with your retainers in your mouth.

As part of your orthodontic treatment, the office has provided you with one set of retainers. If your retainers are lost, damaged, worn out, left out for too long, or do not fit after new dental work is completed, you will be responsible for the cost of replacing those retainers. If they no longer fit due to lack of wear or completed dental work, you may need a new digital scan prior to ordering your new retainers. Please contact the office to schedule an in-office appointment.
