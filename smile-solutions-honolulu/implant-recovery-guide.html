Implant Recovery Guide

What to expect while you heal.

Recovery varies by procedure — a single implant heals differently than a full arch — but here’s a general timeline so you know what’s normal, and when to call us.

Right after surgery

  • Some swelling and bruising is normal, especially for full arch cases
  • Bite on gauze as directed to control bleeding
  • Stick to cold, soft foods — smoothies, yogurt, mashed potatoes
  • Take prescribed medication on schedule, don’t wait for pain to build

Swelling peaks, then improves

  • Swelling typically peaks around day 2–3, then gets better
  • Continue soft foods; avoid chewing directly on the implant site
  • Gentle salt-water rinses after meals — avoid vigorous swishing
  • Most patients return to work within 2–4 days, sooner for a single implant

Back to normal, gradually

  • Soreness fades; gradually reintroduce firmer foods
  • Follow-up visit to check healing progress
  • Avoid smoking — it significantly slows healing and raises implant failure risk

Osseointegration — the invisible part

  • Your jawbone is fusing to the implant — this phase has no real symptoms
  • Avoid excessive force on temporary teeth during this window
  • Bleeding that doesn’t stop after firm pressure for 30 minutes
  • Fever, or swelling that worsens after day 3–4 instead of improving
  • Signs your temporary teeth have loosened or shifted

Recovering right now and have a question?

Don’t wait for your next scheduled appointment — call our office directly if something feels off.
