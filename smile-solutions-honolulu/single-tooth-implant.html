Single Tooth Implant

One missing tooth. One precise replacement.

A single implant replaces the root and crown of one tooth — without filing down the healthy teeth next to it, the way a bridge would require.

Root and crown, replaced separately from your other teeth

A titanium post is placed into the jawbone where the tooth’s root used to be. Over a few months, the bone fuses directly to the implant (osseointegration) — creating a foundation as stable as a natural root.

A custom crown, matched to your surrounding teeth in color and shape, is then attached on top. The result functions and looks like the tooth that was there before.

An implant vs. a traditional bridge

  • Doesn’t require grinding down the healthy teeth on either side
  • Typically lasts decades, vs. 10–15 years for a bridge
  • Stops the jawbone loss that naturally follows losing a tooth
  • Cleans like a natural tooth — no floss threader required underneath
