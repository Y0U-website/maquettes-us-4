Implant Dentist

The dentist you see matters as much as the procedure you choose.

Dr. Kim has focused his practice at Smile Solutions on implant dentistry — from a single tooth to a full mouth reconstruction — using 3D-guided planning for precise, predictable results.

Dr. Anthony Kim, DDS

A real conversation, not a sales pitch

Every implant case starts with a 3D scan and an honest look at your bone structure and options — including telling you plainly when an implant isn’t the right choice yet, for example if a bone graft or gum treatment needs to happen first.

Dr. Kim treats the full range of implant care: single tooth replacement, All-on-4, All-on-6, and full mouth reconstruction — so the plan is built around your case, not around what one narrow technique can offer.
