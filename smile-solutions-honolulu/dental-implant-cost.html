What influences the dental implant cost

Six factors that affect the dental implant cost

Two patients getting implant treatment can end up with very different price quotes. A 3D scan lets us know if you’re a good candidate for treatment. And a consultation helps us find out which solutions are right for you.

  • Number of implants needed (up to 6 for all-on-x and up to 4 for snap on dentures)
  • Whether a bone graft or sinus lift is required before implant surgery
  • Single crown vs multiple tooth bridge vs full arch implant bridge
  • Material choice (acrylic vs titanium vs zirconia, fixed vs removable)
  • Oral sedation (anxiolysis) vs full sedation with MD anesthesiologist
  • Necessity of exotic’ implants that require an oral surgeon

This isn’t priced like a filling — here’s why

Implants involve surgical placement, precision 3D imaging, lab-fabricated custom teeth, and materials engineered to last many years. Picking the right materials and procedures can make a huge difference in the final long term outcomes.
