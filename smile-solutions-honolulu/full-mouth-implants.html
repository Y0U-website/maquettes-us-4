Full Mouth Implants

Full Mouth Implants · Honolulu, HI

Dr. Anthony Kim, DDS replaces an entire arch — or both — with permanent, implant-anchored teeth. No slipping, no adhesive, no daily removal. Just teeth that work like your own again.

Full mouth implants isn’t one fixed procedure

It’s a category covering a few approaches — All-on-4, All-on-6, and implant-supported bridges — and the right one depends on your jawbone density, remaining teeth, and bite. Dr. Kim maps your case with 3D imaging before recommending a plan, rather than defaulting to a template.

The common thread across all of them: your new teeth are anchored into the bone, not resting on your gums like a denture — so they don’t move, click, or need adhesive, and they help preserve the jawbone itself.
