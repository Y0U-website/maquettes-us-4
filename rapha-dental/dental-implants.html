Are Dental Implants Right for You?

You may be a good candidate for implants if:

Even if you’ve been told in the past that you don’t have enough bone, we offer bone grafting procedures to prepare your jaw for successful implant placement.

  • You’re missing one or more teeth
  • Your jawbone is healthy and fully developed
  • You don’t want to wear removable dentures
  • You’re looking for a lifelong solution to restore your bite and appearance

The Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Consultation – We assess your oral health, jawbone density, and treatment goals.
  • Implant Placement – A titanium post is placed under local anesthesia or sedation.
  • Healing Period – The implant integrates with your bone over 3–6 months.
  • Final Restoration – We place a custom crown or bridge that blends perfectly with your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the procedure painful? Most patients experience only mild discomfort, and we offer sedation for added comfort.

How long do implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Are they noticeable? Not at all—your implant crown is color-matched and shaped to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Can older adults get implants? Yes! There is no age limit for implants. We evaluate your bone and overall health, not your age.
