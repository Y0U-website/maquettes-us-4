Dr. Max Herszage

Dr. Max is a compassionate and skilled clinician with specialty training in orthodontics and over two years of experience in pediatric dentistry. He earned his dental degree from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine (DDS) where he developed a strong foundation in comprehensive patient care. He then went on to study advanced orthodontic training at CTOR Academy in Hoboken, NJ, a program known for its innovative, research-driven approach to orthodontics.

Dr. Max is experienced in early interceptive orthodontic treatment for growing children, as well as comprehensive treatment for adolescents and adults. He is also highly skilled in aligner therapy, providing modern, discreet options for patients seeking straighter smiles. He is passionate about creating a comfortable, positive experience while delivering personalized treatment plans that help patients of all ages achieve both optimal oral health and confident, beautiful smiles.

Outside the office, Dr. Max enjoys spending time with his dog Nellie and his wife Gabi, sharing his love for sports—especially cheering on the Cleveland Cavaliers and Ohio State Buckeyes—and experimenting in the kitchen, where cooking is one of his favorite ways to relax and get creative.
