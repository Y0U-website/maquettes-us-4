About Us

Individualized Dental Care Dr. Melissa Fan DDS, OSU Implant Fellow and dentist in Dublin Ohio, provides a full range of general, implant, and cosmetic dentistry including CEREC same day crowns and orthodontics (braces, Invisalign, Six Month Smiles and ClearCorrect).

Our mission is to provide the highest quality dental care just for your needs in a friendly and relaxed atmosphere. We are committed to bringing a healthy and beautiful smile to the entire family. We are a team dedicated to improving and maintaining your oral health. Whether you need preventative care, cosmetic dentistry, dental implant or oral surgery, you will love your smile.

Experienced Dental Professionals We offer broad array of services. Our team has the professional experience to realize that there is no such thing as one-size-fits-all treatment. All the treatments are individualized to fit your needs and wants.

The Latest Technology Dublin Family Dental is a state-of-the-art facility incorporating “paperless” charting (computerized record keeping) with digital X-rays and 3D imaging, providing precision imaging with reduced exposure to the radiation of traditional X-rays. The system also allows images to be sent to your insurance providers electronically to have procedures approved instantly, when necessary.

Our Services

Cosmetic, General and Implant Dentistry Whether your teeth are not as ideal in shape, crooked, stained, or chipped, our team can treat your teeth with minimal methods (bonding, veneers, crowns, dentures and more) using the latest techniques and the finest materials. We also offer implant treatment for your need. Call to ask for more details.

Preventative Care Efficient preventative care is key to having a healthy smile. Consistent checkups will allow our team to diagnose oral cancer, gum disease (periodontitis), and tooth decay (cavity) before they spread and become a problem.

Orthodontic Treatments We offer traditional metal braces, 6-month smile (clear brackets), Invisalign, and ClearCorrect treatment. Depending on your needs and wants, we will provide the customized and individualized treatment for you.
