Our Team

Our Doctors

Dr. Chaoyun Melissa Fan DDS, OSU Implant Fellow

Dr. Fan has practiced dentistry for over decades. She has focused her professional career on providing her patients with the highest quality and state-of-the-art technology for dental care. She constantly participates in continuing education courses to stay at the front edge of new technological advances in Dentistry and oral health care. Some of her specialized and advanced trainings include certificates in Implants, Orthodontics, Invisalign and Cerec system. Dr. Fan has a down-to-earth compassionate personality and skilled gentle touch that gives warmth and comfort to her patients.

Dr. Fan is a member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, and Columbus Dental Society.

Dr. Aina Kato-Kelly

Dr. Kato-Kelly is a Dublin native and earned both her B.S. in Molecular Genetics and Japanese and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University. She graduated at the top of her dental class and was inducted into the Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Kato-Kelly is passionate about serving her patients and is committed to their oral health goals. She is a current member of the American Dental Association and the Ohio Dental Association and is fluent in Japanese.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kato-Kelly enjoys skiing, baking, and playing video games.
