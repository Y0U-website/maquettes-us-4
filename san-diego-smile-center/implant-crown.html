Implant Crowns Near You

Replacing a missing tooth is important not only for your smile but also for your oral health and daily comfort. At San Diego Smile Center, we offer implant crowns in Mira Mesa to restore the appearance and function of teeth supported by dental implants. An implant crown is the visible portion of the restoration that sits on top of the implant, completing the look and function of a natural tooth.

Implant crowns are custom-made to match the shape, color, and size of your surrounding teeth. Once attached to the dental implant, the crown provides a durable and natural-looking solution that allows you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Why Choose Implant Crowns?

Dental implants replace the root of a missing tooth, but the implant crown completes the restoration by restoring the visible tooth structure. Implant crowns are designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth while providing the strength needed for everyday chewing. Unlike removable tooth replacements, implant crowns remain securely in place and function much like natural teeth. They help restore your bite, improve the appearance of your smile, and support long-term oral health.

What Sets Us Apart

At San Diego Smile Center, we focus on creating implant restorations that look natural and function comfortably. Our team carefully designs each implant crown to match the color, shape, and alignment of your surrounding teeth.

We use high-quality materials and advanced techniques to ensure durability and precision. Our personalized approach allows us to provide implant crowns that restore your smile while maintaining balance and harmony with your natural teeth.

What to Expect

The process of receiving an implant crown begins after the dental implant has fully integrated with the jawbone. Once healing is complete, your dentist will take impressions or digital scans to design a crown that fits perfectly with your bite.

The custom crown is then attached to the implant using a connector called an abutment. After placement, your dentist will make final adjustments to ensure the crown feels comfortable and functions properly. The result is a natural-looking tooth that blends seamlessly with your smile.

Benefits of Implant Crowns

Implant crowns are designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. This creates a seamless and natural-looking smile.

Made from high-quality materials, implant crowns are built to withstand everyday chewing and biting forces.

An implant crown allows you to eat, speak, and smile comfortably without worrying about movement or instability.

Unlike traditional bridges, implant crowns do not require altering neighboring teeth for support.

With proper care and regular dental visits, implant crowns can provide a reliable restoration for many years.
