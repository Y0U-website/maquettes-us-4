Implant-Retained Dentures Near You

Missing multiple teeth can make everyday activities like eating, speaking, and smiling more difficult. At San Diego Smile Center, we offer implant-retained dentures in Mira Mesa as a modern and secure solution for replacing several missing teeth. This treatment combines the stability of dental implants with the full-arch support of dentures to create a more comfortable and reliable tooth replacement option.

Unlike traditional dentures that rest directly on the gums, implant-retained dentures are anchored to dental implants placed in the jawbone. This secure foundation helps prevent slipping or movement, allowing patients to enjoy improved comfort, confidence, and functionality in their daily lives.

Why Choose Implant-Retained Dentures?

Traditional dentures can sometimes shift while eating or speaking, which may cause discomfort or embarrassment for some patients. Implant-retained dentures solve this issue by attaching the denture securely to implants placed in the jaw.

These implants act as artificial tooth roots, providing stability and helping maintain jawbone health. By supporting the denture with implants, patients often experience better chewing ability and improved confidence compared to traditional removable dentures.

What to Expect

Your treatment begins with a consultation where we assess your oral health and determine whether implant-retained dentures are the right solution for you. Benefits of Implant-Retained Dentures

Implant-retained dentures are anchored securely to dental implants, reducing the movement and slipping commonly associated with traditional dentures.

Because implants stabilize the dentures, patients can often eat a wider variety of foods with greater comfort.

Implants support the denture from beneath the gums, reducing pressure and irritation on the gum tissue.

Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, helping prevent bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.
