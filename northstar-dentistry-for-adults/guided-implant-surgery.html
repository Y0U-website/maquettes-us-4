The Process of Getting Guided Implant Surgery

What to expect at each step.

Step 1: 3D Scan and Digital Impression

A cone beam CT captures your bone; a digital scanner captures your teeth and gums. No trays, no impression material. Together they build an exact picture of what we’re working with.

Step 2: Virtual Planning and Review

Your implant is placed in software first. We check angle, depth, bone density, and how the final crown will sit in your bite. Then we go through the plan with you before you agree to it.

Step 3: Guided Placement

Your custom guide is manufactured to the approved plan. It sits over your teeth and directs the implant to the planned position. Most single-implant appointments take under an hour.

Does guided surgery cost more? Planning adds a step, but it reduces chair time and lowers the risk of complications that cost far more to correct. We’ll go through your specific numbers before you decide anything.

  • Is a CBCT scan safe? A dental CBCT takes seconds, and in the field of view used for implant planning, it delivers a small fraction of the radiation of a medical CT scan. It’s more than a standard dental X-ray and is needed for implant surgery. It is also needed when a plan needs to be changed. It shows bone in three dimensions that a flat X-ray can’t.
  • How long does the appointment take? A single guided implant is typically under an hour. Multiple implants take longer, but less than they would unguided.
  • Can I see my scan? Yes, and we’d encourage it. Most patients find it far more reassuring to look at their own anatomy than to be told about it.
  • What if the plan changes during surgery? It rarely does, and that’s the point. Because we know your anatomy in advance, any adjustment is made from information rather than improvisation.
