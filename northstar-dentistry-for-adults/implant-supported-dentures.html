The Process Of Getting Dentures

What to expect at each visit.

Personal Consultation

Dr. Bunn starts with a dental checkup, digital imaging, and a conversation about your needs. We review missing teeth, remaining teeth, natural teeth, oral hygiene, and treatment options. A staff member or one of our dental assistants can answer questions about dental insurance.

Implant Planning and Care

If implant surgery is right for you, Dr. Bunn plans implant placement with careful planning and CBCT imaging. Some patients need teeth extracted first. We may discuss immediate dentures, new dentures, temporary dentures, or several weeks of healing before the final fitting. New dentures may be adjusted as oral tissues heal.

Final Fitting and Follow-up

Once healing is done, we create your final denture for a proper fit. The final fitting helps us make necessary adjustments. We monitor healing, check your bite, and show you how to clean dentures without regular toothpaste.

Can I get implant-supported dentures the same day? Some patients can receive immediate dentures the same day teeth are removed. These immediate dentures help you avoid going without teeth while allowing patients to smile during healing. Implant-supported dentures usually need healing before your finished denture. Dr. Bunn will explain if a few days or more time is needed.

  • Are implant-supported dentures better than traditional dentures? Implant-supported dentures often feel more stable than traditional dentures. They can reduce movement in the lower arch and improve chewing support. Traditional dentures may still work well for patients who cannot have dental implants. Your dentist can compare dentures, artificial teeth, and teeth needs with you.
  • Will partial dentures protect my remaining teeth? Partial dentures can fill spaces when several teeth are missing. A metal framework may hold partial dentures around remaining teeth, and a metal framework can help the dentures stay steady. If natural teeth stay healthy, partial dentures can restore function without removing those teeth.
  • Do implant-supported dentures need special cleaning? Yes. You still need daily oral hygiene. Clean your dentures as directed, brush your gums, and avoid regular toothpaste unless your dentist says it is safe. Routine dental checkups help protect teeth, dentures, oral health, and overall health.
  • Can I still ask about other dental services? Yes. We can discuss dental services such as teeth whitening, wisdom teeth concerns, partial dentures, complete dentures, and dental care for natural teeth.
