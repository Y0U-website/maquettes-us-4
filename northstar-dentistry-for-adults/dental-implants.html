Dental Implants Are the Best Option for Replacing Missing Teeth NorthStar Dentistry for Adults offers permanent, natural-looking dental implants in Huntersville, NC. Missing teeth do more than leave a gap in your smile—they can cause surrounding teeth to shift and bone tissue in your jaw to deteriorate. That’s where dental implants come in.

A dental implant is a permanent solution that replaces the lost tooth’s root using a titanium screw that fuses with your jawbone—a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong foundation for your replacement tooth, preventing bone loss and restoring full dental function.

  • Step 1: Dental implant consultation Your first appointment includes a full review of your dental and medical history, an exam of your oral health, and digital imaging to evaluate your bone tissue. Dr. Bunn will discuss your goals, answer your questions, and build a treatment plan just for you.
  • Step 2: Dental implant surgery The implant post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. If needed, procedures like bone grafting may be done to strengthen the area. The implant fuses naturally with your jaw through osseointegration—creating a sturdy base for your new tooth.
