Adem Senalan, DMD, FICOI

Dr. Adem Senalan is a dentist with advanced training in implant dentistry, focusing on immediate implant placement and full-arch rehabilitation. He is completing his Master of Science in Dentistry through Jacksonville University’s Comprehensive Oral Implantology program and is pursuing board certification with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and the American Board of Oral Implantology. He is also a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI).

Dr. Adem has placed and restored hundreds of implants, emphasizing immediate implants and All-on-X procedures. With over 300 hours of advanced surgical education, he has trained alongside leaders in the field and lectures with the Chicago Osseo Academy, helping other dentists master immediate implant techniques.

He holds a Moderate IV Sedation Permit and provides advanced implant and surgical care to practices throughout the Chicagoland area.

Outside the office, Dr. Adem enjoys spending time with his wife and two young boys, exploring local parks and museums, and traveling to visit family in Montreal. A lifelong soccer fan, he loves watching international matches and playing in local leagues.
