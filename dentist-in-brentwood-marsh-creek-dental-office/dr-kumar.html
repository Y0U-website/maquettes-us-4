Dental Approach

Dr. Kumar practices with a focus on patient-centered dental care, including a thorough diagnosis and explanation of treatment options. He is very passionate about instilling positive oral health habits.

Mantra

Dr. Kumar is committed to helping patients overcome their dental phobia. You may even hear him break into song to make his office a more comfortable and happy place. He especially enjoys helping his patients feel a sense of empowerment and confidence with the results of their dental procedures. As a result, people’s lives are affected in a positive manner in their work and personal lives.

He currently resides in the Tri-Valley area and enjoys spending his free time with his wife and 13-year-old daughter.
