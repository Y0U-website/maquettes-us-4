How Is Good Oral Hygiene Practiced?

Maintaining good oral hygiene is an important part of preventing cavities, gum disease, and other common dental problems. Daily care, combined with professional dental visits, can help support healthy teeth and gums over time.

In addition to regular routine dental cleanings , there are simple habits you can practice at home to protect your oral health. These include:

  • Brush twice a day: Use fluoride toothpaste and brush all tooth surfaces thoroughly for about two minutes.
  • Clean between your teeth: Floss or use another interdental cleaning method recommended by your dental professional.
  • Limit frequent snacking: A balanced diet and limiting frequent sugary foods and drinks can help reduce the risk of tooth decay.
  • Use fluoride: Fluoride toothpaste can help strengthen enamel and protect against cavities. Your dentist may recommend additional fluoride products when appropriate.
  • Clean your tongue: Gently cleaning your tongue can help remove bacteria and support fresher breath.
