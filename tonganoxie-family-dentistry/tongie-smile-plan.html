What you NEED to know prior to signing up

*We will need your card information to be able to do monthly withdrawals, unless you pay for the full year in advance.

*The monthly withdraws will be going towards you having your plan, NOT your balance on your account.

*You are required to have the plan for a FULL year.

*You CANNOT be insured AND have the Tongie Smile Plan.

*You will receive a yearly renewal notice.

*If on auto-payment, your contract will automatically renew unless told otherwise.

*Be sure to utilize your plan while you have it, including the two cleanings per year.

*The Tongie Discount Plan can only be utilized at McLouth Family Dental, Valley Falls Family Dentistry and Tonganoxie Family Dentistry locations.
