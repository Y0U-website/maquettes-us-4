Oral Surgery

During the extraction itself, our dentists use specialized tools to gently and carefully remove the tooth. Depending on the complexity of the case, a simple extraction may be sufficient, which involves the loosening and removal of a fully erupted tooth. For more complicated extractions, such as impacted wisdom teeth, a surgical extraction may be required. In these cases, our dental team will take all necessary precautions to ensure a safe and successful procedure.

Crowns and Bridges

Missing teeth or underlying dental issues can significantly impact your ability to eat, speak, and chew comfortably. Our custom-made crowns and bridges are designed to seamlessly fit into your dental arch, restoring your oral functionality and allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods without hesitation .

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy not only relieves pain and discomfort, but it also helps preserve your natural smile. By saving your damaged tooth from extraction, we help maintain the structure of your jaw and prevent neighboring teeth from shifting. You’ll be able to eat, speak and smile confidently once again.

Dentures and Partials

Don’t let missing teeth prevent you from smiling with confidence or enjoying your favorite foods. Contact our office today to schedule an appointment and discover how our dentures or partials can transform your smile and improve your quality of life. Let us help you regain the joy and self-assurance that comes with a healthy and complete smile.

Implant services

An implant is a cutting-edge dental solution designed to provide a durable and natural-looking replacement for missing teeth. Composed of titanium and a biocompatible material, implants are meticulously crafted to mimic the natural structure and function of a tooth root. Implants consist of three main components: the implant itself, an abutment, and a custom-made dental crown.
