Dr. Charles Sutera

Nationally Renowned Expert FEATURED IN D r. Charles Sutera was born in the Boston area. He went on to attend Gettysburg College where he earned the prestigious Presidental Scholar Award each year of attendance. He then studied dentistry at Tufts School of Dental Medicine , and after graduating he has practiced in Portland, Boston, and Waltham, MA where he spent several years practicing dentistry with a focus in facial aesthetics.

Dr. Sutera completed his implant training at the Whitecap Surgical Center in Salt Lake City, extensive periodontal training at the GIDE Institute, and has trained in 3rd molar surgery. He continues to maintain certification in proficiency by the Academy of Laser Dentistry, and is a recognized Invisalign provider. He strives to make treatment a comforting process, encourages patient involvement, and is meticulous in achieving the highest level of results.

Outside of clinical practice, Dr. Sutera is highly active in the field of healthcare. He has been featured in several national publications for his diverse contributions in healthcare, Dr. Sutera serves as a thought leader , published journalist , a patented healthcare product developer , and as a dental expert witness for law firms across the country. For more information on the latest buzz check out his media site .

In his spare time, Dr. Sutera enjoys skiing, traveling, contributing time to non-profit organizations, and spending time with his family, miniature goldendoodle Zoey, and new baby boy Maddox.
