Dental Veneers

Porcelain veneers can correct a variety of cosmetic and functional defects of the teeth. Dental veneers can improve alignment, color, and shape of teeth all at the same time. You know those beautiful Hollywood smiles? They weren’t all born that way. What you are seeing is most likely the work of dental veneers by a top cosmetic dentist.

Invisalign

Orthodontics with clear aligners like Invisalign can correct a number of cosmetic defects of the smile. At our office, our philosophy is beauty in function. We use Invisalign to correct functional issues from temporomandibular joint disorder. So orthodontics are a win-win. They improve the smile’s appearance and also can reduce TMJ pain.

Dental Implants

Losing teeth can be very emotional. Tooth loss affects the way we feel about ourselves and also affects our jaw function. Dental implants restore teeth to natural prior function. If you are missing teeth, you can be comforted to know that dental implants can help you look and feel your best. Dental implants are often a powerful component of smile makeovers and full mouth reconstruction.

Dental Bonding

Small defects of the teeth from decay, chips, or spacing can have a dramatic impact. Dental bonding is a simple and easy procedure. The defects of the teeth are reshaped and a composite material is sculpted over the defect to permanently restore the tooth.
