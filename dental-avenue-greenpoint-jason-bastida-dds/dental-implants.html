Dental Implants in Greenpoint…

Unlike removable dentures or temporary fixes, implants offer a permanent, low-maintenance solution that blends seamlessly with your smile.

  • Prevent bone loss and preserve facial structure
  • Provide unmatched stability for chewing and speaking
  • Eliminate the need to alter neighboring teeth, as with dental bridges
  • Are durable and long-lasting with proper care
  • Support single crowns, bridges, or full-arch dentures

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a candidate for implants if you:

We’ll evaluate your candidacy using diagnostic tools and may recommend pre-treatment like periodontal therapy or a graft if bone loss is present.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have sufficient bone volume or are eligible for bone grafting
  • Are in good oral and overall health
  • Do not smoke or are willing to stop during healing
  • Are looking for a stable, long-term replacement option

The Dental Implant Process

At Dental Avenue Greenpoint, we tailor each implant case to the patient’s needs, but most treatments follow this process:

We’ll guide you through every step and offer financing options to make treatment accessible.

  • Consultation and Imaging : We begin with a comprehensive exam and 3D scan to evaluate your jawbone and treatment needs.
  • Implant Placement : A small titanium post is surgically placed into the jaw to act as a new tooth root.
  • Healing and Osseointegration : The implant fuses with the bone over several months, creating a stable base.
  • Restoration : Once healing is complete, we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture, matched to your natural tooth color and shape.

Single, Multi-Tooth, and Full-Arch Implant Options

We offer a range of implant services, including:

Our custom restorations are made to function comfortably and restore full aesthetics and bite function.

  • Single Tooth Implants : Ideal for replacing one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown
  • Implant Bridges : Used when multiple teeth are missing in a row
  • Implant-Supported Dentures : Full arch replacements that lock in place and offer far more stability than traditional dentures

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

  • How long do dental implants last?: With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or more—often a lifetime. They are the most durable tooth replacement option available.
  • Are dental implants painful?: Most patients report minimal discomfort, often less than a tooth extraction. Local anesthesia and sedation options help ensure a comfortable procedure.
  • What if I don’t have enough bone for implants?: You may still be eligible after a bone grafting procedure to rebuild lost bone.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance?: Coverage varies. Many plans offer partial benefits. We’ll help verify your insurance and discuss financing options if needed.
  • How do I care for dental implants?: Brush and floss regularly, attend routine cleanings, and avoid habits like smoking or teeth grinding. Implants require the same care as natural teeth.
