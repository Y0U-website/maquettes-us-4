What Are Full-Mouth Dental Implants?

Full-mouth dental implants are also known as implant dentures. This approach to tooth replacement involves inserting dental implant posts in precisely chosen spots in your jaw. Said posts are used to anchor dentures in place so that they can act as replacement teeth.

Full-mouth dental implants come in two main varieties: fixed and removable. We can discuss both options in more detail during your initial consultation, and we can help you figure out which approach is the right choice in your case.

Fixed/Permanent Full-Mouth Dental Implants

When you get fixed full-mouth dental implants, the dentures will be designed to remain anchored to the implant posts at all times; only your implant dentist in Jersey City will be able to remove them. Many patients see fixed full-mouth dental implants as the more convenient option, but bear in mind that this approach generally requires a greater number of implant posts.

Removable Full-Mouth Dental Implants

Removable full-mouth dental implants allow you to take your dentures out at any time. This approach usually calls for fewer implant posts, which is why it is often recommended for individuals with lower levels of jawbone density.

The Full-Mouth Dental Implant Process

An initial consultation will be held so that we can confirm your candidacy for dental implants and create a personalized treatment plan. Depending on the state of your mouth and jaw, preliminary treatments such as bone grafting may be needed. Once all of the preparations are complete, you can undergo dental implant surgery to have a specific number of implant posts inserted into your jaw.

In the months following the surgery, the implant posts will gradually fuse with the jawbone. Once this process is complete, metal connector pieces called abutments can be attached to the posts. Finally, your new dentures will be designed based on impressions of your mouth. The abutments will be used to anchor the final prosthesis to your dental implants; at that point, your sturdy new smile will be complete!

Am I a Candidate for Full-Mouth Dental Implants?

If you have an overall healthy mouth as well as sufficient bone density in your jaw, full-mouth dental implants could be an excellent way to correct your extensive tooth loss. Note that in some cases, you will need to undergo preliminary treatments before you can be considered a dental implant candidate. Our team will help you figure out whether dental implants are right for you during your initial consultation.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

One of the downsides of traditional dentures is that they sometimes slip out of place. In contrast, full-mouth dental implants will remain firmly anchored to your jaw while you’re eating, speaking, and smiling. On top of that, the dental implant posts will give your replacement teeth enough biting force to handle most foods without issue; they will also stimulate your jawbone, which helps prevent bone loss. Finally, full-mouth dental implants can last for a very long time when properly maintained.
