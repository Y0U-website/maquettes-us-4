Bernard Stewart, DDS grew up in a working class family in the Hayward area. His parents, a teacher and his faith, taught him that if he worked hard, he could achieve his dreams.

After graduating from Hayward High School, he attended San Jose State, where he earned his undergraduate degree. He worked his way through school.

Years of planning and hard work were rewarded when he was accepted into the dental school at Northwestern University.
