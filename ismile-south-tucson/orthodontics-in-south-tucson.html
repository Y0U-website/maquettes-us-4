Our orthodontic treatments

Braces

Metal braces are the most reliable and effective option for correcting moderate to severe crowding, spacing, and bite issues. They work around the clock, giving you consistent movement and predictable results. For teens and adults who prefer a classic, proven approach, metal braces remain one of the strongest ways to achieve long-term stability and healthier alignment.

Ceramic braces

Ceramic braces work just like metal braces but use tooth-colored brackets for a more discreet appearance. They’re a great option for adults, working professionals, or older teens who want the results of braces without the metal look. These brackets blend into your natural teeth, offering a balance of strength, efficiency, and aesthetics.

Clear aligner therapy

Clear aligners gently straighten your teeth using a series of removable, nearly invisible trays. At iSmile South Tucson, we create your aligners in-house with 3D printing technology, which helps keep treatment more affordable and gives us complete control over your progress. Aligners are ideal for mild to moderate spacing and crowding, and they allow you to eat, brush, and floss normally throughout treatment.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I need braces or aligners?

It depends on your teeth and your goals. Braces are generally recommended for more complex cases, such as severe crowding or bite issues. Ceramic braces are an aesthetic alternative with the same correcting power. Clear aligners are best for mild to moderate alignment improvements and are popular for adults who want a subtle option.

During your consultation, we’ll explain the differences, show you your alignment digitally, and help you choose the right treatment.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length varies. Most braces cases take 12–24 months, while clear aligners may take 6–18 months depending on consistency and case complexity.

We give you a personalized estimate at your first visit so you know what to expect before starting.

How much do braces or aligners cost?

Pricing depends on the type of treatment and length of time needed. We offer affordable monthly payment options and help you understand your insurance benefits. Many patients choose to start treatment with little or no upfront cost.

Our team breaks everything down clearly so you can make the best choice for your budget.
