The benefits of full arch implants

Full arch implants are one of the most transformative treatments we offer. They help patients regain daily comfort and restore the confidence they may have lost over time.

A secure smile you can trust Your new teeth are anchored to implants for a stable, locked-in feel. No slipping, shifting, or clicking.

A natural look designed for you Full arch implants are shaped and shaded to match your features. The result feels clean, balanced, and natural.

Support for long-term oral health Implants help prevent bone loss, maintain facial structure, and protect your bite. This keeps your smile healthier as you age.

Our full arch implant options

Implant-supported dentures

If you want more stability than a traditional denture but prefer something removable, this option may be a good fit. It offers a secure hold and better chewing strength without adhesive.

Fixed full arch implants

A set number of implants support a permanent, fixed arch of teeth that stays in your mouth at all times. This is the closest replacement to natural teeth in strength and feel. You can chew confidently and smile freely without worrying about movement.

Bone grafting

If you have been told you do not have enough bone for implants, we can evaluate your needs with 3D imaging. Bone grafting rebuilds and strengthens the implant site, giving more patients the chance to qualify safely. You will always understand your options clearly before making a decision.

Frequently asked questions

How long do full arch implants last?

Implants are designed to last many years, often a lifetime with proper care. The restoration attached to the implants may eventually need maintenance or replacement, but the implant posts themselves are intended to be long-term. Routine cleanings and checkups help protect your investment.

Am I a candidate even if I have bone loss or gum issues?

Possibly. Bone grafting, extractions, and periodontal care can significantly improve implant candidacy. Many patients who were told “no” in the past discover that they do have safe options today. Your consultation and 3D scan will give us the full picture.

What if I can’t afford the full cost upfront?

We understand that this is a significant investment. Many families choose payment plans that spread out the cost over time. We walk you through financing options, help you understand coverage, and make sure you feel confident in your plan.
