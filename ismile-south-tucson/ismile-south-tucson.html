Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the most common questions about dental implants in South Tucson.

Do you take my insurance?

Our office accepts almost all PPO insurance plans and many Medicaid and UHC Dual Complete options. Because coverage varies, our team will verify your benefits and go over everything with you before treatment. Our goal is transparency and helping you make the most of your plan.

Do you accept same-day emergencies?

Yes. We reserve several openings every day specifically for emergency visits. Whether you’re dealing with sudden pain, a broken tooth, or swelling, we’ll do everything we can to get you seen the same day and provide treatment, or at minimum, relief and next steps, right away.

How much is a crown, root canal, denture, implant, or extraction?

Costs depend on your insurance, your treatment needs, and the type of materials or techniques recommended. During your visit, our treatment coordinator will explain your options, review your benefits, and provide a clear estimate before anything begins. We also offer affordable payment plans to make treatment stress-free.

Do you offer after-hours or Saturday appointments?

Not at this time. We currently offer Monday–Friday care with early Friday hours, but we are actively planning expanded hours in our future, larger location. If you’re experiencing pain after hours, we recommend calling us so we can help determine next steps.

Do you accept payment plans?

Yes. We partner with Sunbit and CareCredit to offer flexible monthly payments, including interest-free options for qualifying patients. These plans provide support for larger treatments like implants, root canals, and cosmetic services.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes, many members of our team are fluent in Spanish, and we proudly serve the heavily Hispanic South Tucson community. We want every patient to feel welcomed and understood from the moment they arrive.
