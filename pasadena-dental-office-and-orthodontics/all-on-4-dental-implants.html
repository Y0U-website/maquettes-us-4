The Process for All-on-4 Implants in Pasadena

The process of receiving All-on-4 dental implants will depend on your individual needs, but here’s a general overview of the procedure:

  • Initial Consultation: During your first visit, Dr. Tsibel will conduct a detailed examination of your oral health, including X-rays, to assess the condition of your jawbone and remaining teeth.
  • Personalized Treatment Plan: Based on your evaluation, Dr. Tsibel will craft a tailored treatment plan for your All-on-4 implants, ensuring the best possible outcome for your smile.
  • Implant Placement: We will carefully place the four titanium posts into your jawbone and provide a temporary set of teeth while your implants heal and integrate with the bone.
  • Final Restoration: Once your implants have fully fused with your jawbone, we’ll fit a permanent set of teeth to your implants, providing a fully functional and natural-looking smile.
  • Aftercare and Follow-Up: Dr. Tsibel will provide post-treatment care instructions to keep your implants in optimal condition. Regular check-ups will help ensure that your implants function well for years.
