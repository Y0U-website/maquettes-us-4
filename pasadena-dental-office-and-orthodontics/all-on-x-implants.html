All-on-X Implants Explained

The process of getting All-on-X implants is simple, although it does require careful planning. Here’s an overview of what you can expect:

At Pasadena Dental Office and Orthodontics, Dr. Tsibel is known for providing high-quality care and utilizing advanced dental technology to ensure precise and effective treatment. Our office prioritizes patient comfort and offers flexible financing options.

  • First Consultation During your first visit to Pasadena Dental Office and Orthodontics, Dr. Tsibel will thoroughly evaluate your oral health, including X-rays and a physical examination to assess your jawbone and determine the best placement for the implants.
  • Treatment Plan Dr. Tsibel will develop a personalized treatment plan for your All-on-X implants based on your needs. This plan includes the number of implants needed, the restoration type, and the procedure timeline.
  • Implant Placement On the day of the procedure, Dr. Tsibel will place the titanium posts into your jawbone. Depending on your case, we can put you under local anesthesia or sedation to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.
  • Temporary Teeth After the implants are in place, we may place temporary teeth while your implants heal and integrate with your jawbone. This process usually takes a few months.
  • Permanent Teeth Once your implants have fully healed, the permanent set of teeth will be attached. Dr. Tsibel will ensure they fit perfectly and feel natural, completing your smile restoration.
  • Post-Procedure Care and Maintenance After the procedure, Dr. Tsibel will provide detailed aftercare instructions. Regular check-ups will help ensure your implants continue to function well for years to come.
