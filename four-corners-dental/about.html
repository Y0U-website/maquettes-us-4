Dr. Jeremy Kamari

Born and raised in Haverhill, Massachusetts, Dr. Jeremy Kamari has always approached life with focus, discipline, and a deep appreciation for personal connection, values inspired by his parents’ immigrant journey from Italy and Lebanon. His path to dentistry was shaped by a desire to help people through healthcare and the mentorship of a trusted family friend in the dental profession. That early experience sparked a passion that led him to pursue extensive training and education in the field.

Dr. Kamari earned his undergraduate degree from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in 2016, followed by his DMD from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2020. He went on to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of North Carolina – Chapel Hill, where he trained primarily at the Kernersville VA, honing both his technical skill and compassionate approach to care.

With over 350 hours of continued education under his belt, Dr. Kamari is an expert in advanced implant and reconstructive dentistry, including full-mouth rehabilitations, cosmetic makeovers, and implant restorations. He is a proud member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and the Academy of General Dentistry, credentials that reflect not just his clinical expertise but the ongoing commitment to staying at the forefront of modern dentistry.

At Four Corners Dental, Dr. Kamari’s philosophy is to elevate the patient experience through transparency, precision, and meaningful relationships. From complex full-arch solutions to gentle preventive care, he believes that dentistry has the power to change lives, and he’s here to guide patients in making lasting improvements to their health, smile, and confidence.

Whether you’ve been putting off care for years, are exploring cosmetic enhancements, or simply want a provider you can trust for the long haul, Dr. Kamari evaluates each patient holistically. He listens first, then crafts treatment plans that align with your personal goals and lifestyle, always with a steady hand and a calm, expert voice.

Technology that transforms your experience

We stay ahead of the curve to bring you dentistry that’s precise, efficient, and improves your long-term results. From diagnostics to treatment planning, our tools are designed for accuracy, comfort, and clarity.

CBCT scanner This advanced 3D imaging system helps us visualize your entire oral anatomy in high definition — essential for implant placement, surgical planning, and comprehensive diagnostics.

Digital 3D scanner Say goodbye to goopy impressions. Our intraoral digital scanner allows us to capture an exact replica of your teeth and bite with total comfort and real-time visualization.

3D printer Our in-house 3D printing technology allows us to design and fabricate custom dental appliances, models, and surgical guides. This means faster turnaround times, improved accuracy, and personalized treatment solutions tailored specifically to your smile.

Pearl AI Pearl AI is an advanced, FDA-cleared system that analyzes X-rays to enhance diagnostic accuracy, help detect issues earlier, and support more informed treatment planning for optimal patient care.

Soft tissue laser Minimally invasive and highly effective, our diode laser allows us to perform gum therapy and minor surgical procedures with greater precision and faster healing.

Curodont A brand of dental treatment that uses guided enamel regeneration to treat early-stage tooth decay without drilling or pain. It is minimally invasive, with no needles and no drills.

Diagnodent Early detection is key. This diagnostic laser quickly and painlessly detects areas of decay that are invisible to the naked eye, helping us treat issues before they become serious.
