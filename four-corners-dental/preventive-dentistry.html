Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride is a well-researched preventive tool in dentistry that helps remineralize weakened enamel, reversing early-stage tooth decay and making it more resistant to acids from bacteria and sugary foods. At Four Corners Dental, we provide professional fluoride varnish treatments after every cleaning, which are more concentrated than over-the-counter products and offer longer-lasting protection.

Who benefits from fluoride treatments? It&#x27;s not just for kids; adults with a history of cavities, dry mouth, exposed root surfaces, orthodontic treatment, or those who consume acidic foods can benefit greatly.

Dental Sealants

The back teeth, or molars and premolars, are essential for chewing but have deep pits and grooves that trap food and bacteria, making them prone to cavities. Dental sealants provide an effective solution by applying a thin, tooth-colored resin that hardens to create a smooth barrier, preventing decay.

Sealants can last 5–10 years with proper care and are beneficial for both children and adults, especially those with deep grooves. Ask Dr. Kamari if sealants are right for you at your next appointment.

  • Teeth are cleaned and dried.
  • An etching solution is applied.
  • The sealant is brushed on.
  • A curing light hardens it in about 60 seconds.
  • Bite adjustments are made if necessary.

Periodontal Care

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults, often going unnoticed until it progresses. Early detection is crucial, as it can be reversible at the gingivitis stage, where gums may be red, swollen, or bleed during brushing. If untreated, gingivitis can progress to periodontitis, which leads to infection below the gumline, pocket formation, and bone loss. Advanced periodontitis can result in loose teeth and tooth loss.

Risk factors include smoking, diabetes, pregnancy, certain medications, and genetics. If any apply to you, consult Dr. Kamari to create a monitoring plan. Healthy gums are essential for dental health, and we take that seriously.

  • Periodontal probing and charting during exams
  • Scaling and root planing (deep cleaning)
  • Soft tissue laser therapy
  • Periodontal maintenance visits (every 3–4 months for active disease)

Oral Cancer Screenings

Oral cancer is a serious yet treatable disease that dentists can detect early. The five-year survival rate exceeds 80 % when caught early but drops significantly when diagnosed later. This is why oral cancer screenings are standard at Four Corners Dental.

Notably, oral cancer is increasingly affecting those without traditional risk factors, particularly HPV-related cases among non-smokers and non-drinkers.

Be aware of symptoms like persistent sores, unexplained bleeding, lumps, difficulty swallowing, or a sore throat. If you notice any, contact us. We prefer to check out something minor rather than overlook a potential issue.

Night Guards

Bruxism, or grinding and clenching your teeth during sleep, is more common than many realize. Often, individuals are unaware they grind their teeth until a dentist identifies signs like chipped edges or increased tooth sensitivity. If left untreated, bruxism can lead to serious dental damage, jaw pain, headaches, and TMJ disorders.

A custom-fitted night guard provides effective protection. This appliance, made from an impression of your teeth, sits comfortably over your upper or lower teeth, cushioning them and preventing damage caused by grinding. Unlike generic options, a custom guard ensures a precise fit.

You might benefit from a night guard if you experience jaw soreness, headaches in the morning, or your partner hears you grind your teeth at night. The process involves two appointments: taking impressions and a fitting. Many patients see improvement in jaw comfort within a week. Protecting your teeth now is more cost-effective than repairs later. Ask about night guards at your next visit!

Custom Sports Guards

Sports-related dental injuries lead to about 600,000 emergency room visits each year in the U.S., most of which are preventable with a custom mouthguard. If you or your child participates in contact sports or activities with a risk of projectiles, investing in a custom sports guard is a smart choice.

Custom mouthguards , made from an impression of the teeth, fit securely and comfortably, providing consistent protection compared to loose, over-the-counter options. They’re particularly important for sports such as football, hockey, lacrosse, basketball, soccer, and more.

These personalized appliances safeguard your smile by preventing tooth loss, fractures, and soft tissue damage, while also lowering the risk of concussions. Available for all ages, these guards can be customized in various colors, including team colors. They are durable, easy to clean, and designed to last the season with proper care.
