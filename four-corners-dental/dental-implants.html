Bone Grafting

If you&#x27;ve been missing a tooth for a while, the jawbone in that area may have begun to shrink. Without the stimulation of a tooth root, bone volume naturally decreases over time—which can make implant placement more difficult.

That&#x27;s where bone grafting comes in. Dr. Kamari brings extensive expertise in bone grafting. Before placing your implant, Dr. Kamari may recommend a bone graft to rebuild the foundation your implant needs to succeed. Using safe, biocompatible grafting material, we restore the strength and volume of your jaw so it can fully support a lasting implant.

Many patients who were once told they weren&#x27;t candidates for implants are able to move forward after grafting. If bone loss has been a concern for you, don&#x27;t count yourself out—a consultation and 3D scan will give us a clear picture of what&#x27;s possible.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

They Look, Feel, and Function Like Natural Teeth Dental implants are crafted to blend seamlessly with your smile. Each restoration is custom-designed to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth—and because implants are anchored directly in the bone, they feel completely stable. Most patients tell us they forget their implant is even there.

They Protect Your Jawbone and Facial Structure When a tooth root is missing, the bone around it begins to deteriorate. Implants are the only restoration that replaces the root, stimulating the bone naturally through everyday chewing. This helps preserve your jaw&#x27;s density, maintain your facial contours, and prevent the sunken appearance that often follows long-term tooth loss.

They&#x27;re Built to Last With proper care, dental implants can last decades—and for many patients, a lifetime. The titanium post integrates with your bone to create a permanent foundation, while routine cleanings and good oral hygiene keep everything healthy around it. Compared to bridges or dentures that may need replacement or adjustment over the years, implants are a long-term investment that pays off.

Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and routine dental visits, dental implants can last for decades —and in many cases, a lifetime . The implant post itself is designed to be a permanent fixture, while the crown on top may eventually need replacement due to normal wear. Regular checkups help us monitor your implant and keep everything in great shape.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults who are missing one or more teeth are candidates for implants. Ideal candidates have healthy gums, adequate jawbone density, and no untreated dental conditions. Even if you&#x27;ve experienced bone loss, procedures like bone grafting can often make implants possible. A consultation and CBCT scan will give us everything we need to determine the right path for you.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Most patients report that the procedure is more comfortable than they expected. We use local anesthesia throughout the placement, and any soreness in the days following is typically mild and easily managed with over-the-counter pain relief. We&#x27;ll give you clear aftercare instructions to keep your recovery as smooth as possible.

Can dental implants get infected?

While uncommon, implants can develop a condition called peri-implantitis—an infection caused by plaque buildup around the gumline. This is why daily brushing, flossing, and regular professional cleanings are essential. Our team provides detailed guidance after your procedure to help you protect your implant and maintain healthy gums long-term.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of implant treatment depends on several factors, including the number of teeth being replaced and whether preparatory procedures like bone grafting are needed. We accept most PPO insurance plans, and we offer flexible financing through CareCredit, Proceed Finance, and in-house payment plans. Call us at (781) 933-4144 and we&#x27;ll help you understand your options.

Do you offer full-mouth implant restoration?

Yes. For patients who are missing most or all of their teeth—or who are struggling with uncomfortable, unstable dentures—full arch implant restoration can be life-changing. It restores strong chewing ability, natural speech, and a confident smile with a permanent, secure solution. We&#x27;ll walk you through every option during your consultation and help you find the approach that fits your needs and goals.
