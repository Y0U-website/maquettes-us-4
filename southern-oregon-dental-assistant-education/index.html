The practice

The dental assisting program will prepare you to take state certification exams. You’ll be ready to work as a dental assistant in just 8 months!

Teaching in a local dental office provides training with dental equipment not available in a traditional classroom environment.

Training is provided at a local dental office, utilizing real dental equipment and materials. The hands on training is essential to gaining the confidence needed to gain employment as a dental assistant.

We can get you on track to be a dental assistant in as little as 8 months. Not only will you be ready in half the time, but we are also a third of the price of our competitors.

Our Program is currently $6,995 compared to our competitors at over $20,000 with prerequisites. Payment options are avaliable on the “Payment Plans” page.

Our program is geared to have you ready in just 8 months with no prerequisites needed unlike a typical college.

We hold good relationships with dental offices all over the valley and have a good record for getting students opportunities in those offices.

We understand people have work and family to attend to. We hold our classes in the afternoon and on weekends to help get around most day to day activities you may have.
