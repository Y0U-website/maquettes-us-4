Meet Dr. Aarti Jawa

At Vibe Dentistry, exceptional dental care goes beyond cleanings and procedures it’s about trust, comfort, innovation, and genuine human connection. These values are the foundation of the practice and stem directly from the vision of its founder, Dr. Aarti Jawa, DMD, a highly experienced, Connecticut-licensed dentist with more than a decade of diverse clinical expertise.

Dr. Jawa created Vibe Dentistry with one clear mission: to transform the dental experience into one that feels welcoming, modern, and truly patient-centered. Every detail of the practice from advanced technology to the calming atmosphere reflects her belief that dentistry should feel personal, not clinical.

Book Appointment Founder • Owner • Dentist-in-Chief of Vibe Dentistry

At Vibe Dentistry, we believe great dentistry is about more than healthy teeth—it is about helping people feel comfortable, cared for, and confident. That philosophy begins with Dr. Aarti Jawa.

Dr. Jawa is the founder of Vibe Dentistry and is dedicated to providing personalized, compassionate dental care for patients of all ages. She believes that exceptional dentistry combines clinical excellence with genuine human connection, and she strives to ensure every patient feels heard, respected, and comfortable throughout their dental experience.

Dr. Jawa earned her dental degree from Sri Ramachandra Medical College in Chennai, India, where she developed a strong foundation in comprehensive dental care and gained valuable experience treating patients from diverse backgrounds. She later continued her education in the United States at the prestigious Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, further advancing her clinical training and expertise.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Jawa completes hundreds of hours of continuing education each year to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry, technology, and evidence-based treatment. She continually invests in advanced training to provide her patients with the highest standard of care and the latest treatment options available.

Throughout her career, Dr. Jawa has enjoyed all aspects of general dentistry, including preventive, restorative, and cosmetic care. She particularly values helping patients regain comfort, confidence, and function—whether that means relieving dental pain, restoring a damaged tooth, improving a smile, or helping someone overcome years of dental anxiety.

The inspiration for Vibe Dentistry came from an observation Dr. Jawa made throughout her years in practice: while tremendous effort is often devoted to making dental visits comfortable for children, many adults continue to feel anxious, embarrassed, or judged about their oral health.

She wanted to create something different.

A place where patients who had avoided the dentist for years could walk through the door without fear of criticism.

A place where every individual would be treated with kindness, respect, and genuine compassion.

Every aspect of the practice was intentionally designed to create a calm, welcoming environment and reduce dental anxiety. Nitrous oxide is available throughout the office, appointments are never rushed, and every member of the team is committed to providing a supportive and judgment-free experience.

Dr. Jawa believes that patient education is one of the most important aspects of dental care. She takes the time to explain findings, discuss treatment options, answer questions, and help patients make informed decisions about their oral health. Her goal is to build long-term relationships based on trust, transparency, and personalized care.

Outside the office, Dr. Jawa enjoys spending time with her husband, Dr. Arashinder Dhaliwal, a board-certified psychiatrist specializing in child, adolescent, and adult psychiatry, and their young son, who keeps life joyful, active, and full of adventure. She enjoys traveling, exploring new restaurants, trying different cuisines, practicing Pilates, cooking, and listening to music.

Friends, family, and patients often describe Dr. Jawa as caring, thoughtful, and detail-oriented. She believes that the smallest details often make the biggest difference and takes pride in creating an experience where patients feel genuinely valued.

As Vibe Dentistry continues to grow, her vision remains simple: to create a true dental home where patients feel welcomed, respected, and cared for like family—a place where exceptional dentistry meets genuine human connection.

Her promise to every patient is straightforward:

“I will always recommend what is in your best interest, treat you with honesty and respect, and provide the same level of care I would want for my own family.”
