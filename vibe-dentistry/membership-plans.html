Why choose membership plan?

Our membership plans offer a modern alternative to traditional dental insurance by removing unnecessary barriers and focusing on what truly matters—your care.

There are no deductibles to meet, no waiting periods to worry about, and no claim forms to submit. You won’t have to deal with denied procedures or confusing coverage limitations. Instead, you’ll receive immediate access to the services you need, along with clear, upfront pricing.

This model is especially beneficial for patients without dental insurance, those with high-deductible plans, or anyone seeking a more predictable and transparent approach to managing their dental expenses.

Membership plans

At Vibe Dentistry, we understand that navigating dental insurance can often feel confusing, restrictive, and unpredictable. That’s why we’ve created a membership program that puts simplicity and transparency first. Our plans are designed to give you direct access to high-quality dental care with clearly defined benefits, no hidden costs, and no unexpected limitations.

Whether you’re an individual focused on maintaining your oral health or a family looking for an affordable, all-in-one solution, our membership plans provide a reliable and stress-free way to stay on top of your dental care. With preventive services included and exclusive discounts on additional treatments, you can feel confident knowing your care is both accessible and cost-effective.

Family plan

Our Family Plan is designed to simplify dental care for households by bringing everyone under one comprehensive membership. For $900 per year, this plan covers a married couple and up to two children, offering exceptional value while ensuring that every member of your family receives the care they need.

Unlike many traditional options, this plan comes with no monthly fees, allowing you to enjoy the convenience of a single annual payment without ongoing billing. It’s a practical and budget-friendly solution for families who want to prioritize preventive care while keeping costs under control.

By enrolling in the Family Plan, parents can ensure their children develop strong, healthy dental habits early on, while also maintaining their own oral health. Having all family members on the same plan also makes scheduling, tracking care, and planning treatments significantly easier.

Individual plan

Our Individual Plan is thoughtfully designed for patients who want to stay consistent with their dental care while avoiding the complexities of traditional insurance. For an annual fee of $500, this plan covers all essential preventive services needed to maintain a healthy smile throughout the year.

Members benefit from routine exams, professional cleanings, and diagnostic imaging that allow us to detect and address issues early—before they become more complex or costly. In addition to preventive care, this plan provides access to discounted treatment rates, making it easier to move forward with recommended procedures without financial hesitation.

This plan is ideal for adults who value proactive care, predictable costs, and a straightforward approach to maintaining their oral health.

Perio maintenance plan

For patients managing periodontal (gum) disease, regular maintenance is essential to preserving long-term oral health. Our Perio Maintenance Plan is specifically designed to support these patients with more frequent and specialized care.

At $700 per year, this plan includes three to four periodontal maintenance visits annually. These visits go beyond standard cleanings, focusing on controlling gum disease, reducing inflammation, and preventing further progression. Consistent maintenance plays a critical role in protecting not only your teeth and gums, but also your overall health.

This plan is ideal for patients who have undergone periodontal treatment or who require ongoing care to maintain stability and prevent recurrence.

Preventive services included

Preventive care is the foundation of long-term oral health, and every one of our membership plans is built around this principle. By including essential services, we help you stay ahead of potential issues and avoid more extensive treatments down the line.

Each plan includes two exams per year—one comprehensive and one periodic—allowing us to thoroughly evaluate your oral health and monitor any changes over time. You’ll also receive two professional cleanings annually, which are essential for removing plaque and tartar buildup that cannot be addressed with regular brushing and flossing alone.

To support accurate diagnosis and treatment planning, your membership also includes annual X-rays, consisting of bitewings and a panoramic or full-mouth series. These images allow us to detect concerns that may not be visible during a clinical exam.

In addition, one emergency exam per year is included, giving you peace of mind in case unexpected dental issues arise. For younger patients, fluoride treatments are included to strengthen developing teeth and protect against cavities, with an optional add-on available for adults who may benefit from additional protection.

Discounts on additional care

Beyond preventive services, our membership plans are designed to make restorative and advanced treatments more accessible. Members receive exclusive discounts across a wide range of services, helping reduce out-of-pocket costs and making it easier to proceed with recommended care.

You’ll receive a 20 % discount on common procedures such as fillings, crowns, dentures, extractions, and scaling and root planing. These treatments are often essential for maintaining oral health, and reduced pricing ensures they remain within reach.

For more advanced procedures—including dental implants, root canal therapy, and oral surgery—members receive a 15 % discount. These treatments can be significant investments, and our goal is to make them more manageable without compromising on quality.

If you’re looking to enhance your smile, cosmetic treatments and clear aligner therapy are also available at a 10 % discount. Whether your goals are functional, preventive, or aesthetic, your membership supports your journey every step of the way.
