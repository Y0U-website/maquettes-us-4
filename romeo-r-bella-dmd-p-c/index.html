Endodontics involves the diagnosis and treatment of diseases affecting the dental pulp, root, and surrounding tissues. Our endodontic services include root canal therapy, which can save a tooth that has been severely damaged or infected.

Dentures are removable appliances that can replace missing teeth and restore your smile. Romeo R. Bella DMD offers both full and partial dentures, designed to look natural and provide a comfortable fit. Schedule a consultation to find out which are right for you.

Bridges and bonding are effective solutions for replacing or repairing damaged or missing teeth. Romeo R. Bella DMD and his team are skilled in these procedures, helping to restore your smile and improve your oral health.

Teeth whitening is a simple, non-invasive dental treatment used to change the color of natural tooth enamel and enhance the beauty of your smile. We offer professional teeth whitening services to help you achieve a brighter, whiter smile.

Dental implants are a popular and effective long-term solution for people who suffer from missing teeth, failing teeth or chronic dental problems. Romeo R. Bella DMD provides high-quality dental implants that look, feel and function like natural teeth.
