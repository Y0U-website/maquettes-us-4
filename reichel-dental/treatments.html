How often should I have my teeth professionally cleaned?

We recommend scheduling professional teeth cleanings every six months to maintain optimal oral health. Regular cleanings help prevent gum disease, cavities, and other dental issues. Our Registered Dental Hygienist, Julie Fox, provides gentle and thorough cleanings while educating patients on proper at-home oral care.

What are dental crowns and bridges, and when are they needed?

Dental crowns are used to restore and protect damaged or decayed teeth by covering them entirely. Bridges are employed to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth to adjacent natural teeth or implants. Both crowns and bridges help restore function, improve appearance, and prevent further oral health issues.

Are tooth-colored fillings a good alternative to silver fillings?

Yes, tooth-colored fillings made from composite resin are an excellent alternative to traditional silver (amalgam) fillings. They blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing a more aesthetic result, and can also strengthen the tooth structure.

Do you offer dentures and partial dentures?

We provide both full and partial dentures to replace missing teeth and restore your smile. Dr. Reichel works closely with local dental technicians to create custom-fit dentures that are comfortable and functional. Measurements of your face are taken to ensure a natural appearance and proper fit.

Can you help with snoring and sleep apnea issues?

Yes, we offer treatments for snoring and sleep apnea, which can significantly impact your overall health. Untreated sleep apnea can lead to serious conditions like high blood pressure, diabetes, and heart disease. Our team can design a treatment plan to help you breathe easier and improve your sleep quality.
