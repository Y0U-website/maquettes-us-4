FAQs

How does professional teeth whitening work?

Our professional teeth whitening services utilize advanced techniques to remove stains and discoloration, resulting in a brighter smile. We also offer Supersmile Professional Whitening Toothpaste and Accelerator for at-home maintenance, enhancing the effects of in-office treatments.

Are porcelain veneers suitable for me?

Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth, ideal for correcting chips, gaps, discoloration, or minor misalignments. We customize veneers to match your natural teeth, ensuring a seamless and aesthetically pleasing result.

What are the benefits of tooth-colored fillings?

Tooth-colored fillings are made from composite materials that match the natural color of your teeth. They offer a more aesthetically pleasing alternative to traditional silver fillings and can strengthen the tooth structure while providing a natural appearance.

What is involved in a smile makeover?

A smile makeover at Reichel Dental involves a comprehensive assessment of your dental aesthetics and function. We consider factors like facial symmetry, tooth color, and alignment to create a personalized treatment plan, which may include veneers, crowns, bonding, or whitening, tailored to achieve your desired smile.

Do you offer dental implants?

Yes, we provide dental implant services to replace missing teeth. Dental implants serve as a durable and natural-looking solution, restoring both function and appearance. They can support crowns, bridges, or dentures, depending on your specific needs.
