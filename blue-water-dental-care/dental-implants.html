Who’s a Candidate for Implants?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

During your consultation, we’ll use 3D imaging to assess your bone structure and determine the best course of action—whether that’s a single implant or full-arch restoration.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Are tired of removable dentures or bridges
  • Want a stable, long-term solution
  • Have sufficient jawbone (or are eligible for bone grafting)
  • Are in generally good health and committed to oral hygiene

Types of Dental Implant Solutions

We offer multiple implant options based on your unique needs:

Single-Tooth Implant

Replaces one missing tooth with an implant and a custom crown—no impact on neighboring teeth.

Implant Bridge

Replaces several missing teeth using implants as anchors, avoiding the need for multiple individual implants.

Implant Dentures

Secure, removable or fixed dentures anchored by 2–6 implants—offering more comfort and function than traditional dentures.

Full Mouth Implants / All-on-4

A full-arch solution using 4–6 implants to support a permanent, natural-looking set of teeth—ideal for patients missing most or all teeth.

Our Dental Implant Process

Here’s what to expect when you choose dental implants at Blue Water Dental Care:

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging We evaluate your bone health and plan placement using advanced digital scans.
  • Surgical Placement Dr. David Kelly places the implant(s) with precision and care—often with little discomfort.
  • Healing & Osseointegration The implant integrates with your jawbone over 3–6 months for strong, stable support.
  • Custom Restoration We attach a crown, bridge, or denture designed to match your bite and smile.
  • Ongoing Care We’ll schedule follow-ups and provide long-term maintenance guidance.

Bone Grafting and Implant Preparation

If you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone for implants, you may still be a candidate. Dr. David Kelly offers in-house bone grafting to rebuild lost jawbone and prepare for successful implant placement.

We’ll walk you through every step and ensure you understand your options fully.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort, similar to a simple extraction. We use gentle techniques and local anesthetic to help keep you comfortable throughout the process.

How long do implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 15 years or longer, and in many cases, they can last a lifetime.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Some insurance plans offer partial coverage. We will review your benefits and financing options during your consultation.

What if I need to replace several teeth?

You may benefit from an implant bridge or an implant-supported denture, which can restore multiple teeth efficiently.

How do I maintain my dental implant?

Brush, floss, and visit us regularly, just as you would with natural teeth.
