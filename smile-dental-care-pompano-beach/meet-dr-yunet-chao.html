Yunet Chao, DMD

Dr. Yunet Chao is a distinguished dentist whose career blends international expertise with a deep passion for patient care. She earned her dental degree from the Faculty of Dentistry at Raul Gonzalez Sanchez University in Havana, Cuba, followed by a residency in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Calixto Garcia General Hospital. Her professional journey continued in Bogotá, Colombia, where she refined her skills as a general dentist and oral surgeon before moving to the United States to complete her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in New Jersey.

Dedicated to excellence, Dr. Chao completed a Fellowship in Dental Implants and became a proud Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI). She is also certified by the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics (AAFE) in Botox and dermal fillers, allowing her to harmonize dental care with facial aesthetics. Known for her gentle touch and artistic eye, Dr. Chao believes dentistry is not just about restoring teeth—it’s about restoring confidence, comfort, and joy.
