What are the Benefits?

Dental implants offer numerous advantages compared to traditional tooth replacement options, including:

  • Natural Appearance and Feel: Dental implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Because they anchor into the jawbone, they provide a stable and permanent solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring the aesthetics of your smile.
  • Improved Functionality: Dental implants offer improved stability, unlike traditional dentures, which can slip or move while eating or speaking. And, with dental implants, you can bite, chew, and speak with confidence without worrying about discomfort or embarrassment.
  • Longevity: Dental implants can endure a lifetime with appropriate care and maintenance. Further, unlike alternative tooth replacement methods, they offer durability and resilience against decay. They are a lasting solution for missing teeth.
  • Convenience and Comfort: Dental implants eliminate the need for messy adhesives or special cleaning procedures associated with traditional dentures. Once in place, dental implants require no special maintenance. You can care for them just like natural teeth, making them a convenient and comfortable tooth replacement option.

The Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process typically encompasses several stages, including:

  • Initial Consultation: During your first appointment, Dr. Scott Lindsay will assess your oral health, discuss your objectives, and determine your candidacy for dental implants in Centennial.
  • Treatment Planning: Next, Dr. Lindsay will develop a personalized treatment plan based on your specific needs and preferences. This plan will detail the number of implants required, the type of restoration, and the time it takes for treatment.
  • Implant Placement: We surgically position dental implants into the jawbone through a minor surgical procedure. Our practice utilizes advanced techniques and cutting-edge technology to guarantee accurate placement and optimal results.
  • Osseointegration: Following implant placement, osseointegration occurs as the implants gradually fuse with the jawbone. This crucial process typically takes several months to complete.
  • Restoration: Once you achieve osseointegration, we will affix the final restorations, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures, to the implants. This step restores the appearance of your smile.
