Meet the Doc

Dr. Lindsay’s goal is to partner with you, his patient, to help you achieve the level of oral health you desire. He strives to help each patient maintain or restore the comfort, function, and aesthetics of their chewing systems. Dr. Lindsay is committed to continually seeking further education and training so he can provide exceptional care using the best possible techniques, materials, and technology. The ultimate goal is to help patients reach and maintain a level of oral health where they do not need dental work.

Dr. Lindsay and his wife, Mindy, have been blessed with five children, three boys and two girls. He loves the outdoors and enjoys golfing, camping, hiking, fishing, skiing, snowboarding, scuba diving, wakeboarding, wake surfing, mountain biking, dirt biking, and playing his guitar. Believing in community service, he enjoys serving in the Boy Scout program. He has also been involved in providing dental care to needy individuals in third world countries.
