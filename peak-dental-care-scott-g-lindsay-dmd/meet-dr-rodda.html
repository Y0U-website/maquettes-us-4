Dr. Rodda, DDS, MAGD, FICOI

Meet the Doc

Dr. Rodda graduated from The State University of New York at Buffalo with his undergraduate degree in 2007, and earned his doctorate from the School of Dental Medicine in 2011. Immediately following dental school, he completed an intensive General Practice Residency at Wishard Memorial Hospital in Indianapolis, IN. During this residency, he gained extensive experience in advanced surgical and restorative procedures, managing medically complex patients, and I.V. sedation training.

A dedicated lifelong learner, Dr. Rodda has been a member of the SPEAR Study Club and a frequent attendee at national meetings for the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. His hard work culminated in earning Mastership status (MAGD) with the Academy of General Dentistry—a prestigious distinction achieved by less than 2 % of dentists nationwide. He was also awarded an Associate Fellow status from the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Outside of the clinic, Dan and Maria have always loved traveling to Colorado for skiing, snowboarding, hiking, and mountain biking. They fell so deeply in love with the mountains and outdoor lifestyle that they decided to officially make Colorado their permanent home! We are incredibly proud to add a clinician of Dr. Rodda’s caliber to our team. His advanced training in oral surgery, implants, cosmetics, and complex restorative work will allow us to offer even more comprehensive care right here in our office.
