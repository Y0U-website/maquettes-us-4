The practice

We offer a wide variety of cosmetic dentistry services, from veneers and crowns to enamel shaping, to help you obtain the smile you dream of.

We provide same day emergency care and Dr. Van Buskirk is available 24/7 for patients of record.

We offer a variety of dental filling materials, and we even have "shot-less" laser decay removal. (not all decay qualifies)

We fabricate custom bleaching trays for controlled bleaching with great results. We offer the trays at an affordable cost because Dr. Van Buskirk believes everyone should be able to bleach if they desire.

We diagnose and treat inflammation of the gums (gingivitis) to the more advanced stages of periodontal disease. In some cases, gum tissue grafting and reduction, along with laser treatment can be preformed.

We provide root canal treatments to save your natural teeth. Root canal treatments are preformed under microscopy with the ability to view treatment through video.

We replace missing teeth with implants that look and feel just like your natural teeth.

Digital X-Rays provide the very best image with the least radiation and time. We also have a 3D X-Ray for diagnostic purposes.
