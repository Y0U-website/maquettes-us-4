About Ever Smile Dental

A Michigan practice built on trust, clinical excellence, and a genuine love for the people we serve.

Dr. Divya Arvind

Driven by a passion for lifelong learning and evidence-based dentistry, Dr. Arvind pursued advanced research and earned her PhD from the University of Hong Kong, one of the world&#x27;s leading dental institutions. She was honored with the prestigious United Nations Millennium Development Goals Scholarship and has published numerous scientific papers, presented internationally, and served as a peer reviewer for respected dental journals.

After moving to the United States, she completed her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree at the University of Illinois Chicago. Dr. Arvind has advanced training in Invisalign, Botox, and dermal fillers through the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE).

She is passionate about cosmetic dentistry, smile transformations, and helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. Outside the office, Dr. Arvind enjoys spending time with her family and traveling.
