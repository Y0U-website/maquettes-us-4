Doctor

As a Board Certified Orthodontic Specialist (and all-around tooth nerd), I am passionate about crafting beautiful smiles. No cookie-cutter approaches here — we utilize the latest, state-of-the-art technology and methods to provide treatment that is comfortable and customized to your needs. Whether you’re a parent looking for an affordable way to give your child the confidence of their best smile, or an adult wondering if now is the time to pursue your smile transformation — from the most complex situations to the smallest tune-ups, we exist to serve you.

Husband & Father

Artist

Ever since I can remember I’ve been drawn to art and creating things — the artistic aspects of smile design are what particularly drew me to the orthodontic specialty. It’s humbling to have the honor of participating in creating something as personal and significant as the smile you wear for the rest of your life! We take this approach with each unique person, we believe that you deserve a smile that lights up the room.

Far beyond teeth, getting to know the person behind the smile and the aspects of their lives that spark those smiles is my WHY and the real purpose for being in this profession!
