Dr. Steele & Dr. Saxon

Both graduates of the University of Mississippi Medical Center School of Dentistry, both raising their families in this community.

John “Anthony” Steele II, DMD

Dr. Steele graduated from Neshoba Central High School in 2001, earned his degree in Chemistry from the University of Mississippi in 2005, and completed his DMD at the University of Mississippi Medical Center School of Dentistry in 2010. He has owned and operated Steele Family Dental since 2011, and in December of 2019 he partnered with Dr. Anna Clare Saxon to build Airpark Family Dentistry from the ground up.

He has been married to Jennifer Leigh since 2009, and together they have two children. Outside the office he’s an avid runner and competes in marathons.

Anna Clare Saxon, DMD

Dr. Saxon graduated from Leake Academy in 2009, earned her degree in Biology from Mississippi College in 2013, and completed her DMD at the University of Mississippi Medical Center School of Dentistry in 2017. She joined Dr. Steele to open Airpark Family Dentistry, a practice built with many prayers, love and determination.

She has been married to Brian since 2012 and they have three children. When she isn’t in the office, she loves traveling with her family.

The rest of your care team.

Between them, decades of experience — and a lot of patients who have become friends.

Jennifer Leigh Steele

A Registered Nurse since 2004, Jennifer moved into dentistry in 2011 when she and her husband co‑founded Steele Family Dental. What she values most are “the relationships we get to make with patients” — she’s watched families grow up over more than a decade.

Nan Graham, RDH

Nan graduated from the University of Mississippi Medical Center in 1992. She and her husband Jeff have two grown children. After three decades in hygiene, she says her patients “have truly become like family to me.”

Tori Warren, RDH

Tori graduated from Meridian Community College in 2015. She and her husband have two children. The best part of the job, she says, is “getting to know so many wonderful people.”

Emily Crosswhite, RDH

Emily graduated from the University of Mississippi in 2009 with a degree in Biology and a minor in Chemistry, then completed Dental Hygiene at the University of Mississippi Medical Center in 2011. She lives in Philadelphia with her husband Chad and their children, Hudson and Hayden, and enjoys travel and family activities.

Ericka Sullivan, DA

Ericka graduated from Delta Tech Dental Assisting School in 2016. She’s motivated by helping patients get out of pain and feel confident in their smile again by “restoring their oral health.”

Sally Winstead
