Your first appointment, step by step.

1. Check in Hand us your completed paperwork and insurance card. We verify your benefits so you know where you stand before treatment.

2. Exam & X‑rays A full new patient exam — teeth, gums, bite and soft tissue — plus any imaging needed to see what’s below the surface.

3. Your plan We walk you through what we found, what’s urgent versus what can wait, and what it will cost — in plain language.

4. Next visit Cleaning, treatment or simply a six‑month recall — we schedule it before you leave so it doesn’t slip.
