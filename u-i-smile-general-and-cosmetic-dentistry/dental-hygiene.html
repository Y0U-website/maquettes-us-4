Dental Hygiene

Dental Hygiene services offered in Beaverton, OR

Taking good care of your dental hygiene not only protects your teeth but also supports whole-body health, reducing your risk of heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory infections . At U & I Smile in Beaverton, Oregon, Lilian Liu, DMD, and the team provide expert dental hygiene care, including regular cleanings, along with vital oral screenings and education. Call U & I Smile or request an appointment online today to prioritize your overall wellness, starting with a healthy smile.

Why is dental hygiene so important?

Keeping your mouth clean and healthy has obvious benefits, including preventing cavities, tooth loss, and gum disease, and ensuring better breath. Few people realize how vital oral hygiene is to their overall health, since bacteria in the mouth can spread throughout the body.

When you prioritize your dental hygiene, you also protect yourself from:

At U & I Smile, Dr. Liu and the team use the latest technology while providing unhurried, personalized appointments.

  • Cardiovascular disease
  • Respiratory issues
  • Pregnancy complications
  • Diabetes

How often should I schedule dental hygiene appointments?

Generally speaking, you should book a dental hygiene visit every six months. This takes care of your routine oral checks and maintenance, and catches issues early.

Of course, everyone is different. Depending on your needs and risk factors, Dr. Liu may recommend coming in for more frequent appointments.

What can I expect from professional dental hygiene care?

When you have a dental hygiene appointment scheduled at U & I Smile, Dr. Liu and the team perform a comprehensive examination and cleaning while you relax in a comfortable chair.

A hygienist uses specialized instruments to remove plaque and tartar buildup on your teeth, then polishes them with a gritty paste to remove debris while protecting your enamel. They check your mouth and teeth for cavities, swelling, or other signs of oral health concerns, and finish with flossing and rinsing so you can fully enjoy your fresh, clean smile.

A typical appointment takes less than an hour, and Dr. Liu will let you know when to return for follow-ups, whether for preventive care or to address a specific issue.

How can I practice good dental hygiene at home?

Dr. Liu can give you personalized recommendations on how to care for your smile between visits, including:

Daily habits

Flossing, brushing, and using mouthwash prevent buildup and keep bacteria in check. Changing up the type of toothbrush you use and brushing more frequently can also help.

Lifestyle adjustments

Acidic or sugary snacks and drinks can attack your oral health, so limiting them (along with tobacco use) makes dental hygiene easier.
