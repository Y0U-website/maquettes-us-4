About U & I Smile

General Dentistry located in Beaverton, OR

At U & I Smile in Beaverton, Oregon, we combine modern technology with unrushed appointments that give you the time and care you deserve. Led by skilled dentist Lilian Liu, DMD, our team creates a welcoming environment where families and individuals of all ages can enjoy better oral health and a healthy, beautiful smile.

We offer a full range of services to support your long-term oral health, including preventive dental hygiene visits and digital X-rays , as well as carefully crafted fillings, crowns , and bridges . We also provide gentle and effective gum disease treatments, toothache relief, and custom night guards to protect your smile.

If you’re looking to enhance or restore your teeth, we offer clear aligners, professional teeth whitening , dentures, and dental implant restorations, and meticulously tailor your care for natural-looking, lasting results. Children can also learn proper hygiene and oral health habits that last a lifetime through pediatric dentistry from our caring team.

Our inclusive office provides services in English and Mandarin to meet the community’s needs. No matter your dental goals, we’re committed to providing effective, personalized care. To get started, call U & I Smile or book your next visit online today.
