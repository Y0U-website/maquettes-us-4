  • Professional Recognition Dr. Chen has been recognized for his commitment to mentorship of the next generation of dentists, serving the community, and pursuit of excellence and ethics in dentistry. He has been appointed a Fellow of the American College of Dentists and a Fellow of the Pierre Fauchard Academy , of which membership to these organizations is by invitation only. Dr. Chen felt honored, as he was nominated by dentists he has looked up to since he was a first-year dental student.
  • Academic Honors Dr. Chen is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists . Achieving this award requires completing a minimum of one hundred hours of Implant Education and presenting twenty completed implant cases for the ICOI board to review and approve, and an endorsement from an accredited member of the organization. He is also a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry , a credential which only 6 % of general dentists have achieved. This award requires a minimum of 500 hours of continuing education and passing a comprehensive 400-question exam.
