Cosmetic Care Menu

As your concierge cosmetic dentist, Dr. Lilya delivers beautifully tailored solutions uniquely designed to fit you and your smile.

Cosmetic Dentistry 1:08 Cosmetic Smile Makeover From your first visit to a complete smile makeover, Dr. Lilya helps patients achieve the smile of their dreams.

Dental Implant Restoration

Having issues with your existing dental implants? With clinical precision and artistic elegance, Dr. Lilya can restore the strength, function, and beauty of your dental implants.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Implant retained dentures are the perfect solution for denture wearers who want a more permanent solution. Dr. Lilya uses strategically placed dental implants to create a customized, implant-retained denture that snaps securely into place. The result is amazing stability that restores natural function.

Invisalign® and Invisalign® Teen Orthodontic Solutions

Virtually invisible and removable, Invisalign’s clear aligners help patients achieve a beautifully straight smile without the challenge of traditional braces.

Porcelain Veneers

If you want to quickly reinvent and reshape your smile, porcelain veneers may be the solution for you. These custom-designed, tooth-shaped porcelain wafers are bonded to your front teeth to give you a smile worthy of Hollywood.

KoR Professional Teeth Whitening

KoR is the most effective and revolutionary professional whitening system in the world. Let your white shine with both Take-Home and In-Office professional whitening solutions. Your brighter, whiter smile awaits.

Composite Veneers

Composite veneers are made of translucent resin and are meticulously sculpted to your teeth. The result is an immediate and remarkable makeover of your teeth color, size, and shape.

Gum Contouring and Crown Lengthening

Your gums establish the framework for your smile. If you have excess gum tissue, your teeth may appear shorter than normal. Dr. Lilya uses gum contouring to reshape your gumline to create the smile you desire.

Dental Crown

Dr. Lilya can design and place a crown to permanently cover a decayed, damaged or cracked tooth. With proper care, crowns are designed to last for life.

Dental Bridge

If you are missing teeth, Dr. Lilya can custom fit a dental bridge to fill a space that your tooth previously occupied.

Composite Bonding

Composite bonding with Dr. Lilya beautifully repairs chipped or cracked teeth, reduces gaps or spaces between teeth, and hides discoloration or faded areas on the tooth’s surface.
