Your First Visit

Your Experience

We take the time to get to know you, using advanced software and technology to review your information in a comfortable and efficient way. Your new-patient visit includes a comprehensive exam and records with the doctor, along with a gentle cleaning using airflow technology and a gum assessment by our hygienist. Together, we’ll use your oral-health data to create a customized plan designed to maximize the longevity of your teeth and gums.

Our Expertise

Once we fully understand your smile goals, we’ll discuss personalized treatment options to help you achieve them. We encourage you to ask questions at any point—your clarity and comfort matter to us. You can expect complete transparency as we review your options and share our recommendations.

We’ll Take Care of the Details

If you have dental insurance you’d like to use, our team can provide a complimentary benefits check and explain the coverage your plan offers in our practice. For patients without dental insurance, we also offer an annual membership option to help you stay on top of your oral health year-round.
