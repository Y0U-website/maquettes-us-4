Affordable Dental Care You Can Rely On

Patient Information

When it comes to making informed decisions about your dental care, you can rely on Peach Street Dental & Dentures to help every step of the way. Whether you’re coming in for routine teeth cleaning, dentures, dental implants, or dental crowns, we’re here to answer your questions and help you afford your treatment with our financing options.

Located in Erie, PA, our dental clinic welcomes patients from throughout Erie County and the surrounding area.

Dental & Denture Discounts

We offer interest-free payments, 10 % senior discounts, and free exams with x-rays for new patients over the age of 18. We also offer immediate same-day dentures at the time of tooth extractions. You can request more information when scheduling your appointment.

Payment & Insurance Information

We accept most insurance plans. We also accept Visa, MasterCard, Amex, Discover, cash, personal checks, and financing through Care Credit, Lending Club, GreenSky, and third party lenders. If you do not see your insurance provider below, give us a call at (814) 866-7500
