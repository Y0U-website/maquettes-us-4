0 Meet Dr. Kevin Lake Dr. Lake earned his Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences with a minor in American Literature from Rutgers University–New Brunswick in 1992. To help offset the cost of his education, he worked as a phlebotomist in the Department of Pathology at both St. Barnabas Hospital in Livingston and Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital in New Brunswick.

He went on to complete his dental education at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey (now Rutgers School of Dental Medicine), graduating in 1998 with a Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree. During his senior year, Dr. Lake was elected class president, inducted into the Prosthodontic Honor Society, and recognized with multiple scholarships and awards, including the prestigious Law and Ethics in Dentistry Award.

Over the past 25 years in practice, Dr. Lake has continued to expand his expertise in digital, CAD/CAM, and esthetic dentistry through continuing education with Planmeca Academy in Dallas and Chicago. He is currently pursuing advanced training in digital prosthodontics through 3Shape Academy.

Outside of his professional life, Dr. Lake enjoys spending time at the Jersey Shore and enthusiastically supporting his sons in youth sports.
