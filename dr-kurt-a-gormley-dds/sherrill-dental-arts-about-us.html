MEET OUR TEAM

Dr. Gormley was born and raised in Carthage, New York. He graduated from Carthage Central High School in 1983. He went on to attend St. Lawrence University where he graduated with a B.S. Degree in Chemistry. He then continued his education and obtained his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in 1991.

After graduation, Dr. Gormley relocated to Utica N.Y. to complete a one-year residency at Faxton-St. Luke’s Dental Center in New Hartford. He started his dental career in Oneida before becoming a partner with Dr. Joseph Hawellca in Sherrill N.Y. in 1994.

He currently lives locally in Oneida, NY with his wife Kimberly and their two daughters Lillian and Mackenzie. Dr. Gormley loves spending time with family, outdoor sports, and traveling.

Dr. Clark grew up in many places, including Utah, Idaho, Oregon, and Georgia. He received a bachelor’s degree in Exercise Science from Brigham Young University, and his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health in 2010.

After graduating, Dr. Clark went on to become a dental director in dental clinics in Indiana, West Virginia, and Syracuse. He also serves as a Major in the United States Air Force Reserves at the Niagara Falls Air Reserve Station. He is excited to join the team at Sherrill Dental Arts!

He currently lives in Manlius, NY with his wife, Sarah and their five children, three dogs, and seven chickens.
