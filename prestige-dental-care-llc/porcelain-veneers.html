  • From there, Dr. Ilic will discuss whether veneers are the best option for achieving your desired results or if we need to take another route before starting this process.
  • Custom Design and Planning
  • Once evaluated, our expert team will make a road map for your treatment, ensuring the veneers align with your goals and the natural appearance of your teeth. We take precise measurements and create diagnostic molds to visualize the outcome before doing anything on your actual teeth.
  • Preparation & Temporary Veneers
  • Once you’re satisfied with the design plan, we prepare your teeth for the veneers by gently reshaping them. Temporary veneers are then placed, allowing you to experience the look and feel of your new smile throughout the trial period.
  • Fabrication by High-End Aesthetic Labs
  • During the trial period, the details gathered help create your permanent veneers, fabricated by renowned aesthetic labs known for their craftsmanship and use of high-quality materials. This guarantees your veneers will be both beautiful and durable.
  • Final Placement & Follow-up
  • The final step involves delicately placing and bonding the custom veneers to your teeth, culminating in a dazzling smile transformation.
