Dr. Olcese is a graduate of the University of Southern California Dental School, a member of the American Dental Association and the NC Dental Society. He is also fluent in English and Spanish.

Dr. Olcese started serving patients in Wadesboro, NC in 2011, at this very practice, and has remained dedicated to serving the community despite multiple changes in practice ownership.
