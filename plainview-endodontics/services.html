Our Featured Services

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy is a restorative procedure designed to save teeth that are severely damaged or infected. Using advanced techniques and rotary instruments, Dr. Tishbi carefully removes infected pulp, cleans and shapes the canal, and seals the tooth — relieving pain and preserving your natural smile.

Second Chance 02 Root Canal Retreatment When a previous root canal fails to heal or becomes re-infected, retreatment gives the tooth a second chance. Dr. Tishbi removes the prior filling material, carefully cleans the canals again, and re-seals the tooth — often rescuing it from extraction.

Surgical 03 Apicoectomy When conventional root canal therapy is not enough, an apicoectomy provides a surgical solution. A small incision allows Dr. Tishbi to access the root tip, remove infected tissue, and place a filling — resolving infection where instruments cannot reach.

Advanced 04 Regenerative Endodontics Regenerative endodontics uses the body&#x27;s own healing mechanisms to replace damaged or missing pulp tissue. This biologically-based approach is particularly effective for immature permanent teeth, promoting continued root development and long-term tooth survival.

Additional Services

Beyond core procedures, we offer a full range of specialized treatments to address every endodontic need.

CBCT Scan 3D cone beam CT imaging for precise diagnosis — revealing anatomy standard X-rays cannot capture.

Endodontic Consultation A thorough evaluation of your dental pain, symptoms, and imaging to develop a personalized treatment plan.

Cracked Tooth Diagnosis and treatment of cracked teeth causing pain, sensitivity, or structural compromise.

Tooth Injuries Emergency and restorative care for teeth fractured, displaced, or knocked out due to trauma or accidents.

Root Resorption Diagnosis and management of root resorption — where the body breaks down and reabsorbs root structure.

Apexification A procedure performed alongside root canal therapy to promote apical closure in immature permanent teeth.

Internal Bleaching A whitening treatment applied from inside a discolored tooth — restoring natural color after root canal therapy.

Post Removal Safe removal of dental posts from previously treated teeth to allow for retreatment or restoration.
