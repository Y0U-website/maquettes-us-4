Meet Dr. Clegg

Born and raised in Salt Lake City, Sandy and Farmington, Utah, Dr. David Clegg served an LDS mission in Munich, Germany before attending the University of Utah to study medical biology and German. After four years at the U of U, he was accepted to the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine and graduated from UB in 2009. Dave now resides in Herriman where he loves spending time with his wife and children. He enjoys the outdoors, most sports (especially basketball), good food, and going to the movies.
