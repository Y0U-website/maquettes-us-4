Frequently Asked Questions

What services does your office provide?

Our office offers a comprehensive range of dental services, including general dentistry (such as cleanings, exams, and fillings), cosmetic dentistry, restorative dentistry, orthodontics, periodontics & advanced procedures. Call today to learn more!

Do you accept new patients?

Yes, we warmly welcome new patients of all ages! Please contact us to schedule your first appointment , and we’ll be happy to guide you through the initial steps.

What are your office hours?

We are open Monday and Tuesday from 7:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Wednesday from 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM, Thursday from 7:30am to 5pm and every other Friday from 8am-3pm. Please feel free to contact us to confirm availability or to schedule an appointment during these hours.

What are your office hours?

If you have an emergency, call us at (704) 829-3700 to schedule an emergency appointment. We’ll do everything we can to see you on the same day.

What happens during a general dentistry checkup?

During a general dentistry checkup, we will thoroughly examine your teeth, gums, and mouth to assess your oral health. This includes an evaluation for cavities, gum disease, and any other dental concerns. We may also take X-rays if needed and finish with a professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar for a fresh, healthy smile.

What are my options if I need to reschedule or cancel my appointment?

If you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment, please call our office as soon as possible. We ask for at least 24 hours notice. We’ll be happy to work with you to find a more convenient time that suits your schedule.
