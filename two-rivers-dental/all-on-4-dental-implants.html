All-on-4® Dental Implants

  • Reduced Need for Bone Grafting : The strategic placement of the implants often negates the need for bone grafting, which is commonly required in traditional implant procedures.
  • Enhanced Stability and Comfort : Fixed implants offer greater stability compared to removable dentures, reducing discomfort and the risk of slippage.
  • Improved Oral Health : By replacing missing teeth, All-on-4® implants help maintain jawbone density and prevent further bone loss.

The All-on-4 ® Procedure

The All-on-4® procedure typically involves the following steps:

  • Consultation and Planning : A thorough examination, including digital imaging and 3D scans, is conducted to assess bone structure and plan the implant placement.
  • Implant Placement : Under local anesthesia or sedation, four titanium implants are surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Temporary Prosthesis : A temporary set of teeth is attached to the implants, allowing patients to leave the office with a functional smile.
  • Healing Period : Over the next few months, the implants integrate with the bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Permanent Restoration : Once healing is complete, a custom-made permanent prosthesis is placed, providing a natural-looking and fully functional set of teeth.

Considerations and Potential Risks

While All-on-4® dental implants offer numerous advantages, it’s essential to consider potential risks and limitations:

  • Suitability : Not all patients are candidates for All-on-4® implants. Adequate bone density and overall oral health are crucial for the success of the procedure.
  • Post-Operative Care : Following the procedure, patients may experience swelling, discomfort, or bruising, which usually subside within a few days.
