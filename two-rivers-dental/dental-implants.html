Dental Implants

Long-Lasting Solution: With proper care, implants can last decades, making them a cost-effective tooth replacement option.

  • Restore Chewing Function: Implants allow you to enjoy a full diet comfortably.
  • Enhance Smile Aesthetics: Custom restorations look and feel like natural teeth, improving confidence.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants may be right for you if you are missing one or more teeth and have sufficient jawbone density. At Two Rivers Dental in Belmont, NC, we evaluate your oral health, medical history, and personal goals to create a custom treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Our Dental Implant Process

1. Consultation & Planning: Digital x-rays and 3D scans help us design a precise treatment plan. 2. Surgery & Implant Placement: Guided implant placement ensures accuracy, and bone grafting is performed if necessary. 3. Temporary Teeth: Many patients leave with temporary teeth on the same day. 4. Healing & Restoration: After several months of healing, your permanent crown, bridge, or full arch restoration is placed for optimal function and aesthetics.
