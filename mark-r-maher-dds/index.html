The practice

For over 20 years, Dr. Mark Maher’s practice has provided compassionate, conservative dental care to families in Walnut Creek. A graduate of the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, Dr. Maher believes in preserving your natural smile through the least invasive treatments possible.

His philosophy is simple: listen first, treat conservatively, and always put patient comfort at the center of every decision.

Preventive & Diagnostic Dentistry Routine care and early detection to help you keep your natural teeth healthy for the long run.

Restorative Dentistry Natural-looking repairs and replacements when teeth are damaged, infected, worn, or missing.

Cosmetic Dentistry & Orthodontics Smile-focused care for brighter teeth, better proportions, and straighter alignment.
