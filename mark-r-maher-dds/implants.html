Dental Implants & Implant…

Dr. Maher uses 3D scanning and printing extensively for design and fabrication of appliances including implant surgical guides for precise, predictable placement.

  • 02 Bone & tissue assessment Over time, missing teeth can cause the surrounding tissue including jaw bone to atrophy. If your jaw bone has started to atrophy, we can consider bone and tissue grafts. Dr. Maher will discuss those options with you.
  • 03 Surgical placement Once you and Dr. Maher have determined that dental implants are right for you, you’ll be referred to a skilled oral surgeon to have the titanium post implanted. Titanium is a bio-compatible metal, which allows it to eventually become a part of your jaw without the risk of sensitivity or rejection.
  • 04 CEREC crown restoration Dr. Maher uses the CEREC system to create a beautiful, natural-looking crown perfectly customized to match your own teeth, and ready the same day. Working with you using pictures and photographs, he designs a crown that blends in flawlessly. Once ready, it’s attached to your post and your restoration is complete.

A natural, lasting result

Implant restoration replaces missing teeth with permanent crowns that look and function exactly like your own natural teeth, restored to their full functionality without compromising your remaining teeth the way a bridge might.
