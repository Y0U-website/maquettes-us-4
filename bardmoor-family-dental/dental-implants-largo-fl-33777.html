Procedure

The journey towards dental implants can be a life-changing experience, tailored to meet your individual needs and goals. This is a peek at the process:

Our compassionate team will guide and support you throughout the entire procedure.

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Abdelghani spends time understanding your dental concerns and goals. He will conduct a thorough examination and create a customized treatment plan that is based on your oral health.
  • Implant Placement: With precision and care titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as strong anchors for new teeth. This phase of surgery is the basis for a lifetime full of confidence.
  • Integration and Healing: As the implants fuse to the jawbone, over several months, the process is known as osseointegration. Temporary restorations can be used to maintain your comfort and confidence during this period.
  • Abutment Attachment : After osseointegration, abutments can be attached to implants. This allows for a secure connection between implants and final restorations.
  • Final Restoration : This is the moment when custom-made crowns, dentures, bridges, or other restorations are attached to the abutments.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer benefits that go beyond aesthetics. They are a great way to improve your oral health. Here are some of the reasons why dental implants are widely considered to be the best option for replacing missing teeth:

Dental Implants seamlessly blend in with your natural teeth. This gives you a lifelike look and feel that will restore your confidence and improve your quality of living.

Unlike traditional bridges that rely on adjacent tooth support, dental implants don’t. This preserves the integrity of those teeth and minimizes future complications.

With dental implants, you don’t have to worry about discomfort or slippage. You can enjoy your favorite foods, and your favorite activities with confidence knowing that your smile will be secure and stable.

By stimulating the jawbone, and preventing bone loss dental implants promote optimal oral health and well-being. They also ensure the longevity of your smile.
