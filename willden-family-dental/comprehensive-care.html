Restorative Care

Sometimes teeth need repair—and when they do, our goal is to restore function, appearance, and long-term comfort using techniques that are effective, personalized, and grounded in evidence-based care.

At Willden Family Dental, we offer a range of restorative options to help you move forward with confidence, whether you’re dealing with cavities, cracked teeth, or missing teeth. Together, we’ll find the solution that makes the most sense for your health, lifestyle, and goals.

Restorative dentistry is about more than fixing problems—it’s about helping you feel comfortable, confident, and cared for. We’ll walk you through every option so you can make informed decisions at your own pace.

  • Dental Fillings Used to restore teeth affected by decay. We remove the damaged portion and fill the space using composite resin (tooth-colored) or other appropriate materials to restore form and function.
  • Dental Crowns Custom-made caps that cover and protect weakened or damaged teeth. Crowns are commonly used for fractures, large fillings, or after root canal therapy to restore both durability and aesthetics.
  • Dental Bridges Bridges are used to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth (pontics) to neighboring teeth or implants. They help restore chewing ability and maintain proper tooth alignment.
  • Dentures We offer both full and partial removable dentures to replace missing teeth. These custom appliances are designed for comfort, function, and a natural appearance—whether you’re missing a few teeth or an entire arch.
  • Dental Implants Implants provide a stable, long-lasting replacement for missing teeth. Surgically placed into the jawbone, they support crowns, bridges, or dentures and can feel much like your natural teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Whether you’re looking to brighten your smile or correct minor imperfections, cosmetic dentistry at Willden Family Dental is always conservative, thoughtful, and personalized . Our goal isn’t to create a one-size-fits-all “perfect” smile—it’s to help you feel confident and at home in your own.

We offer select cosmetic treatments that can subtly enhance the appearance of your teeth while respecting the natural shape and function of your bite.

Curious whether cosmetic care might be a good fit for you? We’re happy to talk through your options and help you decide what feels right—no pressure, just support.

  • Teeth Whitening A professional-grade option to lighten tooth color and reduce surface stains , helping restore brightness with safe, predictable results.
  • Dental Veneers Thin, custom-crafted shells made of porcelain or composite resin that are bonded to the front of teeth. Veneers can improve color, shape, alignment, and symmetry —especially helpful for chips, discoloration, or gaps.
  • Dental Bonding A minimally invasive procedure where a tooth-colored resin is applied to repair minor chips, close small gaps, or even out discoloration. A great option for fast, affordable aesthetic improvement.
  • Smile Makeovers For patients interested in a more comprehensive transformation, we can build a customized treatment plan that blends cosmetic and restorative care to meet your goals—always with your health, comfort, and preferences in mind.

Additional Services

Oral health is about more than just teeth. At Willden Family Dental, we care for the full system that supports your smile—your gums, jaw joint, bone structure, and the surrounding tissues. These areas often affect both your comfort and your overall health, and we approach them with the same thoughtful, ethical care as everything else we do.

Some services we provide in-house; for others, we work closely with trusted specialists to ensure you’re receiving the highest level of care.

Your gums, jaw, and bone structure are foundational to your oral health. When these areas are overlooked, they can contribute to chronic pain, tooth loss, and even systemic conditions. We believe that caring for your smile means supporting the whole system it relies on—and we’re here to guide you every step of the way.

  • Periodontal Therapy (Gum Health) Healthy gums are essential to long-term oral and systemic health. We offer care for early and moderate stages of gum disease, including: Scaling & Root Planing , Laser Therapy , Personalized Support .
  • Scaling & Root Planing A deep cleaning that removes plaque and tartar above and below the gumline.
  • Laser Therapy A gentle, precise, and non-invasive treatment that targets diseased tissue, disinfects the area, and promotes healing—often without the need for anesthesia.
  • Personalized Support Nutrition guidance, hygiene tools, and individualized strategies to promote healing and prevent disease progression.
  • Minor Oral Surgery We provide simple extractions and other minor procedures when necessary. More complex surgical needs are referred to oral and maxillofacial specialists we trust.
  • TMD Treatment (Jaw Joint Support) We evaluate and manage mild to moderate cases of Temporomandibular Joint Disorders (TMD) —which can cause jaw pain, tension, or difficulty chewing—often with the use of custom mouthguards, exercises, or referrals to trusted providers.
  • Dentures Offered as part of our restorative services, we provide both full and partial removable dentures to replace missing teeth and restore daily function. For some patients, dentures may also be used in combination with implants for added stability.
