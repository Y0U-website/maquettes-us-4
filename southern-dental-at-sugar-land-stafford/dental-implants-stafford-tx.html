How Dental Implants Work: Step-by-Step

1. Consultation & Treatment Planning

We begin with digital imaging, an exam, and a review of your medical history. This helps us determine the best implant approach for your needs.

2. Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Most patients feel minimal discomfort thanks to modern anesthesia and gentle techniques.

3. Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next several months, the implant fuses with the jawbone, creating a strong foundation for your restoration.

4. Abutment Placement

Once healed, a small connector piece (the abutment) is attached to the implant.

5. Final Crown, Bridge, or Denture

A custom-made restoration is placed on the implant, completing your new smile.

Do Dental Implants Hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure. Afterward, mild soreness may occur for a few days. Most individuals return to normal activities within 24–48 hours. Because implants integrate with the bone, the long-term result feels completely natural
