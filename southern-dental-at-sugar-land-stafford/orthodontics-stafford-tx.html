Orthodontics Is Not Just for Kids

Although many people associate braces with middle and high school, orthodontic treatment is beneficial at any age . In fact, more adults than ever are choosing orthodontic care to enhance their smile and improve oral health.

At Southern Dental Associates – Sugar Land, we provide treatment for:

Children

Early evaluations help us identify developmental issues and create a plan to guide proper growth. Some children benefit from early interceptive orthodontics to prevent more complex issues later.

Teens

The teenage years are often the ideal time for braces or Invisalign because permanent teeth are fully erupted and easier to guide.

Adults

Adult orthodontics focuses on both cosmetic improvement and functional correction. Modern treatment options make orthodontics discreet, efficient, and comfortable for busy adults.

Orthodontic Treatment Options at Southern Dental Associates Sugar Land

We offer two primary orthodontic systems tailored to your needs, goals, and lifestyle.

Traditional Fixed Braces

Braces remain one of the most reliable and effective ways to treat complex alignment issues. They can correct severe crowding, significant bite discrepancies, and challenging cases that may not be suitable for clear aligners.

During treatment, a series of brackets and wires gradually guide teeth into their ideal positions. Adjustments occur regularly to ensure steady progress.

Once treatment is complete, retainers help maintain your new alignment and prevent relapse.

  • Metal braces – durable, efficient, and widely recommended
  • Ceramic (clear) braces – more aesthetic for teens and adults

Clear Aligner Treatment (Invisalign Ⓡ )

Clear aligners are one of today’s most popular orthodontic solutions because they can achieve beautiful results with convenience and discretion. Invisalign Ⓡ is ideal for mild to moderate alignment problems and offers a smooth, wire-free alternative to traditional braces.

Benefits of Clear Aligners

Patients wear each set of aligners for approximately two weeks before moving to the next in the series, allowing for controlled, gradual tooth movement.

  • Nearly invisible trays
  • Comfortable and smooth
  • Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing
  • No dietary restrictions
  • Fewer in-office appointments
