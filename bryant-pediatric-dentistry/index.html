Bryant Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric dentists are the pediatricians of teeth. They obtain specialty training in the specific needs of children just as pediatricians do.

The practice

Dr. Bryant completed 2 years of additional training after dental school to specialize in the unique needs of children.

She’s trained to treat infants, children, and adolescents, including those with special healthcare needs.

She’s trained to monitor and understand the growth of children’s skeletal and dental structures.

She specializes in understanding the comprehensive nature of your child’s oral and overall health.
