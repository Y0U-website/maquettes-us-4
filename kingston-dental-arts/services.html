General Dentistry

We are dedicated to helping our patients maintain a healthy mouth and smile through routine checkups, personalized care, and patient education. Oral health contributes to the health of your entire body. At Kingston Dental Arts, we are here to help you achieve a healthy mouth and pave the way for a healthier life.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry includes fillings and crowns to repair decayed and fractured teeth, and dental bridges to replace missing teeth and fill spaces between the teeth. Restorative dentistry can prevent tooth loss, improve esthetics and enhance your smile, restore function and comfort, and replace missing teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry & Facial Aesthetics

Everyone deserves to have a beautiful smile that they are confident to show off. We offer a variety of cosmetic treatments to help you achieve the smile you desire. Among the many cosmetic services we offer at Kingston Dental Arts are teeth whitening, Veneers, crowns and bridges, Invisalign, cosmetic bonding, dental implants, Botox therapy and dermal fillers. Our team will work with you to design your ideal smile.

Sedation Dentistry

Is dental fear or anxiety preventing you from achieving a healthy mouth or the smile you desire? We offer a range of sedation options in-office for adults, children, and special needs patients. Sedation options range from nitrous oxide or “laughing gas”, oral sedation, to IV sedation and General Anesthesia (administered by Greg Aprilliano, CRNA of Prime Anesthesia — please see www.primesedate.com for more information).

Pediatric Dentistry

Our team focuses on helping patients to establish good oral health habits that last a lifetime. Bringing your child to the dentist is important for desensitization, evaluation, and early intervention if necessary. It is important from an emotional standpoint to create positive experiences at the dental office at a young age, so the child is less likely to develop fear of the dentist. Our highly trained team will work with the child and parent to ensure good habits are being formed early on.

Oral Surgery

At Kingston Dental Arts, we perform oral surgery procedures including teeth extractions, dental implant placement, bone grafting/socket preservation, and crown lengthening surgery.
