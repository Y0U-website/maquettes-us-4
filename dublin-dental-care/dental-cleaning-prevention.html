Dental Sealants for Cavity Prevention

A dental sealant is a thin, plastic coating that is simply painted on parts of the chewing surfaces of teeth (usually the back teeth) to prevent cavities. The sealant bonds into the depressions and grooves in molars and pre-molars, forming a protective shield over the enamel of the tooth. Although thorough brushing and flossing can remove most food particles and plaque from smooth surfaces of teeth, they cannot always get into all the nooks and crannies of the back teeth to remove the food and plaque. Sealants protect these vulnerable areas from cavities by sealing out plaque and food.

Protect Teeth From the Development of Cavities

Require a Minimally Invasive Procedure to Place

Less Expensive than Having a Tooth Fixed

Made of a Clear or Slightly Colored Material that Matches Tooth Color

Dental sealants are generally recommended for our younger patients . However, we may recommend sealants for adult patients who tend to repeatedly develop cavities.

Fluoride Treatments Help Strengthen Tooth Enamel

Dublin Dental Care offers a wide variety of fluoride treatment methods to strengthen the enamel of our patients’ teeth. Fluoride is a natural mineral which bonds effectively with the enamel on our teeth. While fluoride is found in a lot of natural settings—such as drinking water—a professional application is considered to contribute significantly to strong oral health. We apply fluoride treatment to our patients during each standard appointment, and may recommend a strong application for patients who:

Exhibit Poor Oral Hygiene Habits

Commonly Experience Dry Mouth

Develop Small Cracks in Tooth Enamel

Have Gone Without a Regular Exam for a Prolonged Period of Time

Have Other Health Conditions that Affect Teeth

Are at Risk for Decay

Periodontal Treatment to Restore Gum Health

Dublin Dental Care offers periodontal treatment to restore the health of your gums. Gum Disease can have potentially devastating impacts on your teeth and overall health. Left unchecked, it can cause persistent bad breath, degenerated jaw tissue, and lost or shifting teeth. It can even elevate your risk of heart disease or stroke. Patients may have an increased risk of periodontal disease if they:

Have Contracted Certain Diseases or Illnesses

Are Elderly

Clench or Grind Teeth Constantly

Practice Poor Oral Hygiene Habits

Smoke Regularly

Have Diabetes

Take Some Forms of Medication

Have Not Had Professional Dental Care in an Extended Period of Time

Oral Cancer Screening

Dublin Dental Care screens for signs of oral cancer during every routine examination. Patients who smoke regularly, frequently drink alcohol, or have a history of oral cancers are at an increased risk. During every examination, our dental team will examine all areas of your mouth, face, and neck to uncover any signs of oral cancer. Common symptoms include:

Difficulty Chewing or Swallowing

Swelling of the Lips, Gums, and Other Areas of the Mouth

Rough Spots Found on Inside of Mouth

Numbness in the Mouth, Face, or Neck

Chronic Sore Throat

If you are concerned that you may have oral cancer, please contact our office immediately.
