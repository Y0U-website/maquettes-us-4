What the Dental Implant Process Looks Like

Dental implants offer an excellent, functional, and natural-looking solution for missing teeth. Once a dental implant is placed in the jawbone, it is typically allowed to heal for three to six months. This healing period enables the implant to securely bond with the jawbone before a crown, bridge, or denture is placed on it. During this time, our implant patients receive an appropriate temporary prosthesis (cap, crown, or denture) to cover the implant.

Once the implants are ready to be restored, our dentists at Dublin Dental Care will begin the process of creating and placing the final restoration. Using a very detailed three-dimensional model of your existing teeth and spaces, the replacement appliance or prosthetic device will provide a functional bite and a very natural-looking smile.
