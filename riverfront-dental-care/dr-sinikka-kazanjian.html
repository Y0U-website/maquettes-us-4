Dr. Sinikka Kazanjian

Dr. Kazanjian’s warm caring personality and clinical expertise have allowed her to form wonderful patient relationships in the Ocean County area for over twenty years. Born and raised in Piscataway, NJ, she earned her bachelors of science degree in pre dental science from Lehigh University and her doctor of dental medicine degree from the University of Pennsylvania in 2000. She then continued her training as chief resident at Monmouth Medical Center focusing on general practice and emergency dentistry. Finally, "Dr. K" settled in Toms River and has been happy to call the Jersey Shore home.

Passionate about patient care, Dr. Kazanjian’s goal is to provide treatment in a manner that will make patients feel relaxed and comfortable. She believes in working together with patients to achieve optimal dental health and a beautiful smile.

Dr. Kazanjian participates in numerous courses each year in her pursuit to stay current with the evolving techniques and technologies in the dental field. She is a member of the ADA, NJ DA and Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kazanjian enjoys spending time with her husband and their three children. Her most valued free time is spent with her family.
