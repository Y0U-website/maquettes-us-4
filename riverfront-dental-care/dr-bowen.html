Jeremy Bowen, D.M.D

Dr. Bowen

Dr. Bowen received his doctorate in dental medicine from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, where he graduated at the top of his class and was inducted into the Omnicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. He moved to the Ocean County area to complete a general practice residency at Jersey Shore University Medical Center, a Level I trauma center. There he gained advanced training in all aspects of restorative dental care.

Dr. Bowen is focused on patient communication, collaboration, and delivering high quality comprehensive dental care. His aim is to work with patients in a stress-free manner to help achieve their desired goals in a tailored approach that works best for them. He takes great pride in his work and hopes his patients leave their visits feeling like family. When he is not working, Dr. Bowen enjoys trying new restaurants, cooking, and occasionally playing a round of golf.
