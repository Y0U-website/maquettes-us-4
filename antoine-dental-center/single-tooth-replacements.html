Gum Disease

Another common problem associated with oral health is gum disease. Like tooth decay, gum disease is caused by bacteria in your mouth. The early stages of gum disease are hard to detect because the symptoms are very subtle. Slight gum irritation and bleeding may be the only things you notice, and there’s often no pain, so you may even write off the symptoms you recognize.

Unfortunately, many people don’t begin to suspect they have gum disease until it’s in its advanced stage, also known as periodontitis . At this stage, the gum pockets bulge and swell and become severely infected. At the same time, your gums recede away from your teeth. Your gums are part of your teeth’s foundation, so when your gum mass decreases, your teeth loosen and eventually fall out unless you undergo gum disease treatment early. Additionally, the bacteria ravaging your gums are also harmful to your teeth.

Our Tooth Replacement Options

Dental Implants

Dental implants function, look and feel like natural teeth. We implant a titanium post into the jawbone. After it heals, we place a dental crown on top. This restoration is a secure and sturdy tooth replacement with no diet restrictions. You will be able to handle all the dietary fiber you need to stay healthy and won’t have to deal with any slippage, as you might with dentures. This is often the best option if you are missing a single tooth.

Partial Dentures

Removable partial dentures are designed for patients missing some, but not all, teeth. They attach to your existing teeth and are a budget-friendly, non-surgical option.

Full Dentures

Complete dentures are used when all teeth in an arch (upper or lower) are missing. They rest on the gums and are removable. Today’s dentures are more comfortable and natural-looking than ever, though they may need adhesive and periodic adjustment.

Implant Supported Bridge

If you’re missing several teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can replace them without relying on natural teeth for support. Implants act as anchors for a multi-tooth restoration, offering more stability than traditional bridges.

Traditional Dental Bridge

A dental bridge fills the gap left by one or more missing teeth using neighboring natural teeth as anchors. It’s a good option if the surrounding teeth are healthy and strong, and it provides a functional, fixed solution.

All-on-X Implants (Full-Arch Restoration)

All-on-X is a permanent solution for patients missing most or all teeth in a single arch. Using 4 6 implants, a full set of teeth is securely anchored, offering the strength and appearance of natural teeth without the hassle of traditional dentures.
