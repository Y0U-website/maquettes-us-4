The Implant Placement Process

This simple surgical procedure follows a two-step process. The implants are titanium screw-like posts that replace the root of your natural tooth. Over a period of about four months , your jawbone heals around the implant and absorbs it. After the healing period, we can attach an abutment to the implant to hold your restoration in place firmly. These restorations include porcelain dental crowns, dental bridges, or dentures.

It s not an exaggeration to say that, when it comes to artificial teeth, nothing will be as easy or as comfortable as a dental implant. Our implant and crown combination feels and looks great. After the second part of the two-step procedure, you will recover real biting power. This means that you can go back to eating the foods you love, and the fiber-rich items that keep you healthy and happy.
