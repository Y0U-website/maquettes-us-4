All-On-X Dental Implants in Houston

All-on-X or Teeth in One Day is a full arch teeth replacement option that involves inserting a full arch prosthesis/denture to the mandible with four or more dental implants in one operation. This treatment intends to replace several hugely damaged teeth (or zero teeth totally). All-on-X enables you to receive a complete set of teeth without needing to go through several surgeries and restorations to install a complete set of implants within your mouth. The best thing about All-on-X treatment is that we can generally conduct it in our office in one day under anesthesia.

The outcome is a stable foundation of teeth that are created from Zirconia fixed into the dental implants in Houston. Compared to the metal as a core, Zirconia’s primary benefits are its biocompatibility, superior esthetics, and durability. The All-on-X procedure is FDA approved and has a long record of success.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are prostheses that replace a tooth from root to crown. Implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone for maximum strength and stability. After these posts naturally integrate into the jawbone, they’re topped with a natural-looking porcelain crown, dental bridge, or even a set of dentures.

1. What are affordable dental implants and how do they work?

Affordable dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. Once integrated with the bone, they support restorations such as crowns, bridges, or dentures, restoring the function and appearance of natural teeth.

3. What is the difference between All-on-4, All-on-6, and All-on-X implants?

  • All-on-4 uses four implants to support a full arch of teeth.
  • All-on-6 uses six implants for greater stability.
  • All-on-X (Teeth in a Day) uses four or more implants and provides same-day full-arch restoration.

4. How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime . The restoration (e.g., crown) typically lasts 10 15 years and may eventually need replacement due to wear.

7. What is the implant procedure like?

The entire process may take a few months, depending on individual healing.

  • Initial consultation
  • Implant placement (with sedation)
  • Healing period (3 6 months)
  • Final restoration placement

8. Do implants require special care?

No special care is needed beyond regular brushing, flossing, and biannual dental visits . However, maintaining good oral hygiene is essential to ensure the longevity of your implant.

9. What are the benefits of dental implants over dentures or bridges?

  • More stable and don’t slip
  • Preserve jawbone and prevent bone loss
  • Look, feel, and function like natural teeth
  • Long-lasting and low-maintenance

10. Can smoking affect my dental implants?

Yes. Smoking can negatively impact healing and increase the risk of implant failure. Patients who smoke may require additional evaluation and counseling before implant treatment.
