Different types of dental implants

All types of dental implant procedures share one common goal: to replace your missing tooth root. A dental implant is a titanium screw that is surgically implanted in your jaw; once the implant has healed it will integrate into your jawbone. We can then place a variety of dental restorations on a dental implant including crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Mini dental implants

Mini implants are smaller than traditional dental implants, which means that they aren’t as strong. Despite that drawback, they offer other advantages because they can generally be placed in a single visit, require less healing time, and can be used to immediately stabilize dentures.

If you are missing all of your teeth, you may be a candidate for All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants. These dental implant procedures use a set of 4 or 6 dental implants to support a set of permanent dental bridges.
