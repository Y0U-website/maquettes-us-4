Healthy teeth should be all one color. If you see spots and/or stains on the teeth bring your child to the office for a thorough exam and cleaning.

Most insurances cover at 100 % preventive exams, cleanings and topical fluoride application every 6 months.

Many serious dental conditions can go unnoticed during a standard visual exam, so we employ state of the art digital X-ray equipment in all exam rooms. Digital Panoramic X-ray equipment is available for a more thorough view of your child’s mouth.

This equipment emits significantly less radiation than conventional X-rays.
