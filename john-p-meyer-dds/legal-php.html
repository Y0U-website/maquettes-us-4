We have a strict non-spam policy and will pursue whatever legal steps are necessary to enforce this policy. "Spam," or "Spamming," is also known as Unsolicited Commercial Email (UCE) or Unsolicited Bulk Email (UBE).

Unsolicited Commercial Email is typically defined as the sending of any email message to anyone not personally known by you unless it is in specific response to a request for information from you. This definition is independent of how and by whom the email is actually sent.

Unsolicited Bulk Email is typically defined as the broadcasting of large quantities of unsolicited Email or postings to news groups through any large quantity delivery system. This definition is independent of how and by whom the email is actually sent.
