Dental hygiene

Proper dental hygiene includes fluoride treatments, gum care, digital X-rays, flossing and plaque cleaning.

  • How often should my family and I schedule dental hygiene appointments? It is generally recommended to schedule dental hygiene appointments every six months for routine check-ups and cleanings. However, the frequency may vary based on individual oral health needs. Consult with Dr. Rangel for personalized recommendations tailored to you and your family’s specific requirements.
  • What does a dental hygienist do during a routine cleaning for children and adults? Our skilled dental hygienists perform thorough cleanings, removing plaque and tartar, polishing teeth, and providing personalized oral hygiene education. For children, we focus on creating a positive and educational experience to establish lifelong dental habits.
  • How can I maintain good oral hygiene practices at home for my family? Our dental hygienists provide valuable tips on effective brushing, flossing, and overall oral care practices tailored to each family member. Consistent at-home care is essential for sustaining a healthy smile between visits.
  • Can dental hygienists identify potential dental issues during routine cleanings? Yes, dental hygienists play a crucial role in early detection. They are trained to identify signs of dental problems and can alert the dentist for further examination, helping to address issues proactively before they become more significant concerns.

Fillings

Fillings are most commonly used in order to prevent further damage to a tooth when decay is present. By removing the bacteria and closing off the affected area, no further tooth decay can occur.

  • Is it normal to experience sensitivity after getting a dental filling? It’s common to experience some sensitivity to hot or cold temperatures after a filling. This sensitivity usually subsides within a few days. If it persists or worsens, it’s essential to contact our office for further evaluation.
  • How long does a dental filling procedure typically take? The duration of a filling procedure depends on the size and location of the cavity. Generally, it takes about 30 minutes to an hour. Our skilled team works efficiently to ensure a comfortable and timely experience.
  • Can a filling be replaced if it becomes damaged or deteriorates over time? Yes, fillings can be replaced if they show signs of wear, damage, or decay. Regular dental check-ups help monitor the condition of existing fillings, and our dentist will recommend replacement if necessary.
  • Is the process of getting a dental filling painful? Getting a dental filling is generally not painful. We use local anesthesia to numb the area, ensuring a comfortable experience during the procedure. Any post-procedure discomfort is usually mild and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment should be carried out as soon as possible. If an infected tooth is left untreated for too long, pain can increase dramatically and lead to an abscess around the infected tooth.

  • What symptoms indicate that I may need a root canal? Common signs include severe tooth pain, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling, and tenderness in the gums. If you experience any of these symptoms, it’s essential to seek dental attention promptly.
  • Is a root canal a painful procedure? No, modern root canal procedures are typically not painful. Our dentist will use local anesthesia to ensure you are comfortable during the process. After the procedure, any discomfort can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
  • What happens during a root canal procedure? During a root canal, the infected or damaged pulp inside the tooth is removed, and the space is cleaned, disinfected, and filled to prevent further infection. In some cases, a dental crown may be recommended to restore the tooth’s strength.
  • How long does a root canal procedure take? The duration of a root canal procedure depends on the complexity and location of the affected tooth. On average, the procedure can take one to two hours. Our dental team works efficiently to ensure a smooth and timely experience.
