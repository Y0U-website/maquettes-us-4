  • Can I get Invisalign? Every patient is different. Some patients are excellent candidates for Invisalign while others would benefit more from traditional braces. Schedule a consultation today for further advice.
  • How long does Invisalign treatment typically take? Treatment duration varies based on individual needs, but many people achieve their desired results within 12 to 18 months. Regular check-ups with your Invisalign provider are crucial for monitoring progress.
  • Is Invisalign treatment painful? Invisalign treatment is typically less painful than traditional braces. While you may experience some discomfort or pressure when you switch to a new set of aligners, it is generally mild and temporary. This discomfort is a sign that the aligners are working to shift your teeth gradually.

Ceramic braces

A chieve a stunning, confident smile with our ceramic braces. Designed for discreet orthodontic treatment, these braces seamlessly blend with your teeth. Experience the perfect balance of aesthetics and functionality. Choose ceramic braces for a confident journey to a beautiful smile.

  • What are Ceramic Braces, and how do they differ from traditional braces? Ceramic braces are a discreet orthodontic option that uses clear or tooth-colored brackets and wires for a less noticeable appearance compared to traditional metal braces.
  • Are Ceramic Braces suitable for everyone? Ceramic braces are effective for many orthodontic issues, but their suitability depends on individual cases. Consult with Dr. Rangel to determine if ceramic braces are the right choice for your needs.
  • How noticeable are Ceramic Braces during treatment? While not entirely invisible, ceramic braces offer a more subtle appearance than traditional metal braces. The clear or tooth-colored components make them less noticeable, providing a more aesthetic orthodontic solution.
  • How long does the treatment with Ceramic Braces typically take? Treatment duration varies based on individual needs, but like traditional braces, ceramic braces usually achieve results within 12 to 24 months. Regular follow-ups with Dr. Rangel are essential for monitoring progress.
