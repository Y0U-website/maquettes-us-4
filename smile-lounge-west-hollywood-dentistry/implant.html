Implant Options

Every smile is unique, and your treatment plan will be tailored to your specific needs and goals.

  • Single Tooth Implants Replace one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown.
  • Implant Bridges Restore multiple missing teeth without relying on adjacent teeth.
  • Full-Arch Solutions Replace an entire row of teeth with implant-supported restorations.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures Improve stability and comfort compared to traditional dentures.

Your Implant Journey

The process begins with a detailed consultation and digital imaging to plan precise placement. After the implant is placed, a healing period allows it to integrate with the bone before the final restoration is attached.
