Understanding Orthodontics: More Than Just Braces

Orthodontics is a field of dentistry that focuses not only on aligning teeth and jaws for aesthetic purposes but also on enhancing overall oral function. At Designing Smiles in Hackensack, NJ, our orthodontic treatments go beyond traditional braces, offering tailored solutions to meet the required needs of each patient. Whether you’re a teenager or an adult, our comprehensive approach ensures improved dental health and a more symmetrical smile.

Types of Orthodontic Treatments Available

At Designing Smiles, our range of orthodontic treatments includes traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligners like Clear Aligners. Every option serves a specific purpose and caters to different preferences and lifestyle needs. Metal braces are durable and effective for complex alignments, while ceramic braces offer a less noticeable alternative. For adults seeking a discreet solution, clear aligners provide the aesthetics and convenience desired without compromising on effectiveness.

The Benefits of Orthodontic Care

Orthodontic treatment at our Hackensack Orthodontics Center provides significant benefits beyond a straighter smile. Well aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. Additionally, orthodontics can help correct bite issues that may cause uneven wear, jaw pain, or other chronic problems. Investing in orthodontic care means investing in better oral health and overall well-being.

FAQs

Orthodontics can correct crowded teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and jaw alignment issues. Treatment improves both function and appearance while making teeth easier to clean and maintain long-term.

While treatment can begin at any age, early evaluation around age 7 allows for timely intervention when needed. Many adults successfully undergo orthodontic treatment, and it’s never too late to improve your smile and bite.

Look for orthodontists or dentists with specialized orthodontic training, modern treatment options, and positive patient outcomes. Consider their experience with your specific concerns and available treatment technologies.

Teeth straightening options include traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, lingual braces, & clear aligners like Invisalign. Your dentist in Hackensack will recommend the best option based on your certain needs and lifestyle preferences.

Treatment duration varies from 12 months for minor corrections to 2-3 years for complex cases. Factors affecting the timeline include age, severity of problems, treatment type chosen, and patient compliance with instructions.

Most patients experience mild discomfort when braces are first placed or adjusted, typically lasting a few days. Over-the-counter pain relievers and soft foods help manage any discomfort during the adjustment period.

Maintain excellent oral hygiene with special brushes and floss designed for braces, avoid hard or sticky foods, wear protective mouthguards during sports, and attend all scheduled appointments for adjustments.

Many insurance plans give partial coverage for orthodontic treatment, especially for children. Adult coverage varies by plan, and many dental clinics in Hackensack offer payment plans to make treatment more affordable.

After active treatment, you’ll wear retainers to upkeep your new smile and stop teeth from shifting back. Initial retainer wear is typically full-time, transitioning to nighttime wear for long-term maintenance.
