The practice

A child’s oral health can have a big impact on their overall health and happiness. That’s why we perform kids teeth cleaning and do everything we can to encourage healthy habits — so we can help prevent cavities and other dental issues.

A child’s first experience with a dentist can have a tremendous impact on their lifelong dental health. So we make sure your little one has so much fun during their “Peek-A-Boo” visit, they see the dentist’s office as a fun, safe, happy place!

A Magical Dental Experience We want to make sure your child and family loves coming to Magic Smiles family dentistry. We’re here to provide kids dental care and keep them smiling from the very first time they sit, until the moment they ship off to college.

Unreal Attention to Detail From the cutting-edge technology, meticulous kids teeth cleaning, all the way down to the color of pens at the front desk, we put a lot of thought into every aspect of kids dental care. It all matters.
