Meet Dr. Ritzau

Dr. Kristen Ritzau is the Founder, DDS, Lead Dentist, and Chief Executive Officer of Shoreline Dental Studio. In 2011, Shoreline Dental Studio began in a single dental operatory with only Dr. Ritzau and her husband, and has quickly grown into a 40+ employee, multi-million-dollar practice that serves dental patients from over 100 cities and six states across the nation. Over the past five years, it has grown to help over 6,500 people achieve their dental goals.

Professional background

Dr. Ritzau is also the author of Distinctive Dentistry: Obtaining an Unparalleled Dental Experience and a Healthy Smile for Life. She was also named to the Board of the American Academy of Clear Aligners in 2018 and achieved Gold+ Level Provider status by Invisalign for her clinical excellence with Invisalign. She graduated at the top of her class from University of California, Los Angeles earning the prestigious Alpha Omega International Award.

Dr. Ritzau was the captain of her collegiate soccer team at Point Loma Nazarene University and also earned awards as a two time All-Conference player. She also enjoys running and has completed two marathons to date. She enjoys spending time with her two young children, Hayley and Hunter. She is involved with her local MOPS group and can be found at the beach with her family in her free time.
