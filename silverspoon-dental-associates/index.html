The practice

At Silverspoon Dental, Dr. Tyrus G. Montgomery proudly serves the Bowie community with a commitment to compassionate, high-quality care. With over 25 years of experience, Dr. Montgomery focuses on building lasting relationships and delivering personalized treatment that supports your long-term oral health.

Conveniently located at 12300 Kemmerton Lane in Bowie, MD, our office sits just across the street from Benjamin Tasker Middle School, making it easy for families and local residents to access care close to home.

We offer a full range of dental services designed to keep your smile healthy, confident, and strong. From routine cleanings to advanced restorative and cosmetic treatments, our team provides personalized care tailored to your needs.

Known to many as “Doc,” Dr. Montgomery is originally from Newark, New Jersey and proudly calls Bowie, Maryland home. He earned his undergraduate degree from Hampton University and received his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD) from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey.

Dr. Montgomery completed his residency at St. Elizabeth Hospital in Washington, DC and has served in leadership roles including Division Chief Officer at Southern Maryland Hospital and Maryland State Dental Director.

Today, he continues to proudly serve the Bowie community with honest evaluations, ethical treatment recommendations, and patient-centered care focused on long-term health.
