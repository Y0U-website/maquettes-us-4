The Dental Implant Procedure

Dental implants for tooth replacement vary in procedure from case to case. Dr. Afshari will refer patients to a trusted oral surgeon who will decide how to proceed based on the number of implants he or she will need to install. They also determine whether bone loss might impede the procedure and if you’ll require the extraction of any natural teeth beforehand.

Once the treatment plan is in place, our procedure will then take place in two sessions:

  • Phase one: install the titanium tooth root and then allow it to implant over four to six months
  • Phase two: once the implant has sufficiently adhered to the bone, place an abutment on it to hold your dental crown in place
