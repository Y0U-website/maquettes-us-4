Dental Fillings

At Conyers Dental Associates, we offer high-quality, tooth-colored fillings to our patients to help repair damaged teeth. Our fillings can restore the strength and appearance of your teeth to make you confident in your smile.

We can complete dental fillings in as little as one visit with direct fillings. Indirect fillings for more severe tooth decay, on the other hand, can take several visits to complete. Our practice also offers cosmetic dentistry services like veneers and crowns that help mask and repair very severe damage to your teeth.

Tips for At-Home Oral Hygiene

At Conyers Dental Associates, we understand that getting to the dentist on time isn’t always realistic with a family at home. Even when you can’t make it for a scheduled cleaning or dental exam, there are things you can do at home to improve and protect your overall oral hygiene.

Our dentists recommend that you do the following:

  • Rinse your mouth or chew sugar-free gum after each meal
  • Avoid sugary foods or things like soda or coffee that stain and damage your teeth
  • Brush your teeth in the morning and before bed; try to floss at least once, too
  • Avoid smoking or chewing tobacco
  • Eat a nutritious diet that promotes oral health
  • Protect your teeth during sports & recreational activities
