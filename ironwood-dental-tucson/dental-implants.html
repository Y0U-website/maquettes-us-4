Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots. Surgically inserted into the jawbone, they provide a stable foundation for custom restorations such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Over time, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone—a process called osseointegration—creating a secure, permanent bond. Each implant supports: A single dental crown for one missing tooth
  • A fixed bridge to replace multiple adjacent teeth
  • A full-arch prosthesis such as All-on-4 implants or implant-retained dentures

Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

Implants offer several advantages over other types of tooth replacement:

They’re a preferred solution for patients looking for permanent, low-maintenance results.

  • Stability – Implants are anchored directly into bone, offering superior strength and no movement.
  • Aesthetics – Implant crowns are matched to your natural teeth for a seamless appearance.
  • Longevity – With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Jawbone Preservation – They stimulate the jaw and help prevent bone loss.
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth – Unlike bridges, implants do not require support from neighboring teeth.

What to Expect from the Implant Process

Placing a dental implant is typically done in stages to ensure optimal healing and results. Here’s a general overview:

Throughout the process, our team ensures comfort, safety, and transparency at every step.

  • Consultation and Planning – We evaluate your oral health, review medical history, and use advanced CBCT imaging to assess bone density.
  • Surgical Placement – The titanium post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation for comfort.
  • Healing Period – The implant integrates with the bone over several months.
  • Abutment & Restoration – Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment and custom crown, bridge, or denture.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for implants. Good candidates typically:

During your consultation, we’ll determine your eligibility and recommend any preparatory treatments needed for successful implant placement.

  • Have sufficient bone support or are willing to undergo bone grafting
  • Are free of uncontrolled chronic conditions that could impair healing
  • Do not smoke, or are willing to stop during the healing process
  • Are committed to maintaining excellent oral hygiene

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or more—and in many cases, a lifetime. Routine checkups, daily hygiene, and avoiding smoking are key to long-term success.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. We offer various forms of sedation to ensure a relaxed, comfortable procedure.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes. Implants have one of the highest success rates in dentistry, often exceeding 95 %, when placed by experienced professionals and cared for properly.

What if I’ve been missing teeth for years?

If bone loss has occurred, procedures like bone grafting or ridge augmentation can restore the foundation for implants. We’ll assess your condition and recommend a safe, effective path forward.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Some plans cover portions of the procedure, such as extractions or restorations. Our team can help you understand your benefits and discuss financing options if needed.
