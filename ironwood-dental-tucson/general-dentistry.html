Patient Info Blog Contact Request Appointment What Is General Dentistry? General dentistry encompasses a wide variety of diagnostic, preventive, and treatment services that support the health of your teeth and gums. It includes routine evaluations, minor restorations, and preventive therapies designed to stop dental problems before they start. At Ironwood Dental, we combine clinical experience with modern dental technology to offer proactive, evidence-based care for patients of all ages.

Our General Dentistry Services

We provide a full scope of general dental services designed to prevent disease, detect issues early, and maintain a strong, functional smile.

Dental Cleanings & Exams

Routine cleanings and exams help prevent tooth decay and gum disease by removing plaque and tartar and monitoring your oral health. These visits are key to detecting issues early and keeping your smile in optimal condition.

Dental Fillings

We use durable, tooth-colored composite resin to repair cavities and minor damage. Fillings restore the strength of your tooth while blending naturally with your existing enamel.

Dental Sealants

Sealants are protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth, especially for children. They help prevent cavities in hard-to-reach areas prone to decay.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel and reduces the risk of cavities. We recommend professional fluoride applications during preventive visits for added protection.

Pediatric Dentistry

Our kid-friendly approach makes dental visits easy and educational for children. We focus on prevention, gentle cleanings, and building lifelong oral hygiene habits.

Oral Cancer Screening

We perform a thorough evaluation of the mouth’s soft tissues during exams to check for signs of oral cancer. Early detection can lead to better outcomes and more effective treatment.

TMJ Therapy

TMJ therapy is used to treat discomfort in the jaw joints caused by clenching, grinding, or misalignment. We provide diagnostic evaluations and custom treatment options to relieve tension and restore function.

Occlusal & Sports Mouthguards

Our custom-made mouthguards protect against bruxism (teeth grinding) and sports-related injuries. These appliances are designed for comfort, durability, and effective protection.

White Spot Treatment

We offer advanced treatments to reduce or eliminate white spots on teeth, often caused by early decay or orthodontic treatment. This improves both tooth health and cosmetic appearance.

Tooth Extractions

If a tooth is too damaged to be saved, we perform gentle extractions with a focus on comfort and minimal downtime. We also offer guidance on replacement options when needed.

Therapeutic Botox

Therapeutic Botox is used to relieve jaw pain, tension, and headaches caused by TMJ disorders, teeth grinding, or overactive facial muscles. At Ironwood Dental, we provide precise, targeted Botox treatments to relax affected muscles, reduce discomfort, and improve oral function.

A Personalized Approach to Prevention

At Ironwood Dental, your care plan is never one-size-fits-all. We take time to understand your oral and overall health history, lifestyle, and long-term goals. Preventive care appointments are a collaborative process—designed to educate and empower you to make confident decisions about your dental health. We welcome patients with dental anxiety and provide options for gentle, judgment-free care, including sedation when needed.
