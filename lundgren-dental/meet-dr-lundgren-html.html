Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Most of Dr. Lundgren’s free time is dedicated to her two young daughters, Heidi and Greta. She likes to stay active and fit by doing yoga and Pilates, and she was even a Registered Dietician for a time after her studies at UConn. She is also the official dentist for the Mrs. America Pageant system for the NC and SC states! She previously competed and finished in the top 15, and now she’s proud to help future competitors have the confidence they need both on and off stage.

Fun Facts: Dr. Lundgren’s brother, Nile, is a big part of her life, and you can currently find him on the Netflix reality series Owning Manhattan !
