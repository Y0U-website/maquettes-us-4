Services Rock Island Dental Associates

0 Services Preventative Care Preventative care is the key to a healthy smile. Regular checkups allow us to treat issues before they become a problem. We perform full mouth screenings for periodontal disease and are able to treat and manage gum disease as needed.

Fillings

We offer composite (white) restorations and bonding for both front and back teeth.

Crown & Bridge

Our office uses local dental laboratories with skilled technicians to mill all of our crown and bridgework. We offer all types of crown and bridgework including in office milling, fabrication of crowns using CEREC technology, and ceramic restorations for superior aesthetics and strength.

Periodontal Disease

Our team of skilled hygienists follows the American Academy of Periodontology recommendations and screens all patients for development of periodontal disease. Did you know periodontal disease is linked to many chronic health conditions? If you have signs of periodontal disease your hygienist will discuss nonsurgical treatment such as scaling and root planning to treat areas of bone loss.

Dental Implants

Dr. Meredith and Dr. McIntyre have received advanced training in the restoration of dental implants. We work with local surgeons to restore single, multiple and full arch restorations.

We also offer implant based dentures that provide superior retention to a typical denture. These restorations provide an excellent solution to poor or loose fitting dentures.

Dentures

Our office is proud of the options for aesthetic removable appliances that we fabricate with the talents of our local denture laboratory. Whether you are a first time denture patient or an experienced denture wearer, we are happy to discuss the fabrication steps and expectations of a removable dental appliance.

Technology

Our office strives to provide state of the art technology to ensure that you are getting the best treatment possible. We pride ourselves on a commitment to continuing education above the normal requirements and investment into all our team members. We offer digital scanning technology with Primescan and also utilize ITero digital scanning technologies. Our work is also supported with our 3D CBCT machine. These technologies allow us to not only educate our patients but track progression of certain dental conditions including effects of bruxism and acid erosion over time.
