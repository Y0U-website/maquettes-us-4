She knew early on that she wanted to channel her artistic passion into something meaningful. Dentistry became the perfect blend of creativity, science, and genuine human connection. Today, she is deeply committed to creating smiles that feel authentically you — driven by her belief that a great smile should look effortless, not engineered.

Dr. Sasha completed her undergraduate studies at UC Irvine and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Southern California (USC) , where she refined her technical expertise and deepened her passion for smile design.

Dr. Sasha believes that the highest form of cosmetic dentistry is work that goes unnoticed — not because it’s invisible, but because it looks so perfectly natural that no one suspects a thing. Every smile she designs begins with a deep study of the face.

Every veneer design begins with your face — its proportions, symmetry, and the way you naturally express yourself.

When Dr. Sasha isn’t designing smiles, she’s living the kind of life that keeps her creativity and curiosity alive.

Whether you’re ready to book or simply want to learn more, Dr. Sasha is here to guide you with honesty, warmth, and expertise. Every transformation starts with a conversation.
