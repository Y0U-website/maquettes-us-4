Meet Our Doctors

Doctor Harriet Armstrong

Dr. Harriet Armstrong is a native of Lake Charles, Louisiana, and brings decades of experience and compassion to her patients. She practiced in Southwest Louisiana for twenty-nine years before joining Silsbee Family Dentistry in 2017.

In addition to private practice, Dr. Armstrong has shared her knowledge with future dental professionals as a clinical adjunct instructor at Lamar University’s Dental Hygiene Program since 2016.

She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from Millsaps College and went on to graduate from the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry in 1988.

Dr. Armstrong is married to Dr. Jim Armstrong. Together, they have two adult children and one granddaughter, who bring joy and balance to her life outside of dentistry.

Her years of experience, gentle approach, and dedication to education reflect her lifelong commitment to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.
