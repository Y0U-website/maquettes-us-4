At Silsbee Family Dentistry, we believe going to the dentist should feel more like visiting family than a clinic. That’s why our office was designed to feel like home—warm, cozy…

The practice

Founded in 1990 by Dr. Joyce E. Reed, a Southeast Texas native, our practice has grown alongside the families of this community. Over the years, Dr. Reed cared for patients, their children, and even their grandchildren—building lasting relationships based on trust and compassion. Today, her daughter Erin proudly continues that legacy as our office manager.

We combine this home-like atmosphere with modern dental technology and the latest treatments, ensuring you receive both comfort and quality care. Our team takes time to answer your questions, meet your needs, and even follow up after visits to make sure you’re doing well.
