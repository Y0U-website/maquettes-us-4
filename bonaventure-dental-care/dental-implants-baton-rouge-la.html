What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants serve as artificial tooth roots, providing a strong foundation for replacement teeth that look and function like natural ones. Made from biocompatible materials like titanium, these implants integrate with your jawbone to prevent bone loss and maintain your facial structure.

What Are The Benefits of Dental Implants?

Longevity: With proper care, your dental implants can last a lifetime, offering a durable solution to tooth loss.

Improved Functionality: Implants allow you to speak, eat and smile with confidence, without the worry of slipping or discomfort that is more common with dentures.

Preservation of Oral Health: Unlike bridges, implants do not require altering adjacent teeth, thus preserving your natural teeth and promoting long-term oral health.

What Is The Implant Procedure Like?

We begin with a detailed examination and consultation to determine if implants are the right choice for you. This step includes a discussion of your dental history and expectations so that we can make sure to get you what you want.

The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone carefully by our experienced team, which is then left to osseointegrate (bond) with the bone over a few months.

Once the implant has successfully integrated, a custom-made crown is placed atop the implant, completing your smile with a seamless and natural appearance. Stunning!

What Are Implant-Supported Dentures ?

Implant-supported dentures offer a transformative solution for those missing multiple or all their teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, these are anchored securely with dental implants, offering unwavering stability and a natural feel. They fuse with your jawbone, providing a secure foundation similar to your natural tooth roots, which helps prevent bone loss. With implant-supported dentures, you can experience the freedom of eating, speaking and smiling without worry.

Am I A Good Candidate For Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants are individuals with healthy gums and adequate bone to support the implant. Good oral hygiene and a commitment to regular dental visits are essential. Our team will talk to you and fully assess your situation to determine if implants are the right choice for you.

What Are The Benefits Of Dental Implants For Me?

Dental implants offer several advantages, including improved appearance, enhanced oral function, preservation of bone health and durability. Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require adjacent teeth to be altered, thus maintaining a more natural tooth structure.

What Does The Dental Implant Process Involve?

The process begins with a comprehensive evaluation and planning. The implant is placed into the jawbone through a surgical procedure. After the implant integrates with the bone, which can take several months, a replacement tooth or crown is attached to the implant.

How Long Will My Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. It’s crucial to maintain good oral hygiene and visit your dentist regularly to guarantee the longevity of your implants.

What Is The Recovery Time For Dental Implants?

You can usually return to work and normal activities within a day or two after the implant surgery, though the full healing of the implant site can take several weeks to months. Follow-up appointments allow us to monitor your progress on your pathway to successful healing.
