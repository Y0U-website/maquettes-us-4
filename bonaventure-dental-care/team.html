Meet Our Dentists

Dr. Justin K. Bonaventure

When you visit Bonaventure Dental, you’ll feel right away that you’re not just another patient. You’re someone who deserves genuine care, personal attention and a dentist who truly listens.

That’s exactly what you’ll find with Dr. Justin Bonaventure , a Baton Rouge native who takes pride in helping you feel confident every time you smile. Whether you’re here for a simple cleaning or a complete smile transformation, you can relax knowing that your comfort, your goals and your results always come first.

Dr. Bonaventure earned his Bachelor of Science from Louisiana State University and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from LSU’s School of Dentistry. But what really sets him apart is how he keeps learning, constantly refining his skills to give you the very best that modern dentistry has to offer. Every recommendation he makes is designed around what’s best for you , not just what’s possible.

You’ll also have peace of mind knowing your dentist is a respected leader in his field. Dr. Bonaventure is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Louisiana Dental Association, and the Greater Baton Rouge Dental Association, where he served as President in 2012. His dedication to excellence means you can trust that your smile and your oral health are in expert hands.

Outside the office, Dr. Bonaventure’s world revolves around his family: his wife, Lindsey, and their daughters Layla, Anna Kathryn, Reese and Mary Mims. When he’s not caring for patients, you’ll find him duck hunting, saltwater fishing, playing golf or cooking something delicious for family and friends. At heart, Dr. Bonaventure believes that a confident smile can change everything . His goal is simple: to help you feel at ease, look your best and walk out of every visit knowing you made the right choice for your health and happiness.

Dr. Austin Safford

When you visit Bonaventure Dental, you’ll quickly see that Dr. Austin Safford is all about you : your comfort, your confidence and your health. As a Baton Rouge native, he loves helping people right here at home enjoy smiles that look great, feel natural and stand the test of time.

Dr. Safford’s journey into dentistry began with a lifelong fascination for how the human body works. After graduating from Dunham High School, he earned a degree in Kinesiology with a minor in Biology from Louisiana State University, an education that gives him a deep understanding of the connection between your oral health and your overall well-being.

When you’re in his chair, you can expect a calm, attentive experience. Dr. Safford takes time to understand your goals and makes sure you feel completely at ease before, during and after every appointment. His mission is simple: to help you keep your smile strong, healthy and radiant.

Outside the office, Dr. Safford finds balance in the great outdoors. He loves hunting, playing golf, staying active at the gym and spending time with his wife, Rebecca, and their dog, Sammy. Those quiet, joyful moments fuel his energy and remind him why caring for your smile matters so much, because it’s about living life with confidence and joy. At the end of the day, Dr. Safford’s goal is to make sure you love the way your smile feels and the way it makes you feel about yourself .
