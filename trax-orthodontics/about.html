Meet Dr. Namay

Dr. Robert R. Namay has over 20 years of experience caring for patients’ smiles. A veteran of the U.S. Air Force, Dr. Namay graduated from the OSU College of Dentistry in 1969. He later received his Masters of Science in Orthodontics in 1973.

Whether fixing crooked teeth or fixing bite issues, Dr. Namay enjoys getting to know his patients and educating them through their treatment plan.

He’s highly experienced in placing all kinds of orthodontic appliances including space maintainers and metal and clear braces.
