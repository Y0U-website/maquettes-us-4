How Dental Implants Transform Lives The difference between living with missing teeth and having dental implants goes far beyond appearance. Dr. Ayesha Rahman explains to patients that implants don’t just replace what you’ve lost – they prevent the cascade of problems that missing teeth create over time.

When you lose a tooth, your jawbone begins to shrink because it no longer receives stimulation from chewing forces. This bone loss changes your facial shape and makes adjacent teeth shift. Dental implants halt this process by providing the same stimulation natural tooth roots offer.

The psychological impact often surprises patients the most. Many don’t realize how much they’ve restricted their diet or avoided social situations until they can suddenly eat anything and laugh without covering their mouth.

Initial Consultation and Planning

During this consultation, you’ll learn about timeline expectations, healing requirements, and what temporary solutions might be needed during the implant integration period. Dr. Rahman takes time to address all your concerns and explain exactly what each phase of treatment involves.

Surgical Placement with Precision

The actual implant placement often surprises patients with its comfort. Dr. Rahman uses precise surgical techniques and advanced anesthesia to ensure minimal discomfort. The titanium implant posts are placed with computer-guided accuracy, providing optimal positioning for function and aesthetics.

Integration and Final Restoration

The integration period, typically 3-6 months, allows your bone to grow around the implant in a process called osseointegration. This creates a foundation stronger than natural tooth roots. The final phase involves creating custom crowns, bridges, or dentures that match your existing teeth perfectly.

Comprehensive Implant Solutions

Single Tooth Replacement

When you’ve lost one tooth, a single implant with a custom crown provides the most natural replacement possible. Unlike bridges, which require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth, an implant stands independently. The crown will be indistinguishable from your natural teeth.

Multiple Teeth and Full Arch Solutions

For patients missing several teeth, implant-supported bridges or dentures offer dramatic improvements over traditional options. These eliminate adhesives, slipping, and dietary restrictions while providing stability and confidence.

All-on-4 techniques allow Dr. Rahman to replace an entire arch using just four strategically placed implants, often eliminating the need for bone grafting. Sometimes 6 on top and 4 on the bottom is often required too.

Advanced Technology and Techniques

Marietta Dental Creations uses state-of-the-art planning software, which allows Dr. Rahman to place your implants virtually before surgery begins. This technology ensures optimal positioning while minimizing surgical time and enhancing precision.

In suitable cases, Dr. Rahman can extract damaged teeth and place implants during the same appointment, often with immediate temporary teeth. This approach reduces the number of procedures needed and allows you to leave with a functional smile.

Who Benefits from Dental Implants

The best candidates have adequate bone density, healthy gums, and realistic expectations. Dr. Ayesha Rahman evaluates each patient individually, considering overall health, medications, and lifestyle habits that might affect healing.

Certain conditions, like diabetes or heavy smoking, may affect success rates. Dr. Rahman works with your medical providers to ensure implant treatment fits safely within your healthcare plan. Patients with insufficient bone may benefit from grafting procedures that rebuild the foundation for successful placement.

Long-Term Success and Investment

The success rate for dental implants exceeds 95 % when placed by experienced practitioners like Dr. Rahman. With proper care, implants can last for decades, making them an excellent long-term investment in oral health and quality of life.

Maintaining dental implants requires the same attention as maintaining natural teeth—regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings. The advantage is that implants can’t develop cavities, though the surrounding gum tissue still needs protection.

While dental implants represent a significant initial investment, their longevity often makes them more cost-effective than alternatives over time. Traditional bridges need replacement every 10-15 years, while dentures require frequent adjustments.
