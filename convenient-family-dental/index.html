The practice

For more than twenty years, families across Okemos have trusted our office for gentle, patient-focused dentistry — from a first check-up at age three to crowns, fillings, and root canals for adults. Call and speak with a friendly team that takes the time to listen.

Convenient Family Dental has cared for Okemos families for more than twenty years. Parents who first visited us years ago now bring their own children — and that continuity is something we take seriously.

We keep the experience simple and unhurried: explain what we see, answer questions in plain language, and treat every patient with patience and respect. No pressure, no rushing.

Going to the dentist can be difficult — especially for children, or for anyone who has had a hard dental experience in the past. We believe patients deserve time, explanations, patience, and care that helps them feel comfortable before treatment begins.
