Our Implant Process, Step by Step

Here’s what you can expect on your implant process with us:

  • Comprehensive consultation: Your visit includes x-rays, clinical exams, intraoral scans, and impressions. This thorough assessment helps us understand your needs. The consultation can also be part of your periodic exam.

  • Guided implant placement: Using advanced guided surgery, we place the implant with minimal discomfort, ensuring faster healing.
  • Healing & restoration: After the healing phase, we secure a custom crown, bridge, or denture for a seamless, natural finish.

FAQs

Are dental implants right for me?

Yes, if you’re missing one or more teeth and have healthy gums and sufficient bone structure. We’ll assess your situation during your consultation.

What can I expect during the consultation?

Your consultation includes x-rays, clinical exams, intraoral scans, and impressions to give us a complete picture of your oral health.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, dental implants will last for many years.

What is the cost of dental implants?

Our consultation includes a detailed assessment and CBCT scan and evaluation. If you have insurance we offer complimentary white-glove concierge service for managing dental insurance verification and claim submissions. Let us handle the back and forth, so you don’t have to.

If you do not have insurance, ask us about our In-Office Membership plans or payment plans or helping you move forward with treatment.

What makes guided implant surgery different?

Guided implant surgery uses advanced digital technology to plan the implant placement with extreme precision. This minimally invasive technique reduces discomfort, speeds up healing, and ensures a more accurate fit.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The timeline can vary, but most patients can expect the process to take about 3-6 months from implant placement to the final restoration. This includes healing time to ensure the implant integrates properly with the bone.

Will I need a bone graft before getting implants?

In some cases, a bone graft may be needed if there isn’t enough bone to support the implant. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your bone structure and let you know if a graft is necessary.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

We use guided surgery to make the implant placement as comfortable as possible. With local anesthesia and our precise technique, most patients experience minimal discomfort during and after the procedure.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups are essential to keep your implants healthy and long-lasting.
