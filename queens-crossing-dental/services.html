Problems We Treat

Tooth Pain

Persistent or sudden discomfort in your tooth can signal an underlying issue like decay, infection, or trauma. It’s important to address tooth pain promptly to prevent further complications.

Missing Teeth

Gaps from missing teeth can affect your smile, speech, and bite. Solutions like dental implants, bridges, or dentures can restore both function and appearance.

Teeth Grinding

Bruxism, or grinding your teeth, can lead to enamel wear, jaw pain, and headaches. Custom night guards are a common treatment option.

Stained Teeth

Discoloration from coffee, tea, tobacco, or aging can dull your smile. Teeth whitening treatments can help you achieve a brighter, more confident smile.

Chipped Tooth (Cracked Tooth)

A chipped or cracked tooth can expose sensitive inner layers and increase the risk of infection. Bonding, veneers, or crowns are effective repair options.

Crooked Teeth

Misaligned teeth can impact your bite, oral hygiene, and confidence. Orthodontic treatments like braces or aligners can create a straighter, healthier smile.

Cavities

Bad Breath

Chronic bad breath, or halitosis, can be a sign of gum disease or other health issues. Regular dental cleanings and improved oral care habits can help.

Dental Anxiety

Fear of dental visits can keep you from receiving essential care. Modern dentistry offers sedation and other comfort measures to ease anxiety and provide a stress-free experience.

Gum Disease

Swollen, bleeding, or receding gums can indicate periodontal disease. Early treatment prevents bone loss and preserves your natural teeth.
