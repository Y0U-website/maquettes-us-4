Anthony Weber, DDS

My dentist of 30 years retired and after asking around I was told by multiple people the Dr Weber was wonderful. I have to agree my experience with him and the office has been great. ~ Kristi Emerson

Dr. Weber completed his undergraduate education at Washington State University, where he earned two Bachelor of Science degrees in neuroscience and zoology. He then attended the University of Southern California School of Dentistry, where he graduated magna cum laude with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2013.

Throughout his education, he completed rotations in surgical anesthesia and advanced training in surgical and restorative implant dentistry. Dr. Weber also received training and certification in Dental Implants, Invisalign Orthodontics, Intravenous Sedation, Veneers, Wisdom tooth extractions, Gum Grafting, root canals, dentures, and general dentistry to both pediatric and adult patients.

Dr. Weber is a member of a variety of organizations, including the Academy of Osseointegration, American Dental Society of Anesthesiology, American Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry. He has volunteered his time and dental services to the disadvantaged of Los Angeles, Central America, and Africa.

As one of the founders of Weber Dental Center, Dr. Anthony Weber provides a wide range of dental procedures, such as wisdom tooth extractions, IV sedation, Dental Implants, Gum grafting, InvisalignⓇ orthodontics, root canals, bone grafting, dentures and general dentistry to both pediatric and adult patients in and around Spokane Valley, Washington.

When not at the office, Dr. Weber spends time with family and friends. As an avid outdoor enthusiast, he enjoys hunting, fishing, hiking and trap shooting. He also values quality time with his wife Lori, and their dog Remi.
