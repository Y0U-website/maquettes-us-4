About Our Practice

The professionals at Weber Dental Center in Spokane Valley, Washington, offer a broad range of services, from routine teeth cleaning and root canals to oral surgery and cosmetic dentistry. Together, Dr. Anthony Weber, Dr. Sue Weishaar, and Dr. Lori Weber provide a welcoming and friendly environment for the whole family.

Conveniently located on East Indiana Avenue, the Weber Dental Center team strives to change the way their patients feel about dentists. By providing compassionate and comfortable oral health care, they make going to the dentist an exciting experience that improves smiles and makes patients feel great.

Dr. Anthony Weber earned his undergraduate degree at Washington State University before attending the University of Southern California School of Dentistry, where he graduated magna cum laude with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2013. He strives to help his patients turn their vision of a perfect smile into reality. Through rigorous study and a lifelong devotion to continuing education, Dr. Weber is committed to providing the most cutting edge treatments in dentistry to all patients.

Dr. Sue Weishaar began her undergraduate education at Eastern Washington University and earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Case Western Reserve University. With vast experience in both general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Sue has over 26 years of experience helping patients love their smile.

Dr. Lori Weber received her undergraduate degree from the University of Nevada before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Southern California School of Dentistry in 2014, where she completed rotations in endodontics, prosthodontics, oral surgery, and pediatric dentistry. She has participated in several outreach efforts, providing dental care to people throughout the world.

At Weber Dental Center, the dentists offer a full spectrum of general dentistry services, including sedation and preventive dentistry, as well as veneers, teeth whitening, Invisalign®, wisdom teeth extraction, dental implants, gingival grafting (gum grafting), bone grafting, root canals, dentures, restorative and cosmetic procedures. With over 40 years of combined experience, they offer solutions for all of your teeth, gum, and oral health issues
