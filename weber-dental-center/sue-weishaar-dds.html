Sue Weishaar, DDS

Dr. Sue Weishaar has over 30 years of experience providing general dentistry services to patients in Spokane Valley, Washington. At Weber Dental Center, located on East Indiana Avenue, Dr. Sue is passionate about creating and maintaining happy, healthy smiles.

Focusing on comprehensive care, Dr. Sue addresses whole body issues by identifying and linking the underlying causes of her patients’ dental problems to their systemic health. She provides a full range of general dentistry services, including cleanings and preventive care, as well as fillings, crowns, and smile makeovers.

Dr. Sue earned her certification in dental assisting at Spokane Community College before pursuing her undergraduate degree in dental hygiene from Eastern Washington University. She then attended Case Western Reserve University, where she graduated with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery and went on to complete her residency at the University of Washington.

When she’s not at the office, Dr. Sue loves spending time with her family, including her eight grandchildren. She’s an avid horseback rider and enjoys cooking, fitness, and travel.
