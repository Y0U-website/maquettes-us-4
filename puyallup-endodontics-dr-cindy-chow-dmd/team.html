Team

Alma is a registered dental assistant who has been in the endodontic field for 40 years. She feels truly blessed to have a career doing what she loves. Alma continues to be passionate about the successful results of root canal treatment.

Alma has been married to her high school sweetheart for 40 years and has two grown children. On her time away from the office she volunteers in her daughter s kindergarten class. She treasures her time with her "grand students". Alma and her husband enjoy camping and boating.

Sandi is that first voice on the phone and smile to greet you at the front desk. After sixteen years in the dental field she is still enjoying the relationships she has built with the referring dental offices. She has been with Puyallup Endodontics for nine years. During those nine years she has appreciated the opportunity to spend time with all the wonderful patients that come in our office.

When not working Sandi loves spending time with her family camping or tossing the football around with her husband and son.
