The Benefits of All-On-X Implants

Restored Confidence All-On-X implants give you a beautiful, natural-looking smile, so you can say goodbye to the self-consciousness caused by missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures. You’ll feel great sharing your smile with family, friends, and colleagues.

Improved Functionality Chewing and eating become effortless again with All-On-X implants. Unlike traditional dentures, they stay securely in place, allowing you to enjoy crunchy, chewy, or sticky foods without fear of slipping or discomfort.

Long-Lasting Results With proper care, All-On-X implants are designed to last for decades, making them a durable and worthwhile investment in your oral health. Plus, they help preserve your jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth.

What to Expect From All-On-X Implants Personalized Consultation and Planning Your journey begins with a one-on-one consultation with Dr. Patel at South Jersey Smiles in Moorestown, NJ. Using advanced imaging technology like 3D CBCT scans, he’ll evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and develop a customized treatment plan. This step ensures your implants are precisely placed for optimal results.

Implant Placement Procedure

During the procedure, Dr. Patel will strategically place the implants in your jawbone to provide maximum stability for your new teeth. For your comfort, we offer sedation options, including laughing gas and oral conscious sedation, to ensure you feel calm and relaxed throughout the process. Once the implants are placed, a temporary set of teeth may be provided, so you can begin enjoying your smile right away.

Final Restoration and Follow-Up

After your implants have fully integrated with your jawbone (a process called osseointegration), you’ll return for the placement of your custom-designed final restoration. These replacement teeth are crafted with both aesthetics and function in mind, giving you a radiant, fully functional smile you’ll love. We’ll provide detailed instructions for care and schedule periodic check-ups to ensure long-term success.
