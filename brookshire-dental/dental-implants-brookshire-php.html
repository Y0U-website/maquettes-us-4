Protect Your Dental Implants

When it comes to caring for your dental implants, there are several steps you can take to keep them looking and feeling their best, including:

  • 1. Allowing the anesthetic to wear off before attempting to eat anything
  • 2. Avoid tobacco or smoking until your implants are healed. If possible, try to 3. quit before you have them placed
  • 3. Avoiding any strenuous activity for at least 48 hours after placement
  • 4. Trying not to touch or prod at the implant sites
  • 5. Wearing a mouthguard if you are prone to teeth grinding
  • 6. Wearing a sports guard if you plan to engage in sports-related activities
  • 7. Avoid hard, crunchy foods, as these can damage both natural and artificial teeth
