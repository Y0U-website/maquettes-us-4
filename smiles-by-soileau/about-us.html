Meet Dr. Tony Soileau

Education & Accreditation

Dr. Soileau graduated from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans in 1994. Additionally, he has been president of his local dental society and is an associate professor at LSU School of Dentistry. He has been a faculty member of the Institute of Oral Art and Design (IOAD) in Tampa, Florida and the Pacific Aesthetic Continuum (PAC~Live) in San Francisco. He is a member of the ADDA, LDA, ADA, AGD, AACD, and has his Fellowship in the Academy of Comprehensive Esthetics.
