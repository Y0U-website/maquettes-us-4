What are All-on-4 dental implants at Dental Excellence in Stephenville, TX?

All-on-4 replaces an entire arch of teeth using just four strategically angled titanium implants per arch to support a fixed prosthesis, often eliminating bone grafting. At Dental Excellence in Stephenville, TX, the team places and restores in-house and, with a teeth-in-a-day protocol, in most cases you walk out the same day with a fixed temporary bridge.
