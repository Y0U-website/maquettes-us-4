Why airway dentistry matters

The conventional dental visit looks for cavities, plaque, and gum disease. Those matter. But the mouth is also the front door of the airway, and the way a person breathes, especially during sleep, leaves visible evidence on the teeth, tongue, and bite long before a sleep study confirms a problem.

Dentists with airway-aware training are taught to read those signs. Worn-down teeth, a scalloped tongue, narrow arches in a child, recurrent canker sores in an adult, each can point to airway and oral-systemic issues that ripple into headaches, fatigue, and cardiovascular risk.

At Dental Excellence we incorporate airway assessment into routine checkups for children and into comprehensive exams for adults. When indicated, we coordinate with sleep specialists and ENT physicians; when oral appliance therapy is appropriate, we deliver it in-house using digital scanning and 3D imaging.
