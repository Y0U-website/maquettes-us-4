The practice

Our mission is simple: to help every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning, cosmetic treatment, dental implants, Invisalign, or emergency care, our experienced team is committed to providing personalized treatment using modern technology and the highest standards of care. Dr. Ron Chemmalakuzhy is excited to care for the same community he grew up in. He was raised in Lilburn, Georgia and attended Gwin Oaks Elementary, Five Forks Middle School, and Brookwood High School.

He went on to attend Augusta University, where he completed both his undergraduate studies and his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the Dental College of Georgia.

Dr. Ron is committed to providing high -quality, compassionate dental care and is honored that Dr. Taher has entrusted him to continue serving the patients and families of this practice. He looks forward to building lasting relationships with patients and continuing the tradition of excellent care that Dr. Taher has established over the past 25 years.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ron enjoys playing golf, pickleball, spending time with his family and friends, and traveling with his wife.
