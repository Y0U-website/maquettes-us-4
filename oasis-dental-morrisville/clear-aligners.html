What to Expect: Our Clear Aligner Process

At Oasis Dental Morrisville, your orthodontic care is personalized from day one. Here’s what your journey might look like:

Step 1: Consultation & Evaluation

Dr. Seriki will review your smile goals, examine your bite, and determine if you’re a candidate for clear aligners.

Step 2: Digital Scanning & Custom Plan

We’ll create a precise 3D scan of your teeth and use advanced software to map out your aligner sequence—including a virtual smile preview.

Step 3: Aligner Fitting & Wear

You’ll receive a series of aligners to wear 20–22 hours a day. Each set gently moves your teeth closer to their ideal position.

Step 4: Progress Check-Ins

Quick, convenient appointments help us monitor your progress and make any needed adjustments.

Step 5: Retainers & Post-Treatment Care

After treatment, you’ll receive a custom retainer to help keep your smile looking its best for years to come.
