General Dentistry

To Keep You Smiling

The best way to maintain positive oral health is to visit a dentist regularly for cleanings and exams. At Pinnacle Family Dental , we help you and your loved ones keep your teeth strong and healthy with thorough care. Call us today to schedule an appointment for a checkup with Dr. Kim !

What to Expect

During regular appointments, Dr. Kim will thoroughly examine your teeth and gums to check for any signs of dental issues. An oral cancer screening will be performed, and your teeth will be cleaned to ensure that no plaque or bacteria is built up underneath your gums. These regular checkups help us address any dental health issues before they become bigger, more costly problems down the road. Visit our office or call the team today to schedule a checkup to maintain your dental health.
