Dental Implant Failure & Salvage

  • Trouble biting down : Once your implants are fused to your jawbone, you should be able to enjoy most kinds of foods without any problems.
  • Loose dental implant : In some cases, your dental implants might feel loose due to an issue with the restoration. In other instances, it’s likely the metal posts themselves.
  • Swelling, bleeding, and pus : These are typical signs of infection and should be addressed right away.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

With dental implant salvage treatment, our team will evaluate your mouth and evaluate the underlying reason for your situation. We’ll then develop a plan so that you can return to enjoying an effective and long-lasting solution for rebuilding missing teeth. Your treatment might involve deep cleaning, bone grafting, antibiotics, or some other form of infection control to fix your mouth so that you can have a better chance of successful results.
