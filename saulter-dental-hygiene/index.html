The practice

The benefits of dental cleanings are amazing! By visiting our hygienists every six months you can prevent cavities and loss of teeth, brighten your smile and freshen breath, help prevent gum disease, boost your overall health and even save money!

Fluoride helps to prevent tooth decay by making your teeth resistant to plaque bacteria and sugars that attack your teeth. By having a fluoride treatment you can rebuild the tooth enamel that prevents your teeth from being damaged from something as simple as eating.

While fluoride helps prevent tooth decay from starting, SDF is effective in stopping beginning cavities from getting worse and requiring additional procedures.
