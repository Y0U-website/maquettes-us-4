Dental Services in Dyer, IN

Dental care for every age, all under one roof in Dyer, Indiana.

The Crown Dental Center offers a full range of care — preventive, restorative, orthodontic, cosmetic, surgical, and emergency — delivered the calm, unhurried way. No upselling, no surprises; every plan comes with an itemized estimate before we begin.

Cleanings & Prevention

Preventive care is the quiet foundation of a healthy mouth — and the simplest way to avoid bigger, costlier work later. At The Crown Dental Center, hygiene visits are unhurried, and Dr. A walks you through exactly what she sees.

Each visit can include a comprehensive exam , a professional cleaning — routine or a deeper periodontal (deep) cleaning when gum disease is present — low-radiation digital X-rays , fluoride, dental sealants for cavity-prone teeth, and an oral cancer screening . Most patients are seen every six months; we’ll tell you honestly if you need more or less.

The goal is early detection: catching a small cavity or early gum inflammation now is far gentler than treating it later.

Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth is decayed, damaged, or missing, restorative dentistry rebuilds it — comfortably, and to last. Our approach is conservative: we save natural tooth structure wherever we can.

We provide tooth-colored fillings , crowns and bridges , partial and full dentures , and dental implants placed in-house. Every treatment plan comes with an itemized estimate before anything begins.

Not sure what you need? We’ll lay out the options — including what’s urgent versus what can wait — so you can decide with full information.

Oral Surgery & Extractions

Most surgical needs are handled right here — no referral across town. We focus on a gentle experience and clear, simple aftercare.

That includes simple and erupted tooth extractions , dental implant placement , and bone grafting to preserve or rebuild the jawbone so implants have a solid foundation. Oral sedation options are available to keep you comfortable and ease anxiety.

We’ll explain what to expect at every step and make sure you leave knowing exactly how to care for the area as it heals.

Orthodontics & Invisalign

Straighten your smile without metal brackets. The Crown Dental Center provides Invisalign clear aligners — a discreet, removable alternative to traditional braces that fits adult and teen life.

We start with a 3D digital scan (no goopy impressions) so you can preview your projected result before you commit. Treatment gently guides teeth into place for bite correction , and retainers hold the result afterward. Because aligners are removable, you eat and brush normally throughout.

Most Invisalign treatment takes about 12 to 18 months , though simpler cases can finish sooner — we’ll give you an estimated timeline up front.

Cosmetic & Facial Aesthetics

Cosmetic dentistry — and a little facial aesthetics — to help you look as well as you feel. The result should look like you, only more rested and confident.

We offer professional teeth whitening , custom porcelain veneers , composite bonding , and full smile makeovers . Because a dentist understands facial anatomy intimately, we also provide Botox and dermal & lip filler — a natural-looking complement to your smile — plus TMJ treatment for jaw pain and clenching.

Every cosmetic plan starts with a conversation about what you actually want to change, and an honest take on what will get you there.
