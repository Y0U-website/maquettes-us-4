Understanding the Importance of Dental Implants

Missing teeth can lead to more than just cosmetic concerns. They can impact your oral health by affecting bone stimulation, bite dynamics, and diet choices. Dental implants offer a reliable replacement that maintains jaw health and restores function.

Dental implants are durable replacements made up of a post implanted into the bone tissue and a crown that serves as the visible part. They can replace single or multiple missing teeth, providing a natural look and feel. Our practice also offers All-on-4Ⓡ for full arch restorations.

The journey to a new smile begins with thorough planning, using advanced imaging to ensure precise placement. During the procedure, you’ll receive numbing medication for comfort. After implanting the post and allowing it to fuse with your bone tissue, the crown is attached for a complete restoration.

At Alfredo Alexander DMD, we have an in-house periodontist for implant procedures and offer restoration services for existing implants. Our goal is to provide exceptional care and restore your smile with lasting results.
