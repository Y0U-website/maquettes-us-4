Meet Dr. Michael J. Coulter, DDS

Dr. Coulter has been practicing dentistry at his present Emmaus location since 1990. Dr. Coulter graduated from Temple University School of Dentistry in 1982 and also does 30 hours continuing education every year. He strives on having a modern, relaxed atmosphere and a non-imposing style of dentistry. He believes this fosters better communication between doctor and patients.

He specializes in general dentistry and offers standard dental treatments, including teeth whitening, veneers, cleanings, crowns, cosmetic dentistry, denture work, and much more.

Dr. Coulter is a licensed dentist, always dedicated to providing the best possible care for all of his patients. He treats you like family!

For smiles that last a lifetime, give Dr. Coulter a call today!
