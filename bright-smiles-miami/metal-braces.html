Metal braces

Every orthodontic transformation at Bright Smiles Miami starts with a complimentary, comprehensive consultation. Our specialized orthodontists carefully evaluate your facial aesthetics, dental alignment, and digital X-rays to formulate a customized plan built around your unique health goals. Because metal braces are bonded directly to the teeth for the duration of treatment, they work 24/7 without relying on patient compliance, making them an exceptional, worry-free choice for children and busy teenagers.

Invested in your comfort and long-term health, our team ensures every adjustment visit is quick, comfortable, and rewarding. As treatment progresses, watching your teeth align provides a powerful boost to self-esteem and confidence. Straightening your teeth with traditional metal braces establishes a strong foundation for lifelong oral wellness, giving you a functional, balanced, and vibrant smile that lasts.

  • Unmatched Clinical Control: Provides orthodontists with superior precision to solve severe crowding and complex bite issues.
  • Sleeker, Lower Profile: Modern bracket designs are smaller and contoured to minimize lip and cheek irritation.
  • Customizable & Fun: Young patients can personalize their smile with colorful elastic ties changed at every visit.
