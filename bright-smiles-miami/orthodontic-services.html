Orthodontics

ORTHODONTICS PATIENT INFO BLOG CONTACT MEMBERSHIP PLAN We specialize in orthodontic services in Miami, FL, providing personalized treatments that straighten crooked teeth, improve bite function, and enhance overall oral health. Orthodontic treatment isn’t just about creating a beautiful smile—it plays a vital role in supporting long-term dental stability, reducing wear on teeth, preventing gum disease, and improving the way your teeth and jaws work together. Our experienced team takes pride in helping families achieve confident smiles through every stage of the orthodontic process.

Our treatments address a wide range of concerns, including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, crossbites, deep bites, and other bite irregularities. Traditional braces or aligners may also be combined with specialized orthodontic appliances to guide jaw growth or correct bite relationships.

Today’s Miami orthodontics technology is more advanced, comfortable, and discreet than ever before. We offer a full range of modern options, from traditional metal braces—which remain one of the most effective and reliable treatments—to clear ceramic braces that blend naturally with your smile. We also offer clear removable aligners for patients who prefer a flexible alternative that fits their lifestyle. These advances in orthodontic care allow families with children, teens, and adults to achieve excellent results with less visibility and more convenience than in the past.

Every patient begins with a comprehensive consultation. During this visit, your orthodontist will evaluate your smile, discuss your goals, explain all available treatment options, and recommend the right treatment plan customized to your needs. Our focus is on guiding you through every step of your smile journey with commitment, passion, and dedication to clinical orthodontics. Our orthodontists complete dental school and years of advanced specialty training to deliver the best orthodontic care possible.

Clear Braces

Clear braces offer a discreet alternative to traditional metal braces, using translucent brackets that blend naturally with your teeth. With advanced tieless technology and high-performance archwires, they move teeth efficiently and comfortably while providing excellent control for precise results. Clear braces are ideal for patients who want a subtle appearance without sacrificing the effectiveness of traditional orthodontic treatment.

Tooth Eruption & Shedding

Metal braces are a reliable, time-tested treatment made from durable stainless steel brackets and wires. Modern designs are smaller, more comfortable, and highly effective at correcting a wide range of alignment and bite issues. They remain one of the most efficient options for achieving a straight, healthy smile.

Self-Ligating Braces

Self-ligating braces offer a modern alternative to traditional metal braces by using specialized brackets that hold the archwire in place without the need for elastic ties. This streamlined design can make braces feel more comfortable and easier to clean while still delivering precise, effective tooth movement.

Because there are no elastics, the brackets tend to look smaller and less noticeable. Adjustment appointments are typically quicker, and in many cases, treatment may progress more efficiently. Self-ligating braces are an excellent option for patients seeking a cleaner, lower-maintenance, and potentially faster orthodontic experience.

Post Op Instructions

Angel Aligners provide a clear, comfortable, and discreet alternative to braces. Using advanced digital technology, these custom-made trays gently move teeth into alignment while allowing you to remove them for eating and brushing. They offer an aesthetic, flexible, and effective option for achieving a beautifully aligned smile.

Oral Health & Wellness

Phase I treatment helps guide your child’s growing smile by addressing early bite or spacing issues before all the adult teeth come in. By correcting problems early—such as crowding, jaw growth concerns, or crossbites—we can make room for permanent teeth and often reduce the need for more complex treatment later. Phase I uses simple, child-friendly appliances like expanders, space maintainers, or limited braces to support healthy development and set the stage for a beautiful, balanced smile.

Orthodontic Retainers

After orthodontic treatment is complete, retainers play an essential role in keeping your new smile stable. Worn primarily at night, retainers help maintain the position of your teeth and prevent them from shifting as your bite settles. In some cases, they can also allow for minor adjustments once braces or aligners are removed.During your final visits, your orthodontist will review the different retainer options and provide personalized instructions to ensure long-lasting results and a healthy, confident smile.

Rapid Palatal Expander (RPE)

A Rapid Palatal Expander (RPE) gently widens the upper jaw to create space and improve the way the teeth and jaws fit together. Used mainly in growing children, the expander helps correct crossbites, reduce crowding, and guide healthy facial development. The appliance sits on the roof of the mouth and is activated gradually at home. After expansion is complete, it stays in place briefly to allow the bone to stabilize. An RPE is an effective early treatment that supports proper growth and can simplify future orthodontic care.

Surgical Orthodontics

Surgical orthodontics involves orthognathic surgery and is done to correct misalignments or other abnormalities in the upper jaw and lower jaws. For this type of orthodontics, we work closely with an oral surgeon to create a treatment plan. This surgery and orthodontic treatment solution can help improve speech, breathing, chewing, and appearance.
