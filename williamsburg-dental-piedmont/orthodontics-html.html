Invisalign Clear Braces

For those looking to straighten their crooked and crowded teeth without going through the hassle of metal braces, Invisalign is your answer. Rather than brackets and wires, Invisalign consists of a series of custom-made clear plastic aligners to gradually straighten teeth. They are practically invisible when worn; you can even take them off whenever you need to eat or brush your teeth. With Invisalign , you get the best in comfort, appearance, and effectiveness, all with one treatment.

ClearCorrect Clear Aligners

ClearCorrect Clear Aligners is a discreet orthodontic solution that uses thin plastic trays to straighten teeth over time. They can produce results in as little as six months; however, this depends on the severity of your case. These aligners are generally used for milder orthodontic cases but can produce exceptional results, just like other similar systems.

SureSmile Clear Aligners

SureSmile Clear Aligners may not be as widely known as Invisalign or similar systems, but they are highly effective, as their average treatment time is six months. The trays often come with straight or scalloped edges, depending on your preferred fit and comfort. While wearing SureSmile, you can expect follow-up appointments to occur every one to three months to ensure teeth are moving in the right direction.

Angel Aligners

Angel Aligners offer greater flexibility than Invisalign, as our team may recommend that you wear a set of trays longer if necessary. Instead of a fixed timeline, these aligners are recommended if changes are required along the way. They are still easy to maintain and offer a discreet appearance you can appreciate.

Dandy Clear Aligners

If you want to start orthodontic treatment sooner than you would with traditional braces or other clear aligner systems, Dandy Clear Aligners are what you need. With a 12-day turnaround, you can work toward a straighter set of teeth much sooner.
