Sukhminder Buttar

Sukhminder Buttar , DDS

Member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry

Member of the American Dental Association

Member of the Washington State Dental Association

Member of the King County Dental Association

Member of the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Buttar provides a comprehensive range of dental services, including implants, bone grafting, gum grafting, esthetic crowns, veneers, and wisdom teeth extractions. If you are looking for an implant dentist in Seattle, Dr. Buttar is the best choice.

Dr. Buttar is known for his gentle and creative touch. He enjoys helping each of his patients maintaining the health and function of their teeth, gums, and Overall health. He also helps them to improve their appearance and self-esteem by creating the smile of their dreams. He provides comfortable and personalized care with state-of-the-art techniques and equipment. He cares about his patients and their well-being. He provides them with the best possible personal attention they deserve. Above all, Dr. Buttar is a skilled, knowledgeable, competent, and highly regarded dentist.

Dr. Buttar has completed advanced training in dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, oral cancer and other specialty procedures. He is known as one of the best implant dentists in the Seattle metropolitan area. He continuously takes the latest technological and clinical training to ensure that he remains at the forefront of the areas of his practice. He stays on the top of recent dental science and technology.
