Frequently Asked Questions for Corporate Tech Employees

Dr. Ron Sherman and Dr. Rusul wants you to feel informed, empowered, and confident about your visit.

  • Which Delta Dental networks do you accept? We accept Delta Dental PPO and Delta Dental PPO Plus Premier networks. Most comprehensive health packages offered by corporate employers, such as Amazon or Microsoft, use these specific PPO networks to secure maximum out-of-pocket savings for their staff.
  • Will my insurance cover routine cleanings completely? For the vast majority of standard tech-industry Delta Dental PPO options, routine preventive care, including biannual checkups, standard cleanings, and digital diagnostic X-rays, is covered at 100 % in-network . This means you typically face zero out-of-pocket costs for your primary routine maintenance. You can always confirm with your insurance provider for more information
  • I am a former patient of First Impressions Dental Care. Is my coverage identical? Yes! While our practice has transitioned to The Issaquah Dentist , we remain the same private team in the same facility, offering the same trusted Delta Dental options you have always used.
