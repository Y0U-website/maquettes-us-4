Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Sookyon Lee Dr. Sookyon Lee is a dedicated family dentist providing comprehensive oral health services at SmileOn Dentistry in Centreville, Virginia. With a passion for multi-generational care, Dr. Lee treats patients of all ages, offering specialized attention to everyone from infants to seniors.

Dr. Lee earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from The Ohio State University. She is highly experienced in diagnosing and treating a wide range of dental concerns, ensuring that every patient receives expert clinical care.

During every consultation, Dr. Lee prioritizes patient education. She takes the time to clearly explain specific dental conditions and works collaboratively with patients to develop customized treatment plans that fit their unique needs and lifestyles.

Driven by a commitment to the Centreville community, Dr. Lee believes that great dental health starts with a strong partnership. By listening closely to her patients’ goals and concerns, she helps families maintain and improve their lifelong oral health.

Whether you are looking for a routine dental cleaning, pediatric care, or advanced restorative treatments, Dr. Lee and the team at SmileOn Dentistry are here to provide the high-quality care you deserve.
