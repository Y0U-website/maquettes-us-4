Yass Dastmalchi, DDS

Dr. Yass Dastmalchi received her bachelor’s degree from the University of Minnesota. She then attended the University of Minnesota Dental School. She continued her education at Boston University Goldman School of Dentistry where she received her Certificate of Graduate Studies in Endodontics. She has been practicing endodontics in Lafayette since 1996. When she is not practicing endodontics she loves spending time with her husband and two daughters. She also enjoys traveling, reading and zumba.

Dr. Dastmalchi is dedicated to providing you with the highest standard of care in a comfortable environment.
