Dr. Leila Soltani finished Dental School in 1999, and graduated from the Advanced Education Program in Periodontics as a Board Certified Periodontist from the National University of Iran in 2004. From there, she started her first private practice as a Periodontist and worked in Tehran from 2004-2012.

Dr. Soltani moved to Los Angeles in 2012 and she was able to obtain her Preceptorship in Advanced Implantology from UCLA in 2013.

Dr. Soltani became board certified in Periodontics and Implant Dentistry in 2017.

Currently Dr. Soltani is a Diplomate with the American Board of Periodontology and Implant Dentistry and completed a Fellowship in Advanced Laser Dentistry (2021).
