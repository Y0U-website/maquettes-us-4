Mikhail Seguritan, DDS

Mikhail Seguritan, DDS, is well respected for his aesthetic mastery in dentistry. At Seguritan Dental Arts in Gramercy Park, Manhattan, he enjoys helping patients resolve their dental concerns by applying his dental expertise. Dr. Seguritan strives to help each patient achieve a bright and healthy smile.

Dr. Seguritan graduated from Fordham University with a degree in biology. He continued his education by earning a Doctorate of Dental Surgery from SUNY-Stony Brook Dental School in Long Island, New York.

Today, Dr. Seguritan is a member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Association, New York County Dental Society, International Congress of Oral Implantologists, and the Academy of Laser Dentistry. He is also a member of several dental study clubs.

Throughout his career, Dr. Seguritan has received praise for his gentle and steady touch. He has a reassuring chairside manner that puts nervous patients at ease. Along with his compassionate office staff, Dr. Seguritan provides personalized attention that helps patients feel confident about their dental care.

When he isn’t assisting patients, Dr. Seguritan enjoys a variety of physical activities. He knows that staying active helps promote good physical and mental health, and his hobbies include Muay Thai Kickboxing, skiing, and wakeboarding.
