Location, Directions and Parking

Depending on the time of arrival, some metered parking may be available on North Broad Street or in the immediate vicinity of the dental school. Public transportation is conveniently located close to our dental clinics, including SEPTA bus lines and the Broad Street Line, which stops outside the Kornberg building at the Allegheny Station.

Clinic Hours

Our clinics are generally open Monday through Friday, from 8 a.m. until 5 p.m. Summer hours may vary. Clinic hours vary. Please contact the clinic to confirm its hours of operation. No walk-ins are permitted at this time due to COVID-19 . Most appointments with student doctors occur at either 9 a.m. or 2 p.m. Please see Temple University’s holiday calendar for planned closures .

The Call Center phone number is 215-707-2900. The staff at the Call Center can answer all questions or direct you to the appropriate clinic as necessary.
