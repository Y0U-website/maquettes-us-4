Types of Dental Implants

Single-tooth implants:

A single-tooth implant replaces one missing tooth with a titanium post placed in the jawbone. A custom crown is then attached on top of the implant. The result looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth.

Implant-supported bridges:

For multiple missing teeth, implant-supported dental bridges are anchored by implants instead of neighboring teeth. This protects surrounding teeth while providing a stable, long-lasting restoration.

Implant-retained dentures:

Implant-retained dentures snap securely onto several implants placed in the jaw. They eliminate slippage and offer better comfort, chewing, and speaking compared to traditional dentures.

All-on-4 and All-on-6

These techniques use four to six strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth. They provide a fixed, stable solution—even for patients with some bone loss.
