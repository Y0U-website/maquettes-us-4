All-On-4 Dental Implant Process Placement After your treatment has been approved, our team will schedule your oral surgery and create a detailed surgical plan. Dr. Kerns will make small openings in the gums and securely place four dental implants into the jawbone at strategic angles. Once the implants are in place, the area will be cleaned and sutured to begin the healing process. You’ll leave surgery with a secure, natural-looking set of temporary teeth the very same day.

Healing & Osseointegration

After your All-on-4 surgery, your gums and jawbone will begin to heal. The incision areas typically heal within 1–2 weeks, while the implants will fuse with your jawbone over 3–6 months through a process called osseointegration. During this phase, we’ll schedule follow-up visits to monitor healing and ensure everything is progressing smoothly.

Applying the restoration

Once the implants have fully integrated with your jaw, we’ll place your final restoration—a custom-designed full arch of teeth. Unlike a single crown, this restoration replaces all missing teeth in the upper or lower arch and is securely attached to the implants. It’s crafted to look natural, feel comfortable, and function just like real teeth.

Yes—terms like All-on-4, full mouth implants, fixed full arch, hybrid dentures, and implant-retained dentures are often used interchangeably. They all refer to a treatment that replaces an entire arch of missing teeth using dental implants to support a fixed set of prosthetic teeth. While the terminology may vary, the concept is the same: a permanent, stable, and natural-looking solution for full-mouth restoration.

Both All-on-4 and All-on-6 use dental implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth—the main difference is the number of implants used. All-on-4 uses four implants, while All-on-6 uses six. In general, All-on-6 may offer added stability and support for patients with enough bone density, but both are highly effective, long-lasting options. Your dentist will help determine which is the better fit for your specific needs during your consultation.
