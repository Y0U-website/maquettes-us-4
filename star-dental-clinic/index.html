The practice

Our skilled, compassionate team, under the guidance of Dr. Esther Ghebremichael and Dr. Li, manages everything from regular cleanings and exams for the whole family to emergency treatment, crowns, bridges, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, and sleep apnea therapy.

Star Dental Clinic provides quality dental care conveniently in your neighborhood and caters to your schedule. Your comfort is important to us, and we use 3D imaging and intraoral scanners to eliminate messy impression materials.

We offer comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages and pride ourselves on catering to a diverse group of patients through inclusivity and understanding. We speak many languages at Star Dental Clinic, including Spanish, Somali, Arabic, Chinese, Tigrinya, Amharic, and English.

Rest assured that regardless of your dental needs, we will treat you with the most compassionate, respectful care available.
