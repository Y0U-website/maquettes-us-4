Who Is a Good Candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign clear aligners can effectively treat a wide range of orthodontic issues in children and teenagers. The best candidates are typically children ages 12 and older who have most of their permanent teeth and can commit to wearing aligners consistently.

Orthodontic Issues Invisalign Can Address

During the consultation, our team evaluates each child’s specific orthodontic needs, dental development, and maturity level to determine if Invisalign is the appropriate treatment choice.

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps and spacing between teeth
  • Overbites and underbites
  • Crossbites and open bites
  • Mild to moderate tooth rotations

Invisalign vs. Traditional Braces: Making the Right Choice

Understanding the differences between Invisalign and traditional braces helps families make informed decisions about orthodontic treatment. While both options can achieve excellent results, each has distinct advantages depending on your child’s needs and lifestyle.

Key Differences

Appearance : Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible, while traditional braces feature visible metal brackets and wires that can impact a child’s confidence during treatment.

Comfort : Clear aligners are smooth and comfortable with no sharp edges, eliminating the mouth irritation that can occur with metal orthodontic hardware.

Convenience : Removable aligners allow normal eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing, while traditional braces require dietary restrictions and special cleaning tools.

Activities : Invisalign is compatible with all sports and activities without risk of mouth injuries from metal components.

Appointments : Invisalign typically requires shorter, less frequent visits compared to traditional orthodontic adjustments.

Caring for Invisalign Aligners

Proper care of Invisalign aligners is straightforward and helps maintain treatment effectiveness. Children should rinse aligners with cool water when removing them and brush gently with a soft toothbrush to remove plaque and debris. Daily soaking in cleaning solution keeps aligners fresh and clear.

Daily Care Routine

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene remains important throughout Invisalign treatment. Children should brush and floss after every meal before reinserting aligners to prevent bacterial buildup and maintain optimal oral health.
