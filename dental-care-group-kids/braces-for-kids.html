Braces for Kids

  • Improved Chewing Function : Correcting bite problems enhances your child’s ability to chew food properly, leading to better digestion and nutrition absorption.
  • Boosted Self-Confidence : A beautiful smile significantly impacts a child’s self-esteem and social interactions, often leading to improved academic performance and friendships.
  • Prevention of Future Problems : Early orthodontic intervention can prevent more serious issues from developing, reduce excessive tooth wear, and decrease jaw joint disorder risks.
  • Speech Improvement : Braces can help correct speech impediments resulting from misaligned teeth, leading to clearer pronunciation and improved communication skills.

Orthodontic Issues Braces Can Correct

Modern braces can address a wide variety of orthodontic problems, from simple spacing issues to complex bite disorders. Understanding these common conditions helps parents recognize when their child might benefit from orthodontic evaluation and treatment.

  • Crowded Teeth : Dental crowding occurs when there isn’t enough jaw space for all teeth to fit properly, leading to overlapping and increased decay risk.
  • Spacing Issues : Gaps between teeth can result from missing teeth or jaw size discrepancies, potentially affecting both appearance and function.
  • Overbite Problems : An overbite occurs when upper front teeth significantly overlap lower teeth, potentially causing excessive wear and jaw joint problems.
  • Underbite Correction : Underbites happen when lower teeth protrude beyond upper teeth, affecting chewing, speaking, and facial appearance.
  • Crossbite Issues : Crossbites occur when some upper teeth fit inside lower teeth instead of outside, potentially causing uneven wear and jaw asymmetry.

The Orthodontic Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

Your child’s orthodontic journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation. During the first exam, our orthodontist performs a physical examination, takes X-rays, and may make impressions to thoroughly evaluate your child’s teeth and jaw development. Our orthodontist examines teeth, jaws, and facial structure to identify any alignment issues. Digital X-rays and photographs help create a complete picture of your child’s oral health. We discuss treatment options and answer all your questions during this appointment.

Treatment Planning

After the initial evaluation, we develop a customized treatment plan for your child. This plan outlines specific goals, estimated treatment time, and recommended appliances. We review the plan with parents and explain each step of the process. Cost estimates and payment options are discussed to make treatment affordable for your family.

Braces Placement

The day your child gets braces is exciting ! The appointment typically takes one to two hours, during which brackets are carefully bonded to each tooth. Archwires are then placed and secured with elastic ties or clips. We explain proper oral hygiene techniques and provide detailed care instructions.

Regular Adjustments

Adjustment appointments occur every four to eight weeks throughout treatment. During these visits, we check progress and make necessary wire changes or adjustments. These appointments usually take 20-30 minutes and are important for maintaining treatment progress.

Braces Removal and Retention

After removing brackets and cleaning teeth, we take impressions for retainers. Retainers are essential for maintaining results and preventing teeth from shifting back to their original positions. We provide detailed retainer care instructions and schedule follow-up appointments.

Caring for Teeth During Braces Treatment

Daily Oral Hygiene

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene becomes even more important with braces. Children should brush at least twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Special orthodontic toothbrushes can help clean around brackets and wires more effectively. Brushing after meals helps remove food particles that can lead to decay.

Flossing with Braces

Daily flossing remains essential during orthodontic treatment. Floss threaders or orthodontic floss make it easier to clean between teeth and under wires. Water flossers can supplement traditional floss and help remove food debris from hard-to-reach areas.

Dietary Considerations

Certain foods can damage braces or make cleaning more difficult. Hard foods like nuts, ice, and hard candy should be avoided. Sticky foods such as caramel, gum, and taffy can pull brackets off teeth. Crunchy foods like popcorn and chips can break wires or get stuck in braces.

Protective Measures

Children who participate in sports should wear protective mouthguards to prevent mouth injuries and damage to their braces. Special orthodontic mouthguards are available that accommodate braces while providing adequate protection.

The Importance of Consistent Professional Care

Regular professional pediatric dental care becomes critical during orthodontic treatment. Children with braces should maintain their routine dental exams and professional cleanings every six months to monitor overall oral health. Scheduled orthodontic check-ins every four to eight weeks ensure treatment progresses as planned and allow for timely adjustments.
