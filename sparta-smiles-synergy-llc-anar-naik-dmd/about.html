About Sparta Smiles Synergy

0 About Us Welcome to Sparta Smiles Synergy! The general dental practice of Dr. Anar Naik was founded on the principles of teamwork, compassion and comfort.

Prior to becoming the owner of the practice, Dr. Naik had the good fortune and privilege of working alongside Dr. Stephen Candio and learning from his mentorship for over a year before his retirement. She has aimed to uphold her commitment to quality dental care since Dr. Candio passed the torch to her in November 2021.

Dr. Naik is committed to serving the residents of Sparta and nearby for many years to come.
