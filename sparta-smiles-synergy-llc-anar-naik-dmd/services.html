Classes Sparta Smiles Synergy

  • Orthodontics Orthodontics is the field of braces, Invisalign, and straightening teeth. We currently participate in a program called Candid Pro, a less invasive and more interactive alternative to both braces and Invisalign. For any other cases, we refer to an orthodontic specialist.
  • Oral Surgery Oral surgery involves extractions, implants, and other procedures that require surgery in the mouth. Currently, we perform simple extractions in the office and refer more complicated extractions as well as implant surgery to an oral surgeon.
  • Teeth Whitening We provide both in office teeth whitening services and at home bleaching kits to help improve the brightness and esthetics of your teeth, lasting up to six months. Currently, we use GLO Science for professional whitening, you can read more about the science behind and treatment options included in the system we implement here: https://gloscience.com/pages/glo-professional-treatments-1?srsltid=AfmBOoqwBvl2KHx7nIHcAmRKGVTyu4o8zMsYVMP1HaHcDpzgtqmWsTW_
