How the Dental Implant Process Works

A dental implant is a small titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root. The post is placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure, where it gradually fuses with the bone over several months in a process called osseointegration. Once fully integrated, the implant becomes a permanent anchor for a custom crown, bridge, or denture.

The full implant process typically takes several months from start to finish, but the results are well worth the investment of time. During healing, you will not be without a tooth. We provide temporary solutions so you can continue smiling and eating comfortably throughout treatment. Once your implant has healed, we secure the final restoration in place for a beautiful, permanent result.

The typical stages of dental implant treatment:

Not every patient is the same, and some cases require additional steps like bone grafting before the implant can be placed. We will evaluate your specific situation and explain every step clearly so you always know what to expect.

  • Consultation and 3D imaging to evaluate your jawbone and plan the procedure
  • Placement of the titanium implant post into the jawbone
  • Healing period of three to six months for osseointegration
  • Placement of the abutment, a small connector piece that links the implant to the crown
  • Final restoration with a custom crown, bridge, or denture crafted to match your natural teeth

Tips

Caring for Your Dental Implant

  • Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss around the implant daily to keep the gumline clean and free of bacteria.
  • Keep up with your regular dental cleanings and checkups every six months.
  • Avoid using your teeth to open packaging or chew on hard objects like ice and pens.
  • If you grind your teeth at night, let us know so we can fit you with a protective night guard.
