What Happens During Implant Placement Surgery

Before surgery, we use 3D imaging to map out the exact position of your implant. This planning stage allows us to avoid sensitive structures like nerves and sinuses and ensures the implant is placed at the ideal angle for your final restoration. On the day of the procedure, your comfort is our top priority.

The area is numbed with local anesthesia, and sedation is available for patients who prefer to be more relaxed. A small opening is made in the gum to access the jawbone, and the implant post is carefully placed into the prepared site. In many cases, the gum is then closed over the implant to protect it during healing. The entire procedure usually takes about an hour for a single implant.

Most patients are surprised by how smooth the experience is. With proper planning and gentle technique, implant placement is far less intimidating than it sounds. We stay with you through every phase and are available if you have questions during healing.

What to expect in the weeks after surgery:

  • Mild swelling and discomfort for the first few days, easily managed with medication
  • A soft diet recommended during the first week to protect the surgical site
  • Gradual return to normal activities, usually within a day or two
  • Osseointegration, the process of the implant fusing with bone, over three to six months
  • Placement of the abutment and custom crown once healing is complete

Tips

Supporting Your Healing After Implant Placement

The first few days after implant placement are the most important. Following these steps will support a smooth recovery and protect the healing process.

Keep all follow-up appointments so we can track your progress and plan the next steps in your treatment. Call us if you notice unusual pain, swelling, or any signs of infection.

  • Apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek to reduce swelling during the first 24 hours.
  • Take any prescribed medications exactly as directed by our team.
  • Stick to soft, cool foods like yogurt, mashed potatoes, and smoothies during the first few days.
  • Avoid chewing on the surgical side until the area has started to heal.
  • Do not smoke or drink through a straw, as both can interfere with healing.
  • Rinse gently with warm salt water starting the day after surgery, but avoid brushing the implant site until we clear it.
