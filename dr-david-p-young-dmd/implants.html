A Permanent Solution That Looks and Feels Natural

Losing a tooth can be a deeply unsettling experience. Thanks to today’s advanced implant technology and techniques, you no longer have to live with that gap. Dental implants offer a permanent replacement that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural tooth restoring not just your smile, but your confidence and quality of life.

A dental implant consists of three components. First, a screw-like titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as a stable and durable root. Second, an abutment is attached to the post, forming the base for the final restoration. Third, a custom-crafted crown also called a restoration is placed on top, completing your new tooth with a natural appearance and full functionality
