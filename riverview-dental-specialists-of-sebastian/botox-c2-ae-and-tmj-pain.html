FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cosmetic Botox?

No. The neurotoxins we use are administered strictly for therapeutic dental indications , such as relieving muscle-related jaw pain, TMJ symptoms, headaches, and orofacial discomfort. Cosmetic treatment is not the focus of this service.

How does neurotoxin treatment help with TMJ or pain?

Neurotoxins work by temporarily relaxing overactive muscles. When jaw and facial muscles are constantly tense or overworked, they can cause pain, headaches, and joint strain. Relaxing these muscles can reduce pressure, discomfort, and muscle fatigue.

Am I a good candidate?

Many patients with muscle-related jaw pain, clenching or grinding, facial tension, or recurring tension-type headaches may be candidates. A consultation is required to determine if this treatment is appropriate based on your medical and dental history.

Is it safe?

When administered by a trained, licensed dental professional and used within established therapeutic guidelines, neurotoxin treatment is considered safe for appropriate patients. As with any medical treatment, potential risks and side effects will be discussed during your consultation.

How long do results last?

Results vary, but therapeutic effects typically last 3–4 months . Some patients may require periodic repeat treatment to maintain benefits.

Does it hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Injections are quick and well-tolerated, and no downtime is usually required.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your consultation is complimentary and allows us to determine whether this treatment aligns with your needs before any procedure is scheduled.

Will insurance cover this?

Coverage varies by plan and diagnosis. You may be able to use your HSA benefits for therapeutic neurotoxin treatment. Our team will be happy to discuss potential insurance considerations or self-pay options during your visit.
