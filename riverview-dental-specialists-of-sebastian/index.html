If you struggle with jaw pain, facial muscle tension, frequent headaches, or migraines, you’re not alone — and you don’t have to simply live with it. We’re happy to offer therapeutic BOTOX® and neurotoxin treatment as a comfortable, non-surgical option designed to help reduce muscle-related pain and tension.

In dentistry, these treatments are commonly used to relax overactive facial and jaw muscles that contribute to TMJ disorders, clenching and grinding, orofacial pain, and chronic headaches. By calming the muscles involved, many patients experience decreased discomfort, improved jaw function, and fewer tension-related symptoms.

All treatment is provided by a licensed dental professional with advanced training in facial anatomy and neuromuscular care, right here in the office you already trust.
