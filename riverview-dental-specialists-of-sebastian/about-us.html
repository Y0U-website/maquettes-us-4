About Riverview Dental Specialists of Sebastian

At Riverview Dental Specialists of Sebastian , our mission is to provide exceptional, patient-focused care through advanced dental services in oral surgery, TMJ therapy, sleep apnea appliance treatment, and the non-surgical management of orofacial pain .

We are committed to listening first, treating thoughtfully, and delivering care with compassion, precision, and respect. Every patient is unique, and we strive to create personalized treatment plans that prioritize comfort, safety, and long-term wellness.

By combining specialized training with a supportive, welcoming environment, we aim to reduce pain, restore function, and improve quality of life—helping our patients feel heard, informed, and confident in their care.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Christina Gory, DDS Dr. Christina Gory is an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon with advanced fellowship training in Head and Neck Oncology and Reconstructive Surgery. A South Florida native, she completed her undergraduate studies at Florida State University, earned her dental degree from New York University, completed her Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at Rutgers University, and subsequently pursued her fellowship in El Paso, Texas.

Dr. Gory provides comprehensive oral and maxillofacial surgical care, including dentoalveolar surgery, dental implants, bone grafting, oral pathology, facial trauma, and the management of benign and malignant diseases of the head and neck. She is committed to delivering compassionate, patient-centered care while utilizing the latest surgical techniques and evidence-based treatment approaches. Dr. Gory is passionate about helping patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident throughout their surgical care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Gory enjoys cooking, gardening, sewing, spending time with family, and taking advantage of life along Florida’s coast. As a South Florida native, she is excited to return home to Florida after many years of training and is grateful for the opportunity to care for patients in a community she is proud to call home.

Dr. Gory is proud to provide specialized oral surgery services part time on every other Monday at Riverview Dental Specialists and is committed to delivering excellent care in a calm, compassionate environment.

For Dr. Holly Hamilton, dentistry has always been about more than teeth — it’s about helping people feel better. After more than 20 years of caring for patients in this community , one thing remains at the core of her practice: getting people out of pain and back to living comfortably . Dr. Hamilton is a general dentist who’s entire focus in dentistry now is comprehensive non-surgical TMJ treatment and helping manage sleep apnea with appliance therapy

Dr. Hamilton sees firsthand how jaw pain, facial tension, headaches, migraines, and sleep problems can affect everyday life. That’s why she pursued advanced training in therapeutic Botox® and neurotoxin injections for the treatment of TMJ disorders, orofacial pain, and migraines , as well as oral appliance therapy for sleep apnea . She takes the time to listen, understand what her patients are experiencing, and create thoughtful, individualized treatment plans focused on real relief — not quick fixes.

Patients often comment on how approachable and reassuring Dr. Hamilton is. She believes that when patients feel heard and understood, better outcomes follow. Her goal is simple: help reduce pain, restore function, and improve quality of life in a way that feels comfortable and collaborative.

Outside the office, Dr. Hamilton is an active member of the community and a proud mom of two sons . She enjoys photography, volunteering, and history , and brings that same curiosity and care into her work every day.

Dr. Hamilton is grateful to care for generations of families in the area and considers it a privilege to help her patients feel relief, comfort, and confidence again.

Meet the Team

Corrine

Corrine is one of the welcoming faces you’ll meet at the front desk, and she truly sets the tone for our office. Known for her kindness and patience, Corrine cares deeply about helping patients feel comfortable, supported, and heard—especially those who are dealing with pain or uncertainty.

From your first phone call to your visit in the office, Corrine is focused on making the process as smooth and stress-free as possible. She understands that dental pain can be overwhelming, and she’s always ready to listen, answer questions, and help guide you through scheduling, treatment planning, and next steps with compassion and care.

Outside of the office, Corrine enjoys spending time with her family and brings that same warmth and friendliness into everything she does. She takes pride in being a reassuring presence and helping patients take the first step toward feeling better.

Mike

Mike is the go-to person behind the scenes at our office, making sure everything runs smoothly so you can focus on your care. With over 10 years of experience handling dental insurance, equipment maintenance, and practice IT, he ensures that even as an out-of-network office, your claims, imaging, and clinical notes are filed accurately and efficiently. Mike works tirelessly to help your insurance company reimburse you as quickly as possible, taking the stress out of the process.

Outside of the office, Mike loves kayaking, capturing great moments through photography, and sharing his endless stories—he’s always ready to brighten your day with a smile and a laugh.

Maira

Maira is a kind, caring, and a dedicated dental assistant who plays an essential role in making every patient’s visit smooth and comfortable. She is proficient in all areas of dentistry and takes pride in ensuring that each patient receives the highest quality care. Maira patiently answers questions, explains treatments in a way that’s easy to understand, and goes above and beyond to make sure patients feel supported and at ease during their time in the office.

Outside of work, Maira enjoys spending quality time with her family and makes sure to make memories with the ones she loves!
