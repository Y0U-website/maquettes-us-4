Dental Implants in Naperville, IL

Full dental implants are a popular option for permanent tooth replacement. Unlike traditional dentures , full dental implants are fixed in place and function like natural teeth. They are a reliable solution for individuals with missing teeth or those looking to replace broken tooth implants. Full dental implants provide a sturdy and long-lasting foundation, making them a preferred choice for permanent dental implants. Whether it’s a single tooth or an entire set, full dental implants offer a secure and durable alternative to traditional removable dentures.

If you would like to learn more about these benefits, then schedule a consultation with an implant dentist in your area. We can help you determine if this tooth replacement treatment is the right fit for your unique needs.

  • Other Restorative Treatments: If multiple teeth are missing, one or more implants can be used to support bigger restorative treatments, such as a bridge or implant-supported dentures.
  • Long-Lasting Results: With good oral care at home and regular checkups , a dental implant is a restoration that can last for the rest of your life.
  • Maintain Function: Replacing missing teeth with a tooth implant ensures that you can continue eating and talking as normal.
  • Minimize Complications: Placing the implant post in the bone helps to preserve bone density and prevent complications related to tooth loss. This restoration minimizes the chances of other teeth shifting in the mouth.

Replace Missing Teeth with Dental Implants

Dental implants are designed with several parts to achieve the ideal results for your tooth replacement services. After a tooth has been extracted, an implant post is secured into the jawbone. This post assimilates into the bone. Then a crown is secured to the abutment at the top of the implant post.

Dental implants are effective for replacing one missing tooth, as well as multiple missing teeth. You can fill in the gaps and restore your confidence.
