Dr. Jose Rosalez

Close menu Dr. Jose Rosalez Dr. Jose Rosalez grew up just a few miles from Naperville Smiles Dental. While attending Waubonsie Valley High School he met his wife and mother of his two kids, Elsa and Enzo.

After earning his dental medicine degree, Dr. Rosalez practiced in Chicago for many years. Although he and his wife love the city, they both have fond memories of growing up in the suburbs. In hopes of giving their children the same, they have decided to move back and be closer to their family.

Skilled in all aspects of dentistry, Dr. Rosalez believes in a comprehensive and patient centered approach to oral health care. Dr. Rosalez has received extensive post graduate training to better serve his patients. Staying up to date with the latest technologies and techniques is of utmost importance. That is why Dr. Rosalez spends several weeks each year traveling the country to continue his education. As a patient, you can expect Dr. Rosalez to take his time to truly hear your concerns and help you accomplish your oral health goals with compassion.

Outside of the office Dr. Rosalez enjoys spending time with his family and exploring new places.
