Our Founder

Dr. Laura Justice Founder, Visionary, and AACD Accredited Fellow

Crafting Smiles that Turn Heads Since 1999

Justice Dental was founded by Dr. Laura Justice, one of the top 25 dentists in the nation, whose passion for excellence and patient-centered care continues to shape every aspect of the practice. With a keen eye for aesthetics and a deep understanding of functional dentistry, Dr. Justice has built a reputation for creating smiles that are as healthy as they are beautiful. Her leadership, vision, and commitment to innovation have made Justice Dental a trusted name in advanced cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

Read more

A native of Pikeville, Kentucky, Dr. Justice earned her undergraduate degree in Biology from Transylvania University in Lexington and graduated with honors from the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry in 1989. She has pursued extensive post-graduate education in advanced cosmetic dentistry from leading institutions, including Louisiana State University, The Center for Cosmetic Excellence, The Eubank Institute, and The Kois Center.

Dr. Justice is also the co-founder and past President of the Kentucky Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and has served on the Kentucky Board of Dentistry. She maintains active membership in the Bluegrass Dental Society, Kentucky Dental Association, American Dental Association, American Association of Women Dentists, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, and is a Pre-Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry. She has also served as an Examiner for the American Board of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Recognized for her cosmetic expertise on a national scale, Dr. Justice is one of the official dentists for the Miss Kentucky Pageant and frequently works with television and film personalities to help perfect their smiles.

A strong advocate for continued learning and innovation, Dr. Justice is deeply committed to using the latest technology and techniques to provide comfortable, cutting-edge care. Her distinguished level of skill and dedication has allowed her to change the lives of patients not only in Lexington but across the country, one confident smile at a time.
