While traditional dental bridges have helped many patients replace missing teeth, they come with certain limitations. A bridge typically requires the alteration of healthy neighboring teeth to serve as support for the replacement tooth. This process can weaken those teeth over time and make them more susceptible to decay or damage. In contrast, a single dental implant stands independently in the jawbone, eliminating the need to modify surrounding teeth.

Implants also stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur after tooth loss. This is something dental bridges cannot achieve. Additionally, bridges may need to be replaced every seven to 10 years, while dental implants are designed for long-term stability when properly cared for. For many patients, the natural appearance, improved function, and preservation of oral health make implants a more beneficial choice.

  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure
  • No impact on adjacent teeth
  • Natural look and feel
  • Easy to maintain with regular brushing
  • Restores full chewing function
  • No dietary restrictions

Am I a Good Candidate for a Single Tooth Implant?

Most adults with one or more missing teeth can be candidates for dental implants. However, certain factors need to be evaluated.

  • Adequate bone density and height in the jaw
  • Healthy gums and oral tissues
  • Good overall health
  • Commitment to oral hygiene
  • Do not smoke or willing to quit
  • Realistic expectations

Additional Considerations:

Your dentist will perform a comprehensive exam, which may include 3D imaging, to determine whether your jawbone can support an implant. In cases where bone volume is lacking, procedures like bone grafting or sinus lifts can help prepare the site. Your overall health, medications, and lifestyle habits will also be considered to ensure the highest chance of long-term success.

  • Age is typically not a limiting factor for healthy adults
  • Bone grafting may be an option if bone density is insufficient
  • Certain medical conditions may require special precautions
  • Smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure

The Single Tooth Implant Procedure in Chicago

Replacing a missing tooth with a dental implant is a multi-step process that takes place over several months, allowing for proper healing and integration. Here’s what you can expect:

Initial Consultation (Week 1): During your first appointment, the dentist will evaluate your mouth, review your medical history, take diagnostic images, and create a personalized treatment plan. This is also when you’ll have the opportunity to discuss goals, expectations, and any concerns.

Implant Placement (Week 2–4): The implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia, ensuring your comfort throughout the procedure. For patients needing additional preparation, such as tooth extraction or bone grafting, these steps may be completed first.

Healing Period (3–6 Months): During this stage, the implant undergoes osseointegration, where it fuses with the jawbone. This crucial process creates a strong foundation for your future crown. While healing, you may receive a temporary restoration to maintain appearance and function.

Abutment Placement (Month 4–6): Once healing is complete, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant. This minor procedure is performed under local anesthesia and typically requires minimal recovery time.

Crown Fitting (Month 5–7): Your dentist will take impressions to design a custom crown that matches your surrounding teeth in shape, size, and color. Once ready, the crown is securely placed, completing your new smile. Patients often remark that their single dental implant in Chicago feels completely natural from day one.

How Long Does a Single Tooth Implant Last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime.

The implant post itself is made from biocompatible materials that integrate with bone, providing lasting stability.

Following these guidelines will help protect both the implant and surrounding teeth, ensuring you enjoy your restored smile for many years. If you notice changes in bite, comfort, or crown integrity, prompt dental care will keep your tooth replacement in Chicago functioning at its best.

  • Brush and floss regularly
  • Use antimicrobial mouthwash
  • Schedule regular dental checkups
  • Avoid chewing on hard objects
  • Don’t smoke
  • Address teeth grinding if applicable
