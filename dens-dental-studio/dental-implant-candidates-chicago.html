Am I a candidate for dental implants if I have gum disease?

Gum disease does not automatically disqualify you, but it must be treated before dental implants can be placed. Healthy gums are essential for supporting implants and ensuring long-term success.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Smokers may still qualify for dental implants, but smoking can increase the risk of complications and slow healing. Reducing or quitting smoking can significantly improve the chances of success.

Is there an age limit for dental implants?

There is no upper age limit for dental implants. As long as a patient has good overall health and sufficient jawbone density, implants can be a suitable option for adults of all ages.

What if I don’t have enough bone for implants?

If you lack sufficient jawbone density, procedures like bone grafting can help rebuild the bone and make dental implants possible in the future.

How do I know if dental implants are right for me?

The best way to determine if dental implants are right for you is through a comprehensive consultation that includes an oral exam, imaging, and a review of your medical history.
