Titanium vs Zirconia Implants

When it comes to materials used for dental implants, the two most common options are titanium and zirconia. Each has unique advantages, and your implant dentist in Chicago can help determine which is best for your needs.

Titanium Implants

Titanium has been the standard material for dental implants for decades. It’s strong, lightweight, and biocompatible—meaning it fuses well with the bone. Titanium implants often come in two parts: the implant post and the abutment, allowing more flexibility in placement and restoration. A third component, usually a dental crown, is placed on top.

Advantages of titanium implants include:

  • High success rate and long track record
  • Excellent osseointegration
  • Durability under heavy bite forces
  • More options for various clinical situations

Zirconia Implants

Zirconia implants are made from a tooth-colored ceramic material and are often chosen for their aesthetic appeal and metal-free composition. They are a one-piece design, which can simplify placement in certain cases.
