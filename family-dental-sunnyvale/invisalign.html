Invisalign

An innovative and dedicatedly molded appliance introduced by Smart Track is the best recommended clear aligner by dentists all over the world.

A brilliant alternative to braces, Invisalign, the orthodontic appliance has paved excellent results for patients who are coping with dental dilemmas for years. They are proven to be the most sought-after aligners recommended by dentists because of their easy-to-use feature and much more.

Invisalign are clear aligners that are used to rectify the alignment of misaligned teeth. They are removable aligners made to fit exactly into the shape of the tooth. Aligners are customized and fabricated for patients in order to mend cases of misalignment and gap between the teeth. Aligners can be worn by teens as well adults.

How does Invisalign work?

Before getting aligners, your dentist will take a quick scan which includes preparing 3D images of your teeth. Scans for this process are generally done in order to draw out a treatment plan, recognize the exact movement of the teeth and to rule out an approximate period of treatment.

Invisalign aligners will be engineered using a computerized technology where the aligner is developed with the help of these 3D graphical images. Every edge of the aligner will be custom made to suit your needs.

Aligners are advised to be worn 20-22 hours in a day and have them changed once in two weeks. Aligners provide the patient with the liberty of removing them while eating and brushing helping patients to maintain oral hygiene.

How is Invisalign different?

Invisalign sets them apart from the others as they are known to be the most user-friendly dental appliance amongst patients. Invisalign are removable orthodontic tools making them the next-level alternative to orthodontic treatment.

Here are some of the reasons why we recommend Invisalign:

  • They are translucent in the material making them look virtually invisible. This will allow patients to wear them with confidence, unlike the traditional braces that stands out.
  • Invisalign will not prevent you from eating and drinking your favorite food as they are removable.
  • Invisalign are comfortable to wear as they are non-metallic.
  • Their non-metallic texture prevents any breakage or loosening of wires.
  • Teenagers are more likely to gain confidence while in treatment.
  • Allows patients to easily observe results.

Invisalign Teen

Most teenagers would have a hard time deciding whether this is the right time to consider wearing braces as this will directly affect their self-esteem. Invisalign brings you a whole new advancement in the field of orthodontic care. This clear aligner will, in fact, motivate patients to wear them as their translucent nature will almost mimic the color of the teeth making it hard to notice. Their non-metallic texture will be comfortable for patients to wear them without distracting or disturbing day-to-day activities.

Invisalign Adult

Invisalign provides adults with an opportunity to continue their orthodontic treatment even while in the rush of their busy lives. With absolutely no worries of brackets or wires, these virtually invisible aligners will take a while to notice that they are on. Being removable, they provide patients with the ease of removing them while brushing and flossing.
