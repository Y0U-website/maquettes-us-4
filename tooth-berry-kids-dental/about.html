About Us

Transforming Dentalcare, One Patient at a Time

At Tooth Berry Kids Dental, we believe every child deserves a dental experience that feels gentle, joyful, and empowering, where healthy smiles and happy hearts grow together.

At Tooth Berry Kids Dental, our mission is to make every dental visit a positive, nurturing, and stress-free experience for children and their families.

Our Providers

Dr. Eman Ghoneim DDS, Mc

Dr. Eman Ghoneim was born in Boston, Massachusetts, raised in Alexandria, Egypt, and is a proud mom of four wonderful children. She earned her Bachelor&#x27;s Degree in Dental Medicine and Surgery from the Faculty of Dentistry, Alexandria University, in 1996, completing her internship the following year.

Her passion for children&#x27;s oral health led her to pursue a Master&#x27;s Degree in Pediatric Dentistry in 2004, where she focused her research on the effects of conscious sedation in managing young patients. Over the years, she has gained extensive experience treating children of all ages at Naga Dental Center in Alexandria and Jeddah Dental Center in Saudi Arabia.

After moving to California in 2010, Dr. Ghoneim obtained her dental license from the California Dental Board and has since practiced as a general dentist with a specialization in pediatric care. She has completed numerous psychology and behavioral courses to help children feel comfortable and confident during their visits.

Dr. Ghoneim brings her passion for gentle, compassionate dentistry to Tooth Berry Kids Dental in Carmichael, California, a fun, kid-friendly practice where every child can feel safe, happy, and proud of their smile.

We believe knowledge builds confidence. Our team takes time to teach children and parents how to care for their smiles through fun, easy-to-understand guidance that encourages lifelong healthy habits.

We hold ourselves to the highest standard of pediatric dental care, combining skill, experience, and modern technology to deliver precise, gentle, and effective treatments.

We see parents as partners in their child&#x27;s care. Through open communication, education, and trust, we work together to support bright, healthy smiles that last a lifetime.

Comfort

From the first hello to the final high-five, we focus on creating an environment that feels calm, friendly, and fun, helping every child feel safe and confident in the dental chair.
