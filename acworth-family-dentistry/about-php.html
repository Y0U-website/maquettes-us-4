About Acworth Family Dentistry

Our goal at Acworth Family Dentistry is to provide quality dental care to our patients in a friendly, comfortable environment. We utilize the latest technology in the industry along with the latest computer technology, including digital radiography.

Acworth Family Dentistry offers dental procedures such as teeth cleaning, cosmetic dentistry and restorative dentistry. Whatever prompts your visit—a routine dental exam, dental crowns or bridges, a root canal, a tooth extraction, dentures, or implants—we want your experience to be welcoming and comfortable. We also handle unforeseen dental emergencies, so you can get back to your normal routine as quickly as possible.

Dr. Kimberly Lim and staff will strive to make your experience at our office an enjoyable one. We serve the Acworth, Marietta, Kennesaw, Woodstock, and Dallas, Georgia communities. We understand you have a choice for your family dentistry needs, and we are honored you have chosen us. Contact us today to schedule an appointment!
