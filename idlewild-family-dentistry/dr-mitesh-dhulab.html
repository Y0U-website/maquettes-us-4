Meet Dr. Mitesh Dhulab

Dr. Mitesh Dhulab’s Background and Education

Dr. Dhulab has a strong academic foundation and extensive training in both general and cosmetic dentistry. After earning his bachelor’s degree in biology with a minor in chemistry from the State University of West Georgia in 1998, Dr. Dhulab went on to pursue his dental degree. In 2002, he graduated with a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the prestigious Medical College of Georgia .

With a focus on continually advancing his skills and knowledge, Dr. Dhulab has attended numerous courses, seminars, and conferences worldwide, ensuring that he stays at the forefront of the latest dental technologies and techniques. His commitment to education allows him to provide his patients with modern, effective treatments that deliver outstanding results.

Why Patients Trust Dr. Mitesh Dhulab

As the founder of Idlewild Family Dentistry , Dr. Dhulab’s philosophy of care revolves around building lasting relationships with his patients. His gentle demeanor, attention to detail, and dedication to providing personalized treatment plans are why patients of all ages trust him with their dental health. Dr. Dhulab believes in taking the time to get to know each patient, listening to their concerns, and developing customized treatment solutions that align with their needs and goals.

With years of experience in both general dentistry and cosmetic dentistry , Dr. Dhulab is well-equipped to address a wide range of dental concerns, from preventive care to more complex restorative procedures. His comprehensive approach to dentistry ensures that his patients receive the best care for their overall health and well-being.

Comprehensive Dental Services Offered by Dr. Dhulab

At Idlewild Family Dentistry , Dr. Dhulab and his team provide a full range of dental services to meet the needs of patients of all ages. From routine preventive care to advanced cosmetic and restorative procedures, the practice offers a wide variety of treatments designed to keep your smile healthy and beautiful.

General Dentistry

Dr. Dhulab provides expert care in all aspects of general dentistry , including routine cleanings, dental X-rays , fillings , and more. He is committed to preventive care, ensuring that minor dental issues are detected early and treated before they become more serious.

Cosmetic Dentistry With a passion for enhancing smiles, Dr. Dhulab offers a variety of cosmetic dentistry procedures to help patients achieve their dream smiles. Whether you’re interested in teeth whitening , veneers , or a complete smile makeover , Dr. Dhulab’s artistic approach to dentistry ensures natural, beautiful results.

Restorative Dentistry Restoring your smile’s function and aesthetics is a priority at Idlewild Family Dentistry . Dr. Dhulab provides a variety of restorative treatments , including dental implants , dental crowns , and root canals . His expertise in these procedures allows patients to regain both the form and function of their teeth, ensuring a comfortable and long-lasting solution.

TMJ Therapy If you suffer from jaw pain, headaches, or other symptoms of TMJ disorder , Dr. Dhulab offers specialized TMJ therapy . He works closely with patients to relieve symptoms, improve jaw function, and prevent long-term damage to the teeth and joints.
