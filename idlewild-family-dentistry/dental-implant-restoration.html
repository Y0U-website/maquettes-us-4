When Is Dental Implant Restoration Necessary?

Over time, implant restorations may require attention due to several factors, including:

If you experience any discomfort, notice visible damage to your restoration, or feel that your implant is loose, it’s essential to seek dental implant restoration at Idlewild Family Dentistry to prevent further complications.

  • Wear and Tear : The implant crown or bridge may become worn or damaged after years of use.
  • Chipping or Cracking : Porcelain crowns or other materials can chip or crack due to trauma or biting on hard objects.
  • Loose Implants : The implant restoration may feel loose due to issues with the abutment or the crown itself.
  • Gum Recession : Changes in the gum tissue around the implant can expose part of the implant post, affecting the appearance and function.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restoration

How long do dental implant restorations last?

While the dental implant post can last a lifetime with proper care, the restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) may need replacement after 10-15 years due to wear and tear.

Can a damaged dental implant crown be repaired?

In some cases, minor damage to a crown can be repaired. However, more extensive damage may require the crown to be replaced. Dr. Dhulab will evaluate your specific case to determine the best solution.

What should I do if my dental implant feels loose?

If your dental implant feels loose, it’s important to schedule an appointment immediately. This could be caused by issues with the abutment or restoration, and timely intervention can prevent more significant problems.

How much does dental implant restoration cost?

The cost of dental implant restoration depends on the extent of the repair or replacement needed. During your consultation, we will provide a detailed cost estimate and discuss your payment options.
