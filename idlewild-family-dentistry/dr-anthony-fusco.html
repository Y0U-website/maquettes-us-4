Dr. Anthony Fusco

Dr. Anthony Fusco’s Background and Education

Dr. Fusco earned his Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from Loyola University New Orleans and his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas. He went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Providence Sacred Heart Medical Center in Spokane, Washington, where he gained advanced training in oral surgery, sedation dentistry, and the care of medically complex patients. He has also completed intensive implant training to provide patients with modern, lasting tooth replacement options.

Before moving to Charlotte with his wife, Dr. Fusco practiced in both private practice and public health settings, experiences that strengthened his commitment to delivering personalized and compassionate dental care. Today, he is proud to serve the Charlotte community with a focus on comfort, trust, and long-term oral health.

Why Patients Trust Dr. Fusco

Dr. Fusco is dedicated to building long-term relationships with his patients, offering a wide range of services to meet their unique needs with personalized, attentive care. What he loves most about dentistry is helping patients achieve their goals and seeing the confidence that comes with a healthy, beautiful smile.

Outside the office, Dr. Fusco enjoys golfing, cooking, traveling, and spending quality time with his family. A passionate sports fan, he proudly cheers on the New Orleans Saints (Who Dat!) and the Vegas Golden Knights. He and his wife are excited to share life’s adventures with their young son.
