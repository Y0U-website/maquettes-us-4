Orthodontic Options Available at Shield Dental Care

We understand that every patient’s smile goals and comfort levels are unique. That’s why we offer a full range of orthodontic solutions to meet your needs.

Clear Aligners (Invisalign-Style Treatment)

Clear aligners are one of the most popular orthodontic options for adults and teens who want a nearly invisible way to straighten their teeth. These custom-made aligners gently guide teeth into proper position without brackets or wires.

Clear aligners allow you to straighten your teeth discreetly—perfect for professional adults, college students, and anyone who prefers a low-profile option.

  • Virtually invisible trays
  • Removable for eating and brushing
  • Comfortable, smooth design
  • Effective for mild to moderate alignment issues
  • Faster results in many cases

Traditional Braces

Today’s braces are more comfortable and efficient than ever before. They are especially effective for correcting significant crowding, bite issues, and complex orthodontic concerns.

Traditional braces remain one of the most reliable orthodontic treatments, giving excellent results when guided by an experienced orthodontic provider.

  • Predictable, guided movement
  • Effective for all levels of misalignment
  • Options for clear or tooth-colored brackets
  • Ideal for younger patients and teens

Orthodontics for Children, Teens, and Adults

For Children

Early orthodontic evaluations help identify issues before they become more severe. Some children benefit from early-phase treatment to guide jaw growth and create space for incoming permanent teeth.

For Teens

This is the most common age for orthodontics. Whether choosing braces or clear aligners, we ensure teens get the right balance of effectiveness, appearance, and comfort.

For Adults

It’s never too late to straighten your smile. Adult orthodontics has grown in popularity thanks to discreet and comfortable options like clear aligners.
