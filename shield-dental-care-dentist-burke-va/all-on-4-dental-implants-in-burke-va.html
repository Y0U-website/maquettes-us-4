What are my Options for Implant Dentures?

Semi-Removable Implant Denture (Overdenture)

A Semi-Removable Overdenture offers a practical solution for those seeking a stable denture anchored by implants. Utilizing two to four implants, this type of overdenture can snap in and out, providing significantly better stability compared to conventional removable dentures. The process begins with Dr. Ghorbani placing dental implants into your lower arch, replacing missing teeth. Patients receive a temporary denture while the implants fuse with the jawbone, ensuring the new anchors are ready to support the overdenture.

Non-Removable Fixed Denture (Permanent)

The Non-Removable Fixed Denture, commonly known as the All on 4, provides a permanent solution for individuals looking for maximum stability. This type of denture uses a minimum of four implants, securely screwed into the jawbone for a robust foundation. The initial step involves taking digital impressions, intraoral pictures, and a Cone Beam CT scan of your mouth. These details are sent to our lab to prepare for your surgery, ensuring all expectations regarding the prosthesis’s shape, color, fit, and size are clearly reviewed with you.

On the day of the procedure, a skilled team will ensure a smooth and comfortable experience, with general sedation provided if preferred. The surgery involves placing the maximum number of implants based on your jawbone’s condition. During the healing process, you will receive a temporary set of teeth. Once healing is complete, a final porcelain denture will be fitted. Regular cleaning and proper hygiene practices for your fixed implant denture are essential to prevent tartar buildup, just like with your natural teeth.
