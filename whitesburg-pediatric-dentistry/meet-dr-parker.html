Beyond the Practice

Originally from Winston-Salem, North Carolina, Dr. Parker is pleased to now call Huntsville his home. His wife of more than a dozen years, Riley, is a Huntsville native, and the couple is thrilled to be raising their three children Mary Emens, Weller, and Mac in her hometown. The family is rounded out with Charlie, a Boxer-Bloodhound mix, and Duck, a sheepadoodle.

Time with family is Dr. Parker’s priority when he’s not in the office. He likes to be active and enjoys golf and fishing. The family loves to visit Dr. Parker’s family and friends in North Carolina and tries to get there as often as possible.
