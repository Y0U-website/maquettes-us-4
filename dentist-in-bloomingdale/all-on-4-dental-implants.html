All-on-4 Dental Implants in…

  • Conventional dentures: lowest cost, but removable, slower to eat with, and the jawbone shrinks under them over time — which is why they loosen.
  • Single implants per tooth: ideal when most teeth are healthy; replacing a full arch tooth-by-tooth costs far more than All-on-4.
  • Implant-supported overdentures: a middle path — snap-on stability at a lower cost than a fixed bridge, still removable.

The Process & Timeline

Most All-on-4 patients leave placement day with fixed teeth — the "teeth in a day" you’ve heard about:

  • 1. Consult & 3D imaging: we map your bone, plan implant positions digitally, and give you one all-inclusive number.
  • 2. Placement day: any failing teeth are removed, four implants are placed, and a fixed provisional bridge is attached — you leave with teeth.
  • 3. Healing (3-6 months): the implants fuse with your bone while you eat a progressively normal diet on the provisional.
  • 4. Final bridge: your permanent, custom-shaded arch is fitted and torqued in.

Cost & Financing

Full-arch cases are quoted as one all-inclusive number — imaging, extractions, implants, the provisional, and the final bridge — so there are no mid-treatment surprises. Most arches fall in the $15,000-$25,000 range depending on what your case needs.

Nearly every All-on-4 patient finances some portion. Through flexible dental financing — Cherry, Sunbit, CareCredit, and our in-house plan — treatment becomes a monthly payment, and we’ll show you the numbers before you commit to anything.

Are You a Candidate?

If you’re already wearing a denture, losing teeth to gum disease, or maintaining teeth that fail one by one, you’re likely a candidate. The angled-implant technique avoids grafting in most cases; when bone is genuinely too thin, bone grafting for implants can rebuild it first.

Candidacy is settled by 3D imaging, not guesswork — the scan at your consult tells us exactly what your bone can support, and you’ll leave knowing your options and your number.
