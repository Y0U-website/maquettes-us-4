David Gorenz, DDS

I am very excited to be carrying on the tradition of excellent dental care at Fairview Dental. I enjoy coming to the office each day, and am blessed to have the opportunity to share my passion with my patients and provide comfortable and quality dental care.

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE

A lifelong patient of Fairview Dental, I grew up in nearby Lisle and attended Benet Academy. I continued on to the University of Notre Dame where I graduated cum laude with a Bachelor of Science. My academic journey next led me to The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where I graduated magna cum laude and received my Doctorate of Dental Surgery, along with multiple academic and leadership awards. During my fourth year in dental school, I completed extra course work in the oral surgery department, and gained invaluable experience treating more intensive cases.

Since dental school, I have continued my education by traveling to various seminars and through consistent online learning. In my free time, I enjoy reading the latest literature on new methods and technologies in dentistry. I make use of several of these technologies in the office such as Invisalign Clear Aligners, digital scanning for crowns, and WaveOne Endodontics. I am driven to continue learning as much as I can so that I can provide the most up-to-date dental treatments to my patients.
