I feel very fortunate to be a dentist. It is satisfying to help someone by immediately relieving pain or performing a cosmetic procedure that improves a patient’s smile. I enjoy forming long-term patient relationships it’s fun to see how patients’ lives change over the years!

Prior to becoming a dentist, I graduated from Parkland College with a degree in dental hygiene in 1984. I graduated from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry in 1993 with my Doctorate of Dental Surgery. While in dental school, I received the William Starek Award in Endodontics, American Society of Pediatric Dentistry Award, and Academy of General Dentistry Award.

Although my formal education is complete, I believe that a continuing education benefits all dentists and patients! I regularly attend classes and seminars through professional dental organizations in order to keep up with the most advanced methods and technologies. I am also certified in Lumineers , Invisalign and Cerec same day crowns.

I love being a dentist because I get to help people every day! I’ve been in the dental field since 1984 and have been a dentist for more than 20 years. Some people dread going to the dentist, but I have the ability to turn their opinions around. Our patients actually look forward to coming to see us, and I wouldn’t have it any other way!

Our team is a group of individuals who all work for the same goals: To provide excellent dental treatment and to help patients feel comfortable. Many of us have known each other for many years and are friends outside of work. Patty, a hygienist for over 30 years, and I went to dental hygiene school together.

I grew up in Sterling, and have lived in the Fox Valley area since 1993. My spare time is often spent with my Labradoodle, Coco, and entertaining family and friends. In the summer months, I love to have pool parties and cookouts. When it’s not so nice out, I love to play Guitar Hero or host card tournaments.
