Pediatric Dental Care

When do we begin bringing our child to the dentist?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children begin seeing a dentist at their "Dental Home" as early as age 1, when teeth begin erupting from the gum line. This helps your child become comfortable with visiting the dentist, as well as ensuring healthy oral habits that will last the rest of their life. Dr. Tipton and his staff go to great measures to educate even their youngest patients to ensure good oral hygiene that will last a lifetime.
