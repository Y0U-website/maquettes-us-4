Welcome to Suburban Family Dental – Where Healthy Smiles Begin

At Suburban Family Dental , we understand that a healthy smile is essential for every member of your family. Our experienced and compassionate team is committed to delivering exceptional dental care in a comfortable, welcoming environment you can trust.

Led by Dr. Salvatore Ortolano , who brings over 30 years of experience in creating optimal oral health, our team is dedicated to ensuring your dental visits are both effective and stress-free. Whether you’re here for a routine check-up or a more advanced procedure, your comfort and satisfaction are always our top priorities.

We take the time to clearly explain your treatment options and why we recommend them—because informed patients make confident choices. Our personalized approach means every treatment plan is tailored to meet your individual needs and goals.

Our office is open five days a week , offering flexible appointment times to fit your busy schedule. Whether it’s a routine exam or ongoing care, we make it easy to get the treatment your family needs.

From preventive care and cleanings to restorative and cosmetic treatments, we offer a full range of dental services designed to support every aspect of your oral health. At Suburban Family Dental, our commitment to your well-being has made us a trusted name in Amherst, NY and the surrounding communities.
