About Seaside Dental Hygiene Studio

0 After caring for smiles for almost a decade, I have decided to provide preventative dental care in a more personalized setting that ensures patient comfort.

I prioritize the connection I have with every patient by ensuring you are listened to and feel supported. I have your overall wellness in mind when collaborating on your oral health goals.

I have created Seaside Dental Hygiene Studio to be a place for everyone. You are welcome here no matter how long it has been since your last dental visit or if it is your first time. I invite those with holistic or more natural-based beliefs and offer options that fit within your comfort. It is a pleasure and joy to have your trust!

AAS Dental Hygiene-Wallace State in Hanceville, Alabama 2018

Certification of Local Anesthesia administration-Middlesex Community College, Lowell, Massachusetts 2021

I am an active member of the American Dental Hygiene Association, Maine chapter (MDHA), and the International Association Airway Hygienists (IAAH). I have currently completed 20 hours of myofunctional therapy studies. I prioritize continuing education in my practice. Staying up to date on the latest research and advancements in healthcare and wellness is of utmost importance to provide my patients with modern, quality care.
