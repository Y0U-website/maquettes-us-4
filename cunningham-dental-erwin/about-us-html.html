Dr. Jason Cunningham, DDS

Dr. Jason Cunningham joined his father, Dr. Edgar Cunningham, permanently in 2010, after practicing with him previously from 2000 to 2004. Dr. Edgar Cunningham has practiced in Erwin since 1975. Dr. Jason provides excellence in dentistry to residents of Erwin, Johnson City, and surrounding areas.

He is an outstanding dentist and is known for his gentle touch as well as his sense of humor. He is excellent with children. He is married to Dr. Angela R. Cameron and has two beautiful children, Andrew and Alexis.

Dr. Jason Cunningham believes that partnering with the right dentist is fundamental to your confidence and continued treatment, and you can trust Dr. Cunningham to deliver the compassionate dental care your family deserves.

Dr. Jason Cunningham graduated from the College of Dentistry at the University of Tennessee Memphis and is a member of the American Dental Association, Tennessee Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Academy of General Dentistry, and The American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine.

Comprehensive Dental Services

Our commitment to excellence drives us to continuously invest in advanced training and education, allowing us to offer a comprehensive range of dental services to cater to all your oral health needs. Some of the exceptional services provided at Cunningham Dental include:

  • Bridges and Crowns : Restore the function and aesthetics of your smile with our expertly crafted dental bridges and crowns. These solutions are designed to seamlessly blend with your natural teeth, providing a durable and long-lasting restoration.
  • Fillings: Our dental fillings are carefully selected to match the color of your teeth, ensuring a discreet and effective solution to address cavities and tooth decay.
  • Fluoride Treatments : Strengthen your teeth and prevent decay with our professional fluoride treatments, which are essential to maintaining optimal oral health.
  • Mercury-Safe and Mercury-Free Dentistry : We prioritize your safety and offer mercury-safe and mercury-free dental services, ensuring your dental procedures are as safe and biocompatible as possible.
  • Neuromuscular Dentistry for TMJ Relief : If you’re experiencing issues with your jaw joint (TMJ), our neuromuscular dentistry techniques can relieve pain, discomfort, and related symptoms.
  • Occlusal Disease Treatment: Our comprehensive approach includes diagnosing and treating occlusal (bite) issues, promoting balanced and healthy dental function.
  • Sleep Apnea Treatment : Addressing more than just your teeth, we offer specialized treatment for sleep apnea, improving your overall well-being by promoting restful sleep and reducing the associated health risks.
  • Occlusal Night Guards: If you suffer from teeth grinding or clenching, our custom-designed occlusal night guards protect your teeth while you sleep, preventing damage and discomfort.
  • Sedation Dentistry: Our sedation dentistry options ensure a relaxed and stress-free experience for those with dental anxiety or complex procedures, allowing you to receive the care you need without unnecessary worry.
