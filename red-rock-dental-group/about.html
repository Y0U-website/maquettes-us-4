Meet the Doctor

What drives Dr. Sam is the combination of problem-solving, working with his hands, and building real relationships with patients. He is particularly drawn to dental implants and complex restorative cases, the kind of treatment that takes someone from a compromised situation to something truly life-changing. His favorite services are the ones where the clinical challenge and the human impact overlap.

Outside the office, Dr. Sam is an avid skier, fly fisherman, and mountain biker. He and his wife Sumra are parents to a young son, Reza, and feel lucky to call Central Oregon home, a place that fits both their family and the kind of practice they set out to build.

Your first visit

Here&#x27;s what happens when you walk through our door

We know that trying out a new dental office takes a little trust, and we don&#x27;t take that lightly. From the moment you arrive, the goal is to make sure you feel informed, comfortable, and confident that you&#x27;re in good hands. There&#x27;s no pressure, no rushing, and no surprises, just an honest conversation about where things stand and what your options are.

Patient Experience Your first visit starts with a comprehensive exam using our advanced diagnostic tools, including digital X-rays and 3D CBCT imaging, so we can see exactly what&#x27;s going on with real clarity. Dr. Sam or Dr. Tom will walk you through their findings in plain language, present your options, and give you their honest recommendation.

Free consultations are available for implants, cosmetic concerns, IV sedation, and wisdom teeth, so you can explore your options before committing to anything. If you&#x27;re dealing with a dental emergency, we keep space in the schedule for same-day appointments and have an on-call line for after-hours urgent situations.

Modern tools that make a real difference

At Red Rock Dental Group, technology isn&#x27;t about looking impressive. It&#x27;s about getting better outcomes with less discomfort and fewer appointments. Every tool we invest in is chosen because it makes your experience faster, more accurate, or more comfortable in a way that actually matters.

CEREC same-day crowns

Using the CEREC Primescan and Primemill system, we design, mill, and place your crown in a single visit. No temporary crown, no second appointment, and no traditional impression material. You walk in with a broken tooth and walk out with a permanent, custom restoration.

3D CBCT imaging

Our cone beam CT scanner gives us a detailed, three-dimensional view of your teeth, bone, and surrounding structures. This level of precision is especially important for implant planning, oral surgery, and complex treatment cases, meaning fewer surprises and more predictable outcomes.

Digital intraoral scanner

We use a digital intraoral scanner in place of traditional impression material, so no more goop, gagging, or waiting. The scanner creates an accurate 3D model of your teeth in minutes, which we use for crowns, aligners, and treatment planning.

Digital X-rays

Digital X-rays give us clear, high-resolution images with significantly less radiation than traditional film. They&#x27;re faster, more comfortable, and allow us to share exactly what we&#x27;re seeing with you in real time.

3D printer

Our in-office 3D printer allows us to fabricate certain dental components quickly and precisely in-house, supporting more efficient workflows and better turnaround on select treatments.

TVs and in-operatory monitors

Every operatory has a TV or monitor, whether you want to watch something to take your mind off the appointment or see exactly what we&#x27;re looking at during your exam.

Beverage station and comfort amenities

We offer a beverage station, blankets, and a welcoming environment designed to feel comfortable from the moment you walk in. It&#x27;s a small thing, but the details matter.
