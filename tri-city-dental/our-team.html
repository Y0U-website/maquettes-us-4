Dr. Carolyn Bullock (nee Boettger) and her team are here to care for your dental needs. She has completed a residency on the Navajo reservation in advance general practice techniques and has been practicing dentistry since 2009. She went to school at the University of Iowa, where she and her family are from. She moved to Frederick to be closer to her siblings and their children. She has made a life long commitment to providing dental services to families in her community.

Sharmane, our dental receptionist and insurance coordinator, is a Colorado native and has been in the dental field for almost 20 years. She always likes learning new things and enjoys figuring out the best way to serve our patients from working with their schedules to how their dental insurance benefits are applied.

Mary Distel-Tatar, dental assistant, has been in the field since 1993. She has a large family in the community and grew up on a farm in Longmont. She loves working in the dental field because she gets to interact with people on a one-on-one basis getting to know each person she sits next to.
