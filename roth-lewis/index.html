Meet Dr. Roth Lewis Dr. Roth Rube Harrison Lewis is a proud native of the area. Dr. Lewis was raised on a local dairy farm in Ashford, AL where he learned the value of education, hard work, dedication, discipline, and Christian family values.

Dr. Lewis earned his Doctorate in Dental Medicine from the prestigious University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 2016. While attending the University, Dr. Lewis was named a Deans Scholar for his high levels of academic and clinical achievements.
