Gum Care & Periodontal Health :

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile

Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, can quietly damage your oral health if left untreated. That’s why we monito the health of your gums at every visit and offer advanced care when needed.

Our team uses gentle and effective treatments to stop progression and restore gum health. We also evaluate jawbone health for those considering implants or who’ve experienced bone loss. When your gums are healthy, everything else follows.

  • Red, swollen, or bleeding gums
  • Receding gum lines
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Loose or shifting teeth

TMJ & Migraine Relief :

Chronic headaches? Your jaw might be the culprit

If you suffer from frequent migraines, jaw pain, or facial tension, the root cause may be your temporomandibular joint (TMJ) which connects your jaw to your skull.

Many patients unknowingly clench or grind their teeth, placing pressure on this joint and triggering discomfort. We offer Botox therapy, a proven option to relax overactive muscles and

Treatment is quick, minimally invasive, and can dramatically improve quality of life for those affected. Ask us if this could be right for you.

  • Jaw soreness
  • Nighttime clenching or grinding
  • Lockjaw symptoms

Sedation Dentistry

If you feel anxious about going to the dentist, you’re not alone and we’re here to help. At Appearance Implants and Family Dentistry, we offer multiple sedation options designed to make your visit calm and stress-free.

Whether you’re having a routine cleaning or a more advanced procedure, you can choose from:

  • Oral sedation (a calming pill taken before your appointment)
  • Inhalation sedation (laughing gas)
  • IV sedation (administered during your visit for deeper relaxation)
  • We’ll walk you through each option to find what feels right for you so you can get the care you need without fear or discomfort.
