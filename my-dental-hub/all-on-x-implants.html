What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are an implant system designed to replace a full arch of teeth on the upper or lower jaw (or both). When four titanium posts are surgically placed in the jawbone, they hold a complete set of fixed dentures. The two implants in the back of the mouth are positioned at around 30 to 45 degrees, which increases bone contact and can often avoid bone grafting.

Once the implants are healed and integrated into the jawbone, the permanent prosthetic teeth are screwed onto the implants, which means they cannot be removed. The most common treatment plan involves a temporary arch on the day of surgery, followed by the final permanent arch placed 3–6 months later.

What Are All-on-6 Dental Implants?

All-on-6 dental implants follow the same principle as All-on-4, but use six titanium implant posts to support a full-arch prosthetic. The two additional implants provide a broader base of support, which can improve stability and bite force distribution particularly for patients with higher bone density or those who want maximum strength from their restoration.

All-on-6 implants are compatible with zirconia and ceramic prosthetic arches, two of the most durable and natural-looking materials available. All-on-6 zirconia arches are especially popular because of their strength, stain resistance, and lifelike appearance. Whether you choose All-on-4 or All-on-6, our team at My Dental Hub will help you select the best system for your anatomy and long-term goals.

When All-on-4 or All-on-6 Implants Are the Recommended Treatment Option

We often recommend All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both arches. This permanent restoration may also be beneficial if remaining teeth are failing and you are considering a full-mouth extraction. Here are the most common reasons patients choose full-mouth dental implants:

  • Severe Tooth Loss : Ideal for patients missing most or all of their teeth, or those with remaining teeth that are severely damaged and cannot be saved.
  • Bone Density Loss : Even with moderate bone loss, All-on-4 angled implants can reach denser bone regions. All-on-6 implants may be recommended when additional anchoring points are needed to maximize integration.
  • Replacing Traditional Dentures : If slipping or shifting dentures are affecting your ability to eat and speak, full-mouth implants offer a stable, fixed alternative.
  • Permanent Fixed Restoration : Both systems provide a stable smile replacement that does not need to be removed for cleaning.
  • Zirconia or Ceramic Restoration Preference : Patients who want All-on-6 zirconia or All-on-6 ceramic implants benefit from one of the most durable and aesthetically refined prosthetic options available.

All-on-4 vs. All-on-6: Which Option Is Right for You?

Both All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants are excellent solutions for full-arch tooth replacement, and the right choice depends on your unique anatomy, bone structure, and restoration goals. Here is how the two options compare:

During your consultation at My Dental Hub in Skokie, IL, our team will review your digital imaging and discuss which system aligns best with your goals. Both options are performed in our office and our team will explain and guide you through the whole process that fits your unique needs.

  • All-on-4 : Four strategically angled posts distribute load across the arch. Ideal for patients with moderate bone density who may want to avoid bone grafting. Typically a faster surgical appointment.
  • All-on-6 : Six posts provide a wider distribution of bite forces and additional stability. Recommended for patients with higher bone volume or those seeking maximum support for heavier zirconia or ceramic prosthetic arches.
  • Material Options for All-on-6 : All-on-6 dentures can be fabricated in zirconia (strongest, most lifelike) or ceramic (natural translucency). Both are fixed, non-removable, and designed to last many years with proper care.

What Is All-on-X? The Fully Customizable Full-Arch Implant Solution

Rather than being limited to four or six posts, the “X” refers to the exact number of implants which could be four, five, six, or more determined through a thorough clinical evaluation and digital imaging.

All-on-X dental implants are ideal for patients who want the most personalized approach to full-mouth restoration. Frequently Asked Questions

Are All-on-4 dental implants permanent?

Yes, the implant posts are integrated into the jawbone, offering a long-term solution for smile restoration. With proper care, the prosthetic arch can last for 10–20 years before it needs to be replaced.

How many teeth do All-on-4 implants replace?

All-on-4 implants are designed to replace a full arch, typically using 10–14 prosthetic teeth on each arch.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants?

All-on-4 uses four implant posts to support a full arch, while All-on-6 uses six. The additional posts in All-on-6 increase stability and load distribution, making it a preferred option for patients seeking maximum support or who are choosing heavier zirconia or ceramic prosthetic arches. Our team will recommend the right system based on your bone structure and goals.

What materials are available for All-on-6 dentures?

All-on-6 dentures are available in zirconia and ceramic. All-on-6 zirconia implants are the most durable and stain-resistant option, while ceramic offers excellent translucency for a highly natural appearance. Both are fixed restorations that stay in place permanently.

Is the All-on-4, All-on-6, or All-on-X procedure painful?

Rest assured that you won’t feel pain during surgery, which is performed under local anesthesia with sedation options also available. You can expect some soreness for a few days after the procedure, which can be managed with medication.

What is All-on-X and how does it differ from All-on-4 and All-on-6?

All-on-X is a fully customizable full-arch implant system where the number of titanium posts is tailored to each patient’s specific bone structure and clinical needs. Unlike All-on-4 (four posts) or All-on-6 (six posts), All-on-X does not prescribe a fixed number of implants. Instead, our team uses digital imaging and clinical evaluation to determine the optimal number and placement for your jaw delivering a personalized restoration with maximum stability.
