Single-Tooth Implant

Dental Implant Services

A single-tooth implant replaces an individual missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. The implant integrates with the jawbone to provide a stable foundation for a custom restoration that blends naturally with your smile.

All-On-X

Dental Implant Services

All-On-X is a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses a strategically placed set of dental implants to support a complete restoration. This treatment provides enhanced stability, improved function, and a more secure alternative to traditional dentures.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants help restore chewing ability, improve smile aesthetics, support jawbone health, and provide a long-lasting solution for missing teeth.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Many patients with missing teeth and adequate jawbone support may be candidates for dental implants. A consultation and evaluation can determine the best treatment option for your needs.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental care, dental implants can provide reliable function and appearance for many years.

How is All-On-X different from traditional dentures?

All-On-X uses dental implants to secure a full arch of replacement teeth, providing greater stability, improved comfort, and enhanced chewing function compared to traditional removable dentures.
