Retainers

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child have an orthodontic evaluation?

Many children benefit from an orthodontic evaluation around age seven. Early assessments allow potential concerns to be identified and monitored as the teeth and jaws develop.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Clear aligners can effectively treat many orthodontic concerns. The best option depends on your specific needs, goals, and the complexity of your case.

How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?

Treatment length varies based on individual needs, but many patients complete orthodontic treatment within 12 to 24 months.

Why are retainers important after orthodontic treatment?

Retainers help keep teeth in their new positions and reduce the risk of shifting after braces or clear aligner treatment is complete.
