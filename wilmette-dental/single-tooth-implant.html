What Is a Single-Tooth Implant?

A single-tooth implant is a replacement option used when one tooth is missing. The treatment consists of a small titanium implant that is placed within the jawbone and acts as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant has integrated with the bone, a custom restoration is attached to complete the smile.

Unlike traditional bridges, a single-tooth implant does not rely on neighboring teeth for support. This allows the surrounding teeth to remain untouched while providing a stable and natural-looking replacement. Dental implants are designed to restore both aesthetics and function, helping patients chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Benefits of Single-Tooth Implants in Wilmette

Natural Appearance and Function

Dental implants are designed to closely mimic natural teeth, providing a restoration that looks and functions like your original tooth.

Preserves Surrounding Teeth

Unlike some tooth replacement options, a single-tooth implant does not require support from adjacent teeth, helping preserve healthy tooth structure.

Long-Term Oral Health Benefits

By replacing the tooth root and supporting the jawbone, dental implants help maintain bone density and reduce the risk of future oral health complications.

Did You Know…

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area can gradually begin to shrink over time. Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone much like a natural tooth root, supporting bone health and helping maintain facial structure.
