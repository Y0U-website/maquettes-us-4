What Is Early Orthodontic Intervention?

Early orthodontic intervention refers to orthodontic treatment provided during childhood to help guide the growth and development of the teeth and jaws. This approach focuses on identifying potential concerns before they become more significant and determining whether treatment may be beneficial during key stages of development.

Not every child requires early orthodontic treatment, but some may benefit from addressing issues such as crowding, spacing, bite problems, jaw growth discrepancies, or harmful oral habits. Early intervention can help create more favorable conditions for future dental development and may reduce the complexity of treatment later in life.

Benefits of Early Orthodontic Intervention in Wilmette

Guides Healthy Jaw Development

Early treatment can help direct jaw growth and development, creating a healthier foundation for the eruption of permanent teeth.

Addresses Developing Bite Concerns

Identifying and treating bite issues early may help improve function, comfort, and overall dental development.

May Reduce Future Treatment Needs

In some cases, early intervention can help simplify or shorten future orthodontic treatment by addressing concerns before they become more severe.

Did You Know…

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children receive an orthodontic evaluation by age seven. At this age, enough permanent teeth have typically erupted to identify many developing orthodontic concerns.
