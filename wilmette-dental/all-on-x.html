Our team provides personalized implant treatment focused on improving comfort, stability, oral function, and long-term smile restoration through modern technology and comprehensive care.

What Is All-On-X?

All-On-X is a dental implant treatment that replaces an entire arch of missing teeth using a strategically placed number of dental implants. These implants serve as anchors for a custom full-arch restoration, creating a secure and functional smile that remains firmly in place.

Unlike traditional removable dentures, All-On-X is designed to provide enhanced stability and support. The treatment allows patients to enjoy many of the benefits of natural teeth while improving chewing ability, speech, and overall confidence. Each treatment plan is customized to the patient’s unique oral health needs and smile goals.

Benefits of All-On-X in Wilmette

Secure and Stable Tooth Replacement

All-On-X restorations are anchored by dental implants, helping eliminate many of the concerns associated with traditional removable dentures.

Improved Function and Comfort

Patients can often enjoy greater chewing efficiency, improved speech, and enhanced comfort compared to conventional tooth replacement options.

Supports Long-Term Oral Health

Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone and reduce bone loss, supporting facial structure and long-term oral health.

Did You Know…

All-On-X treatment often requires fewer implants than replacing each missing tooth individually. Strategic implant placement allows a full arch of teeth to be supported efficiently while maximizing available bone structure.

The All-On-X Process

Comprehensive Consultation

We begin with a thorough examination, digital imaging, and treatment planning to evaluate your oral health and determine whether All-On-X is the right solution for your needs.

Implant Placement

A strategic number of dental implants are placed within the jawbone to create a stable foundation for the full-arch restoration.

Final Smile Restoration

After the implants have integrated with the jawbone, a custom full-arch restoration is attached, providing a secure, natural-looking smile designed for long-term function and aesthetics.
