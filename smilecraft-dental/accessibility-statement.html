Accessibility at SmileCraft Dental

We want everyone to be able to use our website comfortably, including people with disabilities. That&#x27;s a priority for us, and we&#x27;ve put real time and effort into making our site easier to navigate for all visitors.

What we&#x27;ve done

Our website uses the UserWay Accessibility Widget — a tool that helps people adjust the site to work better for them. It offers options like larger text, higher contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and more. These improvements help us meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1) , the international standard for accessible web design.

How to use the accessibility menu

Look for the accessibility icon in the corner of any page on our site. Click it, give it a moment to load, and you&#x27;ll see options to customize how the site looks and behaves.

Ongoing work

We&#x27;re not done — accessibility is something we work on continuously. We regularly scan our site with UserWay&#x27;s accessibility scanner to find and fix issues as they come up. Some content may not yet meet every accessibility standard perfectly, but we&#x27;re actively working to improve it.

We&#x27;re here to help

If you&#x27;re having trouble using any part of our website, or you have questions about accessibility, please reach out during normal business hours. We&#x27;ll do our best to help.

Contact

If you&#x27;d like to report an accessibility issue or need help with anything on the site, email us at customerexperience@growth99.com .
