Accessibility Statement

  • Accessibility Statement | Bright Family Dentistry For Every Patient Accessibility Statement Last updated: July 7, 2026 Bright Family Dentistry welcomes every member of the family — and that includes making this website usable for people of all abilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA , and we work to keep the site accessible as it grows. Measures we take Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images
  • Semantic headings, landmarks, and labels for screen readers
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, menus, and forms
  • Color choices tested for sufficient contrast against their backgrounds
  • Motion kept minimal, with animations honoring the “reduce motion” preference on your device
  • Responsive layouts that support zoom up to 200 % without loss of content

Know a way we can improve?

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some content may not yet meet every standard. If you encounter a barrier on this website — or need any material in an alternative format — please tell us. Call (423) 415-1421 or speak with our team at 1421 Armour St., Chattanooga, TN 37412, and we’ll do our best to provide the information you need and fix the underlying issue.

In our office

Our Chattanooga office offers accessible parking and entry. If you or a family member need any accommodation for a visit, let us know when you schedule and we’ll be ready for you.
