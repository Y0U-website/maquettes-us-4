View all services Meet Your Dentist Dr. Robina Habib Dr. Robina Habib

Dr. Robina Habib brings clinical training and patient-focused care to Bright Family Dentistry. With over two decades of experience in dentistry across the United States and internationally, she is known for a thorough, methodical approach and comprehensive treatment planning.

A graduate of Tufts University School of Dental Medicine with a residency in General Dentistry, Dr. Habib has a strong foundation in comprehensive dentistry. Her clinical scope includes restorative dentistry, crown and bridge work, partial and complete dentures, Invisalign, endodontics, and full-spectrum family dental care—delivered with precision and attention to detail.

Her mission is a welcoming, supportive environment where every patient feels respected, heard, and empowered. She is an active member of the ADA , AGD , and Chattanooga Area Dental Society , and welcomes patients in English, Hindi, and Punjabi.

At Bright Family Dentistry, her vision is clear: to deliver exceptional dentistry with elegance, integrity, and compassion—helping every patient achieve a healthy, confident, and radiant smile.
