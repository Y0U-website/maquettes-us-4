About Bright Family Dentistry

A neighborhood dental office in Chattanooga built around one idea: every patient deserves thorough, honest, comfortable care.

Who We Are A True Family Practice Bright Family Dentistry is an independent, doctor-led dental office at 1421 Armour St. in Chattanooga. We see every member of the family under one roof — first checkups, routine cleanings, same-day emergencies, and complete smile restorations.

We&#x27;re not a chain. The people who greet you at the front desk are the same people who help you understand your insurance, and the team in the treatment room takes the time to explain every option before anything happens. Patients from East Ridge, Brainerd, and across Hamilton County — and neighbors over the Georgia line — trust us because we treat them like family.
