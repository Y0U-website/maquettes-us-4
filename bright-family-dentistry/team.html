Get to know our team

Friendly faces you&#x27;ll see every time you walk through our doors in Chattanooga.

Dara Hobbs

Dara began her dental career over 20 years ago, starting as a Registered Dental Assistant before transitioning into office management. Her combined clinical and administrative experience allows her to put that expertise to work for her patients. She is a dedicated patient advocate, committed to making sure patients are well informed — helping them navigate the insurance process and assisting with financial arrangements so every patient receives the care they need.

Dara attended Freed-Hardeman University and Concorde Career College in West Tennessee. Born and raised in Georgia, she is a resident of the Flintstone community and a member of the Rossville Church of Christ.

Outside of her career, she finds great joy in spending time with her loving family — including her 3 children, 5 grandchildren, and her Shih-Tzu, Ollie. Dara loves the great outdoors, and in her free time she enjoys kayaking, gardening, and traveling.

Breanna Houston

Breanna attended Wallace State Community College in Hanceville, Alabama, where she earned her Associate&#x27;s in Dental Hygiene and was awarded the Golden Scaler Award for outstanding clinical performance. She is dedicated to providing high-quality care and support for her patients, focusing on educating them about their oral health and helping them achieve greater confidence through their smile.

Breanna also brings 4 years of experience with a history in orthodontic treatment and assisting with chairside procedures. With strong attention to detail and a compassionate approach to patient care, she is committed to ongoing professional development — staying up to date with the latest advancements in dental practices to ensure the best outcomes for every patient.

Outside of her professional life, Breanna enjoys spending quality time with friends and family, hunting, and going to the river.

Debbie Zackery

With 15 years of dedicated experience in dental care, Debbie brings an unmatched level of expertise and comfort to our clinical team. Born and raised in Chattanooga, she attended Chattanooga State Community College, where she received her certificate as a Registered Dental Assistant. She is certified in dental radiology, coronal polishing, sealants, and nitrous oxide monitoring — ensuring every procedure is safe, smooth, and stress-free — and maintains 24 hours of continuing education every 2 years.

Her unique background also includes experience as a medical assistant, giving her a deep understanding of overall patient health and wellness. Debbie&#x27;s favorite part of her career is building lasting relationships with patients and helping them feel completely at ease in the dental chair.

Outside of the office, she loves spending quality time with her family, traveling to new places, and baking delicious treats.

Grace Cronnon

Grace attended Chattanooga College, where she earned her dental assisting licensure. She is dedicated to making sure patients receive the quality care they need and deserve, and she is passionate about helping her patients feel more confident with their smiles. Grace makes it a priority to build relationships with her patients — and most importantly, to help them feel comfortable in the dental chair. She takes pride in creating a positive, stress-free experience for every patient.

Outside of work, Grace is a wife and mother. In her free time, she enjoys spending time with her son, riding four-wheelers, and feeding their chickens. Grace also loves hosting holiday gatherings for her friends and family.
