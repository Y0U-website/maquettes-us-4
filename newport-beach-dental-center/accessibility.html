Accessibility

  • Color Contrast: We use high-contrast colors to ensure readability for individuals with visual impairments.
  • Alt Text for Images: All important images on our website include descriptive alt text for users who rely on screen readers.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Our website is fully navigable using a keyboard, ensuring access for individuals with motor disabilities.
  • Transcripts and Captions: We provide transcripts for audio content and captions for video content whenever possible.
  • Mobile Accessibility: Our website is designed to be fully responsive, providing an optimized experience across devices, including smartphones and tablets.

How We Can Improve

While we have made significant efforts to improve the accessibility of our website, we recognize that there may still be areas for improvement. If you encounter any issues or have specific accessibility needs, please let us know. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us continue to enhance the website for all visitors.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or need assistance with accessing content on our website, please feel free to contact us.

We are dedicated to providing a positive and accessible experience for all patients and visitors, and we appreciate your understanding as we work to continually improve our website.
