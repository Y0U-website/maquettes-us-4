The Dental Implants Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

During your initial consultation, Dr. Gorospe will examine your mouth and take X-rays to determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants. He will discuss the procedure, costs, and answer any questions you may have.

Implant Placement

After the initial consultation, you will be scheduled for implant surgery. During this procedure, the dental implant, which is a small titanium post, will be placed into the bone socket of the missing tooth.

Abutment and Crown Placement

Once the implant has bonded to the jawbone, an abutment — a small connector post that holds the new tooth — is attached to the implant. Then, a crown, which is made to match your natural teeth, is attached to the abutment.
