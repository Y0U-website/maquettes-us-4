Dental Payment Plans

  • Can financing be used for any dental treatment? External payment plans may be used for many preventative, restorative, cosmetic, and implant services. Availability can depend on the provider, approved amount, and treatment cost. Ask our team whether your planned care is eligible before applying.
  • Can I use a payment plan with dental insurance? You may be able to use CareCredit or Cherry for the portion of your treatment that remains after insurance benefits are estimated. Your available options will depend on your insurance coverage and the financing provider’s terms.
  • Will applying affect my credit score? Applying for a Cherry payment plan uses a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score. CareCredit allows you to check whether you prequalify without affecting your score, although completing a full credit application may involve additional review.
  • How do I choose the right payment option? Compare the payment amount, repayment period, interest rate, fees, and total cost associated with each offer. Review the full terms carefully before accepting a plan, and contact the financing provider directly if anything is unclear.
