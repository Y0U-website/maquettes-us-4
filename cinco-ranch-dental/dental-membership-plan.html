MEMBERSHIP FAQS

  • When can I start using my membership? Your benefits begin immediately after enrollment. There are no waiting periods, so you can begin using the included services and eligible treatment savings right away.
  • What does the membership plan include? Cinco Ranch Dental offers three plan options with essential preventative care, exclusive savings on eligible treatments, and rollover rewards. Specific benefits vary by plan, so review the available options to determine which one best fits your needs.
  • Are there deductibles or hidden fees? No. The membership plan does not include deductibles or surprise fees. You’ll be able to review the plan costs and benefits before enrolling, making your dental expenses easier to understand and anticipate.
  • Is the membership plan dental insurance? No. The Cinco Ranch Dental In-House Dental Plan is a dental savings plan designed to make dental care without insurance more accessible. There are no insurance claims, annual maximums, or waiting periods to navigate.
  • How do I choose the right plan? Review the benefits included with each of the three plan options and consider the type of care you typically need. Our team can answer your questions and help you understand which option may offer the most value.
