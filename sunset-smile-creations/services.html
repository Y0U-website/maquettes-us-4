Smile Design

If you want an aesthetically pleasing, whiter, brighter, natural-looking smile, Smile Design at Sunset Smile Creations can help — no two cases are similar, so Dr. Lubin builds a complete plan around your specific goals and oral health.

Your Smile Design treatment starts with a conversation, not a mold. Dr. Lubin walks through your goals, checks for any oral health issues that need addressing first, and then designs a plan that may combine veneers, whitening, contouring, or restorative work. Most patients complete their plan in 2–4 visits.

If you live outside our South Florida service area — including Orlando and Tampa, where many of our Smile Design patients travel from — we book and pay for your flight and hotel ourselves, as part of your treatment. It&#x27;s not reimbursed after the fact and it&#x27;s not an add-on fee: we handle every arrangement so a Smile Design trip costs you nothing beyond the treatment itself.
