The practice

We have been serving the Cave Creek, Carefree area’s needs for cosmetic and family dentistry for over 20 years. Giving back to the community is a big part of who we are, providing services to St. Vincent De Paul, the American Legion, and the Arizona Mission of Mercy.

Dr. Solberg is a native of Buffalo New York. He graduated from Canisius College in 1990 with a Bachelor of Science Degree. He went on to study dentistry at the State University of New York at Buffalo and graduated in 1994. He then did 2 years of post graduate residency training at The University of Buffalo and Buffalo General Hospital. Dr. Solberg also served as a Dentist in the US Health Service…

He started his private practice in Carefree Arizona in 1998 and over the past 20 years has built a trust and confidence which draws both a local and international clientele. Dr. Solberg speaks Spanish and practices with his wife Cinthya who is fluent in Spanish and Italian. They have 3 grown children. When not in the office they enjoy training in the gym, krav maja, hiking and travel.
