FAQs About Dental Implants

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes, implants can effectively replace any number of missing teeth, from a single tooth to a full arch. We can use individual implants for multiple single teeth, or strategically placed implants to support multi-tooth bridges or full-arch restorations. The specific number of implants needed depends on your tooth replacement requirements and bone structure.

How soon can I return to work after getting dental implants?

Most patients return to work within 2-3 days after their implant placement. If your job involves strenuous physical activity, we may recommend taking additional time off. We’ll provide specific guidance based on your procedure and individual circumstances.

What’s the difference between dental implants and bridges?

Dental implants replace both the root and crown of missing teeth, while bridges only replace the visible portion above the gumline. Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering healthy adjacent teeth for support. Implants also prevent bone loss by stimulating your jawbone, making them a more comprehensive solution for tooth replacement.

What problems could develop with dental implants?

While implants have a success rate above 95 %, rare complications can include infection around the implant, improper healing, or implant loosening. These issues are typically preventable through proper planning, precise placement, and good oral hygiene. Regular dental check-ups help us monitor your implants and address any concerns early.

How do I know if I have enough bone for dental implants?

We use detailed 3D scans to evaluate your bone density and structure during your initial examination. If you lack adequate bone volume, we can perform bone grafting procedures to create a strong foundation for your implants. The success rate for bone grafting is high, making implants possible for many patients who initially may not seem like candidates.
