FAQs About Professional Teeth Whitening

How does professional teeth whitening address deeper stains compared to home treatments?

Professional teeth whitening uses concentrated agents that penetrate beyond surface enamel to target deeper teeth stains effectively. Our advanced whitening techniques allow these professional-grade solutions to break down stubborn discoloration while being carefully monitored for safety. This controlled application helps achieve superior results compared to retail products, especially for persistent stains.

What steps should I take before my whitening appointment?

We recommend scheduling a professional cleaning two weeks before your whitening process to remove surface stains and ensure optimal results. Prior to your appointment, avoid foods and beverages known to cause tooth discoloration for at least 48 hours. If you experience tooth sensitivity regularly, inform our team so we can adjust your treatment accordingly.

Will whitening affect my existing dental work?

Professional whitening affects only natural tooth enamel and won’t change the color of crowns, veneers, or fillings. During your initial consultation, we’ll examine your existing dental work and discuss how whitening may affect your smile’s overall appearance. We can recommend additional cosmetic options if needed to maintain a consistent appearance.

How can I prevent tooth sensitivity during the whitening process?

Our professional whitening techniques include specific measures to minimize sensitivity while achieving your desired results. We apply protective barriers to safeguard your gums and carefully control the concentration of whitening agents. For patients with sensitive teeth, we can adjust the treatment intensity and provide specialized products for use before and after your procedure.

What makes someone an ideal candidate for professional whitening?

The best candidates for tooth whitening have healthy gums and surface enamel without extensive restoration work. Your tooth discoloration should be natural or diet-related rather than caused by medications or dental procedures. During your consultation, we’ll assess these factors and determine if professional whitening will help you achieve your aesthetic goals.
