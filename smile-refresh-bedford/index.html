Many adults come to us with a smile that used to be perfectly straight until life (and a lost retainer or two) caused a little shifting.

The practice

When you say you’re looking for a “refresh,” most orthodontists think you mean a full treatment plan - moving every tooth, over many months, to reach textbook perfection.

We know that’s not what you meant. You just want a straighter, more confident smile without the long commitment or unnecessary complexity.

That’s why our experts focus on what matters most: your most visible teeth, often called the “Social Six.” With Smile Refresh, you’ll skip the hassle of traditional treatment and see results in just a few visits. Here’s how it works…

We’re not wasting any time. At your initial assessment you’ll meet with a Certified Smile Refresh orthodontist to take digital scans, undergo some X-rays, and make a custom treatment plan personalized to your smile. You’ll leave knowing exactly what to expect, with clear next steps and a plan built by an expert, not an algorithm.

At your next visit, your Smile Refresh orthodontist will place your aligners and ensure they fit perfectly and feel comfortable. This in-person fitting is what sets Smile Refresh apart from DIY options your treatment begins under professional supervision, so you can start with confidence knowing everything is on track from day one.

We’ll be with you every step of the way through on-call phone support, regular check-ins as needed, and appointment reminders to make sure you stay satisfied and on track. Simple, supported, and convenient. That’s the Smile Refresh difference.

Most Smile Refresh treatments take about 6–8 months, designed to fit seamlessly into your life. Along the way, you’ll see steady progress toward a smile that feels like you, just refreshed. Simple, expert-led care without the long-term commitment of traditional orthodontics all for a flat fee of $3,500.

Your treatment is planned and overseen by a licensed orthodontist using 3D scans not impressions or at-home kits so every movement is precise and predictable.
