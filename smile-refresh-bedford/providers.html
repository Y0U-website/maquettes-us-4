Frequently asked questions

Smile Refresh is ideal for adults (18+) who have mild to moderate crowding or spacing and want to align their front teeth on the top and bottom. Many patients have had braces before and noticed some shifting over time. If you’re looking for a quick, effective way to refresh your smile without a long-term commitment, this treatment is made for you.

Smile Refresh treatments are $3,500, with transparent pricing from the start. The fee includes your aligners, doctor visits, and your first set of retainers no hidden costs. Insurance can be applied when available, and every Smile Refresh treatment follows a standard payment plan with clear, pre-set terms to keep things simple and predictable.

Most Smile Refresh treatments finish in about 6 to 8 months. You’ll have an initial consultation, a few streamlined in-office visits, and check-ins that fit easily into your schedule. Expert-led, efficient, and designed around your life.

Smile Refresh is offered only through our hand-selected network of trusted and vetted orthodontists who share our commitment to convenience, clarity, and results. Our concierge team makes it easy call in, request an appointment online, or take our quick quiz, and we’ll match you with a Smile Refresh provider near you.

If your orthodontist determines that Smile Refresh isn’t the right fit for your smile goals, don’t worry you’ll still be in expert hands. Our partnered doctors will review your options and recommend the best treatment plan for your needs, whether that’s a more comprehensive approach or another path to achieve your ideal smile. You’ll always leave informed and confident in your next step.
