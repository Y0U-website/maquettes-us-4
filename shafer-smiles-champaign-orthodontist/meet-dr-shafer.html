Experience & Specialties

Care That Puts You First

Tailored for Every Smile Dr. Shafer has been transforming smiles since 1994, and he brings decades of focused orthodontic experience to every patient he treats. Board-certified and specialized solely in orthodontics, he has created over 10,000 beautiful smiles across Illinois and beyond. From traditional braces to advanced Invisalign treatments, including early intervention and complex cases, Dr. Shafer’s care is fully personalized—designed to meet your unique needs, goals, and lifestyle with excellence and empathy.

Precision Meets Passion Every smile Dr. Shafer designs is backed by a deep passion for the craft and a meticulous attention to detail. Inspired by his own orthodontic experience as a teen , Dr. Shafer understands the powerful impact a confident smile can have on a person’s life. He combines his love for learning, patient-first mindset, and advanced clinical knowledge to deliver orthodontic care that is both technically precise and genuinely heartfelt.

Advanced Techniques, Modern Tools Shafer Orthodontics blends trusted experience with the latest in orthodontic technology to make treatment more efficient, accurate, and convenient. From digital scans and Invisalign Smile Simulations to remote monitoring through Shafer Time Saver, Dr. Shafer stays at the forefront of innovation. His board certification and ongoing pursuit of new techniques ensure every patient receives care that’s modern, minimally invasive, and customized for comfort and success.
