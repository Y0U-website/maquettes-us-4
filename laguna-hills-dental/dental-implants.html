What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the treatment of choice for those missing teeth. There is no question that replacing a tooth with a brand new tooth (implant) is better and longer lasting than the traditional bridge or denture. The advantages of dental implants include only involving one tooth at a time so you don’t have to drill on the adjacent teeth as when you do a bridge. Also, they are aesthetically desirable, have unlimited applications, dependable and long lasting.

Applications of Dental Implants

Implants can be used to replace one tooth, many teeth or to secure dentures. When implants are used for a single tooth, it only involves that specific tooth and does not depend on reconstruction of any adjacent teeth. The old fashion bridge can virtually be eliminated with the titanium implant process.
