Why patients with tooth loss choose dental implants

  • April 1, 2022

What are the benefits of dental implants?

There are many advantages to using dental implants to restore the smile after tooth loss, including:

  • Improved appearance Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth, which can help improve your appearance and self-confidence.
  • Improved oral health Dental implants help maintain the health of your jawbone and surrounding teeth since they replace missing teeth and keep the jawbone from shrinking.
  • Improved function Dental implants allow you to eat and speak normally without worrying about embarrassing slippage or instability.
  • Added comfort Unlike traditional dentures, dental implants do not require adhesives or unique cleaning solutions, so they are more comfortable to wear and care for.
  • Reduced risk of tooth decay Dental implants do not decay like natural teeth, so you don’t have to worry about cavities or other dental problems.
  • Long-lasting With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Dental implants vs. other tooth restoration options

Many patients choose dental implants in Laguna Hills, CA, because of their many advantages over other restoration solutions, including partial dentures, full dentures, and dental bridges. Evaluating the smile is the first step in learning more about a patient’s options and making an educated decision regarding what is best for one’s unique needs and budget.
