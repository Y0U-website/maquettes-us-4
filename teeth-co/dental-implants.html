What Are Dental Implants and How Do They Work?

There are two parts to dental implants. To act as a substitute tooth root, a titanium screw-shaped implant is surgically placed into your gums and jawbone.

After that, an implant-supported restoration can be supported by the implant. However, a lot of implants can also support dentures or dental bridges and fix a lot of teeth. Single teeth are frequently replaced with dental crowns.

In the event that Dr. Neal Rosenberg or approve your dental implant, they will set up your initial consultation. Your mouth will be cleaned and anestheticed before the implant is placed into your jaw. Your mouth will then be cleansed and sterilized, and you’ll be sent home to recuperate.

The first healing phase is much shorter than the osseointegration, or the fusion of your implant with your jawbone, which occurs over a period of three to six months. You will be scheduled for a few follow-up appointments at Teeth and Co. during this period. Dr. Neal Rosenberg will take pictures and imprints of your teeth to a third-party lab to determine the restoration procedure.

You will make your final appointment once your mouth has healed and your implant-supported restoration is complete. We will secure your repair to your implant in order to finish the process and give you back your smile.
