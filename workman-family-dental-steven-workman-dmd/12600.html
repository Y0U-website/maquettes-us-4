Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants have a very high success rate and offer the ideal solution for replacing missing teeth. The secret to the success of dental implants is an artificial root that replaces the natural root of the missing tooth.

Root replacement is important for maintaining the healthy structure of a tooth. When a natural tooth is lost and/or removed, the bone in that area of your jaw tends to deteriorate over time. This can lead to problems like movement of surrounding teeth, crowding, misalignment, and possibly gum disease.

State-of-the-Art Implant Technology

Implants are tiny posts that are placed in your jawbone and gum tissue to act as an artificial tooth root. Implants are typically constructed of titanium, the same material found in modern-day joint replacements. This means that, through a process called osseointegration , the implant will attach securely to surrounding bone and tissue and create a stable foundation for the restoration to which it will be attached.

To ensure the success of your dental implant, Dr. Workman uses advanced 3-D cone beam technology to plan the precise placement of your implant.

Images produced with cone beam scans provide us with valuable information about your teeth, your dental bone, and the relationship between your upper and lower jaw. These high-resolution images allow us to more accurately complete your dental implant surgery.

A dental implant offers greater structural support and lasts longer than either a dental bridge or denture. When in place, dental implants essentially function just as your natural tooth roots would, allowing for dental crowns, dental bridges, and dentures to remain fixed in place. Though the restoration may need to be replaced eventually, the dental implant will last a lifetime when properly cared for.

Frequently Asked Questions

How will a dental implant benefit me?

Replacing a tooth that has been lost to disease, decay, or trauma is important. The empty space left in your mouth doesn’t help your appearance, and it’s not good for your oral health either.

Unlike other tooth replacements, such as bridges or dentures, implants are a truly permanent tooth replacement . The titanium implant is placed securely in your jaw, where it will integrate naturally with surrounding bone and tissue. This means that the implant actually becomes part of your dental anatomy.

Dental implants are versatile and can be restored with a dental crown or attached to bridges or dentures for extra support. The presence of a dental implant in your mouth is also helpful because it prevents the bone in the area of the missing tooth from deteriorating.

Once a crown or other restoration is attached to the implant, you can enjoy a fully functional permanent tooth. The restoration may need to be replaced over time, but the implant will never need to be replaced.
