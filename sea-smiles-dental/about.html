Elevating Community Dentistry

Dr. Elias Khoury

Since graduating, Dr. Khoury has gained experience from working in a variety of practices across California, sometimes serving in four different practices within a five-day workweek. This deliberate dive into diverse office cultures, technologies, and patient populations allowed him to sharpen his skills and form a clear vision for the kind of practice he wanted to lead. That vision is now Sea Smiles Dental: a space where quality care and patient trust go hand in hand.

Dr. Khoury’s clinical strengths lie in surgical procedures such as implant restorations , bone grafting, and extractions . Thanks to extensive continuing education, including hands-on implant courses and oral conscious sedation certification, he offers a level of expertise rarely found in general practice. He approaches dentistry comprehensively, making sure patients understand their full oral health picture up front, so they can plan for the long term without surprises down the road.

Outside the office, Dr. Khoury enjoys golfing, trying out new restaurants, going to the gym, traveling, and spending quality time with his wife, family, and close friends.

Your First Visit Made Simple

What to Expect When You’re New to Sea Smiles Dental

Your first visit to our San Pedro office is all about making a great first impression. From the front desk to the dental chair, we prioritize warmth, transparency, and personalized care. You’ll leave your appointment feeling informed, confident, and on the right path to a healthier smile.

Patient Experience We begin with a comprehensive exam, discuss your goals, and walk you through any findings or recommendations. We take our time to answer your questions and never rush you into treatment. You can expect a judgment-free space where trust is valued just as much as your oral health.

Any recent dental records or X-rays (if available)

Technology and Comforts That Support Better Experiences

Sea Smiles Dental is a modern dental office focused on clean spaces, efficient visits, and high-quality clinical care. We invest in advanced hygiene, diagnostic, and treatment technology to make your visits more comfortable and your care more precise. Our ultimate goal is to create a patient-centered environment—so you can feel confident in the care you receive every time you visit.

EMS Airflow® Prophylaxis

Our hygienists use the EMS Airflow® unit to deliver next-level cleanings. This advanced system targets plaque and tartar with precision, using air, water, and powder for a gentler yet more powerful clean, perfect for patients with sensitive teeth or periodontal needs.

Intraoral Scanner

Our advanced intraoral scanner allows for faster digital impressions, more precise diagnostics, and a better overall experience. Think, no more goopy trays or uncomfortable molds.

CBCT 3D Imaging

We use advanced CBCT 3D imaging to plan implant placement with exceptional precision. This detailed view of your bone and anatomy allows for safer procedures, accurate positioning, and a smoother experience from start to finish.

Comfort-First Care Philosophy

Whether it’s our use of advanced anesthetics or a personalized explanation of your treatment plan, comfort and education are always part of the process. We aim to deliver care with ease and empathy every step of the way.
