Meet Dr. Luljeta Isedisha, owner and dentist at Smile Design Dental Team in Dedham, Massachusetts.

Dr. Luljeta Isedisha, MS, DMD

Dr. Isedisha is the new owner of Smile Design Dental Team, where she is proud to continue the tradition of exceptional, personalized dental care that patients have come to trust.

Born and raised in Albania, Dr. Isedisha graduated top of her class with a Master of Science in Dentistry. She then moved to the Boston area and graduated as Valedictorian from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine (GSDM).

During her time at GSDM, Dr. Isedisha received numerous honors, including the Quintessence Award for Clinical Achievement in Periodontics, the Academy of General Dentistry Award, and the Ozar Hasegawa Award for Dental Ethics. Upon graduation, she was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU), the National Dental Honor Society.

An avid lifelong learner, Dr. Isedisha continually pursues advanced continuing education so she can broaden the services she offers her patients. She provides a wide range of restorative, preventative, and cosmetic dental services and is an Invisalign certified provider. Her compassion and gentle chairside manner put patients at ease for every visit from routine cleanings to full smile transformations.

Dr. Isedisha is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the American Association of Women Dentists (AAWD), the Massachusetts Dental Society (MDS), and the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD).

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Isedisha enjoys traveling, exploring new restaurants and coffee shops, and spending time with her family.
