Meet Dr. Granovsky Dentist in Dedham MA

Inspired by her father’s dental career, Dr. Sveta Granovsky graduated in 1994 from Boston University Dental School. She has practiced in Dedham, Massachusetts, since 1997 and continues to provide outstanding dentistry for her patients at Smile Design Dental Team. She is always eager to learn new techniques and procedures and has built a successful career by taking continuing education courses at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Cosmetic Dentistry.

She has committed herself to both educating patients about the importance of dentistry as well as giving back to the community.

Dr. Sveta Granovsky believes in delivering dental care in a kind and gentle manner and is committed to providing outstanding services to every patient at Smile Design Dental Team.

In her spare time, Dr. Sveta Granovsky enjoys traveling and practicing yoga.
