Custom Vs. Store-Bought Options

Store-bought boil-and-bite guards can offer short-term relief, but they often feel bulky and may not distribute forces evenly. A custom dental night guard is designed from impressions or a digital scan of your bite for a precise, comfortable fit. Custom guards are typically more durable, stay in place better, and are tailored to your specific grinding pattern for more reliable protection.

Benefits of Night Guards

  • Protects enamel by reducing wear and microfractures.
  • Helps stabilize the bite and may ease TMJ-related strain.
  • Decreases morning headaches and muscle fatigue.
  • Shields dental work such as crowns and veneers.
  • Promotes quieter, more restful sleep for some patients.
  • Can reduce tooth sensitivity caused by grinding.

The Night Guard Process

The process is straightforward and centered on comfort and accuracy:

  • Evaluation and diagnosis: Our dentist and team review symptoms, examine teeth and jaw joints, and may discuss stress, sleep habits, or acid reflux that can influence bruxism.
  • Impressions or digital scan: A precise model of your bite is made for a custom fit.
  • Laboratory fabrication: The guard is made from high-quality acrylic or flexible materials tailored to your needs.
  • Fitting and adjustments: The appliance is checked, refined for pressure points, and polished for smooth wear.
  • Follow-up: Bite and comfort are reviewed, and care instructions are reinforced.

What to Expect

Most people adjust to a night guard within one to two weeks. You may notice extra saliva at first, which settles as your mouth adapts. Wear the guard nightly as directed to see the full benefits. Clean it daily with a soft toothbrush and mild soap, then rinse and let it dry fully before storing it in a ventilated case. Avoid hot water, bleach, and abrasive cleaners that can warp or scratch the material. With proper care, many custom night guards last two to five years, though heavy grinders may need earlier replacement. Bring your guard to checkups so it can be evaluated for wear and fit.
