Meet the heart behind the practice

Get to know Dr. Sean Chang

Dr. Sean Chang is the founder and lead dentist at Blue Brush Dental. Born in Korea and raised in Newport Beach, CA, Dr. Chang developed a passion for dentistry early on, influenced by his uncle, an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon. Before beginning his dental journey, he proudly served in the U.S. Army as a Tanker from 2002 to 2005, an experience that shaped his strong sense of discipline, resilience, and dedication to service.

Dr. Chang holds dual degrees in Biology and Economics from the University of California, Irvine, and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the NYU College of Dentistry. He went on to complete a residency at New York Presbyterian Hospital in Queens, where he deepened his expertise in oral surgery, pediatric dentistry, and implantology. A dedicated lifelong learner, Dr. Chang regularly attends advanced courses and serves as an Adjunct Assistant Professor at the USC Dental School, mentoring future dental professionals.

With over a decade of clinical experience, Dr. Chang’s approach is grounded in compassion, integrity, and individualized care. Whether performing a routine exam or advanced surgical procedure, his goal is to ensure a seamless, stress-free experience for every patient who walks through the door.

General Dentist

Meet Dr. Kathleen Nguyen

Dr. Kat, a San Jose native, began her journey into dentistry through volunteering in underserved clinics, where she witnessed how oral health impacts overall wellbeing. She earned her Biology degree from UC Santa Cruz while also exploring her passion for design—a background that continues to influence her artistic approach to dentistry.

After completing her Doctor of Dental Medicine at Midwestern University in Arizona, Dr. Kat trained extensively in restorative, pediatric, and cosmetic dentistry while practicing in Scottsdale. Today, she seamlessly blends aesthetics and clinical precision to create beautiful, healthy smiles. Her focus is on patient education, comfort, and crafting custom treatment plans that reflect each person’s unique goals.

Outside the office, Dr. Kat enjoys painting, curating elaborate charcuterie boards, and staying active through fitness and travel. She remains committed to professional growth, regularly attending continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of dental innovation.

Prosthodontist

Dr. Eugene Kim

Dr. Eugene Kim is a board-certified prosthodontist with advanced training from UCLA’s prestigious School of Dentistry, where he also completed his specialty residency in Advanced Prosthodontics. This field of dentistry focuses on restoring and replacing teeth through prosthetics, including crowns, bridges, and dental implants.

Beyond clinical care, Dr. Kim is widely respected as an educator and leader in his specialty. He is the founder and president of Pacific Implant Academy, through which he offers seminars, live surgeries, and training courses for dental professionals across the country. He has also served as a clinical instructor in removable prosthodontics at UCLA.

With a private practice in Buena Park and affiliations with leading dental institutes, Dr. Kim combines clinical excellence with a passion for teaching. His goal is to help both patients and peers achieve optimal results through meticulous technique and evidence-based care.

Endodontist

Dr. Brian Chen

Dr. Brian Chen is a highly skilled endodontist who brings a gentle, compassionate approach to root canal therapy and other advanced endodontic procedures. After earning his dental degree, Dr. Chen completed his specialty training at the USC Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry and has since practiced throughout Southern California.

Specializing in endodontic diagnosis, retreatment, and root-end surgeries, Dr. Chen is known for his precise clinical skills, calming demeanor, and patient-centered care. His focus is not just on resolving dental pain, but on ensuring a comfortable and informed experience for every patient.

Since joining Blue Brush Dental in early 2022, Dr. Chen has become a trusted resource for complex root canal cases and continues to advance his skills through ongoing education and collaboration with colleagues.

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Jee Hoon Ha

Dr. Jee Hoon Ha’s journey to dentistry is rooted in engineering and innovation. Originally from Seoul and raised in Missouri, he holds degrees in Electrical Engineering from KAIST and USC, and conducted AI research in Tokyo before pivoting to dentistry. He earned his DDS from UCLA, where he also contributed to 3D imaging research in bone and dental structures.

Dr. Ha completed his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at New York Medical College / Westchester Medical Center, receiving comprehensive training in anesthesia, general surgery, plastic surgery, and head and neck procedures. His clinical interests span corrective jaw surgery, dental implants, facial trauma, and cosmetic facial procedures.

Blending surgical expertise with an analytical mindset, Dr. Ha brings a unique perspective to patient care. He holds permits in General Anesthesia and Elective Cosmetic Surgery, and is fully certified to perform a wide range of maxillofacial procedures with precision and care.

A better visit starts here

Here’s what to expect at your first visit

Your first appointment at Blue Brush Dental is all about getting to know you. We take time to listen to your needs and goals while performing a comprehensive exam and any necessary diagnostics. Our goal is to deliver personalized, high-tech care in a calm environment where you can feel informed and empowered every step of the way.

Patient Experience Stepping into our office feels more like visiting a cozy café than a dentist’s office. You’ll be greeted with smiles and maybe even your favorite Netflix show playing in the operatory. Our multilingual team ensures that every patient feels understood and cared for—from your first phone call to your final treatment plan. We value relationships, not just results.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first visit:

Completed new patient forms (submitted digitally)

Modern comforts and advanced technology, tailored just for you

Our office is equipped with the latest amenities and dental technology designed to enhance your comfort, improve outcomes, and make each visit feel seamless. From streaming entertainment to precision imaging, everything we do is built around a better patient experience.

Digital scanners

Our practice features four advanced digital scanners, including 3Shape TRIOS® and iTero Lumina™, allowing us to capture fast, comfortable, and highly accurate 3D impressions. These scanners are ideal for Invisalign®, crowns, and implant planning without the discomfort and mess of traditional molds.

VaTech CBCT imaging

With our VaTech 3D CBCT scanner, we capture detailed cross-sectional images of your teeth, bones, and nerves for more informed diagnostics and treatment planning, especially for implants and surgeries.

Zeiss endoscopic microscope

This advanced tool allows our team to perform complex procedures, such as root canal retreatments, with greater precision and visibility for better outcomes and faster recoveries.

Philips Zoom whitening system

Brighten your smile safely and effectively with our in-office Philips Zoom whitening treatment. It delivers radiant results in just one visit with minimal sensitivity.

In-room entertainment and comforts

Relax during your visit with TVs in every operatory, Netflix, YouTube, soft blankets, and stress balls. Enjoy a coffee or tea from our beverage station before or after your appointment—it’s all part of making your experience feel easy and enjoyable.
