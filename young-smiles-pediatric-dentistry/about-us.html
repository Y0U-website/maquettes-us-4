More About Us

Florence Pediatric Dentists are specialists that have specific training and additional education which makes them uniquely qualified in addressing the dental needs of children.

Dr. Alex Young

I grew up in London, KY– home of the World Chicken Festival! My husband Adam, my daughter Amelia, my son Jameson, and I moved to Northern Kentucky from Lexington in 2019; you can find us at the pool, on the golf course, at the soccer fields at the Boone County Arboretum, or at the Cincinnati Museum Center for fun. We also love going to the Cincinnati Zoo to check on “Fiona” the hippo and feed the giraffes. And of course we love cheering for the Bengals! Who Dey!

While new to the community, I am not new to dentistry. I graduated from the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry in 2009. I practiced general dentistry for two years with my father in his practice in London, KY. It was during that time that I realized my love for working with children. I was so grateful for the opportunity to complete my residency in Pediatric Dentistry at the University of Kentucky in 2013 and treated children in Lexington, KY for 6 years.

From our fun-loving team to our sweet patients, there is never a dull moment at our Florence KY practice. We love being able to give our patients a comfortable and positive dental experience.

Favorite Quote: “Attitude is everything!”

As a mom, I understand the importance of finding quality healthcare providers for your children who are approachable and trustworthy.

As a family, we love exploring local parks and visiting the Cincinnati Zoo, Newport Aquarium, and the Children’s Museum at the Cincinnati Museum Center. At our practice, we aim to create a welcoming atmosphere where trust and understanding are paramount. I look forward to meeting you and your family and providing exceptional dental care for your children.

We keep a fun and friendly atmosphere in our office so that your little ones are happy, relaxed, and comfortable.
