Oral Hygiene in Fort Lauderdale

The Foundation of a Healthy Smile

Brushing and flossing alone aren’t always enough to remove plaque and bacteria in hard-to-reach areas. That’s why regular professional cleanings and personalized oral hygiene guidance are key to keeping your smile in top shape.

The Essentials of a Healthy Oral Care Routine

  • Brush Twice a Day – Use a fluoride toothpaste and a soft-bristled brush to clean all surfaces of your teeth.
  • Floss Daily – Removes plaque and food particles from between the teeth and along the gumline.
  • Use Mouthwash – An antibacterial rinse can help reduce bacteria and freshen breath.
  • Eat a Balanced Diet – Limit sugary foods and drinks that can contribute to decay.
  • Visit Your Dentist Regularly – Professional cleanings and exams help catch and prevent problems early.

How to Floss Properly

Flossing is essential for removing plaque and food particles from areas your toothbrush can’t reach, especially between the teeth and along the gumline. Developing the right flossing technique takes practice, but it’s key to preventing cavities, gum disease, and bad breath . Follow these simple steps to ensure an effective flossing routine.

Maintaining a daily flossing habit alongside proper brushing and professional cleanings is the best way to keep your smile healthy and strong .

  • Use the Right Amount of Floss – Cut a piece of dental floss about 18 inches long . Wrap most of it around the middle finger of one hand and the rest around the middle finger of the other hand, leaving a few inches in between for cleaning.
  • Position the Floss Correctly – Hold the floss tightly between your thumbs and forefingers and gently slide it between two teeth using a back-and-forth motion .
  • Curve Around Each Tooth – When the floss reaches the gumline, curve it into a C-shape against one tooth and gently slide it under the gum . Move the floss up and down to remove plaque before switching to the next tooth.
  • Use a Fresh Section for Each Tooth – As you move from tooth to tooth, unwind a clean section of floss to ensure maximum effectiveness and avoid spreading plaque.
  • Don’t Forget the Back Teeth – Make sure to floss behind the last molars, as plaque can easily build up in these areas.
  • Rinse & Check for Bleeding – After flossing, rinse your mouth with water or mouthwash to remove loosened plaque. Some minor bleeding is normal at first but should stop as your gums become healthier with regular flossing.

Choosing the Right Oral Hygiene Products

With so many dental care products available, it can be overwhelming to choose the best ones for your oral health. Here are some expert recommendations to help you maintain a healthy and effective hygiene routine :

Consult with your dentist to determine which products best fit your individual dental needs.

  • Electric Toothbrushes: Brands like Sonicare and Oral-B provide superior plaque removal compared to manual brushes.
  • Oral Irrigators (Water Flossers): These devices rinse away debris but should be used alongside traditional flossing for best results.
  • Interdental Brushes & Rubber Tips: Small brushes can clean between teeth, and rubber tips can massage gums —but proper use is essential to avoid irritation.
  • Fluoride Toothpaste & Rinses: Fluoride helps prevent tooth decay when combined with brushing and flossing. However, fluoride rinses are not recommended for children under six .
  • Anti-Plaque Mouthwashes: ADA-approved rinses can help control early gum disease but should be used as part of a complete oral care routine.

The Importance of Professional Dental Cleanings

Brushing and flossing at home are essential, but even with the best hygiene routine, plaque and tartar can still build up in hard-to-reach areas. Professional dental cleanings remove deep-seated buildup , helping to prevent gum disease, cavities, and bad breath.
