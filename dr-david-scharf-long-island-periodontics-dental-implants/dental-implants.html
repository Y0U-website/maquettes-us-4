Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer both cosmetic and functional advantages, making them one of the most effective treatments for tooth loss.

Natural Appearance and Feel

Dental implants are designed to closely mimic the appearance and function of natural teeth. Your custom restoration is crafted to blend seamlessly with your smile.

Prevent Bone Loss

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to shrink over time. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and help preserve bone structure, preventing facial sagging and bone deterioration.

Improved Comfort and Stability

Unlike removable dentures, implants stay securely in place. You can eat, speak, and smile confidently without worrying about slipping or discomfort.

Long-Term Durability

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for decades and may even last a lifetime.

Better Oral Health

Dental implants do not rely on neighboring teeth for support, which helps preserve your natural tooth structure and overall oral health.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation and Examination

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive consultation and dental evaluation. Dr. Scharf or Dr. Mukhtar will assess your oral health, review digital imaging, and discuss your goals to determine whether dental implants are right for you.

Preparatory Procedures

Some patients may require additional treatments before implants can be placed successfully, including:

These procedures help create a strong foundation for implant success.

  • Tooth extractions
  • Bone grafting
  • Sinus augmentation
  • Periodontal therapy

Implant Placement

During the implant procedure, the area is thoroughly numbed to ensure your comfort. The titanium implant post is carefully placed into the jawbone using precise surgical techniques.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next several months, the implant fuses with the jawbone to create a stable, permanent anchor for your restoration.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, impressions are taken to fabricate your custom crown, bridge, or denture. The final restoration is then attached to the implant, completing your smile transformation.

After an exam and consultation, we will create a personalized treatment plan and meet with you to go over the dental implant procedure and answer all your questions.

Dental Implant Recovery

Recovery after dental implant surgery varies depending on the complexity of the procedure and the number of implants placed. Most patients experience mild discomfort, swelling, or soreness for a few days after surgery.

Recovery Tips

To support healing after dental implant placement:

Most patients can return to normal daily activities within a few days, although full healing and bone integration may take several months.

  • Get plenty of rest
  • Eat soft foods during the healing period
  • Avoid smoking
  • Keep the surgical area clean
  • Brush gently with a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Rinse with a saltwater solution as instructed
  • Take prescribed medications as directed
