Dear Potential Patient,

Do you care about how you are treated when you visit the dentist?

Sadly, for many people, the answer is no. Dental patients routinely endure rude staff, long waits, minimal explanations, and poor service—visit after visit after visit. For many, the thought of trying to switch to a new dentist seems overwhelming—and there’s no guarantee it will be any different if they do.

At my office, YOU come first—your comfort, your education, your choices. My staff and I will always treat you as a valued patient and will never dismiss your feelings, anxieties, or questions. We are here to provide the very best possible care for you, and that means listening to you with compassion and respect and coming up with a treatment plan that fits your needs and your budget.

If you like, you can take a few minutes to browse this site and learn about my education, my board certification, the countries I have lectured in, the articles I have published, and how many years I have been both practicing as a periodontist and teaching other expert dentists how to do dental implants . I have pursued technical excellence during my entire career and have received professional recognition from my colleagues, and all of this is important—but what makes my practice a special and intimate place is the way we treat our patients.

What It’s Like To Be a Patient in Our Office

Our attention to your needs begins with your first call to our office. When the office is not staffed, we forward calls to a cell phone after hours. This allows us to help you by phone when it’s convenient for your schedule. There are instances when we are unavailable, such as during sleep or when unable to pick up the phone. During these times, we opt for voicemail instead of an answering service to ensure that we can directly listen to your concerns in your own words, avoiding any potential misinterpretation by a third party. We will call you back and address your needs quickly.

During business hours, our knowledgeable team members will assist you when you call. If the initial contact cannot address your specific needs, they will relay your call details to the appropriate person. When transferred, the receiving party will already be briefed with the information you provided, eliminating the need for you to repeat yourself.

We send our new patients a welcome packet in the mail. This packet contains your medical history and patient registration forms. We transcribe any information you provide over the phone for you to save you time. You can also download the forms directly from this website if you wish.

Completing your health history at home allows you to have access to necessary information about your medications and any dental benefit information. You can also complete the form at your convenience. At your appointment, simply hand our team member the completed forms to be seen promptly upon arrival. If you have any questions about filling out the forms, feel free to call and one of my team members will be happy to help you!
