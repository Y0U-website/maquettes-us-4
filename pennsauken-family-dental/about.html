Meet Dr. Kyra ChU

Dr. Kyra Chu grew up in Toronto, Canada, where she earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Chemical Engineering from Toronto Metropolitan University before pursuing her passion for dentistry. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the New York University College of Dentistry and completed a General Practice Residency at Mount Sinai Hospital, where she received advanced training in comprehensive dental care, dental emergencies, and the treatment of medically complex patients.

Dr. Chu is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. She believes in helping patients feel informed, at ease, and confident throughout every step of their dental journey. She enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and values building lasting relationships while helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Now residing in New Jersey, Dr. Chu enjoys staying active at the gym, exploring new restaurants, attending live music concerts, and spending time with friends and family.
