What Patients Can Expect

Your First Visit Experience

Your first visit sets the foundation for your dental journey with us. During this comprehensive appointment, Dr. Francis takes time to understand your dental history, concerns, and goals. Our team will perform a thorough examination that includes digital imaging, oral cancer screening, and a detailed assessment of your bite and jaw alignment. This careful approach allows us to create a personalized treatment plan that addresses both immediate needs and long-term oral health.

What to Bring Please bring your photo ID, insurance information, and completed patient forms. If you have recent x-rays or dental records from a previous provider, those are helpful but not required.
