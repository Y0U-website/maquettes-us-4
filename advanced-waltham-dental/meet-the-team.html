Jolmiry Campos, DMD

With over a decade of experience, Dr. Campos is passionate about transforming smiles and improving lives through exceptional dental care. Inspired by her family’s dental background, she earned her first dental degree with high honors and a Research Award in Venezuela, where she also completed an endodontic master degree. After relocating to the U.S., Dr. Campos earned her second dental degree from Boston University, with high honors, and she received the Student Achievement Award in Endodontics and served as a Teaching Assistant.

Committed to ongoing education and excellence, Dr. Campos holds memberships in the Venezuelan Endodontic Association, the American Endodontic Association (AEA), the Massachusetts Dental Society (MDS), and the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD). These affiliations ensure that she stays abreast of the latest advancements in the field and continues to provide high-quality care to her patients.

Prioritizing patient comfort and education, Dr. Campos strives to create a caring atmosphere where patients feel at ease. By fostering open communication and providing detailed explanations of procedures, she builds trust and ensures a positive experience. Patient education is vital; she believes informed patients are empowered to take an active role in their dental health.

Beyond her professional life, Dr. Campos is an avid sports enthusiast and a certified spinning instructor. She enjoys running, hiking, reading, and drawing, which enrich her creativity and personal growth.
