General dentistry provides preventive, restorative, and routine oral care to keep teeth and gums healthy. Regular check-ups help prevent issues and ensure long-term dental wellness.

Cosmetic dentistry enhances the appearance of teeth and smiles through treatments like teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and smile makeovers. It focuses on aesthetics while maintaining oral health.

Orthodontics is a dental specialty that corrects misaligned teeth and jaw issues using braces, aligners, and other appliances to improve function, aesthetics, and oral health.
