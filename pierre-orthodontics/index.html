In practice over 12 years, Dr.Hoggan received his doctorate at the University of Oklahoma Dental School in 2000. He completed his orthodontic residency at St. Louis University and earned a Master of Science degree in Orthodontics in 2002.

The recipient of numerous honors and awards, Dr. Hoggan graduated with distinction and was selected as the recipient of the Uchiyama-Truetzel Award for Orthodontics.

Dr. Hoggan is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the South Dakota Dental Association. He has also conducted and authored original research in the field of orthodontics.
