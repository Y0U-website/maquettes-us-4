COVID 19 Updates and Forms

Orthodontic treatment in the Era of COVID 19 has brought new procedures and requirements. Two additional forms will need to be signed prior to proceeding with orthodontic care. These forms can be downloaded below.

Visiting Our Office Changes.

Pierre Orthodontics has a state-of-the-art facility, complete with digital x-ray and imaging equipment and an autoclave sterilization system that cleans dental instruments to meet surgical standards. While these standards will kill all viruses and bacteria, special considerations have been made to help prevent virus transmission of COVID 19.

Parents and family members please wait in cars. Patients only should enter the building unless special circumstances exist. If you need to talk with staff or the doctor, please call to make arrangements. Please schedule next appointments by telephone. Or, patients can schedule at the chair as is usually done.

Patients only in the treatment area unless special circumstances exist.

Please brush well at home prior to coming to your visit. The tooth brush station is closed until further notice.

Staff and doctors will be wearing different clothing as recommended by national and local authorities.

All employees will be monitoring their health daily to insure a safe environment for all.

A supplemental informed consent will be need to be signed ONCE prior to resuming treatment. Please download the form.
