The All-On-X Process at Complete Dentistry

The All-On-X process begins with a detailed consultation where Dr. Crumpton reviews your dental and medical history, takes digital images, and discusses your goals. From there, a customized treatment plan is developed.

Implant Placement

On the day of surgery, the implants are precisely positioned in the jaw under anesthesia. A temporary, fully functional prosthesis is attached the same day in most cases, so patients leave with teeth in place. This is one of the most appealing aspects of the All-On-X approach, as there is no extended period of going without teeth.

Healing and Final Restoration

Over the following months, the implants fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. Once this is complete, the temporary prosthesis is replaced with a permanent final restoration. A longitudinal study published in PubMed found a prosthesis survival rate of 99.2 % over 10 years of follow-up, underscoring the long-term reliability of this treatment approach.

All-On-X vs. Traditional Dentures

For patients weighing their options, understanding the differences between All-On-X and conventional implant-supported dentures or removable dentures is important. Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can become loose over time as the jawbone naturally resorbs after tooth loss. They can also limit what you eat, which may affect speech.

All-On-X implants, by contrast, are fixed in place and stimulate the jawbone the same way natural tooth roots do. This prevents the bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth and helps maintain facial structure over time. Patients report a dramatic improvement in quality of life, eating comfort, and self-confidence compared to removable alternatives.
