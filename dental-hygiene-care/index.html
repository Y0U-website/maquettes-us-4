Ana is a California-based Registered Dental Hygienist in Alternative Practice with advanced training from the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. Originally from Nicaragua and with a background in business and marketing, she brings a unique blend of clinical excellence and thoughtful, patient-centered care to her practice.

With specialized expertise in dental hygiene and myofunctional therapy, Ana is committed to helping patients improve oral health, optimize oral function, and support airway wellness. Through individualized treatment plans and meaningful education, she empowers patients to take an active role in their long-term well-being.

She is dedicated to creating a warm, supportive environment where every patient feels heard, respected, and confident in their care. Outside of work, Ana enjoys spending quality time with her family and friends and making memories with her three beloved dogs.
