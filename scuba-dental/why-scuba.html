Making Dentistry Accessible and Affordable

At Scuba Dental, we believe that everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care, regardless of their insurance status or budget. That’s why we’re proud to be a Medicaid and CHIP provider in Bellaire, TX. We also accept most major insurance plans, including PPO and HMO policies. Our knowledgeable team will work with you to maximize your benefits and minimize your out-of-pocket expenses.

For patients without insurance or with limited coverage, we offer a range of flexible payment options, including CareCredit financing and our own in-house membership plan. Our goal is to make dental care affordable and accessible for all, so you can focus on achieving and maintaining a healthy smile without breaking the bank.

Comprehensive Care for the Whole Family

Our scuba-themed office isn’t just for kids - it’s for patients of all ages! We offer a full range of dental services, from routine check-ups and cleanings to complex restorative and cosmetic treatments. Our skilled Bellaire dentists and specialists are trained in the latest techniques and technologies, ensuring that you receive the highest quality care in a comfortable, relaxed setting.

For our younger patients, we provide gentle, compassionate pediatric dentistry services, including sealants, fluoride treatments, and custom mouthguards for sports. We also offer orthodontic treatment, including traditional braces and Invisalign clear aligners, to help teens and adults achieve straight, healthy smiles.
