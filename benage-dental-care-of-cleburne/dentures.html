The Denture Process at Benage Dental Care

Initial Consultation: Dr. Benage evaluates your remaining teeth, gum health, and bone structure. He discusses your options and helps you choose the right denture type.

Impressions & Measurements: Precise impressions and measurements are taken to ensure your dentures fit accurately and your bite is properly aligned.

Try-In Appointment: A wax model of your dentures lets you preview the fit, appearance, and bite before the final denture is fabricated. Adjustments are made at this stage.

Delivery & Fitting: Your final dentures are delivered and adjusted for comfort and fit. Most patients need minor follow-up adjustments in the first few weeks as the mouth adapts.

Getting Used to New Dentures

Adjusting to new dentures takes time — typically 4–8 weeks for most patients to feel fully comfortable. You may notice slight changes in speech initially (the mouth adapts quickly), and eating will feel different until you develop new chewing patterns. Dr. Benage will walk you through realistic expectations and provide guidance for the adjustment period. Most patients find they adapt faster than they expected.

Denture Care and Maintenance

  • Remove and rinse after eating
  • Brush gently with a soft denture brush and non-abrasive cleaner daily
  • Soak overnight in a denture solution to maintain shape and hygiene
  • Never use hot water (it can warp the material)
  • Visit Benage Dental Care annually — dentures need periodic adjustments as the mouth changes over time

Traditional Dentures

When you’re missing most of your teeth , life becomes more complicated. Chewing is painful, and you might have to adjust your diet. Speaking becomes difficult. And you may feel self-conscious about your appearance.

With dentures , you can replace all your teeth in one go, and everything will go back to normal. You’ll be able to eat most of your favorite foods again, people will understand you easier, and your smile will make you feel young again.

The Denture Process At Benage Dental Care

Wondering what it takes to get dentures? Here’s what the process looks like.

  • Comprehensive Consultation Your journey towards a confident smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Benage Dental Care. During this initial appointment, our skilled dental team will assess your oral health, discuss your specific dental concerns, and determine if dentures are the optimal solution for your needs.
  • Individualized treatment plan Following the consultation, our team will craft a personalized treatment plan tailored to address your unique dental situation and smile goals. This plan outlines the steps involved in the denture process, ensuring a customized and effective approach to your dental care.
  • Impressions Precise impressions of your teeth and gums are taken. We’ll use these impressions to personalize your dentures so that they fit snuggly over your gums and blend in with your smile.
  • Denture fabrication Next, we’ll send the impressions to a lab where your custom dentures will be created. This process can take a couple of weeks, so we’ll let you know when you can return for your fitting.
  • Denture placement Once your dentures are ready, you’ll return to our office and we’ll let you try on your new teeth. You’ll be able to tell us if anything needs to be adjusted, and we’ll make those changes.
