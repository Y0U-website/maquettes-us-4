What to Expect: The Dental Implant Timeline

One of the most common concerns patients have is how long the process takes. Here is a typical step-by-step timeline for dental implants at Benage Dental Care:

Total treatment time typically ranges from 4 to 12 months depending on your unique situation. Many patients tell us it was far more straightforward than they anticipated.

  • Initial consultation & planning X-rays, 3D scans, and a full evaluation. We determine whether bone grafting is needed and create your custom treatment plan.
  • Bone graft (if needed) If your jawbone requires additional volume, a graft is placed first. Healing takes 3 6 months before implant placement can proceed.
  • Implant placement A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. The procedure itself typically takes 1 2 hours.
  • Osseointegration (healing) The implant fuses with your natural bone over 3 6 months. This stage is critical for long-term stability.
  • Abutment & crown placement Once fully healed, we attach the abutment and place your custom porcelain crown your permanent, natural-looking replacement tooth.

The Dental Implant Process at Benage Dental Care

If you’re looking to replace a missing tooth, Benage Dental Care is here to help! Dental implants are completely safe and require little to no additional care outside of routine oral care. Plus, with the use of our dental sedatives, you will hardly feel anything during the placement process.

  • Initial Consultation Your journey to a restored smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Benage Dental Care. Our caring, skilled dental team will assess your oral health, discuss your concerns, and determine if dental implants are the right solution for you.
  • Treatment Planning Following the initial consultation, a personalized treatment plan is created based on your specific needs and goals. This plan outlines the steps of the implant process and addresses any additional treatments, such as bone grafting, if necessary.
  • Implant Placement The implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone This phase is crucial as it establishes a strong and stable foundation for the replacement tooth. This procedure is often handled in a separate appointment to allow your body time to heal before attaching a prosthetic.
  • Healing Period (Osseointegration) Over the next few months, the implant post undergoes a process called osseointegration, during which it fuses with the surrounding bone. This integration allows an implant to function alongside the existing bone and gum tissue, helping the implants last as long as possible!
  • Abutment Placement Once osseointegration is complete, the abutment is attached to the implant post. Depending on your situation, the abutments may be placed with the implants or during a separate visit. Implant abutments are shaped to perfectly fit your custom tooth crown or prosthetic.
  • Custom Crown Placement The final touch involves placing a custom-designed crown onto the abutment. The crown is meticulously crafted to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, ensuring a seamless and aesthetically pleasing result.
