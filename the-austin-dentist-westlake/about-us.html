OUR Story

For 20 years, Dr. Matthew Horne and our team at The Austin Dentist have provided care that’s personal, reliable and refreshingly stress-free. We’re proud to help Westlake families smile with confidence year after year.

Dr. Horne founded The Austin Dentist in 2005 with one clear priority: to deliver the highest standard of dental care, every single day.

That commitment started early. After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Louisiana State University, he received the Navy Health Professions Scholarship and completed an advanced residency in San Diego. He then served as a Navy dentist for four years, where attention to detail and doing things right the first time were essential.

Those values continue to guide his work today.

Dr. Horne believes quality is more than technical skill. It requires time, focus, and a deep respect for the people you serve. He continues to grow through advanced training at the Kois Center, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the Ickert Implant Teaching Center, and other respected programs focused on clinical excellence and evidence-based care.

At The Austin Dentist, quality means work that stands the test of time, delivered with thoughtful planning and clear communication. Whether you visit us for preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, or a full-mouth reconstruction, you can expect care that is consistent, precise, and centered on your needs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED Questions

What makes The Austin Dentist different from other dentists in Austin, TX?

The Austin Dentist focuses on personalized dental care, advanced technology, and long term oral health solutions for patients in Austin, TX. Our team takes time to understand each patient’s goals while providing comprehensive dentistry in a comfortable environment.

What dental services does The Austin Dentist provide?

The Austin Dentist offers a wide range of dental services including preventive dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, Invisalign, emergency dental care, sedation dentistry, and comprehensive maintenance care for patients in Austin and surrounding areas.

Does The Austin Dentist accept new patients?

Yes, The Austin Dentist welcomes new patients at both the Circle C and Westlake locations. We work with individuals and families looking for trusted general dentistry, cosmetic dental treatments, and long term oral health care in Austin, TX.

What technology does The Austin Dentist use?

The Austin Dentist uses advanced dental technology including digital imaging, CEREC same day restorations, Solea laser dentistry, and modern diagnostic tools to help improve patient comfort, efficiency, and treatment accuracy.

Can The Austin Dentist help patients with dental anxiety?

Yes, The Austin Dentist offers sedation dentistry options for patients who experience dental anxiety or fear of dental treatment. Our team works to create a calm and comfortable experience for every patient.

Does The Austin Dentist provide cosmetic dentistry services?

Yes, cosmetic dentistry services include teeth whitening, Invisalign, smile makeovers, veneers, and other treatments designed to improve the appearance and confidence of your smile.

Where is The Austin Dentist located?

The Austin Dentist has convenient locations serving patients in Circle C, Westlake, and surrounding Austin, TX communities, making it easier for families and individuals to access quality dental care close to home.

Why is preventive dental care important?

Preventive dental care helps patients maintain healthy teeth and gums while reducing the risk of cavities, gum disease, and costly dental procedures. Regular cleanings and exams also support overall oral health and long term wellness.
