How Dental Implants in Dedham Work

Our dentists have completed the advanced training to place dental implants at Dental Park, so our patients don’t have to seek out a specialist. Our dentists use 3-D Cone Beam technology during a simple surgical procedure, which provides a guided outline for precise and accurate implant placement with optimum results.

Once your Dedham dentist successfully places your implant, it’s time to let it heal. Dental implants are made of biocompatible titanium that fuses and integrates with your bone over a few months so it can provide the same stability as a tooth root. Our dentists will monitor your healing progress over the next few months.

When your implant is fully healed and ready, our dentists will place a custom-designed restoration to restore your smile. They’ll recommend a ceramic porcelain crown for single-tooth restoration. Ceramic porcelain is durable and provides the aesthetics of a natural tooth.

Patients who have multiple teeth to replace can also enjoy the benefits of dental implants . Tooth implants can support prosthetics like dentures or bridges to offer multiple tooth replacements.
