Teeth Whitening

Here at D Amore Russo Dental Design Studio DrDDS of Montclair, NJ we take the time to make sure your results look natural while ensuring your comfort and safety throughout your tooth whitening treatment.

Regardless of how well you care for your teeth, it is inevitable that, over time, some yellowing or discoloration will occur. This gradual change can be caused by aging, substances that stain your teeth (such as coffee and tobacco), excessive fluoride, or the deterioration of a tooth’s nerve.

A tooth whitening treatment can be performed either as an at-home or dentist-supervised process in the dentist’s chair. While neither of these methods stops future discoloration, these tooth whitening treatments bring about whiter teeth and help create a brighter smile.

Bonding

Very simply, cosmetic bonding is the process of applying a top layer of resin to teeth. This resin will act as the new layer of enamel for the patient, protecting the tooth and improving their smile. The process for cosmetic bonding only takes a few steps but they must be done with absolute precision to achieve optimal results.

For the cosmetic bonding of a single tooth, the dentist must first remove a small amount of the top layer of the tooth. This will allow the new resin to attach more permanently to the tooth. After that, the resin is applied to the tooth. Once a good amount of resin is on the tooth, the dentist at D Amore Russo Dental Design Studio Dr. DDS of Montclair NJ office will begin shaping it to match the other teeth. After the bonding matches the patient’s other teeth, the resin will be hardened with a UV light permanently to the tooth.

Hygiene

In many ways, the health of the mouth mirrors the health of the body. Diseases in other parts of the body often cause symptoms we can observe in the mouth; likewise, oral problems (like tooth loss) not only reduce an individual s quality of life, but may also lead to problems in other areas. A major goal of modern dentistry is to help you keep your teeth and gums healthy for a lifetime. By following a conscientious program of oral hygiene, you have the best chance at making this goal a reality.
