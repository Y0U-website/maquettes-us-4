Dr. Michela Russo

Dr. Michela Russo is a general dentist who enjoys practicing all aspects of family and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Russo completed her dental education at The New York University College of Dentistry. Dr. Russo then completed a rigorous postdoctoral specialized training program in dentistry for one year at Englewood Hospital and Medical Center.

Dr. Russo has enjoyed working in dentistry since she was 16, when she started as a dental assistant. She is currently a member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, New Jersey Dental Association and American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Dr. Russo loves to grow her passion with all the latest techniques of modern and cosmetic dentistry by seeking the best continuing education. In her leisure time, Dr. Russo enjoys spending time with her husband and two children and loves listening to Italian music and cooking Italian speciaties.
