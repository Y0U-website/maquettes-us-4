0 Clear Aligners Clear aligners offer an effective method for healthy tooth movement while making it easier to brush and floss, all without the visible metal brackets.

What to Expect After a thorough assessment, Dr. Swift and his team will analyze your smile. We will talk about your goals and what you like or don’t like about your teeth. The examination will include a careful evaluation of your teeth and bite to ensure a personalized approach that prioritizes both your preferences and dental health. Using our Trios intra-oral scanner, we will create an accurate 3D scan of your teeth, allowing us to develop a tailored treatment plan just for you.

A preview of your new smile.

With your scan and goals in mind, we will chart the course for your treatment. You will be shown a 3D simulation of your teeth throughout the process, so you’ll have a clear idea of what your final smile will look like from start to finish.

Transforming your smile from your first tray.

We will guide you in using your trays and may add attachments to assist with tooth movement. You will leave with several trays and have follow-up appointments every four to six weeks as your smile gradually straightens.
