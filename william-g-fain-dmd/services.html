Our Services

Cosmetic Dentistry

Thinking about getting a smile makeover? We offer a wide range of cosmetic dentistry procedures including whitening, BOTOX®, and full-mouth reconstruction. The majority of the Continuing Education courses that Dr. Fain has completed have emphasized cosmetic services, ensuring you are in excellent hands.

Teeth Whitening

Let your smile pop with our personalized whitening services.

Crowns

We provide a variety of options, including porcelain crowns, for patients in need of crown procedures, or restoration of their existing crowns.

Veneers

Get beautiful teeth without the added cost or invasiveness.

General Dentistry

Maintaining your dental health is a key part of maintaining your overall health, and we take this full perspective on health to heart. Our office is equipped with a highly-trained team and advanced equipment to cover all your dental needs, including cleanings, root canals, extractions, and many other procedures.

Exams & Cleanings

Keep your teeth in excellent condition with our comprehensive exams and cleanings.

Fillings

Give teeth that are damaged or suffering from cavities a second lease on life with expert tooth filling services.

TMJ Evaluation & Treatment

Tired of a sore jaw that never stops popping? It might be time to have a TMJ evaluation, and we’ll make sure you get the top-of-the-line treatment you deserve.

Implant Dentistry

A clean and healthy smile is important for everyone’s confidence. If you’re looking to receive dental implants – or a restoration of your existing implants – we’ll handle every step of the process.

Implants Restoration

We offer high-quality placement and restoration for all manner of dental implants.

Full Mouth Restoration

If you’re in need of major restoration, our friendly and experienced staff will walk you through every step of the process so that you can leave feeling like your best self.

Now Offering SPARK™ Clear Aligners

Spark™ Clear Aligners offer teens and adults a discreet, comfortable way to straighten teeth. Each aligner is custom designed to fit your smile and gradually guide teeth into place. Because they’re clear and removable, Spark aligners make it easy to eat, brush, and live confidently throughout treatment.
