Meet Dr. Wieder

Nathan Wieder, D.M.D., M.S.

Dr. Nathan Wieder grew up in the Five Towns, and decided there is no better place to start his practice than in his hometown. He takes tremendous pride in the healthy and beautiful smiles of his patients. Dr. Wieder’s treatment philosophy is to personalize the orthodontic experience for each patient.

At Wieder Orthodontics it is our mission to ensure ease, efficiency and patient comfort every step of the way.
