Orthodontics for Children and Teenagers

  • The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children be evaluated by an Orthodontist by the age of 7.
  • Orthodontists are specially trained to properly evaluate dental and jaw development.
  • Jaw discrepancies can be easily corrected at a young age to ensure proper growth and development.

CLEAR ALIGNERS

Clear Aligner appliances such as Invisalign® is a series of clear and comfortable aligners that progressively straighten your teeth. You can remove the aligners whenever you need to, so you can continue to eat what you want during treatment and remove them for that special event.

LINGUAL BRACES

Lingual braces are placed on the inside of your teeth so they are completely hidden. These braces are made from a digital scan of your teeth and are customized to fit your smile. Unlike clear aligners, lingual braces are a great esthetic option that requires less maintenance and consideration.

CLEAR CERAMIC BRACES

Clear braces are an excellent esthetic alternative to traditional metal braces. These braces are discreet and blend into your teeth while straightening your smile.
