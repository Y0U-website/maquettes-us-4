MEET DR. Weihao Wang

Weihao Wang, D.M.D

Dr. Weihao Wang is a noted root canal specialist delivering high quality Endodontic (root canal) treatment in a warm and kind environment.

He earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine and MS/Certification in Endodontics from the prestigious Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine, where as a dental extern he provided comprehensive care in a community outreach program. He also completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry Residency at NYU Langone Health. As an undergraduate, he achieved Magna Cum Laude honors from SUNY Albany. In addition, he is a published researcher and presenter at prominent dental symposia such as the American Association forDental Research.

Fluent in English and Mandarin Chinese, with intermediate proficiency in Cantonese Chinese, he brings expertise and compassion to our dental office. Patients love his easygoing and engaging attitude. Outside of work, he loves traveling, cooking, and spending time with his wife and two children.
