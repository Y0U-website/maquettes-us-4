The surgical staff at Carolinas Oral & Facial Surgery Center also consists of experienced oral and maxillofacial surgical assistants, who are highly trained to assist in administration of IV sedation and surgery. All medical staff are CPR-certified and continuously monitor patients during and after surgery.

Carolinas Oral & Facial Surgery Center conveniently operates offices in Wilmington, Whiteville and Jacksonville.

Our main office, at 1305 Physicians Drive in Wilmington , is a full-service, state-of-the-art oral surgery center. It is also home to The Dental Implant Center, a division of COFSC wholly dedicated to that procedure.

Conveniently located in these communities, the Scotts Hill facility in Wilmington is located at 9020 Senca Dr. Suite 230 , the Whiteville location is 709 Jefferson Street , and our Jacksonville address is 52 Office Park Dr., Suite A .

We serve Wilmington, as well as all of southeastern North Carolina, making your experience as convenient as possible. We realize that no one looks forward to oral surgery, and we strive to make the process as easy and comfortable as possible.
