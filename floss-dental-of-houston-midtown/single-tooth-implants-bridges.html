Single tooth implants and bridges offer the most effective solution for restoring damaged or missing teeth in a specific area. Key advantages include:
  • Natural appearance
  • Robust and natural chewing functionality
  • Secure and permanent attachment
  • Absence of negative impacts on nearby teeth, as seen with other options like bridges.

How it Works

A dental implant is a titanium screw that acts as the foundational support for replacing a tooth or teeth.

A dental implant comprises three essential components:

  • Implant Post (or Body): Serving as the foundation, this is anchored in the jaw to provide stability.
  • Abutment: This component connects the implant post and the crown.
  • Crown: The visible portion in the mouth, which can also be an implant bridge spanning across multiple implants.
