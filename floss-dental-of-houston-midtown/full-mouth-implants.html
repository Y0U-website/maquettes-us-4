How It Works

This procedure encompasses the extraction of any remaining teeth, the placement of 4-6 implants per arch, and the attachment of a low-profile bridge to these implants. Typically, the implants are strategically positioned at specific angles to avoid the patient’s sinuses, thereby eliminating the necessity for costly bone grafts and protracted healing periods.

Am I a Candidate?

Most patients, even those previously advised of insufficient bone for dental implants, are eligible for this procedure. To navigate around the sinuses and leverage the sturdiest bone, your doctor will utilize a mix of straight and angled implants. This approach generally obviates the need for expensive and time-consuming sinus augmentation bone grafts. In your complimentary consultation, your doctor will conduct a thorough evaluation to ascertain whether the All-On-X procedure, also recognized as Teeth in a Day, is a fitting option for you.
