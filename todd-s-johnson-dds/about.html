Dr. Todd S. Johnson, DDS

Dr. Johnson values continuing dental education. He has been a member of the Tacoma Implant Study Club since 2005, and has been involved with multiple Spear Study clubs. Dr. Johnson has also previously served as an affiliate instructor with the Department of Oral Medicine at the University of Washington School of Dentistry.

Angelle

Angelle has lived in Grays Harbor and is celebrating her 5 year anniversary with our Hygiene Team in 2024. She earned her Associates in Dental Hygiene in Boise, Idaho before completing her Bachelors Degree in Dental Hygiene in Klamath Falls, Oregon. She has worked in dentistry since 1999. Her favorite aspects of being a Hygienist are the patients she meets every day and the opportunity to build relationships with each them.

On a more personal note, Angelle is married with 2 beautiful daughters, 1 dog, 2 cats and a rabbit! When she isn’t in the office she enjoys being outdoors, reading and building her knowledge of health and wellness not only for herself, but also for her patients and family!

Molly

Molly has been with our office since September of 2020, but has worked in dentistry since 2012. Her dental career started when she graduated from Dental Assisting School in Spokane in 2011. Molly’s favorite aspect of her position is watching patients’ transformations and seeing patients’ confidence grow with bigger, brighter smiles after treatment! She also really enjoys having a diverse background where she can hop in the back and help sitting chairside or take x-rays whenever help is needed!

She was born and raised on the Harbor where she met her husband who was a fellow volunteer firefighter, together they have a quickly growing 5-year-old son and a German Shepherd Charli girl. When she isn’t in the office, she can most likely be found outdoors riding quads, camping or at the Grays Harbor Mini Raceway watching her son race quarter-midget go carts! Fun fact about Molly is that prior to working in dental, she spent most of her days out in nature as a Park Ranger.
