About Us

Rooted in Community Focused on You

Sabitoni Dental isn’t just a dental practice. Sabitoni Dental is committed to your oral health, essential to overall health, well-being and quality of life.

At Sabitoni Dental, we merge a deep-rooted passion for dental health with a commitment to personalized care. Dr. Alex Sabitoni, a proud Rhode Island native, leads our practice with the knowledge and experience he gained from his extensive education at Boston University. With his Sabitoni Dental team of dedicated professionals, Dr. Sabitoni brings a wealth of expertise and a gentle touch to every treatment.

Raised locally, Dr. Sabitoni believes in more than just dental solutions; he advocates for patient empowerment through education and transparent communication. Our practice stands on the pillars of trust and excellence, providing a spectrum of services that range from routine check-ups to advanced cosmetic and restorative procedures. We celebrate the unique story of each tooth, each smile, each patient that walks into our office.

In our hands, your oral health is nurtured, your preferences are honored, and your dental care journey is collaborative. After all, we’re not just treating teeth; we’re treating neighbors, friends, and family.

Our Values

Our Work Is Heavily Shaped by Our Core Values

Commitment to Excellence

We take pride in our unwavering commitment to excellence in dentistry. By continually embracing the latest advancements and educational opportunities, we ensure that every aspect of our practice reflects the highest standards of oral care.

Empowerment through Education

Informed patients make empowered decisions. It’s our mission to ensure you understand each step of your dental health journey. We share knowledge so that together, we can make choices that align perfectly with your health needs and goals.

Listening and Understanding

Your voice is integral to our process. We actively listen to understand your concerns, valuing the uniqueness of your experience. With us, your ideas and inquiries are met with an open mind, ensuring a tailored approach to your dental care.

Integrity in Action

We stand by the courage of our convictions to do what’s right for your health. Our expertise guides us, but our integrity defines us. We make decisions with a clear focus on what’s best for you, leading with honesty and ethical practice.
