Maintaining Your Dental Implants

To ensure the longevity of your dental implants, consider:

  • Regular brushing and flossing to maintain oral hygiene.
  • Routine check-ups and cleanings at Sonrise Dental.
  • Avoiding habits that could damage the implant, such as chewing hard objects.
