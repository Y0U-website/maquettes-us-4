Why You Need to Replace Your Missing Teeth

Replacing missing teeth is vital for several reasons, including:

Replacing missing teeth with dental implants can restore oral health, functionality, and confidence.

  • Improved Oral Health: Gaps in your smile can increase the risk of gum disease, tooth decay, and other oral health issues.
  • Enhanced Functionality: Missing teeth can make it difficult to eat certain foods and speak clearly, affecting overall quality of life.
  • Preservation of Jawbone: Without the stimulation provided by tooth roots, the underlying jawbone can deteriorate over time, leading to changes in facial structure and appearance.

What to Expect When Getting a Dental Implant

  • Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly and attending regular dental check-ups and cleanings.
  • Avoid chewing on hard or sticky foods that could damage the implants or restorations.
  • Quit smoking, as tobacco use can increase the risk of implant failure and complications.
  • Initial Consultation: During your first visit, Dr. Rob will evaluate your oral health and discuss your treatment goals and options for dental implants.
  • Treatment Planning: Based on your consultation and diagnostic imaging, Dr. Rob will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs and goals.
  • Implant Placement: The dental implant surgery involves placing the titanium implant posts into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort.
  • Healing Period: After implant placement, a period of healing is required to allow the implants to fuse with the jawbone. This process, known as osseointegration, typically takes several months.
  • Abutment Placement: Once the implants have integrated with the jawbone, abutments are attached to the implants. These connectors serve as the attachment point for the replacement teeth.
  • Final Restoration: Finally, custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures are secured to the abutments, completing the restoration process and providing you with a functional and natural-looking smile. Follow any specific instructions provided by Dr. Rob for post-operative care and maintenance.
