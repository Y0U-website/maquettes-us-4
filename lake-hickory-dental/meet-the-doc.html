My name is Dr. Robert Steljes—please call me Dr. Rob. I am honored to have the opportunity to continue caring for the patients of McAllister Family Dentistry.

Over the next few months, McAllister Dentistry will transition to Lake Hickory Dental, with a focus on a smooth and seamless patient experience. We remain in the same Hickory, NC location, and you’ll continue to see the trusted team you know and love.

We proudly accept Delta Dental Premier, Cigna DPPO, and Blue Cross Blue Shield. Our office also features advanced dental technology, including same-day crowns , 3D printing, and 3D X-rays, to provide precise, efficient, and high-quality dental care .
