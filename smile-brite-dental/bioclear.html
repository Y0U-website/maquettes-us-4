What Is Bioclear and How Can It Help you?

Why People Just Like You Choose Bioclear Over Veneers or Bonding.

Bioclear combines the best of advanced dental technology and aesthetics:

  • Minimally Invasive: No drilling or enamel removal required.
  • Durable Long-Lasting: Outperforms traditional bonding in strength and wear resistance.
  • Cost-Effective: Less expensive than porcelain veneers.
  • Natural Appearance: Smooth, glossy, and lifelike results.
  • Quick Comfortable: Typically completed in a single visit.

Who Is a Candidate for Bioclear?

You may be a great candidate for Bioclear if you want to: Close gaps or black triangles between teeth Repair worn, chipped, or uneven edges Restore discolored or misshapen teeth Improve your smile without invasive procedures Dr. Yasmin will conduct a comprehensive smile assessment to determine if Bioclear is right for you.

How The Bioclear Process Works

  • Consultation: Dr. Yasmin evaluates your smile and explains how Bioclear can enhance your appearance.
  • Preparation: Teeth are gently cleaned and isolated—no drilling needed.
  • Matrix Placement: Clear matrices form the precise shape of your new tooth surface.
  • Composite Injection: Heated composite resin is applied to fill and reshape the tooth.
  • Polishing Finishing: The resin is sculpted and polished for a natural, long-lasting shine.

Bioclear FAQs

How long does Bioclear last?

Is Bioclear painful?

Not at all. The procedure is minimally invasive and typically requires no anesthesia. Patients report little to no discomfort during or after treatment.

Can Bioclear fix black triangles between my teeth?

Yes! One of the most popular uses of Bioclear is closing black triangles, which are small gaps near the gum line that often appear after gum recession or orthodontic treatment.

How does Bioclear differ from traditional bonding?

Traditional bonding uses a layer of composite resin applied directly to the tooth, while Bioclear uses a matrix system and heated composite to form a stronger, smoother, and more uniform surface.

Is Bioclear covered by insurance?

Coverage varies depending on your plan and whether the procedure is considered cosmetic or restorative. Our team at Smile Brite Dental can help check your benefits and discuss financing options.

Can Bioclear be used instead of veneers?

Yes! Many patients choose Bioclear instead of veneers because it’s more affordable, reversible, and preserves natural enamel —while still providing beautiful, long-lasting results.

Disability profiles supported in our website

  • Epilepsy Safe Mode: this profile enables people with epilepsy to use the website safely by eliminating the risk of seizures that result from flashing or blinking animations and risky color combinations.
  • Visually Impaired Mode: this mode adjusts the website for the convenience of users with visual impairments such as Degrading Eyesight, Tunnel Vision, Cataract, Glaucoma, and others.
  • Cognitive Disability Mode: this mode provides different assistive options to help users with cognitive impairments such as Dyslexia, Autism, CVA, and others, to focus on the essential elements of the website more easily.
  • ADHD Friendly Mode: this mode helps users with ADHD and Neurodevelopmental disorders to read, browse, and focus on the main website elements more easily while significantly reducing distractions.
  • Blindness Mode: this mode configures the website to be compatible with screen-readers such as JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack. A screen-reader is software for blind users that is installed on a computer and smartphone, and websites must be compatible with it.
  • Keyboard Navigation Profile (Motor-Impaired): this profile enables motor-impaired persons to operate the website using the keyboard Tab, Shift+Tab, and the Enter keys. Users can also use shortcuts such as “M” (menus), “H” (headings), “F” (forms), “B” (buttons), and “G” (graphics) to jump to specific elements.
