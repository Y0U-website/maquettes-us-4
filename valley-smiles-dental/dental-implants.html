A dental implant is the closest thing modern dentistry has to a natural tooth: a titanium post that fuses with your jaw and holds a custom crown that looks, bites, and lasts like the real thing. At Valley Smiles Dental, implants aren’t something we refer out. They’re what Dr. Kumar trained for.

Why patients choose an implant

  • It stands on its own. Unlike a bridge, an implant doesn’t ask neighboring healthy teeth to carry the load.
  • It protects your jaw. The bone under a missing tooth shrinks over time. An implant keeps that bone working.
  • It doesn’t come out. No adhesives, no soaking, no clicking at dinner.
  • It’s built to last decades. With routine care, implants are among the most durable restorations in dentistry.

What we treat

Single tooth. One implant, one crown. The neighboring teeth are never touched.

Several missing teeth. An implant-supported bridge spans the gap on two anchors instead of a removable partial.

Full arch. Four to six implants carry a full set of fixed teeth.

Loose dentures. Implant-retained overdentures snap securely into place. Same denture, no more slipping.

Failing or broken teeth. Extraction and implant placement planned together, so you’re never left with a visible gap.

Your visit, step by step

  • Consult and 3D scan. We map your bone, nerves, and bite digitally, with no guesswork, and you leave knowing your options and your exact cost.
  • Placement. The implant is placed under local anesthetic. Most patients tell us it was easier than the extraction that preceded it.
  • Healing. Three to six months while the implant fuses to bone. You wear a temporary and you eat normally.
  • Your new tooth. A custom crown, shaped and shaded to match the teeth beside it, is placed and adjusted until the bite feels like nothing happened.

Am I a candidate?

Most adults are. You’re likely a good candidate if you have a missing, cracked, or failing tooth, healthy gums, and enough jawbone to anchor the post. If you don’t, grafting often solves it. Smokers, patients with uncontrolled diabetes, and patients on certain bone medications need a closer look first, which is exactly what the consult is for.

Common questions

Does it hurt? Placement is done under local anesthetic, and most patients describe the recovery as milder than an extraction. Over-the-counter pain relief is usually enough.

How long does the whole process take? Typically three to six months from placement to final crown, because the implant needs time to fuse with bone. The visits themselves are short.

How long do implants last? With good hygiene and regular checkups, implants routinely last decades, often for life. The crown on top may need replacing sooner.

Will insurance cover it? Many PPO plans cover part of an implant case. We verify your benefits before treatment and show you the real out-of-pocket number.

What if I’ve been missing the tooth for years? Still very treatable. Long-standing gaps often lose bone volume, so we may recommend a graft first. The 3D scan tells us in one visit.

Are you taking new patients? Yes, including Denti-Cal and Medi-Cal Dental.
