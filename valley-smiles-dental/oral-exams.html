What Are Oral Exams?

An oral exam is a complete evaluation of your teeth, gums, jaw, bite, and oral tissues. Dr. Kumar carefully examines every aspect of your oral health, using advanced diagnostic tools to identify concerns that may not be visible to the naked eye.

Oral Exams Benefits:

At Valley Smiles Dental, we take a comprehensive approach to your oral health. Dr. Kumar’s attention to detail and use of advanced diagnostic technology ensures nothing is overlooked. We take time to explain our findings and involve you in decisions about your care. Your comfort and understanding are our priorities.

  • Early detection of cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer
  • Identification of bite problems and TMJ issues
  • Assessment of existing dental work
  • Personalized treatment planning
  • Prevention of costly emergency procedures
  • Complete review of your oral health history
  • Review of medical and dental history
  • Visual examination of teeth, gums, and soft tissues
  • Digital X-rays as needed
  • Periodontal (gum) assessment
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Discussion of findings and treatment recommendations
