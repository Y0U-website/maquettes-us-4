Dr. Jai, DMD

“Listen carefully, educate thoroughly, discuss every option, and treat every patient like family.”

With over 30 years of dental experience , Dr. Jai earned his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Boston University . In 2009, he founded Healthy Smiles of Georgia — now marking 17+ years serving local families — with a clear vision: modern, state-of-the-art dental technology paired with gentle, patient-centered care. For over 15 years — from the practice’s longtime home on Rucker Road in Alpharetta to our upgraded Roswell location today — he’s helped generations of North Fulton families achieve and maintain healthy, confident smiles.

Advanced Training & Mastership Distinction

Dr. Jai has completed over 1,000 hours of advanced continuing education across cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry, training at some of the nation’s leading postgraduate institutions — including the Pankey Dental Institute, the Scottsdale Center for Dentistry, the Georgia Academy of General Dentistry, and Implant Seminars.

He is currently pursuing Mastership status in the Academy of General Dentistry (MAGD) — a clinical distinction achieved by fewer than 5 % of general dentists nationwide.
