What to Expect at Your First Visit

Plan for 90 minutes. Here’s what will happen, step by step.

Check In

Arrive, check in at reception, and enjoy a beverage from our bar.

Welcome Tour

We’ll show you around the office, introduce you to the team, and answer any questions before you’re seated.

X-Rays

We’ll take X-rays and intraoral camera photos to establish your baseline. You’ll get results instantly, and we’ll explain everything we see.

Exam

Dr. Serena Patel performs a comprehensive oral health exam — teeth, gums, bite, jaw, and oral cancer screening.

Cleaning

Professional scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar, leaving your smile brighter and healthier.

Review & Discussion

We’ll note any areas needing closer attention, give you personalized home care guidance, and talk through any relevant next steps.

Insurance & Financing
