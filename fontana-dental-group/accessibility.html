Our Commitment to Accessibility

We are working to make our website easier to use for people of all abilities, including individuals who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, keyboard navigation, and voice recognition software.

Our goal is to provide a positive online experience for all visitors seeking information about Fontana Dental Group and our dental services.

Website Accessibility and Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our website and make updates when needed to improve usability, readability, navigation, contrast, image descriptions, page structure, and access to important information.

While we strive to make our website accessible, some content may not yet fully meet every accessibility standard. We appreciate feedback that helps us improve.

Third-Party Tools and Birdeye

Fontana Dental Group may use third-party tools and platforms on our website, including Birdeye, to help with patient communication, reviews, messaging, appointment-related features, and online reputation management.

While we do not control the accessibility of third-party platforms, we encourage all technology providers, including Birdeye, to offer accessible digital experiences. If you experience difficulty using a Birdeye feature, review widget, messaging tool, or other third-party feature on our website, please contact our office directly so we can assist you through another communication method.

Need Help Accessing Information?

If you have difficulty accessing any part of this website, or if you need help with any information, please contact Fontana Dental Group directly.

We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information you need through an accessible communication method, such as phone, email, or another option that works best for you.
