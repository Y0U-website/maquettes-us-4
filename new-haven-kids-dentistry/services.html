Why Choose a Kid’s Dentist ?

Dr. Lyly and her team explain the small benefits and big benefits of visiting New Haven Kids Dentistry.

Children who are still growing and learning how the world works might be fearful of visiting the dentist. We get that, and we have the solution.

At New Haven Kids Dentistry , we’re reinventing the experience for kids and parents alike through fun, personalized care.

The choices we make today determine the future, and your child’s smile is no different. So come grow with us!

  • Unlike a family dentist who sees patients of all ages, we work exclusively with kids and individuals with physical and developmental disabilities.
  • When you step into our exciting dental office , our friendly team caters to your family’s unique needs while providing specialized care.
  • Through our prevention, maintenance, and gentle repairs, we can set the right foundation for your child’s healthy teeth and gums.
  • In addition, early intervention helps detect and treat small problems before they turn into big ones.

Expert Care for Children and Special Needs

See how our kids’ dental services help keep your little one smiling!

Our dentists, Dr. Lyly and Dr. Sara Ysaac-Garcia, teach children and parents about proper brushing and flossing, good dental habits at home, and the importance of visiting the dentist.

We also check for problems before they progress, in turn, making treatments less invasive and costly. Our preventative and hygiene services in New Haven, CT aim to protect your child’s natural smile.

Sedation dentistry for kids and special needs helps our patients undergo treatments comfortably. It’s especially beneficial for nervous patients, those with trouble sitting still, and anyone undergoing a procedure like fillings, crowns, or root canals.
