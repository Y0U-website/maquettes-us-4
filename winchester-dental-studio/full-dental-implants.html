Healing and Recovery and Post-Operative Care

After the placement of full dental implants, the journey to a full recovery begins. We provide a detailed care plan to ensure the best outcomes. Post-Operative Care Tips:

  • Rest : Take it easy for the first 24-48 hours.
  • Diet : Stick to soft foods and avoid hot liquids initially.
  • Hygiene : Gentle oral care to avoid disturbing the surgical site.
