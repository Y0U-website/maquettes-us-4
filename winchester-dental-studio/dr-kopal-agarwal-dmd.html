Dr. Kopal Agarwal, DMD

Dr. Kopal Agarwal earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. Originally from India, she also holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from Meerut University — two full dental qualifications rather than one. She is a general dentist treating both adults and children , and is fluent in English, Hindi and Urdu , so patients who prefer any of the three can be seen without an interpreter. She invests in advanced continuing education and dental study clubs, and practises in Winchester, Virginia.
