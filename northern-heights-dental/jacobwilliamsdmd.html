Dr. Jacob Williams, DMD

Dr. Williams provides comprehensive care ranging from preventive and routine dentistry to advanced full mouth rehabilitation, including dental implants, veneers, crowns, bridges, Invisalign, and dentures. He treats both straightforward and complex cases, allowing patients to receive complete care in one place.

He earned his dental degree from Midwestern University School of Dentistry, where he served as senior class president and received multiple honors, including the Dean’s Service Award, Entrepreneurial Spirit Award, and Lifelong Learner Award.

Following dental school, Dr. Williams pursued advanced training in implant dentistry, full mouth rehabilitation, and facial aesthetics. His continued education includes guided bone regeneration, ridge augmentation, 3D guided surgery, airway analysis, orthodontics, occlusal and bite analysis, TMJ therapy, and CAD CAM restorations.

He is committed to using biocompatible materials and carefully selecting treatment approaches that support long term health, function, and durability.

Dr. Williams believes dentistry should be precise, personalized, and never rushed. He is passionate about providing high quality care in a welcoming and supportive environment.
