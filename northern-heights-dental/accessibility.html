Accessibility Statement

Northern Heights Dental is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities. We strive to provide a user-friendly experience for every visitor, regardless of ability.

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards and are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of our website.

Our team is dedicated to making ongoing improvements, including enhancing readability, navigation, and overall accessibility across all pages.

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on our website, have trouble using any features, or would like to request assistance, please contact our office. We take your feedback seriously and will make every reasonable effort to address your concerns promptly.
