High-Quality Dentistry

Delivering Your Child A Brighter Smile

We all know the dentist’s office isn’t always the most exciting place for children and even adults sometimes. At Pitcher Pediatric Dental in Pittsford, NY, you don’t need to worry about that anymore. We provide a comforting, relaxing, and non-judgmental atmosphere for your children to receive the dental care they need.

Whether your child needs a comprehensive dental examination, routine cleaning, fluoride or sealant protection application, fillings, crowns, nitrous oxide (N 2 O or laughing gas), or oral sedation, we can handle it all and ensure they are at ease. Our hygienists specialize in pediatric dental care and are dual trained in N 2 O administration to relieve patients with heightened anxiety.
