The practice

When you bring your child to Pitcher Pediatric Dental in Pittsford, NY, you’re treating them to high-quality preventative dental care. At our dental office, we take a personalized approach with your child and help them learn valuable oral hygiene habits that will last a lifetime.

In addition, we have the newest, updated dental equipment including one of the lowest radiation dosages for X-rays in the area as tested by certified NYS radiation specialists.

Our office is now conveniently located right next to the Pittsford Post Office. Look for us at the corner of Route 31 and Marsh Road, near 490.

We aim to make all children feel comfortable and have a positive experience without fear when visiting our office.

As a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Pitcher is skilled, extensively trained, and highly trusted by her referring colleagues.

While so many offices are commercialized and owned by larger organizations, we aim to focus on direct patient care so we get to know your family and child’s needs.
