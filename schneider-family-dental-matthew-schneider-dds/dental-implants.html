Understanding Dental Implants

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots designed to support custom restorations after tooth loss. Made from biocompatible titanium, they are placed directly into the jawbone where a natural tooth once existed. A complete restoration generally consists of the implant post, the dental abutment, and a custom crown, bridge, or denture. Once integrated with the surrounding bone, the implant serves as a stable foundation for your new restoration, closely mimicking the function and feel of a natural tooth root.

How Dental Implants Support Your Smile

Traditional dentures and bridges replace only the visible portion of a tooth. Implants support both the visible restoration and the structure beneath the gums. Following placement, the implant undergoes a process known as osseointegration, during which the surrounding bone gradually bonds with the titanium post. This process helps create exceptional stability, support normal biting forces, preserve jawbone density, maintain proper alignment, and improve overall oral function. Once healing is complete, a custom restoration is attached to complete your smile.

Options for Replacing Multiple Teeth

Implants can be used to replace a single tooth, several teeth, or an entire dental arch. A single implant can support an individual crown without affecting neighboring teeth. When several teeth have been lost in the same area, implants can support a bridge while minimizing the need to modify healthy teeth. Patients missing most or all of their teeth may benefit from implant-supported dentures, which provide greater stability, improved chewing performance, less movement, enhanced comfort, and freedom from denture adhesives compared to traditional removable options.

Dental Implant Surgery and Placement

Dental implant surgery is typically performed as an outpatient procedure using local anesthesia. During the procedure, a small incision is made in the gum tissue to access the implant site, and the implant body is carefully positioned within the jawbone. In some cases, a temporary crown may be placed while healing occurs. Once the implant has fully integrated with the surrounding bone, a dental abutment and final crown are attached to complete treatment.

Bone Grafting and Implant Preparation

Some patients may require a bone graft before implant placement. A bone graft helps rebuild areas where bone volume has diminished following tooth loss and can improve support for a future replacement tooth. During your consultation, Dr. Schneider will determine whether grafting is recommended based on your specific anatomy and health history.

Recovery After Dental Implant Surgery

Most patients return to normal activities shortly after surgery. Mild swelling, tenderness, and discomfort are common during the first few days and can often be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and post-operative care instructions. The healing process continues over several months as the surrounding bone bonds with the implant, and following your treatment plan and maintaining excellent oral hygiene help support long-term success.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to provide lasting results and many remain successful for decades when properly maintained. Factors that influence longevity include oral hygiene habits, gum health, overall health, routine professional care, tobacco use, and grinding or clenching habits. While restorations may eventually require maintenance due to normal wear, the implant post itself often remains stable for many years with consistent care at our Dublin, Ohio office.

Implants should be cared for much like natural teeth. Daily maintenance includes brushing twice each day, flossing regularly, attending routine checkups, and receiving professional cleanings at Schneider Family Dental. Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants better than dentures for replacing missing teeth?

For many patients, implants provide greater stability and help preserve jawbone structure by mimicking a natural tooth root, which dentures cannot do. Traditional dentures may still be appropriate depending on your oral health and budget. Dr. Schneider will help you compare both options and recommend the most appropriate solution during a consultation at our Dublin, Ohio office.

How long does the dental implant process take from start to finish?

Most cases take several months from implant placement to final restoration because the jawbone needs time to fully integrate with the titanium post through a process called osseointegration. The exact timeline depends on your oral health, whether bone grafting is needed, and how healing progresses. Dr. Schneider will outline a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Does dental implant surgery hurt?

Most patients find the procedure more comfortable than expected because local anesthesia is used throughout. Some tenderness and mild swelling in the days following surgery are normal and can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Dr. Schneider will provide detailed post-operative instructions to help make your recovery as comfortable as possible.

Can dental implants replace all of my teeth at once?

Yes. Implants can support full-arch restorations and implant-supported dentures for patients who have lost most or all of their natural teeth. These solutions provide a stable, fixed alternative to traditional removable dentures and function much more like natural teeth. Dr. Schneider will evaluate your bone support and overall oral health to determine the most appropriate full-arch option for your situation.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants in Dublin, Ohio?

Most healthy adults with adequate bone support and healthy gums are strong candidates for implant treatment. Medical history, tobacco use, and oral hygiene habits all play a role in determining candidacy, and patients with some bone loss may still qualify after bone grafting. Dr. Schneider evaluates each patient individually at our Dublin, Ohio office and will give you an honest assessment of whether implants are the right solution for your smile.
