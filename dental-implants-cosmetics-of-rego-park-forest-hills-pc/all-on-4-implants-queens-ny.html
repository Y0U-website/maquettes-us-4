Implant Placement and Replacement Teeth

Four implants are positioned according to the patient’s anatomy, bone quality, and planned bite. Eligible patients may receive temporary teeth around the time of surgery, but immediate placement cannot be guaranteed. The final restoration is completed after sufficient healing and implant stability. Patients seeking coordinated implant and general dental treatment can also learn more about visiting a dentist in Queens.

Digital Technology Supporting Full-Arch Planning

Dental Implants & Cosmetics uses digital X-rays, three-dimensional scanning, digital impressions, implant-planning systems, and smile-design technology when appropriate. These tools help the dentists evaluate bone, potential implant locations, bite relationships, and restoration proportions. The dentists are also certified FOTONA® laser providers. Laser technology may be incorporated into suitable gum or soft-tissue procedures when clinically appropriate.

Free Consultations, Insurance, and Financing

Free consultations are available for eligible implant services. Patients should confirm the offer when scheduling because a complete examination and diagnostic imaging may still be required to establish candidacy and finalize treatment. All-on-4 cost depends on whether one or both arches are treated, required extractions or grafting, implant components, temporary teeth, final restoration materials, and case complexity.

The practice accepts many major dental plans and offers CareCredit, Alphaeon Credit, and in-house payment arrangements. Healthfirst Medicaid and MetroPlus are not accepted. Review the available dental insurance and financing options.
