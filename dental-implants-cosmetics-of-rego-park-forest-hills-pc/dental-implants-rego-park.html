Dental Implant Options Near You in Rego Park

Full-Mouth Dental Implants

Patients with widespread tooth loss or several failing teeth may consider full-mouth dental implants . Planning may involve both arches and considers how the final teeth should look, feel, and function together.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implants use a planned group of implants to support a complete row of replacement teeth. An evaluation is necessary to determine whether this approach matches the patient’s anatomy and oral health.

Full-Arch Dental Implants

Patients replacing all teeth in an upper or lower arch can learn about full-arch dental implants . This approach may provide more stability than a conventional removable denture.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures use implants to improve the support and stability of a denture. They may be considered when a traditional denture shifts or makes everyday eating and speaking difficult.
