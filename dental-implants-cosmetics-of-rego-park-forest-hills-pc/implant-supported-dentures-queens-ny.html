Implant-Supported vs. Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and underlying tissue. They do not require implant surgery and may remain appropriate for patients who prefer a non-surgical or more economical tooth-replacement option. Implant-supported dentures require surgical placement of dental implants and adequate healing. Potential advantages for suitable patients may include:

  • Improved stability while eating and speaking
  • Less movement against the gums
  • Reduced reliance on denture adhesive
  • Added support for biting and chewing
  • Removable or fixed designs, depending on the treatment plan
  • A restoration customized for the patient’s mouth and smile

Digital Technology for Implant Denture Planning

Dental Implants & Cosmetics uses digital X-rays, three-dimensional scanning, digital impressions, implant-planning systems, and smile-design technology when appropriate.

These tools help the dentists evaluate bone, plan possible implant locations, study the bite, and coordinate the implants with the proposed restoration. Technology supports—but does not replace a complete clinical examination and individualized treatment planning. The dentists are also certified FOTONA® laser providers. Laser technology may be used for suitable gum or oral soft-tissue procedures when clinically appropriate.
