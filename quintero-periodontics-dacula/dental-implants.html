SINGLE TOOTH IMPLANTS

SINGLE TOOTH IMPLANTS

The single-tooth implant, the most commonly used dental implant, comprises two main parts: the implant or post, generally made of titanium with a screw-like structure, and the dental crown, which acts as the restoration and is usually attached to the individual implant.

To install a single dental implant, the post is securely placed into the gum and jawbone, and as the gum heals, a custom-made dental crown is crafted. This crown is then connected to the post through an abutment. Unlike partial dentures, single-tooth implants offer high durability and stability, eliminating any potential movement or shifting.

IMPLANT SUPPORTED DENTURES

IMPLANT SUPPORTED DENTURES

Implant-supported dentures, also referred to as dentures over implants are prosthetic teeth affixed to dental implants surgically inserted into the jawbone. Designed for individuals with multiple missing teeth, these dentures offer stability, functionality, and a natural look. Suitable candidates generally have adequate jawbone density and good overall oral health.

Implant-supported dentures provide a reliable solution, improving comfort and chewing efficiency in comparison to traditional removable dentures. This option is especially advantageous for those seeking a durable, secure tooth replacement solution to restore both oral function and aesthetics for the long term.

Did you know…

Unlike dentures, which may need to be replaced over time, dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are dental implants made?

At Quintero Periodontics, dental implants consist of a titanium post and a custom-made crown. Dr. Quintero places the post, and then your general dentist or prosthodontist creates impressions for the crown, which a lab crafts from durable materials like porcelain. The crown is then securely attached, completing your dental implant.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last over 30 years with proper care, often remaining for life. However, the restoration (like crowns or overdentures) may need replacement due to wear and tear from chewing and biting. Replacements ensure your smile stays healthy and functional over time.

Can dental implants get infected?

To prevent peri-implantitis, a dental implant infection, it’s crucial to keep your implant clean. Follow Dr. Quintero’s post-op instructions and maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly once your implant has healed. This helps preserve the bone around the implant and ensures its long-term health.

Will dental implants stop bone loss?

After tooth loss, the jaw bone weakens due to a lack of stimulation from chewing. Dental implants, acting as artificial tooth roots, transmit chewing forces into the jaw bone, preventing bone loss and maintaining its strength and health over time.

Can dental implants be done in one day?

Some dental implants can be completed in a single day, with a temporary restoration provided to maintain function during healing. Follow-up appointments with Dr. Quintero and our dentist to track healing progress and take impressions for a custom-made, permanent restoration. This final prosthetic ensures long-term durability and a natural appearance.
