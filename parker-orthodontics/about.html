Meet Dr. Joseph Parker

Dr. Parker loves Ellensburg. Whether it’s seeing bale wagons picking up hay or taking a hike up Manastash Ridge, he knows the values of this community and the importance of being part of it. Dr. Parker supports and sponsors kids in our community through various organizations including 4H animals, school projects, and sport travel teams.

Meet Dr. Camille Walker

Raised in Ellensburg, Dr. Camille Walker began dreaming of a career in orthodontics during her senior year at Ellensburg High School. Dr. Walker joins our practice from Beaverton, Oregon, where she led Walker Orthodontics as the owner and orthodontist for a decade. Prior to building her practice, Dr. Walker served in the United States Navy as an active-duty dentist.

Earning her undergraduate degree from Washington State University and doctorate credentials from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, Dr. Walker trained to become an orthodontist at Montefiore Medical Center in New York City, graduating in 2011. She achieved board certification from the American Board of Orthodontics in 2012. Driven by the values of adventure, impact, and community, Dr. Walker believes that a healthy smile can change the world. With this, her passion is helping her patients achieve their best smile through modern orthodontic care.

Dr. Walker is thrilled to be back in her hometown with her partner, Brad, and their pup. Outside of the office, exploring central Washington’s trails and reacquainting herself with Ellensburg are two of Dr. Walker’s favorite activities. Dr. Walker is eager to meet new patients and community members through chairside conversations and service opportunities.
