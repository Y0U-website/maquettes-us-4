What We Look For

During an early consultation, we assess:

Our team uses digital imaging and a thorough exam to develop a personalized plan if treatment is recommended.

  • Tooth eruption patterns
  • Bite alignment and jaw development
  • Speech or breathing issues that may be related to oral structure
  • Habits like thumb-sucking or tongue thrusting

Types of Early Treatment

Depending on your child’s needs, early intervention may involve:

  • Space maintainers or expanders
  • Limited braces or aligners
  • Habit-breaking appliances
  • Regular growth monitoring to track development

FAQs About Early Intervention

Is age 7 too early for orthodontic care? Not at all. This is the ideal time to spot problems and plan ahead if needed.

Will my child definitely need braces later? Not always. Early treatment can reduce or even prevent the need for braces.

How do I know if my child needs an early checkup? If you notice crowding, crossbite, or prolonged habits like thumb-sucking, it’s a good time to schedule an evaluation.
