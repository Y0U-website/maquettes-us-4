FAQs About Orthodontic Consultations

What happens during my first consultation? You’ll undergo a thorough exam, including digital imaging and bite assessments. Based on this, we’ll suggest the best treatment options.

How long does the consultation take? Typically, a consultation lasts about 30 to 60 minutes.

Is there an age limit for orthodontic consultations? No! Orthodontic care is for all ages. We provide treatment plans for children, teens, and adults.

Will I need braces right away? Not necessarily. The consultation helps us decide if and when treatment is needed.
