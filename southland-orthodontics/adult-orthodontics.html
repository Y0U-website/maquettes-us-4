FAQs About Adult Orthodontics

Am I too old for orthodontic treatment? Not at all! Healthy teeth can be moved at any age with the right plan.

How long will treatment take? Every case is unique, but many adult treatments last between 12–24 months.

Will people be able to tell I’m wearing braces or aligners? Not likely. We offer low-visibility and clear options designed for discretion.

Can orthodontics help with jaw pain or headaches? Yes, in many cases realignment can relieve strain and improve comfort.
