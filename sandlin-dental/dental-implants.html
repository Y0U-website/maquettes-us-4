The Benefits of Dental Implants

Natural Feel and Function Dental implants closely replicate your natural teeth, allowing you to brush, floss, and eat with ease.

Esthetically Pleasing Each implant is tailored to match your existing teeth in shape, size, and color. This personalized approach ensures that your new tooth blends in naturally with your smile.

Preserves Oral Health Made from biocompatible titanium, implants integrate with your jawbone, stimulating it much like natural tooth roots. This unique feature helps prevent bone loss and maintains your facial structure, contributing to long-term oral health.

The Implant Treatment Process Placement Once your personalized treatment plan is confirmed, we&#x27;ll schedule your appointment for the dental implant placement. At this appointment, the implant will be gently placed into its precise, pre-planned location based on a 3D X-Ray and digital scan of your mouth. This process allows us to use a guide during placement so we can ensure complete accuracy. After placement, we thoroughly clean the area and apply sutures to promote healing as needed. This step sets the foundation for your new tooth.

Healing & Osseointegration

Healing begins immediately after implant placement. The incision site typically heals within 1-2 weeks, but the full integration of the implant with your jawbone (osseointegration) takes about 3-6 months. We&#x27;ll schedule regular check-ups during this period to monitor your progress and ensure everything is healing as expected.

Applying the Restoration

The final phase is restoration. We create a custom dental crown designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. This crown is then securely attached to an abutment on your implant. With this step complete, your smile is fully restored!
