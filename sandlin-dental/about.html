Get to Know Dr. Jacob Sandlin

Dr. Sandlin founded Sandlin Dental with a clear vision – to create a place where patients receive not only ideal dental care but also an exceptional experience. He is dedicated to building lasting relationships with each patient and working together to achieve optimal oral health. Dr. Sandlin is passionate about staying current with the latest technology and has focused advanced training in implants, clear aligner therapy, and cosmetic solutions.

A proud native of Northeast Arkansas, Dr. Sandlin was inspired by his own family dentist whose mentorship sparked his calling into dentistry. After attending Arkansas State University, he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis.

Outside the office, Dr. Sandlin treasures time with his sweet and caring wife and four amazing children. He&#x27;s actively involved at Southwest Church and enjoys staying connected with his extended family and community.
