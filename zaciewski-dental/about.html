Proud members of the ADA & ODA

Zaciewski Dental is proud to be a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Ohio Dental Association (ODA). These organizations are the leading authorities on oral health in the United States and Ohio, respectively.

Membership in these organizations demonstrates our commitment to providing our patients with the highest quality of dental care. We are always up-to-date on the latest advances in dental technology and techniques. We also adhere to the highest ethical standards in our practice.
