Meet the Doctor

Dr. Nabil Ibrahim

Dr. Nabil Ibrahim has 40 years of clinical experience with an emphasis in the field of cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

He received his BDS degree from Alexandria University, School of Dentistry, Egypt in 1984 , then practiced dentistry in Rome, Italy for 10 years. While working in Italy he also attended the University of La Sapienza in Rome , and the Centro Studi Endodontici in Pistoia where he obtained his certificate in Endodontics. In 1995 he attended the Advanced Standing Program at Case Western Reserve University (CWRU) in Ohio where he received his DDS in 1997. He was appointed Visiting Senior Instructor, a fulltime faculty position at CWRU Dental School where he also was involved in Implant Research.

Dr. Ibrahim has been practicing dentistry in the Boston area since July 1999 with offices both in Lexington and Winchester, MA. As of June 2018 he only sees patients at his dental practice in Winchester, MA.

He is a member of the ADA , AGD and MDS . He is passionate about dentistry and enjoys working with his patients to help them achieve optimal oral health.

He is fluent in French, Italian and Arabic. He enjoys tennis, soccer and taking long walks in the woods with his beagle Lupa.

He cherishes time spent with his two grown-up children
