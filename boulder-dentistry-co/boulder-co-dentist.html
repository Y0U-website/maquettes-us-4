Meet Dr. Katie Johnson

Delivers compassionate, evidence-based dentistry with a focus on collaboration, comfort, and lifelong oral health and overall health.

Dr. Katie Johnson is a proud Colorado native, and knew from the age of eight that she wanted to become a dentist. She pursued that dream by earning her Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Denver before receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Southern California in 2008. Following dental school, she completed an additional year of advanced training through an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Idaho State University and then returned to practice in Colorado.

Committed to lifelong learning and advancing the profession, Dr. Johnson currently serves as visiting faculty for Spear Education in Scottsdale, Arizona, where she helps educate fellow dental professionals. She also lectures nationally on airway-aware dentistry and its role in overall health and patient care.

Dr. Johnson believes that continuing education is essential to providing the highest quality dentistry. She actively participates in and leads study clubs, maintains membership in numerous professional organizations, and has completed well over 1000 hours of advanced continuing education. Her extensive training has allowed her to develop particular expertise in the treatment of temporomandibular joint disorders (TMJD), airway-related conditions, implant restorations, and complex restorative and esthetic dentistry.

Her philosophy of care centers on building trust and creating a comfortable, supportive experience for every patient. Dr. Johnson believes the best outcomes come from a collaborative partnership in which doctor and patient work together to develop an individualized treatment plan that aligns with each person’s needs and goals. She has a special interest in integrative dentistry, taking a whole-body approach that considers how systemic health and function can influence oral health and more complex dental conditions.
