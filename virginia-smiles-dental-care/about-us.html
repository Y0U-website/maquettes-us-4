Show More Dr. Qureshi is passionate about providing excellent dental care to his patients. “My philosophy of care is based on a simple principle,” said Dr. Qureshi. “I believe in treating the person, not just his or her teeth.” Dr. Qureshi believes it is vital for his patients to know that their dentist cares not only about their teeth but holistic health to make sure they stay in good health, be comfortable, and get the best possible smile for life.

Dr. Qureshi is from Manassas, Virginia. He attended New York University College of Dentistry and earned his DDS. He then moved to Texas to practice as an associate dentist, Dr. Qureshi decided to move back to the Ashburn area and opened Virginia Smiles Dental Care.

Dr. Qureshi actively pursues continuing education in endodontics, cosmetic dentistry, periodontics and restorative dentistry to bring the latest technological advances to his patients.

He believes in giving back to his community and volunteers for the Mission of Mercy in Northern Virginia. He has participated in events for the Oral Cancer Society and provided dental care to underprivileged children.

Married with two children, Dr. Qureshi and his wife enjoy biking, hiking, and other outdoor activities.
