Dr. Christine Hammer

A Duluth native, Dr. Hammer is extremely proud to be able to create beautiful smiles in her hometown community.

She attended Holy Rosary and Marshall School here in Duluth, is a graduate of the University of St. Thomas in St. Paul, and completed both her dental school education and orthodontic residency at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. Dr. Hammer has practiced orthodontics in Duluth since graduating residency in 2015. Meet the True North Orthodontics Team

At True North Orthodontics, we pride ourselves on our dedicated team of orthodontic professionals who are committed to providing exceptional patient care. Each member brings unique skills and experiences, ensuring that every visit is both comfortable and effective for our patients.

Our team includes experienced orthodontists, hygienists, and support staff who work collaboratively to create personalized treatment plans. We believe in fostering a welcoming environment where patients feel valued and understood, and our team is always ready to answer questions and provide support throughout the orthodontic journey.

Our Commitment to Patient Care

True North Orthodontics is dedicated to delivering the highest standard of patient care. Our approach focuses on understanding each patient’s individual needs and ensuring that they receive tailored treatment that aligns with their goals for a healthy smile.

We utilize the latest technology and techniques in orthodontics to enhance patient comfort and treatment outcomes. Our commitment extends beyond just orthodontic care; we strive to build lasting relationships with our patients and their families, fostering a community of support and trust.

Services Offered at True North Orthodontics

True North Orthodontics offers a comprehensive range of services to meet the diverse needs of our patients. From traditional braces to advanced clear aligners, we provide options that cater to various preferences and lifestyles.

In addition to standard orthodontic treatments, we also specialize in early intervention for children and orthodontic care for adults. Our goal is to ensure that everyone, regardless of age, can achieve a beautiful and functional smile through our expert services.

Why Choose True North Orthodontics?

Choosing the right orthodontic practice is crucial for your oral health and overall satisfaction. At True North Orthodontics, we distinguish ourselves through our patient-centered approach and our commitment to excellence in every aspect of our practice.

Our experienced team, combined with a warm and inviting atmosphere, ensures that you feel comfortable and supported throughout your treatment. We also prioritize education, helping our patients understand their treatment options and the benefits of maintaining their orthodontic care.
