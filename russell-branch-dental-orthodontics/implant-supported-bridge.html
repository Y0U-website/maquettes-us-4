What is an Implant-Supported Bridge?

An implant-supported bridge is a dental restoration device that we can use to replace multiple missing teeth. Unlike traditional bridges that rely on adjacent teeth for support, we anchor this bridge onto dental implants. These implants act as sturdy roots for the bridge, providing a durable foundation and preventing the bone loss that often occurs with tooth loss.

What is the Process?

The journey begins with a meticulous assessment, ensuring a harmonious fusion between technology and biology. We gently place the titanium anchors within the jaw, creating a steadfast foundation. We then allow some time for your healing.

Once your jawbone has healed and fused with the implants, we set each bridge with exacting care, promising a future graced with confident smiles and unspoken resilience. Embrace this transformative step, and watch as your life’s narrative is punctuated with the strength and beauty of a perfectly restored smile.

The Top Advantages of Implant-Supported Bridges

You may wonder why you should consider an implant-supported bridge. Here are several compelling reasons:

Preserving Oral Health

Placing implants helps preserve and stimulate natural bone, fostering bone growth and preventing bone loss. This is integral to maintaining oral health, especially after tooth loss.

Durability and Comfort

Implant-supported bridges offer unparalleled comfort and durability compared to traditional bridges or dentures. We customize them to fit your mouth perfectly without slipping or causing damage to the surrounding healthy teeth.

Aesthetics

Implant-supported bridges look and feel like natural teeth; they blend seamlessly with your smile because they are custom-made for you. They also restore your natural bite, making it easier to chew and speak.

Longevity

When properly cared for with good oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, implant-supported bridges can last a lifetime. This is far longer than conventional restorations that may need to be replaced every 5 to 15 years.

Convenience

You care for your implant-supported bridge like your natural teeth with daily brushing and flossing. No need for the messy adhesives required for dentures or the special cleaning tools for traditional bridges.

Considering Your Options

Every patient’s needs are unique. During your consultation, we’ll discuss the visual and functional aspects of implant-supported bridges and their impact on your overall dental health. Your lifestyle, budget, and specific dental needs will all play a part in determining if this is the right choice for you.
