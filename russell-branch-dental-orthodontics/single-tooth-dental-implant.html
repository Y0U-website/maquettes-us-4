What is the Placement Procedure?

Here’s what you can expect when getting a single-tooth dental implant at our dental office.

Initial Consultation and Treatment Plan:

Before the implant procedure, Dr. Mekouar will sit down with you to discuss your options and create a plan for your treatment. During this appointment, we’ll assess the health of your teeth and your overall oral health to determine if dental implants are the right choice for you. We’ll also discuss the various types of implants, how they differ, and which option might best suit your needs.

Implant Placement:

Healing and Integration:

After we have placed the dental implant, your body will naturally begin to heal around the implant. This process, known as osseointegration, occurs when your jawbone fuses with the implant, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth. Depending on the individual, this process typically takes anywhere from a few weeks to a few months.

Placement of the Abutment and Prosthetic:

Once your implant has securely integrated with your jawbone, we’ll schedule a follow-up appointment to place the abutment and crown, completing the dental implant process. During this appointment, we’ll put the abutment as a connector between the implant and the crown. Finally, we’ll place the crown or another prosthetic, custom-made to match the shape and color of your surrounding teeth, leaving you with a beautifully restored smile.

Aftercare and Maintenance:

Maintaining proper oral hygiene is essential for the longevity and health of your dental implant. Regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings are crucial to preventing gum disease and other oral health issues. Our team will provide detailed instructions on properly caring for your dental implant and ensuring a healthy, long-lasting smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s Included In The $3495 Price?

The entire implant process is included in the price consultation, x-rays, 3D-modeling, installation of your implant, abutment, and crown, etc. The only thing that is not included is a bone graft which some patients need before they can receive an implant.

Is Total Amount Due Upfront?

No. Half of the total price is due upfront, 25 % is due four week later, and 25 % is due two weeks after that. We also offer financing options.

Does The Whole Process Happen On The Same Day?

No. The implant will be installed first. Around four weeks later, once it has fused to the bone, you will be fitted for an abutment and crown which will be delivered and installed two weeks later.

How Invasive Is The Surgery? What’s The Recovery Time?

Thanks to advanced digital placement and our guided, no-incision procedure, the process is minimally invasive. You’ll experience 90 % less bleeding, significantly reduced pain, and no need for sutures, ensuring a smoother and faster recovery.

Will I Need To Be Put Under For The Surgery?

You will have the option to undergo the surgery using local anasthetic if you prefer.

Can Dental Implants Fail/Be Unsuccessful?

Yes, it is possible for dental implants to fail. However, the success rate is over 95 %.

Is A Bone Graft Necessary Before The Implant Placement?

Some patients do require a bone graft before their implant can be installed. Your doctor will let you know if this applies to you.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution, and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. The implant itself integrates with your jawbone, creating a durable and permanent foundation for your replacement tooth. Maintaining good oral hygiene and attending regular dental check-ups are key to ensuring the longevity of your dental implant.
