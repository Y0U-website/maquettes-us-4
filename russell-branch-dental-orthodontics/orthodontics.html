What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on correcting dental and facial irregularities, particularly tooth and jaw alignment issues. At Russell Branch Dental & Orthodontics, we understand that a straight smile enhances your appearance and improves your oral health and overall well-being. With our expertise in orthodontics, we can help you achieve a properly aligned smile that looks great and functions optimally.

What Does Orthodontics Help Correct?

Orthodontic treatment can address a wide range of dental and facial irregularities. It can effectively correct crooked or crowded teeth, gaps and spaces between teeth, overbites, underbites, crossbites, open bites, and protruding teeth. By aligning your teeth and jaws, orthodontics improves your smile’s appearance and enhances your oral health and overall functionality.

Metal Braces:

Metal braces have been a reliable and time-tested solution for orthodontic treatment. Composed of high-quality stainless steel brackets and archwires, these braces gently apply controlled pressure to guide your teeth into their ideal positions over time. Metal braces are highly effective for correcting orthodontic issues, including overcrowding, gaps, crooked teeth, and bite misalignments. With their durability and affordability, metal braces are a popular choice among patients of all ages.

Ceramic Braces:

Ceramic braces may be the perfect fit if you’re seeking a more discreet orthodontic option. These braces function similarly to metal braces but use tooth-colored or clear ceramic brackets that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. This seamless blend makes them less noticeable and more aesthetically pleasing. Ceramic braces offer an excellent combination of effective treatment and a subtle appearance, making them an ideal choice for patients who desire a discreet orthodontic solution.

Invisalign:

Invisalign has revolutionized orthodontic treatment with its virtually invisible, removable aligners. These custom-made aligners are crafted from smooth, BPA-free plastic and designed to shift your teeth into their desired positions gradually. Invisalign aligners are not only discreet but also comfortable to wear. They can be easily removed for eating, brushing, and flossing, allowing you to maintain excellent oral hygiene throughout your treatment. Invisalign is an excellent option for individuals seeking a more flexible and convenient orthodontic experience. Learn more about Invisalign.

Retainers:

After completing your orthodontic treatment, it is crucial to maintain your beautiful new smile. Retainers play a vital role in keeping your teeth in their corrected positions. These custom-made oral appliances are typically worn at night and help prevent teeth from returning to their previous misaligned positions. Our team at Russell Branch Dental & Orthodontics will provide you with personalized retainer instructions to ensure the long-term success of your orthodontic treatment.
