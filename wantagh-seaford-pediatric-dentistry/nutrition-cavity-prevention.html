Foods and Drinks to Limit

Some foods increase the risk of cavities when consumed frequently. These include:

Limiting these items and encouraging healthier alternatives can significantly reduce cavity risk.

  • Sugary snacks and desserts
  • Sticky foods like gummies, fruit snacks, and caramel
  • Juice, soda, and sports drinks
  • Frequent snacking between meals

Building Healthy Habits for a Lifetime

In addition to good nutrition, cavity prevention includes daily brushing and flossing, routine dental checkups, fluoride treatments when recommended, and dental sealants when appropriate. Together, these steps help protect your child’s teeth as they grow.
