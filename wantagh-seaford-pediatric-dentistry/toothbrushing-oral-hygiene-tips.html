Toothbrushing & Oral Hygiene Tips

According to the American Academy of Pediatrics, children should see a dentist by their first birthday or when their first tooth comes in—whichever happens first. Early dental visits at WSPD Kids help lay the foundation for a lifetime of healthy habits and allow us to track your child’s oral development right from the start. While daily brushing and flossing are essential, regular professional cleanings and exams are just as important to remove plaque buildup and prevent cavities—even with excellent home care.

During a typical check-up, we’ll gently polish your child’s teeth using kid-friendly toothpaste flavors, all in a fun and relaxed environment. They can even watch a favorite movie while we work! Our team will also discuss your child’s diet, demonstrate effective brushing and flossing techniques, and provide personalized tips to support great oral hygiene at home.
