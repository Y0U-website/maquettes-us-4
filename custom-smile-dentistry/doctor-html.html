Autobiography

Growing up in the Bay area, Dr. Daniel Nguyen attended San Jose State University for his undergraduate studies and graduated from the University of Southern California School of Dentistry in 2000 where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and distinguishably graduated top ten of his class with Omicron Kappa Upsilon honors. Dr. Nguyen resides in Chino Hills with his wife and his sons, Kenneth and Steven. When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Nguyen’s hobbies include swimming and photography.
