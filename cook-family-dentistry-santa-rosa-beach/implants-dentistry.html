Implant Dentistry Done in One Office

Planning, placement, and restoration under one roof on the 30A

Dental implants are the closest thing modern dentistry has to a natural tooth. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone, integrates with your bone, and supports a custom crown, bridge, or full arch of teeth. The result looks, feels, and functions like your own tooth, and with good care it can last decades.

At Cook Family Dentistry, we plan, place, and restore implants in the same office. Dr. Cook is a graduate of the Comprehensive Implant Residency Program with advanced training in grafting and full-arch reconstruction. Our 3D CBCT cone-beam scanner and Primescan digital impressions allow us to plan each implant precisely, so placement is predictable and recovery is straightforward. We replace single teeth, multiple teeth, and full arches with All-on-4 and All-on-X solutions, all from one location in Santa Rosa Beach.
