Recognizing When It’s Time for a Dental Cleaning

Regular dental cleanings are crucial for maintaining optimal oral health. Torrey Pines Dental Arts recommends biannual visits for most patients. Here are some signs that indicate it’s time for a professional cleaning:

If you’re experiencing any of these symptoms, don’t hesitate to contact Torrey Pines Dental Arts. We offer same-day appointments for urgent dental needs.

  • Visible plaque or tartar buildup
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Bleeding gums when brushing or flossing
  • Discoloration or staining of teeth
  • It’s been over six months since your last cleaning
