Recognizing the Need for Periodontal Treatment

Identifying periodontal issues early is crucial for maintaining optimal oral health. Torrey Pines Dental Arts encourages patients to be aware of these common signs that may indicate the need for periodontal care:

If you’re experiencing any of these symptoms, we encourage you to reach out to our team for a thorough evaluation and personalized treatment plan.

  • Bleeding gums during brushing or flossing
  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Persistent bad breath despite good oral hygiene
  • Visible recession of the gum line
  • Teeth appearing longer due to gum recession
  • Noticeable spaces developing between teeth
