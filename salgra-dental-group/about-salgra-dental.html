Welcome to

Salgra Dental Group !

In 1999 Dr. Claudia Salas and Dr. Tomasz Grabczak founded Salgra Dental Group in the great city of Pembroke Pines Florida,

after graduating from New York University school of dentistry in 1995.

"At Salgra Dental Group we take great satisfaction in helping you maintain optimal oral health. Our dental practice is devoted to comprehensive and preventive dental care."

We believe our patients should have as much information as possible in order to make important decisions regarding their oral health and treatment options.

You are our most important asset, and we strive to develop long-lasting, trusting relationships with all of our patients.

You can just call or request and appointment in our site.

Our goal is to help you achieve optimal oral health, using state of the art restorative , cosmetic and preventive dentistry combined with the best customer care.

Mission and Purpose

As your dentists our mission is to provide cutting edge excellent

multi-disciplinary family and cosmetic dental care to our South Florida patients , giving them the peace of mind of knowing that their oral health is in excellent hands.

Our Purpose is to eradicate dental disease in all of our patients, to educate them on prevention and homecare resulting in dentally aware patients those who maintain excellent dental health.
