Multiple Implants (Bridge Support)

Purpose

Two or more implants can anchor a bridge, replacing several missing teeth in a row.

Benefits

Stronger than traditional bridges, no need to file down healthy teeth, prevents shifting and bone loss.

Ideal for

Implant-Supported Dentures

Purpose

Several implants are placed to anchor a full denture, making it stable and secure.

Benefits

No slipping, no adhesives, better chewing, more confidence. Helps preserve jawbone compared to traditional dentures.

Options

Removable overdentures

Fixed implant dentures (“All-on-4” or “All-on-6”)

Permanently attached for maximum stability.

Why Choose Implants?

  • Long-term durability and function
  • Prevents bone loss and facial sagging
  • Natural appearance and improved confidence
  • Restores ability to chew and enjoy favorite foods
