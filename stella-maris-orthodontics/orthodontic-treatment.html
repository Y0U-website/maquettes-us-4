Orthodontic Treatments

Orthodontics for All Ages Every smile has a story, and every stage of life deserves thoughtful care.

At Stella Maris Orthodontics, Dr. Whitney provides personalized orthodontic treatment for kids, teens, and adults throughout Willis and the Lake Conroe area.

Early Orthodontic Treatment Age 7 is the ideal time for your child’s first orthodontic check-up, but that doesn’t mean we’ll recommend that treatment start right away.

Early orthodontic care is about thoughtful guidance, not rushing into treatment. Dr. Whitney monitors growth and development and recommends early interceptive care only when it’s truly needed.

When appropriate, Phase I treatment, including Phase I clear aligners, can gently guide growing smiles and support healthy development.

Teen Orthodontics

If orthodontic care is recommended, treating during the teen years allows us to work with the natural growth of the smile, jaws, and facial bones for more effective, efficient results.

Dr. Whitney creates personalized treatment plans for teens using proven treatments designed to support healthy development and growing confidence.

We offer teen-friendly treatments, including custom braces, clear braces, and clear aligners, so your teen can choose an approach that fits their style.

Adult Orthodontics Orthodontic care can be life-changing for anyone who’s ready to experience a healthy, confident smile at age 27 or 70.

Dr. Whitney designs comprehensive treatment plans using esthetic options like clear aligners and custom KLOwen™ braces, carefully selected to support natural-looking results and long-term stability.

This is orthodontic care for adults who want meaningful change, guided with experience and attention to detail.

Types of Braces

Every smile is different, which is why Dr. Whitney offers a carefully selected range of braces options.

From custom KLOwen™ braces to clear braces, metal braces, and clear aligners, we’ll help you choose the treatment that best fits your needs, lifestyle, and long-term goals.

Retainers Once you achieve your new smile, you’ll want to keep it in its best condition for life. A beautiful smile is worth protecting, and wearing your retainers consistently is how you ensure that.

After braces or clear aligner treatment, you’ll receive a set of custom retainers that keep your smile beautifully aligned long after treatment ends.

Dentofacial Orthopedics

Dentofacial orthopedics is about leveraging orthodontic treatment to guide facial growth while the bones are still developing.

This approach may include the use of customized appliances to gently create space, support balanced jaw development, and guide everything into healthy alignment, while working with your child’s natural growth process.

Thoughtful early guidance can make future treatment simpler and create a healthy, harmonious appearance.

Orthodontic Appliances

For smiles that need a little extra guidance, Dr. Whitney may recommend an orthodontic appliance as part of your treatment plan.

These appliances help guide growth and create the space needed for healthy alignment.

Many of our appliances are custom-designed and created in-house using advanced 3D printing technology, which means we can create a precise fit without the need for extra fitting appointments.

Surgical Orthodontics

In some cases, once jaw growth is complete, orthodontic treatment alone may not fully address the underlying concern.

When jaw surgery is recommended, Dr. Whitney partners with trusted surgeons to carefully coordinate care and guide you through the process with clarity and support.

Jaw surgery is never a first-line approach for us. Our focus is on early, interceptive care whenever possible to help guide growth and reduce the need for more invasive treatment later.
