The practice

We are a privately owned Dental Practice in Broad Ripple. We believe in providing the highest quality of dental care to patients of all ages. We strive to stay on the cutting edge of dental technology by offering digital diagnostic tools that can simplify the examination and diagnostic process.

We feel there s only one way to practice dentistry: with concern, clear communication and clinical excellence.

Our responsibility is to help you plan for a future of good dental health and to be your forever dental home. If you have a concern or need additional information, please ask...we want to keep you smiling.

Dr. Kasie Nam Dr. Kasie Nam is dedicated to providing quality dental care to patients of all ages in a welcoming, family-friendly environment. She graduated from Indiana University Bloomington with a degree in biology in 1998, later earning her DDS from Indiana University School of Dentistry in 2002.

She advanced her expertise by completing an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program through Lutheran Medical Center in Arizona in 2003. Throughout her career, Dr. Nam has practiced in Arizona and Georgia before returning to Indianapolis to be near family.

Her commitment to community service includes volunteering at a children’s dental clinic, supporting the Special Olympics, and participating in a dental mission to Guatemala.

Dr. Nam now lives in Carmel with her family including a 10-year-old dog that still has the energy of a puppy. In her free time, she enjoys running, yoga, golfing, reading, skiing, and cherishing moments with her daughter, who is growing up way too quickly.
