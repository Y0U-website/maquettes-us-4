At Tilley Family Dentistry, we believe everyone deserves a dental home where they feel comfortable, heard, and genuinely cared for. Our practice was built on the belief that dentistry should be personal, transparent, and focused on what is truly best for the patient.

Dr. Cole Tilley is committed to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. He believes in building long-term relationships with patients and takes the time to understand their needs, explain treatment options clearly, and deliver care with a gentle touch.

Dr. Tilley’s dedication to continuing education and modern techniques allows him to provide dependable, efficient care while keeping patient comfort a top priority.

Many patients struggle to find a dental office that takes the time to listen and provides treatment without pressure. At Tilley Family Dentistry, we do things differently.

We proudly offer a full range of general and family dentistry services to meet the needs of patients at every stage of life.
