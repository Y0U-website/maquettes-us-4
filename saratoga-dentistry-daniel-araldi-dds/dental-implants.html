Types of Dental Implants Offered at Saratoga Dentistry

We offer several types of dental implant solutions to meet each patient’s unique needs:

Single-Tooth Implants

Perfect for replacing a single missing tooth, this option involves placing one dental implant in the jawbone and capping it with a custom-made crown. It’s the most natural and long-lasting way to replace a missing tooth—without compromising neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you’re missing several adjacent teeth, an implant-supported bridge may be the best solution. Instead of relying on natural teeth for support (as with traditional bridges), this option anchors the bridge to implants, offering greater strength and preserving healthy tooth structure.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Unlike traditional dentures, which can slip or require adhesive, implant-retained dentures snap onto strategically placed implants. They offer better retention, improved chewing power, and greater comfort. These dentures are removable for cleaning but far more secure than conventional options.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

The All-on-4 treatment concept is a revolutionary solution for patients missing all teeth in the upper or lower arch. This method uses just four strategically placed dental implants to support a full arch of prosthetic teeth, often placed on the same day.

This approach is ideal for patients who want the stability of implants without the cost or surgery associated with a full set of individual implants.

  • Immediate restoration of full smile function
  • Less bone grafting required compared to full implants
  • More cost-effective than individual implants for each missing tooth
  • Highly secure, natural-looking, and long-lasting

What If You Don’t Have Enough Bone?

Not all patients naturally have enough bone to support implants, especially if tooth loss has been untreated for years. In such cases, bone grafting can rebuild and strengthen the jawbone, creating a stable foundation for implant placement.

Dr. Araldi will assess your bone structure using advanced imaging technology and determine the best course of action for successful implant treatment.
