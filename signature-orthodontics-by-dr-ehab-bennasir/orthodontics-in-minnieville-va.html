About Minnieville

Minnieville, Virginia, is a suburban community in Prince William County with various amenities and attractions. Here’s a concise guide to what the area has to offer:

Parks and Recreation

Andrew Leitch Park : Features hiking trails, picnic areas, sports fields, and Waterworks Waterpark with slides and a lazy river.

Cloverdale Park : Offers sports fields, basketball courts, and playgrounds, ideal for families and local sports leagues.

Minnieville Forest Community Park : A natural retreat with walking trails and picnic areas.

Neabsco Regional Park : Nearby park with scenic views, a boardwalk through wetlands, bird watching, and fishing.

Attractions

Potomac Mills : One of Virginia’s largest outlet malls with numerous stores, dining options, and a movie theater.

Hylton Memorial Chapel : Event center hosting concerts, conferences, and weddings.

Historic Dumfries : Offers historical tours and programs at the Weems-Botts Museum.

Restaurants

The Electric Palm : Waterfront dining with seafood and American cuisine along the Occoquan River.

Guapo’s Restaurant : Authentic Mexican cuisine with a variety of dishes and beverages.

Astoria Pizza : Casual dining with pizzas, subs, and pasta dishes.

Siam Bistro : Traditional Thai dishes, including curries and stir-fries.

Community Services

Dale City Volunteer Fire Department : Provides fire and emergency medical services.

Potomac Community Library : Offers educational and recreational programs for all ages.

Minnieville Community Center : Includes a fitness center, meeting rooms, and community activity spaces.

Education

Prince William County Public Schools : Serves Minnieville with several elementary, middle, and high schools offering diverse programs.

Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA) – Woodbridge Campus : Provides associate degree and certificate programs.

Transportation

I-95 Access : Convenient for commuters, providing access to Washington, D.C., and other major cities.

OmniRide : Local public transportation system with bus services connecting Minnieville to nearby areas.

Minnieville, Virginia, offers a suburban lifestyle with access to parks, attractions, restaurants, and community services. Its convenient location and variety of amenities make it an appealing place to live or visit.
