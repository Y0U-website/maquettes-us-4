About Lake Ridge, VA

Lake Ridge, Virginia , is a suburban community in Prince William County , known for its family-friendly atmosphere and variety of amenities. Here’s a concise guide to what Lake Ridge has to offer:

Parks and Recreation

Lake Ridge Park and Marina : Features a golf course, mini-golf, boat rentals, fishing, and picnic areas. The marina is a popular spot for kayaking and paddle boating.

Occoquan Reservoir : Provides opportunities for fishing, boating, and scenic walks along the waterfront.

Ridgewood Center Park : Offers sports fields, playgrounds, and open green spaces for various recreational activities.

Lake Ridge Golf Course : A 9-hole course that caters to both beginners and experienced golfers, with beautiful scenery and a relaxed atmosphere.

Attractions

Occoquan Historic District : A nearby district with historic buildings, shops, and restaurants. It’s a popular spot for walking tours and exploring the local history.

Potomac Mills : A large outlet mall offering a wide range of stores, dining options, and a movie theater, just a short drive from Lake Ridge.

Rippon Lodge Historic Site : One of the oldest known homes in Prince William County, offering tours and special events.

Restaurants

Dixie Bones BBQ : Known for its delicious barbecue, offering a variety of smoked meats and classic Southern sides.

Bertucci’s Italian Restaurant : A family-friendly spot serving pizza, pasta, and other Italian dishes.

Zandra’s Taqueria : Offers a variety of tacos and Mexican dishes in a casual dining environment.

Charbroil Grill Brazilian Steakhouse : Features a selection of grilled meats and Brazilian cuisine.

Community Services

Lake Ridge Library : Part of the Prince William Public Library System, offering a range of resources and programs for all ages.

Chinn Aquatics & Fitness Center : Includes a swimming pool, fitness center, and various fitness classes, providing a hub for physical activity and wellness.

Lake Ridge Association : Manages community amenities such as pools, tennis courts, and community centers, fostering a sense of community and providing recreational opportunities.

Education

Prince William County Public Schools : Serves Lake Ridge with several elementary, middle, and high schools known for their diverse programs and extracurricular activities.

Northern Virginia Community College (NOVA) – Woodbridge Campus : Located nearby, offering a variety of associate degree and certificate programs.

Transportation

I-95 Access : Conveniently located for commuters, providing easy access to Washington, D.C., and other major cities.

OmniRide : Local public transportation system offering bus services that connect Lake Ridge to surrounding areas.

Yearly Events

Lake Ridge Fall Festival : An annual event with crafts, food vendors, live music, and activities for children, celebrating the autumn season.

Fourth of July Parade and Fireworks : A community event featuring a parade with local organizations and a fireworks display in the evening.

Summer Concert Series : Held at the Lake Ridge Marina, these concerts provide entertainment for residents and visitors throughout the summer months.

Lake Ridge, Virginia , offers a well-rounded suburban lifestyle with access to parks, attractions, restaurants, and community services. Its convenient location, family-friendly environment, and variety of amenities make it an attractive place to live or visit. Whether you’re interested in outdoor activities, shopping, dining, or community events, Lake Ridge has something for everyone.
