Airway Orthodontics for Children and Adults

For Children: Early intervention can guide proper jaw development, promote nasal breathing, and reduce the risk of future sleep-disordered breathing.

For Adults: Adults can benefit from airway-focused orthodontics through improved jaw positioning, expansion, and coordination with sleep specialists.
