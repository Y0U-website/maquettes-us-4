What makes a dental implant feel more like a natural tooth?

Unlike a removable restoration that rests on the gums, an implant is positioned within the jaw and becomes integrated with the surrounding bone. This creates stable support beneath the replacement tooth. Once restored, an implant can provide a secure biting and chewing experience without relying exclusively on neighboring teeth or the gums for stability.

Can dental implants replace several missing teeth without using an implant for every tooth?

Yes. Implant treatment can be planned differently depending on how many teeth are absent and where the gaps occur. Implants may support individual replacement teeth, a bridge, or a denture. Dr. Patel evaluates the entire mouth before recommending the number and placement of implants necessary to provide appropriate support for the planned restoration.

Dental implants are medical-grade titanium screws that are used to replace your missing teeth.

We wait for about 3-4 months after removing your tooth to let your bones and gums heal. Then we place the titanium implants in your jawbone.

Then we wait another 3-6 months, depending on your bone, to put a tooth on it. Implants are the only process in dentistry that can replace a missing tooth closer to your natural teeth.

Once it is fixed, you don’t have to remove it. You take care of it by brushing/flossing just like a normal tooth.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

It does not require grinding or getting support from any other teeth. If you lose any other teeth around it, you will still have your implant sitting solid.

The process has a 95 % success rate and is the highest among all the procedures in dentistry.

So if you are missing single or multiple teeth, consider getting an implant which is the gold standard to replacing missing teeth.

What happens while I am waiting for my implant to bond with my jawbone?

Healing is an important stage of implant treatment because the implant needs time to integrate with the surrounding bone. This typically takes several months. Depending on your individual situation, a provisional denture may be available during this period. Ontario Dental Center can also provide dietary guidance to help protect the treatment area while healing progresses.

Will dental implants help keep my facial appearance from changing after tooth loss?

They can help preserve the underlying jaw structure. Natural tooth roots provide stimulation that helps maintain bone. After a tooth disappears, that stimulation is lost, allowing bone resorption to occur over time. Because an implant is positioned in the jaw, it restores stimulation to the area and helps maintain bone that supports the contours of the face.

Why does my medical history matter when planning dental implant treatment?

Implant dentistry involves both surgical placement and healing, making your overall health an important consideration. During your consultation, Dr. Patel reviews your medical history along with your dental examination and radiographs. Sharing medications, medical conditions, previous procedures, and any concerns helps the dental team plan treatment around your individual circumstances and coordinate additional expertise when appropriate.

How can I decide whether implants are worth considering instead of bridges or dentures?

The right solution depends on your oral health, preferences, anatomy, and willingness to undergo surgery. Bridges and dentures remain valuable choices for many patients. Implants provide the distinctive advantage of replacing the supportive function of a missing root while creating a stable foundation for replacement teeth. A consultation allows you to compare these approaches based on your own priorities rather than choosing a one-size-fits-all solution.
