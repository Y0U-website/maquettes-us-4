What Is Considered Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry is a field within general dentistry that focuses on the aesthetic appeal of your smile. It’s not just about vanity, though. A beautiful smile can boost your confidence, improve your overall appearance, and even impact your social and professional interactions.

At Smillie Dental, Dr. Joseph Smillie and Dr. Helen Smillie offer a wide range of cosmetic dentistry services in Port Orange. They include teeth whitening, dental veneers, bonding, inlays and onlays, and full-mouth reconstruction.

Teeth whitening is perhaps the most common cosmetic dentistry procedure. It involves using bleaching agents to remove stains and discoloration from your teeth, giving you a brighter, whiter smile. Dental veneers, on the other hand, are thin shells of porcelain or composite resin that are bonded to the front of your teeth to improve their appearance. They can correct a variety of dental issues, including chipped, misshapen, or severely discolored teeth.

Bonding is a procedure where a tooth-colored resin is applied to a tooth and hardened with a special light. This bonds the material to the tooth to improve its appearance. Inlays and onlays, also known as indirect fillings, are used when a tooth has mild to moderate decay or insufficient tooth structure to support a filling.

Lastly, full-mouth reconstruction refers to the process of completely transforming your smile through a combination of cosmetic and restorative dental procedures.

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Worth It?

The value of cosmetic dentistry is subjective and depends on your personal needs and goals. For many people, the confidence that comes with a beautiful smile is priceless. If you’re self-conscious about your teeth, cosmetic dentistry can help you feel more confident and comfortable in social and professional situations.

Moreover, many cosmetic procedures also offer restorative benefits. For example, dental veneers not only improve the appearance of your teeth, but they also provide a protective layer that can prevent further damage. Similarly, inlays and onlays can strengthen a weakened tooth, preventing further decay and the need for more extensive treatment in the future.

So, while the cost of cosmetic dentistry can sometimes be significant, the benefits often outweigh the initial investment, making it a worthwhile consideration for many people.

Does Insurance Cover Cosmetic Dentistry?

Insurance coverage for cosmetic dentistry varies widely depending on your specific insurance plan. In general, most insurance companies do not cover cosmetic procedures as they are considered elective and not medically necessary. However, there are exceptions to this rule.

For example, if a cosmetic procedure also restores function or health to a tooth, your insurance may cover part or all of the cost. Similarly, if a cosmetic procedure is necessary due to an accident or injury, insurance may also provide coverage.

At Smillie Dental, we understand that the cost of cosmetic dentistry can be a concern for many of our patients. That’s why we’re happy to work with you to understand your insurance benefits and explore all available payment options to ensure you can get the care you need.

Please note that it’s always a good idea to check with your insurance provider before beginning any dental treatment to understand what is and isn’t covered under your plan.
