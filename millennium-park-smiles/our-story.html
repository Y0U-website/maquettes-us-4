Our Story

A Legacy of Care Since 1947

From our founding on Michigan Avenue to our current home in the heart of downtown Chicago, we’ve been helping patients smile for nearly eight decades.

Generations of Smiles

Many of our patients are third, fourth, and even fifth generation families. That kind of loyalty isn’t built on marketing — it’s built on trust, results, and genuinely caring about the people who walk through our door.

We treat patients of all ages — from children to seniors — with the same level of personal attention and quality care that has defined this practice since 1947.

Nearly Eight Decades in Downtown Chicago

Millennium Park Smiles has been at 25 E. Washington Street since 1947, making us one of the oldest tenants in the building. That kind of longevity doesn’t happen by accident — it’s built on a foundation of trust, quality care, and genuine relationships with our patients.

Roughly 15 years ago, the practice was thoughtfully reimagined as Millennium Park Smiles, carrying forward a legacy rooted in trust, quality care, and lasting patient relationships. Today, our team of general dentists brings decades of hands-on experience in family dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, implants, and full-mouth reconstruction.

Multigenerational Care

Many of our patients are third, fourth, and even fifth generation families. Grandparents, parents, and children all come to the same practice, and that continuity of care is something we’re proud of.

We treat patients of all ages — from children to seniors. Whether it’s a child’s first dental visit or a complex reconstruction for an adult, we provide the same level of personal attention and quality care.

Full-Scope Care, No Referral Runaround

With 82 years of combined experience between our senior doctors, we handle work that most general practices would send you to a specialist for — reconstructive, cosmetic, implants, and more. We are the opinion and the second opinion.

We’re also not locked into any one implant brand or system. We use products from Zimmer, Nobel Pharma, and others.

Modern Technology, Timeless Care

While our roots go back to 1947, our technology is anything but outdated. We use iTero full-mouth scans that let you see your dental progress over time, digital CBCT 3D imaging for precise diagnostics, Oral ID cancer screening, and Biolase lasers for advanced procedures.

These tools aren’t just buzzwords — they translate directly to better diagnostics, more comfortable procedures, and better outcomes for you.

Personal Attention That Makes a Difference

We greet every patient at the front desk. We check in during visits. We offer refreshments and answer every question thoroughly. We verify insurance and provide breakdowns proactively. To us, that’s just how a dental practice should run. Our patients tell us it’s hard to find that kind of care elsewhere.

We’ve been around a long time, but we’re not dinosaurs. We have modern technology, modern comforts, and a young associate bringing the latest in cosmetic techniques alongside decades of reconstructive expertise.
