Dental Services in Broken Arrow

Dental Services

Located in Broken Arrow, OK, we are your local, comprehensive dental solution for the entire family. Dentistry For You can help you begin your journey to a healthy, beautiful smile.

Family Dentistry

Our staff understands that each situation will guide you to the right solution for you and your family’s dental health. We create custom dental plans for patients of all ages to keep the whole family smiling.

Same-Day Emergency Dentistry

We are always here for emergencies! Dentistry For You offers same-day emergency dental care when you need it most.

Invisible Braces

With invisible braces, you can straighten your teeth without the hindrance of metal braces. Invisible braces give freedom to do and eat whatever you need to do.

Metal-Free Dentistry

Metal-free dentistry is our gold standard of care. If you’re looking for an alternative to braces, metal-free dentistry may be the solution for you.

Root Canals

If you’ve ever had severe tooth pain, you know how pressing it is to get relief. A root canal treatment helps to relieve intense pain due to dental trauma or disease.

Extractions

Wisdom teeth, extra teeth, or very crowded teeth present situations that may mean treatment involving removal. We also have restorative measures to ensure you aren’t left with a gap.

Implants

Losing one or more teeth can be caused by many things. Various traumas, like a fall or car wreck, can cause a tooth to be broken off.

Full or Partial Dentures

We tailor our services to your needs, so you always receive the care you need. Our dentist will customize a plan just for you.

Porcelain Bridges

We offer porcelain bridges to correct issues such as shifting teeth and more. Dental bridges help to replace a missing tooth or gap by joining together adjacent teeth.

Sedation Dentistry

The dentist’s office can be a stressful place for many patients. Dental anxiety is common when it comes to straightforward exams and more complex procedures.

Gum Contouring

Gum contouring is a procedure that can be done for both dental health reasons and cosmetic reasons. Gum contouring can help change the look of your gums.

Oral Surgery

Our services include a variety of oral surgeries that help maintain and improve your dental care. In cases where there is intense trauma, decay, disease, or more, oral surgery may be necessary.

Bone Grafting

Bone grafting might sound scary, but our team of professionals has years of experience when it comes to this procedure. Bone grafting is a periodontal process that aids in restoring lost bone that is integral to your teeth.

Oral Appliances

An oral appliance is a type of therapy that can effectively treat some cases of sleep apnea and help stop continual snoring.
