WHOLE-HEALTH and AIRWAY DENTISTRY

What is Whole-Health & Airway/Sleep Dentistry?

In its simplest terms, you need a healthy mouth, and you need to be able to get oxygen efficiently to have a healthy body!!

Dr. Buse will work with you to identify and understand the underlying oral disease, its causes, and will work diligently to come up with treatment options tailored to you and your goals. We understand that lasting oral health is a life-long commitment. We are here for you! We work with you to implement tools and routines to maintain your oral health at home and give you knowledge to recognize if something is not healthy in your mouth.
