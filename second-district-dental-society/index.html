The Knoxville Area Dental Association in Knoxville, Tennessee, has a rich history that reflects the growth and evolution of dental practice in the region. Established in the early 20th century, the society was founded to provide a professional community for dentists in the area, fostering education, collaboration, and the advancement of dental care standards.

Now, in the 21st century, the Knoxville Area Dental Association has continued to adapt to the changing landscape of dentistry. The advent of digital technology, advanced imaging techniques, and new treatment modalities has kept the society’s focus on continuing education. Regular meetings, seminars, and hands-on workshops ensure that members stay at the forefront of the profession.

KADA is headquartered in Knoxville, TN and supports 18 counties.
