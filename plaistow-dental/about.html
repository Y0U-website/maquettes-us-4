Modern Dental Technology for Optimal Care

Our state-of-the-art technology enhances the precision and efficiency of every treatment, from routine checkups to complex restorative procedures. Designed with your comfort in mind, these tools allow us to deliver exceptional results.

Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays allow us to safely and efficiently see what’s happening beneath the surface of your teeth and gums—areas that can’t be detected during a visual exam alone. Using significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays, this technology helps us identify concerns like cavities, bone loss, and infections early, often before they become more serious. The images are available instantly, so we can review them with you chairside and ensure you fully understand your oral health and treatment options.

Cone Beam Computed Tomography

CBCT provides 3D imaging for detailed views of your teeth, gums and jaw, enhancing diagnostic accuracy for surgical planning and implant placement.

Intraoral Camera/Scanner

We use intraoral cameras to give you a clear, close-up view of your oral health in real time. By showing you exactly what we see—whether it’s a small cavity, a crack, or areas of inflammation—you can better understand your condition and feel confident in any recommended treatment. This technology allows us to be transparent, precise, and collaborative, so you’re always informed and in control of your dental care.

Soft Tissue Laser

The soft tissue laser offers minimally invasive treatment options for gum issues, reducing recovery time and discomfort.

Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramic

Plaistow Dental has embraced CEREC technology for over 20 years, revolutionizing restorative dentistry by crafting same-day dental crowns and bridges with precision and durability.
