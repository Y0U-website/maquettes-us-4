  • Are dental implants painful? Thanks to modern medicine, the implant procedure is typically pain-free. Some mild discomfort may occur during recovery, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
  • What can I eat after getting a dental implant? Stick to soft foods like yogurt, applesauce and mashed potatoes for the first few days. Gradually reintroduce harder foods as your mouth heals, following your dentist’s guidance.
