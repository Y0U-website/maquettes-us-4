Dental Fillings and Sealants

  • What are the different types of materials used for cavity fillings? Cavity fillings can be made from various materials, including composite resin, porcelain, amalgam, and gold. Composite resin and porcelain are popular options due to their natural appearance and durability.
  • How long do dental fillings and sealants typically last? Dental fillings can last 5-15 years depending on the material and how well they are maintained. Sealants usually protect teeth for 5-10 years, but regular checkups ensure they remain effective and are reapplied if necessary.
