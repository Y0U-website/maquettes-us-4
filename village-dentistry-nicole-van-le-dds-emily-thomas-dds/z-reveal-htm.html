Reveal Braces Indianapolis & Greenfield, IN

Looking for an alternative to metal braces?

Reveal is the clear and simple choice. No wires. No brackets. Just clear, convenient comfort—every reason to smile.

Reveal invisible braces are the clear & simple way to straighten your teeth, so you can show off your smile!

With Reveal, your dentist can straighten your teeth using a series of clear, custom, removable aligners. Each aligner moves your teeth just a little bit at a time until you eventually get straight teeth.

How does it work?

You and your doctor will evaluate your teeth and talk about any problems or goals you have for your smile. Once you and your doctor establish Reveal is the right treatment option for you, your doctor will take impressions, photos, and x-rays of your teeth that Reveal uses in manufacturing your custom aligners. Then your doctor writes a prescription for your custom aligners and sends it to Reveal.

Why Reveal?

What can it do?

Reveal can treat a wide variety of issues that keep people from achieving their ideal smiles. Straighter teeth don’t just look better; they work better too. Poorly-aligned teeth can interfere with bite function, wear out quicker, and are more prone to cavities. Ask your doctor how Reveal can help you.
