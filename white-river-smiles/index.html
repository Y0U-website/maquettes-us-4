The practice

We understand your oral health goals are as unique as you. That’s why we’ll actively listen to your concerns, and work to find the right treatment approach. Our team members know there are many ways to help people reach optimal dental health and smiles. We’ll figure out what works with your lifestyle.

Good dental health starts with you understanding why it’s important to keep your mouth in great shape and how to do it. So we’ll provide you with knowledge and practical solutions. We also stress preventive care and minimally invasive treatments, which deliver the best results for your smile.

We serve patients from the surrounding communities, including Lebanon and Hanover, NH.

Dr. Berry and our team look forward to welcoming you to our dental family and starting you on the path to a healthy, beautiful, and confident smile!

We can offer you both minor and major changes. We have procedures that will improve the look of your teeth whether they are discolored, chipped, or anything else.

To help our patients, we are proud to offer many different types of dental restorations depending on their specific needs.

Our goal in offering comprehensive care is to be able to offer any service our patients need. We don’t want to have to refer them to another dentist for any extra care.

Though periodontal disease can affect your health and life, most people don’t do much to ensure they have healthy teeth.
