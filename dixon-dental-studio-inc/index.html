The practice

Dr. Kathryn Dixon is a proud Oregonian whose path in dentistry began with a love of biology at the University of Oregon in Eugene. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU) in Portland and returned to her hometown of Klamath Falls, where she owned and operated a successful private dental practice for more than two decades.

In October of 2025, Dr. Dixon purchased the dental practice from Dr. Joseph Sawyer and established Dixon Dental Studio, Inc., bringing her extensive experience, thoughtful leadership, and patient-centered philosophy to the community of Gladstone.
