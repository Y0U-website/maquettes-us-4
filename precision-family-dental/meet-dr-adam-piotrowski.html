Meet Dr. Adam Piotrowski

Dr. Piotrowski also had additional advanced training in general dentistry, emphasizing Prosthetics (crowns & bridges, dentures & partial dentures, implants, and TMJ splints); Pediatric Dentistry; and Oral Surgery through a General Practice Residency at the Detroit VA Medical Center.

He is also involved in dental mission trips to provide humanitarian relief to the underserved, both within our own borders and abroad. Dr. P has a passion for helping his patients to achieve optimal health. He enjoys collaborating with other medical professionals to help achieve the best outcomes for his patients.

He has always had an interest in alternative medicine, but began his journey down the holistic dentistry path when his wife was diagnosed with thyroid problems and an amazing chiropractor was able to treat her with natural instead of synthetic remedies.

His knowledgeable chiropractor, and other local physicians, helped foster amazing relationships with great people to help push his training to new heights. Along with his dedicated, knowledgeable, and fantastic hygienist, Taylor, they are able to make an impact in their patients’ lives through a unique view of dentistry and its connection to the body.

Helping people achieve their best health through exceptional dental and oral health is what drives Dr. P. He wakes up every day to help heal people in ways he never thought dentistry could achieve.
