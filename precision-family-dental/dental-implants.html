Dental implants are biocompatible, man-made substitutes that replace one or more missing tooth roots. Offered in both full zirconia as well as traditional titanium, this tooth replacement option is surgically placed into the jawbone by our Grand Rapids, MI, dental implant dentists and topped with a dental crown for a seamless restoration.

To ensure that implants are not placed crooked or in a position that would negatively affect adjacent teeth, nerves, or sinuses, our team uses a surgical guide for 100 % precise and safe placement.Dental implants are the most stable and long-lasting solution to the very real problem of missing teeth .

Here’s what you need to know about dental implant surgery and its associated benefits.

Health Benefits of Dental Implants

It is important to understand that your teeth are held in place by tooth roots and surrounding teeth. This means that when you lose a tooth, the teeth surrounding the gap begin to tilt toward the open space. A dental implant not only prevents further tooth loss or crowding, but it also restores lost chewing ability, improves appearance, ends embarrassment, and gives real self-confidence to patients in need.

Because of proven success, preservation of existing teeth and no susceptibility to dental decay (cavities), dental implants are the answer to missing teeth. They are a real breakthrough!

Combining Dentures and Dental Implants

Built to replace a full arch of upper or lower teeth using multiple dental implants as the foundation, All-on-4 implants combine prosthetic teeth (dentures) and, you guessed it, implants to deliver long-lasting stability and a naturally stunning result. This secure base topped by a full set of false teeth is ideal for patients who are either missing most of their natural teeth or face the prospect of extensive tooth extraction.
