3Shape

With 3Shape, we can capture highly accurate digital impressions of a patient’s oral structures, setting aside the need for traditional putty-based images. This technology streamlines the process, making it more comfortable for patients while providing dental professionals with precise data for treatment planning.

Formlabs

We utilize Formlabs to create dental models, crowns, bridges, and implants. The precision of 3D printing ensures accurate fit and customization for each patient.

Nextdent

We also use NextDent to produce accurate physical models of patients&#x27; oral structures. NextDent materials are used to create dental restorations such as crowns, bridges, inlays, onlays, and veneers. These restorations are fabricated using 3D printers, offering a precise fit and excellent aesthetics that match the patient&#x27;s natural teeth.

WHAT WE OFFER

Crown & Bridge

We are dedicated to building well-crafted crown or bridge solutions for our doctor&#x27;s patients. From traditional plastering to digital scans, we have you covered. We offer Zirconia, Emax, Gold Crowns, and PFM (Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal) to build our crowns and bridges.

Implant Solutions

We offer implant solutions for our doctor&#x27;s patients who need a more durable restoration. Our dental implants are constructed with either titanium or zirconia.

Custom Shading

At Technical Crown and Bridge, we use various materials, such as porcelain or composite resins, to custom-mix the colors for the restoration.

Bite Guards

We offer bite guard solutions for our patients. If a patient is grinding or clenching their teeth this can lead to dental issues such as tooth wear, jaw pain, headaches, and even damage to dental restorations.
