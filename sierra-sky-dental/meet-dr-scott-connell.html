Welcome to Sky Dental:

A Personal Message from Dr. Scott Connell, Founder and CEO

Born and raised in Georgia, I’ve proudly called Arizona my home since 2004. Away from the dental chair, I cherish spending time with my wife and three daughters, enjoying a game of volleyball, and indulging in the occasional doughnut (but that’s our little secret).

My dental journey began at the Medical College of Georgia, where I earned my DMD and developed a keen interest in oral surgery and implantology. Since 2002, I’ve been passionately involved in dental implantology, witnessing and contributing to its remarkable evolution. My expertise extends to complex extractions, surgeries, and root canals, ensuring comprehensive care for our patients.

At Sky Dental, a practice I founded with a vision to be more than just a dental office, we are deeply invested in our patients. Our model of dentist-ownership reflects our commitment to prioritizing patient care above all else. My years in the Valley have allowed me to forge lasting bonds with our patients, witnessing their dental journeys and life stories.

My approach to dental care is holistic, focusing not only on immediate needs but on long-term oral health. I aim to guide our patients towards achieving and maintaining a healthy, happy smile for life. Furthermore, I believe cosmetic dentistry should be accessible to everyone. Whether it’s teeth whitening, veneers, or full dental makeovers, we offer a range of cosmetic services to help you achieve the smile you desire.

Together with my dedicated staff, we pledge to work tirelessly to earn your trust and welcome you to the Sky Dental family, where your smile and well-being are our greatest priorities.
