Do You Need Braces?

Braces remain one of the most effective tools in traditional orthodontics, capable of addressing a wide variety of dental concerns. If you’ve been wondering whether braces might be right for you, here are some of the most common issues they can treat:

Crooked or Misaligned Teeth

When teeth grow in at odd angles or out of position, braces can bring them into proper alignment — improving both your smile’s appearance and its function.

Overcrowding

If your mouth doesn’t have enough room for all your teeth, braces can gradually shift them into better positions, creating the space needed for a healthy bite.

Malocclusion

Issues like overbites, underbites, and crossbites put unnecessary strain on your jaw and teeth. Braces can realign your bite, reducing long-term wear and preventing future complications.

Gaps Between Teeth

Unwanted spaces between teeth can be closed with braces, resulting in a more uniform and balanced smile.

Jaw Misalignment

In cases where the jaw itself is contributing to dental problems, braces can be incorporated into a broader treatment plan to improve both function and facial harmony.

No matter the complexity of your case, braces offer a time-tested path to a healthier, more confident smile. One of our orthodontists will assess your needs and walk you through the options that make the most sense for you.
