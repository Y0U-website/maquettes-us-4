Are You A Candidate for Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants work for most adults seeking permanent tooth replacement. Here are key factors determining candidacy:

  • Complete or near-complete tooth loss: Ideal if you’ve lost most/all teeth due to decay, gum disease, trauma, or failed bridges/dentures
  • Healthy gums: Active infections must be treated first; controlled periodontal disease doesn’t disqualify you
  • Adequate bone density: Sufficient jawbone to support implants
  • Good overall health: Controlled diabetes, heart conditions, or osteoporosis is often compatible with proper management
  • Non-smoker or willing to quit: Smoking delays healing by 30 %; we offer cessation support
  • Age 18+: Jaw growth complete; no upper age limit—patients in their 80s succeed regularly

Why Choose Full Mouth Dental Implants Over Other Options?

Traditional dentures or bridges often fall short, causing bone loss, discomfort, and dietary restrictions. Full mouth dental implants stand out by fusing with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, preventing the "sunken" facial appearance that comes with missing teeth. Studies from the International Journal of Oral & Maxillofacial Implants show implant patients retain 90-95 % bone density over 10 years, compared to 40-60 % loss with dentures.
