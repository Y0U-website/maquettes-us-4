Causes of Sleep-Related Breathing Disorders

There are many potential causes of sleep apnea, snoring, mouth breathing, and UARS. Sleep-related breathing disorders can manifest due to functional, behavioral, or structural issues.

At Beyond Smiles of Park Ridge, we’re here to provide you with advanced, personalized therapies for UARS, OSA, snoring, and other sleep-related breathing disorders. To learn more about our treatment options, contact our dental office to schedule an appointment.

  • Functional causes of sleep-related breathing disorders are attributed to physical abnormalities or even bad habits.
  • Behavioral causes of sleep-related breathing disorders are linked to the mind, emotional wellness, and everyday life. Some examples include stress, poor sleep hygiene, diet, mental health issues, and biological desynchronizations.
  • Structural causes of sleep-related breathing disorders are physical abnormalities and flaws, such as nasal obstruction or a narrowed airway.
