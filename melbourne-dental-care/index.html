LEARN MORE Meet Dr. Carlos Lorenzo Dr. Carlos Lorenzo, DMD, is a dedicated and compassionate dentist committed to providing exceptional dental care in a warm, welcoming environment. He focuses on helping patients achieve optimal oral health through personalized treatment plans and an emphasis on preventive care.

Dr. Lorenzo earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University Dental Institute in Downers Grove, Illinois. He is an active member of the Florida Dental Association and the American Dental Association. Passionate about staying current with advancements in dentistry, he regularly participates in continuing education courses to further refine his clinical skills.

Beyond his clinical expertise, Dr. Lorenzo prioritizes patient education. He takes the time to clearly explain procedures and answer questions, ensuring patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident throughout their treatment experience.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Lorenzo enjoys fishing, hiking, gardening, cooking, volunteering, and spending quality time with his family. He is committed to giving back to the community through outreach and service initiatives.
