The practice

At Craig Family Dental, Dr. Owen Atkin, DMD, leads our dedicated team with a clear mission: to provide every patient with the necessary dental work to help them achieve the smile they’ve always wanted. We believe that quality family dentistry goes beyond just treating teeth – it’s about building lifetime relationships with our patients and their families.

Through comprehensive preventative care, advanced restorative dental procedures, and personalized attention, we strive to make dental health accessible and comfortable for everyone who walks through our doors. Our approach focuses on understanding your unique needs and working within your budget to deliver the best possible outcomes.

Choosing the right dental practice for your family is an important decision that affects your long-term oral health care and overall wellness. We stand out in the community because we genuinely care about every patient’s comfort and dental health.

Craig Family Dental provides complete dental treatments for every member of your family, from young children to seniors. Our team understands that different ages require different approaches to dental care. We make pediatric visits fun and educational while providing sophisticated restorative care for adults.

Our clinic uses advanced technology to ensure accurate diagnoses and comfortable treatments. We use digital X-ray imaging for safer, more precise examinations and modern equipment for all our restorative dental procedures. This means more efficient care and shorter treatment times.

Staying current in the world of dentistry is essential to providing the highest quality professional dental services. Our investment in modern equipment reflects our ongoing commitment to excellent care. We want to make sure you receive the most effective treatments available in today’s dental practice environment.

Dr. Owen Atkin, DMD, took ownership of Craig Family Dental in 2010 and has maintained the high standards of care our community expects. Our experienced dental team includes skilled dental assistants and hygienists who work together to create a welcoming, comfortable environment for every patient. We understand that dental visits can cause anxiety, so we go out of our way to help you feel relaxed.

Our caring approach extends beyond technical expertise. We take time to listen to your concerns, explain procedures clearly, and ensure you’re comfortable throughout your visit. Our personal attention helps build the trust and confidence needed for lifelong dental health.
