Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are an oral application used to replace missing teeth. It consists of the dental implant body, abutment and screw. The implant connects directly into the jawbone to fill up the space where the missing tooth used to fit. It is set up to support artificial teeth attached to it.

How much do dental implants cost?

Dental implant fees can run anywhere from $1000 to as high as $4000. The cost will depend on a variety of factors.

Do dental implants hurt?

Dental implants shouldn’t be painful Post-op healing may bring on some discomfort but if any pain is felt the patient should contact their dental provider for a follow-up.

How do dental implants work?

Dental implants work to replace missing teeth through surgical procedures. A metal post is inserted into the jawbone and an artificial tooth is placed over it. The result is a natural looking smile. Dental implants are a popular alternative to dentures or bridgework.
