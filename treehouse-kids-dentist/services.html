Our Services

We provide high quality pediatric dentistry in Springfield and Eugene, Oregon. From Dental Exams, Cleanings, Fillings & Crowns, Fluoride Treatments, Root Canals, X-Rays, to Sedation Dentistry and so much more.

Schedule an Appointment About Us Hygiene Habits

Teeth Cleaning

A healthy mouth starts with routine cleanings every 3 to 6 months for your child. During appointments, we’ll show your child how to floss, brush and develop impeccable hygiene habits they will carry into adulthood.

Dental Exams

Our pediatric dentists and trained staff will explain all of the findings and let you know if your child needs follow-up treatment.

Emergency Dental Care

If your child is experiencing any pain or swelling or loses a tooth traumatically, please reach out to your pediatric dentist for guidance. We are always on call to listen to your concerns and see your children if it is needed.

Fillings & Crowns

We only use composite materials also known as white fillings . Our composite material is BPA free and made of a non-toxic substance.

Fluoride Treatments

Our fluoride treatments are fantastic because there is no wait time for your child to eat or drink!

Learn More Space Maintenance Orthodontics If your child’s tooth has come out too soon for a variety of reasons, from decay to an accident, it’s vital to maintain the space to prevent future space loss and dental problems when permanent teeth begin to come in.

Root Canals

We perform a baby root canal on all primary/ baby teeth to help save an infected or damaged tooth. The procedure involves removing the infected nerve & pulp.

Sealants

Dental sealants fill in the pits and grooves on the chewing surfaces of your child’s permeant back molars. Sealants reduce cavities on the chewing surface by 80 %.

Sedation Dentistry

Nitrous oxide or “laughing gas” is a safe and effective treatment, especially for children. It’s used to sedate and calm patients who may feel anxious.

Silver Diamine Fluoride

Teeth Whitening

We offer in house Boost whitening, that is guaranteed to have your child’s teeth at least two shades whiter!

X-Rays

We take X-rays only when necessary to help locate cavities or other abnormalities. We use age appropriate sensors and take panoramic pictures to assess orthodontic needs and pathology.
