Metal Braces

With traditional brackets and wires that are affixed to the surfaces of your teeth, metal braces shift your teeth over time, addressing everything from minor dental issues to complex bite misalignments. Often viewed as the go-to solution because of their proven record of satisfied patients, metal braces can produce results within two to three years on average.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

An alternative to traditional metal braces is Invisalign clear aligners. These clear plastic trays fit tightly against teeth and shift your smile based on its projected movement. They are removable but must be worn 20-22 hours a day for successful results. Throughout treatment, you must replace your aligner set every two weeks to ensure your teeth continue to shift as intended.
