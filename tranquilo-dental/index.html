The practice

Routine dental exams are important in maintaining healthy teeth and gums. In addition, they help avoid the financial costs associated with more complex treatment plans later on. The Academy of General Dentistry recommends two yearly checkups for people of all ages. At Tranquilo Dental, we are happy to serve the whole family by treating both children and adults.

At your checkup, you will start with diagnostic digital x-rays. The dentist will complete an Oral Cancer Screening - this will check for any abnormalities on your face, neck, lips, tongue, throat, or gums. She will also examine your mouth for any cracked or decayed teeth, evaluate existing restorations, and assess your gums for any evidence of gingivitis or periodontal disease.

Before filling cavities, your dentist will numb your teeth and gums to make the procedure as comfortable as possible. Next, she will remove the decay in the tooth and replace it with either a tooth-colored composite filling or a silver-colored amalgam filling. Once you re done, your mouth will remain numb for a few hours.

If left untreated, a root canal infection can cause complications including swelling, tooth loss, or infection spreading to the face or neck. Root canal treatment preserves your tooth, relieves your pain, and can potentially prevent a dental emergency.

Common reasons for oral surgery or dental extractions include severe tooth decay or infection that make it impossible to save the tooth, advanced gum disease, orthodontic treatment to allow space for tooth movement, and are commonly recommended at an early age for wisdom teeth.

Whatever the reason, at Tranquilo Dental we will fully explain your upcoming procedure so that you understand all that is involved. We strive to provide you with a safe, comfortable experience when needed.

Porcelain bridges use the two surrounding teeth as anchors to hold a false tooth in the place where the missing tooth is.
