Meet Our Dentists in Putnam

Meet the people behind the smiles

At Putnam Family Dental, we believe a great dental experience comes down to one thing: people. Our team isn’t just good at what they do, they’re also kind, thoughtful, and dedicated to making your time with us feel easy and positive. Take a minute to meet the faces you’ll see when you walk through our door.

Dr. Islam Dehis

Dr. Dehis received his Bachelors in Dental Medicine and Surgery from Cairo University, Egypt, where he also completed his General Practice Residency. After his residency, he went on to take additional training in the department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at Cairo University. A lifelong learner, Dr. Dehis continued his education at the Boston University School of Oral and Dental Medicine, where he received a DMD with honors.

Dr. Dehis has an expanded scope of practice, and he believes that there is always room to learn and grow your skills. To understand the needs of anxious and phobic patients, he studied IV Conscious Sedation at the University of Illinois in Chicago. He also participated in the Aesthetic Advantage Continuum at the Rosenthal Institute of Aesthetic Dentistry at the New York University College of Dentistry.

He’s a proud member of the American Dental Association, Connecticut State Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD). He serves as a board member of the New England Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and is pursuing Fellowships with the AGD and AACD.

Dr. Dehis is married to Yasmine, his college sweetheart. They have a daughter Farida, and a son Yousuf. He is into reading, hiking, boating, and woodworking, and loves returning to Egypt whenever he can.

Dr. Alaa Ahmed dmd, dmsc

Dr. Ahmed is a periodontist who is dedicated to comprehensive, patient-focused treatment of gum disease, bone loss and dental implants. He is committed to providing high quality dental care to his patients and works closely with patients and referring doctors to explore all treatment options.

He has won several awards, including: Omicron Kappa Upsilon Award Boston University Award for Patient Care and Practice Management Award of the Northeastern Society of Periodontists Award of the American Academy of Orofacial Pain American Academy of Dental Research Dentsply Award in Basic Science Research Best Doctors List in America for 2019

While he’s not at work, Dr. Ahmed enjoys spending time with his wife, Passant, and their four children: Malak, Adam, Maryam, and Omar. He enjoys reading about science and history.
