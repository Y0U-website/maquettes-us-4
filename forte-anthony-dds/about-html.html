OUR STORY

Dr. Forte is a life-long resident of the West Side of Cincinnati and he is a graduate of Elder High School, Xavier University and The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. His professional memberships include the American Dental Association, Ohio State Dental Association and Cincinnati Dental Society. Dr. Forte and his wife are blessed to have two amazing sons and a daughter-in-law. They are also crazy about their two rescue dogs, Simon and Marvin.

" We recently switched dentists and decided to try Anthony Forte, who was a classmate of mine at Elder High School. We found his dental hygenists to be excellent and Tony was great to work with. My wife and daughters were very happy. We’re glad to have found a dentist we will be going to for a long time." BRANDT J.

Our staff has an average of over 20 years experience in the dental field. We enjoy working together as a team to provide all our patients with a caring, stress-free atmosphere.

Our hygienists have 30 years of collective experience in their careers and will partner with you to keep your smile healthy and beautiful!

Dr. Forte’s dental assistant has worked with him for over 25 years! Her warm smile and friendly manner immediately put our patients at ease.

Setting the bar a bit higher, Dr. Forte shakes the traditional mold of dental practitioners. Our office is incredibly professional, but also believes having a sense of humor and being lighthearted goes a long way. And instead of having a boring office with bland decor, we’ve taken the time to add color and a little style to the office. You will probably agree a warm environment makes for a much better mood.

It is important for you to know we place high standards on everything we do. And while we can tell you how great our staff and office are, it’s how we present ourselves to you that really matters most.
