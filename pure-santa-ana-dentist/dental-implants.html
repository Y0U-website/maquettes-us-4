What is a Dental Implant?

Dental implants replace missing teeth. They are small, titanium screws that are placed in the upper, or lower, jawbone. This screw is the base of the implant that will hold and stabilize the replacement tooth, or teeth. This includes crowns, bridges, and/or dentures.

Dental implants are often chosen by people who have removable dentures or bridges. Implants make a drastic improvement in the ability to chew comfortably.

Dental Implant Step-by-Step Procedure

  • An X-ray will be taken of the jawbone to make sure it can hold the titanium implant.
  • Plastic compound models of the mouth are taken for measurements and locating gaps.
  • The implants are then fitted through a surgical procedure. In approximately 2-6 months, the implant will fuse to the bone and tooth restorations can be attached.
  • An abutement, which attaches the tooth to the implant, can be immediately attached.
  • During the final step, a custom crown is permanently set. It will fit and appear as if it is a natural tooth.

Dental Implants And Old Age

Many older people are worried that age is a determining factor in how well they can enjoy dental implants. The biggest concern regarding dental implants is health. Although some health conditions and diseases can cause problems with the dental implant procedure, our office will provide an extensive evaluation to make sure that you are a good candidate for dental implants. Your health is of the utmost importance to us.

How Will Dental Implants Impact My Life?

One of the major benefits that dental implants can provide is self-esteem. Being able to chew and smile comfortably, knowing that your implants are safe, stable, and natural looking can surely increase your self-image and your quality of life.
