What does Medi-Cal Dental Cover?

According to the state of California each recipient may be eligible for different levels of coverage. Medi-Cal Dental covers all medically-necessary dental care however some patients receive more coverage than others. Patients can visit our medi-cal dental office in Orange County to get full details as we will confirm your eligibility with the state and explain to you in detail what your particular coverage covers.

Medi-Cal & Denti-Cal Coverage for 2022

  • Dental Cleanings Fluoride

What is the yearly limit to Medi-Cal dental?

Great news, kids are covered and there is no annual maximum. Adults who live in a nursing facility also have no maximum.

For adults 21 years of age or older there is no limit for medically necessary treatments but for other services there is a $1800 maximum per year of benefits. In our office we have found for most patients this is more than enough to get you back on the right track to a healthy mouth.

This program changes often and its best advised to contact the state directly for most current and up to date information. Here is a link to the medi-cal coverage book which is available in many languages.
