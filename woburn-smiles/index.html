The practice

Located in the heart of Woburn on Main Street, our practice provides high-quality, compassionate dental care for patients of all ages. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning, restorative treatment, or more comprehensive care, our team takes the time to listen, explain your options, and help you feel confident about your smile.

We know that fitting dental care into everyday life isn’t always easy. That’s why we offer convenient scheduling, including late-evening appointments, and work with most major dental insurance plans. For patients without insurance, or those who need additional flexibility with out-of-pocket costs, our team is happy to discuss payment options and help make care more manageable.

Our bilingual team is proud to serve both English and Portuguese-speaking patients, helping more members of our community feel at home from the moment they walk through our doors.

We also have a special commitment to Woburn’s senior community. Our team works closely with older adults to understand their coverage and available benefits, helping them receive the dental care they need while minimizing out-of-pocket expenses whenever possible.
