Cosmetic Dentistry

A beautiful smile is one of the most sought-after cosmetic features in the world. Our cosmetic dentistry services are designed to enhance the look of your smile — whether through dental bonding , porcelain veneers , porcelain crowns , or dentures . We help patients from all walks of life build confidence in the way their teeth look.

Dental Technology

At Gregg K. Balis, DDS, we invest in state-of-the-art dental technology to deliver more precise, comfortable, and efficient care. From digital X-rays to Invisalign and dental implant restoration, our advanced tools allow us to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry right here in Palm Desert, CA .

General Dentistry

Maintaining a healthy mouth starts with quality general dentistry . Our preventive and restorative general dental services — including fillings , root canals , sealants , and teeth whitening — are designed to keep your smile healthy and your overall well-being in check.

Periodontal Services

Our periodontal services address gum disease and oral inflammation with expertise and care. From periodontal disease treatment to scaling and root planing , our team provides the specialized gum care you need to protect your teeth and overall oral health.

Restorative Dentistry

Whether you need to repair a damaged tooth or replace missing teeth entirely, our restorative dentistry services are here to restore your smile’s function and appearance. We offer dental bridges and dental implants along with regular follow-up care to ensure lasting results.

Does Gregg K. Balis, DDS offer cosmetic dental treatments?

Yes. Our cosmetic dentistry services include porcelain veneers , dental bonding , porcelain crowns , dentures , and professional teeth whitening . We can help you achieve a brighter, more confident smile.

Do you treat gum disease at Gregg K. Balis, DDS?

Yes. Our periodontal services include periodontal disease treatment and scaling and root planing . Our team is trained to diagnose and treat gum disease at various stages to protect your oral health and prevent tooth loss.

Does Gregg K. Balis, DDS offer dental implants?

Yes. Dental implants are one of the most effective and long-lasting solutions for replacing missing teeth. Our team at Gregg K. Balis, DDS offers implant restorations as part of our comprehensive restorative dentistry services.

What technology does Gregg K. Balis, DDS use?

Gregg K. Balis, DDS uses modern dental technology , including digital X-rays that provide clearer images with less radiation than traditional X-rays. We also offer Invisalign and dental implant restoration technology to ensure precise, comfortable treatment.

Does Gregg K. Balis, DDS offer services for the whole family?

Yes! Gregg K. Balis, DDS is a full-service dental practice in Palm Desert, CA that welcomes patients of all ages. Whether you need a routine cleaning, a filling, cosmetic treatment, or gum disease care, our team is equipped to serve your entire family’s dental needs.
