How Do Implant-Supported Dentures Work?

Types of Implant-Supported Dentures

When it comes to implant-supported dentures, there are a few different options to consider.

Each type of implant-supported denture offers its own set of benefits and considerations, so it’s important to discuss your specific needs with your dentist or prosthodontist. They can help determine which option would be best suited for you based on factors such as bone density and oral health.

Remember, choosing the right type of implant-supported dentures will ultimately depend on various individual factors – what works well for one person may not necessarily work well for another.

  • One type is the bar-retained denture, which involves attaching a thin metal bar to several implants that have been placed in the jawbone. The denture then clips onto this bar for added stability.
  • Another option is the ball-retained denture, also known as an overdenture. This type of denture uses implants with small metal attachments that fit into corresponding sockets on the underside of the denture. This allows for easy attachment and removal.
  • A third option is All-on-4 or All-on-6 implant-supported dentures, which offer a more permanent solution. With this method, four or six implants are strategically placed in the jawbone to support a full arch of teeth. These prosthetics provide excellent stability and chewing function.
