We only see a select number of patients a day and never double book appointments. We book enough time for each appointment and devote all of our attention to just one patient at a time, enabling us to address all of the details without missing any diagnosis.

We get patients mainly by referral from patients and other dental/medical colleagues. Excellent, ethical service and long-lasting successful outcome are the only factors for referrals. It also enables us to reduce the need for marketing, so to keep our costs and your costs low.

Conventional dental practice is procedure-based compensation, meaning the more procedures performed, the more reward. Our specialist group uses an outcome-based compensation mechanism. Your long-lasting healthy smile is the primary factor for our fulfillment.

The bottom line is that we enjoy our profession. All of our specialists hold a faculty position at the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine or Temple University School of Dentistry. We are involved in the education of the future dental providers, instilling in them the same passion for excellence we have. We help patients restore their health and smile.
