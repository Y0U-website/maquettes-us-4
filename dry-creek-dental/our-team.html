Chelsea, Registered Dental Hygienist

Chelsea grew up in Ohio. She graduated from the dental hygiene program with an associates of applied science, in 2012. She met her husband while working in Virginia where they welcomed their two children, Averie and Carter. Their family moved to Cheyenne in 2018 to be closer to her stepson, Leyton, and to raise our kids in a smaller community. As a family, they love to go camping, fishing, ski at Snowy Range, and travel throughout! Chelsea personally enjoys crafting, working out, and being outside - especially when taking care of her six chickens and three German Shepherds.
