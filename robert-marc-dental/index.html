The practice

For more than twenty years, Dr. Robert Schulman and his team have treated generations of smiles, while advancing the art and science of cosmetic, restorative and implant dentistry. We are committed to building a long-term relationship with you and will actively engage with you to understand your desires, concerns and expectations.

We re commited to treating you with the same level of high-quality dentistry, compassion, and respect, we’d provide to our own family members.

Our White Plains and Manhattan offices serve the five boroughs, as well as Westchester, New Jersey, Rockland, and Connecticut. Give us a call with any questions or to schedule an appointment, or simply request an appointment on-line!!
