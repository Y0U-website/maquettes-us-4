Robert Schulman, DMD

At Robert Marc Dental, Dr. Schulman’s mission is to combine his passion for providing the highest level of care with developing a relationship built upon empathy and understanding of each patient’s unique situation.

Each patient is different, with unique dental concerns and goals, and with their own stories and experiences to share. I love touching people’s lives while having my own life enriched by theirs. My passion and dedication to the dental profession has only grown in the more than 20 years I’ve been practicing, largely because of the relationships developed with our patients.

Providing the highest level of care for patients motivated me to become a board-ceritified prosthodontist . The excitement and motivating force behind my work comes from creating generations of smiles, while advancing the art and science of general, cosmetic, and implant dentistry. The most fulfilling aspect of our work is seeing the excitement and joy with the results the team delivers and creating wonderful patient experiences.

We are passionate about making every visit enjoyable for our patients. Each of my team members brings incredible skills and talent to our office. Our patients are truly the most important asset to our practice, and we strive to develop long-lasting, trusting relationships with each of them.

We are united in our desire to provide the best personal care: making people’s lives healthier, happier, and better is our greatest reward!
