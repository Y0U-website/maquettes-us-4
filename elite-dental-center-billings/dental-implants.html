Dental Implants Pros And Cons

Every treatment has trade-offs. Understanding the pros and cons helps you make a decision that fits your goals.

Pros

  • Implants look and function like natural teeth for a confident smile.

Cons

  • The process involves multiple steps, which can take several months.
