Best ways to control severe toothache pain until you’re seen

Home care is temporary—it can reduce pain, but it won’t remove the cause.

Go to urgent care/ER if you develop trouble breathing/swallowing, rapidly spreading swelling, fever with swelling, or swelling near the eye.

  • Cold compress on the cheek (10–15 minutes at a time)
  • Avoid chewing on that side
  • Sleep with head elevated
  • Warm salt-water rinses (gentle)
  • OTC pain meds only if safe for you and taken exactly as directed on labels
  • Putting aspirin directly on gums/tooth (can burn tissue)
  • Smoking/vaping
  • Very hot/cold foods if temperature triggers pain
