The practice

We Make Kids Smile is committed to delivering exceptional pediatric dentistry to the Waldorf and Prince Frederick communities. Everyone on our team genuinely cares for the children and families we serve. We understand that early positive dental experiences can make a real difference in a child’s oral health throughout their lifetime. That’s why we provide welcoming, friendly spaces that make…

We welcome babies for their first dental visits and encourage you to bring your child in within six months of their first tooth coming in or by age 1. This lays the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles and familiarizes your child with the dental office. Our gentle approach and child-friendly environment make those early appointments comfortable for parents and babies.

For children with complex dental needs who require extensive treatment, we offer hospital dentistry services. Our team works closely with local hospitals to provide a comprehensive range of oral health care services and full smile rehabilitation treatments. We are committed to achieving the best possible outcomes and optimal recovery for your child.

We Make Kids Smile provides exceptional dental care for children who have special needs. Our experienced team is specifically trained to ensure your child is safe and comfortable throughout every appointment. We approach each patient with patience and understanding and provide the personalized care your child deserves.
