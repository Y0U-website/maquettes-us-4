Boise

Our Boise office is a part of CenterPoint Orthodontics, a locally owned and operated business. We have one staff that travels to each location.

Our Boise office is newly renovated and conveniently located in Northwest Boise and we offer free onsite parking.

Dr. Miler and his team travel to each office for appointments to save you time and provide the services you need close enough to home. Many people often walk or bike to their appointments!

Free parking available right outside our office, we are located right by the local fire station and down the street from multiple schools.

We work around the school schedule and prioritize Weiser appointments on Fridays, so students (and school staff) don’t have to miss school to meet their appointments.

Located conveniently on Main Street, easy to walk to from the middle school.
