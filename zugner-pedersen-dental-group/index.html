The practice

At Zugner & Pedersen, we believe that oral health is an integral part of overall well-being, and our goal is to provide you with the skills, guidance, and care you need to maintain a healthy, radiant smile for years to come.

At Zugner & Pedersen Dental Group, we are proud to be accredited, licensed dental professionals committed to providing exceptional care in a convenient, accessible location off Ridge Road and Route 104 in Webster, NY. We understand the importance of flexibility, which is why we offer a variety of client-friendly appointment times, including Saturdays, to accommodate even the busiest schedules.

We understand that dental care can sometimes feel like an investment, which is why we offer a variety of payment options to ensure quality dental care is accessible to everyone. In addition to accepting major credit cards, cash, and checks, we’ve partnered with Care Credit to offer additional financing options to meet your needs.

Our office is proud to accept Cigna and Blue Cross Blue Shield insurance plans, and we are happy to submit most insurance claims on behalf of our patients. However, we encourage all patients to reach out directly to their insurance provider to verify out-of-network coverage, ensuring transparency and peace of mind.

With over 20 years of experience in crafting beautiful, healthy smiles, Dr. William Zugner and Dr. Todd Pedersen are dedicated to offering the highest level of dental care available. Our team combines expertise, state-of-the-art technology, and a compassionate approach to ensure that every patient feels comfortable and well-cared for throughout their treatment.
