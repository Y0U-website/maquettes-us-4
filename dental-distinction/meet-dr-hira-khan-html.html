Dr. Hira Khan’s Approach

Dr. Khan believe that a smile speaks volumes about an individual’s well-being. Her approach to dentistry extends far beyond traditional dental care. It is about the meticulous restoration of balance, function and aesthetics to enhance not only smiles but lives. She is committed to personalized patient care, taking the time to understand unique needs of her patients.

‘I envision a future where our patients no longer view dental visits as a necessity but as a pathway to experiencing enhanced well-being and aesthetics. With our dedication for patient-centered care, we aim to empower individuals to take control of their oral health and embrace a life of vitality, beauty, balance and a biological healthy lasting smile.’

If you are ready to embark on a transformative journey towards a healthier, more dazzling smiles, we invite you to schedule a consultation at Plano Dental Distinction.

Outside of the Office
