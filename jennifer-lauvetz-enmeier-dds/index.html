As a BOTOX-certified practitioner, Dr. Lauvetz-Enmeier offers this innovative treatment for both cosmetic and therapeutic purposes. BOTOX can help with facial wrinkles, as well as dental issues such as TMJ disorders and bruxism (teeth grinding). We provide safe, effective BOTOX treatments tailored to your individual needs.

Sleep apnea can significantly impact your quality of life. As a sleep apnea-certified dentist, Dr. Lauvetz-Enmeier offers effective treatments to help you get a good night’s sleep. We provide custom-made oral appliances that can help keep your airway open during sleep, reducing snoring and improving your overall health and well-being.
