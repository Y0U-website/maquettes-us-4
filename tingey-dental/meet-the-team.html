Meet The Team

Carma

Front Desk Manager

Carma has lived in the Magic Valley all of her life. She enjoys being outside walking the Snake River Canyon or traveling with her family. She has many years of customer service, 24 years of banking knowledge and 27 years of retail experience, but her favorite has been working for Dr. Tingey. Her husband Randy is part owner of Berry Trucking. They have two children and a grandson that she Loves to spoil. She looks forward to meeting new friends and catching up with old friends at Tingey Dental.

Iris

Iris studied at The College of Southern Idaho. She was raised with military parents travelling around the world. Her favorite part of her job is meeting new people.

Melissa

Melissa studied assisting at The College of Southern Idaho. She was raised in Mexico and moved to Twin Falls during high school, where she learned English. Her favorite part of her job is being able to provide better communication with patients who only speak Spanish.

Lisa

Dental Hygienist

Lisa is a 2003 Dental Hygiene graduate from Truckee Meadows Community College, Reno, Nevada. She has been married for 34 years to her husband, Rick, and has two kids and one grandchild.

Outside work, she enjoys going side by side riding, sprint car racing, and spending time with her family.

Shayna

Dental Hygienist

Shayna studied dental hygiene at The College of Southern Idaho. She was born and raised in Buhl, Idaho. Her favorite part of her job is educating patients about how to improve their oral health and getting to know her patients.

“ A smile is a language everyone understands ”
