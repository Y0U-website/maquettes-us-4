Financing Option

At Tingey Dental we want to make it possible for all our patients to achieve a beautiful, healthy smile so we offer several different payment options.

We accept all major credit cards: Master Card, Visa, and Discover

CareCredit is here to help you pay for treatments and procedures your insurance doesn’t cover. We offer No Interest* financing or low minimum monthly payment options. CareCredit has an instant approval process, so it’s easy to apply.

Now you don’t have to worry about saving up for the procedures you want and need. With CareCredit, the decision is yours as to what you want and when you want it. Please visit the link below to learn more and start your approval process: carecredit.com
