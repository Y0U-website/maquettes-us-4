All-on-X Implants What are All-on-X Implants? All-on-X (such as All-on-4 or All-on-6) is a revolutionary implant technique that provides a full arch of fixed, permanent teeth supported by just 4-6 strategically placed dental implants. This innovative approach eliminates the need for bone grafting in many cases and allows for immediate loading, meaning you can receive a complete set of functional, beautiful teeth in just one day.

Strategic Placement Implants are placed at specific angles to maximize support with minimal bone

Immediate Function Get a full set of teeth on the same day as your surgery

Fewer Implants A full arch with just 4-6 implants instead of 8-10 individual ones

Permanent Solution Fixed in place and doesn’t come out like traditional dentures

Recovery Timeline

First 24 Hours

Rest, apply ice packs, take prescribed medications, and stick to a liquid diet. Some swelling and discomfort is normal.

First Week

Swelling and discomfort begin to subside. Continue with soft foods and careful oral hygiene to avoid disturbing the surgical site.

First Month

Gums continue to heal and temporary teeth are adjusted as needed. Gradual transition to a broader diet while avoiding hard foods.

3-6 Months

Implants fully integrate with jawbone. Impressions for final restoration are taken, and permanent teeth are crafted and placed.
