Multiple Tooth Implants What are Multiple Tooth Implants? Multiple tooth implants provide a permanent solution for replacing several missing teeth. Each implant is surgically placed into your jawbone, creating a strong foundation for natural-looking replacement teeth that function just like your natural ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many implants will I need if I am missing multiple teeth?

The number of implants needed depends on several factors, including the number and location of missing teeth, bone density, and the type of restoration planned. In many cases, it’s possible to replace multiple teeth using fewer implants by utilizing implant-supported bridges.

What are the advantages of choosing implant-supported bridges over traditional dentures?

Implant-supported bridges offer superior stability, prevent bone loss, and eliminate the need for adhesives. They also provide a more natural look and feel compared to traditional dentures, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
