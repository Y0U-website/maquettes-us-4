Single Tooth Implants What is a Single Tooth Implant? A single tooth implant is a state-of-the-art dental restoration that replaces both the visible crown portion of a missing tooth and its root. Unlike traditional bridges that rely on neighboring teeth for support, an implant stands independently, providing a permanent, stable solution that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the experience of getting a dental implant compare to other dental procedures?

Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the dental implant procedure is compared to other dental treatments they’ve experienced. The precise placement is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring you feel no pain during the procedure. Many patients report that the process is less uncomfortable than a tooth extraction. While you may experience mild soreness for a few days afterward, this is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

What makes a dental implant superior to a traditional bridge for replacing a tooth?

A dental implant offers several significant advantages over a bridge. First, it preserves your natural teeth—bridges require grinding down adjacent healthy teeth. Second, implants stimulate and maintain jawbone health, preventing the bone loss that typically follows tooth loss. Third, implants are more durable and typically last much longer than bridges. Finally, implants look and function just like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.
