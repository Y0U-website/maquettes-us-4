Implant Supported Dentures What are Implant Supported Dentures? An implant-supported denture is a revolutionary solution that combines the affordability of traditional dentures with the stability of dental implants. Unlike conventional dentures that rest on your gums, implant-supported dentures are securely attached to dental implants surgically placed in your jawbone, eliminating slipping, clicking, and discomfort while allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

Snap-On Dentures Removable dentures that snap onto implant attachments for security

Bar-Retained Dentures Dentures that clip onto a thin metal bar attached to multiple implants

Fixed Hybrid Dentures Permanently attached to implants, only removable by your dentist

Expert Insight "Implant-supported dentures provide up to 90 % more chewing efficiency compared to traditional dentures, allowing patients to enjoy a wider variety of foods."

Oral Hygiene

Diet

Stick to soft foods for the first few weeks. Gradually reintroduce normal foods as healing progresses, following your dentist’s guidance.

Follow-up

Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments to monitor healing and ensure your implant-supported dentures are functioning properly.

Recovery Timeline

First 24-48 Hours

Rest, apply ice to reduce swelling, take prescribed medications, and stick to a liquid diet. Some discomfort and swelling is normal.

First Week

Swelling and discomfort begin to subside. Continue with soft foods and careful oral hygiene around the implant sites.

2-4 Weeks

Soft tissue healing completes. You’ll wear a temporary denture while implants continue to integrate with bone.

3-6 Months

Implants fully integrate with jawbone. Final impressions are taken, and your permanent denture is created and fitted.
