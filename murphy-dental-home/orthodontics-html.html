Traditional Braces

Even with new products like Invisalign available, traditional braces remain a popular choice for orthodontic care for patients of all ages. Why? They work! While other systems can only address mild to moderate misalignment, traditional braces can handle even the most severe cases. In addition, traditional braces can be used on children, teens, or adults, and with advancements in materials, braces are smaller and more comfortable than ever before, making them a great option for straightening teeth.

Invisalign Clear Braces

Are you looking for a more subtle alternative to traditional metal braces? Invisalign aligners are completely clear and barely noticeable when they’re worn. Because they are removable, you can eat anything you like, including all the foods that are off limits with metal braces. You just need to brush and floss your teeth before putting the aligners back in. Also, compared to traditional braces, the Invisalign system typically takes less time to achieve final results.

Accelerated Orthodontics

One major complaint about traditional orthodontics is the time commitment. On average, metal braces can take two to three years, and Invisalign typically requires 12 to 18 months to complete treatment. However, with accelerated orthodontics, we can speed up this process significantly. Interested in learning more about accelerated orthodontics? Ask Dr. Gandhi during your consultation. She can explain the process and let you know if you are a good candidate for this treatment.
