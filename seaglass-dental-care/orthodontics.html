Everything About Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a specialized field of dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities. The goal of orthodontic treatment in Palm Beach County is to improve the functionality and appearance of the teeth and jaws and give you the perfect smile.

The most common orthodontics treatment is braces, which use brackets and wires to move the teeth into their proper positions over a period of months or even years. Other orthodontic appliances may include retainers, headgear, expanders, and clear aligners such as Invisalign clear aligners.

Orthodontic treatment usually begins with a consultation with an orthodontist, who will assess the patient’s dental and facial structure and determine the best course of treatment. The treatment plan is customized to meet each patient’s unique needs and may involve different types of braces or appliances using advanced technology.

In addition to traditional metal braces, there are also ceramic braces and lingual braces, which are placed on the back of the teeth and are less visible. Clear aligners, such as Invisalign, are also becoming increasingly popular due to their discreet appearance and ability to be removed for eating and brushing.

It is important for both children and adults to receive orthodontic treatment if needed, as dental and facial irregularities can lead to more serious issues such as TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorders, headaches, and speech problems. Additionally, properly aligned teeth are easier to clean and maintain, which can help to prevent tooth decay and gum disease.

Overall, orthodontic treatment is an investment in both oral health and appearance. With proper care and maintenance, the results of orthodontic treatment can last a lifetime.

How long does orthodontic treatment last?

Orthodontic treatment typically lasts between one and three years, depending on the severity of the dental issues being addressed. During this time, patients will need to visit their orthodontist regularly to have their braces or appliances adjusted and to monitor their progress.

Can I put on braces if I have a root canal?

Yes, you can put braces if you have had a root canal. However, it is recommended that you wait until the root canal procedure has fully healed and any necessary restorative work has been completed before getting braces. This is because the root canal procedure can weaken the tooth and the braces may put too much pressure on it, which could cause the tooth to fracture or become damaged. Your dentist or orthodontist will be able to advise you on the best course of action based on your individual needs and circumstances.
