Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are an effective treatment option for many common dental problems, providing patients with a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a healthier, more beautiful, perfect smile. If you are experiencing any of these dental issues, consult with your Dentist North Palm Beach to see if clear aligners are the right treatment option for you.

  • Crowded Teeth: Crowded teeth occur when there is not enough space in the jaw to accommodate all the teeth. Clear aligners can gradually shift the teeth into the correct position and create the space required to alleviate crowding.
  • Overbite: An overbite is a common dental issue when the upper teeth overlap the lower teeth. Clear aligners can help to correct overbites by gradually shifting the teeth into the correct position.
  • Underbite: An underbite occurs when the lower teeth protrude further than the upper teeth. Clear aligners can help to correct this issue by gradually moving the lower teeth into a more natural position.
  • Crossbite: A crossbite occurs when the upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth. Clear aligners can help correct this issue by gradually shifting the teeth into the correct position.
  • Gap Teeth: If you have gaps in your teeth, clear aligners can help to close them by gradually shifting your teeth closer together.
  • Open Bite: An open bite is a condition where there is no overlap between the upper and lower teeth when the mouth is closed. Clear aligners can correct this by gradually shifting the teeth into the correct position and closing the gap.

SureSmile Vs. Invisalign Clear Aligners

If you’re considering teeth straightening options, you may have come across SureSmile and Invisalign clear aligners. Both are popular alternatives to traditional metal braces, offering a more discreet option for people who want to fix their crooked teeth or bite issues, giving a straighter smile. But how do SureSmile and Invisalign aligners compare?

SureSmile and Invisalign treatment both use clear plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. The teeth adjust naturally as you continue wearing them. The main difference between the two is in how those aligners are customized.

SureSmile uses advanced 3D imaging and robotic technology to custom-make your aligners. This means that each aligner is specifically tailored to your teeth and bite, resulting in more precise and efficient treatment.

Invisalign, on the other hand, uses patented software to create a virtual treatment plan for your teeth. Your aligners are then made based on that plan, but they may not be as tailored to your exact needs as SureSmile aligners.

Another difference is in the length of treatment. Since SureSmile aligners are more precise and efficient, treatment times may be shorter than with Invisalign. However, this also depends on the severity of your case and how consistent you are with wearing your aligners as directed.

Both SureSmile and Invisalign systems offer benefits like being able to remove your aligners for eating and brushing your teeth, and their clear design is more discreet than traditional braces. However, they may not be suitable for everyone. SureSmile may be a better option for those with more complex cases, while Invisalign clear braces may be a better fit for those with milder orthodontic issues.

Ultimately, both SureSmile and Invisalign provide effective teeth straightening solutions with their clear aligners. It’s up to you to weigh the differences and decide which one is right for you.
