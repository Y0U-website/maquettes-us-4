The Ceramic Implant Process

Treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation including CBCT imaging to assess bone density and plan precise implant placement. If a biological extraction is needed first, it is performed with our standard protocols including ozone and PRF. The ceramic implant is placed into the jawbone, and a healing period allows osseointegration to occur. Once the implant is fully integrated, a custom ceramic crown is placed to restore function and aesthetics.

What to Expect

The implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia and is well-tolerated. Most patients experience mild soreness for a few days. The healing period before final restoration is typically three to six months, during which a temporary restoration may be worn. With proper care and regular checkups, ceramic dental implants can last a lifetime.
