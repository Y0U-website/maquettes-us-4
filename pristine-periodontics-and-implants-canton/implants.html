How long do dental implants last?

Designed with the patient in mind, dental implants can last a lifetime when properly cared for. Best of all, your implants are as easy to care for as your natural teeth. Just make sure to maintain an oral hygiene routine that consists of brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and rinsing with an antibacterial mouthwash. In addition to at-home care, we recommend visiting Dr. Sayed for biannual appointments so he can make sure both your restoration and oral health are in great condition.
