You may think that the gaps in your smile caused by damaged or missing teeth are just a simple inconvenience to your appearance. However, those little spaces can collect food particles, bacteria, and plaque which is menacing to your oral health.

Your chances of tooth decay and gum disease will increase and can further result in additional tooth loss. Having missing teeth can also affect your well-being as it has been found to correlate with reduced self-confidence and insecurities. If you’re looking to replace your missing teeth in the Detroit area, dental implants can restore both your smile and your self-esteem.

Dental implants are prosthetic teeth designed to replace natural teeth from root to crown. They offer numerous benefits for patients suffering from tooth loss. Known to be one of the most successful restorative treatments, dental implants are a solution that will improve your smile, overall health, and well-being.
