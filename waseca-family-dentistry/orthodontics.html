+ Home Orthodontics Office Hours Mon-Thurs 8am 5pm Contact Info Phone: ( 507) 835-4280 Orthodontics Dr. Christianson is a member of the Academy of General Practitioner Orthodontics, which means he is a general dentist offering orthodontic services.

Whether you want limited orthodontics to straighten out just the front teeth in a shorter period of time, or comprehensive orthodontics to correct alignment and troublesome bite patterns, Dr. Christianson can consult with you on your many options. These orthodontic services are offered to both adults and children.

There are two reasons to pursue orthodontic treatment, functional problems and self esteem.

Functional problems can consist of over and under bites, cross bites, or severe crowding or spacing that can lead to difficulty of chewing, talking, or keeping things clean.

Self esteem can be something as simple as not liking a space between two teeth in your smile, or wanting to straighten your teeth to give you your best smile.

We offer traditional bracket and wire orthodontics, as well as clear aligner therapy which is popular among most adults.
