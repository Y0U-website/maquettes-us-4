With thanks! Maria S. Meet Our Dentists DENTIST Dr. Sarah Gebrail DENTIST Dr. Sarah Gebrail Dr. Gebrail” started her career in dentistry a little earlier than most. At the age of 21, she earned her Bachelor’s degree in oral and Maxillofacial dentistry in 2005 from Misr University for Science and Technology, Egypt.

she moved with her husband to the united states, began to continue her career in dentistry and after attending two years in residency she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree in 2019 from Universidad Del La Salle, Leon, Gto, Mexico .

Following graduation and time in general practice, Dr. Gebrail noticed she worked very well with children & soon realized that her passion was in the treatment of children especially when she served at the Egyptian Army Hospitals as a First lieutenant Dental officer and had the chance to see many Children who needed help while their parents were deployed far from their homes.

Dr. Gebrail is Certified in Oral conscious sedation for minors, carries extensive training in conscious sedation and experience in child behavior management techniques, and establishing a smooth communication channel with all children at different ages.

Having had a negative experience as a child, Dr. Gebrail prides herself in not only providing a positive dental experience but also an educational one for parents.

Dr. Gebrail has a passion to teach and train her students, she was a dental Instructor at Drake College Saint Paul school of Nursing in New York and helped many students establish their careers in the dental industry.
