Securing Your Smile

How They Work

Step 1: CBCT Scan

The process begins with a Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scan. This innovative technology provides a comprehensive 3D image of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures, enabling us to plan the implant placement with exceptional accuracy.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Using the detailed information gathered from the CBCT scan, we proceed with the implant placement. This involves surgically placing the implant into your jawbone, where it will serve as a sturdy foundation for the artificial tooth.

What Happens Next

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Improved Function and Comfort: Dental implants restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence, just like natural teeth.
  • Longevity: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a durable and cost-effective tooth replacement option.
  • Preservation of Jawbone Health: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining the structure of your face.
  • Aesthetics: Dental implants provide a natural-looking and seamless smile restoration, enhancing your overall appearance.
