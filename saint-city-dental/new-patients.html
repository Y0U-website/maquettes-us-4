Let’s Get Started!

We’re excited to welcome you to Saint City Dental. Our goal is to make your first visit easy, informative, and completely comfortable. Here’s how to get started:

Fill out your digital forms online or on our iPads in-office.

Come in for your appointment and enjoy a personalized, judgment-free experience.

Leave with a clear understanding of your oral health and next steps.

What’s included in your first visit

  • Review of your dental and medical history.
  • Digital x-rays and intraoral images, if needed.
  • A gentle, thorough cleaning (age and health dependent).
  • Periodontal screening and cavity check.
  • One-on-one time with your dentist to review findings and answer questions.
  • A personalized care plan that fits your needs and comfort.

What to Bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card, if using insurance.
  • A list of current medications or health concerns.
  • Completed digital forms (or arrive a few minutes early to fill out in-office).
  • Questions you’d like to ask your provider. Any previous dental records, if available.

We accept most insurance and offer financing options

We work with most major dental insurance plans. If you’re unsure about coverage, our front desk team will help you navigate your benefits.

No insurance? No problem. We also accept cash, checks, credit cards, and offer flexible financing options to make care accessible. We’re committed to clear, upfront pricing with no surprise fees.
