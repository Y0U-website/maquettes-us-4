The practice

Welcome to Foothill Modern Dental! We work together to provide a personalized and relaxing experience in a modern environment, utilizing state-of-the-art technologies. Our dedicated staff is motivated to provide you with their knowledge and techniques to maintain your smile for life.

We take a conservative approach, and focus on what will work best for you and your lifestyle.

Dr. Sarno is a Southern California native who earned both her undergraduate degree in Biochemistry and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of California, Los Angeles . She further advanced her clinical training through a General Practice Residency at the UCLA Venice Dental Center , where she gained extensive experience in comprehensive and complex dental care.

At Foothill Modern Dental, Dr. Sarno’s approach is patient-centered dentistry. She believes great dental care begins with listening to the patient. Every treatment plan is carefully designed to be honest, transparent, and tailored to each patient’s goals, comfort, and long-term oral health.

Dr. Sarno takes a whole-person approach to dentistry, focusing not only on beautiful smiles but also on improving overall health, function, and well-being. Her goal is to provide modern, personalized dental care in Foothill Ranch, Lake Forest, and surrounding communities where patients feel heard, supported, and confident in their treatment.

My goal is for every patient to feel cared for, understood, and confident in their oral health.

Comprehensive dental care focuses on the health of the whole person—combining prevention, advanced treatment, and personalized care to support lasting oral health and overall well-being.

Services

From routine cleanings to full-mouth rehabilitation, we provide the full scope of modern dental care.

Same-day crowns use advanced digital scanning and CEREC technology to design, create, and place a custom ceramic dental crown in a single visit—restoring strength, function, and the natural beauty of your smile.

Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to gently straighten teeth and improve your bite—offering a comfortable, nearly invisible alternative to traditional braces. Dr. Sarno is a certified Invisalign provider for mild to moderate cases.

Cosmetic dentistry enhances the appearance of your smile through treatments like veneers, teeth whitening, and Invisalign—designed to improve confidence while supporting long-term oral health.
