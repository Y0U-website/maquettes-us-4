Crowns Dental crowns are custom-made restorations designed to protect and strengthen teeth that have been damaged, weakened, or compromised. A crown covers the visible portion of the tooth, helping restore its shape, function, and appearance.

Each crown is carefully created to provide a comfortable fit and a natural-looking result. The material can be matched to your existing tooth color, allowing the restoration to blend beautifully with your smile. Crowns may be recommended to restore a damaged or decayed tooth, protect a weakened tooth, or improve the appearance and function of your smile.
