Meet Our Team

Established in 1992, Central Dental Care offers complete dental care for every member of your family. Our practice is welcoming to patients of all ages, and our staff is outstanding. We are all committed to helping you and your loved ones achieve and maintain your best smiles. Our care is centered around providing each patient with accessible dental services and great customer service. We are truly patient-driven and will provide you with the guidance and treatments you need. We are your partners in obtaining a smile you are proud of!

Our Dentists

Dr. Jacob Henderson

Dr. Jacob Henderson is a native of Central, Louisiana. He earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans in 2002.

After completing the Louisiana State Board exam and the Western Regional Examining Board competency exam, Dr. Henderson began his private practice career in New Orleans and on the North Shore. He also gained valuable experience in children’s dentistry while working in a pediatric dental practice and has had the opportunity to provide dental care to underserved communities in Jamaica.

Dedicated to continuing education and providing comprehensive care, Dr. Henderson completed advanced surgical training in dental implant placement through the prestigious Misch International Implant Institute in 2008. He went on to achieve Fellowship status at the Institute and earned an additional Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. His advanced training allows him to offer his patients a wide range of options for restoring both the function and appearance of their smiles.

Outside of the office, Dr. Henderson enjoys spending time with his wife and two daughters and cheering on LSU athletics. As a lifelong member of the Central community, he is proud to care for the families in the community he has always called home.

Dr. Ellyn O’Neal

Dr. Ellyn O’Neal is a Louisiana native and proud to call Brusly her hometown. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge before receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans.

During dental school, Dr. O’Neal was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, a national dental honor society recognizing academic excellence and exemplary character.

Dr. O’Neal’s passion for dentistry began at an early age, inspired by her mother, Angie Marks, who served as a dental hygienist at Central Dental Care for more than 25 years. Following in her mother’s footsteps, she is excited to continue caring for the Central community and building meaningful relationships with her patients.

Outside of the office, Dr. O’Neal enjoys spending time with her husband, Hayden and their children, Maggie, Mason, and Parker. She looks forward to welcoming new patients, helping them achieve and maintain excellent oral health, and creating healthy, confident smiles for years to come.

Dr. Vincent K. Williams

Dr. Vincent “Kennon” Williams earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans in 2018. He has also recently completed the Core Curriculum with Spear Education on Comprehensive Facially Generated Treatment Planning.

After graduating from dental school, Dr. Williams moved to Southwest Louisiana, where he practiced in private dentistry for four years. In 2022, he and his family returned to the Baton Rouge area, and he was excited to join our team at Central Dental Care.

Dr. Williams and his wife, Brittany, have been married since 2018 and are proud parents to their son, Bennett, and their dog, Layla. Outside of the office, he enjoys traveling and cooking for his family.

Dr. Williams appreciates the caring atmosphere at our office and the way our team treats both patients and one another like family. He is passionate about building strong, lasting relationships with his patients and strives to provide comprehensive, personalized care to help each patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.
