Financial

Braces are an important investment and we want to make your decision to receive orthodontic care affordable. Our office offers complimentary consultations that include an examination, x-rays and a discussion of recommended treatment.

For your convenience, we offer a variety of flexible payment plans with low monthly payments. Payments are due each month, regardless of when appointments are scheduled. Check with our office staff if you have any questions or would like to obtain more specific information.

Automatic Credit Card Debit:

Monthly payments will automatically be charged to your credit card. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover, & American Express. Please provide us with your card information at your first appointment.

Cash Discount Option:

A full payment within 30 days of the initial banding appointment will result in a discount.

Insurance:

We accept and courtesy file most insurances. Please provide us with your insurance information at your first appointment.
