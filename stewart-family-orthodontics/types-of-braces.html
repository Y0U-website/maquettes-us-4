Types of Braces

Due to today’s technology, there are many options in the types of braces available to patients. The numerous options allow braces to easily fit into your lifestyle and needs.

Metal

Metal braces are the most common type of braces. They are made of high-grade stainless steel and are appropriate for all types of treatment.

Clear

Clear braces are ceramic braces that are clear or tooth-colored and are generally used by patients who want to minimize the visibility of their braces.

Self-Ligating Braces

Damon Q and Damon Clear are revolutionary new systems of braces that are not only faster for your orthodontist to use, they also can significantly shorten treatment time. They are great looking metal or ceramic brackets that are smaller for greater comfort. Plus, they offer better hygiene than conventional braces.
