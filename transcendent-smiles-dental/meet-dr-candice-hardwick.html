Meet Dr. Candice Hardwick

Dr. Candice Hardwick

At the age of 9, after a visit with the Orthodontist, my interest in dentistry was piqued. I truly enjoy that each patient I treat presents a unique problem-solving opportunity and a chance to achieve a healthy and more confident smile. I take pleasure in addressing patient’s concerns regarding their oral health and building long-lasting relationships.

Born and raised in East Point, Georgia, I am happy to be practicing so close to home. I completed my undergraduate studies at Albany State University (Hyperlink) where I earned a bachelor’s degree in biology. In pursuit of advanced studies, I attended Howard University for my Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. I then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program through NYU Lutheran Medical Center.

Here at Transcendent Smiles Dental, we are committed to delivering quality care. Your oral health is our top priority. Our team is comprised of caring individuals each with unique experiences and skill sets that allow us to curate a tailored engagement with the utmost comfort. Collectively we strive to ensure our patients are satisfied after each visit. We are attentive to each patient’s dental concerns and well-being.

I am committed to lifelong learning to provide the highest quality care. I endeavor to consistently improve my skills to provide the optimal treatment modalities and options for every patient. As such, I am a constituent of several professional organizations that provide opportunities for continuing education, including:

• Academy of General Dentistry • North Georgia Dental Society

When I am not at the office, I enjoy traveling, curling up with a good book, or watching a hilarious comedy. My favorite guilty snack is cheesecake topped with fresh strawberries. If I were not a Dentist, I imagine that I would likely have become an accomplished musician as I enjoy playing the violin, piano, and singing.
