If you are missing most or all of your teeth, All-on-4 dental implants may be a life-changing solution. All-on-4 uses just four strategically placed dental implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, offering a stable, long-term alternative to traditional dentures.

What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

A full-mouth restoration solution that replaces an entire arch of missing teeth using only four dental implants. These implants act as secure anchors for a fixed, natural-looking prosthetic arch. Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 implants are permanently secured in place, designed to look and feel like natural teeth, more stable for chewing and speaking, and long-lasting with proper care. Ideal for patients who have lost multiple teeth or are tired of uncomfortable dentures.

Benefits of All-on-4 Implants

By stimulating the jawbone, implants help maintain bone density and prevent a sunken facial appearance. For patients who require additional bone support, bone grafting may be recommended prior to implant placement.

  • Fewer implants required compared to traditional full-mouth implants
  • Faster treatment timeline in many cases
  • Improved chewing ability
  • Prevention of jawbone loss
  • Enhanced facial structure and appearance
  • No need for removable appliances

Are You a Candidate for All-on-4?

You may be a candidate if you are missing most or all teeth in one or both arches, currently wear dentures and want a fixed solution, have sufficient bone support (or are open to bone grafting), or want a long-term alternative to removable dentures. Even if you’ve been told you are not a candidate for traditional implants, All-on-4 may still be possible.
