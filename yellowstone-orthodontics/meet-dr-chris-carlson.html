Family Life

After many years of enjoying the mountains while on vacation, Dr. Carlson and his family are excited to call the Bozeman area home.

A native of Ironwood, Michigan, he met his wife Amy while they were both on the alpine ski team in college.

Married in 1995, they have two adult children, Lauren, who lives in Colorado, and Erik, who currently attends MSU.

Outside of the Office

When not in the office, Dr. Carlson enjoys alpine and backcountry skiing, traveling to new places, aviation (he’s held his private pilot’s license since age 16), and bird hunting.

He’s also an avid gravel and mountain biker, competing in his first Leadville Trail 100 MTB in 2026.
