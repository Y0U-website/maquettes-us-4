Orthodontics for Teens in Bozeman, Belgrade, & Livingston

Giving Your Teen a Healthy Smile

A confident smile can make a big difference in your teen’s life. It boosts self-esteem and helps them shine in school and social settings.

Every teen’s smile is unique, so Dr. Carlson creates personalized treatment plans designed to fit their needs and busy schedules.At Yellowstone Orthodontics, we take pride in helping teens feel great about their smiles and themselves.

Orthodontic Care for Teens

Your teen has more orthodontic options than ever before each designed to fit their unique needs, lifestyle, and confidence level.

From modern metal braces to subtle clear braces and nearly invisible clear aligners, Dr. Carlson works closely with you and your teen to choose the treatment that fits them best.

Together, we’ll find the right balance of comfort, convenience, and results to keep your teen smiling every step of the way.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a great choice for teens who want a low-profile, comfortable way to straighten their teeth. They offer the same powerful results as traditional braces just with the added benefit of being nearly invisible.

Because life happens, extra aligners are often provided in case a set is misplaced. With Dr. Carlson’s guidance, your teen can enjoy a flexible, effective treatment that builds a healthy, confident smile to last a lifetime.

Custom Braces

At Yellowstone Orthodontics, we create customized braces for each teen. Your teen can expect fewer adjustments, shorter appointments, and a more comfortable experience. Our custom braces also help reduce inflammation and treatment time—while delivering precise, beautiful results that last.
