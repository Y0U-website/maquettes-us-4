What are Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures are similar to traditional prosthetics but are secured to titanium posts as opposed to using natural suction and denture adhesive. These teeth can be fixed or removable depending on your preference, but it’s common for most patients to treat them just like they would their natural teeth, never taking them out.
