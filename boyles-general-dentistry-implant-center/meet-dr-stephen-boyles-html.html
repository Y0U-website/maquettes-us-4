What Does It Mean to Be an AGD Master?

Dr. Boyles earned both Fellowship and Mastership status with the AGD, or Academy of General Dentistry. In order to earn this distinction, he completed over 1,100 hours of approved continuing education courses in specific disciplines. He also completed a rigorous exam designed to demonstrate his expertise across different specialties that included about 250 multiple-choice questions on techniques, knowledge, and procedures.

Upon completing these extensive requirements, Dr. Boyles joined a very small group of general dentists (less than two percent) in the United States and Canada who are also AGD Masters.
