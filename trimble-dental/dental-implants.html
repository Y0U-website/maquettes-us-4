What Are Dental Implants

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone where a missing tooth once was. Over time, it fuses with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a permanent, stable anchor for a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration. The result looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth.

Implants are the only tooth replacement option that preserves jawbone structure and prevents the bone loss that accelerates when a gap is left untreated. At Trimble Dental, the team places and restores implants entirely in-house. Patients never need to be referred to an outside oral surgeon or travel to a separate provider for any part of the process.

Why Implants Are the Modern Choice

Benefit 1: Permanent Results Unlike dentures or bridges that rely on surrounding teeth or adhesives for support, implants are anchored directly in the jawbone. With proper care, they can last a lifetime.

Benefit 2: Complete Function Restored Implants allow patients to eat what they want, speak clearly, and smile without hesitation. Patients who were limited by missing teeth or unstable dentures often describe the difference as life-changing.

Benefit 3: Bone Preservation When a tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it begins to deteriorate. An implant is the only restoration that actually stimulates the bone, preserving facial structure and preventing the sunken appearance that develops over time.

Benefit 4: All-on-4 for Full-Arch Cases For patients missing most or all of their teeth, the All-on-4 technique provides a complete, fixed arch supported by just four implants, often completed in a single procedure.

Benefit 5: In-House from Start to Finish The Trimble Dental team handles every step of the implant process at Trimble Dental, from the consultation and imaging to placement, restoration, and follow-up care.

Your Dental Implant Journey

Step 1: Consultation and Imaging Your journey begins with a detailed consultation at Trimble Dental in Menomonie. The Trimble Dental team reviews your dental and medical history, takes 3D cone beam CT images of your jaw, and evaluates your bone density and overall oral health to confirm that implants are the right solution for you.

Step 2: Treatment Planning Using 3D imaging and computer-guided surgical planning software, the Trimble Dental team maps the exact position, angle, and depth of each implant before any procedure begins. You will see the plan on-screen and understand exactly what will happen at every stage.

Step 3: Implant Placement The Trimble Dental team places the titanium implant post into the prepared site using a precision surgical guide. For straightforward placements, the procedure is minimally invasive and more comfortable than most patients expect.

Step 4: Healing and Integration Over the following weeks, the implant fuses with the surrounding jawbone. The Trimble Dental team monitors your healing closely and will confirm complete integration before moving forward.

Step 5: Crown or Restoration Placement Once healing is complete, the Trimble Dental team attaches the custom crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration. Every restoration is designed to match your surrounding teeth precisely in color, shape, and size.

Step 6: Ongoing Care Implants are maintained just like natural teeth, with regular exams and cleanings at Trimble Dental and standard brushing and flossing at home.

FAQ’s

Most adults with adequate bone density and good general health are candidates for implants. The Trimble Dental team will evaluate your bone levels, gum health, and any underlying conditions during a consultation. Patients with insufficient bone may be candidates after a bone grafting procedure, which the Trimble Dental team also performs in-house.

Implant costs vary depending on the number of teeth being replaced, whether bone grafting is needed, and the type of restoration. Trimble Dental offers a variety of financing options so cost does not have to stand in the way of your care. Call the office to discuss your specific situation.

The full implant process, from placement through final restoration, typically takes several months to allow for proper healing and integration. In select cases, a temporary restoration can be placed the same day as the implant. The Trimble Dental team will provide a realistic timeline during your consultation.

Most patients are surprised by how manageable implant placement actually is. Local anesthesia is used throughout the procedure, and sedation options are available for patients who want additional comfort. Post-procedure discomfort is typically mild and well-controlled with over-the-counter medication.
