What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implants are a full-arch tooth replacement solution that uses just four strategically placed implants to permanently anchor a complete set of teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-4 restorations are fixed in place without adhesives, restore the look and function of natural teeth, and halt the bone deterioration that accelerates after tooth loss.

Why All-on-4 Is the Modern Choice

Benefit 1: Permanently Secured, No Adhesives Required All-on-4 restorations are anchored directly into the jawbone with four implants placed at precise angles for maximum support. There is nothing to remove at night, no adhesives to manage, and no worry about your teeth shifting when you eat, laugh, or speak.

Benefit 2: A Beautiful, Natural-Looking Result The prosthetic arch placed over your four implants is custom-designed to look completely natural. Patients consistently report that the result looks and feels far more like real teeth than any removable option they had previously tried.

Benefit 3: Bone Loss Prevention Implants replicate the function of natural tooth roots, stimulating the jawbone and preventing the deterioration that begins immediately after teeth are lost. All-on-4 is the only full-arch solution that actively protects your bone and facial structure over the long term.

Benefit 4: Often No Bone Grafting Needed The four implants in All-on-4 are angled specifically to maximize contact with available bone, which means many patients who would need grafting before conventional implants can proceed directly to All-on-4 without that additional step.

Benefit 5: Same-Day Temporary Teeth In most cases, the Trimble Dental team places a set of temporary teeth on the same day as your implant surgery, so you leave the office with a functioning, beautiful smile. Your permanent arch is delivered three to six months later once integration is complete.

Benefit 6: All Performed In-House Every part of the All-on-4 process, from planning and imaging to implant surgery and final restoration, is handled by the Trimble Dental team at the practice. No referrals, no traveling to outside providers, no starting over with someone new.

Your All-on-4 Journey

Step 2: Evaluation and Candidacy The Trimble Dental team examines your teeth, gums, and bone using digital imaging and 3D cone beam CT scans to assess your candidacy. Your provider determines whether All-on-4 is appropriate for your anatomy and, if so, whether any preparatory treatment is needed beforehand.

Step 3: Surgical Planning Using your 3D scans, the Trimble Dental team plans the exact position and angulation of all four implants digitally. This computer-guided planning ensures the implants are placed precisely, maximizing bone contact and setting up the final restoration for a perfect fit.

Step 4: Implant Placement and Same-Day Teeth On surgery day, the Trimble Dental team places all four implants and attaches a temporary set of teeth at the same appointment. You leave the office with a complete, functioning smile. Sedation options are available for patients who want to be fully comfortable throughout.

Step 5: Healing and Integration Over the next three to six months, your implants fuse with the surrounding jawbone. The Trimble Dental team monitors your progress with follow-up appointments and imaging to confirm that full integration has occurred before moving to the final step.

Step 6: Your Permanent Arch Once integration is confirmed, the Trimble Dental team secures your custom permanent prosthetic arch. The final result is a complete, beautiful smile that is fixed in place, maintained like natural teeth, and designed to last for decades with proper care.
