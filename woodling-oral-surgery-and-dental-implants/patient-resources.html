Patient Resources

When you come to our office for the first time, before you ever sit in the dental chair, you’ll have a chance to meet Dr. Woodling, our entire team, and go on a tour of the office so you can immediately begin to feel comfortable. You’ll then sit down with Dr. Woodling who will ask you about your medical history as well as any pressing dental concerns you might have.

From there, you’ll discuss what you would like to accomplish with Woodling Oral Surgery.

All treatment plans will begin with a thorough review of treatment with our Treatment Coordinator, who will be at every appointment with you. From there, Dr. Woodling will carry out the plan you both decided at earlier appointments. At any time, you can ask for things to be adjusted so they better suit you.

Patient Forms Patient Forms After you have scheduled an appointment with us, you can help yourself save time in the office by filling out our patient forms before you’re scheduled to come see us. Simply click on the link below and complete the forms at your leisure. When the day of your visit arrives, just bring the forms with you, and we’ll get you in to see Dr. Woodling right away! Please let us know if you have any issues accessing our forms.

Financing Financing Would you like to break up the cost of your dental care into smaller monthly payments? We can help you do exactly that in just a few clicks. Through our website, you can qualify for a low-to-no-interest payment plan with CareCredit, our third-party financier that has already helped millions of people nationwide easily afford their dental care. They have a variety of plans that can work for any budget, and you’ll never have to deal with any hidden fees, unexpected charges, or prepayment penalties.

Accepted Insuances Accepted Dental Insurance Dr. Woodling understands that a patient s financial worries should not take precedence over their oral health requirements, as delaying treatment may lead to more severe, costly, and painful issues. At Woodling Oral Surgery, we accept various major dental insurance plans and will assist you in maximizing your benefits by filing claims on your behalf.

Typically, dental insurance plans cover extractions as advanced restorative care at around 50 % of the cost. However, coverage percentages may vary depending on the specific plan.
