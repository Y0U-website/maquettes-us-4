General Dentistry-Patient Check-ups and Reconstructive Services

As a part of proper dental care, we encourage our patients to brush and floss along with a six-month routine cleaning appointment. This will allow us to catch major problems before they happen and help you maintain a healthier smile. A professional cleaning includes x-rays and a complete exam that will help detect any issues, such as gum disease, cavities, or decay.
