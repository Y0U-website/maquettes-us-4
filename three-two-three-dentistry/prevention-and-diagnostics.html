Prevention & Diagnostics

  • Why do I need X-rays if my teeth look and feel healthy? Not every dental problem can be seen or felt. X-rays can reveal issues below the surface, such as cavities between teeth, changes in the bone, infections, or developing problems. They give our team a more complete picture so we can focus on prevention and address concerns early.
  • How often do I need digital X-rays? There is no one schedule that works for everyone. The frequency depends on factors such as your age, dental history, current oral health, and risk for developing problems. Your dentist will recommend X-rays based on what is appropriate for you rather than taking them unnecessarily.

COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT INTRA-ORAL PHOTOGRAPHS

  • What are intra-oral photographs? Intra-oral photographs are high-resolution images taken inside your mouth using a small dental camera. They allow both you and your dentist to see detailed images of individual teeth, gums, existing dental work, and areas that may need attention.
  • Are intra-oral photographs uncomfortable? No. Taking intra-oral photographs is quick, non-invasive, and comfortable. The small camera is gently positioned inside your mouth to capture detailed images without the use of radiation.
  • Why does my dentist take intra-oral photographs? Intra-oral photographs help document your current oral health and make it easier to identify and explain concerns such as cracks, worn teeth, damaged restorations, or changes in the gums. They can also be compared over time to help monitor your smile and guide future treatment.

COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT iTero Scans

  • What is an iTero scan? Yes. Digital dental X-rays use very low levels of radiation, and they allow our team to capture detailed images quickly. We only recommend X-rays when they are appropriate for your individual oral health needs.
  • Are iTero scans comfortable? Yes. iTero scanning is quick, comfortable, and non-invasive. Instead of biting into trays filled with impression material, a small scanning wand is gently moved around your mouth to capture the images needed to create your digital model.
  • What are iTero scans used for? iTero scans can be used for a variety of dental treatments, including clear aligners, crowns, bridges, restorative dentistry, and monitoring changes in your teeth and bite. The detailed 3D images also make it easier for your dentist to explain recommendations and help you understand your treatment options.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Oral cancer screenings are an important part of protecting your long-term oral and overall health. During your examination, our team carefully checks your mouth, tongue, gums, and surrounding tissues for unusual changes that may require further evaluation. At Three Two Three Dentistry , we include oral cancer screenings as part of preventive care to help identify potential concerns as early as possible.

COMMON QUESTIONS ABOUT Oral Cancer Screenings

  • What happens during an oral cancer screening? During an oral cancer screening, your dentist examines your lips, tongue, gums, cheeks, throat, and other areas of your mouth for unusual sores, discoloration, lumps, or changes in the tissues. The screening is typically quick and can be completed as part of a routine dental examination.
  • How often should I have an oral cancer screening? Oral cancer screenings are often performed during routine dental exams. Your dentist may recommend a screening schedule based on your individual health history and risk factors.
  • Why are oral cancer screenings important? Oral cancer can sometimes develop without obvious symptoms in its early stages. Regular screenings give your dental team an opportunity to identify suspicious changes early and recommend additional evaluation when necessary.
