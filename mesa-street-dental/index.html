The practice

Missing most or all of your teeth can affect how you eat, speak, smile, and feel every day. All On 4 Dental implants in El Paso, TX may help restore a full arch of missing teeth with a stable, implant-supported solution.

At Mesa Street Dental, we help patients explore full-arch implants, fixed dentures, implant dentures, and permanent tooth replacement options. If you want a secure denture alternative or full smile restoration, your visit starts with a personalized consultation.

All On 4 implants may be used for patients who are missing all teeth, have failing teeth, or want a more stable alternative to removable dentures.

All On 4 treatment uses four strategically positioned implants to support a fixed full-arch restoration. These implants act as artificial tooth roots and help hold the replacement teeth securely in place.

Unlike traditional removable dentures that rest on the gums, an All On 4 restoration is supported by implants placed in the jaw and can only be removed by a dental professional.

If you are missing only one or several teeth, traditional dental implants may be a better option.

All On 4 is not right for every patient. Our El Paso dentist will check your gums, bone support, bite, medical history, and overall oral health before recommending treatment.

Whether you have extensive tooth loss, failing teeth, or uncomfortable dentures, Dr. Sean Harrell can evaluate your jawbone, oral health, and treatment goals to determine whether a fixed full-arch restoration is appropriate.
