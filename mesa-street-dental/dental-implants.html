Dental Implant Options We May Discuss

Single Dental Implant

One titanium implant and a custom crown can replace a single missing tooth without using neighboring teeth for support.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Two or more implants can support a bridge that replaces several neighboring missing teeth.

Implant-Supported Denture

Implants can help secure a removable denture and provide greater stability than a traditional denture.

Fixed Full-Arch Restoration

Several implants can support a fixed restoration that replaces an entire upper or lower arch of teeth.

All-on-4® Dental Implants

Four strategically positioned implants to support a fixed full-arch restoration.

The right option depends on the number of missing teeth, jawbone support, oral health, medical history, budget, and preferred final restoration.
