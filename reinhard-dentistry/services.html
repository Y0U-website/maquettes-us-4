The office provides a broad mix of services so patients can stay with one trusted team as their needs change.

Preventive & diagnostic

Comprehensive evaluation / oral examination

Visual exams and radiographs help detect cavities, issues with existing dental work, gum recession, bone recession, and other abnormalities in the mouth, head, and neck.

Dental cleaning

Cleanings remove plaque and tartar using ultrasonic instruments, hand scaling, and polishing to support gum health and cavity prevention.

Custom mouth guards

Custom athletic mouth guards provide a more precise, comfortable fit than generic sporting-goods options and can be especially valuable for athletes wearing braces.

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening

Both in-office and take-home whitening options are described in the existing site content.

Bonding

Tooth-colored bonding repairs or reshapes teeth while preserving a natural appearance.

Inlays and onlays

Porcelain or composite partial restorations can be used when a filling is not enough and a full crown is not yet necessary.

Invisalign

Clear aligners gradually move teeth without metal brackets or wires and can address crowding, spacing, crossbite, overbite, and underbite.

Veneers

Porcelain veneers recreate the natural look of teeth while improving color, symmetry, and overall smile aesthetics.

Dental implants

Implants replace tooth roots and create a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth.

Restorative dentistry

Composite fillings

Tooth-colored fillings restore cavities in a natural-looking material, often in a single visit.

Crowns

Crowns fully cover and protect severely damaged teeth and are typically completed across two visits.

Bridges

Bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring a false tooth to neighboring teeth.

Dentures

Complete and partial dentures restore function and appearance for patients missing some or all teeth.

Root canals

Root canal therapy removes infected or inflamed pulp, disinfects the inside of the tooth, and seals it before restoration.

Extractions

When a tooth cannot be saved, extraction may be necessary before healing and replacement planning.
