About Our Practice

Established in 1973, Kirkland Family Dental Care is honored to provide excellent dentistry to the local community. Our mission is to deliver exceptional dental care to patients in a gentle, caring atmosphere.

You will never be just another chart number at Kirkland Family Dental Care . You will be welcomed with a smile and seen on time for your appointment. Our doctors use the latest techniques and modern dental technology to ensure each patient receives the best care available. Our beautiful office overlooks Juanita Beach Park and has relaxing views of the serene setting of Juanita Creek.

Healthy smiles are made here! Book your appointment now .

Kirkland Family Dental Care is located at 9750 NE 120th Pl STE 2, Kirkland, WA 98034 . If you have any questions about our practice or would like to schedule an appointment, please give us a call or text at (425) 823-8300 . We look forward to meeting your family!

Our team is proud to be involved with several professional dental organizations & programs.

Dr. Navneet K. Chahal (she/her)

Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Chahal completed her Bachelor in Dental Surgery (BDS) in India, and she earned her Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry Degree at Boston University. With over 10 years of dental experience, Dr. Chahal excels at providing comprehensive dental care to all patients in an environment that is safe and comfortable. She has been consistently providing the best care possible to all of her patients while valuing compassion and respect above all.

She is an active member of the American Dental Association, Washington Academy of General Dentistry, Washington State Dental Association, Alleman Center Of Biomimetic Dentistry and King County Dental Association. In order to care for her patients using the latest advances in dentistry, Dr. Chahal is always learning from the best in the field by attending continuing education courses with Spears Study Club and Harvard University CE programs.

In her free time Dr. Chahal likes to do yoga, try new recipes, and spend time with her two beautiful daughters (Avnoor and Anayat) and her husband Dr. Singh. Please stop by to say hello to Dr. Chahal, she is looking forward to helping you with all of your dental needs!

Dr. Jagjeet Singh (he/him)

Doctor of Dental Medicine

Dr. Jagjeet Singh is a highly experienced dentist who has been serving patients in Washington State since 2013. A graduate of Boston University School of Dental Medicine (2009), Dr. Singh is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident, and beautiful smiles.

He has advanced training in dental implants, dental surgeries, All-on-X full mouth reconstruction, porcelain veneers, and cosmetic dentistry. To ensure his patients always receive the highest level of care, Dr. Singh regularly attends countless continuing education courses and advanced dental training programs, staying at the forefront of the latest techniques, materials, and technology in modern dentistry.

Patients appreciate Dr. Singh’s caring approach, gentle touch, and ability to explain treatment options clearly. He believes in building long-term relationships and making every patient feel comfortable during their dental care journey.

Outside the office, Dr. Singh enjoys spending time with his wife (also a dentist) and their two daughters. He is passionate about yoga, fitness, hiking, outdoor activities, and traveling.

If you are looking for an experienced and compassionate dentist in Washington who specializes in dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, and full mouth reconstruction, Dr. Jagjeet Singh is here to help you smile with confidence.

Dr. Jagjeet Singh
