Dr. Amanda Dibble - General Dentist

Dr. Amanda Dibble believes that a healthy, beautiful smile has the power to change lives. As a family dentist, she is dedicated to providing gentle, high-quality care that focuses on both oral health and overall wellness.

She takes pride in helping her patients achieve naturally stunning smiles and finds deep satisfaction in making a positive difference in their lives.

Her journey into dentistry began after earning a Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics from the University of Colorado at Boulder. She then completed her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine in 2009, followed by Advanced Education in General Dentistry through the United States Air Force. Dr. Amanda served four years on active duty, stationed at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base in Ohio and Minot Air Force Base in North Dakota, before returning to the West Coast with her husband, Dr. Pat Dibble.

Dr. Amanda is committed to staying current with the latest dental treatments and technology through continuing education. Her advanced training includes infection control for dental and hospital settings, traditional orthodontics and clear aligner therapy, and oral sedation. She is also an active member of professional organizations, including the Kois Center Graduate program, the Interdisciplinary Study Group Northwest, and the USAF Reserves, which provide ongoing opportunities for learning and collaboration.

Drs. Amanda and Pat Dibble live in Auburn, WA, with their two daughters, Regan and Addison. Outside of the office, Dr. Amanda enjoys spending time with her family and supporting the activities of her daughters, Regan, as a competitive dancer and Addison as a horseback rider and member of the local track club.

If you would like to schedule an appointment with Dr. Amanda or have any questions, please get in touch with our office today.
