0 Welcome to Stadium Dental At Stadium Dental, we are committed to creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere for all our patients. Our focus is on delivering top-quality general, cosmetic, and pediatric dental services to meet your unique needs and preferences.

Our team is dedicated to ensuring that your dental experience is as comfortable and stress-free as possible. We understand the importance of personalized care and strive to make every visit a positive and enjoyable one for you and your family.

I was born and raised in Jefferson City and am so grateful to practice dentistry in my hometown. I graduated from Jefferson City High School then attended Mizzou where I obtained a Bachelor’s in Biology. I attended the University of Missouri – Kansas City School of Dentistry.

I returned to Jefferson City after graduating dental school and practiced for 2 and a half years before opening Stadium Dental in 2024. I am thrilled to have the opportunity to provide high quality dental care to this amazing community.

I married my high school sweetheart, Taylor, and we enjoy spending time with our daughter, Callie, and our dog, Koda. During our free time, we enjoy camping, traveling, and hiking. We truly enjoy living in Jefferson City, and I look forward to providing dental care to this community for many years to come!
