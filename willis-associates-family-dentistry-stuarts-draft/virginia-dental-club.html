Virginia Dental Club — the questions we hear most.

Is Virginia Dental Club dental insurance?

No — and that&#x27;s the point. It&#x27;s a flat-rate membership program run by our practice, not an insurance plan. There are no deductibles, no annual maximums, no waiting periods, no claims to file, and no surprise denials. You pay a simple monthly fee, your preventive care is included, and everything else is discounted at the visit.

What does Virginia Dental Club cost?

Pricing is set per person and varies based on plan tier and family size. Current rates are listed on the Virginia Dental Club sign-up page, and the front desk can quote your exact monthly cost based on your situation. Families and households signing up together usually save the most relative to standard fees.

What&#x27;s included with the membership?

Routine cleanings (or periodontal cleanings when needed), comprehensive and recall exams, all routine X-rays, oral cancer screening, and emergency exams during clinical hours. Beyond that, you get meaningful discounts on fillings, crowns, root canals, dentures, implants, whitening, and Invisalign — basically everything else we do.

Can I use Virginia Dental Club with my dental insurance?

Virginia Dental Club is designed as an alternative to insurance, not a supplement. If you already have dental benefits through work that cover most of your care, you probably don&#x27;t need the membership. It&#x27;s built for the Stuarts Draft families and individuals who don&#x27;t have dental coverage at all or have plans that leave them paying mostly out of pocket.

How do I sign up?

Two ways. You can sign up online through the Virginia Dental Club portal in about five minutes — pick your plan, enter your details, and your benefits start the same day. Or call our front desk at 540-414-8423 and we&#x27;ll walk you through it over the phone or in person when you come in.
