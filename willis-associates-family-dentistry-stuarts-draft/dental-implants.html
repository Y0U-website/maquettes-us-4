Dental implants — the questions we hear most.

How long does a dental implant take from start to finish?

Most single-tooth implants take four to six months from placement to final crown. The implant post goes in during a short surgical visit, then the bone heals around it for three to four months before you come back for the permanent crown. We give you a realistic timeline at your consult so you know what to expect at every step.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with reasonable bone and gum health are good candidates. Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, or heavy bone loss can complicate things, but they rarely rule implants out entirely. Dr. Weatherwax takes a 3D CBCT scan at your consult so you get a clear answer based on your own anatomy, not a guess.

Does the implant surgery hurt?

The surgical visit itself is done under local anesthesia, with nitrous or oral sedation available if you&#x27;d like to be more relaxed. Most patients tell us the recovery felt closer to a tooth extraction than anything dramatic. Mild soreness for a couple of days, managed with ibuprofen, is the usual story.

What if I&#x27;ve already lost bone where the tooth used to be?

Bone loss is common when a tooth has been missing for a while, and it doesn&#x27;t disqualify you. A bone graft can rebuild the site before the implant is placed, or sometimes during the same visit. Dr. Weatherwax handles grafting in-house, so you won&#x27;t be sent to a separate specialist for it.

Will my insurance cover any part of an implant?

Some dental plans cover part of the crown that sits on top, and a few cover part of the implant itself, but most leave a meaningful balance. We verify your coverage before treatment and walk you through every number. Virginia Dental Club members get a discount on implant work, and CareCredit financing is available too.
