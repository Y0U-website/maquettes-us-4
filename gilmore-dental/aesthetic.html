Aesthetic Enhancement Services

We use advanced intraoral cameras, panorex 3-D cone beam scanning and imaging, CEREC technology, and the Isodry® dental isolation system to yield accurate results for your peace of mind.

Veneers are an excellent way to redesign and reshape your entire mouth with very thin ceramic shells that create a fuller and brighter smile by covering imperfections on the front surface of teeth and enhancing their color, shape, length, and size.

Have you ever thought: “I don’t like my smile"? Sometimes we think "it is what it is" or "I’m just stuck with it.”

With the exciting advances in technology and materials, that couldn’t be further from the truth. Dr. Gilmore works closely with patients to deliver the smile they’ve always dreamt of. Aesthetic enhancement starts with photographs and an impression to create a model of your future smile, serving as a blueprint for your makeover. You’ll have control over the shape, size, and color, allowing for modifications before the final design. It’s a fascinating process with you in the driver’s seat!

Who likes impressions that make you gag? Say goodbye! Digital impressions allow us to save the files for easy remakes for Occlusal Guards, Retainers, bridges, crowns, dental designs, bleaching and more.

Digital Impressions are a great alternative to conventional impressions since they reduce your time in the chair and give us a more accurate impression of your teeth.

With our in-house Sprint Ray 3D Printer, we can speed up the process of getting your Smile Design, Occlusal Guards and Retainers. We can have them ready for you the same or next day without the hassle of having to send those cases to the lab.

We store files for easy reprints too! Consider joining our Retainer Program for quick and affordable retainer replacements.

KöR Whitening is the only system proven to effectively whiten tetracycline-stained or "dark" teeth in a short time, and a unique advantage is that it can be done at home! The unique KöR-Seal™ trays are made of thin clear flexible vinyl which allows comfortable wear for most patients, even while sleeping.

Suitable for all ages, KöR Whitening helps teeth absorb oxygen to dissolve stain molecules, resulting in a bright, healthy smile without harming your teeth or gums. Get your bright smile back and restore your self-confidence with KöR Whitening!

Ortho FX Clear Aligners offer a convenient solution to achieving a beautiful smile with their NiTime system. By wearing the aligner for only 9-12 hours at night, you can enjoy the freedom to eat, drink, and talk hassle-free during the day.

Say goodbye to traditional aligner constraints and embrace a more flexible and effective method of aligning your teeth for the smile you’ve always dreamed of!
