The practice

Located in the heart of Redmond, OR, our practice welcomes a community of patients who leave with bright smiles. We pride ourselves on providing dental care that stands out from the rest. Our passionate dentist and friendly staff ensure every appointment feels comfortable and enjoyable.

I dreamt of becoming a dentist as a young boy. Through a family friend, I was introduced to dentistry and I knew from that moment it was what I wanted to pursue.

My undergraduate studies were completed at Washington State University and Graduate studies at Tufts University in Boston, MA.

I’m very blessed to have a beautiful wife and enjoy living and working in Central Oregon.

Our family recently expanded! We now have an incredibly beautiful 16 year old (driving!!) and decided to start over with an absolutely darling baby girl who came to us in December 2021! We are beyond blessed.

I would love the opportunity to meet you, earn your trust, and become your long term dentist.

My philosophy is simple, to give you all the education you want and need so that you can make an informed decision about your oral health and treatment needs. Giving you all your options allows you to remain in control. I also believe in giving it to you straight. You deserve to know the truth about your oral health.

Together, we can determine what treatment is going to be best for you.
