Meet Dr. Jamie Park

Experience. Integrity. Excellence.

I would like to thank my patients for putting your trust and confidence in me as your dentist for the past 20+ years of dental practice. I’ve enjoyed working with each of you, and I’ve truly celebrated with you whenever you brought back stories of compliments that you received from friends and family, or from colleagues and clients at work, following a cosmetic treatment.

Although cosmetic dentistry is one focus of my practice, a major focus of my practice will continue to be in providing general and family dentistry -- helping our patients achieve and maintain overall dental health and excellence.

I truly believe that a striking smile is the best accessory that any of us have. The power of a great smile, and the negative impact of an embarrassing smile, is not to be underestimated. Believe it or not , many of us live our daily lives hiding our true smiles or subconsciously smile with as little teeth showing as possible.

I’ve had the pleasure of working with many patients to design and achieve terrific smiles. These patients usually are amazed at how large and often their smiles appear afterwards, and frequently comment at what a "smiley" person they’ve become! I couldn’t be happier for them, and I’m thankful that I was able to be a part of that transformation.

I encourage you to come and experience true excellence, experience & integrity in dentistry. I look forward to welcoming you to my office.
