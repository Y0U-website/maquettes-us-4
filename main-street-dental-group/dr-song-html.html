Meet Dr. Silvia Song

Experience. Integrity. Excellence.

Dr. Silvia Song is originally from Horsham, PA - a small suburb outside of Philadelphia. She completed her undergraduate education at Gettysburg College where she studied both biology and studio art . Dr. Song’s outstanding talent in the arts combined with her love for the sciences led her to pursue a career in dentistry, and are still extremely valuable to her and her patients today

After graduating from Gettysburg College, Dr. Song returned to the Philadelphia area to attend dental school at Temple University where she excelled in both pre-clinical and clinical course work.

Dr. Song’s exceptional hand skills , thoughtful problem solving, and eye for aesthetics enable her to provide the highest quality of dental work for her patients. Dr. Song holds herself to a very high standard when it comes to compassionate care , excellent craftsmanship , and predictable outcomes .

Outside of the office, Dr. Song enjoys taking care of her many plants and spending time at home with her husband. She is an avid coffee lover and enjoys making coffee at home as well as visiting different cafes in the area.
