Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How much do dental implants cost in Laredo, TX?

Implant cost depends on whether you need bone grafting, the type of restoration, and how many teeth are replaced. Davenport Dental gives a clear estimate at your consultation and offers financing options.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Most implants take 3–6 months from placement to final crown, allowing the implant to fuse with the jawbone. Davenport Dental will give you a timeline specific to your case at your Laredo consultation.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth and enough jawbone are good candidates — a short exam confirms it. Request a consultation at Davenport Dental in Laredo, TX to find out.

Is getting a dental implant painful?

Placement is done under local anesthesia, and most patients report less discomfort than expected — similar to an extraction and managed with OTC pain relief. Davenport Dental focuses on keeping you comfortable throughout.
