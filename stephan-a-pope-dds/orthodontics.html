Orthodontic Treatment FAQs

How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?

Treatment length varies depending on the complexity of alignment issues. Some cases may take several months, while others may require a longer treatment period.

Are clear aligners effective for straightening teeth?

Yes. Clear aligner systems such as SureSmile can effectively treat many alignment concerns while offering a more discreet orthodontic option.

Is orthodontic treatment only for teenagers?

No. Many adults choose orthodontic treatment to improve alignment and enhance their smiles later in life.

Will orthodontic treatment affect daily activities?

Most patients adjust quickly to orthodontic appliances. Clear aligners can often be removed temporarily for eating, brushing, and flossing.
