When Might You Need a New Implant Restoration?

In some cases, an existing implant may remain healthy, but the restoration on top becomes worn, loose, or damaged. You may need restoration services if you experience:

We evaluate your current implant and restoration, determine the underlying issue, and create a new, high-quality restoration that meets your needs. We also coordinate with local specialists for any required implant reattachment or surgical support.

  • A loose or broken implant crown or bridge
  • Visible wear or cracks on your current restoration
  • Difficulty chewing or discomfort when biting
  • An outdated or poorly fitted appliance

Our Implant Restoration Process

  • Consultation and Imaging We begin with a thorough exam and digital X-rays or 3D scans to assess your existing implant and bone health.
  • Design and Fabrication Using precision dental impressions , we craft your custom restoration. Material options include ceramic, porcelain-fused-to-metal, or zirconia.
  • Final Placement Your restoration is securely attached and adjusted to ensure proper bite and aesthetics. For patients receiving a full arch, we ensure the denture or bridge integrates comfortably with your implants.
  • Follow-Up and Maintenance We’ll review care instructions and schedule periodic checks to ensure the restoration lasts as long as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restorations

How long does a dental implant restoration last?

With proper care, implant restorations can last 10 to 15 years or longer. Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental cleanings help maximize their longevity.

What happens if my implant crown comes loose?

If your restoration feels loose or unstable, contact us right away. It may be a simple matter of re-cementing the crown or replacing a worn attachment.

Can I upgrade my existing denture to an implant-supported version?

Yes. Many patients with traditional dentures opt to transition to implant-supported versions for added stability and comfort. We&#x27;ll assess your eligibility and discuss the best restoration solution.

Will my new restoration look natural?

Absolutely. We match your restoration to your natural tooth color, shape, and alignment for a seamless result. Our cosmetic dentistry expertise ensures a natural look you can feel confident about.

Does dental insurance cover implant restorations?

Coverage varies depending on your provider and plan. Our insurance and financing page offers more information, and we’re happy to verify benefits and discuss payment options with you.
