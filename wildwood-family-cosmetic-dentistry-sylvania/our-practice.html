Honoring Our Heroes

Wildwood Family & Cosmetic Dentistry proudly gives back to those who have served our country. We offer a select number of free dental services, such as cleanings, extractions, and more, to US Veterans at our Toledo location on a first-come, first-served basis.

This is our way of showing appreciation for your service and sacrifice.

If you’re a veteran, join us and let us take care of you as a small token of our gratitude. Don’t forget to bring your military ID to verify your service. We look forward to serving you!

  • Date: Friday, November 13, 2026
  • Location : 3900 Sunforest Court, Suite 119, Toledo, OH.
  • Eligibility : US Veterans (Military ID required).
  • Services : Select dental services available for free.
  • Sign-up : First come, scheduled appointment required.

Flexible Financing with Cherry

Dental care should be accessible, and Cherry makes that possible. This convenient financing option lets you break your treatment cost into manageable monthly payments, with 0 % APR plans available for qualifying patients.

The application process is fast, simple, and won’t affect your credit score. Whether it’s a cosmetic upgrade or an unexpected procedure, Cherry helps you move forward with care on your terms.

Dr. Michael O’Neill, DDS, Owner

Dr. Michael O’Neill graduated with honors from Bowling Green State University and attended dental school at Ohio State University. He completed a General Practice Residency Program at the University of Toledo Medical Center and has been a practicing dentist since 2007.

Dr. O’Neill is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Seattle Study Club, the Ohio Dental Association (ODA), and the Toledo Dental Society. His goal as a dentist is to establish a lifelong relationship of trust with every patient by providing innovative dental care in a comfortable, relaxed atmosphere.

He and his wife, Jaime (also a dentist), have 4 children. Outside work, he enjoys fitness, cooking, skiing, wakeboarding, and spending quality time with his family.

Dr. Emily Spetka, DDS

Dr. Emily Spetka is an experienced and licensed general dentist by the State of Ohio. She earned her undergraduate degree in Biology and her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University. Following graduation from dental school, she completed a General Practice Residency in her hometown at the University of Toledo Medical College.

Dr. Spetka loves being a part of the Wildwood Family and Cosmetic Dentistry team. She is a compassionate person who cares for the health of her patients and looks forward to developing lifelong relationships while serving the Toledo community. She is dedicated to providing an excellent patient experience in a calm environment. Dr. Spetka is a member of the American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, a member of the Toledo Dental Society, and Spear Education. She has completed multiple courses in facial aesthetics providing wrinkle relaxer and is certified to place dental implants.

When not at work, she enjoys spending time with her kids and her dog. In her spare time, she loves hiking, cooking and baking, reading, restoring furniture, and spending time with friends and family.

Melissa, COO, EFDA, BS

Melissa joined the team in 2022. She has worked in this field since 1999 and uses her vast experience to foster the best patient care possible. Melissa earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Toledo and went on to study at the Case Western Reserve School of Dental Medicine.

Melissa is an outgoing, motivated, passionate leader with an enthusiasm for all things dental. From integrating operations to helping out at locations as needed, Melissa’s favorite part of the job is being able to assist with all aspects of care.

When she’s not at work, Melissa is the head cheerleading coach and substitute teacher for a local school district. She also owns ANA Farms, LLC, and is a co-owner of KNG Showlambs, LLC.
