Root Canal Therapy

When it comes to dental health, root canal therapy is a procedure that often gets a bad rap. However, it&#x27;s an essential treatment that can save your natural teeth and prevent the need for dental implants or bridges. Root canal therapy is a procedure performed by Dr. Daniel Krauss at South Shore Dentistry in Wellington.
