Visiting Our Practice

What To Expect

When you walk through the doors of South Shore Dentistry, you’ll be greeted by our kind-hearted, familiar team and a bright, relaxing atmosphere. Our highly modern office makes every dental visit with us streamlined and simple. After we get you checked in, Dr. Krauss and our team will revitalize and protect your smile with preventive care. Our private operatories feature comforting modern amenities, so you can sit back and relax while we treat your teeth. Dr. Krauss will then share his findings with you and provide a personalized treatment plan for flourishing oral health.
