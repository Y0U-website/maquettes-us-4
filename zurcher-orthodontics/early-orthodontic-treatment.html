When to Consider Early Orthodontic Treatment in Draper

Parents often wonder when the right time is to bring their child in for an orthodontic evaluation. According to the American Association of Orthodontists, children should have their first orthodontic check-up by age 7. By this age, enough permanent teeth have emerged, and the jaw has developed sufficiently for an orthodontist like Dr. Zurcher to identify potential issues.

Some signs that your child may benefit from early orthodontic treatment in West Jordan include:

If any of these issues are present, it is important to schedule an orthodontic evaluation with Dr. Zurcher as soon as possible. Early detection and treatment of orthodontic problems can significantly improve your child’s oral health and overall well-being.

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Mouth breathing
  • Thumb sucking beyond the age of 5
  • Speech difficulties
  • Protruding teeth
  • Crowded or misaligned teeth
  • Jaws that shift or make sounds
  • A bite that does not meet properly

What to Expect During Your Child’s First Visit

At Zurcher Orthodontics, we strive to make your child’s first orthodontic visit a positive and informative experience. During the initial consultation, Dr. Zurcher will comprehensively examine your child’s teeth, jaw, and bite. This may include taking X-rays, photographs, and impressions to get a complete picture of your child’s orthodontic needs.

After the evaluation, Dr. Zurcher will discuss the findings with you and recommend a personalized treatment plan if necessary. He will explain the proposed treatment in detail, including the expected duration, the type of appliances, and the benefits of early intervention. Our team is committed to answering your questions and ensuring you and your child feel comfortable and informed about the treatment process.
