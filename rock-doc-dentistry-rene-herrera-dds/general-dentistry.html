Children’s Dentistry

We believe strongly that dental visits don’t have to be traumatic for children. We take the time to reassure them and to alleviate any initial fears they may have.

General Dentistry

General dentistry is designed to keep your family’s teeth and gums as healthy as possible. It includes routine cleanings, checkups, and advice on oral hygiene best practices. Routine checkups are your first line of defense when it comes to maintaining good oral health. Nearly all dental issues can be prevented or delayed with proper care, and early detection significantly decreases your chances of developing serious problems. Gum disease, for instance, can be halted and even reversed if it’s caught in the initial stages.

Cosmetic Dentistry

We also help our patients achieve the smile of their dreams through state-of-the-art cosmetic dentistry. We can help with teeth whitening as well as implants and crowns for those suffering from stained or broken teeth.

Restorative Dentistry

We can perform dental extractions to remove teeth that are decayed to the extent that they can’t be saved and that are causing you pain, perform root canal treatments, and fit patients with bridges or dentures.

We’re open every weekday from 9 a.m. through 5 p.m. Please don’t hesitate to reach out to us at your convenience via phone or through our website for more information on how we can help ensure good dental health for you and your family.
