About Studio Smiles

Conveniently located in South Amboy, we are close to South Amboy High Middle School and just off Route 35. Our dental practice wants to restore and enhance your teeth, helping you maintain a natural look that is healthy and bright. Using conservative, state-of-the-art dental procedures, the result will be beautiful giving you long lasting smiles!

Our dental team can also provide comprehensive treatment planning, and use restorative and cosmetic dentistry to achieve your desired results. In the case of a dental emergency, contact our team immediately to see if we can find time in the schedule to see you for same-day treatment.
