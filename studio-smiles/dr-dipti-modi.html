Dr. Dipti Modi

Dr. Dipti Modi, a South Amboy family dentist received her DDS from the Columbia University School of Dental and Oral Surgery in New York . Since then, Dr. Modi has been dedicated to keeping up with the latest dental technology. Her continuing education focuses on dental implants , cosmetic dentistry and orthodontics . Dr. Modi is also certified in Invisalign and clear correct provider, the clear and invisible alternative to traditional metal braces.

Studio Smiles NJ is conveniently located near major highways like the garden state parkway, Route 9 and Route 35. There is also ample parking in front of the building. As practicing general dentist for over 25 years, Dr. Dipti Modi has loved serving the South Amboy community and will continue doing so for many years to come.

Whether you are looking for an entire dental cosmetic makeover, or simply a high quality general dentist for your regular cleaning and checkup, she and her staff will provide the services using state of the art technology, all while being in a friendly and caring environment. The office utilizes digital x-rays, which reduce your radiation exposure by up to 90 %.

Studio Smiles NJ see patients of all ages, starting from 1 years old to 100 years young! In her spare time, Dr. Modi likes to read, dance and exercise. She has two lovely kids and an extremely supporting husband.
