Dental Financing

Our dental office is partnered with US Bank through UniFi, a patient financing program that lets you break up the cost of your dental treatment into smaller, budget-friendly monthly installments. It’s an excellent option for patients without dental insurance or those in need of extensive care, and UniFi typically carries lower fees for patients than other financing options.

Applying only takes a few minutes, and most patients hear back almost immediately. Qualified applicants can take advantage of 0 % interest plans for up to 12 months, making it easy to move forward with the care you need without paying everything upfront.

To get started, simply scan the QR code with your phone’s camera and complete the short application. Prefer to talk it through first? Give us a call and our team will be happy to walk you through your options.
