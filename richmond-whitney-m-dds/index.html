Dr. Whitney Richmond is a Maple City native and graduate of Goshen High School. After earning her undergraduate degree from Purdue University and her doctorate at Indiana University School of Dentistry, she returned to join Dr. John Deuschle at a local practice just down the road from her childhood home. Today, she prides herself on providing quality, affordable dental care to her friends and…

In her spare time, Dr. Richmond is also an accomplished artist whose sculptures have been featured at Purdue University and throughout Goshen and Elkhart. She is an active member of the Goshen jeweler’s guild, and loves donating her time to charitable causes in her hometown.

Dr. Richmond is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and North Central Dental Society (NCDS), and is supported by a talented and friendly staff that began working together in 1983. Read below to learn more about them!

Ellen attended Goshen High School and Goshen College before joining our practice as a dental assistant to Dr. Deuschle in 1982. Ever since then, she’s been proud to take care of each of our patients as if they were part of her own family.

Ellen lives in Goshen with her husband Mike, and is a regular attendee at Brenneman Memorial Missionary Church. One of her favorite summertime activities is taking her five children to The Chief for ice cream, and enjoying the city’s parks with her three beautiful grandchildren.
