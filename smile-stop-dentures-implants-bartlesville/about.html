MEET OUR DENTISTS

John Lard, DDS

Dr. Lard was born and raised in Oak Ridge, TN.

He graduated from The University of Tennessee Knoxville with a BA in psychology and from the University of Tennessee Memphis College of Dentistry in 1999.

Dr. Lard and his wife Stacy are the parents of four children and have two grandchildren.

He is committed to continuing education and has maintained a strong emphasis on restorative, cosmetic, and dental implant dentistry along with orthodontics at our Bartlesville dental office.

Dr. Lard is concerned with the comfort and satisfaction of his patients and makes great efforts to that end.

Wes Thompson, DDS

Dr. Thompson was born and raised in Bartlesville, Oklahoma.

After graduating from Bartlesville High School, he served a two-year LDS mission in Vladivostok, Russia. He later graduated from Brigham Young University with a BS in Nutritional Science and from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry with a Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

He is thrilled to be back in Green Country helping the good people of this area. Dr. Thompson and his wife, Callie, have four children.

He continues to learn through continuing education courses to keep his skills and knowledge current with proven technologies and advancements. His continued training has focused on dental implant techniques and oral surgery as well as restorative and cosmetic dentistry at our Bartlesville dental office.

He strives to help every one of his patients feel at ease and satisfied with their dental health care. Dr. Thompson loves the family aspect of dentistry and hopes you will feel right at home with our dental family.
