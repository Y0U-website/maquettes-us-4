New Patients and Payment Information

New Patients

The first step in starting orthodontic treatment at our clinic is to schedule an initial consultation appointment with our welcoming staff. During this appointment, our orthodontists will determine whether orthodontic treatment is appropriate for you and what type of treatment is best suited for your case. For those who need orthodontic treatment, we offer a variety of comprehensive orthodontic services ranging from traditional metal or clear braces to Invisalign.

* To expedite the registration process, you may print and complete the “Ortho New Patient Form” below and bring this to your first appointment.

Unfortunately, we do not accept patients who :

* These patients may contact the orthodontics clinic at the UCLA Faculty Group Dental Practice where our full-time faculty members practice.

  • Wish to transfer from other offices or programs
  • Already have braces on their teeth
  • Wish to have only a retainer or a retainer replacement
  • Require services beyond the scope of teaching in the clinic

Payment Information

We accept all PPO insurances with Orthodontic coverage. Such insurance plans include Delta Dental, Guardian, Metlife, and more.

We also accept Medi-Cal/Denti-Cal and CCS insurance members, but in order to receive coverage patients need to meet certain requirements for orthodontic treatment. The requirements are as follows:

If you have any questions please do not hesitate to contact our office.

  • Patients need to be between the ages of 13 to 21 and must meet the necessary Denti-Cal points. This can be determined at the initial consultation.
  • Patients who are surgical cases or are craniofacial anomaly or cleft lip/palate cases.
  • We offer exceptional and compassionate care at affordable rates. What distinguishes us from other clinics is that we offer monthly payment plans with 0 % interest with a required initial deposit. We also provide insurance information to help you get the care you need.
  • There is a $50 fee for the initial consultation appointment.
