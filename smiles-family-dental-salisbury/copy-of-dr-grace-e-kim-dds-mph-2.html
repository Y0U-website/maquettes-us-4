OUR HISTORY

Smiles! Family Dental has proudly served the Salisbury community for decades. Founded by Joseph Heher, DDS, FIDC, the practice opened at its current Mount Hermon Road location and provided exceptional dental care for 54 years.

Following Dr. Heher’s retirement, Smiles! Family Dental reopened under Grace Kim, DDS, who is honored to continue his legacy of compassionate and high quality care.

Joseph M. Heher, DDS, FICD, embodies more than the title of a dentist; he represents a beacon of compassion, wisdom, and community spirit. Hailing from the heart of Maryland, his entry into the field of dentistry was serendipitous, ignited by a simple suggestion from his mother as he stood on the precipice of high school graduation. Little did he realize then that this recommendation would sculpt his life s purpose with precision and passion.

In 1972, Dr. Heher found his professional home, Smiles, in Salisbury, MD, where he seamlessly integrated himself into the fabric of the community. Known for his expert skills, genuine warmth, and infectious humor, Dr. Heher s practice became a sanctuary for countless individuals seeking not just dental care but also solace and camaraderie.

Beyond the confines of his clinic, Dr. Heher became a stalwart advocate for professional growth and altruism. Whether attending symposiums to stay abreast of cutting-edge advancements, imparting his knowledge to budding dentists at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, or volunteering his services at the Holly Center, his commitment to service others knows no bounds.

In March 2022, Dr. Heher confronted unforeseen health challenges, yet his unwavering dedication to his patients remained steadfast. Though fate dictated an earlier retirement than planned, Dr. Heher reflected on his career with profound satisfaction, knowing that he had left an indelible mark on the lives of countless individuals.

As he gracefully transitions into retirement, Dr. Heher leaves behind a legacy illuminated by acts of kindness, professionalism, and unwavering dedication to patient care. His philosophy, rooted in prioritizing the dignity and well-being of each individual, will continue to resonate within the hallowed halls of Smiles!.

Among the many highlights of Dr. Heher s illustrious career was witnessing the ascent of his mentee, Dr. Grace Kim. With boundless generosity and encouragement, Dr. Heher nurtured Dr. Kim s journey, confident in her ability to carry forth his legacy with the same fervor and commitment at Smiles!.

Here s to Dr. Joseph M. Heher not just a dentist, but a cherished friend, a revered mentor, and a cornerstone of our community. With heartfelt gratitude, we extend our deepest appreciation for the countless ways in which he has enriched our lives. Through his skilled hands and compassionate heart, he has not only brightened our smiles but also illuminated the path to a brighter, more compassionate world. ​ ​

Dr. Bruce A. Baxter, DMD, was a beacon of compassion and dedication in the field of dentistry. After graduating from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 1975, he embarked on a remarkable 48-year journey in Bath, NY, where his heart for his patients illuminated every aspect of his practice.

Specializing in General Dentistry, Dr. Baxter s commitment to his patients went beyond mere treatment; it was a profound connection rooted in genuine care and empathy. His warm demeanor and genuine concern for their well-being earned him not only their trust but also their deep respect and admiration.

Dr. Baxter s practice was more than just a workplace; it was a sanctuary where patients found solace in his gentle approach and unwavering dedication to their oral health. He took the time to listen to their concerns, educate them about their dental needs, and tailor treatments to suit their individual preferences and comfort levels.

Beyond his exemplary patient care, Dr. Baxter was also a pillar of support for his community. Whether through charitable endeavors or simply lending a compassionate ear, he touched the lives of countless individuals, leaving an indelible mark of kindness and generosity.

Furthermore, Dr. Baxter s heart for mentorship shone brightly throughout his career. Recognizing the importance of nurturing the next generation of dentists, he eagerly shared his knowledge and experience with aspiring young professionals, guiding them with wisdom and encouragement as they embarked on their own journeys in the field.

Beyond his professional endeavors, Dr. Baxter harbored a lifelong passion for art, which he initially explored during his formative years but set aside to pursue his scientific and healthcare pursuits. However, in 1997, he reignited his artistic flame, embarking on a serious painting journey under the tutelage of renowned Corning artist, Tom Buechner. Immersed in Buechner s Friday evening portrait group, Dr. Baxter honed his skills and embraced a distinctive style characterized by thick layers of oil paint, imbuing his works with a sense of loose, gestural expression.

Dr. Baxter s artistic repertoire spans various subjects, from captivating landscapes to intimate portraits, each bearing his signature blend of figurative representation and abstract design. His canvases pulsate with vibrant hues and dynamic compositions, capturing the essence of his subjects with honesty and compassion.

As he retired in August 2023, concluding a remarkable half-century career in dentistry, he left behind a legacy as a consummate healthcare professional. And he enjoys his retired life as a talented artist whose canvases breathe life into the world of art.

So here s to Dr. Bruce A. Baxter, our dentist, our friend, our mentor. Thank you for everything you ve done for us. You ve truly made our smiles brighter and our lives better. Your unwavering dedication to our oral health and your compassionate care have left an everlasting impact on our community. And as you embark on this new chapter of your life as a talented artist, may your canvases continue to inspire and bring joy to all who behold them. We are forever grateful for your kindness, guidance, and the warmth of your heart.
