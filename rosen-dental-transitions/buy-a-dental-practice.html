What to Consider When Buying a Dental Pratice for Sale

If you’re unsure how to assess a practice’s health through its management software, it’s essential to bring in an expert who can.

Your dedication, time, and financial investments in your career deserve more than a gamble. In collaboration with Rosen Summit Dental Advisors , we delve deep into the practice’s software, extracting 10-20 critical reports. This ensures you sidestep potential pitfalls, from mismanaged account receivables and inconsistent billing to overlooked insurance claims. Beyond safeguarding, we spotlight growth avenues by analyzing production metrics for each provider, procedure by procedure.

Can you acquire the practice, manage loan repayments, cover taxes, and still ensure fair compensation for your work?

If you’re uncertain about the financial aspects of purchasing a practice, it’s essential to seek guidance. We conduct thorough analyses to pinpoint expense categories that deviate from industry standards, helping you identify potential savings or areas that might require more investment. Drawing from our expertise in dental lending, we can suggest financing options tailored to enhance your practice’s cash flow, often without necessitating a down payment.
