Brent Kvittem, DDS, MS

Meet Dr. Brent

Dr. Brent, as he is called by the children who visit the office, lives in Prior Lake with his wife, Lisa. They have three grown daughters: Greta, Ava, and Ella. Dr. Brent grew up in Eagan and graduated from Rosemount High School in 1986. He then attended St. Olaf College. When he wasn’t studying or playing football, he enjoyed mentoring and helping local troubled middle schoolers with their problems.

Education and Professional Activities

Dr. Brent has been with Children s Dental Care since 1997, and a partner since 1999. He enjoys covering emergencies for the Minnesota State High School League, and assisting young at-risk athletes with mouthguard use.

Beyond the Office

Dr. Brent enjoys running, biking, and family time at the cabin.

A Message from Dr. Brent:

After 30 wonderful years practicing with CDC, I have made the decision to quietly ease into retirement.

I have been incredibly blessed and grateful for the opportunity to work alongside the exceptional families, employees, and doctors who have made CDC what it is today. It has always been a collaborative effort, and it is the dedication of all of us especially our families that has allowed CDC to grow into such a special place. Thank you for the trust you placed in me and for allowing me to be part of so many children s lives. It has been one of the greatest privileges of my life.

As for me, you will still see me around from time to time, as I ll continue to work a few days here and there. And if you happen to see me out and about, please don t hesitate to say hello. I m not going anywhere other than the occasional vacation and I look forward to crossing paths with many of you in the years ahead.

Thank you, from the bottom of my heart, for making these past 30 years so meaningful. It has been an honor to care for your children and to be part of this incredible community.
