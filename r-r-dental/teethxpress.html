What is TeethXpress?

When your natural teeth are too damaged to be treated, opting for replacement teeth is often the best option. TeethXpress, also known as All-on-4 or All-on-X, is a specific type of tooth restoration method that includes a full-arch dental implant, usually placed in a single appointment at the dentist’s office, to provide a natural-looking smile without the need for multiple procedures or months of waiting for the final result.

Who Can TeethXpress Help?

TeethXpress may help if you’re dealing with:

  • A full arch of missing teeth
  • Multiple failing or severely damaged teeth
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Difficulty chewing certain foods
  • Changes in speech caused by unstable teeth or dentures

TeethXpress can help restore the normal rhythm of daily life. You will be able to enjoy meals with fewer limitations, speak more comfortably, and stop thinking about your teeth every time you laugh, eat, or meet someone new.

How the TeethXpress Process Works

Step 1: Evaluation

Planning is a big part of a successful implant treatment. The dentist will examine your teeth, gums, bite, jaw structure, and overall oral health. Specifically, we’ll be making sure you don’t suffer from gum disease and have sufficient bone structure to support the implants.

We’ll also talk about what you want from treatment—whether that’s eating better, improving appearance, moving away from dentures, or a combination of goals. All of these details help shape the treatment plan for the best possible results.

Step 2: Personalized Treatment

Before the TeethXpress procedure, you may need to have damaged teeth removed. These extractions, as well as the rest of the procedure, will be done under local anesthesia.

TeethXpress uses a small number of strategically placed implants, usually four to six, to support a fixed full-arch restoration. We will carefully insert the implants into your jawbone and then attach an abutment to each implant. The abutments connect your dental prosthetic to each implant, providing a solid and sturdy base for your new smile. If you already have a well-fitting denture, we can even modify it to attach to your new implants instead of requiring a new denture to be made.

Step 3: Recovery and Next Steps

You may be prescribed a medicated mouthwash for a couple of weeks following the procedure. It will take a couple of months for your implants to fuse with your jawbone. We will provide post-op care instructions and make sure you know what to expect during recovery before and after the procedure.

Routine oral hygiene and dental cleanings are necessary to check that all is well for the long-term care of your implants. Otherwise, your new smile will not require special care once the healing process is finished!

FAQ About TeethXPress Same-Day Dental Implants in Hicksville, NY

Am I a candidate for TeethXPress?

Many adults may qualify, but candidacy depends on oral health, bone support, medical history, and whether any teeth need to be removed first. A consultation is required.

Are same-day dental implants really done in one day?

Yes. For qualified patients, implants can be placed in our office within a matter of hours.

Is TeethXpress different from All-on-4 or All-on-X?

TeethXpress, All-on-4, and All-on-X all refer to full-arch implant treatment. The big idea is the same: a complete set of replacement teeth is secured to a small number of dental implants. The exact number of implants depends on your jawbone, bite, oral health, and treatment goals. At your evaluation, we’ll explain how many implants you may need and whether TeethXpress is the right fit for your smile.

Is TeethXpress painful?

Most patients have minimal or no discomfort during surgery. Because the procedure is performed under local anesthesia, you will not feel any pain while the surgeon works. Some discomfort will occur as the anesthesia wears off, but this is normal and often less intense than expected.

How do I know if I need TeethXpress or another implant option?

That depends on how many teeth are missing or failing, the condition of your gums and jawbone, and what you want from treatment. If the problem is limited to one or two teeth, a different implant approach may make more sense. If you need a full-arch replacement and want a fixed option, TeethXpress may be the better fit.

How do I care for TeethXPress after healing?

R+R Dental will provide home-care instructions and schedule routine follow-ups. Brushing, professional cleanings, and regular exams help keep the implant restoration and gums healthy.
