The practice

Welcome to Aloha Dental! Aloha Dental exists for the benefit and convenience of our patients and their families. We are a diverse group of people serving a diverse group of patients in a welcoming environment. Our purpose is to take care of you to the best of our abilities in a family-friendly environment.

We offer same-day dental appointments whenever possible, and dental emergencies are seen the same day. Your time is important to us!

The latest technology radio-graphs are taken with minimum exposure. Intraoral photos are taken at every visit so you can visualize every treatment that may need to be completed. We are happy to provide you a copy of your color photos for you to take home.

We offer family-friendly care and will see a whole family at the same time. We want your children to have a great experience at the dentist s office. And yes! Parents are welcome to stay with their children in the back.

Aloha Dental prides itself on being able to complete most procedures in our office including extractions, bone grafting, TMJ treatment and root canals, to name just a few. There may be times when you may need to be seen by a specialist.
