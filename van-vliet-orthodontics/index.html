The practice

Dr. Martin Van Vliet and staff are passionate about creating healthy, functional, and beautiful smiles. Dr. Van Vliet is a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. He has served the local community for more than 35 years and is always excited to meet new patients.

"Van Vliet Orthodontics has provided the highest quality of patient care for over 40 years, and it is an honor to continue this tradition. With strong family ties to the Hudson Valley, it is my passion to always put patients at the heart of the practice. I am excited to brighten our community with beautiful and healthy smiles for the generations to come."
