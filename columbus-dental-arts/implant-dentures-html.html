What Are Implant Dentures?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as prosthetic tooth roots when they are surgically placed into the jawbone. They fuse with the surrounding tissue, so they can provide a strong base of support for the artificial teeth that get attached to them. There are a few different types of implant dentures, including fixed and removable options. During your consultation, we can help you determine which one best fits your unique situation.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

Fixed implant dentures are designed to stay in the mouth 24/7; they are only removed when you visit a dental professional. Many patients like the idea of having their smile in place around the clock. However, this option does require a greater number of implant posts than removable dentures.

Removable Implant Dentures

Removable implant dentures may need just 2 5 posts per arch to support prosthetic teeth. The teeth are held in place via ball- or bar-shaped attachments, and they are easy to remove for daily cleaning. This option combines the convenience of traditional dentures with the reliability and strength of dental implants.

The Implant Denture Procedure

The implant denture procedure includes four main steps:

Although the implant denture process can seem lengthy, it delivers amazing benefits that are well worth a longer treatment!

  • Consultation: We will determine if implants are right for you and start the treatment planning process.
  • Implant placement surgery: One of our partner specialists will place implant posts at pre-planned positions in your jawbone.
  • Osseointegration: Over a period of a few months, the implants will bond with your jawbone. You may need a second procedure to place attachments on top of your implants.
  • Restoration: Our team will design and place your beautiful new denture.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You might be a suitable candidate for implant dentures if:

Even if you are not sure whether you qualify for implant dentures, we encourage you to visit us for a consultation. Most patients can get this form of tooth replacement, either immediately or after some preliminary care.

  • You have lost all or most of your teeth throughout one of your dental arches.
  • Your jawbone is strong enough and large enough to support dental implants.
  • You are free of active gum disease and other conditions that might negatively affect your treatment’s outcome.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures offer some remarkable benefits:

  • Stability: You will not need to worry about your dentures slipping out of place.
  • Strength: In some cases, the bite force provided by implants is almost equal to that of natural teeth.
  • Beauty: We design implant dentures to complement the natural features of each patient.
  • Longevity: Dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime. The restorations that get attached to them can also stand the test of time.
  • Jawbone preservation: Dental implants prevent the jawbone deterioration that normally occurs after tooth loss.
