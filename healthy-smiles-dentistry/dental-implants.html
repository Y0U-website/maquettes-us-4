Dental Implants

If bone loss has occurred, you may still qualify for dental implants after procedures such as bone grafting or sinus augmentation. Thanks to advances in implant dentistry, more patients than ever are excellent candidates for treatment.

Dental implants in Flower Mound are surgical-grade titanium root replacements designed to provide a permanent foundation for replacement teeth. Once placed beneath the gums, these artificial roots integrate with the jawbone to create exceptional stability. A custom-designed dental crown is then attached to the implant, producing a restoration that closely resembles a healthy natural tooth in both appearance and function.

Unlike traditional dental bridges, implants do not rely on adjacent healthy teeth for support. This means your natural teeth remain untouched, preserving more of your healthy tooth structure. Dental implants also help prevent jawbone deterioration, maintain facial contours, and reduce the risk of additional tooth movement caused by untreated tooth loss.

Whether replacing one tooth, several teeth, or an entire arch, dental implants offer one of the most predictable and successful restorative dental treatments available today. Their combination of aesthetics, function, comfort, and durability makes them the preferred choice for both dentists and patients seeking a long-lasting tooth replacement solution.

Did You Know

Approximately 30 million Americans live without natural teeth in one or both jaws, making tooth loss one of the most common oral health concerns among adults. Fortunately, advances in implant dentistry have transformed the way missing teeth are replaced. Millions of people have already restored their smiles with dental implants, and that number continues to grow every year as more patients discover their long-term benefits.

Modern titanium dental implants were introduced during the 1950s after researchers discovered that titanium naturally bonds with living bone. This remarkable property revolutionized restorative dentistry and continues to make implant treatment one of the most successful procedures in modern healthcare, with success rates exceeding 95 % in many healthy patients when properly planned and maintained.

Interestingly, the concept of replacing missing teeth dates back thousands of years. Archaeologists have uncovered evidence showing that ancient civilizations, including the Egyptians, Mayans, and other early cultures, experimented with replacing missing teeth using carved shells, stones, and other natural materials. Today’s dental implants are the result of decades of scientific research, advanced materials, and continually improving technology, making them safer, stronger, and more predictable than ever before.

Beyond restoring smiles, dental implants can also improve nutrition, speech, self-confidence, and overall quality of life. Many patients report feeling younger and more confident after completing treatment because they no longer have to hide their smiles or worry about loose dentures while eating or speaking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Choosing dental implants is an important decision, and it’s natural to have questions about the treatment process, recovery, and long-term results. Below are answers to some of the most common questions patients ask when considering dental implants in Flower Mound.

Are dental implants right for me?

You may be an excellent candidate for dental implants if you have one or more missing teeth, severely damaged teeth that cannot be restored, or dentures that no longer fit comfortably. The ideal candidate generally has healthy gums, adequate jawbone density, and is in good overall health.

Even if you’ve been told in the past that you weren’t a candidate because of bone loss, modern dentistry offers solutions such as bone grafting and sinus lift procedures that can often make implant treatment possible. Age alone is rarely a limiting factor. Many healthy adults in their 60s, 70s, and even 80s successfully receive dental implants and enjoy the benefits for many years.

The best way to determine your eligibility is through a comprehensive dental examination that includes digital X-rays or 3D CBCT imaging. Your dentist will evaluate your jawbone, gum health, bite alignment, medical history, and overall oral health before recommending the treatment option that’s best suited to your needs.

What should I expect if my dentist and I decide dental implants are right for me?

Dental implant treatment is completed in several carefully planned stages to ensure predictable, long-lasting results. While every patient’s treatment plan is unique, the process generally includes the following steps:

Initial Consultation Your dentist will perform a comprehensive examination, review your medical history, take digital images, and discuss your treatment goals. This appointment helps determine whether dental implants are the right solution for your smile.

Treatment Planning Using advanced digital technology, your dentist creates a personalized treatment plan that identifies the ideal implant location while avoiding nearby nerves and sinus structures. This precise planning helps maximize implant success.

Implant Placement The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Many patients are surprised to find that the procedure is more comfortable than expected and often involves less discomfort than a tooth extraction.

Healing and Osseointegration Over the next three to six months, the implant naturally bonds with the surrounding bone through a biological process called osseointegration. This creates the stable foundation needed to support the final restoration.

Abutment and Final Restoration Once healing is complete, a small connector called an abutment is attached to the implant. A custom-crafted porcelain crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture is then secured to complete your smile. The finished restoration is designed to match your surrounding teeth in shape, size, and color for a natural-looking result.

Throughout each phase of treatment, your dental team will monitor your healing and answer any questions to ensure a smooth and comfortable experience.

What type of post-treatment care will I require?

Following implant surgery, it is normal to experience mild swelling, tenderness, bruising, or slight bleeding for a few days. These symptoms typically improve quickly and can usually be managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain medications, cold compresses, and plenty of rest.

Your dentist may recommend eating soft foods such as yogurt, mashed potatoes, soup, smoothies, eggs, and pasta for the first week or two while the surgical site heals. Avoid smoking, drinking through straws, or eating hard, crunchy foods during the initial healing period, as these activities can interfere with recovery.

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is essential for long-term implant success. Brush twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss carefully around the implant, and attend regular dental cleanings and examinations. Just like natural teeth, dental implants require consistent care to keep the surrounding gums healthy.
