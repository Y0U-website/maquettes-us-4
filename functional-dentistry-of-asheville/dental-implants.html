What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts placed in the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Once healed, an implant supports a crown, bridge, or denture—restoring appearance and function in a way that closely mimics natural teeth.

As part of our restorative dentistry services, dental implants are planned with precision and care to integrate seamlessly with your bite and surrounding teeth.

Dental Implants Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants safe?

Yes. Dental implants are a well-established, safe treatment when planned and placed properly.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular care, implants can last many years—often decades.

Is implant placement painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. We use gentle techniques and ensure you’re comfortable throughout care.

Can implants replace dentures?

Yes. Implants can support dentures to improve stability and comfort.
