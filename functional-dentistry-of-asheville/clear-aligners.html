Clear aligners at Functional Dentistry of Asheville offer a discreet, comfortable way to straighten teeth and improve bite alignment—without traditional braces. Designed to fit seamlessly into daily life, clear aligners gently guide teeth into better position while supporting long-term comfort, function, and oral health.

Our approach goes beyond aesthetics. We evaluate how alignment affects your bite, jaw joints, and tooth wear to ensure results look natural and feel right.

What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are a series of custom-made, removable trays that gradually move teeth into proper alignment. Each set is worn for a short period before progressing to the next stage of movement.

As part of our orthodontics care, clear aligners are planned with precision using digital imaging to ensure predictable, controlled results.

Clear aligner treatment may help correct:

In some cases, improving alignment can reduce stress related to teeth grinding & TMJ treatment .

  • Crooked or crowded teeth

Why Clear Aligners Matter

How Clear Aligner Treatment Works

Your treatment begins with a conversation about your goals.

  • Comprehensive Evaluation We assess alignment, bite, and overall oral health.
  • Digital Imaging & Planning Digital scans are used to map tooth movement and design aligners.
  • Customized Treatment Plan We review timelines, expectations, and outcomes clearly.
  • Clear Aligner Wear Aligners are worn daily and changed periodically to guide movement.
  • Progress Monitoring We monitor alignment to ensure comfort and effectiveness.
  • Retention Phase Retainers help maintain results after treatment is complete.

Clear Aligners Frequently Asked Questions

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Treatment length varies, but many cases are completed within several months to a year.

Do clear aligners hurt?

Some pressure is normal when starting a new aligner, but discomfort is typically mild and temporary.

How often do I wear aligners?

Aligners are usually worn most of the day and removed only for eating and cleaning.

Can adults use clear aligners?

Yes. Clear aligners are popular with adults seeking discreet orthodontic care.

Will I need retainers afterward?

Yes. Retainers help maintain alignment after treatment.
