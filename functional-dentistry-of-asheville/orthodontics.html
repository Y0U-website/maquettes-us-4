What Is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is the area of dentistry focused on correcting misaligned teeth and bite issues. Proper alignment helps teeth function efficiently, reduces uneven wear, and supports long-term oral health.

At our practice, orthodontics is closely connected to restorative dentistry and TMJ care , allowing us to evaluate how alignment affects comfort, jaw balance, and stability throughout the mouth.

Why Orthodontics Matters

Misaligned teeth can place excess stress on certain teeth and jaw muscles. Over time, this may lead to:

Orthodontic treatment helps distribute forces more evenly across your bite, supporting comfort and long-term stability.

  • Increased tooth wear
  • Cracked or chipped teeth
  • Jaw joint strain
  • Headaches or facial discomfort
  • Gum issues due to difficult cleaning

Orthodontics Frequently Asked Questions

Are clear aligners effective?

Yes. Clear aligners are effective for many mild to moderate alignment concerns.

Is orthodontic treatment only for kids?

No. Many adults benefit from orthodontic care.

Does orthodontic treatment hurt?

Some pressure is normal as teeth move, but discomfort is usually mild and temporary.

How long does treatment take?

Timelines vary based on complexity. We’ll review expectations during your consultation.

Will I need retainers after treatment?

Yes. Retainers help maintain alignment after orthodontic care.
