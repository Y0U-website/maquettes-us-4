What Is a “Complex” Implant Case?

A dental implant case may be considered complex when standard placement is not straightforward due to anatomical, functional, or health-related challenges.

These cases require careful evaluation and a customized treatment plan—not a one-size-fits-all approach.

  • Significant bone loss
  • Long-term missing teeth
  • Multiple missing teeth
  • Previous implant failure
  • Gum disease–related tooth loss
  • Bite instability or heavy bite forces
  • Jaw joint (TMJ) involvement
  • Medical or structural considerations

Conditions We Commonly Address

Complex implant care may help patients who:

In many cases, complex implant care works alongside restorative dentistry to rebuild stability and comfort.

  • Have been missing teeth for many years
  • Have been told they lack sufficient bone for implants
  • Wear loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Have failing bridges or dental work
  • Have experienced implant complications in the past
  • Grind or clench their teeth
  • Have jaw discomfort or bite imbalance
