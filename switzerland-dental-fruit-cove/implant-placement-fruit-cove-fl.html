What is Implant Placement & How Does it Work?

An implant placement near you involves placing a small titanium post into the jawbone to serve as a new root for a missing tooth. After the bone integrates with the implant, the implant provides a firm foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

Comprehensive evaluation

Examination of the mouth, jawbone, and bite alignment.

Digital imaging

We use X-rays or 3D scans to see the jaw clearly and decide the best spot for your implant.

Implant placement

A tiny titanium post is carefully put into the jawbone while your area is numbed for comfort.

Healing and bonding

Over a few months, the implant naturally joins with your jawbone to stay secure and strong.

Restoration attachment

A custom crown, bridge, or denture is placed on the implant for a natural look and function.

Patients typically experience minimal discomfort, and a dentist near you ensures a calm, comfortable experience throughout the procedure.

When Do You Need Implant Placement?

The implant placement near you may be recommended in the following situations:

Teeth that are missing because of cavities, accidents, or gum disease.

Trouble eating or talking because of missing teeth.

Desire for a permanent, stable alternative to dentures

Bone preservation after tooth loss to maintain facial structure

Timely implant placement prevents shifting of surrounding teeth, jawbone deterioration, and long-term oral health issues.
