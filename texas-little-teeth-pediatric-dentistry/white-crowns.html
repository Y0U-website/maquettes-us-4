FAQs

How long can white crowns last for kids?

With proper care and maintenance, white crowns can last several years, providing long-term dental protection.

How often should children visit the dentist?

Regular dental check-ups every six months are recommended for children to maintain optimal oral health and address any emerging issues promptly.

Do you provide sedation for anxious children during dental procedures?

We offer various sedation options to help anxious children relax during dental treatments. Our priority is their comfort and well-being.
