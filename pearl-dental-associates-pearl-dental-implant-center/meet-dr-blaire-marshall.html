Get to Know Blaire Marshall, DDS

D r. Marshall has come to Pearl Dental Associates all the way from Houston, TX, and we couldn’t be happier to welcome her onboard. With over eight years of experience so far, she truly believes in the ability of good dentists to positively impact patients’ lives – one smile at a time! Keep reading below to learn more about her.

With over eight years of experience so far, she truly believes in the ability of good dentists to positively impact patients’ lives

where did you study dentistry?

Dr. Marshall completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Oklahoma and pursues continuing education on a regular basis. She has a passion for correcting misaligned teeth with Invisalign® Clear Braces in particular and helping them see just how easy it is to transform their smile for the better! As someone who personally suffers from obstructive sleep apnea, she’s also very interested in helping people overcome disordered breathing during sleep.

What do you like most about being a dentist?

More than anything, Dr. Marshall loves working closely with patients. Her primary mission is to establish and develop trusting relationships over time with each person in her treatment chair. She understands very well that every patient (and smile) is unique, and she deeply cares about exceeding your expectations. She can’t wait to help new arrivals look and feel their best!

what do you do outside of the office?

When she isn’t hard at work, Dr. Marshall loves spending quality time with her friends and family, traveling, and trying out new restaurants.
