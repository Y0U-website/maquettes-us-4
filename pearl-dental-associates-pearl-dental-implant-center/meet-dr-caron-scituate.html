MEET OUR DENTIST | Scituate And Holbrook, MA Dr. Nicole Caron Follow us on social media Get to Know Nicole Caron, DMD Dr. Caron is originally from Weymouth, MA, and she actually had the opportunity to work at Pearl Dental Associates throughout her years in dental school as an intern! We’re thrilled to now have her as a full-fledged member of our team. If you’d like to learn more about her, just continue reading below.

Dr. Caron achieved her Bachelor of Science at Emmanuel College, followed by her Master of Science at Boston University.

Why did you decide to become a dentist?

Dr. Caron discovered her passion for dentistry at a young age, when she underwent orthodontic treatment. Oral healthcare felt like the perfect fit for her personal strengths and talents – and several years later, she still feels entirely confident that she made the right decision!

Where did you study dentistry?

Dr. Caron achieved her Bachelor of Science at Emmanuel College, followed by her Master of Science at Boston University. Finally, she pursued her dental doctorate at the University of New England College of Dental Medicine in Portland, Maine.

What do you do outside of the practice?

In her free time away from work, Dr. Caron spends as much time as possible on the beach. She loves collecting sea glass! She also grew up as a competitive figure skater and still goes out to the ice rink as often as she can.
