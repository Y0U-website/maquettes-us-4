The Process for Getting Implant Dentures

Getting implant-retained occurs over several months and requires several visits to Twickenham Family Dentistry. Our caring dental team understands this is a big step and is committed to guiding you through this process to ensure it is comfortable and stress-free!

Initial Consultation with Our Dentists

You will first meet with Dr. Cook at our Huntsville, AL, dental office . They will thoroughly evaluate your oral health to determine if you are a good implant candidate. Using advanced technology like 3-D cone beam CT scans and panoramic x-rays, they determine how many implants are needed and their optimal placement.

Dental Implant Placement and Healing

The small titanium implant posts are placed surgically into your jawbone by our experienced and skilled dentists. This step is done under local anesthesia, and we will ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.

After placement comes healing, which requires 3 6 months. This gives the implant posts time to fully integrate and fuse with the jawbone, and this process is called osseointegration .

Attaching Your Custom-Crafted Dentures

We understand that the appearance of your dentures is crucial. Our dental team will take all the time needed to plan and custom-craft dentures that look natural and complement your smile and facial features. When implant healing is complete, our dentists attach your dentures securely to the implants.

Traditional Dentures in Huntsville, AL

Our traditional dentures are a classic choice for those needing full or partial tooth replacement. They’re an affordable and time-tested solution for restoring your smile and improving your ability to chew and speak with confidence.

Implant Dentures in Huntsville, AL

We offer implant dentures for those seeking a more stable and permanent solution. This innovative option involves anchoring the denture to dental implants surgically placed in the jawbone. This ensures a secure fit and helps maintain jawbone integrity, preventing the bone loss commonly associated with traditional dentures. Implant dentures are an excellent choice for enhanced comfort and functionality.
