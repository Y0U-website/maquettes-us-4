What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Thanks to technological advancements, many different tooth replacement options are now available. If you need to replace a full upper or lower arch of teeth and don’t want removable dentures, All-on-4 dental implants may be right for you.

If All-on-4 dental implants are right for you, your dentist will strategically place four titanium implants into your arch, choosing the spots with the highest bone density. This will ensure stability for your new teeth. These implants will eventually fuse to your jawbone to give you maximum strength.

Next, your beautiful new set of teeth will be permanently placed onto the four implants. You will regain your smile and the ability to eat your favorite foods and improve your oral health.

Are You a Candidate for All-on-4?

Because each patient is different, your dental professional can assess your dental condition and advise whether All-on-4 dental implants may be right for you. They will conduct an examination, including x-rays and 3-D images, to check for candidacy. As a general rule, however, the following characteristics can make you a viable candidate for dental implants:

  • Your bone density is enough to hold the implants in place.
  • You have tried dentures and want a different option, or you don’t want removable dentures.
  • You are interested in a cost-effective implant option.
  • You are a non-smoker.
