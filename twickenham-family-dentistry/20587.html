Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are an excellent tooth replacement option for a variety of reasons. Dental implants are easy to maintain, they look and feel natural, and they provide the same function as a natural tooth, which keeps your jawbone healthy.

Dental implants are also a long-lasting tooth replacement option. But how much life will you really get out of a dental implant? The answer is one reason why we love dental implants! With proper care and maintenance, the dental implant itself can last a lifetime .

To fully answer this question, however, we need to address the parts of your dental implant: the dental crown, which is the tooth or the visible portion of the tooth replacement, and the abutment, which attaches the implant to the crown.

Since the crown functions as the tooth, it is exposed to more damage than the implant, which means it may need replacement at some point. However, with an excellent oral hygiene routine and adherence to some precautions, like avoiding hard or chewy foods, even the dental crown can last over a decade.

How Do I Care for a Dental Implant?

Fortunately, caring for dental implants is simple. Care for them as you care for your natural teeth.

At Twickenham Family Dentistry , we recommend that you brush and floss your teeth at least twice every day. Also, for the benefit of your natural teeth and your dental implant, you should visit our dental office at least twice a year for a checkup and dental cleaning. Regular dental cleanings are a vital part of maintaining your oral health and prolonging the life of your dental implant.

Additionally, try to avoid eating hard or sticky foods, as mentioned above, and try to break habits that could damage the dental crown attached to your implant, like chewing on your fingernails.

If you grind your teeth, Dr. Cook may also recommend a nightguard to help protect your teeth from pressure and wear.

Other Factors That Affect the Lifespan of a Dental Implant

In addition to proper care and maintenance, other factors can affect the lifespan of your dental implant.

Location of the Implant

Implants at the back of the mouth generally do not enjoy the same longevity as implants near the front of the mouth. This is due to the intense pressure that your back teeth undergo when chewing.

Gum Disease

With gum disease, the gums may recede, causing damage to your natural teeth and your dental implant. A good oral hygiene routine and regular visits to the dentist can help prevent gum disease. Also, we highly recommend avoiding tobacco products, such as cigarettes, to keep your gums healthy.

Do dental implants hurt?

Dental implant placement is not painful because your dentist thoroughly anesthetizes the area before starting work . After the anesthesia wears off, you may notice mild swelling and discomfort, but this is short-lived and easily treatable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Why Dental Implants Are Worth It

When it comes to tooth replacement, dental implants are the preferred choice of most dentists and patients. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants replace both the tooth root and the crown, providing unmatched stability, strength, and longevity.

A dental implant in Huntsville, AL , is a small post made from biocompatible titanium that integrates naturally with your jawbone. Once the implant has fused and healed, your dentist attaches a custom-made dental crown, creating a restoration that looks, feels, and functions like a real tooth.
