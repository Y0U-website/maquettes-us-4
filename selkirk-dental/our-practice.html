Our mission

Friendly care close to home

Since our doors opened in 2022, we have been on a mission to set the standards for excellence through community stewardship, utilizing modern technology, and creating unique experiences that leave a lasting impression.

Whether you haven’t been to the dentist in a while or are just in the market for a great family-friendly dentist, look no further! Selkirk Dental can take care of all your dental needs. We see patients of all dental backgrounds and never judge or lecture patients. Instead, we focus on finding individualized solutions to help patients maintain optimal oral health.

Our comfortable office provides a relaxing atmosphere, featuring modern amenities such as blankets, headphones, coffee, water, and kids’ activities.

Schedule your appointment today, and let us elevate your oral health!

Dr. David Bowen

Dr. David Bowen (pronounced Bo-win) is joining the Selkirk Dental family! Dr. Bowen was born and raised in Moscow before choosing to follow in his grandfather, mother, and older brother in dentistry. He graduated from Walla Walla University before completing his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at Loma Linda University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Bowen has been practicing for 10 years in Bozeman, Montana and is excited to move himself and his family back home to Idaho. Dr. Bowen is passionate about building strong relationships with patients. He treats each patient as he would a family member, making sure their dental goals are being met.

Dr. Bowen keeps his skills sharp through his involvement in several top tier organizations, such as the ADA and Spear Study Club. Dr. Bowen might be the tallest dentist you meet. At 6’11", he is can see over most others’ heads. He also enjoys a birds-eye view as a licensed pilot for 20 years, and just completed his IFR training. Be sure to welcome him to the area when you spot him in the community!
