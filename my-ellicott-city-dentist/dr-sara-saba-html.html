FAQs

How do I find the best place to get dental work done?

It’s only natural to want to find a skilled dentist you feel like you can trust to help you build and maintain the most beautiful and healthiest version of your smile. If you’re seeking a new provider, a quick internet search can likely produce a local list of results to choose from.

But if there are many different options, you might struggle to narrow them down to pick the right practice to meet your needs. It can help to take a few moments to write down your essential priorities for dental healthcare. For instance, do you need someone who is in-network with your insurance policy? Do you have children who also require care? Are there specific procedures you anticipate wanting or needing?

Once you’ve identified what really matters to you, it can be easier to pick a provider who checks off the right boxes.

How much does a routine checkup and cleaning in Ellicott City usually cost?

Most patients can expect to pay between approximately $90 and $200 for a standard examination and professional dental cleaning in Ellicott City, but the cost of your visit can vary depending on your unique circumstances.

For instance, if you carry a dental insurance policy, up to 100 % of routine services, including exams, cleanings, and X-rays, might be included at no additional cost to you. But if our team finds signs of issues, like cavities or gum disease, you’ll be responsible for paying for the required treatments.

What’s the best way to find a skilled provider who places dental implants?

Dental implants are the only replacement teeth that are embedded directly into your jawbone, which often requires the involvement of a specially trained expert, such as a periodontist, prosthodontist, or oral surgeon.

But many general dentists also undergo special educational courses or certifications to provide this service. For example, at our office in Ellicott City, Dr. Bui has completed advanced programs to both place and restore dental implants in the office, and is a proud member of the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics, making her an expert who can help you rebuild a beautiful smile.

What level of education is required to become a dentist?

Dentists must meet specific criteria to become licensed to work with patients, which usually begins with their education. First, they usually earn a bachelor’s degree in an appropriate field, then must achieve either a DMD (Doctor of Dental Medicine) or DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) degree from one of more than 70 institutions that are accredited by the American Dental Association. Next, they can choose to focus on one of 12 officially recognized specialty topics.

At our practice, Dr. Aziz studied Neurobiology and Physiology at the University of Maryland at College Park and then graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. Meanwhile, Dr. Bui attended the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, and then achieved her dental doctorate from the Indiana School of Dentistry.
