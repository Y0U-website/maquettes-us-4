Dr. Blake Adams, DDS (Implantologist)

Dr. Blake Adams grew up in Arizona and completed his undergraduate studies at Arizona State University in the field of Microbiology. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) degree from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2008. For more than 11 years he practiced as a general dentist, but his passion for implant dentistry drove him to pursue advanced training and specialization.

In 2018, Dr. Adams entered a three-year residency program in Implant Dentistry at Loma Linda University, where he trained with some of the world’s leading implantologists. During his residency he was selected as Chief Surgical Resident and also mentored fellow residents and graduate students. He has since earned a Master’s in Implantology from UNLV and the California Implant Institute, and continues to advance his knowledge through ongoing teaching and speaking engagements.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Adams is an accomplished endurance athlete. He has completed multiple marathons, including the Boston Marathon, as well as half and full Ironman triathlons. When he’s not training or competing, he enjoys spending time with his wife of 25 years and their four children, two of whom are still at home in Arizona.

Missy Toliver Practice Manager
