Personalized Patient Care: We take time to understand your concerns, answer questions, and create customized treatment plans designed around your needs.

Convenient Scheduling: Our Middletown office offers convenient scheduling options to help make dental care accessible for busy families and professionals.

At SmileCross Dental of Middletown, we believe dental care should be affordable and accessible for everyone. Our team works with many major dental insurance providers and will help you understand your benefits and maximize your coverage.

For patients without dental insurance, we also offer a convenient in-house Dental Membership Plan . This plan provides preventive care such as exams, cleanings, and X-rays, along with discounted pricing on many treatments throughout the year, without deductibles, waiting periods, or annual maximums.

Our goal is to provide flexible payment solutions that make maintaining a healthy smile simple and stress-free.
