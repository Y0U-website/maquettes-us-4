Attleboro Family Dental Care is your hometown dental practice. We offer a wide range of general dentistry services at our office, as well as specialty care. As a comprehensive dental practice, we offer a variety of services, including:

Invisalign ​ – If you’ve always wanted straighter teeth, but you don’t like the idea of metal braces, Invisalign is the answer. These clear plastic aligners are comfortable, convenient, and discreet.

Periodontics ​ – Our periodontal services include procedures to treat gum disease and repair the damage it causes, as well as cosmetic procedures that improve the appearance of your smile by recontouring the gums.

Sleep Apnea​ – Many patients find relief from sleep apnea with a custom sleep appliance that repositions the jaw in order to keep the airway unobstructed throughout the night.

Endodontics ​ – When a tooth has damage or decay that reaches the pulp, a root canal is needed to save the tooth and prevent the need for an extraction. With the modern endodontic techniques we use at our practice, getting a root canal is no more painful than having a cavity filled.

Dr. Hikmat Hannawi practiced as a dentist in his native Syria before relocating to Massachusetts and attending Boston University School of Dental Medicine’s Advanced Standing Program in 1999. After graduating, Dr. Hannawi worked as an associate at another practice and then founded his own practice, Attleboro Family Dental Care, in 2000.

Services

General and Family Dentistry ​ – While preventive care is at the heart of what we do as dentists, we also offer restorative dentistry to restore function and appearance to teeth that have suffered damage or decay. We provide dental fillings, crowns, bridges…

Same-Day Crowns ​ – Same-day crowns streamline the process of getting dental restorations, allowing us to eliminate the usual two-week waiting time and restore your tooth in a single two-hour appointment.

Dental Implants ​ – Dental implants allow us to replace missing teeth both above and below the gumline for results that look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry ​ – Our cosmetic dentistry services include smile makeovers that are designed with care and attention to detail to ensure realistic results. We also offer dental veneers, teeth whitening, bonding, and Snap-On Smiles.
