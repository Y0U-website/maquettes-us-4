DR. MARINA

Dr. Marina’s Goal is to Clean Up, Detox, Repair, Rebuild and Maintain Your Oral Health.

Here you will receive dental care which is HOLISTIC – this means Dr. Marina will not just diagnose and treat your problem teeth, she will take the extra step to evaluate your entire health and determine how to treat your mouth to help your body thrive.

It has been long established that oral health is a window to what is going on inside your body – if you have poor health, you will definitely have a difficult time maintaining a healthy mouth. Conversely a poorly maintained mouth can potentially cause chronic disease in the body – especially if there are toxic materials present.

Dr. Marina’s years of experience have lead her to develop a unique, systematic approach to treating her patients. She takes her time to develop a treatment plan that has the best compatibility to your specific health status. Unless there is a dental emergency that has to be taken care of immediately, there is no rush. Your story will be carefully listened to and you will be treated with care and compassion.
