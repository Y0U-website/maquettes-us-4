FAQs About All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • What exactly are All‑on‑4 dental implants? All‑on‑4 is an advanced technique for full-arch tooth replacement using just four dental implants. These implants support a fixed prosthetic bridge, providing a stable, permanent, and natural-looking smile, unlike removable dentures.
  • How do All‑on‑4 implants differ from traditional implant methods? Traditional implant methods may require 6 to 10 implants per arch and often involve bone grafting. All‑on‑4 uses only four strategically angled implants to leverage existing bone density and reduce the need for grafts. This results in a simpler, faster, and often more affordable treatment.
  • Am I a good candidate for All‑on‑4 treatment? Ideal candidates are those who are missing most or all teeth and want a long-lasting solution. A key advantage of All‑on‑4 is that its angled placement can accommodate patients with moderate bone loss, potentially avoiding bone grafting. Still, a full dental evaluation and 3D imaging are needed to confirm candidacy.
  • What is the recovery and procedure timeline like? The surgical placement generally takes about 2–3 hours per arch. Many patients leave with temporary fixed teeth the same day (“immediate load”), though final permanent prosthetics are placed after healing. Initial recovery may last a few days, with full integration taking a few months.
  • How long do All‑on‑4 implants last, and do they preserve jawbone? With good oral hygiene and routine dental checkups, All‑on‑4 implants can last 20 years or longer, often a lifetime. Importantly, like natural teeth, they stimulate the jawbone, helping prevent bone loss and maintaining facial structure.
