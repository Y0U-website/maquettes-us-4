Same-Day Dental Implants in…

A few months later, once the implants have fused with the bone, we swap the temporary set for your final, permanent teeth.

  • Any failing teeth come out. If you still have teeth that need to go, we remove them at the start of the visit.
  • The implants go in. We place the implant posts into the jaw. For a full arch, this is usually four implants, the same approach as All-on-4 .
  • A set of teeth goes on. We attach a sturdy set of temporary teeth to the implants the same day. These are fixed in place. You eat soft foods at first while you heal, but the teeth do not come out.
  • You go home with a smile. No gap, no waiting period without teeth.

Who is a good fit for same-day implants

Same-day works best when there is enough healthy bone to hold the implants firmly on the day of surgery. That firm initial grip is what lets us load teeth right away. We confirm this with the 3D scan, not a guess.

Good candidates often include people who:

If the scan shows the bone is not ready for immediate loading, we will tell you honestly and lay out a staged plan instead. Rushing it helps no one.

  • Are missing most or all teeth on an arch
  • Have failing teeth that need to come out anyway
  • Are fighting with loose dentures and want something fixed
  • Have enough bone, or the kind of bone, that the scan shows can support immediate teeth

Why doing it in one place matters here

Same-day treatment only works when the whole team is in sync, because a lot happens in one visit: the scan, the extractions, the implant surgery, and the teeth. At practices that refer the surgery to one office and the teeth to another, "same day" usually is not on the table at all. We place and restore implants in house, so the planning, the surgery, and the teeth all happen with one team that knows your case. That is what makes the single-visit timeline possible. Frequently asked questions

Do I really get teeth the same day? Yes. We place the implants and attach a fixed set of teeth in the same visit. They are temporary teeth at first. Your permanent set comes after a few months of healing, once the implants have fused with the bone.

Does it hurt? We keep you comfortable with sedation and local anesthetic during the procedure. Most patients are surprised how manageable the recovery is, with some soreness for a few days afterward.

What can I eat after same-day implants? Soft foods at first while everything heals. We give you clear instructions, and you gradually return to a normal diet as you recover.

Is everyone a candidate? No. Same-day implants need enough healthy bone to hold the implants firmly on the day of surgery. Our 3D scan tells us for sure. If you are not a candidate for the immediate version, we offer a staged plan instead.

How is this different from regular implants? With traditional implants, you often wait months between placing the implant and getting the tooth. Same-day implants give you fixed teeth on the day of surgery, so you are never without teeth.
