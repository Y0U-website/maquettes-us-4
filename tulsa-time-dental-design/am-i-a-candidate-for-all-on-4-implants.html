Frequently asked questions

Can I get All-on-4 if I have bone loss? Often yes. All-on-4 is specifically designed to use the bone you still have by angling the back implants. Many patients who were told they need bone grafts for traditional implants still qualify for All-on-4.

Is there an age limit? No. What matters is your health and your jaw, not your age. Many of our patients are in their 60s, 70s, and beyond.

What if I still have some teeth? If those teeth are failing, they are usually removed as part of the treatment and replaced along with the rest. We assess this at your exam.

Will smoking stop me from getting implants? Not automatically, but it does raise the risk of problems and slows healing. We will be upfront with you about how it affects your case.

How do I know for sure if I qualify? A consultation with a 3D scan gives you a definite answer. We can usually tell you the same day whether All-on-4 is a fit and what your plan would look like.
