Dental Discount Plan

If you do not have dental insurance, our plans are a terrific way to keep your oral health on track without compromising your budget. To qualify for plan discounts, you must pay patient costs when services are provided. For cast restorations or prosthodontics, this is at the preparation or impression visit. In addition, you must be seen by the dentist at least once every six months and comply with his recommended course of treatment.

  • Policy maximums
  • Submitting claims
  • Claims denied due to pre-existing conditions
  • Billing surprises

Dental discount plans do not include the following:

  • Services that, in the dentist’s professional opinion, are not necessary or recommended based on the patient’s dental health.
  • Services related to conditions or injuries addressed by worker’s compensation or employer’s liability laws.
  • Services that are provided to the plan member at no cost through a municipality, county, or other political subdivision.
  • Services that cannot be performed due to the patient’s physical or emotional health.
  • Theft or loss of bridgework or dentures.
  • Dental implantation.
  • Experimental procedures.
  • Endodontics, periodontics, pedodontics, or oral surgery requiring the services of a non-participating dentist or specialist.
