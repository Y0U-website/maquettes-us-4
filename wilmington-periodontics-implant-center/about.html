Meet Our Doctors

Dr. John Buyer is a third-generation dentist with an exceptional background in clinical practice, education, and military service. He earned his Bachelor of Science from The Citadel and his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Indiana University. Dr. Buyer completed extensive postdoctoral training, including a Master of Public Health from the University of South Carolina, a Master of Science from the Medical College of Georgia, and a Master of Strategic Studies from the U.S. Army War College.
