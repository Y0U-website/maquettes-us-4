What is a Periodontist?

More information on Periodontist, Periodontics, and Implant Dentistry:

Periodontists treat cases ranging from mild gingivitis to more severe cases of periodontal disease. Periodontists offer a wide range of treatments, such as scaling and root planing (the cleaning of infected root surfaces), root surface debridement (the removal of damaged tissue), and regenerative procedures (the reversal of lost bone and tissue). Periodontists can also perform surgical procedures for patients with severe gum disease. Additionally, periodontists are expertly trained in the placement, maintenance and repair of dental implants.
