Which tooth-replacement option is right for you?

Dental implants, dentures, and crowns each solve a different problem. Implants are the closest thing to a natural tooth: they replace the root, protect your jawbone, and can last a lifetime, which makes them ideal for one or more missing teeth. Dentures can be a good fit when many teeth are missing, or budget is a priority, while crowns restore a tooth that’s damaged but still has a healthy root. At your consultation, we’ll help you weigh comfort, longevity, and cost to find the right path for your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last for decades and often a lifetime. Because the implant replaces the tooth root, it also helps preserve the surrounding jawbone that dentures can’t.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with one or more missing or failing teeth are good candidates. During your consultation, Dr. Saggar reviews your bone health and overall oral health to confirm whether implants, including same-day and All-on-4 options, are right for you.

Do dental implants hurt?

Implant placement is done with local anesthesia, and most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is. Any soreness afterward is usually mild and eases within a few days with over-the-counter pain relief.
