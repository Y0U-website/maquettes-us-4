Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist for a checkup?

Most patients benefit from professional cleanings and exams every six months. Regular visits to our Jericho general dentist help prevent cavities, gum disease, and costly future treatments, all while keeping your smile feeling fresh and healthy.

What is Airflow Therapy, and how is it different from a regular cleaning?

Airflow Therapy by EMS uses a gentle stream of air, water, and fine powder to remove biofilm and stains without scraping. It’s comfortable, efficient, and ideal for patients with sensitivity or those who prefer a more advanced, spa-like cleaning experience.

Are dental sealants only for children?

No. While sealants are common for kids, adults can benefit too. They protect hard-to-reach grooves and reduce the risk of decay, especially in areas that are difficult to clean with a toothbrush.

How do you detect cavities early?

We use digital imaging, intraoral cameras, and laser diagnostics to spot early signs of decay, often before you feel any discomfort. This allows for smaller, more conservative restorations.

Do you accept insurance for cleanings and exams?

Yes. We accept most insurance plans on an out-of-network basis and provide in-office financing for flexibility. Our team helps you understand your coverage and plan for care that fits your goals and budget.

What does a general dentist do?

A general dentist is your primary care provider for oral health. At Cloudlake Dental, that includes routine exams and cleanings, fillings, crowns, sealants, mouthguards, and emergency care, as well as guidance on preventing problems before they start. When a concern needs specialized treatment, we’ll coordinate your care and walk you through the next steps.
