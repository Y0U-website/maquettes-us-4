Accessibility Statement

We will continue to review the site on a regular basis and update this statement to reflect changes as they are made.

  • Added descriptive alternative text to our social media icons and to the credential and award logos displayed throughout the site
  • Added a “Skip to Main Content” link so keyboard and screen reader users can bypass repetitive navigation
  • Added a visible pause/play control to our homepage video so users can stop autoplaying motion
  • Updated our page heading structure to follow a proper hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3) for clearer screen reader navigation
  • Corrected the domain reference and contact information on this page

Enabling the Accessibility Menu

The Cloudlakedental.com accessibility menu can be enabled either by hitting the tab key when the page first loads or by clicking the accessibility menu icon that appears on the corner of the page. After triggering the accessibility menu, please wait a moment for the accessibility menu to load in its entirety.
