Veneers

Our minimally-invasive techniques and technologies create beautiful, custom-made veneers.

Veneers are made from natural tooth-colored materials and are designed to fit perfectly over the front of your teeth, hiding imperfections and transforming your teeth.

We deliver the durable, functional, and stunning smile you’ve always wanted without removing any of your natural tooth.

Teeth Whitening

To meet your individual needs, we offer both in-office whitening and custom, at-home kits.

In-office whitening is achieved through a comfortable, non-invasive, light-activated treatment.

At-home cosmetic dental services include customized trays, created from a digital scan of your teeth, and advanced whitening gels. Your at-home trays can be used again for future re-whitening.

Dental Implants

When you’re suffering from missing or badly damaged teeth, dental implants are the recommended solution.

Cloudlake uses 3D imaging to create a completely customized and computer-generated restoration.

The result is a highly accurate placement that blends naturally with your teeth, transforms your smile, and speeds healing time. The majority of our patients can return to work the same day.

Invisalign

Invisalign clear aligner systems are designed to discreetly and accurately straighten your teeth.

Compared to traditional braces, Invisalign trays are more comfortable as they fit over your teeth and are made from a soft material.

Since its invention, clear aligners now allow us to complete both simple and challenging cases. They’re one of the dental services our patients love most.
