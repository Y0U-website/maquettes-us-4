A beautiful smile is more than just teeth—it’s about aesthetics and confidence. At ERA Dentistry, Dr. Rojas focuses on enhancing the look and health of your smile, paying close attention to your teeth, gums, and overall oral health. Whether through cosmetic treatments or aesthetic cleanings, we provide personalized options to elevate your smile.

ERA Dentistry offers Invisalign, a modern orthodontic solution using clear, comfortable aligners. Gradually straighten your teeth without the hassle of traditional braces. The nearly invisible aligners fit seamlessly into your lifestyle for a discreet and effective transformation.

Elizabeth Rojas, DDS, is a general dentist who has been dedicated to improving the oral health of her patients for over 20 years. At her practice ERA Dentistry, she is dedicated to providing her patients with the most advanced equipment and modern dental facility possible.

After receiving her Bachelor of Arts in biology from California State University, Northridge, she went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Northwestern University Dental School in Chicago, Illinois. When deciding where to open her practice, returning to her hometown of Van Nuys was a priority.

Misaligned teeth or an improper bite? Orthodontic treatments at ERA Dentistry address alignment issues to improve your smile’s appearance and function. Dr. Rojas ensures precise and personalized care for a smile that’s both stunning and healthy.
