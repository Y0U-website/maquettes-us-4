Dental Implants

Dental implants look and feel just like natural teeth. Implants are an excellent option for anyone who is missing a tooth. For our patients in Michigan, dental implants are long-lasting and have a less than 1 % rejection rate. Read the FAQs about getting a dental implant.

They are made from titanium and act as artificial tooth roots. Implants are placed in your jaw and look, feel, and function like a natural tooth.

Dental implants give a strong foundation for a bridge , crown , or implant-supported dentures .

Advantages of Dental Implants

  • Enjoy your favorite foods
  • Improve your speech
  • Boost your self-confidence
  • Keep your teeth in place
  • Next best thing to healthy, natural teeth

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Implants can last a lifetime, if cared for properly. Brushing and flossing after every meal is important to make sure your implants are maintained. Be sure to visit your Pointe Dental Group dentist in Michigan for regular exams and cleanings.

Dentures

Complete dentures replace all the missing teeth in the jaw bone. Complete dentures can be immediately placed after extractions for maximum comfort and aesthetics. We offer dentures in Michigan at two locations: Grosse Pointe and Shelby Township. Contact us for a consultation .

Partial Dentures

These are used when one or more natural teeth remain in the upper or lower jaw. A partial denture usually consists of replacement teeth attached to a gum-colored acrylic base that is connected to your existing teeth and covers all areas of missing teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Another option for replacing your teeth are implant-supported dentures. A combination of implants and dentures are used together, which aids greatly in keeping dentures stable.
