Meet The Doctor

Dr. Ryan Clance

During dental school, Dr. Ryan held numerous leadership roles and was honored with both the Charles W. Fain Pediatric Dental Award and the OKU Ethics Award. His passion for humanitarian work led him to organize mission trips to the Dominican Republic and the Bahamas, where he provided dental care to underserved communities.

Outside of work, Dr. Ryan is an enthusiastic golfer, often practicing his “air” swings between patients. On Saturdays, he proudly supports his beloved Florida Gators, and on Sundays, you can find him cheering for Liverpool FC and the 49ers. When not working, Ryan loves spending time with his wife, Dominique, and their two children, Connley and Charlotte. The family enjoys traveling, beach outings, cooking, and outdoor adventures. They are excited to be part of the Nocatee community and are eager to become even more ingrained and involved in the area.
