Dr. Robertson then entered the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Residency Program at Howard University Hospital in Washington, DC, and subsequently transferred branches into the US Navy Reserve. Upon graduation of the OMS program, she was commissioned as a Lieutenant Commander, and succeeded in being the 1st African American active duty female Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon in the U.S. Navy.

Dr. Robertson is a member of the American Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons, National Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, National Dental Association, Georgia Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, Eweil Neil Dental Society, Tennessee Air National Guard Association, Omicron Kappa Upsilon, and Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc.
