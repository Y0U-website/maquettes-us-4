The practice

Led by Dr. Firas Marsheh, Simply Endodontics provides expert diagnosis and treatment of complex dental conditions affecting the inner structures of the teeth.

Dr. Firas Marsheh is a highly skilled and experienced endodontist committed to delivering exceptional care with compassion and precision. With extensive training in advanced endodontic techniques, Dr. Marsheh specializes in complex root canal cases and microsurgical procedures.

He believes that patient education is essential to successful treatment and takes the time to clearly explain diagnoses, procedures, and treatment options. His calm demeanor and gentle approach help patients feel at ease, even during complex procedures.

Dr. Marsheh works closely with referring dentists to ensure seamless communication and optimal outcomes for every patient.
