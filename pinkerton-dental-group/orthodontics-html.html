Traditional Braces

When most people think about orthodontics, traditional braces are what come to mind first. These tried-and-true metal brackets and wires have helped countless smiles over the years, using steady, careful pressure to gradually shift teeth and correct bite problems. Not to mention, modern braces are more comfortable and easier to wear than ever before!

Spark Clear Aligners

Clear aligners have slowly but surely become a desirable alternative to traditional braces due to their convenience and discretion. Spark clear aligners are custom-made for you and worn over your teeth for 22 hours each day. The trays are designed to apply pressure, just like metal braces, to gently move teeth into their ideal positions.

Invisalign

Invisalign offers the same level of convenience and comfort as Spark clear aligners. Many patients are able to easily navigate orthodontic treatment since the aligners are removable, transparent, and easy to integrate into their daily routine.

Six Month Smile

Six Month Smiles offers another fast, discreet way to straighten your teeth using unique clear brackets and wires. As the name suggests, it offers a quicker route to confidence-inspiring results compared to braces or clear aligners!
