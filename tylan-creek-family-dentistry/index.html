The practice

Dr. Halbert has been working in dentistry for 30 years, and he s proud to spend his career in service to his community. He graduated from Clemson University in 1983 and then went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine at The Medical University of South Carolina in 1987. He has advanced training in many fields, including dental implants, sleep medicine, and aesthetic dentistry. His favorite part…

Over time, Dr. Halbert has dedicated thousands of hours to residents of Upstate South Carolina through his work with the Greenville Free Medical/Dental Clinic and the Children’s Dental Clinic. He also regularly opens his doors to the less fortunate for free-of-charge dentistry and spends time in the local schools assisting with children’s dental health care and education courses. He enjoys…

Originally from Baton Rouge, LA, Dr. Halbert moved to Upstate South Carolina in 1968. He is the only child of two hardworking, church-going parents. Outside of the dental office, his hobbies include fishing, hunting, golfing, and raising beef cattle. He has also coached varsity high school baseball for 13 years and counting!

Dr. Burns has been working in dentistry since 2008. He has his Bachelor of Science from Ohio University and his Doctor of Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University. While at OSU College of Dentistry, he was actually able to combine his love of sports and dentistry, serving as a dentist for one of the student teams. He also received valuable training in both sports dentistry and dental trauma.

In addition to enjoying dentistry, Dr. Burns takes great pride in his ability to build strong, trusting relationships with his patients. He has an outgoing, kind, understanding personality, and he always looks forward to converting anxious newcomers into calm, devoted patients who look forward to their appointments.

Originally from Lebanon, OH, Dr. Burns spent three years practicing in an underserved area of Ohio before choosing to move his family to Simpsonville. He and his wife, Katie, have two daughters named Claire and Caroline, a dog, Annie, and a cat, Simon.

In his free time, he enjoys following any sport, especially Ohio State football, being outside with his kids, hiking, boating, and fishing. Before going to dental school, Dr. Burns actually worked in highway construction for two years!
