What You Should Know

  • Both monthly and annual payment options are available.
  • The annual payment option reflects a discounted rate equivalent to one free month.
  • The Wellness Plan is a 12-month commitment , regardless of whether you choose monthly or annual payments.
  • There is no additional activation fee to begin your plan.
  • The full annual amount is due at the time of enrollment when choosing the annual payment option.
  • Wellness Plan benefits are valid only for treatment performed at Dr. Parrella’s office .
  • The plan does not cover treatment provided by specialty dentists, including oral surgeons, periodontists, or orthodontists.
  • Invisalign is not included in the Wellness Plan.
  • Wellness Plan savings cannot be combined with dental insurance, medical insurance, or other dental discount plans.
  • Benefits do not roll over to the following year.
  • If you receive discounted services and subsequently cancel your plan, you are responsible for paying the discounted amounts received.
