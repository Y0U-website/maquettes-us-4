Dr. Franco Obour, DMD

Meet Dr Franco

Founder of Obour Dental | Berkeley Heights, NJ

A New Standard of Patient-Centered Dentistry

Dr. Franco Obour represents a new generation of modern dentistry, one rooted in compassion, science, and connection. With a calm, attentive demeanor and a patient-first philosophy, Dr. Obour strives to redefine what it means to feel truly cared for at the dentist.

Education & Clinical Background

His path began with a deep curiosity for healthcare and the human experience. After earning his Bachelor’s degree in Biology from The College of New Jersey and his Master of Biomedical Science from Rutgers Biomedical and Health Sciences, Dr. Obour worked as an associate scientist at Pfizer, where precision and empathy shaped his approach to patient care.

He later earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, refining his expertise across all aspects of general and restorative dentistry.

Professional Affiliations & Philosophy of Care

Today, as a proud member of the Academy of General Dentistry, New Jersey Dental Association , and American Dental Association , Dr. Obour continues to blend innovation with warmth, combining advanced techniques with a genuine dedication to patient comfort.

His mission is simple yet powerful: to deliver modern, stress-free dentistry that feels deeply personal — where every visit is comfortable, every smile is cared for, and every patient is treated like family.

Founding Obour Dental (2025)

n 2025, Dr. Franco Obour founded Obour Dental with a clear vision: to create a practice where modern technology, thoughtful design, and personalized care come together to elevate the dental experience.
