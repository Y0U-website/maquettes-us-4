How Implant Cost and Complexity Are Determined

There&#x27;s no flat number we can quote you before we look in your mouth. Any dentist who gives you one over the phone is guessing. What drives your cost isn&#x27;t the implant itself, it&#x27;s the situation around it.

The most common case we see is a single missing tooth. One implant, one crown, done in-house from start to finish. That&#x27;s the job most patients come in expecting, and for a lot of people it really is that simple.

Before you get a number from anyone, ask what it includes. Does it cover the health history review and exam? Does it include the crown, or just the implant post? Is grafting already priced in, or is that a surprise waiting for you at the next visit?

A real treatment plan answers those questions before you leave the chair. Anything less isn&#x27;t really a plan, it&#x27;s a placeholder.
