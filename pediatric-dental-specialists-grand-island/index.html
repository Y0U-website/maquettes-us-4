The practice

We understand it’s not always easy to manage family schedules. That’s why at Pediatric Dental Specialists–Grand Island, we offer combined appointments (including for orthodontics!). Bring all your kids at once to save time and streamline your schedule.

Our pediatric dental office is so much more fun than a regular dental setup. We’ve designed our practice to be kid-friendly and put nervous children at ease. It’s 100 % tailored to our young patients’ needs. Our team loves nothing more than making every dental visit a fun and engaging experience.

Every child is unique. Our patient-centered approach allows us to tailor our care to accommodate your child’s specific needs. We also offer various sedation options to create a calm, positive dental experience for your child.

Our specially trained pediatric dentists diagnose and treat children of all ages. We’ve created an inviting environment here at Pediatric Dental Specialists–Grand Island. It’s a happy and fun place where kids enjoy learning how to take care of their teeth.

Boost your child’s confidence and self-esteem with cosmetic dentistry. We provide teeth whitening, tooth-colored fillings, pediatric dental crowns, and even pediatric partial dentures. Your child will be smiling in no time with a treatment plan tailored just for them.

Pediatric Dental Specialists–Grand Island offers a wide range of orthodontic treatments. We use an interceptive approach so our team can diagnose and address orthodontic issues early on. Being proactive leads to a smoother orthodontic experience, better overall results, and amazing smiles.

Introducing your child to the dentist early helps them establish healthy dental habits for life. We’re trained specifically to deal with children’s dental needs, and that includes babies and toddlers. Our pediatric dental specialists will make each appointment fun and enjoyable for your kid.
