Meet Dr. Lehn

Dr. Lehn and his wife, Elizabeth, are incredibly grateful to be a part of the Hastings and Grand Island communities. They have seven young children, Catherine, twins Juliana and June, Margaret, James, Maryanne, and David. He enjoys coaching youth sports and spending time with his family, whether at the park, bike riding, swimming, or simply playing in their backyard.

Dr. Lehn is available for appointments at the Grand Island and Hastings locations.
