Invisalign

THE CLEAR ALTERNATIVE

Invisalign clear aligners are a groundbreaking addition to orthodontics. Since they are more discreet than traditional metal braces, they are perfect for children who might feel self-conscious about orthodontic treatment. Invisalign’s clear, removable aligners allow for a snug fit and unmatched comfort.

Invisalign is best suited for children who have mild to moderate crowding or spacing issues. The system works by gently shifting your child’s teeth into their correct positions over time. There is a new set of aligners every few weeks with slight modifications, each set bringing your child’s teeth closer to the desired result. The best part? Invisalign aligners are hardly noticeable and can be removed for eating, brushing, and special occasions.

Braces

STEP INTO A STRAIGHTER SMILE.

Braces are still one of the most effective methods for straightening a crooked smile. Through gentle pressure, braces assist in realigning the bite to its correct position. Braces are a suitable treatment to fix a range of dental issues, from simple crowding or spacing problems to underbites, overbites, or crossbites. The result is a beautiful straight smile that your child can proudly show off. However, braces aren’t just about creating a beautiful smile—they are an investment in your child’s future dental health.

Palatal Expanders

PAVING THE WAY TO A WELL-ALIGNED SMILE

A palatal expander is a common pediatric orthodontic appliance that gradually widens the upper jaw to rectify issues caused by a narrow palate. Left unaddressed, a narrow upper jaw can lead to future oral health problems, like impacted teeth and crossbites, that may then require extractions to fix. Though a palatal expander is only a temporary part of your child’s orthodontic treatment plan, the benefits of improved alignment and function are long-lasting.

Space Maintainers

SAFEGUARDING YOUR CHILD’S FUTURE SMILE

Space maintainers play a crucial role in pediatric dentistry. These innovative devices prevent neighboring teeth from shifting into spaces left by the premature loss of baby teeth, ensuring that there is sufficient room for the permanent teeth to properly emerge. This helps your child avoid overcrowding and promotes a healthier, more aligned smile in the long run. It’s worth noting that space maintainers are custom made to ensure they are both comfortable and effective for your child. Once the permanent teeth begin to erupt, we can easily remove these appliances.

Retainers

KEEPING YOUR CHILD’S SMILE ALIGNED

Retainers are an essential part of the orthodontic process to hold the teeth in their final, correct positions. We make sure they have a snug but comfortable fit to provide just enough pressure to stop the teeth from moving back to where they were. While it might seem like an extra step, using retainers protects that straight, healthy smile your child has worked so hard to achieve.
