What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are considered one of the most advanced tooth replacement options available today. It consists of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a crown, bridge, or denture is attached to restore the smile.

Because implants integrate with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, they closely mimic the function of natural teeth.

Which Option Is Right For You?

If you are missing teeth, how do you choose which tooth replacement is best for you? It often comes down to your oral health, budget, timeline, and long-term goals.

Are you looking to replace a tooth quickly? Do your surrounding teeth already require crowns? Do you need a budget-friendly option? Or are you not a candidate for implants? A dental bridge may be the ideal option for you.

Those with a healthy jawbone and who want a more permanent solution may find dental implants the better choice. They work to preserve neighboring teeth and provide long-term benefits to oral health,

It is important to understand that every smile is unique and there is no one-size-fits-all solution. That’s why we take the time during a consultation to evaluate bone health, gum condition, bite alignment, and the like before making any decision on treatment.

All in all, both dental bridges and dental implants will replace missing teeth while improving comfort, appearance, and overall quality of life.
