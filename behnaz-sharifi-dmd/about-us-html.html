Proudly Serving Foothill Ranch, Orange County CA since 2018

Sharifi Cosmetic & Family Dentistry was established in 2018 by Dr. Behnaz Sharifi with the goal to serve the community of the Foothill Ranch and broader Orange County area.

Here at Sharifi Dentistry, everyone in the office strives to offer warm and compassionate care to all families of all ages. We treat our patients like family and invite every person to open and honestly ask any questions they may have about their oral health needs. Our team takes great pride in what we do best, leaving you smiling brightly with confidence! Every team member has a wide range of experiences and specialized skills that some have been curating for decades!

Visit our office and meet your new dedicated smile team! We are conveniently located with an abundance of parking and easy access along with many other medical professionals on Towne Centre Drive! We offer a wide variety of services ranging from crowns, whitening, composite restorations, root canals, implant, gum and laser technology, and much more! Visit the team so many trust to be your next oral healthcare specialists and achieve the smile of your dreams.

Meet the Team

Dr. Sharifi is a graduate of Nova Southern University of Florida, NSU as a Doctor of Dental Medicine. Since 2005, she has been practicing with other associates to improve the oral health of her patients and brighten their smiles. In 2018, Dr. Sharifi opened up her own practice with 5 trusted team members in order to treat her patients like family. She is fluent in both English and Farsi and offers comprehensive care in both languages and encourages all to be open and honest so that she may provide the best care possible.
