The practice

Brooklyn Dental Collective is a woman-founded and woman-owned NYC dental practice with an emphasis on bespoke dental care. Individualized dentistry is an important key to optimal dental health, and it’s foundational to our practice philosophy.

At Brooklyn Dental Collective, we believe in a few simple concepts:

Life is challenging. There are bills to open and pay, appointments to not forget, deadlines to meet, dishes to do, passwords to recover, dogs to walk and kids to feed. It’s a lot.

We can’t do much about that, but we can make it easy for you to reach your dentist. Hop on our website or text us at any time of the day or night to make an appointment or ask a question, and we’ll get in touch with you as soon as we’re back in the office.

And when you have your first appointment, we’ll take up as little of your day as possible by doing a pre-visit phone call to go over your paperwork and discuss your goals. Not that you’ll mind being here—we make it pretty pleasant.

Our Brooklyn office is modern, clean, professional, everything you hope to see when you visit the dentist. And we’re also laid back. We want you to enjoy your time here. This should be a break in your day, not one more task to make your day hectic.

At its most basic level, dentistry is meant to keep you chewing and talking without pain or other problems. We do that!

We also take things a bit farther to help you achieve your best bite function and most brilliant smile, because we know the health of the mouth reflects and affects the whole body’s health.
