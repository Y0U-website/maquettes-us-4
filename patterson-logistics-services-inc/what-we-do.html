What We Do

We deliver Trusted Expertise, Unrivaled Support every day

Patterson strengthens the oral and animal health markets in North America and the United Kingdom. From small private practices to large group networks and production operations, we guide our customers with bold solutions and a personal touch.

We are much more than a distributor – we are an indispensable partner. As a 145+-year-old company, we are leaders in our industries, well known and well regarded for a number of factors.

Service excellence is the cornerstone of our business. At Patterson, customers find unrivaled expertise as we provide the latest technology, software, equipment and innovations to save time, add profitability and help them deliver the best healthcare.

Dedicated people Whether a local rep or a team devoted to an entire division, customers always have a go-to person for questions and troubleshooting. Our teams make it easy and convenient for our customers to do business with us.

Business solutions include everything from patient education to consulting on large capital equipment to drive business and differentiation in a customer’s market.

Unrivaled expertise Our customers can invest in their practices knowing they are supported by the expertise of the industry’s most responsive sales, service and support teams.

Ease and convenience We make it easy to do business and our unwavering customer focus provides the confidence that we will be there when and how our customers need us.

Dentists, veterinarians and producers of protein are our customers

Our job is to make the business of running a dental practice, a vet clinic or a production facility easier. We do that by carrying all the supplies these industries need – but also with the Patterson difference: unrivaled service along with expertise in solutions for their business.

Dental

Patterson Dental has strengthened the dental industry since our company’s founding in 1877. Today, Patterson Dental continues to partner with dental practices of all sizes with expertise, products, technologies and services needed to grow productive, modern practices and keep them running smoothly.

Vet

Patterson Veterinary is the largest distributor for companion veterinarians, including equine veterinarians. We provide all the technology, products, expertise and solutions needed to deliver exceptional animal care and drive profitability across the companion, production and equine markets. Our Equine division is the only one of its kind, with people and products devoted exclusively to horses.

Animal Health International

Animal Health International provides veterinarians and producers with technology, products, expertise and solutions needed to drive profitability and ensure the wellness and productivity of the animals in their care. Producers of beef, dairy, swine, poultry and mixed veterinary practices can safely and sustainably deliver wholesome products knowing they are supported by our dedicated, knowledgeable sales, service and support teams – and an unmatched distribution network.

Service is what sets us apart

Our legendary customer service dates back to our founding in 1877 and accounts for the longevity of our company. All products, software and equipment are backed by our expert customer service. Extensive training means our team is able to resolve issues during the first call.

Locally, our service technicians repair and maintain all the pieces of equipment we sell – even those we don’t. Highly trained, they’re experts in the field, helping with installation, training and preventative maintenance as well.

National and local teams

Our 7,500+ Patterson employees serve thousands of customers across the globe, with corporate offices in St. Paul, Minnesota; Loveland, Colorado; Edmonton, Alberta; Stoke-on-Trent, U.K. Local branches ensure our customers can meet person to person whenever they would like.

Logistics – state of the art and lightning fast

Health care can’t wait, so our more than 70 fulfillment centers with over 2.6 million square feet are strategically located across North America, providing superior service levels. Utilizing a mix of Patterson self-delivery fleets and best-in-class third-party delivery partners, we’re able to consistently provide our customers with fast shipping and a wide array of products and services.

Products – diverse portfolio of quality items

Patterson works with thousands of suppliers to support the needs of our customers and the communities we serve. How we conduct business is as important as the products and services we offer our dental and animal health customers. Patterson strives every day to protect the trust that our business partners, customers, and consumers place in Patterson.

From the time products are loaded onto our dock until they reach our customer, we apply policies, procedures, employee training, communication, auditing and monitoring that strive to maintain product safety by complying with applicable laws and regulations.
