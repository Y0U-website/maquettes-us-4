Kid Friendly Advancements and Enhancements

From the moment you enter our office, you are greeted with a smile. We want you and your family to have a comfortable and relaxing experience while in our care. We provide preventative family dentistry, such as regular checkups and cleanings. It is important to schedule regular appointments with the dentist, recommended every six months, so that they can catch any problems early enough.

  • Child related coloring books, games and books
  • Light covers with balloons, flowers, clouds and fish
  • Laser Dentistry virtually no shots , no drilling , and no pain
  • Children are encouraged to bring a friend (favorite toy or stuffed animal for moral support or in the modern world, their iPads)
  • Explanations in words that children understand and showing the child what we plan to do helps ease anxiety
  • Adolescents are treated as young adults
  • We encourage question asking from children as well as their parents.
  • Preventative Dentistry with sealants and preventive resin restorations
  • Invisalign
  • Sports Mouth Guards
  • Sealants a protective barrier against decay for newly erupted teeth
