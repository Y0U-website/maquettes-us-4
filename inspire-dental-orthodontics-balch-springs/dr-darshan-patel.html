Hello, I’m Dr. Darshan Patel proud to be a Garland dentist at Inspire Dental and Orthodontics . I grew up in Garland, Texas After earning my BA in Biology from UT Austin, I pursued my passion for dentistry at Baylor College of Dentistry. It’s incredibly rewarding to make a difference in their oral health and overall well-being. I also enjoy giving back to the community through dental outreach, which has always been an important part of my practice.

When I’m not at the office, I love exploring new coffee shops, diving into anything astronomy-related, reading, staying active with outdoor activities, and spending time with my family and friends.
