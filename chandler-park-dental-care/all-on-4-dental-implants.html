frequently asked questions .

All-on-4 is a full-arch tooth replacement method that uses four dental implants to support a complete set of fixed teeth. The two back implants are placed at an angle to make the most of your available bone, often avoiding the need for bone grafting. The result is a permanent, non-removable arch of teeth that looks and functions naturally.

Traditional implant treatment replaces individual teeth, with one implant supporting one crown or a few implants supporting a bridge. All-on-4 replaces an entire arch at once using only four implants, making it more efficient and often more affordable than placing many individual implants. It is designed specifically for patients missing most or all teeth in an arch.

Traditional dentures rest on the gums, can slip while eating or speaking, and are removed daily. All-on-4 teeth are fixed to implants and stay in your mouth permanently, functioning like natural teeth. They do not require adhesives, restore far more chewing power, and preserve the jawbone, which continues to shrink under conventional dentures.

These temporaries stay in place while your implants heal. Once the implants have fully fused with the bone, typically after a few months, they are replaced with your stronger, final custom teeth.

Most adults missing all or most teeth in an arch, or facing the loss of failing teeth, are candidates. Because the angled implant design maximizes existing bone, many patients who were told they lacked bone for implants can still qualify without grafting. We confirm candidacy with a 3D scan and health review during your consultation.

The procedure is performed with anesthesia, and sedation options are available so you are comfortable throughout. Most patients report that recovery is more manageable than expected, with swelling and soreness for a few days that is well controlled with medication. Many return to normal light activity within a few days.

The implants themselves can last a lifetime with good care, as they fuse permanently with your jawbone. The attached arch of teeth is highly durable and typically lasts 10 to 15 years or longer before normal wear may call for maintenance or replacement. Good hygiene and regular checkups are the keys to long-term success.

You care for All-on-4 ® teeth much like natural teeth: brush thoroughly and clean beneath the arch daily using tools we recommend, such as a water flosser or special brushes. Regular professional cleanings let us maintain the restoration and check the health of your implants and gums. Your hygiene routine is simple but important.
