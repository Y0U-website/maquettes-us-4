About Our Pediatric Dentist in Wellesley, MA. At Perla Pediatric Smiles , we are a boutique pediatric dental practice in Wellesley, MA, dedicated to creating a positive dental experience for children and teens. Our compassionate team loves working with kids and strives to make every visit fun, supportive, and stress-free.

We provide gentle pediatric dental care in a welcoming environment designed specifically for young patients. From routine checkups to specialized treatments, our team offers a full range of pediatric dental services to support every child’s oral health. Learn more about what to expect during your child’s first visit.

Our practice proudly serves families in Wellesley, Newton , Needham , Natick, Framingham, Weston , and Waltham, offering personalized care tailored to each child’s needs. We are also experienced in caring for children with special healthcare needs, ensuring that every patient receives the attention, patience, and support they deserve.

Dr. Niloufar Moaziniya, board-certified pediatric dentist serving families in Wellesley, MA.

Dr. Niloufar Moaizniya (Dr. Nilo)

Dr. Niloufar Moaziniya, known to many families as Dr. Nilo, is a board-certified pediatric dentist committed to providing gentle, compassionate dental care for children and teens.

She earned her dental degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and completed a two-year pediatric dentistry residency at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where she gained extensive experience in:

• hospital dentistry • restorative pediatric dental care • sedation and behavior management • general anesthesia for complex pediatric cases

Dr. Nilo specializes in caring for children of all ages, including patients with special needs, and is passionate about creating positive dental experiences that help kids feel safe and confident during every visit.

As a mother, she understands how important it is for families to feel comfortable and supported when choosing a dentist. She approaches every patient with kindness, patience, and the highest standard of pediatric dental care.

• American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry • American Board of Pediatric Dentistry • International Association of Pediatric Dentistry • American Dental Association • Massachusetts Dental Society

Outside the Office Outside of the practice, Dr. Nilo enjoys spending time with her husband and daughter, exploring new foods, swimming, hiking, and staying active.
