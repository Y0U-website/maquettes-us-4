Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is Invisalign faster than braces? In some cases Invisalign can be quicker than braces, especially for mild to moderate cases. Treatment time depends on your individual needs and how often you wear your aligners. Invisalign works fast because each set of aligners is custom made to move your teeth incrementally. During your Invisalign consult we will give you an estimate of treatment time. Patients love knowing how long their treatment will be.
  • Do you wear Invisalign all day? Yes, to get the best results you need to wear your Invisalign aligners for 20-22 hours a day. They are designed to be worn all day and night so your teeth can move continuously. You can remove them for meals and to brush your teeth but you need to stick to the recommended schedule. If you wear them less than the advised time your treatment may take longer than expected. Consistency is key to get your desired results on time.
  • Can Invisalign cause problems? While rare, you may experience slight discomfort or speech adjustments as your teeth move. It’s normal to feel a bit of pressure the first few days of wearing a new set of aligners as this means they are working. Some patients may notice slight changes in speech initially but this usually resolves within a few days. Our team is always here to address your concerns during your Invisalign appointments. By following the care instructions you can minimize any potential problems and have a smooth treatment.
  • Can you tell if someone has Invisalign? Invisalign aligners are almost invisible. The clear plastic blends in with your natural teeth so they are hard to see. Most people won’t even notice unless they look very closely. That’s why Invisalign is a popular choice for those who want discreet orthodontic treatment. You can smile, speak and interact without feeling self conscious.
  • Does Invisalign cause headaches? Mild headaches can occur the first few days of a new set of aligners as your teeth move. This is because your teeth are adjusting to the gentle pressure of the aligners. Most patients find the headaches are temporary and go away within a day or two. Stay hydrated and take over the counter pain relief if needed. If the problem persists let us know at your next visit and we’ll make sure you’re comfortable.
