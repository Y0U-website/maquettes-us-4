Why Choose InStyle Dental?

Whether it is a simple cleaning or involves needing to meet with a Botox specialist , we always strive to meet your dental needs. We prioritize the patient’s chief concerns while stressing the importance of comprehensive exams to achieve optimal oral health.

Our team of doctors offer a variety of professional orthodontist and prosthodontist services for all ages. You will find a complete range of options for your dental, periodontal, implant, and prosthodontic treatments. Here at InStyle Dental, we take a holistic approach to general , restorative , and cosmetic dentistry focusing on the person as a whole and not just the teeth.

We encourage you to browse our website or give us a call to learn more about our practice and services ! We take pride in serving patients across New York, NY; Dobbs Ferry, NY; and the surrounding areas.
