Types of Dental Implants

Dr. Hanna Zharkina DDS, a highly trained prosthodontist serving patients across New York City, offers a full range of dental implants NYC patients can rely on. Every treatment plan is fully personalized based on your oral health, bone density, and smile goals.

Single Dental Implants Single dental implants are ideal for replacing one missing tooth. This option restores the function and appearance of your smile without affecting neighboring teeth. Dr. Zharkina uses the most advanced techniques to ensure the implant looks and feels like your natural teeth.

Implant-Retained Bridges For patients missing multiple teeth, implant-retained bridges provide a durable and natural-looking solution. This method avoids the need for traditional bridges that rely on adjacent teeth for support. Instead, the implants serve as a sturdy base, protecting your oral health and jawbone structure.

Implant-Retained Dentures - Overdentures Say goodbye to loose dentures with implant-retained overdentures. This solution combines the stability of dental implants with the convenience of removable dentures, ensuring better function and comfort for patients.

All-On-4 Restoration All-On-4 is a revolutionary procedure for patients needing a full-arch replacement. Using just four implants, Dr. Zharkina can secure a full set of replacement teeth, giving you a beautiful smile in less time.

Mini Dental Implants (To Stabilize Dentures) Mini dental implants are smaller in size but offer big benefits. They are often used to stabilize dentures, making them more secure and comfortable. This less invasive option is perfect for patients with minimal bone density.

Sedation for Dental Implants We understand that dental implant surgery can feel intimidating. That’s why we offer sedation options to keep you relaxed and comfortable throughout the procedure. Your comfort and peace of mind are our top priorities.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Getting dental implants is a multi-step process designed to ensure long-lasting results and a natural-looking smile. At InStyle Dental, we follow a detailed approach to make your journey as smooth and comfortable as possible. Here’s what you can expect during the dental implant procedure:

Personal Consultation

The journey begins with a personal consultation with Dr. Zharkina. During this visit, your oral health will be thoroughly reviewed, including an examination and X-rays. Dr. Zharkina will determine if dental implants are the right option for you based on factors such as bone density, overall health, and specific dental needs.

Grow your business

A personalized treatment plan is created, outlining the steps needed for your dental implant procedure. If necessary, preparatory procedures like bone grafting NYC or a sinus lift will be planned to ensure a stable foundation for the implants.

Preparatory Procedures (if needed)

Bone grafting, if the jawbone lacks sufficient density, a bone graft may be performed to strengthen the area for implant placement. Sinus lift, if the upper jaw lacks bone near the sinuses, this procedure adds bone to create adequate support for the implants.

Dental Implant Surgery

The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone during a straightforward procedure. Local anesthesia or sedation options are used to ensure your comfort. The implant is positioned to integrate with the jawbone, forming a secure base for replacement teeth.

Healing Period

After the surgery, a healing period of several weeks to months is required for osseointegration, where the implant fuses with the jawbone. Temporary teeth may be provided during this time to maintain functionality and appearance.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant has successfully integrated with the bone, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant. The abutment serves as the anchor for the custom replacement teeth.

Crown or Bridge Attachment

A custom-designed crown, bridge, or denture is created to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth. This restoration is securely attached to the abutment, completing the dental implant process.

Final Adjustments and

Smile Reveal

Dr. Zharkina will ensure the new teeth fit comfortably and align perfectly with your natural bite. After any necessary adjustments, your new, fully restored smile is revealed!

FAQs

  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution, and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. Maintaining good oral hygiene, including daily brushing and flossing, is essential for their longevity. Regular visits to the dentist for cleanings and checkups also help ensure the implants stay in excellent condition. Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants are highly durable and resistant to decay. Taking care of your overall health and avoiding habits like smoking can further extend the lifespan of your dental implants.
  • How many dental implants should I get? The number of dental implants you need depends on the extent of your tooth loss and your individual treatment goals. If you’re missing a single tooth, one implant with a custom crown is usually sufficient. For multiple missing teeth, implants can support a bridge or even a full set of dentures. During your consultation, we’ll assess your oral health and determine the most effective and cost-efficient solution. Whether you need one implant or several, we’ll design a plan that ensures your smile is fully restored.
