Our Dental Hygiene Services

At InStyle Dental, we offer a full range of dental hygiene services designed to help patients maintain optimal oral health while preventing common dental problems.

Professional Teeth Cleaning and Polishing Professional teeth cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains that accumulate over time and cannot be eliminated through brushing and flossing alone. Regular cleanings help protect your teeth and gums while promoting long-term oral health.

Plaque and Tartar Removal Plaque is a sticky bacterial film that forms on teeth daily. When plaque hardens into tartar, it can only be removed through professional dental care. Our team carefully removes plaque and tartar deposits to reduce the risk of cavities and gum disease.

Gum Disease Prevention and Root Planing Gum disease prevention starts with early intervention and care. We offer deep cleaning procedures including root planing to treat and manage gum disease. These treatments reduce inflammation, stop the progression of periodontal disease and support long term oral health.

Patient Education on Home Care Practices Education is key to maintaining oral health between visits. Our team will give you personalized tips and guidance on brushing, flossing and overall hygiene. By knowing how to do it correctly you can keep your smile healthy and bright.

Benefits of Dental Hygiene

Good dental hygiene provides more than just a sparkling smile. Here are some of the top benefits:

  • Prevents Oral Diseases : Routine cleanings help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other common dental problems. Early detection and care protect your teeth and gums in the long run.
  • Improves Overall Health : Oral health is closely linked to conditions like heart disease and diabetes. Keeping your mouth healthy supports your overall well-being.
  • Boosts Confidence : A clean, bright smile can significantly improve your self-esteem. Fresh breath and healthy teeth make social interactions more comfortable.
  • Supports Long-Term Savings : Preventive dental care reduces the need for costly treatments. Investing in regular hygiene visits now can save you money later.
  • Promotes Healthier Habits : Personalized education helps you adopt better brushing, flossing, and hygiene techniques. These improved habits support optimal oral health every day.
  • Prepares for Dental Hygiene Licensure : The dental hygiene program prepares students for national and state board examinations, ensuring they are eligible for dental hygiene licensure. This licensure opens up diverse opportunities in the field, allowing dental hygienists to take on various roles.

The Dental Hygiene Process

At InStyle Dental we use a structured dental hygiene process to deliver efficient and comfortable care. Here’s what to expect during your visit:

Assessment

We start with a full assessment of your oral health and medical history. This includes a review of your dental habits, lifestyle and any medical conditions to create a personalized care plan. Visual examination and diagnostic tools such as X-rays if needed to detect any problems early.

Diagnosis

Using the dental hygiene diagnosis process we identify problems such as plaque, gum disease or other oral health issues. This step allows us to pinpoint the areas that need attention and ensure your treatment is tailored to you.

Cleaning

Our hygienists remove plaque, tartar and stains from your teeth. This includes scaling to the gum line and polishing to leave your teeth feeling smooth and clean. Our dental hygiene practice emphasizes practical training and competencies required in the field to deliver high quality care.

Education

Education is part of our process. We give you personalized tips and tools to help you maintain your oral health at home including brushing and flossing techniques. We also discuss infection control and other prevention methods to support your long term health.

Therapeutic Services

For patients who require more attention we offer targeted therapeutic treatments. These include deep cleaning procedures like root planing to treat gum disease or nitrous oxide analgesia if needed.

Our goal is to leave you with a clean healthy smile and the knowledge to maintain it between visits. By following this process we deliver the highest standard of dental hygiene care.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Hygiene

  • Can dental hygienists do fillings? Hygienists are experts in cleaning and preventive care, but they don’t do fillings. Fillings are a restoration procedure that only licensed dentists can do. However, hygienists are important in identifying cavities or areas of concern during cleanings. They work with the dentist to ensure patients get timely and comprehensive care. By catching problems early, hygienists play a big role in your oral health.
  • Can hygienists do teeth whitening? Yes, hygienists can do teeth whitening under a dentist’s supervision. At InStyle Dental, our hygienists are trained in safe and effective whitening methods that deliver results. Using the latest techniques and tools, they will make the process quick, comfortable, and tailored to you. Whitening done by professionals helps you get whiter teeth while protecting your enamel. Book an appointment with our team to see if teeth whitening is for you.
  • How much does it cost to clean all teeth? The cost of a professional clean can vary depending on the level of cleaning required and your dental insurance. At InStyle Dental, we give you clear and upfront pricing at the time of appointment. Most insurance covers the majority of the cost, so it’s an affordable way to look after your oral health. If you have no insurance, we have flexible payment options to suit you. Contact us to get a quote tailored to your situation.
  • Is professional teeth cleaning worth it? Yes, professional cleaning is worth it. Cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can’t. This preventive measure helps prevent cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems. Plus, cleanings leave your teeth feeling smooth, your breath fresh, and boost your confidence. Investing in professional cleaning saves you money in the long run by preventing costly treatments.
  • How many times should you get a dental cleaning? Most patients should have a professional clean every 6 months for optimal oral health.
