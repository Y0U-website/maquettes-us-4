The practice

Dr. Sam Sadati stands at the pinnacle of excellence in cosmetic and implant dentistry, earning recognition as South Florida’s only American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD) Accredited Fellow—a distinction shared by fewer than 80 dental professionals worldwide. Renowned for his expertise, Dr. Sadati not only achieved this prestigious status but also dedicates his time to mentoring and serving…

His achievements extend far beyond, holding Diplomate status with the American Board of Oral Implantology (DABOI), Fellowship and Mastership status with the Academy of General Dentistry (MAGD and FAGD), Diplomate and Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantology (DICOI/FICOI), and prestigious fellowships with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (FAAID), and the International…

Dr. Sadati, a distinguished leader in dentistry, is the only dentist to hold such an elite combination of credentials. Committed to giving back, he contributed as an adjunct clinical faculty member at NOVA Southeastern University, mentoring and inspiring the next generation of dental professionals.

“For many dentists, dentistry is just a job. For me, dentistry is a passion. I believe this is the difference between ordinary and exceptional care. I love to design healthy, beautiful smiles for my patients. No words can express that joyful, wonderful moment!”
