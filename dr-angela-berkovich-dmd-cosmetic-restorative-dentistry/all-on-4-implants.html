What is an All-on-4?

Permanent dentures with All-on-4 implants are held firmly in place by posts inserted into your jawbone. The All-on-4 technique restores your smile with natural-looking, natural-feeling teeth if you’ve lost teeth due to decay, injury, or aging.

Four, and occasionally six, implants hold and support a fixed restoration known as an All-on-4 or All-on-6. They are fixed, feel and look very similar to your natural teeth, don’t need to be removed at night, and don’t cover the palate of your upper arch.

In addition, they are made of porcelain, which, in contrast to a set of dentures, is a strong and attractive material that needs little to no maintenance.

All on 4 Dental Implant FAQs

The All-On-4 dental implant procedure is a major advancement in dental implant surgery. With this cutting-edge treatment, a full arch replacement is made possible in a matter of hours, with a minimal amount of dental implants.

Using the All-on-4 procedure, a full set of top or bottom teeth can be replaced with only four dental implants per arch. We have a cutting-edge process that allows us to provide you with results the same day.

Only patients with significantly impacted teeth receive this therapy, which entails a full dental rehabilitation.

A denture or bridge that is supported by four fixed dental implants is referred to as a All-On-4 denture or bridge. Occasionally, overdentures are used in place of traditional dentures, which use suction or adhesive to stay in place. Overdentures snap over implants. By fastening screws to four strategically placed implants, the All-On-4 procedure securely fixes the bridge in place. Only a dentist is able to remove these because they are permanently glued in place. A typical All-On-4 procedure consists of 3 phases:

With All-on-4 implants, your recovery time will probably be substantially shorter than it would be with standard implants, which normally take three to eight months to heal. Better stability is promoted by the ability of an All-on-4 system to bond to your bone more readily than separate implants.

After surgery, you can carefully rinse your mouth with salt water to clean the implant site. As with your natural teeth, you can then take care of your dental implants in the same manner. Regular dental cleanings are crucial, as are daily teeth brushing and flossing. Prior to your surgery, our dental specialists will go through more specific oral hygiene recommendations that best suit your health needs.

You are qualified to receive All-on-4® for teeth-in-a-day treatment if your jawbone structure is sound. Dental implants might cling to healthy jawbone structure. The four implants won’t withstand forceful activities like food chewing if your jawbone doesn’t have enough volume and bone density.

Your permanent bridge is not put into the implants the same day in order to protect patient health. The majority of the time, if the implants fit comfortably on the accessible bone, patients don’t need to have bone grafting or sinus lifts. On the fitted implant, dental implant angle adjustment may occasionally be done to ensure a precise fit. The supply of artificial teeth is then completed with precise bridge placement.

Many patients plan to be away from work for many days, mistakenly thinking that getting All-on-4® implants is painful; however, it is a well-tolerated, minimally invasive out-patient procedure that takes about two hours per set in our dental office.

Will your All-on-4 implants appear fake? Absolutely not! You may get effective, long-lasting, and realistic-looking replacement teeth with the All-on-4 procedure. Only if you tell them will others know you have implant-supported dentures!

  • Preparation: Any currently compromised teeth are taken care of, as well as any diseased and damaged tissue.
  • Implants: To install implants, two titanium implants are positioned in the front of the jaw, and two more implants—two of which are angled—are positioned in the back. This provides a more solid and nearly permanent fit when compared to traditional dentures.
  • Placement: We’ll make any required changes to make sure your new ceramic dental bridge is comfortable and a perfect fit when we attach it to the implants. This contemporary procedure can be completed in a single day! Depending on your specific situation, healing could take three to six months.
