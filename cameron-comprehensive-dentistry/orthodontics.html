Our Orthodontic Services

We offer modern orthodontic options tailored to your lifestyle and needs, including:

  • Traditional Braces: Highly effective for correcting a wide range of orthodontic issues, using metal brackets and wires to gently shift teeth into proper alignment.
  • Clear Aligners (e.g., Invisalign®): A popular alternative for many adults and teens, clear aligners are virtually invisible, removable trays that gradually straighten teeth. This option offers greater flexibility for eating and oral hygiene.

The Orthodontic Journey at Our Cameron Dental Office

Your journey to a straighter smile begins with a thorough consultation:

  • Initial Assessment: Dr. Hutchison will evaluate your bite, teeth, and jaw structure using X-rays, photos, and digital scans.
  • Personalized Treatment Plan: We’ll discuss the best orthodontic option for you, outline the treatment duration, and answer all your questions.
  • Active Treatment: Regular appointments will be scheduled to monitor progress and make adjustments.
  • Retention: Once active treatment is complete, retainers are essential to maintain your new, straight smile.
