Dr Suveera Jammu

Restorative, Cosmetic & Family Dentistry

For Dr. Jammu, dentistry is more than a profession—it is a commitment to caring for people, earning their trust, and helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Her journey in dentistry spans two countries, two dental degrees, and years of dedication to her profession. She earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from Sri Guru Ram Das Institute of Dental Sciences & Research in Amritsar, India, where she practiced dentistry for five years before moving to the United States.

Determined to continue her passion for dentistry, Dr. Jammu took on the challenge of completing dental school again in the United States and earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

With experience practicing dentistry in both India and the United States, Dr. Jammu brings a thoughtful and well-rounded approach to patient care. She believes great dentistry begins with listening, building trust, and educating patients so they can make informed decisions about their oral health. Her approach is compassionate, conservative, honest, and personalized to each patient’s needs.

Whether providing preventive, restorative, or family dental care, Dr. Jammu strives to make every patient feel comfortable, respected, and genuinely cared for.

Outside of dentistry, she enjoys spending time with her husband and their two young children. Her family keeps her grounded and reminds her of the importance of treating every patient with the same care and compassion she would want for her own family.

Dr. Jammu is grateful to call California home and looks forward to caring for families in her community and building lasting relationships with her patients.

“My promise is simple: I want every patient to feel heard, respected, and genuinely cared for.

I will take the time to listen to your concerns, explain your options clearly, and help you make decisions that are right for you. My goal is not only to improve your oral health and smile, but also to create a comfortable experience and build lasting relationships based on trust, honesty, and compassion.
