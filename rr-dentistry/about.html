Welcome to RR Dentistry

We have taken care of Georgetown smiles since 1996. Our team believes in combining state-of-the-art technology with Texan hospitality. This is why our mission is to reflect Christ through compassionate care, restored smiles, and exceptional experiences.

RR Dentistry is a superior restorative, cosmetic, and sedation dental practice located in Georgetown, Texas. Our practice offers a wide array of cutting-edge dental technologies and treatments that improve the appearance of patients’ smiles and help them breathe easier.

Some of the many dental treatments available at RR Dentistry include laser-assisted apical endodontic surgery with a 3D microscope to treat tooth infections, Invisalign®, KöR® teeth whitening, CEREC® same-day crowns, bridges, dental implants, and dentures, as well as veneers, fillings, and professional teeth cleanings.

Other service offerings include gum surgery using a Waterlase® laser and treatments for sleep-disordered breathing, such as HealthyStart® orthodontics, Solea® Sleep laser treatments for snoring, and custom-fitted Vivos System® oral appliances.

The skilled dentists at RR Dentistry take a comprehensive, patient-centered approach to treatment. They look forward to improving patients’ smiles and enhancing their sleep quality. Call the RR Dentistry office to schedule an appointment or book online today.
