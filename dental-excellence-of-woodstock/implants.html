Implants

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that serve as anchors for replacement teeth. They are made of titanium, a metal that is biocompatible with the human body, allowing it to be safely fused with the jawbone through osseointegration. The implant is surgically placed into the jaw and allowed to be bonded to the bone, creating a stable base for an artificial tooth.

A dental implant restoration consists of three parts: the post, the abutment, and the restoration. The post is a screw-like metal piece inserted into the jawbone. The abutment is the connector piece that attaches to the post and holds the restoration in place. The restoration is the crown or bridge that is attached to the abutment. At Dental Excellence Of Woodstock, dental implants can help replace a single missing tooth, multiple missing teeth, or even an entire arch of teeth, as they can be used to support crowns, bridges, or dentures.

The Procedure for Dental Implants

The dental implant process begins with a thorough oral exam and X-rays to determine if a patient is qualified for the procedure. If the patient’s gums and jaw are healthy, the dentist will move forward with treatment planning. Once the treatment plan is finalized, our dentist will begin the procedure.

First, the dentist in Woodstock, GA, will make a small incision in the gum tissue where the implant will be placed. Then, they will drill a hole into the bone and insert the implant. Over the next several months, the bone will heal and fuse with implant through osseointegration. Once it has healed, the dentist will place an abutment on the implant and secure a crown on top.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Prevent Bone Loss

When you lose a tooth or multiple teeth, the bone in your jaw begins to deteriorate. This bone loss can lead to further tooth loss, facial sagging, and even changes in your bite. Dental implants can prevent this bone loss by stimulating the jawbone the same way tooth roots do. The implant post is inserted into the missing tooth’s bone socket, which stimulates the bone and prevents it from deteriorating.

Better Speech

Missing teeth can affect the way you speak, making you slur your words or make pronouncing certain words difficult. Dental implants in Woodstock, GA, look and function like natural teeth, so you won’t have to worry about speech impediments.

Improved Oral Health

Missing teeth can cause various oral health issues. For example, the gap left by the missing tooth can trap food particles and bacteria, leading to tooth decay and gum disease. The teeth surrounding the gap can become loose and shift out of place. You can avoid these issues and maintain good oral health by replacing missing teeth with implants.
