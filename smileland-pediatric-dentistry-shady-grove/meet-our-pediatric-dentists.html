Karim Elrafei, D.D.S.

Dr. Karim Elrafei, born in Boston, MA, attended the University of Maryland Baltimore County and received a Bachelor of Science in Biology. Upon graduating from college he attended the University of Maryland School of Dentistry where he received his DDS. Dr. Elrafei practiced general dentistry for a number of years, most recently in Hyattsville, MD and downtown Baltimore at Health Care for the Homeless.

His training in Pediatric Dentistry was completed at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry where he served as Chief Resident. Residency offered him the great opportunity of treating children in underserved communities and those with special health care needs, as well as exposure to treatment under various forms of sedation.

Dr. Elrafei lives in Ellicott City, MD and spends his time enjoying various forms of science fiction with his younger brother; who is twice his size. In addition, he embarks on quests to explore exotic food with the rest of his family.

Iman Soleymani, D.D.S

Dr. Iman Soleymani is a caring and dedicated pediatric dentist who strives to make every child’s dental visit a positive and confident experience. With a genuine passion for working with children, he focuses on creating a welcoming and comfortable environment for both kids and their families. Trained and qualified in providing care under sedation and general anesthesia, Dr. Iman is prepared to safely manage children with a wide range of needs.

Dr. Iman earned his Bachelor of Science from George Mason University and a Master in Biomedical Science from Barry University. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Howard University College of Dentistry, completed advanced education in general dentistry at NYU Langone, and continued his pediatric specialty training at Saint Christopher Hospital for Children in Philadelphia, PA.

Outside the office, Dr. Iman enjoys spending time with his wife and two daughters, traveling, trying new foods, making pottery, and watching Disney movies together. He is passionate about giving back to the community and supporting children’s health and well being beyond the dental chair.
