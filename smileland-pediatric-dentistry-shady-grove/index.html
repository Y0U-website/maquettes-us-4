Pediatric dentists have had 2-3 years of additional, specialized training to care for young children and adolescents. From the first tooth to adolescence, we help your child develop a healthy smile and manage situations that are unique only to children.

Our pediatric dental office is designed to ease nervous children (and their parents!) with the best, kid-friendly dental atmosphere. We take extra time to understand the personality of each child and our team works hard to accommodate every personality – from super chatty to super shy.
