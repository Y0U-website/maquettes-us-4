Meet Your Ponte Vedra Beach Dentist

Eric L. Townsend, DDS

Dr. Townsend is a Jacksonville native. He and his wife, Joy, have been married since 1981 and have two sons, Eric Jr. and Jared. Dr. Townsend volunteers his time to mission organizations that provide dental care to underprivileged children in Mexico and Romania. Outside the office, he enjoys boating, fishing, woodworking and is an avid Florida Gators Football Fan!
