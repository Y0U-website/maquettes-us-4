What Are Dental Implants?

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

When you choose to get dental implants in Ponte Vedra Beach, our team will make sure that you’re fully aware of every step that will need to be taken in order to help you rebuild your smile from the roots up. While the dental implant process isn’t exactly the same for every patient, it can generally be broken down into four main stages. Below is a brief overview of these stages and what you can expect from them.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

You’ll start your journey toward a recompleted smile by visiting our office for your initial consultation. During this visit, your implant dentist in Ponte Vedra Beach will perform a thorough examination of your mouth and jaw in order to determine whether dental implants are a good fit for you. If they are, we’ll explain what you can expect during the rest of the dental implant process and work with you to create a customized treatment plan.

Note that not every patient will be able to have dental implants placed right away. In many cases, you may need to have certain types of preliminary dental work done before you can receive your implant posts. Such procedures may include bone grafting, gum disease treatment, or tooth extractions.

Dental Implant Surgery

On the day of your dental implant surgery, a small incision will be created in your gums. This will give us access to your jawbone, allowing us to place the implant posts. The location and angle of the posts will be carefully determined ahead of time. Once the posts are in place, your gums will be sutured shut.

You don’t need to worry about experiencing discomfort during your dental implant surgery. Before the procedure begins, we’ll administer a local anesthetic to numb your mouth; as a result, you may feel some pressure during the treatment, but there will be little to no pain.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

After the surgery, you’ll need to wait for your implant posts to fuse with the bone around them. This is called osseointegration, and it’s essential for giving your dental implants the stability they need to act as reliable artificial tooth roots.

After about 3 to 6 months, osseointegration will be complete, and you’ll be ready to receive your abutments. These are small metal connector pieces that are carefully attached to your implant posts.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

The last step of the dental implant process is to design and place the prosthesis that will act as your replacement tooth or teeth. Impressions of your mouth will be taken in order to design a natural-looking crown, bridge, or denture. Once the prosthesis is complete, we can secure it to your implant posts via the abutments, thus giving you the confident new smile that you deserve.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants can do much more than replace missing teeth. Not only will they provide greater stability and longevity, but they can even improve other aspects of your oral and overall health. Our team will walk you through the process as well as the benefits of dental implants so that you can determine if this is a viable treatment option for you. Until then, here are some of the advantages you can expect to enjoy with these tooth replacements.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • Maximum Comfort : Dental implants, unlike other restorations/prosthetics that rely on your gums or other natural teeth, will be supported directly by your jawbone. This means you shouldn’t feel any discomfort from them as you might expect with options like dentures or dental bridges.
  • Boosted Confidence : Since dental implants are embedded into the jawbone, you shouldn’t expect them to move out of place like other treatment options. Due to their stability and lifelike function, you can feel extra confident that they won’t move around while eating, speaking, or smiling.
  • No Dietary Restrictions : With your new titanium posts, you’ll be feel to enjoy 90 % of your natural biting power, meaning you can eat all of your favorite foods without much worry.

Health Benefits

  • Preserve the Jawbone : Dental implants will serve as your new tooth roots, meaning they’ll stimulate proper circulation and promote healthy bone growth in the long run. This also means your metal posts can help maintain your strong and youthful jawbone, preventing it from shrinking over time.
  • Improved Oral Health : Since dental implants help prevent jawbone deterioration, any other natural teeth can remain in place. With this treatment, you’ll be able to avoid further tooth loss as well as other issues like malnutrition and indigestion.
  • Protection for Natural Teeth : Dental bridges often require healthy teeth to be altered to make room for the prosthetic. Implants, however, don’t require removing natural enamel from existing teeth.

Long-Term Benefits

  • Youthful Appearance : Dental implants are the only tooth replacement solution that can rebuild missing teeth from the roots up while maintaining your jawbone. In turn, this means you’ll be able to preserve a more youthful facial structure in the long term.
  • Long-Lasting & Successful Results : Not only are dental implants incredibly durable and natural-feeling, but they can also last several decades to the rest of your life with proper care. They also have a success rate of over 95 % even 10 years after being placed.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

If it’s not your jawbone that is the problem but your oral health, there are steps you’ll need to take to address the problem before you receive your dental implants. If you have gum disease or tooth decay, it will be imperative that you have the necessary procedures to eliminate these issues. Otherwise, it could negatively impact your implant after it has been placed.

Missing 1 Tooth

For those missing one tooth, an implant supported dental crown will be recommended. A custom crafted, tooth-colored dental crown is attached to an implant post, recreating the root and crown of missing teeth.

Missing Multiple Teeth

As an alternative to a traditional bridge, you can enjoy the stability of dental implants. 1 to 2 implant posts anchor it to your jawbone, so there is no need to reshape your adjacent teeth. The posts act as new roots to stimulate your jawbone to protect your remaining natural teeth from complications.

Missing All Teeth

If you are tired of living with the irritation and hassle of traditional dentures, you may be a candidate for full mouth replacement using dental implants. Traditional dentures rely on suction or an adhesive to hold them to your gum tissue. Implant dentures use the security of the implant posts. On average, 2 to 6 posts anchor your denture to your jawbone, so you will never need to worry about slipping or irritation. Special fixtures are placed on each post to attach your custom-made denture.

All-on-4 is another type of implant denture. This specialized method uses 4 implant posts to support a full custom-made denture. Often, we can fit you with a temporary denture the same day as your placement surgery, so you do not need to go without your teeth. After you have healed, your final denture is attached to your posts.

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

Types of Dental Implants

It is important to understand that no two dental implant cases are exactly alike. Patients who are missing a single tooth need just one dental implant. Logically, that costs less than using dental implants to replace an entire arch of lost teeth. Also, there are various dental implant techniques that can have a bearing on price. During your consultation, your dentist will begin to design your custom treatment plan, and our team will provide you with a cost estimate. Feel free to ask any questions you may have about the price information we give you.

The Stages of Dental Implant Treatment

Dental implant treatment takes place across multiple stages, each of which has its own unique price. Because some stages of the process happen weeks or months apart, you will have time to plan your budget for each portion of your journey. Some of the steps in your treatment process may include:

  • Your initial consultation
  • Preparatory treatment, such as gum disease therapy, extractions, or bone grafting
  • Your implant placement surgery
  • Placement of your implant abutments (small connector pieces that will hold your new teeth in place)
  • Design and placement of your implant-supported crown, bridge, or denture

Are Dental Implants Worth the Investment?

Most patients who receive dental implants would wholeheartedly agree that they are a worthwhile investment! They offer more and greater benefits than any other form of tooth replacement. For example, they provide an incredibly strong bite force, and they prevent the deterioration in the jawbone that often occurs after tooth loss. Plus, they have the potential to last a lifetime. Their longevity may make them a much better long-term monetary value than other tooth replacement options that have a lower upfront cost but that need to be replaced every few years, such as traditional dentures.

Does My Dental Insurance Cover Dental Implants?

Dental insurance policies vary greatly in their approach to dental implants. Some of them cover half the cost of implants, up to the amount of the plan’s annual maximum. Others do not cover the implants themselves, but they may cover preparatory treatments or the final restoration. Our team will help you navigate your insurance and get as much out of it as possible. We can also help you apply for low-interest financing through CareCredit, Community First, or Citi Health Card.

Dental Implant Post-Op Instructions

Congratulations on having your new dental implants surgically placed! Now it’s time for the recovery process to begin. But don’t worry! Below, your implant dentist in Ponte Vedra Beach will let you know what you can expect during this time period, including which symptoms are normal, and what you can do to help your newly restored teeth heal. Should you ever experience any intense, abnormal pain or other strange side-effects not mentioned here, please don’t hesitate to contact us for guidance.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

Immediately following your dental implant surgery, your biggest priority is to leave the forming blood clot(s) untouched so that healing happens smoothly. To that end, please be sure to follow these instructions:

  • Never spit; instead, wipe with tissues or swallow your saliva.
  • Do not use drinking straws.
  • Don’t smoke or use tobacco products for at least the first day.
  • Keep your fingers and tongue away from the surgical site(s).

Common Side Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

When recovering from dental implant surgery, some mild side-effects are perfectly normal and quite common, especially in the first several days after placement. These symptoms may include:

Each of these effects should be temporary and should gradually subside with time. If this is ever not the case, let us know as soon as possible.

  • Intermittent bleeding. To help lessen this, gently bite down on gauze or a soft washcloth. Bleeding may continue for multiple days.
  • Swelling over the first 72 hours that can last for over a week. Applying cold or warm compresses can provide relief.
  • General discomfort, which can be reduced or eliminated with OTC or prescription pain relievers as directed.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

In the first few days after your surgical dental implant placement, we recommend eating a softer, non-abrasive diet which includes foods such as:

You may resume a normal diet as soon as you feel up to it; however, we recommend limiting any overly crunchy or tough foods for as long as possible and avoiding directly chewing on the dental implant site(s).

  • Mashed potatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Hummus
  • Ice cream
  • Pudding
  • Soup (not extremely hot)
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Pasta

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

Caring for your dental implants in Ponte Vedra Beach as they heal is a must. Here are some best practices to ensure you do so successfully:

  • On the day after your surgical procedure, it is okay to resume brushing your teeth as usual but be very gentle around your surgical site(s).
  • Rinse your mouth well two or three times a day (ideally after eating) with saltwater. Use prescription mouth rinse if you have it.
  • Avoid any mouthwashes that contain high levels of alcohol such as Scope or Listerine.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

Congratulations! The hardest part of the dental implant journey is now behind you. When your new crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant(s), you may experience some minor, temporary sensitivity in the gum tissue, but this should be easy to control with pain medication. At this point, there should be no further swelling, bleeding, or extensive recovery. In fact, you should be ready to show off your newly restored smile that very same day!

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Congratulations on your new dental implants! These life-changing prosthetics have the amazing potential to keep your smile complete, healthy, and strong for a lifetime. However, that’s only as long as you take proper care of them. Neglecting to protect your dental implants from damage and infection could cause them to fail or need replacement. However, the great news is that with a few simple precautions and healthy habits, you’ll be able to enjoy your newly rebuilt smile for the rest of your life!

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Although they look and feel real, the good news about dental implants in Ponte Vedra Beach is that they cannot get cavities like your natural teeth can. However, this doesn’t mean that you don’t still need to practice excellent dental hygiene. That’s because even with dental implants, you can still get dangerous oral health conditions like gum disease and peri-implantitis that can cause them to fail.

Eat a Healthy Diet

The great news is that with dental implants, you can eat whatever you’d like with no restrictions! This means you can enjoy a full, nutritious diet. However, it’s important to avoid overindulging in hard, sticky, or sugary foods, as these can be problematic. Instead, select foods that are high in calcium and vitamin C to help keep your jawbone strong and your gum tissue healthy.

Break Bad Habits

Bad oral habits can cause your dental implants in Ponte Vedra Beach to break or fail. Thus, be sure to quit dangerous behaviors like smoking or using tobacco products, as these can slow down the healing process and encourage infection. Furthermore, never use your teeth (real or prosthetic) as tools, and avoid chewing on any hard objects like pen caps or fingernails, as these can damage your restorations.

Protect Your Dental Implants

While your dental implants are extremely strong, they’re not indestructible. Therefore, if you play sports be sure to wear a mouthguard to protect them, and if you have bruxism (teeth grinding) wear your nightguard to keep them safe from extra wear and tear.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Even if you have no remaining natural teeth, it is still extremely important to keep up with your regular dental checkups. Please be sure to continue visiting your implant dentist in Ponte Vedra Beach every six months for your preventive exams and cleanings so we can monitor your dental implants and spot any issues as soon as possible.

Does Dental Implant Surgery Hurt?

After your surgery is complete, you can expect some soreness and tenderness around the implant; however, this is completely normal. The actual process of having implants placed will not hurt in any way. This is because our team of dentists will administer anesthesia prior to beginning the surgery. This will make you comfortable and put you at ease, allowing you to feel no pain during the procedure.

Can I Take Dental Implants Out?

Honestly, no you can’t take dental implants out once they’re placed. Only a qualified dentist could remove them properly without harming your smile.

Don’t forget that dental implants fuse with your jawbone. As tissue grows and holds them in place, they become highly secure and firmly rooted. Trying to take them out yourself would damage your mouth, putting your grin in even worse shape. Meanwhile, a good dentist has the tools and resources to remove implants (when necessary) without causing harm.

The only exceptions to these facts are implant dentures. Some of these restorations can be removed for cleaning, depending on how they’re made.

Am I Too Old to Get Dental Implants?

No patient is ever too old to get dental implants. On the contrary, you can receive them in the latest years of your life.

What really impacts dental implant candidacy is one’s oral health. As long as your gum and jaw tissues are pretty strong, you can receive the posts in your 30s, 40s, or even your 80s and 90s. The implant treatment just moves a bit more slowly for older folks, and it involves a longer recovery.
