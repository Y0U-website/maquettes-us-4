Process for Getting a Dental Implant?

The dental implant in La Habra, CA involves several steps over 3-9 months. First, your dentist evaluates your oral health and jawbone density. If needed, bone grafting precedes implant placement. During surgery, a titanium post is inserted into the jawbone. After osseointegration (bone fusing to implant), an abutment is attached. Finally, a custom crown is placed on the abutment. The procedure requires local anesthesia and possibly sedation. Recovery varies between stages, with minor discomfort managed by medication. Success rates exceed 95 % with proper care and maintenance.
