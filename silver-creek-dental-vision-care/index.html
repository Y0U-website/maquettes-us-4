The practice

It is our privilege in 15 years (and counting) to serve over 15,000 patients in Everett and its vicinity.

Families have returned to our clinics year after year. Close relationship has been nourished over three generations. Our youngest patients are about 1 year old, and the oldest was 105 year old. We are proud of many young patients finishing high schools, attending colleges, and some graduating from dental and medical schools.

We continue to provide comfortable treatments at affordable fees, working within insurance allowance or family financial budget. Thank you for allowing us to take care of your dental and vision needs and look forward to serving you.
