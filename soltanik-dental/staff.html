OUR STAFF

Meet the dedicated professionals who make Soltanik Dental a trusted family practice with over 50 years of combined experience.

The practice is led by sibling dentists Dr. Valeria Soltanik and Dr. Fernando Soltanik, a sister-and-brother team serving patients in Aventura.

Meet Our Expert Team

Dedicated professionals with decades of experience, committed to providing exceptional dental care to our community.

Dr. Valeria Soltanik

Dr. Valeria Soltanik has been practicing dentistry in Florida for almost two decades. She obtained a Bachelors in Science, a major in Biology and minor in Chemistry from Barry University where she graduated as Magna Cum Laude.

Dr. Valeria Soltanik pursued her dental degree at Nova Southeastern University, as well as an Advanced Education in General Dentistry. She speaks fluent English, Spanish, Portuguese and French. She is very passionate about music, so much that she is a part of the Executive Committee of the New World Symphony.

Dr. Fernando Soltanik

Dr. Fernando Soltanik earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from Barry University. Before pursuing dentistry, he practiced law in New York and New Jersey after receiving his Juris Doctor from American University Washington College of Law.

He later earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, combining his analytical background with a patient-centered approach to care. Dr. Soltanik is committed to providing thorough, personalized treatment and helping patients feel comfortable and informed throughout their dental experience.

He is fluent in English, Spanish, and Portuguese.

Dr. Kamin Tehrani

"Dr. T" has been practicing dentistry for almost three decades. He is a Georgia State University graduate and obtained his dental degree from the New York University.

He speaks fluent English, Spanish and Farsi.
