Multiple Tooth Implants

When multiple teeth are missing in a row, strategically placed implants can support a fixed bridge without relying on neighboring teeth. This approach restores both function and aesthetics while maintaining the integrity of your remaining natural teeth and jawbone structure.

  • Replace 3-4 consecutive missing teeth with 2 implants
  • More stable than traditional bridges
  • Prevents further bone deterioration
  • Easier to clean and maintain than removable partials

Full Mouth Reconstruction

A complete smile transformation for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth. This comprehensive treatment combines multiple implants with custom prosthetics to restore full oral function, facial structure, and confidence. We design each case individually based on your bone quality, health status, and aesthetic goals.

  • Comprehensive evaluation and custom treatment plan
  • Combines implants, bone grafting if needed, and prosthetics
  • Restores facial contours and prevents premature aging
  • Permanent, fixed solution for edentulous patients

Full-Arch Implant Consultation

Patients who need to replace many or all teeth can request a full-arch implant evaluation. The dentist reviews oral health, bone support, medical history, restorative goals, alternatives, and whether referral or additional procedures may be appropriate before recommending a treatment path.

  • Individual examination and diagnostic planning
  • Discussion of fixed and removable tooth-replacement options
  • Review of timing, limitations, maintenance, and alternatives
  • Personalized estimate after the clinical evaluation

Dental Implant FAQs

How long do dental implants last? ➕

With proper hygiene and professional maintenance, implants can last several decades or even a lifetime. Crowns or prosthetics may need periodic replacement due to normal wear.

Am I a candidate if I have bone loss? ➕

Often yes. Bone grafting or use of angled implants can make treatment possible even after long-term tooth loss or denture wear.

Does insurance cover implants? ➕

Many plans contribute to portions like exams, imaging or the crown. We provide a pre-treatment estimate so you understand any out-of-pocket amounts.

Are implants painful? ➕

Most patients are surprised how comfortable the procedure is. Minor soreness usually resolves within a few days.

How much do dental implants cost in Aventura? ➕

Costs depend on number of implants, bone quality and restoration type. A personalized estimate follows your consultation & 3D assessment.

What if I need to replace all my teeth? ➕

Full arch solutions (All-on-X or implant overdentures) can restore an entire smile with fewer implants than replacing each tooth individually.
