All-on-X Dental Implants in Dry Ridge, KY

Living without most or all of your natural teeth changes nearly everything about how you eat, how you speak, and how you feel about yourself in social situations. Conventional dentures can restore some function, but they come with well-known limitations: they shift when you chew, require adhesive to stay in place, and do nothing to prevent the gradual bone loss that follows tooth loss. For patients who have spent years managing those tradeoffs, the question is not whether there is a better option. The question is whether it is accessible to them.

At Friendly Dental Co in Dry Ridge, KY, the answer is yes. Our team performs All-on-X full arch implant procedures as part of our comprehensive dental implant services, bringing one of dentistry’s most transformative tooth replacement solutions to Grant County patients who would otherwise need to travel to a major urban market to access this level of care. If you are dealing with extensive tooth loss or teeth that can no longer be saved, All-on-X may be the permanent solution you have been looking for.

The X in All-on-X refers to the number of implants used, which may vary depending on the patient’s bone density, jaw anatomy, and clinical situation. The most common protocols use four implants per arch, though some patients benefit from five or six. In each case, the posterior implants are typically angled to maximize contact with available bone, often allowing patients to proceed without bone grafting even when some bone loss has already occurred.

A longitudinal study published through the National Institutes of Health examining All-on-4 outcomes across 3 to 17 years of follow-up found implant survival rates of approximately 94.7 % for maxillary cases and 93 % for mandibular cases over extended observation periods, confirming that well-executed full arch implant treatment delivers highly predictable long-term outcomes.

Who Makes a Good Candidate for All-on-X?

All-on-X is designed for patients who are fully edentulous, meaning they have no remaining teeth in an arch, or for those whose remaining teeth are failing and cannot be saved through conventional treatment. It is also an excellent option for long-term denture wearers who are experiencing bone loss, instability, or chronic discomfort and are ready for a permanent, fixed solution.

Ideal candidates are in reasonably good overall health, do not have uncontrolled systemic conditions that would impair healing, and have sufficient bone volume to support implant placement. Patients who smoke are advised of the increased risk of complications and are counseled on managing that risk as part of the treatment planning process.

During your consultation, your doctor will conduct a thorough clinical examination, review your bone density and jaw structure using advanced imaging, and discuss whether All-on-X, a modified protocol, or a different approach is best suited to your specific anatomy and goals.

Will I leave with teeth on the same day as surgery? +

In most cases, yes.
