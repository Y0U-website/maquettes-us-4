Dr. Josh Thomas

Dr. Josh Thomas is Kentucky born and raised and proud to be caring for families in the community he calls home. He earned his undergraduate degree from the University of the Cumberlands before attending the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry, where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree.

After dental school, Dr. Thomas completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency through the United States Army. He then served for several years at Fort Stewart, Georgia, caring for service members and their families. His time in the Army strengthened the values he brings into the office every day: trust, service, discipline, and treating people the way he would want to be treated.

Dr. Thomas enjoys helping patients feel heard, comfortable, and confident in their care. He believes dentistry should be personal, thoughtful, and focused on helping people keep healthy, functional smiles for years to come.

Outside of the office, Dr. Thomas enjoys spending time with his wife and growing family, staying active, being outdoors, and staying involved in his community. He feels grateful for the opportunity to care for patients and build relationships that last.

Dr. Chlotielde Moran

Dr. Chlotielde Moran is a graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry and brings a love for restorative dentistry with a special emphasis on esthetics. She enjoys helping patients restore their smiles in a way that feels natural, comfortable, and uniquely their own. Dr. Moran is also looking forward to incorporating orthodontics into her practice as she continues expanding the ways she can care for her patients.

Kentucky has become home for Dr. Moran and her family, with her husband being a Crittenden native. When she is not in the office, you will most likely find her outdoors spending time with her husband and their two boys.
