Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last? +

Dental implants are built to last. A large NIH-published cohort study following more than 10,800 implants across 4,247 patients for up to 22 years found a 10-year cumulative survival rate of 96.8 % at the implant level. That long-term reliability is a big part of why implants have become the preferred standard of care for replacing missing teeth in patients who are good candidates.

Do I need a bone graft before getting an implant? +

Not always, but some patients do. If you’ve experienced bone loss from missing teeth or gum disease, you may need a bone graft to rebuild a solid foundation before implant placement. Our Cynthiana team performs bone grafting in-house, so this step is coordinated as part of the same treatment plan rather than a separate referral.

What’s the difference between a single-tooth implant and an implant-supported bridge? +

A single-tooth implant uses one implant post topped with a custom crown to replace one missing tooth. An implant-supported bridge uses two implant posts to support a fixed bridge that replaces multiple consecutive missing teeth, without altering the healthy teeth on either side. Which one is right for you depends on how many teeth you’re missing and where they’re located, which we’ll go over during your consultation.

Will my implant treatment be handled entirely at your Cynthiana office? +

Yes. We manage every phase of your implant journey in-house, from the initial surgical placement and any necessary bone grafting to the final crown or restoration. That means continuous, coordinated care from a team that knows your case from beginning to end, without being referred out to a separate specialist.
