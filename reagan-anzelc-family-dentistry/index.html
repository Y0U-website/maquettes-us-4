The practice

What does that mean? Everything from fillings and crowns to root canal therapy, implants, oral surgery, periodontal surgery, and sedation, provided by doctors with extensive postdoctoral training.

Dr. Zachary Reagan focuses on implants, oral surgery, and sedation. Dr. Sofia Reagan focuses on root canal therapy and restorative care. Together, they offer a broad range of treatment within one family dental practice, allowing many patients to receive care from doctors they already know and trust.

We understand that feeling comfortable at the dentist means something different for everyone. For some, it is a warm welcome, clear communication, and never feeling rushed. For others, it may mean nitrous oxide, mild or moderate oral sedation, or IV sedation. Whatever you need to feel at ease, your comfort is an important part of how we care for you.

Meticulous cleanings, examinations, and digital imaging for every member of the family, with unrushed visits and honest guidance.

Tooth-colored fillings, crowns, and bridges, planned conservatively and crafted to last, along with traditional full and partial dentures.

Precise, calm root canal therapy from both Drs. Reagan, preserving natural teeth that might otherwise be lost.

Surgical extractions, from a single tooth to full-mouth extractions, bone grafting, and implant placement and restorations, performed with experienced hands and a deliberately unhurried approach.

Nitrous oxide, mild and moderate oral sedation, and IV sedation administered by Blue Sky Anesthesia Associates, for anxious patients and longer combined visits.

Les soins

Single-tooth implants through full-arch restoration, surgically placed and restored in-house by Dr. Zachary Reagan, an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.
