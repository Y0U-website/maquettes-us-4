Meet Dr. Pamela Liu

Your Dentist in Deer Park, TX

The Professional Side

Dr. Pamela Liu hails from sunny Southern California where she grew up in the suburbs of Los Angeles enjoying the beauty of the West Coast. She graduated as class valedictorian and subsequently attended Amherst College in Massachusetts. Here, she graduated with a degree in Art and Art History while honing her skills in painting and sculpture. She also published medical research and was inducted into Phi Beta Kappa during her undergraduate years.

After college, she moved to Philadelphia to attend dental school at the renowned University of Pennsylvania where she earned the prestigious Dean’s Scholarship, awarded only to the top entrants of the incoming class. Dr. Liu excelled in her coursework while at the Ivy League institution and was awarded the prestigious Excellence in Operative Dentistry Award upon graduation.

During her dental career, she has developed a special skill set for treating young and nervous patients. Her easy-going nature and passion for treating people of all ages shows during every visit. Dr. Liu believes that it is critical for her patients to understand what is going on in their mouths, and that they understand the options available to address their concerns.

Dr. Liu has also created a training program for dental assistants in Deer Park, Texas.

The Personal Side

After living in Massachusetts and Pennsylvania, Dr. Liu is happy to be enjoying the warm weather of Texas since moving to Houston with her husband, Dr. Juston Reary, several years ago.

Dr. Liu chose the city of Deer Park, TX to establish her practice because she believes in creating long-lasting relationships with patients, and she wants each individual at TexSmiles Dental to feel like family. Treating people with a smile, honesty, and integrity is at the core of what she does every day.
