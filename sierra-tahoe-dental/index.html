Les soins

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. After healing, they support a custom restoration such as a crown, bridge, or denture.

Implants are often recommended when a tooth cannot be saved or has already been lost due to decay, trauma, or periodontal concerns.
