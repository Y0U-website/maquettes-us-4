About Us

Dr. Andrew J. Baker, DMD

Dr. Andrew J. Baker, DMD is a friendly and compassionate dentist that constantly aims to give his patients the highest quality care. Dr. Baker received his Bachelors of Science in Biology at Northeastern University, before going on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Tufts University.

Dr. Baker has had a lifelong passion for dentistry always striving to follow in his grandfather and father’s footsteps. Dr. Baker is dedicated to offering relationship-driven care and helping his patients achieve beautiful smiles through personalized care.

Dr. Baker resides in Braintree with his wife, Michelle and their r escue dog, Luke. In his spare time, Dr. Baker enjoys spending time with his family, playing golf and traveling.
