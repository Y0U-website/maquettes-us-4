A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once healed, it supports a crown, bridge, or denture for permanent tooth restoration. Benefits of implants include: Natural appearance and function
  • Prevents bone loss and facial sagging
  • No slipping or clicking (unlike dentures)
  • Doesn’t affect neighboring teeth
  • Designed to last decades with proper care

Our Implant Offerings

1. Single Tooth Implants

Perfect for replacing one missing tooth. We place a titanium implant, allow it to heal and integrate, then top it with a custom dental crown for a seamless match to your natural smile.

2. Implant-Supported Bridges or Partials

If you&#x27;re missing multiple teeth in a row, implant-supported bridges offer a more secure, stable solution than removable partials .

3. All-on-X Dental Implants

This advanced solution replaces all upper or lower teeth using 4–6 strategically placed implants to support a full-arch bridge. All-on-X is a popular alternative to traditional full dentures and offers dramatic improvements in function and comfort.

We’ll help determine the best option based on your needs, health, and goals.

What to Expect During the Implant Process

We also offer same-day implant-supported temporaries in certain cases to reduce downtime and help you smile confidently during healing.

  • Consultation & 3D imaging We assess bone health, discuss your goals, and create a custom plan.
  • Surgical placement The implant(s) are placed under local anesthesia.
  • Healing & osseointegration Bone fuses with the implant over 3–6 months.
  • Restoration We place a crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis once healing is complete.

Do Dental Implants Work with Medi-Cal?

Medi-Cal may cover limited aspects of implant care when deemed medically necessary, such as extractions or surgical prep work (e.g., bone grafting ). However, full implant placement and prosthetic restoration are generally not covered.

How long do dental implants last?

‍ With good oral hygiene and routine care, implants can last 20+ years — often a lifetime.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

‍ No — it’s performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report only mild soreness afterward.

What’s the difference between All-on-X and regular dentures?

‍ All-on-X implants permanently secure a full-arch bridge to the jaw, offering more stability and bone preservation than removable dentures.

Am I a good candidate for implants?

‍ If you’re in good general health and have enough bone (or can receive bone grafting ), implants may be right for you.

Does Medi-Cal cover dental implants?

‍ In most cases, no. Medi-Cal may cover related services like extractions, but not the implant or crown. We’ll walk you through financing options.
