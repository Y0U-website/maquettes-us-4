About The Brooklyn Dental Center

At The Brooklyn Dental Center, experienced general and cosmetic dentist Chinar Mahadkar, DDS, provides complete oral health care to people of all ages in the Flatbush and Prospect Lefferts Gardens neighborhoods of Brooklyn, New York, and throughout New York City.

The Brooklyn Dental Center provides comprehensive and patient-centric oral health care in a warm and welcoming environment. The practice is conveniently located, has plenty of parking, and features the latest in diagnostic technology, including intraoral scanners and digital X-rays.

People from all over New York City visit The Brooklyn Dental Center for preventive, general , and cosmetic dentistry . Dr. Mahadkar offers various treatments, including orthodontics with Invisalign and ClearCorrect as well as teeth whitening with Philips Zoom! , Icon , and take-home whitening kits.

She also offers traditional oral health care, such as teeth cleaning, dental implant restoration , dentures, and ceramic zirconia dental crowns.

At The Brooklyn Dental Center, Dr. Mahadkar emphasizes a holistic and integrative treatment approach. She takes great pride in building strong relationships with her patients and always makes time to listen to their concerns and treatment goals.

Call The Brooklyn Dental Center today to schedule a new patient consultation, or book your appointment online.
