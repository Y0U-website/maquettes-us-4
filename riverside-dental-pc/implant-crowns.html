Implant Crowns in Tucson, AZ

  • Durability : With proper care, implant crowns can last for decades, making them a long-term solution for missing teeth.
  • Preserve Jawbone Health : The implant stimulates the jawbone, helping to prevent bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.
  • No Impact on Surrounding Teeth : Unlike bridges, implant crowns do not require altering the surrounding teeth, preserving your healthy teeth.

What to Expect From the Procedure

  • Placing the Implant : The first step is the placement of the dental implant into the jawbone. This procedure is typically done under local anesthesia and takes a few months for the implant to fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Attaching the Crown : Once the implant has fully integrated with the bone, we’ll place the custom-made crown on top. This crown is securely attached to the implant, completing the restoration of your tooth.
