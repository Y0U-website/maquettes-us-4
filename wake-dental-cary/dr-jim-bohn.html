Dr. Jim Bohn

Dr. Bohn is committed to continuing education and takes many advanced courses each year to enhance his skills to better serve the dental care needs of his patients and provide them with a gentle and comfortable dental experience

Dr. Jim Bohn has been delivering gentle and comfortable dental care to patients for over 20 years. After earning his Bachelor’s of Science in Biology from Penn State University and a Doctorate of Medical Dentistry from Temple University School of Dentistry, he gained valuable experience as an associate dentist before establishing his own successful practice in Lewisburg, PA. In 2009, Dr. Bohn fulfilled a lifelong dream by relocating to Cary, North Carolina, with his family.

Patient-Focused Approach

Dr. Bohn is known for his light-hearted sense of humor, which instantly puts patients at ease. He is a firm believer in a conservative, low-pressure approach to treatment. Dr. Bohn presents patients with all available options, empowering them to make informed decisions about their oral health without feeling pressured. He values building relationships with his patients and their families, making each visit a personalized and positive experience.

Comprehensive Dentistry and Expertise

Having practiced in a rural setting for much of his career, Dr. Bohn has developed a broad range of skills to meet the diverse needs of his patients. He is proficient in performing root canal therapy, difficult extractions (including most impacted wisdom teeth), and denture fabrication. Dr. Bohn has also been surgically placing dental implants since 2001. His passion is for precision dentistry. He is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry and a local chapter of the Seattle Study Club, demonstrating his ongoing commitment to learning and implementing the latest dental techniques.

Commitment to Patient Well-being

Dr. Bohn and the team are devoted to providing a welcoming and supportive environment. He is committed to enhancing his skills through extensive continuing education, ensuring that his patients receive the highest quality care and a gentle, comfortable dental experience. Dr. Bohn is a strong advocate of option-based treatment plans.

Dr. Bohn enjoys outdoor activities with his family, fishing, traveling, and aviation, and he holds a private pilot’s license. He’s also an avid and competitive golfer. Dr. Bohn’s family regularly attends church in Cary. Of note, his brother, Jason, is a professional golfer on the PGA Tour, with victories including the 2005 BC Open and the 2010 Zurich Classic of New Orleans.
