Common Service Areas

Admissions (Welcome and One Stop Center) 507-285-7557 Assists students through the process of applying to RCTC, financial aid, and other enrollment questions.

Business and Workforce Education 1-800-247-1296 or [email protected]

College Relations and Marketing 507-536-5604

Advising and Counseling 507-285-7260 The Counseling and Career Center assists students with a variety of issues including career and educational planning, academic difficulties, goal setting, or personal concerns.

Financial Aid 507-285-7271 The Financial Aid office offers diverse aid programs and helps students through the process of determining their eligibility for financial awards

International Student Services 507-285-7557 International Student Services provides information on visa and immigration regulations, assistance in the enrollment process, orientation to RCTC and the Rochester community as well as academic advising.

Maintenance 507-285-7266 Please use the website to place maintenance work orders .

Records and Registration 507-285-7557 Assist students with registration issues, requesting transcripts, and other record-related questions.

Security 507-280-5050 Rochester Community and Technical College Campus Safety/Security provide escort service during hours of darkness for persons walking on campus. To request an escort, call 280-5050.

Student Support Services (SSS) Program 507-280-3543 The SSS program provides a range of services that include academic assistance and advising, transfer planning, career information, test accommodations, and one-on-one peer tutoring.

Technology Support Center (TSC) The Technology Support Center (TSC) is located in Goddard Library GL100. 507-536-5555, or 1-800-247-1296 and [email protected]

Tutoring Services 507-280-5534 Tutoring Services provides drop-in peer tutoring to all students in a variety of subject areas.

Welcome and One Stop Center 507-285-7557 The Welcome and One Stop Center assists prospective students by providing information about the college and scheduling campus visits, the academic skills assessment, orientation activities, and assisting with current student enrollment.
