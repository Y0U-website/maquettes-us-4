Meet Morrisville, NC, Dentist: Narender Dudee, D.D.S.

Dr. Narender Dudee joined and purchased Smile Forever Family Dentistry in December 2022. He has over 20 years of dental experience.

Dr. Dudee graduated from the University of Pune in 1997 and practiced in India for several years. After relocating to the USA, he completed the Advanced Standing program at the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine to earn his DDS degree. He has served as faculty at BU Henry Goldman School of Dental Medicine.

If you are looking for a reliable and trusted dentist in Morrisville, our dentist at Smile Forever Family Dentistry is here to help you reach your smile goals. Whether you need a routine exam or root canal therapy, you can expect professional, respectful care. His compassionate approach helps build a relationship and create a personal connection. Our dentist’s caring chairside manner can help you feel more comfortable while in our care. Through discussion, he will work with you to determine what you want from our services and the results you want after treatment.
