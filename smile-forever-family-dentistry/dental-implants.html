What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process at Smile Forever Dentistry involves several carefully coordinated stages. It begins with a comprehensive evaluation, including X-rays or 3D cone beam scans to assess your bone density and anatomy. Dr. Narender Dudee will develop a customized treatment plan and, if needed, coordinate any preparatory procedures such as bone grafting or extractions.

The implant post is placed in a minor surgical procedure performed under local anesthesia. A healing period follows during which the implant fuses with the bone. Once integration is confirmed, we attach an abutment to the implant and secure your custom restoration. Throughout every phase, the team at our Morrisville, NC, dental office keeps you comfortable and fully informed.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer unparalleled benefits compared to other tooth replacement options. Because the titanium post replaces the tooth root, implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent the bone loss that typically follows tooth extraction. This preserves facial structure and helps you maintain a more youthful appearance over time.

Implants are also the most durable tooth replacement option available. With proper care, they can last a lifetime. They require no adhesives, don’t restrict your diet, and are maintained like natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and dental visits. Patients at our Morrisville, NC office consistently report that dental implants have transformed their quality of life.
