Our Mission

We pride ourselves with a friendly, professional, down home atmosphere. Our patients are here for us to provide them the best possible alternative to restore and maintain optimal dental health. It is understandable that there are people that have not had the opportunity to restore their smile and we like to offer options for that goal.

Dr. Hilliard and McMillan take time helping their patients understand the suggested treatment and have answers to questions they may have. Our business office team is always ready to explain your dental insurance and it’s coverage. Christon and Barbara like for everyone to know what is expected to make sure there are no surprises in the financial portion of each visit.

Tracy and Amanda are exceptional in their field of Dental Hygiene and treat each patient with comfortable and thorough care. Katelyn and Meagan help to make each and every patient feel relaxed and they provide the doctors with quality assistance. They understand it is rare that anyone is excited when it comes to dental treatment and through kind and caring conversation help to reduce any anxiety.
