Single Tooth Implant

  • $3,000–$6,000
  • Best for replacing one missing tooth
  • Implant + custom abutment + crown
  • Looks and functions like a natural tooth

Implant Bridge

  • $7,000–$12,000
  • For 2–4 adjacent missing teeth
  • Two implants anchor a fixed bridge
  • Strong, permanent, looks natural

Implant Overdenture

  • $10,000–$25,000
  • Snap-on denture over 2–4 implants
  • Removable for cleaning, very stable
  • Lower upfront cost than fixed full arch

All-on-4 Full Arch

  • $20,000–$45,000 per arch
  • 10–14 teeth on 4 implants
  • Permanent, removable only by dentist
  • Best value when most of an arch is gone

From consultation to new smile

Consultation

30-minute meeting with Dr. Dennis. CBCT scan, exam, treatment plan discussion, written cost estimate.

Surgical Placement

Implant placed with computer-guided planning. Sedation options discussed. Same-day temporary for full-arch cases.

Healing

3–6 months while the implant fuses to bone. Soft-food diet first week. Check-ins along the way.

Final Restoration

Custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis placed. Color- and bite-matched. Done.
