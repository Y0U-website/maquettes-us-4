Meet the Team

The people behind your care

At Saratoga Smiles, our clinical team works together with the same philosophy that guides our doctors: careful attention, clear communication, and genuine respect for every patient who walks through our door.

Our team has been carefully assembled over years, not months. Many of our staff members have been with the practice for over a decade. That consistency means you see the same faces, receive the same standard of care, and build relationships that make every visit easier.

Your Care Team

Why Team Continuity Matters

When you see the same team members at every visit, your care improves. They know your history, understand your preferences, and can identify changes early. At Saratoga Smiles, low staff turnover is not an accident — it reflects a practice culture built on mutual respect and shared purpose.
