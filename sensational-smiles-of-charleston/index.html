Dental implants are a modern solution for tooth loss, offering a reliable, long-term fix. Dr. Richard Rutecki provides both implant placement and restoration in one location, ensuring smooth treatment and fewer visits. Implants act as artificial tooth roots, securely supporting restorations like crowns, bridges, or dentures. With the help of 3D CBCT X-rays and Digital CEREC scanning, we create…

CEREC crowns are created and placed in just one visit, eliminating the need for temporary crowns or multiple appointments. Made from pure porcelain, these crowns match the look and feel of natural teeth, providing both beauty and convenience.

3D cone beam scans provide detailed, high-quality images of teeth and jaws, helping to detect issues that traditional X-rays might miss. They also emit less radiation, ensuring a safer imaging experience.

CAD CAM technology allows us to take high-quality images of your teeth and design dental restorations in-office, all in one visit. This process saves time, reduces discomfort, and produces exceptional results.
