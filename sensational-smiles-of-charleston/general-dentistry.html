Gentle Dentist

  • Teeth Cleaning Even with regular brushing, plaque and tartar can build up. Professional cleanings, including traditional or laser (ultrasonic) cleanings, remove deposits and prevent future issues. For excessive buildup, a deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) may be needed.
  • Oral Cancer Screening Early detection of oral cancer saves lives. A quick, painless screening is recommended every six months, especially for high-risk individuals like smokers, heavy drinkers, and men over 60.
  • Dental Fillings Dental fillings treat cavities and come in materials like composite, porcelain, and gold. At our office, we use tooth-colored composite fillings for a natural look, ensuring your health is our top priority.
  • CEREC Crowns CEREC crowns are created in-office in one visit. These porcelain crowns look and feel like your natural teeth, without the need for messy impressions or temporary crowns.
  • Dental Bridges Bridges fill gaps from missing teeth, preventing shifting teeth and improving your bite. They are durable, cost-effective, and can last up to 30 years with proper care.
  • Invisalign Braces Invisalign clear aligners offer a convenient, discreet alternative to traditional braces. These removable aligners address crowding, spacing, and bite issues with comfort and flexibility.
  • Gum Disease Treatment Gum disease affects over 80 % of adults, but effective treatments like cleanings, scaling, root planing, or laser surgery can restore gum health.
  • Endodontics & Root Canals Root canals treat infected teeth, alleviating pain and preventing further spread of infection. Laser root canals offer a quicker, more comfortable process.
  • Oral Surgery & Sedation Oral surgery includes implants, wisdom tooth extractions, and bone grafting. Sedation options, from nitrous oxide to oral sedation, help ensure a stress-free experience.
  • Pediatric Dentistry Pediatric dentists specialize in children’s oral health, creating a comfortable and fun environment for young patients.
  • 3D Cone Beam & CAD CAM Technology Advanced imaging technologies like 3D cone beam and CAD CAM enable more precise diagnostics and efficient treatments, all in one visit.
