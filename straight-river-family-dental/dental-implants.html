Restore Your Smile with Confidence

Dental implants consist of a titanium post that replaces the tooth root and supports a crown, bridge, or denture. Unlike traditional restorations, implants bond with your natural bone, providing unmatched stability and longevity.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Choosing dental implants offers long-term benefits compared to removable dentures or traditional bridges:

Whether you&#x27;re missing a single tooth or several, implants offer a permanent solution that integrates seamlessly with your smile.

  • Preserves jawbone structure by preventing bone loss
  • Feels and functions like a natural tooth
  • Does not rely on adjacent teeth for support
  • Restores full chewing power and speech clarity
  • Designed to last decades with proper care

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or longer. They are designed to be a permanent solution and are highly durable when combined with good oral hygiene and regular dental visits.

Does the dental implant procedure hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and many patients report minimal discomfort. Some soreness is expected after the placement, but it can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Care for implants the same way you care for natural teeth—by brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and maintaining regular dental cleanings . Avoid smoking and biting hard objects to protect your restoration.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes, dental implants have a very high success rate and are considered one of the safest and most predictable procedures in restorative dentistry. Our use of digital scanners and advanced planning tools ensures added precision and safety.

Will insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage varies by plan. While some dental insurance policies cover part of the procedure, others may not. Our team will help you understand your benefits and provide financing options if needed.
