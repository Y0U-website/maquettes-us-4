FAQs About Pediatric Dentistry

Q: At what age should my child start seeing a pediatric dentist? A: Your child should see a dentist by their first birthday or when their first tooth appears.

Q: How can I prevent cavities in my child’s teeth? A: Encourage daily brushing and flossing, limit sugary snacks, and consider dental sealants or fluoride treatments for extra protection.

Q: Does your office treat children with dental anxiety? A: Yes! We take a gentle approach and create a welcoming environment to help ease any anxiety your child may have.

Q: Are baby teeth really that important? A: Absolutely! Baby teeth are essential for chewing, speech development, and guiding permanent teeth into place.
