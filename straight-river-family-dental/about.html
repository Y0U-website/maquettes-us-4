About us

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Kurt Erickson

Dr. Erickson and Dr. Seth Huiras founded Straight River Family Dental in 2023 after acquiring the practice of Dr. Richard Dresser. They are partners in three general dentistry practices including Fountain Lake Family Dental in Albert Lea, MN and Montgomery Family Dental in Montgomery, MN. He is a current member of the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, and the Southern District Dental Society. Dr. Erickson’s professional interests include clear aligner orthodontics, dental implants, and airway dentistry.

Dr. Erickson and his wife reside in Owatonna, MN with their three lovely children. He is a huge sports fan and loves cheering on his favorite Minnesota teams in his free time (Skol Vikings!). Dr. Erickson welcomes new patients of all ages and looks forward to meeting you, your family, and caring for your dental needs.

Dr. Seth Huiras

Dr. Seth Huiras grew up near Random Lake, WI, a small farming community near Sheboygan, WI. He then moved to Northfield, MN where he attended St. Olaf College. He graduated with a degree in chemistry and a concentration in statistics. Dr. Huiras developed his dental skills at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, before then, he was a dental technician at Certified Dental Ceramics in Rosemount, MN.

While at dental school, Dr. Huiras completed externships at the Hibbing Community Dental Clinic, the Willmar Regional Dental Clinic, and the Native American Community Clinic of Minneapolis. Dr. Huiras was a member of the American Student Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and served as the President of the Hispanic Student Dental Association his senior year. While in dental school, Dr. Huiras was selected for the Pediatric Dental Honors course, which also taught him lifelong skills in kids dentistry.

Dr. Huiras enjoys the Owatonna, Montgomery and Albert Lea communities and their surrounding areas. He welcomes new patients of all ages and enjoys working with the Straight River Family Dental, Montgomery Family Dental and Fountain Lake Family Dental teams. He practices general dentistry including basic fillings and bonding, placement of implants, root canal treatments, crown/veneer and bridge work, treatments for temporomandibular joint disorders, and pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Zach Lindquist

Dr. Zach Lindquist grew up in Clear Lake, IA. He attended the University of Iowa where he got his B.S. in Health and Human Physiology. After graduation he decided to stay in Iowa City to obtain his DDS degree. He graduated from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry in 2017 and moved to Colorado to begin his career in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains. Dr. Zach spent 5 years in private practice in the Denver area before moving back home where he’s spent the past year working at a Community Health Center in Mason City, IA.

Dr. Zach and his wife, Paige, love spending their free time outdoors with their two dogs Murphy and Coco. Whether it be skiing, hiking, camping, or just lazily floating on the lake, Zach and Paige are always looking for an excuse to get out into nature.
