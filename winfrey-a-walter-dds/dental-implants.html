How the Dental Implants Process Works

The process is designed to be precise, comfortable, and predictable.

1. Personalized Consultation

Dr. Walter Winfrey evaluates your oral health, reviews imaging, and discusses your goals. Together, you develop a clear plan for placement, healing, and restoration.

2. Implant Placement

The implant post is placed into the jawbone during a carefully planned procedure. Comfort options are available. Most patients return to normal routines within a day or two.

3. Healing and Integration

Over several weeks, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. During healing, a temporary restoration may be placed to protect the area and maintain appearance.

4. Final Restoration

Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant. The restoration is designed to match your natural teeth in shape, shade, and function.
