Crown lengthening is performed to correct a smile where the teeth are covered with excess gum tissue. Crown lengthening exposes more of the natural tooth by reshaping or recontouring bone and gum tissue. This treatment can be performed on a single tooth, multiple teeth, or the entire gum line.

A tooth extraction is a dental procedure that removes a tooth from the mouth. Extractions are considered to be a last resort treatment and are only performed when one or more teeth poses a threat to your oral health. There are two types of tooth extractions: (1) A simple tooth extraction is performed on a tooth that is visible in the mouth and can be removed with forceps. (2) A surgical tooth extraction is performed on a tooth that is not visible in the mouth and requires an incision to be made in the gum tissue.
