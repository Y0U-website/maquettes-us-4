About Pristine Dental

It will not take you long to discover why Pristine Dental is such a unique dental practice. From the moment you enter our office, you’ll understand our commitment for top quality dental care with high comfort. Our amenities include a beverage bar, children’s play area and TV entertainment to help you relax and feel at ease while you are here with us. Whether you need a restorative procedure for damaged teeth, periodontal therapy to improve your gum health or cosmetic dentistry to boost your smile beauty, we value the importance of compassionate, patient-centered care at all times.

Our family dental practice is equipped with the latest technology in dentistry. This means that we can provide procedures that are faster, less invasive and more effective.

Beyond our comfortable office environment and state-of-the-art procedures, we also help you finance your dental care by accepting insurance and providing flexible financing plans.
