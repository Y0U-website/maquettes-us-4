Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Most patients with good oral health and sufficient jawbone mass are good candidates for implant dentures. That said, even if you don’t qualify for treatment right away, we might be able to help with preliminary procedures like a bone graft and gum disease therapy.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures offer several unique benefits that you wouldn’t be able to enjoy with just dentures alone. Here’s a look at how this restoration could change your life:

  • Superior strength and stability will allow you to eat anything you want and speak without difficulties.
  • Implant posts stimulate jawbone growth and will help prevent further bone loss .
  • With proper care, you can rely on your restoration for years to come.
  • Thanks to their seamless design, caring for your implant dentures is easy . You can brush and floss them like you would real teeth.
  • Implant dentures are made from quality materials. Not only will they look natural , but they’ll feel natural , too!
