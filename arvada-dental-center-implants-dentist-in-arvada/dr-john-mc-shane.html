Dr. John McShane

Dr. John McShane is a second generation dentist with over ten years of experience. Originally from Pennsylvania, he has spent the better part of the last 15 years living in Colorado and is proud to call it home. Dr. McShane&#x27;s goal is straightforward: help each patient achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile while making sure they feel comfortable and well cared for at every visit. His top priority is listening -- really listening -- and building an individualized treatment plan around each person&#x27;s specific needs and goals.

Why Choose Dr. McShane? Dr. McShane believes that great dental care starts with trust. He takes the time to understand what each patient wants from their smile and what concerns they are walking in with. His patient-first philosophy means no rushed appointments, no unnecessary treatment, and no one-size-fits-all plans. Every patient leaves with a clear understanding of their oral health and a path forward they feel confident about.
