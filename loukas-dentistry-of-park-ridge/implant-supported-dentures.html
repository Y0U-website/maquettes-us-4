What it costs

An implant overdenture sits between a conventional denture and a fixed All-on-4 bridge, and the exact figure depends on how many implants you need, whether grafting is required, and whether we adapt your existing denture or make a new one.

For context, a single implant in this area typically runs $3,000 to $5,000 complete. Our implant cost guide explains what moves that number. You will receive a written plan before anything begins, and CareCredit and in-office payment plans are available.

Frequently asked questions

Can my existing denture be converted?

Often, yes. If your current denture fits reasonably and the teeth are in good condition, housings can frequently be fitted into it rather than starting over. Bring it to your consultation and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth adapting.

How many implants do I need?

Two can transform a lower denture that has never been stable. Four gives noticeably better support and is what we suggest when you want it to feel close to fixed teeth. Upper dentures generally benefit from four.

Does it still come out?

Yes, by design. You take it out to clean it and the attachments underneath, which is genuinely easier to keep hygienic than a fixed bridge. It will not come out on its own while eating or speaking.

My denture keeps getting looser. Is that normal?

It is common, and it is caused by bone shrinking under a denture that does not load it. Implants slow that process because they transmit force into the bone the way natural roots did.

Will the attachments wear out?

The nylon inserts wear with use and are designed to be replaced. It is a routine, inexpensive swap at a normal appointment, and it is also how we adjust retention.
