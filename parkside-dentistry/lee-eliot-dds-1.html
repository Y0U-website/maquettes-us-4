LEE ELIOT, DDS

Dr. Eliot attended Oklahoma State University where he received his undergraduate degree in Chemistry in 1977. In 1982, he graduated from the University Of Oklahoma College Of Dentistry. In July of 1982 he opened his private practice in Edmond, OK where he has been serving the needs of patients for over 30 years. Dr. Eliot holds memberships with the American Dental Association, the Oklahoma Dental Association, and the Oklahoma County Dental Association.

Dr. Eliot recognizes and embraces the fact that dentistry is an ever-changing and dynamic profession. Therefore, he strives to stay current on the latest technological advancements in dentistry. Dr. Eliot frequently participates in various continuing education programs on a variety of topics, to maintain the most current treatment methods and options available.

Dr. Eliot resides in Edmond with his wife of 35 years, Debbie. He has four grown children and five grandchildren (and counting). He is an active member of First Presbyterian Church of Edmond. Also, Dr. Eliot has been an avid supporter of Edmond Public Schools Athletics for many years. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his supportive family, traveling to the mountains, attending sporting events, and lending a hand for any renovation project (including volunteering with Habitat for Humanity).
