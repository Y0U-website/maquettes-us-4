TenBrook’s Retainer Replacement Program

Protect Your Smile with Ease

Vivera ® Retainers

Our Retainer Replacement Program provides patients with traditional retainers. Vivera retainers are provided as an upgrade.

From the makers of Invisalign ® , Vivera retainers are clear, comfortable, plastic retainers designed to hold your smile in place after orthodontic treatment.

Vivera retainers are approximately 30 % stronger than traditional retainers. Their unique strength helps keep your teeth in the desired position, as well as reducing the risk of cracking or breaking.

How Vivera Retainers Work

First, we make a scan of your teeth using iTero technology. Custom-made retainers are then molded from the scan, offering a precise fit and a more comfortable feel.

Your scan is securely stored in the cloud. This will allow you to easily order replacement retainers at anytime.

Finally, you can rest easy knowing your smile will be preserved for life with your new, custom-fitted Vivera retainer!
