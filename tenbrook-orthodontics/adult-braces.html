Faster Results. Minimal Discomfort.

Since your smile is one of the first things people notice about you, we want to make sure you make a great first impression. That is why our patented T1 braces and other TenBrook treatments are designed to straighten your teeth faster and with much less discomfort than traditional metal braces.

In just a few months, your friends and family will notice a big improvement as we create your new, perfect smile!

Are you dreaming of a perfect smile but dread the look of traditional metal braces? If so, our T-Clear Minis are the answer! TenBrook T-Clear Mini braces combine the fast teeth movement of our T1 Braces with the near invisibility of Invisalign aligners.
