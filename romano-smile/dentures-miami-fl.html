Dentures

  • Enhancing Facial Structure Wearing dentures can improve the appearance of your face and profile. The absence of teeth can cause your facial structure to sag or collapse inward.
  • Improve Your Speech and Chewing Missing teeth can affect your ability to speak clearly or chew effectively. Dentures can help you regain these abilities and improve your quality of life.
  • Boost Confidence and Self-Esteem An open smile is good for your mental and physical health. Dentures can boost your confidence and restore a natural smile.

Our Denture Fitting Process

  • Consultation Dr. Romano assesses your needs and discusses the best options to improve your oral health. She will also explain the denture procedure.
  • Impressions We take accurate impressions of the mouth to create dentures that fit comfortably and snugly.
  • Trial Fittings You will have several trial fittings before the final denture can be made to ensure that it fits perfectly.
  • Final Adjustments After your dentures have been made, Dr. Romano makes final adjustments to the fit in order to ensure comfort and function.
  • Follow-Up Appointments After the initial appointment, we schedule additional appointments to adjust your dentures if necessary and to ensure you are satisfied with your new look.

There Are Many Types of Dentures Available

  • Conventional Dentures Once all the teeth have been removed and your gums have healed, you will be fitted with a custom-made full denture.
  • Immediate Dentures These are temporary dentures made prior to your teeth being removed that can be placed immediately to help the healing process.
  • Overdentures These are placed over a few natural teeth or dental implants; overdentures offer increased stability.
