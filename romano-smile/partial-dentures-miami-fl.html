Partial Dentures

  • Enhance Smile and Facial Aesthetics Partials can help improve your smile by filling the gaps caused by missing teeth.
  • Improve Your Chewing and Speech Missing teeth can affect the clarity of your speech and ability to speak. Partials can help restore these functions.
  • Preventing Teeth Shifting Filling in the gaps with partials prevents natural teeth from shifting and causing misalignment.

How We Fit Partial Dentures

  • Initial Consultation Dr. Romano conducts a thorough evaluation of your dental health. He will discuss your options with you and determine if partial dentures would be a suitable option for you.
  • Impressions To create a model of your mouth, we take accurate impressions of the teeth and gums. The model is then used to create your custom-fitted removable partials.
  • Fittings and Adjustments Dr. Romano will conduct a fitting to ensure they are comfortable and functional after your partials have been made. The adjustments are made to ensure the best fit for optimal functionality.
  • Care for Your Partials Our staff will provide instructions on how to take care of your partials, and we can schedule follow-up visits to ensure that your adjustment to your new dentures goes smoothly.

Different Types of Partial Dentures

  • Acrylic Partials These are aesthetically pleasing and will match your natural gums. An acrylic base partial will be bulkier than a metal framework partial. They can be used as immediate temporary solutions and can be more expensive.
  • Metal Frame Partials These partials have a metal frame, which is stronger, tends to last longer, and is more stable than acrylic.
  • Flexible Partials Made of a lightweight plastic material that adapts to the contours of your mouth. This provides a comfortable fit as well as a more attractive appearance.
