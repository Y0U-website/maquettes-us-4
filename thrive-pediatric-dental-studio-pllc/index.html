The practice

​​Here, children learn while they visit. Every appointment gives families a deeper knowledge of their child’s health and the steps they can take to support it.

With full attention on every patient, Dr. White partners with families to foster dental health and guide children toward their fullest potential.

Today, research shows oral health is influenced by far more than genetics. Early growth, airway development, and daily habits all play a role. Our compassionate and thorough approach ensures nothing is overlooked and every child receives the support they need.

Dr. White centers his care on the idea that teaching is as important as treating. He listens closely to every child and family with empathy, making sure they feel comfortable at all times. Every factor is taken into account to create treatment that fits each child. His years of experience allow him to offer guidance based on real-world results while keeping every visit supportive and reassuring.

"I love interacting with kids and their families, hearing about their plans and adventures, and learning what goes on in their lives. I find it a privilege to see how patients develop and grow.”

Dr. Kevin White brings nearly 10 years of experience in providing compassionate pediatric dental care to children and families across South-Central Kentucky. As a native Lexingtonian who graduated from Bryan Station High School and Transylvania University, Dr. White is deeply rooted in the local community.

His journey in dentistry began at the University of Kentucky, where he earned both a Master’s degree in 2007 and his DMD in 2012. Dr. White honed his specialized skills during a pediatric dental residency at the renowned Ann & Robert H. Lurie Children’s Hospital in Chicago, where he proudly served as Chief Resident. Since becoming a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry in 2016…

In his free time, Dr. White is a devoted family man. He and his wife are raising their two young children in Lexington and are active members of their church community, where he teaches Sunday School and sings in the choir. Dr. White is thrilled to be establishing his own practice in his hometown and looks forward to welcoming your family to Thrive Pediatric Dental Studio.

Les soins

At Thrive, prevention is at the heart of everything we do. By establishing the proper foundation of growth, we can prevent future dental and developmental challenges.

We combine routine pediatric dentistry with airway assessment, offering families a level of care that goes beyond the ordinary.
