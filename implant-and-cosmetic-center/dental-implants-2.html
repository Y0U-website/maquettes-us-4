Our Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging → Comprehensive exam and personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement → A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing & Osseointegration → The implant naturally fuses with your bone.
  • Abutment & Crown Placement → A custom crown is attached for a natural look.
  • Follow-Up Care → Regular checkups to ensure long-term success.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Want a permanent alternative to dentures
  • Have healthy gums and adequate bone density
  • Are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene
