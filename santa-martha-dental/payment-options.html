Financing Options

Get the dental care you need with flexible monthly payments through Sunbit . With a quick application process and plans designed to fit a variety of budgets, financing your treatment has never been easier. Contact our office to learn more or see if Sunbit is right for you.

CareCredit : When unexpected illness/injury strikes, unexpected expense strikes as well. We understand this and therefore accept payment through the CareCredit program. It takes just 5 minutes to complete an application and will allow you to break down your payment into 6 monthly installments. You can apply yourself online at CareCredit.com/apply .
