What Do You Do Outside of the Dental Office?

When he isn’t busy caring for patients, Dr. Malecki is a big Chicago Bears and Chicago Blackhawks fan. He grew up playing hockey and still enjoys occasionally lacing up his favorite skates when he can find some free time in his busy schedule. Other favorite hobbies include staying fit, playing golf, fishing, traveling, and reading a good book. Running is a definite passion in particular so far, he’s successfully completed the Boston Marathon, Chicago Marathon, NYC Marathon, Chicago Half Marathon, and Rutgers Half Marathon!

Dr. Malecki looks forward to meeting and caring for the patients of Sommers Family Dental.
