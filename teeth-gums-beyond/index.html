The practice

As soon as you walk in and become part of our dental family, we will focus on comprehensive examinations. The overall health and status of the teeth and gums and its surrounding structures will be carefully evaluated. Oral cancer screening will be done. Digital xrays will be utilized as well to assist in obtaining a more definitive diagnosis.

We will discuss with you all the Risks, Benefits, and Alternatives in terms of the treatment proposed, but ultimately, you, the patient, will decide. We’re here to guide you to make the most reasonable treatment decisions.

We take Infection Control seriously. We strictly follow and maintain sterilization and contamination protocols recommended by American Dental Association (ADA) and Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA).

Lastly, we want to create a lasting relationship with our patients that would not just give you great smiles but a healthy mouth that leads to a healthy body!
