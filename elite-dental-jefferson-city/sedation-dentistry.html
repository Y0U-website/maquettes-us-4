The Process What to Expect: Step by Step Here’s exactly what a sedation appointment looks like no surprises.

Call & Tell Us About Your Anxiety

Just call and say you’re anxious about dental visits. Our team hears this every day. We’ll talk through your history, your concerns, and which sedation option makes sense for you no pressure.

Relaxed Consultation

We’ll review your health history, take any needed X-rays, and create a treatment plan all at a pace that feels comfortable. You’re in control of how fast we go.

Sedation Day: You Barely Have to Do Anything

For oral sedation you’ll take a pill beforehand and arrive relaxed. For IV sedation we’ll place a small IV and you’ll be calm within seconds. Then you remain calm and responsive while we complete your care.

Recover, Head Home, and Rest

You will recover in our comfortable recovery area, often surprised the procedure is already over. A driver takes you home. Most patients tell us they wish they’d called sooner.
