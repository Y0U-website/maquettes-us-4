What Is Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent option for restoring lost or broken teeth. At least two surgeries are needed for implants. The first consists of metal posts being implanted into your jawbone. These posts remain hidden under your gumline for a period of 3 to 6 months while you heal from your procedure.

After your healing process, we will have you come back for your second surgical procedure, during which your gums are lifted to expose the metal posts. Special extensions are then attached to the posts. Finally, your dental implants are permanently affixed to the extensions.

The healing process following the second surgery is far shorter than the first. Soon, you will enjoy the many benefits of your new dental implants.
