  • Understanding Endodontics What is a root canal? A root canal is a treatment used to remove infected or damaged pulp inside a tooth, clean and disinfect the canals, and seal the space to eliminate infection and save the tooth. Why is endodontic treatment important? Endodontic treatment is essential for saving teeth that are infected or severely damaged on the inside. By removing the infected pulp, cleaning the root canals, and sealing the tooth, this treatment relieves pain, stops the spread of infection, and helps preserve your natural smile for the long term.
  • System & Diagnosis How do I know if I need a root canal? Common indicators include persistent tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling, discoloration, or pain when biting. Dentists may also use X-rays to detect pulp or root issues Can a tooth still get infected after a root canal? Yes, while root canals are highly effective, infection can recur if the tooth wasn’t fully sealed, if new decay develops, or if the restoration is damaged. In such cases, retreatment may be necessary.
  • Treatment Experience & Recovery Will a root canal hurt? With effective local anesthesia and modern techniques, root canals are no more painful than receiving a filling. Infections themselves tend to cause more pain than the procedure. How long does a root canal take and what is recovery like? Usually completed in one session lasting between 30 90 minutes. Minor discomfort or soreness may last a few days afterward, and most patients can return to normal activities soon after.
