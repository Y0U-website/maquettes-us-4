  • Understanding Veneers What exactly are dental veneers? Thin shells (made of porcelain or composite resin) bonded to the front surfaces of teeth to correct discoloration, chips, gaps, and misalignment. Do veneers look natural? Absolutely. High‑quality porcelain mimics natural teeth in color, translucency, and shine - when fitted properly, veneers blend seamlessly with your smile.
