What Are Dental Implants?

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

Once you’ve decided to move forward with dental implants, it’s important that you understand the process that will take place. Yes, it does require having much patience, as it can often take several months or even a year before you’re able to see a new and improved look. However, the investment you’re making in time and money will certainly be well worth the wait once you see and experience your dental implants. With more than 3 million people who can attest to their benefits and worthwhile investment, you’ll be pleased with your decision to choose this type of tooth replacement.

But before you try to rush the process, which is certainly not advisable, learn a bit more about how dental implants work in Kernersville.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Scheduling a consultation with Dr. Walker is the first step you’ll need to take when preparing for dental implants. It is during this visit that he will examine your smile and determine if you are eligible for treatment. Should your jawbone appear weak, a tooth needs to be removed, or gum disease is evident, he will likely recommend preliminary treatments to address these problems first.

The timeline for treatment with implants varies case by case and depends on many factors. A patient who is getting only one implant may only need a few months total, and someone replacing all their teeth may require up to a year to complete treatment. If you currently have gum disease or require other procedures before starting implant treatment, taking care of these first can add to the total time. During your consultation, we can formulate a treatment plan and give you a realistic timeframe for your treatment.

Dental Implant Surgery

Placing dental implants requires minor surgery. Dr. Walker is partnered with some of the best local surgeons to ensure this part of your dental implant treatment goes as smoothly as possible. You will first receive local anesthesia to numb the area around the vacated sockets. Then, one or more implant posts will be inserted into your jawbone, where they will stimulate new bone growth around it. Your implant dentist in Kernersville will make sure to angle each implant to ensure maximum support.

Once all your implants are successfully in place, they will close the gum tissue before sending you home.

Dental Implant Osseointegration/Abutment

Depending on the number of implants you need to have placed, your recovery can take anywhere from 3-6 months. The reason for this length of time is because osseointegration must occur. This is the process of the implants fusing with the bone and surrounding tissues. If this is unsuccessful, your implants will remain loose and eventually fail. To keep this from happening, you’ll need to make some serious changes to your lifestyle, especially if you smoke. Anything that can weaken your immune system and make it harder for you to heal will need to be addressed before receiving dental implants.

Once your mouth is fully healed, you’ll return to see Dr. Walker for placement of your metal abutments. These are small metal appliances that serve as connector pieces and attach the customized restoration to your dental implants in Kernersville.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

As you near the end of your treatment, the last step in the process is receiving your custom-made restoration. Based on the number of teeth that need to be replaced, you will receive either an implant dental crown, implant bridge, or implant denture. Made from high-quality materials, you can count on your new teeth to mimic the color, shape, and size of your regular teeth, giving you a natural appearance that looks and feels better than ever.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Because dental implants replace the tooth’s root as well as the crown, they can improve your life in ways that other tooth replacement options simply can’t. The biocompatible properties of the implant posts essentially trick your body into believing that your natural teeth were never lost in the first place. In short, it’s the next best thing to having your real teeth back! Here are just a few of the life-changing benefits of dental implants in Kernersville.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • Dental implant posts are firmly embedded in your jawbone, and they’re topped with crowns, bridges, or dentures that have been fully personalized to look natural. You’ll be able to smile confidently knowing that your teeth won’t slide around, fall out, or appear artificial.
  • People who wear traditional dentures often feel restricted in terms of the foods they can eat. In contrast, dental implants in Kernersville won’t limit your diet. By replacing the missing tooth’s root structure, your implant will have the same chewing power as your original tooth. You should be able to enjoy everything on your plate!
  • Instead of spending time taking your dentures out to clean and soak them, you can care for dental implants with ordinary brushing and flossing. This makes your daily oral care routine much faster and easier to maintain.

Health Benefits

  • With dental implants, there are fewer places for bacteria and plaque to accumulate as there are with traditional dental bridges and dentures. As a result, implants lower your risk of gum disease.
  • To hold a dental bridge in place, some enamel must be removed from two of your existing teeth. Dental implants, on the other hand, are completely independent, so none of your healthy tooth structure needs to be modified.
  • By stimulating the jawbone like natural tooth roots do, dental implants prevent the bone loss that gradually occurs after teeth are removed. Since a weakened jawbone has difficulty supporting your remaining teeth, dental implants reduce your risk of further tooth loss. As an added bonus, they help you enjoy a more youthful appearance by preventing the facial collapse caused by jawbone loss.

Long-Term Benefits

  • With proper maintenance, dental implants can last for over 30 years maybe even the rest of a patient’s life! All you have to do is keep up with your oral hygiene and regular checkups with an implant dentist in Kernersville.
  • Even after more than a decade since placement, dental implants are successful in over 95 % of cases.
  • Dental implants are actually the most cost-effective way to replace missing teeth. While they cost more than dentures and bridges at first, these traditional prosthetics will need to be replaced every five to ten years. Plus, they require special cleaning products and adhesives. Over time, you’d end up spending more than you would have by getting dental implants from the start.

No matter how many teeth you’ve lost, there is a dental implant solution that can help you regain your smile and your confidence. During your initial consultation, we’ll make sure you’re a good candidate and then create a custom treatment plan depending on how many teeth you’re missing.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

In order to qualify for dental implants, you need to be in good oral health, and your jawbone needs to be sufficiently strong to support the implants. However, even If you have gum disease or a weak jawbone currently, we can easily fix these problems with periodontal therapy, bone graft, or other preparatory procedures, enabling you to get dental implants once you’ve healed. Then, we can determine how many implants you need and what type of restoration is right for you depending on the extend of your tooth loss.

Missing 1 Tooth

Dental implants for single missing teeth are placed in the missing tooth’s socket inside the gums, stimulating the bone. After the gums and bone envelop the implant, an abutment piece is attached. Finally, a custom, natural-looking dental crown is positioned on top, completing the process and giving you an artificial tooth that functions and appears just like a natural one.

Missing Multiple Teeth

If you have several gaps in your smile, you can take advantage of an implant bridge or an implant partial denture. By anchoring to two dental implants, the bridge can replace three or more consecutive teeth efficiently.

Missing All Teeth

For total tooth loss, implant dentures can restore your complete smile without any of the slipping. With four to six strategically placed posts, you can enjoy the benefits of dental implants and the beauty of dentures.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

If you take excellent care of your dental implants, they may be able to keep a smile strong, healthy, and complete for a lifetime. However, if they are not protected from damage and infection, they may fail sooner and require expensive replacements. Thankfully, there are a few simple preventive measures you can take to keep your implants and the rest of your mouth healthy and infection-free. Here are a few tips from the staff at our Kernersville office for keeping your restored smile in great shape.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Even though dental implants cannot become infected, it’s still crucial to practice excellent oral hygiene to prevent gum disease and other oral infections that can lead to their failure. A proper daily at-home oral cleaning regimen includes brushing at least twice, flossing at least once, and rinsing with antibacterial mouthwash to eliminate harmful plaque and leave your mouth feeling fresh and minty.

Eat a Healthy Diet

It’s important to eat right after receiving dental implants so that your body can heal properly, fight off infections, and form a sturdy bond between your jawbone and your new tooth. It’s especially important to get enough protein and vitamins C and D to keep your gums healthy. However, excessive consumption of sugary foods and drinks can cause harmful bacteria to multiply rapidly in your mouth, leading to oral infections that can increase your risk of implant failure. Take care to avoid chewing too vigorously or frequently on crunchy or sticky foods.

Break Bad Habits

Certain bad habits can make your implants more likely to fail. Never use your smile to open bottles or packages or to chew on inedible items like ice, paperclips, and writing utensils, as these can crack, chip, or break your teeth and dental implants alike. Tobacco use is especially bad for dental implants, so it’s best to quit smoking or chewing a few weeks before the placement surgery and at least a few months afterward. If you have to quit your tobacco habit for that long anyway, why not take the opportunity to give it up completely?

Protect Your Dental Implants

While your dental implants are incredibly durable, they are not invincible. When you play sports or ride your bike, take care to wear an athletic mouthguard to protect your smile from injuries. If you grind your teeth in your sleep, wearing a nightguard to bed can protect your implants and natural teeth from damage.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Come see us at our office in Kernersville every six months for a routine exam and a cleaning. During your visit, Dr. Walker will thoroughly clean your mouth, inspect the area for any problems, address any issues he finds, and advise you as to better at-home oral hygiene practices so your dental implants and natural teeth can stay in great shape for years to come.

Dental Implants FAQs

What Can Cause Dental Implants to Fail?

As you may already know, dental implants have an impressive success rate of over 95 %. That said, it is possible for them to fail. Usually, it’s due to one of two reasons. The first is a form of gum disease called peri-implantitis. The second is when the jawbone and implant don’t fuse together properly, which is referred to as failed osseointegration. It’s also possible for chronic teeth grinding, tobacco use, and certain medications to cause dental implant failure, which is why we will cover your dental and medical history at your initial consultation.

Will I Have to Take Off Work for Dental Implant Surgery?

Most patients need to take a day or two off of work (unless they schedule their procedure on a Friday afternoon and use Saturday and Sunday to recover). That said, we sometimes recommend taking three or four days off, like if your job is physically demanding. Simply put, the amount of time you’ll need to dedicate solely to recovering depends on factors unique to you, including your career and the number of dental implants being placed.

Can I Get Dental Implants If I Smoke?

Although smoking won’t automatically disqualify you from getting dental implants, it is something we need to seriously consider. After all, the use of tobacco products can negatively impact the condition of your gums, delay healing, and even interfere with the implants’ ability to fuse with your jawbone. At the very minimum, you’ll need to refrain from using cigars, cigarettes, e-cigarettes, and the like for two weeks before and three months afterward.

Will People Be Able to Tell That I Have Dental Implants?

If the gaps in your smile were noticeable or you were missing all of your teeth, then the people around you will certainly notice a difference (in the best way possible, of course!). That said, the dental implants themselves are extremely lifelike since they are custom-made for each patient. That means they will blend in with any natural teeth you still have, suit your facial structure perfectly, and match your aesthetic goals to ensure you feel completely confident with your new smile.

Am I Too Old to Get Dental Implants?

Candidacy for dental implants is determined by several factors outside of age, including the density of your jawbone, your commitment to a solid dental care regimen, and whether or not you smoke. In other words, you shouldn’t let your age stop you from scheduling a consultation with us. Once we have a better understanding of your dental and medical history, we can determine if dental implants are the right tooth-replacement solution for you.

Are Dental Implants Removable?

Perhaps because some replacement teeth, such as full or partial dentures, are often intended to be removed and reinserted, some patients wonder whether this is also true of dental implants. However, these replacement teeth are unique in that they’re the only ones that are placed directly into your jaw.

It can take anywhere from 3 to 6+ months to recover because your mouth must generate enough new bone to envelope the support post of your new tooth, making it a more permanent part of your mouth. As a result, it’s generally not intended to be removed once it’s placed.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Getting dental investments can require both time and resources, and it’s natural to wonder whether they’ll last long enough to be worth it. Thankfully, these restorations are known to be incredibly durable and can remain in place for 25+ years if cared for correctly.

Most patients can help maintain their new teeth by brushing and flossing twice daily, taking care not to press too deeply into the pocket around their implant, which could damage its protective seal. You can also avoid eating anything too hard, sticky, or crunchy, and avoid smoking or using other tobacco products, which can potentially contribute to implant failure.

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

You’re not alone if you’re wondering whether this procedure hurts, but due to advanced dental technology and techniques, that’s usually not the case. Dr. Walker partners with local oral surgery experts to improve the likelihood of success while minimizing potential risks.
