The practice

Dr. Shumaker loves his patients and considers them the most rewarding part of his practice. Many of his patients have been with him for over 30 years, creating lasting relationships built on trust and exceptional care.

We believe in honest dentistry. Dr. Shumaker is committed to providing only the care you truly need—never recommending unnecessary treatments or procedures. This patient-first philosophy has earned us the trust of the Lynnwood community for decades.

At Alderwood Dental, experience is our priority. Our team is trained in world-class general dentistry, focusing on care that will leave patients with healthier smiles. We provide high-quality dental care in a comfortable environment.

Let us help you maintain your smile. Dr. John Shumaker D.D.S. and staff look forward to seeing you for all your dental needs.

At your appointments we take pride in our soothing office environment with lofted ceilings, green surroundings, and natural light.

Invisalign treatment is a cornerstone of our practice. Dr. Shumaker has extensive experience creating beautiful smiles with these nearly invisible aligners.

We use advanced 3D imaging to create a customized treatment plan that gradually shifts your teeth into their ideal position, giving you the confident smile you deserve.

We utilize modern improvements that make the patient visit play out in the gentlest, safest, and most comfortable manner.

Services
