A Rich Legacy

Sherwood Dental Care has deep roots in Baton Rouge, originally founded by Dr. Steven Collins’ father and now proudly led by Dr. Collins and his wife, Lauren. Together, they continue the tradition of offering exceptional, personalized dental care. At Sherwood Dental Care, Dr. Collins takes the time to personally diagnose and treat every patient, ensuring a tailored and consistent approach to your care. Unlike corporate chains, our family-owned clinic treats every patient like family, from the youngest to the oldest, with warmth, care, and efficiency in our conveniently located office.

Dr. Steven Collins, Jr.

Dentist, DDS

Dr. Steven Collins, Jr. combines a deep passion for dentistry with a commitment to continuous learning, ensuring each patient receives the highest quality care. When you sit in his chair, you can expect attentive, personalized service, clear explanations of each procedure, and thorough answers to all your questions.

Dr. Collins earned his degrees in Microbiology and Dental Surgery from Louisiana State University, where he was also a proud member of the Phi Beta Kappa and Golden Key honor societies. He is an active participant in the Greater Baton Rouge Dental Association, Louisiana Dental Association, American Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry, staying at the forefront of dental advancements.

Building Relationships with Every Smile

Dr. Collins values the opportunity to treat entire families, fostering trust and building long-term relationships with his patients. I enjoy working closely with my patients to help maintain their dental health and establish lifelong connections, he says. His dedication to continuing education allows him to provide the latest in dental care, ensuring patients always receive exceptional treatment.

Choose Dr. Collins for a dentist who is genuinely dedicated to your family’s dental health and overall well-being.
