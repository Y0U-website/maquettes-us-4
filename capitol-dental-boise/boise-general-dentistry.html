Cavity Fillings

  • At Capitol Dental, we approach cavity fillings with a blend of precision, care, and the latest in dental materials to ensure your teeth not only look natural but feel strong and healthy too.
  • The process involves removing the decayed portion of the tooth and filling it with a durable material that restores its integrity and function. Our goal is to make your tooth look and feel like there was never a cavity to begin with.
  • Dental procedures can be daunting, so we offer sedation options tailored to each patient’s comfort level. Wether it’s gentle relaxation from nitrous oxide or the deeper ease provided by IV sedation, we ensure your experience is as comfortable as possible. Oral sedation is also available for those who prefer a simple, yet effective approach to anxiety management. Each option is part of our commitment to personalized, compassionate care, ensuring you can receive the treatment you need without stress or fear.
  • With Capitol Dental, cavity fillings are more than just a procedure; they’re a step towards maintaining your oral health and ensuring your smile stays bright and pain-free.

Root Canals

  • Root canal therapy is a crucial procedure designed to save and restore a tooth that has been severely damaged or infected.
  • At Capitol Dental, we focus on making this essential treatment as comfortable and efficient as possible. Our experienced team uses state-of-the-art technology to carefully remove the infected or damaged tissue from inside the tooth, cleanse the area to ensure it’s free from bacteria, and then fill and seal the tooth to prevent any future infections.
  • We understand that many people may feel apprehensive about root canals due to misconceptions about pain. However, with advances in dental techniques and anesthesia, most of our patients report feeling minimal discomfort during the procedure-comparable to having a filling placed. Our sedation options are available to help ease any anxiety, ensuring you’re relaxed throughout the process.
  • A root canal can relieve dental pain and save your tooth from extraction, preserving your natural smile. Trust Capitol Dental to guide you through the process with empathy, expertise, and the utmost care for your oral health.

Tooth Extractions

  • We consider tooth extraction as a last resort, but sometimes it’s necessary for the health and comfort of your smile.
  • Whether it’s due to severe decay, infection, or preparing for orthodontic treatment, our team is here to ensure the process is as smooth and pain-free as possible.
  • We use precision and care to remove a tooth, focusing on your comfort and well-being throughout the procedure. Our advanced technology and methods minimize discomfort and promote a quick recovery.
  • We understand the idea of an extraction can be daunting, so we offer a range of sedation options to suit your needs, ensuring a calm and comfortable experience.
  • After the extraction, we’re committed to helping you explore all of your options for replacing the missing tooth, if necessary, and ensuring your oral health remains on track.
  • With Capitol Dental, you’re in caring hands, every step of the way.
