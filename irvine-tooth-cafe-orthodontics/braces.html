What to Expect with Traditional Braces

The process of getting traditional braces includes:

  • Initial consultation and orthodontic evaluation.
  • Customized treatment planning and application of braces.
  • Regular adjustments to gradually align the teeth and jaw.
  • Post-treatment retainers to maintain alignment.

Caring for Your Braces and Teeth

To care for your braces and maintain oral health:

  • Practice diligent oral hygiene, including brushing and flossing around braces.
  • Avoid hard and sticky foods that can damage braces.
  • Attend regular check-ups for adjustments and monitoring.
  • Follow all care instructions provided by our orthodontists.
