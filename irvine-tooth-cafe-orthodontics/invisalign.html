How Does Invisalign Work?

The Invisalign process begins with a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Sona or Dr. Huang , our expert orthodontists. During the consultation, we will take 3D digital impressions of your teeth to create a personalized treatment plan. These digital scans allow us to map out the exact movements of your teeth over time, giving you a preview of the final result before you even start.

Once your Invisalign aligners are ready, you&#x27;ll wear each set for about 1-2 weeks, depending on your treatment plan. The aligners should be worn for at least 22 hours a day for the best results, but they can be removed for meals and dental hygiene.

Throughout your treatment, you’ll visit our Irvine office every few weeks for a quick check-up to ensure your progress is on track. Treatment time varies based on the complexity of your case, but many patients complete their Invisalign journey in as little as 12 to 18 months.

Invisalign vs. Traditional Braces

When comparing Invisalign vs. traditional braces , there are several key differences. While traditional braces use metal brackets and wires to move teeth, Invisalign uses clear plastic aligners, making them a more aesthetically pleasing option for many patients. Additionally, the ability to remove the aligners means fewer dietary restrictions and easier oral hygiene. Invisalign is an excellent option for patients who want a more flexible and comfortable way to straighten their teeth, while traditional braces may still be the better choice for complex cases requiring more precise control.

Clear Aligners vs. Invisalign

While clear aligners and Invisalign are often mentioned interchangeably, it’s important to note that Invisalign is a specific brand of clear aligners. Invisalign aligners are custom-made with advanced technology and backed by years of clinical research, offering predictable and proven results. However, other clear aligner systems may be available depending on your needs, so it’s best to consult with our orthodontists to determine the most suitable option for your case.

Invisalign FAQs

1. How much does Invisalign cost? The cost of Invisalign varies depending on the complexity of your case. During your consultation, we’ll provide a detailed estimate and discuss financing options available at our practice.

2. Is Invisalign suitable for all types of orthodontic issues? Invisalign can effectively treat a wide range of orthodontic issues, including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. However, more severe cases may require traditional braces . Our orthodontists will evaluate your case to determine if Invisalign is right for you.

3. How long does Invisalign treatment take? Most patients complete their treatment within 12 to 18 months, but treatment time can vary depending on the severity of the case.

4. Are there any restrictions with Invisalign? Since the aligners are removable, there are no restrictions on what you can eat. However, it’s important to wear your aligners for at least 22 hours a day to ensure the best results.

5. How often do I need to visit the dentist during Invisalign treatment? You’ll typically need to visit our office every 6 to 8 weeks for progress checks. These appointments are quick and ensure that your treatment is going smoothly.

6. Can I remove Invisalign aligners for special occasions? Yes! Invisalign aligners can be removed for special occasions, but they should be worn for the recommended 22 hours per day for optimal results.
