Meet Dr. Nathan Pham

After graduating Magna Cum Laude with a Biochemistry major from UT Arlington, he earned his DDS from the renowned UT School of Dentistry in the Houston Medical Center. Dr. Nathan holds the esteemed title of Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI), showcasing his commitment to advanced training. Specializing in implantology through training with Dr. Justin Moody at Implant Pathway in Temple, Arizona, Dr. Nathan Pham stays at the forefront of dental advancements.

His dedication to ongoing education ensures patient comfort and well-being, making him a trusted and compassionate dental professional in the Houston community. Outside the office, Dr. Nathan enjoys quality time with his three children, Tin, Ben, and Natalie, exploring public parks and libraries in the Houston area.
