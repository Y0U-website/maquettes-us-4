About SenovaCare

SenovaCare is an insurance provider offering prepaid health plans that give long-term care residents access to portable and onsite medical services—including dental, vision, podiatry, audiology, physical therapy, and behavioral health.

SenovaCare does not deliver direct care. Instead, we provide insurance plans that connect residents to licensed providers who deliver care onsite at the facility. We handle the logistics, coverage, and coordination.

We partner with assisted living facilities, skilled nursing homes, memory care communities, adult residential care homes, and independent living facilities.

We currently serve a growing number of facilities across multiple states. Contact us to confirm availability in your area or to request expansion.

Eligibility, Enrollment, and Payment

Our plans are available to Medicaid-eligible residents, those with private insurance, and uninsured individuals, depending on the state and plan structure.

You can use our online enrollment form or contact our Membership team for step-by-step guidance through the eligibility and sign-up process.

Qualified Medicaid residents can access our services at no additional out-of-pocket cost. Premiums are paid from the resident’s income, and Medicaid reimburses the facility directly.

Yes, we offer options for uninsured residents and accept private pay. Our team can walk you through plan details and pricing.
