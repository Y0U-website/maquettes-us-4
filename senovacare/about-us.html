Our History

Who We Are

SenovaCare began as SeniorDent, the nation’s original onsite dental care provider for long-term care facilities. Founded in 1995, SeniorDent revolutionized how residents accessed essential dental services, bringing cleanings, exams, and restorative procedures directly into the facility setting.

As demand grew for broader in-facility care, SeniorDent expanded into new healthcare disciplines and rebranded as SenovaCare — unifying dental, vision, podiatry, audiology, physical therapy, and behavioral health under one insurance-backed model. Today, SenovaCare continues to support thousands of residents with portable and onsite care that simplifies operations and improves outcomes.

To expand access to healthcare for long-term care residents through insurance-based onsite delivery, ensuring comfort, dignity, and proactive prevention are standard—not optional.

To simplify healthcare for long-term care communities through prepaid insurance plans that provide consistent, onsite medical services tailored to the needs of each resident.

Transparency, dignity, collaboration, and accessibility. We believe every resident deserves care that’s easy to access and tailored to their needs.

Care Compliance Program (CCP) The SenovaCare Care Compliance Program helps your facility stay compliant with state and federal requirements. We offer annual Basic Dental Assessments for residents, quarterly status reports, electronic records, and in-service training — all designed to ensure your team is prepared during surveys and inspections. Our goal is to be a proactive partner at every stage of care and compliance.
