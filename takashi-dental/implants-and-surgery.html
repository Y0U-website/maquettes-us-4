Surgical and Implant Dentistry

0 Surgical and Implant Dentistry Dr. Takashi brings over 25 years of clinical and research experience to implant and surgical dentistry, helping patients restore comfort, function, and confidence with thoughtful, precise care. Whether you need a tooth extraction, treatment for infection, or a dental implant to replace a missing tooth, we take a careful, step-by-step approach, starting with a detailed evaluation and a clear discussion of your options, timeline, and expected healing.

In addition to decades of clinical practice, his PhD research focused on bone regeneration , providing an added layer of scientific expertise that supports careful treatment planning for procedures such as extractions, site preservation, bone grafting, and dental implants.

Because these treatments can involve surgery, planning and comfort are a priority. We focus on safe, conservative decision-making whenever possible. We always inform you of the least invasive approach that achieves a stable, long-lasting outcome while supporting healthy healing. From consultation to recovery, we’ll guide you with clear instructions, attentive follow-up, and a calm, supportive experience every step of the way.
