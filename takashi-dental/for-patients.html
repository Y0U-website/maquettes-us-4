Whether you’re here for a routine check-up, cosmetic enhancements, or specialized care, our team is dedicated to making your first visit smooth, comfortable, and stress-free. What to Expect at Your First Visit Your first appointment is all about you —your comfort, your concerns, and your dental health goals. During your visit, you can expect: A review of your medical and dental history
  • A thorough exam of your teeth, gums, and bite
  • Digital X-rays (if needed) to get a complete picture of your oral health
  • A personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs

Insurance & Payment Options

We are proud to accept all PPO dental plans. We are also in-network with many major dental insurance plans , and we gladly will help you maximize your benefits. Please don’t hesitate to reach out to us with any questions.

For patients without insurance, we offer flexible payment options to make care accessible and affordable.

Preparing for Your Appointment

To make your first visit smooth and efficient, we encourage you to complete our new patient packet in advance and bring the following with you:

Please arrive 10–15 minutes early to get settled and enjoy our comfortable reception area.

  • A valid photo ID
  • Your dental insurance card (if applicable)
  • A list of current medications
