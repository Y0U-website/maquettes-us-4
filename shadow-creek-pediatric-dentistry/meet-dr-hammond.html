About Dr. Nancy I Hammond, DDS MS

With over 30 years of experience serving Iowa, Dr. Hammond has had the privilege of getting to know many families. Throughout this journey, she has been a steadfast advocate of children’s dental health from birth through college. As she embarks on the next phase of her career and returns to private practice at Shadow Creek Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Hammond hopes to treat each child as if they were family in a relaxed and compassionate environment.

When Dr. Hammond isn’t treating patients, she loves to spend time with her six children, their four spouses and 10 grandchildren. She also has two special furry friends, Ranger and Tess, who will be visiting the office frequently. She enjoys the outdoors and spending time at the lake with friends and family.

About Shadow Creek Pediatric Dentistry

Shadow Creek Pediatric Dentistry is a newly constructed, state-of-the-art dental clinic in Waukee, Iowa offering a movie theater, children’s play areas and in-operatory video streaming. Our new equipment, expertly designed sterilization center and low radiation digital x-ray equipment help to create a safe and relaxed environment for children.
