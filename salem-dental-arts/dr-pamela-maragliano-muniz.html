Pamela Maragliano, DMD

Hi! I’m Dr. Pam Maragliano! I’m thrilled that you are here checking out my website, because I am incredibly proud of Salem Dental Arts and I could not be more delighted for you to see what we are about! I’ve been a dentist for over 20 years but took over this practice in 2014.

One of my favorite things about building this dental practice is that I am surrounded by a team of people that love what they do as much as I love taking care of people and collaborating with patients to not only get healthy, but to stay healthy. There are over 57 medical conditions that can be worsened by inflamed g ums and bacteria that cause cavities and I am committed to doing my part to not let this happen!

I also love making beautiful smiles; I am humbled and honored when people seek me out for minor, or sometimes significant changes to their smile.

About 10 years ago, I was invited to work on the editorial side of dentistry and in 2021, I was promoted to chief editor of Dental Economics Magazine. When I am not in the office, I usually can be found in front of my computer, curating my latest issue, traveling, or hanging with my Cavalier King Charles Spaniels, Senorita Diente and Pandapple Martini.
