What Is Invisalign?​

Invisalign is a modern, comfortable, and virtually invisible way to straighten your teeth using custom-made, clear aligners. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign clear aligners are removable and discreet — so most people won’t even know you’re wearing them.

Each set of aligners is made with advanced 3D computer imaging technology to ensure a precise, personalized fit. Invisalign treatment has helped millions of people achieve beautiful, straight smiles — and you could be next!

Why Choose Invisalign Over Metal Braces?

Invisalign is the clear alternative to metal braces. Instead of brackets and wires, Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners that gently shift your teeth into place over time. Here’s why many patients in Sunnyvale prefer Invisalign:

  • Virtually Invisible: No metal brackets or wires — just clear, smooth aligners.
  • Removable Aligners: Take them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss.
  • Comfortable Fit: Custom-molded to your teeth for a snug, smooth fit.
  • Faster Results: Noticeable improvements in your smile often within a few months.

How Does Invisalign Work?

Invisalign treatment involves wearing each set of aligners for about two weeks, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. As you switch to the next set in the series, your teeth gradually shift into alignment.

What Invisalign Can Fix?​

Invisalign can effectively address a range of dental issues, including misaligned teeth, overcrowding, gaps, and bite irregularities. Its discreet and customized approach offers a versatile solution for achieving a confident, straighter smile. Invisalign can be used to treat the vast majority of common problems for adults and teens.

A crossbite is a misalignment of the upper and lower teeth when the upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth, causing an improper bite. It can affect both children and adults, and if left untreated, it can lead to various dental complications.

Crowded teeth, also known as dental crowding, occur when there is insufficient space in the jaw for all the teeth to align properly. This can result in teeth that overlap, twist, or sit at irregular angles, leading to various dental problems if left unaddressed.

An open bite is a dental condition where the upper and lower teeth do not meet when the jaws are closed, leaving a noticeable gap between them. This gap can be in the front, sides, or both and can lead to various dental complications if left untreated.

Invisalign FAQs

How is Invisalign different from traditional braces?

Unlike metal braces, Invisalign aligners are clear, comfortable, and removable. There are no brackets or wires involved, which makes it easier to maintain oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods during treatment.

Am I a good candidate for Invisalign?

Most teens and adults with mild to moderate teeth misalignment, gaps, crowding, or bite issues are good candidates for Invisalign. During your consultation at Sunnyvale Dental Care, Dr. Bhawna Gupta will evaluate your smile and let you know if Invisalign is right for you.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case. On average, Invisalign treatment takes between 9 to 15 months. Dr. Gupta will provide a more accurate timeline after your initial assessment.

How often do I need to wear my aligners?

For the best results, you should wear your Invisalign aligners for 20 to 22 hours a day. Only remove them when eating, drinking anything besides water, brushing, or flossing.

Will wearing Invisalign aligners affect my speech?

Some patients notice a slight lisp during the first few days of wearing aligners. However, this typically goes away as your mouth adjusts.

Are Invisalign aligners painful?

Invisalign is generally more comfortable than traditional braces. You may feel slight pressure when you switch to a new set of aligners, but this is a sign that the treatment is working.

Can I eat and drink with my aligners in?

No. Invisalign aligners should be removed when eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent staining and damage. You can eat normally after removing them.

How do I clean my Invisalign aligners?

Gently brush your aligners with a toothbrush and rinse them with lukewarm water. Avoid hot water, as it can warp the plastic. Dr. Gupta may also recommend Invisalign cleaning crystals or special products.

How often do I need to visit Sunnyvale Dental Care during treatment?

You’ll typically visit Dr. Bhawna Gupta DDS every 6 to 8 weeks to monitor your progress and receive your next set of aligners. These appointments are quick and convenient.

Is Invisalign treatment covered by insurance?

Many dental insurance plans offer coverage for Invisalign just like traditional braces. Our team at Sunnyvale Dental Care can help you check your benefits and assist with financing options if needed.
