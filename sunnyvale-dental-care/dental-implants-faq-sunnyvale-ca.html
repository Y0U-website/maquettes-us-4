Dental Implants FAQ

Dental Implants – General Information

Book an Appointment

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, they support crowns, bridges, or dentures that look and function like natural teeth.

Unlike dentures or traditional bridges, dental implants are fixed in place and do not rely on neighboring teeth for support. They help preserve jawbone structure and provide superior stability and comfort.

Yes, dental implants are a well-established dental treatment with decades of research and high success rates when placed by an experienced dentist.

Candidacy & Eligibility

Most healthy adults with missing teeth and sufficient jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. A comprehensive dental exam is required to confirm suitability.

Age alone is not a limiting factor. Many older patients successfully receive implants as long as they are in good overall and oral health.

Patients with bone loss may still qualify for implants with procedures such as bone grafting to strengthen the jawbone before implant placement.

Dental Implant Procedure

The process includes implant placement, a healing period for bone integration, and the attachment of a custom-made crown or restoration.

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia is used, and post-procedure soreness is typically manageable with prescribed or over-the-counter medication.

Treatment time varies but usually takes three to six months, allowing proper healing before the final restoration is placed.

Types of Dental Implants

Single-tooth implants replace one missing tooth with a standalone implant and crown, preserving nearby healthy teeth.

All-on-4 implants use four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth, offering a stable alternative to full dentures.

An implant-supported bridge replaces multiple missing teeth using implants as anchors instead of natural teeth.

Cost & Insurance

The cost depends on the number of implants, type of restoration, and whether additional procedures are needed.

Some insurance plans provide partial coverage for implants or related procedures. Coverage varies by provider and plan.

Many dental offices offer flexible payment options or financing plans to help make implant treatment more affordable.

Healing & Aftercare

Initial healing usually takes a few days, while full integration with the jawbone occurs over several months.

Patients are advised to avoid hard foods, smoking, and strenuous activity during early healing to ensure proper recovery.

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth, including daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups.

Longevity & Results

With proper care, dental implants can last decades and often a lifetime.

Implant failure is rare, with success rates over 90 %. Proper planning, oral hygiene, and follow-up care reduce risks.

Yes, implant restorations are custom-designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and function.
