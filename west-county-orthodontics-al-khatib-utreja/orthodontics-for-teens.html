Treatment Options for Every Teen

No two teens are alike and neither are their treatment preferences. That’s why we offer a full range of orthodontic solutions designed with comfort, discretion, and lifestyle in mind:

METAL BRACES

Still a top choice for teens with more complex alignment needs. Today’s low-profile metal braces are smaller, smoother, and even customizable with fun color ties.

CLEAR BRACES

For a more subtle look, ceramic clear braces blend in with your teen’s natural teeth while offering the same reliability as traditional options.

INVISALIGN FOR TEENS

Our clear aligner system is ideal for image-conscious or active teens. These removable trays allow them to eat what they like, brush and floss normally, and smile confidently in photos.

We’ll help you and your teen choose the treatment that best fits their lifestyle, goals, and level of responsibility.

FAQs: Teen Orthodontics

What’s the best age for a teen to start orthodontic treatment?

Most teens begin treatment between ages 12–16, when all or most of their permanent teeth have erupted and growth is still ongoing.

Should my teen get braces or Invisalign?

It depends on their case and lifestyle. We offer both and will guide you toward the right choice during the consultation.

How long does teen treatment take?

Most teen orthodontic treatment plans last 12–24 months depending on the complexity of the case and how well they follow instructions.

How can I make sure my teen wears their aligners?

Invisalign Teen includes compliance indicators. We also offer regular check-ins and encourage open communication to help them stay on track.

Does orthodontic treatment hurt?

There may be some discomfort when braces are adjusted or new aligners are used, but it’s usually mild and temporary.
