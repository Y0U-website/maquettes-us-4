Meet Dr. Al Khatib

Feras Al Khatib, BDS, DMD, MS, Cert Orth

Dr. Al Khatib is committed to evidence-based practice and has extensive experience with advanced clear aligner therapies, including Invisalign, Angel Aligner, and other cutting-edge solutions. He loves sharing his knowledge, frequently lecturing both online and in person to teach other orthodontists and advance the field.

Known for his meticulous approach and patient-centered practice, Dr. Al Khatib ensures that each patient receives personalized, high-quality care. His philosophy is simple: everyone will be treated as family and given the best possible care. Dr. Al Khatib resides in St. Louis with his wife and three children. When he’s not teaching or crafting beautiful smiles, he enjoys reading, hiking, traveling to explore new places, and learning about new technologies.
