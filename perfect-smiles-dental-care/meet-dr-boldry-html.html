Tracy Boldry, DMD, MS

Dr. Boldry is also one of 300 dentists worldwide who was selected to be a product evaluator for the Clinicians Report Foundation, a not-for-profit dental scientific research group that tests the newest dental materials, techniques, and equipment for publication in the Clinicians Report Monthly Newsletter. Today, she attends countless hours of continuing education and monthly study club meetings to keep current on the latest trends in dentistry and what benefits they can offer her patients.

  • 1989 University of Pittsburg Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine Doctorate of Medical Dentistry . Top 1/3 in her graduating class and received the Award for Clinical Competence
  • 1991 University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry , Specialty Prosthodontic Residency Program: Prosthodontic Degree and Board Certification.
  • 1992 Fellow for Maxillofacial Prosthetics , provided care and training for patients needing custom facial and ocular prosthetics in addition to prosthetic cleft palate management and post oral cancer care.
  • 1990-92 Completed a 3-year Master of Science Research Project in Oral Biology.
  • 2011 The Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation (DOCS): Certification in Oral Sedation
  • 2015 Dr. Gordon Christensen Oral Surgery Techniques Workshop
  • 2016 Osteoready Surgical Implant Certification
  • 2016-2017 Advanced Cardiac Life Support Certification

Do You Do Any Community Work?

Locally, Dr. Boldry donates her services through Dental Lifeline Network of Kansas and Give Back a Smile. She has participated multiple years in our Dentistry From the Heart event. Globally, she has pioneered a dental mission clinic in Eastern India. Since 2009, she has selected and traveled with small teams to provide preventive care and fluoride treatment to the 200 children in New Life Children’s Home and School.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Boldry grew up in Pennsylvania, developing a strong interest in the fine arts, gymnastics, and the outdoors. She moved to Kansas to continue her education and fell in love with the area, eventually marrying and putting down roots to establish a professional career.

Today, Dr. Boldry is happily married to Thomas, a podiatrist with several locations in the metropolitan area. They currently reside in Shawnee with their son Jacob and have raised two wonderful daughters as well named Hannah and Claire. In her spare time, she loves to decorate their home herself (even after suffering a broken wrist from her art), completing all kinds of projects from painting to wallpapering. She also enjoys making stained glass, paintings, and a variety of arts and crafts.

Dr. Boldry loves horses and loves to ride but has never been a cat lover. However, she was converted when her daughter begged her to rescue a shelter cat named Sage. Sage moved into their home and that warm and loving soft-bodied animal won her over heart and soul. Bozwell is the canine member of the family; he is a Goldendoodle, adorable and rambunctious. Bozwell and Sage bring the unconditional love of animals into their lives!

As a family, they also take every opportunity to RV and enjoy all of the beauty that our great land has to offer.
