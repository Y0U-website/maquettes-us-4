The practice

Our dental practice proudly serves the Nutley area and its neighboring communities with exceptional care and expertise.

At Sunshine Smiles of Nutley, Dr. Mariam Habib and our team prioritize your comfort above all else. Our goal is to ensure you leave with not just a beautiful, healthy smile, but also with the utmost comfort. Trust that each visit with us is designed to put you at ease, knowing you’re in the care of professionals dedicated to your well-being.

Dr. Habib, raised in a family of dentists in Florida, pursued her D.D.S. at the University of Tennessee and completed a residency in New Jersey. After working in Manhattan, she opened her practice in 2021. She’s dedicated to volunteer work, including missions to Bolivia and Egypt, and stays current with the latest dental technology through ongoing education with the American Dental Association.

Dr. Mariam Habib, your dependable dentist in New Jersey, along with her team, has dedicated years to refining their skills and tailoring a top-notch patient journey. Our facility boasts cutting-edge technology, enabling us to deliver remarkable cosmetic outcomes at an accessible cost. From routine dental services to veneers and implants, Sunshine Smiles of Nutley is honored to be your family’s…
