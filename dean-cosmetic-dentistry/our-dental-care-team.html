Dr. Donnie Dean – Dentist

Dr. Donnie Dean is a renowned dentist in Knoxville, Tennessee, known for his unwavering dedication to advancing dental care. With a career spanning over decades, Dr. Dean has continually pursued excellence through ongoing education and embracing cutting-edge technology. His passion for dentistry is matched only by his commitment to his patients, ensuring they receive the highest quality of care. Dr. Dean’s approach is rooted in the belief that learning never stops, and he is always eager to expand his knowledge and skills.

Dr. Dean has been married to his wife since 1990. Their story began on a cruise ship, where they experienced love at first sight aboard the Love Boat. Together, they have built a beautiful family with their two children, Delaney and Hudson. Beyond his professional life, Dr. Dean enjoys moments spent fishing, boating, golfing, and traveling with Margaret. Their shared adventures and the strong bonds with their friends, whom they consider family, are a testament to the fulfilling life they lead together.

Dr. Donnie Dean finds joy in a variety of activities that keep him engaged and inspired. Whether it’s casting a line into the serene waters while fishing, feeling the wind in his hair while boating, or perfecting his swing on the golf course, Dr. Dean embraces each moment with enthusiasm. His love for travel has taken him and his wife, Margaret, to breathtaking destinations, creating cherished memories along the way. These hobbies not only provide relaxation but also fuel his passion for life and dentistry.

Dr. Dean is passionate about providing the most advanced and comfortable dental care possible. He’s constantly learning, with extensive training from top institutions like the Pankey Institute and the Spear Institute. Dr. Dean is a pioneer in dental technology, having embraced innovations like the CEREC system since 2001. He uses cutting-edge tools like 3D imaging and the Solea laser to make your dental experience as gentle and effective as possible.

Dr. Dean believes in a team approach to care, ensuring everyone feels comfortable and involved. In 2020, he built a beautiful new facility to house his practice, allowing him to continue expanding his expertise and offering even better care to his patients.

At Dean Cosmetic Dentistry, our team is committed to providing exceptional care and ensuring every patient feels at home. Each member brings unique skills and expertise, contributing to a collaborative and supportive environment.
