Dental Implants

With Dr. Donnie Dean’s extensive experience and advanced technology, you can rest assured knowing your dental health and satisfaction are in expert hands.

  • Convenience: Unlike dentures, dental implants are a permanent solution. Say goodbye to adhesive and the potential embarrassment of loose-fitting restorations.
  • Improved Functionality: Implants allow you to chew, speak, and smile naturally without discomfort or worry.
  • Preserve Bone Health: Implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent bone loss, maintaining your facial structure and preventing a “sunken” appearance over time.
  • Confidence Boost: You’ll enjoy a smile that looks and feels natural, giving you the confidence to enjoy social interactions fully.

The Dental Implant Procedure at Dean Cosmetic Dentistry

At Dean Cosmetic Dentistry, we carefully plan and execute every procedure step using the latest technology to ensure minimal discomfort and a speedy recovery.

  • Consultation and Planning: Your dental implant journey starts with a comprehensive consultation. We’ll use advanced tools like 3D Imaging and a Digital X-ray to assess your jawbone health and determine the best action. Our team will also discuss your goals and create a customized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: In a straightforward procedure, the dental implant is surgically placed into the jawbone with the precision of the Solea Laser and the skill of Dr. Donnie Dean.
  • Healing Process: Once placed, the implant needs time to integrate with the jawbone in a process known as osseointegration. This typically takes a few months. During this period, your implant will strengthen and stabilize.
  • Custom Restoration: After the implant has healed, we use the Primescan Intraoral Scanner to design and create a custom crown that matches your natural teeth. The crown is securely attached to the implant, finalizing the process.

Taking Care of Your Dental Implants

Ensuring the longevity of your dental implants is simpler than you might think. With these helpful tips, your implants can remain functional for decades:

  • Daily Care Routine: Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush and floss gently around the implant to prevent plaque buildup.
  • Regular Checkups: Schedule regular cleanings and checkups at Dean Cosmetic Dentistry. Dr. Donnie Dean and his team will monitor the health of your implants during these visits.
  • Healthy Diet: Avoid excessively hard or sticky foods that could damage the implant or the crown.
  • Use Whitening Trays: Ask us about our whitening trays to ensure your implants match your natural teeth’s brightness over time!
