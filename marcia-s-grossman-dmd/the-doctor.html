Education and Services

Dr. Grossman earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Boston University School of Dental Medicine and has since built a reputation for her expertise in both general and cosmetic dentistry. She specializes in conservative, minimally invasive cosmetic techniques, including composite bonding and porcelain crowns, designed to enhance smiles while preserving natural tooth structure.

Her practice offers a comprehensive range of services, from routine check-ups and cleanings to cosmetic and restorative treatments. Whether patients are addressing discolored, chipped, or crooked teeth or seeking solutions such as crown replacements, Dr. Grossman provides honest, high-quality care that is tailored to individual needs.

Using the latest dental technology, Dr. Grossman develops facially driven treatment plans that balance aesthetics and function, ensuring her patients leave with smiles that are not only beautiful but also healthy and functional. Her commitment to personalized care and outstanding results has earned her the trust and loyalty of her patients.
