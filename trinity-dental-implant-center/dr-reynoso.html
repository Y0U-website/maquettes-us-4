Meet Dr. Reynoso

Dr. Reynoso is a highly empathetic and skilled dentist with an aim on oral surgery and clear aligner orthodontics, including treatments like Invisalign and SureSmile. At Trinity Dental & Implant Center, she focuses on wisdom teeth extractions, general dentistry, and cosmetic procedures, helping patients achieve healthy, radiant smiles with personalized care.

What sets Dr. Reynoso apart is her deep compassion and understanding for her patients. She takes the time to listen to their concerns and ensure they feel supported throughout their dental journey, creating a comfortable and stress-free experience.

Her care extends beyond the office. Dr. Reynoso is devoted to giving back, particularly through her work with abandoned elderly individuals. She provides support both financially and with her time to those in need at local asilos (elder care homes) in Jalisco, bringing hope and kindness to the most vulnerable members of the community.

Dr. Reynoso’s dedication to both her profession and humanitarian efforts reflects her unwavering commitment to improving lives, one smile at a time.
