Meet Dr. Madrigal

Dr. Apolinar Madrigal is a compassionate and skilled dentist with a global reputation for excellence in dentistry. As a Key Opinion Leader, he has lectured internationally and trained thousands of dentists, sharing his expertise in advanced dental techniques and innovations.

Dr. Madrigal specializes in general, cosmetic, and implant dentistry, including All-on-Four Implant procedures, full-mouth rehabilitations, and smile makeovers. At Trinity Dental & Implant Center, he combines cutting-edge technology with a personalized approach to ensure every patient achieves a healthy, confident smile.

When he’s not practicing or teaching, Dr. Madrigal enjoys spending quality time with his family and serving the Ceres, CA, community he proudly calls home.

Beyond the office, Dr. Madrigal is dedicated to making a difference both locally and abroad. He actively supports orphanages in Mexico, giving his time and resources to improve the lives of children in need. See photos below and how you can help.
