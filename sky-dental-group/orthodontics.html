Phase 2 - Active Treatment

Using the diagnostics, we develop a customized treatment plan :

  • Fixed Appliances - Traditional braces or lingual braces (behind the teeth).
  • Removable Appliances - Invisalign, headgear, or facemasks.
  • Regular adjustments ensure steady progress.

Phase 3 - Retention Phase

After alignment, braces or devices are removed, and custom retainers are created to maintain results. Retainers stabilize teeth while the jawbone adapts to the new alignment.

Our Orthodontic Services

Traditional Braces

Classic metal or ceramic braces for reliable tooth alignment. Ideal for complex bite corrections.

Headgear

External devices that correct severe bite and jaw issues. Usually used in children and teens.

Removable Appliances

Devices like Invisalign or facemasks that can be removed as needed. Perfect for flexibility and comfort.

Retainers

Custom retainers maintain your new smile after active treatment.

Invisalign

Clear, nearly invisible aligners for adults and teens who want discreet orthodontics.

Impacted & Missing Teeth

Specialized treatment for teeth that are trapped in the jaw or missing, ensuring proper alignment.

Crowding & Spacing

Corrects overlapping or widely spaced teeth for a balanced, healthy smile.

Orthodontic Disorders

Treatment for malocclusion, crossbite, overbite, underbite, and other bite-related issues.

Brushing & Flossing Guidance

We teach proper dental hygiene during treatment to prevent cavities, staining, and gum issues.
