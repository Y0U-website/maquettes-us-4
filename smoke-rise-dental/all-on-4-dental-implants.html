Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

All-on-4 dental implants go beyond restoring your appearance. They restore the full function, health, and confidence that missing teeth take away. Here is what you gain with this treatment:

  • Permanent, fixed teeth: Your new teeth stay in place at all times. No removal at night, no adhesives, and no shifting while eating or speaking.
  • Preserved jawbone: The titanium implants stimulate your jawbone, much like natural tooth roots, helping prevent bone loss and keeping your facial structure intact over time.
  • Restored bite strength: All-on-4 implants restore bite force close to natural levels, so you can eat the foods you enjoy without restriction.
  • Fewer implants, faster treatment: Four implants per arch means less surgical time, faster healing, and lower overall cost compared to individual implants for every tooth.
  • No bone graft in most cases: The angled rear implants work with your existing bone, so most patients move straight to surgery without additional grafting procedures .
  • Natural appearance: Your custom prosthetic arch is designed to match your gum line, tooth shape, and facial features, resulting in a look that matches your own teeth.
  • Easier maintenance: You care for All-on-4 implants the same way you care for natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, and routine check-ups are all it takes.
  • Long-term cost efficiency: Unlike dentures , which require frequent relines and replacements, All-on-4 implants are a one-time investment that holds its value for decades.
  • Improved speech and comfort: All-on-4 implants restore clear, confident speech and eliminate the discomfort that comes with ill-fitting or loose dentures.
  • Better quality of life: Patients report greater confidence, improved nutrition, and stronger overall well-being after completing their All-on-4 treatment.

The All-on-4 Treatment Process at Smoke Rise Dental

At Smoke Rise Dental, the All-on-4 process is straightforward, efficient, and built around getting you to a full, functioning smile as quickly as possible.

Step 1: Consultation and 3D Imaging

Your treatment starts with a detailed consultation at our Stone Mountain office. We use cone beam 3D imaging to evaluate your jawbone structure, map your anatomy precisely, and plan optimal implant placement before any procedure begins.

Step 2: Tooth Extraction and Implant Placement

If you still have failing teeth, we remove them and place the four titanium implants in the same appointment in most cases. The rear implants are angled at a precise degree to maximize contact with the available bone, giving you a strong, stable foundation.

Step 3: Temporary Teeth on the Same Day

In most cases, we attach a temporary set of fixed teeth on the day of surgery. You leave our Stone Mountain office with a complete set of functioning teeth. These temporary teeth protect the implants as they heal and let you resume normal activity right away.

Step 4: Final Prosthetic Placement

After your implants fully integrate with the jawbone, typically within three to six months, we place your final custom prosthetic. This final arch is crafted for strength, comfort, and a natural appearance designed to complement your face and bite.
