Dental bonding is used for both cosmetic dentistry and restorative dentistry purposes. Bonding is an easy and efficient way to quickly change the appearance of your teeth. You may also benefit from bonding when you have a chipped or broken tooth. Our dentist uses a composite resin for dental bonding.

The resin is tooth-colored to match your natural teeth and give your smile a smooth, blended look. You can continue to take care of your teeth the same way you always have, and we encourage you to keep coming back every six months for routine dental care.

A dental crown provides a protective cover for a tooth that has sustained damage for one reason or another. Dental crowns have long been used to protect a damaged tooth or a tooth that has received a root canal.

A dental crown can also reshape or recolor a tooth to make your smile more uniform. Our porcelain dental crowns are custom designed to fit your smile.
