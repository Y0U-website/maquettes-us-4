CLEANINGS

Dental cleanings are essential in maintaining optimal oral health. A dentist or dental hygienist can help remove plaque and tartar build-up from your teeth and gums during your visit. Having prolonged and excessive build-up can lead to severe gum disease, which can lead to the loss of teeth.

FILLINGS

Fillings are done to restore natural form and function to damaged and/or decayed teeth. The affected area is restored with the dental material of choice to return to it s original shape. Current dental materials for fillings include resin (composite) and amalgam (silver).

ROOT CANAL

Root canal treatment is often done to relieve dental pain and save your teeth. Patients typically need a root canal when there is inflammation or infection in the roots of a tooth through the process of decay or trauma. (aae.org)

CROWN

A crown can help strengthen a tooth with a large filling when there isn’t enough tooth remaining to hold the filling. Crowns can also be used to attach bridges, protect a weak tooth from breaking or restore one that’s already broken. A crown can also be a good way to cover teeth that are discolored or badly shaped. (ada.org)

SEALANTS

Sealants are applied in a thin coat to the chewing surfaces of your molars. It covers the deep pits and grooves that put you at risk of developing tooth decay. Children and adults both can benefit from sealants. The earlier in life they are applied, the greater protection sealants offer. (jada.ada.org)

TEETH WHITENING - BLEACHING

Professional teeth whitening can enhance your appearance and overall confidence. Using a state of the art whitening system, Zoom Lightspeed, we can help you achieve that brighter smile without breaking the bank. Zoom whitening gives you the confidence to do what you do best, shine. (usa.phillips.com)

DENTAL IMPLANTS AND CROWNS

Dental implants are surgically placed in your jawbone, where they serve as the roots of missing teeth. Because the titanium in the implants fuses with your jawbone, the implants won t slip, make noise or cause bone damage the way fixed bridgework or dentures might. And the materials can t decay like your own teeth that support regular bridgework can. Dental implants and crowns offer a unique and better way to replace missing teeth. ( www.mayclinic.org )

EXTRACTIONS

Nobody wants to lose a tooth; as each tooth has a purpose in our oral cavity. However, when a tooth is decayed or infected beyond repair, it may need to be removed to prevent further damage to the surrounding structures.

BRIDGE

If you re missing one or more teeth, a fixed bridge may be an option for you. Much like a crown, a dental bridge can restore and strengthen already weakened teeth, as well as providing replacements for those that are missing. Using a bridge as a way to restore missing teeth can also help to maintain occlusion stability.

DENTURES

Dentures are removable appliances that can replace missing teeth and help restore your smile. Whether you’ve lost all of your natural teeth or just a few from gum disease, tooth decay or injury, replacing missing teeth will benefit your appearance and your health. (ada.org)

VENEERS

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells crafted of tooth-colored materials designed to cover the front side of teeth. They are an option for correcting stained, chipped, decayed or crooked teeth. (ada.org)

ORTHODONTIC BRACES

Orthodontic treatment can help people of all ages achieve a healthy and beautiful smile. If you have crowded or crooked teeth, misaligned teeth, or open spaces, braces may be an option for you. Braces can also help correct malocclusions to achieve a more stable and functional bite.

SOFT TISSUE LASER THERAPY

Diode laser can also be used to treat cold sores, herpetic lesions, and root sensitivity.
