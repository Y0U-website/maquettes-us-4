Our Team

Get to know the incredible team at West Wind Dental!

You can count on us to take care of your oral health needs with comprehensive treatment plans and some of the most advanced services used in modern dentistry. Every staff person here is dedicated to providing the very best general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry procedures you need every time you visit West Wind Dental in Portage, Michigan.

Dr. Andrew Hamilton, DDS, FAGD

Dr. Hamilton is originally from the Kalamazoo area. He was a standout athlete and scholar for Schoolcraft Community Schools.

Upon graduation, he attended Michigan State University, where he received a Bachelor’s degree in Human Nutrition/Human Physiology. MSU athletics is a very big part of his life and he attends as many sporting events in a given year that his schedule will allow.

Outside of the office, Dr. Hamilton’s top priority and joys are his family; he has two beautiful daughters, Layla and Bree with his wife Jolie. The little spare time left over is spent Steelhead and Salmon fishing and the occasional round of golf.

Administrative Team

Jolie H., CDA, COA

President, Patient Care & Financial Coordinator

Jolie joined our team in early 2015, coming to the office with over 18 years of experience, starting when she was a high school student at Portage Central. After attending the EFE (Education for Employment) program at Kalamazoo Valley Community College, Jolie started her first job as a dental assistant and fell in love with dentistry. She desired to continue her education, so she decided to pursue DANB (Dental Assisting National Board) where she completed her education becoming a Certified Dental Assistant, and then going even further Jolie later gained a certification in Orthodontics.

Through the years she has expanded her knowledge as well as her experience not only in the clinical aspect (back office) of dentistry but also in the clerical (front office) aspect. Although you may occasionally see her working alongside one of the doctors, her true passion is in the front office. Currently she oversees all of the patient accounts as well as insurance and specializes in patient relations.

For fun, Jolie enjoys family time with her husband, two little girls, their two cats and their dog. She also loves running, gardening, and canning.

Jenni K., RMA, BS ORM

Director of Operations

Jenn has been a long-time resident of the Portage area, where she worked in Family Practice and Pediatrics before making a switch to the dental world, where she has spent the last several years.

After years of working in clerical and clinical positions in local medical practices, Jenn recognized her passion for problem solving, process improvement and leading people within the healthcare setting. With that realization, she went back to school to obtain a Bachelor of Science degree in Organizational Management, with a focus in Human Resources. In the years following, Jenn had the opportunity to expand her skills working in medical practice management, overseeing clinical and clerical departments.

Jenn brings her dedication to both professional excellence and exceptional patient care to West Wind Dental as the Director of Operations, overseeing our clerical and clinical departments. It is her goal to continue to grow alongside the practice and is committed to doing her part to ensure that we are providing safe and reliable care to our patients. In order to grow both personally and professionally, Jenn regularly participates in continuing education courses.

In her spare time, Jenn loves spending time with her husband Chad, their families, and adorable fur babies, Clementine Rose and Oscar. She loves to travel, enjoys music and has a passion for reading that often results in her happily living up to her “Book Nerd” nickname.

Front Office Team

Amanda D., RDA, CDA

Practice Support Assistant

With over 20 years of experience in dentistry, Amanda brings a rare blend of clinical expertise and front office excellence to the practice. Beginning her career chairside, she developed a deep understanding of patient care, clinical workflows, and what it truly takes to create a comfortable, efficient dental experience.

Today, Amanda uses that hands-on knowledge to elevate the front office experience. Cross-trained in both clinical and administrative roles, she bridges the gap between the operatory and the front desk—helping patients feel informed, supported, and confident every step of the way. From scheduling and treatment coordination to answering questions with clarity and compassion, she ensures every patient interaction is seamless and personalized.

Amanda is passionate about creating a welcoming environment where patients feel valued—not rushed—and where communication is clear from check-in to check-out. Her unique perspective allows her to anticipate needs, improve efficiency, and support both the clinical team and patients at a high level.

Outside of the office, Amanda enjoys spending time with her kids, weightlifting, cheering on Detroit sports (especially the Tigers), and sharing a good (and sometimes silly) joke.
