Orthodontics for Teens

Orthodontic treatment is nearly a rite of passage for teens throughout Dunn, with most patients getting started between the ages of 10 and 16.

Dr. Barnes can easily detect spacing, crowding, bite, and alignment issues during the teen years, and their smile and jaw are developed enough to support orthodontic treatment.

Many teens express concern about the appearance of braces as they straighten their smiles, so we offer modern treatment options to make the experience more comfortable and discreet.

At Skyblue Orthodontics, your teen can choose between clear aligners, metal braces, or clear ceramic braces. Each of these choices offers unique benefits while transforming your teen’s smile .

Orthodontics for Adults

You can transform your smile at any age with adult treatment at Skyblue Orthodontics. If you’ve always felt self-conscious about your smile or noticed your teeth shifting as you age, we’re here for you.

Misaligned teeth can cause jaw pain, difficulty keeping teeth clean, and an unsatisfactory smile, as well as long-term dental issues that can develop due to lingering bacteria, uneven wear, and trouble chewing and speaking.

Dr. Barnes can address these issues and more through orthodontic treatment to give you the healthy smile you deserve. In most cases, orthodontic treatment is as effective for adults as children!

Reasons to Consider Orthodontic Treatment

Straight Teeth Are Healthier

When your teeth are properly aligned, it’s easier to keep them clean through consistent brushing and flossing habits. This helps prevent other oral issues, including problems affecting your overall health.

Straight Smiles Build Confidence

Successful orthodontic treatment at Skyblue Orthodontics can help boost your self-confidence, create a better quality of life, and allow you to smile freely for the first time.

Untreated Oral Issues Will Get Worse

Oral issues won’t improve on their own, so if you delay or avoid treatment, you can develop more serious health issues that take longer to resolve.
