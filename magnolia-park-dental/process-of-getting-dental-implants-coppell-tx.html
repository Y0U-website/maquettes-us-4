1. Initial Consultation and Evaluation

The first step in your dental implant journey is a thorough consultation with our expert dental team. We’ll assess your oral health, take digital scans, and determine whether you’re a good implant candidate. Factors such as bone density, gum health, and overall medical history play a crucial role in the success of your implants. If you need preparatory treatments like bone grafting, we’ll discuss those options during this visit. Additionally, we’ll take the time to answer any questions you may have and explain the expected timeline for your specific case.

2. Treatment Planning and Preparation

Once we determine you’re a good candidate for dental implants, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan. This phase includes precise planning using advanced imaging technology to ensure the correct placement of the implant post. Those procedures will be completed before the implant surgery if extractions or bone grafting are necessary. At this stage, we will also discuss sedation or anesthesia options to ensure a comfortable experience.

3. Implant Placement Surgery

4. Healing and Osseointegration

Healing time varies from patient to patient but usually takes three to six months. During this period, the implant bonds with the bone, creating a durable and stable base for the replacement tooth. We’ll schedule follow-up visits to monitor your healing progress and ensure everything proceeds as planned. Maintaining good oral hygiene and following our care instructions is essential to support proper healing. Temporary restorations can be provided to maintain aesthetics and functionality while the implant integrates.

5. Attaching the Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we’ll place the final crown, bridge, or denture onto the implant. This custom-designed restoration will match your natural teeth in color and function, giving you a seamless and confident smile. We use high-quality materials to ensure durability and aesthetics. After the final attachment, we’ll provide aftercare instructions, including tips on proper brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups to help you maintain your dental implants for years.
