Oral Surgery in Palmdale

Many issues that require the attention of an oral surgeon cause physical soreness or make daily tasks more difficult. One of the most common needs for an oral surgeon is the removal of wisdom teeth. If wisdom teeth are not removed, partially erupted wisdom teeth can cause pericoronitis, a bacterial infection. Wisdom teeth that haven’t yet erupted can cause cysts to develop, which in turn can damage gum tissue and even bone.

You do not have to live with these sources of discomfort. Modern health advances have made treatments available that can eliminate pain that interrupts your day and makes life harder. Your quality of life is worth a visit to Posada Dental.

Do I Need An Oral Surgeon?

An oral surgeon specializes in addressing injuries, discomfort and damage within hard or soft tissues of the mouth, jaw, neck or other facial areas. These treatments are often more complex than what would be performed during a normal visit to your dentist.
