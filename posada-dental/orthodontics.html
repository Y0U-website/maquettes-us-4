Why Do I Need Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment helps make sure that teeth are properly aligned, healthy, and look good too! Orthodontics realign teeth so that it is easier for you to chew, bite, and speak. Some common reasons that you may need orthodontic treatment are: underbites, overbites, crooked or unevenly spaced teeth, as well as crowded teeth. Teeth that aren’t well aligned can cause abnormal wear of tooth enamel and also be harder to clean. At Posada Dental, we strive to keep you feeling your best with straight, beautiful teeth.

Let Us Help Your Teen Smile Again

Does your teen avoid smiling because they have crooked teeth? If so, you should give serious thought to their concern about their appearance. Crooked teeth can have profound effects on your teen’s self-esteem and social skills. Additionally, even one crooked tooth could throw the bite off, hinder proper cleaning and cause functional problems later on, including gum disease, bite problems, cavities and speech disorders.
