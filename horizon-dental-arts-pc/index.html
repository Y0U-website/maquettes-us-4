For those looking to replace missing teeth, dental implants are often regarded as the most effective solution. Dental implants have become more and more popular as the technology has advanced and patients see how implants look and feel just like natural teeth.

Many patients who have given up hope of having a beautiful, healthy smile again due to missing, cracked, or broken teeth have been amazed by the results of their dental implants. They are truly like having a second chance at your own teeth!

A dental implant is a post that is placed in your jaw and which mimics your tooth’s root. A replacement tooth or bridge is then attached to the implant. If you have lost one or more teeth due to periodontal disease, injury, or some other reason, dental implants may be an option for you to restore your smile without the need for removable dentures or other uncomfortable replacements.
