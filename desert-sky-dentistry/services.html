What Is an Implant-Supported Bridge with Screw Retention?

An implant-supported bridge with screw retention is an advanced dental restoration used to replace multiple missing teeth or even a full arch of teeth. Instead of relying on traditional dentures, this treatment uses two or more titanium dental implants placed into the jawbone to act as strong, artificial tooth roots.

The replacement teeth (bridge) are securely attached to these implants with screws, creating a stable and natural-looking smile. Because the bridge is screw-retained , it can be removed by your dentist for maintenance or adjustments—making it a convenient and long-lasting option.

Implant-Supported Bridge in Glendale, AZ – A Stable Solution for Missing Teeth

An implant-supported bridge is a modern and reliable dental restoration designed to replace multiple missing teeth. Instead of relying on traditional dental bridges or removable partials, this treatment uses two or more titanium dental implants placed into the jawbone to act as secure artificial tooth roots.

The replacement teeth are then attached to these implants, creating a strong, natural-looking bridge that restores both function and appearance. Unlike removable options, an implant-supported bridge is cemented in place for long-term stability and cannot be taken out by the patient.
