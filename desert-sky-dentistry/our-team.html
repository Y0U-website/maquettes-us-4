Meet Your Dental Team

We have assembled a highly trained, professional and caring dental team who pledges to listen to you and your needs for the best experience possible. Your satisfaction and healthy beautiful smile is the most important goal of each of our members.

Upon graduating with numerous awards and honors from the University of Oklahoma dental school, Dr. Naik was selected to participate in the Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) program.

After years of dealing with the Oklahoma winters, Dr. Naik was enticed by the southwestern charm and weather of Phoenix. After practicing as an associate dentist in another office for eight years, she started her own private practice which emulates her caring personality and gentle clinical approach.

Born in Mesa and raised in Phoenix’s Moon Valley area, Marnie is a proud third-generation Arizona native. After earning an Associate of Science degree from Paradise Valley Community College, she graduated from Apollo College’s Dental Program in 1995. With over 30 years of experience in the dental field, she originally chose dentistry for its excellent work-life balance — and quickly discovered a deep passion for helping others. Her commitment to patient care is rooted in a belief that oral health plays a vital role in overall wellness and confidence.

Outside of work, she is devoted to family life. Together with her spouse, she is raising a daughter and an adopted daughter who bring endless joy and entertainment. They enjoy traveling, hiking Arizona’s beautiful trails, and caring for their two beloved dogs — a true animal-loving household

Edith was born in Mexico and raised in Dallas, Texas. With over six years of experience in the dental field, she has been a part of the Desert Sky Dentistry team since February 2022.

Edith is passionate about helping patients achieve life-changing transformations through the confidence of a beautiful smile. Outside of work, she cherishes time with her son Milan, and family.

She is also a devoted dog owner to a GoldenDoodle and a Mini Poodle, and enjoys taking them on walks and exploring the outdoors.

Gabby, Clinical Assistant and Treatment Coordinator

Gabby was born in Arizona and was raised in Sonora Mexico. She returned to Arizona when she was 13 years old. She has been in the dental field for over 13 years. More than half of that time was served in the back office. She is also our very own in-house whitening specialist. Gabby loves helping patients improve their smile as well as maintain their overall oral health, and has recently begun helping us out in the front with treatment planning and insurance!.

Gabby is married with 3 children Lui 13, Delylah 9, and Sarah 6. In her spare time she enjoys going to Church, spending time with her family, and visiting her family in Mexico.
