What Happens During a Dental Cleaning

A typical dental cleaning visit is quick, comfortable, and focused on prevention. During your appointment, we will:

If needed, we may also recommend x-rays to monitor your oral health more closely.

  • Examine your teeth and gums
  • Remove plaque and tartar buildup
  • Clean and polish your teeth
  • Floss thoroughly between teeth
  • Review your oral health and answer questions

How Often Should You Get a Cleaning?

Most patients benefit from a cleaning every 6 months , but some may need more frequent visits depending on their oral health.

You may need more frequent cleanings if you:

We’ll personalize your cleaning schedule based on your needs.

  • Have a history of gum disease
  • Experience frequent plaque buildup
  • Have ongoing dental issues
  • Are undergoing certain treatments

Signs You May Need a Cleaning

Even if it hasn’t been 6 months, you may benefit from a visit if you notice:

  • Bleeding gums when brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Yellow or hardened buildup on teeth
  • Gum sensitivity or swelling
  • It’s been a while since your last visit
