Dr. Kamiya Khatwani

Dr. Kamiya Khatwani, DDS, is a general dentist serving San Leandro and the surrounding East Bay communities. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in 2019.

Dr. Khatwani is committed to providing gentle, patient-centered care in a calm and welcoming environment. She believes dentistry should never feel rushed or confusing, and takes the time to listen, explain treatment options clearly, and help patients feel comfortable and informed.

Her approach focuses on preventive care, clear communication, and building long-term relationships based on trust and transparency.

Education & Philosophy

Dr. Khatwani’s education and clinical philosophy emphasize conservative, patient-first dentistry focused on long-term oral health.

• Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS), Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine (2019)

‍ • Comprehensive training in preventive, restorative, and general dentistry

Rather than rushing appointments, Dr. Khatwani prioritizes thorough examinations, thoughtful treatment planning, and clear explanations. Patients are encouraged to ask questions and take an active role in their care, ensuring they feel confident and informed at every step.

Dr. Khatwani emphasizes preventive dentistry as the foundation of lifelong oral health. Her approach focuses on early detection, patient education, and personalized care plans that help patients avoid more complex dental issues in the future.

By combining modern technology with conservative treatment strategies, she helps patients maintain healthy smiles while minimizing unnecessary procedures and long-term costs.
