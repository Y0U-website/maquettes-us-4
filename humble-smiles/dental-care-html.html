Dental Care

Humble Dentist Dr. Sheeba George Robert is an Expert in General Dentistry and Aesthetic Restorative Dentistry

Dr. Sheeba G Robert and our team have honed our skills in general dentistry , and we’re proud to offer our patients the latest aesthetic restorative procedures. Whether you’re looking for a dental home for your family or you’d like to restore your smile with dental mplants , bridges , or dentures , Dr. Sheeba G Robert can help. We offer nearly every service in our office, and we always provide the most conservative and comfortable care possible.

Your First Visit

When you visit our Humble, TX dental office for the first time, Dr. Sheeba George will want to meet with you to discuss your goals and concerns. She’ll perform a comprehensive exam many patients tell us that it’s the most thorough exam they’ve ever received. This may include X-rays, an oral cancer screening, and periodontal charting. After your exam, Dr. Sheeba G Robert will meet with you personally to discuss which treatments will be beneficial to your oral health.
