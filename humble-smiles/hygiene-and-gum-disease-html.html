What is Gum Disease?

Gum disease is an infection of the gums that results when plaque and bacteria build up on the teeth. This infection has very minor symptoms in its early stages, so many patients may not notice a problem until it’s progressed to an advanced stage. Symptoms of gum disease include:

  • Reddened, swollen, or tender gums
  • Gums that bleed while brushing
  • Bad breath or metallic-tasting mouth
  • Loose teeth or pockets between teeth
  • Changes in the way teeth fit together
