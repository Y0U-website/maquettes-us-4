Saying Yes to Smiles

What Sets Us Apart?

We’ve curated a dental office space that is fun, inviting, and relaxing for young patients. With board-certified experience, personalized treatment plans, and an approach to care that prioritizes your child’s well-being above all else, Oui Smile Pediatric Dentistry is the place to be.

Minimally Invasive Care Young smiles are sensitive and malleable, which means that they need an extra-sensitive approach to care. Each of our treatments is minimally invasive, so we can prioritize quality care without exposing them to unnecessarily complex treatment.

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist Becoming a board-certified pediatric dentist requires doctors to go through rigorous training and continuing education to ensure that they’ve mastered the art of pediatric dentistry. Dr. Jenny Garcia’s expert approach combines the latest dental techniques and tools with a gentle, compassionate bedside manner, making her the ideal doctor for your child&#x27;s smile.

Personalized Treatment Plans Every smile is unique, and we treat them that way. We’ll never apply a one-size-fits-all approach to your child’s care. Instead, we take the time to get to know them on a holistic level so we can provide the best possible treatment for their smile.

Positive and Empowering Experience We love empowering young patients to take their dental health into their own hands. This means teaching them fun ways to maintain healthy habits like brushing and flossing, and it means making dental appointments exciting and fun!

Board Certified Expertise

Get To Know Dr. Jenny Garcia

Dr. Garcia was born and raised in Hollywood, CA, and she’s thrilled to be serving the community that helped raise her. She stumbled upon dentistry in college when she made friends who were on a pre-dental track. She joined the pre-dental society, switched from pre-med, and the rest is history.

Dr. Garcia attended the University of California, Santa Cruz for her undergraduate studies and the University of California, San Francisco for dental school, and she completed her pediatric residency at the University of Southern California. Throughout her education and training, Dr. Garcia has cultivated a compassionate and expert approach to caring for young smiles.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Garcia loves spending time with her two children and her French Bulldog at the park or on the couch watching movies.

What to Expect

The Experience

When you and your child first walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted by our comfortable, fun environment and our friendly office staff. You’ll be invited to sit down and relax as you fill out any new patient forms before your appointment, and your kiddo will get to visit our kid’s play area and take advantage of our awesome amenities. Once the forms are complete and your child’s doctor is ready, we’ll invite you back into one of our operatories.

Your little one will receive a cleaning, comprehensive exam, x-rays, and a fluoride treatment, and Dr. Garcia will sit down with you to discuss the results. If everything looks good, you’ll be able to schedule their next appointment in the front office. If anything needs extra attention, Dr. Garcia will explain the results and take time to address any questions or concerns you might have, and she will describe any further treatment recommendations.
