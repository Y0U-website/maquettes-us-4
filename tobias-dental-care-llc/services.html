Services

Our mission is to partner with you to maintain and improve your oral health. We are dedicated to our patients and your satisfaction is very important to us. If you ever have any questions about our services please feel free to contact us.

Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is faster, more effective, and longer lasting than treatments you find over the counter.

Periodontal Maintenance

Among the causes for periodontal (gum) disease are genetic susceptibility, smoking, and other illnesses like diabetes. Periodontal maintenance involves removing plaque and tartar from above and below the gum line.

Cleaning and Prevention

Dentistry is founded on the principle of prevention. All patients are recommended biannual cleanings and examinations.

Fillings

Used to prevent the spread of tooth decay or correct any cosmetic damage, a filling is one of the ways our dentists preserve your health.

Crowns, Bridges, and Veneers

If you have a damaged or decayed tooth, we can help you choose the best solution.

Root Canal Treatment

If bacteria has reached the tooth’s nerve through a cavity (decay) or a fracture, the tooth’s canal and chamber may need to be cleaned, shaped, sterilized and sealed to prevent further infection.

Extractions

We make every effort to preserve your natural teeth. However, extractions are necessary when decay has made the tooth unsalvageable or when you have an advanced periodontal disease.

Implants

A dental implant replaces the form and function of a natural tooth, which consists of a root and a crown.

Dentures

Missing teeth can make your cheeks look hollow and your mouth look sunken. More importantly, your diet can suffer.
