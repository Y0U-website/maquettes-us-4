Giving Back

Every year approximately 5 million teeth are lost to sports-related injuries!

For the last four years we have had the privilege of providing custom made mouth guards for the McCook High School football team! We take several days and have the players come in to our office and we take impressions, build models and construct their guards.

Our goal in this contribution is helping minimize the risk of tooth damage from sports related injuries. Thank you for working with us!
