The practice

Why Choose Siouxland Orthodontics? Modern care. Hometown relationships. Orthodontic treatment is a big decision for your family. We make it feel simpler with personal guidance, modern treatment options, and care that fits everyday life in Siouxland.

From digital scans to braces and clear aligners, we use modern orthodontic technology to make treatment more comfortable, clear, and efficient.

You are never just another appointment. Our doctors and team take time to listen, explain your options, and build relationships that last throughout treatment.

Ready to Learn About Your Options? Schedule a no-pressure orthodontic consultation with our team. We’ll listen to your concerns, evaluate your smile, and explain whether treatment may be helpful.

There is no obligation to begin treatment. Our goal is to make your first visit as smooth, comfortable, and productive as possible.

Before your appointment, our team may review insurance information and help with any applicable copay, records fee, or claim-submission requirements. When possible, we may also gather information in advance to save you an additional trip.
