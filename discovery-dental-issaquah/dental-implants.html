The Process of Receiving Dental Implants

You must have sufficient bone strength in the area to receive the dental implant for Dr. Vanama to complete the procedure.

If you do not, you will need a bone graft first. This involves removing a small amount of bone from one area of your mouth and transferring it to the implant area. After completing an oral exam, x-rays, and impressions of your mouth, your dentist in Issaquah is ready to start the first phase of providing you with dental implants.

Once healed, Dr. Vanama attaches an abutment and a crown to the titanium post. They are constructed of porcelain and metal, which makes them indistinguishable from your natural teeth.
