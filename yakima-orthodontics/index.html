Dr. Parker was immediately drawn to the area due to its similarity to his hometown. After finishing his prerequisites at Utah State University, Dr. Parker moved to the upper Midwest where he completed his dental degree at the University of Michigan in 2008, graduating near the top of his class, he then attended Saint Louis University where he spent an additional 30 months receiving specialty training in orthodontics.

Orthodontics became a passion for Dr. Parker while in dental school. It just clicked for him. He loved the engineering side of orthodontics and more importantly he loved connecting with patients and seeing the difference a confident smile can make. Dr. Parker feels very fortunate to be part of such a positive change for his patients.

Dr. Parker knows the values of this community and the importance of being part of it. Dr. Parker supports and sponsors kids throughout the Yakima Valley through various organizations including 4H animals, school projects, and sport travel teams.

Raised in central Washington, Dr. Camille Walker began dreaming of a career in orthodontics during her senior year at Ellensburg High School. Dr. Walker joins our practice from Beaverton, Oregon, where she led Walker Orthodontics as the owner and orthodontist for a decade. Prior to building her practice, Dr. Walker served in the United States Navy as an active-duty dentist.

Earning her undergraduate degree from Washington State University and doctorate credentials from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, Dr. Walker trained to become an orthodontist at Montefiore Medical Center in New York City, graduating in 2011. She achieved board certification from the American Board of Orthodontics in 2012.
