General dentistry focuses on the overall maintenance of oral health through preventive care, diagnosis, and treatment of common dental issues. It includes services like routine cleanings, fillings, and oral exams to keep teeth and gums healthy. Regular visits to a general dentist help prevent more serious dental problems and ensure long-term oral health.
