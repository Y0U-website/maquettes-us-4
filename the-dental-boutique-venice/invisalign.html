Revolutionizing Orthodontic Treatment

At The Dental Boutique Venice, we’re proud to offer Invisalign, a groundbreaking alternative to traditional braces. This innovative system uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions. Invisalign is virtually invisible, allowing you to straighten your teeth discreetly. The aligners are removable, making it easy to maintain your oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.

Invisalign FAQs

Invisalign aligners are made from clear, BPA-free plastic, making them an excellent option for those who want to straighten their teeth without drawing attention to their orthodontic treatment.

While undergoing Invisalign treatment, you should visit your dentist every 6 to 8 weeks. These appointments are essential for monitoring your progress and making any necessary adjustments.
