She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Florida Dental Association, the Central Florida District Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the Dental Society of Greater Orlando.

She resides in the Winter Park area with her husband of nine years, James, and their three dogs. In her free time, Dr, Whitehead enjoys attending Orlando City soccer games, Florida State Seminoles football games, and doing CrossFit training.

Dr. Whitehead also participated in the 2019 Florida Dental Assocation Foundation’s Missions of Mercy. Florida Mission of Mercy (FLA-MOM) is a two-day professional dental clinic, that provides dental care to the under-served and uninsured in Florida, at no cost to them. At the 2019 FLA-MOM, over 1,800 patients were treated and more than $2 million was donated in dental care.
