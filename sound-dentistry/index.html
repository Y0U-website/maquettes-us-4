The practice

Dr. El-Ghadi is a graduate of the esteemed Harvard School of Dental Medicine. After receiving his dental degree, he achieved his prosthodontics certificate from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and is among the 1 % of prosthodontists who are board-certified. His unmatched skill allows his patients to enjoy lifelike dental prosthetics.

Dr. Govostes completed both his dental doctorate and periodontal specialty certificate at world-renowned Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He offers a wide range of custom-tailored treatments to address gum disease and preserve natural smiles. He also places dental implants and is committed to staying up to date with the latest advancements in his field.

Dr. Kitsos is passionate about providing comprehensive general dentistry to her patients, helping them maintain healthy and whole smiles for a lifetime. After finishing her dental doctorate, she completed a General Practice Residency with Philadelphia Veterans Affairs. She strives to make each patient’s experience positive, informative, and pressure-free!
