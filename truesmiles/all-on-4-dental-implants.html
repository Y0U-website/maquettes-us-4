What makes All-on-4 dental implants different from traditional implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a full-arch restoration solution that only uses four implants per arch. This aims to eliminate the need for bone grafting and can be ideal for patients with low bone density.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take, and what can I expect during recovery?

The All-on-4 procedure typically takes about 2-4 hours per arch. Recovery time varies among patients, but many can return to normal activities within a few days. Initial healing usually occurs within a few weeks, but full integration of the implants into the bone can take several months. During recovery, following our instructions regarding diet, oral hygiene, and follow-up visits is crucial for successful healing.

Are All-on-4 dental implants permanent?

All-on-4 dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting, permanent solution. With proper care and maintenance, including regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene, and avoiding habits harmful to dental health (like smoking), they can last many years or even a lifetime.

Can All-on-4 dental implants be used for both upper and lower jaws?

Yes, All-on-4 dental implants can be used for both the upper and lower jaws. They provide a stable and secure foundation for fixed dentures, restoring functionality and aesthetics to patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both jaws.

How do I maintain my All-on-4 dental implants?

Maintaining your All-on-4 dental implants involves regular brushing and flossing, just like your natural teeth. It&#x27;s also important to schedule regular dental visits for cleanings and check-ups to ensure the health of your implants and the surrounding tissue. Special attention might be needed to clean under the arch of the prosthesis to prevent plaque buildup.
