FAQs

Your Guide to Invisalign Treatment: FAQs

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, removable clear aligners that gently shift your teeth into place over time. Each set of aligners is worn for about 1–2 weeks before moving to the next, gradually guiding your teeth toward your ideal smile.

Invisalign is designed to treat a wide range of cases, from mild crowding and spacing to more complex bite issues. The best way to know if you’re a candidate is to schedule a consultation, where we’ll use digital scans to assess your smile.

Treatment length varies by case, but most patients finish in 6–18 months. You’ll start to see changes within weeks, as your aligners begin gently shifting your teeth.

Some patients notice a slight adjustment period, but most adapt within a few days. Because Invisalign aligners are thin and custom-fit, any speech changes are typically minor and temporary.

The cost depends on the complexity of your case and length of treatment. At Forest Smiles, we provide transparent pricing and flexible payment options to make Invisalign accessible for every patient.
