At Forest Smiles, we believe that exceptional dentistry starts with trust, comfort, and personalized care. Located in the heart of NYC, our practice is designed around you combining advanced technology with a compassionate approach that makes every visit stress-free.

Whether you’re here for preventive checkups, cosmetic treatments, or restorative care , our team is dedicated to helping you achieve lasting oral health and a smile you feel proud of. From Invisalign® and whitening to dental implants and root canal therapy, we provide complete dental solutions under one roof.

More than just treating teeth, we’re here to support your overall well-being. Every patient is different, which is why we take the time to understand your needs and create care plans tailored to your lifestyle and goals.

Invisalign® Aligners Straighten your teeth discreetly with Invisalign® clear aligners. Comfortable, removable, and nearly invisible, they’re designed to fit your lifestyle while delivering predictable results.

Teeth Whitening Brighten your smile safely and effectively with professional teeth whitening. Our treatments lift stains and restore confidence in just one visit.

Dental Implants Restore missing teeth with dental implants that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. A permanent solution for lasting comfort and confidence.

Root Canal Treatment Relieve pain and save your natural tooth with gentle root canal therapy. Modern techniques make treatment simple, safe, and stress-free.
