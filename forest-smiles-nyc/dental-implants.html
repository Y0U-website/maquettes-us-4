Who Qualifies for Dental Implants?

Most adults with missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. The best candidates typically have:

Healthy Gums

Implants need a strong foundation, so gums should be free of active disease.

Adequate Bone Density

A stable jawbone is necessary to anchor the implant securely in place.

Good Oral Hygiene

Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits help ensure long-term success.

Even if you’ve experienced some bone loss, procedures like bone grafting can often make implants possible. At Forest Smiles, we evaluate your smile with advanced imaging and create a treatment plan tailored to your needs. In most cases, patients find they’re eligible and can enjoy the long-term benefits of dental implants.

Dental Implant Process

The Dental Implant Process

At Forest Smiles, we make the implant journey simple and supportive from start to finish. Using advanced technology and personalized care, we guide you through every step toward a stronger, more confident smile.

Consultation & Planning

At Forest Smiles, we use digital CBCT imaging to take precise measurements and create a personalized plan that ensures your procedure is safe and comfortable.

Implant Placement

A small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it serves as a secure foundation for your new tooth.

Healing & Integration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone in a natural process called osseointegration, creating lasting stability.

Restoration

A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant, completing your smile with natural-looking, fully functional results.
