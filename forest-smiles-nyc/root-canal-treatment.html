When to Call Your Dentist

Signs You May Need a Root Canal

A root canal is often needed when the pulp inside your tooth becomes infected or inflamed. Knowing the warning signs can help you seek treatment early and avoid more serious complications. Common symptoms include:

Severe Toothache

Persistent or throbbing pain that doesn’t go away.

Prolonged Sensitivity

Sharp or lingering discomfort when eating hot, cold, or sweet foods.

Swelling

Tenderness or puffiness around the gums near the affected tooth.

Darkened Tooth

A tooth that appears discolored or darker than surrounding teeth.

Gum Tenderness

Pain, soreness, or a small pimple-like bump on the gums near the tooth.

Step-by-Step Root Canal Care

At Forest Smiles, we make the root canal process as simple and comfortable as possible. With modern techniques and advanced technology, most treatments feel similar to getting a filling. Here’s what to expect:

Diagnosis

We begin with an exam and digital X-rays to confirm infection and plan treatment.

Cleaning & Removal

The infected pulp inside the tooth is carefully removed, and the area is cleaned and disinfected.

Filling & Sealing

The tooth is filled with a biocompatible material and sealed to prevent reinfection.

Restoration

In many cases, a custom crown is placed to strengthen and protect the tooth for long-term use.
