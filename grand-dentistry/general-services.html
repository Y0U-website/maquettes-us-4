Preventative Cleanings

Caring for your teeth is an everyday task to keep your mouth healthy. While brushing and flossing can help prevent the bacteria in your mouth from growing, it’s important to receive our expert stamp of approval. Patients should receive a dental cleaning every six months, or as recommended by your doctor. We’ll remove any plaque and build-up that accumulates in your mouth that you wouldn’t see during your regular dental routine. We may be the experts, but sometimes we need a closer look as well. That’s why we use digital and low-radiation x-rays.

Periodontal Cleanings With Laser

Laser Periodontal Gum surgery. Some moderate to severe cases of periodontal disease which traditionally required open gum surgery with the gum specialist and now be treated in our office. Laser gum surgery involved the very latest laser technology “Solea Perioguide” to treat these cases without opening up the gums. A special CO2 gum laser is used to decontaminate and reshape bone leading to bone and gum regrowth.

Dental Fillings

Fillings are done to restore cavities, repair broken teeth, rebuild wear, or cover sensitive areas. Often a filling can be done using a laser instead of a drill, avoiding a shot and numbness!

Laser Periodontal Gum Surgery

Some moderate to severe cases of periodontal disease, which traditionally required open gum surgery, can now be treated in our office. Laser gum surgery uses the latest “Solea Perioguide” CO2 laser to treat these cases without opening the gums. This technology helps decontaminate and reshape bone, leading to gum and bone regrowth.
