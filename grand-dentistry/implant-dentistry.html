Dental Implant FAQs

What is a dental implant procedure?

A dental implant is a dental replacement tooth option for missing teeth. It’s a titanium post that is surgically placed by our dentists where your teeth are missing. A natural-looking replacement tooth is then placed on top as a crown, or used to support a bridge. Depending on your needs, we may recommend a crown, a bridge, or implant-supported dentures—options that can be customized to your needs.

Why do I need dental implants?

When natural teeth are lost, your jawbone weakens and loses the ability to chew properly, which can affect health over time. Your face shape and smile may also change, which can impact confidence. That’s why dental implants are your smile saver, helping patients feel like themselves again. They not only restore your physical appearance, but also provide complete restorative support for oral health. Dental implants also support the bone and help protect long-term oral health, so patients can feel comfortable and regain confidence with a natural look.
