What Are Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term option designed to replace missing teeth at the root. Dr. Les Neville places a small titanium post into the jawbone, which fuses with bone to support a crown, bridge, or full-mouth restoration.

Unlike removable options, implants restore strength, stability, and appearance while protecting surrounding teeth. Using modern dental technology, our team helps Bowling Green patients achieve a healthy, functional smile that looks and feels completely natural.

Whether you need to replace one tooth or several, implants provide lasting support for your oral health.

Signs dental implants may be right for you.

Dental implants may be ideal if you are missing one or more teeth and want a permanent, secure solution. You might benefit if you avoid certain foods, deal with loose restorations, or feel unhappy with your smile.
