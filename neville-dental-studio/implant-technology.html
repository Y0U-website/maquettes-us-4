Implant technology

Dental implants have changed the way that teeth are replaced, and the technology continues to improve. With dental implants, you can permanently replace one tooth or a complete arch for a functional, beautiful new smile. At Neville Dental Studio, we offer the latest options Click to open in this tab in dental implants with our permanent implant teeth at our dental centers in Horse Cave, Bowling Green, and Scottsville, KY.

Dental implants have been around for decades, but they have improved immensely as the technology has improved. Dental implants are permanent screw-in teeth that are inserted into the jawbone. The implant adheres to the bone and becomes a permanent part of your mouth, which can be attached to different dental restorations, such as dental crowns for single tooth replacement or a completely new upper or lower arch.

Dental implants are ideal for full mouth reconstruction. If you need to replace all your upper or lower teeth, we can use dental implant technology to create a permanent new smile. The new screw-in teeth will be attached to your jawbone—they will look and function like your natural teeth and can last a lifetime with the right maintenance.
