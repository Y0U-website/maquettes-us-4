Dr. Anderson and Dr. Yang are attentive dentists that provide each patient with the time and attention they expect and deserve.

Dr. Karl Anderson, DDS, MS, AIAOMT, NMD, IBDM, DICOI

Dr. Karl Anderson holds a Dental and Masters of Science degree from the University of Minnesota Dental school. He received training at UCLA in the early eighties in Branemark surgical training course, and he is a graduate of the Misch Institute. His specialty degree is in the field of periodontics. Dr. Anderson has placed and restored dental implants since 1985. He has been involved in the development of several implant products.

He holds and is a Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantology, the largest global implant organization. Dr. Anderson also is a certified biological dentist with the Integrative Academy of Biologic Dental Medicine.

Dr. Karl Anderson is passionate about the outdoors and enjoys spending time up North with his wife, children, and grandchildren. Outside of his professional life, Dr. Anderson dedicates much of his time to traveling to Kenya, where he works closely with the children and communities supported by his nonprofit organizations, Giving Hands and Anderson’s Ray of Hope Orphanage. Through these organizations, he has built lasting relationships and continues to make a positive impact on the lives of many. You can see some of these projects on www.givinghandsnonprofit.org, every child deserves a home.
