The practice

After a wonderful experience with a friendly orthodontist as a child, Dr. Jim Thacker knew he wanted to provide that kind of personable, individualized care himself someday.

His commitment to creating healthy, beautiful smiles that last a lifetime began a Bachelor of Science Degree from Ohio State University in 1978, followed by his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from Case Western Reserve University in 1982.

Upon completion of a two-year graduate residency, he was awarded his degree in Orthodontics from Boston University in 1984.

At Thacker Orthodontics, Dr. Jim brings over three decades of premier orthodontic expertise to our Cincinnati and Hillsboro communities. Originally opening his doors in Mt. Lookout in 1986, Dr. Jim’s practice quickly grew to meet the needs of local families, eventually settling into our permanent, award-winning Cincinnati facility.

In 2003, Dr. Jim opened an additional office in Hillsboro to better serve the community and his patients. In 2006, the Anderson location was awarded the Anderson Township Beautification award for Small Businesses.

Having these two state-of-the-art facilities allows Dr. Jim and his staff to provide care to patients on a much more convenient basis.
