Meet Dr. MaryEvan Thacker…

In 2014, Dr. Mary Evan completed a one-year Craniofacial Biology program at the University of Kentucky Department of Orthodontics.

She and Dr. Jim Thacker worked together researching the clinical significance of mandibular dental arch expansion using an E-Arch appliance in the mixed dentition.

Dr. MaryEvan completed her three-year orthodontic residency training at the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine (where Dr. Jim Thacker also received his orthodontic training!)

She graduated with a Certificate of Advanced Graduate Study in Orthodontics and a Masters of Science in Dentistry from Boston University.

Dr. MaryEvan is board certified as a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. Only 54 % of orthodontists in the American Association of Orthodontics achieve this level of board certification.

  • American Dental Society of Anesthesiology Horace Wells Senior Student Award
  • Professor Leonard C. Waite Outstanding Performance Award: Pharmacology and Dental Therapeutics
  • Top 10 % in Basic Sciences: Microbiology, Pharmacology
  • Membership to The Delphi Society for outstanding academic performance and professional interest in biodental sciences.

Outside the Office

Outside of orthodontics, Dr. MaryEvan loves all things outdoors.

She, her husband, and their daughters, Emma Sloan and Frances "Frankie", enjoy exploring with their dogs, hiking, biking, waterskiing, and paddleboarding.

They spend lots of time at the lake in the summer and strap on snowboards and snowshoes in the winter.

In her free time, she also attends pilates, spinning, barre, and other fitness classes to keep her healthy and fit for sprinting around the office every day.
