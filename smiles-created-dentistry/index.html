New 2026 AACD Accredited Member Congratulations Dr. Dane ! We are so proud of Dr. Dane for being inducted into the prestigious American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry!

Since cosmetic dentistry is not legally recognized as a distinct specialty by dental boards, any general dentist can market themselves as a cosmetic dentist. The AACD fills this gap by offering rigorous educational standards and verifying a dentist’s actual cosmetic abilities.

"This is the highest and most respected mark of verified excellence in the field. To become accredited, a dentist must pass intensive written and oral examinations and submit rigorous clinical case documentation to a board of peer experts, proving their skills in procedures like veneers, bonding, crowns, and smile makeovers"
