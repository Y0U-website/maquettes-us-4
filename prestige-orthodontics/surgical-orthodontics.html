Comprehensive Surgical Orthodontic Services

Our comprehensive range of surgical orthodontic services includes:

When you choose Prestige Orthodontics for your surgical orthodontic needs, you can expect:

  • Severe underbites or overbites
  • Jaw misalignment
  • Facial asymmetry
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking due to jaw problems
  • Pre-surgical orthodontic preparation: We work closely with patients to prepare their teeth and jaws for surgery, ensuring optimal alignment and function.
  • Orthognathic surgery: Our skilled surgeons perform precise jaw realignment procedures to correct bite irregularities and enhance facial harmony.
  • Post-surgical orthodontic refinement: Following surgery, we continue orthodontic treatment to fine-tune the bite and achieve the desired results.
  • Ongoing care and support: We provide comprehensive follow-up care to monitor progress and ensure long-term stability and satisfaction.
  • Expertise: Our team consists of highly skilled orthodontists and surgeons with extensive experience in surgical orthodontics.
  • Compassionate care: We understand that undergoing orthognathic surgery can be a significant decision. That’s why we prioritize patient comfort, education, and support every step of the way.
  • Advanced technology: We utilize cutting-edge diagnostic tools and treatment techniques to deliver precise, efficient, and predictable outcomes.
  • Convenient locations: With two conveniently located offices in La Verne and Diamond Bar, accessing top-quality surgical orthodontic care has never been easier.
