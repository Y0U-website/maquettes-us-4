Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign

What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a clear aligner system that straightens teeth using a series of custom-made, removable aligners. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible and can be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing. Many patients choose Invisalign because it offers a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a healthier, more confident smile.

Am I a candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign can successfully treat many orthodontic concerns, including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites. The best way to determine whether Invisalign is right for you is through a complimentary consultation at Prestige Orthodontics.

How much does Invisalign cost?

The cost of Invisalign depends on the complexity of your case and the length of treatment. In many cases, Invisalign is comparable in cost to traditional braces. During your complimentary consultation, we will review your treatment options and provide a personalized estimate.

Does insurance cover Invisalign?

Many dental insurance plans that include orthodontic benefits can help cover Invisalign treatment. Our team will verify your benefits and help maximize your insurance coverage to make treatment as affordable as possible.

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Both Invisalign and braces can produce excellent results. Invisalign offers the advantages of clear, removable aligners and greater flexibility, while braces may be recommended for certain complex tooth movements. We will help determine which treatment option is best for your specific needs and smile goals.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies from patient to patient, but most Invisalign treatments take between 12 and 24 months. The length of treatment depends on the complexity of your case and how consistently the aligners are worn as instructed.

How many hours a day do I need to wear Invisalign?

For the best results, Invisalign aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours per day. They should only be removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing.

Can Invisalign fix overbites, underbites, and crossbites?

Yes. Invisalign can effectively treat many bite issues, including overbites, underbites, crossbites, open bites, crowding, and spacing problems. During your consultation, we will determine whether Invisalign is the best solution for your specific orthodontic needs.

Can adults get Invisalign?

Absolutely. Invisalign is one of the most popular orthodontic treatment options for adults because the aligners are discreet, comfortable, and easy to fit into busy professional and social lifestyles.

Can teenagers get Invisalign?

Yes. Invisalign is a popular choice for many teens because the aligners are clear, removable, and comfortable. Invisalign Teen includes features specifically designed to accommodate growing patients and active lifestyles.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most patients experience mild pressure or discomfort for a few days when switching to a new set of aligners. This is a normal sign that the aligners are working to move your teeth. Any discomfort is typically temporary and manageable.

Can I eat whatever I want with Invisalign?

Yes. Since Invisalign aligners are removable, you can continue enjoying your favorite foods without many of the dietary restrictions associated with traditional braces. Simply remove the aligners before eating and brush your teeth before putting them back in.

Will Invisalign affect my speech?

Some patients notice a slight change in speech during the first few days of treatment. Most patients adjust quickly and speak normally after a short adaptation period.
