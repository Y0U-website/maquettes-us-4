Our Doctors

Dr. Chi

From the moment you meet Dr. Cigdem Yilmaz Sastim (Dr. Chi), you feel it: calm confidence, genuine warmth, and the sense that you’re finally in the right hands. Patients often describe her care as thorough, gentle, and honest—and many take the time to leave glowing five-star feedback about how clearly she explains options and how comfortable she makes every visit.

Dr. Chi’s journey in dentistry is built on both deep academic training and years of real-world clinical experience. She earned her dental degree in Istanbul and went on to complete an intensive postgraduate Certificate in Periodontology, strengthening her foundation in gum health and long-term oral stability—skills that matter in nearly every smile transformation. After moving to the U.S., she completed her Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) at Rutgers, graduating with strong academic distinction and bringing her global experience into modern, patient-centered care in New Jersey.

Over the years, Dr. Chi has focused her continuing growth on what patients want most: results that look natural, feel comfortable, and last. She has pursued extensive hands-on training in implant dentistry, including modern implant planning workflows and advanced surgical education—because replacing missing teeth isn’t just about a tooth, it’s about restoring confidence, bite strength, and quality of life. When you’re considering implants, her approach is careful and personalized: listen first, evaluate thoroughly, and build a plan that fits your goals and long-term health.

What truly sets Dr. Chi apart is how she blends precision with empathy. If you’ve ever felt rushed, judged, or overwhelmed at a dental office, you’ll notice the difference immediately. She takes time to understand your concerns, walks you through choices in plain language, and treats you with the respect and kindness every patient deserves—especially if you’re anxious or have had a bad experience in the past.

Dr. Chi also brings an academic side to her clinical work. She has contributed to research, presented in professional settings, and taught as a clinical instructor—experience that keeps her thinking sharp, curious, and committed to the highest standards of care. And she continues to invest in new techniques and technology through ongoing education, so your treatment stays modern, efficient, and comfortable.

If you’re looking for a dentist who combines experience, advanced training, and truly attentive care, Dr. Chi welcomes you to Smile Perfection—where your comfort matters, your questions are always welcome, and your smile is treated with the patience and precision it deserves.

Dr. Jimenez

Dr. Jimenez is a compassionate and highly trained general dentist who brings a strong background in comprehensive dentistry, restorative care, surgical procedures, periodontal treatment, emergency dental care, and implant planning. He is committed to providing thoughtful, comfortable, and personalized care for every patient he sees.

Dr. Jimenez earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Virginia Commonwealth University through the International Dentist Program. He also completed his dental education at the National Autonomous University of Mexico, one of the most respected universities in Latin America. In addition, he completed advanced training in Periodontology, where he focused on the diagnosis and treatment of gum disease, including both surgical and non-surgical periodontal care.

To further expand his clinical expertise, Dr. Jimenez also earned a Fellowship in Implantology from the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. His training allows him to take a comprehensive approach when evaluating patients’ oral health, gum health, restorative needs, and long-term treatment options.

Fluent in both English and Spanish, Dr. Jimenez enjoys building strong relationships with patients and helping them feel informed and at ease during their visits. His calm approach, attention to detail, and dedication to patient education make him a wonderful addition to the Smile Perfection family.

Dr. Jimenez looks forward to meeting our patients and helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Languages: English and Spanish Clinical Interests: General dentistry, restorative care, periodontal treatment, oral surgery, emergency dental care, and implant

Quality Committed to quality, helping you achieve a smile that stands the test of time.

Expertise Creating beautiful smiles, with every detail tailored to enhance your natural look.

Comfort Focused on comfort to provide gentle and relaxing care.
