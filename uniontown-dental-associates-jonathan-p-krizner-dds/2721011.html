Jonathan Krizner, DDS

Dr. Jonathan has nearly 30 years of experience as a clinical dentist and dental ceramist, allowing him to perform every aspect of your restorative dentistry from start to finish.

Constantly seeking to further his understanding and dental skills, Dr. Krizner regularly pursues continuing education courses at some of the country’s top

dental institutions, such as the world renowned Pankey Institute for Advanced Dental Education.

He has received training in and offers, Invisalign orthodontics, veneers and Cerec single visit crowns. All of his systems are continuously updated to offer the latest technology.

Jennifer Harr, DDS

Dr. Jennifer Harr, is a 2011 graduate of West Virginia University School of Dentistry and worked as a part-time clinical faculty member in the Department of Pediatric Dentistry. She joined our practice in 2011.

Dr. Harr also focuses on continuing education training in order to provide her patients with the best care possible including Cerec single visit crowns and Six Month Smiles clear braces and Implants. She currently resides in New Stanton, PA with her husband, Jacob, her daughter Lily and their cat Sammy.

Joslyn Bilitski, DDS

Dr. Joslyn McWilliams Bilitski is originally from Canonsburg. She received her undergraduate education from Case Western Reserve University in 2003 and her dental degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2006. Upon graduation from dental school, she completed a general practice residency at the VA Pittsburgh Healthcare System. She started her professional career in Uniontown at Fayette County Community Action Agency. Then, she practiced for 11 years at a dental office in Roscoe, PA where she provided caring, comprehensive treatment to patients of all ages.

She attends continuing education courses in restorative dentistry, esthetic dentistry, crown and bridge, dentures and partials, implants, endodontics, surgery and dental sleep medicine to ensure that she is providing the best, most up-to-date care for her patients. She is passionate about helping her patients achieve and maintain a beautiful, healthy smile.

Dr. Bilitski lives in Stockdale with her husband Barry and son Reid. She joined the practice in December 2019. She is so excited to join such an amazing, patient-focused, updated office.

Cherie - Dental Assistant

Cherie has worked in the dental field for 27 years before joining our practice in 2019. Prior to Dr. Krizner’s office, she started out as a dental receptionist and was trained on the job to become a dental assistant. She assisted for 12 years and then continued her education at WCCC completing the EFDA (expanded function dental assistant) program. She has worked as an EFDA for 10 years. When not at the office, Cherie enjoys spending time with her boyfriend Brad and two Yorkshire pups, Mia and Macy.
