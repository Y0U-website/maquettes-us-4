About Quail Ridge Family Dental

DENTAL CARE FROM PEOPLE WHO CARE.

Quail Ridge Family Dental is a family practice with an upbeat, friendly environment. Our dental team is knowledgeable and highly trained, and our dentist and staff are gentle and can put any patient at ease.

Meet the Doctor

Tyson Beck, DDS

Dr. Howard

Dr. Ken Howard was born and raised in the Tri-Cities and graduated from Kamiakin High in 1990. He furthered his education at CBC, then transferred and graduated from Washington State University in 1996. He received degrees in Biology and Microbiology, with a minor in Molecular Biology. In 1996 he was accepted to Howard University’s School of Dentistry, in Washington D.C., graduating in the top of his class.

Dr. Howard’s passion was to start a family practice, because he enjoys interacting with everyone, from the young to the elderly. Dr. Howard didn’t have to think twice about where he would want to set up his practice. The Columbia Basin has a lot to offer. It allows his family to partake in camping, fishing, skiing, hunting, and hiking activities, some only a short drive away.

Dr. Howard and his staff have discovered that the key to having a strong practice is treating patients as if they were family. They enjoy helping all generations of families maintain a healthy dental lifestyle in a caring environment.
