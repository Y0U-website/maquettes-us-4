Insurance and Financing FAQs

Will you take my dental insurance?

Yes, we work with most major dental insurance plans. Our staff will clearly explain your benefits and file claims for you.

What if I don’t have health insurance?

That’s okay. We can help you pay for treatment by giving you options like financing and other ways to pay. We also have specials for patients with no insurance. Call us at for more information.

What are the terms of your financing?

They let you pay for care in smaller, more manageable monthly payments, which is helpful for bigger procedures.

Will I know my costs before treatment?

Definitely. We provide a breakdown before you start, so you’ll know what’s covered and what your portion will be.

Do you have any specials?

Yes, many preventive services like exams and cleanings may be available at little or no cost, depending on your plan. Ask our team for details.
