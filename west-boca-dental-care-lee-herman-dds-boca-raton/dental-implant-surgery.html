Who Can Get Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults who have lost one or more teeth are candidates for implants. You may benefit from:

If your jawbone is thin or weakened, additional treatments such as bone grafting may be recommended first to ensure stability.

  • A single tooth implant procedure if just one tooth is missing.
  • Mini dental implant surgery is an option for people with low bone density or who need to stabilize their dentures.
  • Full mouth dental implant surgery is needed if you need to replace all of your teeth.

The Step-by-Step Dental Implant Surgery Process

We think that patients are more sure of themselves when they know what to expect. This is how our process usually goes:

For many patients, same-day dental implant surgery may be possible, meaning you’ll leave with a temporary tooth in place while healing continues.

  • Consultation and imaging: We take X-rays or 3D scans to plan your treatment.
  • Preparation: Any necessary pre-treatments, such as extractions, bone grafting, or gum therapy, are done.
  • Implant Placement: A titanium post is carefully put into your jaw while you are asleep or under anesthesia.
  • Healing and osseointegration: The bone naturally grows together with the implant over the course of 2–3 months.
  • Restoration: A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is put in place to finish off your new smile.

What to Expect After Surgery

It’s normal to have some mild swelling, pain, or bleeding for the first few days. We will give you detailed instructions on how to care for your home, and we may suggest a temporary diet of soft foods. Most patients quickly get back to their normal lives, and their healing continues over the next few months.

Regular checkups with our team make sure your implant heals properly and stays healthy for many years.

Dental Implants Surgery FAQs

How long does the step-by-step dental implant process take?

Most treatments take several months, including healing time. Some patients qualify for same-day dental implant surgery.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

No. Most patients don’t feel much pain during the procedure thanks to modern anesthesia and sedation.

What is the difference between traditional implants and mini dental implant surgery?

Mini implants are smaller and less invasive than regular implants. They are often used to keep dentures in place when the bone density is low.

If I’ve had dentures for years, can I still get full mouth dental implant surgery?

Yes, a lot of patients with bone loss still qualify. You might need bone grafting or All-on-4 implants.

How long do implants stay in place?

Dental implants can last for decades or even a lifetime if you take care of them.
