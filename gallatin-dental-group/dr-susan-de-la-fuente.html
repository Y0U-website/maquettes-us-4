Dr. Susan De La Fuente

She graduated in 1986 from the University of the East, Manila in the Philippines with a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. She took the Dental Board Examination and became a licensed to practice dentistry in California in 1992.

She’s been practicing dentistry for over 30 years and she enjoys helping all her patients. She takes numerous courses to stay up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry in order to provide the best treatment possible to all her patients.

In her spare time, she enjoys spending time with her family and the outdoors. She also enjoys cooking.
