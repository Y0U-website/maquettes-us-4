Dr. Michael Vert D.D.S.

Dr Vert is a pediatric dentist who specializes in children’s dentistry. He received his bachelor’s degree in biology from the University of California at Irvine in 1990. He then went on to the "prestigious" University of Southern California (USC) where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1994.

Dr. Vert was heavily involved in school organizations and activities. He served as the Senior Class President in 1993-1994 and the AADS Western US President in 1993-1994. Dr. Vert also received the highest award of his class, the "Behavior Award" for four years of outstanding humanistic qualities in his class of 1994 and the "Outstanding Service Award".

Dr. Vert decided to specialize in Pediatric Dentistry. He received his certificate in Pediatric Dentistry in 1996 from USC. Dr.Vert is specialized and trained to better deal with the needs of the "little ones". He is the "New Kid on the Block"and is fully committed to providing excellent dental care.

Dr. Vert is a member of the CDA, ADA, and the AAPD. Dr. Vert enjoys water-skiing, camping, playing the saxophone, writing, music and cinema.
