Orthodontics for Kids

The Gallatin Dental Pediatric team keeps a close eye on your child’s developing teeth because, contrary to popular belief, there are times when early orthodontic intervention is required. In these situations, a two-phase intervention is best:

A crossbite occurs because the top jaw is narrow compared to the bottom jaw. The top plate is the floor of the nose. When this palate is too narrow, breathing will be affected. Treatment is required to prevent breathing disorders.

Crossbites prevent the normal function of the jaws, leading to the overworked tempero-mandibular joints-TMJ, causing dysfunction. Crossbites prevent the top and bottom teeth from being aligned adequately relative to one another, affecting the smile’s aesthetics.

A crossbite appliance is worn to widen the top jaw relative to the bottom jaw and correct crossbites. Children’s bones are malleable early on, so there is a window of time for effective treatment.

HABIT BREAKING APPLIANCES (thumb sucking, tongue thrust)

Thumb or finger sucking and tongue thrust habits cause a gap between the top and bottom front teeth. The best time to address these habits is by the time the permanent teeth start to come in.

Parents are often successful at breaking these habits with bitters on the fingers or a reward chart. But when all else fails, a Tguard appliance can be very effective.

Orofacial Myofunctional Therapy (OMT), in addition to the guard, helps resolve oral habits. OMT aims to retrain muscles in and around the mouth to obtain a normal posture for the tongue, lips, cheeks, and jaws, reducing the need for orthodontic treatments.

  • Improved nasal breathing (airway-focused orthodontics)
  • Prevent extraction of permanent teeth (taking adult teeth out to gain space)
  • Reduce the need for future jaw surgery
  • Allows face to grow optimally
  • Improved function and esthetics

Orthodontics for Teens and Adults

Traditional braces successfully realign teeth. Brackets fixed onto the teeth with wires apply force on specific teeth to gently move them over time.

Invisalign uses a series of invisible aligners that change each week and slowly move teeth over time.

Invisalign is an effective option for responsible patients who wear and change their aligners as required. We love Invisalign because you can maintain proper oral hygiene by removing the aligners for eating, brushing, and flossing.
