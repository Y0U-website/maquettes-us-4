Am I a Candidate for All-On-4 Dental Implants?

The good news is more and more patients are eligible for dental implants than ever before, even if you have been missing teeth for a very long time. During your exam, we’ll confirm the current status of your oral health, making sure that no tooth decay or gum disease is present. We will also examine your current bone volume in order to confirm that your jaw can effectively hold dental implants. If you have good oral health, healthy gum tissue, sufficient bone volume, and a considerable amount of missing teeth, chances are likely that you’re eligible for All-on-4 dental implant treatment.

All-On-4 FAQs

Why am I only receiving a temporary denture on the day of my implant placement?

Although it would be nice to have your entire procedure completed in one day, this is simply not the case. Instead of receiving a finalized denture the day of your implant placement, you will be required to wear a temporary one instead, and here’s why:

  • It takes time for dental lab technicians to create a customized denture. Because your impressions will be sent to a lab for creation, it will require ample time for technicians to create a prosthetic based on the specifications provided by your dentist.
  • It is necessary that your mouth fully heals before receiving your final denture. While at home recovering, your temporary denture will apply less pressure than a permanent one, allowing your tissues and bone to fuse to the implants.

Can I receive All-On-4 dental implants on one arch?

Yes, you can choose to have All-On-4 dental implants in Edison placed solely on the top or bottom arch, but you and your dentist will need to discuss the potential for future tooth loss. If you have implants placed on the lower arch but there is a strong possibility that you could be living with missing teeth along the top arch, it may be best to have the full procedure completed at the same time. This will allow you to avoid additional time and money spent later on. However, Dr. Shah will ensure your smile is properly cared for no matter what you decide.

How successful is All-On-4 implant placement?

All-On-4 implants have an extremely high success rate. When placed on the lower arch, they have a 98.2 % success rate, and when placed on the upper arch, it is a 97-98 % success rate.

If you are feeling apprehensive about the idea of having All-On-4 dental implants put into place, these percentages can help put your mind at ease.

Once I receive my implants, how often will I need to see the dentist?

It is still in your best interest to see Dr. Shah every six months for regular dental checkups and cleanings. This not only allows us to take proper care of both your restoration and natural teeth, but it also gives us the chance to examine your denture and ensure no problem areas are developing. Having your restoration checked frequently will allow you to enjoy it significantly longer.
