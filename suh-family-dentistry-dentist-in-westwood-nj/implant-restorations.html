What We Check During An Implant Restoration Appointment

Before starting your treatment, we carefully check your oral health and mouth condition to make sure everything is ready. An implant restoration Westwood NJ, is planned with your comfort and long-term results in mind.

Examining the Jaw and Bone

We look at your jaw and the bone where the implant will go. This helps us understand the strength and shape of your jaw so the implant will be stable. Checking the bone also helps prevent future problems and makes sure we restore the dental implant Westwood NJ.

Checking the Surrounding Teeth

We take a close look at the teeth around the missing spot. Our dentist can then plan placement so your new tooth fits naturally and does not disturb nearby teeth. Healthy neighboring teeth make the restoration more comfortable and stronger.

Gums and Soft Tissues

Healthy gums are very important for an implant. We check for any inflammation or tenderness that might affect healing. Your gums need to be healthy if you want your new tooth to look natural and stay secure for years.

Bite and Alignment

We also need to check if your upper and lower teeth come together when you bite. This way, it will be easy to see if your new implant supported crown Westwood NJ, will fit comfortably and work naturally with your other teeth. We check for any uneven pressure or strain that could affect healing.

What to Expect During Your Visit

Talking Through Your Case

We start by sitting down with you and going over your tooth. You can share what has been bothering you or what you are hoping to fix. We listen carefully and answer your questions.

Checking the Implant Area

We take a close look at the implant and the area around it. This helps us see how everything is healing and if it is ready for the next step. We also check your gums and nearby teeth.

Fitting the Crown

We place the crown on top of the implant and check how it fits. Small adjustments are made so it feels natural when you close your mouth or bite. We take our time here so nothing feels off.

Final Placement

Once everything feels right, the crown is fixed in place. We make sure it is secure and comfortable before you leave. You will be able to use it like your normal tooth.

Guidance Before You Leave

Before you go, we will explain how to take care of your new tooth. If you have any doubts, we can clear them right there so you feel relaxed going home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question?

How long does an implant restoration take?

It depends on your case. In most visits, placing the crown is done in one appointment, but we make sure everything fits properly before finishing.

Will the new tooth feel different?

At first, it may feel slightly new, but you will get used to it very quickly. After a short time, it will feel like your own natural tooth.

Can I eat normally after the visit?

Yes, but we usually suggest starting with softer foods for a short time. Once you feel comfortable, you can go back to your normal eating routine.

How long will the implant restoration last?

With good care and regular checkups, it can last for many years. Keeping your teeth clean and healthy plays a big role in how long they stay in good shape.
