Oral Cancer Screening

Oral cancer screening is an important part of family dentist Westwood NJ care. During the checkup, we check your lips, tongue, and gums for any early signs of problems. These screenings help detect problems before they become serious and make treatment much easier if something is found.

Night Guards

Night guards are custom-made devices that protect your teeth from any grinding or clenching while you sleep. During dental checkups Westwood NJ, we can check if a night guard is right for you and fit it comfortably to prevent wear or damage to your teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still have a question?

How frequently should my family visit the dentist?

Most patients benefit from a checkup every six months. Regular visits help catch small problems early and keep teeth and gums healthy.

What is generally included in a preventive dental visit?

A preventive visit mostly includes a dental exam, cleaning, fluoride treatment, and sometimes sealants or screenings for oral health issues.

Are dental checkups safe for children?

Yes! Our team makes children feel comfortable and supports healthy dental habits from the very first visit.

How do preventive treatments help avoid bigger problems?

Preventive care helps detect cavities, gum issues, and other dental problems early, so they can be treated before they become more serious.

Does preventive care cover adults and seniors, too?

Absolutely. Everyone benefits from regular preventive care, which helps maintain teeth and gums at any age.
