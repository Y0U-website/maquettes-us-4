What We Check During A Comprehensive Dental Exam

When you come in for dental exams Westwood NJ, we take the time to look closely at your teeth and gums. Every checkup helps us find small problems before they become bigger, so your smile stays healthy.

Teeth Evaluation

We examine your teeth carefully for cavities, cracks, or worn spots. This helps us spot issues early, so you do not end up with pain or complicated treatments. During dental exams Westwood NJ, we also look at any previous dental work to make sure it is still in good shape.

Gum Health Assessment

Healthy gums protect your teeth and overall oral health. We check for redness or bleeding that could show early gum problems. By noticing changes early, we can give guidance to keep your gums strong and prevent bigger issues.

Digital X-Rays

Some problems hide between teeth or under the gums. We use digital X-rays during a dental checkup Westwood NJ, to see these areas clearly. This helps spot any issues before they cause discomfort.

Bite and Jaw Check

How your teeth fit together affects your smile and comfort. We check your bite and jaw movement to make sure everything is aligned. This can help prevent uneven tooth wear and other long-term issues.

Full Mouth Review

We review your whole mouth, including teeth and gums, to understand your overall dental health. Every new patient dental exam Westwood NJ, includes this step, so we can give personalized advice and a care plan that fits your needs.
