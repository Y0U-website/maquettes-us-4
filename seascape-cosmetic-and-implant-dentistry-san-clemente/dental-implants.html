The Dental Implant Procedure: Step-by-Step

  • Initial Consultation: During your first visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health, take X-rays, and discuss your goals for the procedure.
  • Implant Placement Surgery: The implant post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation.
  • Healing Period: The bone and implant fuse together in a process called osseointegration, which typically takes 3–6 months.
  • Crown Placement: Once the implant is secure, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing your restoration.

What to Expect During Your Recovery?

  • Initial discomfort may occur, but it can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
  • Swelling and bruising are common around the implant site but usually subside after a few days.
  • Diet modifications are recommended for the first few weeks—avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods while your implant heals.
  • Follow-up appointments will be scheduled to monitor healing and ensure the implant is integrating well with the jawbone.

Maintaining Your Dental Implants

  • Good oral hygiene: Brush twice daily and floss once to keep your implants and surrounding teeth clean.
  • Regular dental check-ups: Schedule biannual check-ups to monitor the health of your implants.
  • Avoid excessive force: Avoid chewing on hard items like ice, pens, or hard candy to prevent damage to your implants.
