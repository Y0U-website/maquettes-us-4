Meet the Doctors

Dan Tracy, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Dan Tracy joined the Perfect Smiles team after graduating from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine followed by a general practice residency at the University of California, San Francisco.

He has since discovered that cosmetic dental procedures are his favorite because he can positively transform so many aspects of his patient’s life by giving or enhancing their smile.

Dr. Tracy’s enthusiasm about dentistry quickly puts patients at ease and he always looks forward to helping patients smile confidently!

We would say the qualities that describe Dr. Tracy are: genuine, selfless, caring, and thoughtful. He is an amazing and inspirational person for all who know him. We are honored to have him on our team and proud of his dedication to the field.

In 2023 he was honored with the 10 Under Ten Award by the Massachusetts Dental Society, which recognizes ten dentists each year for their contributions to their communities and to the profession.

He has spent many years volunteering at community events and mission trips overseas along with the Perfect Smiles team. During his free time, he enjoys watching Boston sports teams and cooking.

I love helping patients be able to smile confidently!

Madhuri Patel, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Madhuri Patel is a skilled, likable, and empathetic dentist. Patients love her because she provides excellent dental care in a gentle and relaxed manner. With a passion for family dentistry, she recently moved to Rhode Island from New York City after completing her dental school at the largest and busiest dental school in the U.S., NYU College of Dentistry . Her coworkers and our patients appreciate her commitment to her work and the well-being of her patients. She always carries a positive attitude along with a calm demeanor.

During her free time, she enjoys traveling, hiking, and volunteering at community events and dental mission trips.

“I love to provide a beautiful and confident smile and educate patients about the importance of oral health.”

Giovanna Cusumano D.M.D

Dentist

Dr. Giovanna Cusumano is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered dental care that transforms smiles and improves lives. A proud member of the Perfect Smiles team, Dr. Giovanna combines her deep expertise in dentistry with a genuine passion for helping patients feel confident and comfortable with their oral health.

Her journey into dentistry began at the age of six, driven by a fascination with the intersection of science, art, and medicine. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree in neuroscience from Michigan State University before graduating from Tufts Dental School. During her externship in Winslow, AZ, Dr. Giovanna had the privilege of serving members of the Navajo community, an experience that shaped her commitment to providing care to underserved populations.

At Tufts, Dr. Giovanna demonstrated her leadership skills as president of the American Association of Women Dentists and treasurer of the Italian Dental Association. She was also honored with the Tiberi Family Scholarship, reflecting her strong connection to her Italian heritage.

Outside the office, Dr. Giovanna enjoys painting and drawing, expressing her creative side through art. She cherishes time spent with her family and her beloved cat, Josephina.

“I treat every patient like family, taking the time to ensure they feel comfortable and confident in their smile.”

Javier Ramirez D.D.S

Dentist

Dr. Ramirez joined our team with decades years of experience and a strong commitment to helping patients achieve both oral and overall health. He began his dental career in Medellín, Colombia, where he graduated from the University of Antioquia and provided care to underserved communities with compassion and dedication.

After moving to the United States, Dr. Ramirez continued his training at the University of Illinois at Chicago, bringing a global perspective and a deep belief that oral health is the foundation of total well-being. Fluent in Spanish and Portuguese, he is proud to connect with patients in the language they’re most comfortable speaking, making every interaction more personal and welcoming.

He especially enjoys forming lasting connections with his patients and helping them feel confident in their smiles. His calm, genuine approach immediately puts people at ease, and he’s passionate about making every visit a positive experience.

We would describe Dr. Ramirez as thoughtful, compassionate, dependable, and deeply committed to patient care. His presence brings warmth and kindness to our team, and we’re proud to have him with us.

Outside the office, Dr. Ramirez enjoys spending time with his wife and four children. He’s known for his great sense of humor and loves making people laugh something his family and patients truly appreciate!

“I strive to help you look good, feel good, and stay on the path to a healthier you.”

Erica Kite D.M.D.

Dentist

When you meet Dr. Erica Kite, you quickly notice her warm, energetic, and genuinely caring approach—not to mention her radiant smile that puts patients instantly at ease. Her goal in patient care is to make every visit feel comfortable, supportive, and stress-free, no matter a patient’s needs or past experiences.

Originally from Cranston, Dr. Kite believes every patient deserves to be treated like family. She takes the time to listen, communicate clearly, and build lasting trust, ensuring each person feels heard, respected, and confident in their care. Her approach reflects the heart of Perfect Smiles’ core values— leading with compassion, fostering a positive culture, and bringing a cheerful spirit into every interaction.

Dr. Kite began her career from the ground up, first as a high school intern and then as a dental assistant for nearly a decade. This hands-on experience gave her a deep appreciation for every step of the patient journey. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Boston University and a Biology degree from Syracuse University , and continues to expand her expertise through ongoing education, including Invisalign clear aligner therapy .

She is especially passionate about helping patients who feel anxious or overwhelmed, creating a judgment-free, relaxed environment where everyone feels safe and supported.

Patients often describe Dr. Kite as positive, funny, and calming —someone who makes it easy to smile. She’s proud to be part of the Perfect Smiles team, where her values and energy align perfectly with our mission to deliver compassionate, uplifting care every day and promote total health and wellness.

“I’m passionate about educating and caring for patients like family so every visit feels comfortable, supportive, and even a little fun, while we work together toward a healthy, confident smile.
