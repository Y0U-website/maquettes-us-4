Hide Smile Flaws With Veneers or Bonding

You can choose dental veneers or dental bonding to cover imperfections like chips or stains. Our dentists can help you select the cosmetic dental procedure that is best for your smile. Among the factors to consider:

Teeth contouring is another option for subtly altering the appearance of your teeth. Our dentists can correct an uneven edge on a tooth, for example.

  • Degree of Coverage – In general, because they cover the entire front surfaces of teeth, veneers can cover a wider range of flaws than bonding.
  • Cost – Bonding is more affordable than veneers.
  • Time – Bonding can usually be completed in a single appointment. Veneers typically require two or three visits.
  • Durability – Veneers resist staining and hold up to wear and tear better than bonding. Veneers should last at least a decade or two with proper care.

At Perfect Smiles, we offer different kinds of orthodontics, and our Seekonk, MA dentists will help you select the one that is best for you.

Some smiles may need traditional metal orthodontic braces , and you can get those at Perfect Smiles. We offer less conspicuous choices too, including:

  • Clear Ceramic Braces – Thanks to their crystal clear brackets, these braces are far less noticeable in your smile than traditional braces. They straighten teeth in the same way as metal braces, so they’re often a better choice for those who care about concealing their treatment.
  • ClearCorrect & Invisalign Clear Aligners – These plastic aligners are not only nearly invisible, they’re removable too. Take them out for meals and eat whatever you’d like!
  • Limited Orthodontic Treatment – When you are wanting a small amount of work done, like making room for an implant or closing the gap between your front teeth, we offer ClearCorrect Flex, which allows us to move only the specific tooth or teeth you want to focus on while keeping the treatment hidden from plain sight.

Enjoy the Advantages of Clear Aligners

Our team can design an orthodontics treatment plan just for you, using clear aligners from respected brands ClearCorrect or Invisalign. Whether you’re looking for perfect alignment or just want to straighten the teeth that show when you smile, we’ll create a customized plan using 3D software. A special 3D printing lab then makes the aligners that will produce your desired results.

Clear aligners are less costly and clearer than some other aligners and are the preferred way to straighten teeth. In addition to their versatility and affordability, they are:

  • Easy – You wear each clear aligner about three weeks before switching to the next one in the series. You’ll need fewer visits to our Seekonk, MA office during your treatment than braces require.
  • Convenient – Take out your aligners to eat, to brush and floss your teeth, and even for special occasions. (Just make sure you wear them at least 22 hours a day.)
  • Practically Invisible – Most people probably won’t even notice you’re wearing them! This is why aligners are the preferred choice for professionals and students. Straighten your smile without drawing any attention to treatment.

Braces Are Still Best for Some Smiles

For less noticeable straightening, you can get clear ceramic braces. These crystal clear brackets blend easily into your smile.
