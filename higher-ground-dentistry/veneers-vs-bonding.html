Porcelain Veneers

Ever wonder why some smiles look effortlessly perfect — naturally white, beautifully shaped, and completely symmetrical? For many people the answer is porcelain veneers. A single versatile treatment that can correct color, shape, size, and minor alignment issues all at once — without lengthy orthodontic treatment or aggressive dental work.

At Higher Ground Dentistry , we take a conservative approach to veneers — preserving as much of your natural tooth structure as possible while crafting restorations that genuinely look like they belong to you. The goal is never a dramatic artificial transformation. It’s a smile that looks like the best version of yours.

What Are Porcelain Veneers?

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin, custom-made shells crafted from high-grade dental ceramic — bonded directly to the front surface of your teeth to instantly transform your smile. Think of them as a precise, permanent upgrade to the teeth you already have.

Unlike dental crowns, which require significant reshaping of the tooth, veneers are minimally invasive. In most cases only a tiny sliver of enamel — about the thickness of a fingernail — is removed to ensure your veneers sit flush and look completely natural. The result is a stronger, more beautiful smile that still looks and feels like yours.

At Higher Ground Dentistry, every set of veneers is custom designed to match your facial features, skin tone, and smile goals — so the end result looks refined, not artificial.

Veneers vs. Dental Bonding: A Detailed Comparison

Patients in Upland often ask — should I get veneers or dental bonding? Here’s a simple breakdown to help you decide:

Natural translucency — mimics real enamel

Good results but can look flat over time

High — glazed surface resists coffee and wine

Low — porous material stains like natural teeth

The Bottom Line: Bonding is a great affordable option for small quick fixes. If you’re looking for a long-lasting complete smile transformation, porcelain veneers deliver superior and more permanent results.

Our Conservative Approach to Veneers

Traditional veneer techniques sometimes required removing a significant amount of healthy tooth structure — which caused sensitivity and was irreversible. At Higher Ground Dentistry we take a different approach.

Whenever possible we use a no-prep or minimal-prep technique — meaning little to no enamel is removed before placing your veneers. This preserves your natural tooth structure, reduces post-treatment sensitivity, and keeps your teeth stronger long term. Your enamel is irreplaceable, and we treat it that way.

The result is a veneer that bonds securely to your tooth, looks completely natural, and causes far less discomfort than older techniques — without compromising on quality or durability.

Your Veneer Journey at Higher Ground Dentistry

Getting porcelain veneers is a straightforward process — and we walk you through every step so there are no surprises.

1. Smile Design We start by studying your facial features — your eye line, lip symmetry, and skin tone — to design veneers that look like they naturally belong to you. The goal is a smile that enhances your appearance without looking overdone.

2. Tooth Preparation A tiny, precise amount of enamel is gently removed from the surface of each tooth — typically no more than the thickness of a fingernail. This creates the perfect fit for your custom veneers.

3. Custom Fabrication Your veneers are crafted from high-quality dental porcelain by skilled ceramists — built to match the exact shade, shape, and translucency we designed together in step one.

4. Bonding At your final visit your veneers are permanently bonded to your teeth using a strong dental resin cement. Once placed they seal the tooth surface and blend seamlessly with your natural smile.
