What to Expect During the Denture Process

Step 1: Consultation and planning

The dentist examines oral health and discusses denture types and fit preferences.

Step 2: Impressions and measurements

Customized molds ensure accurate bite and appearance.

Step 3: Fitting and adjustments

Initial fitting is adjusted for comfort, bite, and confidence.

Step 4: Follow-up visits

Additional refinements ensure proper long-term fit as gums and bones change naturally

Caring for Your Dentures

Daily care tips

Good denture hygiene keeps breath fresh and prevents irritation.

  • Brush dentures gently with a soft toothbrush
  • Store dentures in water when not wearing
  • Clean gums and tongue regularly
  • Schedule routine dental checkups

Common Concerns: Realistic Expectations

It may take a few weeks to adjust to speaking and chewing with new dentures. Soreness is normal during the adjustment period and fades with consistent wear and gradual dietary changes.
