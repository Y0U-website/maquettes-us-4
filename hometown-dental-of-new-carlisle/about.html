Get To Know Dr. Sean Verma

Dr. Sean Verma is a proud native of the Dayton area, where his passion for dentistry was inspired by his family dentist. With guidance and mentorship from Dr. Eric Schneider, Dr. Verma discovered his calling to help others through dental care. He earned his undergraduate degree from Wright State University in 2012, followed by his dental degree from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in 2016. After completing a rigorous general practice residency in Cleveland, Dr. Verma returned home to serve the community that shaped him.

Dr. Verma fell in love dentistry because of its unique blend of science, art, and service. Whether he’s crafting a perfectly fitting crown or performing oral surgery, he enjoys the challenge of combining precision and creativity to restore smiles, as it allows him to make an immediate and lasting impact on his patients’ confidence and oral health. Dr. Verma’s dedication to continuous education ensures he stays on the cutting edge of dental advancements, providing the highest standard of care.

Outside the office, Dr. Verma is an avid reader who enjoys exploring the outdoors through hiking and running. He finds inspiration and balance in nature, often taking advantage of the scenic trails around Dayton. His hobbies reflect his commitment to personal growth, a value he brings to his practice as he continually seeks ways to enhance the patient experience.

At Hometown Dental of New Carlisle, Dr. Verma is dedicated to fostering a welcoming environment where patients feel valued and cared for.
