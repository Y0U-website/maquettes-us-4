If a dental implant has become worn, loose, or damaged over time, or if you’re completing a new implant placement, implant restoration is essential for long-term oral health. A well-maintained, properly fitted restoration ensures that your bite remains intact, your jawbone stays strong, and your smile continues to look its best.

At Hometown Dental of New Carlisle , we use advanced tools like the 3Shape intraoral scanner to take accurate, mess-free impressions and craft restorations that are precise, comfortable, and built to last. Each patient and treatment plan is unique. Our team takes great pride in making sure that your restoration works with your lifestyle and goals.

The Benefits of Implant Restoration

Improved Function and Comfort Implant restoration restores the full strength of your bite, so you can eat comfortably and speak clearly. You&#x27;ll enjoy the stability of a natural tooth without worry of slippage or discomfort.

Natural-Looking Results We design each restoration to blend seamlessly with your smile. Using modern imaging and scanning technology, we craft a crown that mimics the color and contour of your natural teeth.

Long-Term Oral Health Restoring your implant keeps surrounding teeth aligned and helps prevent bone loss in the jaw, promoting overall dental stability for years to come.

The Implant Restoration Treatment Process Comprehensive Evaluation and Digital Scan Your first step begins with a detailed evaluation by Dr. Sean Verma. Every implant restoration is custom-designed based on your needs. Precision Placement and Follow-Up

Once your restoration is completed, we carefully secure it to the implant post, making any necessary adjustments to ensure proper bite alignment and comfort. Once your restoration has been placed, you can immediately return to your normal routine, without worrying about any downtime or recovery.
