Three easy steps to get the smile of your dreams!

Step 1: Consultation

Begin your journey with a consultation with our 3 On 6™ certified doctor. We will take scans of your mouth to determine your candidacy for the 3 On 6™ treatment. We’ll also discuss personalized payment and financing options to help you feel confident about your treatment journey.

Step 2: Implant Placement and Temporary Smile

At your next appointment, we’ll perform the implant surgery with various sedation options for your comfort. You’ll wake up with new implants and beautiful temporary teeth.

After surgery, your implants will need time to heal, ranging from weeks to months. Our skilled lab will then design your final smile according to your preferences, including the ideal shade.

Step 3: Your Stunning Final Bridges

Once healed, you may receive a second set of temporary teeth to perfect your look, or our in-house lab will craft your final zirconia bridges. During your final appointment, the doctor will place these bridges (no anesthesia required) and ensure you’re thrilled with your new smile.

Your permanent 3 On 6 TM smile will allow you to enjoy life with confidence—just brush and floss to maintain it. Smile, speak, kiss, and chew freely!
