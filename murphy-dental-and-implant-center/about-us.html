Our Office & Technology

Our modern office design creates a comfortable, welcoming environment from the moment you arrive.

Technology enhances every aspect of your care: Trios digital impressions eliminate messy molds, CBCT 3D imaging (Vatech) enables precise treatment planning, Pearl AI diagnostics catch concerns early, digital X-rays minimize radiation exposure, intraoral cameras show you exactly what we see, Piezo ultrasonic scalers provide gentle cleanings, and advanced sedation equipment ensures comfort during procedures.

We also offer innovative treatments like PRP therapy, Arestin localized antibiotic therapy, and Curodont for early decay remineralization.

Dr. Jenny Tai

Periodontist and Cosmetic Dentistry Specialist

Dr. Jenny Tai is an accomplished dentist with unique expertise, having completed a rigorous four-year, dual-specialty program at the University of Pennsylvania where she earned her periodontal prosthesis certificate. This extensive training makes her proficient in cosmetic, restorative, periodontic, and implant dentistry—one of the few dentists in Texas with such comprehensive credentials.

Her commitment to education earned her the Best Teacher title from the Department of Periodontics at the University of Pennsylvania. Dr. Tai continues contributing to the field through her work with the graduate prosthodontics program at Texas A&M University College of Dentistry, where she served as clinical adjunct professor.

Dr. Ted Ling

Periodontist and Dental Implant Surgeon

Following graduation from residency, Dr. Ling served as adjunct assistant professor at Texas A&M University College of Dentistry. He mentored post-graduate residents in planning and executing dental implant surgeries, sharing knowledge and advancing the field.

Dr. Erica Chan

General and Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Erica Chan graduated from the University of California, Davis with a Bachelor of Science in Neurobiology, Physiology, and Behavior as a distinguished Regents Scholarship recipient. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University, Illinois, graduating at the top of her class.

After practicing in Indiana, Dr. Chan relocated to North Dallas with her family. Renowned for excellent clinical skills and compassionate care, she prioritizes effective communication and patient education to achieve superior outcomes. As a general dentist, she maintains keen interest in clear aligner treatments and digital dentistry, constantly updating her knowledge through continuing education. Outside the office, Dr. Chan is an avid traveler who has explored 17 countries and counting with her family.

Dr. Avital B. Schaffer

Comprehensive and Restorative Dentistry

Dr. Avital B. Schaffer earned her Bachelor of Arts in Biology from the University of Maryland, Baltimore County before completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, where she graduated with honors in restorative dentistry. She later completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Texas A&M University, receiving advanced training in comprehensive restorative care, implant dentistry, cosmetic treatment, oral surgery, and root canal therapy.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Schaffer has completed more than 250 hours of continuing education and is actively pursuing her Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry. She is a certified Invisalign® Provider known for her conservative, patient-focused approach and dedication to helping patients feel informed and comfortable throughout treatment. Outside the office, Dr. Schaffer enjoys spending time with her husband and two children, playing tennis, working out, baking, and reading.
