General Dentistry

Teeth Cleanings

During routine teeth cleanings, our team removes plaque and tartar and polishes your teeth with cutting-edge electric handpieces and laser technology. Our dentist will then examine for cavities, gum disease, and other oral health issues, and discuss any concerns you may have.

Digital X-Rays & Examinations

With intraoral and digital x-ray technology, we can detect cavities, impacted teeth, and tooth decay faster and more efficiently than standard x-rays. We also use panorex technology to examine patients for sleep apnea, TMD/TMJ disorders, and other problems in the oral cavity.

Composite Fillings

Using laser technology, local anesthesia, and oral sedation, our team will help you feel as comfortable as possible during a cavity filling. We can fill your cavity with a composite resin that resembles the natural color of your teeth. This material bonds tightly to the surface to prevent decay and keep bacteria out.

Root Canals

Tooth Extractions

Tooth extraction may be necessary if you have an infected, decaying, or broken tooth. We’ll work closely with you during and after treatment to help you recover fast and feel comfortable every step of the way. We also perform wisdom tooth removal.

Periodontal Treatment

Plaque and tartar buildup can lead to two forms of gum disease known as gingivitis and periodontitis. Routine teeth cleanings can typically treat and remove gingivitis, but in cases of periodontitis, root planing and scaling may be necessary. These non-surgical procedures deep clean the affected areas and remove plaque and tartar buildup.

Sedation Dentistry

If you feel nervous about visiting the dentist, sedation dentistry can help you stay calm and comfortable during your appointment. Our office offers several sedation options that are safe, effective, and tailored to your needs. Before your procedure, our dentist will review your medical history, explain your options, and recommend the best sedation method for you.

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

A light sedation inhaled through a small mask, nitrous oxide helps you feel relaxed while remaining fully awake. The effects wear off quickly, so you can return to your normal activities right after your appointment.

Oral Conscious Sedation

Taken in pill form before your visit, oral sedation provides a deeper sense of relaxation while allowing you to remain responsive. This option is helpful for patients with moderate dental anxiety or for longer procedures.

Implant & Restorative Dentistry

Dental Implants

Dental implants are mini titanium rods that restore missing or extracted teeth by acting like natural roots. When placed into the upper or lower jawbone, these implants integrate with the bone, creating a stable base for teeth. Once the implant and bone have fused and healed, the dental implant provides an anchor for a dental crown or prosthetic tooth.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are individual caps that restore the look, function, and strength of a tooth. They’re often anchored to dental implants or administered after root canal treatment. They can also restore cracked, worn down, or discolored teeth. Dental crowns resemble your natural teeth and are made from a variety of materials, including composite resin, ceramic, or porcelain.

Dental Bridges

If you have multiple missing teeth, dental bridges can correct the gaps and restore your natural smile. Like dental crowns, they’re usually administered once a set of dental implants has healed. Dental bridges not only restore smiles but also prevent future bone loss and jaw damage.

Dentures

Whether you’ve lost your teeth to gum disease, injury, or progressive tooth decay, dentures provide a stable, long-lasting solution. These removable appliances make it easier to speak and eat, all while preventing facial muscle sagging. Conventional dentures are placed after the gum tissue has healed following an injury or extraction. Immediate dentures are administered right after teeth are removed, and they require routine adjustments as the jawbone heals.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

Our teeth lose their natural color over time as a result of age, diet, and lifestyle. If you’re struggling with yellowing or discoloration, professional teeth whitening lasts longer than over-the-counter whiteners. Our dental office utilizes a customized whitening tray that matches and enhances the natural color of your teeth.

Dental Veneers

Dental veneers are thin porcelain shells that cover individual teeth to create a fuller, brighter, and redesigned smile. This transformative procedure can address a variety of cosmetic concerns, including discolored, chipped, worn-down, and misaligned teeth.

Invisalign®

Invisalign is a cutting-edge orthodontic treatment that provides a state-of-the-art alternative to traditional metal braces. Utilizing a series of clear, removable plastic aligners, Invisalign gradually shifts your teeth into their desired positions without the discomfort and aesthetic drawbacks associated with metal braces.

This innovative approach to teeth straightening not only offers a more discreet option for individuals of all ages but also allows for a more comfortable experience throughout the treatment process. Unlike conventional braces that can cause irritation and require frequent adjustments, Invisalign aligners are custom-made for your teeth and can be easily removed for eating and oral hygiene.
