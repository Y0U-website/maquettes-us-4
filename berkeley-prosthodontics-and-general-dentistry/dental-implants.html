Our Berkeley Dental Implant Options

Since we anchor the dental implant in your jaw, we have several restoration options. Single crowns are the most common restorative procedure; however, we can also use implants to provide support for a fixed dental bridge or dentures.

There are various ways we use implants to secure dentures. The basic procedure involves placing up to four dental implants at different points in the jaw. We design your dentures with attachments that correspond to the location of the implants. When the process is complete, you are left with beautifully natural dentures that fit and feel as much like natural teeth as possible.

During your consultation with Dr. Au, we will go over all your tooth replacement options and determine which option is the best fit for your budget, lifestyle, and smile needs.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

At Berkeley Prosthodontics and General Dentistry, Dr. Au highly recommends dental implants for patients who are interested in preventing further tooth loss and bone deterioration. One of the main advantages of dental implants is how easy they are to maintain.

Due to their permanent nature, dental implants will not shift or move. Once they have adhered to your jaw bone, you won’t need special tools or routine to care for your dental implants. Maintenance is as easy as daily brushing and flossing at home along with regular dental check-ups and exams with Dr. Au.

Dr. Au will be able to track how your dental implants are healing, along with any potential oral health issues. If you feel like something isn’t right, or there might be a problem with your dental implant, call our dental office immediately so we can ensure everything is on track.

With proper care, our Berkeley patients can expect to enjoy their dental implants for a lifetime. If the top part of the restoration cracks or chips, it can be easily replaced. If at any time you suspect something has changed with your dental implant, or something just doesn’t feel right, please contact Berkeley Prosthodontics and General Dentistry immediately so we can arrange a dental check-up with Dr. Au.
