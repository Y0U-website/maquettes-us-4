Dr. Amy Au - Berkeley Prosthodontist

Dr. Au received her Bachelor of Arts degree in Applied Science from the University of the Pacific. She then attended the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (DDS).

During her studies, Dr. Au found her passion for restorative dentistry and went on to attend three additional years of training in the Advanced Prosthodontics Program at the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry and receive her Masters of Oral Science degree. She is a Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists (ACP).

As part of her commitment to providing her patients with cutting-edge restorative dentistry, Dr. Au stays current with the latest advancements in dentistry, including state-of-the-art technology and updated techniques and materials. She relentlessly strives for excellence and has been recognized for her achievements with numerous honors and awards, including:

2nd Place in John J. Sharry Prosthodontics Research Competition, ACP Tau Kappa Omega, College of Physicians and Surgeons, Honor Society OKU Clinical Excellence Award in Removable Prosthodontics Quintessence Research Award for Outstanding Research Achievement Listing in Who’s Who Among Students in American Colleges – Outstanding Merit and Accomplishment Hinman Award for Excellence in Research

Dr. Au is also an active member of the following associations:

Pacific Coast Society of Prosthodontics, Active Member and Past Editor California Dental Association American College of Prosthodontics Fellow (ACP) International Association of Dental Research (ADR) American Dental Association (ADA)​ Berkeley Dental Society

Dr. Au was born in Taipei, Taiwan, and raised in the San Francisco Bay Area. She is a foodie and a yogi. In her spare time, she enjoys traveling with her significant other, Matthew, who is also a dentist.

Jane Wang, DMD

Dr. Wang is a prosthodontist trained at the University of California, San Francisco. She received her DMD from Tufts University and certificate in Advanced Education in General Dentistry from NYU Langone. During her training, she was Chief Resident and was awarded for her graduate research in remineralization of caries under fluoride treatment.

Dr. Jane Wang is passionate about motivating her patients to achieve their oral health goals. From preventative therapy to complex oral rehabilitations, Dr. Wang provides tailored treatment plans to each patient’s needs.

Currently, Dr. Wang is an associate clinical professor at the University of the Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry. She enjoys mentoring dental students in dentistry and prosthodontics. She is also involved in various outreach programs providing care to elderly populations and veterans. She is an active member of the Pacific Coast Society for Prosthodontics, San Francisco Dental Society, American College of Prosthodontics, California Dental Association, and more.
