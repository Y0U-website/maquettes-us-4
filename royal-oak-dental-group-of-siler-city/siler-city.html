Scroll to Top Dr. Blake Jones, DMD Hailing from Idaho Falls, ID, Dr. Blake Jones grew up enjoying sports and the outdoors. Dr. Jones attended Brigham Young University in Idaho, graduating with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. After graduation, he attended Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine where he received a Health Services Scholarship from the United States Air Force.

During his time in the USAF, Dr. Jones was stationed in Royal Air Force Lakenheath, where he would hone his skills and meet his fellow co-founders of Royal Oak Dental Group . After being discharged from the USAF, the Jones family moved to North Carolina, and formed Royal Oak Dental Group with several other Air Force dentists.

Petteri Salo, DDS

Dr. Petteri Salo loves having the opportunity to work close to home and serving the residents of Pittsboro. After completing his dental training at the UNC Adams School of Dentistry, Dr. Salo and his wife bought a home in Pittsboro and have greatly enjoyed living in the area ever since. He originally hails from Finland but has been a resident of North Carolina for over 30 years and is committed to providing exceptional dental care for his home community.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Salo enjoys spending time with his family and friends, going hiking, biking, and working on home improvement projects. You may see Dr. Salo around town with his wife and their three energetic, very friendly Border Collies, Ell, Willa, and Lucy. The five of them are always looking for their next outdoor adventure. Dr. Salo loves grilled food, and you can find him manning the grill in almost any weather. He also openly admits he has a sweet tooth but makes sure to brush and floss every day!
