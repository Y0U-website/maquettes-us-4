What Are All-on-X Dental Implants?

All-on-X dental implants are specialized dental implant procedures in which our dentists, Dr. Travis Wills and Dr. Ben Hiener , can replace an entire tooth arch using as few as four to six implants. All-on-X implants offer a comprehensive solution for people with edentulism (complete tooth loss) or multiple missing teeth.

Unlike traditional implants that occasionally require your dentist to place separate implants for each missing tooth, the All-on-X dental implant procedure only requires four to six implants placed strategically in your mouth. They are carefully positioned so they can provide support and stability for full arch teeth.

The All-on-X Implant Procedure in Shady Spring, Lochgelly, and Bluefield, WV

At our dental offices in Shady Spring, Lochgelly, and Bluefield, our dental team, led by Dr. Travis Wills and Dr. Ben Hiener , begins the All-on-X implant procedure by conducting a thorough evaluation to determine whether you’re suitable for the treatment. The evaluation phase will consider factors such as medical history, bone density, and overall health. If you are a suitable candidate for an All-on-X, our dentist will perform dental imaging to plan the implant procedure perfectly.

Before the surgery, you will be given local anesthesia or oral sedation to ensure you’re relaxed throughout the procedure. Our dentist will then expertly and strategically insert the dental implants into the jawbone. Two or more dental implants will be placed vertically in front of the arch, and an equal number of implants will be angulated towards the back. Strategically placing the implants this way eliminates the need for bone grafting in many cases and effectively uses the available bone support.

Once the implants are securely placed, our team of professional dentists will attach a temporary set of prosthetic teeth to support healing and osseointegration . After a few months, the implants will have fully fused with your jawbone, providing a solid foundation for the final restoration.

After the healing period, our dentist will remove the temporary prosthetic teeth and replace them with a customized permanent dental arch. The replacement teeth are carefully designed to match your natural tooth color, shape, and bite alignment, giving you a pleasing and beautiful smile.
