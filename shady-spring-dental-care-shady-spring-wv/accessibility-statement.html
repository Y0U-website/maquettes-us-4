Accessibility Statement

General Accessibility Commitment

At Shady Spring Dental Care, we strive to ensure that our dental services in Shady Spring and Lochgelly are accessible to individuals with disabilities. As part of our commitment, we have invested a significant amount of resources to make our site easier to navigate and fully compliant with accessibility standards. We strongly believe that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence, and we aim to make our website a welcoming experience for all visitors.

Accessibility on ShadySpringDentalCare.com

Shady Spring Dental Care makes the UserWay Website Accessibility Widget available, which is powered by a dedicated accessibility server. The software allows ShadySpringDentalCare.com to improve its compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2) and Americans With Disability Act (ADA), ensuring our site meets high standards for accessibility. These features are specifically designed to support people with visual, auditory, and mobility-related disabilities.

How to Enable the Accessibility Menu

To activate the accessibility menu on ShadySpringDentalCare.com click the accessibility icon located in the corner of the page. After clicking, please allow a moment for the accessibility menu to load completely. This menu allows you to customize how you interact with our website for improved usability.

Disclaimer on Accessibility

ShadySpringDentalCare.com continues its efforts to improve the accessibility of its site and services. This is done in the belief that it is our collective moral obligation to allow seamless, accessible, and unhindered use also for those of us with disabilities.

Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on ShadySpringDentalCare.com fully accessible, some content may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards. This may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.
