Meet Our Doctors

Eric Hogan BS, DDS

Dr. Eric Hogan graduated with high honors from Montana State University in Bozeman with a degree in Microbiology in 1999. He attended University of Minnesota Dental School where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree, graduating with high honors in 2003. In MN, Dr. Eric practiced in a large group practice with six other doctors and was fortunate to be mentored by a very diverse group of professionals.

In the spring of 2006, Dr. Eric returned to Miles City to continue the Hogan Legacy. He and Dr. Robin Hogan purchased the dental practice from his father, Dr. Richard Hogan. The office was completely remodeled and updated to include digital radiographs and other technology. Continual improvements are made as they become available.

Dr. Eric is passionate about continuing education and cutting-edge dentistry. His repertoire is extensive, including dental implants, temporomandibular joint dysfunction (TMD), laser surgery, and smile design. He is an Associate Fellow in the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and a member of The Academy of General Dentistry, Montana Dental Association, American Dental Association, and the Spear Study Club.

Dr. Eric treasures time building things with his children. He enjoys remodeling homes, restoring vintage Fords, and spending time outdoors. He is grateful to provide patients state-of-the-art dental care in his hometown.

Robin Hogan RDH, DDS

Dr. Robin grew up in San Francisco, CA, where she began her career in dentistry as a registered dental assistant, then a registered dental hygienist, and gained extensive experience with office management. During this time, she worked in a variety of dental practices, including endodontic and periodontal specialty clinics.

Dr. Robin attended UC Berkeley where she prepared for dental school and graduated with honors from University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2006 with her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Dr. Robin attended Leadership Miles City in 2011-2012 and gained an increased awareness of how to serve our local community. Dr. Robin enjoys all facets of general dentistry, especially treating children.

She is actively involved with office administration, children’s outreach projects, educational events in local schools, and dental clinics for children. She is a member of the Montana Dental Association, American Dental Association, and the Spear Study Club.

Drs. Robin and Eric Hogan met in dental school and have five children (four boys and one girl). She enjoys bike riding and traveling, and cherishes time spent with her children. She has served on the leadership team for MOPS (Mothers of Preschoolers) International and volunteers at Jefferson Elementary School and Sacred Heart School.
