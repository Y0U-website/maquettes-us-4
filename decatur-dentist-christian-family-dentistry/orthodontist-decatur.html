What are some Orthodontic treatments that we offer?

There are several orthodontic procedures that can help correct various dental issues, such as:

  • Six-Month Smile : Six-month smile is a quick orthodontic treatment that uses clear trays to align your front teeth, giving you a straighter smile in just six months!
  • Braces : Metal or clear brackets are bonded to teeth and connected with wires to apply pressure and shift teeth into the correct position.
  • Invisalign : Clear, removable aligners are custom-made to gradually shift teeth into the desired position.
  • Retainers : Once braces or Invisalign treatment is complete, retainers are used to maintain the new alignment of the teeth.
