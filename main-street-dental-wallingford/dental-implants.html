The Dental Implant Process

At Main Street Dental, we specialize in the restorative phase of dental implant treatment, ensuring your new smile is both functional and beautiful. Here’s how the process works:

Consultation & Treatment Planning

During your initial consultation, we’ll assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if dental implants are the right solution for you. If you’re a candidate, we’ll refer you to a trusted oral surgeon or periodontist for the surgical placement of the implant post.

Healing & Osseointegration

After the implant post is placed, your jawbone will need time to heal and fuse with the post in a process called osseointegration. This typically takes a few months and is essential for providing a stable foundation for your restoration.

Custom Restoration

Once your implant has fully healed, you’ll return to Main Street Dental for the restorative phase. We’ll take detailed impressions and work with a trusted lab to create a custom crown, bridge, or denture that perfectly matches your natural teeth.

Final Placement & Follow-Up

Your restoration is securely attached to the implant post, completing your new smile. We’ll provide guidance on maintaining your implant and schedule follow-up visits to ensure long-term success.
