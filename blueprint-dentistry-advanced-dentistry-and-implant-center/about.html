Meet our doctor

Dr. Kevin Kross

Dr. Kevin Kross brings over 16 years of experience in dentistry and a lifelong dedication to care that changes lives. With a degree in Biomedical Science from Western Michigan University and a Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, Dr. Kross has invested extensively in his education. This commitment took him to The Dawson Academy, where he completed a transformative two-year post-graduate program focused on comprehensive treatment planning and advanced techniques in occlusion and TMJ function.

As a respected faculty member at The Dawson Academy, Dr. Kross occasionally lectures to share his expertise with other dental professionals. He is also a founding member of the Advanced Dental Implant Study Club and is affiliated with the Academy of Osseointegration, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and the West Michigan District Dental Society.

Dr. Kross prides himself on creating life-changing results for his patients. From implant dentistry to smile makeovers and veneers, he thrives on seeing the impact his work has on his patients’ lives. Beyond his clinical skills, Dr. Kross places a strong emphasis on patient-focused care, helping patients make the best decisions for their unique needs without feeling pressured.

Grounded by his upbringing in a blue-collar family, Dr. Kross has developed a sophisticated yet approachable style of care. Outside the office, he&#x27;s a passionate golfer, avid cook, and traveler. As a father of four, he appreciates the value of both precision and personalization, bringing a balance of technical expertise and genuine connection to every patient interaction.

Meet our doctor

Dr. Kaylin Wolter

Kaylin Wolter, DMD has been a member of the Blueprint Dentistry team since 2024. As a West Michigan native, she’s excited to serve the surrounding lakeshore community. To be more involved in the area, Dr. Wolter reserves time to see dental patients in need from the Holland Free Health Clinic.

Before starting her career, Dr. Wolter was introduced to Dr. Kross and his wife, Dr. Mailloux, both of whom inspired her initial interest in the dental field. She brings a young, fresh perspective to Blueprint being able to quickly connect with patients while keeping the environment vibrant and professional.

Dr. Wolter completed her undergraduate studies at Grand Valley State University before moving to Kentucky to attend the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. She spends her free time coaching and competing in CrossFit. She also enjoys any type of outdoor activity like biking, swimming and running.

Your comfort, our priority

Advanced amenities and cutting-edge technology for better care

We believe that the comforts and tools we use should complement and elevate the care you receive. From a refreshingly calm and modern office to state-of-the-art dental technology, every detail is designed to make your visit enjoyable, efficient, and effective.

CBCT Imaging Our advanced CBCT (cone beam computed tomography) imaging allows us to capture incredibly detailed 3D images of your teeth and jaw. This ensures precise diagnostics and treatment planning, especially for services like implants and complex rehabilitative cases.

In-house lab To streamline procedures and provide the highest levels of quality, our in-house lab ensures customized restorations that meet our exacting standards. Faster turnaround times and superior results mean your smile transformation happens on your schedule.

Digital scanning No goop. No gagging. No messy impressions. With digital scanning, we can create a highly accurate 3D model of your smile in minutes using a small handheld scanner. This gives us a cleaner, faster, and more comfortable way to plan treatments like crowns, veneers, aligners, nightguards, and more.

Guided implant surgery Beautiful implant results start with precise planning. Guided surgery allows us to digitally map your treatment before your procedure, then use a custom surgical guide to place your implant with exceptional accuracy. That means better positioning, a more streamlined experience, and restorations that are designed to look natural, not bulky, awkward, or “implant-looking.”

Smile imaging Curious what a new smile could do for you? With smile imaging technology like SmileCloud or BiteBot, we can help you preview your potential results before treatment begins. Whether you’re considering veneers, whitening, aligners, or a full smile makeover, this technology gives you a clearer vision of what’s possible and helps us design a smile that fits your face, features, and goals.
