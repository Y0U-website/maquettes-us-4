FAQs Twin City Smiles

0 FAQs What can I expect during my fist visit? When you arrive at Twin City Smiles, Alexa and Lashay will greet you with a warm welcome at the front desk. You can relax in our cozy waiting area while filling out a few short forms so we can learn about your medical and dental history. This helps us tailor your care and provide the best treatment options for you. With an average wait time of under 5 minutes, you’ll be called back quickly for your cleaning, exam, and x-rays. Afterward, the doctor will review any findings with you and discuss next steps for your smile.

How much will my visit cost?

Your dentist will review your concerns and provide a treatment plan with options. Then, our financial coordinator will explain costs, insurance, and financing so you can choose what works best for you.

When do you start seeing children?

At Twin City Smiles, we love caring for little smiles! We recommend bringing your child in by age two. These early visits help us make sure their teeth are developing properly and give parents tips for keeping their child’s smile healthy. From toddlers to teens, our team makes dental visits fun, comfortable, and stress-free.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Toothache or broken tooth? We’ve got you covered! Twin City Smiles offers same-day emergency care—and because dental emergencies never happen at the “right” time, we’re also available after hours when you need us most.
