Lost Permanent Tooth

Losing a permanent tooth can affect your smile’s full function and comfort. If you have just knocked out a tooth, try to recover and clean the tooth. You may attempt to place the tooth back in its socket or transport it to us in a cup of saliva. If we cannot reattach the tooth, we will recommend a dental implant or other restoration.

Damaged Tooth

A deep crack or fracture in one or more teeth can expose the tooth’s root to decay and increase sensitivity and pain. To prevent further damage, a restoration to cover a damaged tooth is important. If you have just damaged a tooth, rinse your mouth with water and apply pressure to the tooth with a clean cloth. Visit our office, and we will provide you with a restoration.

Lost or Broken Restoration

Contact our office if you damage or lose a dental crown , bridge, or denture. We provide prompt, professional denture repair service with our in-house lab so you are out the door and back to your busy schedule as quickly as possible. You don’t have to face the embarrassment of broken dentures, crowns, or bridges. We can help you regain your smile and confidence on the same day.

Tooth Pain

There are many common causes of tooth pain, from tooth damage to cavities. Dental infections and cavities are the most common causes of toothaches and tooth pain. If patients experience severe tooth pain, we can provide emergency root canals. A root canal removes infected nerves, vessels, and cells within the tooth.

We will perform a dental extraction if severe decay is present and the tooth cannot be saved. Our office ensures that patients feel comfortable during extractions, providing local anesthetic and dental sedation.

Are Dental Emergencies Preventable?

Emergencies can be preventable with the right care. For instance, wearing a custom-made mouthguard for contact sports can prevent tooth damage or loss. Casing these bad habits can prevent tooth pain and damage if you chew your nails or chew on ice. Vising the dentist regularly can also potentially catch dental problems early on to prevent future complications. We can also provide tips to keep your teeth healthy at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is classed as a dental emergency? A dental emergency is any situation that requires immediate attention to save a tooth, alleviate severe pain, or stop ongoing tissue bleeding. This can range from a knocked-out tooth, fractured or chipped tooth, to severe toothaches or dental abscesses. Other emergencies include lost crowns or fillings, uncontrolled bleeding after dental trauma, or swelling around the mouth, face, or neck.

If you’re in Port Charlotte and you experience any of these conditions, it’s crucial to seek immediate help from an emergency dentist like Dr. Ariosto Rosado at Sun Smile Dentistry.

Is gum swelling a dental emergency? While mild gum swelling may not necessarily constitute a dental emergency, it is a sign that should not be ignored. It could indicate the presence of gum disease, an infection, or an abscess. If the gum swelling is accompanied by severe pain, bleeding, or it results in difficulty breathing or swallowing, it then becomes a dental emergency. In such cases, it’s vital to seek immediate attention from a qualified professional.

What can I do if tooth pain is unbearable? Unbearable tooth pain is a clear sign of a dental emergency. It could be due to an abscess, a severe infection, a cracked tooth, or advanced decay. The first step is to call an emergency dentist like Dr. Ariosto Rosado at Sun Smile Dentistry for immediate help.

How do you get rid of tooth pain ASAP? If you’re experiencing tooth pain, the fastest way to get rid of it is by seeing an emergency dentist. They can diagnose the cause of the pain and provide appropriate treatment. However, if you’re unable to see a dentist immediately, there are a few things you can do to alleviate the pain temporarily.

This includes rinsing your mouth with warm salt water, taking over-the-counter pain relievers, and applying a cold compress to the affected area. However, these are only temporary solutions and it’s important to seek professional help as soon as possible. If you’re in Port Charlotte, Dr. Ariosto Rosado at Sun Smile Dentistry can provide fast and effective relief from your tooth pain.
