Dr. Kale Langley was born and raised in south-central Kansas. He earned his undergraduate degree in Human Biology from the University of Kansas and his dental degree from Creighton University School of Dentistry. After dental school, he completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at UCLA.

This residency focused on treating patients with complex dental needs, including implant placement and restoration, aesthetic dentistry, and full-mouth rehabilitation.

Dr. Langley is an active member of the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, and Minneapolis District Dental Society. He also participates in a local Spear Study Club. In addition, he serves on the Creighton Alumni of the Twin Cities Board and the Creighton Dental Alumni Advisory Board.

In 2013, Dr. Langley moved to Minneapolis with his wife, Brooke, to be closer to their families. Their daughters, Ronja and Ravelle, along with their dog, Jovi, keep them busy at home.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Langley enjoys hiking, golfing, running, and biking. He will cheer for the Twins as long as they are not playing the Kansas City Royals.
