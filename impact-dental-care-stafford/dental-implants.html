Types of Dental Implants: Single Tooth Restoration

Single-tooth implants represent the gold standard in tooth replacement technology. Each custom implant consists of a titanium post that acts as an artificial root, topped with a precisely crafted crown that matches your natural teeth. We offer same day placement options for eligible patients, allowing you to leave our office with a temporary crown while your permanent restoration is crafted to perfection.

Are Implants Safe?

Dental implants stand as one of the most thoroughly researched and reliable procedures in modern dentistry. With a remarkable 98 % success rate and decades of clinical research supporting their use, implants have transformed millions of lives. The titanium posts are completely biocompatible, and the FDA has extensively verified their safety for long-term use. When placed by our experienced dental team, implants carry minimal risk and provide predictable, lasting results.

Are Implants Worth It?

While bridges or dentures might seem more affordable initially, dental implants offer superior long-term value. Think of implants as a permanent investment in your health and confidence. Unlike temporary solutions that require replacement every few years, implants can last a lifetime with proper care. They prevent the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth, maintain your facial structure, and allow you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence. The boost to self-esteem and quality of life makes implants a worthwhile investment for most patients.

Dental Implant Process

Your journey to a restored smile follows a carefully planned sequence.

Initial Consultation During your initial consultation, we’ll perform a comprehensive examination using advanced 3D imaging to evaluate your unique needs. This allows us to create a precise treatment plan tailored to your specific situation.

Pre-Treatment Phase If needed, we’ll address any preliminary work such as extractions or bone grafting. The better prepared your mouth is, the smoother the dental implant process will go!

Implant Placement When you’re ready for implant placement, our gentle surgical techniques and modern anesthesia options ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. For qualifying patients, we offer same-day temporary crowns, so you never have to go without teeth.

Final Restoration The final phase involves placing your custom-crafted permanent crown, carefully matched to your natural teeth and adjusted for perfect bite alignment.

Dental Implant FAQ

How long do implants last? With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last a lifetime.

Is the procedure painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return to normal activities quickly. Any post-procedure sensation is typically managed well with standard pain medication.

Can anyone get implants? Most healthy adults qualify for implants. We carefully evaluate each case considering factors like bone density, oral health, and medical history to ensure the best possible outcome.
