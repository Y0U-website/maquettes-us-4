How the Dental Savings Plan Works

Choice One states that active members can begin using the plan without a waiting period and that the plan does not place a cap on eligible savings. Members may also add dependents, with the adult dependent rate shown separately from the primary-member rate.

  • Choose a membership. Review the available adult or child option and its current benefits.

  • Receive applicable benefits. Included services and member discounts apply according to the current plan terms.

Is a Savings Plan the Same as Dental Insurance?

No. Choice One is a licensed discount savings plan offered through participating providers. It does not pay claims to Steger Smiles or reimburse members for dental treatment. Members remain responsible for paying the cost of care after any applicable plan discount.

The plan is not a qualified health plan under the Affordable Care Act. It should not be described or used as dental insurance. If you already have dental coverage, contact our team before enrolling so you can understand how the available payment options may apply to your care.

Who May Want to Consider the Plan?

The Choice One Savings Plan may be useful for individuals or families who do not have traditional dental insurance and expect to receive routine preventive care. Your individual needs, anticipated treatment, and total costs determine whether the plan offers value for you.

Before enrolling, review the current Savings Summary, participating-provider information, limitations, renewal terms, cancellation terms, and fees on the official website.
