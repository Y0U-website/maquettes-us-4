Your next exam and cleaning should be scheduled according to your needs. Gum condition, cavity history, dry mouth, tobacco use, medical factors, and previous treatment can all influence the interval. The schedule may change as your risk changes.

Continue brushing twice daily and cleaning between your teeth. Contact the office before your next planned visit if you develop swelling, significant pain, trauma, or another new concern.

A preventive appointment can provide a baseline for future comparisons. Notes, gum measurements, and any clinically indicated images help the dentist determine whether an area is stable or changing. This is especially useful when a tooth or restoration does not need treatment now but should be checked again at a later visit.

If you wear a removable dental appliance, bring it to the appointment. The team can look for wear, buildup, or changes in fit that may deserve attention.
