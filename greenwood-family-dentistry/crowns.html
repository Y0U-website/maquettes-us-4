When is Dental Bridge Considered?

Dental bridges are used to fill the gap when one or more teeth are missing. Crowns are used with bridges on the teeth next to the gap. It helps to hold a false tooth in place. As a result, it keeps your bite aligned and stops nearby teeth from shifting out of place.

Pros and Cons of Dental Bridges

The pros and cons of dental bridges are outlined below.

Pros

  • Fills missing gaps
  • Restores normal chewing
  • Prevents neighbouring teeth from shifting

Cons

  • Needs healthy neighboring teeth
  • Doesn’t stop bone loss
  • May need future replacement

When Implant Crowns are Required?

An implant crown is a tooth-shaped cap — the visible part of a dental implant — that replaces a missing tooth from the root up. The dentist surgically places a titanium post directly into the jawbone, then attaches a custom crown on top once the bone heals around it. Dentists typically craft implant crowns from zirconia or porcelain, giving them the look, feel, and function of a natural tooth.

Pros and Cons of Implant Crowns

Let’s have a look at the pros and cons of implant crowns below.

Pros

  • Feels like real teeth
  • Preserves jawbone density
  • Lasts many years

Cons

  • Requires a surgical procedure
  • Higher upfront cost
  • Takes time to heal (Usually 4-9 months)

Your Treatment Options

Honestly, there is no single answer to the question of whether crowns, bridges, and implant crowns are better. However, you can consider the following factors before making a decision.

Condition of the Teeth and Oral Health

If most of your teeth are still healthy, then a crown is a good choice. While people with missing teeth must go for an implant crown when gums are healthy and have enough jawbone. Bridges are a good option when your mouth needs treatment first.

Consider Your Budget

Crowns and bridges are cheaper upfront than implants. However, implants can save money in the longer term since they last decades.

Emergency Treatment Options

If you have a tooth emergency such as pain, structural damage, bleeding or detachment of tooth or previous implant, then you should consult a dentist immediately. An emergency dentist NJ like Greenwood Dentistry can analyze your situation and suggest the right fix that can involve crown, bridge and implant crown restoration or extraction can also be required.

Waiting on any of these tends to make the eventual fix more involved, not less. A cracked tooth left uncrowned can fracture further and become unsalvageable. A gap left unaddressed lets surrounding bone resorb, which can rule out a straightforward implant later and push you toward a bone graft instead.
