Your Treatment Options

How aggressive the treatment needs to be depends almost entirely on how far the disease has progressed by the time it’s caught:

Early-Stage Gingivitis

Caught before pockets form, gingivitis is fully reversible with a thorough cleaning and better home care — no root planing required yet.

Scaling for Mild Periodontitis

With pockets around 4 to 5 millimeters, scaling clears out the bacteria and tartar driving the inflammation, typically over one or two visits.

Our Scaling & Root Planing Treatment Process

Periodontal Exam / Scaling / Root Planing & Follow-Up

Periodontal Exam Scaling Root Planing & Follow-Up Periodontal Exam Your visit begins with a comprehensive periodontal evaluation. We will examine your gums, measure the depth of any periodontal pockets, and review digital X-rays if needed to assess the health of the bone supporting your teeth. This will helps determine the stage of gum disease and allows us to create a treatment plan based on your oral health needs.

During the Procedure

Before treatment begins, a local anesthetic is used to numb the treatment area for a more comfortable experience. Using specialized dental instruments, including ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments, plaque, tartar, and harmful bacteria are carefully removed from below the gumline.

Root Planing

The exposed root surfaces are then smoothed to reduce areas where bacteria can collect and to encourage the gums to heal against the teeth. Depending on your condition, treatment may be completed one section, or quadrant, at a time.

Follow-Up

It’s normal to experience mild tenderness or temporary tooth sensitivity for a few days after treatment. Most patients return to their regular activities the same day. Brushing, flossing, and following the home care instructions provided by your dentist are important for the healing process and reducing the chance of future gum infections.
