Dr. H. Wayne Chesser

Meet Dr. H. Wayne Chesser, a Yuma, AZ native whose journey to dentistry is as unique as his approach to patient care. Initially working as a government contractor at the Army Test Center, his career took a significant turn when he decided to pursue his growing interest in healthcare. After earning an associate’s degree in business, Dr. Chesser was inspired to dive deeper into the sciences. Thanks to a grant from the Science Foundation, he transitioned into a Biology program and later, the Colorado School of Dental Medicine, where he trained to become the dentist he is today.
