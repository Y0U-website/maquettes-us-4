Pediatric Dental X-Rays: Essential for Complete Oral Health

Dental X-rays are key to maintaining your child’s oral health because they help dentists spot problems that aren’t visible during regular check-ups. Here’s why your child might need dental X-rays:

How often your child needs X-rays depends on their specific needs, oral health history, and risk for dental problems. Children typically need X-rays more often than adults because their mouths grow and change quickly, and they get cavities more easily. Our dentists carefully check each child to decide when dental imaging is needed.

  • Finding cavities between teeth early before they cause pain
  • Tracking tooth development patterns
  • Spotting impacted teeth or growth issues
  • Checking injuries to teeth and supporting structures
  • Planning orthodontic treatment
  • Checking the position of teeth that haven’t erupted yet
  • Finding infections at the tooth root
