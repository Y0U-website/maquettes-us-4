Pediatric Dental Cleanings

Our gentle dental cleanings create the foundation for good oral health in children. Using kid-friendly tools, our hygienists remove plaque and tartar while making each visit fun and educational. These regular cleanings prevent cavities and gum disease while allowing us to monitor your child’s developing teeth and address concerns early.

Pediatric Preventative Dental Care

Prevention stands at the core of our approach at Wilmington Pediatric Dentistry. We provide fluoride treatments to strengthen enamel, dental sealants to protect chewing surfaces, and personalized hygiene instruction based on your child’s age and needs. Our goal is to build habits that support your child’s oral health throughout life while reducing the need for more complex treatments.

Pediatric Dental X-Rays

We use low-radiation digital x-ray technology specifically designed for children to safely examine beneath the surface of teeth and gums. These tools help us detect hidden issues like cavities between teeth, impacted teeth, and developmental concerns. We follow strict safety protocols, using x-rays only when necessary for proper diagnosis and treatment planning.
