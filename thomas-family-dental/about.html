Get to Know

Dr. Letrisha Thomas

Dr. Thomas is known for her thoughtful, whole-patient approach. She blends modern technology with a calm, educational atmosphere, taking time to truly understand each patient’s needs and goals. Her care philosophy centers on meeting families where they are—with clarity, empathy, and treatments designed to support lifelong health. Outside the office, Dr. Thomas stays busy with her husband, four kids, and their family pets. She’s often on the sidelines at local sports games or volunteering with community organizations that serve families in need.

Your First Visit

Compassionate Care and Clear Communication

From your very first visit at Thomas Family Dental, you’ll feel welcomed, informed, and cared for. Our team takes the time to understand your health goals and concerns, especially when it comes to unique needs like airway development or frenectomy evaluations. We’re here to offer answers—not judgment—so you can take the next step toward better oral health with complete confidence.

The Experience Our first visits typically begin with a warm welcome and a one-on-one conversation with your dental team. We’ll review your health history, take any necessary digital images or impressions, and develop a personalized treatment plan. You’ll leave feeling supported and clearly informed about any recommended next steps.

What to Bring We ask that all required information be submitted 24–48 hours before the appointment so we have time to verify dental benefits (if applicable) and update your chart.

Your dental insurance card (if applicable)

Completed new patient forms (if available beforehand)

Any questions or concerns you’d like to discuss

Modern Tools, Cozy Comforts

LightScalpel CO2 Laser

Ideal for performing precise, gentle frenectomies with minimal discomfort and faster healing, the LightScalpel CO2 laser allows for smoother treatment outcomes, especially for infants and young children.

Primescan Digital Impressions

Say goodbye to messy impressions. With Primescan, we capture accurate, high-resolution digital scans in seconds, ensuring a more comfortable experience and highly precise restorations.

Red Light Therapy

Used as a non-invasive healing aid, Red Light Therapy promotes faster tissue recovery and reduces inflammation—supporting wellness before and after a variety of treatments.

Coffee & Tea Station

We want you to feel at home from the moment you walk in. Help yourself to a warm cup of coffee or tea from our beverage station while you wait—we’re all about those little comforts.

Kids&#x27; Play Area

Our small play space gives little ones a chance to relax, explore, and stay entertained before their appointment. It’s just one of the ways we make visits easier for families.

Educational Monitors in Treatment Rooms

Each of our treatment rooms is equipped with monitors so we can walk you through X-rays, digital scans, and treatment plans together—helping you feel informed and empowered every step of the way.

Stress Balls & Noise-Reducing Headphones

Feeling anxious? We’ve got you. We offer soft stress balls and comfortable noise-reducing headphones that can connect to your phone or device so you can listen to your favorite music or podcast during treatment.

weighted blankets

We offer soft, weighted blankets to help ease anxiety and promote relaxation during your appointment. It’s one of the many ways we make your comfort a priority every step of the way.

Invested in Our Community

Through this commitment, we aim to help restore comfort, confidence, and quality of life while contributing to a more caring and supported community.
