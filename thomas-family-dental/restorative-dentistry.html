Restorative Dentistry

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns restore damaged teeth by covering and protecting what remains of your natural tooth. When you have a cracked or broken tooth, these custom caps not only protect the remaining tooth structure but also improve how your teeth work together when you bite. If you’ve had a root canal, your dentist will typically recommend a crown to strengthen the treated tooth. With several material options available, crowns support both your dental health and appearance—giving you back a smile you can feel good about.

Extractions

Extractions are always a last resort because preserving your smile is our top priority. However, sometimes a problematic tooth causes more harm than good. In these cases, Dr. Thomas may recommend an extraction to restore your oral health. Using gentle techniques and local anesthesia, decayed or damaged teeth can be safely removed to relieve discomfort or make space for dental implants, a bridge, or another type of tooth replacement to relieve discomfort.

Dental Implant Restorations

We proudly offer dental implant restorations to help patients regain a complete, confident smile. If you&#x27;ve had a dental implant placed by a specialist, we can restore it with a custom-made crown that looks and functions just like your natural tooth. We take special care to ensure the color, shape, and fit blend seamlessly with your existing teeth. Implant restorations are a durable, long-lasting solution for missing teeth—and we’re here to make sure your final result feels just right.

Dental Bridges

If a missing tooth is leaving a space in your smile, a dental bridge may be the perfect solution. The space left behind by one or several missing teeth can be permanently filled, restoring both function and appearance. Attached securely to the teeth on either side of the space, your bridge stays in place when you eat or speak and is color-matched to blend seamlessly with your natural smile. Schedule a consultation today to see how a dental bridge can help you feel confident again.
