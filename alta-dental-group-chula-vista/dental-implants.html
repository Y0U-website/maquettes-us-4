Give Us a Call What Are Dental Implants? Dental implants are the gold standard for tooth replacement in Irvine, offering a permanent solution that closely mimics the structure and function of natural teeth. Unlike bridges or dentures that rest on top of the gums, dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, creating an artificial tooth root that fuses with your natural bone through a process called osseointegration. This solid foundation supports a custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

At Alta Dental Group in Irvine, our comprehensive approach to dental implants combines advanced surgical techniques with artistic restoration design to create results that are both functional and aesthetically superior. Our multidisciplinary team—including an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, a board-certified periodontist, and a dental anesthesiologist—works together under one roof to provide seamless, coordinated cosmetic dental services throughout your implant journey.

From replacing a single missing tooth to full-mouth rehabilitation, dental implants offer unparalleled stability, longevity, and natural appearance. They preserve jawbone structure, maintain facial contours, and allow you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence—benefits that make them the preferred choice for Irvine patients seeking long-term tooth replacement solutions.

The procedure preserves bone structure in the jaw while eliminating the need to reduce neighboring teeth, as would be required with traditional bridges. Benefits of Dental Implants

Unmatched Stability and Function Dental implants provide a level of stability that no other tooth replacement option can match. By anchoring directly to your jawbone just like natural tooth roots, implants allow for normal biting and chewing forces without movement or discomfort. This solid foundation means you can eat virtually any food with confidence, speak clearly without worry, and smile naturally in any situation.

Bone Preservation and Facial Contours Unlike removable dentures or bridges, dental implants actively stimulate and preserve jawbone through regular chewing forces. This stimulation prevents the bone resorption that typically occurs after tooth loss, maintaining your natural facial contours and preventing the premature aging appearance associated with long-term tooth loss and bone deterioration.

Longevity and Value With proper care, dental implants often last a lifetime, making them an excellent long-term investment compared to other tooth replacement options that typically require replacement every 5-10 years. This exceptional durability, combined with their ability to preserve oral health and prevent complications associated with tooth loss, makes implants the most cost-effective solution over time.

Natural Appearance and Confidence Modern dental implant restorations are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth in both appearance and function. The custom-designed crowns, bridges, or dentures supported by implants are crafted using advanced digital technology to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and alignment, creating a seamless smile that looks completely natural and enhances your confidence in every interaction.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Irvine

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium implant post typically integrates permanently with your jawbone, while the crown or restoration may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after implant placement. We offer various sedation options at our Irvine office to ensure your complete comfort, and prescribed medications manage any post-operative discomfort effectively.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Traditional implant treatment typically takes 3-6 months from placement to final restoration, allowing time for osseointegration. However, many Irvine patients qualify for our same-day teeth protocols, receiving provisional restorations immediately after implant placement.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone are candidates for dental implants. During your consultation at our Irvine office, we evaluate your specific situation and discuss any preparatory treatments that might be beneficial.
