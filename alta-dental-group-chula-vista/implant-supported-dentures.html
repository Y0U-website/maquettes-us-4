Implant Supported Dentures in…

  • Bone Preservation and Facial Structure Unlike traditional dentures that accelerate bone loss, implant supported options actively preserve jawbone through stimulation similar to natural tooth roots. This preservation maintains facial contours and prevents the aged, collapsed appearance often associated with long-term conventional denture use.
  • Improved Comfort and Confidence By eliminating palatal coverage (in upper dentures) and preventing pressure points on sensitive gum tissue, implant supported dentures provide superior comfort during daily wear. The security of knowing your teeth won’t move or dislodge during social interactions restores confidence and eliminates anxiety about embarrassing denture moments.
  • Long-term Value and Oral Health Though initially more expensive than conventional dentures, implant supported options provide superior long-term value through greater durability, reduced bone loss, and elimination of ongoing costs for adhesives and frequent relines. The preservation of jawbone and surrounding structures also contributes to better overall oral health for years to come.
