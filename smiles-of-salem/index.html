General Dentistry is an area of dental care that focuses on the overall health and well-being of your teeth and gums. It focuses on preventative treatments such as fluoride treatments to strengthen the enamel, and cavity fillings.

Dental Implants are a type of dental restoration that can be used to replace missing teeth. A dental implant is a small titanium screw that is placed into the jawbone to serve as a replacement for the root of a missing tooth. Once the implant has healed, an artificial tooth (crown) is then attached to it.

Periodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on preventing and treating the structures that support and surround teeth, including gums and other tissue in the mouth. This field helps protect, maintain, and improve the health of your teeth by diagnosing and treating any diseases related to periodontal areas.
