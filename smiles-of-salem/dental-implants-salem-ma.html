The Dental Implant Process at Smiles of Salem

Many patients who visit our dentist in Salem are curious about what to expect with dental implants. Here’s a simple overview of the process:

Consultation & Planning At your first appointment, our dentist will perform an exam, take digital X-rays, and determine if dental implants in Salem are right for you. We’ll discuss your goals and create a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement The titanium post is placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia, ensuring a comfortable experience.

Caring for Your Dental Implants in Salem

Dental implants are designed to last, but they still require good oral care. At Smiles of Salem, we provide all our patients with instructions to keep their implants healthy for years to come.

Brush and Floss Daily Just like natural teeth, implants should be brushed twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste.

Schedule Regular Visits with Your Dentist in Salem Routine checkups allow us to monitor your implants and keep your gums healthy.

Avoid Hard Foods Chewing on ice or hard candy can damage your implants or restorations.

Stop Smoking Smoking interferes with healing and increases the risk of implant failure.

Protect Your Smile Use a nightguard if you grind your teeth and avoid using your teeth as tools.

With these simple steps, dental implants in Salem can serve you well for decades.
