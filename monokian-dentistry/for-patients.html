Welcome to Monokian Dentistry! Our Marlton and Haddonfield, NJ, dental offices are places where patients can feel relaxed and comfortable receiving the dental care they need. We understand and respect our patient’s time and will never rush care for any reason. It is also important for us to ensure that patients can request care at times most convenient to their own schedules. We can work with patients to create a routine cleaning schedule customized to their dental needs.

If you are a new patient, feel free to review this page for information about your first dental visit, safety and health precautions we take daily in our offices, and resources for both locations.

We have created a comfortable office environment for our patients in both locations, offering TVs in all operating rooms so patients can relax and watch TV during their dental care.
