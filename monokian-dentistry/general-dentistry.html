General Dentistry Services

Our general dental treatments help patients maintain beautiful, healthy smiles. Learn more about each of our general dental treatments with links below:

Custom Mouthguard

If you or your child play contact sports like hockey, lacrosse, or basketball, you need to wear a sports mouthguard. We work with a local dental lab to create custom sports mouthguards that protect natural teeth and braces from damage.

Emergency Dentistry

We provide emergency dental treatment for patients who require immediate dental care. Common dental emergencies that we can treat include broken dental restorations, lost permanent teeth, and tooth pain.

Family Dentistry

Our dental offices provide dental care for the whole family. It s important to ensure that children receive dental care at a young age to help them maintain good oral health over their lifetime. Family dental care can address oral health problems at every stage of life.

Root Canal

A root canal is a dental procedure that removes the infected dental pulp from within the tooth. The dental pulp consists of the nerves, blood vessels, and cells that keep teeth alive. If an infection reaches the root canal and dental pulp, we need to remove the inflamed pulp and clean the root canal. A root canal ensures that patients retain their permanent teeth.

Tooth Extraction

We recommend tooth extractions if patients have severely damaged, infected, or impacted teeth. Our team ensures that patients feel as comfortable as possible during extraction treatment with local anesthetic and sedation. If we extract permanent diseased or damaged teeth, we can provide dental implant treatments and restorations that look like natural teeth.

Q&A: General Dentistry Services

Do you have questions about general dental treatments? Read answers to these commonly asked questions:

Is a root canal painful?

We make sure that patients feel as comfortable as possible during their root canal treatment. Our dentists use a local anesthetic to numb the tooth and ensure patients do not feel any pain. We will also recommend ibuprofen or another pain medication to ensure that patients feel minimal pain or discomfort as they heal.

How do I know if I have a dental emergency?

Dental emergencies are sudden, severe oral health issues. If you have severe pain, tooth damage, or tooth loss, you require professional dental care to prevent further damage to your smile. Dental trauma or toothaches are not only uncomfortable but can create increased problems if patients do not receive immediate care.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend that patients visit the dentist twice a year or biannually. If patients have chronic dental problems, we will recommend a more frequent schedule. For instance, we can provide regular periodontal cleanings for patients with recurring gum disease.
