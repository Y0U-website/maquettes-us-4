The practice

Our Hanford Family Dental Center team follows the golden rule of treating our nearby dental patients how we would want to be treated. We want you to have an enjoyable and positive experience at each of your visits, which is why we’re always professional, courteous, and are sure to respect your time. Many of our long-term patients choose us because of our friendly and knowledgeable team!

Over the years, we’ve received so many word-of-mouth referrals that we had to expand our office! Today, our modern, state-of-the-art facilities feature the latest comfort amenities and dental technology, including a digital impression system and TVs in each of our operatories. We take pride in creating a clean and professional environment that feels comfortable.

Our team takes pride in offering high-quality dentistry that’s accessible. We’re happy to be an in-network provider with many major PPO dental insurance plans, meaning we offer discounted rates and fees that help you save from steep out-of-pocket costs. We also offer third-party financing at little-to-no interest upon approval.
