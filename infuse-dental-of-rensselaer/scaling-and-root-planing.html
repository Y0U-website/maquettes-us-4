Does The Procedure Hurt?

Since the process of scaling and root planing in Rensselaer, IN is slightly more invasive than routine dental cleaning, it may cause pain and discomfort. But the good news is that we perform this procedure under local anesthesia, which numbs your teeth and gums for a few hours. So, during the process, you will not feel pain or sensitivity.

FAQs

Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning procedure used to treat gum disease. Scaling involves removing plaque and tartar from the tooth surfaces and below the gumline, while root planing smooths the root surfaces to help the gums reattach to the teeth and halt the progression of periodontal disease.

You may need this treatment if you have red, swollen, or bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, gum recession, deep periodontal pockets, or loose teeth. During your dental checkup at Infuse Dental, Dr. Elvi Roci will measure your gum pockets and determine if deep cleaning is necessary.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. You may experience some mild soreness or sensitivity in the days following treatment, but this typically resolves on its own. Our team provides aftercare instructions to help manage any temporary discomfort.

Depending on the severity of your gum disease, scaling and root planing may be completed in one or two visits. In more advanced cases, the treatment may be divided by quadrants of the mouth over multiple appointments to ensure thorough cleaning and patient comfort.

You may experience some gum tenderness, tooth sensitivity, and minor bleeding for a few days. It is important to maintain good oral hygiene, use any prescribed antibacterial rinses, and attend follow-up appointments so Dr. Elvi Roci can monitor your gum healing and recovery.
