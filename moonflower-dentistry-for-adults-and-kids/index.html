Implant: A biocompatible titanium screw-like fixture surgically implanted into the jawbone. The implant seamlessly fuses with the bone, thereby establishing a strong and stable foundation through a process known as osseointegration.

Abutment: Following osseointegration, an abutment is affixed to the implant and functions as the connecting piece for the replacement tooth crown, ensuring a secure and seamless fit.

Dental implants serve as titanium artificial tooth roots. They are surgically placed within the jawbone, creating a sturdy foundation for replacement teeth. They serve as a lasting solution for individuals who are missing teeth due to various reasons, such as decay, gum disease, injury, and/or congenital conditions. Multi-tooth implants, also referred to as implant-supported bridges , are an ideal choice when replacing several missing teeth. Instead of relying on natural teeth for support, implant-supported bridges anchor securely to dental implants. These implants are strategically placed within the jawbone, giving enhanced stability and durability.
