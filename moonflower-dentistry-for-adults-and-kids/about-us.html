Patient Resources

Emergencies

A dental emergency typically involves severe pain, bleeding, or trauma to the mouth. If you experience any of these symptoms, it is crucial to seek immediate dental care to prevent further complications and ensure the best possible outcome for your oral health.

If you are experiencing a dental emergency, please call our dental office. We will consult with you on the severity of your emergency and get you scheduled accordingly.

Financing Options

Moonflower Dentistry accepts most major insurances along with credit cards, Apple Pay, checks, and cash. For uninsured patients, we offer our Loyalty Program with a monthly subscription to ensure preventative care for all our patients.

Please call our dental office to verify that your insurance is accepted or to learn more about our Loyalty Program.

Late Policy

A late cancellation is considered a cancellation less than two business days before your scheduled procedure. For late cancellations and no-shows, you will be charged $75 for hygiene appointments and $150 for restorative appointments.

We appreciate your time and dedication to our practice. Our Late Policy exists to ensure that each appointment is given the time and attention necessary to provide you with the care you need.
