  • Removability : Unlike traditional braces, aligners can be removed for eating and drinking, promoting better oral hygiene.
  • Comfort : Made from smooth materials, aligners reduce the likelihood of irritation or discomfort.
  • No Dietary Restrictions : Since aligners can be removed during meals, there are no dietary limitations.
  • Fewer Check-ups : Clear aligner treatments typically require fewer in-person visits, convenient for those with busy schedules.

Disadvantages

  • Compliance : High patient compliance is necessary to achieve desired results. Aligners should be worn for at least 20-22 hours per day.
  • Suitability : Clear aligners may not be suitable for complex orthodontic cases requiring significant tooth movement or severe bite correction.
  • Treatment Duration : While discreet, clear aligners may require a longer treatment period compared to traditional braces for certain cases.
  • Initial Discomfort : Some initial discomfort or pressure may occur as the teeth begin to shift.
  • Cost : Depending on the case complexity, the overall cost of treatment and maintenance can be significant.
