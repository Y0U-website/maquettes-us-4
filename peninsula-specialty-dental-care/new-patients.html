New Patients Description Peninsula Specialty Dental Care

Not sure if we accept your plan? Call us at (650) 654-1854 — we’re happy to verify your benefits before your visit.

Financing Options We want cost to never be a barrier to the care you need. We offer flexible financing options to help you get started. Ask our front desk about available payment plans at your visit.

How long will my first appointment take? Plan for approximately 60–90 minutes for a new patient visit that includes an exam, X-rays, and cleaning. If you have complex concerns or specialty needs, it may run a bit longer — and that’s okay. We’d rather take the time to do it right.

Do I need to bring anything? If you have recent dental X-rays from another office, bring them or ask your previous dentist to forward them — it saves you from duplicate imaging. Also bring your insurance card and a valid photo ID.

What if I have dental anxiety? You’re not alone, and we take this seriously. Let us know when you call or in your registration form, and we’ll make sure your doctor and team are prepared to make your visit as comfortable as possible. We work at your pace.

Can I be seen for a dental emergency? Yes. We do our best to accommodate dental emergencies the same day. Call us at (650) 654-1854 and let us know you need urgent care.

Do you treat children? Yes, we welcome patients of all ages. Dr. Nelia Barkhordar has extensive experience with family and pediatric dentistry.

Where do I park? Parking is available in our office back lot. See our Parking Information page for details.

  • In-network with many major carriers
  • Out-of-network PPO benefits also accepted
  • HMO, Medi-Cal, and Denti-Cal are not accepted
