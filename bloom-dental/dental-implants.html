Dental Implants (Main)

  • Implant-Supported Bridges : A great solution when multiple adjacent teeth are missing—without altering neighboring healthy teeth.
  • Full Mouth Dental Implants : Replace all teeth in the upper and/or lower jaw using multiple implants. This option offers a permanent, non-removable solution.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures : Available in fixed (hybrid dentures) or removable (implant-supported overdentures). Both provide greater comfort and stability compared to traditional dentures.

The Dental Implant Process: What to Expect

We make the dental implant journey as smooth and personalized as possible: Initial Consultation & 3D Imaging – We assess your oral health, bone structure, and discuss your goals.

  • Implant Placement Surgery – The titanium posts are placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation.
  • Healing & Osseointegration (3–6 Months) – Your implants fuse with the bone, creating a strong foundation.
  • Abutment Placement – Connectors are attached to the implants to support your final restoration.
  • Final Restoration – Custom-made crowns, bridges, or dentures are securely placed, completing your new smile.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer several key advantages over traditional tooth replacement options:

  • Prevents Bone Loss – Stimulates jawbone to maintain volume and strength
  • Permanent & Durable – With proper care, implants can last decades
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth – Unlike dental bridges, implants do not require grinding down neighboring teeth
  • Improved Function – Eat, speak, and smile with confidence
  • Natural Look & Feel – Designed to match your existing teeth for seamless aesthetics
