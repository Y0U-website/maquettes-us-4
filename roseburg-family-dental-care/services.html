General & Preventive Services

Our “Preventistry” services focus on preventative care to help our patients avoid cavities, Periodontal Disease, and other preventable oral issues!

Regular Dental Cleanings

  • Performed by our dental hygienists every three, four, or six months
  • The frequency of the cleaning depends on your individual needs
  • Cleaning sessions generally take 45-60 minutes
  • Our hygienists use gentle methods to remove calculus and plaque from your teeth
  • Cleanings are crucial for preventing gum disease and tooth decay

Dental Exams

  • Check for dental issues such as cavities, gum disease, oral sores, and oral cancer
  • Look for other health issues such as nutritional deficiencies, diabetes, and hormonal irregularities
  • Exam includes: digital imaging, photography, jaw joint inspection, and a thorough examination of your mouth and screening of surrounding tissues

Tooth-Colored Fillings

  • Fillings are used to restore teeth affected by decay or breakage
  • We use composite materials that match the color of your natural teeth
  • Composite materials are layered into the tooth
  • An intense visible light is used to cure the composite material
  • The tooth surface is then shaped and polished to ensure the filling blends in seamlessly
  • The tooth-colored filling is virtually undetectable in your smile

Digital X-Rays

  • Digital X-rays use 80 % less radiation than traditional radiography
  • They are quick and can detect developing issues better than a visual exam

Periodontal Care

  • Periodontal Disease (Gum Disease) can lead to tooth loss and jawbone deterioration
  • Signs include: swollen, tender, or inflamed gums
  • Gentle, soft-tissue laser treatment is used to treat Periodontal Disease
  • The treatment produces minimal bleeding and pain

Dental Sealants

  • Applied to the back molars with deeply grooved chewing surfaces
  • They are made of flowable resin
  • Sealants help to seal out food and bacteria
  • They create a smoother surface that is easier to clean by brushing
  • Sealants are suitable for both children and adults
  • The application process is quick and painless
  • Sealants can last for several years with proper care
  • Regular check-ups are important to ensure the sealants are still intact and effective.

Cosmetic & Restorative Services

Our cosmetic and restorative dentistry services are minimally invasive and can help improve the appearance and functionality of your teeth, giving you a beautiful and healthy smile!

Treating the Anxious Patient

  • This option is for patients of all ages who experience anxiety during dental procedures
  • The patient is minimally sedated but stays awake in a state of relaxation

Snoring and Sleep Apnea Care

  • Dr. Braun can prescribe dental appliances to support the treatment of sleep disordered breathing (apnea) when CPAP has failed
  • Patients who struggle with apnea and snoring often also have TMJ dysfunction. Dr. Braun can cater to patients in need of this level of care

Crowns & Bridges

  • A crown is a cap placed over a severely damaged or decayed tooth that cannot be fixed with just filler material
  • A crown restores the strength, function, and appearance of the tooth
  • A dental bridge is used to replace a missing tooth
  • Tooth loss can lead to shifting of surrounding teeth, difficulties chewing and speaking, and decreased confidence in your smile
  • A bridge is permanently cemented in your smile and functions like natural teeth

Dentures & Partials

  • Complete dentures are for patients who have lost all their teeth, while partial dentures are used when some natural teeth are still present
  • Dentures can restore the health, function, and aesthetics of your smile
  • All dentures are custom-made to fit securely and comfortably in your mouth
  • Dentures can help you chew and speak with ease
  • Dentures can prevent facial sagging, giving you a more youthful appearance

Porcelain Veneers

  • Veneers are porcelain laminates custom-made to fit on the front of your existing teeth
  • Veneers can transform the appearance of teeth by concealing misalignments, gaps, chips, and cracks
  • They are resistant to discoloration and can be customized to achieve your desired smile
  • Bonding veneers requires removing a small amount of tooth enamel
  • This treatment is often irreversible

Periodontal Care

  • Periodontal Disease (Gum Disease) can lead to tooth loss and jawbone deterioration
  • Signs include: swollen, tender, or inflamed gums
  • Gentle, soft-tissue laser treatment is used to treat Periodontal Disease
  • The treatment produces minimal bleeding and pain

Teeth Whitening

  • Teeth whitening reverses discoloration caused by the effects of coffee, tea, smoking, and aging
  • This is a non-invasive treatment that whitens the shade of teeth by several shades

Bonding

  • Liquid resin is used to rebuild and reshape the tooth’s shape
  • Often used in cases of chipped, broken, or misaligned teeth
  • Treatment is affordable, can last up to 5 years, and can be done in a single appointment

Tooth Extractions

  • Teeth that are broken or infected beyond repair often require extraction
  • We will make this treatment as comfortable as possible and discuss tooth-replacement options

Root Canal Therapy

  • If an infection has reached the tooth pulp a root canal should be performed
  • The infected pulp is removed to prevent bacteria and decay
  • The tooth is then filled with a medicated material and often restored with a crown

Implant Restorations

  • Permanent tooth replacement option
  • Roseburg Family Dental Care does not perform the surgical portion of an implant
  • We can refer you to a dental surgeon
  • After the surgical procedure, our team can restore the implant with a crown or prosthetic
  • You’ll leave with an implant that looks and functions like a normal tooth
