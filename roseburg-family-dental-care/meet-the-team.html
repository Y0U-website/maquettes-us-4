Meet The Roseburg Dental Care Team

Committed to Caring

At Roseburg Family Dental Care, we believe that promoting healthy smiles goes beyond providing standard dental procedures . That’s why we endeavor to bring compassionate care and education to all our Roseburg, Oregon patients.

Dr. Braun and her entire team of Dentists, Hygienists, and Assistants take time to understand your dental health priorities, all the while determining your needs that will treat disease and help prevent future problems. Dr. Braun’s team believes that a committed partnership between her dental team and the patient is essential for ultimate success in achieving a lifetime of trouble-free dental health.

Whether your visit is for a healthy mouth cleaning, a cavity filling, or teeth whitening service, our team guarantees that you will be treated with the utmost care!
