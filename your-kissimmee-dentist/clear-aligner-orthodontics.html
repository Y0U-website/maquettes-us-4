Clear Aligners in Kissimmee, FL

Clear aligners are generally less painful than traditional braces. Some people may experience temporary discomfort for a few days at the beginning of each new stage of treatment as the teeth begin to move. This discomfort, often described as pressure, indicates that the aligners are working.

Clear aligners suit most teens and adults who want to straighten their teeth. A consultation with a certified provider can determine if clear aligners are the right choice for you.

Wearing your aligners for at least 20 to 22 hours per day is recommended for the best results. You should only remove them for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing.

Some people might notice a slight lisp at the beginning when wearing their aligners. However, this is usually temporary and disappears as you become accustomed to wearing them.

  • Comfort: Made from smooth plastic and custom-fitted to your teeth, clear aligners minimize irritation and discomfort often associated with the wires and brackets of metal braces.
  • Convenience: With clear aligners, the days of dietary restrictions are over. Since they’re removable, you can enjoy all your favorite foods and easily maintain your oral hygiene by brushing and flossing without obstruction.
  • Predictable results: Utilizing advanced 3D computer-imaging technology, clear aligner treatment allows you and your dentist to visualize your complete treatment plan from start to finish. This technology enables a more predictable outcome, providing you with a clear picture of your future smile.
  • Time efficiency: Clear aligner treatment typically requires fewer dental visits than traditional braces. With no need for wire tightening, your appointments will focus on progress checks, saving you time and inconvenience.
