Cleaning, Hygiene, & X-Rays

Prevention saves you time, money, and complications. Comprehensive cleanings, hygiene, and digital X-ray services are the cornerstone of a healthy, strong smile. By removing plaque and tartar that can lead to gum disease and decay, and utilizing advanced imaging to uncover hidden issues, we set the stage for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments are a critical defense against tooth decay, especially for patients at high risk. By applying a high concentration of fluoride, we can strengthen enamel, making it more resistant to acid attacks from plaque, bacteria, and sugars in the mouth. This simple, quick treatment reduces the likelihood of cavities and gum disease.

Emergencies

No need to wait in pain, we understand dental emergencies require swift, effective action. That’s why our team is prepared to provide immediate care when you need it most. Whether it&#x27;s a toothache, broken tooth, or injury to your gums, we prioritize your comfort and dental health, offering same-day appointments to alleviate pain and address the problem. We are here for you.

Periodontal Care

Periodontal (gum) disease is a leading cause of tooth loss in adults, making treatment essential. Our periodontal services range from scaling and root planing to more advanced therapies, all aimed at stopping the progression of gum disease. This not only preserves your natural teeth but also protect your overall health from systemic conditions.

Sedation

We understand that dental anxiety may stop you from receiving the care you need. That’s why we offer a range of sedation options, from nitrous oxide (laughing gas) to oral sedatives, ensuring your dental experience is comfortable and positive. Get the stress-free care you deserve today.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Early detection of oral cancer can dramatically increase survival rates, which is why we include comprehensive screenings as part of our dental exams. Using the latest technology, we can identify early signs of oral cancer, often before symptoms become apparent. This proactive screening is a critical aspect of our commitment to your overall health and well-being.

Night Guards

For patients experiencing bruxism (teeth grinding) or TMJ issues, our custom-fitted night guards offer a simple, effective solution. By preventing the wear and tear on your teeth caused by grinding, night guards protect your smile and alleviate jaw pain and headaches. Crafted with precision, they fit comfortably, ensuring a good night&#x27;s sleep and long-term oral health.

Fillings

Modern dental fillings are not just functional, they look natural too. At Yarris Family Dental, we use tooth-colored materials to restore decayed teeth, ensuring your fillings blend seamlessly with your natural smile. Our fillings are durable, long-lasting, and an integral part of our conservative approach to preserving your natural tooth structure.

Crown and Bridge

Crowns and bridges are foundational to restorative dentistry, offering solutions for damaged or missing teeth. Crowns are custom-crafted to match your natural teeth, restoring both appearance and function. Bridges fill the gap left by missing teeth, anchored securely with crowns for a natural look and feel.

Dentures

Whether you require partial or full dentures, our custom solutions are designed for comfort, functionality, and aesthetics. Yarris Family Dental takes pride in creating dentures that look natural and enhance your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
