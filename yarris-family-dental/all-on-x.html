What to Expect

The All-on-X Process

Personalized Consultation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation, where we assess your dental health, discuss your goals, and explain the All-on-X process. This step includes detailed imaging to plan your treatment precisely.

Preparation and Planning

For some patients, preparatory procedures such as tooth extraction or bone grafting may be necessary. We then proceed with detailed planning, utilizing advanced imaging to determine the optimal placement of implants.

Implant Placement

The All-on-X implants are placed in strategic locations in your jaw during a carefully planned surgical procedure. This step is designed for minimal discomfort and optimal outcomes. It will take a few months for your implants to fuse with your jawbone. This creates a strong foundation for your replacement teeth.

Attachment of New Teeth

Once the implants have healed, a full arch of high-quality, custom-made teeth is securely attached to the implants, transforming your smile and bite functionality in just one day.
