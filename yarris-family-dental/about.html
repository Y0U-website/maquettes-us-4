Dr. Douglas

Dr. Douglas Yarris grew up in Napa and graduated from Vintage High School in 1975. While at Vintage High School, he played men’s golf. He was just 18 years old when he qualified for the 1976 Kaiser International Open Invitational that was hosted at Silverado Resort.

He graduated from Stanford University in 1980 where he earned his Bachelor&#x27;s of Science degree in Human Bio and played for the Stanford golf team. He followed in his father’s footsteps and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of the Pacific Dental School in 1983.

Dr. Douglas Yarris coached his son’s golf team at Vintage High School from 2003-2006 where they went 72-3 with three Monticello Empire League championships. Dr. Douglas Yarris is currently on the Athletic board for Stanford University and is a faculty member at The University of the Pacific Arthur A Dugoni School of Dentistry.

Dr. Travis

Dr. Travis Yarris was born in Napa and graduated from Vintage High School in 2006 where he played varsity golf. He earned his Bachelor&#x27;s of Science degrees in Physiology and Neuroscience at the University of California, San Diego.

After completing his undergraduate degree, Dr. Travis Yarris worked as a wine laboratory chemist for two years at Trinchero in St. Helena where he learned the science behind winemaking and made his own 2012 vintage. Dr. Travis Yarris then attended the University of the Pacific, Arthur A Dugoni School of Dentistry, earning a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree to become the third generation dentist in his family.

While in dental school, he served as student body vice president of the University and tutored fellow colleagues in Pharmacology. Dr. Travis Yarris was selected to participate on a mission trip to Jamaica where he gained surgical experience by offering care to those in need. In 2021, he was awarded a fellowship in dental implant surgery by the International Congress of Oral Implantology (FICOI).

In his freetime, Dr. Travis enjoys playing golf with his sons Luca and Theo. He also exercises almost daily and enjoys powerlifting. But most of all, Dr. Travis loves enjoying the beauty that Napa has to offer, spending time with family, biking the Napa vine trail, and enjoying Napa’s remarkable food and wine scene.

Cutting-Edge Technology

We&#x27;re setting the standard for personalized treatments and cutting-edge techniques. Our practice is equipped with tools that redefine what&#x27;s possible in dental care.

3D X-Rays

3D X-ray technology goes beyond traditional imaging, offering a 360-degree view of your oral structure. This allows for more accurate diagnoses and precise treatment planning, ensuring you receive the best outcome.

CT Machine

The CT machine in our office is a game-changer for implantology and complex dental procedures. It provides detailed 3D images of your teeth, jawbone, and soft tissues, enabling us to plan and execute treatments with unprecedented precision.

3D Cameras

With our advanced 3D cameras, we capture high-resolution images of your teeth and gums. These detailed visuals aid in accurate diagnosis and allow us to show you, in clear terms, what&#x27;s happening in your mouth.

3D Printers

Our in-house 3D printers are at the heart of innovation. From creating precise dental models to fabricating surgical guides and custom dental appliances, these printers ensure that every aspect of your treatment is tailored to your specific needs.

AI CAD Software

Leveraging AI-powered CAD software, we design restorations that are not just functional but also aesthetically pleasing. This software helps us create customized crowns, bridges, and other restorations with a level of accuracy and artistry that was previously unimaginable.
