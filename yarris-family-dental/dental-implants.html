The Dental Implant Process

Comprehensive Consultation

Your dental implant journey begins with a thorough consultation with Dr. Travis. During this initial meeting, we assess your oral health, discuss your dental goals, and address any concerns. Utilizing advanced imaging techniques, we create a detailed plan tailored to your needs, ensuring your goals are met.

Preparation and Planning

Based on the consultation findings, we may recommend preparatory procedures such as bone grafting for patients with limited bone density. These treatments are designed to enhance the jawbone&#x27;s structure, providing a solid foundation for the implant. Our meticulous planning phase ensures that every aspect of your treatment is optimized for success.

Implant Placement

With a personalized treatment plan in place, the next step is the surgical placement of the dental implant into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation to ensure your comfort. Our use of cutting-edge technology and techniques minimizes discomfort and promotes a smooth, efficient healing process.

Healing

After the implant is placed, a period of healing (called osseointegration) begins. During this time, the implant fuses with the jawbone in a process that typically takes several months. This critical phase ensures the implant is securely anchored in the jaw, providing a stable foundation for the new tooth.

Abutment Placement and Restoration

Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant. This component serves as the connection point for the final restoration, such as a crown, bridge, or denture. The restoration is custom-crafted to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, fully restoring your smile&#x27;s functionality and aesthetics. You’ll go home with newfound confidence and a brilliantly restored smile.
