Meet Dr. Carolyn Kittell

Owner of Smile Essentials

Cosmetic & Restorative Dentist

Dr. Carolyn Kittell is a cosmetic and restorative dentist in Greenwood Village known for blending advanced technology with an artistic eye. Her work is rooted in the belief that great dentistry should enhance your natural beauty conservatively, comfortably, and with long-lasting results. As the founder of Smile Essentials, Dr. Kittell has built a practice that reflects her core values: precision, compassion, and personalized care .

A native of Laguna Beach, California, Dr. Kittell attended the University of Oklahoma on a full athletic scholarship for volleyball. There, she earned a Bachelor’s in Business Administration, followed by a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene.

After five years practicing as a dental hygienist, her passion for clinical excellence and patient connection led her to pursue a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS), also at the University of Oklahoma.

Practice Philosophy & Clinical Approach

With over 20 years in dentistry, Dr. Kittell brings deep clinical experience and a personalized approach to every patient. She focuses on conservative, natural-looking treatment plans prioritizing your comfort and long-term oral health.

Whether she’s designing a cosmetic smile makeover or restoring a single tooth, Dr. Kittell believes in treating each patient as she would a close friend or family member with respect, kindness, and intention.

Technology, Innovation, and In-House Lab

At Smile Essentials, Dr. Kittell leads a fully digital, in-house workflow from the initial scan to final restoration. She works directly with her on-site dental lab to craft custom veneers, crowns, and smile designs tailored to each patient’s features. This high-touch process allows her to maintain full control over the aesthetics, function, and fit resulting in elevated outcomes without outsourcing.
