Dental care focused on you: the Fee for Service treatment model At Reflections Dental Studio, we believe that dental care is just not about the teeth and gums, it’s about a relationship built on trust, comfort, and understanding. Our practice uses a fee-for-service payment model — an approach that prioritizes your dental well-being, preferences, and contentment.

As a fee-for-service practice, we’re motivated by the desire to provide personalized, high-quality dental care that meets your unique needs. We’re not bound by the restrictions of insurance-based approaches, we prioritize what truly counts: ensuring your dental well-being receives the utmost attention and excellence.

Patient choice

In a fee-for-service model, patients have the freedom to choose their dental provider based on factors such as reputation, expertise, and personal preference. This allows patients to seek out dentists whom they believe will provide the highest quality of care.

Flexible treatment options

Dentists operating under a fee-for-service model are not constrained by restrictions imposed by insurance companies or government-funded programs. This means they can offer a wider range of treatment options to patients, tailored to their individual needs and preferences, without concerns about insurance coverage limitations.

Personalized care

With patients directly paying for services rendered, there is a stronger patient-dentist relationship where the dentist can devote more time to understanding the patient’s specific needs and concerns. This can lead to more personalized treatment plans and better communication between the dentist and the patient.

Emphasis on preventative care

Fee-for-service dentistry can incentivize dentists to focus on preventive care and patient education to maintain oral health and prevent the need for costly treatments in the future. Dentists may spend more time educating patients about proper oral hygiene practices and recommending preventive measures such as regular cleanings and exams.

Prompt access to care

Patients may experience shorter wait times for appointments and treatments in a fee-for-service model since there are no pre-authorization requirements or delays associated with insurance approvals.

Questions and answers about the Fee for Service treatment model

A new, patient-centered approach to your dental care.

What insurance do you accept?

Any and all. We are out-of-network with all insurance but we will be happy to file your dental insurance claims on your behalf at no cost to you. We are a Fee-for-Service practice. We do offer an in house RDS Dental Plan for those with no dental insurance.

Do you take “this” insurance?

Yes. We file with most ALL dental insurance companies, but we are a fee-for-service practice which means our patients receive the highest standard of care because we are focused on your treatment. We will be happy to file your dental insurance claims on your behalf, where in some cases the reimbursement goes directly back to you, the patient / insurance subscriber.

What happens once an insurance claim is filed?

We are happy to file insurance claims on our patients’ behalf at no additional charge. Even though you are paying the office directly, we will manage all the paperwork and forms needed for reimbursement for your plans’ coverage at no extra charge.

How much will I pay on my first visit?

Depending on the care you need we will give you an estimate. Most dental insurances will pay their portion directly to the office for out-of-network providers. This results in the patient paying the portion not covered by insurance on the day of service. A select few will send the reimbursement to the patient in which case you would pay the full amount for the service on the day of service.

Why are you out of network?

We want to provide the quality of care you deserve. Insurance restricts what is covered and

how often you can have some services. It allows us to use the best materials and labs available so we can deliver to our high standards. We are removing the middleman who limits the care they will pay for.

This is all very confusing to me. Can you simplify things?

With our fee-for-service we treat insurance coverage as a coupon payment. Due to the limitations and restrictions we will help you understand what your benefits are as we understand them. We do not work for insurance companies. Our relationship is with the patient.

What are your payment options?

Insurance reimbursements based on out-of-network benefits
