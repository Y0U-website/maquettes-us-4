Dental Services in Chattanooga, TN

Digital Dentistry

Digital dentistry employs cutting-edge tools like 3D scanning, digital imaging, and computer-aided design (CAD) software to improve dental care. It enhances processes such as diagnosis, treatment planning, and restorations, delivering superior accuracy, efficiency, and comfort compared to conventional approaches.

Single Visit Crowns Using our on-site laboratory and cutting-edge CAD/CAM technology, we can create and produce your personalized, tooth colored (porcelain) restorations in one, 90-minute visit, delivering lasting and beautiful results. The best part is that there are no goopy impressions or temporaries involved!

Digital Dentures Digital dentures utilize advanced 3D scanning and printing technology to create precise, custom-fit traditional dentures, implant retained Snap-On dentures , and All-On-4® dentures. These solutions offer enhanced comfort, durability, and aesthetics, streamlining production for faster delivery and improved patient satisfaction.

Mouthguards Mouthguards are essential for individuals who clench or grind their teeth during sleep. Using a digital scan instead of messy impressions, custom-fitted, 3D printed bite guards can be created for people of all ages, including children and teens involved in high school sports.

Surgical Dentistry

Surgical dentistry focuses on advanced procedures to address complex dental issues, such as tooth extractions, dental implants, and bone grafting. Utilizing precise techniques and modern technology, it ensures effective treatment, enhanced recovery, and improved oral health for patients of all ages.

Dental Implants At Harrison Family Dentistry, we use only the highest-quality dental implants—those with the strongest research backing—to digitally plan and surgically place them in the jawbone to support custom crowns. This permanent solution for missing teeth restores function, enhances aesthetics, and promotes long-term oral health.

Snap-On Dentures Snap-on dentures are removable dentures that securely attach to dental implants using special snap-in attachments. They offer improved stability over traditional dentures, making eating and speaking easier, while still allowing for easy removal and cleaning by the patient.

Wisdom Teeth Removal Wisdom teeth often require removal due to impaction, crowding, or pain. In our office, we perform most wisdom tooth extractions, including sedation, without needing a surgeon referral, using advanced techniques for comfort and quick recovery.

Sedation In-office IV sedation and nitrous oxide (laughing gas) provide deep relaxation for dental procedures. Administered by trained professionals, these safe, controlled methods ensure comfort for anxious patients or complex treatments while maintaining consciousness.

Fixed Hybrid Dentures Fixed hybrid dentures, also known as All-On-4® dentures, are a permanently screwed-in, implant-supported solution that replaces a full arch of missing teeth. They offer exceptional stability, natural appearance, and improved chewing function, combining the benefits of dental implants with the aesthetics of custom prosthetic teeth.

Tooth Extractions Teeth may require removal due to severe decay, damage, crowding, or infection. In our office, we efficiently perform almost all types of extractions using advanced techniques, including sedation to ensure patient comfort and immediate bone grafting to provide jaw support.

Root Canal Therapy A root canal is a dental procedure to save a damaged or infected tooth by removing the pulp, cleaning the canal system contained in each tooth root, and sealing it. This treatment relieves pain, prevents infection, and restores tooth function.

Cosmetic Dentistry

If you are not happy with your smile, come see us at Harrison Family Dentistry where we would love to assist. Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and smile. Treatments may include whitening, veneers, bonding, and reshaping, helping patients achieve a brighter, more balanced smile while often enhancing overall oral health and self-confidence.

Clear Aligners SureSmile® is a system of clear aligners that are a discreet, removable alternative to braces that gradually straighten teeth using custom-made, transparent trays. They offer comfort, convenience, and minimal lifestyle disruption, making them a popular choice for teens and adults seeking a straighter smile.

Tooth Bonding Tooth bonding is a quick, non-invasive cosmetic procedure that uses tooth-colored resin to repair inconsistent tooth sizes, chips, and gaps on front teeth. It enhances the appearance of your smile in a single visit, providing natural-looking, long-lasting results with minimal enamel removal.

Gum Recontouring Gummy smile? Gum recontouring, or gingivectomy, is a cosmetic procedure that reshapes the gumline by removing excess gum tissue with a laser. It creates a more balanced, symmetrical smile, often exposing more of the natural tooth for improved aesthetics and easier oral hygiene.

Teeth Whitening KöR Teeth whitening is a safe, effective cosmetic treatment that removes stains and discoloration from the enamel, brightening your smile several shades. This professional whitening system is the best available and delivers fast, noticeable results and is customized to achieve a natural, radiant appearance.

Veneers Veneers are thin, custom-made shells of porcelain or composite resin that bond to the front of teeth to improve their shape, color, and alignment. They offer a durable, natural-looking solution for enhancing your smile with minimal tooth preparation.

Complete Dentistry

Complete whole mouth oral healthcare enhances overall health by preventing cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups promote healthy gums and strong teeth, boosting confidence and reducing risks of serious health issues.

Pediatric Dentistry Pediatric dentistry focuses on children’s oral health, providing preventative care, cleanings, and treatments like sealants and topical fluoride. It ensures healthy teeth and gums, fostering lifelong dental habits through gentle, kid-friendly approaches, regular checkups, and education for parents.

Fillings: Resin & Porcelain Dental fillings restore decayed teeth, enhancing function and appearance. Natural tooth-colored fillings are versatile and blend seamlessly. Tooth-colored fillings made from porcelain are the most durable and stain-resistant, offering superior aesthetics. Both ensure strong, healthy teeth through expert application and regular care.

Laser Dentistry Laser dentistry offers precise, minimally invasive treatments for gum recontouring, tongue ties, and gum disease. Using advanced technology, it reduces discomfort, speeds recovery, and enhances outcomes, ensuring healthy teeth and gums with gentle, effective care for all ages.

Sealants Dental sealants protect teeth from decay by coating chewing surfaces with a thin, protective layer. Applied quickly and painlessly, they shield vulnerable areas, ensuring long-lasting oral health for children and adults through preventative care.

Oral Cancer Screening Oral cancer screenings detect early signs of cancer in the mouth and throat. Using advanced techniques, our thorough exams ensure timely intervention, promoting oral health and peace of mind through preventative care for all patients.

Teeth Cleanings Professional teeth cleanings remove plaque and tartar, preventing cavities and gum disease. Our gentle and skilled hygienists polish teeth for a brighter smile, ensuring optimal oral health and confidence through personalized, gentle care during regular dental visits.

Periodontal Therapy Professional periodontal therapy treats gum disease, reducing inflammation and preventing tooth loss. Our expert team uses advanced techniques like scaling and root planing to restore healthy gums, ensuring long-term oral health and a confident smile.

Technology

CEREC Restore your smile in just one visit with CEREC same-day crowns. Using advanced 3D technology, we design and mill a durable, all-ceramic crown right here. This eliminates messy impressions, temporary crowns, and multiple appointments, giving you a beautiful, healthy result.

PrimePrint Experience cutting-edge care with our DS PrimePrint 3D printer. This advanced technology allows us to quickly create precise digital dentures, surgical guides, bite spints, and more in-house. The result is a faster, more accurate, and more comfortable experience for you.

Axeos CBCT We use the Axeos CBCT for a low-dose 3D scan, providing incredibly detailed 3D images of your oral structures, like teeth and bone. This advanced technology allows for early and precise diagnoses and more predictable treatment planning, ensuring a safe and comfortable experience.

Digital X-rays Our digital intraoral x-rays prioritize your safety and comfort. This technology uses significantly less radiation than traditional film, providing instant, high-resolution images. This allows for faster, more accurate diagnoses and better patient communication for a more efficient visit.

SPEAR Patient Education We use the SPEAR patient education system to help you fully understand your dental health. Through high-quality videos and animations, we can visually explain diagnoses and treatment options, empowering you to make confident, informed decisions about your care.

Photography Digital photography is a key tool in modern dentistry. We use high-resolution images to accurately document your oral health, allowing us to show you your teeth, plan treatments, and track your progress. It’s an excellent way to involve you in your care.
