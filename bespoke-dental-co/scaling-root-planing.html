Scaling & Root Planing

  • Root Planing : After scaling, the roots of the teeth are smoothed out to encourage the gums to reattach to the teeth, reducing the depth of periodontal pockets.

When is Scaling and Root Planing Needed?

This procedure is typically recommended for patients showing signs of gum disease, such as:

  • Red, swollen, or bleeding gums
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Loose teeth
  • Receding gums

The Benefits of Scaling and Root Planing

Undergoing scaling and root planing can have numerous benefits for your oral health:

  • Halts the Progression of Gum Disease : By removing plaque and tartar, this treatment helps stop gum disease in its tracks.
  • Reduces Inflammation and Bleeding : It helps soothe inflamed gums and reduce bleeding.
  • Prevents Tooth Loss: By addressing the underlying cause of gum disease, scaling and root planing can prevent tooth loss.
  • Improves Overall Health : Treating gum disease can positively impact your overall health, as it’s linked to systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes.
