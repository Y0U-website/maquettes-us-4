The practice

Dr. Schuessler graduated from Oklahoma State University with a degree in Chemical Engineering. Following three years with Dow Chemical and Conoco, he decided to further his education in dentistry. Dr. Schuessler graduated from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, and practiced as a General Dentist in Edmond for six years, focusing on Cosmetic Dentistry.

He then attended Vanderbilt University for three years as a resident in Orthodontics. He began his orthodontics practice in Stillwater in 2005. Dr. Schuessler is married to Christa, and together they have three boys - Karsten, Eric, and Tripp. They are very active in sports, church, and love to travel.
