Services

Cleaning & Prevention

Your smile is our top priority. Dental cleaning removes dental plaque and tartar from your teeth, ensuring a healthier and brighter smile.

Composite Fillings

A composite filling, which matches the color of the tooth, is used to repair a tooth that has been damaged by decay, crack or other issues.

Crowns (Caps)

A crown, also known as a cap, is a covering that encases the entire surface of a tooth, restoring it to its original shape and size.

Fixed Bridges

A dental bridge is a permanent (non-removable) appliance and is an excellent option for replacing missing teeth.

Dentures & Partial Dentures

A denture is a removable dental appliance designed to replace missing teeth and surrounding tissue. It helps restore both function and appearance, allowing individuals to chew, speak, and smile more naturally.

Tooth Extractions

If you re dealing with extremely sensitive periodontal disease, it s crucial to seek professional dental care. Early intervention can prevent further damage, alleviate discomfort, and improve oral health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of teeth, gums, and smiles through various procedures like whitening, veneers, and bonding. It enhances both aesthetic appeal and self-confidence for a brighter, more radiant smile.

Periodontal (Gum) Disease

Periodontal (gum) disease is an infection of the tissues that support the teeth, primarily caused by plaque buildup. If left untreated, it can lead to gum inflammation, tooth loss, and other serious health issues.

Dental Emergencies

Dental emergencies can be both terrifying and extremely painful. These situations often require immediate attention and can cause significant distress.

Dental Anxiety And Fear

Dental anxiety and fear refer to the feelings of apprehension or dread some individuals experience before or during dental visits. These emotions can prevent people from seeking necessary care, potentially worsening their oral health over time.

Endodontics Therapy

Endodontics therapy involves the diagnosis and treatment of issues related to the dental pulp and tissues surrounding the roots of a tooth. The most common procedure is root canal therapy, which removes infected pulp to save the tooth and alleviate pain.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment is a procedure used to address an infected tooth pulp, involving the removal of the infected pulp and the cleaning and sealing of the tooth s interior.
