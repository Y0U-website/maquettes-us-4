Providing you with exceptional dental care requires a combination of skill, education, mutual trust, and a safe environment where we meet all your needs. To us, you are a unique individual, and we take the time to get to know you so we can create a dental treatment plan that is personalized to fit all your needs.

We also know that many people find it difficult to keep dental appointments because of anxiety. In addition to a supportive team who puts your needs first, we offer both nitrous oxide and oral sedation to help you relax and focus on getting the care you need for a healthy smile.

Dental visits aren’t high on anybody’s list of enjoyable activities. We understand this, so we take care of all the details that might get in the way of receiving excellent dental care.

Budgeting for dental treatment can be a challenge for many people, so we want to do everything we can to make your family’s care accessible and affordable.

We accept numerous dental insurances, including Aetna, Blue Cross/Blue Shield, Cigna, Delta Dental, Guardian, Humana, MetLife, United Healthcare, and will file all claims as a courtesy to you. Understanding how your dental insurance applies to your treatment can be a challenge, but we will be happy to guide you and ensure you get the maximum benefit to which you are entitled.

Don’t have dental insurance? Don’t worry! We take all major credit cards, and we also offer third-party financing through CareCredit, Lending Club Patient Solutions, Proceed Financial and Lending Point. These interest-free and low-interest programs make it possible for you to receive the important dental care you need today and extend your payments over several months.

Please visit our Dental Savings Plan page to learn how we can help provide you with affordable dental care in North Miami.
