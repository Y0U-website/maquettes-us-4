Discover Exceptional Dental Care with Dr. Sylvan Fain: Your Trusted Miami Dentist

Originally from Toronto, Ontario, Dr. Sylvan Fain earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Western Ontario. He later moved south and completed advanced clinical training through the Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program at Nova Southeastern University.

Gentle, Personalized Dental Care with a Comfort-First Approach

Dr. Fain firmly believes that dental care should never be an intimidating experience; instead, it should be a comfortable and calming one, entirely centered around you as the patient. He is dedicated to transforming how individuals perceive and experience dentistry by prioritizing their comfort and fostering trusting, long-term relationships.

With a strong passion for continuous learning, Dr. Fain consistently engages in continuing education courses covering various dentistry aspects, including restorative, cosmetic, implant, and sedation techniques. This unwavering commitment allows him to provide an extensive range of high-quality services while ensuring that each treatment is performed with the utmost precision and care, tailored to meet each patient’s unique needs.

Dr. Fain’s gentle approach to dentistry is uniquely personalized to each patient. Having experienced the challenges of wearing braces for five years due to severely crooked teeth, he truly understands how dental care can greatly affect one’s confidence. He recognizes the importance of feeling heard and comfortable in the dental chair. Dr. Fain invests time in listening to his patients, answering their questions, and tailoring each treatment plan to address their specific needs and concerns. His compassionate and attentive demeanor makes every visit a positive experience.
