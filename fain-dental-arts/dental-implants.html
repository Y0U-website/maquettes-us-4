How does a dental implant work?

When you lose a tooth because of an accident or advanced decay, it’s not just the white protective covering (crown) you lose. You also lose the root, which keeps your tooth stable and secure. Teeth replacements like dentures and bridges are effective, but they only replace the “crown” part of your tooth.

Dental implants recreate your entire tooth, replacing your crown alongside the root. The “root part” of the implant system is made of high-quality titanium, renowned for its biocompatibility and durability. The titanium fixture fuses with the jawbone over time, forming a rock-solid foundation to support a crown, bridge, or denture.
