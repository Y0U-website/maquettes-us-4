Dental Implants

  • Planning you can see: Dr. Perdue positions the implant on screen with modern implant planning technology and walks you through it on the monitor.
  • Grafting when needed: Socket preservation, ridge augmentation, or a sinus lift, done here using donor tissue when bone has thinned.
  • In-office placement: Dr. Perdue places the post under local anesthetic, with nitrous oxide available. A single implant often takes under an hour.
  • Healing: The bone grows around the post over the following months. You get a temporary tooth where it matters for your smile.
  • Your custom crown: Once the post is solid, we scan again and fit a crown , bridge, or denture matched to your other teeth.

Comfort During

Implant Treatment

Implant surgery sounds bigger than it feels. Local anesthetic keeps the area numb, nitrous oxide is there if you want the edge taken off, and you can ask for a blanket, ear plugs, or something from the drink bar before we start. We speak Spanish, too.

Nobody here will push you into a plan. If a bridge, a partial, or simply waiting makes more sense for your mouth and budget, Dr. Perdue will say so. Many patients drive in from Millcreek, Murray, and Cottonwood Heights for exactly that reason.

Caring for Your Implant

After Surgery

  • First few days: Some swelling and tenderness are normal. Over-the-counter pain relief and soft foods usually handle it.
  • First two weeks: Brush gently around the site and skip smoking, straws, and hard or crunchy foods on that side.
  • Long term: Brush and floss it like a natural tooth and keep your regular cleanings.
