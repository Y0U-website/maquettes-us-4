Visiting Our Office

What Patients Can Expect

On your first visit, you can expect to be greeted warmly by our office manager, Nicole. Whether you’re coming in for a routine cleaning or for surgery, you’ll be put at ease with our relaxing, laid-back office environment. You are our priority, so we’ll make sure you feel comfortable asking any questions about our services and your treatment. Dr. Garcia-Gurule and the rest of our staff will gladly give you all the information that you need. From start to finish, you’ll be treated with respect and kindness.

A Relaxing Space

Our goal is to put you at ease and give you a positive experience going to the dentist. We do this by cultivating a calm, friendly environment throughout the office.

Technology for Increased Comfort

Nitrous Oxide and Implants

At Belen Family Dentistry, we spare no expense to make sure that our patients are comfortable and cared for. With nitrous oxide (laughing gas), your dental surgeries will be over in no time, with minimal discomfort for you. If you’re in need of implant treatments, Dr. Garcia-Gurule&#x27;s implant expertise and our digital x-ray technology take the guesswork out of your treatment.
