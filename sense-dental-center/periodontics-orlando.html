Expert periodontics: transformative care for your dental health

Periodontics stands as a specialized branch of dentistry dedicated to preventing, diagnosing, and treating periodontal (gum) disease and conditions affecting the structures that support the teeth. Understanding what periodontics is helps patients appreciate its crucial role in maintaining oral health. The definition of periodontics encompasses various treatments aimed at preserving the gums, bones, and ligaments around the teeth.

Frequently asked questions

Periodontics is a specialized field of dentistry focused on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease and conditions affecting the supporting structures of the teeth, including gums, bone, and ligaments. Healthy gums are essential for maintaining a stable, long-lasting smile, and periodontic care helps prevent tooth loss, bone deterioration, and chronic infection.

You should consider seeing a periodontist if you experience symptoms such as bleeding gums, gum recession, chronic bad breath, loose teeth, or gum inflammation. Periodontic care is especially important if gum disease has progressed beyond what routine dental cleanings can treat. Early intervention helps stop disease progression and preserve your oral health.

Periodontic treatments include gum disease therapy, dental deep cleanings, scaling and root planing, and advanced procedures for managing periodontal infections. These treatments are designed to remove plaque and bacteria below the gum line, reduce inflammation, and promote healing to restore healthy gum tissue and support structures.

Dental deep cleaning is a general term often used to describe advanced gum cleaning procedures. Scaling and root planing is a specific periodontal treatment that removes plaque and tartar from tooth roots and smooths them to help gums reattach. Both are essential in treating and controlling gum disease, depending on its severity.

Periodontics plays a critical role in preserving the foundation of your teeth. By treating gum disease and maintaining healthy gums and bone, periodontic care helps prevent tooth loss, protects dental restorations, and supports overall oral and systemic health. Ongoing periodontal maintenance ensures lasting results and a healthier smile.
