Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to act as replacement tooth roots. They provide a stable, permanent foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike traditional options, implants do not shift, slip, or damage surrounding teeth.

You may need dental implants if you are missing one or more teeth, struggle to chew or speak clearly, have loose dentures or bridges, want to avoid bone loss in the jaw, or experience frequent discomfort or self-consciousness when smiling. A comprehensive consultation at Sense Dental Center can determine whether implants are right for you.

Sense Dental Center offers customized implant treatments, including single-tooth implants, implant-supported bridges, and full-mouth dental implants for complete restoration. Each option is tailored to deliver long-lasting, natural-looking results.

The cost of dental implants depends on the type of treatment, such as a single-tooth implant or a full-mouth restoration. At Sense Dental Center, we provide affordable dental implants in Orlando with flexible options, and our team gives you comprehensive information so you can make an informed decision.

Unlike removable dentures, dental implants function like natural teeth, offering strong and stable support for chewing and biting. They look and feel natural, and they stimulate the jawbone through osseointegration, which helps maintain bone density, prevents bone loss, and preserves the natural contours of your face.
