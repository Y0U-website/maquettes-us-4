Frequently asked questions

Oral surgery refers to a range of surgical procedures used to treat complex dental and oral health conditions that cannot be addressed with routine dental care. It may be necessary for issues such as impacted wisdom teeth, severe tooth damage, jawbone loss, dental implant placement, TMJ disorders, or gum reshaping procedures like crown lengthening. Oral surgery helps restore function, comfort, and long-term oral health.

At Sense Dental Center, we provide comprehensive oral surgery services including tooth extractions, bone grafting, surgical placement of dental implants, TMD/TMJ treatment, and crown lengthening. Each procedure is performed using advanced techniques and tailored to your individual needs to ensure safe, precise, and effective results.

Oral surgery is typically very manageable and performed with your comfort as a top priority. Local anesthesia is commonly used, and sedation options may be available depending on the procedure. After surgery, mild swelling or discomfort is normal and can be managed with prescribed or over-the-counter medication. Our team provides detailed post-operative care instructions to support a smooth recovery.

Recovery time varies depending on the type of oral surgery performed. Simple procedures like tooth extractions may require only a few days, while more advanced treatments such as bone grafting or implant surgery may involve a longer healing period. We closely monitor your recovery and provide follow-up care to ensure proper healing and long-term success.

The cost of oral surgery depends on the complexity of the procedure and your specific treatment plan. At Sense Dental Center, we offer transparent pricing and discuss all costs before treatment begins. Our team works with you to explore affordable options and ensure you receive high-quality care without unexpected expenses.
