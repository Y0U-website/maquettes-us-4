Frequently asked questions

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and smile. At Sense Dental Center in Orlando, we offer veneers, teeth whitening, crowns, and smile design to enhance your confidence and oral aesthetics.

Some of the most requested cosmetic dental procedures include porcelain veneers, composite veneers, teeth whitening, dental crowns, bonding, and orthodontic alignment. Our Orlando clinic customizes each treatment plan based on your goals and budget.

Most treatments are minimally invasive and performed with local anesthesia. Patients experience little to no discomfort.

Composite resin veneers typically last 5-7 years on average when properly cared for. Some sources report a broader range from 4 up to 8 years. In particularly well-maintained cases, they may last up to 10 years, though that is not the norm.

Porcelain veneers can last between 10 to 15 years with proper care. At Sense Dental Center in Orlando, we use high-quality porcelain materials that resist stains and mimic the natural translucency of real enamel. With regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene, your veneers can keep your smile beautiful for many years.

Implant-supported dentures are a modern, secure alternative to traditional removable dentures. They are anchored directly into the jawbone with dental implants, preventing slipping or discomfort. This solution offers better chewing ability, improved comfort, and a natural appearance — making it one of the most popular restorative options at our Orlando clinic.
