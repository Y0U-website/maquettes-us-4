Pharmacies operate to ensure patients and community members have access to affordable prescriptions.

New Patient? Thank you for considering Johnson Health Center for your health care! To get started, please give us a call - we look forward to speaking with you.

We offer multiple ways for existing patients to schedule appointments, including via text or on our website (same-day visits only). It’s easy, fast, and can be done at your convenience.

Our mission is to provide affordable and comprehensive healthcare with quality services and strong community partnerships to improve access to healthcare for all.

Johnson Health Center is a non-profit, independent, Federally Qualified Health Center serving Lynchburg, VA and the counties of Amherst, Bedford and Campbell. The health center is governed by a board of directors composed of at least 51 % of our patient population.
