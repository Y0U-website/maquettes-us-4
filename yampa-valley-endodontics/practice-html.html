Technology

GentleWave Procedure

The GentleWave procedure is a state of the art fluid-irrigation protocol for the use of Endodontic cleaning and disinfection . Yampa Valley Endodontics utilizes the most recent iteration of this technology--The G4 GentleWave System.

Conventional root canal therapy allows only 5-10mL of liquid disinfectant cycled through the canal system via simple needle irrigation. The GentleWave procedure allows the practitioner to cycle 350mL of sonically activated fluids through the canal system--an unprecedented technological advance.

The GentleWave procedure achieves less postoperative pain , increases likelihood of single visit treatments , results in a cleaner canal system with less instrumentation , treats complex teeth that cannot be cleaned by conventional methods.

Microscope

Dr. Troxel utilizes a microscope to perform all root canals and surgical procedures under high magnification . The microscope illuminates and magnifies an otherwise dark and minute area. The canals of your teeth can be as narrow as a strand of hair, and without the aid of a microscope can be easily missed.

CBCT

Dr. Troxel is trained on advanced radiography, including dental CT scans. These scans offer a three dimensional view of a tooth, and allows Dr. Troxel to identify unusual anatomy, missed canals, cracked teeth, and hidden infections. Dr. Troxel has invested in a brand new state of the art CBCT, the Carestream 8200 3D with CS MAR technology. CS MAR is cutting edge proprietary technology which allows Dr. Troxel to significantly reduce metal scatter and artifact production in order to better visualize hairline cracks and miniscule canal morphology.

Digital X-rays

In addition to CBCT’s, Dr. Troxel also utilizes digital two-dimensional x-rays. Digital x-rays allow lower exposure to patients, while yielding a higher resolution product. They are used before, during, and after treatment to confirm progress and quality.

Ultrasonics

The ultrasonic is an acoustic instrument, which allows Dr. Troxel to conservatively access difficult areas of your tooth. These instruments are used to treat calcified canals, remove broken instruments, locate extra canals, and remove posts.

EndoActivator

The EndoActivator uses polymer tips and sonic energy to agitate the antibacterial irrigants used during treatment. Activated fluids promote deep cleaning into hard-to-reach canal anatomy. Inadequate cleaning of these spaces can lead to re-infection. Evidence suggests that the use of the device and its disinfecting capabilities promotes the long-term success of root canal therapy.
