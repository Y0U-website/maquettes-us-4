Since 1967, Tri County Tech has been dedicated to one goal: helping students succeed. Our award-winning educators are experts in their fields who provide the hands-on training you need to launch your career with confidence.

We make your success affordable and accessible. Our adult programs offer competitive tuition with in-house financial assistance , while high school programs are completely free to Washington, Nowata, and Osage county students. Our application process is simple and 100 % online.
