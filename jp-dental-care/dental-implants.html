Dental Implants

Dental implants also assist in supporting your speech, comfort, and chewing abilities that are typically affected when you have missing teeth.

  • Prevents tooth misalignment
  • Restores jaw strength
  • Provides a natural appearance

What is the procedure for a dental implant?

When you arrive at JP Dental Care to receive a dental implant, the team examines the problem area, taking X-rays if necessary. Next, they create a treatment plan tailored to your needs that considers how many teeth need replacing and the health of your surrounding teeth and jawbone.

If your jawbone lacks the density necessary to receive a dental implant, you may need a bone graft. This surgical procedure replaces missing bone in your jaw with bone grafting materials to encourage new bone development.

Once you’re ready to begin the procedure, the team administers local anesthesia, sedation, or general anesthesia, depending on your preference. When the anesthesia takes effect, the team begins surgically placing metal implants into your jaw.

After the procedure, you must give your jaw time to properly heal, eating soft foods and icing your face daily. Once your jaw has healed, you’ll return to the office for the team to mount the dental crown, bridge, or dentures to the implant.
