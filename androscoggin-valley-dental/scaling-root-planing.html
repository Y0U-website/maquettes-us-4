Scaling & Root Planing

  • Targets infection below the gumline Cleans deeper areas where routine cleanings cannot reach. Great for: periodontal pockets
  • Supports gum healing Smooths root surfaces to discourage bacteria and promote healthier attachment.
  • Protects long-term tooth stability Helps preserve bone and reduce the risk of future tooth loss.

What To Expect During Treatment

Treatment begins with an exam and gum measurements to identify areas of concern. Plaque and tartar are carefully removed from above and below the gumline, followed by smoothing of the tooth roots. Many patients choose local numbing for comfort, and treatment may be completed in sections depending on the severity of infection.

  • Comfort-focused local numbing options
  • Thorough cleaning beneath the gumline
  • Root smoothing to reduce bacterial buildup
  • Follow-up measurements to monitor healing

Aftercare And Long-Term Results

Mild tenderness or sensitivity is common for a few days as gums heal. Keeping the area clean at home is essential, and additional hygiene recommendations may be provided based on your needs. After treatment, gum health is re-evaluated and a maintenance schedule is discussed to help prevent future flare-ups.
