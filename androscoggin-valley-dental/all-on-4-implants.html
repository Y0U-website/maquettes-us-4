What the Process Looks Like

Treatment begins with a comprehensive exam and imaging to assess bone support, bite alignment, and overall oral health. If All-on-4 is appropriate, implants are placed to create a strong foundation for fixed teeth. Follow-up visits help monitor healing and ensure comfort, fit, and long-term stability.

  • Comprehensive evaluation and imaging for precise planning
  • Bite assessment to support comfort and durability
  • Guided follow-ups to track healing and fit
  • Home-care guidance to protect implants and gums

Who May Be A Candidate?

All-on-4 may be a good option if you are missing most or all teeth, struggle with removable dentures, or have failing teeth that need a long-term solution. Bone support, gum health, and medical history are all considered to determine the safest and most effective plan.
