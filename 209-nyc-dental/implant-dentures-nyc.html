Implant Denture Overview

Implant dentures are a modern solution for those suffering from total tooth loss . They combine the security of dental implants with the aesthetics of custom-made prosthetics. Unlike traditional removable dentures that rest on the gums, these are anchored directly and securely to your jawbone using several dental implants , which act as strong, artificial tooth roots.

Types of Implant Dentures

At 209 NYC Dental, we offer solutions for both full-arch and partial tooth loss. They fall into two primary types: implant-retained (removable) and implant-supported (fixed) prostheses. Each offers unique advantages based on your needs, bone structure, and lifestyle, and both can be used to restore several missing teeth or an entire arch.

Fixed Implant Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are fully supported by 4–8 dental implants and are screwed into your jawbone, offering maximum stability and function. They do not touch the gum tissue and are only removable by your dentist, hence they are often called permanent or fixed dentures .

Key Features:

  • Permanently secured with 4–8 implants per arch
  • Not removable by patient; only by your dentist
  • Common systems: All-on-4, All-on-X , zirconia hybrid dentures

Ideal Candidacy:

  • Patients with healthy jawbone density
  • Those seeking a permanent solution that looks and feels natural

Removable Implant-Retained Dentures

Often called snap on dentures , implant overdenture this option provides a dramatic increase in stability. Your denture clips securely onto several dental implants but can be removed at home for easy cleaning. It’s an excellent and accessible solution for eliminating looseness.

Key Features:

  • Held in place by 2–4 implants
  • Removed daily for cleaning
  • Common systems: Snap-in dentures using locator attachments, Bar-retained overdentures clipped onto a support bar

Implant Supported Denture Benefits

The decision to move forward with implant treatment is an investment in your quality of life. Patients who choose implant-supported dentures enjoy benefits that go far beyond aesthetics.

  • Secure Fit: Implants eliminate slipping and adhesives, allowing you to confidently engage during meals and meetings.
  • Restored Chewing Power: You can enjoy steak, apples, and favorite foods again with up to 90 % natural bite strength.
  • Enhanced Speech and Appearance: Fixed restorations allow you to speak clearly and smile confidently without clicking or sunken facial features.
  • Bone Preservation: Titanium implants stimulate the jawbone to prevent the natural deterioration that occurs after tooth loss.
  • Overall Health Support: A restored bite allows you to easily digest fiber-rich, nutritious foods for better full-body wellness.
  • Simple Maintenance: Fixed dentures require no soaking or paste; you simply brush them like natural teeth.
  • Long-Term Value: While the initial cost is higher, implant solutions last longer and significantly reduce future replacement needs.

Ideal Patient Candidates

Not everyone is immediately eligible for implant dentures, but many qualify or can become eligible with preparatory treatment. Ideal candidates demonstrate:

  • Adults with generally good oral and systemic health.
  • Individuals with sufficient jawbone density or those willing to undergo bone grafting .
  • Non-smokers (or patients willing to quit smoking to ensure proper surgical healing).
  • Patients committed to maintaining excellent oral hygiene and attending follow-up care visits.

  • Fixed Implant Supported Dentures: Typically range from $16,000–$30,000+ per arch.

Treatment Cost Factors

The total cost of your treatment depends on several specific variables:

  • The total number of implants needed to support the arch.
  • The type of denture prosthetic chosen (fixed vs. removable).
  • Whether preparatory procedures like extractions or bone grafting are required.
  • The materials used for the final denture (e.g., highly durable zirconia vs. acrylic).
