Get to Know

Dr. Tyler Powell

As a Michigan native and second-generation dental professional, Dr. Tyler Powell brings advanced surgical expertise and a calming presence to every patient interaction. After earning his undergraduate degree from the University of Michigan, Dr. Tyler continued his dental education at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, followed by an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at Wichita State. There, he developed a special interest in complex restorative procedures, oral surgery, and implant placement.

Dr. Tyler is known for balancing precision with genuine compassion, keeping even the most advanced procedures approachable and stress-free. Patients trust him for his calm demeanor, attention to detail, and skill with treatments like same-day crowns, guided implant surgery, extractions, and 3D-printed solutions.

His passion for surgical innovation ensures care stays cutting-edge while maintaining a deeply human connection. Whether you&#x27;re visiting for a routine check-up or an implant consultation, Dr. Tyler&#x27;s goal is to help patients feel informed, empowered, and cared for every step of the way.

Outside the operatory, Dr. Tyler is a proud husband, outdoor enthusiast, and loyal Michigan Wolverine. His love of local community life and commitment to clinical excellence continue to shape the friendly, forward-thinking atmosphere of Smiles For Life.

Get to Know

Dr. Kiera Powell

Dr. Kiera Powell carries forward a proud legacy of dental excellence rooted in empathy, family values, and artistry. A graduate of Ohio Northern University and the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, Dr. Kiera completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Wichita State University. She focuses on restorative and cosmetic dentistry with an eye for precision and beauty.

Her patients love her for her calm, thoughtful chairside manner and her dedication to building lasting relationships—whether you’re improving your smile with clear aligners or receiving preventive care. Dr. Kiera is especially passionate about clear aligner therapy, including SureSmile, and cosmetic smile makeovers that reflect each patient&#x27;s unique facial aesthetics and personality.

She is devoted to simplifying complex treatment plans, helping patients explore possibilities through cutting-edge technologies like digital scanners and AI-powered diagnostics. Dr. Kiera meets every patient where they are—she believes people should never feel rushed, judged, or anxious when receiving care.

Dr. Kiera understands how to cater to families, busy professionals, and patients with long-standing fears of dentistry. Her warm, steady presence and commitment to patient education make her a pillar of the practice—and a favorite among patients of all ages.

Get to Know

Dr. Lisa Powell

With decades of experience and a legacy built on trust, Dr. Lisa Powell continues to be a cornerstone of the practice she helped create. As the mother of Dr. Tyler and mentor to Dr. Kiera, Dr. Lisa brings a wealth of knowledge and genuine warmth to the care she provides.

Patients often describe her as deeply attentive, kind, and reassuring—someone who takes the time to listen and truly understand patient needs. Known for her all-encompassing dental expertise and gentle approach, Dr. Lisa has built strong relationships with multiple generations of families.

Her dedication to staying current with advancements in technology, materials, and technique is matched only by her belief in individualized attention and long-term oral health. Dr. Lisa’s patients appreciate that she never takes a one-size-fits-all approach; instead, she develops tailored care plans based not only on clinical need—but on each patient’s lifestyle, goals, and comfort level.

Her legacy lives on not only through her clinical excellence, but through the next generation of Powell dentists she proudly works alongside every day. Together, they continue to redefine what modern, patient-first dental care looks like.

Starting Your Journey

What to Expect from Your First Visit

We believe your first visit should be as stress-free and informative as possible. Our team will get to know you, understand your smile goals, and prioritize your comfort across every step of care. With digital forms, spa-like touches, and cutting-edge diagnostics, we aim to redefine what feeling “at-home” in the dental chair really means.

Your First Experience You’ll be welcomed with kindness and care from the moment you step through our doors. Our front desk will guide you through a seamless check-in process. During your appointment, we take time to listen, educate, and co-create a personalized plan based on your goals and needs—not corporate quotas.

What to Bring Please bring the following items to your first appointment:

Completed digital patient forms (sent via Modento)

Any medical or dental records if previously arranged

Innovative Tech. Modern Comforts.

At Smiles For Life, we’ve designed our office—and our approach—with one goal in mind: to elevate your experience. With a full suite of high-end technologies and relaxing amenities, we strive to make your care easier, faster, and more comfortable than ever.

Same-Day Crowns (CEREC)

Forget multiple visits—our in-house CEREC technology allows us to design, craft, and place custom ceramic crowns in just one appointment.

Implants and CBCT Imaging

Our state-of-the-art CBCT scanner provides ultra-clear 3D imaging for everything from precision implant placement to sleep appliance design, enhancing both safety and outcomes.

3D Printing

With in-house 3D printers, we fabricate surgical guides, models, and prosthetics with exceptional accuracy—delivering better results, faster.

AI-Powered Cavity Detection

We use AI tools like Second Opinion to enhance diagnostic precision, helping us detect issues early and explain findings through easy-to-follow visuals.

Digital Impressions

No more goopy trays—digital scans provide quick, clean, and accurate models for creating aligners, crowns, and more.

Comfort Menu

Enjoy thoughtful touches like heated neck wraps, paraffin wax hand dips, and essential oils during hygiene visits.

Digital Patient Communication

With Modento, you’ll get text alerts, appointment reminders, and send-and-sign digital forms right from your phone—for less paperwork and more peace of mind.
