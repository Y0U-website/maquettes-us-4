The practice

To serve as a safety net health care provider for the residents of St. Joseph and Buchanan County living in poverty.

Routine primary care, chronic disease management, and specialty referrals.

Specialty health services, programs, and exams for both men and women.

We provide immigration medical exams for immigrants seeking legal residency.
