Dr. Reardon

Dr. Reardon is an Alabama native. He grew up in Montgomery, graduated from Auburn University and attended UAB for dental school. Upon graduation, Dr. Reardon moved to the Chattanooga area and has been practicing here ever since. He is very passionate about dentistry and has continued to advance his knowledge over the years through continuing education. He travels to Scottsdale, AZ often to learn from some of the top minds in dentistry at Spear Education and is going on his 5th year as the leader of a local dental study club.

Dr. Reardon has two children, Camp (10 years old) and Emily (8 years old) that keep him busy and active. They spend much of their free time outdoors where they enjoy mountain biking, backpacking, fly fishing and rock climbing and they always have an exciting adventure planned!
