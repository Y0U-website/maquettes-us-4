Advanced dental technology at Georgetown Dental Aesthetic in Washington, DC.

Dr. Alathari has integrated CAD/CAM and CEREC technology into daily practice since 2009. Cone Beam 3D imaging and in-house CAD/CAM milling support same-day crowns, accurate implant placement, and custom restorations with efficient turnaround.

He uses high-strength, biocompatible dental materials to support durable, aesthetic outcomes.
