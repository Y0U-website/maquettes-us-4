Dental Implants Technology

Cone-Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT)

Cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) is a revolutionary technology that significantly enhances dental care. It provides detailed 3-D images of a patient s oral and maxillofacial structures, including teeth, bones, nerves, and soft tissues.

With CBCT, our dentists can visualize a patient s dental anatomy from multiple angles, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of their oral health. This precision is particularly beneficial for dental implant placement, where exact positioning is critical for long-term success.

Additionally, CBCT is invaluable in the early detection of dental issues, like impacted teeth, root canal complications, and structural abnormalities. Early detection allows for timely intervention and more effective treatment.
