What Is the Same-Day Crown Process?

1. To create a strong and beautiful crown in one day, we start with a digital scan of your mouth with the latest technology in intraoral scanning.

2. Next, we will prepare your tooth and scan the new surface so we can design the new crown.

2. Once the crown is designed, our milling machine constructs your dental crown from biocompatible, durable porcelain in as little as 5-10 minutes!

3. Once your crown is complete, we will test fit it to the tooth.

4. The final steps include polishing your new crown, checking the fit and color, and bonding it to your natural tooth or dental implant.

That’s it! The entire process takes about 1.5-2 hrs but fully eliminates the need for a second visit or to wear a temporary crown.

Benefits of Same-Day Crowns

Other than the fact that you can have a new dental crown in one visit to Seymour Dental Associates, the other benefits of same-day crowns are as follows:

  • Tooth function If your tooth is broken or damaged, it can affect how you eat and talk. Same-day crowns give you back your tooth’s function.
  • Tooth preservation Because a same-day crown requires that we remove only a small amount of your natural tooth, most of your tooth structure is preserved.
  • Tooth functionality and comfort The precise technology used for same-day crowns will give you the same comfort, fit, and feel as your natural tooth.
  • Improved aesthetics Your same-day crown will look fantastic and blend seamlessly with the rest of your smile.
