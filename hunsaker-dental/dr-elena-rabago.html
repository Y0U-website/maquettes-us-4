Elena Rabago, DDS

Dr. Rabago was born and raised in Southern California. She graduated Cum Laude from La Sierra University in Riverside, CA with a major in Psychology and a minor in Biochemistry. Dr. Rabago earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at University of California, San Francisco.

Her love for the Green Bay Packers led her to move to Wisconsin. Despite enjoying life in the Midwest and eating a lot of cheese curds, Dr. Rabago wanted to live on the west coast, close to family and establish roots in PNW with her partner. They fell in love with the beautiful state of Oregon and moved to Salem in 2020, where they enjoy exploring the outdoors with their two dogs.

Dr. Rabago has always had a passion for helping others and went on many mission trips growing up. She constantly strives to provide a comfortable and personalized experience for each patient to ensure their expectations are met. She enjoys hiking, watching the Packers play, going to concerts, and traveling.
