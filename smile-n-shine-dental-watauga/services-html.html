Services

We offer a variety of services to promote oral health, prevent dental issues, and address dental problems. Routine Dental Exams and Cleanings: Regular check-ups to assess overall oral health. Professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar.

X-rays: Radiographic imaging to identify issues not visible during a regular exam.

Fluoride Treatments: Application of fluoride to strengthen tooth enamel and prevent cavities.

Dental Fillings: Repair of cavities with materials such as amalgam or composite resin.

Root Canal Therapy: Treatment to remove infected or damaged pulp from a tooth.

Tooth Extractions: Removal of a tooth, often due to severe decay, infection, or crowding.

Dental Crowns: Placement of a cap over a damaged tooth to restore its shape and function.

Bridges: Replacement of missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth to adjacent natural teeth.

Dentures: Removable appliances to replace missing teeth and surrounding tissues.

Dental Implants: Surgical placement of artificial tooth roots to support prosthetic teeth.

Orthodontic Services: Correction of misaligned teeth and jaws using braces or clear aligners.

Periodontal (Gum) Treatment: Management of gum diseases, including scaling and root planing.

Teeth Whitening: Cosmetic procedure to lighten the color of teeth.

Mouthguards: Custom-fitted appliances to protect teeth during sports or prevent teeth grinding.

Oral Cancer Screening: Examination for signs of oral cancer during routine check-ups.

Cosmetic Dentistry: Various procedures to enhance the appearance of teeth, such as veneers or bonding.

TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint) Treatment: Management of jaw joint and muscle disorders.

Conscious Sedation Dentistry: We offer nitrous oxide to help anxious patients relax during dental procedures.
