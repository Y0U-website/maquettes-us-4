Welcome To Smile n Shine Dental

At Smile n Shine, we’re more than just a dental practice; we’re a family dedicated to providing unparalleled dental care to our patients. Our mission is clear - to enhance the health and well-being of those we serve, promoting health and wellness through the highest quality healthcare.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Manjusha Tummala, lovingly known as Dr. T, brings over 15 years of experience in Dentistry and a heartfelt commitment to your oral well-being. The pursuit of her passion for dentistry brought her to the prestigious UCLA School of Dentistry in California, where she received exceptional training and honed her craft.

Driven by her desire to offer personalized care and foster lasting relationships with her patients, Dr. T decided to establish her own practice. She believes in being a true advocate for her patients and providing comprehensive, cutting-edge dental care. Her focus is unwavering - to deliver quality dentistry and offer tailor-made solutions to meet each patient’s unique needs.

Visit us to experience the warmth, compassion, and expertise of Dr. T and her dedicated team. Your radiant smile and well-being are in trusted hands with Dr. Manjusha Tummala - Your Caring Dentist.

Outside her dental practice, Dr. T is a loving wife and mother to a teenage son, sharing her home with a furry friend, her pet dog. She loves exploring different cultures, trying local food, and getting involved in the community.
