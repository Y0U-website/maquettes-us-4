Our Mission

​ At Vida Dental, we strive to treat all our patients with respect and without judgement. Our old-school approach to dentistry, powered by modern technology, means we deliver the best possible results without giving you the run-around for your treatment. As a locally-owned, non-corporate office, you will see one doctor and one assistant for your care. Dr. Jauregui takes the time to understand your goals and educate you on all of your options.

We’ll help you live your life to the fullest with a healthy and confident smile!
