Our tooth-colored fillings are made from biocompatible composite resins instead of silver amalgam. We understand that’s a mouthful! What this means is our fillings not only repair the damage done by cavities but also provide a much more natural look and feel.

Periodontal disease (also known as gum disease) can lead to bone loss. We have treatments available to help stop gum disease!

Dental implants are small metal posts that we implant directly into your jaw and under your gums. They are perfect replacement options because dental implants can also be used to secure other appliances, like bridges and dentures.
