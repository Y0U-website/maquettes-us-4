Dental Implants

  • How long does it take to get dental implants? The process of getting dental implants typically takes several months and involves multiple steps, including the initial consultation , implant placement, healing period, and final restoration . Your dental implants doctor will provide a detailed timeline based on your specific needs.
  • What is the success rate of dental implants? Dental implants are highly effective, boasting success rates as high as 98 %, according to various studies. With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime, providing a reliable and effective solution for tooth replacement .
