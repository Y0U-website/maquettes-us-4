The practice

We are a family-owned dental practice with over 25 years of experience with patients . We look forward to warm, welcoming, and personal relationships with all of our future patients!

Dr. Steve Taranov is a General Dentist who first received his dentistry license in Ukraine in 1991. He graduated from NYU College of Dentistry and received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery license in 2000. Dr. Taranov has undergone extensive training in root canal treatment, implant placement, cosmetic, restorative and prosthetic work.

After more than 20 years practicing in New York City, Dr. Taranov is taking his skills, expertise, and passion to Wilmington, North Carolina where he moved with his wife, who is a Dental hygienist. He is excited to start a new chapter in dentistry here.

Dr Taranov prides himself on treating his patients as a whole, using the best materials to achieve beautiful and functional results unique to each person.

His patients describe him as friendly, compassionate and meticulous. He tries to make everyone who visits him as comfortable as possible, knowing that not everyone enjoys a trip to the Dentist. Dr Steve Taranov always takes his time to listen to the patients and then to explain the procedures and present comprehensive treatment choices to the patients.

He always strives to minimize any discomfort during dental procedures. His focus is on exercising his knowledge and skills to improve his patients oral health and resolve their dental problems .

We use the latest top-of-the-line dental instruments, and digital x-rays that reduce radiation.

We accept most insurance plans such as Delta Dental, GEHA, Aetna, Cigna, Guardian, Metlife, etc.
