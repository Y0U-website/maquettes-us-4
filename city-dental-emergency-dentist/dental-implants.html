What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, typically made from biocompatible titanium, surgically placed into the jawbone. They serve as a sturdy foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth that are designed to match your natural teeth in both appearance and function.

  • Durability : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Convenience : No need to remove for cleaning; maintain them as you would your natural teeth.

The Dental Implant Process at City Dental

Comprehensive Consultation

  • Medical History Review : Understanding your overall health to ensure you’re a good candidate.

Implant Placement

Using precise surgical techniques, the implant is carefully placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Dr. Taranov’s expertise ensures minimal discomfort and optimal positioning. The procedure involves:

  • Making a small incision in the gum tissue to expose the bone.
  • Drilling a tiny hole into the jawbone where the implant will be placed.
  • Inserting the titanium implant, which serves as the new tooth root.
  • Closing the gum tissue over the implant to allow for healing.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration . This natural healing process creates a solid foundation for your new tooth. During this time:

  • Bone cells grow and integrate with the implant surface.
  • You’ll receive guidance on caring for the implant site to promote proper healing.
  • Regular check-ups ensure the implant is integrating successfully.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant has securely fused with the bone, a minor procedure is performed to attach an abutment:

  • The gum tissue is reopened to expose the implant.
  • An abutment—a small connector piece—is attached to the implant.
  • The gums are allowed to heal around the abutment, not over it, ensuring easy access for the crown placement.

Crown Fabrication and Placement

The final step involves crafting and placing the artificial tooth:

  • Impressions of your mouth are taken to create a custom-made crown.
  • The crown is designed to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth for a seamless look.
  • The crown is securely attached to the abutment, completing your smile restoration.
