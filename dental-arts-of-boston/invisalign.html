Straighten Your Teeth Discreetly With Invisalign in Boston

If you’ve always wanted straighter teeth but dreaded the idea of metal braces, Invisalign is the answer. This clear aligner system gently moves your teeth into place using a series of custom, nearly invisible trays — no brackets, no wires. For busy Back Bay professionals, students, and anyone who values a discreet option, it’s a comfortable, modern way to transform a smile. Invisalign can:

  • Straighten crowded, crooked, or rotated teeth
  • Close gaps between teeth
  • Correct many common bite issues
  • Be removed to eat your favorite foods and brush normally
  • Improve your smile without anyone noticing you’re in treatment

See Your New Smile Before You Start

Ever wondered what straighter teeth would look like on you? Take a quick selfie with the Smile View tool and preview your Invisalign results in seconds.

See Your New Smile How Invisalign Works at Dental Arts of Boston It starts with a consultation and digital scan. We map out your custom treatment plan and create a series of aligners, each worn for a couple of weeks before moving to the next. You’ll wear them about 20–22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink anything but water, and brush. Because the trays are smooth and custom-fit, most patients find them far more comfortable than braces.

Throughout treatment we monitor your progress to keep everything on track. When you’re finished, we’ll discuss retainers to protect your beautiful new smile — and if you’re considering other cosmetic touches like whitening or bonding, aligner treatment is a great time to plan them.
