Unlock Your Dream Smile With Cosmetic Dental Care

Investing in your smile through cosmetic care can change your life. At Dental Arts of Boston, your dentist will focus on your unique needs. Our team will take time to understand your smile goals. Plus, your dentist will use the latest technology, such as digital X-rays and digital scans, to plan your treatment.

Your dentist will begin by discussing the changes you’d like to see in your appearance . Our team will ensure that your treatments meet your vision for your ideal smile. You’ll never feel rushed or pressured. We’re here to guide you to a smile you’ll love showing off. Plus, they will check your oral health to ensure you are ready for cosmetic procedures.

Call 857-376-6559 for an appointment with your cosmetic dentist in Boston. You can also request an appointment online .

  • Dental Veneers – Veneers are thin shells that cover your teeth to fix chips, stains, or gaps. Prepless veneers need little to no tooth alteration. Standard veneers involve some enamel removal for a perfect fit.
  • Teeth Whitening – Brighten your smile quickly in our office or gradually at home. In-office treatments offer fast results, while at-home kits let you whiten on your schedule. Both will give you outstanding results.
  • Tooth Bonding – Your dentist will apply tooth-colored resin to repair small chips or gaps. It’s a quick, painless way to improve your smile’s look. Treatment takes about one appointment.
  • Smile Makeover – If you want to make several changes, a makeover is a custom plan combining various treatments to transform your smile. Your dentist will use a coordinated approach to address multiple concerns.
  • Gummy Smile Treatment – Using laser technology, your dentist can reshape gum tissue to show more of your teeth. It’s a gentle way to balance your smile. You’ll experience minimal to no discomfort during the procedure.
  • Clear Aligners – Straighten your teeth discreetly with ClearCorrect or Invisalign. You can take off your aligners for eating, drinking, or cleaning your teeth, making your orthodontic journey easier. You can expect to finish treatment in about 12 to 18 months.

Address Your Dental Health Concerns for a Healthy Start

Your smile’s beauty begins with good health. During your cosmetic consultation, your dentist may spot signs of infection or damage. If so, we’re here to help. Treating these issues is crucial before enhancing your smile’s appearance. Here are some treatments we might recommend:

Remember, addressing these issues not only improves your oral health but also ensures the success of any cosmetic treatments. At Dental Arts of Boston, we’re committed to giving you a smile that’s not just beautiful, but healthy too.

  • Root Canal Therapy – A root canal can save your smile. Your dentist will gently remove the infected pulp, clean the area, and seal it to prevent future problems.
  • Dental Crowns – For teeth with severe damage, a crown acts like a protective cap. It restores your tooth’s shape, size, and strength.
  • Gum Disease Treatment – Healthy gums are the foundation of a beautiful smile. We offer deep cleaning and laser therapy to treat gum inflammation and infection.
  • Tooth-Colored Fillings – Small cavities can be fixed with natural-looking composite fillings. They blend seamlessly with your teeth while stopping decay in its tracks.

Cosmetic dentistry has the power to transform your smile. We offer many beneficial treatments that can repair and beautify any smile, no matter the condition. We can repair teeth that are chipped, cracked, pitted, worn, uneven, stained, crooked, or misshapen to give you a beautiful smile. The options are nearly endless.

Cosmetic dentistry covers a wide range of services, and each patient has different needs and goals. Because of this, it isn’t possible to give you a price on your cosmetic treatment without first performing an exam and working up a treatment plan. Once we know exactly what you want and need, we can give you an estimate on your smile investment.

The permanence of cosmetic dentistry depends on the specific procedure and maintenance routine. Some treatments, like dental crowns or veneers, can provide durable results for many years with proper care. Other procedures, such as teeth whitening, will require periodic touch-ups to maintain the desired results. Your dentist will discuss the longevity and maintenance requirements of each cosmetic dentistry option with during your consultation so you’ll have a clear understanding of the expected duration.
