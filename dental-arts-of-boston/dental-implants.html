Restore Your Smile Keep Your Jaw Strong

Made of durable titanium, implants function similar to your own tooth roots. Your dentist will surgically place them in your jawbone. Then, the implants will fuse with the bone in a process known as osseointegration. This builds a strong and stable foundation for your replacement teeth. Not only will you enjoy more of the foods you love again, you’ll also avoid bone loss that often accompanies missing teeth.

Your dentist will work closely with you to develop a custom treatment plan, using advanced technology such as CBCT equipment and digital X-rays. You can have your entire implant treatment done here in our office – from implant placement and any necessary surgery to the restoration with new teeth.

  • Implant Crowns – These are ideal if you’re missing just one tooth. A single implant pairs with the crown to close the gap. Your restoration looks and functions like a natural tooth. You can care for it just as you would your other teeth.
  • Implant Bridges – If you’re missing several teeth in a row, your dentist can place an implant-supported bridge. Instead of using nearby teeth as anchors, your bridge is supported by dental implants. This keeps the surrounding teeth intact. It’s a more secure and durable solution.
  • Implant Dentures – If you’re missing an entire arch of teeth, implant-supported dentures are a great option. These dentures are anchored to implants, providing a more stable fit than traditional removable dentures. You won’t need messy adhesives, and you’ll enjoy greater comfort when eating and speaking.
  • All-on-4 (or 6) Implants – This full-arch solution requires only four (or six) implants per jaw. It allows you to restore your smile with fewer implants and a shorter recovery time.
  • Same-Day Smile – Your dentist can place your implants and temporary restoration in a single visit. This allows you to achieve your new smile in less time. You’ll leave our office feeling confident as ever about your appearance and enjoying oral function.
  • Full Mouth Reconstruction – If you need more extensive work, a full mouth reconstruction plan combines various restorative options , including dental implants, to rebuild your entire smile. Your dentist will discuss your specific needs and recommend the best combination of treatments to give you optimal results.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

To ensure your implants remain in top condition and serve you well for years to come, you’ll want to keep up with a diligent oral care routine. Here are some key practices to incorporate into your daily life:

By following these guidelines, you’re not just maintaining your implants. You’re investing in a lasting, confident smile. Remember, a little extra care goes a long way in preserving your oral health and the longevity of your dental implants.

  • Commit to thorough brushing twice daily, using a gentle touch with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride-rich toothpaste.
  • Make flossing a non-negotiable part of your routine. This daily habit helps eliminate plaque buildup and food particles that can compromise your implants.
  • For optimal implant health, consider cutting back on alcohol and quitting smoking. These lifestyle changes can significantly reduce the risk of complications.
  • Don’t skip your regular dental check-ups. These appointments allow your dentist to assess your implants’ condition and address any potential issues early on.

Dental implants are small posts made from titanium that your dentist inserts into your jawbone to serve as replacement teeth roots. If you are missing one or more teeth, implants function as a base for several types of new replacement teeth, including crowns, bridges, dentures, and full arches of fixed teeth.

Having a dental implant placed is not painful because your jaw will be numbed, plus you can stay completely relaxed with dental sedation throughout your procedure. Once you go home, you might require pain relievers for a day or two, but most of our patients report only taking over-the-counter medication following their dental implant procedure. Most feel well enough to return to work the day after.

If you are missing any number of teeth, you owe it to yourself to consider getting implants. They are designed to function as artificial roots for your replacement teeth. Implants make crowns, bridges, and dentures more secure. They prevent bone loss by stimulating your jaw, and they restore your biting power so you can eat anything you want.
