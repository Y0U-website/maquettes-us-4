Design a Flawless Smile With Veneers in Boston

You deserve a smile you’re proud to show off. Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells that bond to the front of your teeth, instantly correcting the flaws that years of wear, staining, and minor damage leave behind. At Dental Arts of Boston, every veneer is individually designed for a natural, radiant result. Veneers can:

  • Cover stubborn discoloration that won’t respond to whitening
  • Repair chips, cracks, and worn edges
  • Close small gaps and improve the shape of your teeth
  • Create even, symmetrical proportions across your smile
  • Give you a camera-ready smile that lasts for years

Choose the Right Veneers for Your Smile

When you choose veneers with us, you’re not getting a one-size-fits-all result. During your consultation, your dentist reviews the options best suited to your goals. e.max veneers, made from strong lithium disilicate, closely mimic natural enamel and offer outstanding durability — a favorite among our patients. Minimal-prep veneers are an ultra-thin, more conservative choice that preserves more of your natural tooth structure.

The process is comfortable and efficient. We capture digital impressions, design your veneers around your facial features, and typically complete treatment in about two visits. Many patients choose to whiten first so the entire smile looks its brightest.

Not when they’re well made. Porcelain shares the translucency of natural enamel, and precise design lets veneers sit naturally against your gumline — so the result looks like your own beautiful teeth.

The process is comfortable. If a small amount of enamel needs to be removed, we use local anesthetic, and sedation is available if you’re nervous.

Porcelain doesn’t respond to whitening the way natural teeth do, so we recommend whitening before your veneers are made, then matching them to your brightest shade. Touch-ups keep the rest of your smile in sync.
