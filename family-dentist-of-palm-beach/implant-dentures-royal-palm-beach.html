Implant-supported denture questions, answered

All-on-4 is a technique that anchors a full arch of teeth to just four dental implants. It provides a secure, natural-feeling result — often with a same-day temporary — without needing an implant for every tooth.

Most implant-supported dentures use between two and six implants per arch. A snap-in (removable) overdenture often uses two to four, while a fixed full-arch bridge typically uses four to six. We recommend the right number after imaging.

Both options exist. Snap-in overdentures clip onto the implants and are removed for cleaning, while fixed full-arch dentures stay in permanently and are cleaned like natural teeth. We help you choose based on comfort and budget.

Coverage varies — some plans contribute toward the denture or the implants. We verify your benefits before treatment, provide a written estimate, and offer in-house financing to make treatment affordable.
