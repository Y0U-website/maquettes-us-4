Dental implant questions, answered

A single dental implant at our Royal Palm Beach office typically ranges from $1,750 to $3,600 depending on whether a bone graft is needed. Free consultations include a full treatment plan and written cost breakdown, and in-house financing is available for qualified patients.

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post fuses with your jawbone, and only the crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years of normal wear.

The full process is completed over 2–3 visits across 3–6 months, allowing time for the implant to fuse with the bone before the final crown is placed. Same-day temporary solutions are available so you never go without a tooth.

Many dental plans cover a portion of implant treatment, especially the crown. We verify your benefits before treatment and give you a written estimate. In-house financing covers the remainder for qualified patients.
