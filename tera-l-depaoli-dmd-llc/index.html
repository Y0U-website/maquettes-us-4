The practice

Tera L. DePaoli, DMD has been a general dentist since 2005. Dr. DePaoli completed an associate’s degree from Hagerstown Community College in 1999 and continued her studies at Clarion University of Pennsylvania where she received her Bachelor’s degree in 2001. In 2005 she received her doctorate of dental medicine from The University of Pittsburgh, School of Dental Medicine.

Once graduated, Dr. DePaoli completed a one-year hospital-based residency program at St. Elizabeth’s Health Center in Youngstown, Ohio.

She is currently a member of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, and Dental Society of Western Pennsylvania.

Dr. DePaoli and her husband have two daughters, Ella and Elisia, twin boys, Domenic and Dante, and reside in Mars, PA. Dr. DePaoli’s husband is a Team Sales Account Manager for Dick’s Team Sports HQ.

Camilyn Clemenza, DMD grew up in the small town of Hermitage, Pennsylvania. She obtained a Bachelor of Arts degree from Washington Jefferson College, with a major in Biochemistry. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Pittsburgh, School of Dental Medicine.

Following dental school, she completed a general practice residency at St. Elizabeth’s Youngstown Hospital, located in Youngstown, Ohio. During her residency she received additional training in all aspects of dentistry. She is well equipped to treat a myriad of dental concerns on patients of all ages.

In her free time, Dr. Clemenza enjoys spending time with her family, including her four month old daughter and yellow Labrador retriever. She also likes to cook, hike, and spend time outdoors. Meet Our Staff Jillian Dental Hygienist

Jillian is a 2002 graduate of the University of Pittsburgh. She is a wife and mother of 2 daughters and resides in Butler county. She has been happily working with Dr. DePaoli since January 2011; as we continue to grow as a practice and family. She enjoys forming relationships with her patients and helping them with their needs.
