Our Office

Our Office In Canby

Our neighborhood office located in Canby, Oregon is designed to provide you the highest quality care in a friendly and comfortable environment. We pride ourselves on staying up-to-date with the latest technology, utilizing digital x-rays, records, photographs, and our iTero scanner to effectively communicate with you and your dentist or other medical providers upon request.

We are conveniently located in downtown Canby, across from Wait Park and William Knight Elementary School. The office is also easily accessible for residents of: Oregon City, Wilsonville, Woodburn, Molalla, Aurora, Hubbard, Silverton, Keizer, and Salem.
