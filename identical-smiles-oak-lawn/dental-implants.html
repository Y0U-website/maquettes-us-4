Specialized post-op care instructions might be needed if you have a sinus lift or dental bone grafts at the same time as your tooth implant surgery.

  • Using an ice pack on the outside of your face can help to reduce swelling and manage pain. We recommend 15 minutes on and 15 minutes off during the first 24 hours after your implant surgery.
  • Do not rinse, spit forcefully, or use a straw for the first few days.
  • Avoid any strenuous activities for 2 days. Allow yourself time to rest.
  • Only eat soft foods, such as smoothies, scrambled eggs, mashed potatoes, soup, and yogurt for a few days. Don’t drink any hot liquids during this time (warm temperatures are ok).
  • Don’t chew on the side of the mouth where the implant is located until you have clearance from your dentist.
  • After the first 24 hours are over, you can gently rinse your mouth with warm salt water. Be careful about brushing around the tooth replacement site because you need to avoid any disturbance to the surgical care.
  • Don’t use any tobacco products during the healing time, including cigarettes, chewing tobacco, or vapes, since these things can slow your healing and increase the risk of implant failure.
  • Contact our office right away if the swelling, pain, or bleeding gets worse after the first few days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it possible to place dental implants on the same day as tooth extraction?

Yes, if you have the right bone and gum conditions, then it might be possible to schedule an implant on the same day as the extraction. Each patient is unique, which is why we will map out your individual treatment plan in advance.

What happens if I don’t have enough bone to hold the implant?

Our bone graft dentist can help with personalized procedures to rebuild the bone and support your dental implants. These treatments can often be combined, with a sinus lift and/or bone graft to support your restorations.

Are dental implants permanent?

Yes, with proper placement and consistent care, dental implants are designed to last for many years. Dental implants are considered a permanent restoration and can often last a lifetime for patients with good oral health.

What type of restoration will be used with my dental implants?

Each patient is unique, which is why we customize the restorations based on your smile goals. Our team at Identical Smiles offers everything from a single tooth replacement to full dental implants and more. Contact us for an evaluation and personalized recommendations.
