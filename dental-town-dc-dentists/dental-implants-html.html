What are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small post made of titanium that is surgically placed into the jawbone in the space left by a missing tooth. There, it forms a bond with the bone (osseointegration), making it an extremely strong foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. They essentially become brand new tooth roots.

The 4 Step Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Dental Implant Consultation: The first step is to meet your dentist, discuss your goals, and have your mouth scanned. This will reveal all of your options and help determine if you’re a good fit for implants.
  • Dental Implant Surgery: If you decide to proceed, you’ll be referred to a local specialist to have your implants placed. We only partner with the finest experts in town to ensure you enjoy a seamless experience and a successful procedure.
  • Healing Period: Your implants will need about three to six months to bond with your jaw, and once your mouth is ready, we’ll attach abutments to your implants. These serve as connectors between them and your new teeth.
  • Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s): The big day has arrived! We’ll attach your new teeth and make a few final adjustments. The last step is making sure you love the result you see in the mirror.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Longevity: Nothing lasts as long as dental implants. They have a 20+ year lifespan on average, multiple times longer compared to traditional bridges and dentures.
  • Strong: While regular dentures restore about 20 % of a patient’s bite force, implants can bring back around 70 %, leading to easier chewing and a more varied diet.
  • Aesthetically Superior: High-quality porcelain restorations sit in the mouth like real teeth, no obvious metal clips or thick bases that stand out.
