Dr. Eric Davis

Growing up in Southampton, New York, Dr. Davis was inspired by a family legacy in dentistry and developed a passion for helping others feel confident in their smiles. After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University with honors in implantology, he completed a rigorous general practice residency in Brooklyn, where he refined his skills in full-mouth rehabilitation, implant dentistry, and cosmetic design.

Dr. Davis is Kois-trained and holds honors in implantology, with advanced training in veneers, digital workflows, and AI-powered diagnostics. His love for aesthetics and cutting-edge tech led him to launch Davis Dental Aesthetics, a boutique practice that reflects his belief that dentistry should be personalized, empowering, and design-forward.

With a special interest in no-prep and ultra-thin porcelain veneers and smile makeovers, Dr. Davis uses precision and artistry to create results that are natural, timeless, and uniquely tailored to each patient. He’s also a certified Invisalign provider and leverages digital smile design tools so you can preview your transformation before it begins.

Approachable, confident, and always detail-focused, Dr. Davis is committed to offering patients a luxury-level experience where trust, innovation, and aesthetic excellence converge.

Outside of the office, Dr. Davis enjoys traveling and staying active, whether training for IRONMAN competitions or playing competitive soccer. Most of all, he values spending quality time with his wife, Kelly, their son, Callum, and their golden retriever, Nash.

Full digital diagnostics

Your visit includes high-end studio photography, 3D scanning with TRIOS or iTero, a CBCT scan (when needed), and comprehensive X-rays. This technology gives us a complete, accurate picture of your oral health and helps us build a custom treatment plan just for you.

Smile preview & treatment planning

Using advanced smile design software, Dr. Davis will create a mock-up of what your new smile could look like so you can envision your results before committing to treatment. You’ll then meet with our treatment coordinator to review your options, costs, and next steps.
