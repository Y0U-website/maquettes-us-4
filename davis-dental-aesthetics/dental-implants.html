What are dental implants?

Dental implants are permanent replacements for missing teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They are ideal for anyone who has lost a tooth due to decay, injury, or gum disease. Dental implants are a form of restorative dentistry in Lake Success, NY , that improve your confidence and oral health by supporting your bite and preventing future bone loss.

Types of dental implants

A single implant is used to replace an individual missing tooth, restoring both function and aesthetics. Dr. Davis creates a custom implant crown designed to blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth for a natural result. This solution prevents bone loss and prevents shifting of neighboring teeth. Patients appreciate that the restoration feels completely natural and requires no special maintenance beyond routine care.

Implant-supported bridges are ideal for replacing multiple missing teeth in a row. Instead of relying on natural teeth for support, the bridge is anchored securely to dental implants for improved strength and longevity. This approach protects healthy teeth from being shaved down and offers a more stable alternative compared to traditional bridges. Dr. Davis designs each implant bridge to support your bite and blend beautifully with your smile.

Implant-retained dentures use a small number of implants to secure your denture in place, giving you greater stability and comfort compared to removable dentures. They reduce slipping and clicking and allow for improved chewing power and speech. Dr. Davis works closely with you to create a comfortable, natural-looking smile that enhances your everyday quality of life.

Full-arch implant solutions replace an entire arch of missing or failing teeth using strategically placed implants. This option provides a fixed, life-changing alternative to removable dentures. Full-arch implants restore full function, look incredibly natural, and maintain the health of the jawbone. Dr. Davis partners with trusted specialists for surgical placement while designing the final restoration for an aesthetic, functional result.

The dental implant process

Consultation and digital evaluation

Dr. Davis begins with a comprehensive exam, digital scans, and imaging to determine if dental implants are the right option for you. He takes time to understand your goals and explain your treatment pathway.

Implant placement

A trusted surgical specialist places the implant into the jawbone. This implant post replaces the tooth root and provides a stable foundation for the final crown or bridge. Healing time varies, but most patients are comfortable throughout the process.

Custom restoration

Once the implant has integrated with the bone, Dr. Davis designs and places a custom crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration. The result is a natural-looking, durable tooth replacement that enhances your smile and confidence.

Frequently asked questions

We’re here to help you feel informed, empowered, and confident in your care.

Most patients experience very little discomfort during the implant process. Local anesthesia keeps you comfortable during the procedure, and most people report that recovery feels similar to having a tooth extracted. Our team provides detailed aftercare instructions to ensure you heal smoothly.

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution. With proper oral hygiene and routine dental visits, the implant post can last several decades. The restoration on top may need replacement over time due to normal wear, but the underlying implant is incredibly durable.

Most healthy adults with enough jawbone density are good candidates for implants. Even if you have bone loss, treatments like bone grafting may still make implants possible. Dr. Davis will evaluate your oral health and discuss all your options during your consultation.
