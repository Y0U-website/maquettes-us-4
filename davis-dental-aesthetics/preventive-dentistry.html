The benefits of preventive dentistry

Preventive care helps maintain your smile’s health, beauty, and function, saving you time, money, and stress in the long run. With regular visits and digital diagnostics, we’re able to catch small issues before they become big problems.

Maintain long-term oral health Preventive care reduces the need for an emergency dentist in Lake Success , as well as your risk of decay, gum disease, and tooth loss, helping you keep your natural teeth for life.

Save time and money on future treatments Catching issues early prevents costly procedures and long recovery times down the line.

Enjoy a stress-free experience Our digital tools, personalized approach, and relaxing amenities make routine care something to look forward to.

Our preventive dentistry treatments

Every preventive visit begins with a comprehensive dental exam focused on whole-mouth health. Dr. Davis takes the time to understand your history, lifestyle, and long-term goals. He’ll perform a detailed visual exam of your teeth, gums, and bite, looking for signs of decay, gum disease, or functional issues. This personalized, unhurried approach ensures that no detail is missed and that you feel fully informed and in control of your care.

Our advanced diagnostic suite includes iTero, TRIOS, CBCT imaging, and the groundbreaking Diagnocat AI platform. These tools allow us to capture high-resolution 3D images of your teeth, jaw, and bite with incredible accuracy. Diagnocat AI adds another layer of precision by using artificial intelligence to analyze X-rays and scans, flagging potential issues even before they become visible to the eye. This cutting-edge technology means earlier detection, smarter treatment planning, and more confident care.

Our cleanings are designed to be thorough, comfortable, and tailored to your needs. We gently remove plaque and tartar, polish the teeth, and provide recommendations based on your lifestyle and goals. Combined with our luxury comforts and high-touch care, these cleanings feel more like self-care than a chore.

Sealants are a fast, effective way to protect the deep grooves of molars that are prone to trapping bacteria. We apply a thin, durable coating to the chewing surfaces of your back teeth, creating a smooth, easy-to-clean surface that resists cavities. Sealants are a non-invasive treatment that provide long-term protection with zero discomfort.

The preventive dentistry process

Concierge check-in and comfort setup

You’ll be welcomed by our concierge, offered your choice from our comfort menu, and guided through a seamless check-in process using our iPad system.

Complete exam and digital diagnostics

We’ll take digital scans, studio photos, and any needed X-rays or CBCT imaging. Dr. Davis performs a thorough exam and discusses findings with you clearly and transparently.

Personalized plan and ongoing support

If any issues are found, we’ll create a tailored plan that prioritizes your goals, schedule, and lifestyle. If everything looks great, we’ll set you up for your next visit and send you home smiling.
