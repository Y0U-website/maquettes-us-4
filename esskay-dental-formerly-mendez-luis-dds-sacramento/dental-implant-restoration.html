Who Is a Candidate?

Ideal for patients whose implant fixtures are fully integrated but need new crowns or abutments, our process ensures you’re free of active gum disease, maintain good oral hygiene, and seek to improve the fit, function, or appearance of your existing implant restorations.

Our Implant Restoration Process

  • Consultation & Imaging: We review your history, then take CBCT scans to evaluate implant position and bone support.
  • Occlusal Analysis: A detailed bite assessment ensures balanced force distribution across your restoration.
  • Custom Design: Using CAD/CAM technology, we craft abutments and crowns that match your anatomy and smile.
  • Trial & Adjustment: We verify fit, shade, and occlusion, making refinements for optimal comfort.
  • Final Placement: Your new restoration is secured, and we confirm seamless integration with adjacent teeth.
