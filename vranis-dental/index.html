General and Aesthetic Dentist

Washington D.C. native, Pamela Vranis, DDS, is a general dentist with a focus in restorative and aesthetic dentistry.

The practice

Dr. Vranis attended the Johns Hopkins University where she majored in Neuroscience and was a 4-year varsity athlete on the women’s soccer team and captain her junior and senior years.

She went on to earn her DDS at the University of Maryland, School of Dentistry where she was selected to be one of three students in her graduating class to be a part of the Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program for her final year.

Since then, Dr. Vranis has been practicing in the Washington, D.C. area, where she has tailored her practice to treat every patient individually with a detailed approach. She is incredibly passionate about building strong bonds with her patients, improving oral health and creating the best possible functional and aesthetic outcomes.
