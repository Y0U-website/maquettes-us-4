Comprehensive Dental Services for the Whole Family

At David M. Huffaker, DDS PLLC, we offer a wide range of services to keep your family’s smiles healthy and bright. From teeth whitening to routine check-ups, we specialize in ensuring that your dental needs are met with expert care.

Proactive Treatments for Lifelong Oral Health

Exams & Cleanings

Regular dental exams and cleanings are essential to keep your teeth healthy and detect any dental issues early. The ADA recommends dental checkups every 6 months.

TOOTH-COLORED FILLINGS

Repair and preserve the health of your teeth with white composite resin fillings, shade-matched to your natural tooth color.

CROWNS

Protect your natural tooth after extensive decay or root canal therapy with a BruxZir Zirconia crown, beautiful and long-lasting.

X-rays

Digital X-rays with the latest AI integrated overlay software to accurately detect cavities, bone loss, radiographic calculus, and more for efficient treatment.

Sealants

Especially effective in children, sealants act as a protective barrier for molar teeth helping to prevent cavities.

Dental Fluoride

Help prevent decay and strengthen your teeth with regular fluoride treatments that contribute to long-term oral health.

IMPANTS

Once the dental implant has integrated, restore your tooth with a strong, natural-looking, BruxZir Zirconia implant crown and abutment.

BRIDGES

Restore your full smile and improve your oral health with a dental bridge on natural teeth or implants.

Night Guard

Prevent teeth grinding and lessen jaw tension with a custom made nightguard.

WHITENING

Brighten your smile with custom made whitening trays or purchase a whitening kit from our office.

HOME CARE EDUCATION

Our hygienists help you achieve the very best home care routine with their compassionate and comprehensive hygiene education.

SPECIALISTS

We pride ourselves on having excellent working relationships with local dental specialists to ensure that you receive the highest level of dental care available.
