Dr. David M. Huffaker

Dr. David M. Huffaker was raised in Centerville, Utah, the second of six children to Dr. Todd and Maureen Huffaker. After observing a dentist in Bogota, Colombia restore a friend’s aching tooth, Dr. Huffaker determined to learn how to help others overcome thier dental concerns.

Dr. Huffaker is married to Chelsea Hinckley, also a Davis County native. They have four beautiful children who keep them happily busy. He enjoys spending time with his family traveling, exploring new places, attending local cultural events, trying out new restaurants, and relaxing with a good movie.
