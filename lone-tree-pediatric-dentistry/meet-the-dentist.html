More About Dr. Nick

Following college, Dr. Nick attended Boston University’s Goldman School of Dental Medicine. In addition to his commitment to his dental education, Dr. Nick engrossed himself in many extra-curricular activities. These activities included being an executive member of the American Student Dental Association chapter, establishing a dental sealant program at inner-city elementary schools and volunteering his time and dental services at a dental clinic in Nicaragua.

After over 20 years of dental education and work experience, plus moving between two different states, Dr. Nick and his family are happy to call Lone Tree home. Dr. Nick is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. As a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist, Dr. Nick is committed to high quality, compassionate and conservative dental care for your child. Dr. Nick is incredibly proud of Lone Tree Pediatric Dentistry, its team, and our reputation in the community. He is looking forward to creating lasting relationships with the patients and parents of the practice.
