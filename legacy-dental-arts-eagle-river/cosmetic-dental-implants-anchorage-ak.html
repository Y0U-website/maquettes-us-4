What Are Cosmetic Dental Implants?

Cosmetic dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Unlike removable dentures or bridges, implants use a biocompatible titanium post placed into the jawbone to create a stable, lasting foundation. A custom ceramic crown is then attached on top to match your natural teeth. The result is a restoration that can blend seamlessly with your smile while also helping preserve jawbone density over time.

Key Benefits of Cosmetic Dental Implants

Cosmetic dental implants may offer advantages that other tooth replacement options often cannot match. Patients in the Anchorage area typically report meaningful improvements across several areas of daily life. Here are five benefits that set implants apart from other solutions:

▸ Natural Appearance: Our implant crowns are custom-crafted to match the color and shape of your surrounding teeth, creating a seamless, natural-looking result.

▸ Jawbone Preservation: Because the titanium post integrates with the jaw, implants may help prevent the bone loss that often follows tooth extraction.

▸ Long-Term Durability: With proper care, dental implants are designed to last many years and can withstand normal chewing forces like natural teeth.

▸ No Damage to Adjacent Teeth: Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require shaving down neighboring healthy teeth for placement.

▸ Improved Confidence: Patients often find that a restored, stable smile can make everyday activities like eating, speaking, and laughing feel more comfortable and enjoyable.

How the Implant Process Works at Legacy Dental Arts

Patients can generally expect the full implant process to span several months. Here is what the journey with our team typically looks like:

1. Consultation and Planning: We review your oral health, take imaging, and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

2. Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Sedation options may be available for patients who prefer a more relaxed experience.

3. Healing and Integration: Over the following months, the bone grows around the post in a process called osseointegration, creating a firm anchor.

4. Abutment Attachment: Once healing is confirmed, a small connector piece is attached to the post to prepare for the final restoration.

5. Crown Placement: A custom ceramic crown is secured to the abutment, completing your new tooth.
