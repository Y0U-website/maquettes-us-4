FAQs

  • What Are Dental Implants, and How Do They Work? We place a titanium post into your jaw as an artificial root. Over several months, the bone grows around that post (osseointegration), anchoring it firmly. Then we attach an abutment and a crown or bridge to restore your tooth. Dental implants in Anchorage, AK, from Legacy Dental Arts feel like natural teeth and help preserve bone.
  • How Long Does It Take to Get a Tooth With an Implant? From planning and surgery to placing the final restoration, expect this process to take several months typically 3 to 6 months. The initial surgical healing and bone integration need time. Once fully healed, we attach your crown or prosthesis. The result is a durable, stable tooth replacement designed to last many years.
  • How Much Do Dental Implants Cost in Anchorage, AK? Cost depends on the complexity, number of implants, bone grafting needs, and type of restoration. We review your case in a consultation and provide a custom quote. We also help with financing options and insurance to make dental implants more affordable for our patients in the Anchorage area.
  • How Do We Care for Dental Implants Over Time? We ask patients to maintain excellent oral hygiene brushing gently, flossing around the implant, and using interdental brushes when needed. Regular checkups every six months allow us to examine and clean your implants. Avoiding habits like chewing ice or hard candy helps preserve implant integrity. With proper care, most dental implants last decades.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

  • Since dental implants are installed by fusing artificial material directly with your jawbone as a foundation, they are rock solid. Implants will provide a spot for us to attach replacement teeth as needed.
  • Since the basis of dental implants is this foundation system, you have the flexibility to install both permanent and removable replacement teeth there. Either way, they will be strongly attached because of the implant system, but you can choose the solution that best matches your lifestyle.
  • Unlike bridges that need to slightly wear down adjacent teeth to be installed, you can install implants without the need to minimize anything in the area.
  • Because implants are fitted so firmly in your jaw, they will look and function like a normal tooth. While other options might shift or appear artificial, implants are very close to authentic in every way.
