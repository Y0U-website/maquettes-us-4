Common Questions About Cosmetic and General Dentistry

your questions answered about our dental services

01. What types of cosmetic dental services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of cosmetic services, including teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, smile makeovers, and gum contouring to enhance the appearance of your smile.

02. How often should I have a dental check-up?

We generally recommend a dental check-up and cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health and detect any potential issues early. However, it is common to need cleanings every 3-4 months to maintain a healthy smile for some.

03. What are dental implants and how can they help me?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth. They can restore your smile’s function and aesthetics, and help prevent bone loss.

04. Do you offer any sedation options for anxious patients?

Yes, we understand that some patients experience dental anxiety. We offer various sedation options to help you feel comfortable and relaxed during your treatment. Please discuss your concerns with us.

05. How much do your cosmetic dental services cost?

The cost of cosmetic dental services varies depending on the specific treatment and individual needs. We will provide you with a detailed treatment plan and cost estimate during your consultation.

We accept a variety of payment options, including major credit cards, insurance, and financing plans. Please contact our office for more details.
