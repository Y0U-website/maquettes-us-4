Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics & Invisalign in Houston

01. How long does Invisalign treatment take? Treatment time varies for each individual, depending on the complexity of your case. On average, Invisalign treatment can range from 6 to 18 months, but some cases may be shorter or longer. We’ll provide an estimated timeline during your consultation.

02. Is Invisalign painful? When you first switch to a new set of aligners, you may experience some mild pressure or discomfort, which is a sign that your teeth are moving. This sensation is typically temporary and subsides within a day or two.

03. Can Invisalign fix all bite issues? Invisalign is effective for a wide range of orthodontic issues. While it can address most common alignment problems, some complex cases may still require traditional braces. A thorough evaluation at our Houston office will determine if Invisalign is right for you.

04. What’s the cost of Invisalign in Houston? The cost of Invisalign treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case and the length of treatment. We offer flexible payment options and can help you understand your insurance benefits during your consultation at our Houston practice.

05. Do I need a retainer after Invisalign? Yes, wearing a retainer after your Invisalign treatment is crucial to maintain your new smile. Retainers prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original positions, ensuring your results last a lifetime.
