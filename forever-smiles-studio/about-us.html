Welcome to My Dental Clinic, Where We Care for You Like Family

Hello, I’m Dr. Ailin Chao.

First of all, I want you to know something.

I fully understand that for many, visiting the dentist can cause nerves or even fear due to past experiences.

That’s why my main goal in creating Forever Smiles Studio was to create a safe, judgment-free space. You’re not here to be scolded, you’re here to be cared for.

The reason behind Forever Smiles Studio

I created Forever Smiles Studio out of a dream of having an intimate, trusting space where I could personally guide you through every treatment you truly need.

After working in larger settings, I felt like the personal touch and continuity of patient care was lost.

That’s why I decided to form a team I already fully trusted, each with extensive experience in their respective fields, and people who share my same values.

The goal has always been to build a clinic where your well-being is our sole and true priority.

Mission and the Values ​​that Guide Us

Our mission is simple: to always make you feel comfortable, heard, and safe. To achieve this, my team and I are guided by these core values:

Honesty

I will always speak to you with clarity and transparency.

Integrity

My recommendation will always be the best for your health.

Empathy

I understand that visiting the dentist can be anxiety-inducing, so my team and I will provide you with compassionate, gentle, and loving care.

Excellence

I am personally committed to providing you with the highest quality results.
