Why Pelham Drive

We chose 101 Pelham Dr because Waynesboro is home — and the families along this corridor deserve a dental practice that feels made for them, not dropped into the neighborhood. Many of the families you’ll see in our waiting room have lived within a few blocks of this office for two or three generations. That kind of community bond doesn’t show up everywhere. We’re proud to be part of it.

Dr. Sang Kim leads the practice with a quiet, attentive style and a clear promise: every patient leaves comfortable, informed, and confident in their care. We don’t just treat teeth — we care for the people behind every smile. That starts with how you’re greeted at the front desk and runs all the way through how a treatment plan is explained, scheduled, and paid for.

We’re open Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm — five full days a week, because shift workers, service-industry folks, parents, retirees, and small-business owners all keep different schedules. And when insurance is in the way of care, the Virginia Dental Club steps in. No insurance, no problem — that’s not a tagline; it’s how the practice is actually built.

We’re here for the long term. The families on Pelham Drive, in Stuarts Draft and Fishersville, in Lyndhurst and across the 22980 corridor — we’d be honored to be your dental home.

What you can expect from a visit on Pelham Drive

Walking into a new dental office can feel like a leap, especially if it has been a while since your last cleaning. We design every step of the visit so the leap feels small. From the first phone call to the final handshake, the goal is for you to leave feeling listened to, informed, and welcomed back.

Most first appointments at Willis Family Dentistry Waynesboro run about an hour. We start with a real conversation — what brought you in, your medical history, and any past dental experiences that did not work for you. Then a comprehensive exam, gentle digital X-rays only as needed, an oral cancer screening, and a professional cleaning when time allows. By the time you leave, you will know exactly what your mouth looks like, what (if anything) needs attention, what it costs, and how we would like to space it out.

Patients drive in to Pelham Drive from Stuarts Draft, Fishersville, Lyndhurst, downtown Waynesboro, and from across the 22980 corridor. Whether you live three blocks from the office or you are coming in from out of town, we aim to make the visit worth the trip — with a warm greeting, an unhurried appointment, and a treatment plan that fits your real life.

Willis Family Dentistry Waynesboro was built on a simple bet: that the families of this corridor deserve a calm, modern dental practice that treats them like neighbors, prices treatment honestly up front, and meets them where they are — insured or not, anxious or returning after years away. That bet has shaped everything from how the office is laid out to how the schedule runs to how every treatment plan is explained at the chair. We are honored to be your dental home, and we plan to be here for the long term.
