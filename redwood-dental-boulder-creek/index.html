The practice

Dr. Sulmaan Hassan combines technical precision with a deeply personal approach to dentistry. Having grown up with challenging dental experiences, he used to avoid the dentist himself, so he understands how negative experiences can shape the way people feel about dental care. He’s seen how this fear often leads to neglected oral health and is passionate about changing that cycle for his patients.

Dr. Hassan believes that a comfortable, positive visit can completely reshape how someone feels about taking care of their oral health. His philosophy centers on comfort, education, trust, and zero-judgment, ensuring every patient feels truly cared for from the moment they walk in. He has also assembled a team that shares these same values, creating an environment where patients feel supported…

After earning his DDS, MS, and BS from the University of the Pacific, Dr. Hassan completed advanced training in dental implants, clear aligners, and cosmetic dentistry, earning fellowships from both the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics.
