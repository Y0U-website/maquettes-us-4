Our Mission

At Redwood Dental, we believe a visit to the dentist should feel easy, empowering, and genuinely human. Our mission is simple: to educate through clear, simple language and empower you to take control of your oral health, supported by exceptional care and a team that truly works in sync.

We take time to listen, explain, and connect—so you never feel rushed, confused, or “sold to.” Every visit is built on empathy, honesty, and comfort, because we understand how past dental experiences can shape how people feel about care today.

Nestled among the redwoods of Boulder Creek, our office blends modern dentistry with warmth and authenticity. From the smallest details to the biggest results, our goal is to create a space where you feel heard, cared for, and confident—and to make sure you leave every visit thankful you came.

Get to Know Your Doctor

Meet Dr. Sulmaan Hassan

Dr. Sulmaan Hassan grew up in Saratoga, CA, and understands firsthand how impersonal dentistry can feel. As a child, he spent countless hours in the dental chair dealing with recurring cavities, TMJ disorder, and sleep apnea—and often left feeling unheard and overwhelmed.

He remembers being talked at with fingers in his mouth, being told he had invisible problems he somehow caused, and then facing a front desk bill for thousands of dollars. His teeth felt fine, but he left confused, anxious, and powerless.

No one ever explained the fundamentals of oral health or connected his habits to his recurring issues in a way that made sense. It wasn’t until he met a dentist who took the time to teach and empower him that everything changed. For the first time, he understood how to take control of his own oral health—and that moment became the foundation of his philosophy today.

Dr. Hassan set out to become the kind of dentist he always wished he’d had: one who listens, educates, and empowers patients to take control of their health. Zero confusion. Zero anxiety. Total confidence.

He completed his undergraduate and graduate education at the University of the Pacific, earning a Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry, a Master of Science in Biology, and a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS). He has since pursued advanced training in dental implants, clear aligners, and cosmetic dentistry - which he loves for their ability to transform both smiles and confidence. Dr. Hassan is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI) and the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics (FAAIP).

Outside the office, you’ll find him exploring new restaurants, cooking, playing pickleball, practicing yoga, or spending time with his family.

Get to Know Your Doctor

Dr. Mesk Samara

Dr. Mesk Samara’s journey into dentistry began in an underprivileged neighborhood in Chicago, where access to consistent healthcare and oral health education was limited. Growing up without guidance or preventive care, she saw first hand how lack of access can impact confidence and long-term health. Everything changed when she shadowed a dentist who prioritized education and empathy, helping her understand the “why” behind oral health and inspiring her to pursue dentistry with purpose.

Today, Dr. Samara is driven by a deep commitment to compassionate, patient-centered care. She believes in treating every patient like family and approaches dentistry as a balance of science and artistry, creating smiles that are healthy, natural, and thoughtfully designed.

She earned her undergraduate degree in Biological Sciences and her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Illinois at Chicago, where she also conducted research in biomaterials and salivary biomarkers. Her dedication to excellence and leadership in patient care earned her the prestigious Dr. Rice Scholarship.

Outside the office, she enjoys baking, horseback riding, hiking, and discovering new places in the community.

Comfort Meets Cutting-Edge Technology

We blend a soothing, modern space with technology that improves accuracy, efficiency, and your overall comfort. Every tool we invest in is chosen to make your care better and your experience easier from start to finish.

Ceiling TVs & Comfort Amenities

From weighted blankets and lumbar pillows to ceiling-mounted TVs with Netflix, we’ve designed every treatment room for serenity. Your experience should help you relax, even if you’ve been nervous at the dentist in the past.

TRIOS® Intraoral Scanner

This powerful scanner replaces traditional goopy impressions with fast, precise 3D scans. It’s a gamechanger for comfort and accuracy in everything from clear aligners to crowns.

CBCT & SprintRay 3D Printing

Our advanced CBCT imaging gives us a dynamic, 3D view of your oral anatomy—critical for implants, surgical planning, and diagnostics. Combined with our in-house 3D printer, we can enhance restorative work with more efficiency and precision.

Smarter Care with AI Technology

Your smile deserves the most advanced care, and that’s where AI technology comes in. With AI-powered imaging and analysis, we can catch potential issues earlier and create treatment plans tailored just for you. This means fewer surprises, greater accuracy, and a healthier smile with confidence in every step of your care.

AirFlow technology for cleanings

With AirFlow technology, your dental cleanings are gentler, faster, and more comfortable. Instead of traditional scraping, a fine jet of air, warm water, and gentle powder removes plaque and stains, even in hard-to-reach areas. You’ll leave with a brighter, smoother smile and a refreshing feeling you’ll love.

Sedation Dentistry

We offer a range of sedation dentistry options to help ease dental anxiety and ensure your visit with us is comfortable and enjoyable! From gentle chairside manner to sedation dentistry techniques, we strive to create a calming environment where you feel at ease throughout your dental treatment. We aim to provide you with the care you need while minimizing any stress or discomfort associated with dental visits, ultimately promoting better oral health and well-being.
