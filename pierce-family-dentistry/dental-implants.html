Dental Implants in San Diego

  • Long-Lasting : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a durable and cost-effective solution.
  • Improved Functionality : Implants allow you to eat, chew, and speak with confidence, as they function just like natural teeth.
  • Bone Preservation : Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur with missing teeth and preserving the natural shape of your face.
  • No Impact on Neighboring Teeth : Unlike dental bridges, implants do not require the alteration of adjacent healthy teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

At Pierce Family Dentistry , we make the dental implant process as smooth and comfortable as possible. Here’s what you can expect during your implant treatment:

1. Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation, where Dr. Brandon Pierce or Dr. Jennifer Pierce will assess your oral health and determine if dental implants are the right solution for you. We will use Dental X-Rays or 3D imaging to evaluate the condition of your jawbone and ensure it can support the implant.

2. Implant Placement

During the implant placement procedure, a titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. This procedure is typically done under local anesthesia, and sedation options are available for patients who may feel anxious.

After the implant is placed, the jawbone will need time to heal and fuse with the implant. This healing period usually takes several months, during which the implant becomes securely anchored in the bone.

3. Custom Restoration Placement

Once the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, a custom-made dental restoration, such as a crown, bridge, or denture, will be attached to the implant. This restoration is designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, restoring both the appearance and function of your smile.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth. To ensure the longevity of your implants, we recommend:

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, providing a permanent solution to missing teeth.

  • Brushing twice a day and flossing daily to keep the implant and surrounding gums healthy.
  • Regular dental check-ups and cleanings to maintain your oral health and monitor the condition of your implant.
  • Avoiding hard foods or habits that can damage your implant, such as chewing ice or using your teeth as tools.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most patients who are in good oral and overall health are candidates for dental implants. However, certain factors, such as insufficient bone density or underlying health conditions, may require additional treatments or considerations. If you’ve been told in the past that you are not a candidate for dental implants, we can explore options like bone grafting or sinus lifts to improve your chances of successful implant placement.
