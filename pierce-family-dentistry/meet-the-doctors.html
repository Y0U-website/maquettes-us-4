Meet the Doctors

Outside the office, Dr. Jennifer loves spending time with her husband, Dr. Brandon Pierce, and their three children. She is an avid reader, enjoys singing in the car, and is a proud member of multiple book clubs. Known for her love of Harry Potter, Dr. Jennifer brings a vibrant energy to the practice, making her a favorite among patients.

Dr. Brandon Pierce, DDS

Dr. Brandon Pierce has been surrounded by dentistry his whole life, with both his parents serving as dentists. He brings a lighthearted and energetic presence to Pierce Family Dentistry, ensuring patients feel at ease from the moment they walk through the door. Dr. Brandon earned his Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Science from Brigham Young University and went on to receive his Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). Upon graduation, he was awarded the Academy of Operative Dentistry Award for Excellence in Operative Dentistry.

When he’s not caring for patients, Dr. Brandon is an active member of his community and enjoys spending time with his family. He’s a weekend beach volleyball enthusiast, an indoor soccer player, and a fantasy baseball competitor. His sense of humor and easy-going nature make him a beloved figure at the practice, complementing Dr. Jennifer’s expertise.
