Dr. Julia Lee grew up in the Bay Area and has over 20 years of experience providing comprehensive dental care as a trusted general dentist. She received her Bachelor of Science in Biology at the University of California, Davis, and then went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of California, San Francisco.

"When I treat a patient, I always ask, ’would I do this procedure on myself?’ If the answer is no, then I won’t do it on my patient."

Dr. Lee takes a patient-centered approach to dentistry. She believes in educating her patients so they can make informed decisions about their oral health. By taking the time to explain treatment options, she empowers patients to choose the care that is right for them.

Her services span the full spectrum of dental care — from preventative and general dentistry to reconstructive and cosmetic procedures. Whether you need a routine cleaning or a complete smile makeover, Dr. Lee has the expertise and technology to deliver outstanding results.
