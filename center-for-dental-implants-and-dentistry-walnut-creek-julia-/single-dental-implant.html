Why Choose a Single Implant Over a Bridge?

Missing teeth are perhaps the most obvious and challenging obstacle to a beautiful smile. If you have one or more missing teeth, Walnut Creek Implant Dentist Dr. Julia Lee will show you how Dental Implants can solve your problem permanently. For tooth replacement, there simply is no solution that is more durable or more lifelike than a custom-designed dental implant.

Why Single Implants Beat Dental Bridges

Dental implants address both the visible missing tooth and the missing tooth root under the gum. They prevent bone loss by providing a replacement root seated in the jaw bone. Replacing a missing tooth promptly helps prevent natural teeth from shifting out of place. A dental implant is a titanium screw that replaces the tooth root. Implants are embedded directly into the jawbone for stable support. With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.

A single dental implant replaces a missing tooth with a standalone post and crown, leaving neighboring teeth completely untouched. A dental bridge, by contrast, requires grinding down the adjacent teeth to serve as anchors. Implants are also more durable and help preserve the jawbone in the area of the missing tooth.
