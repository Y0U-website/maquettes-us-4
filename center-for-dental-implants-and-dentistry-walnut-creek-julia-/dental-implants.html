Dental Implants in Walnut Creek, CA

Permanent & Natural-Looking Tooth Replacement Solutions

Dental implants are widely regarded as the most durable and lifelike replacement option for missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they fuse with the bone to create a permanent, stable foundation for replacement teeth.

Dr. Julia Lee specializes in implant dentistry and has helped countless patients in Walnut Creek restore their smiles with natural-looking, long-lasting dental implants. Whether you are missing a single tooth or multiple teeth, dental implants can provide a solution that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them an excellent investment in your oral health and overall quality of life. They also help preserve jawbone density and prevent the facial sagging that often accompanies tooth loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most adults with good general health and sufficient jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. Key factors include healthy gums, adequate bone volume to support the implant, and a commitment to oral hygiene. During your consultation, Dr. Julia Lee will evaluate your bone structure with 3D imaging and discuss whether bone grafting may be needed before placement.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post that fuses with your jawbone is designed to be permanent. The crown placed on top typically lasts 15–25 years before it may need replacement due to normal wear. Regular dental checkups and good at-home hygiene are the keys to long-term implant success.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you should feel little to no pain during surgery. Mild soreness, swelling, and bruising are normal for a few days afterward and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most patients report the procedure is far more comfortable than they anticipated.

The full process typically spans 3–6 months, which includes the implant placement, a healing phase (osseointegration) where the implant fuses to the bone, and the placement of the permanent crown. In some cases, same-day or immediate-load implants are possible, which Dr. Lee will discuss with you during your evaluation.

Coverage varies by plan. Some dental insurance policies cover a portion of implant surgery, while others treat it as cosmetic. Our team will review your specific benefits and help maximize your coverage. We also offer flexible financing options including CareCredit to make implants accessible.
