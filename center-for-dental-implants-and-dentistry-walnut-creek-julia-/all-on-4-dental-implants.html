What Are All-On-4 Dental Implants?

All-On-4 dental implants are a groundbreaking solution designed for individuals who have lost all or most of their teeth. This innovative technique uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of fixed, non-removable teeth.

Unlike traditional dentures, All-On-4 implants are permanently anchored to your jawbone, providing exceptional stability, comfort, and a natural appearance. The procedure can often be completed in a single day, giving you a brand new smile immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can slip or cause discomfort. All-on-4 implants are anchored directly into the jawbone with four titanium posts, providing a stable, fixed arch that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth. You never remove them, and they help preserve jawbone density — something traditional dentures cannot do.

All-on-4 is designed to replace an entire arch of teeth — either upper, lower, or both — using just four strategically placed implants per arch. The prosthetic arch contains 10–14 teeth, restoring full chewing function and a complete smile with minimal implants.

Yes. Once fully healed, All-on-4 implants allow you to eat most foods you enjoy. During the initial healing period (a few months), we recommend a soft diet. After integration is complete, you can return to a full diet including fruits, meats, and crunchy foods — far beyond what traditional dentures allow.

Many patients receive a provisional set of teeth on the same day as their implant surgery. After the healing period of approximately 3–6 months, a final permanent arch is placed. The total treatment timeline from consultation to final restoration is typically 6–9 months.

All-on-4 care is straightforward: brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush, use a water flosser or floss threader to clean underneath the bridge, and attend regular professional cleanings every six months. Avoid excessive biting force on very hard foods and your implants can last many years.
