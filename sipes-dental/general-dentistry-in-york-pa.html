FAQs

How often should I visit the dentist for a checkup? Most patients should visit the dentist every 6 months for a routine cleaning and exam. However, those with specific dental conditions may need more frequent visits.

Are tooth-colored fillings durable? Tooth-colored fillings are highly durable and can last for many years with proper care. They are bonded directly to the tooth, providing strength and stability.

Does a general dentist do fillings? General dentists are trained to perform a wide range of procedures, including examinations, x-rays, cleanings, and simple fillings. Most general dentists also handle crowns, bridges, dentures , and some specialized procedures like root canals or deep cleanings.

Are fillings covered under general dentistry? Fillings are considered part of general dentistry. They restore damaged teeth to their normal function and shape. General dental care also includes preventive treatments like check-ups and cleanings, as well as procedures like scaling, polishing, and extractions.
