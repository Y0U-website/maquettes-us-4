FAQs about Hybridge Dental Implants

How is Hybridge Full Arch different from traditional dental implants? Traditional implants typically replace one or a few teeth at a time with individual crowns or bridgework — a process that can take up to 18 months. Hybridge Full Arch is a specific protocol that restores an entire arch of up to 12 teeth using 5 to 6 implants as a foundation. The final restoration is a durable, cross-linked PMMA and chrome-cobalt bridge, completed in a fraction of the time and at less than half the cost of conventional full-arch bridgework. Dr. Sipes follows this precise protocol at every step.

How much does Hybridge cost at Sipes Dental in York, PA? Hybridge treatment starts at $19,495 per arch. Every case is different — final cost depends on the number of remaining teeth, available bone, and whether bone grafting is needed. Dr. Sipes provides an exact estimate at your initial consultation, and financing options are available.

Is Dr. Sipes a certified Hybridge provider in York, PA? Yes. Dr. Bryan Sipes is a certified Hybridge provider, having completed the specialized training required to offer the Hybridge Full Arch protocol. Only established leaders in implant dentistry are considered for this certification.

Can I still get Hybridge implants if I’ve been told I don’t have enough bone? Possibly, yes. The Hybridge protocol is designed to work with the bone most patients already have. Many patients previously told they weren’t candidates for implants have gone on to complete Hybridge treatment successfully. A CT scan is used to assess available bone and whether grafting is needed — Dr. Sipes will evaluate your case at your consultation.

How soon after Hybridge surgery can I return to work? Most patients resume normal activity within a few days of surgery. Discomfort is typically minimal, though some patients experience mild bruising. In most cases, three to five days is sufficient recovery time before returning to a full schedule.

Are there foods I need to avoid with a Hybridge Full Arch restoration? Hybridge is built for normal daily function, with a few limitations. Avoid foods that require heavy crushing — hard nuts, ice, hard candy, popcorn kernels, bones, and hard pretzels — since these can chip or crack the restoration. If damage occurs, repairs are usually straightforward and often completed in-office.

Will dental insurance cover Hybridge treatment? Coverage varies by policy — contact your insurance carrier directly to check whether dental implants are included in your plan, and note that any coverage is subject to your annual maximum. As a general rule, insurance benefits alone aren’t usually enough to be a deciding factor in treatment. Medicaid, Medicare, and standard medical insurance do not cover Hybridge. Our team is happy to help you review your coverage — call us with questions.

Is there an age limit for Hybridge dental implants? Age alone is not a disqualifying factor for Hybridge. Current Hybridge patients range from 21 to 93 years old. What matters more than age is your overall health and healing capacity, both of which are evaluated before treatment begins. Patients who smoke may experience slower healing. Dr. Sipes will review your medical history and, when appropriate, coordinate with your physician before proceeding with treatment at Sipes Dental in York, PA.
