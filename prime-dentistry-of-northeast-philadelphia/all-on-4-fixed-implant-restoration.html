6 Reasons to Consider All on 4 Implants in Philadelphia, PA

If you’re missing teeth or have been told that you need dentures, the All-on-4 Fixed Implant Restoration could be an excellent solution for you. Here’s why:

1) Fewer Implants Required : Traditional dental implants often require one implant per tooth, but the All-on-4 method anchors a full arch of teeth using just four implants. This means fewer surgeries and less discomfort.

2) Faster Healing Time : With fewer implants required, the healing time is significantly reduced. You can have your new teeth in place sooner, getting back to your normal life quickly.

3) Improved Bone Health : The All-on-4 implants stimulate your jawbone just like natural teeth roots do, helping to maintain bone density and prevent further bone loss.

4) Longevity : With proper care, All-on-4 implants can last a lifetime. In contrast, traditional dentures and bridges often need to be replaced.

5) Immediate Results : Unlike traditional implants that can take months to complete, with All-on-4, you can have a full set of teeth attached to your implants on the same day.

6) No Need for Bone Grafting : In cases where the jawbone is insufficient to support traditional implants, bone grafting is usually necessary. With All-on-4 implants, even patients with low bone density can get implants without the need for bone grafting.

How to Take Care of your Implants after the All-on4 Treatment?

Once you’ve had your All-on-4 treatment, it’s crucial to take proper care of your new implants to ensure their longevity and maintain your oral health. Here are some important steps to follow for aftercare:

  • Maintain Proper Oral Hygiene: Just like natural teeth, your new dental implants also need regular cleaning. Brush your implants at least twice a day and floss once a day. You should also rinse with an antibacterial mouthwash daily.
  • Use the Right Tools: When cleaning your implants, use a soft-bristle toothbrush to avoid scratches or other damage. Also, avoid toothpaste with harsh abrasives. Water flossers can be a beneficial addition to your oral hygiene routine.
  • Regular Dental Visits: Continue to see your dentist for regular check-ups and professional cleanings. This allows your dentist to monitor the health of your implants and address any potential issues early.
  • Manage Swelling and Bruising: After the All-on-4 implant surgery, it’s common to experience some swelling and bruising. Manage these symptoms by applying ice packs to the affected areas.
  • Avoid Hard or Sticky Foods: Be gentle with implants as they integrate with your jawbone the first 6-8 weeks. Avoid very hot, hard, crunchy, or chewy foods which could damage the implants while they heal.
  • Wear a Nightguard if you Grind your Teeth: If you have bruxism (teeth grinding), wear a custom nightguard so you don’t put too much pressure on implants while sleeping. The nightguard protects your investment!
