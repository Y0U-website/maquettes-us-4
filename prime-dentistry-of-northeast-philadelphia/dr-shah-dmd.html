Dr. Pathik Shah DMD MHA BDS

Dr. Shah DMD MHA BDS

Dr. Shah, a graduate of Sawyer Business School at Suffolk University, received his dental degree from Boston University. His passion for comprehensive dental care has driven his career from its beginnings in Vermont to his current established practice in Northeast Philadelphia. For many years, Dr. Shah has been dedicated to improving his patients’ oral health, quality of life, and confidence through advanced dental treatments.

Offering a wide range of dental services, Dr. Shah has extensive training in Implant Dentistry, Full Mouth Rehabilitation, Sedation Dentistry, and Endodontics. He has successfully completed hundreds of dental implant cases, utilizing advanced techniques such as guided surgery, 3D imaging and 3D printing for precise implant positioning. His expertise extends to implant restoration, where he employs the latest materials and techniques to create natural-looking and durable implant crowns and bridges, Implant Overdenture( Snap on Dentures) & Hybrid (all-on-4 appliances).

One of Dr. Shah’s specialized areas is Advanced bone grafting with A-PRF (Advanced Platelet-Rich Fibrin). This cutting-edge technique uses the patient’s own growth factors to enhance bone regeneration and healing, particularly beneficial in preparing sites for dental implants or repairing bone loss due to periodontal disease.

Dr. Shah is also known for his prompt attention to dental emergencies. He quickly alleviates patients’ pain through procedures like emergency root canals, extractions, and same day crowns. His calming approach and expertise in emergency dentistry help ease stress during urgent situations. Beyond emergencies, Dr. Shah provides routine and restorative treatments, always focusing on long-term oral health and fostering genuine relationships with his patients.

As a commissioned officer in the US Army holding the rank of Captain, Dr. Shah proudly serves his country. This sense of service extends to his community involvement, where he is deeply committed to giving back through local events and overseas missionary trips. He believes in providing judgment-free, compassionate dental care to all his patients.

Dr. Shah’s dedication to continuing education ensures he stays at the forefront of dental advancements. His combination of extensive training, years of experience, and a compassionate approach has made him well-regarded in the Northeast Philadelphia community. Dr. Shah and his team welcome the opportunity to provide comprehensive dental care for you and your family for years to come, with a promise of exceptional skills and a gentle, understanding manner in all aspects of dentistry.
