The Implant Bridge Procedure

Placing an implant bridge typically involves several steps:

Our skilled implant dentists use the latest technology and techniques like surgical stents and PRF bone grafts to ensure the best possible outcomes. We also offer sedation options for a comfortable treatment experience.

  • Treatment planning Advanced 3D CT scans are taken to create a precise surgical guide (stent) for optimal implant placement
  • Bone grafting (if needed) If jawbone density is insufficient, a bone graft using platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) from your own blood may be performed to encourage bone growth
  • Implant placement Titanium posts are surgically inserted into the jawbone using the custom surgical stent for guidance
  • Healing & osseointegration Over a few months, the implants integrate with the surrounding bone
  • Abutment & bridge placement Abutments are attached to connect the custom bridge prosthetic, completing your smile

Candidates for Implant Bridges

Most people missing multiple teeth in a row are good candidates for implant bridges, even if some bone loss has occurred. During your consultation, we will evaluate your unique case and create a personalized treatment plan to address your specific needs and goals.

If a bone graft is recommended, PRF derived from a small sample of your own blood can be used to stimulate healing and new bone growth. This advanced technique enhances the success of bone grafting procedures.

Process to Approach us for Implant Bridge

Consultation and Planning

During the initial consultation, the dentist evaluates your oral health, takes X-rays or 3D scans, and discusses your goals. A personalized treatment plan is created, detailing the implant bridge procedure.

Implant Placement

In this step, the dentist surgically places dental implants into the jawbone at the missing teeth sites. The implants are allowed to heal and integrate with the bone over a few months, providing a stable foundation for the bridge.

Bridge Attachment

We’ll provide you with detailed aftercare instructions, including tips for pain management, oral hygiene practices, and dietary advice. We also schedule follow-up visits to monitor your recovery, make any necessary adjustments, and answer any questions you may have.
