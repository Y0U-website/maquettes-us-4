About Emerald Coast Smiles by Design

Dr. Tilley is a native of Pensacola, FL and has been practicing dentistry in her hometown since 1998. She is an alumna of LEAP with the Pensacola Chamber and has been a long-time sponsor of Fiesta of Five Flags. She is also a sustainer with the Junior League of Pensacola.

Dr. Tilley is a graduate of the University of Alabama School of Dentistry, and has undergone extensive training to bring you and your family the highest level of care possible. Dr. Tilley keeps up with the latest in dentistry by attending continuing education seminars on topics such as oral surgery, implants, lumineers, periodontal disease, cosmetic procedures, and much more.

Aside from the help she gives to her patients, Dr. Tilley is also involved in several community organizations including Ballet of Pensacola, Fiesta Forces, Pensacola Children’s Chorus and sponsors of multiple school and charitable fundraisers and events.

LVI Preferred Providers

Emerald Coast Smiles by Design is an LVI Preferred office. LVI stands for the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies and is a prestigious post-graduate school where dental professionals can continue their education with an emphasis on the newest cosmetic procedures and state of the art technology.
