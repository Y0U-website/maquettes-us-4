Dental Implants for Pensacola, FL

If you are missing teeth, there are a number of options available to help you create a full smile. While dentures are popular for those missing a lot of teeth, they can often slip and make you feel uncomfortable as well as give you difficulty when eating and talking. Another option is getting dental implants which have been around for about a quarter of a century.

Dental implants provide everyday people with replacement teeth that feel and function like natural teeth. The tooth is permanently attached to the jaw allowing people to chew virtually anything while maintaining their facial contours and natural appearance.

Patients can receive one or multiple dental implants to correct any imperfections. Dental implants will give you years of having a beautiful smile with proper oral hygiene like brushing and flossing. Are Implants Right For You?

To qualify for dental implants, you must have good oral health, good overall health and:

If you’re considering implants, your mouth must be examined thoroughly by one of our dentists. Ideal candidates for dental implants must have healthy gums and teeth. Our local Pensacola, FL dentist will evaluate your jawbone’s strength and overall oral health to make sure there is great support for the implant root. As a caution, the use of tobacco (smoking or chewing) may be linked to a higher rate of implant failure.

  • Missing teeth- If you want to change the look of your smile and replace missing teeth, dental implants are a great option.
  • Bone- Dental implants are a great option for patients who have adequate bone to secure the implants into.
  • No health conditions- Underlying health issues such as diabetes, severe anemia, or hypothyroidism may hinder you from getting dental implants. Talk to your dentist about alternatives.
  • Speech- If you’d like to improve your speech, dental implants can help.
