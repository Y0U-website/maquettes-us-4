Payment

Payment is typically collected at the time of treatment; however, claims are submitted to your insurance first, and you will be responsible for any remaining balance once your insurance has processed the claim. All payment options should be discussed with one of our Scheduling Coordinators or our Business Administrator.

We accept Cash, Checks, Care Credit, Visa, Mastercard, and Discover.

For patients with dental insurance, we work closely with your provider to help estimate out-of-pocket costs and maximize your available benefits. We also offer guidance for patients exploring financing or alternative payment options to help make care more accessible and manageable.

Treatment Plans

If it is discovered that you need any dental treatment , a detailed treatment plan will be prepared prior to the beginning of any procedures. Treatment may be adjusted if your dental needs change, and the patient will be notified of any updates.

For patients with insurance, we take coverage into account when developing treatment plans and provide estimates to help you understand your expected financial responsibility in advance. This ensures you can make informed decisions before beginning care.

Dental Insurance

At Badger Dental Group, we work with many dental insurance providers and strive to help patients maximize their available benefits. Our team assists patients in understanding coverage, estimating out-of-pocket costs, and navigating treatment planning before care begins.

Dental insurance is a contract between you and your insurance provider, and coverage varies depending on the services you receive and your specific plan. While we are happy to help you understand your benefits and estimate coverage, patients are ultimately responsible for any costs not covered by their insurance. Your payment due on the day of service will depend on the treatment being provided, and our team will communicate these details once your treatment plan has been finalized.

We will do everything possible to ensure you receive the full benefits available through your insurance plan. If your insurance company has not paid their estimated portion within three months from the start of treatment, the remaining balance will become the patient’s responsibility at that time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do they evaluate and treat TMJ/TMD?

Yes, Badger Dental Group offers comprehensive evaluation and treatment for TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorders to help relieve pain and improve jaw function.

Do they offer dental implant restorations?

Yes, we provide dental implant restorations to help replace missing teeth and restore your smile. We coordinate with a trusted oral surgeon who places the implant, and then we create a custom crown or denture designed to fit securely on the implant.

Who are the dentists at Badger Dental Group?

The dental team at Badger Dental Group consists of experienced and caring dentists dedicated to providing personalized dental care in Grand Forks, ND. The team includes Dr. Jeremy Badger, DDS, and Dr. Kathryn Korsmo, DDS.

Do they offer same-day crowns with CEREC technology?

Yes, they use advanced CEREC technology to provide same-day crowns, allowing patients to receive durable restorations in a single visit.

What are Badger Dental Group’s hours and phone number?

For the most accurate and up-to-date hours and contact information, please visit their official website or call the office directly.

Does Badger Dental Group provide Invisalign clear aligners?

Yes, we offer Invisalign clear aligners as part of our orthodontic services. Invisalign is a discreet, comfortable alternative to traditional braces for straightening teeth.

Does Badger Dental Group offer preventive cleanings and exams?

Yes, our experienced team offers preventive cleanings and exams.

What cosmetic dentistry options are available, like whitening or veneers?

Yes, Badger Dental Group offers cosmetic dentistry options such as teeth whitening and veneers to enhance the appearance of your smile.

Can I talk with someone before deciding on treatment?

Yes. We welcome questions and want you to feel informed before moving ahead. Our team is here to talk through concerns, priorities, and next steps.

What makes Badger Dental Group different from other dentists in Grand Forks?

We combine preventive, restorative, cosmetic, emergency, TMJ/TMD, Invisalign, and implant restorative care with a personal, relationship-based approach. Our goal is to provide excellent dentistry in a caring, comfortable environment.

Do you work well with patients who feel nervous about dental visits?

Yes. We take extra time to help anxious patients feel informed, comfortable, and supported throughout their visit. We believe great dental care should feel calm and respectful from start to finish.

Can your office treat the whole family?

Yes. We care for patients across ages and stages of life, and we work hard to make every visit feel welcoming and personalized.

How do you help patients understand their treatment options?

We focus on education and clear communication. We walk patients through what we see, what their options are, and what to expect so they can make confident decisions.

Do you use modern dental technology?

Yes. Our office emphasizes advanced techniques and technology to improve comfort, precision, and efficiency in treatment planning and restorative care.

I have dental anxiety. What can I expect at my first visit?

You can expect a judgment-free visit where we move at your pace, explain each step clearly, and focus on helping you feel comfortable before we begin any treatment.

What should I do if I have sudden dental pain or an urgent issue?

Call our office as soon as possible. We do our best to help patients with urgent dental needs quickly and will guide you on the next best step.

I have not been to the dentist in a long time. Will I feel judged?

Not at all. Our job is to help you move forward with confidence. We meet patients where they are and focus on practical, personalized care.
