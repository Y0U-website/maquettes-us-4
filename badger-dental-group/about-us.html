Dr. Jeremy Badger, DDS

Dr. Jeremy Badger, DDS graduated from the University of North Dakota in 2007 followed by the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry in 2011. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Badger moved back home to pursue his professional career. This began with the purchase of the practice of Dr. Curtis Tanabe and Dr. Robert Olson.

Dr. Badger enjoys working with his excellent staff, finding comprehensive solutions for patient’s dental concerns, making in-office restorations with the CAD/CAM unit, and especially interacting and getting to know and care for his patients. When not working, Dr. Badger love the outdoors, hunting and fishing, golfing, and riding his motorcycle. In addition, he is an avid Fighting Sioux fan.
