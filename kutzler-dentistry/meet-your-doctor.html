Dr. Kim Kutzler

She graduated from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2015 and has been practicing in the central Wisconsin area since. Over the years, she developed a special interest in comprehensive treatment planning, implant dentistry, and airway health. She is passionate about helping patients not only restore their smiles, but improve their long-term health and quality of life.

Kutzler Dentistry was born from a dream to build something more personal — a small, private practice designed around connection, thoughtful care, and community.

If you’re looking for a dental home where you feel seen, respected, and genuinely cared for, Dr. Kutzler would love to welcome you.

Life Outside the Office

Dr. Kutzler’s passion for growth extends well beyond office hours. She frequently dedicates her evenings and weekends to advanced continuing education, including coursework at the Spear Education Institute in Arizona and active participation in local study clubs alongside other local dentists dedicated to clinical excellence.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Kutzler is a proud mom of four young children. She and her husband, Sam, embrace the beautiful chaos of a busy household and rarely see a dull moment! To unwind, Dr. Kutzler enjoys weightlifting, running, and reading — whether she’s diving into her own book or snuggled up sharing a story with her kids.

A Personal Note I am truly grateful to be building this practice. Creating a place where patients feel comfortable, cared for, and confident in their treatment has always been my goal. It’s a privilege to bring that vision to life, and I look forward to welcoming you.
