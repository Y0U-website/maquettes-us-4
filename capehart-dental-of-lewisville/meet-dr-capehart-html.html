Meet Dr. Christopher Capehart

Dr. Christopher C. Capehart was born and raised in Winnsboro, Texas, and now lives in the Lewisville area. Dr. Capehart attended and graduated Summa Cum Laude from the University of Texas at Dallas, where he earned his bachelor of science in biology in December of 2000. He then went on to receive his doctor of dental surgery degree and graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio. Dr. Capehart graduated in the top 10 % of his graduating dental school class and was elected to the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Capehart has a passion for dentistry. What he likes most are the relationships he develops with his patients. He enjoys treating the whole family. A beautiful smile can change a person s whole outlook, says Dr. Capehart, and everyone deserves to smile with confidence.

As a member of the American Dental Association and the Dallas County Dental Society, Dr. Capehart stays active in the dental community. He stays abreast of the latest methods being used in dentistry and participates in continuing education programs.

Dr. Capehart enjoys spending time with his wife and his Labrador Retrievers, Lex and Layla. He enjoys walking his dogs and playing fetch with them. Dr. Capehart is an avid golfer and he also enjoys tackling home improvement projects. He is a loyal and passionate fan of the Dallas Cowboys and tries to never miss a game.
