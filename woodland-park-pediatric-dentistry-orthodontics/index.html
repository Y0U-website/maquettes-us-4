Dr. Jacob Zitterkopf’s passion is transforming smiles and improving the lives of patients by enhancing their oral health and confidence. Every treatment plan is tailored to meet individual needs, ensuring not only straighter teeth but also better breathing, sleeping, and overall well-being. It’s incredibly rewarding to see the positive impact a healthy smile has on self-esteem and quality of life.

Dr. Beck loves what he gets to do every day! He enjoys engaging with his patients and breaking down the “scary dentist” stigma by creating easy, uplifting interactions. He understands the sacred trust between a parent and their child’s healthcare providers and strives to earn that trust by his gentle, non-invasive treatment approaches.

At Woodland Park Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics, we’re passionate about creating beautiful smiles with clear aligners—a treatment option that’s perfect for all ages. Our patients love the convenience and comfort, and we love helping them achieve their best smiles!
