The practice

If you had a root canal treatment that was unsuccessful, then come and see Dr. McInerney for a retreatment procedure. We perform this second root canal treatment with skilled, thorough care, making sure that all areas of your tooth have been cleaned of harmful infection.

If injury, wear, or stress have caused fractures to develop in your teeth, then come and visit our endodontists for solutions. We offer dental restorations and root canal treatments to bind and protect cracks as well as repair any damage they caused, renewing the strength and structure of your teeth.

Patients who still experience inflammation and infection in the roots of their teeth after an endodontic treatment should visit our office for endodontic surgery. Also known as an apicoectomy, this procedure involves the removal of the tooth root and sealing of the canals in order to protect your smile.
