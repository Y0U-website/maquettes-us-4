Your Consultation Agenda

Friendly Welcome

Our front desk team checks you in. If you’ve completed your patient forms at home from our patient resources page, you head straight to the chair. If not, no worries. We have everything you need.

Conversation with Dr. Collins

Before any imaging, Dr. Collins sits down with you to understand what brought you in. Your goals, your concerns, your history. This conversation shapes everything that follows.

Imaging & Exam

Digital X-rays, a 3D intraoral scan, and a careful exam of your teeth, gums, and bite. This gives Dr. Collins the full picture of your oral health and bone structure.

Plan Review & Cost

Dr. Collins walks you through your options, including the implant-supported crown or restoration that finishes the case. Whether it’s a single implant or full arch reconstruction, you see your imaging, hear the recommendation, and get a written estimate.

Next Steps Are Yours

You leave with a clear plan and zero pressure to commit. Want to schedule? Great. Want to think about it? Also great. The decision is yours, on your timeline.

Implant Consultation FAQ

How long does an implant consultation take?

Plan for about an hour. That covers a real conversation with Dr. Collins, digital X-rays and a 3D scan, a careful exam, a walk-through of your options, and a written cost estimate. You’re never rushed out the door.

Do I need a referral for an implant consultation?

No referral needed. Call our Springfield office directly at 541-287-7874 and we’ll get your implant consultation on the schedule, usually within a week or two depending on availability.

What happens after the consultation?

That’s entirely up to you. You leave with a clear plan and a written estimate, and the decision is yours on your timeline. Some patients schedule the same day, others take weeks to decide. Your plan doesn’t expire.

Should I bring my old X-rays to the implant consultation?

If you have recent X-rays, bring them. They add helpful history. We still take our own digital imaging and 3D scans during the visit, since implant planning needs a current, detailed picture of your bone and bite.
