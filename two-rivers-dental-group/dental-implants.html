Dental Implant FAQ

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades, often for the rest of your life. The titanium post fuses with your jawbone, and the crown on top typically lasts 10 to 15 years or longer before needing replacement. Regular checkups and good home care are what protect the investment.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Most patients report far less discomfort than they expected. Placement is done with local anesthetic using precise, minimally invasive techniques, and soreness afterward is usually managed with over-the-counter pain relief for a few days.

How much do dental implants cost?

Cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced, whether bone grafting is needed, and the type of restoration. At your consultation, Dr. Collins walks through the exact cost for your case before anything begins, and payment plans are available.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Most cases take three to six months from placement to final restoration, because the implant needs time to fuse with your jawbone. Some situations move faster and some take longer. Your treatment plan lays out the timeline for your specific case.

What implant training does Dr. Collins have?

Dr. Collins completed the AAID MaxiCourse, a 300-hour continuing education program in implant dentistry run by the American Academy of Implant Dentistry that combines lectures with hands-on surgical training. He places and restores implants at our Springfield office, and refers to trusted local oral surgeons when a case calls for bone grafting or specialist care.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with healthy gums and adequate jawbone are good candidates for dental implants. If your bone needs strengthening first, we coordinate bone grafting with trusted local oral surgeons. A consultation at our Springfield office with 3D imaging gives you a clear answer.
