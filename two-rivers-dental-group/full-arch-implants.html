Which Full Arch Option Fits Your Situation

Are most or all of your upper or lower teeth missing or failing?

Full arch implants make sense when an entire arch needs to be replaced or restored. If you’re only missing a few teeth, single tooth implants may be a better fit.

Do you have enough jawbone to support implants?

Most patients do, even those who’ve worn dentures for years. If bone is limited, we coordinate a referral for bone grafting before the implant phase. Dr. Collins reviews your 3D imaging during your consultation.

Do you want a fixed bridge or implant-supported dentures?

A fixed bridge stays in your mouth permanently. Implant-supported dentures snap onto implants and can be removed for cleaning. Both work, and the right choice depends on your goals and your jawbone.

Full Arch Implant FAQ

How many implants are needed for a full arch?

Most full arch cases use four to six titanium implants per arch, placed strategically where your bone is strongest. That’s enough to permanently anchor a full fixed bridge of teeth, which is why you may have heard the term All-on-4.

Are full arch dental implants better than dentures?

For most patients who qualify, yes. Full arch dental implants stay fixed in place, let you eat anything, and preserve your jawbone, while removable dentures can slip, restrict foods, and leave the bone underneath to keep receding. Dentures cost less up front, so they remain the right call for some patients. A consultation lays out both paths honestly.

Do I get teeth the same day as surgery?

In most cases, yes. Your implants are placed in a single appointment and most patients leave that same day with a temporary set of teeth attached. The permanent bridge goes on three to six months later, after the implants have fused with your jawbone.

How long do full arch implants last?

The implant posts are designed to last 20 years or more with proper care, often the rest of your life. The bridge itself is built for decades of daily use, and routine checkups let us catch normal wear early and keep everything solid.

How much do full arch dental implants cost?

Full arch cases vary widely based on how many implants you need, whether bone grafting comes first, and the type of final restoration. At your consultation at our Springfield office, Dr. Collins walks through the exact cost of your plan before anything begins, and payment plans are available.
