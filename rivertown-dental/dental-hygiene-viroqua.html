Why Dental Hygienists Choose Rivertown Dental

Intentional Scheduling For yourself and your patients. We ensure that your day to day feels blissful, while respecting a healthy work life balance. Find flexible scheduling designed around your life.

Culture This is the foundation of our everything, and apart of our everyday experience. From wellness & self care dollars to help you recharge, PTO that matched your years of experience, to a supportive team who genuinely enjoy working together.
