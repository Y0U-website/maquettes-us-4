Meet the Doc

Dr. Harris is married to Molly Harris (formerly Molly Warren) who is also an Ark City native. She received her doctorate degree from Wichita State University as a Family Nurse Practitioner and works at the Ark City Clinic. They have a two-year-old son, Cohen, and a baby girl, Ainsley Jo Harris, born in September 2021. It was an easy decision for their family to move back home as the community and close family will make a great place to raise children.

In his spare time, Dr. Harris enjoys golfing, cooking, taking trips to the lake, and fishing. His favorite hole at the Ark City Country Club is number 17 (where he proposed to Molly) and one of his signature meals is buffalo chicken pizza he cooks on Chief’s game day.

Dr. Harris is happy to be home and serving the community he was raised in.
