Procedures

Preventive Periodontal Maintenance We provide an individual recall program for each patient to be seen periodically by our Hygienist for exams,cleanings, root planning and scaling, x-rays and fluoride treatment.

Cosmetic Dentistry Dr. Razek is highly trained in aesthetic dentistry offering patients veneers, bonded fillings, porcelain crowns , onlays, tooth whitening and smile make overs.

Restoration Dentistry We provide State Of The Art Bonded (tooth-colored) fillings, crowns, and fixed bridgework for all ages.

Implant Dentistry Dr. Razek works closely with select Periodontists and Oral Surgeons after implant placement to restore missing tooth areas with customized crowns, bridges and removable implant based prosthesis.

Prosthetic Dentistry We offer a comprehensive line of removable prosthesis varying from full dentures to partial dentures to combination implant based removable type dentures.

Bite & Snore Guard Therapy We provide an advanced technology Bite Splint and/or Snore Guard appliance customized for each patient to deal with grinding, clenching and snoring depending upon the patient’s needs.
