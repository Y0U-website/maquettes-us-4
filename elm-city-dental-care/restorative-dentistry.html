Extractions

Extractions are always a last resort because preserving your smile is our top priority. However, sometimes a problematic tooth causes more harm than good. In these cases, your dentist may recommend an extraction to restore your oral health. Using gentle techniques and local anesthesia, decayed or damaged teeth can be safely removed to relieve discomfort or make space for dental implants, a bridge, or another type of tooth replacement.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth do not always have enough room to grow in comfortably, which can lead to pressure, crowding, inflammation, or other problems over time. When removal is the healthiest option, we’ll walk you through what to expect and keep the process as smooth and comfortable as possible. Our goal is to protect your long-term oral health while helping you feel informed and at ease every step of the way.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth with strength, stability, and a natural look. By replacing both the tooth root and the visible tooth, implants help restore comfortable chewing, clear speech, and confidence in your smile. They can also support your long-term oral health by helping preserve the jawbone after tooth loss. Whether you are missing one tooth or several, dental implants can help you feel more like yourself again.

Anxiolysis

Anxiolysis is a mild form of anxiety relief designed to help you feel calmer and more comfortable during dental care. When appropriate, a prescribed medication is taken before your appointment to ease nervousness while allowing you to remain awake and responsive throughout your visit. It can be a helpful option for patients who feel especially anxious about treatment and want a little extra support.

We understand that dental emergencies can happen at any time, which is why we prioritize same-day appointments whenever possible and deliver compassionate, efficient care in a comfortable environment.
