Get to know your dentist

Meet Dr. David Levy

Following dental school, Dr. Levy was commissioned into the U.S. Army and began his career with an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency in Colorado Springs. The AEGD program gave him a strong clinical foundation across a wide range of procedures, sharpening both his diagnostic eye and his technical skills before he ever stepped into private practice. That training, combined with six years of real-world experience, is what allows him to catch things that other providers sometimes miss, something patients mention again and again.

Dr. Levy then served 12 months in Egypt as part of a peacekeeping mission, where he was the senior dentist among a team of three, providing dental care in a demanding operational environment. Afterward, he spent three years at a large dental clinic at the Pentagon, where he finished his Army career. These experiences, caring for soldiers, leading teams, and working under pressure, reinforced his commitment to thoroughness, calm under any circumstance, and putting patients at ease no matter what they&#x27;re facing.

After leaving the Army, Dr. Levy spent six months practicing private dentistry in Australia before returning to the United States with one goal in mind: owning a practice where he could do things the right way. That meant a place where patients are treated as individuals, where quality is never compromised, and where the team is genuinely invested in every person who walks through the door. That place is Elm City Dental Care.

Outside the office, Dr. Levy spends his time with his wife, young son, and two Golden Retrievers, hiking, skiing, cooking, scuba diving, and traveling. He is also currently working toward earning his Private Pilot License. He brings that same curiosity and drive for growth to everything he does, including the practice he&#x27;s building in Keene.

Precision and comforts where it matters

We&#x27;ve invested in the amenities and technology that make a real difference, not just for the quality of your care, but for how you feel during every visit. Whether you&#x27;re here for a cleaning or a crown, you&#x27;ll have access to comforts that help you relax and tools that help us deliver results you can see and feel.

Settle in and unwind

Before your appointment begins, you&#x27;ll have the option to choose from our comfort menu, a curated set of amenities designed to help you feel at ease from the moment you sit down. Weighted blankets are available for gentle, calming pressure, while supportive neck and leg pillows can help you settle more naturally into the chair.

Headphones and entertainment

Every treatment room is set up to help you settle in and feel more at ease. Put on Bose noise-cancelling headphones, choose your own music or podcast, or relax with a personal TV featuring premium streaming options. However you prefer to unwind, you can make the space your own during your visit.

A holistic care approach

Prefer a more whole-body approach to relaxation? Aromatherapy and acupuncture are available to help create a calmer, more grounded experience before and during your appointment. These thoughtful additions give you another way to settle your body and mind, so your visit can feel less clinical and more comfortable from the start.

Dental anxiety solutions

If dental anxiety tends to feel more significant, we can also discuss pre-appointment anxiety medication when appropriate. Let us know what has made past visits difficult or what helps you feel more at ease, and we’ll shape the experience around you.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Sleep apnea goes beyond nighttime snoring; frequent disruptions to your breathing drain your energy and prevent deep recovery. At Elm City Dental Care, we take a personalized, comfortable approach to your airway health. We design custom solutions that help you breathe effortlessly, settle into a truly restful night, and wake up feeling like yourself again.

Same-day crowns available

When you need a crown, waiting weeks for a lab-fabricated restoration isn&#x27;t your only option. Our same-day crown capability means we can design, mill, and place your crown in a single visit, saving you time and eliminating the need for a temporary crown in the meantime.

Air-flow hygiene treatment

Our hygiene team uses guided biofilm therapy with advanced airflow technology to gently remove plaque, biofilm, and surface stains from areas that can be difficult to clean with traditional instruments alone. The result is a thorough, comfortable cleaning tailored to your oral health needs.

Guided biofilm therapy

Guided biofilm therapy offers a more precise, personalized approach to professional dental cleanings. By identifying and gently removing biofilm, plaque, and surface stains, we can target the areas that need the most attention while supporting healthier teeth and gums. It’s a thorough, comfortable way to make your preventive care even more effective.

Smile design software

For patients considering clear aligners or cosmetic treatment, we use smile design software to show you a visual mockup of potential results before any treatment begins. Seeing what&#x27;s possible makes it easier to ask questions, set realistic expectations, and feel confident moving forward.

Your first visit

What to expect when you visit us

Our goal is simple: you walk out knowing more about your oral health than when you walked in, with a clear plan and a next appointment already on the books.

Giving back to those who served

Honoring a commitment to serve

Service to the military community has been part of Dr. Levy’s path for many years. While attending Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, he completed an externship at the Boston Veterans Affairs Medical Center, where he cared for veterans and developed a deeper understanding of the oral health needs many service members face. During that time, he also founded the Uniformed Services Student Dental Association, creating opportunities for fellow dental students to support veterans throughout the Boston community.

After graduation, Dr. Levy spent five years serving as an Army dentist, including time in a combat zone. That chapter of his career strengthened a connection to the military community that continues today. At Elm City Dental Care, he remains committed to caring for Veterans Affairs patients and giving veterans, service members, and military families a place where they feel genuinely welcomed, respected, and well cared for.
