Why should I choose dental implants?

At Elm City Dental Care, we look at the bigger picture before recommending an implant. Dr. Levy considers your bite, bone health, overall oral health, and what you want your smile to feel like long term. Because implants are supported within the jawbone, they can restore a strong, stable foundation for eating, speaking, and smiling more naturally. They can also help preserve bone in the area of a missing tooth. While implants are a larger upfront investment, the goal is a durable, thoughtfully planned solution that fits your health, your smile, and your life for years to come.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Restored Function Dental implants create a stable foundation that can make eating, speaking, and smiling feel more natural again. Because they are supported within the jawbone, they are designed to feel secure rather than removable or temporary.

Support beyond the missing tooth Replacing a tooth is not only about filling the space you can see. Implants also stimulate the jawbone in the area where a tooth has been lost, helping support the structure and health of your smile over time.

Planned for the long term Dr. Levy looks closely at your bite, bone health, and overall oral health before recommending implant treatment. The goal is not simply to replace a tooth, but to create a durable solution that makes sense for your smile now and years from now.

Bone Grafting

Not every smile is immediately ready for an implant. When additional bone support is needed, Dr. Levy can incorporate bone grafting into your treatment plan to help create a stronger, more stable foundation for the implant and improve its long-term support.

CBCT Imaging

CBCT imaging gives us a detailed 3D view of your teeth, jawbone, nerves, and surrounding anatomy before treatment begins. Dr. Levy can use that information to evaluate bone levels, choose the right implant position, and plan your care with more confidence and less guesswork.

The Implant Treatment Process Implant Placement Once your treatment plan is finalized, the implant is placed into the jawbone where the missing tooth root once was. The area is then cleaned and closed so the healing process can begin. This crucial step creates the stable foundation your future restoration will rely on.

Healing Begins

After placement, the implant needs time to bond with the surrounding bone. The gum tissue typically begins healing within the first couple of weeks, while the deeper integration process can take several months. We will schedule follow-up visits to ensure your healing stays on track.

Smile Restored

Once the implant has fully integrated, a custom restoration is created to match your surrounding teeth. This restoration is attached to the implant with a small connector piece, completing your new tooth with a secure, natural-looking result.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a single tooth implant?

A single tooth implant is a permanent solution for one missing tooth. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone to act as a tooth root, topped with a custom crown that restores the visible part of the tooth. Because the implant fuses with the bone, it doesn&#x27;t rely on neighboring teeth or removable parts. It looks natural, feels comfortable, and stays secure while you eat, speak, and smile.

How are dental implants made?

Dental implants are made from medical-grade titanium, which bonds with the jawbone during healing for lasting strength and stability. The restoration on top is custom-designed for your smile, and once in place, the two work together to replace the missing tooth completely.

How long will my dental implant last?

Dental implants are known for lasting many years, and with proper care, the implant post can often last for decades. The crown attached to the implant may eventually need to be replaced because of normal wear, but the implant itself is designed to provide long-term support. Good home care, routine dental visits, and healthy gum tissue all play an important role in protecting your implant over time.

Can my dental implant become infected?

Yes, infection around a dental implant can happen, although it is not common when the implant is cared for properly. Brushing, flossing, following aftercare instructions, and keeping up with recommended checkups can help reduce your risk. If you notice tenderness, swelling, bleeding, or discomfort around an implant, it is best to schedule an evaluation promptly.

Do dental implants stop bone loss?

Dental implants preserve bone by stimulating the jawbone the way a natural tooth root does. Dental implants preserve bone by stimulating the jawbone the way a natural tooth root does. When a tooth is missing, the bone in that area no longer receives pressure from chewing and begins to shrink. By replacing the root, an implant maintains bone density and supports the natural shape of your face.

Can I get an implant in one day?

In some cases, an implant and temporary tooth can be placed on the same day, depending on your bone health, the location of the missing tooth, and your overall treatment needs. The implant will still need time to heal and bond with the bone before your final custom restoration is placed. A consultation is the best way to find out if same-day treatment is right for you.
