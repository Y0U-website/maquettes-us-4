Meet the Doctor

Brian Zulawinski, DDS

Dr. Brian Zulawinski is a dedicated dental professional who earned his Bachelors of Science Degree in biology from Northern Illinois University. He completed his dental degree at Indiana University in 2000. He continues to pursue additional training for implant dentistry, clear aligner therapy and is passionate about treating sleep apnea . A strong advocate for medical ethics, he stays committed to providing the highest level of care by continuous education and keeping up to date with latest dental technologies and techniques.

Dr. Zulawinski was born and raised in Morton Grove, IL. Dr. Zulawinski, his wife, Caryl, and son Christian have a great love for baseball. When he is not baseball spectating, he spends his time with various hobbies and spending quality time with his family. Playing a round of golf, periodically, is a treat for him.
