Implant-supported bridges — the strongest way to replace several missing teeth.

What Is an Implant-Supported Bridge?

When several neighboring teeth are missing, an implant-supported bridge is the most recommended treatment for replacing them. Instead of filing down healthy adjacent teeth like a traditional bridge, an implant bridge is anchored by dental implants placed directly in the jawbone — preserving your remaining natural teeth while restoring full function.

An implant bridge may replace three or more missing teeth using just one or two implant posts, making it a cost-effective and long-lasting solution.

Implant Bridge vs. Traditional Bridge

A traditional bridge relies on your natural teeth on either side of the gap to support the replacement teeth. This requires grinding down those healthy teeth, permanently altering their structure. If one of the supporting teeth develops a problem, the entire bridge may need to be replaced.

An implant-supported bridge is anchored by implants placed in the jawbone, so your healthy neighboring teeth are left completely untouched. The implants also stimulate the bone, preventing the jawbone deterioration that typically follows tooth loss. The result is a stronger, longer-lasting restoration that protects your oral health.

Why You Shouldn’t Wait to Replace Missing Teeth

When multiple teeth are missing, the consequences go beyond appearance. Acting sooner leads to better outcomes and more treatment options.

Bone Loss Accelerates

Without the stimulation of tooth roots, the jawbone begins to resorb and shrink rapidly, which can make future implant placement more difficult.

Remaining Teeth Shift

Surrounding teeth will drift into the open spaces, causing bite problems, crowding, and increased risk of losing additional teeth.

Eat & Speak With Confidence

An implant bridge restores full chewing function and eliminates the self-consciousness that comes with visible gaps in your smile.

A Permanent, Low-Maintenance Solution

Unlike partial dentures that must be removed and cleaned separately, an implant bridge is fixed in place and cared for just like your natural teeth.
