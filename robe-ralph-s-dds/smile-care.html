Our Services

Enjoy a family-owned practice designed to be a love-driven, exceptional dental experience for general, family, cosmetic, and restorative treatments.

Family & General Dentistry Prevention-focused dental care to keep your smile healthy.

Free Second Opinion Visits

Need a second opinion? Schedule a visit and we’ll offer our expertise.

Preventive Care

Your regular dental appointment provides preventative treatment to thoroughly clean the teeth. Attending your regular “cleaning” appointment will keep your mouth healthy.

Pediatric Care

We provide comprehensive dental care for children beginning with their first birthday as recommended by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Tooth Extractions

A simple tooth extraction involves the removal of a tooth due to decay, disease, or trauma.

Tooth Colored Fillings

Composite fillings use a tooth-colored resin to fill in cavities. As a result, fillings blend in naturally with the rest of your mouth.

Periodontal Treatment

To restore healthy gums, periodontal treatment is primarily utilized to clean the pockets around the teeth to prevent bone damage. We offer scaling & root planning, tissue grafting, as well as referrals to specialists.

Root Canal Therapy

Designed to remove nerves from the infected pulp of a tooth, root canal therapy eliminates infection and pain while also reducing the chance of future infection.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealant is applied onto the tooth enamel to protect teeth from plaque, decay, and subsequent cavities for up to ten years with normal chewing.

TRIOS 3D Digital Scanner

Enjoy gunk-free digital impressions of your teeth and gums for precision treatment planning.

Digital X-rays

Intraoral Camera

With real-time chairside imaging, the intraoral camera allows patients to see exactly what the dentist sees.

FYGG Nano Hydroxyapatite Varnish

We offer a biocompatible, fluoride-free dental varnish to promote remineralization, reduce tooth sensitivity, and support oral health.

Periodontal Laser Treatments

Advanced laser technology allows us to gently and precisely treat gum disease with less discomfort and faster healing. By targeting bacteria and diseased tissue while preserving healthy gums, laser therapy supports a healthier mouth and a stronger smile.

Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry Boost confidence and while refreshing and restoring your smile.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Restore function, comfort, and confidence with a customized treatment plan designed to rebuild your smile. Full mouth rehabilitation combines restorative and cosmetic dentistry to create a healthy, natural look that feels as good as it functions.

Dentures & Implant Dentures

Whether you’re missing several teeth or a full arch, dentures can help you smile and chew again with confidence. For extra stability and a more natural feel, implant dentures are secured to dental implants—no slipping, no worry, just a smile that feels like your own.

Professional Teeth Whitening

Faster. Fresher. Brighter. Our professional whitening treatment is more effective than over-the-counter options. Your refreshingly whiter smile awaits.

Dental Implants

Whether you are missing one tooth or multiple teeth, dental implants with our team offers a lasting and natural looking tooth replacement solution.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures provide a secure, comfortable way to replace multiple missing teeth by anchoring your denture to dental implants, helping prevent slipping and restoring confidence when eating, speaking, and smiling.

SureSmile® Orthodontic Solutions

Virtually invisible and removable, SureSmile’s clear aligners help patients achieve a beautifully straight smile without the challenge of traditional braces.

Botox® for TMJ

Botox® smooths out wrinkles and can also effectively relieve TMJ symptoms and jaw tension to alleviate headaches, migraines and facial pain. Millions use Botox® to restore a fresher, more youthful appearance.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers provide a non-surgical solution to contour and shape your face for a younger-looking you. They are often used to plump thin lips, enhance shallow contours, smooth wrinkles, and even diminish the appearance of scars.

Porcelain Veneers

Experience a stunning smile makeover in a short time with ultra-thin veneers designed to minimize any impact on your existing teeth and custom-designed to create the smile you desire. Bonded to the front of your teeth, precision crafted teeth covers effectively treat discolored, worn, chipped, broken, misaligned teeth or teeth with gaps.

Composite Bonding

Designed to match your natural tooth color, composite bonding beautifully repairs chipped or cracked teeth, reduces gaps, or spaces between teeth, and hides discoloration or faded areas on the tooth’s surface.

Porcelain Crowns

Dental crowns offer an aesthetically pleasing and healthful treatment for decayed, damaged, or cracked teeth. We custom design and place a dental crown to permanently cover the affected teeth, ensuring your crown matches your natural tooth color and fits your existing smile.

Dentures

Whether you need a partial set of dentures or a complete set, we will assess your situation, make recommendations, answer all your questions, and deliver the option you choose.

Dental Bridges

If you are missing teeth, we can custom fit a dental bridge to fill a space that your tooth previously occupied.

Amalgam Restorations

If you have a silver filling that has been damaged, we can restore it.
