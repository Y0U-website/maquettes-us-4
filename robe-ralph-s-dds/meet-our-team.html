Joanne

Joanne joined our office in 2025 but first worked with Dr. Eckdahl early in her career. She brings more than 10 years of dental office experience and now serves as our patient coordinator, helping patients stay on track with their oral health. Joanne enjoys connecting with patients and creating a friendly, helpful experience at every visit. In her free time, she loves spending time with her family, traveling to the Wolf River in Fremont, and staying active through barre, hip hop, boating, and outdoor adventures.

Caitlyn

Caitlyn graduated from Madison Technical College with a degree in Dental Hygiene. She previously worked as a pediatric dental assistant, gaining valuable experience in patient care and communication. Known for her gentle touch and warm personality, Caitlyn is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. In her spare time, she enjoys baking, spending time outdoors, and being with her friends and family.

Becca

Becca has been part of our team since 2019 and sees patients on Thursdays. She has practiced as a dental hygienist since 2004 and loves building lasting relationships with her patients, whom she considers like family. Patient comfort and oral health are always her top priorities. Outside of work, Becca enjoys time with her husband and two yellow labs, weekends at her cottage, cycling classes with friends, backyard bonfires, and cheering on the Green Bay Packers.

Vicki

Vicki joined our team in 2008 and has been a valued part of the practice ever since. She earned her associate’s degree in dental hygiene from Madison Area Technical College before moving to Milwaukee in 1990. Vicki enjoys collaborating with her colleagues while also forming meaningful one-on-one connections with patients. She loves traveling—especially her yearly trips to Mexico—as well as cheering for the Brewers and Packers. In the summer, she takes full advantage of Milwaukee’s outdoor activities and spends time up north with family and friends.

Elaine

Elaine has been with our office since 1988 and is known as both an outstanding dental assistant and the go-to for keeping everything running smoothly. She loves helping patients maintain healthy, beautiful smiles and has been a cornerstone of our team for decades. Elaine is married with three children and seven grandchildren who keep her busy. When she’s not at the office, she enjoys remodeling projects and gardening.
