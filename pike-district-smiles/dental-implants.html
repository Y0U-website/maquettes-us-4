Dental Implants in Bethesda, MD

Dental implants give you a lifelong natural tooth replacement that actually helps regenerate bone in your jaw for an alternative to dentures and bridges in Rockville.

What to Expect During Your Procedure for Dental Implants in Bethesda, MD?

First, you’ll come in for an exam and x-rays with Dr. Nikkhah to ensure you’re a candidate for tooth replacement. We’ll review your oral health and medical history, discuss your goals for your smile, and evaluate any existing issues such as tooth decay or gum disease.

For most people, there will be two procedures to place the dental implants in Bethesda, MD. The first is the placement of the post, which is done in a surgical procedure. For a few months following this appointment, the actual implant will need to heal and bond with your jaw bone. Patients may receive a temporary restoration during this time.

Then, you’ll receive your final restoration, which is typically a dental crown affixed to the end of your dental implant. The entire process can take several months, although most patients find that the duration of this process doesn’t affect their daily life in North Bethesda.

The Benefits of Dental Implants in Bethesda, MD

  • Strength . Since the post bonds with your jaw bone, tooth replacement are the strongest alternative to your natural teeth and give you the ability to eat any foods you like.
  • Durability . While your dental crown will eventually need to be replaced, your actual implant never will, giving you a lifelong replacement that’ll save you money in other replacement options over the course of your life.
  • Aesthetics . Tooth replacement not only look like your natural teeth, but allow you to increase the density of your jaw bone, which tends to diminish once natural teeth are lost. This regeneration can help you have a fuller, more youthful appearance.
  • Confidence . Because of their strength, durability, and aesthetic appeal, dental implants provide patients with more confidence than dentures or bridges and a way to feel secure with their smile!
