Implant dentures help with chewing ability, improve dental hygiene, preserve bone health, and offer long-term comfort.

These are individual implants made of titanium that are precisely placed and implanted into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. After the initial examination, we will discuss how many implants are needed and proceed with the next steps of implanting the dentures.

Partial Dentures

Partial or removable dentures are a common and cost-effective option for patients who are missing several teeth but still have remaining natural teeth. These implant supported dentures help replace missing teeth and restore both function and appearance.

Compared to traditional dentures, partials offer improved stability and comfort. Special connectors are used to attach the denture to dental implants, providing a secure fit that feels more like your own teeth. Even though these dentures can be removed for cleaning, they offer great support helping to prevent bone loss and maintain your facial features.

Permanent Dentures

Fixed implant supported dentures offer a more permanent and durable solution for tooth loss. Unlike removable dentures, these are non-removable and are surgically anchored to the jawbone using dental implants. This procedure provides excellent stability for chewing and eating your favorite foods while improving your overall oral health.

At Relax Dental, we proudly offer All-on-4 dentures, a same-day solution designed to replace the entire arch of missing teeth with just four strategically placed implants. This innovative treatment helps patients who may not have enough bone density for traditional implants, reducing the need for bone grafting. All-on-4 implant dentures offer a secure, natural-looking smile that can help prevent bone loss, enhance appearance, and allow you to enjoy a confident, lasting result.
