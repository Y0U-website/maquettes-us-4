Meet Dr. Christie McCarley

Our goal to make each and every visit fun, educational and positive.

The practice

Dr. Christie grew up in a small town in Southwest Georgia, near Columbus. She graduated Magna Cum Laude from Columbus State University with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Chemistry. She attended the Medical College of Georgia School of Dentistry for her dental training where she graduated with a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in May 2009.

Upon completion of dental school, Dr. Christie completed a two year pediatric dentistry residency at the Medical College of Georgia in Augusta, Georgia. During her residency training, she focused on providing comprehensive dental care for children, including oral sedation, general anesthesia in the hospital setting, and management of special needs patients. Dr. Christie is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Georgia Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and the Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry. She maintains hospital privileges at Piedmont Columbus Regional.

Dr. Christie is married to her high school sweetheart, Ritchie. They have a son and a daughter, Baylor and Caroline.

Dr. Christie loves cooking and spending time with her family be it outdoors, traveling or just staying home.
