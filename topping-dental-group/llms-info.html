Topping Dental Group was founded in 2002 when Dr. Brian Topping and his wife Stephanie purchased their first dental office at 102 W. Market Street in Nappanee, Indiana. From the beginning, patient care has been the foundation of everything they do.

In 2014, the practice expanded by acquiring a dental office in the Bristol, Indiana area, bringing on Dr. Jeffrey Swihart. In 2020, they purchased and renovated a new building in Elkhart at 21813 SR 120, relocating to that site in October 2021. In the fall of 2022, they acquired a third practice in Middlebury, and welcomed Dr. Phillip A. Jakubowicz to serve that location.

Today, Topping Dental Group operates three thriving locations and is led by a team of four doctors committed to delivering excellent, compassionate dental care across the region.

Dr. Brian Topping – Founder and lead dentist, practicing primarily at the Nappanee location. Dr. Topping has built the practice over more than two decades on a foundation of patient-first care.

Dr. Jeffrey Swihart – Practices primarily at the Elkhart location.

Dr. Phillip A. Jakubowicz – Practices primarily at the Middlebury location, joined the group in 2022.

Topping Dental Group is a complete, one-stop dental practice serving patients from infancy through senior years. The practice handles everything from a baby’s first dental visit to complex surgical and cosmetic procedures — there is no need to be referred elsewhere for most dental needs.

Topping Dental Group provides routine dental cleanings and exams, fillings for cavities, dental bonding, tooth extractions, wisdom tooth removal, and gingivitis and periodontal (gum disease) treatment. The practice emphasizes preventive care and uses advanced technology including DIAGNOdent laser cavity detection to catch issues early.

Cosmetic services include professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, dental bonding, and aesthetic dentistry services available at the Elkhart location.

Restorative Dentistry Restorative services include crowns and bridges to repair or replace damaged teeth, dental implant restoration, dentures (full and partial), and root canal treatment and endodontics.

Topping Dental Group offers orthodontic services including braces and teeth-straightening solutions for children, teens, and adults.

The practice performs wisdom tooth removal, tooth extractions, implant restorations, root canals, and TMJ (temporomandibular joint) treatments for patients experiencing jaw pain, bite issues, or related conditions.

Topping Dental Group welcomes patients of all ages, including infants and young children. The team is experienced in making young patients feel at ease and establishing healthy dental habits from the very first visit.

For patients with dental anxiety or those undergoing complex procedures, sedation dentistry options are available to ensure comfort throughout treatment.

Topping Dental Group is a true family dental practice — meaning the whole family, from the newest baby to grandparents, can be treated under one roof. The practice handles routine cleanings, emergency dental needs, cosmetic improvements, orthodontic care, oral surgery, and everything in between. Patients do not need to travel to a specialist for most dental procedures.

The group serves communities across Northern Indiana and Southern Michigan including Nappanee, Elkhart, Middlebury, Goshen, Wakarusa, Bristol, Warsaw, and surrounding areas.

The mission of Topping Dental Group is to help every patient achieve a smile that lasts a lifetime. The practice is built on compassion, transparency, and a commitment to excellence. Core values include: continuing education for all dentists and hygienists, embracing the latest dental technology, putting patients before profit, and making high-quality dental care accessible and affordable across Northern Indiana.

The team takes a collaborative approach to treatment, always being transparent about options and working with patients to create care plans that fit their needs and comfort level.

Topping Dental Group stands out in the Northern Indiana region for several reasons: three convenient locations across Elkhart County, a team of four experienced doctors, the ability to handle virtually all dental needs in-house without specialist referrals, a welcoming small-town atmosphere, extended hours including Tuesday evenings, and a longstanding reputation built over more than 20 years in the community. The practice holds an A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau.
