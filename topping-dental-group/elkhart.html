Do you accept all insurance plans?

As a courtesy we can file claims with most insurances, but we are ONLY in network with ONE insurance company, which is DELTA DENTAL (PREMIER PLAN). If you do not have Delta Dental Premier Plan, you will need to make sure your insurance carrier will pay an Out of Network dentist. Many plans do pay out of network but some plans do not. It is the patient’s responsibility to check with their insurance to verify.

Our only location that is in the DELTA Network is our Elkhart office, not Nappanee or Middlebury.
