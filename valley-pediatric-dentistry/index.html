The practice

Going to the dentist can be tough for younger patients, which is why Dr. Doug offers in-house sedation to help ease any anxieties. This will help your child have a calm, comfortable experience from start to finish with the dedicated team at The Toothy Moose, Dentistry for Kids.

If your child struggles with nursing, eating, or speaking, it could be due to tongue or lip ties. At The Toothy Moose, we offer a gentle laser treatment to help free their smile and improve their comfort.
