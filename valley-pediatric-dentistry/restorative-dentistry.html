Pediatric Dental Fillings

If your child has a cavity, it can cause pain and affect their oral health. Dental fillings are a quick, simple, and affordable way to treat cavities in kids. Even though baby teeth eventually fall out, they play an important role in shaping the path for adult teeth. A filling can keep your child’s tooth healthy and prevent future issues, ensuring a bright and comfortable smile as they grow.

Pediatric Pulpotomies

If your child has a toothache, it could be a sign of an infection. A pulpotomy, sometimes called a "baby root canal," can stop the infection and relieve pain in just one visit. This procedure restores your child&#x27;s oral health, easing discomfort and sensitivity so they can get back to smiling, laughing, and enjoying being a kid.

Pediatric Dental Emergencies

When your child faces a dental emergency, acting quickly can relieve pain and speed up recovery. Unsure if it’s an emergency? Give us a call. Our team will help assess the situation, offer advice for at-home care, and get an appointment scheduled as soon as possible, ensuring your child can get back to enjoying their childhood.

Pediatric Dental Crowns

We understand that seeing your child in pain can be frightening. Toothaches and damaged teeth can be a big concern for kids, but pediatric dental crowns can help restore both the function and appearance of their teeth. If you need a solution for your child&#x27;s damaged or decayed teeth, our white zirconia crowns are designed to provide a durable, long-lasting solution to keep their smile healthy for years to come.
