Meet Dr. Abedini

Dr. Aryan Abedini

Founder of Love Dental U.S. Navy veteran

Science, artistry, and a whole lot of heart.

Dr. Abedini grew up in Maryland, studied psychology at the University of Maryland, then earned a master’s in biomedical sciences and his dental degree at Midwestern University, graduating near the top of his class.

Before opening Love Dental, he served in the U.S. Navy, caring for the men and women who serve this country. That experience shaped how he practices: thorough, calm, and always putting the person in the chair first.

In the summer of 2022, he and his family moved to Avondale and built Love Dental from the ground up, the kind of dental home he always wanted to practice in.
