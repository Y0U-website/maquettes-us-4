Full-Arch Implants & Dentures…

A whole new smile, in as few as four implants.

If you’re missing most or all of your teeth, or living with dentures that slip, full-arch implants can give you a permanent, natural-looking set of teeth that let you eat, laugh, and speak with total confidence. This is one of the things we do best.

One arch. A handful of implants. Teeth that stay put.

Full-arch treatment, often called All-on-4 or All-on-X, replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth with a fixed bridge anchored to as few as four to six implants. No plate covering the roof of your mouth, no adhesive, no taking them out at night.

Because the implants fuse with your jaw, they preserve bone and keep your face looking like you. For many people it is the difference between hiding their smile and finally feeling like themselves again.

What to expect

Consultation & 3D planning

We take a 3D scan of your jaw and bite, talk through your goals, and build a precise plan with a clear, honest quote.

Teeth in a day, when possible

The implants are placed gently and, in many cases, we attach a fixed set of temporary teeth the same day. You never leave without a smile. Sedation is available.

Healing

Your implants fuse with the bone over a few months while you wear your temporary teeth. We keep you comfortable and check in along the way.

Your final teeth

We place your custom, permanent arch, shaped and shaded to look natural. You walk out chewing and smiling like it’s always been yours.
