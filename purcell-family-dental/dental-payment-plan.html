  • No exclusions for pre-existing conditions

Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment do you accept? We accept various payment methods, including cash, checks, all major credit cards, and convenient third-party options, including CareCredit ® and Sunbit.
  • How do I schedule my preventative care? Simply give our office a call to set up an appointment at your convenience.
  • How can I use my member-exclusive discount for treatment? Using your member discount is easy! Schedule your appointment, let us know you’re a member, and we’ll ensure your savings are applied instantly.
  • Can I add members to my account? Yes! Our dental savings plan is family-friendly, so everyone in your household can join.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Our membership plan is exclusively available at Purcell Family Dental and cannot be transferred to other dentists.
