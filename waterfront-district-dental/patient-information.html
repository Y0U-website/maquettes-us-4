Your First Visit

Your first visit to Waterfront District Dental is all about getting to know you and your smile goals.

We’ll take the time to explain your treatment options and answer any questions so you can make informed decisions about your oral health.

During this appointment, Dr. Bart Knowles , and our team will:

Perform a comprehensive oral health exam

Take digital X-rays and photos as needed

Review your dental and medical history

Discuss your concerns, goals, and expectations

Create a personalized care plan tailored to your needs

Insurance & Financing

At Waterfront District Dental, we believe quality dental care should be accessible and affordable. That’s why we accept most major PPO dental insurance plans and offer multiple payment options, including cash, checks, major credit cards, and flexible financing through Carecredit.

Whether you’re using insurance or considering advanced or elective treatments, our knowledgeable team will help you understand your benefits, file claims, and choose the best payment plan for your budget. We also provide detailed estimates before any procedure, so you always know what to expect.
