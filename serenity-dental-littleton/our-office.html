High Standards

With our state-of-the-art equipment, we provide personalized solutions that address each patient’s unique concerns. Moreover, through comprehensive treatment planning and advanced procedures, we help our patients achieve optimal dental health. In the event of a dental emergency, we also prioritize urgent care by making every effort to offer same-day appointments.

Education & Prevention

Uncompromising Safety

Because patient safety is one of our primary concerns, we therefore adhere to a very strict sterilization and cross-contamination protocol. In addition, our practice meticulously follows the standards established by the Center for Disease Control (CDC), the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), and the American Dental Association (ADA). As a result, these combined measures ensure the highest level of care and protection for every patient.

Training & Expertise

Dr. Erica Odegard and Dr. Tricia Doukas are determined to provide patients with all that modern dental technology can offer. To achieve this, our staff regularly attends dental conventions, lectures, and meetings to learn about new techniques and tools within the industry. In addition, we are proud members of the American Dental Association, the Colorado Dental Association, the Metro Denver Dental Association, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. Moreover, this commitment helps us stay at the forefront of dental care for our patients.

A Positive Experience

We spend considerable time with every patient so their concerns are heard and understood. Moreover, our caring approach, combined with a comfortable environment, utilizes the latest modern dental technologies and techniques. To further enhance your comfort and entertainment during treatment, we offer movies and headphones. In addition, the waiting room features free WIFI, electronic magazines, iPads, and a fridge stocked with cold beverages. Ultimately, we look forward to warmly welcoming you to our Littleton dental practice.
