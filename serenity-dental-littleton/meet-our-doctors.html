Tricia Doukas

DDS

As one of our doctors, Dr. Doukas believes that every person deserves a healthy smile. For this reason, she delivers gentle, comprehensive dentistry with care and compassion in a relaxed environment. Moreover, she is dedicated to continuing education and uses state-of-the-art equipment and procedures to provide personalized solutions. Working closely with her team, Dr. Doukas is committed to treating patients like family. Ultimately, she looks forward to getting to know you and discussing your individual needs.

Continue Reading

Dr. Doukas, one of our doctors, graduated with honors from the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry. Additionally, she was inducted into the Tau Kappa Omega Dental Honor Society and received the distinguished American Academy of Oral and Maxillofacial Radiology Award. Before that, Dr. Doukas completed her undergraduate studies at Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska, graduating magna cum laude with a Bachelor of Science in biology.

Moreover, Dr. Doukas is a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, Colorado Dental Association, and the Metro Denver Dental Society.

On a personal note, Dr. Doukas grew up in a small town in Kansas. Today, she and her husband, Nick, are thrilled to call Colorado their home. During her free time, Dr. Doukas enjoys hiking, mountain biking, camping, skiing, working out, yoga, Broncos football, and cooking with her husband.

If you would like to schedule an appointment with Dr. Tricia Doukas, DDS, contact us today!
