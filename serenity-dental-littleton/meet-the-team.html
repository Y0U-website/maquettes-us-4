Meet Our Serenity Dental Team

Providing Experienced Dental Care to Your Family

Traci

Office Manager

Traci grew up in St Paul, Nebraska, a rural town of less than 2,500 people. She moved to Colorado in 1999 and loves the lifestyle here. Traci has worked in the dental field for more than 20 years and is a member of the American Academy of Dental Office Managers. She is a proven professional, and a dedicated wife and mother.

Her free time is spent enjoying walks, indulging in TV, and organizing closets (a perfect hobby for an office manager).

Courtney

insurance coordinator

Courtney is an insurance coordinator with five years of experience in the dental field maximizing insurance benefits. She’s known for her positive attitude and energy. In her spare time, she enjoys hiking, reading, and spending time with her husband and Lucy her cat.

Van

Dental Hygienist

Van has been in the dental field since 2014 and graduated from Rio Salado Dental Hygiene School. She is dedicated to treating patients with optimal care and helping them improve their oral health with the latest technology, products, and procedures available. Listening to her patients’ concerns and exploring solutions together is something she enjoys. She recently moved back to Colorado to be closer to her family. When she is not helping patients, she enjoys hiking, snowboarding, and traveling with her husband.

Katia

Dental Hygienist

Katia is a registered dental hygienist and recent graduate from the Community College of Denver’s Dental Hygiene Program. She is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable while providing personalized, high-quality care and educating them on achieving lifelong oral health. She enjoys building meaningful relationships with her patients and creating a positive dental experience for every visit.

Outside the office, Katia loves spending time outdoors hiking and exploring Colorado’s beautiful mountains and trails. She also enjoys traveling, spending time with family and friends, and making memories with her dachshund, Weenie. She is excited to be getting married this August and looks forward to this new life chapter with her husband.

Madeleine

Assistant

Madeleine earned her Bachelor’s degree in Biology from Western Colorado University and has a passion for the science behind dentistry. She enjoys helping patients feel comfortable and cared for while supporting them on their journey to a healthy smile.

Madeleine has spent most of her life in the state and recently moved from the mountains back to the Front Range to be closer to family. Outside of the office, she enjoys knitting, cooking, and spending time outdoors with her dog and fiancé. Her friendly personality and dedication to patient care make her a valued member of our dental team.

Millie

Comfort Dog

Millie is Serenity Dental’s sweetest team member—and definitely the furriest! With her gentle nature and wagging tail, Millie loves greeting patients and bringing a little extra calm to their visit. Whether she’s offering snuggles, a soft paw, or simply relaxing nearby, Millie helps make every appointment feel a little more comforting. She’s happiest when she’s spreading smiles and making new friends—two- or four-legged!
