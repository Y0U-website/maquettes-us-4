Our Vision

To bring patient-centered dentistry to the community we call home

Founded by Dr. Panousis and Dr. Hodge Jain—both local to the area—the practice combines clinical excellence with a welcoming, modern environment designed around comfort, trust, and long-term relationships.

We believe great dentistry is about more than treating teeth. It’s about knowing our patients, having honest conversations, and providing continuity of care you can count on for years to come. From transparent treatment planning to a relationship-driven approach, every detail of our practice was intentionally designed to feel personal, elevated, and genuinely centered on you.

As a practice offering preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and family dentistry, we’re committed to making exceptional care both efficient and accessible—while never losing the warmth and familiarity of a hometown dental office.

Meet the Dentists Who Call Kennett Home

Meet Dr. Paraskevi Panousis

Born and raised in Kennett, Dr. Panousis co-founded The Dental Studio of Kennett to bring a more thoughtful, personalized approach to dentistry back to her hometown. She is passionate about building lasting relationships with patients while providing modern, comprehensive care in a comfortable setting.

Meet Dr. Lauren Hodge Jain

Dr. Hodge co-founded The Dental Studio of Kennett with a shared vision of creating a dental experience centered on trust, transparency, and exceptional care. She enjoys helping patients feel confident in their smiles through personalized treatment and a compassionate approach to dentistry.
