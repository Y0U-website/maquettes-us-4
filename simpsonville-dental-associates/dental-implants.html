Dental Implants FAQs

  • How long does it take to receive dental implants? The entire process can take several months, including healing time after surgery. However, modern technology allows some patients to receive restorations in fewer appointments.
  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. Local anesthesia and sedation options ensure a comfortable experience.
  • Can dental implants replace multiple teeth? Yes, implants can support single crowns, fixed bridges, or full dentures, depending on your individual needs.
  • Will dental implants affect my oral health? Dental implants help preserve jawbone density, prevent bone loss, and protect surrounding teeth, improving overall oral health.
  • Are dental implants covered by insurance? Many dental insurance plans cover part of the procedure. Our team helps you navigate dental insurance claims and financing options to maximize coverage.
