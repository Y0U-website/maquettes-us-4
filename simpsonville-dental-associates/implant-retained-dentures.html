FAQs About Implant-Retained Dentures

Are implant-retained dentures permanent?

The dentures may be removable or fixed, depending on your treatment plan. The dental implants themselves are designed to be a permanent part of your smile.

How does dental implants cost compare to traditional dentures?

The cost is typically higher than traditional dentures because implants require additional procedures and materials. However, many patients save money over time because implant-supported solutions often require fewer replacements and adjustments.

Is there an adjustment period?

Yes. Most patients experience a short adjustment period as they adapt to their new smile. The secure fit often makes adaptation easier than with traditional dentures.

Can implant-supported dentures replace multiple teeth?

Yes. Implant-supported dentures can replace multiple teeth, a full arch, or an entire row of missing teeth, depending on your needs.

What if I have bone loss?

Bone loss does not automatically prevent treatment. During your initial consultation, our dentist will evaluate the amount of bone available and discuss possible treatment options.
