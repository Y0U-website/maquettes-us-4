At Home Dental Care

In addition to regular visits to the dentist, the best way to keep your mouth healthy is to practice excellent oral care at home .

  • Brush and floss regularly. Use mouthwash daily.
  • Make sure your toothpaste contains fluoride for the most effective prevention against cavities.
  • Avoid tobacco products
  • We also encourage you to take extra precautions when eating hard foods and candies. We don’t want you to break your teeth!

Frequently Asked Questions About Preventive Dentistry in Olathe

What does preventive dentistry include?

Preventive dentistry covers everything we do to protect your smile before problems develop. At Serenity Dental, that includes routine exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, dental sealants, and oral cancer screenings. We also guide you on brushing, flossing, and building habits that support your oral health between visits.

How often should I schedule visits to the dental clinic in Olathe?

Most patients benefit from visiting our dental clinic in Olathe every six months. However, Dr. Silva may recommend more frequent appointments if you have a history of gum disease, cavities, or other ongoing concerns. Consistent visits make it much easier to catch small issues before they turn into costly treatments.

Is preventive dental care covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans cover preventive dentistry services such as cleanings and exams at little or no cost to you, since insurers recognize that prevention saves money in the long run. We recommend calling our office before your visit so we can help you understand your benefits and avoid any surprises.

At what age should children start preventive dental care?

We recommend scheduling your child’s first visit around their first birthday or when their first tooth appears. Starting early helps children build positive associations with dental care and allows Dr. Silva to monitor their development from the very beginning.
