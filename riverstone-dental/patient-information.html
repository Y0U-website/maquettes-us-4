Why is Fluoride Important for My Dental Health?

Fluoride plays a key role in strengthening tooth enamel and preventing the onset or progression of tooth decay. At Riverstone Dental, we apply a long-lasting fluoride varnish designed to adhere to the teeth, harden immediately upon contact, and therefore, reduce the risk of ingestion. This treatment is especially beneficial for those at higher risk of tooth decay, and we may also recommend fluoride toothpaste for daily use.
