Preventive Dentistry 预防齿科

Preventive care is the foundation of everything we do. Every new patient relationship begins with a comprehensive examination that includes a full-mouth series of digital radiographs, periodontal charting, oral cancer screening, and an unhurried conversation about your dental history and current concerns. We use this baseline to build a personalized prevention plan that evolves with you over time. 预防是我们一切工作的基础。每位新患者的就诊都从一次全面检查开始，包括全口数字X光片、牙周检查、口腔癌筛查，以及充分了解您的牙科病史和当前关注的问题。我们以此为基础，为您制定随时间不断调整的个性化预防方案。

Routine visits include professional cleanings performed by our hygiene team, who remove calculus and biofilm from areas that daily brushing and flossing cannot reach. We assess your risk factors for caries and periodontal disease at each appointment and adjust the frequency of your visits accordingly. For patients at elevated risk, we offer prescription-strength fluoride treatments, antimicrobial rinses, and dental sealants on both primary and permanent teeth. 常规复诊包括由我们的口腔卫生团队进行的专业洗牙，清除日常刷牙和使用牙线无法触及的牙石和菌斑。每次复诊时，我们会评估您龋齿和牙周病的风险因素，并相应调整复诊频率。对于高风险患者，我们提供处方级氟化物治疗、抗菌漱口水以及乳牙和恒牙的窝沟封闭。

Our goal is straightforward: catch problems when they are small, manage risk factors before they cause damage, and keep you out of the treatment chair as much as possible. Most of the complex procedures in dentistry become unnecessary when prevention is done well and done consistently. 我们的目标很简单：在问题很小的时候及时发现，在风险因素造成损害之前加以控制，尽可能减少您坐在治疗椅上的时间。当预防工作做得好且持之以恒时，牙科中大部分复杂的治疗都可以避免。

Dental Cleaning & Hygiene 洗牙与口腔卫生

Professional dental cleanings at ZS Dental Care go beyond what brushing and flossing alone can accomplish. Our hygienists use ultrasonic scalers and hand instruments to remove plaque and tartar buildup above and below the gum line, followed by thorough polishing to leave your teeth smooth and refreshed. 致尚牙科的专业洗牙服务远超日常刷牙和使用牙线所能达到的效果。我们的牙科卫生师使用超声波洁牙器和手动器械，清除牙龈线上下的牙菌斑和牙结石，随后进行彻底抛光，让您的牙齿光滑清爽。

We recommend cleanings every six months for most patients, though some individuals with a history of gum disease or heavy tartar buildup may benefit from more frequent visits. Each cleaning appointment includes a comprehensive oral exam so we can catch early signs of decay, gum inflammation, or other concerns before they progress. 我们建议大多数患者每六个月进行一次洗牙，但有牙周病史或牙结石较多的患者可能需要更频繁的检查。每次洗牙都包括全面的口腔检查，以便在蛀牙、牙龈炎症或其他问题恶化之前及早发现。

For patients with early-stage gum disease, we offer deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) to remove bacteria from periodontal pockets and help the gums heal and reattach to the tooth surfaces. We focus on patient education as well, showing you effective brushing and flossing techniques tailored to your specific needs. 对于早期牙周病患者，我们提供深度洁牙（龈下刮治和根面平整），清除牙周袋中的细菌，帮助牙龈愈合并重新附着在牙齿表面。我们同样注重患者教育，根据您的具体需求指导有效的刷牙和使用牙线技巧。

Restorative Dentistry 修复齿科

When decay, fracture, or wear compromises a tooth, restorative treatment aims to return it to full function and appearance while preserving as much natural structure as possible. We offer composite fillings, root canal therapy, and crowns — the three procedures that address the vast majority of damaged teeth we see in practice. 当蛀牙、断裂或磨损损害了牙齿，修复治疗旨在恢复其完整功能和外观，同时尽可能保留天然牙齿结构。我们提供补牙、根管治疗和牙冠修复——这三项手术涵盖了我们在临床中遇到的绝大多数牙齿损伤情况。

Composite fillings are used for small to moderate cavities and are color-matched to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. For deeper infections that reach the nerve, root canal therapy removes the diseased pulp, disinfects the canal system, and seals the tooth to prevent reinfection. When a tooth has lost significant structure, a custom crown restores its shape, strength, and bite alignment. 树脂补牙适用于小到中等程度的龋洞，颜色与天然牙齿完美匹配。对于感染已深入牙髓的情况，根管治疗会清除病变牙髓、彻底消毒根管系统并密封牙齿以防止再次感染。当牙齿结构严重缺损时，定制牙冠可恢复其形态、强度和咬合关系。

Every restorative case begins with a thorough examination and digital imaging so we can assess the extent of damage accurately. We explain your options clearly — including the option of monitoring rather than treating — so you can make an informed decision. Our goal is always the most conservative, long-lasting restoration that fits your situation. 每一例修复治疗都从详细的检查和数字影像开始，以便准确评估损伤程度。我们会清楚地向您解释各种选择——包括观察随访而非立即治疗的方案——让您做出知情的决定。我们的目标始终是最保守、最持久、最适合您情况的修复方案。

Dental Implants 种植牙

Dental implants are the closest thing modern dentistry has to replacing a natural tooth. A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone, where it integrates over several months to create a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis. We use cone-beam CT imaging and guided surgery protocols to plan each implant case with sub-millimeter precision, reducing surgical time and improving predictability. 种植牙是现代牙科中最接近天然牙的替代方案。将一根小型钛合金柱体植入颌骨，经过数月的骨整合，为牙冠、牙桥或全口修复提供稳固基础。我们采用锥形束CT和导板手术方案，以亚毫米级精度规划每一例种植，缩短手术时间，提高可预测性。

We handle the full scope of implant treatment in-house: single-tooth replacements for isolated missing teeth, implant-supported bridges for multiple adjacent gaps, and full-arch solutions for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth. When bone volume is insufficient, we perform ridge augmentation, sinus lifts, and socket preservation grafts to build the foundation needed for long-term implant success. 我们在诊所内提供全面的种植治疗：单颗缺失的单颗种植、多颗相邻缺失的种植支撑牙桥，以及适合全口或大部分牙齿缺失患者的全弓修复方案。当骨量不足时，我们进行骨脊增量、上颌窦底提升和拔牙窝保存术，为种植体的长期成功奠定基础。

Recovery expectations vary by case. Single implants in healthy bone typically involve mild discomfort managed with over-the-counter medication and a few days of dietary modification. More involved procedures like bone grafting or full-arch placement may require a longer healing phase. We provide detailed post-operative instructions and schedule follow-up appointments to monitor healing at every stage. 恢复情况因人而异。健康骨质中的单颗种植通常只有轻微不适，服用非处方止痛药并调整几天饮食即可。骨增量或全弓种植等较复杂的手术可能需要更长的愈合期。我们会提供详细的术后指导，并安排复诊全程监测愈合情况。

Emergency Care 急诊服务

Dental emergencies rarely happen at convenient times, and pain does not wait for a scheduled appointment. We reserve capacity in our daily schedule specifically for urgent cases, which means same-day evaluation is almost always available when you call before midday. If you are experiencing severe pain, swelling, a knocked-out or fractured tooth, or a lost restoration, contact us immediately. 牙科急诊很少发生在方便的时候，而牙痛不会等到预约时间。我们每天专门预留急诊名额，因此在中午前致电，当天即可就诊。如果您正在经历剧烈牙痛、肿胀、牙齿脱落或断裂、或修复体脱落，请立即联系我们。

Our emergency protocol prioritizes rapid diagnosis and effective pain relief. We begin with a focused clinical exam and any necessary imaging to identify the source of the problem, then move directly into treatment or stabilization. Common emergency visits include pulp therapy for acute toothaches, re-cementation of dislodged crowns, management of dental trauma from sports or falls, and drainage of localized infections. 我们的急诊流程优先确保快速诊断和有效止痛。首先进行有针对性的临床检查和必要的影像检查，确定问题根源，然后立即进行治疗或稳定处理。常见急诊包括急性牙痛的牙髓治疗、脱落牙冠的重新粘接、运动或跌倒造成的牙外伤处理，以及局部感染的引流。

After the immediate issue is resolved, we discuss next steps and schedule any follow-up treatment that may be needed. Emergency visits are not a substitute for ongoing care, but they are an essential safety net, and we believe every patient should have access to one without a long wait. 当紧急问题解决后，我们会与您讨论后续步骤，并安排可能需要的进一步治疗。急诊不能替代日常护理，但它是必不可少的安全保障。我们认为每位患者都应该能够及时获得急诊服务，而不必长时间等待。

Pediatric Dentistry 儿童齿科

Children experience the dental office differently than adults, and our approach reflects that. We take extra time during first visits to let young patients explore the environment, ask questions, and build comfort before any instruments are introduced. This investment in rapport pays dividends for years, turning dental care into a familiar routine rather than a source of anxiety. 儿童对牙科诊所的体验与成人不同，我们的方法也因此有所调整。初次就诊时，我们会留出充分的时间，让小患者熟悉环境、提问、建立安全感，然后才引入任何器械。这种对信任关系的投入会在未来多年持续回报，让看牙成为一种熟悉的习惯，而不是焦虑的来源。

Clinical care for children includes age-appropriate examinations with gentle technique, professional fluoride treatments, and pit-and-fissure sealants applied to newly erupted permanent molars to prevent decay in their most vulnerable grooves. We monitor dental development, eruption patterns, and bite relationships at each visit and refer to pediatric specialists or orthodontists when early intervention would be beneficial. 儿童临床护理包括采用轻柔手法的适龄检查、专业氟化物治疗，以及在新萌出的恒磨牙上进行窝沟封闭，预防最易发生龋齿的沟裂部位。每次复诊时，我们监测牙齿发育、萌出规律和咬合关系，并在早期干预有益时转介给儿童牙科专科或正畸医生。

We also spend time with parents and caregivers, discussing brushing technique, diet and snacking habits, fluoride use, and age-specific risk factors. Our goal is to equip families with practical knowledge so that good oral health habits take root early and carry through adolescence and beyond. 我们也会与家长和看护人沟通，讨论刷牙技巧、饮食和零食习惯、氟化物使用以及不同年龄段的风险因素。我们的目标是让家庭掌握实用的口腔健康知识，使良好的口腔习惯从小养成，伴随孩子们度过青少年时期及以后。

Invisalign 隐适美矫正

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, virtually invisible aligners to gradually move your teeth into the desired position. Unlike traditional braces, there are no metal brackets or wires — just smooth, comfortable plastic trays that you can remove for eating, brushing, and flossing. Each set of aligners is worn for one to two weeks before advancing to the next in the series. 隐适美采用一系列定制的、几乎透明的矫正牙套，逐步将牙齿移动到理想位置。与传统金属牙套不同，没有金属托槽或钢丝——只有光滑舒适的塑料牙套，进食、刷牙和使用牙线时均可自行摘取。每副牙套佩戴一到两周后更换下一副。

Treatment begins with a digital scan using our Shining 3D intraoral scanner, which creates a precise 3D model of your teeth. From this model, your clinician designs a complete treatment plan that maps every stage of tooth movement from start to finish. You can preview your projected results before committing to treatment, so there are no surprises along the way. 治疗从使用我们的Shining 3D口内扫描仪进行数字扫描开始，生成精确的牙齿三维模型。医师根据模型设计完整的治疗方案，规划从开始到结束的每一个牙齿移动阶段。您可以在决定治疗前预览预期效果，全程透明无意外。

Most adult cases are completed in 12 to 18 months, though mild crowding or spacing may be resolved in as few as 6 months. We schedule check-in appointments every six to eight weeks to monitor progress and provide your next sets of aligners. Invisalign is effective for a wide range of orthodontic concerns, including crowding, spacing, overbite, underbite, and crossbite. 大多数成人病例在12到18个月内完成，轻度拥挤或间隙问题最快6个月即可解决。我们每六到八周安排一次复查，监测进度并提供下一组牙套。隐适美适用于多种正畸问题，包括牙列拥挤、牙缝过大、深覆合、反颌和交叉咬合。
