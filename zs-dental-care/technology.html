Dental Technology

Technology & Equipment 技术与设备

Accurate diagnosis and predictable outcomes depend on the quality of instrumentation. We invest in advanced imaging and digital scanning systems that allow our clinicians to work with greater precision and less patient discomfort. 精准的诊断和可预期的治疗效果离不开先进的设备支持。我们持续投入高端影像和数字化扫描系统，让医师能够以更高的精度和更少的不适感为患者提供诊疗服务。

Digital X-Ray System 数字X光系统

Our digital radiography system captures high-resolution diagnostic images at up to 90 % less radiation than conventional film X-rays. Images appear on-screen within seconds, allowing immediate chairside review with patients. Enhanced contrast and detail make it easier to detect early-stage decay, hairline fractures, and bone density changes that traditional film can miss. 我们的数字化影像系统能够拍摄高清诊断图像，辐射量比传统胶片X光最多降低90 %。图像在数秒内即可显示在屏幕上，方便医师在椅旁与患者即时查看讨论。更高的对比度和细节表现力，使早期龋齿、细微裂纹及骨密度变化等传统胶片难以发现的问题得以清晰呈现。

Cone Beam 3D Imaging (CBCT) 锥形束三维成像 (CBCT)

Cone beam computed tomography produces full volumetric scans of the jaw, teeth, and surrounding anatomy in a single 15-second rotation. This technology is essential for implant planning, where precise measurement of bone width, height, and proximity to nerves determines surgical success. We also use CBCT for airway analysis, impacted tooth assessment, and TMJ evaluation, replacing the need for hospital-based CT referrals. 锥形束CT通过一次仅需15秒的旋转扫描，即可生成颌骨、牙齿及周围组织的完整三维影像。该技术在种植牙规划中尤为重要——骨骼宽度、高度及与神经距离的精确测量直接关系到手术成效。我们还将CBCT用于气道分析、阻生牙评估和颞下颌关节检查，免去了患者前往医院做CT的不便。

Shining 3D Intraoral Scanner Shining 3D口内扫描仪

The Shining 3D intraoral scanner captures highly detailed digital impressions of your teeth and gums in minutes, replacing traditional impression trays and putty. The scanner generates precise 3D models used for Invisalign treatment planning, crown and bridge fabrication, and surgical guide design. Patients experience greater comfort, and clinicians benefit from faster turnaround and improved accuracy for all restorative and orthodontic workflows. Shining 3D口内扫描仪可在数分钟内完成牙齿和牙龈的高精度数字取模，彻底取代传统的印模托盘和材料。扫描仪生成精确的三维模型，用于隐适美治疗规划、牙冠牙桥制作和手术导板设计。患者体验更加舒适，医师也能获得更快的加工周期和更高的精度，满足各类修复和矫正治疗的需求。

Your Treatment Journey 您的诊疗流程

Every treatment follows a structured process designed for thoroughness, transparency, and patient comfort. 每一项治疗都遵循严谨的流程，确保全面、透明，并以患者的舒适为核心。

Consultation 初诊咨询

Your first visit begins with a thorough discussion of your dental history, current concerns, and treatment goals. We take the time to understand your priorities before any clinical examination, so every recommendation is grounded in what matters most to you. 您的首次就诊将以详细的病史沟通开始，了解您当前的口腔状况和治疗期望。我们会在临床检查之前充分了解您的需求，确保每一项建议都以您最关心的问题为出发点。

Diagnosis 诊断评估

Using digital radiography, 3D imaging, and comprehensive clinical examination, we build a complete picture of your oral health. All findings are reviewed with you on-screen, with clear explanations of what we see and what it means for your care. 通过数字X光、三维影像和全面的临床检查，我们为您的口腔健康建立完整的诊断档案。所有检查结果将在屏幕上与您逐一回顾，清楚地解释我们的发现及其对后续治疗的意义。

Treatment Planning 制定方案

Your clinician develops a detailed plan that addresses both immediate needs and long-term health. When multiple approaches exist, we present each option with realistic timelines, expected outcomes, and transparent cost breakdowns. 医师将为您制定详细的治疗方案，兼顾当前需求和长期口腔健康。当存在多种治疗路径时，我们会逐一介绍每个方案的预期时间、治疗效果和透明的费用明细，供您充分考虑后做出选择。

Procedure 实施治疗

On treatment day, your care team follows the plan with precision, using the advanced technology and techniques described above. Comfort management is tailored to each patient, and we check in throughout to ensure you feel informed and at ease. 治疗当天，诊疗团队严格按照既定方案操作，运用上述先进设备和技术。舒适管理根据每位患者的情况量身定制，治疗过程中我们会持续与您沟通，确保您了解进展并感到安心。

Follow-up Care 术后随访

Recovery instructions are provided in writing, and our team follows up by phone within 48 hours of any procedure. Subsequent visits are scheduled to monitor healing, evaluate results, and adjust your preventive care plan as needed. 我们会提供书面的术后恢复指导，并在任何治疗完成后48小时内进行电话随访。后续复诊将跟踪愈合情况、评估治疗效果，并根据需要调整您的预防保健计划。
