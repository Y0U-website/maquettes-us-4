What are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures in Danville combine traditional dentures’ natural appearance with dental implants’ stability. Unlike regular dentures, which sit directly on your gums and can sometimes slip or shift, Implant-Supported Dentures are attached to implants surgically placed in your jawbone. These implants act as artificial tooth roots, providing a secure and stable foundation for the dentures.

The Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Switching to Implant-Supported Dentures offers numerous life-changing advantages. Here’s why patients in Danville are opting for this innovative dental solution:

  • Enhanced Stability & Comfort: You won’t have to worry about your dentures slipping while you eat or speak. Implants anchor the dentures securely in place, offering unparalleled stability.
  • Improved Chewing Ability: Enjoy your favorite foods again! Implant-supported dentures function like natural teeth, making it easier to chew various foods.
  • Aesthetic Appeal: Implant-supported dentures look and feel like natural teeth, boosting self-confidence and smiling at you, which is worth showing off.
  • Long-Term Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective long-term solution compared to replacing traditional dentures every few years.
  • Prevention of Bone Loss: Dental implants help maintain the integrity of your jawbone, which naturally deteriorates when teeth are missing. This preserves your facial structure and prevents a sunken appearance often seen with conventional dentures.
  • Convenience: Say goodbye to messy adhesives. Implant-supported dentures eliminate the need for creams or pastes, simplifying your daily oral care routine.

Who Are Good Candidates for Implant-Supported Dentures?

Are you a good fit for Implant-Supported Dentures? Here are a few factors to consider:

Sufficient Bone Density Because we place implants in your jawbone, candidates must have enough healthy bone to support them. If bone loss is present, procedures like bone grafting may be an option.

Good Oral Health Candidates should have healthy gums and be free from infections or untreated oral conditions before implanting

Non-smoker or Willing to Quit Smoking can interfere with the healing process and the long-term success of implants. If you smoke, we may ask you to quit before beginning the procedure

Commitment to Oral Hygiene Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential to prevent issues and ensure the longevity of your implants.

The Implant-Supported Denture Procedure

Are you curious about what getting Implant-Supported Dentures entails? Here’s a simplified overview of the process:

During your first visit, your dentist will thoroughly examine your medical history and evaluate your jawbone using imaging such as X-rays or CT scans.

This procedure involves surgically placing dental implants into your jawbone. Depending on your needs, you may have two to four implants per arch. Healing from this step can take a few months as the implants integrate with the bone in osseointegration.

While your dental implants heal, temporary dentures can be provided so that you don’t have to go without teeth during the healing period.

Once osseointegration is complete, small connectors called abutments are attached to the implants. These will secure your dentures.

Custom-designed dentures are attached to the abutments, completing the process. Your dentist will ensure a proper fit and make any final adjustments.

Regular check-ups with your dentist will keep your dentures and implants in optimal condition for the long haul.
