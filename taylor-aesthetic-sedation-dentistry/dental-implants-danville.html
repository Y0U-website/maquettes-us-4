What to Expect When Getting Dental Implants

Dr. Taylor has successfully transformed the smiles of countless patients in Danville and beyond. His expertise, combined with a compassionate approach to patient care, ensures you receive the highest standard of treatment tailored to your unique needs.

Begin your journey with a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Robbie Taylor. This consultation thoroughly examines your oral health, including X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess bone density and structure. Dr. Taylor will discuss your goals, address any concerns, and determine your candidacy for dental implants.

Following the assessment, we will craft a personalized treatment plan. This plan outlines the number of implants needed, the type of restoration, and the overall timeline.

Next is the surgical placement of the dental implants. This step is typically done under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Dr. Taylor precisely places the implants into the jawbone, and you may experience minimal discomfort during this brief procedure.

Post-surgery, a crucial phase begins called osseointegration. This is the process where the implants fuse with the jawbone, creating a strong and stable foundation. The healing period varies but typically takes a few months.

Once osseointegration is complete, abutments – small connector pieces – are attached to the implants. These are anchors for the final restorations and are crucial for ensuring a secure fit.

The moment you’ve been waiting for – the attachment of the final restorations. Whether it’s crowns, bridges, or dentures, these are securely fixed to the abutments, completing your new, functional smile.
