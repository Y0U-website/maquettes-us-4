Meet Kyle Rowley, DDS

Dr. Rowley grew up near Walla Walla. After graduating from Walla Walla High School, he earned his Bachelor’s Degree at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. It was there that he discovered his passion for dentistry.

He moved back to Washington and completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Washington School of Dentistry. He moved to the Tri-Cities in 2018 and has been practicing here ever since.

Dr. Rowley is passionate about providing gentle care with the best quality work possible. He loves keeping up with the newest technology and materials used in modern dentistry. Dr. Rowley enjoys being a dentist because it allows him to give his patients a better smile and healthier teeth.

Dr. Rowley is proud to be an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Washington State Dental Association (WSDA), and the Benton-Franklin County Dental Society.

When he’s not doing dentistry, you’ll find Dr. Rowley enjoying time with his amazing wife and four kids. He also enjoys playing sports, watching football, woodworking, and spending time outdoors.
