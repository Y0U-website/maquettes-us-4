How long do dental implants last?

A well-cared-for implant can last the rest of your life. The titanium post that goes into your jawbone fuses with the bone and tends to stay put for decades. The crown on top of the implant usually lasts 10 to 15 years before it shows enough wear to be replaced, similar to a regular dental crown. Brushing, flossing, and routine cleanings with our Kennewick team are what keep the whole thing in good shape over the long term.
