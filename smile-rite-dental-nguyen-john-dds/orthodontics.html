Frequently Asked Questions

Does Medicaid Cover Orthodontic Treatment For Kids? Medicaid does not usually cover braces unless it’s deemed medically necessary. However, here at Smile Rite Dental we provide braces at a reasonable cost, with in-house payment plans that let you get the smile you deserve without breaking the bank.

At What Age Should My Child See An Orthodontist? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early detection of bite or alignment issues can lead to better treatment outcomes.

How long does orthodontic treatment take? Treatment time varies based on the complexity of the case. Most children wear braces for 12 to 24 months, followed by a retainer to maintain results.

Do braces hurt? Braces may cause slight discomfort initially and after adjustments, but this typically goes away within a few days. We provide tips to manage any discomfort.

What types of braces do you offer? We offer a variety of braces, including:

  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbite, underbite, or crossbite
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Speech problems due to misaligned teeth
  • Traditional Metal Braces (most common and effective)
  • Ceramic Braces (less noticeable, blend with natural teeth)
  • Retainers & Post-Treatment Care to maintain results
