Frequently Asked Questions

How long will my orthodontic treatment take? The length of treatment can vary depending on the complexity of your case, but most patients complete their treatment in 12 to 24 months. Our orthodontist will give you a more specific time frame after your initial consultation.

Do I have to choose traditional braces, or can I get Invisalign? Both traditional braces and Invisalign are excellent options, but the right choice depends on your specific orthodontic needs. For more complex cases, traditional braces may be necessary, while Invisalign is an ideal option for those seeking a more discreet treatment.

How much does orthodontic treatment cost in Highlands, TX? The cost of orthodontic treatment can vary based on the type of braces or aligners you choose. We offer affordable payment options and will work with you to help maximize any insurance coverage you may have. Contact us for more detailed pricing information.

Will my insurance cover orthodontic treatment? We accept many insurance plans and will verify your coverage to ensure you get the benefits you’re entitled to. Our team can also provide payment plans if you don’t have insurance coverage or need assistance.

Can adults get braces or Invisalign? Absolutely! Orthodontic treatment is not limited to children or teenagers. Many adults seek braces or Invisalign to correct issues like crooked teeth or bite problems. It’s never too late to get a smile you’re proud of!

How often will I need to visit the orthodontist during treatment? Typically, you’ll need to visit us every 4-8 weeks to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments to your braces or aligners.

What happens after my braces or Invisalign treatment? After your orthodontic treatment, you’ll need to wear a retainer to maintain your newly aligned smile. Our orthodontist will provide instructions on how often to wear it to ensure your teeth stay in place.
