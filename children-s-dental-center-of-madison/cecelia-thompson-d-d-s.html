Cecelia Thompson, D.D.S.

Dr. Cecelia “Cece” Thompson joined Children’s Dental Center of Madison in July 2014. She and her staff strive to create positive experiences for their young patients as they receive excellent dental care.

Dr. Cece completed her Doctorate of Dental Surgery at Marquette University’s School of Dentistry, Milwaukee, Wisconsin. She practiced general dentistry in Madison prior to attending and earning a Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry from the University of Buffalo and Women and Children’s Hospital of Buffalo, Buffalo, New York.

Dr. Cece grew up in Madison and was a high school math teacher in the Madison area prior to pursuing her career in dentistry. Dr. Cece and her family are ecstatic to be back home and to place solid roots in Wisconsin! Her husband, Christopher, and their children enjoy spending their time in the beautiful Wisconsin outdoors — swimming, traveling and raising animals.

Professionally, Dr. Cece is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, the Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Dane County Dental Society.
