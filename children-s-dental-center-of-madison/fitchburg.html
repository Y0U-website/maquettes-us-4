The American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), American Dental Association (ADA),,

and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) each recommend establishing a “Dental Home” for your child by the age of one. Studies have shown that a child who has a regular dental home is more likely to receive the preventive and routine oral health care necessary for a happy and healthy smile. We hope you choose Children’s Dental Center of Madison in Fitchburg as your child’s Dental Home!
