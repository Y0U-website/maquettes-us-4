What Are Dental Implant Restorations?

Dental implant restorations are the visible crowns, bridges, or dentures attached to implants surgically placed in your jawbone. While the implant itself serves as an artificial tooth root, the restoration is what you see and use every day. Think of it as a two-part system: the foundation and the structure built on top.

These restorations are custom-crafted to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and function. Modern materials like porcelain and zirconia create results so lifelike that most people won’t notice the difference between your restoration and your natural teeth. The connection between implant and restoration is engineered for stability, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile without worry.

The beauty of implant restorations lies in their versatility. Single crowns replace individual teeth, bridges span multiple missing teeth, and full-arch restorations can transform an entire upper or lower jaw. Each option is designed to work with your unique anatomy and aesthetic goals.

The Restoration Process at Seattle Smiles Company

Your journey to a new smile follows a clear, predictable path from initial evaluation through final placement.

We begin with a free 3D CT evaluation to examine your bone structure, plan precise implant placement, and design restorations that integrate seamlessly with your facial features. This imaging-first approach ensures your final result is planned with exceptional accuracy before a single incision is made.

After implant placement, a healing period allows osseointegration to occur — your jawbone fuses with the titanium implant, creating a foundation as stable as natural tooth roots. This process typically takes several months, though immediate temporary restorations may be placed in some instances so you leave with a smile.

Once healing is complete, we use digital scanners to capture exact impressions without messy materials, ensuring your crown, bridge, or denture fits perfectly. We carefully adjust the restoration for proper bite alignment and natural appearance before final placement. Our team remains available 24/7 for any questions or concerns throughout recovery.
