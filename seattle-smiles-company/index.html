The practice

Seattle Smiles Company offers a full array of dental services to meet your oral health needs. Whether you’re seeking routine preventive care or advanced surgical procedures, our experienced Seattle dentist and professional team deliver personalized treatment using state-of-the-art equipment at our downtown location.

Our general dentistry services form the basis of excellent dental health. We provide comprehensive dental cleanings and exams to prevent issues before they develop, and tooth extractions when necessary. Understanding that dental emergencies don’t follow a schedule, we offer emergency dental care available 24/7 with same-day appointments.

Our general services also include protective treatments such as night guards for teeth grinding and sports mouthguards to keep your smile safe during athletic activities. Additionally, we offer advanced preventive services, including oral cancer screening for early detection and sleep apnea treatment, to boost your overall health and quality of life.

We specialize in restorative dentistry solutions that rebuild and restore damaged or missing teeth. Our dental implants provide a permanent solution for missing teeth, and we can help patients who have been told implants aren’t an option due to bone loss or other complications. Dr. Ajay Kashi completed his initial dental training at Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences in Bangalore, India, and furthered his expertise with a Doctorate in Biomedical Engineering from the State University of New York Downstate Medical Center. He is also an esteemed member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

Since founding Seattle Smiles Company in 2023, Dr. Kashi has been dedicated to delivering exemplary dental care, building lasting relationships and ensuring that every patient receives the attentive care they deserve. He also travels around Seattle to perform implant surgery at other offices as well as make house calls for seniors and others who need at-home care.
