Who Is a Good Candidate for All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 works well for patients who are missing most or all of their teeth in one or both arches, have severely damaged teeth requiring full extraction, or currently wear dentures and want a stable, permanent alternative. Good candidates have adequate bone density in the front jaw regions and maintain good general health.

Certain conditions, such as uncontrolled diabetes or active gum disease, need to be managed before treatment. Smoking can affect healing, so we discuss this during the consultation. Age is rarely a limiting factor. Dr. Kashi works with patients from their twenties to their eighties, with treatment plans tailored to each person’s bone quality and overall health.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Implants

Here are answers to the questions patients ask most often before their first consultation.

With proper care, All-on-4 implants are designed to last for many years. The implants themselves, once fully integrated with your jawbone, can last a lifetime in many cases. The prosthetic arch may require maintenance or replacement over the decades, depending on the materials used and how well the restoration is cared for. Regular cleanings and follow-up visits at Seattle Smiles Company support long-term success.

Many patients told they need bone grafting for traditional implants can avoid it with the All-on-4 technique. The angled rear implants are specifically designed to work with available bone in areas where density is typically preserved even after tooth loss. A free 3D CT evaluation gives Dr. Kashi an accurate picture of your bone and allows him to determine the best path for your specific anatomy.

The procedure is performed under sedation, so most patients report little to no discomfort during surgery. We offer multiple sedation levels, including IV sedation and general anesthesia administered by certified registered nurse anesthetists. Post-operative discomfort is typically managed well with prescribed medications, and most patients are surprised by how manageable the recovery is.

All-on-4 uses exactly four implants to support a full arch, making it effective for most patients without requiring bone grafting. All-on-X uses five to eight implants and is recommended for patients with specific anatomical considerations or those who want additional support. Dr. Kashi will assess which approach is better suited to your bone structure and goals during your consultation.

All-on-4 restorations are cared for similarly to natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss or use interdental brushes around the implant connection points, and attend regular professional cleanings. Unlike dentures, there are no soaking solutions or adhesives required. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and ongoing support through our practice and membership plan.
