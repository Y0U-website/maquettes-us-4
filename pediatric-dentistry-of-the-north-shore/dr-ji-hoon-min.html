Dr. Ji-Hoon Min

Meet Dr. Ji-Hoon Min Dr. Ji-Hoon Min Dr. Ji-Hoon Min (Dr. Ji) was born in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada and grew up in the Upper Peninsula of Michigan. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biopsychology, Cognition, and Neuroscience from the University of Michigan, followed by both his Master of Science in Medical Physiology and Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio. Dr. Ji went on to complete his pediatric dentistry residency at the University of Illinois Chicago.

He is a proud member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Illinois Society of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, and Chicago Dental Society.

Dr. Ji is dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care tailored to the unique needs of infants, children, and adolescents. Whether it’s a routine cleaning or specialized treatment, Dr. Ji strives to create a warm, welcoming environment where children feel comfortable and families feel confident in his care.

In his free time, Dr. Ji enjoys playing golf, trying new restaurants with his wife, and practicing his DJing. Fun fact: he used to coach junior golf during dental school! Dr. Ji looks forward to meeting—and growing—with you and your family!
