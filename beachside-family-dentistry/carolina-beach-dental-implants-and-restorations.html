Frequently Asked Questions

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that are placed in the jaw to support a replacement tooth or bridge.

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Most healthy individuals with sufficient jawbone density are good candidates for dental implants.

The cost of dental implants can vary depending on the complexity of the case and any additional procedures needed, but they are generally considered a long-term investment in oral health.
