Questions? We’re Here to Help!

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office during business hours, using our online contact form, or booking through our patient portal . We’ll work with you to find a convenient time that fits your schedule.

We accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and MetLife. Our front desk team will verify your benefits and help you understand your coverage before your visit.

We offer flexible payment options including cash, credit cards, and SUNBIT , PatientFi , & HFD financing . We also provide in-house payment plans for larger treatments to help make dental care more affordable.

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), a list of current medications, and any previous dental records or X-rays. Arriving 15 minutes early helps us start your appointment on time.

Yes, we provide emergency dental care for urgent situations like severe tooth pain, dental trauma, or broken teeth. Call our office immediately, and we’ll arrange prompt treatment to address your emergency.
