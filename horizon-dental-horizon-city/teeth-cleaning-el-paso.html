The Power of Comprehensive Dental Exams

A thorough dental examination is far more than a quick look at your teeth. Our experienced dentists at Horizon Dental conduct detailed assessments that can reveal problems before they become painful or expensive to treat.

During your exam, we carefully inspect every aspect of your oral health. We check for signs of tooth decay, gum disease, oral cancer, and other conditions that might not cause symptoms in their early stages. Our dentists also evaluate your bite, jaw function, and the overall alignment of your teeth.

Regular dental exams, X-rays, and cleanings form the foundation of excellent oral health. What to Expect During Your Exam Your comfort and understanding are our priorities. We take time to explain what we’re looking for and why each step matters for your long-term oral health.

We examine your teeth for signs of wear, cracks, or decay. Even tiny cavities that you can’t see or feel get identified during these visits. We also check existing fillings, crowns, and other dental work to ensure they’re still functioning properly.

Your gums receive special attention during the exam. We measure the depth of pockets around each tooth to detect early signs of gum disease. Healthy gums are crucial for keeping your teeth secure and your mouth free from infection.

The soft tissues in your mouth, including your tongue, cheeks, and throat, are also carefully examined. This screening helps us detect oral cancer and other conditions that can develop without obvious symptoms.

Advanced Digital X-Ray Technology

Digital X-rays have revolutionized how we diagnose and treat dental problems. At Horizon Dental, we use the latest digital imaging technology to provide accurate diagnoses while minimizing your exposure to radiation.

These detailed images reveal problems that can’t be seen during a visual examination alone. X-rays show us what’s happening beneath your gums and between your teeth, areas that are impossible to see otherwise.

Types of Dental X-Rays We Use

Different types of X-rays serve specific purposes in your dental care. Bitewing X-rays help us detect decay between teeth and monitor bone levels around your teeth. These images are especially important for identifying problems in their earliest stages.

Periapical X-rays show the entire tooth, from crown to root tip. These images help us evaluate the health of tooth roots and surrounding bone structure. They’re essential for diagnosing abscesses, root fractures, and other conditions affecting the tooth’s foundation.

Panoramic X-rays provide a complete view of your entire mouth in a single image. These comprehensive images help us assess wisdom teeth, jaw joints, and overall oral development patterns.

Safety and Comfort in Digital Imaging

Digital X-rays use up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film X-rays. The images appear instantly on our computer screens, allowing us to discuss findings with you immediately. This technology also eliminates the chemicals needed for film processing, making our practice more environmentally friendly.

We follow strict safety protocols to protect you during X-ray procedures. Lead aprons and thyroid collars provide additional protection, though the radiation exposure from digital dental X-rays is extremely minimal.

Professional Teeth Cleaning: Beyond What You Can Do at Home

Even the most diligent home care routine can’t replace professional teeth cleaning. Our skilled dental hygienists use specialized tools and techniques to remove plaque and tartar that accumulate despite regular brushing and flossing.

Professional cleanings go far beyond surface cleaning. We remove hardened tartar that forms below the gum line, where your toothbrush and floss can’t reach. This thorough cleaning helps prevent gum disease and keeps your mouth healthy between visits.

The Deep Clean Difference

Our hygienists use ultrasonic scalers that vibrate at high frequencies to break up tartar deposits. These advanced tools clean more effectively and comfortably than traditional hand instruments alone. The process removes bacterial buildup that can lead to cavities, gum inflammation, and bad breath.

After scaling, we polish your teeth with special pastes that remove surface stains and smooth tooth surfaces. This polishing step makes it harder for plaque to stick to your teeth, helping maintain your oral health between visits.

We also provide personalized instruction on proper brushing and flossing techniques. Many people are surprised to learn they’ve been using incorrect methods for years. Our team shows you the most effective ways to clean your teeth and gums at home.

Early Detection Saves Money and Discomfort

The combination of thorough exams, advanced X-rays, and professional cleaning creates a powerful prevention system. Early detection of problems means simpler, less expensive treatments and better long-term outcomes for your oral health.

Small cavities can be repaired with simple fillings. When cavities go undetected, they can grow large enough to require crowns, root canals, or even tooth extraction. Regular checkups help us catch these problems when they’re easiest and least expensive to treat.

Gum disease in its early stages can often be reversed with professional cleaning and improved home care. Advanced gum disease requires more extensive treatment and can lead to tooth loss if left untreated.
