Understanding Your All-On-Four Options

At Horizon Dental, we offer comprehensive All-On-Four solutions tailored to your specific needs and oral health situation.

Full Arch All-On-Four Implant Dentures

Perfect for patients who are missing all or most of their teeth in the upper or lower arch, full All-On-Four treatment provides complete smile restoration.

  • Eliminates the bulky palate coverage of traditional upper dentures
  • Restores natural taste and temperature sensation
  • Provides superior retention and stability
  • Maintains proper lip support for a youthful appearance
  • Prevents tongue interference common with traditional lower dentures
  • Eliminates the rocking motion that plagues conventional dentures
  • Provides the chewing power needed for your favorite foods
  • Maintains proper facial height and jawline

Partial All-On-Four Solutions

For patients who still have some healthy natural teeth but need to replace multiple missing teeth in a row, partial All-On-Four offers an excellent solution.

  • Patients missing 4-6 consecutive teeth
  • Those with failing bridges that need replacement
  • Individuals who want to avoid removable partial dentures
  • Patients seeking a permanent solution for multiple tooth loss
  • Preserves healthy natural teeth
  • Eliminates the need for removable appliances
  • Provides seamless integration with existing teeth
  • Offers superior function compared to traditional bridges
