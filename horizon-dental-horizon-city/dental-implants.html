Preserve Your Natural Teeth

Traditional bridges require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth to support the replacement tooth. Dental implants eliminate this need, preserving your remaining natural teeth and maintaining better overall oral health.

Prevent Bone Loss

When you lose a tooth, the underlying jawbone begins to deteriorate. Dental implants stimulate the bone just like natural tooth roots, preventing this bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.

What to Expect During Your Dental Implant Procedure

We understand that knowing what to expect can help ease any anxiety about your treatment. Our team will guide you through each step of your dental implant journey.

Initial Consultation and Planning

Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation including:

  • Digital X-rays and 3D imaging to assess bone density
  • Complete oral health examination
  • Discussion of your goals and expectations
  • Creation of your personalized treatment plan

Implant Placement Surgery

On the day of your surgery, we ensure your comfort with local anesthesia and sedation options if desired. The procedure typically involves:

  • Precise placement of titanium implants into your jawbone
  • Temporary tooth placement (when appropriate)
  • Post-operative care instructions for optimal healing

Healing and Integration Period

The healing process, called osseointegration, typically takes 3-6 months. During this time:

  • Your implants fuse with your natural bone
  • We provide temporary teeth to maintain your appearance
  • Regular check-ups ensure proper healing
  • We monitor your progress and address any concerns

Final Restoration Placement

Once your implants have fully integrated, we complete your treatment by:

  • Taking precise impressions for your permanent teeth
  • Crafting your custom restorations in our partner lab
  • Placing your beautiful, permanent teeth
  • Making final adjustments for perfect fit and comfort
