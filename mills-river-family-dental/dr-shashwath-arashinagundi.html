Dr. Shashwath Arashinagundi

Dr. Shashwath Arashinagundi is a proud South Carolina native with a strong passion for providing thoughtful, patient-centered dental care. He earned his undergraduate degree from Clemson University before going on to complete his dental training at the Medical University of South Carolina, where he developed a comprehensive clinical foundation and a commitment to excellence in dentistry.

As a dentist, Dr. Arashinagundi finds the most fulfillment in building meaningful relationships with his patients. He understands that visiting the dentist can feel intimidating for many people, and he takes pride in creating a welcoming, comfortable environment where patients feel heard, respected, and at ease. His approach is centered on clear communication, gentle care, and helping each patient feel confident not only in their treatment, but in their smile.

Dr. Arashinagundi is dedicated to staying current with advancements in dental techniques and technology, ensuring his patients receive high-quality, modern care tailored to their individual needs. Whether it’s routine preventive care or more complex treatment, he is committed to delivering results that support long-term oral health and overall well-being.

Outside of the office, Dr. Arashinagundi enjoys spending time outdoors and staying active. You’ll often find him on the golf course, exploring hiking trails, or experimenting in the kitchen with new recipes. He values balance in both his personal and professional life and enjoys connecting with others through shared interests and experiences.

He is excited to be part of the Western North Carolina community and looks forward to serving the wonderful people of the region for years to come.
