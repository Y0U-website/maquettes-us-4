Meet Dr. Brian Pearce, co-owner and one of our friendly and experienced dentists!

Hello my name is Dr. Brian Pearce. I recognize receiving dental care for some can be a daunting and even fearful experience. I am honored that you are trusting me to help heal and maintain your mouth and look forward to meeting you and hearing your story.

When you take a seat in my dental chair you are my VIP for that appointment. My sole purpose will be to understand your needs and provide the highest quality of care I can give you. Simply put, I want to take care of you.

I received my undergraduate degree from BYU in business and received my dental degree from Midwestern College of Dental Medicine in Arizona. I then spent four years serving in the United States Navy as a dentist at Parris Island. It was my privilege to provide dental care for the future first line defenders of freedom at one of two marine recruit training centers in the US, located in Beaufort SC.

I am happily married to the love of my life as we raise our four vibrant children. My favorite hobby is spending time with my family. I also enjoy swimming, boating, boogie boarding, skateboarding, biking, and exploring with my wife and kids. I welcome you to our dental family and am excited to meet you.
