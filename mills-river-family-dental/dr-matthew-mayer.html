Dr. Matthew Mayer

Meet Dr. Matthew Mayer, one of our experienced and friendly dentists!

Dr. Matthew Mayer was inspired to become a dentist by his own family dentist near Greensboro, who always had a smile and treated everyone with respect. Each day in his career, Dr. Mayer feels fulfilled serving the community and helping people achieve their ideal health and smile goals.

Over the years, he has served with Habitat for Humanity, the Bainbridge Island Land Trust, Medical Teams International, the Foundation for International Medical Relief of Children, and Missions of Mercy. Contributing to the local community and reaching out to international communities in need are part of his core values.
