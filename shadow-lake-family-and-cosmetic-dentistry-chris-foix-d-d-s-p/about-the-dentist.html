About Shadow Lake Family and Cosmetic Dentistry

Shadow Lake Dental has always maintained a distinguished reputation in the Papillion, Omaha, and Bellevue, NE areas as a top notch source for quality dental care using the latest proven materials and minimally invasive methods. Dr. Foix and his staff members treat patients the way that they want to be treated and strive only for 100 % patient satisfaction.

Dr. Foix graduated from Creighton Dental School with a degree in Dental Surgery in 2001. Prior to attending Creighton, he attended Creighton Prep High School and the University of Nebraska-Omaha where he earned a B.S. in Biotechnology. Dr. Foix loves being a dentist, attending continuing education and expanding his skill set.

Dr. Foix treasures spending time with his wife Susan and their three children: Jack, Lily and Henry. He’s been a fan of Nebraska college football (Go Big Red!!!) since his father took him to his first game at historic Memorial Stadium when he was 7 years old. Besides live sporting events, Dr. Foix loves to travel with his wife and family, fishing, loves reading and is a fan of Jimmy Buffett.

Dr. Foix is a member of the American Dental Association, Nebraska Dental Association and the Omaha District Dental Association. He has served as President of the Omaha Dental Society and as a Delegate to the NDA. Dr. Foix regularly provides dental care for patients through charitable organizations.
