Our Mission and Commitment

At Clearstone, Drs. James Vu and Tina Vo take a gentle and conservative approach to dentistry, focusing on prevention, education, and truly understanding each patient’s needs. Their experience and compassion guide every decision, helping you feel confident and comfortable in your care.

From teeth whitening and crowns to dentures, invisible aligners, implants, and more, we offer a full range of general and cosmetic dentistry services designed to restore and enhance your smile. We treat every patient with kindness and respect, always putting your best interests first.

Our goal is to build lasting relationships and serve our community for many years to come welcoming every patient into the Clearstone family. We’d love to meet you. Schedule your appointment today

Core Values

Patient comfort is central to every treatment we provide We maintain strict standards for quality and safety Efficient care designed to respect your time and wellbeing Dr. Vo grew up in Longview, Texas, and later earned her degree in Biochemistry from Baylor University in Waco. After her first dental visit post-graduation, she discovered her true passion for dentistry and committed herself to pursuing the profession.

She went on to attend Baylor College of Dentistry, where she developed strong clinical proficiency in root canals, crowns and bridges, dentures and partials, surgical procedures, and all aspects of general dentistry. Dr. Vo is dedicated to lifelong learning and has continued to expand her skills through advanced training in cosmetic procedures, clear aligner therapy, and oral conscious sedation. She is known for her gentle, compassionate approach and strives to ensure every patient children and adults alike receives comfortable, attentive, and personalized care.

Outside the office, Dr. Vo enjoys life with her college sweetheart and their two young daughters. She loves spending time with family, tackling puzzle games, and cheering on Dallas sports teams with her husband and friends.

Tina Vo DDS

Dr. Vu is a New Orleans native and after completing his high school education at Jesuit High School, he went on to Baylor University and began his Texas roots. After receiving his Bachelor of Arts degree from Baylor, Dr. Vu earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas at Houston School of Dentistry. Dr. Vu and his wife moved to Allen in 2015 where they are excited to raise their three children and call Allen home.

Dr. Vu has taken many hours of continuing education to provide comprehensive dentistry for his patients. He is detail oriented and always looks out for the best interests of his patients. As a patient himself, Dr. Vu is not a fan of the dental experience and has adopted a philosophy of making that experience as pleasant as possible.

Out of the office, Dr. Vu enjoys spending time with his family as much as possible whether it be traveling, skiing, youth sports or just spending time at home. Dr. Vu is also the official dentist for the Rough Riders and formerly Allen Americans. He thoroughly enjoys serving Allen and it’s surrounding community.

James Vu DDS
