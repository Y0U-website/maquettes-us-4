Dental FAQs

  • How do you ensure patient comfort during treatments? Our team uses gentle techniques and modern anesthesia options to minimize discomfort, and we prioritize clear communication throughout your appointment.
  • What are your office hours and appointment policies? We are open Monday, Wednesday, Thursday from 8 AM to 5 PM and Tuesday from 8 AM to 2 PM. Appointments can be scheduled online or by phone, and we ask for 24 hours’ notice for cancellations.
  • Do you accept dental insurance? Yes, we accept most major insurance plans. Our staff can assist you with verifying coverage and explaining your benefits before treatment.
  • What should I do if I have a dental emergency? Contact us immediately for urgent issues like severe pain, bleeding, or injury. We offer same-day emergency appointments to address your needs promptly.
