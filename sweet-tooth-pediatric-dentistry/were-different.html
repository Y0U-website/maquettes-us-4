Meet Dr. Devina Shah

Dr. Devina Shah is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist and also a New Jersey native! She is passionate about developing relationships with children and their parents, building trust, and boosting confidence.

Dr. Shah believes strongly in being a positive force for change. She has volunteered on several dental outreach trips to the Dominican Republic, Cambodia, and Mexico, providing free pediatric dental care to children with limited access to care. She has also participated in Give Kids A Smile, a national charity event providing free dental care for several years.

In her spare time, Dr. Shah enjoys baking (she has a sweet tooth!), photography, traveling, and spending time with family, friends and her pup, Bailey!
