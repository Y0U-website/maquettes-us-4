Dental Visits for Teens and Children

Park West Dental Care also welcomes families looking for a family dentist in Idaho Falls . Appointments for teens ages 14–18 are typically scheduled for approximately 60 minutes , while appointments for children under 14 are generally about 30 minutes .

We recommend beginning regular dental exams around age 3, unless you notice a concern with your child’s teeth or oral health earlier.

For younger children, their first appointment may simply be a fun “happy visit.” This gives your child an opportunity to explore the dental office, meet our friendly team, and become comfortable with the sights and sounds of a dental visit.

When your child is ready, we can brush and count their teeth, let them take a ride in the dental chair, and send them home with a new toothbrush and a special prize . Our goal is to help children develop a positive relationship with dental care from an early age.
