The practice

Experience complete dental care designed to restore, enhance, and maintain your oral health. From preventive care to advanced restorations, our team delivers personalized solutions for every smile.

Hi, I’m Casey Bennett, DMD and I’m proud to follow in the footsteps of my dad, who is also a dentist. From an early age, I watched how dentistry could improve not only smiles, but confidence and quality of life, and it inspired me to build a career around thoughtful, patient-first care.

I’m known as a digital dentist with a focus on conservative adhesive dentistry approaches designed to preserve tooth structure while creating results that feel natural, beautiful, and comfortable. My goal is simple: deliver dentistry that looks great, functions beautifully, and is as efficient as it can be.

Beyond the chair, I’m deeply involved in education and industry advancement. I’m a faculty member at the MOD Institute in Charleston, South Carolina, where I help create online content for the MOD Pro services and teach courses focused on 3-D printing and digital design. It’s something I genuinely love because when clinicians stay current and continue learning, patients benefit.

I also stay connected to the community that has become home. We’ve enjoyed supporting local events, and most recently we sponsored the annual Cajun Festival on James Island at the County Park a great celebration with great people.

Recognition and professional growth matter to me as well. I was recently published in Dentistry Today (April issue) with an article covering Sprintray’s new flexible partial denture resin, reflecting my ongoing interest in practical innovations that improve outcomes and patient comfort.

When I’m not practicing dentistry or teaching, I’m focused on family life being a father of two to Henry (7) and Lillian Beatrice ( Bea, 2). I’m married to my wife Abby, and we’ve lived on James Island since 2013. I spend most of my free time playing outside with the kids backyard time, walks, kickball, trampoline sessions, and bike rides. I’m also lucky to spend time making music with my band…

I believe great dentistry is built on trust, communication, and modern tools used with care. If you’re looking for a dentist who blends conservative, aesthetic dentistry with digital precision, I’d be honored to help you take the next step toward the smile you want.
