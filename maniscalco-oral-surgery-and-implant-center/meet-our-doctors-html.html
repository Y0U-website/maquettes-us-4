Board-Certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Oral surgeons aren’t required to attain board certification in order to practice Dr. Maniscalco has made that commitment voluntarily. Board certification represents the highest level of education, training, knowledge, and experience possible, with recertification required every 10 years.

Our Story

Today, Board Certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon Dr. Lee Maniscalco leads the practice with a focus on advanced implant dentistry and complex bone grafting procedures. Through extensive continuing education, including over 100 hours of advanced surgical education with Michael Pikos, a world-renowned authority in implant surgery, Dr. Maniscalco incorporates the latest techniques and innovations into patient care.

This advanced training allows our practice to offer solutions for patients who may have previously been told they are not candidates for dental implants. By combining sophisticated imaging technology with precise surgical techniques, we are often able to restore function and confidence in complex cases.
