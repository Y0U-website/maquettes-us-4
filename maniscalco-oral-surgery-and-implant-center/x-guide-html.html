X-Guide Dental Implant Navigation in Birmingham, AL

Precision matters when it comes to dental implant placement. Even a small deviation in the angle or depth of an implant can affect its long-term stability, along with how your final restoration looks and functions. At Maniscalco Oral Surgery and Implant Center , we use X-Guide dynamic 3D navigation to give your surgeon a clearer, more accurate way to plan and place each implant.

X-Guide extends the capabilities of our cone beam imaging system, allowing your surgeon to visualize your jawbone, teeth, and surrounding soft tissue during the procedure itself, not solely from imaging captured beforehand.

This real-time feedback allows for adjustments in the moment, so your treatment plan can be followed with a level of control that traditional freehand methods cannot match. For patients considering dental implants in Birmingham, AL, this technology is one more way our practice works to protect your time, comfort, and results.

What to Expect with 3D X-Guide Implant Surgery

Before your procedure, our team uses X-Guide’s planning software to map the ideal position, angle, and depth for your implant based on your own anatomy. If you’ve also had an intraoral scan, we can incorporate that information too, so your new implant is planned with your bite and adjacent teeth in mind.

During surgery, a small clip tracks your jaw’s exact position while a chairside monitor displays the drill’s location relative to your planned path. The system’s patented X-Point Target gives your surgeon a single, continuously updated view of the drill and surrounding anatomy, similar to following turn-by-turn directions rather than a static map.

Because this guidance happens in real time, our team can respond immediately to what they see instead of relying only on a plan set before the procedure began.

Your X-Guide Dental Implant Questions, Answered

It extends the capabilities of cone beam imaging by adding real-time guidance during the actual surgery, rather than relying only on a treatment plan created beforehand.

Yes. X-Guide tracks your jaw, teeth, and soft tissue using a radiation-free camera system rather than additional X-rays. These real-time images are combined with your original cone beam scan to guide your surgeon during placement, so no extra radiation exposure is needed during the procedure itself.

Traditional surgical guides are typically fabricated in advance and fixed once created. X-Guide’s navigation software allows our team to make adjustments in real time during the procedure and, in some cases, complete guided surgery the same day rather than waiting on a separate guide.

Because X-Guide allows for precise placement with fewer adjustments, many patients experience a smoother procedure and a more predictable recovery. Your surgeon can tailor treatment to your specific anatomy from the first step, which supports both function and appearance in your final results.

We use this technology on all of our dental implant patients. The system is completely safe and it works alongside the cone beam imaging already used for implant planning. During your consultation, our team will briefly explain how guided imaging will be incorporated into your treatment.
