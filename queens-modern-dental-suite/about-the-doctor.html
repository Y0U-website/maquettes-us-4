Dr. Circe Lassegue

Dr. Circe Lassegue is a cosmetic dentist in Queens Modern Dental who always puts her patients’ health at the forefront of her decision making process. After graduating from the University of Florida with a Bachelor’s of Science degree in Psychology, she went to continue her studies at New York University College of Dentistry. Dr. Lassegue completed her first year of General Practice Residency at BronxCare Health System followed by a year of General Practice Residency in Advanced Prosthodontics at Montefiore Medical Center.

While school may have brought her to NYC, Dr. Lassegue has made a home in the city. She has enmeshed herself as part of the community by teaching residents at Harlem Hospital to become more proficient practitioners. When out of scrubs, Dr. Lassegue likes to experience all that New York has to offer, from Broadway plays and dining in the city to traveling upstate to commune with nature.

Dr. Lassegue is dedicated to comprehensive, patient-focused care integrating the most updated biomedical, clinical, and behavioral sciences. Her general, cosmetic, & pediatric dentistry treatments are based on the latest biomaterials and technologies to provide patients with the highest standard of care. With her advanced education in Prosthodontics , Dr. Lassegue enjoys focusing on restoring patients’ smiles with fixed or removable prostheses.

Dr. Charles Buchanan

Dr. Charles Buchanan is a general dentist in Queens Modern Dental focused on delivering the highest quality care with minimal anxiety. After obtaining his Bachelors of Science in Biology from the State University of New York at Albany, Dr. Buchanan earned his doctorate from New York University College of Dentistry. He continued his training in a rigorous residency program at Bronxcare Health System. After focusing on pediatric patients while in Texas and returning to the Bronx to serve the homeless, Dr. Buchanan has returned to Queens to continue his passion for family and aesthetic dentistry.

Outside of the practice, Dr. Buchanan loves trying new cuisines and thrill chasing on the slopes and at theme parks. He also enjoys family time, with his wife and son, exploring new sights in New York City.
