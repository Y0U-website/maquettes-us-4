What Is Bone Grafting?

Bone grafting is a procedure that rebuilds areas of the jawbone that have weakened or deteriorated over time. When a tooth is lost, the surrounding bone may begin to shrink, reducing the amount of support available for future dental implants. Bone grafting helps restore lost bone volume and creates a stronger foundation for long-term dental health.

Who Needs Bone Grafting?

Many patients are surprised to learn they may need bone grafting before receiving dental implants. If there is insufficient bone available to support an implant, grafting may be recommended to strengthen the area and improve treatment outcomes.

FAQ Section

Do I need bone grafting before dental implants?

Some patients require bone grafting when there is insufficient bone available to support a dental implant.

Is bone grafting painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort, and treatment is performed with local anesthesia for comfort.

How long does bone grafting take to heal?

Healing times vary, but most grafts require several months before implant placement.

Can bone grafting prevent future bone loss?

Yes. Bone grafting helps restore and preserve jawbone volume, improving long-term oral health.
