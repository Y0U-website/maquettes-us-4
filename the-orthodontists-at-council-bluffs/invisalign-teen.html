How does Invisalign Teen work?

The Invisalign Teen system is a set of customized clear aligners that are easily removable, enabling adolescent patients to straighten their smile without anyone knowing they are going through orthodontic treatment. Here are a few points to better explain the Invisalign Teen system:

  • Wear each set of aligners for approximately 1-week
  • Remove to eat, drink, brush, and floss Teeth will gradually move with each set of aligners
  • Visit The Orthodontists every 8-10 weeks
  • Total treatment time averages 6-15 months depending on the case
  • Average number of aligners needed is between 18-30

What are the benefits of Invisalign Teen?

Free replacement trays Want to know what the best thing about Invisalign Teen is? Free refinement trays included in comprehensive treatment (up to 6)! Lost your Invisalign tray? No sweat – we’ll replace it for you! This is what makes Invisalign Teen a great option for many of our teenage patients. At your first visit, one of our talented doctors will be able to tell you if you’re a candidate for Invisalign Teen treatment.

Compliance Indicator Tabs with Invisalign Teen Want to know if your teenager is wearing their Invisalign trays like they’re supposed to? We can tell! Invisalign Teen comes with Compliance Indicator Tabs. These tabs indicate whether the patient has been wearing their trays like they are supposed to or not. These tabs make patients more compliant with Invisalign!
