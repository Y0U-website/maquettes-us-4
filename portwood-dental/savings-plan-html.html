WE SEE IT ALL THE TIME

A patient calls in asking if we have availability in our schedule to come in right away for an “Emergency Exam”. They are experiencing pain, and now dental treatment is extremely important. The diagnosis is often the same, substantial decay has compromised tooth structure, it often requires a root canal and crown, or an extraction followed by a bridge or implant.

Can it be avoided? Yes, absolutely. Timing and consistency are critical; we can prevent problems, and catch them early for an easy fix. If only there were some kind of motivation that was as powerful as pain.

We don’t want your dental visit to be something that is avoided. That is why we’ve designed a solution that makes regular care affordable, and a reward program to motivate you so pain doesn’t have to.

Please share our plan with friends, family, and others in our community. Help them to understand the progression of dental disease so we can convert the daily, pain motivated, emergency visits into celebratory, healthy, consistent care.
