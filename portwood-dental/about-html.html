I met my wonderful husband, Seth, during dental school. We have been married for five years and we have a sweet puppy named Max, a rat terrier, Chihuahua, and Australian shepherd mix we adopted through Dante’s Hope.

I have always enjoyed being active in the community and giving back. There is always a sense of fulfillment when you are active in improving the lives of those around you.

Portwood Dental hosts a biannual philanthropic drive at our office. In the summer, we host a "Take a Bite out of Hunger" food drive to benefit the Baton Rouge Food Bank. During Christmas, we host a "Deck the Halls" of Our Lady of the Lake Children’s Hospital toy drive to benefit the local children’s hospital and help families receiving care during the holidays.

Personally, I am a committee member of the Louisiana Mission of Mercy project through the Louisiana Dental Association. This project is a day of free dental work for the underprivileged and needy throughout the local areas. I have participated in many of these projects before and this is my first year as a committee member. I have been a participant of the Tooth Bus as well, where we partner with Healing Place Church to provide dental work to the underprivileged and needy in the community. I also participate with Habitat for Humanity to build homes for those in need.

I hope that in giving back to the community, I can leave it a little better than when I found it or brighten even one person’s day by my actions.

Dr. Seth O’Shee

Dr. Seth O’Shee truly enjoys being a family dentist in Baton Rouge, LA, saying “being able to work with my hands and create beautiful smiles is very rewarding.” He loves making connections with people and making sure they are given the best care in the world. A Louisiana native who hails from Ville Platte, Dr. O’Shee attended Louisiana State University and LSU School of Dentistry.

When he isn’t taking exceptional care of his patients, Dr. O’Shee likes to play video games, kayak, and of course, follow LSU football and basketball (Go Tigers!)

Dr. Camille Cusco

I am a proud New Orleans native and graduate of St. Mary’s Dominican High School. I moved to Baton Rouge to pursue my undergraduate studies at LSU, where my passion for dentistry first took root. At 20 years old, during my Sophomore year, I joined the dental community as an Expanded Duty Dental Assistant, gaining hands-on experience and a deep appreciation for dental care. I graduated from LSU in 2021 and went on to earn my Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the LSU School of Dentistry in May of 2025.

Education and Experience During my undergraduate years, I served on the Executive Board of the Pre-Dental Society as the Media Representative and was deeply involved with the Student Christian Medical Association, where I held roles as both Vice President and Media Representative. I was also an active member of Kappa Delta sorority, through which I volunteered with organizations such as the Girl Scouts of America, Live to Serve, and Habitat for Humanity.

During dental school, I served as the class historian and was an active member of several organizations, including the American Student Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the ASDA Pre-Dental Committee, the ASDA Wellness Committee, and the Periodontics Interest Group. I participated in multiple service opportunities, including Ozanam Inn Extraction Clinic and Give Kids a Smile, an event which provided dental care to children in need in the New Orleans area.

Professional Affiliations I’m a member of Spear Education and CDOCS, and I participate in a Baton Rouge study club with local dentists and specialists. These resources allow me to stay current with the latest advancements in dentistry and continually grow in my practice.

Meet Our Team

Brenda - Dental Assistant

Brenda joined Portwood Dental in 1993 after moving to the Baton Rouge area when her husband got out of the Air Force. She will be one of the team members assisting Dr. O’Shee during dental procedures. She has over 38 years of dental experience and is certified as an Expanded Duties Dental Assistant. Through the years, she has held a variety of positions, including assisting chairside as well as business office functions. She thoroughly enjoys assisting since it gives her an opportunity to interact with patients, and she constantly strives to make every patient’s visit enjoyable!

Brenda and her husband, Mike, have two children, Brandon and Nathan and one grandson, Cohen. In her spare time, she enjoys boat riding, watching LSU football, and going to the beach.

Cigi - Registered Dental Hygienist

Cigi is originally from Metairie, Louisiana. She received her bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene from the LSU School of Dentistry in 2008. Upon graduation, she moved to Baton Rouge to join the Portwood Dental team. She is responsible for providing preventive dental care and dental cleanings for our patients, and enjoys being a part of their oral health journey.

Cigi and her husband Jason have a daughter named Chloe and a dog, Sprout. In their spare time, they love to spend time with family and friends. Cigi likes to read and is a member of a book club, and is involved in Most Blessed Sacrament Church and women’s bible study.
