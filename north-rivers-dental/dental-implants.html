How Does it Work?

Dental implants work with an initial examination of your mouth and teeth. If you have great jawbone health, we can begin the dental implant process. Otherwise, you will need to undergo bone grafting to reinstate a strong jawbone to support your implant.

The damaged or decayed tooth will be removed, and the implant will be anchored. After a healing period, a dental crown will be affixed on top for a naturally beautiful smile. You will be provided with local or general anesthesia throughout the procedure to alleviate pain.
