Dr. Ray isn’t an implantologist, but he does restore dental implants. If you’re interested in getting dental implants, Dr. Ray and the team can coordinate the placement procedure with a nearby dental surgeon. Then, once your mouth heals, they can design and manufacture custom restorations.

Dental implants are also convenient. You don’t need to remove or soak them like dentures.

To get dental implants, you need to have enough bone to support them. You also need to have healthy gums. If Dr. Ray and the team determine that you have gum disease or low bone volume, they might recommend a prerequisite treatment, like scaling and root planing, oral antibiotics, or a bone graft.
