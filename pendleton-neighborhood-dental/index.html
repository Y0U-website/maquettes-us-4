The practice

From preventive care and routine cleanings to emergency visits, restorative treatment, implants, and clear aligners, Pendleton Neighborhood Dental provides practical, patient-centered care for every stage of life.

Dr. Kerills Habashi is committed to providing honest, practical dental care that feels clear, comfortable, and patient-centered from the very first visit. His approach focuses on listening carefully, explaining treatment in straightforward terms, and helping patients make informed decisions without pressure.

Immediate relief for intense pain, abscesses, or sudden swelling.

Quick repairs to restore your smile and prevent further damage.

Prompt restoration to protect sensitive teeth from infection.

Whether you’re looking for a routine checkup, a dental office with convenient evening and Saturday hours, or honest care you can actually count on, we’re here and ready to help.
