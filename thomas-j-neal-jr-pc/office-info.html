  • 0 Dental Treatment Options Each patient is given a dental examination at each teeth cleaning appointment and treatment visit. The patient and dentist then discuss present oral health needs, treatment options, range of elective services, and the patient’s desired outcome and budget. A patient’s treatment plan may include one or several of the following treatments or services: Preventative Dental Care Daily oral hygiene instruction
  • Early care
  • Cleanings & Digital Imaging (X-rays)
  • Sealants
  • Fluoride Treatment
  • Treatment for physical or behavioral problems that affect dental health such as thumb sucking, snoring, and teeth grinding or clenching
  • Restorative Dentistry Fillings
  • Implants
  • Crowns and Bridges
  • Root Canal
  • Extraction
  • Partials and Dentures
  • Cosmetic Dentistry Teeth Whitening
  • Veneers

Payment Options

Our office accepts the following payment methods: Cash, Personal Check, Credit / Debit Cards (with a 3 % fee), or Private and commercial dental insurance.

We do not accept Medicaid or Peachcare at this time.

Dental Records

We keep up-to-date and detailed records of your dental history and treatment in our practice. These documents include a history of all services and procedures, the dentist’s notes, x-rays, and reports on any dental or medical lab records that may exist. These records are the property of our practice and are kept secure in compliance with professional legal requirements and federal privacy regulations. We are glad to work with you to transfer copies of any of your dental records to practices or parties who may require them.

Insurance

Our office manager will be glad to assist you in determining any insurance coverage you may have that applies to our services and file claims on your behalf to maximize your benefits.

Referrals

Should you require treatment beyond the scope of our practice, we will refer you to specialists with whom we have established and trusted relationships. Our business office will assist you with scheduling and directions to specialists’ offices and will forward any information in your dental records that they require.

Access to Treatment

Our practice does not discriminate against any person on the basis of race, color, national origin, disability, or age in acceptance, treatment, services, or activities.

HIPAA/ Privacy

We take the privacy of your medical and dental information seriously. Our office, our policies, and our practices comply with The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1995 (HIPAA; Pub.L. 104-191, 110 Stat. 1936, enacted August 21, 1996). Rest assured that your privacy is respected and protected.
