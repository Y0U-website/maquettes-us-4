Dental Implants vs. Other Tooth Replacement Options

Dental implants are one of several ways to replace missing teeth. Depending on your oral health, number of missing teeth, preferences, and treatment goals, alternatives may include traditional bridges or dentures. Dental implants differ because the implant is anchored in the jawbone and is designed to replace the root portion of a missing tooth.

The best tooth replacement option varies from patient to patient. During a consultation, our team can explain the advantages and considerations of each approach and help you determine which option is appropriate for your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants take?

Dental implant treatment can take several months because the implant needs time to integrate with the jawbone before the final restoration is placed. Treatment time varies depending on your individual needs and whether additional procedures are necessary.

Do dental implants look natural?

Dental implants are designed to closely resemble natural teeth. A custom restoration is created to complement your existing teeth in shape, size, and appearance.

Can dental implants replace multiple missing teeth?

Yes. Depending on your individual situation, multiple implants can support individual restorations, bridges, or other implant-supported solutions.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Candidacy depends on factors including your oral health, gum health, jawbone structure, and overall treatment needs. A consultation allows your dentist to evaluate your situation and discuss appropriate options.

How do I care for dental implants?

Dental implants should be cared for with consistent oral hygiene and regular dental visits, just like your natural teeth. Your dental team can provide specific recommendations based on your restoration and oral health.

Are dental implants permanent?

Dental implants are designed as a durable, long-term tooth replacement option. Their longevity depends on factors including oral hygiene, regular dental care, overall health, and proper maintenance.
