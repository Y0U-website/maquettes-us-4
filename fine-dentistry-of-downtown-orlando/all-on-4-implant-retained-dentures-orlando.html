Placing a full denture

The process of making a full denture varies in each case, and it usually involves a series of appointments. The first step is to take impressions of your mouth. An accurate model is made from these impressions, and the lab uses this model to create the denture base. In many cases, a wax rim is mounted on the base for you to try on. This helps confirm the correct relationship between the upper and lower teeth.

The lab then creates a wax replica of the final denture, called a wax-up. You will try on the wax-up, and we will note any changes to the fit of the base and the color, shape, and placement of the teeth. We then send the wax-up back to the lab, and they process it to create the final denture.

When the denture is ready, you try it on, and we make any necessary adjustments.

How are Traditional Dental Implants Different from All-On-4 Dental Retained Dentures?

6-8 implants are necessary for a full fixed bridge in traditional implant restoration . Standard full-arch teeth replacement frequently involves bone grafting to increase the density of the upper jawbone since these titanium screws are inserted directly into the jawbone.

With the All-on-4 procedure, a complete top or bottom set of teeth is repairable on just four implants. The placement of the implants is such that it does not require much bone density attached to the jawbone.

Most All-on-4 patients can forgo the necessity for a bone graft by increasing the interaction between the bone and the implant.

Are Removable Dental Implants Available for All-on-4 Implant Retained Dentures?

They are not. A dentist in Orlando, FL, may only remove the fixed bridge. Moreover, the All-on-4 teeth never need adhesive or powder to keep in place. It doesn’t slip or pop out.

FAQs – Dentures Treatment in Orlando

How should I care for my dentures?

Brush your dentures daily with a soft brush and denture cleanser. Soak them overnight and rinse before wearing. Clean your gums and tongue to avoid bacteria buildup. See your dentist regularly to ensure a proper fit.

What is the average price for dentures in Florida?

Costs range based on type and customization. Traditional dentures may cost $1,000–$3,000 per arch. All-on-4 implants treatment is more premium but offers long-term benefits. We provide detailed cost estimates after consultation.

What is the most comfortable denture to wear?

Many patients find implant dentures or All-on-4 implants the most comfortable due to their secure fit. These reduce gum irritation and eliminate the need for adhesives.

What type of dentures are most natural-looking?

Denture implants with porcelain or high-grade acrylic teeth mimic the translucency and contour of natural teeth. Implant-supported options provide the most realistic appearance and function.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

Yes. While traditional dentures may require some adjustments, implant-retained options like All-on-4 allow you to eat a full variety of foods confidently, including tougher items like steak or apples.

How long do dentures last?

Traditional dentures typically last 5–8 years. With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last longer, and the implants themselves often last 15–20 years or more.

What are the benefits of All-on-4?

This treatment provides immediate, full-mouth restoration using just four implants. It offers better stability, quicker recovery, and greater confidence. It’s an ideal long-term solution for many patients.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With excellent hygiene and routine dental care, All-on-4 dental implants Orlando patients receive can last 15 years or longer. The prosthetics may need adjustments or replacement after 10–15 years.
