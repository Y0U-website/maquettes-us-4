0 services Dental Prophylaxis Dental Hygiene cleanings are an important part of our overall well being. Chronic inflammation can have effects on the whole body. Maintaining regular cleanings helps ensure you know the best way to care for your mouth daily and keeps your gums and teeth healthy.

Dental x-rays

Dental x-rays are an important tool for the dental professional and patient. This helps us ensure we are seeing every aspect of your teeth, gums, and bone allowing us to spot problems before you may even feel there is a problem. With digital imaging, it is quick and more comfortable for the patient and uses minimal radiation.

Sealants

Pit and fissure sealants are a great prevention tool for keeping decay out of the deep “pits and fissures” in molars that have recently erupted in the mouth.

Whitenings- In-office treatment and custom-fit trays

In-office whitenings are a great way to get a confident smile! With in-office whitening treatments, you can get shades whiter in one appointment. Take-home whitening trays are also a great way to get a whiter smile and maintain your whiter smile from all the things we enjoy that can darken our smiles daily.

Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal Therapy also known as a deep cleaning or scaling and root planing is utilized to help stop the disease process in periodontal disease which causes bone loss, attachment loss, bad breath, and bleeding inflamed gums. Periodontal disease affects a large portion of the population. It is very important to treat it in the beginning phases and maintain your regular care to ensure a healthy stable mouth.

Silver Diamine Fluoride

Silver diamine fluoride can stop decay in its earliest stages.

Air powder polishing

Air polishing is a technique in which Baking soda or glycine powder, water, and air remove biofilm and stain. This device is helpful for implants, braces, and stains from foods like coffee, tea, and wine.

ultrasonic scaling

Ultrasonic scalers are an efficient and effective way to clean biofilm and tartar off your teeth. Ultrasonics make your appointment quicker and easier on you as a patient.

fluoride varnish

Fluoride varnish is a great way to help improve sensitivity and prevent decay.
