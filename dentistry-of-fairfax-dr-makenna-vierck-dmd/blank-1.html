Accessibility arrangements in the organization

At Dentistry of Fairfax, we are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all patients. Our first floor is fully ADA-compliant and wheelchair accessible, featuring thoughtfully designed operatories, restrooms, and a front desk that ensure comfort, safety, and ease of access. We believe that exceptional care begins with accessibility, and we’re proud to offer a space where every member of our community can feel supported.
