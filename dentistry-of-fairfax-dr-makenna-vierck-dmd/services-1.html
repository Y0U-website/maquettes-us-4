Providing Comprehensive Dental Care in Marin County, California.

General Dentistry

General dentistry includes regular checkups, cleanings, and treatments to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Cosmetic Dentistry Cosmetic dentistry enhances the appearance of your smile through treatments like custom porcelain veneers, crowns, fillings, and professional whitening.

Implant Dentistry Implant dentistry replaces missing teeth with natural-looking, permanent restorations that help you smile and chew with confidence.

Invisalign Clear aligner treatment straightens your teeth using custom, nearly invisible trays for a comfortable and discreet smile transformation.

Same-Day Crowns Same day crown dentistry uses advanced technology to design and place a custom, permanent crown in a single visit.

Teeth Whitening Professional teeth whitening safely brightens your smile with fast, effective results you can’t get from at-home products.

Neuromodulator Therapy Neuromodulator therapy relaxes overactive muscles to relieve jaw pain, TMJ symptoms, and enhance facial balance for a smoother, more comfortable appearance.

Laser Dentistry Laser dentistry uses precise, gentle light technology to treat soft tissue and gum concerns with minimal discomfort and faster healing.

Snore-Guards "Sleep & Airway"

We offer custom-fit snore guards designed to help you sleep more peacefully by gently improving nighttime airflow and reducing snoring.
