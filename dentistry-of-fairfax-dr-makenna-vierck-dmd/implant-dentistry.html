  • Implant Dentistry | Dentistry of Fairfax top of page Implant Dentistry What Is Implant Dentistry? Dental implants are a modern, long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots that support crowns, bridges, or dentures. They restore both function and aesthetics, allowing patients to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Benefits of dental implants: Natural look and feel
  • Permanent and durable
  • Preserve jawbone health
  • No impact on surrounding teeth

The Implant Process: What to Expect Getting dental implants is a multi-step journey tailored to your needs. Here’s a simplified overview:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Want a permanent alternative to dentures
  • Have healthy gums and adequate bone density
  • Are committed to good oral hygiene
  • Consultation & Imaging We assess your oral health and bone structure using our new CBCT scanner.
  • Treatment Planning Dr. Vierck designs a personalized plan using 3D imaging and digital tools.
  • Implant Placement The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing Period Over several months, the implant integrates with the bone.
  • Restoration - A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached for a natural finish.
