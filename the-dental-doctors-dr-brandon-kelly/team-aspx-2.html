Dr. Berg & Team

Dr. Stephanie Berg Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS)

Dr. Berg completed undergraduate training as a biologist (BSBio) at Luther College . She then completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree at the University of Minnesota . Dr. Berg has surgical interests, already placing a number of implants and is working towards advanced procedures such as bone grafting.

Dr. Berg has been a long time patient of the practice - ask to see our picture of her when she was a 5 year old - often described as "cute." Interestingly, she told us in middle school she was going to be a dentist - never wavering from that goal! Sadly, Dr. Berg has an interest in softball (a non-sport as compared to soccer).

Dr. Berg and her husband have two young children (one of each), so now she’s also a mom.
