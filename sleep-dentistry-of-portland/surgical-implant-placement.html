4. Healing & Osseointegration

Implant needs time to integrate with the bone, which usually takes from 3 to 6 months. A temporary restoration may be placed to maintain function and aesthetics during this healing period.

5. Abutment & Final Restoration

✔ A custom abutment is attached to the implant to support the final restoration.

✔ A custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached for a natural look and feel.

✔ Your new tooth, bridge or denture is now fully restored and functional, so you smile with confidence!

Request An Appointment Recovery & Aftercare Tips ✔ Take prescribed medications to manage discomfort and prevent infection.

✔ Use ice packs to reduce swelling in the first 24 hours.

✔ Eat soft foods like yogurt, mashed potatoes, and smoothies for the first few days.

✔ Avoid smoking and alcohol use to support proper healing.

✔ Maintain good oral hygiene but avoid brushing directly on the surgical site for a few days.

✔ Attend follow-up appointments to ensure proper healing and implant success.
