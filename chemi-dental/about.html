Meet the dentist and the philosophy behind Chemi Dental in Horizon City, TX.

Dr. José Hernández

Dr. José Hernández has proudly served the Horizon City and greater El Paso communities for years. From the moment he graduated dental school, his mission has been simple: to provide compassionate, top-tier care tailored to every individual.

Fluent in both English and Spanish, Dr. Hernández loves connecting with our diverse community. He firmly believes that a great dental experience starts with communication — ensuring you fully understand your treatment options and feel completely confident in your smile.

To deliver the best possible care, Dr. Hernández continuously advances his skills through ongoing education and invests in the latest dental technology — including the Gemini soft-tissue laser for faster, more comfortable treatment, a digital intraoral scanner that replaces messy impressions with a precise 3D scan, and 3D CBCT imaging for detailed diagnostics. The result is care that’s more accurate, less invasive, and easier on you from start to finish.
