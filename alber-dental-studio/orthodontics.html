What to Expect at Your Visit

Consultation & Records

Dr. Alber evaluates your bite, takes digital scans and X-rays, and discusses your goals and lifestyle before recommending Invisalign or braces. If Invisalign is the right fit, the scan process can begin at the same appointment.

Treatment Planning & Appliance Fitting

A detailed treatment plan is mapped out with a realistic timeline and cost breakdown. For Invisalign, custom aligners are fabricated from your digital scans. For braces, brackets are bonded to the teeth and the archwire is placed at a subsequent appointment.

Active Orthodontic Movement

Teeth shift gradually through a series of aligner trays or periodic wire adjustments. Progress check appointments are scheduled every 6 to 8 weeks. Most cases run 12 to 18 months for Invisalign and 18 to 24 months for braces.

Retention & Long-Term Stability

Once active movement is complete, custom retainers are provided. Consistent retainer wear — typically nightly long-term — is the single most important factor in keeping your results stable after treatment ends.
