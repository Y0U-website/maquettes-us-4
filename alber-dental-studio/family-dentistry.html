What to Expect as a New Patient

Comprehensive New Patient Exam

Treatment Planning & Prioritization

Dr. Alber presents findings clearly and explains which issues are urgent, which can be monitored, and what options are available. For new family patients, we build a treatment plan that fits each member’s timeline and priorities without pressure.

Ongoing Care & Clinical Continuity

One of the core advantages of a single-practice family dental home is that Dr. Alber accumulates clinical context over time. He knows each patient’s history, which areas need monitoring, and what the long-term trajectory looks like — information that is lost when families use multiple practices.

Scheduling & Coordination

We make scheduling appointments for multiple family members straightforward. Our team coordinates timing efficiently so families can get care without excessive back-and-forth.
