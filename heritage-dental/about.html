About Heritage Dental

Heritage is more than a name.

For a lot of families, "the dentist" used to mean a person — not a chain. Someone who watched your kids grow up, knew which tooth had given you trouble for years, and didn’t have to ask why you flinched at the X-ray machine.

Heritage Dental was built around that idea. We’re a single-location practice in Hilliard, Ohio, owned by Dr. Ankit Patel. We see grandparents, parents, and kids — often in the same week, sometimes in the same hour. The chart on the wall is the same chart it was last year. The hygienist is the same hygienist. The plan we made together still applies.

The technology has moved on, of course. We use digital impressions, modern composites, and low-radiation imaging. But what we offer is older than that — the kind of dentistry where you’re a name, not a chart number, and the relationship is the real product.

Ankit Patel, DDS

Dr. Patel earned his dental degree from NYU College of Dentistry, one of the country’s most demanding programs, and completed a General Practice Residency at the VA Hospital in Brooklyn — where he trained alongside surgeons treating veterans with complex, long-deferred dental needs. He continued with a residency at Mount Sinai before settling into general practice.

He chose Hilliard intentionally. After years of working in larger systems, he wanted a practice small enough to know his patients by name — where a teenager he saw for braces five years ago could bring her own daughter in for a first cleaning. That kind of arc is the whole point.

At Heritage, Dr. Patel handles preventive care, cosmetic work, restorative procedures, and emergencies under one roof. He believes most patients leave the dentist confused about what was done and why. He’d rather you leave understanding exactly what’s going on and what the next step is.

Three things we won’t compromise on

Trust

If we don’t think you need it, we’ll say so. If we do, we’ll explain why in plain English. The relationship outlasts any single appointment.

Craft

Dentistry is a trade with a long tradition of careful hands. Modern tools amplify that — they don’t replace it. We take our time.

Community

We’re not a regional chain. We’re one office, one block, one ZIP code. The neighborhood is the practice, and the practice is the neighborhood.

A space built for calm

We designed the office to feel less like a clinic and more like a place you wouldn’t mind being for an hour. Warm light, wood accents, quiet music, no cable news in the lobby.

For anxious patients, we offer comfort measures available on request, and a hand-signal system so you can pause us at any time. When more is needed, we’ll talk through sedation options — what’s appropriate for the procedure and what to expect.

If you’ve avoided the dentist for years, you’re exactly the patient we want to see. Most appointments end with patients telling us they expected worse. That’s the goal.
