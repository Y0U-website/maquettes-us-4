Accessibility

Accessibility is ongoing work

Heritage Dental aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We treat accessibility as something we keep improving rather than a finished checkbox and we welcome your feedback.

How we’ve built this site

Steps we’ve taken so the site works with keyboards, screen readers, zoom, and other assistive technology:

  • Semantic HTML with clear landmarks header, navigation, main content, and footer plus a skip to content link.
  • A logical heading structure and descriptive, unique page titles.
  • Full keyboard operability, with a visible focus outline on every link, button, and menu.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; purely decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Clearly labeled form fields.
  • Body text and core interface colors chosen to meet WCAG AA contrast, with layouts that reflow when you zoom in or use a small screen.
  • Respect for your device’s reduce motion setting animations are minimized when you ask your system to limit motion.

Visiting us in person

Free parking is available directly in front of the office and along the street. The entrance is at ground level with no stairs, and the office is wheelchair accessible. If you need a specific accommodation for your visit, let us know ahead of time and we’ll arrange it.

Run into a problem? Tell us

If any part of this site or a visit is hard to use, we want to fix it. Call 614-529-4378 or email [email protected] and we’ll help directly. It helps to tell us the page or task, and the device or assistive technology you’re using, so we can reproduce the issue.

Some embedded tools like online booking and maps come from outside providers. We choose accessible vendors where we can, but we don’t fully control those interfaces; contact us and we’ll help you complete anything by phone.
