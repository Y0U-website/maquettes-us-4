Insurance & Payment

Expanded Medicaid coverage

We’re proud to accept Delta Dental Triadvantage Medicaid (formerly CareSource) and Molina Medicaid , in addition to most major PPO insurance plans. Walk-ins are welcome.

Not sure whether your plan is in-network? Call 614-529-4378 with your member ID and we’ll check before you book.

Bring your card

Bring your insurance card to your first visit and we’ll handle the rest verifying benefits and explaining your coverage in plain language.

We’re in-network with several plans and out-of-network with others either way, we’ll verify your specific plan before any appointment and explain what to expect. For larger treatment plans, we offer financing options that break the cost into predictable monthly payments, laid out before any work starts.
