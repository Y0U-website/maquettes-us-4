The practice

Dr. Neha Sharma is the founder and lead dentist of MOD DENTIST, a modern dental practice created to make exceptional care feel comfortable, thoughtful, and completely personalized.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Roseman University College of Dental Medicine, and is passionate about combining advanced dental technology with a gentle, detail-oriented approach — with clinical interests spanning cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign , and comprehensive restorative care.

Dr. Sharma founded MOD DENTIST with a clear vision: a dental experience where patients feel heard, never rushed, and genuinely cared for. She believes excellent dentistry begins with trust — explaining options clearly and building treatment plans without pressure or judgment.
