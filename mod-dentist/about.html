Meet Dr. Neha Sharma, DMD

Founder of MOD DENTIST — where advanced technology meets unhurried, judgment-free care.

Dr. Neha Sharma is the founder and lead dentist of MOD DENTIST, a modern dental practice created to make exceptional care feel comfortable, thoughtful, and completely personalized.

Education & Training

Dr. Sharma earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Roseman University College of Dental Medicine. She is passionate about combining advanced dental technology with a gentle, detail-oriented approach to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. Her clinical interests include cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign , restorative dentistry, and comprehensive care tailored to each patient’s individual goals.

Why MOD Dentist

She founded MOD DENTIST with a clear vision: to create a dental experience where patients feel heard, never rushed, and genuinely cared for. From the calming environment to the latest digital technology, every detail of the practice has been intentionally designed to provide a more comfortable and elevated approach to dentistry.

Dr. Sharma believes that excellent dentistry begins with trust. She takes the time to explain treatment options clearly, answer questions, and develop practical treatment plans without pressure or judgment. Whether a patient is visiting for routine care, transforming their smile, or returning to the dentist after many years, her goal is to make every appointment feel positive and empowering.

Outside the Office

Outside the office, Dr. Sharma enjoys spending time with her family, traveling, exploring new restaurants, and continuing her education through advanced dental training.

She looks forward to welcoming you to MOD DENTIST and helping you achieve a smile that looks beautiful, feels healthy, and is uniquely yours.
