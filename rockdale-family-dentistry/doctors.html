Meet Dr. Ryan E. Ranjbar — Conyers, GA Dentist

Biography

Professional Experience

Dr. R earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Medical College of Georgia. During his training, he received the Award of Merit from the American Prosthodontic Society for his excellence in prosthodontics. He is also actively involved in community service as a member of the Remote Area Medical Volunteer Corps, providing dental care to underserved communities. Dr. R maintains active memberships with the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Personal Interests & Hobbies

Outside of the office, Dr. R enjoys spending time with family and friends, playing tennis, hiking, working out, and traveling.

In addition to his active hobbies, Dr. R is fluent in both English and Persian, allowing him to connect with and serve a diverse patient community with ease and understanding.
