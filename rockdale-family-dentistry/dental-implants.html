The Implant Procedure, Simplified

A clear, patient-friendly overview of the process—from planning to final restoration.

1. Consultation & Planning

Your journey begins with a one-on-one consultation to discuss your goals, followed by a comprehensive examination and imaging to create your personalized treatment plan.

2. Site Preparation

If needed, we may perform preliminary work like a bone graft to ensure your jaw provides a strong and healthy foundation for the implant.

3. Implant Placement

On surgery day, we use advanced, computer-guided technology to precisely and gently place the implant into your jawbone.

4. Healing & Aftercare

We provide comprehensive aftercare instructions and guide you through the healing process for a smooth recovery.

5. Final Restoration

Once you are healed, we take digital impressions for your final, beautiful crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth.
