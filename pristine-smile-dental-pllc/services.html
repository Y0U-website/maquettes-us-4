Bonding

Dental bonding is a cosmetic procedure that uses a tooth-colored composite resin material to enhance your smile. This procedure is used to repair chips, close down gaps or change the shape and color of a tooth. Unlike other cosmetic dental treatments, such as porcelain veneers, dental bonding is completely reversible.

Zoom Whitening

A brighter smile can change how you see yourself – and how the world sees you. At Pristine Smile Dental, we offer Zoom Whitening, a professional in-office treatment that delivers stunning results in just one visit. Using advanced LED light-activated technology and a powerful whitening gel, Zoom can lighten your teeth up to 8 shades – safely, effectively, and with minimal sensitivity. Walk out with a noticeably whiter smile in under an hour.

Invisalign®

A straighter smile can boost your confidence – and make every first impression count. At Pristine Smile Dental, we offer Invisalign®, the clear, comfortable alternative to metal braces. Using a series of custom-made aligners, Invisalign gradually shifts your teeth into perfect alignment – without wires, brackets, or dietary restrictions. Most treatments are completed in 6 to 18 months, and the aligners are virtually invisible. Enjoy the freedom to smile confidently at every stage.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment is used to eliminate the infection in a tooth when the bacteria in dental decay damage the nerve. The affected tooth is isolated, and anesthetized. The canal which houses the nerve of the tooth is cleaned, disinfected, reshaped, and filled. After this treatment is completed, the tooth can be restored with a crown or sometimes a composite.

Crowns and Bridges

If a tooth is badly broken or has had root canal treatment a single crown can protect the tooth from fracturing. The tooth is reshaped in order to fit a restoration over it that is strong enough to withstand chewing forces. A bridge is a dental prosthesis that serves to replace single or multiple teeth. The teeth near missing teeth are reshaped and have crowns that are fabricated in a way to replace missing teeth. This option is sometimes a better option than an implant if surgery is something you want to avoid.

Nitrous Sedation

A calmer visit can make all the difference – especially for our youngest patients or those with dental anxiety. We offer nitrous oxide sedation (also known as “laughing gas”) to help children and adults with anxiety feel relaxed, safe, and at ease during treatment. This gentle, fast-acting option is inhaled through a small mask and works within minutes. Best of all, it wears off quickly – so patients can return to normal activities right after their visit.

Extractions

When a tooth is badly broken and causing you pain you have two options. Treat the pain and infection with a root canal if the tooth is restorable or have the tooth removed. We provide you with options so you can make the best decision for yourself.

Partial and Complete Dentures

After extractions are performed it can be difficult to chew your food and smile. A partial or complete denture is a dental prosthesis that can replace a few or many teeth. Making smiling and chewing possible again is one of the greatest joys that we can share with you here with our team.

Implant Restorations

Implant restorations are a great option to replace single missing teeth or multiple missing teeth. After seeing a specialist who places the implant for you, you return to our office where we restore the implant with an abutment and crown.

Periodontal Therapy

If your gums bleed and you haven’t had a cleaning in some time a regular cleaning may not be enough to get your gums to a healthy state. With a deep cleaning we make sure you are comfortable by anesthetizing your teeth and gums. Now we clean beneath the gums to remove the plaque that has accumulated over time, and in some cases place antibiotics directly beneath the gums.

Dental Cleaning

Prophylaxis dental cleanings are used to clean healthy teeth in order to prevent bacteria populations from growing and causing dental issues like tooth decay and gum disease. Prophylaxis cleanings remove plaque and tartar from the front, back, and sides of teeth using a dental scaler or water stream.
