The Benefits of the PD Membership Plan

In addition to costing less than a traditional dental insurance plan, the PD Membership Plan offers much more:

  • No restrictions: On top of fully covering your preventive care, our plan can help you save on many services that aren’t included on normal insurance, like cosmetic procedures (whitening), dental implants, Invisalign, and more.
  • No deductibles: Once you sign up and pay, you can instantly start using your benefits to save.
  • No claim forms: You’ll never be denied for coverage. You can rely on your plan every time you come to see us.
  • No annual maximums: Regular insurance usually has a cap on how much it will pay for dental care each year. Our plan doesn’t. You can use it to literally save as much as you need.
  • No phone trees: Have a question about our plan or an issue with a charge? Rather than spending hours on the phone trying to talk to a person, you’ll just need to call our front office, and we’ll help you right away.
