WHO IS DR. GRETCHEN GALVIN A DENTIST WHO TRULY CARES

Dr. Gretchen Galvin is a nationally recognized dentist known for her expertise in dental veneers, dental implants, and IV sedation. She is the founder of a leading online continuing education platform that has trained thousands of dentists across the country and helped solidify her reputation as one of Buffalo’s most in-demand cosmetic dentists. After earning her degree from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in 2015, she provided implant placement and IV sedation at multiple practices throughout Buffalo, serving as their dedicated implant and sedation specialist.

Dr. Galvin later launched two highly successful dental practices in the US Virgin Islands and founded Dial Dental, the first dental telehealth platform. She is also the co-founder of DentistOffices.com, the largest dental education and growth platform in the world, serving thousands of patients a day. At The Buffalo Dentist, she performs a wide range of general dentistry services, with advanced training in veneers, implants, all-on-4 full-mouth solutions, and sedation techniques. Her focus is always on delivering high-quality, comfortable care to every patient.
