What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. Whether you’ve lost a tooth due to decay, injury, or gum disease, implants offer unmatched stability and functionality. At The Buffalo Dentist, we specialize in restoring smiles with modern implant technology tailored to your needs.

Understanding Dental Implants

A dental implant consists of three parts: a titanium post that is placed in the jawbone, an abutment that connects the post to the restoration, and a crown that mimics your natural tooth. This structure replaces the root and visible portion of a missing tooth, delivering excellent durability and aesthetics.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

One of the most common questions we receive is, how long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental visits, implants can last 25 years or more. The crown portion may eventually need replacement due to wear, but the titanium post can remain in place for life with good oral hygiene.

Dental Implant Healing Stages

The process of getting a dental implant occurs in several stages. Here’s what you can expect during the dental implant healing stages:

Every patient heals at a different pace, and we closely monitor your progress to ensure optimal results.

  • Initial Consultation: Includes imaging, exam, and treatment planning
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone
  • Osseointegration: The bone fuses with the implant (3–6 months)
  • Abutment Placement: Connects the implant to the crown
  • Crown Restoration: The final crown is placed for a complete, functional tooth

Are Dental Implants Painful?

Another common concern is, are dental implants painful? Most patients report only mild discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, and post-operative soreness typically resolves within a few days. We offer sedation options for added comfort and will provide detailed aftercare instructions to manage any discomfort during recovery.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

  • Look and function like natural teeth
  • Prevent bone loss and facial sagging
  • Restore chewing ability and speech clarity
  • Long-lasting and reliable solution
  • Preserve surrounding teeth

Are You a Candidate for Implants?

Dental implants are ideal for most adults with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density. During your consultation, we will evaluate your health and determine the best implant options for your smile goals.
