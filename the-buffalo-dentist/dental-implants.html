What Are Dental Implants?

What Are They?

They are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial roots. Once healed, they are topped with a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth and are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement.

All-on-4 Implants

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, All-on-4 dental implants offer a full-arch restoration using four strategically placed implants. They are total replacements and will completely restore your ability to talk and eat.

Implant Crown Restorations

An implant crown is used to replace a single missing tooth. After the implant is placed and integrated with the bone, a crown is custom-fitted to restore the tooth’s appearance and function. Implant crowns are highly durable, natural-looking, and can last for decades with proper care.

Dental Implants Cost

The cost of implants can vary based on the number of teeth being replaced, the need for bone grafting, and the type of restoration. On average, a single dental implant with a crown ranges from $3,000 to $5,000. All-on-4 implant solutions generally range from $15,000 to $30,000 per arch.
