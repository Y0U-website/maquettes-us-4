Dental Implant Cost in Buffalo, NY

Dental implants are one of the most effective and long-lasting solutions for replacing missing teeth. However, we understand that patients often wonder, how much are dental implants? At The Buffalo Dentist, we provide transparent pricing, flexible payment options, and personalized treatment plans so you can make informed decisions about your dental health.

How Much Are Dental Implants?

The tooth implant cost varies based on your specific needs. On average, a single dental implant—including the post, abutment, and crown—can range from $3,000 to $5,000. This price includes diagnostics, surgical placement, and the final restoration.

Keep in mind, while the upfront cost may be higher than alternatives like bridges or dentures, implants offer a permanent solution that can last a lifetime—making them a worthwhile investment in your long-term health and confidence.

Cost of Full Mouth Dental Implants

Patients who need extensive restoration often ask about the cost of full mouth dental implants. Full arch treatments, such as All-on-4 implants, typically range from $15,000 to $30,000 per arch. This treatment replaces an entire row of teeth using just four implants and a fixed prosthetic for optimal comfort and stability.

We provide a complete breakdown of costs at your consultation, including all materials, lab work, and follow-up visits—so there are no surprises.

Factors That Influence Dental Implant Cost

Several variables can affect the final price of your treatment:

During your consultation, our team will assess your oral health and recommend the most efficient, cost-effective approach to restore your smile.

  • Number of implants needed
  • Type of restoration (crown, bridge, full arch)
  • Need for bone grafting or sinus lifts
  • Imaging and surgical guides
  • Material used for crowns and prosthetics
