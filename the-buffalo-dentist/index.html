The practice

In 2015, Dr. Galvin earned her degree in dentistry from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine. She continued on to place implants and offer IV sedation at over 5 separate offices across Buffalo, as their In-House Implant & Sedation Expert .

She later went on to open 2 separate, and wildly successful, practices in the US Virgin Islands. During this time she founded the countries first online dental tele-health platform, Dial Dental. She is also the co-founder of the worlds largest dental education and growth platform, DentistOffices.com .

Dr. Galvin performs all aspects of general dentistry, such as veneers, dental implants, sedation dentistry, wisdom tooth extractions, and much more.
