Single Tooth Implants in Buffalo, NY

Have you lost a single tooth and are seeking a permanent, natural-looking replacement? At The Buffalo Dentist, we offer single tooth implants to restore your smile with stability, function, and aesthetics. Whether you’re comparing options or exploring pricing, we’re here to guide you through the process with care and transparency.

What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant replaces one missing tooth with a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone. Once healed, a custom-made dental crown is secured on top, creating a seamless restoration that looks and feels like your natural tooth. Unlike traditional bridges, implants don’t require altering adjacent teeth.

Single Tooth Denture vs Implant

When replacing a single missing tooth, patients often compare a single tooth denture vs implant. A removable denture, or “flipper,” is a less expensive temporary option, but it lacks stability and can impact speech and chewing.

In contrast, an implant is a permanent, fixed solution that prevents bone loss and preserves jaw structure. Though the upfront investment is higher, the long-term benefits in comfort, function, and durability make implants the preferred choice for most patients.

Single Tooth Implant Cost

The average single tooth implant cost ranges from $3,000 to $5,000. This includes the implant post, abutment, crown, and all necessary procedures. Costs may vary depending on the complexity of the case and whether additional treatments like bone grafting are needed.

Single Tooth Implant Cost Without Insurance

If you’re wondering about the single tooth implant cost without insurance, we provide clear pricing and flexible payment options. Without insurance, patients can still access affordable care through third-party financing or in-house payment plans. We’ll work with you to make treatment manageable and stress-free.

Why Choose a Dental Implant for One Tooth?

  • Preserves bone and facial structure
  • Does not affect neighboring teeth
  • Permanent, stable, and natural in appearance
  • Improves speech, chewing, and confidence

Is a Single Tooth Implant Right for You?

If you’re missing one tooth and have sufficient jawbone density, you may be a great candidate for an implant. Our team will evaluate your oral health and design a treatment plan customized to your needs and goals.
