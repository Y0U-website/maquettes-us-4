All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dentures: Why This Method is the Gold Standard

If you’re comparing tooth replacement options, understanding the difference between All-on-4 and traditional dentures is key.

Traditional Dentures:

  • Removable and may slip or shift
  • Require adhesives
  • Can cause sore spots and irritation
  • Do not prevent bone loss
  • Limit food choices

All-on-4 Dental Implants:

  • Fixed and permanently secured
  • No adhesives needed
  • Comfortable and stable
  • Preserve jawbone health
  • Allow full chewing function

Why Patients Choose All-on-4:

All-on-4 implants provide a modern, permanent alternative that restores both function and confidence, making them the preferred gold standard for full-mouth restoration.
