Types of Overdentures: Ball-Retained vs. Bar-Retained Solutions

Overdentures come in two primary types: ball-retained and bar-retained systems, each offering different levels of support.

Ball-Retained Overdentures (Locator Attachments)

  • Individual implants use ball-shaped connectors
  • Denture snaps into place
  • Easier to clean and maintain
  • More cost-effective option

Bar-Retained Overdentures

Dr. Neko Webb and Dr. Tyler Fyfe will evaluate your jawbone health, lifestyle, and goals to recommend the best option.

  • A metal bar connects multiple implants
  • Denture clips securely onto the bar
  • Offers greater stability and retention
  • Ideal for patients needing extra support

The Process: Getting Your Custom-Fitted Overdentures

The overdenture process involves implant placement followed by a custom denture designed for a secure fit.

Step-by-Step Process:

  • Digital imaging and exam
  • Assessment of bone density and oral health
  • 2 4 implants placed in the jaw
  • Healing period begins
  • Implants fuse with bone (osseointegration)
  • A temporary denture may be used
  • Precise impressions taken
  • Denture crafted for comfort and aesthetics
  • Denture attaches securely to implants
  • Adjustments made for optimal fit

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant-Supported Overdentures

  • What are overdentures? Short Answer: Overdentures are dentures that attach to dental implants for added stability. Details: They snap or clip onto implants, preventing movement and improving function compared to traditional dentures.
  • Are overdentures removable? Short Answer: Yes, overdentures are removable. Details: You can take them out for cleaning, but they remain securely attached during daily use.
  • How many implants are needed for overdentures? Short Answer: Typically 2 to 4 implants per arch. Details: The exact number depends on your bone density and desired level of support.
  • Do overdentures hurt? Short Answer: The procedure is comfortable with anesthesia. Details: Mild soreness after implant placement is normal and temporary.
