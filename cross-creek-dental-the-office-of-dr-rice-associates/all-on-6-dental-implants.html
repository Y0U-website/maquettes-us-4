All-on-6 vs. All-on-4: Which Full-Arch Solution is Right for You?

The main difference between All-on-6 and All-on-4 is the number of implants used, which directly impacts stability and load distribution.

A consultation at Cross Creek Dental will determine the best option based on your jawbone health and goals.

  • Number of Implants All-on-4: 4
  • All-on-6: 6
  • Stability All-on-4: Strong
  • All-on-6: Maximum Stability
  • Bone Support All-on-4: Moderate
  • All-on-6: Enhanced Support
  • Ideal For All-on-4: Moderate bone loss
  • All-on-6: Lower bone density or higher bite force
  • Longevity All-on-4: Long-lasting
  • All-on-6: Even greater durability
  • Maximum strength and longevity
  • Better force distribution for chewing
  • Added security for long-term success

Step-by-Step: The All-on-6 Implant and Restoration Process
