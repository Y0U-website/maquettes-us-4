Implant Dentistry

Dental implants are possibly one of the most beneficial preventative and restorative procedures in modern dentistry. And at Cross Creek Dental, Dr. Rice and his associates use only the highest-quality materials for our implants, and we can perform the implant surgery right here in our Fayetteville office. No referrals needed!

Our state-of-the-art dental office uses an advanced x-ray system called Panorex (or panoramic x-ray ) for diagnostic imaging. This machine allows our dentists to get a complete picture of your upper and lower jaws, joints and sinuses. Panorex imaging helps to uncover underlying issues that may need to be handled before you receive your dental implants, such as impacted teeth, gum disease or oral cancer. With our thorough examination procedures, you can rest assured that your teeth are in capable hands!
