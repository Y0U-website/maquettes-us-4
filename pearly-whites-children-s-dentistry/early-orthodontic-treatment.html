Early Orthodontic Treatment

Often, our patients with suffer from malocclusion and habits which can impede on their development from an early age. We follow up with our patients and can provide counseling and cessation. We can also provide limited orthodontic measures to help alleviate these problems as soon as they are diagnosed. We work closely with our orthodontic colleagues and can refer when needed.
