Dr. Monika Patel, DMD, MBS

Dr. Patel currently holds active dental licenses in the states of Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware. She has privileges to perform surgical cases at the Crozer Health Brighton Lakes Surgery Center in Glen Mills, PA. In addition to that, she regularly performs Oral and IV sedation cases in office!

During her free time she enjoys spending time with her family, cooking, traveling and running her food blog.

Dr. Dev Patel, DDS

Dr. Patel is fluent in Spanish and enjoys working with patients from all cultural backgrounds. He loves helping kids form a fantastic relationship with visiting the dentist and strives to make every dental visit a positive experience for your child. He is excited to get to know the patients at Pearly Whites Children’s Dentistry and form a close relationship with each family! In his free time, Dr. Patel enjoys exploring new Philly restaurants with his wife, travelling, and watching his favorite baseball, hockey, and football teams. (Go Cardinals, Blues, and Chiefs!)
