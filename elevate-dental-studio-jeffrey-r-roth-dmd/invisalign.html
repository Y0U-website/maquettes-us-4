How Invisalign® Works

Digital 3D Scan​

No gooey impressions! We map your smile with precision scanners.

Custom Aligners​

A series of aligners made just for you, changing every 1–2 weeks.

Gradual Alignment​

Each tray gently moves your teeth toward your ideal smile.

Check-Ins​

Quick follow-ups ensure steady progress and comfort.

Is Invisalign® Right for You?

Designed for Teens, Adults, and Busy Lives Whether you’re preparing for a big event, refining your smile, or correcting lifelong alignment issues, Invisalign works for mild to moderate cases, including:

  • Crowded or spaced teeth
  • Overbites, underbites, crossbites
  • Relapse after past orthodontic treatment
