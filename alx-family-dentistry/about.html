A Lifelong Passion for His Craft

Get To Know Dr. Dayne Patterson

Although born in Colorado, Dr. Dayne Patterson has spent most of his life in Northern Virginia. One of his fondest memories of his childhood in Colorado was his visits to his local dentist—he even enjoyed watching his parents get their teeth cleaned! This planted the seed for Dr. Patterson’s passion for dentistry, and his interest was confirmed once he realized how much he loved science, art, working with his hands, and helping others.

Dr. Patterson completed his undergraduate studies at Virginia Tech, earning his Bachelor of Science in Biology. He then attended dental school at the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry, where he was awarded his DDS degree. Next, he completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco to expand his knowledge and gain further experience. Before settling in the Alexandria area, he also briefly practiced in San Diego.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Patterson enjoys working out, playing and watching sports, exploring the ever-changing world of technology, and spending quality time with his wife and three daughters.

Committed to His Community

Get To Know Dr. Jon Williams

Jon W. Williams, Jr., DDS was born into a military family at Fort Campbell, Kentucky. After moving around most of his childhood, his family settled in Virginia, where he graduated from high school. He went on to attend the University of Virginia and graduated in 1983 with a Bachelor of Arts in Biology. He then earned his DDS from Virginia Commonwealth University’s School of Dentistry in 1987.

Following graduation from dental school, Dr. Williams began practicing dentistry as an associate dentist, and after several years, bought the practice in the Wilton Woods area of Alexandria, where he practiced for over 30 years before the practice relocated to Eisenhower Avenue.

Dr. Williams and his wife, Robin, have three adult children. In his free time, he enjoys golf, reading, traveling, gardening, and supporting his beloved Virginia Cavaliers.

Personalizing the Patient Experience

Your First Visit

First, you’ll be greeted by our friendly and welcoming staff, and get to sit back and relax in our comfortable office nestled in the Alexandria community. Grab a drink from our beverage station and enjoy TVs in our waiting room equipped with streaming services.

Next, you’ll meet with one of our hygienists for a thorough cleaning, followed by a meeting with Dr. Patterson or Dr. Williams for a comprehensive oral exam, complete with any necessary X-rays. We always spend time addressing your concerns and discussing the state of your dental health with you before outlining a unique treatment plan tailored to your needs. Before leaving, we can also schedule any future visits and answer any questions you may have regarding payment and insurance.
