The practice

From crowns to cavity fillings, we offer an array of restorative care treatments to ensure you can smile and chew comfortably. Restorative treatments are essential in maintaining optimal oral health by providing support, structure, and functionality.

As an established, long-term practice, ALX Family Dentistry has set itself apart from the rest by combining personalized patient care, innovative technology, preventive treatments, and a safe, trusted environment.

Patient-Centered Decision Making We want to make sure you are fully informed and empowered while feeling comfortable and safe in our care. Your best interest is our priority, and while we invest in educating our patients on the pros and cons of every treatment, the final decision is ultimately always yours to make.

Conservative, Minimally-Invasive Care We are heavily focused on prevention and practicing conservative dentistry with minimally-invasive treatments to address problems and establish healthy lifelong habits. Our focus is recommending the most appropriate care for your situation rather than to push or sell treatments.

Decades of Community Trust For nearly 60 years, our practice has been a pillar of the Alexandria community. It has maintained a reputation for providing quality, comprehensive dental care to patients of all ages in a friendly neighborhood environment and we vow to continue this trust for many more years to come.

Personalized Treatment Plans Every patient is unique, which is why we create personalized treatment plans that are tailored to your dental needs and goals. Both Dr. Patterson and Dr. Williams are committed to building trusting relationships with patients, as well as crafting specific, customized plans.

Services

Providing patients with preventive care is our highest priority. From routine cleanings and regular checkups to dental health education, we empower our patients with knowledge and habits to avoid costly and invasive treatments down the road.

When a dental filling isn’t enough, a dental crown will be used to save your natural tooth. High-quality, lab-made dental crowns can relieve discomfort, improve appearance, and repair structural damage while protecting your tooth from further decay.

When it comes to replacing a missing tooth, dental implants can restore health, function, and appearance. Dental implant restorations provide a strong and secure foundation that blends seamlessly with your natural smile.
