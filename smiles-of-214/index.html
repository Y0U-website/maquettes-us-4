The practice

Whether you’re a professional navigating a demanding schedule, a public figure who values discretion, or someone seeking a truly elevated dental experience, Smiles of 214 offers an unparalleled standard of care tailored to your lifestyle.

We specialize in general and cosmetic dentistry , full mouth rehabilitation , and transformational smile design —with treatment plans grounded in clinical excellence, advanced technology, and aesthetic precision. Every detail of your visit is curated for comfort, ease, and excellence.

For those returning to care after time away, we offer a judgment-free, welcoming environment where trust is rebuilt through compassion, transparency, and results.

At Smiles of 214, we honor the legacy of the past while redefining what modern dentistry can be. Whether you’re stepping into care for the first time or seeking a higher caliber of service, we invite you to experience a practice where discretion meets distinction , and every smile is treated like a signature.

Our general dentistry services include routine exams, cleanings, fillings, and comprehensive care to maintain your oral health. We focus on prevention and early intervention for optimal dental wellness.

Enhance your smile with professional teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding. Our cosmetic treatments are designed to boost your confidence and give you the radiant smile you deserve.

We offer Invisalign® Clear Aligners to straighten your teeth comfortably and discreetly. These clear aligners offer a modern alternative to traditional braces for a beautiful, aligned smile.

Save your natural tooth with our gentle and effective root canal treatment. We remove infection, relieve pain, and restore your tooth’s health, helping you avoid extractions and maintain a strong smile.

Les soins

Regular dental checkups, cleanings, and exams help keep your smile healthy and prevent issues before they arise. We also provide fluoride treatments and sealants for extra protection.

Laser technology provides safe, effective treatment for gum disease and other dental issues with minimal discomfort. We offer LANAP® (Laser-Assisted New Attachment Procedure) to regenerate healthy bone and gum tissue without traditional surgery.
