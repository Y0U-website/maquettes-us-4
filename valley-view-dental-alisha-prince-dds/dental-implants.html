Benefits of Dental Implants

Natural Appearance Dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function like your natural teeth.

Durability With proper care, implants can last a lifetime, making them a long-term investment in your oral health.

Improved Oral Health Implants help preserve the natural structure of your jawbone and prevent the deterioration that often accompanies tooth loss.

Enhanced Comfort Unlike dentures, implants are permanently fixed in place, eliminating the discomfort and inconvenience of removable appliances.

Improved Confidence Enjoy the confidence that comes with a complete, beautiful smile and the ability to eat, speak, and socialize without worry.

Our Dental Implant Process

At Valley View Dental, we follow a meticulous and patient-centered approach to dental implants:

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Alisha Prince conducts a comprehensive examination, including X-rays and 3D imaging, to determine if you are a suitable candidate for implants.
  • Treatment Planning: Based on your unique needs, Dr. Prince creates a personalized treatment plan outlining the entire process from start to finish.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort.
  • Osseointegration: Over a few months, the implant integrates with the jawbone, providing a strong and stable foundation for the new tooth.
  • Abutment Placement: Once osseointegration is complete, the abutment is attached to the implant post.
  • Crown Placement: Finally, a custom-made crown is secured to the abutment, completing your new, natural-looking smile.
