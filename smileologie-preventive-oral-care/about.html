0 Who we are Raghda Salman, RDH, IPDH is the face behind Smileologie.

Raghda moved to Maine at the age of 15 where she graduated from Deering High School and then attended the University of New England to earn her bachelor’s degree in Dental Hygiene. Since hygiene school, Raghda has made it a promise that she provides ethical quality care to all of her patients. Raghda has years of experince working with all ages in both public and private dentistry.

Raghda also works as an adjunct faculty at the UNE Dental Hygiene Clinic and loves to stay connected with updated dental studies and research. Her main goal is to contribute into building a healthier future by educating her patients on the importance of oral health care and how it is linked to their entire systemic health.

When she is not at the office, she enjoys doing Crossfit, trying new food spots, and going on walks with her husband and enjoy the beautiful nature Maine has to offer.
