Dr. Jolanta Smyda

DDS, University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry

Dr. Smyda always strives for excellence. She believes in giving the highest quality dental care and takes extra measures to make sure her patients are comfortable. Dr. Smyda has developed a particular gift for working with adults and children to establish sound dental habits to assist patients in their lifetime of optimal dental health. She is dedicated to staying abreast of the latest developments in the dental field.

Dr. Smyda is a member of American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association and South Plains District Dental Society. She was awarded the Regional 2014 Leadership Award from Smile on and Develop Talent.

Dr. Smyda and her husband Gregory are parents of two children, Jacob and Elizabeth.

Meet Our Team

Janice Warren - Registered Dental Hygienist

Janice Warren was raised in West Texas. She attended Hardin-Simmons University where she received a Bachelor of Behavioral Science degree and graduated summa cum laude. Janice graduated from the University of Texas Health Science Center, Dental Branch, School of Dental Hygiene in Houston in 1983.

During her career, Janice has combined teaching and practicing dental hygiene. She enjoys working with people of all ages, teaching them how to keep a healthy smile.

Janice lives in Sudan. She has two children and five grandchildren. She enjoys reading, traveling, and spending time with friends and family.

Kendra Homer - Patient Care Coordinator/Office Manager

Kendra has worked in the dental field for over 10 years, the last 5 with Dr. Roy Burk and now with Dr. Jolanta Smyda.

She was born and raised in Littlefield. She and her husband Steve live in Shallowater with their 3 children.

She enjoys assisting patients with their dental needs.
