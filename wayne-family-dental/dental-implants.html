Candidates For Dental Implants In Wayne, MI

If you are considering dental implants for missing tooth replacement, the first step is to consult with our Wayne, MI, dentists. Most people qualify for this treatment, but we have to consider certain factors before making the final decision. The two most important factors for implant success are proper oral hygiene and sufficient bone thickness.

Those with poor oral hygiene may not qualify for this treatment because the risk of failure is higher among them. Since implants are inserted in the jawbone, you must have adequate bone thickness for their placement. If you have severe bone loss, we may recommend bone grafting surgery to replenish your jawbone.

We will also record your dental and medical history to identify other possibilities that may hinder the success of implant surgery. After a complete evaluation, our team will help you make the best decision for your oral health. Our Dental Implant Procedure Getting customized dental implants for the residents of Wayne, MI, requires multiple appointments. The steps of the procedure are:

  • Consultation: During your initial visit, we will examine your oral health and take digital X-rays to determine if implants are the best option for you. We will discuss your treatment expectations to create a personalized plan.
  • Implant Surgery: In this procedure, we will surgically insert the titanium metal posts in your jawbone. It is done under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain or discomfort. Afterward, we will suture the incision and allow the site to heal.
  • Healing Period: After the surgery, you will have to wait for 3 to 6 months before the next step. During this period, the implant posts fuse with the surrounding alveolar bone through a process known as osseointegration.
  • Final Restoration: Once the implants have healed, we will connect them with abutments and place a dental prosthesis. Depending on your requirements, it could be a crown, bridge, or denture.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Implants offer a permanent, durable restoration that fuses with the bone for excellent retention, and they look and feel natural. They let you bite and chew comfortably, prevent bone loss and support facial muscles to maintain a youthful appearance, and require no special maintenance beyond routine oral care.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most people qualify, but the two most important factors for success are good oral hygiene and sufficient jawbone thickness. If you have severe bone loss, bone grafting surgery may be recommended first, and the team will review your dental and medical history before making a final decision.

What does the dental implant procedure involve?

The process takes multiple appointments: a consultation with an exam and digital X-rays, implant surgery to place the titanium posts under local anesthesia, a healing period, and placement of the final restoration. It results in a natural-looking crown, bridge, or denture depending on your needs.

How long does healing take after implant surgery?

After the posts are placed, there’s typically a 3 to 6 month healing period. During this time the implant fuses with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration before the final restoration is attached.

Can implants replace more than one tooth?

Yes. Whether you’ve lost a single tooth or an entire arch, treatment can be personalized to your needs, using one or more implants to support a crown, bridge, or denture.
