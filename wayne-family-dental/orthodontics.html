Is Orthodontic Treatment Safe For Kids And Teens?

Many orthodontists suggest getting a clinical evaluation when your child’s permanent teeth begin to appear. This usually occurs around the age of 6 or 7 years. It is a transition period during which the baby teeth get replaced by permanent teeth. If any irregularity occurs during this stage, it can impact the alignment of your child’s teeth in the long run.

The best time to achieve orthodontic treatment is between the ages of 10 and 14. During this time, your kid’s jaw is still developing, so it is easier to straighten their teeth. Our Wayne, MI, dentist offers comprehensive orthodontics to address various dental concerns among kids and teens. Rest assured that the treatment measures are safe and do not harm your child’s teeth or jaws.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is the field of dentistry that deals with the alignment of teeth and jaws. While a minor issue like a single crooked tooth may only affect appearance, a major irregularity can impact how your teeth and jaws function, so orthodontic treatment is used to correct misalignment for a straighter, healthier smile.

What problems can orthodontic treatment correct?

Orthodontics can address crowding, spacing (gaps), overbite, underbite, crossbite, and open bite. These issues can affect the teeth, the jaws, or both, and Dr. Swarna develops a personalized plan after a detailed evaluation.

What orthodontic options does Wayne Family Dental offer?

The practice offers traditional metal braces and Invisalign clear aligners. Braces use stainless steel brackets and wires to gently reposition teeth, while Invisalign uses a series of transparent, removable aligners for a discreet alternative for mild to moderate misalignment.

Is orthodontic treatment safe for kids and teens?

Yes. Many orthodontists recommend a first evaluation around age 6 or 7, when permanent teeth begin to appear, and the best time for treatment is often between ages 10 and 14 while the jaw is still developing. The treatment measures are safe and don’t harm a child’s teeth or jaws.

What happens at an orthodontic consultation?

The dentist discusses your concerns and goals, performs a detailed clinical evaluation, takes digital X-rays, and records impressions of your teeth. This information is used to plan the orthodontic treatment that best fits your needs.
