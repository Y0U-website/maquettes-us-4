Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Bhavani Swarna, DDS

Dr. Swarna, a distinguished graduate with a DDS from the University of Detroit Mercy Dental School and a Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Jawaharlal Nehru Medical College in Dharwad, brings over 20 years of expertise to the realm of general and cosmetic dentistry. Having completed over 800 hours of continuing education, she boarded on her professional journey, gaining valuable experience in Michigan and Ohio before founding Wayne Family Dental in 2006.

Active in professional circles, Dr. Swarna is affiliated with esteemed organizations such as the Detroit Dental Society , Michigan Dental Association (MDA), and American Dental Association (ADA). Her commitment to excellence and tailored care is underscored by her philosophy, evident in her attentive approach to patient concerns and determination to optimize oral health.

Recipient of the American Academy of Esthetic Dentistry Award in 2004, Dr. Swarna extends her impact beyond dentistry, contributing to the Wayne Chamber of Commerce and supporting local businesses and events. Amid her accolades and professional achievements, she treasures the relationships forged with patients and staff alike. Married for 25 years, Dr. Swarna finds joy in spending time with her husband and two daughters.
