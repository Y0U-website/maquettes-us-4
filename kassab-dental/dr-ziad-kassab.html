Dr. Ziad Kassab

Dr. Ziad Kassab is a highly accomplished and skilled dentist, renowned for his expertise in cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and his ability to reverse previous dental mistakes. With an illustrious career spanning over 20 years, Dr. Kassab has become a trusted name in the field, known for his meticulous approach, compassionate care, and commitment to delivering exceptional results.

Dr. Kassab’s journey into dentistry began at the prestigious University of Southern California (USC) Dental School, where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree. During his time at USC, he immersed himself in comprehensive dental education, honing his skills and knowledge in various disciplines. The rigorous training at USC equipped him with a strong foundation in dental practices and instilled in him a deep commitment to excellence.

To further refine his expertise in dental implants, Dr. Kassab pursued advanced training at the renowned UCLA A-Z Dental Implant Program. Under the guidance of distinguished mentors and professors, he gained comprehensive knowledge of implantology, including surgical placement, bone grafting techniques, and restorative procedures. This certification from UCLA solidified his proficiency in dental implants, enabling him to offer cutting-edge solutions to his patients.

Dr. Kassab’s commitment to staying at the forefront of dental advancements is evident in his dedication to continuing education. He actively participates in professional conferences, seminars, and workshops, ensuring that he remains abreast of the latest techniques and technologies. By staying current, he can provide his patients with the most innovative and effective treatment options available

Dr. Ziad Kassab’s remarkable career as a dentist is defined by his commitment to excellence, compassionate care, and dedication to helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles. With his USC dental school education, UCLA A-Z dental implant certification, and 20 years of experience, he continues to make a positive impact in the field of dentistry, one smile at a time.
