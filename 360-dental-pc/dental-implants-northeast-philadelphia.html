Dental implants are designed to feel completely natural, so you can smile, speak, and eat with confidence again. They are the most advanced method for tooth replacement, offering superior results compared to other options like dentures or bridges.

  • The Abutment: The abutment is the connector that attaches the crown, bridge, or denture to the implant post.
  • The Restoration: The restoration is the visible part of the dental implant, such as a crown, bridge, or full denture, which mimics the appearance and function of natural teeth.

The Dental Implant Procedure

The process of getting dental implants generally takes several months and involves several stages. However, the results are permanent, making dental implants a long-term investment in your oral health and smile.

Consultation and Evaluation

The first step to getting dental implants is a thorough consultation with Dr. Sitkovetskiy. During this visit, he will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and take X-rays or 3D scans of your jawbone to assess the amount of bone available for implant placement. Bone density is an important factor in determining whether you are a candidate for dental implants, as the implants need to fuse with the bone to ensure stability. If bone loss has occurred, additional procedures like a bone graft may be recommended to ensure that your dental implants have a solid foundation.

Implant Placement Surgery

Placement of Abutments and Restorations

Once the implants have successfully integrated with the jawbone, the next step is to attach the abutments and restorations. The abutments are small connector pieces that are placed on top of the implants and serve as the anchor for the final crown, bridge, or denture. Custom restorations are designed to match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth, giving you a beautiful, seamless smile.

For single-tooth implants, a custom crown is placed on the abutment to restore the missing tooth.

For patients who need to replace multiple teeth, implant-supported bridges or full dentures can be created to restore the full function of the smile.

Aftercare and Follow-Up Visits

Once your dental implants and restorations are in place, it’s important to maintain proper oral hygiene and schedule regular follow-up visits to ensure the success of the implants. Dr. Sitkovetskiy will provide you with instructions on how to care for your implants, including recommendations for brushing, flossing, and avoiding foods that could damage the implants during the healing process. With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer several advantages over other tooth replacement options. These benefits include:

  • Permanent Solution Dentures or bridges need to be replaced every few years, but dental implants are a permanent solution to missing teeth. Once the implant has fused with the jawbone, it becomes a stable, secure foundation for your restorations, allowing you to enjoy a full, natural-looking smile for years to come.
  • Improved Function Dental implants restore full functionality to your mouth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile confidently. Dental implants stay securely in place, allowing you to enjoy the foods you love without worrying about your teeth.
  • Natural Appearance One of the main reasons patients choose dental implants is because they offer a natural-looking solution to missing teeth. Dental implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, providing a beautiful, restored smile that looks and feels like the real thing.
  • Bone Preservation When you lose a tooth, the jawbone that once supported the tooth begins to deteriorate. Dental implants help to preserve the bone tissue by providing stimulation to the jawbone, preventing bone loss and facial collapse that often occurs with missing teeth.
  • Enhanced Comfort Because dental implants are securely anchored into the jawbone, they are far more comfortable than dentures, which can slip or rub against the gums. Dental implants feel like natural teeth, providing you with a stable and reliable solution for tooth replacement.
  • Easy Maintenance Unlike dentures, which require removal for cleaning, dental implants are treated just like natural teeth. You can brush, floss, and care for your implants in the same way you would with your natural teeth. Regular checkups and professional cleanings are essential for maintaining your implants.

Types of Dental Implants at 360 Dental PC

At 360 Dental PC, we offer a variety of implant dentistry solutions to meet the needs of every patient. These include:

  • All-on-4 Dental Implants For patients who need a full arch of teeth, All-on-4 dental implants provide a permanent solution. This technique uses just four implants to support an entire arch of teeth, providing the stability of implants with fewer posts.
