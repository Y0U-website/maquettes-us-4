How All-on-4 Dental Implants Work

The All-on-4 dental implant procedure is a relatively simple and efficient process. It involves the following key steps:

  • Consultation and Evaluation Before undergoing the dental implant procedure, you will meet with Dr. Sitkovetskiy for a thorough consultation. During this appointment, we will evaluate your oral health, take digital X-rays, and discuss your goals for tooth replacement. Based on this information, Dr. Sitkovetskiy will design a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.
  • Implant Placement Surgery On the day of the surgery, Dr. Sitkovetskiy will place four titanium implants into your jawbone, two in the front and two in the back at an angle. The angled placement maximizes bone support, which is ideal for patients who may have experienced bone loss. Most patients are given local anesthesia or sedation to ensure a comfortable procedure.
  • Attaching the Prosthetic Teeth Once the implants have been placed and allowed to fuse with the bone, we will attach a full set of custom-made prosthetic teeth (the dental bridge). In most cases, this happens within 24 hours, allowing you to walk out of the office with a complete set of new teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.
  • Healing and Aftercare During the healing period, your new implants will fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This typically takes 3-6 months, during which time your implants will become a permanent part of your jaw. Dr. Sitkovetskiy will provide detailed aftercare instructions, including tips for managing any swelling, discomfort, or bruising during your recovery.
