Benefits of All-on-X Dental Implants

At 360 Dental, we understand how life-changing a complete smile can be. All-on-X is more than a cosmetic upgrade it restores oral function, self-esteem, and long-term dental care.

Why Patients Choose All-on-X?

For those who have struggled with missing teeth or poorly fitting appliances, All-on-X offers a dramatic upgrade in quality of life and oral health.

  • Same-Day Teeth: You leave surgery with a full set of non-removable, functional teeth
  • Stable & Secure: No more slipping or clicking like with removable dentures
  • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent deterioration
  • Improved Confidence: Smile, laugh, and eat freely without embarrassment
  • Long-Term Value: With proper care, All-on-X can last for decades

Maintenance & Longevity of All-on-X

Like any investment in your health, your All-on-X dental implants require care to ensure long-lasting results. Fortunately, maintenance is simple and becomes second nature.

  • Daily Routine Brush at least twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Flossing can be done with threaders designed for implants, or many patients find a water flosser more convenient for cleaning around a fixed bridge. An alcohol-free mouthwash helps keep your mouth fresh and minimizes bacteria buildup.
  • Professional Care Routine checkups with your Philadelphia dentist every six months are vital. During these visits, we’ll monitor gum health, examine the integrity of the implants, and take X-rays as needed to ensure everything remains stable. Professional cleanings also remove plaque and tartar in areas that are hard to reach at home.
  • Habits to Avoid Even though dental implants are strong, they are not indestructible. To protect your prosthesis, avoid chewing ice, biting hard candy, or using your teeth to open packages. These habits can damage the prosthetic teeth or place unnecessary pressure on the implants.
  • Long-Term Expectations With excellent home care and regular checkups, the titanium implants themselves can last a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth may need replacement every 10 15 years due to normal wear, but this is significantly longer than the lifespan of traditional dentures. In terms of both durability and aesthetics, All-on-X full arch dental implants are one of the best long-term solutions available today.

Life With All-on-X: What Patients Can Expect Day to Day

One of the most exciting parts of receiving All-on-X full arch dental implants is how seamlessly they integrate into everyday life. For many patients, it feels like turning back the clock and reclaiming the comfort and confidence of natural teeth.

Patients frequently describe the treatment as getting their life back. The ability to eat without restrictions, smile without hesitation, and interact without worry is what makes All-on-X such a transformative option.

  • Eating Patients no longer have to worry about limiting their diet to soft foods. Crunchy apples, chewy meats, and even corn on the cob are back on the menu. Unlike dentures, there’s no need for messy adhesives or fear of slipping during meals.
  • Speaking Dentures can sometimes cause lisps or muffled sounds, but All-on-X supports clear, natural speech. Whether you’re in a business meeting, giving a presentation, or talking with friends, you’ll feel confident expressing yourself.
  • Confidence Social interactions change dramatically once you no longer worry about loose appliances. Patients often say they smile more, laugh freely, and even enjoy having photos taken again.
  • Cleaning Caring for your new teeth is straightforward. Brush and floss just like natural teeth. Many patients also choose to use a water flosser to easily clean around the bridge and implants. Our team will demonstrate the best cleaning techniques during your follow-ups.
  • Comfort No more sore spots, no bulky acrylic covering the roof of your mouth, and no embarrassing clicks or slips. The streamlined design of All-on-X dental implants feels comfortable and natural from day one.

The All-on-X Process at 360 Dental

Dr. Mikhail Sitkovetskiy and the 360 Dental team make the process as smooth and supportive as possible. Every step is focused on your comfort, education, and results.

During your first visit, Dr. Sitkovetskiy will perform a full exam, take 3D imaging, and discuss your goals. If All-on-X is right for you, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan.

On the day of surgery, the implants are strategically placed in areas of strong bone. Temporary teeth are attached the same day, so you won’t leave without a smile.

Your implants will fuse with the jawbone over the next few months, creating a strong foundation for your permanent teeth. This natural process, called osseointegration, is what makes dental implants so durable.

Once healing is complete, your final set of teeth, designed for fit, aesthetics, and durability, will be secured in place. You’ll leave with a smile that looks natural and feels strong.

For patients with anxiety or complex cases, we also offer sedation options to make the dental implant procedure as comfortable as possible.

Advantages Over Other Dental Implant Procedures

  • Single Dental Implants: Ideal for replacing individual missing teeth , but require multiple surgeries and longer treatment time if many teeth are missing.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-X

What Is All-on-X?

What Is The

All-on-X Procedure?

  • Is All-on-X painful? Most patients report minimal discomfort. We use local anesthesia and offer sedation for a stress-free experience. Post-surgical discomfort is usually managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • How Long Does the All-On-X Procedure Take? The surgery itself typically takes a few hours. In most cases, patients receive a temporary set of teeth the same day.
  • Will I Be Without Teeth During Healing? No. You’ll receive a fixed temporary prosthesis on the day of surgery, so you won’t have to go without teeth.
  • How long do All-on-X implants last? With good oral hygiene and regular maintenance including dental exams & cleanings the implants can last a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth may need replacing after 10 15 years.
  • Is All-on-X better than regular dentures? Yes, for many patients. All-on-X provides superior stability, comfort, and aesthetics compared to traditional removable dentures.
  • What if I’ve been told I don’t have enough bone? Thanks to strategic implant placement, All-on-X often works even in cases with bone loss. In some cases, bone grafting may be required before implant placement.
  • How do I care for All-on-X implants? You’ll need to brush, floss, and maintain excellent hygiene at home. Our team will guide you through effective techniques based on our home care recommendations and may suggest tools like a water flosser. Regular dental X-rays will also be used to monitor implant health.
  • Is the Procedure Safe? Yes. All-on-X has a high success rate when performed by an experienced provider. At 360 Dental, we use advanced imaging and planning software to enhance precision and reduce risk.
  • Can I Eat Normally With All-On-X? Yes. After the initial soft-food healing period, most patients return to eating a wide variety of foods, from steak to crunchy vegetables.
  • Can Smokers Get All-On-X? Smoking increases the risk of implant failure, but patients who quit before and during recovery often do well. Your Philadelphia dentist can discuss safe options and support quitting if needed.
  • How Do I Clean Around All-On-X Implants? Use a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss threaders, or a water flosser, and regular rinsing. Our team will demonstrate effective techniques for keeping your implants healthy.
  • Are All-On-X Dental Implants Permanent? Yes. With proper care, the implants themselves can last a lifetime. Prosthetic teeth may need replacement after 10 15 years, but the implant foundation remains secure.
  • How Successful Is the Procedure Compared to Traditional Implants? All-on-X has a high success rate, often comparable to or better than placing multiple individual dental implants. The key factors are good oral hygiene, overall health, and regular professional care.

Costs & Financing Options

  • Number of Implants Required: While All-on-X typically uses 4 6 implants per arch, some patients may need additional support based on bone quality or bite strength. The number of implants directly affects both cost and treatment time.
  • Materials Used for the Prosthesis: High-quality, durable prosthetics are designed to mimic natural enamel in both look and function. Choices may include acrylic bridges, porcelain bridges, or zirconia, each with different durability and aesthetic properties.
  • Preparatory Procedures: Extractions, bone grafting, or sinus lifts may be needed before implant placement. These preparatory steps ensure a solid foundation for your full arch dental implants and can add to the overall investment.
