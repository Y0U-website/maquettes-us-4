Are Dental Implants Right for Me?

Ideal candidates for implants are those in good oral and general health, with sufficient bone density to support the implant. Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, procedures like bone grafting may still make implants a viable option for you. This surgical dental procedure has a 90 to 95 percent success rate, with many lasting for decades, making this a valuable investment into your current quality of life and future happiness.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for missing teeth and can last a lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups.

How Long Is The Recovery Period For Dental Implants?

The initial recovery period after implant placement generally lasts a few days, but osseointegration (the healing process where the implant fuses with the jawbone) can take several months.

How Do I Care For My Dental Implants?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth, involving good oral hygiene practices such as daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups.
