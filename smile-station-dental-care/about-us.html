Meet Dr. Scott Bridges

When you walk through the doors of Dr. Scott Bridges’ office, you’re getting a hometown experience built around you. As someone who grew up right here in Paducah, Dr. Bridges understands what it means to take care of neighbors. His passion for helping people feel confident about their smiles has shaped every part of his practice.

You deserve a dentist who never stops learning. That’s why Dr. Bridges completes over one hundred hours of continuing education each year, so you benefit from the latest technology, advanced sedation options and beautiful, lasting results in cosmetic dentistry. Every visit is designed to help you feel relaxed, informed, and completely confident about your care.

And because Paducah is home, giving back is part of who he is. Whether he’s volunteering in the community or serving up barbecue with the award-winning Buzzard Bros. charity team, Dr. Bridges believes that taking care of people extends far beyond the dental chair, because your smile, your comfort and your trust always come first.

Our Caring Team

The Smile Station team is committed to creating a relaxed, friendly, and welcoming environment for every patient. From the moment you step through our doors, you will feel the warmth and dedication that define our practice. Our team understands that visiting the dentist can be daunting. Thus, we strive to make sure every visit is as pleasant and stress-free as possible. We offer sedation dentistry to help alleviate anxiety and make your experience comfortable and relaxed.

Patty

Norma

Norma was born and raised in Chicago. She lives in Western Kentucky by choice and loves it! She loves the atmosphere and patients and enjoys being an advocate for better health. She works hard to help people understand that dental health is important to their overall health. Outside of work, she visits with her grandgirls, travelling, reading, hiking, working and attending church.
