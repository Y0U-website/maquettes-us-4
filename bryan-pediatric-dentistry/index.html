We have made sure our office has all the technology and comforts to make this possible​!

We are pleased to be able to offer complete dental care for your child. Prevention is key in dental health. Children are recommended by the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry to have regular dental check ups starting as soon as the first tooth erupts. We are also able to offer other preventive care, such as cleanings, sealants and fluoride treatments.

Whatever treatment needs may arise, we can help you choose a treatment option and time frame that both you and your child are comfortable with. As with anything, there is nearly always more than one choice.

Dr. Kristi received her Bachelor s of Science (BS) degree in Mathematics and Computer Science from St. Joseph s in Northwest Indiana. She attended on a full academic scholarship and graduated with honors.

Her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree is from the Indiana University School of Dentistry. Dr. Kristi was lucky enough to receive many awards and scholarships during dental school, including the ADA academic scholarship and Omicron Kappa Upsilon award, given to the top 10 % of dental scholars.

These achievements allowed her to be selected by the Children s Hospital of Philadelphia and University of Pennsylvania to complete her fellowship and specialty residency in Pediatric Dentistry. She is Board certified in Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Kristi and her husband, Clay, have three daughters and two cats (also girls!).
