Understanding Your Dental Insurance

Dental insurance can be complex. Here’s a straightforward explanation of how most plans work:

Annual Maximum

Most dental insurance plans have an annual maximum (typically $1,000-$2,000). This is the total amount your insurance will pay for dental care in one calendar year. Once you reach this limit, you’re responsible for any additional costs until the next year begins.

Deductible

Your deductible is the amount you pay out-of-pocket before your insurance starts covering services. Common deductibles range from $50 to $100 per person or per family. Preventive services like cleanings and exams often don’t require meeting the deductible first.

Coverage Levels

Most dental plans divide services into three categories with different coverage percentages:

Coverage percentages vary by plan. We’ll verify your specific benefits.

  • Preventive Care (100 %) - Exams, cleanings, x-rays, fluoride treatments
  • Major Procedures (50 %) - Crowns, bridges, dentures, implants

Pre-Authorization For Major Treatment

For more extensive treatment (crowns, bridges, dentures), we can submit a pre-authorization to your insurance company. This gives you a written estimate of what your insurance will cover before you commit to treatment. There’s no obligation, and it helps you plan financially.

What If I Don’t Have Insurance?

Not having dental insurance doesn’t mean you can’t receive quality dental care. Many of our patients don’t have insurance, and we’re committed to making treatment affordable for everyone.

We believe financial concerns should never prevent someone from getting the dental care they need. Whether you need a routine cleaning or more extensive treatment, we’ll work with you to find a payment solution that fits your situation.

Cost Should Never Prevent You From Getting Care

We understand that dental treatment can be a significant investment. But we also know that delaying necessary care often leads to more complex and expensive problems down the road.

If you’re concerned about cost, please talk to us. We’ll work with you to create a treatment plan that prioritizes your most urgent needs and fits within your budget. Many patients choose to space out treatments over several months or focus on preventive care to avoid more costly procedures later.
