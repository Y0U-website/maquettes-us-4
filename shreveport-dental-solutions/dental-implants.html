Dental Implants in Shreveport, Louisiana

At Shreveport Dental Solutions, we specialize in dental implants to restore missing teeth with long-lasting, natural-looking results. Dental implants are designed to function like natural teeth, helping restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

We use advanced technology and precise planning to complete your implant procedure with accuracy and efficiency. Because our doctors have advanced training in implants, we provide comprehensive care in one location without sending you elsewhere.

Dental Implants at Shreveport Dental Solutions

Learn more about dental implants at Shreveport Dental Solutions and how they are a permanent and highly-effective way of replacing missing, damaged, or severely decayed teeth.

Process

Consultation: We evaluate your oral health and determine if implants are the right solution. Digital imaging: We use advanced scans to plan your implant placement. Treatment planning: We create a customized implant plan tailored to your needs. Implant placement: We place the implant securely into the jawbone. Restoration: We complete your smile with a custom crown or restoration.

Next Steps

Follow all post-procedure instructions for proper healing. Maintain good oral hygiene to protect your implant. Attend follow-up visits to monitor healing and progress. Avoid hard foods during initial healing as recommended. Contact our office with any concerns during recovery.

FAQ

  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants can last many years or even decades with proper care and regular dental visits.
  • How many dental implants can I get done at once? The number of implants depends on your needs, and multiple implants can often be placed in a single visit.
  • What should I do if my dental implant fails? If an implant fails, contact our office right away so we can evaluate the area and recommend the best next steps.
