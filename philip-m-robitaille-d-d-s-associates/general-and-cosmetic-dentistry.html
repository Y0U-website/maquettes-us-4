Fillings

Cavities are one of the most common dental concerns — but treating them is simple and comfortable with modern dentistry. At our office, we use durable, natural-looking tooth-colored fillings to restore strength and protect your smile.

Getting a filling is a quick and comfortable procedure:

In many cases, treatment can be completed in a single visit.

  • The decayed area is gently removed.
  • The tooth is cleaned and prepared.
  • The composite material is placed and shaped.
  • A special light hardens the material for a secure bond.
  • The filling is polished for a smooth, natural finish.

Bridges

Missing teeth can affect more than just your appearance — they can impact your ability to chew comfortably, speak clearly, and maintain proper alignment of your remaining teeth. Dental bridges provide a reliable, natural-looking solution to restore both function and confidence.

With proper care, dental bridges can provide many years of dependable function.

Getting a dental bridge typically involves:

Our team ensures a comfortable experience and precise fit for natural-looking results.

  • Restores your smile and facial structure
  • Improves chewing and speaking ability
  • Prevents neighboring teeth from shifting
  • Maintains proper bite alignment
  • Provides a fixed, stable solution
  • Preparing the teeth adjacent to the missing tooth.
  • Taking precise digital impressions for a custom fit.
  • Placing a temporary restoration if needed.
  • Securing your permanent bridge for long-lasting stability.

Same Day Crowns

When a tooth is damaged, weakened, or severely decayed, a dental crown can restore its strength, function, and appearance. With advanced digital technology, we’re proud to offer same-day crowns — allowing you to receive a custom, permanent crown in just one visit.

No temporary crowns. No multiple appointments. Just efficient, precise care.

Traditional crowns typically require two visits and temporary restorations. With same-day crown technology, we can complete the entire process in a single appointment.

You leave the office with your permanent crown the very same day.

The result is a restoration that fits comfortably and blends seamlessly with your natural smile.

  • One convenient visit
  • No messy traditional impressions
  • No temporary crowns
  • Precise digital design
  • Durable, natural-looking ceramic materials
  • The tooth is carefully prepared.
  • A digital scan creates a precise 3D model of your tooth.
  • Your custom crown is designed and milled in-office.
  • The crown is bonded securely into place.
