The practice

Located in Somerset, Massachusetts, our practice combines state-of-the-art technology with a gentle, patient-focused approach to help you achieve a healthy, confident smile that lasts a lifetime.

At Philip M. Robitaille, DDS & Associates, we provide comprehensive general and cosmetic dental services designed to protect, restore, and enhance your smile. Whether you need routine preventive care or advanced restorative treatment, our team delivers precise, comfortable dentistry using modern technology including Solea® laser dentistry.

Our goal is simple: keep your smile healthy, functional, and beautiful for life.

Preventive care is the foundation of long-term oral health. Regular visits allow us to detect concerns early and help you avoid more complex treatment in the future.

Professional dental cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that daily brushing and flossing can’t eliminate. Routine cleanings help prevent cavities, gum disease, and bad breath while keeping your smile fresh and bright.

Comprehensive dental exams allow us to evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health. We screen for cavities, gum disease, oral cancer, and other potential concerns to ensure early detection and timely care.

Advanced digital X-rays provide detailed images of areas not visible during a visual exam. This technology allows us to diagnose issues accurately while minimizing radiation exposure.

Gum disease (periodontal disease) is a common but serious condition that can lead to tooth loss if left untreated.
