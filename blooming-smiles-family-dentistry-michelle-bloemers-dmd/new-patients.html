Request Your First Appointment

Your first visit is really about us getting to know each other. We’ll ask about your medical history and any medications, take a full set of x-rays (if necessary), and do a complete exam of your teeth, gums, and jaw, along with an oral cancer screening. From there, Dr. Bloemers will walk you through what she found and build a treatment plan together with you, one that fits your needs and your timeline.
