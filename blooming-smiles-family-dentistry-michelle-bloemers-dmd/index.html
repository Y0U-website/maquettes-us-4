Meet Dr. Michelle Bloemers, DMD Michelle grew up in Michigan, where her mother’s dental office became a second home. She spent her high school summers there as a dental assistant, and by the time she graduated with her degree in Human Biology from Michigan State, she’d already spent years learning dentistry from the inside.

During her third year of dental school at OHSU, she was diagnosed with breast cancer and underwent surgery and chemotherapy while continuing her studies. She graduated on time, with honors, in the top 10 % of her class, and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society.
