A professional dental cleaning at least twice a year can improve your oral health. During a dental cleaning, you’ll receive diagnostic and preventive services from your dentist as well as any needed educational information.

Reviewing and updating medical history, including information about heart problems, pregnancy, diabetes and medications, which may have an impact on your oral health

What is a Filling? A filling is a way to restore a tooth damaged by decay back to its normal function and shape. The dentist will use a composite resin for restorative material to mimic the same color of natural tooth structure.

Les soins

A dental crown is a tooth shaped "cap" that is placed over a tooth to restore its shape and size, increase strength, and improve your appearance. We also use crown to cover a dental implant and to make a cosmetic modification (veneers).
