A Family-Friendly, Conveniently Located Dental Office

At our dental office, we treat every patient with compassion, respect, and a gentle touch. When you visit our office, you can expect a pleasant experience every time. We welcome all members of the family, from children and adults to seniors and handicapped patients.

Our dental office nestles in the quaint hamlet of Thornwood in Central Westchester County, NY. Just a short drive away from the White Plains area, we are conveniently located near Pleasantville, Chappaqua, and Armonk.

About Dr. Vito Racanelli, DDS

Dr. Vito Racanelli grew up in Valhalla, NY. He graduated from Westlake HS and Fordham University undergraduate. He decided that dentistry was his calling. He pursued his dental degree at NYU College of Dentistry where he received certificates and awards for general and pediatric dentistry. After gaining some valuable experience, he opened his own office in Thornwood where he has been serving the community for more than 30 years.

Dr. Racanelli is a firm believer in supporting local schools and the Chamber of Commerce. A member of the American Dental Association and the Ninth District Dental Society, the doctor is passionate about his profession. He continues his education to provide his patients with the latest and greatest dental care. In his spare time, Dr. Racanelli enjoys the outdoors and spending time with his wife and daughter.
