Dr. Moetaz Homsi

Meet Dr. Homsi, the founder of Primavera Dental Group. After earning his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry, Dr. Homsi spent a few years serving patients before pursuing a fellowship in prosthodontics at the prestigious University of Washington School of Dental Medicine. He then went on to complete a three-year residency in prosthodontics along with a Master of Science degree at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Passionate about ensuring his patients receive excellent clinical care, Dr. Homsi has received multiple awards in the field of prosthodontics. Patients benefit from his extensive training and experience with full mouth rehabilitation, dental implant treatment, surgical and restorative solutions, and cosmetic transformation.

Beyond serving patients, Dr. Homsi loves to spend time with his wife, young son, and their puppy Rocky (named after his favorite movie). He also enjoys cooking, trying new food, and capturing memories and smiles through photography.

Your Journey to Optimal Health

A Comprehensive First Visit

Your new patient exam at Primavera Dental Group is thorough and personalized. Dr. Homsi will review your medical history, discuss your concerns and goals, and perform a comprehensive oral examination. This includes digital X-rays, intraoral photos, and a detailed assessment of your teeth, gums, and bite. We&#x27;ll then create a tailored treatment plan to address your unique needs and answer any questions you may have.

What to Bring

Please bring your ID, insurance information (if applicable), a list of current medications, and any recent dental X-rays. If you have specific concerns or questions, jot them down so we can address them during your visit.
