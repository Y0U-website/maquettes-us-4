The Benefits Of Dental Implants

Natural Appearance and Feel Dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function like your natural teeth. This means you can eat, speak, and smile with confidence, knowing that your implants are indistinguishable from your natural teeth.

Improved Oral Health Unlike traditional bridges, which require reducing adjacent teeth, dental implants do not affect surrounding teeth. This preserves your natural tooth structure, promoting better long-term oral health.

Durability and Longevity With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Their durability makes them a cost-effective solution in the long run, reducing the need for frequent replacements or repairs.

The Dental Implant Treatment Process

Initial Consultation and Evaluation

The journey to a confident smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Primavera Dental Group. During this visit, Dr. Moetaz Homsi will assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine if dental implants are the best solution for you. This includes taking X-rays and impressions to ensure precise planning and placement of the implants.

Implant Placement Surgery

Once you are deemed a suitable candidate, the next step is the surgical placement of the implant. This is a minor procedure performed under local anesthesia. The titanium post is carefully inserted into the jawbone, where it will serve as the new root for your replacement tooth. After placement, the implant will need time to integrate with the bone, a process known as osseointegration, which typically takes a few months.

Abutment and Crown Placement

After the implant has successfully fused with the jawbone, an abutment is attached to the implant post. This serves as a connector between the implant and the crown. Once the gum tissue has healed around the abutment, a custom-made crown is placed on top, completing the restoration. The crown is crafted to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, ensuring a seamless and natural appearance.
