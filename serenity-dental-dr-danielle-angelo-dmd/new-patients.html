New Patients at Our Brick, NJ…

  • New Patients at Our Brick, NJ Dental Office | Serenity Dental Skip to main content What happens at your first appointment 1 A comprehensive exam We look at your teeth, gums, bite, and existing dental work, and talk through anything that has been bothering you.
  • 2 A full set of digital X-rays Radiographs let us see what an exam alone cannot — between teeth, below the gumline, and around the roots.
  • 3 A cleaning Most new patients have their cleaning at this same appointment rather than coming back for it separately.
  • 4 A treatment plan, if you need one If anything needs attention, we explain what and why, and lay out the options before you decide anything.

Complete your forms before you arrive

New patient forms are completed digitally, and we ask for them at least 24 hours before your appointment. We text you a link once your appointment is booked, or email it instead if you prefer. You can also start them here at any time.

What to bring

  • Photo ID or driver&#x27;s license
  • Your insurance card, if you have dental insurance
  • Your completed new patient forms
  • A method of payment, if any is due on the day

If you find dental visits difficult

Oral sedation is available in selected cases. If you think that might make treatment manageable for you, raise it when you book and we can discuss whether it is appropriate.

Booking and scheduling

Routine cleanings are usually booked six months ahead — we will schedule your next one while you are still in the chair. If you have not booked in advance, we can normally fit you in within a week.

For a dental emergency outside office hours, call or text (732) 920-6677 . Messages to that number reach a doctor directly.

Ready to book your first visit?

Send us a message and we will get back to you, or call or text the office directly.
