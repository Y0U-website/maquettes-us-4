A dental hygienist works under a dentist’s supervision and provides preventative oral care. They work directly with a variety of patients and help them properly care for their oral health.

Day-to-day responsibilities of dental hygienists include cleaning and polishing teeth, taking X-rays and molds and performing administrative duties such as updating charts and recording patient dental histories.
