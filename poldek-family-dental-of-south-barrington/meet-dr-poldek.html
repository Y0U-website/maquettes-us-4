Beyond the Practice

I’ve lived in Schaumburg since 2003 but am originally from Ancona, a small farming community in Central Illinois. I’m an early riser and enjoy starting my day with a workout. Outside the office, my greatest joy is spending time with my wife and our two children. Our weekends are often spent cheering them on at their sporting events. Meet Dr. Chase Small

I’m Dr. Chase Small, and I’m proud to be back where it all began, South Barrington, Illinois. I was born and raised here and attended Rose, Prairie, and Barrington High School. It means so much to return to the community that shaped me and now have the opportunity to care for the families and neighbors I grew up around.

One of the things that drew me to Poldek Family Dental is the genuine relationships Dr. Poldek and team build with every patient. My goal as your dentist is to provide compassionate, personalized care for patients of all ages. I want every visit to feel comfortable and stress-free, and I strive to ensure each patient leaves feeling confident in their smile and oral health. I treat every patient like family and am dedicated to delivering the highest quality care possible.

Outside the office, you can find me spending time with my golden retriever, Bear, cheering on the Chicago Bears, working out, snowboarding, or enjoying time outdoors. I bring that same energy and enthusiasm into my practice every day.

I look forward to meeting you, learning about your goals, and partnering with you to keep your smile healthy and beautiful for life.
