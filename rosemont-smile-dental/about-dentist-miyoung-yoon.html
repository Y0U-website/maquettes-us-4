Dr. Miyoung Yoon

Dr. Miyoung Yoon provides comprehensive, patient-centered dental care with a focus on prevention, comfort, and long-term oral health. She is proud to serve families in the La Crescenta community with personalized, one-on-one care.

After completing her dental training at the University at Buffalo and her Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency, Dr. Yoon has focused on building long-term relationships with patients through thoughtful, honest care.

She welcomes patients from La Crescenta, Montrose, Glendale, and nearby communities who are looking for clear communication, gentle treatment, and a dentist who takes time to listen.

Background & Experience

Dr. Yoon graduated from the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine and completed her Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the University at Buffalo. She has been practicing general dentistry with a strong commitment to clinical excellence and patient care.

Before relocating to La Crescenta, Dr. Yoon lived in Buffalo, New York with her family. Her move to the Crescenta Valley area came after her husband accepted a position at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL). Since then, she has been proud to serve and care for the local Foothill community.

Teaching & Professional Involvement

Dr. Yoon is passionate about dental education. She previously served as an adjunct clinical professor at the College of Dental Medicine at Western University of Health Sciences and at the University at Buffalo. She also provides selective shadowing opportunities for students interested in pursuing a career in dentistry.

She is a member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, San Fernando Valley Dental Society, and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Approach to Care

Dr. Yoon focuses on preventive and minimally invasive dentistry, helping patients preserve their natural teeth whenever possible. She believes in building strong doctor-patient relationships based on trust, clear communication, and personalized treatment planning.

Whether you need a comprehensive exam, routine cleaning, or treatment for a sudden dental concern, her goal is to provide thoughtful, comfortable care for every member of your family.

Services Offered

In addition to general dental services such as cleanings, fillings, crowns, root canal therapy, and extractions, Dr. Yoon also provides cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, clear aligner treatment, Botox for teeth grinding, whitening treatments, white spot removal, and minimally invasive cavity care.
