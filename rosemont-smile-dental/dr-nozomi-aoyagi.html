Dr. Nozomi Aoyagi

Dr. Nozomi Aoyagi provides compassionate, detail-oriented dental care with a focus on patient comfort and long-term oral health. She is dedicated to helping patients feel at ease while delivering thoughtful, personalized treatment.

With a gentle approach and strong clinical foundation, Dr. Aoyagi creates treatment plans that support both function and aesthetics.

She welcomes patients from La Crescenta, Montrose, Glendale, and surrounding communities who are looking for clear communication, gentle care, and personalized treatment.

Background & Experience

Dr. Aoyagi earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the UCLA School of Dentistry. She provides comprehensive general dental care for patients of all ages, helping individuals and families maintain healthy smiles through preventive, restorative, and personalized treatment.

She takes pride in building strong relationships with her patients and creating a comfortable, supportive environment for every visit.

Approach to Care

Dr. Aoyagi believes in preventive and minimally invasive dentistry, with an emphasis on preserving natural teeth and supporting long-term oral health. She values clear communication so patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their care.

Whether you are visiting for routine care or more involved treatment, her goal is to provide gentle, thoughtful care tailored to your individual needs.

Services Offered

Dr. Aoyagi provides a full range of general dental services, including cleanings, fillings, crowns, root canal therapy, and extractions. She also offers cosmetic care, braces and clear aligner treatment, and preventive services to help patients maintain healthy, confident smiles.
