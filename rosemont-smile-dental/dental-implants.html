Treatment timing varies from patient to patient. We will explain each step clearly and help you understand what to expect throughout the process.

  • Implant placement: The implant is placed into the jawbone to serve as the foundation for the replacement tooth.
  • Healing and integration: Over time, the bone heals around the implant in a process called osseointegration, which helps the implant become stable and secure.
  • Final restoration: Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or implant-supported restoration is placed to complete your smile.

Implant Restoration Services

If you already have a dental implant in place and need it restored, Rosemont Smile Dental also provides implant restoration services. We can restore dental implants with custom crowns, bridges, or other implant-supported restorations designed for proper fit, natural appearance, and long-term function.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

The best way to find out whether dental implants are right for you is through a consultation. Your oral health, bone support, overall health, and healing ability all play a role in treatment planning. Age alone is usually not the deciding factor.

Some medical conditions, gum disease, and tobacco use may affect healing and treatment timing. If needed, we may coordinate with your physician or discuss preliminary care before moving forward.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution. With good oral hygiene, routine dental care, and healthy surrounding bone and gum tissue, implants can last for many years.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients find dental implant treatment more manageable than they expected. During your consultation, we will explain the procedure, healing process, and ways we help keep treatment as comfortable as possible.

Can a dental implant replace one missing tooth?

Yes. A dental implant is often an excellent option for replacing a single missing tooth without affecting the neighboring teeth.

Can implants support more than one missing tooth?

Yes. Implants can support a crown, bridge, or other restoration depending on how many teeth are missing and what treatment is most appropriate for your smile.

How do I know if I am a candidate for dental implants?

The best first step is a consultation. We will examine your oral health, review your goals, and determine whether implants are a good option for you.
