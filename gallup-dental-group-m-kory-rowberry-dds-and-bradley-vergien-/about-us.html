Meet the Team at Gallup Dental Group

At Gallup Dental Group, we strive to provide you with the best service possible in a warm and welcoming environment!

M. Kory Rowberry, DDS

Dr. Rowberry is a 1985 graduate of the Boyne School of Dentistry at Creighton University in Omaha, Nebraska. Dr. Rowberry maintained a private practice in Gallup for 17 years and worked on the Navajo Nation for 7 years before opening Gallup Dental Group in May of 2010. Dr. Rowberry has resided in Gallup for the past 32 years.

Brad Vergien, DDS

Dr. Vergien is a 1986 graduate of the University of Missouri at Kansas City School of Dentistry. Since 1986 he worked in a variety of private practices on the Navajo Nation before opening Gallup Dental Group in May of 2010. Dr. Vergien resided on the Navajo Nation for 17 years before moving to Gallup in 2003.

Jessica Valdez

She has been working in the Dental Field since 2012. She is from Grants, NM. She is a member of the American Dental Hygiene Association.
