Who dental implants help

From one tooth to a whole smile, there is an implant solution for every situation.

A single missing tooth An implant with a porcelain crown replaces the tooth and its root, without touching the neighbouring teeth.

Several missing teeth Two implants can support a bridge that replaces three or four teeth in a row, or anchor a partial denture.

Loose or uncomfortable dentures Implants lock a denture in place so it never slips, or replace it with a fixed bridge you never take out.

A full arch of failing teeth All-on-4 replaces every tooth in the upper or lower jaw with a fixed bridge on four implants, fitted the same day.

How it works: four phases

It is a process, and we plan it carefully. We want your new teeth to last as long as possible.

  • 1 Evaluation and planning, with both doctors Dr. Ovcharenko checks your health and gums and takes X-rays and a 3D CBCT scan of the site. Dr. Ortiz-Parsons examines your bite and plans the new tooth. You tell us how you want your smile to look; everything is planned around that end result.
  • 2 Implant placement, about an hour Under local anesthesia and sedation if you choose, Dr. Ovcharenko places the titanium implant. Recovery is usually easier than after a tooth extraction. You get a temporary tooth while the bone heals around the implant over three to four months.
  • 3 Your new tooth Dr. Ortiz-Parsons takes a digital impression and our trusted lab crafts the crown. For front teeth, a temporary crown first shapes the gum so the final tooth looks like it grows from it. Three to four weeks later, your porcelain crown is fitted.
  • 4 Maintenance Cleanings every three to six months keep the implant and the gum around it healthy. Looked after, implants last for decades.

Why patients choose implants

Implants are excellent, but your own teeth are still better. Brush, floss and keep your checkups so we can help you keep the teeth you have.

  • Looks like your own tooth Porcelain or zirconia crowns on implants are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth.
  • Built to last Research shows a 10-year success rate above 97 percent. With good care, implants serve for decades.
  • Protects your jawbone Dentures press on the bone and shrink it over time. Implants stimulate the bone and keep it, and your face, from changing shape.
  • Eat what you like No dietary restrictions. Implant teeth chew almost as efficiently as natural teeth.
  • Speak clearly Nothing slips or clicks. Implant-supported teeth stay firmly in place.
  • Saves healthy teeth Unlike a bridge, an implant needs no support from the teeth beside it, so they stay untouched.
