The practice

Part of easing your concerns is explaining what you can expect from our practice.

You and your family can take advantage of a full range of dental services here at Pine Grove Dental Arts.

We’d love to help you achieve a healthier, stronger smile. Whether you’re ready to schedule a visit or have questions about our biomimetic approach, our friendly team is here for you.

At Pine Grove Dental Arts, we practice biomimetic dentistry —restoring teeth to their natural form, function, and strength using advanced composite and ceramic materials. This minimally invasive approach mimics real tooth structure, preserving more of your natural enamel while delivering strong, long-lasting results that protect your smile.

When you have missing teeth, an alternative solution to dentures is dental implants. While dentures are removable, dental implants provide permanent new teeth to restore your mouth’s function and appearance.

CEREC is wonderful because in only one visit you can get a smile you will be proud of! The process uses the latest technologies, allowing us to make artificial teeth right in our office.

When your teeth are missing, it greatly affects your smile. At the same time, the missing tooth has the potential to negatively affect your oral hygiene because of unprotected gum exposure.

Dental inlays and onlays are considered a conservative method of treating damaged teeth. They are used instead of placing a crown.
