What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first appointment is all about getting to know you and your oral health.

Review your medical and dental history Take necessary digital X-rays Perform a comprehensive exam Provide a gentle professional cleaning (when appropriate) Discuss any findings and personalized treatment recommendations

We take time to answer your questions and explain your options—no rushing, no pressure.
