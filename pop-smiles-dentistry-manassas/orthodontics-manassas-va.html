Tried-and-true, traditional braces remain one of the most effective methods for correcting a wide range of orthodontic issues, from simple crowding to complex bite problems. Modern braces are smaller, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever before. They consist of high-grade stainless steel brackets and archwires that apply gentle, continuous pressure to guide teeth into alignment. This option is often the most reliable and cost-effective solution for comprehensive treatment.

  • Removable: You can take them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss, allowing you to maintain normal oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods.
  • Comfort: With no metal brackets or wires, clear aligners are smooth and comfortable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Embarking on an orthodontic journey comes with many questions. Here are answers to five common inquiries we receive at our Manassas practice.

1. Am I too old for orthodontic treatment?

Absolutely not. Teeth can be moved at any age, as long as your gums and jawbone are healthy. In fact, a growing number of our orthodontic patients are adults who are finally investing in the smile they have always wanted. We offer discreet options like Invisalign® that are perfect for professional adults.

2. How long will my orthodontic treatment take?

The duration of treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case and the type of treatment you choose. On average, comprehensive orthodontic care can take anywhere from 12 to 24 months. Simpler cases may be completed in as little as six months. We will provide you with a personalized time estimate during your consultation.

It is normal to experience some mild discomfort or pressure for a few days after your braces are first put on or after an adjustment. This is a sign that your teeth are moving. The soreness is temporary and can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Modern appliances are designed to be as comfortable as possible.

4. How much do braces or Invisalign® cost?

The cost of orthodontic treatment depends on the length and complexity of your case. During your consultation, we will provide a clear, all-inclusive quote for your treatment. We work with many dental insurance plans that offer orthodontic benefits and also provide flexible financing options to help make your new smile affordable.

5. Do I still need to see my regular dentist during orthodontic treatment?

Yes. It is very important to continue seeing your general dentist for regular checkups and cleanings every six months. Braces and other appliances can make it more challenging to clean your teeth, so professional cleanings are essential for preventing cavities and gum disease during your orthodontic journey.
