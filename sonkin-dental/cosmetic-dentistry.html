Cosmetic Dentistry in Elmont, NY

Cosmetic dentistry is the dental specialty that focuses on the aesthetics of your smile, with the goal of improving the color, shape, size, spacing, and overall appearance of your teeth.

At your consultation, the dentist will work with you to evaluate your current smile and determine which cosmetic dental treatments can help you achieve your goals.

Common Cosmetic Procedures

Teeth whitening is one of the most popular cosmetic dental treatments. A simple teeth whitening procedure can make teeth appear several shades whiter and brighter. Patients can whiten their teeth at home with over-the-counter products, but professional treatments are much more effective. These procedures can remove years of staining to reveal a brilliant, white smile.

Dental veneers are thin porcelain shells that replace the outer enamel surface of teeth. They are custom-made to fit your teeth, and they are stain-resistant. That means your veneers will stay white and bright for years to come.

Your dentist will need to take an impression of your natural teeth to send to the dental lab. There, they will fabricate your veneers using the highest quality porcelain materials.

If your tooth is decayed, damaged, or cracked, a dental crown is often a treatment option. A dental crown is a porcelain cap that is placed over a tooth. A dental crown can repair a chipped, decayed, or broken tooth. A dental crown is also used for teeth that are misshapen, crooked, or discolored. Dental crowns are strong, durable, and can last many years. They are a great long-term dental solution.

Dental bridges are fixed (non-removable) appliances used to replace one or more missing teeth. A bridge restores your smile by filling gaps and stopping the surrounding teeth from shifting. Our dentists may suggest a bridge if two or more adjacent teeth are missing.

Dental implants are an extremely popular treatment for missing teeth. They are an excellent long-term solution for missing teeth, and patients who choose dental implants typically report high satisfaction rates.

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots. They are surgically placed and fuse with the jawbone over time. When properly placed and restored, dental implants can last a lifetime and prevent a myriad of oral health issues.

Orthodontic treatment improves the look of teeth that are crooked, crowded or have shifted over time. Braces straighten teeth using brackets and wires to move them into the right position. Braces can also help improve teeth that are protruded or to close any gaps between teeth. Invisalign aligners align teeth while being virtually invisible.

A gum recontouring treatment reshapes the gum line, exposing more of the tooth. This treatment is often recommended for those who have a “gummy smile,” meaning too much of their gums show when they smile. Gum recontouring can also be beneficial for those who have an uneven gum line.
