Dental Implants in Elmont, NY

Dental implants are permanent, artificial tooth roots that are placed into the jawbone. Dental implants are made of titanium and fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Dental implants restore dental function and aesthetic appearance. Dental implants can support single dental crowns, dental bridges, and dentures.

Benefits Of Dental Implants

Dental implants are a great way to replace missing teeth. Implants are permanently secured in the jaw bone, so patients don’t have to worry about them slipping out of place or feeling uncomfortable. Dental implants also help preserve bone structure in the jaw. The major benefits offered by dental implants include the following:

  • Appearance : Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth. They do not move or slip within the mouth, so they do not cause speech impediments.
  • Comfort : Dental implants replace the roots of teeth. The implants provide the same function as natural teeth roots, preventing bone loss.
  • Safety : Like natural teeth, dental implants do not decay.
  • Durability : Dental implants generally last longer than dentures.

Dental Implant Procedure

A dental implant is a titanium post that is placed into the jawbone and acts as an artificial tooth root. A dental crown, bridge, or denture is then mounted to the post, giving the patient a natural-looking smile. The procedure of implant placement is as follows:

To determine a patient’s candidacy for the procedure, they may need to undergo a series of images and X-rays. Someone with missing teeth but otherwise good oral health, jawbone density, and free from periodontal disease can undergo the dental implant procedure.

First, your dentist will start the procedure by numbing the treatment area and preparing it for the dental implant by cleaning the gum and bone tissue. Then, they will place the dental implant in the jaw. The dental implant will act as the root of the tooth. Once the gum is fully healed, your dentist will attach a connector post to the dental implant. The connector post will act as the tooth’s base, and the dentist will attach a restoration, such as a crown, to the connector post.

Dental Implant Success Rates

Dental implants are one of the most popular tooth replacement options available today. With improved dental technology and techniques, dental implant success rates have improved tremendously in recent years. In fact, dental implants have a proven success rate of over 95 %. This makes them one of the most successful dental procedures available.
