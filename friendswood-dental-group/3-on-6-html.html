3 on 6 FAQs

Who Is a Good Candidate for 3 on 6 ?

There are several different factors when considering who is a good candidate for 3 on 6 . During your consultation with us, we will examine your smile and provide you with our honest recommendation. Here are some of the factors that we will look at.

  • Bone Levels: Implants require adequate support, so we must consider your bone shape, density, and structure through imaging.
  • Oral Health: Healthy dental implants require healthy gum tissue.
  • Bad Habits: If you frequently clench or grind your teeth, this is something we need to consider.
  • Medical History: Certain medical conditions and medications can influence treatment planning.

Is 3 on 6 Better Than All-on-4?

This all depends on your unique anatomy, treatment goals, and plan for your smile. Different approaches are needed for different patients. During your consultation with us, we will examine your smile and discuss what is important to you when replacing your missing teeth. Then, we can recommend a treatment method to best meet your needs, whether that be 3 on 6 , All-on-4, or another tooth replacement solution.
