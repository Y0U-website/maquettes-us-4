Theurer Orthodontics’ History

Established in 1988, Theurer Orthodontics has been a cornerstone in the Antelope Valley, dedicated to crafting beautiful smiles for over 35 years. Dr. Theurer, with his wealth of experience, has led the way in orthodontic care, employing cutting-edge technology and the latest advancements to ensure each patient achieves the smile they’ve always desired. Driven by a passion for excellence, Dr. Theurer and his team are committed to making your dream smile a reality.

Thanks to our passionate and dedicated team, we’re committed to bringing beautiful, wide, and natural smiles to the Lancaster, Palmdale Tehachapi and Antelope Valley regions. Our best-in-class team would love to show you the transformative power of orthodontics today at our local Lancaster practice.
