Interceptive Treatment

Interceptive treatment prevents potential dental issues from occurring and focuses on guiding the growth and development of the jaw and teeth. By identifying problems such as overcrowding, misalignment, and bite issues early on, interceptive treatment can reduce the need for more extensive orthodontic procedures in the future.

Interceptive treatment can involve the use of appliances, such as expanders, partial braces, or space maintainers, to create a healthier environment for permanent teeth to erupt. Not only does this improve the function and appearance of your smile, but it also boosts self esteem, confidence and sets the foundation for a lifetime of good oral health.

Airway Aware

A constricted airway can severely impact the growth and development of a child and should be considered when evaluating a child’s overall health. Signs of a restricted airway include snoring, bedwetting, mood swings, behavior problems, teeth grinding, mouth breathing, and difficulty paying attention.Dr. Theurer uses i-CAT FLX (3D cone beam x-ray) to take a CT scan and evaluate the airway of every patient.

If a child under the age of 12 has a constricted airway, Dr. Theurer can initiate an airway protocol which takes advantage of the growth of the child to expand the airway. This can often prevent obstructive sleep apnea and improve the overall health of a child.

Retainers

Retainers play a crucial role in maintaining the alignment of your teeth after your orthodontic treatment, ensuring your beautiful new smile lasts a lifetime. Custom-made for comfort and effectiveness, our retainers are designed to hold teeth in their new positions while the jawbone and gums adapt.

Wearing your retainer as prescribed is the key to preventing teeth from gradually shifting back to their original positions. With various options available, including clear and fixed retainers, we’ll help you choose the best type to suit your lifestyle and keep your smile looking pristine. Our commitment to your lasting smile includes ongoing support and guidance on retainer care and use.

Technology

LiCAT Flex 3D imaging technology allows us to create detailed 3DE images of the oral and facial structures, providing invaluable data for diagnosis and treatment planning and ensuring precision in every procedure. This detailed 3D visualization leads to more accurate diagnoses and customized treatment plans, enhancing overall treatment effectiveness.

The iTero 3D scanner eliminates the need for traditional dental impressions, offering a comfortable, accurate, and faster alternative for creating digital models of the mouth. This technology enhances patient comfort and treatment planning, ensuring a perfect fit for orthodontic appliances and aligners.

AcceleDent is an innovative device that uses gentle micropulses to accelerate tooth movement, potentially reducing treatment time by up to 30 % and enhancing comfort. This non-invasive technology complements any orthodontic treatment, making the journey to your perfect smile faster and more comfortable.

With Dental Monitoring, we can track your treatment progress remotely, allowing for timely adjustments and fewer in-office visits, making your orthodontic journey more convenient than ever. This advanced system ensures that your treatment is progressing as planned, offering peace of mind and increased flexibility in scheduling.
