Invisalign

Invisalign has revolutionized orthodontics by offering a nearly invisible way to straighten teeth. This modern treatment uses clear, removable aligners to achieve a perfect smile.

Invisalign aligners are custom made from advanced 3D imaging technology. Each set of aligners applies gentle pressure to move teeth incrementally. Patients typically switch to a new set of aligners every one to two weeks.

Advantages of Invisalign

  • Discreet appearance: The clear aligners are virtually invisible, making them an excellent choice for adults and teens who want a subtle treatment option.
  • Comfortable fit: Made from smooth, BPA-free plastic, Invisalign aligners are more comfortable than traditional braces.
  • Removable convenience: Aligners can be removed when eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing, allowing for better oral hygiene and no dietary restrictions.
  • Shorter treatment time: In some cases, Invisalign treatment may be quicker than braces.

Treatment Process

  • Consultation and scanning: Dr. Montgomery will evaluate your teeth and use digital imaging to design your custom aligners.
  • Receiving aligners: You’ll receive a series of aligners, each designed to make gradual adjustments.
  • Regular checkups: Periodic visits to Q & A Dental Care ensure your treatment is on track.
  • Final results: Once treatment is complete, a retainer is provided to maintain your new smile.

Caring for Invisalign Aligners

  • Clean aligners daily with a soft brush and lukewarm water or the recommended cleaning solution.
  • Avoid consuming hot beverages while wearing aligners to prevent warping.
  • Store aligners in their case when not in use to avoid damage or loss.

When Should You Consider Orthodontics in Macedonia, OH?

Orthodontic treatment is suitable for individuals of all ages. However, starting early often leads to the most effective results.

For Children

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early intervention can guide jaw growth, create space for erupting teeth, and reduce the need for more invasive treatments later.

For Teens

Adolescence is an ideal time for orthodontic treatment as the jaw is still developing. Options like braces and Invisalign Teen are tailored to fit their lifestyle.

For Adults

It’s never too late to improve your smile. Modern orthodontic solutions cater to adult patients seeking discreet and effective treatments.
