Why Choose Dental Implants in Macedonia, OH?

Dental implants have numerous advantages over traditional tooth replacement options like dentures and bridges. Here are some of the key advantages of dental implants:

Natural Look and Feel

One of the most significant benefits of dental implants is their ability to look and feel just like natural teeth. The implant post is fused with your jawbone, providing a stable foundation for the crown. This means that your dental implant will stay securely in place while you eat, speak, and smile. Unlike dentures that can slip or shift in the mouth, dental implants serve as a permanent solution that provides the comfort and confidence of natural teeth.

Improved Functionality

Dental implants restore full functionality to your mouth. Whether you need to chew, bite, or speak, dental implants provide a stable base for your replacement tooth, enabling you to eat all your favorite foods and engage in social activities without worry. You won’t have to worry about your replacement tooth shifting or affecting your speech, which is often a concern with traditional dentures.

Preservation of Jawbone Health

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone underneath begins to deteriorate over time due to the lack of stimulation from tooth roots. Dental implants help prevent bone loss by stimulating the jawbone as the implant fuses with it. This process, known as osseointegration, preserves the integrity of the jawbone and helps maintain your facial structure.

Long-Term Durability

Dental implants are designed to last for many years, often a lifetime, with proper care. Unlike traditional bridges and dentures that may need to be replaced or adjusted over time, dental implants are a long-term solution. The titanium material used for implants is highly durable, and once the implant has been successfully integrated with the bone, it becomes a permanent fixture in your mouth.

Improved Self-Confidence

With dental implants, you can smile, speak, and eat with confidence. Unlike removable dentures that may cause embarrassment due to slipping or discomfort, dental implants are permanent and stable as a tooth replacement solution. You can feel secure in social settings and enjoy life without worrying about your teeth. Contact us to learn more.

The Dental Implant Procedure

The process of getting dental implants typically involves several stages, including consultations, surgery, and recovery. Dr. Quentin Montgomery at Q & A Dental Care will guide you through each step of the process to ensure your comfort and the success of the procedure.

Initial Consultation and Evaluation

The first step in the dental implant process is a thorough consultation with Dr. Montgomery. During this visit, he will evaluate your oral health, review your medical history, and determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants. X-rays and 3D imaging may be used to assess the health of your jawbone and the position of your remaining teeth.

If you have insufficient jawbone density, Dr. Montgomery may recommend a bone grafting procedure to ensure that there is enough bone to support the implant. Bone grafts help stimulate new bone growth, creating a solid foundation for the implant.

Implant Placement Surgery

Once you have been deemed a good candidate for dental implants, the next step is the surgical placement of the implant post. This procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to ensure that you are comfortable throughout the process. Dr. Montgomery will make a small incision in your gum tissue to expose the jawbone and then carefully place the titanium post into the bone.

After the implant is placed, the gum tissue is sutured closed, and you will begin the healing process. This step usually takes several months as the bone fuses with the implant as the former heals (osseointegration). During this time, Dr. Montgomery will monitor your progress and ensure that the implant is healing properly.

Placement of the Abutment

Once the implant has been successfully integrated with the jawbone, a second minor procedure is needed to place the abutment. The abutment is a small connector that attaches to the implant post and provides a stable base for the final crown. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and the gum tissue is reopened to expose the implant post. After the abutment is placed, the gums are sutured again, and you will need some additional healing time.

Custom Crown Fabrication and Placement

The final step of the dental implant process is the creation and placement of your custom crown. Dr. Montgomery will work with a dental laboratory to design a crown that matches the color, size, and shape of your natural teeth. Once the crown is ready, it will be attached to the abutment, completing the restoration.

The result is a fully restored tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth. Dr. Montgomery will ensure that the crown fits comfortably and securely, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Types of Dental Implants in Macedonia, OH

There are different types of dental implants, each designed to meet specific needs. Dr. Montgomery will discuss the best options for you based on the location of your missing teeth, your bone density, and your overall oral health.

  • Single implants: A single implant replaces one missing tooth, serving as a permanent and natural-looking solution.
  • Multiple implants: Multiple implants can replace several missing teeth, either individually or by supporting a bridge or partial denture.
  • All-on-4 implants: This advanced technique involves placing four implants in the upper or lower jaw to support a full set of replacement teeth. All-on-4 implants are an excellent solution for patients with significant tooth loss.
  • Implant- supported dentures: For patients who need to replace an entire arch of teeth, implant-supported dentures provide additional stability and comfort compared to traditional removable dentures.

Implant-Supported Bridges

An implant-supported bridge is a dental restoration that replaces multiple missing teeth by anchoring the bridge to dental implants rather than relying on neighboring teeth for support. This option is suitable for patients who have lost several teeth in a row but still have healthy teeth or gums surrounding the gap.

In contrast to traditional bridges that require the alteration of adjacent healthy teeth to support the bridge, implant-supported bridges involve placing one or more dental implants into the jawbone. These implants serve as the foundation for the bridge, providing superior stability and ensuring that the bridge will not shift, slip, or cause discomfort.
