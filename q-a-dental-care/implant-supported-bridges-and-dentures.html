Advantages of Implant-Supported Restorations

  • Enhanced stability: Implant-supported restorations are securely anchored to dental implants, which act as artificial tooth roots. This ensures that the prosthetics stay firmly in place during speaking, chewing, and smiling.
  • Natural look and feel: These restorations are custom designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing a realistic appearance. The implants also stimulate the jawbone, maintaining a natural facial structure.
  • Preservation of bone health: Tooth loss often leads to bone resorption in the jaw. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone deterioration and preserving facial contours.
  • Improved functionality: Implant-supported bridges and dentures restore chewing and speaking capabilities to near-natural levels, enabling you to enjoy a varied diet and speak confidently.
  • Long- term durability: With proper care, dental implants can last for decades, making them a worthwhile investment. The prosthetics attached to implants also have a longer lifespan compared to traditional options.

The Implant-Supported Bridge and Denture Process in Macedonia, OH

Initial Consultation

During your first visit to Q & A Dental Care, Dr. Quentin Montgomery will perform a comprehensive examination, including digital imaging and a review of your dental and medical history. This assessment helps determine if you are qualified for implant-supported restorations.

Treatment Planning

Once you’ve been confirmed as qualified, a customized treatment plan is developed. This includes determining the number of implants needed, their placement, and the design of your bridge or denture.

Implant Placement

The dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, a process called osseointegration occurs, where the implants are fused with the bone as the latter heals, creating a stable foundation.

Temporary Restoration (If Needed)

While the bone around the implants heals, a temporary restoration may be provided to maintain aesthetics and functionality.

Attachment of Abutments

After osseointegration is complete, small connectors called abutments are attached to the implants. These will hold the final bridge or denture in place.

Final Restoration

The custom-made bridge or denture is secured to the abutments, completing your smile transformation. Dr. Montgomery will ensure a precise fit and make any necessary adjustments for comfort. Contact us to learn more.

Types of Implant-Supported Dentures

  • Fixed implant-supported dentures (hybrid dentures) : These dentures are permanently attached to implants and can only be removed by a dentist. They demonstrate maximum stability and mimic the feel of natural teeth.
  • Removable implant-supported dentures (snap-in dentures): These dentures snap onto implants for a secure fit but can be removed for cleaning. They are more stable than traditional dentures while demonstrating flexibility.

Post-Procedure Care and Maintenance

  • Oral hygiene: Implant-supported restorations require diligent care to prevent plaque buildup and gum inflammation. Brush and floss daily, paying special attention to the gum line and around the implants.
  • Regular dental checkups: Schedule regular visits to Q & A Dental Care for professional cleanings and to monitor the health of your implants and surrounding tissues.
  • Lifestyle adjustments: Avoid chewing on hard objects, such as ice or pens, to protect your prosthetics. Consider wearing a night guard if you grind your teeth while sleeping.
  • Dietary habits: While implant-supported restorations allow you to enjoy most foods, it’s advisable to avoid excessively sticky or hard items that could damage the prosthetics.
