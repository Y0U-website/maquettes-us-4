Strengthen Your Smile

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride has the unique ability to “remineralize” weakened teeth, and fight back against cavities. In a fluoride treatment, Dr. Reddy will apply a thin layer of fluoride gel to your teeth before rinsing it away. The high concentration of fluoride helps strengthen your teeth to aid in the prevention of tooth decay. This non-invasive treatment can be done after any six-month cleaning and oral exam to keep your smile healthy and strong.

Prevent Cavities

Dental Sealants

Sealants consist of a plastic resin that is applied to your rear teeth in liquid form. After applying the liquid, a UV light is used to harden the resin and create an invisible plastic barrier. By preventing food particles, acid, and bacteria from contacting your enamel directly, dental sealants can stop cavities from forming for up to 10 years.

Fight Back Against Gum Disease

Periodontal Care

Gum disease affects nearly half of all American adults, and can have serious long-term oral health consequences. Fortunately, Dr. Reddy can help eliminate gum disease with a deep cleaning, also know as scaling & root planing to remove bacteria from between your teeth and gums, and smooth your teeth to prevent further bacterial buildup. Along with antibiotics, deep cleaning can help resolve most mild to moderate cases of gum disease and get more severe cases under control.
