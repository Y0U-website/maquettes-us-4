Our Practice

Making smiles in River Grove wider and brighter since 1983

We aim to educate our patients about the importance of their oral health in a relaxing and comfortable environment, including the importance of comprehensive care. We strive to create a proactive mentality towards your general health and well-being. Regular checkups aid in early diagnosis of problems, that, when treated in the early stages of disease reduce painful and often costly treatment. Having a reactive mentality towards one’s health, ie., avoiding the problems until the severity of pain forces the need for treatment, can cause more unneeded pain and suffering.

We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we have created a soothing environment and an experience that exceeds your idea of traditional, painful dental care. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we give our patients the opportunity to have optimum dental care. Give us a call today or stop by our office and start your journey toward dental health!

Gregory R. Bertagna, D.D.S.

Gregory A Bertagna, DDS has been practicing dentistry for over 35 years in River Grove, Illinois. A graduate of Holy Cross High School, he attained both his undergraduate and doctoral degrees with honors from the University of Illinois, here in Chicago. After spending three years as a clinical instructor at the dental college, he opened his private dental practice, which has now grown to the present office, incorporating Doctors Soderquist and Siracusa.

He is active in numerous dental study clubs, and has spent countless hours pursuing continued education in the dental field. His goal is to provide comprehensive, compassionate and affordable dental care to all his patients and to respect their concerns, fears, and apprehensions. He has been married for over thirty years, and has two children.

He enjoys gardening, wood working, and tennis.

Frank M. Siracusa, D.D.S.

Dr. Frank Siracusa has been in the practice of general dentistry since 1989. He grew up in Elmwood Park and graduated from Holy Cross High School in River Grove. He has an undergraduate degree from UIC and DDS degree from University of Illinois. He has completed countless hours of continuing education.

He is a member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, and Chicago Dental Society. Dr. Siracusa has two adult children and resides with his wife, of over thirty years, in the western suburbs.

Dr. Siracusa believes all patients should be treated in a respectful and friendly enviroment. He enjoys treating patients of all ages.
