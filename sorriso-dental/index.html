Biological dentistry is a type of dentistry that takes a holistic approach to oral health and its impact on the entire body. It recognizes that oral health is intimately connected to overall health, and that dental treatments can have a significant impact on the body as a whole.

Biological dentistry differs from traditional dentistry in several ways. For example, it emphasizes the use of biocompatible materials for dental restorations, such as fillings and crowns, to minimize the risk of adverse reactions or allergies.
