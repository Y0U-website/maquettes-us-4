ABout OUR BIOLOGICAL DENTAL PRACTICE IN MORGANTOWN

Why Choose Us

Are you looking for a holistic dentist in the Morgantown & Cheat Lake, WV area to provide thoughtful, holistic care? We here at Sorriso Dental believe oral health is essential to overall wellness. We are committed to providing holistic and biological dental care to help you and your family stay healthy. Our approach to dental care encompasses much more than just traditional methods. Our focus on holistic dentistry means we prioritize your overall health by utilizing biocompatible materials and advanced healing techniques.

In addition to biocompatibility and dental allergy testing, we use the latest biomimetic materials to restore your smile with minimally invasive techniques. Our restorations are designed to look and feel like your natural teeth, providing a seamless and comfortable experience.

With our commitment to a holistic approach, you can be confident that your dental health is in good hands.

Advanced Biological dental Therapies

Our approach goes beyond holistic dentistry. We also provide biological therapies, such as amalgam removal and cavitation treatments, to improve or prevent the negative impact these can have on a person’s health.

We offer many nontraditional healing tools, such as ozone, to speed your recovery safely and quickly. Ozone is an 03 gas that painlessly kills cavity-causing bacteria, desensitizes uncomfortable teeth, and quickens healing. In addition, we offer PRF, plasma rich fibrin, prepared from your blood, so you know it is biocompatible to improve recovery dramatically.

Dental Anxiety Solutions

We understand dental visits can cause anxiety. At Sorriso Dental, we strive to make every visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Our office is serene and dedicated to using the safest materials, advanced holistic therapies, and anxiety management techniques to deliver unsurpassed care. With us, you are heard, respected, and treated like family.
