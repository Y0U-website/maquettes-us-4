FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs that my teen may need orthodontic treatment?

Crowded teeth, poor bites, awkward bite alignment, uneven teeth proportion within the mouth, and difficulty chewing or breathing are all strong signs that your teen may be in need of orthodontic treatment. Even if these issues seem minor now, they can worsen over time if left untreated. Addressing orthodontic concerns early can help protect your teen’s long-term oral health and prevent more serious problems down the line.

What are the benefits of teens’ orthodontics?

While patients can be consulted for orthodontic care at any age, the teenage years are one of the best times to correct teeth and jaw issues and prevent orthodontic problems from worsening in adulthood. Your teen’s jaw and teeth are nearing their final adult stages but are still malleable and receptive to treatment. If your teen’s crooked smile makes them self conscious or gets in the way of their daily life, braces or clear aligners can give them a confidence boost that they will carry on into their adult years and beyond.

What causes orthodontic problems in my teen?

For most teens, orthodontic problems stem from genetic or inherited traits. However, dental diseases, early loss of baby or adult teeth, an accident, or a habit like prolonged thumb sucking may have caused significant smile or bite issues for your teen. Dr. Nova will create a thorough treatment plan to ensure your teen’s unique needs are met and corrected for a lifetime of optimal oral health.

What age should my teen be seen by an orthodontist?

We recommend that all children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7, as advised by the American Association of Orthodontists. However, if your child hasn’t been seen yet and is now in their early teens, it’s definitely not too late! Many teens begin treatment between ages 11 and 15, once many or all of the permanent teeth are in. If you&#x27;re noticing crowding, bite issues, or spacing, it’s a great time to schedule a consult so we can assess their smile and discuss if treatment is needed.

How much does teens’ orthodontics cost?

The cost of teen orthodontic treatment can vary depending on the complexity of the case. During your consultation visit, we will provide an exact quote for treatment. We offer low down payment, flexible, no-interest payment plans, and accept most insurance plans to make treatment as affordable and manageable as possible. Our goal is to help your teen achieve a healthy, confident smile without financial stress.

Types of orthodontics for teens

Metal Braces Metal braces are one of the most common, affordable, and durable options for patients looking to correct mild to severe tooth and jaw misalignment. Utilizing a series of metal archwires, these medical grade stainless steel brackets are a reliable and effective option to treat a wide range of orthodontic issues.

Ceramic Braces Ceramic braces refer to brackets that are made from a tooth-colored ceramic material. They function like traditional metal braces and utilize a series of metal wires to gradually move teeth into the desired position. These brackets are a great option for patients seeking treatment with a less noticeable look.

Clear Aligners Clear aligners, like Invisalign®, are a series of custom-made, removable trays that gradually move teeth into better alignment. Each set of aligners is worn for about one to two weeks before switching to the next in the set. Made from smooth, transparent medical grade plastic, they’re nearly invisible and fit snugly over the teeth—making them a popular choice for teens who want a more discreet option without brackets or wires.

Braces

Our modern braces are sleeker and more comfortable than what parents might remember from their teen years. With options including traditional metal and more discreet ceramic brackets, we help teens find the right balance between effectiveness and appearance. Today&#x27;s braces work more efficiently, potentially reducing overall treatment time—something every teen can appreciate.

Retainers

Once your teen’s braces or aligners come off, retainers play a crucial role in keeping their smile looking great. Teeth naturally want to shift over time, and retainers help hold them in their new, straight positions. We’ll provide custom retainers and guide you on how often to wear them to maintain long-term results. Consistent retainer use is the key to protecting the time and effort put into orthodontic treatment!

How it works: The teens&#x27; orthodontics process Consultation At your teen&#x27;s orthodontic examination, Dr. Nova will assess how the teeth and jaws are developing to determine the right timing for treatment. This includes taking X-rays and photos to evaluate alignment, bite, and growth patterns. Dr. Nova will discuss any concerns, review treatment options if needed, and answer your questions—helping set the stage for a confident, healthy smile.

Placement and Fitting

Once it’s time to start treatment, Dr. Nova will carefully place and fit the braces or Invisalign to ensure optimal comfort and effective results. Our team will also share helpful tips to keep your teen’s braces or alginers in great shape throughout their smile journey.

Next Steps

Follow up appointments will be scheduled every 6-10 weeks so Dr. Nova can monitor your teen’s progress. Depending on your teen’s bite and alignment issues, treatment can last anywhere from six months to two years.
