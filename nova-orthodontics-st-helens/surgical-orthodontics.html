Schedule your Consultation Visit our practice Call (503) 642-5666 Do I Need Surgical Orthodontics? Surgical orthodontics may be necessary if you have a significant jaw alignment issue that can’t be fully corrected with braces or clear aligners alone. This includes conditions like severe overbites, underbites, open bites, crossbites, difficulty closing your lips comfortably, or facial asymmetry that impact not just the appearance of your smile, but also your ability to chew, speak, or breathe properly.

This approach combines orthodontic treatment with corrective jaw surgery to reposition the jaws into a more balanced, functional, and harmonious alignment. It’s often recommended for adults and older teens whose facial growth is complete and who need more than tooth movement to achieve their desired results.

During your consultation, we also discuss non-surgical alternatives. You’ll have the opportunity to understand all your options and decide whether non-surgical treatment alone can meet your goals or if surgical orthodontics will provide the best outcome for your smile and overall health.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the entire process take?

The entire process of surgical orthodontics typically takes 18-24 months. This includes about 6-18 months of pre-surgical orthodontics, the surgery itself, and 6-12 months of post-surgical orthodontics. However, every case is unique, and your treatment time may vary based on the complexity of your specific situation.

Are there age limits for jaw surgery?

There&#x27;s no upper age limit for jaw surgery, as long as you&#x27;re in good general health. For younger patients, we typically wait until jaw growth is complete before considering surgery. This usually means waiting until at least age 16 for girls and 18 for boys, but it can vary from person to person. During your consultation, we&#x27;ll evaluate your growth and development to determine the best timing for your treatment.

Benefits of Combined Orthodontics and Jaw Surgery

Improves Your Facial Balance Orthodontics in conjunction with jaw surgery can significantly change how your face looks. It creates better harmony between your facial features, which often leads to a more attractive appearance and boosts your confidence.

Proper Jaw Relationships and Improved Function By fixing jaw misalignment, surgical orthodontics can make it much easier for you to bite and chew. In some cases, improved breathing is achieved. Many patients find relief from chronic jaw pain and notice their overall oral health improves. Teeth wear down less when they are fitting together and functioning properly.

Enjoy Results That Last Combining orthodontic treatment with jaw surgery often leads to more stable, long-lasting results than braces alone, especially for severe cases. This approach fixes both teeth and jaw issues, improving how your mouth works and looks for years to come.

The Surgical Orthodontics Treatment Process Initial Consultation and Planning Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam, including X-rays and photos to help us design a personalized treatment plan. We’ll take time to understand your goals, explain the process in detail, and answer any questions you have. At this stage, we’ll also refer you to one of our trusted oral surgeons so we can align your care as a team.

Pre-Surgical Orthodontics

In this phase of treatment, we will use braces or aligners to align the teeth within each jaw in preparation for corrective jaw surgery. During this time, your bite will gradually change as part of the planned process. This preparation is key to ensuring that your teeth fit together correctly during and after your surgery.

The Surgery and Recovery

Once your teeth are in the correct position, you will be referred back to the oral surgeon to perform the surgical portion of your treatment. This is done with your braces still on your teeth. After your surgery is complete, the surgeon will monitor your healing for a few weeks and then they will send you back to us to make any finishing touches to get the smile and bite perfected.

Post-Surgical Orthodontics

In this the final phase of treatment, we make precise adjustments with braces or aligners to fine-tune your bite and ensure your teeth fit together perfectly. This step helps stabilize the results, enhance function, and complete your smile transformation.
