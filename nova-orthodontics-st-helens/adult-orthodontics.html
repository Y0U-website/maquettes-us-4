Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs that I may need adult orthodontic treatment?

Some common signs that you may benefit from adult orthodontics include:

- Teeth that don’t meet properly (overbite, underbite, crossbite)

- Shifting teeth or bite changes over time

- Worn or uneven teeth from misalignment

- A desire to improve your smile and confidence

Even if you’ve had braces in the past, changes in your bite or alignment as an adult can still make orthodontic treatment beneficial.

What are the benefits of adult orthodontics?

Adult orthodontics offers a range of benefits, from improving bite function and easing jaw discomfort to enhancing your smile and overall oral health. Straighter teeth are easier to clean, helping prevent cavities and gum disease, and can also protect existing dental work from uneven wear. With discreet options like clear aligners and ceramic braces, treatment is more comfortable and less noticeable—making it a smart, confidence-boosting investment at any age.

What causes my adult orthodontic problems?

Adult orthodontic issues can result from a variety of factors, including genetics, missing teeth, teeth grinding or clenching, and gum disease. Shifting teeth are also common with age or after not wearing retainers following earlier orthodontic treatment. These changes can affect your bite, tooth alignment, and overall oral health—making adult orthodontics an effective way to restore balance and function to your smile.

Is it too late for me to receive adult orthodontic treatment?

It is never too late for you to receive the adult orthodontic care you deserve. Today, one in four orthodontic patients is an adult. Untreated misaligned teeth may lead to other problems later in life such as difficulty cleaning teeth, gum disease, or difficulty chewing. While many patients look to orthodontic solutions for esthetic reasons, properly aligned teeth also offer functional improvements that support better dental health and long-term stability.

How much does adult orthodontics cost?

A multitude of factors come into play when determining the cost of your adult orthodontic treatment. The cost varies depending on the severity of your misalignment, as well as appointment visits, insurance type, and more. Our team recommends scheduling a consultation with your orthodontist to find out how much you’ll pay for your adult orthodontic treatment.

Types of orthodontics for adults

Invisalign Invisalign is a clear aligner system made from a durable patented plastic material called SmartTrack. Many adults opt for Invisalign as it is the most subtle, discreet way to align a beautiful smile over time.

Ceramic Braces While similar in function to traditional metal braces, ceramic braces are made up of tooth-colored ceramic material that matches the natural shade of your smile. Many adult patients choose ceramic braces as a less-noticeable alternative to metal braces.

Metal Braces Traditional metal braces are one of the most common and affordable options to correct mild to severe tooth and jaw misalignment. These braces are composed of durable, medical-grade stainless steel brackets and archwires to produce a natural, straight smile.

Retainers

Maintaining your investment is crucial, especially for our adult patients who have waited years for their ideal smile. We offer both digital scan retainers for maximum comfort and traditional impression options for same-day delivery when needed. Our adult patients particularly appreciate our flexible retainer options that accommodate busy professional schedules while ensuring long-term stability of their orthodontic results.

Airway Friendly Orthodontics

Orthodontic care can do so much more for you than just aligning your teeth. Airway orthodontics can reshape your mouth to open up your airway to its ideal state for better breathing and more restful sleep.
