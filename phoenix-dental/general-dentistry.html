Root canal therapy

  • Persistent or severe tooth pain, especially when chewing or applying pressure
  • Prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold temperatures
  • Swelling or tenderness in nearby gums
  • Darkening or discoloration of a tooth
  • Pimple-like bumps on the gums (sign of infection)

What Is Root Canal Therapy?

  • Relieves pain caused by infection or decay
  • Preserves your natural tooth for long-term function
  • Prevents the need for extraction and more extensive dental work
  • Protects surrounding teeth and overall oral health
  • Modern techniques ensure a comfortable, minimally invasive experience

Crowns and bridges

What Are Crowns & Bridges?

Bridges: Fixed restorations that fill gaps left by one or more missing teeth, anchored to adjacent healthy teeth.

  • Reestablish natural bite and speech
  • Protect and strengthen weakened or damaged teeth
  • Maintain facial structure and prevent teeth shifting
  • Natural-looking, long-lasting results
  • Personalized fit and shade for a flawless smile

Tooth removal

  • Severe tooth decay or infection
  • Advanced gum disease affecting tooth stability
  • Trauma or injury to a tooth
  • Overcrowding or preparation for orthodontic treatment
  • Impacted or problematic wisdom teeth

What to Expect During Your Visit

  • Relief from pain and infection
  • Protection of adjacent teeth and gums
  • Prevention of further oral health complications
  • Foundation for restorative solutions like implants or bridges
