Dental Implants

A dental implant consists of a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone, topped with a custom crown. Over time, the implant fuses with the bone (osseointegration) creating a stable, permanent foundation that prevents bone loss and maintains your facial structure.

Common Questions

Are dental implants right for me?

Our team will evaluate your individual needs, oral health, and jawbone to determine if dental implants are the best solution for replacing missing teeth.

Does getting dental implants hurt?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. Palm City Dental Arts offers sedation and numbing options to help keep you comfortable throughout the implant procedure.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Treatment time varies based on your individual case. After the implant is placed, it typically needs several months to bond with the jawbone before the custom crown is attached. Dr. Pichardo will provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of dental implants depends on your individual treatment needs and the restoration required. Palm City Dental Arts accepts most insurance plans and offers flexible financing options. The team can provide a personalized cost estimate during your consultation.
