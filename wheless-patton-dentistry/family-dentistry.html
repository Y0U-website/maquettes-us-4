Why Choose Wheless & Patton Dentistry?

As a locally owned and family-operated practice serving Reidsville since 1979, Wheless & Patton Dentistry is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in a friendly environment. We treat every patient like family and offer a wide range of services under one roof for added convenience.

What Sets Us Apart

Our commitment to continuing education and modern dental technology allows us to provide effective, personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s needs.

  • Family-owned and operated since 1979
  • Comprehensive dental care for all ages
  • Same-day emergency services available
  • Pain-free dentistry techniques that prioritize comfort
  • Experienced, caring dental professionals
  • Clear aligners and invisible tray options
  • Accepting new patients
  • 5-year warranty on crowns, bridges, and implants
