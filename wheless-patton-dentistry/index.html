The practice

At Wheless & Patton Dentistry, we’re proud to care for smiles throughout Reidsville and the surrounding communities. When you visit our office, you’ll be treated like family with modern dental care, a gentle touch, and the kind of Southern hospitality that makes you feel right at home.

Dr. Wheless, Dr. Patton, and our entire team are committed to creating a comfortable, stress-free experience for patients of all ages. Whether you’re bringing your child in for their first checkup or visiting for your own routine cleaning, we’re here to ensure you feel relaxed, informed, and genuinely cared for.

Your time, comfort, and budget matter to us. We provide personalized recommendations, honest guidance, and flexible financing options to help make high-quality dental care accessible for your entire family.

We’re currently welcoming new patients and would love for you to join the Wheless & Patton Dentistry family. Contact us to schedule an appointment, and experience warm, dependable dental care right here in Reidsville.
