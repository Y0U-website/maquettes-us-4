Welcome to Plourde Family Dental We are a family dental office in Bath, Maine committed to providing high quality care with compassion, warmth, and humor.

The practice

Dr. Plourde is dedicated to providing comprehensive and quality dental care with a disarming demeanor . He graduated from Tufts University, where he earned a B.S. in Chemical and Biological Engineering and an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering. He went on to attend Tufts University School of Medicine and graduated as a Doctor of Dental Medicine in 2013.

Prior to Plourde Family Dental, Dr. Plourde practiced for seven years as an associate dentist in Portland, Maine. He was honored to take over the practice of retiring Dr. Tom Anglim in 2020, and is happy to serve the greater Bath community.

Our talented and knowledgeable team of dental professionals brings years of experience, and a deep commitment to ensuring your comfort.
