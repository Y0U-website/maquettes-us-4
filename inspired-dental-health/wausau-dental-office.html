About Us

What Sets Our Wausau Dental Office Apart?

We know what it’s like to sit in a dental chair, and we are committed to making every visit to our Wausau dental office a comfortable, stress-free experience. When you visit our practice, have peace of mind knowing our passion resides in your smile and ensuring you reach a state of optimum dental health. Have questions about your teeth? Ask us anything, and we will happily provide expert advice and guidance.

Inspired Dental Health is a general dental practice located in Wausau, Wisconsin. Our clinic specializes in comprehensive preventative dentistry, restorative services, surgical procedures, and cosmetic dentistry. We dedicate ourselves to treating every patient with diligent thought, preparation, respect, and concern for their health. Meet Your Dentists

Skilled and Compassionate Professionals Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. Tessa Laber

Background

Dr. Laber was raised in northwest Wisconsin. She received her undergraduate degree in Biology from the University of Wisconsin-Eau Claire in 2008. She then attended the University of Michigan School of Dentistry and received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2012. After graduating from dental school, Dr. Laber practiced in the Wausau area for eight years. Dr. Laber is a member of the American Dental Association, the Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Portage County Dental Association.

Fun Facts

When she is not at the office, Dr. Laber likes to spend time at the lake with her husband, Tyler, and daughter, Lucy. She also enjoys traveling, cooking, trying new recipes, staying active outdoors, and gardening.

Dr. Wenhui Sun

Background

Dr. Wenhui Sun moved to Wausau from the Washington D.C. area. Dr. Sun completed a seven-year combined dental program through the University of Connecticut and graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Public Health and Doctorate of Dental Medicine degree.

After dental school, Dr. Sun pursued a comprehensive general dentistry residency at the Summa Center for Dental Health in Akron, Ohio. She then went on to join a private practice in the D.C. area before coming back to the Midwest.

Dental Style

Dr. Sun emphasizes comprehensive preventive care and works with each of her patients to improve their oral health. She enjoys learning and is always striving to stay up to date with continuing education courses each year. For the past few years, she has focused on learning more about pediatrics, sleep/airway dentistry, TMJ disorders and esthetic-focused restorative procedures.

Fun Facts

After having lived and worked in big cities for many years, Dr. Sun, her husband AJ, and their two dogs are all thrilled to be living in Wausau. They enjoy curling, traveling, and trying new foods.

Dr. Jessica Mepham

Background

Jessica Mepham earned her Degree in Dental Surgery in 2015 from University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, Tennessee. She completed a one-year residency following graduation and practiced in Memphis for 5 years before moving to Central Wisconsin.

Dental Style

“The most rewarding aspects of dentistry are helping patients overcome their dental anxiety and improving their confidence levels by creating beautiful smiles.”

Fun Facts

Dr. Jess loves spending quality time with her family of five. Her hobbies include drawing, jump roping, reading and spending time outdoors.
