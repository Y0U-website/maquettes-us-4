About Quality Dental School of Technology

Career training in East Orange, New Jersey since 1998.

Our History Quality Dental School of Technology was established in 1998 to provide adult learners in the East Orange, New Jersey area with focused, practical career training in the dental and healthcare fields. For more than two decades, the school has helped students develop the technical knowledge, professional habits, and hands-on skills needed to pursue entry-level opportunities in dental assisting, dental laboratory technology, and related healthcare support roles.

The school was founded on the belief that career training should be accessible, practical, and directly connected to the skills employers expect. That commitment continues to guide the programs, instruction, and admissions process today.

Our Mission

To provide adult learners with focused, hands-on career training that develops the technical skills, professional habits, and confidence needed to pursue entry-level opportunities in the dental and healthcare fields.

Practical Instruction Training is built around real procedures, equipment, and professional responsibilities — not abstract theory alone.

Accessible Education Programs are designed for adult learners, including those returning to school after time away from formal education.

Honest Guidance Admissions staff help prospective students understand programs, requirements, and realistic expectations before enrolling.

Community Commitment Located in East Orange, New Jersey, the school is committed to serving the local community and surrounding region.

What We Teach Quality Dental School of Technology offers career training programs in Dental Assisting, Dental Laboratory Technology, and Certified Nursing Assistant preparation. Each program is designed to give students practical, hands-on experience with the tools, materials, and procedures relevant to their chosen field.

Program availability, schedules, and course content may change. Contact Admissions for the current catalog and enrollment information.

Instruction and Learning Environment Students at Quality Dental School of Technology learn through a combination of classroom instruction and hands-on practice. Instructors guide students through procedures, answer questions, and help develop the accuracy, discipline, and professional habits that entry-level positions require.

The school maintains a focused learning environment designed for adult students who are serious about beginning a new professional direction. Class sizes are kept manageable to allow for individual attention and adequate practice time.
