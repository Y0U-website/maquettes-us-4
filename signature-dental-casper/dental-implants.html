Are Implants Worth the Investment?

While traditional options like bridges or dentures might seem more affordable initially, dental implants often prove more cost-effective over time. Unlike temporary solutions that may need frequent replacement or repair, implants can last a lifetime with proper care. Consider them an investment in your long-term oral health and quality of life.

The Dental Implant Process Your journey to a restored smile involves several carefully planned steps:

Initial Consultation

Meet with Dr. Milmont or Dr. Gallup to discuss your goals and evaluate your oral health. They’ll create a personalized treatment plan and review financing options.

Implant Placement

Using precise computer-guided technology, we carefully place the implant in your jawbone. This outpatient procedure is performed under local anesthesia for your comfort.

Healing Period

Over 3-6 months, your implant naturally bonds with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This bonding helps protect and strengthen your jawbone throughout your lifetime. During this healing period, you’ll wear a temporary crown so you can continue eating and smiling.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach your custom-crafted crown, creating a beautiful, natural-looking tooth that functions just like your original.
