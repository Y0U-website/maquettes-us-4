Improved Facial Appearance with Dental Implants

Dental implants do more than replace missing teeth for improved function and aesthetic quality. Because a dental implant supports and preserves the bone tissue within the jaw, it can also help to maintain facial contours and the shape of your mouth as a whole.

A patient whose bone tissue has deteriorated over time will experience a noticeably diminished jawline, a common problem that leads to a sunken appearance. With dental implant services at Los Gatos Dentistry, Dr. Phan can help you maintain not only your smile and your bite but also the shapely contours of your face.
