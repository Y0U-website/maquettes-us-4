What is Ozone Therapy?

Ozone (O₃) is a form of oxygen that has been used in medicine for decades—especially in Europe—for its remarkable ability to destroy harmful microorganisms while supporting healthy tissue. In recent years, more U.S. dental offices have begun incorporating ozone because of its safety record and impressive results.

How it Works

Ozone targets pathogens by disrupting their cell walls, causing them to break apart and die—without harming your healthy cells. In dentistry, this means we can:

Eliminate bacteria, viruses, and fungi at the source of infection Promote faster wound healing and bone regeneration after dental procedures Support the body’s natural ability to restore balance in the mouth

Preventive Benefits

Ozone Therapy isn’t just for treating infections—it can also help prevent problems before they start. Ozone can remineralize and harden tooth enamel, making it especially helpful for patients with sensitive teeth or early signs of decay.

Why We Use It

As a biological dental office, we seek the most conservative, effective solutions for your oral health. Ozone Therapy allows us to treat infections without relying solely on chemicals or antibiotics, while also encouraging your body’s own healing response.
