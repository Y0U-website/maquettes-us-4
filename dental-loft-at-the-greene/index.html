The practice

Dr. Bui was born and raised in Dayton, Ohio. She always loved the dentist growing up, but she didn’t consider a career in dentistry until she was in middle school and her hygienist asked her about becoming a dentist. Having orthodontics (twice!) and one jaw surgery only served to further cement her dreams.

After receiving her Bachelor’s degree from Wright State University, she went on to complete her dental education at The Ohio State University. For nearly a decade now, she’s been proud and honored to deliver care to the community where she grew up. She is a member of the ADA, ODA, and Dayton Dental Society.

Dr. Bui’s approach to dentistry has always involved communication, education, and a strong dose of humor. Her philosophy is that for a patient to understand and truly take part in the comprehensive approach to their oral health needs, she wants to help them understand the process and trust their health care providers.

She feels very fortunate to come to work everyday with a group of people she considers her work family and realizes that it takes a team to make a great experience for any patient.

When she’s not at the office, Dr. Bui enjoys spending time with her son and husband doing anything outdoors and visiting Costco. You can regularly find her at her son’s soccer, tennis, piano or swim lessons. As a family, they enjoy cheering on the Buckeyes and the Bengals as well as traveling!
