A New Location, Same Exceptional Care

We’re excited to welcome you to our new location in the Lynnwood area, conveniently located near Alderwood Mall.

We opened this location with our patients in mind—to make it easier for those who travel from different areas of Snohomish County to access the same compassionate, personalized dental care they have come to expect from DDS Dental.

Our Lynnwood-area office offers a more intimate and relaxed environment , allowing us to spend the time getting to know you, listening to your concerns, and making sure you feel comfortable throughout your visit. Whether you are looking for a new dental home, need emergency care, or simply want a more personal approach to dentistry, our team is here for you.

This new location allows us to expand our ability to serve patients throughout the area while maintaining what matters most to us: exceptional care, patient education, comfort, and treating every patient as an individual.

More convenience. A more intimate setting. The same compassionate DDS Dental care.

We look forward to welcoming you to our new Lynnwood-area location.
