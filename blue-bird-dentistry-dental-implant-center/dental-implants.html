Why Choose Dental Implants?

Look and Feel Natural

Modern dental implants are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth. We carefully design each restoration to match:

Most people won’t know you have implants unless you tell them!

  • The color of your existing teeth

  • Your facial features and smile line
  • Your bite and jaw alignment

Restore Full Function

Unlike dentures that reduce your chewing power by up to 70 %, dental implants restore nearly 100 % of your natural bite force. This means:

  • Eat all your favorite foods without worry
  • No dietary restrictions
  • Bite into apples, steak, corn on the cob
  • Speak clearly without slipping dentures
  • Laugh and smile with confidence

Prevent Bone Loss

When you lose a tooth, you also lose the bone stimulation that root provides. Without stimulation, your jawbone begins to deteriorate—a process called resorption. This causes:

Dental implants stimulate your jawbone just like natural tooth roots, preventing bone loss and preserving your facial structure.

  • Sunken facial appearance (premature aging)
  • Shifting of remaining teeth
  • Difficulty fitting dentures over time
  • Weakening of the jaw

Protect Your Other Teeth

Traditional bridges require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth to serve as supports. This permanently damages those teeth and makes them more vulnerable to decay and breakage.

  • Stand independently

The Dental Implant Process: Step by Step

Understanding what to expect can help you feel confident and prepared for your implant journey.

Step 1: Complimentary Consultation

Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we’ll:

No Pressure: This consultation is about education and exploration. We want you to feel informed and comfortable with your decision.

  • Examine your teeth, gums, and bite
  • Take digital X-rays to assess bone density
  • Use 3D cone beam CT imaging for precise planning
  • Check for any issues that need addressing first
  • What teeth need replacement?
  • What are your aesthetic expectations?
  • What’s your budget and timeline?
  • Do you have any concerns or questions?
  • Single implants vs. multiple implants
  • Immediate placement vs. delayed placement
  • Bone grafting needs (if any)
  • Sedation preferences
  • Detailed timeline
  • Step-by-step procedure explanation
  • Transparent cost breakdown
  • Financing options

Step 2: Preparatory Treatments (If Needed)

Some patients need preparatory work before implant placement:

Tooth Extraction: If the damaged tooth is still present, we’ll perform a gentle extraction . In many cases, we can place the implant immediately after extraction, reducing overall treatment time.

Bone Grafting: If you’ve had missing teeth for a while, you may have experienced bone loss. Bone grafting rebuilds the foundation needed to support implants. The process involves:

Gum Treatment : Gum disease must be treated before implant placement to ensure successful integration and long-term health.

  • Placing bone graft material where bone has deteriorated
  • Allowing 3-6 months for new bone to form
  • Creating adequate bone density for stable implant placement

Step 3: Implant Placement Surgery

The day of your implant surgery, here’s what happens:

  • Local anesthesia ensures complete numbness
  • Sedation options available for relaxation
  • Most patients report minimal discomfort
  • Small incision in the gum tissue
