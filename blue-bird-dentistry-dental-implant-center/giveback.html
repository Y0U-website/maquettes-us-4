How All-On-4 Differs from Traditional Implants

Traditional full-mouth dental implant restoration typically requires 6-8 implants per arch and often involves bone grafting procedures, which can add months to the treatment timeline. All-On-4, by contrast:

  • Uses only four implants per arch
  • Rarely requires bone grafting
  • Allows for immediate function (same-day teeth in many cases)
  • Costs significantly less than traditional full-arch implants
  • Reduces overall treatment time from months to just one day

Surgery Day - Your New Smile in One Day

For many patients, All-On-4 is truly a same-day procedure. Here’s what to expect:

Morning: Any remaining problematic teeth are removed, and the four implants are precisely placed in your jawbone using advanced surgical techniques.

Afternoon: A custom-designed temporary prosthesis is attached to your new implants, giving you a fully functional set of teeth before you leave our office.

We offer comfortable sedation options to ensure you’re relaxed throughout the procedure.

Healing and Your Permanent Restoration

Over the next 3-6 months, your implants will fuse with your jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration. During this time, you’ll continue to wear your temporary teeth, which look and function remarkably well.

Once healing is complete, we’ll replace your temporary restoration with your final, permanent prosthesis—crafted from high-quality materials for exceptional durability and a natural appearance.

Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants

Immediate Results

Walk in with missing or failing teeth, walk out the same day with a beautiful, functional smile. This immediate transformation has profound effects on patients’ confidence and quality of life.

Eat What You Love

Unlike traditional dentures, All-On-4 implants are anchored securely in your jaw. Enjoy steak, apples, corn on the cob, and all your favorite foods without worry. No more dietary restrictions or embarrassing slippage.

Preserve Your Facial Structure

Missing teeth lead to bone loss, which causes the sunken, aged appearance many denture wearers experience. All-On-4 implants stimulate your jawbone just like natural teeth, preserving your facial contours and preventing that collapsed look.

Long-Term Investment

With proper care, All-On-4 implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime. When you calculate cost over time, they’re often more economical than constantly replacing failing dentures or bridges. We offer flexible financing options to make your new smile affordable.

Easy Maintenance

Care for your All-On-4 restoration just like natural teeth—brush twice daily, floss, and visit us for regular cleanings. No messy adhesives, no soaking in solution overnight, no special cleaning tablets.
