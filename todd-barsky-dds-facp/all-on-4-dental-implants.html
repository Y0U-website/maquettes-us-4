Who is a Candidate for an All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 Implant Procedures are a great option for most patients. They virtually eliminate* the need for bone grafting which decreases the length of time you spend in recovery. Patients that cannot or do not want to undergo bone grafting, but want fixed, permanent replacement teeth are the first potential candidates, as all-on-4 dental implants virtually eliminate the need for bone grafting.

All-on-4 dental implants are also a good option for individuals who want a change from broken, missing, or damaged teeth, or removable dentures, to fixed, permanent replacement teeth, but do not want to or cannot undergo a full dental implant replacement procedure.

Finally, all-on-4 dental implants are a good option for patients that do not have the time to schedule the multiple visits that other dental implants take, and want the procedure to be completed as quickly as possible.

An All-on-4 Implant Procedure is ideal for our patients who want a change from unhealthy teeth or wearing a removable prosthesis, to fixed, beautiful teeth in just one day. The non-removable denture option can be completed with as few as 4 implants, and no bone grafting.

The Science Behind All-on-4 Dental Implants

Because your dental implants are extremely durable and naturally strengthen your jaws, as few as 4 implants are needed to support your non-removable denture. Our surgeon strategically places each of the 4 implants in an area that compliments the strength of your natural bone. 3D Scanning technology is used to place the implants in a way that is extremely efficient and accurate.

Once the implants are emplaced, your jaw will begin to form new bone around each of the artificial titanium roots. This enables the implant to support more weight than a natural, individual tooth. The doctor is present alongside the surgeon throughout the entire procedure. After the implants are installed, the doctor places your full arch prosthesis over the implants on the very same day.
