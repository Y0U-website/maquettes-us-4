As we begin to see more news about the COVID-19 (Corona virus), South Everett Cascade Dentistry would like to inform you about what we are doing to protect the health and safety…

The practice

We are doing all we can to keep our patients healthy. We ask if you:

1) have a fever or symptoms of lower respiratory illness (e.g., cough, shortness of breath).

2) have had close contact with a confirmed COVID-19 patient within 14 days of symptoms.

3) have traveled to/from affected geographic areas (overseas, high risk areas, or on a cruise ship) within 14 days of symptoms.

Appointment(s) will need to be rescheduled to at least 2 weeks from the last travel date and/or when symptoms resolve.

Your health and well-being are of great importance to us and we promise to do everything in our power to keep you safe.
