Dr. Delina Parkhurst

Dr. Parkhurst’s passion for dentistry began in high school over 25 years ago when she trained to be a dental assistant. The experience drove her to pursue and education and a career in Dental Hygiene, which she practiced for 10 years while working toward her ultimate goal of becoming a dentist. Dr. Parkhurst has a passion for the art and science of dentistry, with an ultimate goal of building professional long lasting relationships with patients based on trust and honesty. Her goal is to provide you with the best comprehensive care possible.

When not practicing her passion; Dr. Parkhurst lives in New Jersey with her spouse, daughter, and their dogs Ginger and Cinnamon. Her hobbies include spending time with her family, sightseeing, hiking, and various arts and craft projects.
