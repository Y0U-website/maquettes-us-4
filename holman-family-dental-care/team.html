Meet the Team

heidi cook, D.M.D.

Born and raised in the Boston area, Dr. Cook has always had a love for science and art. She pursued her interests in biology and art at college earning a Bachelor of Science degree from St. Lawrence University in upstate New York. A desire to serve people along with her background in science led her to a career in dentistry. She earned her D.M.D. at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston.

After dental school, she stayed in New England where she began her career in private practice. In 2009 she and her family relocated to Chapel Hill. Upon moving to North Carolina, she continued her dental work in a community health center as an Assistant Dental Director and as adjunct faculty at UNC Adams School of Dentistry. She is a member of the North Carolina Dental Society and American Dental Association.

Outside of the office she serves on the Board of Directors for multiple local non-profit organizations and is a volunteer service dog trainer. She enjoys spending time with her husband and two daughters, hiking, working in the garden, cooking, and supporting local food.

Rachel Ungaro, D.D.S.

While originally from Rochester, NY, Dr. Ungaro has been glad to claim the triangle area as home since early childhood. Growing up, her love for anything active and disposition toward adrenaline culminated in her first passion - skateboarding. Working as an instructor at the local skatepark, Sk8-Cary, provided opportunities in coaching children as well as gaining appreciation for people from diverse backgrounds. It became her goal to be invested in community regardless of demographics, reputations, or stereotypes.

Dentistry showed itself as a means to serve people while also developing long-lasting relationships, bringing this desire to fruition. She attended UNC-CH for both her undergraduate and dental training, earning a B.S. in Biology and her D.D.S. with honors. In dental school, Dr. Ungaro was actively involved in the Student Health Action Coalition as well as the Global Girls Empowerment Foundation, allowing her to provide care to those in need both locally and abroad.

She sought advanced training in general dentistry at the Prisma Health Richland Hospital in Columbia, SC. While there, she gained experience in the treatment of medically complex patients, hospital dentistry, surgical procedures, and conscious moderate sedation. Dr. Ungaro is honored to now be serving the community where she first fell in love with dentistry.

Reina Ransom, DDS

Dr. Reina Ransom is a dedicated dental professional who grew up in Stafford, Virginia. She earned her undergraduate degree from Hampton University and went on to receive her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of North Carolina Adams School of Dentistry. Throughout her life, Dr. Ransom has had a passion for helping and encouraging others. She makes it her mission to be an active member of every community she joins. This sense of purpose has inspired her to pursue several leadership roles in both dentistry and civic life.

Known for her warm, engaging approach, Dr. Ransom is deeply committed to patient education and empowering individuals to take control of their oral health. She treats patients of all ages and has extensive experience working with young children. She looks forward to serving you and your entire family.

Outside of the office, you can find her binge-watching The Real Housewives, traveling with family and friends, listening to Beyoncé, or exploring the Triangle’s best food spots.

Shaina Holman, D.D.S., Ph.D., FAGD, D. ABDSM

Dr. Holman graduated from East Chapel Hill High School where she also met her husband. Her passion for science, teaching, and art was on clear display early in her life. During high school she was able to do research at Duke University, resulting in her first publication in a peer-reviewed journal! Since then, she’s been fortunate to publish over a dozen peer-reviewed research articles.

She went on to graduate with distinction from Duke University with a degree in Biological Anthropology and Anatomy. Her passion for dentistry began during college while doing a project for her Ethnobiology class in Costa Rica. She saw how dentistry combined her love for people and her love of science, research, art, and community impact.

Dr. Holman loves giving back to the dental community and currently serves as the North Carolina State Area 2A Coordinator for her sorority chapter, Eta Phi Zeta in Chapel Hill, part of Zeta Phi Beta Sorority Inc. She is a member and past-president of the Durham Orange County Dental Society, a member of the American Association for Dental Sleep Medicine, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the American Dental Association. She also serves as Historian for Jack and Jill of America, Inc., Greater Chapel Hill Chapter.

She is married to her high school sweetheart, Trevor Holman. They live in Chapel Hill with their three children.
