Guided Biofilm Therapy

Guided Biofilm Therapy (GBT) is a modern, minimally invasive approach to dental cleanings that gently removes plaque and biofilm using warm water and advanced airflow technology. You&#x27;ll love how comfortable it feels, with warm water and zero scraping, while clinicians love how precisely and effectively it supports long-term oral health.
