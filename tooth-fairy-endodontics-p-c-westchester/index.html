The practice

Your comfort and peace of mind are our top priority and we pride ourselves on the level of care and attention we give to your teeth.

Dr. Yang was born in White Plains, New York and grew up surrounded by dentistry as both her parents graduated from NYU College of Dentistry—her mom a general dentist and her father a prosthodontist. Her parents’ dental office was built as an extension of their family home, and she watched them deliver quality care to their friends, family, teachers and neighbors until they retired.

To Dr. Yang, a dental office was more than a place where teeth got fixed, it was where loved ones and community came for help and welcomed as guests into their own home.

Dr. Yang is a graduate of Boston University and Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine , where she received her DMD and her MSD and CAGS for her post-doctoral endodontic training.

Dr. Yang is trained in the most current endodontic technologies, including microscope endodontics. She is dedicated to providing the highest quality, attentive, and gentle care to all her patients.

In her free time she enjoys exploring new gluten-free restaurants, spoiling her mini schnauzer(Rocky) and spending time with her friends and family.
