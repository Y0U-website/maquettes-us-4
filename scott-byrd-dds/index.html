X-Rays are an effective preventive and diagnostic tool that provide our team with important information not visible during a visual dental exam. Our team will utilize this important information to safely and accurately detect any dental abnormalities and then create a treatment plan.

Sealants are an efficient method for preventing decay on the biting surfaces of teeth. They are primarily placed on the permanent molars as they erupt in a child’s mouth.

If you have a tooth that is decaying or damaged beyond repair, we can perform quick, hassle-free extractions.

Teeth can be subject to decay and cracks. When this occurs, a dental filling is needed to restore the tooth. During a filling appointment the decay will be removed and replaced with either amalgam or composite material.
