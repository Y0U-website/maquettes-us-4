How It Works

This procedure encompasses the extraction of any remaining teeth, the placement of 4-6 implants per arch, and the attachment of a low-profile bridge to these implants. Typically, the implants are strategically positioned at specific angles to avoid the patient’s sinuses, thereby eliminating the necessity for costly bone grafts and protracted healing periods.

Your doctor will embed between four and six implants per arch, and during the integration of implants with your jawbones and the healing of your mouth, temporary fixed bridges will be installed. Once healing is complete, your doctor and our in-house lab technician will make your final teeth in either zirconia or acrylic, depending on the package you choose.

Am I a Candidate?

Most patients, even those previously advised of insufficient bone for dental implants, are eligible for this procedure. To navigate around the sinuses and leverage the sturdiest bone, your doctor will utilize a mix of straight and angled implants. This approach generally obviates the need for expensive and time-consuming sinus augmentation bone grafts. In your consultation ($99 deposit, credited toward treatment), your doctor will conduct a thorough evaluation to ascertain whether the All-On-X procedure, also recognized as Teeth in a Day, is a fitting option for you.
