The practice

Invisalign is a modern and discreet alternative to traditional braces, designed to straighten your teeth using a series of custom-made, clear aligners. These virtually invisible aligners gradually shift your teeth into their desired positions without the need for metal brackets or wires.

These treatments will enhance your natural beauty by smoothing fine lines and wrinkles, restoring volume, and boosting confidence. Do you have chronic pain, muscle discomfort or TMD? Dr. Pritchard treats these as well!
