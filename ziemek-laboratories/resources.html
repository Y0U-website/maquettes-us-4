Trios

  • Login to your 3shape Communicate account in a web browser.
  • Go to the “connections” section and select “Add Connection”.
  • Search for us by name “Ziemek Laboratories” or email: cadinfo@ziemekdentallab.com then press connect.

Medit Scanner

  • Login to your Meditlink.com account in a web browser.
  • Go to the “Partners” section.
  • From there go to the “Search for Partners” option and search “Ziemek Laboratories”. Send us a partnership request.

Sirona Primescan

  • Go to your Connect Case Center account and sign in.
  • Search for us by name, “Ziemek Laboratories” or email: cadinfo@ziemekdentallab.com .

E4D-Planmeca Planscan

  • Select Find a lab option on your scanner
  • Search for either Ziemek Labs or CADinfo@ziemekdentallab.com
  • Add Ziemek Labs
  • Select Ziemek Labs when submitting scans

Carestream

  • Search for and add Ziemek Laboratories .
