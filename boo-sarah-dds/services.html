Orthodontics

Orthodontics is devoted to building beautiful smiles by bringing teeth, lips, and jaws into proper alignment. Straight teeth function better, are easier to clean, and are more likely to last your lifetime. We can improve most tooth and jaw alignment problems at any age. There are orthodontic solutions for every lifestyle and budget. Fees for orthodontic care vary with the complexity of the treatment. Ask Dr Boo about anticipated treatment fees, payment options and insurance benefits.
