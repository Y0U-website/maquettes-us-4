SINCE 1961, THE BOO FAMILY OF DENTISTS HAS TREATED GENERATIONS OF FAMILIES RIGHT HERE IN WHITE BEAR LAKE.

Dr. Sarah Boo received a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota in 1989 and began her career by returning “home” to practice dentistry with her father.

“I love treating new families, but it always makes my day to see someone come in who we’ve treated for decades.”

Over the years, Dr. Boo’s also enhanced her private practice with an ongoing education in cosmetic dentistry. Her services are a combination of cosmetic and family dentistry, treating patients of all ages while using the most advanced and innovative equipment in the industry.

As someone who grew up here, practices here, and lives here, Dr. Boo’s deeply invested in this community. So if you’re looking to start a long-term relationship with a dentist who has your best interest (and smile) at heart, look no further.
