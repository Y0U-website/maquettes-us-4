Rootstown Dental Implants

  • If you’re approved for treatment, Dr. Yun will create a customized plan for your procedure using x-rays and images of your mouth.
  • During your implant placement procedure, your mouth will be numbed and a small titanium implant will be surgically placed into your jaw and gums. Then, the area will be cleaned and sutured shut.
  • It takes about 3-6 months for your dental implant to “osseointegrate” and permanently bond with your jaw bone.
  • Dr. Yun will create a dental crown that will fit over your implant, and restore the aesthetics of your smile.
  • Once your mouth has healed completely, Dr. Yun will bond your crown in place permanently to complete the procedure.

The Benefits of

Dental Implants

Dental implants never shift or move when you eat, speak or chew, which means they are much more stable than dentures. They also have a very long lifespan of 30+ years, and do not require any special care beyond brushing and flossing. Implants also look and feel totally natural, and help support the strength of your jaw after tooth loss.
