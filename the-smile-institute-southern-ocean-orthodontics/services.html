Bonding

Guess What?! Braces Don’t Hurt!

We don’t have to get you numb and you can eat as soon as you leave the office. This is all made possible by a process called bonding. Each tooth is thoroughly cleaned and dried before the brace is placed. A special orthodontic adhesive placed behind the brace will bond the brace to the tooth in as little as 20 seconds. Many patients don’t even realize we’ve put the braces on by the time we’re done. We often hear, That’s it?

So don’t be worried or scared the process is easy and the area under the brace is totally sealed and waterproof. The day the braces come off is also very easy and the enamel is unchanged and looks the same way it did the day the braces were placed.

Interceptive Orthodontics

Normal Bite

This 8-year old may benefit from orthodontics later. But, his jaw and tooth relationships and adequate space rule out the need for early treatment

Open Bite

Harmful habits such as tongue thrust and thumb sucking are best corrected early to prevent open bite and misshaped jaws.

Deep Bite

Early treatment of a deep bite can establish a more favorable facial height and prevent biting into the upper gums and excessive wear of front teeth.

Crowding

Timely treatment of crowding may involve preserving or regaining space, and guiding the eruption of permanent teeth into favorable positions.

Protruding Upper Teeth

This 8-year old has already fractured his protruded front teeth. Interceptive treatment can retract the front teeth and balance the size and shape of the jaw.

Cross Bite

(Lower teeth outside upper teeth). Orthopedic forces can often be used to correct a cross bite, establish proper function of the teeth, improve facial profile, and prevent the need for future surgery.

Early treatment will give your orthodontist the chance to:

  • Create a healthy arch form
  • Lower the risk of trauma to protruded front teeth
  • Correct harmful oral habits
  • Improve appearance
  • Guide permanent teeth into a more favorable position
  • Use a child’s natural growth to produce optimal results.

Some common problems 2-Part Treatment can address:

  • Crossbite or Underbite
  • Crowding , misplaced or blocked-out teeth
  • Protruding teeth
  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Jaws that shift, make sounds, protrude or are recessed
  • Finger sucking or other oral habits
  • Missing or extra teeth
  • Teeth that meet in an abnormal way or not at all
