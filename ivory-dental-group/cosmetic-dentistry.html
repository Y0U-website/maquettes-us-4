Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are the one of a kind smile make-overs of today. In just 2-3 visits they change the size, shape, position, and color of teeth. A thin layer of high-strength, esthetic porcelain, called a veneer is bonded to the front surface of the teeth. The porcelain’s translucent nature perfectly resembles the natural beauty of teeth, providing absolutely gorgeous results.

Porcelain (Ceramic) Crowns

Similar to veneers, porcelain crowns drastically improve the appearance of teeth. Porcelain Dental Crowns cover the entire tooth and do more than aesthetically enhance a tooth’s appearance. By covering the tooth with high-strength porcelain, crowns will strengthen and restore a tooth. Because every smile is unique, sometimes the most effective course of action is to use a combination of porcelain veneers and crowns.

Dental Ceramic Bridge

A Porcelain Bridge can also be used to replace missing teeth, both front and back, because of its high quality and strength. Porcelain bridges yield incredible results and restore the natural beauty of your smile.
