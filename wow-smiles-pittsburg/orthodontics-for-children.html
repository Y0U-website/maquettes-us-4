What are signs to look for to know my child’s bite isn’t right?

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Breathing through the mouth
  • Thumb-sucking
  • Crowded, misplaced, or blocked-out teeth
  • Jaws that are too far forward or back
  • Biting the cheek or into the roof of the mouth

What are the Benefits of Early Screening?

  • Avoid Problems - Make space before the teeth arrive and damage adjacent permanent teeth or become impacted
  • Fewer extractions, less need for jaw surgery by making space for all the teeth, guiding eruption, and harmonizing the growth of the jaws
  • Improved self-esteem for a better smile at an earlier age
  • Improved nasal airway
