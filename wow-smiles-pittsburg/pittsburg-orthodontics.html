WHY IS ORTHODONTIC TREATMENT IMPORTANT?

A person’s smile is one of the first things you notice from someone so having a BIG BEAUTIFUL SMILE helps improve your confidence! Straight teeth contribute to better oral health and bite function. A bad bite can wear down teeth, making chewing and talking difficult, and create stress on supporting bone and gum tissue. Proper alignment prevents future dental problems.

WHY CHOOSE AN ORTHODONTIC SPECIALIST?

At WOW! Smiles, our doctors are Orthodontic Specialists. What does this mean for you? And, why should you see an Orthodontist for braces or Invisalign rather than a Dentist? Orthodontists are dental specialists who straighten teeth, align the jaw, and improve the function of smiles. Orthodontists receive specialized training and 2-3 years of education beyond dental school, to become experts at diagnosing, preventing and treating dental and facial irregularities to create the smile of your dreams.

Only those who have completed an Orthodontic Residency may call themselves Orthodontists. They’re more qualified than a general dentist or non-specialist at creating smile transformations.

All WOW! Smiles doctors are Orthodontic Specialists.
