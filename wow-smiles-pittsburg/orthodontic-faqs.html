The American Association of Orthodontists (“AAO”) recommends that all children between the ages of 7 and 8 have an initial orthodontic screening. Early assessment helps identify and correct potential issues, especially those not yet visible, while jaws are still developing, and adult teeth are coming in. You should check in with the orthodontist around the time it feels like the tooth fairy is visiting almost weekly!

A person’s smile is one of the first things you notice from someone so having a big beautiful smile helps improve your confidence! Straight teeth contribute to better oral health and bite function. A bad bite can wear down teeth, making chewing and talking difficult, and create stress on supporting bone and gum tissue. Proper alignment prevents future dental problems.

Teeth, like the rest of your body, naturally move throughout life as we age. This natural shifting is why consistent retainer wear is so important after your braces. We always say, “Nighttime for a Lifetime,” or at least as long as you want your teeth to stay straight. You should think of your retainers like pajamas for your teeth!

Wisdom teeth can cause shifting after Orthodontic treatment. Most people don’t have room in their mouths to hold these additional four teeth.
