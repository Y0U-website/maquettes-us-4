MEET YOUR ORTHODONTISTS

Dr. Eric Wu

Dr. Eric Wu is an internationally recognized orthodontist, educator, and clear aligner innovator with more than 20 years of experience practicing in the Bay Area. A Silicon Valley native, Dr. Wu completed his dental training at prestigious University of Pennsylvania, his orthodontic residency at University of Pittsburgh, and advanced general dentistry training at University of California, Los Angeles.

Known for combining artistry with cutting-edge technology, Dr. Wu specializes in creating highly aesthetic smiles using the latest advancements in braces, digital orthodontics, and clear aligner therapy. He has been manufacturing custom orthodontic aligners in-office since 2014 and is a founding member of uLab, a pioneering orthodontic clear aligner company. Widely regarded as a leader in digital smile design and in-office aligner printing, he lectures internationally and teaches orthodontic specialists how to integrate modern technology into patient care.

Dr. Wu is also a certified Pitts Masters instructor and has mastered the progressive Pitts 21™ bracket system and protocols, helping patients achieve transformational, life- changing smile outcomes. In addition to serving as a consultant and key opinion leader for several orthodontic startup companies, he remains deeply involved in advancing the profession through education and mentorship. He has taught at multiple dental universities, including Roseman University of Health Sciences, University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, and University of Nevada, Las Vegas.

Having practiced in Palo Alto for many years, Dr. Wu has worked closely with the Stanford Airway Team and is passionate about the life-changing impact of early orthodontic airway treatment and its role in supporting healthy cognitive development in children.

When he is not practicing or teaching, Dr. Wu enjoys spending time with his two children, Misa and PJ. He is excited to bring state-of-the-art orthodontic care for patients of all ages to the Monterey Peninsula.
