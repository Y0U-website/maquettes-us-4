Website Accessibility

Bliss Dentistry is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience of all visitors to our website and have applied the relevant accessibility standards to ensure inclusivity. Our accessibility policy was approved and became effective October 6, 2020.

We have applied commercially reasonable efforts to comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards. If you encounter a page or section of our website that you cannot utilize due to a disability, please contact our accessibility coordinator at Modern Practice Consulting, LLC. Our accessibility coordinator can be reached at this email address: website@modernpractice.com . We will respond within two business days to all relevant communication.

Please contact us at the email address above if you have any questions or need additional assistance.
