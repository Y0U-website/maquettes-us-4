Dentures

Complete Dentures (Full Dentures) Complete dentures replace all teeth in the upper or lower arch, restoring your ability to smile, eat, and speak with confidence. Each set is custom-made to fit comfortably and look natural, using durable materials that mimic the look of real teeth and gums. Whether you’ve recently had extractions or are replacing an older set, our Tulsa team designs dentures that feel secure, balanced, and natural.

Implant-Supported Dentures (Overdentures) Implant-supported dentures, also called overdentures, snap securely onto a few strategically placed dental implants for enhanced comfort and stability. This option prevents slipping, improves chewing power, and helps maintain bone health for long-term comfort. You’ll enjoy a more confident smile that feels closer to natural teeth without the looseness or irritation of traditional dentures.

Immediate Dentures (Same-Day Dentures) Immediate dentures are placed on the same day as your extractions so you never have to go without teeth. They act as a temporary solution while your mouth heals, allowing you to smile confidently right away. Once healing is complete, we adjust or replace them with a permanent set for the best long-term comfort and appearance.

Partial Dentures Partial dentures are designed for patients missing several but not all of their natural teeth. They fill gaps comfortably while blending seamlessly with your remaining teeth for a full, natural-looking smile. We offer flexible and metal-framework options depending on your needs, helping restore both function and confidence.

Denture Repairs Over time, dentures may chip, crack, or loosen due to natural wear. Our office provides fast, affordable denture repair services to restore comfort and functionality often the same day. Whether you need a small fix or a full rebasing, we’ll help you get back to smiling quickly.

Denture Relines (Soft & Hard Relines) If your dentures no longer fit as snugly as before, a reline can restore comfort and stability. We offer both soft relines for added cushioning and hard relines for long-term support and durability. Relining refreshes the fit against your gums, reducing sore spots and improving retention without replacing your dentures entirely.
