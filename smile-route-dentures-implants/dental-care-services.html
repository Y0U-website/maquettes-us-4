Dental Care

Our Dental Care Services Dentures in Tulsa, OK Dentures are a reliable way to restore your smile and confidence after tooth loss. We offer both traditional dentures that rest comfortably on your gums and implant-supported overdentures for enhanced retention, strength, and comfort. Each set is custom-crafted for a secure, natural-looking fit that helps you eat, speak, and smile with ease. Whether you’re getting dentures for the first time or replacing an existing set, our Tulsa team ensures your new smile feels as good as it looks.

Dental Implants Dental implants are the most advanced solution for replacing missing teeth from a single tooth to a full arch. These small titanium posts act as artificial roots, securely supporting crowns, bridges, or dentures. They look, feel, and function just like natural teeth, preserving your bone health and smile aesthetics. At Smile Route Dentures & Implants ( formerly Smile Stop ) in Tulsa, we design every implant treatment for lasting comfort, confidence, and strength.

Extractions & Oral Surgery Tooth extractions and oral surgery are sometimes necessary to protect your oral health or prepare for dentures and dental implants. Our office provides gentle, precise extractions including wisdom teeth removal and pre-prosthetic extractions using advanced techniques to ensure comfort. With oral sedation and a caring touch, we make even complex procedures feel stress-free. Whether you need a single tooth removed or full-mouth extractions for dentures, your comfort and healing come first.

Restorative & Family Dentistry Restorative and family dentistry focuses on repairing damaged teeth and maintaining your long-term oral health. Our services include composite fillings, dental bonding, root canals, crowns, and bridges all designed to restore your smile’s strength and beauty. We combine modern materials with personalized care to create long-lasting, natural-looking results. From small cavities to full smile restorations, our Tulsa dental team is here for every member of your family.

Cosmetic Dentistry & Smile Makeovers Cosmetic dentistry helps transform your smile’s appearance with treatments like veneers, whitening, and dental bonding. Whether you want a subtle enhancement or a complete smile makeover, we customize each plan to fit your goals and budget. Using modern materials and technology, we help you achieve a brighter, more balanced smile that looks completely natural. It’s never too late to love your smile again.

Sedation & Comfort Dentistry We understand that visiting the dentist can cause anxiety that’s why we offer oral sedation and comfort-focused care to help you relax. Our gentle approach and calming environment make it easy to get the treatment you need without fear or stress. Sedation dentistry is ideal for extractions, implants, and patients with dental anxiety. You can sit back, breathe easy, and let our experienced team take care of your smile comfortably.

Emergency Dental Care Dental emergencies can happen anytime from sudden toothaches to broken teeth or denture problems. Our team provides prompt, compassionate care to relieve pain and protect your smile when you need it most. Whether it’s a chipped tooth, infection, or lost filling, we prioritize same-day treatment whenever possible. If you’re experiencing a dental emergency in Tulsa, we’re here to help you get comfortable fast.

Your Smile Deserves Thoughtful, Reliable Care Whether you are seeking relief from dental pain, considering dentures or implants, or looking to improve your smile’s appearance, we are here to support you with skill and compassion.
