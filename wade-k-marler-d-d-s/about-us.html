Wade Marler, DDS

Education and Continuing Education

I completed dental school at the Ohio State University, earning my Doctor of Dental Surgery. I am also a graduate of the Kois Center’s “Advancing Dentistry Through Science,” an intense program giving dentists the knowledge they need to be current and practice at an advanced level. Patients can know they are receiving high-quality care directed by the most current science.

Professional affiliations with the Washington State Dental Association and King County Dental Association also provide opportunities for continuing education, which I take advantage of throughout the year.

Outside the Office

I currently reside in Renton with my wife, Linda, and our five boys: Spencer, Kaleb, Brycen, Grant, and Emmett. They keep us busy with scouting and basketball, but we do love taking family vacations when we have the time.

Serving others is a priority even when I’m not in the office. I am active in my church and the in-house dentist for Vine Maple Place, a facility that educates and helps homeless, single parents become self-reliant.

Meet Our Team

At the office of Wade K. Marler, DDS, PS, our dentist and team offer a variety of useful services, helping our patients to get life-changing results. One of the services we proudly offer is a full-mouth reconstruction. This treatment has helped many of our patients drastically improve their quality of life.

Our team is like a family, with many of our team members being with us for years. We take pride in being a friendly, welcoming team that works as a cohesive unit. With years of experience, our dentist and team can help you reach your optimal level of oral health in an enjoyable environment. Schedule your appointment with Dr. Wade K. Marler at (253) 852-0480. We offer complete dentistry in Covington, Washington, and the surrounding areas.
