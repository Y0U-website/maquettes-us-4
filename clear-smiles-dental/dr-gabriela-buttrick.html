Dr. Gabriela Buttrick

Dr. Gabriela Buttrick is an organized, reliable, and detail-oriented dentist with extensive expertise in both General Dentistry and Periodontics. With advanced training through the University of Florida’s Advanced Education in General Dentistry program and an Immersive Fellowship in Periodontics from Texas A&M Baylor College of Dentistry, Dr. Gabriela Buttrick offers a comprehensive skill set aimed at delivering high-quality dental care.

Currently serving as an Adjunct Clinical Assistant Professor at the University of Florida in Hialeah, Dr. Gabriela Buttrick integrates academic teaching with clinical practice, mentoring dental students anI d contributing to developing advanced clinical curricula. This role complements ongoing practice as a General Dentist at Clear Smiles Dental where Dr. Gabriela Buttrick specializes in various services from implant planning and placement to restorative and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Gabriela Buttrick’s professional journey includes valuable experience at private practices in Hallandale, FL, and as a former AEGD Resident at the University of Florida. This diverse background is further enriched by hands-on roles as a Dental Assistant and Registered Dental Hygienist, demonstrating a commitment to patient care from multiple perspectives within the dental field.

Dr. Gabriela Buttrick is known for a patient-centered approach, addressing each individual’s concerns with empathy and a commitment to comprehensive care. Proficient in both English and Spanish, Dr. Gabriela Buttrick effectively communicates with a diverse patient base, ensuring that every patient receives personalized and empathetic care.

In addition to core competencies in comprehensive general dental care, Dr. Gabriela Buttrick excels in digital dentistry, including iTero, CEREC, and CBCT technologies, and offers expertise in prosthodontics, nitrous oxide administration, Invisalign, and botulinum toxin administration.
