Meet the Doctor

Dr. Evelyn Critchfield

Dr. Critchfield’s journey into dentistry is rooted in compassion, resilience, and a genuine love for people. Born in Cuba, she moved to the United States at just seven years old and grew up in Southwest Florida, where she learned the value of hard work, connection, and community. Fluent in both English and Spanish, she takes pride in making every patient feel seen, heard, and truly understood.

From an early age, Dr. Critchfield knew she wanted to make a difference in people’s lives. Dentistry became the perfect path allowing her to combine artistry, science, and empathy to transform not just smiles, but confidence and overall health. For her, it’s never just about teeth it’s about helping people feel comfortable, cared for, and proud of their smile again.

After graduating at the top of her class from both Florida Gulf Coast University and the University of Florida College of Dentistry, she returned home to Naples to serve the community that helped shape her. Over the past 10 years, she has dedicated herself to mastering both general and advanced dentistry, continually expanding her skills so she can provide comprehensive, high-level care in one place.

Three years ago, she took a leap of faith and became the owner of Serenity Smiles a practice built on her vision of what dentistry should feel like: warm, honest, gentle, and patient-focused. Every detail of the experience is designed with her patients in mind, from easing dental anxiety to delivering beautiful, lasting results.

Dr. Critchfield has advanced training in complex procedures including occlusal rehabilitation, prosthetic reconstruction, bone grafting, dental implants, and full-arch restorations like All-on-4 and All-on-6. But what truly sets her apart is her approach taking the time to listen, educate, and create personalized treatment plans that align with each patient’s needs and goals.

Outside the office, her greatest joy is her family. She loves spending time with her husband, daughters, and their dog, and together they enjoy traveling and exploring new places creating memories that mirror the same sense of care and connection she brings to her patients every day.

At the heart of it all, Dr. Critchfield’s mission is simple: to provide dentistry that feels different where patients feel safe, valued, and confident in their care.
