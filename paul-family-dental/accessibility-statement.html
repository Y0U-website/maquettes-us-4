Accessibility Statement

  • Accessibility Statement - Dr. Paul Family Dental

Paul Family Dental is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all patients and visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and 2.2, Level AA standards, which define requirements for improving accessibility for people with disabilities.

Accessibility Features

Our website strives to: • Support keyboard navigation • Provide readable text with sufficient color contrast • Include alternative text for images where appropriate • Maintain clear headings and page structure • Support screen reader technologies • Provide accessible forms and navigation elements

Ongoing Improvements

We recognize accessibility is an ongoing effort and are continually improving website accessibility as technologies evolve.

Feedback & Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website or need assistance, please contact us so we can provide the information or service you need through an accessible method.
