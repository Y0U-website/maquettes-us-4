Implant-Supported Bridges

  • Healing time of several months allows bone and gums to integrate with the implants.
  • Once healed, impressions are taken to craft a precise mold of your mouth.
  • A lab technician creates custom crowns matching your natural tooth color.
  • You may receive a temporary bridge during this fabrication period.
  • Finally, the permanent bridge is secured to the implants by either screws or dental cement.
