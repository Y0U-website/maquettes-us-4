The practice

Dr. Colleen Intatano is a general dentist practicing in Union, NJ. She received her doctoral degree from New York University, College of Dentistry and then completed her general practice residency at Peninsula Hospital Center in Queens, New York.

Dr. Intatano is well versed in all aspects of dentistry including endodontics, fixed & removable prosthodontics, restorative and cosmetic dentistry. She is also certified in Botox and Injectable fillers.

Dr Intatano is an active member of the Union County Dental Society, the New Jersey Dental Association, American Dental Association. Dr. Intatano treats each patient as a friend, each case as a personal challenge, and each happy smile as reward for her work.
