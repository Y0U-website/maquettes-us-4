What to Expect During Treatment

Understanding each step helps you feel prepared and informed from day one.

Comprehensive Consultation:

A detailed exam, 3D imaging, and photos guide an accurate diagnosis and confirm your candidacy for dental implants in Douglasville, GA. Your customized plan outlines the number of implants, restoration type, and an estimated timeline.

Digital Planning:

Advanced imaging and precise measurements help determine optimal implant positions for bite balance, bone support, and aesthetics. This careful planning promotes comfort and long-term stability.

Gentle Placement:

Local anesthesia helps keep you comfortable while the implant is placed with minimal disruption to surrounding tissues. Many patients return to routine activities soon after the appointment.

Healing and Integration:

Over the following weeks, the implant and bone fuse in a process called osseointegration. A temporary solution may be used during healing to maintain appearance and function.

Final Restoration:

A custom abutment and lifelike crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis are secured once healing is complete. Careful adjustments fine-tune your bite and comfort.

From the first scan to the final polish, Douglasville implants are delivered with attention to detail, comfort, and function. Clear home-care instructions and scheduled check-ins support a smooth recovery.
