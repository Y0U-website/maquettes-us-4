Meet Our Dentist in Douglasville, GA

Dr. Anya is a dentist and healthcare entrepreneur with broad clinical experience spanning cosmetic, implant, restorative, and comprehensive general dentistry. He is the owner of Douglasville Cosmetic & Implant Dental Studio, where his work emphasizes ethical decision-making, comprehensive treatment planning, and long-term outcomes that support whole-person health.

Dr. Anya earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Howard University College of Dentistry and strengthened his clinical foundation through work in high-volume, emergency, and multi-operatory practices across Georgia and the U.S. Virgin Islands. These environments refined his ability to manage complex cases, collaborate across disciplines, and deliver consistent care in fast-paced, diverse healthcare settings.

As a practice owner, Dr. Anya bridges frontline clinical care with operational execution, including workflow design, service standards, and patient experience. His leadership philosophy emphasizes accountability, clarity, and sustainability while maintaining access, safety, and dignity for every patient.
