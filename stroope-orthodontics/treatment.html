Types of Orthodontic Treatments

Let’s learn a little bit more about each of the orthodontic treatments we have available. If you’re still not sure what’s right for you, it’s no problem. Come on in and have a chat with Dr. Stroope . She’ll be able to point you in the right direction.

Invisalign® Clear Aligner Therapy

Invisalign was designed for people who wanted to straighten their teeth but didn’t want to deal with the appearance of braces. Some even call it the "clear choice" when it comes to orthodontic treatment. Invisalign is a type of aligner therapy , which means that instead of brackets and wires attached to your teeth it’s all about nearly invisible, totally removable trays.

Metal Braces

Metal braces have been around for centuries and for good reason. They can fix even the most complex orthodontic cases (like severe overbites or underbites), all while being the most cost-effective choice. Just because they’re a time-tested option doesn’t mean they’re old-fashioned: metal braces happen to sleeker and more comfortable to wear than ever before.

Clear Braces

If you’re all about braces technology but want something a little less noticeable, then clear braces could be right up your alley. They work the same way but the metal brackets are swapped for translucent ones that blend in right with your teeth.
