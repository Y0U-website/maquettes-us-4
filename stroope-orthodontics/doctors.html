About

Dr. Stroope is what we call bonafide. She’s the genuine article, certified to provide an array of treatments and orthodontic care options like Invisalign, temporary anchorage devices (TADS), SureSmile, Insignia, lingual braces, accelerated orthodontics with Propel, and laser treatments. She is a Board Certified Orthodontist, and she’s even extensively trained in the conservative treatment of temporomandibular joint dysfunctions.

Dr. Stroope practiced for a number of years around her hometown of Hot Springs before heading north. She couldn’t be more excited to be working on the smiles of Northwest Arkansas.

In her spare time, Dr. Stroope loves hiking, camping, snow skiing, making mosaic art, and of course, Razorback football. She also loves spending time with her husband, Gavin, her son Grayson, who was born 12/9/16, and her daughter Madeleine, who was born 8/16/18.
