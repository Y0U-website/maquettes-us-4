The Dental Arts of Bedford Implant Process

  • Step 1: Initial Consultation Our expert dentists will carefully examine your mouth and take x-rays of your head, jaw, and teeth to find out if dental implants are right for you. If they are, we’ll move right ahead to placement.
  • Step 2: Implant Placement To ensure your comfort, you will receive anesthesia before the surgical process begins. Once it kicks in, we will make an incision in your gums, place the dental implant, and then stitch the gums closed over the implant. This will take some time to heal (3-6 months) and even more time for the jawbone to fully integrate with the implant. The jawbone must heal while the implant secures itself firmly into the gum tissue.
  • Step 3: Implant Restoration Once the tissue heals and the bone has fused with the implant, we’ll ask you to come back for your second surgery. This time, we will create another incision in your gums to expose the implant. We will then attach an abutment to the dental implant and stitch the gums closed, leaving the abutment exposed. The abutment is the point of connection between the implant and your restoration. This will take about two weeks to heal.
  • Step 4: Artificial Tooth Attachment Eventually, when you have recovered, we’ll attach an artificial custom-made replacement tooth onto the abutment. After this is done, your new tooth will look just like a real one, your smile will be restored, you’ll gain maximum chewing capabilities, and you’ll finally be all done!

Immediate Dental Implants

Don’t want to wait so long for your dental implant? Nervous about how your smile with multiple missing teeth will look? No worries, we also offer immediate dental implants in Bedford, NH.

An immediate dental implant, or Teeth in a Day , is placed in the jawbone immediately after a single tooth (or teeth) extraction. It is usually placed in the same visit as the extraction but can be done after two weeks and still qualify as an immediate dental implant.

This safe and effective treatment for replacing missing teeth is a good option if you have enough natural bone and an implant secure enough to support immediate placement and pressure on the new tooth.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Bedford, NH

The result is a restoration that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth helping you eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

  • What is a dental implant, and how does it work? A dental implant is a titanium post that is surgically placed into your jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Over time, the implant naturally fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth. Once healed, a dental implant can support: Crowns for single-tooth replacement
  • Bridges for multiple missing teeth
  • Dentures for full-arch restoration
  • Who is a good candidate for dental implants? Good candidates for dental implants are individuals in generally good health who have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support an implant. During your initial consultation at Dental Arts of Bedford, we’ll evaluate your oral health using x-rays and digital imaging to determine whether implants are right for you. If needed, we may recommend bone grafting before proceeding with implants.
  • What’s the difference between regular and immediate dental implants? Regular dental implants involve a multi-stage process where healing time is required between implant placement and crown attachment. Immediate dental implants, also known as Teeth in a Day , are placed at the same time as tooth extraction or shortly after allowing for quicker tooth replacement. Immediate implants are ideal for patients with sufficient bone and healthy surrounding tissue.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The timeline for dental implants can vary depending on your treatment plan: Traditional Implants (3 6 months): Healing time is needed for the bone to fuse with the implant before attaching the abutment and artificial tooth.
  • Immediate Implants (same-day): The implant and a temporary restoration are placed in a single appointment, reducing the overall treatment time. Final restorations may still require a healing period for best results.
  • Is the dental implant procedure painful? The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during surgery. Some discomfort or swelling is expected post-surgery, but it can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers or medication prescribed by our dental team. Most patients report that the experience is much more comfortable than anticipated.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last 15 years to a lifetime. They are made from biocompatible materials that are designed to integrate with your body and resist decay. Good oral hygiene and avoiding smoking are essential for long-term success.
  • What is the recovery time after getting an implant? Initial recovery typically takes a few days to a week, depending on your individual healing ability and the complexity of the procedure. However, the full integration of the implant with the jawbone (osseointegration) takes 3 to 6 months. You’ll be given personalized aftercare instructions to help ensure a smooth healing process.
  • Are dental implants better than dentures or bridges? Dental implants offer several advantages over traditional dentures and bridges. They are permanent, don’t shift or move, help preserve jawbone density, and look and feel more natural. Unlike bridges, implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth for support, which helps maintain the health of neighboring teeth.
  • How much do dental implants cost in Bedford, NH? The cost of dental implants varies based on each patient’s unique needs, including the number of implants required and any preparatory procedures such as extractions or bone grafting. At Dental Arts of Bedford, we provide a clear, personalized quote during your initial consultation so you know exactly what to expect.
  • Can I get a dental implant the same day my tooth is removed? Yes! We offer immediate dental implants for qualifying patients. If you have sufficient bone density and no infection present, we can place an implant during the same appointment as your tooth extraction. This method speeds up the process, minimizes the number of surgeries, and helps restore your smile sooner.
