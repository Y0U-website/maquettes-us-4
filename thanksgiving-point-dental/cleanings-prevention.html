The Process of a Dental Cleaning

A dental cleaning is performed in conjunction with a dental exam to ensure your teeth and gums stay as healthy as possible. During a cleaning, our hygienists use specialized tools to remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing and flossing may miss. The cleaning process also helps prevent gum disease, cavities, and other dental issues.

What Happens During a Dental Cleaning?

Here’s what you can expect when you visit us for a dental cleaning:

Dental cleanings are important for everyone, but they are especially critical for individuals with a history of gum disease, cavities, or poor oral hygiene. Most people need a professional cleaning every six months, though some may need them more frequently, depending on their oral health.

  • Plaque and Tartar Removal – First, our hygienist uses an ultrasonic scaler to break up plaque and tartar buildup, which regular brushing and flossing cannot remove. The ultrasonic tool emits gentle vibrations that help loosen the buildup, followed by a scraping tool to remove it from the surfaces of your teeth.
  • Polishing— Once we have removed the tartar from your teeth, we will polish them with a gritty paste to help remove any remaining stains and leave them feeling smooth and fresh.
  • Flossing – After polishing, we floss between your teeth to ensure there’s no remaining plaque or debris. This step also allows the hygienist to check for any early signs of gum disease.
  • Fluoride Treatment – We may apply a fluoride treatment to strengthen your enamel and protect your teeth from decay. Fluoride is especially beneficial for patients at higher risk of cavities.
  • Dental Sealants – Dental sealants are a preventive treatment that can protect your teeth from cavities by sealing the grooves on the chewing surfaces. If you want to receive dental sealants during your cleaning, our team at Thanksgiving Point Dental can apply them to help safeguard your smile for years to come.

Receive an Oral Cancer Exam During Your Dental Cleaning and Exam

At Thanksgiving Point Dental, we believe in comprehensive care. In addition to your routine cleaning and exam, we also conduct an oral cancer exam as part of our standard procedure. Oral cancer screenings are crucial for detecting potential issues early when treatment is most effective.

An oral cancer exam involves a physical examination of the mouth, tongue, cheeks, gums, and throat to check for signs of cancer or precancerous conditions. If we find anything unusual, we may recommend further testing. These exams are quick and non-invasive, but they can be life-saving. Regular oral cancer screenings paired with your dental cleaning in Lehi give you the best chance of early detection and treatment.

Digital X-Rays and 3D Imaging Enhance Your Dental Cleanings and Exams

Technology plays a crucial role in providing the best possible care. At Thanksgiving Point Dental, we use state-of-the-art digital X-rays to get a detailed view of your teeth, gums, and underlying bone structure. These X-rays help us detect cavities, infections, and bone loss that may not be visible during the physical exam. Digital X-rays provide a safer, faster, and more detailed image than traditional X-rays, making them an invaluable tool for our team.

Additionally, we use advanced 3D dental imagery to enhance our diagnostic capabilities further. 3D imaging allows us to see your mouth in three dimensions, providing a more accurate view of complex dental structures, such as your jaw and teeth roots. This technology is beneficial for planning procedures like dental implants or for detecting issues that may not be apparent through regular X-rays. Together, digital X-rays and 3D imaging ensure we can provide the most effective care during your dental cleaning in Lehi.
