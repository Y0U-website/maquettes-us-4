The practice

When you visit Thanksgiving Point Dental, your smile and oral health is our top priority. Dr. Armstrong, Dr. Clark, and Dr. Stringham are dedicated to providing you with the affordable, personalized, and gentle care that you deserve. Thanksgiving Point Dental provide dental services that include dental exams, teeth cleaning, teeth whitening, dental implants, dentures, crowns, root canal therapy…

Dental implants are a great way to replace missing teeth and also provide a fixed solution to having removable partial or complete dentures. Implants provide excellent support and stability for these dental appliances.

Veneers are very thin pieces of durable, tooth shaped porcelain that are custom made (for shape and color) by a professional dental laboratory. They are bonded onto the front of teeth to create a beautiful and attractive smile.

Orthodontics is specifically concerned with diagnosing and treating tooth misalignment and irregularity in the jaw area. Initially, orthodontic treatments were geared toward the treatment of teens and pre-teens, but these days around 30 percent of orthodontic patients are adults.

Dr Stringham is an experienced and compassionate dentist who is committed to serving his patients with a gentle heart and gentle hands. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from the University of Southern California in 2006.

Dr Stringham did his undergraduate training at BYU and earned a degree in Human Physiology. He grew up in Montana where he learned to love the outdoors playing tennis and skiing. His wife Jane and their 4 children love to scuba dive and visit the ocean. His goal is to treat his patients like he would his own family to help them feel and look their best.

Dr. Clark grew up in the Salt Lake area and returned to Utah to be near family and the beautiful Wasatch Front. He has been married for 30 years, is a proud father of four, and recently became a grandparent.

He chose dentistry because it blends science, artistry, and meaningful personal connections—bringing deep fulfillment to his life. Dr. Clark is excited to be part of the Thanksgiving Point Dental team and is committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered care to the Lehi community and beyond.
