Dr. Dara Kim, DDS

My passion for dentistry stems from wanting to focus on providing high-quality oral care for the community. Seeing how transformative dentistry can be and the confidence it brings to my patients is truly rewarding. Developing a personal connection with patients is most important for me as each individual’s circumstance is unique. Outside dentistry, I enjoy spending time with family, traveling, and trying new experiences. We recently came to the Pacific Northwest and are thrilled to call this our home. We cannot wait to discover all that this area has to offer.

I graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Ohio State University. I then went on to attend the University of Southern California School of Dentistry, located in Los Angeles, California. Upon graduating, I relocated to New York to further her education through General Practice Residency at Wyckoff Heights Medical Center. During my residency, I practiced a wide range of dentistry, from oral surgery to pediatric dentistry.

I am a member of The Academy of General Dentistry and the American Dental Association. This allows me to continue to expand my knowledge and maintain the best treatment options available in dentistry today.

Oral health goes beyond just healthy teeth. I pride myself on not only educating my patients but taking the time to make them feel comfortable with the care they are receiving. I ensure my patients leave my chair feeling educated and comfortable and receive a full systemic check-up.

While attending the University of Southern California, I practiced in mobile clinics and health fairs to provide oral care for patients in under-served areas of Southern California. It felt amazing to combine my love of Dentistry with giving back to my local community. Being new to the area, I am eager to be involved and help bring excellent patient care to my new home.

Dr. Trang Dang, DDS

I always thrive to provide outstanding dental care for each individual patient, understanding their health, background and respecting their clear goals of treatment.

I earned my Bachelor of Dental Surgery from University of Medicine-Stomatology in Saigon, Vietnam in 2008, then moved to the United States and completed my Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Southern California in 2012.

After graduating I have been fortunate to enrich myself caring for very diverse patient populations, from working in various private practice settings in California and Washington, as well as being an instructor for a dental assistant program in Renton, WA, and assistant professor at Loma Linda University School of Dentistry, California.

In my spare time I enjoy cooking with my family and traveling abroad with my husband and two sons.
