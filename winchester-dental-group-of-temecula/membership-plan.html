We know you may have questions about how our membership plan works, so we’ve answered some of the most common inquiries below.

Can I use this membership plan if I already have dental insurance? No, our membership plan cannot be combined with traditional dental insurance. However, many of our members find that our plan offers better value than insurance, especially if their employer plan has high deductibles, low yearly maximums, or limited coverage for major procedures. If you’re comparing options, consider that our plan has no yearly maximum, no waiting periods, and includes preventive care at no additional cost beyond your membership fee.

What happens if I need to see a specialist for treatment? Your membership benefits apply to all services provided by our doctors at Winchester Dental Group in Temecula. If we refer you to an outside specialist (such as an oral surgeon or periodontist), your membership discounts do not apply to their services, and specialist fees are excluded from the plan. However, the 20 % discount does apply to many advanced procedures we perform in-house, including dental implants and full mouth restorations .

Is the membership fee refundable if I move or change dentists? Membership fees are non-refundable and non-transferable. Your membership is valid for 12 months from your enrollment date at Winchester Dental Group in Temecula. We encourage you to use all of your included preventive visits and take advantage of the treatment discounts throughout your membership year. If you’re unsure whether the plan is right for you, our team is happy to discuss your specific dental needs and help you determine the potential savings before you enroll by scheduling a consultation .
