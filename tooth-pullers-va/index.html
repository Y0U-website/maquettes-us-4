Extractions We safely and gently remove damaged, infected and painful teeth. We also are able to remove teeth for preparation for dentures.

IV moderate sedation We offer IV moderate conscious sedation to help patients feel calm, comfortable, and relaxed during dental treatment. A consultation appointment is required prior to scheduling dental extractions with IV sedation to ensure this option is appropriate for your needs. Patients receiving IV sedation must arrange for a designated driver on the day of treatment.

Nervous patients – Nitrous oxide For patients who feel anxious or nervous about dental treatment, we offer nitrous oxide or “laughing gas”. The effects wear off quickly making it a great option for patients that need to drive themselves to and from appointments.

Wisdom tooth extractions We provide safe and efficient removal of impacted or problematic wisdom teeth.
