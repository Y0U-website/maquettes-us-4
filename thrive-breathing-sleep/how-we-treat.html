Diagnostic Services

i-CAT Tek Scan Intraoral Scanners Videography and Photogrammetry i-CAT We use an i-CAT (cone beam CT) scan to obtain a state-of-the-art, low-radiation, 3D image of your airway, jaw joint and surrounding structures. This detailed imaging allows us to precisely identify any narrowing or obstruction in your airway, guiding our recommendations for treatment to ensure the root cause is addressed. All images are also reviewed for pathology by radiologists.

Tek Scan

By measuring bite pressures with incredible precision, the TekScan system improves diagnostic accuracy and treatment effectiveness. The TekScan can identify what occlusal asymmetry or imbalance is present at baseline, and it can track small changes as they occur throughout treatment. These data enable more accurate, targeted adjustments to orthotics, appliances, or your occlusion to improve comfort and function.

Intraoral Scanners

Our digital intraoral scanners replace traditional impressions with a fast, comfortable, and mess- free alternative. This technology creates highly accurate 3D models, allowing us to track changes in your arches and tooth positions over time ideal for monitoring growth and treatment progress.

Videography and Photogrammetry

We capture your posture on photo or in video or by photogrammetry (3D photography). We use principles from the Postural Restoration Institute to evaluate our work’s impact on your gait.

We document aspects of the connection between physical therapy and dentistry so that you can better understand what you experience.

Treatment

Airway Aligner Protocol

Our airway-focused clear aligner system combines modern orthodontic techniques with an awareness of how tooth movement influences breathing biomechanics. By optimizing tooth position and arch form, these clear aligners improve airway volume while straightening teeth esthetically improving your smile while improving respiratory function.

Myoaligners

Myoaligners are removable appliances that restore heavily worn or dysfunctional dental arches. They cover your teeth with a lifelike, 3D-printed shell designed to recapture the natural shape, vertical dimension, and function of your bite. By restoring your occlusion to a position that guides the jaws back into the optimal position, Myoaligners help improve overall stability and comfort.

These advanced appliances also encourage better muscle coordination, supporting a more balanced oral environment. Whether dealing with significant wear or other functional challenges, Myoaligners provide a noninvasive way to improve bite alignment, reduce strain on the jaw joints, and promote long-term oral wellness.

Myoaligners are FDA-approved for treatment of chronic migraines.

Postural Restoration Institute Splints (PRI splints, the Postural Restoration Institute dental splint) & Videography Analysis

These splints work to normalize proprioceptive force on the maxilla, helping you feel more grounded in space. This has immediate effects on gait, can relax chronic muscle tension in the head, neck and shoulders, and can reduce symptoms of vertigo or dizziness. Patients who come to us through referrals from physical therapists may have been recommended these.

We confirm the splint’s proper fit and function by examining your gait, which we record on video if you permit. The result of this process supports healthier posture and more natural movement patterns ultimately improving your overall comfort and well-being.
