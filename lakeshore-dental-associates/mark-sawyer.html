Meet Dr. Sawyer

Dr. Sawyer is married to his wife, Marilyn, an elementary school nurse. Together they have three adult children.

Dr. Sawyer received his DDS degree from Illinois University College of Dentistry i n 1984. He has been the owner of Lakeshore Dental Associates since 1987. He has been voted Best American Dentist for the past four years. Dr. Sawyer has over 1500 hours of continuing education in dentistry and is a member of the following organizations.

"It is an honor and a privilege to serve the patients in my practice; they are the reason I come to work with a smile on my face every day. I enjoy meeting new patients and getting to know them and their families as we work together to improve their oral health. I appreciate it when patients want to play an active role in their dental experience. I joined the oral healthcare field to help change the way people think about going to the dentist’s office. When patients tell me that they did not like to visit the dentist before, but now they love it, I know I’ve been successful!"
