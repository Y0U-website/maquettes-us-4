All-on-4 Dental Implants are a modern alternative to traditional dentures or full-mouth implants. Using just four dental implants strategically placed in the jaw, we can support a full arch of non-removable replacement teeth — upper, lower, or both. Unlike removable dentures, the All-on-4 system is fixed in place, allowing you to: Eat your favorite foods with confidence
  • Speak clearly and naturally
  • Maintain your facial structure
  • Avoid adhesive creams or daily removal
  • Smile without worry of shifting or slipping

How the All-on-4 Process Works

The All-on-4 treatment is carefully designed to restore your entire smile with fewer implants and minimal downtime. Here’s how the process typically works:

The entire process is led by Dr. Frederick L. Jones, Jr. , supported by Dr. Brandie White-Ates and Dr. Gage Gregory — each with extensive training in restorative and implant dentistry.

  • Initial Consultation We evaluate your oral health, take 3D images, and determine whether you’re a good candidate. Even patients with some bone loss may qualify without needing bone grafting.
  • Treatment Planning Using advanced digital imaging and planning software, we precisely map out the implant placement for optimal support and long-term success.
  • Implant Surgery Four titanium implants are placed into the jawbone. Two implants are positioned vertically in the front and two are angled in the back for maximum stability.
  • Temporary Prosthesis In most cases, we place a temporary set of teeth on the same day, allowing you to leave with a functional smile.
  • Final Restoration After a few months of healing and implant integration, we attach your permanent, custom-designed prosthesis.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

How long do All-on-4 Dental Implants last?

With proper care and regular checkups, All-on-4 implants can last decades. The implants themselves are made from medical-grade titanium and designed to integrate permanently with your bone.

Will I be without teeth during the process?

No. In most cases, we place a temporary set of teeth on the same day as your surgery, so you’ll never have to go without a smile.

Are All-on-4 implants removable?

No. All-on-4 prosthetics are fixed in place and can only be removed by your dentist. They are not taken out daily like traditional dentures.

What if I’ve already lost bone in my jaw?

One of the advantages of All-on-4 is that it often avoids the need for bone grafting. The posterior implants are placed at an angle to maximize support, even in areas with reduced bone density.

How do I clean my All-on-4 implants?

You’ll brush and floss around the prosthesis like you would natural teeth, and we’ll provide special tools and instructions to ensure long-term hygiene and implant health.
