Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, implants can last decades — often a lifetime. They’re one of the most durable and reliable dental restorations available.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth and sufficient bone density are good candidates. Even if you&#x27;ve been told you&#x27;re not, options like mini implants or bone grafting may still allow for implant placement.

What’s the difference between All-on-4 and traditional implants?

All-on-4 implants support a full arch of teeth with just four implants, offering quicker results and often avoiding bone grafts. Traditional implants may require more individual posts and healing time for full-arch restoration.

Does getting implants hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia and is typically well-tolerated. Most patients report less discomfort than expected and resume normal activities within a day or two.

How do dental implants compare to bridges or dentures?

Implants are more stable, comfortable, and long-lasting. Unlike bridges, they don&#x27;t require grinding down adjacent teeth, and unlike dentures, they won’t slip or require adhesive.
