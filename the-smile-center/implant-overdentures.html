Implant overdentures are removable dentures that are anchored to dental implants placed in the jawbone. Unlike traditional dentures that sit on the gums, overdentures “snap” onto strategically positioned implants, giving you enhanced retention, comfort, and chewing ability. They are often used for: Patients missing all or most of their teeth
  • Individuals dissatisfied with loose-fitting full or partial dentures
  • Those who want a more affordable option than fixed implant-supported dentures

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Overdentures

How are implant overdentures different from regular dentures?

Regular dentures rest on your gums and rely on suction or adhesives. Implant overdentures are anchored by implants in your jaw, giving you a more stable and comfortable fit.

How many implants are needed for overdentures?

Most implant overdentures require between 2 and 4 implants per arch, depending on your specific case and the desired level of stability.

Can I remove the overdentures myself?

Yes. Implant overdentures are designed to snap on and off your implants, making them easy to clean and maintain at home.

Are implant overdentures permanent?

The implants are permanent, but the overdentures themselves are removable. This makes them easier to care for and replace over time if needed.

What’s the lifespan of implant overdentures?

With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime. The overdenture appliance itself may need to be replaced or relined every 5–10 years depending on wear and usage.
