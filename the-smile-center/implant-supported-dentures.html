Frequently Asked Questions About Implant-Supported Dentures

How are implant-supported dentures different from traditional dentures?

Traditional dentures sit on the gums and rely on suction or adhesive. Implant-supported dentures are anchored into the jawbone, providing a more secure, stable, and natural-feeling solution.

Are implant-supported dentures permanent?

They can be either fixed (permanent) or removable, depending on your needs. Both options are highly stable and supported by implants for superior function.

Do I need an implant for each missing tooth?

No. Full arch implant-supported dentures usually only require 4–6 implants per jaw to support an entire row of teeth.

What is the recovery time after implant placement?

Healing typically takes 3–6 months while the implants integrate with your bone. Temporary dentures may be worn during this period.

Can I eat normally with implant-supported dentures?

Yes. You’ll be able to chew more effectively and enjoy a wider range of foods compared to traditional dentures.
