About Us

The Viola Startzman Clinic is a modern, welcoming community health center dedicated to providing high-quality, affordable health care to everyone in our community. We believe in a comprehensive approach to your well-being, offering multiple services for your physical, dental, and mental health needs.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and quality health care to all members of our community, regardless of their insurance status or ability to pay.

Your first line of defense for health and wellness.

A healthy smile is a vital part of your overall health.

Your emotional well-being is just as important as your physical health.

Our Story

The Viola Startzman Clinic was founded in 1973 by Dr. Viola Startzman, a visionary physician who believed everyone deserves access to quality health care. Dr. Startzman&#x27;s vision has guided us for over 50 years, and we are proud to continue her legacy of providing quality, affordable care to our community’s health.
