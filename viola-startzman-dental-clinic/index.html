The practice

The Viola Startzman Clinic is a community-based health center committed to providing accessible, affordable health care for all. We believe in a collaborative approach to your well-being, offering comprehensive services for your primary, dental, and mental health needs. We are more than just a clinic–we are your partners in health.

We are a community health clinic that values everyone. Whether you have insurance or not, our doors ar open. We offer a sliding fee scale and accept all insurance plans, ensuring affordable access to quality care.

The Viola Startzman Clinic speaks your language and understands our community&#x27;s unique health needs. From the boardroom to the exam room, we share a common goal: to provide exceptional care in a comfortable, welcoming environment

Your whole health matters. Our integrated services mean you can address your physical, dental, and mental health needs in one place, saving you time and simplifying your care.

Serving the Wayne and Holmes County community and beyond for over 30 years.

Comprehensive Care for Every Need - From primary care and dental health to mental well-being and specialized services, we offer a wide range of healthcare solutions to support your whole heath journey.

We are your partners in wellness education and community health with the goal to guide you towards a healthier, happier life.

Primary Care: Family health services from vaccinations to chronic disease management and preventative healthcare services. Dental Care: Affordable dental care for all ages, from cleanings to complex procedures. Mental Health Care: Support for your emotional well-being through individual evaluations, in person or via telehealth.
