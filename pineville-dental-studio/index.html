Our preventive services help maintain your oral health and prevent future dental issues.

We focus on oral health needs of infants, children, and adolescents. It involves preventive measures like regular check-ups, cleanings, and education on proper oral hygiene.

Invisalign® are clear aligners which are custom made, transparent trays used to straighten teeth without traditional braces. They are worn for most of the day and are removed for eating and oral hygiene.
