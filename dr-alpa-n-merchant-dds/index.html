Dr. Merchant subscribes to the philosophy that the alignment and interaction of your teeth can reveal underlying issues within your head and neck region, and these issues may have far-reaching consequences for your overall well-being. By carefully analyzing the way your teeth fit together, Dr. Merchant believes it is possible to gain valuable insights into potential stresses, strains, and misalignments that might be affecting your head and neck , and to identify how these issues could be impacting the rest of your body. During a comprehensive examination, Dr. Merchant will conduct a thorough assessment of all the muslces of head and neck responsible for jaw function. This will involve the use of electromyography (EMG) to measure muscle activity, jaw tracking with a magnet to monitor jaw movement, and sonography to visualize the jaw joint and identify any abnormalities.

In addition to the technological tools, Dr. Merchant will also perform a hands-on evaluation of the head and neck muscles, noting any areas of tension or discomfort. The patient’s expectations for treatment outcomes will be discussed, and a posture analysis will be conducted to assess any postural imbalances that may be contributing to the patient’s symptoms.
