Background

Initially, Dr. Min Gyu Kwak’s intellectual curiosity was anchored in the theoretical world; he envisioned a future as a scientist, deeply captivated by the elegant, abstract complexities of mathematics and physics. However, his life’s trajectory shifted profoundly following the birth of his son, who faced significant medical challenges from the start.

As he stood by his son’s side through a grueling series of surgeries and long rehabilitative roads, Dr. Kwak experienced a profound internal transformation. He realized that his true calling was not in a lab, but in a clinical setting where he could directly alleviate the suffering of others.

Today, as an orthodontist, he finds his greatest fulfillment in restoring the confidence of his patients and impacting their lives far beyond the dental chair. Remarkably, his original love for science has not been lost; instead, it has been integrated into his practice, where he applies the precision of physics and mathematical logic to the meticulous design and execution of every orthodontic treatment plan.

Treatment Philosophy

His primary mission is to provide straightforward, transparent care that puts your needs ahead of everything else. You will never feel pressured in his office. He won’t recommend treatment unless you need it. He’s told many patients they don’t need orthodontic solutions. When orthodontics is the right path for a patient, he listens closely to their goals and gives them every option to achieve it. He empowers them through education to make the best decision for their smile.

His doctoral research was honored as the 2016 Cover of the Year by the Journal of Dental Research.
