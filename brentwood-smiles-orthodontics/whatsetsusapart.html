What Sets Us Apart

Independent, transparent, and dedicated to personalized care — not corporate quotas. Every treatment plan is built on honesty, precision, and your best interest at heart.

Independent and Transparent

Free from the constraints and agendas of corporate-owned practices, Dr. Kwak provides care centered entirely on the patient’s wellbeing. You are all that matters. We are dedicated to personalized care, not product quotas – meaning you get honest, tailored treatment plans focused purely on your needs.

Exceptional Skill

Trained at Stony Brook and UCLA, Dr. Kwak brings years of private practice experience to the table. He specializes in treating the most challenging orthodontic problems that require a high level of expertise and a commitment to precision. By refusing to compromise on quality, Dr. Kwak ensures every patient achieves impeccable, efficient results.

Sincere Integrity

We offer free consultations that are treated as an opportunity to educate each patient and family in a friendly, no-pressure environment. We won’t treat you if you don’t need it. Honesty and integrity guide our care as we empower patients without coercion, sales, or anything other than your best interest at heart.

Real Relationship

We believe that clinical excellence should never come at the expense of a personal connection. You will never encounter the impersonal, high-volume atmosphere of an ’orthodontic factory’ here. Instead, you are welcomed into our family right away, a bond that is built on a bedrock of transparency and integrity. We are on a constant mission to fill your day with good vibes and laughter because we believe a healthy smile starts with a happy heart.

Flexible Financing Options

Brentwood Smiles Orthodontics accepts all major dental insurance plans to make the most of your orthodontic benefits and minimize your out-of-pocket costs. All of our patients are also offered zero-interest, flexible payment plans.
