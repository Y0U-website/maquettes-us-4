Get to Know Dr. Beydoun

As our children s dentist, Dr. Beydoun enjoys working with all kinds of patients and there’s certainly never a dull moment! No two people are identical, so it’s very fulfilling to provide an individualized approach of care and really get to know them over time. Seeing them leave the dental office with big smiles on their faces always makes his day! To learn more about him, keep reading below:

Why Did You Become a Children s Dentist?

Dr. Beydoun became interested in the oral healthcare field when he was only 16. He spent the summer shadowing his uncle and immediately became hooked! He saw firsthand the meaningful, long-term relationships his uncle had built with his patients, how compassionate he was throughout the treatment process, and how much the patients trusted and admired him in return. He also witnessed how much artistry was involved and how it blended together perfectly with the scientific aspects.

Today, Dr. Beydoun views his work as a personal challenge to make each patient’s dental experience highly relaxing and personal. He loves forming relationships with them and their parents so that they change from people who hate the dentist’s office into people who love coming to visit him! He also enjoys that dentistry is one of the few areas of healthcare that focuses on prevention first instead of just treating symptoms!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Originally from New York, Dr. Beydoun chose to attend The City College of New York before pursuing his doctorate at Boston University. He also has his Master’s in Biology and is a certified Invisalign provider. He is a current member of the American Dental Association.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Whenever he’s not busy at work, Dr. Beydoun enjoys ice skating, jogging, cooking delicious meals, and trying out lots of new restaurants in his local area.
