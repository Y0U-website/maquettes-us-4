Your questions answered about dental implants and how they can transform your life.

Dental implants consist of a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone as an artificial root, topped with an abutment and a custom crown. They offer a permanent, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth that feels and functions like real ones.

Costs vary by case: a single implant (including abutment and crown) ranges from $3,000–$6,000 or more. Additional procedures like bone grafting increase the price. Many Pasadena practices provide financing; insurance may cover portions. Schedule a consultation for a personalized quote.

Surgeons perform the procedure under local anesthesia, often with sedation for comfort. Patients typically experience minimal pain during surgery and mild soreness afterward, managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Many return to work the next day.

The process spans 3–9 months. Placement surgery takes 1–2 hours per implant, followed by 3–6 months of healing (osseointegration). Final crown attachment occurs after healing. Some Pasadena cases allow same-day temporary teeth.

Dental implants boast a 95–98 % success rate, among the highest for tooth replacement. Proper care and an experienced Pasadena provider maximize longevity.

Treat them like natural teeth: brush twice daily, floss (using implant-specific tools), and attend regular check-ups. Avoid hard foods initially. Good hygiene prevents peri-implantitis and ensures decades-long durability.

With excellent oral hygiene and routine care, implants often last a lifetime. Crowns may need replacement every 10–15 years due to wear.
