What is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant is the most effective way to replace a missing tooth without affecting the surrounding teeth. The procedure involves placing a small titanium post into the jawbone, which acts as an artificial root. Once the implant fuses with the bone, a custom ceramic crown is attached, restoring the tooth’s appearance, strength, and function. Unlike bridges or partial dentures, a single implant stands alone, preserving the health of nearby teeth and preventing bone loss in the jaw.

A single dental implant (including the crown) typically ranges from $4,000 to $5,000 . If you only need a single tooth crown, the cost usually ranges from $1,500 to $2,000 , depending on the materials used and the complexity of the case.

Single tooth implants are designed to last for decades, and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are crucial for maintaining their longevity. This ensures that your smile remains healthy and vibrant.

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the implant procedure. We use advanced techniques and sedation options to enhance comfort. This approach helps ensure a smooth experience with reduced pain during recovery.

Initial recovery typically takes 1 to 2 weeks, but full healing can take 3 to 6 months. Most patients can return to their normal activities within a few days. Your specific timeline may vary based on individual circumstances.

Candidates usually have one or more missing teeth and good overall health. Sufficient bone density is essential for the success of the procedure. A thorough consultation will help determine your eligibility.
