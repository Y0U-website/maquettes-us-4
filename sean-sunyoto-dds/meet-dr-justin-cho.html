Dr. Justin Cho, DDS

Dr. Justin Cho, DDS, is a leading general dentist in Los Angeles known for his commitment to delivering comprehensive, patient-centered care. He practices a wide range of dentistry, with expertise in cosmetic dentistry, oral surgery, root canal therapy, implant placement, and Invisalign treatment. Beyond clinical care, Dr. Cho is an active member of multiple professional organizations and serves as Chair of Continuing Education, leading annual programs that educate and inspire hundreds of fellow dentists.
