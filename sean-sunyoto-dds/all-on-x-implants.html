What to Expect: Your All-on-X Journey

1. Advanced Digital Evaluation

We begin with a CBCT scan and digital intra-oral scan to assess bone structure, bite, and smile goals.

2. Personalized Treatment Plan

Your doctor designs a customized full-arch plan, determining whether bone grafting is needed and reviewing cost and financing options.

3. Surgical Day

Under gentle sedation, 4–6 implants are placed with guided precision. You’ll leave the same day with a fixed temporary bridge that looks and feels like natural teeth.

4. Healing and Integration

Over the next 3–4 months, your implants integrate with your bone. We schedule progress check-ups to ensure healing and comfort.

5. Final Prosthesis

Your permanent bridge is crafted for long-term strength and aesthetics, designed to restore your smile’s natural look and function.

6. Ongoing Care

Routine maintenance visits and hygiene appointments keep your implants and gums healthy for years to come.
