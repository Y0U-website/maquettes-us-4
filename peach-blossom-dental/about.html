What To Expect From Your Visit

Sit back and relax in our waiting room, and enjoy a complimentary drink from our beverage bar. Once it’s time for your appointment, your hygienist will bring you back to one of our modern patient rooms. You’ll get x-rays and a teeth cleaning. Then, Dr. Wright will examine your mouth and look for signs of common oral health issues. If she notices any problems, she’ll let you know and discuss your options for treatment. If not, we&#x27;ll schedule your next six-month visit.

What to Bring

Besides your smile, you’ll need to bring your insurance information and a method of payment like cash or a card. If you have any medical conditions, it’s a good idea to bring a copy of your medical history and a list of prescriptions you’re taking, too.
