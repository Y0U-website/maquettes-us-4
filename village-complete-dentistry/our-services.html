implants

Don’t let missing teeth affect your quality of life. With dental implants, we can help restore the function and appearance of your smile.

Why Implants?

  • Single implants are easier to clean and maintain than traditional bridges
  • Implant-retained or supported dentures increase bite strength and stability of the denture resulting in less or no need for adhesive
  • They provide stimulation of the bone to reduce bone loss and atrophy after a tooth is extracted

TMJ + Jaw Disorders

Tight jaw, pain in your neck, or frequent headaches? We can help protect your teeth from clenching and grinding.

What is TMJ?

  • Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) dysfunction syndrome can show up as pain in the ear, neck or jaw, and often includes headaches. Once the problem is properly diagnosed, treatment is often simple.
  • "Bite guards" or "night guards" are commonly used to treat the signs and symptoms of dysfunction and get patients out of pain.
  • A bite adjustment or "equilibration" is often the final stage of treatment for patients needing small changes. Severe cases can require orthodontics, bite reconstruction, or jaw surgery.

Restorative + Cosmetic

Fillings to teeth whitening, we personalize your care based on what we see today and what you’ll need tomorrow. We’ll look at your entire mouth, determining what’s going on beneath the surface before recommending or treating anything.

how it works:

  • Veneers are thin porcelain coverings that are bonded to your existing tooth structure in order to improve aesthetics, correcting small, misshapen, worn, discolored, or cracked teeth
  • Crowns also correct misshapen, worn, discolored or cracked teeth, but can cover and protect teeth with existing fillings or deep decay
  • "Bonding" or tooth-colored filling material can preserve the natural look of the teeth while repairing small chips or fractures, restoring small areas of decay, or changing the shape of teeth
