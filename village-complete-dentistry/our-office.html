Baker, DDS, who began his dental career in Prairie Village in 1983. He moved to this Corinth location in 1998, where he continued his small, relationship-based practice until Dr. Blair’s arrival in 2021. We’re committed to continuing our high standard of care and serving you and your family for the long haul—childhood through retirement.

Meet Dr. Blair

Dr. Blair Edgington is a second-generation dentist hailing from Wichita, KS. She attended Baker University for her undergraduate studies, earning a Biology degree with a minor in English and Art. During her senior year, she was given the opportunity to hold a senior art show, which was typically only offered to art majors. After working with her father and his team she found the perfect mix of two of her passions in dentistry: science and art. She followed in her father’s footsteps, receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery from UMKC School of Dentistry.

Dr. Blair has advanced training in the analysis of bite problems, disorders of the jaw joints, and smile design.

She’s practiced in the Kansas City area since 2009 and lives in nearby Westwood. In her spare time, she enjoys creating pottery, reading, going on family bike rides, and water skiing at Table Rock Lake in the summer. She and her husband, Jason, have four children and an adorable dog named Bunny!
