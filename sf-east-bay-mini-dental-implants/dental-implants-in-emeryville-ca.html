The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process begins with an initial consultation, where Dr. Rose Magno assesses your oral health and bone structure through an exam and detailed 3D imaging. Based on this evaluation, Dr. Magno will develop your customized treatment plan to address your specific needs and goals while keeping your budget in mind.

With traditional dental implant surgery, the metal post is placed into the jawbone to act as a prosthetic tooth root. This process requires a healing period of several months , during which the implant integrates with the bone. After the treatment site has healed, an abutment is attached to the post, and a custom restoration is attached to the abutment. This multi-step surgery creates a strong foundation for replacement teeth, but it can be time-consuming and may not be suitable for everyone due to the extended healing period and bone density requirements.

Thanks to the unique features of mini dental implants, Dr. Magno performs the mini implant procedure in a much more streamlined and minimally invasive way. First, she gently creates a tiny opening in the gums—no incision needed. Then, she’ll guide the small implant into place. Because mini implants are exceptionally stable as soon as Dr. Magno anchors them in the bone, she can immediately attach a temporary restoration. With mini implants, no long healing periods are needed. When your final restoration has been fabricated in the dental lab, we’ll simply replace the temporary one.

Dr. Magno’s precise and efficient technique delivers stability, comfort, immediate results, and a faster recovery for her patients.
