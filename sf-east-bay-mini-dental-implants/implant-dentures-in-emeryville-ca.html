Why Choose Implant Dentures?

When you lose a tooth, you don’t just lose the crown (the part that shows above the gums). You also lose the root (the structure concealed below, embedded into the jawbone). When you chew or bite, your root stimulates the jawbone, helping maintain its mass and volume. However, when you lose a tooth, you also lose this stimulation. As a result, your bone will begin to resorb or deteriorate.

Unfortunately, anyone who has lost a tooth suffers from bone resorption to some extent. Over time, the jawbone loses density and shrinks, changing your facial structure. For those with conventional dentures, this means that your prosthetic, which once fit snugly against your gums and jaw, can start to shift and slide. Implant dentures solve this problem, as dental implants are used as artificial tooth roots to stabilize the denture and maintain jawbone health.

The Implant Denture Process

At San Francisco East Bay Mini Dental Implants, Dr. Magno uses mini dental implants to secure dentures. These narrow-diameter implants are less than 3 mm wide, or about half the width of conventional implants. And unlike conventional implants, which consist of two pieces, mini implants are a single piece. Due to their slim size and one-piece design, Dr. Magno can place them using a minimally invasive technique.

To place mini implants, Dr. Magno will gently prepare a small pilot hole in the gums and jawbone. Then, she will carefully position the implant in the hole, where it quickly anchors into the bone. Since mini implants are highly stable immediately after placement, Dr. Magno can attach a temporary denture on the same day ! In just one appointment, you can go from having no teeth to enjoying a functional smile!

Because the procedure doesn’t require incisions or stitches, healing time is significantly shorter! With conventional implants, you need to wait months for your gums to heal before the procedure is complete. In contrast, mini implants make the road to implant dentures much smoother. In just a few weeks, you’ll have a beautiful custom denture that looks, feels, and functions perfectly for everyday use.

Implant Denture Options

Using mini dental implants, Dr. Magno restores smiles with dentures that stay secure in your mouth. She offers various options to choose from, depending on your specific desire.

  • Semi-permanent roundhouse bridge: This restoration functions similarly to your natural teeth, as you never take it out! This design creates a seamless appearance and allows for a strong, stable bite, making it an excellent upgrade from conventional dentures.
  • Permanent roundhouse bridge: Dr. Magno cements this roundhouse bridge onto 10-12 mini implants. This makes it a permanent solution that stays in your mouth at all times. It is designed to function the most like natural teeth and offers a strong, reliable bite and a natural appearance.
