About Dental Implants

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into your jawbone to serve as the root of a replacement tooth. Over time, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable, permanent foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. The result is a restoration that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

At Jennifer Lopez Dental in Miami, Dr. Lopez uses CBCT cone beam imaging to plan each implant placement with precision. This 3D technology allows her to evaluate your bone density, map the exact position of the implant relative to nerves and sinuses, and minimize surgical time. Whether you are replacing a single tooth or considering a full-arch solution like All-on-4, Dr. Lopez will guide you through every step with clear communication and a focus on your comfort.

Most adults with good overall health and adequate jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. Conditions like gum disease or bone loss do not automatically disqualify you. Dr. Lopez may recommend periodontal treatment or bone grafting before implant placement to ensure a strong foundation. During your consultation at our Sunset Drive office, she will review your medical history, take 3D imaging, and provide an honest assessment of your options.

Dental implants offer advantages that other replacement options cannot match. Unlike bridges, implants do not require reducing healthy adjacent teeth. Unlike dentures, they will not slip or require adhesives. Implants also stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone deterioration that naturally follows tooth loss. Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with sufficient jawbone density are good candidates. Dr. Lopez uses CBCT imaging to evaluate your bone and determine the best approach, including bone grafting if needed.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The full process typically takes three to six months, including healing time for osseointegration. In some cases, a temporary tooth can be placed on the same day as the implant surgery.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you feel no pain during surgery. Post-operative discomfort is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.
