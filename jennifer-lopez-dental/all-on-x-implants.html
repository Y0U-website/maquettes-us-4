About All-on-X®/Hybrid Dentures

Dr. Lopez offers All-on-X® implants in Miami, FL, to restore full arches of missing or failing teeth with a stable, natural-looking result. Also known as hybrid dentures, this treatment provides a fixed way to rebuild your smile with strength, function, and aesthetics in mind. Dr. Lopez and her team focus on careful planning, precise execution, and a supportive experience from start to finish.

All-on-X implants are a full-arch treatment that uses a small number of strategically placed dental implants to support a fixed bridge. Instead of placing an implant for every missing tooth, this approach secures an entire upper or lower arch with typically four to six implants, depending on your jaw structure and bone quality. This restoration is often referred to as a hybrid denture because it combines implant support with a full-arch prosthesis that stays in place and is removed only by the dental team.

Patients comparing full-arch options often want to know how this differs from implant-supported dentures. This treatment secures a non-removable bridge to implants, while implant-supported dentures may be removable or snap-in. Both can be effective solutions, and the right choice depends on your goals, gum health, anatomy, and preferences.

What to Expect

Your step-by-step guide to all-on-x®/hybrid dentures at Jennifer Lopez Dental .

Initial Consultation & Planning

You will discuss goals, review your medical history, and receive 3D imaging and photos for precise treatment planning. Dr. Lopez prioritizes what matters most to you and outlines the treatment phases so the process feels clear and manageable.

Guided Placement Day

Any necessary extractions are completed, and implants are placed at strategic angles to make the best use of available bone. When appropriate, a provisional bridge can be secured the same day so you leave with a confident smile.

Healing & Integration

Over the next several months, the implants fuse with the bone. In many cases, the full timeline is about 6-8 months from implant placement to the final restoration. You will follow specific care instructions and attend check-ins to support healthy healing.

Final Custom Bridge

Once healing is complete, a premium final restoration is crafted with careful attention to bite, speech, and smile aesthetics, guided by a full dental-facial analysis.

Ongoing Maintenance

Routine visits help protect your investment through professional cleanings, at-home hygiene guidance, and periodic evaluations.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-X®/Hybrid Dentures

How many implants are used in All-on-X?

The "X" represents the number of implants used for your specific case, most commonly four to six. The plan is based on your bone quality, bite, and aesthetic goals.

Will I leave with teeth the same day?

Many patients receive a provisional bridge on the day implants are placed. This depends on bone stability and overall health. You will receive a clear plan before treatment begins.

How long does treatment take?

In many cases, the full process takes about 6-8 months. This includes implant placement, healing time, and delivery of the final custom restoration.

How long do results last?

With good home care and regular professional maintenance, this treatment can provide many years of function and aesthetics. Routine checkups help protect both the implants and the final bridge.

How does this compare with implant-supported dentures?

This option is fixed in place, while implant-supported dentures may be removable or snap-in. The best choice depends on your preferences, anatomy, and oral health.

Am I a candidate if I have bone loss?

Many people with bone loss still qualify because implant positions and angles are planned to make the best use of available bone. A 3D evaluation confirms your candidacy and options.
