About Jennifer Lopez Dental

Jennifer Lopez Dental was founded on a simple idea: dental care should feel personal. Located on Sunset Drive in Miami, our practice serves patients across Kendall, South Miami, Coral Gables, and the greater Miami-Dade area with a focus on comfort, transparency, and lasting results.

Dr. Jennifer Lopez brings a hands-on approach to every treatment, whether it is a routine cleaning or a complex restoration. She takes the time to listen, explain your options, and build a plan that fits your goals and your life. Our team shares that same commitment to making every visit a positive experience.

Want to see our office before your first visit? Browse our photo gallery for a look at our modern treatment rooms and welcoming environment. We also offer flexible financing options including CareCredit and Cherry to make quality dental care accessible for every family.

OUR PROMISE TO YOU

Choosing a dentist is personal. At Jennifer Lopez Dental , we have built our practice around values that shape how we care for every patient who walks through our doors. These are more than words on a wall. They guide the experience you can expect at every visit.

Honesty & Transparency

We explain your options clearly, answer your questions honestly, and never recommend treatment you do not need. You will always understand what to expect before any care begins.

A Patient-First Philosophy

Your comfort, time, and goals guide every decision we make. From flexible scheduling to gentle techniques, every part of your experience is designed with your needs in mind.

Commitment to Excellence

Dr. Lopez is committed to continuing education and invests in advanced technology so you can benefit from effective, modern care right here in Miami.
