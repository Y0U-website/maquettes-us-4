How the Implant Process Works

At our office, we’ve designed a collaborative process to provide exceptional care and results. While dental implants are not placed in our office, we work closely with a trusted periodontist nearby to ensure the best outcomes. Here’s what to expect:

  • Comprehensive Evaluation : During your consultation, Dr. Anderson will assess your dental health and determine if teeth implants are the right solution for you.
  • Implant Placement by a Specialist : You’ll be referred to our skilled periodontist partner, who will place the dental implant securely into your jawbone.
  • Healing and Integration : The implant will fuse with your jawbone over several months, creating a stable base for your restoration.
  • Custom Restoration : After the implant has fully healed, you’ll return to our office where Dr. Anderson will place a custom-designed dental crown, bridge, or denture to complete your smile.
