We’re open and new patients are welcome !

Welcome ! The information below highlights our new safety protocols following the California Department of Public Health. These new protocols are in addition to our existing, extensive, infection control protocols:

We are committed to keeping you safe and healthy! We have resumed regular hygiene visits and are rescheduling many patients that we had to cancel due to the closure. Thank you for your patience as we reach out. We can’t wait to see you!

  • Pre-appointment screening questions: We will ask a series of questions before your appointment over the phone or via email. Forms are available online on Request Appointment page.
  • Taking your temperature: Before entering the treatment room to check for fever. Everyone, including employees, will be screened with a health questionnaire and forehead temperature reading daily.
  • Check-in: Text or call us when you arrive and wait in your car. We are limiting the number of people in our reception area and have removed magazines.
  • Face masks: Each person in the office will be required to wear a mask (including us), our team will let you know when to remove the mask for your appointment.
  • Parents & Guardians: We ask that only one guardian attend the appointment with their dependent.
  • We have new medical-grade HEPA air filtration units in treatment areas and throughout the office!
  • Once in the operatory we will ask you to pre-rinse with a hydrogen peroxide-based rinse to lower the pathogens in your mouth.
  • We will continue to use high speed suctioning and isolation tools when generating aerosols (Vanguard HVE, Isolite, Rubber Dams, Daily disinfection using UV Light and Fogger ). We will have additional PPE including hair coverings, long jackets, face-shields and N95 masks.
