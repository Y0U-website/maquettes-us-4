Dental implants

If you already have a dental implant in place, the final step is restoring it with a crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth. We provide implant restorations that are custom fitted, shaded, and shaped to blend in with the rest of your smile.

Completing Your Implant With a Custom Restoration

A natural-looking finish for your dental implant.

An implant restoration is the visible replacement tooth that attaches to your dental implant, completing the process once the implant itself has healed and integrated with your jawbone. Depending on how many teeth you’re replacing, this can be a single crown, a bridge that spans multiple teeth, or a full denture supported by several implants.

Unlike the implant post underneath it, which stays hidden below the gum line, the restoration is what you and everyone else actually sees and uses every day. It’s custom-shaded and shaped to match your surrounding teeth, so it looks and functions like a natural part of your smile rather than something separate from it.

Restorative Dentistry The Dental Implant Restoration Process Once your implant has healed and integrated with the jawbone, we take an impression and design a custom crown, bridge, or denture to attach to it. The restoration is shaped and shaded to match your surrounding teeth, completing the process so your implant looks and functions like the rest of your smile.

Taking an Impression

We capture a precise digital or physical impression of your implant site once healing is complete. This ensures the final restoration fits securely and comfortably against your gum line and bite.

Designing Your Restoration

Your crown, bridge, or denture is custom-designed to match the size, shape, and shade of your surrounding teeth. This step is what makes the final result look like a natural part of your smile rather than a replacement.

Fitting and Finishing

Once your restoration is ready, we check the fit, bite, and appearance before securing it in place. From there, it functions and feels like the rest of your natural teeth.

Comfortable, Precise Restorations

Fitting an implant restoration is a detail-oriented process, and small differences in fit or bite can affect how comfortable your new tooth feels day to day. We use digital impressions and careful bite checks to make sure your restoration sits precisely against your gum line and works well with your natural bite before it’s finalized.

Once your restoration is in place, it should feel like a natural part of your mouth, not something you’re constantly aware of. If anything ever feels off, whether it’s a bite adjustment or general wear over time, we can check and fine-tune your restoration at your regular visits.

If you have an implant that needs a crown, bridge, or denture, or your current restoration isn’t feeling quite right, we would be glad to take a look. You can reach out through our contact form to schedule an appointment, and our team will help you find a time that works.
