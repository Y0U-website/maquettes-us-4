Meet Dr. Tina Panchal

For Dr. Tina Panchal, dentistry has always been about more than treating teeth. It is about building relationships with the families and neighbors she has known for years, right here in the community where she grew up.

A Dental Career Rooted in the Edison Community

Dr. Panchal earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine (formerly UMDNJ) in 2007 and completed her General Practice Residency at Monmouth Medical Center in Long Branch, NJ, the following year. In 2015, she opened her own practice working alongside her father, Dr. Nalin Panchal, who had spent over 35 years as an orthodontist in the same community.

In 2021, she acquired Dr. Moses’ established dental practice and merged both locations into our current office at 817 Inman Ave in Edison. Today, we treat patients of all ages, starting from as young as 1.5 years old, and offer everything from routine cleanings to veneers, crowns, bridges, implant restorations, and SureSmile clear aligners.

Where Advanced Dentistry Meets a Familiar Face

Dr. Panchal takes a conversation-first approach with every patient, especially those who come in feeling anxious or unsure about dental care. Before any treatment begins, she sits down to understand your concerns, your goals, and what fits your budget and timeline, so you always know what to expect and never feel pressured into a plan you are not ready for.

A Name Edison Families Already Know

Dr. Panchal’s father, Dr. Nalin Panchal, spent more than 35 years as an orthodontist just across the street from our practice, treating generations of local families. That legacy means something in a neighborhood where many longtime dentists have retired or been replaced by rotating corporate offices with no lasting ties to the community.

Patients often tell Dr. Panchal that her father treated them as kids, and now she is treating their own children. It is the kind of continuity that is hard to find, and one of the reasons so many families in the Edison area trust the Panchal name with their care.
