Dental Sealants

Cavities often form in the deep grooves of your back teeth, where a toothbrush cannot always reach. Dental sealants create a smooth, protective barrier over those grooves.

Preventative Dental Care Why Sealants Are Beneficial A dental sealant is a thin coating applied directly onto the chewing surface of a back tooth. Once it hardens, it creates a smooth, protective layer over the pits and grooves where bacteria tend to collect. According to the CDC , sealants can prevent up to 80 % of cavities for two years after application and continue protecting against 50 % of cavities for up to four years.

Quick, Comfortable Application

There is no drilling and no anesthesia required. Most patients are back to eating and drinking normally within minutes.

Effective for Every Age

Sealants are ideal for children right after their molars come in, but adults with deep grooves or a history of cavities can benefit too.

Pairs With Regular Cleanings

We check sealants at your regular cleanings and check-ups and can reapply one if it has worn down.

What to Expect at Your Appointment

A sealant appointment fits easily into a standard visit and does not require any special preparation. During your exam, we look at the depth and shape of the grooves on your back teeth to determine whether sealants would help protect them. If sealants are recommended, applying them usually takes just a few minutes per tooth, and you can eat and drink normally right after.

Is Getting a Dental Sealant Painful? No. Applying a sealant does not involve drilling, numbing, or removing any tooth structure. Most patients feel little more than a light brushing sensation on the tooth.

Sealing a single tooth usually takes just a few minutes. Multiple teeth can be sealed in the same visit, often as part of a routine cleaning appointment.

Many dental insurance plans include coverage for sealants, particularly for children and teens. We accept a wide range of PPO plans and can help you understand your specific coverage before treatment.

Sealants can last several years with normal wear. We check them at your regular visits and can repair or reapply a sealant if it becomes worn or chipped over time.
