Accessibility Statement

  • Alt text on meaningful images and ARIA labels on functional icons.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, with visible focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements (AA-level minimums).
  • Responsive design that adapts to assistive technologies and zoomed text.
  • "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
  • Reduced-motion respect for users who set a system preference.
  • Captions and transcripts wherever video or audio is provided.

What we do at our office

  • Accessible entrance with no stairs or other physical barriers at the front door.
  • Accessible parking immediately adjacent to the entrance.
  • Accessible restroom available to patients.
  • Spanish-speaking staff on duty every day we are open.
  • Large-print materials available on request.
  • Service animals welcome.
  • Quiet rooms and noise-reducing accommodations available on request for patients with sensory sensitivities.

Need an accommodation?

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our website, in scheduling, in billing, or in our office — please tell us. We will work with you to provide the information, service, or accommodation you need.

If you’d like a written response, please include your name, the page or service you had trouble with, the assistive technology you use (if any), and how we can best contact you.

Third-party content

Some third-party services embedded in our site (for example, Google Maps) may not be fully under our control. We choose third-party services that prioritize accessibility, and we provide alternative ways to access the same information whenever possible — for instance, address and directions are available as plain text on the same page as an embedded map.

Filing a formal complaint

Most accessibility concerns can be resolved by contacting us directly. If you believe our office has discriminated against you on the basis of disability, you may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Justice under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act. Information on how to file is available at ada.gov .
