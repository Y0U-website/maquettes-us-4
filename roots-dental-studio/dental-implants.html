Dental Implants

  • How long does it take to get dental implants? The dental implant process typically takes several months. After the implant is placed, a 3-6 months healing period is necessary for the post to fuse with the jawbone. Once healed, the crown or restoration is added, completing the process.
  • What are the benefits of dental implants? Dental implants restore the natural look, feel, and function of your teeth, improving your confidence and oral health. Unlike other solutions, implants prevent bone loss, provide lasting durability, and don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support. They’re the gold standard in tooth replacement.
  • What are full arch dental implants? Full arch dental implants, also called All-on-4, are a permanent tooth replacement solution for individuals missing most or all teeth in the upper or lower jaw. The dentist places 4–8 dental implants that act as anchors supporting a custom prosthetic, such as a fixed bridge or implant-supported denture, made from durable materials like porcelain or zirconia. Unlike traditional dentures, full arch implants provide superior stability and a natural appearance and help preserve jawbone health by preventing bone loss.
