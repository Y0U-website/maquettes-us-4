Child Plan (through age 17)

  • Includes 2 routine exams and 2 cleanings

Add-On: Periodontal Maintenance Plan

$215/year

Plan Disclaimer:

In-house coverage must be paid in full and will expire on the day before the one-year anniversary date. If the Additional Perio plan is needed as an add-on, the coverage will expire on the same date as the existing adult or child plan, no matter the sign-up date. The price shall remain the same. *New patients or existing patients who decide to sign up for the in-house insurance within 30 days of their last preventative or diagnostic appointment can use their payment toward the plan sign-up cost.
