Can I eat and drink while wearing Invisalign aligners? No, you should take out your Invisalign aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. Eating with them in can lead to stains, buildup, or even damage, while hot drinks may warp the plastic. Always remove your aligners before meals and rinse your mouth before putting them back in.

How often should I wear my Invisalign aligners? For the best results, wear your Invisalign aligners for 20 to 22 hours a day, only removing them for eating, drinking (except water), brushing, and flossing. Wearing them consistently ensures your teeth shift as planned and keeps your treatment on track.
