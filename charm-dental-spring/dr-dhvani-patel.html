About Dr. Dhvani Patel

With dental training and clinical experience in both India and the United States, Dr. Patel combines strong technical skills with a calm and thoughtful approach. Patients appreciate her attention to detail, gentle care, and ability to make dental visits feel comfortable and stress-free.

She proudly serves families throughout Katy, Richmond, and surrounding Southwest Houston communities, helping children and adults achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized dental care.
