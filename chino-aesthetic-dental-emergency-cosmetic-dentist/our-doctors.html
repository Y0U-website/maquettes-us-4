Dr. Bijal Joshi

Dr. Bijal Joshi has been a dentist for more than 20 years and always provides professional, ethical, personalized dental care to her patients. Dr. Joshi opened her dental practice with a goal of creating a practice that has an environment of modern technology combined with gentle, personalized space for making patients comfortable and relaxed.

Chino Aesthetic Dental is a state-of-the-art dental facility, built with a team of dental professionals who are knowledgeable, caring, and fun loving – so that you can be in the presence of friends who can help you achieve your perfect smile. Dr. Joshi loves taking continuing education to learn different things. Dr. Joshi continues to study the lasts advances in dentistry, always looking for new and improved ways to create healthy, beautiful smiles for you and your family.

Dr. Carlos Rivero, DMD

Exodontist, General Dentist

Dr. Carlos Rivero, DMD, is a seasoned dentistry practitioner based in San Diego, CA, with over 14 years of experience in the field. A proud alumnus of the University of Florida College of Dentistry, Dr. Rivero graduated in 2010 and further honed his skills during a comprehensive residency at the Birmingham VAMC. He currently practices at his clinic, where he maintains a stellar reputation among his patients, evidenced by an impeccable average rating of 5.0 out of 5 from twelve reviews.

Dr. Rivero is committed to accessibility and convenience, welcoming new patients and accepting multiple insurance plans. A bilingual professional, he is fluent in both English and Spanish, enabling him to serve a diverse community with exceptional care and understanding.

Dr. Nisarg Patel, DMD

Periodontist

Dr. Nisarg Patel is a committed periodontist with a deep passion for promoting healthy gums and overall oral health. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Louisville, Kentucky. After completion of training in advanced education in General Dentistry, Dr. Patel completed specialized residency in periodontics at the University of Kansas City.

With a focus on preventive care, Dr. Patel believes in empowering his patients through education about gum health and its connection to systemic wellness. He actively engages in community outreach, conducting workshops and seminars to raise awareness about periodontal disease and its prevention.

Known for his gentle and thorough approach, Dr. Patel combines the latest advancements in periodontal treatment with personalized care to help his patients achieve optimal gum health. In his free time, he enjoys exploring national parks with his wife and 3- year old daughter.

Dr. Rashid Kamdar, D.D.S.

Orthodontist

Dr. Rashid Kamdar Graduated from UCLA Dental School at the top of his class with a degree of dental surgery (DDS). Then he moved to New York to attend a general practice residency at St. Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx. He then completed his Orthodontic specialty training at the UNLV School of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics.

Dr. Kamdar is a Premier Invisalign Provider. He is also an active member of American Association of Orthodontics, American Dental Association and all of their constituent Societies. Speaking both Spanish and Farsi, we feel very fortunate that Dr. Kamdar joined our team.
