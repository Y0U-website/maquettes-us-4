Common Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants actually last? Because we focus on a lifelong oral health trajectory , these restorations are designed to be a permanent investment. When placed with the steady hand of an experienced clinician and maintained with regular hygiene, the titanium root can last for many decades. Most patients find that with proper care, their implant never needs to be replaced.

Am I a candidate for implants if I have experienced bone loss? We thoughtfully and patiently review your options using our digital imaging tools, even if you have been missing teeth for a long time. Modern techniques such as bone grafting can often rebuild the foundation necessary for a successful titanium post . We will provide an honest, conservative assessment of your candidacy during your initial consultation.
