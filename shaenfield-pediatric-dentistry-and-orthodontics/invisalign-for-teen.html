What is Invisalign for Teens?

Invisalign for teens uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners to gradually shift teeth into their correct positions. These aligners are made from a smooth, BPA-free plastic that is virtually invisible when worn.

Why Choose Invisalign for Teens?

Discreet and Aesthetic

Invisalign aligners are clear, making them an excellent option for teenagers who prefer a more discreet orthodontic treatment. They are barely noticeable, allowing teens to smile confidently throughout their treatment.

Comfortable and Removable

Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners have no metal parts that can irritate the gums and cheeks. They are also removable, making it easy for teens to maintain their oral hygiene and enjoy their favorite foods without restrictions.

Customized for Each Patient

Invisalign aligners are custom-made to fit each patient&#x27;s unique dental structure. Our orthodontists use advanced 3D imaging technology to create a precise treatment plan that ensures optimal results.

Our Invisalign for Teens Services

Initial Consultation and 3D Imaging

We start with a comprehensive consultation and 3D imaging of your teen&#x27;s teeth. This allows us to create a detailed treatment plan and visualize the final outcome.

Custom Aligner Fabrication

Based on the treatment plan, a series of custom aligners will be created for your teen. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before moving on to the next set.

Regular Progress Check-Ups

Regular check-ups with our orthodontists ensure that the treatment is progressing as planned. Adjustments can be made as necessary to keep the treatment on track.
