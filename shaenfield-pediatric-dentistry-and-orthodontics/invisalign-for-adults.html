Invisalign for Adults in San Antonio

What is Invisalign for Adults?

Invisalign for adults uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners to gently and gradually move teeth into their correct positions. These aligners are made from smooth, BPA-free plastic, making them comfortable and practically invisible when worn.

Why Choose Invisalign for Adults?

Discreet Appearance

Invisalign aligners are clear, making them an ideal choice for adults who want to straighten their teeth without the noticeable appearance of traditional braces. They allow you to maintain your professional and social life with confidence.

Comfort and Convenience

Invisalign aligners are smooth and comfortable, with no metal brackets or wires that can cause irritation. They are also removable, allowing you to eat, drink, brush, and floss with ease.

Effective and Customized Treatment

Invisalign aligners are tailored to fit each patient&#x27;s unique dental structure. Our orthodontists use advanced 3D imaging technology to design a precise treatment plan that ensures optimal results.

Our Invisalign for Adults Services

Comprehensive Consultation and 3D Imaging

We begin with an in-depth consultation and 3D imaging of your teeth to create a detailed treatment plan. This allows us to visualize the final outcome and customize your aligners accordingly.

Custom Aligner Fabrication

Based on your treatment plan, a series of custom aligners will be created. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before moving on to the next set.

Regular Progress Check-Ups

Regular check-up with our orthodontists ensure that your treatment is progressing as planned. Adjustments can be made as necessary to keep the treatment on track.
