The Most Natural Option to Replace Missing Teeth

A dental implant restoration is the best replacement for a lost tooth on the planet. There is simply nothing available today that looks or functions more like a natural tooth than a dental implant restoration!

Dental implants do more than just give you a beautiful smile. Other benefits of dental implants include:

  • Improved bite and dietary selection
  • Prevention of existing teeth shifting toward an empty tooth socket
  • Prevention of bone loss in your jaw
  • Decreased wear and tear on existing teeth

The Lifespan of Dental Implants

Your dental implant post, which is made of titanium, should last a lifetime provided you take care of your gums and visit our top Springfield, IL dentist regularly. Remember, your dental implant is located below the gumline and not exposed to wear and tear, but the restoration tooth is located above the gumline to enhance your smile.

While your dental implant is meant to last forever, your restoration tooth may need replacing in the future due to wear or trauma.

The better you care for your restoration tooth, however, the longer it will last. Our dental team can provide tips to help you get more mileage out of your restoration.

Dental Implants vs. Bridges

An implant is a threaded titanium post that is placed into the jaw where the bone will integrate around it. Once the implant heals, an abutment and crown are attached to this implant. This implant crown will look and function just like the rest of the teeth in your mouth.

The biggest positive in choosing an implant versus a bridge is that we do not have to drill on the adjacent tooth to accomplish our goal of tooth replacement. However, in most cases, the implant process will cost slightly more than a bridge, and it will take longer to complete compared with a dental bridge.

Before dental implants, bridges were the go-to for tooth replacement. Dental bridges still provide excellent results as well.

Dental bridges involve shaping down teeth on either side of the missing tooth. This allows a dental crown to fit between the two shaped teeth.

A bridge over an implant makes sense when there isn’t adequate bone for implant placement.

How Dental Implants Work

Dental implants are known for their longevity and durability. They are titanium tooth roots implanted in the jawbone. These roots integrate into the bone over several weeks. When fusing finishes, we top the implants with dental crowns that act as new teeth. We can also fit you with a fixed denture, using up to six dental implant roots.

With dental implants, you’ll not have to restrict crunchy foods or avoid complex chewing. You’ll also enjoy the easy maintenance brush, floss, and see your dentist regularly for checkups!

Do I need to replace the dental crown on an implant?

Depending on how well you care for an implant , the dental crown may need to be replaced over time.

Crowns, like all restorations, wear over time. However, if you care for it properly, the implant itself has the potential to last a lifetime.

The good news is that there are steps you can take to ensure the health and longevity of your implant crown. Here s what you should know.

Avoid Hard Foods

Hard or crunchy foods could damage your porcelain implant crown, causing breakdown or fractures. Cutting hard foods into smaller bite-sized pieces will help protect your restoration.

Commit to a Consistent Home Care Routine

Daily brushing and flossing will protect your smile and the health of your restorations, like fillings and crowns.

Brushing and flossing around your dental implant is simple because it s just like a natural tooth. So, caring for your dental implant is straightforward.

Visit Your Springfield Dentist Every Six Months

Your dentist will check the health of your restorations with an exam and x-rays during your routine checkups every six months. They can inform you of any wear or breakdown of your dental implant that could cause future problems.
