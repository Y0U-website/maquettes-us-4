Discreet, Modern Orthodontics with Invisalign

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth using a series of custom‑made, clear aligners—without the brackets, wires, or bands of traditional braces. Designed to align crooked or misaligned teeth gradually and comfortably, Invisalign combines the precision of orthodontics with the aesthetics of cosmetic dentistry.

Each set of aligners is crafted from smooth, transparent plastic and fits comfortably over your teeth, applying gentle pressure to guide them into their ideal position over time. Because the aligners are nearly invisible and removable, Invisalign allows you to transform your smile discreetly—without disrupting your daily routine or drawing attention to your treatment. The result is a straighter, more confident smile, achieved comfortably and subtly.

Who is a good candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign may be a good option for teens and adults with concerns like crowded teeth, spacing, mild bite issues, or teeth that have shifted over time. The best way to know if Invisalign is right for you is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Merissa Richter, who can evaluate your smile, bite, and treatment goals.

How does Invisalign work?

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made clear aligners that gradually move your teeth into better alignment. Each aligner applies gentle, controlled pressure to specific teeth. Dr. Merissa Richter will create a customized treatment plan and guide you through each stage of the process as your smile progresses.

Is Invisalign better than braces?

Invisalign can be a great alternative to braces for many patients because the aligners are clear, removable, and comfortable. Unlike braces, Invisalign does not use brackets or wires. However, braces may still be recommended for certain complex orthodontic needs. Our West End Dental team can help you compare both options during your consultation.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Invisalign treatment time varies based on the complexity of your case. Some patients may only need several months, while others may need a year or longer. During your consultation, Dr. Merissa Richter will create a personalized treatment plan and provide a more accurate estimate based on your specific needs and goals.

Can adults get Invisalign?

Yes. Many adults choose Invisalign because it offers a discreet way to straighten teeth without the appearance of traditional braces. Invisalign is especially popular with adults who want to improve their smile while maintaining a professional appearance at work and in social settings.

Is Invisalign painful?

Invisalign should not be painful, but you may feel mild pressure or soreness when starting a new set of aligners. This is normal and usually means the aligners are gently moving your teeth. Most patients find Invisalign comfortable and easy to fit into their daily routine.

How often do I need to wear Invisalign aligners?

For best results, Invisalign aligners typically need to be worn most of the day and night, only being removed for eating, drinking anything besides water, brushing, and flossing. Dr. Merissa Richter will provide detailed instructions tailored to your treatment plan.

Can I eat and drink with clear aligners?

You should remove your Invisalign aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This helps prevent staining, damage, and trapped food particles. After meals, brush your teeth before putting your aligners back in to keep your teeth and aligners clean.
