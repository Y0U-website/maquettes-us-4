Costa Mesa, CA Dentist

Get to Know Dr. Iris Zhou

Dr. Iris Zhou is so excited to be using her extensive education and natural talent to help patients at Advanced Dental Care smile. For her, taking care of patients like she would her own family is extremely important it’s what brings compassion and transparency to her job. She looks forward to getting to know you personally, but, for now, you can keep reading to learn a bit more about her and her background.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

For Dr. Iris Zhou, working with patients in a judgment-free way is extremely important to her. For more than three years now, she has worked diligently to tailor every treatment plan to each patient’s desires and lifestyle. Her dedication to providing personalized, compassionate, and honest care is what makes her so exceptional!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Iris Zhou’s journey to becoming the wonderful dentist she is today began at UC Davis. From there, she chose to pursue her dental doctorate in Los Angeles at the UCLA School of Dentistry. During her time there, she received the Award of Restorative Excellence, which is a testament to both her clinical skills and natural talent! Today, she is proud to be serving patients here in Costa Mesa.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Away from the office, Dr. Iris Zhou loves spending time with her husband (who is also a dentist!) and their Australian Shepherd. One of her favorite things to do on the weekends is explore new cafes to find good coffee. After all, she did work as a barista in Sacramento, so she knows a thing or two about lattes, cappuccinos, Americanos, and cold brew. Here’s something you may not know about her: she’s an American Yoga Alliance certified instructor.
