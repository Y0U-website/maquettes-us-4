Get to Know Dr. Hadi Shbeeb

For Dr. Shbeeb, the most fulfilling part of his job is treating patients at the highest level using the most up-to-date dental technology. As an endodontist, he is an expert when it comes to providing root canal therapy, which can stop the most painful toothaches while allowing patients to keep their natural teeth. To learn a little more about Dr. Shbeeb ahead of your first appointment, read on below.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

A Santa Barbara native, Dr. Shbeeb attended the University of California, Irvine for his bachelor’s degree, and he went on to earn his DDS degree at the University of Southern California. This was followed by completing a General Practice Residency at the VA Hospital in Loma Linda.

What Kind of Advanced Dental Training Do You Have?

In order to become an endodontist, Dr. Shbeeb had to take specialty training at the University of California, San Francisco, during which he focused solely on providing all types of endodontic care. Since then, he has attended several microsurgical and endodontic continuing education courses. He is also an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, American Association of Endodontists, and the American Academy of Endodontists.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Shbeeb is married, and when he’s not at work, he enjoys traveling, snowboarding, playing soccer, and teaching at the local dental school.
