What does pediatric dental insurance cover?

Pediatric dental insurance typically envelops a spectrum of dental care services, from preventive to more extensive procedures. Regular check-ups, cleanings, x-rays, fluoride treatments, and other preventive services are generally covered. In addition, many insurance plans provide coverage for essential treatments such as fillings, extractions, and sometimes even orthodontic care.

Preventive Care and Your Child’s Dental Health Plan

The adage prevention is better than cure’ holds particularly true in pediatric dentistry. Most pediatric dental plans underscore the importance of preventive care. These services often include routine cleanings, exams, and fluoride treatments, all designed to thwart dental issues before they escalate.

Navigating Deductibles and Copays in Pediatric Dentistry

Deductibles and copays are part and parcel of most dental insurance plans. A deductible is the amount you pay out-of-pocket before your insurance kicks in, while a copay is a fixed fee you pay for each visit or service. Our knowledgeable staff at Pediatric Dentistry on Kimball can help demystify these terms and explain how they apply to your selected services.

What should I do if my insurance doesn’t cover a necessary pediatric dental procedure?

Sometimes, a child’s dental needs extend beyond the realm of what insurance covers. Pediatric Dentistry on Kimball takes a compassionate and proactive approach to such scenarios.
