How Do You Clean a Baby’s Teeth and Gums?

Oral hygiene should begin even before a baby’s first tooth appears. Establishing early routines helps reduce bacteria and supports healthy development.

Parents can care for their baby’s mouth by following these steps:

Parents should supervise brushing to ensure proper technique and encourage consistent habits. These early steps support healthy preschool dental care and reduce the risk of cavities.

  • Gently wipe the gums with a clean, soft cloth
  • Brush teeth using a soft toothbrush
  • Use a small amount of fluoride toothpaste
  • Brush twice each day

What Causes Cavities in Baby Teeth?

Cavities develop when bacteria in the mouth produce acids that weaken tooth enamel. Young children can be especially vulnerable if certain habits increase exposure to sugars.

Common causes of early childhood tooth decay include:

Addressing these factors early helps reduce the risk of cavities and supports stronger tooth development.

  • Frequent sugary drinks or snacks
  • Drinking milk or juice before bedtime
  • Poor brushing habits
  • Extended bottle use
  • Limited exposure to fluoride

How Can Parents Prevent Cavities in Young Children?

Parents play a key role in protecting their child’s oral health. Establishing healthy habits at home and scheduling regular dental visits can greatly reduce cavity risk.

Our pediatric dental office works closely with parents to create preventive strategies that support long-term oral health.

  • Brushing teeth twice daily
  • Limiting sugary foods and beverages
  • Encouraging water between meals
  • Maintaining regular dental visits
  • Discussing fluoride treatments when appropriate
  • Asking about protective dental sealants
