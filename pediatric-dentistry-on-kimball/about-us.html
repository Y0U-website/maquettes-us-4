Our Mission

At Pediatric Dentistry on Kimball, our goal is to provide a fun, educational experience for our patients that sets the standard for pediatric care. Moreover, we want to help kids establish a lifelong, proactive relationship with dentistry. Through our values of Positivity, Education, and Honesty, we help ensure your kids have a reason to smile about their oral health.

Positivity

Dr. Vera and our team believe in the power of positivity. As we are the first experience many of our patients have with a dental clinic, positivity is critical to establishing a long-term relationship with oral health.

Education

At Pediatric Dentistry on Kimball, we promise to provide an educational experience where kids can learn about their oral health and establish good health habits to last a lifetime.

Honesty

Honesty goes hand-in-hand with trust. Dr. Vera is always transparent in his process to build trust, and in doing so, he becomes an ally for his patients in their journey to establish positive health habits.

Anxiety-Free Pediatric Dentist in Brooklyn, NY

To make your kid’s experience as comfortable as possible, Dr. Vera offers Nitrous Oxide. This is a safe and effective technique to help reduce anxiety. Commonly known as “laughing gas,” this mild sedative can aid communication as we provide quality care. If your kiddo needs extra help, attention, and dedication Pediatric Dentistry on Kimball is here to provide a positive dental experience tailored to their needs.
