The practice

After earning a degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio, Dr. Chitty has continued to pursue advanced training in Invisalign, implants, and cosmetic and restorative treatment, staying current with the latest techniques and technology. Known for a gentle approach and attention to detail, Dr. Chitty strives to make every visit positive and stress-free.

Outside the office, Dr. Chitty enjoys ballroom dancing, karaoke, and traveling. He values spending time with family and giving back to the community, volunteering with the Sexual Assault Center and Nashville Launch Pad.

Daphne is our Chief Operating Officer. She has been with Rivergate Dental for 25 years. During that time she has formed many great and valuable relationships with patients and colleagues alike. She takes great satisfaction with giving the patient the best experience they can have from the time they arrive until they depart from the office.

She and her husband are avid music collectors with over 2,000 vinyl records in their collection. She also enjoys traveling & singing and writing songs in her spare time.

Ericka is an Expanded Function Dental Assistant. She started her career in dentistry in 1993 and joined our team in 2021. Along with her Expanded Functions license, she has 20 years of experience in cosmetic and implant dentistry, IV and oral sedation, and dentures. Her goal is to build and maintain patient relationships by providing them with a comfortable, positive visit.

Ericka and her husband love spoiling their grandson and outside of work she enjoys quilting, cooking, and working in her flower garden.

Amanda is a Registered Dental Hygienist. She has been in practice for 15 years, since graduating with high honors and receiving her degree in Dental Hygiene of Allied Health Sciences. Considering herself to be a true dental nerd, she is always eager to take continuing education courses to stay current in her practice and provide quality, evidence-based care.

Of particular interest to her is dietary impact on oral health. As a patient advocate, she helps her patients to understand the state of their oral health, how it correlates to their whole-body health or disease and how they can modify their daily practices to achieve optimum oral health and an aesthetic smile.
