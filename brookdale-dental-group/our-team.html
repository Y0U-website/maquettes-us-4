Dr. Anthony G. DeVincentis, D.M.D.

Born and raised in Newark, New Jersey, Dr. Anthony G. DeVincentis (“Dr. Tony”) attended First Avenue Grammar School and Essex Catholic High School in Newark. He earned his Bachelor of Arts degree in Chemistry from Wilkes University (formerly Wilkes College) in Wilkes-Barre, PA.

He went on to receive his Doctor of Medical Dentistry degree from The University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey – New Jersey Dental School (Currently named Rutgers School of Dental Medicine).

After completing his training, Dr. Tony worked as an associate in the Scotch Plains, NJ area for four years before purchasing a dental practice in Upper Montclair in 1989. The practice expanded and, with his brother Dr. Vincent P. DeVincentis joining first as an associate and later as a partner, the office ultimately relocated to its current home in Bloomfield, New Jersey, becoming Brookdale Dental Group.

Dr. Tony is an active member of the Western Essex Study Club, Jersey Shore Study Club, and ITI Study Club. He also generously volunteers his time and dental services with Healing the Children Midlantic NA, through which he was honored with the prestigious Sergio Award for outstanding service and as a mentor for a High School student through the Student Partner Alliance.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Tony is an avid golfer who enjoys spending time on the course, along with relaxing at the Jersey Shore and working on cars. Much of his time is also devoted to his amazing wife, Jane, and his beautiful daughter, Francesca, enjoying family life whenever he is away from the office.

Dr. Vincent P. DeVincentis, D.M.D.

Born and raised in Newark, New Jersey, Dr. Vincent P. DeVincentis attended Seton Hall Preparatory School and earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Seton Hall University. He went on to earn his Doctor of Medical Dentistry degree in 1990 from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey, New Jersey Dental School (now Rutgers School of Dental Medicine).

Following graduation, Dr. Vinnie joined his brother, Dr. Anthony G. DeVincentis, as an associate in his Montclair practice. In 1994, the practice relocated to Bloomfield, New Jersey, where it became Brookdale Dental Group, and Dr. Vincent became a full partner alongside his brother.

Dr. Vinnie has dedicated his professional career to building a quality general dental practice that is truly patient-centered, treating every patient like family rather than simply a client. He is committed to providing the highest level of care in a comfortable and welcoming environment, and he truly enjoys helping patients improve and maintain both their dental health and their smiles. Dr. Vincent is an active member of the Western Essex Dental Association and remains committed to continuing education in order to provide the most advanced and up-to-date care for his patients.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Vinnie is deeply devoted to his family and enjoys spending time at his condo in Wildwood Crest. An avid reader, he especially enjoys relaxing with a good novel.

Known as “Coach Vin” by many throughout his hometown of Newark, New Jersey, Dr. Vincent P. DeVincentis has dedicated more than 40 years to coaching and leadership in North Newark Little League. In 2006, Dr. Vinnie and his late twin brother, Joseph, founded the Roseville Sports Group of North Newark, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization created to further support and expand opportunities for local youth through sports and community programs. Dr. Vinnie currently serves as President of both the North Newark Little League and the Roseville Sports Group of Newark.

He was also instrumental in bringing the MLB-RBI (Reviving Baseball in Inner Cities) Program to the youth of Newark, helping create meaningful opportunities for young athletes throughout the community. He currently serves as the program’s Director.

Annie Anderson, RDH

Annie, RDH began her career in the dental profession in 1985 as a dental assistant, building a strong foundation in patient care early on. She went on to earn her Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene in 1992 from Union County College, advancing her clinical skills and deepening her commitment to oral health. Annie has been a Dental Hygienist since 1992 and a valued member of the Brookdale Dental Group (BDG) family since 1995.

She has worked alongside Dr. Tony and Dr. Vinnie for over 30 years, building long-standing relationships with both her colleagues and her patients. Dr. Tony first met Annie when she was working as a dental assistant in Fanwood, where he began his career after graduating. He immediately recognized her talent, dedication, and strong work ethic, and didn’t hesitate to bring her onto his team as soon as the opportunity arose.

Throughout her years in practice, Annie has built lasting relationships with her patients, who appreciate her genuine warmth, kindness, and compassionate approach. She partners with each patient in their care, focusing strongly on prevention, education, and helping individuals understand how to maintain lifelong dental health. Her dedication and personable nature have made her an essential part of the Brookdale Dental Group family, and she is deeply respected by both patients and colleagues.

Outside of the office, Annie enjoys spending time with her husband and their sweet dog. She loves traveling, boating, and fishing, as well as going on long walks with her dog, enjoying weekly date nights with her husband, and spending as much time as possible with her nieces and nephews. Above all, Annie values time with the people she loves most and believes that is what matters most in life.
