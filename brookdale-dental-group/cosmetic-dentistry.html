Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry

Our cosmetic dental treatments are about more than restoring brightness to your smile. Each service is designed to help improve your oral health and give you renewed confidence in your grin. Since opening in 1989, our doctors and team have dedicated themselves to providing each patient with thorough, compassionate dental care. We incorporate advanced technology into our practice and complete continuing education courses to ensure we remain at the forefront of quality patient care .

Our cosmetic dentistry services include:

Veneers or laminates are applied directly to your teeth to correct misaligned or crooked teeth. This restoration is fabricated from chairside-bonded resin (plastic) or lab-processed porcelain. One of the greatest benefits of porcelain veneers is they are almost undetectable once applied because they take on the natural color of your teeth. They can help totally transform and renew your smile.

We offer patients both in-office and at-home whitening systems, and will work with you to determine which treatment best fits your needs. Our team has provided whitening services for a number of years, and we are committed to helping you achieve long-lasting, beautiful results, with minimal follow-up. For more information about our whitening services, please contact our office or speak with a member of our team during your next appointment.

Our dental bonding services can help straighten, close spaces, recontour, and change the color of your teeth. We use composite resins which are sculpted to fit your tooth and enhance your smile. This procedure is typically minimally invasive and results are obtained in one visit.

Providing Modern, Compassionate Care & Treatments

No matter how you are trying to correct or improve your smile, we will work with you to find the solution which fits your needs. It is our goal to restore strength, beauty, and structure to your teeth.

Our restorative dentistry services include:

Crowns are used to restore function and strength to a tooth that is badly worn, broken, or overfilled. In addition, this enhances cosmetics by restoring shape, contour, and color to the tooth. A crown can be made from a range of materials including porcelain, porcelain fused to a gold substructure, or all gold.

If you are missing one or more teeth, a dental bridge may be utilized to fill in the gaps in your smile. This keeps your remaining teeth from shifting, restores functionality, and increases esthetics. The bridge is permanently cemented to crowns on adjacent teeth.

Dental implants provide another tooth replacement option. Titanium posts are surgically inserted into the jawbone, and once the area as healed, the restoration is fabricated. We provide implant services to enhance the retention of full or partial dentures.

Our practice almost exclusively provides tooth-colored fillings and restorations. This type of restoration provides a tooth with renewed stability, strength, and durability.

To complement this restoration service and reduce the amount of drilling, we utilize the Nex Carries Detector. This is a non-invasive handheld device used to aid in the diagnosis of tooth decay. This system is 95 % accurate and aids in better early detection
