Frequently Asked Questions about Invisalign Clear Aligners

How long does the treatment take?

Treatment duration varies based on individual needs but typically ranges from 12 to 18 months.

Is Invisalign treatment painful?

You may experience mild discomfort when switching to a new aligner set as your teeth adjust.

Can I eat with my aligners in?

No, you should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water.
