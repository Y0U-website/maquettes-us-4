0 Meet Dr. Alyssa Kotin Dr. Alyssa Kotin is excited to serve the Whitinsville community as a Board Certified Orthodontist. A Central Massachusetts native, she is honored to continue Dr. Carrie Webb’s legacy of providing personalized, high-quality orthodontic care.

Dr. Kotin earned her Bachelor of Arts in Molecular Biology from Colgate University in Hamilton, NY, before returning to Boston to complete her Doctor of Dental Medicine and Certificate in Orthodontics at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. While at Tufts, she graduated cum laude, was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society, and received the Furman Scholarship Award in Orthodontics during her residency training.

With advanced training in a wide range of treatment modalities—including both braces and clear aligners—Dr. Kotin enjoys creating customized treatment plans to help each patient achieve a confident, healthy smile. She loves working with children, teens, and adults, and is committed to making the orthodontic experience positive and comfortable for every patient.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kotin enjoys traveling, exploring new restaurants, and spending time with her family and friends.
