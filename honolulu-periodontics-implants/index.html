Dental implants are the most predictable way to replace missing teeth but the outcome depends heavily on planning, surgical precision, and the quality of the supporting bone and gum.

At Honolulu Periodontics & Implants, we focus exclusively on implant surgery and periodontal care, allowing us to deliver highly controlled, long-term results.

Dental implants are titanium screws or posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone. Successful integration of the dental implants provide a sturdy foundation to support a fixed (crowns or bridges) or removable (dentures).
