Dental Bridges

  • Enhanced Aesthetics : They provide a natural-looking appearance by filling the gap.
  • Maintained Facial Structure : Bridges help in maintaining the shape of your face and preventing facial sagging.
  • Distributed Bite Forces : The bridge distributes the bite forces evenly across the neighboring teeth.

How Long Do Dental Bridges Last?

The lifespan of dental bridges can vary based on factors like oral hygiene, regular dental check-ups , and individual habits. On average, they can last between 5 to 15 years or even longer with proper care.
