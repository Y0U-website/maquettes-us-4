Meet your dentists

Dr. Kasra Tajik

Trained at USC. FAGD-credentialed. Deeply committed to your particular smile.

Dr. Kasra Tajik is a skilled cosmetic dentist with over 15 years of experience who blends his artistic precision with a genuine commitment to patient care. A graduate of the USC Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, Dr. Tajik also holds a Master’s degree from the UCLA Anderson School of Management. As a member of Omicron Kappa Upsilon and the American Dental Association, he brings world-class expertise to every patient interaction.

Focused on creating tailored solutions, Dr. Tajik is credentialed as a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD) and has specialty training in dental implant restoration, teeth-whitening, and porcelain veneers. By utilizing the latest technologies and advanced sedation techniques, he strives to ensure patients not only receive exceptional results but also feel at ease throughout their treatment.

His passion extends beyond the office. In 2013, Dr. Tajik joined a USC-led initiative in Kenya, providing much-needed dental care to underserved communities, treating over 300 patients in just three weeks. Whether he&#x27;s transforming smiles internationally or locally in North Hollywood, his unwavering dedication ensures every patient enjoys life with a confident, lasting smile.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Tajik enjoys skiing, motor car racing, and mountain biking. His love for innovation and adventure is the same energy he brings to nurturing remarkable patient relationships and exceptional outcomes.

Dr. Naji Hamoui

Burbank-raised. WesternU-trained. Dedicated to whole-person dental care.

Hamoui also has experience in cosmetic dentistry and is skilled in a wide range of dental treatments, including dental implants, tooth whitening, dental crowns, dental bridges and more. He is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the California Dental Association (CDA).

If you’re looking for a dentist in North Hollywood that offers top-quality care from an experienced practitioner, look no further than Dr. Naji Hamoui. Contact him today to schedule an appointment.

For Dr. Naji Hamoui, dental care is about more than just a smile—it’s about the health of the whole person. A Burbank native, Dr. Hamoui is proud to serve the community where he grew up. After graduating from UCLA and Western University of Health Sciences, he returned home to provide general and cosmetic dentistry to his neighbors in Burbank and North Hollywood.

Dr. Hamoui takes a uniquely integrative approach to his practice. By considering every aspect of a patient&#x27;s well-being, he ensures that every treatment plan is as comprehensive as it is effective. Whether he is performing complex cosmetic transformations or routine preventive care, his goal is to provide a comfortable, top-quality experience. He remains dedicated to the highest standards of the profession as a member of both the American Dental Association (ADA) and the California Dental Association (CDA).

When he isn’t helping patients achieve their best health, Dr. Hamoui stays active and practices the wellness he advocates for, prioritizing fitness and maintaining a healthy lifestyle. A self-proclaimed foodie, he loves exploring the diverse culinary scene in town or cooking up something special at home. On game nights, you’ll find him rooting loudly for the Lakers, but his favorite way to spend a weekend is simply enjoying quality time with his wife and children.

When you visit Dr. Hamoui, you’re not just a patient; you’re a partner in achieving your best health.

Faces behind Modern Smiles

A Team Committed to Excellence

At Modern Smiles, our team is united by a shared commitment to thoughtful, personalized care. From the moment you arrive, you’ll notice we are built on welcoming environment, attentive service, and a team genuinely invested in your experience.

Your first visit

what to expect

From the moment you arrive, your first visit is designed to feel welcoming, comfortable, and unrushed. You’ll be greeted by our team, introduced to our modern, relaxed space, and guided through your visit at a pace that feels right for you.

Patient Experience Your first visit begins with a review of your dental and medical history, followed by a discussion of your oral health goals. Our team will guide you through the exam process, ensuring you feel informed and cared for every step of the way.

Technology & Amenities

Enhancing Your CARE Experience

At Modern Smiles, advanced technology and thoughtfully curated amenities come together to elevate your experience. Leading-edge tools support precise, efficient care, while intentional comforts create a relaxed, welcoming environment from start to finish.

iTero Scanner

Our digital scanning technology eliminates the need for traditional impressions, offering images that make treatments like Invisalign more comfortable and efficient.

Diagnostic Imaging

Using 3D CBCT diagnostic imaging, we can diagnose and plan treatments with an unprecedented level of accuracy, ensuring better results tailored to your needs.

Comfort Amenities

Relax before and during your appointment with amenities such as HDTVs, chairside entertainment, warm towels, and refreshments to make your visit enjoyable.

Giving Back to Our Community

Dentistry done With Purpose

At Modern Smiles, giving back is an important part of who we are. In 2013, Dr. Tajik participated in a dental mission in Kenya, providing care to more than 300 patients, including many children receiving their first-ever dental visit.

That same spirit continues to guide our work today. Whether serving our local North Hollywood community or supporting outreach efforts abroad, we believe dentistry has the power to create meaningful impact one smile at a time.
