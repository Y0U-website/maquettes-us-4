Services

The experienced dental team at River City Dental Red Wing use the most advanced dental techniques to help you achieve a beautiful smile while enjoying the most comfortable treatment process possible.

In addition to the cosmetic and restorative treatments and we offer such as porcelain crowns and dental implants, we offer comprehensive general dentistry options so that you can maintain the health of your teeth and gums. These treatments can address a number of dental issues and work to prevent future problems. Our goal is to make your dental treatment as effective and comfortable as possible while keeping you in “the drivers seat”.

Please browse through the many advanced treatment options available at our office, and contact us today for more information.

General & Cosmetic Services

Dental Examinations

At your initial exam and every cleaning appointment, we will perform a comprehensive dental exam. We examine the teeth and check for cavities or areas showing abnormal wear. We often take Digital X-Rays, which allow us to see the areas in between or under the teeth. At these exams, we also check for oral cancers or ulcers on the gums, tongue or lips. A head and neck cancer screening is also conducted. Lastly, we assess your jaw to check for any joint dysfunctions. Dr. Steger and Dr. Cuevas recommend you have a comprehensive dental exam twice a year.

Dental Cleanings & Gum Treatments

We have many levels of periodontal care and offer a variety of cleanings and gum treatments to fit any patient’s need. After your initial exam, Dr. Steger or Dr. Cuevas will recommend the type of dental cleaning or gum treatment that is right for you.

Dental Sealants

A sealant is a clear BPA-free plastic coating brushed and cured to the chewing surface of your teeth to prevent cavities from forming in the deep pits and grooves.

Dental Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride helps prevent tooth decay by making the tooth more resistant to acid attacks from plaque bacteria and sugars in the mouth. It also reverses early decay.

Pediatric Dentistry

River City Dental is proud to provide Pediatric dental care in Red Wing. Dr. Cuevas and her assistants Heather and Shelby strive to give every child a bright and healthy smile. Check out our “No Cavities Club” that kids love. Make an appointment with us for pediatric dentistry today and see why we’ve been the local choice for families since 1993.

Dr. Steger and Cuevas have a strong belief in patient education and preventative dental care. It’s even more important to start this education and preventative care at a young age. At River City Dental we believe in teaching your child how to properly maintain their teeth so that future dental problems such as cavities do not occur.

With our help, you can make your child’s first visit to the dentist positive and enjoyable. You should inform your child about the visit and tell them that our staff will explain all procedures to them. We happily invite you to stay with your child during their visit. There we can work as a team to ensure that your child takes the first steps in proper dental hygiene.

Advanced Techniques

Composite Fillings

Our dental team will perform a consultation with you to review your current needs with respect to your specific filling needs.

Sealants

Our team will perform the application of sealants during one of your bi-yearly regular checkups.

Cosmetic Treatment

River City Dental can correct imperfections in the appearance of the your mouth.

Crowns and Bridges

Crowns and bridges are used to restore and enhance teeth that are damaged, or to take the place of missing teeth.

Root Canals

Root canal treatment (also referred to as root canal therapy or endodontic therapy) is made necessary when a cavity is allowed, through neglect, to reach all the way to this pulp.

Dentures and Implants

A denture is a removable replacement for missing teeth and surrounding tissues. There are two types of dentures available, including partial and complete dentures.

Snoring Appliances

Snoring can be a real problem, not only for the person who snores, but for the partner and other family members who live with the snorer.

TMJ Protection

At River City Dental our team custom designs mouth guards and night guards for you, which are made of flexible plastic and molded to fit the shape of your teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Tooth whitening can brighten up your entire face, and it is a procedure that is constantly in demand.

Inlays & Onlays

Inlays and onlays are known as indirect fillings, because unlike a standard filling that is done right in our office, both are custom made at our dental laboratory and cemented or bonded to the surface of the tooth by Dr. Steger or Dr. Cuevas .
