Patient Info

Our doors are always open to welcome new patients who are ready to receive the best in dental care.

With our array of dental services, it’s no surprise we are regularly expanding our family of patients. We provide complete family dental care and services to patients from all dental backgrounds and make every effort to ensure your appointment is as comfortable as possible. We provide superior care to get you onto the path of improved dental health.

Forms

Please take a moment to print and complete your patient forms prior to your dental appointment. You can email, mail, or bring your forms with you to your initial appointment. If you would prefer to complete your forms in our office, please arrive 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment time.

Dental Insurance, Payments & Financial Arrangements

We are in network for Delta Dental , Delta Dental Premier , and we also work with most other dental insurance providers. However, we are not taking any new patients with Medical Assistance, Minnesota Care, or other state funded insurance plans at this time.

We accept Visa, MasterCard, and Discover and we offer patient financing through CareCredit .
