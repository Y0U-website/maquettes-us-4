Dental Services

Comprehensive Dental Care

Whole Mouth Exam and checkup From your first appointment, a complete and comprehensive examination of the mouth and related organs will be performed, which will allow us a complete and accurate diagnosis, to propose the most efficient treatment plan.

Oral Hygiene, cleanings, sealants, fillings, and extractions These basic services are offered with the appropriate technologies and materials from the clinical and aesthetic point of view.

Dental crowns, bridges, dentures, and full mouth reconstruction. The design of these products meets the most demanding standard, worked by top-level laboratories that ensure the best quality, functionality, durability, and aesthetics.

Root canal therapy This therapy is practiced with the greatest safety and best results. Allows to preserve the tooth with all its structure and functions.

Orthodontics, Braces and Invisalign. Adults, teenagers, and children will be able to correct their teeth and design them as desired, to display a beautiful smile and achieve the required functionality with the latest techniques, materials, and the most efficient corrective program.

Implant placement and restoration Our dental implants allow to recover the functions and aesthetics of lost teeth with therapeutic programs, technologies and latest generation products that guarantee the best results in the shortest time at the lowest cost.

Dental Surgery Some oral problems, as wisdom teeth, require surgery treatment that are performed in the most quiet and safe conditions.

Cosmetic dentistry, Veneers, and Whitening The Smile Design allows you to feel comfortable and happy with the look achieved. Aesthetic, cultural, and social demands impose the need to improve our image. Kendall Complete Dental satisfies the highest demands of the state of the art and makes possible the appearance changes that you need.

Emergency dental care We offer emergency dental care during office hours. If you experience a dental emergency, call us, we are here to help you. If the dental health is at risk, we will do everything to take care of you as soon as possible. While dental emergencies are rare, they can happen, and it’s important to know how to take care of your teeth.

Promotional and Preventive behaviors and Oral Cancer screenings Oral health promotion is provided by our professionals for all patients, aiming to improve their oral health and general wellbeing using tailored oral health actions and messages. Primary prevention focuses on maintaining good habits in oral health to avoid or decrease the frequency of caries and periodontal diseases.

Advanced Dental Technology

Digital X-rays provide advanced imaging options in real time, clearer photos, less time, and less radiation.

Intraoral Camera allows to see every detail of your teeth and mouth as uncovering cracked teeth, plaque deposits, and incipient cavities.

Laboratory support

Kendall Complete Dental fully understands the importance of the laboratory that makes crowns, bridges, veneers, and other dental prosthetics. Each patient has unique structural, functional, and aesthetic requirements, so we work closely with the lab and technicians to customize all restorations.

Lab uses the highest quality materials and latest technologies to ensure the accuracy and aesthetics of the restorations and the precision of the fit prior to placement in mouth.

Insurance

We accept most major credit cards and PPO Insurance plans. For patients with larger treatment needs, we’ll find a comfortable and feasible way to finance your treatment.
