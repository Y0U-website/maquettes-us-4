Meet Your Denton Periodontist

Naveen Karim, DDS

Dr. Karim studied at UT Dallas before pursuing her doctorate at Baylor College of Dentistry. Today, she has been working in this field for over four years and counting. She loves being able to save teeth through her dedicated efforts, and treating such wonderful patients is always a pleasure.

Dr. Karim is originally from Copenhagen, Denmark, and she speaks five languages! She and her husband have been married for 10 years. They have a son together who is three. When she isn’t busy at work, you can find her biking, traveling, trying new restaurants, and watching movies.
