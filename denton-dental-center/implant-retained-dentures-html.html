What Are Implant Dentures?

While technology has made regular dentures look and feel better than ever, you will inevitably deal with slipping and irritation. Not to mention, you will need to have them replaced every few years. Dental implants resolve the common complaints of traditional dentures. By surgically placing a small number of implant posts into your jawbone, your denture is firmly secured in place. Depending on your specific circumstances, preferences, and needs, Dr. Balington may recommend one of the two kinds of implant dentures:

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

An implant denture can be permanently fixed in place, only able to be removed by an implant dentist in Denton. This allows you to brush and floss your replacement teeth as if they were your real ones. Fixed implant dentures usually require four to six implant posts and a greater amount of healthy jawbone density, but they deliver more long-term flexibility.

Removable Implant Dentures

Like regular dentures, implant dentures can be removable for cleaning purposes. Removable implant dentures tend to only require two to five implant posts, making it possible to receive them even if you have slightly less jawbone density.

The Implant Denture Process

Although getting implant dentures takes longer than receiving a conventional denture, the long-term benefits of implant dentures make them worth the wait!

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Dental implants are versatile enough for almost all healthy adults to benefit from them. That said, good candidates for implant dentures usually have the following qualities:

  • Adequate overall health (enough so to undergo minor oral surgery).
  • Willingness to complete any necessary prep work, such as bone grafting.
  • Commitment to maintaining good dental hygiene after receiving implants.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Because dental implants in Denton mimic your original tooth roots, implant dentures provide several benefits that are not possible with traditional dentures, including:

  • A success rate of more than 95 percent.
  • Natural-looking smile that allows you to smile confidently.
  • Teeth that reliably stay in place.
  • Simulation of your jawbone, which prevents premature sagging or wrinkling of your facial features.
  • Restored chewing power to eat your favorite foods.
  • Easy maintenance no messy adhesives required!
  • A cost-effective solution that should last for 30+ years without having to be replaced.
