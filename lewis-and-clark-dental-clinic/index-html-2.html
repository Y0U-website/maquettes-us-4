  • Sign Up for Classes Registering for courses at Lewis and Clark is easy and convenient. New students will need to apply before continuing. You MUST see an academic advisor before registering if: You’re a new student.
  • You’re a returning student wishing to change your degree or certificate program.
  • You’re on academic probation or are returning from academic suspension.

Ways to Register

Your adviser can enroll you in classes if you choose, or you can enroll yourself online, in person or over the phone.

Online

To register online, you must meet all the following criteria.

If you do not meet the criteria, please visit your academic advisor .

  • Have a BlazerNet ID
  • Be a returning student
  • Be in good academic standing
  • Be informed about the courses you need for graduation and the modes of instruction that are available to you.

By Phone

Call (618) 468-2222 or 1-800-YES-LCCC (1-800-937-5222).

In Person

Contact your academic advisor or email enroll@lc.edu to schedule an appointment.
