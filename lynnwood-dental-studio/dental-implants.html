When Should You Consider Dental Implants?

Common reasons to consider dental implants in Lynnwood include:

  • Missing one or more teeth due to trauma, decay, or aging.
  • Severe tooth decay or damage that cannot be repaired.
  • Poorly fitted dentures causing discomfort or frustration.
  • Advanced periodontal disease resulting in tooth loss.
  • Desire for a durable, natural-feeling alternative to removable dentures.
