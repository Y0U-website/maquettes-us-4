Dental Implant Aftercare for…

If you have any concerns or questions about your recovery or post-operative care, please contact our office to speak with our team or schedule a follow-up appointment. Your healing and comfort are our priority.

  • Dental Implants Dental Implants Post-Implant Care Instructions To support a smooth recovery and reduce the risk of complications following your implant placement, it is important to carefully follow these post-operative care instructions: Do not rinse your mouth for the remainder of the day after your procedure. Starting the day after, rinse gently with warm salt water (½ teaspoon of salt in 8 oz. of water) every 2-3 hours for several days. Afterward, rinse after meals and at bedtime for two weeks.
  • Take prescribed medications, antibiotics, and mouth rinses exactly as directed. Use mild over-the-counter pain relievers such as Tylenol, Advil, or ibuprofen to alleviate discomfort.
  • Begin brushing your teeth the day after the procedure, but avoid the surgical site for several days to prevent irritation.
  • If a temporary crown was placed, avoid biting down on it until cleared by our dentist.
  • Refrain from wearing dentures, partials, or other oral appliances until permitted by your dentist.
  • Swelling is normal. For the first 48 hours, apply ice or cold compresses to the side of your face in 15-minute intervals. After 48 hours, switch to heat until the swelling subsides.
  • Use lip balm or Vaseline to keep your lips moist and prevent cracking.
  • Avoid operating machinery for at least 12 hours following your procedure.
  • Do not smoke or use tobacco products, as they can compromise the healing process and lead to implant failure.
  • If the cover screw or healing abutment becomes loose or falls out, contact our office immediately. Bring the component to your appointment for replacement.
  • If you use oral contraceptives and were prescribed antibiotics, use an alternative method of birth control during and until completing the antibiotics course.
