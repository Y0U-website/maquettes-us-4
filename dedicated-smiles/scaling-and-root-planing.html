Scaling & Root Planing Treatment

By addressing the source of infection and irritation, this procedure helps preserve your natural teeth and improve overall oral wellness.

  • Stops the progression of periodontal disease
  • Removes harmful bacteria that cause tooth decay and bad breath
  • Prevents gum recession and bone loss
  • Promotes a healthier and more confident smile

How the Procedure is Performed

At Dedicated Smiles , we prioritize patient comfort during every scaling and root planning. Local anesthetics or sedation options are available for patients who require additional comfort. The procedure may be performed in quadrants, addressing one section of the mouth at a time for thorough cleaning and minimal discomfort.

The treatment may also be combined with adjunctive therapies, such as:

  • Antibiotic therapy to eliminate residual bacteria
  • Guided tissue regeneration to support gum and bone health
  • Periodontal maintenance for long-term care and prevention
