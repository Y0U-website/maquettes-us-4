Veneers

Veneers are one of the most transformative treatments we offer, and one of the ones Dr. Hulver enjoys most. Custom-crafted to cover chips, gaps, staining, or uneven shape, veneers are designed case-by-case so the final result complements your face, not just your teeth.

Meticulous planning: Every case starts with digital photos and a detailed discussion of shape, shade, and proportion

Built to last: We use high-quality materials selected for durability as well as beauty

A smile that’s still yours: The goal is always a natural, elevated result, never an overdone one

Cosmetic Bonding

For smaller corrections, a chip, a gap, or an uneven edge, bonding is a conservative, same-visit way to refine your smile without reshaping healthy tooth structure.

Minimally invasive: Bonding preserves as much of your natural tooth as possible

Fast, effective results: Most bonding is completed in a single visit

Seamlessly matched: Tooth-colored material is shaped and polished to blend with your smile

Clear Aligners

Straightening your smile shouldn’t mean putting your life on hold. Metropolitan Dental Centre offers CandidPro clear aligners, a doctor-directed system that starts with an in-person exam with Dr. Hulver, not a mail-order impression kit. From there, much of your treatment can be monitored remotely, which is unique to the brand and works especially well for busy working patients.

Doctor-directed from day one: treatment begins with an in-person exam and a plan built by Dr. Hulver

Progress checks from home: between visits, you photograph your teeth about every 14 days using the CandidApp and a CandidScan device

FDA-cleared remote monitoring: CandidMonitoring, included standard on every case, uses AI to flag tracking, hygiene, and compliance issues and routes them to Dr. Hulver

Fewer office visits, same oversight: you spend less time in the chair without giving up dentist supervision

General Dentistry

Good dentistry starts with the fundamentals, and we hold ourselves to a high standard on all of them. Whether it’s been six months or a few years since your last visit, you’ll be met here with zero judgment, clear communication, and a plan that makes sense.

Cleanings & Exams

Your regular visit will be done thoroughly. We’ll check your teeth and gums, review any needed imaging, and give you a genuinely thorough cleaning.

Fillings

When a cavity needs treatment, we restore it with strong, tooth-colored materials and give you honest, unhurried recommendations, never upsold, never rushed.

Extractions

Sometimes removing a tooth is the healthiest option. When it is, we’ll walk you through exactly why, what to expect, and what comes next, with a gentle approach throughout.

Root Canals

A root canal can relieve pain while saving your natural tooth. We remove the infection, seal the tooth to prevent future issues, and keep you comfortable and informed the entire time.

Gum Disease Treatment

Healthy gums are the foundation on which everything else is built. If gum disease is present, we focus on calming inflammation, halting progression, and protecting your teeth with a supportive, judgment-free approach.

Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth, or several, need more than a filling, restorative treatment rebuilds strength, comfort, and confidence without losing sight of how your smile looks.

Dental Crowns

A crown protects a damaged or weakened tooth and restores it to full function. Ours are designed to look completely natural and built to last for years.

Bridges

A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth and keeps your bite stable, preventing the shifting that can follow tooth loss.

Dentures

Modern dentures can be comfortable, stable, and natural-looking. We take the time to fit them properly so you can eat and smile with confidence again.
