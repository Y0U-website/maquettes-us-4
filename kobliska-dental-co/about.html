Dr. James Sunwoo

Dr. James Sunwoo enjoys helping people and providing excellent care on an elite level at Kobliska Dental Co. Besides being a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon, Dr. Sunwoo also focuses on cosmetic surgery and orthognathic surgery. He earned his medical doctorate degree from the University of Southern California School of Medicine and his dental doctorate degree from the Columbia University School of Dental Medicine. In his free time, Dr. Sunwoo enjoys spending time with family and friends, hiking, traveling, and watching his favorite team, the Los Angeles Lakers.
