Dental Insurance Our office is proud to accept most major insurance plans. We always strive to help our patients maximize their dental benefits to help minimize what they have to pay out-of-pocket.

Our knowledgeable team can file, track, and update your family’s insurance claims so you can focus less on paperwork and more on your oral health. For specific information regarding the coverage you may receive, we recommend reaching out to your provider directly.

Our staff would be happy to answer any other additional questions!

Flexible Financing It’s not uncommon for patients to want to split their treatment costs into manageable monthly payments, especially for services like orthodontics or unexpected emergencies. Our practice accepts Proceed Financing , Cherry Payment Plans and CareCredit which can be used to divide the cost of any treatment, including cosmetic services, into a flexible payment plan. For patients who do not want to go through a third-party, we also offer in-house financing, which allows patients to customize their payment plans even further.
