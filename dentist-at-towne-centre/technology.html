Technology

Technology

We use advanced dental technologies to ensure precise, efficient, and comfortable treatments. Our equipment is safe, highly effective, and meticulously maintained. Here are some of the cutting-edge tools we utilize:

Digital X-Rays

At Dentist at Towne Centre in West Chester, OH, we utilize advanced digital X-rays to enhance and magnify your teeth for precise diagnosis and treatment. This cutting-edge technology allows us to capture detailed images of your teeth and display them instantly on a computer screen.

With digital X-rays, we can examine your teeth and surrounding structures with exceptional clarity, ensuring accurate treatment planning. Additionally, they emit significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays, making them a safer and more effective diagnostic tool.

Panoramic X-Ray

A panoramic X-ray machine captures a comprehensive, 360-degree view of your teeth, sinuses, and jawbone by rotating around your head. This advanced imaging technology provides a more complete picture than standard X-rays, allowing for a thorough evaluation of your oral health.

Dr. Mandeep Pannu utilizes panoramic X-ray images as a crucial tool for accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning.

Intra-oral Camera

The intraoral camera is a compact, pen-shaped device designed to capture detailed images of your teeth, gums, mouth, and tongue from inside your mouth. Its small size and sleek design allow for comfortable use while providing clear visuals from multiple angles. This safe and effective tool is suitable for patients of all ages.

The intraoral camera plays a crucial role in both diagnosis and treatment planning. To ensure your health and safety, we use a new disposable cover for every patient. After your exam, the captured images can be digitally stored, printed, or deleted as needed.

Cavitron 300 Series

Discover the latest breakthrough in the Cavitron family of ultrasonic scaling systems—a solution that redefines comfort and precision. Designed to elevate every procedure, this system features a cutting-edge digital operating mechanism that minimizes heat output, reducing water consumption for improved patient comfort.

Additionally, its quieter operation ensures a more serene experience, setting it apart from other ultrasonic systems. Embrace the perfect balance of advanced technology and effortless control with this remarkable addition to the Cavitron lineup

Nomad™ X-Ray Gun

The Nomad™ handheld X-ray system is a fully portable, battery-powered device, offering complete cordless convenience.

With the Nomad™ , we can capture precise and efficient scans while minimizing radiation exposure compared to traditional systems. Designed for comfort and ease of use, this innovative technology allows for seamless imaging in any treatment room.

Rotary Endo Handpiece

The Rotary Endo Handpiece is a cutting-edge dental instrument designed to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of endodontic procedures.

Engineered with advanced technology, this handpiece offers unparalleled control and reliability, making it an essential tool for dental professionals.

Gemini EVO™ Laser

The Gemini EVO laser is a cutting-edge tool that combines powerful performance with versatile functionality. Delivering a peak power of 100 watts through super-pulsed technology, it ensures faster cutting, reduced heat, and remarkably clean soft tissue incisions.

Equipped with dual wavelengths (810 nm and 980 nm), the laser offers three operational modes, as the wavelengths can be utilized independently or in tandem. To streamline your workflow, the Gemini EVO features 16 preset procedures categorized for efficient and user-friendly application.

iTero Scanner

At Dentist at Towne Centre, we use the iTero Scanner to comfortably create digital images of your teeth. This advanced technology replaces the need for messy molds, providing faster and clearer impressions for procedures like Invisalign, crowns, and bridges. You’ll enjoy quicker appointments, better-fitting treatments, and an overall more comfortable dental experience.

TRIOS 3Shape Scanner

We proudly offer the TRIOS 3Shape Scanner, a fast and accurate tool that captures detailed, full-color images of your teeth. This scanner helps our dentists quickly plan treatments like crowns, implants, and orthodontics without uncomfortable molds. You’ll experience shorter visits, precise treatment plans, and clearly see how we can improve your smile.
