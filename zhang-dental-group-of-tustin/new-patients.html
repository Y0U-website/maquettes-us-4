New Patients

Your first visit, without surprises

Trying a new dentist shouldn’t feel like a gamble. Here’s exactly what to expect at Zhang Dental Group of Tustin.

(714) 836-5600 Text Us Book Online What your first visit looks like You’ll be seen promptly, because we value your time. What happens next depends on what you came for.

If you want a full picture of your oral health, we’ll do a comprehensive exam: digital X-rays, an intraoral scan, and a careful look at your teeth and gums. Then we’ll sit down and talk through what we found, answering every question before recommending anything.

If you came in for one specific problem, we’ll focus on that. No surprise add-ons, no treatment plan for things you didn’t ask about. You decide what happens next.

The technology working for you

Digital X-rays use less radiation and give us sharper images than film. Intraoral scans replace goopy impressions and let you see what we see, on screen, in real time. And if you prefer to handle scheduling and communication online, we’re set up for that too.

Here’s how we keep costs clear

Dental insurance

We’re in-network with most PPO plans. Before any treatment, we’ll tell you what your insurance covers, so the price never catches you off guard.

Membership plan

No insurance? Our DentalHQ membership plan keeps preventive care affordable, with member savings on additional treatments like whitening or cosmetic work.

Financing and payment plans

We accept Cherry , which lets you split your treatment into monthly payments, and we offer payment plans for clear aligner patients pursuing Invisalign . If you have another financing need, ask. We’re open to finding a way that works.

A practice your whole family can settle into

Many of our patients start with a single cleaning and end up bringing their spouse and kids. That’s by design. We treat all ages, and our services cover everything from children’s checkups to crowns and dentures, so your family’s care stays in one place with a team that knows you.
