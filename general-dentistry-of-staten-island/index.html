The practice

General Dentistry of Staten Island sits at 2936 Hylan Boulevard in Oakwood , where there has been a dental office for about 30 years. After a stretch under corporate ownership, Dr. Nicholas Diaz, DDS bought the practice and returned it to private, family ownership.

That change matters to you. One dentist knows your name, follows your care from the first exam to the final crown, and recommends treatment because you need it, not because a quota says so. We work as a fee for service practice, which keeps those decisions between you and Dr. Diaz.

From routine cleanings to full mouth reconstruction, Dr. Diaz handles a wide range of care under one roof, so most patients never need to be sent across town.

Lafayette College · Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry

Dr. Diaz grew up a few blocks from this office and graduated from Monsignor Farrell High School in Staten Island. He earned his Bachelor’s degree at Lafayette College in Easton, Pennsylvania, then his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry in Richmond, Virginia.

He completed a General Practice Residency at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital in New Brunswick, New Jersey, working alongside leading providers across oral surgery, endodontics, crown and bridge, and care for medically complex patients. He has authored two publications in the Journal of Dental Education and remains a committed lifelong learner.

After years of building his skills outside Staten Island, Dr. Diaz brought his care back to the community that raised him. When he is not at the office, he spends his time with his wife and family, who are part of this neighborhood too.

New patients are welcome. Call the office and our front desk will take care of the rest.
