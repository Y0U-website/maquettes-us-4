Are all-on-4 dental implants worth it?

Dental implants are an investment in your smile and oral health. All-on-4 dental implants are the most recommended solution for full-mouth restoration.

  • Stability: Dental implants secure your teeth firmly in the jawbone. Don’t expect embarrassing slippage when talking or eating.
  • Durability: The all-on-4 technique is a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. With good care, dental implants can last several decades.
  • Aesthetics: We restore your dental implants with materials that mimic your natural teeth in almost every way.
  • Simple maintenance: Dental implants are maintained like natural teeth—no need for cumbersome maintenance techniques.
  • Jawbone preservation: Dental implants stimulate the jawbone to regenerate, preventing bone loss, which is synonymous with prolonged tooth loss.
  • Affordable: The all-on-4 technique is costlier than traditional dentures but cheaper than replacing every missing tooth with an implant. Plus, patients rarely need expensive grafting techniques.
