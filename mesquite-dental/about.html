Meet the Doctors

Dr. Karley Schneider

Dr. Karley Schneider grew up in Arizona where she developed a love for the desert landscapes and met her husband Luke. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Arizona before earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Oregon Health and Science University. Dr. Schneider has also trained with the American Academy for Facial Esthetics and received the Award for Leadership from the American Dental Association.

In 2016 Dr. Schneider began working with her close mentor and friend, Dr. Robert Brei. During her time with Dr. Brei, she developed close relationships with the patients and team. When Dr. Brei retired in 2022, Dr. Schneider was honored to continue the work and culture her mentor began as Mesquite Dental.

Dr. Schneider has been practicing Dentistry since 2014 and is passionate about helping her patients maintain functional and aesthetically beautiful teeth in a comfortable and fun environment. She recognizes the importance of continuing dental education and is working towards her Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Schneider and her husband Luke have two wonderful young children and spend their time traveling, swimming and enjoying the sights and shops in Tucson. Dr. Schneider’s daughter Lily was diagnosed with type-1 diabetes at two years old, and their family works regularly with the Juvenile Diabetes Research Foundation to support life saving research. She hopes to one day find a cure.
