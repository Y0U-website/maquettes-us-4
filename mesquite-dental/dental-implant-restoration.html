What Is Dental Implant Restoration?

Dental implant restoration refers to the process of placing the crown, bridge, or denture on top of a surgically inserted titanium implant post. This is typically the final step in your dental implant journey.

Already have an implant but the crown is worn or damaged? We also specialize in implant crown replacement and maintenance.

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Restorations

How long does a dental implant restoration last?

With proper care, implant-supported restorations can last 15 years or more. The titanium post often lasts a lifetime, while the crown or bridge may need periodic replacement.

Does the procedure hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Restorations are non-surgical, and we ensure a gentle process through the use of modern techniques and local anesthesia.

Can I get a new crown on an old implant?

Yes. If your existing implant post is healthy and stable, we can design and place a new custom crown to replace a worn or damaged one.

What if I lost multiple teeth?

Implant-supported bridges or dentures can be used to replace several missing teeth at once. This is often a better long-term alternative to removable dentures.
