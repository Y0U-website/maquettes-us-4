What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone where teeth are missing. Over time, the implant integrates with the bone—a process called osseointegration—creating a strong foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

Unlike removable dentures, implants offer a permanent, fixed solution that doesn’t rely on other teeth for support. Learn more about how we restore implants with custom dental implant restorations .

  • The implant post, which replaces the tooth root
  • The abutment, which connects the implant to the restoration
  • The restoration, which may be a dental crown, bridge, or denture

Benefits of Dental Implants

Patients choose dental implants for their durability, aesthetics, and comfort. Benefits include:

Implants also help protect the health of surrounding teeth by eliminating the need for shaving them down for a traditional dental bridge .

  • Improved chewing and speech function
  • Natural appearance that blends with existing teeth
  • Preservation of jawbone density and facial structure
  • No slipping or discomfort like with traditional dentures
  • Long-term value with proper care

What to Expect During the Implant Process

Getting dental implants is a multi-step process, carefully planned for long-term success:

We use modern surgical techniques to ensure minimal discomfort and offer ongoing support throughout your treatment.

  • Consultation and digital imaging : We start with a full exam and advanced scans using digital impressions to plan placement.
  • Implant surgery : The titanium implant is surgically placed in the jaw under local anesthesia.
  • Healing and integration : Over several months, the implant fuses with the jawbone.
  • Abutment and restoration : Once healed, we attach the abutment and place a final custom crown, bridge, or denture.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last? With good hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last decades or even a lifetime. The restoration (crown or denture) may need occasional replacement due to normal wear.

Is dental implant surgery painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, and recovery is typically smoother than expected.

Can I get a dental implant if I’ve been missing a tooth for years? Yes. We often treat patients with long-term tooth loss. In some cases, additional procedures like bone grafting may be needed before implant placement.

How much do dental implants cost? Costs vary depending on the number of implants and whether additional procedures are required. We offer transparent pricing and guidance through your insurance and payment options .

What if I’m missing all my teeth? Full-arch implant solutions, such as implant-retained dentures, can replace all upper or lower teeth with just a few implants. This option offers more comfort and stability than traditional dentures.
