The practice

We take pride in offering the latest orthodontic treatments and technologies available for children, teens, and adults. We offer a full range of braces to choose from, including metal braces , ceramic braces , and Invisalign clear aligners.

Dr. Casey O’Conor is a graduate of The Ohio State University, where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery with honors. He completed a general practice residency with the United States Air Force in Saint Louis and practiced general dentistry for two years in Panama City, Florida.

Dr. O’Conor received his specialty certification and a master’s degree in orthodontics from the State University of New York in Buffalo. He has been practicing orthodontics in Cleveland for more than 30 years. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. Only 20 percent of orthodontists have achieved this status.

Dr. O’Conor is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, Great Lakes Association of Orthodontists, Cleveland Society of Orthodontists, American Dental Association, Greater Cleveland Dental Society, Lorain County Dental Society, West Shore Study Club, Composite Study Club, and Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society.

He is actively involved in numerous civic organizations, including the Cleveland YMCA, Chamber of Commerce, Kamm’s Corners Development Corporation, and Rotary International. Our office was named North Ridgeville Business of the Year for our community involvement.

Dr. O’Conor is married to Kathy and has five children. He likes to spend his free time with his family, watching sports, and occasionally playing golf.
