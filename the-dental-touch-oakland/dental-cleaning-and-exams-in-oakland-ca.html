A comprehensive dental exam includes several important components. First, we review your medical history and discuss any concerns or changes in your oral health. Then we perform a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, tongue, throat, and jaw.

We check each tooth for signs of decay, cracks, or damage, and use digital X-rays when needed to see between teeth and below the gum line. We evaluate your gums for signs of periodontal disease, examine your bite and jaw function, and screen for oral cancer. Finally, we discuss our findings, answer your questions, and create a personalized treatment plan if any issues are detected.

A regular dental cleaning (prophylaxis) removes plaque and tartar from the visible surfaces of your teeth and just below the gum line. This maintenance cleaning is appropriate for patients with healthy gums and is typically completed in one appointment.

A deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) is a therapeutic procedure for patients with gum disease. It removes tartar and bacteria from deep pockets below the gum line and smooths root surfaces to help gums heal and reattach. Deep cleanings usually require local anesthesia and may be completed over multiple visits. During your exam, we’ll assess your gum health and recommend the appropriate type of cleaning.
