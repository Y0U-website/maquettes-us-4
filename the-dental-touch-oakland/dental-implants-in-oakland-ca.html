Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support and actually stimulate your jawbone to prevent the bone loss that occurs after tooth loss. The implant becomes a permanent part of your jaw, and the restoration attached to it looks, feels, and functions just like a natural tooth. Dental implants are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement because of their durability, natural appearance, and ability to preserve oral health.

Most adults with missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have adequate jawbone density, healthy gums, and good overall health. We evaluate your bone structure, medical history, and oral health during your consultation to determine if implants are right for you.

If you have insufficient bone, we may recommend bone grafting to build adequate foundation before implant placement. Certain health conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or active gum disease need to be managed before proceeding with implants.

Even if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate for traditional implants, All-on-4 may still be an option - the angled implant placement often works even when bone density is reduced. The best way to know if you’re a candidate is to schedule a consultation. We’ll perform a comprehensive evaluation and discuss all your tooth replacement options.

The dental implants procedure typically happens in stages over several months. First, we surgically place the titanium implant post into your jawbone under local anesthesia. This appointment is usually comfortable, and most patients return to normal activities within a few days.

Over the next 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone through osseointegration, creating a solid foundation. During this healing period, you may wear a temporary restoration. Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment (connector piece) to the implant and place your custom crown, bridge, or denture.

For All-on-4 implants, the process is faster - we often place the implants and attach a temporary full arch of teeth in a single day, so you leave with a complete smile. The permanent restoration is placed after healing is complete. Throughout the entire process, we ensure your comfort and keep you informed about what to expect.

All-on-4 is an innovative implant technique that replaces an entire arch of teeth using just four strategically placed implants. The implants are positioned at precise angles to maximize contact with your existing bone, eliminating the need for bone grafting in most cases.

A full arch of fixed teeth attaches to these four implants, providing a permanent, non-removable solution that looks and functions like natural teeth. Unlike traditional dentures that slip, click, and require messy adhesives, All-on-4 implants stay firmly in place while you eat, speak, and smile.

The All-on-4 procedure can often be completed in one day - we place the implants and attach a temporary set of teeth in a single visit, so you never go without teeth. After healing (usually 3-6 months), we replace the temporary teeth with your permanent, custom-crafted restoration. All-on-4 is ideal for patients missing all or most teeth in an arch, or those with failing teeth that need full-arch replacement.

Dental implants offer significant advantages over traditional dentures. Implants are permanently fixed in your mouth - they don’t slip, click, or require removal for cleaning. You can eat all your favorite foods without worry, speak clearly without adjustments, and never deal with messy denture adhesives.

Dentures implants (implant-supported dentures) combine the best of both worlds - the stability of implants with the affordability of dentures. Even a few implants dramatically improve denture retention and comfort.

Traditional removable dentures rest on your gums and can cause bone loss over time, changing your facial structure and requiring frequent adjustments or replacements. Dental implants preserve and stimulate jawbone, maintaining your facial structure and preventing the "sunken" appearance that often develops with dentures. While implants cost more initially, they typically last a lifetime with proper care, making them more cost-effective long-term than dentures that need replacing every 5-10 years.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium implant post that fuses with your jawbone is designed to be a permanent part of your mouth. The crown, bridge, or denture attached to the implant may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself typically lasts decades.

Implant longevity depends on good oral hygiene, regular dental checkups, and healthy lifestyle habits. Patients who brush and floss daily, avoid smoking, and visit us for professional cleanings have the highest success rates - over 95 % of dental implants are still functioning perfectly after 10 years.

The investment in dental implants pays off over time - while initial costs are higher than other tooth replacement options, implants’ durability and permanence make them the most cost-effective long-term solution for missing teeth.
