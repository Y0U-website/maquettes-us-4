What to Expect During Your Appointment

Your Fayetteville dental cleaning begins with the removal of plaque and tartar buildup from your teeth. Plaque is a sticky film of bacteria that can accumulate even with regular brushing, and when it hardens into tartar, it can only be removed by a dental professional. Eliminating this buildup helps prevent cavities, gum inflammation, and infection. Once your teeth are clean, they will be polished to remove surface stains and give your smile a smooth, refreshed appearance. Flossing is also performed to clean the spaces between your teeth and ensure no debris is left behind.

Following the cleaning, Dr. Mike Choe or Dr. Vanessa Jackson will perform a thorough examination of your mouth. This exam involves evaluating your teeth, gums, tongue, and other oral tissues for signs of decay, gum disease, or other issues such as oral cancer or bite problems. Dental X-rays may also be recommended to assess areas that are not visible to the naked eye, including beneath the gumline and between the teeth. These images allow our dentists to detect early signs of tooth decay, bone loss and other conditions before they become more advanced.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Cleanings & Exams

How long does a typical dental cleaning and exam take?

A standard dental cleaning and exam usually takes between 45 minutes to an hour. This allows enough time for a thorough cleaning, a complete evaluation of your teeth and gums, and any necessary X-rays or consultations without feeling rushed during your appointment.

Is it normal to experience sensitivity after a cleaning?

Mild sensitivity after a dental cleaning is not uncommon, especially if it has been a while since your last visit. This sensitivity usually fades within a day or two. Using a toothpaste designed for sensitive teeth can help reduce discomfort and protect enamel between visits.

Why are professional cleanings better than at-home care?

Even with excellent brushing and flossing habits, professional cleanings remove hardened deposits and reach areas that are difficult to clean at home. Dental professionals use specialized tools and techniques to ensure a deeper clean and to monitor for subtle changes in oral health that may otherwise go unnoticed.

What should I do between visits to maintain my oral health?

Maintain a consistent daily routine that includes brushing twice a day, flossing once daily and using mouthwash as recommended. Eating a balanced diet, limiting sugary snacks and drinking plenty of water also support strong teeth and gums. Staying consistent helps reduce the need for more intensive treatments later.
