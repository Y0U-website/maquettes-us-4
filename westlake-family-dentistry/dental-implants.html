How Dental Implants Restore Your Smile

After the titanium post is placed, a period of healing is necessary to allow the bone to bond with the implant in a process called osseointegration. This step is crucial, as it creates a secure base that can support the artificial tooth or teeth. Once healing is complete, a custom dental restoration is attached to the post. This may be a crown for a single tooth, a bridge for several missing teeth, or a full or partial denture for more extensive tooth loss.

Each restoration is carefully crafted to match your bite, facial structure and smile. The result is a seamless replacement that enhances both function and appearance. Because dental implants in Fayetteville, NC, integrate with your natural bone, they offer a stable and secure fit that does not shift or slip like traditional dentures. They also help prevent bone loss in the jaw, which is a common consequence of tooth loss.

Our dentists and team will work closely with you throughout the implant process to ensure that your restoration fits your smile perfectly and that your implant placement is as smooth as possible.

Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

One of the most appealing aspects of dental implants in Fayetteville, NC, is their ability to function just like your natural teeth. You can eat, speak and smile with confidence, knowing that your implant will remain secure. Additionally, dental implants require no alteration of adjacent teeth, which preserves the overall health of your remaining natural teeth. With proper care, including good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for many years or even a lifetime.

Implants in Fayetteville also contribute to your long-term oral health by maintaining the structure of your jawbone and supporting the surrounding facial features. This helps prevent the sunken facial appearance that often accompanies tooth loss. Because of their stability, appearance and health benefits, implants are considered a gold standard in tooth replacement.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long does the dental implant process take from start to finish?

The entire dental implant process typically spans several months. It begins with an evaluation, followed by implant placement and a healing period. Once the area has fully healed, a permanent restoration is added. Timelines can vary depending on your oral health and the complexity of the case.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure, often less than expected. Local anesthesia is used to ensure comfort, and post-operative soreness is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain medication. Any discomfort tends to fade within a few days as the healing process progresses.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Not everyone is an ideal candidate. Our experienced dentists will assess your overall health, gum condition and jawbone density to determine if implants are right for you. Individuals with certain medical conditions or insufficient bone support may require additional procedures before implants can be placed. You may also need to receive treatment for any pre-existing dental issues, such as gum disease or cavities, before you can receive an implant. A consultation is the best way to determine your suitability.

How do I care for my dental implants after placement?

Caring for dental implants involves maintaining excellent oral hygiene. Daily brushing and flossing , along with regular dental checkups, help keep the implant area healthy. Avoiding tobacco and following instructions from our dentists and team will promote healing and long-term success. Implants do not decay, but the surrounding tissues require ongoing care.
