Meet Dr. Anthony Baird, DDS The Bountiful dentist who has been treating the whole person, not just the tooth, since 2003.

Dr. Anthony Baird, DDS

Dr. Baird grew up in Utah and went into dentistry for reasons that still hold true today: he loved working with his hands, building real relationships with patients, and being a trusted part of his community. He trained at Virginia Commonwealth University, then came home and opened Millcreek Family Dental from scratch.

More than twenty years later, what fires him up most are transformations, watching a patient who used to hide their smile finally find it again. That pull toward bigger, more complex work led him to the Kois Center, where he has completed most of the curriculum on bite, jaw function, and full-mouth rehabilitation. It is why he can keep cases in-house that many dentists send away: single-tooth implants planned on a 3D scan, same-day crowns, smile makeovers, and worn-down bites rebuilt from the ground up.

Outside the office, he frequently enjoys fly fishing, mountain biking, skiing, and time with family. He is also the dentist who hands out his cell number, because when a dental problem hits after hours, he would rather you call him than panic alone.

Meet The Millcreek Family Dental Team

Our team is a huge part of why our small-town, family-feel practice works. Our hygienists are the engine behind the thorough care patients take notice of. They perform oral cancer screenings at every visit and are trained specifically to deliver our structured gum-health program.
