New Patients

Our goal at Zirbel Orthodontics is to help you smile. Dr. Cassandra Zirbel and her team are known for making orthodontics fun for the Eagan and Cottage Grove communities! Our orthodontic team understands that every patient is unique in their needs and we will go above and beyond to give you the personalized care you deserve. During your first visit to our dental office, we will listen to the goals you have for your smile and will discuss what treatment options will help you get there.

What to Expect

Dr. Cassandra Zirbel and her staff will be there for every step of your process to ensure that you receive the best orthodontic care possible. Here’s what you can expect during your initial complimentary consultation.

Review your new patient forms and medical history forms

Complete your initial exam with x-rays

Customize your treatment plan

Discuss financial plans, insurance coverage, and payment options

Get started on your orthodontic journey!
