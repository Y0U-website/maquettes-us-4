Who we are

Dr. Cassandra Zirbel and our team are committed to providing orthodontic care that not only creates beautiful, healthy smiles, but that can truly transform a person’s life.

Dr. Cassandra Zirbel

My Story

After high school, I attended the University of Minnesota and majored in physiology and then enrolled at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, where I was awarded my Doctorate of Dental Surgery. This was followed by studies at the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio, where I was chief resident in orthodontics. Then it was on to the University of Texas Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences for a Master of Science in cellular and structural biology.

I love being part of a field that requires me to be a lifelong student. I am committed to providing the best care to my patients, and this can only be achieved by continuing to learn and expand my skills, through participation in continuing education courses. I am a member of the American Association of Orthodontists and the American Dental Association, and I am certified through the American Board of Orthodontics, all of which provide me with access to meetings, lectures, and relevant oral healthcare literature.

I was born in St. Paul, grew up in Cottage Grove, and graduated from Park High School. My husband Justin, myself, our son Rylan and daughter Emmerson now call Woodbury our home. My family is my first priority, and I love spending time with them. Being outdoors is also important to me, whether I am playing a round of golf, fishing, or being on the water. Travel is wonderful, too, as I love to experience new sights, sounds, and cultures.

My Story

I grew up in Edina, Minnesota and graduated from Edina High School. I pursued secondary education at St. Olaf College and earned a Bachelor of Arts in Biology. I then attended the University of Minnesota where I obtained a Doctorate of Dental Surgery and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honors Society. I continued my education at the University of Minnesota, receiving an orthodontic specialty certification and a Master of Science in Dentistry.

To best serve our patients, I value the importance of life-long learning and improvement. I frequently attend continuing education courses and participate in several professional associations. I am a member of the American Association of Orthodontics, Minnesota Association of Orthodontics, and College of Diplomates of the American Board of Orthodontics. Additionally, I am a board certified orthodontist through the American Board of Orthodontics, demonstrating my commitment to excellence for our patients and specialty.

Outside of work, I love spending time with my family. I am married to my dental school sweetheart, Zach. We eat, sleep and breathe teeth as he is also an orthodontist. We have a young daughter, Lettie, and an Aussiedoodle dog who are the center of our lives!
