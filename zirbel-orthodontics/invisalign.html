Invisalign®

Whether you’re a teen or an adult, Invisalign® is a constantly-evolving technology that provides discreet, fast treatment for all lifestyles. Dr. Cassandra Zirbel is a Platinum certified Invisalign® provider who is highly trained in treating many types of orthodontic cases with Invisalign®.

Why choose Invisalign® Over Traditional Braces?

Discreet & Comfortable

Clear aligners mean that no one knows you are going through treatment. Straighten your teeth and fix your bite without the use of traditional braces and wires.

Better Oral Hygiene

You can brush and floss daily without encumbering brackets to maneuver around. Spots and stains in hard to reach places are never a concern!

You Can Eat What You Love

Unlike traditional braces, aligners can be taken out as often as needed. You can continue to eat your favorite foods.

Fast & Affordable

On average, patients with Invisalign® require fewer visits to the office for checkups, saving you time and money.
