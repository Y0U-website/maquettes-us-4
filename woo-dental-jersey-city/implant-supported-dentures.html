Clear answers, before your visit.

Implant-supported dentures are prosthetic teeth anchored securely to dental implants placed in your jawbone. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on your gums, these restorations provide superior stability and prevent bone loss while allowing natural eating and speaking.

The number of implants depends on your specific needs and the type of restoration you choose. Most patients need 2-4 implants for a lower denture and 4-6 implants for an upper denture.

The initial implant placement and temporary denture fitting can often be completed in one day. The final restoration is typically placed after 3-4 months of healing, ensuring optimal integration and long-term success.

Most patients report that their implant-supported dentures feel remarkably similar to natural teeth. The secure fit allows you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

Daily cleaning and regular dental check-ups are essential for maintaining your implant-supported dentures. We provide detailed care instructions and schedule regular maintenance visits to ensure long-term success.

The investment varies based on your specific treatment plan and insurance coverage. We offer multiple financing options and work with many insurance providers to make treatment affordable.
