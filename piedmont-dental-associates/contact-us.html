DENTAL Resources

PROSTHODONTICS

Prosthodontics are state-of-the-art procedures and techniques, including dental implants, bridges, crowns, dentures, fillings, occlusal disease treatment, and TMJ/TMD treatment.

MAXILLOFACIAL PROSTHETICS

Maxillofacial Prosthetics involves rehabilitation of patients with defects or disabilities that were present when born or developed due to disease or trauma.

Fixed Partial Hybrid

The term Fixed Partial Hybrid refers to the teeth being supported by partial dental implants, a prosthodontics procedure for total rehabilitation of the edentulous patient
