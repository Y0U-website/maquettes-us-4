Dr. Hanna Smith

Dr. Smith brings warmth, compassion, and exceptional communication to each patient’s experience. A proud graduate of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill for both her undergraduate and dental degrees, Dr. Smith is known for her down-to-earth nature and ability to truly listen. She prioritizes understanding her patients’ goals and concerns to create thoughtful, personalized treatment plans.

Professionally, Dr. Smith has a special interest in extractions and dentures, and she is continually expanding her skills- recently planning to pursue advanced continuing education courses in dental implants.

Outside the office, Dr. Smith is a proud mother of two, an avid artist, and the illustrator of the children’s picture book, Scary-O-Typed! She finds joy in staying active, exploring the outdoors, and spending time with her family and beloved dogs.

With her calming presence and dedication to patient-centered care, Dr. Smith helps make dental visits a more positive and comfortable experience for everyone.
