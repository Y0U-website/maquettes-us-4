Accessibility Statement

Tumlin Family Dentistry strives to ensure our website is technologically accessible, while being welcoming and informative to all.

Our website is designed with the goal of meeting the standards set with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0.

If you encounter any issues or find anything that is not accessible, please reach out to us so we can address the concerns. We regularly update our practices to ensure we meet our accessibility goals.
