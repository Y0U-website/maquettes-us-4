All-on-4 and All-on-6 Dental Implants in York, Maine

Tooth loss can significantly impact your quality of life, affecting your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. At York Village Dental, we specialize in advanced implant dentistry, offering both All-on-4 and All-on-6 full-arch solutions to restore your smile.

All-on-4 and All-on-6 procedures are ideal for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth and want a stable, long-lasting, and natural-looking alternative to traditional dentures.

Understanding All-on-4 and All-on-6 Dental Implants Dental implants have transformed the way we approach full-arch tooth replacement. For patients missing most or all of their teeth, All-on-4 and All-on-6 implant systems offer a life-changing solution that combines aesthetics, function, and long-term oral health. While both options use strategically placed implants to support a complete set of prosthetic teeth, they differ in design and suitability based on a patient’s specific dental needs. Below, we explain each method in detail to help you better understand your options.

What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are designed to support a full arch of replacement teeth using only four implants placed in the jaw. This technique is ideal for patients who have experienced some bone loss, as it avoids the need for bone grafting by angling the two rear implants to maximize bone contact and stability. Typically, two implants are placed vertically at the front of the jaw, and two are inserted at an angle in the back, providing a secure foundation for the full-arch prosthesis.

One of the most appealing aspects of the All-on-4 method is its efficiency—many patients can have their implants and temporary teeth placed on the same day, which is why the procedure is often called “Teeth in a Day.” This allows for a rapid return to normal eating, speaking, and smiling without the long wait traditionally associated with implants.

In addition to immediate results, All-on-4 implants help preserve jawbone health by stimulating bone tissue, which prevents the resorption that typically follows tooth loss. The prosthetic teeth are designed to look natural, feel comfortable, and function like your original smile, providing a transformative solution for individuals who have struggled with removable dentures or missing teeth.

What Are All-on-6 Dental Implants?

All-on-6 dental implants follow the same principle as the All-on-4 system but use six implants instead of four. This provides an enhanced level of support and stability, especially for patients with adequate bone density in the jaw. With two additional implants, the pressure from daily functions like chewing and speaking is distributed more evenly, which can help prolong the life of both the implants and the prosthetic arch.

This approach is often recommended for patients who want maximum structural support or who have higher bite forces that require added reinforcement. It’s also ideal for those seeking a more permanent and secure alternative to dentures or for patients whose oral anatomy allows for the extra implants without the need for bone grafts.

The All-on-6 process generally includes a detailed consultation and 3D imaging to assess the bone structure and plan implant placement precisely. Like All-on-4, temporary teeth may be placed the same day, followed by a healing period of several months while the implants fuse with the jawbone. Once healed, a permanent, custom-made arch of teeth is securely attached to the implants, delivering a confident smile and improved oral function.

Choosing between All-on-4 and All-on-6 often comes down to bone availability, lifestyle needs, and personal preference—factors your dental team at York Village Dental will carefully evaluate during your consultation.

Benefits of Choosing All-on-4 and All-on-6 Implants

Choosing implant-supported full-arch restoration comes with a wide range of advantages. From improved comfort and appearance to lasting durability, All-on-4 and All-on-6 dental implants are designed to significantly enhance your quality of life. Here are the top benefits you can expect from these advanced treatment options:

• Immediate Functionality: Many patients receive temporary prosthetic teeth on the same day as implant placement. • Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate jawbone health and help prevent bone loss over time. • Natural Appearance: Custom-crafted prosthetics look and feel like real teeth. • Durability: With proper care, implants can last 20 years or longer. • Improved Confidence: Patients regain the ability to eat, speak, and smile without worry.

Who Is a Candidate?

Understanding whether you’re a suitable candidate for All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants starts with a conversation about your health, dental history, and long-term goals. These implant systems aren’t for everyone, but many patients with missing teeth can benefit. You may be a good candidate for All-on-4 or All-on-6 dental implants if you:

• Are missing most or all of your teeth in one or both arches. • Have sufficient jawbone or are eligible for bone grafting. • Are looking for a stable, permanent alternative to dentures. • Are in good overall health and free from conditions that affect healing.

The Procedure: What to Expect

From your first consultation to receiving your new smile, the All-on-4 or All-on-6 implant process is carefully designed to be efficient, comfortable, and tailored to your needs. Here’s a step-by-step overview of what you can expect:

1. Consultation and Planning: Comprehensive exam, X-rays, and 3D scans to develop a custom treatment plan. 2. Implant Surgery: Placement of four or six dental implants using advanced surgical techniques. 3. Temporary Teeth: Attachment of a temporary prosthesis so you leave with a full smile the same day. 4. Healing Period: Osseointegration takes place over 3 to 6 months as the implants bond with the jawbone. 5. Permanent Restoration: Final prosthetic teeth are secured once healing is complete.

Aftercare and Maintenance

Dental implants require routine care to ensure their longevity and functionality. While they don’t decay like natural teeth, they still need to be kept clean and healthy. Follow these simple maintenance tips to protect your investment:

• Brush and floss daily as you would with natural teeth. • Use a water flosser to clean around implants. • Schedule regular checkups for professional cleaning and monitoring. • Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol to ensure implant success.
