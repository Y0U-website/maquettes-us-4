What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants in Cortland restorations are durable, natural-looking dental restorations created with titanium posts implanted into the jawbone, where they securely attach. These implants don’t need special adhesives, and there are no restrictions on what you can eat. Dental implants are excellent for restoring your smile’s look and function, whether you’re missing one tooth or several.

Our Cortland, OH, dentist’s office provides the following dental implant systems:

Single Dental Implants

These are designed to replace a single missing tooth and its root. It’s capped with a custom-shaded dental crown, perfectly matching your smile.

All-on-4® Dental Implants

A long-lasting solution for those tired of traditional removable dentures. This system gives you a new set of teeth on the same day! Four titanium implants are integrated into the jawbone, providing a secure base for custom dentures.

Hybrid Dental Implants

Our dentists permanently attach dentures using dental implants, which are ideal for widespread tooth loss concerns and a cost-effective solution to tooth replacement.

Implant-Retained Dentures

These are partial or complete dentures sitting atop dental implants for a secure fit. They promote healthy bone retention, offering a more stable and healthier alternative to traditional dentures.
