Beyond the Practice

A lifelong resident of Ohio, Dr. Bedich married his high school sweetheart, Robin, in 1985. They have two sons and love spending time with their growing family, especially their grandchildren. Outside the office, Dr. Bedich is an avid fan of The Ohio State Buckeyes and Pittsburgh Steelers and he enjoys scuba diving, attending NASCAR races and concerts, and traveling with his loved ones. He is also active in the local community as a member and past president of the Cortland Rotary and serves as a lector at Blessed Sacrament Church in Warren.

In his free time, Dr. Bedich also enjoys photography, outdoor activities, and learning about history and science. His curious, hands-on nature carries over into both his personal life and his dental philosophy—always striving to explore, understand, and deliver the best in everything he does. Those who know him appreciate his sense of humor, approachable demeanor, and the genuine care he shows both in and out of the office.

What Sets Dr. Bedich Apart

Dr. Bedich combines advanced skill with genuine compassion to make dental care more comfortable and accessible for every patient. With extensive training in sedation dentistry, he provides care for individuals with dental anxiety, special healthcare needs, or complex treatment requirements.

His patients benefit from a full range of services—often completed in fewer appointments—with a focus on comfort, efficiency, and long-term results. Whether you are seeking advanced restorative work or simply want a dentist who listens and cares, Dr. Bedich is dedicated to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.
