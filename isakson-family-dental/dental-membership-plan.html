Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment do you accept? We accept cash, checks, major credit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay for membership payments and treatment costs. We also partner with Cherry financing to provide flexible monthly payment options for larger dental procedures.
  • When can I start using my membership? Your in-office membership plan benefits begin as soon as your annual membership payment is received, with no waiting periods before you can start using your coverage.
  • How can I apply my member-exclusive discount toward treatment? Simply schedule your visit and mention that you’re enrolled in the Basin Dental in-office plan. Our team will verify your active membership at your appointment so you can immediately begin receiving your plan benefits and savings.
