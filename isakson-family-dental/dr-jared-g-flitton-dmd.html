The Personal Side

Outside the office, Dr. Flitton enjoys spending time with his wife, Becky, their sons Connor, Jackson, and Basin, and their dog Oscar, who he jokingly refers to as the “favorite child.” He loves staying active outdoors and is always looking for his next adventure, whether that means traveling, skiing, surfing, or discovering great new restaurants.

Snow skiing is one of Dr. Flitton’s biggest passions, and he is also a major fan of live music, especially 90’s alternative and rock. When he’s not at the office or outdoors, you can often find him listening to podcasts and audiobooks covering everything from personal development to innovations in science, technology, and health. He’s also a devoted Atlanta Braves fan and enjoys keeping up with baseball whenever he can.

One thing patients quickly learn about Dr. Flitton is his impressive ability to remember movie quotes, especially from 90’s cult classics. His easygoing personality, adventurous spirit, and commitment to lifelong learning help create the welcoming and relaxed atmosphere patients appreciate at Basin Dental.
