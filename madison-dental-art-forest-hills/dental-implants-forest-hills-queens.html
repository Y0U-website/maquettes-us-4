THE DENTAL IMPLANT PROCEDURE

Our skilled dentistry team follows a precise, multi-step treatment plan:

Placement Surgery: A titanium post is surgically placed into the bone. The bone must heal and bond with the post over several weeks.

Abutment Phase: A small connector is attached to the implant to support the final crown.

Final Restoration : We place a custom dental crown to restore the function and appearance of your teeth.

For individuals with advanced periodontal disease or bone loss, additional procedures may be recommended to ensure the success of your tooth replacement.

PROTECTING YOUR INVESTMENT Caring for your dental implants is simple. Frequent brushing, flossing, and routine visits to your dentist help ensure your oral health remains optimal.

Preventing Disease: Proper oral care prevents periodontal issues around the implant.

Routine Dental Services : Regular visits allow us to monitor the bone and the stability of the crown.
