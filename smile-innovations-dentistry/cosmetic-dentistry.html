Teeth Whitening

We help patients achieve a brighter smile that still feels natural, not overly white or artificial-looking. We’ll help you determine the most appropriate option based on your goals and tooth sensitivity. Professional teeth whitening may reduce stains and discoloration caused by:

  • Coffee and tea
  • Wine or staining foods
  • Simple aging
  • Tobacco use
  • Everyday wear

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made ceramic coverings designed to create symmetry and balance in a smile. Because cosmetic concerns are sometimes related to grinding, bite issues, or tooth wear, we also evaluate how your teeth function together before recommending treatment. With veneers, we may be able to correct:

  • Chipped or worn teeth
  • Deep discoloration
  • Uneven tooth shape
  • Small gaps or space issues
  • Mild asymmetry

Cosmetic Bonding

Cosmetic bonding uses tooth-colored composite material to improve the appearance of small imperfections while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible. It often requires minimal alteration to the natural tooth for patients seeking subtle refinements. Cosmetic bonding may be used to repair or improve:

  • Small chips or cracks
  • Minor gaps between teeth
  • Uneven or worn edges
  • Localized discoloration
  • Mild shape concerns

Smile Makeovers

For some patients, cosmetic concerns due to multiple issues like discoloration, missing teeth, asymmetry, failing dental work, and more. A smile makeover allows us to look at both aesthetics and function while building a treatment plan around your priorities, timeline, and comfort level. A smile makeover combines cosmetic and restorative dentistry to improve:

  • Smile appearance
  • Tooth wear
  • Function and comfort
  • Balance and symmetry
  • Long-term stability

Facial Esthetics, Botox® Xeomin®

Your smile does not exist separately from the rest of your face. Facial muscles, tension patterns, lip movement, and surrounding features may all contribute to overall facial harmony and expression.

Dr. Hungerford has extensive post-graduate training in facial esthetics, and can provide Botox and Xeomin treatments as part of a comprehensive approach to facial balance and rejuvenation. These treatments may be used cosmetically or as part of care for:

  • Fine lines and wrinkles
  • Jaw tension and TMJ
  • Clenching and grinding
  • Facial symmetry concerns

Platelet-Rich Fibrin Treatments

Platelet Rich Fibrin (PRF) contains your own platelets and growth factors. The resulting fibrin matrix can be used to accelerate healing and stimulate natural tissue regeneration.

  • Wrinkles and fine lines
  • Uneven skin texture or tone
  • Scars from acne
  • Pores that are enlarged
  • Damage from the sun or dark spots
  • Dark circles or eye bags
  • Skin that appears dull

Micro-needling with PRF

Using platelet-rich fibrin, this advanced skincare treatment is a popular choice for enhancing skin tone and texture. It stimulates the body’s natural healing process while minimizing cellular damage. Treatments are minimally invasive, quick, comfortable and require little recovery time.

  • Sun Spots
  • Acne Scars
  • Large Pores
  • Fine Lines Wrinkles
  • Signs of Aging
  • Hyperpigmentation
