Digital X-Rays

Digital dental X-rays allow us to look beneath the surface and identify concerns that may not be visible during a visual exam alone. These detailed images help us evaluate tooth structure, bone health, alignment, and any issues that may be developing.

Digital imaging also uses significantly less radiation than traditional X-rays while helping us diagnose issues earlier and more accurately.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments support strong tooth enamel and may reduce the risk of cavities for both children and adults. This quick preventive treatment can be especially beneficial for patients with sensitivity, dry mouth, frequent decay, or changing oral health needs. We may recommend fluoride treatment when it supports your individual oral health goals.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants provide an extra layer of protection against cavities by covering the deep grooves where bacteria and food particles often collect. Sealants are commonly recommended for kids as permanent molars develop, though some adults may benefit as well.

Oral Cancer Screenings

As part of your preventive dental care, we perform quick and simple oral cancer screenings during routine exams. We look for abnormal tissue changes or other concerns that may require further evaluation, since early detection plays an important role in protecting your health.

Family Dentistry

We provide family dentistry in Lincoln, NE, for children, teens, adults, and seniors. From first dental visits to ongoing preventive care, we work to make every experience feel welcoming and supportive. For patients who enjoy a little extra comfort, Cleo, our therapy dog, is ready for optional snuggles during your visit.

Restorative Dentistry

Even with excellent preventive care, dental problems can still happen. If you’re dealing with tooth pain, damaged teeth, missing teeth, or recurring dental concerns, we’ll take time to understand the root cause and help you explore clear treatment options. We offer both composite resin (tooth-colored) fillings and silver amalgam fillings.

Dentures Partials

Removable dentures are a common option for replacing missing teeth. This can be a full denture that replaces an entire arch or a partial denture that replaces a few teeth. Through a series of appointments, you will choose the perfect smile for you. With the help of our amazing lab, Dr. Meghan will fabricate the natural-looking smile you deserve.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-Supported dentures (overdentures) are complete dentures that snap into 2-4 dental implants. This is commonly used to keep a lower denture from slipping when speaking or chewing. Many patients love how simple it is to adapt to these more secure dentures.

Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies can feel stressful and disruptive. Whether you’re experiencing a toothache, broken tooth, swelling, or sudden discomfort, our team will do everything we can to help you feel supported and cared for as quickly as possible. If you have an emergency during office hours, we’ll make every effort to see you the same day. If your emergency occurs after hours and you are an established patient of the practice, please call the office at 402.484.8444, then press 1 to be directed to Dr. Meghan.

Dental Extractions

Teeth need to be removed for a variety of reasons, whether they are impacted wisdom teeth, crowded baby teeth or severely compromised by decay or periodontal disease. Dr. Meghan’s training in extractions allows her to confidently and comfortably remove teeth when necessary. Nitrous oxide sedation is available. Occasional referral to an oral surgeon may be needed.

Laser Dentistry

Laser dentistry allows greater precision for certain treatments while supporting comfort and healing. We use this technology to improve your experience where clinically supported, keeping your unique needs at the center of your care. Dr. Meghan may use laser dentistry for treating gum disease, early cavities, enamel erosion, and root canal therapy.

Dental Anxiety Comfort

Anxiety with dental appointments can have a major impact on a person’s overall well being. Research has shown that people with dental phobia tend to have more cavities and missing teeth. We do our very best to make even the most anxious person comfortable by offering pillows, blankets, television, and relaxing options including nitrous oxide.
