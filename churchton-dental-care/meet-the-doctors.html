Our Doctors

As a dental specialist, Dr. Dersley is one of Maryland’s top experts in helping patients with major dental problems. He has helped his patients overcome such problems as: embarrassment with their teeth, severe dental anxiety, social problems due to not being able to eat a meal comfortably, and chronic dental pain due to unhealthy teeth.

Dr. Dersley believes that when you can smile with confidence, chew comfortably and have a healthy mouth, life is better. His focus is to provide you with a plan that will ensure comfortable care, minimal pain, and a confident and healthy smile for a lifetime.

Dr. Gabriel graduated magna cum laude from the University of Richmond with a BS in biology and chemistry. He then stayed down in Richmond, VA and graduated dental school from Virginia Commonwealth University. Since then, Dr. Gabriel has been in private practice serving the Maryland and Washington, DC areas. He understands that everyone can be a little anxious at the dentist and does his best to make sure you are comfortable, confident, and relaxed.

When he is not in the office, Dr. Gabriel enjoys hiking with his dog Emme and cooking with his wife. Most recently Dr. Gabriel is learning to play the ukulele!

Dr. Murphy is a University of Maryland School of Dentistry graduate who recently completed a General Practice Residency at Wellspan York Hospital in York, Pennsylvania. In residency, she received advanced training in the hospital and emergency settings and provided routine dental care to the underserved population. As a Southern Maryland native, she is excited to return to her home state and provide for her community.

Before becoming a dentist, she earned her undergraduate degree in Biology at Salisbury University and received a Masters of Public Health from West Virginia University.

In her free time, she enjoys longboarding, sailing on the Chesapeake, exploring National Parks, and spending time with her family, friends, and her lovable retriever, Goose.

Dr. Jenkens is excited to continue his dental career in Southern Maryland, serving the local Churchton community. Born and raised in Maryland, he graduated from St. Mary’s College of Maryland with a B.A. in Biology while competing in Division III lacrosse. He went on to earn a Master of Science in Physiology and Biophysics from Georgetown University before attending the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine.

As a dental student, he co-founded Healthy Smiles , a fund dedicated to helping patients improve access to dental care and treatment that would otherwise be unattainable due to financial barriers. After earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree, Dr. Jenkens completed advanced surgical and clinical training in general dentistry through a General Practice Residency at Mountain Area Health Education Center in Asheville, NC.

Outside of the office, you’ll find Dr. Jenkens on the golf course, playing pick-up sports, or hiking along scenic trails. He also enjoys soaking up the Chesapeake Bay lifestyle with family and friends.
