  • Bed-wetting
  • Choking or drooling

  • Learning and behavior disorders
  • Pauses or absence of breathing
  • Problems at school

  • Teeth grinding
  • The rib cage moves inward when they exhale
  • Unusual sleeping positions, such as:
  • On the hands and knees
  • With the neck hyperextended

Treating Sleep Apnea

If you’ve already undergone a sleep study, we’ll proceed to an evaluation. If you haven’t had a sleep study, we can accommodate an at-home sleep study where you will your bed and pillow to ensure your comfort.

In addition to a standard oral evaluation, we also perform a snoring analysis to understand why you snore. We do this by making you snore. The resulting analysis determines if and how a sleep apnea device could help you.

We can craft a custom-fit device by making an impression of your teeth. Next, we send it to the lab where it will be made especially for you. We’ll ensure a proper fit in our office once the lab has made the device.

After you’ve worn the device for a while, you’ll return to our office for a checkup. The purpose of this 30-minute visit is to ensure the device is working properly for you and that your sleep is improving.
