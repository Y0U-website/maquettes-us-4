Personalized Care for Every Age

Dr. Lee is an expert in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics with focuses on smile and facial esthetics, occlusal function, and health of teeth, gum, and airway. She creates a personalized treatment plan for each patient with a pleasing smile arc, facial balance, healthy bite, and optimal airway.

Our ability to perform advanced procedures in-office — from microimplants and skeletal expanders to laser recontouring and corticopuncture — not only enables creative treatment plans utilizing mechanics typically impossible with traditional orthodontics, but also provides great convenience and savings for our patients.

Early & Growth Treatment Expert in oral habit modification, maxillary expansion, and jaw growth guidance. We design compact digital expanders that are less bulky and more comfortable — fabricated from a digital scan without extra appointments for spacers or goopy impressions. Dr. Lee also utilizes headgears, chin-cups, and Invisalign Mandibular Advancement to maximize growth potential, preventing extraction and jaw surgery in the future.

Advanced Adult Care Dr. Lee can help our adult patients do the same, incorporating techniques such as conservative dental recontouring for simple and moderate cases, and other advanced procedures for complex cases if needed, including microimplants (TADs) and maxillary skeletal expander (MARPE) placement, gum recontouring and exposure of certain impacted teeth with laser, corticopuncture to facilitate challenging movement, and piezoelectric midpalatal incision.

In-Office Advanced Procedures Our ability to perform these procedures in our office not only allows us to offer conservative and creative treatment plans utilizing mechanics typically impossible with traditional orthodontics, but it also expedites treatment progress and provides great convenience and saving for our patients.

ABO Board Certified

Both Dr. Lee and Dr. Jung are Diplomates of the American Board of Orthodontics

The Highest Standard of Care

The American Board of Orthodontics (ABO) is the only certifying board recognized by the American Dental Association for the specialty of orthodontics. Board certification is a voluntary process that goes beyond the minimum requirements for practicing.

A board-certified orthodontist has demonstrated clinical knowledge, judgment, and skills beyond graduation from an accredited program. This voluntary commitment represents a dedication to the highest standards of patient care and continued professional development.

Both Dr. Lee and Dr. Jung have voluntarily pursued and achieved ABO Diplomate status — a distinction held by a minority of practicing orthodontists. This reflects their commitment to providing excellence in orthodontic care.
