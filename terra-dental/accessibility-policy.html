Accessibility policy

0 ACCESSIBILITY At Terra Dental, we are dedicated to making our website accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities. Inclusion is a top priority for us, and we are constantly working toward making our website more accessible, no matter what device or assistive technology you are using.

We strive to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and applicable provisions of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA). Our ongoing accessibility efforts aim to make www.terradental.nyc as accessible and user-friendly as possible. We regularly review our website and test it with accessibility tools to identify and address potential barriers. We welcome your feedback and encourage you to reach out with any questions or concerns.

Terra Dental’s website is designed to be compatible with commonly used assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, and speech recognition software.

Contact Us Should you have any difficulty accessing the content of this site or have accessibility concerns, please contact our concierge team: Email: concierge@terradental.nyc

Text Size If the text on this site appears too small, you can easily adjust it using the built-in zoom feature available in all modern browsers. Select the appropriate option from your browser’s menu or use the following keyboard shortcuts:

Zoom in: Ctrl + (Windows) or Cmd + (Mac) Zoom out: Ctrl – (Windows) or Cmd – (Mac)

Motion Controls We aim to ensure that moving, blinking, scrolling, and auto-updating elements on our website include options to pause or stop them where possible.

Volume Control We aim to ensure that video and audio content on our website includes accessible volume controls, typically located in the bottom-right corner of the media player.

Third-Party Content While we strive to ensure the accessibility of our entire website, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content that may be linked to or embedded within our site.
