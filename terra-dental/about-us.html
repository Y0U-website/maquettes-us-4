Our Story

Terra Dental is a modern luxury dental clinic in Hell’s Kitchen, Manhattan, where clinical excellence meets thoughtful, one-of-a-kind experiences.

We designed our space and experience around one idea: that dentistry should feel like self-care. Every detail is intentional — noise-cancelling Beats headphones and your pick of Netflix, Disney+, or Apple TV during treatment, Gentle Monster sunglasses to soften the overhead light, warm hand towels, and a complimentary beverage while you settle in — so you feel at ease from the moment you arrive.

While we offer comprehensive dental services — from cleanings and restorative work to Invisalign, professional whitening, and veneers — we are cosmetic dentists at our core. We craft functional, natural-looking, and extraordinarily beautiful teeth.

We believe in continuity of care. No call centers. No unfamiliar faces. Just the same team delivering a seamless, attentive experience, every time.

At Terra, your oral health is personal .

Dr. Joyce Suh, DMD

Dr. Joyce Suh (Dr. Ji Yen Suh, DMD) is the founder and lead dentist at Terra Dental, a luxury cosmetic dental practice in Hell’s Kitchen, New York City. She brings a vision of modern dentistry rooted in artistry, excellence, and genuine care. Known for her gentle demeanor and eye for detail, she combines aesthetics, function, and long-term care to create naturally beautiful, exceptionally lasting results. Minimally invasive dentistry, where less is more.

A graduate of Wellesley College, Dr. Suh earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine with distinction from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, where she was inducted into both the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Honor Society and the Gamma Pi Delta Prosthodontic Honor Society. She completed her residency at Mount Sinai’s Icahn School of Medicine, where she later served as Assistant Professor and mentored residents in advanced clinical care.

Dr. Suh is passionate about integrating aesthetic treatments with Invisalign to achieve natural, beautifully-balanced smiles. She is an active member of the American Academy of Clear Aligners, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, and the New York State Dental Association, with advanced clinical training from the Spear Education Institute and certification in Botox for therapeutic and aesthetic enhancement.

Warm, detail-oriented, and deeply committed to her craft, Dr. Suh ensures every experience at Terra feels comfortable and intentional, while empowering patients to look and feel their best.
