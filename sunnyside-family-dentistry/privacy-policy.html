Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide through website forms, appointment requests, patient paperwork, phone calls, and other communications. This may include:

  • Name, Phone number, and Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Insurance information
  • Health or dental information submitted through patient forms

How We Use Your Information

  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Communicate regarding treatment and care
  • Respond to questions or requests
  • Send patient forms and office updates
  • Process insurance and payment information
  • Improve patient experience and office operations

Text Messaging & Phone Communications

By providing your phone number through our website forms or patient communication systems, you consent to receive phone calls and text messages from Sunnyside Family Dentistry regarding appointments, treatment, billing, and office communication.

  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Reply STOP to opt out of text messaging at any time.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Information may only be shared when necessary for healthcare operations, insurance processing, payment services, website functionality, or as otherwise required by law.

Data Protection

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party tools or services, including appointment systems, communication platforms, analytics tools, financing providers, or payment processors. These providers maintain their own privacy practices and security policies.

Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to help improve website performance, understand visitor activity, and enhance user experience.

Your Rights

  • Request updates to your information
  • Ask questions about this policy
  • Request removal from text communications
