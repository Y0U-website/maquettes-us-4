At Steely Dental in Mishawaka, Indiana, we believe dentistry is about more than healthy teeth, it’s about restoring comfort, renewing confidence, and supporting your total well-being. Led by Dr. Morgan S. Steely, DDS, our mission is to provide exceptional, patient-centered care in a warm, welcoming environment where every patient feels valued, understood, and respected.

Dr. Morgan Steely has devoted his career to improving lives by addressing dental and oral health issues that often impact a person’s comfort, function, and quality of life. His expertise extends across five key areas of care: TMJ therapy, sleep disorders, tongue-tie treatment, clear aligner orthodontics, and comprehensive general dentistry.

He holds a BS in Business Management from Andrews University and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry. Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Steely has advanced training with The Breathe Institute, The American Academy of Oral & Systemic Health, Orthobrain™, and Dr. Steve Olmos’s TMJ & Sleep Therapy Centre International.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Steely treasures time with his wife and two children, enjoys gardening, boating on Lake Michigan, and making memories with family and friends.
