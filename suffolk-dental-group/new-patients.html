New Patients

During your initial appointment, we will perform various tests to assess the condition of your oral health and to create a dental plan based on your unique, individual needs. We look forward to meeting you!

Missed Appointments

Your scheduled appointment time is reserved exclusively for you. Lateness and missed appointments are disruptive to both our office and other patients who also have a timely schedule. We generally call our patients one or two days prior to their appoint­ments for confirmation. However, this call is a courtesy of our office to you, and you are ultimately responsible for keeping your appointments.

We do realize that unpredictable situations may occur that force you to reschedule your appointment. In these circumstances, we would appreciate a twenty-four hour notice if possible. Two or more missed appointments without any notice will result in dismissal from our office.
