About Morgan Hill

Morgan Hill is often described as a hidden gem of the South Bay Area. Named after early settler Hiram Morgan Hill, this city showcases agricultural roots and quiet beginnings now blossoming into a vibrant community. Nestled in the Santa Clara Valley, Morgan Hill is surrounded by lush rolling hills, creating a picturesque setting for families.

The climate and fertile soil in Morgan Hill create ideal conditions for vineyards. Morgan Hill is renowned for fine wineries and vineyards such as Guglielmo Winery and Morgan Hill Cellars. These locations offer wine tastings and serve as venues for local events and celebrations.

For outdoor enthusiasts, Anderson Lake County Park provides opportunities for hiking, biking, and boating. The city hosts popular festivals and events throughout the year. The annual Morgan Hill Mushroom Mardi Gras Festival honors local mushroom farming with food, arts and crafts, and entertainment. The Taste of Morgan Hill features a car show, live music, and local vendors showcasing crafts and culinary offerings.
