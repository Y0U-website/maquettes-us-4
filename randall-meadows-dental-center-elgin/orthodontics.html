What to Expect During Your Visit

When you visit us for orthodontics near you, you’ll be in good hands.

  • First Visit: The dentist in Elgin, IL will check your teeth and jaw, talk about what you want, and suggest the best treatment plan for you
  • Treatment Planning: Once your teeth have been examined, the dentist will discuss the options available and assist you in selecting the most appropriate treatment for your situation.
  • Initiation of Treatment: Treatment will commence as per your schedule. You will be treated with aligners, braces, or some other treatments to shift your teeth step by step.
  • Follow-Up and Adjustments: You will be coming very frequently during treatment to adjust and check your treatment.
