What are full dental implant supported restorations?

Full dental implant supported restorations are a type of dental implant procedure that provides patients with a permanent, natural-looking solution to missing teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, which can slip and slide in the mouth, full dental implant supported restorations are anchored to the jawbone using titanium posts. These posts act as artificial tooth roots and support the implant-supported dentures or bridges.

What types of full dental implant supported restorations do you offer?

At ROC Perio & Implants, we offer a variety of full dental implant supported restorations, including fixed, immediate loading, and removal overdentures. Fixed implant-supported restorations are permanently anchored to the jawbone and cannot be removed by the patient. This type of restoration is ideal for patients who want a permanent, stable solution to missing teeth.

Immediate loading implants are a type of fixed implant-supported restoration that can be placed and restored on the same day. This technique is ideal for patients who want to leave our office with a new, fully functional set of teeth in just one visit.

Removal overdentures are implant-supported dentures that can be easily removed by the patient for cleaning and maintenance. This type of restoration is ideal for patients who want a more flexible solution to missing teeth.

What are the benefits of full dental implant supported restorations?

There are many benefits to choosing full dental implant supported restorations over traditional dentures or bridges. First and foremost, dental implants are the most natural-looking and functional solution to missing teeth. They can restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Additionally, dental implants are incredibly durable and long-lasting. With proper care, your dental implants can last a lifetime. They also promote bone health and can prevent further bone loss in the jaw.

How do I know if I&#x27;m a good candidate for full dental implant supported restorations?

If you have one or more missing teeth and are in good overall health, you may be a good candidate for full dental implant supported restorations. However, every patient is different, and we recommend scheduling a consultation with one of our experienced periodontists to determine the best treatment plan for your unique needs.
