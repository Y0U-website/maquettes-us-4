The practice

In September 2023, The Dental Lab proudly acquired Colonial Dental Lab, bringing over four decades of trusted craftsmanship in South Jersey and the Mid-Atlantic region into our growing nationwide network. While the name has changed, the people, principles, and passion behind the work remain the same.

Colonial Dental Lab—founded in 1976—earned a reputation for high-quality restorations, reliable service, and close relationships with the dentists it served. That legacy now continues as The Dental Lab – West Berlin, with expanded capabilities, faster turnaround times, and the full support of our digital and implant-centered workflows.

Our West Berlin location continues to be staffed by the same experienced team you’ve trusted for decades. The only difference? Now we have even more tools to support your success, from digital case submission to nationwide shipping and expanded lab services.

Contact our staff with any questions you may have and we will be in touch shortly.
