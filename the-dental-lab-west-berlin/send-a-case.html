Packing your Case

Protect your case and avoid delays by packing carefully. Broken models can lead to poor fits and re-impressions.

Follow these steps: • Disinfect all items and seal them in a bag. • Wrap each model in bubble wrap. • Fill the box with extra bubble wrap to prevent shifting.

Articulator/Face-bow: Remove and wrap models separately, or if models stay on, separate and wrap each part securely. Wrap face-bow separately.

Crowns/Bridges/Shade Tabs: Use a sealed bag or container, then wrap.

Photos/Slides: Place in a sealed plastic bag.
