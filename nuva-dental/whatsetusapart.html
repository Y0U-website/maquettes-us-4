KNOW A LITTLE MORE ABOUT THE DOCTOR

Dr. Eva Napoles is a General Dentist graduated from College of Dentistry Medical University in La Habana, Cuba in 2000. She then, after three more years of studies became a Prosthodontic specialist graduating at the top of her class. In 2010, She graduated from the University of Florida School of Dentistry, receiving her American Accreditation.

As a consummate professional with more than 20 years of experience in dental arts, Dr. Napoles takes over 100 hours of continuing education credits each year. This level of commitment gives Dr. Napoles and the Nuva Dental Team the ability to provide our patients with the latest dental techniques at the highest standard of care.

Dr. Napoles has a passion for dentistry and helping people optimize their overall health through their dental care, she practices the principles of Health Centered Dentistry. She emphasizes giving her patients personal attention, making them feel comfortable and creating a pleasant environment for them.

Dr. Napoles has extensive training in Cosmetic Dentistry, Implantology and laser dentistry, she[ is a member of the American Dental Association, Central Florida Dental Association, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, International Congress of Oral Implantology and American Society of Implant and Reconstructive Dentistry.

Dr. Napoles resides in Vero Beach with her husband, Jaro and her son, Noah. She spends her time away from the office with her family and friends, cooking, entertaining and traveling.
