Les soins

A dental implant is a permanent replacement for your damaged or missing tooth. We surgically position a steel tooth root into the jawbone below the gumline. Once your implant heals and securely fuses to the jawbone, we create a natural looking crown as a cap.

For patients interested in cosmetic dentistry or dental implants, we also offer our complimentary Southern Dental Care CandidPro consultation and Southern Dental Implants Consultation.
