Dr. Devin Harr

Dentist In Rupert and Burley, Idaho

Dr. Harr loves all aspects of General Dentistry and has a special interest in cosmetic and implant dentistry. I love being a part of the transformation that occurs in people’s lives as they gain confidence through improving their smiles and oral health .

He graduated with Honors from Missouri State University in 2013 and went on to complete his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the University of Nevada Las Vegas. After dental school, Dr. Harr and his family spent most of the last 5 years living and practicing in Alaska. After being away for several years, they are excited to be close to family in the Magic Valley area. Dr. Harr will join us at both locations on various days during the week.

He and his wife Claire have 6 amazing kids (3 boys and 3 girls). While living in Alaska his family learned how to truly appreciate the outdoors and spent their time fishing, hiking, kayaking, camping, mountain biking, skiing, 4-wheeling, etc. Dr. Harr enjoys anything high adventure... and has been skydiving, bungee jumping, rock climbing, white water rafting, etc. One of the things, apart from family, that drew Dr. Harr to this area was the similarity in lifestyle that people enjoy here with the mountains and the river so close.

Most recently his family has been getting involved in the unique sport of American Ninja Warrior. This past year he had an amazing opportunity to compete in Season 14 of the show! "It has been such a fun family event, my kids get excited helping me build and try out new obstacles. Its amazing to see how quickly they improve as they set goals to complete each one".
