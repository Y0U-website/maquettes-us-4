Dr. Nathan Catmull

Dentist In Rupert and Burley, Idaho

Dr. Catmull was born and raised in the Mini-Cassia area. He is a graduate of Minico High School and BYU-Idaho. Dr. Catmull attended Creighton University where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Dr. Catmull has a passion for excellence in the dentistry he performs and has dedicated himself to continual education throughout his career. Honestly, his favorite part of his job is getting to know the people he serves each day! He highly values the relationships of his patients and the trust he gains from them.

His greatest joy is his family! He and his wife Elise are the parents of five wonderful kids. In his free time, Dr. Catmull coaches baseball and loves spending time with his family outdoors. They enjoy playing on the river in the summer and skiing in the winter. Their kids are extremely involved in various sports and dance recitals which keep them busy when they are not playing together.
