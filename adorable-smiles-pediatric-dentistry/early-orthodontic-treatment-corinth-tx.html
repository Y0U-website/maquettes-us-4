When Should My Child Have Their First Dental Visit? Your child’s first dental visit should happen by their first birthday or within six months of the first tooth coming in. Early dental visits help us monitor growth and development, provide guidance for healthy habits, and identify any concerns early so your child can build a strong foundation for lifelong oral health.

How Often Should My Child Have a Dental Checkup? Most children should visit the dentist every six months for regular dental checkups and preventive dental care. These visits may include a dental exam, growth and development monitoring, digital X-rays when needed, professional teeth cleanings, fluoride treatments, and personalized oral hygiene guidance to help keep your child’s teeth healthy and prevent cavities.

How Can I Help My Child Maintain Good Oral Hygiene at Home? Healthy habits at home make a big difference when it comes to preventing cavities and protecting your child’s oral health. You can help keep your child’s smile healthy by brushing twice a day with a pea-sized amount of fluoridated toothpaste, flossing daily once teeth begin to touch, limiting sugary snacks and drinks, encouraging fluoridated water, and offering a well-balanced diet.

Why Is Preventive Dentistry Important for Children? Pediatric preventive dentistry helps protect your child’s teeth before problems become bigger concerns. Regular preventive dental care can help reduce the risk of cavities, support healthy oral development, detect concerns early, and lower the chance of needing more extensive treatment in the future.
