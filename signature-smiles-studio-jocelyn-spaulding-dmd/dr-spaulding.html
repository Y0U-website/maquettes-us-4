Education, Training & Credentials

Dr. Spaulding’s training is unusually deep for a general dentist. She earned both her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) and a Master’s in Education from the University of Pennsylvania , one of the nation’s top dental schools.

Dental School & Advanced Degrees

At Penn, Dr. Spaulding completed a rigorous clinical and academic curriculum that gives her a foundation in both precision dentistry and patient education. Her Master’s in Education reflects her belief that informed patients make confident decisions.

Residency & Hospital Affiliations

She completed her hospital-based residency at NY Presbyterian Queens , an affiliate of Weill Cornell Medicine, where she treated complex cases in a hospital setting. International Training & Continuing Education

Dr. Spaulding has pursued training well beyond what is expected. She studied at Witten/Herdecke Universität in Germany, one of Europe’s leading medical universities. OUR CORE PHILOSOPHY

Our philosophy is to blend the wisdom that comes from decades of combined clinical experience with carefully selected advancements in dentistry. We take what has been validated through research and proven in practice, then combine it with our own experience and judgment as healthcare providers. In doing so, we are able to offer care that is modern, thoughtful, and centered on what is genuinely best for our patients.

Team & Patient Well-being

I see every member of my team as an important contributor to the care and experience our patients receive. Each person brings their own knowledge, experience, and perspective, and together we help support not only our patients’ oral health, but also their overall well-being and quality of life.

Evidence-Based Education

Because of this, I am committed to investing in ongoing education and advanced training for both myself and my team. Dentistry is constantly evolving, and I believe it is our responsibility to stay current with new research, techniques, and advancements so we can provide care that is both effective and meaningful. We strive to use evidence-based treatment approaches, allowing us to make recommendations that are grounded in sound science and proven clinical outcomes.

Thoughtful Innovation

At the same time, I believe experience matters. While I enjoy learning about new technologies and innovations, I am not someone who adopts every new trend simply because it is new. I prefer to see technologies and treatment methods stand the test of time before incorporating them into our practice. Once I have evaluated their effectiveness, reviewed the supporting research, and seen their value demonstrated in real-world clinical settings, I can confidently determine whether they will truly benefit our patients.
