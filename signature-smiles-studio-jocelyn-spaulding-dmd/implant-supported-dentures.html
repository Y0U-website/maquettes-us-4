What to Expect: Your Implant-Supported Denture Journey at Signature Smiles Studio

We believe in walking you through every stage so you feel informed and at ease. Here is how the process unfolds.

Initial Consultation and 3D Imaging

Your first visit begins with a conversation about your goals. Dr. Spaulding examines your mouth and takes a CBCT scan to assess your bone volume and structure. This imaging lets her plan the exact position and angle for each implant. You will leave with a clear understanding of your timeline and options.

Implant Placement and Healing

The implant placement itself is performed in our office with your comfort in mind. Options like nitrous oxide sedation are available. After the implants are placed, a healing period of several months allows them to fuse with your bone in a process called osseointegration. During this time you may wear a temporary restoration.

Final Restoration and Fit

Once healing is complete, your custom implant-supported denture is crafted and secured to the implants. Dr. Spaulding ensures the fit, bite, and appearance meet her standards. The result is a full arch of teeth that looks natural and feels like part of you.

What Happens If You Wait? The Cost of Leaving Full Arch Tooth Loss Untreated

When teeth are missing, the body begins to resorb the jawbone that once supported them. Over time this bone loss can change the shape of your face, cause remaining teeth to shift, and make future implant placement more complex. Nutritional choices may narrow as chewing becomes harder. The longer you wait, the more likely you will need additional procedures like bone grafting before implants are possible. Addressing tooth loss sooner preserves your bone and keeps treatment more straightforward.
