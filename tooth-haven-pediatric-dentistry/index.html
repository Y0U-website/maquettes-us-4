Book Appointment Frequently Asked Questions About Tooth Haven Your Child’s Dental Care Visiting Tooth Haven What is a pediatric dentist? A pediatric dentist is a dentist with additional specialty training in caring for the oral health of infants, children, teenagers, and patients with special health care needs.

Pediatric dentists understand children’s oral development and use child-friendly techniques to help make dental visits comfortable while providing care that’s tailored to growing smiles.

We care for infants, toddlers, children, and teenagers. Our team provides personalized dental care to support healthy smiles through every stage of childhood.

We recommend scheduling your child’s first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months after their first tooth appears. Early visits help monitor oral development and establish healthy dental habits.
