Dental Implant Cost

Factors That Shape the Cost of Dental Implants

Every implant procedure is different, and designed to meet individual oral health and smile goals. In some cases, preparatory treatment is necessary to create the right environment for dental implant success. Factors and preparatory procedures that influence the overall cost of dental implants include:

Tooth Extractions

The need to extract hopeless or failing teeth factors into dental implant cost. In many cases, we can extract teeth and place dental implants within the same visit.

Bone Grafting

Dental implants require sufficient bone levels for support. If bone loss has occurred, we may need to perform a bone grafting procedure before placing dental implants .

Gum Health

Dental implants work best in a healthy periodontal environment, as they rely on healthy gums and bone for support. Any areas of gum disease must be treated before starting implant surgery.

Type of Procedure

The number of missing teeth to replace and type of implant procedure, such as single, multiple or full arch dental implants , affect overall cost.
