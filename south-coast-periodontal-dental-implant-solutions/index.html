The practice

Many patients choose a periodontist to correct gum recession , excess gum tissue and other soft tissue concerns that affect the appearance of the smile. Through precision soft tissue procedures such as gum grafting , gingivectomies and crown lengthening, Dr. Faiella reshapes the gum line to create a balanced, beautiful smile and improve periodontal health.

Oral conditions certainly impact the smile, but also have an effect on systemic health . Dr. Faiella addresses each patient’s periodontal and implant needs with the mouth-body connection in mind.
