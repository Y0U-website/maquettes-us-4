Preventive Treatment Options

We offer a wide variety of preventive treatment options at Houska Dental. Dr. Randy practices conservative care and treats patients of all ages and walks of life. Our office is a judgment-free zone, so get the treatment you need today!

Cleaning & Hygiene

Routine teeth cleanings and oral exams are recommended every six months to keep your smile healthy and strong. Dr. Randy and our team will clean and polish your teeth, check on your oral health, and look at the condition of your existing dental work to ensure a clean bill of health in Vermillion.

Digital X-Rays

We use advanced digital x-ray technology to provide you with unique insights into your oral health and to minimize exposure to radiation. X-rays let us look for hidden issues with your oral health or existing dental restorations like fillings, crowns, and more.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments can be included with any teeth cleaning. Dr. Randy will apply a fluoride-rich compound to your teeth and leave it in place. This attracts minerals to your teeth, strengthening them and even reversing very minor decay known as “soft spots.”

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants use clear, durable plastic resin to coat your rear teeth. This resin is specifically designed to prevent cavities from forming. With proper care, dental sealants will protect you from cavities for 5 years or longer. This is an ideal treatment for cavity-prone patients of all ages.

Periodontal Care

Whether you’re suffering from mild gingivitis or a more serious case of gum disease, we offer treatments like deep cleaning (scaling and root planing) and other periodontal treatments to restore your oral health.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Dr. Randy provides oral cancer screenings as part of every six-month consultation at our office. This gives you peace of mind, especially if you’re high-risk due to smoking, excessive sun exposure, and other risk factors.

Night Guards

Teeth grinding (bruxism) can damage your teeth and lead to further oral health issues. We offer comfortable, custom-fitted nightguards to protect your smile at Houska Dental.
