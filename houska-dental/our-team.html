Implant Pathway

Dr. Randy is a proud board member of Implant Pathway , a nationally recognized continuing education program that trains dentists to place implants on patients that cannot afford the cost of care. Founded and directed by Dr. Justin Moody, Implant Pathway provides dentists with new skills to take home to their patients all while restoring the smiles of veterans, victims, and vulnerable individuals. By participating in Implant Pathway at New Horizon Institute, licensed dentists get world-class training and patients receive life changing implants at zero cost.
