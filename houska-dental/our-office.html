Stepping into our office feels like you’re visiting the home of a dear friend. A home that has ergonomic dental chairs and TVs in every operatory, so you can sit back and stream your favorite show (‘Teen Mom 2’ anyone?) while you receive treatment.

Technology

We believe in staying up-to-date with the latest advancements in dental techniques and technologies. By keeping up with the latest dental tools, we can provide you with the best care possible and make your visit feel like a breeze.

CEREC Primescan

This intraoral scanner processes over 1 million 3D points per second, resulting in a hyper-accurate model of your smile that we can use to diagnose and plan treatment.

Cone Beam Computed Tomography

Our CBCT scanner allows us to get a full image of the underlying structures of your teeth and gums, including bone, nerve pathways, and the surrounding soft tissues of your smile.

3D Printer

We can custom-craft all of our own dental crowns, implants, and other prostheses in-house with our very own 3D Printer, saving you time and ensuring your prosthetic fits your smile.
