Why implants are the closest thing to a natural tooth

An implant replaces the root as well as the visible tooth. That means it is fixed in place, you brush and floss it like your other teeth, and it helps keep the jawbone healthy where a tooth is missing.

Unlike a bridge, an implant doesn&#x27;t need neighboring teeth to be reshaped for crowns, and unlike a removable denture it won&#x27;t slip when you talk or eat.

Who is a candidate

Most healthy adults with a missing tooth can have an implant. Enough bone and healthy gums are needed; if bone has been lost, grafting can often rebuild it first. Smoking and uncontrolled diabetes can affect healing, so we&#x27;ll talk through your health history at the consultation.

Cost and financing

Implant treatment is priced per case because it depends on the number of teeth, bone health and the final restoration. Some dental plans contribute toward implants. You&#x27;ll get a written treatment plan and estimate before you decide.
