Prosthodontist Dr. Grace Lee was born in South Korea. She earned her dental degree from University of Maryland Baltimore College of Dental Surgery and received Specialty Certification and a Master of Science degree in Prosthodontic Dentistry from University of Texas Health Center at Houston. She completed the residency rotation in the Section of Oncologic Dentistry and Prosthodontics at the University of Texas, M.D. Anderson Cancer Center Houston, Texas.

Dr. Lee is a board eligible prosthodontist with over 20 years of experience. She specializes in implant restoration, esthetic, and reconstructive dentistry. She is committed to keep up with the latest technology and procedures to provide the highest quality care for patients. Her calming demeanor is known to put patients at ease with their dental experiences.

Dr. Lee had practiced in Northern Virginia for 15 years, but she decided to move to Pennsylvania to be close to the family.

In her free time, Dr. Lee loves spending time with her family. She enjoys spending time outdoors hiking and working out.
