Root Canal

Root Canal Treatment is a procedure aimed at saving a severely damaged or infected tooth. During the procedure, the infected pulp inside the tooth is carefully removed, and the space is cleaned and sealed to prevent further infection. Our expert team at Pure Smile Dentistry, North Wales, PA, is skilled in providing this essential treatment to relieve pain and preserve your natural smile.

Oral Surgery

Oral Surgery encompasses a range of surgical procedures related to the mouth, teeth, and jaw. At Pure Smile Dentistry in North Wales, PA, our experienced oral surgeons perform various treatments, including tooth extractions, dental implant placement, and corrective jaw surgeries. Rest assured that we prioritize your comfort and safety throughout every procedure.

Periodontal

Periodontal Treatment focuses on diagnosing and treating gum disease to maintain healthy gums and prevent tooth loss. At Pure Smile Dentistry in North Wales, PA, our dedicated team offers various periodontal therapies, including scaling and root planing, to remove plaque and tartar buildup and promote gum health. Proper periodontal care is essential for a strong foundation to support your teeth and overall oral health.
