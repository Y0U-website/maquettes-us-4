Meet Dr. Azizyan

Dr. David Azizyan, DMD

Dr. David Azizyan, DMD, is a highly skilled implant and cosmetic dentist dedicated to transforming smiles through advanced dental solutions. With extensive experience in implantology and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Azizyan focuses on single tooth and hybrid-retained implant teeth and comprehensive smile rehabilitations. He is a graduate of UCLA (BS 2011), Drexel University College of Medicine (MS 2013), and LECOM College of Medicine (DMD 2017), and has pursued advanced post-graduate education to stay at the forefront of modern dental techniques.

Dr. Azizyan has completed specialized training in implant dentistry, digital smile design, and full-arch rehabilitation, ensuring patients receive state-of-the-art care. Dr. Azizyan has taken the Spear Curriculum and completed the Rutgers University Maxicourse programs. Passionate about patient education and personalized treatment, he combines artistry with cutting-edge technology to deliver life-changing results. As the Clinical Director of All On Implants, Dr. Azizyan is committed to providing exceptional, high-quality care in a welcoming and innovative environment.

Modern Implant Dentistry in a Comfortable Environment Providing compassionate, gentle dentistry is the heart of our practice. By offering accessible financing options, we are able to lower barriers to superior dental care, which allows us to make real differences in our patients’ lives. Everything we do at All On Implants Philadelphia is with you in mind. We love to have fun with our patients and hope that we will be able to form lasting friendships as we help maintain your smile. We always provide honest, straightforward advice and strive to keep you informed every step of the way.
