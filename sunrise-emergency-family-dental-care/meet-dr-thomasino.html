Meet Dr. Thomasino

Michael J. Thomasino, DMD is the founder, owner and operator of dental offices throughout North Florida. His practices offer a range of services including complete, cosmetic and emergency dental care and are located on the Northside, Central and Westside areas of Greater Jacksonville. Sunrise Emergency & Family Dental Care is the latest addition to his family of practices with the objective to serve comprehensive dental needs, including but not limited to, Preventative, Restorative and Cosmetic procedures.

In 2006, he opened Jacksonville Emergency Dental, the city’s first acute dental care center, dedicated to treat the growing need with two locations. Dr. Thomasino has built a trusted reputation by providing convenient, quality, friendly and affordable dental services to his patients. Now, Sunrise Emergency & Family Dental Care, will offer complete dental services, cosmetic procedures and emergency care with an expert team of professionals ready to serve.

IF YOU HAVE A FEAR OF DENTAL WORK

In fact, some studies show that up to 75 % of Americans experience some level of dental related fear and 20 % avoid dental care because of it. If you have a fear of dental work, we want you to know that making you feel comfortable and safe at our office is our first priority. Dental anxieties and phobias present themselves in a wide variety of ways, and specific fears vary from person to person. Dental anxiety may be mild to moderate, and often takes the form of a general sense of worry and apprehension when thinking about an upcoming procedure.
