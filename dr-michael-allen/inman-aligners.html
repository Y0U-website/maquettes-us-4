The Inman aligner is similar to a retainer in appearance and is designed with nickel titanium coil springs that power the aligner bows. These aligner bows are in opposition to each other, and they work to gently guide your front teeth into their optimal positions. Benefits of the Inman aligner include:

A faster treatment most cases can be completed in six to 18 weeks

A removable appliance to fit your lifestyle and help you maintain good oral hygiene

An improved appearance as well as improved oral health

Inman Aligners for Quick Front Teeth Alignment

Please note that while your Inman aligner is removable, you will need to wear it regularly in order to achieve the best results from your treatment. When we provide this treatment, our dentist will discuss with you how long you should wear the aligner each day. You will need to remove the aligner before eating, and you should clean both the aligner and your teeth before placing the appliance back into your mouth.
