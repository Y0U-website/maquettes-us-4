Meet the Doctor

Dr. Artour Khachatrian has over 30 years of professional experience in General Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry, and Dental Restorations. He has training in minimally invasive dentistry, advanced techniques in bonding and veneers. Dr. Khachatrian attended the School of Dentistry at the Medical University of Yerevan in Armenia, and then received his Post-doctoral from the Dental School of Loma Linda University in California. Dr. Khachatrian specializes in Cosmetic Dentistry and provides patients with beautiful smiles.
