Our Damascus Location | New Smiles Kids Dentistry

Dr. Naru Baliga DMD | Pediatric Dentist | Damascus & Frederick, MD

New Smiles Kids Dentistry is proud to serve families in Damascus, Maryland and the surrounding communities. Our Damascus office is led by Dr. Naru Baliga, DMD , a Pediatric Dentist and board-certified Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, providing specialized dental care for infants, children, and teenagers in a warm, welcoming environment designed just for kids.

Why Damascus Families Choose New Smiles Kids Dentistry

Dr. Baliga completed an additional 2–3 years of pediatric dental residency training after dental school, specializing exclusively in children’s dental health — from newborns through teenagers, including children with special health care needs. Our entire office is designed with kids in mind: bright, friendly spaces, a team that speaks “kid,” and behavior management techniques that help even the most nervous children feel at ease.

We believe every child deserves a healthy, confident smile — and we make it our mission to build positive dental experiences from the very first visit.
