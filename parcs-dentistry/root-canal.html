The root canal you’ve been dreading is easier than you think.

Dr. Nguyen treats the tooth fully numbed and unhurried, so you keep your natural tooth without the horror story.

Signs you may need a root canal

Lingering tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold that doesn’t fade, pain when chewing, swelling near a tooth, or a darkening tooth. These can signal infection in the pulp. If you’re in pain now, it may be a dental emergency .

What the visit is actually like

We numb the tooth completely, then remove the infection and seal the inside while you relax with a ceiling TV. Most patients tell us it felt no worse than a filling. The relief usually starts the same day.

Protecting the tooth afterward

A treated tooth is more brittle, so we usually finish with a dental crown to protect it for the long run.

Treatment benefits A root canal treats an infected or inflamed tooth by removing the pulp inside, cleaning and sealing the canal, and saving the natural tooth. It relieves the pain it’s known for rather than causing it. Dr. Nguyen performs root canals in-house at PARCS Dentistry in Las Vegas, with sedation available.

Does a root canal hurt?

No, that’s the myth. We numb the tooth fully, and the procedure relieves the pain you came in with.

How long does a root canal take?

Many are completed in one to two visits, depending on the tooth.

Is it better to pull the tooth instead?

Keeping your natural tooth is almost always better for your bite and bone. We’ll give you an honest recommendation.
