What Are Different Types of Dental Implants?

When you choose dental implants in O’Fallon, IL , we may go over various types based on your situation.

Single-Tooth Implants

Missing just one tooth — or a few teeth in different spots? That’s what single-tooth implants are for. They replace a tooth with a post and crown, providing a great alternative to bridges.

All-on-X Implants

Have you lost multiple teeth on your upper or lower arch (or both)? These implants use fewer posts to replace several teeth in a row, making them a good alternative to removable dentures that may slip out of place.

Mini Dental Implants

Don’t have enough bone density for traditional implants? Mini implants are a lot thinner, taking up less space inside your jaw and speeding up the implant process.

Implant-Supported Bridges and Dentures

Thanks to their versatility, implants can serve as support and stability if you choose dentures or bridges to replace teeth.
