What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They serve as a strong foundation for dental crowns , bridges, or dentures, making them a reliable option for tooth replacement.

Dental implants offer unmatched stability and are the gold standard for permanent tooth replacement.

  • Implant post – integrates with the jawbone
  • Abutment – connects the implant to the crown
  • Crown – the visible, natural-looking tooth

The Dental Implant Process

We follow a precise, patient-friendly process to ensure long-term success:

1. Consultation & Digital Evaluation

Your implant dentist conducts a full exam with digital X-rays and 3D imaging to assess bone health and design a personalized plan.

2. Bone Grafting (If Needed)

If bone density is insufficient, grafting may be recommended to support long-term implant stability.

3. Implant Placement

The titanium implant is gently placed into the jawbone using advanced techniques for comfort and precision.

4. Healing & Osseointegration

Over 3–6 months, the implant naturally bonds with the jawbone, creating a strong foundation.

5. Final Restoration

A custom crown or prosthetic is placed, completing your natural-looking tooth replacement.
