Services We Provide

We offer implant dentistry services, including dental implants and implant-supported restorations, and other tooth replacement options that can improve your smile and overall oral health.

Single Tooth Dental Implants

Single-tooth dental implants are the industry standard for replacing missing teeth. A single-tooth dental implant is a titanium post that is surgically placed into your jaw. Over a few months, the post will bond with your jaw and provide a natural, functional root replacement. An abutment and dental crown are attached to the post and create a lifelike, fully functional artificial tooth.

Implant Supported Bridges

Implant-supported bridges are useful for people with more than one adjacent missing tooth. Rather than attaching the implant to two crowns, the bridge connects to two strong dental implants for multiple teeth.

Implant Supported Dentures

If you have many missing or compromised teeth, implant-supported dentures may be your best choice. You can choose from fixed or removable dentures. Implant-supported dentures do not cause rocking and fit problems like traditional dentures for artificial teeth.

Full Arch with Only Four Implants

These dentures are unique because the dentist can install them on the same day as the tooth extractions. Immediate-load implants secure the full denture arch.

Mini Dental Implants (MDIs)

A mini dental implant or MDI is a viable solution for patients who do not want to undergo the extended process of getting traditional implants. Mini dental implants use significantly smaller posts than single-tooth implants, making the surgery less invasive.

Guided Dental Implant Placement

Our specialized software helps us assess your case in advanced and create custom surgical guides for each implant procedure. This process allows us to place implants precisely, avoiding most complications.

Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Despite stubborn rumors, most patients can become a candidate for dental implants. If you have oral health problems such as gum disease or tooth decay, we must correct those before placing a dental implant. It would be best to tightly control underlying medical conditions such as diabetes or high blood pressure. If you have severe jawbone loss, you might need bone grafting. Within these parameters, there is no reason to avoid dental implants.

Frequently Asked Questions about the Dental Implant Procedure

How long does it take to get dental implants?

The entire process takes about 6 months from start to finish. It may take longer if you have special circumstances. After placing the implants, the gum and jaw need time to heal and bond with the post. After a few months, the dentist places the abutment and crown for the replacement tooth . Mini dental implants and full arches anchored by four implants have a shorter process.

How long does a dental implant last?

The posts themselves could last a lifetime with proper care. The crown may last about 7 years.

Are dental implants painful?

You will receive appropriate anesthesia. Some patients also choose sedation. The healing process may involve some temporary swelling, bruising, and discomfort.

How do I care for my dental implants?

You should care for a dental implant exactly how you would care for healthy natural teeth. Be sure to brush and floss as recommended.
