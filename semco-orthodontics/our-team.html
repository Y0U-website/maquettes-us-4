Our Team

Your Orthodontist Dr. Robert S. Semco, DMD, MS, D. ABDSM Dr. Semco graduated first in his class from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He did his orthodontic residency at the Mayo Clinic, which accepts only one resident per year. He is a Board-certified Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics and the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine, certified to treat patients with obstructive sleep apnea, snoring and TMJ disorders.

Dr. Semco has received many awards and belongs to many professional societies, too numerous to list, to keep abreast of the latest treatment techniques to provide you with state-of-the-art orthodontic treatment and dental sleep medicine.

When not providing you with excellent care he is loves going to continuing education conferences or having wonderful times with his family and friends.
