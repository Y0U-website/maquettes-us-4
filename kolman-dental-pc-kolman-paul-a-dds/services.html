Services

Kolman Dental, P.C. is proud to offer a variety of services to help you maintain or create a beautiful smile. Whether you simply need a gentle cleaning or a toothache that needs attention we will take care of you in a professional and efficient manner.

We currently offer dentistry for the entire family including: gentle cleanings, tooth colored fillings, veneers, crowns (cap), bridges, periodontal disease treatment (scaling and root planning), root canal treatment (endodontic therapy), extractions, dentures, removable partials, implant restorations, in office whitening (one appointment), take home whitening and limited orthodontic treatment (Clear Correct™).

Our office also offers digital x-rays. This allows for greatly reduced radiation exposure, faster imaging and better diagnosing.

Our main concern is helping you maintain your oral health and smile. Every patient over the age of 18 receives a head and neck cancer screening exam. By utilizing our extensive knowledge of the oral tissues we are able to visually inspect for any atypical changes. We will use our expertise to help you have a healthy and beautiful smile.
