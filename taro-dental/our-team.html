0 Our Team Dr. Rachel DiPasquale Dr. Rachel DiPasquale grew up in the Seattle area and attended Seattle University where she earned her Bachelor’s Degree. She then attended the University of Southern California Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry where she earned her DDS degree. She completed a one year GPR at the University of Washington.

Before moving to Honolulu in 2014, she worked on a Native American Indian Tribe and provided dental care for tribal members for 3 years and is the former Dental Director at Waikiki Health. She is the 2025 President of the Hawaii Dental Association (HDA). She is active in the tripartite at the national and state level and serves on programs and task forces for the HDA.

Member of : ADA, HDA, Honolulu County Dental Society

Professional Affiliations: PBN’s 40 under 40 in 2019
