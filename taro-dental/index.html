Taro Dental is conveniently located in the Hawaii Kai Executive Plaza of the Hawaii Kai Towne Center, nestled in the building between Outback Steakhouse and the Extra Space Storage building.

Our experienced dentists are adept at diagnosing and treating a wide range of dental issues, from routine cleanings and preventive care to complex restorative and cosmetic procedures.

Our clinic is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, ensuring that you receive the most effective and efficient care available. Whether you are seeking routine dental check-ups, teeth whitening, orthodontic treatment, or restorative solutions, our team is here to provide expert guidance and support every step of the way.
