  • Durability: With proper care, a single-tooth implant can last a lifetime, providing a permanent solution to tooth loss.
  • Preserves Oral Health: Unlike traditional bridges, which require the alteration of adjacent teeth, single-tooth implants leave your neighboring teeth untouched, promoting better long-term oral health.
  • Enhanced Functionality: You can enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions, as single-tooth implants restore full chewing capacity.

How is a single-tooth implant placed?

  • Consultation: Dr. William B. Bohannan will evaluate your oral health and discuss the treatment plan tailored to your needs.
  • Implant Placement: A small titanium post is surgically implanted into your jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root.
  • Healing Period: The implant fuses with the jawbone during a healing period, ensuring a stable foundation.
  • Abutment Placement: A connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant, serving as the anchor for the crown.
  • Crown Placement: A custom-made crown, crafted to match your natural teeth, is securely placed on the abutment.

Do single-tooth implants require special care?

No, single-tooth implants don&#x27;t demand any extraordinary maintenance. Simply follow your regular oral hygiene routine, including brushing, flossing, and routine check-ups. With proper care, your implant will remain healthy and functional for years to come.

Are single-tooth implants noticeable?

Not at all! Single-tooth implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth. Their seamless integration into your smile ensures that they are virtually indistinguishable from your surrounding teeth.

Are single-tooth implants covered by insurance?

The coverage for single-tooth implants can vary depending on your insurance plan. Our team at Peninsula Oral Surgery Burlingame can assist you in navigating the insurance process to maximize your benefits and make the procedure affordable.

Can a single-tooth implant match my natural tooth color?

Absolutely! Our advanced technology and materials allow us to create custom crowns that match the shade and shape of your natural teeth perfectly. Your smile will look completely natural, and no one will be able to tell the difference.
