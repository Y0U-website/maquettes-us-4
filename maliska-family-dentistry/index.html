Born and raised in Bryan, Dr. Patrick Patke is excited to return home and serve the community that has always been special to him. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Health from Texas A&M University before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The University of Texas San Antonio School of Dentistry.

Committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered care, Dr. Patke strives to make every patient feel comfortable and valued while helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles.

When he is not in the office, Dr. Patke enjoys building Legos, trying different coffee shops, and exploring new places with his fiancée, Jaqueline. They plan to get married in May of 2027 and build a future rooted in Bryan. Dr. Patke is proud to serve families with care and commitment for many years to come.
