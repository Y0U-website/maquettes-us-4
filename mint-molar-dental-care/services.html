General & Restorative Dentistry

Whether you’re treating a cavity, repairing a damaged tooth, or replacing missing teeth, our restorative services are designed to restore the health, function, and appearance of your smile. We use modern techniques and high-quality materials to provide durable, natural-looking results tailored to your needs.

Our general & restorative dentistry services include:

  • Scaling and root planing (deep cleanings)
  • Periodontal maintenance
  • Tooth-colored composite fillings
  • Custom night guards and sports mouth guards
  • Root canal therapy
  • Dental crowns
  • Dental bridges
  • Implant-supported crowns, bridges, and dentures
  • Complete and Partial dentures

Oral Surgery

Some dental conditions require minor surgical treatment to protect your oral health and preserve your smile. We offer a variety of in-office oral surgery procedures using gentle techniques and a patient-focused approach to maximize comfort and promote smooth healing.

  • Tooth extractions
  • Some wisdom tooth extractions
  • Complex extractions with bone grafting
  • Dental implant placement
  • Frenectomy
  • Gingivectomy

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile is one of the first things people notice. Cosmetic dentistry focuses on enhancing the appearance of your teeth while maintaining their health and function. Whether you’re looking for a brighter smile, straighter teeth, or a complete smile transformation, we offer personalized treatment options to help you achieve your goals.

Our cosmetic dentistry services include:

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Traditional orthodontics
  • Clear aligner therapy, including Invisalign®
