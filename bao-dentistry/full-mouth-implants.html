FAQ Full Mouth Implants

What are full mouth dental implants?

Full mouth implants replace an entire arch or both arches of teeth using multiple dental implants to support fixed, non-removable restorations.

How are full mouth implants different from dentures?

Full mouth implants are fixed in place and anchored to the jawbone, while dentures are removable and rest on the gums.

Are full mouth implants permanent?

Is the procedure painful?

The procedure is performed with local anesthesia, and most patients experience manageable discomfort during recovery.

How long does full mouth implant treatment take?

The timeline varies by case and healing needs. Treatment may take several months from start to final restoration.

Will full mouth implants look natural?

Yes. The restorations are custom-designed to match natural tooth appearance and facial structure.
