The practice

Dr. Teresina Wong is a 1990 graduate of University of the Pacific, School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California; one of the top dental university in the nation. Dr. Wong built her Benicia private practice from scratch in 1994, serving patients from different parts of the bay area from as far as Sacramento to Fresno.

Dr. Teresina Wong is an active member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, the Napa-Solano Dental Society, Hands-on Implant Institute, and Global Dental Implant Academy Study Group.

Besides managing her dental practice, Dr. Teresina Wong devotes majority of her time to her family. She enjoys the outdoor; biking, swimming, tennis, skiing, as well as gardening, baking, cake decoration and piano.
