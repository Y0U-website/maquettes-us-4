What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small rod that’s surgically inserted into the jawbone, replacing the entire structure of a missing tooth from the roots up to the chewing surface. Since these posts are made from biocompatible titanium, they’ll naturally fuse with the surrounding bone tissue over time. Once this process is complete, we can attach any number of prosthetic teeth to the implants.

The Dental Implant Process

The first step on your journey toward a newly rebuilt smile is a consultation with Dr. Makadia. Dental implant restoration is the final step before you get to see your new smile. Dr. Makadia will place an abutment on top of each implant. After this, the new crown (tooth) will be anchored to the abutment, and it will give you a natural-looking smile. Whether your replacement is a sturdy crown or removable prosthetic, like a bridge or denture, one thing is for sure — after your dental implant restoration is in place, you will be proud to show your complete smile once again!

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

It is no secret dental implants cost more than conventional tooth replacement solutions, but they are more cost effective long term. There is no set amount you can expect to pay because the cost is determined by your individual treatment plan. Since your treatment consists of multiple phases, you can pay for each aspect separately, giving you more time. We will be happy to give you a personalized estimate during your initial consultation and discuss your payment options to keep your new smile affordable. We are just a phone call away for setting up your initial consultation.

Types of Dental Implants

Several different types of dental implants can be used to replace your missing teeth. The number of teeth that you are replacing, the materials used, and the types of implant posts can influence the cost. Some common types of implant posts include:

  • Traditional implant posts
  • Mini implant posts
  • Ball and socket implants
  • All On 4 implants

What are the Stages of Dental Implant Treatment?

Your treatment is performed in multiple stages, each with a separate cost. By paying for each step separately, it is easier to afford your new smile since you do not need to pay the entire amount upfront.

  • Initial Consultation: To plan your treatment, we will take some digital x-rays and a CT scan.
  • Additional Procedures: It is common to need additional treatments beforehand, like tooth extractions or a sinus lift.
  • Implant Placement: The procedure needed to surgically insert your implant posts into your jawbone.
  • Abutment & Restoration: You will need a second minor surgery to place special fixtures called abutments on the implant posts. These are needed to connect your custom made restorations.

Are Dental Implants Worth the Investment?

Dental implants are more affordable than traditional treatments long term because they have the potential to last for several decades. Not to mention, they improve your oral health which lowers your overall dental expenses. The implant posts stimulate your jaw to prevent bone loss to keep any remaining natural teeth firmly in place. Stopping dental drift reduces your risk of tooth decay, gum disease, alignment issues, and additional tooth loss.

Does My Dental Insurance Cover Dental Implants?

Your insurance carrier will not cover the entire cost of dental implants, but it may pay for certain aspects of your treatment, such as your restorations. We will work on your behalf with your insurance company to lower the amount you need to pay out of pocket. We also accept several payment options, including CareCredit, to cover any remaining balance.
