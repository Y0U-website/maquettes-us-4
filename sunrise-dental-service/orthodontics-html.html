Traditional Orthodontics

Braces are a treatment that has stood the test of time in providing the quality results patients want. The system uses metal brackets and wires to gradually shift the teeth into a more functional and attractive orientation. Traditional orthodontics is great for patients of all ages and can address many problems that would otherwise require surgery to correct.

Invisalign Clear Braces

The Invisalign system uses a series of clear, plastic aligners to gradually shift the teeth into place, a method that allows a patient to enjoy beautiful, well aligned teeth without anyone ever knowing they are undergoing orthodontic treatment. This is something both busy adults and image conscious teens can appreciate. Plus, the trays are removable, so a patient doesn’t have to alter their diet or oral hygiene habits throughout the treatment process.

SureSmile

Similar to Invisalign, SureSmile is also a clear aligner treatment that can straighten the teeth both discreetly and comfortably. Each treatment is custom-made for a patient based on a digital scan of their teeth, and the edges of the trays are smoothed to ensure a seamless fit. The biggest difference between SureSmile and Invisalign is the price SureSmile is notably more affordable while still being able to deliver the same high-quality results.
