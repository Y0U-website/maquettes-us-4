Payment Options

We work with ALL major PPO dental insurance plans and accept all credit cards. For patients without insurance coverage, we offer a comprehensive membership plan along with discounted rates for both new and existing patients.

Note: We are not in-network with OHP. We do not accept DHMO, HMO or medicaid/state funded plans.

Prepare for Your Appointment

To help us prepare for your appointment, we’ll need to collect some important information, including your medical history and background. We’ll email you the necessary forms ahead of time, so you can conveniently complete them on your phone, tablet, or computer before arriving at our location.
