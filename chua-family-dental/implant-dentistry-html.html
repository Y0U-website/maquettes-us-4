Common Questions about Implant Dentistry

What are dental implants?

Dental implants serve as tooth roots. Dr Chua inserts a titanium post into the jaw. This post contains one of the teeth replaced. The implant becomes strong and natural upon healing, as you eat or speak.

An implant tooth does not rest on the gums like dentures. It remains attached to the bone, making it strong and stable. This renders implants as one of the surest ways of replacing missing teeth in the present world.

This is provided by such modern implant care where patients are able to smile, chew, and speak again confidently. Most of these individuals claim that implants are like their natural teeth.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are effective in a large number of patients. Implants can be a good option in case you have one or more missing teeth. They also work on broken or irreversible teeth.

The tooth implant might be as expensive as a root canal, a build-up, and a crown to some patients. The point of difference is that implants can have a long service life. They are also used in protecting the jawbone, which may shrink with the age of missing teeth

How Dental Implants Work

Surgery of implanting the teeth is direct and simple. Dr Chua cuts a little hole in the gum and gets the place ready to implant. Then he carefully and precisely fits the implant into the bone.

The process can be performed in less than 30 minutes. A lot of patients experience minimal or no pain. The majority of them resume normally the same day. Directed implantation assists in making the procedure predictable and smooth.

When it is fixed, the bone gradually fuses with the implant. This forms a good foundation for the replacement tooth. The crown is then glued to the implant after the healing, and then it completes your smile.

Dental implants should have a lifetime. Implants do not decay, as opposed to natural teeth. This titanium material connects well with the bone and remains strong.
