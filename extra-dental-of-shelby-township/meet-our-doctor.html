Meet Dr. Yaseen

Dr. Yaseen pursued her dream of becoming a dentist by enrolling in the University of Illinois at Chicago School of Dentistry, where she graduated with honors in 2012.

Over the years, Dr. Yaseen has continued to refine her skills and knowledge by attending various dental conferences and seminars. She has also invested in modern dental technology and equipment to ensure that her patients receive the best possible care. Dr.Yaseen is currently a member of the American dental Association ( ADA) and the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD)

Dr. Yaseen is known for her warm and friendly demeanor, which makes her patients feel more comfortable during their dental appointments. She takes the time to listen to their concerns and answer their questions, ensuring that they have a clear understanding of their treatment options. Her calm and reassuring manner has earned her the trust and respect of her patients.

Away from the office, Dr. Yaseen always looks forward to spending the time with her two kids. She also enjoys traveling and personal fitness.
