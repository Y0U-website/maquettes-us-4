Step 2: Comprehensive Smile Evaluation

Our evaluation goes far beyond your teeth. Using advanced digital scans, X-rays, and 3D imaging, we assess your teeth, bite, jaw alignment, and how your mouth functions in everyday life. This includes examining patterns of breathing, and speaking, to ensure that your treatment supports overall oral function and wellness.

Understanding all of these factors helps us design a treatment plan that not only straightens teeth but also promotes comfortable function, long-term stability, and overall oral health.

  • Bite alignment and jaw positioning
  • Tooth and jaw growth patterns
  • Breathing and oral posture
  • Speech and swallowing habits
  • Lifestyle factors, including oral habits and daily routines

Step 3: Personalized Treatment Plan

After your exam, Dr. Miranda will create a custom treatment plan tailored to your needs and lifestyle. Every plan considers aesthetics, function, and long-term oral wellness.

Our goal is to ensure every patient feels informed, confident, and empowered as they begin treatment.

  • Recommended treatment type (braces, clear aligners, or early intervention)
  • Timeline for your care and key milestones
  • Strategies to support proper jaw development, oral function, and healthy habits
  • Clear guidance on what to expect at each appointment

Step 4: Begin Your Treatment

Once your treatment plan is ready, we start your orthodontic journey with careful guidance and ongoing support. Whether you choose braces or clear aligners, we provide the tools, education, and encouragement needed to achieve your healthiest, most confident smile.
