Smile Makeovers

We will work with you to understand your desires and recommend the best procedures to create a natural-looking, confident smile. The goal is to create a harmonious, natural-looking smile that boosts confidence and improves overall facial aesthetics. Here at Dr. Kathy Zahedi DDS & Associates, we will work closely with patients to customize the treatment plan based on individual needs and desires. We consider smile makeovers to focus on the visual aspects and the functionality and health of the teeth to achieve a balanced and attractive result.

  • Chips or cracks? Veneers or crowns can cover imperfections.
  • Misalignment? Braces or Clear Aligners can straighten things out.
  • Missing teeth? Implants or bridges can fill the gaps.

What procedures are available for a smile makeover?

No two smiles are alike, and neither are our treatment plans. After a thorough evaluation of your concerns, needs, and dental issues, our professionals design a personalized treatment plan just for you.

  • Implants : Combat jawbone tissue loss with stable dental implants, supporting bridges, crowns, or dentures. Osseointegration ensures stability and stimulates new bone tissue formation.
  • Sinus Lift : Address upper jaw bone loss with a sinus lift, creating a secure foundation for dental implants by adding bone density.
  • Crowns and Bridges : Affordable alternatives, restoring bite and function by replacing missing teeth. In-house CEREC® technology ensures quick production of all-porcelain crowns.
  • Dentures : Choose full or partial dentures based on your needs, guided by our expert team considering various options.
  • Tooth-Colored Fillings : Opt for seamless restoration using tooth-colored composite resin over amalgam fillings for a natural look.
  • Periodontal Care : Combat gum diseases with specialized oral hygiene and deep cleaning as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.
  • Root Canal Therapy : Painless modern root canal therapy eliminates infection and toothaches, followed by crown placement for normal function restoration.
