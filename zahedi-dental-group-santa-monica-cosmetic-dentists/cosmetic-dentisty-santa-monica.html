Frequently Asked Cosmetic Dental Questions

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive and involve little to no discomfort. Dentists often use local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort during treatments. ​

Typically, dental insurance doesn’t cover procedures considered purely cosmetic. However, if a treatment also improves oral health or function, partial coverage might be available. ​

Anyone looking to improve their smile’s appearance and who maintains good oral health is a potential candidate. It’s essential to consult with a qualified cosmetic dentist to determine the best treatment plan for your needs.​

General dentistry focuses on maintaining oral health by preventing and treating issues like cavities and gum disease. Cosmetic dentistry, on the other hand, aims to enhance the appearance of your smile, though some procedures can also improve oral health.
