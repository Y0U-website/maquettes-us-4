What Is Implant Restoration?

Implant restoration is the process of repairing or replacing the visible portion of a dental implant, such as the crown, bridge, or denture, while preserving the implant post whenever possible. Before recommending treatment, we&#x27;ll carefully evaluate your implant and surrounding tissues to ensure the implant remains healthy and stable.

Implant restoration may be recommended if you have

  • A loose implant crown
  • A chipped or cracked implant restoration
  • Worn or damaged implant-supported teeth
  • A missing implant crown
  • Difficulty chewing comfortably
  • Changes in your bite or implant function
  • Cosmetic concerns with an older restoration

What to Expect During Your Appointment

Your visit begins with a comprehensive examination and digital imaging to assess the condition of your implant and restoration. If the implant post is healthy, we&#x27;ll repair or replace the restoration with a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture designed to match your natural smile. Our goal is to restore optimal function while ensuring a comfortable, secure fit.

Benefits of Implant Restoration

Timely implant restoration helps protect your investment and maintain the health of your smile.

  • Restores comfortable chewing and speaking
  • Improves the appearance of your smile
  • Replaces worn or damaged restorations
  • Helps maintain proper bite alignment
  • Protects the underlying dental implant
  • Extends the life of your implant treatment
