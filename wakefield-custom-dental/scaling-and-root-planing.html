What Is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing, often called a deep dental cleaning, is a specialized treatment for patients with early to moderate gum disease. Unlike a routine cleaning that focuses on the visible surfaces of your teeth, this procedure removes plaque, tartar, and bacteria from beneath the gumline and smooths the tooth roots to help the gums reattach and heal properly.

Scaling and root planing may be recommended if you have

  • Bleeding gums while brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Gum recession
  • Deep periodontal pockets
  • Early to moderate gum disease
  • Plaque and tartar buildup below the gumline
