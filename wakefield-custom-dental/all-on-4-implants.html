What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is an innovative implant solution designed for patients who have lost most or all of their upper or lower teeth. Instead of replacing each missing tooth individually, four dental implants are placed in the jawbone to securely support a full arch of custom prosthetic teeth. This approach offers a permanent alternative to traditional removable dentures while improving comfort, stability, and chewing ability.

All-on-4 may be an ideal solution if you

  • Are missing most or all of your teeth
  • Wear loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Have multiple failing or severely damaged teeth
  • Want a fixed, non-removable tooth replacement
  • Are looking for a long-term solution to tooth loss
  • Have sufficient jawbone to support dental implants
