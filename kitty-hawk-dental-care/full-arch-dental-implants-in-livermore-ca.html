What are implant-supported dentures?

Implant-supported dentures represent a contemporary solution for replacing teeth by securing a complete set of dentures to dental implants that are inserted into your jawbone. Unlike traditional dentures, they are securely attached, providing superior stability and a natural feel. No more worrying about slipping dentures or messy adhesives. Implant-supported dentures stay put while you talk, eat, and smile!

How do full-arch dental implants work?

Full-arch dental implants involve strategically placing four to six implants in your jaw to support a custom-made denture. The implants are the foundation that holds your denture securely in place. This setup allows you to fully enjoy the functionality of natural teeth without the hassle of removable prosthetics.

Are implant-supported dentures comfortable?

Absolutely! Many of our patients find implant-supported dentures far more comfortable than traditional ones. Since they are firmly anchored, there is no rubbing or shifting against your gums, and they feel much more like natural teeth. Plus, they allow you to bite and chew your favorite foods without any restrictions.

Can anyone get full-arch dental implants?

While full-arch dental implants are a great option for many people, a consultation is necessary to determine if they’re right for you. Dr. Kimberly King evaluates your oral health, jawbone density, and overall health to create a personalized plan. Even if you have experienced some bone loss, options like bone grafts may make you a strong candidate.

How long does the process take?

The timeline varies depending on your specific and individual needs, but the process typically takes a few months. Once the implants have been inserted, a recovery period is necessary for them to bond with your jawbone. Once healed, your custom dentures are securely attached, giving you a beautiful and functional smile.

How do I care for my new implant-supported dentures?

Caring for your implant-supported dentures is straightforward and similar to caring for natural teeth. Brush and floss daily to maintain the health of both your implants and gums. Dr. Kimberly King and her team also provide tips and recommend routine dental checkups to ensure your smile stays in great shape.

What are the advantages of selecting full-arch dental implants instead of conventional dentures?

Complete arch dental implants provide many benefits compared to conventional dentures. They provide unmatched stability, prevent jawbone loss, and look and feel more natural. Additionally, they eliminate the common frustrations associated with traditional dentures, like slipping, discomfort, and limited food choices.
