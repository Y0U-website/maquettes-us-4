What to Expect During a Cleaning

Your appointment starts with a comprehensive evaluation by our dental hygienist, who will assess the condition of your teeth and gums. Based on the findings, we’ll decide the best cleaning option for you and then one of our hygienists will proceed with cleaning.

Types of Dental Cleanings

Regular Cleaning (Prophylaxis)

For patients with healthy gums, a routine cleaning focuses on removing plaque, tartar, and surface stains to keep your smile clean, polished, and protected against gum disease. If early signs of gum problems are present, our dental team may suggest more frequent cleanings to help prevent further issues.

Deep Cleaning (Scaling and Root Planing)

If you’re experiencing symptoms like bleeding or sensitive gums, tartar beneath the gum-line, or deep periodontal pockets, we may recommend a deep cleaning. At South Scottsdale Dental Care, deep cleanings are designed to remove harmful bacteria and buildup below the gum-line, supporting gum healing and helping to stop the advancement of gum disease.

Periodontal Maintenance

After a deep cleaning, ongoing periodontal maintenance is essential for preserving long-term gum health. This treatment involves removing plaque and tartar from both above and below the gumline, thoroughly cleaning each tooth, and finishing with a polish. Our dental team may recommend this continued care to help prevent gum disease from returning and to promote overall oral wellness.

Why Teeth Cleanings are Important

Professional cleanings do more than enhance your smile—they play a vital role in preventing serious dental issues down the road.
