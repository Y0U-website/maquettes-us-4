We have provided a list of tips that will help make your family trip to the dentist enjoyable and go as smoothly as possible. Enjoyable as possible:

Children can sense discomfort, and if a parent is afraid of the dentist, it may make the child be afraid without knowing why. For this reason, we recommend that if a parent is afraid of the dentist, that the other parent brings the children to the dentist.

We are proud to be a member of the ADA (American Dental Association) and love to share some additional links that may help to make the dentist more enjoyable for your children.
