All-on-4 Dental Implants

A Permanent, Life-Changing Solution for Full-Arch Tooth Replacement

A Full Smile with Just Four Implants

If you’ve lost most or all of your teeth in one arch and want a stable, long-term solution, All-on-4 dental implants could be exactly what you’re looking for. This advanced technique restores a full set of teeth using just four strategically placed implants—offering a permanent, natural-looking alternative to removable dentures.

What Are All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 is a surgical procedure that allows for full-arch restoration. Here’s how it works:

The result? A full, fixed smile that looks, feels, and functions like your natural teeth.

  • Any remaining teeth in the arch are extracted (if needed).
  • Four dental implants are placed in carefully selected positions within the jawbone.
  • A full arch of prosthetic teeth is securely attached to the implants—often on the same day.

Key Benefits of All-on-4

  • Fixed in Place: No slipping, no adhesives—just a secure, stable bite.
  • Lifelike Appearance: Custom-designed to match your features and enhance your smile.
  • Eat and Speak with Confidence: Enjoy your favorite foods again and speak clearly without worry.
  • Supports Bone Health: Implants help preserve the jawbone and prevent further deterioration.
  • Fast Results: In many cases, patients leave with a brand-new smile in just one visit.

Is All-on-4 Right for You?

  • Are missing most or all of your upper or lower teeth
  • Want a permanent alternative to traditional dentures
  • Have adequate jawbone support (or are open to grafting if needed)
  • Are ready for a smile that restores comfort, function, and confidence
