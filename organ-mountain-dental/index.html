The practice

Dr. Wolf isn’t your typical dentist, and Organ Mountain Dental isn’t your typical practice. Originally from the Kansas City suburbs, he’s known for his down-to-earth personality and approachable care. He earned his Bachelor&#x27;s degree from the University of Kansas and his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry in 2014.

At Organ Mountain Dental, our team is dedicated to making every visit comfortable, efficient, and personalized. With years of experience across all areas of dentistry, we work together to deliver high-quality care while creating a welcoming, friendly environment for every patient.

Dental Implants Permanent, natural-looking dental implants to replace missing teeth and restore full function. Enjoy improved chewing, speech, and confidence with long-lasting implant solutions in Las Cruces.

Full-Arch Solutions (All-on-X / 3-on-6) Replace a full arch of teeth with All-on-X or 3-on-6 dental implants. This full mouth dental implant solution provides a secure, long-lasting smile designed for comfort, function, and aesthetics.

Same-Day Teeth Get same-day teeth with advanced dental implants and our in-house lab. Walk in with missing or failing teeth and leave with a fully restored smile in just one visit.

Bone Grafting Bone grafting helps rebuild jawbone structure to support dental implants. This procedure creates a strong, stable foundation for long-term implant success and full mouth restoration.

Oral Sedation Relax comfortably with oral sedation dentistry, taken as a simple prescribed tablet before your visit. Ideal for dental anxiety, it keeps you calm and at ease while you receive care safely and stress-free.
