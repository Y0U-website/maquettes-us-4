What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are a restorative solution that combines the affordability of dentures with the stability of dental implants. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on the gums and rely on adhesives, these dentures snap onto titanium implants surgically placed in the jawbone. This connection prevents the denture from slipping or shifting, providing a more comfortable and functional alternative for patients who are missing an entire arch of teeth.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

We focus on providing a solution that eliminates the common frustrations of traditional removable teeth. By utilizing dental implants, we offer a level of security that transforms your daily experience:

Eliminate Slipping and Sliding

The secure attachment to implants ensures your dentures stay firmly in place, allowing you to speak and laugh without the fear of your teeth shifting.

Improved Chewing Ability

With a stable foundation, you can enjoy a wider variety of foods that are often difficult to eat with standard dentures, significantly improving your nutrition and quality of life.

Jawbone Preservation

The implants stimulate the jawbone, which helps slow down the bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth, maintaining your natural facial shape over time.

The Implant-Supported Dentures Process

Initial Digital Assessment

We use advanced 3D imaging to evaluate your jawbone and determine the precise placement for the implants that will support your denture.

Personalized Treatment Planning

Our 3D design software allows us to plan the placement in minutes, ensuring the implants are positioned for maximum stability and a perfect snap-on fit.

Implant Placement Surgery

Two or more titanium implants are carefully placed in the jaw. Our minimally invasive techniques focus on a comfortable procedure and a faster recovery time.

Healing & Integration

During the healing period, the implants fuse with your natural bone. We provide temporary solutions through our in-house lab so you are never without teeth.

Final Denture Customization

We craft or modify your denture in our on-site lab to ensure it snaps perfectly onto the implants. This in-house control allows for a faster final fit and direct savings for the patient.
