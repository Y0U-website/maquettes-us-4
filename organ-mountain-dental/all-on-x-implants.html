What Are All-on-X Dental Implants?

All-on-X implants replace an entire arch of missing teeth using a few strategic titanium posts. This "X" represents the number of implants (usually 4, 5, or 6) that provide a stable, permanent foundation. It is the gold standard for full-mouth restoration, offering a natural look and feel while preserving jawbone health without extensive grafting.

The All-on-X Implants Process

Consultation

We perform a detailed digital scan and evaluation of your entire jaw structure to determine the optimal number of implants needed to support your new smile.

Digital 3D Smile Design

Using advanced 3D software, we design your entire arch in-house. This allows us to plan precise placement and preview your final aesthetic results in minutes.

Strategic Implant Placement

The implants are carefully positioned to create a high-strength foundation. Our minimally invasive technique focuses on stability and preserving your natural tissue.

Temporary Same-Day Restoration

Thanks to our in-house lab, we can often provide a beautiful, functional set of temporary teeth on the same day, so you never have to be without a smile.

Final Zirconia Bridge Fitting

Once healed, we attach your custom zirconia bridge. Our 3x6 segmented design ensures a 40 % lighter feel and a perfect fit that blends with your natural gum line.
