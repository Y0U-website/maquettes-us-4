What to expect from the dental implant process

Every case is unique, but most implant journeys include these steps:

1. Consultation & planning

  • Comprehensive exam, x-rays, and often a 3D scan
  • Review of your medical history and goals
  • Discussion of your options (implants vs bridge vs denture)
  • Clear treatment plan, including estimated fees and insurance benefits

2. Implant placement

  • A specialist places a small titanium post in the jawbone where the tooth is missing.
  • The area is numbed and you’ll receive detailed after-care instructions.
  • Over a few months, the implant integrates (fuses) with the bone.

3. Healing & temporary tooth (if needed)

  • During healing, you may wear a temporary tooth or appliance to fill the space.
  • We’ll see you for checks to monitor healing and comfort.

4. Final crown or restoration

  • Once the implant is stable, we take digital impressions.
  • A custom crown, bridge, or implant denture is crafted to match your smile.
  • Dr. Vora attaches and adjusts it so your bite feels natural.
  • We’ll see you for checks to monitor healing and comfort.
