What to Expect From Cleaning & Hygiene

Comprehensive Exam

Your appointment begins with a thorough examination by Dr. Homan and our team. We’ll review your oral history, take digital X-rays or CBCT scans as needed, and evaluate your teeth and gums for any signs of decay, inflammation, or other concerns. Each step of the process is explained carefully, so you feel confident and informed about your oral health status.

Professional Cleaning

Using advanced tools, we’ll gently remove plaque and tartar buildup from your teeth and along the gumline. Our precision techniques ensure you stay comfortable and stress-free throughout your cleaning. Polishing and flossing your teeth are the finishing touches, leaving your smile feeling smooth, fresh, and radiant.

Personalized Recommendations

To maximize the benefits of your cleaning, we’ll provide individualized tips for at-home care tailored to your specific needs. We’ll discuss any follow-up treatments if needed and schedule your next visit to keep your smile on track. Our goal is for every patient to leave feeling empowered to maintain a lifetime of excellent oral health.
