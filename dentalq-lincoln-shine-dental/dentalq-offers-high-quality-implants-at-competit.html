What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants offer an innovative solution for patients missing multiple teeth. This advanced procedure uses just four strategically placed implants to support a complete arch of replacement teeth. As a result, you get a permanent, stable option that feels and functions like natural teeth. Enjoy eating, speaking, and smiling with renewed confidence.
