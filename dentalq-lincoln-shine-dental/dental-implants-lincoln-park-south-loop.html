What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth. These titanium posts act as artificial roots, supporting crowns, bridges, or dentures. Not only do implants improve the look of your smile, but they also prevent bone loss and enhance your ability to chew and speak.

Dental Implants: The Process

Getting dental implants at DentalQ involves a few key steps to ensure long-lasting results: Consultation & Evaluation: We start with a detailed examination to assess your oral health, including X-rays or 3D scans. Implant Placement: The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. This will act as the foundation for your new tooth. Healing Period: Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your bone in a process called osseointegration. Restoration: Once healing is complete, we attach the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture, restoring your smile to full function.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Natural Look & Feel: Implants look, feel, and function just like natural teeth. Durability: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime. Improved Oral Health: Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering nearby teeth, preserving your natural tooth structure. Bone Preservation: Implants stimulate bone growth, preventing the bone loss that occurs with missing teeth.
