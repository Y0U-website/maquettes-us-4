Phase One Orthodontics: Building the Foundation for Lifelong Oral Health

Phase One orthodontics usually begins between ages 6 and 10, before all permanent teeth have come in. While many kids don’t need braces until their teenage years, some benefit from early care to address jaw growth concerns, significant crowding, or bite problems.

This stage of treatment is all about preparation. We guide jaw development and make space for permanent teeth to erupt in healthier positions. By intervening early, we can often reduce the chances of impacted teeth and may help children avoid future tooth extractions or even jaw surgery. Benefits of Phase One orthodontics include:

At Legacy Hill Dentistry, we design personalized early treatment plans centered on your child’s growth, comfort, and long-term smile success.

  • Guiding proper jaw growth
  • Creating space for incoming permanent teeth
  • Reducing the risk of impacted teeth
  • Correcting harmful oral habits (thumb sucking, tongue thrusting)
  • Improving facial balance and bite alignment
  • Lowering the likelihood of more complex treatment later
