Meet Dr. Lauriant P. Azangue, DDS

Dr. Lauriant P Azangue, DDS, is a seasoned professional with over 15 years of experience in the dynamic field of dentistry. Having practiced in diverse locations like South Carolina, California, and the Dallas area, he finally found his home in El Paso five years ago, where he has been serving the community ever since.

Education

Dr. Azangue’s journey into dentistry began at Rutgers University in New Jersey, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree. His thirst for knowledge didn’t stop there; he further pursued a two-year residency program at the University of Minnesota. Here, he specialized in Temporomandibular Disorders (TMD) and Orofacial Pain, equipping him with the skills to manage complex dental issues.

Skills and Experience

Beyond general dentistry and TMD and Orofacial Pain management, Dr. Azangue is also proficient in Oral Surgery, including wisdom teeth removal, Implant placement, and restoration, endodontics (root canal therapy), and Invisalign, among others. His extensive training and multiple certifications are a testament to his commitment to excellence in dental care.

Dr. Azangue’s experience extends beyond civilian practice he has served the US military across the country for over 13 years, providing top-notch dental services to those who serve our nation.

Passion and Philanthropy

At the heart of Dr. Azangue’s practice is his passion for his patients. His mission is to eliminate pain, restore smiles, and improve quality of life through exceptional dental care.

Outside his practice, Dr. Azangue is an avid philanthropist. He frequently travels abroad for medical and dental missions, providing much-needed care in underserved communities.

In Dr. Azangue, you’ll find more than a dentist; you’ll find a dedicated healthcare professional committed to your well-being, both in the clinic and beyond.
