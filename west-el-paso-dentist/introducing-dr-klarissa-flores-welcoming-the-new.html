Introducing Dr. Klarissa Flores…

We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Klarissa Flores to the West El Paso Dentist team. With a passion for dentistry that began in her childhood and a dedication to improving patient lives, Dr. Flores brings both expertise and enthusiasm to our practice.

Inspiration and Passion for Dentistry

Dr. Flores’s journey into dentistry started during her own orthodontic treatment as a child. Fascinated by the process, she knew she wanted a career in healthcare. As she delved deeper into the dental field, she discovered that this was her true calling. What she enjoys most about dentistry is the ability to help people and enhance their quality of life, whether through a simple cleaning or a full smile restoration. The ever-evolving nature of dentistry keeps her engaged and passionate about her work.

Memorable Achievements

One of Dr. Flores’s most cherished experiences is her involvement with Texas Mission of Mercy events, which she began attending as a pre-dental student. Over the years, she has volunteered at these events, providing free dental care to those in need. Her participation in these events has been profoundly rewarding, reinforcing her belief that everyone deserves access to dental care. This commitment to community service is something she plans to continue throughout her career.

Staying Ahead in Dental Advancements

Dr. Flores is committed to staying at the forefront of dental technology and techniques. She regularly participates in continuing education courses and enjoys reading research articles and attending clinician-led webinars. This dedication to learning ensures that her patients receive the most up-to-date and effective dental care.

Life Beyond the Dental Office

Outside of work, Dr. Flores leads an active and fulfilling life. She enjoys spending time with her dog, Lola, visiting local coffee shops, and exploring new places. She is also passionate about fitness, frequently attending the gym, and is currently embracing the challenge of learning to golf.

Schedule Your Next Visit With Dr. Flores!

We invite you to meet Dr. Flores and experience the exceptional care she provides. Whether you’re a new patient or a returning one, you can trust that your dental health is in capable and caring hands. Welcome to our dental family, Dr. Flores!
