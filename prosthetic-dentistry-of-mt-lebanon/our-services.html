Dental Services for Every Patient’s Unique Needs

At Prosthetic Dentistry of Mt. Lebanon, PC, we are dedicated to providing dentistry tailored to each individual. Our team specializes in advanced techniques and modern technology, ensuring every patient receives personalized attention and high-quality results.

What We Specialize In

General Dentistry

General dentistry services help maintain healthy teeth and gums through routine checkups and cleanings to patients of all ages from childhood through adulthood.

Dental Implants

Dental implants restore function and aesthetics, providing a permanent solution for missing teeth.

Dentures & Partials

Dentures and partials are custom-fit to help you eat, speak, and smile confidently while maintaining facial structure.

Crowns & Bridges

Crown and bridge options address damaged or missing teeth with durable, natural-looking restorations.

3D Scanning Technology

3D scanning technology enhances precision in diagnostics and treatment planning for every procedure.

Advanced Restorative Dentistry

Experience in advanced restorative dentistry allows us to handle complex dental cases, restoring both function and appearance.

Cosmetic

We believe that a beautiful smile radiates positive energy and confidence. Our professional in-office and at-home whitening treatments, bondings, and veneers can help patients achieve their brightest smile.

Transform Your Smile
