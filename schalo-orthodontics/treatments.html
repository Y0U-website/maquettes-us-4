Treatments Early Treatment Here at Schalo Orthodontics, we are able to enhance someone’s smile and correct orthodontic issues at any age. However, the American Association of Orthodontists has concluded that if a child begins orthodontic screenings at the age of 7, it is easier to anticipate what problems and treatment may lie ahead.

Around the age of 7, a child’s first adult molars emerge, giving us the ability to evaluate the entire mouth and predict things like overbite, teeth crowding, large gaps, underbite, and so on. This proactive approach not only enhances your child’s oral health, but also boosts their self-confidence by promoting a beautiful, healthy smile from a young age.

Adult Treatment

Braces

Braces are a highly effective orthodontic treatment for correcting misaligned teeth and bite issues. They work by applying gentle, continuous pressure to gradually shift teeth into their proper positions.

Metal braces are most common as they are made from stainless steel and attached to the front of the teeth. Many improvements have been made to this treatment over the years and even this traditional method has become sleeker and less noticeable.

Clear braces are similar to the structure of the traditional stainless steel. However, they are designed to blend with the natural color of your teeth, making them a more discreet option.

Invisalign ®

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners to straighten teeth discreetly and comfortably. These aligners are virtually invisible, making them an excellent option for individuals who want to improve their smile without the noticeable appearance of traditional metal braces.

One of the significant advantages of Invisalign is its removability, allowing patients to eat, drink, brush, and floss without any restrictions. This makes maintaining oral hygiene much easier compared to traditional braces. Additionally, the smooth plastic aligners are more comfortable and less likely to cause irritation to the gums and cheeks.

Technology

We’re committed to staying up-to-date with the latest technology!

Our iTero scanner provides an instant, precise 3D model of a patient’s teeth, capturing every detail with accuracy. This technology also enhances patient comfort by eliminating the need for traditional molds, making the process smoother and more convenient.

Digital X-rays provide a more precise and detailed image of your teeth and jaw, enhancing diagnostic accuracy and treatment planning. They also emit significantly less radiation compared to traditional X-rays, ensuring a safer experience for patients.

iCAT Cone Beam technology offers a comprehensive 3D view of your dental structures. This advanced imaging system provides detailed visuals with minimal radiation exposure, ensuring a safer and more effective orthodontic experience.
