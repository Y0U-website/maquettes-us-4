Getting Started

Get Started First Visit We can’t wait to meet you on your first visit to our office! Upon entering, we will get you acquainted with our office and staff, allowing you to feel right at home. Your appointment will consist of an overview of your dental records, an initial examination, discussion of potential treatment options, and when treatment should begin. This is also a great time to ask any questions you may have.

At the end of your appointment, we will provide you with a complete clinical diagnosis regarding the nature of the problem, a written summary of the findings, and a detailed plan that outlines your treatment, goals and estimated costs. We understand that each patient’s orthodontic treatment is different, and we promise to give you the personal attention you deserve.

Patient Forms

Patient forms are essential for gathering important information about your health and dental history before your visit. These forms help us tailor your treatment plan to your specific needs and ensure that we provide the best care possible. We strive to make the process as simple and efficient as possible, so you can complete the forms online or in our office with ease.

Finance & Insurance

At Schalo Orthodontics, we offer flexible payment plans and interest-free financing options to make orthodontic care more accessible. We also accept a variety of insurance plans, including Delta Premier, to help manage the cost of treatment.

Our goal is to provide you with the best possible care while accommodating your financial needs. We understand that everyone’s financial needs differ. That’s why we are committed to working with you to find a payment solution that fits your budget.
