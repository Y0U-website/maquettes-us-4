An orthodontist is a special type of dentist who realigns your teeth and jaws. This is most commonly treated with braces in children and young adults.

Most children have their first orthodontist appointment at about age 7, since alignment issues begin when baby teeth begin to fall out. This is why the earlier a child’s bite is treated, the better chance that they will avoid future dental issues.

At Windsor Family Dentistry, your child’s dentist and orthodontist will work together to decide what type of treatment plan is best for your child.

Braces: Metal braces are common for children between the ages of 9 and 14. The majority of children who are treated with braces have them for at least a year, but treatment times change from case to case.

We are fortunate today that we live in an era when missing teeth can be replaced using techniques that very closely resemble natural teeth without having to alter any surrounding teeth. Dental implants can replace teeth using a form of artificial root replacement that anchors directly into jaw bone.
