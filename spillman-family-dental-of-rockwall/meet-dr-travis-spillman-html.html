Get to Know Dr. Travis Spillman

Dr. Spillman has been a part of the dental world for over two full decades now, and he still finds new things to love about his job every day. He enjoys working so closely with other people, having the ability to create, and spending his time problem-solving in ways that make other’s lives better! Plus, since he was born and raised right here in Rockwall, he feels proud to give back to his local community, which has given him so much over the years. If you’d like to learn more about him, just keep reading below.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Amazingly enough, Dr. Spillman’s desire to become a dentist originated right in this very dental office! He also decided that dentistry was the right fit for him because it would give him the freedom to be his own boss and spend quality time with his family while also improving smiles.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Spillman and his wife Michele have three children and are very active members of their local community. They enjoy spending quality time on the lake, in the mountains, or on the beach.
