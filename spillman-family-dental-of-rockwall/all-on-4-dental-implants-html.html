How Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Work?

Am I a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Most people who are missing most of or all of their teeth in one or both arches make a good candidate for All-on-4 dental implants. Even if you have experienced bone loss due to tooth loss, you might still be able to get All-on-4 because of the strategic angle placement of the dental implants. Just keep in mind that with any tooth replacement, it is critical that you maintain an excellent dental hygiene routine. It’s also best to stay away from unhealthy lifestyle habits, like smoking and chewing tobacco.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

There are many different benefits that you can experience with All-on-4. Here are some of the ones that stand out the most:

  • Fixed: All-on-4 dental implants are fixed to the mouth, so they can be brushed just like natural teeth. You don’t need to take out your dentures to clean your smile either.
  • Comfortable: All-on-4 dental implants feel much different from your natural teeth. Once you have adjusted to them, they shouldn’t feel much different from your natural teeth.
  • Bite Force: All-on-4 dental implants restore much more of the chewing power than traditional dentures. This allows patients to enjoy a full, nutritious diet.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-4 dental implants prevent jawbone loss. This will help you maintain a more youthful appearance.

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Implants

All-on-4 dental implants have numerous things in common with implant dentures. here are some of the notable differences between them:

  • Number of Implants: Implant dentures require up to eight dental implants to be fully supported. This increases their costs.
  • Bone Grafting: To get implant dentures, many patients require bone grafting. The strategical placement of All-on-4 dental implants minimizes the need for bone grafting.
  • Quicker Process: It can take many months to get implant dentures. All-on-4 dental implants often allow patients to show of their new smile on the same day!

All-on-4 FAQs

Does All-on-4 Carry Any Risks?

Similar to most other medical and dental procedures, All-on-4 does carry a small level of risk. That said, the chances of complications during your treatment are rather low, especially for those who already practice a great oral hygiene routine and who do not use tobacco products, which can be a detriment to your oral/overall health and healing process. The most important part of this treatment is that you take extra care of your oral health during recovery. Should you notice any unusual swelling or pain around your implants, notify our team immediately so that we can resolve the issue.

Will People Be Able to Tell That My Teeth Aren’t Real?

One of the reasons that All-on-4 implants are incredibly popular is because they provide both stability and natural-looking results. Due to their extensive training in dental implants and restorations, you can be sure to enjoy lifelike pearly whites that function very similar to your normal teeth. Also, the shape, size, and color of your prosthetics will be personalized to match your unique smile and facial characteristics. This means that most people passing by won’t even be able to notice the difference.

Is All-on-4 Better for Replacing the Top Teeth or Bottom Teeth?

The good news is that All-on-4 treatment can be used to address both the upper and lower dental arches. Whether you’re missing teeth at the bottom or the top of your smile, our team can develop the right treatment plan so that you can regain a complete bite. If you need to undergo a full mouth reconstruction, we might recommend that you receive All-on-4 on both upper and lower arches at the same time. By doing so, you’ll be able to streamline the process, allowing you to save money while also minimizing how long your recovery will end up taking.
