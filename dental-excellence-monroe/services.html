Frequently Asked Questions

At Dental Excellence, we offer a variety of braces to suit individual needs and preferences. These include traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, lingual braces, and clear aligners. During your consultation, we’ll help you choose the most suitable option based on your lifestyle and orthodontic needs.

The duration of the teeth whitening process can vary depending on the degree of discoloration and the desired level of whiteness. A professional in-office whitening session at Dental Excellence typically takes about one to two hours.

We strive to accommodate our patients’ needs as efficiently as possible. However, as denture creation and fitting are multi-step processes that require precise measurements and custom lab work, we usually cannot offer same-day appointments for this service. We assure you that we will work diligently to provide your new dentures as quickly as possible.

We recommend scheduling dental check-ups and cleanings every six months. However, your dentist may suggest more frequent visits if you have a history of periodontal disease, are a smoker, or have other risk factors for oral health problems.

We accept all major credit cards, cash, and personal checks. For specific insurance questions, please get in touch with our office.
