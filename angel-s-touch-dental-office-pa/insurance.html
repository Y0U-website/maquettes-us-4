Every insurance plan has limitations, exclusions, and complexities that may render estimates of coverage inaccurate. Insurance filing and reimbursement may change daily.

If you are uncertain as to whether the services we provide are covered by your insurance company, please contact your company prior to your visit. Regardless of your insurance benefits, we will recommend the treatment that is best for you.

Most insurance companies should respond to the claim within 30 days .

Any insurance claim denied or remaining unpaid after 30 days will automatically become the responsibility of the patient.

Should your insurance pay more than is estimated, we will promptly mail you a refund. If a balance remains after your insurance has sent payment, our office will send you a statement due upon receipt.

Please be advised that you are fully responsible for all fees charged by this office regardless of your insurance coverage.
