About Us: Fighting Poverty. Lifting Lives.

At Samaritan House, we celebrate a culture of belonging: it is at the core of who we are and the way we deliver services to our neighbors living in poverty. We create fair and accessible opportunities, and we recognize that the varied cultural tapestry of our community makes San Mateo County strong and vibrant. Our central mission is to preserve dignity, promote self-sufficiency, and provide hope with compassion to everyone who turns to us for help.

Through our case management services, community health clinics, food programs, emergency shelters, and financial empowerment programs, we are committed to fostering an environment where all feel valued, respected, and are free from discrimination.

At Samaritan House, we believe that serving a client is not a single transaction but the start of an on-going relationship to move individuals and families towards self-reliance.
