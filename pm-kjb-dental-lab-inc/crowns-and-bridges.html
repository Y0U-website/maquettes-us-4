The benefits lend some weight to the fact that crown and bridge treatments treat several dental problems.

  • Look: Being restorations, they are produced to resemble the color, shape, and size of natural teeth so that they blend in seamlessly with the smile.
  • For a long time, if taken good care of, crowns and bridges could last for many years and provide long-term solutions.
  • Protection: Crowns protect teeth from further decay or fracture, whereas bridges prevent the adjacent teeth from drifting out of position.
  • Confidence Booster: The restored smile means a lot to boosting confidence in someone while interacting at work or with friends, and even posing for family pictures.

How Are Crowns and Bridges Made?

The process of making crowns and bridges is very precise and also needs skills of both arts and science. It all starts in the dental office and is handed off later to a crown and bridge laboratory, where technicians turn the restoration into reality. Here are the step-by-step processes for the making of these restorations:

Next, the dentist will make sure that the restoration does not feel uncomfortable to the patient and that it has a natural appearance. The patients will then get instructions on how to care for their new crown or bridge. This process can take a few weeks, depending on how complicated the case is and the materials used. Temporary restorations may be put in place to protect the exposed teeth while the permanent crown or bridge is being fabricated.

  • Initial Consultation and Assessment: The dentist examines the patient’s teeth and discusses the patient. It may need to carry out X-rays or digital scans to assess the state of the tooth structure and bone around it.
  • Preparation of Teeth: For a crown, the dentist prepares the tooth by mildly shaping it so that a cap can be placed over the tooth. For a bridge, however, the two teeth on either side of the missing tooth, the abutment teeth, are prepared to bear the restoration.
  • Shapes or Digital Scans: The dentist makes an impression of the teeth using either a mould or a digital scanner. This results in a perfect model of the patient’s mouth, which is then sent to the crown and bridge dental lab.
  • Designing the Fixed: The technicians using the impressions or digital scans provide a crown or bridge design in the lab, applying advanced software like CAD/CAM systems for exact resolution.
  • Designing the Replacement: They used the tool mold and computer images to design it.
  • Material Selection and Creating: They made it for crafted from the material of choice-working zirconia, porcelain fused to metal, or ceramic. Then it goes to the lab, after that it gets a shape, and then it polishes the crown or bridge so that it will match the patient’s natural teeth.
  • Fitting and final: The restoration is sent back to the dentist, who will then verify its fit, make any adjustments necessary, then finally cement it in place.

How Do You Take Care of Crown and Bridge Tooth Repairs?

  • Always brush your teeth twice.
  • Keep cleaning after having food with water.
  • Skipping hard food can break the bridge easily, which can damage the crown.
  • When using a crown and bridge tooth, always visit a dentist for a checkup. It makes it clear whether everything is well or not.
  • If you mostly have grinding teeth while sleeping, then try to use a nightguard that protects the tooth.
  • Some food is sticky. It can stick to your teeth for a long time and also give you discomfort. Sticky candies can pull on the bridgers and make them loose.
