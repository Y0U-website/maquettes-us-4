Dr. Shahin Shamsian, DDS, General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Shamsian is a graduate of Univeristy of Southern California, one of the best in the country. He has been a dentist for years and has been through several post graduate courses in different fields of dentistry since receiving his DDS degree.

His emphasis on patient comfort as well as keeping up-to-date with the latest advancements in dentistry enable him to provide his patients with the best quality of care in the field. Dr. Shamsian has a wide range of experience in different areas of dentistry, from cosmetic and restorative dentistry to surgical treatments and root canal treatments.

His approach to your treatment starts with patiently listening to you. Then, after a complete and thorough examination, he reviews and discusses the diagnosis and treatment options with you.

"I am proud to provide my patients with the best in dental technology, treatment options and patient comfort. We welcome you to our advanced dental office in Tarzana. It is my pleasure to address all your questions and concerns."

Dr. Payman Kakoli, DDS, Endodontist

Dr. Kakoli is a graduate of the University of Maryland where he earned both his undergraduate and dental degrees. He completed General Practice Residency and Endodontic Residency. Shortly along the way, he earned a Master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences.

Dr. Kakoli is an incredibly devoted endodontist who is passionate about treating his patients. He’s highly skilled in diagnosing and treating tooth pain and performing root canals.

Dr. Armand Keuroghlian, DDS, Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Keuroghlian is an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon who graduated from Harbor-UCLA. He received his DDS degree from UCLA School of Dentistry and completed a 1 year internship at Parkland Memorial Hospital in Dallas, Tx.

Prior to graduating from the UCLA School of Dentistry, Dr. Keuroghlian obtained a Biology degree from the University of California, Irvine. While working toward his dental degree at UCLA, he researched implants and their integration with bone and received numerous awards, including the OMFS Student Award and Dean’s Leadership Institute. While in dental school, he gravitated toward the surgical aspect of dentistry, so he decided to continue his education in oral and maxillofacial surgery at Harbor-UCLA.

While patient care is his first priority, Dr. Keuroghlian hopes to have an academic role in the future. Dr. Keuroghlian enjoys sports, hanging out with his friends, and traveling. He is also fluent in Armenian.
