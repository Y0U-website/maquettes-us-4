New Patient Information

Welcome New Patients!

On your first visit

We treat you as a respected guest of our dental practice. During your new patient visit, we will take and diagnose radiographs, clean your teeth, and conduct a thorough examination of your mouth, gums, and surrounding soft tissues, including an oral cancer screening. We listen carefully to determine your needs, desires, and goals for optimal oral health. Then, in easily-understood language, we clearly communicate a comprehensive treatment plan, answering all of your questions and making sure you leave our office with a better and more complete understanding of your oral health.

As a way of saying “Thank You” for choosing our dental practice, Dr. Smith and Dr. Hascall provide their Comprehensive Exam at no charge* to patients without dental insurance on their first visit to our office. This represents a savings of over $70.00! (*In absence of periodontal disease.)

Please call us at (402) 932-9349 , or use the contact form at the bottom of this page to ask us a question or schedule an appointment. We look forward to meeting you!
