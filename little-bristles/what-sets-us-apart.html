What Sets Us Apart

Our goal is for children and their families to understand the importance of dental health and to establish a lifetime of healthy smiles!

  • By helping nervous children overcome their worries, we help them develop confidence and increase their self-esteem. We reinforce that with rewards, praise, and encouragement, as we teach children how to keep their smile clean and healthy.
  • We understand the need for anxiety-free, painless dental care, so we use the Solea dental laser for many needle-free procedures and offer safe sedation options, too.
  • We are equally as attentive to parents, listening to their concerns and expectations and respectful of their wishes. By establishing open communication with families, and inviting their questions, we build trust. At each visit we talk about the child’s dental health, teach good home dental hygiene practices, and encourage the development of healthy habits.
