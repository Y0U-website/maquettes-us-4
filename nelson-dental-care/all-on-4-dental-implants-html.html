Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

Some noteworthy benefits of All-on-4 dental implants include:

  • Your denture will be permanently fixed in your mouth , so you can care for it it similarly to natural teeth.
  • You can expect to enjoy a strong bite force . In fact, you should be able to easily eat all of your favorite foods.
  • Your smile will look completely natural . We carefully design All-on-4 dentures so they are as lifelike as possible.
  • Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent it from deteriorating.
  • You can undergo tooth extractions, implant placement, and placement of your new teeth in a single day .

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dental Implants

There are a few significant differences between All-on-4 and traditional dental implants:

  • Treatment timeline. Start-to-finish treatment tends to be much shorter with All-on-4 than with traditional implants.
  • Number of surgeries. All-on-4 usually requires just one surgery, whereas traditional implants can necessitate 2 or more surgeries.
  • All-on-4 uses fewer dental implants than most full-arch tooth replacement plans with traditional implants. Therefore, its cost is often lower.
  • All-on-4 is only for full-arch replacement. Traditional implants can replace any number of missing teeth.
  • Using more implants in a traditional treatment may lead to a slightly stronger, more stable denture than what you would get with All-on-4
