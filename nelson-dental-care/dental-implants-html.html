What are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed directly into the jawbone in order to restore the root structure of a lost tooth. This can then be used to support a variety of restorations that can replace everything from a single tooth to an entire smile. Since these implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, they provide these new teeth with unrivaled stability and chewing power. Dental implants are truly special because they are the only treatment that can do this!

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Receiving dental implants involves a much longer and more intricate process than traditional dentures or bridges, but these amazing prosthetic devices can deliver a range of incredible benefits that conventional dental restorations cannot. Initial Dental Implant Consultation

During your consultation, our team at Nelson Dental Care will speak with you about your medical history and goals for the treatment and conduct a thorough examination of your smile. Based on our observations, we’ll outline a treatment plan and walk you through it so you know what to expect. Once you consent to moving forward with the plan, we’ll refer you to our trusted oral surgeon to schedule your implant placement surgery. Dental Implant Surgery

We partner with an expert, local oral surgeon for this part of the process. The procedure will begin with you receiving an appropriate anesthetic to ensure that you feel no pain and possibly sedation treatment if you need extra help remaining still and calm throughout the surgery. When you are ready, your oral surgeon will carefully insert your implants into your jawbone and close your gum tissue around them. They’ll then place protective caps on the posts to prevent injuries during the healing process.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

The following three to six months after your surgery, your implants will fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Once this is completed, our team will attach small metal connector pieces called abutments to the implants through a minor surgical procedure. After you’ve healed from this treatment, we’ll be ready to take bite impressions to provide the data necessary to craft your final restorations.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

When we receive your final restoration, we’ll secure it to your abutments to complete your smile. After fixing your crown, bridge, or denture in place, we will check to make sure it fits and make any final adjustments. When we’re sure your new bite is functional and comfortable, you’ll be ready to show your new smile off to the world.

Day-to-Day Benefits

Often enough, patients first notice dental implants’ day-to-day benefits. These include upsides like:

  • Lifelike Results Dental implants have porcelain surfaces and fuse with your jaw, so they look and act exactly like natural teeth. Friends and family won’t even notice they aren’t real teeth.
  • More Confidence Since they restore your full smile, dental implants should give you a confidence boost. They’ll leave you reassured of your appearance in social settings, so you won’t have to worry about your looks.
  • Better Eating & Speaking When dental implants fuse with your jaw, they become highly secure and restore most of your bite force. These effects help you chew and speak as usual.
  • Low Maintenance It doesn’t take much effort to maintain dental implants. Unlike dentures or dental bridges, they can remain stable with good oral hygiene alone; no creams or adhesives needed.

Health Benefits

Once familiar with their daily upsides, you should consider dental implants’ health benefits. Some of the more notable ones are:

  • Support for Teeth Did you know other teeth can tilt and fall out if you don’t treat tooth loss? Well, dental implants fix that problem by filling in your smile gaps.
  • Less Oral Disease Left unchecked, smile gaps can house bacteria that cause cavities, gum disease, etc. The good news is that dental implants fill those gaps when they restore your grin.
  • Increased Jaw Strength A patient’s jaw will erode (due to lack of stimulus) when they suffer from untreated tooth loss. Thankfully, dental implants prevent (and reverse) this erosion.
  • Improved Nutrition Because they restore your smile’s bite force, dental implants broaden your meal choices. That means they’ll help you get crucial vitamins and minerals.

Long-term Benefits

Even with the points above, please don’t think dental implants only have immediate perks. They also have several long-term benefits, including:

  • High Success Rate Due to their traits, dental implants nearly always succeed when placed by qualified dentists. The posts even have a documented ten-year success rate of 98 %!
  • Lasting Results Dental implants are made of durable titanium, so they can potentially restore your smile for a lifetime. Average ones alone work for 15-20 years, while well-maintained posts can last 30 years or more.
  • Cost-effective Care Even with their initial price, dental implants should save you money over time. Their lasting effects (see above) ensure they don’t need much repair or replacement work. On the contrary, they let you keep funds otherwise spent on follow-up dental care.

Dental Implant FAQs

Perhaps after reading all of the above information on dental implants, you’re considering getting them, but you still have a few questions. After all, implants are quite an investment in your smile. Not to worry; we have gathered some of the most commonly asked questions below and answered them for your convenience. Why do I need a bone graft?

Some people need a bone grafting procedure before they can receive dental implants in Metairie. This is because when you’ve been undergoing tooth loss, your jawbone begins to deteriorate. It no longer receives stimulation from your natural tooth roots, leaving it to atrophy. As a result, your jawbone is no longer strong enough to accommodate dental implants. Bone grafting solves this problem. By grafting artificial bone tissue onto your jawbone, it will ensure that it has the strength to support as many implants as you need.

What causes implant failure?

Smoking is one of the biggest causes of implant failure in the first few months after implant placement. This is because smoking (or chewing) tobacco significantly slows down the oral healing process, which is crucial to your implant becoming one with your jawbone. We highly suggest quitting smoking before considering dental implants.

The other main cause of implant failure is infections within either the bone or soft tissue in the mouth. Having uncontrolled diabetes or gum disease can greatly increase your risk of implant failure. That’s why Dr. Nelson, your dentist in Metairie, recommends getting those conditions managed before undergoing implant placement surgery.

Does it hurt to get dental implants?

The vast majority of patients report only a minimal amount of discomfort with their procedure. In fact, many prefer implant placement to having a tooth extracted. However, as with any kind of oral surgery, you can expect some degree of swelling and discomfort for a few days afterwards. This can be managed by applying a cold compress to the area and/or taking over-the-counter pain medications as directed on the bottle.

After implant surgery, can I return to work?

The answer to this question depends on the complexity of your case. If your surgery is relatively small, such as getting a single implant, then you might just be able to resume your day as normal afterwards. If, however, your case is more complex, we might recommend taking a couple of days to rest and recuperate before returning to work.

How long do dental implants last?

Implants are designed to easily last for at least 30 years. They can last longer, even for the rest of your life, if you take proper care of them, which involves regular brushing, flossing, and checkups and cleanings with Dr. Nelson.
