Dental Services

Our Services

We offer services to provide for you through every stage of life. From infancy to adolescence and from college to retirement, we’re here to meet your dental needs.

Same-day crowns

The latest 3D technology allows us to complete your crowns, onlays, and veneers in one appointment. No impressions, no temporary crowns, no hassle.

Clear aligner orthodontics

Virtually invisible aligners straighten your teeth without metal wires or food restrictions. A perfect smile has never been so easy to achieve for teens and adults.

All Digital Office

Digital x-rays give you the least radiation possible, electronic medical records make communication faster than ever, and optical scanning has eliminated the need for many uncomfortable dental impressions.

Laser dentistry

Modern diode lasers now allow dentists to perform surgeries with no bleeding and no stitches, heal cold sores, desensitize teeth, and kill bacteria without antibiotics.

Implant Crowns

We can use the latest implant technology to give you the smile you have always wanted, without the need for crowning any more teeth or switching to dentures.

Sleep Apnea

This lethal disease is growing rapidly in the US. If a patient can’t use a CPAP machine or simply hates using one, then a dental appliance is the next best step in eliminating this serious condition. We look at you and your mouth and then specifically choose the appliance that will best fit your needs.

Sleep hygiene

For some patients, restful sleep is nearly impossible to get, but they do not actually have sleep apnea. Sometimes changing their sleep environment or routine is enough to find the good night’s sleep they have been looking for. Come speak with our staff about your “sleep hygiene.”

Teeth whitening

We combine one in-office session with a complete take-home custom tray kit to give you the most beautifully white smile possible.

Conscious Sedation

For patients who need a bit more TLC, we offer the benefits of combining nitrous oxide and a mild sedative to allow you to remain comfortable and relaxed throughout your entire procedure.

MI Paste Plus

When brushing and flossing still aren’t enough to keep cavities away, MI Paste Plus may still be a solution. Without harsh chemicals, this super-saturated mineral paste can rebuild damaged enamel. A wonderful side-effect to this enamel repair is a drastic reduction in tooth sensitivity.

Simple cleaning

For patients with overall good oral health, we’ll tidy up and polish around each tooth for that professionally cleaned feeling.

Deep Cleanings

For patients with more extensive gum needs, we offer comfortable and gentle deep cleaning services.

Fillings

For simple or small needs we offer beautiful, durable tooth-colored restorations and cost-effective, safe silver fillings.

Children s Care

We welcome your child to our practice. In order to avoid creating unpleasant childhood memories of the dentist, we go the extra mile to make sure your child is comfortable, communicated with, and empowered while in our office. Please call and schedule a free First Visit Tour for them.

Crowns

If the needs of your tooth require something more substantial than a filling, we offer a variety of tooth-colored and golden crowns, inlays, and onlays.

Dentures

We offer complete and partial plates, along with comprehensive denture planning and maintenance services.

Emergency Care

Don’t let a toothache ruin your night or your weekend. We are here to care for you when you need after hours help. Please don’t hesitate to call 541-758-1505 to reach one of our on-call doctors.

Root Canals

Sometimes fillings aren’t the best choice to get you out of pain. For those times, we offer calm, quiet root canal therapy to get you back to a normal life again.

Extractions

When no restoration will be able to successfully treat a tooth, we can carefully remove it and work with you to plan the most cost-effective and esthetic way to replace it.
