Meet Dr. Todd D. Hoggan

Orthodontist Dr. Todd D. Hoggan has been a practicing orthodontist for over 15 years. Dr. Hoggan received his doctorate at the University of Oklahoma Dental School in 2000. He then completed his orthodontic residency at St. Louis University and earned a Master of Science degree in Orthodontics in 2003. The recipient of numerous honors and awards, Dr. Hoggan graduated with distinction and was selected as the recipient of the Uchiyama-Truetzel Award for Orthodontics.

Dr. Hoggan is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, and the Minnesota Dental Association. He has also conducted and authored original research in the field of orthodontics. Dr. Hoggan is actively involved in developing and using the latest technologies--including developing custom orthodontic software and appliances for the benefit of his patients.

Dr. Hoggan is a certified Invisalign and Clear Correct clear aligner expert with many years experience in the correct application of these technologies.

An avid snow and water skier, Dr. Hoggan’s recreational interests also include golf, fly fishing, surfing and playing the drums. While he has many interests, most of his extra time is typically spent with his family of four enjoying their company.
