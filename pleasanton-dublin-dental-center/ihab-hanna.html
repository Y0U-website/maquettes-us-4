Ihab Hanna

Dr. Ihab M. Hanna graduated, with honors, in 1994 from Cairo University. Afterwards, he spent three years in training and hospital residency . He completed two years of General practice residency ( 1998-2000 ) at the University of Illinois , as a chief resident, where he acquired additional training in Dental Implants, sedation and special care dentistry.

He completed the University of Miami Implant continuum (2001-2002) He started his Redwood city Practice in 2002, focusing on implant dentistry In 2008, He founded the Redwood city dental Institute, offering certificates for Dental assistants to apply for the RDA license exam In 2009, he founded the RWC implant club mentoring General dentists to incorporate implant dentistry in their practice safely and predictably. In 2013, He acquired a second practice in Dublin, Ca and it was relocated to Pleasanton CA in 2017, Pleasanton Dublin Dental Center

Dr. Hanna is a member of ADA - CDA - SAN MATEO AND ALAMEDA DENTAL SOCIETIES A Diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology - International Congress Oral Implantology and the International dental implant association A Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.
