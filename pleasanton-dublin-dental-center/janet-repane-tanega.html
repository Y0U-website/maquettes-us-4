Personal Life & Interests

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Tanega enjoys spending quality time with her husband of 31 years, Richard, and their four children - Helene, Richard Jr., Reece, and Raymond. She is also a proud grandmother to Darien, Jeffrey Jr., and Jayda-Rae. Her family also includes three beloved dogs: Coco, Wags, and Bella.

Dr. Tanega values living a healthy and active lifestyle. She enjoys traveling and embracing experiences that enrich her personal and professional life.

In order to provide her patients with the best service possible, Dr. Tanega regularly attends multiple seminars a year to continue her education in general, orthodontic, and cosmetic dentistry, as well as implant surgery. She is a seasoned expert in the practice of orthodontics (among other services) and has been practicing orthodontics since 1998. In 2012, she became a certified preferred provider for Clear Aligners.
