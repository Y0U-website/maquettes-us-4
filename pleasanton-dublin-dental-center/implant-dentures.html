Patient Reviews

Dentures in Pleasanton, CA, are tooth replacement solutions that are used to restore missing, severely decayed, or broken teeth. They restore the patient’s ability to eat and speak, and also improve their overall appearance. Traditional dentures are removable and require adhesives to stay in place. Implant-supported dentures are supported and kept in place by dental implants.

Dentures in Pleasanton, CA can be used as a replacement for an entire set of teeth or only part of the missing natural teeth. Complete dentures replace an entire set of teeth, while partial dentures only replace a few teeth.

How Do Implant Dentures in Pleasanton, CA Work?

Dentures in Pleasanton, CA, are engineered to provide a convenient solution for missing teeth. They are used by patients who have undergone tooth extractions or have naturally lost their teeth.

Dentures function by providing support to the facial structure, which directly affects your cheeks and lips. Dentures are designed to look and function like natural teeth.

Dentures can replace all or only a few of the patient’s natural teeth. They are inserted after the decayed or damaged teeth have been removed and once the gums have healed.

Immediate dentures are inserted as soon as the teeth have been extracted. The tissues heal beneath the dentures.

Implant-supported dentures use implants for support and stability. The implants are first inserted into the jawbone, and then the denture is securely attached to them.

What Does a Denture Look Like?

A traditional denture has two parts, the base and the artificial teeth. The base is made from acrylic and designed to look like natural gums. The artificial teeth can be made from porcelain, ceramic, or resin and look and feel like natural teeth.

Implant dentures are made up of three parts: the implant, the attachment, and the artificial teeth. Contact us to learn more.

Partial Dentures in Pleasanton, CA

Partial dentures in Pleasanton, CA, are used as a replacement for only a specific set of teeth. A useful restorative technique to replace one or more missing teeth, these dentures help patients improve their speech and ability to eat. There are several types of partial dentures, as follows:

  • Transitional Partial Denture: These are plastic dentures that function as temporary tooth replacements. They help the patient while they recover from the tooth extractions.
  • Permanent Partial Denture: These are lightweight partial dentures designed for permanent use. They can be supported by implants, are more comfortable, and look more natural than transitional dentures.

Types of Dentures Available in Pleasanton, CA

Modern dentistry offers a variety of denture options to meet different clinical needs:

Complete Dentures

Used when all natural teeth in an arch are missing. These rest directly on the gums and can be either conventional or immediate.

Partial Dentures

Ideal when a few natural teeth remain. They prevent remaining teeth from shifting out of place and restore a natural bite.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Provide the highest level of stability by anchoring the denture to implants placed in the jawbone.

Snap-On Dentures

A removable denture option that securely snaps onto dental implants, offering both stability and convenience.

Implant-supported dentures further enhance comfort, stability, and function, closely replicating natural teeth.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dentures?

You may be a suitable candidate for dentures if you:

Your dentist will assess your oral health and recommend the most suitable option.

  • Have multiple missing or severely damaged teeth
  • Want an affordable alternative to replacing each tooth individually
  • Have adequate gum and bone structure to support the denture
  • Prefer a removable or implant-supported tooth replacement
  • Are seeking a solution that quickly restores function and appearance
