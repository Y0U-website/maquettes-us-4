Dr. Sliman Sayage, DMD

Associate Dentist

Welcome to Uno Dental, where your smile is our priority. I’m Dr. Sliman Sayage, a dedicated dentist who provides compassionate and comprehensive dental care to patients of all ages.

I graduated from A.T. Still University Missouri School of Dentistry and Oral Health while continuing in advanced training and education in the latest dental techniques and technologies. I am a member of the Texas Dental Association, Texas Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association, and American Dental Education Association. Whether you need a routine check-up, cosmetic dentistry services, or restorative treatments, we offer personalized care tailored to your needs.

I have cultivated a deep passion for improving my patients’ oral health and well-being. My team and I prioritize patient comfort and strive to create a relaxing environment where patients can feel at ease during their visits.

Beyond clinical excellence, I believe in building lasting relationships with my patients based on trust, integrity, and open communication. Your dental health is essential to me, and I am committed to helping you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile that you can be proud of.

Outside of the office, I enjoy trying new restaurants and experience tasting many diverse meals in my hometown of Houston. After work, I usually focus on body fitness, nutrition, and yoga to maintain a healthy lifestyle balance. Family is very important to me, so I spend time with them.

I look forward to welcoming you at Uno Dental and creating a journey to optimize your oral health. Contact us today to schedule your appointment and discover new dentistry that can make a difference in your life.
