Recent studies have shown that good oral hygiene is essential not only for dental health but also for overall health and well-being. Our comprehensive dental hygiene program is designed to preserve teeth and the supporting structures by preventing the onset, progression, and recurrence of dental disease.

To maintain optimal oral health, we recommend periodic professional dental cleanings and encourage a good dental home care regimen in conjunction with them. As part of our office’s preventive program, your current home care practices will be reviewed. Our team will help make recommendations as to what areas require improvement and instruct you in the proper methods of brushing and flossing.
