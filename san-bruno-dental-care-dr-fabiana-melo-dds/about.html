0 Meet The Doctor Fabiana Melo DDS, MS* *Master of Science in Oral Implantology I have been practicing dentistry for over 20 years.

I received my dental degree from the University of Sao Paulo, Brazil (UNAERP) in 1998. I achieved additional training in Restorative and Prosthodontics dentistry at the University of California, Los Angeles where I received a dental license for the state of California. In 2018, I was awarded a prestigious fellowship award by the International Congress of Oral Implantology (ICOI).

I have also graduated from the respected Goethe University in Frankfurt, Germany with a Master’s of Science Oral Implantology.

I have a great passion for dentistry and have motivated me to continue pursuing dentistry by completing hundreds of hours in continuing education courses focusing on laser, dental ceramic crowns, veneers, cosmetic dentistry, placement and restoration of dental implants with use of the newest technologies.

I speak English, Portuguese, and Spanish. Lastly, I am dedicated to my patients and will listen to all your dental needs to continue and carry out the quality of care you deserve.
