Meet the Team

Meet our wonderful team of professionals dedicated to you and your family’s dental health.

Dr. Jamie Paychev, DDS

AKA "Dr. Jamie"

In 2022, I was chosen by Dr. Walker to carry on this practice as she takes the next step in her career as a full-time faculty member of her alma mater, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I am honored to continue the legacy of excellent dental care.

I was born and raised in the Bay Area and from very early on in life, I knew I wanted to make a career out of improving the health of others in a way that is personable, technical, and artistic. I graduated from University of California, Santa Barbara, where I earned my Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences degree while enjoying the sunshine playing beach volleyball. I also worked part time as a dental assistant and in the Army Reserves.

Following college graduation, I was commissioned into the Army Medical Corps and later the Army Dental Corps. It has been a privilege to serve both my country as well as my community through the Army Reserves, which allowed me to also work in private practice while spending weekends and summers continuing training and utilizing my skillset to serve others.

When not at the office, I spend my time on adventures with my two Goldendoodles, playing volleyball, skiing, and biking. I also am a member of the International Team for Implantology, American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and the Mid-Peninsula Dental Society where I attend regular courses to stay current with medical and dental findings, new techniques and technology.

Dr. Andrew Cheshire, DDS

After receiving a bachelors in violin performance at Indiana University Jacobs School of Music, I attended University of Central Florida for science coursework prior to pursuing my dental degree at University of Florida College of Dentistry. I graduated dental school with high honors, earning awards in clinical dentistry for exceptional patient care and clinical skill in restorative and prosthodontic dentistry.

My training includes experience across all areas of general dentistry, including prosthodontics, endodontics, oral surgery, periodontics, orthodontics, and pediatric care.

I am committed to evidence-based dentistry and patient education to provide our dental family with the tools, knowledge, and treatment to have confidence in their oral health.

Fitting to the Stanford Family Dental name, I moved to the Menlo Park area with my young family (my wife, Marlena, and my son, Michael) to continue practicing dentistry as my wife pursues her PhD at Stanford University in environmental engineering.

I am very excited to work alongside Dr. Jamie Paychev to provide exceptional patient-centered care. Dr. Jamie and I share a similar passion for giving back to our community through dentistry and a conservative treatment philosophy which prioritizes patient needs. I look forward to meeting and serving you and your family!

I continue to stay up-to-date with medical and dental best-practices through continuing education courses and maintain active membership in the American Dental Association, California Dental Associate and Mid-Peninsula Dental Society. Outside of dentistry, I enjoy playing violin in church on Sundays, going on hikes with my family, and cultivating hobbies such as wheel throwing (pottery), surfing, and golfing.

Lauren Wilson

Office Manager, Registered Dental Assistant

With over 40 years of experience in dentistry, I bring a deep passion for patient care to everything I do. My journey began at Foothill College, where I completed the Dental Assisting Program in 1979, inspired by an orthodontist who showed me the impact this field can have on people’s lives. I have loved my work ever since.

As a Registered Dental Assistant and office manager for 32 years, I am committed to creating a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, valued, and cared for. Ensuring that every patient comes first is at the heart of my approach.

Outside the office, I am a proud mother of three wonderful daughters and “Nana” to eight amazing grandchildren. I cherish time with my family and friends, who bring so much joy and balance to my life.
