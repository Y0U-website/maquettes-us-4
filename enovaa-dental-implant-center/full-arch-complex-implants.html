What Are Pterygoid Implants?

Pterygoid implants are longer, precisely angled implants that anchor into the dense pterygoid bone at the back of the upper jaw. This advanced technique allows patients with severe posterior upper-jaw bone loss to receive fixed full-arch teeth—often without the need for bone grafting or sinus lifts .

  • Severe bone loss in the back of the upper jaw
  • Failed implants or previous implant complications
  • A history of advanced gum disease or infection
  • Long-term denture wear with poor stability
  • Been told they’re not a candidate for traditional implants

What Are Trans-Sinus Implants?

Trans-sinus implants are longer, specially angled implants that pass through the sinus cavity and anchor into strong bone beyond the sinus wall. This advanced approach allows patients with limited upper-jaw bone height to receive stable, long-term implants—often without the need for extensive bone grafting .

  • Moderate to severe bone loss in the upper jaw
  • Enlarged sinuses that limit traditional implant placement
  • Failed implants or prior implant complications
  • A history of periodontal disease or infection
  • Been told they need sinus lifts or grafting before implants
