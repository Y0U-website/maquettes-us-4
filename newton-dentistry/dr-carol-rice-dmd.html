Dr. Carol Rice DMD General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Carol Rice DMD is a General Dentist at Newton Dentistry. Dr. Rice is a highly skilled and compassionate dentist with over 30 years of experience in restoring beautiful smiles. A graduate of Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Rice specializes in dental implants, crowns and bridges, helping patients of all ages achieve optimal oral health and confidence.

Dr. Carol Rice has also served as a clinical consultant for The Dental Advisor, contributing her expertise to the dental community. Having grown up in Massachusetts, Dr. Rice is proud to serve the local community, bringing both experience and a personal touch to every patient interaction. With a dedication to the highest standards of care, Dr. Rice is committed to delivering exceptional results to every smile.
