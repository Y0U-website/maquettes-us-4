Dr. Priyanka Pandya prides herself in taking the time to listen to each patient’s questions and concerns. She understands the link between oral health and its role in a patient’s overall health. The mission at Downers Dental is to provide the highest quality dental care.

Dr. Pandya graduated from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry. She continued her passion for dentistry by taking advanced courses in wisdom teeth extractions, placing and restoring implants, cosmetic dentistry, and Invisalign. Her goal is to provide comprehensive care at Downers Dental.

Dr. Pandya prioritizes providing individualized care in a compassionate environment. She welcomes questions and conversations while encouraging patients to be involved in maintaining their oral health. She is an experienced dentist who utilizes her knowledge and skills to provide state-of-the-art dental care to patients of all ages.

Dr. Pandya enjoys spending time with her husband, Nehal, and two beautiful kids Ethan and Zoey. She enjoys taking the kids to the park, riding bikes, and playing various sports. She also enjoys cooking at home and learning new recipes.

Dr. Pandya is affiliated with American Dental Association (ADA), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics (AAIP), and Chicago Dental Society (CDS).
