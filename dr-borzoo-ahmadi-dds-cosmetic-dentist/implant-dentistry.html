What is Implant Dentistry?

At our practice in West Hollywood, implant dentistry involves the placement of artificial tooth roots, usually made of titanium, into the jawbone to support dental prosthetics such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. This procedure restores functionality and aesthetics to your smile, providing a permanent solution for missing teeth .

Screw Retained Implant Crown

The screw retained implant crown is a popular choice for its stability and ease of maintenance. This method involves attaching the crown to the implant with a screw, providing a secure and durable restoration. One of its unique benefits is the ability to easily access and repair the crown, if necessary, without damaging the implant itself. It is commonly used for single-tooth replacements or in cases where the implant needs to be easily retrievable for future adjustments.

Cement Retained Implant Crown

The cement retained implant crown offers a natural-looking and aesthetically pleasing restoration. In this procedure, the crown is cemented onto the implant, creating a seamless and permanent attachment. One of its unique benefits is its ability to provide excellent esthetics, as the crown closely resembles a natural tooth. It is often preferred for its stability and ability to evenly distribute chewing force evenly, making it suitable for single-tooth replacements and multi-unit restorations .

Single Implants

Single implants are ideal for replacing individual missing teeth with precision and reliability. This procedure involves placing a single implant into the jawbone and attaching a custom-made crown to restore the missing tooth. One of its unique benefits is that it preserves adjacent teeth, as they do not need to be altered or used as support for the restoration. Single implants offer a permanent solution for tooth replacement , restoring both function and aesthetics to the smile.

Bridges

Implant-supported bridges are a versatile option for replacing multiple missing teeth in a row. This procedure involves placing implants into the jawbone and attaching a bridge prosthetic to restore the missing teeth. One of its unique benefits is its ability to provide stability and support for the bridge without relying on adjacent teeth. Implant-supported bridges offer a long-lasting solution for restoring the bite and preventing bone loss in the jaw .

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Full mouth reconstruction is a comprehensive treatment plan to restore the entire mouth’s function and aesthetics. This procedure combines restorative techniques, including dental implants, to rebuild the smile from the ground up. One of its unique benefits is its ability to address multiple dental issues simultaneously , such as missing teeth, damaged teeth, and bite misalignment. Full mouth reconstruction can dramatically improve oral health, restore confidence, and enhance overall quality of life.

Implant Dentistry Recovery and Aftercare

After undergoing implant dentistry in West Hollywood , patients may experience some initial discomfort and swelling, which can be effectively managed with prescribed medications and ice packs. The healing times may vary from person to person, but most patients can expect to see some initial results within a few weeks as the implants get integrated with the jawbone.

It is highly recommended that you avoid strenuous activities and stick to soft foods during the recovery period to minimize discomfort and promote healing. It is also crucial to follow the post-operative instructions given by the dentist, including proper oral hygiene practices and attending follow-up appointments to ensure proper monitoring. You should avoid smoking and alcohol consumption during the healing process to get the best possible results.

Proper oral care, including regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings, is essential for maintaining the longevity of the implants. Taking care of the incisions can help prevent infection and minimize scarring. Routine dental visits can provide ongoing assessment of oral health and implant stability . Overall, with proper care and maintenance, implant dentistry can provide long-lasting results and a restored smile for many years.

Implant Dentistry Frequently Asked Questions

Are Dental Implants painful?

While discomfort during and after the procedure is possible, it is typically managed effectively with local anesthesia and pain medication. Most patients report minimal discomfort during the recovery period .

What are the risks associated with Dental Implants?

Although rare, risks associated with dental implants may include infection, implant failure, or damage to surrounding structures. However, with proper care and adherence to post-operative instructions, these risks can be minimized.

Are Dental Implants permanent?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can indeed be a permanent solution for tooth replacement . They are designed to fuse with the jawbone, providing a stable and long-lasting foundation for prosthetic teeth.

Do Dental Implants look and feel natural?

Yes, dental implants closely resemble natural teeth in both appearance and function . They provide a seamless and natural-looking smile while restoring the ability to chew and speak comfortably.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

The cost of dental implants can vary depending on factors such as the number of implants needed, the complexity of the procedure, and any additional treatments required. It is best to consult with a dental professional for an accurate cost estimate based on individual needs and circumstances.
