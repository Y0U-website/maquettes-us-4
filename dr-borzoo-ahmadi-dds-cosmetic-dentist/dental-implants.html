Dental Implants Beverly Hills

Dental Implants in Beverly Hills with Dr. Borzoo Ahmadi, DDS

Missing a tooth affects more than just your appearance. It can cause problems with chewing, speaking, and the alignment of your bite, and it can also lead to jawbone loss over time. Many people struggle with temporary solutions or uncomfortable dentures, which can impact their daily lives. With dental implants in Beverly Hills , patients have a long-lasting way to replace missing teeth. This treatment restores your bite, protects your oral health, and gives you a result that looks and feels natural.

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small posts made from titanium or zirconia that are placed into your jawbone during a minor surgery. These posts act like artificial roots for your teeth. Once they bond with the bone, they provide a strong base for crowns, bridges, or even full sets of teeth, giving you stable and natural-feeling results.

Today, dental implants are one of the most effective options for replacing missing teeth . They work like natural teeth, help prevent jawbone loss, and keep your mouth healthy without needing support from nearby teeth.

  • Best for: Replacing a single missing tooth, multiple teeth, or stabilizing full dentures.
  • Treatment type: Surgical restorative dentistry.
  • Anesthesia or numbing: Local anesthesia, with various sedation options available.
  • Appointment length: 1 to 2 hours for the surgical placement procedure.
  • Downtime: 1 to 3 days of localized rest and modified diet.
  • Pain level: Mild to moderate soreness post-surgery, easily managed with medication.
  • How long it lasts: The implant post can last a lifetime; the crown may need replacement in 10–15 years.
  • Cost factors: Based on 3D imaging, implant material, bone grafting requirements, and lab-crafted restorations.

What dental problems do Dental Implants address?

  • A single missing tooth or multiple missing teeth
  • Deteriorating or reabsorbed jawbone
  • Chewing difficulty and bite misalignment
  • Shifting and tilting of adjacent teeth
  • Speech changes caused by gaps in the dental arch
  • Loose, uncomfortable, or ill-fitting dentures
  • Teeth damaged beyond repair by trauma or deep infection

How do Dental Implants work?

The dental implant process relies on a biological phenomenon called osseointegration. First, a small titanium screw is precisely placed into the jawbone beneath the gum line. Because titanium is highly biocompatible, the jawbone naturally heals and fuses directly to the implant material over a period of 3 to 6 months. This creates a solid foundation.

After the jawbone has fully bonded with the implant, we attach a small connector called an abutment. Then, your new crown or bridge is placed on top. This setup replaces your whole tooth, from root to crown.

Replacing the tooth is only part of the process. Our approach to implant dentistry in Beverly Hills covers each stage, candidacy, bone health, placement, and the final restoration so the result looks and functions like the tooth it replaces.

What are the benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the only option that replaces both the root and the crown of your tooth, giving you long-lasting benefits for your health.

  • Function: Restores strong, stable chewing and biting function.
  • Comfort: Eliminates the slipping, clicking, and gum irritation associated with traditional dentures.
  • Durability: The titanium implant acts as a permanent fixture in your mouth.
  • Appearance: Custom crowns are shaded and shaped to match your facial aesthetics perfectly.
  • Oral health support: Stimulates the jawbone to prevent atrophy and premature facial aging.
  • Preservation of nearby teeth: Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth for support.

Who is a good candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a good candidate for Dental Implants if…

You may be a good candidate if you are missing one, several, or all of your teeth. You need to have enough jawbone to support the implant, or be open to bone grafting if needed. Healthy gums and a commitment to good oral hygiene are also important.

Dental Implants may not be the right fit if…

Dental implants may not be right for you if you have uncontrolled diabetes or certain autoimmune conditions that make healing difficult. Active gum disease needs to be treated before getting implants. Heavy smokers who cannot stop during healing also have a higher risk of implant failure.

How should I prepare for Dental Implants?

  • Exam and imaging: We perform a comprehensive clinical exam and take 3D CBCT scans to evaluate your jawbone density, nerve pathways, and sinus cavities.
  • Medical history review: We review your overall health, identifying any conditions that could affect bone healing.
  • Medication review: We review your daily medications and provide individualized instructions, including whether any medication changes or pre-operative antibiotics are necessary.
  • Sedation or numbing planning: We finalize your comfort protocols, choosing between local anesthesia or deeper sedation options for the surgery.
  • Transportation planning: If you opt for IV sedation, you must arrange for a trusted adult to drive you home from our Beverly Hills office.
  • Pre-appointment fasting: If undergoing sedation, you will receive specific fasting instructions to follow before your surgery.

What happens during a Dental Implant appointment?

  • Imaging confirmation: We review your 3D surgical guide to ensure absolute precision during placement.
  • Numbing and sedation: We administer local anesthesia and your chosen sedation to ensure you feel no pain.
  • Site preparation: If a failing tooth is present, it is gently extracted. A small incision is made in the gum tissue to access the jaw.
  • Implant placement: The jaw is carefully prepared using specialized instruments, and the titanium implant is secured into the bone.
  • Sealing and temporary placement: A healing cap is placed over the implant, and the gums are sutured closed. If aesthetically necessary (such as for front teeth), a temporary cosmetic tooth may be placed.
  • Aftercare instructions: We review your post-operative guidelines, provide gauze, and discuss pain management.

What does getting Dental Implants feel like?

During the actual procedure, you will not feel any sharp pain. You will feel the initial pinch of the local anesthesia injection. As the site is prepared, you will experience a sensation of deep pressure and feel the vibration of the instruments, along with a whirring or drilling sound.
