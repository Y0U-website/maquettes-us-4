0 Knowledgeable. Dedicated. Passionate. Honeycomb Dentistry is a family-friendly dental office located in the South Minneapolis area, serving patients in the Twin Cities metro and beyond. In August of 2026, Dr. Schulhof took ownership of the practice from Dr. Evgeny Bashirov. Prior to Dr. Bashirov was Dr. Thomas Bruss, who was the owner since 1985.

Dr. Schulhof and the team at Honeycomb Dentistry look forward to carrying on the strong reputation Drs. Bashirov and Bruss created in the Minneapolis community.
