Dr. Curley

Dr. Curley is a graduate of the College of the Holy Cross in Worcester, MA, and earned his D.D.S. degree from Creighton University in Omaha, NE. After his time in the Midwest, Dr. Curley returned to the Hudson Valley and completed his residency training at the V.A. Hospital in Castle Point, NY, where he was proud to serve our nation’s veterans.

Dr. Curley resides in Orangetown with his wife and two children. When he is not working, he loves to spend time with his family and taking long walks around town; say hello if you see him out and about! Dr. Curley also participates in several area adult ice hockey leagues.

Dr. Curley is a member of the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Association, the 9th District Dental Association, the Rockland County Dental Society and the Hudson River Study Club.
