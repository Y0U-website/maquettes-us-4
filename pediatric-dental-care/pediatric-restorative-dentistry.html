Pediatric Dental Crowns

If your little one is in need of a solution for damaged or decayed teeth, pediatric dental crowns are here to save the day. We meticulously select the most appropriate crown for your child&#x27;s unique needs, restoring those tiny teeth to their former function and appearance. With dental crowns, your child can say goodbye to discomfort and enjoy a durable solution that will keep their smile shining bright.

Pediatric Pulpotomies

Baby teeth are important. They help children eat comfortably, speak clearly, and hold space for the adult teeth developing underneath.

When a cavity becomes very deep and reaches the nerve of a baby tooth, a pulpotomy may be recommended. Often called a "baby root canal," this procedure removes the affected portion of the nerve while preserving the healthy portion of the tooth. A protective crown is then placed to restore strength and function.In many cases, a pulpotomy allows us to save the tooth, relieve discomfort, and help your child avoid more extensive treatment.

Our goal is always to keep your child&#x27;s smile healthy, comfortable, and growing exactly as nature intended.

Pediatric Sedation

Our dental practice offers pediatric sedation to help children feel calm, comfortable, and at ease during their dental visits. Sedation can be especially helpful for kids who experience anxiety, have trouble sitting still, or need more involved treatment. By tailoring sedation options to each child’s age, comfort level, and medical history, we’re able to provide gentle, stress-free care while ensuring your child’s safety every step of the way.
