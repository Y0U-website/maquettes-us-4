The Process of Getting Dental Implants

What to expect from consultation to final restoration.

Step 1: Personalized Consultation

Your dental implant consultation includes digital imaging, a full oral health review, and treatment planning with your dentist and oral surgeon.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Using local anesthesia, the implant is placed into the jawbone. Some patients benefit from sedation dentistry for added comfort during oral surgery.

Step 3: Healing and Restoration

After healing and osseointegration, your final restoration is attached, completing the dental implant procedure with natural-looking prosthetic teeth.

FAQs About Dental Implants

Common questions from patients considering implants.

  • How long does the dental implant process take? The full process can take several months because of healing and bone integration, but each phase supports long-term stability.
  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients report minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia and sedation dentistry help keep the procedure comfortable.
  • Can implants replace dentures? Yes. Unlike traditional dentures, implants stay fixed and feel more like natural teeth.
  • What if I have bone loss? Bone grafting and advanced oral surgery techniques can often rebuild bone for implant support.
  • How do I care for dental implants? Regular brushing, flossing, and dental visits help protect implants and maintain oral health.
