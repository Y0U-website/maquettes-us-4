Our Other Orthodontic Offerings

In addition to traditional metal braces, we offer other types of braces as well, including:

Ceramic braces More discreet than traditional braces, ceramic braces use tooth-colored ceramic brackets, making them less noticeable. If you are concerned about the aesthetics of your smile while wearing braces, consider ceramic braces.

Lingual braces With lingual braces, the brackets and wires are placed on the inside of your teeth, making them nearly invisible when you smile.

Self-ligating braces This type of braces eliminates the need for ligature elastics or rubber bands. Instead, they use special metal or ceramic brackets to hold your archwires in place.

The Benefits of Braces in Mamaroneck

Before you begin an orthodontic treatment plan, here are some of the benefits of wearing braces that you may not have considered:

Braces Are Affordable

Thanks to payment plans and dental insurance, braces are more affordable than you may think. During your orthodontic consultation, we will discuss your orthodontic costs with you.

Braces Do More than Straighten Teeth

Everyone knows that braces straighten your teeth, but did you know they can also correct your jaw alignment, which can help you chew and talk better? A properly aligned jaw can also keep your teeth from uneven wear.

Braces Are Not Painful

You may have heard stories about how braces hurt your teeth. However, at Roshe Dental, we can assure you that after the initial discomfort of the pressure from the brackets and wires, your braces will not hurt.

Braces Are the First Step

When your dentist removes your braces, your teeth will begin moving back into their original position. That’s why you will need to wear a retainer for about a year to keep your teeth aligned. Thankfully, Roshe Dental has an in-office 3-D printer that can make your retainer on the same day we remove your braces.
