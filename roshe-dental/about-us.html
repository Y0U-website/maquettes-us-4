Dr. Daria Royzman

Dr. Daria Royzman has been providing periodontal and dental implant services for over 15 years and proudly serves the patients at Roshe Dental. Dr. Royzman believes continuing education is vital to her profession, so she regularly attends advanced training courses to stay apprised of the newest dental trends and technologies.

After graduating with honors from the New York University College of Dentistry, Dr. Royzman graduated from the University of Pennsylvania, a prestigious Ivy League university, with her certificate for graduate training in periodontology.

She has been recognized by the American Board of Periodontology for going above and beyond her required educational obligations, and she served as the president of the New York chapter of the American Association of Women Dentists.

Dr. Dennise Muñoz

Dr. Dennise Mu oz graduated from the School of Dental Medicine at SUNY Stony Brook in 2003 before continuing her education to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program. During this time, she provided dental care to the Sioux Indians at the Pine Ridge Reservation in South Dakota. Still today, Dr. Mu oz continues to provide treatment to underserved communities.

With more than 18 years of experience in general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Mu oz is committed to staying current with the latest dental technologies to provide the best care to her patients. She loves building long-term relationships with her patients while providing excellent dental care.

Dr. Mu oz is a member of the American Dental Association, the Ninth District Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

When she isn’t caring for patients, she spends most of her time with her husband, taking their two children to different activities, traveling, crocheting, and playing with their rescue cat, Kevin.

Dr. Stephanie A. Tran

Dr. Stephanie Tran graduated from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry before furthering her education with a General Practice Residency program at SUNY Stony Brook University Medical Center. There, she gained experience in managing dental trauma and surgeries.

After practicing general dentistry, she attended the University of Tennessee Health Science Center to complete her postgraduate endodontics specialty residency. As an endodontist, Dr. Tran specializes in providing endodontic care to adults and children.

Dr. Tran is multi-lingual, speaking fluent Vietnamese and French, as well as conversational Spanish and Mandarin Chinese; this helps her better serve the diverse New York communities where Roshe Dental is located.

Dr. Tran is a member of the New York State Dental Society and the American Association of Endodontics, and she believes that continuing education is an integral part of providing quality dental care to her patients.

When she isn’t working, Dr. Tran enjoys traveling, trying new restaurants, cooking, and going to the beach with her dog. Although she was raised in California, she calls New York her home.
