What Is a Dental Implant?

The dental implant process consists of three parts: the implant, the abutment, and the restoration. The implant is a titanium post placed into your jaw, acting as your tooth’s root. Once the implant goes through the healing process known as osseointegration and becomes part of your jawbone, we add the abutment and restoration.

The abutment is a connector that attaches the implant to the dental restoration. If you are replacing a single tooth, the restoration will likely be a dental crown. However, for several missing teeth, we can attach a dental bridge to two or more dental implants, and if all your teeth need to be replaced, we offer either All-on-4 dental implants or implant-supported dentures.

When you visit our dental office for a consultation, we can discuss which implant option best fits your needs.

The Importance of Replacing Missing Teeth

When you have missing teeth, it affects your oral and overall health in the following ways:

Biting and chewing Missing teeth keep you from eating the healthy foods you love, which can affect your health. Also, your remaining teeth have to withstand additional biting force.

Shifting teeth If you have a gap in your smile, your remaining teeth will shift into the open space. This can cause TMJ pain or misalignment.

Aesthetic appearance Without a complete set of teeth, your face will begin to look sunken and older.

What Are the Advantages of Dental Implants in Mamaroneck?

Other than the natural-looking appearance of dental implants, we recommend dental implants as your best tooth replacement option because of their ability to:

Preserve Your Jawbone

Without dental implants, your jawbone begins to shrink where your teeth used to be. Dental implants preserve your jawbone health.

Preserve Your Remaining Teeth

For a traditional dental bridge, enamel from the adjacent teeth has to be removed for dental crown placement. With dental implants, your healthy teeth can be left alone.

Require Minimal Care

You will treat dental implants just like your natural teeth. All you have to do is brush and floss them regularly and visit your Mamaroneck dentist every six months for routine exams and cleanings.
