What you can expect

We treat you as family.

The same team, visit after visit, and the time to explain what we find.

A personable approach Our personable approach to dental care, combined with innovative techniques and services, will give you plenty to smile about. We take the time to explain what we find and go over your options with you.

We care about your whole family We care about you, your family and your friends. We see children of all ages, and we offer nitrous oxide for our high-anxiety patients.

Care in one friendly office We’re a general dentist office with visiting specialists, so periodontal treatment, implants, root canals, extractions and oral surgery are taken care of right here, by clinicians who know our office.
