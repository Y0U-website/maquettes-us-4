Our dental services

From cleanings and fillings to crowns, implants, root canals and extractions the care your family needs, in one friendly office.

Preventive & general care

Cleanings, exams, digital X-rays, oral cancer screenings and resin fillings for children of all ages through adults.

Crowns, bridges & dentures

Crowns and bridgework taken from a digital scan, implant restoration, veneers, and removable dentures and partials.

Gums, root canals & extractions

Periodontal therapy, implants, root canals, and extractions and oral surgery taken care of by our visiting specialists.

Digital scans for crowns

We scan the tooth instead of taking a putty impression, and your crown is designed from that scan.

Extractions & wisdom teeth

Extractions and wisdom teeth removal with Dr. Bahrani, in our office no referral out.
