Emergency Dental Services We Offer

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth is too badly damaged or infected to save, an extraction removes the source of the problem. We numb the area, carefully remove the tooth, and guide you through aftercare to relieve discomfort and protect the surrounding teeth and gums.

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal treats an infection deep inside the tooth without removing the tooth itself. Our dentists remove the infected tissue, then clean and seal the area. This helps ease the pain, and prevents the infection from spreading.

Dental Fillings

Whether a filling has come loose or decay has left part of your tooth exposed and sensitive, a new filling can repair the area. We clean the tooth and restore it with durable, tooth-colored material, bringing back its shape, strength, and function.

Dental Crowns

If a tooth cracks, breaks, or loses an existing crown, a custom dental crown can restore its protection and support. We fit the new crown securely over the tooth to help prevent further damage and make everyday biting and chewing comfortable again.
