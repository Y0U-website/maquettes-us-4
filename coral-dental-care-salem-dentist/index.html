Services

For visits that help us clean, check, and protect your natural teeth.

A dental bridge uses your existing teeth, on either side of the gap, as anchors to hold a replacement tooth securely in place. No surgery, no removable pieces, just a fixed, natural-looking fit that lets you bite and chew comfortably again.

A dental implant replaces your tooth from the root up. We place a small screw into your jawbone, let it heal, then top it with a custom crown. It helps protect your bite while looking, feeling, and functioning just like your natural teeth.

Correct your teeth by straightening them using a series of custom-made clear aligners, no metal brackets or wires involved. You switch to a new set every couple of weeks, and your teeth gradually shift into place.

If a slightly chipped tooth is your main concern, veneers are your best bet. Thin, custom-made shells are placed over the front of your teeth, creating a natural-looking finish that can conceal any imperfections.
