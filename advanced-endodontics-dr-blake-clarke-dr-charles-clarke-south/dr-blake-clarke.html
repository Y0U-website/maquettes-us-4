Dr. Blake Clarke Board-Certified Endodontist

Dr. Blake Clarke was born and raised in St. Louis, Missouri. He attended Saint Louis University, where he met his wife, before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri Kansas City. After dental school, he completed his specialty residency training in Endodontics at the University of Minnesota, where he served as Chief Resident.

He now resides in St. Louis with his wife and their three dogs. He enjoys playing and watching hockey, traveling, spending time with his dogs, and trying new restaurants.
