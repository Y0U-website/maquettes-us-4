Dr. Chad Smart, DDS

Being an orthodontist is more than just a job to me; it’s a calling and a passion. It means having the opportunity to transform lives daily by creating beautiful, healthy smiles. My profession allows me to blend art and science, utilizing advanced techniques and technologies to achieve remarkable results. Every day, I am driven by the desire to improve my patients’ self-confidence, oral health, and overall well-being. It’s incredibly fulfilling to know that my work can have such a profound and lasting impact on people’s lives.

One of the aspects of my work that excites me the most is the transformative power of a beautiful smile. Witnessing the boost in confidence and happiness that a new smile brings to my patients is incredibly rewarding. Each time a patient looks in the mirror and sees their new smile for the first time, it’s a powerful reminder of why I love what I do.

Education

I graduated with honors from California Lutheran University in 1992 with a Bachelor of Science in Biology before attending the UCLA School of Dentistry, receiving numerous academic and clinical awards. An elected member of the Omicron Kappa Upsilon national dental honor society, I graduated Suma Cum Laude with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1996.

I then spent two additional years at UCLA receiving specialty training in orthodontics. This specialty training focused heavily on craniofacial growth and development as well as all aspects of orthodontic theory and practice.

Continual Learning

I’m particularly interested in integrating the latest advancements in digital imaging technology, which allows for precise treatment planning, enhanced accuracy of treatments, and a more comfortable experience for my patients.

Another area that truly excites me is the development of clear aligner therapy, such as Invisalign®. These advancements offer effective treatment options that are less noticeable and more convenient, providing a great alternative to traditional braces.

To enhance my stills and further my knowledge, I am active in several professional organizations that provide ample opportunities for continuing education:

The American Association of Orthodontists

Dr. Brian Dansie, DDS, MS

Dr. Dansie, a board-certified pediatric dentist, grew up in a small town in rural Utah. He attended dental school at the Ohio State University in Columbus, Ohio – Go Buckeyes! After realizing that pediatric dentistry was his favorite part of his dental education, he completed a 2-year pediatric dental residency at Nationwide Children’s Hospital and a master’s degree at Ohio State focusing on the oral health of premature infants and children with special needs.

He was raised hiking, camping, and four-wheeling all over the Rocky Mountains. He enjoys the great outdoors and getting some fresh air in nature. He played basketball and football growing up and still coaches his kids in basketball, baseball, football, and soccer. Dr. Brian and his wife, Rachel relocated to the Pacific Northwest from California with their three children in 2015.

If you ask him why he went into pediatric dentistry, the answer is simple. He loves kids! His first rotation in dental school was in pediatrics, and he knew instantly that nothing else in dentistry would be as rewarding to him as working with children. He genuinely enjoys laughing and joking with kids all day. He believes that instilling confidence and self-worth in a child can start at the dental office with caring and compassionate pediatric dentists.
