Sharing Your Information

We will only share your information in the following situations:

  • With Your Consent: We may share your information with other healthcare providers, family members, or insurance companies with your explicit authorization.
  • For Treatment, Payment, or Healthcare Operations: Sharing PHI with specialists or labs involved in your care. Providing information to your insurance company for billing purposes.
  • As Required by Law: We may disclose information in response to a court order, subpoena, or other legal obligation.
  • For Public Health and Safety: Reporting to authorities as required by law, such as in cases of communicable diseases or emergencies.

Protecting Your Information

We are committed to safeguarding your personal and health information. We use a combination of administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of electronic health records.
  • Secure access controls for authorized personnel.
  • Regular staff training on privacy and data security.

Your Privacy Rights

As a patient, you have specific rights regarding your personal and health information:

  • Right to Access: You can request copies of your health records at any time.
  • Right to Amend: You may request corrections to your personal or health information if you believe it is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Right to Restrict: You can ask us to limit the use or disclosure of your information.
  • Right to Confidential Communication: You may request that we contact you in a specific way (e.g., only via email or phone).
  • Right to a Copy of This Privacy Policy: You can request a paper or electronic copy of this Privacy Policy at any time.
