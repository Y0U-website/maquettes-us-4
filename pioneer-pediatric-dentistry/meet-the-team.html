Our Doctor

Each child has unique dental needs, and Dr. Kirby Goodwine completed several years of specialized pediatric dental training in order to provide patients with the highest quality of individualized care. Dr. Goodwine is dedicated to helping parents and patients feel educated and supported throughout their time in our office.

MEET DR. KIRBY GOODWINE

Dr. Clifford Kirby Goodwine is originally from Elizabethtown, Kentucky. He graduated with honors from the University of Kentucky in 2015 and then attended the University of Louisville School of Dentistry before graduating in 2019. Afterward, Dr. Goodwine continued his training at the University of Louisville, completing a two-year residency in pediatric dentistry and obtaining his master’s degree in June of 2021. He currently lives in Lexington with his wife, Lauren, and children, Madilyn and Aiden. He enjoys working out, cooking/grilling, and hanging with his chocolate lab named Jackson.

He’s quite the adventurer himself! He loves sky diving, mountain biking, and river rafting.

AFFILIATIONS

  • American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry Diplomate
  • American Board of Pediatric Dentists Board Certified Member
  • American Dental Association
  • Kentucky Society of Pediatric Dentistry
  • Southeastern Society of Pediatric Dentistry
