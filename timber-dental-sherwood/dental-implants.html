Why are Dental Implants so great?

No more hiding your smile

Dental Implants are custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your existing teeth, so they blend right in. You can smile, talk, and laugh with confidence, knowing that your teeth are staying put!

No more avoiding foods you love

Get ready to enjoy all the foods you love once again! Because dental implants are anchored securely to your jaw, you won’t have to worry about your teeth shifting or moving around. With dental implants, you can confidently bite into crunchy apples, savor juicy steaks, and enjoy all your favorite foods to the fullest!

No more chronic tooth pain When you lose a tooth, your other teeth can become unstable and shift, causing chronic tooth pain. Dental implants stimulate the bone and prevent deterioration, keeping your remaining teeth stable, and helping you avoid chronic tooth pain.

Dental Implants Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What’s a dental implant? Implants are biocompatible, artificial tooth roots that are anchored inside of your jaw, just like your real teeth. They’re durable enough to support a crown (for single tooth replacement), bridge or denture (for multiple tooth replacement).

Plus, implants last for the rest of your life in the majority of cases. There’s no better investment that you can make when it comes to restoring a healthy bite.

What are implants made of? Dental implants are usually made out of titanium or a titanium alloy because it’s biocompatible, which means it’s well-tolerated by the human body and does not cause an adverse immune response. Plus, it’s strong and durable enough to withstand the constant pressure and force of chewing and biting. In some cases, other materials such as zirconia may also be used.

How long do dental implants last? Implants last for the rest of your life in the majority of cases. There’s no better investment that you can make when it comes to restoring a healthy bite.

Am I a candidate for dental implants? Dental implants may not be an option if you have certain medical conditions or insufficient bone density in your jaw. It’s always a good idea to consult with a dentist or oral surgeon to determine whether dental implants are right for you.

How long does the procedure take? The process of getting dental implants typically takes several months and involves multiple stages. The exact duration can vary depending on several factors, including the patient’s individual needs and the complexity of the case.

Generally, the dental implant process can take anywhere from a few months to a year or more, depending on the individual case. Your dentist will be able to provide a more specific timeline based on your needs at your consultation. Book your consultation with us today!

What should I expect during the procedure? Dental Implants are permanent replacement teeth that look and feel like natural teeth. They offer the same biting force as your teeth would. You care for your dental implants the same way you would your natural teeth. Plus, implants last for the rest of your life in the majority of cases. There’s no better investment that you can make when it comes to restoring a healthy bite!

How long does it take to heal after the procedure? The healing process after dental implant surgery can take several months. Your dentist will be able to provide a more specific timeline and more details for your individual case at your consultation appointment. Schedule your visit today!
