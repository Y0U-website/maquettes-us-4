About the service

Dental implants offer a permanent, natural-looking solution for missing teeth—and at Integrative Holistic Dentistry in Chapel Hill, we take it further by using biocompatible, metal-free options designed to protect your whole-body health. Our implant solutions restore chewing function, preserve bone structure, and enhance aesthetics without compromising your immune system or long-term well-being. Every case begins with a detailed evaluation of your bone health, bite alignment, and overall physiology to ensure optimal outcomes.

We offer both traditional titanium implants and ceramic (zirconia) implants for patients with sensitivities or metal-free preferences. Our holistic approach includes ozone therapy during surgery to reduce inflammation and prevent infection, along with PRF (platelet-rich fibrin) to accelerate healing naturally. We carefully plan each placement using 3D imaging and digital scans for precision, comfort, and lasting success.

Whether you&#x27;re missing one tooth or exploring full-arch solutions, our implant care is focused on restoring both confidence and oral function. Patients across Chapel Hill trust us for safe, compassionate implant placement that honors both health and aesthetics.
