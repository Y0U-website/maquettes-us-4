Non-Metal Ceramic Implants

Metal-free ceramic dental implants offer strong, natural, and biocompatible tooth replacement for long-term health and beauty in Chapel Hill.

About the service

For patients seeking a metal-free, immune-friendly tooth replacement, ceramic dental implants offer a safe, natural alternative. At Integrative Holistic Dentistry in Chapel Hill, we use zirconia implants to restore missing teeth while preserving biocompatibility and aesthetics. These non-metal implants integrate naturally with your bone and gum tissue, offering exceptional strength without triggering sensitivities.

Zirconia implants are corrosion-resistant, temperature-neutral, and less likely to attract plaque compared to traditional titanium. Their white color blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth for a more natural look, especially in the front of the mouth. We pair implant placement with ozone therapy and regenerative support like PRF to optimize healing and reduce inflammation.
