About

Dr. Tricia Lorenzi, PharmD, FDN-P

Dr. Tricia Lorenzi grew up in the small town of Charleroi, Pennsylvania, just outside of Pittsburgh. As the daughter of a dentist, her curiosity about health sciences began early, which led her to earn her pharmacy degree from the University of Pittsburgh and later her Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD) from Shenandoah University. During her career as a pharmacist, she has been a trusted and readily accessible healthcare community resource for over 20 years.

While completing her PharmD, her own personal health challenges and those within her family opened her eyes to the therapeutic role of integrative and foundational health. This experience led her to become a Functional Diagnostic Nutrition Practitioner (FDN-P), blending her extensive background in pharmaceutical science with a heart for whole-person care.

Dr. Lorenzi lives in the Chapel Hill area with her husband and enjoys spending time with her family, staying active outdoors, sewing, being a lifelong learner, and growing in her relationship with Christ.
