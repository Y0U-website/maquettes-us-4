About

Dr. Michael Thomas, DDS, AIAOMT

Dr. Thomas was born in Newark, New Jersey and raised in Charlotte, North Carolina. His education involves attending North Carolina Central University for his undergraduate degree. He then completed his dental education at the former University of North Carolina School of Dentistry (now UNC Adams School of Dentistry).

Dr. Michael Thomas strives to provide the highest quality of care in all aspects of dentistry and continually educates himself in the latest advances in dental technology. With over 15 years of experience and a life long learner, Dr. Thomas continues to further his education and skills by taking countless hours of continuing education.

Dr. Thomas loves being a positive role model in his community. He currently lives in Durham, NC with his lovely wife Monica. Dr. Thomas and his wife are active in their church as well as the local community. He spends much of his free time with his family, playing tennis, golf and enjoying the outdoors.

Professional Organization and Society Memberships :

  • International Academy of Oral Medicine and Toxicology ‍
