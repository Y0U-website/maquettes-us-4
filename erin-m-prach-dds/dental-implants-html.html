What Are Dental Implants?

4 Step Dental Implant Process

While dental bridges and traditional dentures can be placed relatively quickly, the dental implant process usually takes several months. This might seem like a long time to wait for a new smile, but the benefits that you enjoy with strong, sturdy dental implant posts will be worth the wait. We work alongside specialists to make sure that everything goes smoothly during your dental implant process. Keep reading to learn more about the four main steps of getting implants: consultation, surgery, osseointegration, and delivery of a final restoration.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Since not everyone is a good candidate for implant surgery, we’ll need to perform an initial consultation to make sure that you can undergo the procedure. We’ll need to review your oral health and go over your medical history to see if there are any conditions that might complicate implant placement. Also, bone grafts, tooth extractions, and other preparatory procedures may need to be performed before you can receive your dental implants. A personalized treatment plan that includes the estimated timeline and cost will be created by the end of the consultation.

Dental Implant Surgery

We will refer you to a separate specialist who can place your dental implants. First, they will numb your mouth so that you don’t feel any pain during the treatment. Then they will open up the gum tissue to expose the jawbone underneath and make holes for the implant posts to be inserted into. Once the implants have been carefully placed at the desired locations at carefully chosen angles, the gums will be sutured shut, and a protective cap will be attached to the implant posts.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

Osseointegration is when the implant posts naturally merge with the jawbone. This is what makes them so sturdy and reliable compared to traditional forms of tooth replacement. It does not happen overnight, though; the usual timeframe is three to six months, although it should be noted that everybody’s mouth heals at a different rate. After osseointegration is complete, a second (albeit smaller) surgery is required so that a metal connector piece called an abutment can be placed on top of the implants; they will be what holds the final restoration in place once it has been made.

Delivery of Dental Restoration(s)

Your mouth will need to fully recover from the previous step before we can take the necessary impressions to design the final restoration. The impressions will be sent to a trusted dental lab where experienced ceramists will create a crown, bridge, or denture out of materials that closely resemble your natural teeth. The final result will be sent to our dental office, and we will personally handle the process of attaching the final restoration to your implants via the abutments. Your new teeth should be almost completely indistinguishable from the rest of your natural smile once we’re done.

Day-to-Day Benefits

We use our teeth every day to speak, eat, and smile. For that reason, it only makes sense that you’d want a tooth-replacement solution that’ll allow you to live your day-to-day life with ease. Fortunately, dental implants can help you do just that by giving the following benefits:

  • More biting strength. Since implants replace the entire structure of a missing tooth, they’re able to withstand the chewing of virtually any food.
  • Easier oral hygiene. With dental implants, you don’t have to worry about incorporating complex steps into your oral hygiene routine or purchasing special cleaning products. All you need to do is care for them as you would care for natural teeth!
  • Increased confidence. Unlike dentures, your implants will stay firmly in place once they’ve successfully fused with the surrounding bone tissue. They’ll allow you to eat, speak, and smile with much more confidence.

Health Benefits

Aside from appearance, dental implants in Casper offer multiple health advantages as well. Some of those include:

  • Jawbone preservation. Because dental implants stimulate the jawbone, they can keep it strong, healthy, and functional for a lifetime.
  • Protection for natural teeth. It’s common for teeth to shift after tooth loss. Fortunately, dental implants can prevent that from happening by serving as a placeholder to ensure every tooth stays exactly where it belongs.
  • Better overall health. Studies show there’s a correlation between tooth loss and an increased risk of multiple diseases. Because implants are so similar to natural teeth, it’s reasonable to believe they can help lower such risks.

Long-Term Benefits

By choosing to get dental implants now, they can help you for years to come with the following benefits:

  • High success rate. Dental implants have an impressive success rate of about 95 percent even 20 years after they’ve been placed.
  • Youthful appearance. Since implants stimulate blood flow in the jaw, they help prevent its deterioration and the sunken, aged look that often happens with tooth loss.
  • Long-lasting results. Unlike dentures and dental bridges, your implants can last decades without needing replacement. Speaking of dentures, your implant dentist in Casper offers implant-supported dentures !

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Virtually every grown, healthy adult can potentially undergo the dental implant process. Before moving forward, however, we’ll have to consider certain main factors that make a good candidate, such as:

If we detect an underlying problem, we’ll do everything we can to get your mouth back into shape so that you’re ready for the dental implant process. This could involve preliminary procedures like gum disease treatment, bone grafting, or even tooth extractions. Once you’ve undergone the necessary services and we’ve properly prepared your oral health, we can resume the dental implant process.

  • Good Overall Health : The dental implant treatment involves minor oral surgery, meaning you’ll need to be sufficiently healthy to undergo the procedure and to optimize your healing period.
  • Great Oral Health : Of course, your gums and the rest of your smile will have to be free of any existing issues like decay or periodontal disease, which can increase the risk of implant failure if left untreated.
  • Adequate Jawbone Mass : For your dental implants to work, they’ll need to fuse with your bone tissue via osseointegration. That’s why our team will place your metal posts in the thickest parts of your jawbone, which will ensure a higher success rate and longer lifespan for your restoration(s).

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

Like other dental treatments, the cost of your dental implant tooth replacement plan will vary based on your specific situation. What Are the Stages of Dental Implant Treatment?

Initially, you will visit our dental office for a consultation, where Dr. Prach will evaluate the condition of your oral health and how many teeth you’re missing. If you have any preexisting oral health issues or need a bone graft, these problems must be treated before beginning surgery.

Next, you’ll come in for surgery, where we will make small incisions in your gumline to access your jawbone. After, we will place your dental implants, allowing for three to six months for the titanium to fuse with your bone. Lastly, you will come into our dental office and Dr. Prach will place your permanent restoration onto your implants for a beautifully functional smile.

Dental Implant Frequently Asked Questions

Compared to the other methods of replacing missing teeth, dental implants in Casper offer the highest number of benefits by far. Due to their amazing longevity, sturdiness, and lifelike appearance, they have exploded in popularity in recent years. However, it’s important that you’re prepared with as much knowledge about the treatment as possible before you undergo the procedure. That’s why we’ve taken the time to answer some of the questions about dental implants that we get asked the most frequently here at our dental office.

What Is the Success Rate of Dental Implants?

It varies from patient to patient depending on their oral health and habits. For a generally healthy person with good oral hygiene, dental implants boast a success rate of 95-98 %. Certain habits can decrease this percentage, such as smoking or uncontrolled diabetes. That’s why we recommend quitting your tobacco habit or getting your blood sugar levels under control before receiving implants.

How Do I Make Sure My Dental Implants Last?

With proper maintenance, your implants can easily last for several decades. This involves diligent at-home oral hygiene, including daily brushing and flossing. Although the implants themselves can’t get cavities, the gums supporting them can still become infected and contribute to implant failure if the infection is not treated in a timely manner. Additionally, you should attend regular checkups and cleanings with your dentist in Casper. At these visits, we can ensure your implants, as well as any of your remaining teeth and your gums, are still in good condition.

What Are Dental Implants Made Of?

These days, most dental implants are made of a titanium alloy, which is similar to what is used in hip implants, knee implants, and shoulder implants. The main reason that titanium is used is its incredible biocompatibility. Once the implant has been placed in your jawbone, it actually promotes growth and eventually becomes one with the bone. This allows it to form such a powerful and lasting bond. In addition, allergies to titanium are extremely rare, making it a perfect material for this form of tooth replacement.

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

Your mouth will be numbed with a local anesthetic before your procedure begins, so you shouldn’t feel any pain. The jawbone doesn’t have many nerve endings either. While the surgery itself shouldn’t hurt, you may experience some soreness for a few days afterward. This is temporary and can be managed with your prescription and recommended over-the-counter medications. Cold compresses can also be useful. If you have any concerns, give us a call so we can help!

Can I Take Dental Implants Out?

Dental implants are fixed to the bone, so they cannot be removed. Dental professionals are the only ones who have the tools to safely remove them. Some types of implants dentures can be removed at home for cleaning, but the implants themselves should always remain stationary in the jaw.

Are Dental Implants Safe?

For most patients who have adequate oral health, dental implants are very successful when placed by a qualified professional. If you have low jawbone density or gum disease, the dentist can fix these issues with specific preliminary procedures to make implant surgery safer and more successful. If you have diabetes, cancer, or other medical conditions that could make surgery risky, we will discuss them during your consultation.

Am I Too Old to Get Dental Implants?

There is no upper age limit for dental implants once you become an adult. If you have good oral health and sufficient jawbone density, you can be pretty much any age and still qualify for dental implants. Seniors are more likely to have other health issues that could complicate dental implant treatment. During your consultation, we will discuss your health history and examine your smile to determine if dental implants are the best solution for you!

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

As mentioned earlier, dental implants have the potential to last for decades upon decades. They might even let you enjoy the benefits of a full smile for the rest of your life! However, this is only the case if you take good care of them. On the bright side, dental implant care is pretty straightforward; just follow these five simple steps.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Peri-implantitis, an infection of the tissue around an implant, is a leading cause of dental implant failure. Most cases of peri-implantitis can be prevented via thorough oral hygiene. Your teeth cleaning routine should include:

  • Twice-daily brushing Always be sure you brush the area along the gumline.
  • Regular flossing Floss gets into the tight spaces that your toothbrush cannot reach. You may not be able to floss between your artificial teeth if you have a bridge. However, you might be able to use a water flosser to clean beneath them.
  • Lots of hydration Water rinses away bacteria and food particles that may be lingering in your mouth.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Dental implants are durable enough to stand up to most foods, so you should feel free to eat all of your favorite treats. However, keep in mind that just like some foods are harmful for your natural teeth, some foods tough on dental implants. You should limit your consumption of hard, sticky, and sugary foods. Instead, focus on enjoying a balanced diet that is rich in nutrients that promote oral health, including calcium and vitamin C.

Break Bad Habits

Some of the worst habits for individuals with dental implants include:

  • Smoking Tobacco increases your risk of infections and contributes to dry mouth. Every effort you make to quit will be worth it!
  • Using teeth as tools It can be tempting to use your teeth to open packages or cut the tags off of new clothes, but doing so might damage your restorations.
  • Chewing on hard objects Ice, jawbreaker candies, and pen caps are just a few things that might harm your implants if you chew on them.

Protect Your Dental Implants

If you often wake up with a sore jaw, it is possible that you grind your teeth at night, which can harm your implants. You may need to wear a custom mouthguard from a qualified dentist while you sleep to protect both your natural and replacement teeth. Similarly, if you play sports, you should also wear a custom mouthguard.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

You should be in the habit of visiting Dr. Prach at least twice a year for a cleaning and checkup. She will examine both your teeth and your implants. If there are any signs of a problem, she may be able to address the issue before it becomes serious. Our team may remove your implant restorations in order to thoroughly clean your mouth.
