Enhancing Smiles with Expert Care in Peabody, MA Since 2024

At our practice, we prioritize educating our patients in a comfortable and safe environment while delivering comprehensive care tailored to their needs. Understanding that dental anxiety is common, we have created a soothing atmosphere and an efficient scheduling system to ensure minimal wait times. By integrating the latest technology with proven traditional techniques, we are committed to providing the highest quality dental care in Massachusetts.

Ahram Shin, D.M.D

Dr. Shin is a Restorative Dentist. She holds BA in Biology and International Global Studies from Brandeis University and received her DMD in Dentistry from Boston University Goldham School of Dentistry.

Dr. Shin brings a solid clinical background to the practice, and she is well skilled in all the traditional facets of dentistry as well as the newer technologies. Dr. Shin is a second generation dentist in her family along with her brother.

Originally from South Korea, Dr. Shin moved to the United States in her teens. After obtaining her degrees in MA, she started her professional career in Rhode Island, where she met her now husband. They moved back to Massachusetts in 2022, where she considers her true home.

Dr. Shin’s dedication to serving others is evident through her involvement in various mission teams. She has provided free dental care to underserved communities in Nicaragua and Haiti, and has also contributed to Mission of Mercy in Rhode Island, where she helped deliver essential dental services to those in need. Her commitment to global and local humanitarian work highlights her passion for improving access to healthcare for all.

In her free time, she enjoys trying new restaurants with her husband, supporting local businesses and is trying desperately to pick up golfing.

Michael Eidelman, D.M.D

Dr. Eidelman has proudly served the Peabody community for almost 40 years, earning the trust of generations of patients since 1985.

Along with our dedicated team, he always has been committed to providing advanced dental care in a warm and welcoming environment, ensuring that every patient feels comfortable and at ease.

Being a grandfather of three beautiful grandkids himself, he worked hard to build a family-oriented practice that LS Family Dental is today; now we have the privilege of treating second and third-generation patients while also welcoming new faces.

Dr. Eidelman is passionate especially about esthetic dentistry, but also specializes in restorative and preventative dentistry, offering personalized care tailored to your needs.

Outside of work, Dr. Eidelman enjoys spending time with his grandchildren and playing golf.

With state-of-the-art equipment and a team with extensive experience, you can be confident you’re receiving the highest quality treatment for your smile.
