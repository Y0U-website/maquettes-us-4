Caring for Your Dental Implants After Treatment

Most patients recover from implant surgery faster than they expect. Following a few simple guidelines after each stage of treatment will keep your results on track and support healthy healing.

After Your Procedure

Stick to soft foods for the first few days after implant placement. Avoid smoking or using tobacco during the healing period. Take any prescribed medications exactly as directed. Do not poke or prod the surgical area with your tongue or fingers. Use a cold pack in the first 24 hours to reduce swelling if needed.

Daily Preventive Care

Brush your implant crown twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush. Floss around the implant carefully—a water flosser works especially well. Avoid biting down on hard items like ice or hard candy. Schedule your regular checkups at LS Family Dental every six months. Let us know right away if you notice any pain, loosening, or unusual sensitivity.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Peabody, MA

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Over time, the bone fuses with the post in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation. A custom-made crown is then attached on top to restore the visible tooth. The result is a replacement that looks, feels, and functions almost identically to your natural tooth.
