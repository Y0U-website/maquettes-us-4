Henry Huang, DDS and Fei Xu, DDS

In 2012, Dr. Henry and Dr. Fei decided to move to the Capital District to settle down and start a family. Through their joint effort, they were able to open Bethlehem Town Family Dental in October 2015 and set their own meticulous standard in dentistry.

During college, Dr. Henry worked for 4-H youth program via Cooperative Extension as a tutor, site leader, and summer camp counselor. He also worked at a popular cafe in Cornell University’s college town. Through these experiences, he learned the importance of community responsibility, customer service, and management.
