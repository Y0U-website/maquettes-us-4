What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment that uses clear, customized aligners to straighten teeth. Unlike conventional braces, these plastic aligners are virtually invisible and fit snugly over your teeth, making them a discreet option for improving your smile. Invisalign aligners are removable, allowing you the chance to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain an oral hygiene routine with ease.

Who is a good candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign treatment works well for both teens and adults who have mild to moderate orthodontic issues. These may include crooked teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites, or crossbites. Ds. How long does it take to straighten teeth with Invisalign in Boise, Idaho?

The treatment duration differs depending on a patient’s needs. However, most Invisalign patients complete their treatment in 12-18 months. Mild cases often see results in as little as six months, while more complex cases may take longer. How do invisible dental aligners work?

Invisalign aligners gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment using a series of custom-designed trays. Each set of aligners is worn for about 1-2 weeks and exerts gentle pressure to guide your teeth into the desired position. Regular check-ups at Northwest Dental Center will ensure that your progress is on track.

Can I eat and drink with Invisalign?

You can remove your Invisalign aligners during meals, allowing you to enjoy all of your favorite foods without any restrictions. Unlike traditional metal braces, there are no wires or brackets to worry about when eating. After meals, be sure to brush your teeth and rinse off the aligners before putting them back in.

How do I care for my Invisalign aligners?

Keeping your aligners clean is simple. Brush them each day, gently with a soft toothbrush, and rinse them using lukewarm water daily. Avoid using very hot water to rinse, as it can warp the plastic material. Also, remember to store your aligners in their protective case whenever you are not wearing them to prevent loss or damage.

Does Invisalign cost more than traditional braces?

The cost of Invisalign can differ based on the complexity of your orthodontic case. On average, Invisalign is comparable in price to traditional braces.
