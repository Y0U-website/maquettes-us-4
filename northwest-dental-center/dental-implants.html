What are the stages of implant treatment?

  • Placement. This may require that a damaged or diseased tooth be removed, and then the implant itself is placed into the jaw bone. It can be placed on the day of tooth removal or within three months from loss or extraction.
  • Abutment. The abutment is the portion of the dental implant that is above the gumline and is used as the foundation for a dental restoration such as a crown, bridge, or denture.
  • Restoration. Depending on the repair performed, the crown, bridge, or denture is then cemented or screwed onto the implant. In some cases the abutment and restoration are fabricated together saving time and expense.
