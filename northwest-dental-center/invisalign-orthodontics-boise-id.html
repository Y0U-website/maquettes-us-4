What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an awesome teeth-straightening alternative to traditional braces! Instead of metal brackets and wires, Invisalign uses nearly invisible, custom-made aligners that gently shift your teeth into place. Think of it as the clear, comfortable, modern way to get your dream smile.

How long does Invisalign take?

This is a great and common question our team at Northwest Dental Center hears! Treatment time varies depending on your unique smile situation, but most of our patients wear Invisalign orthodontics for twelve to eighteen months. Don’t worry, though! Our providers will give you a timeline specific to your needs so you know what to expect.

Does Invisalign hurt?

Not at all! Well, maybe a little at the start, but your teeth are moving. This means progress! You might feel some mild pressure or discomfort for the first few days of wearing a new aligner, but this indicates they are working their magic.

Can I still eat my favorite foods?

Absolutely! That’s why patients love Invisalign. With this orthodontic treatment, you can remove the aligners while you eat. This means no food restrictions. Enjoy those crunchy chips, sticky caramel, or chewy bagels guilt-free. Just make sure to brush before putting your aligners back in!

How often do I need to wear the aligners?

For the best results, you must wear your aligners for twenty to twenty-two hours a day. Only take them out to eat, drink (anything besides water), brush, and floss. Stick to this routine, and your smile will thank you!

Can I still play sports or play a musical instrument?

Absolutely! Invisalign is perfect for people with active hobbies. Since they’re removable, you can safely take them out when playing contact sports or blowing into a trumpet. Plus, there is no risk of cutting your mouth on metal braces!

How do I take care of my aligners?

Taking care of your aligners during treatment is super easy. All you need to do is rinse them regularly and brush them gently with a clear, mild soap. Avoid toothpaste, as it can scratch the aligners. Keep them clean, and they’ll keep your teeth in tip-top shape.
