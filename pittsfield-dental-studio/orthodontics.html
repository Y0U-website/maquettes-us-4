What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry that specializes in correcting teeth and jaw alignments. Orthodontic treatments can help manage a range of dental issues, such as crooked or crowded teeth, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and gaps between teeth. Correcting these issues can improve oral health and improve your smile&#x27;s appearance.

Invisalign in Ann Arbor, MI

Invisalign is an innovative orthodontic treatment that uses a series of clear, removable aligners to move your teeth into their proper position slowly. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible and allow you to straighten your teeth without drawing attention to your treatment. They are also removable, which means you can eat, brush, and floss normally during treatment.

Nightguards for TMJ Disorder

Nightguards are a type of mouthguard specifically designed to be worn during sleep to protect against the effects of bruxism or teeth grinding, which can lead to tooth damage and jaw disorders.

At Pittsfield Dental Studio, patients experiencing symptoms of bruxism can find relief with custom-fitted nightguards. Regular use of a nightguard can minimize the stress on teeth and the temporomandibular joint (TMJ), helping to prevent premature wear of teeth and decrease the possibility of developing TMJ disorders.
