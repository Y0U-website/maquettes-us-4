Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a candidate for dental implants in Gainesville?

You would be a candidate for dental implants in Gainesville if you have healthy gums, missing teeth, and adequate jawbone. Even if bone loss has occurred, procedures such as bone grafting would qualify your mouth as a candidate for implants. A visit with the Georgia Smile Team would help determine the next step.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are permanent and will survive 15–20 years or more if proper oral hygiene is employed. Their lifetime can be extended further with regular oral hygiene and dental examinations. The crowned tooth on the implant can be replaced after 10–15 years due to ongoing use and wear and tear.

What is the recovery time after getting dental implants?

Your initial healing from dental implant surgery will usually be 1 to 2 weeks. However, full integration of the implant with the jawbone, known as osseointegration, will occur between 3 and 6 months. Your healing would depend on your own needs and the intricacy of the procedure.

Are dental implants better than dentures?

Dental implants provide a more secure and natural-appearing option than dentures. They are implanted directly into the jawbone, eliminating slipping and supporting bone health. Although implants can be more expensive in the beginning, they are potentially more cost-effective in the long run as they are more durable and require less maintenance.
