Dr. Matt Dunford, DMD

General Dentist

Dr. Matt Dunford went to South Gwinnett High School in Snellville, GA. He earned an undergraduate degree in Medical Biology from the University of Utah and received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree (DMD) from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. Dr. Dunford moved back home to Georgia in 2001 and started practicing dentistry in Dawsonville. In 2014 Dr. Dunford began practicing in Gainesville, GA, where he serves the community with pride and clinical excellence.

Dr. Ala Yassin, DDS, MSD

PERIODONTIST

Dr. Yassin is a board-certified Periodontist trained at the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine, with 8 years of experience in general dentistry! Dr. Yassin is passionate about providing Georgia Smile Team patients with optimum care for all of their periodontal dental needs. With emphasis on oral health, the relationship between periodontal disease and overall health, and implant dentistry, she is kind, patient and deeply devoted to excellence in patient care!

Dr. Jashneek Cheema, DDS

General Dentist

With a diverse dental background that began with a degree from one of India’s top dental schools and further training in the U.S., Dr. Cheema brings a well-rounded approach to patient care. Known for a positive attitude, attention to detail, and a collaborative spirit.

Dr. Cheema strives to make every patient feel comfortable and cared for. Focusing on restorative treatments and periodontics, Dr. Cheema ensures personalized care tailored to individual needs. With a commitment to staying current on the latest techniques and innovations, patients can trust they’re receiving top-quality treatment. She believes in open communication, patient education, and preventive care to help everyone achieve their healthiest smile. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Cheema is a nature lover who enjoys biking, hiking, and experimenting with new recipes in her free time.
