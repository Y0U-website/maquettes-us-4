Financial Information & Membership Options

Caring for Your Smile, Your Way—With or Without Insurance

We understand that dental insurance can be confusing and limiting—and we’re here to make it as easy as possible. While Lake Pointe Dental is not in-network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna, Metlife, Health Choice, or Guardian, that doesn’t mean we can’t still work with your insurance. In fact, we do it every day.

  • Verify your benefits to help you understand your coverage.
  • Submit your claims on your behalf to make things hassle-free.
  • Help you maximize your reimbursement as much as possible.

Why More Patients Are Choosing Membership Plans

An Easier, More Affordable Alternative to Traditional Dental Insurance

If you don’t have dental insurance—or you’re frustrated with the limitations that come along with it—our Lake Pointe Dental Smile Savings Plan might be a better fit. It’s designed to be simple, transparent, and tailored to what you actually need.

For many patients, our Smile Savings Plan offers more value than traditional insurance, especially for families or individuals who want predictable costs and access to quality care without the fine print.
