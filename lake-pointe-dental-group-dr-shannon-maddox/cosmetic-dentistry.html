Veneers

Unveil your new, improved smile.

Dental veneers can totally transform an imperfect smile. We use porcelain veneers, which are considered one of the sturdiest and longest-lasting solutions. These thin porcelain shells are meticulously crafted to create your new, unique smile. Veneers are a great option to mask imperfections such as gaps, chips, misalignment, misshapen teeth, and discoloration. Porcelain veneers are inherently stain resistant, and you can choose a shade that matches your other natural teeth or go a few shades whiter for a brighter smile.

Crown Lengthening and Gum Contouring

Improve your look

Crown lengthening and gum contouring are treatments that gently remove excess gum tissue and bone when necessary. It helps reveal more of your natural tooth structure to create a proportionate smile, as well as ensure enough tooth structure for restorative treatments like crowns. At Lake Pointe Dental Group, we work with a periodontist to ensure our patients get the best possible results. Crown lengthening and gum contouring can make a big difference to a “gummy” smile. You can be assured that your comfort is our top priority and that you will receive exceptional care throughout the process.

Crowns

Strength and support for damaged teeth

We can restore strength and support to a damaged tooth with a custom-made dental crown. We offer full-ceramic options such as lithium disilicate, zirconia, and feldspathic porcelain. These are all durable materials that we can color-match for a natural look. At Lake Pointe Dental Group, we also occasionally use porcelain fused to metal (PFM) if the patient’s needs fit that option. If you need a dental crown, Dr. Maddox will discuss all appropriate available options with you so you can decide what’s best for you.
