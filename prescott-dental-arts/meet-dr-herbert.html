Meet Dr. David Herbert

Dr. Herbert provides high-quality care for both routine needs and complex treatments. He uses the latest technology and techniques.

  • Smile design with veneers and cosmetic bonding
  • Full mouth rehabilitation to restore function and appearance
  • Digital dentistry with same-day crowns and advanced imaging
  • Laser therapy for soft tissue treatments
  • TMJ relief for jaw pain and bite problems
  • Oral sleep appliance therapy for mild to moderate sleep apnea
  • Tooth extractions and molar root canals without referrals

Gentle Approach and Patient First Mindset

Dr. Herbert is known for his skills and for how he treats people. He listens, explains, and makes sure patients are comfortable every step of the way. Children, teens, adults, and seniors love his calm demeanor and personalized care.

Local Roots

Dr. Herbert and his family have been proud residents of Prescott, Arizona , since 2014. He is deeply committed to the local community, understanding the importance of a safe, friendly, and respectful practice for patients of all ages. As a parent and a professional, he values these qualities and strives to incorporate them into his practice. His strong ties to the local community should make you feel right at home.

Giving Back

Dr. Herbert believes in using his skills to give back outside of his daily work. Ask how you or a loved one can benefit from charity-based dentistry, or follow him to the next Arizona Mission of Mercy event. These big free dental clinics help hundreds of people in a few days.

Dr. Herbert’s spirit of service is a reflection of his values. He firmly believes that everyone, regardless of their background, deserves quality care. His commitment to providing this level of care makes you feel valued and respected as a patient.

Complete Dental Care Near You

Patients in Yavapai County and surrounding areas choose Dr. Herbert for all their oral health care needs in one place. He offers various services, from basic cleanings and exams to cosmetic upgrades and complete restorations. His care is efficient, effective, and long-term. You’ll need fewer outside referrals with our new equipment and committed team. This means a smoother and more convenient experience for you.

Experience the Difference

When you visit Dr. Herbert, you’re not just another name on a chart but part of the dental family. His goal is to make each visit easy and stress-free. He wants to provide care you can trust for years.

Dr. David Herbert offers skill, kindness, and modern care all in one place. He can help you smile with confidence
