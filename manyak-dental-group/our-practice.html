Dr. Tanya Manyak

Personalized Comfort Menu

As you settle in, we&#x27;ll offer a personalized comfort menu featuring wired earbuds, soft earplugs, soothing essential oils, premium lip balm, an ergonomic jaw rest, a cozy blanket, a warm scented face towel, or even a visit from our comfort dog, Bailey. We&#x27;ve carefully curated this menu to help you relax, and to transform your dental experience.

Comprehensive Exam

Our comprehensive exam goes beyond just checking your teeth. We assess your overall oral health, including gum health, bite alignment, and jaw function. We use advanced imaging technology to create a detailed view of your oral structures. This supports conversations about your diagnosis to ensure that you always understand the WHY behind our recommendations.

Oral Cancer Screening

As part of your comprehensive exam, we perform an enhanced oral cancer assessment using the VELscope system to ensure that your smile is healthy from the inside out. This proactive step allows us to detect even the smallest abnormalities early, giving us the opportunity to address potential concerns before they become serious.

Cleaning

Our gentle yet thorough cleaning removes plaque and tartar, leaving your teeth feeling fresh and polished. We use this time to educate you about proper oral hygiene techniques tailored to your specific needs.

Next Steps

We conclude by discussing our findings and collaboratively creating a personalized treatment plan. Whether it&#x27;s preventive care or more complex procedures, we ensure you understand all your options and feel confident in your dental health journey.
