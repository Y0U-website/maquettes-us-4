Comprehensive Oral Surgery & Implant Solutions

At Manyak Dental Group, we pride ourselves on offering complete dental implant solutions under one roof. From your initial consultation to the final restoration, every step is handled in-house by our experienced team. Whether you require a single tooth replacement or full-arch restoration, dental implants fully restore the function and appearance of your smile. We&#x27;re committed to providing comfortable, effective treatments that not only restore your smile but also prioritize your overall oral health and well-being.

Cavity Fillings

Laser technology makes it possible to treat early cavities without the need for traditional drilling or numbing. Instead, we gently remove decay with pinpoint accuracy for a faster, quieter, and more comfortable experience for both kids and adults.

Cold Sore & Herpetic Lesion Treatment

Laser therapy is a fast, effective way to treat cold sores and herpetic lesions. By targeting the area with gentle laser energy, we can relieve pain, speed up healing, and even stop an outbreak if caught early. Treatment is quick, requires no anesthesia, and can reduce the frequency and severity of future flare-ups. For best results, schedule a visit as soon as you feel tingling or burning and our team will take it from there.

Gum Contouring and Gingivectomy

Whether you&#x27;re looking to reshape uneven gums for a more balanced smile or need to remove inflamed gum tissue due to gum disease, laser treatment offers a precise, low-discomfort option. There&#x27;s usually little to no bleeding, and healing time is quicker than with traditional methods.

Frenectomy (Tongue-or Lip-Tie Release)

A laser frenectomy is a simple way to release a tight or restricted tongue or lip tie. It&#x27;s a quick procedure with minimal discomfort, and because there&#x27;s no cutting or stitches, healing tends to be much easier—especially for infants and young children.

Tooth Prep for Crowns or Restorations

Preparing a tooth for a crown or filling doesn’t always require a drill. In many cases, we can use a laser to shape the tooth with accuracy and less discomfort. It’s a great option for patients who prefer a quieter, more relaxed experience.

Minor Surgical Procedures

Lasers allow us to perform minor oral surgeries with fewer stitches, minimal swelling, and an overall easier recovery experience. For example, if you have an unusual bump or sore in your mouth, we can use a laser to remove a small tissue sample (biopsy) for further evaluation, usually with little to no bleeding and minimal discomfort.

We also use lasers to uncover teeth that haven’t fully erupted, such as impacted canines or premolars. This is especially helpful during orthodontic treatment, making it easier to guide the tooth into place without the need for traditional surgery.

Long-Lasting

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Unlike other tooth replacement options that may need regular replacement, implants offer a durable, permanent solution. Their longevity makes them a cost-effective choice in the long run, providing value and peace of mind.

Preserves Jawbone

One of the unique benefits of dental implants is their ability to preserve your jawbone. The implant post acts like a natural tooth root, stimulating the bone and preventing the deterioration that often occurs with missing teeth. This helps maintain your facial structure and overall oral health.

The Implant Process

We&#x27;ve designed our oral surgery and dental implant process to ensure you receive personalized, comprehensive care every step of the way. Here&#x27;s what you can expect:

Initial Examination and Diagnosis

Your journey begins with a thorough examination and consultation. We&#x27;ll use advanced imaging technology to assess your oral health and discuss your concerns and goals. This comprehensive evaluation allows us to develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

Treatment Planning

Based on your examination, we’ll create a detailed treatment plan. We&#x27;ll explain all aspects of the proposed procedure, including any preparatory steps, the surgery itself, and the recovery process. We take extra time for you to ask questions and voice any concerns so you feel fully informed and comfortable with the plan.

The Surgical Procedure

On the day of your procedure, our team will ensure you&#x27;re comfortable and relaxed. We use the latest surgical techniques and technology to minimize discomfort and promote faster healing. For dental implants, we place the implant to provide a strong foundation for your new tooth. The placement takes up to an hour, but it takes a few months for the implant to merge with your natural jawbone.

Custom Restoration & Aftercare

As your implant integrates with your natural jawbone, a process that typically takes a few months, we’ll schedule check-ins to monitor your progress. Once the implant has fully fused, we’ll design and place your custom restoration to restore the look, feel, and function of your natural tooth. We&#x27;ll provide detailed aftercare instructions on how to properly maintain your new restoration.

FAQs

Still have questions? Our team is here to help you.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The implant itself, which is integrated into the jawbone, can last several decades. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear and tear. Regular dental check-ups and good oral hygiene are essential for maintaining the longevity of your implants.

How long does it take to recover from oral surgery?

Recovery time varies depending on the specific procedure and individual factors. Most patients can return to normal activities within a few days to a week after minor oral surgeries. For more complex procedures or dental implant placements, full recovery may take several weeks to months. We will provide a detailed timeline and recovery instructions specific to your procedure.

Are dental implants right for everyone?

While dental implants are an excellent solution for many patients, they&#x27;re not suitable for everyone. Factors such as bone density, overall health, and lifestyle habits can affect candidacy. During your consultation, our experienced team will assess these factors and discuss whether implants are the best option for you. If implants aren&#x27;t suitable, we&#x27;ll recommend alternative solutions to restore your smile and oral function.
