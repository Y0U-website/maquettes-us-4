Types of Sedation

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

Nitrous oxide is a mild sedative that helps children relax during treatment. Children remain awake and can respond to instructions throughout the procedure.

Oral Sedation

Oral sedation helps children feel calm and relaxed before treatment begins. It may be recommended for children with moderate dental anxiety or for longer appointments.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is sedation dentistry safe for kids?

Yes. When performed by a trained pediatric dental team, sedation is held to strict safety standards. Your child’s health history and weight are reviewed before any sedation is ever recommended.

2. Will my child be asleep?

Not necessarily, most kids remain awake and simply feel calm and relaxed. Only deeper sedation options involve sleep, and those are used only when truly needed.

3. At what age can a child receive sedation?

There’s no single cutoff age; eligibility is based on your child’s weight, health history, and the procedure being done.

4. How long does sedation last?

Nitrous oxide wears off within minutes after the mask is removed, while oral sedation can leave a child drowsy for a few hours. We’ll give you a clear timeline before your appointment.

5. Can sedation help with dental anxiety?

Absolutely, reducing fear is one of the main reasons we offer sedation for kids. It helps children get through treatment without distress, which also makes future visits much easier.

6. Is laughing gas safe for children?

Yes. Nitrous oxide is one of the most studied and widely used sedation options in pediatric dentistry. It’s fast-acting, easy to control, and leaves the body quickly once removed.

7. Does insurance cover sedation dentistry?

Coverage depends on your specific plan and the reason sedation is needed. Our front desk team is happy to review your benefits and explain any out-of-pocket costs before your visit.
