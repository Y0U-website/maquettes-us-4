Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which dental insurance plans do you accept?

We accept many major PPO dental insurance plans and work with a wide range of providers. Contact our office with your insurance details, and our team will gladly help confirm your coverage.

2. How can I verify my child’s insurance coverage before the visit?

Simply call our office or submit your insurance information through our appointment form. We’ll review your benefits before the appointment and explain what services may be covered.

3. Can insurance be used for fillings, crowns, or extractions?

Yes, many plans help cover restorative treatments such as fillings, crowns, pulp therapy, and extractions. Our team checks your benefits in advance so you understand your expected costs.

4. Will I know the cost before treatment starts?

Yes. We always provide a clear treatment estimate before starting care, including what insurance may pay and any remaining balance, so there are no surprises.

5. Do you help file insurance claims?

Absolutely. Our office team handles claim submissions and paperwork to make the process simple and stress-free for parents.
