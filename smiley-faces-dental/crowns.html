2. Will the crown fall off or come loose?

Very rarely! We cement them securely, and both stainless steel and zirconia crowns are made to stay put until the baby tooth naturally falls out (usually years later). We double-check the fit so it feels perfect.

3. Can my child eat normally right after getting a crown?

Yes, in most cases! Once the numbing wears off (1–2 hours), they can eat soft foods right away. We just say skip super-hard or sticky stuff for the first day to let everything settle.

4. Do crowns affect how permanent teeth come in?

No, they actually help! Crowns hold the right space in the jaw so adult teeth have room to grow straight. Removing a baby tooth too early can cause crowding or crooked teeth later, so crowns help prevent that.

5. Are zirconia crowns better than silver ones for front teeth?

Zirconia crowns look just like real teeth, no metal shine or line, so front teeth stay natural and kids smile confidently without shyness. For back teeth, stainless steel crowns are best: super strong for chewing, and since they’re hidden, the silver color doesn’t matter.

6. What happens when the baby tooth with the crown falls out?

Nothing special! The crown comes out with the baby tooth naturally. We check it at regular visits to make sure everything’s on track for the permanent tooth coming in healthy and straight.
