Meet Dr. Andy Engel

28 years. One practice. One dentist who knows this town.

Dr. Andrew W. Engel grew up in Boise, Idaho before his family relocated to Oregon in 1988. An avid athlete from an early age, he had his sights set on Olympic mogul skiing at Idaho’s Bogus Basin and Sun Valley — he decided dentistry was the safer option. The mountains’ loss, his patients’ gain.

Dr. Engel moved to Bend in 1998, helped establish Century Dental Group, then founded Living Dental Health to fulfill a lifelong goal of building something of his own. The name says it all — helping patients address oral health issues so they can live healthy, full, active lives in Central Oregon.

His approach to patient care is part detective, part craftsman. He listens first, then pulls from a tool belt that most dentists simply don’t have. His dream patient: a good communicator who wants to be involved in their own care — someone he can get to know by name and wave to around town.

When he’s not in the office, you might find him up at Mt. Bachelor skiing with friends, or with Bend’s local car community — cars have been a lifelong passion. His greatest joy is family — wife Francie, son Sean, daughters Ally and Rhone, and Murphy the Corgi.
