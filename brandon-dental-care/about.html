Staff

Meet the Team

Dr. Toby Brandon

DMD • Owner

Dr. Toby Brandon, a Belmont native, was born and raised in Mississippi. He received his bachelor’s degree from Mississippi State University followed by his DMD from the University of Mississippi School of Dentistry in 2019. In his spare time he enjoys spending time with his wife Emily and his three children. He also is an avid outdoorsman, golfer and Mississippi State fan.

Jennifer Gaston, RDH

Hygienist

Jennifer has been a valued member of our dental team since 2002. She is a 1997 graduate of the Northeast Mississippi School of Dental Hygiene, she is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles. Jennifer enjoys brightening smiles, building lasting relationships with her patients, and providing gentle, compassionate care. She is committed to making every visit comfortable while helping patients maintain excellent oral health for years to come.

Mandy Ellis

Financial Coordinator

As the Treatment and Financial Coordinator , I play a key role in ensuring every patient feels informed, confident and supported throughout their dental journey. Amy Sheffield

Office Manager

As the first smiling face you’ll see when you arrive, Amy is passionate about creating a warm, welcoming environment and helping every patient feel comfortable from the moment they walk through the door.

Amy White, RDH

hygienist

A Saltillo native, Amy was raised on a farm rooted in over 80 years of family tradition, shaping her strong work ethic and commitment to caring for others. Outside of work, she enjoys the outdoors, boating and creating special memories with her family.
