Our Team

Dr. Nakhaei attended Towson State University and graduated in 1996. She obtained her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Howard University College of Dentistry in 2001. Following graduation from dental school, Dr. Nakhaei completed a General Practice Residency (GPR) program at Saint Elizabeths Hospital in Washington DC for one year. This exposure allowed her to treat patients with different medical conditions and disabilities by working alongside physician colleagues in the operating rooms and emergency department.

Dr. Nakhaei has over 20 years of experience in private practice. She serves patients in the Baltimore area. She is known by many as a personable and caring professional who has a keen eye for detail and a gentle touch. She takes the fear out of dental appointments.

Dr. Nakhaei continues to pursue quality continuing education courses to stay on top of the most recent technology and advancements in restorative, cosmetic, and implant dentistry. Her practice provides top-tier general dentistry in multiple areas such as preventative care, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry.

Dr. Nakhaei is a member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Maryland State Dental Association, and National Dental Association. In addition, she volunteers her time and services to Mission of Mercy and Maryland Foundation of Dentistry Donated Dental Services which provides free dental care for individuals in need.

When Dr. Nakhaei is not in the office, she spends quality time with her husband and daughter. She enjoys watching movies with her family. She loves to dance like nobody’s watching. Also, she does calligraphy and sewing when she gets the chance.

Dr. Jude Restivo - Dentist

Dr. Restivo obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland in 1960. He served as a Captain in Dental Corps in the United States Army. Dr. Restivo has more than 60 years of experience as a dentist.

Dr. Restivo went to University of Maryland, College Park and graduated in 1956. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 1960. He served as a Captain in the Dental Corps in the United States Army and was honorably discharged in 1962.

Dr. Restivo opened this office in 1962 and has served in the Baltimore area for over 60 years!

Dr. Restivo is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. He participated in the Maryland Foundation of Dentistry Donated Dental Services.

Currently, Dr. Restivo plays bluegrass music with the Satyr Hill Band. He plays the fiddle and sings. Also, Dr. Restivo plays the violin with the Contemporary Music Ensemble at Saint Michael The ArchAngel Catholic Church. He loves to spend time with his family and dental staff that work alongside him. In addition, he loves to tell jokes, you are more than welcome to ask him for one!
