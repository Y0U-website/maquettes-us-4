Secure Your Dental Implants: Tips…

Dental implants are regarded as one of the most in-demand and effective procedure for teeth restoration nowadays. In the U.S alone, there are around 3 million people who already have implants, and this figure is estimated to increase by 500,000 each year according to the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. Some celebrities like Taylor Hicks, Ed Helms, and Christie Brinkley even opted for an implant procedure to make sure their smile will always stay perfect.

Although dental implants are made from durable materials such as titanium for the post and porcelain for the prosthesis, it can still be subjected to damage. It has been told that implants would not be affected by decay like the natural teeth, but this does not mean that proper hygiene practices should be stopped.

  • Selecting a toothbrush is vital. The practice is not only beneficial for the implants but also to the healthy, natural teeth. It is best to choose a soft and thin bristle type of brush whether manual or electric to make sure that all areas of the teeth are cleaned adequately without causing any damage to its surface. Consider using an interdental brush to cover the areas around the prosthesis and post. Observe proper brushing and flossing regularly to keep the implants, teeth, and other oral structures healthy.
  • Use a Fluoridated toothpaste. While dental implants cannot get any cavities, the natural teeth and gums can still be vulnerable to this problem. Using a fluoridated toothpaste and mouthwash ensures that the structures are well protected. Fluoride acts as a barrier to get rid of harmful substances that can damage any supporting structures.
  • Watch out for certain foods and beverages. Make sure to limit the intake of sugary or acidic foods that can cause damage to the teeth or the other structures in the mouth. While porcelain prostheses are more resistant to staining, the natural teeth, however, are not. Excessive intake of foods or beverages with intense colors can discolor the teeth, leaving an unsightly and uneven effect on ones smile. Reduce or entirely avoid eating sticky foods for they are harder to be removed once they are caught in between teeth.
  • Do not use the implant-supported prosthesis as tools. Implants may be designed to be durable, but that does not mean that it can be used to open bottles or plastic packets. The prosthesis placed may chip if not taken care of properly resulting in a damaged dental implant.
