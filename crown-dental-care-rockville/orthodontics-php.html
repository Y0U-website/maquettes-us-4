Am I a Good Candidate for Orthodontics?

If you are dissatisfied with how straight your teeth are and what a beautiful, straight smile, orthodontics may be the right option for you. Orthodontic services are used to correct badly aligned or crooked teeth, a crowded mouth or a poor dental bite.

You will need to have your full set of adult teeth erupted, which for most people, is after the age of 12. If you are well into adulthood and are considering getting orthodontics, don’t hesitate to speak to us as it is never too late.
