All-on-X: A Smarter Full-Arch Solution

Strong, stable, and designed to restore your full smile with fewer implants

If you’ve lost most or all of your teeth on the upper or lower arch, the All-on-X system offers a complete, fixed solution. Instead of using an implant for every missing tooth, this method supports a full arch of teeth using just four to six implants placed at strategic angles for maximum strength.

At Crown Dental Care in Rockville, we use this advanced approach to help patients achieve a full smile faster often with fewer procedures and less healing time.

Why All-on-X Works So Well

This treatment is ideal for patients dealing with significant tooth loss, loose dentures, or failing dental work. Every case is planned with digital imaging to ensure precise placement and long-term results.

  • Replaces an entire row of teeth with just 4 to 6 implants
  • May allow for same-day placement of a temporary smile
  • Avoids the need for bone grafting in many cases
  • Restores bite strength and improves facial structure
  • Easier maintenance compared to removable dentures

How the All-on-X Process Works at Crown Dental Care

Step 1: Consultation & 3D Imaging

Your journey starts with a detailed exam and advanced imaging. We’ll scan your mouth using CBCT technology to check your bone health and plan your treatment with accuracy.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Using guided surgical techniques and digital planning, our dentist places 4 to 6 implants to support your new full arch. Sedation is available for a comfortable, stress-free experience.

Step 3: Healing Phase

Over the next few months, your implants will fuse with your jawbone. This creates the strong foundation needed to support your final smile.

Step 4: Final Smile Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach a natural-looking prosthetic custom designed to fit your face, function, and goals.

Am I a good candidate for implants?

Most adults with good general health, adequate jawbone density, and healthy gums qualify for dental implants Rockville MD. Patients with bone loss may require grafting first. Dr. Nguyen evaluates every case individually and gives you an honest recommendation at your consultation.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants Rockville MD patients receive at Crown Dental Care can last decades or a lifetime with proper care. The implant post fuses with the jawbone over time, creating a stable permanent anchor. The crown or restoration on top typically lasts 10 to 20 years with good oral hygiene.

How long does the implant process take?

The full process for dental implants Rockville MD at Crown Dental Care typically takes three to six months, including the healing period before the final crown is attached. Cases requiring bone grafting may take longer. Dr. Nguyen provides a personalized timeline at your consultation.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Placement of dental implants Rockville MD is performed under local anesthesia, so you feel pressure but not pain during the procedure. Most patients find recovery manageable with over-the-counter relief. Dr. Nguyen and the team ensure you are comfortable at every stage.
