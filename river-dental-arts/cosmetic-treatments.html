Cosmetic Treatment

0 Cosmetic Treatment Cosmetic dental treatment is often indicated due to a patient’s desire to improve their smile, in a subtle or even more dramatic way. Other times, restorative needs such as dental breakdown, genetic anomalies, or trauma require the need to replace or esthetically restore damaged teeth.

Whatever the cause, Dr. Emily Zeisler and her skilled team will listen carefully to your desires and concerns to create a cosmetic solution that will restore and enhance your smile.

Dental Bonding

Bonding is an alternative to veneers and can be used as a restorative procedure for teeth that are chipped, cracked, discolored or misaligned. The bonding procedure can often be completed in a single office visit, and can improve the appearance of a tooth significantly. However, since the plastic resin used is not as strong as your natural tooth enamel, it is more likely to stain, chip or break than natural teeth. Bonding typically lasts three to five years before need of repair.

Veneers

Veneers are a cosmetic dental restoration that can transform misshapen, discolored, or misaligned teeth. Veneers are the best option when the teeth are in good condition, but in need of cosmetic improvement. Veneers are designed to look just like natural teeth and are a conservative cosmetic treatment option that require minimal changes to tooth structure.

Whitening

If you are seeking to brighten your smile, our custom, easy-to-use whitening trays are an excellent option. These clear whitening trays are well-liked by many of our patients because they are a comfortable, easy way to improve your smile. If you are finishing orthodontics, getting ready for a big life event, or planning some restorative treatment, we can make custom trays in the office on the same day as your visit. We provide a prescription whitening gel that you’ll be able to put into the trays at home and use yourself.
