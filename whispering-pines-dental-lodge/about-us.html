Meet Dr. Petra Lee, DMD FAGD

About Us

Meet Dr. Petra Lee, a highly skilled and accomplished dentist dedicated to providing exceptional care through the perfect blend of art and science. She is passionate about helping those who experience dental anxiety and fear. She understands the courage it takes to face that fear and come to their first appointment. She works to build trust and create a calm, welcoming environment where patients can feel safe, supported and empowered.

“I want to encourage everyone to make that first step and not view themselves as cowards any longer. Change of thoughts change reality.”

Dr. Lee earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2006 from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine, after completing her Bachelor of Science in Biology, with honors, from Armstrong University in Savannah, GA.

Her passion for biological sciences, coupled with her unwavering commitment to the field of dentistry, has propelled Dr. Lee to the forefront of her profession. As a proud member of the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), she actively supports their mission to deliver the highest quality dental care. In recognition of her dedication and expertise, Dr. Lee received the prestigious AGD Fellowship Award in 2014, an honor awarded to only those dentists who consistently demonstrate excellence and stay at the cutting edge of their field.

Dr. Lee’s pursuit of excellence is reflected in her extensive education and training. She completed over 500 hours of advanced dental education, focusing on IV Sedation, Crown and Bridge, Occlusion, Implant restorations, Full Mouth Reconstruction, and Cosmetic Dentistry.

This expertise allows her to provide comprehensive, personalized dental care tailored to each patient’s needs.

At the heart of Dr. Lee’s practice is a deep commitment to transforming smiles and improving oral health. Whether it’s a routine check-up or a more complex procedure, she strives to make every dental visit an experience that combines the art and precision of dentistry.

Choose Dr. Petra Lee for an exceptional dental experience where personalized care and the highest standards of treatment meet. Your smile is in expert hands.
