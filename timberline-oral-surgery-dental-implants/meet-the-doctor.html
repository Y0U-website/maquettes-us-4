Dr. Jessica Lee, DDS

Dr. Jessica Lee grew up in the beautiful city of Honolulu, Hawaii, and now calls Spokane, Washington home, where she enjoys the stunning scenery and the warmth of the local community. She knew from an early age that she wanted to be the first dentist in her family, and her passion for providing exceptional care led her to pursue a career in oral and maxillofacial surgery.

Dr. Lee earned both her undergraduate and dental degrees from the University of Washington, and she is board-certified by the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (ABOMS) . She is also an active member of professional associations including the American Dental Association (ADA), Washington State Dental Association (WSDA), and the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS).

For Dr. Lee, the foundation of any doctor-patient relationship is trust, reliability, and communication . She understands that patients often come in feeling apprehensive about more invasive procedures, and she strives to create a comfortable environment where they feel informed, supported, and confident in their care.

What Dr. Lee finds most rewarding about her profession is the opportunity to provide specialized surgical care while building meaningful relationships with her patients. Previously, she has volunteered by giving presentations to church youth groups, sharing her knowledge and inspiring the next generation.

With a commitment to excellence and patient-centered care, Dr. Jessica Lee combines her surgical expertise with genuine compassion to ensure every patient has a safe, positive experience.
