A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone where the natural tooth root once was. Over time, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a secure foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. Because they restore both form and function, dental implants are widely considered the gold standard in tooth replacement.

At Timberline Oral Surgery & Dental Implants , our Spokane oral surgeons specialize in advanced implant procedures, helping patients regain confidence, comfort, and long-term oral health. Whether you need to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth, we offer customized implant solutions designed to fit your needs.

Titanium implants are highly biocompatible and bond naturally with surrounding bone—a process called osseointegration. This is what makes dental implants so durable and long-lasting. In fact, studies show that more than 93 % of dental implant procedures are successful, making them one of the most reliable options for tooth replacement available today.

Small posts that protrude through the gums are then attached to the implant. These posts provide stable anchors for artificial replacement teeth.

Is getting dental implants painful? Most patients are surprised at how comfortable the dental implant process can be. At our Spokane office, we use advanced technology, precise planning, and sedation options to ensure a smooth procedure. While you may experience some mild soreness during healing, pain is typically well-managed with prescribed medication and proper aftercare.

Why choose dental implants over other tooth replacement options? Dental implants offer unique benefits that dentures and bridges cannot match. Because implants are placed into the jawbone, they function like natural tooth roots, helping preserve bone health and facial structure.
