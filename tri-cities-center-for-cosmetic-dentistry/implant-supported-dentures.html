FAQs

What are implant-supported dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are removable dentures that attach securely to dental implants placed in your jawbone, providing superior stability compared to traditional dentures. The implants serve as anchors that prevent the dentures from slipping or moving during eating and speaking. This innovative solution combines the comprehensive tooth replacement of dentures with the stability and bone preservation benefits of dental implants.

What are the disadvantages of implant-supported dentures?

The primary disadvantages include higher initial costs compared to traditional dentures and the need for surgical implant placement, which requires adequate bone density and healing time. Some patients may experience temporary discomfort during the healing process, and the treatment timeline is longer than conventional dentures. However, most patients find that these temporary inconveniences are far outweighed by the long-term benefits of improved function and comfort.

Are implant-supported dentures removable?

Yes, implant-supported dentures are designed to be removable for easy cleaning and maintenance, though they attach securely to implants during wear. You can remove them at night for cleaning and oral hygiene, then easily snap them back into place each morning. This removability allows for thorough cleaning of both the dentures and your mouth while providing secure attachment when in use.

Are implant-supported dentures worth it?

Most patients find that implant-supported dentures provide exceptional value through improved quality of life, better nutrition from expanded food choices, and enhanced confidence in social situations. The initial investment typically pays for itself through reduced need for denture adjustments, adhesives, and related dental problems over time. Studies show that patients report significantly higher satisfaction rates with implant-supported dentures compared to traditional alternatives.

Can I eat my favorite foods with implant-supported dentures?

Yes, implant-supported dentures restore much of your natural chewing ability, allowing you to enjoy most foods that were off-limits with traditional dentures. You can bite into apples, eat corn on the cob, enjoy nuts and seeds, and confidently chew tougher meats without worry about denture movement. While extremely hard or sticky foods may still require some caution, your dietary options expand dramatically compared to conventional dentures.
