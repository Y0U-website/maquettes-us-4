FAQs

What is an implant-supported crown?

An implant-supported crown is a single tooth replacement that consists of a titanium implant surgically placed in the jawbone and topped with a custom porcelain crown. The implant acts as an artificial tooth root, providing stable support for the crown restoration. This creates a permanent solution that looks, feels, and functions exactly like your natural tooth.

Do implant-supported crowns use abutments?

Yes, implant-supported crowns typically use abutments as connecting pieces between the implant and the final crown restoration. The abutment screws into the implant and provides the proper shape and angle for the crown attachment. This component ensures optimal fit and allows for easy maintenance or replacement of the crown if needed in the future.

What is an implant-supported bridge?

An implant-supported bridge is a restoration that replaces multiple adjacent missing teeth using dental implants as anchor points instead of natural teeth. Two or more strategically placed implants support a connected series of crowns that span the gap where teeth are missing. This approach provides superior stability and preserves the health of surrounding natural teeth.

What does a dental implant-supported bridge look like?

A dental implant-supported bridge appears as a seamless row of natural-looking teeth that blend perfectly with your existing smile. The individual crowns are connected and crafted from high-quality porcelain that matches your natural tooth color and translucency. From the outside, it’s virtually impossible to distinguish the bridge from your natural teeth in terms of appearance and function.

Would gums grow over implant-supported bridges?

No, healthy gum tissue will not grow over properly designed implant-supported bridges, as they are positioned at the appropriate level relative to your gum line. The bridges are designed with proper contours that allow for easy cleaning while maintaining healthy gum tissue around the implants. However, maintaining excellent oral hygiene is essential to prevent gum inflammation or recession around the implant sites.
