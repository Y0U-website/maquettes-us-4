SureSmile Orthodontics

Clear aligner orthodontics is a modern approach to straightening teeth that utilizes transparent, custom-fitted aligners

Same Day Crowns

Same-day crowns (CEREC) offer the convenience of having a dental crown designed, fabricated, and placed in a single visit.

Implants

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a strong foundation for replacement teeth.

Tooth Extraction

General and wisdom tooth extraction involves the careful extraction of problematic or impacted teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic dental procedure designed to brighten and enhance the color of teeth.

General & Family Dentistry

Family dentistry provides services that meet the oral health needs of patients of all ages within a family-oriented environment.

Preventative Checkups

Preventative checkups are essential for maintaining oral health, detecting issues, and preventing extensive problems.

Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells applied to the front surface of teeth, enhancing their appearance.

Dentures

Dentures are removable dental appliances that replace missing teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics for improved oral well-being.

Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal therapy involves specialized care for gum diseases aimed at preserving gum and overall oral health.

TMJ Disorders

TMJ disorder involves jaw joint issues, causing pain and difficulties in jaw movement.

Root Canals

Root canals efficiently treat infections or damage within the pulp of a tooth, preserving teeth and alleviating associated pain.
