Our Services Modern Family Friendly Dental Care River Rock Dental offers a full range of general & cosmetic dental treatments at our office to help you achieve your perfect smile.

Clear aligner orthodontics is a modern approach to straightening teeth that utilizes transparent, custom-fitted aligners

Same-day crowns (CEREC) offer the convenience of having a dental crown designed, fabricated, and placed in a single visit.

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a strong foundation for replacement teeth.
