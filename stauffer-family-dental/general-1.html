Oral Hygiene Instructions

oral hygiene insruction

Of course, we all know how to brush our teeth but sometimes there are little things that we are missing that can help maintain our oral health even better! At every cleaning at Stauffer Family Dental in Westerville, OH, we will make any necessary recommendations or give advice on how our patients can do even better with their oral hygiene at home.

Brushing

Flossing

Flossing is recommended once a day, usually in the evening. We recommend using traditional floss versus the floss picks. We recommend C-shaped flossing and wrapping the floss around each tooth. Water piks are a great adjunct to flossing but not a replacement.

Oral Hygiene with Braces

We recommend patients with braces use an electric toothbrush. We like Oral-B or Sonicare. We also recommend using a water-pik. Those brackets can be very hard to clean around and these two tools make it easier for the patient. We still recommend flossing to prevent cavities between the teeth and promote gum health.
