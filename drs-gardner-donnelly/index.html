For over almost 50 years, we have been providing our clients with quality dental services.

We’ve been at our present location for almost 50 years. Between Dr. Gardner and Dr. Donnelly they share more than eighty years of experience.

The practice

They practice general dentistry and offer their patients both quality and compassionate care in almost all of the disciplines in dentistry: Cosmetic, restorative, including implant restorations, oral surgery, full mouth rehabilitation, partial and full dentures We also provide Endodontics (root canal) and periodontal procedures, including soft tissue management.

Our staff, some of whom have been with us for more than twenty years, are very professional as well as congenial.

They’re also very experienced dealing with the many different insurance companies. They make every sincere effort to know our patient’s needs, as do our doctors.

We’re a private practice, not a clinic, and therefore the office and staff take great pride in the reputation they’ve enjoyed over the many years.

The overall design of the office was to offer an energy efficient establishment which includes “passive design”, and comfort for our patients with ample parking for more than thirty-eight cars on the premises.

The fireplace and aquarium add to the pleasant warmth and coziness of the atmosphere. With this ambience we hope to allay the fears and discomfort that is often associated with the dental office.

Our office mission is to provide the best and most comprehensive dentistry available in both a warm and comfortable setting. We also take pride in seeing our patients in a timely fashion. After all, our patients’ time is as important as ours. Some of Julia Gardner’s artwork is also on display.

Lastly, we also have a Dental laboratory on the premises. This adds to the efficacy and our ability to supervise the dental crown and bridgework that we provide for our patients.

Services
