What Is Holistic Dentistry?

Holistic dentistry, also known as biological dentistry, focuses on restoring and maintaining oral health through safe, non-toxic, and body-conscious methods. Unlike conventional dentistry, which treats isolated dental concerns, holistic dentistry considers the interconnectedness of the entire body, physical, emotional, and energetic. The goal is not only a healthier mouth, but a healthier life.

This approach eliminates the use of harmful materials like mercury and fluoride and embraces natural solutions that align with the body’s own wisdom. It’s a philosophy built on integrity, respect for the individual, and a deep understanding of how oral health affects every system within us.
