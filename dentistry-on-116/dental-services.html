Dental Services

Dr. Helton and the entire Dentistry on 116 team take your oral health seriously. We offer dental services in a high-tech office with personalized care.

Common Procedures

From regular exams and teeth cleaning to extractions, fillings, and more. The Dentistry on 116 team knows the importance of maintaining good oral health and provides the services to keep your smile its healthiest. Learn more about our most common services.

Cavity Detection

Catching cavities early makes all the difference! CariVu uses patented transillumination technology to identify caries (cavities) and cracks on the surface of your teeth, as well as in the hard-to-see areas. Our CariVu technology allows us an almost transparent view of your teeth in order to catch cavities early.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Your smile is one of your most important features. Dentistry is continually evolving, and with a variety of advanced treatment options, achieving a beautiful, healthy, aesthetic smile is made easy for patients of all ages. Are you ready for a smile makeover?

Invisalign

Looking to get the beautiful straight smile you&#x27;ve always wanted? Invisalign may be for you. Using a series of virtually invisible, removable and comfortable aligners, Invisalign can help you achieve a beautiful smile without the discomfort of braces. We offer services for adults and teens.

Teeth Whitening

‍Everyone wants a brighter, whiter smile. There are many teeth whitening options that are available between professional whitening services and at-home whitening treatments.

Sedation Dentistry

Our sedation dentistry allows patients who experience anxiety around dentist visits to receive a variety of dental treatments safely and comfortably.

Oral Cancer Screening

Early detection of oral cancers can play a major role in a patient&#x27;s chances of survival. Early diagnosis and treatment can increase survival rates by up to 85 %. Dentistry on 116 is proud to offer oral cancer screenings using the best technology for early detection.

Pediatric Dentistry

Your child will feel at ease from the moment your family arrives at our office because our team is trained to use pleasant and non-frightening words to describe treatments. The more your child understands their visit, the better they will feel.
