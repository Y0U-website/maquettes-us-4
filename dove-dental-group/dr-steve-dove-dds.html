Dr. Steve Dove, DDS

Beyond his practice, Dr. Steve is a mentor to dentists across the nation, coaching them to elevate their craft and create their own legacies of stellar patient care. When he’s not shaping smiles or guiding colleagues, you’ll find him at church, cherishing family time or powering through local triathlons with the same grit he brings to his work.

In Dr. Steve’s words: “I call my patients ‘friends’ because they’re more than just teeth and charts. When they step into our office, I want them to feel, ‘This place gets me.’ We don’t just treat smiles—we transform lives.”

  • Excellence : He’s not content with “good enough.” Dr. Steve doubles down on continuing education, soaking up knowledge to stay at the top of his game.
  • Dr. Kelly Dove, his wife : A fellow dentist and his ultimate partner-in-crime, she shares his ambition and cheers him on, making them dentistry’s dynamic duo.
