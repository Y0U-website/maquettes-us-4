Meet The Doctors

A confident smile can change everything — how you feel, how you express yourself, and how others see you. At Skale Dental Professionals in Northbrook, IL, we believe everyone deserves a smile that reflects their best self. Our cosmetic dentistry services are designed to enhance both the beauty and health of your teeth using advanced technology, precise techniques, and a personalized approach. Whether you want to correct discoloration, repair chips, or achieve a more balanced smile, our team can help you reach your goals with artistry and care.

Ryan and his father bring a family feel to the practice of dentistry, ensuring each patient is treated with compassion and respect while receiving excellent dental care.

Dr. Ryan has recently gained even further advanced education through an arduous TMJ and Sleep Therapy residency program and completed over 200 hours of continuing education specifically focused on TMJ pain and Sleep Disordered Breathing. In 2016, he was accepted as a Diplomate in the American Board of Craniofacial Dental Sleep Medicine after passing a written examination and submitting successful case studies to the board. He is a “Fellowship-Eligible” in the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine and the American Board of Craniofacial Pain.

Dr. Ryan Skale is a member of the ADA, ISDS, CDS, AACP, ABCDSM, AADSM, and AGD. He is working toward a fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry.

40 Under 40 Nomination

Dr. Skale was honored to be nominated by Incisal Edge Magazine’s 40 Under 40 in 2021 for completing an AEGD residency at Bay Pines VA in Florida, where he provided free, comprehensive care to veterans. He began a focus on TMJ and sleep therapy and was recommended for Diplomate status by Drs. Allen Moses and Steven Olmos, two “founding fathers” of dental sleep medicine. He is a Diplomate with the American Board of Craniofacial Dental Sleep Medicine and a Fellow of the American Board of Craniofacial Pain.

Dr. Darryl Skale Dr. Darryl has always been a great leader. He grew up in Dayton, Ohio, and was captain of the varsity lacrosse team at The Ohio State University as an undergraduate before receiving his dental degree from Ohio State in 1979. He was highly involved at the dental school and served as the treasurer of the professional dental fraternity Alpha Omega. He moved to the Chicago area with his beautiful wife to start his professional career.

In 1984, Dr. Skale opened the doors to his practice in Northbrook, IL, and has been an integral part of the community ever since. Darryl may be better known as Coach Skale than Dr. Skale because any time not spent at the office or with his family is on the sidelines of the practice field.

He began coaching for Northbrook Youth Soccer in 1989 and was a pioneer in developing the Girls Traveling Soccer Program in Northbrook. He served as Director of Youth Soccer for the Park District from 2000 to 2006, and his commitment to youth soccer got him elected into the Northbrook Park District Hall of Fame in 2006. Believe it or not, he is still coaching today as an Assistant Varsity Lacrosse Coach, a position he has held for 20 years. Previously, he stood as President of the Spartan FC soccer club for many years.

Dr. Wei Ting Ho

Dr. Ryan Skale is thrilled to welcome Dr. Wei Ting Ho, board-certified periodontist, onto the Skale Dental Professionals team, as Dr. Ho was one of Dr. Ryan’s surgical mentors while Dr. Ryan was a predoctoral student at Ohio State. Dr. Ho was hand-picked by Dr. Ryan, who believes that comprehensive therapy is the key to the highest level of success. Having a surgeon in-house at Skale Dental allows for the best collaborative treatment approach and working relationship with our doctors and team. And best of all, the treatment is done within the comfort of our Skale Dental office.

Dr. Ho’s patients applaud his gentle and friendly manner, along with his detailed and thorough approach to treatment. His advanced surgical skill set in the field of periodontics gives our patients the opportunity to improve their oral health, save teeth, replace missing teeth, improve function, and esthetics. The most common procedures in helping rebuild smiles are with treatment utilizing tissue regeneration and dental implants.

Dr. Wei Ting Ho is a diplomate and board-certified with the American Board of Periodontology (2009). He is a graduate of The Ohio State University, where he received his M.S. and Certificate of Periodontology in 2009. Dr. Ho received his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from China Medical University in Taichung, Taiwan, in 2002. Dr. Ho also served as a professor of Surgical Science at Marquette University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Wei Ting Ho is an incredible addition to our team at Skale Dental Professionals.

Dr. Sharon Goodzey Drs. Ryan and Darryl Skale are thrilled to welcome and introduce Dr. Sharon Goodzey to join our Skale Dental Professionals team and family.

Dr. Sharon Goodzey is an accomplished dentist with over 25 years of expertise in general and esthetic dentistry, delivering exceptional, patient-centered care tailored to meet the unique needs of each individual.

A graduate of Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Goodzey has built a reputation for her compassionate approach and unwavering commitment to excellence. She is dedicated to staying at the forefront of advancements in dental techniques and technologies through ongoing professional development and continuing education.

Dr. Goodzey believes in fostering a collaborative relationship with her patients, empowering them to actively participate in their dental health journey. She takes the time to listen and understand their specific concerns, ensuring that her treatment plans are both personalized and effective. Whether enhancing smiles through esthetic dentistry or promoting oral health through preventative care, she is passionate about helping patients achieve and maintain their best oral health.

Outside her practice, Dr. Goodzey treasures quality time with her family, often enjoying gatherings and creating lasting memories. Her genuine warmth and dedication extend beyond her professional life, reflecting her holistic approach to well-being and care.
