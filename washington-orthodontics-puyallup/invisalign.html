Comprehensive Smile Assessment

Your path to a straighter smile starts with a thorough examination at Washington Orthodontics . We’ll evaluate your oral health, discuss your smile goals, and determine if Invisalign or clear aligners are the right choice for you. Our team will address any concerns and answer your questions, ensuring you feel confident about your treatment options.

Customized Aligner Design

Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. This digital plan allows us to show you a preview of your expected results and provides a clear timeline for your treatment. We’ll collaborate with you to fine-tune the plan, ensuring it aligns perfectly with your goals.

Starting Your Aligner Treatment

Once your custom aligners are ready, we’ll provide you with your first set and guide you through the wearing process. You’ll wear each set of aligners for about 1-2 weeks before moving to the next in the series. We’ll ensure you understand how to use and care for your aligners properly for the best results.

Aligner Care and Oral Health Tips

We’ll equip you with detailed instructions on maintaining your aligners and preserving good oral hygiene throughout your treatment. This includes proper cleaning techniques for your aligners and advice on keeping your teeth healthy while wearing them. Our team is always available to answer any questions you may have about aligner care.

Ongoing Progress Checks

Regular check-ups at Washington Orthodontics will allow us to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments to your treatment plan. These appointments ensure your teeth are moving as expected and give you the opportunity to discuss any concerns. Even after your active treatment is complete, we’ll provide retainers and schedule follow-up appointments to maintain your new smile.

FAQs About Clear Aligners

How long does Invisalign take?

At Washington Orthodontics, we’ve seen Invisalign treatments range from 6 to 18 months, depending on individual needs. Your treatment duration will be influenced by factors like the complexity of your case and how consistently you wear your aligners. During your consultation, we’ll provide a personalized timeline based on your unique smile goals.

Can Invisalign fix overbite?

Absolutely. Invisalign can correct many overbite cases using advanced technology and customized aligner design. At Washington Orthodontics, located in the heart of Factoria we also treat patients in Bellevue, Issaquah and the Eastside with modern techniques—often using small attachments or elastics to enhance results. We’ll assess your specific case and let you know if Invisalign is the right choice for correcting your bite.

Is Invisalign cheaper than braces?

Invisalign and braces are often similar in cost, with pricing based on your individual treatment needs. Some patients find that Invisalign is slightly higher in price, but well worth the added flexibility and low-profile appearance. We also offer flexible payment plans to make both options more accessible—whether you live near The Landing in Renton, the Issaquah Highlands, Lakemont, or Mercer Island’s town center. No matter where you’re located on the Eastside, our team is here to help you get the care you need without added financial stress

How to clean Invisalign aligners?

Keeping your Invisalign aligners clean is simple. We recommend rinsing them every time you remove them and gently brushing with a soft toothbrush and lukewarm water. If you’re out hiking Cougar Mountain (as a true PNW native) or grabbing a bite in downtown Issaquah or Bellevue, just keep a travel case with you to keep them safe. For a deeper clean, you can use the Invisalign cleaning system or a gentle antibacterial soap—just avoid hot water to prevent warping.

Are clear aligners better than braces?

Both clear aligners and braces have their own advantages, and the right choice really depends on your smile goals and daily routine. Clear aligners offer more freedom during meals, make brushing and flossing easier, and are nearly invisible—making them a popular choice for patients with busy, active lifestyles.

Whether you’re commuting from Talus or Grand Ridge Plaza in Issaquah Highlands, running errands near Fairwood Shopping Center in Renton, spending time with family by Mercerdale Park on Mercer Island, or heading out for a hike near Lewis Creek Park in Lakemont, clear aligners fit easily into your day. During your consultation, we’ll walk you through your options and help you choose the best treatment for your needs.

Do clear aligners hurt?

Clear aligners are generally more comfortable than braces, but it’s normal to feel a little pressure when you switch to a new set. That gentle pressure means your treatment is working. Most of our patients—whether they’re heading to class, working from home, or enjoying a trail day—adjust within a few days. And if you ever need help, our team is just a quick call or visit away.
