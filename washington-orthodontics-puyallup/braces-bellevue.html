Comprehensive Smile Assessment

Your journey to straighter teeth begins with a thorough examination at our Bellevue or Federal Way office. We use modern imaging to get a detailed view of your teeth and jaw structure, then talk through your smile goals and any concerns you may have. This in-depth consultation helps us understand your unique needs and explain how braces can benefit your specific case.

Crafting Your Custom Plan

Based on your smile assessment, we develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your individual needs. This plan outlines the type of braces best suited for you, your estimated treatment time, and any additional steps that may enhance your results. We walk you through each part of the process so you understand what to expect and feel confident about your decision to move forward.

Precise Placement

Once you are ready to start, we carefully place your braces, paying close attention to positioning so your teeth move in a predictable, healthy way. Whether you choose traditional metal braces or another option from our orthodontic toolbox, our team applies everything with precision and care. You will also receive clear instructions on how to care for your braces and what the first few weeks will feel like.

Ongoing Care and Adjustments

Throughout treatment, you will visit us regularly for adjustments and progress checks. These appointments allow us to monitor how your teeth are moving, make any needed changes, and answer questions as they come up. Our goal is to keep your braces experience as smooth and comfortable as possible, whether you are seeing us in Bellevue or Federal Way.

Celebrating Your New Smile

When your braces come off, we celebrate your new smile and move into the retention phase. We fit you with a custom retainer to keep your teeth in their new positions and schedule follow-up visits as needed. Our commitment to your care continues well beyond the day the braces are removed, because we want your results to last for many years to come.

FAQs About Braces in Bellevue

How long will I need to wear braces?

Your treatment timeline is as unique as your smile. Most patients wear braces for about 18 to 24 months, but your exact timing will depend on the complexity of your case and how your teeth respond to treatment. At Washington Orthodontics, we use thoughtful planning and modern techniques to keep your treatment efficient while still protecting your long-term oral health.

How will braces affect my daily routine?

Adjusting to braces is often easier than people expect. You will need to avoid a few hard or sticky foods and spend a little extra time brushing and flossing. Most patients, from students to busy professionals, find that these changes quickly become part of their normal routine. We give you simple, practical tips so you know how to eat, clean your teeth, and stay comfortable throughout treatment.

Are braces suitable for adults, or are they just for teenagers?

Braces are not just for teens. Many adults visit our Bellevue and Federal Way offices because they want a healthier bite, easier cleaning, or a more confident smile. Adult orthodontic treatment can use traditional braces or clear aligners, depending on your needs and preferences. During your consultation, we discuss all your options so you can choose an approach that fits your goals and your daily life.

How often will I need to visit Washington Orthodontics during my treatment?

Most patients visit us every 4 to 6 weeks for adjustments and progress checks. We do our best to schedule appointments around school, work, and family obligations. With two locations in Bellevue and Federal Way, it is easier to find a time and place that fits your schedule. Our team will work with you to keep your treatment on track without disrupting your routine.

What happens after my braces are removed?

Once your braces are removed, you move into the retention phase to protect your results. You will receive a custom retainer and instructions on when and how to wear it. We may also schedule occasional check-ups at our Bellevue or Federal Way office to make sure everything stays stable. The retention phase is a key step that keeps your new smile aligned, healthy, and confident for years to come.
