The practice

At Paragon Family Dental, we’re dedicated to your Dental Health and strive to provide highest quality dental care to Chula Vista and San Diego communities.

Dr. Kaur graduated from University of California, San Francisco, where she earned Doctor of Dental Surgery( DDS ) degree in 2010. She also has Advanced Implant Training from California Implant Institute. Since 2010 she has been resident of San Diego and has been passionately serving patients in San Diego communities.

Dr. Kaur’s passion is to provide high-quality dental care to her patients. She, in particular, enjoys working with children with whom she will quickly develop rapport and trust. Dr. Kaur strives to create a comfortable, friendly and relaxing dental experience and environment for patients through understanding and meeting patient’s individualized needs.

During her spare time, Dr. Kaur is also a passionate baker, always testing new recipes to perfect her baked goods. She loves to travel, explore outdoors and spending time with family and friends.
