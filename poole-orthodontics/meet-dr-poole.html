Morris N. Poole, DDS

American Board of Orthodontics, Certified Orthodontist

Practice Consultant

Having worked in the field of orthodontics for more than 40 years gives Dr. Poole an overview of the profession that few orthodontists have. He served as president of the American Association of Orthodontists. Now retired from practicing orthodontics, he is involved in advising the team with respect to practice management, patient care, and office procedures.

Dr. Poole’s family consists of his wife Dixie and seven children, one of whom, Morris L. Poole, DDS, owns and practices orthodontics at Poole Orthodontics. Dr. Poole has a small farm that he manages and enjoys the outdoors. He has also served on community boards and committees, which has brought him great satisfaction. One of his little-known achievements is that he is the owner of a New Zealand driver’s license where they drive on the left!
