Creating Stunning Smiles With Trusted Expertise

At Poole Orthodontics, we’ve been crafting beautiful smiles in Logan and Providence, UT since 1978. As a second-generation, board-certified orthodontist, Dr. Poole brings 45+ years of experience. We offer free consultations too. Our digital scanning allows for 3D-printed trays, ensuring faster and more comfortable braces applications. We’re honored to have received the HJ News Reader’s Choice Awards for Best Orthodontist, Best of Northern Utah, and Best Customer Service.

As a locally and family-owned practice, we’re deeply invested in our community, supporting both Cache County and Logan City schools. We understand that orthodontic care is a significant decision, which is why we offer consultations within a week and accept insurance, handling all the paperwork for you. Whether you’re seeking treatment in English or Spanish, we’re here to guide you through your orthodontic journey. Ready to start your path to a radiant smile? Contact us today!
