Achieve the Smile You’ve Always Wanted

At Poole Orthodontics in Logan and Providence, UT, we’re dedicated to creating beautiful, healthy smiles for patients of all ages. With 45+ years of experience, our board-certified orthodontist combines expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver exceptional care. As a second-generation, family-owned practice, we understand the importance of personalized treatment and strive to make every patient feel comfortable and confident throughout their orthodontic journey. We offer a range of services, including adult braces, and Invisalign.

Our commitment to excellence has earned us recognition as the Best Orthodontist in the HJ News Reader’s Choice Awards and Best of Northern Utah. We utilize advanced digital scanning technology to create 3D-printed trays, ensuring faster and more comfortable braces applications. With free consultations, flexible scheduling, and a bilingual team fluent in English and Spanish, we’re here to make your orthodontic experience as smooth as possible.
