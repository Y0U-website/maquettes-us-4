0 Meet Our Team Dr. Brian Chang grew up in Palo Alto and went to the University of the Pacific in an accelerated dental program. Through this prestigious program, he finished college in 3 years followed by 3 years of dental school in San Francisco. Upon graduating in 2018, Dr. Chang moved to Chicago to work in the city’s South side in a clinic serving underprivileged communities from 2018-2020.

After moving back to the Bay Area, Dr. Chang worked as an associate dentist at his childhood dental office where he provided premium dental care to his patients. He is committed to bringing the same focused and quality care to his community now in South San Francisco.

In his free time, Dr. Chang enjoys exploring San Francisco, trying new restaurants, and spending time outdoors.
