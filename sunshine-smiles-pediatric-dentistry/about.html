About Us

Specialized Pediatric Dental Care for Growing Smiles

We understand that visiting the dentist can feel intimidating for little ones, which is why we focus on a bright, upbeat, and fun environment that helps children feel at ease. Our team works patiently and gently to ensure kids feel safe, supported, and confident during every visit, helping them build a positive relationship with dental care from an early age.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional pediatric dental care while teaching healthy habits that last a lifetime. We proudly serve infants, children, adolescents, and patients with special healthcare needs, offering personalized care tailored to each child’s unique stage of development.

Your child’s healthy smile starts here, and our friendly pediatric dental team is always ready to welcome your family.

Our Mission

At Sunshine Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, our mission is to create positive dental experiences that help children feel comfortable, confident, and supported. We focus on gentle pediatric dental care that encourages healthy habits and builds strong smiles from an early age.

Our Values

Our values guide how we care for children and communicate with parents at every visit. We believe in a child-first approach that emphasizes comfort, prevention, and clear guidance to help families feel confident in their child’s dental care.

Our Kid-Friendly Pediatric Dental Office in Gardner, KS

Our office is designed to help children feel comfortable the moment they arrive. From a welcoming atmosphere to a gentle, child-focused approach, we’ve created a space where kids can relax and parents can feel confident about their child’s dental care.

What Makes Us Different

Choosing the right dentist for your child matters. At Sunshine Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, we focus on gentle care, clear communication, and creating positive dental experiences that help children feel confident and comfortable at every visit.

Gentle, Child-Focused Care We take a calm, patient approach to help children feel relaxed and supported during their dental visits. Every treatment is tailored to your child’s comfort and emotional needs.

Pediatric Dentistry Specialists

Our care is focused exclusively on infants, children, and teens. This allows us to address developing smiles with age-appropriate techniques and preventive strategies.

Welcoming, Kid-Friendly Office

Our office is designed to feel bright, friendly, and non-intimidating. A comfortable environment helps reduce dental anxiety and makes visits more enjoyable for kids.

Clear Communication with Parents

We take time to explain treatments, answer questions, and guide parents on at-home care. This partnership helps families feel informed and confident in their child’s dental care.

Trusted by Local Families

Families in Gardner, KS and nearby communities trust us for compassionate care and consistent, high-quality pediatric dentistry focused on long-term oral health.
