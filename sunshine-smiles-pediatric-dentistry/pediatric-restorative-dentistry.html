Our Restorative Dentistry Process for Kids in Gardner, KS

Gentle Exam and Screening

We begin with a thorough but gentle exam to evaluate your child’s teeth, gums, and oral development. If necessary, digital X rays may be taken to identify cavities or hidden concerns between teeth. Our team carefully explains the findings and recommended treatment options to parents.

Restorative Treatment

Depending on your child’s needs, treatment may include fillings, crowns, or other restorative procedures to repair and strengthen the tooth. Our team uses kid friendly techniques and focuses on comfort throughout the visit so your child feels relaxed during treatment.

Aftercare and Prevention

Once treatment is complete, we provide guidance on brushing, flossing, and dietary habits to help prevent future cavities. We also recommend routine checkups so we can monitor your child’s oral health and keep their smile strong as they grow.

FAQs About Pediatric Restorative Dentistry

What is pediatric restorative dentistry?

Pediatric restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and restoring the function and health of a child’s teeth. At Sunshine Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Adela Casa uses a conservative approach to treat issues like tooth decay, cavities, or dental injuries. Our restorative services include tooth-colored fillings, baby root canals (pulpotomies), and durable dental crowns designed specifically for growing smiles.

When does a child need restorative dental treatment?

Restorative care is necessary when a tooth is damaged by decay or trauma. Common signs that your child may need a restorative visit at our Gardner office include:

  • Visible dark spots, holes, or chipping on the teeth.
  • Sensitivity to hot, cold, or sugary foods.
  • Persistent tooth pain or "bubbles" on the gums (a sign of infection).
  • Difficulty chewing or a sudden change in eating habits.

Are tooth colored fillings safe for children?

Yes. At Sunshine Smiles, we use BPA-free composite resin for our dental fillings. These "white fillings" are a safe, mercury-free alternative to traditional metal amalgams. They bond directly to the tooth structure, allowing Dr. Casa to preserve more of your child’s natural tooth while providing a seamless, natural-looking result.

Do baby teeth really need to be repaired if they have cavities?

Yes, maintaining baby teeth is vital for your child’s long-term oral health. These teeth serve as "space maintainers" for permanent teeth. If a baby tooth is lost prematurely due to an untreated cavity, it can cause alignment issues and crowding when adult teeth begin to erupt. Furthermore, untreated decay can lead to pain and infections that may affect your child’s overall development and well being.

What if my child is nervous about dental treatment?

We specialize in helping anxious children feel at home. Our Gardner office is designed to be a "dental home" where kids feel safe and supported. For children who need extra help relaxing, Dr. Casa offers several sedation options, including:

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas): A gentle way to help kids stay calm while remaining fully awake. Oral Conscious Sedation: Helpful for children with higher anxiety levels. Specialized Care: As a board certified specialist, Dr. Casa is highly trained in behavioral guidance and treating patients with special healthcare needs.
