Benefits of Our Pediatric Dental Services

Specialized Pediatric Expertise As a board-certified pediatric dentist, Dr. Adela Casa has years of advanced training focused entirely on the unique oral health and behavioral needs of developing smiles. From infants to teens, your child is in the hands of a true specialist.

Anxiety-Free, Kid-Friendly Environment Our Gardner office is intentionally designed to turn a dental visit into a fun, stress-free experience. With a welcoming atmosphere, gentle team, and safe sedation options, we help even the most anxious children feel completely relaxed.

Inclusive Care for All Children We proudly offer specialized pediatric dentistry for children with special healthcare needs. Dr. Casa’s compassionate, patient-centered approach ensures that every child receives the tailored accommodations and gentle care they deserve.

Our General Dentistry Process for Kids in Gardner, KS

Welcoming & Comfort First

We start every visit by making sure your child feels completely safe and at ease. We introduce them to our friendly team, explain our dental tools using fun, kid-friendly language, and let them explore the dental chair at their own pace.

Comprehensive Exam & Digital Imaging

Dr. Casa performs a gentle, thorough examination of your child’s teeth, gums, and jaw development. If necessary, we use low-radiation digital X-rays to check for hidden cavities between teeth and ensure their permanent teeth are developing properly beneath the surface.

Personalized Parent Guidance & Next Steps

Before you leave, we sit down with you to review our findings in plain, simple language. We share customized tips for at-home brushing, flossing, and nutrition tailored to your child’s age, answer any questions you have, and help you schedule their next routine checkup.

Do you accept families who live outside of Gardner?

Absolutely! Dr. Casa and our team proudly welcome patients from all over the surrounding communities. Many of our families travel to us from Olathe, Spring Hill, Overland Park, Lenexa, Shawnee, De Soto, Edgerton, Wellsville, and Baldwin City for our specialized pediatric care.

How can I schedule an appointment at your Gardner location?

Scheduling is simple! You can request an appointment directly through our website using the Request Appointment button , or you can call our friendly office team during business hours at (913) 884-3747.

What should I bring to my child’s first visit to your office?

For your first visit, please bring your child’s dental insurance card (if applicable) and a photo ID. To save time, you can also fill out our patient intake forms online prior to your arrival. If your child is transferring from another dentist, letting us know ahead of time helps us request their previous dental records.

Are your pediatric dental services available for children with special needs at this location?

Yes. Our Gardner office is specifically designed to accommodate and support children with special healthcare needs. Dr. Adela Casa has advanced training dedicated exclusively to treating infants, children, and teens, ensuring a gentle, patient, and tailored approach for every child’s unique comfort level.
