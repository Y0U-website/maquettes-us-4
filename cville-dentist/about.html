Our Story

Cville Dentist was founded on a simple belief: that every person in Charlottesville and the surrounding Albemarle County area deserves access to dental care that is both clinically excellent and genuinely compassionate. From our very first patient, we set out to build a practice where advanced technology meets a warm, personal touch.

You’ll find us on Commonwealth Drive, just off the Route 29 corridor. The community we serve is wide, UVA faculty and grad students, young families in Forest Lakes and Hollymead, retirees who’ve been in town for decades, and everyone in between. Whatever brought you in, we’ll remember your name, your story, and the way you take your coffee after a cleaning.

Our job is simple: help you keep your smile healthy for decades , with preventive care first, honest answers when something needs attention, and a clear plan you actually understand. We measure success by the patients who keep coming back and bring their families with them.

What We Stand For

Patient-Centered Care

Every treatment plan starts with listening. We take the time to understand your goals, answer your questions, and tailor our approach to your unique needs.

Clinical Excellence

Our team pursues ongoing education and invests in advanced technology so you receive the most effective, evidence-based treatments available in modern dentistry.

Community Connection

We are proud to call Charlottesville home. From supporting UVA athletics to sponsoring local events, we are deeply invested in the community we serve.

Comfort & Trust

We know that visiting the dentist can feel stressful. Our warm, unhurried approach and transparent communication are designed to put you at ease from the moment you arrive.
