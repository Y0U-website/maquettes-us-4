Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about dental implants at our Charlottesville office.

With good home care and regular checkups, dental implants can last decades, and often a lifetime. The titanium post fuses with your jawbone, while the visible crown may need replacement after many years of normal wear.

Implant placement is done with local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Most patients describe the recovery as easier than expected and manage any soreness with over-the-counter pain relievers.
