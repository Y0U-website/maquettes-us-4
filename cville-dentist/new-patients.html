Welcome, New Patients

We are excited to welcome you to our Charlottesville dental family. Here is everything you need to prepare for your first visit.

Your First Visit

Your first appointment at Cville Dentist is all about getting to know you. We will review your dental and medical history, take any necessary X-rays, and perform a comprehensive examination of your teeth, gums, and overall oral health. This thorough evaluation allows us to create a personalized care plan that addresses your unique needs and goals.

We understand that choosing a new dentist is an important decision, especially if you are new to the Charlottesville area or have not visited a dentist in a while. Our team is committed to creating a comfortable, judgment-free environment where you can ask questions and feel confident about your care. Whether you are a UVA student, a young professional, or a family looking for a dental home in Albemarle County, we are here for you.

To make your first visit as smooth as possible, we encourage you to complete your patient forms ahead of time, bring your insurance card, and arrive a few minutes early. If you have dental anxiety, please let us know so we can take extra steps to ensure your comfort.
