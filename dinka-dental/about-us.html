Meet the Dinka Dental Dentists!

Catherine Osinski Dinka, D.D.S

Dr. Catherine Osinski Dinka is a 1988 graduate of the University of Detroit School of Dentistry.

Dr. Cathy co-founded Dinka Dental with her husband John Dinka, D.D.S., in 1989.

Dr. Cathy is a member of the Macomb District Dental Society , the Detroit District Dental Society , the Michigan Dental Association , the American Dental Association , the Academy of General Dentistry , the Pierre Fauchard Academy , the UDM School of Dentistry Alumni Board of Directors and Healthy Kids Dental .

Dr. Cathy continues to do what she loves caring for every patient as if they are a member of the family. She enjoys being with people and traveling.

John Dinka, D.D.S.

Dr. John Dinka is a 1986 graduate of the University of Detroit, now the University of Detroit Mercy , School of Dentistry . After several years of providing dental care as an associate, he co-founded Dinka Dental with his wife Catherine in 1989.

Dr. John is a member of the Macomb District Dental Society , the Detroit District Dental Society , the Michigan Dental Association , the American Dental Association , the Academy of General Dentistry , the Pierre Fauchard Academy , the UDM School of Dentistry Alumni Board of Directors , the Dental Lifeline Network of Michigan , and Healthy Kids Dental .

Dr. John enjoys providing comprehensive dental treatment to his patient family by creating and maintaining beautiful smiles for all.

Outside of the office, Dr. John has been involved for over 20 years as an adult leader with the Cub Scouts of America and Boy Scouts of America programs, in addition to being an avid classic and muscle car enthusiast.

Ingrid Almquist, D.D.S.

Dr. Ingrid, who grew up in Chicago, Illinois, attended North Park University and is a 1999 graduate of the University of Michigan School of Dentistry .

After graduating from dental school, Dr. Ingrid completed a one-year general residency program at the Veterans Administration Hospital in Ann Arbor, MI.

Dr. Ingrid joined Dinka Dental as an associate in 2001. She derives great satisfaction by helping her patients find and keep their beautiful smiles. Her passion is oral health maintenance and education. She looks forward to having you as a patient.

Jenna Dinka, D.D.S.

We are thrilled to announce the newest member of our dental family, Dr. Jenna Dinka!

Dr. Dinka brings with her an exceptional wealth of knowledge, expertise in the latest dental techniques, and an unwavering passion for creating beautiful smiles. As the daughter of esteemed dentists, Dr. Catherine Osinski Dinka, D.D.S, and Dr. John Dinka, she proudly upholds the legacy of a distinguished family tradition in the field of dentistry.

Dr. Jenna Dinka, a 2023 graduate of the University of Detroit Mercy, School of Dentistry , is the newest member of our dental team. Dr. Jenna brings with her an exceptional wealth of knowledge, expertise in the latest dental techniques, and an unwavering passion for creating beautiful smiles.

Dr. Jenna is an affiliate of the Macomb District Dental Society, the Detroit District Dental Society , the Michigan Dental Association , and the American Dental Association . She is also a member of Alpha Sigma Nu Honor Society and Delta Sigma Delta Dental Fraternity .

When not in the office, Dr. Jenna enjoys painting, her pet fish, and being with family and friends.

Hayden Sutton, D.D.S.

Dr. Hayden Sutton is a compassionate, patient-focused dentist dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable and confident at every visit. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (D.D.S.) from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry in 2023 and brings over two years of experience providing comprehensive, high-quality care.

He believes great dentistry starts with listening and building genuine relationships. His approach combines modern techniques with a gentle touch to create a relaxed, welcoming experience for every patient.

Dr. Sutton maintains memberships with the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, Macomb District Dental Society, Pierre Fauchard Academy, and Omicron Kappa Upsilon, and serves on the UDM School of Dentistry Alumni Board.

Outside the office, he enjoys trying new coffee spots, rock climbing, and listening to vinyl records. He works alongside his wife, Jenna, also a dentist at Dinka Dental, and is proud to be part of the Dinka family practice with Drs. Catherine and John Dinka.
