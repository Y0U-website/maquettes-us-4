How Do All-on-X Dental Implants Work?

Unlike traditional dentures that rely on suction or metal clasps to stay in place, All-on-X dental implants are secured directly into the jaw using just four strategically placed titanium posts.

Am I a Candidate for All-on-X Dental Implants?

If you’re missing most or all of your teeth along the upper or lower arch, you may be a great candidate for All-on-X. At Westchester Gentle Dentistry, we start with a thorough consultation and exam to ensure dental implants are a safe and suitable option. Ideal candidates need to meet three primary requirements: good oral health, stable overall health, and sufficient jawbone density to support the implants. If any of these elements are lacking, we’ll discuss the steps needed to get you ready for treatment.

What Are the Benefits of All-on-X Dental Implants?

  • Security: Because the implant denture is anchored directly into the jawbone, it’s far more secure than a traditional denture. This stability lets patients enjoy a wider range of foods with confidence.
  • Comfort: All-on-X dentures are more compact than removable ones. They don’t need large plates or bulky hardware, allowing for more natural tongue movement and a better fit. Plus, the lack of shifting means fewer sore spots and more enjoyable meals.
  • Longevity: While standard dentures typically need replacement every five to seven years, All-on-X implants can last for decades and potentially a lifetime with proper care.

All-on-X vs. Traditional Dental Implants

Traditional implant solutions often require one implant per tooth or two for a small bridge, making full-mouth replacement time-consuming and costly. All-on-X simplifies things by using just four implants to replace an entire arch. This approach streamlines the process, cuts down on cost, and makes same-day smile transformations possible. It’s a less is more solution that delivers reliable, long-term results.
