Implant Placement

The selected type of implant is inserted in the jawbone under precise techniques and local anesthesia. Sedation may also be offered for added comfort. An incision is made in the gums to expose the jawbone, where a small hole is precisely created to place the titanium implant. It is carefully placed to support healing and long-term health.

Healing & Integration

After dental implant surgery, your body begins a natural healing process called osseointegration, where your jawbone grows around and bonds with the implant. This step is critical for the stability and strength of your new tooth. The healing phase typically takes 3 to 6 months, although it may vary based on your individual health and bone density.

Abutment & Final Restoration

Once the implant has fully healed, your dentist places a small connector called an abutment on the implant. This piece supports the final crown, which is custom-made to match your natural teeth in shape and color. The result is a complete, natural-looking tooth that restores normal function.
