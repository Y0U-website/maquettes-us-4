WHAT SETS US APART FAQs

Does it matter if my orthodontist is board certified?

Yes. Board certification through the American Board of Orthodontics is voluntary, and only about one in three orthodontists complete it. It requires presenting finished patient cases to a panel of examiners for judgment. Dr. Walker is a board-certified orthodontist who also defended her Master&#x27;s Thesis on the use of Invisalign in young children, which means the doctor directing your care has had her clinical results independently examined, not just her coursework.

What is the difference between a private orthodontist and a corporate chain?

At a private practice like Walker Orthodontics, one orthodontist owns the practice, sets the standard of care, and answers only to her patients. Corporate chains and private-equity-owned groups often rotate doctors between locations, so you may see a different provider at each visit and treatment decisions can be influenced by production targets. Dr. Erin Walker plans your treatment personally, sees you at every appointment, and is accountable for your result from your first scan to your final retainer.

Will I actually see Dr. Walker at every appointment?

Yes, every single visit. As a single-doctor boutique practice, there is no rotating cast of providers and no chance your treatment plan changes hands midway. Dr. Walker performs your exam, reviews your progress personally, and makes every clinical decision herself. Patients from Westlake Village, Thousand Oaks, Agoura Hills, and Oak Park choose us specifically because the doctor they meet at the consultation is the doctor they see through the end of treatment.

What is an airway-friendly orthodontist?

An airway-friendly orthodontist looks beyond straight teeth to how the jaw, palate, and facial structure affect breathing, sleep, and long-term development. Signs like mouth breathing, snoring, or restless sleep in a child can be connected to a narrow palate or airway restriction. Dr. Walker evaluates airway health as part of every exam and uses treatments such as palatal expansion to support healthy breathing while creating a beautiful smile, which is especially valuable for growing children.

How is Walker Orthodontics different from mail-order aligner companies?

Mail-order aligners move your teeth without an in-person exam, x-rays, or a doctor monitoring how your roots, gums, and bite respond. At Walker Orthodontics, every case starts with a comprehensive exam by a board-certified orthodontist, and your progress is checked continuously, in office and through Grin remote monitoring. You get the convenience that makes mail-order tempting, with the specialist supervision that protects your teeth. If something needs adjusting, Dr. Walker catches it early.

Do fewer office visits mean less supervision?

No, the opposite. Every Walker Orthodontics patient is set up with Grin remote monitoring, so Dr. Walker reviews scans you take from your phone between appointments. Combined with our fully digital office and in-house 3D printer, this means she often checks your progress more frequently than a traditional schedule of in-person visits allows, while your child misses less school and you make fewer trips to the office.
