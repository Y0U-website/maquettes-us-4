INVISALIGN FAQs

How does Invisalign work?

Invisalign straightens your teeth using a series of clear, custom-made aligners that fit snugly over your teeth and shift them a little at a time. At Walker Orthodontics we take a fast, comfortable digital scan with an iTero scanner, so there are no messy impression trays, and Dr. Walker maps out your full treatment before you ever start. You wear each set of aligners about 20 to 22 hours a day, swapping to the next set roughly every one to two weeks until your smile is where it should be.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

For most cases, yes. Invisalign moves teeth just as effectively as braces when it&#x27;s planned and supervised by an orthodontic specialist. The key difference is who&#x27;s directing your care: Dr. Walker is a board-certified orthodontist, so your aligners are custom-designed and monitored by an expert rather than mailed to you without an exam. For certain complex bite issues braces may still be the better tool, and Dr. Walker will tell you honestly which option fits your case at your consultation.

Can my teenager or younger child get Invisalign?

Yes. Walker Orthodontics offers Invisalign Teen for young adults who want to straighten their teeth without impacting their confidence, and Invisalign First for children roughly ages 6 to 10 as part of early (Phase I) treatment. Dr. Walker has advanced training in early treatment and clear aligners for growing patients, so she can determine whether your child is a good candidate now or whether it&#x27;s better to monitor their growth first.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign cases take about 12 to 18 months, though simpler cases can finish faster and more complex ones take longer. Wearing your aligners the recommended 20 to 22 hours a day is the single biggest factor in staying on schedule. Because we have an in-office 3D printer, we can produce new aligners and appliances quickly and keep your treatment moving without long waits.

Do I have to deal with messy molds or impressions?

No. Instead of the old goopy impression trays, we use an iTero digital scanner to capture a fast, accurate 3D image of your teeth, which is far more comfortable and lets you see your treatment plan digitally. That same scan drives your custom aligners, so your Invisalign is made to fit only your teeth. It&#x27;s a big part of why patients across the Conejo Valley find treatment here easier than they expected.
