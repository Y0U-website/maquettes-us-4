At Walker Orthodontics, we pride ourselves in offering the latest and best braces technology. Early treatment

While every child might not need early treatment, the American Association of Orthodontists does recommend an orthodontic consultation for all children by age 7.

An orthodontist can evaluate your child’s tooth eruption and facial growth at this age and determine if early treatment can help correct uneven jaw growth, a crossbite, overbite, underbite or sleep disturbances and habits such as thumbsucking.

TREATMENT FAQs

What orthodontic treatments does Walker Orthodontics offer?

Should I choose clear aligners or braces?

Both straighten teeth effectively, and the right choice depends on your case and your preferences. Clear aligners like Spark and Invisalign are removable and nearly invisible, which many teens and adults prefer, while custom braces such as KLOwen and Damon Ultima can be the most efficient choice for complex movements and don&#x27;t rely on you to keep them in. Dr. Walker will walk you through the trade-offs so you can make a confident decision, because there&#x27;s no one-size-fits-all answer.

At what age should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first orthodontic check-up by age 7. Seeing an orthodontist early doesn&#x27;t usually mean starting treatment right away. It lets Dr. Walker catch issues like crowding, crossbites, or jaw-growth and airway concerns while a child is still developing, when they&#x27;re easiest to guide. If nothing needs attention yet, we&#x27;ll simply monitor your child&#x27;s growth at no cost until the timing is right.

What is airway-focused orthodontics?

Airway-focused orthodontics looks beyond straight teeth to how your child&#x27;s jaws, bite, and airway are developing, because a narrow palate can be linked to mouth breathing, restless sleep, and crowded teeth. Using treatments such as palatal expansion, Dr. Walker can help create more room and support healthy breathing and facial growth, ideally while a child is still growing. It reflects her philosophy of building broad, healthy, beautiful smiles rather than just aligning teeth.

How long does orthodontic treatment take, and what does it cost?

Most treatments run about 12 to 24 months depending on complexity, and cost varies with the type of treatment and how much correction your teeth need. Walker Orthodontics offers flexible monthly payment plans, reviews your insurance benefits with you in advance, and provides a free initial consultation so you know your options before you commit. Do I have to wear a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Retainers are what keep your new smile from drifting back, so they&#x27;re an essential final step of any orthodontic treatment. Walker Orthodontics offers several options, including clear, colorful, permanent, and removable retainers, and Dr. Walker will recommend the best fit for your case and how often to wear it. Wearing your retainer as directed protects the investment you just made in your smile.
