General Dentistry

Caring for your teeth should be a top priority. You only get one set of adult teeth; regular dental visits are vital to keeping them healthy and strong. Salem Family Dental Care, your Salem, IL, dental team , offers a complete list of dentistry services to help you protect your teeth and your smile. From cleanings to X-rays, we have a full list of services to keep your teeth healthy.

Preventive Dentistry to Protect Your Health

When you visit our dental clinic for general dentistry, we focus on preventing and treating tooth decay and gum disease. Regular visits, typically two times a year, are a key to protecting against these common problems. When combined with good at-home dental hygiene, general dentistry is one of the best things you can do for your oral health.

In addition to the treatments offered at these twice-a-year appointments, they give us a chance to peek inside your mouth to see how well you are doing with your oral health care. Our dental team can suggest how to better protect your oral health and what techniques will work better for you with your at-home routine.

Finally, oral health screenings can actually protect your physical health in other ways. Many oral health concerns are actually indicative of underlying health issues that may be going unnoticed. For example, gum disease is often connected to other inflammatory conditions and even cardiovascular diseases. Our dental team can recommend other checkups that can protect your health.

Schedule Oral Health Screenings and Cleanings Today

It’s easy to ignore your oral health until you develop problems, but taking a preventive approach is always better. Schedule an oral health checkup with the Salem Family Dental Care team today, and ensure you are doing all you can to protect your gum and tooth health. Our caring team will make your experience a pleasant one as you work to ensure you are as healthy as possible.
