Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is a dental practice that enhances the cosmetic appearance of your smile. Cosmetic dentistry in Salem, IL , focuses on improving the look of your teeth, gums, and mouth.

The Different Types of Cosmetic Dentistry Explained

There are several options and procedures that fall under the blanket term “cosmetic dentistry.”

The following are some of the most common cosmetic dentistry treatments available.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are used to replace one, several, or an entire arch of missing teeth. Implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to anchor dental crowns, bridges, and dentures.

Dental Veneers

Dental veneers are extremely thin shell-like coverings. They are usually made of porcelain or composite resin and placed over the fronts of your teeth. Dental veneers are used to improve the look of your teeth. Veneers can cover severely stained teeth, fix chipped or cracked teeth, fill gaps between teeth, even out worn down or uneven teeth, or extend the length of your teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is used to improve the look of teeth that have tea, coffee, nicotine, or age-related stains. Teeth whitening can be done with at-home DIY whitening kits; however, you will get much better and longer-lasting results when you opt for professional teeth whitening at the dentist’s office. Professional teeth whitening procedures can usually be done in one visit.

Orthodontic Treatments

Orthodontic treatments such as metal braces and clear dental aligners are considered cosmetic dental treatments. Metal braces consist of metal wires and brackets that gradually move your teeth into their proper positions. Clear dental aligners do the same thing, except they use a series of clear plastic aligner trays.

Tooth Contouring

Tooth contouring is used to correct minor dental issues. It’s used to improve the appearance of your teeth. Your dentist in Salem, IL , will reshape your tooth by removing a small portion of enamel. After shaping, the tooth will be smoothed and polished.

Tooth Bonding

Tooth bonding uses a tooth-colored resin that is added to your tooth to fix or conceal imperfections such as cracks, chips, or severe stains. The tooth will then be smoothed and polished. Tooth bonding is used when a more dramatic improvement is needed (versus tooth contouring).

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are made of composite, ceramic, porcelain, metal, or a combination of porcelain fused to metal. They are custom-made caps that are crafted to fit over your entire tooth. Crowns are used to cover decayed, chipped, cracked, or broken teeth after the corrective dental procedure has been completed.

Gum Contouring

Gum contouring, also referred to as gum reshaping, is used to improve the appearance of your gum line. The gum contouring treatment can correct a “gummy” smile when you have too much gum tissue that shows when you smile. It’s also used to even out an uneven gum line. The procedure involves removing the excess gum tissue or reshaping the existing gum tissue.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry focuses on dental procedures that pertain to restoring chewing ability and overall oral health.

What Is Restorative Dentistry Used For?

Restorative dentistry in Salem, IL , is used to correct cavities, tooth decay, missing teeth, damaged teeth, broken teeth, and severe oral trauma.

Common Restorative Dentistry Procedures

Here are the most common restorative dentistry procedures.

  • Dental Implants
  • Dental Crowns
  • Dental Bridges
  • Dentures
  • Fillings
  • Root Canals

Why Is Restorative Dentistry Important?

Restorative dentistry is important to your overall health and keeping your teeth healthy and strong. It will also improve your ability to speak, eat, and chew properly.

Additionally, oral infections can spread to other parts of your body, creating new health issues that could lead to more severe diseases. Therefore, seeing a dentist in Salem, IL , regularly will help you maintain healthy teeth and gums.

The Benefits of Restorative Dentistry

There are many ways your oral and overall health will benefit from restorative dentistry procedures.

Eliminate Pain

Cavities, abscesses, infected teeth or gums, and anything else in your mouth causing you pain can be eliminated with a restorative dentistry procedure.

Restore Chewing Ability

Restorative dentistry will help restore the functionality of damaged or diseased teeth and unhealthy gums.

Preserve Jawbone Density

When you have missing teeth, the part of the jawbone where those teeth are missing will begin to deteriorate. Immediately replacing missing teeth with dental implants will prevent bone deterioration and bone loss from occurring.

Improve the Appearance of Your Smile

Missing or decaying teeth impact the look of your smile and your confidence. Restorative dentistry procedures will improve your smile while helping you maintain your oral health.

Prevent Dental Issues from Getting Worse

Restorative dentistry will help prevent ongoing dental issues from getting worse and new dental issues caused by ongoing dental issues from occurring. For example, if you have a cavity and you get it filled immediately, that cavity won’t get worse, and your tooth won’t decay completely and have to be removed.

What Is the Difference Between Restorative Dentistry and Cosmetic Dentistry?

Restorative dentistry mainly deals with improving your oral health and how well your teeth function. Cosmetic dentistry mainly focuses on improving the appearance of your smile.
