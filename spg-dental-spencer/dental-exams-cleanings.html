These two parts of preventive care work together. A comprehensive exam establishes a starting point at your initial visit, while regular checkups help monitor changes over time.

Your Dental Exam

Your dentist and hygienist assess your teeth, gums, and oral tissues, with imaging when appropriate.

  • Diagnostic X-rays: Review images when needed to check for decay, bone loss, cysts, tumors, and tooth or root position.
  • Oral cancer screening: Check the face, neck, lips, tongue, throat, gums, and other tissues for concerning changes.
  • Gum disease evaluation: Assess the gums and supporting bone for signs of periodontal disease.
  • Tooth decay examination: Check tooth surfaces for decay using dental instruments.
  • Existing restorations: Evaluate fillings, crowns, and other dental work.

Your Professional Cleaning

Professional cleanings, also called dental prophylaxis, are usually performed by a registered dental hygienist.

  • Tartar removal: Remove hardened plaque, also called calculus, with professional dental instruments. It can collect above and below the gumline.
  • Plaque removal: Clear away the sticky bacterial film that builds up on teeth and can contribute to gum inflammation.
  • Teeth polishing: Remove remaining plaque and surface stains that brushing and scaling may leave behind.
  • Home-care guidance: Discuss brushing, flossing, and ways to care for your smile between visits.
