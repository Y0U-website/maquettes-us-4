meet the Dentist

Thomas M. Vaughn, D.M.D.

Dr. Vaughn graduated number three in his class from UAB School of Dentistry in 2013. After graduating, he did an associateship at Clairmont Cosmetic and Family Dentistry in Birmingham, as a general dentist. He then practiced at Sarrell Dental Center, where he focused on Pediatric Dentistry, serving the needs of children across the state of Alabama.

He has been married to his high school sweetheart, Laura, for fifteen years, and they are both from the Madison area. They have three children, Grace, Molly, and John.

Dr. Vaughn and his family are excited to be back in the Huntsville area and eager to serve the needs of the community they call home.

Meet the Team

Our experienced team takes pride in providing quality dental care in a warm, family-friendly environment. Each member of our team plays an important role in ensuring you have a positive experience and a short wait.

Donna and Cindy will greet you upon arrival and are dedicated to making sure your visit is enjoyable and your insurance claims are processed efficiently. Our hygienists, Hayley, Kristie, and Roxanne, have many years of experience providing routine cleanings and non-surgical periodontal therapy, and they strive to provide thorough yet comfortable care. Tina, our dental assistant, is Dr. Vaughn’s right hand and helps ensure that all procedures run smoothly and efficiently.

It truly is a team effort, and we are all dedicated to serving your needs.
