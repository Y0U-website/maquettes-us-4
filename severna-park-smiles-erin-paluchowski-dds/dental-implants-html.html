What Are Dental Implants?

With a dental implant, rather than a new tooth sitting on top of the gums like with a traditional bridge or denture, it’s anchored into the jawbone using a small titanium post. Once in place, it actually bonds with surrounding bone just like a real tooth root, and then it can be attached to a crown, bridge, or denture.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation : We can find out if dental implants would be a good fit for you at a consultation where we’ll examine your mouth, ask about your goals, and walk you through the treatment.
  • Surgery : Dr. Paluchowski can perform your implant placement surgery right here to deliver a more convenient and cohesive experience. The posts will be placed into your jawbone using a procedure that can be completed in a single appointment in most cases.
  • Healing : Over the next three to six months, your mouth will heal, your implants will meld with the jawbone, and you’ll eventually receive abutments that will serve as connectors to your new teeth.
  • Restoration : The big day has arrived! When your mouth is ready, Dr. Paluchowski will attach your new teeth to your implants, make a few final adjustments, and then you’re done!
