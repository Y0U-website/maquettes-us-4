At Schertz Dental Group, we understand that visiting the dentist can feel overwhelming, especially if it’s your first time.

Our priority is to ease your concerns and make your experience as comfortable as possible.

We’re here to ensure that your visit is smooth, stress-free, and tailored to your needs.

To help us serve you better and make your appointment efficient, please bring any dental X-rays taken within the last six months to your first visit.

We’ll ask you to complete a few simple forms where we’ll gather important information about your medical history, current medications, dental history, and any dental insurance coverage you may have.

These online forms allows us to provide the best possible care from the moment you arrive and avoid the hand cramp before your appointment 😉
