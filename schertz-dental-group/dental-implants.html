How much is a dental implant?

A single tooth dental implant cost $2499. This includes:

  • All necessary xrays and CT scans
  • Removal of a broken, failing or bad tooth
  • Any bone grafting that may be necessary
  • Placement of the implant
  • Permanent placement of the abutment and porcelain cosmetic crown

What if I only need the implant and no tooth removal or bone grafting?

A single tooth implant costs $1699. This includes:

All necessary xrays and CT scans Placement of the implant Permanent placement of the abutment and porcelain cosmetic crown

What if I need to replace all my teeth?

We have different solutions for patients that require replacement of all teeth in the jaw. The costs to replace a full arch of teeth (upper or lower) ranges from $5,000 to $15,000 depending on the situation.

What are the healing and recovery like?

The dental implant procedure is less invasive than tooth removal. Most patients experience mild discomfort for 1-2 days that can be comfortably controlled with over-the-counter medications such as ibuprofen or Tylenol. Usually, an implant placed toward the back of the mouth is left to heal comfortably, while dental implants placed in the front or cosmetic zone have different options for temporary teeth until the implant heals. You will never be without teeth!
