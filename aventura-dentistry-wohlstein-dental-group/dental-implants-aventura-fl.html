Dental Implants Aventura

A dental implant consists of a small titanium post placed into the jawbone, which acts as an artificial tooth root. Once healed, a custom crown , bridge , or denture is attached to restore the missing tooth or teeth.

With proper care, dental implants can last decades—or even a lifetime.

  • Are anchored securely into the jawbone
  • Do not rely on neighboring teeth for support
  • Prevent bone loss and facial collapse

Dental Implant Options in Aventura, FL

Single Dental Implants

A single dental implant is ideal for replacing one missing tooth. After the implant fuses with the jawbone, a custom crown is placed on top, restoring natural appearance and function while preventing nearby teeth from shifting.

Implant Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are secured to dental implants rather than resting on the gums. This provides:

They are an excellent option for patients missing most or all teeth.

  • Improved stability
  • Better chewing ability
  • Enhanced comfort
  • No slipping or adhesives

Implant Supported Bridge

When multiple teeth are missing in a row, implant-supported bridges provide a fixed, long-lasting solution. Implants act as anchors, eliminating the need to grind down healthy teeth.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Also known as full-arch implants, this solution replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth using multiple strategically placed implants. It restores full function, improves facial structure, and dramatically boosts confidence.
