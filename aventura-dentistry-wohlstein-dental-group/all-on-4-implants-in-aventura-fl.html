Most patients leave the office with a secure, functional smile the same day or within 24 hours.

  • Immediate attachment of a temporary fixed arch in many cases
  • Healing and integration, followed by placement of the final custom prosthesis

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

Patients choose All-on-4 implants because they offer significant advantages over traditional dentures and conventional implant methods:

  • Full-arch restoration with only four implants
  • Often no need for bone grafting
  • Faster treatment and recovery time
  • Fixed, non-removable teeth
  • Improved chewing, speech, and comfort
  • Natural appearance and confident smile
  • Long-term durability and stability

Who is a Candidate for All-on-4?

All-on-4 dental implants may be ideal if:

A consultation at Aventura Dentistry is the best way to determine if this treatment is right for you.

  • You are missing most or all teeth in one or both arches
  • Your teeth are failing due to decay or gum disease
  • Traditional dentures feel loose or uncomfortable
  • You’ve been told you lack sufficient bone for standard implants
  • You want a fixed, permanent alternative to dentures
