Computer Guided Dental Implants

This advanced approach removes much of the guesswork involved in traditional implant surgery.

  • Precision Treatment Design: The software determines the optimal implant position, angle, and depth.
  • Guided Implant Placement: Using a custom surgical guide, implants are placed accurately and efficiently.
  • Restoration & Healing: After healing, a custom restoration is attached for a natural, comfortable smile.

Benefits of 3D Computer-Guided Dental Implants in Aventura

  • Increased Precision: Accurate placement improves comfort, aesthetics, and function.
  • Reduced Risk: Lower chance of nerve or tissue damage.
  • Minimally Invasive: Often less cutting and fewer sutures.
  • Faster Recovery: Less post-operative discomfort and downtime.
  • Predictable Results: Digital planning allows for reliable outcomes.
  • Improved Patient Comfort: A smoother, less stressful experience.

Who Is a Candidate for Computer-Guided Dental Implants?

Many patients who qualify for traditional implants are also excellent candidates for computer-guided implant placement. This technology is especially helpful for patients with limited bone, complex anatomy, or dental anxiety. A consultation at our Aventura dental office will determine whether this approach is right for you.
