Brushing the Right Way

Whether you use a manual toothbrush or an electric one, technique matters.

Brush at least twice a day, especially before bedtime.

  • Hold the toothbrush at a slight angle toward the gums
  • Use gentle, circular motions rather than aggressive scrubbing
  • Brush all surfaces of your teeth, including chewing surfaces
  • Clean the inside surfaces just as thoroughly
  • Lightly brush your tongue to help reduce bacteria and bad breath

The Importance of Fluoride Toothpaste

Using fluoride toothpaste is essential for protecting tooth enamel. Fluoride strengthens the outer layer of the teeth, helps prevent cavities, and reduces harmful bacteria in the mouth. In some cases, fluoride can even help reverse early stages of tooth decay.

Antibacterial Mouth Rinses

Antibacterial mouthwashes can be a helpful addition to brushing and flossing. These rinses reduce bacteria, freshen breath, and support gum health. Your dentist or hygienist can recommend the most appropriate rinse based on your specific needs.
