Adult & Pediatric Orthodontics

Orthodontics focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and treating malocclusions or misaligned teeth and jaws, primarily using braces or aligners to improve tooth function and appearance. Smile Every Day specializes in corrective orthodontic procedures and appliances for all ages from toddlers to elderly patients. If you need to schedule an appointment, call our Davie , Plantation , or Tamarac office to get started.

Appliances & Early Interception

Early interception identifies and addresses dental issues in children to help them avoid severe problems in adulthood. By guiding jaw development and making space for permanent teeth, early treatment reduces the need for more complex procedures later on. Call today to schedule a consultation for your little one(s).
