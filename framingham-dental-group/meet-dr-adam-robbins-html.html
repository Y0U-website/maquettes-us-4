Meet Dr. Adam Robbins

Dentist Framingham, MA

Get to Know Dr. Adam Robbins

Dr. Robbins has been practicing in the Worcester and Metrowest area for over 11 years. He places a high value on patient communication and making everyone feel comfortable in his chair. He loves the way dentistry combines hands-on skills, patient interaction, and the biological sciences, all while providing an important service to community members of all ages.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Robbins graduated Cum Laude from Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Previously, he earned his Sc.B at Brown University, where he studied biology. Dr. Robbins is a member of the Worcester District Dental Society, the MA Dental Society, and the American Dental Association.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Robbins loves getting outside with his family and dog. He is an avid reader (ask him for a good recommendation!) and maintains a long streak with the NY Times crossword. His medium of choice, aside from teeth, is clay. He and his wife have been taking pottery classes since 2023.
