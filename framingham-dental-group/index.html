The practice

Dental phobia is a very common problem among people of all ages, which is why our nearby dental team does everything possible to create a welcoming, relaxing environment. If a little extra help is needed, sedation dentistry can help calm frazzled nerves! We offer oral conscious and nitrous oxide dental sedation.

Many patients dream of a gap-free smile, but they shy away from the thought of wearing metal braces. Thankfully, there are image-friendly alternatives in today’s modern world! We offer both Invisalign and ClearCorrect, which feature subtle clear aligner systems.

Even a healthy smile may not project the ideal image you want. In that case, let our dentists guide you through a custom smile makeover plan! We offer several cosmetic services that can brighten, straighten, reshape, and generally improve the appeal of your teeth in lasting ways.

Dental emergencies never tend to happen at convenient times. Thankfully, our team in your area is prepared for the worst! We offer same-day appointments whenever possible to alleviate discomfort quickly and stabilize teeth in serious situations, so don’t hesitate to contact us right away.
