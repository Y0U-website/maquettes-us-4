All-on-X Dental Implants

If you are missing most or all of your teeth, All-on-X dental implants offer a stable, natural-looking, and long-lasting solution. This advanced full-arch restoration replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth using as few as four to six strategically placed implants.

At our office, we provide comprehensive All-on-X treatment—from consultation to final restoration—so you can regain your smile, confidence, and ability to eat comfortably.

What Is All-on-X?

All-on-X is a full-arch implant treatment that uses multiple dental implants to support a fixed bridge. Instead of replacing each tooth individually, a full set of teeth is secured to implants that act as artificial tooth roots.

The “X” refers to the number of implants used, which varies depending on your bone structure and treatment needs.

This treatment is commonly recommended for patients who:

  • Are missing most or all teeth
  • Have failing teeth
  • Currently wear dentures and want a more stable option
  • Have significant bone loss but want to avoid extensive grafting

Benefits of All-on-X

✅ Fixed & Non-Removable

Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-X teeth are securely attached and do not move.

✅ Natural Look & Feel

Designed to resemble natural teeth in shape, color, and function.

✅ Improved Chewing Ability

Eat many of the foods you may have avoided with dentures.

✅ Bone Preservation

Implants stimulate the jawbone, helping prevent bone loss.

✅ Long-Term Solution

With proper care, implants can last many years.

The Treatment Process

1. Consultation & 3D Imaging

We perform a comprehensive exam, digital scans, and treatment planning.

2. Implant Placement

Dental implants are strategically placed in the jawbone.

3. Temporary Fixed Teeth

In many cases, a temporary set of teeth is delivered the same day or shortly after surgery.

4. Final Restoration

After healing (typically 3–6 months), your final custom-designed teeth are placed.

Are You a Candidate?

A personalized consultation is required to determine candidacy.

  • Are in good general health
  • Have sufficient bone (or are a candidate for grafting)
  • Do not have uncontrolled medical conditions
  • Are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene

What to Expect After Surgery

  • Mild swelling and discomfort for a few days
  • Soft-food diet during healing
  • Detailed post-operative instructions provided
  • Regular follow-up visits to monitor healing
