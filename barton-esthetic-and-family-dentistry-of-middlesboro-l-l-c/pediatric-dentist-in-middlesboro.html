How can I give my children the…

Perhaps the best aspect of bringing your child to the dentist is that it harbors an appreciation for oral care. This appreciation will inspire your child to care for their oral health throughout their lifetime, which benefits their overall health.

At Barton Esthetic and Family Dentistry , we make recommendations and give tips on how your family can best care for their teeth. For example, drinking fluoridated water can prevent cavities. Receiving dental sealants in childhood also prevents cavities and can save your family money by avoiding expensive dental care. We recommend these sealants to our young clients because over half of children in this country have had at least one cavity. If your child develops a cavity, we offer gentle and effective treatments.

Americans lose millions of school hours and billions of dollars in lost productivity at work annually because of emergency dental appointments. These appointments also disrupt a family’s regular routine. Avoid frustration and lost time by implementing good oral care practices and establishing a relationship with a dentist.
