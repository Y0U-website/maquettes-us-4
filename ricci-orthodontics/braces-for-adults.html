WHY SHOULD I CORRECT CROOKED TEETH AS AN ADULT?

There are also reasons for teeth to shift as an adult that need to be addressed. Those with late wisdom teeth eruptions commonly experience teeth shifting. Other dental issues may result from car accidents or other events causing dental trauma.

  • Your smile is often the first feature that people notice. The best way to make a great impression is when you have a smile that you are proud to share with the world.
  • A beautiful, symmetrical smile helps with your confidence when it matters most. Successful orthodontic treatment will boost your self-confidence, improving your relationship with others and giving you the freedom to smile without holding back.

ARE ADULT BRACES DIFFERENT?

No different than teenagers, adults can benefit from a more beautiful smile and improved oral health from wearing dental braces . But because adult mouths are no longer growing, orthodontic treatment may take slightly longer than for teens. Most of our adult clients require wearing braces for about a year and a half to three years.

Once we remove your adult braces, you’ll need to continue to wear a retainer. This will work to preserve all of the benefits of your newly straightened teeth.
