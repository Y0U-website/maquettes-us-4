TruDenta®

TruDenta® is a new, comprehensive system that allows us to treat a variety of orofacial disorders. The orofacial disorders that TruDenta® helps with include vertigo, migraines, headaches, and tinnitus. If you suffer from these problems, ask our team if TruDenta® is right for you.

Two-Phase Orthodontics

Two-phase orthodontics provides young patients their best chance at the proper function, health, and aesthetic as they grow. The first stage corrects abnormal jaw growth while the second helps with permanent teeth erupting. It helps to reduce the need for extractions later on while also allowing the teeth and jaws to function properly.
