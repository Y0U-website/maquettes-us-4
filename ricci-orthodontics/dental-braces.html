TYPES OF BRACES

There are many different types of braces outside of traditional braces available now to achieve the ideal smile and bite. Read about your options for dental braces to determine which would work best for you:

  • Ceramic Braces : made out of a special clear material so as to not absorb stains
  • Colored Braces : rubber bands apply additional pressure as well as adding a personal touch
  • Lingual Braces : positioned on the back of teeth that are harder to notice
  • Metal Braces : affordable, durable, and require less time to fix dental issues
  • Spacers for Braces : rubber bands keep back molars from fitting too close together
  • Wilckodontics Accelerated Braces : metal braces and surgery correct teeth four times faster
