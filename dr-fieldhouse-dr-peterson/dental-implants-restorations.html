Dental Implants

Dentures and bridges are effective tooth-replacement options, but neither can match the superiority of dental implants. These restorations are anchored in the jawbone to provide optimal stability and comfort, just as your natural teeth do.

  • Abutment: It connects the implant post to the final restoration, providing a stable base for your crown or denture.
  • Custom crown: It’s the functional, top-most part of your dental implant. It’s custom-crafted to match your natural teeth for a seamless appearance.

What are the implant options available?

La Grange Park Dentistry offers several dental implant options tailored to your unique needs.

  • Single-tooth implant: The dentist combines a titanium post with a dental crown to replace an individual missing tooth with a natural-looking, standalone solution.
  • Implant-supported bridge: This solution replaces a few missing teeth in a row by anchoring a custom restoration to implants rather than relying on neighboring teeth.
  • Full-arch dental implants: Replace an entire arch of missing teeth with 4-6 dental implants.

Are dental implants worth it?

Missing teeth is more than a cosmetic issue. Imagine missing conversations, dodging photos, and avoiding your favorite foods because of tooth loss! Gaps in your smile disrupt your everyday life, but dental implants can help you regain your smile and life.

Here’s a closer look at the benefits of dental implants in La Grange, Illinois.

  • Natural look: Your dental implants blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, making them virtually indistinguishable when you smile or speak.
  • Regain your confidence: Feel like yourself again with stable, comfortable teeth that feel like your own.
  • Chew confidently: Regain the chewing power to eat all your favorite foods again. From apples to juicy steak and salads, dental implants help you regain the freedom to enjoy meals again.
  • Preserve your bone structure: The stimulation from dental implants helps preserve the health and stability of your jawbone.
  • Long-term satisfaction: Dental implants can last many years, even a lifetime, with good maintenance.
