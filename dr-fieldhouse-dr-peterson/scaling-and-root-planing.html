Scaling and Root Planing

Many patients don’t realize how deep cleanings improve their oral health. Gum disease is progressive and can cause tooth loss without proper treatment. SRP is an effective treatment for stopping the infection in its tracks, saving your teeth, gums, and overall health.

  • Root planing: After scaling, the dentist polishes the roots of your teeth to create a smooth surface. Harmful bacteria have a hard time clinging to polished surfaces, so planing prevents future infections. It also encourages healthy gums to reattach to your teeth, closing the deep pockets over time.

What can I expect during scaling and root planing?

Patients may experience swelling, bleeding, and tooth sensitivity after treatment. Our team will provide you with post-op instructions to help you heal faster and without complications. We’ll prescribe medications to manage pain and infection. We’ll also recommend specific diet and oral hygiene practices to maximize your healing. Patients recover within 5-7 days, but more time is needed for full healing and re-attachment.

What are the benefits of deep teeth cleaning?

Your oral and systemic health are interconnected. So, SRP not only improves your smile but also your overall well-being.

Here are the benefits of deep teeth cleaning in La Grange Park, IL:

  • Effective treatment: Many patients respond to deep cleaning and rarely need surgical interventions.
  • Improved comfort: Receded gums can cause extreme sensitivity and pain when chewing. Deep teeth cleaning promotes healing, restoring your comfort when eating or speaking.
  • Better aesthetics: Eliminating unsightly pockets and yellow-looking tartar restores your beautiful smile.
  • Fresher breath: Treatment eliminates odor-causing bacteria lodged in the deep pockets to promote fresh breath.
  • Prevents further damage: Deep teeth cleaning helps gums heal naturally, protecting your teeth and jawbone from damage.
  • Improved systemic health: SRP reduces the risk of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, respiratory infections, and other conditions associated with periodontal disease.
  • Faster healing: Many patients experience notable improvements within days, and post-op complications are minimal.
