Laura Wittenauer, DDS

Dr. Laura Wittenauer is welcoming new patients to her practice of esthetic and comprehensive dentistry. After graduating from the University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry, she established her practice in Lawrence, Kansas. She and her family relocated to Southern California to allow her husband, Rick, to attend USC School of Dentistry Endodontic Residency program.

Dr. Laura’s practice focuses on building relationships with her patients by taking the time to listen to the needs and values of each person and then educate them on the best treatment to achieve optimum oral health.

Dr. Laura is active in many professional organizations including the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, American Dental Association, California Dental Association and the Orange County Dental Society.

Dr. Wittenauer and her husband Rick enjoy spending time with their adult children. As a family they enjoy attending USC and KU sporting events, working out, running, cooking, golfing and giving back to the community where Dr. Laura was raised.
