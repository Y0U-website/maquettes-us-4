Meet Our Orthodontist

Dr. Brandon Khor

Dr. Khor graduated second in his class at the OHSU School of Dentistry and then went on to complete a residency in orthodontics at Virginia Commonwealth University. Dr. Khor is a member of the prestigious national dental honor society, Omicron Kappa Upsilon, whose members are chosen based on their scholarship, professionalism and character to serve as examples among their peers.

Dr. Khor is back home in the Northwest. He brings a wealth of current knowledge and expertise in the newest orthodontic treatment techniques and advancements in approaches. Most importantly, Dr. Khor brings a passion for excellence and a love of helping people change their smiles for the better.
