Early orthodontic treatment, also known as Phase 1 treatment, is an essential step in orthodontics for kids. Starting orthodontic care at an early age can help identify potential issues and correct them before they become bigger problems later in life.

What is

Phase 1 Treatment?

Phase 1 treatment typically begins around the age of 7, when most children have a mix of primary (baby) teeth and adult teeth. Early orthodontic treatment focuses on identifying and addressing dental issues that could complicate future treatments. By working with our orthodontist in Vancouver and Ridgefield, we can help guide the development of your child’s teeth to promote better long-term oral health.

Benefits of

Phase 1 Treatment

Phase 1 treatment helps us detect issues that could make future orthodontic care more difficult or require more intensive treatment down the line. By addressing these issues early, we can reduce the need for more complex procedures later.

One of the key goals of early orthodontic treatment is to create enough space for adult teeth to come in properly. This is particularly important for permanent teeth that may otherwise be crowded or improperly positioned.

If there are any issues that could affect the long-term health of your child’s adult teeth—such as bite problems or misalignment—Phase 1 treatment can help correct them while your child is still growing. This helps to ensure that their adult teeth will come in correctly and remain healthy.
