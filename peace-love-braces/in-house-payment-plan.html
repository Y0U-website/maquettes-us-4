In-House Payment Plan at Peace Love Braces

Orthodontic treatment is an investment in your smile and your confidence. At Peace Love Braces, we believe cost should never prevent you or your child from getting the care you deserve. That’s why we offer a convenient in-house payment plan designed to make orthodontic treatment affordable and stress-free. Instead of paying the entire treatment fee up front, our monthly payment plan allows you to spread the cost over the course of treatment.

Monthly Payments Made Simple

Our in-house payment plan is created with our patients in mind. It allows families to start orthodontic treatment right away while paying for care gradually over time.

Ability to use your FSA or HSA card for payments

This means you can focus on achieving a healthier, more confident smile without worrying about financial barriers.

How the Payment Plan Works

Getting started with our in-house payment plan is simple and straightforward.

During your consultation, our team will evaluate your smile and create a customized orthodontic treatment plan.

Once your treatment plan is confirmed, we will review your monthly payment plan.

A down payment is due on the day your treatment begins.

Your first monthly payment begins the following month, and payments continue throughout your treatment period.

Payment Plan Length

Orthodontic treatment times can vary depending on each patient’s needs. For many patients receiving traditional braces, treatment typically lasts about 18 to 24 months. To make payments manageable, we usually align your payment plan with the length of your treatment or close to it. This helps keep monthly payments predictable and easier to budget.

Designed for Your Convenience

Our in-house payment plan eliminates many of the barriers patients face with traditional financing options. Because we do not require credit checks and do not charge interest, families can start orthodontic care sooner without the stress of complicated approval processes. Additionally, if you have a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Health Savings Account (HSA), you can conveniently use those funds to make your monthly payments.
