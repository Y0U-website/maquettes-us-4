Longbranch Dental Center is Greenville’s family and cosmetic dentist

If you want to have a smile that looks great and is healthy as well, it’s important that you visit Longbranch Dental Center for expert cosmetic dentistry. Serving the greater Greenville area, the cosmetic and family dentists at Longbranch Dental Center are committed to providing patients with smiles that they can be proud of.

Dr. Weaks and his staff understand that getting the dental care you need can be a financial worry for many people. It’s for this reason that our Greenville area office provides cosmetic dentistry financing options and payment plans for all patients who may need assistance. It’s our goal to make your treatment process as comfortable, pleasant, and problem-free as possible.

If you live in the Greenville area and wonder if cosmetic dentistry is the right option for you, contact Longbranch Dental Center today. We look forward to meeting with you in person.
