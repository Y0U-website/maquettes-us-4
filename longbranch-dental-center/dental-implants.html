Replace Missing Teeth in Greenville with a Dental Implant

Our cosmetic dentists will improve your smile and help to restore you confidence

Do you have a missing tooth? A dental implant is a popular solution replace missing teeth and our highly trained Greenville cosmetic dentists at Longbranch Dental Center will work with you to repair your smile.

For Greenville patients not wanting dentures or a fixed bridge and crown to replace a missing tooth, a dental implant is a good option. Dental implants can fill in the gap between missing teeth and stop the growth of bacteria that can flourish in the open gum. Implants are made from medical-grade titanium that fuses with the jawbone, securely keeping the implant in place. Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth and are the permanent solution to missing teeth.

Dental implant surgery is performed at our Greenville office. The entire process can take between six to nine months, depending on how long it takes your jaw to heal from each procedure. We will work with your schedule to ensure you don’t feel rushed during your treatments.

Advantages of dental implants

Most dental insurance providers don’t cover the cost of dental implants. To help reduce the out-of-pocket expenses associated with oral surgery, we offer an in-house dental membership plan and a variety of financing options . Our friendly staff will be able to assist you with selecting the best plan for your dental needs.

  • A long-lasting solution for a missing tooth
  • Feels and looks like your natural teeth
  • Closes the gap between missing teeth
  • Reduces the likelihood of gum disease and tooth decay by preventing bacteria from forming in the open gum
  • Restores your beautiful smile
