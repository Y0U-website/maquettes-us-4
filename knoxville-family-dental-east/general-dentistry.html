General Dentistry

By prioritizing general dentistry, you are taking an essential step towards maintaining a healthy, beautiful smile and overall well-being.

Regular visits to our family dental centre allow us to monitor your oral health, catch potential issues early, and provide the necessary treatments to keep your smile radiant.

  • Preventive Care: Regular cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants help protect your teeth from decay and gum disease.
  • Restorative Treatments: We provide fillings, crowns, bridges, and dentures to restore the function and appearance of damaged or missing teeth.
  • Diagnostic Procedures: Our state-of-the-art imaging technology allows us to detect and diagnose any dental problems early on, ensuring prompt and effective treatment.

Services Offered at Knoxville Family Dental

At our family dental center, we offer a comprehensive range of general family dentistry services to meet the needs of every member of your family. Our services include:

Routine Cleanings and Examinations: Regular check-ups and professional cleanings are essential for maintaining optimal oral health and detecting potential issues early on.

Fillings and Restorations: We use high-quality materials to restore decayed or damaged teeth, ensuring long-lasting and natural-looking results.

Root Canal Treatments: Our skilled professionals can effectively treat infected or inflamed tooth pulp, relieving pain and saving your natural teeth.

Tooth Extractions: In cases where a tooth cannot be saved, we perform gentle and safe extractions to prevent further complications.

Dental Sealants: These protective coatings are applied to the chewing surfaces of molars, acting as a barrier against decay.

Periodontal (Gum) Treatments: We offer effective treatments to address gum disease, including scaling and root planing, to restore your gums to optimal health.

Oral Cancer Screenings: Regular screenings allow for early detection and successful treatment of oral cancers.

Dental Emergencies: Our team is equipped to handle dental emergencies promptly, providing relief and preventing further complications.

Cosmetic and General Dentistry Services

In addition to our comprehensive general dentistry services, our dental center in Knoxville also offers a range of cosmetic dentistry options to help you achieve a stunning, confident smile.

From professional teeth whitening to veneers and cosmetic bonding, our team at Knoxville Family Dental can transform the appearance of your teeth while prioritizing your overall oral health.

Teeth Whitening: Our safe and effective whitening treatments can brighten your smile by several shades, restoring the natural, youthful youthful luster of your teeth.

Veneers: These thin, custom-made shells are bonded to the front surfaces of your teeth, instantly improving their shape, color, and alignment.

Cosmetic Bonding: This minimally invasive procedure uses tooth-colored resin to enhance the appearance of chipped, discolored, or irregularly shaped teeth.

Our skilled cosmetic dentists will work closely with you to understand your goals and develop a personalized treatment plan that harmoniously blends aesthetics with optimal dental function.
