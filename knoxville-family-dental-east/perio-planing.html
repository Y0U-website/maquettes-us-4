Benefits of Perio Scaling and Root Planing

Undergoing scaling and root planing offers numerous benefits for patients with moderate to severe gum disease. These include:

By taking a proactive approach and seeking professional treatment, patients can effectively manage their gum disease, protect their natural teeth, and maintain good overall oral health for years to come.

  • Improved gum health: By removing harmful bacteria, plaque, and tartar, this procedure helps reduce inflammation and allows the gums to heal and regain a healthy state.
  • Prevention of further progression: Scaling and root planing disrupts the cycle of gum disease, preventing it from advancing to more severe stages and potentially leading to tooth loss.
  • Gum tissue regeneration: In some cases, the treatment can stimulate the regrowth of gum tissue, helping to reduce the depth of periodontal pockets and improve the attachment between the gums and teeth.

Aftercare and Maintenance

After undergoing dental scaling and root planing, proper aftercare and maintenance are essential to ensure the best possible outcome and long-term gum health. Here are some important steps to follow:

By committing to proper aftercare and scheduling regular check-ups and cleanings, you can maximize the benefits of your periodontal treatment and significantly reduce the risk of gum disease recurrence or progression.
