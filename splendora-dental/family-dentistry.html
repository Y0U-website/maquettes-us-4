Splendora Family Dentist

At Splendora Dental, we are concerned with oral hygiene and the health of your teeth at every stage of life. Our Family Dentists are specially trained to address not only the unique needs of children of all ages, but also the needs of their parents. We understand that family dental health is all about you, our patient.

We recognize how stressful going to the dentist can be for children, that’s why we take extra care to create an inviting environment that will put you entire family at ease. Our Family Dentists are specially trained in pediatric dentistry to help children develop a positive association with the dentist! Our services include Cleaning, Exam, X-Ray, Cavity Identification and Filling, Treatment for Gum Disease, Fluoride Treatments, and more! Please give us a call or schedule an online appointment today! We can’t wait for you to meet our warm and inviting staff!
