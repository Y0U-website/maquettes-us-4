EARLY ORTHODONTIC TREATMENT

At Parkland Orthodontics, we understand the importance of early orthodontic treatment for setting the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles. Our early treatment options are designed to address issues like crowding, misalignment, and bite problems before they become more complex.

By starting treatment at an optimal age, we can guide jaw growth, improve facial symmetry, and create space for incoming permanent teeth, reducing the need for more extensive treatments later. Our compassionate team works closely with parents to ensure each child receives personalized, gentle care, making the journey to a confident smile both positive and effective.

When do I need orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic issues should always be taken seriously. They not only impact your facial aesthetics but can greatly affect your oral function and your overall health. Orthodontic treatments like Braces help you get a healthier and straighter smile, which in turn, improves your overall oral function. Misaligned teeth cause bite issues, and they are harder to clean. This can increase the chance of tooth decay.

With the advancement in technology, it has never been easier to achieve a beautiful and healthy smile. We offer clear braces, clear aligners, custom braces, as well as lingual braces. Orthodontics is the best way to invest in a healthy and radiant smile. Contact Parkland Orthodontics today and let us help you address your orthodontic issues and get you the smile you deserve!

BREATHING ISSUES

BITE ISSUES

Major bite problems, including crossbite, overbite, underbite, etc., can be cured with various orthodontic treatments.

CROWDED TEETH

These not only hamper your facial aesthetics but make cleaning your teeth tougher. Orthodontic treatment like braces helps to rectify this.

GAPS BETWEEN TEETH

This issue can be rectified through effective orthodontic treatments.
