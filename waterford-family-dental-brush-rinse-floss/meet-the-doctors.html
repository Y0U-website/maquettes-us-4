Dr. Tiffany Danyal

Dr. Tiffany Danyal believes in the power of a beautiful, healthy smile. She thrives on affecting the lives of her patients in a positive way. Genuine smiles are exceedingly important to her, which is why she focuses on treating her patients with respect and compassion while only settling for using the latest knowledge and technology in her treatments. Seeing a smile on the faces of her patients at the end of their appointments is one of her greatest rewards.

• Graduated with Honors from Detroit Mercy with BS in Biology at the young age of 19

• University of Detroit-Mercy School of Dentistry where she earned her DDS in 2012

Dr. Sarah Rassam

Dr. Sarah obtained her Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of North Florida, and Doctorate of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD) from the University of Florida. After graduating dental school, she moved to Michigan with her husband, where they decided to settle down. Dr. Sarah loves spending quality time with family and friends, especially with her little daughter, Grace.

Dr. Sarah strives to provide the best patient-focused and comprehensive care, treating every patient with honesty and compassion. Her top priority is providing quality dental care to her patients, while caring for the individual behind the teeth, treating them like family.

Dr. Amogh Naik

Dr. Amogh Naik has always been drawn to dentistry from a young age. He loved the idea of working closely with people and being a valuable part of their health care. He believes that developing long term relationships allows him to help his patients achieve their long term dental goals. His philosophy for dentistry is simple Do what is best for the patient, and provide care to the highest standard.

Dr. Naik grew up in Grand Blanc, Michigan. He attended Western Michigan University, where he studied biomedical sciences and chemistry. He then went on to obtain his dental degree from the University of Michigan, and afterwards completed a residency in general dentistry with the University of Detroit Mercy and St. Johns Hospital.

He has been practicing in the Metro Detroit area since 2014.

Dr. Naik believes in the importance of lifelong learning, and is continually seeking higher education to advance his knowledge and skill set to provide the best possible care for patients. He has completed several courses in restorative dentistry through The Pacific Aesthetic Continuum, SPEAR, and The Dawson Academy. He has also been trained through the AAID Maxicourse in implant dentistry, and is certified in Invisalign® Treatment.

In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his wife Jackie, and black lab Louie. He loves to travel, play golf, tennis, and snowboard.

Dr. Michael Saba

Dr. Saba obtained a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Wayne State University and a Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) from University of Pittsburgh. Since graduation, Dr. Saba is a proud member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of General Dentistry, Michigan Dental Association, and Macomb Dental Society, where he is actively serving as the Macomb Journal’s Editor and board member.

Dr. Saba enjoys bringing his patient’s biggest and brightest smiles forward in any way that he can. His annual continuing education allows him to always provide the latest and greatest dental procedures to bring the most benefit to his patients. He excels in cosmetic dentistry cases, such as bondings and crowns, as well as full rehabilitation cases with dental implants. Dr. Saba is proud to be certified as a top level provider in Invisalign clear aligners, which offers his patients services from minor cosmetic adjustments to moderate and severe orthodontic rehabilitation.

Dr. Saba enjoys the outdoors and enjoying quality time with his wife and three daughters. His musical instrument of choice is percussion (ask him about it!). He is a faith-based man who is involved in his parish and enjoys giving back to the community. He lives by the saying Treat others the way you would want to be treated. He invites you to visit him and become a part of his patient family!

Dr. Marian Israil

Dr. Marian Israil is from West Bloomfield, MI. She obtained her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. Dr. Israil’s interest in dentistry began at a young age. The reason she chose dentistry is due to her passion for the field, especially in enhancing patients’ oral health and to help patients feel proud and confident of their smile. She performs a wide range of dental procedures including crowns and bridges, root canals, cosmetic procedures, and Invisalign.

Dr. Israil loves to build long term relationships with her patients to make them feel comfortable during every dental visit. One of her main goals is to educate patients of all ages on the importance of oral health and reinforcing preventive care.

Dr. Israil spends her free time with her husband, daughter family & friends. She also enjoys traveling and working out!
