How Clear Aligners Work

During your first visit, Dr. Samia will conduct a comprehensive examination of your teeth and discuss your orthodontic concerns. If clear aligners are deemed suitable for your specific smile, digital impressions or 3D scans will be taken and imported into virtual mapping software. These images serve as the foundation for creating your custom aligners, ensuring a precise and comfortable fit once you’re ready to begin treatment. The computerized program maps your tooth movement so that we can print custom trays that progressively move your smile as you change them out every two weeks.

Benefits of Clear Aligners

It’s not hard to see why so many adults choose clear aligners over traditional braces for straightening their teeth. The treatment has many advantages that can potentially make it the better option for you and your smile. If you’re still trying to decide whether clear aligners are a worthwhile investment in your case, here’s a closer look at some of the most notable benefits they have to offer.

Improved Appearance

One of the most obvious upsides of clear aligners is that they aren’t as noticeable as traditional braces. Because the aligners are made out of see-through materials, the average person is highly unlikely to notice that you’re wearing them. Thus, you can complete the tooth-straightening process without having to worry about attention being drawn to your smile for the wrong reasons.

Enhanced Comfort

Compared to traditional braces, clear aligners are less prone to irritating the soft tissues inside your mouth. This is because they don’t have any metal brackets or wires to worry about; instead, they are fully composed of smooth, biocompatible plastic. As a result, you can look forward to a more comfortable orthodontic treatment.

Conveniently Removable

Traditional braces are designed to remain attached to your teeth 24/7. Clear aligners, on the other hand, are made so that you can take them out of your mouth at any time. Because of this fact, there’s no need to avoid your favorite foods during your treatment; you can simply take your aligners out right before a meal or snack, thus allowing you to eat or drink anything you want.

Easier to Clean

It can often be tricky to clean traditional braces; there are many nooks and crannies where food can become trapped. In comparison, maintaining clear aligners is much simpler, as you can just take them out of your mouth to give them a gentle, thorough brushing. On top of that, the removable nature of your clear aligners means they won’t get in the way when you’re brushing and flossing your teeth.

Fewer Office Visits

Clear aligners don’t usually require as many follow-up visits as traditional braces since there’s no need to periodically adjust them. You’ll generally only need to visit our office every 6 to 8 weeks. These appointments will give us a chance to confirm that your teeth are moving as intended.

Shorter Timelines for Treatment

The exact timeline for your treatment will depend on the nature of your alignment issues and how severe they are. With that being said, while braces sometimes have to be worn for years to achieve the desired results, the average clear aligner treatment can often be completed in just 12 to 18 months, meaning you won’t have to wait as long for the straighter, more confident smile that you deserve!

Who Can Clear Aligners Help?

If you are considering clear aligners, we invite you to visit Dr. Samia for an individual assessment. Our friendly and knowledgeable team will guide you through the entire process, from initial consultation to the completion of your treatment. In the meantime, you can read on to learn more about the common orthodontic problems clear aligners can fix!

Crowded Teeth

Crowding can make it harder to clean your teeth and can cause irregular enamel wear. Over time, this leads to cavities, gum disease, and even TMJ disorder. That’s why we recommend discussing your treatment options with Dr. Samia sooner rather than later. The longer you wait, the greater your risk becomes.

Gaps Between Teeth

Irregular spacing in your smile not only affects your appearance but can also impact the security and comfort of your bite. The good news is that clear aligners can close any gaps, giving you a smile that not only looks better, but is healthier overall.

Overbite

An overbite occurs when your upper teeth overlap the lower ones more than normal. This can cause uneven tooth wear, jaw discomfort, or speech issues. Clear aligners gradually reposition the teeth to create a balanced bite. If you have a mild to moderate overbite, Dr. Samia can design a personalized treatment plan to correct the issue, often using clear aligner accessories like elastics or attachments.

Underbite

With an underbite, your lower teeth extend in front of the upper teeth when biting down. This can lead to chewing difficulties, jaw strain, and tooth damage over time. Clear aligners can help guide the teeth into better alignment. Like overbites, the treatment often requires the use of additional accessories to correct jaw placement.

Crossbite

A crossbite happens when some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth instead of outside. This misalignment can cause gum recession, enamel wear, and overall discomfort, not to mention making oral hygiene much more difficult. Our team uses precise, step-by-step adjustments to achieve a healthier, more functional bite with clear aligners.

Open Bite

When your upper and lower teeth don’t touch when your mouth is closed, it’s called an open bite. This alignment issue can make biting and chewing less efficient, affecting digestion. Clear aligners, in conjunction with accessories like elastics, work by moving your teeth into proper contact, ensuring comfort and long-term stability for your bite.
