The practice

Dr. Maryam Torabian has called Sacramento home since 2001. She graduated summa cum laude from California State University, Sacramento, before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) with high honors from the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco.

During her studies, she was inducted into the Tau Kappa Omega Dental Honor Society. She then began her career as an Associate Dentist in Berkeley while serving as a part-time faculty member teaching Dental Anatomy at UOP — an experience that deepened her passion for evidence-based, high-quality dentistry.

Dr. Raveena Kanwar is a proud Sacramento native. She grew up in the area, attended St. Francis High School, and later continued her education at UCLA. She went on to pursue her dental degree at Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 2017.

Following dental school, Dr. Kanwar completed an additional year of advanced training through a General Practice Residency at the Cleveland VA Medical Center. During this residency, she became proficient in a wide range of procedures and developed a strong interest in oral surgery.

In 2019, Dr. Kanwar returned home to be closer to family and friends and has been serving patients in the Sacramento community ever since. With seven years of experience, she is dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-focused care in a comfortable and supportive environment.

Outside the office, Dr. Kanwar loves to travel, explore new places, and spend time with her mini goldendoodle, Nemo.
