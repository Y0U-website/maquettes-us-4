Dr. Heather Wright

Dr Heather Wright was born in Louisville and has lived here for most of her life. She attended Sacred Heart Academy and Transylvania University before she earned her Doctorate in Dental medicine from the University of Louisville in 2005. She then completed advanced training in a general practice residency in 2006 also at the University of Louisville. She is committed to providing quality care in a gentle and caring environment.

Dr Wright is a member of many professional organizations and study clubs. In 2016 she was recognized by her peers as one to the top general dentists in Louisville . She attends continuing education throughout the year to ensure she is providing the highest level of care. When she is not is the office, Dr Wright enjoys spending time with her husband and two daughters. She also enjoys running, gardening, and spending time with Mello her white Labrador retriever.
