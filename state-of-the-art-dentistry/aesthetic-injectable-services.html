NEUROTOXINS

We offer a variety of neurotoxins that combat the formation of wrinkles and repeated contractions and of facial muscles. Through temporary relaxation, toxins are used to soften and improve the appearance of facial lines.

BOTOX®

The most popular, FDA-approved injectable, BOTOX® Cosmetic softens the look of wrinkles and facial lines. Most commonly used on forehead wrinkles, crow’s feet, frown lines and the horizontal lines between the eyebrows. Results last for three to six months.

Dysport

Dypsort is a prescription injectable used for wrinkle prevention. Dysport® creates a natural look of smoother lines on the between the eyebrows or for moderate to sever frown lines, temporarily diminishing wrinkles. Results last for three to six months.

Xeomin

Xeomin, an FDA-approved injectable, treats moderate to sever frown lines between the eyes and is made from the same toxins as BOTOX®. Xeomin is most commonly used to block muscle movement for all areas of the face. Results last for three to six months.

DERMAL FILLER

We treat patients with dermal filler to restore volume loss, reduce the signs of aging and increase fullness. Dr. Wright will recommend the best dermal fillers for her patients based on the area of concern and aesthetic goals.

Juvederm Products by Allergan

JUVEDERM® XC

An injectable used to treat moderate to severe facial wrinkles and folds by correcting the parentheses lines around the nose and mouth, by adding volume to the skin and giving a smoother appearance. Results last up to one year.

JUVÉDERM® Ultra Plus & Ultra XC

A gel injectable used to add fullness and plump lips. Ultra XC is also approved to smooth and correct moderate to severe parentheses lines, such as the nasolabial folds. Results last up to one year.

JUVÉDERM VOLUMA® XC

A gel injectable that instantly adds volume beneath the skin to lift and contour the cheeks. VOLUMA XC is recommended for age-related mid-face volume loss to provide a more youthful profile. Results last up to two years.

JUVEDERM VOLBELLA® XC

A gel injectable used to augment lip fullness and correct lipstick or smoker lines while smoothing the skin around the mouth. Results last up to one year.

JUVÉDERM VOLLURE™ XC

A gel injectable used to treat moderate to severe facial wrinkles, volume loss and folds, like the “parentheses lines” around the nose and mouth. VOLLURE™ XC temporarily corrects facial wrinkles and gives a smoother appearance. Results last up to 18 months.

Restylane® Products by Galderma

Restylane® Refyne

Refyne® is used for the treatment of mild to moderate lines and wrinkles in the nasolabial folds and marionette lines, but also for lip enhancement. Results are natural in appearance and last up to one years.

Restylane® Silk

Restylane® Silk smoothes wrinkles and fine lines to encourage a more youthful appearance. The Silk® product includes .3 % lidocaine.

Restylane® Lyft

This filler is primarily used to add volume to cheeks, smooth smile lines and also add volume to the back of hands. The hyaluronic particles in Lyft are among the largest in the Restylane group of fillers. Lyft is recommended for use in combination with Restylane®.

Restylane® Defyne

This filler from Restylane® is used for the treatment of moderate to severe smile lines or laugh lines. Refyne can also be used to replenish lost facial volume. Results last up to one year.
