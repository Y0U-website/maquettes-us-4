New Patients

Our office accepts most major PPO dental insurance policies, including Medicare PPO dental supplement plans. We do not accept HMO plans or Medicaid. When you call to schedule, we will get your insurance policy information and verify your coverage prior to your appointment- so please have your card ready.

  • We are accepting new patients! Please call (502-426-5344) to inquiry about our openings. Your first appointment may be the most important time we’ll spend with you. We’ll discuss your goals and your past and present dental and medical history. Expect to spend an hour and a half to two hours with us. Please arrive 10-15 minutes ahead of your scheduled appointment to fill out new patient paperwork or you can request the paperwork through email. On your first visit you can expect: A pleasant and productive experience
  • Questions about your concerns and your dental and medical health history
  • Answers about how lifestyle factors can affect your dental health
  • A thorough, yet gentle, examination of your teeth, gums and mouth
  • $100 off in-office whitening if you book the service within the next month
  • Prevention is the best policy for dental health, so don’t wait to make an appointment.
