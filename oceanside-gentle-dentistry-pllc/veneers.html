HOW LONG DO DENTAL VENEERS LAST?

Veneers have the power to last a lifetime! Teeth veneers are strong. They are particularly resilient to chipping and staining.

The job of the patient is to carefully preserve the veneers. Keep up with your regular flossing and cleaning, at least twice a year, and see our hygienist.

The veneers could be harmed by chewing on hard items just like they hurt your teeth. In fact, we usually say it takes as much force as a natural tooth to crack a porcelain veneer.

To customize each and every porcelain veneer to perfection, we only use the highest quality porcelain. At Oceanside Gentle Dentistry, a brand new smile is waiting for you!
