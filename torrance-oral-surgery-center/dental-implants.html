Dental Implants Overview And Benefits

Dental implants replace missing teeth with a strong, fixed option. They are made of titanium, a material your body accepts well. The implant fuses with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This creates a stable base for a new tooth.

Implants let you eat, speak, and smile without worry. They also protect your oral health. When a tooth is lost, nearby teeth can shift and bone can shrink. An implant stops this process. Unlike a bridge, an implant does not require altering healthy adjacent teeth. It is a long-term solution that can last for decades with proper care.

Who Is A Suitable Candidate For Dental Implants

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you have one or more missing teeth. Good overall health and enough jawbone density are important factors. Certain conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking can affect healing. Dr. Yagoubian evaluates your medical history and uses 3D imaging to check your bone structure.

If you have bone loss, you may still qualify. Bone grafting can rebuild the foundation needed for a successful implant. Age is not a barrier for most healthy adults. A consultation with Dr. Yagoubian will determine if this treatment fits your needs in the Torrance area.

The Dental Implant Procedure Explained

The process begins with a thorough exam and a 3D scan. Dr. Yagoubian plans the exact position of your implant before surgery. On the day of the procedure, he places the titanium post into your jawbone. Dr. Yagoubian may also use Exparel, a long-lasting local anesthetic, at the surgical site to help manage pain in the days following the procedure.

You then enter a healing period of about three to four months. During this time, the implant bonds with the bone. After healing, your restorative dentist attaches a post and a custom crown. The result is a tooth that looks and functions like a natural one.

Dr. Yagoubian provides detailed instructions for each step. He also calls you personally after surgery to check on your recovery.

Bone Grafting And Its Role In Implant Candidacy

Bone grafting is often the first step for patients who lack sufficient jawbone. After a tooth is lost, the surrounding bone can shrink. Up to half of the bone may resorb within a year. A graft rebuilds this foundation so an implant can be placed securely.

Aftercare Tips For Long-Lasting Dental Implants

Care for your dental implant like a natural tooth. Brush twice a day and floss daily to keep the surrounding gum healthy. Attend regular dental check-ups so your dentist can monitor the implant. Avoid chewing hard items like ice or hard candy that could damage the restoration.

If you grind your teeth, a night guard can protect your implant. Dr. Yagoubian provides clear aftercare instructions after your procedure.
