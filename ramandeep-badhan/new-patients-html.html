New Patients

Welcome to Cambria Smiles! If you’re new to our dental office, then this is the page for you. Below, you’ll find information about what to expect during your first visit as well as our multitude of payment options. Of course, if you have any questions after looking everything over, you can always give our team a call . We’re always ready to provide the answers you need, and we hope to meet you soon!

Your First Visit

When you come to see us for the first time, our goal is to learn everything we can about both your dental needs as well as you as a person. Our front desk team will help you organize your paperwork before escorting you to your treatment room. There, you’ll get to meet your dentist, and they’ll spend a few minutes chatting with you before performing an oral exam. After that, they’ll go over their observations with you and recommend any additional treatments you might need. If time allows, you’ll then receive a refreshing cleaning.

Dental Insurance

At Cambria Smiles, we make it easy for our patients to use their dental insurance, no matter their plan or provider. We will be more than happy to submit all insurance forms for you and help you recover the most from your benefits. With us, saving on your care will be one of the easiest parts of your appointments! To learn how we can use your particular plan, give us a call today.

Dental Financing

Do you currently NOT have dental insurance? Do you require quite a bit of dental care and worry that you won’t be able to afford the out-of-pocket costs? If so, then we have two payment options that will help your care fit easily into your budget.

First, you can sign up for the Kleer Membership Program. For one low annual fee, you’ll instantly gain access to a wide variety of completely and partially covered services, ranging from checkups and cleanings to crowns and more. Basically, it offers many of the same benefits as traditional insurance, but for a fraction of the price!

Or, you can sign up for a low-to-no interest payment plan through CareCredit. CareCredit is a third-party financier that has already helped many of our patients afford their care, and you can sign up for a plan today simply by clicking on the link below!

Military Discount

At Cambria Smiles, if you are a veteran or active duty military personnel, you will automatically receive a 10 % discount on ALL dental services! It’s just our little way of saying Thank You! to the men and women who have done so much to protect our freedom. Just bring your military ID or Veterans card when you come to see us, and you’ll instantly receive the discount.
