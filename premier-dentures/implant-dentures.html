Common Questions About Implant Dentures

Yes, you are still able to wear your dentures like normal. Small adjustments may be needed to relieve any pressure away from the implant site.

Daily cleaning is simple—remove the denture and clean the denture with a soft brush and non-abrasive denture cleaner. To clean the implants, brush the implant and surrounding gums with a toothbrush or use a Waterpik for a deeper clean.

Relines are less frequent than with traditional dentures, but sometimes needed as the bone around the implant shrinks and changes over time, causing space between the gums and denture. This can allow food to get trapped.
