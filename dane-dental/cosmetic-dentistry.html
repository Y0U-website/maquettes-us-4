0 Cosmetic Dentistry What Is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry refers to a variety of dental treatments aimed at improving the overall aesthetics of your smile with a focus on refining the appearance of the teeth, gums, and bite. This includes improvements to tooth color, shape, and position.

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Right For You?

If you’re feeling less than confident about your smile, a cosmetic treatment plan might be right for you. Whether you’re a coffee drinker dreaming of whiter, brighter teeth or someone who’s always shied away from flashing a smile, we have a range of treatment options to meet your needs available right here at Dane Dental.

Cosmetic teeth whitening is a lightening process that improves the existing color of your teeth by treating any stains or yellowing.

Typically made of porcelain or a variety of other esthetic materials, veneers are thin covers that adhere to your teeth, giving your smile a more uniform, natural-looking shape.

Resin build-ups are a simple treatment for restoring your teeth to their original height and shape using a filling material that’s bonded directly to your tooth.

Invisalign is a transparent alternative to braces used to adjust and align your teeth for a straight and natural-looking smile. Click here for more information.

What To Expect

To ensure cosmetic dentistry is the right treatment option for you, we’ll start with a consultation. What concerns do you have about your smile? What improvements would you like to see? After gathering notes on what you’re looking for, we’ll conduct a comprehensive exam to determine if any other issues need to be addressed.

From there, we’ll put our recommendations into a custom treatment plan. Depending on your budget and desired outcome, we’ll provide a few different options to help you achieve what you’re looking for.

Once you’ve decided on the cosmetic treatment plan that’s right for you, we’ll help you get everything organized by scheduling your appointments, discussing payment plans, and answering any questions you may have.
