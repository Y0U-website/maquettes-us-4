Your first appointment with Square Smiles is designed to answer the basic questions that most patients or their parents have. Your initial consultation will also give you the opportunity to: Meet our team
  • Learn more about orthodontics
  • Receive a comprehensive initial exam
  • Find out whether treatment is necessary

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER THE INITIAL CONSULTATION?

You’re one step closer to achieving the smile you’ve always wanted! After your consultation, we’ll schedule your first appointment, where Dr. Joffre will get you started with your new orthodontic treatment. This appointment generally takes about an hour, and during this time our team will be available to make sure that you are as comfortable as possible.

VISITING THE DENTIST DURING ORTHODONTIC TREATMENT

It is very important that you continue to visit your family dentist once every six months, even during your orthodontic treatment, for teeth cleanings and routine dental checkups. If extra dental care is needed, we will be happy to coordinate with your family dentist to make sure that you are receiving the best care possible.
