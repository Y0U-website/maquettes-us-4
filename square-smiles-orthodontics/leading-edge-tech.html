DIGITAL X-RAYS

Digital x-rays have become an integral part of modern orthodontics, and Square Smiles is at the forefront of this technological shift. Digital x-rays offer:

  • Impeccable detail: Exceptionally detailed images facilitate a precise picture of your oral health, enabling a highly accurate diagnosis and tailored treatment planning.
  • Instant feedback: Immediate results eliminate processing time, equipping Dr. Joffre and his team to discuss your orthodontic needs promptly, ensuring you stay informed every step of the way.
  • Enhanced safety: Compared to traditional x-ray technologies, digital x-rays emit significantly less radiation, making it a safe choice for patients of all ages.
  • Less waste: Digital imaging reduces chemical waste and eliminates the need for the disposal of used film materials, aligning with our commitment to sustainable practices.
  • No shipping: Easy electronic sharing eliminates the need to mail physical copies, reducing paper consumption and decreasing the carbon footprint associated with traditional mailing practices.

CBCT SCANS

Cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) scans have become a game-changer in dental imaging technology. We have made this advanced technology a cornerstone in our practice, providing:

  • Detailed imaging: CBCT scans produce clear and detailed 3D images of teeth, soft tissues, nerve pathways, and bone in a single scan. They offer a level of detail even x-rays cannot provide, enabling highly accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning.
  • Reduced radiation exposure: Compared to traditional CT scans, CBCT scans emit a significantly lower level of radiation, reaffirming our commitment to patient safety.
  • Minimal scan time: CBCT scans are quick and seamless, taking only a few seconds to complete a full-mouth scan. This elevates comfort and reduces overall time spent in our office.
  • Efficient workflow: The digital images produced by CBCT scans can be easily shared with other specialists as needed, improving efficiency of treatment.
