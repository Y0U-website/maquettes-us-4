Dental implants are a revolutionary solution for replacing missing teeth, offering a permanent and natural-looking alternative to traditional dentures and bridges.

A dental implant is an artificial support made of a biocompatible material, at Dentist of Aventura we use titanium, which is implanted into the jaw’s bone and used to support a denture, in other words a crown, to fully replace a lost tooth. The implant usually consists of two main parts: the intraosseous implant screw and abutment supporting the denture.

At Dentist of Aventura , we offer same-day implants, providing a convenient solution for restoring your smile. In the first stage, a titanium implant is placed into the jawbone. In the second stage, an abutment is attached to the implant to hold the crown securely. In some cases, stages one and two may need to be completed in separate appointments to allow adequate time for the tissue to heal. Consult with our experts to see if same-day implants are the right choice for you.

After the healing period, which takes about three months or less, an abutment is placed in the intraosseous part. The intraosseous part refers to the portion of a dental implant, screw, or other structure that is positioned within the bone.

After the osseointegration process is completed, the implant becomes one with the bone tissue, and crowns are attached to the abutment. Osseointegration is defined as the direct structural and functional connection between the living bone and the surface of a load-bearing implant.

Can you place same-day-implant?

In many cases, healthy individuals may qualify for same-day implant placement, where a tooth is extracted and the implant is placed during the same visit. This approach reduces the number of procedures and overall treatment time. Consult with our dentist to determine if you are a suitable candidate for this advanced procedure.

Why Do You Need an Implant?

1. Prevent bone loss

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. This process, called bone resorption, and can lead to:

Dental implants, l ike natural teeth, stimulate the jawbone, preserving its strength and preventing bone loss.

  • A weakened jaw structure.
  • Changes in facial shape cause a sunken or aged appearance.
  • Increased difficulty in performing future restoration.

2. Restore chewing functionality

Missing teeth can make eating your favorite foods difficult and uncomfortable. Over time, this can lead to:

Dental implants function like natural teeth, allowing you to chew and enjoy a wide range of foods without discomfort or limitations.

  • A restricted diet which could be lacking essential nutrients.
  • Digestive issues from improperly chewed food.

3. Enhance speech

Missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures can cause speech difficulties, such as slurring or mumbling. Dental implants provide a stable and secure fit, enabling clear and confident speech without worrying about slurring.

4. Prevent teeth from shifting

When a tooth is lost, neighboring teeth may begin to shift into the empty space, leading to:

Dental implants fill the gap, maintaining the proper alignment of your teeth and preserving your oral health.

  • Misalignment of your bite.
  • Difficulty cleaning crowded areas, increasing the risk of decay and gum disease.
  • Getting food stuck in the misaligned space, which could cause bad breath.
  • A negative impact on your overall oral health.

5. Improve self-confidence

A missing tooth can make you self-conscious about your appearance, affecting your willingness to smile or engage in social activities. Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth, boosting your confidence and making you smile with pride.

Why our patients choose us:

  • Natural and aesthetic appearance. Well-fitted implants look virtually like real teeth, so you won’t be embarrassed to smile.
  • Comfort. Our modern implants won’t cause discomfort and work like your healthy real teeth. No food gets stuck under the implants, and over time, they fuse with the patient’s bone and won’t fall out.
  • Long service life. Implants, with proper care, will last over twenty years, much longer than other types of dentures.
  • Keeping your teeth and gums intact. Implant placement will remove any imperfections without damaging the teeth or tissue next to them.

Are dental implants right for you?

Dental implants are suitable for most adults with good oral health and sufficient bone density. During your consultation, our team will evaluate your:

  • Oral health: Addressing gum disease or decay beforehand.
  • Bone structure: Ensuring there’s enough bone to support the implant.

How are Implants Installed?

Let’s examine the steps of implantation to gain an even better understanding of dental implants and how they work.

Examination

Your visit begins with an initial consultation, during which the doctor interviews you, collects your medical history (anamnesis) and complaints, conducts an oral examination, and assesses the condition of your teeth and gums.

Diagnosis

We will perform a 3D scan with our highly modern scanner to assess the condition of your your teeth, gums, and jaw bone. This will provide our dentist a detailed map of your mouth and will provide a clear picture on how the implant will be placed.

Oral sanitation

Before placing an implant, we will evaluate if you have tooth decay and if you require fillings. We will also determine if you have gum disease. Finally, we will offer to performing professional oral hygiene treatment if it’s needed.

Tooth extraction and/or implant placement

Depending on the type of surgery you have chosen, the doctor will administer local anesthesia, extract the tooth and place an implant, or place an implant in place of the missing tooth. In some cases, if bone loss is pronounced, it may be necessary to increase the volume of bone tissue beforehand, i.e. perform bone grafting.

Replacement of the temporary crown with a permanent one

It is carried out 3-6 months after the implant placement after the osseointegration (engraftment) processes are completed. Engraftment is when the blood-forming cells you received on transplant day start to grow and make healthy blood cells. It’s an important milestone in your transplant recovery.

A permanent crown can be made of different materials. Zirconium dioxide or pressed ceramics are used to bridge implants or single crowns.
