At Guilliot Dental, we are devoted to providing high-quality dental care to the community of Lafayette and all of Acadiana. Our Lafayette location is in the heart of Downtown Lafayette and has been in the Guilliot family for decades. First owned by Dr. Roy Guilliot, who served Lafayette for over 40 years before retiring in 1983 and now over 38 years later his sons, Dr. J Albert Guilliot, and grandson, Dr. Jared W. Guilliot, are continuing the family practice of serving Lafayette’s dental needs at its Jefferson Street location. We have also expanded into Broussard, LA to see more of Acadiana.

Our dental practice prides ourselves in having a high standard of excellence in personalized dental care. We offer education and preventative dental care to keep our patients smiling in addition to restorative , cosmetic dentistry , and orthodontics . In the case of a dental emergency, we offer emergency dental services for you and your family in your time of need. Our wide range of services also includes tooth extraction procedures , dentures , and more that helps you have a beautiful, healthy smile.

Our family of doctors wants to ensure that all of our patients feel like family when you’re at Guilliot Dental. Whether it is preventative care, dentures, or an unfortunate dental emergency, we’re here for you when you need us and we can’t wait to see your smile!

We look forward to servicing you, Lafayette! Make an appointment today!
