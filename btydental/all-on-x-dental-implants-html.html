All-on-X Dental Implants in Anchorage, AK

All-on-X is a full-arch implant solution that replaces an entire row of missing or failing teeth using just 4 to 6 strategically placed implants. The result is a fixed, permanent set of teeth that looks natural, holds firmly in place, and can last decades with proper care. At btyDENTAL, we offer free All-on-X consultations at all 8 of our Anchorage locations.

What Is All-on-X?

All-on-X (also called All-on-4 or All-on-6 depending on the number of implants used) is a dental implant technique designed for patients who are missing all or most of their teeth on the upper jaw, lower jaw, or both. Instead of placing one implant per tooth, All-on-X uses 4 to 6 implants to anchor a full prosthetic arch.

The implants are placed at precise angles to maximize contact with available bone, which means most patients qualify even if they have experienced some bone loss. A fixed prosthesis (your new teeth) is then attached to those implants, giving you a complete, stable smile in far fewer appointments than traditional implant methods.

Unlike dentures, All-on-X implants do not come out. They are permanent, they do not slip, and they function like natural teeth so you can eat, speak, and smile without thinking about them.

Types of All-on-X Prostheses at btyDENTAL

Not every All-on-X solution looks or functions the same. We offer several prosthesis types depending on your anatomy, goals, and budget.

FP1 Prosthesis

The FP1 replaces the tooth portion only (the white crowns) without adding artificial gum tissue. It is a good fit for patients with minimal gum recession and works well where the natural gum line is still visible and healthy. It tends to be more affordable and requires fewer appointments to complete.

FP3 Prosthesis

The FP3 replaces both the teeth and the gum tissue (the pink portion). This is the most aesthetic option for patients who have experienced gum recession or bone loss, as it fills in the gaps for a fuller, more natural-looking smile. It requires more visits to complete but delivers a highly balanced, natural result.

Implant-Supported Overdenture

This option looks similar to a denture but clips securely onto the implants rather than resting on the gums. It can be removed for cleaning. This is a good middle-ground option for patients who prefer a removable solution but want far more stability and comfort than a traditional denture provides.

One of the advantages of All-on-X is that the angled implant placement is specifically designed to work with patients who have experienced some bone loss. Many patients who have been told they are not candidates for traditional implants qualify for All-on-X.

The only way to know for certain is a consultation. Our team will review your imaging, bone structure, and health history and give you a straight answer.

  • Are missing all or most of your teeth on one or both arches
  • Have failing teeth that need to be extracted
  • Are currently wearing dentures but want a permanent, fixed solution
  • Have sufficient jawbone density (or are open to a bone graft if needed)
  • Are in good overall health and do not smoke (or are willing to quit)
  • Have well-managed diabetes or other chronic conditions

The All-on-X Process at btyDENTAL

  • Free Consultation: We review your dental and medical history, take imaging (X-rays or CT scans), and walk you through your options in plain language. No pressure, no obligation.
  • Custom Treatment Plan: Your dentist builds a plan around your specific bone structure, aesthetic goals, and timeline. You will know the full cost, number of visits, and what to expect before anything begins.
  • Implant Placement: The 4 to 6 implants are placed under local anesthesia or sedation. Most patients are surprised by how manageable the procedure is. A temporary prosthesis is often attached the same day so you leave with a full smile.
  • Healing and Integration: Over the next several months, the implants fuse with your jawbone. We check in regularly throughout this phase to monitor your progress.
  • Final Prosthesis: Once integration is complete, your permanent, custom-designed arch is attached. This is your long-term smile.

How Much Do All-on-X Implants Cost in Anchorage?

All-on-X costs vary depending on the number of implants, the type of prosthesis, whether extractions or bone grafts are needed, and whether you are treating one arch or both. In Anchorage, a single-arch All-on-X typically ranges from $15,000 to $30,000.

That number can feel significant, but consider that All-on-X lasts 20 to 30 years while dentures need replacing every 5 to 10 years and require ongoing costs for adhesives, repairs, and adjustments. Over a lifetime, All-on-X is often the more cost-effective choice.
