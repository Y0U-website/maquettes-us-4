Dental Implant Options We Offer

Single-Tooth Implants

The gold standard for replacing a missing tooth. A titanium post is placed in the jawbone and topped with a custom crown that blends seamlessly with your surrounding teeth. Strong, permanent, and indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

Best for: Patients missing one or a few individual teeth.

All-on-X Implants

Replace an entire arch of teeth (upper, lower, or both) using just four to six strategically placed implants. All-on-X gives you a full, fixed set of teeth in a fraction of the time and cost of replacing each tooth individually.

Best for: Patients who are missing most or all of their teeth on one arch, or those dealing with failing teeth who want a permanent solution.

Full-Arch Restoration

For patients who need comprehensive reconstruction, we offer full-arch implant solutions customized to your anatomy and aesthetic goals. We walk you through every step, from initial scans to final placement.

Best for: Complex cases requiring full upper or lower arch replacement.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good general health are candidates for dental implants. You may be a great fit if you:

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have a fully developed jawbone
  • Have adequate bone density (or are open to a bone graft if needed)
  • Do not smoke, or are willing to quit during the healing period
  • Are managing any chronic conditions like diabetes

What to Expect at btyDENTAL

Getting dental implants at btyDENTAL is a straightforward, well-supported process:

We’re with you every step of the way, and every one of our 8 Anchorage locations offers the same high standard of care.

  • Free Consultation - We review your X-rays, evaluate your bone health, and walk you through your options.
  • Treatment Planning - Your dentist creates a customized plan based on your needs and timeline.
  • Implant Placement - The titanium post is placed under local anesthesia. Most patients are surprised by how manageable it is.
  • Healing Period - Your implant fuses with the jawbone over several months. We check in throughout.
  • Crown or Restoration Placement - Your permanent crown or arch is attached. You leave with a complete, functional smile.
