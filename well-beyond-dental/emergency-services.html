Emergency Dental Services in Reno, NV

Toothache

A toothache can be a dental emergency if the pain is severe and persistent, and is often an indication of an underlying dental issue. A toothache can be caused by a number of things, including tooth decay, gum disease, a cracked tooth, or an abscess. If left untreated, a toothache can worsen and lead to more serious dental problems, such as infection or tooth loss.

If you are experiencing a severe toothache, it’s important to seek emergency dental care as soon as possible. Well Beyond Dental can diagnose the underlying cause of the toothache and provide treatment to alleviate the pain and prevent further damage. Treatment may include a filling, root canal, or tooth extraction, depending on the severity of the toothache and the underlying condition.

Ignoring a toothache can lead to more serious dental problems and may require more extensive and costly treatment in the future. So, if you have a toothache, don’t delay seeking emergency dental care.

Broken or Chipped Tooth

A broken or chipped tooth can be considered a dental emergency, especially if it is causing pain, discomfort, or affecting your ability to eat or speak. A broken or chipped tooth can occur due to trauma or injury, biting down on hard foods, or as a result of tooth decay.

If you have a broken or chipped tooth, it’s important to seek emergency dental care as soon as possible. The experts at Well Beyond Dental can evaluate the severity of the damage and provide treatment to restore the tooth. Treatment may include bonding, a filling, a crown, or veneer, depending on the extent of the damage.

Ignoring a broken or chipped tooth can lead to further damage or infection, which may require more extensive and costly treatment in the future. It’s important to address the issue promptly to prevent complications and preserve the integrity of your teeth. So, if you have a broken or chipped tooth, contact us to schedule an emergency dental appointment.

Lost Filling or Crown

Losing a filling or crown means the affected tooth may be vulnerable to further damage or infection. A filling or crown can become dislodged due to injury, tooth decay, or wear and tear over time.

If you lose a filling or crown, it’s important to seek emergency dental care as soon as possible. The Well Beyond Dental team can evaluate the damage and provide treatment to restore the tooth. Depending on the extent of the damage, treatment may involve replacing the filling or crown, performing a root canal, or even extracting the tooth if it cannot be saved.

Ignoring a lost filling or crown can lead to further damage or infection, which may require more extensive and costly treatment in the future. It’s important to address the issue promptly to prevent complications and preserve the integrity of your teeth. So, if you lose a filling or crown, contact Well Beyond Dental to schedule an emergency dental appointment.

Abscess

An abscess is a dental emergency that requires immediate attention from a dentist. An abscess is a pocket of pus that forms in the tooth or gums due to a bacterial infection. It can cause severe pain, swelling, and fever, and if left untreated, it can spread to other parts of the body and cause serious health problems.

If you suspect you have an abscess, it’s important to seek emergency dental care as soon as possible. Well beyond Dental can diagnose the abscess and provide treatment to alleviate the pain and prevent the infection from spreading. Treatment may include draining the abscess, prescribing antibiotics, performing a root canal, or even extracting the affected tooth.

Ignoring an abscess can lead to more serious dental and medical problems, including sepsis, which is a life-threatening condition. So, if you suspect you have an abscess, don’t delay seeking emergency dental care from Well Beyond Dental.
