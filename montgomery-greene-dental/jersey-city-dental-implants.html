Dental Implants in Jersey City

Missing teeth can be a real pain, both literally and figuratively. Dental implants have become popular for people looking to replace their missing teeth. But before you commit to dental implants, it’s important to understand both the pros and the cons.

Advantages of Dental Implants

If you are skeptical about consulting a dentist in Jersey City for dental implants, here are some points to shape your perspective:

Dental implants are designed to look like your natural teeth. They even integrate into the bone in your jaw, providing stability and security that other tooth replacement options can’t match.

Dental implants can last a long time with proper care, especially if you get them done by an experienced dentist in Jersey City. Other tooth replacement options, such as removable dentures and fixed bridges, typically need to be replaced every 5 to 10 years.

As dental implants integrate with your jawbone, they provide unmatched stability and support for the final restoration as well as preserving the remaining bone. This support can help prevent bite problems and improve your oral health. Additionally, dental implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support, which means if those teeth become damaged, they will not affect the implants.

  • Dental Implants Look and Feel Like Natural Teeth
  • Dental Implants are Durable and Long-Lasting
  • Dental Implants Improve Your Oral Health

The Downside of Dental Implants

As with anything, not everyone is a candidate for dental implants to fix broken teeth. Here are some factors that might repel you from getting dental implants done:

Once the titanium posts are placed in your jawbone, they are there for good. While the artificial teeth attached to the implants can be replaced or removed, you cannot remove the implant without surgery. This is something to keep in mind if you are considering dental implants but are unsure about whether or not you will want them permanently.

While dental implants have come down somewhat in recent years, they are still more expensive than other options for replacing missing teeth, such as bridges or dentures. Additionally, insurance companies rarely cover the full cost of dental implants, so you can expect to pay for them out-of-pocket.

  • Dental Implants Are Not A Reversible Procedure
  • Dental Implants Can Be Costly

Pros of Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are another successful treatment to replace missing teeth and restore your bite. Here’s why:

When you get dental bridges, the replacement teeth look like your real ones and are even better at times. They’re also the same color as your natural teeth. That blend makes dental bridges hard to spot. Dental bridges don’t just improve the way your mouth looks. They can also make it easier to chew and speak.

An empty space in your mouth can cause problems with your bite and put extra stress on your remaining teeth. Dental bridges close the gap created by missing teeth. This maintains the shape of your face and alleviates stress on your bite.

  • Replacement Teeth That Look And Feel Natural
  • Fills The Empty Space Left By A Missing Tooth

Cons of Dental Bridges

Here’s what might make dental bridges a not-so-good choice for you:

While the permanent dental bridge is being made, you will have temporary crowns. These are usually acrylic and less comfortable than your natural teeth, making eating and speaking more difficult.

Like anything else, dental bridges require regular maintenance and care. You must brush and floss carefully around the bridge to keep your gums and supporting teeth healthy. It’s also important to regularly see your dentist in Jersey City for professional cleanings and checkups.

When you lose a tooth, it’s important to replace it as soon as possible. Advantages of Dentures

Dentures are a smart choice if you want the liberty to choose between permanent and temporary teeth replacement. Here’s what makes dentures good for missing teeth:

Dentures are one of the most affordable options for replacing missing teeth. They typically cost less than dental implants or fixed crowns, and you don’t have to worry about the additional costs of implant surgery, such as bone grafting.

Dentures are very easy to care for. You need to remove them at night and brush them with a soft-bristled toothbrush and denture cleanser. There’s no need for special equipment.

Dentures are also convenient because they can be removed and placed back in your mouth as needed. This means you can take them out and put them back when you eat and drink.

  • They’re Affordable
  • They’re Easy to Care For
  • They’re Convenient

Cons of Dentures

Dentures are not without their respective cons; you must be aware of them. So, here it goes:

Dentures are not your natural teeth. It will take time for your muscles to learn how to hold them in place and for your brain to get used to the sensation of having something in your mouth that wasn’t there before. This learning curve can be frustrating, but you must be patient as you adjust to your new dentures.

  • They Take Some Getting Used To
  • Dentures Require Special Care
