Welcome & Medical History Review

We start by getting to know you—not just your teeth. We&#x27;ll review your complete medical history, current medications, allergies, and any concerns. This includes discussing systemic health connections like cardiovascular health, sleep quality, TMJ symptoms, and lifestyle factors that impact your oral health.

Comprehensive Clinical Examination

Our doctor performs an in-depth evaluation that goes far beyond "just checking for cavities." This thorough exam includes:

  • • Oral cancer screening
  • • Complete digital x-rays with AI analysis
  • • Periodontal (gum) assessment with probing
  • • TMJ and jaw function evaluation
  • • Bite and occlusion analysis
  • • Tooth wear and bruxism assessment
  • • Airway and sleep health screening
  • • Decay detection and restoration evaluation
  • • Caries risk assessment
  • • 3D scan
  • • Oral hygiene instructions
  • • Nutrition counseling

Professional Cleaning

During your new patient visit, the doctor performs a thorough cleaning tailored to your needs—whether it&#x27;s a standard prophylaxis or if we identify the need for deeper periodontal therapy. We remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and provide personalized home care instructions.
