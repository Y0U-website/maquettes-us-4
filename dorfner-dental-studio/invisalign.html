The Four Stages of Your Invisalign Treatment Journey

Clear aligner treatment follows four predictable stages designed to gradually straighten your smile. While mild alignment cases may take as little as six months, more complex bite issues can require up to two years. Your exact treatment timeline depends on your individual needs and how consistently you wear your aligners.

Stage 1: Digital Planning

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive consultation and detailed digital scans that allow Dr. Rachel Dorfner to map each tooth movement before your custom aligners are fabricated. This personalized planning process helps ensure predictable, precise results from the very beginning.

Stage 2: Active Aligner Wear

During the active treatment phase, you’ll wear your aligners for 20 to 22 hours each day, removing them only to eat, drink, and clean your teeth. You’ll typically switch to a new set of aligners every one to two weeks. Some tightness with each new tray is a normal sign that your teeth are moving as planned. In some cases, small tooth-colored attachments may be used to guide more complex movements. If you’re wondering does Invisalign hurt , most patients describe the discomfort as mild and temporary.

Stage 3: Refinements

After your initial series of aligners, some patients benefit from a short refinement phase using additional trays to fine-tune the final tooth positions. You can read our detailed guide on the Invisalign treatment timeline to learn more about how this process works.

Stage 4: Retention

Once your treatment is complete, you’ll receive a custom retainer to help maintain your new smile and prevent your teeth from gradually shifting over time. Wearing your retainer as instructed is an important part of preserving your long-term results.

Invisalign vs. Braces: Choosing the Right Path for Your Smile

Choosing between clear aligners and traditional braces depends on your bite goals and lifestyle. Clear aligners provide a more discreet appearance, easier hygiene, and removable trays, making them ideal for mild-to-moderate alignment issues. Braces are generally recommended for complex structural rotations or cases where keeping trays in for 22 hours a day is a challenge.

Treatment times are often comparable, though some direct movements can wrap up more quickly. Read our guide on whether Invisalign is faster than braces to see how timelines compare.

Existing dental work and your bite structure also shape your candidacy. You can get Invisalign with a crown if the restoration is stable and healthy. Clear trays can also resolve structural bite issues, which we cover in our guide on whether Invisalign can fix a crossbite .

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to some of the most frequently asked questions the Dorfner Dental Studio team receives from patients like you.

How long does Invisalign take?

Most Invisalign treatments take between 12 and 18 months, though mild alignment issues can be resolved in as little as 6 months. Complex cases involving significant structural movement can extend up to 24 months. Your overall timeline depends on your daily wear time and whether you require refinement trays. Read our detailed breakdown on the Invisalign treatment timeline page for more specifics.

Is Invisalign faster than braces?

Invisalign can be faster than traditional braces for mild-to-moderate cosmetic alignment issues, but overall speed depends heavily on patient compliance. Because clear aligners are removable, they must be worn for 20 to 22 hours per day to stay on track. To see a detailed comparison of treatment speeds, read our article on whether Invisalign is faster than braces.

How much does Invisalign cost, and what affects the total price?

Invisalign treatment costs vary based on the structural needs of your smile. The total price is determined by the complexity of your alignment issues, how many trays you need, and any required refinements. We provide a complete, transparent breakdown of costs during your consultation. To keep care accessible, we offer flexible payment plans through CareCredit and Cherry financing.

I’m interested in Invisalign®. Do you offer consultations? Can I get Invisalign if I have crown?

Yes, you can typically undergo clear aligner treatment if you have a dental crown. The primary requirement is that the crown must be stable, healthy, and securely bonded to your natural tooth. Because aligners slide over your teeth rather than pulling on brackets, they safely accommodate most restorations. Read more in our guide on whether you can get Invisalign with a crown.

What are the benefits of dental implants? Can Invisalign fix a crossbite?

Clear aligners can successfully correct many mild-to-moderate cases of front and back crossbites. However, severe crossbites rooted in complex skeletal or jaw issues may require traditional braces or other orthodontic approaches. We evaluate your bite structure during your consultation.
