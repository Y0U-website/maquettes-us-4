Dental Implants in Dunedin, FL

Whatever your tooth replacement needs are a single tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire arch, we will tailor your implant solution to suit you perfectly.

Your Dental Implant Journey at TLC Dentistry

  • Replaces a single tooth or multiple teeth If you are missing a single tooth, multiple teeth, or all upper or lower teeth, implants provide a solution.
  • Promotes jawbone health Dental implants provide jawbone stimulation, helping prevent bone loss and maintain your jawbone’s strength and integrity.
  • Supports surrounding teeth Implants help maintain the alignment of surrounding teeth by filling the gap in your smile.
  • Improved chewing function Replacement teeth are stabilized by the implant in your jaw and restore your ability to chew and speak comfortably.
  • Natural appearance Our implant crowns, bridges, and dentures blend perfectly with your existing teeth for a seamless smile enhancement.
  • Long-term success rate Dental implants are a proven tooth replacement solution with an impressive 95 percent success rate over 10 years.

Initial consultation and planning

Our dentists are experienced in implant dentistry and carefully evaluate your needs to see if you are a good candidate. They use digital x-rays and 3-D cone beam scans to ensure there is sufficient bone mass in your jaw and to plan the precise placement of your implant for long-term success.

Implant placement surgery

With your comfort as our top priority, our dentists perform your implant surgery under local anesthesia at our Dunedin dental office. Before beginning, they take time to ensure you’re at ease and understand the procedure.

Healing and osseointegration

Implant placement is followed by 3 6 months of healing, where the titanium post fuses with your jawbone; this process is called osseointegration. We will monitor throughout the healing period to ensure the process is smooth and worry-free.

Tooth Restoration

The final step after the implant fully integrates is attaching your tooth restoration. We use a dental crown to restore a single tooth; for multiple missing teeth, we would recommend an implant bridge; if you require full-arch tooth replacement, we recommend an all-on-x implant-retained prostheses or a removable "snap-in" implant-supported denture.
