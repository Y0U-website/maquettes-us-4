Dr. Natalia Yaldoo, D.D.S.

Dr. Natalia Yaldoo, formerly Dr. Radzikowski ("Dr. Rad’), is our distinguished dentist deeply committed to providing exceptional care. Her educational journey began at Western Michigan University, where she earned a Bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Sciences, instilling in her a strong foundation for her future in dentistry.

She furthered her knowledge at the renowned University of Michigan School of Dentistry, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2017, proudly representing the Wolverines.

Dr. Alexander Yaldoo, D.M.D.

Dr. Yaldoo, a dedicated dentist, earned his Bachelor of Science degree in 2014 from Oakland University in Auburn Hills, MI. He then pursued his passion for dentistry at the Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health, graduating in 2019 with a Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry degree, complemented by a certificate in Public Health.

Dr. Yaldoo’s journey as a dentist began in Michigan before he embraced the vibrant community of Florida in 2021.

The relationships we build with our patients are based on honesty and transparency. With clear and open communication, we help you understand your treatment options and what to expect during your appointment.

Empowering our patients is important to us, and our knowledgeable team provides education and information to help you understand your oral health needs and make confident decisions about your care.
