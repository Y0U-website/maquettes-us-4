Invisalign Clear Braces

At the start of the Invisalign process, an impression of your mouth is taken. The impression is used as the basis to create several clear aligners, each of which is to be worn for about two weeks for at least 22 hours a day. Each aligner pushes the teeth slightly closer to their desired alignment. In about 12 to 18 months, you’ll have a much straighter smile to admire in the mirror. Remember that since Invisalign is removable, it won’t get in the way of eating your favorite foods like traditional braces do.
