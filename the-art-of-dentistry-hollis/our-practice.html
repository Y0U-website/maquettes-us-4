Meet the Doctor

Dr. Yousef Sidki

Dr. Yousef Sidki brings a refined, global perspective and an artistic eye to comprehensive dentistry. A native of New England, he combines the region’s tradition of excellence with insights gained from studying dentistry across cultures and learning from clinicians around the world. His philosophy centers on meaningful human connection and delivering care that feels both elevated and deeply personal.

With a deep passion for his craft, Dr. Sidki blends science and artistry to create stunning, functional smiles. He has completed extensive training across a wide range of advanced procedures, including veneers, crowns, dental implants, and the use of Botox for both therapeutic and aesthetic dental applications. These disciplines allow him to deliver results that are precise, harmonious, and truly transformative—helping patients feel confident, rejuvenated, and genuinely cared for.

Known for his natural charisma and approachable style, Dr. Sidki connects with patients on a personal level. Whether you’re feeling anxious about your visit or excited to enhance your smile, you’ll find a trusted partner who listens, explains, and cares with genuine compassion. He spends much of his private time with his family, enjoying music, or traveling—often combining leisure with advanced training in cosmetics, surgery, and emerging dental techniques. His passion for learning keeps him on the cutting edge of his craft.

Technology and amenities designed to elevate your care

At Art of Dentistry, your comfort and results are never an afterthought. We’ve equipped our modern office with innovative tools and thoughtful amenities that make your experience smoother, gentler, and more precise—because elite dental care should also feel amazing.

Trios digital scanner

No more goopy impressions! Our Trios digital scanner makes imaging your teeth fast, comfortable, and accurate. It’s perfect for everything from crowns and bridges to clear aligners and digital smile design.

Sprintray 3D printer

With in-house 3D printing capabilities, we can rapidly create long-term temporary restorations and custom appliances. It’s quick, convenient, and gives us complete control over your dental designs.

Heated massage chairs

Comfort starts in the chair. Our heated massage chairs help you relax during your visit—making even routine appointments feel like a break from your day.

Nitrous and oral conscious sedation

If you’re nervous about dental work, we’ve got you covered. We offer nitrous oxide ("laughing gas") and oral conscious sedation to help you feel safe, calm, and in control—every step of the way. IV sedation coming soon!

STA Wand

This advanced tool helps deliver numbing agents gently and painlessly, reducing discomfort and eliminating the anxiety many patients associate with traditional anesthesia. It’s one more way we prioritize your comfort.

IV Sedation

For a deeper level of relaxation, we offer IV sedation to keep you calm and comfortable throughout your procedure. It’s a safe and effective option that helps eliminate anxiety while allowing you to remain responsive.

elevated detailed photography

We use advanced photography techniques to capture every nuance of your smile, providing high-quality images that aid in treatment planning and achieving the best possible results.
