Tuyen Nguyen, DMD

Dr. Nguyen was born in Vietnam and grew up in Boston. She went to the Mather School and Boston Latin School before graduating from Boston College in 2008. After college, Dr. Nguyen moved to Greenville, Mississippi where she taught 4th and 5th grade math and science through Teach for America, a non-profit organization. She then worked as a program coordinator for families with young children, focusing on family engagement activities prior to receiving her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Medicine.

Dr. Nguyen strives to provide the best quality care and most comfortable dental experience to all her patients. Gentle and caring, she can calm even the most anxious patients. Little fact, she likes to sing while working!

Dr. Nguyen lives in the South Shore with her partner and two rescued dogs, Ollie and Pepper. She enjoys camping, hiking, and spending lazy days at the beach. She loves all fall related activities especially apple picking. Ask her about her favorite apple variety; it may not be what you have in mind!

Pamela Regan, DMD

Dr. Pamela Regan graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Tufts University. She received her Doctorate Of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. She then went on to receive her Post Graduate Degree in Endodontics from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. She has limited her practice to endodontics and has practiced since 1999. Dr. Regan is a member of the Mayflower Study Club, the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Society, the South Shore Dental Society, and the American Association of Endodontics.

Dr. Regan enjoys spending her free time running, working out and spending time at the beach with her two children and her dog Ziggy.

Christine

Christine graduated from Middlesex Community College in 2005 and resides in Braintree. She loves being a hygienist and building relationships with patients. She finds it rewarding to be involved with educating patients on oral health as well and the connection to their overall health.

Christine enjoys hiking with her dog Henry, spending time with her son, reading, and going to the beach. An avid animal lover, Christine also has two cats and a frog.

Hanh

Hanh is a dedicated and skilled dental hygienist with 16 years of experience in providing comprehensive dental care and hygiene services. A graduate from University of New Haven with a Bachelor’s in Dental Hygiene, she is passionate about educating patients on the importance of oral health and implementing preventive dental care practices.

Throughout her career, Hanh has gained extensive experience in pediatric dentistry, periodontal therapy, or advanced dental technologies, making her proficient in a wide range of dental hygiene procedures. She is committed to using her expertise to help patients achieve and maintain optimal oral health. Continuously striving to enhance her skills, Hanh keeps herself updated with the latest advancements in dental hygiene through ongoing education and professional development.

She is a member of Colgate’s Oral Health Advisory Board, which allows her to collaborate with peers and contribute to the dental hygiene community. Outside of the dental office, Hanh enjoys her entertaining family, travel, volunteering in Vietnamese community programs to encourage her kids to learn about cultural traditions. Most importantly, she loves spending time with her beautiful garden roses with more than 40 different types.
