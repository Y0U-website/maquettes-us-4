WHO WE ARE

Helena Dental is dedicated to providing outstanding dental care tailored to the individual needs of each patient. At the forefront of this commitment is Dr. Robert T. Mallory, DMD, a highly respected practitioner whose expertise and patient-first philosophy shape the heart of our practice. Our mission centers on delivering compassionate, high-quality care while creating a welcoming and supportive environment for both patients and staff.

Patient well-being is our top priority at Helena Dental, driving our investment in advanced dental technologies, ongoing team development, and a full spectrum of comprehensive services. Conveniently located in the heart of the Helena community, our modern facility is thoughtfully designed to combine comfort with cutting-edge innovation—ensuring a seamless, exceptional experience every time you visit.
