Frequently Asked Questions About Digital Dentistry

Is digital dentistry safe?

Absolutely. The technologies used in digital dentistry, including intra-oral scanners and 3D imaging, are FDA-cleared and have been refined over years of clinical use. These tools emit significantly lower radiation than traditional X-rays , and because they are non-invasive, they carry virtually no risk. Your Wesley Chapel dentist will always review your health history and tailor every digital tool to your specific situation.

Does insurance cover digital dentistry procedures?

Many insurance plans cover digitally assisted procedures the same way they cover traditional ones, since the treatment itself (a crown, an implant, a set of aligners) is what’s being billed, not the technology used to create it. Coverage varies by plan, so our team will help you review your benefits before treatment begins and identify any out-of-pocket costs upfront.

How is digital dentistry different for children and patients with dental anxiety?

It tends to be a much better experience. Children and anxious patients often struggle most with traditional impressions, which can trigger gagging and discomfort. Because intra-oral scanners replace those bulky molds with a small wand that glides gently around the mouth, the process is faster and far less stressful. For patients who dread dental visits, that reduction in chair time and physical discomfort can genuinely change how they feel about coming in.

Do I need to do anything special to prepare for a digital dentistry appointment?

No special preparation is needed. Just brush and floss as you normally would before your visit. Our team handles everything else, walking you through each step so you always know what to expect.
