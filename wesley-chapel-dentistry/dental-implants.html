Dental Implants: Structure & Use

A dental implant replaces both the root of the tooth (the part below the gum line) and the crown (the part above the gum line). An oral surgeon inserts the tiny, surgical-grade titanium implant posts in the jawbone to create a secure foundation for custom dental restorations. Dr. Kalarickal designs replacement teeth and mounts them atop the posts following the healing period. Dental implants are strong, long lasting, and the only tooth replacement method that preserves jawbone mass.
