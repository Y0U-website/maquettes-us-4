ABOUT Sunrise Dental

Sunrise Dental is a general and cosmetic dental practice serving Reston, Virginia, and the greater Washington D.C. metropolitan area. Amy Ton, DDS, and her team of dental specialists offer comprehensive dentistry, including orthodontic and periodontal services for adults, teens, and children. The team takes an integrative approach to oral health care, always taking the time to understand each patient’s unique situation and recommend the best course of treatment.

Sunrise Dental strives to build long-lasting relationships with patients, offering everything from preventive dental care and deep cleanings to advanced periodontal procedures and wisdom teeth extraction . We pride ourselves in taking a conservative approach to treatment, building trust through honesty, openness, and patient education.

The team is extensively trained and certified in the latest dental technologies, including Invisalign® and laser dentistry with the Biolase® Waterlase and Epic X™ laser systems. Sunrise Dental also has dentists on staff who are certified by the California Implant Institute, upholding the best standards when it comes to dental implant surgery and prosthodontics.

Along with advanced restorative dental care and periodontics , the team offers customizable cosmetic solutions. Dental veneers can improve a single flawed tooth or dramatically transform a smile. Teeth whitening is an ever-popular option to brighten natural teeth and boost confidence.

Sunrise Dental creates a friendly environment for patients, whether they’re interacting with the office team or in the dental chair. Visiting the dentist can be an enjoyable experience for all.

The team of specialists at Sunrise Dental is proud to help patients enjoy confident, healthy smiles that last a lifetime. Rest assured, you’re in good hands. Call or request an appointment online to get started today.
