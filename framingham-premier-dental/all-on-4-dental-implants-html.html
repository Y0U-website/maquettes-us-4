Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

There are many benefits that you can experience with all-on-4 dental implants. Here are some of the most prominent ones:

  • Fixed: All-on-4 dental implants are fixed to the mouth, so they can be brushed just like natural teeth. You don’t need to take out your dentures to clean your smile either.
  • Comfortable: All-on-4 dental implants feel much more natural than traditional dentures. After the adjustment period is complete, they shouldn’t feel much different from your natural teeth.
  • Bite Force: All-on-4 dental implants restore more chewing power than traditional dentures. This allows patients to enjoy a full, nutritious diet without restrictions.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Unlike traditional dentures, all-on-4 dental implants help to prevent jawbone resorption. This will help you maintain a more youthful appearance.

All-on-4 Dental vs. Traditional Implants

All-on-4 dental implants have many things in common with implant dentures. Here are some of the notable differences between them:

  • Number of Implants: Implant dentures require up to 8 dental implants for support. This increases their cost.
  • Bone Grafting: To get implant dentures, many patients require bone grafting.
  • Quicker Process: It can take many months to get implant dentures. All-on-4 dental implants often allow patients to show off their new smile on the same day!
