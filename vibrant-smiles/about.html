A Passion for Excellence

Get To Know Dr. Nika Padyab

Dr. Padyab&#x27;s journey to dentistry began after she moved to Arizona in 2004, where she attended middle school and high school in Chandler. She was first drawn to the profession by the warm, inviting atmosphere of her family dentist&#x27;s office. These visits never felt clinical—they were more like gathering with friends. Seeing a team that truly loved caring for patients inspired her to volunteer at a non-profit dental clinic and explore the field firsthand.

During her volunteer work, Dr. Padyab had an eye-opening realization: oral health is critically important yet often overlooked. She discovered how our teeth impact everything from our ability to speak and smile to our overall confidence and wellbeing. This powerful insight transformed her interest into a true calling.

After earning her Bachelor&#x27;s degree from Arizona State University, Dr. Padyab completed her Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences at Midwestern University, where her thesis focused on antibiotic resistance. She then achieved her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Midwestern University in Glendale, AZ, building the expertise that defines her practice today.

Dr. Padyab founded Vibrant Smiles with a clear mission: to provide every patient with exceptional, personalized care. She is committed to helping patients achieve beautiful, confident smiles in a comfortable, welcoming environment. She cherishes the relationships she builds and is dedicated to making every visit a positive experience.

Beyond her private practice, Dr. Padyab shares her expertise as an adjunct faculty member at Midwestern University&#x27;s dental simulation clinic, mentoring the next generation of dental professionals. She proudly serves on the College of Dental Medicine Arizona&#x27;s alumni council and continuously enhances her skills through continuing education.

When she&#x27;s not creating beautiful smiles, Dr. Padyab loves traveling and spending quality time with her husband and their three playful rescue dogs—Lulu, Remy and Enzo—who keep life exciting with daily adventures!

A Vibrant Team

The Vibrant Smiles team is made up of compassionate dental professionals who love helping patients thrive. Whether we’re getting you checked in for your appointment, cleaning your teeth, or assisting Dr. Padyab with a procedure, we’re passionate about the work we do to help you look and feel your best.

What to Expect The Experience From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll be welcomed into an atmosphere of elegance and comfort, where our warm and attentive team ensures you feel right at home. Relax in our inviting guest lounge with a refreshing drink as you take a moment to fill out your new patient forms. Once you’re ready, our friendly hygienist will escort you to one of our modern, fully equipped operatories.

During your visit, you’ll enjoy a thorough cleaning, a comprehensive exam, and necessary x-rays. Dr. Padyab will personally review the results with you, providing a detailed explanation. If everything looks great, we’ll help you schedule your next visit with ease.

Should any areas require special attention, Dr. Padyab will take the time to discuss her findings, address your questions, and outline any treatment recommendations in a clear and reassuring way. Your care and comfort are our top priority every step of the way.

Caring for Our Community

Giving back

At Vibrant Smiles, our commitment goes beyond dentistry—we’re dedicated to making a positive impact in our community. We’re honored to partner with Foothills Animal Rescue, an organization devoted to rescuing, healing, and finding loving homes for animals in need.

For every new patient who joins our practice, we make a contribution to support their life-saving mission. When you choose Vibrant Smiles, you’re not only investing in your health—you’re also helping provide shelter, medical care, and second chances to countless animals in need.
