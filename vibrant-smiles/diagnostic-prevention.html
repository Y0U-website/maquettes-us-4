Preventive care is the foundation for long-term oral health. Even if your teeth feel fine, small issues like plaque buildup, tiny cavities, or gum inflammation can go unnoticed without regular checkups every six months.

Diagnostic & preventive dentistry allows us to identify these signs early on, helping you avoid more invasive procedures down the line. Think of it as a wellness check for your mouth that’s proactive rather than reactive. Not only does this protect your teeth and gums, but it also has a significant impact on your overall health.

Many systemic health concerns, including heart disease and diabetes, have been linked to poor oral health. By staying on top of diagnostic care, you’re taking a huge step toward maintaining a healthier body and greater peace of mind. The Benefits of Diagnostic & Prevention

Catch Problems Early Routine exams and digital X-rays allow Dr. Padyab to detect issues in their earliest stages, often before you feel any symptoms. Early intervention can save you time, money, and discomfort.

Maintain a Healthier Smile Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, keeping your teeth cleaner and brighter while reducing your risk of decay and gum disease. A healthy smile is a confident smile.

Access Affordable, Ongoing Care With our in-house membership plans starting at just $325/year, routine diagnostic & prevention care is more accessible, even if you’re without traditional dental insurance. It’s part of our commitment to patient-first dental care.

The Diagnostic & Prevention Treatment Process

Comprehensive Exam & Health History

Comprehensive Exam & Health History

Your visit begins with a detailed look at your oral and overall health history. Dr. Padyab takes the time to understand any concerns you may have and thoroughly examines your mouth, head, and neck for signs of dental or systemic conditions. This holistic approach helps ensure that no detail is overlooked while keeping your comfort a top priority.

Digital X-rays & Imaging

Digital X-rays & Imaging

Using low-radiation digital imaging, we capture high-resolution photos of your teeth and jaw. These images help us detect issues like hidden decay, impacted teeth, or bone loss, things we can&#x27;t always see with the naked eye. It’s quick, easy, and doesn’t require any uncomfortable film in your mouth.

Gentle Cleaning & Personalized Recommendations

Gentle Cleaning & Personalized Recommendations

Our skilled hygienists provide a thorough, gentle cleaning to remove plaque and tartar from areas your toothbrush can’t reach. Then, based on your unique needs, Dr. Padyab will discuss personalized home care tips and a wellness plan. Whether you need sealants, fluoride, or a roadmap for future gum care, we guide you every step of the way.
