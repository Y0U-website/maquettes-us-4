Frequently Asked Questions

How soon can I book an appointment?

Usually within a few days! We offer real-time online scheduling and flexible hours; just choose a time that works best for you.

Do I need insurance to book a visit?

Not at all. We welcome both insured and non-insured patients. If you don’t have coverage, we’ll walk you through options like our in-house savings plan or flexible payments.

What if I’m nervous about going to the dentist?

You’re not alone, and you’re in the right place. We’ve designed every detail around your comfort, from warm blankets and noise-cancelling headphones to gentle care and honest conversations.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Yes. We’ll confirm your benefits, share a clear estimate ahead of time, and go over all your payment options — no surprises, ever.

Do you use modern technology to improve my experience?

Absolutely. From goop-free digital scans to overhead TVs and online forms, we’ve invested in technology that makes your experience smoother, faster, and more comfortable.

What if I haven’t been to the dentist in years?

No judgment here. Whether it’s been a few months or many years, we’ll meet you exactly where you are and move forward together — at your pace.
