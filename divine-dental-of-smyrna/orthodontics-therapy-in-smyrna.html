  • WHY ORTHODONTIC TREATMENT IS IMPORTANT Orthodontic care can address a wide range of concerns that affect both oral health and daily comfort, including: Crooked or crowded teeth
  • Gaps or spacing issues
  • Overbite, underbite, or crossbite
  • Jaw misalignment
  • Difficulty cleaning between teeth
  • Uneven tooth wear
  • SURESMILE ALIGNERS SureSmile Aligners offer a clear, comfortable, and highly precise alternative to traditional braces. These custom-made aligners gradually shift teeth into proper alignment using advanced digital planning for accurate results. Benefits Of SureSmile Aligners Clear and discreet appearance
  • Removable for eating and cleaning
  • Smooth, comfortable fit
  • Digital treatment planning for predictable outcomes
  • Fewer in-office visits compared to traditional braces
  • WHAT TO EXPECT WITH SURESMILE TREATMENT Your orthodontic journey with SureSmile Aligners begins with a comprehensive evaluation and digital scans of your teeth. Using this information, a personalized treatment plan is created to map each stage of tooth movement. You will wear each set of aligners for a short period before progressing to the next set. Over time, your teeth gradually shift into their ideal positions. Most patients find SureSmile Aligners easy to maintain and comfortable to wear throughout treatment. Item Link
  • RETAINERS After orthodontic treatment is complete, retainers play a critical role in maintaining your results. Teeth naturally tend to shift over time, and retainers help keep them in their corrected positions. Why Retainers Matter Preserve orthodontic results
  • Prevent teeth from shifting back
  • Support long-term bite stability
  • Protect your investment in orthodontic care
  • WHO CAN BENEFIT FROM ORTHODONTICS Orthodontic treatment may be right for you if you: Want to improve tooth alignment or spacing
  • Experience bite-related discomfort or jaw strain
  • Have difficulty cleaning crowded teeth
  • Notice uneven wear on teeth
  • Want a healthier, more confident smile
  • CARING FOR YOUR ORTHODONTIC APPLIANCES Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential during orthodontic treatment. Patients should: Brush and floss daily
  • Clean aligners or retainers as directed
  • Attend scheduled follow-up visits
  • Report any discomfort or fit concerns promptly
