Missing One Tooth? We Can Help in…

If you are missing one tooth or a small number of adjacent teeth, a single tooth implant may be an ideal solution.

If bone loss has occurred due to a missing tooth, bone grafting may be recommended to rebuild support. During your consultation, our team will review your medical history, evaluate your teeth and gums, and use digital imaging to determine if an implant is the right option for you.

With consistent care, a dental implant can last for many years and support long-term oral health.

  • HOW THE IMPLANT PROCESS WORKS Assessment and Planning We begin with a comprehensive exam and advanced imaging to evaluate bone structure and determine precise implant placement. Implant Placement A titanium post is carefully placed into the jawbone. Initial healing occurs within days, while full integration with the bone typically takes three to six months. Restoration Once healing is complete, a custom ceramic crown is securely attached to restore the appearance and function of your tooth.
