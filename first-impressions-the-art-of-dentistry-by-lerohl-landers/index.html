A comfortable atmosphere makes the dental experience more enjoyable

Our dental office in Sandwich, Illinois is surrounded by nature. Large trees, flowering gardens, birds, squirrels, and other wildlife along with inviting seasonal foliage make for a more open and relaxed atmosphere during dental appointments.

Treating patients with the most effective, least invasive treatments available

We have comfortable operatory chairs for the patient, along with seats and padded benches for family so that our patients can feel relaxed throughout their appointments.

We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) free of charge for any appointment in our office. This service is very helpful for creating a relaxed and comfortable dental or hygiene (cleaning) visit at our office.
