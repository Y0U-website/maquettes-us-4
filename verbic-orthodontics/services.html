Services Invisalign ® Invisalign The virtually invisible way to straighten your teeth and achieve the smile you have always wanted. It comes in two types: Invisalign for adults and Invisalign Teen.

Invisalign Teen

Invisalign Teen is similar to Invisalign for adults, except that it has a tiny compliance indicator dot on the back. This dot will change color from blue to white to indicate that your teens are wearing their aligners for at least 22 hours daily.

Angel Aligners

Angel Clear aligners are made with a proprietary military polymer, master control s , which is designed to be durable, flexible and BPA-free. Similar to other clear aligner treatments, Angel uses a series of trays to move your teeth into your ideal positions, but with better contact surface area and more advanced force retention.

Angel aligners also stain less and are more comfortable since the edges of the aligners are smooth and scalloped. There advancements make for a more effective clear aligner treatment for a beautiful, straight smile- faster.

TMJ/TMD

The teeth on your upper and lower jaws typically work in pairs, so the pressure on your joints is evenly distributed when you chew or bite down. Temporomandibular Joint Disorder can occur when this pressure becomes uneven. For example, if crooked or missing teeth cause you to have an uneven bite balance.

In treating a temporomandibular joint disorder, Dr. Verbic will focus on finding ways to realign and re-balance your teeth and bite to reduce uneven strain on the jaw joints. Other treatment options may include orthodontic care or the replacement of missing teeth, which can help to correct the underlying problem and thus provide long-term relief.

Retainers

Transforming your teeth into a smile you’re proud to show off starts in the office, but it’s important to remember that to make sure your smile lasts a lifetime, you will need to wear a retainer. We want you to keep your beautiful smile, and we do that by including two sets of retainers with your orthodontic treatment.

Options for retainers include any combination of fixed (permanent) retainers, clear removable retainers, or traditional Hawley retainers.
