At Bespoke Dental, we provide a comprehensive range of services to maintain a healthy mouth and a brilliant smile. Our range extends from preventive treatments to modern treatments that prioritize not only clear dental care but also ease and convenience for patients in all age groups!

Dr. Cindy Rask knew she wanted to be a dentist from a young age. Growing up, her family valued their oral health, and her and her siblings all received orthodontic treatment at an early age. She loved her orthodontist and wants to create exceptional experiences for her patients just like she had. She graduated with a bachelor’s degree in biology from Florida International University.

Through her advanced training with Spear Education , Dr. Rask stays at the forefront of modern dentistry. This ongoing commitment allows her to refine her skills, integrate the latest techniques, and provide patients with the highest standard of comprehensive, evidence-based care.
