The practice

I began my career in dentistry as a dental hygienist and worked part time while I attended dental school, gaining valuable real-world experience. I graduated from Marquette University with a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene and then earned my Doctor of Dental Surgery at Marquette University School of Dentistry. Go Golden Eagles!

Throughout my career I have practiced in a variety of settings, including private practice and community health. I also was a full-time clinical professor at Marquette University School of Dentistry for several years. One of my passions is mentoring students and new graduates; helping them to grow into skilled and compassionate professionals.

I have lived in southeastern Wisconsin all my life. I have two wonderful adult children, one that lives here in Wisconsin and one in Washington. In my free time, I spend time riding my horse, walking my dogs, and enjoying the outdoors as much as possible. I also love sports! I grew up watching the Packers, Brewers and Bucks with my family and have passed that interest on to my kids.

I look forward to meeting you and helping you achieve a healthy smile! We are here to provide you reasons to smile!
