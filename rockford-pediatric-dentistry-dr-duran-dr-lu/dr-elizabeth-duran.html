Dr. Elizabeth Duran

“Kids are the best part of humanity; they will always put a smile on your face. Working with children, there is NEVER a boring or unhappy day at work! I love what I do.”

Dr. Liz has extensive training in all forms of behavior management techniques to help your child feel safe and comfortable at the dentist. Most often, she is able to complete treatment with her gentle personality and laughing gas alone. Depending on your child’s needs, Dr. Liz also has years of experience in sedation dentistry. Working alongside teams of anesthesia doctors, she has completed hundreds of cases under IV sedation and general anesthesia. She loves working with children with special needs, medically complex children, and children with dental phobias. Dr. Duran also speaks Spanish.

Dr. Liz lives in Rockford with her husband, two children, and sweet cat, Stella. When she’s not busy playing with her son and daughter, Dr. Liz loves to play piano and flute, work out, and cook healthy meals for her friends and family.

Our Dental Team

Our doctors have gone through additional years of training so that they are best equipped to provide a safe and enjoyable visit for your child. Like a pediatrician, we are experts in providing dental care for children.

Empowering Children

We love what we do! Nothing brings us more joy than seeing your child laugh, leave with a smile, and feel excited for their next visit! Your child will get to pick their own toothbrush and flavor of toothpaste and enjoy fun prizes

Comfortable Environment

Often, positive childhood dental experiences can manifest into a lifetime of oral health. Our goal is to provide a safe, comfortable, and fun environment for your child, while providing state-of-the-art pediatric dental care.
