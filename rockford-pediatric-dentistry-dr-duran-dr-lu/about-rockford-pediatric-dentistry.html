Rockford Pediatric Dentistry

Our greatest goal is to empower children and their families to establish healthy oral habits—habits that continue for generations to come. Often, positive childhood dental experiences can manifest into a lifetime of oral health. Our goal is to provide a safe, comfortable, and fun environment for your child, while providing state-of-the-art pediatric dental care.

As moms ourselves, we empathize with parents and treat every child as if they were our own.

Our doctors have gone through additional years of training so that they are best equipped to provide a safe and enjoyable visit for your child. Like a pediatrician, we are experts in providing dental care for children — from analyzing growth and development to diagnosing orthodontic needs and treating cavities on young children. Depending on your child’s needs, we also work with anesthesiologists to provide sedation options for your child. With years of experience, we are eager to provide the highest standard of pediatric dental care to the Rockford community.

Beyond our credentials, we love what we do! Nothing brings us more joy than seeing your child laugh with our team, leave with a smile, and feel excited for their next visit! Your child will get to pick their own toothbrush and flavor of toothpaste, enjoy fun prizes, and pick a fun movie to watch during their appointment.

There is nothing more rewarding to us than watching your child grow into a happy and confident dental patient, ready to take on the world with their healthy smile!
