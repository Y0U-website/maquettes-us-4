Fluoride Treatments While it is widely known fluoride treatments are particularly helpful for children with developing teeth, fluoride treatments can also be of great value to older patients. Teeth are worn down over time from “normal wear and tear” may be hypersensitive to hot and cold, and/or more susceptible to periodontal (gum) disease.

In these instances, fluoride treatments can be both a preventative and therapeutic measure. The most common fluoride treatment is a take home rinse which is used daily.

Pit & Fissure Sealants

Unfamiliar to many, pit and fissure sealants, are a treatment to prevent tooth decay in children. Pit and fissure sealants are a protective coating placed over the chewing area of the back teeth, and generally not applicable or useful for adults.

It is important to know that while pit and fissure sealants are often covered by insurance, it is also true an age limit for coverage often applies, usually 14 years of age.
