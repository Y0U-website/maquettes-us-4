Fillings & Restorations FAQ

Dental fillings are materials like composite or amalgam used to repair cavities or minor tooth damage, restoring function and structure.

The procedure is performed with local anesthesia, ensuring comfort, with minimal discomfort during or after treatment.

With proper care, composite fillings last 5-10 years, amalgam 10-15 years, and inlays/onlays up to 20 years or more.

Fillings treat smaller cavities, while restorations, like inlays or onlays, repair larger damage or structural issues.
