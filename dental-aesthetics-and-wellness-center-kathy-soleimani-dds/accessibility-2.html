Accessibility

We take accessibility seriously in our practice and on our website.

If you find this website difficult to use for any reason, we are available to help.

Our email address is ksoleimani@yahoo.com

This site is designed to the best of our ability to be navigated and understood by users with disabilities. We try to make it compatible with screen readers, keyboard navigation, and magnification and dictation tools.

Our site is hosted on mainstream platforms using a variety of utilities that are often outside of our control. It is monitored to maintain accessibility, but we understand that users’ needs vary. In the event an issue is found, we will make every effort to offer acceptable accommodations.
