Dr. Soleimani is recognized for her meticulous, detail-oriented approach to dentistry, ensuring that every patient receives personalized care tailored to their individual needs. As part of our eco-conscious commitment, we use only BPA-free and BIS-GMA-free composites and sealants, reducing exposure to potentially harmful chemicals and supporting long-term wellness.

Dr. Kathy Soleimani is a highly trained dentist with a love for creating beautiful, healthy smiles!
