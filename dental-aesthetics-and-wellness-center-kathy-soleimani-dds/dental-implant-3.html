What are dental implants?

Dental implants act as artificial tooth roots, creating a strong foundation for natural-looking restorations. Each implant includes:

⦿ A ceramic or titanium post placed in the jawbone

⦿ A custom restoration, such as a crown, bridge, or denture attachment

This allows us to replace one tooth or multiple teeth while preserving long-term oral health.

How are implants placed?

Dr. Soleimani uses a minimally invasive, laser-assisted approach combined with advanced iTero® digital scanning :

⦿ Small, precise holes are created in the jawbone using the Fotona LightWalker® laser to minimize bleeding and accelerate healing.

⦿ iTero scanning ensures restorations fit comfortably and look natural.

⦿ The ceramic or titanium post is then placed and allowed to fuse with your bone before the restoration is attached.

This high-tech, biomimetic approach means less discomfort, faster healing, and longer-lasting results.

Advantages of Ceramic & Titanium Implants

⦿ Ceramic SDS implants are 100 % metal-free, ideal for patients with sensitivities or seeking holistic options.

⦿ Both ceramic and titanium integrate with bone to prevent shifting and preserve jaw health.

⦿ Laser placement + digital scanning reduces surgical trauma.

⦿ Biomimetic restorations mimic natural teeth while preserving tooth structure.

⦿ Ceramic and titanium implants are stable, strong, and designed to last for decades.

⦿ Eat, speak, and smile confidently with secure, natural-feeling replacements.

⦿ Ceramic blends seamlessly with gum tissue, avoiding dark shadows sometimes seen with titanium.

⦿ Restorations match the color, shape, and look of natural teeth.

Am I a candidate for implants?

⦿ Have missing teeth and want a permanent solution.

⦿ Have healthy gums and adequate bone density.

⦿ Prefer minimally invasive, biocompatible care.

Patients with uncontrolled chronic illness, heavy smoking, or certain cancer treatments may not qualify—but Dr. Soleimani will recommend the best restorative path for your smile.

Implants & Dentures

⦿ Overdentures snap onto implants for stability without adhesives.

⦿ Fixed implant dentures attach securely for a permanent solution that only a dentist can remove.

This creates superior comfort, stability, and long-term function.
