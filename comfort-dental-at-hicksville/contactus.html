Comprehensive Care Under One Roof

From preventive cleanings to advanced restorative work, we offer a full spectrum of dental services — all delivered with the gentle, attentive care you deserve.

Exams & Diagnostics

Thorough evaluations using digital X-rays, panoramic imaging, and intraoral photography for precise diagnoses.

Our diagnostic process includes comprehensive and periodic oral evaluations, digital bitewing and periapical X-rays, full-mouth panoramic radiographs, and intraoral photography. Digital imaging reduces radiation exposure compared to traditional film and produces instant, high-resolution images. We also use Pearl AI technology to annotate your X-rays in real time, so you can see exactly what we see and understand your diagnosis clearly.

Cleanings & Prevention Professional cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants to maintain optimal oral health for children and adults.

Regular professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that brushing and flossing alone cannot reach. Our hygienists provide both adult prophylaxis and children’s cleanings, along with fluoride varnish treatments to strengthen enamel and dental sealants to protect the grooves of back teeth from decay. We also offer personalized oral hygiene instruction and develop individualized care plans based on each patient’s needs.

Cosmetic Dentistry Porcelain veneers, composite bonding, and aesthetic restorations to enhance the appearance of your smile.

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth — including their color, shape, size, alignment, and overall smile aesthetics. Porcelain veneers are thin shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, gaps, discoloration, or uneven sizing. Composite bonding uses tooth-colored resin to reshape or repair teeth in a single visit. We work with each patient to determine the right approach based on their goals and the condition of their teeth.

Crowns & Bridges Premium porcelain and zirconia crowns, onlays, and bridges to restore full form, function, and beauty.

A dental crown is a custom-made cap that covers and protects a damaged or weakened tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance. We use porcelain and zirconia materials that are color-matched to blend with your natural teeth. Onlays and inlays are a more conservative option when only a portion of the tooth needs restoration. A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent teeth, restoring your bite and preventing neighboring teeth from shifting.

Root Canal Therapy When root canal therapy is needed, we coordinate care and, in most cases, refer to a trusted endodontist.

Root canal therapy is recommended when the pulp (soft tissue inside a tooth) becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay, cracks, or trauma. The procedure involves removing the damaged tissue, disinfecting the interior of the tooth, and sealing it to prevent reinfection. The tooth is then typically restored with a crown. Whether a root canal is performed in-house is determined on a case-by-case basis; in most cases, patients are referred to a trusted endodontist for this specialized care, and we coordinate treatment with them to ensure a smooth experience.

Periodontal Care Comprehensive gum disease treatment, from scaling and root planing to osseous surgery and tissue regeneration.

Periodontal (gum) disease affects the tissues that support and surround your teeth. Left untreated, it can lead to bone loss and tooth loss. Treatment begins with non-surgical scaling and root planing — a deep cleaning that removes bacteria and tartar from below the gumline. For more advanced cases, our board-certified Periodontist Dr. Nicholas Toscano provides osseous surgery, guided tissue regeneration, bone grafting, crown lengthening, and localized antimicrobial delivery.

Dental Implants Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement — every step of the implant process is completed in-house, from extractions to surgical placement, custom abutments, and final restoration.

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as an artificial tooth root. Over time, the implant fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture. Implants help preserve jawbone density and prevent the shifting of surrounding teeth. Our team handles every step of the implant process in-house — including extractions performed as part of your implant treatment, bone grafting, and sinus augmentation — so your entire care stays under one roof from start to finish.

Dentures & Nightguards

Partial and flexible dentures are available on a case-by-case basis, along with occlusal guards and nightguards to protect your teeth while you sleep.

Partial dentures replace multiple missing teeth and are removable for daily cleaning. We offer traditional partial dentures and flexible partials when they are the right clinical fit for the patient, and otherwise focus on other restorative options. Nightguards and occlusal guards are custom-fabricated appliances worn during sleep to protect against teeth grinding (bruxism), jaw clenching, and TMJ-related discomfort. All appliances are made from impressions of your teeth for a precise, individualized fit. We also provide repairs and adjustments as needed.
