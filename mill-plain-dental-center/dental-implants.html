What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. Over 3 to 6 months, the implant fuses with the bone through a biological process called osseointegration literally becoming part of your jaw. Once integrated, we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture on top.

Dental implants are the gold standard for tooth replacement because they look, feel, and function like natural teeth and unlike dentures or bridges, they preserve jawbone density rather than letting it deteriorate. They’re considered permanent tooth replacement because the implant itself can last a lifetime.

Start with a Consultation Consultation We evaluate your missing tooth situation, jawbone density, and overall oral health with digital X-rays and 3D CBCT imaging. This gives us a complete picture of your bone, nerves, and adjacent teeth - essential for precise planning.

Digital implant planning session

Using 3D imaging software, we plan the implant placement precisely before surgery - exact position, angle, depth, and final tooth position. For patients searching "where can I get digital implant planning in Vancouver, WA," this step is what separates predictable outcomes from guesswork.

Implant placement surgery

The titanium implant is placed in the jawbone under local anesthesia (with sedation options if you prefer). Using digital surgical guides, placement is precise to fractions of a millimeter. Most patients are surprised by how routine this feels.

Osseointegration (healing phase)

Over 3 to 6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone - literally becoming part of you. This is the most important phase for long-term success. We see you for brief check-ins; no chairside time is needed during most of this period.

Custom restoration design

Once integrated, we take digital impressions and design your final restoration - crown, bridge, or denture, depending on your case. The custom restoration is fabricated to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and translucency.

Final restoration placement

Your permanent crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant(s). We check fit, bite, and aesthetics. You walk out with your new permanent teeth - and now you understand why patients call implants life-changing.
