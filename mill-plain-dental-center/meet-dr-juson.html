Meet Dr. Natalya Juson

Before your first appointment, take a moment to get to know your dentist, Dr. Natalya Juson. You can trust Dr. Juson and her dedicated team to provide exceptional dental care in a comfortable, compassionate setting. Contact Mill Plain Dental Center today for an appointment.

Hi, I’m Dr. Natalya Juson. I attended college at Portland State University and graduated from OHSU School of Dentistry in 2008. Dentistry is my passion. I’ve been a dentist for over 15 years, and it brings me joy and a sense of fulfillment. I have worked for many years in public health dentistry in Multnomah County. In 2020, I relocated to Vancouver, Washington, with my husband and four kids. We enjoy sports, traveling, and spending time together.

I am passionate about helping my patients improve their dental health and well-being. I find great fulfillment in helping patients attain a healthy smile while having a pleasant experience in my clinic. With the combination of being a lifelong learner of both cutting-edge clinical knowledge and the use of technology, my goal is to provide my patients with the best experience possible.
