Our Services

What We Offer

We offer a broad range of dental services ranging from dental cleanings to emergency treatment of dental swelling and pain. We specialize in general dentistry with added treatment of all forms of gum disease including gum grafts and the Pin Hole Technique. We proudly offer dental implant dentistry from surgical placement to final restoration. We are proficient in dental extractions and other oral surgery procedures. We also provide IV sedation. We want our patients to be able to eat what they want more so than eating what they can.

Sedation Dentistry

Dr. Linatoc is one of the few dentist qualified to perform IV sedation for dental patients. He maintains a conscious sedation permit with the Georgia Board of Dentistry. This allows Dr. Linatoc to perform on patients with special needs.
