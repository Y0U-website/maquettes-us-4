Giving children a positive start in dentistry.

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist & Owner

"I became a pediatric dentist to help children succeed from the very beginning with their dental health. I connect with children directly and help them focus on their strengths and capabilities. It gives me a great sense of purpose to know that our office can help families have positive experiences with us."

Dr. Hae Jin Kim is a second-generation Korean-American who was raised in Boston and Los Angeles. She chose dentistry because she genuinely cares about others and wants to help them with their problems. The best part of her job is forming relationships with children and families. She enjoys watching children grow up, especially those who were once anxious become more confident with every visit.

Through the love and support of her family, Dr. Kim pursued the specialty of children’s dentistry. Today, she’s a pediatric dentist and co-owner at Plover Pediatric Dentistry in Potomac, MD.

Outside of the office, Dr. Kim enjoys running marathons, is a Board Member of Girls on the Run, is actively involved with the Montgomery County Road Runners, and volunteers with the Maryland Medical Reserve Corps.

"I let parents know that I want to understand them and their concerns. With children, I have found that if you start by talking with them about things they are interested in, they often become more engaged with their dental care."

Dr. Pedro M. Alvarez grew up on the St. Lawrence River in a small rural town in upstate New York. His father was a dentist and his mother a nurse, so he was exposed to healthcare early on. Dr. Alvarez started as a general dentist but found that many adults have anxiety because of a bad experience as children. So he set out to make a difference as a pediatric dentist.

In his free time, he enjoys spending time with family and friends, running, sailing, doing handiwork, and learning how things work.
