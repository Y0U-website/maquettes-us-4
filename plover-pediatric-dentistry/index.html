The practice

Your Child Is Our Primary Focus At Plover Pediatric Dentistry, your child is the hero. We pride ourselves on providing individualized care while sharing our expertise, so your child feels empowered to smile more! We make their visit all about them and give them our undivided attention.

We want to support our young patients by educating them first and giving a sense of security and peace. We are open to their curiosity because we believe it is knowledge that will overcome their fear.

From the very start, we make your child’s visit fun and educational. We are a patient-centered practice, which means we put your child’s needs first to ensure they feel comfortable and confident in the care we provide.

Our team Learn more about our team is like one, big, happy family who work together to make kids (and parents) smile. Our Potomac dentists have created a kind, caring, and compassionate culture where children and parents feel part of our family.

With more than ten years experience, our Potomac pediatric dentists know what is needed to make your child’s experience unique. Our doctors love interacting with children on their level, so they instantly feel calm and relaxed.

“I try to listen and respect each child and family, imparting dentistry in a useful, understandable, and affirming way.”

“I thrive when helping children address their anxiety at a young age. I always try to reinforce positive experiences so they look forward to visiting the dentist.”

Through proactive dentistry, dentists can assist with life-long learning and set your child up for future smile success.
