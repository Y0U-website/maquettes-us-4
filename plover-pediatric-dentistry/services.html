Sealants for Kids

Protect your child’s teeth against cavities.

Your child’s smile is still growing, and sure, baby teeth fall out, but that doesn’t mean we can let cavities occur on primary or permanent teeth. Sealants are one of the easiest ways to prevent cavities from forming in children who are still learning to brush.

Our Potomac, MD pediatric dentist applies a safe coating to your child’s teeth to prevent cavity-causing bacteria from reaching the surface. Despite their small size, baby teeth play a big role in your child’s dental health and alignment of teeth, so let’s keep them healthy.
