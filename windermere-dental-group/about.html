Meet Dr. Susana Moncada

Behind every confident smile at Windermere Dental Group is the vision of Dr. Susana Moncada — a dentist known for blending advanced clinical training with an artistic eye and a patient-first mindset.

Originally from Venezuela, Dr. Moncada graduated with honors from the Universidad Central de Venezuela before continuing her education in the United States. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Nova Southeastern University, again graduating with honors, and is a member of the prestigious Omicron Kappa Epsilon dental honor society.

As a native Spanish-speaking dentist, Dr. Moncada provides a fully bilingual (English/Spanish) experience. This ensures patients feel heard, understood, and confident in every decision regarding their care.
