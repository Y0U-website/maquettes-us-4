The practice

Sunrise Dental of Olympia serves the residents of Olympia, Washington, and many of the surrounding communities and counties. The professional staff at the facility offer crowns, bridges, dentures, root canals, extractions, and teeth cleanings (please note that extractions and root canals are on a case-by-case basis, evaluation is required beforehand).

If you have any questions or would like more information on how we can care for your teeth and mouth, please contact us today.

Having a dental practice perform a checkup and regular cleaning gives a preventive approach to dental care. These general dentistry treatments help prevent problems with your teeth, gums and jaw bones, or they catch and manage problems before they worsen.

A common request we receive is how to make a smile whiter and brighter. This is a relatively simple and quick treatment option that can be performed periodically.

When you have missing teeth, an alternative solution to dentures is dental implants. While dentures are removable, dental implants provide permanent new teeth to restore your mouth’s function and appearance.

Our team of skilled professionals combines expertise with a compassionate approach to ensure your comfort and confidence during every visit.

At Sunrise Dental of Olympia, we offer a wide range of dental services, including crowns, bridges, dentures, root canals, extractions, and teeth cleanings.

We understand that our patients have busy lives, which is why we offer appointments during early evenings and one weekend a month.
