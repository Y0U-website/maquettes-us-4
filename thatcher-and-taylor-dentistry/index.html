The practice

Our practice was founded in Sun City Center over 50 years ago and we take pride in the relationships we have built in the community. Although we are the oldest practice in the local area, we provide modern dental care and the most advanced technology available to ensure a healthy and lasting smile.

Please call to schedule a thorough evaluation, and we will work with you to create an individualized plan that will meet your dental health needs and goals.

Providing great patient care is the primary goal of our team. From the moment you enter our practice we want you to feel like a member of the family.

At Thatcher and Taylor Dentistry, we specialize in Prosthodontics, also known as Prosthetic Dentistry. Prosthodontics is one of the nine recognized specialties of the American Dental Association and focuses on the diagnosis, treatment planning, rehabilitation and maintenance of the oral function, comfort, appearance and oral health of dental patients.

Our service is at the core of everything we do. Our dental team in Sun City Center works together to provide a warm, comfortable, and relaxing environment.

We always want our patients to feel informed and confident they are getting the best treatment possible. Learn more about our practice and your dental health.
