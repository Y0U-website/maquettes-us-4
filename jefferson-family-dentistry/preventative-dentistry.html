Preventative Dentistry

At Jefferson Family Dentistry, we believe in fostering overall wellness through comprehensive preventive care. This cornerstone of our practice includes thorough dental exams to assess your oral health, meticulous teeth cleaning to remove plaque and tartar buildup, vital oral cancer screenings to ensure early detection, and the application of protective sealants to shield vulnerable tooth surfaces from decay. By focusing on these proactive measures, we aim to help you maintain a healthy smile and prevent future dental issues, contributing to your long-term well-being.

Dental Exams

A dental exam at Jefferson Family Dentistry is a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health. You can expect a thorough visual inspection of your teeth and gums, where we’ll check for signs of decay, gum disease, and other potential issues. The dentist will also evaluate your bite, check for signs of TMJ disorders, and perform an oral cancer screening. We’ll discuss your oral hygiene habits and address any concerns you may have, providing personalized recommendations to help you maintain a healthy smile.

Teeth Cleanings

A professional teeth cleaning at Jefferson Family Dentistry is designed to be a thorough and comfortable experience. Our skilled hygienist will begin by meticulously removing plaque and tartar buildup from all surfaces of your teeth, paying close attention to both above and below the gumline. This process is essential for preventing gum disease and maintaining healthy gums.
