Meet Our Dentists

Dr. Sarwat Zaffer is proud to lead Jefferson Family Dentistry with a vision rooted in compassionate, high-quality care. With over a decade of experience practicing dentistry across India, Texas, New York, and New Jersey, she brings a global perspective and a patient-centered approach to every treatment. Now living in Lake Hopatcong with her husband and young daughter, Dr. Zaffer is deeply committed to serving the local community. Her philosophy is simple: a dental office should feel like a welcoming space where families are treated with kindness and respect.

Dr. Enrique Giralt is the founding dentist of Jefferson Family Dentistry and has been proudly serving the community for over 30 years. With a deep commitment to patient care, he continues to provide exceptional dental treatment while fostering long-term relationships with generations of families. Dr. Giralt remains dedicated to staying current with advancements in dentistry to ensure his patients receive the highest standard of care.

Theresa has been a valued member of Jefferson Family Dentistry for nearly 20 years. She takes great pride in building lasting relationships with her patients and is committed to helping them maintain healthy, confident smiles. Outside the office, Theresa enjoys reading, traveling, and cherishing time with her grandchildren.

A proud New Jersey native, Jeanne has been delivering exceptional dental hygiene care for many years. Known for her thorough approach and warm demeanor, Jeanne takes the time to connect with each patient, ensuring every visit is both comfortable and meaningful. Outside the office, she’s a devoted mother of three and loves spending time visiting her children.
