Insurance Information

At Jefferson Family Dentistry, we strive to make quality dental care accessible to everyone. We proudly accept a wide range of insurance plans, including United Health Care, Medicaid, Aetna, Medicare, MetLife, Delta Dental, Humana, and Cigna. For those without traditional insurance coverage, we also welcome Fee For Service patients. Our goal is to simplify the financial aspects of your dental care, ensuring you can focus on achieving and maintaining a healthy smile. Please contact our office to verify your specific plan details and explore how we can help you maximize your benefits.

Financing Options

At Jefferson Family Dentistry, we understand that achieving optimal oral health is an investment in your overall well-being. To make quality dental care accessible for every member of your family, we are pleased to offer CareCredit, a trusted healthcare credit card. CareCredit provides flexible financing options, allowing you to manage your dental expenses with convenient monthly payments. This means you can prioritize your family’s smiles without delaying necessary treatments or procedures.

We believe that financial considerations shouldn’t be a barrier to exceptional dental care. With CareCredit, you can choose from a range of payment plans, including options with shorter-term, interest-free periods, or longer-term plans with reduced APRs. Our team is here to assist you in understanding how CareCredit can help you achieve your dental goals, making it easier to maintain healthy smiles for years to come. We are committed to providing transparent and supportive financial solutions, ensuring that your focus remains on your family’s oral health and comfort.
