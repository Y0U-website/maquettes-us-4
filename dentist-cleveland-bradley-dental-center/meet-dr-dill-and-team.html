Meet Dr. Dill And His Team

Mark S. Dill, DDS/Owner

Dr. Mark Dill is a Chattanooga native and owner of Bradley Dental Center, where he blends his passion for patient care with precision and integrity. Married to his wife, Laura, since 2003, they have three children, and he draws inspiration from his close-knit family. Dr. Dill’s philosophy of care is to provide the most conservative treatments with the best long-term outcomes. He approaches every patient as if they may have dental anxiety, going at a comfortable pace and explaining every step building trust through clear communication and empathy.

Dr. Dill’s favorite part of dentistry is educating his patients, not just about procedures but about their options ensuring they understand the why behind their care. He takes great pride in his highly trained staff and the positive culture of his practice, often hearing patients rave about the team’s energy and warmth.

Toria, COO/Registered Dental Assistant

Toria is our Office Manager and a Registered Dental Assistant. She has been with us since 2009. She received her certification through the Chattanooga College dental assisting program in 2009. She is a Registered Dental Assistant through the State of Tennessee. She received extra qualifications in dental radiology and nitrous oxide monitoring, and currently serves on the Chattanooga College Administrative Board. She is also a member of the American Dental Assisting Association.

Toria and her husband, Gary, have been married since April 2006 and currently reside in Georgia. They enjoy spending time with their sons, Kyle and Aiden. Toria and her family are members of Salem Baptist Church. She also enjoys camping, Georgia football, reading, and spending time with family and friends.

Sherry, Office Manager

Sherry is a Registered dental assistant through the state of Tennessee and graduated in 2008 from Chattanooga college with summa cuma lada.

She is licensed in nitrous oxide monitoring, dental radiology, coronal polishing, and sealant placement.

Sherry enjoys the opportunity in helping change a person’s life by changing their smile and helping patients overcome the fear of dentistry with comforting care. Sherry has been with Nooga Dentistry since 2021.

Sherry and her husband have been married since 2003 and have one child together, his name is Brantley. They love going on adventures with their son, spending time out on the lake on their boat, camping, hiking and drag racing.
