Meet Dr. Aldin

Dr. Dalia Aldin

Dr. Dalia Aldin is a Houston native who grew up in the area. She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Houston, graduating with honors (Go Coogs!), before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from UT Health San Antonio, where she also graduated with honors

Dr. Aldin is bilingual in English and Arabic and is currently learning Spanish. Outside of dentistry, she enjoys animal rescue, playing tennis and pickleball, attending concerts, exploring Houston’s diverse food and cultural scene, and spending time with family, friends, and her beloved cats. She feels privileged to care for patients in the community and looks forward to building lasting relationships while helping each patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.
