What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant replaces a single tooth. Oftentimes, they are the only way to fully restore both function and aesthetics to your smile. This is especially true when you’re missing one or more missing teeth.

At its core, a dental implant is a titanium screw installed in the jawbone. Over time, this metal fuses with the jawbone to secure the structure of the surrounding teeth. Once this post is ready, your Houston dentist attaches a cap to the top.

Virtually indistinguishable from a natural tooth, dental implants offer a host of benefits. Moreover, with dental implants, Houston patients have a stronger support structure for dentures or dental bridges.

The Benefits of Dental Implants: Houston Smiles that Last

With dental implants, Houston dentists have an excellent way to restore or improve your smile. Offering an array of benefits, this technique provides you with a beautiful smile that lasts a lifetime.

Natural Look and Feel

One of the main benefits of an implant is that it restores your chewing power. Many patients feel no difference between their implanted teeth and natural teeth. With your implant, you can eat, brush, and floss completely normally.

Long-Lasting

While a dental bridge lasts about a decade, with dental implants, Houston patients have something that lasts a lifetime with proper care. Made from titanium, these implants integrate into the jawbone. Because it is biocompatible and non-toxic, the body does not reject it.

All in all, that alone makes them a powerful replacement for teeth.

Prevent Bone Loss

When you have missing teeth, the jawbone in the empty space starts to deteriorate from a lack of stimulation. Without the structure of an implant, bone loss begins in the first year and continues to impact the surrounding teeth.

Improved Stability

The gap from a missing tooth causes the surrounding teeth to shift towards the gap. As this pulls your teeth out of position, it impacts your bite and appearance. With dental implants, Houston dentists improve the stability of your jaw and teeth.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

With dental implants, Houston patients have a reliable replacement for missing tooth root. A natural root stimulates healthy growth in the jaw bone. However, when you lose a tooth and the root, this stimulation ends. As the regeneration process stops, the bone begins to deteriorate.

To replace this stimulation, we use a small titanium post. Set in your jaw, the titanium fuses with the bone as it heals and replaces the root.

Because titanium is biocompatible, it forms a secure bond with the surrounding bones and tissues. After the healing process completes, the implant provides a stable and secure foundation for the restoration.

In this case, the restoration is a crown, bridge, or denture attached to the post. Together, they restore the function of your smile. While we use advanced technology to place implants at our Houston dental office, the results are pretty basic.
