Treatment and Prevention

Periodontal disease can only be treated with the help of a dental professional, and should not be allowed to remain for very long. Less severe forms of periodontal disease, like gingivitis, can be treated with good dental hygiene and regular visits to your dentists. Brush your teeth at least twice a day, floss, and rinse well.
