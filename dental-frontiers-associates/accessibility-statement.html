General

Dentalfrontiers.com strives to ensure that its services are accessible to people with disabilities. Dentalfrontiers.com has invested a significant amount of resources to help ensure that its website is made easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities, with the strong belief that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort and independence.

Accessibility on Dentalfrontiers.com

Dentalfrontiers.com makes available the UserWay Website Accessibility Widget that is powered by a dedicated accessibility server. The software allows Dentalfrontiers.com to improve its compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.0).

Enabling the Accessibility Menu

The accessibility menu on Dentalfrontiers.com can be enabled by clicking the accessibility menu icon that appears on the corner of the page. After triggering the accessibility menu, please wait a moment for the accessibility menu to load in its entirety.

Disclaimer

Dentalfrontiers.com continues his efforts to constantly improve the accessibility of its site and services in the belief that it is our collective moral obligation to allow seamless, accessible and unhindered use also for those of us with disabilities.Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on Dentalfrontiers.com fully accessible, some content may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards.
