What’s the difference between a bridge and an implant?

In the case of a bridge, both teeth on each side of the missing tooth trimmed down, an impression is made and sent to a lab. The lab fabricates a 3 unit structure that is sent back to the dentist to replace teeth. The bridge is then cemented onto those two neighboring teeth. An advantage to implants is that no other teeth need to be touched and they can be flossed individually. Also, when a dental bridge breaks or decays, the entire bridge may need to be removed. This is not the case with a dental implant, which functions just like an individual tooth.

I’m missing some teeth. What is the best way to replace them?

Dental implants are a terrific way to replace teeth. It is a minimally invasive procedure. Implants replace the chewing forces lost by a missing tooth. They also hold the bone and prevent other teeth from shifting.

Important to Replace Missing Teeth

It is important to replace missing teeth . As we lose teeth, the forces of our bite are shifted to the remaining teeth. It is important to replace our back teeth because that is where the majority of our chewing and force is applied.

Advantages of Dental Implants

Dental implants are stronger than your natural teeth because a dental implant fuses to the bone. They also do not decay. They do require regular maintenance, just like your other teeth.

Natural-Looking Dental Implants

Dental implants feel and function like a normal tooth. Cosmetics is very important and they should look just like your other teeth.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants do not require special tools like threaders or adhesives. You brush and floss your implants the same way you do your natural teeth. You also visit your dentist at least twice per year for dental exams and teeth cleanings again, the same recommendation applies if you do not have dental implants.

During your dental exams in Annapolis, your dentist checks your teeth and implants and looks for emerging oral health conditions that may pose a threat. He treats those issues and restores your oral health. A dental cleaning removes the bacteria that lead to decay, periodontal disease, and restoration failure.

Proper care ensures that your remaining teeth and implants can last for life!
