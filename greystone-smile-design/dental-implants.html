Are dental implants worth it?

Dental implants are becoming more popular today for a number of reasons. Implants are utilized to offer patients a foundation for new restorative teeth where natural teeth are missing or have been extracted. The implant offers the patient the opportunity to regain normal function of the tooth without being forced to resort to a bridge or a denture. If an implant can be placed, it is always the treatment of choice for missing teeth.

Though dental implants cost more than other dental restorations including dental bridges, partial dentures, and traditional full dentures, they are a wonderful investment for many of our patients .

Dental implants can last a lifetime.

Aside from maintaining bone density in the jaw and keeping the jawbone from shrinking, dental implants have other benefits that patients can consider as well. These restorations are natural-looking and fully functioning , acting just as previous natural teeth did at one time.

This stable, strong, and durable restoration is often touted as the “gold standard” in tooth replacement .

Dental Implants For Dentures (Snap-In Dentures)

A great option to replace all of your teeth is an implant retained denture . Some may call this a “ Snap-In Denture ”.

False teeth are false teeth, right? Not exactly. There are a few options available for replacing teeth, and dentures retained with dental implants are becoming the standard of care — and for good reason.

Photo above shows a model of false teeth (denture) and 4 dental implants to retain and support the denture.

Regular dentures can rub against the gums, and they have nothing to hold them in place — especially in the lower jaw. Because there are no teeth or implants to stimulate it, the bone shrinks, and chewing can become close to impossible.

But implant-supported dentures draw strength from the bone. They aren’t meant to rest on the gums; but instead the bone anchors them in place. This gives them added strength and prevents them from slipping . In addition, dental implants also stimulate the bone, and as a result, the bone is far less likely to resorb or shrink or create any sore spots.

Mini implants are placed in the jaw and anchored firmly in the surrounding bone. Then attachments are placed in the underside of the denture that connect to the implants. The denture is then snapped onto the implants, keeping it stable and secure .

All-on-X (Four or Six) Dental Implants (Teeth in a Day)

A minimally invasive solution with a fixed full-arch restoration for high patient satisfaction.

A relatively new treatment called the All-on-X Dental Implant Procedure uses four or more implants on the top and four or more on the bottom, to which a fixed full-arch restoration are connected.

The All-on-X (Four or Six) treatment concept is a cost-efficient, graftless solution that can provide patients with a fixed full-arch prosthesis on the day of surgery.

Patients with edentulism (toothlessness) are often eligible for the All-on-X (Four or Six) treatment concept. A thorough evaluation of any patient is necessary in order to choose the right treatment and to establish a predictable treatment outcome.
