Meet. Dr. Buchanan

Trenton H. Buchanan, DMD, FAGD

Trenton H. Buchanan, DMD, FAGD, is a restorative dentist for the entire family . His commitment to excellence not only emphasizes the technical skills of dentistry, but also reflects strong components of kindness and compassion .

Top Rated Dentist team at Greystone Smile Design in the Hoover/Highway 280 area of Birmingham, AL.

Only 6 % of General Dentists in the USA & Canada have earned their AGD Fellowship.

Why Choose Dr. Buchanan? Dr. Buchanan In The Media Request Appointment

Dr. Buchanan graduated with honors from Auburn University in 2002 and earned his doctorate from the University of Alabama School of Dentistry in 2006. Looking to further his education, he completed a general practice residency at Denver Health Medical Center in Denver, Colorado, with a focus on medically compromised patients, oral surgery, and advanced treatment planning. Dr. Buchanan practiced for 2 years in Denver before moving back to Birmingham.

Located just 15 minutes Southeast of downtown Birmingham with free parking.
