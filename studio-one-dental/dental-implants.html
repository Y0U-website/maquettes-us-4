Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Patients who may face challenges include those with significant bone loss, uncontrolled gum disease, or certain medical conditions that impair healing. In many of these cases, preparatory treatments like bone grafting or periodontal therapy can make implants a viable option. The Dental Implant Process

A multi-step journey to a permanent, natural-feeling smile

Consultation and Digital Imaging

Dr. Gonzalez evaluates your oral health, takes detailed digital scans of your jaw, and discusses your tooth replacement options. Together, you create a plan tailored to your goals and budget.

Implant Placement

A small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. This post acts as an artificial root that will support your new tooth or restoration.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the following months, the jawbone naturally fuses with the titanium implant in a process called osseointegration. This creates a rock-solid foundation for your restoration. A temporary tooth is provided during this period.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant has fully integrated, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached. This will hold your permanent crown, bridge, or denture.

Custom Restoration

A custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis is designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. It is securely attached to the abutment, completing your smile.

Advantages of Dental Implants

Why implants are the preferred choice for tooth replacement

Natural Look and Feel

Implants are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Most people cannot tell the difference between an implant and a real tooth.

Preserve Jawbone Health

Unlike bridges and dentures, implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that occurs when teeth are missing.

No Impact on Neighboring Teeth

Implants stand on their own without needing to alter or rely on adjacent teeth, unlike traditional bridges.

Eat What You Love

With implants securely anchored in bone, you can eat your favorite foods without worry, from apples to steak.

Long-Lasting Investment

Dental implants are designed for durability. With proper care, the implant post can last a lifetime, with only the restoration potentially needing replacement over time.

With proper care, including regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene, dental implants can serve you well for many years. The titanium post integrates with your jawbone and functions as a permanent anchor. The crown or restoration on top may need replacement over time due to normal wear.

Good candidates for dental implants have adequate jawbone density to support the implant, healthy gums, and no uncontrolled medical conditions that could affect healing. If bone loss is present, bone grafting may be an option. A thorough evaluation with Dr. Gonzalez determines your candidacy.

All-on-4 is a full-arch restoration technique that uses four strategically placed implants to support a complete set of fixed teeth on one jaw. Two implants are placed vertically and two at an angle, which often eliminates the need for bone grafting. It is a cost-effective way to replace an entire arch of missing teeth.
