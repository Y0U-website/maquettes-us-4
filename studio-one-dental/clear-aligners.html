Your Clear Aligner Journey

Consultation and Digital Scan

Dr. Gonzalez evaluates your teeth, bite, and jaw alignment. We take a comfortable digital scan of your mouth and discuss your goals, timeline, and budget.

Custom Treatment Plan

Using your digital scan, we create a personalized treatment plan that maps every stage of your tooth movement. You can preview your projected results before starting.

Receive Your Aligners

Your custom aligners arrive at our office. We fit the first set, teach you how to wear and care for them, and set up your check-in schedule.

Wear and Progress

You wear each set of aligners as directed, switching to the next set on schedule. Periodic visits let us confirm your progress and provide your next sets.

Retainer and Results

Once your final set is complete, a retainer helps maintain your new alignment. Your straighter, more confident smile is ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

The cost of clear aligner treatment depends on the complexity of your case. At Studio One Dental, we offer competitive pricing and flexible financing to make treatment accessible. We discuss all costs during your consultation.

Clear aligners are effective for a wide range of orthodontic cases, including crowding, spacing, and mild to moderate bite issues. More complex cases may still benefit from traditional braces. A consultation with Dr. Gonzalez will help determine which option is best for your situation.

Treatment duration varies by case, but most patients complete clear aligner treatment in six to eighteen months. Simpler cases with minor crowding or spacing may finish sooner, while more complex alignment issues take longer. Your estimated timeline is discussed during your consultation.

Many dental insurance plans include orthodontic benefits that cover a portion of treatment costs. Coverage amounts and age limits vary by plan. We recommend confirming your orthodontic benefits with your carrier before beginning treatment.

Traditional braces use brackets and wires bonded to the teeth to move them into alignment. Clear aligners are removable, transparent trays that gradually shift teeth with no visible hardware. Aligners offer the advantages of being discreet, comfortable, and easy to remove for eating and brushing.
