Meet Dr. Juan Manuel Gonzalez

Your Humble Family Dentist

At the heart of our team is Dr. Juan Manuel Gonzalez , an outstanding professional with a passion for dentistry and a track record of excellence. With over a decade of experience in general dentistry, Dr. Gonzalez has established his reputation as a highly skilled and compassionate dentist.

Originally from Monterrey, Mexico, Dr. Gonzalez graduated from UANL in 2011 and completed the General Dentistry program at UTHSCSA. His dedication to providing efficient and compassionate dental care is reflected in every patient who visits our clinic.

Dr. Gonzalez and his bilingual team are committed to the dental health and general well-being of families across Humble , Atascocita, Kingwood, and the surrounding communities, delivering comprehensive care from preventive checkups and cleanings to cosmetic procedures and dental implants.
