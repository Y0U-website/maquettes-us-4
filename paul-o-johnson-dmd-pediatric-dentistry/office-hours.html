Office Hours Johnson Pediatric Dental

Office Hours

We are open to serve you, answer questions or schedule an appointment during the following hours: Monday – Thursday: 7:00 a.m. – 4:30 p.m.

Appointments

All patients are seen by appointment only. We respect your valuable time and will make every effort to limit waiting to an absolute minimum. Because of the nature of a children’s dental practice, emergencies and special needs of individual patients do arise which interrupt our schedule. We appreciate your patience and understanding if this should occur.

If you find it impossible to keep your scheduled appointment, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance so that we may give this time to another waiting patient and schedule another appointment for you.

Scheduling

Scheduling of dental treatment is done with the patient’s best interest in mind. For this reason, extensive treatment and treatment on young patients are scheduled during morning hours. Although parents are welcome to accompany their child in the dental operatory for all procedures, some patients do better for dental treatment if the parents are not in the room during procedures. We will discuss our recommendation for your child prior to necessary treatment.
