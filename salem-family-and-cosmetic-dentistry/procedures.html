Dental Implants in Salem, OR

Salem Family & Cosmetic Dentistry provides dental implants for patients in Salem, Oregon, from the first consultation through long-term care. A missing tooth affects more than your smile. It changes how you chew, and over time the bone where the tooth used to be can begin to shrink. A dental implant replaces the whole tooth, root and all, so it looks, feels, and works like a natural tooth.

How an implant works:

  • A small post is placed in the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone
  • A custom crown attaches on top, matched to your natural teeth
  • It stands on its own, without relying on the teeth beside it for support

Frequently Asked Questions

Dental Implants

  • How long do dental implants last? With good care, a dental implant can last decades, and many last a lifetime. The implant post fuses with your jawbone and becomes a permanent part of your mouth. The crown attached to it may need replacement after fifteen to twenty years of normal wear. Brushing, flossing, and regular checkups matter as much for implants as they do for natural teeth.
  • Is a dental implant better than a bridge? It depends on your situation, and we’ll walk you through both. An implant replaces the tooth root as well as the crown, so it doesn’t rely on the teeth around it and it helps preserve the jawbone. A bridge is anchored to the neighboring teeth, which means reshaping healthy teeth to support it. Bridges often cost less up front and take less time. Implants usually last longer.
  • Do dental implants hurt? Most patients are surprised by how manageable it is. The procedure is done with local anesthetic, so you won’t feel the placement itself, and soreness afterward is usually similar to having a tooth removed. It typically settles within a few days and responds well to over-the-counter pain relief.
  • How much do dental implants cost? There’s no one-size price for implants, because every case is different. Your estimate depends on how many teeth are being replaced, whether you need a bone graft before the implant can be placed, the type of crown or restoration, and what your dental insurance covers. After an exam, we’ll give you a personal estimate and walk you through insurance and financing options before any treatment begins.

Gum Disease & Periodontal Care

  • What are the signs of gum disease? The most common early signs are gums that bleed when you brush or floss, redness or swelling along the gumline, and persistent bad breath. As it progresses you may notice gums pulling away from your teeth, sensitivity, or teeth that feel loose. Early gum disease is often painless, which is why it frequently goes unnoticed until a cleaning catches it.
  • Does a deep cleaning hurt? We use local anesthetic for deep cleanings, so you shouldn’t feel discomfort during the appointment. Your gums may be tender for a day or two afterward and your teeth may be temporarily sensitive to hot and cold. Most patients tell us it was easier than they expected.
  • Do I need a referral to see a periodontist? No. Dr. Makkattil, a periodontist, sees patients right here in our office once a month for evaluations, scaling and root planing, gum grafting, bone grafting, and crown lengthening. You get specialist care without a second office, separate paperwork, or a wait for an outside referral.
