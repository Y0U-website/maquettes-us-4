A Simple Alternative to Dental Insurance

Traditional dental insurance can be helpful, but it can also come with annual maximums, waiting periods, denied claims, deductibles, and restrictions on certain procedures.

Our membership plan keeps things simple. You pay one annual fee, receive included preventive care, and save on additional treatment completed at Groovy Dental.

There are no claim forms, no waiting periods, and no surprise insurance denials.
