Stealth Teeth Straightening with ClearCorrect

A straight smile has never been easier to enjoy. Clear plastic trays, known as aligners, can work the magic of braces without the inconvenience of mental braces. Combined with sophisticated 3D software, this technology brings a perfected smile within reach for most adults and mature teens. Your teeth are as individual as you are, but you are no longer stuck with the one-solution-fits-all traditional braces.

We’re dedicated to helping patients achieve the smile of their dreams with ClearCorrect.

More choices than ever exist for many procedures in dentistry allowing for highly customized care for your situation and needs. Our doctor can help you discover your path to a straighter smile using ClearCorrect™.

Completely Removable

Removable trays can be taken out for short periods allowing normal eating and cleaning. Enjoying an apple without the intrusion of brackets and wires makes many patients prefer aligners. ClearCorrect works extremely well for adults, but responsible teens can be candidates too. Patients of any age can experience the perks of straight teeth with aligners. It’s never too late to discuss ClearCorrect for a bright, straight smile.

How ClearCorrect is different.

Comfortable.

Smooth plastic surfaces offer an unequaled comfort level to the tongue and cheeks compared to other forms of straightening.

Efficient.

Gradual pressure on specific teeth with each aligner means less stress and shortened treatment times, in many cases.

Convenient.

We dispense several sets of aligners at once, allowing you to change to a new one at home every two weeks.
