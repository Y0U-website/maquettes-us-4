Oral health is an essential part of your family’s overall health.

Make Sure They Keep Up with Regular Cleanings and Exams.

It’s important to remember that professional exams and cleanings are just as essential as a dedicated home care routine. A regular home care routine of brushing, flossing, and rinsing combined with periodic professional exams and cleanings are the best way to keep your teeth. Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that is below the gum and in-between teeth. Even the most meticulous home care requires the help of a hygienist! Your hygienist uses special instruments that can reach places a toothbrush can’t.

Family-friendly care sets a foundation for lifetime dental health free of anxiety and avoidance.

Whether introducing a child to their first visit, straightening a teen’s smile, or filling in the gaps for a grandparent, dental visits are important for all ages.

Healthy Teeth, Healthy Family

Your optimal health is our priority. We also carefully track many potential changes to your family’s teeth and mouth. From cancer screening to enamel erosion, many small problems can be kept from growing into far more serious conditions. Today there are many oral care brushes, picks, rinses, pastes, tongue scrapers, and other unique inventions on the supermarket shelves. While many are valuable, others may be too expensive or even unnecessary.

When your dedication to outstanding oral home-care meets with our commitment to personalized clinical coaching, the results truly prove the concept of synergy. A trouble-free mouth can provide a lifetime of increasing dividends with immeasurable benefits to the quality of your days.
