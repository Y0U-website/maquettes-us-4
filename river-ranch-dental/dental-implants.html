Why Choose Dental Implants

Dental implants stand out as a superior choice for tooth replacement options. Here’s why:

Long-Lasting Durability

Dental implants are crafted to last a lifetime with proper care, unlike dentures or bridges, which typically need replacement every 5-10 years.

Aesthetic Perfection

Implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, restoring your smile to its full glory without anyone noticing you’ve had work done.

Superior Functionality

Unlike dentures that might slip or bridges that rely on adjacent teeth for support, implants mimic the strength and functionality of natural teeth. They allow you to chew, speak, and smile with ease and confidence.

Comparing Implants to Dentures and Bridges

When you choose River Ranch Dental , you choose quality, durability, and a commitment to excellence in dental care.

  • Dentures require adhesives and regular adjustments, often leading to discomfort and inconvenience.
  • Bridges depend on surrounding teeth for support, potentially weakening them over time.
  • Dental implants , on the other hand, do not rely on adjacent teeth and are securely anchored to the jaw, offering unmatched stability and reliability.

Step 1: Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation by Dr. David Rivers. Using state-of-the-art diagnostic tools, we assess your oral health and jawbone structure to determine your eligibility for implants. Have dental anxiety? Don’t worry—we offer sedation dentistry to ensure a stress-free experience.

Step 2: Implant Placement

Dr. Rivers will surgically place the titanium implant post into your jawbone during this stage. The procedure is quick and minimally invasive, thanks to advanced surgical techniques and sophisticated technology.

Step 3: Healing Period

Osseointegration, where the implant fuses with the bone, typically takes a few months. During this time, you’ll have a temporary crown to maintain aesthetics and functionality.

Step 4: Crown Placement

The final step involves attaching your custom-designed crown to the implant and completing your smile restoration. At this point, your implants are as functional and natural-looking as your original teeth.

Our team ensures every patient is well-informed and at ease throughout the procedure.
