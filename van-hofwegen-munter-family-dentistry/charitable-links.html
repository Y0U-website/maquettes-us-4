American Dental Association

Give Kids A Smile

Give Kids A Smile is an event to provide free, desperately-needed dental services to local qualifying children from low-income families. This event is part of the American Dental Association’s Give Kids a Smile Program, founded by the American Dental Association. In Iowa last year, over 4,100 children were served by nearly 170 Iowa dentists and over 600 other volunteers. Nationwide, 53,000 dental team members participated in Give Kids a Smile. Van Hofwegen & Munter Family Dentistry enjoys participating in this worthwhile event.
