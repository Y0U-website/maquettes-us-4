The practice

We are happy to provide people of all ages with clear, practical information about our office, staff, philosophy and services. Van Hofwegen & Munter Family Dentistry offers a complete range of services in two convenient locations. From regular check-ups to cleanings, cosmetics, endodontics, and oral surgery we offer many services that will improve or maintain your oral health.

We hope you’ll be confident in choosing us to help you with all your family dental needs. Our friendly and knowledgeable staff is committed to excellence in dentistry and is always learning the latest in dental techniques.

Our office is an affordable choice for you, with many financing options available. We accept new patients of any age and would enjoy the opportunity to care for you and your family.
