Dr. Fitzgerald has been awarded Fellowships in the American College of Dentists, the Pierre Fauchard Academy and the Long Island Academy of Dentistry. Gina Sajnani, D.M.D., M.S.Ed.

Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Gina Sajnani, also known as “Dr. Gina” is a board-certified pediatric dentist with over twenty years of experience in her specialty. Dr. Gina is a New York City native. She graduated from SUNY Stony Brook with Honors Distinction and a double major in Biochemistry and Sociology. She then received her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania. She also obtained a Master’s of Higher Education while at Penn. Dr. Sajnani completed her pediatric dentistry specialty training at NYU Langone, Brooklyn.

While juggling a career, being a busy and dedicated mom and wife, she still finds time to enjoy travelling, dance choreography and being very involved in her local community. Often, you will always catch her smiling or sharing her bubbly personality around town.

Michelle Kamel, D.D.S.

Periodontist

Dr. Michelle Kamel received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from New York University, College of Dentistry graduating with honors in periodontics. Dr. Kamel then continued her post doctorate training in Periodontics & Implant Dentistry at NYU College of Dentistry where she gained extensive clinical knowledge and training in placing dental implants and treating periodontal disease.

During her training, Dr. Kamel has traveled both globally and locally to treat dental diseases in underserved populations in Kathmandu, Nepal and in various neighborhoods in New York City.

She is known to practice comprehensive patient care with a compassionate approach to the needs of her patients. Dr. Kamel is consistently staying current with advanced technology and techniques to optimize patient care.

Dr. Kamel is board eligible from the American Academy of Periodontology and is a proud member of the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society, the American Dental Association, the New York State Dental Association, the Nassau County Dental Society, and the American Academy of Periodontology.

In her free time, Dr. Kamel enjoys spending time with her family and friends, drawing, traveling, and trying new restaurants.
