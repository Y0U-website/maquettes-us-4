0 Dental Implants in Garden City, NY A Lasting Solution for Missing Teeth Missing teeth can affect much more than the appearance of your smile. They can impact the way you eat, speak, and feel about your smile, while also affecting the health and function of the surrounding teeth.

At Garden City Center for Dental Excellence, we offer comprehensive dental implant care to replace missing teeth with solutions designed to look, feel, and function naturally. Our multidisciplinary team works together to carefully plan each case and provide personalized treatment based on your individual needs.

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. As the area heals, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone, creating a stable foundation for a custom dental restoration.

Once healing is complete, the implant can be restored with a natural-looking dental crown, bridge, or other implant-supported restoration.

When one tooth is missing, a dental implant and custom crown can replace the tooth without relying on the neighboring teeth for support.

The final restoration is carefully designed to complement the shape, color, and proportions of the surrounding teeth for a natural-looking result.

Dental implants can also be used to replace several missing teeth. Depending on your individual situation, an implant-supported bridge may allow us to replace multiple teeth using strategically positioned implants.

Our doctors will evaluate your oral health, available bone, bite, and treatment goals to determine the most appropriate approach.

For patients who are missing most or all of their teeth, dental implants may be used to provide additional support and stability for a denture.

Implant-supported options can improve comfort, confidence, and chewing function compared with a traditional removable denture for appropriately selected patients.
