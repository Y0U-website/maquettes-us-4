Dental Implants in Wilmington Delaware

Dental implants in Wilmington are essentially surgical screws manufactured from medical grade titanium. Dental implants are placed into the jawbone through a small incision on the gums and are allowed to fuse with the jawbone over three months. Once healed, they serve as a foundation on which dental crowns, dental bridges and dentures are placed. Dental implants in Wilmington are the most superior and advanced teeth replacement alternative, and rightfully so. In this blog, we elaborate on the benefits of dental implants in Delaware.

What are the benefits of getting dental implants?

Behaves Like Natural Teeth

One of the most significant advantages of dental implants in Wilmington is that it behaves like natural teeth and restores full chewing capabilities. This is not possible with traditional dentures. Since they are anchored and fused to the jawbone, they imitate natural teeth and look just like them once restored with a crown.

Prevents Bone Loss

When you lose your natural teeth, the jawbone is no longer stimulated by the teeth’s root and does longer serve the intended purpose. Therefore it wears off and undergoes the process of resorption. The jawbone continues to shrink in size, thus disturbing the facial proportions. The way the facial skin wraps around the face changes, leading to facial sagging. Dental implants provided by a periodontist near you are the only teeth replacement alternative that makes sure this does not happen by stimulating the bone, thus preventing bone loss.

Restores Speech

Losing teeth can alter speech as well. The quality of speech is closely controlled by the placement of teeth and the tongue. Losing teeth can compromise the quality of your speech, and you may not be able to enunciate correctly. Dentures can also alter your speech. However, since dental implants in Wilmington feel and function like natural teeth, they allow you to speak naturally and restores your speaking ability.

Supports Adjacent Teeth

Losing teeth creates a gap in your mouth, making the adjacent teeth free to move into this space. This can lead to misalignments and also disturbs oral harmony. Dental bridges take the support of adjacent teeth to place fill this gap. However, dental implants placed by Dr. Sanz at Wilmington Periodontics and Implants are directly anchored to the bone. This prevents the adjacent teeth from moving and keeps them stable. Dental implants also do not need support from adjacent teeth like dental bridges do.

No Mishaps

The main problem with traditional dentures is that they can become loose and can even dislodge while you are talking, laughing or eating in public. This can be a source of embarrassment and consciousness in one self. Dental implants in Wilmington are firmly anchored to the bone and will never be the reason for your shame and consciousness.

Easy care and maintainance

Once dental implants are fused to the jawbone, they do not require any special care or attention. All you need to do is use a soft-bristled toothbrush and brush as you usually do. Brush twice a day and floss regularly to maintain your implants, and they will last you a lifetime.

Permanent Solution

Dental implants are a one-time investment. With proper care, dental implants placed by Dr. Sanz at Wilmington Periodontics and Implants can last you a lifetime. On the other hand, dentures have to be changed every 2-3 years as they wear off and become ill-fitted.
