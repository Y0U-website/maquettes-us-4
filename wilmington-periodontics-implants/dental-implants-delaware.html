What are dental implants in Wilmington?

A dental implant is a surgical fixture shaped like a screw made of medical-grade titanium and serves as an artificial root placed into the jawbone through an incision on the gums. This dental implant is allowed to heal and integrate with the jawbone over the course of three months. Once healed and integrated, dental implants act as a foundation over which a crown, a denture, dental bridge or other dental prosthesis is fixed.

Unlike traditional dentures, a dental implant provided by a skilled Delaware periodontist near you is fixed to the jawbone; this means you won’t have to remove it every day. It also offers a much more comfortable experience.

This is why Dr. Sanz, a board-certified periodontist in Wilmington, advises getting the missing teeth replaced as soon as possible with the help of dental implants. Dental implants will continue to stimulate the jawbone, thus preventing its resorption and eliminating the chances of any complications that come when living with missing teeth. Dental implants will also restore your chewing capability and speech and will also give you a more youthful appearance.

Procedure of Dental Implants in Delaware

During your first visit to our dental clinic, Wilmington Periodontics and Implants, your periodontist will examine your teeth and gums. They may also take some x-rays or advice a CBCT to analyse your bone pattern and determine your treatment plan. Next, they will discuss this treatment plan with you and schedule your dental implant surgery.

How to care for your dental implant in Wilmington?

After your dental implant surgery, your periodontist in Wilmington will instruct you on how to care for the new surgical site. They may also prescribe some antibiotics and painkillers to you. Ensure that you are following their instructions to the fullest as the healing of your implants dramatically depends on proper oral health care.
