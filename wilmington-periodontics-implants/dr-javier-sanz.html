Biography

Dr. Javier Sanz is originally from Barcelona, Spain. After recieving his dental degree, he went on to complete a speciality in Prostodontics in the Universidad Internacional of Catalunya. Since his passion for dentistry laid in surgical approaches, he decided to pursue his speciality in Periodontics. After being in private practice for a few years he moved across the world to study periodontics at the State University of New York at Buffalo. While there, not only did he get a strong clinical and research background, but he also met his wife Nancy.

Six years later, his wife wanted to return to the United States to be close her family and they decided to settle in the Northern virginia área with their newborn son. In Virginia, Dr. Sanz was in private practice as well as working as an adjunct faculty in the periodontal department of the Virginia Commonwealth University where he enjoyed educating future periodontists on surgical techniques and dental implantology.

In October 2016 Dr. Sanz and his family decided to move to Wilmington. Dr. Sanz joined Dr. Michelle Broder’s periodontal specialty office while she was gradually transitioning on to teaching. His main interests include implants, bone regeneration, and periodontal plastic surgery. He strives to utilize the most advanced techniques to provide patients with optimal care and treatment outcomes.
