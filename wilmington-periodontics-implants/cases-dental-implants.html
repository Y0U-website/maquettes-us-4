Dental Implants Cases

Single implants

Replacing a broken tooth due to a fracture, endodontic failure or non treatment decay is really common. Most of the implant treatment that we perform at Wilmington Periodontics and Implants are single implants in the esthetic area.

Single Implant Cases

Case 1

Upper right incisor had to be removed due to a fracture. Extraction and immediate implant placement and a temporary tooth was placed the same day. The final tooth was placed. No one can tell the difference between the implant and the natural tooth that was lost

Case 2

Upper left lateral had to be replaced due to a decay. Same day extraction, immediate implant placement and temporaritation. excellent esthetics.

Case 3

Upper right lateral had to be replaced due to a fracture. Extraction, implant and provisional Crown in a day.
