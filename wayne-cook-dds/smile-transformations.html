Every smile starts with a conversation. We take time to understand what you want, evaluate your options, and carefully plan the result before treatment begins.

See It Before We Finish It

Every case begins with a wax mock-up. We use that mock-up to create your temporary restorations so you can see the proposed shape, size, and overall design in your own smile.

You have input before the final restorations are made.

“I’ll probably be more picky than you are. I want your smile to look great.”

Permanent restorations are never placed unless you are happy with the result. If something about the design is not right, I am glad to send the case back for changes and redesign.

The Greatest Reward

I’ve had patients tell me they’ve hidden their smile for years. They only half-smile in pictures, cover their mouth when they laugh, or simply don’t feel confident because they’re ashamed of their teeth.

There is nothing more fulfilling than seeing that change—the tears, or simply the confidence you see when they look in the mirror and smile without thinking about it.

Sometimes there are tears. Sometimes it’s confidence. Both mean everything.

“Seeing someone genuinely proud of their smile is one of the greatest rewards of what I do.”
