New Patients

Your First Visit Should Feel Different.

Whether it has been six months or six years since your last dental visit, you are welcome here. We take time to listen, explain what we see, answer your questions, and help you feel comfortable.

There Is No Hurry.

It only takes one bad dental experience to affect how you feel about dental treatment for a lifetime. If you are nervous, tell us. We will take the time you need.

I do not believe in pushing through discomfort. If you begin to feel anything, we stop—even if we are almost finished. Your comfort comes first.

A Different Kind of Dental Experience

Patients who arrive genuinely terrified often begin to relax after a few visits because they know we will listen, explain, and stop when they need us to. Trust can replace fear when you know what to expect.

What to Expect

We Listen

Tell us what is bothering you, what you want, and what concerns you about dental treatment.

We Evaluate

We carefully examine your oral health and use appropriate imaging when it helps us understand what is happening.

We Explain

We show you what we see, explain your options clearly, and make sure your questions are answered.

We Decide Together

Treatment decisions are made with you—not for you. There is no pressure to rush into care.

What to Bring

To help your first visit go smoothly, please bring:

• Your photo ID • Dental insurance information, if applicable • A current medication list • Any questions or concerns you want to discuss

New Patient Forms

Complete your paperwork at home if you would like. If not, that’s okay—we can help when you arrive.

You Will Never Be Rushed Through Fear.

If you have been putting off dental care because of fear, embarrassment, or a previous bad experience, please tell us. Our job is not to judge you. Our job is to help you move forward comfortably, one step at a time.
