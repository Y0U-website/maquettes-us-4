When something hurts, call.

Dental pain, swelling, a broken tooth, or a lost restoration deserves a direct conversation. Call (770) 954-1180 so the team can explain the next available appointment and whether more urgent medical care may be appropriate. For trouble breathing, uncontrolled bleeding, major facial trauma, or rapidly increasing facial swelling, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Tell us what makes dental care difficult.

Fear, past experiences, sensitivity, and uncertainty can all make a visit harder. The team can adjust the pace, explain what is happening before beginning, and discuss available comfort options. Nitrous oxide and prescription anxiety medication may be considered when clinically appropriate.

Tools that support a clearer diagnosis.

Clarity Dental uses digital X-rays, a CEREC intraoral scanner, and laser technology for selected gum procedures. Technology supports the evaluation, but the recommendation still begins with the clinical findings and your individual needs.

Not sure which service fits?

You do not need to diagnose the problem before making an appointment. Describe the symptom or concern, and the team will help identify the right type of visit.
