Paris Little, DDS.

Georgia native. Meharry Medical College graduate. Dentist and practice leader serving McDonough.

The person comes before the procedure.

Dr. Little begins by asking what brought you in, what concerns you most, and what you hope will feel different after treatment. She reviews the findings, explains the available paths, and makes a recommendation based on your health and goals.

Her aim is simple: treat people with the same attention and honesty she would want for her own family.

Life beyond the operatory.

Outside the practice, Dr. Little spends time with family and friends, enjoys being outdoors, travels when she can, and keeps a running list of restaurants worth visiting again. Her son is her pride and joy, and two energetic dogs make sure life at home never gets too quiet.
