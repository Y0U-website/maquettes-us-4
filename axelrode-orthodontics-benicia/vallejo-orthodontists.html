Dr. Ryan Gaw Dr. Ryan Gaw, DDS, received his undergraduate degree in Molecular and Cell Biology from University of California, Santa Cruz. Dr. Gaw completed his dental degree at UCLA School of Dentistry. He attended the NYU Langone Health San Francisco Program and earned his AEGD Certificate. Dr. Gaw completed his orthodontic residency at Roseman University of Health Sciences.

Dr. Gaw was inspired to pursue orthodontics through his own personal orthodontic journey. As a child he was self conscious about his teeth and rarely smiled. Dr.Gaw’s orthodontic passion and unwavering commitment to helping him attain a healthy, beautiful smile provided the motivation for him to dedicate his profession to transforming smiles and providing patients with the self esteem to achieve their dreams.

Dr. Gaw values effective communication in orthodontics and patient care. Ensuring that patients feel heard and fully understand their treatment plans is critical. Dr. Gaw was a college TA and enjoys teaching, particularly patient education. He firmly believes that equipping patients with knowledge enriches their experience and improves their outcomes.

In his free time, Dr. Gaw enjoys playing basketball, spending time with friends and family, skiing, traveling, and trying out new food places!
