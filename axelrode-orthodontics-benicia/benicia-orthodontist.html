Dr. Vicky Chiu Dr. Vicky Chiu’s early years were spent under the sunny skies and among the saguaro-filled mountains of Tucson, Arizona. Her commitment to excellence is evident as she continuously engages with the latest research and innovative techniques in orthodontics to provide top-tier care to her patients.

Beyond her professional life, Dr. Chiu cherishes the time she spends with her family and friends. She also enjoys the companionship of her fluffy Pomeranian, making the most of her moments outside the office. Her balance of professional dedication and personal life enriches her ability to bring smiles to her patients’ faces, both through her skill and her warm, caring personality.

Invisalign®

Invisalign® uses clear plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their optimal position without wires or brackets commonly used in traditional braces.

Braces

Braces are bonded brackets, arched wires and elastic bands that move crowded or spaced teeth into the proper position for appearance and function.

Clear Braces

If you’re looking for a solution that allows you to keep your teeth cleaner without rubber bands and is less noticeable than traditional braces, then Clear braces are the way to go.

Emergencies

Broken braces and wires, soft-tissue injuries, or loose brackets and bands -- we’re here to help with your orthodontic emergencies.

Orthodontic Appliances

We offer several different varieties of orthodontic appliances including upper expansion appliances, quad helix, twin blocks appliances, and more.
