How Do Implant Dentures Work?

Implant dentures are secured to dental implants attached to your jawbone, unlike traditional replacement teeth that can easily shift and slip in your mouth while you speak or eat. Dentures retained by implants have a gum-colored acrylic base with natural-looking replacement teeth attached to it. These are usually made from materials like ceramic or porcelain to mimic the look of your natural enamel.

After your dental implants have been surgically placed and fused with your jawbone, our team will attach your restorations to them. Because they’ll be secured to such a solid and stable foundation, you’ll be able to go through your daily life without worrying about embarrassing moments caused by moving dentures in Arlington Heights.
