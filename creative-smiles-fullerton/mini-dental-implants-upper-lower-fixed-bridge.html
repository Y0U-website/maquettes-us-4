What is mini dental implant

Like the full mouth permanent dentures, mini implants are made of titanium alloy. The only difference is that they are smaller in size than the traditional ones. They are metal posts with a precision attachment (a ball fastened at the outer end). The bone merges and grows with the alloy from the time it was instilled, which is approximately three to six months. After the healing period, the dentist will place a suitable restoration through a rubber O ring and a socket onto the precision attachment.

Mini dental implants vs regular implants

The size is what differentiates a standard implant from a mini one. These smaller ones have a diameter that ranges from 1.8 to 3.3 mm. The standard restorations, on the other hand, have a diameter that ranges from 3.4 to 5.8 mm in width.

The mini implant is a 2-piece structure while the standard one is a 3-piece structure.

An abutment is applied separately to the body for standard ones. This can be instilled once or by applying a simple local anesthetic. These rarely require sutures.

What are mini dental implants used for

Both the traditional and mini restorations are used for the same reasons. My Creative Smiles dentists included the following reasons for their use:

  • Restoration of a Single Tooth In the past, a bridge is required to restore a single tooth. The bridge supports the remaining good teeth but weakens them in due time. With mini restoration, a new artificial root is placed into the jawbone, which is similar to the structure of a natural tooth.
  • Restoration of Multiple Teeth Individual artificial roots and support multiple missing teeth, making them look, feel, and work like natural ones. If the teeth are adjacent to each other, a single artificial root can support two or three.
  • Complete Mouth Restoration A comprehensive dental procedure, a complete mouth restoration has a great impact on the mouth and everything in it. This procedure is usually recommended for aesthetic reasons. My Creative Smiles offers a number of options about full mouth rehabilitation.
