Full mouth restoration procedure

Constructing and placing an all on four, five, six, or eight implants involves two processes: the arrangement and the loading processes.

  • The arrangement of Dental Implants Our dentist will administer a local anesthesia to ensure you are totally pain-free and comfortable. Conscious sedation may be given for those who are extremely anxious. The dentist will cut over the soft tissue lying over your jaw bone. New roots will be inserted in this area. Lifting a surgical flap, the dentist will drill a small hole in your jaw, screw the metallic roots in place, making sure controlled forces are applied.
  • L oading Process – In the traditional procedure, an abutment is used to insert a full prosthesis to the new roots as soon as the surgical area has healed completely. With the modern dental procedure, the loading process can take place immediately. For immediate loading, our dentist will prepare the dentures and their abutments and will load them right after the completion of the surgery.

Benefits of all on 4

The all on four restorations have more benefits to offer than the traditional prostheses like bridges and dentures.

Get that new, attractive smile with all on four restorations. Set an appoint with us today. Visit us in Fullerton, California now. We are just 10 minutes away from Laguna Beach and Irvine.

  • Quick Aesthetic Results – The all on four can be loaded as soon as the the surgery was completed. This allows you to have a new, visually appealing smile in one day! The traditional method takes several months of waiting before placing the prosthesis.
  • Quick Recuperating Period At My Creative Smiles, our dentists perform minor surgery to immediately accommodate the placement. With only four artificial roots needed for permanent restorations, the procedure, as well as the recuperating period are quicker.
  • No Bone Grafting You can have new permanent prostheses without grafting the bone. Bone resorption is less in the front jawbone, leaving this area clear and sturdy to place the two new roots. The other two new roots are placed at an angle at the back jawbone. This is done to make way when the space between the lower and the upper jawbone is restricted than normal. Our dentists use a mini-implant technique in some cases.
  • Durable – Traditional dentures require frequent replacements or repairs. Permanent dental prostheses are sturdier and durable compared to traditional dentures. Fixed dentures are also easier to clean and maintain and can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • More Comfortable – Permanent dentures are not only durable but they are also comfortable and visually appealing. They are just like your natural teeth so there are no issues of them sliding off, or affecting your speech, and even eating limitations. They are so perfectly natural that you will not feel having lost your teeth at all.
