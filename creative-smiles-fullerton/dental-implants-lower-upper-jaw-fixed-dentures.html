WHY ARE THEY NEEDED?

  • Prevents bone resorption, as they simulate the stimulation given by the roots of your natural teeth.
  • Restores the correct chewing of food and speech
  • Prevents impairment of the facial structure and sunken cheeks
  • Prevents the adjacent tooth from shifting or moving into the vacant space occupied by the missing tooth. The shifting of tooth makes them susceptible to cracking and fracture.
  • Prevents the overeruption of natural teeth in the opposite arch caused by missing antagonist teeth
  • Reinstates qualify of life, particularly for those with missing or decayed teeth and dentured patients who have to suffer the restrictions of temporary dentures.

DENTAL IMPLANTS PROCEDURE

Depending on the number of lost teeth, one or multiple titanium posts may be implanted during a permanent tooth surgery. Using pressure controlled drills, the dentist will insert these posts into your jawbone.

Preparatory procedures may be required by some patients prior to placing new artificial roots. This can happen if a patient has insufficient jawbone that requires bone grafting to create a sturdy base for the placement and to boost the bone density. If bone grafting is required, you need to allow six to nine months for your jaw to heal completely and for the titanium posts to merge into your jaw.

If bone grafting is not necessary, you can immediately receive mini dental implants. Mini dental implants are dinner in diameter compared to the traditional ones. They can be placed immediately even in less dense bones because they are smaller in shape and size.

A healing time of four to six months, also known as osseointegration, takes place to allow the gums and bone to merge around the artificial root.

You have several dental appointments during the recuperation stage, where your dentist will evaluate the general healing of the surgical area.

During the recuperation phase, a customized restoration is fabricated in the lab, so it will be ready once the areas have fully healed. Once the surgical site has completely healed, the new restorations will be arranged and you can then have dentures that look and feel natural and a gorgeous smile. With proper care, permanent implants can last you a lifetime.
