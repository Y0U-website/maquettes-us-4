Dental Implant FAQs

Dental implants are titanium posts placed in the jaw to support replacement teeth such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. They are intended to provide a stable foundation that looks and functions like natural teeth.

Many adults with missing or failing teeth may be candidates, but suitability depends on gum health, bone support, bite, medical history, and oral hygiene. A consultation and examination are the best way to determine next steps.

Care typically includes consultation and planning, imaging when needed, surgical planning, implant placement, a healing period for the implant to integrate with bone, and placement of the final restoration. Your dentist will outline the sequence that applies to you.
