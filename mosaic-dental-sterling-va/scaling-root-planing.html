Scaling & Root Planing Sterling

The primary goals of scaling and root planing are to eliminate harmful bacteria, reduce inflammation and prevent the progression of gum disease. This treatment is often completed over one or more visits to Mosaic Dental, depending on the extent of the buildup and the condition of your gums. We offer anesthetic options to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.

Scaling and root planing may be combined with other periodontal therapies, such as antibiotic treatments , to enhance your results and provide you with the most effective treatment possible. For many patients, this deep cleaning is sufficient to control gum disease, allowing them to maintain healthier gums and teeth.

  • Root Planing: After scaling, root planing focuses on smoothing the roots of your teeth. Smoothing the tooth roots helps remove bacterial toxins and discourages further bacterial growth, promoting reattachment of the gums to the teeth and healing.
