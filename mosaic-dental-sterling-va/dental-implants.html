Dental Implants Sterling, VA

Dental implants are a reliable and long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth, restoring both the function and appearance of your smile. At Mosaic Dental, we specialize in providing high-quality dental implants to help you achieve a natural-looking and confident smile. Dental implants are designed to look, feel and function like your natural teeth, making them a popular choice for patients seeking a permanent restoration option.

A dental implant consists of three main components: the implant post, the abutment and the restoration. The implant post, usually made of titanium, is surgically placed into the jawbone where the tooth is missing. Over time, the jawbone fuses with the titanium post in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation for the replacement tooth. This integration helps prevent bone loss, which is a common issue with missing teeth.

Once the implant post has successfully integrated with the jawbone, an abutment is attached to the post. This abutment acts as a connector, holding the custom-made implant restoration securely in place. This restoration is carefully crafted to match the shape, size and color of your natural teeth, ensuring a seamless blend with your smile. Depending on your needs, your implant restoration could be a dental crown , replacing a single tooth, or a bridge or dentures , replacing multiple teeth.

Dental implants offer several benefits beyond aesthetics. They improve chewing efficiency, speech and overall oral health by preventing adjacent teeth from shifting. With proper care, including regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene practices, dental implants can last a lifetime.

FAQ

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from titanium that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They serve as a sturdy foundation for a variety of dental restorations such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Implants mimic the function and appearance of natural teeth, offering a permanent and reliable solution for tooth replacement.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants offer several benefits, including improved appearance, restored ability to chew properly, prevention of bone loss, and long-lasting durability. They support the remaining teeth, prevent shifting, and maintain facial structure.

Who is a candidate for implant dentistry?

Most individuals with good oral and general health are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone density to support the implant, and are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene. Those with chronic conditions such as diabetes or heart disease may require special consideration, so it is important to consult with us to evaluate your specific situation.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post that integrates with the jawbone is highly durable and designed to last many years. However, the crown or prosthetic attached to the implant may require replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear and tear.

What is the best way to care for my dental implant?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. You should brush and floss daily to remove plaque and prevent gum disease. Regular dental checkups are essential to monitor the implant&#x27;s condition and ensure surrounding tissues remain healthy. Avoid chewing on hard objects and refrain from smoking, as these habits can negatively impact implant longevity.
