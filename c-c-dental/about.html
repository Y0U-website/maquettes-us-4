About C & C Dental

C & C Dental is a premier general, cosmetic, and aesthetics dental practice in Sugar Land, Texas. Led by Tim Cashion, DDS, and Teresa Cody, DDS, the practice serves entire families, including children, men, and women in Sugar Land and the surrounding communities of Richmond, Houston, Missouri City, Rosenberg, Katy, Stafford, and Meadows Place, Texas.

C & C Dental offers exceptional dental care in a relaxing, friendly setting. The team of dentists are an extraordinary group of talent with expertise in many areas of dentistry. They bring the perfect balance of comfort, skill, and joy to the office, putting even the most anxious dental patients at ease.

The practice provides comprehensive general dentistry services and orthodontics care for every member of the family. C & C Dental offers professional teeth cleanings, gum disease treatments, root canals, sleep medicine, and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) treatments.

The practice specializes in WaterLase® laser dentistry and noninvasive facial recontouring using Agnes and Scarlet RF systems for jawline and chin shaping. Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and other alternative injections at C & C Dental promote natural hair restoration.

The skilled dentists offer Invisalign®, tooth-colored fillings, teeth whitening, dental veneers, All-on-4® dental implants, crowns, bridges, and numerous other cosmetic dentistry options. The practice uses innovative laser scanners for ultra-small cavities.

The C & C Dental dental experts enjoy perfecting the look and health of patients’ smiles using the latest advances in procedures and technologies. They compassionately welcome new and existing patients to the practice.

Meet Our Providers

Dr. Tim Cashion and Dr. Teresa Cody, a husband-and-wife team of dentists that have been in practice for over twenty years, bring a good balance of fun, comfort, experience, and joy to the office. At our office, we offer dental care and orthodontics for every member of your family.

Tim Cashion, DDS

Tim Cashion, DDS, is a skilled and practiced Doctor of Dental Surgery specializing in General Dentistry. Dr. Cashion sees patients at C & C Dental, a premier general, cosmetic, and aesthetic dental practice in Sugar Land, Texas.

Led by Dr. Cashion and his wife Teresa Cody, DDS, the practice serves entire families, including children, men, and women in Sugar Land and the surrounding communities of Richmond, Houston, Missouri City, Rosenberg, Katy, Stafford, and Meadows Place, Texas.

Dr. Cashion first considered dentistry in elementary school. He remembers being teased for having spaces or gaps between his teeth. He knows what it’s like to feel self-conscious about your smile and the lack of confidence it can bring.

He asked his orthodontist about braces, but the orthodontist told Dr. Cashion his bite was acceptable and that he should talk to his dentist about a new procedure being done called bonding — this was the early 1980s. That decision changed his life.

Dr. Cashion had cosmetic bonding done to close the spaces and make a fuller smile and remembers the freedom it brought not to have to cover his teeth every time he smiled. His personal life experience and this single and monumental dental appointment cemented his decision to pursue dentistry as a career.

His goal is to develop the jaws to the proper size, align the teeth, and make room for the tongue, which improves breathing. Thus, not only is form improved appearance, but also functionality.

Dr. Cashion’s knowledge and expertise benefit patients but also provide tremendous satisfaction. It is with compassion and gratitude that Dr. Cashion serves his patients. Providing the best care for each and every patient is what it’s all about.

Teresa Cody, DDS

Teresa Cody, DDS, is a knowledgeable and talented Doctor of Dental Surgery specializing in General Dentistry and Aesthetics. Dr. Cody sees patients at C & C Dental, a premier general, cosmetic, and aesthetic dental practice in Sugar Land, Texas.

Dr. Cody never thought she’d end up a dentist. Her childhood dentist did not believe in anesthetics. This painful experience left her petrified and made her a challenging patient due to fear.

Her practice offers amenities like televisions to entertain patients during procedures and new anesthetics – no shot necessary. Dr. Cody also provides topical cream to numb gums, specially formulated and made by the compounding pharmacist. In addition, high-tech equipment makes every job more manageable. She has a special interest in orthodontics and aesthetics.

Dr. Cody and Dr. Cashion met in dental school. They have two children, Erin and Neal. Their marriage is a prime example of the phrase “never say never,” as Dr. Cashion, a born and bred Texas Aggie, swore he would never marry a Texas Longhorn growing up. As fate would have it, Dr. Cody is a Texas Longhorn.

Erin needed braces due to severely overcrowded teeth. Most orthodontists she talked to wanted to pull teeth. Dr. Cody decided that she would do it herself if she couldn’t have her daughter treated the way she wanted.

Thus began the new passion of her career. After hundreds of hours of orthodontic training, she established a goal of non-extraction braces whenever possible.

Neal has Down syndrome. Dr. Cody and Dr. Cashion understand the difficulties in getting dental treatment. They have a thorough knowledge of the anatomy and physiology of the child’s needs. Often, there is severe crowding and no room for the tongue, resulting in subsequent speech problems and deep palates.

As an adjunct to their busy general family practice, Dr. Cody and Dr. Cashion are particularly interested in treating special needs children. They started a non-profit 501c3 called Changing Minds Foundation to support further research and treatment of individuals with Down syndrome.

Dr. Gia Kenner, DDS

She received a bachelor’s and master’s degree from Hampton University and her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry. Dr. Kenner also completed a General Practice Residency at Meharry and a Dental Oncology Fellowship at Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center in New York City. It was during COVID that Dr Kenner decided it was time for a career change and she transitioned to private practice.

Dr. Kevin Baethge, DDS

Becoming a dentist was not just a career choice for me; it was a calling inspired by profound moments of observation and connection. Growing up, I had the privilege of watching my father, a dedicated community servant, transform lives through his work. His efforts went beyond the clinical; he built relationships, alleviated fears, and restored confidence in people’s smiles. Witnessing this profound impact left an indelible mark on me.

In addition to my father’s influence, my love for science and problem-solving made dentistry a natural fit. Dentistry requires a delicate balance of technical precision and artistry. The ability to identify an issue, devise a solution, and implement it to create functional and aesthetic results brings a sense of fulfillment unlike any other. It’s a perfect harmony of intellect and creativity, and that excites me every day.

Every patient interaction is an opportunity to make a difference—to ease discomfort, boost confidence, and educate on the importance of oral health. I strive to mirror the compassion, patience, and dedication that my father exemplified and to honor the lessons I’ve learned through my own journey.

Dentistry is more than a profession for me; it’s a way to contribute to the well-being of others while continually challenging myself to grow and improve. It’s a privilege to play a role in someone’s health journey, and I’m grateful every day for the path that led me here.
