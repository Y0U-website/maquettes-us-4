The practice

All-on-X (also called All-on-4 or All-on-6) is a modern implant solution that replaces an entire set of missing teeth using as few as four to six implants per arch. The result is a strong, fixed smile that looks, feels, and functions just like natural teeth. You can eat and smile confidently again!

• Placement of four to six implants in one comfortable appointment

• Final, custom-crafted smile that looks and feels like your own teeth

We handle every step in-office, so your journey is smooth, comfortable, and coordinated by one trusted team.

In many cases, we can place your implants and attach a temporary smile the same day.

We offer gentle sedation and Twilight Dentistry for your comfort. Most patients are surprised at how easy the process feels.

With proper care, dental implants can last decades — even a lifetime.

Most patients are excellent candidates. We’ll review your oral health and bone structure to confirm what’s best for you.
