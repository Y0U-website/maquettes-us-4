Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implant Treatment?

Most adults qualify for dental implant treatment, but a comprehensive evaluation is essential. Ideal candidates typically have:

  • Healthy gums and no uncontrolled periodontal disease.
  • Adequate jawbone density (or the ability to receive bone grafting).
  • Good overall health.
  • Missing teeth or failing teeth that require replacement.

Dental Implant Crown & Final Restoration

The dental implant crown is the visible portion that looks like a natural tooth. It may be made of porcelain, zirconia, or hybrid materials. It must match surrounding teeth in shape, color, alignment, and occlusion.

For full-mouth restorations, the implant crowns may be part of a fixed bridge or denture-like prosthesis (e.g. All-on-4 ). Regardless of configuration, the precision of the crown connection is critical for longevity and comfort.

Complete Dental Implants Aftercare, Maintenance & Longevity

Once your implants are placed and restored, you must maintain them like natural teeth:

  • Daily brushing and flossing
  • Regular dental checkups and cleanings
  • Avoid harmful habits like smoking or biting extremely hard foods
  • Monitor prosthetics periodically
  • Eat a balanced, nutrient-rich diet to support bone and gum health.
  • Seek immediate attention for any discomfort, mobility, or signs of infection around the implant
  • Use a night guard if you grind or clench your teeth to protect the implants
