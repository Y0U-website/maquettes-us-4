Common Orthodontic Problems

Orthodontics helps in the correction of any problems in bite and alignment, which may affect the chewing process and stability of the teeth.

  • Overbite : Upper front teeth extend far over the lower teeth, which may cause uneven pressure and tooth wear.
  • Underbite : It is an extension of the lower teeth in front of the upper teeth and may impair the chewing and jaw balance.
  • Overlapping Teeth : Teeth overlap each other which may trap plaque and make cleaning difficult.
  • Crowded Teeth : Less space causes teeth to shift and pack closely together.
  • Crooked Teeth : Teeth grow out of normal alignment and may affect bite contact.
  • Rotated Teeth : Teeth turn out of position which may disturb proper tooth alignment.
  • Impacted Teeth : Teeth remain partially or fully trapped in the jawbone.

Who May Need Orthodontic Care?

  • Children with early bite problems or jaw growth issues.
  • Adults seeking straighter teeth and better bite balance.
  • People with difficulty chewing due to poor tooth alignment.
  • Patients with teeth that trap food and are hard to clean.
  • Individuals with jaw strain caused by uneven bite contact.
  • Anyone wanting a better smile appearance and tooth position.

Clear Aligners for Teeth

Clear aligners are custom plastic trays that move teeth step by step through programmed pressure. Each aligner is worn for a set period to guide tooth movement safely within the bone. Many patients choose clear aligners for teeth for comfort and discreet appearance.

  • Removable aligners support oral hygiene.
  • Gradual movement reduces tissue stress.
  • Effective for mild to moderate alignment issues.

Invisalign Teeth Straightening

Dental Teeth Braces

Retainers
