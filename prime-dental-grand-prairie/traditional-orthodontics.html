The Classic Way to Straighten Teeth .

Traditional orthodontics uses metal or ceramic braces to gradually move teeth into their ideal positions. This method applies gentle, consistent pressure through brackets and wires that are adjusted periodically to guide teeth alignment over time.

While modern alternatives like clear aligners have become popular, braces remain unmatched in their ability to handle complex dental and bite issues. They are durable, customizable, and suitable for both teens and adults seeking comprehensive results. With today’s smaller, sleeker designs, braces are more comfortable and discreet than ever before.

Why Choose Traditional Braces ?

Braces offer a level of precision and control that’s ideal for complex orthodontic cases. They can correct severe crowding, spacing, and bite irregularities with predictable outcomes. Since they’re fixed in place, you don’t have to worry about forgetting to wear them or losing them — consistency is built into the treatment.

Today’s braces are also more comfortable and aesthetic, with ceramic options that blend in with your teeth. Plus, traditional orthodontics is often more cost-effective than clear aligners, making it an excellent choice for patients seeking a dependable, long-term solution for straightening their smile.

Our Step-by-Step Approach .

Every visit to Prime Dental follows a proven, patient-friendly process designed for your comfort and complete care.

Consultation & Evaluation

We begin with a comprehensive evaluation, including X-rays and digital scans, to understand your bite and tooth alignment. From there, we design a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific goals.

Braces Placement & Adjustments

Once your braces are placed, we schedule regular adjustment visits to fine-tune your progress. These visits ensure that your teeth move efficiently and comfortably toward their ideal positions.

Retention & Smile Maintenance

After your braces are removed, retainers help keep your new smile in perfect alignment. We provide guidance on post-treatment care to make sure your results last a lifetime.
