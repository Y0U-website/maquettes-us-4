Meet Dr. Isabella Woodson

Dr. Isabella Woodson is a South Florida native who is honored to serve the community she has always called home. Born and raised in Miami in a Colombian and British family, she grew up surrounded by different traditions, perspectives, and cultures. Her family instilled in her the values of perseverance, empathy, and giving back—principles that continue to influence the way she approaches both dentistry and her relationships with patients.

Her interest in healthcare began at an early age, inspired by the opportunity to combine science with meaningful, personal connections. Dr. Woodson attended Florida International University, where she earned her bachelor’s degree, followed by a Master’s in Biomedical Science. She later received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts.

For Dr. Woodson, dentistry is a unique blend of science, artistry, and human connection. She enjoys getting to know her patients and understanding what matters most to them, whether that means improving their oral health, enhancing their smile, or simply helping them feel more at ease during their visits. She believes patients should feel informed and empowered throughout their care, and she takes the time to listen, explain treatment options, and create an experience centered around their individual needs.

Dr. Woodson has a strong interest in comprehensive and esthetic dentistry and is dedicated to expanding her knowledge through ongoing education. She enjoys incorporating new techniques and advances in dentistry into her practice when they can improve the patient experience and treatment outcomes. Her goal is to provide thoughtful, modern dentistry while maintaining the personal connection that makes each patient feel genuinely cared for.

As someone with deep roots in South Florida, Dr. Woodson especially values the opportunity to care for people from a wide variety of backgrounds. She hopes to create an office experience where patients feel comfortable, respected, and confident in the care they receive.

When she is not practicing dentistry, Dr. Woodson enjoys discovering new restaurants, traveling, and spending quality time with her family and friends. She also loves staying active through Pilates and yoga. She looks forward to getting to know her patients and being part of their dental journey for many years to come.
