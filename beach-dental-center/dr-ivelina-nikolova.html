Meet Dr. Ivelina Nikolova

Dr. Nikolova earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. She then completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the University of Pennsylvania, where she received advanced training in comprehensive treatment planning, restorative dentistry, oral surgery, implant dentistry, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, digital dentistry, and the care of medically complex patients.

Passionate about lifelong learning, Dr. Nikolova is committed to staying at the forefront of modern dentistry through continuing education. She has a special interest in implant dentistry, digital workflows, esthetic restorations, and minimally invasive techniques that help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles.

Originally from Bulgaria, Dr. Nikolova is fluent in English and Bulgarian and is conversational in French and Russian, with a working knowledge of Spanish. Her multilingual background allows her to connect with patients from diverse cultural backgrounds and helps foster clear, comfortable communication throughout their care. She understands that visiting the dentist can be stressful for many people and takes pride in creating a calm, supportive experience where patients feel comfortable asking questions and actively participating in their treatment decisions.

Outside of the office, Dr. Nikolova enjoys traveling, staying active, spending time with family and friends, and exploring new cultures and cuisines. She feels privileged to make a positive difference in her patients’ lives and looks forward to helping every patient achieve a lifetime of healthy smiles.
