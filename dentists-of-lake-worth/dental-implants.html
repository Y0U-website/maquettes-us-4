How Long Does It Take To Replace Missing or Damaged Teeth Using Dental Implants?

Since every patient is unique, the estimated amount of time to complete the process is based on their needs, jawbone condition, medical and dental history, and the materials and technique that your dentist employs. However, complex conditions may take a few months or more than a year to be replaced.

What Are the Benefits of Using a Dental Implant?

It Behaves Like Your Natural Tooth

Perhaps the biggest benefit of a dental implant is the fact that it fully restores your chewing power. You can finally eat your favorite foods with your implants in place. What’s more, you won’t have a hard time keeping them clean because they allow you to brush and floss normally.

It Can Last a Lifetime

While a dental bridge may only manage to last up to ten years, dental implants can last a lifetime because they integrate with your jawbone.

It Prevents Bone Loss

The absence of tooth roots triggers bone loss in the jaw . However, with dental implants that work to replace your tooth’s roots, the jawbone gets much-needed stimulation for natural bone growth.

It Helps Prevent Gum Disease

Did you know that a missing tooth gap can easily trap food and bacteria? This sets the stage for the development of gum disease . With a dental implant in place, your chances of having gum disease are slimmer.

It Restores Your Facial Appearance

Facial sagging is one of the most unwanted effects of bone loss due to missing teeth. Without the placement of dental implants, the distance between the tip of your nose and chin will gradually close and the lower third of your face will collapse.

When this happens, you’ll develop excess wrinkles around your mouth, your child will appear more pointed, and your lips will look thinner, and you’ll look a lot older than your actual age. Dental implants will instantly restore your youth and prevent premature aging.
