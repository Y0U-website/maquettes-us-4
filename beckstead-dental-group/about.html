Davis Beckstead, DDS

Dr. Beckstead graduated from the prestigious University of the Pacific dental school in San Francisco. While a dental student, he was able learn and perform all aspects of dentistry.

Dr. Beckstead was born and raised in Los Altos, California. He is a third-generation dentist with his dad, Mark Beckstead, DDS, who has been a periodontist (gum specialist) in this area for over 30 years. His dad has played a big role in him wanting to be a dentist. It was working at his dad’s office at a young age that ignited his desire to become a dentist himself. Seeing the changes that he made for his patients along the way helped him see what dentistry is all about, which are the relationships and friendships with all of you.

This allowed him to find his passion helping patients achieve a healthy and functional smile. Seeing the changes in patients and the confidence they gained after creating their smile was truly eye opening. He loves being able to help patients achieve their goals of having a smile that they love.

His wife, Johnna, and he have three wonderful children that keep them very busy. Vivian is at Addison elementary. She loves dancing, singing and being a big sister to her two younger brothers. Sam is full of energy and loves race cars. He is rarely found without a toy car in his hand, racing it through the house. Bennett has been a great addition to our family. He is a great baby, and he puts up with Vivian and Sam, who are kissing and hugging him all the time.

Emily Chung, DMD

My firsthand experience with extreme dental anxiety is exactly what drove me to become a different kind of dentist. My goal is to be the friendliest clinician you’ve ever had, providing a gentle, warm, and welcoming experience where —I promise— nobody gets tied to the chair!

Originally from Hong Kong (the land of dim sum!) and raised in the Bay Area, I am a proud foodie, I mean, UC Davis Aggie! I earned my doctorate at Roseman University of Health Sciences in Utah, where I was honored to receive scholarships and multiple awards for clinical excellence and leadership. However, my true specialty is lifelong learning. Beyond completing hundreds of hours of continuing education, I am currently pursuing advanced mastery at the renowned Kois Center to ensure I’m bringing the most sophisticated cosmetic and restorative techniques back to our community.

For me, dentistry is a craft that I love to share. I find deep purpose in serving communities far beyond the clinic walls. My next adventure will be a mission trip to Myanmar where I’ll be providing dental care to underserved children as part of my honeymoon, because what other way could make a celebration more meaningful! That is my story, and I can’t wait to meet you, listen to yours, and most importantly, help you achieve a smile you’re proud to show off.
