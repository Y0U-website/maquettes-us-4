Keeping Palo Alto Smiling with Expert Dental Cleanings

During a tooth cleaning and exam, a dental hygienist will take x-rays of your teeth and mouth and remove tartar and plaque. Then, the dentist will examine your mouth and look at the X-rays to check for tooth decay or other concerns.

Incorporating regular dental cleanings and exams into your healthcare routine is an investment in your oral and overall health. These visits not only ensure your mouth stays healthy but also play a crucial role in maintaining your well-being.

  • Remove Plaque and Tartar: Plaque is a sticky layer of bacteria that can harden into tartar if not removed. Daily brushing and flossing are essential but may not eliminate all the plaque. Professional cleanings ensure that plaque and tartar are thoroughly removed from areas your brush can’t reach.
  • Prevent Tooth Decay and Gum Disease: By removing plaque and tartar, dental cleanings help prevent the development of cavities and gum disease, both of which can lead to pain and possible tooth loss if left untreated.
  • Screen for Oral Cancer: Regular visits allow your dentist to conduct oral cancer screenings, an essential step in detecting early signs when treatment is most effective.
  • Maintain Overall Health: Good oral health is linked to your overall health. Frequent cleanings and exams can help prevent conditions related to heart disease, stroke, and diabetes.
  • Detect Dental Problems Early: Early detection of issues such as tooth decay, gum disease, and even broken fillings can save time, money, and discomfort. Your dentist can address problems before they become more serious.
  • Benefits of Dental Hygiene Cleanings: Beyond just keeping your teeth and gums healthy, professional dental cleanings provide a host of benefits, including fresher breath, a brighter smile, and the opportunity to detect potential health issues before they become severe. Regular cleanings also contribute to a lower risk of heart disease and can improve your overall health by keeping bacterial infections at bay.

How Frequently Should I Get My Teeth Cleaned?

People of all ages need to regularly see the dentist for these cleanings because they are a huge part of preventive oral health. They keep the plaque from hardening into tartar and causing decay and infections.

We generally recommend having your teeth cleaned about every six months. For people without any other dental concerns, this standard works well.

But, every patient is different; some people need more frequent cleanings to fight off gum disease, others need more frequent exams to watch for oral cancer or diseases for which they may be at risk.

Here at Beckstead Dental Group, we will make recommendations based on your dental history and your concerns.

How Often Should I Bring My Kids?

The recommendations are pretty much the same for kids. You should take your baby to the dentist around the time their first tooth comes in unless there is a pressing dental issue. Visits in the first few years of their life are mostly just to check for any potential problems and help your child feel comfortable at the dentist’s office.

After that, most kids should see the dentist every six months for a cleaning. Our team works hard to create a calm, welcoming environment for people of all ages. We make things fun while also being efficient and thorough.
