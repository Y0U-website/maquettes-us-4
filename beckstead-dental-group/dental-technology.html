3D iTero Scanning

The 3D iTero scanner allows us to offer advanced restorative and orthodontic dental treatment to all of our patients.

This device uses a small wand to scan the inside of your mouth and send the images to a computer. WIth these precise scans, we can then make customized clear aligner trays for the Invisalign system. So, you can get straighter teeth without the hassle.

Digital X-rays

Digital x-rays are a key part of dentistry. With these x-rays, our dentists and hygienists can get a view of your teeth and jawbone structure.

Intraoral Camera

An intraoral camera is an effective, simple-to-use technology that allows you, the patient, to see the inside of your mouth. The hygienist or dentist will use this small camera device to get a better view of your teeth or to show you what is happening inside of your mouth.

This technology can even lead to better oral hygiene habits and prevent tooth decay.

Laser Technology

At Beckstead Dental Group, we incorporate dental lasers to support a range of dental procedures. Dental lasers are utilized for precise decay removal, gum contouring, and the treatment of periodontal disease, offering a less invasive alternative to traditional methods.

The use of lasers can reduce discomfort and potentially shorten healing times, making dental visits more comfortable for patients. For further details on how we use laser technology in our treatments, feel free to contact us.

Oral Cancer Detection

By using specialized technology, our dentist can detect abnormal cells in your mouth. Early detection of oral cancer can prevent serious symptoms and even be lifesaving. So, find out more about our oral detection technologies by reaching out today.
