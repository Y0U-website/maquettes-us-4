Why Replace My Missing Teeth?

Besides filling in any tooth gaps and restoring the look of your smile, missing teeth can make it difficult to chew, speak, and more. Over time, they can cause your teeth to shift or even cause loss of jawbone density. So, it’s important to get a replacement tooth to prevent health issues and negative impacts to your life.

Dental implants are a great option for replacement teeth because they work to offer both an artificial tooth root and artificial tooth.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

With dental implants, a titanium post is surgically placed into the jaw. You’ll be given pain management to ensure the process is as comfortable as possible. Your jaw and gums will heal around the post.

Once your mouth heals, a replacement tooth—whether a single tooth crown or a full set of implant-supported dentures—is attached to the post. This titanium post creates a permanent foundation for your article tooth.

What Makes Dental Implants Such a Great Option?

While there are other options to replace missing teeth, dental implants are popular for a few reasons. Here’s what makes dental implants a good choice for many patients:

Dental implants can offer relief from pain and discomfort. So, if you’re interested in this effective treatment option for missing teeth, call our office today.

  • They last a lifetime: The titanium post heals and fuses to your jawbone. So, dental implants usually last your entire life.
  • They can support multiple teeth: A dental implant post can actually support multiple missing teeth. You can even restore an entire arch of teeth with 4–6 dental implants.
  • They look natural: Once dental implants heal completely, they look just like your natural teeth as the crown on top is matched exactly to you.
  • They restore functionality: Because the dental implant provides such a strong foundation, your replacement tooth is strong and healthy.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

When considering dental implants in Palo Alto from Becksted Dental Group , it’s important to determine if you are a good candidate for this procedure. Ideal candidates for dental implants have good overall health and sufficient bone density to support the implant. Healthy gums are also critical, as gum disease can jeopardize the success of the implant.

Non-smokers or those who are willing to quit smoking are generally better candidates, as smoking can impede healing in the mouth. If you have one or more missing teeth, stable oral health, and are looking for a durable, long-term solution, you might be an excellent candidate for dental implants in Palo Alto. A consultation with our expert team at Beckstead Dental Group can help confirm if dental implants are the right choice for you.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Once your dental implants are placed, caring for them is crucial for ensuring their longevity and your overall oral health . Just like your natural teeth, dental implants require regular brushing and flossing to prevent the build-up of plaque and to maintain healthy gums. It’s recommended to use a soft-bristled toothbrush and a non-abrasive toothpaste to avoid scratching the surface of the implants.

Additionally, regular dental check-ups and cleanings are essential. These visits allow your dentist to monitor the condition of your implants and address any issues early on. With proper care, dental implants in Palo Alto can last a lifetime, making them a worthwhile investment in your oral health and functionality.
