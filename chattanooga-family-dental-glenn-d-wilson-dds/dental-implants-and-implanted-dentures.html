What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, typically crafted from titanium, which seamlessly fuse with your jawbone. This fusion creates a robust and enduring anchor capable of supporting replacement teeth. From single implant crowns to full sets of permanent dentures, implants can stabilize various forms of implant-supported restorations, promising both functionality and aesthetics.

What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implanted dentures securely affix full arches of replacement teeth to implant posts, offering stability and comfort while eating or speaking. While Chattanooga Family Dental offers traditional dentures, implant-supported dentures may be a better option for some patients. By attaching the dentures to the jawbone using implants, implanted dentures can be more stable for day-to-day functions. Implanted dentures can be both removable and non-removable

Our Efficient Implant Process

Beginning with a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health, we ensure optimal conditions for the implant procedure. Utilizing advanced imaging technology, including digital radiography and CT scans, we precisely plan the placement and dimensions of your implants. Following a single surgical procedure for implant placement, your mouth undergoes a healing process before the final restoration of your smile.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Factors such as healthy gums, overall health, and adequate jawbone density are crucial in determining your candidacy for implants. We assess these factors thoroughly to maximize the success of your implant treatment. For patients with insufficient jawbone density, bone grafts or sinus lifts may be recommended to prepare for implant placement.

Extractions to Safeguard Your Oral Health

While preserving your natural teeth is paramount, if extraction is necessary due to damage or decay, our skilled team offers extractions with your oral health in mind. We discuss replacement options, often recommending dental implants for their ability to replicate your natural tooth structure seamlessly.
