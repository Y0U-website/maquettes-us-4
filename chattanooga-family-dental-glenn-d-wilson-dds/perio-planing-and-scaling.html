Understanding Periodontal Scaling and Root Planing

At the heart of dental perio scaling and root planing lies a meticulous process designed to combat gum disease and restore your oral health. This comprehensive procedure involves a deep cleaning that goes beyond regular brushing and flossing, targeting the areas below the gum line where harmful plaque and tartar can accumulate.

During the scaling portion, our skilled dental hygienists use specialized instruments to carefully remove plaque and tartar deposits from the tooth surfaces, both above and below the gum line. This thorough cleaning eliminates the breeding ground for bacteria that can cause gum inflammation and disease progression.

Following the scaling, the root planing phase focuses on smoothing the root surfaces of your teeth. This crucial step helps prevent future buildup and creates an environment conducive to the reattachment of healthy gum tissue to the teeth. By eliminating the rough surfaces where bacteria can thrive, dental perio scaling and root planing effectively disrupts the progression of gum disease.

This comprehensive approach is essential in managing and reversing the effects of periodontal disease, which can lead to tooth loss, bone deterioration, and other severe dental complications if left untreated. By prioritizing periodontal scaling and root planing, you’re taking proactive steps towards maintaining a healthy mouth and preventing the escalation of gum disease.
