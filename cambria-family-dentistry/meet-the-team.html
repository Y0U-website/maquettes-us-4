Meet the team

A Team Focused on Personalized Care

Krystal - Registered Dental Hygienist (RDH)

Kimberley - Registered Dental Assistant in Extended Functions (RDAEF)

Kimberley is a Registered Dental Assistant in Extended Functions (RDAEF) at Morro Bay Family Dentistry with over 25 years of experience in the dental field. As a restorative specialty assistant, she is dedicated to delivering high-quality dentistry while helping patients feel comfortable and cared for during their visits.

Kimberley earned her RDAEF certification from the Extended Function Dental Assistants Association (EFDAA) in 2012. She is passionate about building lasting relationships with patients and supporting excellent clinical outcomes through skilled and attentive care.

Outside the office, Kimberley enjoys spending time with her family, hiking, trail running, baking, and—of course—enjoying good food.

Yola - Dental Assistant (DA)

Yola is a caring dental assistant with over six years of experience helping patients feel comfortable and supported during their visits. She enjoys building genuine connections and takes pride in creating a welcoming, positive experience for everyone who walks through the door.

Outside of work, Yola enjoys traveling, visiting her family in Indonesia, and gardening, which reflects her caring and nurturing personality both inside and outside the office.

Kylee - Scheduling and Financial coordinator

Kylee has been a Scheduling and Financial Coordinator at Cambria Family Dentistry since August 2024. She enjoys expanding her knowledge of dental procedures and hygiene so she can help patients feel informed, confident, and comfortable with their care. She is passionate about making sure patients understand their treatment and enjoys sharing her knowledge with friends and family to help them better understand and maintain their dental health.

Outside of the office, Kylee loves spending quality time with her husband and son, Noah. As a family, they enjoy playing board games, spending time outdoors, and making memories together. Kylee and her husband are also excited to welcome their second son in March 2027!

Kylee is passionate about helping others and is always looking for ways to brighten someone’s day, both in and outside of the office.

Roberta - Scheduling Coordinator

Roberta has lived in Cambria since 2002 and has been working in dentistry for over 7 years. She enjoys helping patients feel comfortable and making sure they have a positive experience at the office. Roberta is a proud mom and grandmother. In her free time, she loves spending time with her family and connecting with people in the community. She enjoys socializing and being part of the small-town atmosphere that makes Cambria such a special place.
