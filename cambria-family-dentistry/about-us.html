About

We are a locally owned practice that values quality over volume. That means we take the time to understand your concerns, explain your options clearly, and help you make confident decisions about your care. Whether you are visiting us for ongoing maintenance or a more complex dental need, our goal is the same: to provide treatment that supports your health, your comfort, and your confidence.

Our philosophy is rooted in the connection between oral health and overall wellness. A healthy smile affects far more than appearance alone. It influences how you eat, speak, feel, and live each day. That is why we approach dentistry with a long-term mindset, focusing on care that is thoughtful, personalized, and designed to support lasting results.

Patients often appreciate that our office feels different from a rushed or impersonal dental setting. Located in downtown Cambria near Lynn’s, our practice combines the charm of a welcoming small-town environment with the benefits of modern technology. Our office is located in a converted home, creating a more relaxed atmosphere, and we offer dedicated parking next to and behind the building for added convenience.

Comfort also plays a major role in the patient experience we provide. From the moment you arrive, our team works to create a calm and supportive environment. We offer amenities such as blankets, neck pillows, iPads, and iPods to help make visits more pleasant and stress-free.

Dr. Sorina Ratchford brings a gentle, educational, and health-focused approach to patient care. She believes dentistry should improve both wellness and appearance, and she takes pride in helping patients invest wisely in their smiles. Her recommendations are always centered on what is best for the individual, with an emphasis on clear communication and personalized attention.

At Cambria Family Dentistry, we are proud to serve patients who value trust, comfort, and high-quality care. We aim to be more than just a dental office. We strive to be a long-term dental home where patients feel cared for, understood, and confident in the treatment they receive.
