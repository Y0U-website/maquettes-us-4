Meet Dr. Sorina Ratchford & Team

A Team Focused on Personalized Care

We are proud to provide care in a setting that feels warm and approachable while still offering the benefits of modern dentistry. Patients often appreciate the calm atmosphere of our downtown Cambria office and the friendly faces that greet them each time they visit.

Dr. Sorina Ratchford

Dr. Sorina Ratchford leads Cambria Family Dentistry with a gentle, detail-oriented, and health-focused approach. She believes dental care should support both wellness and appearance, and she takes time to help patients understand their oral health and treatment options clearly.

Her philosophy is rooted in the connection between oral health and overall well-being. She is committed to helping patients make informed decisions and invest wisely in their smiles through care that is thoughtful, personalized, and centered on long-term results.

Dr. Ratchford also believes that every aspect of dentistry should be approached with both function and aesthetics in mind. Her goal is to provide care that not only improves oral health, but also helps patients feel comfortable and confident in their smiles.

Outside the office, Dr. Ratchford enjoys spending time with her family and taking advantage of the natural beauty of the Central Coast through hiking and trips to the beach.
