Understanding Your Orthodontic Options

Orthodontic treatments have evolved, offering multiple solutions to address misaligned teeth and bite issues. Our Tarzana orthodontist is skilled in a range of treatments, ensuring you receive the best option for your smile.

Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces remain a reliable choice for correcting various orthodontic issues. They offer durability and effectiveness, making them suitable for both mild and complex cases.

Ceramic Braces

For those seeking a more discreet option, ceramic braces offer the same functionality as metal braces but with tooth-colored brackets, blending seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Lingual Braces

Lingual braces are placed behind the teeth, making them virtually invisible. They’re an excellent option for those who prioritize aesthetics during their orthodontic journey.

Guided Orthodontic Journey with Our Orthodontist

From the initial consultation to the final reveal of your transformed smile, our orthodontist will be with you every step of the way. With a patient-centric approach, we ensure your orthodontic experience is comfortable, efficient, and results in the smile you’ve always desired.

Clear Aligners

Invisalign’s clear aligners discreetly transform your smile by gently shifting teeth into alignment without the need for traditional braces. Enjoy the confidence of an inconspicuous and comfortable orthodontic solution.
