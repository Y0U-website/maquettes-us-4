Specialized Equipment for the Best Results in Diagnosing Dental Issues

A CBCT scan creates a 3D view and cross-sections of a target area in your mouth. The information this scan provides is critical for several situations, including performing a complex root canal or tooth extraction, or searching for the source of pain or infection. A CBCT gives us valuable information and feedback about your dental condition before a dental procedure.

How do our patients benefit from this state-of-the-art Imaging?

A CBCT scan ensures that we can diagnose your dental issues with the best possible accuracy.
