Restorative Dentistry

Whether it is because of an accident, improper care, or a variety of other reasons, teeth can get damaged and may need repairs. At Centre Smiles Dental, we offer a number of restorative dental services for patients seeing a variety of issues ranging from cavities to broken teeth and are ready to help you get the restorative treatment that your smile needs. Helping to ensure that your teeth retain their look as well as their integrity.

Our Restorative Services:

Dental Fillings

Cavities are caused when bacteria eats through the enamel layer of a tooth. If you have a cavity, it’s important to make sure it is filled properly to prevent further damage. Our team offers filling services to our patients, whether they are in need of a filling to help prevent further damage to a tooth, or an emergency filing should the cavity be causing pain.

Denture Stabilization

Many patients with dentures choose to have them secured through the use of additional dental implants. Helping to ensure that your smile stays stable and secure while chewing, talking, and more, we are ready to help with our denture stabilization services.

Dental Implants

Replacing a lost tooth can be highly beneficial to your smile. Our team is ready to provide high quality dental implants through our dental implant services to help restore your smile after you have a tooth removed.

Extractions

Sometimes if a tooth is damaged beyond reasonable restoration, its removal can become a necessity for ensuring your smile’s health. Our team of dental professionals are ready to help provide the fast and effective tooth extractions you need when you need them.

Dental Veneers/ Crowns

Sometimes a cosmetic procedure like a dental veneer or a crown can also be used to restore a tooth’s integrity after a cavity or dental injury. Helping to repair teeth from cracks, cavities, and chips, we are ready to help restore your smile with our dental veneer and crown services.
