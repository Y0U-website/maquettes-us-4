Our Story

Wellspring Dentistry was established in 2025 under the leadership of Dr. Adreel George, who envisioned a dental clinic where every patient feels cared for, understood, and valued. Built on a philosophy of trust, quality, and community connection, Wellspring Dentistry is committed to delivering personalized dental care for individuals and families in Trenton and surrounding areas.

Our Mission

To create a welcoming environment where healthy smiles begin and long-lasting confidence grows.

Dr. Adreel George

A Personalized Approach to Modern Dentistry

Dr. Adreel is committed to delivering elevated, patient-centered dental care focused on comfort, precision, and long-term oral health. With advanced training in restorative and comprehensive dentistry, he believes every patient deserves treatment that is both clinically exceptional and genuinely compassionate.

A graduate of the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine with a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree, Dr. Adreel continues to pursue ongoing education in modern dental techniques, implant therapy, sedation dentistry, and full-mouth rehabilitation to provide advanced solutions tailored to each patient&#x27;s individual needs.

At Wellspring Dentistry, Dr. Adreel&#x27;s philosophy is centered on creating a calm, welcoming environment where patients feel heard, respected, and confident in their care.

Beyond the Practice

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Adreel values health, wellness, and maintaining an active lifestyle. He is passionate about fitness and promoting healthy living, believing that overall wellness plays an important role in confidence, energy, and long-term quality of life — principles that closely align with his approach to patient care.

In addition to clinical practice, Dr. Adreel has spent over five years mentoring and coaching foreign-trained dentists through the CAAPID process, helping aspiring dental professionals navigate their path toward practicing dentistry in the United States. His passion for mentorship and education reflects his dedication to supporting growth, confidence, and professional development within the dental community.

Known for his approachable nature and attention to detail, Dr. Adreel strives to build meaningful relationships with patients and families while delivering care that feels personalized, comfortable, and modern.
