Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Dr. Bancroft has always loved helping people and feeling like she’s truly making a difference in their lives. Today, she prides herself on taking a team approach to dentistry. She never leaves her patients in the dark about their available options instead, she takes the time to fully explain every detail of their care plan so that they can obtain and maintain optimal oral health successfully. Building trusting relationships is important to her!

What Do You Like to Do Outside of the Dental Office?

Dr. Bancroft comes from a big family (including 15 nieces and nephews) whom she visits often. In her spare time, you’ll find her hiking, camping, traveling, running, cycling, ATVing, and doing pretty much anything outdoors. In fact, she chose to live in Colorado for the beautiful outdoors and the friendly people and she hasn’t been disappointed by either!
