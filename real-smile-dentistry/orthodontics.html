At Real Smile Dentistry, our Miami orthodontics treatments are guided by a physiologic, airway-centered approach. That means we are not only straightening teeth — we are aligning your bite so your smile looks beautiful and functions properly. Our team takes a conservative approach to care, recommending only what truly supports your long-term health.

From interventional orthopedics for kids to clear aligners and traditional braces for teens and adults, we tailor every treatment to your goals.
