At Real Smile Dentistry, we bring world-class dental care to three convenient locations. Whether you visit our North Miami, Miami Beach, or Hollywood office, you’ll receive expert care tailored to your total health and well-being.

Visit us at our

Miami locations

North Miami

Our North Miami office provides cutting-edge dental solutions with a focus on physiologic and airway-centered dentistry. Experience personalized care that goes beyond just treating teeth — we prioritize your comfort, health, and long-term wellness.

Miami Beach

Located in the heart of Miami Beach, our practice offers the same high-quality, comprehensive care in a serene and modern environment. We use advanced technology and a patient-first approach to ensure optimal oral health.

Hollywood

Get natural-looking veneers in a single visit at our newest Hollywood location, led by Dr. David Bitchatchi, the exclusive U.S. educator for the FirstFit veneer system. You’ll get high-level cosmetic and restorative care paired with our whole-health approach.
