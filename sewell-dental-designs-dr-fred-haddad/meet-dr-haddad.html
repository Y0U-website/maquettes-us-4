Meet Dr. Haddad

Dr. Haddad’s purpose at Sewell Dental Designs is to empower his patients so they can achieve and maintain excellent dental health and have a smile they are happy to show off. He decided on this path after receiving treatment from a competent and friendly dentist when he was a teen.

Dr. Haddad obtained his Bachelor of Science degree at George Mason University in Virginia and went on to earn his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry degree (DMD) from Temple University Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry. He is currently recognized as a fellow and master by the American and New Jersey Academies of General Dentistry and maintains memberships in the International Association of Orthodontics and the National Society of Dental Practitioners.

Dr. Haddad has been married to his lovely wife Silvana since 2000 and enjoys spending his free time with her and their two children, Christopher and Jessica.
