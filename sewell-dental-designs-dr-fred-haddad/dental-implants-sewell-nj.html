Dental Implants in Sewell, NJ

For permanent, fixed tooth replacements, there is no better solution than dental implants. Our patients who have received implants love the way they feel and look just like natural teeth. Finally, they can forget about their tooth loss and also enjoy eating whatever they like again rather than having to restrict their diets as with dentures.

When you consult with us about a tooth replacement, we start with a careful examination of all your teeth, gums and the bone in your jaw. Your mouth and bone should provide a healthy foundation for your dental implant. Next, we will carefully plan exactly where to place the implant which acts as the root of your new tooth. After ensuring the bone in this area is strong and solid, the titanium implant will be placed. The patient will now go home to allow the bone to heal and bond to the titanium.

After a few months, we will complete the implant by adding a white crown (the restoration ) that exactly matches the patient’s other teeth.

Because of our deep interest in and care for our patients, we ensure that each patient is comfortable every step of the way until the crown is finally placed. We then ensure that the patient knows just how to keep this new, replacement tooth clean.

Once the implant is complete, our patient doesn’t have to worry about special extra maintenance or what to eat. A dental implant is as though Mother Nature helped you grow a new tooth.

Dental Implants: A Permanent Solution for Tooth Loss

We understand tooth loss can affect your self-esteem, oral health, and quality of life. We offer dental implants as the gold standard for tooth replacement. Dental implants are a fixed, permanent solution, unlike traditional dentures and bridges. They not only look like natural teeth, but they also restore full functionality to the smile.

Implant Benefits

Natural-Looking and Functional: Dental implants are a marvel for their seamless integration with your natural teeth. Many of our patients comment on how hard it is to differentiate between implants and original teeth. This aesthetic quality restores the smile and boosts confidence. Implants are also unique in that they function just like natural teeth. The implants will allow patients to speak, bite, and chew with confidence. Patients who had difficulty eating certain foods because of missing teeth are now able to eat a variety of foods without restriction, improving their quality of life.

Comfortable: Dental implants also eliminate the inconvenience and discomfort of removable dentures. Implants eliminate the need for adhesives and worry about falling out of place when eating or speaking. Patients can eat their favorite foods without restrictions, as their implants will provide the strength and stability needed to chew and bite.

Easily Maintained: Another benefit of dental implants is that it is easier to maintain them versus other options that are available for tooth replacement. All you need to do is brush, floss, and have regular dental exams. Dental implants are popular because of their simplicity and ease of care.

Why Choose Sewell Dental Designs for Implant Services?

Comprehensive Consultation: Sewell Dental Designs offers a comprehensive consultation to begin your journey towards dental implant treatment. During this first appointment, our experienced dental team will examine your mouth, including your teeth, gums, and jawbone. This assessment is vital for determining if you are a good candidate for dental implant surgery.

Personalized Treatment: If you are a suitable candidate, we will plan the exact placement of your implant in the jawbone. We use 3D modeling and advanced imaging to ensure the optimal placement of the implant, which serves as the artificial roots for your new teeth.

Expert Implant Placement: After the treatment plan has been finalized, our dental professionals will proceed to surgically implant the titanium implants into your jawbone. The procedure is done under local anesthesia for your comfort. After a sufficient amount of time, the natural process of osseointegration will take place. During this period, the implant will fuse with the surrounding bone tissue and provide a stable foundation for the new tooth.

Healing Process Inquiries: Our team is available to answer any questions or concerns you may have during the healing process. We prioritize your comfort and well-being at each stage of the implant procedure, ensuring a positive experience.

Customized Restoration: After the implant has integrated fully with your jawbone (usually after a few weeks), we move on to the final step, which is the placement of a custom-made crown. The crown, which is made to match your natural teeth in size, color, and shape, is attached securely to the implant to complete your new smile.

Ongoing Care: Sewell Dental Designs’ commitment to your oral care doesn’t stop after your implant treatment. We offer personalized advice on maintaining good oral hygiene and adequately caring for your implants. Regular check-ups ensure that your implant will last for many years.
