DENTAL IMPLANT CROWN REPAIR IN PALM BEACH GARDENS, FL

Over time, the crown attached to a dental implant may become worn, cracked, loose, or damaged due to normal daily use, accidental injury, or changes in your bite. While the implant post is designed to provide long-lasting support, the crown portion may occasionally require repair or replacement to maintain proper function, comfort, and appearance.

Repairing an implant crown helps restore your ability to chew comfortably while maintaining a natural-looking and complete smile. Depending on the condition of the restoration, treatment may involve adjusting the crown, re-securing it, or replacing it entirely to achieve the best possible result.

At Dr. Steven Weiss’s office in Palm Beach Gardens, FL, our team carefully evaluates each dental implant restoration to determine the most effective repair solution. We focus on restoring the strength, comfort, and aesthetics of your smile while helping patients maintain a healthy, functional, and confident result for years to come.
