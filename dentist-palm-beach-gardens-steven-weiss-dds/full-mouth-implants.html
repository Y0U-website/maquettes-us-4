Full mouth implants provide a long-term alternative to traditional dentures by replacing an entire upper arch, lower arch, or both with secure implant-supported restorations. Dental implants act as artificial tooth roots, creating a strong foundation for replacement teeth while helping support jawbone health and providing exceptional stability.

Many patients choose full mouth implants because they offer improved comfort, chewing ability, speech, and confidence compared to removable dentures. The fixed restoration is designed to look, feel, and function more like natural teeth, allowing patients to enjoy daily activities without the inconvenience of removable appliances.

General Dentistry

General dentistry focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of common dental issues. Services may include dental exams, teeth cleanings, fillings, crowns, fluoride treatments, gum disease treatment, oral cancer screenings, tooth extractions, and other preventive and restorative procedures.

You should schedule an appointment if you experience tooth sensitivity, bleeding gums, chronic bad breath, tooth discoloration, gum recession, mouth sores, or if it has been more than six months since your last dental visit. Early treatment can help prevent more serious dental problems.

During your initial visit, your dentist will perform a comprehensive examination of your teeth and gums, review your oral health history, discuss any concerns, and may recommend digital X-rays if needed. Based on the findings, a personalized treatment plan will be created to help maintain or improve your oral health.
