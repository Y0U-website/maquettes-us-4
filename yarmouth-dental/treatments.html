Root Canal

Root canal therapy (endodontic treatment) is performed when the nerve of a tooth becomes inflamed or infected due to deep decay, trauma, repeated procedures, or cracks. During treatment, the infected tissue is carefully removed, the inside of the tooth is cleaned and sealed, and the tooth is restored to help prevent further damage. Root canal therapy helps save your natural tooth, allowing it to function comfortably and effectively for years to come.

Hygiene

Routine cleanings and maintenance form the foundation of effective dental care. Each cleaning is tailored to your individual needs based on the health of your teeth, gums, and supporting bone.

Periodontics

Gum disease (periodontal disease) is most often caused by plaque, a sticky film of bacteria that constantly forms on the teeth. If not removed through regular brushing, flossing, and professional dental care, plaque can irritate and inflame the gums. The earliest stage, gingivitis, may cause red, swollen, or bleeding gums. If left untreated, gum disease can progress, causing the gums to pull away from the teeth and form pockets where bacteria will collect. Over time, this can damage the bone and supporting structures that hold the teeth in place, potentially leading to tooth loss.

A crown is a custom-made cap that covers and protects a tooth that is too damaged or decayed to be repaired with a filling. They are often required after root canal therapy and are also used on dental implants to replace missing teeth. While crowns were traditionally made from gold or porcelain fused to metal, modern materials such as high-strength ceramic or porcelain allow for natural-looking, durable results.

Whitening

Today’s whitening treatments offer a safe and effective way to brighten your smile and reduce many common stains and discolorations. Depending on your goals, whitening can be completed in a single office visit or with professional take-home whitening systems designed for convenient at-home use.

Fillings

Dental fillings are used to restore teeth that have been affected by decay or minor damage. By replacing lost tooth structure, fillings help protect the tooth and restore its natural function and appearance.

Implants

Dental implants are often considered one of the best options for replacing one or more missing teeth. Designed to attach securely to your jaw, implants offer exceptional stability and strength. The result is a replacement tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like your natural teeth.

Bridges

Dental bridges are used to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring an artificial tooth to the healthy teeth on either side of the space. A bridge can restore your smile, improve chewing and speaking, and help maintain the alignment of your remaining teeth.
