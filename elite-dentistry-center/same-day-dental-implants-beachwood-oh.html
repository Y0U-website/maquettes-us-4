Types of Implant Solutions We Offer

  • Single-Tooth Implants Replace one missing tooth without altering healthy neighbors.
  • Multiple-Tooth Implant Bridges Secure, fixed replacements for several teeth—no removable parts.
  • Full-Arch “All-on-4” and “All-on-6” Implants For patients missing most or all teeth. Four to six implants support an entire arch of fixed teeth that restore chewing power and facial shape.
  • Implant-Supported Overdentures Snap-in dentures anchored by implants for comfort and stability.

How the Same-Day Implant Process Works

  • 3D Consultation & Digital Planning We capture a CBCT scan and iTero digital impressions to map nerves, bone, and soft tissue with precision.
  • Guided Implant Placement Computer-guided surgery lets Dr. Butriy place the titanium posts accurately and comfortably—often with minimal anesthesia.
  • Immediate Provisional Restoration Custom temporary crowns or bridges are attached before you leave, so you smile and chew the same day.
  • Healing & Final Restoration Over 8–12 weeks, the bone bonds to the implant. Then a final ceramic crown or full-arch bridge is placed for lifelong strength.
