Meet Dr. Mehrotra

Dr. Mehrotra is board certified in oral and maxillofacial surgery , with extensive training and experience in surgical procedures and administering adult and pediatric anesthesia. He maintains active licenses in New Jersey and holds active certification in Basic Life Support, Advanced Cardiac Life Support, Pediatric Advanced Life Support, and Advanced Trauma Life Support. Dr. Mehrotra is also an active member of the American Association of Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons (including NJOMS), the American Dental Association, and the American Medical Association.

Advanced Training in Oral Surgery

Early Life

Dr. Mehrotra comes from a rich lineage of doctors and dentists. His grandfather (Dad’s side Dr. P.N.Mehrotra) was the first private dental practitioner in his home city of Lucknow, and Dr. G.D.Kapoor (Mom’s side) was one of the pioneer cardiologists of his time in India.

Community Involvement

Dr. Mehrotra strongly believes in giving back to the community, and since his dental school days, he has made sure to regularly volunteer in and sponsor community events, helping organize free dental camps, helping in “Give Kids a Smile,” the Columbia Free Dental Clinic, and many more such.

Outside the Office

Outside of work, Dr. Mehrotra loves spending time with his wife Mahima, daughter Sharvi, and the rest of their family and friends. He is also a regular practitioner of Sahaja Yoga Meditation and loves to read, explore new places and cuisines, and play cricket, tennis, and table tennis in his free time.
