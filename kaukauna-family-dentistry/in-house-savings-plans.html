Sign-up for a Dental Savings Plan

Each savings plan runs for one year from the date you sign up, giving you a full 12 months of predictable, discounted access to preventive care. For new adult patients joining our practice, there is a one time setup fee that covers a new patient comprehensive exam and a full set of x-rays.
