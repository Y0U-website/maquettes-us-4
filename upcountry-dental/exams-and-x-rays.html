Exams and X-rays

New patients wanting regular treatment will have a comprehensive examination at the first visit. This includes digital radiographs (x-rays), periodontal charting which is measurements of your gum health around each tooth, and a dental evaluation for any potential problems with your teeth or bite and cosmetic concerns. This allows us to have a great understanding of your dental health and monitor for changes over time.

We can also do limited exams for patients with pain or one urgent concern.

Our digital radiographs offer high quality diagnostic information almost instantly at a much lower radiation dose than traditional film or medical radiography. With this technology we can get your diagnosis more safely, quickly, and accurately than ever before.
