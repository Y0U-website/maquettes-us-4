Our Implant Services

Single Tooth Implants

A single tooth implant is a permanent replacement for a single missing tooth. This option allows you to regain your smile and bite with confidence.

Multiple Tooth Implants

If you’re missing multiple teeth, multiple tooth implants may be the right option for you. With multiple tooth implants, you can get back to eating the foods you love and speaking with confidence.

All-on-x Implants

All-on-x implants are a revolutionary solution for people who have lost all their upper or lower teeth. This option provides you with a full arch of teeth supported by only four dental implants.

Implant-Supported Dentures

If you’re missing most of your teeth and have dentures that slip and shift, implant-supported dentures might be the solution for you. With implant-supported dentures, you’ll enjoy improved stability and comfort.

Immediate Load Implants

Immediate load implants are a great option for those who want to replace missing teeth quickly. With this option, you can have your new teeth placed on the same day as your implants.

3D Printed Tooth

Desert Hills Dental is proud to offer cutting-edge 3D printed implants. With our in-office technology, we can provide you with precise, customized implants for a perfect fit and optimal results.
