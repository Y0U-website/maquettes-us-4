Benefits of All-On-4 Implants

Here are some of the benefits you can enjoy from undergoing All-On-4 dental implant treatment:

  • Completely personalized results that are natural-looking
  • A solution that can last decades to a lifetime with proper care
  • Stronger biting power compared to those with traditional dentures
  • Easier maintenance due to dentures being permanently fixed to your mouth
  • Dental implants stimulate jawbone, preventing tissue deterioration from tooth loss

All-On-4 vs. Traditional Implants

All-On-4 is an optimal solution for patients who need to replace an entire arch of missing teeth, especially if they don’t have adequate jawbone density. This treatment is less invasive due to needing fewer implants, and it can typically be done with just one surgery instead of two. That said, traditional dental implants do offer maximum stability and strength. Our team will walk you through the differences and discuss which option would be best for you when rebuilding your smile.
