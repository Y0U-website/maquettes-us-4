Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures might be a good fit for you if:

If you are unsure of whether you qualify for implant dentures, visit us for a consultation. Most adults with extensive tooth loss can get this procedure, either right away or after some preliminary care.

  • You have lost all or most of your teeth throughout one of your dental arches.
  • You have adequate jawbone density to support implants.
  • You are in fair oral and overall health.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures offer some truly amazing benefits:

  • Strength and stability. Implant dentures can restore much more of your bite force than traditional dentures. You can expect to be able to eat virtually any food with ease.
  • Jawbone preservation. Dental implants stimulate your jawbone and help to keep it healthy and whole. In contrast, traditional dentures may actually accelerate bone loss in the jaw.
  • Dental implants may last throughout your lifetime. The denture that is attached to them may also endure for many years.
  • In some ways, taking good care of an implant denture is just like caring for natural teeth.
