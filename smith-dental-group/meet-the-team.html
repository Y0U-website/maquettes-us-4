Dr. B. Lance Smith D.D.S.

Dr. Smith’s self-made path to dentistry is unique and inspiring. From a young age, his high character values have been felt by those around him. As a junior in college and chemical engineering major, Lance was accepted on the spot during his interview for dental school. In 1987, he earned his D.D.S. (Doctor of Dental Surgery) from The University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. For the past 37 years, he has been serving the Tulsa community as a practicing dentist.

Outside of the office, Lance enjoys spending time with his friends and family. He has been married to his high school sweetheart for almost 40 years. Him and MaryBeth are members of their church, and often travel to their favorite spot in Norman, Oklahoma for football games and weekend getaways. Lance is an avid golfer and loves nothing more than an evening round under a setting sun.

Dr. Blake L. Smith D.M.D.

Blake is known for his inherent ability to soothe his patients from the fears that come with dental care. His passion and integrity are easily felt by those around him. Young Dr. Smith is thrilled to be back home working alongside his lifelong mentor. Blake loves having the privilege of soaking up Lance’s knowledge, while also adding his own clinical pearls to our growing practice. Smith Dental Group couldn’t be happier to have Blake officially on our team, and it’s a pleasure to watch his touch shine through our practice.

Outside of the office, Blake spends his time with his beautiful wife Nicole and their daughter. Together, they enjoy spending time with friends and family in the Tulsa and Dallas areas, attending church, rooting for their Sooners, spending evenings on the golf course, and summer days at the pool with their little one.
