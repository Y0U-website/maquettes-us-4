About Smith Dental Group

Smith Dental Group was founded by Dr. B. Lance Smith D.D.S. who has been providing optimal dental care in the Tulsa area since 1987. He is joined by two of his children Dr. Blake L. Smith D.M.D. and Meghan Smith, who has been Lance’s chairside dental assistant for over 10 years.

This father-son doctor duo is devoted to helping you and your family bridge the gap between fear and trust at the dentist. Our friendly team and brand-new office invite a warm, cozy, and non-clinical environment for you to relax in and enjoy. Both of our licensed doctors’ pride themselves on being relatable, qualified, and trustworthy providers that you and your family can depend on.

We are committed to excellence in dentistry and keeping your best interest at heart. Our doctors stay ahead of the field by seeking advanced dental training with the latest technology to ensure their patients receive top-notch care.

Smith Dental Group can provide you with a wide range of services, all conveniently under one roof. Helping you and your family maintain or achieve a healthy, beautiful smile in a stress-free manner is what we do best!
