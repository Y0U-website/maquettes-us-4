Digital X-Rays

Dental x-rays are a great preventative, diagnostic tool that provides important information not visible during a regular dental exam. Our dental team will utilize this important information to safely and accurately detect any dental abnormalities and then create an accurate treatment plan.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns are necessary when a tooth is severely damaged or has suffered from advanced decay and cannot be fixed with a bonded filling material. Crowns cover the remaining tooth and restore its function, strength, and esthetics. Several crown material options are available, and our doctors will discuss these individually depending on the area of the mouth that needs to be restored.

Bridges can be utilized when tooth loss occurs due to gum disease, decay, or injury. The loss of the tooth can create greater problems for your dental health if it is not corrected. The teeth next to the missing tooth (or teeth) can begin to move and result in pain, discomfort, and look unsightly. With bridges, our doctors will close any gaps in your mouth. The bridge looks and acts just like natural teeth are permanently cemented and helps maintain the rest of your teeth.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a great alternative to traditional bridgework, and dentures. They are safe and look and feel just like natural teeth. Implants are permanently anchored into the patient’s jawbone, and no preparation of adjacent teeth is required.

It is essential to replace missing teeth because if left with spaces, your teeth can shift and cause various other dental and medical problems. Dr. Bartlett has extensive experience in treatment planning and designing implant solutions custom-tailored for each patient. When your dental implants are placed, your jaw will bond and attach to the implant, making them very strong and functional. All surgical implant services are completed in-house, so our patients don’t have to rush around to a specialist’s office.

What are the benefits of implant dentistry?

Dental implants offer a range of benefits, including:

  • Allows you to keep the rest of your teeth healthy.
  • Dental implants help prevent the deterioration of your jawbone, keep your face structure in place, and keep your still existing teeth from shifting.
  • Helps to maintain anatomy.
  • When you have missing teeth, you run the risk of the bones in your face shrinking.
  • Bone loss can lead to your jawline receding and changes in your facial structure.
  • Security.
  • Expertly placed dental implants will not move around, which means most denture problems are eliminated.

What new technology is being used in office?

In our office we make every effort to stay up to date with new technology and changes in the dental profession. We are proud to offer our patients digital x-rays with every cleaning and comprehensive exam.

We also have a Cone Beam Computed Tomography system (CBCT). This allows our doctors to view the oral cavity in 3D to aid in earlier diagnosis of tooth infections, visualization of impacted wisdom teeth, and comprehensive evaluation of dental bone levels for implant placement.

In 2021, we purchased a dental laser. This tool aids our doctors in treatment of periodontal disease, cosmetic gingival procedures, biopsies, and allows select fillings to be completed without the need for anesthesia.

Our latest technology investment was an intra oral scanner. This allows our doctors to complete crowns, bridges and implant restorations without the need for a traditional impression. Impressions can often cause patients to gag and feel discomfort. This new tool sends our dental labs exact scans of the patient’s oral cavity without the discomfort.

What is the difference between a regular and deep cleaning?

A “regular” cleaning is recommended for all patients every 6 months. At this appointment, X-rays may be updated, your teeth will be cleaned by our dental hygienists, and the dentist will provide a full exam to check for any cavities or other oral conditions.

A deep cleaning is scheduled for patients that have Periodontal disease. Periodontal disease leads to heavy plaque and calculus (calcified plaque) build-up beneath the gum tissue. If not removed, bone loss can occur around teeth, leading to mobility and potential tooth loss. At a deep cleaning appointment, you will receive anesthesia for comfort, and all plaque and calculus existing beneath the gum line will be removed. In some cases, we will use our dental laser to sterilize deep gum pockets to promote healing.

What is an implant? Can I have implants placed at this office?

An implant is a Titanium post that is placed within bone in a space where a tooth is missing. The post then integrates within your natural bone – giving it strength and rigidity. At Smiles By Design, we can restore a single tooth or a full arch with dental implants. All work can be done at our office- from bone grafting and implant placement to restoration.

What types of preventive dental care do you provide for both children and adults?

Preventive treatment is very important to us at Smiles by Design. We encourage adults and children (age 12 months and up) to see the dentist every 6 months. Biannual visits allow us to professionally clean your teeth and to update X-rays to check for decay and other dental issues. For children, we recommend fluoride varnish during their check-ups to help protect their teeth from decay. Once a child’s permanent teeth erupt, we can place sealants on permanent teeth to block out the chewing surface from getting cavities.

For adults, one preventive service that we recommend is a nocturnal orthotic or “nightguard” for our patients that struggle with clenching and grinding their teeth at night.

Can you describe your approach to pediatric dentistry and what makes it child-friendly?

Both Dr. Bartlett and Dr. Fuston are happy to treat all members of your family- even the little ones! Both of our doctors are parents themselves and know how important it is to establish a “Dental home”. Our goal is to avoid childhood fears of the dentist that carry into adulthood. We practice the “tell- show- do” method. We take time to explain or “tell” your child what will happen at their appointment. We then “show” our instruments to the child and allow them to hold and touch them to see that they are not scary. Finally, we “do” the treatment.

Both doctors are certified to administer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) if the child requires extra help during the appointment.
