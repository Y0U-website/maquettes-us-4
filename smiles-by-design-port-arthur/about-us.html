About Smiles By Design

Smiles By Design became a reality in 2004 when Dr. Bartlett and his wife moved to the Port Arthur, TX, area to work as an associate in a thriving dental clinic owned and operated by Dr. Doug Becker. In 2006, Dr. Bartlett and his wife purchased the business and changed the name to Smiles By Design shortly after. After years of continued growth, we built and opened our new family dentist clinic building in January 2012.

Meet Our Team

Dr. Scott Bartlett

When Dr. Bartlett is not treating patients at Smiles By Design, he enjoys spending time with his wife Jeanifer, and his two sons Tyson and Mike. Since moving to the area in 2004, he has become very involved in the community and his church. Dr. Bartlett currently serves on the Board of Trustees for Port Neches Groves Independent School District and as a deacon at First Baptist Church Port Neches. If Dr. Bartlett is out of the office, you will most likely find him in the woods or at the lake. His hobbies include hunting, fishing, playing golf and traveling with his family.

Dr. Madison Fuston

Dr. Fuston grew up in Southeast Texas and attended school in Evadale and Buna before moving to Houston and graduating from Summer Creek High School. Dr. Fuston and her husband, Spencer, were high school sweethearts meeting through their involvement in marching band. Dr. Fuston and her husband now live in Winnie with their daughter, Jacqueline “Jackie”. Dr. Fuston is very involved with her church family- First Baptist Beaumont. In her spare time, Dr. Fuston enjoys baking and spending time with family.

Dental Care to Maintain a Healthy, Beautiful Smile

At Smiles By Design, we offer a range of affordable dental services to help our patients achieve the smile they have always wanted. Whether you need a dental implant, dentures, teeth whitening, or just a regular dental cleaning, we have the tools and knowledge necessary to help you. Our facility is fitted with state-of-the-art equipment to provide the highest quality implant placement services.

Dr. Bartlett and his dental team work hard to provide a warm and welcoming environment for every patient. We take the necessary time to discuss one-on-one with each patient about their dental options and what would be best for their overall oral health.

Our Service Promise

We see only one patient at a time, so there is no sitting and waiting to be seen. We schedule as much time as needed so our patients don’t feel rushed or pressured to accept treatment. We work to fit in convenient appointments for our patients and work around their schedules as much as possible. Patients leave happy, knowing they receive the best care from a team that truly cares about them.
