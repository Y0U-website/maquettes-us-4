Dental Implants in Metro Detroit

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up — a titanium post is placed in the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root, topped with a custom crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth. Unlike a bridge or denture, an implant stands on its own and helps preserve the jawbone underneath it.

Why Patients Choose Us for Dental Implants

Feels and functions like a natural tooth Implants are anchored in the jawbone, giving them stability a denture or bridge can’t match.

Helps preserve your jawbone Missing teeth can lead to bone loss over time — an implant helps prevent that.

Doesn’t rely on neighboring teeth Unlike a bridge, an implant stands independently without altering healthy teeth nearby.

Planned precisely with 3D imaging Our in-house CBCT technology maps your jaw in detail before treatment begins.

What to Expect

Your consultation starts with a detailed 3D scan of your jaw to plan your treatment precisely. The implant process happens in stages — placing the titanium post, allowing it to integrate with the bone, then attaching your custom crown — with your dentist guiding you through timing at each step.
