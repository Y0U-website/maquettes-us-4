What Sets Us Apart

Dr. Diers is a Board Certified Orthodontist who practices state of the art specialty care using the most advanced technology available. His holistic and detailed approach to your treatment will provide you with not only a beautiful, healthy smile for years to come but also an enhanced wellbeing that comes from an improved airway and healthy jaw joints. His philosophy of treating the whole person ensures that your results go beyond your beautiful smile. Our patients experience improvements to sleep quality, TMJ function, and an enhanced vitality for the entire body.

Treatments

Healthy smiles for healthy families start with professional family dentistry. We offer many comprehensive services at Nelson R. Diers Orthodontics.

Orthodontics

We all want to look our best, and many orthodontic treatments can help individuals reach that important goal. Even more important is helping our patients achieve a healthy, well-functioning, stable bite.

Accelerated Orthodontics

Periodontally accelerated osteogenic orthodontics is a kind of orthodontic treatment that focuses on correcting bite issues as well as jaw misalignment problems.

Early Interceptive Treatment

With interceptive orthodontics, we aim to find developing issues as well as signs of future problems, and intercept them before they can damage a patient’s teeth, mouth, or jaw.

TMJ Therapy

The temporomandibular joints, or TMJ, are responsible for the movements of your mouth. When the joints become irritated, you may be faced with extreme pain and complications performing basic tasks like biting, chewing, speaking.

Dental Sleep Therapy

If you’re waking up in the morning feeling tired and poorly rested, your throat hurts, or are waking up feeling like you’re choking, then there’s a good chance that you suffer from a sleep disorder called sleep apnea.
