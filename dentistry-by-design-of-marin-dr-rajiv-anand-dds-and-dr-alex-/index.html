Experienced Team Our skilled team at Dentistry By Design of Marin has years of experience in all aspects of dentistry. We are dedicated to providing exceptional care and personalized treatment plans for every patient.

He pursued his passion for dentistry at Northwestern University Dental School and then completed a 2-year residency in an AEGD program (Advanced Education in General Dentistry) at UCSF, where he focused on enhancing oral health.

Dr. Alex Schmotter DDS Alex graduated from Cal Poly SLO with a degree in Biology emphasizing in Anatomy and Physiology. His fathers positive influence led him into Dental School at the University of Pacific.
