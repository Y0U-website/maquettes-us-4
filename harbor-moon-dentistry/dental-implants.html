Restoring your smile from the root up

A missing tooth affects more than your smile. The bone that used to hold the root starts to shrink once nothing is pressing into it. Teeth on either side of the space begin to lean toward it. Chewing shifts to the other side of your mouth, and over a few years that shows up as wear, soreness, and a bite that no longer meets the way it used to.

Gig Harbor dental implants address the part of the tooth you cannot see. A titanium post takes the place of the root, the surrounding bone grows tight against it over the following weeks, and a custom crown goes on top once that bond is solid. At Harbor Moon Dentistry, Dr. Michael Neish plans the case, performs the surgery, and delivers the final restoration himself.

Understanding same-day dental implants

The term same-day dental implants can mean different things, so it’s important to understand what’s included.

Same-day implant placement

A damaged or failing tooth is removed, and the dental implant is placed during the same appointment. Dr. Michael Neish offers this approach when the surrounding bone is healthy enough to provide the stability needed for successful healing.

Same-day teeth (immediate loading)

A temporary or permanent tooth is attached to the implant on the day of surgery. Harbor Moon Dentistry does not recommend this approach because newly placed implants need time to fuse naturally with the jawbone before supporting a crown.

By allowing the implant to heal before placing the final restoration, Dr. Neish helps create the stable foundation needed for long-term success.

K L Does Dr. Neish place the implant himself?

He places it himself and restores it himself. From the CBCT scan through the surgery to the day the crown goes on, one dentist and one set of records handle the case.

K L Can you get a dental implant the same day a tooth is extracted?

Often, yes. Dr. Neish places Gig Harbor implants at the time of extraction when the bone and socket can support it. What he does not do is attach a functioning tooth to that implant the same day, because loading an implant before it has fused to the bone puts the whole result at risk.

K L How long does the dental implant process take?

Most of the timeline for Gig Harbor dental implants is healing rather than chair time. After placement, integration takes weeks to months depending on the site and whether grafting was involved. Dr. Neish maps your specific schedule at the first visit so you know what each stage requires.

K L Do dental implants come with a warranty?

Yes. Implants placed at Harbor Moon Dentistry carry a one-year limited warranty. Dr. Neish will walk you through exactly what it covers before you agree to treatment.

K L Does dental insurance cover dental implants?
