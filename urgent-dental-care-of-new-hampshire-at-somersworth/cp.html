Financial Considerations:

By scheduling an appointment through our online system, you acknowledge and agree to these terms. We look forward to providing you with excellent dental care.

  • Insurance Verification: While we attempt to verify insurance prior to your appointment, this may not always be possible on shorter notice. Please be prepared to pay out-of-pocket if we cannot verify your insurance in time, in which case we will submit all claims to your insurance and reimburse you accordingly. We do not accept state Medicaid insurance.
  • Minimum Costs: Emergency appointment evaluations typically range from approximately $180 to $320, depending on the types of X-rays required. This is a minimum estimate and may increase based on your specific needs. If more extensive X-rays are necessary for accurate diagnosis, these will be required and charged accordingly. We do not negotiate on necessary diagnostic procedures as they are crucial for your proper care.
  • New patient hygiene appointments have an estimated cost of $130 for dental cleaning, $200 for a full series of X-rays, and $130 for a comprehensive exam and treatment plan.
  • Payment: Payment will be due at the time of service if you do not have insurance, or if we are unable to verify your insurance before your appointment.
