Implant Bridges for Consecutive Missing Teeth

When several teeth are missing next to one another, an implant-supported bridge may provide an effective solution. Instead of placing an implant beneath every missing tooth, strategically placed implants can support multiple replacement teeth within a single restoration.

This approach often helps restore larger gaps while preserving healthy neighboring teeth. Traditional bridges may require modifications to adjacent teeth. Implant-supported bridges avoid placing that responsibility on healthy tooth structure.

Restoring Larger Gaps Without Affecting Healthy Teeth

One of the primary advantages of implant-supported bridges involves protecting surrounding teeth. Because the restoration receives support from implants rather than neighboring teeth, healthy teeth can remain untouched.

Preserving natural tooth structure whenever possible often contributes to better overall oral health. Many patients appreciate this conservative approach to restoring their smile.
