About

0 Dr. Wisam Musa, DDS Dr. Wisam Musa is a dedicated and highly trained dentist committed to providing exceptional, patient-centered care. He earned his Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) in 2013 from the University of Science and Technology and further advanced his clinical skills with three years of training in Oral Surgery at New York University.

Dr. Musa completed a two-year General Practice Residency (GPR) at MetroHealth Medical Center in Cleveland, Ohio, where he served as Chief Resident. His advanced training allows him to confidently manage a wide range of dental procedures, from routine care to more complex surgical treatments.

In addition, Dr. Musa completed an Implant Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) and is a member of the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland—demonstrating his ongoing commitment to excellence and continuing education.

Known for his gentle approach and attention to detail, Dr. Musa prioritizes patient comfort and clear communication at every visit. He is fluent in English, Spanish, and Arabic, allowing him to serve a diverse community with ease.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Musa enjoys traveling, watching and playing soccer, and viewing insightful documentaries. At the center of it all is his family, with whom who values spending quality time and creating lasting memories.
