Our Services

Comprehensive Healthcare Services Tailored to Your Needs

Medical Services

Our medical services encompass comprehensive evidence-based primary care, chronic disease management, preventive screenings, and acute care, all delivered by our dedicated team to ensure your overall health and wellness.

Behavioral Wellness Services

Mental health is just as important as physical health. We provide support for a variety of mental health conditions and substance use disorders to help you achieve emotional well-being and resilience.

Psychiatry Services

Our psychiatry services offer expert mental health care, including diagnosis, medication management, and therapeutic support for various psychiatric conditions, ensuring compassionate and comprehensive treatment.

Counseling and Case Management

We provide professional counseling to help individuals navigate life’s challenges. We offer case management services to connect patients with community resources, ensuring comprehensive support and care coordination.

Dental Services

Good oral health is a key part of overall health. Our dental services include cleanings, exams, fillings, extractions, and preventive education to help you maintain a healthy smile.

Community Engagement

We actively engage with our community through outreach programs, health education workshops, and partnerships with local organizations, fostering a healthier, more connected community.

Request an Appointment

Ready to take control of your health? Schedule an appointment with us today! You can call us at 217-600-7000 or use our online appointment request form.

Vision Care

Coming soon, we will provide comprehensive vision care, including eye exams, glasses, and treatment for eye conditions, to ensure clear and healthy vision for all community members.

Health Education

Our health education empowers patients and families with clear, practical information to help prevent illness, manage chronic conditions, and make confident decisions about care.
