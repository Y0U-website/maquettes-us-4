The practice

At Wynne Downtown Dental, our first and foremost priority is the health and safety of our patients and staff. We follow strict sanitation and infection control protocols to ensure a clean, safe environment for every visit. Your well-being and comfort are at the heart of everything we do.

Conveniently located in Wynne, AR, our office provides a warm, welcoming environment for all patients. We only use state-of-the-art equipment in order to provide the best, most comfortable experience possible.

Exams & Cleaning Routine dental exams are vital for maintaining oral hygiene and health.

Crowns & Veneers Restore your smile with a wide range of restorative dentistry services.

Teeth Whitening Enjoy a brighter smile with a variety of industry-leading cosmetic services.

Our staff is dedicated to making sure that your entire experience is the best it can possibly be. We only hire team members who are passionate about you and your family’s dental health.

Whatever your needs are, our team is trained to listen and offer the best advice and guidance in choosing the services that best meet your needs. Let us assure you that every visit to our office offers worry-free dentistry that you deserve and a smile you can show off to the world.

From routine exams & cleanings to the latest in professional whitening options, Wynne Downtown Dental is your source for worry-free, modern dentistry!
