Insurance and Billing

While we accept most insurance plans, we do not participate in specific Health Management Organization programs. We are happy to file your insurance claims for you and will verify acceptance of your plan.

In addition, we accept checks, cash, or credit cards. For patients who would like to enlist in a payment plan, we work with CareCredit. As long as you are free to choose your own dentist, we are happy to file insurance claims for reimbursement. Ready to schedule an appointment?
