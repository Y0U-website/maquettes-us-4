Itero Scanner

The latest addition to our practice, the Itero Scanner is a 3D scanner which we use to scan your teeth and make a “digital impression”. This eliminates the need to take physical impressions and can be used to fabricate a number of dental treatment items such as Invisalign trays, night guards, as well as crowns and bridges.

CEREC

We use this technology to design and mill crowns in-house. This system utilizes CAD/CAM technology to create crowns in just one visit. It saves you both time and money, as we can receive impressions and have their crown created and fitted within the same appointment.

Pax 2000

In the wake of Covid 19, we are taking the extra step in ensuring an even safer environment for our patients. We are now introducing the Pax 2000 Extraoral Dental Suction System. This system, used by our dentist and hyegeinists, allows for safe removal of extra aerosols and droplets that are created while dental treatment is being performed. Not only does it do that, but the system is able to filter out and stop 99.97 % of any viruses, germs or dust that would end up contaminating operatories and clothes.

Air Scrubber

Our air scrubber is an environmental conditioning system. Using certified Space Technology, this system attaches to our HVAC system. It then continuously purifies the air and reduces particles that can irritate allergies and asthma as well as protect people from contaminants and pollutants.

UVCBOX Light

The UVCBOX Light is a tripod supported, portable room sterilizer. Using UVC light technology, when placed in the room after all patients for the day are seen, it begins it’s sterilizing session and disinfects all surfaces and air inside the room within minutes. Leaving all operatories completely sterilized and ready for the next day! UVC Light technology and has been tested by the CDC and proven to kill not only Covid-19 but also cold, flu and other pathogens as well.

Moravision

The Moravision is a digital 3D video microscope system that delivers realtime full HD resolution viewing of procedures and displays it on a three dimensional screen. This gives us clearer vision of what we are treating, more depth perception and also enhances our ability to adequately treat and also document certain procedures. Just another way we are raising the bar in patient care! Learn more about what sets us apart at https://moravision.com/

Oral DNA

We are now offering non-invasive salivary diagnostic testing that can be quickly administered chairside. These tests are administered by a simple 30 second swish and gargle that are collected and sent to a lab to help identify possible hidden oral pathogens that could potentially threaten your oral and systemic health. Please visit oraldna.com/for-patients to learn more!
