Financial Options

Transparent, Affordable Payment Options

Insurance

Stress-free process

We accept most traditional insurance plans, including Cigna , Healthchoice , Delta Premier , DNOA , GEHA , Careington , Aetna , LHI , and Optum . In-network or not, we have years of experience helping our patients maximize their dental benefits.

Savings Plan

No insurance? Not a problem

We offer an annual membership plan that can be renewed each year, providing dental services at a low yearly fee for those without insurance.

Financing Options

Get care now, pay later

We believe every patient deserves access to high-quality dental care when they need it most. To make treatment more affordable, we partner with trusted third-party financing providers, including Cherry , CareCredit , and Alphaeon .

Patient Portal

A Modern Experience

Our office is equipped with modern technology to provide you with the best care possible. That includes our online Patient Portal, which you will have access to once you become a patient.

In our Patient Portal, you will be able to request appointments, view your family’s upcoming appointments, and complete and edit patient forms on your schedule once you have had your first appointment.
