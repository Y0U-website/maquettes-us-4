Dr. Kevin B. Carter, DMD

Primary Dentist

The Smile Louisville is pleased to announce that Dr. Kevin B. Carter joined our practice October 2014.

Dr. Carter was born September 11, 1957 at Fort Campbell, Kentucky and has lived in Louisville most of his life. He attended Butler High School and graduated from Doss High School in 1975. Dr. Carter subsequently earned a Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Louisville. School of Arts and Science, and a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 1983.

Dr. Carter has spent the previous three decades in the private practice of dentistry while recently retiring from a simultaneous 30 year career in the United States Army Reserve. Dr. Carter has been deployed overseas numerous times in support of United States military humanitarian operations in Honduras, El Salvador, Panama, and Ecuador and combat operations in Iraq and Afghanistan.

Dr. Carter has been a member of the American Dental Association, Kentucky Dental Association, and Louisville Dental Society for over 30 years.

Dr. Carter is married to Penny Crawford Carter of Louisville. Together they’ve raised two sons, Oliver and Neil. Dr. Carter enjoys primarily outdoor activities such as cycling, running, Scuba, fly fishing, hiking the Appalachian Trail, traditional grilling, and bizarre foods.

Dr. Bob E. Spiller, DMD, MS

Orthodontist

Dr. Spiller was born in Ft Riley, Kansas and spent his early years living in different states/countries with his military family. He eventually graduated from Frankfurt American High School in Frankfurt Germany.

Dr. Spiller attended Centre College of Kentucky and The University of Louisville School of Dentistry graduating with a DMD in 1980. Upon graduation he joined the US Army and was active duty for 12 years. During that time he returned to The University of Louisville School of Dentistry and completed a 2 year orthodontic residency, graduation with a MS in oral biology.

Dr. Spiller left active duty in 1992 and entered private practice in Southern Indiana and recently retired after 25 years. He maintained his military career by joining the US Army Reserves and retired after 31 years of service.

Dr. Spiller is board certified and teaches orthodontics at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. He is a member of the ADA, AAO, KAO and Southern Indiana Dental Association. He and his wife Pam have 2 children Rebecca and Robert. His hobbies include running, cycling, golf and eating out at local restaurants.
