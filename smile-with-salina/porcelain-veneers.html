Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain Veneers in Inglewood, CA Book an Appointment A Natural-Looking Way to Transform Your Smile Porcelain veneers can improve the appearance of teeth that are chipped, stained, uneven, worn, or slightly misshapen. Dr. Salina Mah offers porcelain veneers in Inglewood, CA for patients who want a more polished smile while keeping the result natural and balanced.

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that are bonded to the front of the teeth. They can be designed to improve color, shape, symmetry, and spacing. During your consultation, Dr. Salina Mah will evaluate your teeth, discuss the changes you would like to make, and explain whether veneers are a good fit for your smile.

Custom Designed for Your Smile Each veneer is planned around the shape, size, and color that best complements your teeth and facial features.

Natural Appearance Porcelain reflects light in a way that closely resembles natural tooth enamel, helping veneers blend naturally with your smile.

Long-Lasting Smile Improvement With proper care, porcelain veneers can provide durable cosmetic improvement for many years.

What Can Porcelain Veneers Improve? Veneers are a versatile cosmetic treatment and can address several concerns at the same time. They may be used to improve teeth that do not respond well to whitening, repair the appearance of minor chips, create more even tooth shapes, or reduce the appearance of small gaps.

Because veneers change the visible surface of the tooth, careful planning is important. Dr. Salina Mah will review your bite, tooth structure, and cosmetic goals before recommending treatment and will explain what kind of result you can realistically expect.

Discoloration and Staining Veneers can cover stubborn discoloration and create a brighter, more even tooth color.

Chipped or Worn Teeth Minor chips, worn edges, and uneven tooth shapes can often be improved with custom porcelain veneers.

Small Gaps and Uneven Spacing Veneers may help improve the appearance of certain gaps or spacing concerns without changing the entire smile.
