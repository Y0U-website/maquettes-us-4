Dental Bonding

Dental Bonding in Inglewood, CA Book an Appointment A Simple Way to Improve Small Smile Imperfections Dental bonding is a conservative cosmetic treatment that can improve the appearance of chipped, uneven, discolored, or slightly spaced teeth. Dr. Salina Mah offers dental bonding in Inglewood, CA for patients who want to make targeted changes to their smile without more extensive treatment.

During bonding, a tooth-colored resin is carefully shaped and applied directly to the tooth. The material is then hardened and polished so it blends naturally with the surrounding teeth. Because bonding usually requires little or no removal of natural tooth structure, it can be a practical option for many minor cosmetic concerns.

Conservative Treatment Dental bonding typically requires minimal preparation and preserves most of your natural tooth structure.

Natural-Looking Results The bonding material is selected and shaped to closely match the color and appearance of your natural teeth.

Often Completed in One Visit Many bonding treatments can be completed during a single appointment, depending on how many teeth are being treated.

What Can Dental Bonding Improve? Dental bonding can be used for a variety of small cosmetic changes. It may help repair minor chips, improve uneven edges, cover small areas of discoloration, close certain spaces between teeth, or change the shape of a tooth that looks out of proportion with the rest of your smile.

Bonding is not the right solution for every cosmetic concern. Dr. Salina Mah will examine your teeth and bite, discuss what you want to improve, and explain whether bonding, veneers, whitening, or another treatment may give you the most predictable result.

Chipped or Uneven Teeth Bonding can restore the appearance of small chips and smooth irregular tooth edges.

Small Gaps Between Teeth Tooth-colored resin may be used to reduce the appearance of certain minor spaces between teeth.

Shape and Color Improvements Bonding can help improve slightly misshapen teeth or cover localized discoloration.
