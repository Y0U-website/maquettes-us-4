Implants, plainly answered.

The most common questions we get before the consult.

  • Am I a candidate? Most healthy adults are. We check bone density, gum health, and medical history at the consult. If bone is thin, we can build it up first.
  • How long does the whole process take? 3 to 6 months from placement to final crown for most cases. Full-arch can be same-day if bone allows.
  • Does it hurt? The procedure itself is done under local anesthetic, you feel pressure, not pain. Most patients take ibuprofen for a day or two after.
  • How much does an implant cost? Single implants typically run $3,500, $5,500 all-in (post + abutment + crown). Full-arch varies widely. We give you the exact number in writing at the consult.
  • Does insurance cover implants? Some medical and dental plans cover part of the restoration. We verify before you commit and file the claim.
  • Do you do sedation? Yes, nitrous, oral, and IV options depending on your comfort level and the procedure.
