Meet The Team

Prosthodontist Janean Bray Janean began her dental career in Kentucky and studied Expanded Function Dental Assisting at the University of Louisville. After years of clinical experience chairside, she transitioned to the front office, where she helps patients navigate treatment and feel comfortable throughout their care. Known for her warm personality, Janean enjoys building relationships with patients. Outside the office, she loves spending time with family, traveling, cooking—especially barbecue—and exploring her creativity through crafting and floral design.

Front Office JACQUE DZWONKOSKI Jacque is a proud Arizona native and mother of two with over 15 years of experience in dentistry, including six years as a dental hygienist. She is passionate about helping patients improve their home care routines and believes education and prevention are key to maintaining healthy smiles. Known for her upbeat personality, Jacque helps patients feel comfortable and well cared for at every visit. Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with her family, staying active, and camping in Flagstaff on the weekends.

Dental Hygienist Kelly MORSE Kelly, a Georgia native who has lived in Arizona since 2007, has been married for over 30 years and is the proud mother of four and grandmother of two. She began her dental career in 1999 as a dental assistant, building extensive experience in patient care and clinical support. Known for her friendly, approachable personality, Kelly helps patients feel comfortable at every visit. Outside the office, she enjoys reading, crafting, camping, and spending time with family and friends exploring the outdoors and making memories.

Dental Assistant MINU CASH Minu, an Arizona native, has over 20 years of experience as a dental hygienist and is passionate about helping patients improve their oral health through education, prevention, and personalized care. She enjoys spending time with her children and grandchildren, staying active, and building meaningful relationships with patients. Outside of dentistry, Minu is a published author with a creative spirit, enjoying decorating, reading, and inspiring others to pursue their passions.

Dental Hygienist RENIAH MERCIER Reniah is an Arizona native, wife, and mother with over 15 years of experience in dentistry. She enjoys helping patients understand their oral health and loves seeing how dentistry can positively impact people’s lives. At Valley Dental Group, Reniah works part time assisting with scheduling, patient coordination, and front desk operations to help ensure a smooth and welcoming experience for every patient. Outside the office, she enjoys paddle boarding, reading, hiking, and traveling.
