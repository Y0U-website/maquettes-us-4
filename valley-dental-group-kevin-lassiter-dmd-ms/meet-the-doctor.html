Meet The Doctor

0 PERSONALIZED CARE FOR EVERY SMILE DR. KEVIN LASSITER DMD, MS MEET KEVIN: Dr. Lassiter provides comprehensive, relationship-centered dentistry focused on natural esthetics, long-term function, and thoughtful patient care. He believes exceptional dentistry blends science, artistry, and trust—helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles that look and feel completely natural.

Known for his integrity and meticulous attention to detail, Dr. Lassiter takes a highly personalized approach to treatment. He listens carefully to each patient’s goals and designs care that prioritizes lasting oral health while preserving the natural beauty of the smile. His philosophy centers on conservative, precise dentistry that serves the patient’s long-term wellbeing.

EDUCATION:

Dr. Lassiter earned his bachelor’s degree from Arizona State University before receiving his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine . He later completed advanced prosthodontic training through the United States Army in Augusta, Georgia in 2022, where he developed extensive experience in complex restorative dentistry, dental implants, and full-mouth rehabilitation.

honored to serve:

Dr. Lassiter is an Army veteran who had the privilege of serving as a dentist in the United States Army, where he cared for service members and their families. These experiences helped shape his professional perspective and deepened his appreciation for building genuine, trusting relationships with those he serves. Today, he carries that same sense of duty into his practice and is truly honored to care for every patient who walks through the door, striving to provide thoughtful, compassionate dentistry that supports lifelong oral health.

OUTSIDE THE OFFICE:

Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his family, discovering new restaurants, and perfecting the craft of a well-made cocktail.
