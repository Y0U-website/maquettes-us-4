Laser Dentistry

Also known as no-drill dentistry, this new method of dental care involves using a powerful but safe laser. This means that there’s little to no pain and thus little need for anesthesia — making your visit far more comfortable.

Because typical comfort and pain-relieving techniques aren’t needed as much, laser dentistry is more time-efficient. Multiple procedures can be scheduled for the patient on the same visit.

  • Little to no pain — minimal anesthesia required
  • Multiple procedures in a single appointment — saves your time
  • DIAGNOdent pen for accurate cavity detection diagnostics
  • Safe, precise, and proven technology trusted by dentists worldwide

Digital X-Ray Machines

Most importantly, the radiation dose received by a digital X-ray is significantly lower than that of a traditional radiogram, making routine check-ups safer than ever. Digital X-rays make it easier for doctors to check on dental implants, jaw health, and deeper factors invisible to the naked eye.

  • Significantly lower radiation exposure than traditional X-rays
  • Instant digital images downloaded directly to computer
  • Easy side-by-side comparison with historical X-rays
  • Ideal for monitoring implants, jaw bone, and deep dental structures
  • Enables more accurate and confident treatment planning
  • Used for implant planning, orthodontics, and complex extractions
  • Reduces the need for exploratory procedures — see everything first
