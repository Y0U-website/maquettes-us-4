The practice

Conveniently located in downtown Peekskill, our dental office welcomes patients from Cortlandt Manor, Buchanan, Montrose, Croton-on-Hudson, Mohegan Lake, Shrub Oak, Yorktown Heights, Ossining, Sleepy Hollow, Putnam Valley, and surrounding communities.

We combine decades of clinical experience with modern dental technology, including Primescan digital impressions, to make treatment more precise, comfortable, and efficient. Our goal is to provide high-quality dentistry in a relaxed, welcoming environment while helping our patients maintain healthy smiles for years to come.

We believe great dentistry is about more than treating teeth it s about building trust. We take the time to listen to our patients, explain treatment options, answer questions, and help you feel comfortable and informed about your dental care.

We are welcoming new patients and are proud to provide dental care in both English and Spanish.
