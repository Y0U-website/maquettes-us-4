Melinda Gray Lucas DMD

Dr. Melinda Gray Lucas grew up on a farm in Brooksville, MS. She is married to Dr. Alan Lucas, and together they have two beautiful daughters. Dr. Melinda has two brothers, one of which is also a dentist.

As a young girl, Dr. Melinda always enjoyed her visits to the dentist. She enjoyed them so much, she ended up working for her dentist during her college years.

In addition to these positive experiences, Dr. Melinda chose dentistry because helping people is what gives her the most joy. Whether she helps you remove years’ worth of pain or makes your smile brighter, she does everything with your needs in mind.

As a people person, Dr. Melinda is always building meaningful relationships with her patients and coworkers. Her creative nature and incredible skills are an enviable combination when creating beautiful smiles.

Away from dentistry, Dr. Melinda enjoys playing tennis, being outdoors, playing with her pets, cooking, and crafting.

I had my first appointment with Dr. Alan Lucas yesterday. I loved the location. It has a beautiful view overlooking the lake while you get your dental work done. Dr. Lucas and his staff were awesome.

Meredith Gray Lucas DMD

Dr. Meredith Gray Lucas was born and raised in Hattiesburg, MS, by her parents Dr. Alan and Melinda Lucas.

Growing up, she was engulfed in dentistry and enjoyed assisting her parents in their dental practice. During her educational years, she traveled to Honduras with her father on a mission trip through Baptist Medical Dental International Opens a new window to the official website . It was on this trip that she realized she truly wanted to pursue a career in dentistry.

To achieve her new goal, Dr. Meredith moved to Jackson, MS, where she attended the University of Mississippi Medical Center and received a Master’s in Biomedical Sciences and also her Doctorate in Dental Medicine.

During your appointments, Dr. Meredith places a particular emphasis on patient education, so you always feel informed and empowered. No matter how small your procedure, she’ll explain each step and answer any questions. Your comfort and relaxation are her top priority!

Outside the office, Dr. Meredith enjoys running with her dad and her dog, Sissy. She also adores spending time with her family and friends.

Zachariah Lea Crawford DMD

Dr. Zachariah Lea Crawford lives in Hattiesburg, MS, with his lovely wife, Jodi, beautiful son Cole, and two puppy dogs, Bella and Jaxon.

As a child, Dr. Crawford had always been interested in the medical field, thanks to both his parents being medical physicians. However, he only started thinking about becoming a dentist after a soccer accident at the age of 12. He had to have a root canal, and Dr. Lucas was the one who performed the procedure. This experience was the beginning of Dr. Crawford’s journey in dentistry.

As a professional, his love for helping people has remained his primary motivation. Also, he enjoys the daily challenges dentistry presents and how he can help solve them.

He has significant experience as he has served on the Mississippi Board of Donated Dental Services. He has also been the secretary, treasurer, and president for the South Mississippi Dental Society.

Outside of the office, Dr. Crawford is an avid outdoorsman. He loves to go fishing and to spend time with his family.
