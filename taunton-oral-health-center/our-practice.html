Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Fang had extensive scientific insights, evidenced by 13 leading publications, including 10 papers in peer-reviewed journals and 3 books or book chapters.

Before entering dentistry in the USA, Dr. Fang had been worked at leading institutes like Harvard Medical School, Harvard University, and University of Massachusetts Dartmouth, before cofounding a leading genetic diagnostic company in China and serving as the Chief Scientific Officer, with 6 patents approved.

Dr. Fang had been many honor and award holders at the university, provincial, and national levels, including 2001 National Excellent Doctorate Dissertations, administered by the State Council and the Ministry of Education, the People’s Republic of China, 2001. This prize only awards the top 100 candidates from all the fields of social science and natural science across the country and Dr. Fang was the first one who won this honor in Dentistry, a well-defined milestone in China dentistry history.

Lastly, Dr. Fang had been one of the world-leading collectors in Chinese ancient jades, circa from 4,000 BCE to 200 CE, and Song porcelain (with many imperial pieces in Ru, Jun, and Ge wares, circa 1100 CE), the aims of which were to define or redefine the China civilization.
