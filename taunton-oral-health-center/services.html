Services

As one of the leading oral health care providers, we utilize the multidisciplinary, science or evidence - based approaches to formulate the right or the most accurate diagnosis and to provide the treatment with the enduring quality and the best outcome.

Our doctor came from strong medical background, followed by 6 years dedicated Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and its associated scientific training, and another 15 years basic medical science researches, in different job capacity, from research scientist to the Chief Scientific Office as a human geneticist. These credentials significantly augment our understanding of dentistry and, consequently, allow us to provide the standard setting services.

Although we can arbitrarily divide our practice into Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Esthetic Dentistry, Operative Dentistry, Prosthodontics, Periodontics, Endodontics, among others, the real essence is that we are the truly integrated oral health practitioner. We do not set up the boundary for our practice and are able to serve patients with all kinds of oral health and dental conditions. We practice with the ultimate INTEGRITY and excel at whatever we do. Therefore, our scope of practice is virtually unlimited.
