Taunton Oral Health Center is an integrated dental practice, located in the City of Taunton, Massachusetts.

The practice

The Taunton Oral Health Center was established in 2003, initially operating out of Taunton High School. Later, it relocated to a standalone facility in current downtown Taunton location at 80 - 86 Main Street.

The center’s history was deeply connected to community health initiatives in the region, particularly those aimed at expanding access to care for underrepresented and low- income populations.

Today, Taunton Oral Health Center is continuing to carry this legacy and expanding to serve the patients from all economic backgrounds with the same standard, the highest in the industry.
