Dental Implants

Why Choose Dental Implants? Natural teeth are made up of two important parts: the visible crown above the gumline and the root structure beneath it. While many traditional tooth replacement options restore appearance, they do not replace the tooth root that helps support your jawbone and maintain long-term oral health.

Dental implants are unique because they replace the entire tooth structure—from root to crown. The implant itself is placed into the jawbone, where it integrates with surrounding bone to create a stable foundation for your restoration. This added support helps preserve bone health while providing exceptional comfort, function, and durability.

With proper care, dental implants can be a long-lasting solution for missing teeth.

Dental Implant Restoration Options

Your implant restoration will depend on the number of teeth being replaced, your oral health needs, and your personal preferences. Our team will work closely with you to create a treatment plan designed to restore both appearance and function.

Single-Tooth Implants

When replacing one missing tooth, a custom-crafted dental crown can be attached to the implant for a natural-looking result without affecting surrounding healthy teeth.

Implant Bridges

For multiple missing teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge can restore your smile using strategically placed implants for support and stability.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Patients missing several or all teeth may benefit from implant-retained dentures. These restorations can often provide improved comfort, security, and function compared to traditional removable dentures and may be designed as fixed or removable depending on your goals.
