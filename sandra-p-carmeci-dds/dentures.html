Dentures

A denture is a removable prosthesis used to replace a full arch of missing teeth. It is usually made of reinforced acrylic resin and are removable.

A Complete denture is a removable prosthesis used to replace all or some teeth after the gums have healed following surgical extractions.

A partial denture is used to replace some missing teeth where some natural teeth remain. It is removable and typically relies on the remaining teeth for retention and stability of the prosthesis. Most partial dentures also incorporate a metal framework, but can be all acrylic for temporary purposes as well.
