Do you or your loved one snore loudly during the night? Do you wake up feeling exhausted even after a full night’s sleep?

Are you plagued by daytime fatigue, trouble concentrating, or frequent headaches?

You may be one of over 60 million Americans who experience obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) if so.

OSA is a common but serious sleep disorder that often goes undiagnosed.

Over 80 % of cases go unnoticed, even though many people struggle with related health issues like high blood pressure, heart disease, and diabetes without realizing sleep apnea may be the underlying culprit.
