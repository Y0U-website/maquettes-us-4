Sleep Apnea Testing

At-home sleep test kits make it a simple process to track your sleep patterns and get a diagnosis from a sleep professional.

Unlike sleep studies done in a lab, at-home tests allow you to sleep in your own bed while using a device to monitor data including your breathing patterns, oxygen levels, and heart rate. How it works Begin the process with a consultation with a professional to assess your symptoms and discuss your medical history. You will be helped to order an at-home sleep test kit. Use this as instructed while you sleep to collect the data your physician needs. Afterward, your physician will collect the data for analysis.

Why it’s effective

At-home sleep testing is convenient, non-invasive, and provides accurate insight into your natural sleeping habits, making it more precise in determining if you have sleep apnea. A professional can help you create a treatment plan that works for your needs and lifestyle once the results are analyzed.

CPAP is highly effective but might not be the best fit for every patient.

Some may prefer treatment options that do not cover their mouth or may not need more than a mouthguard to address their symptoms. However, CPAP is the answer to your problems for those who have tried these methods and found no resolution.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

After you’ve been diagnosed, our board-certified sleep dentists and physicians can help you treat your sleep apnea.

Sleep apnea can create major health problems long-term if you do not seek treatment.

These include heart disease, stroke, and diabetes.

You can get insight into a variety of treatment methods with the help of our sleep professionals, including CPAP and oral appliances.

What Is CPAP?

Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) is a common and effective treatment for sleep apnea.

This includes wearing a mask connected to a machine that delivers a constant airflow to keep your airway open during sleep.

A CPAP machine prevents breathing interruptions, which reduces symptoms, such as snoring and daytime fatigue.

Why Choose CPAP?

CPAP is a tried and true sleep apnea treatment method with a proven track record of improved breathing and reduced health risks.

CPAP machines are now more advanced than ever, with adjustable settings to improve your comfort while sleeping.

Your sleep physician will make sure the mask fits well so you can experience better sleep with ease.

What Are Oral Appliances?

Oral appliances are custom-fit devices you wear during sleep, similar to a sports mouthguard.

They gently move your jaw and tongue forward to keep your airway from collapsing at night.

Why Choose Oral Appliances?

Oral appliances are convenient, compact, portable, and silent.

They are excellent for those who may find CPAP uncomfortable or travel often and want a smaller device.

Our sleep dentists and physicians will work closely with you to design a device that fits perfectly and improves your sleep quality.

Which Treatment Is Right for You?

Your sleep dentist or physician will work with you to determine the best treatment method for your condition, lifestyle, and preferences.

Some patients—especially those with severe sleep apnea—benefit more from CPAP, while others will find oral appliances easy and effective.

Sometimes, a combination of treatments is recommended for optimal results.

The Results

Most patients notice significant improvements in sleep patterns, energy levels, and overall health within weeks of starting treatment.

Our goal is to help you achieve restorative sleep and better overall well-being no matter which treatment you choose.
