Our preventive care services at Shin & Kang Dental include professional cleanings, exams, digital X-rays, and fluoride treatments to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Regular hygiene appointments help prevent cavities, gum disease, and more complex dental issues. We educate our Barstow patients on daily brushing and flossing techniques and tailor preventive plans based on individual risk levels.

Symptoms: Bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, tartar buildup, tooth sensitivity, red or swollen gums.

Post-op Instructions: Maintain good brushing habits twice a day, floss daily, and limit sugary foods. Visit the dentist every six months for regular checkups.

FAQs

Why are regular cleanings important?

They remove tartar and plaque that cause decay and gum disease—things you can’t remove at home.

What happens during a hygiene visit?

We clean and polish teeth, check for cavities, examine your gums, and take X-rays if needed.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and restoring damaged, decayed, or missing teeth. At Shin & Kang Dental, our restorative solutions help you regain your natural bite and smile aesthetics while improving oral health and comfort.

Crowns

Dental crowns are custom-made caps placed over damaged or root-treated teeth. They restore shape, function, and appearance. Crowns are ideal for cracked teeth, deep cavities, or cosmetic improvements.

Symptoms: Weak, broken, or severely worn teeth; pain when chewing; visible cracks.

Post-op Instructions: Avoid hard/sticky foods, brush gently around the crown, and return for a bite check if needed.

Fillings

Fillings are used to restore teeth affected by minor decay or cracks. We offer tooth-colored composite resin fillings that blend naturally with your teeth.

Symptoms: Sensitivity to sweets or temperature, food getting stuck, visible holes.

Post-op Instructions: Avoid chewing on the side for a few hours, mild sensitivity is normal, follow-up if pain persists.

FAQs

How long do crowns last?

With good care, crowns can last 10-15 years or longer.

Are fillings permanent?

Composite fillings last 5-10 years and may need replacement over time.

Teeth Whitening Services

Brighten your smile with professional teeth whitening at Shin & Kang Dental. Our safe, in-office procedures deliver noticeable results in just one visit.

Symptoms: Yellowing from coffee, tea, wine, smoking, or aging.

Post-op Instructions: Avoid staining foods for 48 hours. Use sensitivity toothpaste if needed.

FAQs

How long does whitening last?

Results can last 6–12 months with proper maintenance and avoiding staining foods.

Does it hurt?

Some experience temporary sensitivity. We do our best to minimize discomfort.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth, designed to replicate the look, feel, and function of natural teeth. At Shin & Kang Dental, our Barstow patients trust us to restore their smiles with durable and aesthetic implant treatments.

Symptoms: Missing tooth or teeth, loose dentures, difficulty chewing, bone loss in jaw.

Post-op Instructions: Eat soft foods for the first 48 hours, avoid smoking, follow antibiotic and oral hygiene instructions strictly. Swelling and mild discomfort are normal in the initial days.

FAQs

Are dental implants painful?

The procedure is performed under anesthesia, and post-op discomfort is usually manageable with medication.

How long do implants last?

With good oral hygiene, implants can last 25 years or more.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal therapy is essential for saving teeth that are badly decayed or infected. At Shin & Kang Dental, our Barstow patients receive advanced, gentle root canal treatments that eliminate infection while preserving natural tooth structure.

Symptoms: Severe toothache, prolonged sensitivity to hot/cold, swelling around the gums, darkened tooth, pimple on the gums (abscess).

Post-op Instructions: Avoid chewing on the treated side until the crown is placed. Mild soreness is normal and manageable with over-the-counter medication. Maintain good oral hygiene and follow-up with your dentist.

FAQs

Is a root canal painful?

Do I need a crown after a root canal?

Yes, a crown is typically recommended to protect and strengthen the tooth post-treatment.

Dentures

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth and surrounding tissues. At Shin & Kang Dental in Barstow, we provide both full and partial dentures that are customized for comfort and aesthetics.

Symptoms: Difficulty eating or speaking, multiple missing teeth, sagging cheeks or facial muscles, gum discomfort from tooth loss.

Post-op Instructions: Practice speaking and eating slowly as you adjust, keep dentures clean with a soft brush, soak overnight in cleaning solution, and return for any adjustments.

FAQs

How long do dentures last?

With proper care, dentures typically last 5–8 years before needing replacement or relining.

Will dentures feel natural?

Modern dentures are lightweight and carefully fitted for comfort, though an adjustment period is normal.

Emergency Dental Treatment

Dental emergencies can happen suddenly and require immediate attention to alleviate pain, prevent further damage, and save teeth. At Shin & Kang Dental in Barstow, we provide prompt emergency care for issues like knocked-out teeth, severe toothaches, abscesses, or broken restorations.

Symptoms: Intense pain, bleeding, swelling, broken tooth, loose or knocked-out teeth, signs of infection (pus, bad taste).

Post-op Instructions: Follow prescribed medication directions, use cold compresses to reduce swelling, maintain a soft diet, and avoid disturbing the treated area. Seek follow-up care as directed.

FAQs

What qualifies as a dental emergency?

Severe pain, swelling, trauma, bleeding, and tooth loss are common emergencies. If you’re unsure, call us to assess your situation.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

We accept emergency walk-ins whenever possible, but calling ahead allows us to prepare for your care efficiently.
