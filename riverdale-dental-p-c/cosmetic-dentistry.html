Dental Bonding

Dental bonding is a quick, cost-effective method to correct various dental imperfections like chips, cracks, or gaps between teeth. The process involves the application of a composite resin material, matched to the natural color of your teeth. This resin is meticulously molded to cover imperfections and then hardened using a special curing light.

The final result is polished to blend seamlessly with the surrounding teeth, enhancing your smile’s appearance in just one visit. The procedure is aesthetic and strengthens the affected teeth, offering both beauty and functionality. Dental bonding is an excellent choice for those seeking immediate results with minimal tooth alteration.

ZOOM® Teeth Whitening

ZOOM Teeth Whitening at Riverdale Dental, P.C., is a revolutionary procedure offering visible results in about an hour. It’s an ideal solution for those seeking instant enhancement to their smile. The procedure involves applying a professional-strength whitening gel to the teeth, which is then activated by a special light.

This process breaks down stains and discolorations, revealing a smile that’s several shades lighter. ZOOM is perfect for those with a busy lifestyle or an upcoming special event where they want to look their best. Unlike over-the-counter products, ZOOM provides a safer, more controlled, and more effective whitening experience, tailored to your specific needs by our professional team.

Dental Veneers Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of your teeth to improve their appearance. They’re a great option for addressing a variety of cosmetic concerns, such as teeth that are discolored, chipped, misshapen, or slightly misaligned. Veneers provide a long-lasting cosmetic solution, creating a bright, uniform smile that’s resistant to staining.
