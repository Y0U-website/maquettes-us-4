Preventative Dentistry

Bronx, NY

The Importance Of Prevention Prevention is the key to long-term oral health, and at Riverdale Dental, P.C., we emphasize the importance of this partnership in care. Regular cleanings and exams are more than just routine procedures — they are opportunities for us to work together in maintaining and enhancing your oral health.

You play an active role in preventing dental issues by keeping up with these visits and practicing diligent oral hygiene at home — such as brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and using mouthwash. This proactive approach is beneficial for your oral health and overall well-being, as it helps catch and address potential problems before they develop into more serious conditions. Our team is here to guide and support you in adopting the best practices for your oral care routine.

Cleanings & Exams

Regular cleanings and exams are the bedrock of preventive dentistry at Riverdale Dental, P.C. Our dental hygienists are skilled in thoroughly cleaning your teeth and creating a relaxing and comfortable environment for your visit. By meticulously removing plaque and tartar, which are the primary culprits behind tooth decay and gum disease, we’re actively preventing these common dental issues.

During your exams, our dentists employ the latest in diagnostic technology, including digital x-rays and intraoral cameras. These tools enable us to get a detailed view of your oral health, spotting any concerns like cavities, gum disease, or early signs of other oral health issues. This level of thorough care and advanced technology ensures that we can provide prompt and effective treatment, keeping your smile healthy and bright.

Oral Cancer Screenings At Riverdale Dental, P.C., we prioritize your overall well-being, and this commitment extends to conducting oral cancer screenings as a part of our routine exams. These screenings are quick and non-invasive, but they hold immense importance. Oral cancer, if not detected early, can be life-threatening, and these screenings play a crucial role in catching any signs at an early stage.

We use advanced screening techniques to examine your mouth for any signs of abnormal tissues, lumps, or discolorations that might indicate the presence of cancer. Early detection vastly improves the prognosis and treatment success rate. We’re committed to caring for your smile and ensuring your overall health is monitored and protected.

Dental Sealants

The sealant is a clear or tooth-colored plastic material that is applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth. Once applied, it bonds into the grooves of the teeth, forming a protective shield. This barrier prevents cavities and makes it easier to keep these hard-to-clean areas free from plaque and food debris. Sealants are durable and can last for several years, but we’ll check them during your regular visits to ensure they’re intact and effective.
