Same Day Dental Crowns in Santa Cruz

Sometimes a tooth needs a little extra care to stay healthy and strong. A dental crown is a reliable way to protect a tooth that has been damaged, worn down, or weakened over time. At Harbor Dental, we create custom crowns that look natural and feel comfortable, helping you eat, smile, and speak with confidence.

What Is a Dental Crown? A crown is a custom made cover that fits over your natural tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance. Think of it as a protective shell that helps keep your tooth safe while blending in beautifully with your smile. Each crown is designed to match the color and contour of your surrounding teeth, so it looks and feels just like your own.

When Do You Need a Crown?

Crowns can be helpful in many different situations. They can protect a tooth that has extensive decay or a very large filling, restore one that is cracked, broken, or worn down, and strengthen a tooth after root canal therapy. Crowns are also often utilized to improve the appearance of teeth that are discolored or misshapen. In every case, the goal is to preserve your natural tooth while restoring comfort, function, and confidence.

Types of Crowns We Offer

Porcelain ceramic crowns and zirconia ceramic crowns are highly esthetic, tooth-colored crowns that mimic natural enamel. Porcelain ceramic crowns are more translucent and are often used for front teeth with enhanced esthetics. Zirconia ceramic crowns are extremely strong and often used for back teeth where chewing force is greatest. Gold crowns are very strong and long-lasting, often used for back teeth where chewing force is greatest. Our team will help you choose the best crown material based on your goals, budget, and where the crown will be placed in your mouth.

What to Expect During Treatment

The process of getting a crown usually takes two visits. At the first appointment, the tooth is carefully reshaped, and digital impressions are taken to ensure a perfect fit. A temporary crown is placed so your tooth stays comfortable and protected while your custom crown is being crafted. At the second visit, your permanent crown is securely bonded in place and adjusted for a natural fit.

Caring for Your Crown

With good daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups, crowns can last for many years. Just like your natural teeth, they benefit from consistent care to keep your whole smile healthy.
