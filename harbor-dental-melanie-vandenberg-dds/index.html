The practice

Born and raised in Santa Cruz, Dr. Vandenberg understands the unique spirit of our community and is proud to serve the same neighborhood where she grew up. Patients trust that they’re cared for by someone who is invested in the wellbeing of our community.

We take the time to listen, explain, and ensure you feel at ease—whether it’s your first visit in years or your regular checkup. Our friendly team creates an environment where patients of all ages feel welcome and relaxed.

Modern dental tools and techniques allow us to provide precise, efficient, and comfortable treatments that help protect and enhance your smile.

Dr. Vandenberg has earned multiple awards in general dentistry, endodontics, and operating dentistry, reflecting a commitment to high standards and exceptional results.

Our in-house membership plan offers preventive care and discounted treatments, making it easier for patients without dental insurance to maintain a healthy smile.

We recommend only what’s needed, clearly explain your options, and help you make informed decisions about your dental health. You can expect honesty, clarity, and respect at every step of your care.

We provide prompt dental evaluations for a variety of needs, including preoperative clearance before surgery, kindergarten and school enrollment, and medical clearance prior to starting treatments such as bisphosphonate therapy—helping ensure patients are healthy, safe, and ready for their next step in care.
