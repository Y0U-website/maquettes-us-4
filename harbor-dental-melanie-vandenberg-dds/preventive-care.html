What is a dental implant?

After your implant surgery, our Harbor Dental team will provide you with a variety of care instructions and tips to follow until the gums heal from surgery. Some of these tips include:

What To Expect Being well-prepared for your appointment will ensure that the doctor has all of the needed information to provide the best possible care for you. It also will help relieve any unnecessary anxiety you may be feeling. Educate yourself on your symptoms by reviewing the content on this Web site. Also, take some time to review our staff page and familiarize yourself with the doctors. We look forward to your first visit.

  • Consuming only soft foods (preferably, liquids) for the first two days after surgery
  • Not rinsing the mouth out for the first 24 hours
  • Taking painkillers to alleviate discomfort
  • Keeping the head elevated and resting as much as possible for at least the first 12 hours after your surgery
