The Dentists

Dr. Allie is a graduate of The Medical College of Virginia and has been in her practice for over 20 years.

Dr. Allie is a Senior Master Fastbraces® provider and has dedicated her professional career to providing her patients with comfortable and personalized dental care. She takes pride in her dentistry and spends quality time with each of her patients to understand their individual dental needs. It does not matter who she is treating, she believes that every appointment is a chance to get to know her patients, educate them about their oral health, and help them take care of their teeth.

Dr. Allie is passionate about the art of orthodontics, specializing in Fastbraces® technology to efficiently grant patients a perfectly straight smile.

She enjoys addressing the needs of the whole family and she helps every patient achieve and maintain their optimal dental health with current diagnostic and treatment options. ” I believe that preventative care and education is the key to dental health and a healthy beautiful smile.”

Dr. Allie Tran, DDS

Dr. Cuong Tran is a highly skilled dentist who offers a comprehensive array of dental services, encompassing everything from implant, and root canal therapy, to both simple and surgical extractions, as well as bone grafts and sinus augmentations.

A graduate of Indiana Dental School, Dr. Cuong has honed his skills over 25 years, perfecting his craft. With an impressive track record as an associate dentist at esteemed practices, he is a seasoned dental expert.

His commitment goes beyond clinical expertise; Dr. Cuong places a strong emphasis on patient education, and understanding the significance of maintaining healthy oral habits. He recognizes that every patient is unique and tailors his consultations and treatment plans to address individual needs and concerns with the utmost sensitivity and care.

Dr. Cuong Tran, DDS

Before attending dental school, Dr. Nguyen earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Medical Technology from the University of Maryland at Baltimore. While there, she received the Award of Excellence in Clinical Dentistry. In 2001, Dr. Nguyen graduated from Baltimore College of Dental Surgery at the University of Maryland, with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Always determined to increase her knowledge even further, Dr. Nguyen attends dental lectures, meetings, and education courses including a nine-month dental implant training program with Dr. Arun Garg at the Implant Seminars Institute.

After graduating from dental school, Dr. Nguyen relocated to Virginia and started her dental profession there. Dr. Nguyen is fluent in both English and Vietnamese, she has two adorable Pekingese dogs named Coco and Tango, and a English Cream Retriever named Cosmo.

She loves cooking and baking as well as traveling around the globe, exploring places.
