Your Healthcare Credit Card

CareCredit is a credit card designed exclusively for healthcare expenses, including dental procedures. With no upfront costs, no annual fees, and no pre-payment penalties, CareCredit provides a flexible way to manage your dental care. It allows you to spread payments over time, keeping your regular credit cards and cash available for other important needs.

CareCredit offers easy-to-manage monthly payment plans for procedures that are often not covered by insurance, such as cosmetic and restorative treatments. Plus, you can use your CareCredit card again for follow-up treatments or for any family member at any participating CareCredit provider.

Welcome to Brookshire Dentistry

Making an Appointment

Booking your first visit with Brookshire Dentistry is simple! Call us at (817) 577-9200, and one of our friendly team members will gladly assist you in scheduling your initial examination and dental treatments. If you need to cancel or reschedule, we kindly ask that you notify us at least 24 hours in advance so we can accommodate other patients.

What to Expect

When you arrive, you’ll be welcomed into our warm and inviting lobby by our caring staff. We will provide you with new patient paperwork, including a health questionnaire and forms for your insurance and contact details. You’ll also receive important information about our office, including how we handle patient privacy and care.

Initial Examination

Your first visit will last approximately one hour. Our skilled team will take the time to get to know you, discuss your oral health habits, and listen to any concerns you may have. At Brookshire Dentistry, we believe in empowering our patients through education, so we’ll provide personalized tips on maintaining your oral health. A thorough cleaning will be performed by our hygienist, followed by an examination by Dr. Salomeh, who will recommend any necessary treatments based on your needs.

Pain Concerns

We are committed to ensuring that your visit is as comfortable as possible. If you require treatment, Dr. Salomeh will discuss your pain tolerance and develop a customized pain management plan. Our goal is to minimize discomfort and provide effective care so you can return to your normal routine quickly.

Dental Insurance

We work with a variety of insurance providers to ensure our patients receive the care they need. To find out if we accept your insurance, give us a call at (817) 577-9200. Our goal is to make quality dental care accessible, and if your insurance doesn’t cover all treatments, we’ll work with you to find a payment solution that suits your needs.

At Brookshire Dentistry, we believe that financial concerns shouldn’t stand in the way of great dental care. We offer a range of payment options to meet our patients’ needs, including cash, checks, credit cards, and financing plans. Whether you have insurance or not, we’re here to help you get the treatment you deserve at a price you can afford.

Call today to discuss your payment options and see how Brookshire Dentistry can help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile!

Financial Policy

Brookshire Dentistry provides high-quality dental care to families throughout the Hurst, TX, area. We understand that each dental patient has different financial needs. We will work with you to find a solution that enables you to get the dental care you need, with or without insurance.

Call our office to ask about our different payment options, which may include cash, check, financing, and credit cards. We provide budget-friendly dental care, so call today and learn how Brookshire Dentistry is the solution to all your oral health needs.
