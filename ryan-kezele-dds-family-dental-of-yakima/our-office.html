Welcome to Our Office

Meet the Team Behind Your Smile

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted by friendly, experienced faces who genuinely care about your comfort and health. We’re more than a dental office — we’re your Yakima dental family!

  • Caring, experienced dental professionals
  • Bilingual team (Se habla español)
  • Over 50 combined years of experience
  • Voted Best of Valley — Yakima Business Times

Welcoming Reception Area

From the moment you arrive, you’ll feel at home. Our warm, comfortable reception area is designed to help you relax before your appointment. Our friendly front desk team will have you checked in quickly and easily!

  • Comfortable, relaxing waiting area
  • Friendly check-in process
  • Knowledgeable, welcoming front desk staff
  • New patient forms available online to save time

Clean, Safe & Comfortable

Your safety is our top priority. We follow strict sterilization protocols and maintain the highest standards of cleanliness and infection control throughout our entire office.

Modern Treatment Rooms

Our treatment rooms are equipped with the latest dental technology to provide you with the highest quality care in a comfortable, stress-free setting. Every detail is designed with your comfort in mind.

  • State-of-the-art dental equipment
  • Comfortable, adjustable dental chairs
  • Digital X-rays (90 % less radiation)
  • Calm, relaxing atmosphere

Advanced Technology

We invest in the latest dental technology to make your visits more comfortable, efficient, and effective. Our advanced equipment helps us provide precise diagnoses and exceptional treatment results every time.

  • Intraoral cameras for detailed imaging
  • Digital X-rays for accurate diagnosis
  • Advanced sterilization systems
  • Modern sedation dentistry equipment

Private Consultation Room

Have questions about your treatment plan, financing options, or dental concerns? Our private consultation room provides a comfortable, quiet space where Dr. Kezele and our team can sit down with you and discuss your dental health without distraction.

  • Private, comfortable consultation space
  • Review X-rays and treatment plans
  • Discuss financing and insurance options
  • Ask questions in a relaxed setting
