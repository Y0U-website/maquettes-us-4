Meet Dr. Ryan Kezele

Welcome to Yakima Healthy Smiles!

Dr. Ryan Kezele is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care to families throughout the Yakima community. With a passion for helping patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles, Dr. Kezele combines advanced training with a gentle, personalized approach to dentistry.

Whether you need a routine cleaning, cosmetic dentistry, or restorative treatment, Dr. Kezele and his team are here to make your experience comfortable and stress-free.

🎓 Education & Professional Background

Dr. Kezele graduated at the top of his class from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2002, earning awards in multiple disciplines. His academic excellence was recognized with national honors at graduation — but his learning didn’t stop there.

Dr. Kezele consistently pursues advanced training to bring the latest techniques and technology to his Yakima patients. His areas of expertise include:

  • Oral Surgery & Surgical Extractions
  • Root Canal Treatment
  • Cosmetic Dentistry
  • Sedation Dentistry
  • Advanced Equipment & Modern Techniques

⚕️ My Approach to Dental Care

Dr. Kezele focuses on providing gentle dentistry for patients of all ages — from young children to seniors. He believes that every patient deserves compassionate, high-quality care in a comfortable, stress-free environment.

By combining advanced equipment with proven techniques, Dr. Kezele delivers precise, effective treatment with minimal discomfort. His patient-centered approach means you’re always informed, comfortable, and in control of your care.

  • Gentle, personalized care for every patient
  • Advanced technology for precise diagnosis & treatment
  • Expertise in sedation for anxious patients
  • Clear communication and informed decision-making
  • Committed to continuing education & latest techniques

🏘️ Community Involvement

Dr. Kezele has a deep commitment to the Yakima Valley community. He takes special pride in serving children, the elderly, and our veterans — groups that are close to his heart.

Beyond his clinical work, Dr. Kezele regularly donates time and prizes to the Yakima Valley and is actively involved in community events and charitable activities.

🎖️ Special Discounts Available!

Dr. Kezele proudly offers discounts for Senior Citizens, Veterans, and Cash Pay patients . Ask our team for details when scheduling your appointment!

🌊 Beyond the Dental Office

When he’s not caring for Yakima smiles, Dr. Kezele enjoys staying active and making the most of the beautiful Pacific Northwest. His hobbies include:

  • Water sports & outdoor recreation
  • Golf
  • Travel & exploring new places
  • Donating time and prizes to the Yakima Valley community
