Meet Our Team

We’re more than just a dental office — we’re a family! Get to know the caring, experienced team behind every smile at Ryan Kezele D.D.S. | Yakima Healthy Smiles.

Dr. Ryan Kezele, D.D.S.

"Focused on providing gentle dentistry for patients of all ages. Proud to offer quality dental care to the Yakima Valley community!"

Outside the office: Water sports, golf, travel, and giving back to the Yakima Valley through donations and community involvement.

Tara

Isabel

Outside the office: Loves traveling, exploring new cultures, and trying new cuisines wherever she goes!

Emily
