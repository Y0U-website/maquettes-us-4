What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts expertly placed in the same bone that once supported your natural teeth. Modern porcelains produce a life-like result that may leave you completely unaware that they aren’t your natural teeth. You’ll often enjoy the strength and function of nature with this remarkable tooth replacement option in Yakima.

An excellent choice for one or more missing teeth, implants can also support dentures or bridges . The stability and sense of confidence you develop with implants will leave you pleasantly surprised. Whether replacing a single tooth or restoring your entire mouth, there’s an implant solution for you at Yakima Healthy Smiles.

The Dental Implant Advantage

Unlike traditional bridges or dentures, dental implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth for support. This means we don’t have to alter your healthy teeth to replace missing ones. Implants also stimulate your jawbone just like natural tooth roots, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs after tooth loss.

At our Yakima dental office, patients consistently report that dental implants feel more natural and comfortable than any other tooth replacement option. Most people can’t tell the difference between their implant and their natural teeth - and neither can anyone else.

The Dental Implant Process

Careful planning followed by implant placement sets a solid foundation for an array of restorative options. After the healing period of 3-4 months, crowns or bridges will be custom-made and attached to the implants with small screws or dental cement.

Numerous components help Dr. Kezele deliver the best result when completing the final stage. But it’s the customization of your case from initial planning to final placement that makes all the difference. This allows a full range of services to be considered at our Yakima office, from the replacement of one tooth to restoration of the entire mouth.

Single Tooth Replacement

For one missing tooth, a single implant is placed and topped with a custom porcelain crown. This is the most conservative tooth replacement option, requiring no alteration of adjacent teeth. The result looks, feels, and functions exactly like your natural tooth.

Multiple Teeth Replacement

When several teeth are missing, an implant-supported bridge can replace them without affecting healthy neighboring teeth. Strategically placed implants support a custom bridge that restores full chewing function and appearance.

Full Arch Replacement

For patients missing all teeth in one or both arches, implant-supported dentures provide unprecedented stability and confidence. Unlike traditional dentures that slip and slide, implant dentures snap securely into place, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile without worry.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants in Yakima?

Most adults with missing teeth are excellent candidates for dental implants. You need adequate jawbone density to support the implant, healthy gums free of gum disease , and good overall health for proper healing.

During your consultation at Yakima Healthy Smiles, Dr. Kezele will perform a comprehensive examination including 3D imaging to assess your bone structure. Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, you may still qualify for implants with bone grafting procedures. We’ll discuss all options during your personalized consultation.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Maintaining your dental implants is simple - care for them just like your natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss daily, and visit Yakima Healthy Smiles for regular professional cleanings and checkups every six months.

With proper home care and regular professional maintenance, your dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself typically remains strong and functional for life.

Dental Implant FAQs - Yakima Healthy Smiles

How much do dental implants cost in Yakima?

Dental implant costs vary based on individual needs, number of implants, and complexity. A single tooth implant typically ranges from $3,000-$6,000. At Yakima Healthy Smiles, we provide detailed cost estimates after your consultation and offer financing options to make implants affordable.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium implant post fuses permanently with your jawbone. The crown on top may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself typically lasts for life. Dr. Kezele provides ongoing care for Yakima patients to ensure implant longevity.

Are dental implants painful?

Most Yakima patients report minimal discomfort during implant placement. We use local anesthesia and sedation options to ensure comfort. After surgery, mild soreness is normal and easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients return to normal activities within 24-48 hours.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. You need adequate jawbone density, healthy gums, and good overall health. At Yakima Healthy Smiles, Dr. Kezele evaluates each patient with 3D imaging and comprehensive exams to determine candidacy. Even patients with bone loss may qualify with bone grafting.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The complete dental implant process typically takes 3-6 months in Yakima. This includes implant placement, 3-4 months healing time for osseointegration, and final crown placement. Some cases qualify for same-day temporary teeth. Dr. Kezele provides a detailed timeline during your consultation.

Do dental implants look natural?

Yes! Modern dental implants look and feel completely natural. At Yakima Healthy Smiles, we use high-quality porcelain crowns custom-matched to your natural teeth. Most people won’t know you have an implant unless you tell them.
