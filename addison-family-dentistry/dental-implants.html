Understanding Implants

A dental implant is like a sturdy foundation that replaces the root of a missing tooth with a small, biocompatible titanium post placed in your jaw. Once it heals and settles in, we attach a beautiful, custom-made crown (or bridge or denture ) that functions and feels like a real tooth.

Eating your favorite foods, an unselfconscious smile, and teeth that don’t shift or slip are all possible again!

Dr. De Vincenzo will ensure that your crown is the perfect color, shape, and size to give you a seamless, natural-looking smile that you can be proud of.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Stability: With implants, you have a stable, durable tooth replacement that will stay in place
  • Functionality: Implants feel and function like your natural teeth
  • Protection: Implants don’t need you to modify your existing teeth, so you can conserve the natural enamel of your neighboring teeth
  • Improved Oral Health: Implants are easy to care for with regular brushing, flossing, and checkups. They also prevent other issues like bone loss or gum recession
  • A Seamless, Natural Esthetic: We design implants that match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth to make sure your smile looks and feels like you
  • Durability: If you take proper care of your implants, your new smile can last you a lifetime

Am I A Candidate For Implants?

  • Generally Healthy Implant placement is a surgical procedure, so you’ll need to be in good overall health to heal properly.
  • Enough Bone to Support It Your jaw needs to have enough bone to hold the implant securely. If not, bone grafting may be needed first.
  • Healthy Gums Gum disease? We’ll need to treat it before placing your implant.
  • Committed to Good Oral Hygiene Brushing, flossing, and regular checkups help your implant last for years to come.
  • No Smoking Smoking can interfere with healing and reduce your implant’s success rate.

Our Comprehensive Implants Procedure

1. The Initial Consultation

At your first visit, Dr. De Vincenzo will examine your smile and use advanced 3D imaging to see if implants are a good fit. If there are issues like bone loss or gum concerns, he’ll walk you through next steps before moving forward.

2. The Procedure

Dr. De Vincenzo will place the implant right here in our Addison office. He’s specially trained in implant surgery, so you’ll be in skilled hands.

We’ll numb the area completely, and offer sedation if you’re feeling nervous. The procedure involves a small incision, placing the implant post in your jaw, and closing the area to heal. Simple, precise, and done with care.

3. The Healing Period

After surgery, your implant will slowly fuse with your jawbone — a process called osseointegration. While you heal, we’ll provide a temporary tooth so you can eat, speak, and smile comfortably.

Once healing is complete (usually 2–4 months), we’ll place a small connector called an abutment. This is what your custom crown will attach to.

4. The Final Placement

Once your custom crown is ready, we’ll bring you in for one last visit to fit and polish it. From there, it’s all yours — strong, natural-looking, and ready for everyday life.
