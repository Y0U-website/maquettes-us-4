What to Expect When Getting an Implant-Supported Crown

Getting implant-supported crowns involves a straightforward, step-by-step process.

Implant-supported crowns are a reliable and effective solution for replacing missing teeth. They restore the function of your bite and help you smile with confidence again. The proper care offers a long-lasting way to support your dental health and overall well-being. Ready to get your smile back? Prime Dentistry is here to help you make it happen!   • Initial Visit: Your dentist will check your teeth, discuss your goals and needs, and create a treatment plan you are comfortable with. They ll also take pictures or scans to determine whether your jawbone is strong enough for an implant.
  • Placing the Implant: The dentist will administer sedation and carefully insert the implant (a small metal post) into your jawbone.
  • Healing Time: Over the next few months, your bone will grow around the implant, strengthening and securing it.
  • Adding the Abutment: Once the implant is solid, the dentist will add a small connector piece called an abutment.
  • The Crown: Finally, they ll attach a custom-made crown on top of the implant that looks and works just like your natural teeth.
  • Care and Follow-Up: Your dentist will explain how to take care of your implant and check your progress during follow-up visits.
