Meet the Founders

Dr. Eric Tran

Dr. Tran grew up in Doylestown, Pennsylvania, and was drawn to dentistry because it sits at the intersection of his lifelong interests: science, medicine, and hands-on artistry. As a child, he loved making things and working with his hands. As he got older, he became fascinated by health and the way small, precise decisions can change how someone feels day to day.

He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry from Temple University, then continued at Temple to earn his Doctorate of Dental Medicine. In practice, Dr. Tran has built his approach around helping patients feel more comfortable, more informed, and more in control of their oral health.

His favorite procedures are the ones that blend function and aesthetics, including crowns , bridges, veneers , and clear aligner therapy . These treatments require careful planning and an eye for detail, but they also have the power to improve how patients chew, smile, and carry themselves.

What sets Dr. Tran apart is his calm, conversational approach. He takes time to explain what he sees, talk through your options, and help you understand the “why” behind his recommendations. For patients who have carried dental anxiety for years, that clarity can make the entire experience feel different.

Outside the office, Dr. Tran enjoys cooking, video games, and spending time with friends, family, and Sammi, the puppy he shares with Lisa.

Lisa Kwon, RDH

Lisa Kwon grew up in York, Pennsylvania, and first found her way into dentistry through the front desk of a dental office. What started as an introduction to the field quickly became something more meaningful. She loved connecting with patients, helping them feel comfortable, and seeing how the right care could improve someone’s confidence and health.

She earned her Bachelor’s degree in Kinesiology from Temple University, where she and Eric first met, then completed her dental hygiene training at Montgomery County Community College. Her background gives her a unique view of the patient experience because she understands both the clinical side of care and the emotions patients bring with them to the office.

As Co-Founder and Office Manager, Lisa helps shape the rhythm and feel of Vault Dental. From digital forms and scheduling to the way patients are welcomed and guided through their visits, she is focused on creating an experience that feels organized, calm, and personal.

Lisa believes a comfortable visit begins long before a patient sits in the chair. It starts with clear communication, a welcoming front desk, and a team that treats each person like a neighbor rather than a name on the schedule.

Outside the office, Lisa enjoys spending time with friends, family, and Sammi, the puppy she and Eric adore.

What to expect

Your first Vault Visit

Your first visit at Vault Dental is designed to help you feel comfortable from the start. Digital forms allow you to complete your paperwork ahead of time, so your time in the office can be focused on your care. Once you arrive, we will guide you through the process, answer your questions, and make sure you know what to expect before anything begins.

Patient Experience Your visit may include a comprehensive exam, necessary X-rays, a professional cleaning, and a conversation about your goals or concerns. Dr. Tran will walk you through his findings in plain language and explain the “why” behind any recommendations, so you can make decisions with confidence.

What to Bring To make your first visit smooth, please bring:

Your dental insurance card, if applicable

Any recent dental records or X-rays, if available

Modern tools and thoughtful comforts

Vault Dental was designed from the ground up to feel calm, modern, and comfortable. Our technology helps us plan treatment with greater accuracy, while our office comforts help make your time in the chair feel less stressful.

TRIOS intraoral scanner

Our TRIOS scanner replaces traditional impression trays with a quick, comfortable digital scan. This allows us to create a precise 3D model of your teeth for treatments like crowns, bridges, and clear aligners without the mess or discomfort of goopy impressions.

CBCT 3D imaging

Cone Beam CT imaging gives Dr. Tran a detailed three-dimensional view of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This can be especially helpful for planning implant-related treatment , evaluating wisdom teeth, and understanding concerns that may not be visible on a standard X-ray.

A-dec dental chairs

Our A-dec dental chairs are designed for ergonomic support and comfort, helping you feel more relaxed during both routine and longer visits.

Ceiling-mounted TVs with Netflix, Hulu, and Disney+

Each treatment room includes a ceiling-mounted TV with access to streaming options, so you can focus on something familiar while we focus on your care.

Nespresso Beverage Station

A fresh Nespresso or cold drink may seem like a small detail, but small details can change how an appointment feels. Our beverage station is one more way we make your visit feel more welcoming, comfortable, and cared for from the moment you arrive.

Digital paperless forms

All new patient paperwork can be completed digitally before your appointment. That means less time with a clipboard and more time focused on your care.

Paws, Purpose & Community

Helping Pups Find Home

Vault Dental is proud to partner with Home at Last Dog Rescue, a nonprofit based in Lansdale, PA. As passionate dog parents ourselves, this cause is close to our hearts. For every five new patients we welcome, we donate to help support vaccinations and medical treatment for a rescued dog.

It is one small way we can care for our community beyond the walls of our practice. When you choose Vault Dental, you are also helping a local rescue dog get closer to the healthy, safe future they deserve.
