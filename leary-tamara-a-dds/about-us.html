Our Practice Philosophy

Drs. Leary, Burroughs & Woods Family Dentistry was organized in 1995. Our guiding philosophy from the beginning was to build a successful practice based on the highest standards of integrity, professionalism, technical competence, and patient care.

We’re happy to note that our practice is flourishing, as so many of our patients refer their friends and family to us. We have a commitment to providing comprehensive dentistry to our patients. We regularly attend local and national continuing education courses to constantly update our knowledge and skills. It is our goal to customize treatment plans to each individual’s needs and to be able to provide the majority of the needed services here in our office.

Dr. Tamara A Leary

I was born and raised in Rockville, Maryland. My Bachelor of Science was in Zoology from the University of Maryland. I graduated in 1990 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery also from University of Maryland. After graduation, I volunteered for the U.S. Army and attended an extensive one year Dental Residency Program in Ft. Jackson, South Carolina. Over the next four years, I served as an army dentist in Wurzburg, Germany and Ft. Meade, Maryland, gaining additional skills and experience.

I have had the pleasure of being in private practice in Gambrills since 1995. I opened this office in October of 2000. We have enjoyed such growth and support from the community that in 20O3 Dr. Burroughs joined our group and most recently Dr. Woods in 2OO7. I truly enjoy my profession and continue to enhance my skills in a variety of cosmetic and surgical areas. In the last few years I have been excited to add implant surgery to my practice.

Volunteering in the community has always been a priority of mine. I have attended five Maryland Mission of Mercy events around the state and regularly treat patients from the Donated Dental programs, The Department of Aging and The House of Hope Homeless Shelter. I have two wonderful grown children and live locally. I enjoy my family, friends, traveling and many outdoor activities.

Dr. Michelle Woods

I grew up in Albany, New York. In 1984, I graduated with an Associates degree in Dental Hygiene then later furthered my education and received a Bachelor of Science degree in Microbiology.

I worked as a hygienist for seven years before going back to school to become a dentist. In 1995, I graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry earning a Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree. Right out of dental school, I completed a one year residency in general dentistry at Eastman Dental Center (Rochester, N.Y.).

I worked as a dentist in the Maryland area for over ten years. In 2011, I joined Drs. Leary and Burroughs and became the third partner in our practice.

I reside in Laurel with my husband Larry and son Dylan. Besides my passion for dentistry, I enjoy traveling, shopping, and spending time with my family and friends.
