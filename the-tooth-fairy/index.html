The practice

We make sure your time with us is as comfortable and stress-free as possible. That means offering various treatment approaches to help cater to each patient. It also means approaching from the standpoint of teaching rather than lecturing. We know some people avoid going to the dentist because of fear of judgement, but we believe that information and education are key to successful long term oral…

We value new technological advances in the field of dentistry, and educating ourselves is a priority so that we can serve your families. Because our practice has such a focus on children, we use the Solea carbon dioxide laser to provide the absolute best treatment!
