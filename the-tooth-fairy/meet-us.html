About Our Office

Dr. Annette is a general dentist who has committed her career to providing kids the absolute best experience possible during dental appointments. We think you will see our passion after your very first appointment!

As a practice, we feel we have absolutely found our niche in taking the best care of our patients! We know that kids don’t always love going to the dentist, but we find that a lot of kids are excited to come back and see us! And that is our ultimate goal- to help kids grow up to be adults with no fear of the dentist.

We genuinely love seeing our patients and their families! We try to put our own touch on every interaction so that the experience is positive for everyone involved. We love hearing that patients can’t wait to come back and see us!
