Meet the doctor

Dr. Shai Patel, Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) Dr. Shai Patel is known for his gentle, conservative approach, helping even the most hesitant patients feel at ease. With over a decade of experience and advanced training from Howard University, Dr. Patel brings an unmatched level of care to every patient he treats. He takes pride in staying updated on the latest dental innovations, allowing him to deliver top-notch services in both general and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Patel’s compassionate nature and friendly demeanor quickly put patients at ease, making dental visits a positive experience. His dedication to patient care extends beyond technical expertise – he’s committed to building long-lasting relationships with his patients, offering guidance and support throughout their dental journeys.

Life Outside the Office: Outside of Precision Smiles, Dr. Patel enjoys spending quality time with his wife, Sheree, and their two children, Alaina and Kellan. He’s an outdoor enthusiast who loves traveling, camping, and water activities. Dr. Patel is also an avid Chicago Bears fan, so feel free to chat with him about his favorite team!
