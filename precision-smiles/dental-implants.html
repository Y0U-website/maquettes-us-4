Types of Dental Implants Available at Precision Smiles

At Precision Smiles Frisco , we offer a variety of dental implant options to accommodate different dental conditions, preferences, and health requirements. Dr. Patel will guide you in selecting the best option based on your unique needs and goals.

  • Single Tooth Implants Single-tooth implants are ideal for patients missing one tooth. This type of implant replaces both the root and crown of the missing tooth, blending seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges For patients missing multiple adjacent teeth, implant-supported bridges offer a stable, long-term solution. Instead of relying on neighboring teeth, these bridges are anchored by implants, providing superior stability and preserving the health of surrounding teeth.
  • Full Arch Implants (All-on-4®) All-on-4® implants are designed for patients who need a full set of upper or lower teeth. With this technique, a complete arch of teeth is supported by just four implants, offering a less invasive solution with a secure and natural-looking result.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures Implant-supported dentures offer a more secure, comfortable alternative to traditional dentures. They snap onto implants, providing stability while allowing easy removal for cleaning.

The Process of Getting Dental Implants at Precision Smiles

The dental implant process at Precision Smiles Frisco is carefully designed to ensure comfort, precision, and successful outcomes. Here’s what you can expect:

  • Initial Consultation and Evaluation During your first visit, Dr. Patel will perform a thorough examination, including digital imaging and X-rays, to assess your jawbone health and determine if you’re a candidate for dental implants. We’ll also discuss alternative options, such as Dental Bridges if they better suit your needs.
  • Treatment Planning and 3D Imaging We use advanced 3D imaging and digital planning technology to create a precise treatment plan. This technology helps us map the optimal placement for each implant, ensuring a secure fit and natural look.
  • Implant Placement Once your plan is set, Dr. Patel will surgically place the titanium implant posts in the jawbone. The area is numbed for comfort, and we offer sedation options for a relaxed experience. Over the following months, the implants will fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Abutment Placement and Restoration After the implants have fully integrated, a small connector called an abutment is placed on each implant to hold your restoration securely. For single implants, a custom Dental Crown is attached, while implant-supported bridges or dentures may be added as needed.
  • Final Adjustments and Aftercare Dr. Patel will ensure that your new teeth fit comfortably and look natural, making any necessary adjustments. Our team will provide guidance on caring for your implants, including regular check-ups and professional cleanings at Precision Smiles to maintain optimal health and longevity.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last? ‍

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular check-ups, good oral hygiene, and professional cleanings will help maintain the longevity of your implants.

Are dental implants painful to get? ‍

The implant procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure comfort, and we offer sedation options to make the experience as relaxed as possible. Most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery.

Can I get implants if I’ve been missing teeth for a long time? ‍

Yes, many patients can still receive implants even after prolonged tooth loss. Dr. Patel will assess bone density to determine if a bone graft is needed for additional support.

Are dental implants covered by insurance? ‍

Some dental insurance plans cover a portion of the cost of implants. Our team at Precision Smiles will help you understand your coverage and discuss financing options if needed.

How do I care for my dental implants? ‍

Implants require similar care to natural teeth. Brush and floss daily, and visit Precision Smiles for regular check-ups and cleanings to maintain implant health.
