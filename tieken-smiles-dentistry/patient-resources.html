Exceptional Care, Transparent Pricing

At Tieken Smiles Dentistry, we prioritize quality, comfort, and long-term results over insurance restrictions. By operating as a fee-for-service practice, we have the freedom to provide uncompromising care without limitations on materials, techniques, or treatment options.

While we are a fee-for-service practice, we are happy to help you maximize your out-of-network insurance benefits. Our team will file claims on your behalf and provide detailed estimates to help you plan for your treatment. If you have questions about your coverage, we’re here to help!
