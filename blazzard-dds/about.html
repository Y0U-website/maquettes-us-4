About

Dr. Alex Blazzard, DDS

Dr. Alex Blazzard has been helping others and enjoying life in Mesquite, Nevada since 2005. Having been born and raised in St. George, Utah, Dr. Blazzard feels right at home in the heat. Although he is well respected and immensely loves his work, nothing is closer to him than his amazing wife, Heather and their family of 5 wonderfully chaotic kids.

When time permits, Dr. Blazzard enjoys golf, tennis, classic cars and volunteering at the Elementary Schools. Dr. Blazzard looks forward to many more years of serving, living and experiencing the great community of Mesquite, Nevada. Stop by and meet him today.
