Personalized Orthodontic Treatments for Every Smile

At Lake Hills Family Dentistry, we believe everyone deserves a smile they can feel proud of. Orthodontic treatment goes beyond straightening teeth, it improves oral health, enhances facial balance, and boosts self-confidence. Whether you’re dealing with bite issues, crooked or crowded teeth, or just want a more aligned smile, our team is here to guide you with expert care and modern options tailored to your lifestyle.

We offer a range of orthodontic solutions for both teens and adults. From nearly invisible aligners to traditional braces, we’ll help you choose the right path based on your goals, comfort, and dental needs. Your journey to a healthier, straighter smile starts right here.

Discover a Straighter Smile That Lasts

Explore custom orthodontic solutions at Lake Hills Family Dentistry, from discreet aligners to long-term retainers, we’re here to help you achieve and maintain a confident, healthy smile with personalized care.

ClearCorrect

ClearCorrect is a clear aligner system that straightens teeth using custom-made, removable trays. It’s a great choice for patients seeking a less noticeable, more flexible way to correct misalignment or spacing. With ClearCorrect, you’ll wear a series of aligners that gradually shift your teeth into place. It’s discreet, effective, and built for comfort.

Retainers

Retainers are custom-made appliances that help keep your teeth in their new positions after orthodontic treatment. They’re essential for maintaining your results and preventing your smile from shifting back over time. At Lake Hills Family Dentistry, we offer both fixed and removable retainers to suit your lifestyle.

Keep Your Smile Strong and Cavity-Free

Dental sealants are a smart investment in your child’s long-term oral health and your peace of mind. When combined with daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental visits, sealants add an extra layer of protection against decay in hard-to-clean areas.
