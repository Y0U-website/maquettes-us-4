When a tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it shrinks. Not only does this affect your smile, it also changes the shape of your face causing you to look prematurely aged. Dental implants prevent this premature aging.

With dental implants, healthy teeth are not compromised. Like natural teeth, dental implants stop bone loss.
