Education and Dental Training

Dr. Tony Hong grew up in Vancouver, Canada and pursued his undergraduate education at Vanderbilt University in Nashville, where he earned his bachelor’s degree.

After completing his undergraduate studies, Dr. Hong moved to Boston to attend BostonUniversity, where he earned both his Master’s degree in Oral Health Sciences and his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD).

His academic training provided a strong foundation in comprehensive dental care, allowing him to diagnose and treat a wide range of dental conditions while focusing on long-term oral health.

Commitment to Continuing Education

Dentistry continues to evolve with new technology, materials, and treatment techniques. Dr. Hong regularly participates in continuing education courses to stay current with the latest advancements in dental care.

This commitment to ongoing learning allows him to incorporate modern dental techniques and technologies into patient treatment. By staying up to date with developments in dentistry, Dr. Hong strives to provide efficient care while improving patient comfort and treatment outcomes.

Patients receiving services such as Dental Implants , Cosmetic & Smile Enhancement , and Restorative Dentistry benefit from this modern approach to treatment planning.

A Patient-Centered Approach to Dentistry

His approach to patient care focuses on:

Clear communication and patient education

Preventive care and long-term oral health

By building trust with patients and providing clear explanations of treatment options, Dr. Hong helps patients feel confident and informed about their dental care.

Life in Colorado

After completing dental school, Dr. Hong decided to move to Colorado because of his love for nature, adventure, and outdoor activities. The mountains and outdoor lifestyle made Colorado an ideal place to live and practice dentistry.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Hong enjoys spending time outdoors and taking advantage of the many recreational opportunities the region offers.

Caring for the Highlands Ranch Community

At Clear Smile Dental Care, Dr. Hong enjoys serving families throughout Highlands Ranch, including the Westridge community near Redstone Park and the Westridge Recreation Center. He is proud to help patients maintain healthy smiles while building long-term relationships within the community.

From preventive care to advanced dental treatments, Dr. Hong works to ensure every patient receives thoughtful and personalized dental care.
