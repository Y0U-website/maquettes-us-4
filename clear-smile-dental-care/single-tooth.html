A Long-Term Solution for Replacing a Missing Tooth

A single tooth implant is a dental restoration used to replace an individual missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. The implant acts as an artificial tooth root that supports a custom crown designed to blend naturally with your smile.

When a Single Tooth Implant May Be Recommended

A dental implant may be recommended when a patient is missing one tooth and has sufficient jawbone to support the implant.

Single tooth implants are commonly used when:

In cases where a damaged tooth must be removed, procedures such as Extractions & Wisdom Teeth Removal may be performed before implant placement.

  • A tooth has been lost due to injury or trauma
  • Severe decay requires tooth extraction
  • A failed dental restoration cannot be repaired
  • A tooth is missing due to gum disease

The Dental Implant Process

Replacing a missing tooth with a dental implant typically involves several stages to ensure the implant integrates properly with the jawbone.

Once complete, the implant functions like a natural tooth.

  • Consultation and Evaluation The dentist evaluates your oral health and determines whether a dental implant is the best solution.
  • Implant Placement The titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone where the missing tooth once was.
  • Healing and Bone Integration Over time, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone to create a stable foundation.
  • Placement of the Final Crown A custom Dental Crown is attached to the implant to restore the appearance and function of the tooth.

Protecting Your Implant and Oral Health

Dental implants are designed to last many years with proper care. Maintaining good oral hygiene is important for long-term implant success.

Routine Cleanings & Exams help ensure both implants and natural teeth remain healthy.

  • Brushing and flossing daily
  • Attending routine dental visits
  • Avoiding excessive pressure on the implant
  • Monitoring gum health around the implant

FAQs

What is a single tooth implant?

A single tooth implant is a dental restoration used to replace one missing tooth by placing a titanium implant in the jawbone and attaching a custom crown on top.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and good oral hygiene, dental implants can last many years and are considered one of the most durable tooth replacement options.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure is typically performed with local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort during implant placement.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The timeline varies depending on healing and treatment planning, but the full process may take several months to allow the implant to integrate with the bone.

Are dental implants better than bridges?

Dental implants replace the tooth root and do not rely on neighboring teeth for support, which can make them a strong and long-lasting option for tooth replacement.
