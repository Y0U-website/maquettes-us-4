Education and Dental Training

Dr. Roxana Ghaderi was raised in Dallas, Texas and completed her undergraduate studies at The University of Texas at Austin, where she earned a degree in Neuroscience.

She later attended Texas A&M School of Dentistry in Dallas, where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. Her dental training prepared her to provide comprehensive dental care, including preventive, cosmetic, and restorative treatments.

A Compassionate Approach to Patient Care

Dr. Ghaderi is passionate about creating a positive and comfortable experience for every patient. She understands that dental visits can feel stressful for some individuals, and she works to ensure that patients feel supported throughout their care.
