A Long-Term Solution for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are designed to replace both the visible portion of a tooth and its root. A titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone, where it integrates with the surrounding bone to create a stable foundation for a custom dental restoration.

Because implants replace the tooth root, they help preserve bone structure and maintain long-term oral health.

Patients in Highlands Ranch, including those in the Westridge neighborhood near Redstone Park and the Westridge Recreation Center, often choose dental implants because they provide stability and natural function.

  • Implant post placed within the jawbone
  • Abutment that connects the implant to the restoration
  • Custom crown or prosthetic tooth

Dental Implant Treatment Options

Dental implant treatment can be customized depending on how many teeth need to be replaced and the condition of the jawbone.

Single Tooth Implant

Single tooth implants replace an individual missing tooth using a titanium implant and custom crown designed to match surrounding teeth.

All-on-4® Full-Arch Implants

All-on-4 implant treatment replaces an entire arch of teeth using four strategically placed implants to support a fixed prosthetic arch.

Implant Supported Dentures

Implant supported dentures, also called snap-in dentures or implant overdentures, attach to implants to provide greater stability than traditional dentures.

Computer-Guided Implant Surgery

Computer-guided implant surgery uses advanced 3D imaging and digital planning technology to place implants with a high level of precision.

Bone Grafting & Ridge Augmentation

Bone grafting and ridge augmentation procedures help rebuild areas of bone loss in the jaw so patients can become candidates for dental implant placement.

The Dental Implant Process

Dental implant treatment involves several steps designed to ensure long-term stability and successful healing.

The timeline for implant treatment varies depending on healing and the complexity of the case.

  • Consultation and Evaluation The dentist examines your oral health and uses digital imaging to evaluate bone structure and treatment options.
  • Implant Placement A titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone where the missing tooth was located.
  • Healing and Osseointegration The implant integrates with the surrounding bone over time, creating a stable foundation.
  • Placement of the Final Restoration A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implant to restore the appearance and function of the tooth.

FAQs

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from titanium that are placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last many years and are considered one of the most durable tooth replacement options.

Implant placement is typically performed with local anesthesia and modern techniques designed to keep patients comfortable during the procedure.

Most adults with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone may be candidates for dental implants. A dental evaluation is required to determine eligibility.

If bone loss has occurred, procedures such as bone grafting or ridge augmentation may help rebuild the jawbone so implants can be placed successfully.
