Helping Patients Overcome Dental Anxiety

Dental anxiety is more common than many people realize, and it should never prevent someone from receiving the care they need. Dr. Aleman has extensive experience helping anxious patients feel relaxed throughout treatment through IV Sedation Dentistry in a carefully monitored setting.

Patients often choose IV sedation when they:

His calm, patient-centered approach helps individuals receive important dental care while feeling supported every step of the way.

  • Experience moderate to severe dental anxiety
  • Have had difficult dental experiences in the past
  • Need multiple procedures completed during one visit
  • Have a strong gag reflex
  • Prefer a more relaxed treatment experience
