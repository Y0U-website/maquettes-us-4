When Should You Consider Them?

Are you missing at least one tooth? If so, you can explore implants as a possible solution for replacing it. You might think about this option if you have an empty space in the front of your mouth, affecting your smile. Or you may want this treatment if you have a gap in the back or on the side, making it harder to chew.

Who’s a Good Candidate for Tooth Implants?

A suitable candidate typically has enough jawbone to support the implant post, along with healthy teeth and gums. In some cases, treatments such as gum grafts or gum contouring may be recommended to improve gum health and create a stronger foundation for treatment.

Custom Implant Planning

Successful dental implant treatment begins with careful planning. At Commonwealth Dentistry, we use advanced digital technology to create a personalized treatment plan that supports precise implant placement and predictable results.

CBCT 3D Imaging

We use cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) to capture detailed 3D images of your teeth, jawbone, nerves, and surrounding structures. These scans help us evaluate bone quality and determine the ideal location for each implant.

Digital Treatment Planning

Using your CBCT scan, we create a customized digital treatment plan that maps the precise position of each implant. This virtual planning allows us to personalize your care while improving treatment accuracy and efficiency.

3D-Printed Custom Surgical Guides

When appropriate, we create 3D-printed custom surgical guides based on your digital treatment plan. These guides help ensure precise implant placement while supporting a more predictable and efficient procedure.

What to Expect During the Implant Process

Dental implant treatment is typically completed in several phases:

  • Initial Consultation: We perform a comprehensive examination, review digital imaging, and discuss your treatment goals.
  • Implant Placement: A titanium implant post is precisely placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing and Integration: Over the following months, the implant fuses naturally with the surrounding bone in a process known as osseointegration.
  • Final Restoration: After healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the implant.

How Long Do They Last?

With proper oral hygiene and routine dental care, implant posts can last for many years and often decades. The attached restoration may require maintenance or replacement over time due to normal wear. Regular dental checkups and good home care can help maximize the longevity of your dental implants.

FAQs

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia is used during treatment, and soreness afterward is usually mild and temporary.

How long does the full dental implant process take?

Treatment time varies by case. Some patients finish in a few months, while others may need more time for healing.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Yes, but smoking can slow healing and increase complications. Quitting or reducing smoking can improve results.

Will dental implants look natural with my smile?

Yes. Implant restorations are custom-made to match the shape and color of your natural teeth.

Does dental insurance cover dental implants?

Coverage depends on your plan. Some policies may cover portions of treatment or related procedures.
