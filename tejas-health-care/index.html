The practice

Our team of doctors, nurse practitioners, behavioral health consultants and referral specialists will assist you in choosing a physician, transferring medical records, entering your medical information into our state-of-the-art electronic medical records system and making appointments for you and your family.

In addition to accepting Medicaid, Medicare and Private Health Insurance plans, we also serve those individuals that are not covered by health insurance. Patients are charged for services on a sliding fee scale based on the patient’s family size and income. Tejas Health Care provides health care everyone can afford.
