POLICY REGARDING NO SHOWS

Regular Medical, Dental, and Behavioral health attention is essential to maintaining your health. It is important to keep scheduled appointments. Patients who have five no-show appointments or late cancelations in a 6-month period may be prevented from scheduling appointments in advance for a period of six months. Patients are able to schedule same day appointments or request to be seen as a walk in.
