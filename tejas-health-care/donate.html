Donate

A donation to Tejas Health Care is a thoughtful way to honor someone’s life. Your gift can recognize milestones in a person’s life or express the sense of loss when someone loses their battle with sickness. Once your gift is received, Tejas Health Care will send a card to the honoree or family acknowledging your gift.

With a planned gift to Tejas Health Care, you can combine your desire to give to charity with your overall financial, tax, and estate planning goals. Planned giving gives you a special connection with the health care center. You will help those who receive care in the facility — for now and for years to come.

Tejas Health Care will receive a portion of the purchase price* when you shop at smile.amazon.com from the AmazonSmile Foundation, at no cost to you. It’s a simple and automatic way for you to support Tejas Health Care every time you shop.

*0.5 % of the purchase price from your eligible AmazonSmile purchases.

There is an easy way to possibly double your support through a corporate matching gift program. Many companies will match their employee’s charitable gifts. Sometimes, employers will even match a contribution made by a friend to support you in fundraising for an event. Here’s how to take advantage of a Corporate Matching Gift Program:

Contact your Human Resources department and ask if your employer has a matching gift program.

If so, ask for a matching gift program form to complete and submit a copy to your company and one copy to us. This way we know that you are providing a matching gift.

If your company does not have a corporate matching gift program but is interested in making a matching gift, please provide them with the following information:

Tejas Health Care’s - Helping Hands for Health | 753 E. Travis St, La Grange TX. | 78945 | Federal Tax ID # 75-3260266 | Tejas Health Care is a 501(c) (3) non-profit organization |

If your company uses an online matching gift process, access your company’s website and submit a matching gifts request. Print a copy of your request for your records.
