Financial Information

The cost of orthodontic treatment varies depending on individual needs and your specific treatment. One of our doctors will do a complimentary consultation and then we can provide you with the cost for your treatment. We are glad to provide payment plans with low monthly payment and low down payment options so that orthodontic treatment fits your budget. We have always believed in being generous and will do our absolute best to make orthodontic treatment affordable for every person that comes to our office.

Dental Insurance:

Many other offices do not accept insurance and the patient is responsible for either paying more money out of pocket or collecting from the insurance company themselves. At Samra Orthodontics we take a different approach. We are glad to be providers for ALL PPO insurances so that our patients can have one less thing to worry about when it comes to their smile.
