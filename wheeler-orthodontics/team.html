Aaron Rondon DDS MS

Dr. Rondon strives to stay abreast of the latest advancements in the field of orthodontics and maintains memberships with numerous prestigious organizations including the American Dental Association, American Association of Orthodontists, California Dental Association, California Association of Orthodontists, San Joaquin Dental Society, Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontics . He also offers lectures and is a published author.

Dr. Rondon was born in Stockton, CA and is married with two boys. He volunteers his services with the Students for International Missionary Organization during their dental mission trips to Ecuador, El Salvador. He deeply appreciates all athletic events and enjoys discussing them with patients. He also enjoys poker and considers it a sport!
