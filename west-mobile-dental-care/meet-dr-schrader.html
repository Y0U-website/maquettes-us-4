Meet Dr. Schrader

MEET DR. ED SCHRADER OF WEST MOBILE DENTAL CARE

Dr. Schrader grew up in Clinton, Mississippi, prior to attending undergraduate school at Wake Forest University . While enrolled, he was awarded a research fellowship grant with the Welker Group and was published in the Journal of Bioorganic and Medicinal Chemistry for cancer therapy research. He graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry in 2005 after receiving the Blackbyrd Scholarship for possessing the 2nd highest overall grade point average for a Chemistry major.

Dr. Schrader is married to Dr. Brooke Weber Schrader, a pediatric dentist and native of Mobile. They have two imaginative girls and an adventurous boy who enjoy chasing and tormenting their yellow lab, Josie. Outside of the office, Dr. Schrader enjoys traveling, cooking, playing basketball, and spending time with his family.
