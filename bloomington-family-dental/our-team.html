John Van Scoyoc, DDS

Dr. John Van Scoyoc, originally hailing from the vicinity of Illinois Wesleyan’s campus and educated through the Bloomington public school system, is a distinguished figure in the field of dentistry and a passionate advocate for the dental health of the Bloomington community .

A former college athlete in two sports, cross country, and track, Dr. John achieved the status of a two-time All-American athlete during his college career. His crowning achievement came in 2014 when he was inducted into the Miami University Athletic Hall of Fame, a profound honor that marked a highlight in his life.

Armed with a Zoology degree from Miami University, Dr. John pursued his dental education at the University of Illinois at Chicago, where he had the fortunate opportunity to meet his wife and partner, Dr. Stacey. After graduation, the couple made the decision to return to Dr. John’s hometown to establish their own dental practice, Bloomington Family Dental. Their goal has always been to provide a comfortable and technologically advanced environment for their patients, adapting and growing with the ever-evolving landscape of dental technology.

Dr. John’s dedication to the Bloomington community goes beyond his dental practice. He wase been actively involved in the McLean County Health Department for 10 years, serving in the children’s dental clinic. Dr. John has also contributed his time to providing free dental care, including the Gary S. Johnson Free Dental Clinic, and served as the dental chairperson for the Lighthouse Free Clinic, assisting Russian Adoptees.

Mentorship is a key aspect of Dr. John’s professional life. He finds joy in mentoring new dentists, students, and children, hosting predental college student observers and interns in their dental office over the years. He has lectured to the Illinois State University Pre-Dental Club on numerous occasions.

Within the dental community, Dr. John has played a prominent role in the McLean County Dental Society, holding various offices, including President in 2005 and receiving the President’s Award in 2006. On the state level, he has served as a Delegate, Alternate Delegate, and Student Delegate to the Illinois State Dental Society. From 2021-2023, Dr. John served on the Illinois State Dental Society Board of Trustees as the Central District Trustee.

On the national stage, he has represented his profession as an Alternate Delegate and Delegate to the American Dental Association House of Delegates since 2021.

Beyond his dental commitments, Dr. John has volunteered on the Mulberry School Board, served as a Prairie Thunder Team Dentist, and contributed his time to Bloomington High School football and track programs. He was involved in the Bloomington Normal Sunshine Rotary and is now a mentor in the Sigma Chi Fraternity Sig Connect national program.

Outside of his professional and volunteer commitments, Dr. John is known for his love of gadgets and the outdoors. A sports enthusiast, he enjoys activities such as swimming, sailing, scuba diving, snorkeling, surfing, golf, walking, and landscaping. His love for travel and adventure is complemented by a passion for staying in shape through regular workouts.

A multifaceted individual, Dr. John finds time for quiet moments with an enjoyable read, delving into journals, pop culture, non-fiction, and enjoying a diverse range of music. Above all, he treasures his role as a father to three amazing children, who inspire him to continually strive for the best in both his personal and professional endeavors.
