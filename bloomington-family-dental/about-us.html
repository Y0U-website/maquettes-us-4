ABOUT US

Who We Are

We help you and your family maintain happy, healthy smiles in a professional, friendly environment. Bloomington Family Dental is a family-owned, two-dentist practice that utilizes proven techniques and new technology to give patients thorough check-ups, cleanings, and procedures.

We are a family-friendly practice with decades of experience working with people from ages 1-100. Bloomington Family Dental treats every patient like a member of our own family. When you visit us at 908 N. Hershey Rd Suite 2 in Bloomington, Illinois, you will see first-hand why our patients feel at home and how we’ve been able to grow our business over the past two decades.

OUR MISSION We live by our mission to provide quality, state-of the-art, comprehensive oral health care in a relaxed, friendly, and family-oriented environment.

OUR HISTORY

Bloomington Family Dental was founded by husband and wife, Bloomington native John Van Scoyoc, DDS, and Stacey Van Scoyoc, DDS. After spending seven years practicing at different offices in town, John and Stacey made the decision to go into business together in October 2001. The practice has grown and added new team members along the way while staying at the same location. John and Stacey are passionate about their work, their business, and the Bloomington-Normal community.
