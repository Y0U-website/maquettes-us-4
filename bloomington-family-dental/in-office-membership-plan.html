FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I sign up for the In-Office Membership Program?

For existing patients, scroll down and click the Get Started button underneath the program that has been selected by the Doctors for your personal preventative care plan. You may pay online. Once your online payment is processed, a member of the Bloomington Family Dental Staff will contact you to answer any questions you may have and get you started with the program. Another option is to call Bloomington Family Dental and speak to a member of our staff to enroll over the phone.

For new patients, scroll down to the Contact Us to schedule a personalized New Patient Consultation. You will meet our team and a customized treatment plan will be created for you. Once that plan is created, the Doctors will prescribe you a preventative care plan so you may sign up for the appropriate Membership Program.

We understand that dental discount plans vary between providers, so please do not hesitate of contact us with any questions.

Is this an insurance plan?

No, this program is not an insurance plan. Our In-Office Membership Plan provides our patients with discounted prices on preventative care, as well as a range of common dental procedures. You will pay the discounted rate at the time services are provided.

Can I sign up my whole family?

Yes, you can sign up an entire family or any individual. This offer is not valid when using a dental benefit of any kind.

I am an existing patient, can I join?

Yes, all existing patients are invited to join our In-Office Membership Plan.

What happens if I become insured while a member of the program?

Our In-Office Dental Membership Plan is for uninsured patients or patients who choose not to use their dental benefit (for example, if they have out of network dental benefit plan). If you become insured, or choose to use your dental benefit, your membership will be terminated.

I have a dental benefit; can I sign up for this Dental Membership Plan?

This offer is not valid in combination with a dental benefit of any kind. We sincerely appreciate your loyalty to the practice.

I have a FLEX plan; can I submit this annual fee for reimbursement?

Most FLEX plans reimburse dental procedures, but we advise you to contact your employee benefits provider to confirm the parameters of their coverage.
