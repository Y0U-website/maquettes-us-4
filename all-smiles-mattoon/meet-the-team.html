Meet the Team

Outside of work she loves spending time with her kids and grandbaby.

Laura- Dental Assistant Laura has been a dental assistant since 1989 when she graduated from the assisting program at Lake Land College. She is an Effingham, Illinois native and is married to her high school sweetheart! They have been married since 1990 and have one daughter named Lindsay who lives in Teutopolis with her husband and 2 daughters of their own. Laura has 2 dogs named Ace and Guss!

Lezlie- Business Assistant Lezlie is one of our business assistants and you can see her smiling face when you walk in the door. She will help you schedule appointments and answer any questions you may have with insurance.

Outside of work she has 2 sons and a granddaughter on the way! She has a spoiled cat and loves to garden.

Madison - Dental Assistant Madison is certified in dental assisting and radiography. She has always enjoyed the dental field as her step mother is a hygienist. Madison had her own experience in dental work when her front teeth were knocked out in 8th grade. In the office you can find her stocking rooms with supplies, chart prepping, sterilizing instruments, helping patients understand their treatment and assisting the doctor chairside.

She is the mother of a 2 and half yr old son. He is very rambunctious and keeps everyone laughing and on their toes constantly. She uses her free time to be outside in their backyard, fishing/swimming, going on trips and visiting family every weekend!

Renee- Dental Hygienist Renee has been a dental hygienist since 1988. Not only is she a member of our team in the office, she is also a Hygiene instructor at Lake Land College! She truly enjoys sharing her experience with the students. Mattoon is her home town and she has been seeing several of her patients and their families for over 33 years!

She and her husband have been married since 1992 and they are expecting their first grandchild in 2027! Her and her husband are huge dog lovers and they have 3 mini long haired dachshunds that are spoiled rotten.

Outside of the office, she enjoys gardening, reading and spending time with her boyfriend Michael. They spoil their two dogs Steve and Wayne. Her most favorite part of her job is educating patients on their oral heath and helping people attain confident, healthy smiles. Her goal is to make the patient comfortable and build a trusting bond with every patient that sits in her chair. She is committed to ensuring each person leaves with a brighter smile then what they walked in with!

Tara- Business Assistant Tara has been in the dental field since 2020, starting out as a dental assistant and working toward her goal of working in the front office. Her favorite part about working in the dental field is watching patients learn to love their smile again.

In her free time she loves spending time with her husband and son. You can find her at car shows, drag races or just reading in the outdoors.
