Ebad Muqtader DMD Dr. Ebad Muqtader graduated with his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from UIC College of Dentistry. He chose dentistry because he is a hands on type of person and he enjoys how interactive dentistry can be. His priority as a healthcare provider is to provide the highest quality of care to his patients while also building collaborative relationships to ensure he meet their needs. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys spending time with his family and friends, cooking, traveling, and being outdoors.
