We understand that selecting the right dentist is an important decision affecting many aspects of your life, not just your teeth. How your dentist treats you—and your oral health—affects your smile, how you look and feel, what others think of you, aspects of your overall health, your schedule, and your well being.

At your first appointment, Dr. Mandefro and her team will set the stage for a caring relationship with you based on a thorough understanding of your needs by analyzing the structural and functional factors of your jaw joints, muscles, and teeth. This personalized information about your dental health will be referenced in a comprehensive approach to your care.

At Soulard Family Dentistry, listening to you and understanding your desires is our priority.
