Our Treatments

DIGITAL RADIOGRAPHY

We use digital radiography to acquire an image of your tooth that can be displayed on a chair-side computer monitor. Digital radiography allows us to expose patients to far less radiation than the traditional film technique while providing us with a tool for patient education. The image on the monitor is large enough for the patient to see, and can be altered using the computer program to provide diagnostic information.

MAGNIFICATION AND ILLUMINATION

We use high-power magnification and illumination to better view the small canals inside your tooth. This advantage allows a more thorough treatment of your tooth in both non-surgical and surgical root canal procedures.
