The team discusses oral hygiene, dietary practices, habits and family dental history with families. The practice encourages a first visit within six months of the first erupted tooth or by age one, whichever comes first.

Les soins

The practice focuses on early prevention, age-appropriate education and quality treatment for pediatric patients. Families can discuss oral hygiene, diet, habits and their child’s dental history with the team.
