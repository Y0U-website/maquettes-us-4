What is your life like outside of Beverly Dental Center?

Dr. Oh ended up moving from Detroit to Texas in 2011 because he couldn’t stand the cold. He likes the Texas heat much better! Today, he and his wife have two young sons, and he always enjoys spending time with his fun and loving family. Other hobbies include golfing, snowboarding, playing table tennis and regular tennis, and simply making people happy, which is his favorite part of being a dentist.
