Treatments Smiles for Every Age From kids starting their orthodontic journey to adults perfecting their smile, we provide care for every stage of life. The AAO recommends an orthodontic assessment by age 7 to address potential issues early. For teens, we offer confidence-boosting treatments, and adults can enjoy discreet, modern options. At Sunrise Orthodontics, we’re here to ensure your journey is comfortable, effective, and tailored to your needs.

Braces

Braces remain one of the most effective ways to achieve a straight, healthy smile. We offer advanced options, including self-ligating, traditional, and ceramic braces, designed to suit your style and orthodontic needs. Adding colorful elastics can make braces fun for kids and teens and let their personality shine. Whether for kids, teens, or adults, braces deliver precise results for lasting confidence.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners provide a more aesthetic alternative to traditional braces. With removable trays, you can eat, brush, and floss as usual while your teeth gradually shift into place. Designed to fit the busy lifestyles of teens, adults, and professionals. This modern approach combines confidence, comfort, and practicality for your orthodontic journey.

Palatal Expanders

A palatal expander helps guide your child’s smile as they grow. By gently widening the upper jaw, it creates space for permanent teeth to come in properly and helps improve the bite. The process is simple and comfortable, and our team will walk you and your child through each step. An expander sets the stage for a healthy, beautiful smile that lasts for years to come.

Retainers

Retainers are essential for maintaining your smile after orthodontic treatment. It helps prevent teeth from shifting and ensures your results last for years to come. We’ll discuss the advantages of removable or fixed options, and provide a retainer that fits comfortably and supports your long-term dental health. Protect your investment and enjoy a confident smile for life.

Technology

We’re proud to offer advanced orthodontic technology that enhances both accuracy and comfort. From digital impressions to 3D imaging, every tool we use is designed to make your treatment easier and more effective. Let our modern approach lead you to a confident smile.
