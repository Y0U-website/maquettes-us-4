About Us

About Us Dr. Steven Dent Approach Credentials Personal Dr. Steven Dent’s approach to orthodontics is rooted in compassion, precision, and a commitment to patient education. Known for his warm and approachable demeanor, he creates a welcoming environment where both kids and adults feel at ease.

Dr. Dent earned his dental degree from Dalhousie University in Nova Scotia, Canada, and completed advanced training in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics at Western University in Ontario. His expertise, combined with years of hands-on experience, allows him to deliver exceptional care tailored to each patient’s needs.

Dr. Dent grew up working on a dairy farm and has always enjoyed basketball, the great outdoors and spending time with family. He is always on the hunt for a good burger and fries. He hopes to one day buy a tractor to allow him to help more efficiently with hurricane clean up.
