Our Technology

0 What We Use (and why it matters) At Complete Dentistry, we blend expert care with modern dental technology to give you a smoother, safer, and more comfortable experience—every time you visit.

Intraoral Cameras in Every Room Ever wonder what the dentist sees? These small, high-resolution cameras show detailed images of your teeth and gums on a screen—so you’re part of the conversation, not just the diagnosis.

3D CT Scan & Panoramic X-Ray Our digital imaging system captures a full, 3D view of your mouth, jaws, and surrounding structures. It’s critical for placing implants, evaluating bone levels, diagnosing infections, and planning treatment with surgical precision.

Trios 5 (Digital Scanner) Say goodbye to messy, goopy impressions—and no more gagging or choking on trays of impression material. The Trios 5 captures incredibly accurate 3D scans of your teeth in seconds, helping us create better-fitting crowns, aligners, nightguards, and more.

Ultrasonic Cleanings (Cavitron Technology) Instead of scraping by hand, we use ultrasonic scalers to gently remove plaque and tartar. It’s faster, more effective, and more comfortable—especially for deeper cleanings.

Intraoral Suction Isolation System (Isolite®) Isolite keeps your mouth open comfortably, suctions water continuously, and isolates the area we’re working on—all at once. This improves your comfort, speeds up procedures, and makes longer appointments feel easier on your jaw.
