About

Desert Dental is a full service dental practice proudly serving the community of the North Hollywood neighborhood of Los Angeles, California. Under the leadership of Brian Shahangian, DDS, and Bita Shahangian, DDS, Desert Dental has grown into a trusted dental health resource for people of all ages.

The practice offers the full scope of general dentistry services, with a special focus on preventive dentistry. Avoiding dental decay and gum disease begins with outstanding oral hygiene, and comprehensive dental exams catch issues in the earliest stage when treatment is often most effective.

Restorative treatments include fillings, crowns and bridges, dental implants and All-on-4 , root canals, and full mouth reconstruction. Emergency dentistry is also available to address unexpected needs.

Desert Dental understands the importance of the aesthetics of a smile, too, which is why they offer the very latest cosmetic dentistry options. Dental veneers, professional teeth whitening, bonding, Invisalign , and tooth-colored fillings are all available.

With the mission statement Your Smile is Our Passion, it comes as no surprise that the entire team at Desert Dental dedicates their focus to delivering unparalleled service in a warm, welcoming treatment environment.

Considerable time and effort go into researching which state-of-the-art technology to bring to practice. Cone beam CT scanning, the iTero intraoral scanner, digital X-rays, and Perio Restore oral cleansing gel are just some of the tools and products that represent the heights of the industry.
