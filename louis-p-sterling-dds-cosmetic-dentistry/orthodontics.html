What Is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics focuses on aligning teeth and correcting bite issues to improve both appearance and function. Through clear aligners, traditional braces, or modern alternatives, orthodontic treatment gently moves teeth into ideal positions, helping you achieve a straighter, healthier, more confident smile.
