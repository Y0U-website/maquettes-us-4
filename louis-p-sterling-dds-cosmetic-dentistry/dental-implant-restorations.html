What Is Dental Implant Restorations?

Dental implant restorations replace the visible portion of your dental implant with a strong, natural-looking crown. Once your implant has healed, we customize the final tooth to match the color, shape, and alignment of your smile so the result looks seamless and functions just like the real thing.
