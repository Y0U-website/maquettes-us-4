The process, step by step.

We begin with a consultation and 3D cone-beam CT scan, mapping your jaw in detail so we can plan the placement with precision. If everything looks good, we place the implant under local anesthesia — most patients are surprised how little they feel. You’ll go home the same day with a temporary crown. After 3–4 months of healing, we place the permanent crown.

The total timeline sounds long, but you’re not in our chair for most of it. Total in-office time is usually four to five short appointments.
