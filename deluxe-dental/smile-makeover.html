Smile Makeover

A smile makeover is a customized treatment plan that combines multiple cosmetic dentistry treatments to improve the appearance of your teeth and smile. It typically involves a combination of treatments such as teeth whitening, dental veneers, dental bonding, orthodontics, and more, tailored to address specific concerns like discoloration, misalignment, chipped or damaged teeth, and overall smile harmony.

At Deluxe Dental, we offer a smile makeover to create a more radiant, balanced, and visually appealing smile, ultimately boosting an individual’s self-confidence and overall oral well-being by addressing a range of cosmetic and functional dental issues.

Possible Smile Makeover Procedures

Dental Veneers

Porcelain veneers are a great way to improve the appearance of your smile. They are thin shells of ceramic bonded to the front of your teeth to cover imperfections such as chips, cracks, gaps, stains, and more.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are custom-made caps that fit over your existing tooth structure to restore its shape, size, strength, and appearance. They can be used to repair damaged teeth or improve the appearance of discolored or misshapen teeth.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that replace missing teeth. They are made of titanium and are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they are fused with the bone over time. Once the implant has fused to the bone, a crown is placed on top of the implant to replace the missing tooth. Dental implants can be used to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth.

The Benefits of Smile Makeover

Improved Oral Health

Our dentist in Kilgore, TX, may recommend a smile makeover if you have teeth that are misaligned or crooked. These issues can lead to other oral health problems, such as tooth decay and gum disease. You can reduce your risk of these complications by correcting these issues with cosmetic dentistry treatments.

Better Functionality

If you have missing teeth or other problems with your teeth, it can be difficult to eat certain foods and speak clearly. A smile makeover can help restore your mouth’s function so that you can eat and speak normally again.

Enhanced Aesthetics

A smile makeover in Kilgore, TX, can improve the appearance of your smile, making it more symmetrical and attractive. This can give you a more youthful look and help you feel better about your appearance.

Increased Comfort

If you are unhappy with how your teeth look, you may be self-conscious about smiling or laughing. This can lead to feelings of discomfort and even embarrassment. A smile makeover can give you the confidence to show off your smile without feeling embarrassed.
