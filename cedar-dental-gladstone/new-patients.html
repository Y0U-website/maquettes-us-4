New Patients

  • 0 Forms & Polices Welcome to our practice! Thank you for the trust you have shown in us by choosing our office for your dental care. In order to familiarize you with policies and payment plans offered, they are listed below. Our commitment is to provide the best possible service. Please don’t hesitate to ask if you have any questions. OFFICE HOURS: Monday – Friday 8am – 5pm with lunch typically at noon
  • CANCELLATIONS: Please give us at least 48 hours’ notice if you are unable to keep your dental appointment. Giving us at least 48 hours’ notice allows us time to make a new appointment for you and give your original one to another patient. It also allows us to see our patients on time and helps us give more affordable dental care for all of our patients.
  • PAYMENTS: For patients with no dental insurance, payment is due the day of service ‐ unless previous arrangements have been made. The estimated portion not covered by insurance is due the same day that service is provided. Visa, MasterCard, cash, and checks are accepted. We also accept payment plans exclusively through CareCredit and can help you with the application process in the office.
  • AFTER HOURS EMERGENCIES: Please call office phone number for the most current information provided based on the current week (ie doctor on call, phone numbers to contact, vacations, closures, etc.)
  • HOLIDAYS: We will be closed on most major U.S. Holidays (Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Years, Independence Day, Memorial Day, and Labor Day)
