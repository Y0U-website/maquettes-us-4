Orthodontics

0 Are you unhappy with your smile? We can help. When it comes to your smile, not all cosmetic issues require invasive procedures like crowns, bridges, implants, etc. Sometimes your perfect smile can be achieved with a series of clear aligners. This revolutionary approach to orthodontics allows you to straighten your teeth without traditional metal brackets. Treatment can last anywhere from 4 to 18 months depending on the amount of movement required to achieve your desired results.

Straight teeth are more than just pretty.

Fact: The straighter your teeth, the easier is it to keep them clean and therefore avoid further dental problems. Most people who consider their home care to be good, are unaware that when your teeth are severely crowded or rotated it is extremely difficult to remove plaque and bacteria that cause decay and periodontal disease with simple brushing and flossing.

Is it affordable?

Straightening your teeth has never been more affordable. Thanks to a multitude of financing options, as well as reduction in the cost of producing aligners, there is no reason to let cost become a barrier to your dream smile. We recommend applying for Care Credit to finance treatment up to 18 months at 0 % interest or SoFi for extended term low interest financing. That means you could make payments as low as $179/month toward your orthodontic treatment.
