Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a discounted membership rate for children or additional family members? Yes! We love welcoming entire households to our practice. Additional family members can register for $50 less than the standard individual rate. Our front desk team will be happy to apply this discount when enrolling your family members together at check-in or checkout.

What happens if I move away from Brooklyn before my membership year ends? Because our annual membership fee is non-refundable and cannot be prorated, we are unable to issue refunds if you relocate before your 12 months are up. However, your coverage remains fully active through your original end date. If you move nearby or return to Brooklyn for visits, you are always welcome to use your remaining cleanings, exams, or 15 % treatment discounts until your plan expires!

How does an in-office dental savings plan compare to traditional dental insurance? Traditional corporate insurance plans are packed with hidden friction points like deductibles, waiting periods, missing tooth exclusions, and low annual maximum coverage caps. Our private plan eliminates the middleman entirely. There is zero approval process, no waiting windows, and no caps. Your coverage benefits become active the exact day you sign up, giving you direct, uninterrupted access to our multi-doctor team.

What exactly is included in the $575 annual private dental plan fee? We believe in total financial transparency with zero hidden pricing fluff. Your $575 annual membership explicitly covers: 2 comprehensive or recall exams, 2 professional routine cleanings (hygiene prophylaxis), advanced oral cancer screenings, and professional fluoride varnish treatments. Additionally, you receive 1 dedicated emergency exam with all necessary diagnostic X-rays included at no extra cost.

What if I have gum disease and require deeper cleanings? Does this plan cover periodontal maintenance? Yes! While your annual membership fee covers 2 standard preventive cleanings, all specialized gum treatments, including scaling and root planing (deep cleanings) and additional periodontal maintenance visits, receive a flat 15 % discount off our standard fees. Our doctors will co-diagnose your gum health and apply your membership savings directly to any recommended care.

Does the 15 % membership discount apply to major dental treatments like crowns or root canals? Yes. If our doctors identify a structural or restorative issue during your co-diagnosis exam, your membership automatically unlocks a guaranteed 15 % discount on subsequent treatments completed at our Brooklyn Heights office. This includes general fillings, custom porcelain crowns, deep periodontics gum therapies, root canal treatments, and more.

Are higher-value specialized treatments like dental implants, clear aligners, or Botox® included in the 15 % discount? Yes! Unlike corporate insurance plans that exclude specialized or aesthetic care, our membership unlocks a flat 15 % discount on all treatments across our office, including dental implants, clear aligners, and Masseter Botox®.

Can I combine my 15 % membership discount with third-party financing like Cherry or Sunbit? Yes, absolutely! You can apply your 15 % membership plan discount directly to your total treatment cost and then split the remaining balance into manageable monthly payments online through Cherry or Sunbit.

Can I use the membership plan to get a discount on cosmetic treatments like teeth whitening? Absolutely.
