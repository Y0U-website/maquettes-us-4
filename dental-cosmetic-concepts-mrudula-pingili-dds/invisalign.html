Can Invisalign® be used to treat all types of alignment issues?

Invisalign® can be used to treat a wide array of alignment problems, but they’re not always ideal for every patient. During the initial consultation, the teeth and bite alignment will be carefully evaluated to ensure Invisalign® is the most appropriate option for achieving optimal results.
