The practice

Toddler dental care should begin with a first dentist visit by age 1 or within 6 months of the first tooth erupting. Regular checkups are recommended every 6 months to monitor development and prevent decay. Early, consistent care, including brushing twice daily with a tiny smear of fluoride toothpaste, is essential for healthy, cavity-free teeth.

The American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) recommends children have their first check-up with an orthodontist by age 7. This initial, usually complimentary visit allows for early identification of bite, jaw, and tooth issues, often during a mix of baby and permanent teeth.
