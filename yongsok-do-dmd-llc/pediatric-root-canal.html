Welcome to Keiki Dental, where we specialize in providing top-quality dental care for children on Oahu, HI. Our team is dedicated to creating a positive and comfortable experience for our little patients while ensuring their oral health needs are met.

One of the most common procedures we perform at Keiki Dental is a root canal for children. While the thought of your child needing a root canal may seem daunting, it is a very common and necessary treatment in certain cases.

When Is a Root Canal Necessary?

A root canal becomes necessary when the pulp or nerve of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed due to deep decay, repeated dental procedures, or trauma to the tooth. This can cause severe pain and discomfort for your child and can potentially lead to more serious oral health issues if left untreated.

What Root Canal Treatments Do We Offer?

At Keiki Dental, we offer both pulpotomy and pulpectomy procedures to address different stages of infection or inflammation in a tooth’s pulp. A pulpotomy is a partial removal of the infected pulp, while a pulpectomy involves removing all of the pulp from the tooth. Our experienced pediatric dentists will determine which procedure is best for your child’s specific case.

How Can We Assist Your Child?

We understand that root canal treatment can be intimidating for children, which is why we offer various comfort measures to help ease any anxiety or discomfort. We provide laughing gas and conscious sedation to help your child relax and feel at ease during the procedure.

Plus, we have created a fun and welcoming environment for children to make their dental visit an enjoyable experience. Our office is adorned with colorful murals, and our waiting areas are equipped with televisions and books to keep kids entertained. Our friendly and caring staff will also make sure your child feels comfortable and at ease during their visit.
