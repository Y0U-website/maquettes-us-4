Cosmetic Dentistry

  • Dental Bonding : A quick, affordable way to repair small chips, gaps, or discoloration in a single visit. Tooth-colored resin is applied and sculpted for a natural-looking finish.

  • Porcelain Veneers: Thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth to correct chips, stains, gaps, and uneven alignment for a dramatic smile transformation.

FAQs About Cosmetic Dentistry

Q: How long do cosmetic results last? A: With proper care, teeth whitening can last up to a year, and veneers or crowns can last 10 to 15 years or more.

Q: Will my insurance cover cosmetic procedures? A: Insurance often covers crowns for health reasons but typically not purely cosmetic work. We offer flexible financing for aesthetic treatments. Visit our payment and insurance page for details.

Q: Is cosmetic dentistry safe? A: Yes. Our materials and techniques meet the highest safety standards. Dr. Yu will discuss any concerns before we begin.

Q: What cosmetic dental services does a cosmetic dentist in Bellaire TX offer? A: At Bright Value Dental, we offer teeth whitening , dental bonding , dental crowns , and porcelain veneers. During your consultation, Dr. Yu will recommend the best options based on your smile goals and oral health.

Q: Can cosmetic dentistry fix multiple issues at once? A: Absolutely. Many patients combine treatments like whitening, bonding, and crowns as part of a smile makeover plan. We work with you to prioritize treatments and create a timeline that fits your schedule and budget.
