FAQs

Q: Are Valplast Dentures Comfortable? A: Yes, Valplast partials are lightweight and flexible, reducing sore spots and irritation. Many patients find them more comfortable than traditional metal-clasp partials because the material adapts to the natural shape of your gums.

Q: How Do I Care for my Dentures? A: Remove and gently brush them daily, soak overnight in a denture cleaning solution, and maintain regular dental checkups . Dr. Yu can also check the fit at each visit to make sure your dentures stay comfortable over time.

With proper care, a well-made set of dentures typically lasts 5 to 10 years . Over time, your gums and jawbone can change shape, so periodic adjustments or a reline may be needed to maintain a good fit.

Most patients adjust to eating with dentures within a few weeks. We recommend starting with softer foods and gradually working up to firmer items. Dr. Yu will give you specific guidance based on your type of denture.
