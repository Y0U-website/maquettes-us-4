Who Is a Candidate for Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Patients with significant tooth loss or failing teeth are excellent candidates for full mouth implants. Adequate jawbone density is needed, but our 3D planning allows many patients to avoid complex bone grafting. Dr. Genc evaluates your oral health using advanced 3D imaging and creates a personalized treatment plan, ensuring optimal function, fit, and aesthetics.

The Full Mouth Dental Implants Surgical Procedure

The full mouth implant process begins with a comprehensive consultation and digital planning. During the procedure, four implants are surgically placed in the jaw. For suitable cases, we fabricate fixed teeth in-house within 24 hours, so patients leave with a fully restored smile the same day. Follow-up visits ensure proper healing and long-term success.
