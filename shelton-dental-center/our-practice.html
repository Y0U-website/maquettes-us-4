Our Dental Practice

Our mission

Friendly care close to home

Shelton Dental Center has been a cornerstone of oral care in the Shelton, WA, community for over 20 years. Dr. Brett Will joined Shelton Dental Center in 2018 as an Associate Dentist. It didn’t take long for him to see that the practice embodied the values he aspired for his own clinic: a place where patients were treated with kindness and respect, while actively engaging with the community to promote oral health through comprehensive dental services. Dr. Will acquired Shelton Dental Center in early 2023.

You will never be just another chart number at Shelton Dental Center. Our mission is to provide compassionate and quality dental care on a timely and efficient basis. We aim to build lifelong relationships with patients in a fun, friendly environment. Our team loves sharing a few smiles and laughs with patients during appointments.

Our office provides a relaxing atmosphere, equipped to meet all your dental needs. Our landscape design emulates and celebrates the beauty of the surrounding mountains, featuring native plants and rockery sourced from a quarry near Mount Rainer. Every spring, you can see lush pockets of trilliums blooming around our building. Additionally, we offer modern amenities, such as plush blankets, comfy pillows, and headphones, to make appointments as comfortable as possible.

Dr. Brett Will

In his free time, Dr. Will enjoys outdoor activities such as mountain biking and spending quality time with his friends and family.

Dr. Lindsay Buell

Dr. Lindsay Buell was born in Arizona and raised in Indiana. After completing her bachelor’s degree at Purdue University, she attended Oregon Health and Science University School of Dentistry. She is focused on helping patients find the right treatment plan for them to feel happy and confident with their smiles. Dr. Lindsay participates in continuing education courses to provide the most current treatment and care. She hopes to make patients feel as comfortable as possible in the dental office by allowing them to feel heard, trusted, and safe.

Dr. Lindsay lives in Olympia with Dr. Tommy and their dogs, Maui and Phoebe. They enjoy spending time outdoors (hiking, snowshoeing, camping, paddleboarding) and cheering on the Purdue Boilermakers during football and basketball seasons.

Dr. Tommy Ollmann

Dr. Tommy Ollmann was born and raised in Woodbury, Minnesota, just minutes away from the Wisconsin border. He attended the University of Wisconsin, Madison where he got his Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Microbiology. He received his DMD degree from Oregon Health and Science University School of Dentistry. During his time there, he also met his wife, Dr. Lindsay! Tommy strives to provide excellent dental care and a positive, comfortable experience for his patients.

In his free time, he likes to play guitar, golf, and go hiking with Lindsay and their dogs, Maui and Phoebe.

Modern Technology

Modern technology, old-fashioned care
