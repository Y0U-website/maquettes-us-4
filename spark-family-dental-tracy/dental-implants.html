Dental Implant Frequently Asked Questions

Get the answers to the most frequently asked questions about dental implants so that you can feel confident about your decision to get dental implants.

The cost of a dental implant can vary depending on the type of implant, the number of implants, the complexity of the procedure, and other factors.

It depends on many factors, such as the type of implant, the surgeon’s experience, the practice’s location, and whether or not you need a bone graft. Generally, a single dental implant can cost anywhere from $1,000 to $3,000.

The entire implant process can take anywhere from several months to a year. The first step is placing the titanium screw, which takes about an hour. The next step is the placement of the abutment, which takes place about two weeks later. The final step is the dental crown placement, which takes place about two weeks after the abutment placement.

Dental implants are a more permanent solution than dentures, and they can help you avoid the hassle of removing and reinserting your teeth daily. Dental implants are also more secure and can provide a more natural-looking smile. On the other hand, dentures may be a more affordable option for some people, and they can be easier to care for than dental implants. Talk to your dentist about which option is right for you.

A dental implant is a titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to act as a tooth root. A dental crown is a porcelain or ceramic tooth placed on top of the dental implant to replace the visible portion of a natural tooth.
