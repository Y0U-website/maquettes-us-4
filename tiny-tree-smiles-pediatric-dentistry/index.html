The practice

Dental accidents happen. That’s why we offer same-day and after-hours support to help relieve pain quickly and guide parents through any urgent situation with clarity and compassion.

At Tiny Tree Smiles, we combine clinical excellence with heartfelt care. We’re not just treating teeth, we’re nurturing relationships, reducing fear, and helping children grow into confident, healthy adults. With a thoughtful, inclusive approach, Dr. Jessica Ton creates a supportive dental home you and your child can rely on for years to come.

Preventive and educational approach We take time to explain the “why” behind everything, helping families build healthy habits that last, without guilt or judgment.

Culturally sensitive communication We honor diverse backgrounds and speak clearly, offering inclusive care that supports every parent and child in our community.

After-hours support Parents can reach us through a dedicated phone line for after-hours questions and emergencies, ensuring peace of mind when it matters most.

Special needs expertise With training in behavior management and inclusive care strategies, we provide personalized treatment for children with special health care needs.

Les soins

Dr. Jessica Ton is a board-certified pediatric dentist with deep roots in the San Jose and Milpitas community. Having grown up in an immigrant family, she understands how confusing and overwhelming healthcare can feel for families, and she’s here to change…

Whether your child needs a filling, a stainless steel crown, or space maintainers after an extraction, we provide gentle, effective treatments that restore oral health with care.
