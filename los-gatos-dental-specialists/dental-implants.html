What are dental implants?

Dental implants are tiny metal posts that stand in your jawbone to support restorations, which replace missing teeth. Common dental restorations include crowns, bridges, and dentures.

The dental implants act like the roots of your natural teeth, supporting both the restorations themselves and the jawbone where they sit. Without dental implants or the original tooth roots, the bone can weaken and deteriorate.

Over the course of a few months after your first implant surgery, a process called osseointegration happens. During this process, your natural jawbone fuses with the biocompatible titanium of the dental implant.

Once this happens, you have a sturdy base for your custom restorations from Los Gatos Dental Specialists.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Dental implants are an excellent option for replacing missing teeth, but not everyone can get them safely.

To find out if you’re a candidate for dental implants, you must come in for a consultation with Los Gatos Dental Specialists first. They take X-rays and pictures of your teeth to check for any conditions that need treatment before you can consider getting implants.
