Dr. Stephen Comstock

He had a previous career in architecture and was an illustrator for two medical textbooks including a handbook for periodontal disease control that was co-authored by our periodontist, Dr. Coleman. He received specialized training and certification in the treatment of orthodontic issues using Invisalign. Dr. Comstock uses Invisalign to help patients resolve crowding, spacing, and other orthodontic problems. He is dedicated to creating a calm atmosphere for his patients and empowering them to take control of their oral health.

The Personal Side

Outside of Drum Hill Dental, Dr. Comstock enjoys spending time with his wife and their four children. Dr. Comstock is a novice open-water swimmer, even swimming some races with his wife and son. While most of his free time outside of work involves cheering on his kids at swim meets and soccer games, he also enjoys home improvement and landscaping projects.
