Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays, a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s dental health, are used only when deemed necessary by the dentist. These x-rays emit a fraction of the normal radiation, ensuring a safer experience, especially for those who may have concerns about exposure. We also take extra precautions by using lead and thyroid apron shielding to further limit radiation.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We paint a clear, safe resin on your child&#x27;s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, keeping away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Nutritional Counseling

We believe in a "whole health" approach, which includes yummy, healthy food choices. It&#x27;s not always easy to change eating habits, but it&#x27;s definitely worth it. Eating well benefits not only your child&#x27;s smile but their overall wellbeing. Let us help guide you in creating tooth-friendly menus that the whole family will love!

Oral Habits

Building a strong foundation for healthy oral habits will establish your child&#x27;s dental routine for their entire lives. With our educational, compassionate care, we are here to give your child the guidance they need to have healthy dental hygiene that will stick with them for a lifetime.

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied to help strengthen their teeth. We use a special fluoride formulation that allows your child to eat and drink right away, so there&#x27;s no waiting period. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals and helping to protect their smile.

Pediatric Sedation Dentist

Curodont

Curodont offers patients a gentle, non-invasive way to treat early tooth decay without drills, needles, or removal of healthy tooth structure. By using advanced biomimetic technology to support natural enamel regeneration, it helps stop cavities in their earliest stages and preserve long-term tooth health. The result is a more comfortable experience, reduced anxiety, and a proactive approach to preventive dental care.

Pediatric Dental Emergencies & Injury Prevention

Early Orthodontic Screenings

At our pediatric dental practice, we believe healthy smiles start early. That’s why we offer early orthodontic screenings to monitor your child’s growth and development. By evaluating the alignment of teeth and jaw as early as age seven, we can identify potential concerns before they become more complex. Early screenings allow us to guide proper jaw growth, create space for emerging teeth, and address habits that may impact your child’s smile. Our goal is to provide proactive, gentle care that sets the foundation for a confident, healthy smile that lasts a lifetime.
