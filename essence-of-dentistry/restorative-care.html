Restorative Dentistry

Why Should I Get

Restorative Care?

Deciding to move forward with restorative care can help you maintain the health and functionality of your teeth and prevent future dental issues. Restorative treatments are designed to alleviate pain, adjust your bite, and restore the beauty of your smile, which can significantly improve your quality of life.

Fillings

Fillings are one of the most common restorative treatments. They’re used to repair minor tooth decay or fractures. We carefully match the filling material to your tooth color to guarantee a natural look and feel. This quick and effective treatment can save a tooth and prevent further decay.

Bridges

Bridges are used to fill the gap created by one or more missing teeth. They consist of crowns for the teeth on either side of the gap, with a replacement tooth or teeth in between. Bridges not only restore the appearance of your smile but also help distribute the forces in your bite properly by replacing missing teeth.

Dentures

Dentures are removable appliances that can replace missing teeth and help restore your smile. Whether you need a full set of dentures or partial ones to fill in the spaces, they are designed to look natural and feel comfortable, helping you to speak, eat, and smile with ease.

Root Canals

Root canal therapy is a procedure used to treat infection at the center of a tooth. It&#x27;s a way to save teeth that might otherwise need to be removed. The treatment involves removing the damaged area of the tooth, cleaning and disinfecting it, and then filling and sealing it to prevent further infection. This process alleviates pain and restores the tooth&#x27;s function.

Dental Implants

Dental implants offer a permanent solution for missing teeth. They involve placing a metal post directly into the jawbone to act as a root, onto which a custom-made crown is attached. Implants mimic the look, feel, and function of natural teeth and can last for decades with proper care.

Nitrous Sedation

Nitrous sedation, also known as laughing gas, is a safe and effective way to reduce anxiety during dental procedures. It&#x27;s administered through a mask that fits over your nose, allowing you to relax without putting you to sleep. The effects wear off quickly after the mask is removed, making it a convenient option for those who feel nervous about dental work.
