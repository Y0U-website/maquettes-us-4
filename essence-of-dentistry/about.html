Dr. Alison Han, DMD

For the past 23 years, Dr. Alison Han has worked at several dental clinics around the Redmond, WA area. Today, she is honored to bring the highest level of dentistry to her patients in Redmond. Dr. Han is fluent in Mandarin, Cantonese, French, English, and has basic Spanish skills, making her well-equipped to communicate with a wide range of patients.

Dr. Han completed her dental degree (DMD) at McGill University and went on to complete a residency in general practice at the University of Washington. She also studied at the Kois Center, a top-tier program for dentists looking to dive deeper into providing comprehensive patient care. This program covers everything from treating diseases to improving the look and function of patients&#x27; smiles — and it’s all grounded in evidence-based dentistry.

Driven by a love for learning and tackling complex health care problems, Dr. Han recently worked with a team to develop an AI tool aimed at making dental and medical offices run more smoothly. This project took over three years and involved creating a brand-new app from the ground up.

Aside from pursuing her passions, Dr. Han enjoys spending time with her two daughters. Through her eldest daughter, she continues to follow her love of crew. She is very involved with her youngest’s education and has gained extensive knowledge in neuroplastic therapies and research.

Dr. Grace Song, DDS

Dr. Grace Song earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California. After graduation, she relocated to Washington, where she continues to practice dentistry with a focus on patient-centered care and lasting oral health.

Dr. Song is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable in the dental chair while providing high-quality, comprehensive treatment tailored to each individual’s needs. In her free time, Dr. Song enjoys spending time outdoors and exploring new restaurants in the area. She also loves relaxing with her pets and cherishes quiet moments with family and friends.
