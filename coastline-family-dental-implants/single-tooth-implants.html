What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are an alternative option to dentures. Permanently cemented bridges can now be supported by strategically placed implants, removing the need for dentures and providing a more natural look to your smile.

A dental implant recreates the whole tooth structure, beginning with the root. Using a titanium implant post, a dental implant also restores the root of the tooth. Your jawbone will fuse with the post, giving a secure base for the restoration, which is affixed using an abutment. This procedure produces a near-perfect copy of your real teeth.
