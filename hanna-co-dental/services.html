Our Services

From preventive care to smile transformations, we offer a complete range of dental services tailored to your unique needs.

General Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care for the whole family. Our general dentistry services include routine check-ups, cleanings, fillings, and preventive care to keep your smile healthy and bright. We use the latest techniques and technology to ensure your comfort during every visit.

Pediatric Dentistry

We love helping children build healthy smiles in a fun, comfortable, and welcoming environment. Our team is dedicated to making every visit a positive experience, helping kids feel relaxed while developing healthy dental habits that last a lifetime. With TVs in every treatment room, nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for added comfort when needed, and a dentist with advanced training in caring for young patients, families can feel confident their child is receiving compassionate, high-quality dental care.

Orthodontics

Straighten your smile and improve your bite with personalized orthodontic treatment designed to fit your lifestyle. As a preferred Invisalign® provider, we offer high-quality clear aligner therapy to help you achieve a healthier, more confident smile—comfortably and discreetly.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with our premium cosmetic dentistry services. From porcelain veneers and dental bonding to smile makeovers, we craft stunning results tailored to your unique facial features. Rediscover your confidence with a radiant, natural-looking smile.

Teeth Whitening

Achieve a dazzling, luminous smile with our professional teeth whitening treatments. Our in-office and take-home whitening options deliver dramatic results safely and effectively. Say goodbye to stains and discoloration — your brightest smile awaits.

Sleep Apnea Care

Enjoy more restful sleep with personalized treatment for snoring and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). We provide comfortable, custom oral appliance therapy to help reduce snoring, improve sleep quality, and support your overall health. Untreated sleep apnea has been linked to serious health conditions, including an increased risk of high blood pressure, heart disease, heart attack, and stroke.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies can happen when you least expect them, and we re here when you need us most. Whether you re experiencing a severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, or another urgent dental concern, our team provides prompt, compassionate care to relieve pain, restore your smile, and get you back to feeling your best.

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth cannot be saved, we provide gentle, comfortable tooth extractions with your health and comfort as our top priorities. Using modern techniques and a compassionate approach, we help make the procedure as stress-free as possible while guiding you through your tooth replacement options to restore your smile.

Botox

Enhance your smile and refresh your appearance with professional Botox® treatments provided by a trained dental provider. Botox can help soften the appearance of fine lines, relax overactive facial muscles, and address concerns such as jaw tension and TMJ-related discomfort. Our approach focuses on natural-looking results while helping you feel confident and comfortable.
