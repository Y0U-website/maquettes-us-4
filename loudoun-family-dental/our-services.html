Dental Implants

If one or more missing teeth have impeded your oral health, dental implants may be right for you. Dental implants are made of materials that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They are carefully placed and anchored in the gap left by missing teeth, restoring the patient’s ability to use their mouth. Dental implants are ideal for patients who’ve lost some teeth but still have many natural teeth remaining.

Dental Veneers

Dental veneers return teeth to a shimmering white color and polish. They are often made of porcelain and are applied to the surface of a tooth. Dental veneers correct the effects of discoloration and permanent staining, and they can also reshape the tooth after chipping and cracking. You can expect your veneers to remain durable and withstand wear and tear for years. They are best suited for patients who want to correct their teeth’s cosmetics and appearance.

Dentures

We offer and apply full and partial dentures to patients depending on the significance of their tooth loss. Full dentures are best for patients with most or all their teeth missing, and they can be applied to either the top or bottom arch. Partials are used to replace sections of gaps with at least some natural teeth intact. We can examine your teeth’s condition and determine if full or partial dentures are better for you.

Invisalign®

With Invisalign®, you can correct misaligned teeth without drawing attention to your treatment. This innovative dental solution is among the clearest and most transparent in dentistry. They comprise nearly invisible, removable trays that gradually straighten and align the teeth over time. Feel free to take them out while eating or smiling and put them back in for continued alignment when you’re done. You’ll receive top-quality results after treatment without having to think about or see the progress you’re making.

Dental Emergencies

Loudoun Family Dental is here to treat you when the unexpected happens. We offer same-day and walk-in emergency dental appointments for patients of all ages. Urgent dental issues can occur for many reasons, ranging from sudden tooth trauma to longstanding, underlying issues. If your tooth has been damaged or you’re experiencing chronic and severe toothaches, our team will see you for an appointment. You can depend on us to restore your health, alleviate pain, and get you back to your normal life.
