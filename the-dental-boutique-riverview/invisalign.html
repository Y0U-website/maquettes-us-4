What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is a clear orthodontic aligner system that is used to straighten teeth without the use of traditional metal braces. The aligners are made of a smooth, comfortable material that is virtually invisible when worn. Each pair is custom-made to fit over your teeth and provide light pressure that gradually moves your teeth into the desired position.

Benefits of Invisalign Treatment

There are many benefits to choosing Invisalign for your orthodontic treatment. One of the main benefits is that the aligners are virtually invisible, which means you can straighten your teeth without anyone knowing there’s something over your teeth.

Plus, Invisalign aligners are removable, which means you can eat, drink, and brush your teeth without any major changes to your daily routine. Simply remove them during meals and when it’s time to brush or floss.

Wearing Invisalign is more comfortable than traditional braces, as they do not have any wires or brackets that can irritate your gums and cheeks. The custom-contoured shape fits snuggly to your teeth, so you never have to worry about something coming loose or metal rubbing the inside of your mouth.

The Invisalign Process

During your Invisalign consultation, we will carefully examine your teeth and discuss any specific bite misalignment concerns. We’ll also use advanced digital mapping and 3D scanning technology to create a customized treatment plan built around your unique smile.

Invisalign software will use the impressions to create a digital mock-up of your proposed treatment. From there, a series of 3D-printed clear aligners are specifically designed to move your teeth into the desired position.

Most Invisalign treatments average about 12 months from start to finish, but the length can vary from person to person. You’ll want to plan to visit our office every 4-6 weeks to check your progress and receive your next few sets of aligners.

Wear each set of aligners for 22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. You’ll transition to the next pair of aligners in the series once every two weeks.

Who Qualifies for Invisalign Treatment?

Invisalign is appropriate for most patients over the age of 12, including adults of all ages. Investing in Invisalign can also help with conditions such as TMJ disorder, risk of tooth decay, and periodontal infections.

Choosing to straighten your smile with Invisalign can help boost your oral health as well as your self-confidence. When you feel more comfortable with the way your smile looks, you’ll feel more relaxed sharing it with the people around you.
