What Are Dental Veneers?

The veneer is a type of "false face," that hides cosmetic flaws or staining in your smile. They conceal your front teeth, allowing everyone to see your confident smile whenever you laugh or talk. Porcelain veneers from The Dental Boutique in Riverview, FL can be placed side by side, often on 6-10 upper front teeth, depending on how wide your smile is. This broader approach allows us to enhance multiple aspects of your smile in a consistent approach to care.

Dental veneers are made of long-lasting, high-quality porcelain and provide consistent brightness over time with no risk of staining. Veneers can make your smile appear brighter and more symmetrical by correcting any issues such as tooth shape or enamel irregularities behind them. The porcelain is also durable, allowing you to eat a typical diet with only a few minor modifications.

Caring for Your Porcelain Veneers

Despite the fact that dental veneers are made from porcelain, they are still bonded to your natural smile. Brushing and flossing on a daily basis are essential for keeping them clean and supporting the health of your teeth behind them. Plan to keep your six month checkups as usual. If you have a habit of clenching your teeth, we recommend wearing a nightguard.

  • How long do porcelain veneers last? Porcelain veneers can last up to 20 years with proper care, while composite veneers can last up to 10 years. It’s important to practice good oral hygiene and avoid habits that can damage veneers, such as biting nails or chewing on hard objects. Regular dental checkups and cleanings can also help extend the lifespan of veneers.
