The practice

Individualized Dental Care We work as a team, dedicated to improving and maintaining your oral health. Whatever your dental needs, you will be in great hands.

If you are looking to be treated by the same dentist at all your visits, you’ll be happy to know Dr. Alvarez is the sole practitioner in the office. Have peace of mind knowing your trusted doctor is there for you every time.

Experienced Dental Professionals Our team has the professional experience to realize that there is no such thing as one-size-fits-all approach to treatment.

Dr. Alvarez has been practicing in his private office in Coral Springs/Parkland since 2008; and has 20 years of experience in the area. He is a graduate of NSU Dental School and the University of Florida.

He has extensive experience and performs a wide variety of dental services, specializing in family, cosmetic, and implant dentistry.
