The practice

Dr. Mike Choi was raised in Los Angeles. He graduated from the University of Southern California with a major in Biological Sciences and a minor in Sociology. After his undergraduate studies, he attended the USC School of Dentistry. He then completed an advanced specialty program to become a board eligible Prosthodontist.

Prosthodontics is a dental specialty that focuses on diagnosis, treatment planning, rehabilitation and maintenance of oral function, comfort, appearance and health of patients with clinical conditions associated with missing or deficient teeth. Prosthodontists undergo 3 years of additional training to restore oral function to an expert level using implants, crowns, bridges, veneers, inlays…

Dr. Choi was an Associate Professor of Clinical Dentistry at the University of Southern California, School of Dentistry before focusing on private practice. Dr. Choi is a member of the American College of Prosthodontists the American Dental Association, California Dental Association and the Santa Barbara-Ventura County Dental Society.

In his spare time, Dr. Choi likes to spend time with his wife and 2 children. He also enjoys photography and participating in vintage car rallies.

Prosthodontics is one of the nine recognized specialties of dentistry and it benefits you by concentrating on the whole mouth. Instead of just treating one tooth at a time, Dr. Choi focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and maintenance of the entire oral function. Creation of a scientifically-based treatment plan. Scientifically-based treatment plans are seldom covered in regular dental schools, while they are taught in-depth to prosthodontists.
