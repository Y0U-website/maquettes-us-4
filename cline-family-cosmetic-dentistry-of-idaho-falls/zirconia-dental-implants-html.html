Restore Your Smile with Lifelike Zirconia Dental Implants

For patients looking to restore their smile with dental implants but for whom titanium posts are not an option, zirconia implants can give your smile a second chance. These metal-free implants are safe for those with metal allergies, or who simply want to limit their exposure to metal. They are also beneficial for patients with gum sensitivity, who are prone to plaque buildup, or who simply want a more aesthetic restoration for teeth that are visible when you smile or speak.

What Are Zirconia Dental Implants?

Zirconia dental implants are made of a white crystalline oxide of zirconium, which is a ceramic. It is an ideal material for use in dental restorations because it is highly resistant to cracking, thermal expansion, and even corrosion. It is sometimes used to make metal-free dental crowns due to its incredible strength, durability and the ability to color match it to your natural teeth.

Conversely, titanium dental implants offer the following benefits over zirconia:

  • Zirconia is stronger than titanium and highly biocompatible.
  • Zirconia implants are white and blend in more naturally with teeth.
  • Zirconia is better at inhibiting the growth and adhesion of bacteria, making it ideal for abutments.
  • Titanium implants have been used longer and have more long-term data available on their benefits and safety.
  • Titanium implants are generally more affordable than zirconia.

Are Zirconia Dental Implants Right for You?

Ultimately, Dr. Cline will be happy to help you determine which option is best for your smile based on your individual oral health, budget, and goals. However, if you fit the following criteria, durable, lifelike zirconia dental implants may be right for you, if you:

  • Are sensitive to or would like to limit your exposure to metal.
  • Would like a more aesthetic restoration for smile-visible teeth.
  • Are prone to tooth and gum sensitivity.
  • Struggle with excess plaque and bacteria buildup.
