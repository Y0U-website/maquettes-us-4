What to Expect at Your First Visit

Here are a few tips that will make your first visit to our Palm Beach Gardens dental office a little easier.

Upon first entering the office, you will be greeted by one of our knowledgeable office staff members. You will be introduced to one of our highly skilled dental assistants who will walk you through your first visit. Dr. Nijem, or Dr. Laszlo will perform an exam of the hard and soft tissue areas of the mouth and develop a personalized treatment plan that will address your needs and wants.

It may be necessary to take X-rays so that we may accurately assess your oral health. We will then discuss how we may sequence your treatment plan so that you can achieve a beautiful healthy smile in a timely fashion. Our friendly treatment coordinators will help you to schedule your appointments and assist with financial arrangements, when necessary.

Patient comforts

Mild Sedation

In addition to the utilization of a mild sedative, our office is designed to help people relax. Our entire team is warm, and they really listen to their welcome patients. This service provides a way for people to let go of their fears of visiting the dentist. This method is safe and helps many get the dental attention that they deserve. We provide a serene atmosphere to make your visit all the more pleasant. After trying this service, you may actually look forward to your next visit. Patients come first at this compassionate practice.

Our compassionate staff understands that not everyone loves going to the dentist office. We will work with you to make you feel comfortable, cared for, and right at home in our dental office. We are not a large chain where you will see a different doctor at each visit. Our doctors each have decades of experience in the dental field and are all graduates of the University of Florida Dental School.

Our office is conveniently located directly across from the Palm Beach Gardens Hospital on Burns Road. Located between Alternate A1A and Prosperity Farms Road, we are easy to find from I-95 and the Florida Turnpike. Whether you are coming from Jupiter or Palm Beach, Wellington or West Palm, our office is easy to find with ample parking.

We are located on the second floor of the Gardens Professional Arts Building in Suite 212, which is at the north side of the building. The building has ramps and elevators, making it easily accessible for those in wheelchairs or walkers. Our office has several operatories that are large enough to accommodate patients who are unable to move from their wheelchairs.
