Dr. Raj Patel, DMD

A proud Florida native, Dr. Patel grew up in Panama City Beach and graduated from J.R. Arnold High School. He went on to earn his bachelor’s degree in Biology (with a minor in Business Administration) from the University of Florida— Go Gators! —and received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Bradenton, Florida.

After practicing in Jacksonville, he and Dr. Yassar Patel relocated to Destin to be closer to family and serve the community through comprehensive, compassionate dental care.

A Personalized Approach to Dentistry

Dr. Patel’s philosophy is simple: treat every patient the way you’d treat a member of your own family. That means creating a welcoming environment, listening closely to your concerns, and guiding you through treatment with honesty and clarity. Whether you&#x27;re coming in for a routine dental exam , cleaning , or more advanced care, Dr. Patel ensures that every step is tailored to your comfort, health goals, and long-term oral wellness.

Services & Clinical Interests

Dr. Patel provides care across a wide range of dental services, with a focus on:

Preventive care like exams, cleanings, fluoride, and oral cancer screenings

Restorative dentistry including fillings , crowns & bridges , and root canals

Implant dentistry including dental implants , bone grafting, and implant restorations

Emergency dental care for sudden pain, injuries, or infections

TMJ and facial pain treatment , including night guards and Botox therapy

Committed to Your Comfort and Confidence

Dr. Patel is known by patients for his gentle, down-to-earth demeanor and clear communication . He takes pride in helping patients of all ages feel at ease and empowered in their dental care. Whether you’re new to the area or just looking for a dental home that feels more personal, Dr. Patel is here to help you smile with confidence.

Outside the Office

When he’s not caring for patients, Dr. Patel enjoys staying active and spending time outdoors. He’s a lifelong sports fan—especially of all things Boston—and loves playing basketball and tennis. He also enjoys movies, game nights, and hanging out with his two Bengal cats, Major and Siren.
