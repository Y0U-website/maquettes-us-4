Education & Training

Originally from Erie, Pennsylvania, Dr. Yassar completed her undergraduate studies at Gannon University , earning a bachelor’s degree in Biology. She went on to earn her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Bradenton, Florida.

After practicing in Jacksonville, she relocated to the Destin area to continue her dental career closer to family and the beautiful Gulf Coast.

A Passion for Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry

Dr. Yassar is especially passionate about the art and precision of cosmetic and restorative procedures. With a background in fine arts and a love for aesthetics, she’s able to combine technical expertise with visual harmony—crafting smiles that are both functional and beautiful .

Cosmetic bonding for chipped or uneven teeth

Smile makeovers using whitening , bonding, and reshaping

Dental crowns and bridges for restoring strength and beauty

Night guards and TMJ therapy to relieve jaw tension and protect teeth

Her work is guided by a belief that dentistry is not one-size-fits-all —and that everyone deserves treatment as unique as their smile.

Compassionate, Patient-Centered Care

Dr. Yassar’s approach to dentistry is rooted in empathy, communication, and trust . She takes the time to listen, answer questions, and ensure patients feel informed and empowered at every stage of treatment.

She works closely with patients of all ages—from children visiting for their first dental exam to adults restoring their oral health with implants , fillings , or dentures .

Outside the Office

When she’s not caring for smiles, Dr. Yassar enjoys expressing her creativity through art and design —especially working with acrylic paints and Prismacolor pencils . She also loves hiking, rollerblading, karaoke, and playing board games.

Dr. Yassar looks forward to welcoming you to Serenity Smiles and helping you achieve a healthier, more confident smile—through care that’s every bit as personal as it is professional .
