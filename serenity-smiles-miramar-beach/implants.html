Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

Patients choose implants over other tooth-replacement options for several reasons:

  • A natural look and feel that blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth
  • Improved chewing function compared to removable dentures
  • Long-term durability, often lasting decades with proper care
  • Preservation of jawbone health, which helps maintain facial structure
  • No need for adhesives or nightly removal, unlike traditional dentures

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long does the dental implant process take?

The full process, from placement to final crown, typically takes several months to allow the implant to fuse with the jawbone. Your dentist will provide a personalized timeline based on your specific case.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report that the procedure is more comfortable than expected, with manageable discomfort during recovery that can be controlled with standard pain relief.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and good oral hygiene, dental implants can last decades and, in many cases, a lifetime.

What happens if I don&#x27;t have enough jawbone for an implant?

We may recommend a bone grafting procedure to build up the jawbone before implant placement, giving the implant a strong, stable foundation.
