Disability-Friendly Dental…

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time project. We review new content and features against the same standard, and we welcome feedback that helps us improve.

  • Visible labels on all form fields, and form confirmations that are announced to screen readers
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Improved color contrast throughout the site
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences, and a pause control for moving content
  • Page landmarks, headings, and a skip-to-content link for assistive technology users

Found a problem? Please tell us

If any part of this website is difficult for you to use, we want to know about it. We take accessibility reports seriously and will work to address problems promptly. And if you’re unable to access any information on this site, contact us — our team will gladly provide it another way, over the phone or in whatever format works for you.
