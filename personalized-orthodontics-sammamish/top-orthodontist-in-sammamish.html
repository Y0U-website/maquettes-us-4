What are the benefits of orthodontics?

Orthodontic treatments are often associated with two general benefits: functional (correcting the bite and straightening the teeth) and esthetic (mostly alignment of the teeth).

Benefits of orthodontic treatment depends on the complexity of the problems. Our new patients learn about all the issues with their teeth and bite. We also address the benefits of correcting each issues. For example, patients with severe crowding can floss and improve their dental health and avoid long-term bone loss around crowded teeth.

Patient with minor issues the benefit can be as simple as building confidence which indirectly improve the psychosocial health. Working with numerous cosmetic dentists on an interdisciplinary treatment plans, patients enjoy the collaboration of orthodontic care in conjunction with a restorative plan to place veneers in the best position or a building an implant-supported crown.
