Understanding Invisalign

Invisalign is one type of aligner systems which are alternative treatment option for traditional metal braces.

Align technology was the first company that developed Invisalign clear aligners for teeth straightening. Invisalign aligners work through gradual tooth movement by each set of aligners. Removable aligners move the teeth by placing forces on the teeth.

Similar to traditional braces, these clear aligners gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. The catch is that you are in charge of wearing clear aligners close to 22 hours a day.

A digital impression is required to start the treatment process by creating series of aligners. Patient starts with a new set of aligners and transition to the next set often every 7 days.

Why choose Invisalign treatment?

While Invisalign does require a degree of discipline, the benefits it offers outweigh those of traditional braces. It doesn’t matter if you are closing spacing between the teeth or a comprehensive Invisalign teen (aligners for teens) treatment.

Its most apparent advantage lies in its inconspicuousness, providing a less noticeable alternative for orthodontic correction.

Furthermore, due to their removability, these aligners facilitate improved oral hygiene and fewer dietary restrictions. However, neglecting oral hygiene with clear aligners can lead to significant cavities requiring extensive dental care.

An alternative to Invisalign is lingual braces, invisible braces placed behind your teeth that provide similar aesthetic benefits of Invisalign without the process’s challenges.

Why Invisalign orthodontist is better than Invisalign dentist?

Selecting the right Invisalign provider is a crucial step towards achieving your dream smile. Accessibility to a great dental health and high quality dentistry is vital.

Rather than settling for any provider for your orthodontic care, it’s recommended to choose an orthodontist with a robust orthodontic background and extensive experience with clear aligners.

Orthodontists possess a competitive edge in understanding the complexities of tooth movement. This expertise is gained through an additional 2-3 years of specialized training beyond their dental degree (DDS/DMD). This extra academic rigour endows them with a more profound comprehension of orthodontics and treatments like Invisalign.

While a general dentist (and some cosmetic dentists) might offer a lower cost of Invisalign treatment , this could suggest a less thorough approach to your care. Therefore, it’s prudent to consult with various providers, understand their proposed treatment plan, and ensure your comfort and confidence in your choice before committing.
