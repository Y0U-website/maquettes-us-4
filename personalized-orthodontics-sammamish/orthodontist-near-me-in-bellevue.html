Professional Qualifications

Qualifications are the first thing to look for when choosing an orthodontist.

Many dentists claim to offer orthodontic treatment, but actual orthodontists are specialists in their field.

Dentists often have letters like DMD or DDS after their name. DMD stands for Doctor of Medical Dentistry. DDS means Doctor of Dental Surgery. A qualified orthodontist will have a Master of Science in Dentistry (MSD) or be a Certified Orthodontic Specialist. They will also have a DDS or DMD title. Orthodontists are dentists who pursue additional education and training in a orthodontic specialty field .

In addition to checking an orthodontist’s credentials, you should look for other signs of their skill.

A qualified and experienced orthodontist is someone you can trust. They can help you get the beautiful smile you want.

  • Does the orthodontist have board certification?
  • Have they been practicing for a couple of years or for decades?
  • Have they contributed to any research in their field?
  • Do they help educate, train, or mentor other dental professionals?

Clear Details and Policies

When you look at an orthodontist’s information online, pay attention to how easy it is to find details. A good orthodontic practice will have a lot of information online. This makes it easy for you to reach out to them.

Treatment Approach

You can learn a lot about an orthodontist’s treatment style from patient reviews and the information on their website.

Some orthodontists are serious, some are light-hearted and make the treatment experience fun. Some cater to kids, while others prefer treating adults. Some orthodontists use traditional methods. Others are always seeking new technologies to improve the treatment experience.
