The practice

At The Cabana, we pride ourselves on fostering a culture that is as warm and welcoming as our sun-kissed surroundings. Our team comprises a dynamic group of entrepreneurs, each bringing their unique talents and expertise to the table, all united under the banner of The Cabana. Together, we are dedicated to providing you with unparalleled service and an unforgettable spa experience.

The Cabana s inception traces back to the vision of our founder, Hannah Hepler, a licensed esthetician. What began as Hannah Cabana Tans, LLC, a bespoke spray tanning business, soon evolved into something much greater. With the addition of laser teeth whitening services, Hannah recognized the potential to create a holistic spa experience that went beyond conventional boundaries. Thus, The Cabana…

Whether you re seeking a personalized beauty treatment or simply looking to unwind, The Cabana is your ultimate destination for indulgence. Step into our oasis of serenity and let us pamper you from head to toe. Your journey to total bliss begins here, at The Cabana.

We look forward to welcoming you into our tranquil retreat and providing you with an experience that is nothing short of extraordinary.
