About Us

Our friendly team at River Rock Dental Group is committed to providing you with quality care in a comfortable, welcoming environment. When you visit us, you can expect to receive personalized treatment and for us to do our utmost to ensure that you feel at ease. To make your appointment with our dentist and learn more about comprehensive dental care in Belgrade, Montana, please call us today at 406-388-0033 . We look forward to meeting you soon!

Dr. Timothy Lund attended dental school at Creighton University, achieving his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2004. While attending dental school, he won many student awards and gained significant clinical experience, especially in the areas of implant dentistry and full mouth reconstruction. He has completed advanced training in complex implant cases and digital implant placement. View Full Bio

Melissa joined our practice in 2016. She has been a dental assistant since 2004, and was an oral surgery assistant for 6 years. She originally studied nursing at Montana State University in Bozeman before entering the dental field. She is a certified dental assistant, and also has CPR certification. She married Dr. Lund in 2016, and now has 2 dogs named Gus and Bailey. She loves to work with her husband and considers the rest of the team family. Outside of work she loves cooking and baking, camping, hunting, and spending time with her family.
