COVID-19 INFO

Your Dental Health and COVID-19

Infection control has always been a top priority in our practice. We will continue to follow the guidelines that are given by the state for the safety of our patients and staff. As of April 3rd, 2023, some of the enhanced protocols that have taken place at your previous appointment may no longer be in force. We will always continue to recognized the advice given from the CDC to help us establish a safe environment.

We want to thank you for your commitment to our practice and most importantly to your oral health and overall health in general. Please continue to follow the advise of the scientific community and if there are any questions you may have please ask our doctors. We should all be expecting for more unexpected situations to turn up and hopefully we will still have your support in the future. Your health remains our priority and the reason we remain committed to our community for close to 40 years now. We look forward to seeing you and if you have any questions please call our office.

  • After April 3rd 2023 you will no longer need to wear a mask although we will still have them available for patient use. Our clinical staff will continue to wear a level three mask or higher.
  • If you are ill and having symptoms please call to reschedule. We will do our best to get you back in for an appointment once you are feeling better. If you have a toothache while experiencing symptoms please call and we will schedule you accordingly.
  • Our added air purifying systems in the office will remain in place.
  • Please make sure you are making your appointment, if you need to cancel please give us at least 24 hours notice in advance
  • If you are a higher risk individual please call and we can help accommodate your appointment to help reduce risk even further.
