Dr. Pablo R. Prado, DMD: Dedicated Family Dentist in Lake Worth, FL

Meet Dr. Pablo R. Prado , a highly skilled and compassionate dentist originally from Peru, who has been proudly serving the South Florida community for years. Known for his warm demeanor, exceptional care, and commitment to staying at the forefront of dental advancements, Dr. Prado provides comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages in a comfortable and welcoming environment at Prestige Dental Care .

Education and Expertise: A Path to Excellence

Dr. Prado’s journey into the world of dentistry began right here in South Florida . After completing high school, he pursued his Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences: Molecular Biology at Florida Atlantic University , where he developed a passion for oral health care. This strong academic foundation fueled his ambition to make a tangible difference in people’s lives. Dr. Prado then attended the highly prestigious University of Florida College of Dentistry , where he earned his Doctorate in Dental Medicine (DMD) and laid the groundwork for what would become an illustrious dental career.

Dental Crowns: A Core Treatment in Dr. Prado’s Practice

One of the common treatments performed by Dr. Prado is the placement of dental crowns , which are crucial for restoring the function and appearance of damaged teeth. Dental crowns are a key treatment option for those who suffer from extensive decay or tooth injury. Crowns provide a protective cap over a compromised tooth, restoring its integrity while improving aesthetics.
