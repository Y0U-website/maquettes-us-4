Single-Tooth Implants

Throughout the process, our team in Arlington, Texas, is committed to ensuring your comfort and satisfaction at every step.

  • Implant Placement: In a subsequent visit, our dentists will place the titanium implant into the jawbone. We use advanced techniques to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.
  • Healing Period: Your jawbone will need time to heal and integrate with the implant. During this period, you will have follow-up appointments to monitor progress and ensure everything is healing properly.
  • Abutment Attachment: Once healing is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant. This small device connects the implant to your new tooth.
  • Crown Placement: Finally, a custom-made crown is placed onto the abutment. This crown is designed to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, providing a seamless and natural look.

Benefits of Single-Tooth Dental Implants

Single-tooth dental implants in Arlington, Texas, offer several advantages that contribute to improved oral health and quality of life. These implants provide a reliable solution for missing teeth.

  • They restore your smile with a natural appearance and secure fit, enhancing confidence and comfort.
  • Improved chewing efficiency ensures you can enjoy a varied diet without restrictions, promoting better nutrition.
  • Unlike traditional bridges , implants do not affect neighboring teeth, preserving overall dental health.
  • A dental implant helps maintain jawbone integrity, reducing the risk of bone loss commonly associated with missing teeth.
  • Durable and long-lasting, implants are a cost-effective choice, minimizing the need for frequent replacements.
