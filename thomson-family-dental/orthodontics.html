Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

At what age should my child have their first orthodontic evaluation?

We recommend that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early evaluation allows us to identify and address potential issues before they become more serious, which can simplify treatment and improve outcomes.

How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?

The length of orthodontic treatment varies depending on the complexity of the case and the type of treatment chosen. On average, treatment with braces or clear aligners can take anywhere from 12 to 24 months. During your consultation, we’ll provide a more accurate estimate based on your specific needs.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Clear aligners, like Invisalign®, are highly effective for treating a range of orthodontic issues, particularly mild to moderate cases. However, traditional braces may be recommended for more complex cases. Our dentists will help you determine the best treatment option based on your specific situation.

It’s common to experience some discomfort after braces are first applied or after adjustments. This discomfort typically lasts for a few days and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most patients find that they adjust to wearing braces within a short period.

How often will I need to visit the dentist during orthodontic treatment?

Patients with braces typically need to visit the dentist every 4 to 6 weeks for adjustments. If you’re using clear aligners, you may have check-ups every 6 to 8 weeks to monitor progress and receive new aligner trays.
