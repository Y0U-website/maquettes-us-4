If you’ve recently completed treatment through our clear aligners or other orthodontic services , a retainer is a critical next step.

  • Protect the results of braces or clear aligners
  • Support bone and tissue stabilization after treatment

Types of Retainers We Offer

Every smile is different, which is why we offer multiple retainer options based on your needs and lifestyle.

Removable Retainers

These are custom-crafted appliances made from clear or acrylic materials. They are easy to remove for eating and cleaning while still providing reliable support.

Fixed Retainers

Also known as bonded retainers, these are placed behind the teeth and remain in place for continuous retention. They are ideal for patients who want a low-maintenance solution.

Our team will help you determine which option best fits your goals and daily routine.

The Retainer Process at Thomson Family Dental

Getting a retainer is a simple and efficient process designed to ensure long-term success.

Post-Treatment Evaluation

We assess your teeth after orthodontic treatment to determine the most appropriate retention method.

Digital Impressions

Accurate digital scans are taken to create a retainer that fits comfortably and securely.

Custom Fabrication

Your retainer is crafted using high-quality materials for durability and precision.

Delivery and Guidance

Once your retainer is ready, we ensure a proper fit and provide detailed instructions for wear and care.

Routine visits, including dental hygiene appointments , allow us to monitor your retainer and ensure continued effectiveness.

Protecting Your Smile for the Long Term

Retainers are a long-term commitment that play a vital role in preserving your smile. Consistent use helps prevent the need for additional dental or orthodontic work in the future.

If shifting has already occurred, we may recommend corrective options through our restorative dentistry services or orthodontic solutions to realign your teeth.

Retainer Care Tips

Proper care will extend the life of your retainer and keep it functioning properly:

Following these steps helps ensure your retainer remains comfortable and effective.

  • Clean your retainer daily using a soft toothbrush
  • Store it in a protective case when not in use
  • Avoid exposure to heat to prevent warping
  • Bring it to your dental visits for routine evaluation

FAQs About Retainers

How long will I need to wear my retainer?

Most patients wear retainers full-time initially, then transition to nighttime use. Long-term wear is typically recommended to maintain alignment.

What happens if I don’t wear my retainer?

Teeth can gradually shift out of place, potentially undoing the results of orthodontic treatment.

Are retainers comfortable?

Yes. Custom retainers are designed to fit your teeth precisely, making them easy to wear once you adjust.

Can retainers be replaced if lost or damaged?

Absolutely. If your retainer is lost or broken, contact our office promptly to have it replaced and prevent shifting.
