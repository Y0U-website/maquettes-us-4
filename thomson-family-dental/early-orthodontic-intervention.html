Signs Your Child May Need Early Orthodontic Intervention

  • Crowded or Misaligned Teeth: If your child’s teeth appear crowded or misaligned, early evaluation can determine if intervention is needed.
  • Protruding Teeth: Teeth that stick out can be more prone to injury and may require early correction.
  • Difficulty Biting or Chewing: If your child has trouble biting or chewing, it could be a sign of a bite problem that needs attention.
  • Mouth Breathing or Snoring: These can be signs of airway issues that may be related to jaw alignment problems.

The Early Orthodontic Intervention Process

Initial Evaluation

We begin with a comprehensive orthodontic evaluation, including X-rays and digital imaging, to assess your child’s dental and skeletal development.

Customized Treatment Plan

Based on the evaluation, we’ll develop a personalized treatment plan that may include space maintainers, partial braces, or other orthodontic appliances to guide the development of your child’s teeth and jaws.

Monitoring and Adjustments

As your child grows, we’ll monitor their progress and make any necessary adjustments to their treatment plan, ensuring the best possible outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions About Early Orthodontic Intervention

At what age should my child have their first orthodontic evaluation?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. This allows us to identify and address any issues early.

Will my child need braces after early intervention?

Early intervention can reduce the need for braces later on, but some children may still require braces as they grow. However, the treatment may be simpler and shorter.

Is early orthodontic treatment covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans cover early orthodontic treatment, but coverage can vary. We can help you understand your benefits and explore payment options.
