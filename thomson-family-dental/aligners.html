What to Expect at Thomson Family Dental

Getting started is straightforward. Here is how the process works from your first visit through the end of treatment.

1. Consultation and Evaluation

One of our dentists will evaluate your teeth, bite, and overall oral health to confirm that clear aligners are the right fit for your goals. If you have any existing concerns such as cavities or gum issues, we may recommend addressing those first. Our dental hygiene team can help get your mouth in the best possible shape before treatment begins.

2. Custom Treatment Plan

We use 3D imaging technology to map the precise movement of your teeth throughout treatment. You will be able to see a virtual preview of your results before a single aligner is made. Our dental technology allows us to plan your treatment with a high degree of accuracy.

3. Aligner Fabrication

Your custom aligners are fabricated based on your treatment plan. Each tray is designed to move your teeth a small, controlled amount so the process is gradual and comfortable.

4. Progress Checkups

We schedule periodic checkups to confirm your teeth are moving as planned and to provide your next sets of aligners. You will wear each set for about 22 hours a day, removing them only to eat and clean your teeth.

5. Retention

Once treatment is complete, we fit you for a retainer to keep your teeth in their new position. Wearing your retainer as directed is the key to lasting results.

Frequently Asked Questions About Clear Aligners

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Treatment time varies based on the complexity of your case. Most patients complete treatment in 6 to 18 months. During your consultation, your dentist will give you a more specific estimate based on your 3D scan.

Do clear aligners hurt?

Most patients feel mild pressure for a day or two when switching to a new set of trays. This is normal and a sign that the aligners are working. The discomfort is generally much less than what patients experience with traditional braces.

Can I eat and drink with aligners in?

You should remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This prevents staining and keeps the trays in good condition. Because they come out easily, most patients find this a much more comfortable experience than eating with traditional braces.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

For mild to moderate cases, clear aligners produce results comparable to traditional braces. For a deeper look at how the two compare, see our guide on straightening teeth without traditional metal braces . The key to success with aligners is wearing your trays for the recommended 22 hours per day.

How much do clear aligners cost in Thomson, GA?

The cost depends on the complexity of your case and the length of treatment. Thomson Family Dental accepts most major dental insurance plans and can discuss financing options at your consultation. Contact our office to get an accurate estimate based on your specific needs.

What happens after treatment is complete?

After treatment, you will be fitted with a retainer to hold your teeth in place. Teeth naturally want to shift back over time, so consistent retainer wear is important for maintaining your results long term.
