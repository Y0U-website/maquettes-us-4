Meet Fulshear Dentist

Why Did You Become a Dentist?

Dr. Fifadara actually comes from a family of oral healthcare professionals her father, her uncles, and her cousins are all dentists too! Seeing this, it’s easy to see that dentistry runs deep in her blood, and she was inspired to follow in their footsteps from a young age. She also a genuine passion for helping others and greatly enjoys the artistic side of the work as well. This field was truly a perfect fit for her.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Fifadara chose to attend Tufts University of Dental Medicine in Boston, graduating in 2009. Following the completion of her doctorate, she pursued a one-year residency in general dentistry at Queens Hospital in Jamaica, NY. She also has a Certificate in Advanced Dental Implant Research & Education and is a certified provider for both Invisalign Clear Braces and Lumineers.

Having lived in different cities, Dr. Fifadara has gained valuable experience in all facets of dentistry, from cosmetic treatment to oral surgery. As a true dental professional, she makes it a priority to stay up-to-date on the latest advancements in the field in order to provide the most current care options to her valued patients.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Originally from Albany, NY, Dr. Fifadara has a very kind, loving, and supportive family who taught her the importance of education from a young age. She and her husband have two wonderful children. In her free time, she enjoys traveling, practicing photography, and spending quality time with loved ones. She really loves to dance too!
