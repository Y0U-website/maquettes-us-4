What Should You Expect During a Routine Dental Cleaning?

We strive to deliver happy, healthy smiles! Depending on the condition of your teeth and gums, your dental cleaning may include the following:

Dental Scaling: We will start by removing plaque and tartar buildup using a dental instrument called a scaler. At Three Stones Dentistry, we use hand scalers and an ultrasonic scaler that vibrates at a low frequency creating sound waves that blast away the most stubborn plaque and tartar deposits from your teeth and gum line.

Polishing and Flossing: A sophisticated electric toothbrush and special-engineered toothpaste remove any excess plaque and surface stains, leaving your teeth looking whiter and brighter. Next, professional flossing cleans between your teeth, removing any remaining debris.

Additional Preventive Dental Treatments

Here is a look at some of the preventive dentistry services we offer:

Fluoride Treatment : Fluoride is a common mineral found in many food products and in drinking water. It has been proven to help lower the risk of tooth decay and cavities. Depending on your smile’s needs, we may recommend fluoride treatments after completing your dental cleanings.

Dental Sealants : Sealants are thin protective coatings we apply to the chewing surfaces of your molars and premolars. The material seals out plaque, bacteria, and food particles, lowering the risk of tooth decay and cavities.

According to the American Dental Association, you should schedule regular dental visits at intervals determined by your dentist. Individuals with healthy teeth and gums typically require dental checkups and cleanings twice a year. However, patients with periodontal disease or those at risk of dental problems may require more frequent care.

Routine dental cleanings, also known as prophylactic cleanings, remove plaque and tartar deposits from your teeth and gum line. That helps lower the risk of tooth decay and gum disease, both of which can lead to severe tooth damage and even tooth loss.

Aside from your oral health, gum disease can be a serious matter. If not treated, the infection can damage the surrounding teeth and even enter the bloodstream, posing a risk to your overall health.

Professional dental cleanings freshen your breath and remove some surface tooth stains, leaving you with a clean and fresh smile. They also offer a golden opportunity to discuss your home oral hygiene habits and provide a complete evaluation of your oral health.
