What Are Dental Implants?

A natural tooth has two main parts. The crown is the portion you see when you smile, and the root sits below the gums, holding the tooth securely in place. A dental implant rebuilds the entire tooth structure from the tip to the root, leaving you with results comparable to natural healthy teeth.

But what exactly is an implant? The implant portion of the restoration is a small titanium post we place beneath the gum. During the healing period, the implant fuses to the jawbone, replacing a missing tooth’s root and providing an incredibly stable foundation for an implant restoration made to match your smile.

Here is what you can expect during the implant procedure:

Preparation: At Three Stones Dentistry, we use advanced CBCT or cone beam computed tomography scans to provide highly detailed 3D images of your teeth and bone structures, leading to more precise implant placement and avoiding vital structures.

Dental Implant Placement: This is the process of surgically placing the implant in your jaw. Before the procedure, we will numb the area with a local anesthetic for your comfort. Dr. Fu will make a small incision in your gum to access your jawbone, where he will place your dental implant.

After Implant Placement: The healing process will begin. The implant will fuse to the bone in a process known as osseointegration, which can take 4 - 6 months. After healing, we will place a connector known as an abutment to anchor your replacement tooth or teeth. The end result is a long-lasting, natural-looking, functional smile!

Some of the most outstanding benefits of dental implants include the following:

  • Single Tooth Restoration : This is a perfect solution if you are missing a single tooth. A crown placed on an implant will restore your beautiful smile.
  • Implant Bridges : These are ideal for replacing two or more adjacent missing teeth. Implant bridges are secured to dental implants rather than natural teeth.
  • Overdentures : Also known as implant-supported dentures, these dental prosthetics are more stable than traditional removable dentures. No more having to deal with problems such as slipping and shifting.
