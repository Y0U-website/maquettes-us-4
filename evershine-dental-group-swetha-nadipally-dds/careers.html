General Dentist

Description

We welcome newly-graduated applicants who meet our qualifications. Our ideal candidate must be experienced in Surgical Extractions, Root Canals, and Crown & Bridges,Fillings

Responsibilities

Examine, diagnose and provide dental treatment along with quality service to our patients Use and utilize a variety of our modern dental instruments and digital technology Provide oral hygiene instructions to the patients Ability to build up good rapport with patients and your team Provide excellent chair side communication skills to enhance the patient experience and satisfaction Maintain and supervise necessary patient treatment records

Registered Dental Assistant (RDA)

We are seeking a skilled Dental Assistant to join our team. The ideal candidate will have experience in dental assisting and possess knowledge of various dental procedures and terminology.

Responsibilities - Assist the dentist during a variety of treatment procedures - Prepare patients for dental work - Take and develop dental x-rays - Manage medical records and documentation - Utilize 3D scanners for digital impressions - Provide chairside assistance for prosthodontic and endodontic procedures - Perform four-handed dentistry techniques efficiently - Maintain compliance with HIPAA regulations

Experience - Previous experience in dental assisting is preferred - Familiarity with Dentrix software is a plus - Knowledge of medical terminology related to dentistry - Ability to handle medical documentation accurately - Proficiency in handling 3D scanners and other dental equipment

8 hour shift Monday to Friday Weekends as needed
