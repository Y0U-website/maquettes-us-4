What are implant overdentures?

Why choose implant overdentures?

  • Improved Stability: The denture attaches to dental implants, helping it feel more secure during everyday use.
  • Removable for Cleaning: Implant overdentures are designed to be removed daily for cleaning and maintenance.
  • Comfort & Confidence: Added support from implants can make dentures feel more stable and comfortable.

The Implant Overdenture Process

  • Evaluation & Planning: We evaluate your mouth, bone support, and goals to determine whether implant overdentures are a good option.
  • Implant Placement: Dental implants are placed and allowed to heal before the denture is attached.
  • Denture Attachment: Once healing is complete, your custom overdenture is made or adjusted to snap onto the implants for added support.
