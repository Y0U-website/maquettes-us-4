  • Are missing one or more teeth, or have a tooth that cannot be saved
  • Want a fixed option instead of a removable partial or denture
  • Do not want healthy neighboring teeth shaped down for a bridge
  • Have healthy gums and enough jawbone, or are open to bone grafting if needed

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Feels like your own tooth: Implants do not slip, click, or come out.
  • Protects your jawbone: An implant stimulates the bone like a natural root, helping prevent the bone loss that follows a missing tooth.
  • Leaves healthy teeth alone: No drilling down the teeth on either side.
  • Long-lasting with good care: Implants can last decades, especially with good home care and regular dental visits.
  • Eat what you love: Chew normally, with no denture adhesives.

The Dental Implant Process at MK Dental Studio

  • Consultation and evaluation: Dr. Khanani starts with a comprehensive exam , digital X-rays, and an intraoral scan to review your bone, bite, and goals.
  • Surgical planning and placement: Our trusted oral surgeon partner takes a 3D (CBCT) scan to plan precise placement, then places your implant.

Caring for Your Dental Implant

Brush twice a day, floss or use a water flosser around the implant, and keep up with your regular cleanings. Implants cannot get cavities, but the gums around them can still become inflamed. If you grind your teeth, a custom nightguard helps protect both your implant and your natural teeth.

Dental Implant FAQs – Fulshear, TX

Most adults who are missing a tooth, or have a tooth that cannot be saved, may be candidates. Healthy gums, enough jawbone, and good overall health support success. Dr. Khanani will review your exam, X-rays, and health history to recommend the best option for you.

Implant placement is done with local anesthetic, and sedation options are available through your surgeon. Most patients describe mild soreness for a few days that is usually managed with over-the-counter medication and aftercare instructions.

Usually about 3 to 6 months from implant placement to your final crown, mostly to allow the implant to heal into the bone. Extractions or bone grafting can add time.

With good care, a dental implant can last decades. How long it lasts depends on many factors, including your oral hygiene, gum health, smoking, teeth grinding, overall health conditions such as diabetes, and keeping up with regular checkups.

Age alone is not a barrier. Your overall health and bone quality matter more than your age.

Bone grafting can often rebuild the area so an implant can be placed. Your surgeon will discuss whether grafting is needed.

Implants do not affect the neighboring teeth and help preserve jawbone. Bridges are faster and may cost less upfront. Dr. Khanani will walk you through both options.

Many dental plans cover a portion of implant treatment, especially the crown. Our team will verify your benefits and give you a written estimate before treatment.
