Advantages of All-on-4 Implants

  • Permanent solution for full-mouth tooth loss
  • Only four implants required per arch
  • Immediate function with same-day placement in many cases
  • No removable dentures or adhesives
  • Preserves facial structure and jawbone integrity
  • Restores a youthful, natural-looking smile
  • Less invasive than placing individual implants for each tooth

What to Expect

1. Consultation and 3D Imaging

We use advanced diagnostic tools, including 3D CT scans , to evaluate bone structure and precisely plan the implant placement.

2. Implant Placement

Four implants are surgically placed in the upper and/or lower jaw—two in the front and two at an angle in the back to maximize contact with bone.

3. Temporary Prosthesis

In many cases, a temporary, functional set of teeth is attached the same day so you can leave with a confident smile.

4. Final Restoration

After the implants fully integrate with the bone (typically a few months), a permanent custom prosthesis is placed, completing your transformation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the procedure take?

The surgical placement and temporary restoration can often be completed in a single day.

Is All-on-4 painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Sedation options are available, and we ensure a smooth recovery with guided post-op care.

Are All-on-4 implants permanent?

Yes, the implants are permanent. The final prosthetic may need maintenance over time, but the implants themselves can last for decades.

Will I be without teeth during healing?

No. We provide a temporary fixed denture on the same day of surgery so you never have to go without teeth.

Does insurance cover this treatment?

Coverage varies by provider. Our team will work with you to maximize your benefits and provide flexible financing options.
