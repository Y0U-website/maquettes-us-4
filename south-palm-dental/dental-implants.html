The Implant Process

Step 1: Consultation and Digital Planning

We begin with a detailed consultation and diagnostic imaging to assess your bone health, map your anatomy, and determine the best implant type and placement approach.

Step 2: Surgical Placement

Under local anesthesia or sedation, the implant is precisely placed in the jawbone using traditional or computer-guided techniques. Healing time varies, but the implant fuses naturally with the bone in a process called osseointegration.

Step 3: Restoration

Once healed, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached—completing your new smile with lifelike precision.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort, and we offer sedation options for added comfort. Recovery is usually easier than expected.

Can anyone get implants?

Most healthy adults are candidates, but patients with significant bone loss may require grafting. A consultation will determine your eligibility.

Are implants better than bridges or dentures?

Implants are more secure and long-lasting than traditional restorations, and they help preserve your jawbone.

How much do dental implants cost?

Costs vary depending on the type and number of implants. We’ll provide a full breakdown and help you navigate your insurance options.
