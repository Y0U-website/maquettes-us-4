How the Process Works

  • Consultation and Evaluation We start with a comprehensive exam, digital imaging, and a discussion of your goals.
  • Implant Placement 2–4 dental implants are strategically placed into your jaw. These serve as the foundation.
  • Healing Period The implants are given time to fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration.
  • Attachment of the Overdenture Once healed, the custom-fitted denture is fabricated and secured to the implants using special attachments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are implant overdentures permanent?

No. They are removable by the patient but attach securely to implants for everyday wear.

How many implants are needed?

Most patients require 2–4 implants per arch for proper support.

Is the procedure painful?

We use local anesthesia and sedation options to ensure your comfort throughout.

Can existing dentures be converted?

In some cases, existing dentures can be modified to work as overdentures. We will assess compatibility during your consultation.

How long do they last?

With proper care, implants can last a lifetime. The overdenture itself may need replacement over time due to normal wear.
