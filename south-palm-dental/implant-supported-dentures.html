Frequently Asked Questions

How are implant-supported dentures different from traditional dentures?

They are anchored to dental implants, offering superior stability, no slippage, and better bone preservation.

Are they removable?

They can be either fixed (non-removable) or removable depending on your preferences and clinical needs.

How long does the process take?

From implant placement to final denture delivery, the process typically spans a few months, including healing time.

Do they require special care?

Care is similar to natural teeth—daily brushing, regular dental visits, and proper hygiene around the implant sites are essential.

Does insurance cover this procedure?

Coverage varies. We’ll help you review your plan and provide transparent pricing and financing options if needed.
