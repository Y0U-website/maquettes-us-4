Aligning The Bite For Proper Breathing And Sleep Orthodontics goes beyond just straightening teeth—it’s also about creating a healthier, more functional smile. When the upper and lower jaws don’t align properly, it can lead to issues such as uneven tooth wear, TMJ disorders, jaw pain, and even sleep-related problems like sleep apnea. By aligning the teeth and ensuring the jaws fit together correctly, orthodontic treatment not only enhances the appearance of your smile and facial balance, but also supports better oral health and improved airflow.
