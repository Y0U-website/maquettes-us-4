Dental Implants

  • What is the success rate of implants? When placed and restored by Dr. Skomial ,dental implants have an over 98 percent success rate.
  • How much will dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants varies from person to person depending on their specific case. We recommend reviewing our cost of dental implant page to get a better understanding, then coming in for an examination and consultation to learn more!
  • Where do I get the crown for my dental implant? Right here in our very own office! We are a unique practice because we have an in-house dental lab. This allows us to have a very hands-on approach when customizing your restorations, ensuring your new teeth match your smile goals and facial aesthetics. Dr. Klein is a trained and certified prosthodontist who specializes in the aesthetics of teeth, meaning you are in the hands of a specialist!
  • Do I qualify for dental implants? If you have any missing or failing teeth, you are likely a candidate for dental implants! The biggest causes that prevent the placement of implants is lack of bone volume or gum disease. Both of these conditions are treatable and after they are taken care of, you will be able to begin the implant process.
  • Will it hurt to have implants placed? Thanks to sedation dentistry, having dental implants placed is not painful for most people. Our doctors are certified to offer you the most advanced and soothing sedation possible. For many patients, they simply feel a slight pressure at the site of their dental implant.
  • Can I have a tooth removed and an implant placed the same day? Yes! Depending on your needs, we can offer you teeth in a day, which allows Dr. Skomial to extract teeth, place implants and restore them all during the same appointment!
  • How do I care for my dental implants? The best way to ensure your implants last, is to treat them just how you would normal teeth. Regular brushing and flossing, along with routine dental appointments for checkups and cleaning can ensure you keep your implants for the rest of your life!

Dental Implant Surgery

Some patients may require one or more treatments before receiving dental implants. In many cases, these procedures can be completed in the same appointment as your surgery to eliminate return visits. We offer extractions of failing teeth, bone grafting and sinus lift. Beginning implant treatment with a healthy mouth is essential to ensure the long-term success of your dental implants. In most cases before you leave, we’ll place a new tooth or set of teeth to complete your smile.

Final Restoration

As your treatment site heals around your implants, you’ll continue to use your initial lifelike and functional restoration. After allowing time for your jawbone to heal and form a long-term and reliable bond with your dental implants, you’ll return to our office to receive this final restoration. With proper care, you can expect your natural-looking and reliable dental implants to last a lifetime.
