FINANCIAL FAQ

In these difficult economic times, we do realize that dental treatment can become expensive . We strive to make our fees as reasonable as possible without sacrificing quality. Please contact our office with any questions you may have. Here are the answers to some commonly asked questions.

What insurance do you accept?

We do accept most PPO insurances and are preferred providers with many. We will file all necessary claims and will work with you to maximize your insurance benefits. Please be aware, however, that you are ultimately responsible for payment of the care you receive.

We also accept DentaQuest All Kids. We firmly believe that all kids deserve quality dental care and we are privileged to be able to provide it.

What payment methods do you accept?

For your convenience, we accept cash, personal check, Mastercard, Visa and Discover.

Do you offer financing?

Financing is available through Care Credit, a third party health care financing company.
