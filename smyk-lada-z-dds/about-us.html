About Us

MEET THE DOCTOR

Dr. Smyk’s practice philosophy centers around optimal dental health and compassionate care. She treats adults as well as children as people, not just teeth. She believes that patients need to take an active role in their treatment decisions and is ready and willing to answer any and all questions you may have.

Dr Smyk is of Ukrainian heritage and speaks Ukrainian fluently. She also speaks some Spanish. When she is not practicing dentistry, Dr. Smyk enjoys the outdoors, reading, spending time with family and friends and is a big Chicago sports fan.
