The practice

Our dental practice serve patients from Midwest City, Oklahoma City, Choctaw, Del City, and Moore. Our offerings include restorative dental services, a dental treatment for sleep apnea, Invisalign®, and more.

Dr. Storm’s primary goal is for patients to feel at home. The entire office strives to build relationships in a relaxing environment, and we place a specific focus on patient comfort.

At TimberView, your Midwest City dentist, our comprehensive services will ensure you maintain excellent oral hygiene from teeth cleanings and fillings to bridges and dentures. Click below to learn about our various dental services.

Obstructive Sleep Apnea is a severe sleep disorder where your breathing repeatedly stops and starts. This condition is quite common and can lead to severe complications if left untreated. Visit our Sleep Apnea page to learn more.

The HealthyStart System addresses sleep-disordered breathing in children by using a custom mouthpiece to open the airway.

We offer blankets and headphones, purify the air and create a relaxing environment using essential oils, and even have TV’s mounted above our treatment chairs so you can watch Netflix during your visit.

We are constantly searching for the latest equipment to make sure our practice offers the best dental experience. Our iTero scanner even allows you to preview your new smile before beginning your Invisalign ® treatment!

Our incredible team members go above and beyond each day to make sure our patients feel at ease and cared for during their visit. We communicate clearly with patients so there are no surprises.
