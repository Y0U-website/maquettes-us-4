The practice

Since 2003, we have served the O’Fallon area, and our team has continued to grow along with the many families here at Boardwalk Family Dental. Our experienced team provides high quality and compassionate care to our patients of all ages and dental needs. We have the latest in dental technology to provide the treatment that works best for your smile.

Hello! I’m Dr. Eric Hurtte. I grew up in the small town of Taylorville, IL and attended Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. In 2003, I opened Boardwalk Family Dental.

From a first dental visit as a young child to patients who have been going to the dentist for decades, our team is experienced and trained in caring for patients of all ages. Often, we can schedule your family’s appointments at the same time. We take pride and are appreciative that a lot of our new patients come from referrals from family and friends.

Our office continually upgrades and is trained on the latest dental technology so we can provide better and more comfortable dentistry. Often, we can ditch those messy impressions and use a digital scanner to create a virtual model of your teeth.
