Registered Dental Hygienist

Stephanie

Expanded Functions Dental Assistant

Natalie

Natalie is an Expanded Functions Dental Assistant (EFDA) and Certified Dental Assistant (CDA) with a long-standing career in dentistry. Originally from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, she has been working in the dental field since 1994.

Her EFDA license allows her to perform advanced technical procedures, including placing amalgam and composite fillings and fabricating temporary crowns. In addition to her hands-on experience, Natalie also has a strong background in education. She served as an EFDA instructor at the Bradford School in Pittsburgh, teaching post-secondary students for over a decade before the school closed.

After that transition, she returned to full-time patient care in Pennsylvania before deciding she was ready for a new chapter. She then relocated to Florida in pursuit of both personal and professional growth. Today, she lives in Daytona Beach and enjoys everything the area has to offer.

Natalie plays a key role in both clinical care and patient experience. She specializes in facial aesthetics, clear aligner therapy, sleep dentistry (including sleep studies and digital scanning for oral sleep appliances), Zoom whitening, and removable prosthetics. She is also passionate about welcoming and guiding new patients, helping them feel comfortable and confident in their care from the very first visit.

Outside of the office, Natalie enjoys spending time at the beach, being outdoors, and listening to music. She is also an artist who works primarily with pencil and charcoal, creating portraits and crafting unique resin pieces.

She has found a true sense of belonging in her dental home with Dr. Sara Dean and values the welcoming atmosphere of the practice, as well as the sunny lifestyle Florida provides.

CRDH (Certified Registered Dental Hygienist)

Laura

Laura is a dedicated dental hygienist with decades of experience in patient care and preventive dentistry. She began her career by earning her Certified Dental Assistant (CDA) license in 1999, working with an orthodontist while completing her dental hygiene studies. She graduated from the Daytona State College Hygiene Program in 2003 and has been caring for patients ever since.

She spent 15 years in pediatric dentistry before transitioning into family dentistry, where she enjoys working with patients of all ages. In 2025, she joined the practice with Sara Kohen Dean and looks forward to welcoming both new and returning patients.

Born and raised in Ormond Beach, Laura has a deep connection to the area. She enjoys spending time by the water, whether it’s relaxing at the beach or heading out on the boat to the inlet or nearby springs. Outside of work, she loves baking, trying new recipes, and spending time with her two daughters. Her family also includes a 12-year-old Standard Poodle named Chew, who keeps the household lively and well-loved.

Laura is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable and informed during their visits. She focuses on education, prevention, and personalized care, taking the time to build trust and ensure each patient feels at ease. With a strong commitment to continuing education, she stays current with the latest advancements in dental hygiene to provide thoughtful, attentive care every day.
