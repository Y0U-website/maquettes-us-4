Benefits of

Dental Implants

Choosing to restore your missing teeth in Ormond Beach with Sara Kohen Dean, DMD is a vital step toward protecting your long-term oral health. Dental implants offer meaningful benefits that support a confident, active lifestyle.

Restoring Your Facial Esthetics

Tooth loss can lead to a sunken appearance as your jawbone naturally resorbs over time. Implants stimulate bone growth, preserving your natural facial structure and promoting a more youthful appearance.

Improving Oral Functionality And Health

Unlike traditional bridges that require altering healthy adjacent teeth, implants stand independently. This preserves your natural tooth structure while providing the strength needed to chew your favorite foods comfortably.

Elevating Your Daily Confidence

Implants are securely anchored in your jaw, meaning they will not slip or click when you speak or eat. You can navigate social and professional settings with absolute peace of mind and a radiant smile.

Dental Implants FAQs

Dental Implants FAQs

We believe in a clear, honest approach so you feel fully informed before beginning your dental implants journey. Review these common questions to learn more about our approach to implant dentistry.

Am I A Good Candidate For Dental Implants?

Most adults with missing teeth and adequate bone density are wonderful candidates for implants. We will use advanced 3-D imaging during your consultation to evaluate your bone health and design a predictable treatment plan.

Will My Dental Implants Look And Feel Natural?

Yes, dental implants are carefully designed to blend seamlessly with your natural smile. Each restoration is custom-crafted to match the shape, color, and proportions of your surrounding teeth. Because implants are anchored into the jawbone, they feel stable and function much like natural teeth in everyday use.

How Long Does The Implant Process Take?

Because implants must integrate naturally with your jawbone, the entire process takes a few months. Our team will map out realistic expectations and specific timelines for your unique healing phases.

Does The Implant Procedure Hurt?

Your comfort is our highest priority, and we use precise, state-of-the-art technology to ensure a gentle experience. Many of our patients report that the procedure and recovery are much easier than they initially anticipated.

How Do Dental Implants Compare To Bridges Or Dentures?

Dental implants are designed to replace the entire structure of a missing tooth, including the root, which helps preserve bone and maintain long-term stability. Unlike bridges, implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support. Compared to traditional dentures, implants provide a more secure fit, allowing you to eat, speak, and function with greater confidence.
