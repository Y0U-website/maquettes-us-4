  • Bringing your little one for orthodontic treatment at an early age ensures that they maintain an enhanced smile, improved tooth functionality, and better oral health in the long run.
  • The results of orthodontic treatment are permanent.
  • Brushing, flossing, and carrying out other oral hygiene practices becomes easier.

When Do I Need an Orthodontic Treatment?

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth in children call for orthodontic treatment.
  • You may opt for the treatment if you have difficulty chewing or biting food.
  • Your child may require an orthodontic treatment if they suck their thumb or fingers or have bad oral habits.
  • Mouth breathing is another sign you may need orthodontic treatment.
  • Orthodontic treatment is required in case of other problems like crowded, misplaced, blocked-out, or protruding teeth, poorly aligned jaw, difficulty speaking, teeth grinding, etc.

What are the Options of Orthodontic Treatments?

Conventional braces include wires that connect to brackets attached to the surface of your teeth. When the wires are tightened, they apply gentle pressure on your teeth, thus helping to guide them in an ideal position. They are suitable for both adults and children.

Invisalign is looked upon as an increasingly sought-after alternative to traditional braces. While braces have a wired appearance, Invisalign appears near-invisible. We will give you customized clear aligner trays to wear for 22 hours a day. These trays have to ideally be changed every two weeks.

Orthodontic treatment also includes bone grafting surgery if you do not have enough jawbone for other procedures like dental implants. Even if you have a broader jaw, this procedure shapes it and gives you sharp features.
