Dr. Joseph Ghazal

Dr. Joseph Ghazal was born and raised in Tulsa, Oklahoma. He attended Union Highschool and then graduated from the University of Oklahoma with a Bachelors in Chemical Biosciences in 2015. He graduated as a Doctor of Dental Medicine along with a Certificate of Public Health in 2020

Dr. Ghazal has known that he wanted to become a dentist since he was 17, so he worked as a dental assistant in undergraduate college before attending Missouri School of Dentistry in St. Louis.

Dr. Ghazal is what some would call a Swiss army knife, as he has skills and interest in many different areas of dentistry including cosmetic dentistry, implants, dentures, and orthodontics. Dr. Ghazal’s passion for dentistry is evident in his work, but what sets him apart is his infectious personality and humor. Dr. Ghazal spends his off-time with his wife and family, with his dogs, travelling, and trying new and interesting cuisines.
