The practice

​​​ At Daniel S. Nam DDS & Associates , we offer modern, comprehensive dental care in a warm, welcoming environment where everyone is valued and respected. From routine check-ups to advanced cosmetic and restorative treatments, our experienced team uses cutting-edge technology to deliver personalized care tailored to your needs.

We know dental visits can be stressful, so we focus on your comfort with thoughtful touches like our Wallaby Care Therapeutic Hug Blanket for calming, deep-pressure support, and ethically-sourced LXMI Lip Balm to keep your lips nourished. Your well-being is our priority, and we re dedicated to making every visit as relaxing as possible.

We’re proud to serve a diverse community and offer care in multiple languages to make every patient feel at home. Our team speaks Cantonese, English, Korean, Mien, and Spanish, so you can communicate comfortably and confidently during your visit.

Dr. Daniel Nam earned his BA in Piano Performance from UCLA in 1996 before graduating from the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in 2002. He currently has private practices in both San Francisco and Oakland. Since 2003, Dr. Nam has also served as the Director of Dental Services at the Berkeley Free Clinic, where he provides care to homeless and low-income families.

In addition to his private practice, Dr. Nam is a staff dentist at Alta Bates Medical Center, working with the Emergency Department, ICU, oncology, and rehabilitation departments at both the Summit and Ashby campuses. He also serves as a clinical faculty member at the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry, where he teaches on Monday evenings.

Dr. Jennifer Hom earned her B.S. in Microbial Biology from UC Berkeley before completing her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Temple University in Philadelphia, PA, in 2014. She then pursued a General Practice Residency at Community Regional Medical Center in Fresno, CA.

During her residency, Dr. Hom discovered her passion for community health and spent six years serving at a community health center in Fresno. In 2021, she relocated to the Bay Area, where she embraced the opportunity to provide compassionate, high-quality dental care at Just Health 510.

Outside of her professional work, Dr. Hom enjoys crafting, exploring new restaurants with her family, and spending time outdoors with her dogs.
