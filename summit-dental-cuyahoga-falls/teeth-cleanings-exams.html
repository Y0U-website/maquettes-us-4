How a Cleaning and Exam Works

Most routine visits are calm, comfortable, and done within about an hour.

  • We welcome you, review your health history, and confirm your insurance before anything starts.
  • If images will help your care, we take quick, low-radiation digital X-rays .
  • Your hygienist gently removes plaque and tartar, then polishes your teeth smooth.
  • Dr. DePolo examines your teeth, gums, bite, and any existing dental work.
  • We talk through what we found, answer your questions, and set your next visit.

Your Options for Cleanings and Exams

If you are new to us, your first visit usually includes a full exam, digital X-rays, and a cleaning. It gives us a complete picture of your smile and gives you time to settle in.

After that, most patients do best with a cleaning and exam every six months. Some smiles need a different rhythm, and we will tell you honestly if yours does. If your gums show early signs of trouble, we may recommend gum care on a closer schedule.

Families often book back-to-back visits so everyone is seen in one trip. Kids and adults are both welcome here. If your exam turns up something that needs care, like a cavity, we explain your options first. Nothing happens without your say-so.

The Benefits of Getting This Done Right

Prevention is the least expensive dentistry there is. Catching a cavity early usually means a small filling instead of a bigger repair later.

There is also the way your mouth feels afterward. Smooth teeth, fresher breath, and a brighter surface are the immediate rewards.

The bigger reward is peace of mind. You leave knowing exactly where your smile stands, with no guesswork and no surprises.

How to Know You Are Due for a Cleaning and Exam

If any of these sound familiar, it is time to schedule:

A steady toothache moves this to the top of the list. Call us and we will get you scheduled.

Waiting rarely saves money. Tartar keeps building and decay keeps spreading, so a fix that would have been small grows in size and cost.

An exam also finds what you cannot. Digital X-rays reveal decay between teeth and under old fillings long before anything hurts. Dr. DePolo also watches for gum changes and wear since your last visit that you would never spot at home.

Ask yourself a few quick questions. When was my last cleaning? Is there a spot I avoid chewing on? Do I already know I am overdue? Honest answers usually point the way.

A cleaning and exam is the right starting point for almost everyone. If we find something that needs its own visit, we will map it out with you calmly and clearly.

  • It has been six months or more since your last cleaning.
  • You can feel rough buildup along the gumline with your tongue.
  • Your gums look red or bleed a little when you brush or floss.
  • Your breath does not stay fresh, even right after brushing.
  • A tooth has turned sensitive to hot, cold, or sweets.
  • Food keeps catching in the same spot, or floss snags there.

What Does a Cleaning and Exam Cost?

The cost of a cleaning and exam depends on a few things. It depends on whether this is your first visit, whether X-rays are taken, and what your plan covers.

Preventive visits are the ones dental plans are most likely to cover in full or close to it. We accept most major dental plans. Call us and we will confirm your benefits before you come in.

We also offer CareCredit financing and take cash, checks, and credit cards. You will know your costs before any treatment begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often do I need a cleaning?

Most patients benefit from a cleaning and exam every six months. We will let you know if your smile needs a different schedule.

Will my cleaning be covered by insurance?

Preventive cleanings and exams are usually covered in full or close to it by most plans. We take most major dental plans and offer CareCredit financing, and we check your benefits ahead of time.

Does a cleaning hurt?

For most people a cleaning is comfortable. If your teeth or gums are sensitive, tell us and we will adjust to keep you at ease.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring your insurance information and a list of any medications. Give yourself a few extra minutes for new-patient paperwork, or fill it out ahead if it is available online.

How long does a cleaning and exam take?

Plan on about an hour for most routine visits. New-patient visits can run a little longer, and we will tell you what to expect when you book.

Can my whole family be seen here?

Yes. We care for kids and adults alike, and we are glad to schedule family visits together whenever we can.
