Meet Dr. Jenna

Dr. Jenna Obeng

In her spare time, Dr. Jenna loves spending time with her husband and two daughters. She loves to stay active and enjoys making memories with her family.

Dr. Jenna is a member and fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, and her professional affiliations include the American Dental Association, Florida Dental Association, Volusia Dental Academy, and SPEAR study club.
