Is the implant procedure painful?

Our patients in Washington, DC are often surprised by the lack of pain. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and conscious sedation may be an option for added comfort and relaxation. Most patients report minor discomfort during and after implant placement.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implant posts can last a lifetime. Depending on the amount of wear and tear, the crown (visible part) may need to be replaced in 10 to 15 years. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits will help maintain their longevity.

Are dental implants better than dentures or bridges?

Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants integrate with the jawbone, preventing bone loss and preserving facial structure. They can also offer superior stability, function, and aesthetics. That said, your doctor will walk you through all available options for an informed decision.
