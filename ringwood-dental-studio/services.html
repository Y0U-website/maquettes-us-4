General Dentistry

Extractions

Dental extractions involve the removal of a tooth, usually due to decay, infection, or overcrowding. This procedure helps prevent further dental issues and can be a necessary step in preparing for treatments like braces or dental implants.

Fillings

Composite dental fillings are made from a tooth-colored resin that blends seamlessly with natural teeth, providing a discreet and durable solution for cavities. They are a popular choice because they can be easily shaped to match the natural contours of the tooth for a more aesthetic result.

Digital Dentistry

Digital dentistry uses advanced technology, such as 3D imaging and CAD/CAM systems, to improve the precision and efficiency of dental procedures. It allows for more accurate diagnoses, customized treatments, and faster restoration processes, enhancing both patient experience and treatment outcomes.

Emergency Care

Emergency dental care involves prompt treatment for urgent dental issues like severe pain, trauma, or infections. It aims to alleviate discomfort, prevent further damage, and restore dental health as quickly as possible.

Digital X-Rays

Digital dental X-rays use advanced technology to capture detailed images of your teeth and jaw with minimal radiation. These images are instantly available on a computer, allowing for faster diagnosis and more accurate treatment planning.

Routine Cleaning

Dental cleanings involve the removal of plaque, tartar, and stains from teeth to promote oral health. They are typically performed by a hygienist and help prevent cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues.

Pediatric

Pediatric dentistry focuses on providing specialized dental care for children through adolescence. Our goal is to create a positive, comfortable experience while promoting healthy oral habits that last a lifetime.

Sealants

Fluoride Varnish

Dental fluoride varnish is a fast and effective treatment that helps strengthen tooth enamel and prevent cavities. Applied directly to the teeth, it releases fluoride to protect against decay, especially in hard-to-reach areas.

Restorative Dentistry

Crowns, Bridges, Onlays

Dental crowns are custom-made restorations that cover damaged or weakened teeth to restore their strength, function, and appearance. They can be used as standalone restorations, as part of a dental bridge to replace missing teeth, or as onlays to cover larger portions of a tooth that need reinforcement.

Dentures

Dental dentures are removable prosthetic devices designed to replace missing teeth and restore functionality and appearance. They come in various types, including complete dentures, which replace all teeth in an arch, partial dentures for replacing a few missing teeth, and Valplast dentures, which are flexible and offer a comfortable, discreet option for partial tooth replacement.

Implant Restorations

Implant restorations involve placing a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture onto a dental implant. This solution offers a durable and natural-looking replacement for missing teeth, restoring both function and appearance.

Periodontal Dentistry

Periodontics

Dental deep cleanings, also known as scaling and root planing, are a treatment for gum disease that involves cleaning below the gum line to remove plaque and tartar buildup. This procedure helps prevent further infection and promotes the healing of the gums, often requiring multiple visits for thorough treatment.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers

Dental veneers are a thin, custom-made layer that covers the front surface of teeth to enhance their appearance. They can address issues such as discoloration, misalignment, or chips, providing a natural-looking, long-lasting solution for a perfect smile.

Teeth Whitening

Dental teeth whitening is a cosmetic treatment that lightens the color of teeth by removing stains and discoloration. It can be done professionally in a dental office or with at-home products to achieve a brighter, more confident smile.

Endodontics

Endodontics

Dental endodontics, commonly known as root canal treatment, involves removing infected or damaged tissue from the inside of a tooth to prevent further decay and infection. After cleaning and sealing the root, the tooth is restored with a crown or filling to restore its function and protect it from future problems.

Invisalign

Invisalign

Invisalign is an aligner treatment that gradually moves teeth into alignment without the need for traditional metal braces. The treatment uses a series of clear, custom-fit trays to achieve a straighter smile discreetly.
