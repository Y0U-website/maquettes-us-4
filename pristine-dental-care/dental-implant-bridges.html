At Pristine Dental Care, we understand the impact of missing teeth on your daily life. That’s why we offer dental implant bridges, an innovative solution for restoring your smile. Dental implant bridges are custom-made prosthetic teeth anchored securely to dental implants.

What is a Dental Bridge Implant?

It involves dental implants, which are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as artificial tooth roots. The dental bridge, consisting of one or more prosthetic teeth, is then securely attached to these implants, creating a stable and functional restoration. Unlike traditional bridges, which rely on neighboring natural teeth for support, dental bridge implants are self-supporting.

The Implant Supported Bridge Process

Initial Consultation

Our experienced dental team will carefully evaluate your oral health and discuss your specific goals and concerns. We will take the time to understand your unique needs and develop a personalized treatment plan with our comprehensive services tailored to your situation.

Preparatory Steps

If necessary, teeth extraction may be performed to create space for the dental bridge implant. Additionally, a thorough assessment of your jawbone will be conducted to determine if any bone grafting is needed to ensure a stable foundation for the implant.

Implant Placement

To ensure your comfort, anesthesia will be administered before the implant placement. Our skilled dentist will surgically insert the dental implant into the jawbone. This acts as an artificial tooth root and provides a sturdy foundation for the implant-supported bridge.

Healing and Osseointegration

After the implant and dental crown placement, a recovery period is necessary for proper healing and osseointegration . During this time, the implant will naturally fuse with the jawbone, providing stability and support for the dental bridge.

Abutment and Crown Placement

Following a healing period, an abutment will be placed on the implant. The abutment acts as a connector between the implant and the dental crown. The dental crown, custom-made to match your natural teeth, will then be securely placed onto the abutment, completing the dental bridge implant.

Final Adjustments and Follow-up Care

Once the healing process is complete, our dental team will perform final adjustments to ensure proper bite alignment and occlusion. We will also provide detailed post-treatment instructions to promote optimal healing and hygiene practices. Follow-up appointments will be scheduled to monitor your progress and ensure the long-term success of your dental implant bridge.

What Are The Benefits of Dental Bridges Implants?

Restored Functionality

Dental bridge implants offer numerous advantages in restoring functionality in your mouth. Teeth replacement can enhance your ability to chew and speak with ease. Additionally, they eliminate discomfort often associated with gaps left by missing teeth, improving your overall comfort.

Aesthetics and Natural Appearance

One of the significant benefits of permanent bridge implants and other restorative dentistry services is the return of a complete smile and facial symmetry. These implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing a natural-looking result. The dental bridges are carefully crafted to match the shape, color, and size of your existing teeth, ensuring a harmonious and aesthetically pleasing smile.

Preservation of Oral Health

Dental bridge implants play a vital role in preserving your oral health. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area can begin to deteriorate over time, leading to facial sagging and changes in your appearance. By filling the gap left by missing teeth, dental bridge implants help prevent bone loss and maintain proper facial structure.

Are implant bridges permanent?

Like implant crowns , bridge implants are designed to be a long-lasting solution for missing teeth. On average, dental bridge implants can last for several decades with proper care and maintenance. To prolong the lifespan of dental bridge implants, it is recommended to follow these tips: maintain good oral hygiene, including brushing and flossing daily, attend regular dental check-ups, avoid chewing on hard objects or biting into excessively sticky foods, and seek timely treatment for any dental issues that arise.

Cost of Dental Bridge Implants

The cost of dental bridge implants can vary based on several factors. The number of missing teeth and the complexity of your case will impact the overall cost. Additionally, if additional procedures, such as bone grafting, are required to create a stable foundation for the implants, this can affect the total cost.
