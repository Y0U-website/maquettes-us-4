What Are All-on-X Implants

The All-on-X dental implant technique involves placing 4 to 6 implants into the jawbone to replace your missing teeth in the arch. Unlike traditional removable dentures, we secure the prosthesis (teeth) over the implants, providing a fixed and more stable replacement for missing teeth.

It is ideal for patients who have lost most of their teeth or are seeking a more natural and comfortable alternative to removable dentures. Once the procedure is complete, you will be able to speak more comfortably and enjoy many of your favorite foods without any discomfort.

Benefits of All-on-X Implants

The All-on-X implant procedure offers a variety of benefits, including:

  • Fixed (non removable) solution: The prosthesis is fixed to the implants, providing long-term stability
  • Natural appearance: We customize the prosthesis to resemble your natural teeth and gums, enhancing the look of your smile
  • Improved function: You can bite and chew more easily without worrying about prosthesis moving or coming off
  • Minimally invasive: Only 4 to 6 implants are needed, avoiding the complexity of placing an implant for each tooth

How the All-on-X Implant Process Works At Our Dental Clinic In Harvard, MA

After we finalize your treatment plan for All-on-X implants, our Harvard team will start by injecting a local anesthetic to numb your gums and jawbone. We will make small incisions in the gum tissue to expose the bone. Next, we will carefully position 4 to 6 implants into the bone to act as stable anchors for your new teeth.

In some cases, a temporary full-arch denture may be placed immediately. However, we often recommend allowing your gums and jawbone to heal for a few weeks before placing the final denture. After the healing period, we will start the process of making your final zirconia prosthesis which will be secured to your implants.

How many implants are needed for this treatment?

How long do All-on-X implants last?
