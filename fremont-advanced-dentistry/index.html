The practice

Preventative Dental Care Keeping your smile healthy starts with regular visits! We provide comprehensive preventive dental care, including Dental Exams, Cleanings, Deep Cleanings, and Teeth Whitening!

Restorative Dental Care Whether you’re dealing with a cavity, cracked tooth, or missing teeth, our restorative dental services are designed to bring your smile back to full health and function! We offer a range of treatments including fillings, crowns, bridges, and more!

We utilize the Solea 9.3-micron CO₂ laser, enabling us to perform most dental procedures such as fillings and soft tissue treatments without the need for needles, drills, or scalpels. This means less discomfort, no anesthesia, and faster healing times .

At Fremont Advanced Dentistry, we welcome patients of all ages, from toddlers to seniors. Whether it’s a child’s first dental visit, orthodontic care for teens, or restorative treatments for adults and older adults, we tailor each experience to meet your unique needs. Our team is gentle, patient, and experienced in creating a calm, reassuring environment for every member of your family.

An implant is a titanium device that is integrated into the jaw bone of a missing tooth space. After the implant has healed and integrated into the jaw, a tooth colored crown is fixed onto the implant, restoring both the form and function of the missing tooth
