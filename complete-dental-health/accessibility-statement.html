Accessibility Statement

Tools for Enhanced Browsing

If you have difficulty viewing our webpages, the U.S. Social Security Administration provides helpful guidance for improving your online experience, such as:

  • Having your computer read web pages aloud.
  • Navigating using only your keyboard.
  • Increasing text size and using screen magnification.
  • Adjusting background and text colors for better contrast.
  • Making the mouse pointer more visible.

Media Accessibility (Captions & Volume)

  • Closed Captioning: We strive to ensure that any video content provided is accessible. Most platforms we use, such as YouTube, allow you to toggle captions on for audio assistance.
  • Volume Controls: Please remember that your device (computer, tablet, or phone) has its own volume settings which work independently from the media player on our site.

Disclaimer

Complete Dental Health is committed to ongoing efforts to enhance the accessibility of our website and services. We believe it is our responsibility to ensure that individuals of all abilities can navigate and interact with our website without barriers.

Despite our progress, some content may not yet fully meet the highest accessibility standards due to limitations in current technology. We are constantly seeking solutions to reach the level of excellence our patients deserve.
