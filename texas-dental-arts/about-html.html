What Customer Service Means to Us

When it comes to “customer service” in dentistry, we believe every patient has the right to be heard, and the right to be presented with all viable treatment options. No one should ever leave wondering why a particular course of treatment was recommended. Dr. Unger is known for his patience and his ability to clearly communicate the “why” behind his recommendations. He will thoroughly explain all options and work with you to decide what’s best for you.

Dental work can be intimidating. We’re here to make sure you are comfortable with your options and your decision to move forward with treatment. Our goal is to create a welcoming environment where you feel good about your dental care.
