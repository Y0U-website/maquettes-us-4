Dr. Ivan Valcarenghi

DDS, MAGD, D.ABDSM, D.ASBA

Our mission is to create healthy, confident smiles through compassionate and personalized dental care. Dr. Ivan has been in practice for over 30 years, emphasizing cosmetic & rehabilitation dentistry in a multi-specialty group practice in Elmhurst, Illinois. Patients needing complex, comprehensive, or multidisciplinary rehabilitation dentistry or a cosmetic dentist’s advanced skills & cosmetic experience would benefit from his training.

With over 4000 hours of continuing education, Dr. Ivan is committed to life-long learning. He is a certified Toastmaster & professional speaker, has spoken publicly & professionally often, & has written numerous articles for professional & lay journals & newspapers. He has also contributed to local radio & TV regarding a variety of clinical & practice management topics. He is a graduate of several practice management programs & has obtained education in business and training in marketing & communication.

Dr. Ivan was recently awarded a Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry, was named one of DCA’s Top 100 Doctors for 2019, & is now a Board Certified Diplomate for the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine (ABDSM). The ABDSM is the leading national organization for dentists who treat snoring & obstructive sleep apnea with oral appliance therapy. Earning Diplomate status from the ABDSM is a unique honor that recognizes particular competency in dental sleep medicine.
