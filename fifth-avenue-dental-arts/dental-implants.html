What are dental implants and when to get them?

Dental implants are a great choice for replacing missing or damaged teeth. They provide a long–lasting, natural-looking option that can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or a full set. This restoration not only enhances your smile but also restores its function and beauty.

Dental implants are permanent replacement teeth after the loss of a tooth due to injury, infection, periodontal disease, or irreparable damage to multiple teeth due to decay. Unlike impermanent dentures and bridges, dental implants are set into the jawbone. Dental implants are the gold standard in dentistry because they look, function, and feel like your natural teeth.

San Diego dental implants consist of three main components:

1. The Implant:

This is a small titanium screw surgically placed into the jawbone, where it integrates and serves as a sturdy foundation for replacement teeth.

2. The Abutment:

Positioned on top of the implant, the abutment acts as a connector that links the implant to the crown.

3. The Crown:

The visible part of the dental implant that resembles a natural tooth, providing both functionality and aesthetics for a complete smile restoration.

What Dental Implant Types Are There?

Choosing the right dental implant is key to achieving a healthy-looking smile and functional results. Check out a brief overview of the dental implant types available at Fifth Avenue Dental Arts in San Diego and who they might be best suited for:

Single-Tooth Implant:

Suitable for patients with one or multiple missing teeth in different areas, this option is perfect for patients seeking a restoration that mirrors the look and function of their natural teeth. Single-tooth implants are perfect for those with sufficient jawbone density to support the implant.

All-on-X (All-on-4, All-on-6):

This option is ideal for patients missing most or all teeth in one arch (upper or lower) seeking a full-arch restoration. It is also a great alternative for patients who want a stable, permanent alternative to conventional dentures.

Implant-Supported Dentures:

These are suitable for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth but do not want or cannot have individual implants for each missing tooth. They offer a more secure fit compared to traditional dentures because the denture is anchored by implants.

Each of these dental implant types has its unique set of benefits catered to different needs and circumstances. Dental Implants Process

The process for getting dental implants in San Diego involves several important steps that guarantee the success of the procedure:

Initial consultation and assessment:

In this initial phase, Drs. Ramin Ghassemi and Nina Mojaver assess your oral health, engage in detailed discussions about the treatment options best for your needs, and create a customized plan to restore your smile.

Preparation of the jawbone (if needed):

If the jawbone lacks the density or structure to support an implant, bone grafting or other procedures may be required to prepare the area properly. This step is crucial to the long-term success of the implant. It creates a solid foundation for it to naturally integrate with the surrounding bone structure.

Placement of the implant:

During the dental implant procedure, the titanium implant is positioned and surgically inserted into the bone to serve as an artificial tooth root, providing a strong foundation for the restoration to come.

Healing and osseointegration:

Following the implant’s placement, a healing period is required for osseointegration to take place. This biological process involves the implant’s fusion with the jawbone, promoting not only stability but also long-term durability and strength.

Placement of the abutment:

Once the implant has successfully integrated with the bone through a process called osseointegration, an abutment is attached to securely link the implant to the custom-made replacement tooth.

Attachment of the crown:

The last step in the process is to carefully place a natural–looking crown onto the abutment. This crown is meticulously crafted to restore not only the tooth’s natural appearance but also its full function.

What Are The Advantages of Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer many health benefits that go beyond just a beautiful smile. They work like natural teeth, improving your oral and overall health. One key perk is how they help preserve jawbone density. When teeth are missing, the jawbone can shrink, affecting facial structure and other teeth. When dental implants step in, they keep your bone strong and your face looking its best!

When you have missing teeth, it can sometimes lead to neighboring teeth moving into those empty spaces. This shift might cause alignment issues, make chewing harder, and raise the risk of gum disease. Dental implants fill those gaps, keeping your teeth in place, preserving your bite, and supporting healthier gums.

Leaving missing teeth unaddressed can actually increase the risk of gum disease because those gaps between the teeth can trap food and bacteria. This impacts oral hygiene and can also affect your overall health, connecting to issues like heart disease and diabetes. Dental implants help keep your smile healthy because they can be looked after just like your natural teeth.

Highlighting the efficiency of dental implants, it’s important to note that they boast a remarkable 95 % success rate, beating the outcomes of traditional bridges and dentures. Unlike bridges and dentures that might need replacement or tweaking over time, dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth. They look and feel like your natural teeth, letting you enjoy a regular diet without restrictions.

With dental implants, you get stability and security and improved speech and comfort. Say goodbye to concerns about loose dentures! Are There Any Reasons Not to Get Dental Implants?

There are instances where dental implants are not the right dental solution for a patient. At Fifth Avenue Dental Arts , we evaluate every patient with thorough and individualized care to determine the best course of action for your oral health restoration. Here are some of the reasons why we may not recommend dental implants:

Age restrictions Many patients under the age of 18 are still developing skeletal maturity. We do not recommend dental implants below the age of 16 for female patients or below the age of 17 for male patients.

Osteoporosis Some patients have severe osteoporosis, which requires unique assessment, expertise, and strategy. Though most of the time we are able to successfully overcome this condition, there are times when we may not recommend dental implants.

Smoking Smokers have an increased risk of dental implant failure. The success of the dental implant procedure often requires quitting for a period of time before treatment.

Uncontrolled diabetes Patients with uncontrolled diabetes may need to stabilize their condition before proceeding with dental implants.

Gum disease The presence of gum disease can affect the overall outcome of the procedure and may require treatment prior to undergoing any dental implant procedure in San Diego.

Jaw irradiation Prior jaw irradiation can pose challenges, and additional precautions may be needed during the dental procedure.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Most dental implant screws will last a lifetime with regular brushing and flossing. Though the implant and abutment can stay strong for decades, the crown of the implant may need to be replaced every 10 to 15 years. It all depends on things like wear and tear and the material used for the crown.
