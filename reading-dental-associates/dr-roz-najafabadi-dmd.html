Dr. Roz Najafabadi DMD

Dr. Roz Najafabadi DMD General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Roz Najafabadi DMD (Dr. Roz or Dr. Najafabadi) is a General Dentist at Reading Dental Associates. Dr. Najafabadi earned her doctorate from Tufts School of Dental Medicine in 2013 and completed her residency at Stony Brook University in Long Island, where she learned advanced prosthetic and periodontal techniques and procedures. Dr. Roz is a Boston native who has been practicing dentistry for over a decade. She focuses on building relationships with her patients and in patient-centered care that will improve not only patients’ oral health but also their confidence and self esteem.

Dr. Najafabadi is well versed in all aspects of dentistry from basic preventative care to routine fillings to complex restorations. She is a part of numerous dental groups dedicated to continuing education in order to stay apprised of case complexities and new technologies in order to better serve her patients.
