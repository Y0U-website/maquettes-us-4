Splints and Guards

An occlusal splint or orthotic device is a specially designed mouth guard for people who grind their teeth, have a history of pain and dysfunction associated with their bite or temporomandibular joints (TMJ), or have completed a full mouth reconstruction. During occlusal splint fabrication, an occlusal splint is custom-made using detailed study models on an instrument called an articulator that simulates the movement of the jaws.
