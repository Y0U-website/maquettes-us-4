Mini Dental Implants

  • Design: A traditional dental implant consists of multiple components. Due to the procedure’s invasiveness, the pieces are typically attached during separate visits, prolonging the treatment timeline. On the other hand, mini implants are made of a single piece of biocompatible titanium. That means Dr. Hatland can place a mini implant in one visit. It’s a more streamlined and convenient way to replace your teeth while harnessing the benefits of implant technology.

The Benefits of Mini Dental Implants

Mini dental implants offer several advantages that make them a natural fit for our holistic approach to care. Because the procedure is minimally invasive, we don’t have to cut the gums or place large, aggressive implants into the bone. This gentler technique causes less stress on your body, which leads to faster and more predictable healing. Your body isn’t working overtime to recover, so you can get back to feeling like yourself more quickly.

Most patients experience very little post-op discomfort, and many don’t need anything more than an aspirin. The process is also much quicker than traditional implant surgery, so you’ll spend less time in the dental chair and won’t need weeks of recovery time.

On top of all that, mini dental implants are more affordable than traditional implants, making them a more accessible option for many patients. It’s a simple, comfortable, and cost-effective way to restore your smile without compromising your overall well-being.
