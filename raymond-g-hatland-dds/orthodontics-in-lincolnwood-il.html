Exploring the Orthodontic Process

At Dr. Hatland’s office, orthodontic treatment is simple and straightforward.

Dr. Hatland begins with a consultation, during which he will assess your smile and test your bite. Once he understands your needs, he is better able to craft a custom treatment plan.

Before beginning your orthodontic treatment, Dr. Hatland will need to address any underlying issues. Problems like gum disease and tooth decay can be common among those with misalignments, as their smile is more difficult to clean thoroughly.

Once he is sure your teeth are healthy, Dr. Hatland will begin the orthodontic process.

He will start by bonding these special triangular brackets to your teeth. Then, he will thread a square wire through your brackets, which will apply a gentle but steady pressure. Immediately after the first appointment, Fastbraces® start to move your teeth to their ideal position.

At every checkup you attend (typically every 4-6 weeks), Dr. Hatland will evaluate your progress, make any necessary adjustments, and ensure your smile is healthy. Just like with traditional braces, there are some food restrictions. However, Dr. Hatland will help guide you through the treatment, keeping your smile on track.

Then, in anywhere from 3 months to a year, your smile should be perfectly aligned!

Many dental insurance plans offer orthodontic coverage for children and teens. However, adult orthodontic coverage is often more limited or excluded entirely. Some plans may cover a portion of adult treatment, especially if it’s deemed medically necessary, but it’s common for adult benefits to be reduced compared to those for minors.

Fortunately, Dr. Hatland partners with third-party financing companies that help split your costs into more manageable payments! To find out the cost of your orthodontic treatment, visit Raymond Hatland, DDS, in Lincolnwood, IL, for a consultation.
