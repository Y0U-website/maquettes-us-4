Implant Dentures in Lincolnwood, IL

Dr. Hatland understands that everyone’s needs are different. When restoring your smile with mini implant dentures, he offers various options to choose from. These dentures range in both removability and appearance.

Snap-on Dentures

Similar to traditional dentures, snap-on dentures have a base with an arch of artificial teeth. However, rather than relying on your jawbone for support, this denture relies on mini dental implants!

Snap-on dentures have special housings in their bases with O-rings that allow for removal. For easy maintenance of your prosthetic, simply snap your denture off your implants. Then, when you’re done cleaning, snap them back in place.

With 4 to 8 mini implants for support, this denture restores your smile and provides a strong, stable bite.

Semi-Removable Roundhouse Bridge

A roundhouse bridge replaces all of your missing teeth, but with less of the classic denture base. Instead, these restorations have a sleek design with a smaller base for extra gum support.

A semi-removable roundhouse bridge is secured by 6 to 10 mini implants that Dr. Hatland places strategically around your jawbone. Once secure, this denture is never removed by you!

Instead, it remains in your mouth until routine dental checkups. During these appointments, Dr. Hatland will take your denture out to ensure your implants and gums remain healthy.

Non-Removable Roundhouse Bridge

A non-removable roundhouse bridge may not require a base for support, as it uses 10 to 12 mini implants for the ultimate stability. Additionally, because they secure directly on your implants, these restorations are incredibly natural-looking!

Dr. Hatland cements this denture on your implants, replicating the function and appearance of your natural teeth. With a non-removable roundhouse bridge in place, you can eat all the foods you love without worrying about your dentures slipping in your mouth.

Frequently Asked Questions

The cost of implant dentures depends on many factors. Fortunately, because Dr. Hatland uses mini dental implants, these solutions are typically less expensive than conventional implant-supported dentures.

Snap-on dentures are the least expensive option, as they require the fewest implants. In contrast, non-removable roundhouse bridges require the most implants, making them the priciest option.

However, Dr. Hatland partners with third-party financing companies that help split up your costs! With these flexible financing options, you can handle any out-of-pocket costs with manageable monthly payments.

Dr. Hatland prioritizes his patients’ comfort. Before beginning any treatments, he uses a local anesthetic to numb your smile, keeping you comfortable throughout the procedure.

Additionally, with minimally invasive dentistry techniques, you can enjoy a more comfortable and shorter healing period! Any mild pain or discomfort you do feel after your implant treatment can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain medications.

Implant dentures can last a lifetime, but only if you care for them! Just like your natural teeth, dentures need daily care. Using a soft-bristle brush and denture-safe toothpaste, make sure to brush your dentures twice a day!

For removable implant dentures specifically, it’s important to brush and floss around your implants as well to keep your gums and implants healthy.

Additionally, don’t forget to visit Dr. Hatland for routine dental visits! During these appointments, he will clean your smile and check for any signs of gum disease .

By following these steps, you can help your dentures last you a lifetime!
