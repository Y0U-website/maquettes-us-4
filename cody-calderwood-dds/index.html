Meet Dr. Cody Calderwood

Dr. Calderwood didn’t become a dentist to chase titles — he genuinely loves helping people feel comfortable in the chair and confident in their smile. His favorite part of the job…

The practice

Utah-born and raised, he studied at BYU before earning his dental degree at the University of Maryland, then returned to Park City in 2009. He keeps his care modern through hands-on training, lectures, and active leadership.

He’s a proud dad of six in a blended family with his wife, Kristin. At home and in the office, connection matters most.

"My favorite part of dentistry is getting to know my patients and learning about the things that are important to them."

Dr. Calderwood spent two years living and serving in Hermosillo, Mexico, where he came home with a lot more than language skills. He gained a deep respect for cultures different from his own, and a genuine love for the people in them. For Spanish-speaking patients, that means you can describe exactly what you’re feeling, ask the questions you have, and fully understand your treatment plan, all in…

Dentistry isn’t standing still, and neither is Dr. Calderwood. He regularly pursues hands-on training and continuing education because he believes you deserve care that reflects where dentistry is today, not where it was ten years ago.

As a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry, a distinction earned by only a handful of dentists, he’s logged hundreds of hours of advanced study across every area of dental care. That means whether you’re coming in for a cleaning or something more complex, you’re in experienced hands. His Fellowship in the International Congress of Oral Implantologists also means his implant patients get care…

As a former president of the Utah Dental Association, he’s also committed to not only practicing dentistry but shaping it! This leadership keeps him connected to the best minds in the field, and that knowledge flows directly back to you.

From skiing and mountain biking to boating & hiking, he’s always chasing adventure!
