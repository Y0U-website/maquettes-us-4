Dr. Robert Eisberg, Cary, NC

Serving Our Apex, and Raleigh Patients

Dr. Eisberg, a resident of beautiful Cary, NC, is the owner of Atlantic Family Dental. You can make an appointment with Dr. Eisberg at all three of our locations. He is a graduate of the University at Buffalo Dental School in NY. He has acquired additional training in endodontics, TMD, periodontics, and implants. Dr. Eisberg enjoys learning about and investing in new dental technologies to serve his patients with the latest dental techniques.

In Dr. Eisberg’s free time, he is still performing dentistry! On his off days, you can usually find Dr. Eisberg saving smiles on church community dental buses, at dental outreach initiatives, and in volunteer clinics such as Baptists on a Mission .

When Dr. Eisberg is not caring for patients’ teeth, he is around. Dr. Eisberg has been married to his primary partner for over 18 years, and is a father.

Dr. Bachuc Nguyen, Raleigh, NC

Serving Our Raleigh Patients

Dr. Nguyen is a resident of Raleigh, NC. She brings her warm-heartedness and vast experience to Atlantic Family Dental. She earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2000, and furthered her education with a year residency of Advanced Training in General Dentistry at Marquette University School of Dentistry Wisconsin in 2001. Since then, she has served in general family dentistry for 20 years across WI, TX, and NC, as well as in geriatric dentistry in assisted living communities in CT for 5 years. Dr. Nguyen loves to scrapbook and spend time with her family.

Dr. Nguyen is our Vietnamese-speaking dentist and is bilingual in English and Vietnamese.

Dr. Hee-Jin Park, Apex, NC

Serving Our Apex Patients

Dr. Park enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and sees guests of all ages. She is dedicated to provide gentle, empathetic care to help guests feel at ease. She has a deep compassion to give back to the community and serve the underserved population.

Some of her hobbies outside of work include spending time with her family, reading, traveling, cooking, and baking. She is devoted to a life-long learning and loves staying current with the latest dental technologies and techniques via continuing education.

Dr. Park is passionate about restoring smiles, one smile at a time.
