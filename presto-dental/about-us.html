About

  • Husky Dental/Medicaid Friendly: We proudly accept Husky Dental (Medicaid) and make quality dental care accessible for all Bridgeport families. Our knowledgeable team is here to help you maximize your benefits.
  • Convenient Location: Centrally located in Bridgeport, Presto Dental is easy to reach from anywhere in the area, whether you’re coming from downtown, the East Side, or surrounding communities.
  • Trusted Pediatric Care: As part of the respected All About Kids Pediatric Dentistry family, with four established practices across Connecticut, we bring decades of experience in children’s oral health right to your neighborhood.
  • Modern, Patient-First Approach: Our newly built office features the latest technology and a warm, welcoming environment designed to make dental visits stress-free and even fun; for kids, teens, and adults alike.

Caring Practices

Our practices are designed to be fun, and our team members are dedicated to providing each patient with the individualized care they deserve. We welcome patients of all ages and strive to make every visit a positive experience.

Friendly Staff

You will be greeted by a friendly team member as soon as you walk through the door. We will take the time to get to know you and your family, and we will work with you to ensure that all of your needs are met in a timely and supportive manner.

Inclusive Practice

We are very proud to be an inclusive dental practice, welcoming patients of all backgrounds to experience our high-quality services. Our multi-lingual staff is here to serve families in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. And we accept almost all insurance plans, including Medicaid / Husky Dental plans.

At Presto Dental, we deliver comprehensive, quality care for the children and families in the communities we serve. We are passionate about the work we do and the difference we make in the lives of our patients. Everything we do, we do with the goal of making visiting the dentist fun and comfortable for patients of all ages.

We understand that every family is unique, and we tailor our care to meet the specific needs of each one. We offer a wide range of dentistry services, from cleanings and fillings to more complex procedures like braces, implants, and veneers. No matter what your dental needs may be, we will work with you to find a treatment plan that fits your budget and your schedule.

A Dental Home for Every Family in Bridgeport

At Presto Dental, we believe every child and adult deserves a healthy, confident smile —no matter their background or insurance coverage. Our caring team takes the time to listen, explain, and partner with you on your dental journey. Whether you’re looking for a gentle first dental visit for your child, a trusted dentist for your family, or an orthodontist who understands your needs, we’re here to help.

Proudly Serving the Bridgeport Community

Presto Dental is committed to serving Bridgeport and nearby towns, including Trumbull, Stratford, Fairfield, and beyond. As a new member of the All About Kids Pediatric Dentistry family, we carry forward a legacy of compassionate, expert care trusted by Connecticut families for generations.

Se Habla Español? Falamos Português?

We are multi-lingual! If you happen to speak either Spanish or Portuguese, you’ll be happy to know that we do too. You’ll have no trouble letting us know what you need, where any pain is, or how easy your visit was. Simply tell us your language preferences when you make your appointment or when you come in for your visit!
