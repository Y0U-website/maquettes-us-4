Getting A Teeth Cleaning

Everyone strives to maintain clean and healthy teeth, but there’s no substitute for a professional dental cleaning.

Dental prophylaxis, the technical term for teeth cleaning, is designed to thoroughly clean your mouth in areas your toothbrush and floss struggle to reach. The benefits of teeth cleaning include:

  • Plaque removal – Despite diligent brushing and flossing, it’s challenging to eliminate all plaque and debris from your gums and teeth. Our specialized hygienists utilize various dental instruments to clean and remove any plaque buildup.
  • Healthy gums – Gums that don’t receive regular professional cleanings are prone to pain, bleeding, and gum disease. Biannual teeth cleanings help protect against gum disease, resulting in healthier and stronger gums.
  • Improved oral health – After your teeth cleaning, you’ll leave with a cleaner, whiter smile. You may also notice fresher breath as old bacteria and plaque are removed.
