Dental Implants

Often, people face circumstances that result in tooth loss. Whether a tooth is knocked loose due to an accident or gradually falls out from trauma or decay, losing a tooth can happen to anyone. While maintaining a regular oral hygiene routine helps keep your mouth healthy, it’s important to have a dentist ready to replace a tooth in case of an emergency. At Smile Time Dental, our dentists are well-trained to provide dental implants and have the necessary tools to ensure your experience is comfortable.

If you have a damaged tooth that needs removal or replacement, our team is ready to assess the situation and develop a treatment plan. Feel free to contact our Stockton office for any questions or information about dental implants. We’re happy to assist you!

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants might sound intimidating, but they are completely safe when placed correctly by a skilled professional. These titanium posts are surgically inserted into the lower or upper jawbone and serve as an anchor for the replacement tooth. The custom replacement tooth mimics the appearance of a natural tooth, ensuring it blends seamlessly with the rest of your teeth. This not only improves the cosmetic appearance of your smile but also restores function, making chewing easier and helping to keep your teeth and gums aligned.

Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant procedure is something our team at Smile Time Dental frequently performs. A certified oral surgeon will surgically place a metal anchor into the jawbone to replace the missing roots. Once implanted, the anchor fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This process can take a couple of months, but after the anchor is securely integrated, it will be strong enough to withstand significant force. During the integration period, we will install a temporary crown. This crown will be custom-designed for shape, size, fit, and color to blend with your natural teeth.
