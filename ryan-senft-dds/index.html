The practice

We offer comprehensive dental care for the whole family in Cupertino, CA.

Cosmetic Dentistry – If your teeth are healthy, but you’re unhappy with their appearance, cosmetic dentistry can give you the confidence to smile proudly. Our practice offers teeth whitening, dental veneers, and bonding.

Restorative Dentistry – When a tooth is compromised by dental trauma or decay, restorative dentistry can restore its function, strength, and appearance. These treatments include crowns, dental fillings, bridges, and dentures.

Dental Implants – Dental implants are the only tooth replacement option that restores both the root and the crown of a missing tooth. The result is a stronger bite, more stable restoration, and a youthful appearance.

Obstructive Sleep Apnea – Sleep apnea can often be cured with a simple oral appliance that repositions the lower jaw in order to keep the airway open as you sleep. Patients often find these appliances are more comfortable than CPAP machines.

Endodontics – When the pulp at the center of a tooth becomes irritated or infected, a root canal may be needed to save the tooth and relieve your pain. Today’s root canals are no more uncomfortable than getting a cavity filled thanks to modern technology and local anesthetic.
