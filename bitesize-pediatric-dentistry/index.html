The practice

We offer efficient, comfy dental cleanings for kids and teens. An expert hygienist will banish plaque and tartar and let you know how your child is doing with their oral hygiene routine.

Bringing your child in for regular dental exams with a Park Slope pediatric dentist is the key to preventing oral health issues. If we do spot a problem, treating it early on will be easier and more affordable.

Protective dental sealants are a cavity-fighting superhero! Sealing your child’s permanent molars keeps food, bacteria and acids from penetrating the enamel and causing decay.

As pediatric dental specialists we have a whole range of techniques we use to put kids at ease. But, if necessary, we also offer safe sedation to ensure your child can safely get the care they need.

Oral habits like tongue thrust and prolonged thumb sucking can cause a host of orthodontic concerns. Habit-breaking appliances are effective for stopping habits when nothing else works.

This oral appliance prevents the surrounding teeth from shifting into the space left behind when a baby tooth is lost too early. This way, the permanent tooth has room to erupt.

A: We recommend your child’s first visit by age one or within six months of their first tooth. Our Park Slope pediatric dentists make the experience fun, gentle, and stress-free.
