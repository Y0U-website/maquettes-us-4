When Should My Child See an Orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. That doesn’t mean your child will need braces right away—but it gives us a chance to spot subtle issues early. Think of it like taking a peek at the blueprint before the building’s finished. Our team can spot things like crowding, underbites, crossbites, and habits like thumb sucking that could impact jaw development.

The earlier we catch it, the easier it is to guide. That’s a win for your child—and their future grin.

When should my child get braces?

Braces usually come in around ages 7–14 , but some kids benefit from earlier guidance in Phase 1 treatment, especially for jaw or bite alignment.

What are the signs my child might need orthodontics?

Early or late loss of baby teeth, crowded or spaced-out teeth, overbites, underbites, thumb sucking, and mouth breathing are all reasons to schedule an orthodontic evaluation.

Are early orthodontic treatments painful?

Most kids adjust quickly! Appliances like expanders or spacers may feel weird at first, but they’re designed to be gentle and effective—no scary tools, we promise.

Is it covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans offer partial coverage for orthodontics. We’re happy to check your benefits and walk you through your options.

What if my child has anxiety or special needs?

You’re in the right place. Our team has special training and a flexible, patient-first approach. We take things at your child’s pace, explain everything in kid-friendly terms, and make sure everyone feels safe and seen.
