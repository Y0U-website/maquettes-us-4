Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Tina Wang graduated from Bowdoin College in 1982, and four years later received her D.M.D. Degree at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. She remained at Tufts as an Assistant Clinical Professor for another six years and in 1989, joined the practice with Drs. Thiel and Rubin.

As a top female dentist in Wellesley, Dr. Wang remains active in the Mass. Dental Society and serves as Co-Chair on multiple committees of the Yankee Dental Congress. She is also on the Steering/Advisory Committee which drives the 25,000 person educational and scientific meeting held annually here in Boston. She is currently a member of the Metropolitan District Dental Society peer review committee. This district is the largest in the state.

Outside the practice, Dr. Wang and her husband Chris are managing the active lives of their three children.

Dr. Damian Lee received his dental degree from University of Michigan and completed his specialty in Advanced Prosthodontics training at University of Illinois-Chicago. He is a diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics and treats patients of all prosthodontic needs including fixed, removable and implant prosthodontics.

Dr. Lee has taught in dental education nearly 20 years and currently is a tenured Associate Professor and Chair of Department of Prosthodontics at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He has served as officer for various dental organizations such as American College of Prosthodontists, American Academy of Fixed Prosthodontics, and American Dental Education Association. Dr. Lee has lectured nationally and internationally and enjoys publishing research papers in the area of prosthodontics.

Outside the office, Dr. Lee enjoys travelling, cooking, and spending time with his wife Christina and their two children.

Outside the office he enjoys skiing, golf, hiking, water sports and especially spending time with his wife Tricia, his son, daughter-in-law and granddaughter.

Dr. Xu received his DDS from Henan Medical University, China, in 2000. Upon his graduation, he attended Hiroshima University, Japan, for his first periodontal training combined with a Ph.D. major in tissue engineering for periodontal application.

Dr. Xu attended Boston University Goldman School of Graduate Dentistry and received his second dental degree (DMD) and periodontal certificate in 2015. In addition to private practice, Dr. Xu teaches clinical surgeries and Periodontology at Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine and continues his research in tissue engineering

Dr. Xu’s professional memberships include; The American Dental Association, the American Academy of Periodontology, the American Association of Dental Research, the International Association of Dental Research, the Japanese Society of Periodontology, and the Japanese Association of Conservative Dentistry. Dr. Xu is also a committee member of the Massachusetts Dental Society.

Outside of work he enjoys swimming, kayaking, music, and traveling.

Dr. Corner Gillin is passionate about cosmetic and restorative dentistry. It is her goal to make the dentist feel like a safe space for her patients. She believes her role as a dentist is to help her patients stay healthy and feel comfortable in their own skin, speak with confidence, enjoy meals with loved ones, and share their smile and laughter generously.

Dr. Corner Gillin spends her free time with her husband, Sam, and their golden retriever, Piper. She enjoys reading, pickleball, crossfit, and cooking. She is an avid traveller, who loves to explore new cultures and cuisines.
