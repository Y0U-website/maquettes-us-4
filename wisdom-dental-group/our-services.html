SERVICES

From routine exams to complex restorative procedures, we provide dental care that prioritizes your health and confidence. Whether it’s a quick checkup, a smile makeover, or immediate attention for a dental issue, our experienced team delivers thoughtful, modern care tailored to your needs.

Preventative Dentistry

Routine cleanings & exams, dental sealants, fluoride treatments, mouth guards, digital x-rays, oral cancer screenings

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canal therapy, dental implants, inlays and onlays

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, gum contouring, smile makeovers, orthodontic aligners

Orthodontics

Traditional braces, clear aligners, retainers, orthodontic consultations

Periodontal (Gum) Services

Scaling & root planing, gum grafting, periodontal surgery, laser gum therapy

Oral Surgery

Wisdom teeth removal, tooth extractions, bone grafting, dental implant placement, pre-prosthetic surgery

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric cleanings/exams, fluoride/sealants, space maintainers, behavior management, cavity treatment

Endodontics

Root canal therapy, apicoectomy, pulpotomy/pulpectomy

Emergency Dental Care

Toothache relief, broken/chipped teeth, lost fillings/crowns, avulsed teeth, dental trauma care
