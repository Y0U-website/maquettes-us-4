The practice

At Green Leaf Dentistry, we believe that great dental care starts with really listening to our patients: understanding your goals, your concerns, and your comfort level before recommending any treatment.

Dr. Ferraro built this practice around a simple philosophy: every patient deserves personalized care, and every visit should leave you feeling informed, comfortable, and cared for. Her gentle and expert care has earned the trust of families throughout the area; so much so that some of her patients travel from as far as Connecticut and Massachusetts to see her.

We pride ourselves on offering a wide array of services including routine cleanings and check-ups, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, and full mouth rehabilitation, so your family can get comprehensive care without being referred elsewhere for every specialized need.

We are committed to providing quality dentistry that is affordable for you and your family by participating with several dental insurance plans and offering personalized payment options.
