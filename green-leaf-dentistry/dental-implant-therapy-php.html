What is the difference between a dental implant and an implant crown?

  • Dental Implant: A dental implant is a surgical procedure that replaces a missing tooth. It consists of a titanium screw or post that is implanted into the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. Once the implant is securely anchored, a dental crown is attached to it, creating a natural-looking and functional tooth replacement.
  • Implant Crown: An implant crown is a dental crown placed on top of a dental implant. It is custom-made to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. The crown is the visible part of the tooth replacement and is designed to function like a real tooth for biting, chewing, and speaking.

Who is a good candidate for implant crowns?

Implant crowns are a good option for adults in good general and oral health who have one or more missing teeth. They are also suitable for individuals who cannot wear traditional dentures or bridges due to jawbone issues or discomfort.
