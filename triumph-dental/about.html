About Us

0 Mission Our mission is to bring hope and wholeness to the community of University Place and beyond. By way of an exceptional experience, we believe our clients will be met with a sense of belonging, and a commitment to high quality care through a holistic approach. We strive to reimagine what it means to go to the dentist or physical therapist, and hope to inspire and empower people to triumph over anything that life presents them.

Vision

SENSE OF BELONGING. When someone walks through our door we want them to know this is a safe and respectful place where you can be 100 % you without judgment, prejudice or conditions.

QUALITY CARE. We believe the amount of experience, our collaborative approach to dentistry and our commitment to continuing education allows us to develop the right individual treatment plan for every unique patient.

WHOLE PERSON CARE. Each interaction is an opportunity to fully engage with an individual beyond their oral health. We aim to meet them not only on the physical level, but also the mental, emotional and spiritual level.

RENEWED IMPRESSION. Our goal is to have every patient leave our clinic with a positive impression of what it means to go to the dentist. At the very first visit, we aspire to bring some kind of relief whether it’s physical, emotional, or mental.

INSPIRE AND EMPOWER. We hope to attest to our clients and patients through their experience that there is always hope. We want to inspire and empower them to triumph over whatever life throws at them and share that positivity with someone else in the community.

Ynna Simeon

Ynna Simeon was born and raised in the Philippine Islands. She migrated from the Philippines to Hawaii to pursue her nursing career. She came from Hawaii to Washington State to visit, fell in love with the scenery and beauty, and never left since.

With her heart set on serving and caring for others. She intends to continue her nursing career. She attended Tacoma Community College to complete her associate’s degree in pre-nursing. While studying, she began to work in dentistry to help her wonderful Aunt (Dr. Cheiryl). Only to discover her passion in Dentistry to work as a Dental Hygienist rather than a Nurse. She’s currently applying to different Dental Hygiene School and hopefully, after two years, she’ll be your Dental Hygienist here at Triumph!

Outside work and school, Ynna enjoys reading, cooking, baking, eating Japanese food, watching volleyball, playing pickleball, and running.
