Good dental hygiene is essential to maintaining a healthy smile. Our talented dentist and dental team at Sunlight Dentistry work hard to improve your oral health, but we cannot do it alone. To make sure that your smile stays healthy, be sure to practice good oral hygiene.

The simple dental hygiene tips on this page will help you prevent tooth decay, cavities, periodontal (gum) disease, and other dental problems. In addition, be sure to visit our Roaring Fork Valley dental office every six months for a regular teeth cleaning and oral exam.

To learn more about dental hygiene in Glenwood Springs, Colorado, we welcome you to call or visit our dental office today. Dr. Corey Johnson , and our entire dental team are committed to helping you keep your smile in the best of health!

Dental Hygiene Tips

Brush at least twice a day, or after every meal.

Floss at least once a day, before bedtime.
