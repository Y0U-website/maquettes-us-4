Choose the membership plan that fits your smile

Our membership plans are designed for patients who are uninsured, self-employed, retired, or simply looking for a more straightforward alternative to traditional dental insurance. Choose the plan that best matches your age, oral health needs, and recommended cleaning schedule.

Adult membership

Designed for patients ages 13 and older with regular cleanings and no periodontal disease. A $149 lifetime activation fee applies and includes the first month.

Perio membership

$70/ mo

Child membership

Why patients choose membership

A simpler way to plan for dental care

Dental insurance can feel confusing, especially when you are dealing with waiting periods, annual maximums, deductibles, or limited coverage for the care you actually need. Our membership plan keeps things more straightforward, giving uninsured patients immediate access to preventive care, transparent pricing, and savings on additional treatment at the dental home they already trust.
