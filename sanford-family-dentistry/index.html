More than 50 years in Sanford Our practice has served generations of families in this community for more than 50 years. Many of our patients have deep roots in Sanford, and we are honored to keep caring for the families who have trusted this office for decades.

A warm, home-like experience From your first phone call to your time in the chair, our goal is to help you feel comfortable and welcomed. Our reception area is designed to feel more like a living room than a waiting room, and our team takes the time to get to know you personally.

Honest, personalized treatment planning We use X-rays, intraoral photos, and clear explanations to show you what is happening in your mouth. Then, we tailor your treatment plan around your health, goals, comfort level, and readiness.

Spanish-speaking dental care Dr. Yoncee speaks Spanish and is passionate about helping more members of the Sanford community feel understood and cared for. Our team is continuing to grow this part of the patient experience so we can better serve Spanish-speaking families.

Services

Cleanings, exams, X-rays, and preventive visits help us protect your smile before small concerns become bigger problems. We take the time to explain what we see, answer your questions, and help you feel confident about your oral health.

Crowns and bridges Crowns and bridges can restore damaged, weakened, or missing teeth so you can chew, smile, and speak with greater comfort. Our team will walk you through your options clearly, without pressure or one-size-fits-all recommendations.
