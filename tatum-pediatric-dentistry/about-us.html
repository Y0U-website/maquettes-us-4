Meet the Team at Tatum Pediatric Dentistry

At Tatum Pediatric Dentistry, our mission is simple: to make dental visits fun for kids and stress-free for parents. Every child is treated with compassion, kindness, and respect because we believe in nurturing strong teeth and confident smiles.

A Place Kids Love

Imagine kicking back in the dental chair with a movie on the ceiling, Bluetooth headphones in place, and maybe a bit of laughing gas to help with any jitters. Our dental office is packed with kid-friendly amenities, including an iPad play area to keep young minds entertained while they wait. We also offer conscious sedation and general anesthesia to make sure even the most anxious patients have a comfortable experience.

Giving Back to Our Community

Dr. Guymon and our team believe in sharing smiles far beyond our dental office. From supporting local schools and athletic teams to contributing to missions, we’re proud to help our Scottsdale and Phoenix communities thrive.

Dr. James Guymon

Dr. Claire Carney

Dr. Carney is originally from Southern California and is excited to be living in Arizona! She has hopped coast to coast throughout her education as she went to Duke for college, UCLA for dental school, and back to North Carolina for residency at UNC-Chapel Hill.

Dr. Carney loves spending time with her husband and their golden retriever, Poppy. She enjoys hiking, running, reading, cooking, and watching Duke basketball!

Dr. Marytza Rios

Dr. Rios was born and raised in Tucson and proudly calls Arizona home. She earned her undergraduate degree from Santa Clara University and her dental degree from Loma Linda University. Her passion for working with children led her to complete pediatric specialty training at Rady Children’s Hospital in San Diego. Dr. Rios is a board-certified pediatric dentist, committed to providing gentle, high-quality care for every child she treats.

When not caring for patients, she enjoys spending quality time with her husband and their two boys. She loves cooking, practicing yoga, snowboarding, and exploring new restaurants with her family.

Dr. Bradley Farr

Dr. Bradley Farr, an Arizona native, spent part of his childhood in St. Croix, USVI, fostering an appreciation for education and diversity. He earned a business degree from Arizona State University before completing his DDS at USC and specializing in dental anesthesia at the University of Pittsburgh.

A Diplomate of the American Dental Board of Anesthesia, Dr. Farr is a member of the ASDA and ADSA and serves as Associate Faculty at NYU Langone Health.

He and his wife, Shanell, have three children and love spending time outdoors, visiting the lake and beach, and RVing with favorite trips to the California coast, Moab, and Southwest Colorado.
