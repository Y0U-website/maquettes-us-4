Meet Dr. Sidd

Dr. Siddhi Jindal, known as “Dr. Sidd,” is thrilled to join the Smile Works Team. Growing up, Dr. Sidd spent her summers as a youth camp counselor, where she discovered her passion for working with children-a passion that inspired her to specialize in pediatric dentistry, a decision she calls her best yet! Dr. Sidd strives to provide the highest quality of care possible and create positive, early dental experiences for her little patients.

She completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Rochester before returning to New Jersey to attend Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. There, she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree and graduated with honors in Special Health Care Needs.

Dr. Sidd then pursued her specialty training in pediatric dentistry at Temple University Hospital in Philadelphia, where she was honored to receive the James Roche Award and the Richard Pugh Award from the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry for her academic excellence. She takes pride in her involvement with events like Give Kids A Smile Day and The Special Olympics. Additionally, she serves as Vice President of Outreach for the Vraj Youth Camp, where she also worked as a counselor.

Outside the office, you can find Dr. Sidd cooking new vegetarian recipes, getting splat points at OrangeTheory Fitness classes, and exploring new running trails. She is looking forward to helping bring smiles to the Smile Works community!

Our Team

From the moment you step into our office, you’ll be greeted by our warm and friendly staff. They are specially trained in calming children who are anxious and fearful or those with special needs. They are ready to assist you and your child in any way possible, while setting your child at ease and making them feel relaxed and special.
