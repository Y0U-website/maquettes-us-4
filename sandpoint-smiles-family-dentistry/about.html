Get to know your dentist

Meet Dr. Justin Nelson

Dr. Justin Nelson is proud to offer down-to-earth, prevention-focused dental care to the community of Sandpoint. A native of Idaho Falls, he brings a local’s warmth and a deep respect for patient relationships to every appointment making it easy to feel at ease from your very first visit.

Dr. Nelson originally studied biology at Arizona State University with plans to pursue medicine. But after marrying a dental hygienist and learning more about the field, he found his calling in dentistry—and never looked back. He went on to earn his dental degree from Roseman University of Health Sciences, where he honed the clinical skills and values that shape his patient-first approach today.

What sets Dr. Nelson apart is his belief that dentistry should be minimally invasive, predictable, and rooted in long-term solutions. He’s passionate about helping patients get out of pain, save their natural teeth, and better understand the “why” behind their oral health.

Outside of the office, Dr. Nelson is an avid outdoorsman who loves fishing, snowboarding, hiking, and camping with his wife and daughters. You might even catch him at the local fishing derby, where he and his family have earned a few top spots over the years!

Here’s what your first visit will look like

Personalized care, from the very first hello

Whether it’s been six months or six years since your last dental visit, we’re here to welcome you without judgment. We’ll take time to get to know you, understand your needs, and create a game plan that fits your goals and comfort level.

Patient Experience During your first visit, you’ll receive a gentle but thorough exam, X-rays, and a one-on-one conversation with Dr. Nelson about your dental health. If you’re due for a cleaning and it’s appropriate, we’ll take care of that too. Expect a laid-back, friendly atmosphere where your voice matters and your time is respected.

What to Bring To make your visit smooth and efficient, please bring:

Your ID and insurance card (if applicable)

Any questions or concerns you’d like to discuss

Comfort and innovation, working together

We’re building an experience that feels good from start to finish.

Noise-Canceling Headphones

Nervous about noise? Our noise-reducing headphones help you relax during your appointment—so you can feel calm and focused while we work.

Nitrous (Laughing Gas) for Anxiety

If you feel nervous about dental visits, nitrous oxide, also known as laughing gas, can help you relax. It’s safe, gentle, and wears off quickly so you can feel calm and comfortable during your appointment.
