Meet the Rother Dental Team

Our Team

We are dedicated to delivering the highest quality of care and service

Here at Rother Dental, we are proud to have a team of friendly, energetic and hard-working professionals. Our team is built on a solid foundation of respect, friendship, and cooperation among our staff members as well as with our patients.

Successful individuals in our team possess a positive disposition, professional attitude, a great smile and a polished appearance. It is imperative to have the ability to think independently, to find solutions, and contribute to the advancement of the practice. Our team members have the drive to achieve new goals and go the extra mile to be extraordinary.

Our Dentists

Dr. Noah G. Auman resides in Shippensburg, PA with his wife, Rachel, and their two cats, Kiwi and Junior. He earned his undergraduate degree from Shepherd University and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from West Virginia University. Dr. Auman discovered his passion for dentistry while serving on mission trips to Belize as a dental assistant, where he was inspired by the opportunity to help others through dental care. Outside of the office, he enjoys spending time on the golf course.

Dr. Siddhant Gaidhane was born in India and raised in Kuwait. He is a licensed and trained dentist in both India and the United States and holds a Master of Public Health in Epidemiology and Biostatistics from Texas A&M University. Dr. Gaidhane graduated from the Virginia School of Dentistry in 2020 and was honored as the Best International Student Dentist in 2018.

He married his wife, Rayne Forshey, in September 2024. In their free time, they enjoy traveling, trying new cuisines, and spending time with their three cats, Lewie, Frida, and Juni.

Dr. Mian is a dedicated and compassionate dentist with deep roots in Hershey, Pennsylvania. After spending time living throughout the Northeast, he ultimately returned home to serve the community he knows and loves.

Dr. Mian earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2022, followed by completion of a General Practice Residency at the VA Medical Center in Baltimore in 2023. Through this advanced training, he developed a strong foundation in comprehensive patient care, with an emphasis on clinical excellence and patient comfort.

Outside of the office, Dr. Mian enjoys traveling, spending quality time with family and friends, and exploring new activities. He also has a passion for amusement parks especially Hershey Park, a lifelong favorite.

Dr. Mian is proud to be back in the Central PA area, providing high-quality dental care to his hometown community.

Dr. Emily Kerns is a compassionate general dentist dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. She believes that building trust with patients is just as important as delivering excellent clinical care and strives to ensure every patient feels heard, respected, and at ease during their visit.

Dr. Kerns earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology, with a minor in Astrobiology, from Penn State University before receiving her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine. She graduated with honors, earning Summa Cum Laude distinction, graduating among the top of her class, and was inducted into the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society in recognition of her academic achievement and commitment to excellence in patient care.

Following dental school, Dr. Kerns practiced general dentistry in the Chicagoland area, where she provided comprehensive care to patients of all ages. During this time, she also served as an adjunct faculty member at Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, mentoring dental students and helping educate the next generation of dentists. These experiences strengthened both her clinical skills and her passion for lifelong learning and patient-centered care.

As a first-generation college graduate and the first person in her family to earn a doctorate, Dr. Kerns is especially grateful for the opportunities that have shaped her career. She is passionate about helping patients improve their oral health while creating positive dental experiences through education, empathy, and personalized treatment.

Originally from the Lewisberry area, Dr. Kerns is excited to be practicing dentistry close to home and serving the community where she grew up. Outside the office, she enjoys baking, refinishing furniture, spending time with her husband, and their two cats, and exploring new restaurants and local attractions throughout Central Pennsylvania.
