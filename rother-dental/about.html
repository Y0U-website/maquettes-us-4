From our expert doctors, skillful hygienists, wonderful assistants, and caring front desk staff, all our employees are trained to provide the utmost care and experience to our patients. We know that great patient care starts with great employees. Meet some of the members of our first class team.

Our Mission

At Rother Dental we are dedicated to delivering the highest quality of care and service by putting our patients in the best hands.

We recruit, train, and empower the very best people so that we give everybody the attention they deserve.

Our comprehensive approach to dentistry seeks not only to fix problems, but to understand the underlying causes of oral disease. We want to focus on prevention. Less dentistry is better, but when you need dentistry we want it to last.

In addition to treating local individuals and families, we strive to support our community by committing to outreach and charity.

Our History

Rother Dental has been providing exceptional dental care services to the community of Mechanicsburg, PA since 2017.

Our practice has a long-standing history of excellence and commitment towards improving the oral health of our patients.

Our practice was established with a vision to provide high-quality, compassionate, and affordable dental care to all. Over the years, Rother Dental has grown into a reputable and trusted name in the community, earning the loyalty of our patients through our dedication to their well-being. Our team has continued to uphold the values and principles on which our practice was founded. They have also kept up with the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques, ensuring that our patients receive the best possible treatment.

In the Community

For several years, Rother Dental has been providing exceptional dental care to the Mechanicsburg community.
