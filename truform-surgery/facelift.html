What Is a Facelift?

A facelift is a surgical procedure that lets us rejuvenate the face by reducing looseness or sagging, creating a tighter, fresher look. This can address several cosmetic concerns of the face, including skin folds and loose skin around the jaw. This treatment isn’t designed to treat wrinkles or other skin-related concerns, but it can tackle more substantial structural concerns.

Who Is a Candidate for a Facelift?

Unfortunately, a facelift isn’t a good fit for everyone! This procedure is an option for patients who are looking to tighten the face after having lost elasticity. Ideal candidates should be in good health and should be willing to be open during consultation to make sure that their expectations are reasonable. We’ll go over your medical history to make sure that you don’t have any conditions that would make this procedure unsafe for you. We’ll also go over any general risks so you can make an informed decision.

What to Expect During a Facelift Procedure

Don’t worry–during your consultation, we’ll give you all the information you need so you can come to the surgery prepared. We’ll likely do an examination and take some pictures of your face. You might need to halt or alter certain medications you’re taking, especially blood thinners, to reduce the risk of any issues. Depending on the anesthesia you’re getting, you might need to stop eating for a set period of time before the surgery. Finally, make sure that someone is ready to drive you home and keep you comfortable.

Once you’ve been given anesthesia so you’re comfortable, we’ll go ahead and start the procedure. The type of incision we make will depend on the area we’re working on. After we’ve made precise cuts, we’ll raise the skin in the area to access the tissue underneath. We might remove or reposition fat, and we’ll maneuver your tissue and muscle to give you a tighter look. After the adjustments have been made, we’ll set your skin back in place and close the wounds. This procedure might take several hours to complete–perfection takes time!

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a facelift last?

The duration of your facelift results will depend on your specific treatment and age. Most standard facelift patients experience 10-15 years of results.

What is the optimal age for a facelift?

The best age for a facelift is between 35 and 65 years old. Patients are typically between 45 to 50 years old. Since facelifts help preserve facial appearance, they are best when performed as the beginning signs of aging occur.

Who is not an ideal candidate for a facelift?

Individuals without flexible skin and those who are severely overweight are not the best candidates for a facelift. The best way to determine if a facelift is right for you is through a private consultation with one of our doctors.

What is the recovery time after a facelift at TruForm Surgery?

Most patients can return to non-strenuous activities within 1 to 2 weeks after their facelift procedure at TruForm Surgery, with visible swelling and bruising typically subsiding during that timeframe.

Does TruForm Surgery offer mini facelifts and who is a good candidate?

Yes, both traditional and mini facelifts are available, with mini facelifts being ideal for patients showing early signs of sagging around the jawline or cheeks and seeking a less invasive option with shorter recovery.

What techniques are used for facelift surgery at TruForm Surgery?

Our surgeons use advanced techniques such as the deep plane facelift and SMAS methods to reposition underlying tissues and provide natural, long-lasting rejuvenation results.

Can a facelift be combined with other facial procedures at TruForm Surgery?

Yes, facelifts are often safely combined with procedures like neck lifts, eyelid surgery, laser treatments or facial fat transfer to achieve balanced and comprehensive results.

How long do facelift results last when performed at TruForm Surgery?

Facelift surgery typically delivers results that can last 7 to 10 years, with factors like skin care, lifestyle, and genetics influencing longevity.

How long does facelift surgery take?

How long your facelift surgery takes depends on the results you’re looking for, the complexity of the surgery, and whether it’s being combined with other cosmetic procedures. On average, it can take anywhere from 3 to 5 hours.

What type of anesthesia is used for a facelift at TruForm Surgery?

More often than not, we provide our patients with general anesthesia for a facelift. This ensures you’re completely comfortable, but it will mean you need someone to drive to and from the office.

When can I return to work after a facelift?

Most of our patients can return to work after 1-2 weeks after their facelift surgery. That said, it can depend on the type of job you have. If it’s more labor-intensive, you may need more time off.

What should I avoid after a facelift?

There are several things you’ll want to avoid after your facelift. Strenuous activities, heavy lifting, bending over, alcohol, smoking, and direct sun exposure to your incisions should all be avoided for 2-4 weeks. This ensures a smooth recovery and optimal results.

How long is the recovery process with a facelift?

Recovery from a facelift can take anywhere from 2 to 4 weeks. During this time, any swelling and bruising will subside, and you’ll be able to see your results more clearly. That said, it can take several months to a year for the internal tissues to fully heal and for any scarring to diminish.
