What Is a Neck Lift?

A neck lift is also known as a “lower rhytidectomy ,” and it’s a surgical procedure that lets us tighten and remove the skin around the neck and the jaw. This can minimize the appearance of many concerns. If you’re struggling with extra skin below the chin and neck or the appearance of jowls, a neck lift might be an excellent option for you.

What to Expect During a Neck Lift Procedure

We’ll make sure you’re as prepared as possible on the day of the surgery. You might need to stop or alter certain medications, especially ones that increase the risk of bleeding. This will ensure that things can go as smoothly as possible. Depending on the type of anesthesia we decide on, you may need to stop eating for a certain number of hours before the procedure. Also, make sure that you have a ride home and someone to keep you comfortable during the first couple of days after your surgery.

Once we’ve given you anesthesia, we’ll start the procedure by making one or more incisions. The results that you’re looking for will change the techniques that we use and the type of incision we make. Excess skin and fat might be removed, and we’ll reposition and tighten the muscle and tissue underneath. Depending on whether you’re getting another procedure at the same time, like facial liposuction , this procedure can take one to several hours. After we’ve achieved your desired shape, we’ll close everything up and make sure you’re good to return home and relax.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age for neck lift surgery?

The age best fitted for you to receive a neck lift depends on the state of your skin and your treatment goals. Most patients receive a neck lift between 35 and 65.

How long can a neck lift last?

A neck lift can tighten and tone the neckline for up to ten years before the usual signs of aging begin to redevelop.

How painful is neck lift recovery?

After your neck lift procedure, you may experience mild pain and discomfort. The most common complaint is a feeling of tightness around the neck where the skin was adjusted.

What issues can a neck lift address at TruForm Surgery?

Neck lifts are designed to remove excess skin, tighten underlying muscles, and contour the jawline, effectively reducing neck sagging and double chin appearance.

Is neck lift surgery outpatient at TruForm Surgery?

Yes, TruForm Surgery performs most neck lifts as outpatient procedures; patients typically return home the same day after surgery and follow specific aftercare instructions.

Are there visible scars after a TruForm Surgery neck lift?

Incisions are carefully placed behind the ears and sometimes under the chin, resulting in scars that are minimal and well-hidden in natural skin creases.

Who is a candidate for neck lift procedures at TruForm Surgery?

Ideal neck lift candidates have moderate to severe loose skin, muscle banding, and excess fat around the neck or under the chin, and want a more defined jawline.

How long does neck lift recovery take at TruForm Surgery?

Most patients experience initial swelling or bruising for about 1 week. Typically, patients can resume light activities within 1 to 2 weeks, although strenuous exercise is restricted for several weeks.

How long does a neck lift take?

Most neck lift procedures take anywhere from two to three hours. It can depend on the results you’re looking for and the complexity of the surgery.

What type of anesthesia is used during a neck lift?

More often than not, we use general anesthesia to ensure you’re completely comfortable during your neck lift, but it can depend on the extent of the surgery, your medical history, and your personal preferences.

When can I return to work after a neck lift?

Most patients can return to work 10 to 14 days after a neck lift. At this point, most of the swelling and bruising will have subsided. Desk jobs or light work can often be resumed in about a week or two, while physically demanding jobs may require three to four weeks of recovery time.

When can I start working out after my neck lift?

You can typically start light activities, such as walking, immediately or within the first week after a neck lift to promote circulation. Gentle exercise can begin around 2 3 weeks, but you should wait 4 6 weeks before you return to strenuous, high-impact, or heavy lifting workouts. By limiting strenuous activity, you’re ensuring you heal properly.

Where are the incisions placed during a neck lift?

During a neck lift, incisions are discreetly placed behind the ears and beneath the chin. This makes any scarring difficult to notice once fully healed.
