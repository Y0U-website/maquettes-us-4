New Patient Information

At Supreme Dental Group in Suwanee, GA, we believe every patient deserves personalized, high-quality dental care tailored to their unique needs. Your first visit to our practice is the foundation for a long-lasting, trusted partnership in achieving and maintaining your optimal oral health.

What to Expect as a New Patient

We understand that visiting a new dental office can feel intimidating. Our goal is to ensure your experience is as comfortable and stress-free as possible. From the moment you walk through our doors, we aim to provide a welcoming environment where your concerns are heard, and your oral health goals are prioritized. Taking the time to familiarize yourself with our practice, services, and the expertise of Dr. Zhang is a great first step toward building a confident and comfortable relationship with us.

Your First Visit: A Comprehensive Approach

Your journey toward exceptional dental care begins the moment you arrive at our modern, inviting dental office. A friendly member of our team will greet you with a smile, answer any initial questions, and provide a brief tour to acquaint you with our facility.

If you’ve already completed your new patient forms, we will promptly begin your appointment. This first visit includes:

  • Diagnostic imaging : We’ll take digital X-rays and intraoral images to gain a detailed view of your oral health.
  • Thorough cleaning : Our skilled dental hygienist will provide a meticulous cleaning to remove plaque and tartar, leaving your teeth feeling refreshed.
  • One-on-one consultation : Dr. Zhang will meet with you to review your diagnostic results, discuss your current oral health, and listen to any concerns or goals you have for your smile. This is your opportunity to ask questions and collaborate with us to develop a personalized treatment plan that suits your needs and lifestyle.

Patient-Centered Care

We take pride in making every patient feel valued and respected. Whether you’re here for routine preventive care, a smile makeover, or restorative treatments, our team is dedicated to delivering compassionate and comprehensive dental services that exceed your expectations.

Flexible Payment and Financing Options

To make quality dental care accessible, we offer a range of payment options:

At Supreme Dental Group, we’re here to help you achieve a healthy, beautiful smile while ensuring your experience is seamless and enjoyable. We look forward to welcoming you into our dental family and helping you feel at ease every step of the way.

  • Accepted payment methods : Cash, major credit cards, and other forms of direct payment.
  • Financing options : We partner with reputable financing providers like CareCredit and Cherry, enabling you to manage out-of-pocket expenses conveniently with affordable monthly payments.
