Tooth whitening is either restoration of natural tooth shade or whitening beyond natural tooth shade.

Sometimes, restoration of the underlying, natural tooth shade is possible by simply removing surface (extrinsic) stains (e.g. from tea, coffee, red wine and tobacco) and calculus (tartar). This is achieved by having the teeth cleaned by a dental professional (commonly termed "scale and polish").

Lighter tooth shade can be achieved by chemical whitening, however it should be done by professional guidance in order to avoid permanent teeth sensitivity or deteriorating health of your gum tissue.
