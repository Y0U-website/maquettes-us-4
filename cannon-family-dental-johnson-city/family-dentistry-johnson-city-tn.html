Treating Adults and Children

Whatever the age we love to see you smile. Keeping your smile healthy is important to start from a early age.

At Cannon Family Dental we are committed to your dental health. Our goal is for your entire family to have the best oral health possible.

We see all ages of patients from small children just getting their first teeth to the teen that may need Invisalign, or the more mature patients that require restorative dentistry, you can rest assured that we are here to help you.

We truly are a family dentist and we can also help you with cosmetic goals. Dr Elizabeth Cannon has the training of some of the best practitioners of cosmetic dentistry in the country. Dr Cannon loves helping each patient, no matter what age, achieve their dental goals.

We recommend children and adults to visit every six months for a checkup and cleaning. We like to review your dental health goals and catch any issues in their early stages while they are small and are affordably resolved.
