Mobile Dental services are offered to all schools in the district of Oconee County. Pediatric services include dental cleanings, fluoride treatments and sealants.

The practice

We are a Federally Qualified Health Center providing Primary Care , Pharmacy , Mental Health , and Dental Services .

All services are provided at discounted rates based on a sliding fee scale .

We are committed to providing accessible, high-quality, affordable care with compassion and respect.
