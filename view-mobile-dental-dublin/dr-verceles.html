Meet Dr. Verceles, DDS

Meet The Team

Dr. Tim Verceles is a graduate of UC Davis and attended dental school at UC San Francisco. He completed his General Practice Residency at the VA Hospital in Palo Alto, CA.

Dr. Verceles previous position at Highland Hospital include the past director of the Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program, the director of Special Care Dentistry and Implant Restorative Dentistry.

Dr. Verceles is currently member faculty at the University of Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry . He is an assistant clinical professor in the Department of Diagnostic Sciences and teaches Integrated Clinical Sciences, with the first years, Radiology with the second years, and in the Special Care Clinic with the third year students. He also is on faculty with the Pacific AEGD residents at the Union City AEGD clinic.

Dr. Verceles has advanced training in comprehensive restorative dentistry. He is trained in full mouth reconstruction, cosmetic and implant dentistry. He has an oral sedation permit from the state of California.

He holds a Fellowship and Mastership from the Academy of General Dentistry. He was elected as a Fellow in the American College of Dentists in 2018.
