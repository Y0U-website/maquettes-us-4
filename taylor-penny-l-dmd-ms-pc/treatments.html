All Ages

All Ages

Children

Early orthodontic checkups set the stage for lifelong oral health. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends your child’s first visit by age 7 to catch developing issues early. If treatment isn’t needed right away, we’ll monitor growth and guide you to the best time to begin. It’s all about being proactive, not premature.

Teens

The teen years are a great time to start orthodontic care—most adult teeth are in, and growth is still happening. We offer flexible options like clear aligners and metal or clear braces to match every lifestyle. Whether it’s for function, confidence, or both, we’ve got a treatment that fits. Let’s help your teen feel good about their smile.

Adults

It’s never too late to improve your smile. Whether you missed treatment growing up or are ready for a refresh, we offer discreet, effective options to fit your life. With flexible scheduling and treatment plans, we make orthodontics feel less like a chore and more like self-care. A confident smile looks great at every age.

Braces

Braces

Traditional

Traditional metal braces are a reliable option for correcting even the most complex bite and alignment issues. Today’s versions are smaller, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever. You can even personalize your look with colorful bands for a fun, custom touch.

Ceramic

Ceramic braces blend in with your teeth, offering a more discreet option without sacrificing strength or results. They’re great for teens and adults who want a subtler way to straighten their teeth. Just like metal braces, they’re effective—but with a smoother, low-profile look.

Angel Aligners ™

Angel Aligners ™

Angel Aligners are a modern alternative to braces that use a series of nearly invisible trays to move your teeth comfortably and discreetly. Designed with advanced materials for faster, more precise movement, they’re ideal for patients who want flexibility without sacrificing results. With no food restrictions and easy-to-remove aligners, maintaining great oral hygiene is a breeze—making it a favorite for teens and adults alike.

Expanders

Expanders

Expanders gently widen the jaw to create more space for crowded teeth and improve the bite. They’re most effective during childhood, when the jaw is still developing. This early intervention can help guide facial growth and prevent the need for more complex treatment later. It’s a simple way to set the stage for a healthier, more balanced smile.

Retainers

Retainers

You made it through treatment—now let’s keep those teeth in line! Retainers help maintain your results and prevent any shifting. From fixed to removable options, we’ll help you choose what fits your lifestyle best. Follow your retainer plan, and your smile will stay exactly where it should be.

Splint Therapy

Splint Therapy

If you’re dealing with jaw pain, tension headaches, or clicking sounds when you chew, splint therapy may help. A custom-made splint gently repositions your jaw to reduce stress on your joints and muscles. It’s a non-invasive, comfortable solution often used to treat TMJ disorders and nighttime grinding. With consistent use, many patients experience lasting relief and better jaw function.

Orthognathic Surgery

Orthognathic Surgery

Sometimes, orthodontics alone isn’t enough to correct jaw misalignment. Orthognathic surgery works hand-in-hand with braces or aligners to address more complex bite, function, or facial balance concerns. We’ll work closely with trusted oral surgeons to coordinate every step of your care. From your initial consultation to post-surgery treatment, we’re here to support you through the process with clarity, comfort, and expert guidance.

Habit Appliances

Habit Appliances

Thumb-sucking and tongue-thrusting may seem harmless, but they can affect jaw growth and tooth alignment over time. Habit appliances are custom-made tools that gently help your child break these habits without pain or pressure. They’re comfortable to wear and effective in guiding better oral development. Our goal is to support your child’s healthy growth with simple, proactive solutions.

Technology

Technology

3shape

3shape creates a quick and comfortable 3D scan of your teeth—no goopy impressions needed. It helps us design accurate treatment plans and ensures your aligners or appliances fit just right.

Digital X-ray

Our digital X-rays deliver crisp, detailed images with minimal radiation. They help us spot issues early and plan treatment with precision and confidence.

All-Digital Practice

From virtual check-ins to digital records and treatment tracking, our all-digital workflow makes your orthodontic journey smoother, faster, and more convenient than ever.
