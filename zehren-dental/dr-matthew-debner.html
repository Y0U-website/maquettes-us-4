Dr. Matthew Debner, DDS Dr. Debner is excited to begin his career with Zehren Dental in Winona, MN. A native of Zumbro Falls, he learned early the value of hard work and respect - principles that guide the way he practices today. After earning his undergraduate degree at Winona State University, he completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Iowa, where he developed a passion for patient-centered, evidence-based care that supports both oral and overall health.

Returning to Winona allows Dr. Debner to reconnect with family, friends, and the natural beauty of the area. Outside of the office, he enjoys cooking and sharing meals, fishing Minnesota’s lakes and rivers, and spending time with loved ones. He looks forward to building lasting relationships with his patients and serving the community with compassion and integrity.
