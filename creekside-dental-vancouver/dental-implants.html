Dental Implants

Dental implants are the closest people can get to having natural, healthy teeth. These implants can be life-changing, as they allow people the opportunity to enjoy everyday activities. For millions of people, dental implants give them the boost of confidence that they need to smile, laugh, or eat with ease.

What are dental implants?

Implants act as artificial tooth roots that are placed in the jawbone. They bond with your natural bone and fuse together to be a sturdy base for one or more artificial teeth, which are custom made to match your natural teeth and to fit comfortably. A connector is used on top of the implant to hold and support the crowns or other prosthetic replacement. For people who need support replacement teeth, dental implants are highly effective and look and function very naturally.

Who qualifies for dental implants?

People that have missing teeth can qualify for dental implants. There are guidelines your dentist will use in order to determine if implant replacement is appropriate for each individual situation. While there are other tooth replacement options, dental implants are often a preferred alternative and they can help keep the jawbone healthy.

Are dental implants safe?

Before undergoing a dental implant procedure, you will need to talk in detail with your dentist to determine if you are a candidate. Your doctor will discuss your medical and dental history, look at the overall health of the supporting bones in your facial structure and jaw, and then determine if this type of treatment is appropriate in your situation.

Dental implants are safe and effective, but as with any medical procedure, there can be complications. Talk to your dentist about what you can do to minimize potential problems. People lose their teeth every day, due to tooth decay, periodontal disease, or injury.
