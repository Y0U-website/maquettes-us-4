Our husband-and-wife team is dedicated to providing exceptional care for your family’s complete dental health.

Dr. Amir Sobhi, a Scottsdale native, is dedicated to bringing compassionate and high-quality dental care to his community. He completed his undergraduate studies at Arizona State University before earning his dental degree from AT Still University. With a focus on patient comfort and a commitment to staying current with the latest advancements in dentistry, Dr. Sobhi ensures each patient receives personalized care tailored to their needs.

Outside of the office, Dr. Sobhi enjoys spending time with his wife Paige, their three rescue dogs—and their two rescue cats. A big Arizona sports fan, Dr. Sobhi is always cheering on his favorite local teams. Together, he and Paige are passionate about animal rescue and giving back to their community. Dr. Sobhi looks forward to welcoming you to Sobhi Family Dentistry, where you’ll feel like part of the family.

Paige Behmer, our dedicated Dental Hygienist, is passionate about making patients feel comfortable and confident in their dental care. Growing up in Lake Havasu City, Paige went on to study at Mohave Community College, where she honed her skills and love for patient-centered dental care.

When she’s not at Sobhi Family Dentistry, Paige volunteers with local dog rescues, reflecting her deep love for animals. She also enjoys hiking with her girlfriends all over Arizona, exploring new trails and beautiful landscapes. Alongside her husband, Dr. Amir Sobhi, and their three rescue dogs and two rescue cats, Paige is committed to bringing compassion and care to both her patients and her community.
