What Are Your Dental Implant Restoration Options?

The following are your dental implant restoration options:

  • Single Implant Crowns: Placing a dental crown on a single implant will provide you with a natural-looking and durable solution to restore your smile.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges: Implant-supported bridges are perfect for replacing one or several teeth in a row, enhancing the function and appearance of your smile.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: These removable overdentures attach to implants strategically placed in your jawbone, providing unmatched stability, function, and durability.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Here is a look at some of the benefits of dental implants:

  • They offer a superior solution for replacing missing teeth, delivering incredibly lifelike results.
  • They are made to resemble your natural teeth, enhancing the aesthetics of your smile.
  • They mimic the function of the missing tooth roots, preventing bone loss in the area.
  • They integrate with the jawbone, providing a secure and stable foundation for your replacement teeth.
  • They restore your chewing function, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods without restriction.
  • They help you speak more clearly without worrying your replacement teeth may shift out of place.
  • They fill gaps in your smile, preventing any remaining teeth from moving out of alignment.
  • They boast an incredibly high success rate of over 95 percent.
  • They can last your lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular visits to your dentist.
