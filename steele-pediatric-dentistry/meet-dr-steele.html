Steele Pediatric Dentistry

Dr. Steele and her husband Brian (a St. Clairsville native) are thrilled to be back in the Ohio Valley. Together they have two children, Teddy and Helen. As a parent to two youngsters, she knows the challenges that raising a child can bring and wants parents to feel comfortable asking her and staff important questions about their children’s mouths. When she is not treating patients in the office, Dr. Steele enjoys spending time with her family and cheering on her alma maters’ sports teams.
