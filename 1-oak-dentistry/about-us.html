Dr. Marc Dadkhah

Dr. Marc Dadkhah primarily hails from California but has also called SW Florida home for the past decade. He graduated with a Bachelor’s degree in Biology and Biochemistry from the University of California, Irvine. He also completed an Advanced Master’s program in Research at the University of California, Los Angeles. He attended dental school at Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

Dr. Marc then completed a one-year General Practice Residency at one of the top residency programs in the country, the Albert Einstein Medical Center in Philadelphia, where he received advanced training in multiple aspects of dentistry including Dental Emergencies and Trauma, Implant Dentistry, and Restorative and Surgical Dentistry.

Dr. Marc has published more than 20 articles and scientific papers in well-known journals such as PubMed and the NIH.

Dr. Dadkhah is constantly continuing his education on the latest dental techniques and approaches to offer his patients remarkable and comfortable treatments. He is passionate about utilizing his education, experience, and knowledge of modern technology to provide high-quality dental care. Outside the office, he enjoys traveling, art, and photography.

Dr. Marc has been serving SW Florida for many years, and now he has made the transition from Corporate Dentistry to his own private dental clinic to provide excellent services to his neighbors and bring trust back to the community.

Dr. Andrea Hernandez

Endodontist

Dr. Hernandez grew up in Orlando, FL. She graduated dental school from Nova Southeastern University where she was the recipient of the Endodontics Department Award of Excellence. Following graduation, Dr. Hernandez completed a general practice residency at Wyckoff Heights Medical Center in Brooklyn, NY; she then worked as a general dentist in Cape Coral before returning to school to complete a 26-month residency in endodontics.

Dr. Hernandez completed her post-graduate training at the renowned University of Texas Health Science Center in Houston where she was awarded a Masters of Science in Dentistry and a certificate in Endodontics. Over the past year Dr. Hernandez has received several awards for her research and has been published in the Journal of Endodontics.

Dr. Hernandez speaks English and Spanish fluently. She enjoys traveling, yoga, the beach and spending time with her family.
