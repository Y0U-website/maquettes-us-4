Dr. Derek Schmidt

A lifelong Waukesha resident.

Dr. Cody prides himself in offering a comprehensive approach to dental care, taking the time to meet with and create individual treatment plans that merge patient needs and wants to maximize oral health. He is also passionate about continuing to grow as a clinical provider, enrolling in many continuing education courses on the latest dental techniques in procedures.

After meeting his wife Annie in New York, the Novotnys moved back to Wisconsin to start a family, welcoming a son this past year. While not in the office, Dr. Cody enjoys spending time with his family, as well as hiking, fishing, and cheering on Wisconsin sports teams.
