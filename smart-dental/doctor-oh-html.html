Meet Dr. Oh

Dr. Dongha Oh is a periodontist who provides bone and tissue regenerative procedures, oral health rehabilitation with dental implants, and cosmetic and periodontal surgery. He is a graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, where he completed his dental training and earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine with the honor of the Dr. Earle Banks Hoyt teaching award.

Seeking further expertise in dentistry, Dr. Oh continued a three-year specialty residency in periodontics and implant dentistry at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine. Here, he earned a Certificate in Periodontics and a Master of Dental Science degree. Presently, Dr. Oh is a diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology.

Dr. Oh embraces the concept of comprehensive dental care, with collaboration with your primary dental provider, to reach optimal oral health and a naturally beautiful smile. He always aims to achieve an uncompromised standard of excellence in dental care to assure the best quality dental service that you deserve. Dr. Oh is fully aware of how anxious patients might feel about dental and surgical treatment. He strives to offer a relaxing and positive experience with gentle care.
