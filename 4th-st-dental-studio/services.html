Our mission

To deliver the highest standard of comprehensive dentistry with the utmost integrity and responsibility to our patients and the environment. With a genuine commitment to elevating our patients’ oral and overall health, we provide gentle, compassionate dental care with honesty and authentic patient-centered service.

Your First Visit

Expect nothing but patient-centered family dental care from the Fourth Street Dental Studio team. Unless you are here for an emergency or a specific treatment, your first visit may include a comprehensive dental exam, diagnostic photos, oral cancer screening, a face-to-face conversation with the dentist, and a customized professional cleaning to meet your specific comprehensive dental health needs.

Membership Program & Insurance

Please gather your insurance documents and bring them with you, along with your photo ID, to your appointment. We accept most dental insurances and are in-network with many PPOs. No insurance? Don’t worry. We offer an in-house membership program that lowers the cost of all our dental services. Membership is quick, simple, immediate, and available to anyone no matter the status of your oral health. Call or e-mail us today, and we will provide you with complete information about dental insurance benefits, payment options, and our cost-effective membership program.
