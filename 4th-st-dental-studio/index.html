Dental implants are used to replace a missing tooth’s root. They are made from strong metal, such as titanium, and are screw-shaped. They are most commonly inserted into a patient’s jaw bone. Once the bone and implant fuse together and heal completely, an abutment can be attached to the implant and finally a tooth-colored crown to achieve a comfortable, natural-looking result.

Dental implants have a great success rate along with excellent at-home oral hygiene care. However, just as a person can develop gum disease around their natural tooth, they can also develop gum disease around dental implants. Because of this, it’s important to practice great oral health habits consistently to ensure your teeth implants stay in tip-top shapes for years to come.

The best candidate for dental implants is someone who has great oral hygiene habits, is missing a tooth, and does not need a bone graft, which can be typical for those who’ve been missing teeth for an extended period of time. Additionally, they wouldn’t use tobacco and don’t have larger restorations on the teeth adjacent to the space where the tooth is missing.
