About us

Sagawa NW Dentistry was born from a long history of the best patients, gentle dentistry, and invaluable friendships. Dr. Juan Carlos Sagawa was invited to join Dr. Rose Bailey’s practice in 2012, allowing her growth, time with family, and the opportunity to inch closer to retirement. After ten years working together, Dr. Sagawa took over in a seamless transition and Dr. Bailey closed the books after 26 years of success. In 2022, we became Sagawa NW Dentistry.

DOCTORS

Our doctors are representative of our diverse and growing family of patients living in Thurston County and beyond.

Juan Carlos Sagawa

Juan Carlos Sagawa

Dr. Juan Carlos Sagawa attended the Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia in Lima, Peru, and graduated from their Dental Science Master Program in 2000. Three years later, he moved to the United States to complete the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine and was asked to join their two-year Fellowship in General Dentistry.

In 2007, he moved to Olympia and spent 6 years at a community health center serving the low-income population. In 2013, he joined Dr. Rose Bailey as an Associate Dentist until he took over the practice in 2022.

“Sagawa NW Dentistry has been my dream for a long time,” said Dr. Sagawa. “Dr. Bailey is my mentor, my friend, and my family. I am so honored she believed in me to take over her practice and make my dreams come true.”

Dr. Sagawa lives in Olympia with his wife Kati and their two children Mia and Mateo. He loves surfing, soccer, traveling, salsa dancing, and family camping trips in the summer.

Rose H. Bailey

Rose H. Bailey

Dr. Rose Bailey began her career in dentistry as a dental hygienist in 1978 in the office of Dr. Robert Phillips. A single mom with two children, she worked there for 13 years before going back to school to become a dentist herself, and she graduated from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 1996. She took over the practice when Dr. Phillips retired and practiced compassionate care for our patients until turning the practice over to Dr. Sagawa in 2022.

“I have thoroughly enjoyed my time and feel rewarded if I helped out patients. I worked with an incredible team and have transitioned to a mostly retired dentist now,” said Dr. Bailey. “Dr. Juan Carlos and Ana have made a great contribution to the practice and I am here to help when I can.”

In her free time, Dr. Bailey loves spending time with her family and grandchildren and spending time outdoors.

Joseph Haynes

Joseph Haynes

Dr. Joseph Haynes joins the Sagawa NW team a few times each month as the practice’s periodontist and dental surgeon. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the College of Dental Medicine at Columbia University before a three year residency in periodontics at the Oregon Health and Science University.

Dr. Haynes volunteers at many Tacoma area nonprofits and enjoys a quiet life in the Brown’s Point neighborhood with his husband and dogs.

TEAM

The Sagawa NW Dentistry team proudly support our doctors to give you an elevated experience that will change the way you think about going to the dentist.

Ana Ramirez

Registered Dental Hygienist & Office Manager

Ana Ramirez

Ana is the practice’s longest employee, committing her entire dentistry career to Dr. Bailey and now Dr. Sagawa. She has moved from dental assistant to dental hygiene student to hygienist, and now serves as the office manager for Sagawa NW Dentistry.

“I consider myself fortunate to help the team in many ways because of my time in this office,” Ana said. “I also value the trusting relationships I build with each patient so they can understand and value their dental care.”

Ana enjoys traveling, dancing, and Zumba. She lives with her partner Dwayne in Tumwater and loves their many adventures together.

Kacia Kaul

Kacia Kaul

Alejandra Corona-Avelar

Alejandra Corona-Avelar

Alejandra began her dental career at our clinic during her Clover Park Technical College internship in 2017 and has been with us ever since.

In her free time, Alejandra loves taking care of her family, going on walks, watching TV series with her husband, and planning their next adventures.

Lezly Hartley

Lezly Hartley

After 32 years as a Certified Public Accountant, Lezly retired and began to care for her grandson. Then a patient of Dr. Bailey, Lezly was asked to consider returning to work as the practice’s financial coordinator in 2017. She took the job and maintains in her role today at Sagawa NW Dentistry.

“Having first been a patient and experiencing the kindness, compassion, and laughter of the office, I knew it was a place I wanted to be,” she said.

Lezly enjoys spending time with her grandson, puttering around her house and yard, and traveling to places like Seaside, Chelan, and Mexico with her husband.

Ella Lieske
