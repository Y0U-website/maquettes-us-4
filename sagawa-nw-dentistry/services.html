Services

One of the best things about home is being with family. From baby’s first dental exam to visits for great grandparents—and everyone in between—everyone in your family will feel at home with our range of services.

Preventative

We believe in taking a holistic approach to anything that impacts our bodies and minds, including dental health. Positive oral hygiene is an essential piece of your overall health and well-being, and we’re here to help keep you on track with regular visits and personalized support.

Restorative

If we recognize the need to repair a tooth, we’ll make a plan to restore its function and longevity without over-treatment. Our doctors focus on fixing any problem areas and get you on a path to feeing great.

Cosmetic

When your oral health is stable and you’re ready to take your confidence to the next level, we’d love to discuss our cosmetic services. Tell us about your dream smile and our doctors will work to move you closer to your personal goals.

Surgery

If the need for a dental surgery arises, you’re in caring hands with the Sagawa NW team. From placing dental implants to restorative laser therapies and more, our experienced doctors will swiftly complete the work needed to get you feeling great.

Sleep Health

The link between sleep and oral health is much stronger than you may think. We can assist in identifying breathing and sleeping problems in adults and children, and work in collaboration with your physician for treatments like oral appliance therapy.

New Patient Exam

Coming home to Sagawa NW Dentistry begins with a visit tailored just for you. We’ll spend time getting to know you, discuss your health and dental history, and perform a comprehensive oral exam. Together, we’ll review our findings and begin to craft a holistic treatment plan for you based on your current health and personal goals.
