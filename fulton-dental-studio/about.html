DR. AMITOJ CHANDHOKE

Dr. Amitoj Chandhoke started his path to becoming a doctor at a young age by helping and shadowing his father, a dentist on Fulton Street. Amitoj was motivated to follow in his father s footsteps by witnessing his father s positive impact on the Bed-Stuy community and the confidence his father gave his patients. Dr. Chandhoke strives to build long-lasting relationships with his patients and the community with a positive approach to your dental care.

Using cutting-edge and state-of-the-art technology, he will deliver accurate diagnosis and check all of your restorations crowns, fillings, and bridges. Taking a holistic and unique approach, Dr. Chandhoke can offer you and your family the highest-level care you deserve.

Dr. Chandhoke was born and raised in New York City, where he attended Stuyvesant High School and was accepted into a 7-year combined BA-DDS program. He attended Adelphi University on a presidential scholarship for three years and graduated with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from NYU College of Dentistry in 2017. He completed his post-graduate residency at New York Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital, where he trained under some of the finest instructors and perfected his clinical skills.

Dr. Chandhoke has extensive experience, knowledge, and expertise in general dentistry. He is an Invisalign-certified provider and can help you perfect your smile.

Dr. C enjoys reading, good music, and playing basketball outside the office. Integral to his life are his faith and meditation. He volunteers in discussions on mindfulness to help better mental health and overall wellness
