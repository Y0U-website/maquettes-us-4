All on four

0 Dental Hybrids All-on-Four dental implants are a procedure that could be used to replace a full arch of missing teeth in a single day. The procedure involves placing four dental implants in the jawbone, which are then used to support a fixed prosthesis. This prosthesis can be made of a variety of materials, including porcelain, zirconia, and acrylic.

All-on-Four implants are a great option for patients who are tired of wearing dentures , have extensive tooth decay, or are looking for a permanent solution to missing teeth. The procedure has a high success rate and is associated with a relatively short recovery time.
