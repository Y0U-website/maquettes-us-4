Our Team

Family, community, and healthy living are the building blocks for best dental care at Restoration Dental in Stanwood, Washington

With over two decades of experience, Dentist Scott VonBergen provides expert dental and oral care in the Stanwood, Camano Island and Snohomish County.

For more than 20 years, Dr. Scott VonBergen has been making smiles healthier and brighter. Graduating in 1995 from Loma Linda University’s School of Dentistry, Dr. Scott first took a position at the Guam Seventh-day Adventist Clinic in Tamuning, Guam, where he worked for eight years, becoming their Dental Director in 2000.

After his time in the islands, Dr. Scott, a native north-westerner, relocated to Stanwood with his family, opening Restoration Dental in 2004. In Guam he experienced natural beauty and tight knit community, and the move to Stanwood helped solidify the importance of open spaces and close relationships.

An avid runner, bicyclist, and hiker, Scott is also a member of the Stanwood YMCA where he enjoys working out with Master’s swim, spin class and yoga. He and his wife Sheila, along with their two daughters, love camping, getting their kayaks out on the water, and making music together. He and Sheila first met preparing for a piano concerto competition and the entire family loves sharing the gift of music — playing the piano, guitar, bass, flute, and ukulele between them.

This love of community, sharing, and staying active is a big part of why he went into dental health, to help improve his patients’ quality of life.

“Eating, talking, and smiling are such an important part of people’s health, and whether it’s having the ability to eat the food you want or having the confidence to smile in public, helping people progress and reach their goals has always been satisfying.”
