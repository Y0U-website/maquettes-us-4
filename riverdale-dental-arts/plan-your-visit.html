Your First Visit

WHAT TO EXPECT

First visits take approximately 60 to 90 minutes. This gives our team time to learn about your medical and dental history, discuss any immediate concerns, and complete a thorough evaluation of your teeth and gums.

A prophylaxis (professional teeth cleaning) may be included for new patients who do not have any periodontal issues. If we do identify gum disease requiring periodontal treatment, such as scaling or root planing, we will schedule that for a later time.

X-Rays and Scans

Unless suitable recent scans are transferred from your previous dentist, we will take a full mouth series of digital X-rays, including bitewings. Digital X-rays provide clear images immediately, and they use less radiation than traditional film X-rays. They can reveal problem areas that cannot be seen during a visual evaluation. They also provide a useful baseline for monitoring changes when you come back for future dental checkups.

Periodontal Readings

We will measure the spaces between your teeth and the surrounding gum tissue to assess your current periodontal (gum) health. This process is known as periodontal probing, and it helps to identify any signs of gum inflammation, infection, or bone loss. If scaling and root planing are needed, we will explain why and arrange a separate visit for periodontal treatment.

Evaluation Findings & Recommendations

Dr. Kupferman will explain his findings from your evaluation and imaging and answer any questions you may have. In most cases, you will receive a comprehensive list of recommended treatments and the options available to you. If your needs are more complex, Dr. Kupferman will arrange a separate consultation so that he has sufficient time to assess your case and formulate his recommendations.

Financial Consultation

Riverdale Dental Arts is transparent about treatment fees. Before you proceed, our team will explain the expected costs and answer any financial questions you may have. We can also help you estimate your insurance benefits and anticipated out-of-pocket expenses, giving you a clearer understanding of the expected costs involved before treatment begins.
