Dr. Sheldon Kupferman

Dr. Sheldon Kupferman was born and raised in the Bronx and has lived in Riverdale with his wife, Ilene, since 1990. Except for three years spent in Manhattan during his education, the Bronx has always been his home. It is where he and Ilene raised their four children, established lasting community connections, and built their life together.

Dr. Kupferman jokes that he became a dentist because his mother insisted upon it. His parents were hardworking immigrants who wanted their three sons to attend college and become professionals. They were particularly inspired by a successful dentist in their extended family who was known for his kindness, generosity, and service to others. As Dr. Kupferman considered his own future, he discovered that dentistry offered a great combination of science, artistry, engineering, and helping people.

Following dental school, Dr. Kupferman completed his residency and fellowship through a joint program involving Bronx-Lebanon Hospital, Montefiore Hospital, and Albert Einstein College of Medicine. He began practicing in 1991 and soon purchased a practice from a retiring dentist in Van Cortlandt Village. Seven years later, he acquired a second practice in Riverdale.

Dr. Kupferman looks at how the entire mouth functions as a system. His experience in surgery, endodontics, bone management, and dental implant placement allows him to manage a broad range of dental needs efficiently within the practice.

Outside the office, Dr. Kupferman and Ilene enjoy spending time with their four children and many grandchildren, who now live around the world. Much of their vacation time is devoted to visiting family and bringing everyone together whenever possible. Dr. Kupferman is also a keen cook and self-described foodie who loves experimenting with flavorful, healthy recipes and hosting family, friends, and community members. He enjoys walking in nature, particularly along beaches, and studying the Bible and the Talmud.

Meet the Team

Christa

Business Administrator and Practice Director

Christa supports patients and team members while helping every aspect of the practice operate smoothly. Working behind the scenes, she coordinates office operations, responds to patient concerns, and helps create an environment where everyone feels comfortable, valued, and well cared for. She believes clear communication, compassion, and genuine connection are essential to a positive healthcare experience.

Coming from a family of physical therapists, Christa was naturally drawn to helping others. She initially studied toward a Doctor of Physical Therapy at Mercy University before discovering that her strengths and interests were better suited to healthcare administration and operations. She particularly enjoys connecting with patients, hearing their stories, and helping those who may feel nervous about dental care feel welcome and at ease.

Outside of her work at the practice, Christa works with students as chief assistant at Kumon Hartsdale and as a private tutor. She enjoys spending time with her family, friends, and fiancé, cooking, playing video games, and caring for her two Pomeranians, Ikko and Iya. A talented musician, she also loves singing and playing instruments.

Biljana

Registered Dental Hygienist

Biljana is a dedicated dental hygienist with a degree in dental hygiene and a Master’s Degree in Public Health. She helps patients improve their oral health through individualized preventive care, practical home-care guidance, and education about the connection between oral and overall health.

Biljana enjoys developing lasting relationships with her patients and creating a positive environment where they feel heard, supported, and empowered to take an active role in their oral health care. When not working, Biljana enjoys spending time with family and friends, traveling to new destinations, and continuing to learn about health and wellness.

Faye

Dental Assistant

Faye joined Riverdale Dental Arts in 2022 and brings over 25 years of experience in general dentistry to her role. She holds a Bachelor of Science from Empire State University, part of the State University of New York system.

Faye enjoys developing warm and positive relationships with patients and helping them get the dental care they need. Through her experience, skill, and caring approach, she has formed strong connections with her patients, always ensuring they feel supported. In her free time, Faye enjoys traveling, dining out, and spending quality time with her family.

Teresa

Front Office & Registered Dental Assistant

Teresa has been working in dentistry since 2016. She began her career as a dental assistant and has since gained experience in general dentistry, orthodontics, and patient care. She holds certifications in dental assisting, dental radiology, and medical office and administration.

What Teresa enjoys most about dentistry is the people. She loves getting to know her patients, making them feel comfortable, and creating a warm, friendly experience when they come into the office. She believes that taking the time to listen and connect with someone can make all the difference.

Outside of work, Teresa is an avid reader with a lifelong love of history. She especially enjoys learning about ancient history, philosophy, and the ideas and traditions that have shaped our world.

Our Approach to Your Care

Dignity and Respect

Dr. Kupferman treats every patient with compassion and without judgment. He wants everyone who visits Riverdale Dental Arts to feel welcome, respected, and comfortable receiving care.

Solutions to Improve Daily Life

Being able to eat comfortably and live without persistent dental pain can make a meaningful difference to your health and quality of life. Dr. Kupferman focuses on solving the problems that affect you most.

Achievable Results

A treatment plan is only useful if it is realistic for the patient. Dr. Kupferman establishes clear priorities and develops options around your health, needs, and financial circumstances.
