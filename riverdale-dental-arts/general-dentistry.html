A professional cleaning at Riverdale Dental Arts removes hardened dental deposits and buildup from areas that are challenging to clean at home. Oral Cancer Screenings

EVALUATING YOUR ORAL TISSUES WITH BIOSCREEN

Dr. Kupferman uses BioScreen to evaluate the tissues inside your mouth for signs of abnormal areas that may need further investigation. This small handheld device uses a special light to make unusual tissue easier to see during your evaluation. BioScreen is similar to VELscope and helps to ensure screenings are quick and as comfortable as possible for patients.

Fluoride Varnish

EXTRA PROTECTION AGAINST DECAY

Fluoride varnish is brushed directly onto the teeth to strengthen enamel and make it more resistant to decay. Dr. Kupferman often recommends fluoride varnish for children and adults who frequently develop cavities. It can also be useful for patients who suffer from tooth sensitivity. The varnish takes only a few minutes to apply and can be included as part of your regular preventive dental appointment at Riverdale Dental Arts.

Dental Sealants

COVER THE PLACES YOUR TOOTHBRUSH MIGHT MISS

The grooves in the back teeth are quite narrow, and food and bacteria can easily become trapped there, even with careful brushing. Dental sealants cover these difficult-to-clean surfaces with a thin protective coating. They are often recommended for children, although adults with cavity-prone teeth may also benefit from this treatment. Ask Dr. Kupferman at your appointment, and he can determine whether sealants would be a good option for you.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

NATURAL-LOOKING REPAIRS FOR DECAYED TEETH

Tooth-colored fillings repair cavities and smaller areas of damage using composite material. They provide a very natural-looking finish, as the composite closely resembles the color of enamel. Dr. Kupferman removes the decayed portion of the tooth before placing, shaping, and completing the filling. Once complete, this type of restoration allows the tooth to be used normally. Tooth-colored fillings are also far less noticeable than traditional silver-colored fillings.

Canker Sore Treatment

LASER TREATMENT FOR FASTER RELIEF

Canker sores may be small, but they can make everyday life very uncomfortable. Dr. Kupferman uses a dental laser to treat the affected tissue. Often this can be done without an injection. Treatment can ease discomfort and help the sore to heal in a shorter timeframe. Patients who experience frequent or persistent ulcers may also need further investigation into the possible cause.

Root Canal Treatments

TREAT INFECTION AND DAMAGE INSIDE THE TOOTH

When infection or inflammation reaches the tissue inside a tooth, root canal treatment might be needed if you want to save the tooth. Dr. Kupferman removes the affected tissue, cleans and disinfects the inside of the root canals, and then seals them to prevent further infection. After root canal treatment, your tooth will be fully restored to protect it and allow it to function normally.

Dental Crowns

BEAUTIFUL, DURABLE RESULTS

Riverdale Dental Arts provides zirconia crowns using the BruxZir crown system. Dr. Kupferman takes traditional impressions and sends them to the dental laboratory to create your custom-made restoration. Turnaround is usually just one week, after which time Dr. Kupferman will check that the crown is an accurate fit and works well with your bite, and then he will place it.

Tooth Extraction

REMOVE A PROBLEM TOOTH AND PRESERVE THE SOCKET

When a tooth can’t be saved, Dr. Kupferman can remove it and help you find a solution to restore your smile afterward. Socket preservation is an important part of the process. This involves placing bone-grafting material into the space left by the tooth. It limits the loss of bone in the area and means more treatment choices will be available to you if you later decide to replace the missing tooth with a dental implant.

Periodontal Treatment

SCALING AND ROOT PLANING WITH LASER SUPPORT

If you suffer from periodontal disease or problems with your gums, Dr. Kupferman can offer scaling and root planing as the initial stage of periodontal treatment. He also uses a diode laser to reduce the overall bacterial load and encourage gum healing. Patients who require periodontal care beyond this initial treatment are referred to a specialist.

Nightguards and Mouth Guards

GUARD AGAINST BRUXISM AND INJURY

Riverdale Dental Arts provides custom-made nightguards and mouth guards created from impressions of your teeth. A nightguard can protect against damage caused by nighttime grinding or clenching, while a sports mouth guard cushions the teeth when you play sports. Because all of our guards are custom-made for your mouth, they are a more comfortable fit than the nightguards and mouth guards you can buy over the counter at retail stores and pharmacies.

Sedation Options

FOR MORE COMFORTABLE CARE

Riverdale Dental Arts offers nitrous oxide and moderate intravenous (IV) sedation for patients who need sedation as part of their dental care. This can be useful for patients who have dental anxiety or require more involved and complex procedures. Dr. Kupferman will be happy to discuss sedation options with you at your appointment.
