Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that look and function like natural teeth. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone to serve as an artificial root, with a custom crown attached on top. If you have one or more missing teeth and good overall oral health, you may be a candidate. Dr. Valdes at Galloway Dental Care in Miami offers free consultations to assess your candidacy.

Invisalign treatment typically takes 12 to 18 months, but the timeline can vary based on the complexity of your case. Some patients see results in as little as 6 months.

Some patients experience temporary sensitivity after teeth whitening, especially with stronger treatments. Your dentist can recommend options to minimize discomfort and protect your enamel.

Common signs of gum disease include red, swollen, or bleeding gums, bad breath, and gum recession. If you notice these symptoms, schedule a dental exam for proper diagnosis and treatment.

Dental implants are a permanent, fixed solution that look, feel, and function like natural teeth — unlike dentures, which are removable and can shift or cause discomfort. Implants also preserve jawbone density, which dentures do not. For patients who are good candidates, implants are generally considered the gold standard for tooth replacement.
