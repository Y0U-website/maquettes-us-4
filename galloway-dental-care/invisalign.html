Frequently Asked Questions.

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made clear aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the correct position. Each aligner is worn for 1–2 weeks before moving to the next in the series. As an Invisalign Premier Provider, Dr. Valdes at Galloway Dental Care creates a precise digital treatment plan so you can see your projected results before you even begin.

Most Invisalign treatments take between 12 and 18 months, though some patients see results in as little as 6 months depending on the complexity of their case. During your consultation at Galloway Dental Care, Dr. Valdes will give you a personalized timeline based on your specific needs.

For best results, aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours per day. They should only be removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, and brushing and flossing. Consistent wear is the most important factor in staying on schedule.

Once your teeth have reached their final position, you’ll be fitted with a retainer to maintain your results. Wearing your retainer as directed is essential — without it, teeth can gradually shift back over time.
