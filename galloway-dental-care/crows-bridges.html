How Do Dental Bridges And Crowns Work Together?

As the name suggest, dental bridges bridge the gap left behind by missing teeth. For example, if you are missing a tooth, our dentist can prepare the teeth on both sides of the gap to hold a replacement tooth in place. With one type of dental bridge, this preparation process involves removing some of the enamel and fitting dental crowns over the teeth.

Another option is to have the teeth removed and replaced with dental implants. This type of dental bridge works well in scenarios where the patient has multiple missing teeth in a row. It’s also a good option when the teeth on both sides of the gap are too decayed to be preserved with a dental crown.

A dental crown is a custom cap placed over a single damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, strength, and appearance. A dental bridge uses crowns on adjacent teeth to support one or more artificial teeth that span a gap left by missing teeth. Dr. Valdes at Galloway Dental Care will recommend the best option based on your specific situation.

With proper care, dental crowns and bridges typically last 10 to 15 years or longer. Longevity depends on oral hygiene, bite forces, and the materials used. Porcelain-fused-to-metal and all-ceramic crowns are among the most durable and natural-looking options available at Galloway Dental Care.
