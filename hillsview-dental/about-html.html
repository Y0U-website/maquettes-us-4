Meet the Doctors

Dr. Joshua T. Petersen

Dr. Petersen enjoys providing the highest level of care to his patients and establishing connections with all existing and new patients. His practice encompasses all levels of dentistry including routine fillings, same day crowns, extractions, clear orthodontic aligners, root canals, implant dentistry, and pediatric care. He enjoys the challenges dentistry provides and the steady blend of being a healthcare professional that also involves a bit of artistry. His greatest joy if being able to help get people out of pain and to provide a smile people can be proud of.

In his spare time Dr. Petersen can be found being active in many rec league sports, spending his summers at the lake and on the golf course, and his winters skiing and hunting. He is an avid supporter of the YoungLife organization and supporting the youth in Rapid City
