Our Orthodontic Services

We offer a range of orthodontic solutions to fit every lifestyle and dental need:

Our team will help determine which treatment is best suited for your needs during your free orthodontic consultation.

  • Spark Clear Aligners : Designed with ultra-clear material, making them one of the most discreet teeth straightening options available.
  • Damon Clear Braces : A more aesthetic, self-ligating option that moves teeth faster with less discomfort.
  • Retainers : Custom-made devices that help maintain your smile by keeping your teeth in their new, corrected positions after orthodontic treatment.
  • 3D Imaging : Our advanced 3D imaging creates a precise, three-dimensional view of your teeth and jaw for more accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.
  • Digital X-Rays : A safer, faster alternative to traditional X-rays that provides clear images with significantly less radiation exposure.
  • Dental Expanders : Our dental expanders gradually widen the upper jaw to improve alignment and create more space for your teeth.
