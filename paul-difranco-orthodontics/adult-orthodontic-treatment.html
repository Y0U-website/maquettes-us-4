Addressing Concerns Unique to Adults

Adults often have different orthodontic concerns than younger patients. Our practice is experienced in handling these challenges:

Dr. DiFranco understands the importance of balancing effective care with a busy lifestyle. We strive to make your orthodontic journey as convenient and comfortable as possible.

  • Dental Restorations: If you have crowns, bridges, or implants, we customize your treatment to work around these restorations.
  • Gum Health: Orthodontic care is planned with your periodontal health in mind, ensuring treatment improves your oral health.
  • Efficiency: Advanced techniques and technologies reduce treatment times and minimize discomfort.

The Lifestyle Benefits of Adult Braces

Orthodontic care offers more than aesthetic improvements. Here are some long-term benefits of straightening your teeth as an adult:

A healthier smile means better overall wellness, making adult braces a worthwhile investment.

  • Reduced Risk of Oral Health Issues: Misaligned teeth can lead to uneven wear, decay, or jaw problems.
  • Better Digestion: Properly aligned teeth help you chew food more effectively, aiding in digestion.
  • Cost Savings: Addressing orthodontic issues now can prevent more expensive dental procedures in the future.
