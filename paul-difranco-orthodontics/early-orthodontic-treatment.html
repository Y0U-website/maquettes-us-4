Childhood Orthodontics: More Than Just Braces

Early orthodontic treatment isn t always about getting braces right away. Sometimes it involves:

  • Space Maintainers: These help keep space open for adult teeth to come in properly.
  • Palatal Expanders : These gently widen the upper jaw to make room for crowded teeth.
  • Removable Appliances: These can help guide jaw growth or move specific teeth.

When to See an Orthodontist For Kids

While age 7 is a good general rule, you might want to see an orthodontist earlier if you notice:

Remember, every child is different. Some might need early treatment, while others might not need orthodontic care until their teens. The key is to get that first check-up to know where your child stands and what treatment, if any, is needed.

  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Mouth breathing
  • Thumb sucking after age 5
