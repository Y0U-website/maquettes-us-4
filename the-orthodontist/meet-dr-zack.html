Meet Dr Zack Bracket

The Journey to The Orthodontist

Dr Zack decided early that orthodontics was the career he wanted to pursue. Getting from that first idea to opening The Orthodontist took years of school, training, experience, and a few lessons that had little to do with earning another degree. The journey eventually brought him back to the New River Valley—the place he had hoped to call home—and gave him the opportunity to build the kind of orthodontic practice he first imagined years before.

Initial Inspiration

In ninth grade, Zack needed braces and visited two very different orthodontic practices. One felt busy, quick, and impersonal. The other was smaller, more relaxed, and simply felt like a happier place to be. Zack chose the smaller practice and was especially struck by how relaxed, kind, and happy his orthodontist, Dr Steven Goozh, seemed. The experience stuck with him. Orthodontics looked like a career he could enjoy—and the kind of practice he might want to build someday.

Radford University

Zack began college at Radford University and quickly fell in love with the New River Valley. He knew this was where he wanted to live long term—even though becoming an orthodontist would eventually require years of training elsewhere.

First Impression

Wanting to understand the profession beyond his own experience as a patient, Zack began spending time inside orthodontic offices. He first shadowed Dr Vaughan in Radford, beginning years of shadowing, assisting, and working in different practices as he learned how orthodontic care worked firsthand.

President of the Pre-Dental Club

At Radford, Zack was elected president of the Pre-Dental Club, helping other students—and himself—work toward the goal of a career in dentistry.

Destination: Tufts

Zack left the New River Valley for Boston to attend Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. Getting there was part of the challenge: during his first year, his daily commute often took an hour to an hour and a half each way, switching between bus and train and frequently using the trip as extra study time.

Celebrate With Competition

School wasn’t everything. Zack has always enjoyed competition, and video games became one way to unwind outside the classroom. While playing Overwatch, he eventually reached rank 8 in North America.

President of the Orthodontics Club

Orthodontics remained Zack’s goal throughout dental school. At Tufts, he was elected president of the Orthodontics Club, continuing to pursue the specialty he had planned on for years.

Graduating From Tufts

Zack earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, graduating Magna Cum Laude in the top 5 % of his class.

Becoming an Orthodontist

After dental school, Zack returned to Tufts for specialized orthodontic training. The residency focused entirely on orthodontics and the diagnosis, planning, and treatment behind moving teeth and shaping smiles.

Class President

During orthodontic residency at Tufts, Zack was elected class president—his third student leadership role after serving as president of the Pre-Dental Club at Radford and the Orthodontics Club in dental school.

Learning to Slow Down

Years of school, residency, studying, and constantly chasing the next goal taught Zack an unexpected lesson: there should also be room to enjoy the life being built along the way. That perspective would later influence the kind of doctor—and the kind of practice—he wanted to become.

One More Standard to Meet

Zack became a board-certified orthodontist after completing the additional examination and clinical requirements of the American Board of Orthodontics.

Back to the New River Valley

With his orthodontic training complete, Dr Zack could finally return to the New River Valley—the place he had hoped to call home since his years at Radford. This time, he came back with the opportunity to create the kind of orthodontic practice he had first imagined years before.

A Different Kind of Orthodontic Practice

When it came time to create The Orthodontist, Dr Zack thought back to what first drew him to the profession. He wanted a smaller, more personal practice where patients could feel known, treatment could be individualized, and coming to the orthodontist could actually be something to look forward to. For him, every smile is a different puzzle—there may be more than one way to reach a great result, and the right approach depends on the person behind the smile. That combination of problem-solving, people, and the chance to make smiles for a living is still what makes orthodontics fun.

The First Patient

Years after first imagining the kind of orthodontic practice he might someday create, Dr Zack welcomed the first patient to The Orthodontist. What began as an idea during his own experience with braces was finally a real practice, back home in the New River Valley.

The Goal Is Still Pretty Simple.

Create great smiles, treat every patient as an individual, and build a place where patients, families, and the team can enjoy the process along the way.
