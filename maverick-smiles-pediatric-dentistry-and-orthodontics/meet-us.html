Dr. Mary Alice Hughes, Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Mary Alice grew up right here in Mauldin, SC and is a proud graduate of Mauldin High School. After leaving Mauldin, she made a stop in Columbia, SC to receive her Bachelor of Science in Biology at the University of South Carolina, and in Charleston, SC to obtain a Doctorate in Dental Medicine from the Medical University of South Carolina.

When Dr. Mary Alice isn’t busy taking care of children and their dental needs she enjoys spending time with her family including her tiny but mighty rescue dog, Marvin, running outside, relaxing at the beach, and unconditionally supporting her beloved South Carolina Gamecocks!

Dr. Mary Alice has traveled around the world providing dental care to underserved populations and has received many leadership and clinical excellence awards throughout her education, including the Grace H. Lim Award for Academic and Clinical Excellence and the Earl B. Higgins Student Leadership in Diversity Award. Most recently, Dr. Mary Alice received the SCDA Meritorious Achievement Award and was inducted as a Fellow in the American College of Dentists in recognition of her continued commitment to service and leadership in dentistry.

Dr. Mary Alice is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, also known as a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist. She is so excited to bring her knowledge and clinical skills back to Mauldin (and Fountain Inn!) to serve her hometown and she cannot wait to meet you!

Dr. Michelle Keller, General Dentist

Dr. Michelle Keller grew up in Mauldin, SC, graduating from JL Mann High School. She then continued her education in upstate New York, earning a BA in Biology at Skidmore College, followed by her doctorate from the Medical University of South Carolina College of Dental Medicine.

After graduating dental school, Dr. Keller completed a General Practice Residency at the University of North Carolina Mountain Area Health Education Center. As chief resident of her GPR, Dr. Keller worked with doctors, nurses, and other healthcare providers in the local community to integrate patients’ dental and medical care. She gained extensive experience in hospital and emergency dentistry, as well as advanced training in dental care for pediatric patients and those with special healthcare needs.

Dr. Keller enjoys learning everything she can about dentistry from the science behind our teeth, to the new advances that directly improve care for patients. She regularly attends dental continuing education courses, conferences, and patient advocacy events. She is also actively pursuing board certification in special care dentistry.

While Dr. Keller loves treating patients of all ages and abilities, for the last ten years she has worked extensively with children and adolescents who may be navigating orthodontic care or other new factors affecting their oral health. She also works closely with patients with special healthcare needs and their families to tailor their individual dental experience.

In her personal time, Dr. Keller enjoys exploring new parks and playgrounds with her husband and son. If you come to see her during gardening season, she may have fresh vegetables or blueberries to share!
