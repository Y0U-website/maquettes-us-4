Meet Your Dentist

True Dental has been providing excellent dental care to the Grapevine community since 2012 and Dr. Singratanakul has owned the practice since 2017. Our practice has earned a remarkable reputation for our attention to detail and commitment to patient comfort.

My name is Dr. Anissa Singratanakul, but to make it easier, please call me Dr. Sing.

My interest in dentistry started in High School when I would see my orthodontist for braces. I knew shortly after, that dentistry is the career I wanted. After receiving my Bachelor of Science Degree in Nutritional Science from the University of Nevada Reno, I gained valuable knowledge by working as a dental lab technician for 3 years and then as an oral surgeon’s assistant. I was accepted into the University of Las Vegas Nevada School of Dental Medicine and later received my Doctoral of Dental Medicine (DMD) in 2012.

Whether it was to get out of the Nevada desert or to reconnect with extended family, I found Dallas Texas calling me and decided to make the move. Over the past 5 years, I have been working in various offices in the DFW area starting in Rockwall then to Dallas and now Grapevine, where I can call this home. My years of experience in both private and corporate offices have motivated me to start a practice of my own, and I am happy to say that I am now the proud owner of True Dental in Grapevine.

My mission is to provide the best care possible by listening to your needs, educating you on your specific dental health conditions and then working together to create the best plan possible to set you on the road to long term dental health. I also strive to treat my staff and our patients as friends and family with the hopes that you can call us family for generations to come.
