Welcome to Sloan Creek Dental, your premier family-owned dentist serving families in Fairview and Allen, TX. We are committed to providing compassionate dental care tailored to your needs and comfort, using gentle techniques in a welcoming environment.

We understand that each smile journey is unique, so our dedicated team takes the time to get to know you. We learn about your dental history and your aspirations for your smile. This approach allows us to craft a personalized treatment plan that promotes long-term dental health and aligns perfectly with your goals.

Our office is equipped with state-of-the-art air purification and UV systems to reduce aerosols and particulates. Our radiology equipment features low-dose technology. In addition, we follow all Texas State Board and CDC infection control guidelines to keep our patients and staff safe.

Dr. Feng is an experienced dentist and has earned over 200 hours of advanced training credits in dental implant, TMJ, restorative treatment, airway dentistry, and other areas. Dr. Feng’s belief in lifelong learning combined with the latest advanced technology and materials means your oral health care will be in the best hands for decades to come.
