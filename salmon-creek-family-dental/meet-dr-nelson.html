Meet Dr. Nelson in Vancouver

Dr. Nelson’s father was a family dentist for 42 years. Dr. Nelson worked closely with his father for several years until he moved to Vancouver to start a family. Dr. Nelson served as an LDS missionary in Japan for 2 years, and has traveled to small towns in Mexico to provide dentistry for children.

  • Commitment to Convenience- We offer convenient early morning and lunchtime appointment times to accommodate your busy lifestyle. Patients are seen promptly at their appointment times, and should you ever have to wait longer than 15 minutes, we’ll give you two movie tickets to help make up for the inconvenience!
  • Commitment to Expert Care- I remain committed to becoming the best dentist possible. If you are not satisfied with the care you receive for any reason, I will work with you until you are happy.
  • Commitment to Affordability- I believe everyone should be able to afford a great smile. That’s why we offer flexible payment options and work with all dental insurance providers. Uninsured? We offer competitive fees and honor all major credit cards. Our office also offers interest-free payment plans and CareCredit.
  • Commitment to The Golden Rule – At Salmon Creek Family Dental, we follow the Golden Rule – We treat you like we would want to be treated. From our friendly and compassionate staff to our commitment to patient satisfaction, we make sure to treat every patient like family. We want to be your Vancouver, WA dentist of choice.
