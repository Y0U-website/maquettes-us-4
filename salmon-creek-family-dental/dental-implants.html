Why Replace Your Missing Teeth?

Replacing missing teeth is essential for both physical and psychological reasons. Replacing missing teeth helps maintain the health of your existing teeth and keeps your jawbone strong. Without a tooth in place, the jawbone can lose density, weakening the bite, shifting teeth, and causing further tooth loss. Replacing a missing tooth with a dental implant or bridge can help prevent these issues.

On the psychological side, missing teeth can negatively affect a person’s self-esteem. People with missing teeth often feel embarrassed and self-conscious when they smile, laugh, or talk. Replacing these teeth can help restore a person’s confidence, allowing them to be more outgoing and social. Additionally, having a full and complete smile can help a person feel more attractive, and attractive people tend to be more successful in their personal and professional lives.

What is the Dental Implant Process?

At Salmon Creek Family Dental, the implant process starts with a comprehensive assessment. Dr. Nelson works in conjunction with Oral Surgeons to complete the implant process. He will refer you to a trusted Oral Surgeon that he works with often. The Oral surgeon will evaluate the jaw and consult with Dr. Nelson to arrive at the best way to approach your particular situation.

Once we determine you are a good candidate for dental implants, the oral surgeon places the implant in your jawbone, inserting a titanium post during the procedure and allowing it to heal. After the implant fuses with the bone, Dr. Nelson will attach a connector post, and a replacement tooth, or crown, is placed on top.

Dental Implant FAQs

Dental implants are generally considered to be a safe and effective treatment option. However, it is essential to have a comprehensive evaluation by a qualified dentist to determine if you are a good candidate for the procedure.

Dental implants can last for many years with proper care. The success of the implants depends on the health of the patient, consistent follow-up care from Dr. Nelson and his staff and the best of home care on a daily basis.

What is the recovery time for dental implants?

Recovery times vary from patient to patient, but most patients can expect to resume normal activities within a few days after the procedure.
