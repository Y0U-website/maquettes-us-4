Same-Day Smile Restoration

Teeth-in-a-Day

Extensive tooth loss affects the appearance of your smile and so much more. Eating a balanced diet can become extremely difficult if you are missing some, or all of your teeth. You might also experience serious dental health issues if the tooth is not replaced within a certain period of time. That is where our dental practice can help. Thanks to Teeth-in-a-Day, patients who are missing their teeth can have a functional smile in just a single visit.

How Teeth-in-a-Day Works | Teeth-in-a-Day allows patients who are missing all of their teeth to get a functioning dental appliance placed within one day. The process involves the use of strategically placed dental implants along the dental arch, and a denture to fully restore the function of their smile. Rather than wait several months to experience the benefits of standard dental implants, a temporary denture is snapped into place at the end of the procedure. You will be able to smile, bite, and chew again in no time.

The Extent of a Patient’s Tooth Loss | Candidates for Teeth-in-a-Day are those who have experienced extensive tooth loss along the dental arch, and still have the supporting bone structure. On the other hand, patients who are missing a single tooth, or a few teeth, are more ideal candidates for implant-supported bridges, traditional dental bridges, and other less extensive procedures for a smile restoration.

Bone Density Along the Dental Arch | Dental implants must be anchored into a sturdy jawbone in order to be effective. If a patient is lacking jawbone density, that puts the success of the procedure, and that patient’s dental health, at risk. Only those patients who have not experienced serious bone loss are ideal candidates for Teeth-in-a-Day treatment.

Overall Dental Health and Wellness | Even if you have sufficient bone density in place, there may be other dental health issues that prevent a patient from receiving Teeth-in-a-Day. A patient with gum disease, for example, runs a risk of implant failure due to infection. It is important that other dental health issues be addressed before initiating Teeth-in-a-Day treatment.

Factors Related to the Cost of Treatment | Teeth-in-a-Day is more affordable than traditional implant dentistry but also more expensive than removable dentures. This added cost could make Teeth-in-a-Day a financial burden for a number of households. That said, the convenience and effectiveness of Teeth-in-a-Day may justify the expense. Financing options are also available if the upfront cost of care is too steep. These factors are important to weigh when it comes to any sort of dental procedure.

Weighing Your Options and Making a Smart Choice | It is important to us that patients are able to weigh their options for care and make a confident choice, especially when it comes to treating extensive tooth loss. During your visit to our dental practice, we make sure that all your questions are answered, all of your concerns are addressed, and all potential treatment options are discussed.
