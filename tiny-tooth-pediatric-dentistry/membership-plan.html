Simpler than insurance

Each plan includes:

2 Exams, 2 Cleanings, 2 Fluoride varnish, 1 set of x-rays, 1 Emergency Exam and x-ray, Oral Health Education, 20 % Discount on additional treatment and services PLEASE NOTE: Coverage begins upon receipt of completed and signed contract, and when payment is received in full. Membership extends for one year for each individual child from the date of receipt. Membership fee is non-refundable and non-transferable. Patients with traditional dental insurance are not eligible.
