Dr. Drake Lindholm , DDS

General Dentist

Dr. Drake and his wife, Sarah share a deep love for travel. A notable adventure in their lives was a several-month backpacking journey through Southeast Asia, which enriched their perspectives and fueled their desire to explore even more of the world. Excited to call Dane County home again, they are eager to explore all of the wonderful parks, lakes, and restaurants Wisconsin has to offer.

Dr. Drake brings a wealth of knowledge and a compassionate approach to his work, ensuring that each patient receives personalized and high-quality care. He is excited to contribute his skills and enthusiasm to our practice and looks forward to making a positive impact on the oral health and well-being of our community.
