Our dentist in The Loop conducts a comprehensive examination and develops a customized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

Titanium implant posts are surgically inserted into the jawbone and allowed to heal, providing a strong foundation for the overlying restorations.

Once the implants have integrated with the bone, abutments are attached to connect the final restorations, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

The final restorations are fitted onto the abutments, and follow-up visits are scheduled to ensure optimal fit, aesthetics, and functionality.
