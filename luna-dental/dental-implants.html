  • Luna Family Dental’s step-by-step exam : When you come in, we’ll review your health history, medical conditions and check your teeth and gums. We’ll take detailed X-rays or 3D images of your mouth to see how much bone you have and if it’s ready for an implant.
  • Fixing bone problems if needed : If you don’t have enough bone for the implant to hold onto, we can perform a procedure called bone grafting. This adds more bone to your jaw so it’s strong enough for the implant.
  • Health conditions and lifestyle : Some health problems, like diabetes or bad habits like smoking, can make healing harder. We’ll help you understand what changes might be needed to make the implant successful.
