Meet Dr. Igor Sorin

0 A Local Dentist Dedicated to Personalized, Modern Care Dr. Igor Sorin is proud to serve the North Shore community he has long called home. He grew up in Glendale, Wisconsin, just minutes from the office, and brings both deep local roots and advanced clinical training to every patient experience at Sorin Dental Wellness.

Dr. Sorin graduated from Nicolet High School before earning his Bachelor’s degree in Biology from UW–Milwaukee. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry. Following dental school, Dr. Sorin practiced in New Berlin and Grafton, where he gained the hands-on experience, confidence, and clinical expertise that ultimately led him to open his own practice.

A Patient-First Philosophy

While Dr. Sorin knew early on that he wanted to pursue dentistry, it was after entering practice that he truly discovered how meaningful the profession could be. He values the opportunity to meet people from all walks of life and provide dental care that is thoughtfully tailored to each individual’s needs, goals, and comfort level.

Dr. Sorin believes strongly in patient education and open communication. He takes the time to explain treatment options clearly so patients feel informed, empowered, and never pressured when making decisions about their dental health.

Commitment to Advanced Dentistry

Dentistry is constantly evolving, and Dr. Sorin is committed to staying at the forefront of modern dental care. He continually learns new techniques and embraces advanced technology to deliver care that is efficient, precise, and comfortable without sacrificing quality or longevity.

From preventive care to restorative and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Sorin’s goal is always the same: to provide exceptional care in a calm, judgment-free environment.

Life Outside the Office

Dr. Sorin currently lives in Bayside, just minutes from the practice. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys staying active, traveling, music production, and exploring new restaurants with his wife. He’s also an avid Packers fan and proudly supports Wisconsin sports teams across the board.

Dr. Sorin looks forward to welcoming new patients and families to Sorin Dental Wellness and building long-lasting relationships rooted in trust and personalized care.
