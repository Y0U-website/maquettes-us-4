Repair & Protect Your Teeth

Restorative Dentistry in Willard

We understand that life can get in the way of staying on top of your oral health. If you do develop a cavity or other oral health issues, we are here to help with judgment-free restorative care. Our restorative treatments include:

  • Dental Fillings- We can easily fill a cavity with a composite or amalgam filling to protect your tooth and prevent further decay.
  • Root Canal Therapy- If you have an infected tooth, Dr. Booker will perform a root canal to remove the damaged enamel, sterilize the infection, and save your tooth.
  • Dental Crowns- You may require a dental crown if your tooth is severely damaged, decayed, or discolored.
  • Bridges & Dentures- Dental bridges & dentures provide an affordable, convenient option for replacing missing teeth without highly invasive treatment.
  • Extractions- We will always try to save your tooth whenever possible, but sometimes an extraction and replacement with a dental prosthetic may be the best option.
