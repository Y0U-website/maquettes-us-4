Les soins

Your care is led by four experienced, credentialed dentists — including Dr. Thai Dang (practicing since 1993) and Dr. Homayoun Pourshirazi, a member of the American and California Dental Associations. Alongside our caring hygienists, they focus on prevention…

A dental implant is a small titanium post that acts as a new tooth root. Once it heals into your jawbone, we attach a natural-looking crown, bridge or denture on top. The result is a replacement tooth that’s stable, comfortable and built to last.

Missing or failing teeth shouldn’t keep you from enjoying the foods you love or smiling in photos. We’re a tooth-replacement practice at heart, offering dental implants , implant-supported dentures , comfortable full & partial dentures , and fixed bridges .
