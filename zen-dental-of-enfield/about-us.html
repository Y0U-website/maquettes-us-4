What Is Dr. J’s Approach to Care?

Dr. J’s philosophy is centered on treating the whole person not just their teeth. He brings years of aesthetic dental training, clinical experience, and a strong sense of artistry to every case. His attention to detail ensures that each smile he creates is both stunning and natural-looking. Above all, he values the opportunity to help patients improve their quality of life through confident, healthy smiles.

What Is Dr. J s Background and Experience?

A graduate of New York University’s College of Dentistry , Dr. J was involved in research on biomaterials, which deepened his understanding of the clinical procedures he was learning in real-time. He enjoys all aspects of general dentistry and takes the time to walk patients through their treatment options, believing that a healthy mouth is essential to overall health. Before joining the team at Zen Dental, Dr. J practiced in Chicago, IL, and is currently an active member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry .

You can also find him featured on platforms such as Healthgrades , WebMD , and U.S. News Health for his outstanding work and patient care.
