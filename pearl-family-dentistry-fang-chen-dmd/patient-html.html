Emergency Visit

We understand the urgency to be seen by a dentist when you are in tremendous pain from toothache, knocked-out tooth, broken tooth, bitten lip/tongue or any other dental emergency. Please call our 24-hour phone number (248) 250-9269. We will make every attempt to see you as soon as possible. * Please call 911 immediatly if it is a life threatening emergency.
