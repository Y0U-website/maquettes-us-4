Dental implants are prosthesis for missing teeth. These restorations are constructed using three parts: the root, the abutment, and the crown. The root is made of titanium that works especially well with bone, bonding with the jaw to bolster its strength. The abutment and crown are placed above the implant root to support chewing power, restoring the appearance and function in the process.
