Personal Dental Care

At Austin Park Dental, we don’t have sign-in sheets. Our team kindly greets each patient by name, making sure they receive individualized attention. We spend additional time getting to know each and every one of our patients to ensure we provide the most comfortable and comprehensive dental services specific to them.

Comfort for our patients is one of our top priorities. Our waiting room provides entertainment for children and adults. Our kid’s corner often makes kids want to stay in our dental office even after completing treatment.

We equip each operatory with a television so patients can stay entertained throughout their entire procedure.
