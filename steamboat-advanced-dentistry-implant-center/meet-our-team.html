Jaime Witty, Office Manager

Jaime Witty grew up in Gulfport, MS. She attended the University of Southern Mississippi and obtained her degree in Biological Sciences. She later attended Mississippi College for her Master of Science in Medicine Degree. She and Richard moved to Las Vegas, NV in 2014 and then to Steamboat Springs in 2015. She practiced medicine as a Physician Assistant in Mississippi, Nevada, and Colorado prior to joining Richard and his team at Steamboat Advanced Dentistry & Implant Center.

Jaime, Richard, and their daughter Charlotte have two cocker spaniels, Monty and Millie, that love Steamboat just as much as they do. On the weekends, you can find Jaime and Richard mountain biking, camping, hiking with their dogs, or skiing, depending on the season.

Scarlet, Dental Hygienist

I am from Chattanooga, TN. I moved to Steamboat during the pandemic due to being furloughed and my husband having a great career opportunity in Steamboat. We moved back to the southeast in 2022, and have longed for Steamboat the entire time!

I graduated from Chattanooga State in 2007, and was a recipient of the "Golden Scaler Award". I love learning about new technology, clinical studies, and anything that makes a visit to the dentist as easy and as comfortable as possible. Thankfully, I have joined forces with Dr. Richard Witty who shares my passion for dentistry and patient care.

A little bit about me. I grew up in the southeast. I was born in Memphis, TN. I have lived in Chattanooga, TN for the majority of my life. I am one of 4 sisters, I happen to be the "vintage" model. My teenage years were spent with my Dad who is a veterinarian. This is where my passion for medicine and caring for others was cultivated. My sister followed suit, and she is a dental hygienist in Alabama.

My husband and I enjoy getting outside and fireside snow days with our family feline "Pickles". We also enjoy floating the river, hiking Fish Creek & Emerald, and hitting the gravel roads on our bicycles. We love the community of Steamboat, and we are so lucky to be able to join the family again.

I am looking forward to re-joining the Steamboat Advanced Dentistry and Implant Center family, and I can’t wait to see you at your next appointment!
