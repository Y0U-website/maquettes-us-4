Meet Our DENTIST

Dr. Gardner is a graduate of the University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry and has been in dentistry for over 20 years, practicing as a dentist for more than a decade. She has served the community using her skills while working at Community Clinic of Northwest Arkansas and has served at multiple free extraction events in Northwest Arkansas. Dr. Gardner has continued to expand her knowledge and skills through additional training in Oral Pathology, Orthodontic Clear Aligner Correction, and Implantology.

Dr. Gardner was born and raised in Cuba until she was 15 years old when she moved to the States. Her favorite Cuban dish is arroz blanco, frijoles y ropa vieja con tostones. When she is not in the office, she enjoys all things involving nature and art. She is a family centered wife and mom. Dr. Gardner and her husband, Clarence, have been married for almost 18 years and have 3 wonderful children and Ray, their German Shepard dog. She enjoys baking with her children, gardening with her husband, and spending all her time creating memories and capturing them through photography.

We’re proud to be a family-owned, first-generation dental practice built on trust, compassion, and personalized care. From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll be welcomed like family by a team that genuinely cares about your comfort, health, and smile.

What sets us apart is the relationships we build with our patients. Our doctors and staff take the time to get to know every individual and provide care with honesty, kindness, and attention to detail. We believe every patient deserves to feel heard, respected, and confident in their treatment.

Whether you’re visiting for a routine checkup, cosmetic dentistry, or orthodontic care, our goal is to create a comfortable, family-friendly environment where patients of all ages can feel at home. We look forward to caring for your family’s smiles for generations to come.
