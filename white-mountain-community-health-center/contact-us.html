Hours:

After hours and on weekends, you can reach our on-call nurse service by calling our main number at (603) 447-8900 .

Our doors open at 8 am for our first appointments, but we don’t start answering our phones until 8:30 am. If you need assistance before 8:30 am, please leave a message and someone will get back to you shortly.

If you are having a medical emergency, please call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, call 988. Or to be connected directly to NH mental health crisis services, dial (833) 710-6477 .

Feedback

We welcome feedback on our services! We rely on input from patients to make improvements and ensure that we provide high-quality, compassionate care to every person who walks through our doors.

We always want to know if your experience with us didn’t match your expectations, so we can figure out what went wrong and make it right. Our Quality Improvement and Assurance Committee reviews every patient grievance and uses them to improve our services. Contact us directly so we can be in touch and resolve the issue or answer your questions.

Do you have a healthcare story you want to share with more people?

We’re always looking for patient stories that show the need for the individualized community health services we offer, the effect of policy changes on the people we serve, and the ways we make a difference in people’s lives. If you have a story you’d like to share, please use our feedback form and share your contact information so we can reach out if we have any questions.

Our feedback form is HIPAA-secure and the best way to contact us, but if you’d prefer, you can also call us at (603) 447-8900 or email info@whitemountainhealth.org . Please be aware that sending us an email is not HIPAA-secure, but if you’d like to communicate this way, we can reply with an encrypted email to continue the conversation securely.

If you believe that White Mountain Community Health Center has discriminated against you, you can file a grievance with:

Civil Rights/Section 1557 Coordinator White Mountain Community Health Center 298 White Mountain Highway Conway, NH 03818 Phone: (603) 447-8900 Email: info@whitemountainhealth.org

You may file in person, by phone, by mail, or by email. If you need help filing a grievance, the Civil Rights Coordinator is available to assist you.

You also have the right to file a civil rights complaint with:

U.S. Department of Health & Human Services, Office for Civil Rights (OCR) Online complaint portal: https://ocrportal.hhs.gov Mail: U.S. Department of Health & Human Services 200 Independence Avenue SW H.H.H. Building, Room 509F Washington, DC 20201 Phone: 1-800-368-1019
