If any irregularities are found, a treatment plan will be made. Our office utilizes an intra-oral camera to show patients problem areas.

After it is determined that you do not have active gum disease, our hygienists will scale tartar from areas above the gum line and from between teeth. These deposits contribute to gum disease and removal is essential in preventing gingivitis. Next, the teeth are polished, flossed, and oral hygiene instructions are shared with the patient. Many of our patients are surprised at how helpful our staff’s instruction on brushing and flossing can be for patients of all ages.
