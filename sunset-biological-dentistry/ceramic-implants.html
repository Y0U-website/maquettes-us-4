A metal-free implant that looks completely natural, integrates beautifully, and asks nothing of your body it shouldn’t. For many patients, that distinction matters.

Why We Choose Zirconia

Titanium implants are the conventional standard, and they work well for many people. But some patients react to metal over time, and others simply prefer to avoid it altogether.

Zirconia implants are made from a ceramic that is biocompatible, tooth-colored, and free of metal. They don’t conduct temperature or electricity, they integrate well with the tissue, and there’s no dark line at the gum. For patients with known metal sensitivities or a whole-body health approach, that’s a meaningful option.

Both can be true: titanium has its place, and metal-free deserves to be a real choice.

Precision Before Placement

Replacing a tooth is about more than filling a gap. We begin with a thorough evaluation, including 3D CBCT imaging, to plan precisely and protect the surrounding bone and nerves.

Where the site needs support, we draw on regenerative techniques like PRF to encourage healthy healing. Every step is mapped before treatment begins, so the implant is placed exactly where it should be.
