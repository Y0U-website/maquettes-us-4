Biological Hygiene & Prevention

A Cleaning That Looks After More Than Your Teeth.

A hygiene visit is the most routine appointment in dentistry. It’s also the most overlooked chance to support your whole-body health. We use it for both.

Cleanings, Reimagined Around the Microbiome

Your mouth isn’t supposed to be sterile. It’s supposed to be balanced. A healthy oral microbiome protects your teeth and gums, so the goal of a cleaning isn’t to wage war on every bacterium. It’s to restore that balance.

We use Airflow, a gentle stream of fine powder and water, to remove biofilm and surface stain comfortably, even around sensitive gums and existing dental work. Where it helps, we add ozone and laser to reduce the bacteria associated with inflammation, without harsh chemicals.

It’s a thorough clean that works with your body, not against it.

We Can Look at What’s Actually Living in Your Mouth

Oral microbiome testing lets us move from guessing to knowing. A simple sample can reveal the specific bacteria associated with gum inflammation and decay, so your care can be shaped around you rather than the average patient.

For teeth in the earliest stages of a cavity, we may apply a nano-hydroxyapatite varnish, a material closely related to the mineral your enamel is already made of, to support remineralization before decay advances. More on the science of remineralization and the fluoride debate in the fluoride conversation .

Personalized, not population-level. That difference is the whole point.

Where Oral Health Meets the Rest of Your Health

Your hygiene visit is also where we talk. Oral hygiene instruction and nutritional counseling are part of the appointment, because what happens at the gum line often reflects something larger, and small changes at home compound over time.

Gum inflammation has been associated with whole-body health in a growing body of research. We share what’s well established, what’s still emerging, and what it might mean for you, then leave the decisions in your hands.

Preventive care works best as part of a bigger picture. We often combine it with ozone therapy and the whole-body approach of holistic dentistry .

Healthy teeth start with a healthier mouth, and a closer look.
