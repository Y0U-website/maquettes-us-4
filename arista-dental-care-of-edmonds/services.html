Dental Services in Edmonds, WA

At Arista Dental Care, your trusted dentist in Edmonds , we provide comprehensive dental care for the whole family — from routine checkups to full smile transformations. Dr. Risha Khan and our friendly team proudly serve patients in Edmonds, Shoreline, Lynnwood, and Mountlake Terrace, WA. Whether you’re due for a cleaning, considering cosmetic treatment, or need restorative care, we offer everything you need under one roof. Explore our services below and book an appointment today.

General & Preventive Dentistry

Comprehensive Exam & Composite Fillings

A thorough dental exam is the foundation of a healthy smile. We evaluate your teeth, gums, and overall oral health using digital X-rays and detailed screenings including an oral cancer check. When cavities are found, we restore them with tooth-colored composite fillings that blend naturally with your smile.

Sealants

Mouth Guards

Custom-fitted mouth guards protect your teeth from grinding (bruxism) and sports-related injuries. Unlike over-the-counter options, our professional mouth guards are made from precise impressions of your teeth for a comfortable, secure fit.

Pediatric Dentistry

We love seeing young smiles! Our team creates a warm, welcoming environment for children of all ages. Early dental visits help establish healthy habits, prevent cavities, and set kids up for a lifetime of great oral health.

Comprehensive General, Cosmetic & Restorative Dentistry in Edmonds

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth to correct chips, cracks, staining, or uneven spacing. The result is a flawless, natural-looking smile that’s built to last.

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile by several shades with our professional teeth whitening treatment. Unlike store-bought strips, our whitening solutions are stronger, faster, and customized to minimize sensitivity while delivering dramatic results.

Cosmetic Bonding

Dental bonding is one of the most affordable ways to improve your smile. Using a tooth-colored composite resin, Dr. Khan can repair chips, close small gaps, and reshape teeth often in a single visit with no anesthesia needed.

Smile Makeover

A smile makeover combines two or more cosmetic treatments such as whitening, veneers, and bonding into a personalized plan designed around your unique goals. If you’ve ever wished your smile looked different, a smile makeover may be the answer.

Restorative Dentistry

Crowns & Bridges

Dental crowns fully cap a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its strength, shape, and appearance. Bridges use crowns on adjacent teeth to fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth a durable, non-removable solution for tooth loss.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial root that supports a lifelike crown. Implants look, feel, and function just like natural teeth and can last a lifetime with proper care.

Dentures

For patients missing several or all of their teeth, dentures offer a comfortable and affordable restoration. We provide both full and partial dentures custom-crafted to fit your mouth and restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Root Canals

Orthodontics

Braces & Clear Aligners (SureSmile)

Straighten your teeth discreetly and efficiently with SureSmile clear aligners or traditional braces. Orthodontic treatment corrects crowding, spacing, and bite issues improving both the appearance and long-term health of your smile.

Periodontal & Oral Surgery

Periodontal Treatment

Gum disease is one of the leading causes of tooth loss in adults. Our periodontal treatments including deep cleanings (scaling and root planing) target infection below the gumline to halt the progression of gum disease and restore gum health.

Teeth Extractions

When a tooth is too damaged or decayed to save, extraction is sometimes the safest option. We perform gentle extractions with your comfort as the priority, and can discuss tooth replacement options such as implants or bridges immediately after.

Wisdom Teeth Extractions

Wisdom teeth that are impacted, misaligned, or causing crowding often need to be removed. Early removal reduces the risk of infection, pain, and damage to surrounding teeth. Our team ensures the process is as smooth and comfortable as possible.
