Tech and Amenities

At Modern Dentistry of Minnetonka, you’ll experience nothing but the best of professional dentistry. We’ve designed our space to promote your relaxation and well-being from the waiting room to the chair, and everywhere in between!

TVs in Operatories

Enhance your dental experience with TVs with Disney+, Hulu, and ESPN+ that allow you to relax and enjoy your favorite shows during your visit.

Beverage Station

Make yourself at home with our complimentary beverage station, offering a variety of refreshing options to enhance your overall dental experience.

Headphones

Immerse yourself in a serene dental experience with our complimentary headphones, providing personalized comfort and allowing you to enjoy your visit.

Our office is equipped with tablets for streamlined patient sign-in and personalized entertainment, ensuring a modern and efficient experience tailored to your comfort.
