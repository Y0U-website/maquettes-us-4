Many patients come to South Bay Dental Surgery Center looking for a long-term solution for missing teeth. Implant consultations here focus on comfort, bone support, gum health, and the restorative path that best fits each case.

Implants may be appropriate for patients missing one tooth, several teeth, or teeth that cannot be predictably saved. Candidacy depends on several factors, including overall health, gum health, available bone, smoking history, medications, and the type of final restoration needed.

A successful implant is not just a surgical appointment. It requires accurate imaging, healthy supporting tissue, a stable bite, and a restoration plan that fits your mouth. When additional bone or gum support is needed, grafting or periodontal treatment may be recommended before or during implant treatment.
