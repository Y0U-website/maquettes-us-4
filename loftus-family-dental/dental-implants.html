Eat, Speak, & Smile With Confidence

Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, you’ll love the benefits of dental implants. These replacements for your tooth roots give your bite unmatched stability and oral function. At Loftus Family Dental, we offer a range of implant options to fit your needs, budget, and long-term goals:

Each option includes our gentle approach, advanced computer-guided placement, and personalized care. Dr. Loftus will help you understand which choice makes the most sense for your specific situation. Our team provides ongoing support for you, from planning to your final smile.

  • Implant Crowns – When you’re missing just one tooth, an implant crown gives you back a complete smile. Your dentist places a small titanium post in your jawbone that works just like a tooth root. Once it heals, we attach a beautiful crown that blends perfectly with your other teeth.
  • Implant Bridges – We can replace two or more missing teeth with a bridge supported by implants. Unlike traditional bridges, these don’t rely on your natural teeth for support, so there’s no need to remove healthy tooth structure. You’ll enjoy improved chewing ability and teeth that stay securely in place.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures – If you’re tired of loose-fitting dentures, implant support can change everything. Your denture will fit securely onto strategically placed implants. This helps you avoid embarrassing slips and allows you to eat foods you’ve been limiting.
  • All-on-4 Implants – Even if you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone for implants, an All-on-4 might be your solution. Using just four precisely placed implants, we can support a full arch of teeth. This approach works well even with some bone loss in your jaw.
  • Same-Day Smile – You don’t have to go a single day longer without your teeth. In many cases, your dentist can place your implants and attach temporary teeth in just one visit. You’ll walk out with a functional smile while your custom teeth are being crafted to perfection.
  • Full-Mouth Reconstruction – Sometimes a combination of treatments works best. Our team can create a personalized plan using implants, crowns, bridges, and other restorations. This approach addresses complex dental issues and gives you back a healthy, functional, and beautiful smile.

FAQs

With proper care, dental implants can last for decades, if not your lifetime. The titanium post fuses with your jawbone and integrates with your mouth. The new tooth attached to your implant typically lasts at least five years or more before needing replacement. It depends on your oral habits and how well you maintain your dental hygiene routines.

Even if you’ve been living with missing teeth, you can can get dental implants. The main need is having enough jawbone to support the implant and good overall health. Even if you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone, options like bone grafting or specialized techniques like All-on-4 might work for you. We’ll evaluate your specific situation during your consultation.

The cost varies depending on how many implants you need and what type of restoration will be attached. While dental implants require a higher initial investment than options like dentures or bridges, they often prove more cost-effective in the long run. We can explore third-party financing options to help make this life-changing treatment affordable for you.
