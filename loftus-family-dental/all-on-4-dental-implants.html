Restore a Full Arch of Teeth With Fewer Implants

All-on-4 is a full-arch implant option that uses only four implants to replace an entire row of missing teeth. It’s ideal for a wide range of smiles, even if you have some jawbone loss. Dr. Loftus ensures your procedure goes smoothly and comfortably. Best of all, it happens right here in our practice – with no visit to an outside doctor needed.
