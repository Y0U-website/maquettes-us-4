Frequently Asked Questions About Implant-Supported Crowns & Bridges

How are implant-supported crowns and bridges different from traditional options?

Implant-supported restorations are attached to dental implants in the jawbone, providing greater stability and support than options that rely on neighboring teeth or removable appliances.

Who may be a candidate for implant-supported crowns and bridges?

Many adults with missing teeth may qualify. A comprehensive evaluation can determine whether your oral health and bone structure are suitable for treatment.

How long do implant-supported restorations last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implant-supported crowns and bridges can provide long-lasting results for many years.

Do implant-supported crowns and bridges look natural?

Yes. These restorations are custom designed to blend with your surrounding teeth, creating a natural and attractive appearance.
