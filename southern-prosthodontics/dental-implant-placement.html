The Journey to Restoring Your Smile

The dental implant process involves several steps over a few months. Initially, Dr. Collier will conduct a comprehensive examination to determine if you are a candidate for implants. Factors such as bone density and overall oral health are crucial considerations.

Following the evaluation, the implant surgery is scheduled. During this procedure, the implant is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Afterward, there’s a healing period where the implant integrates with the bone—a process that typically takes 3-6 months.

Once healed, an abutment is attached to the implant to hold the new tooth or teeth in place. Finally, custom-made crowns are attached to the abutments, completing your smile restoration.

Setting Your Expectations for Dental Implant Surgery

Prior to surgery, Dr. Collier will provide detailed pre-operative instructions to ensure your comfort and readiness for the procedure. While some discomfort may occur after surgery, it can generally be managed with over-the-counter pain medication and ice packs. It’s important to follow all post-operative care instructions closely to ensure the best possible healing and outcome.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure which can be easily managed with medications.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most adults in good general health with sufficient jawbone density are candidates for dental implants; however, individual evaluations are necessary.
