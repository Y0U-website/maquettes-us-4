Get to know

Dr. Anna O’Keefe

Dr. Anna O’Keefe’s journey to dentistry began in Spokane, WA, when she realized she hadn’t seen a dentist until age 12 — sparking a fascination with smiles and a curiosity about how oral health shapes our confidence and well-being. She went on to earn her undergraduate degree in biochemistry from Colby College in Maine, followed by her DMD from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where she graduated magna cum laude, received the Academy of General Dentistry award, and was inducted into the Omega Kappa Upsilon Honor Society.

Known for her meticulous attention to detail, Dr. O’Keefe believes in giving every patient all their options — regardless of age, background, or circumstance. Whether she’s restoring a full smile, placing a same-day crown, or simply getting to know a new patient, she treats each person with the respect, integrity, and personal attention she’d give a friend or family member.

Get to know

Dr. Courtney Churchill

Dr. Courtney Churchill grew up in Fairfield, Maine, and knew from a young age that she wanted to pursue a career in dentistry. While still in high school, she began working for a local oral surgeon at just 17 years old. This early experience gave her firsthand exposure to the profession and solidified her passion for helping people improve their health and confidence through dentistry.

She went on to earn her undergraduate degree in Medical Biology from the University of New England. Continuing her education there, Dr. Churchill received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of New England College of Dental Medicine in Portland, Maine.

Following graduation, Dr. Churchill practiced in Portland and Augusta, gaining valuable experience while working her way back to central Maine to be closer to home. These early years in practice helped her refine her clinical skills and develop a patient-centered philosophy focused on trust, communication, and comprehensive care.

Dr. Churchill is committed to lifelong learning and continually expanding her clinical knowledge. She participates in Breakwater Seminars, an extension of the Casco Bay Study Club, where aspiring young dentists collaborate and focus on delivering excellence in comprehensive treatment planning and patient care.

Known for her warm and approachable demeanor, Dr. Churchill strives to create a comfortable environment where patients feel heard and supported. Whether she is helping a patient maintain their oral health or restoring their smile, her goal is to provide thoughtful, high-quality care that helps every patient feel confident in their smile.

Comprehensive records

Your visit begins with a complete set of diagnostic records — including full-mouth X-rays, a panoramic scan, extraoral photos, and a digital intraoral scan with our CEREC system.

A personal, thorough exam

Dr. O’Keefe or Dr. Churchill will spend about an hour with you, performing a full exam, oral cancer screening, and periodontal charting, while discussing your health history, diet, and prior dental experiences.

Co-discovery and planning

Using photos and scans, we’ll review your findings together and walk through all your treatment options. For patients with more complex restorative needs, we also offer an additional dedicated Smile Design visit — a longer, more in-depth consultation that allows us to coordinate care with specialists when needed. You’ll leave with a clear understanding of your oral health and next steps.

Once we’ve completed your comprehensive exam and determined a crown is the right treatment for you, we can design, create, and place it in just one visit. That means no temporary crowns, no return trips for placement — just a stronger, healthier smile the very same day.

3D printing

We print models and custom night guards (also called occlusal guards) in-house, giving you a precise fit and a faster turnaround time. This technology lets our doctors fine-tune every detail, so your guard is both comfortable and effective from day one.

Digital scanning

Our intraoral scanner creates precise computer models of your teeth, making the process faster, cleaner, and far more comfortable. No messy putty required!
