Dental Implant Restoration Results

See how dental implants provide a permanent, natural-looking solution for missing teeth.

Two Stage Implant Placement and Restoration Patient had a missing tooth on the lower left and wanted the best solution for replacement. We took a high resolution CBCT X-ray of the area, fabricated a surgical guide and placed our implant. After a short 4 months, the implant was completely osseointegrated, we took a digital impression and milled an eMAX screw retained implant crown.

Dental Implant Restoration 🦷 Dental Implants in Antioch, CA At Grand Oak Dental Care, our dentist provides precise and lasting dental implant treatments to replace missing teeth with natural-looking results. Using advanced digital technology, we ensure every implant is placed with accuracy and comfort. Implants restore your ability to chew, smile, and speak with confidence while helping prevent bone loss and shifting teeth. 📞 Call Grand Oak Dental Care in Antioch today to schedule your implant consultation and restore your smile!

Single Implant Tooth Replacements Patient came to us looking for a solution to replace two missing teeth that he lost a while ago. We were able to place two individual implants in the area with bone grafting. After the implants had osseointegrated into the upper jaw, we took a digital impression and fabricated two individual esthetic implant crowns restoring the patient’s back bite!

Implant Posterior Bridge Patient has been missing his back teeth for decades and came to us for a solution. We planned a series of implant bridges and singular implant placements in order to restore bilateral posterior occlusion in the most efficient way. Today we were able to deliver the first set, his upper right implant-retained bridge restoring his ability to chew on his right side. Next up, the left side!!
