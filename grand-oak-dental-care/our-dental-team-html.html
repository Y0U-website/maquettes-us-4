Grand Oak Dental Care Meet the Doctor

Dr. Emad K. Alazzeh

Dr. Emad K. Alazzeh is an exceptional dentist with an unmatched passion for providing quality care to his patients. Born and raised in the Bay Area, he finds great pride in providing general and cosmetic dentistry to all the surrounding east bay communities.

Dr. Emad K. Alazzeh graduated from the University of California, San Diego in 2013 with a Bachelors Degree of Science in Biochemistry. Afterwards he graduated from Indiana University School of Dentistry in 2019 and has been practicing in the East Bay ever since.

Our doctor is deeply committed to continuing education, ensuring he stays at the forefront of dental technology. By integrating the latest advancements into his practice, such as using digital dental scanners and providing CEREC same-day crowns, he consistently provides his patients with the most ideal outcomes, reflecting his dedication to their oral health and well-being.
