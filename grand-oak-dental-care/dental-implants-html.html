Dental implants

  • Natural-Looking: The replacement teeth placed on dental implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing a beautiful and realistic result.
  • Improved Functionality: Implants allow you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence, as they function just like natural teeth.
  • Preserves Bone Health: Since implants integrate with the jawbone, they help prevent bone loss that often occurs when teeth are missing.

The Dental Implant Process

The process for placing dental implants typically occurs in several stages, and while it takes time, the results are well worth the wait.

  • Initial Consultation and Assessment: During your consultation, we’ll examine your oral health and take X-rays to determine the condition of your jawbone. If your jawbone is too thin or soft, a bone graft may be necessary before placing the implants.
  • Implant Placement: The next step involves surgically placing the titanium implants into your jawbone. This is typically done under local anesthesia, and the procedure itself is minimally invasive.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: After implant placement, the jawbone needs time to heal and fuse with the titanium implant in a process called osseointegration. This may take several months, depending on your individual healing process.
  • Abutment Placement: Once the implant has fused with the bone, an abutment is placed on top of the implant. This small connector piece will hold the final restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) in place.
  • Placement of Replacement Teeth: Once the abutment is securely in place, your custom-designed replacement teeth will be attached to the implant. These teeth are carefully crafted to match the color, size, and shape of your natural teeth, ensuring a perfect fit and appearance.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a great option for most people who are missing teeth. However, ideal candidates should meet the following criteria:

During your consultation, we will assess your oral health and determine if dental implants are the best solution for you.

  • Healthy gums: You need to have healthy gums that are free from infection.
  • Adequate bone density: Your jawbone must be strong enough to support the implant. If your jawbone has been compromised due to tooth loss, bone grafting procedures can help restore it.
  • Good overall health: Patients should be in good overall health to ensure proper healing and the success of the implant procedure.

The Advantages of Choosing Dental Implants

Dental implants offer numerous advantages over other tooth replacement options:

  • No Slippage or Irritation: Unlike dentures, implants won’t slip out of place or cause irritation, allowing you to speak and eat comfortably.
  • Long-Term Cost-Effectiveness: Though the initial cost of implants may be higher, they are durable and require minimal maintenance, making them a cost-effective solution in the long run.
  • Enhanced Confidence: With dental implants, you’ll regain the ability to smile with confidence, knowing that your replacement teeth look and function just like natural ones.
