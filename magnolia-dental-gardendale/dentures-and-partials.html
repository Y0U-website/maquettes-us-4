Dr. Nathalie Courington

Dr. Nathalie was born and raised in Venezuela where she completed a five year dental program in 2013. She then moved to the United States to pursue her licensure and attended the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry where she obtained her Doctorate in Dental Medicine degree in 2018. Since then she has practiced dentistry in a variety of settings from pediatrics to adult dentistry in North Alabama and Birmingham Area.

Practicing in different settings from corporate dentistry to private practice has allowed her to find her true calling. She has decided to start a practice where she has the time to sit one on one with her patients and address their needs as well as calm their fears in a personal setting and build lifelong relationships. Providing her patients with a calming Christian environment where her patients are loved and treated with grace and kindness is something that is very important to her.

Dr. Nathalie has chosen the area of Gardendale for her practice because she has been attending Gardendale First Baptist Church for many years and has chosen the town where she lives and worship as a way to give back to the community that has given her and her family so much.

Dr. Nathalie believes that God’s will and purpose for her life is to serve and take care of people and specifically to help with their dental fears and give them back the ability to smile. She started her journey in the dental field when she was 18 years old and still gets great satisfaction to be able to help others. She is detail oriented, gentle and very thorough. She is also fluent in both English and Spanish. During her free time she loves spending time with her family and traveling.
