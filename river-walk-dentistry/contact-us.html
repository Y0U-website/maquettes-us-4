We Validate Parking

Please park at the convenient valet parking located at

Alternative parking: in addition to the familiar Mason Street lot, an alternative parking structure at 720 N Water, right across the street from the valet lot, is now available with 8 floors of convenient parking. We will continue to provide valet services at all three lots, so choose the one that suits you best keeping in mind that the alternative structures are self-park.

How to validate parking: Due to changes in management at the parking structures, starting March 1st, 2024, we kindly request your cooperation in providing us with your LICENSE PLATE.

Failure to provide this information will result in a parking ticket. You can share your details at the beginning of your appointment or, if needed, via text message to 414-271-1770 within 24hrs of your appointment.
