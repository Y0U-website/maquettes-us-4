Confident Smiles Start Here

We guide you through the decision-making process and the services necessary to care for your winning smile. With our comprehensive exam and innovative techniques, your confidence in us—and your smile—will be lasting.

Exam & Cleaning

Staying proactive with routine exams and cleanings will keep your smile healthy. A comprehensive dental exam will be performed by your dentist at your initial visit. Professional dental cleanings (dental prophylaxis) are performed by Registered Dental Hygienists.

Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal cleanings are essential in maintaining dental health and keeping periodontal disease under control. Periodontal disease is diagnosed by your dentist or dental hygienist during a periodontal examination—and should always be part of your regular dental check-up.

Laser Therapy

Laser therapy is a modern and innovative treatment option for gum disease. It is a minimally invasive procedure that uses a laser to remove bacteria and infected tissue from the gums. This treatment is becoming increasingly popular among dental professionals and patients alike due to its many benefits.

Tooth Whitening

Composite Fillings

Composite fillings will last many years to give you a long-lasting, beautiful smile. A composite or tooth-colored filling is used to repair a tooth that has been affected by decay, cracks, or fractures, so you can smile with confidence.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers can completely reshape your teeth and smile. Veneers are very thin pieces of durable, tooth-shaped porcelain that are custom-made and bonded onto the front of teeth to create your most winning smile.

Porcelain Crowns

Porcelain crowns are made to match the shape, size, and color of your teeth to give you a natural smile. A crown (or cap) is a covering that encases the entire tooth surface, restoring it to its original shape and size.

Porcelain Fixed Bridge

Put your best natural smile forward. A dental bridge is a non-removable appliance and is an excellent way to replace missing teeth. Dental bridges are highly durable and will last many years to come.

Implants

With a success rate of over 90 %, implants are strong restorations that make your teeth feel new again. Titanium root replacements are made to match your existing teeth, or customized to enhance your existing smile.

6 Month Smiles

Are you uncomfortable with your smile? Chances are, if you are reading this, your smile isn’t something that you are proud of. Six Month Smiles is a revolutionary orthodontic system that provides a fast and economical way for adults to begin smiling with confidence.

Dentures & Partial Dentures

A denture is a removable appliance for the replacement for missing teeth and surrounding tissue. They are made to closely resemble your natural teeth and may even enhance your smile.

Airway Therapy and Expansion Orthodontics

Sleep apnea is a common disorder in which you have one or more pauses in breathing or shallow breaths while you sleep. When we perform our intraoral exam we observe more than just the teeth. We might observe signs that indicate a sleep disorder breathing problem.

Tongue Tie, Lip Tie and More Therapies and Treatment

Myofunctional Therapy

Our trained hygienist Crystal provides personalized myofunctional therapy designed to strengthen oral muscles and improve healthy breathing, swallowing, and tongue posture. She uses gentle, targeted exercises to help patients correct dysfunctional habits that may impact dental development, sleep, and overall oral health. This therapy can also be done in combination with preparing for a tongue-tie release, ensuring optimal healing and long-term success.
