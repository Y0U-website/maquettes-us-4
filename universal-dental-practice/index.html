The practice

Welcome to Universal Dental Practice At Universal Dental Practice, we believe great dental care starts with trust, comfort, and personalized attention.

Led by Hyun Il Kim, DDS, our practice is committed to providing high-quality, comprehensive dental care in a welcoming and comfortable environment.

We proudly serve individuals and families in Indio and the surrounding Coachella Valley communities.

Our goal is simple: to help you feel comfortable, informed, and confident about your dental care at every visit.

Meet Hyun Il Kim, DDS Dr. Hyun Il Kim is a general dentist committed to providing compassionate, comprehensive dental care with a focus on each patient’s individual needs and comfort.

Dr. Kim earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry after completing his Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry at La Sierra University.

Following dental school, Dr. Kim practiced as an associate dentist in Southern California, where he gained experience providing a wide range of preventive, restorative, and general dental treatments. His approach to dentistry emphasizes clear communication, thoughtful treatment planning, and helping patients feel comfortable and informed throughout their care.

Dr. Kim believes that building trust is an important part of creating lasting relationships with his patients. He takes the time to listen to concerns, explain treatment options, and provide personalized care designed to support long-term oral health.
