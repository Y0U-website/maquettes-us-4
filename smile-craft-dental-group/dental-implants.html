Comprehensive Health Assessment

Dr. Surabhi Gajendragadkar examines your medical history, current medications, and overall health status to ensure implant surgery is safe for you. We take 3D scans of your jawbone to measure bone density and identify the exact placement locations for optimal results. This thorough evaluation also includes checking your gums for any infections that need treatment before proceeding with implant placement.

Digital Planning and Design Phase

Using advanced computer software, we create a virtual model of your mouth that shows exactly where each implant will be positioned. This digital blueprint allows us to avoid nerves and blood vessels while ensuring your new teeth will align perfectly with your bite. Dr. Gajendragadkar reviews this plan with you on screen, showing you exactly what to expect from start to finish.

Precise Implant Placement Surgery

The actual implant placement takes place in our sterile surgical suite using specialized drills and guides for pinpoint accuracy. Local anesthesia keeps you completely comfortable while we create small openings in your gums and place the titanium implants into predetermined positions. Most patients are surprised by how quick and straightforward this procedure feels compared to their expectations.

Healing Monitoring and Support

During the crucial integration period, we schedule regular check-ups to ensure your implants are bonding properly with your jawbone tissue. Dr. Gajendragadkar monitors for any signs of complications and adjusts your healing protocol if needed. We provide you with specific instructions for cleaning the surgical sites and managing any minor discomfort during recovery.

Final Restoration Attachment

Once your implants have fully integrated, we take impressions to create your custom crowns that match your natural teeth perfectly. The final appointment involves securing these permanent restorations to your implants and making any necessary bite adjustments. You’ll leave our office with fully functional teeth that look and feel completely natural in your mouth.

FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium screws that replace the roots of missing teeth by integrating directly with your jawbone tissue. A custom crown attaches to the top of each implant, creating a complete tooth replacement that functions independently from your other teeth. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth for support and become a permanent part of your mouth structure.

Do dental implants hurt?

The implant placement itself feels similar to having a tooth extracted, with numbness from local anesthesia preventing any pain during the procedure. Most people describe the recovery as less uncomfortable than they anticipated, with soreness manageable through regular over-the-counter pain relievers for 3-5 days. The discomfort peaks within the first 48 hours and then steadily decreases as your gums heal around the implant site.

Why no dairy after dental implant?

Dairy products can interfere with the blood clotting process that’s essential for proper healing after implant surgery. The proteins in milk, cheese, and yogurt may also encourage bacterial growth around the surgical site during the vulnerable first few days. Avoiding dairy for 48-72 hours helps ensure your body can form stable blood clots and begin the healing process without complications.

Are dental implants safe?

Dental implants have been used successfully for over 50 years with documented success rates above 95 % when placed in healthy patients. The titanium material is the same biocompatible metal used in hip and knee replacements, making allergic reactions extremely rare. Complications are typically minor and related to healing issues rather than the implant itself failing or causing harm to your body.

How long does a dental implant take?

A single implant procedure typically takes 60-90 minutes from start to finish, including preparation and post-surgical instructions. The complete treatment process spans 3-6 months because your jawbone needs time to grow around and integrate with the titanium implant before a crown can be attached. Most of this timeframe involves natural healing at home rather than active treatment appointments.
