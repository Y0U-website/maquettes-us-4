Meet Our Team

0 Meet our Riverbend Dentistry Team Dr. Tyler Slaughter, DDS / Owner & Founder I am incredibly honored to return to Jefferson City. To be able to practice in my hometown is a dream come true.

I graduated from Helias High School then attended Mizzou where I graduated with honors (with a Fisheries and Wildlife/Pre-Dental degree!) I attended the University of Missouri – Kansas City School of Dentistry where I received the Quintessence Award for Clinical Achievement in Restorative Dentistry.

Although I could have practiced right after graduation, I decided to apply to the General Practice Residency Program at Truman Medical Center-Lakewood to expand my knowledge and skills. I believe it was this training that takes our services to the next level for our patients; I received extensive training in performing multidisciplinary comprehensive dental care to a varied patient base.

I practiced here in Jefferson City for 9 months prior to opening Riverbend Dentistry in April of 2014, providing family and advanced cosmetic dentistry. We jumped in with both feet–completing a full remodel of the office, added two additional staff members, added additional office hours, and we haven’t looked back!

Dr. Jonas Miller, Associate Dentist

I could not be more excited to be back home in Jefferson City to practice dentistry in the community where I was born and raised.

I graduated from Helias High School and then attended Truman State University, where I graduated with honors and earned a degree in Biology. I then attended University of Missouri - Kansas City’s School of Dentistry and graduated with Honors in Oral Surgery.

My wife, Lexy, is also a Jefferson City native. We are members of Immaculate Conception Parish, and we could not be more excited to plant our roots and build our lives in Jefferson City, near both of our families.

When I’m not practicing, I’d like to consider myself an outdoorsman. Whether I’m catching trout on a fly rod, chasing deer with my bow, listening to rain on my tent fly, or piling up crappie for a fish fry, I’m happiest when I’m outside.
