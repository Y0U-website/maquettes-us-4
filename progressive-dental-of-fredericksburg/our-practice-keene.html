Our Practice

Welcome to our practice!! We are here for you and your entire family! Our team provides comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages in a relaxed and friendly environment.

Welcome to Progressive Dental of Fredericksburg! Our team is dedicated to making your visit to our office the best it possibly can be. Whether you are visiting with us for your routine cleaning and check up, your child’s first visit to the dentist, or you have a dental emergency, we will do everything we possibly can to make sure you are comfortable and relaxed.

We strive to provide comprehensive dental care and oral health education to our patients. Our friendly team stays current with the latest treatments and materials in the dental field. We work with you to meet your dental needs and focus on preventative treatment. Your oral health is extremely important, and it can affect your overall health quite profoundly. By working together, we will help you accomplish your dental goals and create a relationship based on respect and trust.

Our team is here for you. We always go the extra mile to make sure you are comfortable and have an enjoyable visit with us. We take great pride in knowing our patients enjoy coming to see us. You will notice that all of us take the time to get to know you, and we want you to do the same!

Dr. Scott Shirk DDS

Dr. Shirk is a native of Wilkes-Barre, PA. A standout saxophonist in high school, he completed a bachelor’s degree in Music Education and Saxophone Performance at the Ithaca College School of Music. After college Dr. Shirk taught high school music for 10 years before following his dream of becoming a dentist. Dr. Shirk headed off to the VCU School of Dentistry and graduated in 2013. Dr. Shirk has been practicing in the Fredericksburg area his entire dental career. In addition to general dentistry, Dr. Shirk has taken extensive post doctorate training in Invisalign clear aligners.

Dr. Shirk is an adjunct faculty member of Molis Coaching…the premier Invisalign coach for general dentists.

Outside of the office, Dr. Shirk loves spending time with his family and friends, and traveling. You may spot him out running or riding his bike downtown on a sunny day.
