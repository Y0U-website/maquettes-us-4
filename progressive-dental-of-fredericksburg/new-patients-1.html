New Patients

Are you a new patient?? You will be in great hands…

We make sure all of our new patients feel welcome, safe, and part of our family. During your first visit, Dr. Shirk and one of our hygienists will review your dental and medical history. Our team will examine your teeth and take X-rays. From there, Dr. Shirk will explain any issues you may have, answer any questions you may have, and help you choose options for treatment that work best for you. Every mouth is different, so we customize your treatment plan for your unique dental needs.

We are strong advocates of conservative and preventive dentistry. By educating our patients about proper home oral hygiene, we hope to prevent future dental problems and catch any that may arise early on.

We offer very competitive pricing and payment options for our Invisalign treatment. Be sure to ask us at your visit!

Participating Insurance:

We accept many insurance plans. Be sure to call our office or your insurance carrier to check if we are considered an in-network provider. Please remember most insurance plans have out of network benefits.
