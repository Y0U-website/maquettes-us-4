Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a same-day emergency appointment?

We do our best to accommodate same-day emergency patients. Call us as early as possible to secure your spot.

What is considered a dental emergency?

Severe tooth pain, broken or chipped teeth, lost fillings or crowns, swelling, abscesses, or trauma to the mouth.

Do you accept new patients for emergencies?

Yes. We welcome new and first-time patients and will prioritize urgent care needs.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call our office immediately so we can guide you and get you scheduled as soon as possible.
