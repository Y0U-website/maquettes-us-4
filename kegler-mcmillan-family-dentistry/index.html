The practice

We understand how scary the dentist can be to some. Here we pass no judgment which results in experiencing fewer fears and diminished anxiety.

We offer a comprehensive line of dental services and solutions to take your health and smile to the next level. Come be a part of our family, we’d LOVE to have you!

We are currently accepting new patients of all ages! Our dentists use advanced and comprehensive tools and techniques to provide a wide range of personalized dental solutions to every patient. Our goal is to not only provide exceptional care but to educate you about your oral health along the way.

We’ve set up a comfortable and relaxing environment in an attempt to ease your fears and anxiety about going to the dentist. We care about our patients and their well-being and want to give them the best possible experience.

Prior to your first appointment, we encourage you to fill out our new patient forms in order to get a jump start on the best dental journey you’ve ever had.

We understand how quickly dental costs can add up if you do not have insurance. We’re pleased to offer our patients CareCredit as an affordable way to continue their oral health treatments. In addition to CareCredit, we also accept Visa and MasterCard credit cards.

With the CareCredit website, you can calculate your estimated monthly payments, see if you prequalify and then apply for a CareCredit credit card. You can even submit online payments directly to us. There is even a CareCredit Mobile App so you can conveniently manage your credit card once you’re approved!
