Dr. Dan Kegler, D.D.S.

B.A. in Biology College of St. Thomas, 1971

Doctor of Dental Surgery University of Iowa College of Dentistry, 1975

Residency VA Hospital, New Orleans, LA, 1975-1976

General Practice Kegler, Kegler, & Arend (formerly Dr. A.G. Kegler), 1976-2019

Dr. Kegler retired from full-time practice in the fall of 2019 but is still involved with his local study club, frequently attends continuing education classes, and fills in as needed when Dr. Arend or Dr. Brad Kegler are out of the office.

Dr. Kegler has not only been practicing dentistry in Independence for over 45 years, but he has also been actively involved in the community. He has served as President of the Independence Parks and Recreation Department, was a member of the Lions Club, American Red Cross, and coached numerous youth baseball teams, including serving as coach and business manager of the Buchanan County American Legion baseball team for 11 seasons. He is also a member of St. John’s Catholic Church and the Knights of Columbus.

Dr. Kegler has been married to his wife, Vicki, for over 50 years and they have three adult children. His oldest son Brian lives in Ann Arbor, Michigan with his wife Kaci and their three children, Nolan, Noelle, and Theo. Dr. Kegler’s daughter Leah lives in Alexandria, Virginia. Brad, his youngest son, joined Kegler, Kegler & Arend DDS PC in July 2011, and lives in Independence with his wife Ann and their four children, Claire, Tobin, Cannon, and Brooks.

Dr. Kegler’s other interests include hosta gardening, landscaping, biking, and entertaining his grandchildren. He is a lifelong Chicago Cubs and Iowa Hawkeye fan, bleeding black and gold on Saturday afternoons in the fall.
