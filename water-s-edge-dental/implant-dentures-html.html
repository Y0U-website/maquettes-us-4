What Are Implant Dentures?

An implant denture is a denture that stays anchored in place thanks to sturdy dental implants . Our team will place an appropriate number of implant posts into your jaw so that they can act as artificial roots for your replacement teeth, giving them stability and chewing power comparable to that of your natural teeth.

Implant dentures can either be fixed in place or removable. Our team can help you figure out which approach is right for you when you visit our office. In the meantime, here’s a brief look at both options.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

Fixed implant dentures are designed to stay in your mouth 24/7. With this approach, you’ll be able to brush and floss your prosthesis as if it were a natural part of your mouth. In most cases, about four to eight implant posts are required to support a fixed implant denture.

Removable Implant Dentures

A removable implant denture snaps onto the implant posts in your jaw. You’ll be able to take the prosthesis out of your mouth at any time, allowing you to clean it thoroughly. Compared to fixed implant dentures, removable ones generally need fewer implant posts (typically around two to five), so they’re often a better option for patients with lower bone density levels in their jaws.

The Implant Denture Process

Step one of getting implant dentures is to schedule a consultation with our implant dentist in Port Orange. During this visit, our team will make sure that you’re a viable candidate for dental implants. Additionally, we’ll check to see whether you might need to undergo certain preliminary procedures prior to dental implant placement.

The implant posts will need to be inserted into your jaw via surgery. Our team will be able to handle this procedure in-office. Once the posts are in place, they will gradually bond with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, which normally takes around three to six months. During the final step of the process, we’ll take impressions of your mouth in order to design your final denture. The completed prosthesis will be attached to your implant posts during one last appointment.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures can be an excellent solution for those who have lost most or all of the teeth in their upper or lower jaws. In order to qualify for implant dentures, you must be in good oral health overall, and you need to have a sufficient level of bone density in your jaw. We can discuss your candidacy in more detail if you schedule a consultation with our team.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Replacing your missing teeth with implant dentures allows you to enjoy the following benefits:

  • A Varied Diet: You’ll be able to enjoy all of your favorite foods again thanks to the chewing power provided by your sturdy implant posts.
  • Jawbone Preservation: The stimulation from your dental implants can help prevent bone loss in your jaw.
  • Enhanced Appearance: Your implant dentures will be expertly customized to look as lifelike as possible.
  • Longevity: Implant dentures can last much longer than traditional dentures if you take proper care of them.
