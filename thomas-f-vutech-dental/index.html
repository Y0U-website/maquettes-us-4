Dr. Vutech may recommend a dental implant to replace a missing tooth for several reasons:

There are two phases to installing a dental implant. The first is the surgical phase where it is surgically implanted into the jawbone. It can take a few months for the implant to become incorporated into the bone, after which time it will be firm and stable.

The second step is to create and attach the dental appliance that will affix to the dental implant. This may be a replacement tooth, a crown, a bridge or a partial denture.

At the dental office of Dr. Thomas Vutech , we use a state-of-the-art digital impression system to ensure that your dental implant, crown or bridgework is a perfect fit for your mouth.

A dental implant is not right for everyone. There must be adequate bone in your jaw to support the implant. Also, your gum tissue must be healthy. If you lost your tooth to periodontal disease, then the dentist may recommend bridgework instead.
