Pulpotomies

Is your child experiencing a toothache that just won&#x27;t go away? It might be a sign of a deep cavity, but don&#x27;t worry! Our pulpotomy procedure can help relieve their discomfort in just one visit. A pulpotomy is performed when a cavity reaches the nerve of the tooth. During the procedure, we carefully clean out the infected area and place a special medicine to promote healing. To protect the tooth, a crown is placed over it, ensuring your child’s smile stays bright and healthy. This treatment will restore their oral health and bring back their carefree moments without pain.

Extractions

Sometimes, your child may need a tooth extraction. This is usually necessary when a cavity is large, causing an infection, or for orthodontic reasons. We always explore alternative treatments first, but in some cases, an extraction is the best option to restore your child&#x27;s smile. Rest assured, we use gentle techniques to ensure your child&#x27;s comfort during the procedure.

Space Maintenance

As your child grows, their baby teeth are destined to fall out to make room for their adult counterparts. But when they fall out too early, it can heighten the risk for orthodontic complications later on. We want your child to thrive, which is why we offer space maintenance, a solution that keeps your child&#x27;s teeth from shifting and having misalignment issues as they erupt.
