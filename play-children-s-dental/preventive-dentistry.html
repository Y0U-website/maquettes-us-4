Curodont

At our children’s dental practice, we’re proud to offer Curodont™, an advanced treatment that helps stop cavities before they grow. Instead of drilling, Curodont works by naturally repairing and strengthening weakened enamel. This means less stress for kids, fewer invasive procedures, and healthier smiles that last.

Digital X-Rays

Dental x-rays are a safe and important tool that help us detect cavities, monitor developing teeth, and catch potential problems early—often before they can be seen with just a mouth mirror.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants act as a protective shield for your child&#x27;s teeth! We apply a safe, white, opaque resin to the teeth, which hardens under a special light. This resin creates a durable barrier that helps keep cavity-causing bacteria and food particles away. Sealants are particularly effective for the back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings! This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our dental team will take a good look at your child&#x27;s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we&#x27;ll guide you through the next steps. Regular dental visits can help us keep your child&#x27;s mouth healthy and happy. Contact Play Children&#x27;s Dental today to get your child&#x27;s next check-up scheduled!

Nutritional Counseling

We believe in a "whole health" approach, which includes yummy, healthy food choices. It&#x27;s not always easy to change eating habits, but it&#x27;s definitely worth it. Eating well benefits not only your child&#x27;s smile but their overall wellbeing. Let us help guide you in creating tooth-friendly menus that the whole family will love!

Oral Habits

Building a strong foundation for healthy oral habits will establish your child&#x27;s dental routine for their entire lives. With our educational, compassionate care, we are here to give your child the guidance they need to have healthy dental hygiene that will stick with them for a lifetime.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals.
