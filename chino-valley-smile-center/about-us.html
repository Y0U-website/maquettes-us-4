What to expect when you walk through our door

A calm, unhurried environment We do not double-book or rush appointments. Every patient gets the time they need, with a team that treats a cleaning with the same attention as a more complex procedure.

Family dentistry for every age We welcome patients from a child’s very first dental visit through every stage of adulthood. Many of our patients have brought their children, and now their grandchildren, to this office.

Judgment-free care for all patients Whether you haven’t been to the dentist in years or you’re nervous about a specific procedure, you will not be made to feel embarrassed. Our team meets you where you are.

Modern technology, personal touch Digital X-rays, precise imaging, and current materials let us work faster and more accurately, while still taking the time to explain what everything means before moving forward.
