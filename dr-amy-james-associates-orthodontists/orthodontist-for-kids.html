Treatment Options for Children and Teens

No two smiles are the same—and that’s why we offer a variety of orthodontic solutions to fit each child’s individual needs, comfort level, and lifestyle.

Phase 1 (Early Interceptive Treatment)

For children ages 7–10, early treatment can help create space for incoming permanent teeth, correct jaw growth, and eliminate harmful oral habits. This phase often reduces or even eliminates the need for braces later in life.

Traditional Braces

Metal braces remain a reliable and effective option for correcting more advanced orthodontic problems. Today’s braces are smaller, more comfortable, and more colorful—allowing your child or teen to express their personality while straightening their teeth.

Clear Braces

For image-conscious teens who want a more subtle solution, we also offer ceramic braces that blend in with the natural color of teeth.

Invisalign for Teens

Invisalign is a great option for responsible teens who want a virtually invisible alternative to braces. These clear, removable aligners straighten teeth without wires or brackets—and they’re easy to take out for eating, brushing, and flossing.

No matter which option is chosen, your child will receive expert guidance from a skilled orthodontist for teens and kids who truly cares about their comfort and results.
