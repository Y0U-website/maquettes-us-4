What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically inserted below the gumline and into the jawbone to replace the roots of missing teeth. Due to their biocompatible nature, they’re able to chemically fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration, allowing the replacement crown, bridge, or denture that will be attached to them to function and look completely natural.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are the best tooth replacement option out there. They allow for a wide range of benefits that you can’t experience with dentures or dental bridges. To achieve this, getting dental implants requires a multi-step process that takes place over the course of several months. Everyone’s treatment looks a little bit different, but here are the four main steps that everyone can expect.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The first step of every dental implant process is an in-depth consultation and evaluation with Dr. Tehrani . During this appointment, our team will make you feel right at home with a relaxing experience tailored to you. We’ll capture any necessary diagnostic images, and Dr. Tehrani will thoroughly examine your mouth and go over your oral and health histories with you. Based on her findings, our team will outline your dental implant treatment plan so that you know exactly what to expect from the process.

Dental Implant Surgery

We partner with local oral surgeons for dental implant placement to ensure the best possible results. Don’t worry, though, we’ll make sure this step is smooth and stress-free for you. After the area is numbed with a local anesthetic, an incision is made into the gums so the implants can be placed at precise, strategic locations and angles in the jaw. Then, the gums are closed and protective caps are placed over the ends of the implants too keep them safe as the area heals.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

The following three to six months after your surgery, you’ll rest and heal. This is important, as this period of time is when osseointegration occurs, which is a crucial step in the process. After the area the healed, you will need to undergo another minor procedure in which small metal connectors called abutments are placed on the ends of the implants. This is what your restoration will attach to. Impressions are taken and sent to the dental lab so your final restoration can be customized for you.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Finally, once your implants have fused with the jawbone and your restorations are crafted, Dr. Tehrani will attach them to your implants to complete your smile. If everything looks and feels great, you will be free to show off your new pearly whites to the world!

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

During your consultation with our dental implant dentist in Lutz, Dr. Shabnam Tehrani, she will review your dental and medical history, look at your latest X-rays, complete a visual exam, and learn about your lifestyle and habits. From there, she can determine:

  • If your jawbone is strong enough to support dental implants
  • If your gums are free of infection
  • If any natural teeth you have are healthy
  • If you are willing to break bad habits, like smoking
  • If you are committed to taking good care of your smile

Missing One Tooth

For a single missing tooth, we can place one dental implant into the jawbone. Once the two have fused together, we can secure a single, free-standing dental crown over it to fill the gap in your arch. One excellent benefit of single-tooth replacement with dental implants versus traditional dental bridges is that no alteration of your natural teeth will be necessary. Plus, with proper care and maintenance, it can last for several decades!

Missing Multiple Teeth

If you’re missing multiple teeth in a row or throughout your arch, we can pair implant crowns with implant bridges to rebuild your smile. Implant bridges will be secured to two dental implants surgically placed at either end of the gap to replace consecutive missing teeth. Similar to implant crowns, this option eliminates any need for preparation of your natural teeth. Since the size, shape, and shade of the prosthetic teeth are custom-made, they will look and feel extremely lifelike, too!

Missing All Teeth

If you are missing all of your teeth, traditional dentures aren’t the only option! To eliminate the possibility of any slipping or shifting, as well as restore most of your original bite force, we can anchor a denture to anywhere between four and eight dental implants. This will provide your prosthetic with unmatched support and stability for natural function. Plus, since the implants are inserted directly into your jawbone, they help prevent bone loss and preserve your youthful face shape as well.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Congratulations on restoring your smile with beautiful, lifelike dental implants in Lutz! These durable prosthetics are the world’s only permanent tooth replacement solution, but they won’t last if you don’t take proper care of them! Here’s a closer look at some guidelines you should follow to help keep your gorgeous new grin looking and feeling its best for a lifetime!

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

One of the most incredible benefits of dental implants in Lutz is that unlike real teeth, they can’t develop painful problems like tooth decay and cavities. However, that doesn’t mean you don’t need to brush and floss them regularly. In fact, poor oral hygiene (and a dangerous condition similar to gum disease called peri-implantitis) is one of the leading causes of dental implant failure!

The good news is that caring for your restorations is as easy as it is for real teeth. Just brush them twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss daily, and maintain your regular dental checkups and cleanings with Dr. Tehrani every six months.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Break Bad Habits

Sure, sometimes it can be easier to tear into a package with your teeth than search for the right tool but doing so can put your dental implants at risk. Instead, be sure to avoid using your smile for anything off brand such as ripping open packages, biting your nails, or even gnawing on pen caps. Furthermore, don’t engage in risky behaviors like smoking, vaping, and chewing tobacco, as these can dry out the mouth, slow your healing, and increase your risk of oral infection and dental implant failure.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Dental implants in Lutz are incredibly powerful prosthetics, typically restoring complete or near complete biteforce. However, while they are incredibly durable, they can still become damaged if you aren’t careful. Thus, if you play sports, be sure to wear a mouthguard to protect them, and if you grind your teeth (bruxism) talk to Dr. Tehrani about having a custom nightguard made to keep your dental implants and any remaining natural teeth safe.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Even if you have implant dentures and no remaining real teeth, it is still incredibly important to maintain your regular visits to Dr. Tehrani. During these appointments, we’ll not only polish up your prosthetics, so they look as white and bright as they did on day one, but we’ll also check your jaw health, make sure your dental implants are secure, and screen your gums and soft tissues for serious issues like periodontal disease and oral cancer.

Dental Implant FAQs

Are Dental Implants Safe?

Dental implants have been used successfully for decades and are considered one of the safest procedures in modern dentistry. The titanium posts used are biocompatible, meaning your body recognizes them as natural tissue rather than a foreign object. This is what allows them to fuse so reliably with your jawbone.

Of course, as with any surgical procedure, there are minor risks. Dr. Tehrani will discuss them with you during your consultation and take steps to avoid them as the process moves forward. As long as you’re in good general health with adequate jawbone density, you’ll have very little to worry about.

How Successful Are Dental Implants?

Getting dental implants in Lutz is one of the most successful procedures in modern dentistry. The process is key, relying on a thorough understanding of your oral anatomy through digital imaging, as well as advanced training and excellent follow-up care.

At Allure Dental Boutique, we partner with trusted local oral surgeons for placement to ensure every step is handled with the precision it deserves. We also provide detailed post-operative instructions that will guide your recovery. With care and attention, dental implants boast an impressive 95 % success rate even ten years after placement!

Am I Too Old to Get Dental Implants?

Age alone is rarely a disqualifying factor for dental implants. What matters most is your overall health, the condition of your gums, and whether you have sufficient jawbone density to support the implant posts. This is why your consultation with Dr. Tehrani is such a vital step in the process.

She’ll evaluate your situation and let you know whether implants are a good fit. If not, she’ll share treatments that could make you a better candidate, like bone grafting or other restorative dentistry work. With guidance, many patients well into their 70s and 80s have undergone successful implant treatment and are loving their new smiles and you can too.

Will I Have to Take Off Work for Dental Implant Surgery?

Most patients take one to two days off following implant surgery to rest and manage any initial soreness or swelling. However, the procedure itself is performed under local anesthesia, so discomfort during surgery is minimal. You might feel tired afterward, but it won’t prohibit you from going back to work as long as it’s not physically demanding.

If you have a physical job, you may want to plan for a few extra days of lighter activity. Dr. Tehrani and our Lutz team will give you some post-op instructions, so you know exactly what to expect and can plan your schedule accordingly.
