Your first visit begins with a friendly welcome from our caring office staff. You will likely check-in with our Office Manager Angela, before being escorted to your exam room. After reviewing your dental and medical history, and performing necessary X-Rays, Dr. Van Dyke will complete a comprehensive oral exam to check for abnormalities, cancer, and signs of decay. Dr. Van Dyke may recommend future services or treatments based on your unique health and goals. One of our gentle, effective dental hygienists will complete your teeth cleaning and polish.

Dental X-Rays enable the doctor to see many things we cannot see with a visual exam alone. They help identify potential issues such as decay, oral cancer, bone loss, periodontal issues, and dental abscesses. Our office strives to provide the best oral health exam and treatment, this would not be possible without dental X-Rays.
