What are dental implants?

Before proceeding with implant treatment, it’s important to understand what implants are and how they work. Implants are small titanium posts used to replace lost tooth roots when you lose natural teeth. These posts are surgically placed inside your jaw where they eventually fuse with your bone tissue. This fusing, or osseointegration, process permanently secures the implants in place so they can be used to support your new teeth.

When are dental implants for the whole mouth needed?

Full mouth implants are recommended when you need to replace most or all of your teeth. As every patient is different, Dr. Tijerino and our team will determine how many implants to use for your treatment after a thorough evaluation of your condition. Most people do well with all-on-4 implant treatment, which uses only four implants to support an entire arch of upper or lower teeth. However, more implants can be used as needed.

How long does a full mouth implant treatment take?

The entire process can take anywhere between three and nine months to complete, depending on how long it takes for the implants to heal. The process of osseointegration must be totally complete before permanent restorations can be attached to the implants. During the interim period, we can place temporary teeth to make eating easier for you.

Is the procedure painful?

When performing the surgery, Dr. Tijerino uses local anesthesia to keep you from experiencing discomfort or pain. Many of our patients request sedation as well to help put them at ease. OTC pain medication and ice packs can be used to manage any temporary post-op symptoms such as gum swelling, discomfort, and soreness.

How long do implants last?

With proper oral care, implants can last for life. Implant dentures can last up to 20 years before needing to be replaced. Unlike conventional dentures that are removed for cleaning, implant dentures are fixed. You brush and floss them like natural teeth. You also continue to see your dentist every 6 months for routine cleanings.

Are full mouth implants worth it?

Implants are comparable to natural teeth in every way, allowing you to eat, smile, laugh, and speak confidently. Dental implants are designed to permanently replace missing teeth, restoring your confidence, self-esteem, and quality of life.
