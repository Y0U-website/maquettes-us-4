Are You Uninsured? Do You Have Insurance, But Aren’t In Network?

Many insurance savvy patients will think that most insurance will cover preventative at 100 % and 20 % might not seem great when compared to the 50-80 % that insurance plans generally cover restorative procedures. But when you consider that Plan for Health has no limit compared to a $1,000- $1,500 limit most insurance plans have, and Plan for Health covers more preventative treatment at 100 % than most insurance plans, and Plan for Health covers cosmetic procedures , Plan for Health starts to be a better value than many insurance plans.

Plan for Health gets you savings similar to what people with insurance get, without the limitations. Plan for Health encourages more preventive treatment so that you can avoid expensive, time consuming, painful restorative treatment later on. Because Plan for Health doesn’t have annual limits, you won’t be pressured to risk your health by waiting for the next year to roll around so that you have money in your account again.

If you don’t have insurance, Plan for Health is the best way to get savings similar to those enjoyed by insured patients. If you have insurance and you’re not in contract, and depending on how much annual coverage your insurance provides and how much the treatment you need costs, it can sometimes be worth joining the Plan for Health program even though you have insurance, especially if you want cosmetic treatment.
