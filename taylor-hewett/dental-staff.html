Meet Tom Anderson, DDS

Meet the Dentist, Tom Anderson, DDS

Dr. Anderson grew up in Jamestown, a small town located on Chautauqua Lake in western New York. He graduated from Allegheny College in Meadville, PA in 1989 with a Bachelors in Biology. He then attended the University of Buffalo School of Dental Medicine, graduating in 1994 with Thesis Honors in Periodontology.

Dr. Anderson completed a general practice residency at the Erie County Medical Center in 1995. His solo practice was opened in Clemmons, NC in 1997. Dr. Anderson devotes a significant portion of his time attending the most modern continuing education seminars in dentistry. He believes in educating his patients using “evidence- based dentistry.” His office treats patients spanning from Davie, Davidson, Forsyth, Yadkin and Guilford counties.

He is a member of the Forsyth County Dental Society, North Carolina State Dental Society and the American Dental Association. He has chaired Children’s Dental Health for Forsyth County and volunteers at the Free Dental Clinic of Reidsville and the Free Dental Clinic in Winston- Salem. Dr. Anderson, his wife Teresa, sons Riley and Keller, and Husky named Wayah, enjoy spending time in the mountains of Western Carolina, fishing, kayaking and hiking.

Meet Taylor Hewett, DMD

Meet the Dentist, Taylor Hewett, DMD

Dr. Taylor Hewett has been practicing general dentistry at our office since 2017. A native of Davie County and a patient of the practice since 1999, he returned to his hometown after completing his dental training with a desire to serve the community in which he grew up.

Dr. Hewett is a graduate of Appalachian State University where he earned a Bachelor’s of Science in Exercise and Sports Science in 2013. In 2017, he received his D.M.D from East Carolina School of Dentistry where he graduated 4th in his class and served as class president. He is a member of the American Dental Association, North Carolina Dental Society, and Forsyth County Dental Society.

In his free time, Dr. Hewett enjoys spending time with his wife, son, and two dogs. He can frequently be found fishing or in his workshop.
