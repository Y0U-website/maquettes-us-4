Dental Services in Clemmons

GET THE HIGHEST QUALITY OF DENTAL CARE AT TOM ANDERSON DDS

WE USE SAFE DENTAL PRACTICES

Our office adheres to the most up to date American Dental Association dental practice guidelines for your protection, including the latest OSHA and HIPPA rules, as well as the strictest sterilization procedures. Dr. Anderson and his staff are trained and certified to treat your dental needs. In addition, our office devotes countless hours each year on continuing education to stay at the forefront of the latest research and developments.

WE USE UP-TO-DATE TECHNOLOGY

Our office offers a wide variety of the latest in dental technology. We are proud to offer our patients the lowered radiation and comfort of our full digital panorex and dental bitewing x-ray machine. Long gone are the days of large painful uncomfortable x-rays. Our patients love the ease and comfort of digital radiography.

WE ARE PROUD OF OUR QUALITY PATIENT CARE

From our front office and throughout our entire dental team, we take great pride in serving our patients with utmost professionalism, dedication and above all kindness. We realize that your dental visit can be stressful. It is our promise to treat each patient exactly the way we ourselves would want to be treated.

Crowns and Bridges

Our office never outsources its lab work to other states or countries. We pride ourselves in using local highly trained technicians to ensure the highest quality of materials and workmanship. All crown and bridge dentistry is done right here in the piedmont triad.

Dentures and Partials

Whether you have existing dentures or partials or are transitioning to them, our office takes the time to listen to your needs. While nothing can replace the teeth that you were born with, we pride ourselves in teaming up with the best removal prothesis labs in the piedmont triad to provide our patients the best care and comfort.

Dental Fillings

We offer a wide variety of filling restoration materials. Each material exhibits its own unique characteristics. From cosmetic white fillings, to silver and gold, we will take the time to discuss which is the best choice for you.

Extractions

Dr. Anderson has 25 years of experience with extractions. We are in the tooth saving business and our office realizes that many of our patients will be faced with a dental extraction at some time in their lives.

Dental Veneers and Dental Laminates

Veneers are a good choice to replace the outer surfaces of teeth that are either damaged or unattractive. They are permanently cemented in place resulting in a more attractive and healthier smile.

Smile Makeover

A smile makeover is the process of improving the appearance of your smile through one or more dental procedures. Those procedures could include- veneers, crowns, bonding, implants, and whitening. Other dental specialties could also be involved including orthodontics as well as oral surgery.

Periodontal Care

Periodontitis, also known as gum disease, is a common problem that many patients suffer. However without proper treatment it can result in tooth and bone loss. We work as a team with local Winston Salem periodontists to provide our patients comprehensive choices and care to best suit your unique conditions.

Mouth Guards

Guards are made to help protect our patients teeth from excessive grinding, clenching and gnashing. Some of these habits can occur while you sleep. In many cases symptoms will arise such as jaw and muscle pains. Our goal is to protect your teeth, gums, and joint from further harm. Please inform our staff if you think you are grinding your teeth.

Emergency Care

Should you find yourself needing emergency dental care, rest assured that we are available for consultation as well as treatment of many emergency conditions. Some examples of emergencies- infected teeth, broken teeth, broken fillings, chips, and aching wisdom teeth
