FTCA Deemed Health Center Aviso de FTCA

Vida Y Salud-Health Systems, Inc. (VSHSI) is a Federal Tort Claims Act (FTCA) Deemed Health Center. VSHSI is a deemed employee of the Public Health Service under 42 U.S.C. 233(g)-(n); therefore its medical and dental providers are deemed as federal employees with liability protection coverage under the Federal Tort Claims Act (FTCA) for actions within the scope of deemed employment.

Any claims or notice of medical malpractice claims should be submitted by a claimant to the Office of General Counsel at the address below:
