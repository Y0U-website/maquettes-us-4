The Process of Getting Implant Supported Dentures at Peak Dental Health

From the initial consultation to the final fitting, we ensure every step is clear, comfortable, and tailored to your needs. Our process includes:

  • Consultation: Dr. Nelson assesses your oral health, discusses options, and plans your treatment, utilizing digital Trios impressions for unmatched precision.
  • Implant Placement: Skilled professionals place the implants in your jawbone.
  • Healing Period: A crucial time for your implants to integrate with your bone.
  • Fitting Your Dentures: Once healing is complete, your custom, digitally-impressed dentures are fitted, ensuring comfort and a natural look.
