About

  • What ages do you see at Wave Pediatric Dentistry? We care for children from infancy through age 21 . From first dental visits to ongoing preventive and restorative care, we support patients through every stage of childhood, adolescence, and young adulthood.
  • What is your approach to pediatric dental care? We believe great care starts with trust. At Wave, we explain each step in simple language, involve parents in every decision, and tailor care to each child’s comfort level and needs.
  • How do you help children feel comfortable at the dentist? Our environment is intentionally warm and uncluttered, and we move at a pace that respects each child. We take time to build rapport, reduce anxiety, and create positive early experiences that support confidence over time.
