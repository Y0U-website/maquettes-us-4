MEET Dr. Rawls

Dr. Hunter Rawls was born and raised in Paragould, Arkansas where he went to Greene County Tech before graduating with his bachelor’s degree in biology at Arkansas State University in Jonesboro, Arkansas in 2016. He later attended and graduated from the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry in Memphis, Tennessee in 2021. After practicing in Mountain Home, Arkansas for two years, Dr. Rawls decided to come back to his community to provide service and care to those in Northeast Arkansas.

Dr. Rawls is married to his high school sweetheart, Makenzie Strope Rawls and they have a beautiful baby girl named Riley, and three dogs. The Rawls’ family could not be more excited to be able to serve this great community and build relationships along the way.
