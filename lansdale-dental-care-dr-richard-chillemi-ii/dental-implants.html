Types of Dental Implant Solutions

At Lansdale Dental Care, we offer a range of implant-based restorations to suit your unique needs:

Single Tooth Implants

Ideal for patients missing one tooth. The implant is topped with a custom dental crown for a seamless restoration.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Used when several adjacent teeth are missing. Implants anchor the bridge instead of relying on natural teeth for support.

Full Arch Implants

Multiple implants support a full set of upper or lower teeth for patients missing most or all of their teeth. This includes implant-supported dentures and overdentures.

Mini Implants

A less invasive option for select patients, mini implants are smaller in diameter and can sometimes be placed without bone grafting.

What to Expect From the Implant Process

Dental implant treatment at Lansdale Dental Care typically takes place over several months and involves three main stages:

Dr. Chillemi reviews your dental and medical history, takes digital X-rays , and evaluates your bone density and gum health. He will walk you through your options, the expected timeline, and the full cost estimate.

The titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone during a minor in-office surgical procedure performed under local anesthesia. Nitrous oxide sedation is available for patients who prefer additional comfort. Most patients return to normal activity within a day or two.

Over the following months, the implant fuses with the jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration. Once fully healed, a custom porcelain crown is attached to the implant, completing your new tooth. The result looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults in good general health with sufficient jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. You may be a good candidate if you have one or more missing teeth, a fully developed jawbone, healthy gum tissue, and are in good general health. If you have experienced bone loss, you may still be a candidate after a bone grafting procedure. Dr. Chillemi will evaluate your oral health at your consultation.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last 15 to 25 years or longer — many patients have implants that last a lifetime. The titanium post itself is designed to be permanent. The crown may need to be replaced after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear. Regular checkups and good oral hygiene are key to implant longevity.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Coverage for dental implants varies by insurance plan. Some plans cover a portion of the procedure, while others may cover the crown or related extractions. Our team can help verify your benefits. We offer financing options for patients whose plans do not provide full coverage. Call to learn more.
