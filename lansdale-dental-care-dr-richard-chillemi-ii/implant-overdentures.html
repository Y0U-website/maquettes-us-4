Benefits of Implant Overdentures

Overdentures provide significant advantages over standard removable dentures, especially for those who have experienced discomfort or instability in the past:

For many, implant overdentures restore both function and self-esteem, leading to a better quality of life.

  • Increased denture stability—no more slipping or shifting
  • Improved chewing efficiency and more diverse food options
  • Enhanced speech clarity without the need for adhesives
  • Preservation of jawbone volume thanks to implant stimulation
  • Removable design for easier hygiene and care
  • More secure fit with fewer sore spots or pressure areas

How Do Implant Overdentures Work?

Implant overdentures are supported by a limited number of dental implants—typically 2 to 4 per arch—placed in strategic positions in the jaw. These implants are equipped with special abutments that connect to attachments on the underside of the denture, creating a secure and reliable fit.

Dr. Chillemi will recommend the most suitable design based on your anatomy, lifestyle, and treatment goals.

  • Locator-style connectors (ball or socket-shaped)
  • Bar-retained systems, where the denture snaps onto a small metal bar that links the implants

Who Is a Good Candidate?

You may be a strong candidate for implant overdentures if you:

We’ll conduct a thorough exam and 3D imaging to determine if this solution is right for you.

  • Are missing all teeth in the upper or lower arch
  • Want greater stability than conventional dentures can offer
  • Have sufficient bone to support implants (or are eligible for bone grafting)
  • Prefer a removable denture that’s easier to clean and maintain
  • Are looking for a cost-effective alternative to full fixed implant bridges

Treatment Timeline

1. Consultation & Planning

We assess your oral health and use digital imaging to plan precise implant placement.

2. Implant Surgery

A minor surgical procedure places the implants in your jaw. Healing takes several weeks to a few months, during which the implants integrate with the bone.

3. Attachment of Overdenture

Once healed, we fit your custom overdenture to the implant abutments and ensure optimal retention and comfort.

In some cases, a temporary denture can be worn during healing so you’re never without teeth.

Caring for Implant Overdentures

While implant overdentures are removable, they require proper daily care to ensure longevity:

We’ll guide you through a personalized care plan tailored to your overdenture type and oral condition.

  • Remove and rinse after meals
  • Brush gently with a soft denture brush
  • Clean around the implants and attachments
  • Store the denture in water or a cleaning solution overnight
  • Schedule routine dental visits to monitor implant health and fit

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Overdentures

How do overdentures differ from implant-supported dentures?

Overdentures are removable, allowing for easier cleaning, while implant-supported dentures can be fixed in place. Both offer superior stability compared to traditional dentures.

Will I still need to use adhesive?

In most cases, no. The implant attachments provide a secure fit without the need for glue or pastes.

Can I upgrade my current dentures to overdentures?

Yes—if your existing denture is in good condition, it may be retrofitted to attach to implants. We’ll evaluate your current prosthesis during your consultation.

Are implant overdentures covered by insurance?

Some dental plans may cover portions of the procedure, particularly the denture itself. Our team will help clarify your benefits and explore financing if needed.

How long do implant overdentures last?

With proper care, the denture can last 7–10 years or more, while the implants themselves can last decades.
