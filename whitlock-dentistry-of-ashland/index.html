The practice

At our Ashland, KY dental office, we take a proactive approach to dentistry and believe that prevention lays the foundation for a lifetime of healthy teeth and gums. We also take a conservative approach to dentistry, meaning we provide the services you need to maintain a healthy smile, but we will never pressure you. We respect your personal smile goals!

Our warm and friendly team is here to make you feel relaxed and comfortable while under our care, and we invite you to contact our Ashland, KY dental office today to schedule an exam.

We offer a cozy atmosphere inside our dental office, and when you’re with us, we want you to feel like family.

Our patient-oriented dentist Dr. Dave follows guidelines put forward by the American Dental Association when it comes to patient care and best practices.

This means he recommends a good oral hygiene routine at home.

For our part, we like to see patients at least twice per year for routine exams and professional teeth cleanings in Ashland. Remember, even the best oral care routine at home will not remove the stubborn plaque and bacteria that can lead to adult cavities and even periodontal disease.

However, if you have specific oral health issues, we may customize your treatment schedule to better address your situation.

At Whitlock Dentistry of Ashland, we provide comprehensive services so we can take care of your dental issues in a single location. We work very hard to provide all services in our Ashland, KY dental office.
