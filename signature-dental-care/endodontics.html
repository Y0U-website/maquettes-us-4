Endodontics

Endodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, treating, and preventing diseases or injuries that affect the dental pulp or nerve of the tooth. At Signature Dental Care, the most common endodontic procedure we perform is a root canal, wherein the infected or damaged pulp inside the tooth is removed, and the space is cleaned, shaped, and filled to save the natural tooth.

Endodontists are highly trained dental professionals who perform these intricate procedures. They do so to relieve pain, eliminate infection, and preserve patients’ natural teeth, ensuring their oral health and well-being.

When Is Endodontic Treatment Needed?

  • When there is bacterial pulp infection, often resulting from untreated cavities or dental trauma. This can cause severe pain, swelling, and discomfort if left untreated.
  • When tooth decay progresses to the point where it reaches the pulp, endodontic treatment becomes necessary to remove the infected tissue.
  • A physical injury or blow to the tooth can damage the pulp, leading to pain and infection.
  • If a tooth has a significant crack or fracture, it can create a pathway for bacteria to enter the pulp and cause infection.
  • Sometimes, teeth with multiple restorative procedures may develop pulp problems, necessitating endodontic intervention.

The Benefits of Endodontics

Endodontics in Temecula, CA, allows individuals to keep their natural teeth, essential for proper chewing, speech, and a natural smile. Preserving natural teeth also maintains the balance of forces in the mouth, preventing adjacent teeth from shifting out of place.

One of the primary reasons for undergoing endodontic treatment is to relieve severe toothache and discomfort caused by pulp infection or inflammation. By removing the infected pulp, the source of pain is eliminated.

Dental infections, if left untreated, can spread to surrounding tissues and even to other parts of the body. Endodontic treatment removes the source of infection, preventing the infection from spreading and causing further health complications.

After a successful root canal treatment, the treated tooth is typically restored with a crown by our dentist in Temecula, CA. These crowns are designed to closely match the natural appearance of the patient’s teeth, ensuring a seamless blend within the smile. This restores the tooth’s function and enhances the patient’s overall appearance.

Preserving natural teeth through endodontics ensures efficient chewing functionality. It also maintains proper speech patterns, preventing speech impediments due to missing or damaged teeth.
