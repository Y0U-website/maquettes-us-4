About Brookhaven

A practice rooted in trust, built across generations, and dedicated to the families of Brookhaven and greater Atlanta.

Four decades of trusted care, a new generation leading the way

Brookhaven Village Dentistry has cared for this community for over forty years. What began as Gordon Family Dentistry, founded by Dr. Anita Gordon, grew into one of Atlanta&#x27;s most trusted practices, treating patients who returned with their children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren.

Today, that same care continues under Dr. Myra Scarlett, who brings modern dentistry and the latest technology to the practice while preserving everything patients have always valued here. From routine cleanings to cosmetic and restorative work, Dr. Scarlett and the team deliver thorough, personal care for the whole family, from young children to seniors.

The technology is current, the techniques are advanced, and the welcome is the same one this neighborhood has known for generations. We treat every patient as a guest, take the time to understand your goals, and build the kind of relationship you can rely on for years to come.
