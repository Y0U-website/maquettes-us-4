Grant Pitt, DDS

Dr. Pitt

DDS, DPh, FICOI

Grant Pitt has worked in the healthcare industry as an employee, partner and business owner for over 30 years. While he wears many hats in order to provide the highest level of care, he’s spent many years assembling the best team possible — in experience, knowledge, character, work ethic and paralleled compassion for our dental patients.

Dr. Pitt grew up in northwest Oklahoma City and attended Putnam City North. His parents, Jerry and Sherry, taught and coached in the Putnam City School District for over 30 years. He also has two brothers, Dr. Clint Pitt, who practices dentistry in Mustang, and Dr. Trent Pitt, an optometrist in Oklahoma City. Dr. Pitt was a practicing pharmacist, graduating from Southwestern Oklahoma State University, before deciding to make the move to dentistry. He graduated from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 2007.

Dr. Pitt currently resides in Edmond with his wonderful wife, Becky. As he puts it, “She’s my rock. She deserves more compliments than I have adjectives and adverbs for.” Dr. Grant and Becky have three great kids, Jaxon, Gracie and Ava.

Jaxon, age 28, currently attends the University of Oklahoma’s College of Dentistry, and is a part of the Class of 2029. Gracie, 24 years old, is in her fourth year at the Oklahoma State University College of Osteopathic Medicine. Ava, 21 years old, attends Oklahoma State University, with plans to graduate in May of 2027.

With a natural passion for dentistry, Dr. Pitt enjoys attending continuing education courses, in order to stay current on new technology and approaches to dentistry.

“I enjoy all aspects of dentistry and have a very broad base of health knowledge. This allows me to diagnose and treat 95 % of all dental cases I see in the office.”
