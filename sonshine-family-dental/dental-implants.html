Dental Implants

Sonshine Family Dental Dental Implants Services Contact Us General & family dentistry Dental Implants Dental implants are a long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring confident function. Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, implants replace the tooth root as well as the visible crown, helping support jawbone health. They are designed to look, feel, and function much like natural teeth. At Sonshine Family Dental, we can discuss whether dental implants are the right choice for rebuilding your smile.

About This Service From post to restoration Once fusion has finished, the dental implant restoration — such as a crown, bridge, or set of dentures — is placed on top of the implant post. The restoration is crafted to blend in with your unique smile, providing a comfortable and aesthetic finish.

With proper care, a dental implant can last many years or even a lifetime. Contact us to learn whether dental implants in Avon, Indiana can help you achieve a functional, complete smile.
