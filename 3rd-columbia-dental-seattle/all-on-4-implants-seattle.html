How Are All-On-4 Different Than Dental Implants?

With conventional dental implants, each post is surgically implanted into the gums and individual replacement teeth are attached. With an all-on-4 implant, just four posts are anchored into the patient’s jawbone, and a permanent denture is attached to the implanted posts. This procedure is considered a permanent oral health solution to significant tooth loss, and this implant can last for several years with proper care.

What Are the Benefits of All-On-4 Implants?

There are many benefits to all-on-4 implants. With this restorative procedure, patients regain a healthy oral appearance and smile. In addition, this type of implant replaces an entire set of missing teeth with just one surgery and only four implants. Unlike full dentures, all-on-4 implants are secured in the mouth, so they won’t shift or become loose. The recovery time is also shorter for all-on-4 implants compared to conventional dental implants.

It’s important for patients to care for their dental implants just as they would their natural teeth with proper oral hygiene habits. This includes daily brushing and flossing as well as keeping in-office appointments ever six months for routine dental examinations and professional cleanings .
