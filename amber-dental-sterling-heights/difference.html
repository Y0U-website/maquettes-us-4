Prevention First

Our philosophy is simple: the healthiest dentistry is often the dentistry you never need.

By emphasizing regular preventive care, early diagnosis, patient education, and healthy daily habits, we help patients avoid unnecessary treatment whenever possible. We believe informed patients make healthier choices. We explain every diagnosis, discuss your treatment options, answer your questions, and help you choose the care that’s best for your overall health and your goals.

Meet Your Dental Family

Cori grew up in Romania and moved to the USA in 2007. She worked as a pharmacy technician for four years in her home country. However, she immediately fell in love with the dental field while working as Dr. Ardelean’s first assistant out of dental school after they got married.

The best part of her job is interacting with our patients (who quickly become part of our dental family) plus helping to prevent disease and improve oral health. Each of her patients receives focused attention, quality treatments, and thorough evaluations to promote comfortable and confident hygiene visits.

In her free time, Cori likes to spend time with Dr. Ardelean and their son. She also likes to travel, experience new cultures and foods, and relax on tropical beaches (her bucket list includes Bora Bora). Cori’s passionate about giving back to our community and participates in dental mission trips each summer.

Julie grew up in Clawson, MI and decided to pursue the dental field to help people understand how important their oral health is in relation to the rest of the body. Her favorite quote is, “The prettiest thing you can wear is a smile.”

The best part of her job is patients like you! She focuses on educating patients about the oral microbiome—the community of microbes in the mouth—and how keeping it balanced reduces chronic inflammation throughout the entire body. Her cleanings are tailored not just to brighten your smile, but to safeguard your systemic health. Julie always aims to create a fun experience for our visitors to ease dental fear or anxiety.

In her free time, you can find her spending time at her cabin on the Au Sable River, kayaking year round, riding dirt bikes and four-wheelers, and camping. But, really, it comes down to anything that involves nature.

She loves meeting new patients, getting to know them, and helping them understand what our Sterling Heights, MI dentist has to offer. Her friendly and caring personality creates a positive, welcoming atmosphere for all our visitors.

When she’s not at the practice, Cathy likes to spend time with her family and looks forward to the summer for boating. Her bucket list includes traveling and touring all of Italy, Greece, and France. A fun fact? She can speak multiple languages.
