The Process of Getting Braces

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

Your first appointment includes a full exam and digital images. Dr. Ardelean reviews your bite and discusses your smile goals. We create a personalized orthodontic treatment plan and answer your questions so you know what to expect.

Step 2: Placement and Adjustments

We place brackets and wires carefully on your teeth. You may wear elastic bands depending on your needs. Braces apply gentle pressure to gradually shift teeth. You will visit our office every few weeks for adjustments and progress checks.

Step 3: Monitoring Progress and Final Results

Over time, your teeth move into proper alignment. Most patients wear braces for several months to a few years, depending on the case. Once complete, we remove the braces and provide retainers to protect your results.
