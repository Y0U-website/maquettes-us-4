The practice

Dr. Brian Cilla is a native of West Michigan. Upon completion of his undergraduate studies at the University of Michigan, he earned a BGS with a concentration in Biology. After graduation from the University of Michigan Dental School, Dr. Cilla continued his education and earned a Master’s degree in Periodontology in 1987.

West Michigan Periodontics offers a wide variety of surgical services. We are very conservative in our treatment recommendations and limit surgery to the areas where it is absolutely necessary.

The early stages of periodontal disease can be treated with non-surgical periodontal therapy. Non-surgical periodontal therapy is often the first line of defense and precedes surgical therapy for the more severe cases. This is done to improve the overall health of gingival tissue before surgery and help reduce the areas requiring surgery.

Dr. Cilla specializes in conservative treatment of gum disease and cosmetic procedures to aesthetically enhance your smile as well as diagnosis and clinically treat lesions of oral pathology.

During your course of treatment, Dr. Cilla will work closely with your general dentist. A referral by a general dentist is not required to visit our office, however we always welcome referrals from patients and friends of the practice.

New patients must complete the Medical History and Acknowledgement Form, either in-office or by bringing printed forms on the day of the appointment.
