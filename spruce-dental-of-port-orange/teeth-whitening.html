Teeth Whitening

The process begins with a consultation to assess your teeth, oral health, and the type of stains present. This allows our team to recommend the safest and most effective whitening approach for your smile.

For in-office treatments, a high-strength professional whitening solution is applied carefully to your teeth. The solution penetrates enamel to break up stains, producing a visibly brighter smile in about one hour. Custom take-home kits are also available, allowing you to whiten gradually at your convenience. These trays are designed to fit comfortably and ensure even coverage.

Throughout treatment, our team monitors your progress and provides tips to reduce sensitivity, maintain results, and keep your smile looking its best. With proper care, your brighter smile can last for months or even years.
