Implant Dentistry

Implants to the Rescue!

Replacing missing teeth with lifelike dental implants by a skilled dentist has many benefits. When a tooth is lost and not replaced, the underlying bone structure also starts to deteriorate. This causes premature aging in your appearance and the further loss of surrounding teeth or teeth "drifting" out of place.

Implants prevent these processes, ensuring the health and stability of your teeth, bones and gums. A small artificial tooth root is placed in the bone and then capped by a beautiful dental crown.

" Implants are really a great option. They provide a better way to live and enjoy your life! ."

-Orlando dental implants dentist, Zachary Hodgins
