Orthodontics

The optimum time for Interceptive Orthodontic Treatment is when a patient has baby teeth, or in a mixed dentition (baby and permanent teeth). A patient may require more than one phase of interceptive orthodontic treatment.

Dr. Khan is trained to identify subtle problems with jaw growth and eruption of teeth. Early detection of orthodontic problems may simplify treatment, and result in shorter treatment times. At times, waiting until all the permanent teeth have erupted, or until facial growth is nearly complete, may make correction of some problems more difficult.
