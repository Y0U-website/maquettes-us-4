Dr. Chelian is board-certified. Only a small percentage of orthodontists achieve this.

Dr. Chelian practiced general dentistry in Massachusetts and Rhode Island before showing an interest in orthodontics. He conducted research on Accelerated Osteogenic Orthodontics (Wilckodontics) a method to shorten time in braces and obtained his certification in Wilckodontics.

Most children won’t need braces right away, and it’s often just an observational period. Dr. Chelian will simply monitor your little one’s dental and facial development and let you know when it’s time for treatment.

Whether your teen needs traditional metal braces, clear braces or Invisalign, we’ll be with you every step of the way to achieve a confident smile that will last a lifetime. You’ll notice a big change in your teen’s confidence!

Adults are usually great candidates for Invisalign. With no metal brackets or wires to worry about, you can live life as usual while also achieving the smile you’ve always wanted. It’s a win-win!
