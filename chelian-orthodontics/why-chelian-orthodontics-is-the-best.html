Treatments provided

We proudly treat orthodontic patients of all ages. From children at the age of seven seeking out their first consultation to adults looking to improve their smiles, we have the treatment and the skills to help. We offer:

  • Traditional braces – our bracket and wire braces are made of the highest-grade stainless steel, making them more efficient and effective than ever. They are also much smaller and less noticeable than traditional braces of earlier days.
  • Ceramic braces – Ceramic braces are a great alternative to traditional metal braces, providing the efficiency and effectiveness of bracket and wire braces, but in a more discreet way.
  • Invisalign – Invisalign allows patients to straighten their smiles using clear, removable aligners that fit snugly over the teeth. They’re comfortable and easy to care for, too.
  • Lightforce braces – Lightforce braces are 3-D printed braces that offer the most customized fit. They are efficient, discreet, and comfortable.
