Teen Orthodontics

As a teen you might be a little nervous about getting braces. But don’t worry, getting braces at our office is EASY!

There are several orthodontic treatment options for teens just like you!

TRADITIONAL BRACES

The most popular treatment option for teens is our metal braces. Made of stainless steel, metal braces align teeth using metal brackets and archwires. Braces today are much smaller and much more comfortable than braces in the past!

INVISALIGN TEEN

Looking to straighten your smile without all the metal? We’ve got you covered! Invisalign Teen uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually shift your teeth into position. With Invisalign Teen, there are no issues with school pictures, prom or hanging out with friends. And since they are removable you can eat and drink what you like!
