FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots and provide a foundation for artificial teeth. The implant fuses with your jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration, creating a permanent anchor for crowns, bridges, or dentures. They function and feel like natural teeth once fully integrated and restored.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental maintenance, dental implants can last 25 years or even a lifetime in many cases. The crown or restoration attached to the implant may need replacement after 15-20 years due to normal wear and tear. Factors like oral hygiene, smoking habits, and overall health can affect the longevity of your implant investment.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during implant placement due to local anesthesia and sedation options that keep them comfortable throughout the procedure. Post-operative pain is typically manageable with over-the-counter medications and peaks within the first 2-3 days before gradually subsiding. Many patients report that the discomfort is less than they expected and similar to having a tooth extraction.

Why no dairy after a dental implant?

Dairy products can increase mucus production and may interfere with blood clot formation at the implant site during the critical first few days of healing. The proteins in dairy can also promote bacterial growth in the mouth, potentially increasing infection risk around the surgical site. Most dentists recommend avoiding dairy for 24-48 hours after surgery to optimize healing and reduce complications.

Are dental implants safe?

Dental implants have been used successfully for over 50 years, with extensive research supporting their safety and effectiveness in tooth replacement. The titanium material is biocompatible and has been used in medical devices for decades without adverse reactions in healthy patients. Success rates exceed 95 % when placed by experienced professionals following proper protocols and aftercare instructions.
