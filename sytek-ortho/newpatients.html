First Appointment Expectations

We are excited that you are interested in orthodontic treatment at Sytek Ortho!

During your first visit, our friendly staff members will greet you and make sure we have all of the important information on file for your patient record. Whether scheduling your appointment online or by phone, you will receive a welcome email with the necessary forms to fill out prior to your first visit.

One of our staff members will take a set of diagnostic photos and x-rays so that we can identify your unique treatment needs. During your consultation, Dr. Sytek will review your photos and x-rays with you, discuss your treatment options, and answer any questions you may have about your treatment plan.

If you feel our office will be a good fit for you and your family, our treatment coordinator will help guide you through the next steps for starting treatment. This includes using OrthoFi to review any insurance benefits and select a flexible payment plan that best fits your needs.
