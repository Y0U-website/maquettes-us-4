Dental Implants

Your dental implant dentist will recommend the best option based on your oral health and goals.

  • Implant-supported bridges for multiple missing teeth
  • Full-mouth dental implants for replacing all teeth in the upper, lower or both arches
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution and can last a lifetime with proper care. Routine dental checkups, good oral hygiene and avoiding harmful habits like smoking help extend their longevity.
  • What are the benefits of replacing a missing tooth? Replacing a missing tooth with a dental implant helps to: Restore your ability to chew and speak comfortably.
  • Prevent bone loss and maintain jaw structure.
  • Improve the appearance of your smile for increased confidence.
