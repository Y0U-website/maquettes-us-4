Meet Dr. Payton Judy

Passion for Excellence in Dentistry

Dr. Payton Judy combines strong clinical training with a patient-centered approach to provide high-quality dental care. Born in Kirksville, Missouri, and raised in Gunnison, Utah, he completed his undergraduate studies at Utah State University after serving a two-year mission in Puerto Rico for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. He went on to earn his D.M.D. degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine. His commitment to excellence helps him provide thoughtful, personalized care that supports both oral health and patient confidence.

Dr. Judy enjoys restorative and family dentistry and is excited to serve patients in Oklahoma. His goal is to create a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, informed, and cared for throughout their dental experience. By combining skill, compassion, and a genuine desire to help others, he works to support healthy smiles for life.

Life Beyond Dentistry

Dr. Payton Judy brings warmth and relatability to his role as a general dentist, rooted in his love for family, the outdoors, and meaningful connection. He enjoys fishing, hunting, skiing, and golf, and values any opportunity to stay active outside. His favorite part of life is being married to his wife, Hallie, and spending time wrangling their little boy, Cal.

His appreciation for family life and the outdoors reflects the same grounded, caring personality he brings to dentistry. Dr. Judy’s dedication to his patients extends beyond treatment as he strives to make every visit positive, personal, and encouraging.
