About The Dental Health Center

Meet the Team at The Dental Health Center

The Dental Health Center is a family-owned and operated dental practice that makes comfortable dental care for your family a top priority. Our goal is to deliver superior quality dentistry in a friendly and comfortable environment that helps you feel good about achieving and maintaining a healthy, problem-free smile.

Dr. Daniel Seger and our team work together to ensure that you are comfortable, healthy, and happy. We believe that you deserve the highest standard of dental care, so we are pleased to offer state-of-the-art dental technology that streamlines your treatment and improves your comfort. Modern techniques combined with our gentle approach ensure that your experiences with us are as stress-free as possible.

Please take a moment to meet our excellent team. Give our office a call at (610) 494-0404 to arrange an appointment. Or request an appointment on our contact page .

Dr. Daniel R. Seger

Dr. Seger is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. He stays current with the latest advances in dentistry through continuing education courses. He is currently pursuing a fellowship with the Academy of General Dentistry which recognizes extensive continuing education in advanced techniques and skills.

Dr. Seger met his wife Kate during his time at Temple Dental School. Kate is an intensive care nurse in Philadelphia and shares Dr. Seger’s passion for helping others. Dr. Seger and Kate have two beautiful daughters, Nora and Hailey. In their spare time, they enjoy being active and spending time with their dog Lily.

When not in the office, Dr. Seger provides dental care for several assisted living facilities and nursing homes in the Philadelphia area. He has a lot of experience working with elderly and differently-abled patients and finds it very rewarding to help care for this underserved population.

In his free time, Dr. Seger enjoys fishing, golfing, and snowboarding. Family time is a priority, and he loves spending time with his wife and daughters and enjoying Sea Isle City with them in the summertime.

Tracy Doverspike

Tracy grew up in upstate New York where she graduated from Eastman Dental Assisting School. She then moved to the Aston area and joined The Dental Health Center in 1996. Tracy received her Expanded Functions Dental Assisting license in 2008.

Tracy enjoys getting to know our patients and helping them understand their dental benefits and how to use them to meet their needs. She enjoys being part of a supportive team that works hard to make sure our patients’ appointments are as pleasant as possible.

In her free time, Tracy enjoys traveling, reading, and cross stitching.

Brittany Stagg

Brittany graduated from Ridley High School and followed her passion for clean teeth at the Pennsylvania College of Technology where she received a degree in dental hygiene in 2011.

Brittany has been working at The Dental Health Center since 2012 and loves educating our patients about their oral health, and then seeing an improvement at their next visit. She also values the relationships she has built with her patients and always likes to hear about their family life.

A native of Ridley, Brittany enjoys spending free time with her family and friends, traveling, and taking walks with her fluffy dog Bianca. Brittany and her husband are happily expecting their first child in March 2019.

Linda Adams

Linda had been a patient with us for over 25 years when she became our receptionist in 2011. She enjoys her job as our receptionist because she loves meeting and getting to know our patients. Having a great boss and supportive co-workers makes every day a pleasure.

Linda is a long-time resident of Aston. She is the mother of two lovely daughters and Mom-Mom to her four wonderful grandchildren. In her free time, Linda enjoys spending time with family and vacationing at Delaware beaches.
