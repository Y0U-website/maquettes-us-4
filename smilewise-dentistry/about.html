Get to Know Your Dentist

Meet Dr. Reem Akel

Dr. Reem Akel founded SmileWise Dentistry to deliver the kind of care she would want for her own family: gentle, thorough, and rooted in mutual respect. Originally from Florida, Dr. Akel holds a bachelor’s and master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences and earned her DDS from Virginia Commonwealth University. She’s certified in Invisalign and Botox and is an active member of the American Academy of Clear Aligners. Her use of advanced tools—like the newest iTero scanner—reflects her commitment to precision, efficiency, and long-lasting results.

But what really sets Dr. Akel apart is her patient-first philosophy. Whether she’s helping you understand your treatment plan or working with your preferences around fluoride, she meets you with empathy and flexibility. With a warm demeanor and strong clinical expertise, Dr. Akel is here to support you through every stage of your dental journey.

Your Visit, Made Simple

What to Expect at Your First Appointment

We want your first visit to be the beginning of a great relationship. From the start, we’ll take the time to get to know you, understand your goals, and create a plan that supports your long-term health. No pressure, no judgment—just real conversations, thoughtful care, and a team that’s here to help.

Patient Experience Expect a warm welcome, a peaceful environment, and one-on-one time with Dr. Akel. We’ll review your dental history, perform a thorough exam, and walk you through everything you need to know. To enhance your visit, you can select your amenities from our comfort menu before we get started. You’ll leave feeling confident and cared for.

Modern Comforts Meet Cutting-Edge Care

We’ve designed our space with you in mind—combining spa-like comforts with advanced technology that makes treatment more precise and enjoyable.

iTero Scanner

We use the latest-generation iTero to take a fast, comfortable digital oral health scan—no goop, no mess. This 3D imaging technology gives us a detailed look at your teeth and gums, allowing for accurate diagnoses, more efficient treatment planning, and better long-term results.

Comfort-First Amenities

From warm towels and support pillows to Wallaby™ weighted blankets and noise-canceling headphones, every detail is here to help you relax. Choose from our comfort menu, stream your favorite shows, and enjoy a refreshing drink from our beverage station.

Relaxing, Inviting Atmosphere

With calming design, gentle lighting, and an attentive team, our office helps reduce dental anxiety and encourages a more positive dental experience.
