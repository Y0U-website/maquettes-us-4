The practice

We offers a full range of dental services including equipment service & repair, rentals, consulting and upholstery services to help maximize the value of your dental practice.

Quality Dental Equipment offers a simple method to update your existing chairs and office without spending a fortune. Most of the dental re-upholstery is preformed in-house and in a timely fashion. We offer a variety of different color choices in vinyl to give your office a new modern look.

Our highly trained and qualified service technicians service the entire Detroit metropolitan area. We can travel to your office and provide expert repair and maintenance services onsite. By having most parts in stock, our technicians can have your office up and running with minimal downtime; there is no need to wait for ordered parts.

If you have some equipment you are looking to sell in the Metropolitan Detroit area, we offer competitive and fair prices for all sorts of used equipment including dental chairs, stools, x-ray units, x-ray processors, dental compressors, vacuums, autoclaves, handpieces, dental lights, ultrasonic scalers, instruments, laboratory equipment, curing lights, and more.
