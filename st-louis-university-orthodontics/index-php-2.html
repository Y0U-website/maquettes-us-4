Selection for admission to SLU is based upon a completed online application and a personal interview.

Students interested in enrolling at CADE must complete an online application through ADEA Postdoctoral Application Support Service (ADEA PASS). The deadlines for applications are: July 1 for endodontics, Aug. 1 for periodontics, Sept. 1 for orthodontics, and Oct. 1 for pediatric dentistry.

Successful completion of all of the Center for Advanced Dental Education’s graduate dental program requirements results in a Master of Science in Dentistry (Research) degree, and a specialty certificate upon completion of didactic and clinical requirements.

Our graduate orthodontic, pediatric dentistry and periodontic programs participate in the match program. All applicants to those programs must obtain a match code number from National Matching Services prior to the selection process.

In line with our mission, CADE strives to develop competent oral health care providers and ethical leaders who can effectively and ethically interact in a multicultural society and global economy. We believe that embracing diversity and inclusion is key to realizing our vision of leading in the improvement of health through advanced oral health.

To achieve this vision, we strongly encourage applicants from diverse backgrounds to join our community. Through such inclusivity, we aspire to model excellence in oral health education and practice, preparing our graduates to serve a diverse patient population effectively.

International Advanced Orthodontic Fellowship

The International Advanced Orthodontic Fellowship Program at Saint Louis University offers international orthodontists a unique opportunity to gain academic enrichment, clinical exposure, and research experience within a world-class institution. Through structured learning and mentorship, fellows will be equipped with advanced knowledge and skills to contribute to the global orthodontic community.
