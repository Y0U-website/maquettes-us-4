CADE Hours

Our clinic is open Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

All patients must have an appointment. Endodontic and periodontic patients must be referred by their general dentist.

Records Request

If you are a previous patient and need copies of your records, please fill out the Authorization for Disclosure Form .

Contact us at 314-977-8600, option 3 to request the records.
