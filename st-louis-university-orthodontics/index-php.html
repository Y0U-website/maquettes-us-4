Proven Expertise

At Dental Specialties of Saint Louis University, orthodontic, endodontic, periodontic, and pediatric dental care is provided by Saint Louis University residents who are dentists undergoing advanced specialty training in orthodontics. Residents are overseen and guided by experienced SLU faculty members.

Experienced Faculty

At Dental Specialties of Saint Louis University, residents work under the close supervision of world-renowned professors, ensuring the highest standard of patient care. SLU’s orthodontic faculty has over 850 years of cumulative orthodontic experience.

The clinic also has close ties to surgeons from Cardinal Glennon and other notable institutions who provide care for some of our most complex patient cases.

Accessible Pricing

We recognize and value the opportunity that working at our clinic offers our faculty and staff, and our pricing reflects this benefit. Services at Dental Specialties of Saint Louis University typically run about 30 to 50 % less than private practices, making our services more accessible without compromising quality.

Innovative Treatments

Our direct connection to SLU’s Center for Advanced Dental Education and collaboration with CADE faculty gives our clinic access to the latest dental technology, often before that technology is available to private practices.
