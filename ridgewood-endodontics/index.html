Ridgewood Endodontics provides high quality endodontic care in Ridgewood, NJ. By combining cutting-edge dental technology with years of experience and education, we make root canal treatment a more comfortable procedure for our patients. Our endodontic services include:

Root Canal Treatment – Root canal treatment is needed when the pulp inside a tooth is infected or inflamed. By removing the damaged pulp tissue, we can preserve your tooth and prevent the need for extraction and replacement. We use advanced endodontic techniques and local anesthesia to ensure your comfort and optimize treatment outcomes.

Endodontic Retreatment – Occasionally, a tooth that has had a root canal treatment in the past becomes damaged or reinfected. In many instances the previous root canals were not adequately treated. Endodontic retreatment gives us another chance to correct the situations.

Pulpotomy – Pulpotomy is an endodontic procedure most commonly used for children who have an infected primary tooth. Only the pulp from the crown is removed, saving the tooth from needing to be extracted and preventing orthodontic complications that result from losing a tooth too early.
