What moves the number.

  • How much bone is left. A jaw that has been missing teeth for years often needs rebuilding first. That is a separate procedure, and it is the single biggest variable in a full-arch case.
  • Whether teeth need to come out. Extractions on the same day as placement are common in full-arch work and are included in the plan you approve.
  • The material of the final teeth. Zirconia costs more than acrylic and lasts longer. Both are real options, and the difference is explained before you choose.
  • One arch or two. Rebuilding both jaws is not simply double: the planning overlaps, and the plan reflects that.
