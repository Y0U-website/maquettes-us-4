Everything we do in orthodontics

We keep this focused. We do one thing in orthodontics, and we do it as part of your whole smile and bite, with the same doctor from the first scan to the last aligner.

  • Invisalign clear aligners

Why straighten your teeth here.

  • One doctor, start to finish. The same person scans, plans, adjusts, and finishes your case, and carries it straight into veneers or an implant if that is the plan. No rotating providers, no handoffs.
  • A whole-bite view, not just front teeth. Dr. Slobodian does cosmetic and restorative work and plans how your bite functions as a system, so straightening sets up a healthy result, not just a nice photo.
  • Adult-focused and unhurried. This is a practice for adults, not a family ortho conveyor. We take the time to plan your case and explain it in plain language.
  • Care in your language. We speak English, Ukrainian, and Russian, so nothing about your plan or your cost gets lost in translation.
  • Honest about the right tool. If your case really calls for braces or a specialist orthodontist, we say so. We would rather send you to the right care than oversell an aligner.

How it works, from your first scan to your finished smile.

  • 1 A free consultation and a 3D scan. We take a digital scan of your teeth with a handheld wand, so there are no messy, gag-inducing impressions. It takes minutes.
  • 2 See your result before you commit. From that scan we can show you a preview of where your smile is headed, so you decide with your own eyes, not a promise.
  • 3 Your plan and a clear quote. Dr. Slobodian maps every movement and plans the finish around your bite. You leave knowing the cost, the timeline, and the monthly option, before you start.
  • 4 Your aligners arrive. You wear each set 20 to 22 hours a day and switch to the next every week or two. Most weeks you just live your life.
  • 5 Check-ins with the same doctor. We watch the movement, refine if needed, and keep you on track. No rotating providers.
  • 6 The finish, and keeping it. When your teeth are where they should be, we protect the result with a retainer, because teeth drift back without one. If restoration is next, the same doctor carries the plan straight into it.
