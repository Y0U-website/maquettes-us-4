Why bring a full-arch case here.

  • One doctor, the whole way. Dr. Slobodian plans the case, places the implants, and makes the final teeth himself. No handoff to a separate lab dentist, no rotating providers.
  • Bone rebuilt when others said no. With custom titanium mesh shaped to your jaw, we grow bone back and take on cases other offices turn away.
  • Named implants, not a no-name. We place Straumann and Neodent, two of the most documented systems in the world, so you choose by budget without dropping to an undocumented implant.
  • Planned before we begin. Every case starts with a CBCT scan and a 3D plan, then the implants go in through a surgical guide, backed by an oral surgery background.
  • An honest same-day timeline. You can leave with a fixed temporary arch the same day where it is clinically possible. The permanent teeth come once the implants have healed. We do not promise forever in a single visit.
  • Care in your language. We speak English, Ukrainian, and Russian, and take the time to explain the whole plan in plain words.
