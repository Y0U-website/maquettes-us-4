Dr. Sepideh Basti Dr. Sepideh Basti is a graduate of the University of Southern California, one of the best training institutions in the country. She has practiced cutting edge dentistry for decade and has completed several postgraduate courses in different fields of dentistry since receiving her DDS degree. She believes that creating a good dental experience is possible, which she promotes through her own belief in creating a peaceful, loving environment. Her goal is for the patient to experience a positive, joyful feeling during a visit or procedure.

Dr. Sepideh Basti has a wide range of experience in different areas of dentistry, from cosmetic and restorative dentistry to surgical treatments and root canal therapy. She takes a holistic approach to the dental care she provides her patients. Her emphasis on patient comfort as well as keeping up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry enable her to provide the highest quality of care in the field.
