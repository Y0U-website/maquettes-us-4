The practice

A dental crown is like a protective cap for a damaged or weakened tooth. It covers the entire visible portion of the tooth above the gumline. Our dentist will prepare the tooth, take impressions, and then place the custom-made crown.

A dental bridge replaces missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth (pontics) to adjacent natural teeth (abutments). Similar to crowns, bridges involve tooth preparation and impressions.

Our skilled team uses specialized instruments to gently remove tartar from your teeth and below the gumline. Next, we use a gentle abrasive paste to remove surface stains and create a smooth, shiny finish. Yes, even during a cleaning, we floss your teeth to ensure no debris is left behind. Finally, sometimes we’ll apply fluoride to strengthen your enamel and protect against cavities.

Removing plaque and tartar helps keep your breath minty fresh. Regular cleanings catch issues early, preventing more serious dental problems down the road.

Fluoride is a natural mineral found in water, soil, and certain foods. It has remarkable properties that benefit your teeth. It strengthens tooth enamel, making it more resistant to acid attacks from bacteria and sugars. Fluoride helps prevent cavities by remineralizing weakened enamel. If you have sensitive teeth, fluoride can reduce discomfort.

Both children and adults can benefit from fluoride treatments. Our dentist will apply a concentrated fluoride gel, foam, or varnish directly to your teeth. It’s a simple process that takes just a few minutes.

We believe that a healthy smile is a confident smile. Our polishing and stain removal services are designed to give your teeth that extra sparkle.

​Our skilled dental hygienists use a specialized polishing paste and a rotating rubber cup or brush. This gentle abrasion removes surface stains and polishes your teeth. ​For more stubborn stains, we perform scaling a thorough cleaning below the gumline removing tartar (hardened plaque) and deep stains. Les soins

Even with regular brushing and flossing, plaque can accumulate on your teeth. Over time, it hardens into tartar (calculus), which can’t be removed by home care alone.

Cleanings help prevent gum disease (gingivitis and periodontitis) by removing harmful bacteria and irritants from your gumline.
