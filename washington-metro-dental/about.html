Meet Dr. Alexis Corbitt Washington

Dr. Alexis Corbitt Washington, DDS, is one of Washington Metro Dental’s leading general dentists, with 15 years of experience helping patients achieve their best smiles. Dr. Washington’s journey began at North Carolina State University, where she earned a bachelor’s degree in biological sciences and a master’s degree in nutrition: food sciences before attending the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill to earn her dental degree.

Following dental school, Dr. Washington completed a general practice residency at the Philadelphia Veterans Affairs Medical Center. This comprehensive training further fueled her passion for patient care. During her six years at the DC Veterans Affairs Medical Center, she provided dental care to homeless veterans, exemplifying her commitment to serving the community.

As a lifelong learner and educational enthusiast, Dr. Washington is an active member of the DC Dental Society, the Greater Washington Academy of Dental Studies, and serves as the chairwoman of the Robert T. Freeman Dental Society. She stays up to date with the latest dental advancements to ensure her patients receive the most comfortable, high-quality care in a relaxed environment.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Washington is proud to capture the spirit of her roots in St. Croix, US Virgin Islands. Growing up surrounded by vibrant culture and breathtaking beaches inspired her love of snorkeling and scuba diving, hobbies she continues to explore around the world. When at home in DC, she enjoys spending time with her Cockapoo, Sheba, exploring local parks and dog-friendly wineries.

Meet Dr. Rebecca Salzman

Dr. Rebecca Salzman, DDS, brings her unique blend of art, science, and patient care to Washington Metro Dental. She has always been driven by her passion for the intersection of these fields and chose dentistry to marry her love of helping patients with the pursuit of aesthetic improvements. Dr. Salzman earned her bachelor’s degree in Biology from Claremont McKenna College before conducting malaria research at the National Institutes of Health.

She went on to earn her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland Dental School and completed a general practice residency at Christiana Care Health System in Wilmington, Delaware. Dr. Salzman practiced general dentistry in Charlotte, North Carolina, for five years before returning to Washington, DC, to join Washington Metro Dental.

Dr. Salzman stays at the cutting-edge of patient care through continuing education programs, including the Kois Center and the Greater Washington Academy of Dental Studies. Her dedication to excellence ensures that patients not only receive high-quality dental care, but also enjoy a welcoming and reassuring experience.

Meet Dr. Troy Vagianelis

Dr. Troy Vagianelis, originally from Albany, New York, brings vibrant energy and a forward-thinking approach to dental care at Washington Metro Dental. Graduating Summa Cum Laude from Boston University Dental School in 2020, he completed a residency at the Washington, DC VA Medical Center, where he developed a sensitivity and skill for working with patients experiencing dental anxiety.

Dr. Troy is a true enthusiast of digital dentistry, diving into cutting-edge technology to enhance precision and esthetic outcomes in treatments like crowns and implants. He is passionate about helping patients achieve confidence in their smiles while re-envisioning what dental care can be; a positive, health-enhancing experience.

Outside of the office, Dr. Troy is a dynamic part of the community. You can often find him playing kickball, pickleball, cooking for friends, attending trivia nights, or enjoying the gym. His commitment to creating connections parallels his dedication to his patients.

What to expect at your first visit

From the moment you step into our modern dental office, you’ll notice the attention to detail and patient-centered care that sets us apart. At Washington Metro Dental, we take the time to get to know you, listen to your concerns, and design a treatment plan that fits your specific needs and goals.

Patient Experience Your first visit includes more than just an exam; it’s a partnership. Expect a thorough consultation and time devoted to answering all your questions. With our advanced technology and modern amenities, each step is designed to ensure your comfort and confidence.

State-of-the-art amenities and technology

Washington Metro Dental is equipped with state-of-the-art technology and a variety of comfort-focused amenities, ensuring you enjoy a seamless and relaxing visit. Our approach combines modern innovation with patient-centered care to provide efficient, precise, and comfortable treatments.
