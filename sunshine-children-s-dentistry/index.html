The practice

Together with our amazing Sunshine Team, our dentists ensure that each child receives quality care for their developing smile. Our dentists and Sunshine Team are adored by our patient families, and we love making children SMILE.

We are a well-trained children s dental practice that provides excellent and efficient service in a creative and fun environment. Our referrals come from happy existing patients, county health departments, general dentists, and other dental specialists.

Our goal is for every child to have a positive dental experience that will put a BRIGHT SMILE on their face!
