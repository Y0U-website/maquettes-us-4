How Do Modern Dental Implants Work? Dental implants are medical-grade titanium or ceramic fixtures surgically placed into the jawbone, where they naturally integrate to replace missing tooth roots. Topped with custom zirconia crowns, bridges, or full-arch hybrid prostheses milled in our on-site Roland CAD/CAM lab , they restore up to 90 % of natural chewing power without altering adjacent healthy teeth.

Compare Your Dental Implant Options

Whether you need to replace a single damaged tooth or an entire dental arch, we match the surgical plan to your bone anatomy, lifestyle, and goals.

Locator Fixed Dentures (Snap-On)

Struggling with loose, unstable dentures but not ready for the investment of All-on-4? Our custom Snap-On Overdentures lock mechanically onto implant studs, giving you immediate stability, open-palate comfort, and 60-70 % chewing force—at a fraction of the cost.

1. Titanium Implant Post A biocompatible titanium post placed surgically in the jawbone. It integrates with your bone structure, serving as a strong root anchor and helping preserve bone structure.

2. Custom Abutment The precision-fit connecting stud attached to the implant post. It acts as the secure base designed to support your final prosthetic crown, bridge, or overdenture.

3. Visible Restoration The visible tooth restoration (crown, bridge, or overdenture) custom-shaded and crafted in our laboratory to match your natural smile and bite alignment.
