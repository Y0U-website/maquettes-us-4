GUIDED Biofilm Therapy (GBT)

At Serene Oaks Dental , we combine personalized preventive care with the latest technology, including Guided Biofilm Therapy (GBT) , a modern, minimally invasive approach that gently removes harmful biofilm that causes cavities, gum disease, and inflammation. The result is a more precise, more comfortable cleaning that is safe for natural teeth, veneers, crowns, and dental implants.
