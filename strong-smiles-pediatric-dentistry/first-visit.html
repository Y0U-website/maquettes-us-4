Your First Dental Visit

  • A Quick and Gentle Exam: Dr. Sarah will take a close look at your child’s teeth and gums to make sure everything is growing as it should.
  • Cleaning: If it’s appropriate, we’ll clean your child’s teeth to help them stay healthy and fresh. Don’t worry, it’s gentle and kid-friendly!
  • Growth and Development Talk: As your child grows, so does their mouth! We’ll talk about what’s to come and how to care for their teeth along the way.
  • Personalized Advice: We’ll give you tips on how to keep your child’s teeth clean at home, plus advice on eating healthy and avoiding cavities.

How to Prepare for the First Visit?

Here are a few simple tips to make the visit go smoothly:

  • Talk Positively About the Dentist: Let your child know how excited you are about their appointment. Keep things light and fun!
  • Bring Comfort Items: A favorite toy or blanket can help your child feel secure.
  • Arrive Early: Give yourself extra time to get settled and make your child feel comfortable in the new setting.

Working Together for Your Child’s Healthy Smile!

At Strong Smiles Pediatric Dentistry, we believe that parents are key to helping children maintain healthy teeth. Dr. Sarah will work closely with you, offering advice on brushing, flossing, and how to make dental care a fun part of your child’s daily routine. Together, we can keep your child’s smile bright and healthy for years to come!
