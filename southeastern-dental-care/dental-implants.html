The Dental Implant Procedure

The process of getting dental implants is a straightforward, carefully planned, and comfortable experience. Here’s what you can expect:

  • Consultation Dr. Bui will begin with a consultation to assess your oral health and discuss your tooth replacement options. Together, you’ll decide if dental implants are the right solution for you.
  • Treatment Planning Using advanced 3D imaging technology, Dr. Bui will evaluate your bone structure and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
  • Implant Placement During a minor surgical procedure, the dental implant is placed into the jawbone. Temporary restorations may be provided while the implant integrates with the bone.
  • Healing and Osseointegration Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with the surrounding bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for the final restoration.
  • Final Restoration Once the implant has fully integrated, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture will be attached to the implant, completing your smile.

FAQs About Dental Implants

Dental implants can last 20-30 years or even a lifetime with proper care and maintenance. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement every 10-15 years.

Most healthy adults with sufficient jawbone density are good candidates for implants. Your dentist will evaluate your overall health, bone structure, and gum condition to determine suitability.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you shouldn’t feel pain during surgery. Most patients report mild to moderate discomfort for a few days afterward, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

The complete process typically takes 3-6 months, allowing time for the implant to integrate with your jawbone (osseointegration). The actual surgical placement usually takes 1-2 hours per implant.

Care for implants like natural teeth with regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. Good oral hygiene is crucial to prevent peri-implantitis, an infection that can cause implant failure.
