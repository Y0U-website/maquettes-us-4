Benefits of Clear Aligners

The clear, nearly invisible aligners let you straighten your teeth without noticeable brackets and wires. This is perfect for professionals, students, or anyone who wants to maintain their confidence during treatment.

SureSmile and CandidPro aligners are smooth and comfortable, with no sharp edges to irritate your mouth. Our patients often forget they’re even wearing them!

You can easily remove your aligners to eat, drink, and brush your teeth, allowing for better hygiene and fewer dietary restrictions. No more worrying about food getting stuck in braces.

With advanced 3D imaging technology available at our state-of-the-art Lakeville dental office, your treatment plan is customized to move your teeth efficiently and predictably.

The Clear Aligner Process

Straightening your teeth with SureSmile or CandidPro clear aligners (similar to well-known Invisalign) is a straightforward and personalized experience. Here’s what you can expect from the process at Southeastern Dental Care:

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a consultation where Dr. Bui will evaluate your smile and discuss your treatment goals to determine if clear aligners are the right choice for you. We welcome patients of all ages and offer flexible scheduling to accommodate your busy lifestyle.

Custom Treatment Plan

Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we create a precise digital model of your teeth to map out your treatment and design custom aligners. This cutting-edge technology ensures the most accurate and efficient treatment possible.

You’ll receive a series of custom-made aligners. Each aligner will make slight adjustments to your teeth, guiding them into place over time. The process is gradual and comfortable.

FAQs About Clear Aligners

Treatment time varies depending on individual cases, but most patients see results in 12 to 18 months. Dr. Bui will provide a more accurate timeline during your consultation based on your specific needs.

Clear aligners are designed to be as comfortable as possible. While you may feel slight pressure as your teeth shift, this is temporary and much less intense than the discomfort of traditional braces. Many patients find the treatment surprisingly comfortable.

You should remove your aligners when eating or drinking anything other than water to prevent staining and to avoid food particles getting trapped in your aligners. This also means you can continue enjoying all your favorite foods!

For the best results, wear your SureSmile or CandidPro aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. Consistency is key to achieving your desired results on schedule.

After completing your treatment, you may need to wear a retainer to maintain your new smile. Dr. Bui will recommend the best retainer option for you to ensure long-lasting results.

Many insurance plans provide coverage for orthodontic treatment, including clear aligners. Our team will work with your insurance company to maximize your benefits and file all claims on your behalf.
