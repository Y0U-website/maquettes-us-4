Accessibility Statement

Southeastern Dental Care is committed to providing an inclusive and accessible website experience for every visitor.

Our Commitment

We work to make this website usable by people with a wide range of abilities, technologies, and browsing preferences. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

Current Status

The most recent accessibility audit was completed on August 25, 2026. The review included automated testing, keyboard navigation, screen-reader-oriented structure, form labeling, color contrast, and content reflow at a 320-pixel viewport.

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We will continue to review the website as content, standards, and assistive technologies change.

Known Limitations

The contact page includes a third-party Google Maps embed whose internal accessibility behavior is controlled by Google. The same office address, phone number, hours, parking information, and an external directions link are also provided as standard website text.

Report an Accessibility Barrier

If you have difficulty using this website or need information in another format, please contact our office. Tell us the page or feature involved and the assistive technology or browser you were using, when possible.
