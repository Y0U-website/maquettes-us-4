Our Team

Dr. Dierks

Dr. Dierks is a native of Lincoln, Nebraska. He attended Nebraska Wesleyan University for his undergraduate degree in biological sciences. Following, he attended the University of Nebraska Medical Center and obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, and graduated in 2022 before joining Dr. Doty in Crete!

With many family members in medicine, Dr. Dierks had the same desire to choose a career that would enable him to care for the health of others. Currently, he is a member of the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE), American Dental Association (ADA), and Nebraska Dental Association (NDA). In addition to your usual dental care, Dr. Dierks is trained in Invisalign and Botox, dermal fillers, and TMJ/orofacial pain trigger point therapy.

The most important thing to know about Dr. Dierks is his main desire to provide the utmost quality and up-to-date dental care to his patients while also maintaining a comfortable and enjoyable experience at the office. What he has found to be the greatest perk of working here in Crete is getting to know all the patients and being able to keep up with their dental and personal journeys at each visit.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Dierks is big Husker football fan and has been since before he knew what it was. He enjoys fishing, being out on the lake, running, and exercising. However, he finds his most favorable time spent with his fiancé, family, and friends.

Laura

Raised in Wilber, Nebraska, and being there for most of her life, Laura has developed a true admiration for her hometown and the surrounding area. After becoming a dental assistant, she worked at an orthodontist’s office in Lincoln, followed by working at a dental lab in Wilber. Through these two experiences, she fell in love with being able to play a large part in bringing new and improved smiles to many.

In 2014, Laura began working for Dr. Doty as his office manager while balancing assisting duties. During her time, she has gained great knowledge and wisdom about providing the right and the highest level of care while pushing her passion for what she does to the maximum. While Laura is a keystone in keeping a great flow and upbeat energy day after day, it’s evident that what means the most to her is patients and their comfort and belonging at the office.

Outside of caring for patients and helping to keep the office functioning smoothly, Laura often goes a mile a minute in getting her 3 children to all their sports, dance, 4H, and school events. She has a strong passion for raising and showing horses and volunteering as a 4H leader. She also manages to make time for either running or some type of exercise nearly every early morning. When she does get free time, she loves spending time with her family and friends, as it is clear her biggest passion is people and relationships.
