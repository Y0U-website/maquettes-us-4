About Us

Michael E. Simony, D.D.S.

Michael E. Simony, D.D.S., was the first dental student to ever receive an honorary Concurrent Resolution by the Michigan House of Representatives and Senate in honor of his research and lecture presentation to health professionals across the state. His award-winning presentation topic was on the oral manifestations of AIDS.

Upon graduation from the University of Detroit Dental School in 1992, he was invited to return as a prosthodontic instructor for the school. Dr. Simony completed an internship at the Peter Dawson Center for Advanced Dental Study in Florida in 1993. In 2001, Dr. Simony completed the F.O.R.C.E. International Orthodontic internship in Birmingham, Michigan, as well as the achievement of Invisalign Certification.

Keeping on the forefront of technology and developments is extremely important to Dr. Simony. He is very passionate and has high standards. He pays the utmost attention to every detail, so you can be given the best quality of treatment available today.

Dr. Marco C. Simony D.M.D

Dr. Marco performs multiple facets of dentistry, including implantology, orthodontics, endodontics, prosthodontics, as well as cosmetic dentistry, having studied the methods under pioneers like Dr. Larry Rosenthal. He finds satisfaction not only in befriending patients but also in ensuring patient education and understanding are at the forefront of every appointment. He appreciates the fact that everyone should have a place to call a dental home, where they feel not only comfortable, but confident.
