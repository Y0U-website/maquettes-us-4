About

A family laboratory built on precision, trust, and nearly four decades of experience.

We are a family-owned, full-service dental laboratory with over 38 years of hands-on experience in removable prosthetics. For nearly four decades, we&#x27;ve partnered closely with dental professionals to deliver products that balance function, comfort, and natural aesthetics.

Our Mission

To provide dependable, high-quality prosthetics that doctors can trust and patients can rely on — supporting successful outcomes through precision, consistency, and experience.

Our Origin

Supreme Dental Lab began with our founder Jaime, who earned his Certified Dental Technician credentials with the United States Navy in 1988. That military discipline still shapes every case we deliver — meticulous, consistent, and on time.

Jaime

Jaime became a Certified Dental Technician in 1988, earning his certificate with the United States Navy. With over 40 years of experience, he specializes in over-dentures and screw-retained prostheses. He has shared his knowledge with other technicians and lab managers through volunteer-based live implant training courses, teaching them how to fabricate and convert screw-retained dentures. His passion drives him to never stop learning or evolving.

Gina

Gina keeps our dental lab running efficiently while overseeing all business operations. With over 30 years in the dental field, she works side by side with her husband Jaime and their oldest son Chance — and she&#x27;s the friendly voice you&#x27;ll hear on the phone most days.

Chance

Chance followed in his father&#x27;s footsteps, raised around the lab his whole life. His passion grew during his early days at the Central Arizona Service Shelter, where he participated in surgical implant training courses, helping disabled veterans and homeless patients transform their lives one smile at a time. He brings 10 years of laboratory experience and works alongside his father every day.
