What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial posts placed into the jawbone to support a replacement tooth. They act as artificial tooth roots and serve as a base for a crown, bridge, or denture.

If you are missing one or more teeth, implants may help improve chewing comfort, support your smile, and reduce the need to rely on nearby teeth for support.

When Are Dental Implants Needed?

Dental implants may be a good choice if:

Your dentures feel loose or uncomfortable

You want a long-term tooth-replacement option

You want to help prevent nearby teeth from shifting

You have enough healthy jawbone to support the implant

FAQs

How long do dental implants last?

With good care, dental implants can last for many years. Their lifespan depends on your oral hygiene, bone health, bite, and regular dental visits.

Is the dental implant process painful?

The area is numbed during treatment to help keep you comfortable. Some soreness afterward is normal and can usually be managed with aftercare instructions.

How do I care for dental implants?

Brush, floss, and visit your dentist regularly for checkups. Good home care helps protect the implant, gums, and surrounding teeth.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

People with missing teeth, healthy gums, and enough jawbone may be good candidates. A dental exam can help confirm whether implants are right for you.
