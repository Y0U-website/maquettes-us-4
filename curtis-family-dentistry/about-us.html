During their 30 years in practice, Dr. Nizi Gupta has seen patients of all ages, incomes and concerns. Her conscientious attention to detail and thorough technique make her a professional you can trust for the most long-lasting, comfortable, beautiful care available.

Dr. Nizi Gupta has been working in dentistry for 18 years and counting. She attended college in India and graduated as a trained Dental Surgeon in 2000. After coming to the United States, she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from NYU College of Dentistry. Since 2006, she has been practicing here in Connecticut.

Dr. Gupta enjoys practicing cosmetic and restorative dentistry and has special expertise in performing oral surgery like one-visit root canals. Dr. Gupta’s skills cover many aspects of dentistry, including: cosmetic dentistry, ZOOM teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, pediatric dentistry, root canals, dentures, dental implants, oral surgery, crowns, and bridges. This experience enables her to take good care of her patients by providing gentle services to those who are anxious, making their dentist visit genuinely comfortable.

Continuing education in advanced dentistry is an important part of Dr. Gupta’s ongoing care approach. She and her team regularly attend training and courses in the most current dental techniques. For instance, Dr. Gupta completed her implant residency with Osstem Implants in 2009 and an additional mini Implant residency with 3i implants. She is a current member of the American Dental Association and Connecticut Dental Association. She loves maintaining an up-to-date office and being able to offer her patients the best options in dentistry!

Outside of work, Dr. Gupta loves traveling. Her trips have given her the opportunity to visit places like India, the United Kingdom, France, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Egypt, and more. She loves to interact with people, learn about different cultures, and try different cuisines. She also enjoys musical concerts and theatre. When she is not at the office, she enjoys watching cooking shows,experimenting them and spends time with family.
