Meet Your Dentist, Dr. Mellor.

Dr. Mellor was raised in Colfax, Washington, and is excited to be home and serve the community he loves. His wife, Lindsay, manages our clinic.

About Dr. Mellor & Lindsay Mellor

Dr. Mellor was raised in Colfax, WA. He went to Southern Virginia University for his undergraduate degree where he played football and graduated with a degree in business management. After SVU, he attended the University of Washington Dental School on a military scholarship.

Upon graduation, he started his military payback at Camp Humphreys in South Korea. After he and his family enjoyed two years there, they moved to Maryland where he was stationed at Walter Reed Hospital for another two years. He then decided it was time to come back to his hometown and settle down with his wife, Lindsay, and four kids. He took over the clinic from Dr. Allen Kirkpatrick in July of 2019. He is excited to be here serving the community he loves.

About Dr. Ellis and Katelyn Ellis

Upon graduation, Katelyn and Brady moved back to the area to serve and be a part of the community they grew up in. They continue to enjoy attending sporting events in the community and are always looking for a new adventure. Katelyn and Dr. Ellis will be welcoming their first newborn son in January of 2023.

They are looking forward to being parents and to be in the community that helped to raise them. They look forward to raising their son in the same county they were fortunate to grow up in.

She enjoys visiting friends and family and enjoys assisting in coaching on the St. John Volleyball high school team.

Meet Our Team

RINA -Registered Dental Hygienist

Rina began her dental career as a dental assistant from June 2006- August 2007. She then attended the dental hygiene program at Apollo College where she finished in 2009. From 2009- June 2019 she worked as a hygienist at Bearable in Moscow, ID. Since she is from Colfax and her children have gotten old enough to start school, she wanted to work closer to home and we couldn’t be more grateful have her on our team. She really enjoys her job. She is originally from Weiser, ID, but has lived in Colfax since 2002.

STACEY - Dental Assistant

Stacey has been working as a dental assistant for 26 years, and has worked at this clinic for the past 4. She moved to Colfax in 4th grade and has called it home since. Before working as a dental assistant, she worked at WHMC for 4 years in the medical records and surgery departments. Stacey is married to her husband Mike and has two grown daughters named Tasia and Lexi. When she isn’t working, she enjoys painting, decorating, flower gardening, and playing with her grandkids Greyson and Violet.

JENNIFER - Dental assistant, Reception/Billing

Jennifer is very well known in the community. She was raised in Colfax where she graduated from high school. MANDY - Registered Dental Hygienist
