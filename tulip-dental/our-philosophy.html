(406) 443-3399 1221 Helena Avenue, Helena, Montana 59601 Schedule a New Patient Exam Our Philosophy Welcome to Tulip Dental At Tulip Dental, we believe exceptional dentistry is about more than healthy teeth—it’s about improving your overall well-being. Dr. Patricia Schwartz, DDS, MAGD, and our team provide personalized, compassionate care with a focus on the connection between oral health and whole-body wellness.

As a Master of the Academy of General Dentistry (MAGD), Dr. Schwartz is committed to lifelong learning, bringing advanced education and the latest evidence-based techniques to every patient she treats. Her comprehensive approach allows her to provide high-quality care while helping patients make informed decisions about their long-term oral health.

Our whole body approach goes beyond treating individual teeth. We emphasize prevention, nutrition, periodontal health, and preserving your natural smile whenever possible. By considering how oral health influences the rest of the body, we strive to support healthier gums, stronger teeth, and better overall wellness.

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll find a welcoming, comfortable environment where your goals, concerns, and health are our priority. We are proud to care for patients throughout Helena and across Montana, combining advanced clinical expertise with a personalized approach that helps every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Dr. Patricia Schwartz is a highly experienced dentist with a passion for providing exceptional dental care. With a career spanning over two decades, she has been committed to helping patients achieve and maintain healthy, beautiful smiles.

Dr. Schwartz’s main focus is on saving teeth, and this dedication sets her apart as a compassionate and skilled dentist. She holds the prestigious title of Master in the Academy of General Dentistry (MAGD), showcasing her commitment to continued education and excellence in the field. Her undergraduate studies took her to Boulder, Colorado, where she developed a love for the mountains and skiing. It was this passion that eventually brought her back to the Rockies.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Schwartz enjoys indulging in outdoor activities like hiking and skiing. She appreciates exploring local trails and embracing the beauty of nature. With a zest for travel, she is always seeking new adventures and experiences.

At Tulip Dental, Dr. Patricia Schwartz specializes in both general and cosmetic dentistry. Whether you require routine dental care or desire smile-enhancing treatments, you can trust Dr. Schwartz’s expertise and gentle approach to provide the highest quality dental services.
