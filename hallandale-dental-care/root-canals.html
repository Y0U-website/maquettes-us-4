Root Canals

Reviews Questions Patients Ask Us What is a root canal treatment? A root canal is a dental procedure used to save a badly damaged or infected tooth by removing the inflamed inner pulp tissue, cleaning the canal, and sealing it to prevent further infection.

Common signs include severe tooth pain while chewing, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold temperatures, tender or swollen gums, tooth discoloration, or a pimple-like bump on your gums.

No, modern root canal therapy is not painful. We use effective local anesthesia to completely numb the area, making the procedure as comfortable and stress-free as receiving a standard routine filling.

Saving your natural tooth helps preserve your natural bite structure, prevents surrounding teeth from shifting out of place, and avoids the need for more complex replacements like bridges or dental implants.

A root canal procedure typically takes between 60 to 90 minutes. Depending on the complexity of the tooth and infection, it may be completed in one or two visits.

Yes, most teeth that undergo a root canal require a custom dental crown to restore their structural strength, protect them from fracturing, and return full chewing function.

Recovery is generally fast. You may feel mild tenderness for a few days after the procedure, which can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most patients return to normal activities the next day.

No, once the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or necrotic, it cannot heal on its own. Without treatment, the infection will worsen and eventually require full tooth extraction.

With proper oral hygiene, routine dental cleanings, and a protective dental crown, a tooth treated with a root canal can last a lifetime.

Avoid chewing on hard foods with the treated tooth until your permanent crown is placed. After that, practice regular oral hygiene by brushing twice daily, flossing daily, and attending your routine dental exams at Hallandale Dental Care.
