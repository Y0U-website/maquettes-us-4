Dental Implants

Reviews Questions Patients Ask Us What is a dental implant? A dental implant is a small, biocompatible titanium post surgically inserted into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. It supports a custom abutment and prosthetic crown to look and function like a natural tooth.

When a natural tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it degrades over time. Dental implants act like natural roots, transmitting chewing forces into the bone to stimulate growth and prevent jawbone resorption.

All-on-4 is an advanced full-arch restoration technique where a full set of fixed, permanent replacement teeth is supported by just four strategically placed implants in the upper or lower jaw.

24-Hour or Same-Day Implants allow qualified candidates to have temporary, fully functional teeth attached to their implants during the same appointment as their implant surgery, eliminating waiting time with missing teeth.

Most adults with good overall health, healthy gums, and adequate jawbone density are excellent candidates. If bone density is low, bone grafting can often be performed to build a solid foundation.

The timeline typically ranges from 3 to 6 months. This allows time for the implant to securely fuse with your jawbone before the permanent custom crown or bridge is attached.

Unlike removable dentures, implants are permanently fixed into your bone, never slip or require adhesives, preserve jawbone health, and restore full original biting force.

With proper oral hygiene, regular flossing, and routine check-ups at Hallandale Dental Care, the titanium implant post can last a lifetime, while the restoration crown typically lasts 15 to 25 years.

Care for implants just like natural teeth: brush twice daily, floss daily around the implant post, avoid smoking, and visit Hallandale Dental Care regularly for professional cleanings and check-ups.
