What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant in Las Vegas is a tiny titanium post that is an artificial tooth. It&#x27;s surgically placed into the jawbone, fusing naturally through osseointegration. Once healed, the implant supports a custom crown, dental bridge, or denture that looks and feels like your natural teeth. Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants provide unmatched stability and strength. They don&#x27;t rely on surrounding teeth for support and help preserve your jawbone&#x27;s structure, preventing the loss that often occurs after tooth loss.
