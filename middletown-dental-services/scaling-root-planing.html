Scaling and Root Planing

Our experienced dental professionals use the latest techniques and technologies to provide effective and gentle periodontal care. We are committed to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy smile through personalized treatment plans and comprehensive care.

If you are experiencing symptoms of gum disease or need a thorough cleaning, contact Middletown Dental Services today to schedule your scaling and root planing appointment. Let us help you achieve healthier gums and a brighter smile.

  • Root Planing: After scaling, the root planing process smooths the root surfaces of the teeth. This helps the gums reattach to the teeth and reduces pockets where bacteria can accumulate, promoting healing and preventing further infection.
  • Prevents Progression of Gum Disease: By removing plaque and tartar buildup, scaling and root planing help prevent gingivitis from advancing to more severe periodontitis.
  • Reduces Gum Inflammation: These treatments can significantly reduce gum inflammation and bleeding, leading to healthier gums.
  • Promotes Healing: Smoothing the root surfaces helps the gums reattach to the teeth, reducing pocket depth and promoting healing.
  • Improves Overall Oral Health: By maintaining healthy gums, you can protect your teeth and support structures, ensuring long-term oral health.
