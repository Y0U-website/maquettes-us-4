Meet Dr. Lamons Smile Solutions

Dentistry is a constantly evolving field. Revolutionary technologies and treatments are always being developed. These changes mean a better care experience and stronger, longer-lasting results for patients. It’s my responsibility, as a dentist, to be current on the developments.

Each year I complete many hours of continuing dental education. I’ve studied communication management, sedation dentistry, care for sleep apnea, cosmetic enhancements, and laser treatment, to name but a few topics.

Personal Life

When not caring for my patients, my priority is time spent with my wife, Paula, and my family. My son, Ben, is married to Kristina; my daughter Morgan is married to Matt. Morgan and Matt have two children: Samuel and Levi. My daughter Katie is also a dentist here at Smile Solutions.

I enjoy spending time on the golf links, playing tennis, and being on the lake. I’ve been a Kiwanis member for almost 30 years, and I’m a lifelong member of Asbury Church, too.
