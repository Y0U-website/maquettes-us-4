The practice

Penn’s Commons Dental Group has been providing care for patients of all ages throughout greater Reading/Berks and surrounding counties.

We meet the dental needs of your entire family beginning with preventive infant care and transitioning through all stages of your life.

We boast a friendly, experienced staff. Our doctors and staff offer more than four decades of experience.

We always want our patients to feel informed and confident they are getting the best treatment possible. You can look forward to being treated as a friend every time you return.

Established in 1981 by Dr. James G. Schmoyer and his dental school classmate, Dr. Samuel D. Greenlee with a vision of investing in meaningful patient relationships. The doctors, along with a dedicated staff strive to provide mutually agreed upon treatment tailored to the individual patient.

We are centrally located in Berks County with convenient access to public transportation. Our office building is handicapped accessible and offers two well-light parking lots for your convenience.

We are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience and applying the relevant accessibility standards to our website.

We aim to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines ( WCAG 2.1, Level AA ) where feasible. Please be aware that our efforts are ongoing as we work to implement relevant improvements over time.
