A highly qualified and respected orthodontist, specializing in ‘invisible’ treatments for adults and the best quality care in pediatric orthodontics — with more than 20 years among New York’s most accomplished practitioners.

Dr. Tanya Vaysman earned her Bachelor’s degree in Biology from Fairleigh Dickinson University, graduating Magna Cum Laude, and furthered her education at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. She has received multiple honors, including the Academy of Dental Materials Award and the American Association of Women Dentists Colgate-Palmolive Research Award.

She has spent more than 20 years in private practice on the Upper East Side of Manhattan, treating thousands of adolescents and adults. Now, she is returning to Bergen County, where she grew up. She currently resides with her husband and two children in Tenafly and enjoys being part of and contributing to the Bergen County community.
