Parks, nature, and recreation

For patients who value a healthy, active lifestyle, Lyndhurst offers convenient access to green spaces, trails, and sports facilities.

These parks—many tucked along Brainard Road and surrounding residential streets—provide a peaceful balance to Lyndhurst’s shopping corridors.

  • Acacia Reservation – A former golf course transformed by Cleveland Metroparks into over 150 acres of meadows, woodlands, and paved trails at the corner of Cedar and Richmond.

Serving Lyndhurst, OH And Surrounding Areas

Lyndhurst is a city in Cuyahoga County, Ohio, United States, and an eastern suburb of Cleveland. The population was 14,050 at the 2020 census. A small part of Lyndhurst was originally part of Mayfield Township. It is primarily bordered by the cities of South Euclid to the West, Mayfield Heights to the East, Beachwood to the South, and Richmond Heights to the North/Notheast. These neighboring communities make up the surrounding, dense suburban area on the east side of Greater Cleveland.
