Comprehensive Implant Restoration Services

Dental implants are versatile, and so are our restoration options. Depending on the number of teeth missing and your specific oral health needs, we offer several solutions to complete your smile.

Single Tooth Replacement (Implant Crowns)

If you are missing a single tooth, an implant-supported crown is the gold standard for replacement. Unlike a traditional bridge, which requires grinding down adjacent healthy teeth, an implant crown stands alone. We custom-design your crown to blend perfectly with your surrounding teeth, providing a durable and natural-looking solution that restores full chewing function.

Multiple Tooth Replacement (Implant Bridges)

For patients missing several consecutive teeth, an implant-supported bridge offers superior stability compared to traditional removable partials. By anchoring the bridge to dental implants rather than natural teeth, we preserve your remaining tooth structure and prevent the bone loss that often accompanies missing teeth. This option provides a secure, non-removable solution that feels incredibly natural.

Full Arch Replacement (Implant-Retained Dentures)

Struggling with loose, uncomfortable dentures can be a thing of the past. Implant-retained dentures offer a life-changing improvement in stability and comfort. By snapping your dentures onto strategically placed implants, we eliminate slipping and sliding, allowing you to eat your favorite foods—like a crisp apple or steak—with renewed confidence. This is a popular option for many of our older patients in the Lyndhurst area who want to maintain an active, social lifestyle without worrying about their teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant Restoration

1. What is the difference between an implant placement and an implant restoration? The dental implant process has two main phases. The placement involves surgically inserting the titanium post (the implant) into the jawbone, which acts as the new tooth root. The restoration is the second phase, which happens after the implant has healed. This involves attaching the visible tooth—a crown, bridge, or denture—to the implant. At Silver Family Dental, we specialize in this critical restoration phase, crafting the beautiful, functional teeth you see when you smile.

2. How long do dental implant restorations last? With proper care and maintenance, the dental implant post itself can last a lifetime. The restoration (crown, bridge, or denture), however, is subject to normal wear and tear, similar to natural teeth. Typically, a high-quality implant restoration can last 10 to 15 years or longer before needing replacement or repair. Regular check-ups at our Lyndhurst office are essential for extending the life of your restoration.

3. Is the restoration process painful? No, the restoration phase is generally very comfortable and typically requires no anesthesia. Once the implant has fully integrated with your jawbone, we simply take impressions (molds) of the area to design your new tooth. When the custom restoration is ready, we attach it to the implant. Patients might feel slight pressure during the fitting, but it is not a painful procedure.

4. How do I care for my new implant restoration? You should care for your implant restoration just like your natural teeth. This means brushing twice a day, flossing daily (using special floss for bridges or implants if recommended), and visiting your implant restoration dentist in Lyndhurst for routine cleanings and exams. Good oral hygiene prevents gum inflammation around the implant, which is crucial for long-term success.

5. Can you replace an old crown on an existing implant? Absolutely. If you have an existing implant crown that has become chipped, discolored, or worn down over time, we can replace it. We will evaluate the stability of the underlying implant and the health of the surrounding gum tissue before crafting a brand-new, custom-matched restoration to rejuvenate your smile.
