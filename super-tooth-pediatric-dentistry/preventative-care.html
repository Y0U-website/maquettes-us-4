What To Expect At Your Child’s Preventative Dental Care Appointment

  • Thorough Cleaning To begin, Dr. Ivker or a dental hygienist will perform a quick oral exam and begin cleaning their teeth. Their teeth will be scaled to remove plaque and tartar, then brushed with a high-powered brush, and flossed to remove any remaining food particles and debris.
  • Necessary X-rays X-rays are not required at every dental appointment and are usually taken once a year to ensure your child’s oral development is on track. X-rays may be required if Dr. Ivker suspects cavities or an infection. If Dr. Ivker determines that x-rays are needed, our team will take them with your permission.
  • Comprehensive Exam Dr. Ivker will use special dental tools to check for cavities, gum disease, and other signs of potential oral health issues. She will also view your child’s x-rays and health history to aid in diagnosis. Then, she will discuss with you any problems or potential issues they may have found during the examination.
  • Treatment Plan If your child has dental issues that need to be addressed, such as cavities, gum disease, or an infected tooth, Dr. Ivker will develop a treatment plan and discuss it with you. Once both you and your child understand the issues and how we can fix them, we can schedule your child’s follow up appointment in East Norriton for treatment.
