Why Choose Implant Restorations?

The main benefit dental implants offer is that they function just like natural tooth roots, which prevents bone loss and preserves the jawbone. Implants also maintain the space left by missing teeth, which prevents any remaining teeth from shifting out of alignment.

Implant restorations allow you to speak with confidence and to chew your favorite foods with ease. They also help maintain the shape of your face, leaving you with a youthful smile. Best of all, implant surgery is one of the safest procedures in dentistry with more than a 95 percent success rate. With proper care, your dental implant restorations can last a lifetime.

What Are the Common Types of Implant Restorations?

The following are some of the restorative solutions we offer:

Dental Crowns : Dental crowns placed on implants can restore individual missing teeth. They are made to look just like your natural teeth.

Dental Bridges : Implant-supported bridges work to restore your smile if you are missing two or more teeth in the same area. Rather than attaching these durable restorations to adjacent teeth, they sit securely on dental implants.

Implant-supported Dentures : These restorations offer a durable and effective solution for replacing some or all of your missing teeth. Your implant-supported dentures securely snap to implants, so you won’t have to worry about them shifting out of place and will not have to deal with messy denture adhesives.
