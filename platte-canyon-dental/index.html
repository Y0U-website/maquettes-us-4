Welcome to Platte Canyon Dental, the dental practice of Dr. Christina Linn . We are delighted to provide all the quality dentistry that families in Littleton, CO need to maintain excellent oral health.

Seeking dental treatment can be a challenge for many people, and we understand that. At Platte Canyon Dental, we provide a warm and supportive environment with first-rate patient care to families in Littleton, Columbine Valley, Lakewood, Centennial, and surrounding communities.

While Active Duty in the US Navy, Dr. Linn served as a Naval Dental Officer, where she honed her skills in the entire scope of general dentistry. Today, she remains in the Active US Navy Reserves with a commission as a Dental Officer that trains with a full medical detachment once a month.

The field of dentistry is constantly evolving, and Dr. Linn is committed to bringing her patients the latest and best treatments, materials, and technology. Dr. Linn invests numerous hours in continuing education every year. She is also involved in a study club where local dentists and specialists, who share her passion for dentistry, gather once a month to collaborate and share insights.
