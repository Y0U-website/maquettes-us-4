Camila Diaz, DMD

Dr. Camila Diaz is a general dentist who believes dental visits should feel comfortable, personal, and judgment-free. She earned her dental degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, where she received extensive clinical training, and went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center.

Dr Diaz is committed to providing comprehensive, evidence-based dental care and is dedicated to lifelong learning through advanced dental education and training.

Outside the office, you’ll find her staying active, reading, and soaking up the South Florida sunshine.
