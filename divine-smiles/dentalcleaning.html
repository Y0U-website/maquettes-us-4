Dental Cleaning

We understand the importance of maintaining excellent oral hygiene for a healthy and radiant smile. Our professional dental cleanings are designed to remove plaque, tartar, and stains, ensuring your teeth and gums stay strong and disease-free.

  • Thorough Cleaning Process: Each visit begins with an oral examination, followed by the careful removal of plaque and tartar from your teeth and gumline using specialized tools.
  • Polishing, Flossing & Protection: After cleaning, your teeth are polished to remove surface stains and professionally flossed. An optional fluoride treatment may be applied to help strengthen enamel and prevent cavities.
  • Personalized Care & Guidance: We finish with tailored advice on maintaining your oral health at home, including brushing, flossing techniques, and dietary tips based on your specific needs.
