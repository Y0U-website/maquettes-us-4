Dentist

I’m thrilled to be your dentist and truly look forward to building a long-lasting relationship with you and your family.

I began my academic journey at the University of Texas at Dallas before earning my Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Arizona School of Dentistry & Oral Health. During my time there, I received extensive training in delivering whole-person, compassionate care—something I carry into every patient interaction.

My goal is to provide high-quality dentistry in a friendly, comfortable environment. I’m especially passionate about preventive care and love empowering patients to achieve and maintain healthy, confident smiles.

Outside of the dental office, I enjoy spending quality time with family and friends. I also love staying active—tennis and golf are my go-to ways to unwind and recharge!

I can’t wait to meet you and help you take the next step toward a healthier, brighter smile.
