Embrace the Benefits of Implant Dentures

Implanted dentures offer several advantages over traditional dentures. While every patient’s experience is unique, many enjoy benefits such as:

More Stability

The anchored posts keeping implant-supported dentures in place prevent slipping, moving, and falling out.

More Efficient Chewing

Implanted dentures are stronger and more stable than traditional dentures, allowing you to eat a broader range of food. Traditional dentures, however, may limit the full chewing force you can, preventing you from eating hard or sticky foods.

Better Tasting Ability

Traditional dentures cover the upper palate, where many taste buds reside. The U-shaped form of implant dentures keeps that prime tasting area open.

Improved Aesthetics

Implant dentures also have aesthetic advantages, as they generally look more natural, and their improved stability prevents them from moving while you are talking or eating.

Better Health

When teeth are missing, the jaw bone tends to lose bone density because it no longer receives stimulation. Denture implants provide stimulation to the jaw bone, promoting the retention of bone density.
