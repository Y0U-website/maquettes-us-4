Dental Savings Plans

Affordable Dental Care for Your Family—No Insurance Required

At Lexington Family Smiles , we believe everyone deserves a healthy, confident smile , regardless of their insurance status. If you are self-employed, retired, or your employer doesn’t offer dental benefits, our Dental Savings Plan is designed specifically for you.

This isn’t insurance—it’s a simplified membership that gives you immediate access to premium dental care without the red tape.

Our Standard Plan is designed to provide yearly dental maintenance along with discounts for any additional treatment, helping keep your mouth happy and healthy.

Our Periodontal Plan functions as an add-on to our Standard Plan because most patients do not know they are a periodontal patient until treatment. Once aware, patients are able to add the additional coverage for $100 per member.

CHILD (UNDER 18) STANDARD PLAN $ 249 /year

Upon joining our Dental Savings Plans, each patient also receives one lifetime set of whitening trays and gel. Dental Savings Plans do not include cosmetic dentistry or braces.
