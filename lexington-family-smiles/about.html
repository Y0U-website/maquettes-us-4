Schedule Your Visit at Lexington Family Smiles

We accept new patients of all ages and offer flexible scheduling, including same-day appointments for urgent dental needs. For patients managing costs, our Dental Savings Plan provides a low-cost, no-waiting-period alternative to traditional insurance, and we also accept CareCredit, Cherry, and Sunbit financing, so care is never out of reach. Dr. Moulton is an active member of both the American Dental Association (ADA) and the South Carolina Dental Association (SCDA), and the entire team is committed to upholding that standard of care at every single visit.

Whether your family needs a first dental home, you are switching from a practice where you felt like a number, or you simply want a dentist you can count on for the long term, we are ready to be that practice. Contact us to schedule your visit at Lexington Family Smiles today.
