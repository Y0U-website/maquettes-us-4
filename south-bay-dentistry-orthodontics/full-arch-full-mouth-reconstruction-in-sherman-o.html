What Is Full Mouth Reconstruction?

Full mouth reconstruction goes beyond replacing missing teeth. It is a comprehensive treatment approach that rebuilds the entire smile, bite, and oral function.

Every treatment plan is customized to the patient’s unique needs, goals, oral health condition, and lifestyle.

  • Dental implants
  • Crowns and bridges
  • Veneers
  • Bone grafting
  • Gum treatment
  • Orthodontics or Invisalign®
  • Root canal therapy
  • Dentures
  • Oral surgery

Signs You May Need Full Mouth Reconstruction

You may be a candidate if you experience:

Many patients seeking full mouth reconstruction are looking for a long-term solution that restores both function and appearance.

  • Multiple missing teeth
  • Broken or worn teeth
  • Difficulty chewing
  • Chronic tooth pain
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Jaw discomfort
  • Severe gum disease
  • Extensive dental work that is failing
  • Low confidence when smiling

Benefits of Full Arch & Full Mouth Reconstruction

Improved Appearance

Modern restorations are designed to look natural and enhance your smile.

Better Comfort & Stability

Implant-supported restorations are more secure than traditional removable dentures.

Improved Chewing & Speech

Patients often experience improved bite strength and speech clarity.

Long-Term Function

Dental implants can provide long-lasting support for replacement teeth.

Increased Confidence

Many patients feel more comfortable smiling, speaking, and eating after treatment.

All-on-4® & All-on-X® Dental Implants

All-on-4® and All-on-X® are among the most advanced full arch restoration solutions available today.

These treatments use strategically placed implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth while minimizing the number of implants needed.

Patients who are tired of loose dentures often find implant-supported full arch restoration to be life changing.

  • Fewer implants
  • Faster treatment
  • Improved stability
  • Same-day temporary teeth in many cases
  • Natural-looking results
