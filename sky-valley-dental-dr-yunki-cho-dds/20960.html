Dental Implants

One tooth, several teeth, or implant-supported dentures. Same office.

  • Extract, if the Tooth Is Still There : Sometimes the implant can go in the same visit. Sometimes the site has to heal first. We will say which, before you are numb.
  • Graft, if the Bone Is Thin: Extra months, not extra offices.
  • Place the Implant: Numb, a small opening, the titanium root goes into bone. Nitrous or oral sedation if you want it. You go home the same day.
  • Heal (Osseointegration) : The bone locks to the implant. This is the waiting. Often a few months. You are not in a flipper forever if we can give you something better in the meantime.
  • The Tooth: We scan, design, and restore here. Many implant crowns are done in-office with CEREC instead of a second impression and a two-week temp.

How Long Does the Whole Thing Take?

Simple case: Consult, place, heal, crown, often a few months, not a year of referrals. If you need an extraction and a graft, we add that time on purpose so the implant lasts.

What It Feels Like

Local anesthetic. Most people say the idea is worse than the visit. Sedation is available. You will be sore. You will get written after-care, not a printout from 2014.

What We Will Tell You if Implants Are Not Right

Heavy smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, or not enough bone even with a graft can make this the wrong tool. We will say so and talk about a bridge or a partial. We do not sell an implant to everyone who asks.
