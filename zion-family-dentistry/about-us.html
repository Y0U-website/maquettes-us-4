Meet the Dentists

Amar Pattani, DDS

Dr. Amar Pattani combines a passion for design, structure, and aesthetics with a genuine commitment to high-quality patient care. With a background in electrical engineering from Northwestern University (Class of 1998), he later earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Marquette University. This path allowed him to merge his love of engineering with a desire to help people feel more confident in their smiles.

A caring and compassionate dentist, Dr. Pattani genuinely enjoys working with patients to create treatment plans tailored to their individual needs. He has advanced training and experience in multiple areas, including cosmetic and aesthetic fillings, implant placement, and restorations, endodontics, and orthodontics (including both braces and clear aligners). He is also a certified injector of Botox, Xeomin, and fillers.

Originally from Jacksonville, Florida, and raised in the northwest suburbs of Chicago, Dr. Pattani now calls Illinois home. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys woodworking, carpentry, electronics, circuits, and programming. He’s a true DIY enthusiast who loves fixing things. When it’s time to relax, you’ll likely find him playing volleyball or watching football.

  • Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering, Northwestern University
  • Doctor of Dental Surgery, Marquette University
  • American Dental Association
  • Illinois State Dental Society
  • Chicago Dental Society
  • American Orthodontic Society
  • American Academy of Facial Esthetics

Van Nguyen, DMD

Dr. Van Nguyen is a compassionate dentist dedicated to delivering high-quality oral health care. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Downers Grove, Illinois.

Dr. Nguyen is known for her gentle touch and personalized approach, helping patients feel comfortable and confident during their visits. She believes in evidence-based dentistry and stays current with the latest advancements and technology in the field.

Her training includes general dentistry and braces, and she has completed continuing education in basic straight wire orthodontics. Dr. Nguyen joined the practice in 2024.

Outside of work, Dr. Nguyen enjoys traveling, wandering through Costco aisles, and spending quality time with her family and her dog, Eggroll.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine, Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine (Downers Grove, IL)
