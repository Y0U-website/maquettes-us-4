Botox With TMJ Treatment

LASER THERAPY FOR JAW PAIN RELIEF

Chronic jaw tension, clenching, and TMJ pain can wear down your teeth and affect your quality of life. That’s why we offer a therapeutic approach that combines Botox with laser-assisted TMJ therapy. Botox helps relax overworked muscles, while our Biolase laser promotes healing through photobiomodulation. This stimulates blood flow, reduces inflammation, and supports tissue recovery. This combination treatment delivers comfort and relief for patients struggling with headaches, bite misalignment, and jaw dysfunction, without the need for surgery or long-term medication.

They are always friendly and professional at the Zion Family Dentistry.

Dental Crowns

STRONG, NATURAL-LOOKING RESTORATIONS

If a tooth is weakened or damaged, a dental crown restores its shape and protects it from further harm. We offer high-quality crowns made from zirconia, e.max, porcelain, and gold, tailored to your specific needs and preferences. We also use digital impressions for your comfort and to ensure your crown is customized for an excellent fit.

Bridges

A FIXED SOLUTION TO FILL GAPS IN YOUR SMILE

Dental bridges are an effective way to replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring a custom prosthetic tooth to the neighboring healthy teeth or implants. This restores chewing ability, prevents shifting, and improves oral health, function, and appearance.

Dental Implants

PLACED IN-HOUSE FOR YOUR CONVENIENCE

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. They can replace a single tooth, or replace multiple with a full-arch restoration. We place all implants right here in our office using advanced 3D-guided surgical technology for your convenience. We pre-plan implants with 3D imaging to ensure accurate placement, then use 3D-printed surgical guides for better long-term outcomes. Our implants are either ceramic or zirconia. By keeping the entire process in-house, our team is able to provide personalized service, ensuring quality, consistency, and results you can trust.

Dentures

AFFORDABLE, COMFORTABLE TOOTH REPLACEMENT

For patients missing multiple teeth, dentures provide a stable and functional solution. At Zion Family Dentistry & Implant Center, we offer both full and partial dentures, custom-crafted for a secure fit and natural look. Whether you’ve worn dentures for years or are exploring them for the first time, our team will guide you through your options and ensure your final result has you confidently smiling again.

Implant-Supported Dentures

A STABLE & SECURE OPTION

Implant-supported dentures provide greater stability and comfort than traditional removable dentures. We offer both fixed and removable options, including overdentures (the most common removable type). These dentures anchor to dental implants for a snug fit. They also allow better chewing ability and more confidence in daily life. Unlike some practices, the team at Zion Family Dentistry & Implant Center places implant posts in-house. So you’ll receive continuity of care from that very first consultation to your final fitting.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

RESTORING YOUR ENTIRE SMILE, STEP BY STEP

When multiple teeth are missing or damaged, a full mouth rehabilitation may be the best option to restore both form and function. This process starts by evaluating your bite, tooth wear, and your overall health. From there, we create a treatment plan tailored to your needs—one that supports long-term stability, comfort, and a well-aligned and balanced smile.

The team at Zion Family Dentistry & Implant Center takes a comprehensive approach. If you come in for a missing tooth, but we also see signs of bite collapse or occlusal issues, we won’t just fix the one problem. We’ll help you with all of it so you can have a healthier smile. This approach ensures that your new smile supports your long-term oral health. At our practice, we use digital tools and diagnostics, and we have years of advanced training behind us to help you get the results you’re looking for.
