Rex Johnson, DDS

Dr. Rex Johnson DDS is a highly skilled dentist with a passion for making people happy. After graduating with a BS in Zoology from BYU in 1993, he earned his DDS from the University of Nebraska Medical College, marking the beginning of his dental career. Dr. Johnson’s dedication to excellence led him to complete an orthodontic program at the University of Minnesota in 1999, further honing his expertise in dental care.

Beyond the clinic, he finds joy in spending time with his family. Raised on a ranch near Durango, he loves to spend time in the mountains riding his horses and gathering cows.

Dr. Tonni Faber, DMD

Dr. Tonni Faber is originally from upstate NY where she resided both on her father’s dairy farm raising Holstein cows & Quarter horses and with her Mom and Step-father, who was a dentist, and also had a farm. Along with her siblings, she participated in 4-H with their horses. While working in her stepfather’s dental clinic fabricating crowns and prosthetics she realized her true passion.

Dr. Faber is a member of the American Dental Association, the Colorado Dental Association and the Weld County Dental Society. She has extensive experience in both pediatric and adult dentistry. She enjoys staying informed about new dental technology and treatment for general dentistry by attending dental conferences.

When she is not practicing dentistry, Dr. Faber and her boyfriend Neal have fun adventures traveling on his Harley, camping and fishing, and enjoying the beautiful landscape that is Colorado
