Dr. Tonni Faber is originally from upstate NY where she resided both on her father’s dairy farm raising Holstein cows & Quarter horses and with her Mom and Step-father, who was a dentist, and also had a farm. Along with her siblings, she participated in 4-H with their horses. While working in her stepfather’s dental clinic fabricating crowns and prosthetics she realized her true passion.

Dr. Faber is a member of the American Dental Association, the Colorado Dental Association and the Weld County Dental Society. She has extensive experience in both pediatric and adult dentistry. She enjoys staying informed about new dental technology and treatment for general dentistry by attending dental conferences.
