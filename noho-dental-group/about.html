Skilled, Passionate Dental Staff

A multi-specialty team of doctors, hygienists, assistants and front-desk staff working together to make every visit comfortable, efficient, and personal.

Doctors & Specialists

  • Allen Jacobson, DDS, MDS Orthodontist Dr. Allen Jacobson leads orthodontic care at Noho Dental Group, offering personalized treatment with traditional braces and Invisalign clear aligners for patients of all ages. His practice focuses on functional bite correction alongside aesthetic outcomes.
  • Dr. Kakoli, DDS, MS Endodontist Dr. Kakoli is our endodontic specialist, focused on saving natural teeth through modern root canal therapy. She uses microscope-assisted techniques and rotary instrumentation to make root canals faster, more comfortable, and highly predictable.
  • Dr. Jacob Kohanoff General Dentist Dr. Jacob Kohanoff provides comprehensive general dentistry at Noho Dental Group, from routine cleanings and fillings to crowns, bridges, and cosmetic care. He is committed to conservative, minimally invasive dentistry.

Dental Hygienists

  • Azi Yaghoubian, RDH Registered Dental Hygienist Azi is one of our registered dental hygienists, providing gentle preventive care, periodontal cleanings, and patient education so every visit builds long-term oral health.
  • Gloria Banzali, RDH Registered Dental Hygienist Gloria is a registered dental hygienist known for her thorough, calm chairside manner — especially with patients who feel anxious about cleanings.
