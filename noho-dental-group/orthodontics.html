Types of Braces and Orthodontic Options

We offer both metal braces and clear braces, giving patients flexibility based on comfort and aesthetic preferences. Braces work by applying continuous, gentle pressure to reposition teeth over time, creating lasting alignment and improved oral health.

For patients looking for a more discreet option, we also provide Invisalign® clear aligners, which are removable and nearly invisible, ideal for teens and adults who want straighter teeth without traditional brackets and wires.

In addition, we provide custom retainers to help maintain your beautiful results after your treatment is complete.
