What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the gold standard in modern tooth replacement. At Noho Dental Group, we offer high quality, affordable dental implants in North Hollywood designed to restore your smile, confidence, and overall oral health.

A dental implant is a titanium post that is surgically placed into your jawbone, acting as a secure and permanent foundation for a dental crown. This crown is custom crafted to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth, so your smile looks seamless and natural.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Designed to look and feel like real teeth

Essential for preserving jawbone structure

Easier to maintain than bridges or dentures

How the Process Works

Initial Consultation – We examine your teeth, jawbone, and oral health to confirm you’re a candidate.

Implant Placement – A titanium screw is carefully inserted into your jawbone.

Healing Period – Over 3–9 months, the implant fuses with your bone (a process called osseointegration).

Crown Placement – Once healed, we place a lifelike dental crown that blends perfectly with your smile.

After your implant procedure, you’ll be able to eat, brush, and floss like normal, no special care needed!

If you’re missing teeth or using removable dentures, dental implants may be the perfect solution. Contact Noho Dental Group today to schedule your implant consultation and take the first step toward a complete, confident smile.
