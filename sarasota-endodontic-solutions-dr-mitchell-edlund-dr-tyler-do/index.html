Endodontic therapy becomes necessary when the pulp (the tissue inside the tooth) becomes infected or inflamed. Endodontic issues usually occur due to deep cavities (caries), cracks, chips, repeated dental procedures, root resorption or trauma.

Root canals are one of the most common dental procedures performed by endodontists. Also known as root canal therapy, root canals are performed on teeth with infected pulp to heal the diseased tooth and avoid extraction. This single treatment can help save your natural teeth and prevent you from having to get dental implants or bridges.

Apicoectomy is the most common endodontic microsurgical technique used to save a tooth. Apicoectomy, or root-end resection, can be used to locate missed canals or small fractures that have been previously undetected during initial treatment.

Endodontics is the branch of dentistry focused on the dental pulp and the tissues surrounding the roots of teeth.
