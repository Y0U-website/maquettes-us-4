Our Mission

Our Mission is simple: to provide you with the finest endodontic treatment available in a comfortable, caring environment.

Dr. Mitchell Edlund, Endodontist, specializes in both microscopic and microsurgical root canal treatments. He and and his professional staff believe in a passion for excellence that is measured by a commitment to a quality outcome.

Our patient’s entire experience from the initial phone call, treatment, and the after-treatment inquiry call is designed to be pleasant, relaxed and accommodating.

Practice Profile

Our practice is heavily invested in the latest technology and in continuing education. Dr. Edlund’s years of general and restorative dentistry, as well as his background in Endodontics, is a definite asset in identifying the best course of treatment for challenging cases.

Sarasota Endodontic Solutions boasts the latest technology to facilitate both diagnosis and treatment. All phases of root canal therapy are performed using a dental operating microscope. Our in-house 3-D, cone-beam, CT imaging provides unparalleled analysis in order to determine the best treatment options.
