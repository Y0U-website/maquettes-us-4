Meet the Doctors

Dr. Mitchell Edlund

Dr. Edlund graduated from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2004. He spent the next three years practicing all aspects of general dentistry, with his father, in his home state of Washington. He completed a one-year Endodontic research fellowship program at the University of Florida prior to being accepted into their endodontic residency program. Dr. Edlund was selected, by the faculty, to be the chief resident of his class. Dr. Edlund’s research focused on cone beam CT technology.

Dr. Edlund grew up in the Pacific Northwest. In college he spent a summer in Cuernavaca, Mexico in a Spanish language immersion program. During his dental school training he completed a study abroad program at the University of Malmo, Sweden school of dentistry.

Dr. Edlund is a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics. Becoming a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics places you in a distinguished group of endodontists who have demonstrated exceptional knowledge and skill, dedication to continued professional growth, and a commitment to providing the highest quality of patient care.

In his spare time, Dr. Edlund enjoys spending time with his growing family.
