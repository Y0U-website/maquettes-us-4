About Us

Welcome to Prime Care

As an experienced dental office based in Baltimore, the patients at Prime Care Dental appreciate the warmth, professional integrity, and open communication that define our dental practice. We are determined to help our patients understand their own health so they can be well informed when making important health decisions.

Why Choose Us?

Our team of dental experts offers the most advanced dentistry services with a superior quality of care that exceeds the highest patient expectations. We make your dental care more convenient is by welcoming the whole family to our office.

In our practice, we want every patient to be comfortable from the moment they walk through the door. That’s why we take time to greet you warmly, and really get to know you, your family, and your smile goals. We also offer dental sedation, when patients need a little extra help remaining at ease.

Our Mission

​Prime Care Dental strives to provide the best quality dental care to every patient that visits our office. It is our mission to ensure that each patient we treat feels as comfortable and at home as possible – establishing a family atmosphere conducive to an outstanding dental experience.

We aim to provide quality and comfort for a lifetime of dental excellence for people of all ages. At Prime Care Dental, we work as a team to inform and educate our patients with personalized, state-of-the-art, preventive dental care

We believe in affordable, high quality dental care for all.

Our mission is to provide the highest quality dental care in a relaxed, comfortable and safe atmosphere.

Our Focus

Our practice is working together to realize a shared vision of uncompromising excellence in dentistry.

General Dentistry

Visiting our office every six months gives you the chance to get answers to any questions you may have about your oral health.

Children’s Dentistry

Our dental care team is specially trained to care for baby teeth and address the oral health needs of children.

Cosmetic Dentistry

There are many cosmetic dentistry procedures that we can use including teeth whitening, veneers, crowns, and implants, among others.
