Dr. Todd Christensen

After achieving his bachelor’s degree in medical biology, he attended Dental School at the University of Connecticut and additionally completed a 2-year residency in Dental Anesthesiology at Loma Linda University in southern California.

In 2002, he started his practice in Arizona specializing in in-office anesthesia services for Pediatric dentists. He also created/taught at the University level in the hygiene clinic. In 2018 he transitioned back to full-time clinical general dentistry, and in 2022, after almost 22 years, he focused on General Dentistry for children and left behind the sedation/anesthesia specialty.

He enjoys working with older children and teens and looks forward to making great relationships here in Alaska.

Dr. Christensen was born into a family of 10 children in Farmington, NM, and raised in Mesa, AZ. After high school graduation, he completed a 2-year missionary service for his church in Peru. He then married his longtime friend Cheryl. Thier two sons have their own families now, and their daughter is one of the best sourdough bakers around! They have been blessed with 4 grandkids and love to be outside as much as possible.

When they have free time, they love both the beach and the mountains, road cycling, camping, hiking, and backpacking.

Dr. Hyrum Murdoch

Dr. Murdoch offers root canal treatment and wisdom teeth extractions. Dr. Murdoch received his dental education as a member of the inaugural class of College of Dental medicine Illinois at Midwestern University. Dr. Murdoch followed a career in dentistry after his studies in Exercise Science at Brigham Young University - Idaho. Dr. Murdoch is an active member of the American and Alaska State dental societies.
