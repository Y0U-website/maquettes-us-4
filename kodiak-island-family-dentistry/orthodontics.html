Who Should Get Dental Braces?

We can apply braces at any age, even late into adulthood. Teeth alignment yields best results if applied around 7 years old. Like all bones in children, teeth are easily molded and moved because a child is still growing.

For adults, dental braces are still a healthy option for better smiles. Be sure to ask about Invisalign clear aligners as a more comfortable alternative.

Braces also have health benefits, including alignment of the upper and lower jaws to alleviate the difficulty of cleaning crooked teeth. Alignment helps relieve pressure on temporomandibular joints, which could ease teeth grinding, symptoms of TMJ, and other painful jaw symptoms.

Why Should I Consider Invisalign ?

Invisalign clear aligners are made from SmartTrack material, so they are comfortable and easy to put on and take off.
