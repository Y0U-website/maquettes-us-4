Front Office Team

Shelly

Insurance Coordinator

I’m married with children; my husband Rick and I have 8 kids between the two of us – his three and my five. We have ten grandkids. I moved to Wichita from the Kansas City area, and have lived here since 2001.

Why did you choose dentistry? I’ve worked with accounts receivable and insurance for many years and enjoy helping and working with patients to achieve their dental goals financially.

What is the best part of your job? I enjoy seeing how we as a team help our patients achieve their dental goals.

What aspect of your personality adds the most value to the office? My ability to get to know our patients and help our team make their visits as comfortable as possible.

What do you do outside of the office? My husband and I travel quite a bit yearly. We love going to concerts, going out to dinner on Friday nights, and taking my grandkids to the zoo. Our family is into all sorts of sports, especially basketball, and we enjoy watching our grandkids’ teams play.

We also collect a lot of Beatles memorabilia and cookbooks.

What’s one item on your bucket list? I would love to visit the Amalfi Coast in Italy.

Erika

Patient Care Coordinator

I was born and raised in Wichita and feel lucky to still call it home. I married my high school sweetheart, and together we have two amazing kids—a boy and a girl—who keep life fun and busy.

Why did you choose dentistry? The best part of my job is seeing firsthand how everyone treats each other like family. It’s truly inspiring to watch the strong, trusting relationships that have been built with our patients over time. Being part of that kind of team is something really special.

What is the best part of your job? I love being able to help create a welcoming and comforting environment, especially for patients who feel nervous or anxious about their visit. Whether I’m scheduling an appointment or checking someone in, I do my best to make sure each person feels seen, heard, and at ease.

What aspect of your personality adds the most value to the office? I’d say my calming and friendly nature helps put patients at ease. Dentistry can be intimidating, but a warm smile and reassuring voice can make all the difference. I’m happy to offer that kind of support every day.

What do you do outside of the office? Outside of work, you can usually find me at the park with my kids, planning our next big family adventure, or curled up with a good book and a quiet moment to myself.

The Hygiene Team

Shelby

Registered Dental Hygienist

I’m a born and raised Wichitan. I bleed black and yellow, a WSU grad. My husband is from Wichita as well, he is a lieutenant in the Fire Department. We have two little girls, Summer Shae and Caroline Jo. We spend most of our free time outdoors chasing them around.

Why did you choose dentistry? My mom is a retired dental assistant. I grew up in a dental office. We went to work with her when school was closed and on the weekends to help her clean the office. My sisters and I would “play dentist” often. My sisters were my patients growing up when we played dentist and were my first patients in dental hygiene school as well. Little did they know, they were guinea pigs all those years. I suppose being immersed in the dental world is what peaked my interest in pursuing dental hygiene.

What is the best part of your job? Working in an environment where I can laugh as loud as I want. Dr Volker and his assistants say they can hear me laughing across the building. Laughter is contagious. It brings people joy. It connects people. It is how I connect with my patients.

What aspect of your personality adds the most value to the office? I like to think I’m flexible. I try to go with the flow. I can adapt to most situations.

What do you do outside of the office? My family and I are regulars at the park, zoo and Exploration Place. We love most things in Wichita, local festivals, kayaking down the Arkansas, hole-in-the-wall-bars, live music, and Vegas every once in a blue moon.

Cande

What’s one item on your bucket list? I would love to go to a KC Chiefs game at Arrowhead Stadium! I love to watch the games but I have never actually been to a game at the stadium. Kirsten

Registered Dental Hygienist

I have lived in Wichita my entire life and cannot imagine living anywhere else. I graduated from WSU. My husband and I stay busy running around after our three girls and two dogs.

Why did you choose dentistry? I had severe dental anxiety as a child (and still do to this day!) so I didn’t think that I would end up working in dentistry until I finished high school. I love to pick at things and meticulously clean things with small brushes and toothpicks, so being a dental hygienist came naturally. I feel like my dental anxiety has also made me a lot more empathetic to others who share my anxieties.

What is the best part of your job? I love making connections with patients and looking forward to catching up with them at their appointments.

What aspect of your personality adds the most value to the office? I’m very detail-oriented which comes in handy with cleaning teeth. I am also a quiet observer which helps me to solve problems.

What do you do outside of the office? Having three little girls and two dogs, my husband and I spend most of our free time playing in our backyard or at the park. When I get the chance I enjoy reading mystery books.

What’s one item on your bucket list? Someday I would love to visit Greece.

Dental Assisting Team

Kendra

Dental Assistant

What do you do outside of the office? Outside of work I’m the BIGGEST KC CHIEFS FAN, football season is definitely a favorite in my house! I decorate cakes in my free time, I love being artistic and I get to enjoy the tasty creations. Staying fit is important to me so I love when I can get outside and be active.

Who is your hero? Why? My hero has always been and will always be my mother. She is a strong woman who wears her heart on her sleeve. She takes care of those around her and has set an incredible example for me to live up to. When I choose to take care of those around me I do it in a way I know would make her proud.
