The practice

Dr. Daciana Buse obtained her dental license and her Master’s degree in Oral Medicine from the University of Washington in 2006. Following graduation, she had a faculty appointment at the University of Washington for several years. This experience enhanced her perspective on dentistry in the context of systemic health.

Dr. Buse has a passion for high-end technology and believes that mixing it with detailed attention to patients’ needs leads to optimal clinical results. For the same reason, she became licensed to offer conscious sedation services in 2020. Having this option in her toolkit can put any patient at ease regarding any necessary dental procedure.
