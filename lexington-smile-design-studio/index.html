The practice

Like many offices, we take immense pride in creating beautiful, confident smiles. What makes us different is our commitment to standing true to our core values, understanding the importance of careful diagnosis, quality over numbers, and the personal touch and compassion for our neighbors that is disappearing with our profession as corporate takeover continues.

Continually striving for perfection, only settling for excellence, and always treating the patient behind the smile.

At our office, you can say goodbye to corporate, assembly-line dentistry and place your smile in the care of professionals who focus on one patient at a time. We offer a dental spa-like setting with excellent, precise, and convenient treatment that will have you telling all of your friends.

Our office is equipped with state-of-the-art technology and the latest dental advances that will decrease the number of visits, as well as the length of appointment times. We offer same-day CEREC crowns which allows for your tooth to be digitally scanned and your crown milled/delivered in just one appointment.

Dr. Max works closely with Samantha Merrifield, the local and very talented lead ceramist for Aesthetic Dental Solutions, who will make every effort to personally meet patients at their initial records appointment. This allows our team to design beautifully natural porcelain veneers that will match your new smile to your expectations and individual personality.

We take pride in our methods/ability to alleviate severe dental anxiety for those who have a history of traumatic dental experiences, most of which have lost trust in the dental profession as a whole. We are committed to restoring your faith in the field of dentistry, providing you with honest, compassionate dental care tailored to each patient’s needs.
