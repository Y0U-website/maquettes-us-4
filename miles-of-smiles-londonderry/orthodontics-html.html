How Invisalign Works

Invisalign uses clear, plastic aligners that each represent a new stage of treatment. Worn 20-22 hours a day, the trays apply gentle pressure to teeth so that they will move over time into the desired position. After 1-2 weeks of wear, they are swapped out for the next set in the series, allowing teeth to continually move.

Benefits of Invisalign

Have you ever wondered why more than 17 million patients have chosen to straighten their teeth with Invisalign? If so, you’re in the right place! In this next section, we’re covering a few of the many reasons that clear aligner treatment is so popular.

Shorter Average Treatment Timeline

Oftentimes, patients need to commit 2+ years to the teeth-straightening process with traditional braces. With Invisalign, the average treatment timeline is only 12-18 months. So, if you schedule your consultation this week, there’s a good chance that you’ll have the gorgeous smile you desire by this time next year!

Basically Invisible Aligners

Of course, one of the most obvious perks of Invisalign is how discreet the aligners are. Since they are crafted from see-through plastic and have a low profile, they won’t draw any unwanted attention to your smile. In fact, there’s a good chance that even those who are closest to you won’t be able to tell the difference!

Metal-Free Braces

It’s no secret that traditional braces aren’t the most comfortable. Fortunately, Invisalign aligners don’t have any metal components. As a result, many patients don’t experience any soft tissue irritation during their orthodontic treatment!

Tip: If you do start to experience soreness or discomfort from the movement of your teeth, sip on cool water, use a cold compress, and take OTC pain medication as directed.

No Monthly Adjustment Appointments

Since there are no wires to tighten, there are fewer appointments with Invisalign. In fact, most patients only need to come to our office every 6-8 weeks. That way, we can monitor the movement of your teeth and make sure they are tracking as anticipated. If they are, then he can provide you with the next several sets of aligners and schedule your next visit in a month or so!

Hassle-Free Dental Care

Since traditional braces aren’t removable, patients need to maneuver their toothbrush and floss around the brackets and wires. As you might expect, this is easier said than done. Fortunately, Invisalign aligners are removable, so completing your oral hygiene regimen each morning and evening will be significantly easier. In fact, the only change you need to make to your routine is taking care of your aligners, too.

Freedom to Eat Your Favorite Foods

Last, but certainly not least, you don’t have to abide by a long list of dietary restrictions with Invisalign. In fact, as long as you take your aligners off beforehand, you can enjoy all of your favorite foods! Just make sure to exercise caution with crunchy foods and keep sugary sweets to a minimum to reduce your chances of developing a cavity or cracking your enamel.

Living with Invisalign Aligners

Over the last two decades, Invisalign has helped millions of patients around the world. So, there’s a good chance that you already know quite a bit about it. But do you know what your day-to-day life will look like if you choose to straighten your teeth with clear aligners? If not, that’s okay this next section is covering everything you need to know.

Wearing Your Trays

When it comes to wearing your trays, there’s basically one rule: you need to keep them on for a minimum of 20 hours a day. So, while they are removable, it’s best to take them off only when it’s absolutely necessary, like before brushing your teeth.

Tip: When you first get your aligners, use the stopwatch on your phone to track your wear time. Once you find a routine that works for you, stick with it! That way, meeting the mark becomes second nature.

Cleaning Your Aligners

Like many other patients, you may have chosen Invisalign in Londonderry because the aligners are basically invisible. While they will certainly be clear when you remove them from their packaging, they can quickly discolor if you don’t take good care of them. That’s why it’s so important to rinse them with clean, cool water when you take them off. Then, before you put them back on, use a damp, soft-bristle toothbrush and clear, mild dishwashing liquid to gently clean the surface.

Eating & Drinking

Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign doesn’t come with a long list of dietary restrictions that you need to abide by. In fact, there’s really only one rule: you need to take them off before eating or drinking anything other than Invisalign. Outside of that, just do your best to eat healthy, well-balanced meals and keep sugary sweets to a minimum to prevent tooth decay and other common oral health problems from developing.

Losing or Damaging a Tray

If you accidentally misplace or damage one of your aligners, it’s important that you call our team at Miles of Smiles right away. The sooner you do, the sooner we can determine what to do next. If there is a hairline crack in one of the trays, for example, then we may give you the green light to continue to wear it (with the utmost care, of course). If the damage is significant, on the other hand, then we may ask you to revert to the previous set while we have a replacement made instead.

Attending Progress Visits

Throughout your Invisalign treatment, you’ll need to keep up with your routine dental checkups and cleanings . You will also need to come to our office periodically so we can monitor the movement of your teeth. The good news is that these appointments are usually every 6-8 weeks and last less than 30 minutes each time. So, even if you have a busy schedule, they won’t take up too much of your time.

Invisalign vs. Mail-In Aligners: Which Costs More?

Almost always, mail-in aligners are cheaper. Of course, that doesn’t mean they are better. Quite the opposite: they usually cut costs by skipping the initial exam, relying on DIY impressions, and not hiring licensed dental professionals to oversee the teeth-straightening process. As a result, many patients experience worse misalignment, and some even experience tooth loss. For all of those reasons, we recommend choosing Invisalign, even if the upfront cost is higher.

Invisalign FAQs

Does Invisalign Hurt?

As your teeth begin to move out of their current positions, you can expect to feel some soreness, particularly in the hours following the transition to a new set of aligners. With that said, the large majority of patients report that Invisalign doesn’t hurt, especially when compared to traditional braces. So, if you are considering clear aligner treatment, don’t let fear of occasional soreness or discomfort stop you from scheduling an appointment. When you see the results, the time you invested will be well worth it!

Can You Drink Coffee with Invisalign?

You can drink coffee during your Invisalign treatment, but there is one rule: you need to remove your aligners first. If you don’t, then stains will quickly surface, calling unwanted attention to your braces. Not only that, but the chances of developing tooth decay will increase significantly if you keep your aligners on, which can negatively impact your oral health as well as your treatment plan. So, make sure that you always take your trays out first, even if it seems more convenient to keep them on in the moment.

Why Do I Need to Wear My Aligners for 20+ Hours a Day?

Simply put, if you don’t wear your aligners for 20+ hours a day, then your teeth won’t move as anticipated. If that happens, then it’s only a matter of time before you fall off-track with your treatment plan, delaying your results and potentially requiring a larger financial investment. To prevent that from happening, you should get in the habit of taking them out only when it’s absolutely necessary, like before eating.

What Should I Do If I Lose an Aligner?

If you lose an aligner, then start with this age-old advice: retrace your steps! In the process, look in the waste bin, the pockets of the pants you were wearing earlier, your fridge, and other random places. If you’ve searched high and low, but can’t find them anywhere, then contact our Londonderry office. Don’t worry we aren’t here to judge you. Quite the opposite; we’re here to help you get back on-track with your treatment plan.

Is There Anything I Can’t Eat with Invisalign?

Even though there technically aren’t any dietary restrictions with Invisalign, there are some foods we recommend avoiding (or, at the very least, keeping to a minimum). Taffy and other sticky candies are a great example because they are packed with sugar, they cling to your teeth, and they can cause an attachment to come loose. For similar reasons, we don’t recommend indulging in starchy and crunchy snacks, like potato chips and hard pretzels.
