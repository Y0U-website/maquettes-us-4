Why might my gums need deeper treatment? A regular cleaning focuses mainly above the gumline. Scaling and root planing treats deeper buildup associated with gum disease.

Will I be numb? Local anesthetic is commonly used to keep deeper cleaning comfortable.

What happens afterward? Temporary tenderness or sensitivity can occur. Follow-up and more frequent maintenance may be recommended.
