Restorative Dentistry

At Harborside Dental, we specialize in restorative dental services designed to rebuild and protect your smile. Our goal is always to preserve as much of your natural tooth structure as possible while restoring comfort, function, and aesthetics. You can trust that we will never recommend unnecessary treatments—our care recommendations are always tailored to your unique needs, oral health goals, and budget.

For procedures that require specialty expertise, we partner closely with a trusted network of highly skilled professionals—including Endodontists, Oral Surgeons, Periodontists, Prosthodontists, Sleep Dentistry Specialists, Sedation Specialists, Physical Therapists, and Massage Therapists. This collaborative approach ensures our patients receive the highest standard of comprehensive care, whether they need root canals, implants, gum grafts, or other advanced procedures.

Dr. Rachael Creisher restores dental implants by crafting and placing custom crowns designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. We collaborate with expert specialists who perform the surgical placement of the implant itself, ensuring each patient receives precise and long-lasting results. Dental implants are the most advanced and natural-looking solution for replacing missing or damaged teeth, providing a permanent, stable foundation for a beautiful and functional smile.

If you are missing multiple teeth, we offer custom dentures and bridges, including implant-supported options that provide exceptional stability and comfort. These restorations restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence while maintaining your facial structure and oral health.

Our custom dental crowns are designed to strengthen and protect damaged teeth while blending naturally with your smile. Crowns can cover severe discoloration, restore teeth after root canal therapy, and safeguard dental work for long-term durability.
