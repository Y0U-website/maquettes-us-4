The practice

Dr. Creisher is skilled in all aspects of general dentistry and especially enjoys cosmetic and prosthetic cases that help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. She believes in personalized, preventive care and strives to make every dental experience positive and stress-free.

Following in the proud footsteps of her father and grandfather, both dentists who dedicated their careers to the profession, Dr. Creisher continues her family’s legacy of excellence in dentistry. Her passion for helping others extends beyond her practice—she devotes time to providing free dental care to underprivileged communities through mission trips, particularly in Guatemala.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Creisher enjoys traveling the world, spending time outdoors, and staying active through exercise. She is also dedicated to lifelong learning, continually expanding her knowledge through study groups and continuing education.

A first visit is a time to get to know one another. You will be introduced to our doctors and staff and be led through our cutting edge dental clinic. We will take extra time with children to ensure they will be relaxed on this and future visits. We will discuss your immediate needs. All fees will be discussed in advance.

Usually, a first visit for a dental patient will include xrays and an initial exam. One of our doctors will examine the xrays of you or your child’s teeth then discuss anything that may need attention.

We are in-network with Delta Dental PPO and Premier fee schedules and will gladly submit insurance claims on your behalf as a courtesy.

Our team strives to provide accurate estimates of your patient portion prior to treatment; however, please note that final payment amounts are determined by your insurance company.

We collect anticipated copayments and patient portions on the day of your scheduled visit, and any remaining balance determined by your insurance carrier will be billed to you once your claim has been processed.
