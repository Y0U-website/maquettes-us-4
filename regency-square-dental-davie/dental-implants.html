What Is the Process of Receiving Dental Implants?

Two or three separate appointments are usually required to prepare your smile, place the implant screw, and then attach the customized restoration. The process is quicker at Regency Square dental because our restorations are all made in house. Once the procedure is complete, we provide instructions on how to recover best at home so that your smile stays healthy and that complications don’t arise.
