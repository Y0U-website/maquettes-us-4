What Changes the Cost of Implants?

Several things affect how much you will pay.

How Many Teeth Do You Need to Replace

One implant costs less than many implants. Full mouth dental implants cost much more because you need implants for all your teeth.

The Strength of Your Jawbone

If you have not had teeth for a long time, your jawbone gets weaker. You might need extra bone added before implants can be placed. This costs more money.

The Type of Implant

Different implants cost different amounts. Some are made of titanium. Others are made of different materials that might cost more.

Where the Implant Goes

Front tooth implants sometimes cost more because they need to look perfect. Back tooth implants might cost a little less.

Your City or Area

Costs are different in different places. Seeing a dentist in Davie might cost more than in other areas. Big cities usually cost more than small towns.

How Experienced Your Dentist Is

A dentist near you with lots of implant experience might charge more. But a better experience usually means better results.

Types of Implants and What They Cost

One Tooth Implant

One implant usually costs between $1,500 and $6,000. This price includes the implant, the connecting piece, and the crown.

A Few Implants

For two to four implants, the cost is usually $3,000 to $15,000 total.

All Teeth Implants

Full mouth dental implants cost around $20,000 to $100,000 or more. It depends on how many implants you need.

Some people get implant-supported false teeth instead. This costs less than implants for every tooth but still works really well.

Breaking Down the Implant Process

Getting implants happens in steps. Each step has a cost:

  • First visit and X-rays
  • Adding bone if you need it
  • Placing the implant
  • Waiting time for healing
  • Adding the connecting piece
  • Making and placing the crown

Ways to Make Implants More Affordable

You have choices to help pay for implants.

Dental Insurance

Some insurance plans pay for part of your implants. Call your insurance company to ask what they cover.

Payment Plans

Many dentists are happy to let you pay for treatment over a period of time. There may be an agreement to pay over months or even years.

Discount Plans

Some clinics have programs where the cost of treatment can be reduced.

Dental Schools

Sometimes dentists may offer cheaper implant treatment as students learn how to perform the procedure.

FAQs

Does my insurance pay for implants?

Some insurance plans pay for part of the cost. Usually, they pay 10 to 50 percent. Call your insurance company to find out what they cover.

How long does it take to get implants?

The whole process takes three to nine months. It depends on whether you need extra bone and how fast you heal.

Do I need special care for implants?

No—you care for implants like real teeth. Brush twice a day, floss, and see your dentist regularly.

How long do implants last?

With good care, implants last 25 to 30 years or even your whole life. They are a smart long-term choice.
