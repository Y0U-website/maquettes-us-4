Benefits of Dental Implants

Beyond replacing what is missing, implants help preserve the jawbone that a missing tooth would otherwise let shrink over time, which is one reason they hold up well over the long term for many patients. Because an implant stands on its own, it does not rely on the teeth next to it the way a bridge does, so those teeth stay untouched.

Day to day, that translates into a tooth that looks, feels, and functions like your own, without the shifting or slipping that can come with a removable denture, and without the extra strain a bridge places on neighboring teeth.

Are You a Candidate?

Most adults missing one or more teeth are candidates, provided there is enough healthy bone to support the implant, or a plan to rebuild it with grafting, and any active gum disease has been treated first. A short evaluation and imaging at your consultation confirm exactly where you stand.
