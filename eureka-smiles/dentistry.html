Routine Care

While brushing and flossing at home are important in maintaining optimal oral health, regular oral exams and cleanings as well as routine dental care by your dentist are even more important. To prevent gum disease and tooth decay, our office utilizes advanced technology to clean your teeth and gums more thoroughly than you would at home. Our comprehensive oral exams also include oral cancer screenings. Early diagnosis and treatment of oral cancer are critical aspects to raising the chances of survival.

Periodontal Care

Periodontics is the branch of dentistry concerned with the care and maintenance of the gums and other soft tissues of the mouth. Proper gum care is essential to the overall health of your teeth and mouth. It is especially important to establish good gum care routines early in life, as gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults. Our trained cosmetic dentists and their team always pay special attention to the gums of their patients to ensure that they are healthy. We are vigilant in educating our valued patients on the proper ways to care for their gums.

Endodontics/Root Canal Therapy

The good news is we can help you avoid tooth loss and further damage to your health with root canal therapy. As a form of restorative dentistry, this procedure is designed to remove the infected tissue, sterilize the nerve chamber, destroy all bacteria, and seal the nerve chamber and the tooth. After cleaning and sterilizing the infected area, we will place a custom-made crown on your tooth to reinforce it and even improve its appearance. The crown should give your tooth added support, while looking and feeling natural.
