Seva Select

Seva Select is not dental insurance, is non-refundable and is valid for one year from date of sign up. Seva Select cannot be used for products or combined with other specials.

* Payment must be made at time of service.

  • 2 dental cleanings per year
  • 1 set of recommended yearly X-rays
  • 1 professional fluoride treatment
  • 1 periodontal screenings per year (for adults)
  • 2 oral cancer screenings per year (for adults)
  • 10 % savings on any additional dental treatments*

Periodontal Plan

Exclusively for our uninsured periodontal patients, the Seva Select Periodontal Plan helps maintain healthy gums following periodontal therapy (Scaling and Root Planing). Enrolled patients who have not completed their periodontal therapy (Scaling and Root Planing) will receive the standard plan benefits and a 10 % savings on their remaining treatments.

Seva Select Periodontal Includes:

  • 2 oral exams per year
  • 4 periodontal maintenance cleanings per year
  • 1 set of recommended yearly X-rays
  • 1 professional fluoride treatment
  • 4 periodontal screenings per year
  • 2 oral cancer screenings per year
  • 10 % savings on any additional dental treatments*
