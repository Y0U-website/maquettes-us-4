Why Are Dental Implants a Good Option?

The Dental Implants Procedure

Only patients with healthy gums and enough bone in the jaw are typically good candidates for dental implants. Patients with insufficient jaw bone density may need bone grafting before receiving their implants.

Dental implants can be used to replace any number of missing teeth and offer patients a permanent solution to tooth loss. Visit Mission Creek Family Dentistry at 9460 Cuyamaca St # 101, Santee, CA 92071, or call (619) 596-1128 to learn more about dental implants.
