Dental Implant FAQs

How many implants will I need if I’m missing three or four teeth?

Not every missing tooth requires an individual implant. In many cases, we can use two implants to support a bridge that replaces three or four teeth in a row, making the process more efficient and cost-effective.

Is the procedure for multiple implants painful?

Your comfort is our top priority. We use local anesthesia and offer various sedation options to ensure you feel nothing during the procedure. Most patients in our Connecticut offices report that the recovery is much easier than they anticipated.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular checkups at our offices, dental implants are designed to be a lifelong solution. They do not decay like natural teeth and are built for durability.

Am I a candidate for multiple teeth implants?

Most adults with good general health and adequate bone density are candidates. Even if you have experienced bone loss, we offer bone grafting services right here in Connecticut to help prepare your jaw for successful implants.

How much do multiple teeth implants cost in Connecticut?

The cost varies based on the number of implants and whether any preparatory procedures are needed. During your consultation, we provide a transparent, line-item breakdown of costs and discuss financing options to fit your budget.

How do I care for my implants after the surgery?

Initially, you’ll follow a soft-food diet. Once healed, you care for your implants exactly like natural teeth, brushing, flossing, and visiting your dentist for routine cleanings.
