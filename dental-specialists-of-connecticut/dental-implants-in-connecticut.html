Dental Implants in Connecticut

Dental implants in Connecticut offer a strong, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. At Dental Specialists of Connecticut, we use advanced 3D planning and precise implant techniques to create personalized treatment plans, whether you need to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or a full arch.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Unlike traditional bridges or removable dentures, dental implants replace the missing tooth root and support a natural-looking restoration. They can help preserve jawbone, support a stable bite, and make it easier to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. With good oral hygiene and routine dental care, implants can provide a long-term solution for missing teeth.

Who Is a Candidate ?

Most healthy adults are candidates for implants. We evaluate gum health, bone volume, systemic health, and your smile goals. If needed, we may recommend bone grafting or sinus augmentation to create an ideal foundation. Our specialists will walk you through every step and coordinate seamlessly with your restorative dentist for the final crown or bridge.

Our Implant Services

Personalized implant solutions for replacing one tooth, several teeth, or a complete smile.

Single Implants Replace one missing tooth with a dental implant designed to look, feel, and function naturally.

Implant Bridges Replace multiple missing teeth with an implant-supported bridge without relying on neighboring natural teeth.

Overdentures / Snap‑in Dentures Improve denture stability with removable dentures that securely attach to strategically placed dental implants.

Full‑Arch Implant Solutions Replace an entire arch of missing or failing teeth with a fixed implant-supported restoration. Options may include All-on-4 and other customized full-arch approaches.

Bone Grafting & Ridge Preservation Rebuild or preserve jawbone to create a stronger foundation for dental implant treatment when needed.

Precision Planning, Comfortable Care Using CBCT imaging and digital planning, we carefully plan each implant’s position, angle, and depth before treatment. This technology helps us account for your individual anatomy and create a precise, personalized treatment plan. We also offer options to help make treatment more comfortable for patients who experience dental anxiety.
