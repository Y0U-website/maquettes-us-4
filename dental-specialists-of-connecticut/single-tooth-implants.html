Single Tooth Implant FAQs

What is a single tooth implant?

A single tooth implant is a replacement for one missing tooth. It typically includes an implant post, an abutment, and a custom dental crown.

Is a single tooth implant better than a dental bridge?

For many patients, a single tooth implant is a strong option because it does not require support from neighboring teeth. The best choice depends on your oral health and treatment goals.

How long does a single tooth implant last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, a single tooth implant can be a long-lasting solution.

How much does a single tooth implant cost in Connecticut?

The cost depends on your needs and whether any additional procedures are required. A consultation is the best way to receive a personalized estimate.

Am I a candidate for a single tooth implant?

You may be a candidate if you are missing one tooth and have enough healthy bone to support an implant. A consultation and exam will help determine the best treatment for your smile.
