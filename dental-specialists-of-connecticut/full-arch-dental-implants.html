Our Full Arch Treatment Options

Every smile is unique, so we tailor care to your needs.

All-on-4®

All-on-4® is a full arch implant solution that uses four strategically placed dental implants to support a complete arch of teeth. This option may be appropriate for patients looking for a secure, fixed alternative to removable dentures.

All-on-6

All-on-6 is a similar full arch treatment that uses six dental implants to support a full set of teeth. In some cases, using six implants may provide additional support depending on your anatomy and restorative needs.

Dental Implant FAQs

Is full arch the same as All-on-4®?

Full arch dental implants are the broader treatment category for replacing a full row of teeth with implant support. All-on-4® is one specific full arch treatment approach that uses four implants to support the restoration.

What is the difference between All-on-4® and All-on-6?

Both are full arch dental implant solutions. The main difference is the number of implants used to support the arch. The right option depends on your anatomy, oral health, and treatment goals.

Are full arch dental implants better than dentures?

For many patients, full arch dental implants offer greater stability, comfort, and confidence than removable dentures. However, the best solution depends on your individual needs.

How much do full arch dental implants cost in Connecticut?

The cost depends on your treatment needs, including the number of implants, extractions, and whether one or both arches are being treated. A consultation is the best way to receive an accurate estimate.

Am I a candidate for full arch dental implants?

You may be a candidate if you are missing most or all of your teeth, have failing teeth, or want a more stable alternative to dentures. A full exam and consultation can determine the best treatment for your smile.

How long do full arch dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can be a long-lasting solution. Your doctor will discuss how to protect your investment and maintain your oral health over time.
