The Process of Getting All‑On‑4 Implants

Your simple, three‑step journey to a restored smile.

Consultation & Planning

Your journey begins with a detailed consultation and digital scan. We’ll evaluate your jawbone structure, determine your candidacy, and map a personalized plan to replace your missing teeth while preserving your health.

Placement & Provisional Crown

On surgery day, four precisely positioned implants are placed in the jawbone. You’ll receive a temporary artificial tooth restoration so you can eat, speak, and smile confidently while your implants heal and integrate with the bone.

Final Restoration

Once healed, we fit your custom crown for lasting comfort and a flawless appearance. Dr. Betsy ensures your permanent solution restores function, strength, and beauty—so your smile feels truly like your real teeth.
