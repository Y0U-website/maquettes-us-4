About Dr. Jill Kollar

She worked as an associate general dentist and portrait artist for 3 years before opening her private practice in Windsor, CO in 2015. In 2016 she began teaching outside her practice at Front Range Community College in the Dental Assisting Program. From 2016-2022 she taught didactic and clinical assisting to students and helped transition classes from in-person to online while the clinic was closed due to the COVID pandemic. Her practice served as an externship site for graduating students.

She met her partner, who is an aerospace engineer, in 2018 and they share 3 wonderful children together. On the weekends they spend time with their friends and children on play dates and other child centered events.

Dr. Kollar is currently pursuing a Masters in Computer Science as a member of the National Society of Leadership and Success honor society. She is also an advocate for increasing the accessibility of dental care as a lead contact for Initiative 82 and on the Colorado Dental Association’s workplace shortage task force. Also, she is pursuing a patent for a medical device to alleviate dry mouth for patients with impaired salivary function.

“My philosophy of dental care is based on patient education and personalized treatment options. I want patients to be empowered to make decisions about their oral health. My approach to delivering the most reliable care is to connect with patients and provide treatment that fits their needs. I am an advocate of life long learning and am consistently pursuing creative solutions.”
