What Are Implant Restorations?

A dental implant restoration replaces one or more missing teeth using a small titanium post that integrates with your jawbone to act as a secure, artificial tooth root. Once the oral surgeon places the implant and healing is complete, Dr. Patt designs and attaches a custom restoration—such as a crown, bridge, or denture—that restores your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Dental implants can be an excellent option for many patients. Ideal candidates typically:

  • A single tooth with custom implant crown
  • Multiple teeth with an implant bridge
  • An entire arch with full-arch or All-on-4® implant-supported dentures

Dental Implants for Tooth Replacement

Patients across Boulder choose dental implants because they provide:

  • A natural look and feel, restoring your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence
  • Long-term bone health by stimulating the jawbone and preventing bone loss
  • Durability, with implants designed to last a lifetime and restorations lasting for many years
  • Stability without slipping or shifting, unlike traditional dentures
  • Protection of natural teeth, since implants don’t require support from adjacent teeth

What to Expect with the Restoration Process

  • Initial Consultation - Your journey begins with a thorough consultation at BOCO Dental. Dr. Patt will coordinate closely with your oral surgeon to develop a seamless treatment plan.
  • Healing and Osseointegration - After the implant is surgically placed, your jawbone will need time to heal and fuse with the implant in a process called osseointegration, which typically takes 2–3 months.
  • Healing Cap and Impressions - Once healing is complete, Dr. Patt places a healing cap to shape the gum tissue and then takes digital impressions, photos, and X-rays to design your restoration.
  • Custom Restoration Placement - Finally, Dr. Patt creates a custom crown, bridge, or denture that fits naturally with your bite and smile. At your final appointment, this restoration is securely attached to the implant, completing your transformation.

Dental Implants Types

  • Single-Tooth Replacement – A custom crown attached directly to a single implant.
  • Implant Bridges – Ideal for replacing multiple missing teeth, supported by implants on either side.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures – A secure, full-arch replacement solution, including All-on-4® systems.
