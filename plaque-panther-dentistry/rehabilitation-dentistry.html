Dentistry Needs

For the looks of your smile, the optimal function of your teeth and overall health of your mouth and body, Dr. Harris offers all the services to replace, restore and rehabilitate your teeth and gums.

Tooth Extractions

While the goal at Plaque Panther Dentistry is to protect and preserve the integrity of your teeth, if they are decayed, damaged beyond repair, or need to be removed as part of an orthodontic plan, Dr. Harris can remove teeth. Under local anesthesia, he will extract the tooth and clean the root area to prevent infection.

Dentures

Tooth loss can impede routine daily tasks like eating and speaking. When most or all of the teeth are missing, Dr. Harris can create a natural-looking set of partial or complete dentures to restore the function and esthetics of your smile. Thanks to technological advances, modern-day dentures look more natural than ever, and fit better, too.
