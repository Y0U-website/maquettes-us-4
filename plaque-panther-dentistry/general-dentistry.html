Dentistry Needs

Daily brushing and flossing isn’t enough to ensure the best condition of your teeth. Professional cleaning removes stubborn plaque that leads to decay. Inadequate removal of plaque can also lead to gum inflammation and disease. Also at each visit, Dr. Harris can evaluate your bite and alignment of teeth. As a certified Invisalign practitioner, he’s undergone special training and can evaluate when it may be best to straighten or move teeth.

Preventative Dentistry

At each six-month check-up, our dental hygienist will professionally clean and polish your teeth with specialized equipment. Then the doctor will examine your teeth, gums, soft palate, throat and neck for abnormalities. He’ll perform digital x-rays and a cancer screening.

How to Schedule?

For routine check-ups, call the office to make an appointment or the practice accepts walk-ins, too. Please bring your ID and a copy of your insurance for our records.

Cleanings and Check-ups

At Plaque Panther Dentistry, the focus is on protecting and preserving your teeth through preventive measures. Not having your teeth professionally cleaned is detrimental to your long-term health and wellness. It’s important to be seen by a dentist every six months to correct problems before they become harder to fix.

Oral Cancer Screenings

The sooner oral cancer or precancerous lesions are detected, the better the treatment outcomes. Dr. Harris includes this screening with all check-ups as a preventive benefit to patients.

Digital X-Rays

Dr. Harris believes in the power of advanced diagnostics. He’s invested in the iTero intraoral scanner. This breakthrough equipment pushes the boundaries of obtaining the best picture of the teeth, gums, jaw and mouth. He uses the high-tech wand to capture the images, which can mean more precise outcomes.

Emergency Checkup

Dr. Harris can diagnose a chronic or sudden toothache during an emergency check-up. Or if you have a cracked, chipped or broken tooth from an accident, or loose crown, he will see you right away. Walk-ins are welcome and you can put your trust in him to identify create a plan to address your unplanned problem.

What Qualifies as an Emergency?

Dr. Harris can fix emergency situations by bonding or tooth replacement or addressing infection. If you’ve cracked a tooth by biting on something hard, lost a tooth by getting hit in a game or falling, or have experienced a sudden and painful toothache, he will see you right away. You shouldn’t wait to address these issues.

Cavity Control & Fillings

Prevention is key at Plaque Panther Dentistry. Dr. Harris and his staff educate people about best brushing and flossing practices to avoid plaque and decay. When a tooth does become decayed, when detected by digital x-rays, he uses composite filling to ensure the longest life and function of the tooth.

Dental Bridge

To replace missing teeth, dental bridges fill in the space between existing teeth. This restores chewing function as well as esthetics. A bridge of one or more missing teeth is attached to the other teeth to bridge the gap.

Crowns

When a tooth is broken, cracked, chipped, or compromised by decay, Dr. Harris performs a crown procedure. During the first visit, he makes and impression, conservatively reduces the tooth, and places a temporary crown on. At the second visit, he places the permanent crown. Crowns are necessary to support the bite as well as the look of your smile. If a tooth is lost and not replaced, that could cause teeth to shift, bone loss and chance of infection.
