Plaque Panther

Dentistry

You’ve probably never been to a dentist whose AKA is Plaque Panther. The character is all in good fun and meaningful too. Dr. Harris says the name is an homage to the Marvel Comics superhero Black Panther . The movie made him feel more like he belonged with that class of superhero. The same ideals of fighting for what’s right spill over to his practice of fighting for and protecting his patients’ oral health. The vibe of the dental practice is approachable, and energetic spirit to his mission of saving smiles for all ages and stages of life.

Attention to detail affects the level of passionate care at Plaque Panther dentistry. Dr. Harris wanted to study dentistry most of his life.

HARRIS, DDS

DENTISTRY TEAM

Kyanna Harris

Kyanna was born and raised in South Texas and graduated from Jourdanton High School, which is located in a small town south of San Antonio. After graduating high school, she continued her education and obtained a liberal arts degree from a community college in San Antonio.

Kyanna then went on to earn her dental hygiene degree from Coastal Bend College in Beeville. Always with a smile and a kind word, Kyanna enjoys working with the Plaque Panther team and helping all her patients receive optimum dental care. During Kyanna s free time, she likes to spend time with her friends and family.

Hannah Lopez

Embarking on her journey in dental care in 2018, Hannah was driven by a desire to make a difference in patients’ lives. Graduating from Austin Dental Assistant School, she has continually expanded her knowledge and skills through advanced training and certifications, ensuring our patients receive the highest standard of care. Outside of the dental office, Hannah enjoys arts and crafts, going to arcades, bicycling and spending time with friends and family.

Vivian Langston

Vivian is a dedicated dental assistant who is passionate about supporting patients at every stage of their dental journey. She takes pride in creating a calm, welcoming environment and enjoys helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and genuinely cared for. Being part of a team that improves oral health and builds confident smiles is what motivates her each day. Outside of the practice, Vivian enjoys long walks, doing puzzles, and crocheting to unwind and relax, she also cherishes spending quality time with family, who are at the heart of everything she does.

OUR OFFICE

Within our offices, you’ll find an esthetic that’s modern yet homey, with splashes of black, gray, white and gold. We want you to feel comfy and welcome. Our six operatories offer a host of specialized equipment and diagnostics. Conveniently located in a new medical building near the highway, Dr. Harris is thrilled to open his practice in his hometown.

Our Comfort Menu:
