Shelbie

Shelbie has been a patient here at CFD since she was a young girl. Shelbie graduated Tulsa Technology’s Dental Assisting program in 2017 and did her internship requirements here with us. We loved her so much we didn’t let her leave!!

lorri

Often called by her nickname Tex, Lorri has been with us since 2009.

She graduated from Amarillo College with a Dental Hygiene degree in 1996. She is a very thorough and informative hygienist. Lorri has been with us through thick and thin. She is married and has two boys in college at OSU. She enjoys gardening and taking care of all her animals.

allyson

Allyson is a Collinsville girl, born and raised. She started working with us as a dental assistant just after graduating Green Country Dental Assistant school in 2007. She then decided to further her education and attended Tulsa Community College Dental Hygienist program in 2011. Ally found her way back to us, as a Dental Hygienist, in early 2015. She is a joy to work with, in every way, and is amazing at her job! Her free time is spent raising her two beautiful young girls.

Marley

Marley has been with us since 2023. She graduated from the dental hygiene program at OU in 2014. Marley is from a small town in western Oklahoma. She is married and has two boys. In her free time, she enjoys being outside and hiking.

Miranda

Miranda was raised in Owasso and now lives here in Collinsville. She graduated Tulsa Technology’s Dental Assistant School in 2015 and has been working with us since 2018. She keeps our day organized and on track, always making sure we are ready for our patients. She is married with two small children, and loves to hunt, fish and raise Labradors.
