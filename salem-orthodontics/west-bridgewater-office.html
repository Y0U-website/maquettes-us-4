West Bridgewater Office

This is where it all started. Salem Orthodontics started in this location back in 2005, strategically situated in the center of West Bridgewater at Howard Farms Market Place Plaza at 120 West Center Street (Route 106).

Salem Orthodontics is across the street from West Bridgewater Middle-Senior High School, Spring Street Elementary School. West Bridgewater Public Library and West Bridgewater Police Department are only steps away.

Our office is right next door to Blue Stone Bank, Let’s Talk Speak and Occupational Therapy Services, and Dental Associates of East Bridgewater. Patients will find ample parking to help you get in and out of the office with ease.
