Meet the Dentists

Dr. Siv

Dr. Siv earned her D.M.D. from Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2017. Since then, she has been delivering high-quality dental care across the greater Detroit area, helping patients put their best smiles forward with confidence and comfort.

Her passion for dentistry began in childhood during visits to the dental office with her mother—sparking a lifelong interest in how a smile can be transformed. With a natural talent for art, Dr. Siv blends creativity and precision in every procedure she performs.

She is certified by the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE) in Botox®, dermal fillers, and TMJ therapy—bringing advanced facial esthetic solutions to her patients with a focus on both function and beauty.

Dr. Siv believes in using the latest technology and techniques while creating a warm, welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel at ease. Her goal is to not only improve oral health, but also empower each patient to smile with self-confidence.

She is a proud member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Michigan Dental Association (MDA), and the Detroit District Dental Society.

Outside the office, Dr. Siv enjoys life in Northville, Michigan, with her husband, their daughter and son, and their furry companions—Dani the dog and Stewie the cat. In her free time, she loves painting, cooking, reading, traveling, and practicing yoga and meditation.

Dr. Adeeb Haque Dr. Haque attained his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. He is licensed in the state of Michigan and certified by the Northeast Regional Board. Additionally, Dr. Haque is a member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, and Oakland County Dental Society.

Dr. Haque was born and grew up in the Metro Detroit area. Infact, he still resides in Troy and continues to be an active member of the community. Dr. Haque has a strong belief in dedicating his time and efforts to improving the life of others.

Dr. Haque’s philosophy is to respect and actively listen to his patients. He is sincere and keeps his patient’s best interest in mind and strives to make patients feel comfortable. Dr. Haque is excited to be a part of the Grosse Pointe community and looks forward to meeting you!
