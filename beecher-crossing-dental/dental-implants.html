FAQs About Dental Implants

How long does the dental implant process take?

The entire process can take several months, as it involves the surgical placement of the implant, a healing period for osseointegration (where the implant fuses with the jawbone), and the placement of the final restoration.

Are dental implants painful?

The implant surgery is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during the procedure. Some discomfort is normal after the surgery, but it can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

How do I care for dental implants?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential to maintain the health of the implant and surrounding tissues.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good oral and general health are candidates for dental implants. A sufficient amount of bone in the jaw is necessary to support the implant. If you have bone loss, a bone graft may be recommended.
