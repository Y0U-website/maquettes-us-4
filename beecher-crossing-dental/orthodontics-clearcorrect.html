FAQs About ClearCorrect

How long does ClearCorrect treatment take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of the case, but most patients complete their ClearCorrect treatment within 12 to 18 months.

How often do I need to wear the aligners?

For the best results, aligners should be worn for 20 to 22 hours per day, only removing them for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing.

Is ClearCorrect suitable for all ages?

ClearCorrect is suitable for teens and adults who have fully developed teeth and jaws. It is not recommended for young children.

How do I care for my ClearCorrect aligners?

Clean your aligners daily with a soft toothbrush and mild soap, or use a special aligner cleaning solution. Avoid using hot water, as it can warp the plastic.
