FAQs About Implant Restoration

How long do implant restorations last?

The longevity of an implant restoration depends on factors like oral hygiene, the material used, and how well the restoration is maintained. With proper care, a high-quality restoration can last many years.

How do I know if my implant needs restoration?

If you notice any discomfort, looseness, or visible damage to the crown or bridge, or if your bite feels off, it’s important to schedule a consultation to evaluate the implant.

Can an implant itself be restored if damaged?

The implant post (the part embedded in the jawbone) is very durable, but if it becomes damaged, it may require more extensive treatment. In most cases, implant restoration focuses on the visible components like the crown or bridge.
