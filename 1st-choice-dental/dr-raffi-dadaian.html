Dr. Raffi Dadaian, DMD

Dr. Dadaian has completed hundreds of hours of post-doctoral continuing education, with extensive training in implant dentistry, endodontics, and cosmetic dentistry. This advanced education allows him to provide comprehensive dental care under one roof, offering patients predictable, high-quality results tailored to their individual needs.

Known for his thoughtful approach and attention to detail, Dr. Dadaian believes dentistry should be both precise and personal. He takes the time to listen carefully, educate patients on their options, and design customized treatment plans that prioritize long-term oral health, comfort, and aesthetics.

At 1st Choice Dental, Dr. Dadaian and his team utilize the latest dental technology in a calm, welcoming environment. The practice embraces a holistic and individualized approach to care, ensuring every patient feels informed, respected, and confident throughout their treatment experience.

Outside of the clinic, Dr. Dadaian enjoys spending time with his family, skiing, and participating in a wide range of outdoor activities. He is actively involved in community organizations and is deeply passionate about volunteering dental care in the Republic of Armenia, where he is committed to giving back and expanding access to quality dental treatment.
