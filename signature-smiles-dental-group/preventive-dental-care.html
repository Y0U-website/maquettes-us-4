Fluoride Treatments & Dental Sealants

Fluoride Treatments

Strengthens enamel, repairs early decay.

  • Professional-strength varnish painted on teeth
  • Hardens enamel against acid attacks
  • Repairs microscopic damage before it becomes a cavity
  • Done in five minutes at the end of a cleaning
  • Children: every 6 months, typically 100 % covered
  • High-risk adults: every 3–6 months

Dental Sealants

A physical barrier in deep molar grooves.

  • Liquid resin painted into molar grooves
  • Cured with light — painless, no drilling
  • Stops food & bacteria getting trapped
  • Best for ages 6–14 on new molars
  • Also helps cavity-prone adults
  • Lasts 5–10+ years; checked at every visit

Sealant vs. Filling

A small step today saves thousands later.

  • Sealant: $30–$60 per tooth, painless, no drilling
  • Filling: $150–$450 per tooth, anesthesia required
  • Root canal + crown: $2,000–$3,500
  • Every $1 on prevention saves $8–$50 later
  • Children under 14: sealants typically 80–100 % covered

Oral Cancer Screening at Every Exam

Warning Signs to Report Between Visits

  • A mouth sore or ulcer lasting more than 2 weeks
  • Red or white patches anywhere in the mouth
  • A lump or thickened area in the cheek or under the jaw
  • Numbness or tingling in the mouth or lips
  • Difficulty chewing or swallowing
  • Persistent hoarseness or one-sided ear pain

Common Risk Factors

  • Tobacco use of any kind
  • Heavy alcohol consumption
  • HPV infection
  • Age over 40
  • Prior oral cancer history
  • We examine lips, gums, tongue, palate, cheeks & throat

Home Care — The Other Half of Prevention

Professional visits at our Milpitas practice work best when paired with excellent daily home care. Our team provides personalized oral hygiene instruction at every visit — here is the foundation.

Proper Brushing

  • Soft-bristled brush at a 45° angle to the gumline
  • Gentle circular motions — never scrub
  • All surfaces: outer, inner, chewing, and tongue
  • Replace your brush every 3 months
  • Electric brushes remove more plaque

Daily Flossing

Where most cavities & gum disease begin.

  • Brushing cleans only about 60 % of tooth surfaces
  • Flossing reaches between teeth where decay starts
  • Curve floss into a C shape around each tooth
  • Slide gently below the gumline
  • Water flossers are great for braces & bridges
