iTero® Digital Scanner

Say goodbye to uncomfortable, gooey manual impressions. The iTero digital scanner creates a highly accurate 3D map of your teeth and bite.

  • Invisalign® Accuracy: We use these scans to create precise digital models for Invisalign, ensuring a better fit and more predictable results.
  • Restorative Excellence: For crowns and implants, digital scans are sent instantly to the lab, resulting in a perfectly manufactured tooth that fits naturally.
  • Health Tracking: With time-lapse technology, we can track how your teeth move or wear over time, allowing us to catch issues like TMJ or bite misalignment early.

PreXion 3D Eclipse

For complex procedures like dental implants, we use the PreXion 3D Eclipse. This specialized imaging system provides high-definition, 3D views of your bone structure and dental anatomy.

  • Detailed Diagnostics: It offers multiple scan modes—including Ultra-High Definition—taking as little as 9 to 18 seconds.
  • Safer Surgery: By viewing your anatomy in 3D, our doctors can plan surgeries with pinpoint accuracy, ensuring the safest and most effective results.

CariVu Cavity Finder

CariVu is a handheld, radiation-free device that uses patented transillumination (near-infrared light) technology to detect cavities (caries) and tooth cracks. It makes enamel appear transparent, causing porous lesions to absorb the light and appear dark, allowing dentists to identify decay—especially between teeth—in real-time

Deka CO2 Laser

The Deka CO2 laser is a specialized tool for soft tissue procedures, including gum therapy, root canals, and uncovering implants.

  • Gingival Health: It effectively disinfects periodontal pockets and stimulates bone regeneration, which can often save teeth that might otherwise require extraction.
  • Bloodless Procedures: The laser cauterizes as it works, meaning significantly less bleeding, less swelling, and a much more comfortable post-operative experience.
