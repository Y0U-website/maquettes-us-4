Payment Options

Option 1

CareCredit Accepted We accept CareCredit to help make dental treatment more manageable. Use it for everything from routine visits to more extensive procedures, with flexible financing options that fit your budget.

Option 2

Dental Insurance Our office accepts all insurance plans; however, we are considered out-of-network with all providers. This means coverage and reimbursement will depend on your specific plan’s out-of-network benefits. We recommend contacting your insurance company directly to find out what portion of your visit may be covered.

Option 3

In-House Coverage with Toner Smile Club Don’t have dental insurance? We’ve got you covered. Our office offers an in-house membership program, designed to fit your unique dental needs. With customizable plans and transparent pricing, we make it easy to prioritize your oral health without the hassle of traditional insurance. Ask us how Toner Smile Club can help you save on routine care and treatment!
