Bone Grafting Bone grafting is a procedure performed to increase the volume and density of your jawbone, creating a strong and stable foundation for a dental implant. This is often necessary if the jawbone has thinned due to tooth loss.

Single Tooth Dental Implants

A single tooth implant is a durable and reliable solution for replacing one missing tooth. It consists of a titanium post that acts as a tooth root, topped with a custom-made crown that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Multiple Teeth Dental Implants

When you are missing several teeth in a row, a dental implant bridge can be a great option. Instead of an implant for every missing tooth, a few strategically placed implants can support a bridge of multiple artificial teeth.

Implant Care and Maintenance

Once your implant is in place, proper care is essential for its longevity. Maintaining your implant is similar to caring for your natural teeth and involves regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups to ensure everything remains healthy.
