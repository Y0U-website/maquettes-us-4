The practice

Dr. Elena Stan, DDS is a gentle and compassionate general dentist dedicated to helping patients and families enjoy healthy, confident smiles. Originally from Romania, she earned her dental degree in 2015 before moving to the United States, where she completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) in 2023.

Dr. Stan is passionate about providing high-quality, personalized dental care for the whole family. She takes time to listen to each patient’s needs, explain treatments clearly, and create a calm, comfortable experience—especially for those who feel nervous about visiting the dentist.

Having worked throughout the Bay Area, including Contra Costa County and San Francisco, Dr. Stan combines advanced clinical training with a warm, approachable manner that helps patients feel truly cared for.

She is fluent in Romanian and Spanish, and enjoys connecting with patients from diverse backgrounds. Dr. Stan believes that a healthy smile can make a real difference in a person’s confidence and overall well-being—and she feels honored to be part of each patient’s journey toward better oral health.

Outside the office, Dr. Stan enjoys spending time with her husband, Sebastian, and their daughter, Josephine.
