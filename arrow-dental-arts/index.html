Dr. Adrian Mobilia Graduated from University of Buenos Aires, Argentina in 1991 and later form Loma Linda University, Loma Linda CA. He has been teaching Periodontics in dental schools for more than 20 years.

Adrian currently holds the position of Associate Professor in the Department of Periodontics at Loma Linda University, has research publications in dental journals, contributed to textbooks and lectured at national and international meetings.

During the past 20 years, Dr. Adrian Mobilia has focused his private practice in Periodontics, Implants and Cosmetic Dentistry. With specific training at UCLA, Dr. Mobilia is able to offer treatment for snoring and obstructive sleep apnea, helping individuals and couples to improve their quality of life.

Dr. Adrian Mobilia is member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, Tri-County Dental Society and American Dental Educators Association.
