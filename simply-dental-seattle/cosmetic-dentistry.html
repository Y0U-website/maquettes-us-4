Our Approach to Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry involves creating your ideal smile. The process is a balance between artistry and functionality, and we’ll help you select a treatment plan that meets your goals.

At Simply Dental, delivering exceptional and personalized dental care each and every day is just one reason why our office is so unique. We take the time to get to know our patients and treat each one as an individual because no dental treatment is the same for two people. We strive to exceed your expectations, not just meet them!

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are thin laminates that are bonded to the front of your teeth to improve the appearance of your smile by changing the shape, color, size or length. If you have teeth that are chipped, slightly misaligned, discolored, or perhaps irregular shape veneers may be an option. We, at Simply Dental, work closely with the lab to create veneers that will not only look aesthetic but natural.

Cosmetic Dental Bonding

Bonding is the application of a tooth-colored resin called composite to fix the shape or color of your tooth. Composite bonding can easily repair chips, fractures, decay, and discolored teeth. Unlike porcelain veneers, which are fabricated in a laboratory and require multiple visits, composites can be applied in a single office visit with minimal tooth removal.

Is Cosmetic Dentistry Covered by Dental Insurance?

The short answer: it depends. Some cosmetic procedures are, in fact, covered by dental insurance when the treatment restores damaged teeth and improves your overall oral health. Other procedures, however, are purely cosmetic and might not be covered by insurance as each company is a bit different in the coverage they provide.
