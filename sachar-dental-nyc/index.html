At Sachar Dental NYC, we practice green dentistry! Green dentistry is a general term that describes the high-tech approach to performing dentistry using the least toxic dental materials possible. We want to support a wellness approach to overall health.

Additionally this approach focuses on reducing environmental impact, reducing waste, and using the most conservative dental treatment options possible.

We believe less is more. Our goal at Sachar Dental NYC, is to opt for the least invasive dental procedures possible for any given situation. We want to preserve as much of the healthy teeth as possible.
