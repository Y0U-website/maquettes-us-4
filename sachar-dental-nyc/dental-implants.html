What are dental implants?

Dental implants are permanent solution for missing teeth. The implants is surgically placed into the jaw bone. Once the implants heals, an abutment in crown amounted on top of the implant to replace a missing tooth.

Why would I need dental implants?

Dental implants are used whenever patients of missing teeth for one of many reasons. A dental implant is a permanent solution to replace the tooth. There are many reasons why a patient may be missing a tooth including:

  • Extraction due to any underlying reason
  • Tooth decay
  • Gum disease
  • Tooth fracture and other traumas

Reasons to use dental implants instead of living with missing teeth:

  • Prevent teeth from moving into the where the tooth is missing
  • Improve chewing and jaw function
  • Aesthetics
  • Even load a forces on the remaining teeth
  • A permanent and convenience alternative compared to removable solutions
  • Does not require filing of adjacent teeth like a bridge

What are the different uses for dental implants?

  • Replace single missing tooth with single implants
  • Replace multiple missing teeth with multiple single implants
  • Replace multiple missing teeth with implant supported bridge
  • Anchor an implant supported denture , including All-on-Four Denture

How are dental implants done?

The first step is to extract the problematic or diseased tooth. We will then assess the bone to ensure that it is strong enough to support and implant. In some cases, a bone graft may be necessary. The next step is to surgically placed and implant into the bone. We then wait for the bone to heal around the implant to ensure strength. We then have patients come back on subsequent visits to take molds that will be used to fabricate the tooth replacements, called implant crowns. On the next visit, we mounted the crown to the implant using an abutment.
