Welcome to SDM Patient Care Clinics

Your dental team at the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine has one goal to help you be happy and healthy. As you are likely aware, oral health is essential to general health and well-being at every stage of life. For this reason, our goal is to provide you with outstanding oral health care and to make each and every visit pleasant and comfortable.

Your dental team will be friendly, caring and qualified. You can rest easy knowing that our patient care clinics offer a wide variety of oral health care treatments, including the latest advances in dental therapy, in a relaxed and modern atmosphere. You will receive high-quality care at an excellent value.

We would love to speak with you! Call the SIU School of Dental Medicine with any questions you might have or if you would like to schedule an appointment. You can reach us directly at 618-474-7000. We look forward to hearing from you!
