Patient Care Coordinator

Mary

With three decades of experience in dentistry, Mary brings a rare, well-rounded perspective to her role as Patient Care Coordinator at Smiles of Winter Haven. Having worked as both a Dental Assistant and a Sterilization Technician over the course of her career, she understands the inner workings of a dental practice from every angle — and that depth of knowledge is something patients feel from their very first interaction with her.

Mary is the kind of person who makes the complicated feel simple. She has a true gift for breaking down the often confusing world of dental insurance, ensuring patients leave every conversation fully informed about their coverage, their options, and what to expect. Her fluency in both front- and back-office operations means she’s always one step ahead, quietly making sure every visit goes smoothly for patients and staff alike.

Originally from Miami, Mary was drawn to the slower pace and natural beauty of Central Florida — and she hasn’t looked back. When she’s not at the office, you’ll find her outdoors, soaking in the tranquility that first brought her here. It’s that same calm, grounded presence she carries into her work every day, making patients feel at ease from the very first phone call.

Dental Assistant

Omayra

Omayra bought more than two decades of experience in dentistry when she joined our team in 2025. She earned her Associate degree in Dental Assisting from Ponce Paramedical School in Puerto Rico and holds advanced training in Expanded Functions, equipping her to provide skilled, efficient support across a wide range of procedures. Her extensive background spans multiple dental offices, where she has consistently been known for delivering exceptional care with a genuine commitment to patient comfort.

For Omayra, dentistry is far more than a profession, it’s a calling. She finds deep fulfillment in helping people and takes pride in making every patient feel truly seen, safe, and at ease. Whether greeting someone at the start of their appointment or guiding them through treatment, she brings the same warmth, attentiveness, and clinical expertise to every interaction. Her goal is simple: to ensure that every patient leaves feeling better than when they arrived.

Originally from Ponce, Puerto Rico, Omayra’s journey has taken her through Chicago and North Carolina before she and her husband made Florida home. Outside the office, she loves spending time with her family, soaking up the beach, and staying active. Omayra is fluent in both English and Spanish.

Dental Assistant

Adrienne

For Adrienne, the reward has always been the moment a treatment plan comes full circle. That same warmth and joy she brings to family life is exactly what patients experience every time they see her at our office.
