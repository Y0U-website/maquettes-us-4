The practice

At Smiles of Winter Haven, we are genuinely grateful to our patients for choosing our practice. We treat patients the way we want to be treated. We work hard to build trust and lasting relationships with our patients.

As a patient-centered practice, we consider our patient’s best interests first when making treatment recommendations. Our patients rely upon us for well-explained treatment plans including various financial options with no judgment and no pressure.

Even before the COVID crisis, patient safety has always been our top priority. We stay current with safety advisories from the American Dental Association (ADA), US Centers for Disease Control & Prevention (CDA), and the Occupational Health & Safety Administration (OSHA).

You can rely on Smiles of Winter Haven to practicing rigorous infection control measures to ensure the safety of our patients and our team.

Our practice follows a patient-centered model of care. This means ensuring maximum benefit to our patients when recommending treatment approaches. Your best interest is the most important factor in all treatment decisions.

We treat our patients the way we want our loved ones to be treated when they visit a health care provider’s office. You can expect a warm welcome, a comfortable and comforting environment, clearly explained treatment recommendations, and a dental team that listens to you. We strive to build lasting relationships with our patients.
