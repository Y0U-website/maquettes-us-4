Our Adult Orthodontic Services

Invisalign for Adults

Invisalign is one of the most popular options for adult patients looking for a discreet and flexible way to straighten teeth. These clear aligners are virtually invisible, removable, and customized to fit comfortably in your mouth. Ideal for busy professionals and parents, Invisalign allows you to improve your smile without disrupting your daily routine.

You can eat what you want, maintain your regular oral hygiene, and feel confident during meetings, events, and social outings. At Aloha Orthodontics, our orthodontist for adults is a certified Invisalign provider and has helped countless adult patients achieve beautiful results without metal brackets or wires.

Traditional Braces

While Invisalign is an excellent option for many, some adult cases require more comprehensive treatment. We also offer modern braces for adults, including ceramic (tooth-colored) and low-profile metal brackets. These systems are more comfortable and discreet than braces of the past, and they deliver excellent results for even complex alignment issues.

Our team will help you determine the best braces solution based on your orthodontic needs and desired outcomes.

Teeth Whitening

A brighter smile can make a world of difference in your confidence. That’s why we offer professional teeth whitening services to complement your orthodontic care. Whether it’s after braces or during your Invisalign journey, our whitening treatments safely lift stains and enhance your smile’s overall appearance.

Our whitening solutions are stronger and more effective than over-the-counter products, delivering results in just a few sessions under the guidance of a dental professional.

Two-Phase Treatment for Lifelong Results

In some cases, adult patients who experienced early orthodontic treatment in childhood may require two-phase treatment to maintain or enhance results. This approach ensures that both functional and aesthetic aspects of your smile are fully addressed, especially if previous treatments didn’t resolve bite or alignment issues.

We also welcome adults who have undergone restorative dental work, such as implants or bridges, and need orthodontic care to support overall oral health.
