Cleanings & Exams

Keeping your teeth and gums clean is the foundation of good oral health. We are always accepting new patients .

We are never judgmental about your dental conditions. Join us in our office for a complete and thorough examination in order to determine your current level of dental health.

Emergencies We are here for you when you need it most, helping you get out of pain and get repaired.

We make every effort to get you seen in our office at our first available appointment. Most patients can be seen on the same day, getting you the relief you need from your problem.

Dentures & Partials Dr. Linda Wehner is well known for her skills in denture design and fit.

An ill-fitting denture is as good as no denture at all. Come get your evaluation and treatment plan from one of the most outstanding denture designers in Southern Indiana .

Dental Implants Dental implants and implant supported dentures are fast becoming the standard of care for replacing missing teeth.

Let the doctors at Clifty Family Dental evaluate your condition to see if dental implants are right for you. We use high-tech computer aided software to diagnose and plan your case.

Braces Sometimes teeth need a little straightening . If you have a bit of a crooked smile, come in for a consultation.

If you have always wanted a better-looking, straighter smile, you owe it to yourself to consider having treatment to correct problems like crowding, open spaces or crooked teeth.

Crowns & Bridges When a tooth becomes too damaged for a filling to repair, it’s time to start thinking about a crown .

Bridges are crowns that are used to anchor a fake tooth over the space where a tooth is missing. Bridges can be an excellent option for replacing a short span of missing teeth.

Bone Grafts Good bone structure is important to so many dental issues. Bone grafting can be a game-changer.

We hate to lose teeth. Fortunately we have the ability to save and restore the bone. Keeping your bone could mean everything when considering the future of your dental care.

Fillings Got a cavity? Get a filling while it’s small, and prevent a small problem from becoming worse.

The doctors and expanded duties dental assistants at Clifty Family Dental are highly skilled at creating great looking, tooth-colored fillings that blend in beautifully with your natural teeth.
