Dental Implants for Missing Teeth

  • Improved Oral Health: Unlike traditional bridgework, dental implants do not require adjacent teeth to be filed down. This helps preserve more of your natural tooth structure.
  • Durability: Implants are very durable and will last many years. With good care, many implants last a lifetime.
  • Improved Eating and Speaking: Implants are securely anchored, allowing you to eat and speak with confidence.
  • Convenience: Implants are maintenance-free and eliminate the embarrassing inconvenience of removing dentures.

Ideal Candidates for Dental Implants

While dental implants are an excellent solution for a wide range of dental issues, they are not suitable for everyone. Dr. Joseph Fennell will conduct a thorough evaluation to determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants. Factors considered include:

  • Adequate Bone Density: Sufficient bone in your jaw is needed to support the implant.
  • Good Oral Health: Healthy gums free of periodontal disease are crucial.
  • Overall Health: Conditions such as diabetes or heart disease do not automatically exclude you from getting dental implants, but they may impact the healing process.

The Dental Implant Procedure at Piedmont Family Dentistry

The process of getting dental implants may involve several stages, but our team works to make each step as comfortable and efficient as possible. The implant process typically includes:

  • Initial Consultation: Discussion of your dental history, health assessment, and treatment goals.
  • Treatment Planning: Tailored plan using advanced imaging techniques to guide the implant procedure.
  • Implant Placement: Surgical insertion of the implant into your jawbone.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: The bone around the implant heals and integrates with the implant in a process that can take several months.
  • Placement of the Abutment: Installation of the post that will hold the new tooth.
  • Restoration: Once healing is complete, the new teeth are made and fitted to the post portion of the anchor.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are dental implants? Implants are titanium posts placed in the jaw that act as artificial tooth roots, topped with a natural-looking crown to replace a missing tooth.
  • How long do implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them one of the most durable tooth-replacement options.
  • Am I a candidate for implants? Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone are candidates. We&#x27;ll evaluate your bone and gums at a consultation.
  • Are implants better than dentures? Implants are fixed, function like natural teeth, and prevent bone loss, while dentures are removable. The right choice depends on your needs and budget.
