Dental Implants in Solana Beach

Are you missing teeth, and wish you could show off your smile or chew without concerns about the function of your teeth? Titanium dental implants are a long-lasting alternative to bridges and dentures. Dr. Belderes uses this advanced technology to replace a missing tooth or teeth, letting you have the full use of your teeth and a natural look.

Ready to Get Started?

When you’ve decided you’re ready for dental implants, and we’ve determined that you’re a suitable candidate, we’ll start you on a three-stage process:

If your tooth is already gone, we’ll move right into the second step of placing the implant. The visits are spread out over a comfortable 3-6-month period, depending on the state of your oral health. After the second stage, we offer you a temporary option to cover the implant before the permanent crown is placed so that you don’t have to wait.

  • Extraction of tooth material and letting the area heal
  • Placing the implant and allowing the area to heal
  • Placing a crown on top of the implant

A Quick Recovery and Fast Healing

Dr. Belderes has trained extensively in providing dental implants for patients. This device works with the bone, not the nerves, allowing us to place the implant comfortably. Your home care after the placement of the implant involves taking an over-the-counter pain medication for any discomfort you may experience.
