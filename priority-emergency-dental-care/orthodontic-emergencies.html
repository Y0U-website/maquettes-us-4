Orthodontic Emergencies

  • Broken Wires : Wires that are poking into the gums, cheeks, or tongue.
  • Lost Separators : Separators that have fallen out prematurely.
  • Mouth Sores : Irritations or sores caused by braces rubbing against the inside of the mouth.
  • Trauma : Injuries to the mouth or teeth that impact orthodontic appliances.

Professional Treatment Options

At Priority Emergency Dental Care, we offer comprehensive treatment options to address all types of orthodontic emergencies:

  • Reattaching Loose Brackets or Bands : Our dentist will clean the area and reattach the bracket or band securely.
  • Repairing or Replacing Broken Wires : We will trim, adjust, or replace wires to eliminate discomfort and ensure proper alignment.
  • Replacing Lost Separators : New separators will be placed to continue the necessary space creation between teeth.
  • Treating Mouth Sores : We can provide medicated gels or rinses to promote healing and comfort.
  • Managing Trauma : In cases of trauma, we will assess the damage and take appropriate steps to stabilize and treat the affected teeth and appliances.

Immediate Steps to Take

If you experience an orthodontic emergency, there are a few steps you can take to manage the situation until you can see a dentist:

  • Loose Brackets or Bands : If a bracket or band is still attached to the wire, leave it in place and cover it with orthodontic wax if it is causing irritation. If it has come off completely, save it and bring it to your appointment.
  • Broken Wires : Use a pencil eraser to push a poking wire into a more comfortable position or cover it with orthodontic wax.
  • Lost Separators : Contact us as soon as possible for advice on whether you need a replacement before your next appointment.
  • Mouth Sores : Rinse your mouth with warm salt water to reduce discomfort, and apply orthodontic wax to the brackets causing irritation.
  • Trauma : For serious injuries or if a tooth has been displaced, seek immediate dental care.

What to Expect During Your Visit

  • Prompt Attention : We understand the urgency of dental pain and strive to see emergency patients as quickly as possible.
  • Thorough Diagnosis : Our dentists will perform a detailed examination to determine the exact cause of your pain.
  • Customized Treatment Plan : We will develop a personalized plan to relieve your pain and address the underlying issue.
  • Comfort and Care : We prioritize your comfort and will explain every step of the treatment process to ensure you feel at ease.

Preventing Orthodontic Emergencies

While not all orthodontic emergencies can be prevented, certain precautions can help minimize the risk:

  • Avoid Hard and Sticky Foods : Foods like nuts, popcorn, hard candies, and chewing gum can damage braces.
  • Wear a Mouthguard : Use a mouthguard during sports or physical activities to protect your braces and teeth.
  • Maintain Good Oral Hygiene : Proper brushing and flossing help keep your teeth and braces clean and in good condition.
  • Regular Check-Ups : Attend all scheduled orthodontic appointments to ensure your treatment is progressing smoothly and to address any potential issues early.
