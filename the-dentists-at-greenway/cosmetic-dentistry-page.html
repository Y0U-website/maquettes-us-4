Frequently Asked Questions About Cosmetic Treatments

How long do porcelain veneers typically last? With proper oral hygiene, routine dental checkups, and a commitment to protecting your teeth from nighttime grinding, high-quality porcelain veneers can last anywhere from 10 to 15 years or even longer.

Is professional teeth whitening safe for my enamel? Yes, professional whitening systems used under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist are entirely safe for your tooth enamel and significantly reduce the sensitivity often associated with over-the-counter whitening strips.
