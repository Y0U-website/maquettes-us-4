How I Practice Dentistry

I take a methodical and compassionate approach to dentistry, combining modern digital tools with thoughtful long-term treatment planning. Whether I’m placing a same-day crown, designing an implant-supported bridge, or simply walking someone through their options for the first time, my goal is to make sure you leave feeling informed and confident.

I especially enjoy building relationships with patients and helping those who may have had negative dental experiences in the past feel at ease again.
