Key Benefits

Our restorations are crafted from high-quality ceramic or zirconia, designed to match the shape, color, and function of your natural teeth.

  • Stronger bite stability – Enjoy better chewing power with no slipping or shifting
  • Preserves jawbone – Implants help prevent bone loss in areas where teeth are missing
  • No need to alter healthy teeth – Unlike traditional bridges, nearby teeth remain untouched
  • Natural look and feel – Custom-made for a seamless appearance and comfortable fit
  • Long-lasting results – With proper care, implants and restorations can last decades

What to Expect

For patients needing a faster solution for a single damaged tooth, ask about our same-day CEREC crowns , which can be completed in just one visit.

  • Initial evaluation – We perform a full assessment with digital imaging to determine if implant-supported restoration is right for you.
  • Implant placement – A specialist places the dental implant(s) into the jawbone.
  • Healing period – Your jawbone fuses with the implant over several months (osseointegration).
  • Custom restoration – Once healed, we attach your crown or bridge for a strong, natural result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a traditional bridge? ‍

Traditional bridges are supported by adjacent teeth, which often require reshaping. Implant-supported bridges are anchored by implants, offering a more stable and conservative option.

How long does the entire process take? ‍

The full process, including implant healing, may take several months. We’ll provide a clear timeline based on your treatment plan.

Does it hurt to get implants? ‍

Implant placement is done with local anesthesia and is generally well-tolerated. Most patients experience only mild discomfort afterward.

Is this option better than partial dentures? ‍

Yes. Implant-supported restorations are fixed in place and don’t rely on clasps or adhesives, offering more comfort and function.

Will my insurance cover implant-supported restorations? ‍

Some plans cover part of the procedure. We’ll help verify your coverage and offer flexible payment options if needed.
