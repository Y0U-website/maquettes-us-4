Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement due to their durability and long-term oral health benefits:

If you’re not sure whether implants are right for you, we’ll walk you through your options, timeline, and what to expect based on your oral health and goals.

  • Permanent and secure – Unlike removable dentures, implants stay firmly in place
  • Preserve bone health – Implants prevent bone loss by stimulating the jaw
  • Look and feel natural – Designed to match the appearance and function of real teeth
  • No impact on adjacent teeth – Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering healthy teeth
  • Restore full chewing power – Eat confidently without shifting or discomfort

Our Implant Process

At Peak Dental Utah, we focus on precision, clarity, and long-term success. We partner with trusted local oral surgeons for placement when needed, and handle the full restoration phase in our office.

If you&#x27;re seeking a faster solution, explore our same-day CEREC crowns to see how we streamline the restorative process for qualifying cases.

  • Evaluation and planning – We perform a detailed exam and digital imaging to determine candidacy and map out your treatment.
  • Implant placement – The titanium post is placed into your jawbone by a specialist or referred provider.
  • Healing period – The implant integrates with your bone over a few months (a process called osseointegration).
  • Custom restoration – Once healed, we place a lifelike crown, bridge, or denture for a complete and natural result.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

We’ll perform a full assessment to determine if implants are the best fit for your needs—or recommend alternative treatments such as fixed bridges or custom dentures if preferred.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have healthy gums and bone (or can undergo bone grafting if needed)
  • Are in good general health
  • Don’t smoke or are willing to quit for successful healing

How much do dental implants cost? ‍

The cost varies depending on the number of implants and type of restoration. We provide clear, upfront pricing and offer financing options when needed.

Do implants hurt? ‍

Most patients report minimal discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction. Local anesthesia and sedation options help ensure comfort during and after surgery.

How long does it take to complete the process? ‍

The full process can take several months, including healing time. We’ll discuss your timeline and options during your consultation.

Will insurance cover implants? ‍

Some insurance plans offer partial coverage for implant-supported restorations. We’ll help you check your benefits and provide estimates in advance.

What if I don’t have enough bone for implants? ‍

In many cases, bone grafting can restore implant eligibility. We’ll determine this with digital imaging during your evaluation.
