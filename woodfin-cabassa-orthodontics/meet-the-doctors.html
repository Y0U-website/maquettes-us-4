The Terrific Trio Combining Expertise With Technology to Provide Patients With Beautiful Smiles

Dr. Sal Cabassa

“I love my work as an orthodontist. My greatest joy comes from teaming with patients to create the perfect smile, one that will increase their self-esteem, boost their self-confidence, and transform their lives. I believe in delivering the best orthodontic care possible and enjoy the one-on-one interaction I have with my patients. I like getting to know them as individuals with fascinating lives. We learn from each other, and that is incredibly rewarding.”

Dr. Cabassa was born in North Miami Beach and moved to Gulf Breeze in 1972. He has lived here for more than 40 years. Together with his wife Christy, Dr. Cabassa has two children, Jake and Kate. When not caring for his orthodontic patients, he enjoys spending time golfing, fishing, and boating. He is a long-term member of the Gulf Breeze Rotary Club and supports many local charity organizations. Dr. Cabassa belongs to Christ Episcopal Church in Pensacola and is an active member of his local community.

  • University of Florida, Doctor of Dental Medicine, 1990
  • University of West Florida, Bachelor of Science, Biology
  • University of Florida, Master of Science, Orthodontic Specialization, 1995
  • American Dental Association
  • American Association of Orthodontists
  • Florida Dental Association
  • Southern Association of Orthodontists
  • Florida Association of Orthodontists
  • EscaRosa Dental Society
  • College of Diplomates of the American Board of Orthodontics

Dr. Greg Woodfin

“Hard to believe I’ve been a practicing orthodontist for more than 33 years now. During that time, I have had the opportunity to work with many patients, creating smiles that not only improve their appearance but also enhance their self-confidence and self-esteem. Each patient’s unique orthodontic needs and individual personalities make my professional life rewarding and fulfilling. One of the primary reasons I chose to become an orthodontist is because it is an area of dental care that patients seek out not just because of necessity, but because they want to change their lives.”

Dr. Woodfin was born and raised in Columbus, Georgia, and moved to Pensacola in 1989. Together with his wife, Tara, he has four adult children - three daughters and a son, and five grandchildren. When he is not at work, chances are that you can find Dr. Woodfin spending time with his family, playing with his grandchildren, exercising, or occasionally driving an old car.

Because he believes in giving back to the community, he currently serves or has served on many boards and committees, such as for the YMCA and the Boy Scouts. Additionally, he is an active member of the First United Methodist Church in Pensacola. Dr. Woodfin is particularly proud of working with other professionals to start an annual golf tournament that raises money for PSC students who do not qualify for financial aid but still need tuition assistance.

God has blessed Dr. Woodfin with a wonderful personal and professional life. He not only has an incredible family but also patients who provide him with opportunities to get to know them as they work together to improve their smiles and their lives. For all of this, he is very grateful!

Dr. Sarah Howle

"I love my job because orthodontics uniquely combines science with artistic creativity, in which we can meaningfully impact the lives of others. My own two-phase orthodontic journey as a child sparked my interest in this field, due to the positive difference it made in my life- I knew I wanted to give that experience to others. What I didn’t expect was how much my patients would shape and inspire me in return."

Dr. Sarah Howle is a native of Dothan, Alabama. She graduated summa cum laude from the University of South Alabama with a degree in Psychology before earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree from the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB). After dental school, Dr. Howle pursued her orthodontic training at the University of Michigan, where she earned her Master’s degree in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics.
