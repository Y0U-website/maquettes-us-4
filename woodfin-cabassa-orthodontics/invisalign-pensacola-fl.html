How Does Invisalign Work?

If Invisalign suits your needs, our orthodontist in Pensacola, FL, will create a personalized treatment plan. Advanced digital imaging technology, such as 3D or intraoral scans, will be used to create a precise digital model of your teeth. Based on the treatment plan, a series of custom-made aligners are designed for your teeth. These aligners are made from a clear, smooth, and BPA-free plastic material. They are virtually invisible when worn, making them an attractive option for those who desire a discreet orthodontic solution.

Once your aligners are ready, our orthodontist will guide you on how to wear and care for them. You’ll wear each set of aligners for about two weeks, 20-22 hours a day, removing them only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing. The aligners are designed to shift your teeth into their desired positions gradually. The aligners exert gentle and controlled pressure on your teeth, moving them slightly with each new set.

The treatment plan is based on the predetermined movement of your teeth from the initial digital scan. You’ll have periodic check-up appointments with our orthodontist throughout your Invisalign treatment to monitor your progress. These appointments allow our orthodontist to ensure that your teeth are moving as planned and make any necessary adjustments to your treatment.

The Benefits of Invisalign

Traditional metal braces can be painful and unsightly. Invisalign aligners in Pensacola, FL, are virtually invisible when worn. Made from a clear, smooth plastic material, they blend in seamlessly with your natural teeth. This feature allows you to undergo orthodontic treatment without attracting attention, making Invisalign a popular choice among adults and teens.

With Invisalign, you do not have to worry about food getting stuck in your brackets or wires because you can remove your aligners to eat, drink, brush, and floss. You also do not have to change how you brush your teeth or worry about brushing around brackets and wires.
