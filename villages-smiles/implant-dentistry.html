Optimal tooth replacement with dental implants

Following the loss of a permanent tooth or several teeth. These problems can adversely affect your quality of life, including your ability to speak or eat the foods you enjoy. The loss of a tooth or teeth can also affect your appearance as well as your bite. As a result, the surrounding teeth become less healthy, leading to a heightened risk of wear and tear, and eventual loss .

Providing functional support for the tooth is one of the most effective ways to address this issue with dental implants. Dental implants are titanium posts placed strategically in the jawbone that provide the same functional support as the natural root of the tooth. An artificial tooth is connected to the post once this fusion is complete, and the patient is able to have a bright, healthy smile again. All dental implants are uniquely suited to the needs of each patient.

You can count on us to provide you with the highest quality dental care available. This includes regular periodontal maintenance to repair, correct, and prevent periodontal disease , as well as placing dental implants. In addition, we are committed to providing you with as much information about your oral health as possible.
