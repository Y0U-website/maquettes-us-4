Restorative Dentistry

Restoring your teeth to good form and function.

At Herwig DDS, we offer a range of Restorative Dentistry services that include: TMJ Therapy, Dental Implants, Tooth Colored Resin Fillings, Complete & Partial Dentures and Endodontic Therapy. Visit our Lenexa or Paola office for all of your restorative dental care needs.

Complete & Partial Dentures

Sometimes when patients are missing more than one or all of their teeth the best and healthiest way to restore them is through partial or complete dentures. This can be a very time consuming process that takes many steps. Dr. Herwig takes the care necessary to work with his patients to ensure new dentures fit and look like your natural teeth.

Dental Implants

Dental implants have quickly become the superior method of tooth replacement. Not only do they restore your tooth appearance, but they also restore the function. The procedure is performed by surgically replacing the root of the tooth with a titanium dental implant directly into the jaw bone. Dental implants can replace a single tooth, multiple teeth, or restore full dental arches.

TMJ Therapy

TMJ issues can be very damaging for many and can cause people to suffer with jaw aches, headaches and/or facial pain. With the experience and specialty knowledge passed down from his father, Robert Herwig, DDS, Dr. Andrew customizes a treatment plan for each patient to deliver the best possible results in order to reduce and treat TMJ pain.

Tooth Colored Resin Fillings

Dental materials have come a long way from the old “silver fillings”. Dr. Herwig uses only the best natural looking filling materials available to restore tooth surfaces. Your fillings will be strong, long-lasting and pearly white!

Endodontic Therapy

Endodontic Therapy, aka a Root Canal, can be the best way to treat infected teeth. The process involves using special instruments to remove the infected nerve tissue from inside of the tooth, cleaning the canal spaces and filling the spaces with a special material called gutta-purcha. This treatment allows you to restore your tooth without having to remove it due to infection. Signs that you may need a root canal include: sensitivity when eating, lingering cold sensitivity and/or throbbing pain. If you are experiencing any of these symptoms call us ASAP!
