Removable Snap-On Implant Dentures $8,000 per arch (up to 4 implants) · as low as $105/mo* Four dental implants are placed strategically in the arch to anchor a custom denture that snaps securely into place. Unlike traditional dentures, snap-on implants don’t slip, don’t require adhesive, and prevent the jawbone loss that makes dentures increasingly uncomfortable over time.

The denture can be removed each night for cleaning — making oral hygiene simple and effective.
