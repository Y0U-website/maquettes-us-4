Composite Fillings

A composite filling is natural-looking, making it a much more desirable alternative to metal options. Patients who have previously had large fillings may want to replace them with composite or a dental crown. Patients who need fillings can use composite to repair their teeth while ensuring their smile remains unmarred by dark spots or noticeable metal.

Every composite filling is carefully crafted, molded, and shaped to fit your unique tooth structure. After it’s placed, your dentist will polish the tooth and ensure that it is completely restored to its original appearance.

Tooth Whitening

Whitening your smile is one of the most impactful and easiest cosmetic dental procedures. This simple treatment can be done in an hour or two in our office and is completely non-invasive.

A whitening agent lifts years of stains and discoloration from your teeth to return them to their brightest natural color. Teeth bleaching is also an option for those who want to lighten their natural smile by several shades.

Please note that teeth whitening and bleaching are only effective on natural tooth enamel. When discussing your teeth-whitening options, your dentist will carefully consider the impact of fillings, veneers, crowns, implants, bridges, and any other treatments you have had.

Invisalign®

Invisalign is an alternative to braces that can straighten your teeth without anyone noticing. Using SmartTrack, a special type of dental plastic, Invisalign brings teeth into greater alignment in six to 24 months.

For patients with crooked teeth, spaces between teeth, crossbites, underbites, or overbites, Invisalign could be an effective way to get their desired results.

We will examine your mouth and take images of your teeth when you come in for a consultation to determine if Invisalign is the best option.
