Healthy gums, lasting smiles

Dr. Hsieh is a board-certified periodontist — gum health and orthodontics under one roof means your whole smile is cared for in one place.

Periodontal disease

Gum disease is an infection of the tissues that support your teeth — a leading cause of adult tooth loss. Early signs include bleeding gums, swelling, and persistent bad breath. Caught early, it is very treatable.

Nonsurgical therapy

The first line of defense: deep-cleaning procedures such as scaling and root planing that remove plaque and tartar below the gumline, giving your gums a chance to heal.

Dental implants

A lasting replacement for missing teeth — a titanium post that fuses with the bone and supports a natural-looking crown, without altering neighboring teeth.

Cosmetic surgery

Reshape your gumline for a balanced, confident smile — evening out uneven gums or revealing more of short-looking teeth.

Gum grafting

Restore receded gums by covering exposed roots — reducing sensitivity, protecting against further recession, and improving appearance.

Regeneration

Advanced techniques that help your body regrow bone and tissue lost to periodontal disease, rebuilding a healthy foundation for your teeth.

Crown lengthening

Reshape excess gum (and sometimes bone) to expose more of a tooth — for a gummier smile fix or to prepare a tooth for a crown.

Mini-implants

Tiny temporary anchors that give orthodontic tooth movement precise, reliable support — placed and removed simply in the office.

New patients

Your first periodontal visit includes a comprehensive exam of your gums, bone, and bite, plus a clear discussion of findings and options.
