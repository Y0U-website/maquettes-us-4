Meet Dr. Shreed Sista, DDS

Dr. Shreed Sista, DDS

A born and raised Houstonian, Dr. Sista traded a basketball scholarship for a career in dentistry, bringing the same discipline and drive to the treatment chair that he once brought to the court.

Dr. Sista grew up in Sugar Land, played college basketball, and spent several years working in San Francisco before returning to Texas to pursue dentistry. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center and has never stopped training since, completing extensive post-graduate coursework in both the surgical and restorative sides of implant dentistry.
