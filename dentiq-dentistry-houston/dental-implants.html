Dental Implants: A Long-Term Solution for Missing Teeth

A dental implant is built in three parts: a titanium implant post, an abutment, and a custom crown. Unlike a bridge, which relies on neighboring teeth for support, an implant stands on its own and keeps surrounding teeth intact. Unlike a denture, which can shift or feel bulky, an implant is anchored directly in the jawbone for stability that feels close to a natural tooth.

Missing teeth can affect how you eat, speak, and feel about your smile. Dental implants offer a way to restore both function and appearance, so you don&#x27;t have to choose between comfort and confidence.
