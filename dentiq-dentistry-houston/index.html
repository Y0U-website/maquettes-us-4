At Dentiq Dentistry Houston, our doctors combine advanced training in implant, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry with a genuinely warm chairside manner. They take the time to listen, explain your options in plain language, and build a plan that fits your goals, your comfort, and your budget.

A Texas native who grew up in Sugar Land, Dr. Sista earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center. He believes oral medicine is always evolving, and he has completed extensive post-graduate training in both the surgical and restorative sides of implant dentistry to stay at the forefront of the field.

Dr. Tag earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology at the University of Houston before receiving her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Florida. She spent her final year treating patients in an outreach hospital serving rural communities, and stayed active in the American Student Dental Association throughout her training.

Born and raised in Cleveland, Dr. Stergar studied Chemistry at Cleveland State University while swimming for the varsity team and winning two conference titles. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Texas Health Science Center, where he was honored with the American College of Dentists Outstanding Student Leader award.
