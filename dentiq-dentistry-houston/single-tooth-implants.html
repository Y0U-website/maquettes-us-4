Single-Tooth Implants in Houston, TX

Replace one missing tooth without touching the teeth next to it. A titanium post, abutment, and custom crown built to match your smile.

One Missing Tooth, Replaced from Root to Crown

A single-tooth implant replaces one missing tooth without involving the teeth on either side. We place a small titanium post that acts like a new root, then give it time to fuse with your jawbone so it feels stable and secure.

Once you&#x27;re healed, we attach a custom crown that matches your smile, so you can chew, speak, and smile with confidence again.

You May Be a Good Candidate If You:

Qualifying is simpler than most people expect. A short exam and 3D scan with Dr. Sista is usually all it takes to confirm.

One gap, whether from an extraction, an injury, or a tooth that never came in

A bridge means filing down the two healthy teeth beside the gap; an implant leaves them untouched

If the jaw has thinned since the tooth was lost, the site can be rebuilt first with bone grafting

The crown is shade-matched to your neighboring teeth and anchored in bone, so it bites like the original
