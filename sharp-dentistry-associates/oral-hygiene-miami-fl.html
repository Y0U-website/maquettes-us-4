What does Excellent Oral Hygiene Encompass?

  • Professional Dental Cleanings (Prophylaxis): Our gentle and thorough cleanings remove plaque, tartar (hardened plaque), and surface stains that regular brushing can’t. This prevents cavities and keeps your gums healthy. We recommend these cleanings typically every six months.
  • Comprehensive Dental Exams: During your hygiene visit, Dr. Sharp will perform a meticulous examination of your teeth, gums, and surrounding oral tissues to detect any early signs of decay, gum disease, oral cancer, or other issues. Early detection leads to simpler, less invasive treatments.
  • Gum Disease Screening & Treatment: We meticulously check for signs of gingivitis (early gum disease) and periodontitis (advanced gum disease). Prompt diagnosis and treatment are crucial to prevent tooth loss and protect your overall health.
  • Fluoride Treatments: To strengthen tooth enamel and make it more resistant to decay, we offer professional fluoride applications, especially beneficial for children and adults prone to cavities.
  • Dental Sealants: For children and sometimes adults, sealants provide a protective barrier on the chewing surfaces of back teeth, preventing food particles and bacteria from accumulating in the grooves and causing cavities.
  • Oral Cancer Screenings: A vital part of every comprehensive exam, we screen for any suspicious lesions or changes in your oral tissues, aiding in early detection and improving treatment outcomes.
  • Personalized Oral Hygiene Instructions: Our hygienists will review and refine your brushing and flossing techniques, recommending the right tools and products for your specific needs to ensure effective home care.
  • Dietary Counseling: We provide guidance on how your diet impacts your oral health, helping you make choices that support strong teeth and healthy gums.

The Lifelong Benefits of Prioritizing your Oral Hygiene

  • Prevent Cavities and Tooth Decay: Regular cleanings and proper home care remove plaque and food particles before they can cause cavities.
  • Combat Gum Disease: By keeping plaque and tartar at bay, you significantly reduce your risk of gingivitis and periodontitis, preserving your gums and underlying bone structure.
  • Fresher Breath: Eliminating bacteria and food debris is key to preventing bad breath (halitosis).
  • Maintain Your Natural Teeth: Protecting your teeth through good hygiene means you’re less likely to need fillings, crowns, or extractions in the future.
  • Support Overall Health: Oral health is deeply connected to your systemic health. Good oral hygiene can reduce your risk of cardiovascular disease, diabetes complications, and other systemic issues.
  • Brighter, More Confident Smile: Clean, healthy teeth simply look better, enhancing your confidence and leaving a lasting positive impression.
