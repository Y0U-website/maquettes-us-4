Frequently Asked Questions

All-on-4 dental implants are a specialized implant technique that allows for the replacement of an entire arch of teeth using just four strategically placed implants. This approach offers a stable and durable solution for missing teeth while minimizing the need for additional implants.

The All-on-4 procedure can often be completed in a day. During your initial appointment, our team will place the implants and attach a temporary set of teeth. After the implants have integrated with the jawbone, a permanent prosthesis will be fitted, typically within a few months.

Caring for All-on-4 dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups are essential to maintain the health of your implants and surrounding gums. Our team will provide you with detailed instructions on how to properly care for your new smile.

The cost of All-on-4 dental implants varies based on individual needs and the complexity of the procedure. During your initial consultation, we will provide a detailed breakdown of the costs involved and discuss payment options to ensure you receive the best care within your budget.

Dental insurance coverage for All-on-4 implants can vary. We recommend checking with your insurance provider to determine your benefits. Our office can also assist you with understanding your insurance coverage and exploring financing options if needed.
