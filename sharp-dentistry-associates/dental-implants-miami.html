Over a few months, the implant fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a foundation strong enough to bite, chew, and speak with complete confidence. The result looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

Implant Options for Every Situation

Whether you are missing one tooth or an entire arch, there is an implant solution. At Sharp Dentistry, treatment is tailored to your needs:

Single-Tooth Implants

One implant and one crown replace a single missing tooth without altering the healthy teeth on either side a key advantage over a traditional bridge.

Implant-Supported Bridges

For several missing teeth in a row, a small number of implants can support a fixed bridge, restoring function without a removable appliance.

All-on-4 and Full-Arch Implants

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, as few as four implants can anchor a full, fixed set of teeth a stable, permanent alternative to traditional dentures.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Implants can secure a denture firmly in place, eliminating slipping and the need for adhesives while preserving jawbone.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Permanence - with proper care, implants can last forever, often a lifetime for the implant post itself.
  • Bone preservation - implants stimulate the jaw and prevent the bone loss that follows missing teeth.
  • Natural function - eat, speak, and smile without the limits of removable dentures.
  • Independence - single implants do not rely on grinding down neighboring healthy teeth.
  • Confidence - a restoration that looks and feels like your own tooth.

The Dental Implant Process at Sharp Dentistry

Every case begins with a thorough evaluation. From there, treatment generally follows these stages:

Consultation and 3D Planning

We examine your mouth, review your health history, and use advanced imaging to map bone and nerve position precisely planning the implant before any procedure begins.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed into the jawbone in a carefully planned position. Many patients are surprised by how comfortable and straightforward this step is.

Healing and Integration

Over the following months, the implant fuses with the bone. A temporary restoration can often be worn during this time.

Final Restoration

Once integration is complete, your custom crown, bridge, or denture crafted in our in-house lab is attached, completing your new smile.
