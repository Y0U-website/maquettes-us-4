Dental Implants

  • Multiple tooth implants – Anchor a dental bridge without altering surrounding teeth
  • Full mouth dental implants – Restore an entire arch of teeth with permanent stability
  • All-on-4 implants – A cost-effective solution that uses just four implants to support a full set of teeth
  • Implant-supported dentures – A hybrid solution that offers more comfort and retention than traditional removable dentures

The Dental Implant Process

The process of getting dental implants in Lansing begins with a consultation and a detailed examination to assess your jawbone density, oral health, and candidacy for implants.

If you’re approved for treatment, the procedure typically includes:

Throughout the process, we prioritize your comfort and safety, offering detailed instructions and support every step of the way.

  • Dental implant surgery – The titanium post is placed into the jawbone
  • Healing period – Osseointegration may take several months to complete
  • Bone graft for implant (if needed) – Some patients may require additional bone support before the implant is placed
  • Abutment placement – A small connector is attached to the implant
  • Restoration – A custom dental crown, bridge, or denture is placed over the implant

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Unlike removable dentures or bridges that rest on the gum line or use surrounding teeth for support, dental implants are anchored directly into the bone. This provides numerous advantages, such as:

Missing tooth replacement doesn’t have to be a temporary fix. Dental implants are a permanent solution that supports a lifetime of oral health.

  • Long-term durability and stability
  • Prevention of bone loss and jaw deterioration
  • No need to alter adjacent healthy teeth
  • Improved chewing and speech ability
  • A natural look and feel that restores your confidence

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on the number of implants needed, the type of restoration used, and whether any preliminary procedures like bone grafts are required. While implants may have a higher upfront cost than other options, their long lifespan and benefits often make them a more cost-effective solution in the long run.

During your consultation, we’ll provide a clear breakdown of your personalized treatment plan and go over insurance and payment options to make your care as accessible as possible.
