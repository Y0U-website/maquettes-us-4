What are Dental Implants?

Benefits of Dental Implants

Every year, more than half a million people choose dental implants to replace missing teeth and it’s easy to see why. Dental implants have the ability to restore the full function and beauty of your natural smile. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, implants replicate the entire structure of a tooth, offering unmatched stability. Read along to learn how this method of tooth replacement can rejuvenate your smile, boost your confidence, and improve your overall health.

Day-to-Day Benefits

Dental implants are a true game-changer in daily life. Here’s how they can make a difference:

  • Boosted Confidence Implants are securely anchored in your jawbone, meaning your replacement teeth stay firmly in place. No more having to worry about unwanted slipping or shifting!
  • Expanded Diet Implants restore the same strong biting power as natural tooth roots, allowing you to enjoy a wide variety of foods (like crunchy fruits, chewy breads, and hearty vegetables) that can be difficult with dentures.
  • Simple Care Forget the extra steps required to clean traditional dentures. Implants are easy to maintain! Just brush and floss as you would with your natural teeth. No special cleaners or adhesives needed.

Health Benefits

Aside from improving appearance, dental implants can support better health in these ways:

  • Healthier Mouth Because they’re easy to clean and don’t affect neighboring teeth, implants reduce the risk of gum disease and cavities. They also eliminate the need to file down healthy teeth.
  • Better Overall Health Studies show that poor oral health can raise the risk of systemic issues like heart disease, diabetes, and osteoporosis. By lowering your chance of gum disease, dental implants may help protect your general health. Plus, being able to chew nutritious foods comfortably supports a balanced diet.
  • Stronger Jawbone When you lose a tooth, your jawbone loses the stimulation it needs, leading to bone loss over time. This can weaken the foundation for other teeth and alter your facial shape, causing a sunken, aged appearance. Implants act like natural roots, stimulating the jawbone and helping to preserve your facial structure.

Long-Term Benefits

Dental implants are an investment that pays off in the long run:

  • High Success Rate While surgery can sound intimidating, dental implant placement is a safe and predictable procedure with a success rate of over 95 % when performed by an experienced dentist.
  • Durable and Long-Lasting Unlike bridges and dentures (which typically need to be replaced every 5 to 10 years), dental implants can last 30 years or longer with proper care.
  • Cost-Effective Over Time While the upfront cost is higher, implants can save you money in the long run. Plus, their durability means you won’t have to pay for frequent repairs or replacements.

Are Dental Implants Worth the Investment?

It depends on how long you plan for your new smile to last. While traditional dentures and bridges usually must be replaced roughly every 10 years, properly maintained dental implants have the potential to remain in the mouth for more than 30 years! Consequently, although the upfront cost of dental implants is greater than that of dentures, over time dentures may actually be more expensive. Does Dental Insurance Cover Dental Implants?

Dental insurance companies generally don’t cover as much of the cost of dental implants as they do other treatments. However, many are getting better about providing benefits for this service. Your plan may cover portions of the process, such as the final restoration or preliminary treatments like gum disease therapy, but you’ll need to check with your provider to learn exactly what is or is not covered.
