0 We suggest that every new patient receive a comprehensive exam with all necessary x-rays during their first appointment. This visit generally takes an hour and may not include a cleaning depending on the doctor’s findings and determined treatment plans. At this visit, your dentist will perform the following:

Review past medical history, health and systems review, medication, and allergy background

Review dental history, oral habits, oral hygiene, and the recording of a chief complaint or concern

Complete dental charting of existing restorations, visible decay, gingival (gum) examination, and recording of all other findings
