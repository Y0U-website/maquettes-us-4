The All-on-4 Treatment Process at Palisades Dental Care

From your first consultation to your final prosthesis, every step at Palisades Dental Care is designed to keep you informed, comfortable, and on track.

Consultation and Imaging

Your first visit includes a complete oral exam, digital X-rays, and 3D cone beam CT scanning. We map your bone structure in detail and use that information to design a personalized treatment plan built around your specific needs.

Extractions (If Needed)

In most cases, if any remaining teeth need removal, we perform extractions at the same appointment as your implant placement. This approach keeps your treatment efficient and eliminates the need for multiple separate surgical visits.

Implant Placement

Under local anesthesia or sedation, we place four titanium implants into your jawbone at precise angles. The rear implants are angled to engage denser bone, creating a wider, more stable base of support for your full arch.

Temporary Prosthesis

On the same day as your surgery, you receive a temporary full-arch prosthesis fitted and secured to your implants. You leave our Dumont office with a complete, functional set of teeth and a restored smile.

Healing

Over three to six months, your jawbone fuses with the implants through a natural process called osseointegration. This creates a strong, permanent bond between your bone and the implants, forming the foundation for your final prosthesis.

Final Prosthesis

Once healing is complete, we fit you with your permanent, custom-crafted prosthesis. It is carefully shaped, sized, and shaded to match natural teeth, then securely attached to your implants for a precise, long-lasting fit.

Caring for Your All-on-4 Implants

Your All-on-4 implants are built to last, and a simple daily routine keeps them in top condition for years to come.

Our team provides detailed aftercare instructions following your procedure and remains available throughout your recovery.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • Use a water flosser or interdental brush to clean under the prosthesis
  • Avoid biting very hard objects like ice or hard candy
  • Attend check-ups at Palisades Dental Care every six months
  • Avoid smoking, as tobacco slows healing and raises the risk of implant failure
