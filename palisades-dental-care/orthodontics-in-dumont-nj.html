Orthodontics

If you’re experiencing any of these concerns, a consultation at Palisades Dental Care can help determine the best orthodontic solution for you.

  • Overbite, underbite, or crossbite issues that impact chewing and speaking
  • Gaps or spacing between teeth that affect aesthetics and oral hygiene
  • Crowded teeth that make brushing and flossing difficult
  • Jaw pain or TMJ discomfort caused by bite misalignment

What is Orthodontics?

At Palisades Dental Care, we provide customized orthodontic treatments to create balanced, functional, and attractive smiles. Whether you’re looking for traditional metal braces or discreet options like Invisalign® , we tailor each treatment plan to fit your unique needs and lifestyle.

Benefits of Orthodontics

  • Enhanced Smile Aesthetics – A properly aligned smile improves confidence and appearance.
  • Better Oral Health – Straighter teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Improved Bite Functionality – Orthodontic treatment corrects overbites, underbites, and misalignment for better chewing and speaking.
  • Reduced Jaw Pain & TMJ Issues – Properly aligned teeth reduce strain on the jaw, alleviating discomfort.
  • Long-Term Dental Protection – Orthodontics helps prevent wear and tear on teeth, reducing the risk of future dental issues.

FAQs

Orthodontic treatment can be done at any age, but early evaluation around age 7 can help identify potential issues. Teens and adults can also benefit from braces or Invisalign® for long-term dental health and aesthetics.

The duration of treatment depends on the complexity of the case. Braces typically take 18-24 months, while Invisalign® may take 12-18 months. Some cases may be shorter or longer based on individual needs.

Patients may experience mild discomfort when braces are first placed or adjusted. Invisalign® users may also feel slight pressure when switching to new aligners. Any discomfort is temporary and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
