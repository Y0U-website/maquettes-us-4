Welcoming New & Emergency Patients! MEET YOUR ST. PETE DENTIST Meet Dr. Brian Leeson

The practice

Dr. Leeson and his wife live in St. Petersburg with their 2 boys and rescue cat, Mittens.

Originally from the Northeast, Dr. Leeson earned his dental degree from Temple University in Philadelphia. Committed to giving back, Dr. Leeson donates a portion of new patient proceeds to charities like Tampa Bay Watch and the Syngap Research Fund .

Dr. Leeson and his team dedicate their time to volunteer with the Florida Mission of Mercy Outreach Program, providing assistance to those in need. When not engaged in dentistry, some of the activities he enjoys include cooking, playing sports, and just being outdoors.
