Types of Implant-Supported Dentures

There are several different types of implant-supported dentures, depending on your needs and the number of implants required:

Both types provide superior stability and prevent the discomfort often associated with traditional dentures.

  • Bar-Retained Implant-Supported Dentures: A thin metal bar is placed along your jawbone, with dental implants supporting the bar. The dentures are then secured to the bar, offering a stable and snug fit.
  • Ball-Retained Implant-Supported Dentures: In this design, the implants have ball-shaped attachments that fit into corresponding sockets in the dentures, keeping them securely in place.

Caring for Implant-Supported Dentures

  • Clean Your Dentures Daily: Brush your implant-supported dentures with a soft-bristled toothbrush and mild denture cleaner to remove food particles and plaque. Avoid using regular toothpaste, as it may be too abrasive.
  • Maintain Good Oral Hygiene: While implant-supported dentures are more stable than traditional dentures, it&#x27;s still important to maintain regular oral hygiene. Brush your gums, tongue, and any remaining natural teeth twice a day to keep your mouth healthy.
  • Regular Check-Ups: Regular dental visits are essential for monitoring the condition of your implants and dentures. We will check for any signs of wear or issues with the fit and make necessary adjustments to ensure everything remains in good shape.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: While implant-supported dentures offer better stability, it’s still a good idea to avoid hard or sticky foods that could damage your dentures or implants.
