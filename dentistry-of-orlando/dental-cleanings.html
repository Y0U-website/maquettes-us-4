BENEFITS OF REGULAR TEETH CLEANING

Why you need regular dental cleanings

Our team of dental hygienists strive to provide our patients with a comfortable dental cleaning experience. Your every visit is a chance for our team to spot dental problems at an early stage. Dental cleanings repair cavities before they become larger or cause discomfort. A deep dental cleaning goes beyond a regular cleaning by thoroughly removing plaque and tartar from below the gumline, helping prevent gum disease and maintain long-term oral health. Here are other advantages of regular dental teeth cleaning appointments.

Whiter teeth

We remove built-up stains from food and drinks to give you perfectly polished teeth. There’s no better way to brighten up your smile than professional dental cleanings.

Reduce bad breath

Having clean teeth also means less bad breath. By getting your teeth cleaned by professionals, you’ll enjoy a healthier mouth and fresher breath.

Save money

Having routine teeth cleaning procedures can prevent the development of more costly dental problems in the future.

Prevent cavities & tooth loss

Your teeth may loosen up when cavities are not removed. By preventing plaque buildup, we help you save your teeth from cavities and tooth loss.

Boost overall health

Dental cleanings don’t just give you healthier oral health, having clean teeth also lowers your risk from diseases like diabetes and heart disease.

Promote healthy gums

Bacteria can build up in pockets between your teeth. A deep dental cleaning helps promote gum healing so you can enjoy healthy teeth and fresh breath.

MORE ABOUT DEEP CLEANING FOR TEETH

Frequently asked questions

Generally, the ideal interval for professional dental cleaning is every six months. However, some patients who are prone to having dental issues may need to come for teeth cleaning every three months. While others who experience less or hardly any dental issues can visit their hygienist every nine months.

In between cleaning, you need to practice good oral care to maintain a healthy mouth. Here are some things you can do to maintain healthy teeth and gums:

  • Brush teeth twice a day, and for at least two minutes each time.
  • Floss at least once a day after brushing.
  • Use fluoride toothpaste.
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3 to 4 months or once it’s worn out.
  • Eat a healthy diet and avoid sweets and sweetened drinks.
  • Keep dentures and retainers clean.
  • Visit your dentist for regular checkups.
