Meet Dr. Falletta-Swegman

I am originally from Buffalo, NY. My family was transferred to Lawrenceville in 1976. I am a 1981 graduate of Duluth High School. After attending Emory at Oxford and subsequently Emory University, I achieved early admittance to the Emory University School of Dentistry in 1984 and graduated at the top of my class in 1988.

My years of experience and dedication to treating every patient like family has led me to develop this practice philosophy; to treat every patient as one would treat their family with dignity, respect, kindness, and compassion.

I am joined by a wonderful staff, most of whom have been with the practice for years, in striving to provide the best dental experience possible. We pride ourselves in getting to know our patients on a personal level, and taking the time necessary to explain treatment procedures and to answer any questions so our patients feel both comfortable and informed.
