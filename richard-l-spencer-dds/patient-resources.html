Our office accepts most dental insurance plans, but please familiarize yourself with your insurance plan and pay close attention to co-pays. Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a dental cleaning at Richard L. Spencer DDS?

Our general dentistry team recommends a professional cleaning every six months to remove tartar and plaque, helping maintain optimal oral health and prevent cavities and gum disease.

Do you offer same-day X-rays or digital imaging?

Yes! We use digital X‑rays for same-day imaging, which provide precise pictures for early detection of issues like cavities or infections that aren’t visible during a regular exam.

What types of fillings do you use for cavities?

We use composite (tooth-colored) fillings to restore decayed teeth. These blend seamlessly with your natural tooth shade for a discreet, durable result.

Is fluoride treatment available? Who should get it?

Absolutely. Our fluoride treatments help strengthen enamel and reduce decay—highly recommended for children, teens, and adults prone to cavities or dry mouth.

Do you screen for oral cancer?

Yes, we conduct oral cancer screenings using a hand-held light device to spot abnormalities early and improve treatment outcomes.

What periodontal services do you provide?

We address gum issues with treatments from routine cleanings to advanced scaling and root planing, removing bacteria and smoothing root surfaces to combat gum disease.

What is Dr. Spencer s experience and background?

Can I get crowns and bridges at your practice?

Definitely. We offer crowns for damaged teeth and bridges to replace missing ones, restoring both function and aesthetics.

Do you provide root canal therapy?

Yes. Our office offers root canal treatments to clean infected pulp, seal the tooth, and often place a crown or post for full restoration.

What solutions are available for missing teeth?

We specialize in both partial/complete dentures and implant restorations, offering removable or permanent options tailored for comfort, function, and natural appearance.

Yes! We offer veneers for reshaping teeth and in-office teeth whitening to brighten dull or discolored smiles using safe, effective methods.
