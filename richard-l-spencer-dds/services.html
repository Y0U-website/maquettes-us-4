Restorative Dentistry Services

Periodontal

Periodontal disease can be recognized as gum bleeding, bad breath, loose teeth, and other dental issues. Our team has the skill and expertise to administer treatment according to the severity of the disease. If you notice signs of gingivitis developing, please don’t hesitate to reach out.

Partials & Dentures

Complete or partial dentures can be an effective solution to missing teeth. We can design and implement custom-fitted, removable dentures to restore your smile. Any dentures can restore your eating and speech abilities, give your smile a natural appearance again.

Root Canal Therapy

When a cavity reaches the tooth’s pulp, your tooth’s root can become infected. Allow our team to clean out the infected area and fill it with root canal therapy. A substantial build-up, crown, or bonded post may be necessary to restore the tooth to its normal function.
