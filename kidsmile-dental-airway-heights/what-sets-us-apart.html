ABOUT US

What sets us apart from other Spokane Dentists?

Creating Healthy Smiles for Life

At KidSmile Dental, we offer comprehensive children’s dentistry in a convenient location. You can easily schedule everyone’s treatment at once, so your family doesn’t have to shuffle back and forth or waste valuable time. Our caring team is dedicated to creating a positive experience for each child.

We focus on prevention and education to empower children to maintain healthy teeth from a young age. With ongoing care and oral hygiene, they can reduce the number of dental problems they might face as adults. We believe that a positive relationship with a pediatric dental team can transform how your child views dental care in the future.

Your Child’s First Visit

During your child’s first visit, Dr. Blake and Dr. Ryan will take time to get to know your child and build a relationship. Once your child feels comfortable, we will thoroughly examine their teeth, gums, and jaw. We will take safe digital X-rays to check on structural health and ensure everything is as it should be. Throughout the process, we explain what we are doing, keeping the atmosphere light and cheery.

We strive to provide your child with a fun and safe environment while they are here. If children realize at a young age that the dentist is a fun and positive place, they are more likely to maintain a lifetime of good oral hygiene. With our dentistry for kids, KidSmile Dental works hard to give your child a positive experience every time they visit us.

Flexible Appointments

If your child needs immediate care or faces a dental emergency, we can often start treatment on the same day. There’s no need to worry or spend days waiting for an appointment; we can see you today.

Our insurance-friendly practice collaborates closely with your carrier to help you maximize your benefits and limit out-of-pocket expenses. If a certain procedure isn’t covered under your insurance plan, we offer affordable payment options.

What Parents Have To Say

When Should My Child See The Dentist?

Pediatricians and pediatric dental experts agree: children should have their first checkup by age 1 or earlier if their first tooth is beginning to erupt. At these visits, our KidSmile Dental team equips parents with the resources necessary to ensure their child’s teeth are well cared for. Early visits allow us to screen for potential concerns before they become extensive problems down the road.
