What to Expect, Start to Finish

Implant treatment is a process, not a single procedure. Here is what each stage looks like.

Your free consultation

We take X-rays, create a personalized 3D treatment plan, review your bone health, and walk you through your options. You will leave knowing exactly what the process looks like for your specific case.

Implant placement ‍

The titanium post is placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia. Most patients find it easier than a tooth extraction

Healing (4–6 months)

Your bone naturally fuses with the implant during this time. This process, called osseointegration, is what makes an implant permanent and stable.

Abutment and crown placement

Once healed, we place a custom porcelain crown matched to your natural teeth. The result looks and functions like a tooth that was never missing.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Less than most people expect. It&#x27;s done under local anesthesia, and the most common feedback we get is that it was easier than a tooth extraction. There&#x27;s typically soreness for a few days afterward that&#x27;s manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

How long does the procedure take

From consultation to final crown, most cases take 4–8 months. The majority of that is healing time. We&#x27;ll map out a clear timeline for you at your first visit.
