What is the Kois Center?

The Kois Center is a high-level, post-graduate continuing education program for dental professionals. It focuses on restorative dentistry, implants, and esthetics, and is unique for conducting and publishing its own independent scientific research.

What is a Kois Center Graduate?

A Kois Center Graduate is a dentist who has completed all nine rigorous, multi-step clinical and didactic continuing education courses at the Kois Center in Seattle, Washington. This intensive multi-year curriculum focuses on evidence-based treatment planning, restorative dentistry, prosthodontics, and bite mechanics.
