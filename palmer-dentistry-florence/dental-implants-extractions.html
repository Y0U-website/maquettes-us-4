Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are dental implants a permanent solution for missing teeth?

Yes, dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution. They are anchored securely in the jawbone, much like natural teeth, offering a stable and lasting option to replace missing teeth. With proper care, implants can last for many years, even a lifetime.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

The implant procedure is generally well-tolerated and is performed under local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. Most patients report minimal pain during and after the procedure, and we’ll provide aftercare instructions to help with a smooth recovery.

When is a tooth extraction necessary?

Tooth extractions may be recommended if a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, or causing alignment issues. In cases where a tooth cannot be saved, or when removing it will benefit overall oral health, our team will guide you through the process with comfort and care.

How long does it take to recover from a tooth extraction?

Recovery time varies depending on the complexity of the extraction. Typically, soft tissues heal within a week, while the bone may take several months to fully heal. We’ll provide you with detailed post-extraction care instructions to ensure a comfortable recovery.

Can I replace a missing tooth immediately after extraction?

In some cases, it’s possible to place an implant immediately after extraction. However, this depends on the condition of the jawbone and overall oral health. We’ll assess your situation and recommend the best timing for implants to ensure optimal results.
