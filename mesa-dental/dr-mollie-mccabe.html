Meet Dr. Mollie McCabe

After graduating, Dr. McCabe built her clinical career in Omaha, practicing full-scope dentistry and developing the long-term patient relationships that would come to define her approach to care. In 2015, she and her family relocated to the New Orleans area, where she continued growing as a clinician and deepening her commitment to treating every patient as a whole person. In 2024, the McCabe family made Las Vegas home, and Dr. McCabe joined Mesa Dental — bringing nearly two decades of experience and a genuine warmth that puts patients at ease from the first visit forward.

Rooted in Whole-Person Care

Dr. McCabe’s philosophy centers on a simple but powerful belief: dental care should treat the person, not just the tooth. That means taking the time to understand each patient’s medical history, their concerns, and their goals — and designing care around all of it. It is an approach shaped by years of building long-term relationships in Omaha and New Orleans, and one she brings to every patient she sees at Mesa Dental.

What to Expect at Your First Appointment With Dr. McCabe

At Mesa Dental, Dr. McCabe’s first appointments are intentionally unhurried. She takes time to review your health history, ask about your concerns and goals, and perform a thorough examination before any treatment is discussed. There is no rush and no pressure — just a focused conversation about your dental health and what you can do to support it for the long term.

  • A comprehensive exam covering alignment, bite, pH, and overall oral health — not just a quick cavity check
  • An open conversation about your health history, concerns, and what you actually want from your dental care
  • A clear walkthrough of any findings, including what they mean and what your realistic options are
  • Unhurried time with Dr. Browner, who sees a maximum of two new patients per day by design

Why Choose Dr. Mollie McCabe at Mesa Dental?

Dr. McCabe joined Mesa Dental because it is a practice built around the same values she has held throughout her career. With nearly two decades of clinical experience and a background that spans preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, she offers the kind of comprehensive, attentive care that patients genuinely appreciate — where every visit feels like a conversation, not a transaction.

Whether you are looking for a dentist who takes the time to understand your full health picture, exploring restorative or cosmetic options, or simply want a provider your whole family can trust for the long term, Dr. McCabe and the Mesa Dental team are ready to welcome you.
