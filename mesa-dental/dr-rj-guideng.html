Meet Dr. R.J. Guideng

After graduating, Dr. Guideng joined the faculty at UNLV’s School of Dental Medicine as an Associate Clinical Faculty member, supervising student clinicians across procedures from comprehensive exams to oral surgery. That experience deepened his surgical foundation and reinforced his commitment to precision. Over the years, he has pursued some of the most rigorous credentialing available in implant dentistry, earning his Fellowship and ultimately his Diplomate status with the American Board of Oral Implantology/Implant Dentistry, a distinction held by a small percentage of dentists nationally.

At Mesa Dental, he brings that expertise to every implant case, working alongside Dr. Emily Browner to deliver comprehensive care from single-tooth replacements to complex full-arch restorations.

A Full Scope of Implant Care, All in One Place

Dr. Guideng’s caseload at Mesa Dental covers every stage of implant treatment, from the first consultation through the final restoration. Whether a patient needs a single tooth replaced, a full arch rebuilt, or preparatory work like bone grafting before implants can be placed, he handles it without referrals to outside providers. For patients who also want sedation during surgery, both IV and oral sedation are available, so comfort is part of the plan from the start.

That range of capability matters because implant cases rarely follow a single path. Some patients arrive with healthy bone and a straightforward treatment plan. Others need more involved preparation before a long-term solution is possible. Either way, Dr. Guideng has the surgical training and the clinical focus to see the case through from start to finish.

What to Expect at Your First Appointment With Dr. Guideng

Many patients who come to Dr. Guideng have been managing tooth loss for years, cycling through temporary fixes, living with dentures that never felt right, or putting off a procedure they assumed wasn’t possible for them. His first step is always to give those patients a clear, honest picture of where they stand.

A consultation with Dr. Guideng isn’t a sales pitch. It’s an evaluation. He reviews bone volume, tissue health, bite relationships, and restorative goals before any recommendation is made, because the right implant plan starts with understanding the full picture, not just the missing teeth. For patients who feel anxious about surgery, Mesa Dental is certified to offer both IV and oral sedation, so comfort is part of the plan from the start. Patients leave knowing exactly what their options are, what the process looks like, and what to realistically expect.

  • A comprehensive implant evaluation covering bone density, jaw anatomy, and restorative goals
  • A clear explanation of the recommended approach, timeline, and recovery expectations
  • An honest assessment of candidacy, including any preparatory steps such as bone grafting
  • A conversation about IV and oral sedation options for patients who want more than local anesthesia (numbing)
  • Unhurried time with Dr. Guideng to ask questions and understand your path forward

Coordinated Care Under One Roof

For patients considering a smile makeover that includes both implant work and cosmetic treatment , that coordination is especially valuable. Both doctors are familiar with the case from the start, and the plan accounts for the full picture rather than just one phase of treatment.
