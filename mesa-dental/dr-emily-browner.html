What to Expect at Your First Appointment With Dr. Browner

Most dental offices schedule 20-minute exams and move on. At Mesa Dental, your first appointment is intentionally different. Dr. Browner limits her new patient slots to two per day so she can give you the kind of unhurried, thorough attention that actually tells you something meaningful about your oral health and where it’s headed.

  • A comprehensive exam covering alignment, bite, pH, and overall oral health — not just a quick cavity check
  • An open conversation about your health history, concerns, and what you actually want from your dental care
  • A clear walkthrough of any findings, including what they mean and what your realistic options are
  • Unhurried time with Dr. Browner, who sees a maximum of two new patients per day by design
