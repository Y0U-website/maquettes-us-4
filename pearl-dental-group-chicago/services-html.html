Services

At Pearl Dental Group we have the skills to address all of your preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental needs. Our dentists specialize in oral surgery, periodontics (treatment of the gum), and endodontics.

During your first visit our doctors provide you with a thorough visual and x-ray examinations of your entire mouth. Based on our findings we then recommend the best dental treatment to address your needs that is affordable to you.

The first exam appointment is usually made via a phone call from the patient. You can also make your appointment via our website by clicking on contact us page. Any initial inquiries that you have can be answered and you can let us know of any problems you have experienced in the past. You should find out in advance how long the appointment is likely to take. Our friendly dentists will put you at ease and can handle any procedure you may require.

Pearl Dental Group has a comprehensive list of services including:

X-rays help to diagnose dental problems that are unable to be seen normally.

Periodontal Disease, more commonly known as gum disease affects many adults. Scaling and Root Planning is the most widely used method to treat this disease. Scaling and Root Planning is the process used to remove tartar and plaque build up from the teeth.

This is the process for repairing cavities in the teeth. Our dentists use different type of fillings including, tooth-colored fillings, porcelain filling and amalgam.

Over time teeth become discolored due to bacteria, food, tobacco, and simply due to age. Maintaining a beautiful white smile is something Pearl Dental can help provide. We offer several whitening procedures, ask your dentist which one is right for you.

Repair your smile with Porcelain Veneers. They are stronger than enamel and are able to resist staining better as well. Our dentists can help match the veneers to your natural teeth color as well. Veneers are a great way to repair your smile due to damaged teeth.

Repair damaged, chipped, discolored teeth through Dental Bonding. Through this process a tooth-colored material bonded to the tooth. It can be used to provide a cosmetic solution to gaps in teeth, and even adjusting a tooth’s shape.

Cosmetic Contouring and Reshaping of Tooth

This procedure is used to repair damaged teeth. It can also be used to fix cosmetic needs such as overlapping teeth. It can also be used to correct minor changes and may fix problems without the need of braces.

Plastic resin is bonded to the grooves on a tooth’s chewing surface. This helps protect the tooth and helps prevent cavities forming on the tooth. Sealants make it easier to clean the tooth and prevent plaque buildup.

Root canals save severely decayed or infected teeth through cleaning out the pulp chamber, removing the nerve tissue and bacteria, and then filling it in and sealing the tooth. Root canals can help prevent many problems relating to an abscessed tooth, including swelling and infections.

The following videos helps show the process involved with a root canal

Crowns are fitted over damaged teeth to restore strength, shape, size, or for cosmetic reasons. Crowns can be metal, porcelain-fused-to-metal, all-resin, all-ceramic, and all-porcelain, ask Pearl Dental which one is right for you.

Bridges replace gaps from missing teeth. A bridge is made up of crowns anchored to your actual teeth to fill in gaps and spaces from missing teeth. They not only serve a cosmetic need but also can help you properly chew and speak, and helping to keep your remaining teeth in proper position.

The following video shows how a bridge can help prevent additional dental problems and replace missing teeth.

Dental implants are used to replace missing teeth. They are stronger than bridges and dentures and offer a permanent solution to missing teeth. They help cosmetically, make it easier to eat, and are extremely durable. Ask Pearl Dental if implants are right for you.

Please view the video to see how you can benefit from Dental Implants.

Dentures replace missing teeth. Old age does not mean you have to loose your teeth. Proper oral care can help prevent the need for dentures. Pearl Dental offers complete and Partial dentures, ask us which ones are right for you.
