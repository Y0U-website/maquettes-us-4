Meet your dentist

Dr. Rinal Patel, DDS, MPH

Dr. Patel is passionate about compassionate, personalized dental care and about building relationships that last longer than a single course of treatment. She earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery in India before moving to the United States, where she completed a Master’s in Public Health at the CUNY School of Public Health and graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine’s Advanced Standing Program. A resident of Branchburg, she lives with her husband and their two sons, and enjoy cooking when not in the office.

Class Valedictorian at UNLV and recipient of the DDS Outstanding Student Award.

Inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, Psi Omega Chapter, for scholarship, professionalism and ethics.

Member of the American Dental Association, the New Jersey Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.

What we are striving for

Three things we are not willing to rush

A dental office runs on hundreds of small decisions a week. These are the ones we have decided in advance, so they do not get made under time pressure.

The exam A first visit includes a full look at teeth, gums, bite and soft tissue not a quick check and a cleaning.

The explanation You see what we see, in plain language, with the cost attached, before you are asked to decide anything.

The continuity The same familiar team is here visit after visit. Continuity is most of what makes dentistry work.
