Dental Care is Not Expensive - neglect IS!

Routine care helps you stay focused on your own health goals. At Smiling Faces, we stress consistent routine dental care to prevent the need for extensive treatment and costly procedures. When you neglect the essential maintenance of regular dental visits, it results in the need for more invasive procedures which can quickly become very costly.

Your smile really does make the first impression. Maintaining dental health results in you smiling more and living a longer, healthier life. Preventative care consists of routine care (brushing and flossing twice a day) to remove plaque and calculus so that you prevent tooth decay and gum disease.
