Why Consider Dental Implants?

Missing teeth affect far more than just your appearance. They can lead to issues like difficulty chewing, impaired speech, and even changes in your facial structure. Here are some signs that dental implants might be the ideal solution for you:

If any of these situations resonate with you, Dr. Christy and the team at River Oak Dental in Palm Bay are ready to help determine if dental implants are right for you.

  • Missing Teeth : Whether you’ve lost one tooth or several, implants can restore your smile seamlessly.
  • Bone Loss in the Jaw : Implants prevent further bone deterioration by stimulating the jaw as natural tooth roots do.
  • Loose Dentures or Bridges : Struggling with ill-fitting dental appliances? Implants offer a stable and long-term alternative.
  • Difficulty Eating or Speaking : Missing teeth can make simple activities feel uncomfortable or challenging.
  • Desire for a Permanent Solution : Dental implants last many years and require proper care, making them a worthwhile investment.

The Process of Getting Dental Implants

Getting dental implants is a multi-step process that ensures optimal long-term results. Here’s what to expect when you choose River Oak Dental for your dental implants in Palm Bay:

Initial Consultation

Dr. Christy will perform a detailed evaluation, including digital X-rays and scans, to assess your oral health and jawbone structure. We’ll discuss your goals, address any concerns, and outline a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement

During the surgical procedure, we will carefully place the titanium implant post into the jawbone. Thanks to our advanced Laser Dentistry and Digital Scanner, the process is precise and minimally invasive. Patients often report less discomfort than they anticipated.

Healing Period

The healing phase typically takes a few months. During this time, the implant fuses with your jawbone, providing a secure foundation for your new tooth. We may provide temporary restorations to maintain function and aesthetics.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant is fully integrated, an abutment is attached to connect the post with your custom restoration.

Custom Restoration

Finally, we craft a custom crown, bridge, or denture that matches the look and feel of your natural teeth. The result? A confident, functional smile you’ll love showing off.

Aftercare for Dental Implants

Caring for your dental implants is simple and ensures their longevity. Here are some tips to maintain the health and functionality of your implants:

Our team will provide detailed aftercare instructions tailored to your needs and lifestyle. With ongoing support through our CareCredit Financing or In-House Savings Plan, maintaining your oral health has never been easier.

  • Practice Good Oral Hygiene : Brush and floss daily to keep the area around your implant clean and free of plaque.
  • Schedule Regular Check-Ups : Dr. Christy will monitor your implant during routine check-ups to ensure everything remains in excellent condition.
  • Avoid Hard Foods for a While : Immediately after surgery, avoid hard, sticky, or crunchy foods to allow proper healing.
  • Quit Smoking : Smoking can hinder the healing process and increase the risk of implant failure.
