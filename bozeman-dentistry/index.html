Exceptional Hospitality and Customer Service

Our practice offers a variety of services for patients, from general dentistry to cosmetic dentistry, for patients in the Bozeman to Belgrade, Montana area.

The practice

We look forward to establishing a professional, customer-oriented relationship with our patients that helps them look forward to dental treatments. Our services are individually adjusted and tailored to each patient’s needs using up-to-date treatments and modern technology.

We work with our patients to help them meet their cosmetic and preventative dental needs. You can trust us to take great care of your teeth and to help you prevent future dental problems.

We are happy to have Dr. Gabriel McCormick as the head of our dental team. Dr. McCormick is a Montana native, having received his bachelor’s degree from MSU. After receiving his dental education in Oregon, Dr. McCormick couldn’t resist the beautiful views and natural outlets Montana offers.

Dr. McCormick has been proud to be a part of Montana’s professional dental community since 2011. Dr. McCormick is a proud Bozeman family man with his wife, Flora, and their two kids, Eva and Caleb. Dr. McCormick is involved in the Montana Dental Association and the American Dental Association. He is also a member of the Academy of General Dentistry.

At our office, we get to know our patients and build a professional but caring environment for all of our patients in the Bozeman area. Regular cleanings and general dentistry checkups will keep your teeth strong and healthy.

Restorative dentistry services fix dental problems after they’ve occurred. If you have a chipped, cracked, or otherwise damaged tooth, at our office we offer services that could help you get your full smile back.

Services
