Giving Back

Not only is he passionate about providing the best orthodontic treatment, but he also loves giving back to the youth of the community!

Dr. Kovacs is a major provider for Smiles Change Lives, a national organization that provides life-changing orthodontic treatment for underprivileged children.

He also donates financially to the many schools and youth programs in the Billings and Miles City area.

Education & Experience

Dr. Kovacs attended California State University, Northridge, and ultimately earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of the Pacific.

From there, a post-graduate residency at the University of the Pacific allowed him to develop a greater expertise in orthodontic treatment.
