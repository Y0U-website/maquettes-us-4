Dental implants typically have three parts: 1) Implant : A titanium screw that serves as a root for your new tooth. This is what fuses with the jawbone.

2) Abutment : A permanent connector that supports and holds a crown. 3) Crown : This is the part of the tooth that you can see. It’s usually made of zirconium or porcelain. You can use dental implants to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth or a full upper and/or lower set of teeth.
