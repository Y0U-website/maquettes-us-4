Dr. Shanna Chirco

Dr. Shanna Chirco loves practicing dentistry and interacting with patients. Her passion is helping her patients improve their dental health and smiles and she looks forward to providing each patient quality, caring service in a warm and welcoming environment.

"I love what I do! I love the technical challenges I face each day and also enjoy getting to know all my patients and being fortunate enough to help them out. It is especially rewarding at the end of treatment to get a heartfelt thank you from the patient because they know we care about them and take immense pride in the care we provide.”

Dr. Chirco, formerly Dr. Shanna Koh, is a graduate of UC Irvine and USC’s Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry. After dental school, she worked for more than ten years as an associate at both private practices as well as a large dental corporation. This experience was invaluable for Dr. Chirco as she learned what best worked for patients and how to address every patient’s needs. She has now spent more than a decade restoring and enhancing smiles. She is committed to providing patients with beautiful smiles through outstanding care.

Patients undergoing treatment have her cell phone number and can always get in touch with any questions or concerns.

Patients visiting Dr. Chirco can receive a full range of dental services. She provides root canals, fillings, extractions, bridges, crowns, dentures, Invisalign, teeth whitening, mouth and snore guards, full mouth restorations and general consultations. Treatment is also available for dental emergencies and wisdom tooth problems.
