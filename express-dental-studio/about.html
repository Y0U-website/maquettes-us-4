Get to know your provider

Meet Dr. Ryan Langel

Born and raised on a fourth-generation family farm in Northwest Iowa, Dr. Ryan Langel knows the value of hard work, practical thinking, and building trust. Before becoming a restorative dentist, he earned his degree in Agriculture from Iowa State University and worked alongside his dad on the farm. He also ventured into livestock production, poured concrete through the family business, and invested in real estate, bringing with him a deep understanding of ownership, investment, and grit.

It wasn’t until sitting in the dental chair for a routine cleaning that Dr. Langel had the idea to pivot his entire career. After being accepted to dental school, Dr. Langel went on to complete his dental education at Creighton University School of Dentistry. After honing his skills in associate practice, he founded Express Dental Studio to do dentistry differently.

Dr. Langel started Express Dental Studio with a clear purpose: to practice dentistry well and to give people access to both needed and desired dental care—without the extreme prices often associated with high-end esthetic and restorative services. The practice is built around efficient, same-day care that prioritizes quality, transparency, and long-term value for patients.

He is a member of the American Dental Association and the Iowa Dental Association, holds a certification in dental implants, and has advanced training in digital dentistry, including 3D printing, milling, and patient management. His approach blends clinical precision with a business-minded perspective, allowing him to deliver high-level results while keeping care approachable and cost-conscious.

Patients appreciate Dr. Langel’s down-to-earth attitude, ability to explain treatment clearly, and no-nonsense approach to dentistry. He’s not just about fixing teeth. He’s about helping people feel more confident, and making sure quality care is never out of reach.

Outside the office, you can find Dr. Langel hiking, playing pickleball, spending time at the park with his kids, or cheering on his favorite teams. Family, community, and integrity drive everything he does, both at work and at home.

From first visit to final smile

What to expect at your first appointment

Your first visit to Express Dental Studio is all about creating a smooth, efficient, no-pressure experience. Because we only focus on restorative care: no cleanings, no fluff. You’ll get clearly focused expertise specifically designed to restore your smile.

Patient Experience When you arrive, our team will walk you through your treatment options, pricing, and timing. Most services can be completed same-day thanks to our digital workflow and in-house technology. With transparent pricing and a relaxed environment, we make it easy to say yes to your new smile, without surprises.

What to Bring To help keep your visit quick and easy, please bring:

Completed new patient forms (available digitally or via PDF download)

Any relevant dental records or x-rays, if available

Payment method or financing information (we accept Cherry)

Advanced amenities and tech that make every visit better

We’ve designed our office and equipped our treatment rooms with your comfort and efficiency in mind. From the waiting area to the operatories, everything is set up to make your experience as smooth and stress-free as possible, whether it’s your first visit or your fifth.

Beverage bar

Enjoy a complimentary drink from our beverage station while you wait; just one of the ways we make your visit a little more enjoyable.

TVs in operatories

Every treatment room includes a TV to help you relax and stay entertained while we restore your smile.

In-house milling

Using advanced milling technology, we fabricate durable, natural-looking restorations on-site, in a single visit, saving you time and hassle.

3D printing

Our advanced 3D printing setup allows us to produce custom dentures, night guards, and more, all with a perfect fit and quick turnaround.

CBCT Scanner

We use cone beam imaging to capture high-resolution 3D scans for better diagnosis, treatment planning, and implant placement.

Intraoral Scanner

Forget the goopy impressions. Our intraoral scanner captures detailed images in seconds, making your experience faster and more comfortable.
