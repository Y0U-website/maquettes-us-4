Frequently Asked Questions

  • How long does it take to heal after a tooth extraction? Most initial healing occurs within a few days, though full bone healing takes longer. If planning an implant, your dentist will determine whether immediate placement is possible or if a healing period is needed.
  • Can an implant be placed the same day as the extraction? Yes, if the bone is strong and the area is free of infection, same-day implant placement may be an option. If not, a staged approach ensures the best long-term success.
  • Will the procedure be painful? Your comfort is our priority. Extractions and implant placements are performed with local anesthesia so you feel pressure, not pain. Mild soreness afterward is normal and manageable with simple medication.
  • What if the tooth infection has spread? If infection is present, we may treat it first or remove the tooth and allow healing before placing the implant. Our 3D imaging helps determine the safest and most effective timing.
  • Why is it important to replace a tooth after extraction? A missing tooth can lead to bone loss, shifting teeth, chewing difficulty, and changes in facial structure. A dental implant restores function while preserving long-term oral health.
