Meet the Dentist Western Connecticut Dental Arts

Dr. Stanton graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine and has a Bachelor of Science degree from Manhattan College. Before coming to Western Connecticut to develop his own practice in New Milford, Dr. Stanton worked as a dentist at the Veterans Administration Medical Center in the Bronx, New York and a private practice in Westchester County.

THE MISSION

We strive to provide exceptional care and good health to all who value quality dentistry while supporting the integrity and continued growth of our team. We serve our patients in a courteous, professional and kind manner so that they know they are valued and wish to develop a long-term relationship with us. We offer state of the art care with minimum discomfort. We promote regular preventative care in our office and at home so that our patients can enjoy a lifetime of good dental health.

We continuously strive for excellence in dentistry. Our practice will not accept limitations placed by insurance providers who seek to limit the quality of care for our patients. We provide only the proper and appropriate care for all of our patients. Treating patients with suitable x-rays/records/follow-up visits is a daily prerequisite to the high quality of care we provide. Patients will realize how committed we are to always providing the best care for a lifetime of good health.

So, Welcome.

Dr. Kevin Stanton would like to welcome you to a state-of-the-art dental practice that provides personalized, expert dental care. Dr. Stanton’s training and experience enable him to provide his patients with the best that modern dentistry has to offer.

Through his extensive professional activities, Dr. Stanton keeps abreast of new developments in dentistry and is committed to maintaining a staff that is trained in the latest dental technology.

Western Connecticut Dental Arts is a proud member of the American Dental Association and the Academy Of General Dentistry.
