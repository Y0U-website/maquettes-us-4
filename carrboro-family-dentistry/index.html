Seeing a lack of comprehensive resources for teaching operative dentistry, the study of fillings and the treatment of tooth decay, Dr. Cliff sets out to write his own textbook. He types the original manuscript in his basement on a typewriter, creating what becomes Sturdevant’s Art and Science of Operative Dentistry. The book goes on to become the best-selling dental textbook in the world…

Dr. Roger Sturdevant receives a request from Dr. John Brauer, a former student, to help establish a new dental school in Chapel Hill. He and his son, Dr. Clifford Sturdevant, relocate to North Carolina to join the effort. In 1950, they help found the University of North Carolina School of Dentistry alongside Dean Brauer, becoming the school’s first two professors and shaping its early direction.
