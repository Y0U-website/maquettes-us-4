Digital Experience

Smile Monitoring

Smile Monitoring is a remote monitoring system that tracks your treatment progress from the comfort of your home, meaning you only come in when it is time for the next step in your treatment. By eliminating the need for frequent in-office visits, you reduce the stress of taking time off from work or school for appointments. Smile Monitoring makes your experience easier, more convenient, and flexible while still receiving expert care throughout your orthodontic journey!

Virtual Visits

Virtual visits ease treatment by eliminating extra in-office trips when possible. For certain appointments, you can instead securely meet with us remotely from the comfort of your home. This more convenient option saves you time and eliminates the stress of scheduling around your busy life. With virtual visits, you can continue your care seamlessly, without disrupting your routine!

Smile Predictions

See your future smile before your treatment is complete! With our advanced Smile Prediction technology, you’ll get a glimpse of the final results based on your personalized treatment plan. This feature helps you stay motivated and confident throughout your orthodontic journey by providing a clear picture of your more captivating and healthier smile that awaits you.

3D Imaging

Forget the discomfort of traditional molds. Our 3D Digital Scanning technology captures detailed, accurate images of your teeth and jaw in minutes. This non-invasive process enhances your comfort while providing precise data for a tailored treatment plan. With 3D scanning, we ensure your aligners or braces are a great fit for optimal results.

To ensure the most comprehensive care, we utilize 3D X-Rays that offer a detailed view of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This advanced imaging technology helps us diagnose and plan treatments with incredible accuracy, ensuring the best outcomes for your unique needs. It’s just one more way Smile Orthodontics prioritizes your health and comfort.
