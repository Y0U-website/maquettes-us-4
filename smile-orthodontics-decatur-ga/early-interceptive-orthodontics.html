Dr. Chakote is a board-certified orthodontist—a distinction not all orthodontists achieve – demonstrating a dedication to both expertise and thoughtful treatment. Board certification requires advanced training, rigorous exams, and ongoing education to stay current with orthodontic standards. For children, this means specialized knowledge in growing smiles and bite development, commitment to safe and proven treatment approaches, and personalized plans tailored to your child&#x27;s unique needs

Simplify Later Treatment Early treatment to permit safe eruption of adult teeth can simplify treatment later, such as reducing the need for invasive procedures like 1) tooth exposure to help with eruption or 2) removal of adult teeth.

Healthier Teeth Early crowding (misaligned teeth) trap food and are difficult to clean, increasing risks of cavities and gum disease. Early orthodontic treatment corrects alignment proactively, providing clearance to more easily clean teeth.
