Advanced Technology We don&#x27;t just use technology —we embrace it as a fundamental part of your treatment. From 3D digital scanners, x-rays, and digital braces to remote monitoring (Smile Monitoring), we ensure your orthodontic journey is as efficient, convenient, accurate, and comfortable as possible.

Smile Monitoring We value your time. With Smile Monitoring , scans of your teeth that are sent to us from the comfort of your home, we can track your progress remotely. This ensures that you only come in when it’s time for your next step in treatment. By eliminating redundant appointments, we seek to give you the freedom to focus on your life and routine!

Sit Back and Relax Our office is designed to put you at ease. Forget cold, clinical environments - our warm, welcoming space helps you feel comfortable from the moment you arrive. Feel free to tune into your favorite show during your treatment and zone out!

Personalized Treatments Every smile and person is unique, and so is our approach. We take the time to understand your specific needs, goals, and lifestyle, creating a truly personalized approach to your orthodontic care and providing the best treatment option for you.
