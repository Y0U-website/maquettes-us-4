About Dr. Christine Phan, DMD

Lead Dentist & Founder

Dr. Christine Phan, DMD

Dr. Phan didn’t grow up in one dental office. Over more than 20 years, she’s practiced in cities across the United States — refining her clinical skills and developing her own approach to patient care along the way.

Outside the office, she’s stayed engaged with the broader world — travel, community involvement, and a belief that dentistry is more than a profession. It’s a way to take care of people. That perspective shapes everything that happens here.

When she opened her own practice in Hawthorne, she did it with a simple promise: treat every patient like family . The kind of careful, honest, personal dentistry she’d want for her own loved ones.

Beyond the Chair

Dentistry, the Human Way

For Dr. Phan, dentistry isn’t just a profession — it’s a way to take care of people. That ethic shows up in small ways and big ones. It’s why our team takes the time to actually listen. It’s why we explain treatment options instead of dictating them. It’s why nobody at this practice will ever push you toward something you don’t need.

It also shows up in how we work with people. We file claims with all major insurance plans, offer flexible payment options, and have an in-house Phantastic Dental Plans membership program for patients without insurance. We work with patients on payment plans because real life is messy and dental care shouldn’t be a luxury.
