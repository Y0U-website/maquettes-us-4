About

Saltwater Smile Suite is a hygienist-owned smile studio founded by two Expanded Practice Dental Hygienists . We have completed additional education, training, and certification that allows us to provide preventive dental hygiene care in community settings beyond the traditional dental office.

That clinical foundation shapes how we practice. Beginning in 2027, we will offer orofacial myofunctional therapy, which addresses how the muscles of the mouth and face work together to support breathing, swallowing, chewing, and jaw comfort. Through personalized exercises and guided education, this therapy can help with concerns such as mouth breathing, tongue thrust, clenching, orthodontic relapse, and sleep quality, for both children and adults.

We also offer professional teeth whitening and tooth gem services, designed to enhance your natural smile in a safe, comfortable, and elevated setting.

Our approach centers on the health of your smile first, so every appointment feels intentional, personalized, and grounded in clinical care.
