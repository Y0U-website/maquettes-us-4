A native of LaFayette, Dr. Chip Levie has been caring for families in Northwest Georgia for nearly 40 years. As a third generation dentist, Dr. Levie received his Bachelor of Science degree from Emory University and his Doctorate in Dental Surgery from the Emory University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Levie is dedicated to providing high-quality personalized care and continually invests in advanced training and continuing education. He has completed extensive coursework at the prestigious Pankey Institute, focusing on comfort, function, and long-term oral health.

Dr. Levie enjoys life on the family farm with his wife Debra, woodworking, and spending time with his dog, Daisy. He s especially proud to be "Pops" to his grandsons, Tate and Tucker.

Dr. Ty attended Baylor School in Chattanooga before earning a football scholarship to Samford University, where he graduated cum laude with a degree in Biology. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Indiana University School of Dentistry.

He is committed to providing high-quality, modern dental care and continues to pursue advanced training and continuing education. Dr. Ty continues to participate in outreach programs and mission work to expand access to care.

Dr. Ty spends time with his wife, Alli, and their two sons, Tate and Tucker. You ll often find him working out, playing ball with his boys, or watching Samford football.
