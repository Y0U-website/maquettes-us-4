Full mouth Implants

  • Stability and Comfort: Implants provide a secure fit, eliminating slipping and discomfort.
  • Preservation of Bone Health: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing further bone loss.
  • Convenience: No need to remove the prosthesis for cleaning.
  • Cost-Effective: Fewer implants are required compared to other restorative options.

Who Is a Candidate for All-on-4 Dental Implants?

  • Have total tooth loss.
  • Have limited bone density in the jaw.
  • Seek a more affordable solution compared to conventional implants.
  • Want permanent teeth with immediate functionality.

However, All-on-4 may not be suitable for individuals who:

  • Are heavy smokers or have uncontrolled diabetes.
  • Are undergoing chemotherapy or radiotherapy.
  • Have certain medical conditions like bleeding or bone disorders.
  • Want permanent teeth with immediate functionality.

What to Expect During the All-on-4 Procedure

Consultation and Planning

Our Team will review your medical history, perform a comprehensive oral examination, and take 3D imaging to plan your treatment.

Implant Placement Surgery

The procedure involves placing four dental implants under local or general anesthesia. A temporary prosthesis is then attached to the implants on the same day.

Healing Period

Over 3–6 months, the implants fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. Once healing is complete, the final prosthesis is custom-made and fitted.

Aftercare and Recovery with All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Use a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Avoid smoking and strenuous activities.
  • Stick to soft foods initially and avoid chewing near the surgical site.
