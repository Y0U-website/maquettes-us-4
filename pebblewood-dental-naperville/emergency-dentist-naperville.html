Questions Patients Ask Before Calling an Emergency Dentist

What counts as a dental emergency?

A dental emergency is any situation involving severe tooth pain, swelling, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost crown or filling, signs of infection, or any other urgent dental condition that requires prompt attention. If you are unsure, call us and we will help you decide the best course of action.

Can I be seen the same day?

We offer same-day emergency dental appointments in Naperville when availability allows. We encourage you to call as early as possible so we can do our best to accommodate you.

What should I do if I knock out a tooth?

If a tooth is knocked out, handle it by the crown (not the root), rinse it gently with water, and try to keep it moist either by placing it back in the socket, holding it between your cheek and gum, or storing it in a small container of milk. Call us immediately at 630-369-6222.

Do you offer sedation for emergency visits?

Yes. We offer laughing gas (nitrous oxide) to help patients feel more relaxed during emergency dental procedures. Let our team know if you have dental anxiety when you call.

What if I need a root canal or extraction during an emergency visit?

Our team is equipped to perform root canals and teeth removal when necessary. We will explain your options and make sure you understand the recommended treatment before we begin.
