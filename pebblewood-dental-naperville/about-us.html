Meet Our Dentists

Mark Mendiola, DDS

Dr. Mark Mendiola earned his dental degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry after earning his bachelor’s degree from Grand Valley State University.

Dr. Mendiola has been practicing dentistry for more than 23 years. He has completed many hours of postgraduate education to stay current with techniques and technologies that support conservative, comprehensive treatment with a gentle and caring approach.

He has also volunteered in outreach programs, provided health screenings at the Special Olympics, provided dental treatment through Indian Health Services in Montana, and participated in a dental mission to the Dominican Republic.

Larissa Navarro, DDS

Dr. Larissa Navarro was born and raised in Detroit, Michigan. She earned her bachelor’s degree in Biology from Wayne State University and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Detroit Mercy in 2005.

Dr. Navarro has spent much of her career caring for children. As a parent, she understands the daily challenges families face when building healthy habits and helping children feel comfortable with dental visits.

Her approach emphasizes compassionate, gentle care, preventive dentistry, minimally invasive treatment options, and oral health education for children and parents.
