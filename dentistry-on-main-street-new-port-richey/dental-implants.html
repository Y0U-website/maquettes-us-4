What Is The Dental Implant Process?

The path to dental implants in New Port Richey involves a series of appointments spread over several months, including consultation, implant placement, osseointegration, and final restoration placement.

Initial Consultation and Planning

Your implant procedure begins with a comprehensive oral health evaluation, including advanced imaging, to assess bone density and determine the ideal implant placement. We’ll then discuss your health history and goals and develop a personalized treatment plan.

This planning phase is vital for the success of your dental implants. In some cases, you may need preparatory treatments before beginning treatment, such as bone grafting or gum disease treatment . At Dentistry on Main Street, we take the time to answer all your questions and ensure you feel comfortable with the proposed treatment.

During the implant placement procedure , we carefully insert the titanium post into your jawbone. Many patients are surprised to find this process less daunting than expected, often requiring only local anesthesia. However, sedation options are available for patients who need extra relaxation to ensure the procedure is as calm and comfortable as possible.

After placement, a 3-6 month healing period allows the implant to integrate with your bone fully. In most cases, a temporary restoration will be placed during this healing phase so you’re never without teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Good candidates have adequate jawbone density, healthy gums , and good overall health. Age is less important than health status – implants can be appropriate for adults of any age. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate factors like bone volume, gum condition, medical history, and lifestyle to determine if implants are right for you.

How Long Does the Dental Implant Process Take?

The complete dental implant process typically takes 3-9 months from start to finish. After implant placement, a healing period of 3-6 months allows for osseointegration (fusion with bone). Once integrated, the final restoration is attached. Some patients may qualify for immediate or early loading protocols, while others may need preliminary procedures like bone grafting, which extends the timeline.

Are Dental Implants Safe?

Yes, dental implants are safe and effective with a success rate above 95 %. They’re made from biocompatible materials (typically titanium) that integrate naturally with human bone. Implants have been used for decades with excellent long-term results and patient satisfaction.

What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a revolutionary full-arch restoration technique that uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire arch of teeth. Unlike traditional implants that might require 6-8 implants per arch, the All-on-4 technique utilizes tilted posterior implants to maximize contact with available bone, often eliminating the need for bone grafting procedures.
