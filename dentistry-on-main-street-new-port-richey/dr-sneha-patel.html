The Professional Side

Dr. Sneha Patel is a dedicated and compassionate dentist committed to providing high-quality dental care. She offers a full range of services, including implants, prosthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, reconstructive surgery, and full-mouth reconstruction. As a gold-certified Invisalign provider, she specializes in creating confident, beautiful smiles.

Passionate about continuous learning, Dr. Patel regularly attends continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of dental innovations. Her mission is to ensure every patient receives excellent care in a comfortable and welcoming environment, helping them feel confident in their oral health and smile. Originally from Chicago but raised in the sunshine of Florida, Dr. Patel is a true Gator at heart.

She met her husband at the University of Florida , and together, they share a love for great food, new adventures, and cheering on the Gators. When she’s not perfecting smiles, you can find her experimenting in the kitchen, planning her next getaway, dancing to her favorite tunes, or getting lost in a good book. More than anything, she cherishes time spent with family and friends, believing that laughter and connection are just as important as a healthy smile.

Education Doctor of Dental Medicine Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine – Chicago, Illinois Bachelor of Science University of Florida – Gainesville, Florida
