Allen Dentistry Savers Plan

Allen Dentistry Savers Plan is good for use at Allen Dentistry only.

The fees associated with this plan are subject to yearly increases, and Allen Dentistry may increase fees at any time without prior notice. All treatment estimates given will be honored for one year after the prescribed date. All new treatment items planned after the fee increase will be subject to the new fee

Other sales, coupons, offers or deals may not be combined with this plan.

Payment is due at the time of treatment. If you choose to extend your payment for treatment through CareCredit, the discount will be reduced by 10 % due to merchant fees.

Please give us a call so we can answer any questions you may have about our Allen Dentistry Savers Plan.

The goal of this membership is to provide low-cost quality dental care that is dependable and without financial surprises. It is not an insurance plan and cannot be combined with insurance or any other discount plan.

Your benefits run for one full year from the date of enrollment must be used during that time period, and are non-transferable to any other party. Unused services expire at the end of the term and cannot be rolled over. The yearly membership dues are non-refundable even if the patient chooses not to utilize the plan, which is similar to a gym membership.
