Common Dental Emergencies

Toothache Relief

Severe tooth pain can disrupt your daily life and may indicate a severe issue such as tooth decay or an infection. If you experience persistent tooth pain, it’s essential to seek professional help immediately. Our team will diagnose the problem and provide appropriate treatment to relieve your pain and prevent further damage.

Abscessed Tooth

An abscessed tooth is a serious condition characterized by an infection at the tooth’s root or between the gum and tooth. Symptoms include severe pain, swelling, and pus around the tooth. If left untreated, this type of infection can spread and affect overall health. Immediate dental care is crucial to address the infection and save the tooth.

Broken or Knocked-Out Tooth

Accidents can happen, resulting in a broken or knocked-out tooth. Quick action is vital in such cases. If you can, place the tooth back in the socket without touching the root, keep it moist in milk, and seek dental care immediately. At Allen Dentistry, we will assess the situation and do our best to restore your original tooth. We offer various cosmetic solutions to replace the missing tooth if that’s impossible.
