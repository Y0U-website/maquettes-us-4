Implant-Supported Dentures

Why Choose Implant-Supported Dentures in Allen?

Stability and Security for Your Smile

No one wants to worry about dentures that shift or slip. With implant-supported dentures, you get a secure fit that stays in place—no sudden movement, even when you bite into crisp apples or share a laugh with friends. These dentures act like real teeth, letting you eat, speak, and smile naturally. At Allen Dentistry, we’ve seen how a stable smile builds real confidence in everyday life. You’ll likely notice yourself trying foods you used to avoid or joining in more conversations without second thoughts. That’s the power of a solution built to stay put.

Our Implant Process: What To Expect

Getting started at Allen Dentistry is straightforward and supportive. First, you’ll meet Dr. Justin Nylander for a one-on-one visit, where all your questions are welcome. We’ll evaluate your gums and bone, then map out a treatment plan meant only for you. Once everything looks good, we’ll schedule implant placement. After healing, we fit your custom denture to the implants, making adjustments so it feels like it belongs. Wondering about follow-up visits? We’re thorough, checking in along the way and even after your new smile is complete. You’ll never feel rushed or left in the dark.

Custom Solutions for Your Unique Needs

Caring for Your Implant-Supported Dentures

Taking care of implant-supported dentures is straightforward, especially with a little help from our team. We’ll show you how to brush around your implants, clean the denture, and spot anything unusual. Routine dental checkups are part of our approach—it keeps things on track long-term. Worried about maintenance? Most patients find that it becomes second nature, like brushing their natural teeth. We’re always a call away if you need a refresher or want to check in between visits. Your ongoing comfort and oral health are what keep us going. Our support doesn’t stop after that first big smile.
