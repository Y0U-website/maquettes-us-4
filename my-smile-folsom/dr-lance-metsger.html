Meet Dr. Lance Metsger - Dentist

Dr. Lance Metsger provides advanced, personalized dental care designed around each patient’s unique goals. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, graduating as Salutatorian and earning induction into Omicron Kappa Upsilon for academic excellence.

What sets Dr. Metsger apart is his comprehensive, in-house approach—including dental implants, full-arch “All-on-X” solutions, and teeth-in-a-day procedures—allowing patients to receive complete care in one place. He personally meets with every new patient outside the operatory to understand their needs and create a customized treatment plan, ensuring clarity, comfort, and confidence every step of the way.

Dr. Metsger is passionate about delivering dentistry that improves not just smiles, but overall quality of life—whether through routine care or full smile transformations. Outside the office, he enjoys hiking, cooking, gardening, and spending time with his cat, Gracie. He is married to Kayla Keppler, who shares his passion for dentistry and patient care.

If you’re looking for a dentist in Folsom who combines advanced treatment with a personalized experience, Dr. Metsger and the team at My Smile Folsom are here to help you achieve a confident, healthy smile.
