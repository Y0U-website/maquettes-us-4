Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are widely considered the gold standard for tooth replacement. Unlike dentures that might slip or bridges that require changing your healthy teeth, implants act like natural tooth roots. They are small titanium posts that a skilled dentist in Folsom places into your jawbone to provide a sturdy foundation for a custom crown. This process not only fills the gap but also keeps your jawbone healthy and strong.
