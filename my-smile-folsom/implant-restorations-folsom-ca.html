What Are Implant Restorations?

When you lose a tooth, the implant is the titanium post that acts like a new root under your gums. The restoration is the visible part that people see—the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture that sits on top of that post.

At our dental office in Folsom, we focus on creating these beautiful top pieces. We make sure they match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth perfectly. It is not just about filling a gap; it is about restoring the harmony of your mouth so you can speak and eat without a second thought.

Why You Might Need This Service

Living with missing teeth can lead to several problems over time. Your other teeth might start to shift out of place, or your jawbone might become weaker because it doesn’t have a tooth root to support. Choosing implant restorations near you is the best way to prevent these issues. Unlike traditional dentures that might slip or slide, a restoration attached to an implant is fixed firmly in place. It feels just like a real tooth, allowing you to bite into an apple or enjoy a steak with total ease.
