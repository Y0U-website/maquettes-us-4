Types of Dental Implants

At United Smiles Dentistry, we’re here to guide you through the best options for dental implants in Livermore, CA to restore your smile. Contact us today to schedule a consultation and take the first step toward a confident, comfortable smile!

  • Endosteal Implants: Endosteal implants are the most popular choice for local dental implants, as they are securely placed in the jawbone. Using screws, cylinders, or blades, these implants hold bridges or removable dentures in place, offering a reliable solution for missing teeth. The titanium root, similar to a screw, fuses with the bone, providing a sturdy foundation for full-mouth dental implants or single-tooth replacements.
  • Subperiosteal Implants: Subperiosteal implants are placed directly on top of the jawbone, just beneath the gums, offering a great solution for patients with insufficient bone density. A metal frame with posts is securely positioned under the gum, holding false teeth in place as the gums heal. If you’re not a candidate for traditional implants, this procedure could be the ideal choice for you.
  • All-on-4 Dental Implants: All-on-4 dental implants are an excellent option for adults who want to avoid dentures. This innovative procedure uses four titanium implants per jaw, replacing tooth roots with sturdy screws. After a minor surgery of placing implants in the jaw, a bridge is placed creating natural-looking, fully functional teeth that feel and work just like the real thing.
  • Implant Overdentures: If you’re healthy enough for a tooth extraction, implant-supported overdentures offer a fantastic alternative to traditional dentures. Placed on top of implants, these overdentures provide superior stability, making eating easier and speech clearer. Enjoy added comfort, no adhesive hassles, and in many cases, even use your existing dentures.

What Can You Expect During the Dental Implant Process?

  • Consultation: In our office, we will assess your dental health to determine whether you are qualified to undergo dental implants based on the condition of your jaw and the health of your gums.
  • Implant Placement: Eligible patients undergo surgical implant placement, where the implant serves as a stable foundation for prosthetic teeth.
  • Healing Period: After implant placement, there is a healing phase where the implant fuses with the surrounding bone tissue.
  • Restoration: In the last phase, a prosthesis specially made for the implant is placed on it, providing both function and aesthetic appeal.

FAQ

What are dental implants, and how do they work?

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that mimic natural teeth. A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as an artificial root, and is topped with a custom-fabricated crown for a natural look and function.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Ideal candidates have good oral health, sufficient jawbone density, and healthy gums. If you have missing teeth and want a long-term, natural-looking solution, dental implants may be right for you.

How much do dental implants cost in Livermore?

The cost of dental implants varies based on factors like the number of implants, additional procedures, and crown material. At United Smiles Dentistry, we offer competitive pricing and financing options to make implants accessible.

Will insurance pay for dental implants?

Some dental insurance plans cover part of the implant procedure, but coverage varies. We recommend checking with your provider, and our team can assist in maximizing your benefits.

Are dental implants painful?

Implant surgery is performed under local anesthesia or sedation, ensuring minimal discomfort. Most patients report mild soreness post-procedure, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
