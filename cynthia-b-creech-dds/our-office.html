Our Office

Our Office Nestled around the sleepy San Lorenzo River and guarded by towering redwoods, Ben Lomond offers its own unique culture. It is home to Highlands Park which formerly hosted the annual Highland Games Festival. In Park Hall the Mountain Community Theater puts on plays several times a year. Local color is on display at establishments like Ristorante Casa Nostra and the Santa Cruz Mountains Art Center.

Big Basin State Park to the north and Roaring Camp to the south, as well as the many hiking trails and swimming holes, provide easy access to enjoying this most beautiful region.

We are proud to offer our neighbors in the San Lorenzo Valley and surrounding areas over 20 years of experience in dental care.

New and existing patients are very warmly welcomed by our caring staff and treated with a gentle approach to all dentistry to allow our patients a positive experience.

To make it easier for new patients to get started, we have made all the patient forms available online which can be downloaded and filled in before visiting our practice. When you reach our practice, one of our team members will assist you with all the requirements such as form submission, Insurance claim cashless treatment, etc. Your appointment with the dentist can take 30- 90 minutes depending upon the type of treatment.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide the highest quality dental care in an atmosphere of trust and confidence.

  • We believe that every patient deserves to be treated with honesty, fairness and the utmost respect.
  • We believe in providing the highest quality of dental treatment to our patients.
  • We believe that we are partners in our patient’s overall health and that by creating healthy mouths, we improve our patient’s overall health and wellness.
  • We believe in educating our patients in regards to their dental needs and treatment options to create informed patients that are part of the decision making process regarding their oral health and treatment.
  • We believe in providing treatment in a manner as gentle as possible with compassion and care

To provide exemplary dental care with commitment to quality, integrity and compassion. Our highest priority is our patients and their overall health. We strive to create long-lasting relationships with our patients and community. We seek opportunities to constantly improve our care through continuing education and new technology.

Insurance and Payment Options

We currently offer the following payment options for treatment performed in our office:

As a courtesy to our patients, we will take care of sending claims to your insurance company for you. We accept most PPO insurance plans even though we are ONLY contracted with Delta Dental Premier. We are not contracted with Medicare, Medi-Cal or any HMO plans, including DeltaCare USA.

As a courtesy to you, we will bill your insurance company but you are responsible for the fees charged by our office, no matter what your insurance coverage may be. We do our best to provide accurate estimates but are not at fault for denial of coverage. Please keep us informed of any changes to your insurance plan.

While we make every reasonable effort to give our patients accurate cost estimates before treatment, we remind you that insurance policies are agreements between patients and insurance companies (not doctors and insurance companies).

Pre-treatment estimates can be sent to your insurance company before treatment is done. This is only done on request, so notify us if you would like to be sent a pre-treatment estimate. Also note that some insurance companies can take 30-60 days to process these requests. So please be patient with us.

Cash Patients (Those Without Insurance) We have many patients who do not have dental insurance coverage. We are able to offer a 5 % discount to cash patients for paying the complete balance the same day as treatment.

Care Credit We accept Care Credit, which is a credit card provided for health care services. Care Credit offers NO Interest plans, which can be spread over 6, 12, 18 or 24 months depending on the amount charged, with monthly payments as low as $25.00. Care Credit is accepted in many healthcare provider offices. It is easy to securely apply online and can even be done from your smartphone.

Dental Discount Plans Dr. Creech is not currently contracted with any dental insurance discount plans. We do however offer an in-house membership plan. Please see call for more information.

Veterans Dr. Creech is proud to serve the needs of our Veterans. Although she is NOT currently contracted to work with The Department of Veterans Affairs.

Cancellation/Missed Appointment Policy

We value our patients’ time and have reserved time especially for you in our schedule. Should you need to change or cancel an appointment, we require 48-hour notice. This advance notice allows us the time needed to call other patients wanting to be seen and offer them your appointment slot.

Our late cancellation fee is $75 per hour scheduled.
