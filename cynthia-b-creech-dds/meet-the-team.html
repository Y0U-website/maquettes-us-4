Cynthia B. Creech, DDS

Dr.Creech believes that dentistry is a field with continual learning opportunities. She takes several continuing education classes every year. She is a member of the Santa Clara and Monterey Dental Societies, as well as a member of the Spears Study Club, an advanced professional dental educational group. Outside the office Dr. Creech enjoys hiking, music, reading, traveling, spending time with her family and friends riding motorcycles and racing sidecars.

Kevin Ma, DMD, Associate Dentist

Nicole, Registered Dental Hygienist

Ann, Registered Dental Assistant

Ann has been a Registered Dental Assistant for over 18 years, having graduated from Bryman College in 1999. She was born and raised in Ben Lomond. Her considerable experience in the dental field makes her a wonderful addition to our staff. Ann has a gentle touch and compassionate nature that puts our patients at ease. Outside of work Ann enjoys gardening, scrapbooking and spending time with her family.

Diana, Registered Dental Assistant

Brenda brings a wealth of knowledge to our practice as our lead Registered Dental Assistant and our Back Office Manager. Brenda was raised in Santa Cruz County and received her Dental Assistant education from Bryman College in San Jose in 2004. She is passionate about patient care and makes sure that our patients feel comfortable at every appointment. Brenda loves spending time with her family and two dogs.

Jenny, Patient Coordinator

Jenny has lived in Ben Lomond for many years and has raised her family here. We are excited that she has joined our office and look forward to many more years to come!
