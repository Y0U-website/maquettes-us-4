Meet Matthew Lee, DMD

Matthew Lee, DMD

Beyond his clinical work, Dr. Lee is an active member of the Snohomish County Dental Society, where he serves on the executive committee, and is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Lee became a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry in July 2025, which is a distinction made to only a few dentists that have completed a rigorous amount of continuing education as well as passing an exam equivalent to a board certification exam.

When he is not working, Dr. Lee enjoys skiing in the winter, exploring and traveling to new places, and trying out new food spots with his friends and family. He values continuous learning and is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care to his patients.

Dr. Lee looks forward to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile!
