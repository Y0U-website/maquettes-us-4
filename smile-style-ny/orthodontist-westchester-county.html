Metal Braces

Metal braces are a popular orthodontic treatment that has been used for many years to straighten teeth and correct bite problems. They consist of brackets, which are bonded to the teeth, and wires that are threaded through the brackets. The wires are tightened periodically, putting gentle pressure on the teeth to gradually move them into the desired position. Over time, the teeth shift and become straighter and better aligned.

At Smile Style NY, we recommend metal braces to treat a wide range of orthodontic issues, including crowded or crooked teeth, gaps between teeth, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. They are especially effective for cases that are more complex and require a higher degree of movement or control. Metal braces are also a good option for patients who are not good candidates for other orthodontic treatments, such as Invisalign or ceramic braces.

Ceramic Braces

If you are looking for a more discreet alternative to traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces are a great choice. They work in a similar way to metal braces but use clear or tooth-colored brackets and wires that blend in with the teeth, making them less noticeable.

Ceramic braces are recommended for patients who have moderate to severe orthodontic problems that require the use of braces, such as crowded or crooked teeth, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. Dr. Eder uses the latest technology and materials in ceramic braces, which make them more durable and stain-resistant.

Invisalign

Invisalign is an innovative and effective orthodontic treatment that has helped millions of people achieve a straighter, healthier smile. It’s a popular orthodontic treatment that uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. The science behind Invisalign is based on the principles of orthodontics and biomechanics, which involve the application of gentle and consistent pressure to the teeth.

To begin your Invisalign journey, you’ll start with a consultation with Dr. Eder. She will use a specialized scanner to take digital impressions of your teeth, creating a 3D model that will be used to design a custom treatment plan. This plan will map out the movement of your teeth throughout the treatment.

Retention

After your braces or Invisalign treatment, your teeth have been moved into their new positions, but the surrounding bone and tissues need time to adjust and stabilize. This is where retainers come in. Retainers help to hold your teeth in their new positions while the surrounding tissues adapt and prevent them from shifting back to their old positions. Think of them as a safety net for your newly straightened teeth.

We offer retainers in a variety of types and materials, including clear plastic, wire, and even some that are cemented to the back of your teeth. Dr. Eder will recommend the type of retainer that is best for your case and lifestyle.
