Dental Implants in Garden City, GA

  • Implant Placement Dr. Verrillo surgically places the titanium implant into the jawbone using advanced techniques learned through his specialized training in implant dentistry.
  • Healing Period: Over several months, the implant integrates with your jawbone through osseointegration, creating a strong, stable foundation for your restoration.
  • Abutment Placement: Once integration is complete, a connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant to support your final restoration.
  • Final Restoration: Your custom crown, bridge, or denture is designed and attached to the implant, completing your tooth replacement with natural appearance and function.
