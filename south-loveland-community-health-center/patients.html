What to Expect

When you visit Sunrise the first time, you can expect the following:

1) Our friendly, bilingual front desk staff will greet you. You will fill out paperwork such as your health history, insurance information, and/or income, and our privacy policies. 2) We will call you back to an exam room to meet with your provider and other health care team members. 3) Please see our front desk staff before you leave - payment is due at the time of your visit.

Sunrise serves everyone, and fees are based on your ability to pay. We screen for eligibility for Health First (Medicaid) and Medicare, CHP+, subsidies on the Health Insurance Exchange, and our sliding fee scale. We accept most forms of private insurance and can set up payment plans. See below for information

Accepted forms of Identification

(original documents only):

  • State of Colorado drivers license or state identification card (valid)
  • Social Security Cards (for all members)
  • Birth Certificates (for all members)
  • Proof of Immigration Status: Resident Card
  • Naturalization Certificate
  • US passport
  • Work permit
  • Photo ID

Accepted forms for Proof of Income

  • All check stubs received from the previous month for the household
  • Employer Verification letter (if no pay stubs)
  • W-2 s and Federal Tax Forms (most current)
  • Unemployment Compensation Award Letter
  • Worker’s Compensation Award Letter
  • Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
  • Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)
  • Payments Receipts from pension plans
  • Award letter from Aid to the Needy and Disabled or Old Age Pension (OAP)
  • Profit and loss statement, ledger for self employment (proof of business expense)
  • Verification of Colorado address (Lease, utility bill with name & current address)
  • Vehicle registration
