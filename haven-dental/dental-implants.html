Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

With proper care and good oral hygiene, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium post that serves as your tooth root is designed to fuse permanently with your jawbone. The crown attached to the implant may need replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear.

Most adults with missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. Dr. Hsieh will evaluate your oral health, bone density, and overall health during your consultation. Conditions like gum disease or insufficient bone may require treatment before implant placement.

Most patients report less discomfort than they expected. We use local anesthesia during the procedure, and nitrous sedation is available for anxious patients. At Haven Dental, we also offer comfort amenities like warm towels and noise-canceling headphones to keep you relaxed throughout your visit.

The complete implant process typically takes three to six months, allowing time for the implant to fuse with your jawbone. During your consultation, Dr. Hsieh will provide a personalized timeline based on your specific needs and healing factors.

Costs vary depending on the number of implants needed and complexity of your case. During your consultation, Dr. Hsieh will provide detailed cost information and discuss payment options, including financing through CareCredit and Cherry. We work with you to create a treatment plan that fits your budget.

Caring for a dental implant is similar to caring for your natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss regularly, and visit Haven Dental for routine checkups and cleanings. With proper care, your implant can provide a lifetime of confident smiles.
