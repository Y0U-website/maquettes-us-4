What Is Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment moves your teeth into proper alignment using braces or clear aligners. Dentists call crooked teeth malocclusion, and it makes brushing harder, wears your bite down unevenly, and can strain your jaw over time. Fixing it protects the teeth underneath, not just how your smile looks. A lot of cases also touch on general dentistry, since crowded teeth trap plaque and lead to cavities faster than straight ones do.

Some patients come in purely for the look of a straighter smile. Most end up caring more about the bite once they understand what an uneven one does over ten or twenty years: worn enamel, jaw soreness, teeth that chip because they’re taking pressure they weren’t built for. Crowded teeth are also harder to clean around the gum line, and that’s often how mild Periodontics gum disease gets started in the first place. Orthodontic treatment fixes the cause, not just the symptom.

Clear Aligners Vs Braces — What’s the Difference?

Clear aligners are removable trays that shift teeth gradually and stay nearly invisible. Braces use fixed brackets and wires and handle more complex crowding or bite problems that aligners can’t reach on their own. Dr. Ripa tells you which one fits your case at your first exam, not after you’ve already picked one.

Orthodontic Services We Offer in Brooklyn

Clear Aligners

Clear, removable trays that shift your teeth gradually. Take them out to eat, brush, and floss like normal. Most adults searching for orthodontics near me pick this option because nobody notices you’re wearing them at all.

Traditional Braces

Metal brackets and wires built for the cases clear aligners can’t fully handle: heavy crowding, bigger bite corrections, teeth that need more force to move into place. Still the most reliable fix for complex orthodontic treatments. In the rare case where a bite correction needs jaw surgery too, we coordinate directly with our oral surgery team, so you’re not managing two separate offices.

Fast-Acting Braces

For patients who want to move through treatment faster without cutting corners on the result. We review your case first to confirm you’re a candidate, then set a realistic timeline instead of a guess.

Retainers & Follow-Up Care

Treatment doesn’t end when the braces come off or the last aligner tray is done. A custom retainer holds your new bite in place, and we check in to make sure everything’s settling the way it should. Orthodontic Treatment FAQ

How much does orthodontic treatment cost in Brooklyn?

It depends on your case and whether clear aligners or braces make more sense. You’ll see the exact number in writing after your exam, and we take Care Credit along with most PPO and Medicaid plans, so it’s rarely all out of pocket.

Am I too old for braces or clear aligners?

No. There’s no age cutoff on orthodontic treatment. Plenty of our adult patients start in their 30s, 40s, and beyond, usually with clear aligners since they’re nearly invisible at work or in meetings.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Most cases run 12 to 24 months. Fast-acting braces and milder clear aligner cases can wrap up sooner, depending on your bite and how consistently the aligners get worn. Dr. Ripa gives you a specific timeline at your first visit.

What’s the difference between clear aligners and braces?

Clear aligners are removable trays best suited to mild or moderate cases. Braces use fixed brackets and wires and handle more complex crowding or bite corrections that aligners can’t fully manage on their own.
