EXPLORE OUR ORTHODONTIC TREATMENTS

Welcome to Willis Orthodontics, where we offer a range of orthodontic treatments designed to suit your unique needs. Dr. Willis provides various treatment options, ensuring personalized care tailored to achieve your dream smile!

METAL BRACES

Metal braces, a traditional and reliable option for straightening teeth, effectively address a wide range of orthodontic issues. Made of high-quality stainless steel, these braces have advanced in technology to become more comfortable and efficient than ever before.

CLEAR BRACES

For those seeking a discreet alternative to traditional metal braces, clear braces offer a subtle solution. Made of translucent materials, such as ceramic or porcelain, clear braces blend seamlessly with your natural tooth color, providing an aesthetically pleasing option for orthodontic treatment.

CLEAR ALIGNERS

Dr. Willis offers clear aligners, similar to Invisalign , as a convenient and virtually invisible way to straighten your teeth. Similar to Invisalign, clear aligners are custom-made trays that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. With no metal wires or brackets, clear aligners offer unparalleled comfort and aesthetics, allowing you to achieve a beautiful smile discreetly.

PALATAL EXPANDERS

Palatal expanders, recommended as part of a comprehensive orthodontic treatment plan, are used to widen the upper jaw and improve bite alignment. Especially beneficial for patients with narrow dental arches or skeletal discrepancies, palatal expanders enhance overall facial symmetry by gently expanding the palate.

SURGICAL ORTHODONTICS

Dr. Willis collaborates closely with oral surgeons for surgical orthodontic solutions. This collaborative approach ensures comprehensive care and optimal outcomes for patients requiring advanced orthodontic treatments.
