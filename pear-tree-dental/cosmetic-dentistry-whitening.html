A dental crown is a restorative device used to cover or "cap" a damaged or weakened tooth, restoring its shape, size, strength, and appearance. Typically made from materials such as porcelain, ceramic, metal, or a combination of materials, crowns encase the entire visible portion of the tooth, providing protection and support. Crowns are custom-made to match the color and contour of the natural teeth, ensuring a seamless blend with the surrounding dentition. By reinforcing the tooth and enhancing its functionality, crowns help prevent further damage and maintain overall oral health.
