Preventative

  • 0 Preventative Care… What is it? At Pear Tree Dental, we are committed to helping you keep your teeth and gums healthy. Regular dental check-ups and daily oral hygiene practices can significantly reduce the risk of dental issues. Maintaining good oral health brushing and flossing 2x a day is essential for your overall well-being.
  • Visiting your dentist every six months for a routine examination and cleaning.
  • Getting x-rays taken at the dentist to catch any cavity forming early to prevent further damage

Did you know?

At Pear Tree Dental we are equipped with digital x-rays which emit 90 % less ionizing radiation than traditional x-rays. This also allows us to enhance the picture to better diagnose and treat our patients.

Our solution: we have found the most effective and interceptive treatment is to perform scaling and root plaining (deep cleaning above and below the gum line).

Signs and symptoms of periodontal disease: bleeding, swollen or tender gums, bad breath that doesn’t go away, loose teeth, pus between your teeth and gums, or a change in the way your teeth fit together. Early symptoms of minor Periodontal Disease can easily be treated at our dental office. More serious forms of Periodontal Disease may require referral to a specialist.
