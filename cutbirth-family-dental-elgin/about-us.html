Building on 35 years of small town Dentistry

We&#x27;ve been caring for Texans since the 90&#x27;s.

1990s

Dr. Preston Cutbirth and his wife Elizabeth launched the first Cutbirth dental practice in 1990 in Magnolia, TX to serve his hometown community.

2000s

The Magnolia practice grew rapidly, leading to the addition of a partner and the expansion into a second office in Tomball, TX.

2010s

As the Magnolia and Tomball practices grew, Dr. Cutbirth added an additional partner and expanded with an office in Huntsville, TX.

2020s

With the Magnolia, Tomball, and Huntsville offices thriving, the partners added a 4th location outside of The Woodlands, TX.
