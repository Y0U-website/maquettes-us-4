ABOUT DR. EGAN

Dr. Mary J. Egan has been providing dental care to the Sarasota and Bradenton areas for the last 15+ years and is excited to be opening her new practice this year!

Dr. Egan completed three years of nursing school and went on to receive her Bachelor of Science in Health Sciences from Marquette University in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Dr. Egan continued her post-graduate training and earned her Doctor of Dental Sciences degree in 1992 from the University of Iowa. While in Dental School, she served two years as the president of The American Society of Dentistry for Children. She then practiced in Denver, Colorado for over ten years as a general dentist.

Dr. Egan and her family moved to Florida in 2002 to enjoy the beautiful Florida lifestyle. In 2005, Dr. Egan completed a yearlong pedodontic residency program at the University of Florida. She has since practiced locally in Manatee and Sarasota Counties limiting her practice to children s dentistry. Dr. Egan and her family live in Sarasota and enjoy spending time on the water, playing tennis, and soaking up the Florida sunshine!
