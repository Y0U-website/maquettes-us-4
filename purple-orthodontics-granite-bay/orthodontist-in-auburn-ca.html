Invisalign Provider in Auburn, CA

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment offered at Purple Orthodontics, perfect for patients of all ages seeking a discreet and comfortable way to straighten their teeth. With Invisalign, you’ll receive a set of custom-made, clear aligners that are both convenient and discreet. Our aligners are designed to gently and effectively move your teeth into the desired position, providing a convenient alternative to traditional braces. We are pleased to provide Invisalign in Auburn, CA , to patients of all ages.

The process of Invisalign begins with a thorough consultation and digital scan of your teeth. With the utilization of cutting-edge 3D imaging technology, Dr. Marina Milstein will meticulously craft a customized treatment plan that details the exact movements of your teeth during the entire duration of the treatment. Each pair of aligners that you receive should be worn for about two weeks before moving on to the next set.

Braces in Auburn, CA

At Purple Orthodontics, we specialize in providing high-quality braces in Auburn, CA . that cater to the unique needs of each patient. Braces have long been a trusted method for correcting misaligned teeth and jaws, using a combination of brackets, wires, and bands to gently guide your teeth into their optimal positions. Our practice offers several types of braces , including traditional metal, Damon self-ligating metal, and Damon self-ligating clear braces.

The benefits of braces extend beyond achieving a beautiful smile. Having properly aligned teeth can greatly enhance your oral health. It allows for easier cleaning of your teeth, which in turn reduces the chances of developing cavities and gum disease. Braces can also alleviate issues related to bite alignment, such as difficulty chewing or speaking clearly.
