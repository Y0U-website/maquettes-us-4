Addressing Your Concerns: The Corbin Dentists’ Approach

I’m missing a single tooth. Why not just leave the gap?

  • The Problem Within weeks of losing a tooth, the adjacent teeth begin to shift into the empty space. This causes bite misalignment, crowding, and creates trap areas where decay can flourish on healthy teeth. Furthermore, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate without the stimulation of a root.
  • The Solution A single implant stops this domino effect immediately. It maintains the proper spacing of your smile, prevents bone resorption, and restores 100 % of your chewing function.

I have bone loss. Can I still get an implant?

  • The Problem When a tooth is missing for a long period, the jawbone melts away. You can lose up to 25 % of bone volume within the first year alone, which often makes traditional implant placement difficult.
  • The Solution At Corbin Dentists, we use advanced 3D CT Imaging to assess your bone density with sub-millimeter precision. If bone loss is present, we can perform bone grafting right here in our office to regenerate a strong foundation for your new implant.

I’m missing a front tooth, and I’m embarrassed to smile

  • The Problem A missing front tooth is more than a health issue; it’s a confidence issue. It affects how you speak, laugh, and interact with the world.

The Anatomy of a Single-Tooth Dental Implant

It is a three-part system engineered to replicate your natural tooth from root to crown.

  • The Implant Fixture (The Root): A medical-grade titanium post placed into the jawbone.
  • The Abutment (The Connector): A precision-engineered piece that connects the root to your new tooth.
  • The Crown (The Tooth): The visible porcelain part, handcrafted to match your surrounding teeth.

The Implant Procedure at Corbin Dentists

Consultation & 3D Imaging

We take a 3D scan to create a digital model of your mouth. This allows us to plan the exact depth and angle for the implant.

Preparatory Procedures

If necessary, we perform extractions or bone grafting to ensure the implant has a stable, healthy environment for long-term success.

Implant Placement

Using minimally invasive techniques, the titanium fixture is placed. This typically takes less than an hour under local anesthesia.

Healing (Osseointegration)

Over the next few months, your bone naturally fuses with the implant post. We will monitor your progress to ensure perfect integration.

Final Restoration

Once healed, we attach your custom-designed porcelain crown. You leave our office with a fully functional, beautiful new tooth.
