Advantages Over Traditional Implants

There are several reasons patients choose All-on-4 dental implants in Woodland Hills over other full-arch restoration methods. Because only four implants are required, the procedure is generally less expensive and less invasive. The tilted back implants allow us to avoid anatomical structures like the sinus cavities in the upper jaw, meaning even patients with some bone loss may be eligible.

All-on-4 dental implants in Woodland Hills offer faster recovery and fewer appointments than conventional approaches. With a fixed prosthesis, you won’t need to remove dentures for cleaning; instead, you simply brush and floss as recommended. In terms of aesthetics, we provide natural-looking teeth that support facial contours and help you feel confident when eating, speaking and laughing.

Caring for Your All-on-4 Prosthesis

  • Brush twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste.
  • Clean underneath the prosthesis by flossing or using a water flosser to remove trapped food particles and plaque.
  • Schedule regular dental visits to monitor implant stability and gum health.
  • Professional cleanings allow hygienists to gently clean around the implants and reinforce proper home-care techniques.
  • Avoid chewing hard objects such as ice or pens to prevent damage to the prosthesis.
  • If you grind your teeth, wear a custom night guard to protect the implants.
