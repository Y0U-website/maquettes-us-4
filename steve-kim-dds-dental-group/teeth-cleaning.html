Our Torrance Teeth Cleaning Treatment Process

Examination

The teeth cleaning process begins with a thorough examination of your teeth and gums. Dr. Kim will use a small mirror to check around your teeth and gums for any signs of gingivitis (inflamed gums) or other potential concerns.

Removing Plaque and Tartar

Using a tool called a scaler, Dr. Kim will then scrape off plaque and tartar from your tooth surfaces and gum line. You&#x27;ll hear a scraping sound, but this is normal. The more tartar there is in your mouth, the more time they&#x27;ll need to scale.

Cleaning and Flossing

Once your teeth are completely tartar-free, they will be cleaned with a high-powered electric brush and gritty toothpaste, a process known as prophylaxis. After this, professional flossing will be done. Even if you floss regularly at home, nothing beats an expert flossing session.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a professional teeth cleaning?

We recommend most patients come in for a professional cleaning about twice a year. Regular visits let us stay ahead of plaque and tartar buildup before it turns into something like gum disease or decay.

What does a professional cleaning remove that brushing at home can&#x27;t?

A professional cleaning removes tartar, which is plaque that&#x27;s hardened onto your teeth and can&#x27;t be brushed away no matter how thorough you are at home. Clearing it out is what protects you from the tooth decay and gum disease that tartar buildup tends to cause.

Can a dental cleaning affect more than just my teeth, like my overall health?

Yes, keeping up with cleanings does more than protect your teeth: they&#x27;ve also been linked to a lower risk of heart disease. On top of that, each visit gives us a chance to catch issues early, like cavities, broken fillings, or the first signs of oral cancer.

What happens during a routine teeth cleaning appointment?

We start with an exam of your teeth and gums to check for any early signs of concern. From there, we use a tool called a scaler to remove built-up plaque and tartar, then finish with a thorough polish and professional flossing.
