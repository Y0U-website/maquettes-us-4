These implants are typically made of titanium, a biocompatible material that fuses with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This integration provides stability and strength that are comparable to your natural teeth.

All-on-X dental implants, also known as full-arch implants, are a specific dental implant procedure designed to replace an entire arch of teeth in your upper or lower jaw. The "X" in All-on-X signifies the number of dental implants required to support the full arch of replacement teeth.
