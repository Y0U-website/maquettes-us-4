  • WHAT ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS? Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that Dr. Bekov surgically places into your jawbone to support replacement teeth or bridges. They serve as a sturdy foundation for various dental prosthetics, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. These implants are typically made of titanium, a biocompatible material that fuses with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration.
