Our Mission

At Distinctive Smiles, we treat our patients like family. Located in the heart of Las Vegas, our practice is built on the belief that everyone deserves access to high-quality dental care in a comfortable, welcoming environment. As a Las Vegas dentist, we re dedicated to supporting the health and well-being of our community, with a strong focus on the connection between oral health and overall wellness.

Meet Our Doctor

Experienced, caring, and deeply committed to your family s smile.

Dr. Trexton Davis

Dr. Trexton Davis is proud to be a true Las Vegas local–raised right here in the valley. After graduating from UNLV with degrees in both biology and entrepreneurship (because why stop at one?), he went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at the UNLV School of Dental Medicine. Before dental school, Dr. Davis served a two-year mission for his church in Portland, Oregon, where he learned the value of service, hard work, and that it really does rain…a lot.

During his undergraduate years, Dr. Davis married his high school sweetheart, and together they ve built a busy and joy-filled life with their four wonderful children. Family means everything to him, and he brings that same caring approach to his dental practice, treating every patient as if they were part of his own family.

When he s not honing his craft, Dr. Davis can often be found exploring the outdoors, especially hiking around Red Rock, or cheering on the Vegas Golden Knights. When he s not doing that, he s usually enjoying time with his wife and kids, whether that s a simple family dinner, a weekend adventure, or refereeing a spirited game of living room soccer.

Dr. Davis is passionate about creating a dental experience that combines clinical excellence with genuine kindness. His goal is simple: help patients smile with confidence while maybe even cracking a smile or two in the chair.
