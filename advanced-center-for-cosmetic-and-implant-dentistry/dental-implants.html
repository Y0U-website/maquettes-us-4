What Makes Our Dental Implants More Reliable?

Want to avoid complications and achieve long-lasting success with your dental implant ? Then choose our Miami dentist, Dr. Oscar Cascante .

He utilizes a CBCT scanner , which produces a 3D image of your smile. It shows your teeth, bone, sinuses, nerves, and more all at once. This helps Dr. Cascante determine the precise placement for the implant post for the best outcome.

The result? A durable, natural-looking smile!

  • Consultation and CBCT scan to confirm if you’re a candidate.
  • Dr. Cascante maps out your treatment then surgically places the post.
  • Once your implant has healed, an abutment is placed to secure your restoration.
  • Your restoration (crown, bridge, denture) is custom fabricated and placed.
