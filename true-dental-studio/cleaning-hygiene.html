The Benefits of Cleaning & Hygiene

Healthier Gums Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing alone can’t tackle, helping to prevent gum disease and support overall oral health.

Brighter Smile Cleaning & hygiene not only improve health. These routine visits also polish away surface stains, plaque, and tartar for a naturally brighter and fresher-looking smile.

Whole-Body Health Regular dental cleanings can reduce inflammation linked to systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes, promoting overall wellness.

What to Expect From Cleaning & Hygiene Comprehensive Exam When you arrive at True Dental Studio for your cleaning & hygiene visit, the first step is a thorough examination. Dr. True carefully assesses your teeth, gums, and overall oral health, often incorporating advanced diagnostics like X-rays and a wellness scan. These tools allow us to catch concerns like cavities, gum disease, or TMJ issues early. During the exam, we’ll discuss any questions or goals you have to ensure your care is tailored to you.

Gentle Cleaning

After your exam, our team will perform a professional teeth cleaning to remove plaque and tartar from those hard-to-reach areas. Using gentle yet effective techniques, we’ll clean along the gumline and between teeth to ensure a thorough cleanse without discomfort. We also polish teeth to smoothen surfaces and lift stains, rejuvenating both the health and beauty of your smile. Throughout the process, your comfort is always our priority.

Personalized Care Plan

Before you leave, we’ll provide you with a plan tailored to maintaining your smile’s health and beauty at home. Dr. True will also discuss any recommended follow-up treatments, like additional gum care or fluoride applications, to ensure lasting results. Finally, we’ll answer all your questions and help schedule your next visit. With our collaborative approach, you’ll leave feeling both confident and well-informed about your oral health.
