DDS

Dr. Jay Ahn, DMD

Dr. Jay Ahn was raised in North Jersey, obtained his undergraduate degree at Northwestern University in Chicago before moving back to New Jersey where he received his dental degree from the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2019.

Dr. Jay Ahn was raised in North Jersey, obtained his undergraduate degree at Northwestern University in Chicago before moving back to New Jersey where he received his dental degree from the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in 2019. The next year, he completed a one year general practice residency (GPR) in Monmouth Medical Center and has taken hundreds of hours of continuing education (CE) courses including several in extractions, implants and botox among others.

For the past three years prior to joining the team, Dr. Ahn had been working in four very different offices with diverse patient backgrounds and is comfortable adapting to the needs of his patients.

One thing about his work that Dr. Ahn most enjoys is being able to build a relationship with his patients and educating them on oral health and nutrition. He has advised and motivated many patients to be aware of their oral conditions and helped them take steps to make positive changes.

Dr. Ahn is passionate about health and nutrition and runs a 5k around his home every morning. He is an avid travel enthusiast with expertise in all things points and miles, hotels and airlines. He also comes from a tennis family with his sister, formerly a Top 100 pro in the WTA who played Serena Williams at the US Open.
