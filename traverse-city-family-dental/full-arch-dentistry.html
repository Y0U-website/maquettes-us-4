What Is Full-Arch Dentistry?

Traverse City Dentist

Full-arch dentistry is a comprehensive solution for patients missing most or all of their teeth in an arch. Using dental implants as secure anchors, an entire set of replacement teeth can be supported on just a few implant posts. This approach provides a stable, functional, and natural-looking alternative to traditional removable dentures.

The treatment process involves careful planning, detailed imaging, and the placement of implants into the jawbone. These implants serve as strong foundations for a custom full-arch prosthetic that restores your ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently. Unlike removable dentures that may shift or cause irritation, implant-supported full-arch restorations remain fixed in place, offering the closest possible experience to natural teeth. Dr. Mark Madion may recommend this option for patients seeking a long-term, life-changing solution that combines strength, aesthetics, and comfort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Traverse City Dentist

Why would I consider full-arch dentistry?

We may recommend full-arch dentistry if you’ve lost most or all of your teeth in one or both arches. Using strategically placed dental implants, we can restore your entire smile with a secure, natural-looking prosthetic that improves function, comfort, and long-term oral health.

How does full-arch dentistry differ from traditional dentures?

Unlike removable dentures that can slip or cause irritation, full-arch restorations are anchored by dental implants for superior stability. This means you can eat, speak, and smile confidently without worrying about movement, adhesives, or discomfort associated with traditional dentures.

What can I expect during full-arch restoration treatment?

Your treatment begins with a detailed consultation and imaging to plan your implant placement. After placing the implants, a healing period allows for integration with your jawbone. Once healed, we attach your custom-designed restoration, creating a beautiful, fully functional new smile.

How long does a full-arch restoration last?

With proper care, your implants can last a lifetime, and your restoration can last many years before needing replacement. Regular checkups, daily brushing and flossing, and avoiding smoking all help protect your investment and maintain long-term results.

Is full-arch dentistry right for me?

If you’re seeking a more stable and permanent alternative to dentures, full-arch dentistry may be ideal. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your oral health, bone structure, and goals to determine whether implant-supported restoration is the best solution for your smile.
