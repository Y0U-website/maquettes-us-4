The practice

We believe dentistry should look at the big picture. We aren’t here for a quick fix or a sales quota; we consider how your smile supports your overall health and long-term well-being. Whether you are coming to us for routine care or complex restoration, our priority is building a lasting relationship and being your dental home for life.

Dr. Ron Yaros is the founder of Aspenwood Dental Associates and the Colorado Dental Implant Center. He has been practicing in the Denver area for over 35 years, with a focus on dental implants and cosmetic dentistry.

After earning his undergraduate degree at the University of Denver, Dr. Aaron Sun received his DDS at the University of Colorado. Dr. Sun uses his advanced training and education to offer a variety of implant and cosmetic options to his patients.

General procedures typically correct the functioning of your teeth while helping improve cosmetic problems. We provide comprehensive general dentistry that is ideal for adults and children.

Our restorative dentistry can improve the function and appearance of your smile. Whether you’re missing a single tooth or multiple, our team can help give your smile the care it needs.

Family dentistry involves providing care to each and every member of your family. From toddlers to seniors, we’re able to offer comprehensive dental care for all your loved ones.

We’re centrally located to serve the entire Aurora and Denver Metro area, with easy access from major corridors and neighborhoods.
