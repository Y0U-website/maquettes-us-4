Welcome to W Paul Burns DDS

It takes years to develop unparalleled dentistry skills, such as technical proficiency, treatment planning, and patient communications. Residents of El Dorado, Arkansas, who rely on W. Paul Burns, DDS, for their dental care reap the many benefits of his nearly three decades of clinical experience.

Dr. Burns is a highly sought-after cosmetic and family dentistry specialist whose self-named practice has also become an invaluable resource for his community. Patients enjoy intentional, personalized care with no pressure to select unnecessary treatments and no rushed, assembly-line appointments.

At W. Paul Burns, DDS, the practice offers preventive dentistry as a primary focus, providing comprehensive dental exams and professional cleanings. Dr. Burns and his team also empower patients to take control of their dental health and hygiene.

When issues arise, proficient restorative treatments can correct dental decay, gum disease, and other dental health problems. The team provides implant crowns , same day crowns, dentures, bridges , and dental implants. Tools and technology from Dentsply Sirona are among the most advanced options within the dental industry.

They also offer cosmetic dentistry to improve a patient’s smile and confidence, using the very latest in dental technology that can create porcelain veneers, Invisalign , dental bonding, and tooth-colored fillings.

A great deal of time and effort goes into researching dental technology and selecting the best diagnostic and treatment platforms to introduce to the practice. Team members attend ongoing training to learn how to make the most of these advanced tools.

When seeking dental care, patients can count on affordability. W. Paul Burns, DDS, accepts most major types of dental insurance and files claims for patients. The practice also offers financing options for the uninsured and provides Kleer Membership, a basic preventive dental care plan for a low monthly cost.

To experience top quality dental care in a warm and welcoming dental practice, schedule a visit to W. Paul Burns, DDS. Book online or call the friendly administrative professionals today.
