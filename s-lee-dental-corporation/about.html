A brief professional biography of Sean S. Lee, D.D.S.

After receiving his DDS degree from the UCLA School of Dentistry, as an avid researcher, he has completed numerous research projects and published more than a hundred publications including abstracts in the dental journals. As of this writing, he holds a faculty position as a professor in a dental school in California. He has authored nine books, for example, BREATH: causes, Diagnosis and Treatment of Oral malodor, Second Edition and MOD: Modi Operandi of Dentistry . These books were intended to provide information on oral malodor and practice management for clinicians.

Diligent in helping his patients and colleagues, some of whom have had concerns regarding all issues well beyond the mouth, Dr. Lee began to study and write. Sharing, with no frills, his experiences and issues that have arisen during his professional career is most appreciated by other researchers, dental professionals, and the public. He values his patients.
