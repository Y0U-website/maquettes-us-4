Dr. Gary Sanchez graduated in October 2002 from Centro Escolar University College of Dentistry located in Manila, Philippines.

Dr. Sanchez enlisted in the United States Air Force, and served from January 1992-1996, where he worked as a dental assistant with residents of the Advanced General Dentistry program.

After leaving the military, he decided to take the state board for his dental license in California and achieved his goal in May 2006. He has been practicing dentistry ever since and is an active member of the ADA, CDA, and the Southern Alameda County Dental Society.

When not working at the office, Dr. Sanchez loves to spend time with his wife and 2 boys; and, with their newest family member Beau, a Chocolate Lab. You might even catch Dr. Sanchez and his wife taking Beau for a walk around the neighborhood.
