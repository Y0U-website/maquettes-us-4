Outside of the Dental Office, What Does Dr. Tim Like to Do?

Dr. Tim and our own Dr. Liz met while in dental school and are now the proud parents of two wonderful boys, Everett and Beau. Most of his free time is dedicated to them, and he also plays guitar in a rock cover band called Man Hands with fellow dentists. He likes to stay busy by woodworking and doing various projects around the house, and to relax, you’ll often find him watching UConn basketball.
