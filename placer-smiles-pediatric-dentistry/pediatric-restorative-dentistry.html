Pediatric Pulpotomies

Is your child experiencing a toothache while eating? It might be a sign of a large cavity, but fear not! Our pulpotomy procedure can alleviate their discomfort in a single visit. Think of a pulpotomy as a "baby root canal" that stops the cavity before an infection starts. By choosing this treatment, we can restore your little one&#x27;s oral health and bring back their bright smile, allowing them to continue enjoying all the laughter and carefree moments of childhood.
