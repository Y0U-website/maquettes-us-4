Dr. Cody Wiktorski

Dr. Cody Wiktorski is an extremely skilled, board-certified orthodontist who is passionate about helping patients of all ages achieve healthy, confident smiles. He takes a thoughtful, personalized approach to treatment and believes clear communication and trust are the foundation of great orthodontic care. Dr Cody has been practicing orthodontics in the Buffalo, NY region for the last few years and is excited to continue making an impact at Winning Smiles.

In his free time, Dr Cody enjoys hiking, reading, and training individuals in Youth Mental Health First Aid. He has been a national instructor through the National Council for Mental Well-Being for the last 3 years and continues to teach school districts in the Western New York area when he is not in the office seeing patients.

Dr. Kathleen Rusnak

Dr. Kathleen Rusnak is a native of Buffalo, NY. She completed her undergraduate studies in biology at SUNY Geneseo and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine where she graduated magna cum laude. Dr. Kathleen has a passion for serving the oral health needs of children and went on to complete a two-year residency in pediatric dentistry from the University at Buffalo.

Dr. Kathleen is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, and Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. Dr. Kathleen is a board-certified pediatric dentist and is passionate about providing dental education to patients and parents. She believes that each child should have a positive dental experience. Outside the office, Dr. Kathleen and her husband have two cats and enjoy hiking and SCUBA diving.

Dr. Michael Ehlers

Dr. Michael Ehlers attended UB School of Dental Medicine, where he earned the title Doctor of Dental Surgery. He is also involved in Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association and New York State Dental Association.

An accomplished dentist and local dental leader and mentor, Dr. Ehlers brings over 35 years of experience to dentistry. His areas of expertise include general dentistry, complex dental issues, restorative and prosthetic dentistry, dental implants, sedation and oral surgery. A true diehard Buffalo Bills fan, he’s held season tickets for more than 30 years.

Dr. Jenna Heitzler

Dr. Jenna Heitzler believes great orthodontic care starts with trust, connection, and treating every patient like family. A Westchester, New York native, her educational path somehow kept her in cold climates, earning her Bachelor of Science degree at the University of Wisconsin–Madison and her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine.

She completed her residency in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics, along with a Fellowship Certificate in Pediatric Orthodontics, at the Consortium for Translational Orthodontics Research (CTOR) in Hoboken, New Jersey. She is comfortable treating patients of all ages and prides herself on building genuine connections, whether that means guiding a child through their first orthodontic experience or chatting with adults about life while perfecting their smile.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Heitzler enjoys traveling, staying active through workout classes, experimenting with healthy homemade ice cream recipes using her Ninja Creami, spending time with family and friends, and taking long walks with her goldendoodle, Ryder.

Dr. Ashley Acevedo

Dr. Ashley truly enjoys developing a unique relationship with her patients and their families throughout their treatment. She prides herself in treating each patient like family and ensuring that she understands their individual treatment needs. Her philosophy is to provide exceptional orthodontic treatment and personalized care in a friendly, comfortable environment.

A NYC native, Dr. Ashley graduated from Stony Brook University where she received a Bachelor of Science in Business Management. She went on to receive her dental degree from the University of Connecticut and completed her specialty training in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at the University of Buffalo in 2017. During her residency, she became Invisalign certified, completed her master’s thesis in the field of 3D Imaging, and attended many continuing education courses where she learned the newest technologies and techniques in orthodontics.

Dr. Ashley is committed to lifelong learning in order to provide the highest standard of care to her patients and help them achieve beautiful smiles. Her research has been published in the American Journal of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics and she is an active member of several professional associations and organizations.
