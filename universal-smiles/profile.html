WELCOME TO UNIVERSAL SMILES

Tooth decay is the most common chronic disease affecting U.S. Children. It affects five times more children than does asthma. Children with oral diseases miss over 51 million hours of school each year.

Universal Smiles is a mobile dental team that provides professional quality preventive dental care to students. The services provided are dental examinations, dental cleanings, fluoride treatments, dental sealants and oral health education. No needles or anesthetic is ever used.

Our Mission is to always provide warm, professional dental services and oral health education to children and their families.
