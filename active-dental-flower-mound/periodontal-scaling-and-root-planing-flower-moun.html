Who Qualifies for Scaling and Root Planing Treatments

Scaling and root planing are often recommended for individuals experiencing mild to moderate gum disease. During your visit, our team will examine your gum health, looking for signs of inflammation and measuring the space between your gums and teeth. Starting treatment promptly can significantly improve your chances for successful recovery. Individuals with pocket depths greater than 5 mm may find these treatments particularly beneficial.

The Periodontal Scaling and Root Planing Process Explained

At Active Dental, we ensure a comfortable experience during your periodontal treatment. To begin, we will numb one side of your mouth to minimize discomfort. Our skilled hygienists utilize specialized tools to meticulously clean below the gum line and around the teeth. This process involves removing hard deposits of tartar and plaque, followed by polishing the root surfaces to promote healthy gum healing. For optimal results, you may need a follow-up appointment to complete the treatment on the opposite side of your mouth.

How Scaling and Root Planing Promotes Gum Health

Effective scaling and root planing is crucial for reducing inflammation, improving gum health, and enhancing your overall oral hygiene. By eliminating harmful bacteria and promoting the closure of gum pockets, we can effectively halt the progression of gum disease and support your body’s natural healing processes.
