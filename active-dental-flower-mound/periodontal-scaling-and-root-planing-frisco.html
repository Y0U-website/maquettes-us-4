Frequently Asked Questions About Periodontal Scaling and Root Planing

This is a non-surgical treatment that involves deep cleaning of the gum line to remove plaque and tartar, helping to treat and prevent gum disease.

Typically, each side of the mouth is treated in separate appointments, with each session lasting about an hour, depending on the severity of gum disease.

Local anesthesia is applied to minimize discomfort during the treatment, and post-treatment soreness is usually manageable with over-the-counter medications.

Many patients notice improvements in gum health and reduced symptoms shortly after their treatment.

Regular dental visits, good oral hygiene practices, and following our post-treatment care recommendations will support your ongoing gum health.
