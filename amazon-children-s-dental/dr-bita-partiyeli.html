Meet Dr. Bita Partiyeli

Dr. Bita Partiyeli provides gentle, compassionate pediatric dental care for children at Amazon Children Dental in South Los Angeles.

With a focus on children’s oral health, Dr. Bita is dedicated to creating a comfortable and positive dental experience for every child, including children with special needs.

Our goal is to help children develop healthy smiles and positive dental habits that last a lifetime.
