Consultations for Kids Braces

Start Your Child’s Orthodontic Journey

Every child’s smile develops differently. An orthodontic consultation gives our team an opportunity to evaluate your child’s teeth, bite, and jaw development and discuss whether orthodontic treatment may be appropriate.

Whether you have concerns about crowded teeth, spacing, or the way your child’s bite is developing, we’re here to help you understand the next steps.

Why Preventive Dental Care Matters Regular dental visits help protect your child’s smile now and for the future.

Orthodontic Care That Grows With Your Child

Not every child needs orthodontic treatment at the same age. Some children may simply need their dental development monitored, while others may benefit from orthodontic treatment.

Our goal is to help families understand their child’s developing smile and make informed decisions about the appropriate next steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child see an orthodontist?

Around age 7 for an initial evaluation, even if no issues are obvious.

Do adults need orthodontic treatment too?

Yes—many adults choose braces or Invisalign to improve alignment and oral health.

What’s the difference between braces and Invisalign?

Braces are fixed and highly effective for complex cases; Invisalign uses clear aligners and is more discreet.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Typically 12–24 months, depending on the case.

Does insurance cover orthodontic treatment?

Some plans partially cover treatment, especially for children. Call us and we can review your options!

Do braces hurt?

There may be mild discomfort after adjustments, but it usually subsides within a few days.

What foods should be avoided with braces?

Sticky, hard, or chewy foods like caramel, popcorn, and hard candy..

How do I brush and floss with braces?

Using special tools like floss threaders and interdental brushes helps keep teeth clean.
