Dental Cleanings SLC, UT

Our dental team will begin with an initial comprehensive exam and x-ray. This will help us view any impacted or erupted teeth. This initial exam will allow our team to tailor your dental cleaning to your specific oral health needs.

Our dental hygienist will use a scaler to remove any plaque and tartar between your teeth and the gum line. Keep in mind that tartar can only be removed at a dental office with the right training and tools.

Once the plaque and tartar are removed, our dental hygienist will use a powerful toothbrush to further clean your teeth. Then, we’ll floss your teeth to prevent bacteria build up and gum disease.

After brushing and flossing, we’ll remove any leftover toothpaste with a quick rinse. If necessary, we may provide a fluoride treatment to strengthen the enamel of the teeth.
