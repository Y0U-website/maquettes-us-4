Root canals become necessary when the pulp of your tooth becomes infected. During a root canal, the dentist clears out the infected pulp from the tooth. A root canal may be the last way to prevent tooth loss, which is why it is important to have tooth problems treated as soon as possible.

Having wisdom teeth removed may be essential in protecting your smile. Wisdom teeth can cause overcrowding and even infection, if the tooth becomes impacted. They can make it more difficult to clean your teeth, leading to even more problems. We offer wisdom tooth removal services to prevent all of these problems from occurring.

Veneers are one of the most efficient ways to drastically improve your smile. They are thin ceramic shells that are placed over your teeth. Veneers are able to completely customize your smile, allowing you to choose size, shape, and shade of white. Contact Avenues Dentistry to see if Veneers are the right option for you!

Dentures are ideal for replacing a full set of teeth. They can greatly improve your quality of life by allowing you to chew food properly and restoring your smile. Our Salt Lake City dental office specializes in dentures and dental fittings, contact us today!

Dental bridges are an option for replacing multiple teeth. They are typically anchored to the surrounding teeth, so they do require healthy teeth left in the mouth. Dental bridges are able to replace one or multiple teeth, depending on your needs.

No method of whitening is quite as effective as an in-office whitening treatment. We are able to brighten your teeth by several shades, usually in less than an hour. Teeth whitening treatments can significantly increase your confidence, as well as the appearance of your smile.
