Initial Consultation

We assess your dental and overall health to determine if implants are the right solution for you. This may include digital imaging and 3D scans to evaluate your jawbone structure.

Implant Placement Surgery

During the procedure, we make a small incision to insert a titanium post into your jawbone. This acts as a foundation for your new tooth.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with your bone through a process called osseointegration. This ensures the implant is stable and strong enough to support a restoration.

Restoration

Once the implant is fully integrated, we place a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture on top of the implant, completing the restoration process.

Aftercare & Maintenancelacement Surgery

Following your treatment, we provide instructions for aftercare to ensure proper healing. Regular check-ups and good oral hygiene will help your implant last for many years.
