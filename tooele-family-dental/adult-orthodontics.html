Adult Orthodontics

Not only does adult orthodontics help with the aesthetics of your smile, but it also helps address functional dental issues like improving bite, reducing wear on the teeth, and improving your overall oral health. Some adults can face different or unique challenges when it comes to orthodontic treatment such as slower movement of the teeth and possibly some bone density issues. We have the solution with our advancements in orthodontics, we can manage and overcome these obstacles together.

  • Ceramic braces This option is great for a less noticeable look thanks to the tooth-colored brackets.
  • Lingual braces This customized option is placed behind the teeth so they can offer a more discreet treatment.
  • Clear aligners This option provides the convenience of being removable, and almost invisible option for treatment. This is the most popular thanks to the convenience and aesthetics of the aligners.

Common Myths

Myth: Orthodontic treatment is only for kids and teens. Fact: Orthodontic treatment is for patients of all ages, not only just for kids. There is no age limit to who can receive treatment and improve your smile.

Myth: Adult orthodontic treatment takes longer. Fact: This will depend on your specific treatment plan. However, adult orthodontics can be just as efficient as the ones used to treat children and teens.

Myth: Adult orthodontics is only for cosmetic purposes. Fact: There are so many benefits to receiving orthodontic care other than aesthetics, they can help improve the function of your oral health which can include improving bite alignment and jaw function.
