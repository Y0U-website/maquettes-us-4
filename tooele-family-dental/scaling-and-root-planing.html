What is Dental Scaling and Root Planing

Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning that cleans the tartar and plaque deep below the gums. This cleaning helps the gums start fresh and heal from all of the bacteria and infections that the plaque and tartar cause. If you have gum disease, the scaling and root planing cleaning may be the treatment that could help get the gums back to their health.

In most cases, gum disease will begin by the build up of bacteria and plaque that is below the gums which can cause inflammation. This will make it more difficult to clean the gums sufficiently because of sensitivity.

The Benefits for Your Gum Health

By getting a scaling and root planing cleaning, you can help protect and fight against gum disease. This type of treatment will help reduce the inflammation and create a clean slate for the gums to heal and attach to the tooth.

What to Expect

The dentist will use an ultrasonic scaler that vibrates to remove tartar and plaque from your teeth. Finally, hand instruments are used to remove any remaining small pieces of tartar.

Common Concerns

Is scaling and root planing painful? No. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you will not feel any pain during the treatment.

How long does the recovery take? Most patients are able to go back to their normal activities right after the procedure. Some patients may experience sensitivity or discomfort, but that should be temporary and go away within a short period of time.
