Our Story

New River Valley Orthodontics was founded with one simple goal: to deliver exceptional orthodontic care in a warm, family-friendly environment. Located in the heart of the New River Valley, we proudly serve Blacksburg, Christiansburg, Radford, and the surrounding communities.

Our practice combines the latest technology with genuine compassion. Whether you are a child, teenager, or adult, we believe every patient deserves a personalized treatment plan and a smile they are proud to show the world.

Julie Weeks

Julie was born and raised in Floyd and is married to her husband, Danny. She has been a dental assistant since 1994 and started in orthodontics in 2014. She loves building relationships with her orthodontic patients, educating them on the importance of good oral health, watching them grow up, and seeing their transformation when their braces come off. You can find Julie working on teeth during the week and working as part of their excavating business on Fridays. Her favorite thing and goal is to live at the beach one day!
