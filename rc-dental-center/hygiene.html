How often should you have a dental hygiene visit?

Most patients would benefit for a dental hygiene visit every 6 months. However, the frequency can depend on many factors. If you are more susceptible to plaque and calculus accumulation, or if you have history or signs of periodontal diseases, you might want to have dental hygiene or periodontal maintenance services every 3 - 4 months.
