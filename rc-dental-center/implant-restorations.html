0 Implant Restorations What is dental implant? Dental implant is an artificial root usually made of Titanium material placed into the jaw bone surgically. After the surgical procedure, it allows osseointegration of the artificial root and your own bone, which would take about 4-6 months before final dental implant restorations. Dental implant is an alternative treatment option to removable dentures or dental bridge for missing tooth/teeth.

What is dental implant restoration?

Dental implant restoration is crown, bridge or denture that screwed, cemented or fitted on top of the dental implant to replace the missing tooth/teeth. Dental implant restoration is the part you would see in the mouth. Once dental implant had been integrated with your own bone and ready for restoration, impression would be taken for fabrication of your dental implant restorations.
