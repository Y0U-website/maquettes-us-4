What to Expect When Getting All-on-X Dental Implants

  • First Consultation During your initial consultation, Dr. Tsibel will thoroughly examine your oral health. He’ll review your X-rays and physically examine your jawbone to assess its structure. By evaluating your jaw, Dr. Tsibel will determine the most effective approach for implant placement.
  • Customized Treatment Plan After reviewing your needs, Dr. Tsibel will develop a treatment plan tailored to your goals.   • Implant Placement Procedure On the surgery day, Dr. Tsibel will place titanium posts into your jawbone. Depending on your comfort level, Dr. Tsibel will offer local anesthesia or sedation to ensure a painless experience. He’ll carefully plan the placement of the implants to ensure optimal results.
  • Temporary Teeth Once Dr. Tisbel has placed the implants, he will provide temporary teeth that stay in place while the implants heal and fuse with your jawbone.
