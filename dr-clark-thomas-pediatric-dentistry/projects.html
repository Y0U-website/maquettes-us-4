Why Pediatric Dentistry?

Why Choose A Pediatric Dentist?

We are specialists in pediatric and adolescent dentistry, focusing exclusively on the developmental years–the most important years–of your child’s dental care. In many cases, you may have come to us because of a referral by your general dentist or pediatrician, but you don’t need a referral to see us. We are happy to make your appointment directly.

What Does A Pediatric Dentist Do?

A pediatric dentist is specially trained and certified to meet the particular needs of children and teens. This is a very special certification that requires two additional years of extensive training and continuing education.

What Are The Advantages?

As a pediatric dentist, Dr. Thomas and his staff can help prevent dental-related issues that can be corrected more easily in childhood. In all likelihood, care now will also be less expensive than problems later. At Thomas Pediatric Dentistry, your child will be safe, comfortable and well cared for. We provide a truly satisfying experience that, at this stage in your child’s development, will lay the groundwork for a lifetime of dental health.
