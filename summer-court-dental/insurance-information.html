Insurance Information

Summer Court Dental is proud to say that we are Premier contracted members with Northeast Delta Dental.

As members of Northeast Delta Dental we are participants in the H.O.W. (Health through Oral Wellness) Program. This program will increase your dental preventative coverage to include up to 4 dental cleanings and/or periodontal maintenance. It also includes 4 fluoride treatments, as well as sealants at any age! This increase of benefits is done at NO charge to you or your employer. Please feel free to talk to our front office staff for more information.

For our Dartmouth Students with NO dental insurance, we are pleased to inform you are enrolled in the Basix program by your school. This program entitles all new patients to have a New Patient Exam, Bite-wing X-rays, and a Dental Cleaning all done at a rate of $150. (This is a HUGE SAVINGS!) All future services in our office (ie. restorative needs, dental examinations, cleanings, ) will be done with the 20 % Basix Program promotional.

Summer Court Dental also offers various In-House referral programs!

For our n ew esthetic side of our practice we are offering promotional discounts for Micro-Needling, Botox, and Fillers. Inquire in office for more details.

For dental referrals we are offering FREE Opalescence Whitening To-Go! These are upper and lower whitening trays, that are quick and easy to apply with no mess, and NO sensitivity.
