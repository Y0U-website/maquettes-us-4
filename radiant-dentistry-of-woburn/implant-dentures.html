What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are a type of overdenture that attaches to dental implants placed in your jawbone. Instead of resting on your gums alone, the denture snaps onto small attachments on the implants. This gives you a secure fit that stays in place while you eat, speak, and laugh.

How They Differ from Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures sit directly on your gum tissue and rely on suction or adhesive to stay put. Over time, they can become loose as your jawbone changes shape. Implant-supported dentures use implants to mimic the function of natural tooth roots. That means no slipping, no sore spots, and no worry about your denture moving out of place.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

For many people, traditional dentures bring daily frustrations. Food gets stuck underneath, speech can be muffled, and you may avoid certain foods. Implant-supported dentures solve these problems by providing a stable foundation.

Stability, Comfort, and Confidence You Can Count On

When your denture is anchored to implants, you can bite into an apple or enjoy a steak without hesitation. You no longer need pastes or adhesives. Your denture feels natural and secure, and you speak clearly without clicking. Many patients tell us they feel more confident smiling and socializing. And because implants stimulate your jawbone, they help preserve your facial structure and prevent the sunken appearance that can develop with traditional dentures.

Are You a Candidate for Implant-Supported Dentures?

Most people who are missing most or all of their teeth on an arch can be candidates. You need enough healthy jawbone to support the implants, or you may be a candidate for bone grafting. Good overall health and healthy gums are also important. Dr. Austria will perform a thorough exam using 3D CBCT imaging to assess your bone density and plan your treatment. Even if you have worn dentures for years, you may still qualify.
