What Is a Dental Bone Graft and Who Needs One

A dental bone graft adds bone material to your jaw where bone has been lost. The graft creates a strong foundation for a dental implant . Without enough bone, an implant cannot fuse properly and may fail over time.

Common Reasons Jaw Bone Is Lost

Why Bone Grafting Matters for Implant Success

A secure implant needs enough healthy bone to hold it. A bone graft creates that foundation. The result is a longer-lasting, more stable replacement tooth that looks and feels natural. Skipping a needed graft often leads to implant failure or the need to consider alternative options like implant-supported dentures that work with less bone.

The Bone Grafting Procedure Step by Step

Numbing

Local anesthesia ensures you stay comfortable throughout.

Incision

A small opening is made in the gum to access the bone.

Graft placement

The graft material is placed and secured.

Closure

The gum is stitched closed to protect the graft.

The procedure itself typically takes less than two hours. Most patients tell us they were surprised by how smooth the experience was.

Healing and Next Steps Toward Your Implant

After the graft, you will follow simple care instructions to protect the site. Most patients return to normal activities within a few days. Once the bone has healed enough, usually 3 to 6 months, you can proceed to implant placement. Dr. Austria uses digital implant planning to ensure the implant goes exactly where it needs to. For patients needing multiple teeth replaced, options like All-on-4 dental implants may also be discussed once your bone has healed.
