What Is Digital Implant Planning?

Digital implant planning uses cone‑beam CT (CBCT) technology to create a three‑dimensional view of your teeth, jawbone, nerves, and sinuses. Instead of relying on traditional X‑rays and guesswork, we visualize the entire surgical area in precise detail. This allows Dr. Austria to determine the ideal position, angle, and depth for your implant before any incision is made.

How CBCT Imaging Creates Your 3D Treatment Map

A CBCT scan is quick and comfortable — you sit upright while the machine rotates around your head. The resulting data is assembled into a digital model that you can see on our screen. This map reveals bone density, identifies hidden anatomy, and lets us plan around structures that could complicate surgery.

From Scan to Surgical Guide: The Planning-to-Placement Process

After your scan, Dr. Austria uses specialized software to plan the implant placement virtually. Once the plan is finalized, we design a custom surgical guide — a template that fits over your teeth and guides the drill with pinpoint accuracy. On the day of surgery, the guide transfers the digital plan directly to your mouth, making the procedure faster and more predictable. For patients needing bone volume, we often coordinate this process with bone grafting .

Why Precision Planning Makes a Difference for Your Implant

Moving from a freehand approach to computer‑guided implant surgery dramatically reduces uncertainty. The difference is not just in accuracy — it affects your recovery, your comfort, and your long‑term result.
