All-on-4 Dental Implants in Woburn, MA — Full-Arch Restoration

The All-on-4 approach replaces a full arch of missing teeth using just four strategically placed dental implants. Unlike removable dentures, these implants provide a stable, permanent foundation. You get a smile that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

What Is the All-on-4 Procedure?

How Four Implants Support a Full Arch

The name describes the method. Four titanium posts are placed into your jawbone. The two front implants go in straight, while the two back implants are angled to maximize contact with existing bone. This angled placement helps avoid the need for bone grafting in many cases. A custom set of prosthetic teeth is then attached to the implants. The result is a full arch of teeth supported by a solid foundation.

Teeth in a Day — What That Actually Means

During your surgery, we place the implants and attach a temporary set of teeth. You leave our office with a functional, fixed smile on the same day. This temporary arch allows you to eat soft foods and speak clearly while your implants fuse to your bone. After 4 to 6 months of healing, the final permanent teeth are placed. You never have to go without teeth.

Who Is a Candidate for All-on-4 in Woburn?

Common Candidacy Factors

Most patients with significant full-arch tooth loss can be candidates. You need enough jawbone to support four implants. If you’ve been missing teeth for a while, some bone loss may have occurred. In those cases, Bone Grafting can build up the area before or during implant placement. Good overall health, no active gum disease, and a commitment to oral hygiene also matter.

How CBCT Imaging and Digital Planning Support Your Case

We use 3D CBCT imaging to measure your bone density and volume. This scan lets Dr. Austria plan implant placement down to the millimeter. It helps identify any need for grafting before surgery. Digital planning reduces surprises and improves precision. You can see a preview of your new smile before any work begins.

The Procedure Step by Step

On surgery day, you receive local anesthesia and sedation for your comfort. Dr. Austria places the four implants following the digital plan. After implant placement, a temporary set of teeth is attached. You wake up with a full arch of teeth. Recovery is typically a few days of rest and soft foods. Follow-up visits monitor healing. When your bone has fused completely, we replace the temporary arch with your final custom teeth.

Recovery and Long-Term Care

You’ll have some swelling and mild discomfort for 2-3 days. We give you instructions for ice packs, pain relief, and diet. Most patients return to work within a week. Long-term care is simple. Brush and floss around the implants like natural teeth. Regular checkups ensure everything stays healthy. With good care, your All-on-4 implants can last for decades.
