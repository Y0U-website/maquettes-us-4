At Seaport Family Dentistry, your oral health is our top priority.

  • Expert Care: With years of expertise, we provide thorough, patient-centered services you can trust.
  • Commitment to Prevention: By focusing on routine care, we help you avoid uncomfortable and costly dental issues.

Expect More From Seaport Family Dentistry

Professional Teeth Cleaning

Routine professional cleanings go above and beyond what regular brushing and flossing can achieve. Our team will carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup while polishing your teeth for a fresh, clean feel.

  • Scaling: Removing harmful plaque and tartar around your gumline.
  • Polishing: Smoothing and shining your teeth for a brighter appearance.
  • Fluoride treatments (optional): Strengthening your enamel to prevent cavities.
  • Prevent gum disease and tooth decay with deep cleaning.
  • Enjoy fresher breath and a polished smile.
  • Safeguard your oral health long-term.

Effective Dental Fillings

Even with preventive care, cavities can occasionally develop. Our dental fillings are designed to repair tooth damage efficiently and restore your smile seamlessly. Using durable, tooth-colored materials, we ensure fillings blend naturally with your teeth.

  • Removal of decayed tooth material with care.
  • Placement of a smooth, long-lasting filling that matches your natural teeth.
  • Preservation of your tooth’s structure and functionality.
  • Quickly address and repair cavities to prevent further damage.
  • Maintain your smile’s appearance with natural-looking results.
  • Avoid more extensive dental treatments down the road.

Comprehensive Dental Exams

Regular dental exams are essential for identifying issues early and preserving your oral health. During your appointment, our skilled team will meticulously review your dental situation.

  • Perform a detailed assessment to check for cavities, gum disease, and other concerns.
  • Screen for oral cancer to ensure early detection of any abnormalities.
  • Use advanced diagnostic tools, such as X-rays, to assess your dental health below the surface.
  • Catch small issues before they become major problems.
  • Protect your smile and overall health with early detection.
  • Receive personalized care tailored to your needs.
