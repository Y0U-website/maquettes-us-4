What is An Orthodontic Emergency?

An Emergency Orthodontist in Everett is any situation where an accident could have damaged your braces or clear aligners, making you require professional care to remedy the situation fully and continue your treatment without delays. Please keep in mind that orthodontic emergencies are not situations that can put your life at risk.

This will depend on the nature of the emergency. If you have an Emergency Orthodontics Everett, then yes. How Does Emergency Dentistry Work?

Everett Emergency Dentistry focuses on delivering any and all required care within the first 24 hours after an accident that could have knocked out a tooth or caused considerable damage to a patient’s orofacial region. These treatments require an emergency setting, so you should go to an ER as soon as possible if you notice swelling constant bleeding, or persistent pain.

How Do I Find An Emergency Dental Treatment Center?

Please get in touch with your general dentist, as they will have contact information for the nearest urgent care center or specialist available to provide you with the required care. Whatever you do, please don’t downplay or underestimate the gravity of your problem and seek out help as soon as possible.
