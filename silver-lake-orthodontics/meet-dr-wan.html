Meet the Doctors

Dr. Sherry Wan grew up in Ann Arbor, MI and lived all over the US before settling down in the beautiful Pacific Northwest with her fiancé, Michael.

Dr. Wan is experienced in early interceptive (children’s), adolescent, and adult orthodontic treatment, as well as complex, surgical, and interdisciplinary orthodontic care. She is also experienced with digital orthodontics and clear aligner treatment with Invisalign. Dr. Wan has conducted research on orthodontic topics such as anterior open bite and also has numerous scientific publications in peer-reviewed journals.

In her free time she enjoys paddle-boarding, trying new recipes, tending to her indoor plant garden, and venturing out on walks with her three dogs and fiancé.

Dr. Wan prides herself on providing personalized, precise, and detail-oriented orthodontic treatment where every patient can feel well taken care of, and she looks forward to creating more smiles in our community.
