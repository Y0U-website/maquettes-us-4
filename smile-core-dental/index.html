Full Mouth Dental Implants At Smile Core Dental in Phoenix, AZ, we offer state-of-the-art dental implants, the gold standard for tooth replacement. Dental implants are a permanent solution that will restore your oral health and your ability to smile with confidence.

If you re experiencing pain due to the condition of your teeth or struggling with basic functions like chewing your food without discomfort, you may qualify for medical necessity coverage for your dental implant treatment. Unlike traditional dental insurance, medical insurance may cover the cost when these health issues are documented.
