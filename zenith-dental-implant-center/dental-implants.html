What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants have three parts: screw-like implant posts fabricated from biocompatible titanium that serve as artificial tooth root replacements. The posts fuse with your underlying bone over a few months, anchoring the dental restoration.

After the fusion process is complete, abutment posts are inserted into the posts to enable permanent attachment of a dental crown, bridge, or full mouth prosthesis. In some cases, abutments are built into the implant posts rather than being a separate part.

Our Dental Implant Options

Single Implants

A single dental implant restores natural-looking function and appearance. In addition, a dental implant replaces a single missing tooth without sacrificing the health of neighboring teeth. The other common treatment for losing a single tooth, a traditional dental bridge, requires modification of adjacent teeth to support the false tooth in the center, with the bridge cemented in place.

Dental Bridges

An implant-supported bridge is a restorative solution to replace multiple adjacent missing teeth without a denture. This restoration typically requires two implants to support the porcelain bridge, which provides a functional and aesthetic replacement for missing teeth. Dental implants help stimulate and keep your underlying bone healthy, unlike conventional bridges.

Snap-On Denture Implants

Conventional removable dentures rest on top of the gums and rely on suction and denture adhesives to stay in place, which can cause many problems. Snap-on dentures provide a middle ground between traditional dentures and full mouth dental implants. They’re a cost-effective option if you prefer removable teeth and aren’t ready to commit to getting full mouth dental implants.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

All-on-X full mouth dental implants are a streamlined implant solution that replaces missing upper, lower, or both arches using a minimum of four dental implants per arch. Full mouth dental implants simulate the look and feel of natural teeth and stay fixed in place because the implants provide a secure and stable foundation.

In contrast to traditional dentures, full mouth dental implants in Davenport, FL, eliminate movement, gum irritation, food limitations, and a sunken-in look caused by ongoing bone resorption. And if our implant dentists determine it is clinically appropriate, you can leave our practice the same day as surgery with a temporary set of new teeth!

Our Personalized Dental Implant Process

During your initial consultation at Zenith Dental and Implant Center, we’ll discuss your tooth replacement needs and goals. In addition to a thorough oral exam, we use our cone-beam computed tomography scanner to assess the condition of your underlying jawbone and the position of the nerves and sinuses to plan precision implant surgery. We’ll provide an accurate timeline and treatment plan for you and your smile and discuss the dental implant cost in Davenport, FL.

Placing dental implants in predetermined areas of your jawbone is done under local anesthesia and sedation dentistry to ensure your comfort and alleviate dental anxiety. During the surgical procedure, Dr. Anna Dao or Dr. Ly Nguyen makes a small incision and inserts the titanium post beneath your soft gum tissue into the bone. While some swelling and postsurgical pain are normal, over-the-counter pain relievers are typically sufficient to reduce discomfort.

During osseointegration, a process that takes 3-6 months, the dental implants fuse with your jawbone. Our team will provide detailed guidelines and tips to ensure the healing and fusion process progresses without complications. Impressions are taken, and a customized crown, bridge, or full mouth prosthesis is fabricated. This final prosthesis is hand-crafted to the last detail to ensure your new smile perfectly matches your gum and lip line, skin tone, and facial symmetry.
