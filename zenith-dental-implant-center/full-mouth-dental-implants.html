Why Choose Dental Implants?

You have choices when it comes to tooth replacement options, and it’s essential to learn the basics of each before making this potentially life-changing decision. Traditional dentures can irritate your gums, slip, fall out, require food limitations, messy denture adhesives, and nightly removal. Snap-on dentures provide excellent stability, although they still require nightly removal and cleaning.

After full mouth dental implants fuse with your underlying jaw bone, they restore your ability to eat the foods you enjoy, preserve your bone and facial structure, and require the same oral hygiene as your natural teeth! Unlike dental bridges or conventional dentures, all-on-X full arch dental implants are a permanent solution for missing or extracted teeth that can last a lifetime.

Am I a Candidate for All-on-X Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Several health conditions and lifestyle factors can negatively influence dental implant outcomes. Research has shown a higher risk of dental implant failure in people with uncontrolled diabetes, autoimmune disorders, radiation therapy to the head/neck, and poor oral hygiene. In addition, nicotine restricts blood flow to your gums, weakening your body’s immune system and impeding fusion and healing. If you smoke, it’s recommended that you quit several months before surgery and not resume smoking until the implants have been integrated.
