Frequently asked questions

These answers are general information only and do not replace an in-office dental evaluation.

Who may be a candidate for dental implants?

Candidates vary. An in-office exam and imaging are usually needed to determine whether implants are a suitable option.

How long does implant treatment take?

Treatment time depends on your oral health, whether additional procedures are needed, and the type of restoration planned.

Can implants replace more than one tooth?

Yes. Depending on your needs, implants may be used for single-tooth replacement or broader restorative plans.
