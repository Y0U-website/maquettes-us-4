Dental Services

  • • It is time for a routine exam, cleaning, or updated treatment plan.

General Dentistry

General dentistry focuses on maintaining oral health, evaluating dental concerns early, and recommending treatment when teeth or gums need attention.

  • • You are due for a routine exam or cleaning.
  • • You have tooth sensitivity, a cavity concern, or discomfort when chewing.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a tooth replacement option for patients missing one or more teeth and looking for a stable, long-term restoration plan.

  • • You have a missing tooth or need to replace a tooth that cannot be saved.
  • • You want to discuss implant-supported restoration options.

Periodontics

Periodontics focuses on the gums and supporting structures around the teeth, especially when inflammation, recession, or bone support concerns are involved.

  • • Your gums bleed when brushing or flossing.
  • • You have swelling, tenderness, or signs of gum recession.

Oral Surgery

Oral surgery includes procedures such as tooth extraction and surgical treatment when a dental issue cannot be managed with more routine care alone.

  • • A tooth is damaged, infected, or difficult to restore.
  • • You need an evaluation for wisdom teeth removal.

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric dentistry helps children build good dental habits early while giving families a clear plan for preventive care and follow-up visits.

  • • Your child is due for a routine dental visit.
  • • You want help creating a positive start with dental care.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your smile while fitting treatment into your broader oral health plan.

  • • You are unhappy with discoloration, shape, spacing, or wear in your smile.
  • • You want to ask about veneers, whitening, or other cosmetic options.

Invisalign / Clear Aligners

Invisalign and other clear aligner treatment options can help patients who want a more discreet approach to straightening teeth.

  • • You want to ask about straightening teeth without traditional braces.
  • • You have spacing or alignment concerns that affect your smile or bite.

Sedation Dentistry

Sedation dentistry may be discussed when a patient is anxious about treatment or when a procedure calls for a more comfort-focused experience.

  • • You feel nervous about dental treatment.
  • • You have avoided dental care because of fear or past experiences.

Emergency Dentistry

Emergency dentistry addresses urgent dental concerns such as pain, broken teeth, swelling, or injuries that should be evaluated as soon as possible by a dental office.

  • • You have sudden tooth pain, swelling, or a dental injury.
  • • A filling, crown, or tooth has broken and you are not sure what to do next.

Veneers

Veneers are a cosmetic option that may be used to change the appearance of teeth affected by chips, discoloration, shape concerns, or spacing issues.

  • • You want to ask about cosmetic changes to visible front teeth.
  • • You are concerned about shape, discoloration, or minor spacing.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic service for patients who want to address staining or discoloration and ask about smile-brightening options.

  • • Your teeth look darker or more stained than you would like.
  • • You are preparing for an event or simply want a brighter smile.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns are used to restore the shape, strength, or function of a tooth when a filling alone may not be enough.

  • • A tooth is cracked, heavily worn, or weakened.
  • • You have had root canal treatment and need to discuss restoration.

Dental Bridges

Dental bridges are a restorative option that can replace one or more missing teeth, depending on your oral health and treatment goals.

  • • You are missing a tooth and want to discuss non-implant restorative options.
  • • You want to understand how a bridge may affect function and appearance.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment may be recommended when the inside of a tooth is inflamed or infected and the goal is to preserve the tooth when possible.

  • • You have lingering tooth pain, pressure, or sensitivity.
  • • A dentist has told you that a tooth may be infected or needs endodontic treatment.

Wisdom Teeth Extraction

Wisdom teeth extraction may be recommended when wisdom teeth are painful, impacted, difficult to clean, or affecting nearby teeth.

  • • You have pain or swelling near the back teeth.
  • • A dentist has told you your wisdom teeth may be impacted or difficult to maintain.
