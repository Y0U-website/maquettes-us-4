About

Dr Randy Stucki Graduated from Long Beach State University in 1986 with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration Marketing. He then went on to the University of The Pacific School Of Dentistry in San Francisco California where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery with Honors in 1991. Dr Stucki ranked among the top in his class. He has been fine tuning his trade ever since and has done over 1,000 hours of continuing education to ensure that he can provide his patients with the best services available.

Dr Stucki is a family man. He and his wife have been married for over 30 years. In his spare time you can find him spending time with his children and grandchildren. The Stucki’s have lived in Alpine since 1993.

One of Dr Stucki’s highest priorities is making sure that he has the most competent and caring staff available. You will notice the difference when you visit our office. Our patients are like family to us. Our caring staff is committed to providing great service and care to all of our patients. At Dr Stucki’s office you will receive the work that you really need. ” We do not create work rather we do what is necessary”. We are always honest and fair with our patients.
