The practice

Welcome to Signature Dental Studio, your trusted dentist in Hillcrest, San Diego, an inclusive and modern dental office that combines advanced technology with a warm, community-focused approach to ensure every patient feels respected and at ease. Led by Dr. Martin, who’s known for his precision, professionalism, and commitment to long-term results, our practice is built on honesty, education, and…

We use cutting-edge tools like CBCT imaging and iTero smile simulations to deliver precise, aesthetic dentistry without pressure. From the moment you walk in, you’ll be greeted by a team that values transparency, listens without judgment, and takes the time to explain your options clearly so you can make confident decisions about your oral health.

Choosing a dentist is personal. You want skill, yes, but you also want to feel comfortable, respected, and confident in the care you’re receiving. Here’s what makes our San Diego dental office different.

We are proud to be a Black-owned, LGBTQ+ affirming dental practice serving the diverse Hillcrest and greater San Diego community. Our team reflects the diversity we celebrate, and we’re honored to care for patients from all backgrounds. Here, you’ll never feel judged or overlooked. You’ll feel welcomed.

We intentionally keep our practice personal. That means more time with Dr. Martin and Dr. Leun, more thoughtful conversations, and treatment plans designed around your goals. Patients often tell us it feels like family. Once you’re here, you’re not just a number. You’re part of our community.

Modern dentistry should feel modern. We use advanced tools like CBCT imaging for precise diagnostics and iTero technology to show you what your smile could look like before treatment even begins. You can sit back, relax, and even watch TV while we take care of the details.

Dr. Martin and Dr. Leun are known for being thorough and transparent. We don’t upsell, and we don’t push unnecessary procedures. You’ll receive clear explanations, honest guidance, and options that make sense for your health and your budget.

We’re making dentistry easier and more accessible. With convenient online appointment requests, texting options, and plenty of free parking (a rare find in Hillcrest), your visit is simple from start to finish.
