Quick Facts about the Cost of Dental Implants

  • Starting Price: The cost of a dental implant at our practice starts at $2,980 for one implant, which typically includes the implant crown, abutment, consultation, advanced diagnostic imaging, and possibly even I-V sedation.
  • Dentist Experience Matters: Dental implant placement requires specialized training and expertise. An experienced implant dentist can help maximize the potential of precise placement and long-term success.
  • Materials Used: High-quality materials such as titanium implant posts and custom-crafted crowns are designed to replicate the strength and appearance of natural teeth.
  • Number of Implants Needed: Replacing one missing tooth usually requires one implant, while patients missing several teeth may benefit from multiple implants or implant-supported restorations.
  • Advanced Technology: Modern implant planning often utilizes advanced tools such as 3D cone beam CT scans to evaluate bone structure and guide precise implant placement.

  • Insurance May Offer Coverage: Some dental insurance policies may offer partial coverage for implants and/or certain parts of treatment, such as crowns or extractions.

Factors That Can Influence the Cost of Dental Implants

Dental implant treatment is tailored to each individual patient, which means the final cost may differ depending on your oral health needs and treatment goals. During your consultation, our dentist will evaluate your smile and recommend the most effective solution for restoring missing teeth.

Several factors may influence the overall cost:

Number of Implants Needed

Patients missing a single tooth may only require one implant and crown, while those missing multiple teeth may need additional dental implants , an implant bridge , or implant-supported dentures .

Preparatory Procedures

Some patients may need preparatory procedures before implant placement. For example, bone grafting may be recommended if the jawbone has lost density after tooth loss. Strengthening the bone structure can help create a stable foundation for long-term implant success.

Restorative Components

A complete dental implant restoration typically includes three key components:

  • The implant post that integrates with the jawbone
  • The abutment that connects the implant to the restoration
  • The custom dental crown that replicates the visible portion of the tooth

Diagnostic Imaging and Planning

Advanced diagnostics, such as 3D cone beam CT imaging, allow our dentist to evaluate bone structure, nerve placement, and sinus position. This technology can help produce accurate treatment planning and precise implant placement.

Comfort and Sedation Options

Some patients choose sedation dentistry to help them feel more comfortable during the procedure. The type of sedation selected may influence the total cost of treatment.

When comparing dental implant prices, it is important to understand exactly what is included in a quote. Some estimates only reflect the implant post, while others include the crown, abutment, imaging, and consultation. A comprehensive treatment plan provides clarity and helps patients avoid unexpected costs later.

Can You Finance Dental Implants?

Financing programs allow qualified applicants to spread payment for the cost of treatment over time rather than paying the entire amount upfront. Third-party healthcare financing providers can offer flexible payment plans that enable individuals to divide the cost of treatment into manageable monthly installments. During your consultation, our dental team can review available payment options, explain financing programs, and help qualified patients select a plan that aligns with their budget. Payment plans as low as $1,000 a month are available at our practice.

Investing in Your Long-Term Oral Health

Although dental implants may require a greater upfront investment compared with some traditional tooth replacement options, many patients find that implants offer exceptional long-term value. Because implants are designed to integrate with the jawbone and support durable restorations, they can function like natural teeth and last for many years with proper care.

By restoring chewing ability, improving smile aesthetics, and helping maintain jawbone health, dental implants can have a significant positive impact on both oral health and overall quality of life.
