What Is a Cosmetic Dentist?

A cosmetic dentist is a dentist that is extensively trained in cosmetic procedures and considers the aesthetic impact of each treatment. Leading cosmetic dentists like Dr. Shen balance the desire for a more beautiful smile with an emphasis on preserving and enhancing the health of the entire mouth. A cosmetic dentist should have the same high-quality credentials as a general dentist, including completing four years of dental school, as well as advanced education in performing the latest cosmetic techniques effectively and safely.

Questions to Ask Your Cosmetic and Implant Dentist

The initial consultation is an excellent opportunity to get to know a potential cosmetic dentist better. We recommend asking some or all of the following questions to help determine if a dentist is right for you:

  • Are you a licensed dentist?
  • What kind of training in cosmetic procedures have you completed?
  • Are you a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry?
  • What types of technology do you use to perform treatments?
  • Do you have before-and-after photos I can look at?
  • How often do you perform the procedure I’m interested in?
  • Do you believe in a conservative approach to treatment, one that preserves existing healthy structure as much as possible?
  • If I’m unhappy with the result, what are my options?
