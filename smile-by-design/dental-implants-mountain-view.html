What Are Dental Implants?

A beautiful, healthy smile can tell others that you’re open and friendly. However, missing teeth can be a challenging obstacle that limits your confidence and inhibits your desire to smile. As a result, others may perceive you differently than you would like. Plus, missing teeth can cause a number of functional problems, ultimately affecting your ability to chew and speak properly. Customized dental implants from Smile By Design and our experienced implant dentist , Dr. Donian Shen can help restore your teeth with natural-looking results.

Dental implants are comprised of two parts: the titanium root fixture, or post, and the dental restoration that is affixed on top. The titanium post will be placed into the bone below the gumline for a sturdy foundation. As a result, the implant can be stronger than the natural tooth. Once the healing process is complete, the restoration ( dental crown , implant-supported dental bridge, or implant denture) will be attached. Dr. Shen endeavors to provide the most natural-looking outcome possible while delivering exceptional beauty and durability.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants are typically individuals with one or more missing or severely damaged teeth who want a long-term, natural-feeling solution. Good overall health is important, and conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes or certain heart concerns may require a different type of missing tooth replacement procedure. Adequate jawbone density is also essential for supporting the implant post, and Dr. Shen will evaluate this using advanced imaging.

If bone loss has occurred, many patients still qualify for implants after bone grafting—a procedure Dr. Shen is highly experienced with performing. Candidates should also be free of active periodontal disease and be committed to maintaining excellent oral hygiene. During your consultation, Dr. Shen will review all factors to determine whether dental implants or an alternative approach is best for your needs and goals.

Dental Implants vs. Dentures

Dental implants and traditional dentures both restore missing teeth, but they differ significantly in stability, comfort, and long-term function. Implants are anchored directly into the jawbone, which can give them the strength and feel of natural teeth. This also helps prevent bone loss and facial changes that often occur with long-term denture use.

Traditional dentures rest on the gums. Although dentures have seen several advancements over the years, they can still move and slip along the gums, produce soreness, and require dietary limitations. While they may be more affordable upfront, dentures do not stimulate the bone and often require periodic adjustments or replacement. With this in mind, dentures may be the more viable option for certain patients who are not ideal candidates for dental implants.

For patients seeking the most secure, lifelike, and durable tooth replacement option, dental implants typically offer the best long-term value. Dr. Shen can also explore the possibility of implant-supported dentures for individuals missing most or all of their teeth who want added stability without replacing every tooth with an implant.

What Are My Dental Implant Options?

We offer a broad range of implant options to best suit your individual cosmetic goals, oral health needs, and lifestyle. You can learn more about our selection during your initial consultation, and here is a quick overview to help you get an idea of what’s available.

Single Tooth Implants

Individuals who are missing one or more teeth can replace each missing tooth with a dental implant, one for one. This can often provide the optimal result; however, you need to have enough bone structure and room in your mouth for this approach. In the event you are lacking sufficient bone structure, Dr. Shen is extensively trained in bone grafting, which may be an avenue for success in your case. We can discuss this option more thoroughly during your first appointment.

Implant Bridges

If you’re missing more than one tooth in a row, an implant-supported bridge may be preferable to a traditional bridge. Designed to be strong, durable, and natural-looking, a customized implant-supported bridge from our state-of-the-art practice offers a number of benefits.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Tired of ill-fitting traditional dentures that slip? Implant-supported dentures from Smile By Design are custom-made to fit comfortably while giving you a strong bite and a beautiful smile. Additionally, implants require no special maintenance and they can help to preserve the health and integrity of your jawbone. Become more confident in your smile with this innovative treatment.

All-on-4 ® and All-on-6 ®

All-on-4 ® implant dentures can help qualified candidates enhance oral function and restore facial structure by replacing all of the teeth in the upper arch, lower arch, or both for a full mouth restoration. The technique is performed by using either four or six dental implant posts in the arch or arches in need of treatment to support the new prosthetic set of artificial teeth.

What Should I Expect from the Dental Implants Procedure?

When you arrive for your first appointment, Dr. Shen will perform a comprehensive evaluation of your teeth, gums, and oral structures. This will also include an oral cancer screening. Ideal candidates will be in good general health and free of periodontal disease. Dr. Shen will then customize a treatment plan based on your anatomical needs, such as existing gum and bone support, as well as your cosmetic goals. The number of dental implants will also be determined by these factors to ensure the best possible outcome.

The dental implant procedure typically consists of five parts:

After the consultation, your surgery will be scheduled. During this procedure, Dr. Shen will implant the titanium post (or posts, depending on your treatment plan), into your jawbone. It will take some time for the implants to fuse to the jawbone after this operation. Once your bone has taken to the implants, Dr. Shen may need to clear away some gum tissue to expose the implants; however, not everyone will need this secondary surgery.

The next step is the impression. She will take models of your mouth to create a customized restoration that best suits your existing bite and your desired aesthetics. Once the restoration is delivered, Dr. Shen will affix it to your implant. The entire process typically takes between four to eight months to complete. Though patience will be necessary, the result should be worth the wait. Your new dental implants are designed to provide the same appearance and function as your natural teeth and to last for many years to come.

Oral Conscious Sedation

Oral conscious sedation can significantly reduce dental anxiety for individuals receiving implants, particularly if they are uneasy about surgery or dental work in general. This method involves taking a mild sedative in pill form before the appointment. While patients remain awake, they should feel deeply relaxed and at ease during the procedure. For patients who prefer to be completely unaware during the implant procedure, or for those undergoing more complex or lengthy treatments, IV sedation provides a deeper level of relaxation. Your vital signs will be carefully monitored by trained professionals to ensure your safety. This option is ideal for individuals with high dental anxiety, sensitive gag reflexes, or those receiving multiple implants in one visit.

Additional Dental Implant FAQs

Dr. Shen has answered some additional frequently asked questions (FAQs) to help you make the best decision about your oral health care. For customized suggestions, please contact our office.

  • How long does it take to get dental implants? The process of placing dental implants, integrating with the bone, and attaching the permanent restorations is a long one that requires some patience. From start to finish, this can take six to nine months. However, you will never be without your smile. You will have temporary restorations in place, allowing you to smile, eat, and live confidently during the treatment.
  • How do I clean dental implants? Other than maintaining excellent oral hygiene, there are no extra maintenance requirements for dental implants; in other words, implants are designed to match your natural teeth and should be treated with the same care. Moreover, brushing at least twice a day, flossing regularly, and sticking to a generally healthy diet can help your implants retain their shine. In addition, you should attend routine dental appointments for professional cleanings and exams.
  • How do dental implants feel? After healing is complete and the dental implants have completely fused with the jawbone, they should feel just like the surrounding natural teeth. The attached restorations are designed to look and function in the same way as natural teeth as well, providing strong and lasting support.
  • Are there certain foods I should avoid with dental implants? When your implants are placed by an experienced cosmetic dentist, they should have no impact on your ability to eat your favorite foods. That said, we still encourage you to be mindful of your diet and make sure you choose snacks that promote good oral health.
