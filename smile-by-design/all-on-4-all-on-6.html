What Are All-on-4 ® & All-on-6 Implant Dentures?

All-on-4 ® and All-on-6 implant denture techniques use four or six dental implant posts, respectively, to anchor a single full-arch denture to the upper or lower jawbone. The dental implants are placed in precisely identified locations in the jawbone to optimize available bone density for stable and secure denture retention. Implant-supported dentures can provide natural-looking, long-lasting tooth replacement, as well as improve speech, chewing, and hygiene challenges associated with removable dental prosthetics and/or missing or badly damaged teeth.

What Are the Benefits of All-on-4 ® & All-on-6 Implant Dentures?

All-on-4 ® and All-on-6 implant dentures can provide a variety of benefits. One big advantage is that patients can expect immediate improvements when eating and speaking. Other major benefits can include:

  • More secure compared to traditional dentures
  • Long-lasting tooth replacement
  • An overall comfortable fit
  • No food restrictions
  • A balanced and natural-looking smile
  • Jawbone growth and preservation of jawbone density

What is Recovery Like After the All-on-4 ® All-on-6 Implant Denture Procedure?

You can expect some temporary swelling and soreness following the surgical placement of your dental implants. Taking over-the-counter pain medications and following post-surgical care instructions can help promote healing and comfort during this period of recovery. Full healing and osseointegration of the implants can take up to six months. Once your implant denture is placed, you can enjoy smiling, chewing, and speaking with confidence!

How Long Do All-on-4 ® & All-on-6 Implant Dentures Last?

All-on-4 ® and All-on-6 implant dentures are designed to produce long-lasting results. For many patients, they can last up to 20 years or more with the right maintenance and care. Just like regular teeth, as long as you brush and floss regularly and have professional dental cleanings at least twice a year, your All-on-4 ® or All-on-6 implant dentures can last for decades.

How Do You Clean All-on-4 ® & All-on-6 Implant Dentures?

In order to maintain your All-on-4 ® and All-on-6 implant dentures, general oral hygiene practices should be followed. This means brushing and flossing daily, as well as following up with your dentist for cleanings to ensure optimal results. Not only are dental checkups important for removing plaque and bacteria from hard-to-reach areas of the mouth but also a good opportunity for your dentist to make sure there are no problems with your All-on-4 ® or All-on-6 implant dentures.
