Anxiety/Sedation

We get it. Having dental work done isn’t fun, and as much as we try to make things as comfortable as possible at Dental 32, we also offer extra consideration for patients who could use it. Our personalized approach to sedation dentistry allows you to experience a relaxing, anxiety-free dental visit.

If our welcoming smiles aren&#x27;t anxiety-relieving enough, we offer services that take it up a notch for those truly dreading the experience! From nitrous oxide to oral sedation to intravenous sedation , we provide a myriad of options to meet your individual needs. You and your teeth deserve a safe, comfortable dental experience, and we’ll do all we can to find a solution that’s right for you.

Keeping Your Teeth for a Lifetime

Fixing teeth is our thing, and we tend to believe that keeping your natural teeth is usually a better option than anything we humans can fabricate. We enjoy designing your dental plan with you, not for you, and our cautious approach allows you the option to keep your teeth for as long as you’d like.

We practice preventative dentistry using the latest technology to help us identify decay and other oral health problems that can’t be found with traditional methods. No matter what condition your teeth are in today, the Dental 32 team is here to optimize what you have, restore any damages that may have occurred, and have you eating and smiling at your best!

Comprehensive Digital Smile Design

Digital Smile Design is a comprehensive analysis of your facial and dental structures using state-of-the-art videography, digital technology and 3D printing that lets you design your perfect smile and take it for a test drive - before we even touch your teeth!

A smile can change the course of your life, and our mission at Dental 32 is to combine proven methods with the latest in modern dental technology in order to provide a fully customized experience that maintains a focus on you.
