Your Dental 32 Experience

We reserve exclusive time at your first visit so that we can fully get to know what goals you have to keep your teeth healthy or design your new smile. We know how valuable your time is, so we’re committed to maintaining your scheduled appointment time, every time.

We offer special amenities including oral sedation and nitrous oxide for extra calming, pillows and blankets for comfort, customized music and personal iPads. Patients enjoy reserved downtown parking next to our office building for appointments.

New Patient Forms

We invite you to complete our new patient forms in advance so we can maximize our time with you. Please either email the form to hello@dental32okc.com or print and bring to your first appointment!

Flexible Financing

We are committed to understanding your financial barriers and offering flexible solutions. We will happily assist in maximizing your insurance benefits. We accept most major dental insurance carriers, and would be more than happy to confirm if we accept yours over a phone call! We also accept cash, personal checks, American Express, Visa and Mastercard. As additional payment options, we use Cherry and CareCredit to provide you the opportunity for a healthy smile.

We understand that insurance coverage can be expensive and is not accessible to everyone. For those without insurance coverage or employers wanting to give dental benefits to their employees, we have created a Private Dental32 Plan to provide access to affordable dental care for you.
