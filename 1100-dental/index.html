Since 1981, Dr. Kotsopey has practiced dentistry in Trenton NJ, establishing deep roots within the community. Generations of patients have trusted their dental health to him. Dr. Kotsopey strives to provide personalized care to every patient.

The future of our practice is looking bright with the addition of Dr. Peter Kotsopey. Dr. Peter grew up just down the road in Yardville, NJ and is a graduate of Notre Dame High School and the University of Delaware. He earned his dental degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in May 2018.

Peter looks forward to working alongside his father, meeting and treating existing and new patients alike.

The merging of Dr. Santucci’s dental practice with the practice of Dr. Kotsopey has created what we now know as 1100 DENTAL. Dr. Santucci continues to treat his family of life-long patients with the utmost attention to detail and care.
