Welcome to Summit Prosthodontics and Maxillofacial Prosthetics

Welcome to Summit Prosthodontics and Maxillofacial Prosthetics, where we believe that restoring a smile is about more than teeth — it’s about restoring comfort, confidence, and trust. Our doctors bring advanced training, decades of combined expertise, and a passion for excellence to every case. With precision techniques, latest technology, and close collaboration with top dental laboratories, we create individualized solutions for even the most complex restorative and maxillofacial needs.

Our doctors routinely work with dental specialists to ensure success and give patients back function, esthetics, and quality of life. They also work in conjunction with oncologists and other physicians to treat those undergoing radiation or chemotherapy and medically compromised patients.

Excellence in Prosthodontics and Innovation

Our doctors bring decades of combined expertise, supported by advanced education and experience in teaching future dental specialists. We are committed to pushing the boundaries of our field, continually refining techniques and integrating the latest research into our treatments.

Since our founding, we have maintained an unwavering dedication to the art and science of dental restoration. With access to state-of-the-art technology and collaborations with leading dental laboratories, we provide highly specialized solutions. From complex implant restorations and custom prosthetics to cosmetic enhancements, our services are designed for patients seeking the highest standards of care.

Personalized, Patient-Focused Care

We provide treatment on a personalized basis, ensuring that each patient receives the attention and time they deserve. When you visit Summit Prosthodontics, you are welcomed into a professional and compassionate environment, where your concerns are heard, your needs are prioritized, and your treatment plan is tailored to your goals.

Our team works seamlessly together, combining technical expertise with a warm, patient-first approach. We value long-term relationships and believe in fostering trust through consistent, high-quality care.

Your Care, Our Commitment

At Summit Prosthodontics, our doctors are active contributors to professional publications, conferences, and continued education, so you can feel confident that your care is backed by the latest science and techniques. For you, that means innovative solutions, precise results, and a team dedicated to making your treatment as comfortable and successful as possible. Contact us today to schedule a consultation and experience the blend of science, skill, and artistry that defines Summit Prosthodontics and Maxillofacial Prosthetics—where your smile and comfort are our greatest priorities.
