Dr. Alexander Drew

A maxillofacial prosthodontist specializing in complex reconstructions, implants, and prosthetic solutions for head and neck rehabilitation.

Dr. Alex Drew is a Maxillofacial Prosthodontist. He completed his undergraduate training at Johns Hopkins University. He is a graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. After completing dental school, he earned a specialty certificate in Prosthodontics and a Master’s of Science from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine. He then completed a subspecialty fellowship in Maxillofacial Prosthetics and awarded a certificate from Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center.

Dr. Drew is a member of the American College of Prosthodontics and the American Dental Association. He is an associate fellow of the American Academy of Maxillofacial Prosthetics and author to research publications in high-impact scientific journals.

Dr. Drew enjoys spending time with his wife Morgan, family, and friends, playing tennis, biking, and waxing his classic Land Rover Defender. He is a New Jersey native.
