Dental bridges are a popular solution for patients who have lost one or more teeth. These bridges are named as such because they consist of one or more replacement teeth that effectively bridge the gap left by the missing teeth. To secure dental bridges, abutment crowns can be used on each end or precision metal mechanisms can be permanently installed.

At Larwin Square Dentistry, we take pride in custom designing every component of a dental bridge to ensure a natural appearance and optimal functionality.
