The practice

We work with most major PPO dental insurance plans previously honored by Dr. Ortega.

Dr. Shyamli Shah is a compassionate general dentist with over 10 years of experience and more than 6 years serving patients across the Bay Area, including Oakland, San Leandro, Hayward, Milpitas, South San Jose, Fremont, Newark, and Union City.

She earned her dental degree with honors in India (2013) and completed advanced training in General Dentistry at UNLV, graduating in 2020. Dr. Shah has extensive experience working with respected practitioners across the Bay Area, gaining broad clinical expertise in comprehensive general dentistry.

Dr. Shah believes that dentistry should be educational, preventive, and patient-focused. She takes time to explain conditions, review options, and tailor care to each patient’s needs, comfort level, and long-term goals.
