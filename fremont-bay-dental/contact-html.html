Frequently Asked Questions

What are Fremont Bay Dental’s office hours?

We’re open Tuesday through Thursday 8 AM–5 PM, Friday 8 AM–3 PM, and alternate Saturdays 8 AM–4 PM. We’re closed Monday and Sunday. Call to confirm Saturday availability.

How do I book an appointment?

Call us at (510) 296-7018, use the contact form on this page, or email office@fremontbaydental.com and our team will get back to you.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call our office immediately at (510) 296-7018. Walk-ins are welcome, and we make every effort to see emergency patients the same day.

Is there parking available at your Fremont office?

Yes, our office at 4071 Bay St in the Irvington district of Fremont offers convenient on-site parking for patients.
