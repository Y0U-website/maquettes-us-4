Honoring Our Practice Legacy

Our practice was built on a strong foundation by Dr. Cecilia Ortega, who faithfully served the Fremont and Irvington community for nearly 25 years. She earned the trust and loyalty of her patients through compassionate care, clinical excellence, and long-standing relationships.

Dr. Ortega’s dedication created a highly regarded, well-loved practice, and many of her patients have been with the office for generations. Dr. Shyamli Shah is honored to continue this legacy, maintaining the same values of honesty, respect, and patient-centered care while incorporating modern techniques and a strong focus on prevention and education.

This continuity ensures that both long-time and new patients receive the same high level of care the community has come to trust.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Dr. Shyamli Shah?

Dr. Shyamli Shah is a general dentist with over 10 years of dentistry experience and more than 6 years serving patients across the Bay Area. She earned her dental degree in 2013 and completed advanced General Dentistry education at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas (UNLV) in 2020.

Is Fremont Bay Dental the same practice Dr. Cecilia Ortega used to run?

Yes. Dr. Shyamli Shah continues the practice built by Dr. Cecilia Ortega, who served the Fremont and Irvington community for nearly 25 years, maintaining the same commitment to honesty, respect, and patient-centered care.

What professional dental associations is Dr. Shah affiliated with?

Dr. Shah is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the California Dental Association (CDA), and the Southern Alameda County Dental Association (SACDA).

What languages does Dr. Shah speak?

Dr. Shah speaks English, Hindi, and Gujarati. Our staff also speaks Spanish and Tagalog.

Where is Fremont Bay Dental located?

We’re located in a historic building in the Irvington district of Fremont, CA, at 4071 Bay St, near the Irvington Farmers Market and just minutes from the Sundale neighborhood.
