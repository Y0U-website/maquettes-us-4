Frequently Asked Questions

What dental services does Fremont Bay Dental offer?

We provide comprehensive preventive care, restorative and cosmetic dentistry (crowns, bridges, implants, veneers, whitening), emergency dental care, and pediatric dentistry for patients of all ages.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes. We accommodate same-day emergency visits for toothaches, lost fillings or crowns, broken teeth, root canal therapy, and extractions.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Our pediatric dentistry covers first dental visits starting at 12 months, preventive care, fillings, and gentle extractions in a comfortable, age-appropriate environment.

Do you offer cosmetic dentistry like veneers or teeth whitening?

Yes. We offer professional in-office teeth whitening and custom porcelain veneers, along with full smile restorations using crowns and bonding.

Do you remove wisdom teeth?

Yes. We perform in-house wisdom tooth extractions in Fremont, including routine, impacted, and surgical cases, so most patients don’t need a separate referral to an oral surgeon.

Do you accommodate nervous or anxious dental patients?

Yes. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for patients who want it, and every visit is paced with a gentle, unhurried approach so nervous patients feel comfortable and informed at each step.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We accept most major PPO dental insurance plans. Please call our office with your insurance information and we’ll verify your coverage.
