0 Frequently asked questions Why is it important to see the dentist? Routine dental visits can prevent problems such as decay and gum disease and keep your smile looking the best.

How often should I visit my dentist?

It is recommended that most adults visit the dentist twice a year. This will vary from patient to patient, but coming in at least twice yearly is the best way to keep a healthy smile.

How can I maintain good oral hygiene between visits?

Patients should brush twice and floss once daily. Other steps include drinking lots of water, limiting sugary foods and drinks as well as beverages such as coffee, tea and red wine, using fluoride toothpaste and/or mouthwash, and avoiding smoking & other tobacco products.
