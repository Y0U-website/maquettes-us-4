Accessibility Statement

  • A two-layer focus indicator (white ring plus dark halo) that stays visible on light, dark, and colored backgrounds alike
  • Screen-reader semantics verified in the accessibility tree: one h1 per page with no skipped heading levels, accordions that announce expanded/collapsed, a mobile menu that announces open/closed, form errors announced as alerts, and decorative artwork hidden from assistive technology while meaningful images carry descriptive alt text
  • Motion respect: all animation — scrolling marquees, reveal effects, and background video — is disabled when your device requests reduced motion, and the homepage background video has a pause button and no audio track
  • Color pairings contrast-checked against WCAG AA; touch targets on small screens meet a 40px minimum; the site works at 200 % zoom and on screens from 320px wide

Known limitations

Online booking, bill payment, and membership signup are provided by third parties (PatientReach, Moolah, and Kleer) whose pages we don’t control; if you have difficulty with them, call us and we’ll schedule you directly. Automated and in-house manual testing cannot fully substitute for testing by daily assistive-technology users — if our testing missed something you ran into, we want to know.
