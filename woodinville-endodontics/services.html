Services

0 Our Services At Woodinville Endodontics, we specialize in saving teeth through advanced endodontic care. Whatever you may need, our expert team is here to provide gentle, precise treatment tailored to your needs. Your comfort and long-term oral health are our top priorities.

Root Canal Treatment

Relieve tooth pain and save your natural smile with expert root canal therapy. At Woodinville Endodontics, we use advanced technology and gentle techniques to treat infection, preserve your tooth, and restore comfort—often in just one visit.

Root Canal Retreatment

Sometimes a previously treated tooth can develop new issues or fail to heal properly. Our specialized retreatment services address these problems by carefully removing the old filling material, cleaning the canals again, and resealing the tooth to promote proper healing and long-term success.

Apicoectomy (Surgical Root Procedure)

When a root canal alone isn’t enough to heal an infection, an apicoectomy may be the solution. This microsurgical procedure removes the tip of the tooth root and any surrounding infected tissue to preserve your natural tooth and relieve discomfort—offering a precise and effective path to healing.
