Orthodontics for Teens in Prairieville, Denham Springs, & Central

Teens and orthodontic care just go together! That’s because they’ve already gotten their permanent teeth, and while their jaw is still growing and will respond well to treatment, it’s developed enough for Dr. Misuraca to know how it will shape up as an adult. The confidence that comes from a beautiful, healthy smile is something that every teen deserves.

Teen Treatment Options

Your teen has several treatment options available, each tailored to their specific needs, lifestyle, and activities.

From modern metal braces to stylish clear braces to subtle clear aligners, Dr. Misuraca will work closely with both you and your teen to find the treatment plan that’s perfect for their smile.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a great choice for teens who want a treatment that won’t stand out.

At Misuraca Orthodontics, you’ll find that clear aligners for teens often have features particular to their needs, like special tabs at the ends to make space for their growing molars. They could also include wear indicators to help your teen stay on track with their treatment.

If your teen loses or damages their aligners, we can always provide a replacement.

Modern Metal Braces

Modern metal braces are also a great choice and remain our most popular option for teens in and around Central, Denham Springs, and Prairieville. There’s no reason to worry about keeping track of them since they’re fixed in place, and they’re more comfortable and less noticeable than ever before.

Clear Braces

Some teens want the low profile of a more discreet option, but still prefer not to have to keep track of aligners. If that’s your teen, then clear braces are often a great choice. Since they’re made of clear ceramic, they’ll blend right in with your teen s smile, but there’s no need to remember to put them in or take them out for meals. That’s the kind of convenience teens love.

Orthodontics for Adults in Prairieville, Denham Springs, & Central

Love Your Smile at Any Age

At Misuraca Orthodontics, Dr. Misuraca and the team know that there’s no age limit on loving your smile. With modern advancements like clear aligners and clear braces, it’s never been easier to get a smile you’ll love.

Why Adults Choose Orthodontic Treatment

Adult orthodontic treatment can improve more than just the look of your smile. It can straighten teeth that have shifted over time, correct spacing or crowding that has always bothered you, and improve your bite.

Your treatment might be about feeling more confident in photos, conversations, and professional settings. Or maybe you’re more interested in making your teeth easier to clean, reducing uneven wear, or handling bite issues that affect comfort.

Or maybe you want the best of both worlds. Either way, adult orthodontic treatment is your answer.

Treatment That Fits Your Life

Today’s orthodontic options make adult treatment easier to fit into your routine. Choices like clear aligners and clear braces can help you improve your smile with solutions that feel more subtle and more convenient than many people expect.

Misuraca Orthodontics builds your treatment around your goals, your lifestyle, and your smile. Whether you’re revisiting treatment after past orthodontic work or starting for the first time, Dr. Misuraca can help you find an approach that feels right for you.
