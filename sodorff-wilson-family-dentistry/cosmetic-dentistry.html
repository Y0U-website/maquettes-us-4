Why Choose Cosmetic Dentistry?

Everyone deserves to feel their best, and cosmetic dentistry improves facial aesthetics.

A straighter smile without cracks or chips is easier to maintain and improves dental health.

Cosmetic dentistry procedures are safe with little discomfort, and many treatments last for years.

How Cosmetic Dentistry Works

Sodorff & Wilson Family Dentistry will work with you to help create your perfect smile.

After an initial visit and consultation at Sodorff & Wilson Family Dentistry in Spokane Valley, we review your medical history and discuss your treatment options, we’ll then create a custom cosmetic dentistry plan.

Cosmetic dentistry includes many ways to improve the look of teeth and may be needed after an accident or due to natural dental imperfections. The result is a beautiful and confident smile you’ll love showing off.

You deserve to be the best version of yourself, and our cosmetic dentists in Spokane Valley have the dental solutions you’ve been looking for your whole life..

  • Step 1: We’ll meet to discuss your goals, time, budget, and perform an exam of the mouth (including oral health and facial structure). Based on the procedure, we may be able to show what the end result will look like using dental imaging or models.
  • Step 2: A custom plan is made just for you and your needs. We’ll answer any questions you have so that you know exactly what to expect.
  • Step 3: The cosmetic treatment begins, and we’ll help transform your appearance.
