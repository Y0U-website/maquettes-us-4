  • Is Invisalign® cheaper than braces? Invisalign® is similar in cost to braces. Despite the similar cost, clear aligners provide unbeatable benefits like the freedom to live your life without worrying about metal brackets and wires that get in the way of special events or foods you love.
  • How much does it cost to get Invisalign®? The cost of Invisalign® varies based on individual treatment, but some dental insurance providers may cover costs. Please contact Sodorff & Wilson Family Dentistry directly to discuss your smile goals and treatment plan, which will determine the total cost of treatment.
  • Do you wear Invisalign® all day? Invisalign® aligners should be worn each day for 20-22 hours. Wear your trays day and night, and only remove them to eat, drink, brush, or floss. This will ensure you stay on schedule and transform your smile according to the treatment plan. Not wearing your trays as recommended may extend the length of treatment time. Our expertly crafted trays look great and feel better than metal braces. Invisalign® trays are custom-made for each patient using flexible plastic. Additionally, each aligner is individually trimmed for a comfortable fit over your gum line.
  • Does Invisalign® make teeth yellow? No. To avoid staining or a yellow appearance of your Invisalign® trays, it’s important to remove your aligners while eating or drinking and brush immediately after consuming food and drinks. By following proper hygiene, you’ll avoid a yellow appearance of teeth.
