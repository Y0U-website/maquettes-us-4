Implant retained dentures If you&#x27;re looking for a more stable and comfortable alternative to traditional dentures, implant-retained dentures may be just what you need. Unlike traditional dentures that rely on suction or adhesives to stay in place, implant-retained dentures are anchored securely to dental implants that are surgically placed into the jawbone. This means that they won&#x27;t slip or shift, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Implant-retained dentures also offer several other benefits. They can help preserve the bone in your jaw, which can deteriorate over time when teeth are missing. They also provide a more natural-looking and feeling solution than traditional dentures, giving you a more youthful and attractive appearance.
