Periodontal Therapy Periodontal therapy refers to a spectrum of treatments aimed at combating periodontal disease, which affects the structures around the teeth, including the gums, periodontal ligament, and alveolar bone. The primary goal of periodontal therapy is to restore and maintain the health of these structures, which are essential for overall oral health.

Periodontal therapy is tailored to the individual’s specific needs, and the type of treatment is selected based on the severity of the periodontal disease. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are key to the success of periodontal therapy.
