Committed to Exceptional Dental Care for All Ages

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to provide patients of all ages with the highest quality dental care in a clean, comfortable, and welcoming environment. By combining advanced technology with compassionate, patient-centered care, we strive to build lasting relationships through exceptional care that supports lifelong oral health.

Our Core Values (GRACE)

We value our team members, patients, colleagues, and community, and appreciate the opportunity to make a positive difference every day.

We treat every team member, patient, and person who walks through our doors with dignity, courtesy, and appreciation.

We foster a culture of honesty, accountability, and professionalism among our team, ensuring we always uphold the highest moral and ethical standards for our patients and practice.

We are dedicated to supporting one another, continuously growing as individuals, and pursuing clinical excellence so we can confidently and reliably serve our patients and community.

We seek to understand, support, and care for our team members and patients with compassion.

Dear Friends,

All of us here at City Smiles would like to thank you for your continued trust and confidence in our practice; and for allowing us to provide you with your professional dental health care.

We would also like to take this opportunity to highlight a few of the additions that we have made in order to enhance our quality of care, affordability of treatment, and convenience of scheduling.

These state-of-the-art additions include:

Digital Patient Communication software that gives our patients a more convenient and efficient way to contact our office, submit forms, schedule appointments, receive appointment reminders and make payments.

Digital X-ray System , which allows us to improve our diagnosis while decreasing x-ray exposure by 75 %. Since digital X-rays can be electronically transmitted, this allows us more efficiency in our ability to refer patients to specialty care.

Digital Scanners that allow us to provide an elevated level of care increasing, accuracy, turn around times and comfort by doing away with traditional dental impressions

Soft tissue Laser that allows us to treat certain dental conditions more effectively, comfortably and with less recovery time.

AI Software to enhance our ability to interpret x-rays for best treatment diagnosis and treatment outcomes. In addition, we have increased our commitment to continuing education and advancement and will continue to stay committed to that goal of maximized continuing education.

To improve the convenience of care, we expanded our office hours to accommodate patients interested in earlier morning appointments and evening appointments after work.

We also want to make you aware of the parking availability in the lot behind our facility. We have 10 spots designated for our practice. So, if you find parking difficult, you may pull around back via Grace Avenue and park in the lot.

Please let us know if there is anything we can do to service you better. As always, thank you for your confidence in entrusting City Smiles with your dental care. We value the opportunity to service your dental care needs.
