Why Dental Implants Are an Essential Treatment Option

The Dental Implant Process at Dovy Dental

Our dental implant treatment process is carefully planned to ensure safety, comfort, and lasting results.

  • Comprehensive Consultation and Evaluation: Our team conducts a thorough exam, including imaging, to assess bone health and implant suitability.
  • Personalized Treatment Planning: Each implant plan is tailored to the number of missing teeth, bone condition, and patient goals.
  • Implant Placement Procedure: The titanium implant post is precisely placed into the jawbone to serve as a stable foundation.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a strong and secure base.
  • Final Restoration Placement: A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to restore function and appearance.

Preventing Further Tooth Loss with Dental Implants

Dental implants help prevent many complications associated with missing teeth. By stimulating the jawbone, implants reduce the risk of bone loss and facial collapse. They support proper bite alignment and prevent surrounding teeth from shifting. Improved chewing efficiency helps maintain a balanced diet and digestive health. Implants also eliminate the need for altering healthy adjacent teeth, unlike traditional bridges. Maintaining implants with good oral hygiene promotes long-term success. At Dovy Dental, we emphasize implants as both a restorative and preventative solution.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Are dental implants painful?

The procedure is performed with local anesthesia to ensure comfort. Mild soreness during recovery is normal and temporary.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime.

Am I too old for dental implants?

Age alone is not a limiting factor as long as overall health and bone condition are suitable.

How long does the implant process take?

The complete process can take several months due to healing and integration time.

Do you offer dental implants at both locations?

Yes, Dovy Dental provides dental implant services in Houston and Missouri City, TX.
