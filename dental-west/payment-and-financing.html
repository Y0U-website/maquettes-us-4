Financing/Payment Options

Dental West is sensitive to balancing the needs of patients and financing their dental treatment. We feel that there are 2 components to this:

  • Establish treatment plan options for treatment that afford a patient a less expensive option with an acceptable result.
  • Finding a payment option which suits a the patient’s financial objectives

Payment Options
