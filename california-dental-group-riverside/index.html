Dental implants stand apart from other tooth replacement options because they replace the entire tooth structure, including the root. The titanium post fuses with your jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration. This creates a permanent base that won’t shift or move over time.

Getting dental implants involves several steps spread over a few months, but each phase is straightforward. We start with a thorough exam and use digital X-rays to plan your treatment. During surgery, we place the titanium post into your jawbone. After the healing time allows the implant to fuse with the bone, we attach a custom crown that matches your natural teeth perfectly.
