Services

  • 0 Our Services Dental Exams & X-Rays Routine checkups that help detect cavities, gum disease, and other issues early.
  • Teeth Cleanings Removes plaque and tartar buildup to keep your teeth and gums healthy.
  • Fluoride Treatments Strengthens tooth enamel and helps prevent cavities.
  • Dental Sealants A protective coating applied to teeth to reduce the risk of decay.
  • Night Guards & Sports Mouthguards Custom devices that protect your teeth from grinding or sports injuries.

Preventive Dentistry

  • Fillings Repairs cavities using natural-looking materials that blend with your teeth.
  • Crowns Covers and protects a damaged tooth while restoring its strength and shape.
  • Bridges Replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to neighboring teeth.
  • Root Canal Treats infection inside a tooth to relieve pain and save the tooth.
  • Dentures Removable options that replace missing teeth and restore your smile.
  • Extractions Safely removes damaged or problematic teeth when they cannot be saved.

Restorative Dentistry

  • Professional Whitening Brightens stained or discolored teeth with safe, in-office whitening treatment.
  • Custom take-home trays that allow you to whiten your teeth gradually and comfortably at home.
  • Veneers Thin custom shells that improve the shape, color, and appearance of teeth.
  • Cosmetic Bonding Repairs chips, gaps, or uneven teeth using tooth-colored resin.
  • Smile Makeovers A personalized treatment plan to enhance your overall smile appearance.
