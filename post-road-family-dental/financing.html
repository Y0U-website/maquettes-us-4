CareCredit Financing

We proudly accept the CareCredit® healthcare credit card to help make dental care more affordable.

CareCredit allows you to finance recommended dental procedures—including crowns, oral surgery, and routine visits—with convenient monthly payment options. It can also be used for other healthcare services at thousands of providers nationwide.

Applying is fast, free, and easy, with an instant decision. If approved, you can schedule treatment right away—even before your card arrives.

Learn more at www.carecredit.com or contact our office.
