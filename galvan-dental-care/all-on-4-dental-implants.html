All-on

If you are struggling with missing teeth, failing dental work, or loose, uncomfortable dentures, you deserve a solution that restores both your confidence and your quality of life. At Galvan Dental Care, Dr. Felipe Galvan, DDS and our experienced team provide All-on-4® dental implants—a permanent, life-changing alternative to traditional dentures.

This advanced procedure allows you to replace a full arch of missing teeth using just four strategically placed titanium implants, giving you a beautiful, secure smile that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

Why Choose All-on-4® Over Traditional Dentures?

No Slipping or Glues: Implants are anchored permanently into the jawbone, eliminating embarrassing slips, clicking, or messy adhesives.

Eat Your Favorite Foods: Restore your full chewing power so you can confidently enjoy steak, apples, and crunchy foods again.

Protect Your Facial Structure: Unlike dentures which can accelerate bone loss and cause a sunken facial appearance, implants stimulate the bone to preserve your natural, youthful face shape.

Maximum Comfort: The sleek design leaves the roof of your mouth completely open, allowing you to taste your food better and speak clearly.Teeth in a Day: In many cases, Dr. Galvan can place the implants and attach a functional, temporary set of teeth in a single visit.

You do not have to live with the discomfort or self-consciousness of missing teeth. Let our caring team help you smile with absolute confidence again.
