About Our Practice

Galvan Dental Care has been providing quality general dentistry care and cosmetic dentistry to the Danville and Vallejo, California areas since 2006.

Galvan Dental Care specializes in Invisalign, root canals, crowns, dentures, extractions, fillings, cleanings, and bridges. Besides their dental services, however, the Galvan team believes that being a part of a community means being of service to its people.

Galvan Dental Care strives to provide the best service to patients by providing excellent dental care, imparting relevant patient education, and employing an able team of caring staff that works with patients to give them their best possible natural smiles.

Patients can expect to receive advice that helps them make an informed decision about their dental treatment options. From the moment you book an appointment, the Galvan Dental Care team wants to understand your needs and works to meet them.

The office values your time and will work to accommodate busy schedules for working professionals, students, and parents.

Dr. Sohail Rana

Dr. Sohail Rana is a dentist who brings together advanced clinical expertise and heartfelt compassion. Known for his gentle approach, clear communication, and dedication to patient comfort, Dr. Rana believes in treating people—not just teeth. He takes the time to understand each patient’s goals, values, and concerns, crafting personalized treatment plans that reflect both science and empathy. His patients often say they feel truly heard—and genuinely cared for.

Dr. Rana earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pittsburgh, where he trained in implants, digital dentistry, CAD/CAM same-day crowns, aesthetic restorations, and comprehensive care. His clinical journey has taken him across several dynamic practices in the Bay Area—from leading a thriving startup in San Francisco to performing robotic-guided surgeries using Yomi technology.

Before entering dental school, Dr. Rana studied Biochemistry at Eastern Michigan University, where he conducted research aimed at improving chemotherapy effectiveness. This early work reflects his lifelong passion for solving complex problems and making a meaningful impact on people’s lives—a passion that continues to drive his pursuit of clinical excellence in dentistry today.

In addition to his technical skills, Dr. Rana is known for making patients feel at ease. Whether he’s planning a full-arch restoration or helping someone conquer their dental anxiety, his calm demeanor and precision help turn fear into confidence. He continues to grow his expertise in areas like All-on-X implant solutions, complex treatment planning, and advanced digital smile design—all while staying committed to long-term patient relationships built on trust.

Dr. Rana also connects with patients from a variety of backgrounds through his fluency in English, Urdu, and Hindi. He is currently learning Arabic as part of his dedication to lifelong learning and cultural understanding—making dental visits more accessible and comforting for many of his patients.

Outside the operatory, Dr. Rana is an explorer at heart. He enjoys photographing landscapes, hiking scenic trails, diving into books on strategy and innovation, and detailing cars in his garage. A self-proclaimed tech enthusiast, he’s just as comfortable exploring the latest dental technology as he is shifting gears through winding California mountain roads in his convertible.

Whether you’re coming in for a check-up or a life-changing smile transformation, Dr. Rana is here to guide you with expertise, empathy, and a commitment to your overall well-being.
