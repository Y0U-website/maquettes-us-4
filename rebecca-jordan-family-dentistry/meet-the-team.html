Meet The Team

Our Talented and Dedicated Staff

Meet the team at Rebecca Jordan Family Dentistry. Your team, both clinical and administrative, are a well-educated and extremely talented group of dental professionals committed to working with you and your family to create a comfortable, stress-free, and rewarding experience every time you visit our practice.

Reagan joined our practice in mid 2024. With her previous experience as a dental assistant, she enjoys being in the dental field and decided she would like to be working at the front desk now. Reagan loves dancing and being a dog momma!

Mandi joined us in early 2020, eventually becoming our lead and full-time dental hygienist. She brings over 20 years of experience in the dental field. Mandi and her family live nearby in Powell. She is a yoga enthusiast and can often be found lost in a good book.

We are very excited to welcome Kaitlin to our dental team in late 2022 as we continue to grow.

Vesta became a member of Dr. Rebecca Jordan’s team in January of 2015 when her long-time dental employer reached retirement. She brought her expertise in making people feel welcome and right at home when they are here with us. Vesta is always happy to lend a listening ear or share one of the many adventures of her grand-babies or recent sewing projects.

A loving mother of three, Mandi joined us with her positive attitude and big heart in 2018. Always ready to lend a hand or ear, Mandi is most often found multi-tasking around the office. She currently lives in Sunbury with her family and loves the fact that she can be so close to home and family while doing what she loves here in our office.
