Payments & INSURANCE

We offer various payment methods, including Cash, Check, and all major credit cards, including those affiliated with your Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flex Spending Accounts (FSA). We provide financing options through Care Credit.

We will work with most dental insurance plans that will contribute towards your treatment. We gladly file claims on your behalf and only collect estimated copays at the time of service. Our talented team will assist you with all necessary paperwork and efficient claim filings to help you maximize your benefits. Don’t forget to bring your dental insurance information with you when you visit.
