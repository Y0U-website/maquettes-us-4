Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry improves the appearance of a person’s teeth, gums and/or bite. This primarily focuses on improving dental aesthetics in color, position, shape, size, alignment, and overall smile appearance. In our office, services provided are composite (white) restoration, ceramic crowns and veneers, laser surgery for the gums, and various tooth whitening procedures.

Implants

Dental Implants are fixtures that interface with the bone or jaw to support a dental prosthesis such as a crown, bridge, or a denture. Multiple implants can be used to make a denture more solid and can snap into the prosthesis for added stability.

Oral Surgery

Our office offers oral surgery of wisdom teeth, broken teeth, referral extractions, and various problems or pathological conditions of the mouth.

A root canal is a treatment to repair and save a badly-damaged tooth. This procedure involves removing the damaged area of the tooth, cleaning and disinfecting, then filling and sealing it. A treated and restored tooth can last a lifetime with proper care.
