MEET OUR TEAM

A 2016 honors graduate of the Marquette University Dental School, Dr. Park is dedicated to providing the highest quality comprehensive care to ensure that his patients have optimum oral health. To achieve this goal, he is committed to continuing education and lifelong learning.

Dr. Park, who moved to Wisconsin from South Korea in 2004 as an exchange student, is fluent in both English and Korean.

Dr. Martin lives in Franklin with his wife of 36 years, Dr. Jane. They have three children, two sons and a daughter. With more free time now, Dr. Martin and Dr. Jane love to travel and have recently picked up the game of pickleball. In the summer, Dr. Martin can be found waterskiing almost daily when not in the office.

Dr. Kaitlin Jasper is a Wisconsin native, originally growing up in the suburbs of Milwaukee. She then received her Bachelor’s degree in Biology at the University of Wisconsin- Madison with additional studies in Spanish and leadership training. After her undergraduate education, she continued on to pursue her passion for dentistry at Marquette University and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with honors.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Jasper enjoys spending time with family, friends, and her dog Olive, playing tennis, cooking, and watching all forms of Wisconsin sports with her husband, Drew.

Dr. Nataliia Stalynska is a general dentist dedicated to providing comprehensive, patient-centered dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry and enjoys helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized treatment and thoughtful attention to detail.

Dr. Stalynska provides a wide range of dental services, including restorative and cosmetic dentistry, crowns and bridges, root canal therapy, oral surgery, implant dentistry, clear aligner therapy, and comprehensive treatment planning. She has a particular interest in modern digital dentistry and incorporates advanced technology to make treatment more precise, efficient, and comfortable for her patients.

Known for her warm and approachable manner, Dr. Stalynska believes that excellent dentistry begins with listening. She takes the time to understand each patient’s concerns, explain treatment options clearly, and develop an individualized plan that supports both oral health and long-term confidence.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Stalynska enjoys traveling, staying active, spending time with her family, and exploring new places with her husband and their dog Coconut.
