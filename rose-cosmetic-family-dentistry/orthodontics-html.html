Invisalign Clear Braces

If you have misaligned teeth, and you’re not sure you want to commit to traditional braces, then we invite you to consider the Invisalign system. Invisalign clear braces are made from comfortable, virtually invisible plastic. Utilizing a series of aligners that are switched out every two weeks, your teeth are gently and gradually shifted into place. Invisalign clear braces usually take 18-24 months to straighten your smile, and because they don’t require adjusting, you’ll spend less time in the dentist’s office.

Indications for Invisalign

Although Invisalign isn’t designed to treat more extensive orthodontic issues, like rotated teeth, it can still address the following conditions:

Crowded Teeth

Having teeth too close together or overlapping can negatively affect the look and feel of your smile. With Invisalign, the clear aligners will apply focused pressure to different parts so that they can move your teeth into proper alignment while remaining discreet.

Gaps Between Teeth

Spaces in your smile can often feel embarrassing or unattractive. On top of that, they can increase your risk of cavities and gum disease by allowing food particles to become trapped, which can eventually cause decay. Invisalign trays can bridge the gaps between your teeth, guiding them into a safer and more aesthetically pleasing position.

Bite Alignment

Bite misalignment can place excessive pressure on your temporomandibular joint, causing issues like TMJ disorder and teeth grinding/clenching. Though you may need buttons and other additions, Invisalign clear aligners can correct alignment problems like an overbite, underbite, or crossbite.

Invisalign FAQs

Wish you could have a straighter smile? If you want to achieve your dream look without metal wires and brackets, you might be considering Invisalign. The clear aligners move your teeth into proper alignment discreetly and comfortably! Of course, because it’s such a unique process, we understand you may have some questions in mind before committing to treatment. Our team at Rose Cosmetic & Family Dentistry wants you to be as well-informed as possible, which is why we’ve decided to answer some frequently asked questions below.

How Long Does Invisalign Take?

It depends! Since every case is unique, the amount of time it’ll take to complete Invisalign treatment will vary from patient to patient. Many factors must be taken into account, such as the severity of your misalignment and commitment to the treatment plan. The average person can expect to finish Invisalign somewhere between 12 to 18 months. If you have mild dental misalignment, you can finish in as little as six months!

Although 18 months seems like a long time, it’s still about four months shorter than the average treatment length with traditional braces. You can speed up the process by visiting your dentist regularly and committing to treatment instructions.

Does Invisalign Hurt?

Not at all! The smooth plastic design of the clear aligners makes Invisalign one of the most comfortable orthodontic treatment options to date. At first, you may experience mild soreness and discomfort while you get used to the plastic trays. Some patients may characterize this sensation as pain. However, it should go away as you get used to the treatment.

Keep in mind that Invisalign works by shifting your teeth into proper alignment using a small amount of pressure. Each time you switch to the next aligner in the series, you may feel this sensation but that’s how you know it’s doing its job! If needed, you can manage your discomfort with over-the-counter pain medication and cold compresses.

What Happens After Invisalign?

After you finish Invisalign treatment, you will visit your dentist to verify your results. They’ll make sure your teeth look exactly how they should! Once they confirm your smile is in the desired straight position, they’ll brief you on how to maintain your newly aligned grin.

You’ll start your post-treatment journey by wearing a retainer, which will either be a wire one or a clear plastic one that looks similar to Invisalign trays. It’s crucial that you wear your retainer as instructed because it will help preserve the results you’ve spent significant time and money on. Depending on your specific situation, your dentist will recommend how often you need to wear your retainer. Most people start by wearing it full time before gradually decreasing to only wearing it at night.

How Do I Clean My Invisalign?

There are several different ways you can keep your Invisalign aligners clean. You can rinse them throughout the day to prevent debris from sticking to the smooth plastic and use a soft-bristled toothbrush to gently clean them. Don’t use hot water as it can permanently warp the plastic!

Invisalign also sells its own cleaning system that’s designed specifically for maintaining your aligners. It uses cleaning crystals that shake loose food debris and plaque. Another great product to consider is Steraligner, which is a cleaning solution for plastic trays. It’s easy to use and incredibly reliable!
