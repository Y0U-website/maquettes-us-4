What are Dental Implants?

The dental implants that are most commonly used today are called endosteal implants. They’re made to be put in the jawbone so that they can act as roots for replacement teeth. Implants are intended to join with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. As such, they need to be made from a biocompatible material. Titanium is the most common, but zirconia is also a viable option; our dental office offers both kinds of implants to replace any number of teeth.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Step 1: Initial Consultation: We will discuss your needs and oral health to decide whether you’re a good implant candidate. Bone grafts, periodontal therapy, and other procedures may be deemed necessary to prepare the jaw for implant surgery.
  • Step 2: Dental Implant Surgery: On the day of the surgery, your mouth is numbed before the implant posts are inserted into carefully chosen spots in the jawbone.
  • Step 3: Osseointegration/Abutment: After dental implants have fused with the jawbone (which usually takes a few months), an abutment can be attached to them.
  • Step 4: Delivery of the Final Restoration: The final restoration will be fully personalized based on impressions of your mouth. We will attach it to the abutment(s) during the last step of the implant process.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Unlike more traditional tooth-replacement options like dentures and bridges, dental implants are the only solution that can rebuild pearly whites from the roots up. Instead of relying on the natural suction with the gums or support from other nearby healthy teeth, your restoration will be directly anchored to your jawbone. This will provide your new smile with incredible stability along with various advantages you simply won’t find with other restorative dental treatments. Keep reading to learn more about the numerous benefits of dental implants in Las Vegas!

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • Straightforward maintenance : Since dental implants will be embedded in your jawbone and feel like natural teeth, you won’t have to practice any special techniques to clean them. Patients will simply need to brush, floss, rinse with mouthwash frequently, and visit their dentist for checkups and cleanings every six months to make the most of them.
  • Improved confidence : With a firm foundation for your new pearly whites, you won’t have to worry about them shifting or popping out of place. This means you can feel free to show off your smile confidently and enjoy meals comfortably, improving your overall quality of life.

Health Benefits

  • Jawbone preservation : Whenever you lose a tooth or teeth, your jawbone will lack the stimulation necessary to maintain its structure. Without blood circulating to the bone tissue, it’ll begin to deteriorate, altering your facial shape. Since dental implants serve as your new permanent roots, they’ll provide the stimulation needed to promote stronger bone tissue. This means you can preserve your jawbone and keep a more youthful appearance in the long run.
  • Maintain better oral health : Partial or full dentures will mostly rely on natural suction to the gums for support. Without proper care, you may have a higher chance of gum irritation, sores, or infection. Dental bridges require removing some of the enamel from healthy adjacent teeth to make room for the crowns. Over time, these pearly whites can end up at risk of decay, damage, or even extraction. Dental implants are supported by your jawbone, providing a more stable and healthier solution for both your remaining teeth and gums.

Long-Term Benefits

  • An incredible success rate : Dental implants typically have a high success rate of 95 %, even 10 years after being placed.
  • Long-lasting results : Unlike dentures and bridges which usually last around 10 years with proper care, dental implants can go as far as several decades to a lifetime!
  • A money-saving solution : On top of having increased longevity, you also won’t have to replace your restorations as frequently as other tooth-replacement options like dentures and bridges. This can essentially save you much more money down the road.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

As long as your mouth and jaw are healthy and fully capable of supporting implant posts, we can generally replace any number of teeth with dental implants. We’ll perform a full check of your oral health during your initial consultation to determine whether additional treatments need to be performed to make you eligible. Then we can start planning the dental implant process, which will look different depending on how many teeth you’ve lost.

Missing Single Tooth

When just one tooth is missing, a dental implant will be placed in the empty space. After the initial healing phase, a crown will be placed on top of the abutment. Just like the restorations used to repair your teeth, implant crowns can be designed to match your natural smile so seamlessly that most people won’t be able to tell which tooth was replaced.

Missing Multiple Teeth

It typically only takes two implant posts to support a bridge that replaces three or more teeth in a row. This approach minimizes the need to modify existing enamel, as the bridge is being supported by implants instead of your teeth.

Missing All of Your Teeth

Even in the most extensive cases of tooth loss, dental implants remain a viable option. Implant dentures are designed to be supported by a small number of implants usually about 4 to 6. They can be made removable or fixed in place depending on your preferences and personal dental needs.

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

The different parts of the dental implant (i.e., the post, the restoration, and the abutment) all come with their own costs. There are also variables such as the number of implants needed and the necessity of preliminary procedures that will ultimately determine how much you pay for your dental implants. We will give you an estimate during your initial consultation and discuss why implants may be a worthwhile investment in your case.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

After your surgery, it’s important to preserve the blood clot that will form. This will help your gums heal around the implant posts, so it’s crucial you don’t disturb the surgical sites and disrupt this process. Here are a few things to avoid after your procedure:

  • Drinking from a straw
  • Brushing directly on or around your implants
  • Smoking or chewing tobacco
  • Placing your tongue or finger on or near the implant site
  • Eating hard or sticky foods

Common Side-Effects as You Heal from Dental Implant Placement

It’s important to know what symptoms to expect as you’re healing from your surgery so you don’t feel panicked or anxious when they occur. If you notice that your pain, discomfort, or bleeding doesn’t subside within the first few days or gets worse with time, please call us. Here are some common side-effects you can expect following your surgery:

  • Minor discomfort, which can typically be relieved with prescribed or recommended over-the-counter medications.
  • Intermittent bleeding, which can be managed by gently pressing a sterile piece of gauze on the surgical site.
  • Swelling of the gums that can last for a week or more. Typically, this can be helped by placing a cold compress on the outside of your mouth.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

Just like with a tooth extraction, it’ll be important to stick to soft foods for the first few days to preserve your blood clot and allow your gums to heal around the implants. Some foods we recommend purchasing before your surgery include:

  • Protein shakes
  • Smoothies
  • Ice cream
  • Yogurt
  • Cold soup
  • Mashed potatoes
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Pasta
  • Pudding

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

It’s crucial to maintain good oral hygiene after your dental implant placement to keep an infection from developing and prevent the accumulation of bacteria within the mouth. Make sure to:

  • Brush your teeth like normal, paying extra careful attention when cleaning around the implant placement sites.
  • Rinse your mouth with salt water two to three times a day, especially following meals, to clean around your implants. Instead of spitting the water out, let it pour out of your mouth into the sink to keep the blood clot intact.
  • Don’t use mouthwash containing alcohol.

What to Do After Your New Teeth are Attached

Over the next three to six months following your surgery, your dental implants in Las Vegas will fuse with the jawbone. This will allow our team to secure your final restorations to them. You’ll likely feel some sensitivity, swelling, or bleeding in your gums, but this should subside within days following the attachment of your prosthetic. After that, you’ll be able to fully enjoy your newly restored smile!

Dental Implant Failure & Salvage

Even though dental implants are a highly reliable solution for tooth replacement, they can occasionally fail due to infection or trauma. We encourage you to call our office immediately if you notice any problems with your implant posts; our team will identify the root of the issue and take whatever steps are necessary to help you protect your oral health. If your implants need to be removed for your treatment, we can often place new ones once your mouth has sufficiently healed.

Dental Implant FAQs

Dental implants in Las Vegas are the most successful method of replacing lost teeth; however, they are a big investment. Don’t worry if you’re a little apprehensive. We will take the time to explain everything to calm your nerves during your consultation . In addition, here are the answers to a few frequently asked questions about dental implants.

Can I take dental implants out?

No, because they are designed to serve as a lifelong solution for missing teeth. An implant post is surgically placed into the jaw, which your bone will fuse to through a process called osseointegration. After you’ve finished healing, the post can remain in place forever with the proper care. Only a trained dental professional can take them out, like in cases of failure. While you can’t remove the posts, some implant dentures can be taken out for easy cleaning.

Does it hurt to get dental implants?

You’ll require oral surgery to insert the posts, but you won’t feel any pain during the process. Your jawbone doesn’t have any nerve endings. Not to mention, sedation or anesthesia will be used to ensure you are comfortable. However, it’s normal for your mouth to feel sore once the effects of any numbing agents dissipate. An OTC pain reliever and applying cold compresses will reduce your discomfort. A cold compress will also minimize any swelling or bruising. It’s best to eat softer foods until your dentist instructs otherwise.

What are the signs of dental implant failure?

Dental implants are the most reliable method of treating tooth loss. Generally, the risk of implant failure is less than 5 %, which is often caused by an infection called peri-implantitis. If the infection is detected and treated early, you can avoid losing your smile. Contact your implant dentist in Las Vegas right away if you develop inflammation, redness, or bleeding around the post. Don’t wait to see your dentist if pressure on the implant causes pain, or the post feels loose. Your dentist will find the source of the problem and provide the best treatment to avoid dental implant failure.
