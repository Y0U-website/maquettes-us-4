What’s the Solution for Snoring?

Mandibular Advancement Device (MAD) Our go-to solution for snoring prevention is the MAD a small oral appliance that gently holds your lower jaw forward while you sleep. This opens your airway, reduces tissue vibration, and helps you breathe without interruption.

Some devices also stop the tongue from falling back over your windpipe. Your dentist will fit these special appliances to meet your condition.

To learn more about a mandibular advancement device, contact our practice and schedule an appointment and consultation.
