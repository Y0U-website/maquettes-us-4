Southlake Orthodontics & Invisalign Services

Studies show that individuals with straight, healthy teeth and attractive smiles are typically considered more outgoing, charismatic, confident, and even successful. If your teeth are crooked, crowded, oddly spaced, or otherwise misaligned, you may find yourself hiding your smile from the people who matter most in both your professional and your personal life. With traditional orthodontic treatment as well as Invisalign clear braces, Dr. Thomas and her team can help you achieve the healthier, more beautiful smile you’ve always wanted.

Traditional Orthodontic Treatment

Traditional metal braces offer unparalleled straightening ability and are still the first choice for correcting problems like overcrowding, gaps, openbites, crossbites, underbites, overbites, and overlapping teeth. Depending on your unique needs and preferences, we may use metal brackets or tooth-colored brackets to gradually guide your teeth into proper position. Colored bands are also available, allowing you to customize your look to suit your personality.
