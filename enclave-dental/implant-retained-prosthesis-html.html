Dental Implants

  • An implant-retained prosthesis is completely stable, which supports a balanced, nutritious diet.
  • It is easy to speak clearly with an implant-retained prosthetic -- no worry of a denture that causes a whistle or lisp.
  • Dentures that are stable and secure will not slip around, eliminating the trouble of painful oral sores.
  • Ceramic, metal-free dental implants do not introduce harmful materials into the body or blood stream.

Are You a Candidate?

If you are in suitable oral and overall health, you are likely a wonderful candidate for dental implants. As a holistic dentist, Dr. Thomas uses biocompatible, metal-free materials to protect your overall health and wellbeing, which opens the procedure up to even more candidates. During a consultation, Dr. Thomas will evaluate your health, discuss your smile goals, and examine your teeth to determine whether or not you are eligible for this option. If you are, she looks forward to getting started on your tooth replacement right away!
