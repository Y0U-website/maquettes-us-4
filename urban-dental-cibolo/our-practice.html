A small office with a bright smile, helping you look and feel more confident with over three decades of experience at your service.

Zhanna Urban, D.D.S.

Originally from Belarus, Zhanna has over 30 years of experience in the dental field. Graduate of the Minsk Medical Academy, trained as a fellow with the German Dental Association in Berlin as well as more recent graduate of the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry, she has decided to settle in Texas for the next chapter of her life. Having worked with a variety of patients across the globe she is glad to bring her experience to Cibolo in order to provide beautiful and healthy smiles, improving the way her patients look and feel.
