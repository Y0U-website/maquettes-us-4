DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) and DMD (Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry) are two professional degrees in dentistry that are interchangeable degrees that represent the same high level of dental education and qualifications. Some dental schools award DDS degrees, while others award DMD degrees. The main difference between DDS and DMD is the historical origin of the degree title. DDS originated in the United States, while DMD originated in Europe.
