After receiving a dental implant, you need a new tooth to attach to the implant. Called a restoration, this new tooth – typically a crown – is custom designed to look and function like a natural tooth: you can eat the foods you love and smile with confidence!

Orthodontic appliances are made of metal, ceramic, or plastic. They may be removable or they may be brackets bonded to the teeth. By placing a constant, gentle force in a carefully controlled direction, braces slowly move teeth to the proper position.
