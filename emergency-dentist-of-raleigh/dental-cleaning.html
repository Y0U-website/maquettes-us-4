Types of professional teeth cleaning

Healthy mouth cleaning

Your dental hygienist will meticulously clean the surfaces of your teeth and above the gumlines. Using a scaler, they’ll scrap away plaque and tartar buildup to prevent future problems, and gently remove any stubborn stains. A final polish shines and cleans your tooth enamel.

Periodontal (dental deep cleaning)

Need to stop the progression of gum infection, also known as periodontal disease? Professional scaling and root planing effectively target bacteria that have extended deep below the gum line. Scaling involves meticulous removal of these deposits from the tooth surfaces and pockets, while root planing focuses on smoothing the root surfaces to facilitate gum reattachment and prevent bacterial growth.

Periodontal maintenance cleaning

Periodontal maintenance is a specialized cleaning performed periodically to keep periodontal disease in remission. It is a great way to maintain your gum health after your initial periodontal cleaning with your Raleigh emergency dental care team.

Dental cleaning FAQs

What happens during a dental cleaning?

Your Emergency Clinic Raleigh hygienist removes plaque, tartar and surface stains from your teeth. They target areas above and below the gumline using specialized tools. The procedure includes teeth polishing to create a smooth, gleaming surface. Regular dental cleanings contribute to preventing cavities, gum disease and bad breath.

Why do you need regular teeth cleanings?

Regular teeth cleanings are crucial for maintaining oral health and preventing dental problems. Professional cleaning effectively remove plaque, tartar and stains that regular brushing and flossing may miss. Addressing these buildups can significantly reduce the risk of cavities, gum disease and bad breath.

How frequently should you get your teeth cleaned?

The frequency of teeth cleanings depends on your personal needs, but it’s generally recommended to schedule professional cleanings every six months. These regular appointments play a pivotal role in preventing dental issues such as cavities and gum disease.

How long does a dental cleaning take?

The process typically takes about an hour depending on the amount of plaque and tartar buildup, the overall health of your teeth and gums, and whether any additional procedures, like X-rays, are being performed. Regular dental cleanings help maintain optimal oral hygiene and prevent potential dental issues.

Is teeth cleaning painful?

Teeth cleaning may cause some discomfort, especially if you have sensitive teeth or gum issues, but it is generally not painful. Most people find it manageable and worth the benefits of oral health.

Does teeth cleaning remove yellow?

Yes, teeth cleaning can help remove surface stains, including yellowing, caused by food, drinks and tobacco, resulting in a brighter smile.

Do teeth look different after cleaning?

Absolutely! After a professional cleaning, teeth often appear cleaner and whiter, enhancing your overall smile and boosting your confidence.
