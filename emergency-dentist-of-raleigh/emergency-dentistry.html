FAQs about Emergency Dental Care

What is a dental emergency?

A dental emergency is an urgent issue such as severe tooth pain, knocked-out or broken teeth, persistent bleeding, or swelling that requires immediate care to relieve pain, prevent damage, or treat infection.

What emergency dental services does Emergency Dentist of Raleigh offer?

Emergency Dentist of Raleigh provides a wide range of urgent dental services based on your needs. Treatments include severe toothache relief, repair of cracked or broken teeth, abscess treatment, and replacement of lost fillings or crowns to restore function and prevent further complications.

Is a broken tooth considered a dental emergency?

Yes, a broken tooth is a dental emergency. It can cause pain, sensitivity, and increase the risk of infection. Immediate treatment helps prevent further damage and protects your tooth from complications.

Do dental emergencies require oral surgery?

Not all dental emergencies require surgery. Treatment can range from minor procedures to more advanced care such as tooth extractions or root canals depending on the severity of the condition.

What can I expect when I walk into the Emergency Dentist of Raleigh for a dental emergency?

When you walk in, expect immediate and prioritized care focused on relieving pain and treating the issue. You will receive a comprehensive evaluation using modern equipment along with clear communication about treatment options, costs, and aftercare.

Will dental insurance cover dental emergencies?

Dental insurance plans may cover some emergency treatments, but coverage depends on your provider and the type of procedure required.
