  • Airway orthodontics : This is a specialized branch of orthodontics that focuses on the relationship between a person’s dental and facial structure and their ability to breathe effectively. Misalignment of the teeth, jaw, and facial bones can often cause issues related to the airway, such as breathing difficulties and sleep-related breathing disorders.

Early Childhood Orthodontic Treatment

Early orthodontic treatment can help prevent minor problems from becoming far more serious in the future. At RS Orthodontics, we follow the recommendation of the American Association of Orthodontists that children see an orthodontist at age seven for an initial evaluation. Although most children do not need early orthodontic treatment (also sometimes called “Phase One orthodontic treatment”), there are certain bite, jaw, or tooth development problems where early orthodontic treatment can be beneficial.

Some signs your child may need early orthodontic treatment include:

Some missing teeth, extra teeth, or teeth growing in the wrong direction may only be detectable by a trained orthodontist. Other issues like tooth crowding, extra space between teeth, or bad bites can be inherited or caused by injury to the mouth and may necessitate early orthodontic treatment as well. Because every child is different and has different needs, a visit with our orthodontist, Dr. Sebbahi, is the best way to determine if your child needs orthodontic treatment.

If your child exhibits any signs mentioned above, please contact RS Orthodontics and schedule an appointment for an early evaluation.
