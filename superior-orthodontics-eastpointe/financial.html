How orthodontic insurance actually works

An orthodontic benefit is not a payment plan. Most plans carry a lifetime orthodontic maximum. That is one fixed pot of money per person, for life, not per year. When it’s gone, it’s gone, and it rarely covers a whole case.

It reduces the total, not the monthly. Your benefit comes off the overall fee. What’s left is what we spread across your plan, which is why two people with the same insurance can walk out with different monthly figures.

No insurance is normal here. Plenty of our patients have none. The in-house plan was built for exactly that: $800 down, from $188 a month, 0 % interest, no credit application to a third party. We do not accept Medicaid.

What decides the cost of braces

How far your teeth have to move. Mild crowding is a simpler case than a bite that needs correcting. More movement means more chair time, and chair time is most of what you are paying for.

How long your case runs. Your plan is spread across the length of treatment, so a longer case does not automatically mean a bigger monthly payment. Dr. Kole-James sets your timeline at the exam, not before it.

Which appliance you pick. Metal and ceramic braces and our in-house clear aligners are all options here, and the appliance affects the total. Every one of them runs on the same in-house plan: $800 down, from $188 a month, 0 % interest.

Whether you have an orthodontic benefit. If you do, it comes off the total before we work out your monthly figure. If you don’t, the in-house plan stands on its own.

What changes your total

Two people can start the same week and leave with different numbers. Here is what actually moves the figure.

How far your teeth have to move. A little crowding and a bite that needs real correction are not the same job. More movement means more visits and more time in treatment.

How long you are in treatment. Your plan is spread across the months you are actually wearing appliances, so a shorter case and a longer case land on different monthly figures.

Which appliance you pick. Metal braces are the most affordable option we offer. Ceramic brackets and clear aligners sit above them. We will show you the difference at the exam instead of steering you.

Whether you have an orthodontic benefit. If your plan carries one, it comes off the total before we spread the rest. If it does not, the in-house plan stands on its own.
