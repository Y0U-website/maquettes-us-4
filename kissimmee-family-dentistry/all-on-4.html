All-on-Four vs. Traditional Dentures

All-on-Four Kissimmee implants are fixed in place , so they stay secure while you talk, eat, and smile. Conversely, dentures can slip or move around, which can be uncomfortable and embarrassing.

All-on-Four also helps keep your jawbone healthy by stimulating bone growth . Dentures don’t do this, which can lead to bone loss over time.

In terms of how long they last, All-on-Four can work for 20 years or more with good care. Dentures typically need to be replaced every 5 to 7 years.

They also look and feel more like natural teeth. All-on-Four feels comfortable and stable—like your own smile. Dentures may feel bulky, cause sore spots, or look less natural.

Overall, All-on-Four gives you a longer-lasting, more comfortable, and more confident smile .
