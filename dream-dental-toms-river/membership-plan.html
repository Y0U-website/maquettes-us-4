What is a Dream Dental Membership Plan

A membership plan is a simple alternative to traditional dental insurance. For one low monthly payment, you receive your essential preventive care, including cleanings, exams, and x-rays, plus a discount on nearly everything else you may need. There are no claims to file, no waiting periods, and no networks to navigate. Just straightforward, predictable care built around you.

Real Value, Built Into Every Plan

Preventive care included in the Adult Essential plan carries a total annual value of over $1,290 when billed at standard rates. As a member, that same care is included in your low monthly payment, with no surprises and no separate bills for the basics.

Additional Savings Callout: Beyond your included preventive visits, members save 25 % on most other dental services, including fillings, crowns, sealants, and more.

Rollover Rewards Callout: Every preventive visit earns you a $25 Rollover Reward that can be applied toward future treatment, up to 100 % of the cost of a needed service. Rewards do not expire as long as your membership stays active, and family members can combine their rewards to maximize savings together.
