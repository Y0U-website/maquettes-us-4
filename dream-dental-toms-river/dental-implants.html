What Is a Dental Implant ?

A dental implant is a permanent tooth replacement that works from the root up. A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone, fuses naturally with the bone over time, and is topped with a custom crown that looks and functions exactly like a natural tooth. At Dream Dental in Toms River, Dr. Assouline places single-tooth implants with precision and care, giving patients a solution that is built to last a lifetime.

A Permanent Solution, Not a Temporary Fix

Unlike bridges or dentures, a dental implant replaces the entire tooth structure, from the root up. A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone, where it bonds with the bone over time through a process called osseointegration. A custom-designed crown is then attached on top, completing the restoration with a result that is stable, durable, and indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

For patients who have lost a tooth to decay, injury, or extraction, a dental implant is typically the most long-lasting and functional option available. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation and Planning

Dr. Assouline begins with a thorough evaluation of your oral health, bone density, and overall candidacy for an implant. He will walk you through the full process, answer every question, and create a treatment plan tailored to your situation.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed into the jawbone in a carefully controlled procedure. Dr. Assouline prioritizes your comfort throughout, and the team ensures you feel informed and at ease at every step.

Healing and Integration

Over the following weeks, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone. This healing phase is essential to the long-term stability of the restoration. Dr. Assouline monitors your progress closely during this period.

Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, a custom-crafted crown is attached to the implant. The crown is designed to match your surrounding teeth in color, shape, and size, for a result that looks and feels entirely natural.
