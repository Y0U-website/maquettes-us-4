  • What dental services do you offer at Somerset Dentists? We provide comprehensive care including general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, Invisalign, sleep apnea treatment, periodontal therapy, TMJ treatment, pediatric dentistry, emergency dental care, and sedation dentistry. Our goal is to offer complete, high-quality care for your entire family under one roof.
  • Are you accepting new patients? Yes! We’re currently welcoming new patients of all ages. Whether you need routine preventive care or specialized treatment, our team is here to help you feel comfortable and confident from your very first visit.
  • Do you accept dental insurance? We are an out-of-network provider, but we happily accept assignment of benefits and will submit claims on your behalf. Our team ensures you get the most from your insurance plan while receiving the high-quality care you deserve.
  • What should I expect during my first visit? Your first visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, periodontal evaluation, oral cancer screening, and a conversation about your dental goals. We also address any concerns you have and create a personalized treatment plan based on your needs.
  • What is the Dental Savings Plan and who is it for? Our Dental Savings Plan is designed for patients without insurance. For $299 per year, you’ll receive two cleanings, exams, X-rays, fluoride treatments, one emergency exam, and discounts on most treatments. It’s an excellent way to maintain your oral health affordably.
  • Do you offer sleep apnea treatment without a CPAP? Yes! Dr. Pollatos specializes in custom oral appliance therapy, a comfortable, CPAP-free solution for mild to moderate sleep apnea. We also integrate myofunctional therapy to improve breathing patterns and long-term airway stability.
  • How do I know if I’m a candidate for dental implants? Most patients with missing teeth qualify for implants. During your consultation, we evaluate your bone health, gum condition, bite alignment, and overall health. With advanced technology and the expertise of Dr. Babayan, we offer single implants, implant bridges, and All-on-X full-arch solutions.
  • Do you treat children? Absolutely. We provide gentle, education-based pediatric dentistry for children of all ages. Our team creates a calm, positive environment that helps kids feel safe while learning healthy habits that last a lifetime.
  • I’m nervous about dental work. Do you offer sedation? Yes we offer nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation (administered by a licensed anesthesiologist). Whether you have dental anxiety, a sensitive gag reflex, or need multiple treatments at once, sedation can help you receive care comfortably and stress-free.
  • What should I do if I have a dental emergency? Call our office right away. We reserve time daily for same-day emergency appointments. Whether you have a severe toothache, broken tooth, swelling, or a damaged restoration, our team will help you get relief quickly and restore your dental health.
