Dental implants offer life-enhancing advantages beyond simply replacing a tooth.

Implants are designed to last decades often a lifetime with proper care. They don’t decay and are anchored securely in the jaw for ultimate stability.

When teeth are missing, the jawbone begins to shrink over time. Implants stimulate the bone like natural roots, preventing bone loss and maintaining your natural facial contours.

Because implants function like natural teeth, you can enjoy your favorite foods without worry. No slipping, clicking, or discomfort.

Unlike bridges, implants don’t require removing any structure from neighboring teeth. This means your natural teeth stay strong and intact.

What to Expect During Your Dental Implant Treatment

At Somerset Dentists, we make the implant process comfortable, clear, and predictable. You’ll always understand what’s happening and why no surprises, no pressure.

Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam and CBCT imaging (3D scan) to evaluate bone density, spacing, and overall oral health.

We discuss your goals, review your medical history, and create a personalized treatment plan whether it’s a single implant or a full-arch All-on-X restoration.

Using advanced digital software, Dr. Babayan plans the exact angle, depth, and position of your implant for maximum comfort, stability, and esthetics. This ensures precision and minimizes risks during surgery.

The implant post is placed gently into the jawbone in a minimally invasive procedure. Many patients report that implant placement is more comfortable than a tooth extraction.

Over the next few months, your bone naturally fuses with the implant. During this time, you may receive a temporary tooth so you can maintain your appearance and function.

Once healed, we attach the abutment and place your final custom crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis.

Your restoration will be handcrafted for natural appearance, ideal bite alignment, and long-term durability.

We schedule regular check-ins to ensure optimal healing and long-term success. With routine cleanings and proper home care, your implants can last a lifetime.
