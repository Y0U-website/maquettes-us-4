MEET OUR SPECIALISTS

Dr. Anna Bagdonas DDS, RN

Dr. Bagdonas earned her doctorate degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. After graduation, she completed a general practice residency at the Hennepin County Medical Center in Minneapolis, MN. Dr. Bagdonas is passionate about dental sleep medicine and overall health. She has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education specialized in sleep medicine. She studies with the best in the field of dental sleep medicine and TMJ treatment in the United States. Dr. Bagdonas is a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine.

Dr. Bagdonas is also a RN! She graduated Magna Cum Laude from Northeastern University before she went to dental school.

Nicole

Nicole was born and raised on the South Shore of Boston. She brings extensive knowledge of sleep and sleep disordered breathing issues and uses it every day to help patients regain lost sleep. She enjoys meeting new patients and is thrilled to be able to support the best quality of life for our sleep deprived patients.
