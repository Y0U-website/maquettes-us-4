The practice

After high school Dr. Striegel attended the University of Louisville where he graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. He then attended the University of Louisville School of Dentistry where he graduated Summa Cum Laude earning a degree of Doctor of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Striegel is a member of the American Dental Association, the Indiana Dental Association, the Southern Indiana Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

In order to stay up to date with dentistry’s best practices, Dr. Striegel regularly attends advanced continuing education courses all across the country. With the ever changing technological advancements in dentistry this ensures the ability to provide state of the art treatment to his patients.

Apart from dentistry, Dr. Striegel enjoys spending time with his wife, Madison (his high school sweetheart) and dog, Bronny. Both Madison and Dr. Striegel are avid sports fans and love trying new food spots.

“My dream through the entire process of becoming a dentist was the ability to provide care to the community that helped shape me. Southern Indiana is a close knit community built on trust and relationship. I am excited to play a part in this culture.” -Dr. Striegel
