Are dental implant procedures painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the dental implant process. The surgical placement is done under local anesthesia, and we offer sedation options for a completely comfortable experience. Most post-surgery soreness can be easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

How long does the implant process take?

The dental implant process typically takes several months, as it involves the healing period for the implant to integrate with your jawbone. However, this timeline helps to ensure a secure, lasting result that’s well worth the wait.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most people are good candidates for dental implants. However, those with insufficient jawbone density may require a bone graft. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your health and determine your best path forward.

Will my dental implants look natural?

Yes! Our custom-made crowns are designed to match the color, size, and shape of your natural teeth. The result is a beautifully restored smile that blends seamlessly with your existing teeth.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have an extremely high success rate—around 95 % to 98 %. With proper care, they can last a lifetime.

Are dental implants expensive?

While implants are an investment, they are also a long-term solution. Unlike other options that may need to be replaced or adjusted over time, implants offer a permanent fix, saving you money in the long run.
