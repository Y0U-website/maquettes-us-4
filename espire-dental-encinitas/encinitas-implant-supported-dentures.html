What are Implant-supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures combine the same coverage of traditional dentures with the stability of dental implants. Instead of resting on your gums and relying on adhesives, these dentures are secured with dental implants surgically placed into your jawbone. This provides a strong, fixed foundation for your dentures, preventing the slipping and shifting many patients experience with traditional removable dentures. The procedure results in a strong set of teeth that look and feel natural and are stable and comfortable for daily wear.

The Benefits of Implant-supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures offer a range of benefits over traditional dentures. Here’s why so many patients choose this advanced solution:

  • No Adhesives or Pastes: Unlike traditional dentures, which often require messy adhesives to stay secure, implant-supported dentures snap into place and stay put, eliminating the need for adhesives.
  • Enhanced Chewing Ability & Comfort: Certain foods can be challenging to eat with traditional dentures. Implant-supported dentures allow you to enjoy a broader range of foods, improving your overall nutrition and quality of life.
  • Prevention of Bone Loss: Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that often occurs when teeth are missing. This helps maintain your facial structure and prevents the “sunken” appearance resulting from long-term denture use.
  • Natural Look and Feel: Implant-supported dentures are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. You’ll be able to speak and smile confidently, knowing your dentures are securely in place and look completely natural.
  • Long-Term Solution: With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last many years. They’re a durable, reliable solution that provides long-term value.

How do implant-supported dentures differ from traditional dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are anchored by dental implants, making them more secure and comfortable than traditional dentures. They eliminate the need for adhesives and provide a more natural feel.

How long do implant-supported dentures last?

With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last many years, and the implants can last a lifetime. Routine dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential for maintaining their longevity.

Will I be able to eat normally with implant-supported dentures?

Yes! One of the most significant advantages of implant-supported dentures is the ability to eat a wider variety of foods. The stability provided by the implants allows you to chew comfortably without worrying about your dentures shifting.

How long does the process take?

The entire process, including implant placement and healing time, can take several months. The timeline varies depending on each patient’s individual situation, and we’ll provide you with a precise treatment plan during your consultation.

Are implant-supported dentures easy to care for?

Caring for implant-supported dentures is simple. Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing twice a day and flossing around the implants, and continue with regular dental checkups to ensure everything remains in good condition.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the implant placement procedure, and we provide local anesthesia or sedation options to ensure your comfort. Any post-procedure discomfort can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
