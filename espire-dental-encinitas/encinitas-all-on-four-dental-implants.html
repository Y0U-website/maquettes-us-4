How long do All-on-Four implants typically last?

All-on-Four implants are designed for long-term success. With consistent at-home care and regular dental visits, they can last 20 years or more. The titanium implants fuse with your jawbone and often remain stable for life.

Am I a good candidate for this treatment?

Most patients who are missing a full arch of teeth or have failing dentures qualify.We&#x27;ll evaluate your bone structure with 3D scans to confirm, even if you&#x27;ve been told you&#x27;re not a candidate elsewhere.

Will I be in pain after the procedure?

Discomfort is generally minimal. Most patients report less pain than expected and manage it with over-the-counter medications. Sedation ensures a comfortable experience during the procedure. Recovery is often faster than traditional implant surgeries.

How is this better than traditional dentures?

Traditional dentures sit on your gums and can shift while eating or talking. All-on-Four implants are permanently secured to your jawbone. They improve stability, bite force, and long-term bone health. They feel and function more like natural teeth.

Can I eat normally with All-on-Four?

Yes.Once your implants have healed and the final prosthetic is in place, you can eat most foods comfortably—no more dietary restrictions or awkward moments at the dinner table.
