  • What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants? Dental implants are considered the best available solution to replace missing teeth because they preserve the health of your jawbone while improving the appearance of your smile. Some key benefits of dental implants include: Restored biting and chewing ability
  • Prevention of bone loss in the jawbone
  • Improved oral health
  • Permanent solution to replace missing teeth
  • How Long Do Dental Implants Last? On average, dental implants last anywhere from 5 to 15 years with proper care. This means practicing good oral hygiene at home, such as brushing and flossing every day. It also means seeing your dentist twice per year for routine checkups to ensure your teeth, gums, and jaw are all healthy. By following these simple steps, your new dental implants can last a lifetime.
