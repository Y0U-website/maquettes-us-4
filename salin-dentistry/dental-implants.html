Dental Implants FAQ Get answers about dental implants in Bakersfield, CA

1. How long do all-on-4 implants last?

All-on-four implants can last up to 20 years, with proper care and keeping up with regular cleanings and dental visits.

2. Can I get an implant if I have other medical issues?

Dental implants involve oral surgery, and like any other procedure, it is essential to be medically cleared by your dentist and your doctor. Dental implants are standard, and many people are eligible even with underlying conditions.

3. Am I too old for implants?

Dental implants can be placed for patients of any adult age as long as sufficient bone in the jaw is present for implanting.

4. What if I want "metal-free" implants?

There are metal free options for dental implants zirconium implants may be right for you.

5. Can your mouth reject an implant?

Although rare, rejection of dental implants can occur if there is poor dental hygiene, postoperative trauma, or misalignment that arises.

6. Are you put to sleep for dental implants?

Dental implant surgery involves sedation or anesthesia. Our dental office offers various sedation and anesthetic services.
