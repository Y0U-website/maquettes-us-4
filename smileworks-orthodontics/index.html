Dr. Hussaini, or simply “Dr. H,” is truly passionate about orthodontics!

For him, one of the greatest thrills in life is seeing the ecstatic reactions and self-confidence in his patients, brought about by their beautiful, new smiles. He takes great joy in providing quality orthodontic treatment that gives his patients the smiles they deserve.

Dr. Hussaini earned a Bachelor of Science undergraduate degree in biomedical engineering from Virginia Commonwealth University, graduating summa cum laude. He then went on to complete his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the VCU School of Dentistry. He attended the MCV campus and graduated magna cum laude.

Following dental school, Dr. Hussaini completed an orthodontic residency program at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, where he was awarded a Master of Science degree in Orthodontics.
