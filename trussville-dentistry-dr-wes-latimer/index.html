When you’re overdue for dental care, simply getting in the door can be overwhelming. Feeling embarrassed about your teeth, worrying about how the dentist and staff will treat you, and dreading the sheer number of appointments can all hold people back from the healthy teeth they deserve.

At Trussville Dentistry, we offer a welcoming, non-judgemental dental experience with a comprehensive range of services and sedation, so patients who’ve had a break in dental care can maintain their new oral health, in as few appointments as possible.

We believe that your dentist should treat you with respect, no matter what condition your teeth are in or when the last time you had care was. At Trussville Dentistry, we’ve helped thousands of patients with non-judgemental dental care achieve the healthy teeth they had almost given up on.
