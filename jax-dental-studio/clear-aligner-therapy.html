The Benefits of Clear Aligner Therapy

The appeal of clear aligner therapy goes well beyond the cosmetic. The trays are virtually invisible when worn, which matters significantly to adults and teens who want treatment that does not change how they look or carry themselves in the meantime. Beyond discretion, there are practical, day-to-day advantages that make the experience meaningfully different from traditional orthodontics.

Nearly Invisible

The smooth, clear plastic material is barely noticeable in conversation, social settings, or professional environments.

No Food Restrictions

Remove the trays at mealtimes and eat whatever you like. Put them back in when you are finished and rinsed.

Easy Oral Hygiene

Brush and floss your teeth exactly as you normally would. No maneuvering around brackets or wires.

Fewer Office Visits

Because you advance through trays on your own schedule, check-in appointments are brief and less frequent than with traditional braces.
