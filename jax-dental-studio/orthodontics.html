How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

Several signs indicate you might benefit from seeing an orthodontist in Jacksonville. Crowded or overlapping teeth create spaces where plaque and bacteria accumulate, increasing your risk of cavities and the need for gum disease treatment . Gaps between teeth can affect your bite and make it difficult to eat certain foods. Jaw pain, clicking sounds when opening or closing your mouth, or difficulty biting and chewing often signal alignment problems that orthodontic treatment can address.

Consistent wear of aligners and attendance at scheduled adjustment appointments directly impact your timeline. Missing appointments or not wearing aligners as directed extends treatment duration. Our team provides clear instructions and support throughout your care, helping you stay on track for the best possible outcome.

What Happens After Orthodontic Treatment?

Completing your active treatment represents an exciting milestone, but maintaining your results requires ongoing care. Retainers prevent teeth from shifting back to their original positions after your braces come off or you finish wearing aligners. Most patients need to wear retainers full-time initially, then transition to nighttime wear to preserve their beautiful new smile.

Regular preventive dentistry appointments remain essential after orthodontic treatment. Professional cleanings and examinations help protect your investment by catching potential problems early. Maintaining excellent home care through brushing, flossing, and following our team’s recommendations ensures your straightened teeth stay healthy for life.
