Understanding Different Types of Retainers

Retainers come in several varieties, each designed to address specific orthodontic needs and patient preferences. Clear retainers have become increasingly popular due to their discreet appearance and comfortable fit. These custom-fitted appliances are nearly invisible when worn, making them an excellent choice for adults and teens who prefer a less noticeable retention option.

Removable Retainer Options

Clear retainers, including Vivera retainers (made by the creators of Invisalign), provide exceptional durability while maintaining a virtually invisible appearance. These precisely crafted devices are custom-made using digital impression technology for superior comfort and effectiveness. Wearing removable retainers as prescribed by our Jacksonville dental team ensures your teeth remain in their corrected positions long after your orthodontic treatment concludes.

Fixed Retainer Solutions

For patients seeking maintenance-free retention, permanent retainers offer an excellent solution. These wire retainers are bonded to the back surface of your teeth, providing continuous protection against unwanted tooth movement without requiring daily insertion and removal. Many patients appreciate the convenience of fixed retainers, particularly when retention is recommended for extended periods or indefinitely.

The retention phase is critical to stabilize your results, whether you’ve completed treatment with conventional braces or Invisalign clear aligners.
