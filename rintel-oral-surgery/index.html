The practice

Our private office is designed with your comfort in mind, and we offer same-day appointments and direct access to your surgeon for any urgent needs. With transparent communication and a commitment to ethical care, we’re valued by patients and referring dentists alike.

Dr. Rintel provides compassionate, high-quality care with a focus on minimally invasive oral and maxillofacial surgery. He specializes in dental implants, extractions, and facial trauma treatment, and is dedicated to ongoing patient education and comfort.

Dr. Rintel is a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon, holding numerous certifications and professional affiliations.

He uses innovative technology like virtual surgical planning and medical modeling for precise, effective treatments.

We’ve invested in the latest technology to make your experience smoother, more accurate, and more pleasant. CBCT imaging gives us detailed 3D views to plan your treatment with accuracy, while intra-oral scanning replaces traditional impressions. We also use platelet-rich fibrin to speed up healing and enhance recovery.

If you suffer from dental anxiety or have to undergo complex treatments, our in-office IV sedation keeps you relaxed throughout the procedure. With our modern equipment and techniques, we ensure you get exceptional care with less stress and better results.

We’re here to help with all your oral surgery needs. From complex procedures to urgent care, our experienced team works with you to ensure an optimal outcome, making sure you feel heard and taken care of along the way.

When canines get stuck beneath the gums, we safely extract them to maintain alignment and prevent complications.
