Dental Implants

  • Have bone density to support the implant
  • Have healthy gums and no active infection
  • Do not smoke or are willing to quit during healing

Benefits of Getting Dental Implants

  • Natural Look – Designed to match your existing teeth.
  • Strong Bite – Enjoy your favorite foods without worry.
  • Bone Preservation – Stimulates bone, preventing bone loss.
  • No Slipping – Won’t move or shift like dentures can.
  • Protects Other Teeth – Doesn’t rely on neighboring teeth for support.
  • Long-Lasting – Can last decades.
