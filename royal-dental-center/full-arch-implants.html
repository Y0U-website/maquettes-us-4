From Assessment to Your Final Set

  • 1 Full Assessment Your dentist evaluates your remaining teeth, bone volume, gum health, and bite, and lays out the full plan, timeline, and cost before anything starts.
  • 2 Placement Day Any teeth that cannot be saved are removed and the implants are placed in the same appointment, so you are not left waiting between the two.
  • 3 Your Temporary Teeth You leave with a fixed temporary set while the implants integrate with the bone over the following months. You are never without teeth.
  • 4 The Final Set Once healing is complete we fit your finished teeth, refine the shape and shade, and balance the bite so chewing feels natural again.

Full Arch Implants Questions

Usually four to six per arch, depending on your bone and your bite. Your dentist confirms the number after evaluating your jaw, not before.

In most cases yes. You leave placement day with a fixed temporary set, and the final teeth are made once the implants have fully integrated.

They are fixed rather than removable, they do not cover the roof of your mouth, and they preserve jawbone. Dentures remain a valid choice when implants are not practical.

The teeth are real and you do leave with them. What is not accurate is the impression that treatment finishes that day. Implants have to fuse with bone before they can carry full chewing force, and that healing takes months no matter who places them. So what same day honestly means is that the implants go in and a fixed temporary set goes on top in one appointment, and the final teeth are made once the implants have integrated. You are never without teeth in between, which is the part that actually matters to patients.

Differently from natural teeth and differently from a denture. The teeth are fixed, so they do not come out at night, and the surface that needs cleaning is underneath the bridge where it meets the gum. A water flosser along that line, an interdental brush, and ordinary brushing cover it for most patients. We show you on your own set once it is fitted and check underneath at every visit, because the tissue around the implants is what determines how long the whole thing lasts.

Long term denture wear thins the bone underneath, but grafting can often rebuild enough to place implants. Your dentist will tell you honestly what is possible after imaging.
