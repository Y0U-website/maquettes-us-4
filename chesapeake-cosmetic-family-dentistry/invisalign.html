Braces vs. Invisalign

Braces

Traditional metal braces have served many people well, but they have their drawbacks. They can cause more discomfort due to the metal and wire materials used, and once you get them tightened, eating and drinking can be a bit of a pain for a few days afterward. Plus, traditional braces are hard to disguise; you can’t take them out for important meetings or an extra-crunchy meal!

Invisalign

Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign Clear Aligners are made out of durable, clear plastic. They’re more comfortable than the harsh metal of braces, and also more convenient: You can take them out to eat or drink, causing minimal disruption to your daily life. Their clear appearance makes it a more discreet way to straighten your smile.

Why choose Invisalign?

Invisalign Clear Aligners are a comfortable and convenient way to straighten your teeth, adjust your bite, close gaps in your teeth, and more.

The Invisalign process

Consultation

Our Annapolis dentists provide free Invisalign consultations to patients interested in straightening their smiles. You’ll come in to speak with one of our doctors and after a brief exam and iTero 3D scan, we&#x27;ll discuss whether or not you’re a good candidate for Invisalign treatment. If you are, we&#x27;ll show you a simulation of your expected results.

Custom-Fit Clear Aligners

After accepting treatment, we’ll create a digital treatment plan for your case and your custom-fit clear aligners will be fabricated. You&#x27;ll then start your Invisalign treatment and your first Invisalign clear aligners will be delivered.

Periodic Check-Ins

Approximately every eight weeks, you&#x27;ll return for us to check the progress of your Invisalign treatment, and receive new sets of aligners. Each case is unique, and your doctor will have outlined the expected timeline and results of your Invisalign Clear Aligner therapy from the start, so you know exactly what to expect.
