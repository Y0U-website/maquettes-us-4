CEREC - One day crowns

One day crowns are a reality. No second visits. No temporary crowns. Bonded CEREC Crowns offer many advantages. Healthy tooth structure is preserved. CEREC cameras allow us to take impressions without goopy materials. A computer controlled milling machine fabricates the restoration during the appointment. The patient no longer requires a temporary, and leaves the office with a restored tooth. No need for a second appointment!
