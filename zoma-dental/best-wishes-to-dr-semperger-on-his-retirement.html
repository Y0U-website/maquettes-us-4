Our Location

Our office is located in the heart of downtown Farmington , an innovative small town community offering unique restaurants, charming shops, a seasonal farmer’s market, and a wide range of family-friendly events throughout the year. We are proud to represent the small business community and provide specialized dental care that serves all generations of Michigan families.

Our office was renovated in 2023 and outfitted with modern, state-of-the-art dental technology and equipment, including digital X-rays (individual and panoramic), an intraoral camera, and patient monitors for visual educational purposes.
