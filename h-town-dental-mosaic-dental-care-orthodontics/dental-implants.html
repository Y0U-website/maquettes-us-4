Implants are artificial dental roots that are used to replace missing teeth. Implants provide as a sturdy foundation for fixed (permanent) or removable replacement teeth that are designed to look and function like your natural teeth.

The placement of implants is normally done in two stages. The initial step is to place the implant into the jawbone, followed by the placement of your replacement permanent tooth or teeth four to eight months later.

The treatment is normally performed under local anesthesia in your local practice. If you are extremely nervous about the process, your dentist may be able to provide you with anesthesia to help you relax throughout it.

Implants are popular among patients because they are both inconspicuous and long-lasting. We understand that missing teeth make people feel self-conscious. With an implant, you should be able to regain your confidence in your smile, how you eat, and how you speak.

Dental implants are currently not covered by most dental insurance plans. Depending on your insurance plan and/or the source of tooth loss, coverage under your medical plan may be possible. Your dentist and insurance provider should be asked detailed questions about your specific needs and how they relate to insurance.
