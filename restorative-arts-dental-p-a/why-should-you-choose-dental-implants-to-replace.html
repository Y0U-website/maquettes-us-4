Why Should You Choose Dental Implants to Replace Teeth?

Most dentists globally recommend dental implants to replace missing teeth. Tooth implants are incredibly popular, although they are unsuitable for many. However, implants come with many benefits when providing a substitute for a missing tooth.

Dental implants are the only solution that helps to restore your smile and help you regain your self-confidence. In addition, implants help prevent jawbone deterioration after they are embedded in your jawbone to replace your missing natural tooth root.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth root replacements that function as a durable base for affixing an artificial tooth. In addition, the implant ensures your replacement tooth is stable and lasts for life.

Why Consider Implants for Missing Teeth?

Implants are excellent as missing tooth replacements. They ensure the restoration of your smile by holding your replacement tooth. In addition, implants are helpful to replace one or several teeth depending on your unique situation. Here are why you must consider dental implants as missing tooth replacements.

Effective Technique to Restore Your Smile

Implants are the most effective cosmetic dentistry procedure when restoring your smile after missing a tooth. Implants function, feel and appear like natural teeth because they are designed to integrate with your jawbone with time. The integration happens because titanium helps make implants ensuring the biocompatible material fuses with the bone.

The implant feels comfortable as it integrates with the jawbone, ensuring you can smile comfortably because it will not appear different from your natural teeth. Your speech and eating abilities also benefit from implants.

Long Lasting Solution

When you choose dental implants near you as the optimal replacement solution for your missing teeth, you invest in a durable replacement option that remains with you for life. While you can find replacement options like dentures and dental bridges at low prices, they need replacements every five to ten years, unlike dental implants, which stay in your mouth for life with proper dental hygiene. All you need to do is brush and floss the replacement teeth like your natural ones and get six monthly exams and cleanings from your dentist to provide excellent care to the replacement teeth.

Prevent Jawbone Resorption

Bone loss starts occurring soon after missing a tooth. This is because your jawbone does not receive stimulation from the biting and chewing forces and starts deteriorating. However, if you have replacement teeth with dental implants, your jawbone is inspired to ensure it does not resorb. Unfortunately, alternative options like dentures and bridges sitting on your gums do not support your jawbone, making your face sag and appear older.
