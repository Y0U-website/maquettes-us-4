Dental Implants in Sumter, SC

Are you looking for a permanent tooth loss solution? Restorative Arts Dental recommends dental implants. Dental implants are small titanium screws that support dental restorations, including crowns , dentures, and bridges . Implants stimulate the jawbone, thus preventing bone loss. In addition, they offer excellent stability, are low maintenance, and can resolve partial and complete tooth loss.

Types of Implants

Dental implants in Sumter, SC are broadly grouped into three categories: endosteal, subperiosteal, and zygomatic implants.

Endosteal implants are the most common. These are implanted into the jawbone. Subperiosteal implants rest over the bone but under the gums. Finally, zygomatic implants are the rarest of the three and are embedded in the cheekbone rather than the jawbone.

Dental Implants Treatment Process

Before recommending dental implants, our dentists in Sumter, SC thoroughly assess your dental health to determine your suitability for this restorative treatment. You require sufficient bone and healthy gums to undergo dental implant surgery. If you’ve experienced bone loss or gum recession, our dentists near you may recommend prerequisite treatments such as bone grafting and gum contouring.

To perform implant surgery, our dentists begin by sedating the patient. Next, the dentist accesses the bone through an incision in the gums. Then, using a dental drill, the dentist makes holes at predetermined points of your jawbone. Finally, the dentist installs the implants and then sutures your gum incision.

Over several months, the artificial tooth roots fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. After complete healing, the dentist places the abutment or connector posts and then attaches the dental restorations of your choice. The entire treatment process may last up to six months or more.

All-On-4® Treatment

If you’ve experienced complete tooth loss, our dentist may opt for All-On-4® treatment as an alternative to replacing every missing tooth with a single implant. All-On-4 treatment utilizes four implants to support an entire dental arch.

All-On-4 treatment enables individuals who have lost all their teeth to replace their smiles faster and conveniently without the need for multiple surgeries.
