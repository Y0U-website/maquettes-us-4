Invisalign

Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that offers a discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional braces. By using a series of custom-made, clear aligners, Invisalign gradually shifts your teeth into their correct position. The aligners are virtually invisible, allowing for a more aesthetic treatment experience. Moreover, they are removable, which means you can eat, drink, brush, and floss normally. With Invisalign, you can achieve a beautiful, straight smile without the inconvenience and discomfort of metal braces.

Retainers

A retainer is an essential orthodontic device often used after braces to keep teeth in their new, corrected position. Retainers, usually made of plastic and metal, are custom-made to fit each patient&#x27;s mouth. They hold teeth in place to prevent them from shifting back to their original positions, and maintaining the results of orthodontic treatment.

Orthodontics

Space Maintainers

Space maintainers are dental devices custom-made by orthodontists like Dr. Victoria Hutta. They are usually made of metal and are designed to hold the space where a primary tooth has fallen out too early, preventing the other teeth from moving into that space. This is crucial because if the other teeth shift positions, they can block the permanent tooth from coming in correctly, leading to a crooked or crowded smile.
