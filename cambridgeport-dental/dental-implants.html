Replace missing or damaged teeth with dental implants near Cambridge, MA

At Cambridgeport Dental , we specialize in dental implant placement and restoration to help patients regain full function and a natural-looking smile. Whether you need to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth, our skilled team uses advanced digital imaging and precise surgical techniques to ensure long-lasting, beautiful results.

Implants not only replace missing teeth but also help preserve jawbone health, improve chewing ability and enhance facial balance — making them a lasting investment in your oral wellness. We also offer bone grafting when needed to support implant stability.

Process

Initial consultation: We begin with a free comprehensive evaluation, including X-rays and a discussion of your goals to design a customized treatment plan. Implant placement: A titanium post is gently placed into the jawbone to serve as a secure foundation for your new tooth. Healing & integration: Over several months, the implant naturally fuses with the bone, ensuring lasting strength and stability. Custom restoration: A custom-made crown, bridge or denture is attached to complete your smile.

Next Steps

Follow-up care: We’ll schedule visits to monitor healing and ensure that the implant integrates properly. Maintenance: Our team provides guidance on brushing, flossing, and ongoing care to help your implant last a lifetime.

FAQ

  • How many dental implants can you have? You can have one implant for a single tooth or multiple implants to support several teeth or full-arch restorations — even an entire set of upper or lower teeth.
  • How long do dental implants take to fully heal? Full healing and implant integration typically take three to six months, depending on bone quality and overall health.
