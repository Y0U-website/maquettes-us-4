Regardless of your needs, our team of skilled, experienced, and compassionate dental professionals can assist you in keeping your smile healthy while reducing the risk of conditions such as cavities and diseases that can significantly impact the smile.

We offer special amenities, including televisions in each treatment room, neck pillows…

Patients who have specific needs or requests can also consult with Dr. Caton-Hospedales and her team about general, restorative, and cosmetic treatments. With customized dental services and quality care, patients can feel confident in the beauty and health of their smiles for many years to come!

Dr. Caton-Hospedales is originally from Trinidad and Tobago. She moved to New York as a teenager, where she attended Hunter College and earned her degree in Microbiology and Lab sciences before dental school at New York University. She eventually moved to Georgia and purchased Your Smile Dental Wellness Center.

Dentistry is a great fit for Dr. Caton-Hospedales, and she enjoys helping patients who are very apprehensive about dentistry but leave relaxed and with a smile!
