Advantages of dental implants

Dental implants offer several advantages over dentures or bridges:

While some consider dental implants expensive initially, they prove to be the most cost-effective solution for missing teeth over time. Unlike dentures that require multiple replacements, dental implants can instill confidence and lasting results with proper care (hygiene).

  • Implants provide the best type of restoration for missing teeth.
  • They prevent bone loss, restore functionality, and offer aesthetic appeal.
  • Eating becomes pain-free and enjoyable.
  • With dental implants, you can avoid the inconvenience of using retainers.
  • Dental implants spare you the discomfort associated with denture wear, such as gum soreness, loose dentures, and diminished taste sensation.

All-on-6

All-on-6 dental implants present a less invasive and swifter method to restore a full upper or lower set of teeth compared to conventional dental implants. With just six implants per jaw, temporary teeth can often be placed immediately, followed by a permanent set within a few months of full implant integration with the surrounding bone.

Implant-Supported Fixed Bridges

In contrast to traditional fixed bridges, implant-supported bridges preserve the integrity of adjacent teeth and halt the natural bone resorption that occurs when teeth are lost. By maintaining bone in the edentulous areas (where teeth are missing), normal facial contours are retained, avoiding a sunken appearance. Permanently anchored in the jawbone, implant-supported bridges fully restore natural biting and chewing capabilities.

Implant-Supported Overdentures

For individuals missing all teeth in the upper or lower jaw, dental implants offer comfortable, stable, and aesthetically pleasing solutions to restore a functional smile.
