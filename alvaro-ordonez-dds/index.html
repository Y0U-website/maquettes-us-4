The practice

Leveraging cutting-edge technology and boasting more than three decades of practical experience, Dr. Alvaro Ordonez stands as a respected dental professional and international key opinion leader serving the South Florida community.

At our clinic, we extend a range of comprehensive services encompassing both general and aesthetic dentistry, implant procedures, sedation solutions, interventions for snoring and sleep apnea, as well as targeted TMJ therapy and orofacial pain management.

Our practice’s guiding principle is to deliver the comprehensive dental care you envision, without compromising the resilience, functionality and durability of your dental interventions.

Our approach is characterized by comprehensive and gentle methodologies. We don’t merely treat teeth; we prioritize the well-being of each individual we care for!

Dr Alvaro Ordonez is one of the few dentists in the world trained in a full time university program in TMJ Disorders, dental occlusion, orofacial pain and sleep dentistry making him an elite “High Complexity Dentistry practitioner” and a national and international key opinion leader for dental societies, universitiesand biomedical companies around the world.

Dr Ordonez has developed multiple techniques for implantology, bone reconstruction and bone and tissue regeneration as well as instrumentation and equipments currently utilized in implant dentistry all over the world.

In addition to that he is a trained IV sedation dentist licensed by the state of Florida.

His dentistry is the perfect balance between form and function.
