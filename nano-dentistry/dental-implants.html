Dental Implants in Portland, ME

Missing teeth don’t just affect your smile. They impact your ability to eat, speak, and live your life to the fullest. At Nano Dentistry, we use guided implant technology and in-house milling to provide secure, natural-looking implants. Many patients are able to receive same-day tooth replacement, walking out with a stronger, healthier smile in one visit.

Why choose dental implants?

Dental implants offer unmatched stability, longevity, and aesthetics compared to other tooth replacement options. They restore natural function while preventing bone loss and changes to facial structure. With today’s advanced technology, implants are more precise and comfortable than ever.

Natural look and feel Implants blend seamlessly with your smile and feel just like natural teeth.

Long-term solution With proper care, implants can last for decades, making them a wise investment in your health.

Protect oral health Implants prevent bone loss and keep surrounding teeth from shifting.

Our Treatments

Single-tooth implants Replace a missing tooth with a lifelike implant and custom crown designed and milled in-house for a precise fit. With digital planning and guided placement, we protect surrounding teeth and bone while restoring strength, comfort, and natural aesthetics—often with a same-day temporary so you never leave with a gap.

Implant-supported bridges

When multiple teeth are missing in a row, an implant-supported bridge skips the need to crown healthy neighbors. We digitally design your restoration, place implants with a guided approach, and mill the bridge on site for a seamless bite and confident smile with fewer visits.

Implant-retained dentures

Say goodbye to slipping dentures. A few well-planned implants can secure a removable denture so it snaps into place, improves chewing, and feels more stable day to day. Our digital workflow streamlines the fit and lets us refine comfort and aesthetics quickly.

All-on-X

For full-arch rejuvenation, All-on-X places a small number of implants to support a fixed, full-arch bridge. We plan every step with 3D imaging and guided surgery, then design and mill your provisional in-house so you can enjoy a stable, same-day smile while your implants heal.

The dental implant process

Call us today to learn more about dental implants or to schedule a consultation with Dr. Nano.

Step 1

Comprehensive consultation and 3D imaging to assess bone health and create a treatment plan.

Step 2

Guided surgical placement of the implant post using advanced digital technology.

Step 3

Healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone, sometimes with a temporary crown for function and appearance.

Step 4

Placement of the final custom crown, bridge, or denture for a fully restored smile.

Frequently Asked Questions Frequently asked questions about dental implants

Are implants safe?

Yes. Dental implants are a proven, safe solution with high success rates when placed by experienced dentists.

How long does the process take?

Some patients qualify for same-day implants, while others may need several months for healing between steps.

Will implants look natural?

Absolutely. Your restoration is custom-designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth.

Are implants painful?

With guided technology and modern anesthesia, most patients report minimal discomfort.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most healthy adults are candidates, though bone health will be evaluated during your consultation.
