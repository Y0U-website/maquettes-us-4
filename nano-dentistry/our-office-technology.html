Our Office & Technology

At Nano Dentistry, every detail of our office and technology is designed to give patients advanced care with comfort, precision, and efficiency.

A modern office built for your comfort and care

From the moment you arrive at our Westbrook office, you’ll notice the difference. We believe dental care should be as stress-free and seamless as possible, which is why we’ve combined a welcoming, modern environment with some of the most advanced technology in Maine. Whether you’re here for a routine visit or a life-changing restoration, our approach makes dentistry faster, more precise, and more comfortable for every patient.

Transform Your Smile with our Advanced Technology

CEREC same-day crowns

With CEREC technology, we can design and place crowns and bridges in a single appointment, eliminating the need for temporary restorations or multiple visits.

In-house milling & 3D printing

Our professional milling machines and 3D printers allow us to create surgical guides, oral appliances, crowns, bridges, and more on-site, so you leave with same-day solutions

Guided implant surgery

Using 3D imaging and 3D printed surgical guides, Dr. Nano places implants with precision and confidence, helping patients achieve reliable, long-lasting results.

AI-driven digital X-rays

AI-enhanced imaging gives Dr. Nano a clearer, more accurate picture of your oral health, allowing us to detect issues earlier and create more effective treatment plans.

Your first appointment at Nano Dentistry is all about getting to know you and your smile. We’ll begin with a comprehensive exam, advanced imaging, and a professional cleaning if scheduled. Dr. Nano will review your results with you and discuss any recommendations, making sure you leave with a clear plan for maintaining or improving your oral health.
