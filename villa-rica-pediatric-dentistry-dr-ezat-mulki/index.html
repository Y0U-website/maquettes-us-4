We cannot wait to welcome you and your children into our dental family! We would like to invite you to schedule an appointment at our office. Please feel free to call our office during normal business hours or fill out the form below.

There are many reasons to choose Villa Rica Pediatric Dentistry as your child’s pediatric dentist. Below are just a few:

Children’s Dental Specialists. We are a pediatric dental practice specializing in the treatment of children. We only treat children, and Dr. Mulki has had several years of specialized training to be more qualified to meet your child’s unique dental needs.

Comprehensive Children’s Dentistry. Our warm, caring and attentive dental team provides a wide range of children’s dental services to ensure that your child develops and maintains beautiful, healthy teeth. With proper and consistent dental treatment, not only do we help prevent cavities in the future, we teach your child good habits that will last them a lifetime!

One-doctor practice. Tired of big practices where it seems like you see a different doctor each time you visit? At our practice, all of our patients see Dr. Mulki, so that he can build a trusting and personal relationship with his patients and observe the change in the developing dentition.

Parents welcome to accompany their children through treatment. While some pediatric dental offices restrict parents from coming with their children to the treatment rooms, we encourage it. We know that we need to earn your trust, and we want you to see how well we treat your child.

Special Needs Dentist in Villa Rica . Our office has a specially designed dark room that can provide a soothing environment for children with autism, light triggered seizures and other medical conditions with sensory issues.
