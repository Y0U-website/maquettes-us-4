BENEFITS OF DENTAL IMPLANTS VS. ALTERNATIVES

Dental implants offer several advantages over alternatives like dentures and bridges. One of the most significant benefits is that they help preserve jawbone density, unlike dentures and bridges, which do not. Implants are also stable and secure, providing a firm fit that doesn’t shift, unlike dentures that may move around. They feel like natural teeth, giving a more comfortable and realistic experience compared to the less natural feel of dentures and the somewhat realistic feel of bridges.

Additionally, implants do not affect neighboring teeth, whereas bridges may require the alteration of adjacent healthy teeth. In terms of longevity, dental implants typically last over 20 years, far outlasting dentures (5 7 years) and bridges (10 15 years), making them a durable and long-term solution for tooth replacement.

DENTAL IMPLANTS FOR SENIORS

Many people assume they’re too old for implants but that couldn’t be further from the truth. As long as you’re healthy enough for routine dental procedures, you’re likely a candidate. Seniors especially benefit from the stability implants offer after years of using removable dentures. Implants provide renewed comfort, confidence, and functionality allowing seniors to eat, speak, and smile without worry.

DENTAL IMPLANTS Frequently Asked Questions

  • How Long Do Implants Really Last? Most dental implants last over 20 years and with proper care, they can even last a lifetime. While the implant post (which fuses with your jawbone) is incredibly durable, the crown attached to it may need to be replaced every 10 15 years due to normal wear. Keeping up with regular dental checkups and good hygiene can help your implant last as long as possible.
  • Does Getting an Implant Hurt? The procedure itself is done under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during the surgery. Most patients say the experience is much easier than expected often less painful than a tooth extraction. Mild soreness afterward is normal and usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • Can I Get an Implant Immediately After Tooth Removal? In many cases, yes! This is called immediate placement. It depends on the condition of your bone and gum tissue. If the area is healthy and infection-free, we can often place the implant the same day the tooth is removed. We’ll evaluate your eligibility during your consultation
  • What if I Don’t Have Enough Bone? Not a problem we offer bone grafting procedures to rebuild areas with bone loss. This process helps create a strong foundation for your implant. While it may extend the treatment timeline, it’s a crucial step in ensuring long-term implant success and stability.
  • How Do I Clean Around Implants? Dental implants need care just like natural teeth. You’ll brush and floss daily, and we may recommend tools like a water flosser or interdental brushes specifically designed for implants. Our team will guide you on the best techniques to keep the area clean and your implant healthy.
  • Can Implants Be Used With Dentures? Absolutely! Implant-retained dentures snap on to implants, offering superior stability compared to traditional removable dentures. They don’t require adhesives, won’t shift while talking or eating, and provide a much more natural feel and bite.
  • What Are Implants Made Of? Most dental implants are made of titanium a strong, lightweight, and biocompatible metal that integrates well with the jawbone. For patients with metal sensitivities or who prefer a metal-free option, we also offer zirconia implants, which are durable and aesthetically pleasing.
  • Is There an Age Limit? Not at all! Patients in their 60s, 70s, and even 80s can be great candidates for dental implants. Age isn’t as important as your overall health and bone condition. If you’re healthy enough for routine dental work, chances are you can receive implants, too.
