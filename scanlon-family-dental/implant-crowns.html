Our Collaborative Approach to Implant Restorations

  • Consultation and Planning: We evaluate your existing implant or discuss your treatment goals and refer you to a trusted specialist for placement if needed.
  • Digital Imaging and Impressions: Using advanced digital X-rays and precision impressions, we gather detailed information to design your crown.
  • Custom Crown Fabrication: Your restoration is created from high-quality materials that replicate the translucency and strength of natural enamel.
  • Crown Placement: Once ready, we attach the custom crown securely to your implant abutment, making final adjustments for comfort, function, and aesthetics.

Types of Implant Crowns

Depending on your needs, your dentist will recommend one of two primary types of implant crowns:

  • Cement-Retained Crowns: These crowns are permanently cemented onto the abutment that connects to your implant. They provide a clean, natural look and are ideal for single-tooth replacements in aesthetic areas.
  • Screw-Retained Crowns: These crowns are attached with a small, hidden screw for easier future removal or maintenance. They’re often used in areas where accessibility is important.

Caring for Your Implant Crown

  • Brush twice daily and floss at least once a day
  • Use non-abrasive toothpaste to prevent surface wear
  • Visit us for regular cleanings and checkups every six months
  • Avoid using your teeth to open packages or bite hard objects
