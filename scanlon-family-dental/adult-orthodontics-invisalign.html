What Is Invisalign?

Unlike metal braces, Invisalign aligners are:

  • Virtually invisible
  • Smooth and comfortable to wear
  • Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing
  • Free from wires, brackets, and tightening appointments

How Invisalign Works

  • Consultation & Evaluation: We’ll discuss your goals, examine your bite, and determine if Invisalign is right for you.
  • Digital Imaging: Detailed scans are used to create a 3D model of your mouth and map out your tooth movement sequence.
  • Custom Aligner Fabrication: Invisalign uses your model to create a series of precise, medical-grade plastic aligners.
  • Wearing Your Aligners: You’ll wear each set for 20–22 hours per day, removing them only for meals and brushing.
  • Progress Check-ins: You’ll visit our office periodically so we can track your progress and make adjustments as needed.

Advantages of Invisalign for Adults

Many patients appreciate that Invisalign fits seamlessly into their professional and social lives allowing them to maintain confidence throughout the process.

  • Discreet appearance: The clear aligners are virtually invisible to others.
  • Comfort: Smooth plastic aligners eliminate irritation from metal brackets or wires.
  • Convenience: Aligners can be removed for meals, photos, and important events.
  • Easier oral hygiene: Simply remove the aligners to brush and floss normally.
  • Predictable results: Advanced digital planning ensures precise, controlled movement.

Is Invisalign Right for You?

Invisalign works best for mild to moderate alignment issues. Ideal candidates are adults who:

  • Have healthy gums and bone support
  • Are committed to wearing aligners as directed
  • Want a comfortable, discreet treatment option
  • Prefer to avoid the dietary restrictions of traditional braces
