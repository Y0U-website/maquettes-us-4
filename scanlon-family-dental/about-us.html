About

Our goal is to provide exceptional treatment and personalized maintenance plans to help you achieve healthy teeth and gums — so you can smile with confidence.

Our core values are built on honesty, professionalism, kind hearts, and positive attitudes. Our mission is to deliver high-quality dental care while advocating for our patients every step of the way!

Our Mission Statement

Now in her 15th year of practice, Dr. Cory is honored to serve as the owner of Scanlon Family Dental and is proud to carry forward the legacy of the great dentists that have served our patients for over 50 years. It is easy to see that she is deeply committed to providing her patients with honest, high-quality dental care delivered with compassion, while fostering a positive, respectful, and enthusiastic environment for her team.

Dr. Cory is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Missouri Dental Association, and the Greater St. Louis Dental Society.

Outside of the office, Dr. Cory enjoys cooking, running, staying active, and participating in local theatre, both as an attendee and performer. She loves exploring the city of St. Louis and is a devoted sports fan, proudly cheering on the St. Louis Cardinals, St. Louis Blues, and the Missouri Tigers. Her greatest joy is her family: her husband, Ryan, and their three children.

And, if you’re wondering, it is pronounced “Glow-CHESS-key.” 😊

Kimberly Leigh Pappas, DDS

Known around the office as “Dr. K-Leigh,” Dr. Kimberly Leigh Pappas is proud to join the team at Scanlon Family Dental, where her warm, relationship-first approach fits naturally with the practice’s long-standing tradition of caring for patients like family.

Dr. K-Leigh earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry. Originally from Defiance, Missouri, she is excited to begin her dental career serving the community she calls “Home.”

She was drawn to dentistry because it brings together two things she values deeply: healthcare and human connection. For Dr. K-Leigh, dentistry is about more than caring for teeth. It is about helping patients feel comfortable, confident, heard, and well cared for throughout their visit.

Dr. K-Leigh is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Missouri Dental Association, and the Greater St. Louis Dental Society. She is committed to continuing education and to providing thoughtful, high-quality care that patients can trust.

Outside the office, Dr. K-Leigh enjoys trying new restaurants, traveling, listening to music, playing games, and spending time with family and friends.

Robert J. Wheeler, D.M.D.

I was born in Idaho and grew up in Salt Lake City, Utah. I graduated from the University of Utah and then moved to St. Louis to attend Washington University Dental School. After graduating, I served as a Dentist in the Air Force at Offutt Air Force Base in Omaha, Nebraska.

Upon discharge from the military, I returned to the St. Louis area working in Ferguson and Florissant. In 2010, I moved my practice to St. Charles merging with Dr. Ladue and have practiced in the area ever since. I’m a father of five children and a grandfather of 18.

I’m so excited to be merging with Dr. Cory Glowczewski and Scanlon Family Dental, so my patients and staff can be handled by honest and ethical hands.
