At Floss Dental Pearland, we provide general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry in Pearland to keep your smile healthy and confident. Our same-day dental service ensures timely care when you need it most, with comfort and quality at every visit.

OUR SERVICES

General and Preventive Dentistry

Our general dentistry in Pearland emphasizes preventive care that supports lifelong oral health. Routine exams and professional cleanings allow us to identify concerns early, while preventive treatments provide protection against common issues such as decay and gum disease. This approach helps patients maintain strong, healthy smiles with confidence.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Our cosmetic dentistry in Pearland is designed for patients who want to improve the appearance of their smiles in ways that also support overall health. From whitening to veneers, each treatment is carefully selected to address discoloration, chips, or uneven spacing while maintaining the natural look and function of your teeth.

Restorative Dentistry

Our restorative dentistry in Pearland focuses on repairing and rebuilding teeth affected by decay, injury, or everyday wear. After a thorough evaluation, we may recommend treatments such as crowns, bridges, dentures, or implants to restore strength, function, and comfort. The goal is to provide solutions that not only improve your ability to eat and speak but also protect the long-term health and stability of your smile.

Emergency Dental Services

Dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly, and timely care makes all the difference. Our same-day dental service allows us to quickly address concerns such as severe pain, broken teeth, or swelling. By providing prompt attention, we not only relieve discomfort but also reduce the risk of further complications.

Orthodontics

Orthodontic care improves both alignment and bite function, creating benefits that last well beyond appearance. We offer options for children, teens, and adults, ensuring that treatment plans are adapted to each stage of life. Correcting misalignment supports healthier function and contributes to overall dental stability.

Oral Surgery and Extractions

When oral surgery or extractions are necessary, our focus is on delivering safe and supportive care. Whether addressing impacted teeth or other surgical needs, we carefully plan each procedure to minimize discomfort and encourage healthy healing. Clear communication ensures patients feel prepared and cared for throughout the process.
