The practice

Give your family reliable general and cosmetic dentistry services with help from Dr. Kayla Torres in Pawtucket, Rhode Island . Our clinic provides everything you and your family’s teeth need to be healthy and white.

While practicing, he has served as President of the Pawtucket Dental Society and was School Dentist for the East Providence School District for 20 years. He has maintained his affiliation with Tufts Dental as Assistant Clinical Professor in the Restorative Dentistry Department for 25 years. His interests include Running, travelling and his Bulldogs, Gisele & Bentley.

Dr. Kayla Torres was born in Pawtucket, RI and raised in Attleboro Massachusetts. After becoming a board certified dental assistant and working in private practice for 7 years, she went on to graduate from UMASS Dartmouth with a bachelor’s in Biology. She graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and began practicing in Warwick, RI and Bellingham, MA.

While at Tufts she served as a teachers aid in the general dentistry clinic, as well as the radiology clinic.

She enjoys traveling, baking and her 2 dogs Rocky and Bia and her 2 cats Nicki and Cardi.
