Dr. Carlos Dorantes, DDS, MS

Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Carlos Dorantes received his dental degree from Francisco Marroquin University in Guatemala and practiced in his hometown for three years, focusing mainly on children’s dentistry. In 1999, Dr. Dorantes moved to Birmingham and began his specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at the University of Alabama Dental School (UAB). UAB’s Pediatric Dental Residency is a challenging 3-year dental school and hospital-based program; this gave Dr. Dorantes the unique opportunity to practice at the world-renowned Children’s Hospital of Alabama.

When he was not busy with patients or academic work, Dr. Dorantes worked as a part-time medical interpreter at Children’s Hospital. This experience shaped him for years to come, and that strengthened his determination to always work with the underserved. Dr. Dorantes successfully completed his Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry in June of 2002 and was invited to stay as a Fellow Faculty member for the Pediatric Dentistry Department at UAB.

Doctor Dorantes lives in Yakima with his wife Mei and their children, Benjamin and Camila. He enjoys teaching, hiking, and cooking for friends and family. He is also a published author in a peer-reviewed journal and is committed to education, prevention of dental disease, and access to care for children. As an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, he also enjoys attending meetings that enhance his training and introduce him to newer techniques.
