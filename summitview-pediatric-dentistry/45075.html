Dr. Mary Newman, MD

Anesthesiologist

Dr. Mary Newman, MD, is a board-certified, American-trained anesthesiologist with more than 35 years of experience as a physician. She has been a specialist in pediatric dental anesthesia for 9 years. Dr. Newman started her career as a board-certified pediatrician and then trained as an anesthesiologist in a second residency. This combination makes her particularly well qualified to care for pediatric patients.

Dr. Newman’s work experience includes general anesthesia practice at a large tertiary care hospital in Kentucky, ambulatory anesthesia practice at a surgery center in Yakima, and office-based anesthesia practice in Washington. She also served as medical director for Yakima Ambulatory Surgery Center in Yakima for 15 years.

Dr. Newman graduated from Loyola Stritch School of Medicine in Maywood, Illinois, in 1990. She completed her pediatric residency at the University of Texas Southwest in Dallas in 1993. Her anesthesia residency was completed at Vanderbilt University in Nashville in 1997.
