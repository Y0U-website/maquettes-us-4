The Importance of Interceptive Orthodontics

When your child still has some of their baby teeth, it might not seem necessary to discuss orthodontic treatment, so let’s look at how interceptive orthodontics can benefit your child’s overall well-being and oral health:

  • Minimizes treatment With early correction, your child could avoid extensive orthodontic treatment when they get older. This can save them stress and save you money.
  • Guides jaw growth Monitoring and correcting your child’s jaw development can fix skeletal issues and balance their facial structure, enhancing their appearance.
  • Creates needed space Interceptive orthodontics can eliminate your child’s severe tooth crowding; a palatal expander can widen their arch and create space for permanent teeth.
  • Corrects harmful habits If your child sucks their thumb or thrusts their tongue, early interceptive treatment can address and correct any issues caused by these habits.
  • Improves function Once they get the treatment they need, your child can look forward to chewing properly, swallowing with ease, and speaking more clearly.
