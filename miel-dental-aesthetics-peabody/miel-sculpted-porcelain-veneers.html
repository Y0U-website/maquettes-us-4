Sculpted Prepless Veneers (Case by Case)

For select patients, Dr. Torres may recommend conservative Prepless Veneers , placed without enamel removal.

Prepless veneers are not a trend — they are a clinical decision based on anatomy, spacing, and bite. They are only recommended if they enhance facial harmony and longevity.
