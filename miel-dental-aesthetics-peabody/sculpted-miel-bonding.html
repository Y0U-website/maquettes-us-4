What Makes It “Sculpted Miel™”?

1. Proportion & Facial Harmony

Each tooth is hand-designed to match your face shape, lip line, and smile dynamics. We aren’t “adding composite” — we are sculpting teeth .

3. Manual Artistic Layering

Just like painting light and shadow, Dr. Torres layers composite to mimic the natural opalescence, gloss, and texture of enamel.

The Sculpted Miel™ Process

1. Consultation & Smile Mapping

We evaluate proportions, symmetry, personality of the smile, bite function, and your aesthetic goals. No obligations — just clarity.

2. Shade & Shape Customization

We match composite to your natural enamel or design a brighter, more youthful shade depending on your goals.

3. Hand-Sculpting & Layering Dr. Torres builds each tooth with millimeter-level precision. No drilling. No pain. No anesthesia needed for most cases.

4. Gloss, Shine & Texture Polish

We finish with high-luster polishers to create a mirror-smooth, enamel-like surface that resists staining and captures light naturally.

5. Photo Review & Refinement

We review before-and-after images together and fine-tune details until everything is perfectly you .

How Long Does Sculpted Bonding Last?

Longevity depends on: • bite alignment • grinding/clenching • diet • oral hygiene • maintenance polish visits

Bonding is repairable and reversible — making it a flexible, accessible cosmetic option.

Sculpted Miel™ vs. Veneers

Bonding is perfect for subtle to moderate enhancements. Veneers are best for full transformations or whitening-resistant enamel.
