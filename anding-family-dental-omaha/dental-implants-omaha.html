Dental Implants

Following the implant placement procedure, patients continue to visit the coordinating specialist over the course of recovery to ensure proper healing. As soon as the implant has successfully recovered, patients return to Anding Family Dental for the attachment of their restoration. While the entire implant process is longer than traditional restoration options, the long-term benefits of dental implants provide patients with a permanent solution to tooth loss.

  • Fixed Bridges: Ideal for replacing several missing teeth
  • All-on-Four Implants: Recommended for replacing entire arches of missing teeth
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: Removable full arch restorations
