0 Invisalign® in Norridge, IL A clear alternative to braces that straightens teeth and preserves dental health. What is Invisalign®? For patients who would like to avoid conventional braces all together, Dr. Hagerty in Norridge, IL provides an alternative solution with Invisalign clear aligners. This form of orthodontic treatment consists of a series of virtually invisible aligners that slowly and safely move your teeth.

What are the benefits of Invisalign®?

A properly aligned smile not only increases appeal but helps protect long term dental well-being. Orthodontic issues such as gaps or crooked teeth can affect your health, even if these problems seem subtle. No matter how minor the complication may appear, it is recommended to take a proactive approach and seek professional care. If alignment complications are affecting your quality of life such as making speaking and eating difficult, Invisalign can also help.

At your first visit, we will collect a panoramic x-ray, intraoral digital scans, and digital photos. These will be used by Dr. Hagerty to develop a personalized treatment plan. Once the final position of the teeth has been determined digitally, the series of clear aligners, or trays, are fabricated and arrive in our office usually within one month. Each tray is shaped slightly different and patients graduate throughout treatment, switching the trays every one to two weeks until the desired results are achieved.

How Invisalign® works: your treatment process

The trays must be worn 22 hours a day but can be removed during eating and your at-home oral hygiene routine. Dr. Hagerty strategically tracks your alignment progress during appointments, making sure you are compliant with wearing your trays. Once finished, retainers will be fabricated to maintain the new position of your teeth.

How long does Invisalign® take? Because no two plans are alike, treatment length varies. Most of our patients are able to achieve the desired results within 12 to 18 months. Invisalign success is most predictable with optimal patient compliance. The more diligently the at home instructions are followed, the shorter your personalized treatment will be!
