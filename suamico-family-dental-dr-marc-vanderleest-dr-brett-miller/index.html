The practice

We offer a variety of night guards to help patients who grind their teeth or are experiencing possible TMJ disorders/symptoms.

Hi, my name is Dr. Brett Miller and I’m grateful to have the opportunity to practice Dentistry in my hometown of Suamico. After graduating from Bay Port High School, I completed my undergraduate studies at UW-Milwaukee graduating with a degree in Cell and Molecular Biology. I then attended the Marquette University School of Dentistry and graduated in 2022 earning my Doctor of Dental Surgery…

Growing up in Suamico, Dr. Marc was my family dentist. It has been a rewarding experience to come back to my hometown to work with the staff and Dr. Marc, who I had come to know so well.

My wife and I are both from this area, we met at Bay Port and have been together since. We are very excited to now be putting down our own roots in Green Bay. We have two sons who keep us very busy and we love every bit of it!

Outside of the office my wife and I like to travel, attend Packer and Brewers games and spend time with our family and friends. I look forward to meeting more patients from the Green Bay area and establishing long lasting relationships with them and their families.
