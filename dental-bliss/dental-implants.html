Types of Dental Implants We Offer

At Dental Bliss, we prioritize your comfort and care every step of the way. Here’s how our dental implant process works:

We take every measure to ensure the process is smooth, efficient, and stress-free, delivering results that exceed your expectations.

  • Consultation and Custom Plan: Your implant journey begins with a consultation. We assess your oral health, take 3D scans, and discuss the best implant solution for your needs. We’ll also explain the procedure thoroughly so you know what to expect.
  • Surgical Placement of Implants: Using advanced dental technology, we surgically place titanium posts into your jawbone. To ensure your procedure is as comfortable as possible, we work closely with licensed dental anesthesiologists for quality anesthesia and sedation.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Over the next few months, your implants will naturally fuse with your jawbone in osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for the replacement tooth or teeth.
  • Crown Placement: Once the implants are fully integrated, we’ll attach custom-made crowns or dentures designed to match your natural teeth in shape, color, and function.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Proper aftercare is essential to maximizing the lifespan of your dental implants. Here are some tips to ensure your new smile stays healthy and radiant:

Dr. Eliza Gilbert, Dr. Byron Bush , and our team will provide comprehensive aftercare instructions and remain available to address any concerns or questions as you adjust to your new implants.

  • Practice regular oral hygiene, including brushing twice a day and flossing daily.
  • Rinse with an antibacterial mouthwash to keep your gums healthy.
  • Schedule routine check-ups and professional cleanings at Dental Bliss.
  • Avoid nail-biting or chewing ice, as these can damage your implants.
  • Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste to protect the implant surfaces.
