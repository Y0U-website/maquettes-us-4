Meet Dr. Tyler Garrett

Dr. Tyler Garrett and his family are so excited to be living in Flagstaff, AZ. Dr. Garrett and his wonderful wife, Whitney, have three great kids; Sage, Cole, and Branch. Dr. Garrett graduated from the Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine in Glendale, AZ in 2013.

Upon earning his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree, he began an 8-year career in the United States Air Force reaching the rank of Major. During his years in the US Air Force, Dr. Garrett and his family were stationed in Georgia, Hawaii, and in North Dakota where he served as the 5th Operational Medicine Readiness Squadron’s Dental Flight Commander.

Dr. Garrett has continued his training by attending multiple advanced dental education classes. These include courses and hands on training in oral surgery, same-day digital dentistry, and TMJ/Jaw disorders.

Dr. Garrett believes in giving back to the community. He has participated in multiple community dental events and humanitarian trips. He has been able to provide these services in downtown Phoenix, Tijuana, Mexico, and on the Pacific island of Vanuatu.
