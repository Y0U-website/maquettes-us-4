We’re Excited To Introduce Your New Flagstaff Dentist Dr Tyler Garrett

Please help me in welcoming Dr. Tyler Garrett to Oasis Dental Care. Dr. Garrett graduated from Midwestern University in Glendale, AZ in 2013. He has spent the past 8 years practicing dentistry in the United States Air Force.

My wife Maria has also pursued advanced education and a professional degree. She was a licensed and practicing attorney in New York prior to our move to Arizona. We have decided to take advantage of opportunities available to her professionally. Unfortunately, that means leaving beautiful and charming Flagstaff.

We have a very exceptional practice here. A beautiful new location, and truly wonderful people that we work with and provide care for every day. It is for these reasons that we have had a lot of choices in who would be taking over in the care of the office, patients, and staff. Dr. Tyler Garrett was an easy choice for us. He shares our same values in quality and ethical care. We know that you will be in good hands with him.

I would like to take this opportunity to thank you for putting your trust in me as your dentist for these past few years. It has been very rewarding getting to know you all. It has also been very exciting building out a modern facility, with the latest in dental technology, comforts, and conveniences. We have also trained and developed a strong and committed team, dedicated to excellent patient care.

During the transition, I will be around to ensure that things go smoothly, and everyone receives the support they need to succeed. The team that you have come to know, and trust will all be here for you.

Thank you again for your loyalty and confidence.

A Message From Dr Garrett

I am incredibly excited to join the Oasis Dental Care team and get to know all of you. As Dr. Rodda mentioned, I attended Midwestern University in nearby Glendale and always loved getting up to Flagstaff during that time.

My family and I are so happy to be moving to Flagstaff. I have been married to my beautiful wife, Whitney, for the past 14 years. We have three children ages 10, 6, and 3. They are wonderful kids and are thrilled to be here. We love being outdoors in the mountains and pines. Whether that is camping, hiking, or driving around to see new places, we are so excited to be a part of this community.

We continued our Air Force journey from 2017-2020 with the amazing experience of living on the island of Oahu in Hawaii. Serving in the Air Force and caring for the Airmen who serve and protect our country each day was a great honor. It provided me many amazing opportunities that I will always be grateful for.

Now I am here and thrilled to be joining the Oasis Dental Care family. Dr. Rodda and the team have done a fantastic job establishing a great reputation of caring for patients and providing high-quality care. I sincerely look forward to meeting each of you and continuing the legacy that has preceded me.
