The practice

Located in Sherman Oaks, CA, we are your local, comprehensive dental solution for the entire family. Sherman Oaks Dental can help you begin your journey to a healthy, beautiful smile.

Our convenient location in Sherman Oaks, California, means we are just a short drive from the surrounding communities of Encino, Studio City and Van Nuys. We are your local family friendly dental office and treat everything from your family’s checkups and orthodontic needs to cosmetic dentistry and natural-looking implants.

We aim to provide patients with a great experience and value by being honest and gentle while placing an importance on education. We teach our patients how to maintain their oral health while stressing importance of not only total body health and wellness, but the healthy mouth-healthy body connection.

Dental Implants Dental implants, bridges & dentures replace your missing teeth. Dr. Fotovat brings his expertise to direct your tooth replacement at every stage.

Orthodontics Orthodontics can be tailored for nearly any age. Dr. Balabegian & Dr. Fotovat in Sherman Oaks, CA provide the best treatment path with a perfect finished smile.

Dr. Fotovat graduated from New York University College of Dentistry in 2010 and completed a general practice residency at St. Francis Hospital and Medical Center. In 2011, he won the E. Gerard Keen, DDS Award for oral and maxillofacial surgery.

Dr. Fotovat takes frequent continuing education courses throughout the year to stay up-to-date so he can provide his patients with the most modern care available.

When he isn’t caring for his patients, he enjoys spending time outdoors. He is an avid runner, cyclist, golfer and skier and loves traveling.
