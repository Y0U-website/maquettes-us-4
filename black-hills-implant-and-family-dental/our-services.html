Modern Family Dentistry

Black Hills Implant and Family Dental is a small, local, family-owned business. We offer high quality, patient-focused dental care. We take a holistic approach in serving our patients and see our patients as people and not simply a combination of dental issues. We provide a complete range of dental services to help our patients maintain a healthy and beautiful smile.

Cleanings

Emergency Visits

We offer emergency dental services at our office. We do our best to schedule emergency dental appointments within 24 hours of calling. If you call our clinic after hours, please select option two to directly leave a voicemail with Dr. Hettinger. We will treat your emergency with care, attentiveness, and urgency.

Whitening

Everyone deserves to have confidence in their smile. We create personalized dental trays for optimal whitening potential and provide educational tips on how to teeth naturally. Call us today and ask about how to add extra dazzle to your smile.

Orthodontic Treatment

We offer Invisalign services to gradually straighten your teeth. The Invisalign process includes wearing a series of aligners that are clear, plastic, and removable. This option provides you with the ability to straighten your smile without the restrictions of metal braces.

Dentures

Our dentures are custom made with digital scanning technology and are 3D printed to provide a realistic and personalized look with practicality. We offer both partial and complete dentures.

Implants

We offer implant services to fit your unique personal profile. We plan each implant case individually, providing you with a predictable, cost-effective treatment to replace missing teeth.

Crowns

Our state of the art crowns give you a functional and aesthetic option to protect a damaged tooth. We make same day temporary crowns and have a five day turn around for most permanent crown designs.

Fillings

Our dental restorations are composed of modern, tooth-colored material to effectively restore your tooth while providing a natural appearance.
