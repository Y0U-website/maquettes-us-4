A career built on access to care

Before opening Give Me A Smile, Dr. Chery spent years bringing dental care to families who needed it most.

  • Community health dentist Practiced as a general dentist at a Federally Qualified Health Center on Florida’s Gulf Coast before his pediatric residency.
  • Dental director Oversaw two offices and a school-based mobile dental-sealant program bringing preventive care directly to students.
  • Associate Professor of Pediatric Dentistry and Special Needs Mentored future dental professionals with an emphasis on inclusive, evidence-based care.

How Dr. Chery cares for kids

Dr. Chery focuses on creating positive dental experiences in a welcoming, family-friendly environment — emphasizing prevention, education, age-appropriate communication, personalized treatment, and building trust with children and their caregivers.

He provides additional support for children experiencing dental anxiety, sensory challenges, or special healthcare needs, and welcomes families in English, French, and Haitian Creole.
