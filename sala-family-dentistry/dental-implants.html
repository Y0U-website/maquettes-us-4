Restore Your Smile with a Strong, Natural Looking Solution

Dental implants in Reno, NV provide a long-lasting, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. If you want a replacement that feels secure, looks natural, and functions like your own teeth, implants are often the most reliable option.

Many patients worry about surgery, healing time, or cost. Dental implants are designed to be a durable, predictable solution that improves both your oral health and overall quality of life.

What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots placed in the jawbone to support a crown, bridge, or denture.

They are typically made of titanium and integrate with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for replacement teeth.

Because implants are anchored in the bone, they function more like natural teeth than removable options.

FAQs About Dental Implants

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, implants can last 20 years or more; many patients keep them for life.

Are Dental Implants Painful?

The procedure is done under anesthesia, so you shouldn’t feel pain during placement. Some soreness afterward is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

Am I a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone are good candidates. We’ll evaluate your bone density and overall health during a consultation to confirm.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

Cost depends on how many implants you need and any additional procedures required; we’ll walk you through pricing during a consultation.

Are Dental Implants Covered by Insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. Some insurance covers a portion, especially when the implant restores function rather than just appearance.

How Long Does the Dental Implant Process Take?

The full process, from placement to final crown, typically takes a few months to allow the implant to properly fuse with the jawbone.
