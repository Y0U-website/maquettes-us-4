​Each operatory has a television with Netflix, Hulu or Disney+! If there is something you would like to watch during your appointment, just let the hygienist or assistant know!

​Patient care is our top priority, and below are some of the things that we offer!

Nervous for your appointment? We do offer Nitrous Oxide gas in office for cleaning or restorative appointments. Please contact the front desk if you have additional questions regarding pricing or procedure.

Precision Dental prides itself on having a judgement free staff. If you are nervous, anxious or haven’t been to the dentist in awhile, we are here to help. We are here to answer your questions to the best of our ability, and hopefully guide each patient through their own treatment journey.

Patient care and comfort is extremely important to us. Each operatory is equipped with memory foam chairs to ensure that you are comfortable for each appointment.

Traffic wasn’t as bad as you thought? Arrived earlier than expected? We offer a Keurig with options like tea, hot chocolate and coffee for you to help yourself!!
