Micah L. Gray Snyder D.D.S.

After taking into account the way you bite and the appearance you desire for your new crown, the custom design is sent to our IN-OFFICE milling unit to create your permanent, all-ceramic, highly esthetic, and incredibly strong crown. You will have your beautiful new crown within hours, all in the same day! You can watch as your new crown is milled and then Dr. Snyder will add some additional characterization to make your new crown blend in with your other natural teeth. After a quick firing in the special oven, your permanent crown in ready to be bonded to your tooth!

Next Dr. Snyder will use the scanned image of your tooth to create your custom crown design right in front of your eyes!

A small wand is used to scan your tooth and create a 3-D model in the computer.

Cerec or Same-Day Crowns

-Should the situation arise that a crown (or sometimes called a cap) is needed to protect your tooth, we are able to take a Digital Impression and fabricate your new permanent crown in the office the same day! This means no messy impression material, no temporary crowns, and no second appointment!

Digital Radiographs

-Our Digital x-rays provide the highest level of clarity and detail while reducing your exposure by up to 95 % over conventional films.

Intra-Oral Photos

-A camera the size of a pen is used to take close up pictures of your tooth allowing you to see what Dr. Snyder sees.
