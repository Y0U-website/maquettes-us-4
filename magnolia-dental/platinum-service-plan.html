Platinum Service Plan

This plan is for you! We hope you will agree that it represents a better approach to managing your dental expenses and we invite your feedback as you use it and learn how it can help you.

As our world continues to change, we wanted to provide a means of making your dental expenses more predictable, and less costly. In-office plans such as our Platinum Service Plan are becoming increasingly popular with dental patients around the country.

Many of us are accustomed to insurance benefits covering the cost of our preventive care such as cleanings, exams and x-rays. While insurance pays a percentage of additional care, there are also annual maximums, waiting periods, and frequency of service limitations. With the Platinum Service Plan we offeer a plan that removes some of these limitations.

Why choose the Platinum Service Plan?

Who is this plan for?

No insurance? No problem.

This type of program has recently gained popularity in other dental offices and is ideal for our patients that don’t carry insurance through their employer or have lost coverage and are forced to buy individual coverage.

We are excited to share the details with you.

What is the Platinum Service Plan?

Platinum Service Plan Membership

Treatment decisions are made by you and the doctor, not an insurance company. Membership is not limited by your age. The plan covers more services than most dental insurance. All your plan information is kept in your secure electronic record.

Unlike conventional insurance plans, with the Platinum Service Plan there are:

  • NO ID cards
  • NO Member number to remember
  • NO Deductibles
  • NO Yearly maximums
  • NO Waiting periods to begin treatment
  • NO Insurance filing and waiting
  • NO Pre-authorization requirements
  • NO Benefit changes

TERMS & CONDITIONS of the Platinum Service Plan

  • Fees are due at the time of service to enjoy discounts.
  • This is a plan for Magnolia Dental only, therefore, if you are referred to a specialist, they will not offer these discounts.
  • The plan is nontransferable – family members cannot be substituted in for another family member.
  • This plan is for individual use only; no group benefits will apply. This plan cannot be combined with any other dental insurance. We will not submit any dental insurance claim while you are signed up with our plan.
  • Unused benefits do not rollover.
  • Rates for the Plan are subject to change annually.
  • Co-payment is due at the time of service for any treatment rendered. Cash, check, or credit card will be accepted. Additional service discounts do not apply if outside financing is utilized. E.G. Care Credit, Lending Club, etc.
  • When signing up additional family members, there will be a $ 50.00 discount applied to the plan for the referral.
  • Missed appointment fees are ineligible for the plan discount. A missed appointment fee of $35.00 per half hour of scheduled time will be charged for all missed dental appointments. To avoid this fee, please notify our office at least 24 hours in advance if you must change your reserved appointment.
