Comprehensive Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry

Advances in dental technology allow our team at Magnolia to restore and create bright, healthy, beautiful smiles through cosmetic & restorative dentistry in Parker, CO.

What a Cosmetic Dentist Can Do for Your Smile

Here at Magnolia Dental, we offer several cosmetic dentistry options that enhance and improve the surface appearance of an individual’s smile. Teeth Whitening is a popular cosmetic dentistry procedure that we offer at our Parker dental office. Teeth Whitening can be done alone or in combination with dental restorations, such as Veneers or Crowns . As an experienced cosmetic dentist, our goal is to help you achieve natural-looking results that complement your features and boost your confidence.

Types of Dental Restorations We Offer

Restorative dental procedures refer to treatments that restore the shape, function, and appearance of teeth. Chips in, or gaps between, teeth can often be fixed with veneers or crowns . At Magnolia, we can replace missing teeth with tooth bridges or dentures that look and feel like the real thing. Root canals and tooth-colored fillings , on the other hand, restore tooth structure below the surface and help teeth maintain their function.
