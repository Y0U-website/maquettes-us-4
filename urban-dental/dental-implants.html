Caring For Your Dental Implant

What You Need To Know

If you have a single-tooth dental implant, all you need to do is brush and floss normally, and see Dr. Kim for a six-month cleaning and oral exam. There are no other special care precautions required. ‍ Full-arch bridges and dentures require a bit more special care, but are still easy to clean and maintain, ensuring that your new smile will be both beautiful and convenient. Dr. Kim will provide you with comprehensive instructions on full-arch implant care after you receive your bridge or denture.
