Teeth Whitening

At C-Bar Dentistry, our professional teeth whitening services are designed to give you a brighter, more confident smile. Using advanced whitening techniques, we effectively remove stains and discoloration caused by coffee, tea, smoking, and aging. Our safe and comfortable treatments provide noticeable results in just one visit, leaving your teeth several shades whiter.

Extraction

We provide gentle and expert tooth extraction services when necessary to maintain your oral health. Whether it’s due to damage, infection, overcrowding, or wisdom teeth, our team ensures a comfortable and stress-free experience. Using modern techniques and local anesthesia, we minimize discomfort and promote a smooth recovery process.

Restoration

Whether you need fillings, crowns, bridges, or implants, our advanced restorative techniques restore the function and appearance of damaged or missing teeth. We use high-quality materials and state-of-the-art technology to ensure long-lasting results that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. Our personalized approach focuses on restoring both your oral health and confidence, giving you a smile that looks and feels great.

Crowns

Restore damaged or decayed teeth with durable dental crowns. Achieve a natural look and lasting protection for a confident, healthy smile.

veeners

Transform your smile with custom veneers. Correct chips, stains, and gaps for a flawless, radiant look you’ll love to show off.

Fillings

Treat cavities with natural-looking fillings that restore your tooth’s strength and function while blending seamlessly with your smile

Periodontics

Protect your gums and prevent periodontal disease with expert care. Maintain your oral health and safeguard your smile.

Root Canal

Save your tooth with gentle root canal treatment. Relieve pain and preserve your natural smile with expert dental care.

Wisdom Teeth

Remove impacted or problematic wisdom teeth comfortably. Prevent pain, infection, and crowding with expert extraction services.

inlays and onlays

Restore decayed or damaged teeth with custom inlays and onlays. Achieve a seamless, natural look with lasting durability.

Scaling and rooting

Deep-clean your gums with scaling and root planing. Treat gum disease and promote healthier gums for a stronger smile.

Emergency Dental

Get fast, reliable relief with emergency dental care. From toothaches to broken teeth, we’re here to restore your smile.
