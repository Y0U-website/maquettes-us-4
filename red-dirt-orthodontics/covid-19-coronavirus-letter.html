COVID 19 Safety Measures

There are a few temporary additional safety measures & protocols that we will be implementing to keep in mind ahead of your next appointment:

1) We ask that all patients please fill out our Wellness Form BEFORE coming in for your appointment.

2) We kindly require that you reschedule your appointment if you are experiencing any type of fever/coughing/illness.

3) For the time being, we will not be utilizing our waiting room for the safety of our patients & will be practicing CURBSIDE CHECK IN. To check in for your appointment, please text or call us at 405.227.0054 & we will call you in when as soon as we are ready to bring you back!

4) We ask that only ONE guest comes along with patients to their appointments to minimize the number of people in the office and allow for social distancing.

Thank you so much for your patience & understanding regarding these temporary additional protective measures we will be taking giving you the exceptional treatment & smile you deserve while keeping you safe is always our top priority & concern! We can’t wait to see you very soon!
