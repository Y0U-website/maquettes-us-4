FAQs About All-on-4® Dental Implants

1. Am I a good candidate for All-on-4®?

Most patients with multiple missing teeth or significant dental damage qualify for All-on-4®, including those with some bone loss. A 3D scan helps us determine the best treatment approach.

2. How long does the All-on-4® procedure take?

The implant placement and temporary teeth are typically completed in a single appointment, allowing you to leave with a full smile on the same day.

3. Does All-on-4® hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and sedation options are available for additional comfort. Most patients report only mild soreness afterward, which resolves within a few days.

4. How long do All-on-4® implants last?

With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime. The final prosthetic is also long-lasting and may only need replacing after many years of natural wear.

5. How do I care for my All-on-4® restoration?

Daily brushing, flossing (or water flossing), and regular dental checkups are essential to maintain the health of your implants and gums.
