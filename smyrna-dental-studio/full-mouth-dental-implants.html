What Are Full Mouth Dental Implants?

What Are Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants are a lifelong solution for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, implants provide a stable, secure, and natural-feeling set of teeth that function just like real ones.

This procedure involves placing four to six strategically positioned dental implants in the upper or lower jaw, which support a custom-made prosthetic arch. Full mouth Dental Implant Restoration can also be known as All-on-4 or All-on-X. This technique can restore full functionality, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.
