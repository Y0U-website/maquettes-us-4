DENTAL IMPLANTS

Are you missing one or all of your teeth? Dental implants are titanium posts implanted in your jaw that act as artificial "tooth roots", permanently anchoring your new tooth, bridge or denture.

When you choose to have dental implants, you can look forward to many benefits. Patients who have missing or damaged teeth find it challenging to eat foods comfortably or speak clearly. What’s more important is, missing teeth can lead to lost facial features due to the loss of bone structure in the jaw.

Thanks to implants and dental crowns, you can effectively restore both the form and function of your teeth. If you would like to speak with a dentist about your dental implant treatment in Humboldt or Webster City, IA we encourage you to give us a call. We will be happy to schedule a consultation with one of our dentists to help you determine the best treatment plan for your needs.

When you visit NorthPark Family Dentistry, our dentists will refer you to an oral surgeon for the surgical part of implant treatment. This is where you will have metal posts implanted into your jawbone. After a healing process of 3 to 6 months, the oral surgeon will have you come back so that your dental fixtures can be attached to the implants.

Before the development of dental implants, dentures were the only alternative to replacing teeth in large gaps where teeth were missing. Dentures require care and maintenance and may slip or slide and require adjustments over time. Dental implants offer more freedom and permanence.

If you’ve lost a tooth (or teeth) due to injury or decay, dental implants in Humboldt, IA could be the right solution to help repair your smile and rejuvenate your oral health.

A dental implant is a titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root, providing secure support to permanently anchor your new tooth, bridge, or denture. It is topped with a dental crown, providing a stable chewing surface and restoring the appearance of the missing tooth.

Dental implants do require us to refer you to an oral surgeon for placement of the implant, and then you will return to our office for the final restoration, which is the placement of the crown.

On the continuum of dental restoration, dental implants are the strongest and longest-lasting, and they will look, feel, and function the closest to natural teeth. We will schedule you with an appointment to see our dentists for the final restoration of your dental implants. This involves finishing your implant treatment with dental crowns. Our dentists will help you decide which type of crown will serve you best.

Problems with dental implants are rare, but they do happen. If you notice your dental implant has loosened, is causing your gum to swell, or the crown on top of your implant is cracked, call our office right away! A dental implant can’t fix itself and if you try to treat it yourself, you could end up causing more damage to your mouth.
