How Does Acceleration Work?

Acceleration technology quickly moves teeth to their desired position for straight teeth in no time. One commonly used method is the use of high-frequency vibration devices or pinholes called micro-osteoperforations.

The process consists of two major steps: diagnosing and treating. These techniques stimulate the bone around the teeth, allowing your teeth to move more quickly and efficiently. They are best for upper jaw issues like open bites, where upper teeth overlap because of a space between them.

Depending on your procedure, you will likely experience minimal discomfort using accelerated orthodontics, meaning that general anesthesia is unnecessary. You can do an accelerated orthodontics treatment and return to work or home quickly after the appointment.

Our best dentofacial orthopedics know how to give you a perfect smile using minimal orthodontic appliances. As orthodontic specialists, they know how to treat your dental issues like gum diseases and misaligned teeth.
