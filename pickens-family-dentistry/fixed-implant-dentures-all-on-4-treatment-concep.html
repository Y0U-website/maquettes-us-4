How the All-on-4® Treatment Concept Works

While some patients only need one or just a few teeth replaced. Other people have issues with almost all of their teeth. When you need to replace all of your teeth or all the teeth on the top or bottom of your mouth, the All-on-4® treatment concept gets great results with minimal surgeries.

It’s easy to assume that replacing an entire arch of teeth or the entire mouth would require dozens of dental implants, but this isn’t the case. With the All-on-4® treatment concept, four dental implants are placed along the arch of your mouth. These implants act as a stable foundation on which a denture is attached. This is why the All-on-4® treatment concept is known as an implant-supported denture.

The All-on-4® treatment concept makes the dentures your permanent teeth, giving you the smile you deserve and need.

All-on-4® Treatment Concept vs. All-on-6; What’s The Difference?

While All-on-4® treatment concept implants are an option for many people looking to repair multiple missing teeth, there are other choices for implant-supported dentistry. All-on-6 implant dentures are similar, but instead of four implants to support the dentures, the dentist places six implants.

Both options have their pros and cons. The All-on-4® treatment concept is less invasive and easier to use if there is a loss of bone density. On the other hand, All-on-6 implants can provide more support and stability, although the process is a bit more extensive.

The Convenience of Fixed Implant Dentures

Unlike traditional dentures, fixed implant dentures are convenient in many ways. You won’t have to remove them constantly to eat or to clean. Maintenance is pretty minimal, although your dentist may occasionally remove the denture to check your teeth or ensure they are in peak condition.

Once the implants are placed and left to heal, you won’t have to deal with constantly changing your dentures, and your teeth will have a strong support that they wouldn’t get with traditional dentures.
