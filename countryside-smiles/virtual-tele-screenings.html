What is a Virtual tele dental appointment ?

Once connection is live with video, our team member will verify your information, may ask you to have a self check for some vitals eg a Blood pressure reading, height- weight and get you ready for Dr Gill. Virtual screening appointments are short and only provide a path forward for your dental problem.

*if you have an dental emergency, your best choice is to call our office to determine your next steps.
