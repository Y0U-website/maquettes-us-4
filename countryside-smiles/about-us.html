Meet Dr. Jaspreet Gill, DDS

Dr. Jaspreet Gill has always been an active member of the American Dental Association , Texas Dental Association , Dallas County Dental Society, American Academy of Clear Aligners , and American Academy of Implant Dentistry. Dr. Gill also participates in Mission Mercy events and several overseas international dental outreach events in remote areas of North India in order to serve and give back to the community.

Dr Gill is a local resident and supports promoting small businesses. Dr Gill is a active member of Murphy Chamber of Commerce and is proud to serve as Director of Membership on the Murphy Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors. Dr Gill is also an active member of Wylie Chamber of Commerce

Dr. Gill is very patriotic and has family members serving in the US Army so she is always striving to show her gratitude and appreciation to service members, veterans, and their families.

Dr. Gill is fluent in English, Punjabi, Hindi, and Urdu. She comes from a rich Punjabi-Sikh American heritage. Dr. Gill resides in the Maxwell Creek neighborhood of Murphy with her family. In her free time, Dr. Gill enjoys working out, reading books, gardening, spending time with her family, traveling, and volunteering time at the local Sikh Temple where she focuses on her faith, Sikhism.
