Invisalign and Clear Aligner…

The final step will continue until the treatment has been completed. At that point, you’ll undergo an assessment to ensure that the final goal has been reached. If it has, our team will provide you with a special tray that will need to be worn to retain the new position of your teeth.

  • Preparation: This stage of the process involves taking digital impressions of your teeth to aid in designing the trays. Our team will send these images and impressions to the clear aligner manufacturer to create the necessary appliances. Once they arrive at the clinic, our team will fit small resin attachments to your teeth. These will serve as points for the aligner to snap onto to help them remain in place and adjust the position of the teeth.
  • Treatment: You’ll switch aligners every week or two throughout the treatment period. Your teeth will gradually adjust into their final position as you change aligners. Each treatment plan will have specific instructions that will govern the period between each change.
