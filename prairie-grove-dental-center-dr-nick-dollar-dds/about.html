About Us

We Want to Make You Smile

You will always feel welcome at Prairie Grove Dental Center! We take care of the whole family, ages 3 and up, with friendly dental services that meet your needs. From your first visit and routine cleanings to fillings, crowns, whitening, and clear aligners, we can provide the comprehensive care you’re looking for. Our goal is to make your visits feel simple and successful not stressful. Most of all, we’re here to help you smile more, with healthier teeth and a team that genuinely loves taking care of you!

Our Team

Here at Prairie Grove Dental Center, we believe that a smile tells a thousand words, and we are dedicated to giving our patients high-quality dental care. We offer a full range of dental services, from general dentistry to pediatric care for your kids, cosmetic upgrades, and restorative treatments.

You’ll love our amazing staff who will always do their best to make sure that you and your loved ones feel comfortable during your visits. We’re here to make your routine cleanings a breeze and to provide you with the cosmetic and restorative dental services that will have you smiling brighter in no time! Our modern office setting, clear communication, and friendly service will make you feel glad to choose Prairie Grove Dental Center for all your dental needs!

Meet Dr. Nick Dollar

Hello! I am Dr. Nick Dollar. I grew up in Fort Smith, AR. After earning my degree in microbiology from the University of Arkansas, I went on to attend dental school at the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry where I graduated with my D.D.S. degree in 2006. Before moving to NWA, I was the owner of the Paris Dental Clinic in Paris, AR, where I was blessed to be for 15 wonderful years.

When I’m not at the office, I enjoy spending time with my beautiful wife Kim and our precious daughter Lennox. She is the absolute joy of our lives. We also enjoy rooting on the Razorbacks, biking, the outdoors, traveling and snow skiing.
