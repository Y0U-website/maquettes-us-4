General Dentistry in Peachtree Corners, GA

Many patients visit their general dentist regularly to receive cleanings and exams. These appointments allow your dentist to check your mouth for any early signs of decay or disease so that you can be diagnosed and treated before your condition becomes severe. During your exam and cleaning, your dentist will also give you tips and advice on maintaining oral health between visits.

What Types of Services Do General Dentists Provide?

  • Cleanings and exams every six months to check for cavities and gum disease
  • X-rays to look for decay between teeth or below the gum line
  • Sealants to prevent cavities in molars, premolars, and any deep grooves in the tooth’s surface
  • Mouthguards for patients who play sports or have TMJ issues
  • Prescription mouth rinses or toothpaste to address conditions such as dry mouth, gingivitis, enamel erosion, and tooth decay.
  • Dental implants replace missing teeth and restore the appearance of the smile.
  • Whitening to remove stains and brighten the smile
  • Fillings to treat minor to medium-sized cavities
  • Crowns to repair a tooth that is badly decayed or cracked
  • Extractions to relieve pain caused by extensive decay, infection, or abscessed teeth
  • Root canals to treat infected pulp tissue deep within the tooth
