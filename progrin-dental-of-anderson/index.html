The practice

ProGrin’s Anderson office provides comprehensive general dentistry services , from routine checkups and cleanings to root canals, sealants, extractions, TMJ/TMD treatment, and oral cancer screenings. For patients experiencing sudden pain or dental trauma, we also offer same-day emergency dentistry.

Orthodontic care at ProGrin includes both traditional braces and clear aligner therapy, such as Invisalign, allowing patients to select a straightening method suited to their treatment goals. Both options address misalignment, crowding, and bite irregularities under professional supervision.

ProGrin’s restorative dentistry services address tooth damage and loss through treatments such as dental crowns, bridges, bonding, and tooth-colored fillings, as well as full-mouth restorations and metal-free options for patients seeking biocompatible materials.
