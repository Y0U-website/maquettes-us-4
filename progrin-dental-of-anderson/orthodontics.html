ProGrin’s Orthodontic Services

We provide a range of modern orthodontic solutions tailored to fit your lifestyle, budget, and dental needs. The goal of our orthodontics dentist is to find the perfect treatment plan to achieve your ideal smile.

  • Traditional (Metal) Braces: Time-tested and highly effective, traditional braces are a durable and reliable option for patients of all ages. They are excellent for correcting a wide variety of complex bite and alignment issues, delivering predictable and beautiful results.
  • Clear Aligners: For a more discreet path to a straighter smile, we offer clear aligners . These custom-made, removable trays are virtually invisible, making them a popular choice for adults and teens who want to straighten their teeth without the appearance of metal braces.
  • Retainers and Post-Treatment Care: Your journey does not end when your braces or aligners come off. We provide custom retainers and a comprehensive post-treatment plan to protect your investment and ensure your stunning new smile lasts a lifetime.

Orthodontics for Every Age

It’s never too early or too late to achieve a healthy, straight smile. We offer orthodontics dentistry solutions for the entire family.

  • For Children: Early orthodontic assessment can help identify and correct potential bite and jaw issues, setting the stage for a lifetime of healthy smiles.
  • For Teens: Braces and clear aligner adjustments can provide adolescents with a confidence boost and improve their oral health for their adult years.
  • For Adults: You are never too old for the smile you have always wanted. With discreet options like clear aligners , adult orthodontics are more convenient and popular than ever.

Your Step-by-Step Journey to a Straighter Smile

  • Consultation and Smile Assessment: Your orthodontics journey starts with a comprehensive consultation where we take diagnostic images, assess your orthodontic needs, and discuss your goals.
  • Customized Treatment Plan: We design a treatment plan tailored specifically to you, outlining the recommended approach (braces or aligners), timeline, and costs.
  • Treatment: We will fit you with your new braces or provide your first set of clear aligners, giving you all the information you need to care for them throughout your orthodontics treatment
  • Regular Check-ins and Adjustments: You will visit our orthodontic dentist periodically for adjustments and progress checks to ensure your treatment is on track.
  • Retainers & Maintenance: Once your teeth are perfectly aligned, we will create a custom retainer to maintain your beautiful results.
