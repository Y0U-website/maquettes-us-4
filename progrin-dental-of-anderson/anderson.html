Team Qualifications

The Anderson team combines accredited dental training with hands-on clinical experience and dedicated patient support.

Our Anderson team, pictured together at the office.

Together, this team’s combined training and experience allow ProGrin Dental of Anderson to deliver clinically sound, patient-centered care at every visit.

  • Hygienists Our dental hygienists are licensed clinicians trained in periodontal assessment, preventive therapy, and oral cancer screening, and each maintains ongoing continuing education to stay current with evolving standards of care. Their combined years of chairside experience allow them to identify early signs of disease and tailor preventive plans to each patient’s needs.
  • Dental Assistants Our dental assistants complete formal training in radiography, infection control, and patient care, supporting our doctors through every stage of treatment with precision and consistency. Their clinical experience helps procedures move efficiently while keeping patients informed and comfortable throughout each appointment.
  • Patient Care Team Our patient care team brings extensive experience in dental administration, insurance coordination, and treatment planning, guiding patients through scheduling and financial questions with clarity. They ensure every visit begins and ends with a well-organized, welcoming experience.

Financial Information

ProGrin Dental of Anderson works to minimize financial barriers to treatment, with fees that reflect the quality of care and the time our doctors and team dedicate to each patient. Flexible financing is available through CareCredit and iCreditWorks for patients who prefer to spread the cost of care over time.

Dental Insurance

ProGrin Dental of Anderson accepts a range of dental insurance plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BlueCross BlueShield, United Healthcare, and Ameritas. Our insurance specialists verify benefits and manage claims paperwork on behalf of patients. We also offer an in-house dental savings plan, InstaGrin, for uninsured or underinsured patients. Call the Anderson office to confirm coverage before your visit.

Payment Options

We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards, along with flexible financing through CareCredit and iCreditWorks. Ask our patient care team about a payment plan that fits your budget.
