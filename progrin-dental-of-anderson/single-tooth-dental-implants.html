How Dental Implants Help You

Permanent

Implant materials are incredibly durable and not susceptible to decay. When cared for properly, the crown can last 10+ years, and the post can last the rest of your life.

Look Younger

An implant will preserve your missing tooth’s bone structure, which prevents that sunken, aged look.

Save Money

Dental implants cost more upfront, but because they prevent dental problems and don’t need ongoing maintenance, you’ll save money in the long run.

Restore Appearance

No one will distinguish an implant restoration from your surrounding teeth! From its color, shape, and size, every detail will be custom designed to look natural.

Enjoy Food Again

Dental implants don’t move around and cause pain while chewing. It’ll function like a natural tooth, so you can finally enjoy your favorite foods again (like apples and steaks!).

Speak without Embarrassment

Unlike dentures, dental implants won’t cause you to mumble or slur words, give you a lisp, or worse, fall out mid-sentence!

FAQs about Dental Implants

Answered by our expert restorative dentists.

Getting a dental implant doesn’t hurt. In fact, many patients state a tooth extraction causes more discomfort.

During the procedure, your mouth will be numb, so you don’t feel pain. If you need extra assistance to relax, we offer sedation dentistry and a comfort menu.

Our office also uses the latest dental technology, making placing the implant easier and faster.

After the procedure, your implant site may feel tender. This will subside within a few days, and over-the-counter pain medication can help in the meantime.

Dental implant surgery has a 95 % success rate. It’s a very predictable procedure and is one of the most successful surgeries out there.

Keep in mind, the key to achieving success is choosing your dental implant dentist wisely.
