Comfort and Fit: Dental Implants vs. Dentures

Dental implants differ from removable dentures because they are anchored directly within the jawbone. This support helps create stability during everyday activities such as eating and speaking. Because implants do not rely on adhesives or removable components, many patients find them to be a comfortable and predictable tooth replacement option.
