Dr. Armine Grigoryan

Since then, she has worked with different populations in Maryland and spends a significant amount of time volunteering as a dentist for Mission of Mercy clinics state-wide. Dr. Grigoryan loves to spend her free time with her husband and daughter and keep up with the latest fashions.
