kids

If you’re wondering at what age your child should first come in for a consultation, the American Association of Orthodontists recommends age seven for the initial visit. But no matter the age of their first visit, we’ll make sure they’re taken care of!

We’ve made the whole experience as enjoyable as possible for your child with activities, games and snacks available in our living area. Plus, our 7Up Kids Club makes it fun to take care of your health and offers prizes and sweet swag as your child begins to form the habits necessary for growing into fantastic future members of our community.

teens

Along with rocking our high-tech, aesthetically-pleasing clear braces or Invisalign® Clear Aligners, teens love coming to our office to experience everything we have to offer. From the music they love and engaging activities while they’re here, to snacks, drinks and so much more, we’ve made the entire experience the highlight of their day. And that’s all before they get to interact with our friendly, caring team of specialized assistants and doctors and choose from popular low-profile treatment options like Invisalign® to get their smile looking its best.

From the first visit to the final reveal, your teen is going to love it here.

adults
