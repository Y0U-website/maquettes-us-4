The practice

Finding a dentist is about more than choosing someone to clean your teeth. It’s about finding a dental team you can trust—one that takes the time to listen, explains your options clearly, and helps you make decisions that are right for your health and your family.

At St. James Dental Group, we’ve been honored to care for patients throughout Southeast Los Angeles for more than three decades. Many of our patients first visited us as children and now bring in their own children, allowing us to care for multiple generations of the same families.

We believe exceptional dentistry begins with honest recommendations, conservative treatment, and genuine relationships. Whether you’re visiting for a routine checkup, repairing a damaged tooth, or improving your smile, our goal is always the same: to help you achieve lasting oral health in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Every smile is different, which is why we take the time to understand your goals before recommending treatment. We believe patients deserve personalized care—not one-size-fits-all solutions.

Regular exams, professional cleanings, digital X-rays, fluoride treatments, and personalized preventive care designed to keep your smile healthy for years to come.

Gentle dental care for children, teens, adults, and seniors in a friendly environment where every member of your family feels comfortable.

Enhance the appearance of your smile with porcelain veneers, composite veneers, professional teeth whitening, cosmetic bonding, and smile makeover solutions tailored to your goals.

Restore strength, function, and confidence with tooth-colored fillings, dental crowns, implant crowns, bridges, dentures, root canal treatment, and extractions when necessary.
