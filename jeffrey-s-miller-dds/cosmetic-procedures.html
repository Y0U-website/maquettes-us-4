Enhance Your Smile with Personalized Cosmetic Dentistry

Whether you’re looking to whiten stained teeth, repair minor imperfections, or completely transform your smile, Jeffrey S. Miller DDS offers customized cosmetic dentistry solutions designed around your goals. Treatment options may include professional teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, cosmetic bonding, and complete smile makeovers. Every treatment begins with a comprehensive consultation to determine the best approach for achieving natural-looking, long-lasting results.

Bonding

Having a confident and beautiful smile is the key to making an unforgettable first impression – so why not take advantage of cosmetic bonding, a quick solution for achieving your desired look? Bonding will help you gain back confidence in your self-image by repairing chipped or uneven teeth with composite resin. Best yet this treatment can be completed stress free in one visit at the dentist, without requiring sedation! Unlock that gorgeous smile today with affordable bondings services.

Empress® Restorations

Get a smile that lasts with Empress® restorations metal-free and crafted from inorganic material, they provide strength and durability while still looking incredibly natural. Enjoy being able to show off your brilliant pearly whites for years – these veneers, crowns, bridges & more are designed to resist staining or fading!

Dental Implants

Dental implants provide a durable solution for those missing one or more teeth. At Jeffrey S. Miller DDS, we understand the importance of a confident smile. Our dental implants restore not just functionality but also aesthetics, allowing you to enjoy life to the fullest. They are surgically placed in the jaw, providing a strong foundation for tooth replacements.

If you find yourself in need of either a single dental implant to replace a missing tooth or full dentures to restore your complete smile, you will be pleased to learn that dental implants can truly transform your oral health and overall appearance. Our professional team is here to assist you every step of the way on your journey toward restoring your smile. We specialize in cosmetic dentistry services and are committed to providing you with the highest quality care to ensure that you achieve the beautiful and confident smile you deserve.

Porcelain Crowns

To restore a damaged tooth to its original shape and size, porcelain crowns offer an ideal solution. Not only will they give you the most natural-looking smile possible; with proper care, these highly durable restorations can last for many years providing both aesthetic enhancement as well as dependable support in cases of large fillings or root canals. For confident smiles that stand the test of time – porcelain crowns are your best bet!

Porcelain Veneers

Transform your smile with custom-made veneers! Thin, durable porcelain pieces are bonded onto the front of teeth to enhance their appearance and correct dental conditions such as crookedness or discoloration. Veneers provide an alternative solution to crowns for restoring misshapen, small, large and chipped teeth so you can have a dazzling grin in no time!

Straumann Implants

Dental implants are quickly becoming the go-to choice for adults seeking tooth replacement. Not only do they confer a natural appearance and enhanced functionality of speech, eating, and chewing—but Straumann® Dental Implant technology is making them even more desirable! These implants are proven, simple and flexible long term solutions.

Composite Fillings

Restore the look and function of your teeth with composite fillings! Unlike silver amalgam, these tooth-colored plastic and glass mixtures blend in seamlessly with existing teeth to restore their strength, structure, and appearance. This type of filling bonds directly to the surface for a strong foundation but may require occasional replacements since it is not as durable as other options. Speak with your dentist today about what’s best for you restoring your beautiful smile could be just around the corner!

Porcelain Inlays & Onlays

Dental inlays and onlays are the perfect solution for conservatively restoring teeth with large fillings or that have been damaged by decay, trauma, or other factors. With custom-fitted pieces made of tooth-colored composite material, gold, or porcelain these restorations provide natural looking results while preserving more of your original tooth structure than traditional amalgam (silver) fillings and resin composites!

Porcelain Fixed Bridges

An incomplete smile can leave you feeling insecure and unhappy. Dental bridges offer a solution, filling in the gaps between your teeth for an improved look and enhanced confidence. Offering lasting durability and convenient upkeep with regular brushing and flossing, these porcelain-fused-metal creations are one of the most popular options available to restore beauty to any grin! Plus, they provide functional benefits like preventing misalignment of other healthy teeth

Tooth Whitening

Nothing boosts self-esteem like a bright, confident smile! That’s why tooth whitening is one of the most popular ways to rejuvenate and refresh your appearance. With professional systems that can dramatically lighten natural enamel in just a few weeks, you don’t have to worry about replacing old fillings or crowns after bleaching they will match up with your newly glowing grin so no one has any idea what lies beneath the surface. Let go of dullness for good and give yourself an instant confidence boost when you invest in teeth whitening today!
