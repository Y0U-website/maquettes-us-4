Dentistry shouldn&#x27;t feel like a transaction.

When Dr. Agrawal opened BiteSize Dentistry , the goal was simple: build an independent practice that treats the whole patient — not just the tooth in front of us. Dr. Agrawal trained at the Pankey Institute, one of the most rigorous programs in dentistry for comprehensive occlusal care. That training shapes how every exam and treatment plan is built.

You will see the same dentist at every visit. No associates, no rotating providers. We build treatment plans independent of what insurance will cover, then work to maximize your benefits within that plan — not the other way around. And we won&#x27;t recommend treatment you don&#x27;t need.

“We build the treatment plan first, then figure out how insurance fits into it — not the other way around.”

Our core values.

Morality

It&#x27;s our shining star. One may be the best captain but going the wrong direction — we do what&#x27;s right first.

Autonomy

We value the ability to work independently and take responsibility for our work — we expect much of ourselves and each other.

Skill

Applying our practice of morality and autonomy toward skill is a recipe for continued improvement.

Faith

Whether it is religious, spiritual, or in our team and values, faith is required for a group of individuals to operate as a team. It is the bedrock of all our other values.

Care dictated by patients&#x27; needs.

We envision a dental practice where care is directed by practitioner expertise and executed for patients — not prescribed by insurance companies. We strive to marry insurance benefits with sound diagnostics rather than treat only what insurance allows.

Listen first. Then plan.

We listen to our patients first, provide a clear explanation of diagnostic results, and build treatment plans independent of insurance benefits — then retrofit insurance to maximize coverage. This takes more time, but allows for more comprehensive results.
