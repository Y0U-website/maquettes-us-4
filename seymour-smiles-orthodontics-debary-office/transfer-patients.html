We know that there are many families and individuals moving to Florida, and even within Florida. If you have recently relocated to the Sunshine State and in need of an orthodontist to continue treatment, we’d love to see you! Our locations: Debary (near Deltona and Orange City)
  • Wellen Park (between Venice and North Port)
  • Babcock Ranch (Punta Gorda and North Ft. Meyers)
  • Osprey (South of Sarasota near Palmer Ranch and Vamo)

WHAT TO EXPECT

  • We will take your previous contract into consideration and make sure your new financial agreement is fair . Best of all, we don’t charge extra simply because you’re transferring to us.
  • Whenever possible, we’ll continue working with your current braces or aligners no unnecessary changes to your existing hardware. We can also use your current records and X-rays when appropriate, helping make your transition smoother and more affordable.
  • From the moment you join us, we’ll treat you and your family as part of our practice family. No smile gets left behind!
