Two-Phase Orthodontic Treatment

Phase I & II Treatments

Sometimes, creating a healthy smile starts before all of your child’s permanent teeth have erupted.

Two-phase orthodontic treatment allows us to address certain problems with jaw growth, spacing, crowding, or tooth alignment early, then complete full treatment (alignment and bite) after all the permanent teeth have erupted.

Phase I: Start Early if it is necessary

Phase I treatment typically happens between ages 7 and 10 and addresses developing concerns with jaw growth, width, crowding, crossbites, or front tooth alignment. When early treatment is recommended, we aim to keep it short , simple , and affordable , with many Phase I treatments completed in about nine months.

Phase II: Finish the Smile and Bite

Phase I does not replace comprehensive orthodontic treatment. After a typical 12 to 24-month break for continued growth and development, Phase II involves the full set of braces. Addressing key concerns earlier can often make this phase shorter and more efficient, with the goal to make the best path toward a perfect smile and perfect bite for each patient.
