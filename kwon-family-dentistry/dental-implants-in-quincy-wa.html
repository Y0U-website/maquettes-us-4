FAQs about All-On-X dental implants

How long does the All-On-X dental implant procedure take?

The initial procedure, including implant placement and temporary tooth fitting, is typically completed in a single day. You can arrive with missing teeth and leave with a functional, aesthetic smile. The entire process, including healing and permanent restoration, takes about 4-6 months to complete, during which time you’ll wear your temporary teeth.

What is the recovery process like?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort and can return to their normal activities within a few days of the procedure. Some swelling and bruising are normal and typically resolve within the first week. You’ll receive detailed instructions for managing any post-procedure symptoms and maintaining proper oral hygiene during healing.

How do I care for my All-On-X dental implants?

Maintaining your dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings are essential. Unlike traditional dentures, there’s no need for removal or special cleaning solutions. Following proper oral hygiene practices will help ensure the longevity of your implants.

Are All-On-X dental implants permanent?

The implants themselves are designed to be permanent, integrating with your natural bone structure. Proper care and maintenance can last for many years or even a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth attached to the implants may need replacement after several years of use, though this varies based on individual factors.

Who is a good candidate for All-On-X dental implants?

Most adults who have lost or most of their teeth are potential candidates for All-On-X dental implants. Good candidates should have adequate bone density to support the implants and be generally healthy. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate factors like bone quality, oral health, and medical history to determine if this solution is right for you.
