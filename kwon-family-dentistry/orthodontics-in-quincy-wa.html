SureSmile® Aligners

SureSmile® aligners offer a comfortable and convenient solution to straighten misaligned teeth. Unlike traditional metal braces, these custom aligners are nearly invisible, allowing you to smile with confidence throughout treatment.

They’re also removable. You can simply take out your aligner for meals or special occasions, which offers far more flexibility than traditional braces.

One of the major perks of this option is its simplicity. They’re easy to clean and maintain; just pop them out and brush them along with your regular dental hygiene routine. Even better, without any metal wires or brackets involved, you’ll find less irritation in your mouth compared to standard braces.

Furthermore, SureSmile® significantly reduces the check-ups that often come with other bite alignment solutions, while still delivering accurate results for a beautiful smile.

Retainers

Retainers are vital for preserving your teeth alignment after orthodontic procedures. Customized retainers are included as part of top-notch family dental care services. These tools aid in holding the position of your teeth when braces or clear aligners have been taken out, guaranteeing results that stand the test of time from orthodontic treatments.

The focus is on personalized approaches and first-rate patient welfare to help keep those smiles impeccable over the long haul. With regular use of retainers, you can protect your smile from potential shifting and preserve those straightened teeth that took so much work!
