Meet Dr. Idan Snapir

Trusted Van Nuys, CA Dentist

Get to Know Dr. Idan Snapir

With over 20 years of experience in the dental world so far, Dr. Snapir is the lead doctor at OraBell Dental Implant Centers in Van Nuys. OraBell is the premier dental implant provider serving the San Fernando Valley. What he enjoys most about his job is that it truly doesn’t feel like a job! He finds great reward and fulfillment in not only transforming his patients’ smiles for the better, but guiding them to a higher quality of life. Keep reading below if you would like to learn more about him.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Many years ago, Dr. Snapir worked in a photo printing shop. During these days, he’d often get a chance to talk with doctors and surgeons who came in with educational samples of their work. These encounters were what first sparked his dream of becoming a doctor!

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Snapir attended UCLA for his dental doctorate. Since then, he has eagerly pursued continuing education in a number of facets of dentistry, including dental implants, orthodontics, and full mouth rehabilitation. By expanding his clinical skillset, he’s been able to develop comprehensive approaches to dental problems that involve multi-specialty solutions and treatments.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Away from work, Dr. Snapir has a beautiful family that includes his amazing wife and two children. They inspire him to wake up every morning with boundless energy! In his spare time, you can often find him exercising, reading, traveling, trying new foods, or playing with his kids.
