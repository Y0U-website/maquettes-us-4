Why Are Braces Important?

Braces are the foundation of dental alignment. They are important because they can help to prevent more extensive and complex procedures from happening later on in life. In addition, proper teeth alignment can help to reduce the risk of both gum disease and tooth decay. It also leaves you with a beautiful, straight smile that you can be proud to show off!
