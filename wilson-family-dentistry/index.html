The practice

We practice evidence-based care and incorporate the latest technology to provide quality, comfortable and affordable care for our patients.

We serve patients of all ages, children through adults. We offer a wide range of oral health services, from regular cleanings and comprehensive oral exams to the most complex dental cases. We focus on delivering the most advanced, professional care available.

The best way to ensure your oral health and prevent the need for future dental treatment is to maintain your oral hygiene at home along with regular dental cleanings and exams. This includes brushing twice a day and flossing daily. We are happy to teach you proper brushing and flossing technique and answer any questions you might have about keeping your teeth clean.

At Wilson Family Dentistry we offer a range of treatment options to replace missing teeth or spaces. We will take into account your unique case and help you decide which treatment option is the best fit for you.

A beautiful smile is one of the most sought after features in cosmetic medicine. People from all backgrounds and professions want a white, straight smile, and we can help make it a reality.

We are proud to provide compassionate care for our patients and will never turn away anyone who is in pain. After listing to your description of symptoms, we will perform a limited exam of the area that is causing discomfort and take any appropriate x-rays. Often times we can offer multiple solutions to treat your dental pain and/or a referral to a specialist.

Wilson Family Dentistry was founded by Robert “Doc” J Wilson Sr. in 1958. After serving for two years in the US Army Dental Corp, Doc Wilson served the community of Gaithersburg for over fifty years as an active advocate for both the Dental Profession and the Fire Service.

Your initial visit will typically consist of a in depth examination of your gum, bone, tooth health, and any appropriate x-rays. We will also perform an oral cancer screening. At this appointment we will recommend the type of cleaning that is right for you.
