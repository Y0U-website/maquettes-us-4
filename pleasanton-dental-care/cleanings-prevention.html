Cleanings and Prevention…

  • Cavity Prevention: Cleanings remove plaque and tartar, reducing the risk of cavities and the need for fillings.
  • Gum Disease Prevention: Routine cleanings help prevent gum disease by removing the plaque buildup that can lead to inflammation and infection.
  • Fresh Breath: Removing plaque and tartar buildup eliminates the source of bad breath, leaving you with fresh and confident breath.
  • Cost-Effective: Investing in preventive dental care, including cleanings, is often more cost-effective than treating dental problems after they develop.
  • Overall Health: Good oral health is linked to overall well-being, reducing the risk of systemic health issues such as heart disease and diabetes.
  • Personalized Care: Pleasanton Dental Care creates individualized preventive plans to address your specific needs, ensuring your dental health is given the attention it deserves.

FAQs

How often should I schedule a cleaning at Pleasanton Dental Care?

We recommend a cleaning every six months, but some patients may require more frequent visits based on their unique oral health needs.

Do dental cleanings hurt?

No, our gentle approach ensures minimal discomfort during cleanings. Your comfort is our priority. Our team will communicate with you throughout the process to ensure you’re at ease.

What happens if I skip dental cleanings?

Skipping cleanings can lead to plaque buildup, cavities, and gum disease. Prevention is key to avoiding these issues. If you’ve missed a cleaning, don’t hesitate to schedule one now to get back on track.

How can I maintain my smile between visits?

Brushing, flossing, and a healthy diet are essential. Our team will provide personalized tips during your visit, including product recommendations and techniques to keep your smile at its brightest.
