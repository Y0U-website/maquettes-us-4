What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are a full or partial denture that connects to implants placed in your jawbone, rather than resting loosely on your gums the way a conventional denture does. The implants act as anchors, so the denture holds steady when you eat, speak, or smile. You’ll see this treatment described a few different ways online: implant dentures, implant retained dentures, or snap in dentures irvine ca searches often turn up, and in everyday conversation people tend to use these terms interchangeably.

There’s a real distinction worth understanding, though. A denture that snaps onto implants and can be taken out by the patient for cleaning is usually called an implant overdenture. A version that’s more fixed to the implants, removed only by your dentist, is more often described as implant-supported or implant-retained. Bone Preservation and Long-Term Oral Health

When a tooth root is lost, the jawbone beneath it starts to lose density over time because it no longer receives stimulation from chewing. Implants replace that root function, giving the bone something to work against, which helps preserve bone volume in a way a conventional denture simply can’t.

A Natural Look and Feel Tailored to You

Your denture is custom fitted to your bite, jaw shape, and facial contours, so it looks like it belongs in your mouth instead of sitting on top of it. Many patients also notice that speech feels more natural once the constant worry about a shifting denture is gone.

Implant Placement and Healing

Implants are placed in the jawbone during a focused procedure, often with sedation options available for anxious patients. Healing typically takes a few months, during which the bone fuses to the implant, a process that gives the final denture its strength and stability.

Attaching Your Custom Denture

Once healing is complete, your custom denture is fitted and attached to the implants. We’ll check your bite, your speech, and your comfort, making adjustments as needed until the fit feels right and functions the way it should.
