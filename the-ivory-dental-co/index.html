The practice

Discover your brilliance by becoming a member of our patient family for all your dental and esthetic care.

Dentists have comprehensive knowledge of facial anatomy, including skin, tissue, nerves, and bones. This means Dr. Sweeney is well-versed in facial structure and certified in administering cosmetic injectables, including neuromodulators and dermal fillers . She can tailor esthetic treatments to enhance your unique look.

Imagine the convenience of combining your skin rejuvenation with smile restoration . From routine cleanings to teeth whitening and microneedling to nonsurgical facial balancing , we offer a holistic approach to elevate both your smile and your overall facial appearance.

Find our building on Transit Road across from the Transit Valley Country Club located in the Transit Meadows Medical & Professional Park. The spacious parking lot offers plenty of convenient spots.
