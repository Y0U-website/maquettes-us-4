Rachelle, Registered Dental Hygienist

Rachelle has been a registered dental hygienist for 10+ years. She has worked with both adults and pediatrics. Her passion and love of dentistry go beyond the basics of teeth—she will make you feel like The Ivory Dental Co. is your forever dental home!

Rachelle enjoys bonding with her patients and growing friendships so that it feels like you never left. The ease of getting to know you and catching up will make every dental visit serene!

She spends time with her loving family when not at The Ivory Dental Co. She enjoys sharing her little boy’s first-time moments. She would’ve been a chef if she weren’t a dental hygienist. Before her son, she enjoyed being a foodie by exploring new restaurants. Now, she loves to cook Filipino, Thai, and Vietnamese dishes.

Zahra, Dental Assistant

Zahra is an aspiring dental student currently a neuroscience major at Haverford College. Her desire to pursue dentistry stemmed from the first time she shadowed a dental practice. After shadowing more individuals in the dental field, specifically Dr. Sweeney, she knew this career path was the one for her.

Zahra was eager to join The Ivory Dental Co. after seeing how individualized Dr. Sweeney’s treatments were and how comfortable patients felt in her space. Zahra has learned a lot from her experiences within the practice, and her goal is to ensure that your dental experience is as relaxing and seamless as possible!

Outside the dental world, Zahra enjoys the gym, frequenting the beach, traveling, and spending time with her loved ones. She is excited to meet you and promises to make your Ivory Dental experience as awesome as it can be!
