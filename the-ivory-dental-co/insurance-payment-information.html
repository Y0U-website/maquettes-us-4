Perio Plan – $650 Annually

Our Perio Membership Plan considers the needs of patients with periodontal disease or bone loss. With this plan, you save $1,018 annually . It includes the following benefits:

  • Initial comprehensive exam
  • Bi-annual periodic exam
  • 4 periodontal maintenance cleanings
  • Necessary intraoral X-rays
  • 1 full mouth series of X-rays (once every 5 years)
  • 2 fluoride desensitizing treatments
  • 2 oral cancer screenings
  • 1 emergency care visit (exam & necessary X-rays)

Child Plan – $350 Annually

Our Child Membership Plan is for patients 15 and under, so our younger patients have coverage for general and preventative dental care. With this plan, you can save up to $772 annually , and it includes the following benefits:

  • Initial comprehensive exam
  • Bi-annual periodic exam
  • 2 prophy cleanings & necessary intraoral X-rays
  • 1 full mouth series of X-rays (once every 5 years)
  • 2 fluoride desensitizing treatments
  • 2 oral cancer screenings
  • 1 emergency care visit (exam & necessary X-rays)
