FAQs About Orthodontics

When should my child see an orthodontist? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an initial evaluation by age 7. Early exams help monitor jaw development and detect potential issues before they become complex.

Do adults need orthodontics too? Absolutely. Many adults choose to straighten their teeth for functional or cosmetic reasons. We offer clear and discreet options perfect for professional lifestyles.

How long will I wear braces or aligners? Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case but generally ranges between 12 and 24 months. We’ll provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Which is better: Invisalign or braces? Each option has its advantages. Invisalign is great for mild to moderate cases and offers more flexibility, while braces are often more effective for complex alignment issues. We’ll help you choose based on your needs.

Are orthodontics covered by insurance? Some dental insurance plans offer orthodontic coverage. Our team will help you understand your benefits and explore financing options like Cherry if needed.
