What Is TMJ Disorder?

TMD, or temporomandibular joint disease, is a condition that causes you to grind your teeth without realizing it. Stress is the most common cause of this ailment, which can result in long-term, inexplicable face muscle pain. Your enamel may wear off over time due to continuous grinding, producing major complications. As a result, it is vital to diagnose the condition as soon as possible and get treatment from your Palo Alto, CA dentist.

Symptoms of TMJ Disorders

  • Pain in the jaws, ears, or cheeks, as well as perplexing headaches.
  • When chewing, you may experience pain or hear clicking sounds.
  • When you wake up from a nap, your facial muscles are stiff or weary.
  • Clenching one’s teeth, whether awake or sleeping Headaches around the temple area
  • Difficulty opening mouth freely, smoothly, or appropriately
  • Whether closed or open, the jaw feels fixed in place.
  • Misalignment of the bite Discomfort when biting or uncomfortable chewing
  • Teeth that have been chipped or worn on the edges
  • Visible overbite
  • Teeth shifting, gaps, or crowding for no apparent cause
