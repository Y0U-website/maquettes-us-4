Union Avenue Dental Center is a family-friendly dental practice, caring for patients of all ages from Tacoma, WA, and the surrounding communities of Federal Way, Lakewood, Kent, Vashon Island, and Puyallup.

Dr. Sonia Pal and our team provide comprehensive dental care for the whole family. From dental hygiene cleanings and professional exams to seamless dental restorations and complete smile makeovers, we can help you maintain optimal oral health and a beautiful smile at Union Avenue Dental Center.

As soon as you walk through our doors, you will know you are excellent hands. You will be greeted with a smile and seen on time for your appointment. Our dental team gives each patient the individualized attention they deserve and approaches each treatment with a gentle touch.

Our main goal is to provide you and your family with the highest quality of dental care in a supportive and nurturing manner. Schedule an appointment today and experience compassionate dental care at Union Avenue Dental!

Dr. Pal graduated from Temple University School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania in 1995. She went on to complete a one-year general practice residency at St. Francis Hospital in Hartford, Connecticut. She then practiced in various locations in Connecticut, Wyoming, and Washington before joining this practice in March 2007. Dr. Pal lives in Tacoma with her two daughters, Tara and Kiran.
