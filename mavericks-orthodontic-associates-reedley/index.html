Dr. Steve O’Hara was born in Clovis, California. The youngest child of six, he had a fantastic upbringing in the foothills of Clovis, where he attended Dry Creek, Kastner, Clovs West,

and Fresno State where he obtained finance and biology degrees. He attended University of the Pacific Dental school a GPR at San Francisco Veterns Hospital, and finally the UOP orthodontic program. He immediately returned home to Fresno and has been creating beautiful smiles here for 20 years.

Dr. O’Hara met his wife, Monika, a Clovis Unified school teacher, in high school. They have a wonderful daughter, Lily the gymnast, and Rhodesian Ridgeback named Kona, who loves Avila beach. The family fun is traveling, kayaking, fishing, surfing, and big family get togethers.

Born, raised, educated, all in the central valley, Dr. O’Hara lives less than a five-minute drive from the home where he grew up. Fresno is “home” to his family and always will be. He looks forward to meeting families from the Fresno and Clovis area that are interested in a beautiful smile!
