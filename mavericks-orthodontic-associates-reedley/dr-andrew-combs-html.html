Dr Andrew Combs

Dr. Andrew Combs was born and raised in Clovis, where he enjoyed attending Fresno State athletic events, visiting Shaver Lake, and playing tennis at Clovis High School. After high school, he stayed local and earned his Bachelor of Arts in Pre-Health Sciences from Fresno Pacific University. Dr. Combs then attended the UCLA School of Dentistry, where he graduated at the top of his class with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and received the prestigious Alpha Omega Award.

Driven by a passion for orthodontics, Dr. Combs completed a three-year postgraduate residency in orthodontics at UCLA, where he also earned a Master of Science in Oral Biology. His research focused on functional breathing improvement following Maxillary Skeletal Expansion (MSE) treatment. Dr. Combs is a board-certified member of the American Board of Orthodontics and specializes in the latest techniques in braces and clear aligner therapy.

Outside of the office, Dr. Combs enjoys spending time with his family and friends. He married his high-school sweetheart, Tori, and together they have three children: Hayden, Hudson, and Brooklyn. In his spare time, Dr. Combs loves being outdoors, hiking, playing sports, barbecuing, and planning his next adventure.

Dr. Combs looks forward to welcoming you to the office and is dedicated to helping you achieve a beautiful smile.
