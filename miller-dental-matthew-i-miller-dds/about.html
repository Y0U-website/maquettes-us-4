About Miller Dental

A family-owned practice dedicated to providing exceptional dental care with a personal touch.

Our Story

Our dental legacy began in 1958 when Dr. Ken Miller first opened the doors to his practice with a vision of providing compassionate, quality dental care to the Indianapolis community.

In 1989, Dr. Matt Miller joined his father’s practice, continuing the tradition of personalized, patient-first dentistry. Following his father’s retirement, Dr. Matt took the helm and has proudly carried forward the family legacy that began over six decades ago.

Today, Miller Dental continues to treat generations of families with the same gentle approach and dedication to excellence that has defined our practice from the very beginning.

Our Philosophy

We believe that great dentistry starts with understanding your unique needs and concerns.

Prevention First We focus on preventive care to keep your smile healthy for life. Regular checkups and cleanings help us catch problems early before they become bigger issues.

Transparent Communication We take the time to explain everything in terms you understand. You’ll always know what to expect and never feel pressured into treatments you don’t need.

Comfort-First Experience Your comfort is our priority. From our friendly team to our gentle approach, we strive to make every visit relaxing and stress-free.
