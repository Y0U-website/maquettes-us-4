Our Services

Comprehensive dental care for the whole family. We offer a wide range of services to keep your smile healthy and beautiful.

Keep Your Smile Healthy

Regular checkups and cleanings are the foundation of good oral health. We focus on prevention to help you avoid costly dental problems down the road.

Dental Exams Comprehensive oral examinations including oral cancer screening, gum health assessment, and evaluation of existing restorations.

Professional Cleanings Thorough dental cleanings to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains. Keep your teeth and gums healthy and your smile bright.

X-Rays Digital X-rays with up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film. Clear images for accurate diagnosis.

Restore Your Smile

From fillings to crowns, we use the latest materials and techniques to restore the health, function, and appearance of your teeth.

Tooth-Colored Fillings Natural-looking composite resin fillings that blend seamlessly with your smile. Metal-free and mercury-free.

Dental Crowns Custom-made crowns to protect and strengthen damaged teeth. We offer porcelain and ceramic options for a natural look.

Amalgam Fillings Traditional silver amalgam fillings. Durable and cost-effective option for restoring teeth affected by decay or damage.

Night Guards Custom night guards for clenching and grinding. Protect your teeth from wear and reduce jaw pain and headaches.

Dentures Custom-fit full and partial dentures to restore your smile and ability to eat comfortably. Natural-looking results.

Whitening Trays Custom-made whitening trays for at-home teeth whitening. Professional-grade results in the comfort of your own home.
