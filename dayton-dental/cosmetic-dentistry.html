Cosmetic Dentistry

Let Your Smile Do the Talking

A healthy smile not only allows you to keep your body healthy, to speak naturally and eat a balanced diet. A smile is a universal language that allows us to convey our emotions and communicate with others without having to say a word. Is your smile sending the message you want it to? If your teeth are crooked, broken, yellow, or otherwise imperfect, a simple cosmetic solution could change not only your smile, but your sense of confidence and even your life.

Teeth Whitening

At Dayton Dental, we want your smile to be a ray of sunshine that spreads joy to all those who see it. Unfortunately, as we age, things tend to get a bit cloudy (something we’re used to in the Pacific Northwest). The world knows we love our coffee in this neck of the woods, but that shouldn’t be the first thing your smile says about you.

How do teeth stain?

Anything that will stain a white shirt will stain your teeth over time.

A simple teeth whitening treatment can remove years of surface stains, and even those deep intrinsic stains that give teeth their color. We know there are thousands of over the counter whitening products you can pick up with your groceries, but only a professional whitening treatment can give you the results you deserve.

We have in-office whitening treatments so you can get dazzling results in a single office visit. We also have take-home kits available if you prefer to whiten on your own time from the comfort of home.

Cosmetic Bonding

Bonding can correct teeth that are:

At Dayton Dental, we’re all about bonding. While yes we do love to sing Kumbaya and practice trust falls at our morning huddles, we also love bonding teeth to give our patients a more beautiful smile with a simple fix.

Bonding can be both restorative and cosmetic in nature and involves painting over the surface of the teeth. We use a durable and natural-looking tooth-colored resin that is perfectly color matched to blend in seamlessly with your natural teeth. We paint over the surface of the teeth to correct minor imperfections.

Bonding is a less invasive alternative to porcelain veneers for correcting a single problem tooth, multiple teeth, or all your visible teeth. Bonding is a quick, comfortable, and affordable way to make a small change that will make a big impact on your smile and confidence.
