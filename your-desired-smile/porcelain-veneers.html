PORCELAIN VENEERS IN TAMPA, FL

Many of our patients seek our help with a full smile restoration. One of the simplest ways to start this process is by creating a facade out of porcelain veneers. These veneers can correct poor color, shape and texture. We make our porcelain veneers on site using sophisticated cutting-edge software so that we have the perfect fit for you. With veneers, your natural teeth remain largely intact with only a minimal amount of alteration required to fit the veneer.

Our experienced team has perfected the practice of creating an invisible bonding point to ensure that the veneers’ presence goes unnoticed. If you’re looking for a full-smile overhaul, make sure to to schedule a consultation to learn what other procedures Dr. Alicea recommends.

Many patients find that multi-tooth veneer applications can drastically improve practical use such as bite, too. Maintaining porcelain veneers is easier than you would believe! Call us today at 813-580-5244.
