Dr. Steven Kerns

Dr. Steven Kerns , a Lincoln native, graduated from the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry in 1985 and went on to establish Lincoln Family Dental. With a background in Fine Arts Education, Dr. Kerns brings both precision and creativity to his practice. He pursues ongoing education and has advanced training in implant dentistry, as well as Premier Provider status for Invisalign®. In his free time, Dr. Kerns enjoys traveling with his wife, May, along with drawing, painting, reading, and watching movies. He is also a proud grandfather of 13.

Dr. Lincoln Pearson graduated from the University of Nebraska Medical Center Dental School in 2020, following a Bachelor’s degree in Exercise Science from Creighton University. He has been actively serving in the National Guard’s medical detachment since 2016, where he gained valuable experience in the dental field.

Originally from a farm near Holdrege, Nebraska, Dr. Pearson enjoys spending time with his wife, Megan, and their children, Hayes, Monroe, Grant and Abe. Outside of work, he loves golfing, family time, and helping out on the family farm.

Dr. Ryan Matzen graduated from the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) College of Dentistry in 2020, following a bachelor’s degree in Health Sciences from the University of Nebraska at Kearney. He completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD-1) residency in 2021, further developing his skills in comprehensive dental care.

Dr. Matzen proudly served as a general dentist in the United States Air Force for six years, stationed in Virgina, Texas, and the United Kingdom.

Originally from Fremont, Dr. Matzen enjoys spending time with his wife, Kelsey, and traveling whenever possible. Together, they have visited more than 30 countries and hope to eventually visit all 50 states. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys exploring new places and making memories with family.
