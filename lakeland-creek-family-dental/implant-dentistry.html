What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer several benefits over other tooth replacement options. They can help preserve the jawbone, prevent facial sagging, and allow for normal biting and chewing. Moreover, dental implants are durable and long-lasting, making them a cost-effective solution for missing teeth.
