The practice

Periodontics is one of the seven dental specialties recognized by the American Dental Association. Periodontics is the specialty of dentistry that encompasses the prevention, diagnosis and treatment of the tissues that support and surround teeth or their substitutes (such as Dental Implants). It also maintains the health, function and esthetics of these tissues and structures.
