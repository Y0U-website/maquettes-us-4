Implant Restorations in Fairfield, CA

Dental implant restorations are natural-looking permanent prosthetics anchored securely to dental implants. Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as artificial tooth roots. Implant restorations can replace one or more missing teeth and are custom-made for each patient. Restorations like crowns, bridges, or dentures mimic natural teeth in terms of their look, feel, and function.

Implant restorations offer a sturdy, long-lasting solution for missing teeth, restoring the patient’s ability to chew, speak, and smile confidently. At Waterman Boulevard Family Dentistry, we recommend dental implant restorations as they enhance oral function and promote overall oral health by preventing bone loss and maintaining the integrity of the jawbone structure. They provide a permanent and aesthetically pleasing solution, ensuring patients enjoy a natural-looking smile for years.

The Procedure for Implant Restorations

The procedure for dental implant restorations involves several stages, starting with a comprehensive evaluation of the patient’s oral health and jawbone structure. In the initial phase, dental implants, typically made from biocompatible titanium, are surgically placed into the jawbone. Osseointegration occurs following the placement of the implant, allowing the implant to fuse with the bone and ensure the restoration’s stability and durability.

Once the implants have integrated successfully, abutments, which are small connector posts, are attached to the implants. These abutments serve as support for the artificial teeth. In the final stage, custom-made implant restorations like crowns, bridges, or dentures are meticulously designed to match the patient’s natural teeth and securely affixed to the abutments.

The result is a functional, aesthetically pleasing restoration that replaces missing teeth and rejuvenates the patient’s smile, providing long-term durability and natural-looking results. Regular follow-ups with our dentist in Fairfield, CA, ensure the implants and restorations remain in excellent condition, promoting optimal oral health and confidence.
