Proud Locals

The Smile Company, proudly serving the Salt Lake County community since 2024, is grounded in values of trust, care, and community connection. Dr. Jason Teerlink’s vision for the practice is rooted in a deep commitment to offering high quality, conservative treatments that prioritize long term oral health. We take pride in our local heritage and our reputation as a trusted healthcare provider in Bluffdale. Our practice offers personalized care for each patient, making sure that every visit feels like a step towards a healthier, more confident smile.

Quality Care, Comfortable Cost

Everyone deserves outstanding dental treatment without financial stress. If budget worries are holding you back, we have flexible solutions to keep your smile and your wallet healthy!

Let us know what works best for you; we will ensure cost never stands between you and top-tier dental care.

  • Insurance-friendly: We work with most dental insurance plans to maximize your benefits and minimize out-of-pocket costs.
  • No-insurance savings: Not covered? Our Uninsured Discount automatically takes effect at checkout.
  • Flexible monthly plans: Prefer to spread payments out? We partner with several reputable third-party lenders and can process your credit application right in the office. Qualified patients may even secure interest-free financing.
