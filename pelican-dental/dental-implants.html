Dental Implants

Over time, the jawbone naturally shrinks in areas where teeth are missing because the bone no longer receives stimulation from tooth roots. Dental implants are unique because they help preserve bone health by stimulating the jawbone similarly to natural teeth.

Replacing missing teeth early can help prevent additional oral health problems and maintain the overall structure of your smile.

  • Changes in speech
  • Shifting surrounding teeth
  • Jawbone deterioration
  • Changes in facial structure
  • Increased wear on remaining teeth
  • Lower confidence and self-esteem

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants have become one of the most popular tooth replacement options because of their long-term success and natural feel.

Natural Appearance

Dental implants are custom-designed to blend seamlessly with your smile. The restorations are shaped and shaded to look like natural teeth.

Improved Function

Implants allow you to eat, speak, laugh, and smile confidently without worrying about discomfort or slipping dentures.

Long-Lasting Solution

With proper care and routine dental visits, implants can last for decades and may even last a lifetime for many patients.

Bone Preservation

One of the biggest advantages of implants is their ability to stimulate the jawbone and reduce bone loss after tooth loss.

Comfortable Fit

Because implants become part of the jawbone, they feel much more stable and comfortable than removable appliances.

No Adhesives Required

Implant-supported restorations do not rely on messy adhesives to stay in place.

Protects Nearby Teeth

Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require modifying healthy neighboring teeth.

Better Quality of Life

Many patients report increased confidence, improved eating ability, and greater comfort after receiving dental implants.

What Is the Dental Implant Process Like?

Every patient is unique, but most dental implant treatments follow several key steps.

Consultation and Examination Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive consultation at Pelican Dental. We will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine whether implants are the right solution for you. Digital imaging and advanced diagnostic technology allow us to carefully assess your bone structure and treatment needs.

Treatment Planning We create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your smile. This plan may include: • Implant placement • Tooth extractions, if necessary • Bone grafting • Temporary restorations • Final crowns, bridges, or dentures

Implant Placement The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is, especially with modern techniques and local anesthesia.

Healing Period Over the next several months, the implant integrates with the jawbone. This creates the stable foundation needed for long-term success.

Final Restoration Once healing is complete, your custom restoration is attached to the implant. Your final smile is designed to look natural, function properly, and fit comfortably.
