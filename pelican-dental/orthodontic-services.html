Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment offers benefits that extend far beyond appearance. Patients often experience improvements in oral health, comfort, and overall quality of life.

Improved Oral Health Straight teeth are easier to brush and floss effectively. Better oral hygiene can help reduce the risk of cavities, gum disease, and other dental problems.

Better Bite Function When teeth and jaws are properly aligned, chewing and speaking become easier and more comfortable. A healthy bite can also reduce unnecessary stress on the jaw joints.

Enhanced Appearance A straighter smile can significantly improve confidence and self-esteem. Many patients feel more comfortable smiling in social and professional settings after orthodontic treatment.

Long-Term Protection Proper alignment helps distribute biting forces more evenly, reducing excessive wear on teeth and lowering the risk of fractures and other damage.

Improved Comfort Correcting bite issues can help relieve strain on the jaw and surrounding muscles, improving overall comfort and function.

Traditional Bracket-and-Wire Braces Traditional braces have helped millions of patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles and continue to be one of the most effective orthodontic treatments available today. Modern braces are more comfortable and efficient than ever before, making them an excellent choice for patients of all ages.

Using a system of carefully placed brackets and wires, traditional braces apply gentle, consistent pressure to guide teeth into their ideal positions over time. Because braces work continuously, they can effectively address a wide range of orthodontic concerns, from minor spacing issues to more complex bite problems.

Traditional braces are commonly used to treat:

  • Crowded teeth
  • Crooked teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbites
  • Underbites
  • Crossbites
  • Open bites
  • Complex alignment issues

Invisalign Clear Aligners

For patients seeking a more discreet orthodontic solution, Invisalign offers an innovative alternative to traditional braces.

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made clear aligners that gradually shift teeth into their desired positions. Each set of aligners is worn for a designated period before progressing to the next stage of treatment.

One of the reasons Invisalign has become so popular is its convenience. Because the aligners are removable, patients can continue enjoying their favorite foods and maintain their regular brushing and flossing routines without the restrictions that sometimes accompany traditional braces.

Invisalign may be an excellent option for:

  • Mild to moderate crowding
  • Spacing issues
  • Certain bite concerns
  • Adults and teens seeking a discreet appearance
  • Patients who can consistently wear aligners as directed

Benefits of Invisalign

  • Nearly invisible appearance
  • Removable for eating and cleaning
  • Smooth, comfortable design
  • No metal brackets or wires
  • Fewer dietary restrictions
  • Easy maintenance of oral hygiene

Phase 1 Orthodontics for Children

Orthodontic care doesn’t always begin after all permanent teeth have erupted. In some cases, younger children can benefit from early orthodontic intervention, often referred to as Phase 1 Orthodontics. Phase 1 treatment is designed for children who still have a combination of baby teeth and permanent teeth but show signs that their developing smile may benefit from early guidance. By addressing certain concerns while the jaw is still growing, Phase 1 orthodontics can help create a healthier foundation for future development.

What Is Phase 1 Orthodontics?

Phase 1 orthodontics focuses on guiding the growth and development of the mouth, jaws, and emerging permanent teeth. Rather than waiting until all adult teeth have come in, early treatment can sometimes help prevent more significant problems later.

Depending on your child’s needs, Phase 1 treatment may include:

Every child is different, which is why we carefully evaluate each patient’s development before recommending treatment.

  • Palatal expanders
  • Limited braces
  • Invisalign for younger patients when appropriate
  • Space maintenance
  • Growth guidance appliances

Benefits of Early Orthodontic Treatment

Early intervention can be especially beneficial when orthodontic issues are identified while a child’s mouth is still actively growing.

  • Create additional space for incoming permanent teeth
  • Guide proper jaw growth and development
  • Improve bite alignment
  • Reduce severe crowding
  • Correct certain orthodontic problems before they worsen
  • Potentially simplify future orthodontic treatment

When Should My Child Be Evaluated?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children receive an orthodontic evaluation around age 7. At this age, enough permanent teeth have erupted to identify many developing concerns, even though baby teeth are still present. An early evaluation does not necessarily mean treatment will begin immediately. In many cases, we simply monitor development and recommend the ideal timing for treatment if needed.
