The goal of Patricia Schnur, DMD, and the team at Parrish Children’s Dentistry is to provide high-quality oral health care to children and teenagers living in Parrish, Florida…

The practice

Dr. Schnur leads a patient-focused practice that treats every child like a member of the family. Her years of experience and compassionate demeanor allow her to take the fear out of visiting the dentist. This ensures children and adolescents receive the oral treatment they need in a warm, welcoming environment.

Dr. Schnur offers a wide variety of oral health care services, including treatment of cavities with fillings and sealants, teeth cleanings, tooth extractions, and care for baby teeth. She also educates parents on proper oral hygiene for newborns. For children who need orthodontic care, Dr. Schnur refers them to trusted Orthodontists in the area.

For those who experience dental anxiety or have trouble sitting still, Dr. Schnur offers nitrous oxide. This safe, effective sedative has few side effects and makes it easy for kids to relax and receive the treatment they need.

To receive the best, most comprehensive oral treatments for children, partner with the team at Parrish Children’s Dentistry. Schedule an appointment today. Call the office and speak with a friendly staff member, or book a consultation online.

At Parrish Children s Dentistry, your children’s smiles are important to us! We keep kids happy and healthy by focusing on providing exceptional service to promote bigger, brighter smiles. Our goal is to provide a pleasant and positive experience for both you and your children while focusing on your specific needs.

Patricia Schnur, DMD, or Dr. Patty as her patients know her, offers caring and comprehensive dental care at Parrish Children’s Dentistry in Parrish, Florida. Younger patients can expect expert care in a relaxed environment that takes the fear out of visiting the dentist. Dr. Schnur graduated from the University of Florida College of Dentistry in Gainesville, Florida.

She has over 23 years of experience practicing general dentistry in Manatee and Sarasota counties. After deciding that children and adolescents living north of the river need more options when it comes to choosing a dentist, Dr. Schnur focused her practice on children 18 and under. She looks forward to helping the growing community.
