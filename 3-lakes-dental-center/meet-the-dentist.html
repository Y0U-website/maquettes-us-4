Meet Your Dentist in Tamarac, FL

Ganga Verma, DDS

As a dentist, Dr. Ganga Verma is committed to relieving pain, improving patient wellbeing and restoring smiles that help boost self-confidence. Her goal is to provider her patients with the care they need in a comfortable environment, and she values the ability to build long-term, trusting relationships with patients and their families.

Dr. Verma attended dental school at New York University, graduating in 2013, and regularly completes continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of the dental field. She is Invisalign® Certified.

When not in the office, Dr. Verma enjoys traveling and spending time with her family.
