Airway Orthodontics

Supporting Better Breathing & Sleep

Orthodontics is about more than straight teeth. At our practice, we focus on Airways & Orthodontics—an approach that looks at how jaw development, tooth alignment, and facial growth affect breathing, sleep, and overall health at every stage of life.

Why the Airway Matters

The airway plays a critical role in how the body functions—especially during sleep. Narrow or underdeveloped jaws, crowded teeth, or improper bite alignment can contribute to:

Improving airway space can lead to better oxygen flow, which supports brain development in children and cognitive, cardiovascular, and metabolic health in adults.

  • Mouth breathing
  • Snoring
  • Poor sleep quality
  • Daytime fatigue or difficulty concentrating
  • Sleep-disordered breathing and obstructive sleep apnea

Airway-Focused Orthodontic Care for or All Ages (7+)

Airway health matters at every stage of life. Early orthodontic evaluations allow us to identify developmental concerns while the jaw and facial structures are still growing, helping guide proper growth, support nasal breathing, and reduce the risk of future sleep and breathing issues. Addressing airway concerns early can create more space for permanent teeth, improve facial balance, and support healthier sleep patterns during critical developmental years.

For teens and adults, airway-focused orthodontic care continues to play an important role in overall health. Treatment can help improve jaw position, bite alignment, and airway space—often as part of a collaborative approach to managing snoring or sleep apnea. Our focus goes beyond straight teeth to prioritize function, comfort, and long-term wellness, helping patients breathe better, sleep better, and enjoy a higher quality of life at any age.
