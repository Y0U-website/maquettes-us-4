With virtually no wait times, hours that allow for flexible scheduling, and a friendly atmosphere, Ultimate Dental is a great place for the entire family to receive the dental care necessary for oral health. Convenient Hours

Many miss out on their routine dental care simply because they don’t think they have time for it between work, family, and just taking a moment to breathe. At Ultimate Dental, we’re ready to help patients find appointment times that fit seamlessly into even the most hectic schedule. We have both early and evening appointments available during the week, ensuring that you and your loved ones will never have to miss a moment of anything important simply to take care of your teeth.

Dental Emergencies Welcome

It’s great when you’re able to plan your dental visits weeks ahead of time, but when it comes to a painful toothache or knocked out tooth, it’s best to seek out care as quickly as possible. Whatever your emergency might be, you can trust that we’ll be able to help you here at Ultimate Dental. Dr. Hadad and the Ultimate Dental team and the rest of our team have decades of experience, so we’ll know exactly how to get you out of pain right away and repair your smile.
