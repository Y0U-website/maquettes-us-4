The Benefits Of Airway Orthodontics for Kids in Ramsey

Improved Sleep Quality

Airway Orthodontics works to expand your child&#x27;s airway, reducing the frequency of sleep disordered breathing episodes. This can result in a significant improvement in your child&#x27;s sleep quality, leading to more energy, better concentration, and an overall enhancement in their quality of life.

Better Health Outcomes

Sleep disordered breathing in children has been linked to a range of health issues, including behavioral problems, learning difficulties, and even heart problems. By treating SDB early, Airway Orthodontics can help reduce the risk of these complications, setting your child up for better health outcomes in the future.

Non-Invasive Treatment

Unlike some other treatments for sleep disordered breathing, Airway Orthodontics is non-invasive, meaning it&#x27;s less stressful and more comfortable for your child. The treatment involves the use of appliances like habit correctors, fixed expanders and clear aligners to gently and effectively expand the airway over time.

Our Ramsey Airway Orthodontics Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

During your child&#x27;s initial consultation, we will discuss their symptoms and perform a complimentary airway screening. We will develop a customized treatment plan in order to ensure your child is set up for airway success for the rest of their life.

Appliance delivery

If your child is a suitable candidate for Airway Orthodontics, the next step is the fitting of the appliance. This could be a habit corrector, fixed expander or Invisalign, depending on your child&#x27;s needs. These appliances work to gradually expand your child&#x27;s airway, improving their breathing during sleep.

Regular Check-ups and Adjustments

Once the appliance is fitted, your child will need regular check-ups to monitor their progress and make any necessary adjustments. We also screen patients for myofunctional therapy and tongue tie releases, referring suitable candidates to our trusted network of providers. This comprehensive approach ensures your child receives the best possible care and achieves the best possible results.

What are the signs that my child might need airway orthodontics?

Signs that your child might benefit from airway orthodontics include mouth breathing, snoring, frequent waking during the night, teeth grinding, difficulty concentrating during the day, and a narrow palate or crowded teeth. If your child exhibits any of these symptoms, it&#x27;s a good idea to consult an orthodontist who specializes in airway health to determine if treatment is needed.
