Sleep disordered breathing in children has been linked to a range of health issues, including behavioral problems, learning difficulties, and even heart problems. By treating SDB early, Airway Orthodontics can help reduce the risk of these complications, setting your child up for better health outcomes in the future.

Unlike some other treatments for sleep disordered breathing, Airway Orthodontics is non-invasive, meaning it&#x27;s less stressful and more comfortable for your child. The treatment involves the use of appliances like habit correctors, fixed expanders and clear aligners to gently and effectively expand the airway over time.

During your child&#x27;s initial consultation, we will discuss their symptoms and perform a complimentary airway screening. We will develop a customized treatment plan in order to ensure your child is set up for airway success for the rest of their life.

If your child is a suitable candidate for Airway Orthodontics, the next step is the fitting of the appliance. This could be a habit corrector, fixed expander or Invisalign, depending on your child&#x27;s needs. These appliances work to gradually expand your child&#x27;s airway, improving their breathing during sleep.
