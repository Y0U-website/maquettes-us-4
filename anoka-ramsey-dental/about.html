our core values

Patient First

We are committed to placing the needs and well-being of our patients at the forefront of everything we do. By placing the patient at the center of our practice, we aim to build lasting relationships based on trust, compassion, and exceptional dental care.

We Before Me

We’re all in this together. It’s our collective goal to provide exceptional dental care, deliver outstanding patient experiences, and create a fun and enjoyable place to work.

Authentically Transparent

By embracing authenticity and transparency, we create an environment where trust and confidence thrive. Our commitment to being authentically transparent ensures that our patients feel empowered, informed, and engaged in their dental care journey. We believe that an authentic and transparent approach fosters strong patient relationships, encourages open communication, and leads to successful outcomes in oral health.

Excellence & Accountability

We are committed to delivering excellence in everything we do. We take responsibility for our actions, strive for high-quality outcomes, and constantly seek ways to improve. We hold ourselves and each other accountable for meeting and exceeding expectations

Complete Wellness

You are so much more than your mouth. We recognize that oral health is connected to your overall well-being and aim to provide comprehensive care that goes beyond just fixing teeth.

Celebrate Smiles

We celebrate smiles in all their forms. We believe in embracing the fun side of dentistry. Life’s too short and there’s lots to smile about.

Modern Dentistry for You

iTero Intraoral Scanner

Using a laser, this scanner allows us to take a 3D digital scan of your teeth so we can more accurately tailor treatments and oral appliances to suit your needs.

CEREC Intraoral Scanner

With the wave of a wand, we are able to scan your entire mouth in seconds with precision and speed. This gives us the clearest and most accurate images to create custom crowns and other restorations.

Milling Machine

This high-tech machine is useful for fillings, crowns, and replacing missing teeth. We can use it in combination with intraoral scans to design complete or partial dental restorations.

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

These crowns are custom-made the same day you come in for your restoration , and can last even longer than traditional crowns with proper dental care.

Digital X-Rays

Unlike traditional x-rays, digital x-rays use minimal amounts of radiation for more efficient and safer imaging. They also yield quicker and clearer results.

SingleTooth Anesthesia System

Patients can benefit from this computer assisted system for local anesthesia that minimizes discomfort.
