The practice

Dr. Ryan Rhodes is a South Fork native and a Stony Brook University graduate who received his training at NYU College of Dentistry. He subsequently completed an Advanced Education Program in General Practice Residency (GPR) at Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Rhodes and our team understand that receiving dental care can be stressful for some. A critical aspect of our patient care philosophy is cultivating trusting relationships so you feel comfortable and confident in our expertise.

At our comfortable dental office , you will be surrounded by professionals hand-selected for their friendly and caring natures. Their goal is to provide a patient-centered experience by listening to your concerns and delivering gentle, compassionate care that prioritizes your comfort.

One of the things we enjoy most about what we do is taking the time to nurture long-lasting, trusting relationships with our patients. You deserve to feel confident in the high-quality dentistry you receive and, most importantly, in the professionals who deliver it.

Understanding your needs and preferences over time allows us to anticipate issues and provide recommendations that align with your unique vision for your smile. The result is highly individualized care and top-of-the-line solutions that help you enjoy lifelong oral health.

And, because we realize how busy you are, we offer convenient evening hours to fit your family’s hectic schedule.

Dr. Rhodes has logged over 500 hours of continuing education and is pleased to bring his passion for excellence and the latest dental technology to friends and neighbors in Southampton.
