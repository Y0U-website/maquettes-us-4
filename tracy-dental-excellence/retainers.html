Retainers Tracy, CA

Maintain the success of your orthodontic treatment.

If you’ve had orthodontic treatment then you know how much work went into creating that straight, perfect smile. Don’t let all that hard work go to waste! Wearing a retainer is vital to keeping alignment because otherwise (no matter how many years of braces you’ve had in the past) your teeth will shift due to uneven pressures exerted in the mouth.

We offer several types of retainers depending on what characteristics fit your individual needs.

Hawley Retainers

A Hawley retainer is the most traditional variety of retainer, and it consists of an acrylic molded mouthpiece combined with a metal wire to hold teeth in place. It is usually recommended that patients wear the Hawley retainer continuously for the first 6 months after orthodontic treatment, then only at night thereafter. It can be removed for eating and cleaning, and is the most durable type of retainer.

One of the distinct advantages of the Hawley retainer is that its wires can be bent to make further fine adjustments to the teeth’s final position.

Essix Retainers

An Essix retainer is a clear plastic mold that is worn over the entire set of teeth. Being completely clear, they offer the most discreet way to wear a retainer while also being removable for eating and cleaning. They are also thin enough (.02-.03 inches) that speaking can take place normally after a short adjustment period. The Essix retainer is not quite as durable as the Hawley and can be damaged by heavy teeth grinding (bruxism), so we’ll determine if the Essix retainer is right for you depending on the facts of your specific case.

Fixed Retainers

An entirely different type of retainer is called a fixed retainer. Most often used on lower incisors, the fixed retainer uses a small bar on the inside surface to hold teeth in position. Just as the name implies, the fixed retainer is not able to be removed except with the help of a dental professional. In many cases when there is significant likelihood of regression, these fixed retainers are kept in place for life. Patients with a fixed retainer may need to take extra care to prevent plaque buildup, and a floss threader is often necessary to maintain proper hygiene.

Vivera Retainers ®

Studies show that without retainers straight teeth can gradually shift back towards their initial position. This is a common occurrence with all orthodontic treatment, but is one that can easily be overcome. Wearing Vivera retainers after traditional braces or Invisalign treatment means you are keeping your teeth in their new position and protecting your smile and investment.

Vivera retainers are custom made for you with the same state-of-the-art technology used to make Invisalign aligners. Because the retainers are custom made from an impression or scan of your teeth, you will benefit from a precise fit and therefore more comfortable fit. Vivera’s proprietary material is 30 % stronger, which helps maintain your teeth in the desired position. It also reduces the risk of the retainer cracking or breaking, which means a more durable retainer that you can count on.

Now that you’re at the exciting conclusion of your orthodontic treatment, we’ll discuss which retainer option is best for your individual needs, but most importantly make sure to wear it! Just think, you’ve already made the sacrifice of wearing braces to straighten your teeth, so why throw away all that treatment time by letting your teeth slowly shift? Make sure that your smile remains perfect with a retainer!
