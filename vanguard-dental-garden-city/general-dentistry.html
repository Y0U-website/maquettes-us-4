  • EXTRACTIONS We perform extractions of both wisdom and problem teeth. We have decades of experience removing wisdom teeth from mouths to increase space, eliminate discomfort, and pull other teeth too damaged or diseased to be saved.
  • ROOT CANALS Our root canal process is advanced and painless! Let us treat your infected tooth and show you that root canals do not deserve their negative reputation.
  • DENTAL CLEANING Dental cleaning is crucial to long-term oral health. Have us give your mouth a detailed cleaning and inspection.
  • DENTURES Our custom dentures can improve your quality of life and restore your smile. We offer many denture services, including acrylic dentures, denture relines, dental implants, and more.
  • NIGHT GUARDS Save your mouth against night-time grinding! Our custom night guards can save your teeth against irreversible damage and eliminate your jaw pain, tooth sensitivity, and headaches.
  • CAVITIES Have your cavities treated before they become a more significant problem. We can check for and treat any type of cavity!
  • GUM DISEASE Gum disease is a major cause of bad breath and a serious infection of your gums. Treat your gum disease immediately to prevent damage to your teeth and jawbone.
  • JAW PAIN (TMJ) Temporomandibular Joint Disorder, or TMJ, is a pain in the joint that connects your lower jaw to your skull. This pain can be temporary or last decades. Have us treat it with night guards, medications, or dental work and get relief today.
