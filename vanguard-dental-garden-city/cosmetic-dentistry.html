  • CROWNS Partial or full crowns improve tooth function and aesthetics. Repair chips, restore shape, and cover discolorations with our in-house crowns! We can prepare, create and install your crown in the same session with our advanced equipment and expertise.
  • INVISALIGN If you want straighter teeth but don’t want the hassle and looks of traditional wire braces, Invisalign might be the best option. This process uses a series of customized, clear plastic trays that reconfigure and straighten your teeth over time. Invisalign is typically the same cost as metal braces but is nearly unnoticeable and often more comfortable.
  • LUMINEERS Lumineers are a great alternative to traditional porcelain veneers and can be used to improve your smile permanently and painlessly. This treatment bonds a thin, tooth-colored material to your teeth, covering any chips or stains. Lumineers can last up to 20 years and do not cause any discomfort or sensitivity.
