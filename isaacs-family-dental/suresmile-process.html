What is the SureSmile® Process?

The SureSmile® System, including clear aligners, is a series of custom-made, nearly invisible trays designed to shift teeth into their ideal position gradually. Unlike traditional braces, SureSmile® clear aligners offer a discreet, comfortable, and convenient option for orthodontic treatment. Each aligner is tailored to fit your unique dental structure, ensuring effective and precise results.

Virtually Invisible

SureSmile® clear aligners are clear and nearly invisible, making them a discreet option for teeth straightening. You can smile, speak, and socialize with confidence, knowing your treatment is hardly noticeable.

Custom-Fit Comfort

Each aligner is custom-made to fit your teeth perfectly, ensuring a comfortable and snug fit. No metal brackets or wires mean less irritation and more comfort throughout your treatment.

Removable Convenience

Unlike traditional braces, SureSmile® clear aligners can be easily removed. Thus, you can enjoy the freedom to eat, drink, brush, and floss without any restrictions, maintaining your oral hygiene effortlessly.

Efficient Treatment

The SureSmile® process uses advanced technology to create a precise treatment plan, often resulting in faster results compared to traditional braces. Enjoy a streamlined process that fits your lifestyle.

The SureSmile® Process

Initial Consultation

Your journey to a beautiful smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Isaacs Family Dental . During this visit, our experienced dental professionals will assess your dental health, discuss your smile goals, and determine if the SureSmile® System is the right option.

Custom Treatment Plan

Once you decide to proceed with the SureSmile® process, our team will take detailed digital scans of your teeth. Using state-of-the-art 3D imaging technology, we will create a personalized treatment plan showing your teeth’ step-by-step movement over time. You’ll even get a preview of your expected results before starting the treatment.

Receiving Your Aligners

With your custom treatment plan in place, we’ll fabricate a series of clear aligners tailored to your specific needs. You’ll wear each set of aligners typically for 20-22 hours per day for about two weeks. Each set of aligners will gradually move your teeth into the desired position. Regular check-ins with our dental team will ensure that your treatment is on track and make any necessary adjustments.

Achieving Your Dream Smile

After completing the series of SureSmile® clear aligners, you’ll achieve a beautifully aligned smile that you can feel proud of. To maintain your new smile, you’ll receive custom SureSmile® Retainers to wear at night, which helps keep your teeth in their new position.

Enhance Your Treatment Journey with SureSmile® VPro™

Isaacs Family Dental offers the power of a small device that significantly impacts your orthodontic treatment. SureSmile® VPro™ is a convenient at-home device designed to be used for just 5 minutes a day alongside your aligners. It employs gentle vibrations to enhance the fit of your aligners, potentially reducing discomfort and accelerating your path to a stunning new smile. Utilizing SureSmile® VPro™ for a brief daily session can help ensure that your aligners fit snugly and effectively, keeping your treatment progress on track.

Unleash the potential of your orthodontic journey with SureSmile® VPro™. This game-changing device, with its straightforward yet powerful technology, can transform your treatment experience. By enhancing comfort and efficiency, the Isaacs Family Dental team and SureSmile® VPro™ bring you one step closer to the smile you’ve always dreamed of.

Illuminate Your Smile at the End SureSmile®Process

As you reach the end of your treatment with SureSmile®, Isaacs Family Dental can offer you a SureSmile® Whitening Kit to ensure your new smile shines brilliantly. Designed for professional results in the comfort of your home, this kit provides everything you need to achieve a brighter, whiter smile that complements your newfound confidence.

With just 15 minutes of use, you’ll notice remarkable results, enhancing the brilliance of your smile and leaving you feeling radiant inside and out. Say goodbye to any lingering discoloration and hello to a smile that truly dazzles.

Maintain Your Radiant Smile with SureSmile® Retainers

Your smile is an investment worth protecting, and SureSmile® Retainers are here to help. When used as prescribed by Isaacs Family Dental , these retainers play a crucial role in keeping your teeth in their newly aligned positions, ensuring the longevity of your smile.

Crafted with your comfort in mind, SureSmile® Retainers are designed to be worn consistently for 12 months. Their durable construction withstands teeth grinding without compromising their effectiveness, giving you peace of mind knowing that your smile is in good hands.

SureSmile® Retainers are essential to maintaining the results of the SureSmile® process.
