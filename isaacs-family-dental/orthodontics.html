Common Questions About Orthodontics

Which type of orthodontics is best?

Although there are several types of orthodontics available today, at Isaacs Family Dental we believe that the SureSmile® system offers our patients the best of the best. This method uses clear plastic teeth aligners to straighten crooked teeth rather than metal brackets and wires. It’s a comfortable, discreet, and quicker path to a more beautiful smile.

How much do orthodontics cost?

Each patient is different, and therefore each orthodontic case is different. At your initial consultation, we can work up a treatment plan and give you an accurate estimate of the cost of your orthodontic care. We’ll also answer any questions you have so you can make the right decision for your situation.

Can orthodontics help TMJ pain?

Orthodontic care can sometimes help relieve TMJ pain. By correcting your bite, we can take tension off your TMJs, which are situated on either side of your head, and help your jaw feel better. During your consultation, we can discuss your TMJ pain and determine whether orthodontics or another type of treatment will work better for you.
