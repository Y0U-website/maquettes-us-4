How Does SureSmile® Work?

The SureSmile® process is simple, predictable, and stress-free.

We use Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) to capture a comprehensive 3D model of your bite, including your teeth, roots, and surrounding tissues.

Virtual simulations show how your teeth will shift, so you can preview your future smile before treatment begins.

Your personalized plan is sent to SureSmile®’s U.S. facility, where aligners are crafted to match your specifications.

You’ll wear aligners 20–22 hours per day, switching to a new set every 1–2 weeks. Gentle pressure gradually moves your teeth into their proper position.

Our team in Indianapolis or Zionsville will monitor progress with periodic visits and provide updated aligners.

Once your treatment is complete, we provide retainers to protect your results for years to come.

FAQs About SureSmile®

How long does SureSmile® treatment take?

Many people worry that orthodontic treatment will take years. With SureSmile®, results often appear in just weeks. Most patients complete treatment in 6–18 months, and our team will provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Orthodontics isn’t just for kids. SureSmile® aligners are clear, discreet, and designed to fit into busy professional or social lives. Many adults in Indianapolis and Zionsville choose SureSmile® for a straighter smile without the look of braces.

Cost is one of the biggest concerns for families. Many insurance plans cover part of the aligner treatment, and our office helps maximize those benefits. For patients without coverage, we also provide flexible payment options and membership savings.

Some patients expect aligners to feel like traditional braces, but SureSmile® is far gentler. You may notice slight pressure when starting a new set, but it fades quickly. Most patients adjust within a few days.

Can SureSmile® fix issues like overbites or crossbites?

Clear aligners aren’t limited to simple spacing problems. SureSmile® can correct a wide range of concerns, including crowding, gaps, overbites , underbites, open bites, and crossbites. During your consultation, we’ll design a plan tailored to your specific needs.

Aligners can collect bacteria and odors if not cared for properly. Rinse them with lukewarm water, brush gently with a soft toothbrush, and avoid hot water that could warp the material. Our team will provide you with a comprehensive care guide when you start treatment.

What happens after SureSmile® treatment ends?

Straight teeth can shift over time without maintenance. After treatment, retainers are provided to maintain your smile’s position, and ongoing preventive checkups at our Indianapolis or Zionsville office will help protect your long-term results.
