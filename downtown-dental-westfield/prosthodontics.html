  • 1. What is prosthodontics? Prosthodontics is a dental specialty focused on restoring and replacing missing or damaged teeth using prosthetic solutions like crowns, bridges, dentures, and implants.
  • 2. What types of treatments does a prosthodontist offer? A prosthodontist provides treatments such as dental crowns, bridges, dentures, veneers, full-mouth reconstruction, and implant-supported restorations.
  • 3. When should I see a prosthodontist? You should see a prosthodontist if you have missing teeth, need complex dental restorations, or are considering options like dentures, crowns, or implants for improved function and aesthetics.
  • 4. How is prosthodontic care different from general dentistry? While general dentists handle routine care, prosthodontists specialize in complex restorations and cosmetic improvements, often requiring advanced training and techniques.
  • 5. Are prosthodontic treatments covered by insurance? Coverage for prosthodontic treatments varies by insurance provider and plan. Our team can help you understand your benefits and provide cost estimates during your consultation.
  • What does a prosthodontist do? A prosthodontist focuses on restoring and replacing teeth with crowns, bridges, dentures, and implants. Dr. Paul Zhivago, DDS, FACP is a Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists practicing in Westfield, NJ.
  • What options exist for replacing missing teeth? Options include dental implants, bridges, and dentures, depending on how many teeth are missing and your bone health. Downtown Dental tailors full mouth teeth replacement plans individually.
