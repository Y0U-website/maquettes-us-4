About Us

0 Our Philosophy Inclusive Comfort Quality At Downtown Dental in Westfield, NJ, we recognize that tens of millions of Americans delay or avoid dental visits each year according to the American Dental Association. This drives our commitment to creating a welcoming environment where every patient feels comfortable seeking care.

We also understand that dental anxiety is common — Our spa-like atmosphere and comfort-focused approach are designed to help anxious patients receive the care they need.

According to the ADA Health Policy Institute (2024), 100 million Americans fail to see a dentist each year. At Downtown Dental in Westfield, NJ, our modern, welcoming, individualized approach is designed to help patients return to comfortable, consistent care.

Downtown Dental is a place for everyone. Just interested in regular check-ups or cleanings, a cosmetic evaluation or extensive work that requires restorative and surgical specialists? It’s all here under one roof.

We will make you as comfortable as possible in our facilities and you will feel relaxed and at ease with our friendly staff and skillful doctors.

We use state of the art technology to achieve optimal dental care by our specialized team of dental providers to reach the level of quality we strive for and you deserve.
