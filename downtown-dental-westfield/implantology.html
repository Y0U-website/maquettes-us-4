  • 2. Who is a good candidate for dental implants? Most healthy adults with sufficient jawbone density and good oral hygiene are suitable candidates for single or full mouth dental implants. A consultation with our dentist will determine your eligibility.
  • 3. How long do dental implants last? Dental implants can provide decades of reliable function with proper care. A systematic review found a cumulative survival rate of approximately 96.4 % at 10 years, per the Journal of Dentistry (2019) .
  • 4. Is the dental implant procedure painful? The procedure is generally not painful as it’s performed under local anesthesia. Some mild discomfort may be experienced afterward, which can be managed with medication.
