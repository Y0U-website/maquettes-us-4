Life Outside the Office

Originally from New Jersey, Dr. Ellenberg has lived in Pinellas County for over 30 years. He and his wife have three wonderful children—Ellis, Landon, and Miya—as well as two dogs, Pinto and Coa.

When he’s not at the office, he enjoys spending time with his family, reading, and relaxing by the pool. A big sports fan, he attends Tampa Bay Rays and Buccaneers games and loves playing fantasy baseball with his son.
