Privacy Policy

Edward Street Dental, P.C. respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle the information you choose to share with us through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the details you provide, typically your name, email address, phone number, and your message. We only ask for what we need to respond to your inquiry.

How we use it

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your question or request and to schedule and provide dental care. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Protected health information

Please do not send sensitive medical details through the website form. Any protected health information you share with our office is handled in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including HIPAA.

Third-party services

Our website may use trusted third-party services (for example, embedded maps or spam protection) that have their own privacy policies. Following an external link means that site’s policy, not ours, applies.
