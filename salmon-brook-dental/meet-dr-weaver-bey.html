Get to Know Dr. Weaver-Bey

Talented Dentist Granby

Dr. Weaver-Bey has completed two years of a General Practice Residency at St. Francis Hospital in Hartford, CT. Due to an exceptional performance during her first year, she was awarded The William J Rieger DDS Award in recognition of her motivation for and superlative skills in Clinical Comprehensive Dentistry. She was then named Chief Resident at the start of her second year of residency. Dr. Weaver-Bey has practiced both in the private practice setting as well as the hospital setting as an attending at the St. Francis Hospital General Practice Residency.

While Dr. Weaver-Bey is passionate about all facets of dentistry, she is certified in Invisalign, and has a special interest in Aesthetic Dentistry and surgery.

Her professional affiliations include: The American Dental Association, The Massachusetts Dental Society, and The Valley District Dental Society. Dr. Weaver-Bey is licensed to practice dentistry in the state of Massachusetts and Connecticut.

Dr. Weaver-Bey loves dogs, spending time with family, is an avid RunDisney athlete, and Justin Timberlake fan!

Dr. Weaver-Bey’s Educational Background

Dr. Weaver-Bey’s journey into dentistry began with a strong academic foundation, starting at Smith College. This initial phase of her education provided her with a broad liberal arts background, fostering critical thinking and a well-rounded perspective before she specialized in the sciences.

Following her undergraduate studies, Dr. Weaver-Bey pursued advanced scientific training through the Pre-health Post-baccalaureate Program at the University of Vermont in Burlington, VT. This program equipped her with the necessary prerequisites and a deeper understanding of biological sciences, preparing her for the rigorous demands of dental school.

Dr. Weaver-Bey’s Residency and Clinical Achievements

Dr. Weaver-Bey honed her practical skills and clinical judgment during a comprehensive two-year General Practice Residency at St. Francis Hospital in Hartford, CT. This intensive training allowed her to gain extensive experience across various dental disciplines and patient care scenarios.

Her dedication and proficiency during her residency were recognized with The William J Rieger DDS Award. This prestigious accolade was bestowed upon her for her exceptional motivation and superlative skills in Clinical Comprehensive Dentistry, highlighting her commitment to providing high-quality, holistic dental care.
