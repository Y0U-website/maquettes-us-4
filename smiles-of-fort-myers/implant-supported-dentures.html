What to Expect During Treatment

Getting implant-supported dentures is a multi-step process, and knowing the timeline ahead of time makes it feel more manageable.

Implant Placement

Implants are surgically secured within their designated locations inside the jaw.

Healing Period

Over 6 to 9 months, the implants fuse with the bone. A temporary denture may be worn.

Abutment or Bar Placement

Implants are re-exposed and connective hardware is secured to them.

Final Denture Fitting

Your final denture, containing connective hardware, is fitted for easy attachment.

Most patients find the waiting period between surgery and final fitting is the longest part of the process, not the appointments themselves.

Service Overview

Losing all of your natural teeth does not mean settling for a denture that shifts every time you eat or speak. There are two types of dentures, full and partial, and both traditionally rest on the gums without any anchor holding them in place, which is part of why so many denture wearers deal with slipping, sore spots, and a shorter list of foods they feel comfortable eating.

At Smiles of Fort Myers, we offer dental implants as the foundation for a denture that stays put. Dr. Victor Galvan completed advanced training in implant dentistry through the AAID MaxiCourse, so patients across Fort Myers, FL who are missing all of their upper or lower teeth can have a denture anchored by titanium implants rather than one that rests on the gums alone.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are anchored in place by titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone instead of resting on the gums unsupported like a traditional denture. This keeps the denture from slipping or loosening while you eat, speak, or laugh, and many patients find they can chew more foods comfortably than they could with a conventional denture. About 15 percent of adults 65 and older have no remaining natural teeth, according to the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research , making a secure, comfortable full-arch solution a common need rather than a rare one.
