What Getting an Implant at Our Fort Myers Office Involves

Treatment happens in stages, and knowing what to expect ahead of time makes the process feel less unfamiliar.

Consultation and Imaging

We review your dental history and take images to map your bone structure.

Implant Placement

The implant post is placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or IV sedation.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the following months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone.

Final Restoration

A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached, completing your new smile.

Most of the appointments in this process are shorter than patients expect, and we walk through the full timeline before any treatment begins.

A missing tooth changes more than your smile. It can shift the way you chew, place extra strain on the teeth next door, and slowly reshape the jawbone underneath, so many people put off doing anything about it because they assume the only options are a removable denture or a bridge that relies on healthy teeth for support.

At Smiles of Fort Myers, we recommend dental implants whenever a patient is a good candidate, because an implant replaces the missing tooth root instead of only covering the gap above the gumline. How Dental Implants Work

Implants Compared With Dentures and Bridges

Patients considering tooth replacement usually weigh implants against a removable denture or a fixed bridge, and the right fit depends on how many teeth are missing and the condition of the surrounding bone and teeth.

Because every mouth heals differently, the option that fits you best is easiest to confirm during a one-on-one evaluation rather than decided from a chart alone.

Implant Solutions We Offer
