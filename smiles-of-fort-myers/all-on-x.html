What Is All-on-X Full Arch Restoration?

All-on-X is a full arch dental implant technique that anchors a complete set of fixed teeth onto as few as four to six titanium implants. For Fort Myers patients, it replaces an entire upper or lower row of missing or failing teeth with a permanent, natural-looking smile, usually without the bone grafting older methods require.

Because the implants are angled to take advantage of your strongest available bone, most patients skip bone grafts and sinus lifts. That makes treatment faster, more comfortable, and more affordable than traditional full mouth options. The result is a lightweight, fixed bridge built from premium materials that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

Unlike removable dentures, All-on-X teeth never come out. There is no slipping, no clicking, and no adhesive, just a Fort Myers smile that stays exactly where it belongs.
