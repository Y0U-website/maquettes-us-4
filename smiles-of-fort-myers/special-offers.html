Meet Dr. Orta

Dr. Orta’s areas of expertise include oral surgery, endodontic treatments, periodontics, operative, and restorative dentistry. Committed to staying abreast of the latest developments in dentistry and advancing her clinical expertise, Dr. Orta regularly participates in continuing education. Areas in which she has pursued advanced training include endodontics, oral rehabilitation, pain management, and infection control.

Patients appreciate Dr. Orta’s compassionate approach and commitment to building a trusting relationship with each patient. Patient communication is at the forefront of Dr. Orta’s clinical approach. She believes in taking the time to ensure patients understand not only the treatment recommendations but the reasons behind them as well as actions the patient can take to ensure the best possible outcome. An excellent communicator, Dr. Orta is effective at using the appropriate presentation style for a range of patients including children, the elderly, and those with health issues.

Meet Dr. Galvan

Known for his calm, caring presence, Dr. Galvan helps patients feel at ease, especially those who experience dental anxiety. Meet Dr. Schulert

Joining our team in 2026, Dr. Devan Schulert brought several years of experience within our dental group to his new home at Smiles of Fort Myers. Practicing at our sister offices, including Lee Dental Care and Estero Bay Dental, he built a well-earned reputation for compassionate, high-quality dentistry.

Known for his calm, friendly demeanor, Dr. Schulert takes time to listen to each patient’s concerns, explain options clearly, and create treatment plans tailored to individual needs. His approachable style makes even anxious patients feel confident and at ease.
