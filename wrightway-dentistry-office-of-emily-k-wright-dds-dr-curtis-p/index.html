We know how important it is to find a dentist who listens, takes their time, and truly cares about building lasting relationships. At WrightWay Dentistry, we prioritize transparency, personal connections, and patient-centered care, ensuring you feel heard and valued at every visit.

We understand the need for a dependable dentist who can care for every member of your family. At WrightWay Dentistry, we provide comprehensive, family-focused care, ensuring all ages receive the attention and treatment they deserve.

Dr. Emily and Dr. Curt at WrightWay Dentistry provide comprehensive family dentistry, offering thorough teeth cleanings and preventive care to maintain oral health and avoid future issues. They collaborate with each patient to develop personalized plans that protect and enhance smiles for the entire family.

Dr. Curt brings over a decade of experience in emergency dentistry, expertly handling urgent dental needs with skill and compassion. Equipped to provide effective, timely care, he ensures patients receive relief and treatment when emergencies arise.

Dr. Emily and Dr. Curt offer a full range of cosmetic dentistry services, including teeth whitening, veneers, and more, to help you achieve a radiant and confident smile. Their treatments deliver noticeable results while maintaining the health and natural beauty of your teeth.

We are a husband-and-wife dental team dedicated to our motto: "Treat others the way you would want to be treated." With over 10 years of experience in the Charlotte area, we are proud to call this vibrant community our home.

As parents of two young, active boys, you might spot us cheering at a T-ball game or exploring the outdoors. We understand the importance of balance in today’s busy world, and we strive to bring that balance into our dental practice.

Our passion for dentistry is matched only by our commitment to providing Huntersville with an outstanding dental retreat. We look forward to serving you and your family, making every visit a positive experience!
