Missing Lower Posterior Teeth Affecting Daily Comfort and Function

During the consultation, several tooth replacement options were discussed, including removable restorations and fixed solutions. The patient wanted a treatment option that would feel stable, function naturally, and support long-term oral health. After reviewing the benefits of various approaches, posterior dental implants in Manteca, CA, were chosen as the preferred treatment.

The patient appreciated the idea of replacing missing teeth with restorations designed to mimic natural tooth roots and support long-term bite stability. Because the missing teeth were located in the posterior region of the mouth, restoring chewing efficiency and strength was an important priority throughout treatment planning.

Detailed Digital Planning for Precise Implant Placement

Dr. Mircea Mitu and the team at our office began the process with comprehensive digital treatment planning. Advanced digital imaging and records were used to carefully evaluate the patient’s jawbone structure, spacing, and overall oral health before surgery.

Using these records, the precise positioning of each implant was mapped out to ensure optimal placement for long-term stability and function. A customized surgical guide was then created to improve placement accuracy during the implant procedure. This guided approach enabled the treatment team to plan around key anatomical structures while supporting optimal restorative outcomes.

Digital planning also helped improve communication throughout the process. The patient better understood the recommended treatment sequence, the expected healing stages, and how the final restorations would restore both appearance and function. Careful preparation at this stage played an important role in creating a predictable and efficient implant experience.

Implant Placement and Digitally Manufactured Restorations

Once treatment planning was complete, the dental implants were surgically placed into the jawbone in the areas of the missing posterior teeth. The implants were positioned using the custom surgical guide developed during the planning phase, helping ensure precision and consistency during the procedure.

Following implant placement, the healing and integration process began. Over time, the implants fused naturally with the jawbone through osseointegration, creating a strong and durable foundation for replacement teeth.

After adequate healing, custom abutments and crowns were fabricated using a fully digital workflow. This advanced digital process allowed the restorations to be designed with careful attention to fit, bite alignment, comfort, and aesthetics. Because the missing teeth were located in the back of the mouth, it was especially important to create restorations capable of handling everyday chewing forces while still looking natural alongside surrounding teeth.

Throughout the process, the patient remained informed and comfortable with each phase of treatment. The digital approach streamlined many steps while supporting highly customized results.

Restored Function, Stability, and Confidence with Posterior Dental Implants

The final crowns were successfully delivered and cemented onto the implant abutments, completing the patient’s smile restoration. With the new posterior dental implants in place, the patient regained improved chewing ability, better bite support, and renewed confidence in daily activities.

The completed restorations blended naturally with the surrounding teeth while providing the stability and durability expected from implant-supported dentistry. Most importantly, the treatment restored comfort and functionality in an area that plays a critical role in everyday eating and long-term oral health.

This case highlights how advanced digital planning and implant technology can successfully replace multiple missing posterior teeth, delivering natural-looking, long-lasting results.
