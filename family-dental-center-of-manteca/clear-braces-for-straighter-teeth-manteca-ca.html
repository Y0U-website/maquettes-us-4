Can I get dental braces to straighten my teeth if I already have a dental implant?

Yes. Having a dental implant does not automatically prevent you from receiving orthodontic treatment. Dental implants differ from natural teeth in that they are permanently fused to the jawbone and cannot move. However, the natural teeth around the implant can still be repositioned with braces. Through careful treatment planning, your dentist can improve the alignment of your smile while working around the implant’s fixed position. Many patients complete orthodontic treatment even after receiving one or more dental implants.

Will braces move my dental implant during treatment?

No. Dental implants remain securely anchored in the jawbone and do not respond to the forces created by braces. Natural teeth move because they are attached to the bone by the periodontal ligament, which allows gradual movement. Since implants lack this ligament, they remain in place throughout treatment. Your orthodontic plan will be designed around the implant to ensure your bite functions properly and your teeth align as effectively as possible once treatment is complete.

Should I get braces before receiving a dental implant?

In many cases, orthodontic treatment is completed before implant placement. Braces can create the proper amount of space for a future implant and help position neighboring teeth more accurately. Once the teeth are aligned and the bite is balanced, the implant can often be placed in a more ideal location. This approach may improve both the appearance and function of your smile while helping ensure long-term success for the implant restoration.
