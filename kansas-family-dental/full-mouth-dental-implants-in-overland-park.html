Frequently Asked Questions About Total Teeth Replacement

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from durable titanium. We place them directly into the jawbone to create a strong, stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. Our professionals use this method to replace missing teeth with results that look and feel natural and are just as functional. For patients with more extensive tooth loss, we offer full-mouth dental implants in Overland Park—an advanced, long-lasting solution that can restore your smile, your bite, and your confidence.

How long do dental implants last?

When cared for properly, dental implants can last 20 years. At Kansas Family Dental, we focus on long-term success by using high-quality materials and customizing every treatment plan to fit your needs. Our dental implants especially benefit patients seeking a durable, stable solution to widespread tooth loss. With consistent at-home hygiene and regular dental visits, you can help you protect your investment and enjoy a confident, healthy smile for years.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with good oral hygiene and adequate jawbone density can qualify for dental implants. During your consultation, we evaluate your overall health, review your dental history, and assess your bone structure using advanced imaging. getting full-mouth dental implants in Overland Park may be an ideal solution if you’re missing several teeth or struggling with uncomfortable dentures. We look for candidates committed to maintaining oral health, as long-term success depends on regular care and follow-up.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients experience mild discomfort. We use local anesthesia to keep the area fully numb during the procedure and offer IV sedation options. When placing dental implants, our team takes every step to ensure you feel comfortable and informed throughout the process. After the procedure, most patients manage soreness with over-the-counter pain relievers and return to normal activities within a day or two.

Will my implants look natural?

Your implants will look and feel completely natural. We custom design each restoration to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth, ensuring a seamless and confident smile. Whether you’re replacing one tooth or restoring your entire bite with full-mouth dental implants in Overland Park, our goal is to create results that blend beautifully with your natural features. We use advanced equipment and techniques to ensure your new smile is functional and aesthetically pleasing.
