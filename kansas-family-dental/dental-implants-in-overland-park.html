Are dental implants painful?

Most patients feel little to no pain during the procedure thanks to local anesthesia and optional sedation. Mild soreness and swelling are normal for the first 24 to 48 hours and are easily managed with the post-operation instructions we provide.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

If you’re missing one or more teeth, or you’ve been told a bridge or denture is your only option, there’s a good chance implants are worth exploring. The truth is, there’s no one-size-fits-all answer. That’s why we start with a conversation, not a conclusion.

Your provider looks at the health of your gums, the strength of your jawbone, and listens to you speak about your goals for your smile. For some patients, we can place their dental implants in Overland Park right away. Others may need a little prep work, like a bone graft or extraction. Either way, we’ll walk you through your options with honesty and heart.

What does the implant journey look like at Kansas Family Dental?

We are proud to offer complete start-to-finish dental implant treatments right in our office. This means you experience better coordination, convenience, and continuity of care without dealing with the headache of specialist referrals. At a glance, receiving dental implants in Overland Park involves the following streamlined steps:

  • Consultation & Planning Your journey to a restored smile begins with a thorough examination of your teeth. We take images and digital impressions using state-of-the-art tools to build your personalized treatment plan, bringing you closer to a healthier smile. We make sure all your questions get answered and schedule your implant placement appointment.
  • Implant Placement We insert a small titanium post into your jawbone. This acts as a root for your new tooth. After this procedure, we will send you home with thorough after-care instructions. You shouldn’t experience much downtime. We ask that you take it easy for 24 to 48 hours, avoiding exercise and heavy lifting.
  • Healing & Integration Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process known as osseointegration.
  • Abutment & Crown Placement When you visit us for your final appointment, we place an attachment referred to as an abutment to your implant. This attachment connects your implant to the custom-made crown we design for you. Your implant is now held in place, and your journey with dental implants in Overland Park is complete! We equip you with tips and tools to help you take proper care of your implant, ensuring long-lasting results.

Can I be sedated during my dental implant treatment?

Yes, we offer sedation for dental implant treatments! We know how important it is to feel safe and comfortable during more complex treatments like getting dental implants in Overland Park.

A CRNA (certified registered nurse anesthetist) administers IV sedation and is present for the entirety of the treatment. You’re always in the hands of experts at Kansas Family Dental.

Can I really get new teeth in a day?

In many cases, yes . If you’re a candidate for full arch replacement, we can often extract remaining teeth, place your dental implants, and deliver a functional set of teeth that same day, or the next.

The streamlined and efficient process often surprises patients. This isn’t something every office can offer, and for many, it’s a life-changing alternative to long delays and temporary solutions. That’s why so many choose to work with us for their dental implants in Overland Park.

Will I need to take time off work after my implant?

We recommend that most patients take it easy for 24 to 48 hours after implant placement. You may feel some tenderness or swelling, but it’s often manageable with basic aftercare.

Because we perform implant restoration in-house, you won’t need to schedule multiple visits across town. No matter if you’re receiving a single implant or full All-on-4® treatment, we’ll help you plan a timeline that fits your life.

When can I expect to see my final results of my dental implant?

Since it takes time for the implant to integrate into your jawbone, you can expect to see your final results in 3 to 6 months. Once we place the abutment and crown during your final appointment, you’ll experience the look and feel of a new tooth!

Can I get my existing dental implant crown restored at Kansas Family Dental?

Yes! We offer full restoration in-house. There’s no need for referrals.
