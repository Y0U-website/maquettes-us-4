Frequently Asked Questions About Routine Dental Cleanings

What happens during a dental cleaning at Kansas Family Dental?

When you visit us for a routine teeth cleaning in Overland Park, you can expect to be greeted warmly by our front desk team upon arrival. If you’re a first-time patient at Kansas Family Dental, this appointment is not just a chance to clean your teeth. It’s also an opportunity for us to get to know you and gain a better understanding of your dental goals.

At a glance, here’s what you can expect during your routine cleaning:

  • Checkup During a checkup, your expert dentist will inspect your teeth and gums for any signs of decay or other oral health problems. We will take necessary X-rays to gain a complete look at your overall dental health.
  • Cleaning Next, your dental hygienist will use specialized tools to remove plaque and tartar buildup on your teeth. They polish them to give you a radiant and healthy smile.
  • Treatment Plan After gaining a better understanding of your oral health and your personal goals, we might make recommendations to help you achieve your goals and optimal health outcomes. Sometimes, patients need other treatments beyond a teeth cleaning in Overland Park. Thankfully, you’re in the hand of skilled providers who can take care of most treatments all under one roof.

How often should I get a dental cleaning?

Patients at Kansas Family Dental typically schedule their routine dental cleanings every 6 months. During your visit, we evaluate your teeth for any signs of decay or gum disease. If we notice any signs of gum disease, we may have you come in sooner for a deep cleaning.

Whatever the case, we work within your budget and always recommend what is necessary to maintain your oral health and overall well-being. From restorative treatments to oral surgery , we are here to make sure you receive the care you need.

Do routine dental cleanings help prevent gum disease?

Yes, absolutely! When you visit us for a dental cleaning, you’re in the hands of experts who reach the hard-to-reach places in your mouth. Your first line of defense against tooth decay and gum disease starts with a teeth cleaning in Overland Park.
