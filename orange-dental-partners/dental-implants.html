Consultation and Preparation

If you are considering getting dental implants , it is important to understand what to expect during the consultation and how to prepare for the treatment.

During an in-depth dental implant consultation, your dental professional will assess the health of your teeth, gums, and jawbone to determine if you are a suitable candidate for implants or other innovative tooth replacement options. The examination is comprehensive, analyzing every aspect of your oral health, including any relevant medical history.

Cone-Beam Computed Tomography

Cone-Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is an advanced imaging technique for dental implant placement to evaluate the jaw and teeth. CBCT uses a cone-shaped X-ray beam to capture 3D images of the mouth, providing a detailed view of the teeth and surrounding structures. This technology allows dentists to accurately plan the implant placement and identify any potential problems that may affect the outcome of the procedure. CBCT also helps to determine the best location for implant placement, reducing the risk of complications and ensuring optimal results.

Results, Recovery, and Aftercare

The results of dental implant surgery can be seen immediately after the procedure is completed. However, it takes several months for the implant to fully integrate with the jawbone before it can be used as a support for a crown or bridge. You should avoid putting too much pressure on the implant site for a period of time.

The recovery period following dental implant surgery varies from patient to patient . Most people experience some swelling and discomfort in the days after their procedure, but this should subside within a week or two. You may also experience some bruising around your gums or face that will fade over time. It’s important to follow your post-operative care instructions during this period to ensure proper healing and reduce the risk of infection.

Dental Implants Frequently Asked Questions

Are Dental Implants painful?

No, dental implants are not painful . Most patients report that implant surgery is similar to getting a filling and only experience some minimal discomfort afterward.

What are the risks associated with Dental Implants?

Dental implants are generally safe and have a high success rate , but as with any surgical procedure, there are risks involved. It’s important to discuss this during your consultation.

Are Dental Implants permanent?

If you’re looking for a lasting solution to missing or damaged teeth , dental implants are the answer. Not only are they surgically implanted directly into your jawbone, but with good care and maintenance, these implants can last an entire lifetime.

Do Dental Implants look and feel natural?

Yes, dental implants are designed to look and feel like natural teeth . They are custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your existing teeth so they blend seamlessly into your smile.

How much do Dental Implants cost?

Dental implant costs can vary depending on several factors , such as the number of implants needed and the type of implant used.
