Benefits of Receiving Implant Dentures

There are numerous advantages of implant dentures in Lithonia, which make them the ideal option for individuals who need to have missing teeth replaced:

  • Increased Comfort: As implant dentures are attached to implants within your jawbone, they will not shift or slide around as regular dentures could. Therefore, you can eat, smile, and talk without concern for them moving.
  • Improved Chewing Power: With implant dentures, you will be able to chew a wide range of foods. The firm link between the implants and your dentures provides more chewing force than with normal dentures.
  • Long-Term Solution: Implant dentures are meant to last for several years. The dental implants are constructed from long-lasting materials such as titanium, which integrate with your bone over time and give your dentures a solid foundation.
  • Natural Appearance: Implant dentures are designed to look exactly like your natural teeth. If cared for properly, they will restore your smile, allowing you to feel confident in showing off your teeth at any time.
  • Preserve Your Jawbone: Dental implants stimulate your jawbone, reducing bone loss that can happen with missing teeth. This preserves the shape of your face and your oral health.
