The Process of Getting a Single Implant Crown

Getting a single implant crowns near you involves a few simple steps:

Consultation and Planning

We begin with a thorough examination to ensure you are a good candidate for an implant. We will discuss your needs, take x-rays, and create a treatment plan tailored to you.

Implant Placement

During a short surgical procedure, the implant post is placed into your jawbone. You may experience some discomfort after the procedure, but this will go away after a few days.

Healing Time

It usually takes a few months for the implant to fully integrate with your bone. During this time, the implant becomes stable and strong enough to hold the crown.

Attaching the Crown

Once your implant has healed, we will place a custom-made crown on top of the implant post. The crown will match your natural teeth and complete your smile.
