Dr. Aundrea Vereen Eady

DMD

Dr. Aundrea Vereen Eady was born and raised in West Palm Beach, Florida. She attended Spelman College where she earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology. After Spelman, she spent 8 years in Boston, Massachusetts where she matriculated at Tufts University School of Dentistry earning her dental degree and certification in Prosthodontics.

Prosthodontists are masters of complete oral rehabilitation and are dedicated to the highest standards of care in the restoration, enhancement, and replacement of teeth. Dr. Eady continues to hold a faculty appointment at Tufts University in Boston, MA and enjoys traveling with her husband, running and spin class.

Dr. Eady is a member of the American College of Prosthodontics, National Dental Association, North Georgia Dental Society, American Dental Association and the Seattle Study Club.
