If you are missing a tooth or several teeth, it can become harder to eat, speak, and smile. Progressive Dental Group provides dental implants in Lithonia, GA, to restore your entire smile. Dental implants are a permanent and durable solution for replacing teeth. They feel and look exactly like natural teeth, and they remain in place because they are implanted into the jawbone.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small posts made of a strong metal called titanium. Our dentist inserts the posts in your jawbone during a simple procedure. Bone gradually grows around the implant over time. This holds it firm in place, similar to that of a natural tooth root. After the implant heals, a tooth-colored crown is placed on top. This crown is similar to a natural tooth in function, feel, and looks.

When Does A Dentist Recommend Dental Implants?

A dentist will prescribe dental implants near you when a patient has one or more missing teeth. One may be a good fit for getting dental implants if they have a healthy body overall, healthy gums, and sufficient bone in the jaw. Implants are appropriate to restore chewing, enhance speech, support the jawbone, and replace teeth without interfering with the nearby healthy teeth, providing a firm, enduring solution.

In case your bone is too thin or soft, our dentist might suggest a bone graft first to help support the implant.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are so convenient in so many ways, which is why patients prefer them so much. They are designed to look just like your own teeth, with a single crown that fits in with your smile. Unlike dentures, implants don’t move and stay firmly in place. They are also very easy to maintain—brush and floss them just like your own teeth. One of the greatest advantages is that they keep your jawbone in good condition by supporting it, which prevents bone loss in the long term. With the right care, dental implants can last for years, even for a lifetime.

Procedure for Acquiring Dental Implants

The process of getting a dental Implants in Lithonia takes a few visits:

Evaluation

Our dentist near you checks your mouth, takes x-rays, and talks with you about your treatment plan.

Placement

The implant is gently placed into the jawbone. You may feel some pressure, but it’s usually not painful.

Healing Time

Your bone needs time to grow around the implant. This can take a few months.

Final Tooth

Once healing is done, we place the crown on top. Now your new tooth is ready to use!
