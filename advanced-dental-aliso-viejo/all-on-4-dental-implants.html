Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

How does All-on-4 differ from traditional implants?

All-on-4 uses just four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth, whereas traditional methods often require one implant per tooth or more implants per arch. This approach reduces surgery time, recovery, and cost while providing excellent stability. Dr. Tiruveedula at Advanced Dental in Viejo can determine if All-on-4 is right for you based on your bone density and oral health. It’s a streamlined option for a complete, functional smile.

Can I get All-on-4 implants if I have bone loss?

All-on-4 implants are designed to work in areas with low bone density. By angling the implants strategically, they provide strong support even in patients who might not qualify for traditional implants. This often eliminates the need for bone grafting. Dr. Tiruveedula at Advanced Dental in Viejo specializes in assessing your jawbone and creating a personalized plan to restore your smile with All-on-4 implants. Are All-on-4 implants removable?

All-on-4 implants are securely fixed in place and function like natural teeth. Unlike dentures, they are anchored to the jawbone, eliminating the need for removal during cleaning and preventing any shifting. Daily care includes brushing and flossing, just like natural teeth. Dr. Tiruveedula guides you in maintaining your All-on-4 implants to ensure long-lasting results. At Advanced Dental in Viejo, we prioritize comfort and functionality for your new smile.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

The All-on-4 procedure typically takes just one day to complete. During this time, the dental team places the dental implants and attaches a temporary prosthesis, enabling you to leave with a functional smile. After the healing period, the dental team will put a permanent set of teeth for long-term use. What can I eat with All-on-4 implants?

Once healed, All-on-4 implants allow you to enjoy various foods, including hard and chewy items. During the initial healing period, you must follow a soft diet to protect the implants as they integrate with your jawbone.
