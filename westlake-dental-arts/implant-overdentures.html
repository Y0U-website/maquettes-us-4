Frequently Asked Questions

How many implants are needed for an overdenture?

Most overdentures require 2 to 4 implants per arch, depending on your bone health and prosthetic type. We&#x27;ll assess this during your consultation using 3D imaging.

Are implant overdentures comfortable?

Yes. Because they&#x27;re secured to implants, overdentures are more comfortable and secure than conventional removable dentures. They reduce gum pressure and stay firmly in place.

Can I use my current denture with implants?

In some cases, an existing denture may be modified to fit onto implants. However, for the best fit and function, we typically design a new denture specifically for implant attachment.

How do I clean implant overdentures?

They are removed daily for cleaning, just like traditional dentures—but they do not require adhesives. You&#x27;ll also clean around the implant abutments in your mouth.

What if I don’t have enough bone for implants?

If needed, we may recommend bone grafting before implant placement to build up adequate jaw support for long-term stability.
