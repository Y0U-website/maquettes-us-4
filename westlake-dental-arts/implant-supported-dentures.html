Frequently Asked Questions

How many implants are needed to support a denture?

Typically, 4–6 implants per arch are used to anchor a full denture. The number depends on bone health, prosthetic type (fixed or removable), and long-term goals.

Are implant-supported dentures removable?

They can be. Some are fixed in place and only removed by a dentist, while others are designed to snap on and off for easy cleaning. Both provide much more stability than traditional dentures.

How long do implant-supported dentures last?

With proper care, implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime. The denture component may need periodic updates, but implant posts are built for long-term function.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. With the use of sedation dentistry and digital surgical guides, we make the process smooth and manageable.

Am I a candidate for implant-supported dentures?

If you&#x27;re missing most or all of your teeth and have adequate jawbone structure, you’re likely a good candidate. Bone grafting or tooth extractions may be recommended prior to treatment, depending on your needs.
