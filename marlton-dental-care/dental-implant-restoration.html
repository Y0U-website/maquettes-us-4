The Dental Implant Restoration Process

Restoring a dental implant is a multi-step process designed to ensure the restoration functions as naturally as possible. After your implant has fused with the jawbone (a process known as osseointegration), the restoration process can begin. Here’s what you can expect:

By the end of this process, your restored implant will look, feel, and function just like a natural tooth.

  • Assessment and Planning : Dr. Syed Haider will assess your implant and surrounding teeth to plan the best restoration approach. He’ll ensure that your implant has properly integrated and is ready for the final crown, bridge, or denture.
  • Custom Restoration Design : Using state-of-the-art technology, we’ll create a custom-made crown or bridge that matches the shape and color of your natural teeth. This step ensures that your restoration fits seamlessly with the rest of your smile.
  • Placement of the Restoration : Once your restoration is complete, it will be securely attached to the implant. The result is a fully functional, beautiful tooth replacement.

Maintaining Your Implant Restoration

Once your dental implant restoration is complete, it’s important to maintain good oral hygiene to ensure its longevity. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental cleanings are essential to keeping your restored smile healthy. Regular visits to Marlton Dental Care will allow us to monitor your implant and surrounding teeth, ensuring everything stays in optimal condition.
