Meet The Doctor and Team at Bell Family Dentistry

Brent Bell - Dentist & Owner

Dr. Bell graduated from The Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry at UOP in 1994. He has been in private practice in Riverbank since 1997 and has been at his current office location since 2002. He recently completed a mini residency in Oral Sleep Apnea Appliances at UOP.

Dr. Bell places a high value on honesty and always wants his patients to know they can trust him. He is a conservative dentist who would rather start with smaller fixes than drastic measures. Prevention is a key part of his dental philosophy and he educates his patients on things they can do to keep their teeth in great shape.

Dr. Bell does everything in his practice, from fillings to pulling wisdom teeth and he does them all with precision. He has worked hard to offer as many dental services to his patients as he can, so they can bring their families to one place and get everything they need. Dr. Bell is always learning and incorporating new technology, but his primary focus is the comfort and peace of mind of his patients.

Our Dental Philosophy

Genuine Friendship Dr. Bell loves what he does and loves his patients. He can take care of the whole family and is particularly skilled at dealing with children. Because of his genuine care, honest education and attention to detail, it’s no surprise that his patients love him as well. But don’t take our word for it, ask one of them. If you are looking for a doctor you love to visit, give Dr. Bell a try. You’ll see the difference on your first visit.

Honest Education Honest treatment diagnosis is a hallmark of Dr. Bell. He prefers a conservative approach to dentistry helping his patients keep their natural teeth unaltered as long as possible. We believe that when you are empowered with education you can make confident informed decisions. We also understand that trust is earned by our consistent actions.

Excellent Results Dr. Bell is meticulous and likes to get things perfect. Add this to his 17 years of experience and you can rest assured that you are receiving the best results available. His commitment to the latest technology is proof of his dedication to achieving the best results.

Technology We recognize that the right dental technology improves diagnosis, improves treatment, reduces pain and speeds recovery. This is why we offer technology such as digital x-rays and panographs, digital intra-oral cameras and digital iTero impressions. This is another way we demonstrate our commitment to you.
