Proper Home Care Dental Routine​

BRUSH & FLOSS EVERY DAY

One of the simplest and most important parts of maintaining good oral health is brushing and flossing your teeth every day. It is recommended to brush your teeth at least twice a day for at least 2 minutes. Always use a soft toothbrush, and we strongly recommend brushing with an electric toothbrush. In addition to brushing, cleaning the sides of your teeth where your toothbrush can’t reach is equally as important. This is done best with floss or a waterpik.

There are other ways to keep your teeth healthy as well! Routine Cleanings and Exams are an important part of a healthy mouth. In some cases, our dentists may discuss Sealants, Fluoride, Mouth/Sports and Snoreguards to help prevent dental problems.

Routine dental cleanings are very important when trying to maintain good oral health. At Porteous & Burke we advise our patients to schedule either prophy or periodontal maintenance cleanings regularly at 3, 4, or 6 month intervals depending on each individual s needs. We recommend routine dental exams including x-rays once a year during your cleaning appointment. Even though you brush and floss daily, and use toothpaste with fluoride, you can’t get to all of the plaque and tarter that is on your teeth. It hides in places your toothbrush and floss can’t reach.

Fluoride to Strengthen Teeth

Fluoride is a mineral like Calcium that can strengthen our teeth to prevent cavities, reduce sensitivity, and even help children develop stronger teeth from infancy! Living in the Bay Area, we often find parents are concerned about using Fluoride. Feel free to speak with one of our dentists if you have questions or concerns about Fluoride.
