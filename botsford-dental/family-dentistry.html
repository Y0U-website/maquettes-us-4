Advanced Care for Senior Dental Patients

We feel that everyone is entitled to gentle and comprehensive care, not just our youngest patients. We offer effective dental solutions for oral health issues that are experienced by our adult patients.

Dr. Botsford provides dentures who for those patients who have had teeth extracted or who are missing whole arches of teeth. Our services extend to just about everything in dentures partials and full – upper and lower, with options that include implants, implant-supported dentures, and snap-on dentures. We know that restoring your bite function and smile is critical to your health and your well-being, which is why we give you the best of the best in restorative dentistry and denture treatment in Medford.

Our friendly hygienists can even accommodate frequent visits for patients who have lost dexterity and need assistance in thoroughly brushing and flossing.
