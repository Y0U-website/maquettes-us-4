Meet Dr. Victor Nyakundi

Dr Victor Nyakundi Norwood is dedicated to delivering exceptional dental care that blends precision, compassion, and artistry. Known for his warm chairside manner and meticulous skill, he ensures that every patient feels comfortable and leaves with renewed confidence in their smile.

Philosophy of Care

At Affinity Dental, we believe dentistry should be more than just a procedure — it should be a positive experience. From the moment you step into our office, you’ll be welcomed into a modern, spa-like environment designed for comfort and relaxation.

Every smile is unique, which is why Dr. Nyakundi and his team tailor every treatment plan to fit your individual goals and lifestyle. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning, advanced treatment, or a complete smile makeover, our goal is simple: to help you achieve a healthy, radiant smile you can feel proud of for life.

Credentials & Expertis

Dr. Nyakundi’s training and certifications include:

  • Massachusetts Dental License
  • Board Certified in Oral Conscious Sedation Dentistry (DOCS)
  • Certifications in Nitrous Oxide Sedation, Level II Moderate Sedation, Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS), and CPR
  • Extensive experience in restorative, cosmetic, implant, and sedation dentistry
  • American Dental Association
  • Massachusetts Dental Society
  • Academy of General Dentistry
  • American Academy of Implant Dentistry

Beyond Dentistry

When not caring for patients, Dr. Nyakundi enjoys giving back to his community through volunteer work and mentorship. He is passionate about improving access to quality dental care and helping the next generation of dental professionals.

Outside of the practice, Dr. Nyakundi loves spending time with his wife and two sons. Whether enjoying local Norwood activities or simply being together as a family, those moments inspire his passion for creating healthier, happier smiles every day
