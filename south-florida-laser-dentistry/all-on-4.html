Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Implants

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

The initial surgery can often be completed in one day. Many patients leave the office with a temporary fixed prosthesis the same day, followed by a permanent one after healing.

Is the procedure painful?

We use local anesthesia and offer sedation dentistry to keep you comfortable. Most patients report manageable discomfort and faster recovery than expected.

What’s the success rate of All-on-4?

All-on-4 has a high success rate—over 95 % in eligible patients. With proper care and hygiene, results can last decades or even a lifetime.

How do I care for my implants?

All-on-4 prosthetics are brushed and flossed like natural teeth. We recommend regular dental cleanings and checkups to ensure your implants remain healthy and secure.

Will my insurance cover the treatment?

Coverage varies. While some plans offer partial reimbursement, we’ll help verify your benefits and offer financing options if needed.
