Dental Implants in Tamarac, FL

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants provide exceptional stability and prevent bone loss, making them the most advanced solution for missing teeth.

  • An abutment that connects the post to the replacement tooth
  • A custom-made crown that restores the function and appearance of your smile

The Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Restore natural appearance and function
  • Prevent jawbone deterioration
  • Provide a long-lasting, durable solution
  • Improve chewing ability and speech
  • Avoid reliance on adjacent teeth for support

Types of Dental Implants We Offer

Single Tooth Implants

If you&#x27;re missing one tooth, a single implant paired with a custom crown offers a seamless and durable replacement.

Implant-Supported Bridges

For multiple missing teeth, implant-supported bridges provide a stable and long-lasting alternative to traditional dental bridges.

All-on-4® Dental Implants

For patients missing an entire arch of teeth, All-on-4 implants offer a full set of fixed teeth using just four implants. This advanced solution eliminates the need for removable dentures and provides immediate results.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Traditional dentures can shift and cause discomfort. Implant-supported dentures lock into place, providing a secure and stable fit without adhesives.

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging A thorough examination and 3D imaging help determine if implants are the right option for you.
  • Implant Placement A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over a few months.
  • Abutment & Crown Placement Once healed, the abutment is attached, and a custom crown, bridge, or denture is secured for a fully restored smile.
  • Final Adjustments & Follow-Up We ensure proper fit and function, and provide guidance on implant care to maintain long-term results.

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

If you&#x27;re unsure whether implants are right for you, our team will assess your oral health and create a personalized treatment plan.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have sufficient jawbone density or are willing to undergo bone grafting
  • Are in good oral health, free from gum disease
  • Want a permanent solution instead of removable dentures
