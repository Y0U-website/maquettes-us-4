Accessibility Statement

  • Skip navigation link allows keyboard users to jump directly to main content
  • Logical tab order throughout the site
  • Visible focus indicators for interactive elements

Screen Reader Support

  • ARIA labels and landmarks for better screen reader navigation
  • Descriptive alt text for all informative images
  • Properly structured headings (H1-H6) for content hierarchy
  • Form labels and instructions clearly associated with form fields
  • Modal dialogs with proper focus management and ARIA attributes

Visual Design

  • Sufficient color contrast ratios meeting WCAG AA standards
  • Text can be resized up to 200 % without loss of functionality
  • Content is not dependent on color alone to convey information
  • Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes and orientations

Content Structure

  • Clear and consistent navigation throughout the site
  • Semantic HTML5 markup for better structure and meaning
  • Descriptive link text that makes sense out of context
  • Tables (when used) include proper headers and structure

Third-Party Content

Some content on our website may be provided by third parties (such as embedded maps or appointment scheduling tools). While we strive to ensure all content is accessible, we may have limited control over third-party content. We work with vendors who share our commitment to accessibility.

Ongoing Improvements

We view accessibility as an ongoing effort and are continuously working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website. We regularly:

  • Review our website for accessibility issues
  • Test with assistive technologies
  • Update content to meet current accessibility standards
  • Train our staff on accessibility best practices
  • Seek feedback from users with disabilities

Assistive Technologies

Our website is designed to be compatible with commonly used assistive technologies, including:

  • Screen readers (such as JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver)
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Alternative input devices
