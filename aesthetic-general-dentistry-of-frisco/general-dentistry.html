Preventive Dentistry

Routine cleanings and exams are an important part of protecting your dental health. Dr. Patel offers routine plaque and tartar removal, as well as digital x-rays and visual exams to patients of all ages.

Additionally, we offer several other treatments to further enhance your oral health. Oral Cancer Screening

At each of your biannual visits, Dr. Patel will conduct a visual exam to look for the signs of oral cancer. The symptoms typically include red or white patches on the gums, irritation, bleeding, and lumps on the soft tissues. If he discovers any tissue irregularities, he will take a biopsy for laboratory analysis.

These regular screenings could save your life; with early diagnosis, oral cancer patients typically have a very good outlook. It is especially important to receive regular screenings if you are over the age of 40, use tobacco regularly, or have a viral infection such as HPV.

TMJ Treatment

If your temporomandibular joints become inflamed or damaged, it can lead to jaw pain, headaches, neck pain, and other complications. Often, TMJ jaw pain is caused by stress, bruxism (teeth grinding), malocclusion (dental misalignment), head trauma, and other factors.

Dr. Patel offers effective TMJ treatment options . Oral splints can realign your bite to prevent teeth grinding and jaw clenching. Occlusal guards fit over your teeth, and while they will not realign your jaw, they can help to prevent teeth grinding. Splints and guards are custom-made to fit your teeth, and they may be worn only at night or during the day, depending on the severity of your symptoms.

Periodontal Care

Gum disease occurs when bacteria accumulate below your gum line, causing irritation and inflammation. If left untreated, your gums may begin to pull away from the underlying dental roots, and it can lead to tooth loss and jawbone atrophy. We offer advanced treatment for gum disease , including root planing and scaling.

Dr. Patel will remove bacteria from your gums and eliminate the rough spots on your dental roots that often harbor bacteria. This treatment is often the best choice for specific microbial contamination and localized infections.

Tooth Extractions

Dr. Patel performs any scope of tooth extraction needed to set the stage for oral health restoration. If you have widespread dental damage and are considering implants or dentures , he can remove all of your teeth to make room for the restorations . We offer nitrous oxide to help you remain comfortable and relaxed throughout treatment.

Sedation Dentistry

Nearly everyone has felt some degree of anxiety in the examination chair, but the condition can become problematic if you find yourself putting off dental check-ups or ignoring persistent oral discomfort. We offer sedation dentistry, which includes nitrous oxide, which can help you relax and receive the care you need to guard against worsening health issues in the future. In addition to helping dental phobics, sedation may be recommended during larger surgical procedures, or for patients with sensitive gag reflexes or painful medical conditions, like arthritis.
