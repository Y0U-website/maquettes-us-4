Your Complimentary Consultation

At your complimentary consultation, a comprehensive examination is completed, and, if indicated, diagnostic records are taken. Diagnostic records include digital photos of the face and teeth, digital x-rays, and digital scans of the teeth. This information is used to develop a custom treatment plan for each patient.

Once Dr. Remington has evaluated your records, your treatment plan will be reviewed with you in detail, including the treatment time and fees for all services.

Office Visits

The appointment time reserved when your appliances (braces, expanders, clear aligner trays, etc.) are placed is typically between 1 and 1.5 hours. Future appointments are scheduled approximately every 6 and 8 weeks in 20 minute intervals, dependent upon what is planned for each visit. The majority of your appointments will be short. For your convenience, these are typically scheduled before of after school. This allows us to reserve school time appointments for longer procedures.

Patients are seen by appointment only. We make every effort to be on time for our patients and ask that you extend the same courtesy to us. If you cannot keep an appointment, please notify us immediately.

Financial Information

The cost of your orthodontic treatment will vary depending on your individual needs and treatment plan. Dr. Remington and her team will discuss the cost of your treatment with you, as well as available payment plan options. This will be thoroughly reviewed prior to the start of treatment and will enable you to make the best choice for you and your smile.

Your smile lasts a lifetime, and we want to make it as easy as possible for you to receive the best orthodontic care possible.

Insurance Information

Patients are responsible for knowing the extent of their insurance coverage, if any. Our office team will submit all claims and associated paperwork as a courtesy to you.
