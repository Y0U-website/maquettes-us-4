Dr. Kate BURBA

Growing up in a dental family, Dr. Kate Burba initially thought she would pave a completely different path for herself. However, as she grew older, her desire to work in healthcare led her back to the dental office, where she discovered a profound appreciation for the profession. She realized that dentistry offered the perfect harmony of art and science, allowing her to combine her natural artistic skills with advanced clinical care, all while building meaningful, lifelong connections with people.

Joining Burba Dental Partners is an incredibly special milestone for Dr. Kate. Stepping into the family practice, she is proud to be part of a collaborative team that treats patients like family, supports one another unconditionally, and maintains an unwavering commitment to exceptional clinical work.

For Dr. Kate, the most fulfilling part of her day is making a positive, lasting impact on her patients’ lives. She views dentistry as a lifelong journey of continuous learning, constantly striving to hone her skills to provide the highest quality of care. Together with the team, Dr. Kate is dedicated to providing an elevated dental experience: one rooted in comfort, trust, and premium quality care that leaves patients smiling and genuinely looking forward to their next visit.

After all, as Dr. Burba likes to say, " Everybody has teeth, and everybody needs dental care so why not make it an enjoyable experience?

Give Back a Smile (GBAS)

True healing often requires restoring what has been wrongfully taken away. That is why Dr. Randy is incredibly proud to be a dedicated volunteer dentist for Give Back a Smile, a profound national initiative launched by the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD). This vital program works tirelessly to restore the smiles of women and men who have suffered dental injuries as a result of domestic violence or sexual assault.

Through this program, Dr. Burba volunteers his advanced cosmetic dental skills to rebuild fractured teeth and restore shattered confidence. Most recently, our office had the absolute privilege of providing a comprehensive, complimentary smile makeover to Tina, a remarkably brave and well-deserving applicant. Watching our patients reclaim their smiles, their self-esteem, and their lives through this initiative is among the most rewarding work we do.

Rooted in the Salem Community

Our commitment to service hits closest to home right here in the historic streets of Salem. As lifelong residents, the Burba family is heavily invested in local community development and civic engagement, most notably through their active, hands-on leadership within the Salem Rotary Club.

We believe in showing up for our youth and neighbors in tangible ways. Each year, the Burbas proudly roll up their sleeves to volunteer for the Annual Rotary Cookout at the Plummer Home, a dedicated event specifically designed to benefit and support the state’s foster care program. Additionally, our team enthusiastically participates in the Rotary Club’s annual charity auction. The proceeds from this landmark event directly fund local scholarship programs, helping to pave the way for the next generation of leaders, dreamers, and professionals in our beloved hometown.
