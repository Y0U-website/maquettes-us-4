The material beautifully reflects light just like natural tooth enamel, making it virtually impossible for others to tell the difference between your implant and your natural teeth.

- The Dental Implant Procedure -

  • The Consultation & At-Home Prep Your first visit is simple and focused on an oral exam, X-rays, and discussing your personal goals. To prepare for your actual procedure day, we provide clear instructions (such as fasting guidelines) and encourage you to stock up on soft foods and get plenty of restful sleep.
  • Implant Placement & Comfort On the day of your procedure, we use standard anesthesia to eliminate any pain or discomfort. If necessary, a damaged tooth is gently removed or a bone graft is performed to strengthen a weak jawbone, after which the metal, screw-like implant post is safely inserted into the bone beneath the gumline.
  • Healing & Growth (Osseointegration) Following placement, a recovery period of several weeks or months is required to allow the jawbone to naturally grow and heal tightly around the dental implant, creating a rock-solid foundation.
  • Abutment & Custom Tooth Placement Once your implant is fully secured by the bone, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the post. Finally, your custom-crafted, natural-looking crown, bridge, or denture is beautifully fitted on top to complete your brand-new smile.

All-On-4

What Makes All-On-4 Different From Traditional Implants?

While traditional dental implants are the gold standard for replacing individual missing teeth, attempting to restore a full arch using standard methods can quickly become complex, costly, and time-consuming. Understanding how All-on-4 fundamentally changes the process highlights why it has become such a favored solution:

  • Fewer Implants, Maximum Stability Replacing a full row of teeth the traditional way used to mean placing up to eight separate implants across your jaw. All-on-4 offers a much gentler, streamlined approach. By using just four perfectly placed, body-friendly implant posts per arch, we can safely and securely anchor a complete set of beautiful, natural-looking teeth.
  • No Bone Grafting Needed When you lose teeth, the jawbone naturally tends to shrink over time. With traditional implants, patients with bone loss often need a separate bone grafting surgery, and months of healing time, before the implants can even be placed. All-on-4 beautifully bypasses this extra step! By placing the rear implants at a precise, comfortable angle, we can maximize your natural bone structure. This makes the procedure a smoother, safer, and much more accessible choice, even if you’ve been told you have lower bone density.
  • Your New Smile on the Same Day Traditional full-mouth restorations usually require you to wait several months between your initial surgery and finally receiving your new teeth. With the All-on-4 process, your temporary, highly functional new teeth are safely attached to your implants the exact same day as your procedure. You can walk out of our office with a complete, confident smile without missing a single beat.

- Postop Care For Dental Implants -

Long-Term Maintenance & Follow-Ups

Once your initial healing is complete, caring for a permanent dental implant is beautifully simple and wonderfully similar to caring for your natural teeth. Consistent oral hygiene and partnership with our Salem team will protect your jawbone and ensure your beautiful new smile lasts a lifetime.

  • Gentle, Consistent Brushing Brush your teeth thoroughly but gently several times a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush to keep your gums healthy and reduce the risk of inflammation or infection.
  • Daily Flossing & Rinsing Continue to floss daily and use mouthwash exactly as directed. For an extra thorough clean, you can use an oral irrigator (water flosser) to gently clean the hard-to-reach spaces around and below your implant.
  • Special Care for Removable Options If you receive a removable, implant-supported denture, simply take it out at night and allow it to safely soak in a designated denture solution while you sleep.
  • Essential Follow-Up Appointments It is incredibly important to attend all scheduled post-op checkups. These brief, comforting visits allow us to monitor your progress, ensure the implant is integrating perfectly with your jawbone, and catch any early signs of irritation before they become a problem.
  • Routine Professional Cleanings Just like natural teeth, implants rely on a foundation of healthy gums. Regular dental exams and professional cleanings keep your artificial teeth looking pristine and looking exactly the way they should.

- Dental Implant FAQs -

  • How Long Does a Dental Implant Procedure Take? Every patient is different, but it typically takes between four to eight months to complete all stages of the dental implant procedure. The dental implant must go through osseointegration, which involves the implant fusing with the surrounding bone. This process takes between three and six months. The process for getting an All-On-4 Procedure is faster than getting traditional implants. Typically, the patient can expect the surgery to last an hour or two. Time frames vary from patient to patient.
  • Will I Have to Make Drastic Changes to My Lifestyle After a Dental Implant Procedure? Not really. Dental implants are artificial teeth that do not require excess oral hygiene. In many cases, the patient will only have to brush and floss their teeth like normal. Along with that, the patient will be able to continue eating most types of food with dental implants.
  • What Should I Do If My Dental Veneers Fall Off? Dental implants allow patients full function within hours of the surgery. It may take a little bit of time to get used to the implants. Our team may advise patients to avoid hard foods at first.
  • Can I Still Get Teeth Whitening If I Have Other Dental Concerns or Issues? Because our dentist uses anesthesia to numb the patient, the person should take time to recover. Patients should avoid heavy physical activity for the next day or two. Rest is critical during this time. Most patients are encouraged to take several days off from work after implant placement. However, many can return to work within a week. If the side effects reduce and the patient can carry out their daily work routine, it is safe to return.
