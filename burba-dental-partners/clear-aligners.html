- The Benefits of Choosing ClearCorrect -

When compared to traditional orthodontic treatments, ClearCorrect offers unique advantages that fit seamlessly into your everyday life:

  • Nearly Invisible Protection Crafted from clear, crack-resistant, and stain-resistant plastic, these aligners are practically unnoticeable while you wear them.
  • Conveniently Removable You can easily pop them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss, making dental hygiene much simpler than navigating around metal brackets and wires.
  • A Confidence Boost Straightening crooked teeth can dramatically transform your smile, helping you overcome insecurities and feel proud of your look.
  • Highly Effective Results When worn according to your dentist’s exact instructions, ClearCorrect provides a highly reliable path to a beautifully aligned smile.

Key Tips for Daily Life & Care

To get the absolute best results from your ClearCorrect journey, there are a few important guidelines and daily habits to keep in mind:

  • Commit to Wearing Them Because the aligners are removable, it can be tempting to leave them out. However, they can only do their job if they stay in your mouth for the recommended amount of time each day.
  • Watch What You Drink Always take caution when drinking liquids other than plain water while wearing your trays. Sugary or acidic beverages like soda, coffee, and alcohol can get trapped beneath the plastic, potentially leading to cavities and weakened enamel.
  • Keep Your Aligners Sparkling Clean your trays daily by gently brushing the inside and outside with a soft toothbrush and a mild antibacterial soap or specialized cleaning solution. Always brush and floss your teeth thoroughly before putting the aligners back in to keep food particles from staining the plastic or damaging your teeth.
  • Consider the Investment Invisible aligners can sometimes be more expensive than traditional braces, though the exact cost varies from person to person. Our team is always here to help you navigate your expected costs and review your insurance coverage.

Retainers

To keep your custom retainer in top condition, simply clean it regularly with cool water and a gentle toothbrush, store it safely in its protective case, and avoid exposing it to warping heat. It is also highly recommended to bring your retainer to your routine dental check-ups so our team can ensure it is still fitting comfortably and functioning exactly as it should.

- Orthodontic FAQs -

  • Can a Child Get Clear Aligners? Technically, a child can receive ClearCorrect braces. However, people without their adult teeth are not typically optimal candidates for the treatment, as their mouths are still growing and changing rapidly.
  • Can I Get Clear Aligners if I Currently Have Traditional Braces? Yes, ClearCorrect braces can follow another orthodontic treatment. Burba Dental Partners may recommend this for patients who want invisible aligners but also need a more aggressive treatment plan. Talk with our team about switching during an appointment.
  • Do Clear Aligners Hurt? There can be some discomfort with ClearCorrect braces as they are moving the teeth, but this is usually mild to moderate at most. In most cases, you can manage the pain with over-the-counter medication.
  • Can I Get Clear Aligners if I Have Missing Teeth? It is possible to get ClearCorrect braces with a missing tooth. However, they may be more successful in some cases than others. Talking to a qualified professional is the only way to be sure.
