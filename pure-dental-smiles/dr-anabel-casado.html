DR. ANABEL CASADO

Dr. Anabel Casado is graduated from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine in 2020 and received his undergraduate training from Florida International University. She is committed to using only the best materials on the market at reasonable price. She provides a comprehensive dentistry for adults and children Performs surgical extractions, bone grafts, root canals , fillings, crowns, veneers, bridges, partial and complete dentures, “snap-in” over dentures, full mouth reconstructions, and places implants.

Her goal is to provide quality care at an affordable price.

My goal as a dentist is to provide excellent dental care to each patient. I believe that everyone deserves to receive high-quality dental care, regardless of their financial situation. I am dedicated to making sure that every patient feels at ease during his/her appointment. I also educate my clients on proper oral hygiene so they can maintain healthy teeth.
