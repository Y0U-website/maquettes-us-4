The practice

Our approach is simple: we explain what we find, show you your options with the cost of each, and let you decide. You will have a written estimate in hand before any treatment begins.

Care in English and Spanish. Dr. Casado consults in both languages, so nothing about your diagnosis or your options gets lost in translation.

Costs explained before we start. You get a written estimate showing what your insurance covers and what you owe, plus financing through Sunbit and CareCredit if you need to spread the cost.

One dentist who knows you. You see Dr. Casado at every visit, not whoever happens to be on rotation.

I earned my DMD from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine in 2020, after completing my undergraduate degree at Florida International University, and have practiced in Florida since.

I provide comprehensive care for adults and children: exams and cleanings, fillings, crowns, veneers and bridges, root canals, surgical extractions, bone grafts, partial and complete dentures, snap-in overdentures, full-mouth reconstruction, and dental implants including All-on-4.

My goal is straightforward. Everyone deserves good dental care regardless of what they can afford, so I use quality materials at a fair price and tell you what treatment costs before we begin. I want you comfortable during the appointment and confident about looking after your teeth afterwards.

I consult in English and Spanish, and Pure Dental Smiles welcomes patients of every background and gender identity.
