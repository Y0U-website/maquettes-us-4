Dr. Mackenzie King Platt

Dr. Mackenzie King Platt is more than just a dentist she’s a passionate healthcare professional dedicated to creating confident, comfortable smiles for her patients in Lakeville, MN. A proud graduate of the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, Dr. Platt has been transforming dental experiences since 2003.

Her journey in dentistry began with a simple yet powerful mission: to help people feel proud of their smiles and at ease during dental visits. In 2007, she took the bold step of opening Platt Dentistry from scratch, building a practice founded on compassion, skill, and genuine patient care. Her greatest joy comes from those moments when nervous patients leave her chair saying, "That was so easy" a testament to her gentle approach and exceptional clinical care.

What truly sets Dr. Platt apart is her approach to both dentistry and team building. She leads a close-knit team that is more than just professional colleagues; they are genuine friends who enjoy each other’s company both in and out of the office. This warm, supportive environment translates directly into the patient experience, making dental visits something to look forward to rather than fear.

When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Platt enjoys a rich life filled with outdoor adventures. She loves spending time with family on Wisconsin trails, enjoying side-by-side rides, and warming up by bonfires. A die-hard Gopher football fan, she’s always ready to cheer on her team (except when they’re playing the Badgers then she hopes for a tie). Recently, she’s been challenging herself by learning to golf, adding another exciting chapter to her personal pursuits.

Dr. Kyndra Weber

Dr. Kyndra Weber earned her undergraduate degree in Nutrition Science and Psychology from the University of Nebraska - Lincoln before completing dental school at the University of Iowa. She has been practicing dentistry since 2019, working in two other offices before joining the team at Platt Dentistry.

Dr. Weber s passion for dentistry centers on building genuine relationships with her patients while individualizing their care for optimal oral and systemic health. She strives to help every patient achieve their dental goals by ensuring they feel confident, valued, and heard every step of the way. She takes pride in how intentionally the Platt Dentistry team pours into its relationships with patients and with one another, and considers herself lucky to be part of such a caring, professional group.

Outside the office, Dr. Weber is busy cheering on her four kids at their sporting and school events. She enjoys spending time outdoors with her family, exploring local restaurants, shopping, reading, staying active in her church community, indulging in anything coffee-related, and planning new house projects. Her superpower (or survival tactic, with four young kids) is organizing: she has a place for everything and isn t shy with the label maker. She s also an avid sports and college volleyball fan. Go Huskers!

Claire

Claire joined the Platt Dentistry team in August 2025, but you could say her dental roots go back much further than that. In a fun twist of fate, Claire’s mother and Dr. Platt were actually classmates and friends in dental school. Since Claire was born while her mother was still in the program, she and Dr. Platt love to joke that they "attended dental school together!"

A graduate of St. Lawrence University in upstate New York, Claire was drawn to dentistry after shadowing in the field. She was struck by the unique opportunity dental professionals have to build genuine, lasting relationships with patients a level of consistency and connection she found missing in other areas of healthcare.

As a Dental Assistant, Claire is known for her bright, approachable presence. Whether she is diving into the technical "ins and outs" of a complex procedure or jumping in to help her teammates, her goal is always to put patients at ease. She truly values the "family" atmosphere at the office, where laughter is a daily staple.

When she isn’t chatting with patients about their passions and life stories, Claire is almost certainly embracing the outdoors. She is an avid runner, mountain biker, and skier covering both cross-country and alpine trails.

To unwind, she enjoys the focused calm of paint-by-number projects while catching up on her favorite podcasts.

Amber

Amber handles all things insurance at Platt Dentistry. Her role includes verifying coverage and benefits, obtaining pre-treatment estimates, and providing clear cost estimates before treatment begins. Her work gives patients financial clarity up front, helps them get the most out of their benefits, and removes the burden of navigating insurance processes on their own.

Amber holds a Marketing Management degree from the University of St. Thomas as well as a two-year Interior Design degree. She originally joined Platt Dentistry when Dr. Platt asked for short-term help covering the front desk and stayed on after the coordinator returned.

What Amber enjoys most about the team is how much fun they have together and the fact that all team members help each other when needed. She believes it is the people you work with that make a huge difference. The Platt Dentistry team creates an experience where patients feel valued and cared for.

Outside the office, Amber and her family love traveling to National parks. She is especially detail-oriented researching accommodations and activities to make every trip memorable. A self-described minimalist, she chooses quality over quantity every time.
