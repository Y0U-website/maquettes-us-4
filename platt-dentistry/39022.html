The Dental Implant Process at Platt Dentistry

Completing dental implants does take some time typically three to six months for the implant to heal and integrate with your bone.

Here’s a step-by-step look at the dental implant process:

Your team at Platt Dentistry is here to guide you through every step of your smile journey.

  • Initial evaluation During this visit , our dentist determine if you’re a good candidate for dental implants with x-rays, a thorough oral exam, and a health history review.
  • Implant placement You’ll partner with a local specialist to place your tooth implant during a simple surgical procedure.
  • Osseointegration This is the healing process through which your implant fuses with your jawbone, replacing your root.
  • Restoration Our dentist will monitor your healing process, and when it’s completed, they’ll design, fabricate, and place your restoration.
