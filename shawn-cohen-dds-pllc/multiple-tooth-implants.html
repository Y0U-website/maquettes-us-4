Advantages of Multiple Dental Implants

Multiple tooth implants are a very reliable way to replace multiple missing teeth and are suitable for getting rid of loose and uncomfortable partial dentures .

Multiple tooth implants may be preferred to a traditional bridge. A traditional bridge relies on natural teeth for support and requires them to be substantially reshaped or crowned. These teeth can become more vulnerable to infection and decay as the bridge starts to age. Multiple implants can also be used where the existing natural teeth are not strong enough to support a bridge.

An implant bridge protects existing and opposing teeth. It prevents adjacent teeth from shifting position and helps share biting and chewing forces, preventing them from wearing down faster. The opposing dentition is also protected, as the bridge prevents these teeth from moving toward the empty space. When this happens, it can destabilize the teeth and cause difficult-to-correct bite problems.

Disadvantages of Multiple Teeth Implants

All dental implants in NYC require extensive planning, and this is not the quickest way to replace missing teeth. Although multiple tooth implants can last a very long time, this does rely on good patient compliance.

It’s essential to keep up with regular checkups, even if you have no natural teeth remaining. Good oral hygiene is necessary for keeping dental implants free from plaque as otherwise, there is a chance they will become infected and could fail.

Alternatives to Multiple Teeth Implants

The alternative to having multiple tooth implants is to use dentures, which is far from a perfect solution. Dentures rest on the gums and can accelerate bone loss.

Many people also find dentures extremely uncomfortable to wear. A dental bridge may be a possibility if any existing natural teeth are strong enough to support the bridge. This may not be the case where multiple teeth have been lost, as often any remaining teeth are affected by periodontal disease and bone loss and may not be stable enough to support a bridge.
