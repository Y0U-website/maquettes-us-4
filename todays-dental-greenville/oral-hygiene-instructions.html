Oral hygiene is vital because the mouth is a gateway to your body. Poor oral care can lead to gum disease, tooth decay, and bad breath, and may contribute to more serious conditions like heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory infections.

You should brush your teeth at least twice a day, ideally in the morning and before bed, using fluoride toothpaste. Floss at least once daily to clean between your teeth.

Brush gently at a 45-degree angle to the gums for two minutes, covering all tooth surfaces, including the outer, inner, and chewing surfaces.

Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Choose ADA-approved products or ones tailored to your specific needs, such as sensitivity or whitening.

Mouthwash isn t essential but can help reduce bacteria, strengthen enamel, and freshen breath. Choose antibacterial or fluoride rinses based on your dentist s advice.
