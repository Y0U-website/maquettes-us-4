Payment Plans for Veneers & Cosmetic Treatment

Our payment plans aren’t just for emergencies — they work for cosmetic treatment too. Many patients finance veneers through our in-house plan, splitting the cost into monthly payments per tooth rather than paying for a full smile makeover upfront. Because veneers are often placed in stages, the plan fits naturally: start with the teeth that show most when you smile, pay those off over three months, and continue from there. If the sticker price has kept you from the smile you want, ask us to break it into numbers that fit your monthly budget.
