The practice

Friendly, dedicated, energetic and passionate. Dr. Heather Miller’s philosophy is to provide quality care in a warm, comfortable environment, maintain the highest standards of practice and strive for perfection in all aspects of dentistry while building lasting relationships with all of her patients.

Dr. Miller was born and raised in Zionsville, Indiana, and received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Indiana University School of Dentistry. Prior to dental school, she earned a degree in Management from the Kelley School of Business.

Dr. Miller is continually expanding her education on the latest advancements in dentistry, offering cutting-edge techniques and technology to her patients. Dr. Miller is a standing member of the American Dental Association, Illinois Dental Society, Chicago Dental Society, and Academy of General Dentistry,

Dr. Miller currently resides in the North Shore with her husband Austin, children Parker, Kent, Pierce, and their dog Yeti. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Miller enjoys spending time with her friends & family, cooking, traveling, pilates, paddle, tennis, and attempting to play golf. Dr. Miller looks forward to making the North Shore her home and raising her family close to her husband’s hometown.
