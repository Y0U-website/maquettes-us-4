A Virginia native, Dr. Goldhush grew up in the Hampton Roads area as the oldest of three children. Her early love of helping others and making people smile inspired her to pursue a career in dentistry a passion that continues to drive her work today. She earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Virginia, followed by her Dental degree with honors from the Medical College of Virginia. She later completed advanced training in general dentistry at the University of Minnesota Dental School. She has continued her professional education in Dental Sleep Medicine, TMD, and Orthodontics.

Dr. Goldhush has been practicing at Alexandria Old Town Dental since 1991. She remains deeply committed to continuing education and staying current with advances in the dental field.

Outside of the office, Dr. Goldhush stays active by kayaking, swimming, and taking long walks with her dog, Lucy. She loves to read whenever she finds a rare quiet moment. She and her husband are proud parents of three children and reside in McLean, Virginia.
