Our Step-by-Step Case Submission Process

Download the RX Form

Begin by downloading our comprehensive RX form provided above, which is a crucial part of the submission process. Simply click the link provided to access the form. Ensure that you fill it out thoroughly and accurately, capturing all necessary details about your case.

Prepare Your Files

Next, gather all the necessary files for your submission. This typically includes digital impressions, patient photographs, and the completed RX form. We accept a variety of file formats, but please ensure they are clear and of high quality to avoid processing delays. Be mindful of the file size limitations stated on our portal to guarantee a smooth upload process.

Upload Your Files

Once you have your files ready, proceed to our secure upload portal. Here, you can easily upload your documents with just a few clicks. Our portal is designed to be user-friendly, guiding you through each step to ensure all necessary files are included.

Confirmation Follow-Up

After you have successfully uploaded your files, we will process your files and reach out when necessary. We prioritize transparency and communication, ensuring you are kept informed at every stage of the process. Should you have any questions or require additional support, our team is readily available to assist.
