Our Services

At our practice, we offer a comprehensive range of general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry services that are designed to help patients achieve a healthy, happy smile. Each of our services is personalized to meet each patient’s unique smile needs. If you have any questions about our services, feel free to call us at our office.

General & Family Dentistry

At Louisville Family Dental, we put you and your loved ones first. Our top quality dentistry services are tailored towards helping you and your family achieve lasting oral and overall wellness. General dentistry services are vital for the maintenance of a healthy, happy smile. We believe that the best way to solve a dental issue is to keep it from happening in the first place. Our general dentistry services focus on preventative care and maintenance, and helping patients develop good habits that will aid them throughout their lives.

Below are a few of our general dentistry services we provide:

General & Family Dentistry

Dental Hygiene

We offer professional dental cleanings to our patients every six months, or more frequently if necessary. Dental cleanings allow us to remove tartar or plaque buildup that could cause dental problems if left unchecked. A dental cleaning is important for patients who want to maintain great oral health. With regular dental cleanings we can help fight periodontal disease, cavities, and other dental issues.

X-rays and Checkups

Regular x-rays and dental checkups are a vital part of preventative care. By checking your teeth frequently, we can spot potential dental issues before they start, or detect dental problems in their earliest stages, when they are the most easy to treat. We recommend patients schedule a regular appointment every six months.

Oral Cancer Screenings

All patients aged 16 and older receive an oral cancer screening with every clinical exam. It is a routine part of our effort to look out for the well-being of our patients. Oral cancer is a devastating condition that can be very dangerous if left untreated. The key to successful oral cancer treatment is to diagnose the condition as early as possible. With annual oral cancer screenings, we can help protect you and help you feel more secure in your overall health.

Restorative Dentistry

At Louisville Family Dental we feel joy when we help improve someone’s smile. Our restorative dentistry services are available for patients who have experienced tooth damage, or other dental issues. Restorative dentistry treatments improve smiles, elevate health, and restore damaged teeth. By offering these services, we are able to give patients a second chance at a long lasting, healthy smile. Please feel free to contact us during our office hours, with any questions about our restorative dentistry services.

Below are a few of our restorative dentistry services we provide:

Dental Crowns

In cases where a tooth can no longer support a filling or has cracked, a dental crown (or cap) is necessary. Our office will use the material that is the most appropriate for the given case, often utilizing tooth colored materials such as porcelain or zirconia for a natural look.

Dental Bridges

There are several restorative options used to replace missing teeth. In cases where a dental implant may not be appropriate or possible, we can use a series of crowns to “bridge” an area of missing teeth.

Root Canals

A root canal can save teeth that would normally require extraction. Root canals remove harmful bacteria and protect your tooth from further damage after a severe tooth infection. If you experience a chronic, serious toothache, call us at our office today for emergency treatment.

Dental Implants

Our dental implants are created using a titanium alloy that can fuse directly with your bone. Dental implants are the only restorative treatment that can replace both the tooth root and the tooth crown. At Louisville Family Dental, we can place and restore dental implants right here in our office.

Dentures

Our dentures are custom-fitted for each patient and designed to restore functionality and beauty. If you are missing all, or almost all, of your natural teeth, dentures from Louisville Family Dental can give you a second chance at a full smile. We offer implant-supported and traditional dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry services focus on the aesthetics of your teeth, and how happy the appearance of your teeth makes you feel. Our cosmetic dentistry services are designed to help you achieve the dream smile you’ve always wanted. With a cosmetic dentistry treatment from Louisville Family Dental, your teeth can look as beautiful and as healthy as they feel.

Cosmetic dentistry enhances your smile’s natural beauty and boosts confidence. Patients who love their smiles are more likely to share their smiles with other people and feel more positive.

Some of our cosmetic dentistry services include:

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Whitening

Our team is proud to participate in Vanity for Humanity. With teeth whitening from Louisville Family Dental, you can get a brighter smile and help Habitat for Humanity at the same time. When you get one of our teeth whitening kits, we will donate the cost of the kit directly to the charity. You can make your smile beautiful and help make the world more beautiful too.

Cosmetic bonding

At our practice, we can use cosmetic bonding to repair the appearance of minor cosmetic issues to improve your smile. Cosmetic bonding uses a special composite resin that can be molded and bonded to the surface of your tooth to change its appearance.

If you have any questions about our cosmetic services, please contact us during our office hours.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are one of our most versatile cosmetic dentistry services. Using porcelain veneers we can fix the appearance of damage, make your teeth appear whiter, and make your teeth appear straighter without orthodontics. Porcelain veneers can be used to literally reshape your smile.
