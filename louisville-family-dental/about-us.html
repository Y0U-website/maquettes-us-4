Dr. Carrie Leypoldt

Dr. Carrie Leypoldt was born in Fairfax, Virginia and raised in sunny Tampa, Florida. She ventured out to Colorado to enjoy the outdoors and leave the humidity behind. Her dental training was completed at the University of Colorado (Go Buffs!). During her time at CU, Dr. Carrie was awarded an externship and traveled to the remote town of Bethel, Alaska to provide dental care to the underserved through the Indian Health Services. She continues to attend lectures and seminars to provide the highest level of dental care to her patients.

When Dr. Carrie isn’t at the office, you can find her on some sort of adventure. She enjoys traveling, hiking, camping, music festivals (especially if Bluegrass is involved), and is slowly learning how to make it down a mountain on skis (safely!). Dr. Carrie is married to her wonderful husband, Brian. They live in Lafayette with their two fur babies- their dog, Winston, and cat, Hobie.
