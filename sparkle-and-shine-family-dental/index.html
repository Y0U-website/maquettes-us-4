Les soins

Fillings help prevent further damage to a tooth that has decay. By removing the bacteria and closing off the area, further tooth decay is limited.

We get you a perfect fit for your dentures. We are experts in making good-looking dentures with a natural feel . We have a wide range of tooth shapes and colors to customize them to you.

Dental implants are used to support one or more false teeth.They are artificial teeth roots placed into your jaw to hold a replacement tooth or bridge. Reach out to Sparkle and Shine Family Dental for safe dental implant procedures.

Invisalign Aligners are an excellent alternative to traditional teeth straighteners. They straighten your teeth more predictably than wire alternatives and are convenient, discreet, and don’t interrupt your life.

Cosmetic veneers enhance your smile by covering chipped, discolored, or crooked teeth. They are custom-designed for your color preference and facial structure.
