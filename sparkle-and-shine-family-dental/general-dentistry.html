  • Dental Abscess Dental abscesses may cause fever, persistent toothache, sensitivity to hot and cold, swelling in the face, tender lymph nodes in your neck, and a pimple-like bump on your gums near the infected tooth. Get it treated on time to minimize the damage and preserve your dental hygiene. Item Link List Item 2
  • Bleeding Gums Bleeding, soreness, redness, or severe pain in the gums can lead to receding gums and could even damage the bones of the jaws if not treated on time. If you notice bleeding when brushing or flossing, visit us as soon as possible. Item Link List Item 3
  • Broken Crown If your dental crown is broken, your teeth and root are left exposed, causing high sensitivity to hot and cold liquids or foods. It also makes you vulnerable to bacterial infection and tooth decay. Let us help you repair or replace your damaged dental crowns with our cutting-edge equipment. Item Link List Item 4

Dental Hygiene

Save money and protect your teeth with regular checkups. Dental hygiene is essential to maintaining your oral health and crucial to preventing harmful bacteria from entering your body. Reach out to us for fluoride treatments, gum care, digital X-rays, flossing, plaque cleaning, and more.

Fillings

Fillings help prevent further damage to a tooth that has decay. By removing the bacteria and closing off the area, further tooth decay is limited.

Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment should be carried out as soon as possible. If an infected tooth is left untreated for too long, pain can increase dramatically and lead to an abscess around the infected tooth.
