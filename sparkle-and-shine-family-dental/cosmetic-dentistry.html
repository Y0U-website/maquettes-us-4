Veneers

Cosmetic veneers enhance your smile by covering chipped, discolored, or crooked teeth. They are custom-designed for your color preference and facial structure.

List of Services

  • Porcelain Veneers If you are feeling insecure about your smile, it’s time to get porcelain veneers. It can fix minor cosmetic issues that are visible when you smile or talk. Veneers are glossy, bright, and particularly resilient once they bond to the surface of the tooth. Item Link List Item 1
  • Resin Veneers Resin veneers are made with composite resin and are cost-effective. Enhance the look of your teeth with Sparkle and Shine Family Dental’s resin veneers. Talk to our dentists to find the right solution for you. Item Link List Item 2

Dentures

We get you a perfect fit for your dentures. We are experts in making good-looking dentures with a natural feel . We have a wide range of tooth shapes and colors to customize them to you.

List of Services

  • Basic Denteers If you have few to no teeth, basic dentures can help you restore your mouth’s basic function. It may take some time to get used to chewing, speaking, and biting, but these dentures will help you regain confidence. Item Link List Item 1
  • Partial Dentures If you have some healthy and strong teeth left, you can opt for partial dentures, which fill those spaces where some teeth are missing. This denture is attached to a metal piece that holds and keeps the denture fixed to the neighboring teeth. Item Link List Item 2
  • Implant Dentures Implant dentures are fixed dentures that can’t be removed. Since they are more stable, they will not fall out of your mouth. Item Link List Item 3
