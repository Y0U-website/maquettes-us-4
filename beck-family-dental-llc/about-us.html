Welcome to Beck Family Dental

Your Family Dentist in Mason, OH

At Beck Family Dental, we’re dedicated to giving you top-quality dental care in the Greater Cincinnati area at an affordable price and within the parameters of your schedule. When you work with a dentist from our office, you’ll receive efficient, honest and quality care that fits your life.

We know you’re busy, so we’ll work quickly to give you the attention you need and let you get on with your day. We can even complete an appointment during your lunch break. Getting the best care shouldn’t have to make you rearrange your life.

Our Story

We are a private Doctor owned Family Dental Practice serving Greater Cincinnati area. Our Mission is to touch Cincinnati with compassion through excellent service and great dental care.

Our Goal is to serve Cincinnati and the surrounding area with a level of customer service and technology that is unparalleled. We have been helping engineer smiles for our community for over 90 years. Dating back to Dr. Morris Arnold then to his son Dr. Fred and now Dr. Beck have continued on with their same level of personalized service to each of our patients help stay on the forefront of dentistry in the 21st century.

You can trust your smile to Beck Family Dental. Whether you need a convenient office to help you with a routine cleaning, emergency care, or a consult to help you plan your smile that you can rely on for a lifetime, you’ll love our state of the art practice located in Mason, Ohio.

Beck Family Dental, LLC Services

Family/Routine Dentistry: We understand your busy schedule – we offer many different options and flexibility of appointments for teeth cleaning, so we can always fit you in. At Beck Family Dental, we welcome your whole family. We have always been a family dentist and we’re proud to have served three generations of Cincinnati dental families. We use top-of-the-line dental technology to provide quality services to save you time, money and frustration. We are committed to increasing dental health awareness and encourage patients to ask questions during their appointments.

Restorative Dentistry: Restorative dentistry procedures are performed to repair teeth that have been damaged or decayed. Crowns may be recommended to restore the strength and functionality of weakened teeth. Dental bridges or dentures may be recommended to replace missing teeth.

Laser Dentistry: In the space of just a few years, laser dentistry has become central to our practice. We utilize advanced laser dentistry to improve procedures we do.
