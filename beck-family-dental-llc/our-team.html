David A. Beck, DDS

Owner

As a provider of patient-centered dental care for the past 23 years, Dr. David Beck’s passion for dentistry all started with a visit to have his wisdom teeth extracted as a teen. “I was so intrigued that the doctor invited me back to shadow him,” says Dr. Beck.

“From then on, dentistry is all I wanted to do.” He graduated from Howard University College of Dentistry and served in the U.S. Army Dental Corps as captain, later moving to Cincinnati and joining Dr. Arnold’s practice in 2004.

Stephen D. Greiner, DDS

Associate

After discerning the field of dentistry as a way to combine his love for science and artistry along with a desire to help serve the community, he attended Miami University and then the New York University College of Dentistry and received his dental degree with honors. Dr. Greiner has also completed a graduate dental residency at the University of Southern California with emphasis on Special Patient Care and Geriatric Dentistry.

Dr. Greiner enjoys all areas of comprehensive dental care and utilizing the latest techniques and advanced technology. He is excited to be a part of the highly skilled team at Beck Family Dental, LLC and incorporate such a modern practice model. Dr. Greiner is passionate about dental health and providing high-quality, comfortable care.
