HOW DO ALL-ON-4 DENTAL IMPLANTS WORK?

All-On-4 dental implants offer an alternative to traditional implants. The All-On-4 implant process is often called “Teeth in a Day” because it transforms your mouth so quickly.

Using innovative dental technology, All-On-4 replaces all of the teeth on the top or bottom of your mouth with just four implants and a bridge or set of dentures.

Through this efficient process, you can recreate a beautiful smile in just one procedure instead of the three or four required by standard dental implants. With good oral care and regular visits to your Cincinnati-area dentist, your All-On-4 implants can last a lifetime!

  • Four titanium implants are inserted into your upper or lower jaw.
  • The implants bond with your jaw bone to create sturdy, permanent tooth root replacements
  • A provisional full-arch prosthesis attaches to the four implants so that you never have to go without teeth
  • After six months, your provisional prosthesis is swapped for your new permanent teeth that will last decades
