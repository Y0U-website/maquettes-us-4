Family Dentistry in Walnut Creek…

Family dentistry encompasses dental care and services for people of all age groups. Although general dentists receive a comprehensive education before they begin their practice, family dentists take it further by providing quality care to an entire family. They allow you to benefit from their expertise and expanded services that emphasize the prevention and treatment of most recurring dental problems that could affect you at any age.

Proper Care for the Whole Family

As family dentists, we are experienced in providing dental care to both children and adults. They both come in regularly for an exam and teeth cleaning and each is treated in ways unique to their age. These visits will help us to recognize problems before they become too serious. When you decide on a family dentist, keeping these appointments becomes much more manageable.

A Relationship With Your Dentist

Family dentistry enables you to consult the same dentist throughout your life, which will make you feel much more relaxed during your exams and treatments. When we see each member of your family regularly, we are better prepared to identify your needs and take action quickly.

Preventative Oral Health Care

Oral conditions should be identified in their initial stages so that you can avoid major dental problems. As family dentists, we provide preventative oral health care services like in-depth exams to detect problem areas in your mouth. For instance, you should have your teeth cleaned twice a year to remove the plaque and tartar buildup that can lead to decay and gum disease.

Fix for Smile Imperfections

Family dentistry also offers services to correct imperfections in your smile. For teens, this may mean teeth straightening procedures or correction of a misaligned bite. The adults in the family may benefit from tooth whitening procedures, dental bonding, or veneers that can remove or hide blemishes, stains, and impairments.

Repair of Damaged or Lost Teeth

If you lose a tooth, we as family dentists will evaluate your gum health, mouth structure, and other teeth conditions to design a customized plan for tooth replacement. Dental implants, bridges, and dentures are a few options that we can offer for restoring your teeth.

Oral Hygiene Basics

As family dentists, it is our goal to help your family avoid future dental problems by teaching you how to brush and floss properly. When you come in for a routine exam, we will educate you on correct oral hygiene practices. For example, many people brush their teeth too hard or use the wrong toothbrush. We are experienced in helping you find ways to overcome these challenges so that your teeth remain in the best health possible.
