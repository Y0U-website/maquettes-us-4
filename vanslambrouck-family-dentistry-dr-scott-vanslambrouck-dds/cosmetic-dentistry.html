Cosmetic Dentistry

0 Cosmetic Dentistry The goal of cosmetic dentistry is to improve the appearance of your smile by fixing imperfections like chips, cracks, stains, and discoloration.

Our Holland dentist offers a range of dental services to help you smile with confidence, from dental implants to professional teeth whitening.

To learn more about our cosmetic dentistry services, watch the video below.

Smile Makeover

Set a good first impression with a whiter healthier smile. We offer a range of cosmetic dentistry services to restore your smile after injury or illness. The goal of cosmetic dentistry is to correct imperfections in teeth that are visible when you smile or talk.

Crowns & Caps

A dental crown (also called a “tooth cap” by our patients) is used to restore a tooth to its natural strength and appearance. You may be a candidate for a dental crown if your tooth was made fragile by a large cavity, fracture, root canal therapy, or repeated dental procedures.

Dental Implants

Are missing teeth giving you low self-esteem? Schedule a consultation with our dentist to explore your tooth replacement options, including dental implants . Unlike dentures, dental implants fuse to your jawbone for permanent stability.

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are wafer-thin porcelain shells we can bond to the fronts of your teeth to cover chips, cracks, discoloration, and other minor imperfections. If you have ever felt self-conscious about your smile, ask our dentist if you’re a candidate for porcelain veneers .

Cosmetic Bonding

One appointment could be all you need to repair minor imperfections in your smile. If you have ever felt self-conscious about your front teeth, ask our dentist if cosmetic bonding is right for you. Cosmetic bonding can repair small chips, stains, and cavities.

White Fillings

Our white fillings include ceramic and new resin compounds that mimic the appearance of natural tooth enamel. These new materials allow us to save as much healthy tooth structure as possible and achieve the best aesthetic results for your smile.

Inlays & Onlays

Inlays and onlays look like the rest of your tooth, making them virtually undetectable. Inlays are best for smaller restorations, while onlays are ideal for larger ones. Our dentist saves as much natural tooth structure as possible so your smile stays strong and healthy.
