Frequently Asked Questions

Is this dental insurance?

The RD Care Membership Plan is not dental insurance, it is a direct payment arrangement that helps our patients save money. Unlike dental insurance, you don’t have to deal with denied claims, deductibles, waiting periods, or pre-approvals. Instead, we are transparent about what’s covered, as well as the discounts that are available to you for additional procedures in our office.
