Rachel Todd, DDS

Rachel Todd, DDS, a North Texas native, knew she wanted to be a dentist from a very young age. After graduating from Mansfield High School, she attended Texas Christian University where she earned her degree in Biology and was a member of the Horned Frog Marching Band. She went on to receive her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Baylor College of Dentistry in 2016. She has practiced at Waxahachie Family Dentistry, LLP® since 2017.

When Dr. Todd isn’t busy with her two young sons Leo and Rowan, she enjoys traveling, date night with her husband Blake, and staying active with strength training. Dr. Todd is proud to be a life-long learner, and through continuing education, has expanded her dental services to include orthodontics and advanced endodontics. She is passionate about dentistry and its special role in the overall health and well-being of her patients. She can’t wait to get to know you and take care of you and your smile!

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your practice stand out from the rest?

At Waxahachie Family Dentistry, we’re committed to providing you with treatments that are precise and comfortable. We offer a comprehensive list of services that can improve the appearance and longevity of your smile.

With decades of experience between them, our dentists have the skills and expertise to treat a variety of dental conditions with attention to detail and a gentle touch. This will ensure efficent and effective treatments and promote faster healing.

How are you giving back to the community?

We are extremely passionate about providing the Waxahachie community with dental services that are second to none. Through our March to a Million program , we want to give our community one more reason to smile. We’re committed to reaching our goal of contributing $1,000,000 in dental services to the community we proudly serve.

How do you treat patients with dental anxiety?

We offer conscious sedation techniques such as IV sedation, oral sedation, and nitrous oxide to provide the utmost comfort to patients undergoing the slightest anxiety. If you have a low pain tolerance or dental anxiety, be sure to ask about our dental sedation options at your consultation and we’ll be happy to explain the benefits of these services.

What services do you offer that can improve the look of my smile?

We understand the power in having a smile that radiates confidence, which is why we proudly offer several services to transform your look. Whether your teeth are misaligned, misshapen, or you have a missing tooth, we have solutions to your dental problems.

With porcelain veneers , we can cover the surfaces of your teeth for a flawless finish. Dental implants a re a permanent solution to replace missing teeth with a natural look and feel. We also offer Invisalign® to patients who want to achieve a perfectly straight smile without the unsightly view of brackets and wires that come with traditional braces.
