Dr. Allen Petros is proud to serve families in the Mt. Pleasant community and continue the legacy of care established by Dr. Rickey Crane. Having worked closely with Dr. Crane, he understands the values this practice was built on honesty, quality dentistry, and treating patients like family.

Dr. Petros earned his Bachelor of Science from Moravian College in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, and went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry. After dental school, he moved to Allen, Texas, where he began practicing dentistry, later relocating to Rockwall, Texas.
