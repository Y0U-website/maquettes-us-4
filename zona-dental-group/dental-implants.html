Types of Dental Implants Explore the different options available to suit your needs. Dental implants are a versatile and effective solution for replacing missing teeth. At Zona Dental Group, we offer several types of dental implants to meet your specific needs and preferences.

Traditional dental implants are the most common and are suitable for most patients. These implants involve placing a medical-grade titanium screw into the jawbone, providing a sturdy foundation for a custom-made crown or denture.

Mini dental implants are an excellent option for patients with smaller spaces or front teeth that need replacement.

These reduced-diameter implants are less invasive and can be a great choice for areas with limited bone density.

If you’re looking for a quicker solution, same-day dental implants, also known as immediate-load dental implants, might be right for you.

This innovative procedure allows patients to receive their new teeth on the same day as their implant surgery, minimizing downtime and providing instant results.

Our experienced dental team will work with you to determine which type of dental implant best suits your needs, ensuring a personalized and effective treatment plan.

Embarking on the journey to a new smile with dental implants involves a few key steps, each designed to ensure your comfort and the success of the procedure.

During your initial consultation visit, our oral surgeon will assess your oral health, take digital images, and check your bone density. This comprehensive evaluation helps us develop a custom treatment plan for your specific needs. We will also discuss sedation dentistry options to ensure you are comfortable throughout the procedure.

On the day of your surgery visit, our skilled oral surgeon will place the dental implant, a medical-grade titanium screw, directly into your jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation dentistry to prevent pain and ensure your comfort. The implant will act as an artificial tooth root, providing a stable foundation for your new tooth.

After the implant has been placed, your jawbone will need time to heal and integrate with the implant, a process that can take several months. Once the implant is fully integrated, we will attach a custom-made crown, bridge, or fixed denture to complete your new smile. During your follow-up visit, we will ensure that everything looks great and functions with full comfort.

Our dental team will guide you through every step of the dental implant procedure, so you have a smooth and successful experience from start to finish.

Bone Loss Prevention Protect your oral health with dental implants. Dental implants are a solution for replacing missing teeth and play a crucial role in preventing bone loss.

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone that supported it can begin to deteriorate, leading to a range of oral health issues. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, much like natural teeth, keeping it healthy and preventing bone loss.

This stimulation is especially important for patients who have experienced significant tooth loss, as it helps maintain the shape and structure of the face.

By choosing dental implants, you can protect your oral health and maintain a healthy, natural-looking smile.

Our dental professionals will work with you to develop a treatment plan that addresses your needs and helps prevent bone loss.
