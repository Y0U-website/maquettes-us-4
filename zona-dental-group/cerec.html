The Process of Getting CEREC Same-Day Crowns

Step 1: Digital Planning

Your appointment begins with a comprehensive evaluation by Drs. Richardson, Garcia, and Arnone This link leads to Meet Us page . We use an intraoral scanner and digital scan technology to create digital impressions. This eliminates messy impressions and provides detailed images of your teeth and oral health.

Step 2: Custom Design

Next, the dentist designs your custom crown using computer aided design software. The digitally designed restoration is carefully shaped the same way your natural teeth function and appear. We ensure the new restoration supports comfort and appearance.

Step 3: In-Office Creation and Placement

Once the design is complete, a milling machine creates your crown from a ceramic block using CAD-CAM technology. The custom-made restoration is then placed and adjusted during the same appointment. You leave with a permanent restoration after just one visit.

FAQs About CEREC Same-Day Crowns

How long does a CEREC crown last?

With proper oral hygiene and the right care, CEREC crowns can last for many years. Regular dental visits help protect your restoration and oral health.

Are CEREC crowns as strong as traditional crowns?

Yes. CEREC crowns are made from high-quality ceramic materials that provide excellent durability. They offer the same strength and quality as traditional crowns while maintaining a natural appearance.

How long does the appointment take?

Most same-day crown appointments take between one and two hours. Because the crown is created in-office, there is no need to wait weeks for a laboratory to create it.

Can I get a crown after a root canal?

Yes. Many patients receive a crown after a root canal to protect a weakened tooth. CEREC same-day crowns often allow treatment to be completed in one appointment.

What makes CEREC different from traditional methods?

CEREC uses digital impressions, an intraoral scanner, CAD-CAM technology, computer aided manufacturing, and in-office production. This allows us to create CEREC restorations in a single appointment while eliminating temporary crowns and reducing the need for multiple appointments.
