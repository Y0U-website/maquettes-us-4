The Process of Getting Orthodontics in Tucson, AZ

Step 1: Your Free Consultation and First Visit

Your first visit includes a full smile review with our doctor. We take digital x-rays, discuss your concerns, and explore treatment options like braces or Clear Aligners. Our treatment coordinator helps you understand your orthodontic treatment plan and next steps.

Step 2: Custom Treatment Plan Creation

Drs. Richardson, Garcia, and Arnone create a personalized orthodontic treatment plan. We focus on teeth alignment, jaw growth, airway, and breathing. Each plan is designed to help you achieve a beautiful smile with a comfortable experience.

Step 3: Active Treatment And Smile Results

You begin treatment with braces or clear aligners. Our team schedules regular appointments to track progress and make adjustments. Once treatment is completed, we provide retainers to protect your new smile and maintain long-term results.

FAQs About Orthodontics in Tucson, AZ

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time varies based on your needs. Most patients complete treatment in 12 to 24 months. During your consultation, we review your timeline and create a plan that fits your life.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Yes, clear aligners like Invisalign® can treat many cases. They are a great option for teens and adults who want a discreet way to straighten teeth and improve their smile.

Is orthodontics only for kids?

No. Orthodontics is for all ages. Many adults choose treatment to improve their smile, health, and confidence later in life.

Will orthodontic treatment help with breathing and sleep?

In some cases, yes. Airway-focused orthodontics can improve breathing and sleep by supporting proper jaw and airway development.

What happens after treatment is completed?

After your treatment is completed, we provide retainers to maintain your results. This helps protect your new smile and keeps your teeth aligned long-term.
