Preventative Care and Dental Fillings

We recommend two professional cleanings a year to maintain a healthy smile. Routine exams and x-rays help prevent occurrence of cavities and gum disease.

Dental fillings, or dental restorations, are commonly used to treat cavities and are also used to repair damaged teeth due to wear or fracture. The cavity is removed and replaced by a filling material, preventing the cavity from spreading further.

Crowns and Bridges

A crown is a full surface restoration that can restore your tooth to last a life-time.With the latest technology, we work with a high-end dental lab to customize your crown to match your natural teeth.

Partials & Dentures

If dentures and partials make you think of metal hooks and floppy teeth, we can assure you that newer materials allow dentures and partials to resemble natural teeth in appearance and function.

A partial is a removable appliance that hooks onto your natural teeth and replaces missing teeth.

A denture is a removable appliance for patients with no remaining natural teeth.

Please be aware that the quality of your denture makes a difference in fit and longevity, so be sure to choose the right denture dentist instead of the cheapest.We can help you choose the best option that is specific to your needs and wants.

Braces and Orthodontic Solutions

We know that your smile affects your self-confidence. We offer traditional metal braces and clear aligners to straighten teeth.

Traditional metal braces are placed in-office and patient’s return monthly for wire changes.

Clear aligners are worn all day and night and patient’s return every 3 months for additional aligners.

Root Canal Therapy

I’m sure you’ve heard the myth, “Root canals are extremely painful!”Although this is a common misconception, a root canal is very similar to getting a standard filling.With today’s technology and anesthetics, root canals are a simple and easy solution to saving teeth that would otherwise be extracted.

Implants

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots and replace missing teeth.Implants are the standard of care because they provide more chewing function compared to dentures and partials, and have a great long-term prognosis. Like a natural tooth root, an implant resembles a screw and is placed in the jaw-bone.After it is placed, it is not visible in the mouth and a crown is attached to it.

Extractions and Emergency Care

When a tooth cannot be restored, it is necessary to remove the tooth to prevent infection.We know losing teeth is a scary thing, and we take every measure to ensure the most pleasant experience possible.

Whitening and Cosmetic Dentistry

Whiter teeth make everyone look and feel better. To brighten natural teeth, we offer in-office and at-home whitening treatments. To change the shape and color of existing teeth, we offer Veneers and Crowns. Come in for a consultation to learn what the best option is for you!
