Dental fillings, or dental restorations, are commonly used to treat cavities and are also used to repair damaged teeth due to wear or fracture. The cavity is removed and replaced by a filling material, preventing the cavity from spreading further.

If dentures and partials make you think of metal hooks and floppy teeth, we can assure you that newer materials allow dentures and partials to resemble natural teeth in appearance and function.

A partial is a removable appliance that hooks onto your natural teeth and replaces missing teeth.

A denture is a removable appliance for patients with no remaining natural teeth.

Please be aware that the quality of your denture makes a difference in fit and longevity, so be sure to choose the right denture dentist instead of the cheapest.We can help you choose the best option that is specific to your needs and wants.

Traditional metal braces are placed in-office and patient’s return monthly for wire changes.
