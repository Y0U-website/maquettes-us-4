Transform Your Smile with Dental Implants

You might feel embarrassed if you have missing teeth. It can harm your self-confidence not to have a Hollywood smile. Everyone wants to be proud of themselves when they smile in the mirror, after all. Luckily, there are numerous options for rebuilding lost teeth today.

Dental implants are considered to be the most effective and most lifelike replacement for lost teeth. Here at Bayside Family Dentistry in Bayside, New York, Dr. Narine Cazazian highly recommends this treatment if you are worried about your smile after losing a tooth.

Dental implants, including All-on-X (4, 5, 6) implants, offered at Bayside Family Dentistry provide a comprehensive solution for replacing missing teeth. Dr. Narine Cazazian , ensures patients receive personalized care and optimal results.

What are Dental Implants, including All-on-X (4, 5, 6) implants?

  • Dental implants, including the All-on-X (4, 5, 6) technique, are innovative solutions for replacing missing teeth.
  • All-on-X refers to a specific implant protocol where a fixed prosthesis is supported by four, five, or six implants strategically placed in the jawbone.
  • Unlike traditional dental implants, which may require one implant per missing tooth, All-on-X utilizes fewer implants, making it a more efficient and cost-effective option.
  • All-on-X implants provide stable support for dental prosthetics, such as dentures or bridges, offering improved chewing ability, speech, and aesthetics.
  • These implants are suitable for individuals with significant tooth loss or those seeking full arch restoration, providing a permanent and natural-looking solution.
  • The All-on-X technique reduces the need for bone grafting procedures in patients with bone loss, as the implants are placed in areas of optimal bone density.
  • By restoring a full arch of teeth with All-on-X implants, patients can enjoy restored confidence and quality of life without the inconvenience of removable dentures.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants offer numerous benefits, including:

  • Comprehensive Restoration: Unlike other treatments that only address the missing crown, dental implants replace both the crown and root of the tooth, preventing jawbone deterioration.
  • Natural Function: Restored teeth function like natural teeth, allowing patients to enjoy their favorite foods and maintain oral hygiene habits without restrictions.
  • Aesthetic Appeal: Dental implants closely resemble natural teeth in appearance, ensuring a seamless smile restoration.
  • Longevity: With proper care, dental implants can last for upwards of thirty years or even a lifetime, making them a long-term investment in oral health.

What is the dental implant process?

The dental implant process involves several steps:

  • Consultation: Dr. Narine Cazazian evaluates your oral health and determines if you’re a suitable candidate for dental implants.
  • Implant Placement: Titanium implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they integrate with the surrounding bone tissue during a healing period.
  • Healing Period: Over several months, the implant fuses with the jawbone, forming a stable foundation for the restoration.
  • Restoration Placement: Once the implant has fully integrated, a custom-designed dental crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the implant, restoring function and aesthetics.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on individual factors such as the number of implants needed, the complexity of the case, and any additional procedures required. It’s essential to schedule a consultation with Dr. Narine Cazazian to receive an accurate cost estimate tailored to your specific needs.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

While discomfort during the implant placement surgery is minimal thanks to local anesthesia, some patients may experience mild soreness or swelling during the healing period.

Dr. Narine Cazazian ensures patient comfort throughout the procedure and provides post-operative care instructions to manage any discomfort effectively.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The dental implant process typically spans several months to allow for proper healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone.

However, the timeline can vary depending on individual healing rates and the complexity of the case. Dr. Narine Cazazian develops a personalized treatment plan and keeps patients informed at every step of the process.

Are dental implants suitable for everyone?

While most individuals with good oral and overall health are suitable candidates for dental implants, certain factors such as inadequate jawbone density or uncontrolled systemic conditions may affect candidacy. Dr. Narine Cazazian conducts a thorough evaluation to determine if dental implants are the right choice for each patient.

How do I care for dental implants?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth and involves regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups. Dr. Narine Cazazian provides personalized oral hygiene instructions and recommends maintaining a healthy lifestyle to ensure the longevity of dental implants.
