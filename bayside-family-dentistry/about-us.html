Get to Know Us: Your Trusted Dental Team at Bayside Family Dentistry

Your One-Stop Dental Office: Bayside Family Dentistry

Our Journey: Discover the History Behind Our Practice

Bayside Family Dentistry was founded by Dr. Aram Cazazian in 1975. He served the community for almost 50 years with his love and passion for dentistry before retiring in 2024 when he excitedly transitioned the practice to Dr. Narine Cazazian.

Meet Dr. Cazazian: Your Expert in Personalized Dental Care

Dr. Narine Cazazian, affectionately known as Dr. Caz, brings over a decade of experience to Bayside Family Dentistry. Specializing in preventive, restorative, surgical, and cosmetic dental treatments, Dr. Caz is committed to providing comprehensive care to each patient. With her expertise and dedication, she strives to deliver exceptional results and ensure patient satisfaction.

Meet Our Friendly Team: Dedicated to Your Comfort and Care

At Bayside Family Dentistry, our friendly team is here to make your dental experience as pleasant as possible. From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted with warmth and professionalism. Our multilingual staff is proficient in English, Spanish, Russian, Armenian, and Portuguese, allowing us to serve individuals from diverse backgrounds with ease.

Convenient Location and Top-Notch Amenities: Making Your Visit Easy and Enjoyable

Conveniently located across the street from the Bayside LIRR train station and two blocks from Northern Blvd. and Bell Blvd., our office is easily accessible for patients in the area. We also offer free on-premises parking, providing added convenience for our patients. At Bayside Family Dentistry, we prioritize your comfort and convenience every step of the way.
