Meet Dr. Christiane Rocha, formerly Dr. Christiane Murillo, a compassionate general dentist at Jupiter Smile Care Dentistry in Jupiter, Florida.

A healthy smile starts with trust.

After getting married in 2023, Dr. Christiane Murillo became Dr. Christiane Rocha. It is a new name for the same trusted dentist, familiar smile and compassionate care her patients know.

Dr. Rocha is a general dentist at Jupiter Smile Care Dentistry in Jupiter, Florida. A South Florida native, she believes exceptional dental care begins with listening. She takes the time to answer questions, explain treatment options clearly and help every patient feel comfortable, informed and confident in their care.

Dr. Rocha earned her Bachelor of Science in Food Science and Human Nutrition from the University of Florida. She then earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine before returning to South Florida to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the Bruce W. Carter Department of Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Miami.

During her residency, Dr. Rocha received advanced training in comprehensive dental care, restorative dentistry, treatment planning and caring for patients with complex dental needs. She continues to expand her knowledge through ongoing education, combining modern, evidence-based dentistry with a warm and personal approach.

At Jupiter Smile Care Dentistry, Dr. Rocha is committed to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. She strives to treat every patient with the same kindness, respect and attention she would want for her own family.

Outside the office, Dr. Rocha enjoys spending time with her family, relaxing at the beach, exploring South Florida’s local parks and getting lost in a good book. She also loves visiting Walt Disney World—because every smile can use a little extra magic.
