Single Tooth Implant FAQs

Answers to the questions we hear most from patients in Hawthorne and across the South Bay.

The full treatment timeline from placement to final crown is typically 4–8 months. Implant surgery itself takes about an hour under local anaesthesia. Osseointegration — where the post fuses to the bone — takes 3–6 months. Crown fitting is then completed in one or two appointments. You’ll receive a temporary restoration so you’re never without a tooth during this period.

The surgery is performed under local anaesthesia — you’ll feel pressure but no pain during the procedure. Most patients report that the recovery is more comfortable than they anticipated; mild soreness for 2–3 days managed with over-the-counter pain relievers is typical. Many patients return to work the following day. We also offer sedation options for anxious patients.

Yes — this is one of the most important advantages implants have over all other replacements. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to shrink due to lack of stimulation — a process called resorption. The titanium implant post mimics a natural tooth root, transmitting bite forces directly into the bone and halting resorption. Bridges and dentures sit on the surface and provide no such stimulation.
