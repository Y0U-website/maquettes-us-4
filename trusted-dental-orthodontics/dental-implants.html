Dental Implant FAQs

Answers to the most common questions we hear from implant patients in Hawthorne and across the South Bay.

Most adults in good general health are candidates for dental implants. The key requirements are adequate jawbone density (or the ability to build it with grafting), healthy gums, and no uncontrolled systemic conditions. We evaluate your candidacy with a 3D scan and medical review at your consultation.

The titanium implant post can last a lifetime — 40+ years in documented cases. The crown on top typically lasts 10–25 years depending on bite forces, material, and maintenance. With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental implants are the most durable tooth replacement option available.

The surgery is performed under local anesthesia and is generally well-tolerated. Most patients report that it’s far less uncomfortable than they expected — often less than a tooth extraction. Post-operative soreness for 2–5 days is normal and well-managed with prescribed or OTC pain medication.

Some PPO dental plans now include partial coverage for dental implants — typically covering the crown portion. Medical insurance may cover implants when teeth were lost due to accident or medical condition. We thoroughly check all available benefits and offer financing options to make implants accessible regardless of coverage.

Insufficient bone doesn’t automatically disqualify you. Bone grafting and sinus lift procedures rebuild the bone foundation needed for a stable implant. We perform these procedures in-house as part of a coordinated implant plan. Many patients who were told they "couldn’t have implants" elsewhere have been successfully treated here.
