  • Subperiosteal Implants Designed for patients with reduced bone volume, these implants use a metal framework positioned on top of the jawbone and beneath the gum tissue to provide secure support when traditional implants are not an option.

Can Dental Implants Be Done In One Day?

Once you’ve completed your consultation and evaluation with the dentist, it’s often possible to receive dental implants in just a single day. That said, your gums and jawbone must go through a healing phase before the permanent crown or bridge can be attached. During this time, a temporary tooth will be placed to maintain appearance and function. For patients needing several implants, the process may be divided into multiple visits. On average, implants require about 3–4 months of healing before the final restoration can be secured.

Are Dental Implants Painful?

Dental implants are widely regarded as one of the most reliable and effective solutions for replacing missing or damaged teeth. The procedure itself is not painful, as local or general anesthesia is used to keep the treatment area completely numb. Once the anesthesia wears off, some mild soreness or discomfort is common, but it is usually temporary and manageable.

What To Expect After Your Procedure

Like any procedure, time for healing and recovery will be needed. Patients typically experience swelling around the gums and face, slight bruising, minor bleeding, and some pain or discomfort at the implant site. Dentists recommend avoiding hard and chewy foods after the procedure and will likely prescribe you pain medication and antibiotics to help you heal and prevent infection.
