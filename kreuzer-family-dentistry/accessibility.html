Accessibility

  • Full keyboard operability every link, button, menu, and FAQ can be used without a mouse
  • Clearly visible focus outlines, so keyboard users always know where they are
  • Color and contrast chosen to meet WCAG 2.1 AA for readable text and controls
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences, minimizing animation for those who ask for it
  • Descriptive labels and names for icons, links, buttons, and the practice logo
  • A responsive layout that supports browser zoom and text resizing

Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively identifying and addressing any barriers. If you encounter a problem, please let us know your feedback helps us improve.
