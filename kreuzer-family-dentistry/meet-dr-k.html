Meet Dr. K

From Bucharest to Richmond

Dr. Kreuzer graduated summa cum laude from Virginia Commonwealth University in 2005 with degrees in biology and chemistry. In 2009, she earned a Doctor in Dental Surgery, with honors, from VCU School of Dentistry. Dr. Kreuzer’s passion for dentistry, community service, and mentorship has led to many leadership roles and recognitions.

" One of my fondest memories as a dentist is traveling to Jamaica with the VCU School of Dentistry. We helped so many people in the Trelawny Parish community — it was so inspiring to work alongside the students and faculty. "

— Dr. Kreuzer, on volunteering for The Jamaica Project

Care that reaches beyond the office

Community service is at the heart of Dr. Kreuzer&#x27;s work. She has been a pioneer in providing free dental care across the Richmond metro — and regularly visits local schools and nursing homes to promote dental health awareness.

A trusted voice in the profession

Dr. Kreuzer is recognized for outstanding leadership in dentistry across Richmond and the State of Virginia. She works with local and statewide dentists, VCU Dental School, and State of Virginia lawmakers to advance the standards and quality of oral health care in Virginia.

In 2025, Dr. Kreuzer was named a Fellow of the Pierre Fauchard Academy — one of dentistry&#x27;s highest international honors, held by fewer than one percent of dentists worldwide. She is also a 2019 Fellow of the Virginia Dental Association, nominated by her peers.
