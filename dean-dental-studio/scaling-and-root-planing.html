Scaling And Root Planing

Scaling and root planing in St. Charles, IL, also known as deep cleaning, is a dental procedure used to treat gum disease. This non-surgical procedure can help restore gum and oral health. The process involves removing plaque, tartar, and bacteria from the teeth and gums to prevent further damage.

The Process of Scaling and Root Planing

The dentist in St. Charles, IL will perform scaling, which involves using special tools to carefully remove built-up plaque and tartar from above and below the gumline. This helps to eliminate any harmful bacteria that may be present in these areas. Next comes root planing, where the dentist smoothes out the rough surfaces of the tooth roots. This helps to remove any remaining bacteria and provides a clean surface for your gums to reattach.

The procedure may require multiple visits depending on the severity of your condition. The dentist will thoroughly examine your mouth before determining how many sessions are needed.

During each visit, local anesthesia may be used to ensure comfort throughout the process. After treatment, you might experience some sensitivity or mild discomfort, which can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

It’s important to note that scaling and root planing is not a one-time fix for gum disease. Regular dental cleanings paired with good oral hygiene practices at home are essential for maintaining healthy gums long-term. Call us to learn more.

The Benefits of Scaling and Root Planing

Effective Treatment for Gum Disease

One of the major benefits of scaling and root planing is its effectiveness in treating gum disease. Removing the accumulated plaque and tartar from your tooth roots helps eliminate harmful bacteria that can lead to gingivitis or periodontitis. This not only helps prevent further damage to your gums but also reduces inflammation and promotes overall oral health.

Restoring Smile Aesthetics

Another advantage of scaling and root planing is that it can help restore your smile’s aesthetics. When you have excessive tartar buildup beneath your gumline, it can cause your teeth to appear longer due to receding gums. By addressing this issue through deep cleaning, you may notice an improvement in the appearance of your teeth, making them look healthier and more attractive.

Preventing Tooth Loss

Regular scaling and root planing sessions are also beneficial for preventing tooth loss caused by advanced gum disease. By effectively removing bacteria-ridden deposits around the roots of your teeth, this treatment allows the gums to better reattach to the teeth, reducing the risk of the teeth becoming loose or losing them altogether.

Better Overall Health

Furthermore, undergoing scaling and root planing can contribute to better overall systemic health. Studies have shown a link between chronic oral infections like periodontal disease and an increased risk for conditions such as heart disease, diabetes complications, respiratory problems, and stroke, among others. By eliminating these harmful bacteria through deep cleaning procedures like scaling and root planing, we decrease their potential negative impact on our general well-being.
