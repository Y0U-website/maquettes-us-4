Give Your Child a Head Start With Early Functional Orthodontics

What Is Early Functional Orthodontics?

Early functional orthodontics, sometimes called interceptive orthodontics, focuses on guiding a child’s jaw growth and tooth development while their mouth is still growing. Rather than waiting until all the adult teeth have come in, this approach addresses problems while there is still room to correct them naturally.

Since most children settle into their adult smile by around age thirteen, starting early orthodontic treatment gives our team the opportunity to work with your child’s natural growth instead of against it. Dr. Anna Patras and our team commonly recommend an orthodontic evaluation by age seven, even if your child still has plenty of baby teeth.

The Benefits of Starting Early

Early orthodontic treatment through our two-phase approach offers real advantages for growing smiles. Here is why so many families in Sparta trust early functional orthodontics for their kids:

  • Guides jaw growth so there’s enough room for incoming adult teeth
  • Corrects harmful habits like thumb sucking or mouth breathing before they cause lasting damage
  • Improves airway function, which can support better sleep and breathing
  • Reduces the need for tooth extractions later in life
  • Simplifies future treatment, often shortening the time needed for Phase Two braces or Invisalign
  • Builds confidence by improving your child’s smile and speech development sooner

What to Expect During Treatment

Early functional orthodontics typically unfolds in two phases. Phase One usually begins between ages seven and ten and focuses on guiding jaw development, creating space, and correcting bite issues using tools like space maintainers or expanders. After Phase One, there is often a resting period while the remaining adult teeth erupt naturally. Phase Two follows once most or all adult teeth are in, usually involving braces or clear aligners to fine-tune the bite and align the smile fully.

Throughout both phases, our team monitors your child’s growth closely, adjusting the treatment plan as needed to keep everything on track.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance for early orthodontic treatment?

We work as an out-of-network provider and are happy to help you understand your out-of-network benefits before treatment begins.

How do I know if my child needs early functional orthodontics?

An orthodontic evaluation around age seven is the best way to find out. Dr. Patras can identify developing issues with jaw growth, spacing, or bite alignment early.

Is early orthodontic treatment painful for children?

Most children experience only mild, temporary discomfort as their mouth adjusts to any appliances. Our team takes extra care to keep young patients comfortable throughout treatment.

What age should Phase One treatment start?

Phase One generally begins between ages seven and ten, though every child’s development is different.
