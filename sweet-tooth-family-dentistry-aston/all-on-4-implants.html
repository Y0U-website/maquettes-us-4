Detailed Procedure of All-On-4 Implants

The All-On-4 procedure at Sweet Tooth Family Dentistry begins with a personalized consultation by Dr. Pooja Desai to assess your dental health and discuss your goals. Utilizing advanced imaging techniques, we plan the precise placement of the four implants in areas of the jaw with the highest bone density. During the procedure, temporary teeth are placed on the same day, allowing you to go home with a functional and aesthetically pleasing smile. Over the next few months, your implants will integrate with your jawbone, a process known as osseointegration.

All-On-4 versus Traditional Dental Implants

Compared to traditional implants, All-On-4 offers a less invasive solution with a shorter treatment and recovery time. While traditional implants often require multiple surgeries and a lengthy healing period, All-On-4 simplifies the process by using fewer implants and eliminating the need for bone grafts in most cases. This makes it an ideal solution for patients with significant tooth loss who seek an efficient and less cumbersome alternative.

Post-Procedure Care and Recovery

After receiving All-On-4 implants, patients typically experience a brief recovery period. Sweet Tooth Family Dentistry provides detailed aftercare instructions to ensure a smooth transition. Key to the success of your new implants is adherence to recommended oral hygiene practices, including gentle brushing, flossing, and regular check-ups. Following these guidelines helps maintain the longevity and effectiveness of your new teeth.
