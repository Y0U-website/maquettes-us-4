  • Mini implants – Mini implants work the same way as traditional implants, but they are usually much smaller and thinner, making them a good option for patients who do not have enough jaw bone to support a traditional implant. Usually, mini implants are not used to restore a single tooth. Instead, multiple mini implants are used to support an implant-supported bridge or denture.
  • Full-Arch Implants – These implants are used to restore an entire arch (or both arches) of missing teeth. Supported by a series of 4-6 implants, a restoration is attached. These restorations include implant-supported dentures and fixed bridges.

Am I A Good Candidate For Dental Implants?

To be approved for implant surgery, a patient must be in good overall health and have a healthy mouth. This means that you must not have any decayed teeth or be suffering from periodontal disease . Your jaw must also be strong enough to support a dental implant. During your consultation, Dr. Babb will assess your health to determine whether you are a candidate for treatment.

Are Dental Implants Covered By Insurance?

In most cases, dental implants are not covered by insurance. However, some insurance companies will pay for part of the implant procedure. For more information, contact your dental insurance provider, or ask the team be glad to help you understand your insurance coverage and maximize your benefits!
