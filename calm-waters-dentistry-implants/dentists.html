MEET DR. WENTWORTH

DR. NICK WENTWORTH

Dr. Nicholas D. Wentworth graduated from the University of New England College of Dental Medicine in Portland, Maine, where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree and completed two clinical rotations in public health settings. Dr. Wentworth relocated to North Carolina after dental school and practice in Hickory for several years before purchasing a long-standing practice in 2019 in Mooresville. Since then we expanted to Statesville forming Calm Waters Dentistry & Implants .

Two of his greatest passions are working with his hands and helping others, which makes working in the dental field a perfect fit. Dr. Wentworth is dedicated to providing high quality dental care to all of his patients and is excited to serve both the Mooresville and Statesville communities.

LIFELONG DENTAL CARE YOU CAN TRUST

Dr. Wentworth is a member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry , a fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and an inductee of Omicron Kappa Upsilon Dental Honor Society. He lives in Mooresville, North Carolina, with his wife and two daughters. When he isn’t at the office or chasing after his girls, he can be found barefoot skiing on Lake Norman.
