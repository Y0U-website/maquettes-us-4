AM I A GOOD CANIDATE FOR DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Most healthy adults with enough bone in their jaw are candidates for dental implants. Healthy gums and sufficient bone to hold the implant are key factors we’ll evaluate during your consultation at Calm Waters Dentistry & Implants. Your overall oral health and medical history will also be taken into consideration.

HOW LONG DO DENTAL IMPLANTS LAST?

With proper care, dental implants can last for decades or even a lifetime. While the implants themselves won’t get cavities , they still require good oral hygiene to prevent gum disease, which can affect their success. Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups are essential.
