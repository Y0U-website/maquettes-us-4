A Long Island, NY native, Dr. Colleen Ahern chose dentistry on a whim when her parents told her she needed to decide on a career. It wasn’t until later that she fell in love with the industry and helping people become the best, most fulfilled versions of themselves through their smile.

She strives to create an experience that makes her patients feel pampered while they’re hanging out with friends. Not only do her patients love the experience, but with her meticulous treatment planning and skilled hands, they love the results, too.

Anxious about visiting the dentist? Concerned about discomfort? We’ve got you covered. Our sedation options ensure a calm, stress-free experience, making your time in the chair as comfortable as possible.

No matter your past dental experiences, you’ll immediately feel the warmth of our practice. We treat every patient with the same care and compassion we’d offer our own loved ones.

We believe in listening before acting. Our team takes the time to understand your needs, goals, and concerns so we can provide truly personalized dental care.

Les soins

Having a dental practice perform a checkup and regular cleaning gives a preventive approach to dental care.

When you have missing teeth, an alternative solution to dentures is dental implants. While dentures are removable, dental implants provide permanent new teeth to restore your mouth’s function and appearance.

A common request we receive is how to make a smile whiter and brighter. This is a relatively simple and quick treatment option that can be performed periodically.
