FAQs About Dental Implants

Do dental implants hurt?

The implant procedure is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring you’re comfortable throughout. Most patients report less discomfort than expected, often comparing it to a tooth extraction. Post-procedure, you may experience some swelling and mild soreness, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team at WA Dental provides comprehensive aftercare instructions to ensure a smooth recovery process.

How to get help with dental implants?

The best way to start is by scheduling a consultation with an experienced implant dentist. At our office, we offer thorough evaluations to determine if implants are right for you. During this visit, we’ll assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and outline potential treatment options. We’re here to answer all your questions and provide the information you need to make an informed decision about your dental health.

Why no dairy after dental implant?

We typically advise avoiding dairy products for a short period following implant surgery. This recommendation is primarily because dairy can interfere with the absorption of antibiotics that might be prescribed post-surgery. Additionally, some dairy products may increase mucus production, which can be uncomfortable during the initial healing phase. This restriction is temporary, and we’ll provide you with a comprehensive list of dietary guidelines to follow during your recovery.

How long does the dental implant take?

The complete dental implant process usually spans several months. The initial implant placement surgery is often completed in a single appointment. Following this, there’s a healing period of about 3-6 months, during which the implant integrates with your jawbone. Once healing is complete, we’ll place the abutment and your custom-made crown. While this process requires some patience, the long-term benefits of a permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement make it a worthwhile investment in your oral health.
