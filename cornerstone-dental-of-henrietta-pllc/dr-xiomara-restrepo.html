Dr xiomara restrepo

Dr.Restrepo is a caring and experienced orthodontist who is highly trained and skilled in the practice of orthodontics and temporomandibular joint disorders.

Education

In 2001, she graduated and received her dental degree, as class valedictorian, from Universidad del Valle. Upon graduation, she worked as a general dentist in Colombia before moving to Rochester in 2005. She continued her education at the Eastman Institute for Oral Health at the University of Rochester, ultimately completing her Orthodontic Residency in 2012.

Her CV is extremely impressive, including multiple research publications as well as two Temporomandibular Joint Disorder fellowship programs completed at Nova Southeastern University in 2002 and University of Rochester 2006.

Away From The Practice

In addition to private practice, Dr. Restrepo enjoys a teaching position at the Eastman Institute for Oral Health as a Clinical Assistant Professor in the Orthodontic Department. Outside of the office, she enjoys paddle boarding, hiking, and skiing. Most of all, she loves spending time with her son, Jacob, and her dog, Manolo
