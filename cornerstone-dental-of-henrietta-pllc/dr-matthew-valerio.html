Dr matthew valerio

Focused on helping his patients achieve optimal oral health in a fun and relaxed environment, Dr. Matthew Valerio and his team do everything they can to calm any fears and/or anxieties patients may experience. He derives great satisfaction from helping people smile with healthy confidence and would love the opportunity to care for your family’s oral health needs!

Education

Dr. Matthew Valerio earned his Bachelor of Music in 2009 from Eastman School of Music while simultaneously attending the University of Rochester, where he earned a Bachelor of Arts in Biology. He went on to achieve his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2014 from the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine (UBSDM) where he was the recipient of the Richard A Powell Award . He completed a one-year General Practice Residency at Rochester General Hospital and subsequently has been practicing at Cornerstone Dental LLP since 2015.

Away From The Practice

When not caring for smiles, he loves spending time with his wife Barbora and their three young daughters. He also enjoys playing piano, Italian culture, and rooting for the New York Yankees and the Syracuse Orange.
