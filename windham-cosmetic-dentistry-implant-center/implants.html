WHAT IS THE DENTAL IMPLANT PROCESS LIKE?

The process typically involves several steps:

  • Consultation and planning: We’ll assess your needs, review your medical history, and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Surgery: The implant fixture is surgically placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing and Integration: The implant must integrate within your bone. This stage cannot be rushed as it is limited by the body’s ability to heal around the bone. This can take anywhere from 3-6 months.
  • Abutment: The abutment is a component that attaches to the implant and provides the shape for the crown.
  • Crown: A custom-made crown is tooth-colored and cemented to the abutment, completing the entire process.

IS THE DENTAL IMPLANT PROCEDURE PAINFUL?

We make every effort to ensure your comfort during the procedure. We provide TVs with wireless headphones to take your focus off the procedure. You can also bring your own headphones and listen to music/podcasts during the procedure. Sedation is also an option we can consider during the consultation/pre-planning process. There will not be any discomfort during the procedure and most patients report minimal discomfort post-surgery.

HOW LONG DO DENTAL IMPLANTS LAST?

There is no limit to how long an implant will last as long as you maintain ideal oral & systemic health. We recommend consistent hygiene appointments every 3-6 months to best maintain your oral health and monitor any conditions that develop.

WHAT IS THE COST OF DENTAL IMPLANTS?

The cost varies depending on the number of implants needed and the complexity of your case. We’ll provide you with a detailed treatment plan and cost estimate during your consultation & treatment planning appointment.

HOW DO I CARE FOR MY DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth. Brush, floss, and irrigate routinely. Maintain regular checkups and cleanings to stay ahead of any problems that might develop.

WHAT ARE THE LATEST TECHNOLOGIES USED IN DENTAL IMPLANT PLACEMENT?

We utilize advanced technologies like intraoral scanning (no impressions), CBCT (a dental-specific CT scan), and guided surgery to ensure precise implant placement and optimal results.
