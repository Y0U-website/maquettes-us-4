High School Group Visit Request

Bring your high school groups to visit campus in-person! You can pick and choose what you would like your students to take part in, or we can plan the day for you. Your students will have the opportunity to learn about campus, academics, extra-curricular activities, housing and TRIO! Lunch on campus (at no cost to you) is also available throughout the school year.
