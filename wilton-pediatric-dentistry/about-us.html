Meet Dr. Ashleigh Sebro

Even though she’s a board-certified pediatric dentist , Dr. Ashleigh Sebro believes her most valuable experience was gained as a child. Growing up, her dentist, Dr. Jones, was always a positive role model for her. He showed her what it means to be a compassionate dentist who puts kids first. After a particularly positive appointment at age 5, her path toward dentistry was set.

Dr. Sebro has dedicated her life to becoming a dentist who could positively impact children the way Dr. Jones did for her. She received her BSc from McGill University before graduating cum laude with her DDS from SUNY Buffalo. After dental school, Dr. Sebro furthered her training with an advanced education in pediatric dentistry at Temple University Hospital.

Dr. Sebro brings her positive attitude and compassionate disposition to the Wilton community every day in practice. In addition to founding Wilton Pediatric Dentistry she is the founder of The Connecticut Tongue Tie Center . When she is not at the office she enjoys spending time with her husband and two young children.

Meet Dr. Sundas Khan

As a board-eligible pediatric dentist , Dr. Sundas Khan believes in providing her patients with a calming and positive experience at the office. Originally from Islamabad Pakistan, Dr. Khan has always been drawn to hands on and creative work and loves dentistry because it allows her to make a difference in her patient’s lives. Working with children and building long term relationships with families is what drew Dr. Khan to pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Khan initially completed her BDS at Riphah International University. Upon coming to the United States, she completed a post-graduate training program in esthetic dentistry at UCLA. She then furthered her dental training at Boston University where she earned her DMD. Dr. Khan completed her pediatric specialty training at Brookdale Hospital in Brooklyn NY where she received her advanced education in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Khan brings a keen esthetic eye and calm and pleasant demeanor to the Wilton Pediatric Dentistry team. When she is not at the office she enjoys spending time with her husband and new daughter.

The Wilton Difference

Our practice is outfitted with the latest dental technologies, but our real emphasis is on how we interact with our patients. We aim to create lasting bonds with every child we work with so they are well-prepared and unafraid for dental visits as adults. From the moment you enter our office, our goal is to make you and your child feel at home. Our serene atmosphere and child-friendly reception area provide a calming environment from the start. And every time your child visits us, they’ll be greeted by familiar faces ensuring individualized treatment and customized care.
