DR. JULIJA CARTELLI

FAMILY DENTIST IN CAPE CORAL AND FORT MYERS, FL

Born and raised in Lithuania, her mother, who was also a dentist, has always been Dr. Cartelli’s role model. So it’s no surprise that she followed in her foot steps and became a dentist also! Dr. Cartelli studied dentistry at the Kaunas University of Medicine for five years, where she graduated with a Masters Degree in dental medicine. Following graduation she began practicing dentistry in her mother’s office until the love of her life, Vincenzo, asked her to marry him and move to the United States and reside in Boca Raton, Florida.

Shortly after their wedding she was accepted to the Nova Southeastern University International Dentist Graduate Program, where she studied an additional three years and received her Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry degree. In 2011 the Cartelli family decided to move to Cape Coral, where it is a more relaxed and peaceful atmosphere, to raise their three beautiful children.

In her spare time, Dr. Cartelli enjoys traveling, watching movies and reading books. She is extremely interested in alternative medicine as well as the holistic aspect of dentistry. She learned from her mother how important it is to treat all people, especially her patients, with fairness, respect and understanding. She strives to offer the best quality of dentistry according to each patient’s needs and desires.
