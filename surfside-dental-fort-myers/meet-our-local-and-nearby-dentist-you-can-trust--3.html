DR. ARIANA ALMASI KOEHLER

FAMILY DENTIST IN CAPE CORAL AND FORT MYERS, FL

Dr. Ariana Almasi Koehler is a born and raised Floridian. Growing up as a first generation American to immigrant parents in Miami, she attended the University of Miami, where she received her Bachelor Degree in Biology and English Literature. She then went on, wasting no time, to receive her Doctorate of Dental Medicine at Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

Shortly after graduating dental school, Dr. Almasi moved to Fort Myers for a change of pace. Here, she met her husband in 2015 and has been practicing dentistry in the area for over 10 years. Dr. Almasi routinely performs basic and cosmetic dental procedures, in addition to implant surgery and restorations. She is also an AAID Fellow and is certified in Invisalign and Clear Correct .

Throughout her career, Dr. Almasi has always stressed putting her patients first, paying special attention to their dental concerns and functional dental needs. Her priority is to make every dental visit as comfortable and informative as possible, and to alleviate the fear that shadows dental treatment.

However, when she is not in the office, Dr. Almasi enjoys spending time with her two girls and husband, bicycling, hiking, watercolor painting and coming up with new dinner recipes to cook at home.
