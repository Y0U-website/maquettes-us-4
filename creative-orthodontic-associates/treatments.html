Treatment Options Early Treatment Ever wondered why orthodontists recommend seeing kids by age 7? Early treatment allows us to catch small issues before they turn into bigger ones. By guiding jaw growth, creating room for incoming teeth, and correcting harmful habits early on, we can often prevent the need for more complex treatments down the road.

As a parent, you want to give your child the best start—and that includes their smile. Early orthodontic care can make a big difference in your child’s comfort, confidence, and long-term oral health. At Creative Orthodontic Associates, we keep things fun, gentle, and stress-free so your little one feels right at home.

Adult Treatment

Thinking about straightening your teeth as an adult? You’re not alone—and you have options. Whether it’s braces or Invisalign, orthodontic treatment can improve not only your smile but your overall oral health. From small touch-ups to full smile transformations, we’re here to make it happen—comfortably and discreetly.

Aligning your teeth as an adult isn’t just about looks—it’s about function, too. A properly aligned bite can reduce jaw pain, prevent abnormal wear, and make daily habits like brushing and chewing much easier. At Creative Orthodontic Associates, we focus on both aesthetics and long-term health, so your smile works as great as it looks.

Invisalign ®

Invisalign is a clear, comfortable, and discreet way to straighten your teeth—no brackets or wires needed. Using a series of custom-made aligners, it gently shifts your teeth into place while fitting seamlessly into your daily routine. Invisalign gives you the freedom to eat what you want and smile with confidence throughout your treatment.

At Creative Orthodontic Associates, we specialize in Invisalign for kids, teens, and adults across South Miami, Kendall, and beyond. Our board-certified orthodontist, Dr. Guia, creates personalized Invisalign plans that are both effective and convenient.

Teen Treatment

The teen years are the perfect time to align your smile and boost your confidence. We offer flexible options—like braces or Invisalign®—that work with busy school, sports, or social schedules. Whether your teen prefers something bold or barely there, we’ve got them covered.

Families across South Miami, Kendall, and Coral Way trust us to guide their teens through treatment with care, patience, and results. From the first visit to the final retainer, we’re here to make the journey smooth, comfortable, and worth it.

Orthodontic Appliances

Orthodontic appliances go beyond braces and Invisalign clear aligners. These tools are designed to guide jaw growth, correct habits, or create space for permanent teeth—depending on your child’s needs. We use a wide range of orthodontic appliances—like habit appliances, expanders, anterior bite plates, and more—to support proper growth, bite correction, and long-term oral health.

For more complex cases, we also offer options like MARA, Herbst, and reverse-pull headgear. These tools help our doctors deliver precise, effective care—so you can feel confident every step of the way.

Braces

Braces are a popular and effective way to improve both the appearance and function of your smile. Whether you’re dealing with crooked teeth, crowding, or bite issues like an overbite or underbite, braces can help create a healthier, more confident you.

At Creative Orthodontic Associates, we offer several types of braces—including metal, ceramic, lingual, and self-ligating—so you can choose what works best for your lifestyle. Want to keep it subtle? Go with clear ceramic. Prefer to show off your style? Add fun color bands! Whatever your goals, we’ll guide you every step of the way.

Cosmetic Options

Sometimes the smallest details make the biggest difference. At Creative Orthodontic Associates, we offer cosmetic enhancements like tooth whitening, enamel shaping, and gum contouring to help you achieve a polished, balanced smile.

Another option is veneers, which are thin shells of porcelain that are bonded to the front of your teeth to improve their appearance. Veneers can help correct issues like discoloration, chipping, and gaps between teeth.

Whether you’re prepping for a big event or simply want to boost your smile’s natural beauty, our team is here to help. Patients from South Miami, Kendall, and nearby areas love how a few subtle refinements can elevate their entire look. Let’s explore your cosmetic options together!

Retainers

Retainers are a key part of every orthodontic treatment. After completing your journey with braces or Invisalign, retainers make sure your teeth stay in their new positions—so you don’t lose the progress you’ve made.

At Creative Orthodontic Associates, our orthodontist customizes each retainer to fit your smile and lifestyle. Whether you choose removable or fixed retainers, wearing them as instructed is essential to maintaining the results of your orthodontics and keeping your smile healthy and straight for life.
