Procedures Offered

Implants

Dental Implants can permanently replace missing teeth and provide both a functional and esthetic solution

“All-on-X” Implants

Dental Implants replacing the entire dentition of either the top/bottom jaw or the entire mouth to support either a permanent or removable set of teeth

Sinus Lifts

Adding bone in the back area of the top jaw to support a dental implant when the sinus is in the way

Ridge Augmentation

Building bone back in areas of deficient bone to support a dental implant

Peri-implantitis Treatment

Correcting bone loss around an implant

Extractions

Removing teeth that have disease and are no longer healthy and beneficial

Esthetic Crown Lengthening

“Gummy smile” correction which exposes the white part of your teeth that may be abnormally hidden below the gum line

Crown Lengthening

Exposing tooth structure by reducing bone and gums to enable dentist to properly cement a crown

Soft Tissue Graft

Gum grafts can serve two purposes. Sometimes used to cover exposed roots which can cause sensitivity, cavities and esthetic problems, or sometimes used to increase a zone of resilient tissue to stop the progression of recession and prevent bacteria from affecting the bone

Treatment of gum disease to make teeth healthier and allow for better oral hygiene

Gingivectomy

Removing excess gum tissue to allow for normal appearance and function

Biopsy

Removing areas of concern and submitting to pathology lab for inspection

Frenectomy

Removing abnormal attachment of lip to gums or improving ‘tongue-tie’

Tooth Exposure for orthodonist support

Removing bone and/or gum tissue to expose teeth that have not erupted properly, and normally done to support orthodontic movement

Oral Sedation

For those patients that have ‘white coat syndrome’ medicine can be prescribed safely to allow for a more pleasant experience

Deep Cleaning

Specialized cleaning to remove bacteria and tartar buildup below your gum tissue to improve your oral health

Treating Gum Disease
