Invisalign

Invisalign offers a modern, discreet way to straighten your teeth without the use of traditional braces. Invisalign aligners are custom-made clear plastic trays that gently move your teeth into alignment over time. The trays are virtually invisible, removable, and comfortable, making Invisalign an ideal choice for adults and responsible teens who want to straighten their teeth without the appearance of metal braces. Key Benefits of Invisalign:

  •  Clear, nearly invisible aligners
  •  Removable for eating and cleaning
  •  No dietary restrictions
  •  Comfortable and convenient
  •  Effective for mild to moderate orthodontic cases

ClearCorrect

ClearCorrect is another clear aligner system designed to straighten your teeth discreetly and comfortably. Like Invisalign, ClearCorrect uses custom-made clear trays that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. These aligners are removable, making it easy to maintain your oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods without restriction. ClearCorrect is a great option for patients looking for a clear alternative to metal braces. Key Benefits of ClearCorrect:

  •  Custom-made clear aligners
  •  Removable for meals and brushing
  •  Invisible, discreet orthodontic treatment
  •  Comfortable with no metal brackets or wires
  •  A great solution for teens and adults

Braces

Traditional Braces have been a trusted orthodontic treatment for decades and remain a reliable choice for straightening teeth and correcting bite issues. Modern braces are more comfortable and discreet than ever before, with options for clear or tooth-colored brackets. Braces work by applying constant, gentle pressure on the teeth to move them into the correct position over time. They are an effective solution for patients with moderate to severe alignment or bite problems, and are suitable for children, teens, and adults. Key Benefits of Braces:

  •  Effective for all types of alignment and bite issues
  •  Options for clear or tooth-colored brackets
  •  Suitable for patients of all ages
  •  Reliable, long-lasting results
  •  Great for complex orthodontic cases
