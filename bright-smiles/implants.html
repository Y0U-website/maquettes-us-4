What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth root with a biocompatible metal post that integrates with the bone in your jaw. Once healed, it supports a new artificial tooth such as a dental bridge, crown, or denture.

This two-stage procedure begins with the dental implant placement, followed by the attachment of a restoration that looks and feels like a natural tooth.

Unlike removable prosthetics, implants are a long-term solution that functions like your natural teeth, providing strength and stability without compromising surrounding teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the implant procedure painful?

Most patients experience mild discomfort. The surgical procedure is typically done under local anesthesia, and recovery is manageable.

How long do implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last a lifetime.

Do I need a specialist?

In some cases, yes. Complex procedures may involve a dental provider such as a dental specialist or oral surgeon.

Can implants replace a full arch?

Yes, with options like full mouth dental implants or all-on-4 dental implants, we can restore both upper and lower arches with strong, natural-looking teeth.

How do dental implants compare to bridges or dentures?

Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering adjacent teeth, and unlike dentures, they are fixed in place and won’t shift. Implants help maintain bone health and provide a more natural look and feel. Bright Smiles offers this solution at our Cumming, Lawrenceville, and Dawsonville offices.

How soon after losing a tooth can I get an implant?

In some cases, implants can be placed the same day as an extraction. In other cases, healing or bone grafting is recommended first. Your doctor will confirm timing after reviewing your exam and imaging.

Will people know I have implants?

No—implants are designed to blend with surrounding teeth in color, shape, and size for a natural appearance.

What technology does Bright Smiles use for implant placement?

We use digital planning with 3D imaging and a robot to guide our doctors achieve precise, minimally invasive placement designed for comfort and long‑term success.

Do you offer robot-assisted dental implant surgery?

Yes. Bright Smiles uses the Yomi robotic guidance system (robot-assisted dental implant technology). It provides real-time guidance to help your dentist follow the digital plan for implant position, angulation, and depth. This precision can allow for smaller incisions and shorter chair time in appropriate cases. Not all cases are candidates; we’ll confirm at your consultation.

Does the robot place the implant?

No—the dentist is in control at all times. The robotic system does not place the implant on its own; it guides handpiece trajectory and depth while your dentist performs the surgery.

Can smokers get dental implants?

Smoking can slow healing and elevate risk, but many smokers still receive implants successfully. We’ll discuss strategies to improve outcomes during your consultation.

How do I care for dental implants long term?

Brush twice daily, clean between teeth daily, and maintain professional cleanings and exams. We monitor implant health at routine visits in Cumming, Lawrenceville, or Dawsonville.

What happens if I don’t have enough bone for implants?

Bone grafting may be recommended to create a stable foundation. This is a common, well‑tolerated procedure.

Are dental implants safe for older adults?

Yes. General health matters more than age. Many older adults are excellent candidates.

How successful are dental implants?

When placed and cared for properly, implants have a high long‑term success rate. Your individual risk profile will be reviewed during your visit.

How do I start the dental implant process?

It begins with a consultation to review your health history, goals, and imaging. We’ll present a personalized plan and timeline tailored to your needs.
