What are dental implants and how are they placed?

Dental implants are a popular and effective way to replace missing teeth. They are artificial tooth roots that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support a replacement tooth, bridge or denture. Dental implants are made of biocompatible materials that fuse with the bone, providing a strong and stable foundation for the replacement tooth.

The dental implant procedure typically involves several steps. First, the implant is surgically placed into the jawbone by a dental professional like Dr. Bernardini. After the implant is placed, a healing process known as osseointegration takes place, during which the implant fuses with the jawbone over a period of several months.

Once the implant has fully fused with the bone, a connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant. The abutment serves as a base for the replacement tooth or bridge to be attached. Finally, a custom-made replacement tooth or bridge is attached to the abutment, completing the dental implant process.

What are the benefits of dental implants over other tooth replacements?

Dental implants in Staten Island offer several advantages over other tooth replacement options. They are designed to look and feel like natural teeth, provide a strong and stable foundation, and can last a lifetime with proper care. Unlike dentures, they do not slip or shift in the mouth and do not require messy adhesives. Additionally, dental implants can help preserve the jawbone and prevent bone loss, which can occur with other tooth replacement options. They also allow for normal eating, speaking, and oral hygiene routines without the need for special care.
