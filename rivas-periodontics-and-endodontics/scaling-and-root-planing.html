Enhancing Your Overall Dental Health. Scaling and root planing is a critical procedure offered by Rivas Perio for patients who require effective treatment for gum disease.

Why Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing, also known as deep cleaning, is a non-surgical dental procedure performed to address gum diseases such as gingivitis and periodontitis.

This meticulous treatment involves the removal of plaque, tartar, and bacteria from the teeth and below the gumline, promoting healthier gums and overall oral well-being.

Our Treatment Process

Before

Before recommending scaling and root planing, Dr. Rivas will conduct a thorough examination of your gums and supporting structures. This evaluation helps determine the extent of gum disease and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

During

The first step involves scaling, where specialized dental instruments are used to remove plaque and tartar from both the visible tooth surfaces and the hidden areas below the gumline. This process may involve the use of manual tools or ultrasonic devices.

After

After scaling and root planing, Rivas Periodontics will provide you with detailed instructions for at-home oral care and schedule regular follow-up visits. This ongoing care is crucial for monitoring your gum health, preventing disease recurrence, and maintaining optimal oral hygiene.

Benefits of Scaling and Root Planing with Rivas Perio

Gum Disease Prevention

Gum diseases, if left untreated, can lead to severe oral health issues. Scaling and root planning effectively eliminate plaque and tartar build-up, thereby preventing the progression of gum diseases and reducing the risk of tooth loss.

Halting Gum Disease Progression

Improved Oral Hygiene

Through scaling and root planing, a clean foundation is established, enabling individuals to maintain better oral hygiene. The removal of plaque and tartar build-up facilitates more effective brushing and flossing, supporting long-term oral health.

Reduced Gum Inflammation

Fresher Breath

Persistent bad breath is often a result of gum diseases. By eliminating the bacteria responsible for these conditions, scaling and root planing can significantly improve breath freshness.

Prevention of Tooth Loss

Timely intervention through scaling and root planing helps prevent tooth loss. By promoting the health of supporting structures such as gums and jawbone, this procedure ensures the longevity of teeth.
