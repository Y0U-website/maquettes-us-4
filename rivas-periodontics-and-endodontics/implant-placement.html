Implant Placement

  • Dental implants can also be used to replace multiple missing teeth Implant-supported bridges or implant-supported dentures can be customized to replace several teeth in a row or even an entire arch of teeth. Implants serve as anchors for the prosthetic teeth, providing stability and preventing bone loss in the jaw.

Our Treatment Process

Before

If you are deemed a good candidate for dental implants, a comprehensive treatment plan will be developed. This involves taking 3D scans to evaluate the structure and density of your jawbone. The treatment plan will include the number of implants needed, the type of restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) to be placed on the implants, and any additional procedures required, such as bone grafting or tooth extractions.

During

The next step is the surgical placement of the dental implant. The dental implant, which is a small titanium post, is surgically inserted into the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. Over time, the implant fuses with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, providing a stable foundation for the replacement tooth. Dr. Rivas and our team will discuss local anesthesia or other sedation options during your initial visit.

Post-Op

After implant placement, a healing period of several months is necessary to allow the implant to integrate with the jawbone. During this time, a temporary restoration may be placed to maintain functionality and aesthetics. The length of the healing period may vary depending on factors such as the quality of your jawbone and your overall healing ability.

Once the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, a minor surgical procedure is performed to place an abutment. The abutment acts as a connector between the implant and the final restoration. It protrudes through the gum line and provides access for the placement of the artificial tooth or teeth.

After the gums have healed and the abutment is in place, the final step is the placement of the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture on top of the implant or implants. The restoration is designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, providing a seamless and natural-looking result.

Recovery

Regular follow-up appointments will be scheduled to monitor the healing process, ensure the stability and functionality of the implant, and address any concerns or adjustments needed. It is important to maintain good oral hygiene practices and attend routine dental check-ups to ensure the long-term success and longevity of the dental implant.
