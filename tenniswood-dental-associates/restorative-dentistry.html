Restorative Dentistry

Renewing Health, Function, & Structural Harmony

At Tenniswood Dental Associates, we provide a gentle, nonjudgmental approach and advanced restorative therapies to treat everything from minor structural damage to complex tooth replacement, beautifully reviving the natural form and function of your smile.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

Seamless Restorations to Reclaim Your Smile

When you suffer from a damaged or lost tooth, your overall health, bite alignment, and structural stability suffer too. At our Okeechobee, FL dental practice , we engineer premium, durable dental crowns and bridges to replenish lost tooth structure. For an entirely natural appearance, we utilize advanced ceramic and porcelain materials that seamlessly blend with your surrounding teeth.

Premium Dental Crowns

Designed to completely cover or "cap" an individual tooth, a custom crown significantly improves its health, strength, and alignment. Our dentists utilize crowns to restore large areas of decay too severe for a standard filling, rebuild fractured teeth, protect weak tooth structure, restore dental implants, or preserve a tooth following root canal therapy.

Secure Dental Bridges

If you are missing one or more teeth, a custom dental bridge spans the empty space to prevent your remaining teeth from shifting out of place. This essential restoration prevents a misaligned bite, reducing your long-term risk for gum disease and TMJ disorders. The bridge is permanently anchored in place by custom crowns fitted over the healthy teeth adjacent to the gap.

The Restoration Process & Care

Crafting your custom restoration requires two brief visits. During your first appointment, the target teeth are precisely modified, and highly accurate impressions are captured while a temporary appliance is placed for your comfort. Once your permanent crown or bridge is artfully fabricated, you return to our practice to have it securely bonded and meticulously evaluated during a brief dental checkup to ensure a flawless fit.

Root Canal Therapy

The words “root canal” often cause unneeded anxiety, but modern endodontic treatment is a highly conservative, comfortable, and transformative procedure designed to save an infected tooth from extraction. Protected by enamel and dentin, the inner chamber of a healthy tooth contains pulp and root canals that deliver vital nutrients. However, when deep decay or structural cracks breach these outer defenses, bacteria enter the chamber, causing infection, thermal sensitivity, and acute pain.

The Endodontic Procedure

During your procedure, our highly qualified dentists access the inner chamber of the affected tooth to thoroughly clear out bacteria and diseased pulp tissue. The hollow space is then meticulously disinfected, sterilized, and filled with gutta-percha—an inert, biocompatible material that prevents re-infection.

Once the inner chamber is permanently sealed, our expert team prepares the tooth and places a custom porcelain dental crown over it. This completely restores the tooth&#x27;s strength, appearance, and function, allowing you to retain your natural smile for years to come. Any minor post-operative discomfort is easily managed with standard over-the-counter anti-inflammatories.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

While our primary goal is to maintain your natural smile for life, extracting a tooth is occasionally the healthiest choice for your entire mouth. This is most frequently true for wisdom teeth (third molars), which are the last teeth to erupt into the gumline—typically between the ages of 17 and 25. Because modern jawlines rarely have adequate space to accommodate these unnecessary evolutionary additions, they frequently cause severe crowding, misalignment, or painful impaction beneath the tissue.

Meticulous Diagnostics & Patient Comfort

Using high-definition digital x-rays during your regular twice a year checkups, we track the early development of third molars. If an extraction becomes necessary to protect your bite, our team handles the oral surgery process smoothly and gently.

Advanced Cracked Teeth Reconstruction

Urgent Structural Restoration to Prevent Infection

Teeth can chip, crack, or fracture due to physical impact, chewing hard objects, or untreated bruxism (nocturnal grinding). Furthermore, misaligned or missing teeth create uneven bite pressure, leaving your remaining teeth highly vulnerable to structural failure. Leaving a fracture untreated allows bacteria to infiltrate the inner chamber, which is why we assess structural cracks immediately during your dental checkup to prevent infection and decay.

Cosmetic Composite Bonding

For minor surface fractures, chips, and superficial flaws, we utilize cosmetic composite bonding to instantly restore your smile. Our dentists meticulously apply a pliable, tooth-colored resin directly to the tooth structure, sculpting it to match your natural anatomy before curing it with a specialized light for a seamless finish.

Custom Inlays & Onlays

When a crack or fracture damages a significant portion of a tooth&#x27;s chewing surface but does not require a full-coverage crown, we utilize custom porcelain inlays and onlays. These conservative, laboratory-fabricated restorations fit precisely within or over the remaining tooth structure, rebuilding its structural integrity while preserving as much natural enamel as possible.

Full-Coverage Crowns & Root Canals

Deep structural cracks that extend down toward the nerve chamber require advanced clinical intervention to prevent tooth loss. At our Okeechobee, FL dental practice, we protect these vulnerable teeth by utilizing full-coverage ceramic crowns to bind the fractured pieces together, frequently pairing them with root canal therapy to safeguard internal nerve tissue from bacterial damage.

Full and Partial Dentures

Custom Restorations to Reclaim Confidence

Extensive tooth loss can dramatically compromise a patient&#x27;s quality of life, making it difficult to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Custom-tailored full and partial dentures offer a natural-looking, stable solution to replace missing teeth, instantly restoring structural harmony and helping you look and feel younger.

Custom Full & Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are ideal for patients who have multiple missing teeth scattered along a single arch, fitting seamlessly alongside your remaining teeth and held securely by discreet attachments. Full dentures replace an entire row of teeth on the upper or lower jaw, utilizing a customized, gum-colored base fitted with premium replacement teeth that rely on natural suction or specialized secure fitment.

The Implant-Retained Advantage

For patients seeking unsurpassed stability, our Okeechobee, FL dental practice offers state-of-the-art implant-retained prosthetics. By anchoring your denture to biocompatible dental implants, you prevent jawbone degeneration and sunken facial contours. This elite option eliminates the need for messy adhesives, ensuring your teeth never slip or shift while eating, speaking, or laughing.

Long-Term Maintenance & Repair

Whether you choose a traditional prosthetic or implant stabilization, routine care is vital to protect your investment. During your regular dental checkup, our team provides the meticulous maintenance, professional denture relines, and expert denture repairs required to accommodate changing jaw contours and keep your smile functioning perfectly.

Dental Implants

The Premier Solution for Permanent Tooth Replacement

The loss of even a single tooth can compromise your bite, bone structure, and self-confidence. At Tenniswood Dental Associates , we recommend dental implants as the premier solution for tooth replacement. Unlike traditional options that only replace the visible crown, implants replace the entire tooth from root to crown, delivering unmatched stability, natural beauty, and long-lasting durability.

The In-Office Implant Process

While many practices refer patients to outside surgical specialists, our highly qualified team performs the entire implant process in-office for a seamless, comfortable experience. The biocompatible titanium implant post is precisely positioned within the jawbone to serve as a new tooth root. Over a brief healing period, the implant naturally fuses with your bone tissue to form a permanent, secure foundation before we anchor your custom-made, lifelike dental prosthetic.

Tailored Restorations

Your final restoration is engineered precisely to your specific anatomical needs and personal preferences. For a single missing tooth, a custom porcelain crown is meticulously crafted to attach to the implant, seamlessly matching your surrounding teeth. To replace multiple missing teeth or an entire arch, we design specialized bridges or dentures that snap securely onto a strategic number of implants for a fixed, lifelike fit.

Structural Benefits & Longevity

By replacing the tooth root, implants stimulate the jawbone to prevent bone loss and maintain your natural facial contours while leaving neighboring healthy teeth completely untouched. They require no frustrating changes to your diet or at-home dental hygiene . With excellent home care and regular twice a year checkups at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice , your dental implants can easily last a lifetime.
