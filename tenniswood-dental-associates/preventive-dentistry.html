Check Ups & Cleanings

Regular twice a year checkups at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice allow our team to actively protect your oral health and intercept concerns in their earliest stages. Every preventive appointment is built around three core pillars of clinical care:

Advanced Imaging, Screenings & Consultations

During your visit, we use high-definition digital x-rays to map your dental anatomy while our dentists screen for decay, gum disease, TMJ dysfunction, bruxism, and oral cancer. We then discuss our clinical findings with you to design a tailored treatment plan that keeps your smile healthy, whole, and vibrant for life.

Meticulous Professional Cleanings

A vital component of your preventive dental care is a rigorous, professional teeth cleaning. Our skilled hygienists expertly remove plaque and tartar from hard-to-reach areas to prevent periodontal (gum) disease. For patients requiring advanced periodontal therapy, we customize your care with a more frequent cleaning schedule.

Focused Patient Education

An exceptional smile relies on the care it receives between visits. We place a high priority on personalized patient education during your appointment. From instilling excellent at-home dental hygiene habits in children to recommending premium home-care products and refined techniques for adults, we provide the tools you need to maintain a brilliant smile for life.

Children’s Dentistry

At Tenniswood Dental Associates , we deliver exceptional, compassionate dental care tailored specifically for our youngest patients. Our team creates a refined, welcoming environment where children feel completely at ease, ensuring their early experiences at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice are positive, comfortable, and reassuring.

Early Development & Checkups

In alignment with clinical guidelines, we recommend scheduling your child’s first visit by age one to track early oral health. By age three, children should begin regular, twice a year checkups and professional teeth cleanings to monitor growing smiles for early signs of tooth decay and ensure primary teeth remain healthy placeholders for permanent ones.

Advanced Preventative Treatments

To safeguard developing smiles against cavities, we offer specialized preventative therapies during routine visits. This includes meticulously applying dental sealants to create a protective barrier over the deep grooves of molars, alongside professional fluoride treatments to strengthen tooth enamel and defend against decay.

Collaborative Hygiene Education

An exceptional smile relies on the habits formed at home. Our skilled hygienists provide personalized at-home dental hygiene education, demonstrating proper brushing and flossing techniques for both you and your child to ensure you have the guidance and home-care insights needed to keep their smiles vibrant between visits.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants provide an invisible, highly effective shield against tooth decay. This advanced preventative therapy involves painting a durable, tooth-colored protective material directly onto the chewing surfaces of the molars and premolars, significantly reducing the risk of cavities.

Precision Cavity Prevention

By "sealing" the deep grooves and fissures of the back teeth, this treatment prevents food particles and harmful bacteria from accumulating in hard-to-reach areas. This protective barrier seals out decay in the areas most vulnerable to plaque buildup.

Long-Term Protection for All Ages

Meticulous Maintenance

During your regular twice a year checkups, our dentists will meticulously monitor the condition of your sealants. If minor wear or chipping occurs over time, our team can easily repair or reapply the material during your routine dental checkup to ensure your teeth remain seamlessly protected.

Mouthguards and Nightguards

Unconscious teeth grinding and sudden athletic impacts can leave your smile vulnerable to severe damage. At Tenniswood Dental Associates , we design premium, custom-fit mouthguards and nightguards tailored precisely to your unique anatomy, providing a durable barrier to preserve your smile.

Precision Athletic Protection

Engineered for athletes of all ages and sports, our custom mouthguards absorb impact to safeguard your teeth, jaw, and soft tissues. These comfortable appliances are far more effective at preventing fractures and tooth loss than traditional over-the-counter options.

Bruxism & TMJ Relief

Chronic grinding causes worn enamel, headaches, and TMJ discomfort. Often diagnosed during your routine dental checkup , our custom nightguards alleviate jaw pressure and protect your teeth from nighttime wear, ensuring you wake up without tension.

Appliance Care & Longevity

To maintain pristine at-home dental hygiene , brush your appliance before each use and store it in a ventilated case. Bring your appliance to your regular twice a year checkups at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice so our team can evaluate its fit and wear during your routine examination.

Oral Cancer Screening

Vital Protection Through Early Detection

At Tenniswood Dental Associates , your health and longevity are our highest priorities. Because oral cancer affects tens of thousands of Americans each year, the key to successful treatment and survival relies entirely on early detection, which is why we integrate an advanced screening into your routine care.

Proactive Detection & Risk Assessment

Meticulous Clinical Evaluation

Performed during your routine dental checkup at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice , the evaluation requires no special preparation. Your dentist will visually examine the inside of your mouth for any irregular patches, lesions, or sores, while gently feeling the oral tissues to check for underlying lumps or structural abnormalities.

Periodontal Therapy/Treatment

Advanced Care to Protect Your Support System

Your gums are the vital structural support system that holds your teeth in place and directly influences your overall wellness. Because periodontal (gum) disease affects nearly 80 percent of adults in the United States, Tenniswood Dental Associates provides advanced periodontal therapy to intercept infection, protect your oral health, and arrest the progression of the disease.

The Systemic Health Connection

Gum disease begins as a bacterial infection fueled by plaque and tartar buildup, which destroys gum tissue and underlying bone structure if left unmanaged. Because advanced periodontitis is a leading cause of tooth extraction, maintaining healthy gums is critical. Furthermore, clinical research directly links oral inflammation and bacteria to serious systemic conditions, including heart disease, hypertension, and diabetes.

Proactive Screening & Charting

Early warning signs like redness, bleeding, or receding gums can easily be overlooked. To catch issues early, our skilled hygienists meticulously evaluate and chart your gum health during your regular twice a year checkups at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice . This thorough evaluation ensures any signs of infection are identified before they can compromise your smile.

Targeted Therapeutic Solutions

If an infection is detected during your routine dental checkup , we step in with specialized treatments to restore your health. We utilize scaling and root planing to completely remove harmful plaque and tartar from deep beneath the gumline while smoothing the tooth roots to encourage healthy tissue reattachment. When necessary, we also apply localized antibiotic therapy directly into the gum pockets to eliminate remaining bacteria and promote deep healing.

TMJ Treatment Appliances

Persistent jaw pain, earaches, and chronic headaches are often the quiet symptoms of TMJ (temporomandibular joint) disorder. This uncomfortable condition typically stems from an improper bite, misaligned jaw joints, or a past structural injury. At Tenniswood Dental Associates , we look beyond the surface of your teeth to evaluate the entire harmony of your smile, offering specialized TMJ therapy to relieve discomfort.

Comprehensive Joint Evaluation

Diagnosing joint dysfunction begins with a meticulous assessment during your routine dental care. By evaluating your jaw mobility and tracking joint wear during your regular twice a year checkups at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice , our dentists can intercept joint strain before it leads to permanent damage or chronic pain.

Personalized TMJ Appliances

Because every smile is structurally unique, TMJ care is tailored precisely to the root cause of your joint strain to ensure your teeth, muscles, and joints function seamlessly together. We design premium, custom-fabricated orthotic splints and nightguards that comfortably reposition the jaw to immediately alleviate joint pressure and prevent subconscious grinding.

Advanced Bite Optimization

When structural misalignment is the primary cause of your joint dysfunction, we utilize targeted restorative solutions to permanently balance your bite. Following a thorough dental checkup , your personalized treatment plan may feature conservative enamel reshaping, custom dental crowns, or orthodontic alignment to correct how your teeth meet and eliminate muscle tension.
