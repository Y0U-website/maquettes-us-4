Teeth Whitening

Professional System to Revitalize a Dulled Smile

Professional teeth whitening is one of the fastest, most effective ways to reverse stains and revitalize a dulled smile. Over time, extrinsic stains from dark foods, coffee, tea, and tobacco alter your enamel, while intrinsic stains settle deeper into the dentin layer due to past trauma or aging. To combat both types of discoloration safely, we utilize the premium Philips ZOOM! Whitening system.

In-Office Zoom Whitening

Perfect for immediate results, this powerful, light-activated treatment breaks apart deep stains beneath the enamel in just one hour. Performed safely at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice, this clinical option allows you to leave your appointment with a smile up to eight shades whiter, making it ideal for upcoming events or rapid transformation.

Professional Take-Home Kits

For professional results on your own schedule, we engineer custom-fitted delivery trays paired with a professional-strength whitening formula. Worn comfortably at home for a designated period each day, this option delivers remarkable, uniform brightness while minimizing tooth sensitivity through precision-fit engineering.

Long-Term Brightness Maintenance

To protect your investment and maintain a brilliant smile, minor touch-ups can be integrated into your routine care. During your regular twice a year checkups, our team can evaluate your shade, provide professional care advice, and supply whitening refills to ensure your smile remains luminous between your routine dental checkup visits.

Tooth Colored Fillings

Amalgam silver fillings have been a dental standard for decades, but modern dentistry allows for a much more conservative and beautiful alternative. Our practice exclusively utilizes biocompatible, tooth-colored fillings made from a high-strength composite resin blend of medical-grade plastics and glass to safely restore your teeth and maintain a seamless look.

Invisible, Metal-Free Repair

Unlike traditional metal restorations that leave dark, noticeable shadows in your smile, composite fillings are matched to the exact shade of your natural enamel. Once placed and polished by our dentists, the material becomes completely indistinguishable from the rest of your tooth, preserving its natural beauty.

Conservative Tooth Structure Preservation

Unlike metal fillings that require cutting away large wedges of healthy tooth structure to mechanically stay in place, composite resin chemically bonds directly to your enamel. During your procedure at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice, our dentists only remove the precise area of active decay, keeping the maximum amount of your natural tooth intact and strong.

Long-Term Fracture Prevention

Metal fillings expand and contract with temperature changes from food and drinks, which frequently causes teeth to crack, shift, or leak over time. Composite resin eliminates this risk entirely; it bonds seamlessly to the tooth structure to evenly distribute chewing pressure, ensuring your tooth remains sealed and protected during your routine dental checkup evaluations.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers offer an exceptional way to completely conceal moderate to severe aesthetic imperfections on the front teeth. These custom-crafted, ultra-thin shells of premium dental ceramic are permanently bonded to the surface of your teeth, instantly correcting a variety of aesthetic concerns.

Comprehensive Aesthetic Correction

Veneers provide a flawless, uniform look by masking structural and surface flaws. Our dentists utilize them to instantly correct chips, surface cracks, and wear, as well as persistent intrinsic discoloration that resists traditional whitening. They are also an ideal solution for closing gaps, concealing slight misalignments, or reshaping misshapen and unevenly spaced teeth.

Minimal Enamel Preparation

Achieving your ideal smile requires just two comfortable visits to our Okeechobee, FL dental practice . During your initial appointment, our dentists precisely buff a microscopic layer of enamel—often less than a millimeter—to ensure your veneers sit flush and look entirely natural, rather than bulky. Highly accurate impressions are then captured to guide the fabrication process.

Masterful Fabrication & Placement

While your permanent restorations are artfully sculpted by a master laboratory ceramist, temporary veneers are placed to protect your teeth. Once your bespoke porcelain veneers are ready, you return to our practice for a final dental checkup and placement. Our team meticulously evaluates the fit, contours, and shade before securely bonding them into place for a beautiful transformation.

All-Ceramic Restorations

When a tooth requires functional repair, you should never have to compromise the appearance of your smile. At Tenniswood Dental Associates, our dental experts utilize top-quality, metal-free, all-ceramic materials that reflect light identically to natural enamel, completely eliminating dark lines or metallic shadows at the gumline.

Advanced All-Ceramic Crowns

Designed to fully cap a severely decayed, worn, or fractured tooth, an all-ceramic crown restores its original shape, size, and strength. Engineered from high-strength dental porcelain or zirconia, these restorations are built to withstand heavy biting pressure while perfectly matching the translucency and color of your surrounding teeth.

Seamless Ceramic Bridges

An all-ceramic bridge is a sturdy, fixed prosthetic used to replace one or more missing teeth in a row. The replacement tooth is artfully suspended and permanently anchored by custom ceramic crowns placed on the healthy teeth on either side of the gap, restoring your natural chewing stability and bite alignment.

Conservative Inlays & Onlays

Crafted for teeth that require more structural support than a standard filling but do not mandate a full-coverage crown, all-ceramic inlays and onlays offer an ideal conservative alternative. Inlays fit precisely within the inner chewing grooves of the tooth, while onlays drape over one or more of the outer cusps, preserving the maximum amount of your natural tooth structure during your dental checkup restorations at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice.

Invisalign

Enjoy all the benefits of a perfectly straight smile without the cosmetic distraction or discomfort of traditional metal brackets and wires. Invisalign utilizes a series of custom-fabricated, virtually invisible aligners made of smooth, medical-grade plastic to gradually guide your teeth into flawless alignment.

Comprehensive Bite & Alignment Correction

This advanced orthodontic system comfortably corrects a wide range of structural issues. Our dentists utilize Invisalign to resolve overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites, as well as crooked, rotated, overlapping teeth, and uneven gaps or spacing issues.

The Lifestyle Advantage

Because Invisalign aligners are completely removable, they offer unparalleled freedom compared to traditional braces. You can take them out to enjoy your favorite foods without dietary restrictions, and effortlessly maintain your normal at-home dental hygiene routine by brushing and flossing without navigating wires or brackets.

Digital Treatment Tracking

Following a routine dental checkup at our Okeechobee, FL dental practice to ensure your teeth and gums are healthy, our team utilizes high-definition digital imaging to map out your care. This advanced technology creates a 3D visualization of your treatment path, allowing you to view your projected new smile before your first set of aligners is even fabricated.

Commitment to Results

To ensure optimal tooth movement, aligners must be worn for 20 to 22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. You will advance to a new set of custom trays approximately every two weeks, with periodic progress checkups at our office to monitor your alignment and ensure your transformation stays perfectly on track.
