Individual implants for missing teeth

When patients present with missing teeth or the need for tooth replacement, Dr. Kaufman offers personalized treatment options to restore function and aesthetics. For patients who are candidates, single-tooth implants are the preferred solution. Each implant screw is carefully placed within the jawbone to replace individual missing teeth. Dr. Kaufman ensures that every implant is positioned with precision for optimal function and a natural, cosmetic appearance.

Following the integration period, Dr. Marianna Kaufman completes the restoration by placing a custom abutment and crown on each implant, designed to blend seamlessly with natural teeth. For teeth missing in visible areas, a temporary prosthesis is provided to maintain aesthetics during healing. Patients appreciate this implant therapy for its durability, functionality, and lifelike results, making it an ideal solution for tooth replacement.

Dental implants, placed by the team at Kaufman Dental Associates, may be the solution to restoring your chewing function, your smile......your confidence.

Dental implants look and feel most like natural teeth.

Implants allow the palate to be fully exposed so that food tastes its best.

Implants restore lost natural teeth with equal or greater strength allowing extreme confidence when smiling and eating.

Dental implants stimulate bone and prevent its deterioration when teeth are lost. They will make you look younger, longer.

When cared for properly, Implants will usually last a lifetime.

Dental implants are most commonly made of medical grade titanium or non-metal zirconium which is fashioned into various diameter screws.

They are placed directly into the bones of the jaw and become fused to the bone(s) over the course of a couple to several months. Upon integration (fusion), a dentist may use the implant to support a variety of dental restorations.

Dental implants can not be infected with tooth decay and will never require a root canal. From restoring a single lost tooth to a full mouth of teeth, our practice has utilized dental implants to provide life changing results to hundreds of patients over the years.
