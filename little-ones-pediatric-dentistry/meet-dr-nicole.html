Meet Dr. Nicole

"If you are looking for an amazing pediatric dentist check out Little Ones Pediatric Dentistry!!! Dr. Nicole has an incredible gift with her little patients that is hard to describe."

Dr. Nicole

“I want children to have such an amazing visit that they remember all the little details – choosing a movie to watch, selecting a flavor of toothpaste, picking out their new toothbrush, getting fun prizes, experiencing team members who are happy to see them, and having so much fun they can’t wait to come back.”

Raised to serve little ones

Growing up, Dr. Nicole always found herself helping children by babysitting, tutoring, teaching swim lessons, and being involved in community school events. When it came time to choose a career path, she could not imagine working with anyone but children. As the mother of two sweet kiddos, she aims to treat patients as if they&#x27;re her own.

specialized training

board certified pediatric dentist

As a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist, Dr. Nicole is a Diplomate with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD), Ohio Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (OAPD), Ohio Dental Association (ODA), and the American Dental Association.

passion for helping special children

Dr. Nicole has a passion for serving all children, including those with special needs. Her goal is to make sure every child feels special, gets the personalized attention and care they need, and has an amazing dental experience. She loves to sing songs, tell silly stories, and tickle the “sugar bugs” away!

home life

Beyond the office, she&#x27;s married to the love of her life, enjoys being a mom to her two littles, and loves staying involved in the community and supporting local organizations. She enjoys dancing, listening to Latin music, exploring new restaurants, traveling, and spending time with family and friends.
