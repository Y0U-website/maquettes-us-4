All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Loose, unstable, or uncomfortable traditional dentures
  • Bone loss in the jaw that makes individual implants difficult
  • Challenges with chewing or speaking due to missing teeth
  • A desire for a permanent, non-removable solution and a confident smile

What to Expect During the Process

Comprehensive Consultation: Your journey begins with a detailed evaluation, including diagnostics such as digital imaging and 3D scans. This allows us to understand your oral condition and plan implant placement with precision.

Implant Placement: Four to six dental implants are positioned in areas of strong bone support. These implants act as anchors for your new set of teeth, providing stability and long-term function.

Immediate Restoration: In many cases, a temporary set of fixed teeth can be placed immediately after implant surgery. This means you won’t go without a smile while your implants heal.

Final Custom Prosthesis: Once the implants have fully integrated with your jawbone, a custom-designed final prosthesis will be attached, crafted to look and feel like natural teeth.

Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

Permanent Stability: Your new teeth stay securely in place, eliminating the slipping or irritation associated with dentures.

Natural Look and Feel: Designed to match your facial features, providing a confident and healthy appearance.

Better Chewing Function: Enjoy a wider range of foods with strong, fully supported replacement teeth.

Bone Preservation: Implants help stimulate the jawbone to reduce further bone loss over time.

Cost-Effective Full-Arch Solution: Fewer implants are needed compared to replacing each tooth individually, making it a more accessible long-term investment.
