Our Dental Practice

Our mission

A brighter dental experience

Founded in 2012, Element Dental is a purpose-driven dental practice committed to establishing excellence through innovative technology and expertise. Our goal is to provide comfortable, unique experiences that leave a lasting impression.

From the moment you step into our office, you will notice the Element Dental difference. Our friendly front desk staff will welcome you with a smile and ensure you are seen on time for your appointment. Our dentists use the latest techniques and finest materials in dentistry to deliver beautiful, long-lasting results.

Our dental office is designed to offer a relaxing atmosphere, fully equipped to address your oral health needs. We prioritize your comfort with modern amenities, including blankets, headphones, water, coffee, and smoothies.

Schedule your appointment online today and discover the exceptional care that sets Element Dental apart.

Dr. Rachel Fehling, DMD

Dr. Grant Bolle, DDS

Originally from Illinois, Dr. Grant Bolle (pronounced Bo-lee), DDS joined Element in August of 2023. Dr. Bolle graduated summa cum laude from Augustana College, where he majored in Biological Sciences and Studio Art (ceramics.) He then went on to study at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry. Dr. Bolle’s unique blend of experience focuses on both science and aesthetics, allowing him to use creativity to solve problems and provide the highest quality care to patients.

As a newcomer to the area, Dr. Bolle is excited to discover all the incredible outdoor activities and natural wonders North Idaho has to offer. Outside of work, some of his favorite hobbies include golfing, cycling, and photography.
