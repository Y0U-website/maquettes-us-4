Adam Goldberg, DDS, MDS

Dr. Adam Goldberg graduated from the College of William and Mary magna cum laude with a Bachelor of Science in biology. He completed his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine, where he founded and was the president of AADMD, a national organization dedicated to bettering the care of special needs patients. Dr. Adam is Board Certified in Periodontics and Implant Dentistry.

During this time, Dr. Goldberg completed a thesis on treatment outcomes in medically complex patients, in addition to publishing in the Journal of Clinical Advances in Periodontics . He attended Rutgers School of Dental Medicine and earned his certificate in periodontics. He received training at the Institute for Advanced Laser Dentistry and can now perform laser-guided surgery.

Dr. Adam enjoys swimming and competing in triathlons, in addition to other outdoor activities like skiing, golf, and tennis. He also enjoys traveling and has recently returned from Australia where he scuba-dived with whale sharks.

Dr. Adam and his father recently wrote: Goldberg DA, Pinn M, Goldberg AS. 17‐Year Follow‐Up of Ridge Augmentation in a Patient With Linear Scleroderma: A Novel Case Report. Clinical Advances in Periodontics . 2018 Jun;8(2):72-6.
