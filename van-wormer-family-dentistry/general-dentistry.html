General Dentistry

  • Desensitizing Treatments Do you struggle with tooth pain when you eat hot or cold foods? You could be suffering from tooth sensitivity. Be sure to mention your concerns at your next appointment with Dr. Van Wormer and our dental team can discuss your options for desensitizing treatments with you.
  • Oral Cancer Screening Detecting oral cancer early is vital to improve your chances of successful treatment. At Van Wormer Family Dentistry, we include oral cancer screening for all of our patients during their comprehensive exams once they reach the age of 20.
  • Nitrous Oxide We understand many people have a fear of going to the dentist and we want to put your mind at ease. Our office provides nitrous oxide to help our patients relax during treatment. If you’re feeling anxious about your dental appointment, just let our friendly staff know and we’ll be happy to provide this safe, relaxing sedation option for you.
  • Digital X-Rays Dental x-ray technology has come a long way and is still an essential part of ensuring your smile stays healthy. Our team uses digital x-rays to annually check your entire mouth for signs of decay, broken teeth, cracked fillings, and more.
  • Soft Tissue Laser Treatment Our soft tissue laser allows us to quickly and effectively treat cold sores and other lesions. Using a soft tissue laser gives our patients the benefits of faster recovery with minimal discomfort. If you start to feel a cold sore coming on, call us right away to schedule your treatment.
  • Fluoride Treatment Fluoride is a natural mineral that has been proven to safely build strong teeth and help prevent cavities. It supports your tooth enamel by fighting bacteria that can damage your teeth and gums. We provide fluoride to our patients during regular teeth cleanings in the form of a rinse or varnish.
  • Athletic Mouthguards Does your child play sports? Protecting their teeth should be a vital part of their athletic equipment. We create customized athletic mouthguards designed to comfortably protect your child’s smile.
  • TMJ Mouthguards Symptoms of TMJ include significant jaw pain, headaches, jaw stiffness, painful chewing, locking of the jaw, and even changes in how your upper and lower teeth fit together. If you’re diagnosed with TMJ, we can help relieve your pain using a customized TMJ mouthguard.
