It’s an honor to be part of the journey of your kids growing up. Over the years, we see their interests change – from Roblox to a college search, right up to the bittersweet moment when it’s time for them to graduate. The long-term relationship and investment in them is so gratifying to us.

One of our favorite examples of this is The Wand Anesthetic Medicine. To alleviate your child’s fear or anxiety, we use a computerized anesthetic dispensing machine called “The Wand.” Shaped like a pen, The Wand automatically adjusts for your child, and the anesthetic is delivered at a constant pressure and volume so the injection is hardly felt.

At the end of the day, it’s not about us – all our effort is poured into creating a fun and rewarding experience for your child. Having our practice in Southern California, we know what it means to provide the VIP treatment. We will roll out the red carpet for your child and get their smile ready for a photo shoot 🙂 – check out some of the services we offer below!

The best way to prepare for your child’s first dental visit is to consider anything you need from us – we’d be happy to answer any question you may have. For the little ones, feel free to bring along a favorite toy, blanket or other familiar object to help give your child a little extra peace of mind.

Your child’s first visit will include an oral examination and a toothbrush demonstration. Dr. Toni will examine their mouth, with special consideration for what’s most comfortable for you and your child.

We invite parents to stay with their children during the initial examination, as well as any future visits. We look at this as a team approach in overcoming apprehension, gaining your child’s confidence, and providing the safest environment.
