IMPLANT RESTORATION

Are you tired of having missing teeth? Our cosmetic dentistry clinic can help you. We restore dental implants with individual crowns, multi-unit bridges, and implant-retained dentures.

We work closely with the best specialists in the area to provide a state-of-the-art solution for the replacement of missing teeth.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns (caps) are a great solution for protecting and saving your teeth after a root canal or if you have a very large restoration that is breaking down. Bridges are a good option for replacing a missing tooth and protecting the adjacent teeth.

Both of these are great solutions for restoring broken or fragile teeth. We use only the best American labs to ensure beautiful, functional, and durable restorations.

Affordable Dentures

We work with an acclaimed local prosthetics lab to provide custom and lifelike removable full and partial dentures.

We can design your appliance to optimize the look that you want as well as maximize its function. Give us a call to learn more about the affordable dentures options we provide in our practice.
