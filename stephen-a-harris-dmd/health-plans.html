Payment Policy

We participate in most of the major dental insurance plans in the region. We offer payment plans and discounts to assist making quality care affordable to our patients. We accept MasterCard, Visa, American Express, Discover, and CareCredit, as well as cash and personal checks

We will estimate treatment costs before treatment is rendered. Listed above are the dental plans that we currently participate with. If your dental insurance plan is not listed, we will file a claim to your insurance company on your behalf. If you have dental insurance, please be aware that almost all plans have deductibles and co-payments, for which you will be responsible at the time of service. We make every effort to accurately estimate what you will owe as your portion of the bill

If we participate with your plan, we will bill your insurance company for the balance. Payment is due at the time dental services are rendered if you do not have dental insurance, unless prior arrangements have been made. We will readily address any concerns that you may have about your insurance benefits, coverage or payments.
