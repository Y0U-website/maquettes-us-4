The practice

Dr. Jenny Alas and her dental team are experienced and ready to give you the dental care you need. We will take the time to listen to your concerns and make sure you are comfortable during your visit. You can trust that our team will work hard to give you the beautiful, healthy smile you deserve.

We offer a variety of services, including general dentistry, preventative dentistry, teeth whitening, and root canal therapy. Here at Kidz and Family Smiles you can rest assured you’ll receive a tailored service fit for your needs.

Dr. Alas was born and raised in Honduras, she moved to the U.S.A in 1997 and has been a long resident of the state Colorado. She obtained her bachelor’s degree in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from the Metropolitan University of Denver, and her D.M.D (Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry) from the University Of Kentucky College Of Dentistry in 2017.

Dr. Alas, her husband and three children love spending their free time cooking, camping, and fishing, as well as spending time with family and friends; her family has been a part of the lower Arkansas Valley community for the past two years. Dr. Alas is excited about this new chapter and cannot wait to see you all in her new dental practice!
