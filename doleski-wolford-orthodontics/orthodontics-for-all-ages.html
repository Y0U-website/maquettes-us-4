Orthodontics for Children

What Is Early Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment for children—often called interceptive orthodontics or early treatment—aims to guide your child’s jaw growth and incoming permanent teeth. This early intervention can help prevent future problems with your child’s smile.

How Orthodontics for Kids Works

Dr. Doleski and Dr. Wolford recommend orthodontic evaluations by age seven. During these evaluations, they can identify any potential issues with your child’s jaw growth or tooth alignment.

Who Needs Interceptive Treatment?

If your child has bite issues, crowded teeth, or significant spacing problems they could benefit from early orthodontic treatment. Early signs like difficulty chewing, mouth breathing, or thumb-sucking habits that persist beyond the toddler years are signs to make an appointment.

Benefits of Early Treatment

  • Guided growth : Early treatment can help guide the jaw and teeth into proper alignment, preventing more serious issues later.
  • Simpler treatment : Addressing problems early can often reduce the complexity and duration of treatment needed during the teen years.
  • Healthier development : Ensuring proper alignment can help chewing, speaking, and oral health.

Reasons Not to Wait

Without early intervention, children may face more severe alignment and bite issues as they grow, which can complicate future treatment and impact their oral health and confidence.

When you schedule an appointment at our Erie , Lawrence Park, or Corry office, you’ll know your child’s smile is set up for success.

Orthodontics for Teens

All About Teen Treatment

Teen orthodontic treatment typically focuses on correcting alignment and bite issues that become more noticeable as your teen’s permanent teeth come in. This is an ideal time for orthodontic intervention, since their teeth and jaw are still growing and more easily adjusted.

How Orthodontics for Teens Works

Options for teen treatment include traditional metal braces, clear braces, and Solventum Clarity Clear Aligners . Regular check-ups and adjustments ensure that their treatment progresses smoothly and effectively.

Who Needs Treatment as a Teen?

Most teens between 12 and 18 are excellent candidates for orthodontic treatment. It’s especially beneficial if your teen has crooked teeth, overcrowding, gaps, or bite issues like overbites and underbites.

Benefits of Treatment

  • Early intervention : Addressing dental issues during the teen years can prevent more serious problems later.
  • Aesthetic options : Teens can choose from less noticeable options like clear braces or aligners, making the treatment process more appealing.
  • Social confidence : A straight, beautiful smile can greatly enhance a teen’s self-esteem and social interactions.

Don’t Put Off Treatment

Ignoring orthodontic needs during the teen years can lead to more severe dental problems in adulthood, which may require more invasive and costly treatments.

Orthodontics for Adults

What Is Adult Orthodontics?

Orthodontic treatment for adults is designed to correct dental issues that affect your smile and oral health. Whether you’re dealing with misaligned teeth, bite problems, or spacing issues, you can choose from a variety of solutions, including traditional braces, clear braces, and clear aligners.

How Orthodontics for Adults Works

Your orthodontic treatment as an adult starts with a thorough consultation. Dr. Doleski or Dr. Wolford will evaluate your teeth, discuss your concerns, and develop a customized treatment plan.

Depending on your needs and preferences, you may opt for traditional metal braces, more discreet clear braces, or removable clear aligners. We’ll monitor your progress throughout your treatment and make any necessary adjustments to ensure the best results.

Who Needs Adult Treatment?

Orthodontic care is great for adults of all ages. Whether you’re seeking to improve your smile for aesthetic reasons or to address functional issues that have developed over time, we can help. You should be committed to maintaining your oral hygiene and attending regular appointments to ensure the best results.

Benefits of Treatment

  • Improved appearance : Get a straighter, more attractive smile.
  • Enhanced oral health : Correcting alignment issues can make it easier to clean your teeth, reducing the risk of decay and gum disease.
  • Boosted confidence : A beautiful smile can enhance your self-esteem and confidence in personal and professional settings.Don’t Put Off Treatment

Don’t Put Off Your Smile

Delaying your orthodontic treatment as an adult can worsen dental issues, including tooth decay, gum disease, and jaw problems. Addressing these issues sooner rather than later can prevent more complex treatments in the future.
