Meet Your Orthodontist in Erie, Lawrence Park & Corry

Marian S. Wolford, DMD, MS

At an early age, Dr. Marian S. Wolford worked for her father, a general dentist, after school and during summers. This fostered a love of dentistry that would last a lifetime.

Today, Dr. Wolford is committed to giving patients of all ages beautiful smiles they can enjoy for life.

Memberships & Affiliations

Dr. Wolford maintains an active membership with many professional groups, which allows her to keep up with all the latest advancements in the orthodontic industry. These groups include:

  • American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry
  • American Association of Orthodontists
  • American Dental Association
  • Erie County Dental Association
  • Pennsylvania Dental Association
  • Pittsburgh Esthetics Study Club
  • Practice Enhancement Study Club

Education & Experience

Inspired by her early experience with dentistry, Dr. Wolford earned her bachelor’s degree from the University of Pittsburgh before following it up with her doctorate at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine.

She went on to complete a pediatric dentistry residency at the Children’s Hospital of Pittsburgh and then an orthodontic residency at the University of Detroit.

Since 1994, Dr. Wolford has provided excellent orthodontic care for all her patients.
