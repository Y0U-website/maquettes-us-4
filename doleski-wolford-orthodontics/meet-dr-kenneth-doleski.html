Meet Your Orthodontist in Erie, Lawrence Park & Corry

Kenneth A. Doleski DMD, MS

Dr. Kenneth A. Doleski first became interested in orthodontics after receiving treatment as a teenager. Now, he spends his time creating beautiful smiles to give patients the same boost of confidence he got from his time in braces as a teen.

Memberships & Affiliations

As a way to keep up-to-date with all the latest advancements in the orthodontic community, Dr. Doleski maintains an active membership in the following groups:

  • American Association of Orthodontists
  • American Dental Association
  • Erie County Dental Association
  • Pennsylvania Dental Association
  • Pittsburgh Esthetics Study Club
  • Practice Enhancement Study Club

Education & Experience

Dr. Doleski’s early experience as a teen orthodontic patient encouraged him to complete his undergraduate work at Allegheny College and follow it up by getting his doctorate at the University of Pittsburgh Dental School.

He then earned his specialty certification in orthodontics at the University of Iowa Orthodontic School. Dr. Doleski has been providing high-quality orthodontic care since 1991.
