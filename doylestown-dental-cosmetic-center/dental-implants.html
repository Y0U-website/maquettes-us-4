Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most patients in good oral health and with enough bone density are good candidates for dental implants. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your jawbone and overall health to determine if implants are the right option. Patients with certain health conditions or limited bone may still be eligible for additional treatments like bone grafting. Schedule a consultation to find out more about your options.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental checkups, good oral hygiene, and not smoking can help extend the life of your implants. We’ll guide you on how to care for your implants to give you a lifetime of smiles. Unlike other restorations, implants are designed to be durable.

Does the implant procedure hurt?

The implant surgery is done with local anesthesia so you won’t feel anything during the procedure. Some patients feel mild soreness after the procedure but this can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most cases have minimal downtime so you can get back to your daily activities shortly after.

How long is the healing process?

Healing times vary from patient to patient but osseointegration takes a few months. This time allows the implant post to fuse with the jawbone fully and create a stable base for the crown . We’ll guide you through each stage to ensure proper healing and success.

Do I need to modify my diet with a dental crown?

After surgery, soft foods are recommended during the initial healing period. Once fully healed you can eat comfortably and enjoy a wide variety of foods just like natural teeth. With proper care, your implants will feel as secure as your natural teeth.
