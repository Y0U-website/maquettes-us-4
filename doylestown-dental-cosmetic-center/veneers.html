Benefits of Veneers

Natural Looking

Dental veneers are designed to mimic natural teeth, providing a beautiful, realistic appearance. Each veneer is customized to fit perfectly, blending seamlessly with the surrounding teeth for a cohesive look.

Long-Lasting

With proper care, veneers can last over 10 years, allowing you to enjoy your beautiful smile without frequent replacements. This durability makes veneers a cost-effective choice in the long run.

Stain Resistant

Porcelain veneers are stain-resistant, helping maintain a bright, white smile even if you enjoy coffee, tea, or wine. With fewer staining concerns, veneers simplify your dental care routine.

Cosmetic Versatility

Veneers address multiple issues, including gaps, chips, and slightly crooked teeth, all in one treatment. They’re an ideal cosmetic procedure for patients looking to correct several concerns simultaneously.

Confidence Booster

A transformed smile can improve self-confidence, empowering you to smile freely in all aspects of life. Many patients experience a positive boost in social and professional settings after receiving veneers.

The Veneers Process at Doylestown Dental Cosmetic Center

Step 1: Consultation

Your first step is a consultation with our cosmetic dentist to discuss your goals. It is important to check for gum disease before proceeding with veneers. We’ll take X-rays and evaluate your smile to determine the best veneer option.

Step 2: Preparation

In most cases, a small amount of enamel is removed from the teeth to make room for the veneers. This step ensures a perfect fit and natural look once the veneers are placed.

Step 3: Impression

We take an impression of your teeth which is then sent to a dental lab where your custom veneers are made. This process takes a few weeks during which temporary veneers may be placed.

Step 4: Placement

Once the new veneers are ready, we bond them to your teeth with a special adhesive. Each veneer is placed to enhance your smile and feel comfortable and secure.

Step 5: Final Touches

We make sure the veneers are comfortable and look great. With your approval, your new smile is complete, and we’ll give you tips on how to maintain it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do veneers last?

Veneers last 10-15 years with proper care. Brush, floss, and regular dental visits will help extend their life and keep your smile looking great.

Are veneers reversible?

Because a small amount of enamel is removed, veneers are usually a permanent option. We make sure patients fully understand the process before we start.

Can veneers fix slightly crooked teeth?

Yes, veneers are a great solution for slightly crooked teeth as they improve the appearance without orthodontics. They can give you a straighter, more aligned smile.

Do veneers stain like natural teeth?

Porcelain veneers are stain-resistant but it’s still a good idea to avoid staining foods to keep them looking their best.

How much do veneers cost?

Cost varies based on the material and the number of teeth being treated. We offer consultations to give you a quote and go over options.
