Dr. Kelly is proud to serve Pittsburgh and the surrounding areas as the premier board-certified pediatric laser dentist for kids, adolescents, and special-needs patients. She arrived in 2014 after completing her pediatric dental residency, a rigorous two-year advanced training program necessary to become a pediatric dentist. ​

​In March 2019, Dr. Kelly launched Terrific Teeth Pediatric Dentistry. Shortly after celebrating our four-year anniversary, Terrific Teeth moved to its new new location at 530 Chauvet Dr, Pittsburgh PA 15275 next to Target .

Prior to becoming a dentist for kids, Dr. Kelly grew up moving around the world before her family settled in the Washington D.C. area. She earned her undergraduate degree at the College of William and Mary and continued her studies at the Medical College of Virginia School of Dentistry (VCU).

After graduating from dental school, she completed a general practice residency in New York City. She then practiced as a general dentist for 2 years in the D.C. area before transitioning to her pediatric dental residency.
