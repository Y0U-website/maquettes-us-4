ABOUT US

Like our pediatric dentists Dr. Kelly and Dr. Casey our orthodontist Dr. Sencak also received specialized training. Given the age when most people undergo orthodontia, having an orthodontist and pediatric dentist under one roof provides continuity of care, resulting in the most efficient treatment and beneficial outcome for our patients. For children and adult community members seeking orthodontia but aren t patients of Terrific Teeth, our orthodontist can help you look and feel your best!

Our dentists are proud to serve Pittsburgh and the surrounding communities. Patients and their parents looking for a caring and compassionate pediatric dentist and orthodontist come from as close as Robinson Township, Moon, Imperial, McDonald and the entire West Hills, to the South Hills, North Hills, Eastern Suburbs, Ohio River Valley and beyond to include the counties of Allegheny, Beaver, Butler, Westmoreland, Washington, Fayette, Greene and even Ohio and West Virginia!

MEET THE DOCTORS

Dr. Kelly is proud to serve Pittsburgh and the surrounding areas as the premier board-certified pediatric laser dentist for kids, adolescents, and special-needs patients. She arrived in 2014 after completing her pediatric dental residency, a rigorous two-year advanced training program necessary to become a pediatric dentist. ​

​In March 2019, Dr. Kelly launched Terrific Teeth Pediatric Dentistry. Shortly after celebrating our four-year anniversary, Terrific Teeth moved to its new new location at 530 Chauvet Dr, Pittsburgh PA 15275 next to Target .

Prior to becoming a dentist for kids, Dr. Kelly grew up moving around the world before her family settled in the Washington D.C. area. She earned her undergraduate degree at the College of William and Mary and continued her studies at the Medical College of Virginia School of Dentistry (VCU).

After graduating from dental school, she completed a general practice residency in New York City. She then practiced as a general dentist for 2 years in the D.C. area before transitioning to her pediatric dental residency. During her pediatric dental residency, Dr. Kelly served as the Chief Resident and gained specialized training in overall oral health, child growth and development, dental trauma, sedation/anesthesia, and patients with special healthcare needs. Dr. Kelly is a board certified pediatric dentist!

When she is not working, Dr. Kelly enjoys spending time with her husband, son & daughter, family & friends, reading, volunteering and traveling.

Dr. Casey joined the Terrific Teeth family in September, 2023. She began her education here in Pittsburgh, PA, by earning her B.A. at the University of Pittsburgh. While there, she was inducted into the academic honor society Phi Beta Kappa and then went on to complete her dental education at the University of Pittsburgh’s School of Dental Medicine, where she also received a certificate in Public Health. Upon graduating from dental school, Dr. Casey was elected into the Omnicron Kappa Upsilon honors society.

Eager to enter private practice and serve her local community, Dr. Casey moved back to Pittsburgh, PA, and happily found her way to us at Terrific Teeth. ​ Dr. Casey is thrilled to be back in her hometown. While she really enjoyed her time in Colorado where she loved to hike, climb mountains, and snow ski, her heart has always remained in Pittsburgh, where she loves spending time with her extended family and volunteering in her local community. Dr. Casey recently got married in Ireland to her long-time boyfriend, Joe.

Dr. Sencak graduated from North Hills High School and pursued his education at the University of Pittsburgh, where he earned his Bachelor of Science degree. During that time, he was also elected into the nation’s oldest and most prestigious academic honor society, Phi Beta Kappa.

After completing three years of undergraduate education, he was awarded early admission into the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, where he completed his Doctorate degree in Dental Medicine. He was also elected into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, a national dental honor society. Upon completion of dental school, Dr. Sencak earned both a certificate in Orthodontics and a Master of Science degree at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry.

During his residency, Dr. Sencak was able to learn directly from two world-renowned orthodontists, Drs. Lysle Johnston and James McNamara, as well as the President of the American Association of Orthodontics.

The northern suburbs of Pittsburgh have been Dr. Sencak’s home for most of his life. I am honored to be able to dedicate my time and knowledge back to my hometown. Along with supporting local recreation centers and charity fundraisers, Dr. Sencak has also been involved with the Special Olympics and has even organized free dental-care events for the athletes. Aside from work, Dr. Sencak is an avid Pittsburgh sports team fan. He also likes spending time with family and friends, playing sports, and hiking.

Dr. Lang is originally from the North Hills of Pittsburgh. He began his education at the University of Pittsburgh where he obtained his Bachelor of Science in 2011. After finishing his undergraduate degree,he continued at The University of Pittsburgh where he finished his DMD in 2016. He achieved the Pitt trifecta by completing his anesthesia residency in 2019 at the University of Pittsburgh in conjunction with UMPC.

During his anesthesia training he rotated through numerous hospitals completing general anesthesia cases focusing on general surgery, obstetrics, ENT, oral-maxillofacial surgery, and pediatrics.

His passions include working with patients having special health care needs and pediatrics in the field of dentistry. Dr. Lang successfully became board certified in as a Dentist Anesthesiologist in 2020 by American Dental Board of Anesthesiology. He is currently an active member of the American Society of Dentist Anesthesiologists and Diplomat of the American Dental Board of Anesthesiology.

When he is not working, Dr. Lang enjoys spending time on the golf course, watching sports, camping, and relaxing with his wife Kayla, son Cameron and dog, Teddy.
