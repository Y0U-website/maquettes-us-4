The practice

As a parent himself, Dr. Hunter understands how challenging dental visits can be for families, particularly those with small children. That’s why we offer family block appointments. Having everyone seen at the same visit can be more convenient and less stressful. We are also happy to provide your children with TVs, toys, and a play area at our office.

Adults and seniors, children, parents, and teenagers – patients of all ages will receive a warm welcome in our Spring Hill dental office. Dr. Hunter and his highly qualified dental team are well-equipped to serve everyone’s unique needs. Local families from Spring Hill, Thompson’s Station, Columbia, Franklin, and other communities throughout Tennessee enjoy our family-friendly atmosphere…

Dr. Patrick Hunter served in the US Navy as the ship’s dentist on USS Gunston Hall and currently serves in the US Navy Reserve. As a proud father of four, Dr. Hunter loves looking after the dental needs of entire families. Read more about Dr. Hunter.

Dr. Patrick Hunter is committed to improving the lives of those in the Spring Hill community. He volunteers at a free local dental clinic and also sponsors initiatives such as a food pantry for low-income children. Read more about our community involvement.
