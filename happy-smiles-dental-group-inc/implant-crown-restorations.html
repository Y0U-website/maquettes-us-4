Implant Crown Restorations

At Happy Smiles Dental Group, Inc., we specialize in implant crown restorations to restore the beauty and function of your smile. If you have a dental implant but need a new or replacement crown, our custom-designed restorations provide a durable, natural-looking solution.

What Are Implant Crown Restorations?

Implant crown restorations are custom prosthetic teeth attached to dental implants, designed to replace missing teeth permanently. Whether you need a new crown for a recently placed implant or a replacement for an existing one, our high-quality restorations offer a seamless and long-lasting solution.

Benefits of Implant Crown Restorations

  • Natural Appearance: Custom-designed to match your natural teeth.
  • Durable & Long-Lasting: Made from high-quality materials for strength and longevity.
  • Secure & Stable: Attached to a dental implant for a strong, non-removable fit.
  • Restores Full Functionality: Allows normal chewing and speaking without discomfort.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Stimulates the jawbone and helps maintain facial structure.
  • Seamless Integration: Designed to blend perfectly with your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Crown Restorations

  • How long do implant crowns last? With proper care, they can last 10-15 years or longer before needing replacement.
  • Will my implant crown look natural? Yes, our crowns are custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth.
  • Can an implant crown be replaced? Yes, if your crown becomes worn or damaged, we can replace it without affecting the implant itself.
  • Do implant crowns require special care? Care for them like natural teeth—brush, floss, and visit your dentist regularly for check-ups.
  • Are implant crown restorations covered by insurance? Coverage varies by plan. Our team can help you understand your benefits and financing options.
