Single Tooth Implant

At Happy Smiles Dental Group, Inc., we specialize in permanent tooth replacement with single dental implants. If you’re missing a tooth due to decay, injury, or extraction, a dental implant offers a durable, natural-looking solution that restores both function and aesthetics.

What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single-tooth implant is a standalone artificial tooth replacement consisting of a biocompatible titanium post, an abutment, and a custom-made crown. Surgically placed into the jawbone, it fuses with the bone to provide a strong, stable foundation, mimicking the function of a natural tooth.

Benefits of a Single Tooth Implant

  • Permanent & Durable: Designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Natural Appearance: Matches the color and shape of your existing teeth.
  • Preserves Jawbone Health: Prevents bone loss by stimulating the bone.
  • Restores Chewing Function: Eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, implants don’t require altering neighboring teeth.
  • Comfortable & Stable: Feels and functions like a natural tooth.

What to Expect During the Treatment

Consultation & Planning

Dr. Junior Fernandez will evaluate your oral health, bone density, and overall suitability for an implant.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia for a precise, comfortable experience.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over a few months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a secure foundation.

Custom Crown Attachment

A high-quality, custom-designed crown is placed, seamlessly blending with your natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About Single Tooth Implants

  • How long does the process take? Healing typically takes three to six months, after which the final crown is attached.
  • Is the procedure painful? The surgery is performed under local anesthesia, with minimal discomfort during recovery.
  • How long do single-tooth implants last? With proper care, implants can last a lifetime, making them a reliable, long-term investment.
  • Are dental implants better than bridges? Yes, implants preserve jawbone health and do not require modifying adjacent teeth, unlike bridges.
  • How do I care for my implant? Maintain good oral hygiene, regular dental checkups, and a healthy lifestyle to ensure longevity.
