Implant Supported Dentures

At Happy Smiles Dental Group, Inc., we offer implant-supported dentures as a secure, long-lasting solution for tooth loss. If traditional dentures feel uncomfortable or unstable, implant-supported dentures provide enhanced stability, function, and confidence, restoring your smile with a strong, natural-looking replacement.

What Are Implant Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are a fixed or removable full-arch restoration anchored by dental implants. Unlike traditional dentures that rely on suction or adhesives, these dentures attach to titanium implants placed in the jawbone, offering superior comfort, stability, and durability.

Benefits of Implant Supported Dentures

  • Unmatched Stability: No slipping or shifting while eating or speaking.
  • Natural Look & Feel: Designed to mimic the appearance of natural teeth.
  • Improved Chewing Ability: Enjoy a wider variety of foods with confidence.
  • Prevents Bone Loss: Stimulates jawbone to maintain facial structure.
  • Long-Lasting Solution: Durable materials ensure years of reliable use.
  • Increased Comfort: Eliminates gum irritation and discomfort from traditional dentures.

What to Expect During the Treatment

Consultation

Dr. Junior Fernandez will assess your oral health and determine if implant-supported dentures are right for you.

Implant Placement

Dental implants are surgically placed in the jawbone under local anesthesia.

Healing Period

Over the next few months, the implants fuse with the bone to create a strong foundation.

Denture Attachment

A custom-made denture is securely fixed onto the implants, restoring your full smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Supported Dentures

  • How long do implant-supported dentures last? With proper care, they can last 15 years or more, and the implants themselves can last a lifetime.
  • Are implant-supported dentures removable? They can be fixed (permanent) or removable, depending on your preference and needs.
  • Does the procedure hurt? The implant placement is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring minimal discomfort.
  • How do I care for my implant-supported dentures? Maintain good oral hygiene, clean the dentures regularly, and schedule routine dental visits.
  • Are implant-supported dentures better than traditional dentures? Yes, they offer greater stability, comfort, and long-term oral health benefits compared to traditional dentures.
