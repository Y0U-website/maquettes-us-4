Dental Implants

At Happy Smiles Dental Group, Inc., we specialize in restoring smiles and confidence through state-of-the-art dental implants. If you’re missing one or more teeth, dental implants provide a durable, natural-looking solution that enhances both oral health and quality of life.

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible titanium, designed to replace missing teeth permanently. They are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they fuse with the bone to provide a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. This innovative solution looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Durability: Implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Improved Appearance: Restores the natural look of your smile.
  • Enhanced Function: Allows you to eat, speak, and chew comfortably.
  • Bone Preservation: Stimulates bone growth and prevents jawbone loss.
  • Comfort: Unlike dentures, implants feel like natural teeth and don’t shift.
  • Improved Confidence: Enjoy a restored smile and renewed self-esteem.

Our Dental Implants Services

All-on-Four Dental Implants: A full-arch tooth replacement using four strategically placed implants, providing a secure and permanent alternative to dentures.

Single Tooth Implant: A standalone implant that replaces a missing tooth with a natural-looking, fully functional restoration.

What to Expect During the Treatment

Consultation & Planning

Dr. Junior Fernandez will evaluate your oral health, jawbone density, and treatment goals.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia for a comfortable experience.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a strong foundation.

Final Restoration

A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to complete your fully functional, natural-looking smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most patients with good oral and overall health are candidates. We’ll assess your jawbone density and gum health during your consultation.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The full process can take several months, including implant placement, healing, and final restoration.
  • Are dental implants painful? The procedure is performed under anesthesia, and any discomfort is manageable with pain relief medications.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Brush and floss daily, attend regular checkups, and maintain good oral hygiene to ensure longevity.
  • Will my dental implants look natural? Yes! We customize each restoration to match the color, shape, and function of your natural teeth.
