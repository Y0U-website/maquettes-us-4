At Lake Forest Dental Health Care, we’ve crafted a space where families from Mission Viejo, Irvine, Foothill Ranch, Laguna Hills, and Laguna Niguel come to experience dental care with a family dentist that feels like home.

Your Family Dentist: Meet Dr. Tale

A local of Orange County, Dr. Tale is a familiar face to many families seeking a family dentist who combines technical expertise with a friendly, approachable demeanor. His academic journey, decorated with Cum Laude honors from the University of California, San Diego, and OKU honors from Columbia University, underpins his robust clinical experience, particularly in the realms of implant and cosmetic dentistry. With over 23 years of experience placing and restoring implants, Dr. Tale remains at the forefront of dental innovation, continuously enhancing his craft through ongoing education.

The Lake Forest Dental Health Care Difference

So, why choose us as your family dentist? Dr. Tale’s dedication to utilizing the latest technology and materials is just the start. Being part of the American Dental Association and Orange County Dental Association, he’s not only well-versed in current practices but also actively contributes to the future of dental care. Yet, it’s his genuine passion for connecting with patients and nurturing lasting relationships that truly sets our practice apart.

A Day in the Life Beyond the Clinic

What binds Dr. Tale to his community goes beyond the practice’s walls. As a family man and an enthusiast for an active lifestyle, he cherishes his time playing tennis, soaking up the sun at the beach, and creating memories with his wife and sons, Ryan and Mason. This personal connection to family and well-being shines through in the compassionate care he delivers to every patient.
