Fee Policy & Dental Insurance

The fee for endodontic treatment is determined by the complexity of the specific tooth being treated. If after endodontic evaluation, treatment is determined to be necessary, a fee will be quoted prior to treatment. This fee includes all necessary radiographs (dental xrays) and office checks and recalls. In payment for endodontic services rendered we accept VISA, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, CareCredit, cash, and personal check.
