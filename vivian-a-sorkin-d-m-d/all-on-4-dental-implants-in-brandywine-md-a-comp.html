Who Is a Good Candidate for All-on-4?

All-on-4 is designed for patients who are missing a full arch of teeth or who have remaining teeth that are no longer healthy enough to save. People who have worn traditional dentures and are unhappy with the fit, stability, or comfort may also be candidates for this treatment.

The first step in determining candidacy is a thorough evaluation of oral health and bone structure. During this evaluation, the dental team assesses whether the jaw has enough bone to support four implants. Patients who have experienced bone loss may still be candidates because the strategic placement of All-on-4 implants is designed to make the most of available bone.

Because periodontal health affects the success of dental implants, patients with gum disease may need treatment before implant placement. A periodontist in Brandywine specializes in the supporting structures of the teeth, including the gums and jawbone, and can provide the periodontal care needed to prepare the mouth for implants. Every patient’s situation is different, so a personal consultation is the best way to determine whether All-on-4 is the right choice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between All-on-4 and traditional dental implants?

Traditional dental implants support individual replacement teeth, with one implant placed for each missing tooth. All-on-4 uses only four implants to support a full dental bridge that replaces an entire arch. Both options use artificial tooth roots that fuse with the jawbone and help prevent bone loss. The All-on-4 approach, also called Teeth in a Day, is designed as a quick and convenient solution for patients who need complete arch replacement.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

The All-on-4 procedure is often described as Teeth in a Day because it is designed to replace missing teeth in a single day.
