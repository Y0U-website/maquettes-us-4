MEET Dr. KVINSLAND

Dr. Eric Kvinsland was born and raised in Gig Harbor. After graduating from Gig Harbor High School, he attended Washington State University to earn his Business and Human Resource Management degrees and Pacific Lutheran University to pursue a Chemistry degree. Dr. Kvinsland graduated from the University of Washington with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and was honored with the Gutherie Award for Restorative Excellence.

He continues to further his training and education ensuring that he is providing the best and most current dentistry techniques for his patients. In addition to his active involvement in several restorative based dental study groups, Dr. Kvinsland is past-President of the Pierce County Dental Society and served as a board member and delegate for the Washington State Dental Association.

You can’t help but run into Dr. Kvinsland and his family around town! He is an integral member of the Gig Harbor community and served on numerous boards including Pt. Defiance Zoo Society and Communities in Schools of Peninsula. He is also involved in his church, Bible study, and coaching lacrosse.

Dr. Kvinsland is family focused, dedicating much of his time to his wife and their four children. He is a certified pilot and loves the outdoors: fishing, hunting, skiing, golfing, and boating.

Dr. Kvinsland loves all aspects of family dental practice and has a special interest in dental implants and restorative dentistry.
