Dr. Javier Ambriz, DMD

Dr. Javier Ambriz graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, where he was recognized as a Dean’s scholar. After dental school, he completed a hospital-based two-year General Practice Residency at Albert Einstein Medical Center in Philadelphia where he received advanced training in all facets of general dentistry including cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, oral surgery, and endodontics.

Dr. Ambriz is focused on patient-centered treatment and strives to provide his patients with outstanding care. Aside from basic restorative dentistry, Dr. Ambriz’s current scope of practice includes a wide range of cosmetic dental procedures including beautifully crafted crown/veneers, implants, various teeth whitening processes, non-metal restorations and removable prostheses, as well as emergency services, advanced root canal treatments, non-surgical and surgical periodontics, extractions, and oral medicine.

Dr. Ambriz is a member of the Pennsylvania Dental Association, the American Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

When outside the office Dr. Ambriz enjoys spending time with his wife and son, reading, and traveling.

Daniel Schriftman

Daniel is a native of Philadelphia and received a bachelor of arts degree from Penn State University. He then went on to attend the University of Phoenix for his master’s degree in business. As our business manager, Daniel has been running our dental practice for over 15 years. He is a lifetime member of the American Association of Dental Office Managers.

Daniel values the part he plays in helping our patients by explaining their dental treatment to them and suggesting comfortable payment options. He enjoys managing such a great team of caring professionals and working with his dad every day.

Most of Daniel’s free time is spent with his family. He also coaches youth baseball and basketball year-round and is an avid fan of all Philly sports teams.
