The practice

Our goal is for you to leave our office with a memorable and enjoyable dental experience, which is why our welcoming and compassionate staff will do everything they can to make you feel right at home.

Dr. Loper is a graduate of Miami University as a chemistry major. Prior to dental school, he did graduate work in the Department of Pharmacology at Case Western Reserve University. He graduated from the Ohio State University College of Dentistry. Dr. Loper was a board member and past president of the Ohio State University Dental Alumni Society.

Dr. Ted Loper is Dr. Paul Loper’s youngest son and a proud graduate of The Ohio State University. Dr. Ted received a B.S. in Human Nutrition from Ohio State in the class of ’20 and a D.D.S. from The Ohio State College of Dentistry in the class of ’25.
