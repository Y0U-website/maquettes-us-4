The practice

Dr. Chris Kania and our experienced family dentistry team are committed to providing exceptional, personalized dental care in a comfortable, stress-free, and friendly atmosphere. As skilled dental professionals with a personal commitment to our community, our team works diligently to ensure that every patient receives the dental care they deserve.

We go above and beyond standards for cleanliness and safety, as recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA.

At our family dentistry, we pride ourselves in offering our patients an exceptional environment where they can feel comfortable knowing their oral health is in good hands. We develop an individualized plan for each patient. From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments and cosmetic dentistry, we are committed to you.

Visiting your dentist at least twice a year is critical to your dental and overall health. Good dental hygiene is incredibly important in preventing conditions such as tooth decay, gum disease, and other problems. We offer regular and periodontal cleanings, imaging, exams, and oral cancer screenings for both adults and children. Other preventative care services include athletic mouthguards…

We provide a variety of restorative services, including tooth-colored restorations, crowns, veneers, implant restorations, bridges, partials, and dentures. We use advanced technology and high-quality materials to give you the most effective, comfortable experience possible.

Our experienced dentists use state-of-the-art equipment and are trained in the latest, most effective surgical techniques to minimize discomfort when your care necessitates root canals, tooth extraction or implant placement.

Let our team help manage your dental anxiety through nitrous oxide sedation. Nitrous Oxide, also known as laughing gas, provides a general sense of well-being and ease for the duration of your procedure. The effects wear off within minutes of stopping nasal inhalation, so you can quickly return to your day after your procedure.
