All-On-4 FAQs

Due to the metal framework included in your all-on-4 implants, your new teeth will be solid and stable. You&#x27;ll be able to eat most foods but should avoid hard or chewy foods for at least four to six weeks.

As the name suggests, all-on-4 implants only use four implants to replace the teeth on an upper arch, whereas traditional implants utilize anywhere from five to eight. Additionally, the angle of the all-on-4 implants allows for increased support and does not require bone grafting like traditional implants do.

All-on-4 implants are meant to be a long-term solution, with a success rate exceeding 95 % over ten years. With proper care, which includes regular brushing, flossing, and professional dental cleanings, the implants themselves can last a lifetime. Additionally, the lifespan of the prosthetic teeth attached to the implants is typically around 10-15 years, depending on wear and tear. At that time, replacement teeth can be easily attached to the existing implants.

While the all-on-4 procedure itself is minimally invasive, some swelling and discomfort are common for a few days afterward. However, most patients experience very little pain and are able to return to work within one or two days. This downtime is often used to adjust to speaking and eating with your new teeth rather than managing pain.
