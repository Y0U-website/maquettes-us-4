Meet Dr. Matt

Dr. Matt Rhee

Dr. Matt knew he wanted to be an orthodontist when he was in high school. He applied to and was accepted into Boston University’s 7-year dental program. Dr. Matt graduated magna cum laude from his undergraduate studies in 3 years, and summa cum laude from dental school.

He went on to do his orthodontic residency training at St. Barnabas Hospital in NY, where he served as chief resident. To show his dedication to his specialty, Dr. Matt went through the rigorous board certification process with the American Board of Orthodontics. He is passionate about his craft, which is creating beautiful smiles.

Dr. Matt is a diplomat of the American Board of Orthodontics and is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the Northeastern Society of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, the Connecticut State Dental Association, and the Stamford Dental Society.
