Orthodontic Treatments for a Beautiful Smile

Braces Made Fun and Easy

Experience the transformative power of braces at Rhee Orthodontics in Fairfield County, CT. Our personalized treatments offer solutions for various dental conditions, including malocclusion, overbite, and crooked teeth. With a focus on high-quality care and a relaxed atmosphere, Dr. Matt and our skilled team provide a range of options to suit your needs, including traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligner therapy.

Our Orthodontic Treatments

Metal Braces

We offer traditional metal braces as an effective and affordable option for achieving a beautiful smile. Made of stainless steel, these braces are attached to the front of your teeth and allow our Dr. Matt to adjust them in three dimensions. With colorful bands that can be changed at each visit, metal braces can even make your orthodontic journey a fun and personalized experience.

Ceramic Braces

For those seeking a more discreet orthodontic treatment, ceramic braces are a great option. These clear braces work similarly to metal braces but blend in more naturally with your teeth, making them less noticeable. This option is especially popular among adults and teenagers looking for a more subtle and aesthetically pleasing approach to straightening their smile.

Lingual Braces

Rhee Orthodontics understands the desire for a completely hidden braces option. That’s why we offer lingual braces, which are placed on the inside (lingual) surface of your teeth. With lingual braces, you can achieve a straight smile without anyone knowing you’re wearing braces. Dr. Matt will customize these braces to fit the unique contours of your teeth, providing a comfortable and discreet treatment experience.

Clear Aligners

We also offer clear aligner therapy through Invisalign® as an alternative to traditional braces. Clear aligners are a series of virtually invisible, removable trays that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. This popular option appeals to individuals who prefer a more discreet orthodontic solution. Aligners are suitable for older individuals whose teeth and jaws are likely done growing. With clear aligners, you can enjoy the freedom to eat, drink, and maintain your oral hygiene routines with ease.
