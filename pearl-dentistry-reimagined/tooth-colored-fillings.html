What to Expect During Your Filling Appointment

Receiving a tooth-colored filling is a comfortable and straightforward procedure. After carefully removing the decayed portion of the tooth, the area is cleaned and prepared before the composite resin is applied in layers.

Each layer is hardened using a specialized curing light before the filling is shaped and polished to match your natural bite and tooth anatomy. Once complete, your restoration blends naturally with your smile, allowing you to return to your normal routine with minimal interruption.
