With proper oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last for decades. The implant itself is designed to be a long-term solution, while the crown attached to it may need replacement over time due to normal wear.

Most adults with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density are good candidates for dental implants. During your consultation at our Charlotte dental office, we’ll evaluate your oral health, review your medical history, and determine whether dental implants are the right option for you.

Dental implants provide a permanent, stable solution for replacing missing teeth without relying on neighboring teeth for support. While dentures and bridges may be appropriate in some situations, implants often offer improved comfort, function, and long-term oral health benefits.
