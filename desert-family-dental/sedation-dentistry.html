Sedation Dentistry FAQ

What is sedation dentistry?

Sedation dentistry uses medication to help patients feel calm and relaxed during dental procedures. We offer three levels: nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and IV sedation.

Will I be unconscious?

No. All three options keep you conscious and able to respond. You will be deeply relaxed, and many patients have little to no memory of the procedure afterward.

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Yes. When administered by a trained dentist like Dr. Lee, sedation is very safe. He monitors patients throughout every procedure and has completed advanced sedation training.

Can I drive home after sedation?

With nitrous oxide, yes. It wears off quickly. With oral sedation or IV sedation, you will need a responsible adult to drive you home.

What procedures can be done with sedation?

Virtually any procedure, including cleanings, root canals , implants , extractions , and cosmetic treatments.

Do you offer sedation for children?

Yes. We offer age-appropriate sedation options for pediatric patients who experience dental anxiety.

How much does sedation dentistry cost?

The cost depends on the type of sedation and the length of the procedure. We accept most major dental insurance plans and offer financing options. Visit our patient resources page for details.

I haven’t been to the dentist in years because of anxiety. Can you help?

Absolutely. Many of our sedation patients come to us after years away from the dentist. We never judge. Dr. Lee will create a clear, comfortable plan to help you get back on track.
