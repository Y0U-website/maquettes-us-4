Get started with an initial consultation

Meet our team, learn more about orthodontics, and discuss treatment options that best meet your needs.

Our consultations begin with one-on-one attention where we take the time to get an in-depth understanding of your needs. We’ll review your dental and medical history forms. Dr. Zwickel will then perform a complete oral exam with X-rays to determine whether treatment is necessary.

Next step to achieving the smile of your dreams

After your consultation, we schedule your next appointment where Dr. Zwickel places your new orthodontic appliance. This appointment generally takes about an hour, and our team is available to make sure you are relaxed and comfortable.

Progress visits

Seeing the orthodontist every four to eight weeks is critical to your successful orthodontic treatment. We work with you to schedule appointments as conveniently as possible.

Life happens. We get it. If you’re running late or need to reschedule, please let us know and we’ll do everything we can to accommodate you.
