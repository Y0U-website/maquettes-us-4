Getting Started at Sandy Smiles Orthodontics is easy!

Selecting orthodontic care is a big decision, and we’re here to make the process seamless. Your journey to a new smile begins with a comprehensive assessment of your smile, mouth, and face, enabling us to craft a personalized treatment plan. Utilizing state-of-the-art technology, we provide a range of effective and gentle treatment options to help you achieve your smile goals. Our commitment to one-on-one attentive care ensures a smooth sailing path to a straighter smile from start to finish.

During your initial consultation, we also address all financial information, insurance options, and payment plans. Our goal is to make orthodontic treatment as accessible and affordable as possible and we offer flexible payment options that you can customize to help fit your budget.

Take the next step toward achieving the smile of your dreams by scheduling your complimentary consultation today! Discover the confidence that comes with a straight, healthy smile and learn why our patients trust us with their care.
