Payment Options Available

We understand the importance of convenient financial options when it comes to dental care. That s why we accept a wide range of insurance plans (PPO and Medicare Advantage) and offer flexible payment solutions to ensure our services are accessible to all. Payment is due at the time of each service and collected at the end of every appointment. Call or email our office with any insurance questions and they will guide you through the process.

We accept all major credit cards, cash, and checks. We also accept financing with Care Credit and Cherry Payment Options.
