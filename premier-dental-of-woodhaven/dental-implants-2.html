What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent replacements for missing teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. At Premier Dental of Woodhaven, Dr. Ladino provides comprehensive implant services, from placement to restoration. Implants consist of a titanium post surgically placed in your jawbone, which acts as a new tooth root, and a custom crown that restores the visible portion of your tooth.

We offer a wide range of services to help you achieve your goals.

Single Tooth Implants

Replace one missing tooth with a titanium implant and custom-crafted crown for a seamless, natural-looking result.

Implant-Supported Bridge

Replace multiple adjacent missing teeth using strategically placed implants to support a dental bridge.

Implant Overdentures

Secure removable dentures with implants for improved stability, comfort, and confidence when speaking and eating.
