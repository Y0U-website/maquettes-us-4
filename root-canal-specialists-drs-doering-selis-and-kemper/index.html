The practice

We will take every measure to ensure that your procedure is in no way uncomfortable or painful. In most cases we will inject a small amount of anesthesia to gently numb a concentrated area of your mouth. For most patients, the feeling of numbness usually subsides after 2-8 hours depending on how quickly you metabolize the solution.

In some circumstances where we would like to monitor the healing of your tooth, you will be asked to return to our office. We will send a reminder notice to you when you are due for a recall appointment.

The fee for your endodontic treatment will be based on the extent of treatment. During your first visit we will discuss the probable number of visits, their length, and the fees involved. Endodontic fees usually range from $900 to $1,500 and it is our policy that your care is paid for at the time of treatment.
