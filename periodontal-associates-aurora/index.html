Founded in 1974, Periodontal Associates began here in Aurora and has grown into one of Colorado’s most established and trusted periodontal practices. The Aurora office expanded in 2020 to accommodate more patients and advanced technology, continuing our five-decade legacy of excellence in periodontal and implant care.

Dr. Douglas Heller and Dr. Eric Beckman are highly trained periodontists with decades of combined experience in restoring gum health, rebuilding bone, and replacing missing teeth with dental implants.

Treatment for all stages of gum disease, including deep cleanings, laser therapy, and regenerative care to restore healthy support around teeth.

Procedures that rebuild gum tissue, cover exposed roots, and strengthen the foundation around teeth.
