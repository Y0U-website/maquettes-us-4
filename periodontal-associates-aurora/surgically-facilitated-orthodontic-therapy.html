What to Expect During Treatment

SFOT treatment follows a few simple stages:

  • Consultation and planning: Your periodontist and orthodontist work together to evaluate your bone structure, bite alignment, and treatment goals using digital imaging and CBCT scans.
  • Surgical procedure: The periodontist makes small, precise incisions in the bone surrounding the teeth. In some cases, bone grafting or guided periodontal tissue regeneration may be performed to enhance bone support.
  • Orthodontic activation: Braces or clear aligners are typically placed shortly after surgery, while the bone is in an active healing phase.
  • Healing and monitoring: Most patients resume normal activities within a few days as bone remodeling continues for several months.

FAQs

How much faster is SFOT compared to traditional orthodontics?

Teeth can typically move two to three times faster, depending on individual healing response and treatment complexity.

Is SFOT surgery painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. Tenderness and swelling afterward is normal and usually resolves within a few days.

Can SFOT be done with clear aligners?

SFOT works with both clear aligners and traditional braces to speed up movement and improve outcomes. It is, however, case dependant and your orthodontist will work closely with our periodontists to decide what is the best treatment modality for you.

Is SFOT safe for adults?

SFOT is especially beneficial for adults with dense bone or slower bone metabolism, which can make traditional orthodontics more difficult.
