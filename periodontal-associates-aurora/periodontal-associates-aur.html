FAQs

Do you accept dental insurance?

We accept most major dental insurance plans and will verify your benefits before your treatment to minimize surprises. Our team provides estimates in advance and helps you understand your coverage. For any costs not covered by insurance, we offer financing options through CareCredit and Alpheon, making it easier to move forward with the treatment you need.

Is your office accessible?

Our Aurora office is fully wheelchair accessible with free on-site parking and ground-level entry.

Do you offer sedation?

Yes. We offer IV, oral, and nitrous oxide sedation administered by certified anesthesia providers for comfort and safety.

Do you accept new patients without a referral?

Yes! While many patients are referred by their general dentist, referrals are not required. You can contact our office directly to schedule a consultation.
