Meet Our Pasadena Dentist

Dr. Afshin Nateghi, DDS

Dr. Afshin Nateghi is a compassionate and experienced general dentist serving the Maryland community. He provides a full range of services — including root canals, surgical extractions, dental implants, Invisalign® aligners and cosmetic dentistry — all under one roof for a seamless patient experience.

He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine and has completed advanced implant training through Implant Pathway. Dr. Nateghi is especially skilled in dental implant placement and is committed to ongoing education and the latest techniques.

For over seven years, he cared for patients at Strong Smiles in Odenton, earning a reputation for gentle, high-quality dentistry and lasting patient relationships. Known for his calm demeanor and patient-first philosophy, Dr. Nateghi helps patients — especially those with dental anxiety — feel at ease through empathetic, personalized care.

Outside the office, he enjoys time with his two children and relaxing at the piano.

Dr. Ardalan Nateghi, DDS

Dr. Ardalan Nateghi is passionate about providing exceptional dental care with a focus on patient comfort and delivering high-quality results. He offers a wide range of services, from preventive care to cosmetic dentistry and dental implants, helping patients achieve both optimal health and a confident smile. Dr. Nateghi believes in personalized treatment and creating a welcoming environment where patients feel at ease throughout their dental journey.

Outside of the office, Dr. Nateghi enjoys an active lifestyle, including playing tennis, cooking, hiking, and spending quality time with family. He is committed to helping each patient enjoy a healthy, beautiful smile and looks forward to welcoming you to the practice.
