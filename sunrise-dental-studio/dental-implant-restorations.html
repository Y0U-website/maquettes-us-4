Dental Implant Restorations

One of the main advantages of dental implant restorations is their durability. With proper care, they can last a lifetime, unlike traditional dentures or bridges, which may need to be replaced every few years. Additionally, implant restorations look and feel like natural teeth, providing a comfortable and natural-looking smile. Implant restorations also help prevent bone loss in the jaw, which can occur with missing teeth and lead to facial sagging.

Overall, dental implant restorations offer several advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. To learn more about our dental implant restoration options, call a member of our team today.

  • Dental bridges are used to replace one or multiple missing teeth in a row. They are attached to multiple implants to provide stability and support.
  • Dentures can be an excellent option for patients who are missing all of their teeth. Implant-supported dentures are designed to attach to four or more implants in the jaw, providing a stable and secure fit that eliminates the need for messy adhesives.
