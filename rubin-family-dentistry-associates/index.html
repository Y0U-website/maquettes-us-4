Mark Rubin graduated from Queens College in New York and went on to complete his DMD degree from The University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. He completed a General Practice residency at New York’s Goldwater Memorial Hospital before relocating to South Windsor, CT. He opened his dental practice here in South Windsor in 1973 and has been treating patients in town for over 40 years.

Dr. Rubin is a member of the American Dental Association, CT State Dental Association and Hartford Dental Society. He enjoys participating in dental continuing education programs.

He was thankful to have his son, Todd, join the practice in 2004 and is excited at the opportunity to work with his daughter-in-law, Dr. Satoko Ono Rubin, as well. Mark plays tennis regularly year-round and enjoys travelling with his wife and spending time with his family and grandchildren.

Dr. Mark Rubin is now enjoying his retirement and would like to thank his loyal patients for so many wonderful years and great relationships that he will always greatly appreciate. He knows you are in the best of hands with the caring and talented doctors at Rubin Family Dentistry & Associates.
