Meet the Dentists

Mark D. Rubin, DMD

Mark Rubin graduated from Queens College in New York and went on to complete his DMD degree from The University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. He completed a General Practice residency at New York’s Goldwater Memorial Hospital before relocating to South Windsor, CT. He opened his dental practice here in South Windsor in 1973 and has been treating patients in town for over 40 years.

Dr. Rubin is a member of the American Dental Association, CT State Dental Association and Hartford Dental Society. He enjoys participating in dental continuing education programs.

He was thankful to have his son, Todd, join the practice in 2004 and is excited at the opportunity to work with his daughter-in-law, Dr. Satoko Ono Rubin, as well. Mark plays tennis regularly year-round and enjoys travelling with his wife and spending time with his family and grandchildren.

Dr. Mark Rubin is now enjoying his retirement and would like to thank his loyal patients for so many wonderful years and great relationships that he will always greatly appreciate. He knows you are in the best of hands with the caring and talented doctors at Rubin Family Dentistry & Associates.

Todd B. Rubin, DMD

Todd Rubin graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine before completing an Advanced Education in General Dentistry Residency at University of CT. He is an alumnus of Binghamton University and grew up in South Windsor before relocating to West Hartford where he graduated from Hall High School.

Todd is a member of the American Dental Association, Connecticut State Dental Association, Hartford Dental Society and furthers his expertise with the Spear Education Group, a continuing education workshop with other specialists and dentists.

Todd feels fortunate to have had the opportunity to work with and learn from his father, and is thrilled and honored to have his wife join the practice, adding her years of specialty training to what can be offered to our patients. He enjoys traveling to new places with his wife, plays tennis, golf and likes to ski.

Satoko Ono Rubin, DMD, MDSc

She is an avid road biker, runner and usually shoots a better round of golf than Todd.

Dr. Satoko Ono Rubin also has a primary office location in West Hartford Center.

Nicole Skalina, DMD

Nicole Skalina graduated from Brandeis University before earning her DMD degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. Following dental school, she completed a General Practice Residency at Mount Sinai Hospital in Manhattan and was selected to stay on as Chief Resident.

After working in New York City for 7 years she moved to West Hartford with her husband to raise their family. Dr. Skalina believes in forming a partnership with patients in order to reach their oral health needs. She is committed to staying on top of the best treatment options available and therefore regularly participates in a variety of continuing education opportunities in her free time. Dr. Skalina is a licensed Invisalign provider.

She is a member of the American Dental Association, Connecticut State Dental Society and Hartford County Dental Society.

She enjoys spending time with her family and friends, a good Netflix show, baking, exercising and traveling. She is always looking for good book recommendations.

Shaun Thomas, DMD

Dr. Thomas has found a devoted passion in dentistry and helping improve the oral healthcare needs of his patients. He hopes the quality care he provides will make a positive impact in the community he serves. Dr. Thomas firmly believes in developing life-long relationships with his patients with the highest standard of care, professionalism, and communication. He is passionate about patient education and providing his patients with the tools to maintain good oral health.

Dr. Thomas is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Education Association, and American Dental Association.

When he is not practicing dentistry, he enjoys traveling, spending time with his family, exercising and reading. We are honored to have Dr. Thomas join Rubin Family Dentistry & Associates and provide you with the highest standard of care.

Emma Dzialo, DMD

Dr. Emma Dzialo earned both her undergraduate degree in Molecular and Cell Biology and her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Connecticut. During dental school, she was honored with an award from the Connecticut State Dental Association for her work in dental radiology research. She also received the South Park Award in recognition of her dedication to community service and expanding access to dental care.

Following dental school, Dr. Dzialo completed a General Practice Residency at SUNY Upstate Medical University, where she gained advanced training in managing complex cases, treating medically compromised patients, and addressing dental emergencies.

Dr. Dzialo is passionate about providing high-quality, patient-centered care. She believes in building strong relationships through trust, empathy, and clear communication. She is committed to staying up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry to ensure her patients receive the most effective and comfortable care possible.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Dzialo enjoys spending her free time playing tennis, hiking, and exploring the outdoors.
