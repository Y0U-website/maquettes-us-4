Dr. Laura Vaccariello, known to her patients as Dr. Vac — is a Charlotte native who has always been passionate about education and healthcare. She earned her undergraduate degree from Boston College, then spent several years teaching high school Biology in Chicago. Her dedication to science and service led her back to Boston, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Following dental school, Dr. Vaccariello completed a General Practice Residency at Massachusetts General Hospital, a Harvard-affiliated program, where she received advanced clinical training. She then pursued her specialty in orthodontics at Marquette University School of Dentistry in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, where she also earned a Master’s degree. Her master’s research focused on the impact of visible versus invisible orthodontic appliances on adults’ perceived professionalism in the workplace.

Dr. Vac combines her deep clinical expertise with a warm, educational approach to patient care, helping individuals of all ages achieve confident, healthy smiles.

Dr. Vac and her husband, Matt, live in Fort Mill with their two young children, Will and Remi. Together, they enjoy exploring the outdoors, trying out creative new recipes, traveling, and cheering on the Carolina Panthers. Being a part of the Fort Mill community is something Dr. Vac and her family truly value, both personally and professionally.

As one of the few board-certified orthodontists in Fort Mill , Dr. Vaccariello brings a high level of clinical expertise and a deep commitment to personalized care. She is a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics and a member of several esteemed professional organizations, including the American Association of Orthodontists , Southern Association of Orthodontists , South Carolina Association of Orthodontists , and the American Dental Association .

Dr. Vac is also a certified provider of both Invisalign® and 3M™ Clarity Aligners , offering flexible treatment options to meet each patient’s needs. Dedicated to staying at the forefront of her field, she regularly attends continuing education courses to ensure her patients benefit from the latest advancements in orthodontic techniques and technology.

Dr. Vaccariello is passionate about creating confident, healthy smiles—and making every patient feel right at home throughout their orthodontic journey.
