Signs You May Need Endodontic Treatment

Recognizing early signs of endodontic issues is important for maintaining your oral health. Waterford Dental can help you identify potential problems and provide timely care. Watch out for these common signs:

If you notice any of these symptoms, it’s best to seek professional advice soon. Quick action can help save your natural tooth and prevent further issues.

  • Ongoing tooth pain or sensitivity
  • Swollen or tender gums
  • A tooth that’s darker than others
  • Pain when eating
  • A chipped or cracked tooth
  • Long-lasting sensitivity to temperature
