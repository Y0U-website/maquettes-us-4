Dental Implants in Orange and Villa Park, CA

Dental implants are a reliable solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring both function and appearance. Whether replacing a single tooth or several, implants are designed to look natural, feel secure, and support long-term oral health. Advanced planning and precise placement help ensure strong, lasting results.

At Orange Grove Family Dentistry, dental implant placement and restoration are performed using modern digital imaging and proven techniques. The team focuses on comfort, accuracy, and durability, offering additional services such as bone grafting when needed to support implant stability. This comprehensive approach helps patients regain confident smiles and dependable chewing function.

Process

Initial consultation: Treatment starts with a comprehensive evaluation, including imaging and a conversation about your goals to create a personalized plan.

Implant placement: A titanium implant post is carefully positioned in the jawbone to act as a strong base for the replacement tooth.

Healing and integration: The implant gradually bonds with the jawbone over several months, creating a stable, long-term foundation.

Custom restoration: A custom crown , bridge , or denture is placed to fully restore the look and function of your smile.

Next Steps

Follow-up care: Appointments are scheduled to track healing progress and confirm proper integration of the implant.

Maintenance: You’ll receive instructions on daily care and oral hygiene to help protect your implant and support long-term success.

FAQ

  • What aftercare is needed following dental implant placement? After dental implant placement, patients should follow post-operative instructions carefully, including keeping the area clean, avoiding hard or chewy foods, and taking prescribed or recommended medications as directed. Mild swelling or soreness is normal and temporary. Good oral hygiene, avoiding smoking, and attending follow-up visits are essential to support healing and ensure the implant integrates properly for long-term success.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution and can last decades or even a lifetime with proper care. Maintaining good oral hygiene, avoiding smoking, and attending regular dental checkups help ensure their longevity. While the crown or restoration may need replacement over time due to normal wear, the implant itself is highly durable.
  • When is a bone graft necessary for implant placement? A bone graft may be necessary when there isn’t enough healthy jawbone to securely support a dental implant. This can occur due to tooth loss, gum disease, or bone deterioration over time. Bone grafting helps rebuild and strengthen the jawbone, creating a stable foundation so implants can be placed safely and successfully.
