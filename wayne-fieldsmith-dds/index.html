Wayne T. Fieldsmith, DDS

Wayne T. Fieldsmith, DDS, specializes in general dentistry, providing comprehensive oral health care to adults living in Terrell, Texas, and throughout Kaufman County.

The practice

Dr. Fieldsmith began his professional pursuits at Dallas Baptist College in Dallas, Texas, earning a Bachelor of Science in biology with a minor in chemistry. Following graduation, Dr. Fieldsmith enrolled at the UTHealth School of Dentistry in Houston, Texas, and obtained his Doctor of Dental Surgery.

Dr. Fieldsmith opened his private practice in 1975. Since then, he’s provided all-inclusive dentistry to adults, including bridges, crowns, implants, and periodontal treatment. Individuals can also receive root canals , restorative dentistry , laser dentistry , and wisdom tooth removal.

To ensure positive treatment outcomes, Dr. Fieldsmith stays current with new technology. He works under magnification and, whenever possible, provides treatment on-site. When individuals need more specialized treatment, he provides referrals to specialists in the area.

Dr. Fieldsmith proudly holds membership in various professional organizations, including the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, the Dallas County Dental Society, the Texas Academy of General Dentistry, and the Texas Dental Association. Dental implants look just like your natural teeth. What’s more, if you take good care of them and visit the dentist regularly, they often last a lifetime.

Getting dental implants even reduces the risk of potentially serious oral health problems. By replacing missing teeth, you can prevent things like gum recession, shifting teeth, or bone loss.

You need healthy gums and enough jaw bone volume to get dental implants. If Dr. Fieldsmith determines that your jaw can’t support implants (through X-rays or a bone scan), he might recommend bone grafting.

Services

Dental implants are small, screw-like posts that Dr. Fieldsmith inserts into your jaw. The implants integrate with your natural bone over time, providing a foundation for restorations like a bridge, crown, or fixed denture.
