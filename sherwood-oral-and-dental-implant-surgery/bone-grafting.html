BONE GRAFTING

Depending on your anatomy or how long a tooth has been missing there sometimes is not enough bone available to place an implant in. In these cases a bone grafting procedure may needed either before or at the same time as implant placement. There are several types of bone grafts we offer:

All of our bone grafting materials are treated to be safe to use. As technology continues to advance so do available grafting materials and we stay current on the best and most effective materials on the market. And, as the graft heals your body will naturally replace the bone graft with your own bone leaving no difference. Furthermore, through a simple blood draw we can make platelet-rich plasma (PRP) which adds your body s own growth factors to the bone graft to improve its healing and success.
