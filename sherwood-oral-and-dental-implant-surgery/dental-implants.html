What Exactly are Dental Implants?

Dental implant restorations are typically composed of three parts:

Depending on your needs, one implant could replace a single missing tooth while two or more implants can serve as a stable support for the replacement of multiple teeth. Additionally, four to six implants can serve as a foundation to replace an entire arch of teeth which is a service we are proud to offer the Danville community.

  • A titanium implant that functions as an artificial tooth root
  • An abutment which connects to the implant and emerges through the gums
  • A crown fitted atop the abutment to give the appearance of a natural tooth.
