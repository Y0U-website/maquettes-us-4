Our Practice

Making smiles in Valdosta, Georgia brighter since 1994

We aim to educate our patients in a safe environment while offering them necessary comprehensive care. We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we created a soothing environment and a scheduling system that never keeps you waiting long. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we give our patients the best care possible.

J. Scott Summers, DMD

Dr. Scott Summers has been practicing dentistry since June of 1994. He graduated from Lowndes High in 1987 and then went to Valdosta State College. He earned his doctorate in dental medicine from the Medical College of Georgia. When he first started practicing in 1994, Dr. Roy Moody hired him as an associate. Dr. Summers is a member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association as well as the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Summers is married to Dawn and they have 2 children, Faith and Hope. When Dr. Summers has spare time, he enjoys fishing on the "flats".
