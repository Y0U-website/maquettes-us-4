Endodontic procedures are more commonly referred to as a root canal. When bacteria from tooth decay reaches the inside of the tooth it becomes infected. This can cause pain and swelling in the area. A root canal procedure cleans and seals the inside of a tooth. A crown is commonly done after a root canal to strengthen the tooth.
