How Does a Smile Makeover Work?

To get started with your smile makeover, you need to visit us for a consultation. We will evaluate your oral health and ask what you hope to accomplish through treatment. If you have pictures that can help us understand your goals, please bring them.

A smile makeover may include multiple services, such as:

Depending on the treatments included in your smile makeover, the process might take anywhere from a few weeks to several months or longer. We perform multiple treatments simultaneously whenever circumstances allow.

  • Restorative care to make sure your teeth are healthy enough for cosmetic treatment
  • ClearCorrect aligners to address dental misalignment
  • Veneers or Lumineers to disguise imperfections like chips, cracks, small gaps between the teeth, and more
  • Teeth whitening to get rid of stubborn stains
  • Metal-free restorations to restore the appearance and strength of damaged teeth
  • Other services as needed to complete your smile makeover to your satisfaction

The Benefits of Getting a Smile Makeover

A smile makeover has the potential to change your life! Here are some of its most noteworthy benefits:

  • Beautiful, custom results: Cosmetic treatments can provide very natural-looking results. We design each procedure to complement your unique facial features.
  • Lasting results: With good oral hygiene, a reasonable diet, regular dental care, and other measures, your results may endure for years.
  • Better oral health: Crowns, ClearCorrect, and other restorative treatments can directly improve your oral health. Other services might indirectly benefit your oral health by motivating you to stick to excellent habits.
  • Improved confidence: When you feel good about your smile, you may be more likely to enjoy social situations, make a powerful impression at work, and reap other benefits of high self-confidence.

Understanding the Cost of a Smile Makeover

The cost of a smile makeover depends on the specific treatments involved and their extent. During your consultation, we will provide an estimate and help you understand your payment options. For example, we accept CareCredit financing, and we can assist with insurance claims for restorative procedures.

A smile makeover might seem a little pricey, but you can view it as an investment. It can reward you with years of heightened confidence, improved oral health, and a slew of other benefits. Talk to our team to learn more about how this service could change your life.
