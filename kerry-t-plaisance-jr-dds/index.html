Dr. Bostick has a few dentists in his family, including our own Dr. Plaisance, who is his cousin! So, it was in the back of his mind for a long time, and he finally decided it was the path for him when he realized it would give him the chance to help people in pain, improve their smile, and boost everyone’s confidence at the same time.

Dr. Bostick attended the Louisiana University School of Dentistry and has been practicing for over a decade. He remains committed to furthering his knowledge and skills and has completed many advanced courses through Spear Education.

Dr. Bostick grew up in a close-knit family in Destrehan, and he’s happy that his two older sisters have given him five nieces and nephews! He loves spending time with them as well as doing all sorts of outdoor activities, including hunting and fishing.
