The practice

At Severs Orthodontics, we aim to provide you with the highest quality treatment in a pleasant and comfortable setting where you receive personal attention every step of the way.

Our priority is to give patients the smile they deserve while ensuring patient satisfaction. Our patients are unique and deserve orthodontic care that reflects their needs and goals. Dr. Severs and our friendly team will treat you and your family with the kindness, respect, and compassion we want for our loved ones. Most importantly, we’ll create the healthy, beautiful smile of your dreams.

Dr. Severs is a member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the American Dental Association, Midwestern Society of Orthodontists, Illinois State Dental Society, and is a member of the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Severs is dedicated to providing you with the latest orthodontic technologies, advanced systems, and techniques to ensure maximum comfort and efficiency. At Severs Orthodontics, we offer metal braces and clear aligners for people who want to improve their oral health and appearance. Severs Orthodontics offers convenient appointments to accommodate your family’s schedule, and we also book appointments to ensure minimal wait time. We know your time is valuable, and consider this during each visit.

Severs Orthodontics is conveniently located on Maine Street, just a couple of blocks from Quincy High School. Our orthodontic practice is modern and relaxing, and we even have a refreshment station with water and coffee for you to enjoy while you wait.

Dr. Grant Severs is happy to welcome patients from all surrounding areas and to help as many people as possible achieve their dream smile. Please get in touch with us to learn more about our orthodontics practice and set up your next appointment with our fun and experienced orthodontist.
