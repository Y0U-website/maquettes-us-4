Restorative Dentistry

Whether you have a small cavity or need a full smile restoration, we have the skill and technology to repair your teeth so they look, feel, and function like natural ones. Dr. Barbakoff takes a conservative approach — preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

Every restoration is color-matched to your natural teeth and designed for long-term durability. We use high-quality labs and materials because your smile deserves it.

  • Composite fillings — tooth-colored, metal-free restorations that blend seamlessly with your smile
  • Porcelain crowns — custom-fitted caps for cracked, damaged, or significantly decayed teeth
  • Dental bridges — fixed replacement for one or more missing teeth, anchored to neighboring teeth
  • Inlays & onlays — precision restorations for teeth too damaged for a filling but not needing a full crown

Root Canal Therapy

Root canals have a reputation that simply isn’t deserved anymore. With modern techniques and anesthesia, the procedure is about as comfortable as getting a filling — and it saves a tooth that would otherwise need to be extracted.

Root canal therapy is needed when the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, usually due to deep decay, a crack, or trauma. Left untreated, the infection can spread and become a serious health issue.

  • Dr. Barbakoff performs root canal therapy in-office for many straightforward cases
  • We handle single-rooted and select multi-rooted teeth on a case-by-case basis
  • Treatment is followed by a crown to protect and restore the tooth
  • Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the process is

Periodontal Care

Gum disease is one of the most common — and most underestimated — health conditions in adults. It’s a leading cause of tooth loss, and research links it to heart disease, diabetes, and other systemic conditions. The good news: when caught early, it’s very treatable.

Svetlana handles most periodontal maintenance and works closely with Dr. Barbakoff on anything that needs a closer look.

  • Gingivitis treatment — early-stage gum inflammation, reversible with professional cleaning and better home care
  • Scaling & root planing — a deeper cleaning that removes tartar buildup from below the gumline and smooths root surfaces to help gums reattach
  • Periodontal maintenance — more frequent follow-up cleanings (typically every 3–4 months) for patients with a history of gum disease
  • Gum pocket monitoring — regular charting to track your gum health over time

Crowns & Bridges

Damaged or missing teeth affect how you eat, how you speak, and often how confident you feel. Crowns and bridges are fixed (non-removable) restorations that look and function like natural teeth.

We work with high-quality dental labs to ensure every crown and bridge looks natural and fits comfortably. Dr. Barbakoff will walk you through all your options so you can make the choice that’s right for your situation.

  • Porcelain crowns — custom-fitted caps for teeth that are cracked, broken down, or weakened after root canal treatment
  • Fixed bridges — a permanent, non-removable way to replace one or more missing teeth, anchored to neighboring teeth
  • Implant-supported crowns — we place the final crown over implants provided by our specialist partners
  • Inlays & onlays — precision restorations for teeth too damaged for a filling but not needing a full crown

Extractions & Oral Surgery

Sometimes a tooth needs to come out — whether due to decay, infection, crowding, or to make room for a prosthetic. We perform extractions in-office and always prioritize your comfort throughout the process.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth — and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone that acts as an artificial tooth root, topped with a natural-looking crown.

  • Single-tooth implant restorations — a permanent, standalone replacement that doesn’t affect neighboring teeth
  • Multiple-tooth implant restorations — implant-supported bridges to replace several teeth without traditional dentures
  • Implant-supported dentures — secure, snap-in dentures anchored by implants for patients who want more stability than traditional dentures
  • Implant crown placement — we design and place the final crown, bridge, or denture over implants with precision and care

Dentures

Dentures remain one of the most reliable and affordable ways to restore a full smile. Whether you’re missing all of your teeth or just a few, today’s dentures are more comfortable, natural-looking, and functional than ever before.

Dr. Barbakoff works with high-quality dental labs to ensure your dentures look natural and fit well. We’ll take the time to get the shade, shape, and bite right — because your dentures should feel like yours.

  • Full dentures — complete removable restoration for patients missing all upper or lower teeth
  • Partial dentures — removable appliances that fill in the gaps when some natural teeth remain, restoring appearance and chewing ability
  • Immediate dentures — placed the same day as extractions so you’re never without teeth during the healing process
  • Denture relines — adjustments to the fit as your gums and bone change over time, keeping your denture comfortable and secure
  • Denture repairs — fixes for cracks, broken teeth, or clasps to keep your existing denture functioning properly
  • Implant-supported dentures — a more stable, secure option that snaps onto implants for patients who want extra retention

Cosmetic Dentistry

Sometimes your teeth are healthy but you’re not happy with how they look. Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your smile — the color, shape, alignment, and overall look of your teeth.

Dr. Barbakoff takes a conservative, natural-looking approach to cosmetic work. The goal is never to make your teeth look "done" — it’s to make them look like the best version of yours.

  • Porcelain veneers — thin shells bonded to the front of teeth to correct chips, gaps, discoloration, or uneven shapes
  • Dental bonding — a quick, affordable way to repair chips, close small gaps, or reshape teeth using tooth-colored composite resin
  • Tooth contouring — subtle reshaping of tooth edges to create a more even, balanced appearance
  • Smile makeovers — combining multiple cosmetic procedures to achieve a complete smile transformation
  • Tooth-colored restorations — replacing old metal fillings with natural-looking composite or porcelain

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies don’t wait for a convenient time. When something goes wrong — a sudden toothache, a broken tooth, a knocked-out filling — you need help quickly. We make every effort to see emergency patients the same day.

If you’re experiencing a dental emergency, call us right away at (631) 744-3088. We’ll do our best to get you seen as quickly as possible and take care of the problem — or at minimum, manage your pain and stabilize the situation until definitive treatment can be completed.

  • Severe toothaches — diagnosis and pain relief for acute dental pain
  • Broken or chipped teeth — repair or stabilization to prevent further damage
  • Knocked-out teeth — time-sensitive reimplantation when possible
  • Lost fillings or crowns — temporary or permanent replacement to protect exposed tooth structure
  • Dental infections & abscesses — antibiotics and treatment planning to address the source of infection
  • Broken dentures or appliances — repair or temporary solutions to get you through

Night Guards & TMJ Treatment

If you wake up with jaw pain, headaches, or worn-down teeth, you may be grinding or clenching in your sleep. This condition — called bruxism — is incredibly common and can cause serious damage over time if left untreated.

Over-the-counter night guards can’t compare to a custom-fitted appliance made from impressions of your teeth. A professional guard fits better, lasts longer, and actually protects your teeth and jaw joint. If you suspect you’re grinding, bring it up at your next visit — we can usually spot the signs during a routine exam.

  • Custom night guards — professionally made occlusal guards that protect your teeth from grinding and clenching while you sleep
  • TMJ evaluation — assessment of jaw joint pain, clicking, or limited movement
  • Bite analysis — identifying how your bite may be contributing to pain or wear
  • Sports mouth guards — custom-fitted protective guards for athletes of all ages
