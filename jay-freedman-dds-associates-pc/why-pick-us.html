Frequently Asked Questions

Why Is the “Assistant” Working on My Tooth?

Dr. Freedman’s dental assistants, Alyssa and Kathy, are expanded function dental assistants (EFDAs). These highly skilled professionals have completed a two-year college program and passed the Pennsylvania state licensing exam. Comparable to registered nurses in healthcare, EFDAs are trained to provide expert care in dental procedures.

How does dual insurance work?

Coleen, the practice’s insurance specialist, can analyze your benefit packages using online tools to provide an educated estimate of your coverage. It’s important to note that dental insurance plans vary greatly and are tailored by employers, resulting in thousands of unique benefit structures.

How soon can I get an appointment?

For emergencies, same-day appointments are available. The practice also offers a variety of appointment times to accommodate non-emergency patient needs.

Yes, the practice accepts Discover, Visa, MasterCard, and American Express for your convenience.

What if I can’t afford dental care and don’t have insurance?
