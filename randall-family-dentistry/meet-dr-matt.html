Meet Dr. Matt

Patients do not have to be afraid to visit their dentist. They should also have access to their healthcare provider. I work alongside them with their total health in mind. We are a team.”

With over 25 years of experience, Dr. Matt Randall has dedicated his career to providing compassionate, comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages. A proud graduate of the University of Mississippi, he leads Randall Family Dentistry in Prairieville, Louisiana, where his warm, family-focused approach is at the heart of the practice.

Trained in a rural setting, Dr. Matt developed the ability to perform a wide variety of treatments under one roof. Performing services including implants, wisdom teeth removal, root canals, and more, often eliminates the need for outside referrals. With advanced technology and options like conscious sedation, his office provides care that is modern, efficient, and comfortable.

Beyond procedures, Dr. Matt is known for helping patients overcome dental anxiety. By listening carefully and building trust, he has creates a welcoming environment where patients feel at ease and confident in their care.

Originally on the path to medicine, Dr. Matt discovered that dentistry allowed him not just to manage symptoms but to truly fix problems. As he puts it: “I found my passion, I like to work, I like what I do and I like caring for others.”

You don’t have to be afraid to visit the dentist. Dr. Matt believes in providing comfortable, personalized care in a familiar environment—without the need to see a new provider for every service. With advanced training in a variety of specialty procedures, Dr. Matt can perform most treatments right here in our office, ensuring convenience and continuity of care. Every aspect of his approach is centered on your comfort and confidence in the dental chair.
