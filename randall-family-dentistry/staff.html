We love what we do, and we love who we do it for — you! From gentle care to genuine connections, we’re here to make every visit something to smile about. Come meet the people who make our dental family so special!

Kalynn graduated from the LSU School of Dentistry in 2003 and has been a part of Dr. Matt’s team since 2014. Bringing over 22 years of experience as a Registered Dental Hygienist, she has spent her career making a positive difference in her patients’ lives.

Born and raised in Ascension Parish, Kalynn loves caring for the same community she calls home. She’s known for her calming, reassuring approach and takes pride in helping patients feel comfortable during their visits. Dentistry isn’t just a profession for Kalynn, it’s a calling rooted in faith, compassion, and connection.

Outside the office, Kalynn enjoys watching baseball and football, camping, and cheering on her son, Bryant, in his many sports. She and her husband, Justin, have been married since 2012 and love the life they’ve built together.

A fun fact? Kalynn has dyslexia and proudly credits it for giving her the determination to work hard and never give up on her goals. She lives by the words of Saint Teresa of Ávila: “In this life we cannot do great things. We can only do small things with great love.”

Heather became part of our team in 2021, bringing over 20 years of experience in the dental field and a passion for helping patients feel comfortable and cared for. She loves working in a family oriented environment where both patients and team members feel at home.

As the practice’s Insurance Coordinator, Heather takes pride in helping patients understand their insurance benefits and treatment options with ease. Her friendly, approachable nature and attention to detail make her a trusted resource for both patients and coworkers alike.

Originally from Greenville, South Carolina, Heather has called Louisiana home for the past 13 years. Outside the office, she enjoys spending time with family and friends. She and her husband have been married for 24 years and have a teenage daughter who keeps them busy.

A fun fact? Heather is a proud Clemson Tiger and Buffalo Bills fan!
