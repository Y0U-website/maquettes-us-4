Services

At Randall Family Dentistry, we offer a wide range of dental services to meet the needs of every member of the family. From preventive cleanings and exams to restorative treatments, cosmetic dentistry, and more, we’re here to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile at every stage of life.

Preventive dental care focuses on keeping your teeth and gums healthy by stopping problems before they start. We help protect your smile and catch small issues early through routine exams, professional cleanings, and treatments like fluoride and sealants, saving you time, discomfort, and money down the road. Our goal is to help you maintain a strong, healthy smile for life.

Restorative dental care focuses on repairing and rebuilding teeth to restore their strength, function and natural beauty. Whether you are dealing with a cavity, cracked tooth, missing teeth, or more complex dental needs, our team provides customized solutions to bring your smile back to health.

Cosmetic dentistry is all about enhancing the appearance of your smile so you can feel more confident. Whether you are looking to whiten stained teeth, fix chips or gaps, or completely transform your smile, we provide personalized treatments that are tailored to your goals.

Surgical dentistry involves procedures that go beyond routine treatment to address issues like impacted teeth, bone loss or advanced infections. From simple tooth extractions to wisdom teeth removal, bone grafting, and dental implant placement, we are here to ensure your procedure is performed safely and comfortably.

Our emergency dental services are designed to provide quick, effective relief when unexpected dental issues arise. Our team prioritizes same day appointments whenever possible to relieve pain, prevent further damage, and restore your smile. Whether you’re dealing with a severe toothache, broken or knocked out tooth, infection, or any other urgent concern, we are ready to help.

Our happy visits are specially designed to make your child’s very first dental appointment a positive, comfortable, and fun experience. We understand that visiting the dentist for the first time can feel overwhelming, so our caring team focuses on gentle, patient centered care that helps build trust and confidence early on.

SureSmile® is an advanced orthodontic treatment that uses cutting edge technology to create custom clear aligners for a precise, comfortable fit. These nearly invisible aligners gradually move your teeth into the desired position, offering a convenient and discreet way to achieve a straighter, healthier smile.

Our ZOOM! in office whitening treatment is a fast, safe, and effective way to brighten your smile by several shades in just one visit. Using professional grade whitening gel and a special light activated system, ZOOM! removes deep stains and discoloration, leaving you with a noticeably whiter smile.

Solea® is an advanced, all-tissue laser that makes dental visits faster, quieter and more comfortable. Many procedures can be completed with little to no anesthesia, reducing stress and improving the overall patient experience. Safe, gentle and precise, Solea® is a great option for patients of all ages.

Sedation is a safe and effective option we offer to help patients feel relaxed and comfortable during dental procedures. Whether you experience anxiety, have a low pain threshold, or need extensive treatment, sedation can make your visit stress free and smooth.
