Meet your Meridian dentist

Dr. Greg Steiner

Dr. Greg Steiner has been proudly serving the Meridian community since 2001, after purchasing the practice from one of the area’s original dentists. With over 25 years of experience and an unwavering commitment to staying ahead of the curve, Dr. Steiner brings an exceptional level of expertise and innovation to every appointment. Whether he’s restoring smiles with same-day crowns or placing dental implants with precision guidance, Dr. Steiner is passionate about making dentistry better — safer, faster, and more comfortable for his patients.

Dr. Steiner’s educational journey began at Ricks College, where he earned his associate’s degree in 1991. He then obtained his bachelor’s in sociology from Utah State University in 1994, before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri – Kansas City in 1999. Immediately drawn to private practice, he opened his Meridian office just a few years later and has never looked back.

A true tech enthusiast, Dr. Steiner introduced same-day crowns using CEREC technology in 2010, added CBCT imaging in 2012, and now utilizes SprintRay 3D printing to further streamline and enhance patient treatment. He also incorporates PRF/PRP therapy during implant procedures to accelerate healing and optimize results — techniques that truly set his practice apart.

The heart of his dental philosophy is simple: treat people with fairness, compassion, and honesty. Dr. Steiner’s patients aren’t just clients — they’re part of his dental family. He wakes up every day excited to come to work, serve his community, and help people achieve healthier, more confident smiles.

When he’s not in the office, you’ll find Dr. Steiner traveling, riding dirt bikes, playing golf, or spending time with his family. You might even see him at an airport around the world; don&#x27;t hesitate to say hi! His mission is to do everything he can to make every treatment efficient, effective, and as comfortable as possible.

Advanced technology and modern comforts to keep you smiling

Our office is designed with your comfort and health in mind. From industry-leading technology to small touches that help you feel at ease, we’re committed to making every visit efficient, safe, and relaxing. Because your time and wellbeing matter.

CEREC same-day crowns

No more waiting weeks for a permanent crown. With CEREC technology, we design, create, and place custom ceramic crowns in a single visit — so you can get back to your life with a stronger smile, faster.

CBCT imaging

Our in-office CBCT (cone beam computed tomography) gives us a 3D view of your teeth and bone structure for accurate diagnostics and treatment planning — especially for complex cases like dental implants.

Sprintray 3D printing

We use advanced 3D printing to create in-house dental models, surgical guides, and more — improving both speed and precision in your care.

PRF/PRP therapy

Platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy promotes healing after implant placements and surgeries by using your body’s natural healing elements. This innovative technique reduces discomfort and speeds up recovery.

Patient amenities

Enjoy TVs in operatories, streaming options like Netflix and Hulu, a welcoming beverage station, and a clean, modern space designed for comfort. We’ve created a space where patients of all ages can feel relaxed and at ease.

Sedation

We offer in-office sedation to help patients feel calm and comfortable during treatment. A licensed sedation professional comes to our office to safely administer sedation, making your visit as stress-free as possible.
