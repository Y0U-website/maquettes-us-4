Our Difference

The All-on-4 Treatment Process Consultation & Digital Planning Your All-on-4 journey starts with a personalized consultation. Your dentist will take a 3D CBCT scan to assess bone structure, review your medical and dental history, and walk you through your options. Using digital planning tools, a fully guided treatment plan is created—mapping out implant placement, prosthetic fit, and anesthesia preferences. You’ll receive a clear cost breakdown and timeline so you can move forward with confidence.

Implant Placement & Temporary Prosthetic

On surgery day, four titanium implants are placed into the jaw—two at the front and two angled toward the back to maximize contact with bone. This strategic placement often eliminates the need for bone grafts. Once the implants are secured, a custom-made, fixed temporary bridge is attached before you leave. Sedation or general anesthesia is provided for comfort, and the procedure typically takes a few hours. By the end of the visit, you’ll have a new smile that looks and feels incredible.

Healing, Monitoring & Final Prosthetic

Over the next 3–6 months, your implants will fuse to the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. During this healing phase, you’ll return for check-ins so your team can monitor your progress. Once integration is confirmed, a final custom prosthetic—crafted from high-quality zirconia or acrylic—is placed. Your final teeth will be color-matched, contoured, and designed for long-term durability and natural appearance.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is All-on-4 really done in one day?

Yes, in most cases! The surgical placement of the implants and the attachment of a custom temporary prosthetic are completed in a single visit. While the final prosthetic is placed after healing, you’ll never be without a secure, fixed set of teeth during the process. This immediate function is one of the key benefits of the All-on-4 approach.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With proper care, the implants themselves can last a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth, which endure daily wear and tear, may eventually need replacement—typically after 10–15 years, depending on the material and your oral hygiene habits. Routine checkups and cleanings will help protect your long-term investment.

Is the All-on-4 procedure painful?

Most patients are surprised at how comfortable the procedure is. Sedation or general anesthesia is provided during surgery, so you won’t feel any discomfort during the procedure. Afterward, mild swelling or soreness is common for a few days and can be managed with medication and rest. The majority of patients return to light activity within a few days.

What’s the difference between All-on-4 and other implant options?

Traditional full-mouth dental implants often require six to eight implants per arch and may involve multiple surgeries, bone grafting, and longer recovery times. All-on-4 uses fewer implants, typically avoids grafting, and provides a new smile immediately. It’s a streamlined, efficient solution designed to reduce cost, complexity, and downtime—without sacrificing quality or function.
