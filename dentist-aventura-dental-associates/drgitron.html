Dr. Gitron

Dr. Gitron was born in Argentina and in 1999 graduated from the National University of Rosario. After finishing her AEGD (Advanced Education of general Dentistry) at Argentinian Dental Association, she moved to Israel and became licensed to practice Dentistry in that country.

After practicing in Israel, she decided to attend an Advanced Endodontic program at Columbia University in New York, where she became proficient in root canal treatments. She graduated from University of Pennsylvania School of dentistry in 2008 with a Degree as a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD).

After practicing in Philadelphia for three years, she moved with her husband and two kids to Sunny Isles Beach, Florida.

Dr. Gitron’s goal is to provide comprehensive care to her patients and as such continues to strengthen her skills yearly with continuing education.

Dr. Gitron considers whole body dentistry vital to the pursuit of overall health and often sees fit to discuss the well being of her patients with their other health care providers such as dental specialists. She prides herself on patient education and making a positive and lasting impression on her patients.
