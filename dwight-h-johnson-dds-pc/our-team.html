0 Our Team Dr. Dwight Johnson, D.D.S. Dr. Johnson graduated in 1973 from The University of Missouri Dental School. He was president of the junior class of 168 students and graduated six months early in the top 10 %. He served 2 years active duty in the United States Air Force before entering private practice in Jackson, Missouri. He has served the community in family practice and has been on numerous Boards in southeast Missouri. He and his wife, Kathy were instrumental in developing the Jackson Civic Center. Jackson is a wonderful place to raise a family.

He is a Lifetime Member of the Missouri Dental Association, the American Dental Association, and a Member of the Southeast Missouri Dental Association. He has amassed more than a 1500 hrs. of Continuing Education credits in his career.

Dr. Johnson enjoys all areas of family practice: Restorative, Endodontics, Oral Surgery, and most important Preventative Dentistry. He and his staff are dedicated to a safe and comfortable environment for treatment.
