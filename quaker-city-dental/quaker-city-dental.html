High-Quality Dental Care That Lasts

At Quaker City Dental, we believe that high-quality dental care should be accessible. That’s why we offer flexible financing options and an in-house membership plan and are in-network with nearly 20 major insurance providers. Our commitment to transparent pricing means you’ll always know the cost of your treatment upfront—no hidden fees, just exceptional care.

Accepted Dental Insurance & Payment Options

Dental Insurance

In order to make quality dental care more accessible for our patients, we are in-network with nearly 20 dental insurance providers, including Aetna, Ameritas, Sun Life, MetLife, Principal, GEHA and United Concordia Elite.

In-House Membership Plan

No insurance? The Noble Dental Group in-office membership plan can help. For a yearly fee, you’ll receive access to complimentary and discounted dental services with no deductible, waiting period or annual maximum.

External Payment Options

We offer flexible financing options through CareCredit ® , making it easy to apply for and choose a payment plan that fits your budget. Our goal is to ensure that high-quality dental care remains accessible.

Insurance Providers

We provide high-quality care while helping you manage treatment costs. Our Noble Dental and Quaker City Dental locations are in-network with over 20 insurance providers, and we submit claims for out-of-network patients at all of our locations. We accept all PPO dental insurance plans, and our financial team is happy to help you navigate your coverage and make the most of your benefits. Contact your insurance provider for coverage details, and reach out to us for assistance in maximizing your benefits.
