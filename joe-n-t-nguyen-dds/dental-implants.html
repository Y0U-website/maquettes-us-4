TYPES OF DENTAL IMPLANTS

A SINGLE CROWN OR FIXED BRIDGE

The dental implant is placed where the missing tooth was lost. When the implant becomes stable, an abutment is connected to it to secure a dental crown. The tooth-colored ceramic crown then replicates the missing tooth.

If more than one implant is placed, then a bridge can be attached to replace multiple teeth.

IMPLANT OVERDENTURES AND PARTIAL DENTURES

Dentures can be secured into place with dental implants. Specialized clips are used to engage the implants and provide retention. Sometimes two implants are all it takes to eliminate the need for messy denture adhesives.

BAR OVERDENTURES

To increase stability and retention, a specialized custom milled titanium bar is attached to the implants. This bar allows the implants to share the forces of chewing. The denture is then clipped onto the bar.

FIXED PROSTHESIS (TEETH IN A DAY OR ALL-ON-4)

Don’t want your denture to fall out accidentally? An implant-retained fixed prosthesis is a perfect way to replace multiple missing teeth. Four or more implants are placed, and your denture or prosthesis is then screwed onto the implants. The teeth are then brushed and flossed in your mouth; they don’t come out.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are dental implants, and how do they replace missing teeth? A dental implant is a restorative treatment made of three parts: a small titanium post that replaces the tooth root, an abutment that connects the post to the restoration, and a crown or other restoration that looks and functions like a natural tooth. Together, they create a life-like replacement that blends with your smile and restores your bite.
  • Why are dental implants such a popular tooth replacement option in Poway? Dental implants are the only option that replaces the entire tooth structure—from root to crown. They restore biting strength, allow you to eat comfortably, help prevent bone loss and facial collapse, and are designed to look completely natural. With proper care, implants can last for decades, making them a long-term, cost-effective solution.
  • How do dental implants help prevent premature aging and bone loss? When a tooth is missing, the jawbone in that area no longer receives stimulation from chewing and begins to shrink over time, which can lead to a “sunken” or aged facial appearance. An implant post acts like an artificial tooth root, stimulating the bone and helping maintain facial structure and bone density.
  • How are dental implants different from bridges or dentures? Unlike a dental bridge, an implant doesn’t require grinding down healthy neighboring teeth for support. Unlike a removable denture, implants don’t slip, click, or move while you talk and eat. They’re fixed in place, feel more like your natural teeth, and are cared for with regular brushing, flossing, and dental visits.
