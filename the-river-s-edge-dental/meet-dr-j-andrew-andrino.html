Meet Dr. J. Andrew Andrino

Meet Dr. J. Andrew Andrino Contact Us Meet Dr. J. Andrew Andrino Contact Us Dr. J. Andrew Andrino DDS Contact Us Born and raised in Arizona, Dr. Andrino is proud to serve the community he calls home. He earned his bachelor’s degree in Health Sciences from Arizona State University, graduating with honors, before going on to complete his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the prestigious University of Washington School of Dentistry.

Dr. Andrino was inspired to pursue dentistry after witnessing firsthand how a restored smile can transform lives. He is especially passionate about emergency dentistry, finding great fulfillment in helping patients out of pain and restoring their comfort and confidence.

Outside the office, Dr. Andrino enjoys spending time with his wife and children, cheering them on at their soccer games. A true outdoorsman, he loves fishing, hunting, and exploring Arizona’s beautiful landscapes.

Fluent in Portuguese and Spanish, Dr. Andrino also spent two years serving the people of Brazil on a church mission — an experience that deepened his compassion and appreciation for diverse cultures. He’s an avid fan of all Arizona sports teams and takes pride in supporting his local community both inside and outside the office.
