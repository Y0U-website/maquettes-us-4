What is the process like?

We ensure every phase of your dental implant journey is safe, precise, and entirely transparent.

Consultation

We’ll assess your dental condition and determine the best treatment option. If dental implants are right for you, we’ll explain the process to make sure everything is transparent.

Implant Placement

The implant procedure will be completed carefully, ensuring that the implant is well stabilized in the jawbone, just like the root of the tooth, so that healing can take place properly with an estimated time of up to 6 months.

Final Phase

Once the healing process is over, the restoration will be attached to provide better longevity and functionality of the implant. Restorations are custom-made, and can be either bridges, dentures, or crowns. They provide the support needed to the implants, allowing you to enjoy your everyday functions of eating and speaking as usual.
