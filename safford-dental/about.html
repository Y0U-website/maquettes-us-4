Dr. Susan Safford and Her Team

Dr. Susan Safford and her wonderful team are committed to providing a family-oriented environment full of compassion, and creates a stress-free experience for patients young and old. Dr. Safford is committed to creating life-long relationships with her patients. She believes in total body wellness and the connection between a healthy body and mouth. Patients value her gentle, comprehensive approach and precise care.

Dr. Safford received her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree at Marquette University School of Dentistry and her Bachelor of Science in Cell and Molecular Biology at the University of Hawaii. She is continually inspired by the advancements in dentistry and technology while keeping a minimally invasive approach with all of her patients. Dr. Safford enjoys taking continuing education courses every month to stay on the forefront of the latest advancements in dentistry. Over the years, Dr. Safford volunteers her dental work to causes such as Mission of Mercy and Give Kids a Smile Day.

Dr. Safford loves spending time with her family and enjoys exploring Wisconsin. She enjoys playing tennis, hiking, biking, and paddle boarding up North. During the winters, she has fun with dinner and baking recipes!

Dr. Safford is honored to serve Marshfield and the surrounding community and is excited to bring her passion for dentistry and a desire to continue providing her patients and their families with excellent dental care for many years to come!
