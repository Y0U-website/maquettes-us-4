The practice

Our dental team includes general dentists, a prosthodontist, periodontist, endodontist, oral and maxillofacial surgeon, pediatric dentist, and dental hygienists. We work together to plan care around your oral health, comfort, and treatment goals.

Our general dentists and dental hygienists help keep your teeth and gums healthy through examinations, professional cleanings, fillings, and practical advice for care at home. Our pediatric dentist provides care tailored to children, while patients interested in straightening their teeth can explore Invisalign with our team.

Our prosthodontist, Dr. Olga Malkin, DMD, FICOI , provides advanced restorative and aesthetic care, including full-mouth reconstruction . For patients who need several procedures, our clinicians coordinate treatment to address both the appearance of the smile and the way the teeth function together.

We also offer All-on-4 and Teeth-in-a-Day treatment. Suitable patients may receive a fixed provisional restoration on the day of implant surgery, with further appointments for healing assessment and the final restoration.

Our restorative and surgical clinicians work together to plan implant care around each patient’s needs.

Tribeca Advanced Dentistry has a full dental laboratory within our Tribeca office , where we design and manufacture crowns, veneers, and bridges . Our digital workflow includes milling and 3D printing , with the fabrication method selected for each restoration and material.

Having the laboratory on site allows close collaboration between our clinicians and laboratory team on the fit, shape, shade, and appearance of your restorations. It also supports coordination between treatment planning, fabrication, and adjustments.

Our practice uses 3D dental imaging , digital treatment planning, and guided implant surgery when appropriate for your care.
