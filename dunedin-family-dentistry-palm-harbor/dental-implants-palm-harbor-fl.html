What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a long-term tooth-replacement option that replaces the visible part of the tooth and its root. The implant acts as a sturdy foundation that supports a crown, bridge, or denture. Because implants integrate with the jawbone, they closely match the function of natural teeth.

Why Patients Choose Dental Implants

Missing teeth create gaps that can affect nearby teeth, bite alignment, and jawbone health. Dental implants help restore structure and stability while blending naturally with your smile.
