What Is Orthodontics?

Orthodontic care focuses on correcting tooth and jaw alignment. When teeth are crowded, spaced too far apart, or not properly aligned, they can affect both appearance and day-to-day function. Orthodontic care gently guides teeth into healthier positions over time.

Why Orthodontic Care Matters

Orthodontic concerns are not only cosmetic. Alignment issues can make cleaning more difficult, increase wear on certain teeth, and affect how the bite functions. Addressing these concerns early—or even later in life—can help protect your smile.
