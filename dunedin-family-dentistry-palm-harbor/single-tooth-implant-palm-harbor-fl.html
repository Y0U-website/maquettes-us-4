What Is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single-tooth implant is a dental solution that substitutes the root and crown of a missing tooth. Instead of relying on nearby teeth for support, the implant is placed into the jawbone to act as a secure foundation for a custom-made crown. This approach closely matches the look, feel, and function of a natural tooth.

Why Replace a Single Missing Tooth?

It may be tempting to leave one missing tooth untreated, especially if it’s not easily visible. However, even a single gap can lead to changes over time. If you’re considering a single tooth implant near you, early treatment can help prevent future complications and maintain your oral health.

A single-tooth implant may be suggested to help with the following:

Reducing the chance of surrounding teeth shifting out of place

Keeping your bite balanced and comfortable

Supporting jawbone strength where the tooth is missing

Making chewing and speaking feel more natural

Recreating the natural look of your smile

Replacing a missing tooth early can help avoid more complex issues later.

Who May Be a Good Candidate?

Single tooth implants are typically recommended when gums are healthy, and the jawbone can support the implant. If bone loss is present, additional treatment options may help prepare the area.
