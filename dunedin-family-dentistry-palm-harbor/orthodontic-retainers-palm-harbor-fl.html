What Are Orthodontic Retainers?

Orthodontic retainers are custom-made dental appliances designed to help hold teeth in their corrected positions after braces or aligners. Teeth naturally tend to shift, especially after orthodontic treatment is complete. Retainers provide gentle support that helps maintain alignment and protect the progress you have achieved.

Why Retainers Matter After Orthodontic Treatment

Even after teeth are straightened, the surrounding bone and tissues need time to stabilise. Without retention, teeth can gradually move out of place.

Orthodontic retainers may be recommended to help:

Protect the time and effort invested in treatment

Wearing a retainer as advised helps preserve results and supports ongoing oral health.

How Often Should Retainers Be Worn?

Retainer wear schedules can differ from person to person. Some patients are asked to wear retainers full-time at first, while others may transition to nighttime use over time. The schedule depends on how your teeth respond and your risk of shifting.

Orthodontic retainers near you at Dunedin Family Dentistry are most effective when worn consistently and handled with care. Skipping wear or misplacing the retainer can increase the chances of teeth moving out of alignment.
