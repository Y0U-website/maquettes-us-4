What Are All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 implants are a full-arch tooth-replacement solution for patients who are missing many or all teeth in the upper or lower jaw. Instead of using an implant for each tooth, this method uses four implants placed at precise angles to support a full set of secure, fixed teeth.

This technique is carefully planned to maximize stability while often reducing the need for bone grafting. The result is a full smile that looks natural and functions like real teeth.

How the All-on-4 Approach Works

What makes the All-on-4 treatment effective is the way each implant is planned and placed. The process is designed to support function and confidence:

A detailed consultation and digital imaging

Strategic placement of four dental implants

Attachment of a custom full-arch prosthetic

A secure and non-removable full-arch solution

Because of the angled placement of the rear implants, many patients can qualify for this treatment even if they have experienced bone loss.
