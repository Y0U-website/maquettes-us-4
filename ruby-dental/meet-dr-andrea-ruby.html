Dr. Andrea Ruby

Giving Exceptional Dental Care to Her Community

Dr. Andrea Ruby, a Florida native, earned her degree in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology and Anthropology from Yale University .

After college, she conducted research at Brigham and Women’s Hospital in Boston, during which time she enrolled in a post-baccalaureate program at Tufts University .

She continued on to the Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where she received the class award for geriatric dentistry.

Dr. Ruby has worked in low-income clinics and private practices throughout Minnesota, while teaching at the University of Minnesota Dental School and Herzing University.

Dr. Ruby then purchased the practice in 2017, and is excited to treat her neighbors with high standards and compassion.
