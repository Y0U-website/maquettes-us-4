Meet Our Wonderful Team

Our wonderful team works hard to keep our office running smoothly. Meet and get to know them below!

Kathy

Financial and Treatment Coordinator

Kathy, with over 25 years of experience, is the Financial and Treatment Coordinator at Wright Dental Studio. Her career began as a Dental Assistant, and she earned top honors from the Illinois School of Health Careers. Kathy excels in managing scheduling, insurance, and staying abreast of the latest dental advancements through ongoing professional development. Her warm smile and positivity create a welcoming and reassuring atmosphere for our patients.

Outside of work, Kathy cherishes time with her husband Ron and their four Dobermans—Shira, Roxy, Sierra, and Sedonna. They love exploring new destinations in their RV, indulging in diverse cuisines, and making lasting memories. Kathy’s commitment to excellence ensures that every patient feels well-cared-for and valued, contributing to a supportive and enriching experience both in and out of the office.

Bethany

LEAD DENTAL ASSISTANT

Bethany is the cornerstone of Wright Dental Studio as our Lead Dental Assistant. Her dedication to patient care is reflected in her passion for creating genuine connections, ensuring each patient feels valued and at ease. Certified in radiology and coronal polishing, she excels as our in-office digital crown specialist and is always ready to assist with any other office needs, consistently setting a high standard for patient satisfaction.

Bethany takes great joy in transforming smiles, viewing dentistry as a rewarding journey for both herself and her patients. Outside of work, she enjoys family time with her husband and son, Tripp, enjoying poolside relaxation and competitive cornhole games. Her vibrant spirit and all-around support enhance our practice, making each visit a positive and enriching experience.

Charlene

Dental Assistant

Charlene, affectionately known as Char, brings a decade of dedicated experience in dentistry to our practice. Originally from New Mexico, she earned her Associates in Applied Science and certifications in Dental Assisting in Clarksville, TN, in 2012. Char’s commitment to patient care is evident in her genuine passion for ensuring comfort and satisfaction, whether through gentle procedures or meaningful connections with patients.

Char’s dedication extends beyond the office, where she enjoys exploring new destinations with her husband Miles and their four children. Their family adventures include trying diverse cuisines and connecting with people from various backgrounds, creating cherished memories together. Char’s unwavering dedication, vibrant spirit, and love for exploration enrich both our team and the lives of our patients, making every visit a rewarding experience.

Blair

LEAD DENTAL HYGIENIST

Blair, from Owensboro, Kentucky, completed her bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene at Western Kentucky University in 2022. Since joining Dr. Wright’s team, she has become a key part of the practice, bringing both her expertise and enthusiasm for patient care.

As a dental hygienist, Blair excels at building trust and comfort with her patients, ensuring each visit is positive and personalized. Her dedication extends beyond routine care, focusing on fostering meaningful connections with every patient.

Outside of work, Blair enjoys life with her boyfriend Alex and their three cats—Nala, Oliver, and Toulouse. They embrace adventure by exploring new destinations, savoring diverse cuisines, and attending live concerts. Blair’s vibrant personality and commitment to her patients enrich our practice and contribute to a warm, engaging experience for everyone she interacts with.

Haley

Dental Assistant

Haley is a dedicated Expanded Duties Dental Assistant (EDDA) with more than 8 years of experience in dental care. She earned her certification from the prestigious University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 2017, refining her expertise in patient care and procedural assistance. Haley is deeply committed to creating a soothing and supportive atmosphere for her patients, ensuring they feel at ease during their dental visits. Her passion lies in the transformative impact of enhancing smiles, contributing to each person’s journey to renewed confidence.

Outside of work, Haley loves spending time outdoors, diving into a good book, and making memories with her loved ones including her Labradoddle, Renley.
