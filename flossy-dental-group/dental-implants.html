Caring For Your Single Dental Implant

Caring for a dental implant is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups are essential to maintain the implant and surrounding oral health. Our team provides detailed guidance on how to care for your new implant to ensure its longevity and success.

Implant-Supported Bridge

An implant-supported bridge uses dental implants as foundations for multiple adjacent missing teeth, offering enhanced stability and longevity compared to traditional bridges.

Implant-Supported Dentures

This innovative solution provides unparalleled stability by anchoring them to multiple implants. It eliminates common issues with traditional dentures, such as slippage and discomfort, restoring functionality and confidence.

All-On-4® Dental Implants

All-On-4 dental implants are a revolutionary approach for those who require full-arch restoration. This technique involves strategically placing four implants in the jawbone to support an entire arch of teeth. It’s an efficient, less invasive solution that provides immediate improvement in function and appearance, even for patients with minimal bone density.

The Benefits Of All-On-4®

  • Rapid results: One of the most significant advantages of the All-On-4 system is the immediacy of results. Patients can often leave our office with a fully functional set of temporary teeth the same day as their surgery.
  • Less invasive: Strategically placing only four implants is less invasive than traditional methods requiring more implants.
  • Bone grafting is often unnecessary: The All-On-4 technique can often be performed without bone grafts, even in patients with minimal bone density, due to the unique angle and placement of the implants.
  • Long-term durability: All-On-4 implants are designed for longevity, offering a permanent solution to tooth loss.
  • Improved oral health: Beyond aesthetics, this system helps preserve jawbone health and prevents the deterioration that often accompanies tooth loss.

The All-On-4® Process

  • Consultation and planning: The journey begins with a comprehensive consultation to assess your suitability for the procedure. We utilize advanced imaging techniques to meticulously plan the implant placement.
  • Surgical procedure: During the surgery, four dental implants are precisely placed in the jawbone. These implants serve as anchors for a full arch of prosthetic teeth.
  • Immediate temporary teeth: After the implants are placed, a set of temporary teeth is attached, offering immediate functionality and aesthetics while your mouth heals and the implants integrate with your jawbone.
  • Recovery and follow-up: We provide detailed care instructions and schedule follow-up appointments to ensure a smooth recovery and successful integration of the implants.
  • Final prosthesis: Once healing is complete and the implants have fully integrated, a final, custom-made prosthesis is securely attached to the implants, completing your smile transformation.

All-On-4® vs. Traditional Dentures

  • Stability and comfort: Unlike traditional dentures, which can slip and cause discomfort, All-On-4 implants are securely anchored in the jawbone. They offer unparalleled stability and eliminate the need for adhesives.
  • Jawbone health: All-On-4 implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss typically associated with dentures.
  • Maintenance and care: Caring for All-On-4 implants is similar to caring for natural teeth, involving regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups, whereas dentures often require more specialized maintenance.
  • Dietary freedom: With All-On-4 implants, patients can enjoy a wider range of foods, including those difficult to eat with traditional dentures.
