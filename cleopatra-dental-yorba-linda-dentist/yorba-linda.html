Do you offer same-day emergency dental appointments in Yorba Linda?

Yes. We reserve daily appointment slots specifically for emergency patients experiencing severe tooth pain, broken crowns, or dental trauma. Walk-ins are welcome, or call (714) 777-5541 for immediate assistance.

What should I do if I have a severe toothache or a knocked-out tooth?

Rinse with warm water, avoid chewing on that side, and never apply aspirin directly to gum tissue. If a tooth is knocked out, keep it moist in milk and contact our Yorba Linda clinic immediately—prompt care within 60 minutes dramatically improves tooth survival.

What is included in a routine dental cleaning and exam?

A comprehensive hygiene visit includes ultrasonic tartar removal, gentle plaque scaling, enamel polishing, periodontal gum health screening, and a thorough oral wellness exam with Dr. George Mirsal.

How safe are your digital dental X-rays?

Our modern digital radiography sensors emit up to 80 % less radiation than legacy dental film, providing instant, high-resolution diagnostic imaging while ensuring safety for the entire family.

Who is a candidate for dental implants in Yorba Linda?

Most adults in good general health with missing teeth are great candidates. During your initial consultation, we perform a 3D digital CBCT scan to evaluate jawbone density and tailor a single-tooth implant or All-on-4 treatment plan.

How long do dental implants last, and what is the recovery process?

Dental implants fuse biologically with your jawbone through osseointegration. With proper daily brushing, flossing, and regular cleanings, implants function just like natural teeth and can last for decades.

Which dental insurance plans and financing options do you accept?

We are in-network with major PPO insurance providers (Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Guardian, Aetna). For out-of-pocket costs, we offer flexible monthly payment options through CareCredit with 0 % promotional financing.
