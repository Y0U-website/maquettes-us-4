What to Expect

When you first walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted by our comfortable environment and our friendly office staff. You’ll be invited to sit down and relax as you fill out any new patient forms prior to your appointment. Once those are complete and your doctor is ready for you, we’ll invite you back into one of our operatories.

You’ll receive a cleaning, comprehensive exam, x-rays, and an intraoral scan, and Dr. Gill will sit down with you to discuss the results. If everything looks good, you’ll be able to schedule your next appointment in the front office. If anything needs extra attention, Dr. Gill will explain the results and take time to address any questions or concerns you might have, and she will describe any further treatment recommendations.
