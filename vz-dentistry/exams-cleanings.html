What Happens During an Exam and Cleaning?

Your exam begins with a thorough assessment of your oral health. Dr. VanZanten uses modern diagnostic tools to get a clear picture of your dental needs. This may include digital X-rays to view structures not visible to the naked eye.

  • Removal of Plaque and Tartar: Using special tools like a scaler, the dental hygienist will gently remove plaque and tartar build-up from your teeth surfaces and below the gum line.
  • Polishing: After tartar removal, your teeth are polished to remove surface stains and smooth the enamel, which helps prevent further plaque build-up.
  • Flossing and Guidance: The visit often ends with professional flossing and advice on how to maintain oral hygiene effectively between visits.

Preventing Gum Disease

Gum disease affects nearly half of the adult population and can progress without obvious symptoms. Regular cleanings are critical as they help prevent the condition by removing the plaque that causes gum inflammation. Early detection and treatment are vital in preventing the progression of gum disease, which can lead to more severe health issues, including tooth loss and cardiovascular problems.
