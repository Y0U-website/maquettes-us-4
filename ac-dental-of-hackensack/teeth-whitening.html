What to expect from Opalescence Boost whitening at AC Dental in Paramus, NJ.

AC Dental’s in-office Opalescence Boost whitening is priced per session at your consultation, and most patients complete treatment in a single visit. We accept most major dental insurance and offer 0 % APR financing through CareCredit call (201) 489-1084 to confirm your coverage before your visit.

AC Dental uses the Opalescence Boost system, a chemically-activated, dentist-supervised in-office treatment that doesn’t require a curing light. It’s applied chairside at our Paramus office and delivers noticeably whiter teeth in under an hour.

Opalescence Boost is applied and monitored by our dental team throughout the appointment. Most patients experience little to no sensitivity, and any that occurs typically fades within a day. Let Dr. Ghattas know beforehand if you have a history of tooth sensitivity so we can adjust the approach.

Results from in-office whitening typically last 6 12 months, depending on diet and oral hygiene habits. Avoiding coffee, tea, red wine, and tobacco, and keeping up with regular cleanings, helps results last longer. Touch-up treatments are available at AC Dental.
