A single dental implant in New Jersey typically costs between $3,800–$5,500, including the implant post and crown. The final cost depends on your bone health, the number of implants needed, and whether preparatory work (like bone grafting) is required. We offer flexible financing to make treatment accessible.

Most patients are pleasantly surprised — the procedure is performed under local anesthesia and is far less uncomfortable than they expected. Post-surgery soreness is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief for a few days. Dr. Ghattas takes every step to ensure your comfort.

With proper care, the titanium implant post can last a lifetime — it fuses permanently with your jawbone. The porcelain crown on top typically lasts 15–25 years before it may need replacement. Dental implants are the most durable long-term tooth replacement solution available.

The complete process typically takes 3–9 months. After placing the implant post, your jawbone needs 3–6 months to fuse with the titanium (osseointegration). The permanent crown is then attached. Dr. Ghattas will give you a clear, personalized timeline at your first visit.

Coverage varies by plan, but many insurance policies cover portions of the procedure — such as the extraction or the crown. We’ll review your benefits before treatment begins so you know exactly what to expect. We also offer financing options to cover any out-of-pocket costs.
