Single Tooth Implants

For those who are missing a single tooth, our single tooth implants provide a reliable and natural-looking solution. This procedure involves placing a titanium post in the jawbone, which serves as a sturdy foundation for a custom-made crown. Not only does this restore the appearance of your smile, but it also prevents bone loss and maintains the integrity of your surrounding teeth. Enjoy the freedom to eat and speak without worry, all while feeling confident about your smile.

Implant-Supported Bridges

If you have multiple missing teeth, implant-supported bridges can restore your smile effectively and comfortably. This option involves placing dental implants on either side of the gap, supporting a bridge that replaces two or more missing teeth. Implant-supported bridges offer enhanced stability compared to traditional bridges and help maintain the health of your jawbone. This solution provides the aesthetic appeal of natural teeth while ensuring you can enjoy your favorite foods without concern.

Full-Arch Dentistry

For patients with significant tooth loss, our full-arch dentistry option can completely restore your smile and oral function. This innovative approach involves placing a minimal number of dental implants to support a full arch of prosthetic teeth. The result is a secure, beautiful smile that feels natural and functions like your own teeth. Full-arch dentistry not only enhances your confidence but also allows for improved chewing ability and overall quality of life.

Dental Implant Restorations

If you already have dental implants that need restoration, our team at St. Johns Smile Co. is here to help. Whether it’s replacing a damaged crown or repairing an implant that has become loose, we offer tailored solutions to restore the integrity of your existing implants. Our focus is on providing effective restorations that enhance your oral health and maintain the functionality of your dental implants, ensuring you continue to enjoy a healthy smile.
