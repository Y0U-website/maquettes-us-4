Implant-retained Dentures

  • Enhanced chewing and speaking ability . Due to the improved stability of implant-supported dentures, patients can enjoy a larger selection of foods without fearing the dentures slipping out of place or falling out. This also allows for better speech, as the dentures are less likely to move or click while talking.
  • Preservation of jawbone health . Dental implants function almost as artificial tooth roots that stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur with conventional dentures. This maintains a more youthful facial structure and prevents changes in speech or eating habits due to bone loss.
  • Long-lasting results . With proper care, implant-retained dentures can last many years, providing patients with a long-term tooth replacement solution requiring minimal maintenance.
