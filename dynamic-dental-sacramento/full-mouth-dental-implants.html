Full Mouth Dental Implants

  • Improved oral health . Dental implants preserve the integrity and structure of the jawbone by stimulating natural bone growth and preventing bone loss, which can occur with lost or extracted teeth. This maintains the mouth’s overall health, including adjacent teeth and surrounding tissues.
  • Enhanced aesthetics . These dental implants are custom-designed to blend in seamlessly with a patient’s natural teeth, providing a natural-looking and beautiful smile. This helps boost self-esteem and confidence and improves facial contours and appearance.
  • Convenience and ease of care . Unlike traditional dentures, dental implants do not require any adhesives or special cleaning routines. They are both brushed and flossed like natural teeth, making them a hassle-free solution for missing teeth.
  • Long-term cost savings . While the initial cost of dental implants is higher than some of our other tooth restoration options, they are a long-term investment in oral health and can last a lifetime with proper care. This can save money on frequent replacements.
  • Versatility . Dental implants are used to replace a single arch or both arches of missing teeth. This allows patients with varying degrees of tooth loss to benefit from this treatment option, making it a versatile solution for many individuals.
