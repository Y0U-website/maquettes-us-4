Doctor ANDREW J. HUXFORD dds

Dr. Huxford believes in providing his patients with the most advanced dental services available today - particularly when it comes to the use of technology in the dental field. Dr. Huxford graduated dental school from the University of Colorado in 2003. Dr. Huxford founded BDG and has been providing the residents Boulder City, Nevada with family-friendly dental services since opening their doors in 2005.

Dental assistant, julie jacobson
