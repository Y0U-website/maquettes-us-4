DENTURES & PARTIALS

If you are missing some or all of your teeth, Boulder Dental Group is the place to go! Dr. Huxford has done thousands of removable restorations to give patients a natural looking smile. A complete denture is a full-mouth appliance that replaces your natural teeth and supports your cheeks and lips, restoring form and function to your smile. Dr. Huxford and his assistants will work with you to achieve comfortable and practical results.

Crowns

Crowns can be done to restore both shape, size, and especially strength of the tooth. A dental crown is a natural, tooth-looking “cap” placed to protect from further damage or decay.

TEETH WHITENING

Professional trays made at the office with an exact fit to your smile and an at home kit is provided to use at your own pace. Teeth bleaching and teeth whitening are fast, easy ways to brighten teeth and enhance your smile.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are changing the way people live! They are designed to provide a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Dental implants allow patients to regain the ability to eat virtually anything, while knowing that teeth appear natural and that facial contours will be preserved. Our patients with dental implants can smile with confidence.
