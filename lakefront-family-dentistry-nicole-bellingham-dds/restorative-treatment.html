Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth colored (or composite) fillings offer a natural looking solution for fixing cavities or chipped teeth.

Crowns and Bridges

A crown (sometimes called a “cap”) can cover and support a tooth with a large broken filling or cavity when there is not enough natural tooth left or the tooth is starting to fracture (cracked tooth). A bridge can be used to replace a missing tooth, protect a weak tooth from breaking, or restore one that is already broken. Crowns can also be used to cover teeth that are discolored and cannot be whitened or are badly shaped.
