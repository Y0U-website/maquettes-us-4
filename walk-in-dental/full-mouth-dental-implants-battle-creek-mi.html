How do full-mouth dental implants work?

Full-mouth dental implants, often referred to as implant-supported or implant-retained dentures, allow patients to enjoy complete teeth replacement of the entire arch of teeth. Full-mouth dental implants can use anywhere from four to six implants along the arch, which hold and stabilize full dentures. This ensures that patients have the ability to enjoy all their favorite foods without needing to feel self-conscious or worried about their appliances slipping or coming loose. The implants provide confidence and functionality.

What can I expect from the process of obtaining complete teeth replacement using implant-retained dentures?

With full-mouth dental implants, Dr. Khalid Sajjad first takes detailed scans and X-rays of the patient’s mouth to decide the best placement for the implants. The implants are then surgically placed into the natural jawbone, where they will fuse with the bone over a period of several months. Once the implants have fully integrated in with the jawbone through this process, known as osseointegration, custom-made dentures are attached to them and affixed in place.

What are the advantages of full-mouth dental implants?

  • Stability.
  • Strength.
  • Durability.
  • Improved Functionality.
  • Natural Appearance.
  • Reduced bone loss in the jaw.
  • Maintenance of natural jaw and chin contours.
