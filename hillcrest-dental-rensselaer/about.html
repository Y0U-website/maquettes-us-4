ABOUT

WE’RE YOUR DENTAL HOME, CLOSE TO HOME

Hillcrest Dental Center was established with family friendliness in mind. Our founding doctor, Raymond G. Klockow, DDS, is a Rensselaer native and sought to create a family-based practice accessible to the communities of Jasper County.

For over 20 years, we have operated with the health and happiness of our patients at the forefront – your smiles make us smile!

Thanks to the commitment of our staff and patients, we have expanded over the years to offer a broader variety of services, support more staff, and incorporate state-of-the-art technology.

We continue to be dedicated towards improving our patients’ dental hygiene, at all stages of life!

GENERAL, COSMETIC AND EMERGENCY DENTIST SERVICES

As Hillcrest continued to grow, 2011 saw yet another addition of a third dentist, Jack Drone, DDS, a local county resident. Drs. Ahler and Drone now direct their efforts and energy toward providing comprehensive, quality care and emergency dentist services in one location close to home. The Hillcrest priority is to provide the highest standard of oral healthcare and beautiful smiles.

MEET OUR DOCTORS

John Ahler, D.D.S

Dr. Ahler graduated from Indiana University (‘97) and is now a general practitioner, traveling to and providing services for 5 regional care centers, 3 County Head Start facilities, Saint Joseph College, and various local schools.

He and the Hillcrest Family Dental Center Team offer a wide range of services from bleaching and orthodontics consultations to soft tissue and oral surgical procedures.

Dr. Ahler graduated from RCHS in 1983 and continues to remain active in the community as a Cub Scout Master, Rotarian, and Knights of Columbus member.

Jack Drone D.D.S

Hillcrest Dental would like to welcome our Dr. Jack W. Drone to our practice! He graduated from the University of Notre Dame with a Bachelor’s Degree in the Biological Sciences, earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Northwestern University, and has been practicing all phases of general dentistry for 13 years.

Dr. Drone and his family are very glad to be a part of Jasper County again, and they look forward to meeting and helping the patients of Hillcrest Family Dental Center.

OUR OFFICE IN THE COMMUNITY

The Rensselaer community means the world to the Hillcrest Family Dental – our patients are our priority, and we love seeing them thrive beyond our office doors. Many members of our team are Rensselaer natives or grew up nearby, and know the value of the people who live here.

The Hillcrest team extends care and compassion to the community as we do our patients. Our employees coach little league teams, support local businesses, and volunteer.

Hillcrest Family Dental is thankful for the people of Rensselaer!
