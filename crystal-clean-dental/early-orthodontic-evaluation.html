Frequently Asked Questions

At what age should my child get an early orthodontic evaluation?

It is highly recommended that children receive their first orthodontic screening by age seven. At this age, they have a mix of primary and permanent teeth, making it the perfect time to spot any developing alignment issues.

Will my child definitely need braces if they get an evaluation?

Not necessarily. An early evaluation simply allows us to monitor their growth and development. If treatment is needed, we can plan the most effective, gentle approach at the ideal time.

What if my child is nervous about the dentist?

It is completely normal for children to feel anxious. We provide a calm, reassuring environment and use gentle care methods to make the visit a positive, stress-free experience. Let us know ahead of time, and we will work at your child’s pace.

Are early orthodontic evaluations affordable?

Yes, we believe in affordable dental excellence. We accept most insurances and offer flexible payment plans to ensure your child gets the preventative care they deserve without breaking your budget.
