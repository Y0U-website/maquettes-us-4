Meet Dr. Abdullahi

Dr. Safiya Abdullahi graduated from the University of Minnesota with a degree in biology and went to the University of Maryland School of Dentistry for her dental education.

In her spare time, she enjoys reading, exploring other cultures, traveling, hiking, running, and spending as much time as possible with her amazing family.

Dr. Abdullahi’s favorite shows to watch include Friends, The Office, anything within the comedy genre, and documentaries. She is a huge football (soccer) fan and enjoys watching the English premier league and Champions’ league. Lately, she has become a fan of Minnesota’s own United and the Vikings.

Volunteer missions: Minnesota mission of mercy, give kids a smile, dental day.
