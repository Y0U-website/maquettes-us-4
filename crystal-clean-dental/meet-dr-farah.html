Meet Dr. Farah

Dr. Muktar Farah completed his undergraduate degree at the University of Minnesota-Twin cities in 2014, going on to obtain his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Minnesota School Of Dentistry.

Dr. Muktar is very passionate about his patients and the quality of his work. He continues to further his education; taking courses on a variety of treatments using a variety of different state of the art techniques to achieve optimal results.

Dr. Muktar understands the value of organized dentistry and is currently a member of serveral dental associations including the Somali Dental Association.

Dr. Muktar is committed to giving back to the community and providing dental care to the underserved. He participates in the biannual SDA Free Dental Care Day event.

In his free time Dr. Muktar enjoys playing and watching soccer. He is a huge Arsenal fan (unfortunately) and admittedly can’t explain why. He loves mixed martial arts and occasionally you will find him sparring at the gym.

Clinical excellence. Genuine compassion. Care for everyone.

At Crystal Clean Dental, Dr. Muktar Farah, DDS, and Dr. Safiya Abdullahi, DDS, proudly provide compassionate, high-quality dental care for families across Crystal, Minnesota. From routine exams and gentle cleanings to precise fillings and comprehensive preventive treatments, we use advanced care to protect and maintain your oral health.
