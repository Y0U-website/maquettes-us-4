About Our Dental Office In Brunswick

At Surfside Kids Pediatric Dentistry, located in Brunswick, GA, we are passionate about using the latest technological advancements to give your child the best possible experience, both in the dental chair as well as in the waiting room. Parents recognize we are different from the moment they enter the office in all aspects of preventive, restorative, and aesthetic dental we offer. You’ll find that our office & our team are specially designed to meet the needs of small children, teens, and even special needs adults.

Smart TVs Placed above the dental chair with Netflix, Hulu, Amazon Prime

Bluetooth Headsets Sizes for young children and older teens to entertain them with their favorite music.

Cavity Free Club If no cavities are found during the first appointment or recall your child is entered into one of our monthly drawings for awesome prizes!
