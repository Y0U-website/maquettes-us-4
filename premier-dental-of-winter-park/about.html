Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Alec Gonzalez

With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Alec Gonzalez is dedicated to delivering high-end dental care with a focus on patient trust and comfort. Originally from South Florida, he discovered his passion for dentistry while pursuing a Master’s in Molecular Biology at Villanova University. Inspired by the blend of art, science, and human connection in dentistry, he went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Temple University. He later completed an IV Sedation Residency at USC.

Dr. Gonzalez’s expertise spans full-mouth rehabilitation, dental implants, Invisalign, and sedation dentistry. His commitment to clear communication and transparency ensures that patients feel informed and confident about their care. Whether performing life-changing smile makeovers or guiding patients through complex treatments with ease, his goal is to deliver precision-driven, high-quality care in a setting that feels welcoming and stress-free.

Beyond the office, Dr. Gonzalez is passionate about staying active—whether it’s CrossFit, golf, pickleball, or skiing in the mountains of Utah, Colorado, and Montana. His dedication to excellence and detail extends beyond sports and into the high-level care he provides every patient.

Your First Visit, Designed for You

Your First Visit

From the moment you step into Premier Dental of Winter Park, you’ll notice the difference. Our office is designed for efficiency, relaxation, and trust-building. We take the time to listen, answer questions, and ensure you feel completely at ease before moving forward with your personalized treatment plan.

Patient Experience Expect a warm welcome, no-rush consultations, and a thorough assessment of your dental health. We focus on clear, transparent communication, so you’ll always understand your options and next steps. If needed, we offer sedation options to make your visit as comfortable as possible.

State-of-the-Art Dentistry in a Comforting Environment

We’ve combined modern technology with a patient-first atmosphere to create a comfortable and efficient dental experience. From cutting-edge diagnostics to relaxing amenities, everything we offer is designed to elevate your care and ensure your time with us is effortless.

CBCT & Digital Scanners

We use low-dose radiation CBCT imaging and digital scanners (iTero & Trios) for precise diagnostics and treatment planning—minimizing guesswork and maximizing results.

Soft Tissue Laser Technology

Our Biolase soft tissue laser allows for minimally invasive procedures, faster healing times, and more comfortable treatments, whether for gum therapy or cosmetic enhancements.

Luxury Comforts for a Relaxing Visit

Enjoy a beverage station, large-screen TVs, noise-canceling headphones, Netflix, and warm blankets to make your visit feel more like a retreat than a dental appointment.

Investing in Our Community, One Smile at a Time

At Premier Dental of Winter Park, we believe in giving back to the community we serve. While we are still developing our outreach programs, we are actively seeking partnerships with local organizations to make high-quality dental care more accessible to those in need. Our goal is to make a lasting impact beyond our office walls—because great smiles should be for everyone.
