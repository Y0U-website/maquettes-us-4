About Us

0 MEET OUR DOCTORS Dr. Jincy Daniel Dr. Jincy Daniel was born and raised in Chicago, Illinois Dr. Daniel has called the sunny state of Florida her home since 1993. She is a graduate of the NSU College of Dental Medicine for dental school, and because of an advanced program, Dr. Daniel was able to complete both her undergraduate and dental education in just 7 years.

Dr. Daniel is known for having a very gentle and calming approach to dentistry because she understands that dental treatment can sometimes be difficult and can cause anxiety. Her goal is to treat you in the way that suits you best by addressing your questions, fears and concerns to work with you to overcome those obstacles.

When she is not working, Dr. Daniel spends time with her husband and three daughters. Dr. Daniel’s husband is the director of a foster care agency in the Tampa Bay area called A Door of Hope. Together, she and her husband have been actively involved in youth ministries in their local church for many years, and enjoy entertaining friends and family whenever they can find time. She loves to cook, read, and believes her ultimate purpose is to serve others, whether it be in the dental office, or within the greater community.

Dr. Joan Gonzalez Blundell

Dr. Joan Gonzalez Blundell was born and raised in Ponce, Puerto Rico. During her second year of Dental School at the University of Puerto Rico, she decided to join the United States Navy, where she completed her Dental Medicine Doctorate. Once her Doctorate was completed, she attended the Advanced Education in General Dentistry Program in Jacksonville, North Carolina, where she met her now husband, Jason Blundell. After completing 9 years in the Navy, Dr. Blundell moved to the Tampa area with her husband and two children.

Dr. Blundell is passionate about providing outstanding dental care for her patients and ensures they understand the treatment proposed and their options for optimal oral health.

When not furthering her education in dentistry and integrative health, Dr. Blundell enjoys seeing her children participate in sports, carpentry, and spending time at the beach.

Dr. Kelly Ackley

Dr. Kelly Ackley was born and raised in the Florida Keys. She graduated Summa Cum Laude from the University of Central Florida and was honored with the prestigious Pegasus Medallion—one of the university’s highest awards—for her outstanding GPA and research achievements. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Florida in 2012 (Go Gators🐊)

Outside of the office, Dr. Ackley enjoys spending quality time with her husband and their three children. As a family, they love to travel, hike, fish, scuba dive, and snow ski—always embracing adventure together.

Giving back has always been close to Dr. Ackley’s heart. She has volunteered multiple times at a free dental clinic in Lebanon and has participated in mission trips to Malawi, Thailand, and the Dominican Republic, offering humanitarian aid. Closer to home, she has served at local health clinics, “free dental day” events and on mobile dental bus helping provide free care to underserved communities.

“Helping people has always been my passion. Doing mission work was a big inspiration for me to pursue a career in dentistry. There’s truly no better feeling than helping someone achieve a confident smile and better oral health. I love building real connections with my patients—understanding their unique needs, concerns, and goals allows me to provide truly personalized care. Patient education is also very important to me. I want every patient to understand not just what I recommend, but why—so they can feel confident and informed every step of the way.”
