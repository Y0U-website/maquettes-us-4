Meet Parkhill

Thank you for allowing me to help you and your loved ones achieve and maintain healthy smiles! – Dr. Irene Parkhill, DDS

Dr. Parkhill is passionate about helping people, from the patients in her chair to her greater community. After graduating from the University of Washington with a Bachelor of Science in Psychology in 2000, this Seattle dentist was drawn to family practice because of the way it blends art, science, healthcare, esthetics and working with people and families of all ages.

Dr. Parkhill graduated from the University of Washington Dental School in 2004 and completed a General Practice Residency at the VA Hospital in Long Beach, California to further her hands-on skills and academic knowledge. Dr. Parkhill regularly takes advantage of numerous continuing education courses so she can provide her patients with the latest in care as well.

I consider it a true privilege to contribute to my patients’ healthcare as their dental provider. – Dr. Parkhill

Dr. Parkhill’s experience is vast, including work at a Community Health Clinic in Kent, WA and serving on a dental mission trip to Nepal. She’s been a practicing Seattle dentist since 2005 and is affiliated with several regional professional study clubs for dentists and specialists.

Our practice is what it is today because of the wonderful people I have the opportunity to work alongside every day. – Dr. Parkhill

Dr. Parkhill loves diversity! In addition to providing high-quality dental care to a wide variety of individuals and families of all ages and backgrounds, she loves learning about different cultures, languages, traditions and cuisine. In her free time she enjoys spending time with friends and family, traveling and being outdoors in Seattle.

I am so blessed to work with such a great team! – Dr. Parkhill
