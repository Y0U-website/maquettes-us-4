About Parkhill

We at Parkhill Family Dentistry work hard to provide you and your family with a fantastic dental experience. Our passion is serving our patients and their families so they have healthy, beautiful smiles from a West Seattle dentist they can trust!

We offer high quality dental care to children and adults of all ages in a comfortable, relaxed setting. From the moment you enter our inviting waiting room to the time you complete your visit, our goal is to treat each individual with respect and a gentle touch.

Our staff makes it their mission to make you as comfortable as possible so you can relax and rest assured that you’re in good hands.

Whether you’re 4, 44, or 84, our Seattle dental team is happy to assist you with all your oral healthcare needs!

Find out more on our services and frequently asked questions pages, and give us a call to schedule your visit!
