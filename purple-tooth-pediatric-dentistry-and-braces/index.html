The braces finally come off, the smile you and your child have been waiting for is here, and then the orthodontist hands over a small plastic case with a retainer inside. It can feel like an odd anticlimax after months of adjustments and appointments, but that little device is the reason all that hard work sticks around for good. Teeth have a way of drifting back toward their original positions…

At Purple Tooth Dental , we build retainers into every braces plan we create, not as an afterthought but as the final piece that protects everything your child’s smile has already accomplished. Combining pediatric dentistry and braces under one roof means we get to see this whole process through, from the first consultation to the day your child no longer needs to think twice about their retainer…

Braces move teeth into new positions, but the bone and tissue around those teeth need time to settle and stabilize. Without a retainer holding everything in place during that adjustment period, teeth can shift back toward crowding or misalignment, undoing months of progress in a matter of weeks.

We talk with families about this reality early, not to alarm anyone but so everyone understands why wearing a retainer as prescribed is worth the small daily habit. Kids who understand the why behind their retainer tend to stick with it more consistently than kids who are just told to wear it.
