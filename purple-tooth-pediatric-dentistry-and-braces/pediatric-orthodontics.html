  • Tooth timing: Baby teeth falling out much earlier or later than expected
  • Chewing discomfort: Trouble biting or chewing without pain
  • Jaw movement: A jaw that shifts or clicks when opening or closing
  • Crowding: Teeth that don’t appear to be leaving enough room for the permanent set coming in behind them

Why We Try In-Office Treatment First

Every child’s mouth develops on its own timeline, which is part of why we like to see families early and often rather than all at once in middle school. Regular monitoring lets us catch a developing issue while there’s still room to guide it, instead of finding it after the growth window has mostly closed. Families can review what to expect at each stage of care on our patient information page before their child’s first evaluation.

What Happens at an Early Orthodontic Evaluation

An early evaluation at Purple Tooth Dental looks less like a dental appointment and more like a visit to a place your child already recognizes, seasonal displays and all. Dr. Jon will look at how your child’s teeth and jaws are coming together, check for the signs mentioned above, and talk with you about what he’s seeing in plain language. If nothing needs attention yet, we’ll simply build in monitoring at future visits , the same visits your child is already used to.

If Dr. Jon does spot something that would benefit from early intervention, we’ll walk you through the options together before any treatment begins. We’re in-network with Delta Dental and accept Medicaid, and our team can help you understand what’s covered before your child’s evaluation so there are no surprises.
