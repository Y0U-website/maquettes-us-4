Meet Your Dentist

Skilled and Compassionate Professional Committed to Your Optimal Dental Health

Dr. David Aicher

Background

Dr. David Aicher earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University Dental School and went on to spend the next 21 years in private practice before forming the Advanced Dental Professionals office in 2010.

Dr. Aicher is a member of the ADA, WDA, the Central Wisconsin Dental Forum, the Marathon County Dental Society, and the American Equilibration Society. He also is involved in the Wausau Noon Optimist Club.

Approach to Dentistry at Our Dental Office

Committed to continual improvement, Dr. Aicher is focused on staying current with the latest dental technology and teachings.

Fun Facts

In his free time, Dr. Aicher enjoys home remodeling, woodworking, playing golf, and curling. He and his wife Elizabeth also enjoy traveling to spend time with their son who lives in Chicago and their daughter who lives in Baltimore.
