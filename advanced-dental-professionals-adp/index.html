Dr. David Aicher and our experienced dental team are committed to providing exceptional, personalized dental care in a comfortable, stress-free, and friendly atmosphere. As skilled dental professionals, with a personal commitment to our community, our team works diligently to ensure that every patient receives the dental care they deserve.

We go above and beyond standards for cleanliness and safety, as recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA) and OSHA.

We pride ourselves in offering our patients an exceptional environment where they can feel comfortable knowing their oral health is in good hands. We develop an individualized plan for each patient. From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments and cosmetic dentistry, we are a family dentistry that is committed to you.

Visiting your dentist at least twice a year is critical to your dental and overall health. Good dental hygiene is incredibly important in preventing conditions such as tooth decay, gum disease, and other problems. Our experienced dentists use state-of-the-art equipment and are trained in the latest, most effective surgical techniques to minimize discomfort when your care necessitates root canals, tooth extraction or implant placement.
