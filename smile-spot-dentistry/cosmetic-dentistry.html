Smile Makeover

A smile makeover is more than just one treatment-it’s a carefully planned combination of cosmetic and restorative procedures tailored to your goals. Whether you want to address discoloration, chips, gaps, or misalignment, we create a customized plan that may include veneers, whitening, bonding, or contouring to achieve your ideal smile.

Our Venice, FL patients love that we take the time to understand their vision and use digital imaging tools so they can see potential results before starting treatment. Your smile makeover isn’tjust about looks-it’s about creating harmony between function, comfort, and aesthetics.

Professional Teeth Whitening

Everyday habits like coffee, tea, wine, and certain foods can leave stains that brushing alone can’t remove. Our professional teeth whitening treatments deliver brighter, whiter results quickly and safely-often in just one visit.

At Smile Spot Dentistry in Venice, Florida, we use advanced whitening systems that minimize sensitivity while maximizing results, giving you a noticeably brighter smile that still looks natural. Whether you need a quick touch-up for a special occasion or a deeper whitening transformation, we’ve got you covered.

Porcelain Veneers

If you’re looking for a dramatic change without extensive dental work, porcelain veneers might be the perfect option. Veneers are ultra-thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of your teeth, instantly correcting issues like chips, gaps, discoloration, and minor misalignment.

Our dentists in Venice work with precision to ensure your veneers blend seamlessly with your natural teeth-matching color, shape, and size for a flawless yet natural appearance. With proper care, veneers can last many years, making them a long-term investment in your smile.

Dental Bonding and Contouring

For quick and cost-effective improvements, dental bonding and contouring can make a big difference in just one visit. Using a tooth-colored resin, we can repair chips, close small gaps, and reshape uneven teeth. Contouring gently smooths and reshapes the enamel for a balanced, attractive smile.
