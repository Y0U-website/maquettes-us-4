Cleanings & Exams: Prevention is Power

Regular cleanings and comprehensive exams are the backbone of a healthy smile. During your visit, we:

  • Gently remove plaque and tartar buildup
  • Check for early signs of cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer
  • Provide personalized home care tips based on your needs

Tooth Extractions: Comfortable, Gentle Care

When a tooth is damaged beyond repair or causing crowding, a safe extraction can protect your overall oral health. Our Venice dental team uses modern techniques and local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. We also guide you through aftercare and replacement options if needed.

Tooth-Colored Fillings: Strong & Seamless

Gone are the days of noticeable silver fillings. We restore cavities with durable, tooth-colored composite resin that blends naturally with your smile. The material bonds securely to your tooth, helping preserve more of your natural structure while keeping your smile bright.

Relief for Tooth Pain & Jaw Pain

Persistent discomfort isn’t something you should ignore. Whether you’re experiencing sharp tooth pain, sensitivity, or jaw soreness, our Venice office can pinpoint the cause and provide quick, effective relief. Common issues include decay, cracked teeth, TMJ disorders, and bite misalignment-and we address each with a personalized treatment plan.

Gum Disease Treatment: Protecting Your Foundation

Healthy gums are just as important as healthy teeth. We offer scaling, root planing, and personalized periodontal care to treat gum disease at any stage. Early intervention helps prevent tooth loss and supports your long-term oral health.
