The practice

With offices in Santa Barbara and Buellton, Dr. Feusier has treated patients across the region for over a decade. Whether you’re considering braces or clear aligners, we offer options that suit your lifestyle and help you feel confident every step of the way.

Dr. Feusier begins every exam by evaluating facial balance, airway, breathing, and jaw posture, and not just tooth alignment, before building a treatment plan around your long-term health, not just a straight smile.

By leveraging advanced tools like digital imaging, 3D printing, and state-of-the-art brace systems, we ensure precision, efficiency, and exceptional results.

Dr. John R. Feusier, DDS, MS fell in love with Santa Barbara while earning his undergraduate degree at UC Santa Barbara, where he graduated with honors. After graduating from UCSB, Dr. Feusier traveled home to the Bay Area to attend Dental School at the University of the Pacific, and finally on to Baylor College of Dentistry at Texas A&M Health Science Center for his residency.

Dr. Feusier received both his Orthodontic Certificate and his Master’s Degree in Oral Biology from Baylor University, after having received extensive training in all aspects of Orthodontics including braces, Invisalign and surgically-assisted orthodontics.

Choosing an orthodontist is about more than straight teeth. It’s about trust, long-term health, and feeling confident in the care your family receives.

With over a decade of experience serving Santa Barbara and Buellton, Dr. Feusier brings unmatched skill and dedication to every patient.
