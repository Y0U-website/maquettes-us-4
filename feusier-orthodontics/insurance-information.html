Flexible financing options make comprehensive orthodontic care attainable for children, teens, and adults seeking to improve their smiles, bites, and quality of life.

We offer interest-free monthly payment plans that let you spread the cost of treatment comfortably over time, making braces or aligners more affordable for families and individuals.

Because each patient’s needs vary, personalized treatment plans influence pricing based on complexity and duration. Insurance coverage can significantly reduce out-of-pocket expenses, making it essential to confirm benefits early.

Choosing an experienced orthodontist like Dr. Feusier ensures your treatment is safe, effective, and tailored to your needs. Patients can also use HSA or FSA funds, which offer a tax-advantaged way to cover orthodontic expenses and reduce overall out-of-pocket costs.

Invest in quality

Investing in high-quality orthodontics ensures long-term stability, improved oral health, and better facial balance. Well-executed treatment reduces the risk of future dental complications such as uneven wear, gum recession, or TMJ discomfort.

Long-Term Benefits

High-quality appliances and precise planning lead to more predictable outcomes and shorter treatment times. Quality care also includes retainer guidance, follow-up appointments, and long-term monitoring to ensure your results are protected.

Our team is committed to transparent pricing, accessible financing, and maximizing your insurance benefits to ensure high-quality care fits your budget.

Future Smiles Program

We are proud to partner with the Future Smiles Program! This program provides low-income, Santa Barbara County youth, with a severe need for orthodontics access to treatment in our office.

Qualifications

Most patients from Future Smiles are found through our doctors and other local doctors doing screenings at schools. However, if you have not been selected in a school screening and have questions about if you qualify, feel free to ask our office for details.
