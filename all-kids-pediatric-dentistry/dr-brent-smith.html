Dr. Brent Smith

Making Every Visit a Positive One When a child is in his chair, Dr. Smith’s focus is on making them feel comfortable and at ease. He understands that a positive experience at a young age can shape how patients feel about dental care for years to come. His goal is to deliver the best quality care possible while keeping each appointment as calm and straightforward as he can. Whether it’s a first visit or a routine cleaning, Dr. Smith wants every child to leave feeling like coming to the dentist is something they can handle.

Explore all the ways our team supports young patients at All Kids Pediatric Dentistry , or learn more about our dental exams and cleanings and preventive care services.

Professional Memberships

Dr. Smith stays connected to the broader dental community through active professional memberships. These affiliations give him access to ongoing continuing education and the latest research in pediatric oral health.
