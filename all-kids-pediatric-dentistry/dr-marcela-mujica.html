Dr. Marcela Mujica

Her graduate training gave her an in-depth foundation in the behavioral, developmental, and clinical aspects of treating children at every stage of growth. That knowledge continues to shape how she approaches each patient. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry represents over 11,000 pediatric dental professionals nationwide who uphold evidence-based care and patient safety as their guiding principles.

A Philosophy Built Around the Whole Child Dr. Mujica believes that oral healthcare is the foundation of overall physical and emotional well-being, and that belief drives every aspect of her practice. Her approach goes well beyond addressing immediate clinical needs. She pays close attention to each child’s emotional state during a visit, working to reduce anxiety and build genuine trust over time.

Her goal is to transform even the most apprehensive young patients into kids who actually enjoy coming to the dentist. By combining a tailored clinical approach with a warm, personalized chairside manner, she helps children develop healthy habits and a positive relationship with dental care that can last a lifetime.

  • Certificate of Pediatric Dentistry — Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry, Department of Pediatric Dentistry
  • Master of Science in Dentistry (MSD) — Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry
  • Board Certified Diplomate — American Board of Pediatric Dentistry

Professional Memberships and Continuing Education

Staying current with the latest research and treatment advances is central to how Dr. Mujica serves her patients. She holds active memberships in several of the field’s most respected professional organizations.

These affiliations provide access to ongoing education, clinical research, and a network of colleagues dedicated to advancing the standard of care for children. Through continuing education opportunities offered by these groups, Dr. Mujica stays at the forefront of developments in pediatric dentistry , including new technologies, updated treatment protocols, and innovations in patient comfort. We are equally proud of our commitment to inclusive care for special needs patients .

Beyond the Practice

When she is not caring for patients, Dr. Mujica most often spends time with her husband, Ethan, their daughter, Charlotte, and their extended family. She enjoys diverse cuisines, travel, and cultural experiences, and she happily shares stories of her adventures and early years in Peru with patients and families alike.

That sense of warmth and connection she brings to her personal life is the same energy she carries into every appointment. Parents frequently notice that Dr. Mujica has a genuine gift for meeting children where they are, whether that means cracking a joke to lighten the mood or simply taking the extra time to explain a procedure in a way a young patient can understand.
