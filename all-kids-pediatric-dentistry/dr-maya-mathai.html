Dr. Maya Mathai

Her residency at a nationally recognized children’s hospital gave her experience working with patients across a wide spectrum of needs, from routine care to complex cases. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recognizes pediatric dentistry as a specialty requiring at least 24 months of advanced training beyond dental school, and Dr. Mathai’s credentials reflect that rigorous path.

A Dentist Kids Actually Enjoy Seeing Dr. Mathai’s goal is to make every child feel safe and comfortable from the moment they walk through the door. She knows that a positive experience early in life can shape how patients approach dental care for years to come, and she brings that perspective to every appointment. Her residency at a major children’s hospital gave her experience with a wide range of patient needs, and she draws on that depth every day.
