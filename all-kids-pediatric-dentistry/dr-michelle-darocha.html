Dr. Michelle DaRocha

During dental school, she participated in externships abroad in Nicaragua and China, providing dental care to patients in underserved communities. After graduating, she practiced general dentistry in Lawrence, Massachusetts as a Delta Dental Scholar before pursuing her pediatric specialty.

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recognizes pediatric dentistry as a specialty requiring at least 24 months of advanced training beyond dental school, and Dr. DaRocha’s credentials reflect that rigorous path.

A Dentist Who Connects With Every Family One of the qualities that sets Dr. DaRocha apart is her ability to establish a true dental home for her patients. She genuinely enjoys taking care of children and understands that building trust over time, appointment after appointment, is what leads to lifelong oral health. Parents often comment on how she puts children at ease from the moment they walk through the door.

Her multicultural background and fluency in three languages mean she is especially well-positioned to serve the diverse families who call Charlotte home. Whether a family primarily speaks English, Portuguese, or Spanish, they can expect the same level of warmth, clarity, and attentive care from Dr. DaRocha. Learn more about our commitment to serving all children, including those with unique needs, on our special needs dentistry page .

  • Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry (DMD) — Boston University
  • Certificate of Pediatric Dentistry — New York University Langone Dental Medicine at Rady Children’s Hospital, San Diego, California
  • Published Research — Co-authored paper on pediatric obstructive sleep apnea

Professional Memberships

Dr. DaRocha stays connected to the broader pediatric dental community through active professional memberships. These affiliations support her continued growth as a clinician and reflect her dedication to staying current with best practices in children’s oral health.

Through these organizations, she has access to ongoing continuing education, clinical research, and a professional network that helps her bring the most up-to-date care to her patients.

Outside the Office When she is not caring for patients, Dr. DaRocha stays active through yoga, running, and spin classes. She loves to travel with her husband, particularly to Brazil and Portugal, where she has family, and she enjoys learning about different cultures, listening to music, and spending time with her grandmother back in Boston.

That sense of adventure and genuine curiosity about the world translates directly into her work. She approaches every patient interaction with openness and enthusiasm, meeting each child and family where they are. Parents frequently appreciate her ability to explain things clearly and to make the entire visit feel calm and welcoming from start to finish.
