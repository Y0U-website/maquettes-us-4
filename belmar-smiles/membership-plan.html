Who Can Benefit From An In-House Membership Plan?

Our in-house membership plan is a helpful option for patients who are uninsured, self-employed, retired, between jobs or not satisfied with their current dental coverage. It may also be a good fit for families who want a simple way to budget for preventive care without relying on traditional insurance plans.

The plan is also valuable for patients who want to be proactive about their dental health. By including routine exams, cleanings, screenings, X-rays and fluoride treatments, the plan encourages regular visits that can help detect dental concerns early. If treatment is needed, the 15 % discount on necessary dental treatment helps make care more affordable.
