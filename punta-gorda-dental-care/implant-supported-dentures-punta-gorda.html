How do I care for implant-supported dentures?

Even though implant-supported dentures stay firmly in place, they must be removed nightly for cleaning. Brush carefully around each attachment point and soak the denture overnight to keep it clean and odor-free.

How much do implant-supported dentures cost in Punta Gorda?

Cost varies based on the number of implants needed, whether bone grafting is required, and whether you choose ball- or bar-retained attachments. Punta Gorda Dental Care offers personalized consultations to provide an accurate cost estimate for your case.

What’s the difference between implant-supported dentures and traditional dentures?

Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can slip or shift over time. Implant-supported dentures are anchored to titanium implants in the jawbone, giving them significantly more stability, chewing power, and comfort.
