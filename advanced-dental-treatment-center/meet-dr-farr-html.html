Get to Know Dr. Kirsten Farr

Born and raised in Green bay, Wisconsin, Dr. Kirsten Farr has been thrilled to serve patients in the Greenfield community since 2022. After almost a decade in the field, she finds it incredibly rewarding to help people feel more confident in their smiles and improve their daily oral hygiene. Patient education is at the heart of her approach, and she strives to support healthier, happier smiles every time she steps into the office.

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Dr. Farr was introduced to dentistry at a young age—her grandfather was an oral surgeon who absolutely loved his work. Hearing his stories, combined with her own positive experiences at the dentist, inspired her to begin shadowing her general dentist in high school. Over time, her interest in science, attention to detail and artistic nature solidified her decision to purse dentistry as a career.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

She completed her undergraduate education at Marquette University, earning a bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Science, and went on to obtain her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, located just outside of Chicago. During her time in dental school, she distinguished herself as the president of her university’s chapter of the American Student Dental Association.

Dr. Farr is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Academy of Facial Esthetics. She continually advances her skills through continuing education, including Guided Implant Placement and Advanced Invisalgin® Treatment Planning. She has extensive experience in restorative dentistry, fixed and removeable prosthetics, endodontics, extractions, socket preservation, bone grafting, and is certified in Diode Lasers. Recently, she expanded her expertise to include Sleep Medicine, offering both sleep studies and custom-fabricated sleep devices.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Outside the office, Dr. Farr enjoys spending time with her fiancé and her close-knit family. A devoted sports fan, she cheers on the Green Bay Packers and is an avid supporter of Marquette Men’s Basketball. She also has a passion for gardening and has cultivated a growing collection of houseplants, including starting a tomato garden in the summer of 2024. In her free time, Dr. Farr enjoys traveling, exploring new restaurants in Milwaukee, cooking with her fiancé, and relaxing with movies and television.
