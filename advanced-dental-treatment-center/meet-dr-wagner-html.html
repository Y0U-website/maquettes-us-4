Get to Know Dr. Bailey Wagner

Dr. Wagner has been in the dental field for 1O years and considers it to be one of the best decisions she’s ever made! One of the most rewarding parts of her job is building positive and trusting relationships with her patients and peers. She also enjoys the craft of dentistry, utilizing her artistic eye to create beautiful and healthy smiles. Read on to learn more about her background and education before your appointment!

Why Did You Decide to Become a Dentist?

Dr. Wagner began her career as a volunteer at a free dental clinic in her hometown of West Bend, WI. During her time there, she witnessed how quality dental care could transform the lives of others. She had several mentors that, through their generosity and clinical excellence, left a lasting impression. Since then, she continues to provide compassionate, high quality care with the same dedication to helping her patients.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Wagner attended the University of Wisconsin - Green Bay, where she earned her undergraduate degree in Human Biology. She then attended Marquette University School of Dentistry to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree. She holds formal post graduate certifications in lnvisalign clear aligners, diode soft tissue laser therapy, and sleep medicine with a focus on managing sleep apnea. She also has years of clinical experience in oral surgery, root canal therapy, dentures and partials, and restorative dentistry such as crowns and fillings.

Dedicated to staying at the forefront of her field’s best practices, Dr. Wagner regularly pursues continued education and is a member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry, American Academy of Facial Esthetics, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

At home, Dr. Wagner lives with her husband and their three cats - Mollie, Cheeto and Georgie, whom she rescued as a stray kitten! She enjoys several hobbies including world travel, art projects, thrifting, gardening, and cooking her favorite recipes. She loves a good game night with friends and, as a foodie, is always on the lookout for new restaurants to try out in the greater Milwaukee area!
