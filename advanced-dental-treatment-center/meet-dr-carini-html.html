Smile Brighter with William Carini, DDS

It’s really a blessing to be able to have a vocation where you can actually help a person out. It’s really been an enjoyable 20-plus years doing that. - Dr. Carini

Dr. Bill Carini grew up in Milwaukee in a family with 5 children, and he entered into dentistry almost by accident, but he says it’s the best thing that ever happened to him. You can learn about Dr. Carini’s interesting story and more by reading on below.

Why did you become a dentist?

Dr. Carini actually started college as a Zoology major, and after his sophomore year, he found himself in a meeting with his counselor. She basically asked him what he liked, and when he said art and science, she said that dentistry would likely be a better fit than his current major. She was right on the money! Through a simple conversation that lasted less than five minutes he found his path in life and hasn’t looked back since.

Where did you study dentistry?

Dr. Carini attended the Marquette University School of Dentistry, earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery. Ever since graduating, he has taken countless hours of continuing education in order to refine his skillset and expand his scope of care. He has taken many courses dedicated to implant dentistry, attended various trainings with Spear Education, and recently trained to use Botox/dermal fillers with the Academy of Facial Esthetics. He is also a member of the Academy of Laser Dentistry, which helps him stay up-to-date with the latest technology so he can always provide the highest quality care.

What do you like to do outside of dentistry?

Dr. Carini is married and has four children, as well as an attack dog named Lou Lou (who is a 6 &frac12; pound Yorkie). Outside of work, he enjoys spending most of his free time with his family. On the weekends, you’ll likely find him on his boat called the Sea Ray, dining at a new restaurant, or traveling and going camping at places like Lake Winnebago and Riviera Maya.
