Root canals are necessary when the pulp, soft tissue in the root canal, becomes infected or inflamed.

The infection or inflammation can be caused by a number of issues such as: deep tooth decay, repeated procedures on the tooth, or a large crack or chip in the tooth.

Additionally, tooth injury can result in pulp damage even if the tooth has no visible signs of damage such as chips or cracks. It’s important that infection and inflammation is treated quickly, otherwise it can cause pain or lead to an abscess.
