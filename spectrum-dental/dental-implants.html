Dental Implants in San Diego, CA

Once stable, dental implants can replace a single tooth, several teeth, or all missing teeth in one arch or both. At our San Diego office, we offer several implant options tailored to your unique needs.

  • Abutment: The connecting piece between the implant and the final prosthetic tooth.
  • A custom prosthetic tooth: It’s the visible, functional part of your dental implant, customized to blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth.

What is the dental implant procedure?

We have refined the procedure to be as smooth and comfortable as possible. Here is what you can expect:

Dental implants give you a clear path to a stronger, more natural-looking smile. At our office, we keep the experience seamless, comfortable, and rewarding.

  • In-depth examination: We use advanced X-rays and scans to evaluate your bone health and plan your implant procedure with precision.
  • Implant surgery: Our implant dentist uses excellent surgical skills to place a titanium fixture at a predetermined location in the jawbone. We use local anesthetics and sedation to create a comfortable, anxiety-free experience.
  • Healing and integration: Your implant heals and fuses with the jawbone to create a solid foundation for your prosthetic tooth.
  • Final restoration: The dentist attaches a custom crown or denture to restore your smile’s function and appearance.

Are dental implants worth it?

Dental implants do more than replace missing teeth. They restore your life! Whether you have lost one or multiple teeth, move from uncertainty to a solution that feels steady and secure.

The benefits of dental implants in San Diego, CA, include:

  • Natural aesthetics: Enjoy life-looking teeth that look and feel like your own.
  • Eat your favorite foods: With a stronger bite, eating no longer feels like a compromise. Enjoy steak, apples, a hearty bugger, and other foods without hesitation.
  • Stability: Loose teeth can keep you worrying at every moment. Dental implants are securely anchored in the jawbone, ensuring you live your best life without worries.
  • Jawbone preservation: Dental implants stimulate your jawbone to maintain its health and stability to support long-term oral health.
  • Durability: They can last several years or even decades with good maintenance.
  • Feel like yourself again: A beautifully restored smile helps you show the best version of yourself in social settings, at work, and around family.
  • Convenient maintenance: Caring for dental implants is straightforward. Brush and floss your restoration just as you would for natural teeth. There is no need for nightly soaks or other cumbersome maintenance rituals.
