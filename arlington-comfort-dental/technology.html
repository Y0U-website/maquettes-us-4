Digital X-Rays — Less Radiation, More Detail

Digital X-rays expose you to significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays — often up to 80 % less — while producing sharper, more detailed images. Dr. Rhie can pull them up on a screen chairside and walk you through exactly what she sees. That means no waiting for films to develop and no guesswork. For patients who come in for dental exams and cleanings , digital X-rays are a routine part of the visit that helps catch small issues before they grow into bigger ones.

Intraoral Cameras — See What Dr. Rhie Sees

An intraoral camera is a small, pen-sized device that captures high-resolution images of the inside of your mouth. Dr. Rhie uses it to show you what she is looking at in real time — a cracked tooth, early gum changes, or a worn filling — rather than simply describing it. Patients often find this reassuring. When you can see what your dentist sees, treatment decisions feel less abstract and more like a shared conversation about your own health.

Digital Impressions — No More Messy Molds

Traditional dental impressions involve trays filled with putty material that can feel uncomfortable and trigger a gag reflex. The scanner replaces that process entirely. Dr. Rhie uses a handheld wand to capture a precise 3D digital model of your teeth in minutes — no mess, no gagging, no waiting for molds to set. The scanner is especially useful for Invisalign and clear aligner treatment planning, where accuracy directly affects how well your aligners fit and how predictably your teeth move.

Panoramic X-Rays — A Full Picture in One Scan

A panoramic X-ray captures your entire mouth — teeth, jaw, sinuses, and surrounding bone — in a single image taken from outside your mouth. You simply rest your chin on a support and stay still for a few seconds while the machine rotates around your head. Dr. Rhie uses panoramic X-rays to evaluate jaw development, check for bone changes, and get a broad view of your oral health that individual X-rays alone cannot provide.

Why Technology Matters at a Private Practice

At a corporate dental office, technology is often described in brochures but rarely explained to patients. At Arlington Comfort Dental, Dr. Rhie uses these tools as part of an ongoing conversation with you. Because she is the same doctor at every visit, she builds on what she has already seen — your previous scans, your history, your concerns. The dental technology at our Arlington Heights office supports that continuity rather than replacing it.

Are digital X-rays safe?

Yes. Digital X-rays emit significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays — often up to 80 % less. Dr. Rhie uses digital X-rays as a routine part of exams because they deliver clearer images with a much smaller radiation exposure, making them a comfortable choice for patients of all ages.

What is a digital scanner used for?

The scanner creates a precise 3D digital model of your teeth and bite without any impression material. Dr. Rhie uses it primarily for Invisalign treatment planning and for restorations that require accurate fit. The process is quick, comfortable, and eliminates the gagging or mess that traditional molds can cause.
