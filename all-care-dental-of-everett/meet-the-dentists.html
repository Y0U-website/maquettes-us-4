Meet Dr. Jimmy Chen and Dr. Pei-Yun Kao

Jimmy Chen D.M.D

Dr. Jimmy Chen was born in California and raised in Taiwan. He returned to Pennsylvania for high school and proceeded to graduate from Boston University with a B.S. degree in Biochemistry and Molecular biology and a minor in Japanese language and literature. Dr. Chen earned his D.M.D degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

After graduation Dr. Chen received additional training as a dental resident at Hackensack Meridian Health Jersey Shore University Medical Center. During his residency, Dr. Chen honed his dental skills while treating the underserved population in the Jersey Shore area and taking care of dental emergency patients at the emergency room. Dr. Chen also attended monthly lectures given by renowned dentists and medical doctors.

As dentistry is an ever-evolving field, Dr. Chen is focused on providing the most comprehensive and current care for his patients. Apart from staying updated with the latest dental research studies, Dr. Chen also takes continuing education courses year round.

Dr. Chen is bilingual in English and Mandarin Chinese. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Chen enjoys watching various sports and documentaries along with cooking and traveling.

Pei-Yun Kao D.M.D

As a first-generation college graduate and the first dentist in her family, Dr. Kao has always valued hard work, compassion, gratitude, and perseverance foremost in life. She embraces the same philosophy in her patient care.

Dr. Kao values lifelong learning and actively takes continued education courses to stay current with forefront dental technology, materials, techniques to deliver the best comprehensive care possible to her patients. Dr. Kao is bilingual in English and Mandarin Chinese. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kao enjoys cooking, reading, arts, and traveling.
