From the moment you walk through our doors, our goal is simple: to help you feel comfortable while providing dentistry designed just for you. We’re proud to be a Spanish-speaking dental office and happy to assist our patients in both English and Spanish.

No two smiles are the same, and your dental care shouldn’t be either. We work hard to make visits to our dental office something patients don’t dread. From the moment you arrive, we create a welcoming, relaxed environment where you’re greeted like a friend, not just another name on the schedule. We genuinely care about our patients and the Mount Pleasant community we serve.

Dr. Niki and Dr. Becca are known for their gentle, approachable style and for taking the time to truly get to know each patient. It’s this culture of care and connection that sets our office apart from corporate and big box dental offices in town.
