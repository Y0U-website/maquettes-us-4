Meet the Team at Piney Woods Family Dentistry

Serving Mount Pleasant with Heart

At Piney Woods Family Dentistry, we provide patient-focused dental care designed around your individual needs, comfort, and long-term goals. Our locally owned dental office in Mount Pleasant, TX, is built on trust, honest communication, and genuine relationships with the families we serve.

We take a personalized approach to every patient visit. That means listening carefully, explaining your options clearly, and creating treatment plans that reflect your oral health needs, lifestyle, and goals. We never rush appointments or recommend unnecessary treatment; our focus is always on what’s best for you.

Our team is known for being warm, friendly, and approachable. From the moment you arrive, you’ll be greeted like a friend. Our team is especially known for our gentle demeanor and commitment to taking the time to truly get to know each patient.

We’re honored to serve the Mount Pleasant community with honest, high-quality dental care. If you’re looking for a dental home that values communication and personalized treatment, we’d love to welcome you. We also speak Spanish!

Dr. Niki Latiolais

Dr. Niki Latiolais is the lead dentist at Piney Woods Family Dentistry, where she has been proudly serving patients since purchasing the practice in 2015. She completed her undergraduate studies at The University of Texas at Austin and earned her dental degree from the UT Health Science Center at Houston, graduating from dental school in 2008.

Dr. Latiolais is passionate about creating a joyful, welcoming environment for both patients and her team. She loves that laughter fills the office each day and believes that making people smile is the most rewarding part of her profession. Her dedication to teamwork and positivity helps create an experience where patients feel comfortable, cared for, and confident in their smiles.

Her journey into dentistry began during college, when she worked in a dental office and witnessed firsthand how transformative a new smile could be in someone’s life. That experience inspired her to pursue a career focused on improving both oral health and overall confidence.

Outside of the office, Dr. Latiolais enjoys reading, running, spending time at the lake, and traveling with her family. In the summer, she can often be found participating in one of her favorite hobbies: wakesurfing. A fun fact many don’t know: she grew up in upstate New York surrounded by apple orchards, and her favorite apple is the MacIntosh, a regional classic.

Whitley

Whitley is the patient care coordinator at Piney Woods Family Dentistry, where she has been serving patients since July 2020. She began her dental career in March 2019 on the administrative side of dentistry and earned her RDA during COVID in 2020. Although she has experience working both in the front and back office, Whitley ultimately found her passion in the administrative side of patient care.

In her role, Whitley wears many hats. She is responsible for scheduling, coordinating patient care with specialists, maintaining up-to-date patient information, and ensuring the doctors’ schedules remain productive and flow smoothly. As the first face patients see when they walk through the door, she understands the importance of first impressions and strives to make every patient feel welcomed, comfortable, and valued. She especially enjoys helping ease dental anxiety and guiding patients through their treatment journey with confidence and care.

Whitley’s path into dentistry was unexpected. After leaving a six-year administrative role at a machine shop, a friend encouraged her to apply for a front office position at a dental practice. With no prior dental experience, she learned quickly and discovered a career she truly loves. Seven years later, she is grateful that leap of faith led her to where she is today.

Outside of dentistry, Whitley stays busy as a full-time real estate agent. She also loves football and anything related to the sport. One of the most unique and special parts of her life is supporting her daughter’s cheerleading. Whitley and her husband built a 1,250-square-foot home tumbling gym complete with a 30-foot trampoline in their backyard. The family spends most of their week immersed in cheer activities, cherishing every moment together.
