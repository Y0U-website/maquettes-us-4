About

Dr. Yang focuses in crowns, restorative dentistry, and aesthetic treatments, skillfully blending art and science to restore smiles and confidence. Known for her gentle touch, patient-centered approach, and unwavering professionalism, she has earned the trust of families across the community.

Beyond the operatory, Dr. Yang enjoys the company of her husband and three daughters. Her passion for dentistry has inspired her family—two of her daughters are currently attending University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, with one soon to graduate and pursue a specialty in periodontics; eventually make YTs Dental Care a generational family practice. When not transforming smiles, Dr. Yang enjoys cooking and traveling with her family, embracing life’s beautiful moments both at home and abroad.

Whether you’re seeking comprehensive restorative care or subtle aesthetic improvements, Dr. Yang is committed to providing a comfortable, personalized dental experience with lasting results.
