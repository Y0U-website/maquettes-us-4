Our Commitment to Quality Through Specialist Referrals

Our commitment to your care means knowing when to refer to specialists who can provide even better outcomes for specific situations.

Surgical and Orthodontic Referrals

While we handle most extractions and surgical procedures in-house, we refer to local oral surgeons for complex wisdom teeth extractions with sedation and other complex surgical cases. We also refer to local orthodontists for comprehensive teeth straightening. These specialists have additional training and resources that ensure the best possible outcomes for cases requiring their expertise. This quality-focused approach ensures every patient receives the right care from the right provider.

Pediatric Referrals to Specialists

While we provide gentle care for children, we refer complex pediatric cases to specialized pediatric dentists when appropriate. Pediatric specialists have additional training in child behavior management and specialized techniques that ensure the best possible experience and outcome for young patients who need extra support. This quality-focused approach ensures every child receives the right care from the right provider.

Endodontic Referrals for Complex Cases

We perform many root canals in our office, but we refer complex cases and previously treated teeth to local endodontists. Endodontists specialize exclusively in root canal therapy and handle the most challenging cases with specialized equipment and expertise. This patient-first approach ensures you receive the highest level of care when situations are particularly complex.
