Health Benefits

In addition to your daily benefits, you will notice benefits to your health. Here are some of the most prominent ones.

  • Improved Oral Health: Since dental implants replace the lost roots of the missing teeth, they are fully self-supported. They help to prevent future oral health issues related to tooth loss.

  • Prevents Bone Loss: Dental implants are the only tooth replacement solution that preserves your jawbone by stimulating it through routine biting and chewing.

Long-Term Benefits

When you get dental implants, you are making a long-term investment in your smile. Here are some benefits you can enjoy for many years to come.

  • Long-Lasting: With proper care, patients can enjoy dental implants for a lifetime.

  • Save Money: You don’t need to worry about buying adhesives or soaking solutions when you have dental implants. They also don’t require frequent repairs and replacements. Dental implants also help to reduce your risk of health issues, saving your time, discomfort, and money.

Indications for Dental Implants

Whether you’re missing a single tooth or multiple teeth, dental implants can help. To be considered for the procedure, it’s important to have good oral and overall health and enough jawbone density to support the titanium posts. There are also preparatory procedures we can schedule to help with that. Once you’re approved to get dental implants by your dentist in Tullahoma, there will be a variety of new restorative solutions available to you.

Missing Single Tooth

If you have a single gap in your smile that needs a replacement tooth, an implant-retained custom-crafted crown is a great choice. Your existing teeth won’t have to be altered in the process, allowing you to maintain optimal oral health and preserve your natural smile.

Missing Multiple Teeth

If you’re missing multiple teeth or even an entire row, implants can still be the best solution to replace them. Unlike traditional bridges and partial dentures, implant-retained ones offer a longer-lasting and more stable solution.

Missing All Teeth

With implant-retained dentures, a custom-made prosthetic is designed that simply snaps onto a set of implants placed into your mouth. This offers numerous advantages when compared to a traditional removable dentures such as added stability and a stronger bite.

Various factors that can influence the cost of your dental implant treatment include:

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Preventing dental implant failure starts with the consultation and extends well beyond the procedure. That’s why we encourage our patients to do the following:

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Remember, even though your dental implants can’t decay, your gums can develop an infection. In fact, one of the leading causes of dental implant failure is peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease). So, it’s extremely important that you prioritize healthy habits, like brushing, flossing, and rinsing with mouthwash consistently.

Tip: Replace your toothbrush (or toothbrush head) every quarter! That way, you aren’t using frayed, old, and dirty bristles to clean your teeth.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Eating nutrient-dense foods, like fresh fruits and seasonal vegetables, benefits your oral and overall health. So, do your best to incorporate them into each meal. At the same time, limit your intake of sugary, crunchy, and starchy snacks, like potato chips, hard pretzels, caramel-filled candies, and ice cream.

Break Bad Habits

Smoking dramatically increases your risk of dental implant failure, which is why we strongly recommend quitting prior to the procedure. Of course, that isn’t the only bad habit we recommend breaking. After all, even seemingly harmless ones, like biting your nails, chewing on ice cubes, and using your teeth as tools to open things, can also shorten the lifespan of your restoration.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Do you like playing tennis or pickleball with your friends to stay active? Maybe you prefer a solo sport, like rollerblading, lifting weights, or riding your bike. In both cases, we recommend wearing a mouthguard to protect your smile from harm. If you don’t have one already, let us know! We can have a custom one made so it fits comfortably and is the appropriate thickness for the activity.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Another important component of caring for your dental implants is coming to our office every six months for an exam. That way, we can take a look at your teeth, your gums, and any existing dental work you have, looking for signs of trouble in the process. If we notice anything concerning, we can bring it to your attention and determine the best way to proceed in order to minimize your risk of dental implant failure.

Important reminder: If you start to experience abnormal symptoms, like pain surrounding the implant site, don’t wait until your next checkup. Call us right away to share what’s going on and, if necessary, schedule an appointment!

Dental Implant FAQs

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Traditional tooth replacement options like dentures and dental bridges only last 5-10 years before they lose their fit and need to be replaced. On the other hand, since your dental implant is embedded into your jawbone and will actively work to keep it strong, your dental implant should never come loose or need to be replaced. In fact, most patients can easily expect their dental implants to last a lifetime with the proper care!

Can Anyone Get Dental Implants?

Practically anyone with missing teeth can get dental implants. You’ll need to be well enough to undergo minor oral surgery, be free of oral health issues like gum disease, and have adequate jawbone density. Even if you do not initially qualify for dental implants, preliminary procedures like periodontal therapy or a bone graft can get you on the path towards becoming a dental implant candidate.

Am I Too Old for Dental Implants?

There is no upper age limit for dental implants! As long as you are healthy and have a strong jawbone, we can help you replace any number of missing teeth. Many patients who are a bit older will naturally have jawbone deterioration, which can pose a threat to dental implants. Fortunately, with a bone graft, sinus lift, or ridge expansion from one of our trusted local specialists, we can help you achieve the strong, stable smile you deserve.

Am I Too Young for Dental Implants?

We do not recommend dental implants for patients who are under the age of 18 years old. Dental implants fuse with the jawbone in order to stay in place, but a young adult’s jawbone will continue to grow and change until about the age of 18. To ensure your teenager’s tooth replacement is successful, we will wait to place a dental implant until we are certain their jawbone has stopped developing.

Does It Hurt to Get Dental Implants?

At first, hearing that getting dental implants requires minor oral surgery may sound somewhat intimidating. But we’re here to put your fears to rest and reassure you that you have nothing to worry about. In fact, most patients are pleasantly surprised at how easy and comfortable the procedure is! We entrust this vital part of your dental implant treatment to trusted local specialists, who will take every precaution to ensure you are as comfortable as possible while having your dental implants placed.
