Our Frequently Asked Questions! What Is An Oral Surgeon? An oral surgeon is a special type of dental professional who has gone to school for extra years. Oral surgeons are experts in all types of oral surgery. This makes them the perfect choice if you need placement of dental implants or have had a facial trauma that requires surgical attention.

What Is The Difference Between An Oral Surgeon & A DDS? The simplest difference between a dental professional with a DDS and an oral surgeon is the extra training the oral surgeon undergoes. For instance, both Dr. Vickers and Dr. Anver spent an additional two years in school to receive their MD degrees. Consequently, they have the expertise and experience to handle a lot of treatments that might be beyond a dentist’s, periodontist’s, or orthodontist’s capability.

Is It Worth Going To An Oral Surgeon For Dental Implants? When it comes to choosing the right healthcare provider, credentials and experience always matter. Having an oral surgeon place your dental implants gives you tremendous peace of mind and lowers the chance of dental implant failure.

Is It Worth Going To An Oral Surgeon For A Wisdom Tooth Extraction? Wisdom tooth extractions are different procedures than normal tooth extractions. Therefore, you’ll want to put your care into the hands of someone who has the equipment and knowledge to address even the toughest issues.
