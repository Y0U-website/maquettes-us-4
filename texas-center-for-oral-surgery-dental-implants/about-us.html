Our Investment in Top Quality Care With Our In-House Lab

That means diagnosis and treatment that is faster, more precise, more comfortable, more effective, and more predictable. Alongside our credentials as board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons, our state-of-the-art surgical capabilities offer you true peace of mind.

Roland Milling Machines For In-House Zirconia Final Teeth This milling machine allows our technicians to mill dental prosthetic materials such as final full mouth prostheses, crowns, bridges and veneers providing complete quality control and quick turnaround time for our patients.

Asiga Max 3d Printers For Same Day Teeth The Asiga MAX is the world’s most advanced lab 3D printer offering exceptional productivity for our dentists and patients. With 3D print technology, our office can print temporary full mouth dental prostheses, night guards, temporary crowns, custom healing abutments and dental models for implant planning.

PIC Dental Camera for Photogrammetry The concept is photogrammetry. The PIC is an extremely high-tech camera that captures the implant position after it has been placed with almost 100 % accuracy. This technology allows our office to create 3D printed teeth with the highest level of accuracy. With this technology, we can give our patients a same-day smile in what would normally take 2-3 visits over the course of multiple weeks. Since all records are digital, if a prosthesis breaks, we can print a new set of teeth that can be placed within a few hours, as opposed to a few weeks.

Trios Intra-Oral Scanners For Goopless Impressions This is an intraoral scanner that allows our dentist to scan teeth, bites, and tissue with extremely high accuracy. The Itero provides us the ability to plan cases, print dental models and create teeth digitally without ever having to take goopy impressions like in the past.

I-CAT CBCT Scanners For 3d X-Rays This is a Cone Beam CT scanner that produces a quick 3D scan of the anatomy of your teeth, jaw, and face with minimal radiation. This 3D technology provides our dentists the ability to plan cases thoroughly and with ideal accuracy.

Medit Scanner This is an intraoral scanner that allows our dentist to scan teeth, bites, and tissue with extremely high accuracy. The Itero provides us the ability to plan cases, print dental models and create teeth digitally without ever having to take goopy impressions like in the past.

PRF Centrifuges For Quick Healing Times PRF (Platelet-rich fibrin) allows us to utilize the patients concentrated growth/healing factors during procedures. PRF is used for bone grafting, complicated tooth extraction and dental implant surgeries. PRF helps to accelerate the healing process of soft and hard tissue for faster healing time, less pain, less swelling and a lower risk of infection.
