What Are All-on-4 Implants?

All-on-4 implants are an innovative technique in advanced dentistry that replaces an entire arch of teeth using only four strategically placed dental implants. These implants act as anchors, supporting a full set of custom-made prosthetic teeth.

Unlike traditional implant methods that may require one implant per tooth, All-on-4 implants maximize efficiency by using fewer implants while providing the same stability and durability. This minimally invasive approach is ideal for patients seeking a quicker and more affordable solution to full-mouth restoration.
