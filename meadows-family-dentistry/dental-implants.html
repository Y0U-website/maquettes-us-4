What Are Dental Implants and Mini-Dental Implants?

Both dental implants and mini-dental implants provide durable, long-lasting solutions for replacing missing teeth, but they differ in size, application, and suitability:

Dental Implants Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, serving as artificial tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures, offering a permanent and natural-looking tooth replacement option.

Mini-Dental Implants Mini-dental implants are smaller in diameter than traditional implants, making them less invasive to place. They are ideal for patients with insufficient bone density or those seeking a quicker solution. While not suited for all cases, mini-dental implants can stabilize dentures or replace smaller teeth with minimal surgery.
