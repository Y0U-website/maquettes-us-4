The practice

NO CHILD WAITS IN PAIN. If your child lacks insurance and has an infection or dental emergency, call our office. We will provide any necessary urgent treatment at ZERO cost.

Our mission is to eliminate barriers to great dental health. We often provide critical, medically necessary procedures that the Medicaid program doesn t fully reimburse.

Unlike other dental practices in Missouri, we do not charge Medicaid patients for these services. Thanks to our non-profit structure and partners, we are proud to offer this care to 99 % of our patients at no cost.
