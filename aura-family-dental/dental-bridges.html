Frequently Asked Questions

How are dental bridges secured in the mouth?

The first option is to secure the dental bridge to neighboring teeth, with a crown built into the end of the bridge. If these teeth aren’t healthy enough to support the bridge, then it might be necessary to anchor the bridge using dental implants .

What is the best way to take care of my dental bridge?

The daily habits are similar to taking care of your normal teeth. Stay consistent with regular dental hygiene, such as brushing twice a day and flossing once a day. It is also a good idea to maintain consistency with dental checkups twice a year.

How long will my dental bridge last?

With proper oral hygiene, a dental bridge can last between 10 – 15 years. But the length of time depends on the materials used and how consistent you are with caring for your teeth on a daily basis.

What are other alternatives to dental bridges?
