What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots and provide a permanent base for fixed replacement teeth. At Midjersey Smiles, Dr. Anand uses state-of-the-art titanium posts that naturally integrate with your jawbone, creating a sturdy foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most patients with good oral health and adequate bone density can receive dental implants. During your consultation, your dentist will evaluate several factors:

  • Your general health status
  • Bone density and quality
  • Gum health
  • Oral hygiene habits
  • Smoking status
  • Medical conditions that might affect healing

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The success rate for dental implants exceeds 95 percent when patients maintain good oral hygiene and schedule regular check-ups with Dr. Anand at Midjersey Smiles.

What Is the Dental Implant Process Like?

The dental implant journey at our Old Bridge, NJ dental practice typically involves several steps:

  • Initial Consultation : Dr. Anand performs a comprehensive examination, including X-rays and 3D imaging .
  • Treatment Planning : We create a customized treatment plan based on your needs.
  • Implant Placement : The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing Period : The implant integrates with your bone over three to six months.
  • Crown Placement : A permanent dental crown is attached to the implant.

Is the Dental Implant Procedure Painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. Dr. Anand uses advanced techniques and appropriate anesthesia to make the process comfortable, and many patients return to work the next day.

How Should I Care for My Dental Implants?

Maintaining dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth:

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Floss daily around the implant
  • Visit Midjersey Smiles regularly for professional cleanings
  • Follow Dr. Anand’s care instructions

Can Dental Implants Replace Multiple Missing Teeth?

Yes, your dentist can use dental implants to replace single teeth, multiple teeth, or full arches. Options include:

  • Single tooth implants
  • Implant-supported bridges
  • All-on-4 implants
  • Full arch replacements
