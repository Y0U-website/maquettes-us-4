What are Mini Implants?

Mini dental implants are small-diameter titanium screws that serve as artificial tooth roots. Dr. Ruma Anand places these implants directly into the jawbone to support various dental prosthetics, from single crowns to full dentures. Unlike conventional implants, mini implants measure less than three millimeters in diameter while maintaining remarkable strength and stability.

These compact implants integrate naturally with bone tissue through a process called osseointegration. At Midjersey Smiles’ state-of-the-art facility in Old Bridge, NJ, the procedure typically takes just one visit, and most patients are able to resume normal activities the following day.

Mini Implants vs. Traditional Dental Implants

When comparing tooth replacement options, understanding the differences between mini and traditional implants helps patients make informed decisions. Here’s how they differ:

  • Size and Structure: Traditional implants measure 3.5 6 millimeters in diameter and consist of multiple parts. Mini implants are roughly half the size and feature a one-piece design, simplifying the placement process.
  • Surgical Procedure: Traditional implants often require extensive surgery, bone grafting, and months of healing. Mini implants, as performed by Dr. Ruma Anand at Midjersey Smiles, typically involve a minimally invasive procedure without incisions or sutures.
  • Recovery Time: While traditional implants may need three to six months to heal before the final restoration, mini implants can often support the immediate loading of replacement teeth.
  • Cost Considerations: Mini implants generally cost less than traditional implants due to fewer components and simpler surgical requirements.

Mini Implants vs. Dentures

Stability and Security

Unlike traditional dentures that simply rest on your gums, mini implants create a permanent foundation for your replacement teeth through titanium posts anchored in your jawbone. At Midjersey Smiles in Old Bridge, NJ, Dr. Ruma Anand helps patients transition from conventional dentures to mini implant-supported solutions that eliminate embarrassing slippage during eating and speaking.

Function and Comfort

Mini implants allow you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence, providing up to 90 % of natural chewing ability. They distribute biting forces through the implants rather than pressing directly on your gums, eliminating the discomfort and sore spots common with traditional dentures.

Long-Term Benefits

While traditional dentures typically need replacement every five to seven years due to changing mouth shape and bone loss, mini implant-supported dentures offer a more stable, long-lasting solution. The implants stimulate and preserve your jawbone, helping maintain your facial structure over time.

Simplified Maintenance

Patients particularly appreciate that they can brush their implant-supported teeth like natural ones rather than dealing with messy adhesives and overnight soaking. Dr. Anand provides detailed care instructions to help extend the life of your mini implant-supported dentures.
