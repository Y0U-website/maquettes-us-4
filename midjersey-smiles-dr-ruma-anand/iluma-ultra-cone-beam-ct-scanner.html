What is a Cone Beam CT Scan?

A Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scan is an innovative digital imaging technology that creates detailed 3D images of dental structures, soft tissues, nerve pathways, and bone. Unlike conventional dental X-rays that capture only 2D images, the Iluma Ultra CBCT scanner at Midjersey Smiles captures comprehensive volumetric data with a single scan.

At Midjersey Smiles in Old Bridge, NJ, the Iluma Ultra CBCT scanner emits significantly less radiation compared to conventional CT scans while delivering superior image quality. The scanning process typically takes between 10-40 seconds, during which the machine captures hundreds of distinct images that are compiled into detailed 3D representations of the patient’s oral anatomy.

How Does Cone Beam CT Differ from Traditional 2D CT Scans?

The Iluma Ultra Cone Beam CT scanner at Midjersey Smiles represents a revolutionary advancement over traditional 2D imaging technologies in several key aspects:

Dimensionality and Detail

Traditional 2D CT scans produce flat images that can obscure critical details due to overlapping structures. The Iluma Ultra CBCT scanner generates true 3D images that can be rotated, measured, and examined from any angle.

Radiation Exposure

The Iluma Ultra CBCT scanner uses a focused cone-shaped beam that significantly reduces radiation exposure compared to conventional CT technology. Patients in Old Bridge, NJ can feel confident knowing that their diagnostic imaging is being conducted with technology that adheres to the ALARA principle (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) for radiation exposure.

Scanning Time and Patient Comfort

Traditional CT scans can require extended scanning times and often involve uncomfortable positioning. The Iluma Ultra’s rapid scanning capability enhances patient comfort while reducing the likelihood of movement artifacts that can compromise image quality.

Image Processing and Analysis

While conventional CT scans often require substantial post-processing time, the Iluma Ultra CBCT scanner at Midjersey Smiles produces immediate results. The system’s advanced software allows Dr. Anand to manipulate the images instantly, isolate specific anatomical structures, and make precise measurements for treatment planning.

Clinical Applications

Traditional 2D imaging is limited in its diagnostic capabilities. The Iluma Ultra CBCT technology expands the scope of dental diagnostics, supporting advanced procedures like dental implant placement , endodontic treatment, orthodontic planning, and TMJ analysis with unprecedented precision.
