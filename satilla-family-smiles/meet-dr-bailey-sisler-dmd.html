Meet Dr. Bailey Sisler, DMD

Dr. Sisler is an active member of the American Dental Association and stays at the forefront of modern dental techniques to ensure her patients receive the most comfortable and effective care possible.

  • Master of Science in Biomedical Science – University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine
  • Bachelor of Science in Biology – University of Pittsburgh (Magna Cum Laude)

A Heart for Service

Before moving to the Golden Isles, Dr. Sisler dedicated much of her time to underserved communities. As a leader in the Student National Dental Association, she volunteered with Street Medicine at Pitt to provide essentials to the homeless in downtown Pittsburgh.

Her commitment to care has even taken her abroad; she spent time in northern Jamaica providing free dental care—including extractions and restorations—to over 400 patients in need. She brings that same compassionate, patient-first philosophy to every chairside visit here in Brunswick.

Outside of the Chair

Now a resident of St. Simons Island, Dr. Sisler is fully embracing the coastal Georgia lifestyle. When she isn’t helping patients achieve their best smiles, you can find her:

  • Cheering on her teams: She’s a loyal fan of the Baltimore Ravens and the Pittsburgh Penguins.
  • Getting Creative: An artist at heart, she enjoys drawing and loses herself in a good fantasy novel.
  • Staying Active: Whether it’s working out, cooking a new recipe, or traveling to explore new places, she loves staying on the move.
  • Island Living: On the weekends, you’ll most likely find her relaxing at the beach or enjoying a local concert.
