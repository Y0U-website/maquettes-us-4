Who Benefits Most From a Savings Plan?

Our Elite Dental Savings Plan tends to be the best fit for patients who do not have dental insurance through an employer, have coverage they are not satisfied with, or want a higher standard of care without navigating a separate insurer’s network restrictions. It is also a practical option for families managing multiple household members, since child tiers make preventive visits more accessible for kids as well.

Since the plan is administered directly through our Brunswick office, there is no third party involved in approving your care. You and your dentist decide what treatment makes sense, and the plan simply makes that treatment more affordable.

Pairing Your Plan With Financing

For treatment beyond what your savings plan discount covers, we also accept CareCredit, Cherry, and Sunbit financing, which allow patients to spread out the cost of larger procedures over time. Many of our patients combine their Elite Dental Savings Plan membership with one of these financing options, using the plan for everyday preventive care and financing for bigger restorative or cosmetic work. This pairing gives you the flexibility to get the treatment needed on a timeline that works for your budget rather than delaying care indefinitely.

How This Compares to Traditional Insurance

Traditional dental insurance often comes with annual maximums, waiting periods for major work, and networks that may or may not include your preferred dentist. A savings plan works differently. There are no claims to file and no annual maximum limiting how much care you can receive at the discounted rate. You simply pay your membership fee and start using your benefits right away.

This does not mean a savings plan replaces insurance for everyone. Patients with strong employer-sponsored coverage may find their existing plan meets their needs. However, for the many patients without access to that kind of coverage, or those who want to supplement thin insurance benefits, our Elite Dental Savings Plan offers a more direct and transparent path to affordable care.
