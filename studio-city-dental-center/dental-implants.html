COSMETIC DENTAL/TOOTH IMPLANTS

Missing teeth can pose a serious problem for many reasons. In addition to destroying the beauty of a smile, having missing teeth can also impede tasks such as speaking and eating. Furthermore, missing teeth can also cause the remaining teeth to shift and become misaligned. Fortunately, dental implant, which is a part of cosmetic dentistry, can be the solution to this problem.

Patients suffering from tooth loss due to decay, disease, injury, or any other condition should strongly consider getting dental implants. Implant dentistry provides patients with a method of dental restoration that is both desirable and effective. A dental implant is a device that serves as an anchor for artificial teeth and other dental prostheses. Dental implants are most commonly made of titanium . These implants are inserted within the bone of the jaw, and integrate within the structure of the bone.

The implementation of dental implants is a two step process. The first step is an operation in which the titanium implant is inserted into the jawbone. The patient then undergoes a healing process that takes four to six months. During this time, the implant integrates with the surrounding bone until it is firmly secured. This process is called osseointegration. After the healing is complete, a second procedure takes places in which an artificial tooth is attached to the implant.
