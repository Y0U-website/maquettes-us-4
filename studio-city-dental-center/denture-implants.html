Denture Implants in Studio City

Are you missing some or all of their teeth? If dental implants don’t seem to be a viable solution, then dentures might be a better solution.

What is a denture?

A denture functions as a fully removable substitute for missing teeth. If all teeth are missing, then a complete denture is used. Partial dentures can also be constructed to custom fit a patient who is only missing some teeth.

Dentures can make a big impact on your smile.

The procedure

There are several steps involved in making a set of dentures, and the entire process takes a few weeks to complete.

Dentures can be a pretty easy solution for people who want a removable option that improves their smile. If you’re interested in exploring this option, please contact us .

  • Every single aspect of your jaw needs to be measured and an impression can be made.
  • Faux dentures that are the exact shape of the mold will be constructed based on the impressions so that you can try them out for comfort and fit.
  • Once it’s confirmed that the mold fits perfectly or it’s fine tuned until it does, permanent dentures will be constructed from the mold.
