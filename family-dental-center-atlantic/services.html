Services

Your Healthiest Smile Starts Here We offer general, restorative, and cosmetic dental care for all ages. From routine cleanings and root canals to veneers and clear aligners, we’ve got you covered.

General & Preventative

Regular exams and cleanings are essential to your long-term oral health. From x-rays and plaque removal to root canals, we’re committed to helping patients maintain their dental health for a lifetime. At Family Dental Center, we take pride in doing things right the first time to prevent future issues.

Restorative

Repair your teeth and gums with quality treatments and durable solutions. From fillings and implants to bridges and crowns—we’ll take the time to discuss your options and create a personalized plan that fits your needs. Undergoing restorative treatment doesn’t have to be painful. We offer various pain control measures to put your nerves at ease, ensuring you feel comfortable during the procedure.

Cosmetic

Enhance your smile with cosmetic dentistry. From teeth whitening and clear aligners to veneers and Botox, our team is committed to helping you achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. It’s never too late to straighten your teeth or brighten your smile. We offer modern solutions that deliver incredible results.

We believe in accessible dentistry and offer high-quality care at a fair rate.
