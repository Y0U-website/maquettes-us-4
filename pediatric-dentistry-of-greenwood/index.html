The practice

We want you, as a parent, to be comfortable with the care we provide for your youngster. We encourage you to ask questions about your child’s dental treatment and express any concerns you may have.

Contact our office to schedule an appointment with Dr. Turner . Positive dental experiences early on are extremely important to ensure the long-term dental health of your family. We want to help you raise healthy, cavity-free kids!

Going to the dentist doesn’t have to be a negative experience! Because of their young age, I have the unique opportunity to shape my patients’ views and opinions of dentistry, as well as educate them about their oral health so they will understand the importance of a healthy smile as an adult!

My team is an extension of my philosophy. We treat each little one as we would our own. By treating our patients with warmth and compassion, we invite a more pleasant and comfortable experience for them. We also reward our cavity-free patients by hanging their picture on our cavity-free wall and entering their names in a drawing for a gift card.

I attended the University of South Carolina, where I earned a Bachelor of Science in chemistry, cum laude . My dental education was completed at the Medical University of South Carolina, where I obtained my Doctorate of Dental Medicine. I continued at the Medical College of Georgia, where I received my pediatric dental training.

My passion for staying up to the moment means my patients can expect to receive the highest quality and most advanced care in my office. I am committed to excellence, especially when it comes to patient care. I became a board-certified pediatric dentist in 2008, which means I voluntarily passed a written and oral examination to validate my training and knowledge of pediatric dentistry.

I participate in continuing education classes all over the country each year in an effort to stay informed on the most current topics in pediatric dentistry.

I am also a part-time faculty member at the Medical College of Georgia-Department of Pediatric Dentistry. I give several presentations and lectures each year to educate other dentists and healthcare providers about current topics and the clinical practice of pediatric dentistry.

Les soins

I am licensed to practice the specialty of pediatric dentistry in South Carolina and Georgia. I currently have hospital privileges at Self Regional Healthcare in Greenwood and MCG-Children’s Medical Center in Augusta.
