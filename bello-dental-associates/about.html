About Bello Dental Associates Aventura, FL

Welcome to Bello Dental Associates , your trusted dental office in Aventura, Florida . We offer comprehensive general and cosmetic dentistry in a warm, compassionate environment. Led by Board-Certified Dentist Dr. Jenny Bello , our team provides personalized care for patients of all ages.

Compassionate, Modern Dental Care

At Bello Dental Associates, we understand that dental visits can feel intimidating. Dr. Bello’s passion for dentistry began with her own childhood dental anxiety, which is why she focuses on gentle, patient-centered treatment to put every patient at ease.

Our highly skilled team includes specialists in periodontics, endodontics, pediatric dentistry, and oral surgery, ensuring you receive exceptional care all under one roof.

Comprehensive Dental Services

  • Preventive and family dentistry
  • Cosmetic dentistry and teeth whitening
  • Invisalign clear aligners
  • Dental crowns and restorations
  • Dental implants
  • TMJ and jaw pain treatment
  • Emergency dental care

Proudly Serving Aventura & Surrounding Areas

We proudly serve patients from Aventura, North Miami Beach, Sunny Isles Beach, Hallandale, and the greater Miami-Dade County area .
