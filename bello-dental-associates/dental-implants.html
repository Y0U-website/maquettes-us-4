Dental Implants Q & A

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots (typically titanium posts) that are surgically placed into your jawbone to support a replacement tooth or bridge. Once healed, they provide a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture that mimics the look and feel of your natural teeth.

Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants:

  • Preserve bone health and prevent bone loss
  • Prevent neighboring teeth from shifting
  • Offer a stable, long-term solution
  • Look and function like natural teeth

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a good candidate if you: ✔ Have one or more missing teeth ✔ Are in good general and oral health ✔ Have sufficient jawbone to support an implant ✔ Want a long-term tooth replacement solution

Even if your bone has deteriorated over time, advanced treatments like bone grafting may help prepare your jaw for implants. We’ll evaluate your unique situation during your consultation.

How Dental Implants Work

The dental implant process typically includes:

This process ensures a secure and natural-looking replacement.

  • Comprehensive exam & treatment planning We assess your oral health and create a personalized plan.
  • Implant placement A titanium post is placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing & osseointegration Over several weeks, the bone fuses to the implant.
  • Abutment & permanent restoration A custom replacement tooth (crown, bridge, or denture) is attached.

Types of Dental Implants We Offer

Single Tooth Implants

Replace one missing tooth with a natural-looking crown supported by an implant.

Multiple Tooth Implants

Ideal for replacing several missing teeth with implant-supported bridges.

Full Arch / All-on-4 Implants

Support a full set of upper or lower teeth with a stable implant base (ask us if offered in office).

Benefits of Dental Implants

✔ Improve chewing and speaking ✔ Preserve jawbone and face shape ✔ No need to alter adjacent healthy teeth ✔ Long-lasting and reliable ✔ Natural aesthetics and comfort
