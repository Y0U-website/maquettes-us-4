Dental Implants Peachtree…

  • Comfortable – Dental implants offer a pain-free, permanent solution that allows you to enjoy your favorite foods without discomfort once healed.
  • Easy to Care For – Dental implants are permanent and easy to maintain. Simply brush and floss them like your natural teeth, with no need for removable dentures or bridges.
  • Maintain Oral Health – Unlike bulky dental appliances that can cause bone loss and gum irritation, dental implants protect your jaw and gums, promoting overall oral health.
  • Long-Lasting – Designed to last a lifetime, dental implants are the only tooth replacement option that offers such long-term durability.

What Type of Dental Implant Do I Need?

Single Dental Implant

If you’ve lost a single tooth due to decay or injury, a single dental implant can help restore your smile and confidence. A titanium dental implant, combined with a custom-made dental crown, replaces both the root and visible surface of your missing tooth.

With a dental implant, you can smile, speak, and enjoy your favorite foods just as comfortably as you did with your natural tooth!
