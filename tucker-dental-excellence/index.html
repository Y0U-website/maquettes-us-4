Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. They act as artificial tooth roots, typically made from biocompatible materials, such as titanium, and are placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth.

Once integrated with the bone, implants provide a stable foundation for restorations such as crowns, bridges, or full-arch dentures. This allows you to restore both the appearance and function of your natural teeth—making it easier to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

With proper planning and care, dental implants are designed to be a durable, long-lasting solution for tooth replacement.

Dental implants can replace one or several missing teeth without affecting adjacent natural teeth.
