Dr. Rita Huang, DDS

Twitter Linkedin-in Medically reviewed by Dr. Amy Nguyen, DDS Dr. Rita grew up in Cupertino, California — she has deep roots in the Bay Area community she now serves. She earned her undergraduate degree in Biological Sciences from UC Davis before completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Southern California in 2013.

With 12 years of experience, Dr. Rita has developed particular clinical interest in oral surgery and dental implants — complex, life-changing procedures she approaches with steady hands and a patient-first philosophy. Her approach to care is prevention-focused and evidence-based: she stays conservative when possible and recommends treatment grounded in what is genuinely best for each individual patient. For nervous patients, she prioritizes clear communication and ensuring that every patient feels fully informed and in control at every step of their visit.

Dr. Rita has given back beyond her practice through a humanitarian dental mission trip to Bolivia — bringing care to patients who otherwise might not have access to it.

Outside the office, she enjoys beach volleyball and caring for her 14-year-old dog. Dr. Rita speaks English and Mandarin.
