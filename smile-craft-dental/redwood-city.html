Our general dentistry services in Redwood City include thorough examinations, digital X-rays, and personalized treatment recommendations tailored to your oral health history. During each preventive visit, we carefully evaluate your teeth and gums for early signs of decay or disease. Digital imaging allows us to detect problems that may not be visible during a visual examination alone. This proactive approach catches concerns early when treatment is typically simpler and less costly.

Restorative Dentistry Solutions

Life happens, and teeth can become damaged, decayed, or lost over time. Our restorative dentistry services help repair and replace teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics. Dental crowns and bridges protect weakened teeth and fill gaps left by missing teeth. For patients seeking permanent tooth replacement, dental implants offer a durable, natural-looking solution.

Modern restorative materials blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing both strength and beauty. CEREC technology allows us to create same-day crowns in certain cases, eliminating the need for temporary restorations and multiple appointments. This efficiency saves you time while delivering high-quality results.

Dentures and Tooth Replacement Options

Patients missing multiple teeth may benefit from dentures or partial dentures. These removable appliances restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Our team works with you to find the most comfortable fit and natural appearance for your specific situation. We understand that adjusting to dentures takes time, and we provide ongoing support to ensure your satisfaction with your restoration.

Orthodontic Treatment Options

Straightening your teeth no longer requires traditional metal braces. Orthodontic treatment in Redwood City offers a discreet, comfortable alternative for teens and adults seeking a straighter smile. The removable aligners allow you to eat your favorite foods and maintain your regular oral hygiene routine throughout treatment.

Digital treatment planning shows you a preview of your expected results before you begin, helping you feel confident in your decision. Most patients wear their aligners for 20 to 22 hours daily, switching to a new set every one to two weeks as their teeth gradually move into proper alignment.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies don’t follow a schedule. Whether you’re dealing with sudden tooth pain, a knocked-out tooth, or a broken restoration, our emergency dentistry services in Redwood City provide prompt relief. We prioritize urgent cases and work to see emergency patients as quickly as possible.

Common dental emergencies include severe toothaches, abscessed teeth, broken crowns or fillings, and trauma to the mouth or jaw. If you experience severe pain, swelling, or trauma to your teeth, contact our office right away. Quick treatment often makes the difference between saving and losing a tooth.
