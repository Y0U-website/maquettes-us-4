Professional dental cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing cannot address. Your hygienist uses specialized tools to clean below the gum line and polish your teeth, leaving your mouth feeling fresh and healthy. Between professional visits, maintaining good oral hygiene at home through brushing twice daily and flossing once helps protect your teeth and gums.

Each cosmetic treatment begins with a consultation to understand your goals and evaluate your oral health. We then create a customized plan to achieve the results you envision while maintaining the natural function of your teeth. Many patients find that enhancing their smile positively impacts how they present themselves at work and in social situations.

Life happens, and teeth can become damaged, decayed, or lost over time. Our restorative dentistry services help repair and replace teeth, restoring both function and aesthetics. Dental crowns and bridges protect weakened teeth and fill gaps left by missing teeth. For patients seeking permanent tooth replacement, dental implants offer a durable, natural-looking solution.

Modern restorative materials blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing both strength and beauty. CEREC technology allows us to create same-day crowns in certain cases, eliminating the need for temporary restorations and multiple appointments. This efficiency saves you time while delivering high-quality results.

Patients missing multiple teeth may benefit from dentures or partial dentures. These removable appliances restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
