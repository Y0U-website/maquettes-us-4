Dr. Elaha Bashizada, DDS, MS

Dr. Elaha Bashizada grew up in the beautiful state of Washington, where her early passion for healthcare and serving others first took root. Today, is dedicated to creating a positive, comfortable dental experiences for children and families—helping every child build a lifelong foundation for excellent oral health.

Dr. Bashizada earned both her Bachelor of Arts in Medical Anthropology and Global Health and her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Washington. Inspired by her desire to support healthy, confident smiles for young patients, she went on to complete advanced specialty training in pediatric dentistry. She completed her Pediatric Dentistry Residency at the University of Washington/Seattle Children’s Hospital, gaining extensive experience caring for children of all ages and diverse needs.

As a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist and Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry , Dr. Bashizada is committed to providing compassionate, evidence-based care in a warm, welcoming environment. Her goal is to ensure that every child feels safe, supported, and even excited about visiting the dentist.

Dr. Tiffany Jacobs, DDS

Dr. Tiffany Jacobs, a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist at The Kids Dentist Renton, is dedicated to delivering high-quality evidence-based care to her young patients. She creates a warm and enjoyable environment, ensuring their encounters are both memorable and comforting.

Dr. Tiffany graduated with a bachelor’s degree in Mathematics from Georgetown University, followed by her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Columbia University. With her pediatric dental residency at the Yale School of Medicine, where she served as Chief Resident, Dr. Tiffany brings a wealth of experience to her practice. Previously working in Oahu, Hawaii, she now proudly serves the Pacific Northwest community.

Dr. Tiffany’s commitment to giving back extends beyond her practice. She is gives of herself to others in her community and is especially passionate about enhancing access to care for lesser-served populations, locally and globally through her international service in Guatemala, Belize, and Ethiopia.

In her spare time, Dr. Tiffany enjoys exploring nature, practicing yoga, trying new restaurants, and cherishing moments with her husband and daughter.

Dr. Krisha Misa, DDS

Dr. Krisha Misa, DDS is a board-certified pediatric dentist who combines premium, high-quality dentistry with a calm, reassuring approach that helps kids feel safe and helps parents feel confident. She delivers thoughtful dentistry with warmth, patience, and a steady focus on doing what’s best for each child long-term.

Raised in Washington, Dr. Misa is proud to have built her education close to home – earning her Bachelor of Science and Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Washington. She then pursued advanced specialty training in pediatric dentistry at the University of Illinois, where she also completed a Master’s degree, deepening both her clinical expertise and her commitment to evidence-based, child-centered care.

A core focus of Dr. Misa’s care is supporting children with dental anxiety. She understands that fear can show up as tears, silence, resistance, or “wiggles,” and she never treats those moments like misbehavior. Instead, she uses a child-centered, trust-building approach – meeting kids where they are, moving at a pace that feels safe, and explaining what’s happening in a way that makes sense to both children and parents. Her goal is not just to finish an appointment; it’s to help children leave feeling proud, capable, and more comfortable the next time.

Outside the office, you’ll usually find her hunting down a great new restaurant with her husband (she’s serious about a good noodle spot), chasing fresh powder on a snowboard when the season hits, or capturing everyday moments through photography.

Dr. Jared Roberts, DDS

Dr. Jared Roberts completed a Bachelor’s degree at Brigham Young University–Idaho and then went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry (Dr. Brian’s favorite University of all time, GO BLUE!). He practiced as a general dentist in Auburn where he found a passion for working with the pediatric community and decided to complete a specialty training program at the University of Rochester, Eastman Institute for Oral Health, becoming a certified specialist in Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Jared is a Washington native growing up in the Auburn and Lake Tapps communities. After graduating from high school, he spent two years in Argentina serving the people there in many different ways and came back fluent in Spanish. Dr. Jared is an avid mountain biker and outdoor enthusiast. He is always ready to go explore nature, go on a white water rafting adventure, or play on the water with family and friends. He and his wife, Crystal, are thrilled to be coming back to the PNW with their 3 children in tow.

Dr. Brian Dansie, DDS, MS

Dr. Dansie, a board-certified pediatric dentist, grew up in a small town in rural Utah. He attended dental school at the Ohio State University in Columbus, Ohio – Go Buckeyes! After realizing that pediatric dentistry was his favorite part of his dental education, he completed a 2-year pediatric dental residency at Nationwide Children’s Hospital and a master’s degree at Ohio State focusing on the oral health of premature infants and children with special needs.

He was raised hiking, camping, and four-wheeling all over the Rocky Mountains. He enjoys the great outdoors and getting some fresh air in nature. He played basketball and football growing up and still coaches his kids in basketball, baseball, football, and soccer. Dr. Brian and his wife, Rachel relocated to the Pacific Northwest from California with their three children in 2015.

If you ask him why he went into pediatric dentistry, the answer is simple. He loves kids! His first rotation in dental school was in pediatrics, and he knew instantly that nothing else in dentistry would be as rewarding to him as working with children. He genuinely enjoys laughing and joking with kids all day. He believes that instilling confidence and self-worth in a child can start at the dental office with caring and compassionate pediatric dentists.
