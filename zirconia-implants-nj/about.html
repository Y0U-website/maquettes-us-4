About

Dr. Shumantov’s Qualifications and Experience: Dr. Veselin Shumantov holds a dual DDS degree and is a licensed dentist in Europe and the United States. Dr. Shumantov received his U.S. training at NYU College of Dentistry. He has been in practice since 1999 when he graduated from the Dental School at Medical University in Sofia, Bulgaria.

Dr. Shumantov comes from a family of dentists and was exposed early to the professional dental field. His father was the Dental Clinic Director of the hospital at the Ministry of Internal Affairs, and his mother is still actively practicing dentistry in Sofia.

From his parents, Veselin learned not to judge other dentist’s work because one cannot know the circumstances under which the dentist had performed it.

Dr. Shumantov has been active in communicating, learning, and collaborating with the most prominent dentists in New York City, Europe, and Asia. He has a large network of dental specialists that he has learned from and consults with regularly. He often refers to his mentors and is grateful for their inspiration and guidance.

Using a personal touch, interaction, humanity, common sense, skills, knowledge, and technology, Dr. Shumantov provides the safest and most esthetic dentistry that is available today. He enjoys creating individualized, simple, or multi-disciplinary treatment plans, customizing them according to each patient’s priorities and possibilities. Dr. Shumantov executes dentistry with precision, attention to detail, and artistic finish.

Dr. Shumantov is the founder of the Center for Advanced Dentistry, a dental practice that offers both general dentistry as well as specialized dental procedures.

Dental phobia : Fear of the dentist is a problem that prevents many patients from achieving their dream smile. One convenient option is sleep dentistry which includes an IV sedation with a specialist anesthesiologist. Although this is a valid option that the Center for Advanced Dentistry offers, it does carry certain risks.

Dr. Shumantov prefers different and safer approach: one-on-one desensitization and elimination of the fear by changing the patient’s perception of the dentist as a threat . It is not uncommon for Dr. Shumantov’s patients to fall asleep in the dental chair while having a dental procedure performed.

References : References, feedback, and contacts from patients around the globe are available upon request.

Consulting: Dr. Shumantov is a consultant for All Experts on implant dentistry.

TV appearances: Foxnews A Healthy You with Carol Alt featuring Dr. Veselin Shumantov and nutrition expert Dr. Nicholas Gonzalez .

Teaching: Ceraroot zirconia ceramic implants certification courses in New York, USA.
