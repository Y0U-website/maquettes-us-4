The practice

Our goal is to provide state-of-the-art general dentistry in a warm, comfortable environment. We want to give you the highest quality dentistry in the most comfortable way so that you can enjoy a lifetime with a healthy smile. The dental professionals at Verde River Dental are committed to achieving the smile you have envisioned, and we actively seek out the newest technology available to help us…

We believe the best way to serve our patients is to develop a unique and specific treatment plan for every patient who walks through our door. We accomplish this by identifying your needs and wants, while establishing a relationship with you and your family. This is how we ensure consistent results for our patients.

We use the state-of-the-art technology to help our patients receive the optimal result with minimum inconvenience. However, no technology can replace an excellent dental team.

Our office is conveniently located on Main Street, near Old Town Cottonwood and Riverfront Park.

0 Our Services General Dentistry We take a comprehensive approach to your oral health to always provide you with more than one option when it comes to your plan. This allows you to choose the treatment that is right for you. Our team performs a wide variety of dental procedures to provide you with the best care while keeping your best interests in mind.

We can perform a variety of cosmetic procedures to improve your smile - from subtle changes to major repairs. Teeth can be reshaped, spaces can be closed, and worn/misshapen teeth can be restored. Procedures include teeth whitening, crowns, veneers, bonding, and reshaping.

When it comes to dental emergencies, we strive to address the problem and develop a plan that will relieve you of discomfort as soon as possible. Often times, we can begin same day treatment to quickly address any infection or pain. We are also available after hours to discuss any urgent dental issues on the phone with you.

Preventive dental care is important to maintain throughout your life, no matter your age or dental condition. By scheduling regular maintenance cleanings and check-ups with our team, and by practicing good home hygiene, you can keep your smile bright and healthy for many years to come!
