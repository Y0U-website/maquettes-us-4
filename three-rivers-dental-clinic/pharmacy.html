Pharmacy

Our care aims to cater to the unique needs of each individual patient. Need to lower the cost of your medications? We’ve got you covered.

  • Weight Loss Program: Our pharmacy offers a range of injectable weight loss medications, administered under the guidance of our experienced pharmacists. These options provide an effective complement to your weight loss journey, tailored to meet your specific needs and goals.
  • Immunization: Stay protected with our comprehensive immunization services. From flu shots to travel vaccines, we offer a variety of immunizations to keep you and your loved ones healthy and safe. Our trained pharmacists are here to answer your questions and provide personalized recommendations based on your individual health needs and concerns.
  • ED Treatment: Three Rivers Pharmacy offers a low-cost alternative to Viagra, Sildenafil, that doesn’t require insurance.

About our Mail order service

  • Convenience - With our mail-order service, you can conveniently order your medications from the comfort of your own home.
  • Time-saving - Skip the trip to the pharmacy by having your medications delivered directly to your doorstep.
  • Accessibility - Access to a wide range of prescription medications and over-the-counter products.
  • Cost-effectiveness - Save money on your prescriptions with competitive pricing and potential discounts.
