Behavioral Health

  • Primary Care Behavioral Health, where behavioral health providers are integrated into the primary medical care services at TRHC
  • Psychiatric Evaluation and Medication Management services for adult and pediatric patients
  • Care Coordination services to help you connect with services available from us but also in the community outside our clinic
  • Behavioral Health Deflection, including case management and peer support services delivered in collaboration with tribal law enforcement to help individuals with behavioral health needs focus on recovery and avoid the criminal justice system
  • Coming soon: support groups and wellness classes to meet our community needs. Check the website for updates as groups are announced!
