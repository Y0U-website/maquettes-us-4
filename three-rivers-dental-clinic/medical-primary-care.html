  • chronic disease management, and
  • providing acute care

Pediatric Care

Our Pediatric Care team is devoted to the health and happiness of our future generation. We focus on creating a warm, welcoming environment where children and their families feel supported and informed. Offering a range of services from:

  • Care to our most fragile children, our newborns
  • Yearly well child checks to prevent long term illnesses (2-18 years old)
  • Providing care for your child’s immediate illnesses (e.g. fever, sore throat, ear pain, cuts, falls and more)
  • Providing care for children of all ages who have chronic illnesses (e.g. asthma, allergies, diabetes, depression, anxiety and much more)
  • Providing care for our children with developmental challenges
  • Providing care for our children with learning problems in school
  • Providing care for our children with worries, anger, irritability, or other challenges with their lives
  • Providing comprehensive care for our adolescent children
