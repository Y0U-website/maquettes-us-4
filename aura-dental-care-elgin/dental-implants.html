What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a modern solution for missing teeth, durable and natural looking. These small titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone and serve as an anchor for replacement teeth like crowns or dentures . Unlike other options, implants integrate with the natural bone through a process called osseointegration for a stable and long-lasting result. This is why dental implants are the go-to solution for those looking to improve oral health and get their smile back.

In addition to being strong, dental implants look and function like natural teeth. They provide a seamless restoration so adjacent teeth are not affected and bone loss in the jaw is prevented. If you’re tired of dealing with the hassle of missing teeth or partial dentures, dental implants might be the answer you’ve been looking for.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants have many benefits for patients looking to replace missing teeth. Beyond the aesthetics, implants improve oral health, function, and overall well-being. They integrate with your natural bone for a stable base for long-lasting restorations.

  • Confidence You can smile, speak, and eat with confidence knowing your replacement teeth are fixed.
  • Oral Health Implants prevent bone loss and preserve the integrity of adjacent teeth by filling the gap of missing teeth.
  • Durability and Longevity With proper care dental implants can last a lifetime making them a cost-effective solution in the long run.
  • Natural Look and Feel Implants look and function like natural teeth for ultimate comfort and aesthetics.
  • Better Functionality Eat what you want without worrying about dentures shifting or discomfort.

The Dental Implant Process at Aura Dental Care

At Aura Dental Care we put your comfort and success first throughout the entire dental implant process. Our step-by-step process will guide you through every step of your treatment.

  • Consultation and Evaluation During your first visit, we will discuss your goals, review X-rays, and determine if you’re a candidate for dental implants. Additional procedures like bone grafting may be needed.
  • Surgical Placement The implant is placed into the jawbone in a simple oral surgery procedure. Healing takes several months as the implant integrates with the natural bone.
  • Healing and Osseointegration This critical step allows the implant to fuse with the jawbone. We will monitor your progress and guide you through the healing process.
  • Abutment Placement Once healed a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant, ready to support the replacement tooth.
  • Final Restoration A custom-made crown , bridge, or denture is attached to the abutment and you have your new smile.

FAQs

Thinking of getting dental implants? Here are some questions to help you decide:

How many dental implants will I need?

This depends on your situation. Single tooth replacement requires one implant, implant implant-supported dentures may need several.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a high success rate, often above 95 % when placed by an experienced dentist and properly cared for.

Are dental implants expensive?

The upfront cost may seem high but implants are a long-term solution that can save you money in the long run.

Is it painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. We will provide you with pain management options to ensure your comfort.

How do I take care of my dental implants?

Proper care includes brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. This will help maintain your implants and prevent complications like infection.
