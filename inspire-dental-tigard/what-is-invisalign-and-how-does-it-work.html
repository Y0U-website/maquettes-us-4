Understanding Invisalign

Invisalign, offered at Inspire Dental in Tigard, Oregon, is a modern orthodontic treatment using clear, removable aligners to gradually align teeth.

Invisalign Treatment Process

Remember, the duration of treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case but typically ranges from 12 to 18 months. Contact Inspire Dental to schedule a consultation and start your journey to a straighter smile with Invisalign.

Step 1: Consultation

Schedule a consultation at Inspire Dental. Our team will assess your oral health and discuss your goals to determine if Invisalign is the right fit for you.

Step 1: Consultation

Step 2: Digital Impressions

If you’re a suitable candidate, we’ll take digital impressions of your teeth. These impressions are used to create your personalized treatment plan and design your custom aligners.

Step 2: Digital Impressions

Step 3: Receive Your Custom Aligners

You’ll receive a series of custom-made aligners, each designed to make slight adjustments to the position of your teeth.

Step 3: Receive Your Custom Aligners

Step 4: Wear Each Aligner

Wear each set of aligners for 1-2 weeks before moving on to the next set in the series. The aligners should be worn for 20-22 hours per day, removed only for eating, brushing, and flossing.

Step 4: Wear Each Aligner

Step 5: Regular Check-Ins

Attend regular check-ins with our team to monitor your progress. These appointments also allow us to make any necessary adjustments to your treatment plan.

Step 5: Regular Check-Ins

Step 6: Completion of Treatment

Once you’ve gone through all the aligners in your series, your treatment is complete! You’ll be left with a beautifully straight smile.

Step 6: Completion of Treatment
