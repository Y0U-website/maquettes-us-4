More Than a Dental Office — A Legacy of Care

Inspire Dental has been a part of the Tigard community for more than 50 years. In that time, a lot has changed — but our commitment to putting patients first never has.

Our Vision

We believe every smile has the power to shine. That’s why we’re here—to gently support and empower our patients on their journey toward a healthier, more confident smile. Our team is guided by kindness, care, and a simple goal: to always do good, and grow better together.

Our Mission

Our mission is to put patient care at the heart of all we do. Through personalized and compassionate treatment, we help each patient take meaningful steps toward lasting health and wellness. With empathy, intention, and consistency, we aim to make a difference—one smile at a time.
