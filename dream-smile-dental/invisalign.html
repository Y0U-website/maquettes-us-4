What is the Invisalign Treatment Process?

Once you start wearing your aligners, they will gradually move your teeth in the desired direction. Once you have worn your aligners for 7-14 days, you should switch to the next set of numbered aligners. This way, you will see a visible improvement in your smile and tooth alignment with each aligner set.

That’s it! You are now a proud owner of a beautiful smile!

How Invisalign Aligners Straighten Teeth?

Unlike metal braces that remain fixed to the teeth, Invisalign aligners are removable. They are made of medical-grade, flexible, and transparent polymers. Once the aligners are worn over the teeth, they will exert controlled and precise pressure on your teeth using the SmartForce technology. Furthermore, since the aligners are flexible, they won’t cause significant pain and discomfort during treatment, unlike braces.

How Long Does Treatment with Invisalign Take?

The time required for Invisalign aligners to fix an orthodontic issue depends on various factors, such as the severity of the underlying issue, the patient’s age and compliance, and, more importantly, the skill and experience of the dentist. However, mild to moderate orthodontic issues typically take 6-12 months for treatment with Invisalign, while more complex issues may take 24-36 months. We will give you an estimate of your treatment duration after clinical examination and treatment planning.
