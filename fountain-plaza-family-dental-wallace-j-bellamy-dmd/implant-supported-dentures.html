Preparation & Procedure For Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures involve placing and securing dental implants into the jawbone to form a stable base for the denture. This quick, innovative process can truly make all the difference in how your denture fits and functions—drastically expanding the abilities of the denture while also providing unmatched comfort and security.

The procedure involves surgically placing between four and six implants into the jawbone across the dental arch. These implants act as a secure anchor for the denture, which can then be adhered to the implants and adjusted for comfort and fit.
