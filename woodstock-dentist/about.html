Frequently Asked Questions

It is recommended to get a dental cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health and prevent dental issues.

During a dental cleaning, your dentist or hygienist will remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and perform a thorough examination of your oral health.

Dental cleanings are typically not painful. However, you may experience slight discomfort or sensitivity during the cleaning process, especially if you have sensitive teeth or gum disease.

While dental cleanings can remove surface stains, they are not designed to whiten teeth. If you are looking for a whiter smile, your dentist may recommend professional teeth whitening treatments.

Most dental insurance plans cover routine dental cleanings as part of preventive care. However, it is always best to check with your insurance provider to understand your specific coverage.
