An implant procedure is a cost-effective treatment that can be time-consuming, considering the patient’s dental condition. After a complete dental checkup of the patient’s dental health, the dentists initiate the process. Some patients may require the extraction of the tooth before the implants are placed in the mouth. Once the teeth have been removed, the patient must undergo a healing period.

In the subsequent appointment, the dentist provides anesthesia to the patient, which helps numb the treatment area. It alleviates any discomfort caused during the procedure. A small incision is made on the jawbone and the implant is placed inside. A special drill is used for the surgical procedure. The dentists advise a healing period of several weeks before proceeding to the next step.

Once the implant has bonded well with the jawbone, the dentist places an abutment over the implant, which helps to hold the artificial tooth. As per the patient’s preference, a crown or a denture is fabricated to provide a perfect fit for the patient. It is placed on the abutment to provide a functional tooth to the patient.
