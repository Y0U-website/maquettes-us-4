Meet Ammera Martin, DDS.

Following dental school, I pursued advanced post-graduate training at the Kois Center, where I refined my ability to assess how gum and bone health, tooth structure, bite alignment, and facial aesthetics all work together — and how imbalances in any of these areas can affect not just a patient’s smile, but their overall health. I recommend treatment based on the best available science, so patients can feel confident they’re receiving care that is thoughtful, proven, and tailored to their long-term wellbeing.

Outside of the office, I enjoy spending time with my husband and two young children. We love exploring the Pacific Northwest — hiking, mushroom hunting, sailing, and photography are among my favorite pastimes.
