Meet Tyler Fox, DDS.

Dr. Tyler Fox is a board-certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon and Diplomate of the American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery. He is passionate about providing exceptional surgical care while helping every patient feel comfortable, informed, and confident throughout their treatment.

Originally from Temecula, California, Dr. Fox later moved to Eugene, Oregon, where he completed his undergraduate education and met his wife. Together they embarked on the next chapter of life as he pursued his dental education at the University of Utah, earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. He then completed an intensive four-year Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery residency at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center in Memphis, where he received comprehensive training in the full scope of oral and maxillofacial surgery.

In addition to his surgical training, Dr. Fox proudly serves in the United States Navy. His military experience has reinforced the values of service, integrity, teamwork, and excellence—principles he brings to every patient interaction.

Dr. Fox enjoys all aspects of oral and maxillofacial surgery, including wisdom tooth removal, dental implants, bone grafting, oral pathology, facial trauma, and complex dentoalveolar procedures. What he values most is helping patients overcome their anxiety by providing compassionate care, clear communication, and a comfortable surgical experience from the first consultation through recovery.

Outside of the office, Dr. Fox and his wife are raising three wonderful children—two boys and a daughter. As a family, they love taking advantage of the Pacific Northwest by fishing, camping, boating, jet skiing, swimming, and spending as much time outdoors as possible. Dr. Fox also has a lifelong passion for athletics, having played collegiate basketball and competed in track and field.

Dr. Fox is excited to join the team at this practice and looks forward to serving the community. He is committed to treating every patient with the same care and respect he would want for his own family, providing skilled surgical care in a welcoming, friendly, and compassionate environment.
