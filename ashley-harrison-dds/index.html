Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible materials, such as titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone. Once the implant is positioned, it undergoes a process called osseointegration, where it fuses with the bone. This creates a stable foundation for artificial teeth or other tooth replacements like a bridge or denture.

Because dental implants are integrated into the jawbone, they provide unmatched stability and strength compared to other tooth replacement options. They function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

At Ashley Harrison DDS, we offer a range of dental implant procedures to accommodate different needs and goals. Whether you need to replace a single tooth or require a more complex restoration, we have the expertise to develop a tailored treatment plan.
