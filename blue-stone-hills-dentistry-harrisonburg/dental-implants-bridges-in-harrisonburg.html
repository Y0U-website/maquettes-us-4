Dental Implants Bridges…

  • Abutments – Connectors that attach the implant screws to the bridge.
  • Bridge – Custom-made replacement teeth are fitted seamlessly over the abutments.

Procedure Steps

  • Initial Consultation: Our dentist in Harrisonburg, VA, will start by assessing your mouth, taking X-rays, and creating an individualized treatment plan.
  • Placing the Implants: The implants are installed into the jawbone using a dental drill where the bridge will be anchored.
  • Osseointegration: The implants require sufficient time to fuse with the jawbone—this can take 3 to 6 months.
  • Abutment Attachment: After this period, the connectors are attached to the implants.
  • I mpressions and Bridge Creation: Impressions of your mouth are taken to craft a structure.
  • Final Placement: Your finalized bridge is securely attached to the abutments, completing the procedure.

Who is a Good Candidate?

  • You’re looking to replace several missing teeth (in a row).
  • You have healthy gums and bone.
  • You practice good oral hygiene.
