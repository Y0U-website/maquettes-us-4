Dental Implants Harrisonburg

  • Subperiosteal: Positioned under the gums but above the bone, a great option for patients with insufficient bone height but cannot undergo bone grafting.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Implants look and feel like your own teeth.
  • They can last a lifetime (with proper care).
  • Better chewing ability.
  • Stimulates the jawbone, preventing bone loss.
  • Don’t impact nearby teeth.

The Implant Process

  • Consultation and Planning: X-rays and exams to determine if you are a good candidate. If not, our dentist in Harrisonburg , VA, will work with you to choose an alternative.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium screw is surgically placed into the jaw.
  • Healing: Osseointegration takes place over 3-6 months.
  • Abutment Placement: A piece is added to connect the screw with the visible prosthetic.
  • Crown Placement: Your custom-made prosthetic (crown, bridge, or dentures is attached.

Are They Right for You?

  • Healthy gums
  • Enough bone to support the implant (or willingness to get a bone graft)
  • Good oral hygiene habits

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does implant dentistry treatment take near you? Usually, the timeline is approximately 3-6 months that covers the healing time and the process to integrate the implant in the bone to a final restoration. Who is a good candidate for dental implants in Harrisonburg? Optimal candidates have healthy gums, have enough bone density, and are able to perform their routine oral hygiene.
