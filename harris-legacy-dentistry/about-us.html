Meet Our Friendly Dentists In Warrenton

At Harris Legacy Dentistry, we believe that a visit to the dentist should feel like visiting family. Led by dentists Dr. Jeffrey Harris and Dr. Paul Harris, our practice brings over 65 years of combined dental expertise to our neighbors in Warrenton. We aren’t just a dental office; we are a team of personable, fun professionals who are deeply committed to the health and happiness of our community.

Our legacy is built on a foundation of clinical excellence and the lasting relationships we form with our patients. We take pride in offering patient-centric, quality-focused care in an environment where you are more than just a name on a chart—you are a vital part of our dental family.

Our Trusted team

Dr. Paul Harris, DDS

Having achieved his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland at Baltimore, College of Dental Surgery, Dr. Paul Harris has been providing quality-focused, patient-centric general dentistry for 26 years.He’s thrilled to be part of Harris Legacy Dentistry and calls it his dream practice! Dr. Paul Harris has been married over 20 years to wife Sofia, and has two children (and a dog)!

Dr. Jeffrey A. Harris, DDS

Dr. Jeffrey Harris earned his Bachelor of Arts (Psychology) degree at the University of Virginia before achieving his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the Medical College of Virginia. He served overseas for three years as a dentist in the United States Army (Captain) and has been practicing dentistry for almost five decades. He is a long-time member of the American Dental Association.
