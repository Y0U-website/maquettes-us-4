Maintain An Attractive And Healthy Smile With General Dentistry

General dentistry is vital in maintaining and improving oral health for people of all ages. At Harris Legacy Dentistry in Warrenton, we focus on preventing, diagnosing, and treating various dental issues. Regular visits are essential for keeping your smile bright and healthy, as we offer a wide range of treatments and services to address diverse dental needs.

What are the benefits of general dentistry?

Regular dental checkups and cleanings allow us to catch potential issues early on, preventing them from worsening and requiring more extensive treatments later. Routine preventive care reduces your risk of tooth loss and other complications. General dentistry includes cosmetic treatments like teeth whitening and dental bonding, which improve the appearance of teeth, boosting confidence and self-esteem. Oral health is linked to overall health, with studies showing connections between gum disease and conditions like heart disease and diabetes.

What is general dentistry?

General dentistry encompasses a broad spectrum of dental services promoting oral health and preventing dental problems. Our dentists diagnose and treat common dental issues such as tooth decay, gum disease, and oral infections. We’re proud to be the frontline guardians of your oral health, ensuring that your teeth and gums stay in great condition.

What treatments are involved?

Dental Checkups

Regular checkups are the cornerstone of general dentistry. We examine your teeth and gums, looking for signs of decay, cavities, or gum disease.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional dental cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup. This preventive measure helps reduce the risk of cavities and gum disease.

Fillings

If cavities are found during the checkup, our dentists can quickly and effectively treat them with tooth-colored fillings to restore the tooth’s integrity and appearance.

Root Canal Therapy

A root canal procedure may be necessary when a tooth’s pulp becomes infected or damaged. This treatment saves the natural tooth from extraction and alleviates pain caused by infection.

Gum Disease Treatment

Our dentists address gum disease through various methods, such as scaling and root planting, to remove bacteria and promote healing.

Extractions

In cases where a tooth is severely damaged or beyond repair, we may perform an extraction. This prevents the spread of infection and prepares the area for future dental restorations.
