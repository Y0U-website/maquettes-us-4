How All-On-4 Implant Treatment Works

Replacing a full set of teeth with conventional implants would require an implant for every missing tooth. All-On-4 streamlines this process by using just four implants to anchor each dental arch. This approach works even if you have no remaining teeth. According to the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, these four implants provide stable support for your new teeth and rarely require bone grafting. Since two of the implants are placed at an angle, they’re able to reach stronger areas of your jawbone for optimal support and durability.

The procedure itself is similar to getting standard implants, but usually much faster. During your first visit, we’ll take impressions and X-rays to plan your treatment. At your procedure appointment, any remaining unhealthy teeth are removed, your mouth is numbed with local anesthetic, and four titanium implants are placed in your jaw. These implants will quickly fuse to your bone, providing a secure foundation for your new teeth.
