Our Technology-Driven Approach to Implant Dentistry

At Innovative Smiles, we harness the power of advanced technology to ensure your procedure is as safe, precise, and minimally invasive as possible.

Every implant case begins with a Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scan. This powerful tool provides a detailed 3D image of your jaw, teeth, nerves, and sinuses. This eliminates any guesswork, allowing us to plan the exact position, angle, and depth of your implant for maximum safety and optimal results before the procedure even starts.

Using the data from your 3D scan, we can create a custom surgical guide. This template fits precisely over your teeth and guides the implant placement with incredible accuracy. This technique often allows for a smaller incision, which means less post-operative discomfort and a faster healing period for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

It is natural to have questions when considering dental implants. Here are answers to some common inquiries we hear at our Long Beach practice.

1. Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Your comfort is our top priority. The surgical site is completely numbed with local anesthetic, so you will not feel pain during the procedure. We also offer sedation dentistry to help you relax. Most patients report that post-operative discomfort is mild and easily managed with over-the-counter pain medication for a few days, often comparing it to a simple tooth extraction.

Dental implants are considered a permanent fixture in your mouth. The titanium post itself can last a lifetime with proper care. The crown attached to the implant may need to be replaced after 10-15 years due to normal wear and tear, but the implant foundation remains secure in your jaw.

3. Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults who are missing one or more teeth are excellent candidates. The primary requirements are good general health and adequate jawbone to support the implant. Even if you have experienced bone loss, a bone grafting procedure can often make you eligible for treatment. We will conduct a thorough evaluation to determine the best course of action for you.

4. How do I care for my new dental implant?

Caring for an implant is remarkably simple—you treat it just like a natural tooth. This includes brushing twice a day, flossing daily (using special floss for implants if recommended), and attending regular dental checkups and cleanings. While an implant cannot get a cavity, maintaining healthy gums around it is crucial for long-term success.
