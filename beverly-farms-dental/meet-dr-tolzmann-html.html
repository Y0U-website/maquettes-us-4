Meet Dr. Maisie Tolzmann

As a member of our amazing team of doctors, Dr. Tolzmann shares our passion for helping patients with achieving their dream smiles. She finds it incredibly fulfilling to treat pearly whites to ensure they remain as beautiful and as healthy as possible in the long run. Her favorite part of the job is building strong connections with patients and working alongside an amazing team of dental experts. With a drive for improving teeth and providing a positive impact in the lives of others, she is a perfect fit for our team. Read on to learn more about her background and credentials!

Why I Became a Dentist

From the beginning, Dr. Tolzmann had a deep interest in pursuing a career in healthcare as she wanted to help others. However, it wasn’t until she visited her childhood dentist that she considered becoming one herself. After their suggestion, she decided to shadow other dental professionals and work in a dental office during her summer breaks throughout her undergraduate studies. It was these extremely positive experiences that sparked her interest in the field of dentistry. The rest, as they say, is history.

My Dental Education & Training

Dr. Tolzmann first attended the University of Wisconsin-Madison for her undergraduate degree. During this time, she worked in a dental practice performing sterilization and assisting. After graduating with her bachelor of science, she went on to study at the Marquette University School of Dentistry in Milwaukee, WI to earn her dental degree. She then completed a one-year general practice residency at the Cleveland Clinic, which also provided her with additional training in dental procedures, managing dental emergencies, and treating medically compromised patients.

Today, Dr. Tolzmann continues to seek advanced training to refine her skills and expertise. She’s also a proud and active member of several professional organizations, such as the American Dental Association, the Massachusetts Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD).

Life Away From the Dental Practice

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Tolzmann enjoys practicing yoga, reading, running, and cooking and baking. She also loves exploring new restaurants and museums, and has even traveled to Kazakhstan and Ethiopia. Her family lives in Wisconsin, and she loves visiting them whenever she can.
