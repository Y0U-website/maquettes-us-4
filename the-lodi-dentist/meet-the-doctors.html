Meet the Doctors

Providing Quality Cosmetic Dentistry In The Greater Lodi Area

David A. Neal, D.D.S.

Dr. David Neal was born and raised right here in Lodi. He received his Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of California at San Diego and graduated from the University of Iowa Dental School in 1990 with his Doctor of Dental Surgery. After completing his education, Dr. David Neal and his wife, Beth, returned to Lodi to establish his dental practice, The Lodi Dentist.

Dr Neal is a member of California Dental Society, American Dental Society, and San Joaquin Dental Society. He’s a former board member of the San Joaquin Dental Society and of the San Joaquin Dental Society.

In his free time, Dr Neal enjoys playing tennis and golfing along with spending time with his family.

Madison Hittle, D.D.S.

Dr. Hittle was born and raised in Lodi, California. She obtained her Bachelors of Science in Biological Sciences from the University of California Santa Barbara. She then went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with highest honors from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco.

Dr. Hittle strives for excellence in the dental profession by being an active member in the American Dental Association, California Dental Association and the San Joaquin Dental Society. Dr. Hittle’s goal is to provide comprehensive oral health services and education to the people of the Central Valley through evidence based practices and patient centered care. Dr. Hittle values spending time with each of her patient’s, in order to provide them with personalized dental care that enables them to build healthy habits and reach a state of oral health.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Hittle enjoys spending time with her family, traveling to new places and taking her dog for walks.
