Meet the doctor

Heather Campbell, DMD

Dr. Heather Campbell grew up in Gresham, Oregon and has family ties to Bend. She attended Oregon State University where she graduated at the top of her class with a degree in Botany and Molecular Genetics. While dentistry might seem like an unlikely profession after an education in agriculture, the transition after spending many of her younger years working at her fathers dental practice.

During her time at Oregon Health & Science University School of Dentistry she often found herself beaming after a long day in the clinic and realized she had found her calling. Years later this holds true. Dentistry combines many of her passions including science, health, artistry and natural beauty. Most of all she values the relationships she builds with her patients through a comfortable and cheerful environment, while focusing on their unique needs.

Outside of dentistry, Heather and her husband, Rand, have a hay farm in Tumalo and a cattle ranch in eastern Oregon where they spend a lot of time in the fresh air and under the stars. They can be found backpacking with their poodle, Phoebe, cooking for friends, reading, and snowboarding.
