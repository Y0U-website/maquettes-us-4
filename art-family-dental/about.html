Meet your compassionate neighborhood dentist

Personalized, artistic care with Dr. Mousami

Dr. Mousami’s journey into dentistry began in a multigenerational home in India, where compassion and service were values instilled at an early age. With a deep love for both science and art, she found her calling in dentistry, a profession that beautifully integrates both passions. After earning her Bachelor of Dental Surgery in India, she moved to the United States to complete the Advanced Standing Program at the New York College of Dentistry, earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree.

Since then, Dr. Mousami has spent the last seven years practicing in Austin, gaining experience in private practices, corporate dental offices, and federally qualified health centers. Each environment strengthened her vision for a practice that offers quality care built on trust, clear communication, and patient comfort.

Driven by her mission to deliver affordable, honest, and high-quality care to families, Dr. Mousami created Art Family Dental, a boutique space where every patient enjoys personalized attention in a calm, spa-like setting. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning or a restorative procedure, you’ll never feel rushed or overlooked.

At the heart of Dr. Mousami’s philosophy is prevention and early intervention. She believes in creating lasting relationships with patients of all ages, educating and guiding them toward better health and brighter smiles. Her favorite procedures include crowns and cosmetic enhancements, and she’s proud to offer state-of-the-art solutions like Spark Clear Aligners and soft tissue laser therapy.

Outside the office, Dr. Mousami cherishes time with her husband and young son. She enjoys traveling, watching movies, and getting lost in a good fictional book. Her warm and approachable personality inspires confidence in her patients, and she looks forward to treating each family like her own.

Get ready to feel comfortable and confident

Your first visit will feel like home

From the moment you walk into Art Family Dental, our goal is to help you feel comfortable, informed, and cared for. We take time to get to know each patient, explain every step of your care, and tailor your visit around your needs. Whether it’s your first dentist visit or your first time in a while, we’re here to make the experience easy and encouraging.

Patient Experience Your first visit will include a warm welcome, a conversation about your oral health goals, and a comprehensive exam including X-rays and cleaning, unless periodontal treatment is needed. We pride ourselves on unrushed visits that let you feel seen, heard, and well cared for.

What to Bring Please bring the following to your visit:

  • Valid photo ID
  • Insurance card (if using insurance)
  • Completed new patient forms (paper or digital)
  • List of current medications

Modern comforts meet advanced care

Art Family Dental was designed to be a calming retreat from the typical dental office. Lush with modern features and thoughtful amenities, our boutique setup ensures your entire family receives focused, private care that feels more like a spa day than a dental appointment.

Massage chairs

Relax in our plush massage chairs while receiving treatment, designed to ease tension and keep you comfortable throughout your visit.

TVs in operatories with headphones

Catch up on your favorite show or unwind with calming music using our overhead TVs and headphones available in every treatment room.

Beverage station

Enjoy a refreshing drink while you wait, or relax with a good book.

Minimally-invasive approach

We’re proud to offer advanced, minimally-invasive treatments, like Curodont, to restore your smile while retaining as much of your natural tooth structure as possible.

Soft tissue laser therapy

Our advanced soft tissue laser provides gentle precision for treatments without the discomfort or long healing times of traditional methods.

AI-assisted imaging & cbct

Our AI-assisted imaging and CBCT technology give us a detailed 3D view of your smile, helping us diagnose with greater precision and plan treatment with confidence.
