What is preventive dental care?

Preventive care is a great way to take care of your smile.

Regular dental exams can help spot problems early, which increases the likelihood of a successful treatment. This can be anything from getting a cavity filled prior to developing toothache, an extraction before you develop infection, or fitting you with dental appliances such as aligners.

How often do you need preventive dental care?

You should get a dental checkup at least every six months.

Regular dental checkups are important for two reasons: one, it can help prevent and treat gum disease and two, it can stop cavities from causing damage to your teeth.

Benefits of preventive dental care

Everyone wants to have a good smile and healthy teeth. But, this is not possible without regular visits to the dentist.

Preventive dental care is important to prevent several oral problems and improve your oral health.

If you don’t have a regular cleaning, air polishing and X-rays then the idea is that you could possibly be at risk for getting gum disease or severe tooth decay. These are two things that if they aren’t taken care of immediately can lead to having to get a root canal or even a crown.

What does preventive dental care involves?

Between regular dental cleanings and exams, your dentist can detect problems before they become too serious. This is why it’s a great idea to get a chekup on a regular basis.
