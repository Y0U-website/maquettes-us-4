What are endodontic treatments?

Endodontic procedures aim to treat and save the teeth that have been already damaged by the dental cavities and tooth decay.

Endodontists treat problems with the nerves, roots, and surrounding tissue and then will advise their patients about the proper aftercare procedures for each method that will be previously used.

Root canal

Root canal treatment is one of the most common dental procedures performed by endodontists.

It involves the removal and cleansing of the pulp and infection from an infected root(s) of a tooth.

A root canal may be needed for multiple reasons, such as a cracked or broken tooth, toothache, persistent sensitivity to hot or cold foods and drinks and exposed roots due to deep decay.

This procedure is done with local anesthesia so the patient doesn’t feel any pain and only experiences slight discomfort.
