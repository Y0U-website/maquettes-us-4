Services

At Luzid Smiles, we offer a wide range of dental services to meet the needs of patients of all ages, including preventive, restorative, and cosmetic treatments. Some of our services include:

Routine Cleaning and Exams

Prevention is key to maintaining good oral health, and at Luzid Smiles, we offer a range of preventative dental treatments to help keep your teeth healthy and strong.

Fillings, Crowns, and Bridges

Dental crowns, filings and bridges are used to restore damaged teeth and provide strength and stability to your bite.

Teeth Whitening

Our cosmetic services include teeth whitening, veneers, and clear aligners, all designed to give you a brighter and more youthful smile.

Clear Aligners (Invisalign)

Our team at Luzid Smiles offers clear aligner treatments designed to help you achieve a straighter and more beautiful smile, so no outside referrals are needed.

Sedation dentistry

For patients who experience anxiety or need longer treatments, we offer sedation dentistry options to help them relax and feel more at ease.

We use the latest technology and techniques to ensure that our patients receive the best possible care. Our team is committed to staying up-to-date with the latest advancements in dentistry, and we constantly strive to improve our skills and knowledge to better serve our patients.
