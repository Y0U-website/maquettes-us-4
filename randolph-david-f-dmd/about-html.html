David F Randolph, DMD, MAGD

Dr. Randolph has proudly served this community and upheld a tradition of excellence in Lake City over the past 30 years.

Dr. Randolph received his dental degree from the University of Florida College of Dentistry in 1983. He opened this practice in the spring of 1984, and has been providing exceptional dental care ever since. Throughout his career he has completed thousands of hours of continuing education, ensuring he provides his patients with the best care possible.

Dr. Randolph is ardent about the services he provides, and his continuing education credentials are a testament to his values and dedication to both his patients and his field.

Dr. Randolph was awarded his Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry in 2000. It is the Academy’s highest honor and is held by only 3 % of general dentists. The following year, he completed his dental implants training at the University of Georgia, and in 2002 he was recognized as an Associate Fellow in the Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Dr. Randolph is an active member in the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Academy of Implant Dentistry, and the Florida Dental Association.

Amelia A. Randolph, DMD, MS

Dr. Amelia Randolph is proud to uphold the tradition of dental excellence set by her father and grandfather in this community.

Dr. Amelia grew up with dentistry, helping in her father’s practice and volunteering at the VA, where her grandfather was the head of dental services. Dr. Amelia received her bachelor’s degree from Southern Methodist University in 2006. Before entering dental school she earned her master’s in biomedical sciences from Barry University in Miami. She completed her dental training at Nova Southeastern University in the summer of 2012 and joined her father’s practice in July of that year.

Dr. Amelia has proved a valuable addition to the caring, professional service our office is known for, and has sought continuing education courses to remain at the forefront of emerging dental technologies and address all of your dental needs.

Her various advanced training includes cosmetic bonding, periodontal surgery, CAD/CAM prosthetic design and endodontics. In 2014 she completed training in administration of dermal fillers, helping to further enhance and frame your smile.

Dr. Amelia is an active member in the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and the Florida Dental Association.

Our History

Dentistry is in our blood.

Dr. David Randolph and his daughter Amelia are the 3rd and 4th generation dentists, and represent 2nd and 3rd generations practicing here in Lake City.

Dr. David Randolph’s grandfather Earl F Randolph graduated from University of Michigan’s School of Dentistry in 1911. He was in private practice in Niagara Falls, NY for over 40 years.

His son Donald F Randolph graduated from the University of Buffalo’s College of Dental Medicine in 1951. He joined his father’s private practice in Niagara Falls, where he worked until joining the VA hospital system.

In 1970 Donald Randolph and his family moved to Lake City, where he served at the VA until his retirement in 1985. He was chief of dental services for many of those years.

David F Randolph graduated from the University of Florida’s College of Dentistry in 1983. He opened his practice in early 1984, and has been serving the Lake City community ever since.

Amelia Randolph graduated from the College of Dental Medicine at Nova Southeastern University in 2012, and joined her father’s practice that summer.

The Randolph family represent over 4 generations of dental experience spanning over a century.
