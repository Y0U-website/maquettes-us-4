She then obtained her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Toronto in 2007 where she was the recipient of many awards, including the gold medal. She completed her Masters of Science and Diploma in Periodontics at the University of British Columbia in 2010. She received the Kathleen McAnulty Memorial prize for outstanding overall performance in the graduate program. She successfully completed her fellowship examinations in 2010 and is a Fellow of the Royal College of Dentists of Canada.

Periodontal disease is a chronic disease that leads to bone loss and eventual tooth loss. It is an inflammatory condition that is linked to many systemic health problems.

Dr. Lamberts performs many different surgeries, including surgeries to manage chronic periodontitis, gingival grafting, cosmetic gingival recontouring, etc.

Les soins

Dr. Lamberts is experienced in extraction of teeth, bone grafting and dental implant placement.
