Phase 1 Interceptive Ortho Treatment

Phase 1 Interceptive Ortho Treatment (ages 6-12)

Early orthodontic treatment allows correction of :

With the use of removable or fixed functional appliances we are able to guide children’s growth in attempt to:

  • Harmful oral habits (thumb sucking, tongue thrust)
  • Crossbites
  • Crowded teeth
  • Underdeveloped jaws
  • Mouth breathing
  • Deep overbite
  • Underbite
  • Jaw joint problems (causing headaches, ear aches, jaw clicking)
  • Speech problems
  • Snoring
  • Improve appearance and self-esteem
  • Guide permanent teeth into a more favourable position
  • Minimize the need for the future extractions or surgery
  • Reduce treatment time in fixed braces

Phase 2 Braces

Whether you went through Phase 1 interceptive orthodontic treatment or not, we can always help you achieve a beautiful smile and a healthy jaw joint with comprehensive orthodontic treatment with braces.

Invisalign

Invisalign is an orthodontic alternative to the traditional metal braces. They are removable and near invisible aligners which gradually straighten your teeth. Invisalign aligners can be removed for eating, drinking, brushing and flossing. Every Invisalign aligner is unique and is convenient, flexible, and most importantly, discreet. A series of clear aligners are created to help straighten your teeth stage by stage, and every 1-2 weeks, you will switch to a set of new aligners. Invisalign treatment will help improve your bite, alignment and achieve a beautiful smile.

  • Crowding/crooked teeth
  • Spacing/gaps
  • Overbite/deep bite
  • Underbite
  • Protruded teeth
  • Misaligned teeth
