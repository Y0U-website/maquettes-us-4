What Are Dental Implants?

They are titanium posts that act as an artificial tooth root fixed into the jawbone. Dental implants can help stimulate the jawbone and stabilize dental restorations like dentures, bridges or crowns.

Dental implants offer many benefits, including restoring the aesthetics and function of your missing teeth. Implants will also help prevent your jawbone from deteriorating, which can happen as a result of missing teeth.

Most patients will qualify for dental implants if they are missing one or more teeth, although you will need to meet certain oral health requirements. We will first have to evaluate your health to ensure there are no issues that can affect the success of this treatment. If we detect gum disease or a weak jawbone, we will have to treat these issues before we proceed with dental implants.

Dental implants typically last several years, and with the right care, up to a lifetime. It requires only your normal effort to keep the area clean and there is very low risk of tooth decay and fracture. There may be a higher initial cost of treatment but lower maintenance costs over time.

Implant Retained Dentures

We use implants to secure your dentures in replacement of denture adhesives which is strong and secure. At Slipacoff Dental Center we have the right solution for you: Removable implant overdentures – This cost-effective option provides stability where new teeth are attached to two or more dental implants. This will not affect your chew and taste with variety of food. Your teeth can be removed for cleaning and denture adhesives is no longer needed.

Removable overdentures on an implant bar – For a more secure fit, a dental implant bar is secured to your jaw bone with two or more implants, which provides a stronger support for your removable dentures. Your new teeth can be removed for cleaning and denture adhesives is no longer needed.

Fixed implant supported bridges – A more durable option with natural-looking replacement teeth that is secured on four or more dental implants. This solution stimulates bone maintenance, restores chewing function, and maintains taste sensation.
