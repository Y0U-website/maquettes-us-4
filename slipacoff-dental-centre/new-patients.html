New Patients Welcome

Patient Convenience and Comfort

Our office is conveniently located at the corner of Indian Road and Michigan Road in Sarnia and has been for over 35 years. Although our location hasn’t changed, the technology we use certainly has. Advancements now allow us to get you in and out quicker, and we’ve equipped every room with in-office monitors so you can kick back and watch TV in peace while we work.

To make an appointment, you can call our office or request a date and time online , whichever is most convenient for you.

We Work With You

As part of our commitment to our patients, we work with you to achieve the maximum reimbursement for which you are entitled, complete and submit insurance forms on your behalf and provide you with accurate and timely information about any fees not covered by your plan. Latest Dentistry Advancements Technology

We make it a priority to stay abreast of the most current procedures and our entire team receives frequent training on the latest advances in dentistry.

We are continually investing in new technology to better our care and treatment for our patients. Our CEREC machine that allows us to measure, design, produce and install your crown in just one appointment.

As well, our CBCT 3D CT Scanner is the only scanner in Lambton County. This machine helps with evaluating jaws, sinuses, nerve canals and nasal cavities for accurate placement of dental implants, detecting, measuring and treating jaw tumours as well as determining bone structure and tooth orientation.
