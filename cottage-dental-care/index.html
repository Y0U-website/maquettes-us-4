The practice

We are accepting new patients and are available for immediate dental care.

At Cottage Dental Care, we believe going to the dentist should feel comfortable, personal, and even a little enjoyable. Our friendly team takes the time to get to know you, listen to your needs, and make sure every visit leaves you smiling.

Patient-first approach : We take the time to listen, explain, and tailor treatment plans to your goals and needs.

Modern, gentle care: Using the latest dental technology and techniques, we deliver high-quality treatments from preventive cleanings to advanced restorative and cosmetic services.

Comfort & compassion: Our friendly team treats you like family. Whether it’s your first visit or your 50th, we strive to make each appointment relaxed and caring.

Lifelong oral health: Beyond treating immediate concerns, we focus on prevention, education, and positive habits so your smile thrives for years to come.

Our goal is to help you enjoy a healthy, confident smile for life. From kids to adults, we love caring for families in our community and building relationships that last for years.
