Dental Bonding

  • Protect the Root Resin can be used to protect a tooth’s root when it is exposed due to recession.
  • Strengthen Your Teeth – Broken and chipped teeth are at risk for further damage if you do nothing to fix them. Consider dental bonding as a way to strengthen your teeth and prevent further damage.
  • Whiten Stained Teeth – The resin color blends well with your teeth and can work to cover staining.
  • Cosmetic Fixes – Damaged teeth aren’t the only reason you might want bonding. The composite resin also works to close spaces between teeth or reshape them entirely.
  • An Affordable Option – Other methods for teeth repair get more expensive as you go. Tooth bonding, on the other hand, is affordable when compared to other options.
