What is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing are two parts of the same dental procedure. The end goal is to deep clean your teeth and gums to remove all plaque, hardened tartar and bacteria. However, such a procedure is more involved than your routine cleaning, as your dentist must get under your gums. Thus, our patients are put under local anesthesia. You will remain awake during the procedure, but your gums will be numbed for the process.

After scaling and root planing are complete, your dentist will wash out the problematic areas of your mouth, getting rid of any bacteria remaining. Finally, they will apply pressure to your teeth, ensuring gum tissue can adequately recover and grow.

  • Scaling – Either done manually or with an ultrasonic instrument, plaque and tartar are loosened and removed from between your gums, beneath your gums and at the base of the crowns of your teeth.
  • Root Planing – Tartar and plaque continue to be removed while cementum (calcified film) is cleaned directly off the roots of the teeth. Any rough surfaces on your teeth are smoothed out.
