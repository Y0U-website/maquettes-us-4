Types of Abutments/Attachments for Dental Implants

  • Screw-Retained Attachments: Offer a fixed and secure solution for tooth restoration, ensuring stability and eliminating movement.
  • Bar Attachments: Support removable dentures, providing increased comfort and confidence for the user.
  • Locator Attachments (Ball Attachments): Utilize a ball-and-socket design for straightforward denture removal and attachment, ensuring stability.
  • Magnetic Attachments: Enable a strong and easy-to-manage connection for removable dentures, simplifying insertion and removal.
  • Hexagonal Connections (External/Internal Hexagon): Define the implant’s connection shape, affecting restoration stability and placement ease.
  • Resilient Attachments (O-Rings or Stud Attachments): Provide added flexibility and comfort, enhancing the experience for those with implant-supported dentures.
  • Tapered Connections: Ensure a secure fit between the implant and restoration, reducing bacterial entry risk.
  • Custom Attachments: Designed specifically for individual needs, custom attachments guarantee an ideal fit and superior functionality.
