What to Expect During Treatment

Orthodontic treatment begins with an evaluation to assess alignment, bite, and overall oral health. Imaging and impressions may be taken to plan tooth movement accurately. During treatment, periodic adjustments or aligner updates guide teeth into the desired position. Regular visits allow progress to be monitored and adjustments to be made as needed. Maintaining oral hygiene and following instructions provided by your dentist helps ensure consistent results throughout the treatment process.
