Dental Implants An Overview

If a patient is missing one or more teeth, dental implants can provide a long-lasting, natural-looking solution to restore health and function to your smile. Maitland Ave Smile Co provides patients dental implants in Altamonte Springs FL. Dental implants provide a stable foundation to stabilize or even improve the bite and the function of the jaw. Patients can enjoy the benefit of a secure, comfortable, and natural-looking tooth replacement option that will also benefit long-term dental health.

We handle all aspects and phases of the dental implant process in-house. To determine if a patient is an ideal candidate for dental implants, restorative dentist Dr. Clay Miller will perform a thorough dental exam. If missing teeth are left untreated, patients have a higher risk of developing periodontal disease, tooth decay, jaw bone deterioration, facial sagging, tooth damage, and additional tooth loss. Replacing missing teeth can improve oral health, restore confidence, and prevent further damage.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are one of the most natural looking, successful tooth replacement options on the market. Dental implants may be used to support a denture , dental bridge , or dental crown for added stability and comfort. Dental implants are designed to mimic the tooth root and add support for a final restoration. We offer all types of implants and emphasize the benefits they offer, including:

  • Dental implants can prevent bone deterioration and soft tissue loss.
  • Supporting a dental bridge or denture. Typically, dental bridges rely on adjacent teeth which may compromise the teeth in the future.
  • Dental implants can replace one or more missing teeth.
  • Dental implants have a 95 % success rate.
  • Long lasting, durable
  • Prevents facial sagging
  • Improved mobility
  • No dietary restrictions

Dental Implants What to Expect

The dental implant treatment process may differ from patient to patient, depending on oral health condition, bone structure and overall treatment plan. Our team of Altamonte Springs dentists will work with each patient to coordinate all phases of the process for the best possible result. The dental implant process can be explained in two phases.

After the dental implant process, patients should continue their normal oral hygiene routine to maintain a healthy smile. Dental implants should be treated just like regular teeth.

  • Single Tooth Dental Implant
  • Implant Secured Dental Bridge
  • Implant Secured Denture
  • Placing The Dental Implant During the surgical phase of the dental implant process, your dentist will strategically place the dental implant into the jaw bone. Over the course of 3-6 months, through a process called osseointegration, the dental implants will fuse into the jaw bone. The goal is to create a healthy, stable foundation to promote a long lasting, natural looking final restoration solution. In some cases, patients may be able to receive their dental implants and final restoration in one visit.
  • Placing The Dental Implant Restoration The next phase of the dental implant process is the final restoration procedure. Once the dental implants complete the osseointegration process, Dr. Miller will attach the abutment to each dental implant. Once the abutment is placed, a final restoration is permanently secured. Depending on whether a patient is missing one or several teeth, Dr. Miller may attach a dental crown, denture, or dental bridge.
