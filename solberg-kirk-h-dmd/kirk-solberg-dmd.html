Meet Dr. Kirk Solberg

Dr. Kirk Solberg graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of Florida College of Dentistry in 1992. He was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon honorary dental society, earned multiple leadership awards, served as class president for 3 years, and was presented with the American College of Dentists Award for outstanding ethics and professionalism.

Upon graduation, he returned to Altamonte Springs and joined Dr. Williams’ established dental office as an associate, where an ideal business relationship matured into friendship, partnership, and life mentor. While delivering high-quality dentistry is vital, his primary focus is building life-long relationships with his patients based on communication, trust, and compassion.

Dr. Kirk Solberg met his future wife, Michelle, in the 10th grade, before graduating from Lake Mary High School in the first class of 1984. They are blessed with two wonderful children, Samantha and Erik. Dr. Solberg enjoys everything outdoors, particularly his role of assistant scoutmaster and chaplain for his son’s Boy Scout troop, as well as serving in various ministries as camp cook, coach, and mentor, both in and outside of his Northland church home.
