Our Office - Family & Cosmetic Dentistry in Altamonte Springs, FL

Our office at Maitland Ave Smile Co. in Altamonte Springs, FL is a patient-centered dental practice dedicated to the health and wellness of each of our patients. We offer a selection of general, restorative, cosmetic, and pediatric services tailored to your dental needs, goals, and oral health. We are committed to helping our patients achieve, maintain, or restore a healthy, functional smile.

Dr. Clay Miller and Dr. Kirk Solberg are experienced, compassionate dentists who listen to your dental concerns, understand your goals, and create personalized treatment plans based on your needs. We focus on disease prevention and patient education to equip our patients with the tools they need to enjoy long-term oral health.

Our Altamonte Springs dental office is equipped with state of the art technology to provide patients with predictable, long-lasting results. We offer same day dental restoration using CEREC technology, 3-D imaging, and full-service dental implants. Our technology allows for a more conservative, less invasive approach to dental care.
