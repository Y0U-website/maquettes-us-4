The practice

Dr. Ryan Davis is a Board Certified Pediatric Dentist from the Philadelphia suburbs. He graduated from Temple University’s Dental School in 2016. After dental school Dr. Ryan Davis served as a dentist in the United States Navy. While in the military he and his wife lived in San Diego, California, Okinawa, Japan, and Quantico, Virginia.

Dr. Ryan Davis is a graduate from Temple University Hospital’s Pediatric Dental Residency where he was the Chief Resident.

When Dr. Ryan Davis is not at his office, he is working at Special Smiles LTD, which is a dental clinic dedicated to the treatment of special needs individuals – both children and adults! Interested in Special Smiles? Click Here .

Dr. Ryan Davis is also faculty at Einstein Medical Center Hospital, where he trains and educates dental residents.

When not working, Dr. Ryan Davis enjoys running, spending time with his family, and reading. Go Eagles! Dr. Ryan has completed five marathons! One of which was in Japan!

Pediatric Dental Excellence is a full service Pediatric Dental Office. We offer dental care from ages 0 to 17 years old, and do our best to accept most insurances. Every child deserves excellent dentistry – so come check us out!
