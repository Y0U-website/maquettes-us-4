Our Mission

to be a good steward and respect the environment and community we live in.

“Traveling has enriched our perspective on the value of conservation.”

A love of nature photography has led us to visit national parks, volcanoes, and underwater ocean environments. We love seeing wild animals in their natural habitat and want to do our small part to help preserve this beauty for future generations.

When we decided to build a commercial project in our community filled with lakes, wildlife, and park trails; we took it seriously. We didn’t want to be invasive to nature and wanted to help our community grow responsibly. We hope with leadership and help from the city of Baxter to become a recognized solution for future commercial developments.
