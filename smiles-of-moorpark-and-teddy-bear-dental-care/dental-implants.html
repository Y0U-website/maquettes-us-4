A dental implant consists of three components: a titanium post that serves as an artificial tooth root, an abutment that connects the post to the crown, and a custom-made porcelain crown that looks identical to your natural teeth. The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration. This creates an incredibly stable foundation that can last a lifetime with proper care.

Unlike dentures that can slip and cause embarrassment, or bridges that require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth, dental implants preserve your natural teeth and jawbone structure. They prevent bone loss that typically occurs when teeth are missing, maintaining your facial contours and youthful appearance.

Our Moorpark dental implant process begins with an examination and treatment planning. When 3D imaging is appropriate, it can help assess anatomy and plan implant placement. The recommended approach, materials, timeline, and maintenance depend on your oral health and treatment goals.

FAQs Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants How long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last 25 years or more. Many patients enjoy their implants for a lifetime. The crown may need replacement after 15-20 years due to normal wear.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients report less discomfort than they expected. We use local anesthesia during the procedure to ensure you&#x27;re completely comfortable. Post-operative discomfort is typically managed well with over-the-counter pain medication.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general health are candidates for dental implants. You need adequate bone density to support the implant, though bone grafting can help if needed. We&#x27;ll evaluate your candidacy during your consultation.

How much do dental implants cost in Moorpark?

Single dental implants typically range from $3,000-$5,000 including the crown. We offer flexible financing options and work with most PPO insurance plans. Contact us for a personalized quote.

What affects dental implant outcomes?

Outcomes depend on factors such as oral and general health, bone volume, smoking, hygiene, bite forces, and the restoration. We can discuss expected benefits, risks, alternatives, and maintenance during an evaluation.
