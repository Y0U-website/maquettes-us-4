Dental Services at Trinity Dental in Hoschton, GA

Serving Hoschton, Braselton, Winder, Jefferson, Flowery Branch, and Surrounding Communities

Preventative Dentistry

Routine dental exams, cleanings, and X-rays are the foundation of a healthy smile. Our preventative care helps you avoid costly treatments down the road by catching issues early and keeping your teeth and gums in top shape.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Want a brighter, more confident smile? We offer teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, and cosmetic bonding to help you look and feel your best. Every treatment is customized to your smile goals.

Restorative Dentistry

From fillings and crowns to full-mouth rehabilitation, our restorative services are designed to bring strength and function back to your smile. We use durable, natural-looking materials to ensure long-lasting results.

Dental implants are a permanent, secure solution for missing teeth. Whether you need a single implant or implant-supported dentures, our team will guide you through the process with precision and care.

Tooth pain, broken teeth, or sudden swelling? We offer same-day emergency appointments to relieve your discomfort and restore your dental health—fast.
