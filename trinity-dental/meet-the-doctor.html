Meet the Doctor

Meet Dr. Kamarin Moon – Founder of Trinity Dental

Dr. Kamarin Moon has dedicated her career to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles while forming genuine, lasting relationships with every individual she serves. Guided by compassion, skill, and a deep sense of purpose, Dr. Moon is proud to provide comprehensive dental care to families throughout Sells, GA, and surrounding communities.

Dental Implants in Sells, GA and Surrounding Areas – Sells, Calhoun, Aragon, Cave Spring, Rome

A Journey of Dedication and Excellence

Dr. Moon’s path in dentistry began with her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene degree from Augusta University in 2014. After three years of working as a hygienist, she pursued her lifelong dream of becoming a dentist. In 2021, she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from The Dental College of Georgia and went on to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) Residency at The Charlie Norwood Veterans Affairs in Augusta in 2022.

During her residency, Dr. Moon gained advanced clinical training in surgical extractions, dental implants, and other complex procedures, allowing her to deliver exceptional, evidence-based care to her patients.

Experience That Inspires Confidence

Following her residency, Dr. Moon worked as an associate at two dental practices where she developed her expertise in patient communication, advanced restorative techniques, and comprehensive treatment planning. Her experience reinforced her belief that dentistry is about more than clinical skill—it’s about connection, comfort, and care .

The Vision Behind Trinity Dental

Dr. Moon’s dream of creating a practice where patients truly feel at home inspired the founding of Trinity Dental. She envisioned a space built on trust, loyalty, and compassion, where every patient is treated like family. At Trinity Dental, you’ll experience personalized care that focuses not only on your smile but on your overall well-being.

Comprehensive, Compassionate Care Under One Roof

With her extensive training and genuine passion for patient wellness, Dr. Moon provides a full range of dental services—including preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, anterior root canals, and extractions—so patients can receive complete dental care in one convenient location. Whether you’re visiting for a routine checkup or a full-smile restoration, you can trust you’re in capable and caring hands.

Dr. Moon believes that dentistry is more than treating teeth—it’s about caring for people. Her greatest joy is getting to know her patients, understanding their goals, and being part of their oral health journey. Every patient who visits Trinity Dental can expect compassionate, honest care rooted in integrity and excellence.
