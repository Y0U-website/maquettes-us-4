What Are Dental Implants and How Do They Work?

Dental implants are titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots. Surgically placed into the jawbone, they support crowns, bridges, or dentures—offering a stable and lasting solution for missing teeth.

Our experienced team ensures each step—from implant placement to restoration—is done with precision and care.

  • Look, feel, and function like natural teeth
  • Help prevent bone loss and facial sagging
  • Improve speech and chewing ability
  • Eliminate the need for removable appliances
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care
