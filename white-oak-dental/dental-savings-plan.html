Dental Savings Plan

READY TO JOIN OUR DENTAL SAVINGS PLAN? Experience quality dental care in Ooltewah, TN without the hassle of traditional insurance. Call us today to learn how our Dental Savings Plan can benefit you and your family.

  • CONSIDERATIONS & LIMITATIONS This DSP is honored only at White Oak Dental and not at any other office.
  • Your plan’s effective date will be on file with our office.
  • Plans are not transferable or refundable.
  • No cash value for plans.
  • CareCredit financing is unavailable.
  • Existing patients must have a zero balance to qualify.
  • No discount on products.
  • Patients with active periodontal disease must have deep periodontal disease cleanings before being eligible for maintenance cleanings.
  • This plan is not insurance. The following are circumstances in which it cannot be used: With another dental plan or with dental insurance.
  • For treatment which, in the sole opinion of our doctors, lies outside the realm of their capability.
  • For referrals which are covered under automobile medical.
  • For services of injuries covered under worker’s compensation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

In addition, all other dental services are discounted 15 % , except for dental products.

  • What is the White Oak Dental Savings Plan? The Dental Savings Plan (DSP) is an affordable alternative to traditional dental insurance. It provides members with access to quality dental care without the hassle of claim forms, deductibles, or yearly maximums. Members enjoy discounted services and complimentary consultations directly through White Oak Dental.
  • How much does the plan cost and how long does it last? The plan is active for one year from the date of purchase. Pricing is based on household size: Individual: $159/year
  • +1 Person: $199/year
  • +2 People: $249/year
  • +3 People: $299/year
  • What services are included or discounted? Members receive no-charge services such as: One comprehensive exam (initial visit)
  • Two periodic exams (every six months)
  • One emergency exam
  • Routine X-rays
  • Oral ID cancer screening
  • Are there any restrictions or exclusions? Yes. The plan: Is valid only at White Oak Dental .
  • Cannot be combined with other dental insurance or discount plans.
  • Is non-transferable and non-refundable .
  • Cannot be used for services outside the dentist’s capability, or for injuries covered by workers’ compensation or automobile medical coverage.
  • Requires patients with periodontal disease to complete deep cleanings before maintenance cleanings are covered.
  • How do I join the plan or get more information? You can enroll by contacting White Oak Dental at 423-238-5744 . Once payment is received, your plan becomes active immediately, and your effective date will be kept on file at the office.
