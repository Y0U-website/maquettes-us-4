Dr. Chantelle Abrolat grew up in Bellingham, Washington with both parents as dentists. As a result, she became very familiar with a dental home and found her passion for dentistry while helping out in her parents’ dental office. She moved to California to start her own dental career path studying Biological Sciences with a minor in Spanish at the University of the Pacific in Stockton, California.

In her free time, Dr. Abrolat loves to spend her days outdoors with her wife, family, and friends. She loves to ski Palisades and Mammoth Mountain, relax fireside while camping in a national park, and bike around San Francisco. She also enjoys painting across different media like acrylic, watercolor, and gouache.
