Meet Dr. Michael D. Huffer

Michael D. Huffer D.M.D.

Dr. Huffer grew up in Aiken, SC. He attended the University of South Carolina where he received his BS in Chemical Engineering and then attended the Medical University of South Carolina School of Dental Medicine where he obtained his DMD. He completed a GPR at the University of Mississippi Medical Center in Jackson, MS and did his post-doctoral training in endodontics at the University of Alabama-Birmingham. He has practiced as an Endodontist in Columbia, South Carolina since 2013.

When away from dentistry, Michael likes to spend time with his family and friends, and tries to get on the golf course during his free time.
