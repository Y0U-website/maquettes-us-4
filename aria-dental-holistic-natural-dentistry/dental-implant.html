Dental Implant Maintenance & Longevity

Maintaining dental implants is essential to ensuring their long-term success and overall health. Although dental implants cannot decay like natural teeth, the surrounding gums and bone still require consistent care. Daily brushing, flossing around the implant, and routine professional cleanings help prevent inflammation and peri-implant disease. Regular visits to a qualified dental implants dentist allow for ongoing monitoring of bone stability, gum health, and bite alignment, key factors in protecting your investment and preserving implant function.
