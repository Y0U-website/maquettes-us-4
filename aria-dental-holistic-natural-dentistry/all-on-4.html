the concept of ALL-ON-4 ® system

Maintain optimal oral function and aesthetics with All-on-4 ®, also known as same-day teeth replacement. Using state-of-the-art technology, All-on-4 ® treatment replaces traditional dentures, which can be uncomfortable, inconvenient, and poorly fitted. All-on-4 ® turns problematic chewing and speaking into relics of the past.

Using only four dental implants to attach a full dental restoration, your doctor can give you the stable, functional, and cosmetic solution you’ve been looking for. Best of all, you have a set of teeth during procedure and many cases your dentures can be placed on the same day as your implants, so you don’t have to wait to get the restored smile you deserve.

Why All-on-4 ® Technique ?

  • Because All-on-4 ® allows full-arch restoration with only four implants, your treatment and recovery time are greatly reduced.
  • All-on-4 ® lowers the need for bone graft surgery while ensuring greater stability in the existing bone. It can even be used for patients who have been told they do not qualify for implants due to bone loss.
  • The All-on-4 ® solution is more comfortable than traditional dentures because All-on-4 ® implants are more secure and feel like natural teeth.
  • You’ll never have to worry about your false teeth slipping or falling out while you eat or engage in conversation.
  • There is no need for denture adhesive!

Fixed All-on-4 ® or (Teeth in 1 Day)

Teeth in one day is a newly innovative implant-supported system that consists of a non-removable prosthesis (denture) that attaches to 4-8 dental implants on the anterior side of the mouth arch for patients who have low density and limited jawbone.

Fixed Full-arch Implant

Fixed Full-Arch Implant-Supported Denture consists of 4-8 implants and a non-removable prosthetic denture made of ceramic, resin, or zirconium oxide. With fixed implant-supported dentures, there is no need for the fake palate to cover the roof or floor of your mouth. Best of all, you don’t have to take your dentures out or gag with your dentures.

Removable Bar-retained Implant Dentures

Stabilized dental implant overdenture system also known as a bar-retained (removable) implant denture uses a smaller number of dental implants to stabilize a removable denture. The number of dental implants used is done on a case-by-case basis.

Removable Implant-retained Dentures

Implant-supported overdenture also known as the implant-retained denture is a removable prosthesis that attaches to 4 or more dental implants but can be removable by the patient for cleaning purposes. The overdenture will snap into place on the bar attachment. This overdenture is removable, but it requires significant force to remove so it does not become loose like traditional dentures when eating or speaking.

Denture Stabilization

Removable Denture Stabilization consists of a minimum of 2 implants per arch, like removable implant-retained dentures. Implant stabilization prevents facial shape changes or sagging caused by jawbone recession; it also provides support for correct speaking, chewing, and smiling.
