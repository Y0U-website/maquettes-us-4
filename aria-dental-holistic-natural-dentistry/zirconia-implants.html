Is Zirconia Really Metal-free?

There is often some confusion amongst clinicians and patients regarding the difference between transitional metal zirconium and metal-free zirconia (zirconium oxide). Can you really call a zirconia implant “metal free” if it is derived from zirconium? YES.

Zirconia is a ceramic material manufactured from the oxidation of transitional metal zirconium, which is an irreversible chemical reaction that occurs where electrons move from the zirconium to the oxygen molecules. Even though, zirconia contains metallic atoms of zirconium but is not considered a metal due to its oxide structure (ZrO2). The addition of the oxide changes its composition, structure, behavior, and name. As a result, ceramic zirconia has completely different material properties compared to metal zirconium.
