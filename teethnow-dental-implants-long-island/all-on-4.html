What Is The Procedure For All-On-Four In NYC?

With The All-On-4 Dental Implants Technique,

we precisely place four to six dental implants around each arch. On the day of your implant procedure, the All-On-Four bridge is immediately placed on the implants. This bridge serves as a temporary piece for use while the bone and gum tissue surrounding the dental implants heals.

Although the first All-On-Four bridge is usually temporary, you benefit from using it to chew on a modified diet without worrying about a removable denture. The treatment starts and ends within the same day, so you head home with a brand-new set of teeth. These teeth will look and function like natural teeth. What Does The CBCT Scan Do?

Before We Recommend An All-On-4 We Capture A Low Radiation CBCT Scan

  • Allows us to see precise bone density and height the location of nerves
  • Ensures that the dental implants end up in the areas of greatest bone density
  • Allows to see the presence of any cysts or obstructions
  • Provides vital safety and diagnostic data
  • Ensures the right angle for each implant which leads to long-term success
  • Allows plan the dental implant’s placement

Advantages and Disadvantages

Some Advantages And Disadvantages Of The All-On-Four Technique Include:

  • Advantages The All-On-Four technique has reduced the length of the procedure compared to traditional dental implant procedures.
  • It is the least expensive permanent smile restoration method.
  • You experience little to no pain during the All-On- Four procedure. You may choose IV or oral sedation depending what’s best for you.
  • With proper home care and professional maintenance, the All-On-Four dental implants prosthesis can last a lifetime.
