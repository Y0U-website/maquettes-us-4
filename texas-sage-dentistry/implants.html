Implants in McKinney, TX

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically and permanently placed in the jawbone underneath the gums. These posts are then fitted with a dental crown or another type of dental restoration to replace missing teeth.

Dental implants help patients replace missing teeth in a way that looks natural, feels secure, and lasts decades.

How Are Dental Implants Placed?

The first step in placing dental implants is dental surgery. During this step, an incision is made into the gum in order to insert the dental implant. This stage of the dental implant process typically takes anywhere from three to six months, during which time the implant site heals and the implant fuses with the jawbone.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent dental restorations that replace missing or severely damaged teeth. They consist of an artificial tooth root that fuses with your jawbone and a tooth replacement such as a crown, bridge, or denture.

Dental implants are extremely durable. With proper care, they can last as long as a lifetime. They are a reliable, permanent restoration that can help prevent bone atrophy or jaw bone deterioration.

Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth and they also function like natural teeth.

When Are Dental Implants a Good Option?

Dental implants offer a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. They can be used to replace a single tooth, several teeth, or a full arch of teeth. Because they are fused to the jawbone, they are strong, durable, and can last for decades. They are a good option for patients with missing teeth or with dentures or bridges.

How to Care for Your Dental Implants?

Caring for your dental implants is easy. Just like your natural teeth, you’ll need to brush and floss them each day. You should also pay increased attention to your oral health and see your Dentist in McKinney TX twice each year for thorough cleanings and other oral treatments.
