Get to know your doctor

Meet Dr. Daniela Alvarez

Dr. Daniela Alvarez, DMD, brings a unique blend of artistry, precision, and warmth to Ivry Dental. With a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Nova Southeastern University and extensive certifications in neuromodulators and fillers, composite veneers, and laser dentistry, Dr. Alvarez is passionate about delivering care that enhances both function and beauty. She is fully licensed in Georgia and remains committed to staying at the forefront of dental advancements as a member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, and the Hinman Dental Society.

Dr. Alvarez’s path to dentistry began during her summer visits to Colombia, where she was inspired by the care and creativity of her family dentist. That early curiosity grew into a calling, and today she uses that same intentional approach to help every patient feel seen, heard, and empowered. Her clinical talents shine in services such as veneers , composite veneers, smile design , clear aligners , crowns , and bridges. At the core of her practice is a belief in prevention, education, and building long-term relationships.

Because Dr. Alvarez believes dentistry should never feel rushed or impersonal, she founded Ivry Dental to create an environment where high standards and humanity thrive together. Her care philosophy is guided by empathy, respect, and collaboration, grounded in the idea that your experience should be just as meaningful as your results.

Outside the practice, Dr. Alvarez enjoys life’s simple pleasures: reading in the sun, exploring new cities, trying out sushi restaurants, and spending time with her 6-pound shih-tzu, Lucy. She also loves cheering on her boyfriend in the kitchen, where she proudly serves as the expert taste-tester for his newest creations.

Dr. Alvarez looks forward to welcoming patients who value personalized care and are ready to experience dentistry the way it was meant to be.

Thoughtful care from your very first visit

What you can expect as a new patient

Your first visit to Ivry Dental is designed to feel calm, intentional, and comforting. From the moment you walk in, you’ll notice the difference—welcoming faces, a peaceful aesthetic, and a sense of ease that lets you settle in. Our goal is to make your first experience feel refreshingly personalized while giving you the knowledge and confidence to move forward in your oral health journey.

Patient Experience We’ll begin with a comprehensive exam, a set of digital X-rays, and time to learn about your goals and concerns. You’ll never feel rushed or judged—instead, expect a conversation-based experience where we meet you where you are and help you take the next best step, together. If you’re visiting for a consultation or emergency, we’ll focus on your immediate needs with clarity and compassion.

elevating your care

Ivry Dental is more than a dental office—it’s a relaxing space designed with your comfort in mind. We believe that modern care should be both efficient and enjoyable, supported by technology that ensures precision and results, and amenities that help you feel calm and cared for.

Cozy comforts

We want every visit to feel like a well-deserved break from your day. That’s why we offer a curated menu of amenities to help you stay cozy, entertained, and at ease throughout your visit.

Neck pillow Enjoy extra support throughout your treatment with a soft, ergonomic neck pillow designed for comfort.

Blanket Stay warm and relaxed with a cozy blanket—just one more way we make you feel at home in the chair.

Noise-canceling headphones Drown out the dental sounds and tune into a playlist or podcast of your choice using our premium headphones.

Streaming TV With ceiling-mounted TVs in every operatory, you can catch up on your favorite shows or unwind with a movie while we work on your smile.

Lip balm Because the little things matter—keep your lips soft and hydrated before, during, or after your appointment.

Aromatherapy Enjoy a moment of serenity with calming essential oils designed to reduce stress and enhance your sense of well-being.

Beverage bar Help yourself to a fresh cup of coffee, tea, or refreshing water from our beautifully stocked beverage station in the reception area.

Phone charging station Power up your devices while you power up your smile—no low batteries here.

Sensory tools For patients who feel anxious or overwhelmed, we offer fidget tools and other sensory-friendly items to help you feel grounded and at ease.

Advanced Technology

Modern dentistry means blending science and artistry, and our technology helps us do both with confidence. At Ivry Dental, we use advanced diagnostic tools to provide minimally invasive, highly accurate care that’s tailored to your needs and goals.

Digital intraoral scanner Say goodbye to messy impressions—we use a digital scanner to capture detailed images of your teeth quickly and comfortably.

CBCT imaging This 3D imaging tool gives us a comprehensive look at your teeth, jaw, and bone structure, helping us plan even the most complex treatments with precision.

AI-enhanced diagnostics By integrating innovative software into our diagnostic process, we gain deeper insight into your oral health, supporting proactive, high-quality care tailored just for you.
