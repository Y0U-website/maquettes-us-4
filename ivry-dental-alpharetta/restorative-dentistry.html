Ceramic Dental Crowns

Dental crowns restore damaged teeth by covering and protecting what remains of your natural tooth. When you have a cracked or broken tooth, these custom caps not only shield the root but also improve how your teeth work together when you bite. If you&#x27;ve had a root canal or dental implant, your dentist will typically recommend a crown to strengthen the treated tooth. With several material options available, crowns help both your dental health and appearance, giving you back a smile you can feel good about.

Extractions

Extractions are always a last resort because preserving your smile is our top priority. However, sometimes a problematic tooth causes more harm than good. In these cases, your dentist may recommend an extraction to restore your oral health. Using gentle techniques and local anesthesia, decayed or damaged teeth can be safely removed to relieve discomfort or make space for dental implants, a bridge, or another type of tooth replacement.

Ceramic Inlays & Onlays

Sometimes, tooth decay is too extensive to be treated with a dental filling but not extensive enough to require a full crown. In these cases, dental inlays or onlays can provide an ideal middle-ground solution. Inlays are designed to restore the grooves between the cusps of a tooth, while onlays extend further to cover one or more cusps—or even the entire chewing surface—depending on the damage. Both inlays and onlays can be crafted from durable, high-quality materials such as porcelain or gold to restore strength, function, and appearance.

Implant Restorations

Missing teeth can affect both your confidence and your ability to enjoy daily life. Dental implant restorations offer a long-lasting, natural-looking solution to replace one or more missing teeth. By securing a custom crown, bridge, or denture to a titanium implant, you can restore the strength, function, and beauty of your smile.

Unlike traditional options, implant restorations provide stability you can trust—no slipping, shifting, or worrying about adhesives. They also help preserve bone structure and support overall oral health. With proper care, implants are designed to last for many years, giving you comfort, confidence, and a smile that looks and feels like your own.
