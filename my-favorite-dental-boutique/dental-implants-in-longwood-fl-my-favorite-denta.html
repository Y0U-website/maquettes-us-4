What Are Dental Implants and How Can They Restore Your Smile?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth or bridges. They provide a stable foundation for crowns, restoring both function and appearance. The primary benefit of dental implants is their ability to mimic the natural tooth structure, allowing for improved chewing and speaking abilities. Additionally, they help maintain jawbone health by preventing bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth.

How Does the Dental Implant Procedure Work at Our Longwood Clinic?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the potential risks associated with dental implants?

While dental implants are generally safe, there are potential risks involved, including infection at the implant site, nerve damage, and sinus issues if the implant protrudes into the sinus cavity. Additionally, improper placement can lead to implant failure. It’s crucial to choose an experienced dental professional, like Dr. Paula Grisales, who can minimize these risks through careful planning and execution of the procedure. Patients should also follow post-operative care instructions to ensure a smooth recovery.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-term solution for tooth replacement, often lasting 10 to 15 years or even longer with proper care. Factors influencing their longevity include oral hygiene practices, regular dental check-ups, and lifestyle choices such as smoking. The titanium material used in implants is biocompatible and resistant to decay, making them a durable option. Patients who maintain good oral health can expect their implants to function effectively for many years.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most adults with good oral health are candidates for dental implants. However, certain conditions may affect eligibility, such as uncontrolled diabetes, severe gum disease, or insufficient bone density in the jaw. A thorough evaluation by a dental professional is essential to determine if implants are suitable for an individual. In some cases, additional procedures like bone grafting may be necessary to ensure a stable foundation for the implants.

What is the difference between dental implants and dentures?

Dental implants and dentures serve the same purpose of replacing missing teeth, but they differ significantly in design and function. Implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a permanent solution that mimics natural teeth. In contrast, dentures are removable appliances that rest on the gums. While dentures can be less expensive initially, implants offer greater stability, improved chewing ability, and better long-term outcomes, making them a preferred choice for many patients.

How can I prepare for my dental implant consultation?

Preparing for a dental implant consultation involves gathering relevant medical history, including any medications you are taking and previous dental treatments. It’s also helpful to have a list of questions or concerns ready to discuss with Dr. Grisales. Additionally, consider bringing any dental records or X-rays that may assist in the evaluation process. Being well-prepared can help ensure a productive consultation and a clearer understanding of the implant procedure.

What aftercare is required following dental implant surgery?

After dental implant surgery, proper aftercare is crucial for successful healing. Patients should follow their dentist’s instructions, which typically include taking prescribed medications, applying ice to reduce swelling, and adhering to a soft food diet for the first few days. Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential, but patients should avoid brushing the implant site directly until cleared by their dentist. Regular follow-up appointments will also be necessary to monitor healing and ensure the implant integrates properly with the jawbone.
