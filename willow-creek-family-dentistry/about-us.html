About Our Iowa City Dentistry Office

Your Direction for Optimal Oral Health

At Willow Creek Family Dentistry we are committed to helping our patients achieve a lifetime of optimal oral health. We believe that teeth are not a luxury and that every individual deserves to have a dentist and dental team that they can easily talk to and rely on for all their dental needs. Patients should feel safe, and informed. Critically, we strive to ensure our patients are empowered to be an active part of their own dental care.

To learn more about our office in Iowa City or to schedule an appointment, please don’t hesitate to contact our dental office at (319) 339-4456!

Willow Creek Family Dentistry wants to be your first choice when it comes to comprehensive dental care. Our talented dentist provides a comprehensive, modern approach to dentistry. Apart from your oral health, we also focus on your overall well being and give individualized care that benefits the entire body. Our dental office is constantly evolving as well as we strive to be ahead of the curve of new dental practices and procedures.

Individualized Care for All Patients

Our dental office is meticulously designed to provide a safe and comfortable environment for patients of all ages. We are accommodating and always ready to help with any of our patients’ needs. Together with our lead dentist, Dr. Kobi Voshell, and our dental hygienist, Jenna Alberhasky, we are committed to ensuring nothing but the most pleasant dental experience for each of our patients.

Some features that set our dental practice apart include:

  • Emphasis on preventive dental treatments
  • Dedicated to providing the highest level of dental care
  • Always a front runner when it comes to new dental practices, technologies, and procedures
  • Provides free over-the-phone consultations
  • Accepts most forms of insurance
  • We speak Spanish. Se Habla Español

Schedule Your Next Dental Visit Today

Whether it’s time for your semi-annual dental visit or a dental problem has been bothering you for days, trust Willow Creek Family Dentistry to provide you with the most comprehensive dental care. We are here to steer you in the right direction of a lifetime of good oral health.
