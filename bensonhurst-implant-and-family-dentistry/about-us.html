Meet Dr. Chung Hin Lau

Dr. Chung Hin Lau earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree and has dedicated his career to advancing his skills in implant dentistry and orthodontics. He has completed extensive training in implantology, mastering both the surgical placement and prosthetic restoration aspects of dental implants.

Dr. Lau is passionate about using cutting-edge technology to provide patients with dental solutions that genuinely improve their quality of life. His approach combines technical expertise with a warm, personalized touch, ensuring each patient receives care tailored to their unique needs.

His commitment to continuing education keeps him at the forefront of dental advancements, particularly in implantology and Invisalign treatment. Dr. Lau believes in making high-quality dental care accessible to all through transparent pricing and flexible payment options.

When not in the office, Dr. Lau enjoys spending quality time with his family. He is fluent in both English and Chinese, allowing him to better serve the diverse Bensonhurst community.

Our Technology

At Bensonhurst Implant & Family Dentistry, we&#x27;ve invested in advanced dental technology to provide precise, efficient care. Our intraoral scanner eliminates messy impressions, creating accurate digital models for crowns, bridges, and Invisalign treatment.
