How Dental Implants Work

A dental implant is a small titanium post that a dentist places directly into the jawbone, where it takes over the job the natural tooth root used to do. Once the bone heals around the post, it supports a custom crown, bridge, or denture that is shaped and shaded to match your natural teeth.

Researchers at the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research continue to study ways to help implants bond more securely with the jawbone, supporting the same long-term stability we aim for with every placement.

Who Is A Candidate For Dental Implants

Most adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for implants. The main requirements are healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the post. If your jawbone has thinned since losing a tooth, a bone graft can often build up the area so an implant can still be placed. As part of our approach to healing, we also use PRP therapy to help the surgical site recover faster after placement.
