Caring for Your New Denture

Implant-supported dentures are built to last, but a little routine care goes a long way toward keeping them functioning well for years. Most patients remove their denture nightly for cleaning, using a soft brush and a denture-safe cleanser rather than regular toothpaste, which can be too abrasive for the material.

The implants themselves need the same attention as natural teeth would, since healthy gum tissue around each implant is what keeps the whole structure stable. Regular checkups let us monitor the implants, check the fit of your denture, and catch any small adjustments before they become bigger problems. With consistent care, many patients find their implant-supported denture remains comfortable and secure for well over a decade.
