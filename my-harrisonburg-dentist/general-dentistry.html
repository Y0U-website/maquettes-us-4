What General Dentistry Really Means

General dentistry is the everyday care that keeps your mouth healthy for the long run. It covers the routine exams, cleanings, X-rays, and small repairs that catch problems while they are still easy to handle. Most of what we do here is quiet, preventive work, the kind that means you spend less time in the chair over a lifetime, not more. When your teeth and gums are checked regularly, a cavity gets caught as a tiny spot instead of a toothache, and early gum changes get treated before they ever threaten the bone that holds your teeth in place.

Why does this matter so much? Because nearly every dental problem starts small and silent. Decay between teeth, the first signs of gum disease, a cracked filling, even early oral cancer rarely hurt at the beginning. By the time something aches, the fix is usually bigger, longer, and more expensive than it would have been a few months earlier. Steady preventive care is simply the most reliable way to keep your own teeth, your comfort, and your budget on your side.

Questions Families Ask Us

How often should I come in for a checkup and cleaning?

For most people, a cleaning and exam every six months keeps small problems from becoming big ones. If you are managing gum disease or frequent cavities, we may suggest more frequent visits. We set a rhythm that fits you.

What is included in a comprehensive dental exam?

A comprehensive exam is more than a quick look. We review your health history, check every tooth, measure your gums, screen for oral cancer, and take any needed digital X-rays. Then we explain what we found and plan your care together.

Do you treat both children and adults at the same office?

Yes. You can keep your whole family under one roof on Medical Avenue, from your child&#x27;s first visit by age one through a grandparent&#x27;s periodontal care. Caring for everyone in one place makes scheduling simpler and helps us know your family&#x27;s history.

Will my visit be uncomfortable?

We keep every appointment gentle and unhurried. We take the time to explain each step before we begin, pace the visit to your comfort, and welcome a stop signal any time you need a break. Routine cleanings and exams are calm by design.
