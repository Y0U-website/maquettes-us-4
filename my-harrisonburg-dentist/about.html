About Our Practice

Gentle, modern dental care for you and your family, right here in Harrisonburg and the Shenandoah Valley.

A Dentist Built Around You

You deserve a dentist who treats your care as something personal, never corporate. That is the simple idea this Harrisonburg practice is built around: gentle, modern dentistry where you are listened to, your questions are answered, and your time is respected, whether you live in Harrisonburg, raise a family in Rockingham County, or are settling into the wider Shenandoah Valley.

“Success here is not measured in procedures. It is measured in the trust you place in us and the smile you get to keep for a lifetime.”

You will find us at 1947 Medical Avenue, Suite A, inside the Sentara RMH medical corridor and just minutes from I-81 and Port Republic Road. From the moment you walk in, the office is designed to feel welcoming. Your neighbors here include downtown professionals, Sentara RMH staff, young families from Bridgewater and Dayton, retirees in Broadway and Elkton, and friends from across Harrisonburg&#x27;s many communities.

Your visit should leave you feeling cared for, not processed. Dr. Hu focuses on helping you keep a healthy, comfortable smile through preventive care and clear, jargon-free explanations of your options.

Dr. Kevin Hu

Dr. Hu leads the team at My Harrisonburg Dentist with a simple commitment: every patient deserves to feel genuinely heard. He takes the time to understand your concerns, explain your options in plain language, and build a personalized plan that fits your life, so you leave feeling confident and cared for.

He completed his Advanced Education in General Dentistry and a Master of Science at the University of Rochester&#x27;s Eastman Institute for Oral Health, and earned a PhD in Orthodontics from The University of Hong Kong, where he received the HKU Presidential PhD Award. A widely published researcher and an editorial board member for a Nature Publishing Group dental journal, he pairs evidence-based expertise with warm, unhurried care.

What You Can Count On
