Pediatric dentistry

Pediatric dentistry provides preventive, comprehensive and therapeutic oral health care for infants, children, teenagers and young adults to age 20. Pediatric dentistry encompasses a variety of disciplines such as behavior guidance, patients with medical conditions and physical and developmental disabilities, supervision of orofacial growth and development, caries prevention, sedation, pharmacological management and hospital dentistry.

Dr. JV graduated Louisiana State University Pediatric Residency in 2020. She has a heart for kids and they seem to gravitate towards her. Our team of dental experts have the patience, dedication, expertise and also possess the heart to ensure that the highest standard of care is given to every patient, every time.

Special Care (Needs) Dentistry

Here at Southern Magnolia Dentistry, we understand that patients with special needs can often exhibit combative or defiant behaviors, especially when they are fearful of the unknown. We offer a variety of Sedation options to ensure a positive experience for the patient, as well as providing quality dental care.
