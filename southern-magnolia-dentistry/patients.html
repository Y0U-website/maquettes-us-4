My Child’s First Visit: What to Expect?

So you’ve scheduled your child’s first dental appointment now what? As the anticipation builds, you’re probably asking yourself, What goes on at the first appointment?

Your child’s first dentist appointment is basically an information-gathering exercise. It also gives you a chance to meet the pediatric dentist and assess the dynamic between the dentist and your child. No worries!...all of our operatories are arranged to accommodate a parent or guardian.

Here’s what you can expect at your child’s first dental appointment:

1. A warm welcome from our front office staff, as well as a fully stocked play area (sponsored by Timmy’s Toys ) to keep your little one entertained while waiting.

2. A "gentle dental" cleaning that will most likely include x-rays of your child’s teeth by our friendly and patient dental assistants.

3. A brief exam of your child’s teeth, conducted by Dr. JV. She will assess their teeth, along with the gum line and jaw development.

4. A proposed treatment plan will be built in your child’s chart and their next appointment will be scheduled.

*Common treatments and problems that may be addressed include methods to reduce finger sucking, how to properly manage teething, methods on discouraging pacifier use, and other issues related to baby/toddler dental care. While these treatments aren’t required for every patient, they might be administered to your child.*
