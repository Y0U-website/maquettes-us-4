The practice

Our dentist for pediatric in Kendall offers a full range of pediatric dental services to support your child’s oral health at every stage:

We provide safe, tooth-colored fillings to treat cavities and restore your child’s smile. Our approach ensures minimal discomfort while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

For complex procedures or children with dental anxiety, we offer general anesthesia for a stress-free experience. This allows treatments to be completed safely and comfortably in a controlled environment.

Early dental visits play a crucial role in preventing future complications. Establishing care with a Pedia dentist in Kendall at a young age helps monitor oral development and address concerns early.

Tooth decay (cavities) is one of the most common issues in children, often caused by poor oral hygiene and sugary diets. We provide early detection and tooth-colored fillings to restore function and prevent further damage.

Children can experience gum inflammation due to plaque buildup and improper brushing habits. We focus on deep cleaning, education, and preventive strategies to maintain healthy gums.

Teething can cause irritation, swelling, and discomfort in infants and toddlers. We guide parents on safe ways to soothe symptoms and ensure proper oral development during this stage.

Crooked or crowded teeth can affect chewing, speech, and appearance. Early evaluation allows us to recommend corrective measures and monitor alignment.

Services

Our gentle and thorough cleanings help remove plaque and buildup, keeping your child’s teeth and gums healthy. Regular visits also allow us to monitor oral development and prevent future dental issues.

When a tooth is significantly damaged or decayed, pediatric crowns offer a durable and long-lasting solution. They help restore functions, protect the teeth, and maintain proper spacing for future development.

Some children may require early orthodontic intervention to guide jaw growth and tooth positioning. Our team assesses development and suggests timely treatment to prevent complex issues later.
