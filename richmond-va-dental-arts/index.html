At Richmond VA Dental Arts, we understand that every patient is unique, especially those with autism. Our team is committed to creating a safe, supportive and calming environment for individuals and families seeking compassionate dental care. We know that visiting the dentist can be overwhelming, so we offer personalized approaches that cater to sensory sensitivities and communication preferences.

Dr. Faryl Hart works closely with caregivers to understand each patient’s comfort level, needs and routine. We allow extra time for appointments when needed and use clear, gentle communication to make each visit as smooth and stress-free as possible.
