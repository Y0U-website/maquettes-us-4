Comprehensive Care

Comprehensive Care

Comprehensive Care

New patients are always welcome at Spring Hill Dental. At your first appointment, Dr. Kaushish will perform a comprehensive exam to diagnose any dental conditions you may have. We’ll clean your teeth, take x-rays, and analyze everything together. Our goal is for you to become an expert in your oral healthcare. Afterwards, we’ll help you maintain your oral health via hygiene visits tailored to meet your specific needs.

Emergency Care

Comprehensive Care

Comprehensive Care

Dental emergencies happen every day - even if you’ve never been to our office before, we’re here to help. We’ll figure out what happened and if you’re having symptoms, we’ll find out why and solve the issue at hand. We perform emergency extractions, root canals, and fillings here - contact us and we’ll see you as soon as we can.

Implants

Root Canals

Implants

Implants are generally the best method to close spaces to help you smile and chew. An implant is placed in the missing tooth site, and a porcelain crown is cemented on top. The result looks like a natural tooth. We can perform all aspects of the implant process at our office.

Crowns

Root Canals

Implants

Crowns protect teeth that need more support, such as a root canal treated tooth or one that has been broken down by cavities or wear. The crowns we make are usually made of a shatter-proof and esthetic porcelain called Zirconia.

Root Canals

Root Canals

Root Canals

Root canals are performed when (A) a cavity is very close to the tooth nerve, (B) when the infection has passed all the way to the pulp, or (C) to prevent or end severe sensitivity. We can perform them here or refer you to a specialist (endodontist).

Invisalign ®

Veneers and Gum Recontouring

Veneers and Gum Recontouring

Invisalign ® uses proprietary clear aligner technology to safely move teeth into a more harmonious position for an esthetic smile and more effective bite. Most cases take less than 5 months!

Veneers and Gum Recontouring

Veneers and Gum Recontouring

Veneers and Gum Recontouring

Veneers are thin porcelain crowns that are bonded to the front surfaces of teeth that brighten, align, and esthetically reshape your smile. Often, a recontouring of the gums (gingivectomy) is indicated to naturally boost the appearance of the teeth in your smile.

Whitening

Veneers and Gum Recontouring

Whitening

We can brighten your natural teeth in-office or at-home via Opalescence ® products specifically chosen by Dr. Kaushish. These contain a carbamide peroxide formulation with desensitizing agents so you can safely whiten your teeth.

Fillings

Dentures and Partials

Extractions

Fillings or direct restorations are needed when a tooth has a cavity or fractured portion. We offer tooth-colored fillings and more.

Extractions

Dentures and Partials

Extractions

Sometimes, we have to take out teeth that are infected or severely broken down. Dr. Kaushish is trained in safely and comfortably taking out teeth, including wisdom teeth.

Dentures and Partials

Dentures and Partials

Dentures and Partials

If you are missing some or all of your teeth, we can make you a denture to fill in the spaces to help you eat and smile freely again. We offer partial dentures with or without metal.

Nightguards

Sleep Apnea Devices

Nightguards

To be completely honest, nightguards are often prescribed unnecessarily. We’ll make sure it’s indicated for your case and often will recommend a generic one you can buy at most pharmacies. In the event you need a custom-made one, we’re happy to have our lab fabricate one for you.

Retainers

Sleep Apnea Devices

Nightguards

Feel like your teeth are starting to move or lost your old retainer? We can make retainers in-house so you can maintain the position of your teeth.

Sleep Apnea Devices

Sleep Apnea Devices

Sleep Apnea Devices

Do you snore? Unable to cope with a CPAP device? An oral sleep apnea device may be a great option for you. We advise visiting a sleep specialist physician first and if they recommend an oral appliance, our lab will make one for you.
