Meet Samir Hasso, DDS

Dr. Hasso was born in Baghdad, Iraq and that’s where he was awarded his first dental degree, soon after that he decided to move to the US seeking a better life/education. Dr. Hasso was awarded his second dental degree from Loma Linda university in southern California. After graduation he decided to move Colorado since he is a fan of colder climates, winter activities, hiking and just being outside with nature. Dr. Hasso enjoys baking, cooking, and solving jigsaw puzzles in his free time, he enjoys traveling and spending time with his family, friends and his dog Luka.
