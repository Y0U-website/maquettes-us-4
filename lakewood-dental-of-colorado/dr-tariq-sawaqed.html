Meet Tariq Sawaqed, DDS

I’m Dr Tariq Sawaqed, I graduated from university of colorado school of dental medicine in 2009 I got my first dental degree in Jordan in 2002 and my master in dentistry in Jordan in 2007 and Received my DDS degree from the university of colorado in 2009. I have been practicing dentistry in the US since 2009.

I enjoy going to continuing education classes all the time, on average I spend 10-15 hours every month continuing my dental education and learning new things, one of my biggest passions in dentistry is Digital dentistry and 3D printing, we are heavily equipped with equipment and tools for digital dentistry, I believe it is the future of dentistry it make dentistry easier faster,more predictable and affordable to the patients .

I have been at Lakewood Dental of Colorado since 2013. I have been married to my beautiful wife Yara for 16 years, We have three kids Andrew, Ava, and Ethan. On my Free time I enjoy spending time with my family, playing with my kids in out backyard and coaching my kids soccer teams.
