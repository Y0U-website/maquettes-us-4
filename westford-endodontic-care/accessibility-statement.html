WECare Accessibility Statement

General

At Westford Endodontic Care, we believe that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence. We strive to ensure that our services are accessible to people with disabilities, and we have invested significant resources to ensure that our office is welcoming and comfortable for all patients. We have also worked alongside our website developer to ensure that our website is made easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities.

Accessibility on Our Website

Our website, westfordendo.com, employs the UserWay Website Accessibility Widget. Powered by a dedicated accessibility server, the UserWay widget integrates software into our website that offers our users a multitude of accommodation options, improving their user experience and enhancing our overall compliance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1).

Enabling the Accessibility Menu

The accessibility menu for our website can be enabled by clicking the accessibility menu button that appears on the top-left corner of our website. The button is represented by a circle icon with a person in the center like the one pictured here. After triggering the button, simply wait a moment for the accessibility menu to load in its entirety.

Disclaimer

We will continually work to improve the accessibility of the Westford Endodontic Care website in order to offer seamless, accessible, and unhindered access to all users.

Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on this website fully accessible, some content may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards. In some cases, this may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.

We’re Here For You

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on the Westford Endodontic Care website or require assistance, please contact us during normal business hours and we will be happy to assist you in person. You may email the office at office@westfordnendo.com or call (978) 692-2202.

Contact Us

For information on this website, our services and care, and questions about our practice, please contact the office directly at (978) 692-2202 or office@westfordnendo.com . Thank you!
