Financial Planning

Ridgeview Family Dentistry accepts most major credit cards and insurance plans. We also work with CareCredit and iCreditWorks to provide a financial option for patients who need extensive work or who don’t have dental insurance.

You can always get assistance from our office staff with insurance claims and paperwork. In order to ensure that your insurance coverage satisfies your needs and your budget, we will submit claims on your behalf and communicate with your insurance company.
