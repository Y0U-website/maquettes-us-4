3 Ways To Transform Your Smile

Clear Aligners

Clear Aligners use a series of clear, removable aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. The aligners are virtually invisible, so you can straighten your teeth without feeling self-conscious about your appearance. Clear Aligners also offer several other benefits, including improved oral hygiene since you can remove the aligners to brush and floss, and fewer food restrictions since you can take the aligners out to eat and improve your teeth faster than traditional braces.

  • Faster than Braces
  • Nearly Invisible
  • Comfortable and Easily Removable
  • Fewer appointments
  • Precise treatment tracking

Dr. Anne Isinger

Dr. Anne Isinger says her calling as a dentist is not only to diagnose and treat oral disease, but to help people feel like the best version of themselves. The best part of my job is boosting patients’ confidence by improving their oral health! she says.

Dr. Isinger currently resides in her hometown of Leawood, Kansas. She obtained her DDS at Creighton University in 2017 after receiving her BA in Human Biology University of Kansas. She says she enjoys her job at Ridgeview Family Dentistry because it enables her to give back to the larger Kansas City area and demonstrate to patients that going to the dentist’s office isn’t a frightening experience. I enjoy constantly contributing to the solution!
