About Us

Dr. Alex Planes earned his doctorate from Universidad Iberomericana and completed his residency at Columbia University. He then went on to train at Augusta University to become certified in the State of Florida for conscious sedation.

Dr. Planes is a member of several professional organizations, including the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the American Dental Association, Dawson Academy, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. He has also achieved Mastership status from the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

Continually committed to impacting people’s lives for the better, Dr. Planes is passionate about helping his patients receive quality dental care – regardless of their needs and goals. He’s known for his friendly and empathetic chairside manner, and for providing a wide variety of high-quality general dentistry services. He is particularly interested in delivering restorative, cosmetic, and sedation treatments and procedures that restore, rejuvenate, and strengthen his patients’ smiles.
