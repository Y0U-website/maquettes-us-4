At Planes Dental Arts, we offer dental implants in tradition to meet our patient’s needs. If you’r e looking for a long-term solution to tooth loss, get in touch wit h us today!

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth. All-on-4 dental implants use four titanium posts to firmly secure a denture for an entire arch of missing teeth.

Dental implants and All-on-4 implants offer many advantages over traditional dentures or bridges, such as improved bite strength, a more natural look and feel, fewer dietary restrictions, and better overall oral health. In addition, with diligent care and regular checkups with our team, these tooth replacements have the potential to last a lifetime.

Individuals who are missing one or more teeth are likely to be good candidates for these restorative options. Of course, it’s important to undergo a thorough evaluation to determine if you’re a candidate for either service as patients must have adequate jawbone density to sustain one or more dental implants.
