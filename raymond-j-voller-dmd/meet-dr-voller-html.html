Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Voller is the proud father of two grown children: Dr. Rachel Voller Eich, a veterinarian, and Kevin Voller, who works for Google. He also has four grandchildren! In his free time, he plays for the Jentz Band, doing guitar and vocals. He additionally plays bagpipes for the Seton Hill University Pipe Band and serves on the Mars Hunting and Fishing Club.
