Meet Dr. Marker

Doctor Timothy Marker welcomes you to experience dental care with the patient experience in mind.

Dr. Marker and his team combine advanced dentistry with a gentle, friendly style that aims to put patients at ease during their visit.

Dr. Marker has extensive training in complex restorative cases. He has been successfully treating TMD (temporomandibular dysfunction), snoring and sleep apnea for over 30 years.

With over three decades of experience in clinical dentistry, Dr. Marker continues to attend extensive post-doctoral studies in advanced dentistry each year, placing him in the top 2 % of all dentists for advanced education to keep abreast of the latest in both dental procedures and technology.

Whether you need advanced treatment or a routine cleaning, you can be assured that all your dentistry will be performed with the utmost precision and care. Best of all, Dr. Marker takes the time to get to know each and every patient one-on-one, helping you make informed choices about your dental health.

Dr. Marker is a Washington native who earned his Bachelor of Science at Gonzaga University before earning his doctorate from Creighton University. He and his wife, Susan, have five children.

A lover of the great outdoors, you can find Dr. Marker hiking, exploring, and fishing in his spare time.
