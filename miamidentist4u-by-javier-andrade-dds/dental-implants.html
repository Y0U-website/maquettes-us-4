Dental Implants

Dental implants are an innovative and durable solution for replacing missing or extracted teeth. They consist of small titanium screws that are inserted into the maxillary or mandibular bone and serve as a solid base for attaching dental prostheses, such as crowns, bridges or dentures.

Durability

Dental implants are known for their durability and longevity. When fused to the maxillary or mandibular bone, implants provide a solid, permanent foundation for dentures, making them highly resilient and able to withstand daily chewing forces for many years.

Improved Stability and Functionality

Dental implants offer unparalleled stability for dentures, allowing for more natural and comfortable chewing function compared to conventional alternatives, such as removable dentures.

Preservation of Bone Structure

Dental implants help preserve bone structure by preventing bone loss that can occur after tooth extraction. By stimulating bone through chewing load, implants help maintain bone integrity and facial shape
