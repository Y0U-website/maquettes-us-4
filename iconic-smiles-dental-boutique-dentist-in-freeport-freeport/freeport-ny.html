Dental implants in Freeport

At the Freeport office you can replace a single tooth or a full arch. One implant fills the gap left by one tooth. When most of the teeth in a jaw are gone, All-on-4 or All-on-X can hold a fixed full arch, and an overdenture gives a removable set that clicks onto implants, so it stays put when you eat.

Before any plan, we take images and look at spacing, bone support and how your teeth meet. You leave the first visit knowing the phases, roughly how long each one takes and what the finished teeth will feel like.

Invisalign and clear aligners in Freeport

Clear aligners suit adults who don t want brackets at work or in photos. Freeport patients can choose Invisalign or ClearCorrect. We check crowding, spacing and your bite, and if aligners are the right tool, you get a staged plan with check-ins along the way. If your bite needs more than aligners can do, we say so at the start.

Cosmetic dentistry in Freeport

Some Freeport patients want one chipped front tooth repaired. Others want a whole new smile. Bonding usually fixes a small chip or gap in one visit, whitening lifts the shade, and porcelain veneers change the shape and color of the front teeth for longer. We keep the result in proportion to your face, so friends notice you look well rather than noticing your teeth.
