Dental implants in Bellerose

An implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up: a small post in the jaw with a custom crown on top. For a full arch, the Bellerose office offers All-on-4 and All-on-X, where a few implants hold a complete row of teeth, as well as overdentures that attach to implants. Patients who prefer a metal-free option can ask about ceramic implants.

Every implant plan starts with an exam and imaging to check spacing, bone support and your bite.

Invisalign and orthodontics in Bellerose
