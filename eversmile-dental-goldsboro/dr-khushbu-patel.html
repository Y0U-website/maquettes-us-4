Meet Dr. Khushbu Patel

Dr. Khushbu Patel

Dr. Patel was born and raised in India. One of her earliest memories of dentistry is the sweet, nostalgic aroma of bubblegum toothpaste—a scent she fondly associated with comfort rather than fear. Unlike many children, Dr. Patel looked forward to dental visits, which left her with a lasting sense of reassurance that she now strives to bring to her own patients, especially children and adults who experience dental anxiety.

"Often, I’ve found that patients with the highest dental anxiety also suffer from the most significant tooth decay and loss. It doesn’t have to be that way. The right environment—one that’s welcoming, inclusive, and focused on positive experiences—can truly change lives."

Dr. Patel pursued her passion for dentistry at SDM College of Dental Sciences and Hospital in Dharwad, India. After graduation, she gained valuable experience working as an associate at a prosthodontics clinic before moving to New York. There, she attended the University at SUNY Buffalo and graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree and simultaneously worked full-time as a dental assistant, further enriching her understanding of patient care in diverse settings.

From a young age, Dr. Patel’s parents instilled in her the value of service. Weekends were often spent participating in community initiatives—distributing hot meals, blankets, and care for underprivileged children and the elderly. This early exposure to giving back planted a deep-rooted desire to serve those in need on a larger scale.

During her dental school and even after graduation, she never missed an outreach opportunity. These experiences opened her eyes to how limited, access to dental education can be. Many people she met believed dental visits were only necessary when in pain. This realization shaped her approach to patient care: education, prevention, and empathy became the foundation of her practice. She is deeply committed to helping patients understand the importance of routine checkups, oral hygiene, and the powerful connection between oral health and overall wellness.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Patel is an avid dancer, nature lover, and hiker. She treasures time with her family, whose unwavering support and sacrifices have guided her journey. Coming from humble beginnings, Dr. Patel credits her success to their belief in her dreams and their constant encouragement.
