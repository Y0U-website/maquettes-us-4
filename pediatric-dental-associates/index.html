The practice

At Pediatric Dental Associates of Greater Springfield, we offer a comprehensive range of pediatric dental services, from routine exams to specialized treatments. Our team is passionate about promoting good dental habits for children and providing the highest standard of care for our young patients.

Our staff members love working with children and are dedicated to giving them a positive, stress-free dental experience. Every person in our office provides gentle care and a warm, friendly smile to make you and your little one as comfortable as possible.

Our office focuses on preventive dentistry to educate you and your child on how to maintain optimal oral health, We provide digital radiographs, minimally invasive dentistry, sealants, silver diamine fluoride, restorative treatment, extractions, nitrous oxide, and space maintenance.

Our state-of-the-art facility is conveniently located, beautifully decorated, and offers flexible hours to make your child’s visits hassle-free.
