The practice

Every patient has different needs, expectations, and concerns, it is our priority to get to know you. Our goal is to create beautiful, healthy, and properly aligned teeth so our patients can enjoy the physical and psychological benefits gained from smiles that look their best and function properly for a lifetime.

Do not hesitate to book your appointment with Dr. Tina, your true orthodontist specialist in St. Albans, VT.

Although the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) recommends a first orthodontic evaluation at the age of 7, it is never too late to create the smile of your dreams. Orthodontic treatments are possible for the majority of people.

We know that the consultation appointment is important to you. During this visit, we will want to understand your concerns, whether it is for your smile or that of your child. This will allow us to get to know you better, so we can ensure that our recommendations will meet your needs.

The duration of this first appointment is about 60 minutes. You will first have to answer a medical questionnaire. We request that patients bring their own panoramic x-ray, however if one is unavailable then a digital panoramic x-ray will be taken at the time of your appointment.
