Our Practice

Helping you smile brighter and wider for more than 30 years in New York.

Welcome to Dr. Alfred Ho’s Dental Office.

We have two locations for your convenience; one in Manhattan and one in Long Island.

Our team understands that dental health is more than just having pearly whites; it’s about having healthy teeth and gums. So while we provide our patients with comprehensive care, we also educate them about their dental health.

We understand that many people have anxieties when it comes to dental treatment, so we created a peaceful, calming environment to help put you at ease when you come in for an appointment. We use the latest technology along with traditional techniques to give all of our patients the best care possible.

It’s our mission to help each of our patients reach their best overall oral health.

Dr. Alfred Ho, D.D.S. Columbia University, Doctor of Dental Surgery

For me, dentistry is the practice of caring. Every person who enters my office will receive the highest standards of sensitivity and care. My staff and I strive to be attentive to every patient’s needs and responsible for helping them to attain optimal dental health.

I believe that dentistry is an art; that the shape of every tooth is a sculpture that demands perfection. I do every cleaning myself to be sure that everything is beautiful.

Year-by-year, the science of dentistry is advancing. Our practice uses tested and proven technologies to improve the experience of our patients and the quality of our results. Having the latest 3-D X-ray and soft tissue equipment is critical to our surgical practice.

I’ve been practicing dentistry and teaching for 30 years and I’m proud to have made a difference in the health and well-being of my patients. Caring is the heart and soul of my work.
