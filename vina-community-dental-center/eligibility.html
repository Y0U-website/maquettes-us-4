Eligibility: Patients must meet ALL FOUR requirements:

  • Individuals must live in Livingston County for at least 6 months
  • Individuals must be at least 18 years of age
  • Must have NO dental insurance (call for details on exceptions)
  • Families must have an income at or below 250 % of the federal poverty level . Current income levels are reflected in the chart below.

If you do not qualify, please click here to find other affordable dental care options near you.

VINA is not an emergency clinic. If you have an emergency, the University of Michigan School of Dentistry’s emergency clinic number is 734-764-1444 (8 a.m.- 5 p.m., M- F), and the after-hours emergency phone number is 734-936-6267.

Application Procedures:

Patients must complete the Eligibility Forms.

Patient must have supporting eligibility documents

Once the above materials are gathered, please call the office to set up a screening appointment (810) 844-0240

Patients will be required to pay a PREPAID nominal scheduling fee for each visit ($25 or $10). Special programs for seniors and veterans may waive the $10 and/or $25 fee

  • If filling out online: Once you have finished it, send the downloaded copy (with your information) to office@vinadental.org. If anything does not fit in the fillable forms, please let us know!
  • If printing and completing: Please mail or schedule an appointment to drop off your completed application to VINA at 400 E. Grand River Ave., Brighton, MI 48116. PLEASE USE BLACK INK ONLY if you are printing and emailing your application back to the office! Do NOT leave your application with personal information in the black mailbox.
  • Proof of residence in Livingston County (ie: utility bill, car registration, etc.)
  • Proof of family income (ie: last year’s tax return, unemployment statement, or social security statement)
  • Legal photo ID (State ID, Driver’s License, Passport, Etc.)

Services Provided:

  • Exams & oral cancer screenings
  • Cleanings
  • Scaling and root planing
  • Fillings
  • X-rays
  • Dentures (full and partial)
  • Fluoride Treatments
  • Root Canals
  • Oral Surgery/Extractions
  • Flippers
  • Dental Education Materials
  • Crowns
  • Bite guards

Services NOT Provided:

  • Braces
  • Whitening
  • Cosmetic Dentistry
  • Sedation IV or oral
  • Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)
  • Implants

Important Information:

  • Patients must update eligibility once per year.
  • Patients must arrive on time for every appointment.
  • If patient misses an appointment or cancels within 48 hours, the prepaid appointment fee of $25 will be forfeited and patient will need to make another payment to reschedule.
  • Children may not be left alone in the waiting room and must be supervised at all times.
  • VINA does not provide dental care for children. See the link above for other local dental care options.
  • In the event of inclement weather, if Brighton Area Schools are closed, VINA will also be closed.

Accessibility Accommodations

At VINA, we’re committed to creating a welcoming and accessible environment for every patient.

If you need an accommodation not listed here, please contact us — we’ll do our best to meet your needs.

  • Wheelchair-accessible entrance, restroom, and operatory
  • Noise-cancelling headphones
  • Stress balls and comfort items available during appointments
  • Hearing and vision aides
