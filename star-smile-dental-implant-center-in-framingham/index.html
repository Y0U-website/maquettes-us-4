Welcome to Star Smile Dental and Implant Center, where Dr. Kathryn, a highly skilled and compassionate dentist, is dedicated to transforming smiles in Framingham, Massachusetts. Graduating from Boston University’s prestigious Goldman Dental School, Dr. Kathryn has been serving the community since August 2003, providing unparalleled dental services. She is proud to be part of your journey toward a confident and radiant smile.

As a child in St. Petersburg, Russia, Dr. Kathryn experienced firsthand the challenges and discomfort associated with dentistry. This personal encounter ignited her determination to create a different kind of dental experience for her patients. Driven by empathy and compassion, she strives to make every visit as comfortable as possible, putting even the most anxious patients at ease.
