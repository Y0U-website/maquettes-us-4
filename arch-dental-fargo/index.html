The practice

From the moment you walk through our doors, you’ll start a relationship with our entire team where we’ll show you the connection between your smile and body. We’ll explain how we can get your smile to your standards through these four pillars of dentistry:

This pillar involves the beauty of your smile and harmonizing it with your facial features. You want a smile that reflects the true you to capitalize on your natural beauty.

Are you in pain or do you have discomfort when eating your favorite foods or speaking freely with friends? Are you missing teeth? This pillar of dentistry focuses on restoring the function of your bite and teeth so you can enjoy the little things in life without the worry of hiding your smile or being in pain.

The structure pillar takes the approach where we can fix minor problems (broken fillings, decay, etc.) before they become major, complex issues, resulting in expensive dental work. This allows you to have a natural, healthy smile 10-20 years in the future.

Your oral and overall health are intimately connected. Protecting your teeth will protect your body. The biology pillar focuses on looking for signs of periodontal disease or other oral diseases, so we can stop them from progressing and keep you and your smile healthy.

We believe less is more. We invest in your future well-being by designing our treatments to restore broken smiles and prevent smiles from needing to be fixed.
