About us

Your trusted family dentist in Conyers

At Smile Dental Group of Conyers, we’re committed to delivering exceptional dental care in a warm, welcoming environment. Led by Dr. Igor Reizenson, a dentist with over 20 years of experience, our practice provides a comprehensive range of services to ensure your smile stays healthy and vibrant.

As a trusted member of the Conyers community, formerly known as Shurett Dental Group, we have earned a reputation for quality care that patients can rely on. Dr. Reizenson and our dedicated team, many of whom have been with the practice for years, are committed to meeting the dental needs of your entire family. We pride ourselves on creating positive, long-lasting relationships with our patients, helping them feel at ease during every visit.

Igor Reizenson, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Reizenson is the owner of Smile Dental Group of Conyers. Raised in New York City, he graduated from the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine in 2001. Dr. Reizenson completed a General Practice Residency at the Veterans Administration of Western New York before moving to Atlanta in 2002. He is passionate about providing high-quality dental care and creating healthy, beautiful smiles for his patients.

Ericka Edmonds, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Edmonds has been providing high-quality, personalized dental care since 2002. A general dentist with a special passion for pediatric dentistry, she has focused primarily on treating children since 2014. A native of Georgia, Dr. Edmonds earned her undergraduate degree from Mercer University in Macon and her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from Meharry Medical College in Nashville. She went on to complete a general practice residency at The University of Alabama in Birmingham. Dr. Edmonds is a member of the American Dental Association and the Georgia Dental Association.

Chinnaya Taylor, DDS

Dentist

Dr. Taylor is a Georgia native who earned her Bachelor of Science in Microbiology from the University of Georgia and her Doctor of Dental Surgery from Meharry Medical College. She completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency through New York University Langone at Grady’s Infectious Disease Program Clinic and Ben Massell Dental Clinic in Atlanta. Inspired to become a dentist while still in middle school, Dr. Taylor provides gentle, education-focused care and enjoys helping patients feel comfortable and confident about their oral health.
