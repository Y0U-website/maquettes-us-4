  • Dental Care In Pittsburgh | St. Barnabas Health System Skip to content Dental Care for All Ages Dental Care in Greater Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania At St. Barnabas, we are proud to offer a wide array of dental care services, as oral health affects overall health. These include: Checkups and hygiene, which usually include a visual examination and a cleaning of your teeth
  • Cosmetic dentistry, which helps improves the appearance or position of teeth, gums, and/or bite
  • General dentistry, which manages and treats your overall oral health, including gum care, root canals, fillings, crowns, and more
  • Intraoral cameras, which take high-quality images of structures in your mouth
  • Invisalign orthodontic treatment, which uses custom-made clear braces to straightens teeth over time without using metal braces*
  • Periodontal treatment of the gums, which helps heal gum disease and may include preventative measures, medications, cleaning, or surgery
  • Pediatric dentistry, which focuses on the oral health of children from infancy through adolescence
  • Restorative and prosthetic dentistry, which includes any dental process that repairs or restores damaged oral structures like bridges, implants, and more
  • Same-day CEREC crowns, which allow you to get your new teeth designed and fitted on the same day*
  • Dental treatment of sleep apnea, which is a C-PAP alternative that is designed specifically for the patient to treat their sleep apnea*
  • VELscope oral cancer exam, which uses a wireless, handheld scope to visualize oral mucosal abnormalities that might not be visible to the naked eye*
  • Teeth whitening, which involves the application of a whitening gel and then aiming a special heating lamp at your teeth
