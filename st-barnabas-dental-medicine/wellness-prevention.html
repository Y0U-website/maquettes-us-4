Senior Wellness Services

  • Coordination of prescription refills from our in-house pharmacy
  • Scheduling in-house appointments with physicians, dentist, podiatrists, optometrists, and psychiatrists
  • Transportation for appointments
  • Monthly wellness visits to monitor vital signs, blood pressure, pulse, temperature, respiration, and weight for gain or loss, as well as perform routine blood work
  • Psychosocial communication and guidance
  • Immunizations
  • Fitness and nutrition counseling
  • Chronic condition management
  • And more.

Emergency and Outpatient Care

We understand how quickly minor issues can escalate into medical emergencies. That’s why our residents have easy access to medical professionals around the clock. No matter what happens or when, there will always be medical teams nearby to help. Our skilled nursing community also employs experts in diabetes, stroke, chronic pain, wound, and cardiac care. They’ll address new issues promptly, preventing worse troubles down the road.

Wellness and prevention aren’t only available for community residents, however, as our services extend beyond our living facilities. Our team of primary doctors offers high-quality care to patients throughout the Greater Pennsylvania region, helping patients stay in the best of health while living independently or with family. Our primary physicians provide:

  • Routine physicals, wellness checks, and health screenings
  • Medication management and assistance
  • Chronic disease management
  • Women’s health services
  • Nutrition services
  • Illness care
  • Health education
  • Lab tests and diagnostics
  • Immunizations and allergy injections
  • Outpatient aqua, occupational, physical, and speech therapy
  • And more.
