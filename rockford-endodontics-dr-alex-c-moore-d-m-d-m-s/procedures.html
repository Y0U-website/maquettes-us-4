  • Aftercare Once root canal therapy is complete, our doctors will seal off the tooth with a temporary restoration. This protects the tooth but you will still need to see your dentist for the final restoration (either a filling or a crown). You should try to avoid heavy biting or chewing on the treated tooth until you have had it restored by your dentist. The unrestored tooth is susceptible to fracture, so you should see your dentist for a full restoration as soon as possible.
