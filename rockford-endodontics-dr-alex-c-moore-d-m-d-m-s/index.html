Dr. Alex Moore was born and raised in the Rockford area; he is thrilled to return and serve his hometown community.

He attended Belvidere High School and then began his dental education by studying biology at DePaul University. While at DePaul, he worked in a molecular biology research lab studying nerve regeneration. He was able to present his findings at several international conferences as well as become a published author in the Journal of Comparative Neurology.

He became fascinated with modern root canal therapy and elected to specialize in endodontics. He continued his education at Marquette University School of Dentistry where he earned his endodontic specialty certificate and master’s degree.
