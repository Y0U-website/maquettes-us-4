TMJ & Sleep Disorders We Treat

TMJ/TMD

Temporo Mandibular Joint Disorder

When your patient experiences jaw pain, frequent headaches, or clicking and popping sounds while chewing, they may be showing signs of temporomandibular joint (TMJ) dysfunction. Without proper intervention, these symptoms can worsen and disrupt daily life. At the TMJ & Sleep Therapy Centre of the Gorge, Dr. Haynie provides expert, non-invasive care focused on restoring balance, relieving discomfort, and improving long-term jaw health.
