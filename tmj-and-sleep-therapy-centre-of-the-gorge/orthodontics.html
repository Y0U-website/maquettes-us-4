Orthodontics

If you or your child exhibit any of these symptoms, schedule an in-depth evaluation with our team for guidance.

  • Habitual teeth grinding or clenching
  • Tongue-tie or challenges with speech
  • Crowded or misaligned teeth
  • Jaw discomfort or misalignment impacting chewing
  • Limited jaw mobility causing facial or jaw discomfort
  • Misalignment in developing permanent teeth
  • Tongue thrust

Understanding ALF Appliances

The Advanced Lightwire Functional (ALF) appliance gently reshapes oral structures with minimal force, akin to natural tongue pressure, guiding the tongue’s position for proper swallowing. This technique expands the palate, creating more space in the mouth and improving airflow through nasal passageways. By guiding the tongue’s position, ALF ensures lasting benefits from the expansion of the palate.

Advantages of ALF Appliances

Align Crowded Teeth ALFs can help align crowded teeth with or without braces, and improvements can often be made during sleep without daytime appliances!

Arch Expansion For both children and adults, dental arches can often be widened, making surgical procedures or extractions unnecessary. Over 99 % of our patients at The TMJ and Sleep Therapy Centre of the Gorge avoid extractions with this approach.
