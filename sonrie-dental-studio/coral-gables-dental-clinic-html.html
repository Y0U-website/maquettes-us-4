Family Dental Clinic

Tooth Extractions

A Simple or Surgical tooth extraction procedure occurs when a tooth is broken or damaged by decay.

Root Canal(S)

Root Canal it’s a restorative treatment to save your natural tooth along with relieving you from the severe pain.

Dental Fillings

In the filling procedure, decay is removed from the tooth and the surface of the tooth is filled with white-colored materials.

Dental Crowns And Bridges

We replace the missing teeth with a dental bridge or a crown to restore your smile and your life.

Dentures

Dentures are artificial teeth that replace the missing teeth. Made of colored plastic in the shape of the tooth.

Implants

Dental implants are artificial roots which are generally put in to support the crowns to replace the missing teeth.

Veneers

Dental veneers are very thin shells which are used to enhance the appearance of the teeth.

Lumineers

Lumineer is a thin cover that is placed over the teeth to hide all sorts of cosmetic issues.

Periodontal Treatment

We offer periodontal care to all our patients whereby we take care of your health.
