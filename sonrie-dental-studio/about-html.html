About

Sonrie Dental Studio

Our dental Practice is located in the heart of Coral Gables, and was designed to offer you exceptional dental care, from Preventive Care to General Dentistry.

Our main goal is to facilitate a relaxing and non-threatening office environment!

We’re committed to giving exceptional dental care, with a focus on patient satisfaction, well-being and confidence. We hope you enjoy our dental family as much as we enjoy offering dental treatment to you.

Our Doctors

At Sonrie Dental Studio, you will find that we have an outstanding team of dentists who are dedicated to providing dental care and customer service that exceeds your expectations.

Dr. Omarys Reyes

Dr. Maria Rodriguez

Dr Rodriguez started her dental career in Colombia, South America. She migrated to the United States and completed two years of Advanced Education in General Dentistry at the University of Florida.

Since then she has focused her practice in endodontics, oral surgery and dental implant placement along with the practice of general dentistry. She aims for excellence while addressing her patients needs. She offers integrated dental services for all patients.

Francisco Lozano

Francisco Lozano is a dental hygienist based out of Queens, New York. With over ten years of experience in the dental industry, Lozano has the knowledge and expertise to perform a variety of procedures.

Francisco is motivated, responsible and reliable, and always works with the comfort of his patient in mind. He is trained in local anesthesia, nitrous oxide administration, and nutrition based oral health. With a positive attitude and strong work ethic, Francisco makes a valuable addition to our team.
