Exams and Cleanings

We recommend you visit us at least twice a year for dental cleanings and exams. Our expert hygienists provide gentle teeth cleaning services. Teeth cleaning is the removal of dental plaque and tartar from teeth to prevent cavities, gingivitis, and gum disease. At this visit, we will also perform a thorough oral exam and utilize digital x-rays to diagnose and monitor any dental issues. If any dental issues are identified, our doctors will thoroughly explain the concern, and help select the best treatment option for your individual needs..

Crowns and Bridges

Crowns and bridges are used to strengthen and protect your teeth as well as enhance your smile. Crowns are placed over your weakened or decayed tooth to prevent further damage. A bridge replaces a missing tooth by first placing crowns on either side of the missing tooth, and then securing a false crown in the middle gap to replace the missing tooth. Crowns and bridges are usually made out of porcelain or metal alloys. They are extremely durable and do not typically require additional maintenance outside of the recommended care for your own teeth

Veneers

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin shells of ceramic material, which are bonded to the front of a tooth with very strong resin cement. This procedure can be the ideal choice for improving the appearance of the front teeth. Porcelain veneers are also placed to mask discolored teeth or change the shape and appearance of crooked or chipped teeth. Our doctors are excited to help you improve your smile and your confidence by customizing veneers for you!

Whitening

We offer a home bleaching system that gives our patients a whiter, brighter smile. We begin by taking impressions of the patients’ teeth and then fabricate very thin plastic ‘trays’ into which the bleach is dispensed.

We offer general dental services for kids, toddlers and teens. We strive to provide the best treatment to ensure every little patient feels comfortable throughout the process.

Endodontics

If a tooth is damaged by decay or injury, the canals inside the root of the tooth may become infected. If the infection is not treated, it can lead to severe pain, abscess and tooth loss. Fortunately, our doctors can remove the infection from the tooth, which will restore the tooth to normal function, in most cases. This treatment is also known as a root canal.
