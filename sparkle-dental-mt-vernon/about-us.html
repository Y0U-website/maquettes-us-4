Meet Dr. Rohit Z. Patel, DDS

Dr. Patel graduated at the top of his class at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in New York, then did his postgraduate studies there in endodontics, the field of dentistry which includes root canal treatments and addresses dental injuries in order to preserve your natural teeth.

Dr. Patel began practicing dentistry in 1979 as a specialist in endodontics. In 1994, he expanded his practice to become a full-service dental facility, offering general dentistry services and all specialties under one roof at Sparkle Dental.

From the beginning, Dr. Patel’s approach to dentistry has been to give each patient personalized, individual treatment geared towards improving their dental health. Each person has their own specific dental issues which need to be addressed and Dr. Patel has assembled a staff of specialists that can help patients with any type of dental issue they are experiencing.

Our Office The staff at Sparkle Dental care about each patient as an individual person. You’re not just another patient in the office. You get prompt, courteous service from people dedicated to delivering the highest quality dental care. And because we offer all dental specialties under one roof, you can have any dental need addressed at Sparkle Dental.

What We Can Do for You

In addition to our general dentistry services to keep your teeth healthy and clean, we offer the following dental specialties to improve your smile.

We are here to help you with all your dental needs, from routine checkups and restorative procedures to specialty services like dental implants and smile makeovers. And we are always happy to welcome new patients. Call us at (914) 668-1722 to schedule an appointment.

  • Veneers : Dental veneers can repair a variety of problems, including chipped or cracked teeth, gaps between teeth and uneven teeth. Veneers are a great way to improve your smile.
  • Invisalign : The latest approach to straightening your crooked teeth, the Invisalign method uses clear aligner trays which are virtually invisible while you’re wearing them, unlike the traditional metal braces which are very obvious.
  • Teeth Whitening : Much more effective and faster than over-the-counter whitening products, our in-office teeth whitening will let you see immediate results. Your smile will be whiter when you leave our office.
  • Dental Bonding : Used to repair damaged teeth, dental bonding can restore a tooth that has been broken or chipped.
  • Dental Implants : Dental implants are the best way to replace one or more missing teeth. The implant is attached to your jawbone, providing a permanent setting for the replacement tooth. Your new tooth will look and perform just like your natural teeth.
  • All-on-4 : For people who wear dentures, All-on-4 is a way to have your replacement teeth permanently attached so they don’t slip or shift on you while eating or speaking. You won’t need to take your teeth out and soak them overnight anymore!
