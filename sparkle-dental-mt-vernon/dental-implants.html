Dental Implants

Are you missing a tooth? The latest development in tooth replacement technology is dental implants. With implants, your replacement teeth are firmly attached to your jaw so you can eat and smile with confidence and you never have to take them out and soak them overnight. Whether you need a single tooth replaced or several, Sparkle Dental can give you a full set of natural-looking teeth using dental implants.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post which is attached to your jawbone. An artificial tooth is attached to the titanium post, or implant, and remains permanently fixed in your mouth. We use titanium for the implant because it’s a metal which the body will not reject and will not have an allergic reaction to. In fact, your bone will start to grow around the titanium post, fusing to it and anchoring it securely in place. When it comes to your oral health, titanium implants are the best choice to replace missing teeth.

  • How Do I Know If Dental Implants Are Right for Me? Most people who are in good health are excellent candidates for dental implants. Factors which may affect how well implants will work for you are any health issues which would interfere with healing or extensive bone loss in your jaw from missing teeth. All-On-4 Dental Implants

Would you like to replace your dentures with a securely attached set of teeth? Now there’s a solution that can give you a firm bite again without the disadvantages of dentures. All-on-4 refers to placing four dental implants into your upper or lower jaw. A full set of artificial teeth is then attached to the implants, giving you a new set of teeth that won’t slip or shift while you eat.
