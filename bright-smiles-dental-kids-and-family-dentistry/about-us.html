Dr. Vishal M. Bant is the lead dentist at Bright Smiles Dental with over 10 years of experience. He attended the Advanced Standing program at Howard University College of Dentistry where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree and got extensive training in various aspects of dentistry. He graduated with 2nd rank among the entire class and received honorary membership of Omicron Kappa Upsilon national dental honor society. He received his fellowship status in implant dentistry through international congress of oral implantologists

Dr.Bant is multilinguistic and proficient in Hindi, Gujarati. English and Russian languages. He enjoys swimming, yoga and meditation.
