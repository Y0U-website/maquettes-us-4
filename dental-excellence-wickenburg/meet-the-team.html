Listening, Explaining & Caring

Dr. Jones & his team have dedicated their professional careers to providing you & your family with the best that dentistry has to offer. From routine checkups to restorative care, the office of Dr. Josh Jones is your neighborhood headquarters for all things dental.

Josh Jones, DMD

General Dentist

Dr. Jones is an AMAZING dentist with a one-of-a-kind friendly chairside manner. He makes the most anxious patients feel instantly at ease.

He’s happily married to Miranda with three children, Larkyn Landrey and Maverick. They recently purchased land in Grantham Ranch and enrolled their daughters in the Wickenburg Christian Academy.

Doctor’s Resume: Dr. Josh Jones graduated from Midwestern University in 2013. He then accepted a commission in the U.S. Navy as a Naval Dental Officer was stationed at Naval Hospital Camp Pendleton in California, where he completed a one-year General Practice Residency. He was then deployed with the fleet spent the next four years providing high-quality, reliable dentistry to service members their families. He also provided education humanitarian services to citizens of other countries whom he was privileged to meet.

Dr. Jones is comfortable practicing all aspects of dentistry. He prides himself on being a well-rounded, highly adept dental care provider. He believes patient education is the key to helping patients see the value in dental care, he values each every appointment as an opportunity to make a difference in someone’s life!
