What are dental implants?

What is dental implant restoration?

A dental implant restoration is a device designed to attach to your dental implant and replace one or several original teeth. The team at Michael S. Yung DDS creates your restorations to look nearly identical to your original teeth after a thorough examination of their sizes, shapes, and shades. Crown

A crown is a single replacement tooth that attaches to a single implant abutment. Your provider cements the crown to the abutment and makes sure it fits comfortably and looks natural. Denture

A denture replaces several teeth. If you need to restore many teeth in a row, your provider can create your implant restoration with fewer implants than the one per tooth you’ll need for crowns.
