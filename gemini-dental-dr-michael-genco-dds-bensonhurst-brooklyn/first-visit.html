First Appointment With Us

We understand you have a busy schedule — when preparing for your visit to our office, please be advised that your appointment can last between 45 to 90 minutes depending on the procedure.

If your first visit is for a cleaning and exam , the hygienist will take you in for a cleaning. One of our dental assistants will take a full set of X-rays of your mouth, and the doctor will examine your mouth and discuss your X-rays with you.

Upon your first visit, your dental and medical history will be reviewed and saved within our system. The doctor will take an iTero scan of your mouth to digitally map out your teeth . The scan serves as a supplement to your care, allowing the doctor to visually explain any potential problem areas, simulate treatments like Invisalign, and keep a record to track teeth movement and changes to your oral health.
