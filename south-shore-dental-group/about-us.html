Neil Rosenberg, D.D.S.

Neil Rosenberg, D.D.S. is a restorative and cosmetic dentist who earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Georgetown University in Washington, D.C. Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Rosenberg moved to Nantucket where he raised his family and established his first private practice. In 2015, after many years practicing on his own, Dr. Rosenberg decided to sell his private practice and join South Shore Dental Group. He brings with him a depth of talent in restorative, prosthodontic, and implant dentistry.

Prior to dental school, Dr. Rosenberg graduated from Lehigh University with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering.

To further his expertise and deliver exceptional care to his patients, Dr. Rosenberg regularly attends continuing education courses to learn the latest advancements in procedures, materials, and technology.

As the inventor and patent holder of the original prophy cup for the Oral-B disposable prophy angle utilized by dental hygienists during your professional cleaning , Dr. Rosenberg was involved in the product development and manufacturing process.

  • Pre-Med Degree -completed at the Universidad de Guadalajara, Mexico
  • B.S in Mechanical Engineering - Lehigh University

Jennifer Soncini, D.M.D., C.A.G.S.

Dr. Jennifer Soncini brings 30 years of experience in pediatric dentistry to South Shore Dental Group — and she is in love with her job across all levels. From the personalities and treatments, to volume of patients, to the clinical and educational work that she does. She currently holds the Academic Position of Clinical Associate Professor in General Dentistry and in Pediatric Dentistry at the Boston University School of Dental Medicine.

In 2007, Dr. Soncini became a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

She is now a member of the Examination committee for re-certification in Pediatric Dentistry and an appointed oral examiner for the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

Her numerous clinical research studies have been published in the International Journal of Pediatric Dentistry, The Journal of the American Dental Association (JADA), Community Dental and Oral Epidemiology, the Journal of Public Health Dentistry, the Journal of Pedodontics, and the journal of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Soncini’s research has been presented at highly regarded meetings of the International Association of Dental Research, and the American academy of pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Soncini served as Director of the Forsyth School Oral Health Program (Forsyth-Kids) and Dental Director of The Chelsea School Dental Center. She has held teaching appointments at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, Harvard University AEGD program, and Mount Ida School for Dental Hygiene. In addition, she is credentialed at Boston Medical Center, Cambridge Hospital, and Franciscan Children’s Hospital where she treats her hospital cases.

Her experience in the care and management of children in the dental setting is enhanced by her talents in working with children with sensory and developmental disabilities. We are both proud & fortunate to have Dr. Soncini at our practice providing the quality of care for children that our adult patients have come to expect at South Shore Dental Group. Dr. Soncini also speaks Spanish and Italian!

Frank Schiano, D.M.D.

Dr. Schiano’s experience has helped shape his orthodontic practice philosophy. He is hyper-aware of the challenges of multi-disciplinary treatment plans and works collaboratively with other dentists and dental specialists to develop a problem list, treatment objectives, and consistent predictable outcomes for his adult patients. He has the ability to combine the Invisalign® clear-aligner system with traditional braces for cases where esthetics is a concern. He also thoroughly enjoys working with and treating children of all ages.

His service to the community goes unmatched. Dr. Schiano worked tirelessly for over five years to help form the Boston District of the Massachusetts Dental Society. A 2010 graduate of the MDS Leadership Institute, he was awarded the Massachusetts Dental Society’s “10 Under Ten” Award in 2011. He also received the 2011 American Dental Association’s Award for International Service for his leadership in Project Stretch, an international pediatric dental service organization, of which he is an active Board member. In 2014, Dr. Schiano was named one of four MDS “William McKenna Volunteer Heroes”.

When not spending time at the office with his patients, Dr. Schiano enjoys yoga, going to the movies, and eating good food with his closest family and friends. ‍
