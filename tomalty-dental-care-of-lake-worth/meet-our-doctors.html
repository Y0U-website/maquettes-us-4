Meet Reena Rothman , DMD

Originally from New York, Dr. Reena Rothman earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Nova Southeastern University. Dr. Rothman has taken many continuing education courses in restorative and cosmetic dentistry and keeps up to date with the newest technology. She is committed to providing excellent patient care and strives to make dental visits a low-stress and positive experience. In her spare time, Dr. Rothman enjoys traveling, music, photography, baking, and spending quality time with her husband and son Kobe.

Meet Andrea Santo , DMD

Dr. Andrea Santo, born in Brazil and raised in the USA, graduated with highest honors in biology from Florida Atlantic University. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Florida College of Dentistry. She is dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care to her patients.

Meet Dr. Hickman , DMD

Dr. Hickman is a dedicated dentist serving the Lake Worth Beach community. A native of Charleston, South Carolina, Dr. Hickman earned a Master of Medical Science degree from the Medical University of South Carolina before pursuing his dental education at Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree.

Meet Brian Waldau , DMD

Dr. Brian Waldau is an exceptional dentist who combines his professional expertise with a caring approach, ensuring each patient feels comfortable and well-cared for during their visit. His commitment to providing high-quality dental care and his warm demeanor make every appointment a positive experience.
