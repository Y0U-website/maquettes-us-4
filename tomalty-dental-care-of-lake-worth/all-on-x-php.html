What Are All-on-X Implants?

The All-on-X implant procedure is ideal for patients looking to replace their full dentures or restore a failing smile. This innovative treatment delivers beautiful, long-lasting results in just one day. Whether you need implants on your upper arch, lower arch, or both, the All-on-X approach uses four strategically placed implants to support a custom-designed restoration made just for you.

Benefits of All-On-X Implants

Denture-Free Confidence

All-on-X implants provide a stable, long-lasting solution that eliminates the need for adhesives and removable dentures.

Preserved Jawbone Health

By integrating with the jawbone, All-on-X implants stimulate and maintain bone density, preventing the bone loss associated with conventional dentures.

Reliable Chewing

All-on-X implants let you comfortably enjoy all your favorite foods. Their secure design restores natural chewing strength and stability, helping you eat with confidence and enjoy every meal just like you used to.

The All-on-X Process

At Tomalty Dental Care, the All-on-X implant process is simple and straightforward. The procedure is completed in just one day, starting with the removal of any remaining teeth, followed by the placement of dental implants, and the fitting of a temporary set of teeth to aid in the healing process.

Over the next few months, you’ll have a few follow-up visits to monitor your progress. After about six months, once the implants have fully integrated with the bone and your gums are healthy, we’ll take impressions for your permanent set of teeth. The result? A beautiful, strong, and healthy smile that will last for years to come!
