What is Invisalign?

Invisalign presents a modern alternative to traditional braces . It involves a series of virtually invisible, custom-made aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. These aligners are crafted from comfortable, smooth material, offering a discreet and comfortable experience throughout your teeth-straightening journey.

How Invisalign Aligners Are Made

The aligners are made through a combination of having an experienced Beverly Hills dentist and 3-D computer imaging technology . Since there are no brackets or wires, the Invisalign trays are smooth to the tongue and almost invisible to others. Patients love this!

Correct an Imperfect Smile with Invisalign Invisible Aligners

What are the benefits of Invisalign?

Invisalign is the invisible way to straighten your teeth without braces . Invisalign uses a series of clear removable aligners to straighten your teeth without metal wires or brackets. Because the treatment involves clear braces, you will not have to worry about the embarrassment that is often associated with traditional metal braces. Invisalign has been proven effective in clinical research.

The Invisalign Process

The Invisalign process is designed with your convenience in mind. After a comprehensive evaluation at NH Dentistry, Dr. Neil Hadaegh develops a personalized treatment plan tailored to your unique needs. Your custom aligners are then crafted, and you’ll progress through a series of aligner sets, each gently nudging your teeth into their optimal alignment. With regular check-ins, your progress is closely monitored to ensure the best possible outcome.

  • Each set of aligners is worn for approximately two weeks, with removal necessary only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing.
  • With each transition to the next aligner in the series, your teeth gradually shift, progressing week by week, ultimately reaching the prescribed final alignment set by your orthodontist or dentist. Affordable Smiles. Schedule a FREE Consultation !
  • You’ll have appointments with Hadaegh approximately every six weeks to monitor the progress of your treatment and ensure it’s on track.
  • The overall treatment duration typically falls within the range of nine to 15 months , with the average number of aligners worn varying between 18 and 30. However, these figures can vary based on individual cases.

Invisalign Frequently Asked Questions

Is Invisalign uncomfortable?

Invisalign is designed for comfort. You may experience mild discomfort as your teeth adjust to each new aligner, but it’s typically manageable and temporary.

How often should I wear my Invisalign aligners?

For optimal results, wearing your aligners for 20-22 hours a day is recommended, only removing them for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing.

Are there any food restrictions with Invisalign?

Invisalign aligners are removable so you can enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. Simply remove them before eating, and remember to brush them before reinserting.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

The duration varies based on individual needs, but the average treatment time with Invisalign ranges from 12 to 18 months .
