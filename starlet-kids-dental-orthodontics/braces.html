What Are Braces in Sylmar?

Traditional braces are fixed orthodontic appliances used to straighten teeth and correct bite issues. The system consists of small metal or ceramic brackets that are bonded directly to the front surface of each tooth. A thin, flexible archwire is threaded through these brackets and held in place by small elastic bands.

The wire applies constant, gentle pressure to the teeth, slowly moving them into their correct positions over time. During regular appointments, the orthodontist adjusts or replaces the wire to continue guiding the teeth. Modern brackets are significantly smaller, smoother, and less noticeable than those used in the past, making the treatment much more comfortable for young patients.

Why Your Child Might Need Braces

Fixed orthodontic appliances are highly versatile and can address a wide variety of dental and facial irregularities.

Resolving Severe Crowding

When the jaw is too small to accommodate all the permanent teeth, crowding occurs. This causes teeth to overlap, twist, or erupt in the wrong positions. Fixed appliances provide the robust force needed to gradually expand the arch and move each tooth into its proper place, creating a straight and functional smile.

Correcting Complex Bite Issues

Overbites, underbites, and crossbites can cause significant functional problems, including difficulty chewing and uneven wear on the enamel. Fixed appliances are exceptionally effective at correcting these complex bite discrepancies. By aligning the upper and lower jaws correctly, treatment improves both function and facial symmetry.

Closing Large Gaps Between Teeth

Significant spaces between teeth can trap food and affect the appearance of the smile. Fixed appliances apply the precise pressure needed to draw the teeth together and close these gaps permanently. This creates a more uniform smile and prevents neighboring teeth from shifting into the empty spaces.

Guiding Impacted Teeth

Sometimes a permanent tooth fails to erupt properly and remains stuck, or impacted, in the jawbone. Fixed appliances can be used in conjunction with minor oral surgery to gently pull the impacted tooth down into its correct position in the dental arch. This is a complex movement that requires the control provided by brackets and wires.

What Happens If Braces Are Delayed or Avoided?

Choosing not to address significant alignment issues can lead to worsening dental health as a child matures. Severely crowded teeth are incredibly difficult to keep clean, drastically increasing the risk of tooth decay and periodontal disease.

Uncorrected bite problems can lead to chronic jaw pain, temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders, and difficulty chewing. The social and emotional impact of a visibly misaligned smile can also affect a teenager’s confidence and willingness to engage with peers.

Addressing these issues with reliable orthodontic treatment prevents long-term complications and supports overall well-being.

FAQs About Braces in Sylmar, CA

How long will my child need to wear Braces in Sylmar?

Treatment time varies depending on the severity of the misalignment, but most patients wear them for 18 to 24 months. Dr. Partovi will provide a more specific timeline during your consultation.

Do Braces in Sylmar hurt when they are put on?

What foods should my child avoid with Braces in Sylmar?

Patients must avoid hard, sticky, and chewy foods, such as caramel, chewing gum, popcorn, nuts, and hard candies. These foods can break the brackets or bend the wires, which delays treatment.

How does my child brush and floss with Braces in Sylmar?

We will provide special tools, such as floss threaders and interdental brushes, to help clean around the brackets and wires. Brushing after every meal is highly recommended to prevent plaque buildup.

Can my child choose the colors for their Braces in Sylmar?

Yes, the small elastic bands that hold the wire in place come in a wide variety of colors. Patients can choose new colors at every adjustment appointment, making the process more fun.

What should we do if a wire breaks on my child’s Braces in Sylmar?

If a wire breaks or a bracket comes loose, contact our office immediately to schedule a repair appointment. If a sharp wire is poking the cheek, you can cover it with orthodontic wax until you can come in.

Will my child need to wear a retainer after Braces in Sylmar?

Yes, wearing a retainer is essential after active treatment is complete. It holds the teeth in their new, corrected positions while the surrounding bone stabilizes.

Are Braces in Sylmar more effective than clear aligners?

For very complex cases, severe crowding, or significant bite issues, fixed appliances often provide the precise control needed to achieve the best results. Dr. Partovi will recommend the most effective option for your child’s specific needs.
