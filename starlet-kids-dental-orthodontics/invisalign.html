What Is Invisalign in Sylmar?

Invisalign is an advanced orthodontic system that replaces traditional brackets and wires with a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners. These aligners are fabricated using precise 3D imaging of the patient’s mouth to provide a snug, comfortable fit. Each set of trays is designed to make small, calculated movements, gradually shifting the teeth over time.

Patients wear each set of aligners for about one to two weeks before moving on to the next set in the series. Because the trays are made of clear, smooth plastic, they are virtually unnoticeable when worn. Crucially, the aligners are removable, meaning patients can take them out to eat, drink, brush, and floss normally. This system requires discipline, as the trays must be worn for 20 to 22 hours a day to be effective.

Why Your Child Might Need Invisalign

Clear aligner therapy is an excellent choice for responsible teens who want to improve their smile with minimal disruption to their daily lives.

Desiring a Discreet Treatment Option

The most appealing aspect of clear aligners is their near-invisibility. Teens who feel self-conscious about the idea of traditional metal hardware appreciate that they can undergo orthodontic treatment without drawing attention to their teeth. This discreet approach helps maintain their self-esteem throughout the process.

Maintaining Dietary Freedom

Traditional orthodontic hardware comes with a long list of restricted foods to prevent broken brackets and wires. Because clear aligners are removed before meals, patients can continue to enjoy all their favorite foods, including popcorn, apples, and chewy snacks, without worry.

Simplifying Oral Hygiene

Cleaning around fixed brackets and wires can be challenging and time-consuming. With removable aligners, teens simply take the trays out to brush and floss their teeth exactly as they normally would. This makes it much easier to maintain excellent oral hygiene and prevents the white spot lesions sometimes associated with fixed appliances.

Enjoying Greater Comfort

The smooth plastic of the aligners eliminates the risk of sharp wires or bulky brackets irritating the cheeks and lips. While patients still feel pressure when switching to a new set of trays, the overall experience is generally considered more comfortable than traditional methods.

What Happens If Invisalign Is Delayed or Avoided?

When mild to moderate alignment issues are ignored, they rarely resolve on their own and often become more pronounced over time. Crooked or overlapping teeth create tight spaces where plaque accumulates, significantly increasing the risk of cavities and gum inflammation. Minor bite issues can lead to uneven wear on the tooth enamel and contribute to jaw discomfort.

If a teen is hesitant to get traditional hardware, offering a clear, removable alternative helps them still receive the necessary treatment. Delaying care only allows the dental issues to worsen, potentially requiring more invasive and lengthy interventions in the future.

Invisalign in Sylmar FAQs

How many hours a day must Invisalign in Sylmar be worn?

For the treatment to be effective, the aligners must be worn for 20 to 22 hours a day. They should only be removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing.

Is Invisalign in Sylmar painful?

The treatment is generally very comfortable. Patients typically feel some pressure or mild soreness for the first day or two after switching to a new set of aligners, which indicates the teeth are moving.

Can my teen eat with Invisalign in Sylmar in their mouth?

No, the aligners must be removed before eating or chewing gum. Eating with them in can damage the plastic and trap food against the teeth, increasing the risk of cavities.

How does my teen clean their Invisalign in Sylmar aligners?

The aligners should be brushed gently with a soft toothbrush and water, or cleaned using specialized cleaning crystals. Avoid using hot water, as it can warp the plastic.

What happens if my teen loses an Invisalign in Sylmar aligner?

If an aligner is lost or broken, contact our office immediately. Depending on where your teen is in their treatment cycle, we may advise them to wear the previous set or move forward to the next set.

Are there food restrictions with Invisalign in Sylmar?

Because the aligners are removed for meals, there are no food restrictions. Your teen can continue to enjoy all their favorite foods throughout their treatment.

Can Invisalign in Sylmar fix severe bite issues?

Clear aligners are highly effective for mild to moderate crowding and spacing issues. For very severe bite discrepancies or complex tooth movements, traditional or lingual hardware may be recommended instead.

Will my teen need a retainer after Invisalign in Sylmar?

Yes, just like with any orthodontic treatment, wearing a retainer is necessary after the active phase is complete to keep the teeth in their new, straight positions.
