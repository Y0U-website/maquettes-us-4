What to Bring to Your Appointment

To help us serve you efficiently, please bring the following to your first visit:

A valid photo ID

Your dental insurance card or policy information

A list of current medications and health conditions

Any recent dental records or X-rays, if applicable

Arrive about 10 minutes early to complete any remaining paperwork.

Accepted Insurance Plans

We proudly accept all PPO dental insurance plans and will file claims on your behalf to help maximize your benefits.

While we are not in-network with HMO or Medicaid plans, we’re happy to work with you to review out-of-network options and costs.

If you have questions about your specific insurance, please contact our office prior to your visit.

Flexible Payment Options

We make paying for dental care as easy as possible:

Cash, debit, or credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Discover, Amex)

CareCredit® financing available for qualified patients

Transparent treatment estimates provided before any service begins

Need help budgeting for more extensive care? We’ll work with you to create a personalized payment plan that fits your needs.

What to Expect as a New Patient

During your first visit, you’ll receive a full oral exam, digital X-rays (if needed), and a personalized consultation with Dr. Elena Opare, DDS . She’ll discuss your dental history, address any concerns, and walk you through your treatment options — with no pressure or sales talk.

If you&#x27;re due for a cleaning, we’ll typically complete it during the same appointment unless other care is urgently needed.

Family-Friendly Care for All Ages

From toddlers to seniors, we welcome patients at every stage of life. Our team works hard to create a calm, educational, and respectful environment for children, teens, adults, and older adults alike.

Serving Bloomington & South Minneapolis, MN

Our conveniently located dental office off E Bloomington Fwy welcomes patients from Bloomington, Richfield, Edina, South Minneapolis, and surrounding communtiies .

We offer same-day emergency appointments , flexible scheduling, and modern care for busy families and working adults.
