Education & Background

Dr. Opare earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) with Distinction from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry , one of the top dental programs in the country. Her training emphasized evidence-based care, patient-centered communication, and comprehensive treatment planning.

Since graduating, she has completed numerous continuing education courses in:

Clinical dentistry and restorative techniques

Practice optimization and workflow efficiency

Emerging technology and digital diagnostics

Her commitment to lifelong learning ensures that patients benefit from modern, effective, and personalized care.
