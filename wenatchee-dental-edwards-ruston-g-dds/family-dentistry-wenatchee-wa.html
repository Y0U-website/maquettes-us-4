Sleep Apnea Oral Appliances Sleep apnea can lead to serious health issues if not treated, including the risk of heart attack and stroke. Wenatchee Dental understands the importance of quality sleep and offers oral appliances designed to help you get a good night’s rest. We’ll customize an appliance that fits comfortably in your mouth and helps keep your airway open while you sleep, allowing for improved breathing and a better quality of life.

The appliance works by adjusting the position of your jaw and tongue to prevent your airway from collapsing or becoming obstructed during sleep. This helps to maintain proper airflow and ensures that your body gets the oxygen it needs while you sleep. By using an oral appliance, you may also reduce or eliminate snoring, which can disrupt your sleep and affect the sleep of those around you.
