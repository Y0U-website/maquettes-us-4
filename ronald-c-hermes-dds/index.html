Dental implants are an important treatment if you are missing a tooth because it can restore form and function and ensure that you maintain a proper bite. Without an implant, your teeth can shift and you could lose healthy jawbone over time. Dr. Shirley and Dr. Hermes offer dental implant placement and restoration services at our Shreveport general dentistry office.

The road to getting dental implants begins with a consultation with Dr. Shirley or Dr. Hermes. The dentist will determine whether you have enough bone or gum tissue to support the implant or if measures need to be taken to build up more bone or gum tissue.
