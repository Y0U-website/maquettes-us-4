Dr. Bhargavi Dasari

Dr. Bhargavi Dasari is an accomplished dentist, academician, researcher, and yoga instructor. With over 5 years of experience in the dental profession, she has demonstrated a strong commitment to serving underserved communities and advancing oral health globally.

Dr. Dasari holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (B.D.S.) degree from MGR University, a Master’s in Oral Medicine and Radiology from NTR University, and a Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Rutgers University. She is an esteemed member of several national and international dental organizations, including the North Carolina State Dental Society, the American Association of Endodontics, and the Indian Dental Association.

Passionate about cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Dasari is trained in Invisalign, smile design, and the use of Botox and fillers to enhance her patients’ smiles. She takes great pride in helping her patients achieve the confident, beautiful smiles they desire.

Throughout her career, Dr. Dasari has made significant contributions to scientific research and literature, with over 30 published articles. She has also served as a principal investigator for multiple research projects, showcasing her expertise and commitment to evidence-based dentistry.

Recognizing the unique challenges faced by international dentists, Dr. Dasari co-founded the non-profit organization “The American Association of International Dentists,” dedicated to educating, supporting, and advocating for foreign dentists in the United States.

Dr. Dasari’s exceptional qualifications, dedication to dental education and research, commitment to community service, and leadership in the dental community have earned her numerous accolades, including an upcoming honour at the Pierre Richard Academy induction ceremony and International College of Dentistry.
