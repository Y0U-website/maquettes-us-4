Mordehay Rubinchik, DDS Dr. Rubinchik earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from New York University College of Dentistry in 1998. Post graduation, he has made continuing education a priority and returned to New York University to pursue a two-year course of advanced training in the area of Dental Implantology, Surgical and Prosthetic Treatment.

His passion is creating beautiful smiles, and he has recently accomplished his life dream of constructing a dental facility that combines state-of-the-art equipment and technology with a modern, relaxed, and serene setting.

Dr. Rubinchik believes in respect, compassion, comfort, and trust. In striving to provide outstanding personalized dental care, he goes to great lengths to explain your options and alternative dental treatments, and truly values the long-term relationships he develops with his patients.

Dr. Rubinchik is fluent in Hebrew, Russian, and Romanian.
