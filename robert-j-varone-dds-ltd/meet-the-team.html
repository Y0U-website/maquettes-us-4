Meet The Team

Robert J Varone DDS Ltd is a locally owned practice that was founded in 1984. We provide quality dental services and offer surgical extractions within our office. Our office is handicap-accessible and is kid-friendly. Emergency services are available to be seen at all times!

You’re always free to ask any questions pertaining to dental care with any of our staff members. No question is considered insignificant. We’re always happy to help you and will accommodate your needs. Call us today!

Dr. Robert Varone

Dr. Robert Varone is a CEREC-certified practitioner providing dentistry services in the Warwick area since 1984. He can fabricate all-porcelain crowns and veneers and can insert them on the same day. He provides root canal treatments, extractions of all types, implants, bleaching, and a wide range of denture prostheses. He also minimizes the dental anxiety of his patients through nitrous oxide.

Dr. Varone grew up in Providence’s Mount Pleasant section and attended LaSalle Academy. He holds a BS degree in Biology and a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree (DDS) from Creighton, both from the University in Omaha, NE, a Jesuit institution.

Dr. Varone currently maintains active dental licenses in Rhode Island and Florida. He is married and has two young boys.
