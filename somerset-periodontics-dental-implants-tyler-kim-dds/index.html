The practice

Dr. Tyler Kim is a board-certified periodontist specializing in gum disease treatment, dental implants, and cosmetic periodontal procedures. His commitment to continuing education and patient-centered care ensures every patient receives the highest standard of treatment in a comfortable, modern environment.

At Somerset Periodontics & Dental Implants, exceptional care begins long before you sit in the chair. Our team — made up of a dedicated dental hygienist, skilled dental assistant, and warm front desk coordinator — works together every day to make sure your experience is seamless, comfortable, and truly personalized.

From the moment you walk through the door, our front desk team ensures you feel welcomed, informed, and at ease. Behind the scenes and chairside, our clinical team brings precision, compassion, and years of expertise to every appointment — working closely with Dr. Kim to deliver the highest standard of periodontal care.

We are proud of the environment we have built together — one where every patient is treated like family. Our team’s dedication, warmth, and professionalism are what make Somerset Periodontics a place patients trust and return to.

Dental Implants Single-tooth, multiple-tooth, and full-arch implant solutions that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Gum Disease Treatment Scaling and root planing, laser therapy, and surgical intervention to restore gum health at every stage.

Bone Grafting Rebuilding lost bone to support implants and restore natural facial structure after tooth loss.
