The practice

Dr. Ferrara moved to Sidney in 2015 and has worked hard to plant roots in this community. He has invested his time and care into Sidney by joining Lions and the Fire Department as well as many other volunteer activities.

Dr. Ferrara works in all facets of general dentistry and particularly enjoys comprehensive, aesthetic and implant dentistry. Dr. Ferrara perpetually studies new technology through continuing education courses to provide his patients with the latest techniques and treatments offered in dental medicine. Dr. Ferrara is also a member of the American Dental Association and Academy of General Dentistry.
