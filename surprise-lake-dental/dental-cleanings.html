Dental Cleanings & Exams

Dental cleanings and exams are a great way to check up on your oral health. Our experienced dentists and team recommend coming to our dental office every six months for a professional dental checkup to maintain the health and appearance of your smile.

We suggest visiting our dentists regularly for a dental cleaning and exam to help maintain your oral health and provide you with a clean, bright smile.

Digital X-Rays

These examinations often require digital X-rays. These X-rays allow our dentists to view detailed images of your teeth beneath your gums, which helps our dentists to see any additional dental conditions that may not otherwise be readily apparent. With the correct equipment and procedures, digital X-rays are certified by the American Dental Association® as a safe and normal part of your dental examination.
