About our Office

For a decade, Wharton Dental has created sensational smiles for our community. If you’re searching for a compassionate, highly skilled local dentist’s office, you’ve found it! Our top concern lies in providing the ultimate experience for patients of all ages. You’ll enjoy spa amenities and the latest technology in an amazing dental visit.

When your teeth and gums are healthy, they’ll serve you well functionally, but they’ll also give you an unforgettable smile. Your image and self-confidence depend, at least in part, upon your smile. Furthermore, healthy teeth and gums will contribute to a healthy body.

We look forward to building a strong oral healthcare relationship with you and your family here in Wharton, TX! Our team has a lot to offer you that goes far beyond what you might find at a typical office, including:

Spa-Like Amenities

We enjoy being able to provide patients with a beautiful and clean office where they can feel genuinely at ease. Our modern decorations are pleasing to the eye, and we use scented air technology in our lobby to provide a pleasant aroma. Other comforting measures like sedation dentistry are also available!

Modern & High-Tech Dental Technology

We utilize several advanced tools in order to improve the accuracy, comfort, and convenience of your dental appointments, including intraoral cameras, digital X-rays, digital impression systems, and more. We hope to exceed your expectations along every step of the way, from diagnosis to treatment to a healthier, happier smile.

Highly Skilled Dental Team

Dr. Phil Tiet has undergone additional training in various services (including orthodontic treatment, Lumineers, and much more) to better benefit their patients, and our team members have years of experience and hard work backing up their high caliber of care.

Worry-Free Dentistry

Contact our practice today to schedule your first appointment, or if you have any questions for our team. You’ll always be greeted with a warm welcome!
