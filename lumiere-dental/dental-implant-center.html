Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Wicker Park

Dental implants are designed to last decades, and with proper care many patients keep their implants for a lifetime. The titanium post integrates with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, creating a durable, permanent anchor. The crown or restoration attached to the implant may eventually need replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself typically remains intact much longer.

At Lumiere Dental, dental implants are priced between $4,000 and $7,000 per tooth. This includes all components of the process: the extraction if needed, bone grafting, gum regeneration, the implant post, the abutment, and the final crown. We handle the entire procedure in-house using an industry-leading digital workflow, which means you receive coordinated care from a single team without being referred out.

The implant placement surgery is performed under local anesthesia, so you will not feel pain during the procedure. Sedation is also available for patients who prefer a more relaxed experience. After surgery, mild soreness and swelling are normal for a few days and are typically managed with over-the-counter pain relief. Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the recovery is.

The traditional implant process takes several months from start to finish, with the longest phase being the healing period after implant placement (typically three to six months) during which osseointegration occurs. Some patients qualify for same-day implants, where a temporary restoration is placed the same day as the implant post. Dr. Woitas will discuss which timeline is appropriate for your bone density, overall health, and goals.

Smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure by impairing blood flow and slowing the osseointegration process. Smokers can still pursue implants, but Dr. Woitas will discuss the elevated risk and recommend stopping smoking before and after the procedure to maximize your chances of a successful outcome. Every case is evaluated individually, and we are committed to giving you a realistic picture of what to expect.
