Early orthodontic care designed around growth, stability, and long-term confidence, never pressure.

Orthodontics for children isn’t about starting early. It’s about understanding growth and choosing the right moment. At Jawz Orthodontics, we begin with clarity. We carefully evaluate development and recommend treatment only when it truly benefits your child. Many children don’t need treatment right away. But knowing that with confidence? That matters.

An orthodontic evaluation around age 7 gives us insight into how your child’s jaw, bite, and airway development are progressing. It does not always mean early braces, it means clarity at a stage when growth is still adaptable. Most families leave with reassurance.
