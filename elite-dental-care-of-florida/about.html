About Dr. Reynaldo G mez Valenzuela, D.D.S. Committed to Your Smile Our experienced dental team is here to make every visit positive and personalized. With gentle hands and caring hearts.

Dr. Reynaldo G mez Valenzuela is a skilled and compassionate dentist with a broad background in general and advanced dental care. He earned his DDS in 2017 from Universidad Iberoamericana (UNIBE) in the Dominican Republic and practiced there for two years before completing a residency at the Cleveland Dental Institute in Ohio.

During his residency, Dr. G mez focused on treating medically complex patients and those requiring full-mouth rehabilitation, often under general anesthesia or oral sedation in both hospital and outpatient settings. This experience deepened his expertise in comprehensive and specialized care.

In 2023, he returned to Florida to serve his local community and be closer to family.

Dr. G mez is committed to patient-centered care, combining empathy with the latest dental techniques to improve oral health and quality of life. His warm, attentive approach makes patients feel heard, supported, and confident in their care.

Helping people feel good about their smile is the best part of what I do Dr. G mez Valenzuela
