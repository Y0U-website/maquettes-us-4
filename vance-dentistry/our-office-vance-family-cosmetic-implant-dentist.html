Meet The Doctor

Brian D. Vance, D.D.S. Principal Dentist

Brian D. Vance, D.D.S. graduated from the University of Kentucky with a Bachelor of Science in Biology and a year of master’s work in microbiology and immunology in 1994.

He then went on to attend West Virginia University Dental School from 1994-1998, where he graduated at the top of his class. At WVU, he had the good fortune to be mentored by two delegates of the American Board of Prosthodontists. Dr. Vance then completed a general practice residency at Charleston Area Medical Center, where he received extensive training working with medically compromised patients.

Since 1999, he has held several positions in private practice, each affording him an opportunity to hone his dental expertise and skills. He has attended over 600 hours of continuing education in cosmetic and implant dentistry over the past decade alone. His top priority is his patient’s well-being and satisfaction.

When he’s not serving his patients, Dr. Vance enjoys spending time with his wife and two daughters. He also coaches his daughter’s volleyball and softball teams.

His passion for the community enables him to provide outstanding patient care and successfully build a trust based relationship with every patient.
