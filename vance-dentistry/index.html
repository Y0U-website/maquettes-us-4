Les soins

A dental bridge is a dental device that fills a space that a tooth previously occupied. It consists of a filler tooth that is attached to two crowns , which fit over the existing teeth and hold the dental bridge in place.

If you have a severely damaged or decayed tooth that is unable to support a crown, requiring extraction, dental bridges may be a viable dental solution.
