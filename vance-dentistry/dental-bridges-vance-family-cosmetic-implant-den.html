Dental Bridges

What You Need to Know About Dental Bridges

If you have a severely damaged or decayed tooth that is unable to support a crown, requiring extraction, dental bridges may be a viable dental solution.

A dental bridge is a dental device that fills a space that a tooth previously occupied. It consists of a filler tooth that is attached to two crowns , which fit over the existing teeth and hold the dental bridge in place.

Other Treatments

When Bridges May be Necessary

If you want to prevent future dental problems, here are a few reasons you may need a bridge:

  • Shifting of the teeth that can lead to bite problems, also known as dental occlusion and/or jaw problems, and resultant periodontal disease .
  • Bridges safeguard the integrity of existing teeth and help maintain a healthy, vibrant smile.

The Two Main Types of Dental Bridges

Understanding your options for repair can help you make an educated decision. Here are some of your bridge options.

  • A fixed bridge is the most popular and consists of a filler tooth that is attached to two crowns, which fit over the existing teeth and hold the bridge in place.
  • The cantilever bridge is often used when there are teeth on only one side of the span. A typical three-unit cantilever bridge consists of two crowned teeth positioned next to each other on the same side of the missing tooth space. The filler tooth is then connected to the two crowned teeth, which extend into the missing tooth space or end.
