0 Our Team Dr. Courtney Linenberg - Endodontist Dr. Courtney Linenberg has trained at some of the most prestigious programs in the country, including the University of Michigan, New York University, Mount Sinai, and Boston University. With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Linenberg honed her skills at the leading Endodontic practice in New York City, where she mastered the latest technology to enhance patient comfort and treatment outcomes.

When she established her own practice in New Providence, NJ, Dr. Linenberg brought her extensive expertise and a commitment to providing the very best care for her patients. She believes that great communication with referring doctors is crucial for quality care and patient satisfaction, always making herself available for consultations and patient inquiries.

Dr. Linenberg’s dedication extends beyond her practice; she has participated in dental outreach trips, providing free care to underserved populations in Maine, Mexico, and Tanzania. Her passion for teaching led her to serve as a Clinical Assistant Professor of Endodontics at New York University College of Dentistry, where she shared her knowledge and experience with future dental professionals.

Originally from Michigan, then NYC, and now finally settled in Westfield, Dr. Linenberg is excited to call New Jersey home. She enjoys spending time with her family—husband Eric and children Joey and Alexis. The family keeps busy with sports, travel, and skiing. Dr. Linenberg finds peace and beauty in nature. Occasionally, she enjoys a quiet moment with a cup of tea, watching birds.

Her commitment to excellence in endodontics and her compassionate approach make her a trusted and respected figure in the dental community.
