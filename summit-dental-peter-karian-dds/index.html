A missing tooth takes its toll on your appearance, your ability to eat your food comfortably, and your confidence. Even if your lost tooth is not immediately noticeable, you know it’s there when you’re trying to chew the foods you usually eat, or when you smile or laugh with friends or co-workers. It is always on your mind, and it could be causing you to feel self-conscious.

Dental implants are an excellent solution for replacing one or multiple missing teeth because they restore both the root and crown of your tooth, which is the portion you see. This provides you with a brand new tooth that looks, feels, and functions like your natural tooth.

Though a lost tooth at the back of your mouth may not seem too important to replace, this actually couldn’t be further from the truth. Every tooth has its role in how comfortably and effectively you can bite and chew your food. A missing tooth causes your remaining teeth to compensate and could make them work harder, or in ways they were not designed for chewing, resulting in cracks, fractures…

A missing tooth root also affects the health of your jawbone. Without a root to keep your bone stimulated, it s not needed. It begins to dissolve, which could impact your surrounding teeth. Meanwhile, your remaining teeth may start to move and shift out of alignment, changing your bite and increasing your risk for developing other concerns like cavities or gum disease.
