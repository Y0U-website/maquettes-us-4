Dr. Jeffrey D. Weaver

Dr. Weaver grew up in Ohio and received his undergraduate degree from Miami University and his dental degree from the Ohio State University. Since 2000, Dr. Weaver has owned multiple practices in both Virginia and South Carolina. With a passion for both general and cosmetic dentistry, he is a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and has been a certified Invisalign provider for over 10 years.

Dr. Weaver and his wife moved to Greenville in 2020 and have two grown children. In his spare time, Dr. Weaver enjoys traveling, reading, cooking and playing the piano.
