Why Dental Implants Are Essential

Restoration of Functionality: Dental implants function like natural teeth, restoring the ability to eat and speak easily. Unlike dentures, they are anchored in the jawbone, providing stability and preventing bone loss.

Aesthetic Enhancement and Oral Health: Implants offer a cosmetic improvement, filling gaps in your smile with teeth that look and feel natural. They also play a crucial role in maintaining overall oral health by supporting facial structure and preventing the shifting of adjacent teeth.

The Dental Implant Process

The implant procedure at Seaport Family Dental involves several steps:

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Deng assesses your oral health to determine implant suitability.
  • Preparation: Any necessary pre-treatments, such as extractions or bone grafting, are performed. 3D intraoral scan and 3D CBCT Radiographs are taken for digital merging and implant planning. This step ensures accurate implant placement.
  • Implant Placement: Surgical guide used from digital merging and implant placement used to place the titanium implant.
  • Healing: Gingival tissue takes about 1-2 weeks to heal, while implant integration takes approximately 4-6 months. A healing period allows the implant to integrate with the bone for a stronger implant.
  • Abutment Placement & Final restoration : Final position of implant and teeth are scanned for a custom-made abutment and crown. An abutment attaches to the implant and holds the crown.

Maintaining Your Dental Implants

Regular Oral Hygiene: Just like natural teeth, dental implants require regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups to maintain their health and longevity.

Avoiding Harmful Habits: To protect your implants, avoid habits such as smoking and chewing hard objects, as these can compromise implant integrity.

The Enduring Success of Dental Implants

With appropriate care, including regular dental check-ups and proper oral hygiene, dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime, offering a permanent and reliable solution for missing teeth. This enduring nature of implants makes them a highly sought-after option for those looking to restore their dental health. Their stability and strength ensure that patients enjoy all the benefits of natural teeth, including a fully functional bite and the ability to speak and eat.

Patients often report a significant boost in their self-esteem as they no longer feel self-conscious about their appearance. This renewed confidence can positively change personal and professional interactions, enhancing social opportunities and overall life satisfaction. The functional benefits, coupled with the aesthetic improvements, make dental implants a comprehensive solution that substantially enhances patients’ quality of life.
