The Importance of Regular Dental Cleaning and Consultation

Regular dental cleanings are essential for removing plaque and tartar that can’t be eliminated by brushing and flossing alone. These cleanings prevent gum disease, tooth decay, and other oral health issues. During these visits, Dr. Deng can evaluate your oral health, identify any concerns early, and provide tailored advice for your dental care routine.

Our Cleaning and Consultation Process

Our approach at Seaport Family Dental includes:

  • Oral Examination: Dr. Deng conducts a thorough evaluation of your oral health.
  • Professional Cleaning: Removal of plaque and tartar, followed by polishing.
  • Dental X-Rays: If necessary, to assess any underlying issues.
  • Gum Health Assessment: Checking for signs of gum disease.
  • Personalized Consultation: Discussing your oral health and providing tailored advice.
