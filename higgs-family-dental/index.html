The practice

At Higgs Family Dental, formerly Stanley Higgs D.D.S., Inc., we have been proudly serving Santa Fe, Texas, and the surrounding Galveston County area with gentle, personalized dental care since 1980. Our mission is to provide comprehensive dental treatments that prioritize your comfort, health, and smile.
