Meet the Team at Saticoy Plaza Dentistry

At Saticoy Plaza Dentistry, your oral health and well-being are of great importance to us. Dr. Daniel Bacquet has all the skills and training necessary to provide you with outstanding care that exceeds your expectations.

Please take a moment to learn more about his commitment to providing you with the highest quality treatments that dentistry has to offer.

Dr. Daniel Bacquet, DMD

Dr. Daniel Bacquet is a native of Los Angeles and earned his bachelor s degree in biology from California State University at Northridge. While attending his undergrad, he actively volunteered at the Ronald Reagan UCLA Medical Center and UCLA School of Dentistry.

Dr. Bacquet graduated from the College of Dental Medicine - Arizona at Midwestern University’s Glendale Campus with a Doctor in Dental Medicine (DMD) degree. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and the San Fernando Valley Dental Association, where he regularly attends continuing education courses.

Dr. Bacquet earned his certification in dental lasers from the Academy of Laser Dentistry. He has also completed the implant residency program provided by the International Academy of Dental Implantology.

Dr. Bacquet comes from a family of dentists, including his mother and father. His family has been practicing dentistry in the Lake Balboa area for over 40 years.

In his free time, Dr. Bacquet enjoys snowboarding, surfing, exercising, hiking, traveling, and spending time with his family.

Dr. Stephanie Mulick, DMD

Dr. Stephanie Mulick was raised in a military family and moved all over the world until her family settled into their home in Tucson, Arizona. She earned her bachelor’s degree in physiology from the University of Arizona. While growing up, her father was a Dental Technician in the Navy and sparked her interest into the dental field.

In 2017, Dr. Mulick received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine-Arizona. From 2017-2021, she actively served in the United States Navy as a Dental Officer. Her most treasured experience in the military were the two years aboard the aircraft carrier, USS Theodore Roosevelt. During this time she provided dental care for over 5,500 Sailors and successfully completed a 6-month deployment.

Dr. Mulick is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and the San Fernando Valley Dental Association. On her free time, she loves spending quality time with her family and traveling.
