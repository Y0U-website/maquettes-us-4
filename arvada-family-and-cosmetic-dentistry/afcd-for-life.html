With AFCD for Life , we reward the habits that keep your smile strong and bright. A healthy smile isn’t just a one-time goal, it’s a lifelong journey. By staying consistent with your care, you unlock ongoing perks designed to support your oral health, enhance your confidence, and with every visit become more secure in your health and your smile into the future.

FREE Sonicare for life or whitening for life

Whitening for Life

Get a refill of whitening gel at every on time cleaning visit to keep your smile beautiful! Given to you at the end of your first cleaning and after decay or gum disease has been treated to ensure your smile is healthy and bright.

Program enrollment fee waived for new patients! Cannot be combined with any other offer and treatment of all decay & gum disease must be complete prior to starting the program.

Sonicare for Life

Get a new brush head at every on time cleaning visit to help maintain your health! Given to you with professional instructions for maximum health benefits at the end of your first cleaning visit.

New Patients

Starting your dental care journey should feel exciting – not overwhelming. At Arvada Family & Cosmetic Dentistry, we make it even easier to take that first step. As a new patient, you can choose one of our featured AFCD for Life programs (either Whitening or Sonicare) at no cost when you complete your new patient visit.

It’s our way of welcoming you and helping you begin care with added value and confidence. Whether you’re interested in enhancing your smile or better maintaining your health, this offer gives you a meaningful head start.

Existing Patients

We love taking care of our patients—and we love it when you share us with friends and family. As a thank-you, existing patients can receive one of our featured AFCD for Life programs at no cost (either Whitening or Sonicare) by referring someone new to our practice. Once your referral schedules and completes their initial appointment, your reward is yours. And there is no expiration date on this offer for our loyal patients!

To make it easier for you to enjoy the program benefits right away, if you decide to buy an AFCD for Life program (either Whitening for Life at $165 or Sonicare for Life at $125) and refer someone to us who completes their initial visit within a year of your purchase, we will credit the program cost back to you for a refund or a credit towards other treatment we provide you. If you refer a second person to us you can receive the same benefit for the other AFCD for Life program!

It’s a simple way to help someone you care about get great dental care while improving your own health and smile!
