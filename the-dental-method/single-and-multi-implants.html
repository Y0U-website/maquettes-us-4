Who Needs Single and Multi Implants?

Dental implants are suited for patients who have lost one or more teeth due to decay, injury, or periodontal disease. Missing teeth can affect bite balance, spacing, and long-term oral structure. Patients who want a fixed replacement option often explore implants as part of a restorative plan.

Implant treatment is commonly considered when tooth loss begins to impact daily comfort or oral stability. Ongoing follow-up visits ensure proper integration and long-term implant stability.

Advanced Dental Implant Technology

Dental implant technology focuses on rebuilding missing teeth from the root up, starting with careful planning beneath the surface. At The Dental Method, digital imaging and 3D scanning are used to evaluate bone quality, spacing, and bite alignment before an implant is ever placed. This allows implant positioning to be planned with accuracy rather than estimation.

Surgical guides and modern instrumentation help place implants at the correct depth and angle, supporting proper integration with the jawbone. Over time, the implant fuses naturally with the bone, creating a stable foundation designed to function and feel similar to a natural tooth root.

What to Expect During Your Visit
