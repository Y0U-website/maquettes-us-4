About Us

Our Clinic

The Terre Haute office is staffed with the most professional and caring team members. Our entire staff is committed to providing warm, personalized, care that you deserve in the most technology advanced office that is backed by scientific data and research.

Continuing Education

We are firm believers that humans should continue to be educated. Therefore, our staff goes well beyond the minimum continuing educational requirements. In addition, we strive to educate our patients as to their own healthcare status and choices of differing treatment plans.

Infection Control

Infection control is the foundation of endodontics. To protect our patients and ourselves, we strictly maintain sterilization and cross contamination processes using standards recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA), the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), and the Center for Disease Control (CDC). However, we go beyond the minimum standards and expose every operatory to UV-C light after every patient. This allows for additional surface and air quality disinfection.

Modern Technology

Terre Haute Endodontics uses the most advanced 3D imaging along with surgical microscopes to to provide you with the most accurate diagnosis and treatment possible. Additionally, we continue to strive to reduce our environmental impact and carbon foot print through the use of green technology.

Zane M. Lambert, DMD

Dr. Zane M. Lambert was born and raised in Colorado Springs, CO. He graduated from the University of Denver (Go Pioneers!) with a Bachelor of Science degree in Chemistry. Prior to his career in dentistry, he was a laser chemist.

Dr. Lambert continued his post-graduate studies at the Missouri School of Dentistry Oral Health, a school within the A.T. Still University system, where he graduated with a Doctor of Dental Medicine and a Certificate in Public Health. During his time there, he was Chapter President of the American Student Dental Association and was awarded the Academy of Operative Dentistry Award.

Dr. Lambert again continued his post-doctoral studies by completing a General Practice Residency and earning a Certificate for Moderate Sedation Dentistry at Miami Valley Hospital in Dayton, OH. Upon completion of this residency, he returned to Missouri to earn his Certificate in Endodontics from the University of Missouri in Kansas City. Due to Dr. Lambert’s firm belief in education, he is an adjunct clinical faculty member at Indiana University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Lambert is an affiliate of the American Association of Endodontics, the American Dental Association, the International Association of Dental Traumatology, the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology, and is a Trustee for the Indiana Dental Association. Outside of the office, he enjoys spending time with family and friends as a car enthusiast and loves exploring new books.
