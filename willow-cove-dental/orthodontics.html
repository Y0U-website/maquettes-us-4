How is CandidPro different from Invisalign?

While both CandidPro and Invisalign are trusted clear aligner systems designed to gently shift your teeth into place, CandidPro offers the added convenience of FDA-cleared remote monitoring. That means fewer in-office visits and more flexibility without compromising on safety or results.

What are attachments, and will I need them?

Attachments are tiny, tooth-colored bumps that help aligners apply the right amount of pressure in the right places. Not everyone needs them, and CandidPro is designed to work with fewer attachments compared to other systems helping your treatment stay discreet and comfortable.

How long does treatment with CandidPro take?

Your treatment timeline depends on your smile goals and how consistently you wear your aligners. Some patients see results in as little as a few months, while more complex cases may take a year or more. We’ll guide you every step of the way to keep your smile on track.

Is the plastic material in CandidPro aligners safe?

Absolutely. CandidPro aligners are made with Zendura FLX a premium, medical-grade plastic that’s BPA-free and gentle on your mouth. It’s engineered for comfort, durability, and peace of mind.
