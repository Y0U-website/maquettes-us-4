How Can Dental Implants Help Me?

Dental implants are biocompatible posts that replace the root of your missing tooth. They are designed to integrate into your jaw bone and become the foundation for a natural-looking tooth. Your new tooth (or teeth) will have excellent stability and strength. Additionally, they will look and feel like your natural teeth.

How Long Do Implants Last?

Just like natural teeth, implants rely on good oral health care to last a long time. Your dental implants cannot get cavities. However, it is necessary to brush and floss daily to keep the bone and gums surrounding it healthy. When cared for properly, implants can last decades or even an entire lifetime!
