Dr. Troy Olson

Dr. Troy Olson is a one of a kind dentist whose passion resides in making a difference in the individual lives of patients. Originally from Hortonville, WI, Dr. Troy says he’s motivated by caring and treating every patient as if they were his own family. There is nothing more rewarding for him than seeing patients walk out the door happier and heathier each and every visit than when they walked in.

In the earliest of his professional career, Dr. Troy spent 8 years in the U.S. Navy Reserve serving as a Petty Officer Second Class. Additionally, held experience as a lead technician at BioLife Plasma Services. These two branches of his early career days rooted a deep drive for him to discover his true love of the dental field. Dr. Troy received his undergraduate degree from UW-Oshkosh and his doctorate of dental surgery degree from Marquette University in Milwaukee, WI.

Dr. Troy has a passion for cooking and trying new recipes, traveling, a variety of outdoor activities, listening to music of any genre, and loves meeting new people and learning from every opportunity that’s presented to him.

Dr. Troy, his wife Laura, their children Layne and Milo, along their two fur-children Carl (cat) and Finley (dog), reside in Green Bay.
