Garrett Steel, DMD

“You deserve a smile that lifts up you and those around you. My goal is to care for your oral health with the same attention and intention I would give my own, so you feel confident in your smile and completely at ease every time you walk through our doors.”

Dr. Steel is committed to providing comprehensive, patient-centered care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. He enjoys the full scope of general dentistry, including fillings, crowns, dentures, implants, clear aligner therapy, and emergency treatment when patients need it most. Dr. Steel values building lasting relationships with his patients and takes pride in helping them feel informed, respected, and confident in their treatment decisions.

Born in Pocatello, Idaho, and raised in Aloha, Oregon, Dr. Steel is proud to serve the community he calls home. Outside of the office, you can often find him in the kitchen baking a new flavor of macarons or experimenting with other baked creations. He also enjoys exploring new hobbies such as golf, biking, and strength training. At home, he also spends time with his tabby cat, Gator.

Experience

Dr. Steel is passionate about continuing education and staying current with modern advancements in dentistry. He is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Dental Association and regularly pursues advanced continuing education in topics such as CEREC same-day crowns, implants, and Invisalign clear aligner therapy. He is always seeking opportunities to expand his knowledge and refine his skills to provide the highest level of care.
