Why Get Dental Implants?

Even if you’ve been told in the past that you weren’t a candidate due to insufficient bone, advances in techniques like bone grafting have made implants possible for many people who were previously excluded. A thorough evaluation can determine your candidacy and identify any preparatory procedures that might be beneficial.

  • Adequate jawbone density to support the implant (or willingness to undergo bone grafting if needed)
  • Healthy gums
  • Commitment to good oral hygiene
  • Non-smoker status (or willingness to quit, as smoking can compromise implant success)

The Dental Implant Process: What to Expect

Understanding the implant process helps many patients feel more comfortable moving forward. While every case is unique, the general process typically involves:

Initial Consultation and Planning : We’ll evaluate your oral health, take X-rays or 3D scans, discuss your medical history, and create a customized treatment plan.

Implant Placement : The titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone. This procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, and most patients report minimal discomfort.

Healing Period : Over the next 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone through osseointegration. During this time, you can function normally with a temporary tooth replacement if needed.

Abutment and Crown Placement : Once healing is complete, an abutment (connector piece) is attached to the implant, and your custom crown is secured on top.

Throughout the process, we prioritize your comfort and provide clear guidance on what to expect at each stage.

Making the Investment in Your Smile and Health

Dental implants represent an investment in your long-term oral health, functionality, and quality of life. While the upfront cost is higher than other tooth replacement options, the benefits—from preserving your jawbone and facial structure to enjoying a lifetime of confident smiles and unrestricted eating—make implants invaluable for most patients.

Many dental practices, including ours, offer financing options that make dental implants accessible and affordable, allowing you to invest in your smile without financial strain.
