Dr. Sohrab Ansari Dr. Sohrab Ansari grew up in Albuquerque, NM, where he earned a bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry from the University of New Mexico. He continued his journey in dentistry at the University of Michigan, School of Dentistry, one of the top dental programs in the nation. After exploring various cities, Dr. Ansari found his perfect home in the vibrant community of Royal Oak, MI, where he is thrilled to serve patients and be part of such a dynamic area.

Passionate about providing exceptional dental care, Dr. Ansari enjoys all aspects of dentistry. He dedicates significant time each year to continuing education, mastering the latest techniques and advancements in the field to ensure his patients receive the highest quality care. His commitment to excellence is matched by his genuine joy in building lasting relationships with his patients.

Dr. Ansari has extensive training in dental implants and is a proud Fellow of the International Dental Implant Association. He is also deeply committed to giving back, prioritizing community service throughout his career.

Outside the office, Dr. Ansari leads an active and fulfilling life. He cherishes spending time with his three wonderful children and enjoys reading, meditating, practicing yoga, hiking, snowboarding, playing chess, and listening to music. His holistic approach to both personal and professional life reflects his dedication to balance, growth, and connection.
