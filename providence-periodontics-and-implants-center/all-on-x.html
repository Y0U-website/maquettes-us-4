All-On-X is a modern, life-changing dental implant technique that replaces an entire arch of missing or failing teeth using four or more strategically placed dental implants. Variations such as All-on-4 and All-on-6 refer to the number of implants used, while the “X” reflects customization based on each patient’s anatomy, bone structure, and long-term goals.

Unlike traditional dentures, All-On-X provides a fixed, non-removable solution designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

All-On-4 vs. All-On-6: What’s the Difference?

When possible, Dr. Boustani prioritizes maximizing the number of implants, typically using a minimum of six implants, to improve long-term stability and load distribution. When bone conditions allow, placing additional implants helps support the restoration over time. The overall cost remains the same whether four or six implants are used, allowing treatment decisions to be based on what provides the strongest long-term outcome rather than pricing differences.

Dr. Boustani carefully evaluates each patient using advanced 3D imaging to determine which All-On-X approach delivers the best outcome.

  • All-on-4 uses four implants to support a full arch and is often ideal for patients with reduced bone density.
  • All-on-6 uses six implants to provide additional stability and load distribution when bone conditions allow.
