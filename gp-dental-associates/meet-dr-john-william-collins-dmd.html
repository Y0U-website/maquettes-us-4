Meet Dr. John W. Collins, DMD

Passion Dr. Collins looks forward to providing the highest level of care while building meaningful and lasting relationships with his patients. Beyond Smiles Dr. Collins was born and raised in Utah, but has also lived in Louisiana, California, Nevada, and even spent two years in France. He is married to his wonderful wife, Eliza, with whom he shares two daughters, a son, and his favorite child, a Goldendoodle. The Collins family enjoys traveling, visiting national parks, golfing, hiking, skiing, and swimming. They are so happy to be back in Utah after many years away.

Dr. Collins, his wife Eliza, their three children, and their Goldendoodle love exploring new places together and are grateful to call Utah home again.
