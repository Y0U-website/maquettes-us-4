We’ll gladly take the hassle and worry out of your hands and make sure you’re getting the benefits you’re entitled to!

If you don’t have insurance and still need an expensive procedure, what can you do? With CareCredit , those worries are eliminated! CareCredit works just like a credit card that you can use only for medical and dental procedures. Signing up is easy, and once you’re qualified you’ll be able to finance your care at a low monthly rate! You can use CareCredit to finance procedures that are relatively cheap all the way up to complicated and expensive treatments that you never thought you could afford!

Our interest-free in-house financing and payment plan can make your care affordable and easy. If you would like to find out more about our in-house plan, call our office today!
