Meet South Windsor’s Caring Dentist

Sara E. Curcio, D.M.D.

Dr. Sara Curcio is a 2006 graduate of the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine. She went on to complete her residency training at Hartford Hospital. Dr. Curcio has always felt that the most important components of dental care are trust and respect, and she strives to achieve this kind of relationship with her patients to make them feel calm and comfortable at every visit.

She has volunteered her time with Give Kids a Smile and at CT Mission of Mercy. In addition, she has also provided elementary school classrooms around Connecticut with dental health education. Dr. Curcio is currently a member of her local dental society, the Connecticut State Dental Association, and the American Dental Association. She participates regularly in continuing education courses and is committed to life-long learning.
