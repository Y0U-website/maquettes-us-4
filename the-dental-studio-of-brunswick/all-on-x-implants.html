Why should I choose All-on-X Implants?

All-on-X is one of the most advanced and customizable methods of full-arch dental implant restoration. It offers superior stability, a longer lifespan, enhanced comfort, and better long-term oral health compared to traditional dentures. If you’re missing most or all of your teeth and want a permanent, reliable solution, All-on-X could be your best choice.

CBCT Digital Imaging

With cone-beam computed tomography, or CBCT technology, we can provide our patients peace of mind with more effective and informative treatment planning. This reliable technology creates 3D images that ensure precise placement for increased patient comfort and better results.

Implant Expertise You Can Trust

Dr. Jacob Kieffer is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) and an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID), reflecting years of advanced training in full-arch implant procedures like All-on-X.

Gentle Surgery with Acteon Piezotome

We use the Acteon Piezotome to make implant surgery smoother and more comfortable. This ultrasonic tool helps minimize trauma to the bone and soft tissue, promoting faster healing with less pain.

Bone Grafting When Needed

If you’ve experienced significant bone loss, we offer bone grafting to rebuild and reinforce your jaw. This ensures a stable foundation for your All-on-X implants, even in more complex cases.

Healing, Monitoring, and Temporary Prosthesis

Healing and integration (called osseointegration) are critical to long-term success. Over the next 3–6 months, your implants will permanently bond with your jawbone. During this time, you’ll wear a lightweight temporary prosthesis designed to protect the implants and support healing. You&#x27;ll need to follow a soft food diet and attend a few check-ins at our office to monitor progress. As your implants stabilize, we’ll also gather additional impressions and digital scans to start crafting your final custom bridge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is All-on-X, and how is it different from traditional dental implants?

All-on-X is a full-arch dental implant solution that uses as few as four to six strategically placed implants to support a complete set of replacement teeth. Unlike traditional implants, which may require an implant for every missing tooth, All-on-X is more efficient, less invasive, and often avoids the need for bone grafting. It’s ideal for patients with multiple missing or failing teeth who want a stable, natural-looking smile.

Am I a candidate for All-on-X treatment?

If you&#x27;re missing most or all of your teeth—or are facing extensive dental work—All-on-X might be a great solution. Many patients who have been told they don’t have enough bone for traditional implants still qualify for All-on-X thanks to its strategic placement and angulation. The best way to know for sure is to schedule a consultation so we can evaluate your oral health, medical history, and goals.

How long does the All-on-X process take?

The All-on-X process typically involves a few phases: a consultation and 3D imaging, the placement of implants, and then delivery of a temporary set of teeth shortly after surgery. After a few months of healing, your final custom prosthetic is placed. From start to finish, the process usually takes about 4–6 months—but you’ll walk out with a new smile much sooner.

Does the procedure hurt? What is recovery like?

Thanks to modern anesthesia and sedation options, most patients experience little to no discomfort during the procedure. Some swelling or soreness is normal for a few days afterward, but it’s typically manageable with over-the-counter medications. Most patients are surprised by how smooth the recovery is—and how quickly they can return to normal routines.
