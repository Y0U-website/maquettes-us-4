Orthodontic treatments help treat malocclusion, a condition that causes misaligned or improperly positioned teeth. Dental irregularities such as crooked teeth, broken teeth, and spacing issues can affect the way you bite, and they can cause you to lose confidence when you smile. However, with the right orthodontic procedures, you can straighten your teeth and get your bite back to normal.

Orthodontic Treatments at Our Weddington Dental Office

Your smile means a lot to us, which is why our Weddington dental office provides the following treatment procedures to improve the way your teeth look:

Invisalign

Invisalign is a modern alternative to traditional braces that have long been used to help straighten teeth. Invisalign straightens your teeth similarly to traditional braces, but it doesn’t use metal wire or brackets, meaning they look practically invisible when you wear them.

Serene Dental Spa provides Invisalign braces to help our patients straighten their teeth and improve their smiles. These aligners are custom-made of smooth, comfortable, virtually invisible plastic that patients wear over their teeth.

Invisalign braces will slowly and gently shift your teeth into the right position. When wearing Invisalign braces, remember to remove them whenever you eat, brush, or floss. Until your treatment is complete, you should see your Weddington dentist approximately every two weeks to get a new set of aligners.

Braces

Dental braces are made of bands, wires, and brackets. Braces help straighten crooked and crowded teeth, jaw problems, and misaligned bites. Braces also resolve issues related to eating, speaking clearly, and keeping your teeth clean. There are two types of braces that we offer (metal braces and clear ceramic braces) to help straighten and properly adjust your teeth.

Braces are attached to the front of your teeth with an adhesive and are connected by a wire. They are occasionally tightened to pressure your teeth, gradually moving them toward their target position and aligning your jaw as needed.
