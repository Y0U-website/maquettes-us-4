Dental Savings Plan Guidelines

In order to access the savings our dental plan provides, members must adhere to the following guidelines:

  • The plan cannot be used in combination with other dental plans or financing options.
  • The plan discount will be reduced by 10 % if the patient uses CareCredit for payment.
  • Members cannot transfer benefits from this plan to another plan, and all procedures are non-refundable.
  • Only dental procedures are covered under the plan; spa services are separate.
  • If you are referred to a specialist , the discount will not apply to the dental procedure.
  • Should there be a dental treatment needed following any type of injury involving a lawsuit that requires outside medical care , disability , or any type of workman’s comp insurance , the discount cannot be applied.
  • The patient’s portion of payment (if the procedure is not 100 % covered) is due on the day of service.
