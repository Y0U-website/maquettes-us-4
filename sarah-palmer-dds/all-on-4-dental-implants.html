Learn more about All-on-4 implants below.

What Makes All-on-4 Implants Different?

Unlike single-tooth implants that replace teeth one at a time, All-on-4 uses just four implants per arch to anchor an entire set of upper or lower teeth. The implants are positioned at specific angles to maximize stability and support, even in patients with limited jawbone density. That means many people who aren’t ideal candidates for individual implants, or who would otherwise require bone grafting beforehand, can still qualify for this form of full-arch tooth replacement .

All-on-4 is designed for patients missing most of their teeth or those whose remaining teeth need to be removed due to advanced decay or gum disease. Whether you’ve been living with dentures or are facing full-mouth extractions, this hybrid approach can provide a streamlined path to a fixed, functional, and confident smile.

How Will All-on-4 Implants Benefit Me?

All-on-4 implants offer several major advantages over traditional dentures. First, they’re permanent, so no more adhesives, potential slipping, or taking teeth out at night. The implants are embedded in your jaw, providing a strong, stable foundation that won’t shift or loosen over time.

Oral hygiene is simpler, too. Implant-supported dentures can be brushed just like natural teeth. They also help preserve your jawbone by providing the stimulation that’s normally lost when teeth are missing, helping prevent the sunken look that can come with bone loss.

Overall, the All-on-4 implant procedure offers better reliability, greater comfort, and a more natural appearance than removable options, making it a preferred choice for long-term tooth replacement.
