Get to know Dr. Jordan da Costa

Dr. Jordan da Costa brings a thoughtful blend of clinical excellence, compassion, and a global perspective to Prime Dental. Born in Suriname and raised across California, Curacao, and Florida, Dr. da Costa was introduced to dentistry through the example of his father, who is also a dentist. His diverse background has shaped his approachable and understanding nature — making patients from all walks of life feel right at home in the chair.

Dr. da Costa earned his Biology degree from Nova Southeastern University before continuing on to complete his dental education at NSU’s College of Dental Medicine. He holds certifications in CPR-BLS and Nitrous Oxide sedation, and is an active member of both the American Dental Association (ADA) and Florida Dental Association (FDA).

With a special interest in orthodontics, cosmetic procedures, and full mouth rehabilitations, Dr. da Costa approaches dentistry with a detail-oriented mindset and a passion for helping others. He loves combining science and artistry to restore health and confidence in each of his patients. Whether it’s creating a straighter smile or relieving dental pain, he finds joy in making a real difference every day.

What truly sets Dr. da Costa apart is his commitment to accessible, high-quality care. By offering extended hours, accepting a wide range of insurance plans, and focusing on patient comfort and communication, he’s built a dental practice designed to serve the real needs of his community.

Outside the office, Dr. da Costa enjoys golfing, tennis, working out, and traveling — hobbies that keep him energized and inspired in and out of the office.

Here’s what to expect at your first visit

Visiting Prime Dental is a seamless and stress-free experience. From our modern, calming space to our patient-first process, we make it easy to get the care you need with confidence. Whether you&#x27;re here for a check-up or a more advanced procedure, you’ll never feel rushed — and you&#x27;ll always feel heard.

Patient Experience You’ll start your appointment with a warm welcome from our front desk, followed by a comprehensive exam, digital x-rays, and a cleaning — all depending on your needs. Dr. da Costa will take the time to review your oral health, explain your options clearly, and answer any questions you may have. Your comfort always comes first.

What to Bring To make your first visit as smooth as possible, please bring:

Completed digital new patient forms (or access to complete them at the office)

Comfort meets innovation with our amenities and technology

Our office was designed with you in mind — combining modern tools to enhance your care and comforting touches to make your visit relaxing. We’re here to raise the bar for what a dental experience should feel like.

CBCT Our CBCT (cone beam computed tomography) scanner provides 3D images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This helps us plan treatment with greater accuracy and diagnose issues that may be missed with traditional x-rays.

Intraoral scanner Say goodbye to messy impressions. Our digital scanner allows for fast, comfortable, and highly accurate images of your mouth, improving the fit of crowns, aligners, and other restorations.

TVs in every room From the waiting area to the treatment chair, you’ll find TVs throughout our space — helping you relax and pass the time with entertainment during your visit.

Invested in our community

At this time, Prime Dental is not partnered with local organizations or involvement programs — but we’re always open to building new relationships within the Fort Myers, Alva, and Babcock Ranch communities. As our practice grows, supporting the people around us in a meaningful way remains a key part of our future vision.
