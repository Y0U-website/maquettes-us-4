Dr. Becca Tansey, DMD

Dr. Becca likes doing everything outdoorsy . Paddle boarding, camping, backpacking… and she thoroughly takes advantage of all the Pacific Northwest has to offer. She also enjoys traveling, and Dr. Katie would describe her as a health nut.​ Dr. Becca was raised in St. Louis, Missouri and attended Truman State University in Kirksville, Missouri where she obtained a Bachelors of Science degree in Biology. She then pursued her dental school education at Arizona School of Dentistry and earned her degree in 2015.

​She is currently taking advanced training in implant placement taught through Oregon’s Academy of General Dentistry.

She is a great dentist, she is strong and caring, and has a focus on excellence.

Shortly after receiving her dental degree, Dr. Becca moved to Oregon to escape the desert and pursue her love of outdoor activities. During her free time, she enjoys hiking, trail running, camping, yoga, and snowboarding.

She recently married her husband Mike in a beautiful ceremony in central Oregon.

As a dentist, Dr. Becca enjoys educating her patients on oral health and aims to give every patient the best dental experience possible. In addition, she is a member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentists, participates in local study clubs, and is currently taking courses on implantology.
