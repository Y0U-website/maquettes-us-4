Portland Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Katie Ball, DMD

From the moment you meet her, it’s clear that Dr. Katie Ball truly loves what she does. With more than 15 years of experience and a deep commitment to patient-centered care, Dr. Katie brings a blend of clinical expertise, advanced training, and genuine compassion to every visit. As the owner of Bling Dental, she’s created a space where every visit feels more like catching up with a friend than sitting in a dental chair. She believes every smile tells a story and her goal is to help each patient feel confident, comfortable, and proud of theirs.

Dr. Katie understands that a dental chair can bring up nerves, and she’s made it her mission to change that. Her philosophy centers on comfort, transparency, and empowerment. She takes time to listen, answer questions, and build personalized treatment plans that align with each patient’s goals. Whether she’s crafting a beautiful set of veneers or guiding a seamless Invisalign journey, Dr. Katie loves helping patients discover the confidence that comes with a healthy smile they truly love.

Her favorite cases? Cosmetic transformations, cosmetic repairs, and surgical procedures that make a meaningful impact on her patients’ lives. Dr. Katie loves her patients, and she truly loves watching the confidence that comes with a transformed smile.

Off the clock, you’ll find her soaking up family time with her husband Nick and their three kids (Millie, Sydney, and Cooper). True to her Virgo roots, Dr. Katie brings equal parts organization and creativity to the chaos, whether they’re exploring Portland’s parks, cheering on the Blazers, or diving into their next craft project.

If you’re ready to feel more confident about your smile, Dr. Katie is here to help! Book a free virtual consultation and let’s start creating something beautiful.
