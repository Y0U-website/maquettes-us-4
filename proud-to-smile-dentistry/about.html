About Our Practice

At Proud to Smile Dentistry, Patricia Swaintek-Lamb, DMD provides world-class service and the latest dental technology to encourage healthy and beautiful smiles. Patients visit the office in Bernardsville, New Jersey, to receive quality dental care that promotes optimal oral health.

The team at Proud to Smile Dentistry takes a holistic approach to dental treatment because oral health affects the health of a person’s whole body. Patients benefit from routine check-ups and dental cleanings. Restorative treatments like dental implants , crowns, and dentures complete imperfect smiles. Because a beautiful smile boosts confidence and self-esteem, the practice also offers cosmetic dentistry to enhance the appearance of a patient’s teeth and gums.

Call the office today or use the online tool to book an appointment and benefit from the superior treatments and services offered at Proud to Smile Dentistry.
