Jeffrey C. Russo, DMD

Dr. Jeffrey Russo grew up in Somers, CT. He went on to Saint Anselm College in Manchester, NH, majoring in Biology. Dr. Russo continued his education at the University of Connecticut, School of Dental Medicine. He graduated in 2002 with awards in aesthetic dentistry and dental materials.

Dr. Russo very often participates in continuing education and is a member of the American Dental Association , Massachusetts Dental Society , and Connecticut State Dental Society .

In his free time, he enjoys traveling and skiing with his children and photography. He enjoys following professional football, baseball and soccer.
