Patients

  • We ask that you pay your deductible and co-pay at the time of service.
  • Our fees are generally considered to fall within the acceptable range (UCR) by most insurance companies. Some insurance companies pay a fixed allowance for certain procedures, and others pay a percentage of the charge. The benefits available to you are dictated by the policy that your employer has purchased for you.
  • Please update our office staff regarding any changes to your dental insurance, so that we may process your claim in a timely manner.
  • If your son or daughter is enrolled in college you must provide proof to your insurance carrier or claims will be held up indefinitely.

Payment Plans

For treatment fees exceeding $500, patients with good credit can apply for interest-free loans through the office. For more information click here .

Finance Charges

Balances older than 60 days are subject to finance charges at the rate of 1 % per month. Additionally, charges may be incurred for broken appointments and appointments cancelled without 24-hour advance notice.

Emergency Patients

Patients seen on an emergency basis should be prepared to pay cash for needed treatment the day of service, even if you have dental insurance. We will assist you in receiving reimbursement from your insurance company. Emergencies resulting from injuries or accidents involving Workmen’s Comp, etc. will also be handled on a cash basis with our willingness to provide documentation to the insurance carrier involved.

If you have any questions regarding our financial policy, please to not hesitate to ask us. We are here to serve you.
