Turn Missing Teeth into a Lasting Smile

Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, we have a permanent solution for you. Our advanced dental implant options combine surgical expertise with personalized treatments, providing lasting tooth replacements tailored to your needs.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Restores Your Natural Smile Dental implants look, feel, and function like your natural teeth. Smile and eat your favorite foods with confidence.

Preserves Bone Health Implants preserve your jawbone by stimulating bone growth, preventing bone loss that occurs with missing teeth, and maintaining the natural structure of your face.

Convenient and Low Maintenance With proper care, implants require no special cleaning routines or removals like dentures. Simply care for them like natural teeth and enjoy hassle-free maintenance.

Our Treatment Process

Comprehensive Evaluation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive diagnostic evaluation, using advanced 3D digital imaging to create a detailed map of your oral health. We assess factors like bone density and structure to design a customized implant strategy that meets your unique needs. Anxious? We have a range of sedation options, just let us know and we can discuss the right option for you.

Surgical Preparation & Implant Placement

With the planning in place, we proceed with surgical preparation, which may include bone grafting or other procedures depending on your specific case. When ready, we place the implant using state-of-the-art computer-guided techniques. This minimally invasive approach positions the implant precisely, reducing recovery time and minimizing discomfort.

Final Placement

The healing process is carefully monitored, allowing the implant to naturally bond with your bone—a process called osseointegration. This takes a few months so we can provide you with a temporary restoration in the meantime. After sufficient healing, we will place your permanent restoration. You will leave our office with a restored smile and aftercare instructions on maintaining the health of your new implant.
