What is Scaling and Root Planing?

Deep Cleaning Below the Gumline to Restore Gum Health

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical procedure that involves dental deep cleaning below the gumline to treat gum disease. This two-step procedure is crucial for removing plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing and flossing cannot reach. By doing so, it helps reduce gum inflammation and creates a cleaner surface for the gums to heal around the teeth.

During the dental scaling process, specialized dental instruments are used to meticulously remove plaque and tartar removal below the gumline. This step is essential in preventing the progression of gum disease.

Root planing involves smoothing the root surfaces of your teeth. This smoothing eliminates bacterial toxins and creates a clean surface for the gums to reattach properly to the tooth, fostering healing and helping prevent future bacterial colonization.
