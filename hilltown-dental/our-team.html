Dr.

carissa

Bruening

Dr. Carissa Bruening believes exceptional care begins with listening. Her work blends comprehensive diagnosis, prevention-focused care, and thoughtful treatment planning to help patients feel informed, comfortable, and supported in their decisions.

With a background in kinesiology, leadership studies, and a deep interest in clinical biomechanics, she is passionate about helping patients understand how teeth, muscles, joints, posture, and overall well-being are intricately connected. She is particularly drawn to concerns involving function, comfort, and the relationship between the bite and the temporomandibular joints.

Education remains at the heart of her approach. She believes patients deserve to understand not only what is recommended, but why—creating a foundation for confident, collaborative decision-making.

Her commitment to comprehensive care led her to the Pankey Institute, where she completed the Essentials 1–4 continuum and advanced study in TMD and Orofacial Pain, further shaping her philosophy of thoughtful, relationship-centered dentistry.

Care begins with understanding, and trust grows from there.

Dr. Andrea Morris brings a thoughtful, systems-based approach to care rooted in dentistry, public health, and whole-body wellness. Her work focuses on helping patients understand the relationship between breathing, sleep, oral structures, and overall health.

As founder and inventor of Remvia, Dr. Morris guides patients through a diagnostic and educational process designed to uncover overlooked contributors to chronic symptoms and support more informed next steps.

At Hilltown Dental, Dr. Morris sees patients specifically through Remvia consultations and care pathways.

Sometimes the next step in care begins with understanding the “why.”
