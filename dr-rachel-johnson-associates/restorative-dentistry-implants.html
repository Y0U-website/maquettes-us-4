Advanced Treatments for Damaged or Missing Teeth Restorative dentistry focuses on rebuilding the health, function, and appearance of your teeth after damage, decay, or tooth loss. At Rachel Johnson & Associates, we offer a full range of restorative solutions—including composite fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures, and dental implants—designed to help you eat, speak, and smile with ease again.

Depending on your needs, treatment may involve restoring a single tooth with a durable filling or crown, replacing multiple teeth with a bridge or denture, or rebuilding your full smile with advanced dental implants.

Every procedure is carefully planned to preserve as much of your natural tooth structure as possible while restoring long-term strength and aesthetics.
