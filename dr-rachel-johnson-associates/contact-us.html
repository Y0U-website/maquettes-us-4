Do you accept new patients?

Yes! We welcome new patients of all ages, whether you’re due for a cleaning or exploring treatment options like implants, Invisalign®, or sedation dentistry.

Yes—we accept most PPO dental insurance plans and are preferred providers for many major carriers. Our team will help you file claims and understand your benefits.

We accept Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express, and personal checks.

Is there a discount for paying out-of-pocket?

Yes! Patients paying entirely in cash receive a 5 % discount on treatment.

We partner with Cherry Pay and CareCredit to provide flexible financing options that fit your needs.
