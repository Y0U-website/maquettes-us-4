Island’s Edge Family Dentistry

As a locally owned family practice, our primary goal is to provide comfortable, quality care to the community we love.

We take pride in taking the time to get to know our patients and allow our patients time to get to know our amazing dental team! We understand that dentistry is not one size fits all and it’s important to customize each treatment plan to fit your individual needs and goals! We know that providing our patients with clear communication and an honest opinion are integral to doing just that.

Although dental procedures can be invasive and uncomfortable, we do not believe pain should be part of the process! Our team uses modern day therapeutics and experienced hands to make for smooth and efficient dental appointments. With the highest standards in quality care, you are in excellent hands from the moment you walk through our doors. At Island’s Edge Family Dentistry, attention to detail and the pursuit of perfection is always the goal. Whether it is a cleaning, simple filling or a smile makeover, let our staff be your partners in dental health! Continue below to get to know our team.

Our Experienced Team

Dr. Andrew Mullet

Dr. Mullet was born and raised in Berlin, Ohio. He graduated with a Bachelor’s in Science in Molecular and Cellular Biology from Kent State University before continuing on to attain his Doctorate in Dental Surgery at The Ohio State Universityin 2013. Following graduation, he served as a Captain in the US Army Dental Corps for 4 years before settling in the beautiful St. Augustine.

Dr. Mullet enjoys pursuing continuing education in all facets of dentistry but especially preventive procedures, cosmetics, and implants.

Dr. Mullet and his wife, Sabrina, have 2 beautiful children - Penelope and Elias. He spends his down time doing woodwork, honey-do projects, watching (and crying over) Brown’s football, and doing anything active with his family.

Dr. Sabrina Wall

Dr. Wall moved to St. Augustine with her family as a young child and has been calling it home ever since. After graduating from St. Joseph’s Academy in 2004, she went on to receive a Bachelor’s in Science from the University of Central Florida in Microbiology and Molecular Biology. In 2013, Dr. Wall graduated with her Doctorate in Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University. After a little convincing from her VERY wise husband, she went on to serve as an officer in the US Army Dental Corps, later transitioning to the Reserves where she still serves today.

Dr. Wall enjoys general dentistry because it keeps her on her toes. Rarely are any two cases alike, which makes things challenging and fun. And anything with an aesthetic approach is just the cherry on top. She takes pleasure in getting to know her patients, with the goal of developing a rapport that helps them feel at ease in the chair.

In her downtime, she loves biking (does stationary count?), cooking (but mostly the eating part), and spending time doing anything outdoors with her family. She shares two beautiful children with Dr. Mullet, Penelope and Elias, whom she thinks they hung the moon.

Vicki DeVenny

Vicki has over thirty-five years of dental experience beginning as a Dental Assistant after completing a two-year dental assisting program. Vicki brings a wealth of knowledge having worked in general dentistry, oral surgery, orthodontics, and a crown and bridge laboratory.

Born and raised in the beautiful Shenandoah Valley of Virginia, Vicki now resides in St. Augustine and has been at our practice for 23 years. She and her husband Donnie have been married for 34 years and together they share a love of music and enjoying living life with faith, friends, and family. They have a healthy competition in golf, sharing a record of two hole-in-ones. They are patiently waiting on the third to break the tie.

Betty Segui Emery

Gayle Diane Hubbard

Susan Yvon

Susan is a registered dental hygienist and has been practicing for 34 years. Originally from Graceville, Florida, Susan moved to St. Augustine in 2001.

Susan began her dental career in 1975 after graduating from the Dental Assisting Program at Gulf Coast Community College in Panama City, Florida. After moving to Gainesville, Florida, she advanced her knowledge by attending the Dental Hygiene Program at Santa Fe College where she graduated from in 1984. Susan later returned to Santa Fe College as a dental hygiene instructor.

Susan’s top priority as a hygienistis to make each patient appointment as comfortable as possible. She not only enjoys getting to know her patients, but also educating them on proper home care and encouraging them to make their dental health a priority.

Susan’s hobbies include quilting, traveling, cooking and spending time with friends. She has one son, Trevor, and several cats. All of which she adores.
