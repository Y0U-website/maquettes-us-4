Dr. Sohyoun Jun

As an enthusiastic dental professional with deep regard for her community, Dr. Jun is often found volunteering at dental events, such as Give Kids A Smile® and the Oral Cancer Walk, as well as with local charity organizations, such as Mission of Mercy. She is known for her skills in diagnosing and developing treatment plans, as well as her excellent chairside manner. She is ready to provide excellent dental care in both English and Korean.

After nearly 10 years of experience working as a general dentist, Dr. Jun is proud to lead the dedicated dental team at So Good Dental.
