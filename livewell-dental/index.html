Dental implants replace missing teeth while restoring comfort, function, and confidence. They help support proper chewing, speech, and jawbone health. Many patients choose implants as a durable alternative to bridges or dentures.

Dental implant treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation and digital imaging to plan placement with precision. A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone to act as a stable foundation for a replacement tooth. Once healing is complete, a custom restoration is attached to restore natural function and appearance.
