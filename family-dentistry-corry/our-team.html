DR. SCOTT PRUCKNER, DMD

Dr. Scott Pruckner is an Erie native. He graduated from McDowell High School in 1980. He received his BD degree in Biology from Grove City College in 1984, then attended Temple University School of Dentistry where he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine in 1988.

He is a member in good standing of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association and Erie County Dental Association. He is married with three children, and intends to practice Dentistry for many more years in addition to the 33 years he has been in practice already.

DR. CHESTER "CHIP" ZALESKI, DMD (retired)

Dr. Chester Chip Zaleski grew up in Erie and graduated from McDowell High School in 1973. After completing his undergraduate degree in Biology at University of Kentucky, he then attended The University of Louisville School of Dentistry, graduating with honors with a Doctor of Medical Dentistry Degree in 1982.

Dr. Zaleski returned to the area to live and practice in Corry in June of 1982. He began practicing dentistry at Family Dentistry in Corry, shortly thereafter opening another office in Union City in 1985. He is a long-standing member of the American Dental Association, Pennsylvania Dental Association, Erie County Dental Association and is committed to serving the community with the absolute best, most comprehensive and compassionate dental care possible.

In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his wife, 2 daughters and sons in law and grandchildren.
