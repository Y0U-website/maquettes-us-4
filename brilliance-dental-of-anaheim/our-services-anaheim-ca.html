Implants

Dental implants are a sophisticated and long-term solution for replacing missing teeth. They consist of titanium posts that are surgically implanted into the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for artificial teeth (crowns, bridges, or dentures).

Root Canals

Root canal treatment is a procedure designed to address issues with the inner pulp of a tooth, which can become infected or inflamed due to decay, trauma, or other dental issues. During the procedure, the infected pulp is removed, and the space is cleaned and sealed.

Crowns

Dental crowns are custom-made caps that cover a damaged or weakened tooth, restoring its shape, size, and strength. Crowns can be made from various materials, including porcelain, metal, or a combination of both.

Oral Surgery

Oral surgery involves surgical procedures performed in the oral and maxillofacial areas. This can include tooth extractions, jaw surgeries, dental implant placement, and other specialized procedures.

Veneers

Dental veneers are thin, custom-made shells, usually made of porcelain, that are bonded to the front surface of teeth to improve their appearance. Veneers can address issues like discoloration, minor misalignments, and chipped or worn teeth.

Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a dental treatment that corrects the alignment of the teeth and jaw, using devices such as braces or invisible aligners to achieve a straighter and more functional smile.
