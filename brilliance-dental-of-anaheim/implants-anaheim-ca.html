Types of Dental Implants

At our Anaheim CA 92801 dental office, we offer several different types of tooth restoration depending on the type of dental implant(s) you require.

Single Dental Implants

We can replace one missing tooth with a handcrafted crown designed to match your smile.

Implant Bridges

We can replace two or more teeth in a row by attaching a custom-made bridge to your dental implants.

Fixed (Implant-Supported) Dentures

We can attach our high-quality fixed dentures to your All-on-X implants to replace an entire arch of missing teeth.
