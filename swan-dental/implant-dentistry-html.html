Specialized Solution: All-on-4® Dental Implants

If you’ve lost most or all of your teeth and you’re seeking a full arch replacement, All-on-4® offers a revolutionary solution. This technique typically utilizes just four strategically placed implants in the upper or lower jaw to support a complete set of new, fixed teeth. Less implant posts translates to less recovery time and a minimally-invasive process, putting you back on the path to recovery and smiling faster than conventional implant procedures.
