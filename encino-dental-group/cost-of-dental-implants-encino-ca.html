  • Complexity Your dental condition’s complexity can affect the cost of implant treatment. Your price may increase if you have extensive bone loss or misaligned teeth. The need for advanced techniques and technology reflects the pricing of complex cases.

Understanding Cost

It is important to understand the various components that go into the price of dental implants. Encino Dental Practice provides a detailed breakdown of costs during consultation so that you are aware of what to expect.

  • Initial Consultation and Diagnostic Imaging This process begins with a consultation that includes a thorough exam, diagnostic imaging (such as X-rays, 3D scans, or MRIs), and a discussion of treatment options. This step is essential for planning the implant procedure. It may also have a cost. This fee will be added to your overall treatment cost if you proceed with implant placement.
  • Surgical Implant Placement The surgical implant placement is the next major cost component. The cost includes the actual procedure, the implant posts, and any necessary anesthesia or sedation. The cost of surgery can vary depending on the number of implants used, the type and complexity of the procedure, and the anesthesia.
  • Abutment Placement Once the implants have successfully integrated with your jawbone, the abutment will be attached to the implant. This abutment will hold the final restorative. This stage’s cost includes the custom abutment, and the crown, bridge or denture placed over it. Costs are primarily determined by the materials used in the restoration.
  • Additional Procedures The cost of additional procedures, such as sinus lifts or bone grafting, will be included. These steps are essential for your implant’s success, but will also increase the cost.
  • Maintenance and Follow-Up Visits Following the placement and restoration of your implants, you will need to make follow-up appointments in order to monitor the healing and integration process. These visits are usually included in the price, but it is important to consider the maintenance of your implants over the long term. These may consist of regular cleanings and check-ups.
