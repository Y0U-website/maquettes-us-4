Implant Dentistry Frequently Asked Questions

  • What Kinds of Restorations Can Be Supported by Implants? Implants are highly versatile and can be used to support: Single crowns to replace a single missing tooth.
  • Implant-supported bridges to restore multiple teeth in a row.
  • Implant-retained dentures that snap securely onto the implants for improved comfort and stability. Whether you are missing one tooth or multiple, dental implants offer a customizable foundation to rebuild your smile. At Encino Dental, all of these options can be handled in one office by a team familiar with your case, which streamlines your care from start to finish.
  • What Is the Dental Implant Process Like? The implant process is typically completed in stages: Initial Consultation: Your dentist evaluates your mouth and jaw with digital imaging, reviews your medical history, and creates a treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium implant is surgically inserted into the jawbone under local anesthesia or mild sedation for comfort.
  • Healing and Integration: Over the next 3 6 months, the bone integrates with the implant, creating a stable foundation.
  • Abutment and Restoration: Once healed, a small connector (abutment) is placed, and the final restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) is attached. Each step is completed with care and precision to ensure optimal results. At Encino Dental, all services are performed in-house for a smoother experience and fewer appointments.
  • How Do I Get Started With Implant Treatment at Encino Dental? Getting started is easy and commitment-free. Simply schedule a free dental implant consultation at Encino Dental. During this visit, you’ll meet with the team to discuss your goals, review your oral health, and learn about the best options for your smile. You’ll receive a customized treatment plan, a timeline, and pricing information so you can make an informed decision. Whether you’re missing one tooth or considering full-mouth restoration, the team at Encino Dental will walk you through every step with clarity and support.
