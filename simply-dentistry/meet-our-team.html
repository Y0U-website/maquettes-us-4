MEET OUR DENTIST

Dr. Bich-Ngoc “Bicky” Tran

FOUNDER & DENTIST

Dr. Bich-Ngoc “Bicky” Tran brings over 25 years of dental experience to her practice, along with a deep commitment to creating a space that feels safe, trusting, and genuinely happy for every patient who walks through the door. She believes dentistry is not just about caring for teeth, but about caring for people - listening, educating, and building lifelong relationships based on trust.

Today, Dr. Tran is known for her gentle approach, attention to detail, and ability to put patients at ease. She takes pride in helping individuals and families feel confident in their care and comfortable in their smiles.

Outside the office, Dr. Tran is paying tribute to her parents by completing a documentary about her family’s escape from Vietnam and the hardships they endured so she could be here serving patients today. She is close to the finish line , with the film expected to be completed by summer 2026 . To follow the journey and watch the trailer, visit boatpeoplefilm.com .
