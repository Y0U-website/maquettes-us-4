Signs You May Benefit from Dental Bonding

Dental bonding at One Tree Family Dentistry can address various cosmetic and functional dental issues. Our expert team can help you determine if this treatment is right for you. Consider the following indicators that dental bonding might be beneficial:

If you’re experiencing any of these issues, reach out to One Tree Family Dentistry to discuss how dental bonding can help improve your smile.

  • Chipped or cracked teeth
  • Discolored teeth resistant to whitening
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Misshapen teeth
  • Exposed tooth roots due to gum recession
  • Minor tooth decay
  • Desire for a non-invasive smile improvement
  • Need for protective coating on teeth
