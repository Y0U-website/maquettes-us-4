Our Dentists

John Moreau, DDS

Dr. Moreau is an Accredited Member of the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. There are only 377 dentists in the world that have obtained this prestigious status. He is also a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry, an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, and a member of the American Dental Association, Louisiana Dental Association, Central Louisiana Dental Society, and American Association of Dental Excellence.

Dr. Moreau is dedicated to keeping his knowledge current by participating in continuing education all over the world. He won first place in the porcelain veneer division at a national smile gallery competition sponsored by The American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dr. Moreau has been in private general dental practice focusing on cosmetics, implants, and occlusion in Alexandria, Louisiana, since 1984.

Dr. Moreau and his wife, Kay, have three daughters – Lindsey, Jewel, and Jade. Dr. Moreau enjoys traveling, reading, and writing.

Jade Moreau, DDS

Dr. Jade Moreau was elected to the C. Edmund Kells Honorary Dental Society. She was awarded the Department of Comprehensive Dentistry Award for her excellence in Comprehensive Dentistry and was awarded the American College of Prosthodontic Pre-Doctoral Student Achievement Award for her excellence in the area of prosthodontics. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Louisiana Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dr. Jade Moreau developed a passion for the Profession of Dentistry at a young age and looks forward to increasing her knowledge of the ever-changing career of Dentistry! Dr. Jade believes in the importance of continuing education to stay at the forefront of all advances in the Dental field. In her free time, Jade enjoys traveling and fishing with her husband Austin, and fur baby, Peanut. Jade and Austin just had their first baby girl, Hazel Grace, on July 30, 2024.

Ragan Noakes, DDS

Dr. Ragan Noakes graduated from dental school in the top 5 % of her class and was elected to the C. Edmund Kells Honorary Dental Society and the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. She also received the Nakamoto-Porter Excellence in Dental Physiology Award and the Robert B. Brannon Scholarship in Oral Pathology for her excellence in the study of physiology and oral pathology. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Louisiana Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Ragan Noakes is passionate about providing excellent dental care to her patients and making a meaningful impact in her community. She is dedicated to helping patients improve their oral health and feel confident with their smiles! She is committed to lifelong learning and continuing her education to stay at the forefront of the dental field. In her free time, Ragan enjoys spending time outdoors with her husband, Clayton Noakes. She enjoys fishing, reading, running, and doing arts and crafts.
