Dental Implants

Dental implants are a revolutionary solution for replacing missing teeth, offering a permanent and natural-looking result. At Unforgettable Smiles, we provide state-of-the-art dental implant services that act as a strong and durable root for your new tooth. Once healed, a custom-made crown is fitted onto the implant, restoring both function and aesthetics to your smile. With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Clear Aligner Therapy

Clear aligner therapy is an innovative approach to orthodontics that offers a discreet and comfortable way to straighten teeth. At Unforgettable Smiles, we use advanced technology to create customized clear aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. These aligners are removable, allowing for easy cleaning and the flexibility to enjoy your favorite foods without restriction. With regular check-ins, we ensure that your treatment progresses smoothly, ultimately providing you with a beautiful, aligned smile without the hassle of traditional metal braces.

Sedation Dentistry

Nitrous Oxide

At Unforgettable Smiles, we offer nitrous oxide, or laughing gas, as a calming sedation option. When you breathe in nitrous oxide, its soothing effects start within three to five minutes. Our team carefully manages the sedation levels throughout your treatment to ensure your utmost comfort. After your procedure, we provide pure oxygen to clear the nitrous oxide from your system swiftly, allowing you to drive yourself home feeling back to normal.
