What are the Risks?

Dental implant surgery can be painful, but with anesthesia, you will not feel any pain.

Other than pain, there is a risk of infection and damage to the surrounding teeth. Fortunately, it isn’t a serious issue because infections can be controlled with the help of medicine and our staff will help make sure that you don’t feel any discomfort.

Our dentists and dental team’s experience with the surgery will help minimize damage to the surrounding tissues and teeth.

We Also Offer Removable Dentures

In addition to dental implants, we also provide removable dentures as a tooth replacement option. These prosthetic teeth are placed in the moth on the upper or lower jaw or both and facilitate normal oral function. Dentures are removed overnight during sleep and soaked in a cleaning solution for optimal hygiene. They are an economic solution to tooth replacement that is easy to remove and clean and offers the wearer the same benefits as real teeth.
