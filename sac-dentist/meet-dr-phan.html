A Passion for Patient-Centered Care

Dr. Phan’s philosophy goes beyond technical excellence—he believes in fostering genuine connections with his patients. Whether providing preventive care, restorative treatments, or cosmetic solutions, his goal is to create a comfortable and welcoming environment where every patient feels valued and informed.

Life Beyond Dentistry

Outside of his practice, Dr. Phan enjoys the simple pleasures of life. He is an avid hiker and camper, cherishing the time spent in nature with his family: his wife, Cat; their daughters, Michaela and Julianne; and their beloved Siberian husky, Neko. In his spare time, he also enjoys playing pickleball and exploring his passion for photography.
