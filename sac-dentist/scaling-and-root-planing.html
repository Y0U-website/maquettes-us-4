Scaling and Root Planing…

Gingivitis can be reversed with good at-home oral hygiene and standard cleanings. If left untreated, it progresses, and bacteria attack your periodontal ligaments, causing your gums to pull away (recede) from your teeth. Gum recession creates deep periodontal pockets that make perfect hiding grounds for harmful bacteria. Over time, advanced gum disease damages the underlying bone, resulting in bone loss and tooth loss.

  • Gum tenderness and soreness
  • Bleeding, especially when flossing and brushing
  • Tooth sensitivity
  • Chronic bad breath

What can I expect from scaling and root planing (SRP)?

SRP is a non-surgical yet effective treatment for gum disease. Its objective is to remove the unhealthy deposits to promote healing. Here’s what you can expect from this deep teeth cleaning treatment.

Depending on the severity of your infection, you may need 1-3 visits. Your dentist will advise on the sessions required to achieve optimal gum health. Once you beat the infection, you must adhere to diligent oral hygiene and dental cleanings every 3-4 months to prevent it from returning.

  • Examination: The dentist will examine your gums to determine the best treatment option. This may involve visual exams, a periodontal probe, and dental X-rays to establish the amount of lost bone.
  • Preparation: The dentist numbs your gums with local anesthetics to ensure a comfortable dental experience. Sedation dentistry is also available for anxious patients or those with low pain tolerance.
  • Scaling: Dr. Phan uses special tools and skills to remove plaque, tartar, and harmful bacteria from the tooth surfaces and deep pockets.
  • Root planing: We smooth the root surfaces to help gums reattach and to prevent future plaque/tartar buildup.
  • Aftercare: You might experience mild soreness or sensitivity for a few days. Your Sacramento dentist will recommend pain medication, home remedies, and a special diet to manage discomfort and maximize healing. We’ll also recommend optimal oral hygiene, mouth rinses, and antibiotics to prevent bacterial recolonization.

What are the benefits of scaling and root planing?

Patients don’t realize that deep teeth cleaning helps more than their smile. Here are several benefits of periodontal maintenance in Sacramento, California:

  • It’s effective for mild to advanced gum disease
  • Protects your teeth from eventual loss
  • Ensures lasting fresh breath
  • Eliminating unsightly pockets enhances your smile appeal
  • Safeguards the oral-systemic health
