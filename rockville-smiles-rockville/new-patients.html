Parking Information

Parking in Downtown Rockville can be a challenge at certain times of the day. We provide complimentary validated parking to our patients to help ensure your visit to our office is as stress-free as possible. Free validated parking is available at the Public Parking garage located at intersection of East Middle Lane and Gibbs Street. Take the elevator to the first floor which will lead you to Courthouse Square. Our office is immediately on your right as you exit the elevator.

Note: there are also parking spaces in front of our office. Unfortunately, those parking spaces are metered and cannot be validated.
