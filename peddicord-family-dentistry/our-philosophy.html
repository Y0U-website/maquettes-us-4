A Philosophy of Overall Wellbeing

At Peddicord Family Dentistry, prevention and early detection are the most important aspects of general dental care . Sticking to a schedule of routine checkups and cleanings , for instance, can help you prevent issues like tooth decay and periodontal disease , which can require more extensive treatment to address later. If a problem does develop, then detecting and treating it early is the next best thing to prevention. Besides avoiding severe dental conditions, however, prevention and early detection also benefit your overall health in many ways.
