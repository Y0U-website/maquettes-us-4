Sealants

Protect teeth from decay with a thin, plastic coating applied to the surface of molars and premolars, known as sealants. A sealant protects the tooth by dealing deep grooves, creating a smooth, easy to clean surface. While sealants provide protection for several years, regular dental care visits ensure no chipping or wear occurs.

Homecare

A beautiful, healthy smile that lasts a lifetime is our ultimate goal when treating patients. Your home care plays a vital role in achieving that goal. Your personal home care starts by eating balanced meals, reducing the number of snacks you eat and correctly using the various dental aids that help control the plaque and bacteria that cause dental disease.
