Our Invisalign Process

Using the advanced iTero scanner, we create a highly accurate 3D model of your teeth. This ensures a precise fit for your aligners, leading to faster, more effective results—so you can achieve your perfect smile sooner.

We take detailed X-rays to identify the root cause of misalignment and develop a customized treatment plan. We’ll walk you through the process and costs in a clear, straightforward way.

Treatment is structured in cycles, with scans after each phase to track your progress and make adjustments if needed. With our efficient approach, 85 % of patients complete their Invisalign journey in just two cycles.
