Our Services

Kelly Seebaldt, DMD provides comprehensive dental health care to his patients of all ages. From general teeth cleaning to full mouth dental restoration, extractions, root canal therapy, Invisalign aligners and TMJ counseling, Dr. Seebaldt and his staff care about your dental health. When necessary, Dr. Seebaldt works closely with Northwest Florida’s finest Oral Surgeons, Endodontists and Orthodontists.

We are a dental practice that prides itself in quality dental work and personalized patient service that includes:

Contact the office at (850)837-2189 about services offered

  • Problem focused emergency treatment ( See "Contact Us" for after hour’s emergency information)
  • In-office chair-side whitening (take home trays and refills)
  • Comprehensive Periodontal Evaluations
  • Prophylaxis for adults and children
  • Periodontal Therapy (scaling and root planning)
  • X-rays (bitewings and full mouth x-rays)
  • Cancer screenings
  • Crowns, Veneers, crowns after implants and temporary crowns
  • Sealants, composite and amalgam restoration
  • Post and core build-up
  • Surgical and routine extractions - pre-medication or Nitrous Oxide available
  • Root Canal Therapy
  • TMJ counseling for night guard needs
  • Prosthodontics (full and partial dentures)
  • Denture relines, tooth replacement and adjustments
  • Orthodontic retainers
  • Invisalign
