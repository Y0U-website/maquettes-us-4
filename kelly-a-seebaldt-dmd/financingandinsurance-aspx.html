Financing and Insurance

Our office is committed to helping you maximize your insurance benefits. Because insurance policies vary, we can only estimate your coverage in good faith but cannot guarantee coverage due to the complexities of individual contracts. Your estimated patient portion must be paid at the time of service. As a service to our patients, we will bill insurance companies for services. If you have any questions, our staff is always available to answer them.

FINANCING It is our goal to provide you with affordable, quality dental treatment. We offer a variety of payment options to meet those needs. Our practice accepts payment by checks, VISA, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and cash. CareCredit is also available.

CareCredit offers a full range of payment options to meet your dental needs. Payment is interest free for 12 months. It’s easy to apply on line or we will help you apply at the office.

INSURANCE At this time our practice is an "in network provider" for MetLife, Delta Dental and Guardian insurance companies. For all other insurance companies we are always willing to assist our patients in filing to their primary dental insurance company and we will be happy to explain your benefits or answer any questions you have about your dental insurance. A given insurance policy is based on a fixed fee schedule – usual and customary – that the insurance company determines.
