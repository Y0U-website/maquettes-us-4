Frequently Asked Questions About the Dental Concierge Club

Is the Dental Concierge Club an insurance plan?

No. The Dental Concierge Club is a patient loyalty rewards program, not a dental insurance plan. It cannot be used alongside your contracted insurance fees or combined with other offers. It is available exclusively at Pyramid Family Dental in Sparks, NV.

Who is the Premium Plus membership designed for?

Premium Plus is designed for patients without dental insurance who want their preventive care covered and a discount on additional treatment. For $186 a year — a $675 value — it’s one of the most cost-effective ways to maintain consistent dental care without insurance.

Can I use the membership with my dental insurance?

No. The Dental Concierge Club cannot be combined with contracted insurance fees, other offers, or discounts. If you have dental insurance, the free Premium Membership is still available to you for its rewards and seasonal benefits.

Are there any exclusions to the 15 % discount?

Yes. The 15 % discount on additional care does not apply to Clear Aligners, Invisalign Cosmetics, or Dental Implants. All other treatment is eligible for the discount under the Premium Plus membership.

How do I enroll in the Dental Concierge Club?

Enrollment is simple and immediate. You can sign up by email, text, through our website, or in person at the office. Once enrolled, your 500 reward points are available right away and your benefits begin without any waiting period.
