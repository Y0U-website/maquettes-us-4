A Whole-Body Approach to Dental Health

We treat your oral health as part of your overall health — because that’s what the evidence supports. Inflammation, blood sugar, cardiovascular health, and other systemic conditions are all connected to what’s happening in your mouth.

That philosophy shows up in practical ways. We monitor blood pressure at every appointment as a standard part of care, not an afterthought. We look at the full picture during every exam — not just what needs to be fixed, but what might be worth watching and why. Dentistry done well is whole-person care, and we take that seriously.

Same-Day Crowns with On-Site Technology

Most dental offices send crown work to an outside lab — which means two appointments, a temporary crown in between, and a week or two of waiting. We mill crowns on-site using CAD/CAM technology and our Glidewell Mill, which means we can design, fabricate, and place a permanent crown in a single visit.

For patients dealing with a broken tooth or a failed crown, that difference is significant. One appointment, no temporary, no second round of anesthesia. For patients dealing with front teeth or multiple crowns needed, we may use an outside lab.

Gold Invisalign Provider

Gold Invisalign Provider status reflects a higher level of experience with clear aligner treatment — a designation earned through case volume and clinical outcomes, not just certification. We offer Invisalign for both adults and teens, and our experience with the system means we’re well-equipped to handle a range of alignment concerns with predictable results.

Advanced Clinical Technology

The iTero scanner captures precise digital impressions with no putty trays and no discomfort. We use it as a collaborative tool — after scanning, we sit with you upright to review findings together on screen so you understand exactly what’s being recommended before any decisions are made.

Beyond the iTero, we offer a range of advanced preventive therapies most general practices don’t carry — including guided biofilm therapy for gentler cleanings, Curodont hydroxyapatite treatment for early-stage enamel repair, laser bacterial reduction for periodontal support, and soft tissue lasers for precise, comfortable gum care. These tools allow us to do less invasive, more accurate work at every stage of treatment.

Financial Transparency & Accessible Care

Dental care is an investment, and we think patients deserve to understand their options clearly before making any decisions.

We accept most PPO dental insurance plans and proudly serve Medicare patients. Before treatment begins, we offer a complimentary benefits check so you have an estimate of what your plan covers and what to expect out of pocket.

For patients with costs beyond what insurance covers, we offer financing through Cherry , CareCredit , and Healthcare Finance Direct (HFD) — including 12-month interest-free options.

For patients without insurance, our Dental Concierge Club is a membership plan that covers preventive care and provides discounts on other services for a predictable annual fee — no deductibles, no claim forms, no surprises. We’d rather have an open conversation about cost than have financial uncertainty become a reason to avoid care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pyramid Family Dental accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients at all stages — whether you’re due for a routine cleaning, dealing with something that needs attention, or coming in after years away from dental care. There’s no judgment here, just a straightforward conversation about where things stand.

Do you treat patients who feel nervous about dental care?

Yes, and it’s something we’re experienced with. Many of our patients come in with significant dental anxiety — some haven’t been to a dentist in years because of it. We work at a pace that feels manageable, explain everything before we do it, and never push forward until you’re comfortable. You’re always in control of your appointment.

What makes your approach different from other Sparks dentists?

A few things stand out. We take a whole-body approach to dental health, monitoring blood pressure at every visit and looking at the full picture of your wellness. We use the iTero scanner collaboratively — sitting with patients to review findings together rather than just talking at them. We offer same-day crowns milled on-site, Gold Invisalign Provider status, and advanced preventive therapies most general practices don’t carry. And we’re upfront about finances from the start, with multiple options to make care accessible.

Do you offer care for patients without insurance?

Yes. Our Dental Concierge Club is a membership plan designed for patients without dental insurance — covering preventive care and providing discounts on other services for a straightforward annual fee. We also offer financing through Cherry, CareCredit, and Healthcare Finance Direct for out-of-pocket treatment costs.

Do you see Spanish-speaking patients?

Yes. We welcome patients who speak Spanish and are committed to clear, respectful communication for every patient we serve.
