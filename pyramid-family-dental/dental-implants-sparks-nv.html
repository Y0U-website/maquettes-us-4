Single Dental Implant Restoration

A single implant crown replaces one missing tooth by attaching directly to the implant post. The crown is custom-designed to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth — the result looks and feels natural, and because it’s anchored to the implant, it doesn’t affect the teeth on either side.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When several adjacent teeth are missing, an implant-supported bridge restores them using implants as anchors rather than relying on neighboring natural teeth for support. The bridge spans the gap with a fixed, stable result that functions like natural teeth and doesn’t require removal for cleaning.

Implant-Supported Partials & Dentures

For patients missing multiple teeth across an arch, implant-supported partials and dentures offer significantly better retention than removable options alone. The implants anchor the restoration in place, reducing slipping and the discomfort that often comes with it — making eating, speaking, and daily life noticeably easier.

All-on-4® & All-on-6® Dental Implant Restorations in Sparks, NV

All-on-4 and All-on-6 are full-arch restoration solutions that replace an entire upper or lower row of teeth using four or six implants as anchors. We provide the restoration component — the fixed or snap-on arch that attaches to your implants — designed and fabricated with precision for a stable, natural-looking result.

These restorations require careful planning and coordination with the surgeon who placed your implants. If you’ve completed the implant placement process and are ready for your restoration, or if you’re in the early stages of planning full-arch tooth replacement, a consultation with us is a good place to start.

Snap-On Dentures for Added Stability

Snap-on dentures attach directly to implants placed in the jaw, giving your denture a secure anchor rather than resting loosely on the gums. They snap firmly in place during the day and can be removed at night for cleaning.

The improvement in security makes a practical difference. Eating a wider range of foods and speaking with confidence are things patients regularly notice after switching from traditional dentures to implant-anchored ones.

Bone Grafting for Dental Implant Restorations in Sparks, NV

Sufficient bone density is essential for implants to integrate and hold securely. When bone has been lost — which commonly happens after a tooth has been missing for some time — bone grafting may be needed before implant placement can proceed.

We work with patients navigating this process and can help coordinate bone grafting as part of your overall implant treatment plan. We’ll assess your situation during your consultation and walk you through what your plan involves and what to expect at each stage.

Advanced Technology for Precise Implant Restorations

Accurate planning is essential when it comes to implant restorations — a well-designed restoration supports lasting results, and the process starts well before anything is placed.

We use the iTero scanner to capture precise digital impressions of your teeth and existing implants with no putty trays or mess, feeding that data directly into digital treatment planning. CAD/CAM technology guides the design of crowns and restorations with precision that manual processes can’t consistently replicate. Where appropriate, 3D printing supports the fabrication of implant components with reliable, consistent fit. Together, these tools allow us to plan carefully and execute accurately — which matters when you’re investing in a long-term solution.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implant restorations last?

With proper care — routine cleanings, good home hygiene, and regular checkups — implant restorations can last many years and often decades. The implant post itself tends to be very durable; the restoration on top may eventually need replacement depending on wear and location. No restoration comes with a guarantee, but implant-supported restorations are among the most dependable long-term tooth replacement options available.

Are dental implant restorations better than dentures?

For many patients, yes — particularly when stability and function are the priority. Implant-supported restorations are anchored securely and don’t slip, shift, or require adhesives. That said, traditional dentures remain a practical option depending on bone health, overall health, and personal preference. The right answer depends on your specific situation, which is why a consultation matters.

Do dental implant restorations hurt?

The restoration process itself is generally comfortable. Any discomfort associated with implants typically occurs during the surgical placement phase, which is handled by your oral surgeon. Once your implants have integrated and you’re ready for restoration, the process involves fitting, adjusting, and placing your crown, bridge, or denture — most patients find this straightforward and well-tolerated.

Is bone grafting always required for implants?

Not always. Whether bone grafting is needed depends on how long the tooth has been missing, your bone structure, and other individual factors. We evaluate this as part of your consultation and explain clearly what your treatment plan involves.

Do you place dental implants?

We provide implant restorations — the crowns, bridges, dentures, and full-arch restorations that attach to implants. Implant placement is performed by an oral surgeon or specialist. If you haven’t yet had implants placed, we can help coordinate that process and handle your restoration once placement is complete.
