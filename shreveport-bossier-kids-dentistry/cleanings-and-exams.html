Cleanings and Exams

  • Most children need a professional cleaning and exam every six months.
  • Fluoride treatments during cleanings can meaningfully lower a child’s cavity risk.
  • Baby teeth guide the position of permanent teeth, making early care essential.
  • Brushing twice daily at home works best alongside regular professional cleanings.

How Often Do Kids Need a Teeth Cleaning in Bossier City?

Some kids need more frequent visits. Children with a higher cavity risk, braces, or certain medical conditions may benefit from cleanings every three to four months, and our team will let you know if that applies to your child.

What Age Should Cleanings Start?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends a child’s first dental visit by their first birthday, or within six months of their first tooth coming in. Starting early helps kids build comfort with the dentist long before more involved treatment is ever needed.

It also gives our team a baseline for how your child’s teeth and jaw are developing, which makes it easier to spot anything unusual down the road.

Signs Your Child May Need a Cleaning Sooner

Bad breath that doesn’t go away, visible plaque buildup along the gumline, or a child complaining about sensitivity can all be signs it’s time to move up an appointment. When in doubt, a quick call to our office can help you decide.

We’d always rather see your child a little early than have a small issue turn into something bigger between visits.

Why Early Exams Matter for Long-Term Oral Health

Catching Small Problems Early

A cavity caught early is usually a simple filling. A cavity that goes unnoticed for months can turn into a much bigger, more uncomfortable procedure for your child.

Regular exams give our dentist the chance to spot these issues while they’re still small, often before your child has any symptoms at all.

Building Healthy Habits That Last

Kids who grow up with regular, positive dental visits are far more likely to keep up good oral health habits as adults. Our goal with every kids teeth cleaning in Bossier City is to build that foundation early.

We also use every visit as a chance to teach kids, and parents, simple brushing and flossing techniques that make a real difference between appointments.

What Parents Can Do Between Visits

Brushing twice a day, flossing once daily, and limiting sugary snacks all go a long way toward keeping your child’s next cleaning quick and easy. Small daily habits add up to noticeably less buildup at each checkup.

If you’re ever unsure whether something you’re seeing at home is normal, a quick call to our office is always welcome. It’s often easier to address a question early than to wait until the next scheduled visit.
