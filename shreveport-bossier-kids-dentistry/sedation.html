Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recognizes nitrous oxide as a very safe and effective option for treating children’s dental needs. It’s mild, easy for kids to tolerate, and quickly leaves the body through normal breathing once treatment is finished. Nitrous oxide is not addictive, and your child stays fully awake throughout treatment, just in a more relaxed state.

Conscious Sedation

Conscious sedation is often recommended for kids who are a bit more apprehensive, very young children, and children with special needs. It helps calm your child and reduce anxiety or discomfort associated with dental treatment.

Many kids become quite drowsy under conscious sedation, and some even fall asleep, though they won’t lose consciousness. Several different medications can be used, and our dentist will discuss the best option for your child, taking into account their overall health along with the type of treatment they’ll be receiving. We’re always happy to answer any questions you have about these medications.

IV Sedation

IV sedation is recommended for children where conscious sedation may not be the right fit. This type of sedation puts your child completely to sleep for treatment, much like the sedation used for procedures such as tonsil removal.

If our team determines IV sedation is the best option for your child’s specific circumstances, we’ll go over all the details with you in advance and answer any questions you may have before moving forward.

Which Sedation Option Is Right for My Child?

Our dentist considers several factors when recommending a sedation option, including your child’s age, anxiety level, overall health, and the specific treatment being performed. There’s no one-size-fits-all answer, which is exactly why we offer three different levels.

Sedation is often discussed alongside procedures like a pulpotomy , a tooth extraction , or placing a stainless steel crown , since these treatments can feel more involved to an anxious child. Our team will always walk you through the reasoning behind any sedation recommendation.

You’re always welcome to ask questions or share concerns about any option we recommend. Making the right choice together, as a team with you and your child, matters more to us than any single approach.

Preparing Your Child for Sedation

Before any sedation appointment, our team will provide specific instructions to follow, which may include guidance around eating or drinking beforehand depending on the type of sedation planned.

For conscious or IV sedation specifically, plan on arranging for someone to help supervise your child afterward, since grogginess can last for a little while as the sedation wears off. If this is your child’s first visit with us, let our team know so we can help make the whole experience feel as comfortable as possible from the start.

Every family’s situation is a little different, so our team will tailor pre-appointment instructions to your child’s specific sedation plan rather than giving generic guidance that may not apply.
