The Community Directed Diabetes Prevention and Treatment Program provides: Diabetes Clinic Schedule Since the late 1990’s, the Toiyabe Indian Health Project-Special Diabetes Program for Indians, has provided a walk-in clinic for Toiyabe Native patients who are on the diabetes registry. The Diabetes clinics are held on the 3rd Thursday of every month from 8:00-11:00 AM, by appointment.
