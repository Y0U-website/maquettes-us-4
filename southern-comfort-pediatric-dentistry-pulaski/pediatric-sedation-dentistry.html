Who Can Benefit from Pediatric Sedation Dentistry?

Pediatric sedation dentistry can help many children, particularly those who:

  • Feel nervous or scared about visiting the dentist.
  • Have a low threshold to pain or a hypersensitive gag reflex.
  • Need multiple procedures or treatments that are complicated.
  • Have conditions that make it difficult for them to visit the dentist, like sensory sensitivities or behavioral challenges.
  • Are too young to completely cooperate during dental procedures.

What To Expect During Your Child’s Sedation Dentistry Appointment:

  • Consultation : We’ll discuss your child’s medical history and determine the appropriate type of sedation for them.
  • Preparation : Depending on the sedation method, you may need to follow certain guidelines, such as not allowing your child to eat or drink before the treatment.
  • Sedation : Dr. Felix will provide sedation in a safe and comfortable environment. We will monitor your child closely during the procedure to ensure their safety and comfort.
  • Recovery : We will continue to monitor your child after the procedure until they are fully awake from the sedation. They might feel sleepy for a few hours after the procedure.
