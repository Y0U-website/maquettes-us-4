Frequently Asked Questions

What is tongue-tie or buccal frenum?

A tongue-tie or buccal frenum is a tight or thick tissue restricting normal tongue or cheek movement.

How does it affect my child?

It can cause difficulties with speech, eating, and proper oral hygiene if left untreated.

How is it treated?

Treatment involves a simple, quick procedure called a frenectomy to release the tissue.

Is the procedure painful?

Children are numbed with local anesthesia or sedation to ensure comfort during the procedure.
