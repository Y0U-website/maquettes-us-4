Restorative Dentistry in Cartersville

Dental Fillings

Quick, effective cavity repair. Cavities are one of the most common dental issues we treat. Left untreated, they can lead to pain, infection, or even tooth loss. At Sky Dental, we use tooth-colored, composite fillings to stop decay in its tracks—blending seamlessly with your natural tooth for a durable, discreet solution. Fillings can usually be completed in just one visit, helping restore your tooth’s strength and function with minimal downtime.

Dental Crowns

Protect and strengthen damaged teeth. If you have a cracked, broken, or worn-down tooth, a dental crown may be the perfect solution. Crowns are custom-made, tooth-shaped caps that cover and protect the natural tooth underneath. They restore your bite, improve appearance, and can even be placed over a tooth following a root canal or used to complete a dental implant. We use high-quality materials for natural-looking, long-lasting results.

Dental Implants

A permanent solution for missing teeth. When a tooth is lost, so much more than your smile is affected. Dental implants are the gold standard for tooth replacement because they restore both the crown and the root—helping preserve bone structure and maintain long-term oral health. At Sky Dental, Dr. Yearous uses advanced technology and guided implant placement to ensure precise, comfortable treatment. Whether you need one tooth replaced or several, we can help rebuild your smile with stability and confidence.

Tooth Extractions

Sometimes the best next step. We always aim to preserve your natural teeth whenever possible. But in some cases—such as severe decay, infection, or crowding—a gentle extraction may be the best way to protect your oral health. We offer comfortable, efficient extractions using modern techniques, local anesthesia, and sedation options when needed. After healing, we’ll help you explore tooth replacement options like implants, bridges, or dentures.

Fill the gap and restore your bite. A dental bridge is a fixed restoration that replaces one or more missing teeth using crowns attached to adjacent healthy teeth. It’s a great option for patients who aren’t candidates for implants or want a faster, more affordable solution. Your bridge is custom-made to match your natural smile, preventing shifting teeth and restoring full function to your bite.

Dental Bridges

Affordable, reliable tooth replacement. Whether you’ve lost several teeth or all of them, custom dentures can restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with ease. We offer both full and partial dentures at Sky Dental, crafted for a comfortable fit and natural appearance. Our team will work with you to ensure your dentures are secure, functional, and help you feel like yourself again.
