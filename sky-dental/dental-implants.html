What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are surgically placed into your jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once the implant heals and fuses with the bone, a crown, bridge, or denture is attached—creating a permanent, secure, and natural-looking replacement. Unlike dentures or bridges that sit on top of the gums, implants are anchored into your jaw, preserving bone and preventing facial sagging or shifting teeth.
