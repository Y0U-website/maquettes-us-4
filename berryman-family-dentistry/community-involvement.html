Community involvement

We love our oelwein family

Living in Oelwein, Iowa is very important to us. We have a strong sense of community and value the people here. That’s why we give back to our community. We volunteer our time and resources to help those in need, support local businesses, and promote cultural activities. We also take pride in being good stewards of the environment by recycling, picking up litter, and maintaining green spaces. By giving back to our community, we ensure that Oelwein remains a vibrant place where everyone can thrive.

SPONSORING OUR COMMUNITY

We will be helping kids and adults participate in their favorite activities. Because being able to live your passion is definitely something to smile about. If you have a team, group, or anything else, that you think we can help with, please let us know! Or send a nomination of a group you know about. We’d love to help, so contact us here!
