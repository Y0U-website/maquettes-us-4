Dr. Kelli Steinlage

Dr. Kelli Steinlage is an Iowa native who is passionate about the dental health of each of her patients. After graduating from University of Iowa’s College of Dentistry, she completed a residency at Wichita State University. Dr. Steinlage regularly volunteers for Iowa Mission of Mercy (IMOM), a free dental clinic servicing over 1000 patients annually. In her free time, you’ll find Dr. Steinlage enjoying a run, a good book, or cheering on the Iowa Hawkeyes with her fiancé, David.
