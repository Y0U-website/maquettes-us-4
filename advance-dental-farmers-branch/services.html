Family & General Dentistry

Family and general dentistry at Advance dental is centered on prevention! We are committed to quality cleanings, exams and preventative options to help stop common dental problems before they start or become more serious.

Restorative Dentistry

Your mouth is the gateway to your health. That is why when a tooth needs is broken or infected it is important to have it repaired or replaced. Our advanced technology, techniques and materials can provide a complete, revitalized and healthy smile to provide you with full oral health and function.

Cosmetic Dentistry

At Advance Dental dentistry is more than protecting your oral health it is helping you achieve a smile you love. Cosmetic dental treatments can provide solutions to patients wanting to enhance the shape, shade or look of their smile. Come in for a complimentary cosmetic consultation to know your personalized options in cosmetic dentistry!

Implant Dentistry

Whether you need to replace one tooth or an entire row, dental implants can be agreat option to restore your dental health. Dental implants are a permanent procedure that restores function and appearance.

Sedation Dentistry

Does the thought of going to the dentist make you feel anxious or nervous? These feelings are very common, approximately 75 % of the population reports feeling nervous with the thought of a dental appointment. Advance Dental offers options and levels of sedation to offer comfortable options to achieve your oral health.

Invisalign

Invisalign​®​ is a clear aligning therapy that helps straighten crowding, close spaces, and align your bite without the aesthetics and constrictions of traditional braces. Call our office for a complimentary Invisalign consultation to see if Invisalign is a fit for you!
