Dental Implant Process

Initial Consultation

Meet with Dr. Raffoul or Dr. Regan to discuss your goals and evaluate your oral health. We’ll use advanced imaging to assess your bone structure and plan your treatment.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is precisely positioned in your jawbone during a comfortable procedure. Most patients return to work the next day.

Healing Phase

Over 3-6 months, your implant integrates with your jawbone, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth.

Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, we attach your custom-designed crown, perfectly matching your natural teeth.
