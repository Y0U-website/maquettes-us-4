About

  • Dr. Aaron Parkin, DMD Dr. Parkin has been a Kansas City area dentist since 2013 and prides himself on providing affordable, quality dental care while making sure his patients are comfortable and at ease. Dr. Parkin grew up outside Denver, Colorado, attended college at BYU-Idaho and then obtained his DMD at Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania in 2010. He practiced dentistry in Albuquerque, New Mexico until he relocated to the Kansas City area with his wife and three daughters. In Dr. Parkin’s spare time, he enjoys traveling, being outdoors and rooting for the Chiefs!
