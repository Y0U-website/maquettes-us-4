Accessibility Policy

Accessibility is an ongoing process. Websites are regularly updated with new content, software, features, documents, and third-party integrations. We therefore continue to evaluate our website and make reasonable accessibility improvements as technology, standards, and user needs evolve.

Despite our efforts, some content or functionality may not yet fully satisfy every accessibility guideline. Certain older materials, documents, videos, plug-ins, or third-party features may also present accessibility limitations.

If we become aware of an accessibility concern, we will evaluate the issue and make reasonable efforts to correct it or provide the requested information or service through an accessible alternative.

Reporting Accessibility Concerns and Requesting Assistance

We encourage visitors who encounter an accessibility barrier to contact us promptly so that we have an opportunity to investigate the concern, provide assistance, and make reasonable corrective improvements.

If any page, feature, form, document, or service is difficult to access because of a disability, please contact us.

If you cannot access particular information or complete an action through our website, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information, product details, services, or assistance through an alternative accessible method.

Our website may contain links to, display content from, or integrate with third-party websites, applications, plug-ins, payment services, scheduling systems, maps, videos, social media platforms, and other technologies that we do not own, operate, or directly control.

Although we encourage third-party providers to make their content and services accessible, we cannot control or guarantee the accessibility of their websites, applications, content, or technology.

If you experience difficulty accessing third-party content through our website, please contact us. When reasonably possible, we will attempt to assist you, provide the relevant information through an alternative method, or direct the concern to the appropriate third-party provider.
