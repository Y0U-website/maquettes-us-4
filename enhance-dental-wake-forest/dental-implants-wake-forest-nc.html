Treatment Process of Dental Implants in Wake Forest, NC

During your initial consultation, we will discuss your treatment expectations and perform a comprehensive examination to assess your oral cavity. If implants are the right choice for you, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan to optimize your oral health.

The first procedure is implant surgery, in which we will surgically insert the implant posts in your jawbone under local anesthesia.

This step follows a three to six months recovery period during which the implant posts heal and fuse with the surrounding bone.

After complete healing, we will secure an abutment to connect the implant with the desired restoration ( crown , bridge , or denture ). The overall treatment may take a few months, but the results are definitely worth the long wait!
