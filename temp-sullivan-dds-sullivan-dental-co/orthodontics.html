Is Invisalign Right for You?

During your personalized consultation at Sullivan Dental Co., our team will evaluate your teeth, gums, and bite using advanced digital imaging. Invisalign is ideal for many adults and teens looking for an aesthetic alternative to braces.

For more complex alignment concerns, our doctors will discuss the best treatment options to achieve the results you want.

  • Mild to moderate crowding or spacing
  • Overbites, underbites, and some crossbites
  • Relapse from previous orthodontic treatment

The Invisalign Treatment Process

1. Consultation & Planning – We’ll review your goals, perform an exam, and take digital scans of your teeth to create your treatment plan.

2. Custom Aligner Design – Using advanced 3D technology, we’ll design your series of custom aligners to gradually move your teeth into place.

3. Wearing Your Aligners – You’ll wear each set of aligners for 1–2 weeks, at least 22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss.

4. Your New Smile – Once treatment is complete, you’ll receive a custom retainer to maintain your beautifully aligned smile.
