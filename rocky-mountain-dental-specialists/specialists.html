Shannan Johnson, DMD, MS

Board Certified as a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology, Chief of Periodontics/Advanced Education in General Dentistry faculty member at Luke Air Force Base and Offutt Air Force Base

Born and raised in Minnesota, Dr. Johnson traveled to Boston University for dental school where she met her husband, Derrik (a native Coloradan!). Following graduation, she spent the next 13 years serving as a dentist in the Air Force. While on active duty, Dr. Johnson continued her dental training and skill development by completing both Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) and Periodontics residency programs. She also served as a periodontics faculty member for general dentistry residency programs at multiple Air Force bases.

While Dr. Johnson enjoyed serving military members and families in Virginia, Texas, Arizona, and Nebraska, she and her husband always planned to settle in Colorado. They love spending time with their three beautiful children and building relationships in our community. She also enjoys running, hiking, crafting, reading, cooking, skiing, traveling, and being with friends.

Eric Feichtinger, DDS, MSD

Board Certified as a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontology, Chief of Periodontics

Dr. Feichtinger was born and raised in Loveland, Colorado. After receiving a bachelor’s degree from Gonzaga University, he returned home where he graduated from the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine and was inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honorary Dental Society. Dr. Feichtinger completed residency in periodontics at the University of Colorado with a certificate in Periodontics and Implantology as well as Master’s of Science in Dentistry.

Dr. Feichtinger’s passion for dentistry began at a young age when he made several trips to Vietnam with a medical mission team. During these trips he assisted his father who is a general dentist that has served the Loveland area for over 30 years. In his free time, Dr. Feichtinger is an avid skier and enjoys traveling, mountain biking, and sailing with his wife, Alexa, who is also both a Colorado native and a dental specialist. He is dedicated to providing the highest quality of personalized care to his patients.

Paul L. Child Jr., DMD, CDT

Board Certified as a Diplomate of the American Board of Periodontolgy, Chief of Periodontics/Advanced Education in General Dentistry faculty member at Luke Air Force Base and Offutt Air Force Base

Dr. Child is a Prosthodontist and Certified Dental Technician (CDT) that provides all procedures related to full-mouth rehabilitation and ethical, long-term solutions. As a Prosthodontist, he excels in high-end esthetics; dental implants, both surgical and restorative; removable prostheses supported by implants; medical and congenital conditions; and complex cases requiring a team approach. Regardless of the discipline, all the restorations he provides for his patients are custom designed, highly esthetic and natural appearing to ensure your new smile exceeds your expectations.

Dr. Child graduated dental school with High-Honors (summa cum laude) from Case School of Dental Medicine and completed his Prosthodontics specialty training at Louisiana State University Health Sciences Center. Since becoming a Prosthodontist, Dr. Child has provided over 100 continuing education courses in the United States and internationally, authored many articles, and completed research on many dental materials, instruments, equipment, and technologies. He is an avid follower and reader of anything dental, medicine, or technology related.

Dr. Child is a member of many academies and organizations, including the American Academy of Esthetic Dentistry, the invitation-only membership organization for the most accomplished professionals in the field of esthetic dentistry. These elite members perform some of the most sought-after and complicated cosmetic dental procedures available in the world today.

It is not uncommon for many of Dr. Child’s patient’s to seek him out, feeling hopeless about their condition, yet to complete treatment a few months later filled with hope, improved self-esteem, enjoying food again, and even career and life advancements due to their beautiful new smile. Many of Dr. Child’s patient’s travel from out-of-state seeking his solutions, having heard or read about how he treats his patients, then delivers on his promises.

Dr. Child’s patients describe him as a perfectionist with a loving and empathetic demeanor, with just the right amount of humor. He welcomes all and judges no one. He has genuine love for everyone and looks for the positive and commonality in individuals.

Dr. Child takes the necessary time with each patient to listen to their needs and to ensure that all of their options are explored and discussed, giving them the autonomy to choose what is best for them. Dr. Child is highly ethical and places this characteristic above all else in his work and personal life.

Dr. Child’s personal mantra is, “If I don’t have time to do it right the first time, when will I have time to do it over?” As a specialist whom is skilled and experienced in correcting others mistakes made by misdiagnosis, rushed treatment, of ignorance, Dr. Child ensures quality time is spent in each appointment to fully listen to your concerns (and your joys!), ensuring the outcomes are higher than expected.

When not involved in dentistry, Dr. Child can be found reading an inspiring book or spending time with his wife, children, and grandchildren.
