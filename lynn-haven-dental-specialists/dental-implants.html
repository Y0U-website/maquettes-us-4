Components of Dental Implant?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that are permanently anchored to the jawbone, after which the metal implant fuses with the bone through a process called "osseointegration." This creates a strong bond similar to that of a natural tooth root. Once integrated, implants are stable, long-lasting, and functional teeth, just like the real ones. These implants have three main components:

1. Implant

A dental implant is a screw-like post that provides a secure foundation for the new tooth. The implant is inserted into the jaw bone.

2. Abutment

The abutment is not visible in your mouth, but it connects your new tooth to the titanium implant.

3. Crown or Bridge of Teeth

Single Tooth Dental Implants

Our team of specialists can replace your single missing tooth with a natural-looking, tooth-colored dental crown placed on a single dental implant. The single-tooth implant will be customized to match the surrounding teeth, ensuring that no one will notice that a tooth was ever missing.

Implant Supported Bridge

When multiple teeth are broken or missing, an implant-supported bridge offers an effective solution for restoring dental function. This involves strategically placing dental implants to support a custom bridge. An implant-supported bridge not only improves the appearance of your smile but also ensures proper biting and chewing habits.

Implant Supported Dentures

Our implant-supported dentures offer patients a stable foundation for a beautiful and functional smile. Unlike traditional dentures, they don’t require dental adhesives to stay securely in place. These dentures can be easily removed for proper cleaning, yet due to the implant supports, they also help prevent bone recession.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Full-mouth dental implants are often the optimal choice for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth. This all-encompassing solution involves strategically placing four or more implant posts in the jawbone to securely anchor the restoration. This method guarantees that the implants are stable and functional, offering an experience similar in function and appearance to natural teeth.
