Single Tooth Dental Implants

A single implant(s) can support a crown to replace just one missing tooth, which is significantly more durable and natural than traditional bridges.

Multiple Tooth Dental Implants

Occasionally, a patient is missing more than one tooth but still has healthy gums and surrounding teeth. In this case, two or more implants are placed and capped with crowns, leaving the teeth intact.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

Patients who need to replace a full upper and/or lower set of teeth can now opt for full-mouth dental implants. Our specialists will strategically place 4 implants and then attach a full arch of replacement teeth to them.

Financing Frequently Asked Questions

Will applying for financing hurt my credit score?

Good news—applying for financing from our third-party lenders won’t impact your credit score. By answering a few questions, you can get pre-qualified in just 60 seconds.

What credit score is needed for dental financing?

We have had many patients who expect to be rejected due to a low credit score who end up being approved for financing. Most financing lenders require a score of 580, but we can pre-approve you for the implants you need in just 60 seconds.

Is there a credit check for dental implant financing?

As part of our application process, we conduct a soft credit inquiry to assess your eligibility. This inquiry is completely safe and will not affect your credit score in any way. However, if you decide to accept a loan for a purchase or open an account, your credit score may be impacted.

What about implant financing for bad credit?

Even with bad credit, we might still be able to approve you. Fortunately, the pre-approval application will not hurt your credit. The best thing you can do is work with us to complete the application to see where you stand and how much you can get approved for.

Is a down payment required?

Depending on your credit score, a down payment might be required at the time of treatment. We will walk you through your payment options when we provide you with your treatment plan.
