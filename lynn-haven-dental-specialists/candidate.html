How many dental implants do I need?

Typically, a single implant replaces a single tooth. However, if you require dental implants for the entire upper or lower jaw, our specialists can use modern implant methods to apply a fixed full-arch implant prosthesis using only 4-6 implants. Removable full-arch implant prostheses may only require 2-4 implants. We offer these specialized treatments to make sure that no one who needs dental implants slips through the cracks.
