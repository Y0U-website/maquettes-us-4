The practice

We take pride in offering dental services to the whole family. If you have a child who needs their first dental checkup, we can be sure to make the visit comfortable for them.

Maybe you haven’t been to a dentist in a long time, or maybe they thought of enduring a dental exam as stressful. Whether you need a checkup or a new dentist or have a tooth that hurts, we encourage you to make an appointment.

We not only clean teeth during a normal dental visit, but we also do comprehensive exams for both adults and children. Our routine cleanings include a careful exam of gums and mouth to detect gum disease and oral cancer issues. Your dentist or hygienist will talk to you about your dental hygiene regimen and recommend any changes that might be needed.

We can take an inside look at your teeth by taking digital x-rays. If there are any developing issues, we will be sure you understand what’s going on and what to do about it.

Don’t forget that we serve patients of all ages at our Mankato dental office. Don’t hesitate to make an appointment if you are a new patient. We are also available to treat dental emergencies.
