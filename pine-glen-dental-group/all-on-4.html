The All-on-4 Process at Pine Glen Dental Group

Every All-on-4 case at Pine Glen Dental Group follows a structured, patient-centered process designed to deliver predictable results and a comfortable experience from start to finish. Understanding each phase can help you feel confident and prepared before your first appointment.

Your Initial Consultation

The journey begins with a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Rice or Dr. Thelen, where digital impressions and advanced imaging help plan the restorative side of your treatment and coordinate with your surgical provider on timing and goals. Surgical Placement and Restorative Follow-Up

Once a treatment plan is in place, your implants are surgically placed by a trusted oral surgeon under local anesthesia. Depending on your case, a provisional prosthesis may be attached the same day as part of the surgical visit. Once you’ve healed from surgery, our dentists take over for the restorative phase, monitoring osseointegration, which is the process of the implants bonding with the jawbone, over the following months.

Final Prosthesis and Long-Term Care

After the healing period, our dentists craft and place the final restoration, matched to the color, contour, and feel of natural teeth. Patients who want to explore whether All-on-6 implants might provide additional stability in their case can discuss this option with Dr. Rice or Dr. Thelen as well. Why Choose All-on-4 Over Dentures?

Traditional dentures sit on top of the gums and rely on suction or adhesive to stay put, which can lead to slipping, sore spots, and difficulty eating certain foods, often leaving wearers feeling uncertain much of the time. Since they don’t stimulate the jawbone the way natural tooth roots do, conventional dentures also allow bone loss to continue over time, gradually changing the shape of your face.

All-on-4 implants work differently. The titanium implants act as artificial tooth roots, stimulating the jaw and halting further bone resorption. The fixed prosthesis doesn’t move, doesn’t require adhesive, and allows you to eat a full diet with confidence. Many patients who previously relied on full or partial dentures report a dramatic improvement in their quality of life after switching to implant-supported restorations.
