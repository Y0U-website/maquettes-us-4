Implant Restorations

Each option is built using high-quality materials and designed to complement your natural bite, facial symmetry, and aesthetic preferences.

  • Implant-supported bridges : For patients missing multiple adjacent teeth, an implant-supported dental bridge uses two implant posts to anchor a bridge without involving healthy neighboring teeth.

The Implant Restoration Process at Pine Glen Dental

Restoring a dental implant requires careful planning, precise craftsmanship, and a provider who understands how to bring all the pieces together seamlessly. At Pine Glen Dental, every step of the process is handled with the same attention to detail that has defined our care for more than two decades.

Your Consultation and Planning

The process begins with a thorough evaluation of your oral health. We review your medical and dental history, assess the condition of the implant site, and discuss your goals and expectations. If the implant post was placed by an outside oral surgeon or specialist, we coordinate seamlessly with that provider to ensure complete continuity of care.

We use digital technology to plan your restoration with precision. Digital impressions eliminate the discomfort of traditional putty molds and allow us to capture highly accurate measurements of your teeth and bite. These impressions are sent to a dental lab, where your custom restoration is fabricated to the exact specifications.

Placing Your Restoration

Once your custom crown or prosthetic is ready, you will return to our office for placement. Dr. Rice or Dr. Thelen will carefully attach the restoration to your implant post, check your bite for proper alignment, and make any necessary adjustments before you leave. Most patients are pleased to find that placement is simple and comfortable with no recovery time needed.

The materials used for implant crowns are the same high-quality ceramics we use for our ceramic crown restorations . They provide exceptional durability and a lifelike appearance that holds up over time.
