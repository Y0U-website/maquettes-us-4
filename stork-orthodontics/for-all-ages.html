Orthodontics for Adults It is never too late to achieve the smile you have always wanted. One in five orthodontic patients today is an adult, and for good reason. Many adults seek treatment to improve their confidence, boost their oral health, and correct issues they have lived with for years. We offer adult braces in Iowa that are designed to fit seamlessly into your professional and personal life.

Why Adults Choose Orthodontics

Adults often come to us for a variety of reasons. Some want to finally address crooked or crowded teeth that make cleaning difficult, while others are looking to correct a bad bite that causes jaw pain or uneven wear. Many simply want the boost in self-confidence that comes from a straight, healthy smile. Whatever your motivation, we are here to support your goals.

Treatment Options for Adults

The thought of wearing traditional metal braces can be a deterrent for many adults. At Stork Orthodontics, we offer discreet and efficient solutions that align with your professional image and busy schedule.

  • Damon™ Clear Braces: These ceramic braces offer the strength of traditional systems but blend in with your natural teeth for a less noticeable appearance.
  • Invisalign® and Spark™ Aligners: These clear, removable aligners are virtually invisible, providing a flexible and convenient way to straighten your teeth without disrupting your life.
