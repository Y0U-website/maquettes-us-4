In Practice

After my dad passed away and much prayer, my wife and I moved to Iowa to be closer to family. June of 2012 we opened Stork Orthodontics. My vision was an office that felt more like family than just a doctor’s office. A place everyone felt comfortable and was excited to come back. A place parents had peace of mind and kids felt confident.

I am deeply honored to have served the community for over thirteen years now. It is amazing to see the physical and emotional change in a person as they journey from being uncomfortable with their teeth to smiling with confidence and ease. I love when our patients finish treatment and they can’t stop smiling.

Continuing Education

I have always loved learning. In residency we weren’t required to attend any continuing education as we were just learning in school. However, I acquired over 100 hours of continuing education during this time. Each year my team and I meet and exceed the state guidelines for education. My drive to always improve and provide the best care for my patients and families will never end. Every day I am in practice, I am committed to being better today than I was yesterday.

Beyond the Practice Born in Albert Lea, Minnesota and raised in Illinois, I’ve lived in Johnston, Iowa, since 2016, and am thrilled to be raising my family in the community.

My amazing wife of over 18 years, Jenna, has been an integral part of the practice since Day 1. We have three children all gifts from God: Winnie, Wes, and Willow.

My orthodontist and mentor always taught me to keep things in order: God first, family second, and then work. I love spending time with my family and being with loved ones takes top priority when I’m not changing smiles. I love taking daily walks (when the Iowa weather permits), weight lifting, biking, cheering on the Cheifs, dates with my wife, watching my children grow. I’m an avid reader, with a focus on personal and professional growth. I’m consistently learning how to be a better parent, husband, and human, too. I’m finding the Bible to be my absolute favorite!
