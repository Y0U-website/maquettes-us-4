About Orthodontics & Braces

At Stork Orthodontics, we create treatment plans that are tailored to each individual. We know that every smile is unique, and we take the time to listen to your needs and goals before offering personalized recommendations.

How Orthodontic Treatment Works Orthodontic treatment uses braces or aligners to gradually guide teeth into their ideal positions. Braces—whether metal or ceramic—use brackets, wires, and bands to apply gentle, consistent pressure to move teeth over time. For those seeking a discreet option, clear aligners are custom-made plastic trays that fit comfortably over your teeth and shift them into alignment.

The length of your treatment depends on your unique needs and the type of orthodontic solution you choose. Some cases require only a few months, while others may need several years to achieve optimal results. Typically, you’ll visit our West Des Moines office every 4-8 weeks for progress checks and to ensure your treatment stays on track.

  • Improved Oral Health: Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, helping to prevent cavities and gum disease.
  • Better Function: Addressing bite problems can enhance your ability to chew and speak comfortably.
  • Long-Term Prevention: Early treatment can help you avoid more serious dental issues later on.

Duration of Orthodontic Treatments

​​The duration of orthodontic treatment can vary greatly depending on the individual’s needs and desires. Factors such as the severity of misalignment, age, and compliance with treatment can all impact the length of treatment. The orthodontic provider can also determine the length of treatment. On average orthodontic treatment in our office takes 16 months, your treatment could be slightly longer or shorter. Throughout your treatment, you’ll visit our West Des Moines office every 8-12 weeks to advance your treatment and ensure you’re on track for an on-time removal.
