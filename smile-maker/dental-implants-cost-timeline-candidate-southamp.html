Who May Be a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many adults with missing teeth may be candidates for implant treatment, but candidacy must be evaluated individually. Your dentist may review your teeth, gums, bite, bone support, medical history and medications before recommending a plan.

Some patients are ready for implant planning after the initial evaluation. Others may need treatment for gum disease, a damaged tooth, infection or bone loss before the implant phase.

  • Healthy gums or treatable gum disease
  • Enough bone to support the planned implant
  • Ability to maintain daily oral hygiene
  • Smoking or tobacco use
  • Medical conditions that may affect healing
  • Expectations for appearance, function and treatment time

What Is the Dental Implant Timeline?

The timeline varies by patient. A typical process may include:

1. Consultation and Imaging

The dentist evaluates the missing tooth area, nearby teeth, gums, bite and available bone. Imaging may be recommended to help plan implant position and identify important anatomy.

2. Treatment Plan

The treatment plan outlines the number of appointments, proposed sequence of care, expected healing stages and estimated cost. This is also when alternatives such as bridges or dentures may be discussed.

3. Implant Placement

The implant is placed in the planned position using appropriate anesthesia. The dental team provides instructions for the healing period.

4. Healing and Integration

The surrounding bone needs time to heal around the implant. Healing time depends on the treatment site, bone condition, whether grafting was performed and individual healing response.

5. Final Restoration

After the implant is ready to support the restoration, the dentist restores it with a crown, bridge or implant-supported denture depending on the treatment plan.

Ceramic Dental Implants vs Traditional Implant Materials

Some patients ask about metal-free or zirconia implant options. Ceramic dental implants may be discussed for certain patients, but the right material depends on the case. Your dentist can explain the advantages, limitations and clinical considerations for each option.

FAQs About Dental Implants Southampton PA

How much do dental implants cost in Southampton, PA?

Dental implant cost depends on the number of implants, final restoration, imaging, materials and whether additional procedures such as extraction or bone grafting are needed. A consultation is required for an individualized estimate.

How long does dental implant treatment take?

Treatment time varies. It may depend on bone condition, healing response, number of implants and whether preparatory treatment is needed.

Can a dental implant replace one missing tooth?

Yes, one implant and crown may be used to replace a single missing tooth when the patient is a candidate for that approach.

Are dental implants right for everyone?

Not every patient is an immediate candidate. Gum health, bone support, medical history, medications and daily hygiene all matter.

What happens if I need a tooth removed first?

If a damaged or infected tooth needs removal, your dentist can explain whether extraction, healing, grafting or implant planning should happen in stages.
