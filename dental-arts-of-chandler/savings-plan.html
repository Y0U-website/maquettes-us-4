I have insurance—do I qualify for a dental membership plan?

Although dental membership plans have numerous benefits, they also have several limitations. Here are a few points to consider.

  • Insured patients are ineligible: The plan only applies to patients without insurance coverage.
  • Membership is non-transferable: The plan can’t be transferred to other members, even if they are close family members.
  • Membership: Your membership is valid for one year and begins the day we receive full payment or you sign the payment plan.
  • Benefits expire: Like most insurance plans, your membership’s benefits lapse at the end of one year. Unused benefits can’t be transferred to the following year.
