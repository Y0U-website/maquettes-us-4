Orthodontics in Owings Mills, MD

We are especially proud of our orthodontic care because Dr. Jonathan Silverman is a Baltimore Premier Invisalign provider. Using the Invisalign system of clear plastic aligner trays, he can help move your crooked teeth into alignment. Thanks to advanced technology, these aligners are custom-fitted to your teeth and designed to exert pressure the same as metal braces just without all the brackets, wires and tiny rubber bands!

Most patients who receive Invisalign treatment see results in just a few months. And unlike traditional metal braces, straightening your teeth with Invisalign is more comfortable and discreet! Many of the people you see in your day-to-day life may not even realize you are wearing the aligners at all. And because they are custom-made, they do not affect your speech like traditional metal braces sometimes do.
