I am a 3rd generation Oklahoman from Lawton and have been married to my wife, Carol, for 42 years. Carol is also a 3rd generation Oklahoman from Lahoma. My practice started in Kingfisher in 1982 before establishing my current practice in Okarche in 1989. Here I am today, lucky enough to be…

My career has primarily been directed towards general dentistry and my practice has been closely associated with The Center of Family Love, a home for the mentality challenged. I have been fortunate enough to spend almost my entire career treating these wonderful individuals and their needs. I have an interest in developing a strong doctor-patient relationship with each patient that entrusts me with their dental care. Oral health is founded on the principle of prevention. All patients are recommended biannual cleanings and examinations to monitor and improve the health of the teeth, gums and mouth.

Missing teeth can make your cheeks look hollow and your mouth look sunken. Depending on the amount of teeth missing, we may suggest partial dentures or complete dentures.

A dental implant mimics the form and function of a natural tooth, which consists of a root and a crown. The crown is the visible part of your tooth. It is used to chew food and is therefore the most susceptible to cavities.

If you have a damaged or decayed tooth, our dentists can help you choose the best solution.
