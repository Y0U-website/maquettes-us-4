  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Their durability makes them a cost-effective and reliable long-term solution for missing teeth.
  • Is the dental implant procedure painful? The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the surgery. Some discomfort and swelling may occur after the procedure, but this can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The dental implant process can take several months from start to finish. This includes the initial consultation, the placement of the implant, a healing period for the implant to fuse with the bone, and the final placement of the crown, bridge, or denture.
  • Am I a good candidate for dental implants? Most people with missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. However, a thorough evaluation of your oral health, including bone density and gum health, is necessary to determine if implants are right for you.
  • What is the recovery time for dental implants? Initial recovery from the implant surgery typically takes a few days, but the complete healing and integration of the implant with the jawbone can take several months. During this time, it’s important to follow your dentist’s care instructions to ensure proper healing.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. This includes brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and visiting your dentist regularly for check-ups and cleanings.
  • Will my dental implants look natural? Yes, dental implants are designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, providing a seamless and natural appearance.
  • How much do dental implants cost? The cost of dental implants varies depending on the number of implants needed, the type of restoration, and other factors. We offer a range of financing options to make dental implants more affordable.
