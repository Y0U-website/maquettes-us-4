Dawn A. Bova, DMD, MBA

While attending Dental School at Oregon Health & Science University, Dr. Bova was selected by the faculty for Advanced Honors in Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry. Since graduation, she has continued to participate actively in programs for advanced training in cosmetic dentistry and is a member of the prestigious American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

Dawn and her husband have been Mercer Island residents since 2003 and together with their two daughters enjoy being active members of the community. Dawn started her MI practice in 2006 with the philosophy that everyone should be treated as a person first and as a patient second. This philosophy is reflected in every aspect of her practice. Dr. Bova would like to offer you what she believes to be the finest – and most personable – dental care available anywhere.

Favorite part of dentistry: I enjoy building professional and personal relationships with patients. I also like educating patients on ways to achieve and maintain oral health.

Hobbies: Spending time in the great outdoors either hiking, mountain biking, or camping. I enjoy a good book or movies too.

Bragging Rights: Raising three boys and a puppy named Kody.
