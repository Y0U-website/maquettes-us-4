A Conservative, Patient-Centered Approach

Dr. Cohen takes a comprehensive view of dental care. Rather than focusing only on an individual tooth, she evaluates how the teeth, gums, bite, existing restorations, and daily home-care routine affect one another.

Dr. Cohen works alongside prosthodontist Dr. Leonardo Frydman . Their complementary backgrounds allow South Florida Dental Care to provide preventive and general dentistry as well as more extensive restorative, implant, and tooth-replacement treatment.

  • Listening carefully to each patient’s concerns
  • Explaining dental findings in clear language
  • Preserving healthy tooth structure when possible
  • Balancing oral health, function, and appearance
  • Using preventive care to reduce future dental problems
  • Helping patients maintain completed dental treatment
