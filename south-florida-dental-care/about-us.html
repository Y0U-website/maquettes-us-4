A Team Approach to Patient Care

Our dentists are supported by dental hygienists, assistants, and front office professionals who contribute to different parts of the patient experience. Hygienists provide preventive care and monitor the health of the teeth and gums. Dental assistants support examinations and treatment, while the front office team coordinates appointments and administrative communication.

Communication among these team members is especially important when treatment requires several appointments. Patients should understand what has been completed, what remains, and why each step is part of the overall plan.

Visit our Meet the Staff page to learn more about the people who support our patients and dentists.

What Patients Can Expect

We also recognize that some patients feel apprehensive about dental treatment. Please tell us about previous experiences or specific concerns. Depending on the procedure and the individual patient, accommodations or sedation dentistry may be discussed.

  • A thorough evaluation before treatment recommendations are made
  • An explanation of relevant findings and available options
  • Consideration of the health, function, and appearance of the entire mouth
  • Treatment recommendations proportionate to the condition being addressed
  • Coordination among the dentists, hygienists, assistants, and front office team
  • Opportunities to ask questions before making treatment decisions
