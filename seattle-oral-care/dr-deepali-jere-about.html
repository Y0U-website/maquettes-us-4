Dr. Deepali Jere About

Dr. Deepali Jere is a compassionate Prosthodontist trained at the University of North Carolina,Chapel Hill. She specializes in restoring oral health and rebuilding smiles with expertise in implant dentistry.

She strives to provide the highest quality care using a systematic and balanced treatment planning approach based on sound scientific principles.

She focuses on contemporary and aesthetic restorative dentistry with expertise in implants.

From the outset, she is honest and sets clear expectations with her patients. She uses a conservative approach by prioritizing to preserve what naturally exists within the mouth before exploring usage of artificial alternatives. Balancing short term benefits with long term oral health, she strives to deliver lasting smiles.

Dr. Deepali Jere grew up in Bangalore, India. She completed her dental education at UNC Chapel Hill and chose to specialize in prosthodontics so she could help people complete their beautiful smiles. Her favorite procedure is Full arch dental implant restorations. She currently lives in Bellevue with her husband and kids, making use of her free time enjoying her garden, taking walks, and cooking delicious foods.
