Dr. Krishna is an advanced General Dentist with over 15 years of experience in successfully treating a wide range of issues affecting oral health.

She believes that oral health is an important facet of overall health and is her philosophy in treating patients.

She continually invests in honing her skills based on the latest advances in dentistry to provide the best possible care to her patients.

In her spare time, she enjoys giving back to the community. She volunteers with Medical Teams International and Public Health Clinics in the Seattle area. She likes to travel and spend time with her family. She loves to cook and try new recipes. She is fluent in English, Hindi, Tamil, Marathi and is learning Russian.

Dr. Krishna grew up in Nagpur/Chennai, India and completed her undergrad there. She transferred to America in 2003. She did her General Practice Residency at Forum Health in Ohio. She made her way to Seattle in 2009 where she has been practicing as an Advanced General Dentist and completed her implant fellowship, Invisalign certification, and AAFE certification in facial esthetics.

Dr. Krishna loves dentistry and restoring health and beauty to peoples smiles! Her favorite procedures are implant placement and dermal fillers. She currently resides in Bellevue with husband and daughters. In her free time Dr. K enjoys hiking, traveling, cooking, and listening to music. Dr. Krishna is a polyglot, speaking English, Hindu, Tamil, Marathi, and is currently learning some Russian!
