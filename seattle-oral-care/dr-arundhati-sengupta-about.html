Dr. Arundhati Sengupta

Dr. Arundhati Sengupta has extensive training in dental implant surgery, bone grafting, periodontal aesthetic surgery, and treatment of advanced periodontal disease.

Her professional training spans over 17 years of clinical and educational experience in various fields of dentistry with a specialization in periodontics from the University of Washington School of Dentistry.

Dr. Sengupta is a member of the American Academy of Periodontology, the American Dental Association, Washington State Society of Periodontists, Washington State Dental Association, and Seattle King County Dental Society. She is currently an Affiliate Associate Professor at the University of Washington School of Dentistry. Dr. Sengupta is skilled in all periodontal procedures and has vast clinical experience. Her passion includes working with the latest techniques and materials emerging in different fields of periodontics.

In addition to her periodontal expertise, Dr. Sengupta is certified in intravenous sedation. She also has background knowledge and clinical experience with many cases in different areas of restorative dentistry, endodontics, and aesthetic dentistry. Her versatile clinical experience enables her to approach each case with a comprehensive view of treatment.

Dr. Sengupta’s experience and expertise empower her to drive a practice philosophy grounded in knowledge, skill, and personal relationships with her patients. She takes into account each patient’s dental needs, concerns, and requests and involves them in selecting the best treatment option. She treats her patients as family members with empathy and compassion and ensures a high level of ongoing periodontal health for them.

She is friendly, cheerful, gentle, and compassionate with a pleasing chair-side manner. Dr. Sengupta lives in Redmond with her husband and two children and loves spending time with her family and friends.
