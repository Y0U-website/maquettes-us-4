What is Cosmetic Dentistry?

The term cosmetic dentistry refers to any dental procedure explicitly done to improve the aesthetics of your smile. It is no secret that having a perfect smile can improve your self-confidence and create a great impression in a first meeting.

While braces are relatively standard for people in their childhood, they do not give off the best look. One should also consider that we may want to fix the appearance of our teeth at an age when braces are no longer viable.

Cosmetic dentistry can help people who have felt conscious about their smiles for years at an end. Imagine having no stained or discolored teeth that sit in a perfect row! Oh, the satisfaction of flashing your perfect, pearly whites without worrying and feeling sad about how they look. Along with being physically appealing, cosmetic dentistry has several other benefits.

Benefits of Cosmetic Dentistry

Research has shown that less than two-thirds of the American population is happy with their smile. It is hard to admit that even after doing everything to maintain the health of your teeth and keep them clean and bright, they will eventually stain over time or crack due to overuse.

In such cases, cosmetic dentistry is the hero in a cape that comes to the rescue. It would behoove us to know the benefits of getting a cosmetic dentistry procedure.

You can feel young again: With increasing age comes health problems that aren’t restricted to our bones and muscles. Your teeth also face wear and tear and tend to stain and discolor with age, making you seem more aged than you are. In such an event, cosmetic dentistry enables you to get a fresh-faced smile and leave your doddering old look behind.

It improves your appearance aesthetically: To look beautiful is something everyone strives for, and cosmetic dentistry can help you achieve that by fixing the look of your teeth and giving you a bright and brilliant smile.

Cosmetic dentistry procedures are less painful than routine dental procedures: Most types of cosmetic dentistry procedures, like dental veneers, are non-invasive and do not cause irritation or pain.

It will boost your confidence: Having a smile that you believe is imperfect can make you quite conscious, resulting in your laughter being covered by your hand all the time or avoiding people altogether. Cosmetic dentistry can fix that problem and give you the much-needed boost in self-confidence so that you can make rounds in your social circle without inhibitions.

Five Types of Cosmetic Dentistry Procedures to Brighten your Smile

There are several different types of cosmetic dentistry procedures, but the five most common ones that are opted for and easily affordable are listed below for your perusal.

Dental bonding is a cosmetic dentistry procedure used to fix a cracked or discolored tooth. Crooked or angled teeth can spoil our smile. Dental bonding can fix that and can also be used for closing significant gaps that can appear between teeth over time.

It is one of the easily affordable cosmetic dental procedures. As a result, people opt for it a lot as it can give us a smile transformation at a reasonable price. It also requires the least amount of time to perform, and it is quite possible to complete the procedure in one sitting.

Dental Bridges fill in, or bridge, the gaps caused by missing teeth. Using a dental bridge to close gaps can help restore chewing functions for our teeth and avoid any speech impairments.

When we have a missing tooth that creates a large gap between two teeth, the remaining teeth might shift in position and create a messy look. Dental bridges help avoid that and are a convenient solution to tooth replacement.

Another popular type of cosmetic dentistry procedure in recent times has been teeth whitening . As the name suggests, teeth whitening helps to remove any staining on the teeth and lighten the shade of the teeth to make them appear brighter.

Bad eating habits and lack of care can make the teeth yellow and stained. At Minneapolis Dental, we use different teeth whitening agents, depending on the discoloring in teeth a patient is suffering from. Our team provides you with an all-natural look that is healthy and provides you with a shining smile.

Dental veneers can be used to fix the shape of a tooth that you dislike or to cover the discoloration on a tooth. They are also used for aligning teeth that are smaller or larger than your other teeth. This type of cosmetic dentistry procedure is a good option for people with healthy teeth who only wish to have a breathtaking smile.

A smile makeover is a complete transformation of your smile using a combination of different cosmetic surgery procedures, such as porcelain crowns , dental veneers, bonding, dental bridges, and teeth whitening, to complete the transition from an imperfect to a perfect smile.

This procedure allows people unhappy or depressed with their smiles to get a complete transformation, improving their quality of life and creating an excellent first impression on everyone they meet.
