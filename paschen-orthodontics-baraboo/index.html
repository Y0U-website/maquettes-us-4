Paschen Orthodontics has been providing orthodontic care for children, teens, and adults for decades. The first step for every patient is a thorough orthodontic evaluation. This includes a close look at the teeth, jaws, and bite so that we can identify any alignment problems that might cause you stress in the future.

Paschen Orthodontics offers a wide range of orthodontic services for children, teens, and adults. From orthodontic evaluations and interceptive orthodontics to braces and clear aligners, you’re in trusted hands at our practice.

Orthodontic evaluations help our team understand each patient’s bite, any misalignment issues, and potential emerging problems that could require complex treatment later on. It’s the first step before any treatment. A personalized treatment plan will follow if treatment is required.

Clear aligners are a removable option for teens and adults who want to straighten their teeth discreetly. These aligners are far less noticeable than braces and they make less of an impact on your everyday lifestyle. We’ll assess whether you could be a good candidate for aligners at your evaluation appointment.

Retainers are an essential element of orthodontic treatment. They are used for keeping orthodontic results intact after treatments such as braces or aligners are completed. Every retainer is custom made for a perfect fit, and we’ll advise you on cleaning and replacement instructions.

Space maintainers are sometimes recommended for children who have lost their baby teeth earlier than usual. They are a type of appliance that helps fill the gap and preserve the space for the incoming permanent teeth. This treatment supports alignment as the mouth develops.
