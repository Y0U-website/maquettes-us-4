Dental Implants

Expected Dental Implant Timeline The dental implant process typically spans several months, but the result is well worth the time invested. The first step involves an initial consultation, where our dentists will assess your current oral health, review your health history, take necessary imaging (such as X-rays or 3D scans), and discuss your goals. If you’re a good candidate, the implant placement will be scheduled.

During the procedure, a titanium post, which serves as the artificial tooth root, is surgically embedded into the jawbone. It’s once this portion is complete, that the healing period essential for osseointegration (fusion of the implant with the jawbone) can begin. This phase can take anywhere from 3-6 months, depending on individual healing rates and involves your jawbone strengthening around the implant, creating a stable and secure foundation for the other parts of the restoration.

After the area has healed, you’ll return to our Cooper City dental office for the placement of the abutment (connector piece) and porcelain crown . Your timeline may differ if you’ve chosen to receive implant-supported dentures or bridge.

  • Natural appearance: Implants look and function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
  • Improved oral health: Unlike other forms of restoration, implants don’t require the alteration of surrounding teeth, preserving your enamel and overall tooth structure.
  • Bone loss prevention: Implants help stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone deterioration that can occur when tooth roots are no longer there.
  • Convenience: Since implants are permanent, there’s no need to worry about removing them for cleaning like dentures.
