Implants

All implants are not created equal. Like all his materials, Dr. Hood uses the best implant system on the market. Some patients require a bone graph before beginning their implant procedure.

Dental implants are surgical-grade root devices that support permanent tooth prosthetics. These artificial roots are anchored in the bone beneath the gums, where they become fused into the jaw. A crown is mounted atop the implant for a long-lasting and natural-looking smile.

Many patients prefer dental implants, as they offer the same function as natural teeth and help prevent bone atrophy in the jaw.

Dental implants may be used to replace a single missing or damaged tooth or to restore an entire smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer significant advantages for those looking to restore their smiles in Naples, FL.

Implants look and feel like natural teeth, improving both appearance and confidence. Unlike removable dentures, implants are permanently fixed, providing enhanced comfort and eliminating the common discomforts associated with dentures.

Dr. Hood’s professional expertise ensures that each implant is placed with precision, helping to prevent bone loss and maintain facial structure for better oral health.

With proper care, dental implants are a durable, long-lasting solution, allowing patients to enjoy a lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles.

The Dental Implant Process

Dr. Hood’s extensive experience and use of advanced technology guarantee optimal results and a positive experience for every patient.

Here’s an overview of the six key steps in our dental implant process:

  • Initial Consultation : Discuss your needs and evaluate your oral health.
  • Treatment Planning : Personalized plan using advanced 3D imaging.
  • Implant Placement : Surgical insertion of the implant into the jawbone.
  • Healing Period : Allow time for osseointegration.
  • Abutment Placement : Connects the implant to the crown.
  • Final Restoration : Placement of the custom-made crown for a natural look.
