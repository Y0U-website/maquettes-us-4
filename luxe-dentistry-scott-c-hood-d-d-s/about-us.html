Meet our Team at Luxe Dentistry

Dr. Scott Hood

DDS – Proprietor

Dr. Scott Hood graduated, with endodontics honors, in the top 10 of his class, from the University of Michigan Dental School in 1998.

Dr. Hood has attended numerous advanced trainings over the years( Dawson Academy, Cerec Mastery Training, Dentsply Sirona Academy, Dentsply Ankylos Implant Training, and Los Angeles Institute of Esthetic Dentistry, just to name a few). His advanced endodontic training and experience enables his patients to stay in-house for most root canals and other endodontic procedures. His advanced cosmetic trainings make him much sought after, for smile rehabilitation and smile design. He’s the dentist behind numerous high-profile smiles.

Dr. Hood is now bringing his passion for family and cosmetic dentistry, along with his smile design expertise, to Naples. His new concierge location, Luxe Dentistry, where he specializes in premier smiles, is conveniently located in the Granada Shoppes at Immokalee and Tamiami Trail, on the side of Trader Joe’s.

Dr. Hood is a people person and known for his joking around. His passions include golfing and playing with his young daughter, who often takes him captive in her playroom. He enjoys the Naples sunshine, spending time with his family, and living a healthy lifestyle.
