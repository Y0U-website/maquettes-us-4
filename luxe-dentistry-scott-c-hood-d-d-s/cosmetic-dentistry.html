Cosmetic Dentistry

At Luxe Dentistry, Dr. Hood and his team are capable of transforming your smile’s shape, color, alignment, as well as filling in gaps and discreetly restoring decayed or damaged teeth with tooth-colored fillings, clear aligners, whitening, and more.

Modern advancements in cosmetic dentistry have made it possible for patients to achieve nearly perfect teeth with cosmetic enhancements and restorations.

From teeth whitening ( Zoom!, On the Go), veneers, bonding, straightening ( Sure Smile), and contouring to implants, crown & bridge, and more, Luxe Dentistry delivers bright, white and healthy smiles.

We provide in-depth smile consultations, coupled with treatment plans and excellent communication, to ensure the smile of your dreams!
