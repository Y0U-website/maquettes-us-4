About

Modern dentistry with a personal touch

Prestwicke Dental combines modern technology, personalized care, and a welcoming patient experience for families in Algonquin and surrounding communities.

A practice built around you

From the moment you walk in, our goal is to make dental care feel comfortable, straightforward, and personal. Prestwicke Dental combines a welcoming environment with modern technology and individualized treatment planning.

Whether you are bringing in your family, addressing a dental concern, replacing a missing tooth, or improving your smile, our team provides comprehensive care with clear communication at every step.

Patients can expect thoughtful explanations, time to ask questions, and recommendations tailored to their individual needs. Our digital imaging and scanning technology supports careful diagnosis, treatment planning, and clearer communication throughout care. As a locally established practice serving Algonquin since 2002, we value lasting relationships with the families who trust us with their smiles.

What guides everything we do

Patient-First Care We listen first, then recommend care based on your oral health, comfort, and goals.

Family Friendly Welcoming dental care for children, adults, and families at every stage of life.

Clinical Excellence Modern technology and thoughtful treatment planning support precise, high-quality care.

Honest & Transparent Clear recommendations, treatment options, and estimated costs so you can make informed decisions.
