Preferred pricing increases with continuous membership

Eligible treatment performed at Prestwicke Dental receives the preferred-pricing level associated with each consecutive membership year.

  • 1 Year 1 10 % off eligible treatment
  • 2 Year 2 12 % off eligible treatment
  • 3 Year 3 and later 15 % off eligible treatment

How loyalty levels work

  • The loyalty level is based on continuous, uninterrupted Care Club membership.
  • Advancement to the next loyalty level requires renewal without a lapse; reenrollment after cancellation is governed by the current agreement.
  • Preferred pricing applies only to eligible treatment provided while the membership is active.
  • Savings are not retroactive and do not apply to membership fees or services already included in the plan.
