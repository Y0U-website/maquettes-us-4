ClearCorrect

ClearCorrect and Invisalign share a lot of similarities in that both use a series of plastic trays to move the teeth. The big difference? ClearCorrect is better for making smaller, more aesthetic changes, so the treatment usually requires fewer trays. This makes it a more affordable option compared to Invisalign for most patients while allowing them to enjoy the same quality of care.

Invisalign

Tens of millions of people worldwide have achieved the straighter, healthier, and more attractive smiles they desire thanks to Invisalign. Able to correct crooked, crowded, and gapped teeth while allowing a patient to maintain their diet, oral hygiene, and day-to-day appearance, it’s no wonder Invisalign has become the go-to orthodontic treatment for teens and adults.
