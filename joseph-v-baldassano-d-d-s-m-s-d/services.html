Our Professional Endodontic Services

Root Canal Therapy and Regenerative Therapy

Root Canal Therapy for any tooth needing such care is performed utilizing digital imaging, surgical microscopes with digital video cameras, ultra sonic instrumentation and 3 dimensional

Regenerative Therapy for immaturely developed, root delayed, adolescent teeth. This procedure is done by Dr Baldassano to re-start root growth utilizing the patient’s own stem cells. Therefore, enhancing continued root development in an otherwise dead tooth.

Each of these procedures are performed while employing state of the art instruments and techniques.

Retreatment Root Canal Therapy

Retreatment Root Canal Therapy of prior treated root canals is performed for any tooth requiring such care. Occasionally, an older treated root canal can become infected and need care.

Yes, Root canals can be retreated to save your tooth yet again!

This procedure is also performed with digital imaging, using surgical microscopes with digital video cameras, ultrasonic instrumentation and 3 dimensional CBCT limited field imaging.

All to enhance treatment success and save your tooth.
