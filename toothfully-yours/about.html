Dentist in Owings Mills, MD

Dr. Pooja Soni, DDS, DASBA, AADSM Qualified Dentist

Meet Dr. Pooja Soni, DDS, DASBA, AADSM Qualified Dentist, the passionate and dedicated dentist at Toothfully Yours.

Dr. Soni completed her dental education at Terna Dental College & Hospital in Mumbai, India, and further enhanced her skills through the Advanced Standing Dental program at Howard University, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. With a strong foundation in dental knowledge, she has always enjoyed providing exceptional care to her patients. She has recently earned the distinction of being an AADSM Qualified Dentist and a Diplomate of the American Sleep and Breathing Academy (DASBA), further demonstrating her commitment to advanced dental care.

Dr. Soni’s commitment to dentistry goes beyond patient care. She strongly believes in the profound impact that oral health has on overall well-being. To stay at the forefront of her field, she keeps herself abreast with the latest advancements in technology and treatment options. Dr. Soni also actively engages in research and works towards ensuring that her practice incorporates evidence-based techniques.

Driven by a genuine desire to serve the community and help people, Dr. Soni is thrilled to make a positive impact through her dental expertise. She actively participates in community outreach programs and mission trips, providing dental care to underserved populations and making a positive impact on their oral health. Her compassionate approach and personalized care ensure that each patient receives the highest level of attention and treatment.

When she’s not brightening smiles, Dr. Soni embraces the joys of family life. As a proud mother of two children, she cherishes spending quality time with her loved ones. During her free time, Dr. Soni indulges in her passions for dancing, cooking, and traveling, all of which contribute to her well-rounded approach to life.

With Dr. Pooja Soni’s expertise, dedication to education, and passion for research, Toothfully Yours offers exceptional dental care that combines the latest advancements with personalized treatment plans. Trust Dr. Soni to provide comprehensive dental solutions and a compassionate approach that prioritizes your oral health and overall well-being.

Dentist in Owings Mills, MD

Dr. Karen Cao

Dr. Karen Cao is a dedicated dentist who is passionate about providing thoughtful, personalized care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. She believes dentistry is about more than treating teeth it is about building trust, educating patients, and helping each person feel confident in their oral health.

Dr. Cao earned her dental degree from Nova Southeastern University before completing a General Practice Residency at the Veterans Affairs Hospital in North Texas. Through her residency training, she gained experience caring for patients with a wide range of dental needs and medical backgrounds. She enjoys all aspects of general dentistry, with particular interests in restorative, implant, and cosmetic dentistry.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Cao enjoys baking, traveling, creating content, and spending time with the people and pets she loves. She is excited to be part of the practice and looks forward to building lasting relationships with her patients and their families.

Dr. Ashwant Kaur, DDS

Dr. Kaur has always believed that a healthy smile has the power to transform not just your appearance, but your confidence and overall well-being. That belief is what drives her every single day as a general dentist, and it’s what led her to pursue the highest level of training she could.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree along with an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) certificate from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry a rigorous program that broadened her clinical expertise and prepared her to deliver comprehensive, high-quality care across a wide range of dental needs.

Dr. Kaur is known for creating a warm, welcoming environment where patients feel truly heard and at ease. She takes the time to listen to each patient’s concerns, explain treatment options in a clear and straightforward way, and develop personalized plans that align with their individual needs and goals. Whether a patient is coming in for a routine cleaning or a more complex procedure, Dr. Kaur approaches every visit with patience, precision, and a gentle touch.

Outside of her practice, Dr. Kaur cherishes time with her family and brings that same warmth and enthusiasm to everything she does. She enjoys reading novels, cooking, and dancing, and has a love for exploring new places and discovering new things a curiosity and passion for lifelong learning that she carries directly into her professional growth as well.

Dr. Kaur is proud to be welcoming new patients and looks forward to being a trusted partner in your dental health journey.
