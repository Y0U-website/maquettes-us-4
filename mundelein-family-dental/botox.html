Dental Bonding

If you&#x27;re looking for a more conservative way to transform your smile, dental bonding may be right for you. Your dentist will apply a color-matched composite resin to your teeth, shaping and sculpting it to blend seamlessly with the rest of your smile. Then, they&#x27;ll cure it with a special blue light to complete the process. Bonding doesn&#x27;t require any invasive prep, and can cover up minor chips, cracks, and stains, often in a single visit. Schedule a consultation today to find out if dental bonding can help you achieve your dream smile!

Veneers

If you feel insecure about your smile for reasons like chips, gaps, or discoloration, veneers can fix that and more. Veneers totally transform your smile to hide any cosmetic imperfections. They are expertly crafted from porcelain, a thin yet durable material, to attach to the front of your teeth. This gives you a natural, beautiful smile that you will feel confident in. If you are interested in learning more about the benefits of veneers, contact us and we will be happy to schedule you for a cosmetic consult.

Invisalign

Invisalign is a modern, discreet way to straighten your teeth without the wires and brackets required by traditional braces. Using a series of clear, removable aligners, this treatment gently shifts your teeth into alignment, giving you a confident smile and a healthier bite. Because it’s nearly invisible, Invisalign lets you keep your lifestyle and appearance intact throughout your treatment.

Smile Makeover

If you&#x27;re looking to transform your smile and boost your confidence, a smile makeover could be the perfect solution. A smile makeover combines multiple cosmetic and restorative dental treatments tailored to improve the appearance, function, and health of your teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is cosmetic dentistry and how can it help improve my smile?

Cosmetic dentistry is a specialized field of dentistry that focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and overall smile. It can include a wide range of treatments, from teeth whitening and veneers to orthodontics and dental implants.

At Mundelein Family Dental, Dr. Sadia Ahmed uses the latest techniques and technologies in cosmetic dentistry to help you achieve the smile you&#x27;ve always desired. Whether you&#x27;re looking to brighten your teeth, straighten them, or replace missing teeth, cosmetic dentistry can provide effective and long-lasting solutions.

How do I know if cosmetic dentistry is right for me?

If you&#x27;re unhappy with the appearance of your teeth or smile, cosmetic dentistry could be a great option for you. It can address a variety of dental issues such as stained or discolored teeth, gaps between teeth, broken or chipped teeth, misaligned or crooked teeth, and missing teeth.

During your consultation at Mundelein Family Dental, Dr. Sadia Ahmed will thoroughly examine your oral health and discuss your aesthetic goals to determine the most suitable cosmetic dentistry treatment for you.

Is cosmetic dentistry in Mundelein expensive?

The cost of cosmetic dentistry can vary greatly depending on the type and extent of the treatment required. However, at Mundelein Family Dental, we believe that everyone deserves a beautiful smile, and we strive to make our treatments as affordable as possible.

We offer various financing options to help cover the cost of your treatment. During your consultation, we will provide a detailed cost estimate and discuss your payment options.

How long does it take to see results from cosmetic dentistry?

The timeframe for seeing results from cosmetic dentistry can vary depending on the type of treatment. Some procedures, such as teeth whitening, can produce immediate results, while others, like orthodontics or dental implants, may take several months to a year or more.
