DR. LUIS TEIXEIRA

Dr. Luis Teixeira is passionate about treating every patient like family, focusing on closing the gap between their dream smile and current reality. Known for his commitment to clinical excellence, Dr. Teixeira strives to provide exceptional care and results for each patient he sees. Outside the office, he enjoys cycling, sightseeing, traveling, and is an enthusiastic motorsport fan, bringing the same energy and precision from these hobbies to his dental practice.

DR. RUTH CABRAL

Dr. Ruth Cabral is dedicated to providing the best possible care with warmth and compassion, creating a comfortable and enjoyable experience for her patients. She values building strong relationships with each patient and ensuring they feel genuinely cared for at every visit. When she’s not at work, Dr. Cabral loves spending time with her family and has a talent for baking, often creating delicious cakes, pies, and cookies for her loved ones.
