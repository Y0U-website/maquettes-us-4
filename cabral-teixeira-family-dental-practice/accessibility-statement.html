We are dedicated to providing an inclusive experience for every patient and visitor. Your feedback helps us improve.

  • Wheelchair Access: Our office, located at 875 E Canal Dr, Suite #8, Turlock, CA, is fully wheelchair accessible, including the entryway, hallways, and treatment areas.
  • Accessible Restrooms: Our restrooms are equipped with accessible features such as grab bars and sufficient space for maneuverability.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Our website can be accessed and navigated using a keyboard alone for those with mobility limitations.
  • Alt Text for Images: All meaningful images are tagged with descriptive alternative text for screen reader compatibility.
  • Clear Layout & Readability: Our website content is structured and presented in a user-friendly format to support ease of use.
  • Captioned Videos: Videos include captions to enhance accessibility for visitors with hearing impairments.
