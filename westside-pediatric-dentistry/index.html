The practice

Our office offers a wide range of pediatric dental services to ensure that your child’s oral health is looked after from their very first tooth to adolescence and beyond.

Preventive care is all about keeping your child’s teeth healthy in the first place. This includes regular checkups and cleanings, as well as access to things like dental sealants, space maintainers, and orthodontic screenings to catch any potential issues early on.

If your child has a cavity or other dental issue, restorative treatments can help address the problem. We offer fillings, pulp therapy, dental crowns, extractions, and pulpotomies to help restore your child’s teeth and maintain their natural state.

Sealants are a preventive measure to help protect your child’s teeth from decay in hard-to-clean areas. They are a thin protective coating that is applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth, where decay often occurs.
