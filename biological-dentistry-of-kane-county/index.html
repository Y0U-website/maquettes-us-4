The practice

Dentistry should be about more than just keeping you cavity-free and giving you an attractive smile. Our mission at Biological Dentistry of Kane County is to help patients achieve and maintain optimal health from head to toe while empowering them to make confident treatment decisions.

You’ll always work with Dr. Allen, ensuring your care is consistent and tailored to you.

We use hydroxyapatite to fortify the teeth against cavities because it’s safer and more effective.

With Dr. Allen, you won’t just have a dentist; you’ll gain a valuable partner in your health journey. He relies on cutting-edge research as well as tried-and-true techniques and state-of-the-art technology. The result is a more thorough form of dentistry that is fully personalized to each person’s goals and supports their overall health.

The quality of your dental care will always be prioritized over the restrictions of your insurance plan. But we are happy to help patients understand and file so they can take advantage of their benefits.

Compared to traditional tooth replacement options, dental implants are more stable, more natural-looking, and last multiple times longer. And at Biological Dentistry of Kane County, Dr. Allen makes this treatment even more accessible by offering a variety of types of dental implants, some of which aren’t available at most practices.

A dental implant is a prosthetic tooth root that is placed directly into the jawbone to replicate the underlying supportive structure. This is then attached to a restoration on top.

Using the latest science-backed methods and non-toxic materials, our dental office can accomplish things that go well beyond the teeth and gums, helping patients achieve their wellness goals in a way that is tailored to them.
