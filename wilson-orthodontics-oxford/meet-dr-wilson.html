Dr. Wilson, DDS

Dr. Michael J. Wilson, the founder of Wilson Orthodontics, has been providing excellent orthodontic care to the community of Durham and Oxford since 2004. He is committed to researching and utilizing advanced technology and the latest treatment methods to create handcrafted smiles for his patients. Dr. Wilson has received over 300 five-star reviews because he is always striving to make treatment more comfortable and worthwhile for his patients.

Since 2004 Excellent Expertise Our expert team at Wilson Orthodontics has been improving smiles in the community since 2004. Each member of our team is a highly trained orthodontic expert focused on providing you and your family with confidence-building results that you will love and a fun, memorable orthodontic treatment experience you’ll never forget.

Read My Book Durham & Oxford, NC Dr. Wilson, Ortho Professional Education After his own braces experience, Dr. Wilson was inspired to become an orthodontist, equipped with a desire to provide others with the confidence a straight smile brings. His orthodontic journey began at State University of New York at Buffalo where he received his Orthodontic Specialist Certificate and his Doctor of Dental Surgery. Before accomplishing these degrees, Dr. Wilson attended S.U.N.Y. at Buffalo for his undergraduate studies.

Dr. Wilson continued investing in his knowledge after school, becoming a Certified Preferred Invisalign® Provider and the first orthodontist to offer SureSmile® in the Durham, NC, area. His dedication to continual learning ensures every patient receives the highest quality orthodontic care.
