0 Nick Daily, DMD Dr. Daily was born and raised in Indianapolis, IN where he graduated from Lawrence Central High School. He attended Indiana University in Bloomington, IN where he graduated with a degree in Neuroscience. Dr. Daily then moved to Louisville, KY where he graduated from the University of Louisville with his Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD).

Dr. Daily strives to provide the highest standard of care for his patients. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the Indiana Dental Association, the Indianapolis District Dental Society, and the Academy of Sports Dentistry.

Dr. Daily lives in downtown Indianapolis with his little pup named Tank. In his spare time, Dr. Daily enjoys playing guitar, photography, travel, hiking, and spending time with friends and family.

Dr. Johnson grew up in Hartford City, IN. He completed his BA in Biology/Biochemistry at Franklin College in 2003. After completing his undergraduate degree, Dr. Johnson attended Indiana University School of Dentistry where he served as a class officer for all four years and received multiple awards for academics and community service before graduating in 2007.

Dr. Johnson has continued to expand his dentistry skill set throughout his career. He completed his dental residency at Indianapolis VA Medical Center in 2008 and completed an anesthesiology fellowship at Indiana University School of Medicine in 2009.

A born and bred Hoosier, Dr. Johnson moved to Center Grove in 2009 and lived there for 15 years. Recently, he moved to Whiteland with wife Angie, their three teenage kids, and two dogs! They are members of Greenwood Christian Church. When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Johnson enjoys reading, road biking, hiking, video games, music, travel, and cheering on the kids at all their activities.
