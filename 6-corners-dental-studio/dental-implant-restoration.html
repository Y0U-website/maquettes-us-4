The Dental Implant Restoration Process

The implant restoration process depends on your individual treatment needs, but most cases include the following steps:

Evaluation and Digital Imaging

Dr. Hong will examine your implant site, take digital X-rays, and evaluate the surrounding gums and bone structure. If your implant was placed by a specialist, we’ll coordinate closely to ensure seamless care.

Digital Impressions and Smile Design

We take precise digital impressions to design a restoration that matches the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth.

Restoration Fabrication

Your implant crown, bridge, or denture is custom-crafted by a trusted dental laboratory using high-quality materials designed for durability and aesthetics.

Final Placement

Once your restoration is ready, we securely attach it to the implant abutment and make any final adjustments to ensure a balanced bite and natural appearance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are used for dental implant restorations?

Most implant crowns are made from porcelain or zirconia because they provide excellent durability and a natural appearance.

Will my implant restoration look natural?

Yes. Every restoration is custom color-matched and shaped to blend naturally with your surrounding teeth.

Can you replace a damaged implant crown?

Yes. In many cases, a damaged implant crown can be repaired or replaced without removing the implant post itself.

How long do dental implant restorations last?

With proper oral hygiene and routine dental visits, implant restorations can last many years.

How do I care for my dental implant?

Brush and floss daily, attend routine cleanings, and avoid chewing extremely hard objects. We’ll provide personalized care instructions based on your restoration type.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Some dental insurance plans provide partial coverage for implant restorations or related procedures. Our team can help review your benefits and financing options before treatment begins.
