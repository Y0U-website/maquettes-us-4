What age is best to start orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic evaluations can begin as early as age 7, though most treatments start between ages 10–14 or during adulthood. Adults of any age can benefit from alignment correction.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

For many patients with mild to moderate alignment issues, yes. Invisalign is effective when worn consistently and monitored regularly. For complex movements, traditional braces may be recommended.

How long does treatment take?

Treatment length depends on the severity of your case. Most Invisalign treatments last 12–18 months, while traditional braces may take longer. We&#x27;ll give you a personalized timeline at your consultation.

Will insurance cover orthodontics?

Many dental insurance plans include orthodontic benefits. Visit our Patient Info page or contact us to verify your coverage and learn more about payment options.

Do I need to wear a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Retainers help preserve your results by preventing your teeth from shifting back. We&#x27;ll recommend a custom retention plan based on your case.
