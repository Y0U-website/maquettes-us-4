Get to Know Dr. Michal DiCalogero

From a young age, Dr. DiCalogero was drawn to health and medicine as well as creative, hands-on work. Dentistry allows her to combine all of her innate interests into one consistently rewarding profession. She sees herself as a partner in her patients’ overall health and takes pride in educating them while helping restore both function and aesthetics in a way that feels personalized and meaningful.

Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. DiCalogero received her dental degree from the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine and went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital, which provided a strong clinical foundation early in her career.

Since then, she has pursued continuing education across a broad range of general dentistry topics, including ceramic veneers, dental implants, surgical extractions, socket preservation, guided tissue regeneration, and comprehensive treatment planning. Dr. DiCalogero is committed to continually expanding her skills to provide the highest level of care to her patients.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

When she isn’t working, Dr. DiCalogero loves staying active and seeking out new experiences. She enjoys cooking, skiing, skating, and is always excited to try a new sport or adventure. Dance holds a special place in her heart, as it helped her grow out of her shyness as a child and continues to bring her joy. Otherwise, she is always happy to spend time with her husband, friends, and family.
