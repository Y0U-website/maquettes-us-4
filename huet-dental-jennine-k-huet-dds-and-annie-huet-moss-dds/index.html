The practice

At Huet Dental, we pride ourselves on delivering quality dentistry to The Woodlands community.

Our personalized approach to advanced dental care makes a visit to our office a unique and rewarding experience. Our inviting, modern family dental practice focuses on quality and comfort, offering customized care plans and patient-centered services in a relaxed, professional setting.

We know The Woodlands community deserves convenience without sacrificing quality, which is why Huet Dental is committed to simplifying your dental care. From online scheduling and bill pay to our in-house dental savings plan, we want to remove any barriers between you and your best smile.

When you first enter our doors you will be greeted by a complimentary refreshment bar which includes a variety of sparkling and still water, juice, coffee and teas as well as allergy-friendly snack bars.

Once we bring you back for your appointment we offer a warm herbal neck wrap and music or TV of your choice. A freshly laundered fuzzy blanket is also available upon request for those who want some extra warmth and comfort during their appointment. We value providing a relaxing and peaceful environment in our office so that your time with us is as enjoyable as possible.

Each room is equipped with a TV with on-demand features, an Alexa with endless music request capabilities and a large window overlooking a forest with bird feeders frequented by squirrels, blue jays and cardinals.

Dr. Jennine Huet is an award-winning dentist who has been a trusted member of The Woodlands community for over 25 years. Her expertise is reflected in her rigorous professional training and commitment to continuing education. She is proud to offer quality, compassionate dentistry to The Woodlands in partnership with her daughter, Dr. Annie Huet Moss.

In addition to her professional accomplishments, Dr. Huet is an avid humanitarian who enjoys devoting her time to volunteer work.
