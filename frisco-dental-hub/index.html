Services

Scaling and root planing is billed per quadrant (D4341 for quadrants with heavy calculus, D4342 for lighter cases). Most insurance covers a significant portion.

Cost vs. consequences: The cost of scaling and root planing is a fraction of the cost of tooth loss and replacement. A single dental implant (to replace one tooth lost to gum disease) costs $3,000–$6,000. A full-arch implant restoration can exceed $25,000…

Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement. Single implants, implant bridges, and full-arch All-on-4 restorations using 3D CBCT planning and guided surgery.

Clear-aligner orthodontics for teens and adults. Straighten teeth discreetly without metal brackets. Average treatment 12–18 months. Complimentary smile assessment.

Up to 8 shades brighter in a single visit. Safe, gentle, and dramatically more effective than over-the-counter strips.
