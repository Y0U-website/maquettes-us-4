Why Dental Implants Are the Gold Standard

Compared to bridges and dentures, implants are the only tooth replacement that preserves jawbone and functions exactly like a natural tooth.

Prevents Bone Loss

After tooth loss, the jawbone beneath shrinks by up to 25 % in the first year. Implants stimulate the bone just like a natural root — the only replacement that does.

Full Bite Force

Implants restore 90–100 % of natural bite force. Dentures restore only 20–25 %. Eat anything you want — corn on the cob, steak, apples.

Lasts a Lifetime

The titanium post typically lasts a lifetime with proper care. The crown lasts 15–25 years. Compare that to bridges (10–15 years) and dentures (5–7 years before relining).

Natural Appearance

The porcelain or zirconia crown is custom shade-matched to your surrounding teeth. No one will know it’s not real — including you.

Protects Adjacent Teeth

A traditional bridge requires grinding down the two healthy teeth on either side to anchor it. Implants stand alone — no damage to neighboring teeth.

Easy to Clean

Brush and floss an implant exactly like a natural tooth. No soaking, no adhesives, no special tools. Simplest maintenance of any tooth replacement option.
