A single dental implant is designed to replace one missing tooth. An implant supports an abutment and crown without relying on neighboring teeth for support.

The listed package includes implant placement, an abutment and implant crown, and a surgical guide. Payment is required upfront. A clinical evaluation confirms whether the package fits your needs and determines your final treatment plan and cost.

No. Grafting required for implant placement is not included in the listed package. An exam and imaging determine whether grafting or another supporting procedure is recommended.

A candidate generally needs healthy gum tissue, enough jawbone to support an implant or the ability to receive a graft, and medical health that permits normal healing. Tobacco use and some medical conditions can affect healing, so an evaluation is required.

Timing varies with healing, grafting, and the recommended restoration. Implant treatment often spans several months, although selected steps may sometimes be combined. Your dentist will explain the expected sequence after your exam and imaging.

Dental implants are intended as a long-term option. With healthy surrounding tissue, good home care, and professional maintenance, the implant fixture may last for decades. The crown can wear and may need repair or replacement. Individual results vary.
