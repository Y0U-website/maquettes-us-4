Common All-on-X questions.

All-on-X uses four to six dental implants to support a fixed full-arch restoration. It may be an option for people who are missing most or all teeth in an upper or lower arch, have several teeth that cannot be saved, or want a more stable alternative to removable dentures.

The per-arch package includes extractions, grafting, placement of four to six implants, a temporary fixed interim denture, and a final fixed monolithic zirconia hybrid denture. Your evaluation confirms the treatment plan and final fee.

Yes. Each additional implant beyond the included four to six implants is $250. Pterygoid implants are $500 each when clinically required. Other needs identified during your evaluation may also affect your final treatment plan and fee.

A temporary fixed interim denture is included. Whether and when it can be placed depends on your health, available bone, implant stability, and treatment plan. Your dentist will explain the expected sequence before treatment.

Promotional no-interest plans may be available to qualified applicants through third-party financing providers. Terms vary, minimum payments are required, and all applications are subject to credit approval.
