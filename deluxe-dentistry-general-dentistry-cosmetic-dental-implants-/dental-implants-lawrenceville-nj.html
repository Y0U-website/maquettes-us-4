Dental Implants and Gum Disease

Active gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, must be treated and stabilized before dental implants can be placed. Placing an implant into infected gum tissue dramatically increases the risk of implant failure. Dr. Godko treats gum disease in-house at Deluxe Dentistry in Lawrenceville deep cleanings, scaling and root planing, and antimicrobial therapy and monitors your healing before proceeding with implant placement. Once the gum disease is under control, dental implant success rates return to normal levels.

Dental Implants and Smoking

Smoking significantly increases the risk of dental implant failure because nicotine constricts blood vessels, reduces blood flow to the surgical site, and impairs healing. Studies show that smokers have implant failure rates two to three times higher than non-smokers. Dr. Godko strongly encourages patients to quit smoking before dental implant surgery and will discuss this honestly during your consultation. Patients who cannot quit entirely are advised to stop smoking for at least two weeks before and eight weeks after surgery to give the implants the best chance of successful integration.
