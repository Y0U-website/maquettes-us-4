Invisalign Clear Braces

Adults and older teens should consider Invisalign as a possible alternative to traditional braces. See-through aligners can make mild to moderate orthodontic corrections without the need for brackets and wires. Invisalign can be used to shift the teeth into their ideal alignment in about 12 to 18 months. During that time, you can smile with complete confidence knowing that your aligners are virtually invisible to anyone that does not already know that they are there.

Benefits of Invisalign

Wright Family Dentistry offers Invisalign clear braces at our Weatherford dental office, and they’re a great choice for adults who would rather not wear traditional metal braces. What are the benefits?

  • The aligners are clear, nearly invisible, and easy to clean
  • Teeth are gradually repositioned without clunky appliances
  • No uncomfortable, time consuming monthly adjustments
  • Avoid the tooth discoloration and spots associated with bonded brackets
  • No sores associated with metal in your mouth
  • Aligners are removable for eating, brushing, flossing, and for special occasions
  • In most cases, treatment takes less time than with traditional braces

How Does Invisalign Work?

Invisalign uses a 3D computer imaging system to custom design a comprehensive treatment plan, and you’ll even get to see the final position of your teeth. A lab will create a series of customized aligners based on your individual treatment plan. You’ll wear each aligner for two weeks, and Dr. Wright will only need to see you every six weeks to be sure your treatment is on track.

If you’d like to find out if Invisalign is right for you, call our office for a consultation. Dr. Wright will examine your teeth and discuss your options. We can also make other recommendations for cosmetic dentistry treatments to complete your smile makeover.
