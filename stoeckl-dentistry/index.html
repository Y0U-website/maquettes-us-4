The practice

Are you a new patient? Come on in and visit our office! We’re located right off of HWY 16, next to the gas station and in the Oconomowoc Lake Plaza. Enter through the glass atrium and take the elevator or stairs up to the second floor. We are always welcoming new patients. We’ll get your Netflix channel ready, too!

Our team offers a wide range of treatment options in all aspects of general dentistry, including cavity fillings, dentures and dental implants. No matter what your dental needs may be, we’ll get you back to optimal dental health.

Even a small change in your smile can have a large impact on your confidence in personal, professional and other social situations. At Stoeckl Dentistry, we can walk you through your options with cosmetic dentistry.

Everything we do at Stoeckl Dentistry comes down to how we can best serve our patients. By incorporating the best technology into our practice, such as intra-oral cameras, we’re able to provide the best service.

We’ll give you the tools you need to make the best choices about your dental health.
