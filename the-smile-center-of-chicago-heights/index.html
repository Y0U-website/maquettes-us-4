The practice

The Smile Center is a trusted dental clinic in Chicago Heights, Illinois. Composed of skilled and experienced dental professionals, we seek to improve oral health within our community. We provide topnotch services that guarantee high-quality outcomes of comfort, wellness, and confidence for our patients.

No matter what your dental needs are, we can provide you with the solutions that you deserve. With our experience under our belt, there is no problem that is too small or too big for us. Let us help you experience a more confident life with the aid of our team. Rest assured that you will leave our clinic with a brighter smile and a healthier set of pearly whites!

Dr. Dilan Rutland is passionate about helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through compassionate and personalized dental care. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry after completing her Bachelor of Arts in Cellular & Molecular Biology at Barnard College of Columbia University.

Throughout her dental education and professional training, Dr. Rutland has been actively involved in community outreach, oral health education, and volunteer dentistry initiatives serving underserved populations.
