Your Dentist

Dr. Steven Lesmeister, Dentist

After an associateship in Wisconsin, I began practicing in McIntosh, Minnesota in 2010. Since that time, my team and I have provided quality dentistry for our patients in that location and from our second location in Red Lake Falls, which we opened in 2016. In 2018 we relocated to Crookston. We are excited to bring our services to Crookston and its surrounding area.

I believe that continuous learning is essential to providing the best, most current dentistry possible. For that reason, I take a significant amount of continuing education. While I enjoy most aspects of dentistry, my favorite part is getting to know the patients and working with my team. For me, going to work is fun.

Your Administrative team

Joanna, Office Manager

Numbers are my world! I love the financial aspect of the business but I also love being able to help patients understand their insurance. Call and ask for me if you are looking at switching insurance and just want to pick my brain. I always love those calls.

I have shifted to working from home most days but that allows me a lot more patient interaction time on the phone. It has been a welcomed switch. I love my monthly visits to the office when I see a patient who has been with us for years. 2025 will mark 20 years for me at the dental office. I’m looking forward to that day. We joke it was a packaged deal when Dr. Steve bought the practice, he got me along with the deal. I was here to help him with the transition. I’ve been proud to see the growth of the practice over the years.

In my spare time, I enjoy family time above all. Especially playing games or on the lake; boating or fishing. I also enjoy creative arts like needlework, crocheting and knitting as well as scrapbooking.

Ashleigh, Patient Treatment Coordinator/Hygienist
