Our Cosmetic Procedures Include:

Porcelain Crowns

Commonly referred to as caps, are artificial teeth made from porcelain and metal that fit over existing teeth. The advantage of using porcelain crowns is they can be near identical in color to the other teeth in your mouth.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain Veneers are like false fingernails and generally require little or no tooth reduction. They are the same size and thickness as your own teeth and the color, size, and shape can be changed if so desired. When applied over the surface of a tooth, they can cover worn tooth enamel, uneven tooth alignment or spacing and chips or cracks.

Invisalign™ Clear Aligners

Invisalign™ will straighten your teeth using a series of clear, removable teeth aligners that are custom made for you. These teeth aligners are virtually undetectable, so people won’t know that you’re actually straightening your teeth.

Composite Fillings

Composite white tooth fillings are made from durable plastics called composite resins. These fillings are less noticeable and much more attractive than silver amalgam fillings and are similar in color and texture to natural teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Our teeth whitening services provided by Dr. Azimi include both in-home and in-office treatment. Whichever you prefer, he will make sure you achieve a whiter and brighter smile.

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding is tooth-colored composite resin filling material used to restore small areas of decay in posterior (back) teeth, as well as to conceal minor flaws, such as chipped edges, gaps or worn areas in front teeth.
