Meet Our Newnan Dentist

Dr. Ria Sahara, DDS

Dr. Ria Sahara is a compassionate and highly respected board-certified dentist in Newnan, GA, known for her gentle approach and dedication to making dental care a positive experience for patients of all ages. Originally from Chicago, IL, and raised in Holland, MI, Dr. Sahara brings a unique blend of empathy, artistry, and clinical expertise to her practice. She is the owner and lead clinician at Gentle Dentistry of Newnan, PC, where she focuses on helping her patients achieve healthier, brighter smiles in a relaxed and caring environment.

With her welcoming demeanor and a belief that "confidence starts with a healthy smile," Dr. Sahara continues to make a lasting impact - helping patients feel empowered in their oral health while nurturing the development of future leaders.
