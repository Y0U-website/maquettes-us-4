About Gentle Dentistry of Newnan, PC

Dr. Ria Sahara is known for her gentle touch, her ability to ease dental anxiety, and her commitment to creating a calm and welcoming experience for every individual and family who walks through our doors.

In addition to providing dental services in Newnan, GA, we proudly serve patients from Peachtree City, Union City, Atlanta, and surrounding Coweta County communities.

More Than Just Gentle Care

Providing Gentle Dentistry In Newnan, GA

We believe excellent dentistry begins with listening. Before we recommend any treatment, we take time to learn about your goals, concerns, and comfort preferences. Our focus is on preventive care, patient education, and thoughtful treatment planning that supports long-lasting oral health.

We want every visit to feel unhurried and reassuring. Our team strives to build trust one conversation at a time, creating a foundation of comfort that extends through each appointment and every stage of your dental care.

Meet Dr. Ria Sahara

Dr. Ria Sahara, DDS is dedicated to providing high-quality dental care with a gentle, patient-focused approach. She understands that many patients experience anxiety or hesitation when visiting the dentist, and she is committed to delivering care that feels calm, supportive, and encouraging.

Her clinical expertise is matched by her ability to connect with patients on a personal level. Dr. Sahara values clear communication and takes the time to explain treatment options in simple, easy-to-understand terms. Her goal is not only to care for your smile but to ensure you feel confident and comfortable throughout your experience.
