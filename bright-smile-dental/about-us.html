Dr. Victor Popandopoulo Dr. Victor Popandopoulo (Dr. Pop) attended University at Buffalo where he graduated with high honors with a degree in biology. He continued his education in University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine where he minored in oral surgery and received his degree as a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS). In dental school, he was a part of various dental outreach programs such as dental treatment for veterans and children.

Dr. Pop continued his education/practice into residency and attended Kings County Hospital Center in Brooklyn followed by extra surgical training in Woodhull hospital.

Dr. Pop practices a wide scope of dentistry including: restorative, endodontics, oral surgery, and fixed/removable prosthodontics.
