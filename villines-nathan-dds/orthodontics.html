Why Choose Us

Invisalign Aligners Invisalign is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that straightens your teeth without the need for traditional metal braces. These clear, removable aligners are custom-made for your teeth, providing a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a straighter smile. Our team at Weatherford Dental Care is certified in Invisalign treatment, ensuring you receive the highest quality care throughout your orthodontic journey.

Mouth Guards Dentist Protect your smile with our custom-fitted mouth guards. Whether you’re an athlete or suffer from teeth grinding, our mouth guards offer superior protection and comfort. We take precise impressions of your teeth to create a guard that fits perfectly, reducing the risk of dental injuries and alleviating the effects of nighttime teeth grinding. Our experienced team will guide you through the process, ensuring you receive the right mouth guard for your specific needs.
