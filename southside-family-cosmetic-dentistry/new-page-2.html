0 Get to know the Dentist Dr. Jeffrey Taylor was raised in Arab, Alabama and graduated from Arab High School. He graduated from the University of Alabama with a Bachelors degree in science and later received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry. Dr. Taylor joined Southside Family Dentistry in 2010. Dr. Taylor is married to Allycia Taylor and they have two precious children, Claire and Reed.
