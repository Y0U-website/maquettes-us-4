Dental Implants

Dr. Lawrence, Dr. Collie, Dr. Pelais and Dr. Moredock routinely use dental implants to replace missing teeth, and we perform most in our own office. Dental implants are a conservative, esthetic and effective method to restore one or several lost teeth. Dental implants can also be used to anchor full and partial dentures and make them more stable, comfortable and functional.

Cosmetic Dentistry & Full Mouth Rehabilitations

Looking for that ‘Hollywood’ smile? Drs. Lawrence, Collie, Pelais. and Moredock provide a number of options, suited to individual situations and needs. We perform in-office ZOOM! whitening which can provide dramatic results in about an hour. Veneers or crowns can cover unsightly or broken fillings and teeth. Missing teeth can be replaced with implants and bridges. Whatever your goal, Dr. Lawrence, Dr. Collie, Dr. Pelais, and Dr. Moredock have the expertise and the team to give you the smile you always wanted.

TMJ Therapy

Are you suffering from TMJ muscle or joint pain? Headaches or migraines? Do you clench or grind your teeth at night or during the daytime? The doctors have many ways to safely and effectively treat these issues including different types of occlusal guards and botulinum type injections.
