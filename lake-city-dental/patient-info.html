New Patient Info

Please inform our office if you have any pre-existing medical condition that may be of concern during your treatment. This includes diabetes, high blood pressure, artificial heart valves and joints, rheumatic fever, etc. Please also let us know if you are taking any medications for these conditions.

We also recommend that you complete your patient forms prior to your appointment. You can find this paperwork online on our Patient Forms page.

IMPORTANT: If you are under the age of 18, you MUST be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

We can frequently begin your treatment on the same day as your initial consultation. However, if you have a complex medical history or require more complex treatments, we will help you schedule a second appointment with our dentists.
