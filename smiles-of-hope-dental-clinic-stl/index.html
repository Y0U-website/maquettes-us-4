My Account BEL IEFS God There is only one living and true God who reveals Himself to us as Father, Son and Holy Spirit; each with distinct personal attributes. God is the creator of the universe and ruler over everything. (1 Timothy 2:5, 1 Corinthians 8:6, Genesis1:1)

Jesus is the Son of God. He lived a sinless life and made the ultimate sacrifice by dying on the cross for our sins. He arose from the dead three days later which demonstrated His power over death. After His resurection, He ascended to His throne

in heaven to be with God the Father and will return again to take His people home with Him. (Matthew 1:23, Isaiah 9:6, John 3:16, 1 John 5:20)

The Holy Spirit is given upon salvation to live within believers in order to empower them to live a life honoring Christ. This Holy Spirit is the same power that raised Jesus from the dead and provides strength and gifts for believers to carry out the work of God. He also is present in the world to draw people to Jesus. (Romans 8:2-6, Acts 2:1-5, 2 Corinthians 3:16-18)

People were created by God for fellowship with Him. When sin entered the world, we were separated from Him and can only be reunited through Salvation. God loves everyone so much and is consistently working to draw us back to Him. (Romans 3:23,

Salvation is a free gift from God that we must choose to accept. Salvation is not achieved by anything we can do; rather, by the grace of God through our faith in Him. When we profess we are sinners, ask God to forgive us and proclaim Him to be our Lord and Savior, we are saved from our sins. Our salvation is eternal and cannot be lost. (John 3:16, Ephesians 2:8-9, Romans 10:9-10)
