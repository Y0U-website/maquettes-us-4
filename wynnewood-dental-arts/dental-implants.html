Dental Implants Restore Your Smile and Confidence

Dental implants are a life-changing solution for replacing missing teeth, allowing you to chew, speak, and smile with confidence. An implant is a titanium artificial tooth root placed in the jawbone, providing stable support for crowns, bridges, or dentures. At Wynnewood Dental Arts, Dr. Thomas DeFinnis works closely with each patient to create a personalized treatment plan, ensuring a comfortable and effective process.

We offer a variety of implant options to meet different needs. Conventional implants involve placing the root and attaching a crown after healing, while immediate load implants allow temporary teeth in a single appointment. Mini dental implants support dentures for patients seeking less invasive solutions, and restore an entire arch efficiently for those with complete tooth loss.

Types of Dental Bridges

There are four different types of dental bridges. Our dentist will evaluate your needs to determine the best kind of bridge for you.

Traditional or conventional bridges are by far the most popular type of dental bridge. A traditional bridge consists of one or several pontic or replacement teeth supported by abutment teeth on either side of the gap. Conventional bridges are ideal if the patient still has some natural teeth remaining after tooth loss.

A cantilever bridge is slightly different from a conventional bridge. Instead of two teeth, a cantilever bridge is held in place by a single tooth adjacent to the gap.

If you’re missing your front teeth, our dentist may suggest a Maryland-bonded bridge. Like traditional bridges, Maryland-bonded bridges are supported by two teeth. However, instead of dental crowns, Maryland-bonded bridges rely on a framework of metal or porcelain wings for support.

As the name suggests, implant-supported bridges are bridges that are held in place by implants. These are ideal if the patient has lost the natural teeth needed to support a dental bridge.

Dental bridges typically require two dental visits, except in the case of implant-supported bridges, in which case more visits may be necessary. During your first visit, Dr. Thomas DeFinnis obtains all relevant information regarding your dental health and creates a personalized treatment plan. The first visit also involves abutment teeth preparation, during which the dentist reshapes your adjacent teeth and takes your impressions.

On your second visit, Dr. Thomas DeFinnis cements the bridge in place, thus completing your smile transformation.

Partials and Full Dentures

Patients who have lost their teeth and are looking for non-invasive and removable teeth replacement options can benefit from partials and full dentures in Wynnewood, PA. Dentures consist of pontic teeth that are supported by a plastic, gum-colored base. A denture is held in place via a combination of dental adhesive and suction.

Partial dentures replace teeth after partial tooth loss, while complete or full dentures are best-suited for individuals who have undergone total tooth loss or edentulism.

Wynnewood Dental Arts is a trusted provider of custom teeth replacement options, including partials and full dentures near you. If you’ve recently experienced tooth loss and are looking to renew your smile, call Wynnewood Dental Arts today to book a visit with our dentist.

Dr. Thomas DeFinnis starts by examining your dental health to understand your needs. For this process, our dentist often relies on dental x-rays to gather relevant information regarding the health of your gums, teeth, and jawbone. After a comprehensive consultation, our dentist creates a customized treatment plan for you.

To make partial or complete dentures, our dentist takes your dental impressions. These impressions inform the creation of custom, well-fitting dentures.

During your follow-up appointment, Dr. Thomas Definnis checks to see if the dentures are a good fit. Any necessary adjustments are made, and afterward, you’re free to take your new teeth home.

Thanks to advancements in dental technology, patients can choose to combine implants and dentures. Implant-supported dentures have gained a lot of popularity since they offer excellent stability and are a great compromise for patients who want a permanent solution but aren’t too keen on lengthy treatment times.
