What is an iTero Dental Scanner?

The iTero digital scanning system captures detailed 3D images of your teeth and gums. Our dentist at Wynnewood Dental Arts uses the scans generated by the iTero scanner to create highly accurate digital models of your dental anatomy. These models can be used for various purposes, such as orthodontic treatment planning, dental restorations, and implant placement.

The iTero Element Scanner is a non-invasive, radiation-free alternative to traditional dental impressions and provides superior accuracy, speed, and comfort. It uses infrared light and a small camera to capture highly accurate 3D images of your mouth.

Who Is An Ideal Candidate For The iTero Digital Scanner?

An ideal candidate for an iTero scanning system would be anyone undergoing orthodontic treatment, dental restorations, or implant placement. The iTero system can benefit patients looking for a non-invasive, fast, and comfortable alternative to traditional dental impressions.

We use the iTero digital intraoral scanner for patients of all ages, including children at Wynnewood Dental Arts. Patients with a strong gag reflex, difficulty holding their mouth open for a prolonged period, or difficulty biting down on traditional impression materials also find Intraoral scanners like the iTero to be a good option.
