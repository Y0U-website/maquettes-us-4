Sarah Bettag

Emergency Dentist – Weekend Dental Emergency Care

Dr. Sarah Bettag, DDS is a Salt Lake City transplant originally from Seattle, Washington. She attended the University of Michigan School of Dentistry for her doctorate and completed her General Practice Residency at the Detroit John D. Dingell VA Hospital where she received advanced training in dental implants, oral surgery, and cosmetic dentistry.

She has completed many hours of continuing education in cosmetic dentistry, Botox® training for clenching/grinding, Invisalign® treatment, digital dentistry, and facially generated treatment planning. She is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), the American Academy of Facial Esthetics (AAFE), and the SPEAR Study Club.

Her passion for transforming smiles and creating confidence in others is what inspired her to pursue dentistry. She looks forward to developing trusting patient relationships while building healthy and beautiful teeth. In her free time, she loves skiing, hiking, cooking, and exploring Utah.

Whitney Bryant

Emergency Dentist – Weekend Dental Emergency Care - Reno, Nevada

Dr. Whitney Bryant, DDS, is a Reno dentist providing weekend emergency dental care in Reno, Nevada for patients experiencing unexpected tooth pain, dental injuries, infections, broken teeth, and other urgent dental problems. For patients searching for an emergency dentist in Reno on Saturday or Sunday, Dr. Bryant provides an option for getting professional dental care when many traditional dental offices are closed.

Dr. Bryant has deep roots in Northern Nevada. She attended the University of Nevada, Reno, where she studied neuroscience and nutritional science before pursuing her dental education at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from UCSF in 2018 and later returned to the University of Nevada, Reno, where she earned her Master of Business Administration (MBA).

Her experience in dentistry began even before dental school. Dr. Bryant became a Certified Dental Assistant and gained firsthand experience caring for dental patients before becoming a dentist herself. Today, she brings that broad background to patients throughout the Reno area, combining clinical experience with a practical, patient-centered approach to dental care.

As a weekend emergency dentist in Reno, Dr. Bryant understands that dental emergencies rarely happen at convenient times. Severe toothaches, cracked or broken teeth, lost fillings or crowns, dental infections, swelling, and other urgent concerns can develop suddenly—and waiting until Monday isn’t always the best option.

Dr. Bryant focuses on identifying the cause of the problem, helping patients get out of pain, and explaining appropriate treatment options clearly. Her goal is to make an unexpected dental emergency as straightforward and comfortable as possible while helping patients protect their long-term oral health.

Beyond treating patients, Dr. Bryant is actively involved in Northern Nevada’s dental community and serves as President of the Northern Nevada Dental Society. Her professional involvement reflects her longstanding connection to dentistry and the Reno community.

Joe Maio

Emergency Dentist – Weekend Dental Emergency Care

When a dental emergency strikes, you want a dentist who stays calm under pressure and knows exactly what to do. That’s where Dr. Joe Maio comes in.

With more than 20 years of experience, Dr. Maio has become one of Utah’s most trusted names in emergency and restorative dental care. He specializes in weekend and urgent dental treatment, providing fast, effective, and compassionate care when patients need it most.

A graduate of Creighton University School of Dentistry, Dr. Maio went on to serve eight years in the U.S. Air Force, where he completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency. His military experience honed his precision, leadership, and ability to perform calmly under pressure—skills that continue to define his approach to patient care today.

After his service, Dr. Maio founded and led one of Utah’s fastest-growing dental groups, expanding from a single office to eight locations with more than 100 employees. His leadership earned multiple recognitions, including Utah Business Magazine’s 40 Under 40, Best Companies to Work For, and Best of State awards.

Known for his steady hands, calm demeanor, and genuine care, Dr. Maio is the dentist other doctors trust when a case needs immediate, expert attention.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Maio is a proud husband and father. He spends his weekends supporting his son’s journey in professional bull riding and cheering on his daughter’s high school dance performances. When he’s not in the clinic, you’ll find him exploring Utah’s backcountry, studying human performance, or mentoring young dentists entering the field of emergency care

Brady Thompson

Emergency Dentist – Weekend Dental Emergency Care

Habla español

Dr. Brady Thompson’s favorite part of being a dentist is helping patients—especially those experiencing pain or facing a dental emergency. His passion for emergency dentistry began early in his career while serving as a general dentist for the United States Army at Fort Leonard Wood.

Dr. Thompson earned his Bachelor’s degree from Utah State University before receiving his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Virginia Commonwealth University.

During his military service, Dr. Thompson treated new Army recruits with dental emergencies, helping them return to duty as quickly as possible. Working closely with Oral Surgeons and Endodontists, he developed extensive experience in extractions, including wisdom tooth removal, root canal therapy, dental trauma management, and complex restorative procedures. He also regularly served as the on-call dentist for the Army hospital’s emergency department, providing urgent dental care for soldiers and their families.

After completing his active-duty military service, Dr. Thompson returned to his home state of Utah with his family, where he continues to enjoy practicing all aspects of general dentistry. He and his wife, Kennedy, are the proud parents of two young sons, Odell and Owen.

Outside the office, Dr. Thompson enjoys spending time outdoors and takes every opportunity to ski, hike, and waterski. Patients appreciate his clear communication, gentle approach, efficient care, and consistently friendly demeanor, helping them feel comfortable and confident throughout their treatment.
