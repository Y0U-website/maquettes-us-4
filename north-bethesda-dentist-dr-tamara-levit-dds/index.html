The practice

Tamara Levit, DDS, brings over 15 years of experience in general, cosmetic, and emergency dentistry to her self-titled practice, Tamara Levit, DDS, located in North Bethesda, Maryland, serving patients throughout Montgomery County.

Dr. Levit began her professional education at New York University in Manhattan, where she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery. After graduation, Dr. Levit completed multiple post-graduate courses in different fields of dentistry, including urgent care and preventive dentistry.
