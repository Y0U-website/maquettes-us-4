A Modern, Natural Solution for a Missing Tooth

A missing tooth can impact more than your appearance—it can affect your bite, your comfort, and your confidence. At OB1 Dental, single tooth implants offer the most advanced, long-lasting solution for tooth replacement. Unlike traditional bridges or removable options, implants replicate the feel, strength, and appearance of natural teeth. Whether you lost a tooth to decay, gum disease, or trauma, our prosthodontic expertise ensures you receive a restoration that blends seamlessly with your smile.

Why Patients Choose Single Tooth Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for tooth replacement because they do what no other treatment can: act as an artificial tooth root. This provides exceptional stability while preventing the bone loss that occurs after tooth loss. With a single tooth implant, you can chew normally, speak clearly, and enjoy your favorite foods again without concern for shifting or discomfort. Even better, implants protect the health of adjacent teeth—no grinding or crowning required.

Frequently Asked Questions About Single Tooth Implants

Considering a single tooth implant? Explore the most common questions our patients ask before getting started.

Are dental implants worth it?

If you’ve lost a tooth due to injury, decay, gum disease, or any other reason, we recommend dental implants to replace missing teeth. Dental implants come the closest to replicating the look, feel, and function of your natural teeth.

Are dental implants painful?

Dental implants are placed into the jawbone and mirror the same function as the root of a tooth. The procedure for dental implants is usually performed while a patient is sedated. Patients who undergo IV sedation must have an empty stomach and transportation home following the procedure. Most sedation patients will have little to no memory of the procedure occurring.

What are dental implants made of?

Generally, dental implants are made out of a biocompatible metal such as titanium. Biocompatible metals are also used for other common bone implants (such as shoulder, hip, and knee replacements). The visible portion of the implant is usually made out of porcelain and is custom-made to match your existing teeth.

Are dental implants permanent?

Dental implants are designed to fuse to the bone, which makes them become permanent fixtures. Typically speaking, the success rate is nearly 100 %. There are few cases in which the implant will not fuse as intended and must be removed. If this happens to occur, the procedure can be attempted again a few months later.

Are dental implants expensive?

Dental implants are not usually covered by dental insurance, but may be covered under a patient’s medical insurance. Our office and your insurance company can discuss coverage options with you based on your individual case and treatment plan.

How do I care for my dental implants?

It’s easy... just take care of an implant as if it’s a natural tooth. This involves regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. If you have any concerns about your implant, contact us immediately.
