Welcome to Our Practice

Welcome to Pilchuck Dental, the dental practice of Tyler Rumple, DDS!

The practice

We are pleased you stopped by to learn more about us and the excellent oral health care we provide to friends and neighbors in Arlington, Smokey Point, Marysville, Stanwood, and surrounding areas.

Dr. Rumple grew up in Smokey Point, and after two years as a Navy dentist aboard the aircraft carrier USS John C Stennis, he and his wife decided to return to the area to raise their family. He is proud to be able to serve our community, and every day he enjoys seeing friends and family he grew up with.

Pilchuck Dental is a small, family-owned practice, and we believe it’s the personal touches that create a great dental experience. Most of our team has been with us for decades and have built relationships with all our patients. When you arrive at our office, you will be welcomed warmly by name and checked in by our receptionist Pilchuck Dental is truly a place where everybody knows your name!

Many patients get anxious about dental visits, but here at our Arlington dental office, you’ll find a comfortable and soothing environment where we listen to and respect your concerns. Our goal is to provide you with the individualized care and attention you deserve, so we want you to tell us what you need to have the best possible experience with us.

Dr. Rumple dedicates himself to staying current with all the latest trends and technology in dentistry that improve your comfort and oral health. We are also pleased to offer sedation options if you are a patient who needs some help relaxing during treatment.

Pilchuck Dental is conveniently located on Smokey Point Boulevard in the Cumulus Park Building. We are pleased to serve Lakewood, Arlington, and Marysville School Districts as well as patients from Boeing.

We will see you promptly because we know your time is valuable, and we want you to get the most out of your appointment. We’re pleased to offer extended hours on Mondays and Thursdays, so you don’t have to take time off from work or school to get the dental care you need. If you have a dental emergency, we can usually see you the same day.

The cost of dental treatment is a concern for everyone, so we want to help you find ways to get the care you need. Dr. Rumple will always present the most conservative options first and plan your treatment to fit your budget.
