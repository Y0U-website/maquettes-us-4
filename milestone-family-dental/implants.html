Dental Bridges in Sacramento

When one or more teeth in a row are missing, a dental bridge literally bridges the gap: natural-looking replacement teeth anchored securely to the healthy teeth — or implants — on either side. At Milestone Family Dental, bridges are designed digitally and crafted to match your smile in shade and shape, restoring comfortable chewing and a confident appearance.

Traditional and Implant-Supported Bridges

A traditional bridge uses crowns on the neighboring teeth as anchors — and because we make same-day crowns in-house, the process is faster and more precise than you might expect. When the neighboring teeth are healthy and untouched, an implant-supported bridge is often the better choice: it replaces several missing teeth on just two implants, without reshaping natural teeth at all.
