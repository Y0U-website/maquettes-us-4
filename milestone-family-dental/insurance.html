FAQs

Are you in‑network with my PPO? We work with all PPOs and are in‑network with many. If we’re out‑of‑network for your plan, you can usually still use benefits here. Call us and we’ll check for you.

Can I combine the in‑house plan with insurance? No. The membership is designed for patients without dental insurance and can’t be combined with insurance.

Do you take HMO/DMO plans? We’re not a provider for HMO/DMO managed‑care plans or discount‑only programs that require assignment to a specific clinic.
