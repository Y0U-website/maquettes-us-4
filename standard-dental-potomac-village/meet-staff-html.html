Dr. Arta Moaddab

Dr. Arta Moaddab grew up in Potomac, Maryland. He recieved his bachelors degree in Biology from the University of Maryland, College Park in 2005. After graduating, he attended Georgetown University to complete his Master’s degree in Physiology. He then received his dental training at the University of Maryland college of Dental Surgery and graduated in 2012.

He continues his postdoctorate training on his free time and excels in implant prosthetics and orthodontics.

"Most practitioners do a great job fixing what has already broken down but to be exceptional you have to take it one step further, to work with patients and teach them how prevent the breakdowns before they begin."

Dr. Arta finished his training and received his masters degree in Physiology and alternative medicine at Georgetown University and believes strongly in combining oral health with a whole body approach. When clients ask, he often spends time helping improve overall health and well being which include, diet and exercise regiments as an added bonus.
