Services

Dr. Barry Webber is proud to serve as your local and trusted pediatric dentist in Papillion, NE . He’s also board-certified and offers a wide range of pediatric dental services for growing smiles. Children’s dentistry is essential in helping your son or daughter maintain a healthy smile for the rest of their life. Choose Walnut Creek Pediatric Dentistry for excellent patient care, every time.

To learn more about our options for pediatric dental care , please click on the links below. To schedule an appointment, please call (402) 590-2018 or contact us online .

Teeth Cleanings & Exams

Professional teeth cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can’t reach. This helps keep your child’s smile healthy as they grow by preventing cavities, gum disease, and early tooth loss.

Dental Sealants

Fillings

When a cavity develops, the first priority is to remove the decayed portion of the tooth to stop the spread of bacteria. After the tooth is carefully cleaned, we place tooth-colored fillings for kids that match the natural shade of their enamel.

Sports Mouth Guards

While store-bought options exist, they’re often bulky, uncomfortable, or poorly fitted, which means kids may avoid wearing them altogether. Custom sports mouth guards for kids, however, are shaped to fit your child’s exact bite, making them far more comfortable and secure.

Night Guards

A custom pediatric night guard is a small, flexible, and comfortable tray worn over the teeth during sleep. Since it’s custom-fitted, it stays securely in place and allows your child to breathe and sleep normally.

Laughing Gas

Your child inhales the pediatric laughing gas through a comfortable nose mask. Within just a few breaths, they feel calmer, lighter, and more relaxed sometimes even a little giggly, which is how laughing gas got its name.

Oral Sedation

Oral sedation is simple and easy for children to take. Before treatment begins, your child is given a prescribed liquid or pill that helps them feel sleepy, relaxed, and at ease. While they may feel drowsy, they remain conscious and responsive throughout the visit.

IV Sedation

For more complex procedures or for children who experience significant dental anxiety, IV sedation can be an excellent option to ensure a calm, safe, and successful visit. This deeper level of dental sedation helps children relax fully so they can receive the care they need.

Dental Bonding

If your child has a chipped, uneven, or discolored tooth, dental bonding offers a simple, gentle, and effective solution to restore their smile. This cosmetic treatment is especially helpful for young patients who need quick repairs without extensive dental work.

Pediatric Crowns

When a child’s tooth has a deep cavity, significant decay, or structural damage, pediatric crowns provide strong, dependable protection that helps preserve their smile. These crowns are specially designed for growing children and offer a long-lasting solution.
