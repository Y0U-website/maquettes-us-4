Our pediatric dentist in Papillion, NE , offers a full range of services to ensure your child receives the best care. At Walnut Creek Pediatric Dentistry, we provide comprehensive care for growing smiles. This includes everything from routine teeth cleanings to children’s sedation dentistry .

Gentle teeth cleanings and exams help kids feel comfortable, confident, and cavity-free. Our friendly team makes every visit positive, stress-free, and even fun. This sets the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Our in-house oral sedation helps children feel calm, relaxed, and comfortable during dental visits, making treatment stress-free for kids and reassuring for parents who want safe, compassionate care from start to finish, every single visit.

Our IV sedation options provide a deeper level of relaxation for children who need extra support. Safe, carefully monitored sedation helps ensure comfortable, anxiety-free treatment while giving parents peace of mind throughout every step of care.
