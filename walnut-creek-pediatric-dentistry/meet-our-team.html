Meet Our Team

Meet our wonderful, compassionate team at Walnut Creek Pediatric Dentistry! To schedule an appointment with our board-certified pediatric dentist in Papillion, NE , please call (402) 590-2018 or contact us online .

Stacy Office Manager

Marissa Insurance Coordinator

Marissa has been working in the dental field since 2017, bringing valuable experience and a passion for helping both patients and her team. As our insurance coordinator, she takes pride in guiding patients through the insurance process with clarity and care. Always willing to lend a hand, Marissa also enjoys supporting the clinical staff whenever needed.

Born and raised in Omaha, Nebraska, she grew up in a lively Italian family with three sisters. She’s a proud aunt to five amazing kids Karter, Gionni (her godson), Hendrix, Jokobe, and Trinity. In the summer, you’ll often find Marissa at the lake, grilling, laughing, and making lasting memories with friends and family.

Haley Receptionist

Haley was born and raised in Alliance, Nebraska. She moved to Omaha in 2016 to attend culinary school at Metropolitan’s School of Culinary Arts. She then graduated with her degree in hospitality. Haley loves to spend her free time baking for her business, Someday’s Treats. She enjoys trying new local restaurants and going line dancing on the weekends.

Milenka Delivery Director

Milenka was born and raised in Bolivia. She moved to Nebraska in 2013, and married her husband, Brian, that same year. They have one daughter. Before she moved to Nebraska, Milenka worked in a dental office and a family practice. Milenka enjoys skydiving, playing volleyball, and swimming. She also loves music and spending time with her family.

Oscar Dental Assistant

Anna Grace Dental Assistant

Anna Grace is originally from Holdrege, NE. She moved to Omaha in the summer of 2023 with her boyfriend and two dogs. Anna Grace is a caring and energetic dental assistant who loves helping kids smile big and feel brave at the dentist. She graduated from Midwest Dental Assistant school in April of 2025. No matter what health profession Anna Grace chose, she always knew it was going to be working in pediatrics.When not helping little ones learn about healthy teeth, Anna Grace enjoys hanging out with friends and family, watching sports (GBR), shopping, and golfing.

Taya Dental Assistant

Taya has worked in pediatric dentistry since 2021, starting her career with on-the-job training right after high school. She loves working with kids. She is originally from Denton, Nebraska a small town just west of Lincoln. Taya enjoys spending time with her family and friends, cheering on Nebraska football and the Kansas City Chiefs, and being a proud dog mom to her sweet pup, KoKo.

Jaclyn AKA Jackie Dental Assistant

Jackie was raised in the South Omaha and Council Bluffs areas and graduated from Dental Assisting school at Iowa Western Community College in 2022. She loves traveling to Mexico to visit family and enjoys spending time outdoors, especially on the farm surrounded by her favorite animals, including cows and horses. She also loves spending time with her family in Nebraska at gatherings and special occasions.

Jackie is a proud mom to her son, Oziel, and enjoys creating memories with him. She is also a loving aunt to her nephew, Milan, whom she adores and loves to spoil.

Jackie has been passionate about soccer since middle school and continues to play on both women’s and coed teams.
