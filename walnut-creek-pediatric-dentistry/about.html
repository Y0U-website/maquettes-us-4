About Us

At Walnut Creek Pediatric Dentistry, we believe that every child deserves a healthy, confident smile that lasts a lifetime. Led by our pediatric dentist in Papillion, NE , Dr. Barry Webber, our practice provides compassionate, high-quality dental care for infants, children, and teens including children with special needs. Our mission is to help each child build a positive relationship with dental care through gentle techniques, education, and fun.

Meet Our Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Barry Webber is a board-certified pediatric dentist and proud Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry . His advanced training and years of experience allow him to provide exceptional care in a comforting environment. Dr. Webber understands that every child is unique and takes the time to make each visit personalized and stress-free.

Whether your child is here for their first dental checkup or a more advanced procedure, Dr. Webber’s gentle approach helps kids feel at ease while promoting healthy habits that last into adulthood.

Meet Our Team

Our dedicated dental team is passionate about creating positive dental experiences for every young patient who walks through our doors. From the front desk to the treatment rooms, each member of our staff is specially trained to work with children, ensuring visits are fun, educational, and full of encouragement. We take pride in helping kids feel comfortable, confident, and excited about caring for their smiles.

Your #1 Choice For Pediatric Dental Care

Our pediatric dental office is designed with children in mind, featuring a warm, nature-inspired environment that makes every visit engaging and enjoyable. At Walnut Creek Pediatric Dentistry, we’re more than a dental office. We’re your partner in supporting your child’s lifelong oral health.
