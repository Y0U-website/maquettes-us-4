Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We file to all dental insurance companies except Medicare and Medicaid. We are not in network with any dental insurance company; however, we will file your dental claim for you to receive your insurance benefits. Any balance after insurance compensation is the responsibility of the patient.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see and welcome children of all ages. Our doctors have seen children as young as one year of age. We treat each child as an individual patient and use our judgment on when to refer to a pediatric specialist.

At what age should my child see a dentist?

We encourage parents to bring their children as early as one but at least by three years of age. We use our professional experience as a guide to a child’s readiness and willingness in order to provide an optimal dental experience for each.
