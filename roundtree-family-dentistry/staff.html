Our Doctors

Brian M. Roundtree, DDS

Dr. Roundtree is a graduate of E.D. White Catholic High School, received a Bachelor of Science in Kinesiology from LSU, and is a 2009 graduate of LSU School of Dentistry. He worked as an associate for Dr. Robert Foret for 6 years before buying his practice in 2015. He and his wife Lindsay have two sons, Lane and Rhett, and one daughter, Laird. When he isn’t working, he enjoys traveling with his family, coaching his kids in various sports, and devoting his time to local organizations.

Robert J. Foret, DDS

Dr. Foret is a graduate of Thibodaux High School, Nicholls State University, and Loyola School of Dentistry. He spent 2 years in the U.S. Army as a dentist after graduation. He started private practice in 1973.
