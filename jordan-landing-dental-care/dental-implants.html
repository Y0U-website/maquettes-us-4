What are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a medical-grade titanium post engineered to function just like your natural tooth roots. While one end of the post is surgically implanted into the jawbone, the opposite end holds a connective abutment that then secures your artificial tooth typically a crown, bridge, or denture. Because dental implants are incredibly strong and resistant to cavities, they are one of the most popular forms of tooth replacement. In fact, over 500,000 people receive a dental implant every year. They look good, they feel good, and they last a very long time.

The Benefits of Tooth Replacement

Dental implants are the only tooth replacement solution that restores both the tooth root and body. This leads to several important benefits , including:

-Implants may reduce or prevent bone loss in the jaw after tooth loss -Dental implants have a 98 % success rate -Implants can last a lifetime with proper care -Dental implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth -Implants do not affect working teeth and can improve your overall health

Dental implants can replace a single missing tooth as well as multiple missing teeth. They can even anchor a permanent denture in order to improve talking, chewing, laughing, and smiling.

Three Parts to Dental Implants

When learning about a dental implant, it can help to look at them in three parts: the implant itself, the abutment, and the crown. The implant is the titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root and is placed into your gumline (right where your natural tooth roots were). The abutment is the connector piece that attaches to the implant, with the crown sitting (artificial tooth) on top of the abutment.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are known for their strength and durability. Dental implants are made from medical-grade titanium, an incredibly strong metal that is also resistant to corrosion. If placed properly and regularly cared for, dental implants should last the rest of your life. That’s what makes them such a popular option for replacing missing teeth because they last so long. No need to come back in a few years to have it replaced.

Tooth Implant Maintenance

Caring for your dental implants is simple just take care of them like your normal teeth. Brush twice a day, consistent flossing, and regular dental checkups every 6 months will keep your implants in excellent condition.

Are Dental Implants Right For Me?

One of the most common questions we get asked is Am I a good candidate for dental implants? If you can answer yes’ to any of the following questions, then titanium implants might be a good option for you:

-You are missing one or more teeth -You have sufficient amount of bone to support an implant -Your tissue and gums are healthy -You no longer want to deal with broken, hurting, or decayed teeth -Your jawbone has reached its full growth

In order for implants to be successful, you must first have healthy gums and tissues surrounding the implant site. In addition, you need ample bone into which the implant post will be placed. (If you do not have sufficient bone, there are options to augment bone).
