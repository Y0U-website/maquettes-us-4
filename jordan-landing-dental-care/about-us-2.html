About Us Get to know the dental team at Jordan Landing Dental Care!

DOCTOR

Andrew Chappell

Dr. Andrew Chappell graduated from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry in 2018. Born and raised in Salt Lake City, Dr. Chappell enjoys spending time in the outdoors, skiing, fishing, and hiking. He met his wife, Whitney, while attending undergraduate courses at BYU-Idaho. Whitney and Andrew have been married 16 years and have 2 daughters.

Dr. Chappell has attended many CE courses since finishing dental school including advanced training in cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, advanced root canals, clear aligners, and Botox & fillers. During his time in Louisville, Dr. Chappell was selected as an Outreach Scholar to provide dental care to low-income families in rural communities. His passion for helping people understand the value of their smile and his love for dentistry has become an underlying drive for him in his patient care.

DOCTOR

Andrew Erickson

Dr. Andrew Erickson was raised in Springville, UT. He attended BYU and served an LDS mission in Germany. He was accepted to dental school at Oregon Health Sciences, graduating in the class of 1995, and he has been practicing dentistry, expanding his dental and complete health knowledge, and staying up to date with the latest technology and best materials ever since. He opened this location of Jordan Landing Dental Care in West Jordan, Utah, in 2004.

Dr. Erickson is a fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry. Dr. Erickson is highly trained also in orthodontic braces and clear tray aligners treatment. Dr Erickson is very experienced to place implants and restore them. He also does crowns, bridges, root canals, oral surgery, tooth-colored fillings, wisdom teeth extraction, veneers, laser gum procedures, custom teeth whitening, TMJ treatment, and cosmetic dentistry.

In Dr. Erickson’s spare time, he enjoys spending time with his wife, three sons, and grandchild. He and his wife love to live a healthy lifestyle and enjoy taking part in many running marathons and triathlons. Dr. Erickson loves a good adventure whether by land, snow, sand, dirt, or water exploring nature, boating, snowmobiling, and off-roading.

Long-term and new patients alike love Dr. Erickson’s attention to detail and listening to their concerns. He takes the time to explain their diagnosis in detail and the steps and options for a resolution to their dental needs. Patients always compliment how gently he treats their family and how comfortable he makes them feel in the chair before and during procedures.
