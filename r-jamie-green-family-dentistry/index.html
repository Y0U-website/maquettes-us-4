The practice

Dr. Fisher grew up in Madrid, NY graduating from Madrid Waddington Central School and continued her education in Geneva, NY at Hobart & William Smith Colleges. After receiving her degree in biology she went on to the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine. Following graduation, she completed her education with a General Practice Residency at St. Francis Hospital in Hartford, CT. While…

During dental school, Dr. Fisher focused a lot of her time and effort on giving back to those who struggled to receive routine dental care. She was an active member of the school’s local and international outreach groups. She traveled to Knoxville Tennessee, Fiji, and the Dominican Republic with a team of students to provide dentistry in underserved areas.

Dr. Fisher is committed to learning new techniques and practices the most current dentistry. She is passionate about preventative dentistry and enjoys all aspects of general dentistry but particularly enjoys cosmetic cases and improving someone’s smile.

Although a native of the North Country she and her husband fell in love with Saratoga Springs 10 years ago for all that the area has to offer. She resides in Wilton, NY and enjoys spending time at home with her husband Alex and their three kids Samuel, Emily and Audrey. With her free time she enjoys cooking, golfing, and skiing.

Dr. Green is a member of the New York National Guard, as well as several local civic organizations. He enjoys practicing all phases of modern dental care, centered on developing his patient’s lifelong enjoyment of their dentition.
