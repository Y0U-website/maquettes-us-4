Get to know your dentists

Meet Dr. Vinh Trinh

Dr. Vinh Trinh is passionate about providing meaningful care that changes lives—and that calling is deeply personal. Raised in Falls Church, Virginia, Dr. Trinh grew up in a family that lacked access to dental care, which inspired him to pursue dentistry so that he could help others overcome those same barriers. That commitment has fueled over a decade of compassionate, high-quality dental care.

As a provider, Dr. Trinh is especially focused on helping patients feel at ease, getting them out of pain, and guiding them toward long-term oral health through clear, personalized care. He is particularly drawn to treatments like clear aligners, root canals, and extractions—services that either boost confidence or bring powerful relief to patients in distress.

Outside of the office, you’ll find Dr. Trinh spending time with his wife, son, and dog, traveling, or working out. He also volunteers his time, coaching local youth sports teams. He brings the same warm, steady presence into every patient interaction, making sure you feel comfortable and cared for from the moment you arrive.

Get to know your dentists

Meet Dr. Jason Lam

Like his cousin and co-founder Dr. Trinh, Dr. Jason Lam shares a heartfelt mission to make dental care accessible and welcoming for families in his community. He too grew up in Falls Church, Virginia as the child of immigrant parents and has always been driven by a strong work ethic and the desire to help others live healthier, happier lives.

His approach is personal, preventative, and comfort-driven—striving not only to treat dental issues but to form lasting relationships with patients. He enjoys creating smiles through cosmetic dentistry and clear aligner therapy, as well as restoring comfort and wellness with procedures like root canals and extractions.

Outside the office, Dr. Lam enjoys traveling, playing sports, and spending time with his wife, three kids, and their dog. His care is a reflection of who he is—approachable, caring, and committed to making a difference in his community.

Your first visit is just the beginning of something great

When you arrive at Three Creek Dentistry, you can expect a warm welcome, a thoughtfully designed space, and care that truly centers you. Your first visit is all about getting to know you and setting you up for long-term oral health—without pressure, judgment, or confusion.

Patient Experience You’ll be greeted with a smile and guided through your appointment by a compassionate team that listens to your needs and explains your options clearly. We use modern diagnostic tools and take our time to ensure your comfort throughout every step of the visit.

What to Bring To help us serve you better, please bring the following items to your first appointment:

  • Photo ID
  • Insurance card (if applicable)
  • Completed digital forms (or arrive early to complete them in-office)
  • List of current medications

Enjoy care with comfort, precision, and modern technology

We’ve designed our office with your convenience and comfort in mind. Our state-of-the-art technology helps us provide efficient, accurate diagnoses and effective treatment in a relaxing, family-friendly environment.

Trios Digital Scanner

Our Trios Digital Intraoral Scanner makes getting impressions quick, easy, and mess-free. It helps us gather highly detailed 3D images for more precise planning and better outcomes.

Microscope

Using a dental microscope allows us to see the smallest details—bringing greater clarity and accuracy to procedures like root canals and ensuring a higher standard of care.

PAN, CEPH, CBCT Imaging

Advanced imaging capabilities like panoramic and 3D X-rays help us detect concerns early and plan treatment with a full picture of your oral health—literally.

Modern comforts

From cozy blankets and TVs in every operatory to a kid’s play area and mini arcade game, we’ve created a space that feels less like an office and more like home for patients of all ages.
