DO IMPLANT-SUPPORTED DENTURES COME OUT?

Unlike traditional dentures, implant-supported ones snap securely in place over the dental implants, providing you with a set of teeth that don’t wobble or fall out easily. They are removable for cleaning and maintenance but stay firmly anchored when in use. This minimizes the risk of embarrassment and discomfort, providing you with peace of mind.

ARE IMPLANT-SUPPORTED DENTURES EFFECTIVE? Yes, implant-supported dentures are exceptionally effective in restoring both form and function to your mouth. Because the dental implants are placed deep into the jawbone, they provide robust support and longevity that adhesive gels and strips just can’t match. Moreover, they mimic the feel and appearance of natural teeth much more closely than traditional dentures.

HOW ARE IMPLANT-SUPPORTED DENTURES PLACED?

After sedation or anesthesia—options that will be discussed during your consultation—the first step involves making careful incisions in the gums. The dental implants are then placed deep into the jawbone and given time to heal. This healing time varies from three to six months depending on individual factors. The final step is creating an overdenture using impressions of your mouth. This overdenture snaps securely in place over the healed implants.

HOW LONG DO IMPLANT-SUPPORTED DENTURES LAST?

With proper care and regular dental check-ups, implant-supported dentures are designed to last for years, if not decades. The implants themselves are permanent fixtures that won’t require replacement over time, making them a long-lasting and cost-effective solution for your dental health needs.
