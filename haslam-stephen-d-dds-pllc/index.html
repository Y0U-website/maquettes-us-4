Dr. Stephen D. Haslam was born and raised in Bountiful, Utah . Dr. Haslam graduated Cum Laude from the University of Utah with a Bachelor of Science Degree in Medical Biology. He received his Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from the University of Oklahoma in 1987 graduating in the top 5 % of his class.

Following graduation, he attended the University of Texas at San Antonio where he completed a 2 year General Practice Residency program. While there, he received advanced training in all aspects of dental services with special emphasis in cosmetic dentistry, restorative, surgical and sedation dentistry.
