Dental Implants | River Hills Dentistry

  • Am I a good candidate for All-on-X implants? You may be a good candidate if you’re missing most or all of your teeth, have sufficient bone density (or are open to grafting), and are in generally good health.
  • What is the recovery process like? Recovery typically takes a few days to a week for initial healing, with full integration of the implants over a few months. Most patients return to daily activities within a few days.
  • How long do All-on-X implants last? With proper care, the implants themselves can last decades or even a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth may need maintenance or replacement after 10 15 years.
  • How do I care for All-on-X implants? Maintain excellent oral hygiene by brushing and flossing daily, using a water flosser, and visiting your dentist regularly for professional cleanings.
  • What is the cost of All-on-X implants? The cost typically starts at $12,995, which includes surgery, implants, and a custom full-arch restoration. Final pricing depends on individual needs.
  • Can All-on-X implants be placed on both jaws? Yes, All-on-X can be placed on the upper, lower, or both jaws, depending on your specific treatment plan and goals.
  • Will All-on-X implants affect my speech or ability to eat? After a short adjustment period, most patients report improved speech and the ability to eat a wide variety of foods comfortably and confidently.
