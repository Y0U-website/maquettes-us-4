All-on-4 Vs Traditional Implants

All-on-4 Dental implants are indeed a better option than traditional implants because these require just four implants per arch, while the traditional ones need up to 10 posts per arch. Traditional implants often demand bone grafting to replace the missing jawbone, whereas All-on-4 implants require low jawbone density, hence eliminating the need for grafting. This also makes the procedure non-invasive and healing quicker.
