Post-Surgery Care

Your dental implant surgery in Maryland would require a number of rest and recovery days. We would suggest you to stay at home for the first three days for paramount healing. Immediate aftermath of the surgery might bring lightheadedness and nausea, if your dentist administers anesthesia. Hence, try staying around a family member for 12-24 hours following the surgery.

Avoid pulling or probing at the surgery site to prevent sutures from tearing and prolonged healing.

Cut back on swelling by applying ice pack to the area at an interval of 15 minutes for the first day. Use a warm cloth to control swelling on following days.

Avoiding physical activity and keeping heart rate low would expedite the healing process.

Practice regular oral cleaning habits other than flossing for the first two weeks following the treatment.

What can you eat following Dental Implant Surgery?

We suggest you to stay on a soft diet for the first three months to allow the implants integration with the mouth. Any hard or crunchy food might hurt your gums or jaw and invite for another round of surgery to repair the tissues. Here’s the list of the foods you can enjoy: Vegetarian:

  • Tofu
  • Protein shakes
  • Soft ingredient soup
  • Pudding and Yogurt
  • Cooked fruits
  • Soft vegetables
  • Pancakes, waffles and soft loaf bread
  • Oat Meal
  • Mashed Potatoes
  • Broiled fish meat
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Stewed Chicken
