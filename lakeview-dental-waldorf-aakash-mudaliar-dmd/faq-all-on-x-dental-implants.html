(FAQ) All-on-X Dental Implants

A consultation and 3D CT scan will help determine if you are eligible for the procedure.

  • (FAQ) All-on-X Dental Implants - Lakeview Dental Skip to content 1. What are All-on-X dental implants? All-on-X implants are a full-arch tooth replacement solution where 4–6 dental implants support a complete set of teeth on the upper or lower jaw. They are a permanent alternative to dentures and are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. 2. Who is a good candidate for All-on-X implants? You may be a good candidate if you: Have multiple missing teeth
  • Wear dentures and want a permanent solution
  • Have failing or severely damaged teeth
  • Want fixed teeth instead of removable dentures

3. How long does the All-on-X procedure take?

In many cases, dental implants and temporary teeth can be placed on the same day. This approach is often called “Teeth in a Day.”

Your final permanent teeth are usually placed after the healing period.

4. Is the procedure painful?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation , ensuring you remain comfortable during treatment.

Most patients experience mild soreness for a few days , similar to what is felt after a tooth extraction.

5. How long do All-on-X implants last?

With proper care and regular dental checkups, All-on-X implants can last 20+ years or even a lifetime.

6. How much do All-on-X implants cost?

7. Will insurance cover All-on-X implants?

Some insurance plans may cover parts of the treatment.

Our team will review your insurance benefits and discuss financing options during your consultation.

8. How long is the recovery time?

Most patients return to normal daily activities within a few days.

However, full healing of the implants with the jawbone typically takes 3–6 months before the final permanent teeth are placed.

9. How do I care for All-on-X implants?

Caring for implants is very similar to caring for natural teeth:

  • Brush twice daily
  • Floss or use a water flosser
  • Visit the dentist for regular cleanings and checkups
