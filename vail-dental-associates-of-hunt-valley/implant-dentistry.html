What Are Dental Implants?

If you are missing teeth or dealing with ill-fitting, uncomfortable dentures, dental implants represent the state-of-the-art solution for replacing any number of teeth. These small titanium cylinders are surgically placed into the jawbone by a specialist in a simple outpatient procedure.

Once placed, dental implants serve as a durable, long-lasting foundation for fixed, non-removable tooth replacement, allowing you to both chew with ease and smile with complete confidence again. Since we handle both the placement and restoration of your implants, you can receive comprehensive treatment right here in one convenient location.

Dental Implants in Hunt Valley, Maryland

Dental implants are the most advanced solution for tooth loss, serving as artificial roots onto which special attachments secure permanent restorations like single crowns or fixed bridges. This lifelike option effectively restores oral function, much like having your own natural teeth back, allowing you to eat all your favorite foods and enjoy improved speech and confidence. Furthermore, implants protect against bone loss, offering a durable, long-lasting solution whether you are missing one tooth or many.

Implants are also highly beneficial for securing removable full or partial dentures, providing excellent support, retention, and stability that addresses many of the common drawbacks of traditional devices. Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants offer a permanent, stable solution to tooth loss. Reasons individuals should consider dental implants include the following:

Replacing teeth without impacting healthy adjacent teeth.

Preserving jawbone density to prevent facial collapse.

Restoring your ability to chew, speak, and digest food properly.

Securing dentures or bridges for better comfort.

Resolving TMJ pain and bite problems caused by tooth shifting.

Enhancing and restoring your beautiful, natural smile.

The Advantages of Dental Implants

Dental implants stimulate the growth of bone around the implant site through a process known as osseointegration. This helps to combat additional oral health challenges that can result from untreated tooth loss such as shifting or migrating teeth, development of gum (periodontal) infections, loss of jawbone structure, and even loss of additional teeth.

Implant procedures are astoundingly effective, with up to 98 % of the half million implants placed annually in the U.S. having long term success. Currently, dental implant supported crowns, bridges and dentures are the most sophisticated and enduring tooth replacement treatment options available. This level of success in dental implants often depends on the expertise of the provider, highlighting the importance of choosing talented dentists like Dr. Vail for such procedures.
