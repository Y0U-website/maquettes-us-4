Meet Our Doctor,

Shannon Vail, DDS, MS

Upon graduation, Dr. Vail completed her residency year at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center, and continued her service in Norfolk, VA. During this time, Dr. Vail was able to further develop and refine her skills and capabilities while providing extensive dental care to a pool of over 30,000 patients. During her final year of military service, Dr. Vail served as the solo dentist on one of the Navy’s large amphibious ships, providing quality comprehensive care to over 1,200 sailors.

Dr. Vail met her husband, RT, while at the University in Maryland and the couple married in the fall of 2017. After relocating and reviving Dr. Vail’s childhood home in Timonium in the summer of 2023, they now live there with their daughters Burke and Emery and their son Callan ( CJ ). The busy family of five enjoys spending their time playing outside, going for walks around the neighborhood and attending local events and classes on the weekends.

Meet Our Dedicated Team

At Vail Dental Associates of Hunt Valley, we aim to be your comprehensive, one-stop dental home, ensuring every member of your family, from young children to seniors, receives personalized care. We set ourselves apart by making patients feel like family. You will never experience pushy sales tactics or feel rushed into treatment decisions. Instead, you’ll find a comfort-first, education-focused approach where we take the time to listen, explain our findings, and ensure you feel understood and empowered to make informed decisions about your oral health goals.

We prioritize your well-being through a range of thoughtful amenities, including pillows, blankets, stress balls, and headphones, and offer the option of anxiety-relief medication for nervous patients. Our commitment to comfort is matched by our dedication to excellence, utilizing comprehensive, advanced dental technology for precise, efficient care and optimal treatment outcomes.

We believe that dentistry should be a positive experience. Our team provides honest, no-pressure treatment in a warm and welcoming environment, dedicated to making every visit comfortable and building your confidence. By focusing on patient-centered care and utilizing modern equipment, we are committed to providing you with the highest standard of dental care under one roof.
