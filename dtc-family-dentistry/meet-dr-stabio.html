Robert Stabio, DDS

Dr. Stabio was born and raised in Montana. He completed his undergraduate education at the University of Colorado Denver, where he graduated Magna Cum Laude with honors in Biology. Dr. Stabio then attended the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine at the Anschutz Medical Campus and was a recipient of the Chancellor’s Scholarship for Academic Excellence throughout his four-year program.

Before becoming a dentist, Dr. Stabio served in the US Navy as a nuclear power plant supervisor on a submarine. He enjoys applying the experience and engineering knowledge he gained in this highly specialized field to care for his patients. The precision, orderliness, and sharp problem-solving skills gained during his time in nuclear operations have proven to be a tremendous asset to the practice of dentistry. Ask him to tell you an interesting sea-story the next time you see him!

Dr. Stabio enjoys being able to offer a unique service to help people achieve optimal health, function, and beauty of the teeth and mouth. He aims to solve problems, help people overcome fear, and build trusting relationships with his patients. Behind every smile is a human, and Dr. Stabio seeks to recognize and understand the unique qualities of each person he treats. He is also a passionate life-long learner committed to advancing his skills and training in order to stay up to date with the most cutting-edge techniques and advances in dental science.

Outside the office, Dr. Stabio values the time he spends with his wife and two children enjoying the outdoors and going on family adventures.

Dr. Stabio and the staff at DTC Family Dentistry are committed to serving you with integrity, excellence, and compassion and will make your appointment as comfortable and fun as possible!

Professional affiliations: American Dental Association, Colorado Dental Association, Metro Denver Dental Society, Academy of General Dentistry
