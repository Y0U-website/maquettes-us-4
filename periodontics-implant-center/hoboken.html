Dr. Andrawis graduated in 2015 with a doctorate in Dental Medicine degree. With a special interest in soft tissue augmentation and oral plastic surgery, Dr. Andrawis pursued a career in periodontics at the Ashman Department of Periodontology and Implant Dentistry at New York University. During his residency, he presented numerous times at intercollegiate conferences about innovative techniques in dentoalveolar trauma, digital dentistry, and immediate implant placement.

In 2018, Dr. Andrawis became a board-certified periodontist, boarded by the American Board of Periodontology. When he’s not doing surgery, Dr. Andrawis enjoys playing tennis, traveling with friends, and flying his drone.

Dr. Yousef was born in New York, New York but moved to the small town of East Brunswick, New Jersey very early in his life. He is the oldest of three, with two younger brothers. Both of his parents were of Egyptian descent and migrated to the States to give their future children better lives.

As Dr. Yousef grew up, he excelled in his academics and was mentored by a family dentist in high school who encouraged him to pursue dentistry. With the intention of becoming a dentist, Dr. Yousef attended Rutgers University, and upon graduation, applied and was accepted to the University of Maryland School of Dentistry where he ultimately received his doctorate degree. From there, Dr. Yousef pursued his specific passion for surgery by attending New York University to complete his certification in Periodontics and Implantology.
