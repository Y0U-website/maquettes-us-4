HINGHAM ROOTS. THREE DECADES OF CARE.

Hingham Dentistry was established in 1990 by Dr. Virginia Shahinian, who set out to build a practice where excellent dentistry and genuine care for people were inseparable. More than three decades later, that same spirit guides everything we do.

Today, the practice is led by the founder&#x27;s daughter, Dr. Adrina DeVitre, alongside associate dentists and specialists in prosthodontics, endodontics, and periodontics. Patients can complete most of their treatment under one roof with a team that already knows them, their history, and their preferences.

Some patients first came here as children and now bring children of their own. New patients enter that same continuity of care.
