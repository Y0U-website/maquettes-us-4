The practice

Our office understands that your time is important, and that being close to your home or office makes dental care more convenient. Located a short walk from many Capitol Hill neighborhoods, a quick ride on the DC streetcar from Union Station, and a few blocks from Eastern Market, we are nestled in the heart of DC. Our location and hours allow you to fit a visit in before work, or on your break.

Dr. Coley earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from The University of Maryland Dental School .

Since graduation she has practiced in both private practice and public health. She enjoys providing all aspects of comprehensive care and looks forward to continuing that for patients. Dr. Coley appreciates that good oral health is important to each patient internally and externally. That appreciation was what motivated her to become a dentist, and fuels her dedication to compassionately restore…

When Dr. Coley is not chairside with patients, she spends time with her husband, 4 children, and dogs. Dr. Coley is a dedicated mother, community volunteer, and swimmer. She enjoys cheering on her children at their various activities and is a committed member of the Saint Anselm’s Parent Association. When she has free time, you can often find her swimming with her family or volunteering.
