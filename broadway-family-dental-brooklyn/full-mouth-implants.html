What are Full Mouth Dental Implants?

People who can benefit from full mouth dental implants have lost most or all of their natural teeth. Full mouth implants offer an alternative to traditional dentures, and several options are available. Possible solutions include All-on-Four , Teeth in a Day, implant retained dentures, mini dental implants, and more traditional dental implant treatment where several implants are situated evenly throughout the jaw and support a fixed bridge of teeth.

Why Choose Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth implants replace an entire arch of missing teeth using multiple implants, acting as stable anchors for prosthetic teeth. This approach delivers a lasting solution that looks and functions like natural teeth.
