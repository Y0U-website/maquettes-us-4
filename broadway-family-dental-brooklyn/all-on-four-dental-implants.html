What Makes All-on-Four?

All-on-Four is an affordable dental implant treatment suitable for people looking to replace a complete arch of teeth. Just four dental implants are used to secure a fixed bridge of teeth, although if you wish to replace an upper set of teeth, your implant dentist at Broadway Family Dental may recommend using a greater number of implants.   • Consultation and Planning : An implantologist examines the jaw using digital scans and X-rays to plan the implant.
  • Preparation : All remaining teeth in the arch will be removed and sedation options will be discussed to ensure comfort.
  • Implant Placement: The four implants are strategically placed, two of which are angled in the posterior area to maximize contact with the bone.
  • Temporary Bridge : The temporary bridge is often fixed on the same day so that you can go home with a functional smile.
  • Healing : Over the next few months, the implants anchor into the jawbone through osseointegration.
  • Final Bridge : After healing, a permanent bridge is inserted to give a natural look and feel.
