Frequently Asked Questions

How Long Does a Dental Implant Procedure Take?

Dental implant surgery takes around an hour for one implant. After the procedure, you will spend four to six months with temporary crowns. The healing time usually varies between three to six months. It depends on the complexity of the procedure and the patient’s body peculiarities. When the implant metal post is healed, the dentist will install the abutment and the crown. If you need a bone graft before the implant placement, the healing time after the bone grafting also takes three to six months.

Is the Dental Implant Procedure Painful?

The dental implant process usually causes little to no pain. Before surgery, your dentist will use local anesthesia. Once the anesthetic effect disappears, you may experience discomfort easily eliminated with pain medication. Painful sensations depend on the level of pain tolerance and the complexity of the procedure. While some people require prescribed painkillers, others can easily manage pain with NSAIDs.

Because most dental insurance policies categorize dental implants as a cosmetic procedure, they usually don’t cover them.
