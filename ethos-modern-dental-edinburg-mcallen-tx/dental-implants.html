Dental Implants in Edinburg TX

With consistent care, dental implants can remain strong and functional for many years.

  • Floss Regularly – Remove plaque around the implant and gum line to prevent infection.
  • Schedule Routine Check-Ups – Regular dental visits help monitor implant health and gum condition.
  • Avoid Harmful Habits – Limit smoking and avoid chewing hard objects that can damage restorations.
  • Maintain Gum Health – Healthy gums are essential for keeping your implants stable long-term.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural Look & Feel – Designed to match your natural teeth for a seamless appearance.
  • Strong & Stable Support – Fuses with the jawbone for secure, long-term function.
  • Improved Chewing Ability – Restores bite strength so you can eat comfortably.
  • Prevents Bone Loss – Replaces the tooth root, helping maintain jawbone health.
  • Long-Lasting Solution – With proper care, implants can last for many years.
  • Enhanced Confidence – Smile, speak, and laugh without worry.

Do you offer All-on-4 or full-mouth dental implants?

Yes. We offer full-mouth dental implants, including All-on-4, in which four implants support a complete upper or lower arch of teeth. This is one of the most cost-effective full-mouth options for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, and it can typically be completed in fewer surgical visits than traditional implant approaches.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during placement. Mild soreness afterward is normal and manageable.

How Long Does It Take to Recover from Dental Implant Surgery?

Initial healing usually takes a few days, while full integration with the jawbone may take a few months.

Most patients report minimal discomfort, similar to a routine dental procedure. Any temporary soreness can be controlled with medication.

Can I Replace All My Teeth with Implants?

Yes. Full-arch implant solutions can replace an entire upper or lower set of teeth securely and permanently.

No special care is needed. Brush, floss, and attend regular dental check-ups just as you would with natural teeth.
