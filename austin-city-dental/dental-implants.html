Dental Implants in Austin TX

Missing teeth affect more than just your smile — they impact your ability to chew, speak, and feel confident. At Austin City Dental, Dr. Steve Cook and Dr. Melissa Hernandez provide dental implants in Austin TX, the gold standard for tooth replacement. Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth, offering a permanent solution that restores both health and confidence.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to act as a replacement tooth root. Once healed, a custom dental crown , bridge, or denture is attached, creating a strong, natural-looking restoration.

Dental implants are the closest option to having your real teeth back.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Permanent tooth replacement: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Natural appearance: Custom restorations blend seamlessly with your smile.
  • Improved function: Restores normal chewing and speaking ability.
  • Prevents bone loss: Stimulates the jawbone and prevents shrinking that occurs with missing teeth.
  • Protects surrounding teeth: Unlike bridges, implants don’t require support from neighboring teeth.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Even if you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone, advanced procedures like bone grafting may make implants possible.

  • Healthy gums and adequate bone structure
  • Good overall oral health
  • Commitment to oral hygiene and routine dental visits

The Dental Implant Process

Many implant cases are part of a full restorative dentistry plan designed to rebuild function and confidence.

  • Consultation & Planning: Dr. Cook or Dr. Hernandez evaluates your oral health, takes digital imaging, and creates a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing Phase: Over several months, the implant integrates with the bone through osseointegration.
  • Restoration: A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached, completing your new smile.

Dental Implants vs. Other Options

For most patients, implants are the most stable, natural-feeling option for tooth replacement.

  • Dental Implants: Permanent, prevent bone loss, look and feel like natural teeth.
  • Dental Bridges: Fixed solutions but require support from adjacent teeth.
  • Dentures: Removable and may shift, though modern dentures offer improved stability.

FAQs About Dental Implants

Are dental implants painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after placement, managed with over-the-counter medication.

How long do implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last decades — many last a lifetime.

Are implants covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by plan. Many offer partial benefits, and our office provides financing options to make treatment more affordable.

Can implants replace multiple teeth?

Yes. Implants can support single crowns, bridges, or complete dentures to restore one tooth or an entire arch.
