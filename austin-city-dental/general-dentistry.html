What Is General Dentistry?

General dentistry is the foundation of oral health. It includes the treatments and services that most patients need on a routine basis, from checkups and cleanings to fillings and crowns . Regular visits to your Austin dentist are the key to catching issues early, preventing costly dental problems, and maintaining a smile that supports your overall health.

Our General Dentistry Services

At Austin City Dental, we offer a wide range of general dentistry services tailored to your needs, including:

Oral Exams – Comprehensive dental exams allow us to evaluate your teeth, gums, and bite, while checking for cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer.

Digital X-Rays – Our safe, modern x-rays provide precise imaging to detect issues that can’t be seen with the naked eye.

Professional Teeth Cleanings – Routine cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, keeping your teeth polished and your gums healthy.

Composite Fillings – Tooth-colored fillings restore teeth affected by decay or minor damage with natural-looking results.

Porcelain Crowns – Strong, custom-made crowns protect and rebuild weakened teeth while blending seamlessly into your smile.

Dental Bridges – Bridges replace missing teeth, restoring your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Each of these services is available right here in Austin, with care personalized to your specific needs and goals.

Preventive Care: The Key to Long-Term Health

Preventive dentistry is at the heart of general dentistry. By visiting your dentist regularly for exams and cleanings, you can avoid painful and expensive problems in the future. Preventive treatments like sealants and fluoride can also strengthen teeth and reduce the risk of decay, especially in children and teens.

Research shows a strong link between oral health and overall health. Conditions like heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory illness can be connected to gum disease and poor dental hygiene. By taking care of your mouth, you’re investing in your long-term wellness.
