The practice

Dr. Lopez brings over 20 years of experience in the field of dentistry and is supported by a great team of experienced dental hygienists and dental assistants, and an amazing office manager who also ensures our patients are well taken care of!

From our peaceful lobby to our comfortable treatment rooms, you will feel at ease the moment you walk through our door. We are hyper-focused on cleanliness and proper sterilization protocols. We offer a full range of services for your whole family. You can feel confident that we will meet all your dental needs and are excited to have your family join ours!

Preventive dental care is the foundation of our practice. Our clinical plans are tailored to your unique individual needs. This approach helps keep your gums and teeth healthy and also promotes overall general health.

Your smile is one of your most important, noticeable features. It’s probably the first thing others notice about you. We provide cosmetic dentistry options to help your smile look its best.
