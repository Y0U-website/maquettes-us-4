Dental implants are small metal posts that replace your natural tooth roots. During a quick outpatient procedure, Dr. Patel places them into your empty tooth sockets. The implants integrate with your bone and provide a base for restorations, like crowns, bridges, and dentures.

Because dental implants integrate with your jaw, they’re more comfortable and longer-lasting than traditional dental restorations. They also preserve oral health and reduce the risk of common dental issues, including gum recession and bone loss.

Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth. They also require no special maintenance. Simply brush and floss them daily to keep them in good shape.
