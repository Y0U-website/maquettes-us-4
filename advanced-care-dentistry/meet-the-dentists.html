Meet The Dentists behind

Dr. Robert G. Redenz, DDS

A Rare Combination of Skills

Health, stability, and strength is the sum of its parts. Dr. Marxen is one of the few dentists qualified to handle both the biology of your gums and the artistry of your teeth . This dual expertise allows him to solve complex problems others might miss, ensuring your results are built to last a lifetime.

Dr. Marxen is a specialized dental professional with advanced training in both Prosthodontics and Periodontics. This dual expertise allows the practice to handle complex dental problems, including full-mouth reconstructions and necessary implants. The practice carefully evaluates new treatments and technologies, prioritizing innovations that enhance patient outcomes. A commitment to continuing education is pivotal for staying updated with the latest advancements, ensuring the highest standards of care and professionalism.

A Gentle Touch Born From Medical Experience

Dr. Marxen began his career as a phlebotomist, an experience that taught him even necessary procedures should be handled with a reassuring touch . We treat the whole person, not just the mouth, ensuring that every treatment improves your well-being without causing you unnecessary stress.

Safety and Precision in Every Visit

We know fear often stands in the way of health, which is why we aim to break the wall between medicine and dentistry. From advanced technology to IV sedation, your comfort and peace of mind are part of the plan , not the afterthought. Receive the care you need while feeling completely relaxed and safe.

A Higher Standard for Your Dental Care

We are one of the few practices licensed to offer IV conscious sedation, but our approach to comfort goes deeper. Because Dr. Marxen understands the delicate link between medicine and dentistry, our sedation protocols are designed for your complete physical safety and peace of mind. Whether you need extensive restoration or routine care, you can rest easy knowing you are in the hands of a team that prioritizes your comfort just as much as your results.
