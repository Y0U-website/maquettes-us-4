Cleanings, exams, fillings, sealants, and pediatric care — the kind of preventive dentistry that keeps small problems from becoming big ones.

Cleanings & exams (Prophylaxis)

The foundation of every healthy mouth: gentle scaling, polishing, fluoride as appropriate, and a thorough exam. We&#x27;ll show you what we see, not just tell you. Our hygienists take the time to walk you through your X-rays and point out anything worth watching. We believe an informed patient makes better decisions — and we&#x27;d rather catch something early than deal with a bigger problem later. Most adults benefit from a cleaning every six months, though some patients need to come in more often. Learn more about prophylaxis and what a typical visit looks like.

Pediatric dentistry

Our hygienists and dentists are patient with even the youngest visitors. We make the first few visits fun, teach kids how to brush and floss properly, and build a lifelong comfort with the dentist. Dental sealants are available for permanent molars as soon as they come in — a quick, gentle way to protect the grooves where cavities most often start. For families across Weyers Cave and the Shenandoah Valley, we recommend bringing children in for their first visit by age one or within six months of their first tooth. Visit our pediatric dentistry page for more on how we care for kids.

Periodontal maintenance

For patients with a history of gum disease, we offer periodontal maintenance on a 3- or 4-month cycle to keep things stable and prevent bone loss. Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults, but it doesn&#x27;t have to progress. With regular deep cleanings and careful monitoring, we can help you keep your natural teeth for life. Dr. Nair and our hygiene team track pocket depths, gum recession, and bone levels at every visit so nothing slips through the cracks.

Fillings & sealants

Tooth-colored composite fillings for cavities and preventive sealants for kids. Same-day when possible. We use composite resin that matches your natural tooth color — no silver fillings here. Sealants are one of the most effective and affordable ways to prevent cavities in children, and they take just a few minutes per tooth. For more details, visit our preventative care page.

Oral cancer screening

Included in every exam. Early detection saves lives, and it takes less than a minute. Dr. Nair checks the soft tissues of your mouth, tongue, throat, and neck for any signs of abnormality. Oral cancer is highly treatable when caught early, which is one more reason routine visits matter.

Why routine care saves money

A cleaning and exam costs a fraction of what a crown, root canal, or extraction does. Patients who keep up with their twice-yearly visits almost always spend less on dental care over their lifetime than those who wait until something hurts. Prevention is the most affordable dentistry there is — and it&#x27;s the kind we like doing most.
