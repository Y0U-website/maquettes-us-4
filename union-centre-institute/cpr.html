Student Success Stories

How long is the CPR class?

The course is completed in approximately 3 hours and is held entirely in person. It combines classroom instruction with hands-on practice to help participants build confidence responding to real-life emergencies.

Will I receive a CPR certification?

Yes. Participants who successfully complete the course will receive an American Heart Association CPR certification card within a few days of completing the class.

What skills will I learn during the class?

You’ll learn CPR techniques for adults, children, and infants, rescue breathing, choking response, proper chest compressions, AED (Automated External Defibrillator) use, and how to respond effectively during cardiac emergencies.

Is this course hands-on?

Absolutely. The class includes extensive hands-on practice using CPR manikins and other training equipment, allowing you to develop practical skills under the guidance of experienced American Heart Association certified instructors.

Is this course accredited?

This program is offered as continuing education and is not accredited by the Commission of the Council on Occupational Education (COE). It is designed to provide CPR certification, skill reinforcement, and up-to-date training based on current American Heart Association guidelines.
