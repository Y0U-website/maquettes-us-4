Your Career. Your Future.

Build experience in a variety of practice settings including orthodontics, oral surgery, pediatric dentistry, endodontics, periodontics, prosthodontics, and general dentistry.

With additional state-approved education and certification, dental assistants may qualify to perform expanded clinical duties where permitted.

Many dental assistants choose to continue their education through an accredited dental hygiene program to expand their clinical skills and responsibilities.

Experience as a dental assistant can provide valuable clinical exposure for individuals who later pursue dental school through an accredited university.

Student Success Stories

What’s included in the program cost?

Tuition includes all textbooks, materials, certification exam preparation, and clinical externship placement. The only additional costs are optional state licensure fees and certification exam fees.

Do I need prior healthcare experience?

No prior experience is necessary. Our comprehensive program starts with fundamentals and builds your skills progressively. Many of our most successful graduates came from completely different career backgrounds.

Can I work while completing the program?

Yes! Our hybrid schedule is designed for working adults. Online coursework is self-paced, and hands-on sessions are scheduled evenings and weekends to accommodate most work schedules.

What certifications will I earn?

You’ll be prepared for the DANB CDA (Certified Dental Assistant) exam, radiation safety certification, and CPR certification. Additional state-specific certifications vary by location.

How quickly can I start working?

Most graduates receive job offers during their externship or within 30 days of completion. Our career services team actively connects students with hiring dental practices throughout the program.
