FAQs

The dental implant is the titanium post placed in the jawbone by a specialist. The implant restoration is the custom crown that our doctors place on top of the healed implant.

The implant placement and healing process typically takes several months. Once healing is complete, the final crown is placed in a relatively simple appointment.

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the restoration phase. The surgical placement is performed by a specialist using appropriate anesthesia.

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implant restorations can last many years and often decades.

Candidates generally need healthy gums and adequate bone support. A comprehensive evaluation is required to determine if implants are appropriate for your situation.
