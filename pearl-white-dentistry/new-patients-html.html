Your First Visit

When you visit our office for the first time, we give you a warm greeting and take care of your new patient paperwork. You’ll get a good introduction to our office and team before we lead you to our exam room, where we take x-rays and then review your treatment and financial plan. When possible, we’ll also give you a cleaning on the same day. Then we’ll send you home with a sparkling smile and plans to see you soon!

We Welcome Your Insurance!

Dental insurance is a valuable investment in your dental health, and Pearl White Dentistry is pleased to work with a number of insurance plans. Our expert staff files, processes, and follows up on all insurance claims, too, to ensure that you get the most out of your plan and to take the headache out of insurance.

Need Financing? We Can Help!

For patients who do not have dental insurance, or for those who do but could use a little help breaking their bill up into multiple payments, we are happy to offer patient financing. Talk to us about your financial situation and together we will decide how to meet your needs best.
