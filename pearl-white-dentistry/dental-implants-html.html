In all, the procedure for dental implants may last from 3-6 months. Longer treatment times are to be expected when preparatory procedures, like bone grafting or sinus lift, are required to build up bone structure for successful anchoring of the dental implants.

Dental Implants vs. Dentures vs. Dental Bridges

Dental implants, dentures and bridges are three of the most common options for replacing damaged or missing teeth.

These replacements offer many oral health and overall wellbeing benefits. Whether it is improving your jaw strength, brightening your smile or simply making it easier to eat, drink and talk, it is crucial to understand the pros and cons of each option.

Dental implants are typically the most expensive option and take the longest time to produce. Dentures - removable full or partial teeth replacements - often can be made in a matter of days or weeks, even those that are made to fit the unique contours of a particular patient s mouth. Dental bridges , which replace a missing tooth and place crowns on the teeth on either side, can also be completed in weeks.

The primary benefits of dental implants are that they are the most natural looking (and feeling) type of tooth replacement, and they have a longer shelf life. Although the process for getting implants is longer than for dentures and bridges, these replacements last a lifetime. Implants allow people to eat, drink and talk confidently, without fear that a replacement tooth could pop out.

How Dental Implants Work

Dental implants are artificial teeth that are connected to titanium screws and inserted at the root where missing teeth used to be. This gives implants a tight and highly stable fit.

Implants can be used to replace a single tooth or multiple teeth at the same time.

The process begins with a consultation and examination to determine the health and strength of your gums and jawbone. X-rays may be used to help determine precisely where to place the implant.

After the implants are inserted, the bone and implant need time to fuse and heal. This allows for a natural, secure and durable fit that can be adjusted during the process.
