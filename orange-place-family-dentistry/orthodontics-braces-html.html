We make orthodontic treatment more convenient than ever by offering custom-crafted, clear orthodontic aligners right here under our roof. That means that you won’t need to wait in order to begin treatment. Once we determine that you’re a good candidate for the procedure, we’ll walk you through your customized treatment timeline and get to work on crafting your trays ourselves. Before sending you on your way, we’ll show you how to use and maintain your aligners throughout your treatment.

Invisalign

To date, Invisalign has helped more than 17 million patients! The treatment process starts with a comprehensive exam, which is when we determine if you’re a good candidate. If you are, then we will take impressions of your teeth and have a custom series of clear aligners made. Each one should be worn for 20+ hours a day, and for 7-14 days at a time. Usually, patients achieve the results they desire in just 12-18 months, but you’ll see improvements even sooner since the aligners are clear and removable!

Six Month Smiles

At first, Six Months Smiles sounds too good to be true, but it’s not! By only straightening the front six teeth (or the ones that are on display when you smile), we can give you the gorgeous results you desire in a fraction of the time. Don’t worry you won’t have to compromise aesthetics either. The brackets and wires are clear, which makes them considerably more discreet than traditional metal braces. In other words, you get the best of both worlds: virtually undetectable braces and quicker results.
