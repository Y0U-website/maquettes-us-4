What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a post, typically made of titanium, that is placed below your gumline and in your jawbone to replace the root structure of missing teeth. The posts are able to naturally fuse with the jawbone due to their biocompatible nature, allowing them to provide several unique benefits. Once this process is complete, a customized crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the implants to complete your smile.

How Dental Implants Can Save You Money

At first, you may think to get dentures or dental bridges. Their upfront cost is lower than that of dental implants. However, the latter restorations are the more cost-effective choice.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

After investing in this cutting-edge procedure, you’ll want to do everything you can to maintain your dental implants in Beachwood and keep them looking like new. Our team at Orange Place Family Dentistry is always happy to help by providing you with tips and tricks, allowing you to truly get the most out of your newly refinished smile. With proper care, dental implants have been shown to last over 25 years, so we encourage you to take the following steps to help you get there!

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

After your restorations are attached to your dental implants, it’s important that you maintain good dental hygiene to prevent infection or other oral health issues from compromising your smile. You can do this by thoroughly brushing with fluoride toothpaste twice a day for two minutes, rinsing with antimicrobial mouthwash once daily, and visiting your dentist for a checkup and cleaning every six months.

Eat a Balanced Diet

A little-known fact is that what you eat can also impact your oral health. Your dental implants will allow you to seamlessly eat a greater variety of foods, like different proteins, nuts, and more. Be sure to balance your diet by not consuming too many foods with excessive amounts of carbohydrates, as this can attract harmful oral bacteria that can result in decay and infection.

Break Bad Oral Habits

If you use tobacco products, including vaping, e-cigarettes, and nicotine pouches, it’s important to take steps to break these habits soon, as they can impact the health of your dental implants. You can gain access to helpful resources through your primary care doctor or dentist, as well as find a number of free online sources .

Protect Your Dental Implants

If you grind your teeth at night or participate on a local sports team, consider investing in a custom-tailored sportsguard or nightguard in Beachwood . With something as simple as a cushion between your teeth, you can protect your new dental implants, existing teeth, and even your jaw from unexpected trauma and wear and tear.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Every patient, whether they have dental implants or not, should schedule six-month checkups and cleanings with a dentist. Not only will these visits allow our team to spot and treat any small issues before they can compromise the health of your replacement teeth, but they will also prevent the build-up of plaque and tartar, which can lead to peri-implantitis.

Dental Implant FAQs

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants are famously one of the most durable methods of tooth replacement, with the connection to the jawbone affording them startling longevity. 95 % of dental implants are successful at 20 years, and many of them last over 30 without any issue. If you take care of them well, they may even last a lifetime!

To make yours last, brush twice a day, floss daily, and see us regularly for preventive care. We’ll be able help you keep your new teeth around for an incredibly long time!

Will I Have to Take Off Work for Dental Implant Surgery?

Dental implant placement is relatively quick and low-impact, at least as far as oral surgery goes. Most patients only need to take one or two days off at most in order to get their dental implants, and in many situations, they’re able to return to work as soon as the next day.

That said, every situation is different. If your work is especially taxing or if it requires intense manual labor, it may be a good idea to take a little bit more time off in order to ensure that you’re able to get plenty of rest.

Is There Anything I Shouldn’t Eat with Dental Implants?

Long term, dental implants are capable of eating just about anything that your natural teeth can. Of course, sticky and sugary foods are just as problematic for your oral health as they ever were, and it’s still a good idea to enjoy them in moderation. However, your dental implants shouldn’t be a limited factor in what you’re able to eat.
