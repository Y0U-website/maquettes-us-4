Dr. JaTerra Ross and the team at Ross Dental Studio are committed to giving you a smile you can be proud of. Missing teeth can make eating and speaking difficult and can lead to other dental problems. Dental implants provide a lasting solution that prevents these issues and keeps your smile healthy.

Contact us today for a consultation and take the first step towards a healthier, more confident smile.

  • All-On-4 Implants: This method supports a full denture using only four implants, offering excellent stability and a quicker recovery time.
  • How the Dental Implant Process Works Initial Assessment: We start with a check-up to determine whether you need any preliminary work, such as tooth extractions or dental bone grafting .
  • Implant Placement: The implant is surgically placed in your jawbone, followed by a healing period.
  • Abutment Placement: After healing, an abutment is attached to the implant.
  • Custom Tooth: Finally, a custom-made tooth is added, completing your new smile.
  • What types of dental implants exist? Endosteal Implants: These use a titanium rod and post to hold crowns or bridges and are very common.
  • All-on-4 Implants: These support a full denture with only four implants, providing great stability and a quicker recovery.
  • What are the benefits of dental implants? Durable and Long-Lasting: Implants are sturdy and built to last.
  • Natural Look: They blend in seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Improved Dental Health: They help maintain jawbone and facial structure.
  • Easy Care: Treat them like your natural teeth.
  • Boost Confidence: Enjoy eating, speaking, and smiling without worry.
