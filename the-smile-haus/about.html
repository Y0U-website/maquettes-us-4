Your First Visit

A Personalized, Comfortable Experience

At your first appointment, we take the time to understand your individual needs and concerns. Dr. Kim will conduct a thorough examination and discuss any issues or areas of focus. Together, we will create a customized treatment plan that meets your goals and budget.

How to Prepare

Please make sure to fill out the new patient intake forms with medical and dental history 48 hours before your appointment so Dr. Kim and her team can be best prepared for your visit. Please carefully review our office policies.

Our concierge will reach out to you a couple days before your appointment to answer any questions or concerns, provide a complimentary check and breakdown of your insurance benefits, and let you know what to expect at your first visit.

Our new patient exams are over 90 minutes so please plan accordingly.

Designed for Your Comfort

We believe in making your visit as comfortable and enjoyable as possible. That’s why our office is equipped with a wide range of amenities, all focused on enhancing your comfort and creating a relaxing experience. You can even choose your favorite amenities online before your visit!
