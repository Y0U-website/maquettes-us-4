Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

The following are some of the factors to consider when determining candidacy for implant dentistry:

Good Oral Health : Candidates for implant dentistry are generally those with healthy teeth and gums and are committed to maintaining meticulous oral hygiene care to support the long-term success of their restorations. Conditions such as tooth decay or periodontal (gum) disease must be treated before placing your implants. Dr. Hajiyani will discuss any steps needed to get your smile back on track and ready for restoration with implant dentistry.

Good General Health : Candidates for dental implants must be free from health conditions that can interfere with healing, such as uncontrolled diabetes or osteoporosis. Moreover, smoking and using tobacco products can interfere with healing and the osseointegration process.

Adequate Jawbone Density : Most types of dental implants require sufficient jawbone density for support to ensure the procedure&#x27;s success. The good news is that if you have bone loss due to long-term tooth loss, periodontal disease, or other factors, a bone grafting procedure can rebuild the area, helping ensure the procedure&#x27;s success.

Fully Developed Smiles : Implant dentistry is ideal only for patients with fully developed jawbones. In general, most individuals under the age of 18 may not be candidates for dental implants.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

The following are some of the benefits dental implants offer:

  • They Behave Like Natural Teeth : Dental implants mimic the look and function of natural teeth. Most patients can&#x27;t tell the difference between their new implant restorations and their natural teeth.
  • They Prevent Bone Loss : Dental implants act like natural tooth roots, providing stimulation to your jawbone. This helps prevent the bone loss that typically occurs after tooth loss, helping maintain your facial contours.
  • They Restore Function : With dental implants, you can enjoy your favorite foods, even the crunchy or chewy varieties, without restriction. You&#x27;ll also find it easier to speak without slurring and smile with confidence once again.
  • They Keep the Adjacent Teeth Stable : Leaving a gap in your smile can cause your nearby teeth to shift out of position. Dental implants help maintain the alignment of your teeth and bite, helping lower the risk of periodontal disease, tooth wear and fracture, and even temporomandibular (TMJ) pain.
  • They Can Last a Lifetime : Dental implants are made from titanium, a biocompatible material that integrates with the jawbone. With proper care and regular maintenance, they can last for decades or even a lifetime.
