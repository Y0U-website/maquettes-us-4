Frequently Asked Questions

What do dental implants cost in Desert Hot Springs

Dental implants typically range from $3,000 to $6,000 per tooth, including the crown. Costs vary based on the number of implants, need for bone grafting, and materials used. Each treatment plan is unique.

Insurance coverage varies and often does not cover the full cost, though portions such as the crown may be included. While implants have a higher upfront cost, they are often more cost-effective long term than bridges or dentures. Financing and payment options are available to help make treatment manageable.

What are the benefits of dental implants

Dental implants provide both functional and long-term health benefits. They are designed to replace missing teeth in a way that feels stable and natural.

Because implants integrate with the jawbone, they support oral health in ways removable options cannot. This stability makes them a preferred solution for many patients.

  • Look and feel like natural teeth
  • Prevent jawbone deterioration
  • Restore full chewing ability
  • No diet restrictions
  • Preserve facial contours
  • Do not slip or move
  • Protect adjacent teeth
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants are considered a highly reliable tooth replacement option. Large clinical studies report success rates around 95 to 98 percent in healthy patients when implants are placed properly and maintained over time. These results often remain strong even after 10 to 15 years.

Long-term success depends on good oral hygiene, regular dental visits, and avoiding risk factors such as smoking. Implants perform best when they are cared for like natural teeth and monitored routinely.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Most dental insurance plans do not cover the full cost of dental implants. Some plans may contribute toward the crown or provide benefits similar to what they would pay for a bridge or denture. Coverage depends on your specific policy and annual limits.

A pre-treatment estimate helps clarify expected benefits and out-of-pocket costs. Reviewing coverage ahead of time allows patients to plan treatment and financing more comfortably.
