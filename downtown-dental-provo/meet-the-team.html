Dr. Kai Pedersen

Originally from Northern California, Dr. Kai Pedersen received his undergraduate degree from Brigham Young University and graduated dental school in 2021 with honors from Roseman University of Health Sciences in South Jordan, UT.

As a commissioned officer in the United States Army, he completed a dental residency program at Joint Base Lewis-McChord, WA and served as an active-duty dentist at Fort Huachuca, AZ before coming back to Utah.

Kai and his wife Kaela have five children: four girls and one little boy. He has always enjoyed playing and watching sports and has especially loved getting to coach his daughters’ soccer teams. Some of Kai’s other favorite things include graphic design, Disneyland, and spending time with his family.
