3 Ways To Transform Your Smile

Dental Implants

Dental implants represent a cutting-edge solution for missing teeth, offered at our dental office to restore both function and aesthetics. Our experienced dental team utilizes advanced implant technology to provide patients with durable and natural-looking replacements for missing teeth. The process involves surgically placing titanium implants into the jawbone, which fuse with the bone over time to create a strong and stable foundation.

  • Long Lasting
  • Low Maintenance
  • Strength and Durability
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a popular cosmetic dental procedure offered at our dental office to enhance the brightness of discolored or stained teeth. Our skilled dental professionals employ safe and effective whitening techniques to restore a vibrant and radiant smile for our patients. By utilizing advanced whitening agents and state-of-the-art equipment, we ensure optimal results while prioritizing the overall oral health of our patients. The benefits of our teeth whitening services include:

  • Quick and Convenient
  • Long-lasting Effects
  • Enhanced Aesthetics
  • Increased Confidence
  • Customized Approach

Invisalign

Invisalign, offered at our dental office, is a revolutionary orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth using a series of clear, removable aligners. This advanced alternative to traditional braces provides patients with a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a straighter smile. The Invisalign process involves the use of customized, transparent aligners that gradually shift teeth into their desired positions.

  • Faster than Braces
  • Nearly Invisible
  • Comfortable and Easily Removable
  • Fewer appointments
  • Precise treatment tracking

DENTIST

Dr. Beatrice Aker

Dr. Beatrice Aker’s passion for dentistry stems from a love of people, community, science, and art. She believes a combination of deep respect for the basic principles of dentistry, love of human connection, and critical thinking are the keys to success in the dental field.

Dr. Aker enjoys educating her community about dental health and how it can positively impact a person’s entire life. Her choice to pursue general dentistry has allowed her to continue to elevate, grow, and evolve her skills in all aspects of dentistry to offer patients comprehensive and consistent dental care.

Dr. Aker was born in Kansas City, KS and grew up in Abilene, KS. As the youngest of five kids, her adolescence in Abilene was spent working at a local restaurant, playing tennis, acting as a “can-can girl” in Old Abilene Town, volunteering with multiple local organizations, enjoying the usual shenanigans with friends at the fair, and playing in the high school and city bands.

Before she chose dentistry, she completed a bachelor’s degree in English from Loyola University Chicago. She earned her second bachelor’s degree in chemistry from University of Missouri – Kansas City and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from University of Minnesota – School of Dentistry.

Dr. Aker lives in Abilene with her husband, Joseph, and their dog, Barney. When not in the office, Dr. Aker enjoys wheel-throwing ceramics, gardening, experimenting in the kitchen, reading, playing music, and helping on Joseph’s family farm.
