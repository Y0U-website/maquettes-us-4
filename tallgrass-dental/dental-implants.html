Dental Implants in Abilene, KS

Are you struggling to share your smile with others because of missing teeth? By the age of 50, most Americans have lost an average of 12 teeth according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. If you have lost a tooth, there are a few procedures you can consider to replace the missing tooth. Dental implants in Abilene, KS will allow you to restore your smile. Dental implants are deemed a permanent replacement for the original tooth. Teeth are often lost due to disease, decay, damage, or trauma. A dental implant is designed to look and feel and function like a traditional tooth.

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a permanent replacement for a missing tooth. Patients will be able to have a restored smile as the implant looks and function like a traditional tooth. Patients will work with our dentists to select a color for the tooth to help enhance your smile. We find that dental implants are an effective way to restore your smile and restore your confidence!

The Dental Implant Process

Dental implants are surgically inserted into the jaw bone. The implant uses a titanium post that bonds with the jawbone, permanently securing it in place. Tallgrass Dental works with patients to create custom treatment plans designed to restore your smile. Our dentists have been trained in implant dentistry and has the skills and experience needed to ensure your dental implant will be successful.

Patients will come to our office and receive an evaluation of their oral health to determine if they are candidates for dental implants. Certain patients may have health conditions that may require a different type of dental procedure to be used. If we find there is not enough bone density to hold the implant in place, which requires bone grafting. The bone will help to strengthen the area to ensure the dental implant will be successful. Following bone grafting, patients come back several months later to ensure the bone is ready for the procedure.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are one of the only solutions for patients with missing teeth. If you do not want dental crowns or dentures, consider talking to Tallgrass Dental about dental implants in Abilene, KS. Here are some of the reasons why we recommend dental implants for missing teeth:

  • Dental implants are a permanent solution for a missing tooth. Other dental procedures like dental bridges will eventually need to be replaced, and the anchor teeth can become vulnerable to decay.
  • Dental implants only impact one tooth unlike a dental bridge which can implant one or two other teeth.
  • Some patients can receive a dental implant in one or two appointments based on their oral health.
  • Dental implants can be used to replace one or several teeth.
  • Dental implants are used as the bridgework for partial dentures, or can be used as support for snap-in full dentures.
