Accessibility Statement

If you are looking for mouse and keyboard alternatives, speech recognition software such as Dragon Naturally Speaking may help you navigate web pages and online services. This software allows the user to move focus around a web page or application screen through voice controls.

We are committed to ensuring we meet all of your needs. If you require assistance with any of the content on our website, please call our office at (732) 249-0055 and we will do everything in our power to assist you. Thank you!

Use your computer to read web pages out loud
  • Use the keyboard to navigate screens
  • Increase text size
  • Magnify your screen
  • Change background and text colors
  • Make your mouse pointer more visible (Windows only)
