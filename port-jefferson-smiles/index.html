In 1985, Sirona Dental Systems changed the face of dentistry by creating CEREC dental restorations. To date, CEREC is method used by Dr. Halpern that offers single-visit chair side restoration. All-ceramic crowns are fabricated and placed in one easy dental appointment! CEREC is fast, safe, and provides a natural-looking restoration that will stand the test of time.

During the last 20 years, dental implants have become a desirable alternative to other methods of replacing missing teeth. Excellent success rates and a range of available options give dentists a variety of new ways to treat and replace lost teeth.

Gum recession is the loss of gum tissue along the gumline that can occur due to periodontal disease, the natural aging process, or abrasive habits when brushing teeth. Dr. Halpern utilizes the Chao Pinhole® Surgical Technique to fix receding gums. It is a minimally invasive technique that is incision and suture-free.

Dental implants are permanent and immediate solution for replacing missing teeth. They look, feel and function just like your own natural teeth. At Port Jefferson Smiles in Port Jefferson Station, New York, we have successfully performed dental implantation for years, helping patients restore dental functions and quality of life.

Dental implants are placed in stages. After consultation and diagnosis, your dentist at Port Jefferson Smiles will surgically place the titanium post, which serves as the base for an implant. Titanium is highly-compatible and fuses with our jaw bone through the process of osseointegration, which takes place over a period of several months.

Abutment, which serves as the connector between the base and the dental crown, is secured to the posts. A prosthetic dental crown is then securely screwed to the abutment. Dental crowns are individualized and manufactured to match your natural teeth using durable materials.

At Port Jefferson Smiles, we can replace your missing teeth with implant-supported restorations. Our highly-experienced dental implant team will restore your chewing ability, teeth stability and prevent further deterioration of your jawbone.
