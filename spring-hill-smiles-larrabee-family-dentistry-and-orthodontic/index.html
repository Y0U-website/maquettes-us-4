Services

Before we start, we perform an oral exam and use CBCT scans and 3D imaging to get a digital blueprint of your mouth. This step helps us assess whether you have enough bone structure to support the arch and plan out the optimal placement of the implants.

Our lab crafts custom crowns for your implants, ensuring a perfect fit and natural looking results. When your implant crowns are ready, we will place it over your implant post to complete your new smile.

Most patients with good oral health and enough bone density are suitable for dental implants. During your first appointment, we&#x27;ll evaluate the condition of your oral tissue and discuss possible treatments to see if dental implants are right for you.

Invisalign® consists of a set of clear trays that move your teeth into proper alignment. Since they&#x27;re made of clear plastic, they fit snugly over your teeth without being as noticeable as braces.
