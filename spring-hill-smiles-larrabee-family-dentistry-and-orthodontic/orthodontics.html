Our Orthodontic Treatment Options

Metal Brackets

Traditional braces consist of metal brackets and wires that shift teeth into proper alignment. They are highly effective and suitable for all ages.

Clear Brackets

Clear ceramic brackets provide a more discreet alternative to metal braces. They mimic the natural color of your teeth, which makes them less noticeable.

Invisalign ®

Invisalign® consists of a set of clear trays that move your teeth into proper alignment. Since they&#x27;re made of clear plastic, they fit snugly over your teeth without being as noticeable as braces.

Palatal Expander Device (PED)

A PED is a non-surgical appliance that goes in the roof of your mouth. It is used to widen the upper jaw, correct crossbite, and create more space for crowded teeth.

Orthognathic Surgery

In some cases of severe jaw misalignment, we may suggest orthognathic surgery to correct the position of the jawbones.

Craniofacial Orthodontics

These surgeries treat complex craniofacial abnormalities, such as cleft lip and palate. They close the gap in the affected tissue to improve facial appearance, speech, and the ability to eat and breathe.

Are Retainers Necessary After Treatment?

Yes, retainers are necessary after orthodontic treatment to maintain the results. They help stop teeth from shifting back to their original position and are often worn at night.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

The duration of your treatment depends on the complexity of your case. Treatment can vary from a few months to a few years. At your complimentary consultation, our team will discuss your specific needs and build a customized treatment plan with clear expectations.
