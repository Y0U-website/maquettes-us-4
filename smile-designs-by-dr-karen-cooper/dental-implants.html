The Dental Implants Process

Here’s what to expect when receiving dental implants in Savannah

  • Step 1: During your initial consultation, you will meet with Dr. Cooper to determine if you are a candidate for dental implants. Together, you will discuss a personalized treatment plan for your overall health, oral health, and smile goals.
  • Step 2: Using advanced technology, the implant post will be placed precisely into your jaw. Made from lightweight materials like titanium, the post is biocompatible with gum tissue, bone, and nearby teeth.
  • Step 3: Over the next three to six months, your bone and gum tissue will naturally grow and fuse around the implant post. Once healed, it acts as your new tooth root, ensuring long-term stability and support.
  • Step 4: Dr. Cooper will attach the abutment, followed by your custom dental crown. Once complete, your implant, restoration, or bridge will look, feel, and function just like natural teeth.
