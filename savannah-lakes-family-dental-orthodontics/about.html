Our Dentists

Ahmad Jafri DDS

Dr. Jafri began his career as a dental assistant in the U. S. Army, where he gained invaluable experience in military dental care. This role provided him with a solid foundation in dental procedures and patient care, fostering a disciplined and comprehensive approach to dentistry. Following his service in the army, Dr. Jafri pursued further education and training to become a licensed dentist in the United States.

Dr. Jafri has had a diverse career, working for both the federal government and the state of Texas. His roles in these capacities have equipped him with a deep understanding of public health policies and the nuances of providing dental care in various governmental settings. These experiences have honed his skills in dealing with a wide range of dental issues and patient needs, from routine check-ups to more complex dental treatments.

Currently, Dr. Jafri is in private practice, offering his dental expertise to the communities of Alvin and Rosharon Texas. In his private practice, he continues to deliver high-quality dental care utilizing his extensive background and experience to ensure the best outcomes for his patients.

Abuzar Siddiqui MPH, DPH, DDS

Dr. Abuzar Siddiqui completed his first part of dental schooling in Pakistan in 2000. He is a graduate of NYU College of Dentistry Langone. He brings about 10 years of experience. As well he has a Master’s in Public Health (2014), a Doctor of Public Health (2017), and an Advanced Education General Dentistry degree (2019). He joined our practice in 2018. He is known for his sunny personality and for singing to his patients.

Zeeshan Raja DDS, MPH

Dr. Raja with a distinguished career spanning six years across various clinical settings, leverages his educational foundation in public health health-based dentistry, complemented by an extensive residency program. Beyond his professional commitments, he relishes moments of respite with his cherished family.
