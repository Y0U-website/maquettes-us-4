Why Choose Simplyclear Aligners?

According to the American Association of Orthodontists, nearly 75 % of people could benefit from orthodontic treatment. With Simplyclear Aligners, straightening your teeth has never been more accessible or convenient.

  • Virtually Invisible: The clear aligners blend naturally with your teeth, making them barely noticeable.
  • Comfortable Fit: Crafted from smooth plastic, they eliminate the irritation caused by metal braces.
  • Removable Convenience: Eat, drink, and brush without restrictions.
  • Faster Treatment Times: Many patients achieve their ideal smile in 6-18 months.
  • Fewer Dental Visits: Unlike traditional braces, Simplyclear Aligners require fewer in-office adjustments.

General Dentistry

Professional dental cleanings are important for our overall health. It’s recommended that people of all ages visit the dentist at least twice per year for a cleaning so that we can monitor your gums’ health and catch any possible issues before they become larger problems. X-rays are always included in our dental exams as well.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Through cosmetic dentistry, we help to restore people’s confidence in their smiles. Using an in-house Zoom whitening system, we are able to provide modern treatments that help people achieve a bright and healthy smile they are proud to show off.

Restorative Dentistry

Our focus with restorative dentistry is to restore the strength, function, and structure of your teeth after they have been damaged by cavities, tooth decay, or trauma. Restorative dentistry includes fillings, root canals, crowns and extractions, replacing missing teeth with a bridge or denture, and braces.

Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment is essential for proper function and for teeth straightening purposes. Through digital x-rays, we can fully examine a patient’s teeth and jaw and provide a plan to correct the alignment with braces. With better-aligned teeth, you will be able to chew and speak better, have less jaw pain, and have healthier dental and gum health.

Pediatric Dentistry

We believe in creating good dental habits early and that the foundation is set during childhood. That’s why we begin accepting patients at six months old. We offer a fun and positive environment to help children be at ease at the dentist’s office to establish a confident relationship with their dental health.
