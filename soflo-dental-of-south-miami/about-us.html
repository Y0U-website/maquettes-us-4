Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Esteban Parada, DMD, FAGD

Dr. Esteban Parada attended Temple University Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, where he graduated with his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree in 2019. He is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, Xi Psi Phi International Dental Fraternity, Kornberg School of Dentistry Alumni Association and the Florida Dental Association. Dr. Parada is also certified in minimal invasive dentistry and Invisalign® treatment.

Dr. Parada is grateful for the trust his patients have instilled in him over the last few years and is humbled by the ability to make a real difference in their lives. In his spare time, he enjoys rock climbing, soccer and cooking!

Dr. Nhora Ortega, DDS

Dr. Ortega has 20 years of dental experience. She earned her dental degree from Colombian Dental College in 1991 and completed her doctorate in restorative dentistry at the University of Florida in 2002. For 18 years, she’s practiced restorative dentistry in Miami and is a member of the American Dental Association and the Florida Dental Association.

Dr. Ortega focuses on restorative dentistry for all ages and recently added implantology to her practice. She earned a fellowship in implant dentistry in 2018, where she integrates L-PRF and BOTOX® therapy for patient care. Her mission is to provide high-quality dentistry in a caring, friendly, and relaxed environment, driven by her passion for creating beautiful smiles.
