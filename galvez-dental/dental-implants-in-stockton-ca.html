High-Quality Dental Implants

  • Q: How long do dental implants last? A: With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or longer often for a lifetime. Routine checkups and good oral hygiene are key to their longevity.
  • Q: Am I too old for dental implants? A: There’s no upper age limit! As long as you’re healthy and have enough bone support, implants can be an excellent solution for adults of all ages.
  • Q: What if I don&#x27;t have enough bone for implants? A: Bone grafting procedures can often rebuild lost bone and make implants possible, even if you’ve been told otherwise in the past.
  • Q: How do I care for my dental implants? A: Care is very similar to natural teeth: brush, floss, and visit your dentist regularly. Implants are strong but still need professional cleanings to stay in top condition.
  • Q: Can I get a dental implant immediately after a tooth extraction? A: In some cases, yes! Immediate implant placement may be possible depending on the health of the bone and gum tissue. We will evaluate your situation carefully during your consultation.
  • Q: What happens if I delay replacing a missing tooth? A: Delaying replacement can lead to bone loss, shifting of adjacent teeth, and changes in your bite. The sooner you replace a missing tooth, the better for preserving your oral health.
