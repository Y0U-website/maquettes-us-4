We Take Dental Emergencies

Dental emergencies come in many forms, and all of them are painful. When you’re experiencing tooth or gum pain, call our Stockton dental office right away at (209) 465-5823 so we can get you in for treatment. Whether you’ve lost a tooth, have sudden tooth pain, or have lost a filling, call us immediately. We will help to get you the next available appointment with our dentists so you can get the dental care you need.

Acting fast can help to save your teeth and prevent long-term damage. If you aren’t sure that your symptoms count as an emergency, call us anyway. Our caring staff will help determine if you need an emergency appointment and can set you up with one right away if you do.
