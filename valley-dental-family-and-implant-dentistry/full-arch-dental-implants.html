Full-Arch Dental Implants in Trussville, AL

While traditional dental implants are great for replacing individual teeth or a few missing ones, full-arch implants provide a complete, stable, and natural-looking restoration for patients missing all of their teeth. At Valley Dental, our experienced team delivers a customized, long-lasting arch of teeth that restores your smile, confidence, and functionality.

What Are Full-Arch Dental Implants?

Full-arch dental implants are a comprehensive, permanent solution to replace an entire set of teeth on either the upper or lower jaw. Instead of placing individual implants for each missing tooth, full-arch implants use multiple implant posts to support a full row of teeth.

Full-arch implants significantly reduce the number of implants needed, making the process less invasive and more efficient while still offering outstanding strength and durability.

One of the key benefits of full-arch implants is the quick turnaround—this procedure can often be completed in just one day. You’ll walk into Valley Dental and leave with a brand-new, fully functional set of teeth, giving you a whole new reason to smile.

How Does Full-Arch Implant Placement Work?

Full-arch dental implants are anchored using only a few strategically placed implants, usually multiple implants. The implants are strategically placed at optimal angles to create a strong, stable foundation for the teeth that will be securely attached to them.

This technique allows us to deliver an entire arch of teeth that feels secure and comfortable without the need for numerous individual implants.

What Is the Healing Process After Getting Full-Arch Implants?

After full-arch implants are placed, the healing period generally takes about three to six months. During this time, the implants integrate with the bone to form a strong, permanent foundation. The implants are made from biocompatible materials such as titanium or zirconia, ensuring a seamless connection with your oral tissue.

Throughout the healing period, you’ll have the opportunity to review your new teeth to make sure they meet your expectations. Dr. Headley can make adjustments or refinements so that you’re completely satisfied with your new smile.
