What Are Single Dental Implants?

A single dental implant is an effective solution for replacing a missing tooth. The process starts by placing a titanium post into the jawbone, where it naturally bonds to create a strong anchor for a custom artificial tooth .

Once secured, the implant mimics your natural teeth, letting you eat, speak, and smile freely without any discomfort. You can also brush and floss it like the rest of your teeth, making it easy to maintain.

One of the biggest advantages of single dental implants is the freedom to enjoy your favorite foods without limitations. Implants stay firmly in place, unlike dentures, which can move around or become uncomfortable.

During your routine dental check-ups , we’ll make sure your implant stays in top condition, ensuring it serves you well for years to come.

Parts of a Single Dental Implant

A dental implant is made up of three key components, each vital to ensuring your new tooth is functional, durable, and long-lasting.

The Implant

The titanium post that is placed into your jawbone. It acts as the root of your new tooth, providing a solid and stable foundation.

The Abutment

This small connector fits onto the implant and holds the crown in place. It ensures everything stays attached so you can go about your day without worry.

The Crown

This is the visible part of the implant, custom-designed to match the color, size, and shape of your natural teeth.

What Should You Expect During the Implant Procedure?

The process of receiving a dental implant typically takes place over several months. Since the implant must fuse with your jaw, we schedule follow-up appointments to ensure everything is healing correctly before moving forward.

While the process requires some patience, the end result is a permanent, natural-looking solution for your missing tooth. At Valley Dental, we will guide you through each step, from the initial consultation to the final restoration, ensuring your comfort and satisfaction throughout the procedure.
