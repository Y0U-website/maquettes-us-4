About DOWNTOWN DENTIST SF

Downtown Dentist SF in the Union Square neighborhood of San Francisco, offers a full range of preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry services. Experienced general and cosmetic dentist Stefka Petrova, DDS, and her staff are dedicated to providing the best possible individualized care, from routine cleanings to complex restoration work.

Dr. Petrova and her team take a gentle, minimally-invasive approach to dentistry. First and foremost, they educate their patients to take the best possible care of their teeth and gums. After all, good oral health starts with good habits.

Dr. Petrova is well-versed in the latest dental techniques and technologies. She provides state-of-the-art cosmetic treatments, including whitening, veneers, and implants with natural long-lasting results. She also offers orthodontic treatment with Invisalign®, which straightens teeth without the hassle of braces. As a cosmetic dentist, Dr. Petrova performs dental work with equal attention to how it looks and how it functions.

In addition to preventive and cosmetic dentistry, Downtown Dentist SF is available for dental emergencies, providing same-day appointments. It’s the team’s mission to keep patients’ smiles healthy and looking their best, no matter the situation.
