Interceptive (Phase 1)…

  • Create Space for Permanent Teeth – Addresses crowding and misalignment early.
  • Prevent More Serious Issues – Reduces the likelihood of tooth extractions or surgery in the future.
  • Correct Harmful Oral Habits – Helps stop thumb-sucking, tongue thrusting, and other habits affecting dental development.

Signs Your Child May Need Early Treatment

If your child exhibits any of the following, early orthodontic treatment may be beneficial:

The American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) recommends that children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. This allows Dr. Weaver to assess growth patterns and determine if interceptive treatment is needed.

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Early or late loss of baby teeth
  • Difficulty chewing or biting food
  • Mouth breathing or snoring
  • Thumb-sucking past age 5
  • Speech difficulties related to dental misalignment
  • A visibly protruding or recessed jaw

Common Interceptive Treatment Options

  • Palatal Expanders – Used to widen the upper jaw and correct crossbites or crowding.
  • Space Maintainers – Keeps space open for permanent teeth if a baby tooth is lost too early.
  • Partial Braces – Helps align specific teeth before full orthodontic treatment.
  • Habit Appliances – Devices designed to help stop thumb-sucking or tongue-thrusting habits.

The Benefits of Early Orthodontic Treatment

  • Reduces the Need for Future Extractions or Surgery
  • Guides Proper Jaw Growth & Development
  • Improves Facial Balance & Aesthetics
  • Makes Future Treatment Shorter & More Effective
  • Prevents Speech & Chewing Issues
