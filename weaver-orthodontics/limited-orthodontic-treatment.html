Limited Orthodontic Treatment

  • Pre-Restorative Orthodontics – Aligning teeth before cosmetic or restorative dental work (such as veneers, implants, or bridges).
  • Early Intervention for Children – Addressing small developmental issues before they become more complex.
  • Relapse Cases – Realigning teeth that have shifted after previous orthodontic treatment.

Treatment Options for Limited Orthodontics

  • Clear Aligners – A nearly invisible and removable option for minor adjustments.
  • Metal or Ceramic Braces – A short-term solution to refine alignment.
  • Space Maintainers or Expanders – Used in children to guide proper dental development.

Benefits of Limited Orthodontic Treatment

  • Shorter Treatment Time – Typically completed in a few months to a year.
  • More Affordable – A cost-effective option for small adjustments.
  • Targeted & Efficient – Focuses only on specific teeth or alignment concerns.
  • Prepares for Future Dental Work – Helps ensure long-term success of restorative treatments.

Is Limited Treatment Right for You?

Limited orthodontic treatment is ideal if you have mild alignment concerns and are looking for a quick, effective solution. However, if you have bite issues, severe misalignment, or jaw discrepancies, comprehensive treatment may be a better option. Dr. Weaver will assess your needs and recommend the best approach for achieving your ideal smile.
