Preprosthetic Orthodontics

This treatment is particularly beneficial for patients who have missing teeth, severe misalignment, or bone structure concerns that could affect the success of prosthetic restorations.

  • Correcting Jaw Discrepancies – Addressing misalignments to improve the fit of dentures or implants.
  • Stabilizing the Bite – Creating a well-balanced occlusion (bite) to enhance the longevity of prosthetics.
  • Expanding or Reshaping the Jawbone – Using orthopedic appliances to modify bone structure when needed.

Who Needs Preprosthetic Orthopedic Treatment?

Patients who are planning to receive dentures, dental implants, or bridges may need preprosthetic orthopedic treatment if they experience:

A thorough evaluation with Dr. Weaver can determine if orthodontic or orthopedic treatment is needed before proceeding with prosthetic dental work.

  • Missing teeth leading to bite imbalances
  • Jawbone shrinkage or misalignment
  • Teeth that have shifted due to tooth loss
  • Difficulty chewing due to an uneven bite
  • The need for bone expansion before dental implants

Common Preprosthetic Orthopedic Treatments

  • Orthodontic Treatment (Braces or Clear Aligners) – Straightens teeth and creates proper spacing for prosthetic work.
  • Palatal Expanders or Jaw Modification Appliances – Helps adjust the upper or lower jaw structure for a better fit.
  • Bite Correction & Occlusal Adjustments – Ensures that future dental work fits properly and functions well.
  • Space Maintainers or Tooth Realignment – Prepares the mouth for dental implants or bridges by keeping proper tooth alignment.

The Benefits of Preprosthetic Orthopedics

  • Ensures a Proper Fit for Dental Prosthetics – Reduces complications with dentures, bridges, or implants.
  • Improves Long-Term Stability & Function – Enhances comfort and durability of prosthetic work.
  • Prevents Future Dental Issues – Corrects bite and alignment problems before prosthetics are placed.
  • Enhances Facial Aesthetics – Helps maintain natural facial structure and symmetry.
  • Collaborative Approach to Care – Works in coordination with dentists, oral surgeons, and prosthodontists for comprehensive treatment.
