Orthognathic (Jaw Corrective)…

This surgery is typically recommended when orthodontic treatment alone cannot achieve proper alignment of the jaws and teeth.

  • Facial symmetry and balance
  • Breathing and airway function
  • Chewing and speech clarity
  • Jaw pain and TMJ disorders

Who Needs Combined Orthodontic & Jaw Surgery Treatment?

  • Severe Overbite or Underbite – When the upper or lower jaw extends too far forward or backward.
  • Open Bite – When the upper and lower teeth do not touch properly when the mouth is closed.
  • Facial Asymmetry – Uneven jaw growth leading to imbalanced facial features.
  • Chronic Jaw Pain & TMJ Issues – Caused by misaligned jaw structure.
  • Sleep Apnea & Breathing Problems – Restricted airway due to jaw position.
  • Difficulty Chewing or Speaking – Caused by poor jaw alignment.

The Treatment Process

Comprehensive Evaluation

  • 3D imaging, X-rays, and digital scans are used to analyze jaw structure.
  • Dr. Weaver works closely with an oral and maxillofacial surgeon to create a customized treatment plan.

Pre-Surgical Orthodontic Treatment

  • Braces or clear aligners are used to move the teeth into the correct position before surgery.
  • This phase can last 12–18 months, depending on individual needs.

Orthognathic Surgery

  • Surgery is performed by an oral surgeon in a hospital setting.
  • The jawbones are realigned and secured using small plates and screws.
  • Recovery typically takes a few weeks, with initial healing in 6–8 weeks.

Post-Surgical Orthodontic Treatment

  • Once healing progresses, fine-tuning with braces or aligners ensures that the teeth settle into their ideal positions.
  • Treatment is completed, and a retainer is provided to maintain results.

Benefits of Orthognathic Surgery & Orthodontics

  • Restores a Proper Bite & Jaw Function – Improves chewing, speech, and comfort.
  • Enhances Facial Harmony & Aesthetics – Corrects facial asymmetry and jaw discrepancies.
  • Relieves TMJ Pain & Jaw Strain – Reduces tension and discomfort in the jaw joint.
  • Improves Breathing & Sleep Quality – Helps with obstructive sleep apnea and breathing difficulties.
  • Boosts Confidence & Quality of Life – A well-aligned jaw can enhance self-esteem and overall well-being.
