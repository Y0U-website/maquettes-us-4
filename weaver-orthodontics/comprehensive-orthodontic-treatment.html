Our Treatment Options

We offer a variety of treatment solutions to fit your needs, including:

  • Metal Braces – Durable, effective, and ideal for treating mild to severe alignment issues.
  • Ceramic (Clear) Braces – A more discreet option that blends with your natural teeth.
  • Clear Aligners – Virtually invisible trays that offer flexibility and convenience.
  • Surgical Orthodontics – For complex cases requiring jaw surgery, Dr. Weaver provides expert guidance and treatment planning.

The Benefits of Comprehensive Orthodontic Treatment

  • Better Oral Health – Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease.
  • Improved Bite Function – Correcting bite issues prevents wear and tear on teeth and jaw discomfort.
  • Enhanced Facial Aesthetics – Balanced jaw alignment can improve facial symmetry and profile.
  • Boosted Confidence – A straight, beautiful smile enhances self-esteem and overall well-being.

What to Expect During Treatment

  • Initial Consultation & Exam – Dr. Weaver will assess your teeth and jaw alignment using advanced digital imaging.
  • Customized Treatment Plan – We develop a personalized approach based on your needs and goals.
  • Active Treatment Phase – Braces or aligners work to gradually move teeth into their correct positions.
  • Regular Adjustments & Monitoring – Periodic check-ups ensure progress and make necessary adjustments.
  • Retention & Long-Term Results – Once treatment is complete, a retainer helps maintain your beautiful new smile.

Why Choose Weaver Orthodontics?

Dr. Weaver specializes in treating mild to severe orthodontic cases, including surgical orthodontics. We use the latest advancements for more efficient and comfortable treatment.
