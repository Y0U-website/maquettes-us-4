Accessibility Statement

If you experience difficulty accessing our website with your assistive technology, please contact us so we can help.

  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Alternative input devices

Third-Party Content

Some content on our website may be provided by third parties, such as embedded videos or social media feeds. We do not have control over the accessibility of third-party content, but we work to select accessible options whenever possible.

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our website and services to identify areas for improvement. Our team receives training on accessibility best practices and stays informed about current standards and technologies.

Recent Updates

We continuously update our website to improve accessibility, including:

  • Regular testing with screen readers and other assistive technologies
  • Reviewing new content for accessibility before publication
  • Addressing feedback from users about accessibility barriers
  • Updating our practices based on the latest WCAG guidelines

We Want Your Feedback

We welcome your input on the accessibility of our website and services. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, have difficulty accessing any content, or have suggestions for improvement, please let us know.

Your feedback helps us identify areas where we can do better. We take all accessibility concerns seriously and work to address them promptly.

How to Contact Us

If you need assistance accessing our website or services, or if you would like to report an accessibility issue, please contact us:

When reporting an accessibility issue, please include:

We will respond to your inquiry within 2-3 business days and work with you to resolve the issue or provide an alternative method of accessing the content or service.

  • The web page or area where you experienced difficulty
  • A description of the problem
  • The assistive technology you were using, if applicable
  • Your contact information so we can follow up with you

Additional Accommodations

If you require specific accommodations during your visit to our practice, please let us know when scheduling your appointment. We are happy to make arrangements to ensure you have a comfortable and accessible experience.

Examples of accommodations we can provide include:

  • Assistance with forms and paperwork
  • Extended appointment times if needed
  • Quiet treatment environments for patients with sensory sensitivities
  • Communication support for patients who are deaf or hard of hearing
  • Physical support for patients with mobility limitations

Standards and Guidelines

We strive to conform to the following standards:

WCAG 2.1 Level AA Web Content Accessibility Guidelines published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), the main international standards organization for the internet.

ADA Americans with Disabilities Act, which prohibits discrimination against individuals with disabilities in all areas of public life.

Section 508 A federal law requiring electronic and information technology to be accessible to people with disabilities.
