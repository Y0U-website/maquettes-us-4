Meet Dr. Birdi

Dr. Jalpa Birdi combines advanced dental training with a genuinely caring approach that puts patients at ease. Originally from the UK, she brings a unique perspective and gentle sense of humor to her practice in Avondale Estates.

With specialized implant training completed, Dr. Birdi offers comprehensive dental services including complex restorative work, cosmetic procedures, and comfortable oral sedation for anxious patients.

Her down-to-earth personality and commitment to taking time with each patient creates lasting relationships built on trust and exceptional care. She believes every patient deserves personalized attention and treatment plans designed specifically for their individual needs and goals.

Our Vision

We envision a dental practice where advanced clinical care meets genuine compassion, creating an environment where patients feel comfortable, informed, and valued. Our mission is to provide exceptional dental treatments while building lasting relationships with families throughout Avondale Estates and the surrounding communities.

Dr. Birdi’s commitment to continuing education and the latest techniques ensures our patients receive the most effective, comfortable treatments available. We believe in preventive care, patient education, and working together to maintain optimal oral health for life.

Our goal is to be your trusted dental home, where every visit reinforces our dedication to your comfort, health, and satisfaction. We’re building something special in Avondale Estates—a practice where excellent dentistry and personal care come together seamlessly.
