Are Dental Implants Right for Me?

If you are missing one or more teeth, and your mouth is healthy and free from issues like gum disease and tooth decay, dental implants may be the right restorative option for you. A good candidate will have adequate, healthy jaw bone to support a dental implant. If your jaw has been weakened due to bone resorption, you may require a bone graft or sinus lift to strengthen the underlying bone before a dental implant can be placed.
