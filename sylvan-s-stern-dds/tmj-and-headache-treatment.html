Diagnosing Your TMJ Condition

Proper diagnosis is essential for effective treatment. At Sylvan S. Stern, DDS PLC, we use state-of-the-art tools and techniques to identify the root cause of TMJ pain and dysfunction. The process typically begins with a muscle relaxation appliance that eases tension in the facial muscles. Once muscles are relaxed, we carefully observe and track the path of your jaw movement to identify irregularities or misalignments.

Next, we employ a digital recording device to measure the strength and timing of tooth contacts during jaw movement. This precise method allows us to locate bite interference, identify stress points, and determine the exact areas that require correction. By pinpointing the root cause of TMJ dysfunction, we can design targeted treatment plans that minimize discomfort and restore optimal jaw function.

Frequently Asked Questions About TMJ and Headache Treatment

  • What is TMJ, and how does it cause headaches? TMJ stands for the temporomandibular joint, which connects your jaw to your skull. When this joint becomes strained or misaligned, it can cause discomfort that radiates through the jaw, face, and head. Many people experience chronic headaches, ear pain, or tension in the neck as a result. Treating TMJ in Southfield, MI focuses on identifying the source of the problem whether muscular, structural, or bite-related and relieving the pressure that triggers these symptoms.
  • What are the most common symptoms of TMJ disorders? TMJ symptoms can vary from mild irritation to significant pain. Common signs include jaw clicking or popping, limited mouth movement, tenderness near the ears, and frequent headaches or migraines. Some patients also notice their bite feels uneven or that their jaw locks occasionally. If you’re experiencing these symptoms, scheduling an evaluation for TMJ in Southfield, MI can help determine the cause and begin appropriate treatment before the condition worsens.
  • What treatment options are available for TMJ disorders? Treatment for TMJ varies depending on the cause and severity of your symptoms. Many patients benefit from custom oral appliances designed to realign the jaw and relieve muscle strain. Others may require bite adjustments or restorative dental work to correct alignment issues. At our office, we offer a comprehensive range of solutions for TMJ in Southfield, MI, all tailored to provide lasting relief and improved comfort.
  • Can TMJ go away on its own, or does it require professional treatment? In some cases, minor TMJ discomfort can improve with rest, soft foods, or stress management techniques. However, persistent pain or recurring headaches typically indicate a deeper problem that needs professional attention. Without treatment, TMJ issues can worsen and lead to long-term joint damage.
