0 about us: Mele Family Dentistry has been caring for its patients for over 30 years in the beautiful Chestnut Hill neighborhood of Philadelphia. The practice was started by Daniel Mele, III, DMD, in 1988 and its legacy is now being carried on by his youngest son, Corey Mele, DMD.

Dr. Corey Mele’s love of dentistry began as a small child, when he eagerly shadowed his father throughout childhood. Upon receiving his dental degree from Temple University in 2018, he officially began practicing with his father after graduating. In 2020, Dr. Corey Mele, DMD took over Mele Family Dentistry and is honored to be able to uphold the family tradition of providing the highest quality of dental care to his patients.

Corey Mele, DMD signs his diploma from Temple University while his father, Daniel Mele, III, DMD proudly looks on.
