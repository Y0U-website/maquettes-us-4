Questions about fluoride safety for children are common and understandable. Based on guidance from leading pediatric and dental health organizations, fluoride is considered a safe and effective way to reduce tooth decay when it is used in appropriate amounts. Are Dental X-Rays Safe for Children? What Parents Should Know About Radiation

When a pediatric dentist recommends an X-ray, many parents have an immediate question:How much radiation will my child receive, and is it safe?Dental X-rays, also called dental radiographs, use a low level of ionizing radiation to create images of teeth, roots, jaws, and surrounding structures. Although radiation exposure should never be treated casually, modern dental X-rays are considered a valuable diagnostic tool when they are selected carefully and performed using child-appropriate techniques.
