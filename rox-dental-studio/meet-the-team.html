Halbert Campbell III, DDS

Owner and General Dentist

Dr. Campbell leads Rox Dental Studio with a patient first approach. After years as an associate, he purchased the long-standing practice in 2025 to expand access to high quality family dentistry in Roxboro. Patients appreciate his calm chairside manner, easy to understand explanations, and focus on prevention and long term oral health.

Clinical interests: preventive care, tooth colored restorations, gentle extractions, comfortable dentures and partials
