Frequently Asked Questions

How long am I enrolled?

The Dental Assistance Plan expires one year after activation. Enrollment fees and member discounts are subject to change at each renewal. Your plan’s effective date will be on file with our office.

Is the Dental Assistance Plan the same as Dental Insurance?

Our plan is not an insurance plan. Services are not covered in a traditional manner. Services are offered at a reduced fee and full payment is expected when service is rendered. The plan may not be combined with any other plans, discounts, dental insurance, or third parties.
