The practice

For more than five decades, Mid-Florida Dental Associates has been a cornerstone of oral health in Kissimmee. Generations of local families have walked through our doors, and many of our patients have been with us their entire lives.

Under new ownership and refreshed with the latest techniques and technology, our mission remains the same: compassionate, high-quality dentistry delivered in a warm, welcoming environment, with clear communication every step of the way. We proudly serve patients of all ages and offer care in both English and Spanish.

General & Family Dentistry Routine exams, professional cleanings, and preventive care for every member of the family.

Restorative Care Repairing and rebuilding teeth to restore comfort, function, and confidence.

Gum & Preventive Therapy Protecting the foundation of your smile and easing daily wear.
