Can anyone get dental implants?

It doesn’t matter whether you’re missing a couple of teeth or just one— you could be a great dental implant candidate. Good candidates have healthy gums, and their jaws are finished growing.

You might also be a perfect candidate for implants if you’re ready to replace a bridge or partial dentures, or if you’re tired of having dentures that slip. Women or men in search of a more permanent, long-lasting solution to dental work tend to be great candidates for dental implants. What Are The Steps For Getting Dental Implants In Brooklyn, NY?

When it comes to getting a dental implant, there are three main steps that will be taken to perform the procedure. Before the procedure begins, you will come in for an initial consultation, where you meet our team and choose which dental implant option fits best for you. We offer titanium or ceramic dental implants.

After your preliminary exam, our specialists will start the process:

Contact us today and get your dental implant done by highly experienced professionals in Brooklyn, NY.

  • The first step in the process is a short dental surgery. Our professionals will place an implant in your jaw. Implant here plays the part of the tooth root. We place the implant firmly into the jawbone while avoiding damaging the nerves in your mouth. After we replace your tooth with an implant, we will cover it with a healing cap that serves as a shield during the second step.
  • The second step is medically referred to osseointegration. Osseointegration is the term used to describe the process of jaw bone growing around the implant. When it is complete, the bone grows around the implants and holds it in one place, just as it holds your natural teeth. The healing cap that is inserted during the step 1 protects the dental implant during this healing process. This is considered to be the most vital part of the procedure.
  • Once the healing process is complete and your bone fully locks the implant, we will attach a custom crown that will look and function identically to your natural tooth. You will be able to smile confidently again and have a beautiful, new, natural looking tooth in place of your removed teeth.
