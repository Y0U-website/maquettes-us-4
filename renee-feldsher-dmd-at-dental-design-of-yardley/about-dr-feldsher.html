Meet Our Expert Dentists

0 Renée Feldsher, DMD Dr. Renée Feldsher is a distinguished dental professional known for her expertise, precision, and commitment to excellence in patient care. A graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, she earned top honors, including election into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon Honorary Dental Society, the Matthew Cryer Society Award in Oral Medicine, the J. George Coslet Award in Periodontics, and the National Board Achievement Award. Following dental school, she further refined her clinical skills through a General Practice Residency at West Jersey Hospital.

At Dental Design of Yardley, Dr. Feldsher and her team are committed to delivering an elevated, concierge-style dental experience, where patient well-being, comfort, and superior results are the top priorities. She approaches each case with artistry and precision, ensuring that every patient receives the highest level of personalized care.

Outside the office, Dr. Feldsher enjoys maintaining an active lifestyle through fitness classes and walks with her dog, Winston. She and her family reside in Bucks County, PA, embracing a balance of professional excellence and personal fulfillment.
