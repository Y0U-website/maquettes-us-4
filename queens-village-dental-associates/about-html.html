Queens Village Dental Associates is a family practice with an upbeat, friendly environment. Our dental team is knowledgeable and highly trained, and our dentist and staff are gentle and can put any patient at ease.

Meet the Doctor

DR. JONATHAN FLAX

There’s a reason people keep coming to Jonathan Flax, DMD. That’s because there is great satisfaction in going to a dentist you know and trust. Dr. Flax has been practicing for over 25 years. His care and concern for patients has brightened thousands of smiles.

Highly-committed to mastery in the latest in dental techniques and innovations, Dr. Flax has continued to pursue the education he began many years ago at George Washington University where he majored in Psychology. He went on to extend his education, receiving his doctorate from Tufts School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. Dr. Flax is an active member of the Academy of General Dentistry, and is licensed in New York State.

Dr. Flax grew up in New Jersey and moved to New York in 1994. He and his wife, Debbie, have three sons. Outside the office, he enjoys fishing, soccer and travel in his free time.

Dr. Flax looks forward to continuing to serve you.

DR. ERIC SNITOFSKY

When it comes to preserving, enhancing and beautifying our smiles, we all want the best. The best care. The best service. The best results. It’s as simple as that.

Eric Snitofsky, DMD is a unique kind of dentist. Dr. Snitofsky takes a gentle approach, easing any concerns you might have by providing valuable patient education. Dr. Snitofsky listens so he can best understand your particular needs. Then Dr. Snitofsky strives to provide the highest standard of personalized dental care using his skilled training in the latest advances in dental technology.

As a graduate of Queens College in Biology, Dr. Snitofsky went on to extend his education, receiving his doctorate from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. Dr. Snitofsky continued his education after receiving his doctorate by entering into Long Island Jewish’s post-doctoral residency program. Dr. Snitofsky is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry.

He looks forward to bringing out the full beauty of your smile.

DR. MARYAM AKBARI

Dr. Akbari is a full-time oral and maxillofacial surgeon here at Queens Village Dental Associates. She attended University of Pennsylvania where she earned her DMD and MPH degrees (2014). She obtained her MD degree from Louisiana State University of Shreveport in 2018. She completed her oral and maxillofacial training at Mount Sinai Hospital in NY. Dr. Akbari enjoys teaching residents and practicing wide scope Oral and Maxillofacial surgery with a focus on trauma and orthognathic surgery.

Dr. Akbari spends her free time running, biking and spending quality time with her friends and family.
