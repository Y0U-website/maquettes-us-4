How Do Dental Implants Work?

A dental implant is a metal post placed in the jaw that serves as a post to a replacement tooth. This provides a stronger alternative to bridges because they do not require the surrounding teeth for support. Dental implants normally last about 10 to 20 years with proper hygiene and regular dental visits.
