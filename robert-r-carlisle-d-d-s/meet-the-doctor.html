Dr. Robert Carlisle

Dr. Robert R. Carlisle established his dental practice in Benton in 1982 after receiving his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Tennesse College of Dentistry.

He is a graduate of Bauxite High School and Henderson State University. He is a member of various professional organizations and fraternities.

His commitment to doing his best work no matter the procedure or patient will surpass anyone’s expectations. Dr. Carlisle always informs his patients of all treatment options available and offers affordable alternatives to expensive dental procedures when needed as well as provides beautiful cosmetic dentistry.

He and his wife, Laura, are proud members of First United Methodist Church, Benton. Their pride is also obvious if asked about their twin daughters.

He and Laura have just reached grandparent status! They are so very happy to have a granddaughter. Precious little sweeties.
