Our Team

Our Team One team, one plan, one roof . Most pediatric practices refer out for orthodontics, complex restorations, and adult-style work. We don’t. We have both a pediatric specialist and a general dentist working under the same roof, with the same chart.

Dr. Viet Hoang, DDS

Dr. Viet is passionate about creating positive, lasting dental experiences for children and their families.

Originally from Vietnam, Dr. Viet moved to the United States at age 14. He went on to earn dual Bachelor’s degrees in Biology and Psychology from Rutgers University before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. He then completed advanced specialty residency training as a Pediatric Dentist through NYU Langone’s program in Kansas, where he honed his expertise in caring for children of all ages and needs.

Giving back has always been an important part of Dr. Viet’s journey. For nearly a decade, he has volunteered with Operation Smile, joining international missions to provide life-changing dental and surgical care to children in underserved communities around the world.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Viet enjoys expressing his creativity in the kitchen. Cooking is his favorite way to share love with family and friends, and he even dreams of competing on a reality cooking show one day.

Dr. Viet is excited to meet your child and looks forward to providing care with warmth, compassion, and a gentle touch.

Dr. Chad Jensen, DMD

Dr. Chad loves the artistic challenge dentistry brings, and he has always known he wanted to help people, one smile at a time.

Kids have imagination and fire, they can make fun out of anything, and Dr. Chad believes adults could learn a thing or two from that mentality. Watching children learn and enjoy learning is the best part of his job.

He’ll be the first to tell you that the care his patients receive is a team effort. At Super Smiles, two people together accomplish more than two people alone, every member of the team is always right there, ready to help in any way they can.

Dr. Chad graduated with honors in 2012 with his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. His advanced pediatric training includes pediatric oral and IV conscious sedation, emergency management of pediatric sedation, advanced pediatric life support, infant oral health, hospital sedation dentistry, preventive and interceptive orthodontics, pediatric oral pathology, and pediatric laser surgery.

Dr. Chad has been married to his wife Heather for more than a dozen years. Together they have two children, Chance, who loves to build and create new things, and Hadley, who loves to sing and make new friends. Family is and always will be his first priority. The Jensens moved to the Ozarks for the lakes and the outdoors; Dr. Chad is also a licensed pilot, and he enjoys taking his family to places they have never seen.
