Our Office

Our beautiful state of the art office houses four open bay operatories and two private room operatories as well as a TOTs room and a myofunctional therapy room.

Our team is focused on your child as a whole. How the oral cavity affects a child’s growth is important to us. Dr. Cherish has extensively trained to be well versed in tethered oral tissue and their effects on growth and development of the oral cavity and facial structures. We are able to diagnose, treat, and provide appropriate follow-up to tethered oral tissues (tongue and lip ties). We utilize highly efficient technology, a Biolase Waterlase erbium-chromium laser and a Fotona Lightwalker erbium laser which provides minimally invasive treatment with excellent results.
