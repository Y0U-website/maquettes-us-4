When Are Overdentures Indicated?

Overdentures are recommended for patients who:

A consultation with Dr. Santamarina or Dr. Rodriguez will determine the best treatment plan for your needs.

  • Struggle with loose or unstable dentures
  • Experience discomfort when chewing
  • Have significant bone loss
  • Want a more secure alternative to traditional dentures
  • Are not ready for a full fixed (All-on-X) solution
  • Want a cost-effective implant-supported option

Benefits of Implant Overdentures

  • Significantly improved denture stability
  • Better chewing ability and comfort
  • Reduced movement and irritation
  • Easier speaking and confidence
  • Removable for easy cleaning
  • More affordable than full fixed implants
  • Helps preserve bone structure

Consequences of Not Treating Loose Dentures

Implant-supported overdentures provide a long-term solution to these issues.

  • Difficulty eating and poor nutrition
  • Gum irritation and sore spots
  • Progressive bone loss
  • Reduced confidence when speaking or smiling
  • Frequent denture adjustments or replacements
