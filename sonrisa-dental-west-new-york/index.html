The practice

If you are looking for a highly trained and experienced dentist in West New York, you have come to the right place. At our practice, you will receive the highest quality dental care. Our dental office uses the latest state-of-the-art equipment and cutting edge technology and we uphold the strictest sterilization techniques.

We know that many people may feel anxious about coming to the dentist, so it is our goal to make your visit with us as pain and anxiety free as possible. We view it as our mission to educate our patients about all of their oral health care options and to help guide them to choose a treatment plan that is most suitable and appropriate for their needs.

Dr. Turanskiy graduated from Moscow Dental Institute in 1994. After relocating to the United States, he completed his degree in NYU College of Dentistry in 2001, specializing in general and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Turanskiy feels that the relationship that he develops with his patients is the key to his motivation and passion for his chosen profession. His calm and relaxed chair side manner is much appreciated by his patients.

With a keen eye for the aesthetic, Dr. Turanskiy offers a full range of services to those looking for comprehensive dental treatment. Whether it’s a simple filling or a full smile makeover, by offering personalized care, Dr. Turanskiy is able to create the exceptional smiles his patients are seeking.

Dr. Sofia Martirosov comes from a family of three generations of dental professionals. After earning her dental degree from New York University College of Dentistry in 2001, Dr. Martirosov practiced dentistry in New York, New Jersey, and Connecticut. In 2008, she co-founded Sonrisa Dental in West New York, NJ.

Throughout her career, Dr. Martirosov has taken numerous postgraduate educational courses in teeth whitening, clear aligner orthodontics, tooth-colored fillings, porcelain veneers, metal-free crowns and bridges, root canal treatment, tooth extractions, gum disease and more.

In her practice, Dr. Sofia Martirosov aims to provide the best possible results, while also focusing on her patient’s comfort and employing the least invasive methods of treatment. Her goal is to help her patients and their families achieve life-long dental health.
