Step-by-Step Full Mouth Dental Implant Process

Step 1 – Consultation and Evaluation

We examine your gums, jawbone, and any remaining teeth to plan the most effective treatment. Goals and expectations are discussed in detail.

Step 2 – Treatment Planning and Imaging

Using digital scans and 3D imaging, we design a personalized plan. This ensures implants are placed in the optimal position for stability and aesthetics.

Step 3 – Implant Placement Surgery

Titanium posts are surgically placed in your jawbone. For patients needing bone support, grafting may be done to create a strong foundation.

Step 4 – Healing and Osseointegration

The implants fuse with your jawbone over several months, forming a stable base for your new teeth. Temporary restorations may be used during this phase.

Step 5 – Abutment Placement and Final Teeth

Once healing is complete, abutments are attached to the implants. Custom-made crowns, bridges, or full arches are then secured, providing a natural, functional smile.

I. Full Arch Implants

Patients who are edentulous in one of the jaws can have full arch implants, which can restore the entire upper or lower jaw. The implants are laying in the jawbone and the post serves as a support beam for the fixed bridge or bridge. The full arch dental implants can positively impact the way a person communicates and restores their confidence as well as the functionality.

II. All-on-4 Implants

All-on-4 requires only four implants to fully restore an entire full arch. This method requires less time and the patients have a more complete smile much sooner. All-on-4 offers a more stable restoration and is less invasive.

III. Implant-Supported Dentures

Patients who build a preference for a more removable style denture, implant retained dentures can offer the same support as a more permanent style. It eliminates the slipping and the discomfort, which is a common problem built with the more traditional denture style.

IV. Mini Implants

Mini implants are built to be a permanent style implant, but due to jawbone deficiency, some cannot offer the same stability. Mini Implants offer a smaller size and stability, less invasiveness, and short healing.

Recommended Foods After Full Mouth Implants

To protect your implants during the healing phase, opt for soft, easy-to-chew foods, such as:

Scrambled eggs, tender fish, and soft meat

Soft fruits (such as bananas, berries, or melon)

Until your dentist gives you the green light, steer clear of hard, sticky, or crunchy foods. Ultimately, each of these actions minimizes the likelihood of complications and aids expeditious recovery.
