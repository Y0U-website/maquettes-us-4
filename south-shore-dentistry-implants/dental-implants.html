Simple Steps of the Dental Implant Process

Our dental implant treatment in Cudahy, WI, follows clear and easy steps to ensure comfort and accuracy.

Step 1-Consultation and Oral Check

We check your teeth, gums, and jawbone using digital scans. This helps us understand your needs and plan the right treatment. We also explain your options clearly.

Step 2-Placing the Implant

A small titanium post is placed into your jawbone. It works like a natural tooth root and creates a strong base for the new tooth.

Step 3-Healing Phase

The implant slowly joins with your jawbone over time. This step builds a strong and stable foundation for long-term support.

Step 4-Adding the Connector

Once healing is complete, a small connector (abutment) is placed on the implant. It links the implant to your new tooth and ensures a secure, stable fit for the final crown.

Step 5-Placing the Final Tooth

A custom-made crown is placed on top. It matches your natural teeth and gives you a complete, natural-looking smile.

Let’s Look at Your Options

Every smile is different. So, dental treatments should also be different. There are many implant options to match your needs. Each one is designed to feel natural and stay strong for a long time. The goal is simple. Help you eat well. Help you smile again. Help you feel normal.

Single Tooth Implants This is a great choice if you are missing one tooth. A small metal post is placed inside your jaw. After that, a crown is fixed on top. It looks like a real tooth. It works like one too. You can chew without any trouble. Your nearby teeth stay safe and untouched.

Multiple Tooth Implants Missing a few teeth in one spot can feel uncomfortable. This option helps fix that. Implants are placed to hold crowns or a bridge. It fills the gap and brings your smile back. It also keeps your jawbone strong. The good part is that other teeth do not need support this setup.

Full Mouth Implants If most of your teeth are gone, this option can help a lot. Implants are placed in the jaw to hold a full set of teeth. You can eat your favorite foods again. You can speak clearly. You can smile without thinking twice. It also helps your face keep its natural shape.

Implant-Supported Dentures Loose dentures can be frustrating. They may slip when you eat or talk. That can feel embarrassing. Implant-supported dentures stay in place. They are fixed to implants. This gives you a strong and steady fit. You can enjoy meals and talk freely.

Mini Dental Implants Mini implants are smaller than regular ones. They work well for people who have less bone in their jaw. The process is simple. It takes less time. Healing is quicker too. Even though they are small, they still give good support.

All-on-4 Dental Implants This method uses four implants to hold a full set of teeth. It is a quicker option for a full smile. Many people like it because it saves time. It also feels stable and looks natural.

Bone Grafting and Preparatory Treatments Some people may not have enough bone for implants. That is where bone grafting helps. It builds up the jawbone. This creates a strong base. It helps the implant stay secure for many years.
