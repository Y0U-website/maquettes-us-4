Dr. Mateo Vidales, DMD.

Bilingual care

Dr. Vidales speaks Spanish and sees patients in Spanish every week. Being bilingual means our Spanish-speaking neighbors get the same clear explanations and the same chance to ask questions as everyone else, without needing a family member to translate.

Trained in Florida

Dr. Mateo Vidales, DMD was born in Bogotá, Colombia. In the late 90s, he and his family came to the United States and made a life in Sarasota, Florida. He graduated from the University of Florida with a degree in microbiology and cell science, then earned his degree in dental medicine at Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine in Fort Lauderdale.

Whole-mouth philosophy

He believes in treating the whole mouth, because oral health is connected to heart health and overall well-being. That means taking the time to listen, explaining procedures in plain language, and building a plan around what actually matters to you.
