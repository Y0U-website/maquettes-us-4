Availability

After moving from Austin, Texas, to Tampa, Florida, I continued to expand my dentistry expertise. Fluent in English and Spanish, I’ve been able to effectively serve and connect with diverse communities. My pursuit of excellence in dentistry led me to complete specialized training, including the Master Basic Implant Course by Hiossen, Basic Laser Proficiency, and the Hiussin Guided Crestal Sinus Lift Course. Additionally, I’ve undertaken Master Surgery Courses, equipping me with advanced skills in dental surgery.

My philosophy is simple yet profound: “I strive to provide excellent comprehensive oral health care to improve the well-being of my patients in the same manner I would like myself and my family treated.” This guiding principle reflects my commitment to delivering the highest standard of care and fostering a trusting relationship with my patients.

Outside the dental office, I cherish spending time with my family, cycling, taking my kids to the park, and enjoying the beach on my off days. These activities not only provide a balance to my professional life but also keep me grounded and connected to my community.
