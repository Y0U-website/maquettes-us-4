About

Ensuring that our patients will always receive the latest most effective treatments possible

Dr. Vernon Miles Lew grew up in the Bay Area. After attending Lynbrook High School, he attended the University of California at Santa Cruz where he received degrees in Chemistry and Economics. He then attended Loyola University College of Dentistry, in Chicago, where he received his Doctorate degree in Dental Surgery.

Having practiced at the current Saratoga location for more than 20 years, Dr. Lew has maintained a steady family of patients through a combination of state of the art technology, sensible conservative treatment, and genuine devotion to the unique needs of each individual. He applies his unique blend of artistic and technical skills to produce long lasting and aesthetic results for his many patients. Dr. Lew is licensed by the Dental Board of California and is a member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Society, and Santa Clara Dental Society.
