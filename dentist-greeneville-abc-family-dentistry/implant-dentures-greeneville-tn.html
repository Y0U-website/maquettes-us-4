Frequently Asked Questions

  • What Is the Difference Between Traditional Dentures and Implant Dentures? Traditional dentures rest directly on top of your gums and rely on natural suction or adhesives to stay in place, which can sometimes lead to slipping. Implant dentures are securely attached to titanium posts placed in your jawbone, offering a much more stable fit that functions more like natural teeth.
  • Does Dr. Leah Perform the Entire Procedure? Yes. At our Greeneville office, Dr. Leah is the exclusive dentist who places and restores dental implants. From your initial consultation to the final fitting of your dentures, you will be under her direct, personalized care.
  • Can I Eat Anything I Want Immediately After the Procedure? While implant dentures drastically improve your chewing power in the long run, your jaw needs time to heal right after the implants are placed. Dr. Leah will provide you with a temporary soft-food diet guideline to protect your implants during the initial healing phase. Once fully healed, you will be able to enjoy a much wider variety of foods with confidence.
  • Do Implant Dentures Last Forever? Dental implants are designed to be highly durable and long-lasting. However, like natural teeth, their longevity depends heavily on excellent oral hygiene, routine dental cleanings, and your overall health. Additionally, the denture attachment itself may experience normal wear and tear over the years and require minor adjustments or maintenance.
