Dedicated to providing the highest quality dental care for babies, toddlers, tweens, and teens.

The practice

Smiles creates a welcoming, kid-friendly environment starting when you walk in the door. Our waiting room is filled with video games and wall art to make your child’s dental experience fun.

We understand that you, or your child, may feel anxious about visiting the dentist. Drs. Freet, Landers, and Patel are passionate about making your child’s visit to the dentist a healthy and positive experience

We utilize the latest technology including AI to diagnose tooth decay and other oral health issues to protect and preserve your child’s teeth.

Smiles Children’s Dental Center is proud to accept most dental plans including Soonercare, Dentaquest, and Librety Dental, Delta Dental, Aetna, Blue Cross / Blue Shield, Connection Dental and many others. Our experienced staff members will also file your claim as a courtesy for you. Payment for professional services is due at the time that dental treatment is provided. Every effort will be made…
