Dr. John Landers

Dr. John Landers is a general dentist who, along with his wife Gretchen and their children—daughter Gentry and sons Derrick and Parker—are proud to call Tulsa home. A graduate of Edison High School in 1989, Dr. Landers earned his bachelor’s degree from Tulane University in 1993 before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry in 1997, where he graduated as valedictorian.

In 1999, Dr. Landers purchased Dr. Robert Gerety’s dental practice and practiced privately in Henryetta, Oklahoma for more than a decade. In 2016, he joined Children’s Dental Health Center, where he enjoys caring for patients of all ages.

As a father of a son with cerebral palsy, Dr. Landers has a special heart for patients with special needs and their families. His professional affiliations include the American Dental Association, Oklahoma Dental Association, Tulsa County Dental Association, Tulsa Endodontic Study Club, and the Academy of General Dentistry.
