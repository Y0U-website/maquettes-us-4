Dr. David Hudec, Jr. | Managing Partner

Dr. David Hudec, Jr. is proud to be the managing partner of Mayfield Smiles having grown up in Cleveland. He earned his undergraduate degree at Canisius College in Buffalo, New York, and graduated from Case Western Reserve School of Dental Medicine. He provides comprehensive family dentistry to patients in Mayfield and surrounding communities.
