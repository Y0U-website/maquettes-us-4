What to Expect During Your Free Consultation

Step into Confidence and Expertise

Imagine entering a welcoming space where your aspirations for a confident smile meet state-of-the-art dental expertise. During your dental implant consultation, expect conversations that go beyond standard dental charts. We’ll delve into your unique story, understanding your desires and goals to create a personalized treatment plan.

Your journey begins with a one-on-one meeting with our Team Leader/Implant Consultant. It’s crucial to understand your specific goals so that the doctor can tailor your treatment plan precisely to your needs. The Team Leader will take the time to learn about you, followed by two essential imaging scans: a panoramic scan and a CBCT scan. The CBCT scan provides a three-dimensional view, allowing the doctor to assess the availability of bone, including its height and width.

Personalized Treatment Recommendations

The doctor will review these images with you, explaining the details and providing the best treatment recommendations. Once all your clinical questions are addressed, the final step involves working with the Team Leader to develop a funding plan that suits your budget. We offer multiple financing options and will assist you in finding the best one to make your dream smile a reality.

A Comfortable and Informative Experience

We understand that many patients have had unpleasant dental experiences in the past. Our team is dedicated to ensuring you feel well-informed and comfortable throughout your visit. Our goal is to empower you with all the necessary information to make the best decision for your dental health.

Thank you for choosing us. We look forward to meeting you and embarking on this transformative journey together!
