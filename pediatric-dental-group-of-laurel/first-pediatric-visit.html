Bring Your Child to a Fun and Safe Environment for

Their First Pediatric Visit

In agreeance with the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, we recommend scheduling your childís first dental appointment around their first birthday.

At Pediatric Dental Group, we strive to make the first visit as enjoyable as possible so that your child will look forward to trips to the dentist office in the future. Our doctors and staff are dedicated to making you and your child comfortable during your time with us. We understand that comfortability builds trust and limits worry for future visits. Our doctors and staff will explain everything in detail, to both child and parent, and answer any questions. Please feel free to discuss any fears or anxiety.
