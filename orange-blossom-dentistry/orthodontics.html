What to Expect During Orthodontic Treatment

The first step is a comprehensive evaluation. At Orange Blossom Dentistry, we review your dental history, take necessary imaging, and assess the current position of your teeth and bite. This gives us a complete picture before recommending any treatment path. If Invisalign is the right fit, we take digital impressions to create your custom aligner series and map out your full treatment timeline.

Throughout treatment, you will check in with us frequently so we are able to monitor your progress and make any adjustments needed. Our goal is to make the process as smooth and comfortable as possible, and we are here to answer questions at every step. For patients who are anxious about visits, our sedation dentistry options are available to make appointments more comfortable. Once treatment is complete, retainers help preserve your results and protect the investment you have made in your smile.

Many patients find that a straighter smile opens the door to other cosmetic improvements they had been holding off on. If you have ever considered porcelain veneers or other smile enhancements, orthodontic treatment can create an ideal foundation for those procedures.
