When Additional Preparation Is Needed

Not every patient has enough bone density to support an implant right away. If needed, we offer:

  • Bone grafting to rebuild and strengthen the jaw before implant placement
  • Sinus lift procedures for upper jaw implants, when the sinus cavity needs to be repositioned to make room for adequate bone

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing multiple teeth or a full arch, we also offer implant-supported dentures, available in two forms:

  • Snap-in dentures — removable, secured to implants for a stable, comfortable fit
  • Fixed dentures — permanently attached to implants for a non-removable, natural-feeling result

Why Preventive Care Matters More Than Most People Realize

Many patients put off dental visits until something hurts. That’s understandable, but it often leads to more extensive treatment than would have been necessary with consistent preventive care. The American Dental Association recommends that the frequency of visits be tailored to each patient’s unique risk factors, including their history with cavities, gum disease, and overall health. Patients with conditions like diabetes or a history of periodontal disease may benefit from more frequent checkups.

Beyond your teeth and gums, oral health has a documented connection to your overall health. Research published by the American Heart Association links gum disease to a higher risk of heart and blood vessel disease, noting that inflammation caused by gum infections can enter the bloodstream and contribute to wider systemic effects. Keeping up with preventive dentistry isn’t just about avoiding cavities; it’s about maintaining your health from the inside out.
