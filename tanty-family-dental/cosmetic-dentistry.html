Clear Aligners

Be bold with a dazzling, straight-toothed smile

Don’t put your life on hold with traditional braces—innovative clear aligners offer a discreet and comfortable way to achieve a straighter, more confident smile. Unlike metal braces, clear aligners are nearly invisible and completely removable, making them a perfect fit for your lifestyle. Tanty Family Dental uses only the best brands, including SureSmile and Invisalign.

General Information on Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a custom-made series of transparent plastic trays that gradually shift teeth over time. Often, they are preferred to traditional braces for their convenience and aesthetic appeal. Tanty Family Dental will start with a consultation where we will assess your oral health and discuss treatment goals. We’ll take impressions of your teeth to create a series of aligners. Each set will be worn for about two weeks before moving on to the next set. Wear the aligners for at least 22 hours a day to achieve the best results.

Benefits of Clear Aligners

Clear aligners offer a comfortable, near-invisible way to straighten your teeth compared to traditional braces. With the removable design, you can enjoy the freedom to eat and drink without restrictions and maintain great oral hygiene. Clear aligners empower you with a straighter smile and better oral health, all while keeping your treatment discreet.

What Can Clear Aligners Treat?

All of our clear aligners, including SureSmile and Invisalign, can effectively treat a variety of dental conditions, including:

  • Misaligned teeth
  • Overbite
  • Underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite

Clear Aligner Maintenance

  • Clean aligners regularly with a soft-bristled toothbrush and unscented soap.
  • Remove the aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water.
  • Brush and floss your teeth before putting them back in.
  • Avoid using anything that can stain the aligners when cleaning.
  • Store the aligners in their case when not in use.
  • Avoid exposing them to high temperatures or direct sunlight.
  • Follow orthodontist instructions for wearing and switching out the aligners.

Veneer Services

A low-maintenance, cost-effective way to create a perfect smile is with Tanty Family Dental’s porcelain or composite tooth veneers. We work with you to find the best possible solution to all of your smile concerns.

General Information on Veneers

Veneers are thin shells, crafted from either porcelain or composite material, that seamlessly bond to the front of your teeth. Veneers go beyond whitening, concealing chips, cracks, and discoloration for a flawlessly radiant smile. Veneers offer long-lasting results, leaving you with a smile you love for years to come.

The Process of Getting Veneers

The process starts with a quick x-ray to assess your teeth. Then, depending on your choice of porcelain or composite, we’ll either take a mold for custom-crafted porcelain veneers (requiring a second appointment) or apply the resin directly for a beautiful, long-lasting smile in just one appointment. No matter your choice, maintaining good oral hygiene will ensure your veneers continue to shine brightly!

Are Veneers Permanent?

Veneers offer lasting beauty. Unlike whitening, they resist staining and can last for decades. Placement requires removing some enamel, making it a semi-permanent solution. However, veneers can be removed to replace or repair damaged surfaces.

When to get Veneers

Veneers can help resolve a myriad of dental-related issues! Consider getting in touch for a consultation about veneers if you experience:

  • Stained teeth
  • Bruxism (teeth grinding)
  • Chipped or broken teeth
  • Misaligned, uneven, or irregularly shaped teeth
  • Gap teeth

Dental Bonding

Transform your smile with safe, effective, and affordable dental bonding

If you are self-conscious about chips, cracks, gaps, or discoloration in your teeth, then quick and non-invasive dental bonding might be the solution you’re looking for.

General Information on Dental Bonding

The Dental Bonding Procedure

The dental bonding procedure is generally quick and comfortable, taking 30 60 minutes per tooth.

You will be provided with instructions for caring for your bonded teeth. With proper care, your new smile can last for many years, though bonding may need to be repaired or replaced over time. To maintain the results of your dental bonding, it’s important to practice good oral hygiene and avoid habits that can cause damage or staining. Benefits of Dental Bonding Dental bonding is quick, affordable, and requires no anesthesia. Because this process is durable and stain-resistant, your new smile can last for many years.

  • Before your appointment, Tanty Family Dental will thoroughly examine your teeth and discuss your treatment goals and expectations.
  • X-rays or impressions of your teeth will be taken to ensure a precise fit for the bonding material.
  • During the bonding procedure, we will carefully clean your teeth, removing any surface stains and rough spots.
  • Composite resin will be molded to your teeth and shaped to match the natural contours.
  • The material will then be cured and hardened.
  • After hardening, they will be trimmed and polished for a smooth and natural appearance that blends seamlessly with your other teeth.
  • After the procedure, you can expect some sensitivity to hot and cold temperatures.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening produces a brilliant smile by cleaning stained tooth enamel. Tanty Family Dental utilizes advanced, powerful whitening gels to dramatically brighten your smile without the sensitivity you feel from drugstore options.

General Information on Kör Whitening

Traditional teeth whitening methods promise a dazzling smile but can be far from impressive. They often become ineffective after 25 – 35 minutes, and these unsatisfying results can prompt users to try these products multiple times, causing teeth sensitivity and gum irritation. KöR whitening gel at Tanty Family Dental is refrigerated, which allows powerful whitening agents to work for a significantly longer 6 – 10 hours. The gel penetrates deeper and removes more stains for a gorgeous, longer-lasting smile.

The Teeth Whitening Procedure

Achieving brilliantly white teeth is easier and more effective than ever before with KöR:

  • The process begins by consulting with our experienced dental team to discuss your health and teeth whitening goals.
  • Impressions will be taken to create whitening trays that ensure the whitening gel is evenly distributed on your teeth.
  • KöR uses specially formulated whitening gels that are continuously refrigerated to maintain their potency. A series of one-hour whitening sessions will be scheduled.
  • Following your in-office whitening sessions, we’ll provide you with custom trays and KöR whitening gel for home use.
  • Maintaining good oral hygiene and attending regular dental checkups is crucial for long-term effects.
