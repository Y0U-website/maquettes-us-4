Tiny Teeth, Big Smiles

Once your child’s baby teeth start to emerge fully, we may recommend gentle teeth cleanings, polishing, and removing plaque, tartar, or stain buildup. We will also help teach your child how to maintain good oral hygiene, including brushing and flossing techniques.

Common Pediatric Dentistry Procedures

Tanty Family Dental will always seek the best solution for repairing your child’s damaged tooth. Common restorative procedures we provide for kids include:

  • Cleanings to remove plaque, tartar, and stains from the teeth.
  • Fillings to repair cavities with composite resin or amalgam.
  • Sealants that provide a thin coating on the chewing surfaces of molars to prevent decay.
  • Fluoride treatments to strengthen tooth enamel and prevent cavities.
  • Space maintainers to preserve space for permanent teeth and prevent crowding.
  • Tooth extractions to remove damaged or decayed teeth.
  • Crowns to restore a tooth’s strength, function, and appearance.

Same-Day Crowns

Dental crowns cap dental implants or teeth that have undergone a root canal procedure. An intraoral camera and computer-aided design technology allow us to create a 3D model of your tooth. Using this and an in-office CEREC single-visit crown machine, we create a crown that fits your tooth perfectly. The crown is then bonded to your teeth for a flawless finish.

General Information on CEREC Crowns

Unlike traditional methods, which require multiple appointments and uncomfortable impressions, CEREC ceramic or resin crowns take less time and provide better-fitting, more natural-looking results.

Benefits of CEREC Crowns
