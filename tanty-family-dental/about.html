What is a CEREC Machine?

CEREC stands for Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics. This machine uses computer-aided design technology to produce dental restorations in one day. Can Solea Lasers Remove Old Fillings? The Solea laser can be used for most dental procedures, including routine fillings, removal of old fillings, crowns, and more. How Long Does a 3D Dental Scan Take? The process takes less than 20 seconds to complete. The machine rotates around your head, taking 3D images of the jaw, skull, soft tissues, and teeth.
