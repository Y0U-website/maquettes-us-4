About Azure Dental

What You Can Expect

At Azure Dental, we offer comprehensive dental care for your entire family. Our patients can expect a charismatic and well-trained staff to help make their dental appointments as enjoyable as possible. We strive to uphold the highest standard of care while providing patients with a premium dental experience. Dr. Rosamond T. Sonnen provides treatment in all aspects of general dentistry. She also has specialized training in cosmetic dentistry and performs many other procedures including Invisalign, root canal therapy, and oral surgery.

The Azure Dental Team provides high quality treatment while keeping patient comfort as our top priority. Whether your need is for an emergency appointment or to establish a new dental home, Dr. Sonnen and her staff will ensure you get the best treatment and options available. We understand no two patients are alike. Each patient’s treatment is tailored to meet their unique concerns and desires. The Azure Dental Team is dedicated to helping its patients maintain healthy smiles for life.

Meet Our Team

Dr. Rosamond Sonnen

Founder & Dentist

Dr. Sonnen was born and raised in a small town in Texas. It was evident at a young age she had an interest in the dental field. As an infant, she always accompanied her mother to her dental visits. As she grew older, her dentist anticipated her appointments and would create chores and allow her to assist while she was there for her check ups. Dr. Sonnen, of course, grew up and moved to Dallas where she attended Southern Methodist University and graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science degree in Chemistry.

After college, Dr. Sonnen attended prestigious Baylor College of Dentistry Texas A&M University Health Science Center. While at Baylor, she not only completed the core curriculum for general dentistry but also took additional courses in cosmetic and implant dentistry. During her second year, she conducted research on endodontic materials and the results were later published in the Journal of Endodontics. She was the recipient of many awards but most notably, the Academy of Osseointegration Implant Award and General Dentistry Program Award which are only awarded to the top student per class.
