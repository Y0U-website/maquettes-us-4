Lakewood Dentist Dr. David Collins

Dr. David Collins

Your expert Lakewood, CO dentist

Since first beginning his career, Dr. Collins has incorporated the latest technology into his patient care. Through ongoing professional development and peer groups, he keeps his finger on the pulse of new and evolving treatment options, such as dental implants, aesthetic dentistry, and Invisalign.

His advanced skills in comprehensive cosmetic procedures, gives him the expertise required to treat a broad range of problems that other dentists might refer to outside practitioners. Ultimately, his goal is to provide the highest quality care in a comfortable and caring environment.

After an intensive general practice residency at St. Joseph’s hospital in Denver, he joined an established Lakewood practice, which he took over in 1997. He grew the practice into the high-tech and professional Belmar Park Dental Care that patients know today. Dr. Collins and his wife, Lynette, have three children, and together they enjoy Colorado’s many outdoor sports.
