The Process of Getting Implant-Retained Dentures

Our dentist at Belmar Park Dental Care, P.C. helps you get a full smile in one visit.

All on Four dentures at Belmar Park Dental Care, P.C. in Lakewood can replace a full set of teeth or just the upper or lower arch, depending on your needs. Life is busy enough, so we make the process easy for you.

You don’t have to wait a long time to improve your smile or replace your dentures; we offer teeth in a day solutions for your convenience.

  • Step 1 At the initial consultation, we’ll examine your teeth and see if you’re a candidate for implant-retained dentures. You must be in good health and have adequate bone to support the implant.
  • Step 2 Using a 3D scan, we’ll create your new set of teeth, and you can view your future smile before we begin. After the dental implants are placed, we’ll insert temporary dentures the same day to give you a full smile.
  • Step 3 Once the mouth heals and the dental implants fuse with the jawbone, the permanent All on Four dentures are placed. We’ll check them for fit and comfort to ensure that your new, functional smile works effectively.

FAQ’s about Implant-Retained Dentures

  • Can dentures be permanently attached? Yes, dentures can be permanently attached with the All on Four system. Four titanium posts are safely inserted in the place of tooth roots to join with the jawbone and support the upper or lower set of teeth. Implant-retained dentures are not removable and stay in place for a permanent tooth replacement solution. You don’t have to replace each tooth individually because four dental implants retain a full arch of teeth. The process is quick, and there’s minimal discomfort.
  • How long do implant dentures last? While dental implants are considered a permanent tooth replacement in that the metal post fuses with the jawbone permanently, the denture itself (tooth replacement prosthesis) can last 10-15 years or longer if you maintain great oral care. It’s important to continue brushing and flossing, and be sure to visit your dentist every six months for regular exams.
  • Do All on Four look real? Yes, All on Four dentures look real because they are made of a high-quality material and custom-crafted to match your appearance. All on Four dentures are customized in shape, size, and color.
