Services

  • Restorative These procedures repair and return your mouth to as close to its ideal state as possible. Our experience enables us to accomplish simple porcelain fillings, crowns, and even complex implant restorations and bridges efficiently and comfortably.
  • Preventative Perhaps the most important assistance we can give you is determining why problems develop and helping you prevent them by making you aware of how you can help yourself. We will help analyze your habits, oral hygiene techniques and dental health. We will provide you with simple advice, sealants, nightguards, cleanings, fluoride, desensitization and remineralization treatments. when necessary.
  • Periodontal The most common disease in the world is periodontal disease (gum disease): 80 % of the population has some symptoms of periodontal disease. We work to both prevent and control periodontal disease by providing you with advice on oral health care techniques as well as the tools to take care of yourself. In addition, we offer treatments including routine cleanings, scaling and root planing, and localized antibiotics to help control any problem areas.
  • Cosmetic What do you notice when you meet someone? Their smile and their eyes. We can improve your smile with minor reshaping, bonding, or a complete smile makeover using whitening, porcelain corwns, veneers, and implants. These changes can make a huge difference in your self confidence.
  • Cancer Screening Routine cancer screenings, treatment plans and referrals to specialists for additional treatment.
