All-On-4s Dental Implants Dentists

It’s important to note that the suitability of the All-On-4s technique may vary based on individual factors such as overall oral health, bone density, and specific treatment goals.

  • Full Arch Restoration: All-On-4s Dental Implants are designed to support a full arch of replacement teeth, either in the upper or lower jaw. A custom-made dental prosthesis, often in the form of a fixed bridge or denture, is securely attached to the four implants. This provides a functional and natural-looking set of teeth, restoring the appearance, chewing ability, and speech function of the patient.
  • Immediate Function: One of the significant advantages of the All-On-4s technique is that, in many cases, the replacement teeth can be attached to the implants on the same day of the implant placement surgery.
  • Bone Support: The angulation and strategic placement of the implants in the All-On-4s technique take advantage of the existing bone structure. This can often eliminate the need for bone grafting procedures, even in cases where patients have experienced significant bone loss.
