What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small pieces of metal that are surgically implanted into your mouth. These tiny, screw-shaped titanium implants are able to bond permanently with your jaw tissue, and they function as artificial tooth roots. A variety of “implant restorations” can be attached to this implant to restore your smile. Single-tooth implants use one dental crown to replace a single tooth, but multiple teeth or even a full mouth of teeth can be replaced with dental implants.

Single-Tooth Implants

Single-tooth implants are great for restoring one or several missing teeth. They are the simplest type of implant, consisting of one implant post and a porcelain dental crown. Implant-supported restorations often include custom dental crowns designed to blend naturally with your smile.

Full-Arch Implants

Full-arch implants use a series of 4-6 dental implants to support a set of implant dentures. These dentures snap onto the implants, so they won’t move or shift, and they are much more durable compared to traditional dentures.

The Dental Implant Treatment Process

Initial Consultation Interested in dental implants? The first step is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Martinez. He will evaluate your oral health and bone strength, and take x-rays to determine if implants are right for you. He will also discuss your other options, such as partial dentures and dental bridges.

Implant Placement Once you’ve been approved for treatment, you’ll come back to our office to have your implants placed. Dr. Martinez will clean and numb your mouth, then create a small opening in your gums and jaw, and place each implant based on your treatment plan. You’ll be sent home to rest and recover.

Creating Your Restoration It will take 1-2 weeks to heal from your initial treatment, and about 3-6 months for your implants to bond with your jaw bone. During this time, you will come in for follow-ups so that Dr. Martinez can check on your recovery process and begin building your implant restoration, such as a dental crown.

Final Restoration Placement Once your mouth is fully healed and your implant restoration has been completed, you’ll come in for your final appointment. Dr. Martinez will check the fit of your restoration, then bond it into place permanently to restore your smile.
