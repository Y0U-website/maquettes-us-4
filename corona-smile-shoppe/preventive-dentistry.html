Preventive Dentistry Benefits

Prevent Oral Diseases

Why is preventive dentistry important? First and foremost, it allows you to avoid oral health conditions like gum disease (gingivitis) and tooth decay (cavities). It also provides the building blocks for strong at-home oral hygiene habits.

Save Time & Money

By avoiding common oral health diseases and complications, you can protect your time and budget by avoiding the need for further restorative treatments , such as fillings for cavities.

Preventive Treatment Options

Dental Sealants Dental sealants are used to prevent cavities. They are usually applied to the rear teeth, since the molars have large pits and grooves that can catch food and bacteria, contributing to cavities. Sealants consist of a durable layer of dental resin. This resin is applied directly to the tooth in a liquid form. It is then hardened with a UV light to protect your tooth from cavities.

Fluoride Treatments Fluoride treatments are usually done right after teeth cleanings, and while they’re mostly recommended for kids, adults who are cavity-prone can enjoy the benefits, too. In this process, Dr. Martinez will apply a fluoride-rich substance to your teeth, leave it in place for a few minutes, then rinse it away. This attracts minerals to the enamel, which promotes overall tooth strength.

Deep Cleaning (Scaling & Root Planing) We offer treatment for gum disease at Corona Smile Shoppe, such as deep cleanings, which are also called “scaling & root planing.” This procedure is required for patients with gum disease, and helps halt or reverse the progress of the disease. Dr. Martinez will clean deeply between your teeth and gums to eliminate bacteria and prevent further bacterial buildup. One half of your mouth is cleaned at each appointment, so this will take two appointments at Corona Smile Shoppe.

Oral Cancer Screenings Oral cancer is rare, but it’s very important to recognize the signs of this disease as early as possible. That’s why Dr. Martinez provides an oral cancer screening with every six-month exam in Corona. He will look for lesions, bumps, discolored tissue, and any other sign that there may be an issue.

Teeth Cleanings & Oral Exams

Teeth cleanings and oral exams are the most important part of preventive dentistry. During your routine appointment at Corona Smile Shoppe, your hygienist will gently clean your teeth and take x-rays, and Dr. Martinez will examine your mouth to make sure your smile is healthy.
