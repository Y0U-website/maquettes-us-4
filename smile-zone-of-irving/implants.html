2. Guided Tissue Regeneration (GTR): Building Your Foundation

When Needed:

  • Socket preservation post-extraction
  • Sinus augmentation for upper implants
  • Ridge augmentation for narrow bone
  • Periodontal bone loss

Regeneration Techniques:

A. Socket Preservation

  • Extract tooth with minimal trauma
  • Fill socket with:
  • Bovine bone mineral (90 % success)
  • Cover with collagen membrane
  • Suture for primary closure

B. Ridge Augmentation

  • Healing: 2-3 months before implant placement
  • Sinus Lift
  • Key Approaches:
  • 1. Lateral Window: Access through cheek side
  • 2. Vertical lifting Osteotome: Minimal access through socket
