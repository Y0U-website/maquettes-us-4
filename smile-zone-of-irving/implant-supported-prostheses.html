1. Implant Bridges: The Fixed Solution

What They Replace: 3+ consecutive missing teeth Required Implants: 2-6 titanium implants (fewer than individual crowns)

The Process:

  • Digital Planning: CBCT scan + 3D surgical guide design
  • Implant Placement: Flapless surgery (minimal discomfort) when applicable.
  • Healing Phase: 4-6 months for osseointegration
  • Prosthesis Fabrication:
  • 1. Zirconia crowns
  • 2. Pink gingival porcelain for natural gumline
  • Final Attachment: Screw-retained 0

Ideal For:

  • Patients with multiple missing teeth in a row
  • Those wanting permanent, non-removable solution
  • Avoiding partial denture clasps

Benefits:

  • ⏱️ Efficiency: Fewer implants than individual crowns
  • 💎 Durability: Withstands 500+ psi bite force
  • 😁 Aesthetics: Seamless gum-to-tooth transition

2. Snap-On Dentures (Overdentures): The Removable Revolution

  • 1. Implants: 2-6 strategically placed
  • 2. Attachments: Locator® or ball abutments
  • 3. Prosthesis: Acrylic denture with precision clips
  • 1. Secure Fit: Audible click confirms engagement
  • 2. Easy Removal: Gentle pressure releases prosthesis
  • 3. Hygiene Access: Clean implants and gums thoroughly
