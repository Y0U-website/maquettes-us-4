Unlock the Secrets of Your Smile: Advanced Diagnostic Services Explained

At SmileZone of Irving, we believe accurate diagnosis is the foundation of exceptional dental care. Our cutting-edge diagnostic services uncover hidden problems before they escalate – saving you time, money, and discomfort. Discover how our technology and expertise reveal what’s really happening in your mouth:

1. Comprehensive Oral Exams: Your Mouth’s Full Health Scan

  • 1. Visual Inspection: Magnified examination of teeth, gums, tongue, and soft tissues
  • 2. Periodontal Charting: Precise measurement of gum pocket depths (1-3mm = healthy)
  • 3. Oral Cancer Screening: Systematic check for abnormal lesions using VELscope® fluorescence technology
  • 4. TMJ/Bite Analysis: Assessment of jaw joint function and tooth wear patterns
  • Why It Matters: Detects cavities, gum disease, oral cancer, and bite issues in their earliest stages – when treatment is simplest and least invasive.
