Clear Aligners for a Straight, Beautiful Smile

One of the primary concerns people often have about dental braces is the aesthetic impact of the metalwork on their smile. Especially for adults, the prospect of wearing unattractive metal braces for long periods of time can be very discouraging.

Exceptional Dental offers Invisalign® and REVEAL® clear aligners as a solution to traditional metal braces. These are invisible braces that offer an aligning system designed to straighten teeth fast. It also contains no metal.

Treatment consists of a series of custom-made aligning trays. The dentist changes the trays every several weeks to fit the new tooth configuration. In addition to the reduced visual impact, these almost invisible braces can be temporarily removed for important occasions – meaning that treatment duration is patient-controlled. A great number of people report complete satisfaction with both the treatment and the stunning results.

Interested in improving your smile? Reach out to one of our offices today to discuss your options.
