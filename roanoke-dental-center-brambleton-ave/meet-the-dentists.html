Dr. Aaron Smith, DDS

Comprehensive implant dentistry, planned and completed with one team.

Dr. Aaron Smith leads the dental implant program at Roanoke Dental Center, serving patients at both our Electric Road and Brambleton offices. His clinical focus is comprehensive implant dentistry, from single-tooth replacement to full implant-supported restorations, planned with CBCT 3D imaging and carried through from placement to final restoration in-house, so patients complete their entire implant journey with one team.

A Virginia dentist with deep roots in the Roanoke and Smith Mountain Lake communities, Dr. Smith is known for taking time with patients, explaining options plainly, setting honest expectations, and building treatment plans around what each patient actually needs. Implant consultations with Dr. Smith are available at both locations; ask your Roanoke Dental Center dentist or call to schedule.
