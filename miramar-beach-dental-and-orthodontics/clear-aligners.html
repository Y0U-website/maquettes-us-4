A Discreet Way to Straighten Your Smile

What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are removable trays that are custom-designed to gradually straighten teeth over time. Each set of aligners is worn for a specific period before progressing to the next, guiding teeth into proper alignment through controlled and predictable movement.

SureSmile aligners are designed using advanced digital planning technology, allowing for precise treatment planning and efficient results. Because aligners are removable, patients can continue eating their favorite foods and maintain their regular oral hygiene routine.

Benefits of SureSmile Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are popular among teens and adults who want a subtle orthodontic solution. The aligners are nearly invisible when worn, making them an appealing option for patients who prefer a low-profile treatment. Since aligners are removable, brushing and flossing remain easy and comfortable throughout treatment.

SureSmile aligners are custom-fit for each patient and are designed to deliver predictable tooth movement. Many patients also appreciate the flexibility aligners provide, especially for work, school, and social settings.

Clear Aligners vs Traditional Braces

Clear aligners and traditional braces both straighten teeth, but they are designed for different orthodontic needs. Clear aligners are often recommended for mild to moderate alignment concerns and for patients who can consistently wear their aligners as directed. Traditional metal braces are typically better suited for more complex cases that require advanced tooth or bite correction.

During your orthodontic consultation, our team will evaluate your smile and discuss whether SureSmile clear aligners or traditional braces will provide the best outcome for your individual needs.

What to Expect During Clear Aligner Treatment

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive orthodontic evaluation and digital scans of your teeth. These scans are used to create a personalized series of aligners designed to gradually move your teeth into proper alignment.

You will wear your aligners daily and switch to new sets as directed by your dental team. Periodic check-ins allow us to monitor your progress and ensure treatment stays on track. After completing treatment, retainers help protect your results and maintain your newly straightened smile.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Clear aligners can be very effective for mild to moderate orthodontic concerns. For more complex cases, traditional braces may be recommended to achieve the best outcome.

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Treatment time varies by case, but many patients complete clear aligner therapy in 12 to 18 months.

Do I have to wear aligners all the time?

Aligners should be worn for most of the day and night, except when eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, or flossing.

Are clear aligners noticeable?

SureSmile aligners are designed to be clear and discreet, making them much less noticeable than traditional braces.

Can teens use clear aligners?

Yes. Clear aligners can be a great option for teens who are good candidates and able to wear their aligners consistently.

How do I know if clear aligners are right for me?

An orthodontic consultation is the best way to determine whether clear aligners or traditional braces are the right choice for your smile.
