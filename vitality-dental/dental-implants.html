The Dental Implant Consultation Process

Step 1: Complimentary CT Scan When you arrive, our team will take a complimentary CT scan to capture detailed 3D images of your teeth, jawbone, and surrounding structures.

Step 2: Medical History Review A dental assistant will review your medical history, current medications, and any concerns you have about treatment.

Step 3: Doctor’s Evaluation The doctor will review your CT scan and visually examine your mouth. No sharp instruments will be used, and there will be no poking around during the consultation.

Step 4: Treatment Options Based on your scan and oral health needs, Dr. Holmes will explain your dental implant options and recommend a personalized treatment plan.

Step 5: Questions and Next Steps You will have time to ask questions, review the treatment process, and discuss the next steps for restoring your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Has Dr. Holmes completed any additional training or certifications for placing dental implants? Yes, Dr. Holmes has received extensive implant training and has completed over 200 hours of additional completed courses for implants or implant-related procedures. He is also an International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) Fellow.
  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the dental implant process is. During your procedure, we use local anesthesia and also provide sedation to help you feel relaxed and pain-free. After your implant is placed, it’s normal to experience some mild soreness or swelling for a few days, but this can usually be managed with over-the-counter or prescribed medication. Most patients find the discomfort is less than they expected and are back to their normal routine within a few days.
