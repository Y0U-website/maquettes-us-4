Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment are accepted? We accept, cash, check, and credit.
  • When can I start using my membership? Your membership is effective immediately!
  • How do I schedule an appointment for preventative care? You can schedule an appointment online or by calling our office. Schedule Appointment
  • How can I use my member exclusive discount for treatment? Simply schedule an appointment with our office and be sure to tell us you’re a member. When you arrive, we’ll make sure your membership is current and you will be eligible for instant savings off our regular fees.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Priority Care Membership is NOT a dental insurance plan; it can only be used at Vitality Dental. Membership is non-transferable.
  • Can I combine a membership with other dental coverage? No, membership cannot be combined with other coverage.
