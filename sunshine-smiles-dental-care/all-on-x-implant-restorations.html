Superior, Full-Arch Restoration

Are you in search of a long-lasting, gold-standard solution to replace a full arch of missing teeth? At Sunshine Smiles Dental Care , we proudly offer All-on-X implant restorations that deliver stability and function comparable to healthy, natural teeth. Our experienced dentists personalize every step of treatment to enhance your smile and your quality of life.

What Are All-on-X Implant Restorations?

All-on-X implants are a game-changer for those needing to replace a complete arch of teeth. They rely on as few as four dental implants per arch for support, offering a highly stable and secure alternative to traditional dentures.

All-on-X implant restorations can be ideal for patients seeking a stable solution to restore their smiles or for those with bone loss that may interfere with traditional implant placement. Your new restoration will closely mimic the appearance and function of natural teeth, enhancing your smile. Many of our patients tell us it’s easy to forget they’ve had their teeth replaced.

What Are the Benefits of All-on-X Implant Restorations?

All-on-X implants offer numerous advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. The following is a look at some of the reasons these fixed implant dentures continue to grow in popularity day after day:

Faster Process : Unlike traditional dental implants, which require adequate bone quality and density for support, the All-on-X technique involves placing your implants at strategic angles, helping take advantage of the available bone in the area. This minimizes or even eliminates the need for bone grafts, expediting your implant process.

Exceptional Stability : After placement, your implants will integrate with your jawbone, creating a strong foundation for your replacement teeth. With All-on-X implant restorations, you don’t need to deal with traditional dentures slipping or shifting when you eat, speak, or smile.

Restored Function : All-on-X implant restorations closely mimic the function of natural teeth, restoring your ability to enjoy the foods you love without restriction. You will also be able to speak clearly and smile with confidence once again.

Preserving Jawbone : When teeth go missing, the jawbone loses its stimulation, naturally deteriorating and resorbing over time. Dental implants act just like natural tooth roots, helping prevent bone loss.

Long-Lasting : All-on-X implants offer an incredibly high success rate of up to 95 percent, providing a long-lasting solution to restore your smile. These full-arch restorations can last for decades or even a lifetime with proper care and regular visits to Sunshine Smiles Dental Care for dental exams and cleanings , offering excellent value over time.

What Is the All-on-X Implant Procedure?

We will start by evaluating your smile and taking detailed images of your teeth and surrounding structures using digital X-rays or CBCT 3D imaging, helping us plan your implant placement with precision. Our team will place four to six implants per arch and, in most cases, immediately outfit you with a set of temporary teeth, so you leave our office with a complete smile.

As the area heals, your implants will integrate or fuse with your jawbone over the following three to four months, a process known as osseointegration. Once the implants fully integrate with the jawbone, you will transition to your final restorations. Your new implant restoration will look, feel, and function just like natural teeth, enhancing your smile and quality of life.
