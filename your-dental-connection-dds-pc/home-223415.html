About Us

Dentistry With Purpose We believe dentistry is about more than teeth.

Eating is one of the last true luxuries in life, and healthy teeth make it possible.

Our goal is to preserve function, comfort, and long-term oral health so you can live and eat well for years to come.

Advanced, Conservative Care We use modern technology and advanced techniques to deliver precise, effective treatment while preserving as much natural tooth structure as possible.

From laser dentistry to minimally invasive procedures, every recommendation is made with long-term outcomes in mind.

Quality Dentistry at Fair, Affordable Pricing High-quality dentistry should be accessible.

We focus on practical treatment options, transparent recommendations, and care that makes sense for your health and your budget, without unnecessary procedures.

We are committed to providing a wide range of dental services.

General Dentistry

Comprehensive exams , fillings , and preventive care designed to maintain long-term oral health and catch issues early.

Professional Teeth Cleaning

Advanced dental prophylaxis using the Woodpecker Air Polisher to gently remove plaque and tartar while reducing sensitivity.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Veneers , bonding , and whitening treatments focused on enhancing your smile while preserving natural tooth structure.

MEET THE Doctor

Dr. Ronald Sudomir

Dr. Sudomir graduated from the University of Detroit-Mercy School of Dentistry in 1995. After graduating, he opened his practice based on the principle of helping his patients achieve optimal dental health.

Dr. Sudomir is a member of multiple dental organizations, including the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, the Macomb Dental Society, and the Oakland Dental Society.

Dr. Sudomir encourages every patient to participate in their own dental health. He enjoys explaining procedures, ensuring his patients are comfortable, and working alongside them to create a plan for their optimal oral health.

Frequently Asked Questions

What dental services do you offer?

We provide a full range of services including general dentistry, teeth whitening, cosmetic treatments, dental implants, and preventive care.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend visiting every 6 months for a routine check-up and cleaning, or more frequently if advised by your dentist.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Please contact us to confirm whether we work with your specific provider.

What can I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit will include a comprehensive exam, cleaning, and a discussion of any concerns or treatment goals you may have.

How do I know if I need cosmetic dentistry?

If you’re looking to improve the appearance of your smile—through whitening, veneers, or reshaping—cosmetic dentistry may be a great option for you. We’ll guide you based on your goals.

Do you offer emergency dental care?

Yes, we do. If you’re experiencing pain or have a dental emergency, please contact us immediately for same-day care.
