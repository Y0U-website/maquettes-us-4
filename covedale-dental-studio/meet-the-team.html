Meet the Team

A Friendly Team That Cares

We keep your family smiling not just with quality treatment, but with the very friendly and compassionate manner with which it’s provided. Our team members are technically competent with warm personalities, and we consider ourselves lucky to have found such great people.

Clinical Team

Carol

Carol is from Oxford, Ohio and graduated from Sinclair Community College in Dayton, Ohio. She received her hygiene license in 1987. Carol brings 36 years of experience to Covedale Dental Studio.

Carol enjoys interacting with patients and creating long term relationships. Carol and her husband have 2 children and 2 grandchildren. In her free time she enjoys family time, her grandkids’ activities and scrapbooking.

Kendra

Kendra is one of our dental hygienists at Covedale Dental Studio. She graduated from UC Blue Ash in 2012 and has been part of our team since 2017. She loves getting to know her patients and educating them about their oral health. She looks forward to seeing her long time patients and catching up on life with them when they come in to see her.

Kendra lives in Indiana with her husband and two boys, Brooks and Boone. After work, you will find her at any of their sporting events cheering them on. The family loves to go off-road riding in their Jeep. On Sundays, you can find her and her husband at Paycor Stadium cheering on their beloved Bengals.

Teri

Teri has a combined 31 years of experience in the dental field, and is an asset to the Covedale Dental Studio team. She assists Dr. Patel on a daily basis and ensures all treatments and schedules are running smoothly. She was born and raised in Cincinnati and has been a West-sider her whole life. Teri has been part of our dental team since 2002, providing excellent care and building lifelong relationships with her patients.

Brandi

Brandi has been a part of our team since 2015 and brings over 13 years of experience in the dental field. Striving to offer her patients an even wider range of services and care, Brandi pursued advanced training to earn her license as an Expanded Functions Dental Assistant. Outside of work, Brandi enjoys spending time outdoors with her husband, daughters and family.

Corinne

Corinne has been in the dental field as a Certified Dental Assistant for over 30 years. In 2008 she went on to become Board Licensed as an Expanded Functions Dental Assistant. Corinne is proud to be part of a dynamic team of professionals who strive to make patients feel comfortable and valued. In her spare time, Corinne likes to garden and cook, and volunteers her time to help community cats in need.
