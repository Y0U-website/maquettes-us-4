About Dr. Smith

Dr. Smith grew up in Conway Arkansas and stayed there to attend college at the University of Central Arkansas.

Dr. Smith is an advocate and speaker for 3M Clarity Aligners educating other orthodontists and helping to shape and improve treatment for everyone, not just his patients.

It was recently pointed out to him that the correct phrase is “all of a sudden” instead of “all of the sudden”. He’s not sure how he’ll cope with that knowledge moving forward. He hasn’t put gas in his car since early 2016 (Tesla!!). His hobbies are golf and then giving up on all other hobbies. And he’s never played point guard in the NBA but plays one in a fantasy league.
