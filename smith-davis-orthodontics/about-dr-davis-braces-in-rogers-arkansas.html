About Dr. Davis

Dr. Davis grew up in Northwest Arkansas and graduated from the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville.

Dr. Davis grew up in a close-knit family in Northwest Arkansas. He and his wife Jessica, daughter, and two dogs Maltese and a chocolate lab are excited to return home. Even though he didn’t fulfill his lifelong dream of becoming a bull rider, he does dream of it. He loves spending time outside and is a terrible golfer yet loves it anyways. They are thrilled to be part of the Smith and Davis Orthodontics team!

EXPERIENCE & QUALIFICATIONS

Dr. Davis grew up in Northwest Arkansas and attended the University of Arkansas in Fayetteville for his undergraduate degree. He then completed his dental degree at the LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans. Following dental school, he obtained his master of science degree and completed his certification in orthodontics at the University of Alabama at Birmingham.
