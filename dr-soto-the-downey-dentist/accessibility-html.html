Accessibility Statement

En el consultorio. Accesible para sillas de ruedas (entrada a nivel de calle, estacionamiento accesible, baño accesible). Personal bilingüe — todo el equipo habla inglés y español. Asistencia disponible — avísanos antes de tu visita y coordinamos. Visitas sensorialmente amigables — citas más largas, salas más tranquilas, recorridos previos.

  • Bilingual staff: every team member speaks English and Spanish — no translator needed
  • Assistance available: reach out ahead of your visit and we’ll arrange whatever support helps you most
  • Sensory-friendly visits: we accommodate patients with sensory sensitivities — longer appointments, quieter rooms, advance walk-throughs

05 Feedback & assistance.

We take accessibility seriously and want to hear from you. If you encounter any barrier on this website or in our office, please tell us — and please be specific about what didn’t work. We commit to responding within 5 business days.

Report an accessibility issue — or just a question.
