Dr. Imelda Soto, DDS

Raised between two languages and two kitchens, Dr. Soto grew up watching her grandmother greet every neighbor by name — and somewhere between that and a chipped tooth at age nine, she decided dentistry was how she’d do the same.

Twenty-five years later, she’s built that practice her own way — a board-certified, female-owned, single-doctor practice rooted on Stewart and Gray Rd, serving Downey families who’ve been coming since the late ’90s.

One doctor, one relationship: the dentist who starts your treatment is the one who finishes it. No rotating roster, no handoffs between strangers.
