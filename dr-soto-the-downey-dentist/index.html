The practice

Raised between two languages and two kitchens, Dr. Soto grew up watching her grandmother greet every neighbor by name — and somewhere between that and a chipped tooth at age nine, she decided dentistry was how she’d do the same.

Twenty-five years later, she’s built that practice her own way — a board-certified, female-owned, single-doctor practice rooted on Stewart and Gray Rd, serving Downey families who’ve been coming since the late ’90s.

One doctor, one relationship: the dentist who starts your treatment is the one who finishes it. No rotating roster, no handoffs between strangers.

"Care should feel like you’re being looked after — not processed."

A board-certified, female-owned, bilingual practice in a category that doesn’t usually feel any of those things. Here’s what makes the chair different.

Board-Certified DDS Dr. Soto holds a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree and maintains active membership in the California Dental Association. Every procedure meets the highest clinical standards — no shortcuts, ever.

25+ Years of Experience Since the late 1990s, Dr. Soto has treated tens of thousands of patients across Downey and Los Angeles County. Depth of experience means faster diagnoses, fewer surprises, and treatment plans you can trust.

Strict Safety Standards Our office follows OSHA and CDC sterilization and infection control guidelines to the letter. Instruments are sterilized between every patient. Clean gloved hands and a fresh setup, every single time.

Services

Professional cleaning and comprehensive exam to keep your mouth healthy. Recommended every six months — a reminder arrives when it’s time.

Durable restorations that protect damaged teeth and restore full functionality and natural appearance. Color-matched in our chair, not at a lab counter.

Replace missing teeth with titanium implants that look, feel, and function just like natural teeth. Planned in-house, scan to seat, by the same dentist.

Traditional braces and clear aligners to straighten teeth at any age. The right fit depends on your lifestyle and your timeline. We’ll talk through both before anything begins.

Visible results from the very first session. Professional in-office whitening or convenient take-home kits — the right route depends on your enamel, and we’ll explain which suits yours.
