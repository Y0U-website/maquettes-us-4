Complete Evaluation and Candidacy Determination

Our dental team conducts a thorough assessment to determine if you’re an ideal candidate for dental implants, evaluating your overall oral health and specific restoration needs. This initial evaluation considers factors such as the number of teeth missing and the type of restoration you desire for your treatment plan. The comprehensive examination ensures that dental implants are the most appropriate solution for your individual situation before proceeding with treatment.

Surgical Implant Placement with Local Anesthesia

During the first treatment visit, the team numbs your mouth with local anesthetic to ensure complete comfort throughout the outpatient procedure. Dr. Richard Blackburn then places a series of titanium posts into your jaw, with the exact number depending on your specific needs and desired restoration type. Following the placement procedure, you receive prescribed pain medication and detailed care instructions to support proper healing during the integration period.

Integration Period and Healing Monitoring

Over the following several months, your titanium implants gradually integrate with your natural jawbone through the biological process of osseointegration. During this critical healing phase, you follow the provided care instructions while your implants become securely fused with your bone structure. This integration period is essential for creating the stable foundation needed to support your final restorations.

Custom Restoration Design and Laboratory Fabrication

Several months later, when your implants have finished integrating with your jaw, you return for the second visit where impressions of your mouth are taken. These detailed impressions are sent to a trusted laboratory that manufactures your custom crowns, bridges, or implant-retained dentures to precise specifications. The laboratory fabrication process ensures that your restorations will fit perfectly and match your natural teeth.

Final Restoration Attachment and Care Instructions

During your third and final visit, your custom restorations are carefully attached to your fully integrated dental implants and polished to perfection. Dr. Richard Blackburn makes any necessary adjustments to ensure optimal comfort and function with your new restorations. The appointment concludes with comprehensive instructions on how to properly care for your dental implants to maintain their longevity and function.

FAQs

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are custom-crafted artificial tooth roots made of titanium that are surgically placed into your jawbone to replace missing teeth. Over time, these implants integrate with your natural bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. They provide the most natural and permanent solution for tooth replacement available today.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental checkups, dental implants can potentially last a lifetime, making them significantly more durable than other tooth replacement options. The titanium implant itself is designed to permanently integrate with your jawbone, while the crown or restoration on top may need replacement after 15-25 years due to normal wear. This exceptional longevity makes implants an outstanding long-term investment in your oral health.

Do dental implants hurt?

The implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during the surgery, though you may experience some pressure. Most patients report that the discomfort after implant surgery is less than they expected and can be managed effectively with prescribed pain medication. Any post-operative discomfort typically subsides within a few days as the healing process begins.

How to get help with dental implants?

Getting help with dental implants starts with scheduling a comprehensive consultation with an experienced implant dentist who can evaluate your specific situation. During this appointment, your dentist will assess your oral health, jawbone density, and overall candidacy for implant treatment. Many dental practices also offer financing options and work with insurance providers to help make implant treatment more accessible and affordable.

What are dental implants made of?

Dental implants are made of titanium, a biocompatible metal that naturally integrates with human bone without causing rejection or adverse reactions. This medical-grade titanium is the same material used in orthopedic joint replacements and has decades of proven success in the human body. The crown, bridge, or denture attached to the implant is typically made of porcelain, ceramic, or other tooth-colored materials designed to match your natural teeth.

What is the process for getting dental implants?

At Volterra Dental Comprehensive and Aesthetic Dentistry, getting dental implants takes three visits over several months.

If the team determines that you’re a candidate for dental implants, they numb your mouth with a local anesthetic and place a series of titanium posts into your jaw. The number of implants your provider places depends on several factors, including the number of teeth missing and the type of restoration you desire. After the placement procedure, your dentist prescribes pain medication and provides care instructions.

Several months later when the implants finish integrating with your jaw, you return to Volterra Dental Comprehensive and Aesthetic Dentistry. Your provider takes impressions of your mouth and sends them to a laboratory that manufactures crowns, bridges, or a set of implant-retained dentures.

When the lab finishes your custom restorations, you visit Volterra Dental Comprehensive & Aesthetic Dentistry for the last time. Your provider attaches your crowns, bridges, or implant-supported dentures to your dental implants, polishes them, and explains how to care for them.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for your dental implants doesn’t require any special equipment. The team at Volterra Dental Comprehensive and Aesthetic Dentistry recommends brushing your teeth twice daily, flossing your teeth regularly, and visiting the office every six months for an oral exam and professional teeth cleaning.

To prevent damage to your implants, avoid bad habits like biting your fingernails, opening packaging with your teeth, and chewing on hard objects, like ice cubes or pen caps.
