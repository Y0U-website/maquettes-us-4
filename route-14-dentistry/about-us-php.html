Our Core Values The five core values of Route 14 Dentistry are Dental Education, Honest Pricing, Transparent Results, Superior Oral Care and Time.

Dental Education

Before we begin any procedure, our team of doctors will thoroughly explain the diagnosis, the steps of the procedure and exactly what to expect during the process.

Honest Pricing

We understand the overwhelming stress that dental expenses can have on you and your family. With this in mind, our office will provide our patients with honest and up-front pricing before any treatments.

Transparent Results

We don’t hide anything. To provide accurate results, our staff will take high-quality before-and-after photos of the progress and share them with our patients.

Superior Oral Care

Our team of doctors continues to develop their knowledge by regularly attending educational courses, seminars, reading professional journals and publications in all areas of dentistry. In addition, our staff takes high-quality before-and-after photos of the procedure and shares them with our patients.

Time

We know our patients’ time is very important; therefore, our staff and doctors strive to ensure our patients are seen at their scheduled appointment time.
