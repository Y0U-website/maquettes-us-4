Orthodontic Services at Route 14 Dentistry

Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces use brackets and archwires to guide your teeth into the desired alignment. They are recommended for patients with a wide range of orthodontic conditions and are appropriate for patients of all ages.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces use clear or tooth-colored brackets and wires to achieve the same results as traditional braces. They are a more discreet and less noticeable treatment option. Patients of all ages and stages of care can benefit from ceramic braces.

Invisalign

The Invisalign clear aligner treatment system uses a series of custom-made aligners to gradually reposition your teeth. They are recommended for teens and adults with mild to moderate orthodontic issues.

Retainers

We use retainers to help patients maintain their corrected bite following orthodontic treatment. Be sure to wear your retainer as directed for the best possible results.
