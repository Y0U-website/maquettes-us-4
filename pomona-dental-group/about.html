Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Eduardo Sandoval, DDS

Dr. Eduardo Sandoval is a compassionate and dedicated dentist committed to providing exceptional dental care with honesty and integrity. A proud Azusa, California native, Dr. Sandoval earned his Bachelor&#x27;s degree in Psychology from UCLA before pursuing his dental education at the Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara in Mexico. He later expanded his clinical training in Eastern Europe, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Fluent in both English and Spanish, Dr. Sandoval builds strong, trusting relationships with patients from all backgrounds. His approachable nature, clear communication, and genuine empathy create a welcoming and comfortable experience for everyone who walks through his door.

Dr. Sandoval looks forward to meeting you and helping you achieve a healthy, confident smile through personalized, high-quality care.
