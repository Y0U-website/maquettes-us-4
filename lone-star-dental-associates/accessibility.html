Accessibility Statement

Lone Star Dental Associates is committed to facilitating the accessibility and usability of its website, https://www.lonestardentalassociates.com, for everyone. Lone Star Dental Associates aims to comply with all applicable standards, including the World Wide Web Consortium’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.0 up to Level AA (WCAG 2.0 AA).

Lone Star Dental Associates is proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone. Should you experience any difficulty in accessing any part of this website email us at info@lonestardentalassociates.com and we will work with you to provide the information or service you seek through an alternate communication method that is accessible for you consistent with applicable law (for example, through telephone support).
