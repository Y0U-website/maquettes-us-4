Ceramic

Ceramic braces are a great choice for those who want a more discreet option without compromising effectiveness. These braces use tooth-colored brackets, blending in with your natural teeth for a less noticeable look. They work just as efficiently as traditional braces but with added aesthetic appeal. A perfect balance of function and subtlety!

Precision & Innovation in Every Step Precision & Innovation in Every Step Technology Technology 1 Digital Impressions With Trios digital scanning, we can create a detailed 3D model of your teeth in real-time, making your treatment process smoother and more efficient. No more goopy impressions—just quick, accurate, and comfortable scanning.

CBCT

With our CBCT (Cone Beam CT) imaging, we are able to view a detailed 3D view of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures, allowing for highly accurate diagnoses and treatment planning. This technology enhances precision for implants, extractions, and orthodontics.

Ceph X-Ray

Cephalometric X-rays give us a side-profile view of your head, jaw, and teeth alignment, helping us assess growth patterns and design customized orthodontic treatment plans. This specialized imaging allows us to evaluate facial structure, bite alignment, and airway health, ensuring your treatment is tailored for long-term success.
