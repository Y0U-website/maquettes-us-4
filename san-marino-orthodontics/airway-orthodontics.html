Why Should I Get Airway Orthodontics?

Did you know…

Orthodontic treatment can improve airway size, potentially reducing sleep apnea symptoms.

The Benefits Of Airway Orthodontics

Improved Breathing Airway orthodontics can enhance your breathing by expanding the airway. This improvement can lead to better oxygen intake, resulting in increased energy levels and overall health.

Better Sleep Quality By potentially reducing sleep apnea symptoms, airway orthodontics can contribute to more restful and uninterrupted sleep, which is crucial for your physical and mental well-being.

Enhanced Oral Health Addressing malocclusions not only improves your smile but also promotes better oral hygiene, reducing the risk of tooth decay and gum disease.

The Airway Orthodontics Treatment Process

Initial Consultation and Assessment

Initial Consultation and Assessment

The journey begins with a comprehensive consultation at San Marino Orthodontics, where Dr. Siu and Dr. Chee will assess your oral health and discuss your concerns. This step involves detailed examinations and possibly imaging to understand your unique dental structure and any airway issues. The goal is to create a personalized treatment plan that addresses both cosmetic and functional needs.

Customized Treatment Planning

Customized Treatment Planning

Once the assessment is complete, a tailored treatment plan is developed. This plan will outline the specific orthodontic appliances and techniques that will be used to address your malocclusions and potentially improve your airway. The plan is designed with your unique needs in mind, ensuring that you receive the most effective treatment possible.

Ongoing Monitoring and Adjustments

Ongoing Monitoring and Adjustments

Throughout the treatment process, regular follow-up appointments are scheduled to monitor progress and make necessary adjustments. This ensures that the treatment is progressing as planned and allows for any modifications needed to enhance the airway benefits. Dr. Siu and Dr. Chee are committed to providing attentive care and ensuring optimal outcomes for each patient.
