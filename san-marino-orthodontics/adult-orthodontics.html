Why Should I Get Adult Orthodontics?

Did you know…

Orthodontics can improve your speech by aligning your teeth properly.

The Benefits Of Adult Orthodontics

Improved Oral Health Straight teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. By aligning your teeth, you can maintain better oral hygiene, ensuring a healthier mouth and a brighter smile.

Enhanced Self-Confidence A straight, beautiful smile can significantly boost your self-esteem. Whether you&#x27;re at work or socializing, feeling confident about your smile can positively impact your interactions and opportunities.

Better Functionality Correcting bite issues can improve your ability to chew and speak. Proper alignment reduces strain on your jaw and teeth, preventing future dental problems and enhancing your quality of life.

The Adult Orthodontics Treatment Process

Initial Consultation

Initial Consultation

The journey begins with a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Tina Siu or Dr. Daniel Chee. During this session, we discuss your dental concerns, review your medical history, and conduct a thorough examination of your teeth and jaw. This step is crucial for understanding your specific needs and setting realistic goals for your treatment. We use advanced diagnostic tools, including digital X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess your dental structure and plan an effective treatment strategy tailored to you.

Customized Treatment Plan

Customized Treatment Plan

Based on the findings from your initial consultation, Dr. Siu or Dr. Chee will develop a personalized treatment plan. This plan outlines the recommended orthodontic appliances, such as clear aligners or braces, and the estimated timeline for your treatment. We ensure that you are fully informed about each step of the process, including what to expect during your treatment and how to care for your orthodontic appliances. Your preferences and lifestyle are taken into account to ensure the treatment is as convenient and effective as possible.

Active Treatment Phase

Active Treatment Phase

Once your customized plan is in place, the active treatment phase begins. This involves regular appointments where adjustments are made to your orthodontic appliances to guide your teeth into their desired positions. Dr. Siu and Dr. Chee closely monitor your progress, making necessary modifications to ensure optimal results. During this phase, we provide ongoing support and advice to help you manage any discomfort and maintain good oral hygiene. Our goal is to make your orthodontic journey as smooth and comfortable as possible, leading to a beautiful, healthy smile.
