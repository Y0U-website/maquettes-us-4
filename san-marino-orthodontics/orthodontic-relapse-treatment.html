Why Should I Get Orthodontic Relapse Treatment?

At San Marino Orthodontics, we believe in patient-first care. That means using a conservative approach to correct only what’s needed without over-treatment or surprise costs. By carefully assessing your current smile alongside your previous treatment history, we craft personalized solutions that honor your time, investment, and goals.

Did you know…

Teeth can begin to shift within days of not wearing a retainer properly.

The Benefits Of Orthodontic Relapse Treatment

Restore Your Smile Without Starting Over Relapse treatment allows for minor corrections without full braces, using options like limited aligners or partial appliances for faster, targeted results.

Prevent Further Shifting and Jaw Issues Correcting early movement helps prevent more serious alignment problems, discomfort, or bite issues down the road, protecting your long-term oral health.

Boost Confidence and Oral Function Improved alignment not only enhances your smile’s appearance, but also supports better chewing function and avoids future dental complications.

The Orthodontic Relapse Treatment Process

Personalized Smile Assessment

Personalized Smile Assessment

We begin your visit with a complimentary consultation where Dr. Siu or Dr. Chee carefully evaluates your current smile, previous treatment records, and retention routine. This helps us determine if relapse has occurred, how much correction is needed, and which options are most suited to your goals. Straightforward, honest feedback is a signature part of our approach and always pressure-free.

Conservative Treatment Planning

Conservative Treatment Planning

After your smile assessment, we’ll design a fully customized plan that addresses only the issues present, no unnecessary steps or excessive procedures. Many patients benefit from limited aligner treatments or minor appliance adjustments that are very fast and cost-effective. As a multi-generational, family-run office, we treat each patient like extended family, with your best interests always at heart.

Smooth and Supportive Realignment

Smooth and Supportive Realignment

Once treatment begins, we guide you through the realignment process with regular check-ins and clear instructions. We understand that many patients return feeling frustrated about relapse, so we make this phase as supportive and stress-free as possible. With the option to begin treatment the same day as your consultation and benefit from a $300 discount, it’s easier than ever to reclaim your confident smile.
