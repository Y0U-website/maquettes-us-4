The practice

Frank Sanchez, Jr., DDS, treats adults, teenagers and children starting at age 6. He provides preventative, restorative, and cosmetic dental care including filings, extractions, root canal therapy, crowns, bridges, dentures, veneers, and teeth whitening. Plus, his office can usually accommodate same-day emergency dental appointments.

Dr. Sanchez is fluent in English and Spanish. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Univeridad Nacional Pedro Henriquez Urena in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic. He earned a B.A. degree in Pre-Dental Biology from Eastern Connecticut State College.
