Dr. Joshua Lee

Dr. Joshua Lee earned his DDS from New York University College of Dentistry and served five years as a dental officer in the U.S. Army. Dr. Lee developed his passion for wisdom teeth surgery as a dental surgeon in the Army and has safely performed over 5000 wisdom teeth removals. He is a Master of the ICOI and AAIP, and serves as a volunteer adjunct assistant clinical professor at the Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery Clinic of USC School of Dentistry.
