One of our skilled hygienists will gently clean away the plaque and tarter, polish your teeth for a smooth, stain free smile, & provide personalized tips to support your oral health at home.

Fillings

We offer both tooth-colored composite fillings and traditional silver amalgam fillings to meet each patient’s unique needs. Whether you prefer the natural, seamless look of composite or the durability of silver fillings, we remove decay gently and restore your tooth with precision. Our goal is to stop cavities early, protect your oral health, and provide a comfortable, reliable restoration that keeps your smile strong for years to come.

Extractions

We make tooth extractions as gentle, safe, and stress-free as possible. Whether a tooth is damaged beyond repair, causing pain, or creating crowding, our team uses modern techniques and thorough anesthesia to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure. We offer both simple and surgical extractions, as well as clear aftercare instructions to support smooth healing.

X-Rays

Ranges $28-$200 Our x-rays are fast, comfortable, and highly accurate images that help catch dental concerns early—often before you feel any symptoms. With minimal radiation, our imaging technology creates personalized treatment plans that keep your smile healthy and worry free.

Consultations

Every treatment begins with a thoughtful, personalized consultation. You may have a dental concern, want to explore treatment options, or simply be seeking a second opinion—no matter the reason, we take the time to listen, answer your questions, and provide prompt care. When specialized treatment is needed, we coordinate with trusted oral surgeons, endodontists, orthodontists, and periodontists with a goal to guide you every step of the way.

Restorative Services

Crown & Bridge

Starting at $1366 Whether you need a single crown, a multi-tooth bridge, or an implant-supported crown, we offer durable and esthetic options crafted from high quality materials—including porcelain, zirconia, and gold. Crowns reinforce damaged or cracked teeth, while bridges fill in spaces using natural teeth or implants for support. Each restoration is custom made for a precise fit and naturally beautiful look, helping you chew comfortably, protect your oral health, and keep you smiling strong.

Partials & Dentures

Starting at $1755 Our partials and dentures are crafted to restore not only your smile but also your confidence and daily comfort. We offer a full range of removable tooth-replacement options—including temporary flippers, flexible partials, metal-framework partials, and full dentures—each is designed to fit securely and look natural. Conventional prostheses are made after your gums have fully healed or remaking an old set. Immediate prostheses are placed the same day the teeth are removed, allowing you to leave with a complete smile while you heal.
