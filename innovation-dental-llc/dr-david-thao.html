David Thao, DDS

Our Wauwatosa, WI Dentist, dedicated to providing exceptional dental care

Dr David Thao, our Wauwatosa, WI dentist, is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care with a gentle touch. With a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from Marquette University Dental School in 2010, Dr Thao brings many years of experience to Innovation Dental. He is an active member of the ADA, WDA, and Greater Milwaukee Dental Association.

Passionate about oral health, Dr Thao specializes in preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry, ensuring each patient receives personalized treatment in a comfortable environment. Committed to staying current with the latest advancements, Dr Thao combines expertise with a compassionate approach to achieve optimal dental wellness for every patient.

In his free time Dr Thao and his wife are active in their children’s events and activities. When time permits he sneaks away for a little fishing and golfing.

Your smile is our top priority, and Dr Thao looks forward to helping you achieve and maintain excellent oral health for years to come.
