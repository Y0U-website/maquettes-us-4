Get to know

Justin Spiegelman, DMD

Dr. Justin Spiegelman is a general dentist and the owner of Manor Oaks Dental Studio. He is proud to provide personalized, high-quality dental care to patients in Manor, Austin, and the surrounding communities.

Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his wife, Dr. Stern, who is also a dentist at the practice, their two children, Ari and Eli, and their dog, Dazy. He also enjoys traveling, cooking and grilling, fitness, golf, and Miami Hurricanes football.

Get to know

Mai Stern, DMD

Dr. Mai Stern grew up in the suburbs of Boston, Massachusetts, before earning her Bachelor’s degree in Physiology and Neurobiology from the University of Connecticut, where she graduated summa cum laude as an Honors Scholar.

She attended Harvard School of Dental Medicine on a full scholarship through the United States Air Force Health Professions Scholarship Program.

After dental school, Dr. Stern was commissioned as a Captain in the U.S. Air Force, completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Langley Air Force Base, and served four years providing comprehensive dental care to military members in Washington, D.C.

Dr. Stern is passionate about combining the precision of science with the artistry of dentistry to create healthy, confident smiles. She believes every patient deserves to feel heard, comfortable, and empowered in their dental care, and she strives to build lasting relationships founded on trust, compassion, and personalized treatment.

Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Stern continually pursues advanced education to provide the highest standard of modern, evidence-based dental care. She is an active member of the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine and the Academy of General Dentistry.

She now calls Austin home with her husband, their two young sons, and their dog.

Your first visit

A first appointment worth looking forward to

We know that starting with a new dental office can feel like a big step, and we want to make it as easy and welcoming as possible. Your first visit at Manor Oaks Dental Studio is designed to give us a complete picture of your oral health so we can build a care plan that is truly right for you. There is no rushing, no pressure, and no one-size-fits-all approach here.

Patient Experience At your first visit, you will receive a comprehensive exam where we take the time to assess your teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health from every angle. From there, Dr. Spiegelman or Dr. Stern will walk you through exactly what they find and what your options are, in plain language you can actually understand. Our goal is for you to leave feeling informed, confident, and genuinely excited about the road ahead for your smile.

What to Bring Please bring the following to your first appointment:

Your dental insurance card (if applicable)

A list of any current medications or supplements

Any relevant dental records or X-rays from a previous provider (if available)

Modern tools that make a real difference

At Manor Oaks Dental Studio, we invest in technology that directly benefits our patients, whether that means a faster, more comfortable procedure or a treatment outcome that is simply better. Our team stays current with the tools and techniques that set the standard for modern dental care, so every patient receives the quality they deserve.

3D CBCT imaging

Our cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) scanner gives us a detailed, three-dimensional view of your teeth, bone, and jaw structure. This level of precision is especially important for implant planning, surgical procedures, and complex restorative cases, allowing us to treat with greater accuracy and predictability than traditional X-rays allow.

Digital X-rays

Digital radiography gives us high-resolution images of your teeth and supporting structures with a fraction of the radiation used by traditional film X-rays. Images are available instantly, allowing us to review findings with you chairside and move forward with your care without delay.

No goopy impressions required

We use advanced digital scanning technology to capture a precise, detailed image of your smile without traditional impression trays or messy materials. The process is quick, comfortable, and helps us plan your treatment with greater accuracy.
