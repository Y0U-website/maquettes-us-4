Dental Implants

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. This makes them a durable and long-term solution for replacing missing teeth.
  • How long does it take to get dental implants? The process of getting dental implants usually spans several months and invovles multiple appointments. The implant placement surgery is conducted after the initial consultation and treatment planning. This is followed by a healing period of several months, during which the implant undergoes osseointegration, fusing with the jawbone. Once the implant has adequately healed, the final restoration, which may be a bridge, denture, or crown, is attached to the implant to complete the process.
