The Benefits Of All-on-X Implants

Permanent Stability with Fewer Implants All-on-X implants use advanced imaging and placement techniques to maximize bone contact, often requiring only flour or six implants per arch. This results in fewer surgeries and a stable, secure fit.

No More Removable Dentures Say goodbye to messy adhesives and daily maintenance. All-on-X implants stay fixed in place, eliminating the hassles of traditional dentures and enabling a more confident lifestyle.

A Natural Look and Feel Crafted with high-quality ceramic materials and digital smile design protocols, your new teeth are customized for your facial structure and bite, delivering a beautiful, natural-looking smile.

Our Difference

The All-on-X Implants Treatment Process Personalized Planning & 3D Imaging Your journey begins with an in-depth consultation with Dr. Arjang Naminik at Woodland Hills Dental Arts. Using 3D cone beam imaging, he maps out bone density and ideal implant positions. Precise planning ensures optimal function and aesthetics, and our comfort-first amenities, from calming aromatherapy to Netflix entertainment, help you relax every step of the way.

Implant Placement and Temporary Teeth

In most cases, All-on-X implants are placed using a minimally invasive approach, often without bone grafting. The titanium implants are anchored into the jawbone with state-of-the-art precision. Thanks to our in-house capabilities and CEREC technology, most patients receive a full set of temporary teeth on the very same day as their surgery.

Healing and Final Restoration

Over the next 3-6 months, your implants fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. Once fully healed, the final prosthetic is custom-designed and placed, crafted for durability, beauty, and long-term comfort. All follow-up care remains under one roof right here in Woodland Hills, CA, ensuring a seamless recovery journey.
