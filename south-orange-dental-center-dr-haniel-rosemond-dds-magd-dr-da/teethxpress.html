Instagram icon Twitter icon YouTube icon Google icon TeethXpress What is TeethXpress? TeethXpress is a comprehensive dental solution that entails replacing all the teeth in both the upper and lower jaw. This procedure is also commonly known as full arch dental implants or full mouth reconstruction. By opting for this treatment, patients can restore their oral health and improve their overall quality of life by regaining their ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently.

Why Might You Need TeethXpress?

TeethXpress may be necessary if you&#x27;ve lost most or all of your teeth due to gum disease, decay, or injury. This is a long-term solution for individuals who want to restore their smile, improve their ability to eat and speak, and maintain healthy jaw bone density.

Who Is a Candidate for TeethXpress?

Individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth, have good overall health, and have adequate jaw bone density to support dental implants are candidates for TeethXpress. Your dentist will assess your oral health and determine if you&#x27;re a suitable candidate for this procedure.

What Occurs During the TeethXpress Procedure?

The TeethXpress procedure comprises several steps. First, your dentist will extract any remaining teeth and prepare your jaw bone for implant placement. Then, dental implants will be implanted into your jaw bone. After a healing period, abutments and dental crowns will be placed on the implants to create a natural-looking, functional smile.
