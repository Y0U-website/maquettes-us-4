Who is eligible for Single/Multi Teeth Implant Replacement?

If you are someone who has lost one or more teeth but has a healthy jawbone and gums, you may be a good candidate for Single/Multi Teeth Implant Replacement. However, it is important to keep in mind that this procedure is not suitable for everyone. To determine if it is the best option for you, it is recommended that you seek the advice of a dental professional. They can evaluate your individual situation and help you make an informed decision about whether or not to proceed with this type of treatment.

How is the Single/Multi Teeth Implant Replacement procedure performed?

During the Single/Multi Teeth Implant Replacement procedure, the dentist will first insert a small titanium post into the jawbone to serve as the artificial tooth root. Then, a custom-made artificial tooth will be attached to the post. The resulting tooth is a natural-looking and fully functional replacement that can last a lifetime with appropriate care.
