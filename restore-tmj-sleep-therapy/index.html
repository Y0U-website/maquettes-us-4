Temporomandibular joint disorders affect your jaw joints and the muscles attached to them. A severe TMD can make it difficult -if not impossible- to chew and even talk. TMDs can also cause jaw pain, headaches, eye pain, ear pain or fullness, clicking or popping in the joint, a limited range of motion and could result in a locked jaw.
