  • Adult braces : Braces aren’t just for kids — teens and young adults can significantly benefit from our natural-looking braces. Our braces are comfortable and versatile enough to correct bite misalignments and crowded and gapped teeth.
  • Invisalign: For patients who prefer a more subtle orthodontic treatment, we offer Invisalign — a system that aligns teeth with clear, removable braces. After scanning your mouth with an iTero scanner, we make a 3D simulation of your treatment and then use the models to create a series of aligners. Each set of aligners will be worn every two weeks before moving to the next. This process continues until your teeth are perfectly aligned.
