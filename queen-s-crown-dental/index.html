Snoring and sleep apnea are prevalent sleep-related breathing disorders that can significantly impact one’s health and quality of life. Snoring occurs when airflow causes the tissues in the throat to vibrate during sleep, leading to the familiar noise. At Queen’s Crown Dental in Honolulu, we specialize in diagnosing and treating snoring and sleep apnea. Our approach includes the use of custom-made oral appliances designed to maintain an open airway during sleep, thereby reducing snoring and preventing apnea episodes. These devices are tailored to each patient for optimal comfort and effectiveness.

If you or a loved one are experiencing symptoms of snoring or suspect sleep apnea, it’s important to seek professional evaluation. Early diagnosis and treatment can improve sleep quality and overall health.
