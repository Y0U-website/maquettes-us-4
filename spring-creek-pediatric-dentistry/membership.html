Membership Plan Overview

Our in-house membership plan is designed to help families without dental insurance save on their child’s dental care. Members receive comprehensive coverage for routine preventive care and enjoy reduced fees on a wide range of additional services (fillings, crowns, etc). We believe in making excellent dental care accessible and affordable, ensuring your child can have a healthy smile for a lifetime.

Membership Plan Details

Discounts Receive a 20 % discount on any additional treatments or procedures such as fillings, crowns, and extractions, not included in the plan. Discount does not apply to products, sedation options such as nitrous oxide, oral sedation, or fees associated with general anesthesia.

Enrollment

Sign up easily through this form, and a team member will contact you to complete the enrollment. We accept cash and credit card payments.
