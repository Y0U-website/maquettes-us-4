Meet Dr. Kennedy Dr. Kevin Kennedy Jr. Born and raised in Clarksville, Dr. Kevin Kennedy Jr. is a third-generation local small business owner in our community. He understands the privilege and responsibility of service to this community and is dedicated to providing Clarksville, TN and surrounding areas with the highest level of pediatric dental care as a commitment to the families here.

Dr. Kennedy attended Austin Peay State University and continued his education in Memphis at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center. He graduated at the top of his class from the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry in 2010 and then completed two years of specialty training in pediatric dentistry.

Following residency, Dr. Kennedy became the pediatric dentist on staff at St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital in Memphis, where he gained valuable experience caring for children with special health care needs, including childhood cancer and other debilitating disease.

He has remained a leader in our community through involvement in local non-profits, Clarksville Area Chamber of Commerce , and organizations that support our local dental community. He is the creator and host of Clarksville Vibe, a podcast that tells the stories of people who make Clarksville the best place to live in America.
