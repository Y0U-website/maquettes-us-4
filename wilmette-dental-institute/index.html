The practice

Introducing Dr. Nolan Patel, the practice owner at Wilmette Dental Institute. Dr. Nolan was born and raised in the Chicagoland area and is excited to serve the same community in which he grew up!

Dr. Nolan is a proud graduate of the nation’s top-ranked dental school—the University of Michigan. A lifelong Wolverine fan, he truly bleeds blue. You’ll spot Michigan memorabilia throughout the office, a nod to his school pride and the exceptional education that shaped his approach to dentistry.
