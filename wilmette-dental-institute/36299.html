Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a suitable candidate for dental implants?

Dental implants are considered the gold standard of tooth replacement because they provide the stability and functionality of your natural teeth. They also look beautiful and can last a lifetime, making them a popular option with anyone looking to replace one, several, or all of their teeth. So, how do you know if you’re a suitable candidate for dental implants?

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

If you meet the following criteria, you are more likely to be a good candidate for dental implants:

Healthy gums You must have healthy gums free from periodontal disease to be eligible for dental implants.

Sufficient jawbone density Because the dental implant is placed directly into your jawbone, you must have adequate density to support it. If you don’t, you may be able to undergo a bone grafting procedure.

Good oral hygiene To keep your dental implants for a lifetime, you should practice good oral hygiene by brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and visiting your Wilmette dentist every six months. Your dentist will more likely recommend dental implants if you practice good oral hygiene.

How do I care for my dental implants after surgery?

Proper care after surgery is crucial for the success and longevity of your dental implants . If you ve recently received dental implants in Wilmette, IL, follow these guidelines to ensure optimal healing and maintenance.

Immediate Post-Surgery Care

  • Managing Discomfort After surgery, you may experience some discomfort and swelling. Use over-the-counter pain relievers as recommended by your dentist in Wilmette, IL. To reduce swelling, apply an ice pack to the outside of your cheek for 15 minutes on and off.
  • Diet Stick to a soft diet for the first few days following the surgery. Gradually reintroduce solid foods as you heal.
  • Gentle Brushing Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing your teeth gently and avoiding the surgical site initially.
  • Rinsing Rinse your mouth with a saline solution or an antimicrobial mouthwash to keep the area clean and reduce the risk of infection.

Long-Term Maintenance for Your Dental Implants

  • Regular Check-Ups Schedule regular check-ups and professional cleanings to detect potential issues early.
  • Avoiding Harmful Habits Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol consumption, as these can compromise the stability and longevity of your dental implants.

Are there any age restrictions for getting dental implants?

It s natural to wonder if there are any age restrictions associated with getting dental implants . While there’s no specific age at which an individual qualifies for implants, anyone receiving dental implants must have a fully-developed jawbone, which typically occurs between 18 and 24 years of age.

There are, however, other health-related factors that play a significant role in determining if someone is a suitable candidate for dental implants:

So, while age isn t a determining factor in dental implant suitability, a patient’s overall health and the condition of their jawbone are key considerations. Consult with your dentist to determine your eligibility.

  • Overall health . It’s important that anyone receiving dental implants, regardless of age, be in good general health.
  • Bone health . To support dental implants, sufficient jawbone density is crucial. Again, age doesn t preclude implant placement, but bone health is vital. In some cases, bone grafting procedures can help those with bone loss receive implants.
  • Oral health practices . Patients of all ages should maintain good oral hygiene practices and commit to caring for their implants properly to ensure their longevity.
