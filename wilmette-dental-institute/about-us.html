Second-to-None Service with a Smile

Once you are comfortable in one of our treatment rooms, you can benefit from a wide range of dental services to fit your needs:

Cleanings and exams We would like to see you twice a year for preventive cleanings and exams. This helps us keep your teeth and gums as healthy as possible.

Tooth-colored fillings If we notice evidence of tooth decay, we can remove it and use tooth-colored composite resin to restore your tooth s beauty and functionality. No one will even know you have a filling!

Dental crowns Whether you have a broken or damaged tooth or need to strengthen a tooth after root canal therapy, our porcelain dental crowns completely cover your compromised tooth. With dental crowns, you can both keep and protect your natural tooth.

Dental bridges For patients with one or two missing teeth, we can "bridge the gap" with dental bridges. Using your adjacent teeth for support, we can place prosthetic teeth to give you the smile you deserve.

Dental implants Dental implants can replace several or all of your teeth, giving you back your self-confidence and the ability to eat your favorite foods. We can even use dental implants to support full dentures, so you won t have to worry about slippage.

Periodontal disease treatment If you are one of the millions of people who suffer from gum disease, we ve got you covered. We can provide professional cleanings or scaling and root planing to help you manage your gingivitis or periodontitis.
