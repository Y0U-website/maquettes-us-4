As implant dentistry continues to improve, the team at 172 NYC Dental strives to offer the latest techniques with the best results for our patients. All-on-4 dental implants and implant dentures represent the latest advancement in full mouth restoration .

In our office, teeth are removed only when there is no other option. Should a patient present as edentulous (missing all the teeth in the arch) All-on-4 dental implants and implant-supported dentures are a great teeth replacement option and an expeditious and dramatic way of restoring function, esthetics, speech, and self-confidence.

Both of these devices restore all the missing or damaged teeth in the arch by use of the denture (much smaller than the traditional removable one). These dentures are snug and afford great function, they do come in and out of the mouth as desired by the patient.

All-on-4 is a porcelain bridge that restores all the teeth in the arch in addition to lost bone and gum tissue, just like the implant-supported denture. The difference lies in that this device is screwed into the implants and is not removable. It becomes part of your natural dentition.

  • Ball retained: The main difference between ball and bar-retained implants is the mechanism used to attach the denture. In this procedure, a ball-shaped attachment is fastened to the implants, which are then secured onto the denture with a socket-like feature.
