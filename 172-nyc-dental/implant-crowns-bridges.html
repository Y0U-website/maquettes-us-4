Dental Implant Crowns & Bridges

Each of your teeth plays an important role in your mouth’s ability to function properly. If you have experienced tooth loss, we can help. At 172 NYC Dental, we offer numerous options when it comes to restoring your missing tooth. Dental implants , along with an implant crown, bridge implants, or implant-supported denture, can provide our patients with results that look great, feel great, and return full mouth function.

  • Porcelain bridge replacing two or more missing teeth requiring at least two implants, fixed in the mouth.
  • Denture supported by implants (usually two-four implants) that replaces all the teeth in the arch (usually fourteen). The denture is removable, comes in and out of the mouth
  • Four on the floor-four implants hold a prosthesis that is screwed into the mouth and does not come out. Usually replacing ten-fourteen teeth
