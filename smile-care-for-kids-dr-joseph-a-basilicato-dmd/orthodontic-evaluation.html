Signs Your Child May Benefit from an Orthodontic Evaluation

Sometimes developmental issues are obvious, and sometimes they are easy to miss. Knowing what to look for as a parent can help you decide when to bring your child in sooner rather than later.

Beyond visible alignment concerns, there are behavioral and functional signs worth paying attention to. A child who breathes primarily through their mouth, snores frequently, or has difficulty chewing certain foods may have an underlying bite issue contributing to these patterns. Jaw pain, clicking sounds when opening or closing the mouth, and teeth that wear unevenly are also worth discussing during a visit.

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry notes that early orthodontic treatment, when indicated, can reduce the need for more extensive treatment later and can improve a child’s overall oral function and self-confidence. Understanding the benefits of early action is something we are always happy to talk through with families at any stage.

Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontic Evaluations

  • At what age should my child have an orthodontic evaluation? The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first evaluation by age 7, when a mix of baby and permanent teeth are present and developing bite patterns can be assessed. Dr. Basilicato screens for orthodontic concerns as part of routine dental exams, so if he notices anything worth addressing, he will raise it with you at the appropriate time rather than waiting for a separate specialist appointment.
  • What does an orthodontic evaluation look for? The evaluation examines tooth alignment, spacing, bite relationship (how upper and lower teeth meet), jaw development, and crowding. Dr. Basilicato also looks at whether all permanent teeth have erupted on schedule and whether any baby teeth are blocking or delaying their successors. The goal is to identify issues early enough that treatment, if needed, can be timed for the best possible outcome.
  • Does Smile Care for Kids provide braces or aligners? We provide orthodontic evaluations and early interceptive guidance, and we refer to trusted orthodontic specialists when full treatment is needed. Our role is to monitor development and alert you early when an orthodontic concern is forming, so you have time to plan rather than being caught by surprise. We can connect you with appropriate specialists in the Northern New Jersey area when the time comes.
  • What happens if we wait too long to address an orthodontic issue? Some orthodontic concerns are easier and less costly to address during specific windows of jaw and tooth development. Waiting until all permanent teeth are in can mean longer treatment times and fewer options. In certain cases, early intervention with a simple appliance can eliminate the need for more significant treatment later. Catching issues early does not always mean treatment starts right away, but it does mean you have the most choices available to you.
