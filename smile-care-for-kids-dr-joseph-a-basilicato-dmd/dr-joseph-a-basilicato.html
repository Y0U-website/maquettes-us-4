Meet the Doctor

Joseph A. Basilicato, D.M.D

Dr. Joseph A. Basilicato has been providing patients with the highest level of dental care since he began practicing in 2005.

As an undergraduate at Villanova University, Pennsylvania, he was accepted into the extremely competitive Pre-Professional Honors Program that combined his undergraduate studies with his graduate dental program. He received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Pennsylvania in 2003.

He completed his pediatric dental training at Maimonides Medical Center, Brooklyn, New York, where he was Chief Resident during his senior year. He completed his studies and received his Specialty Certification in Pedodontics in 2005.

Dr. Basilicato is an active and involved member of the American Dental Association, the New Jersey Dental Association, the Passaic County Dental Society, and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. He is a member of the Board of the Passaic County Dental Society and has served as its President twice. He is also a Council Chair for the New Jersey Dental Association. He was Valedictorian at DePaul High School, Wayne, NJ, and a member of Alpha Epsilon Delta and the Medical/Dental Honor Society. He graduated Magna Cum Laude from Villanova University in 2000.

Dr. Basilicato began his career in Paterson before relocating to Wayne, where he has long been a resident. He lives with his wife Jeanette and his two children Anthony and Amelia. He enjoys playing the piano and attending concerts at the New York Philharmonic. He is an active amateur radio operator, as well as an avid reader of both classic and contemporary fiction.

Comprehensive Care for Every Child

At Smile Care for Kids, Dr. Basilicato provides comprehensive general pediatric dentistry services, including routine cleanings, exams, fluoride treatments, and dental sealants. For patients who experience anxiety, he offers nitrous oxide sedation to help make visits comfortable and stress-free. He is also deeply committed to caring for children with special needs, and many of those patients continue to see Dr. Basilicato into adulthood. Smile Care for Kids is a single-location, family-owned practice not a corporate chain serving families in Pompton Plains, NJ and surrounding communities.
