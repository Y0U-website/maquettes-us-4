Rooted in excellence.

At the core of everything we do is a commitment to always doing what’s right for YOU.

We treat patients like family. At Dentistry of the Oaks, we have one core belief — to always do what is right for our patients. This belief drives us each day to provide personalized dental care at the highest standard of excellence to each and every patient that comes through our doors. Our team will get to know you personally, listen to your needs and customize your care just like we would for a member of our own family, because to us, you are family.

Our environment is warm and welcoming. We are so excited to welcome you to our practice and show you just how relaxing dentistry can be! Inside our warm environment, you will be greeted by our compassionate team who is dedicated to helping you receive the care you need. We will remain attentive to your needs and cater to your comfort throughout your entire appointment. Apart from our great chairside manner, we also offer pillows, blankets, bluetooth headphones to stream your favorite movies and shows, and even sedation, if needed! You will truly feel at peace here.

We work with the latest dental technology. Technology is a driving force at our practice. This is because we recognize the pivotal role it plays in providing quality treatments. Advanced equipment and up-to-date procedures — such as digital x-rays, cone-beam CT scanners and paperless charting — help us provide more accurate diagnoses, gentler and safer treatments, and faster recoveries. We will continue to invest in the best to enhance your dental experience.

We deliver a wide range of quality services. We’ve said it before and we’ll say it again — we truly want what is right for you. It is our goal to change your life by improving your smile and your lasting oral health, and our wide range of services make this possible! Whether you need to build confidence by straightening your teeth or need to restore function with a dental implant, our comprehensive treatment plans and quality services offer the care you need, the way that is right for you.

Our team is committed to continual learning. Our skilled team has over 1200 hours of continuing education and counting. We pride ourselves in this constant learning in order to be the best for our patients. We truly believe you won’t find another dentist around that is as bright, skilled or experienced as ours! We excel far above average in the level of continuing education we attend every year because it is an absolute priority to us that we are delivering the most advanced dentistry for you and your family.

Our practice is clean and organized. Now, more than ever, it’s easy to see how important it is to have a clean and organized dental practice. You can walk confidently through our doors knowing that our infection control policies, procedures & systems are in alignment with the guidelines provided by the ADA, CDC, and OSHA. We are doing everything we can to ensure the lasting health and safety of our patients and team.
