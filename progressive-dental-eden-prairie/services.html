Cosmetic Dentistry

A beautiful smile is an invaluable asset. Cosmetic dentistry encompasses a broad range of services with the purpose of improving the aesthetics of your teeth and smile. Our cosmetic services include:

  • Smile Design
  • Direct Laminates/Veneers
  • Bleach – Day/Night
  • Chairside teeth whitening and ZOOM

Orthodontics

Orthodontics is the specialty of dentistry that deals with treating misaligned teeth, improper bites, tooth irregularity, disproportionate jaw relationships, and more.

We offer both traditional braces and Invisalign®. When you need orthodontics, we’ll help you determine the best option for you!

Prosthodontics

We offer a wide range of high-quality prosthodontic services, which are treatments for missing, broken, decaying, and damaged teeth. Many of our prosthodontic services can be performed in one-day, which allows for a quick turnaround.

  • One-Day Implants
  • Bridges
  • One-Day Crowns
  • One-Day Dentures
  • Overdenture/Implant Supported Dentures
  • Partials resin or cast meta

Root Canals

Endodontics deals with the tooth pulp and the tissues surrounding the root of a tooth, including root canal therapy and apexification – a procedure performed to encourage the closure of the apex of a young tooth.

Mouthguards

We offer mouthguards and snore guards, which are mouthpieces that are commonly worn to protect the teeth. They are simple and affordable devices that sit comfortably in your mouth and prevent grinding and snoring. Through digital and 3D printing, we offer:

  • Snore Guards
  • Sports Guards
  • NTI Night Guards

Dental Emergencies

Dental emergencies can be very frightening and often painful. Prompt treatment is generally required to alleviate pain and ensure the patient’s oral and overall health. If you’re dealing with a dental emergency, call us immediately at 952-445-6220 , and we will get you in as soon as possible. Should you call after hours, our voicemail will inform you how to get in touch with us. If you are experiencing a true medical emergency, please call 911.

CEREC Crowns

The latest technology in restorative dentistry, CEREC allows us to scan, design, and mill beautiful restorations right in the office. Using sophisticated CAD/CAM systems to produce all-ceramic inlays, onlays, partial crowns, veneers, and crowns, the CEREC system makes receiving dental treatment easier and more convenient for you than ever. When you need a crown, you can walk out the same day with a strong, durable, and cosmetically pleasing crown already in place.

  • Fast camera scans and no silicone impressions
  • No temporary crowns
  • Single-appointment treatment

Oral Surgery

We perform both simple and surgical extractions. We complete surgical planning through tissue simulation and laser-assisted tissue remodeling. We also complete digital scans for procedures as opposed to impressions. We know that surgery can be a stressful experience, so we do everything we can to make it a comfortable experience for you.

Periodontics

We also specialize in periodontics, the branch of dentistry that studies the supporting structures of teeth and the diseases and conditions that affect them. We perform gingivectomies, a periodontal surgical procedure that includes the removal of gum tissue due to disease or for recontouring. We also perform Crown lengthening.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry is the study, diagnosis, and integrated management of diseases of the teeth and their supporting structures and the rehabilitation of the dentition to functional and aesthetic requirements of the individual. We offer composite fillings, as well as other restoration treatments to help you achieve a bright, healthy smile.

X-Rays

Our X-ray technology is state-of-the-art, which allows us to provide you with the most advanced treatments possible. We use CBCT x-ray equipment, as well as digital & 3D machines, which provide an extremely in-depth and comprehensive view of your teeth.

Sleep Apnea Treatment

Sleep apnea is a destructive breathing disorder that occurs when your airway is partially or completely blocked while sleeping. We are highly skilled in sleep apnea treatment and will work closely with you to understand your needs and determine the best treatment option.

Sleep Apnea for Children

The HealthyStart® System opens the airway by bringing the upper and lower jaws forward, allowing for maximum airflow, aiding in the improvement of Sleep Disordered Breathing symptoms and allowing children to live healthy lives.
