About Us

Dr. Kimberly Ahn grew up in Irvine, California. Graduating from the University of California at Irvine with a degree in biology, Dr. Ahn continued on to New York University College of Dentistry where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Dr. Ahn received the CCAPA award which recognizes the top clinicians in the class.
