Our Orthodontic Practice Has been Offering Braces and Aligner Treatment Since 1978

We have proudly serviced the Norwalk & Westport, Connecticut communities since 1978, offering braces and aligner treatment for patients of all ages.

The practice

Our board-certified orthodontists serve patients from two conveniently located offices and offer evening and early morning appointments to ensure orthodontic treatment can fit into even the busiest lifestyle. We offer clear and aesthetically pleasing treatment options.

Our standard of care and commitment to our patients is often unmatched. Our goal is to ensure each patient has the perfect smile, and we have the technology and expertise to make that happen.

As board-certified orthodontists, we are committed to the highest levels of orthodontic care and offer cutting-edge treatment options that result in beautiful, healthy smiles that last a lifetime.

Orthodontic treatment is about so much more than just a straighter smile. An orthodontist can improve the function of a bite, improve facial aesthetics, increase self-esteem, and set you up for a lifetime of good oral health. That’s because orthodontics doesn’t just create a more beautiful smile, it sets the stage for a healthier one, as well.

Gone are the days when the only orthodontic treatment option were metal braces. Today, we have a variety of treatments that can address your needs, and many are discrete and comfortable. Whether you opt for standard metal braces, clear ceramic braces, or clear aligner therapy, we offer everything you need to get the smile of your dreams.

Norwalk Metal braces are still used for a reason they are effective and efficient. Many of our younger patients still use metal braces, and they love how their look can be personalized through colorful elastics.

Norwalk Clear ceramic braces work in the same efficient manner as metal braces, but they are made of material that blends in with your natural tooth color.

Norwalk Clear aligners offer the most discreet way to straighten teeth because the aligners are virtually invisible. Our aligners are crafted in-house by our orthodontists, and then fabricated locally, and are custom created for each individual patient.
