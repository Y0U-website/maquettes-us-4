The practice

We can restore your kiddo’s tooth to its former glory with a pediatric dental crown.

Comprehensive exams are the cornerstone of any good preventative care plan.

Dental sealants are an affordable, effective way to prevent cavities in kids.

Children should have dental checkups every six months. Regular visits allow the dentist to clean your child’s teeth, prevent cavities, monitor jaw and tooth development, and provide fluoride treatments or sealants if needed.

Good cavity prevention includes daily brushing with fluoride toothpaste, flossing once teeth touch, limiting sugary snacks and drinks, and keeping up with regular dental checkups. Your pediatric dentist may also recommend dental sealants for extra protection.

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children see a dentist by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth coming in. Early visits help monitor growth, check for cavities, and get your child comfortable with the dental office environment.

It’s normal for kids to feel nervous. Pediatric dentists are trained to work with children and use a calm, friendly approach. Reading books about the dentist, practicing at home, and visiting a child-friendly dental office can make the experience more positive.
