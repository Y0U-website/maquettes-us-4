General dentistry

Our goal at Chapel Street Dentistry is to help our patients achieve and maintain optimal dental health. Our General Dentistry options will ensure that you and your family have healthy, lasting smiles.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Achieving a picture perfect smile is an aspiration for many people. If the appearance of your teeth are making you self-conscious. cosmetic options can transform your smile and help restore your confidence.

Restorative Dentistry

Our team is dedicated to giving you comprehensive Restorative Dentistry options. Restorative treatment can restore the health and function of your natural teeth.

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric dentistry lays the foundation for a lifetime of good oral health. Primary teeth they aid in proper jaw and facial growth, speech development, help your child eat the nutritious foods they need for overall growth, and maintain space in the mouth for the permanent teeth to erupt.

Dental Implants

Like a dental bridge, an implant bridge utilizes two implants spaced apart to replace multiple missing teeth. This is a more cost effective option to replace three or more missing teeth while only having two implants placed.

Periodontal Treatment

Gum disease (periodontitis) is one of the most common dental health issues, but it’s also completely preventable and treatable. Gum disease develops in stages.

Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies happen suddenly and often require immediate treatment to relieve pain and prevent further damage. Same day care is necessary to treat toothaches, broken teeth and infections.
