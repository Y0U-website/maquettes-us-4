Dental Implant Service Details

Explore how our advanced implant solutions replace missing teeth with natural strength and beauty.

3D CBCT Digital Implant Planning

High-precision 3D bone and nerve mapping

Our state-of-the-art Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scanner captures 3D anatomical images of your jawbone, nerves, and surrounding structures. This ensures pinpoint placement accuracy, optimal safety, and long-term implant success.

Single Tooth Dental Implants

Permanent standalone root and crown replacement

A single dental implant replaces both the root and the crown of a missing tooth without altering or shaving down neighboring healthy teeth. It integrates securely into your jawbone, preserving bone volume and facial structure.

Implant-Supported Bridges & Dentures

Secure multi-tooth replacement with no slipping

If you are missing multiple teeth or a full arch, implant-anchored bridges or overdentures provide firm stability. They eliminate messy adhesives, slipping, and speech slurring associated with conventional removable dentures.

Custom Implant Crowns & Restorations

Life-like porcelain crowns engineered for natural feel

Once your implant post has fully integrated with the jawbone, we craft and attach a custom porcelain crown. Each restoration is shade-matched precisely to your surrounding teeth for a seamless, beautiful result.
