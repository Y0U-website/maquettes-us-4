Dental Cleanings

Preventative Dental Care

Dental cleanings are recommended as a regular part of preventative dental care. Cleanings are performed by a licensed dental hygienist or a dentist to remove plaque, tartar, and stains from the teeth. Routine dental cleanings will reduce the risk of severe dental decay, gum disease, bad breath, and more.

Unfortunately, many people avoid visiting the dentist for annual cleanings and examinations, assuming that they are unnecessary. We cannot stress enough how important regular dental cleanings and examinations are to maintain proper oral health and prevent painful, often costly dental work.

Gunnell Family Dentistry specializes in smarter dental cleanings! Using our modern technology and dental education, we focus on helping our patients fully understand their oral health. We create custom patient treatment plans designed with your specific oral health needs in mind. Each patient will have a clear understanding of their current oral health status and we will discuss future dental health needs like tooth extractions, braces, root canals, dental crowns, and more. We want to make maintaining your oral health as easy as possible!

What is Involved in a Dental Cleaning?

When you visit our Logan, UT dental office, you can expect to spend about an hour or less in the office for your cleaning. We work quickly and efficiently to clean your teeth. Depending upon the amount of tartar and build-up on the teeth, it could take longer to clean the teeth and gums properly. Here is the process of what you can expect during your dental cleaning and examination visit;

  • Examination of the teeth, gums, and jaw (recommended every 6 months)
  • X-rays (depending upon when your last x-rays were taken)
  • Cleaning of the teeth
  • Polishing of the teeth
  • Flossing
  • Gum disease screening (Periodontal gum probing to check for gum recession)
  • Fluoride treatments (**not required for all patients)
