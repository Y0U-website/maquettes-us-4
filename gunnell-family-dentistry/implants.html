family dentistry

cleanings

We provide preventative dental care services with expert dental cleanings. Our experts remove dental plaque and prevent cavities, gingivitis, and periodontal disease with annual dental cleanings.

crowns

Crowns are designed to cover a tooth and protect the root of a tooth. Crowns help to restore the natural look and feel of a tooth and will strengthen the tooth. Modern dental crowns are designed to last longer, take less time to create, and help you enjoy sharing your smile once again!

root canals

When the pulp or root of a tooth has become damaged, a root canal is often the only procedure. Our office staff has specialty training in endodontistry and can perform some root canals procedures in our office. For more complex infections, we can recommend a qualified endodontist. We work hard to help our patients feel comfortable and understand dental procedures like root canals to reduce anxiety, stress, and fear. We want to help you restore your smile, reduce pain and inflammation, and help you get back to feeling your best.

kids dental

Gunnell Family Dental provides kids dentistry services. We strive to provide the best services to optimize your child’s oral health needs. Our team understands how stressful it can be for children to visit the dentist, and we work with kids to help them feel comfortable when receiving dental treatment.

fillings

Dental fillings are used to restore a tooth with cavities or have damage or are in need of repair. Dental fillings are one of the most common dental procedures. We provide multiple dental filling options, but most patients prefer composite (white or tooth-colored) dental fillings to help the filling blend in with the natural color of their teeth. Contact our office to schedule an appointment to restore your teeth.

bridges

Dental bridges solve the problem of missing teeth! If you have a tooth extraction, and you want to replace the gap, a dental bridge is a great solution. We use multiple dental bridge materials including porcelain, gold, and alloys.

x-rays

Dental X-Rays are used to identify problems deep within the structure and root of a tooth. We will use X-rays annually to check on the root structure and overall health and wellness of your mouth. Additional X-rays may be needed for patients experiencing an abscessed tooth or other dental concerns.

Implants

full mouth

If you need to restore your entire smile with dental implants, contact our office. We provide full-mouth dental implant services to give you a smile you can be proud to share.

single tooth

Do you have a missing tooth? Single tooth dental implants will provide a long-lasting result for missing teeth, restoring your smile and providing you with confidence once again.

partials

Have you had a failed bridge or root canal? Have multiple teeth erupted with serious infection? We provide partial dental implants to restore your smile permanently with partial dental implants.

bone grafting

Prior to dental implants, some patients may need bone grafting to strengthen the jaw bone. Contact our office to find out if you are a candidate for dental implants and need bone grafting treatment.

Cosmetic Dentistry

veneers

Stained, chipped, or worn teeth causing you to be embarrassed about your smile? We provide dental veneers, porcelain coverings placed over the front of a tooth, restoring your smile and boosting your confidence!

smile make overs

Smile makeovers will address multiple cosmetic dental imperfections. Cracks, chips, fractured, crooked, and missing teeth are common issues facing dental patients. Our smile makeovers improve the appearance of your smile, letting you be proud to share your smile once again!

teeth whitening

Teeth whitening is one of the most common cosmetic dentistry procedures. We recommend scheduling a consultation to discuss teeth whitening options and to determine the best one to meet your oral health needs. Teeth whitening options include at-home treatments, in-office teeth whitening, and more.

specialty applications

Mouth Guards

We are determined to make sure your mouth and teeth are well protected during sports activities, no matter what your passion is. We provide custom mouth guards to protect your teeth and mouth, allowing you to enjoy participating in your favorite sports.

Sleep Apnea

Do you suffer from sleep apnea? We provide sleep apnea dentistry treatments designed to prevent teeth grinding. We create oral appliances to shift the jaw during sleep, helping to improve air flow and improve sleep.

Night Guards

Do you find yourself waking up with a sore jaw, gums and teeth? Night guards are designed to protect your teeth from excessive grinding during the night. Similar to mouthguards for sports, night guards are formed to your teeth and jaw, reducing further damage to the teeth.
