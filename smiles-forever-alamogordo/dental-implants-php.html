Dental Implants & Bone Grafting

Dental Implants

Permanently replace your missing teeth with minimally-invasive dental implants. Implants are the only restoration that mimics the look, feel, and performance of anatomical teeth. Because of their predictability and strength, they can be used for anything from single tooth replacement to full-arch rehabilitation.

Dental implants give you the freedom of enjoying all of your favorite foods and smiling with confidence. Plus, there are no removable prosthetics to clean. They promote healthy bone and a more youthful facial profile.

Bone Grafting

Periodontal disease and tooth loss can cause resorption (shrinkage) of bone in your jaw. This lack of bone affects your facial profile, tooth stability, and the option of placing dental implants. Fortunately, bone grafting can help.

Bone grafting adds strength and height to your jaw, helping ensure a predictable dental implant process or additional support to neighboring teeth. We may recommend a bone graft immediately after a dental extraction, following periodontal therapy, or before placing dental implants.
