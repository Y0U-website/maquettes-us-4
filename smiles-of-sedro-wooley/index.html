Missing teeth can affect more than just your smile—they can impact your confidence, speech, and ability to enjoy your favorite foods. At Smiles of Sedro-Woolley, we offer dental implants: the gold standard in tooth replacement. Implants provide strength, stability, and a natural appearance that other options simply can’t match.

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as a replacement root. Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to restore your smile.
