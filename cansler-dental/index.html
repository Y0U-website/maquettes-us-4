The practice

When the damage to a tooth is too great to fix with a simple white filling, a crown is the best option to restore the tooth to proper form and function. A crown is a simple restoration that is made in a lab to cover and protect the tooth. They are typically made of a strong porcelain material to look natural and last a very long time.

If you have lost a tooth and the nearby teeth are strong you are a good candidate to receive a bridge to replace the missing tooth. The bridge can also be made of beautiful porcelain and is cemented permanently to the adjacent teeth to give you a worry-free restoration that you can be proud of.

Many patients prefer this look, as metal amalgam fillings are often noticeable. A composite filling bonds to the tooth which helps to support and strengthen the tooth - this helps avoid chipping.
