Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-X Implants

What is All-on-X?

All-on-X is a full-arch dental implant solution that uses four or more implants to support a complete set of prosthetic teeth in one or both arches.

Who is a good candidate for All-on-X?

Patients who are missing most or all teeth and have adequate bone support may qualify for this treatment. A full evaluation will determine suitability.

How long does the All-on-X procedure take?

In many cases, a provisional set of teeth is placed the same day as the implants. The full process, including healing and final restoration, may take several months.

How do I care for All-on-X implants?

Daily oral hygiene and regular dental visits are essential. Our team will provide care instructions to help maintain your new smile.
