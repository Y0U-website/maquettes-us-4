Why Should I Get Dental Implants?

Dental implants are the closest you can get to healthy, natural teeth in look, feel, and function. They allow you to live the way you want to – confidently eating, smiling, laughing, talking, and enjoying all of your everyday activities without worrying about your teeth! They are incredibly durable and will last many years, or even a lifetime, if they are well cared for. Unlike dentures, they don’t slip or click when you talk, laugh, or eat, making them a comfortable and secure option. Dental implants also protect your oral health by stimulating bone growth and preventing bone loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly are dental implants and how do they function?

Dental implants are a durable, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges that sit on top of the gums, implants are designed to function like real teeth because they are anchored securely into the jawbone. This means they don’t move when chewing or speaking and can function just as well as - if not better than - the natural teeth they replace.

A dental implant is made of three key components working together to create a strong, stable, and natural-feeling replacement tooth.

1. The implant post (the “titanium screw”): This small titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone where the tooth root once was. Over the next few months, the bone bonds to the implant in a process called osseointegration . This creates a stable foundation, much like a natural tooth root.

2. The abutment (the “connector”): Once the implant has fully integrated and fused with the bone, a connector piece called an abutment is attached. The abutment extends slightly above the gumline and serves as the attachment point for the final crown.

3. The crown (the “visible, chewing portion”): Finally, a custom-made porcelain or ceramic crown is secured to the abutment. This crown is shaped and color matched to seamlessly blend into your surrounding teeth, resulting in a replacement that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth—often even stronger!

Together, these three parts allow dental implants to restore chewing ability, prevent bone loss, and provide long-lasting results that feel completely natural.

What are the key benefits of choosing dental implants over dentures or bridges?

Dental implants offer a wide range of advantages that extend far beyond aesthetics. Unlike removable dentures—which can slip, shift, or cause sore spots—implants are securely anchored into your jawbone. This stability allows you to eat, speak, and smile with total confidence. Implants also outperform traditional dental bridges because they do not require healthy neighboring teeth to be shaved down to support the restoration.

A major advantage of implants is their ability to stimulate the jawbone , just like natural tooth roots. This prevents the bone loss and facial profile changes that often occur after tooth loss. Implants are also virtually indistinguishable from real teeth and provide exceptional comfort.

How long do dental implants last, and what is their success rate?

With proper placement and good long-term maintenance, dental implants can last 30 years or more , and many patients enjoy their implants for a lifetime. Implant longevity depends on factors such as oral hygiene, bone health, routine dental checkups, and overall systemic health.

Dental implants also boast an exceptionally high success rate—typically 95–98 % —especially when placed by highly experienced clinicians using advanced technology.

What is the dental implant treatment process and recovery time?

Your personalized dental implant journey at Old Town Smiles begins with a comprehensive consultation and diagnostic evaluation. Using cutting-edge digital imaging and 3D scanning technology (without the need for traditional X-rays), Dr. Ahmed or Dr. Barra will assess your bone quality, gum health, and overall suitability for treatment. If necessary, preparatory procedures such as bone grafting or periodontal therapy will be discussed during this visit.

The implant placement procedure is performed right here in our Alexandria office, typically under local anesthesia, with additional comfort options available for patients with dental anxiety. During the procedure, the titanium implant is placed into the jawbone and begins the healing process known as osseointegration , where the bone fuses to the implant. This phase generally takes around 3 months . Many patients opt to receive a temporary tooth during this time, allowing them to smile confidently throughout the healing period.

Once the implant has fully integrated, your custom permanent crown is created using advanced smile design technology and securely attached to the implant. Most patients experience minimal downtime , often returning to normal daily activities within 24–48 hours. Our team carefully monitors your healing through follow-up visits to ensure a smooth, supportive recovery and exceptional long-term results.
