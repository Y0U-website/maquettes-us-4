Cleaning And Hygiene

Regular teeth cleanings and hygiene appointments are the foundation of preventive dental care. During your cleaning, we will thoroughly cleanse your teeth and gums, removing any plaque or debris from every surface in your mouth. You’ll even be able to reverse minor stains to keep your smile bright and beautiful. We recommend scheduling routine cleanings and exams every six months for most patients. You will be given specific recommendations based on your unique needs, after a very thorough assessment.

However, good oral hygiene shouldn’t stop when you leave our office. Keeping up with your at-home brushing and flossing routine is equally as important as attending professional cleanings, and is the best way to keep your teeth and gums healthy. Use a fluoride-rich toothpaste and a soft-bristled toothbrush to brush your teeth at least twice a day for two minutes at a time; and don’t forget to floss! Flossing at least once every day, ideally before going to bed, helps to get rid of food particles, plaque, and bacteria that can’t be removed with brushing alone.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are a great way for patients to protect their teeth from decay. Dental sealants are often recommended for children, but can still be a great option for patients of all ages. Dental sealants can provide protection for your teeth for years after being applied.

Applying dental sealants is a non-invasive procedure that utilizes a special dental resin that is applied to the molars or premolars. Sometimes, the uneven surface of the back teeth are filled with grooves where plaque and bacteria can flourish. These grooves are cleaned with a special micro-abrasive particle, and the special resin is applied. After application, the resin is cured with a UV light, causing it to harden into a powerful barrier between your teeth and the foods, drinks, acid, and bacteria that lead to decay.

Dental CT Scan (CBCT)

A dental CT scan, or CBCT, is a highly diagnostic imaging tool to allow your dentist to see a wide range of relevant anatomic structures, to aid in the most accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. CBCT, or cone-beam computed tomography, is a highly sophisticated device that captures 150-200 images of your teeth, jaw, neck, airway, sinuses, nasal cavity, nerve canals, and other relevant areas of your mouth, in under a minute!

We are proud to be one of the only offices in the area, and among only 10 % of offices in the United States, to have an in-house CBCT scanner. Our CBCT utilizes cutting-edge technology to take a special 3D x-ray, allowing our team to fully diagnose and visualize your condition, as well as plan the most precise and predictable procedure possible, especially when it comes to dental implant surgery as well as root canals. It is also the best imaging tool available to dentists for diagnosis of lesions that can be indicative of serious disease, including cancers of the jaw bone.
