What You Can Expect

Your First Visit

We believe your first visit truly begins with your first phone call. When you call us here at Old Town Smiles, you will be warmly greeted by a member of our Business Team, who will make every effort to answer all of your questions and concerns and find you an appointment time that works best with your schedule.

Upon your arrival at your first visit, you will be taken on a brief tour of our spacious, bright and sunny office, before a private, one-on-one meeting with a member of our Business Team, who will begin to collect relevant information that you would like shared with the doctor and the team. It is very important to us to really get to know you, so that we can best serve your unique needs and goals. We do not rush this process; you will have all the time you desire for this step. If you prefer an abbreviated version of this, that will also be accommodated. We tailor to your preferences!

After meeting the doctor, one of our highly qualified Dental Hygienists will perform a thorough gum assessment , where various measurements are done on each tooth to diagnose your gum health status and determine the most appropriate type of dental cleaning for you. In most cases, the cleaning is done the same day, and will be followed by fluoride varnish.

If you have dental needs that require further follow-up, you will either leave your visit that day with a written treatment plan, or, if your needs are more extensive, we will invite you back for a complimentary consultation to discuss your findings, our recommendations, and your options. If your needs are very complex and you would like to have a spouse or trusted family member with you at the consultation, you are more than welcome to do so.
