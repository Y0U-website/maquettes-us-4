Your Mount Laurel Dentist

Bradley Convissar, DMD

Dr. Bradley Convissar earned his BA in biology from Tulane University in 1999 and his DMD from The University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey in 2003. After completing a 1-year general residency program in Somerdale, NJ, he joined his father’s practice, and he continues to provide the same quality care to the community that his father did when he opened the office in 1984. He keeps up to date with the newest procedures and technologies by taking yearly continuing education courses.

Our Office

Rancocas Woods Family Dentistry has proudly served Mount Laurel and the surrounding towns for over 40 years. We pride ourselves on our commitment to our patients and staff, and enjoy the relationships we have maintained and continue to grow with our patients and the community. Our Rancocas Woods office is located in Mount Laurel Township at the intersection of Creek Road, Rancocas Blvd and Marne Highway. Major roads nearby include I-295 and Rte 38. Our office is ADA accessible and everyone is welcome.

We are proud of our office which fully utilizes state of the art equipment. We have a modern sterilization system that surpasses OSHA requirements.

Our friendly and competent staff is dedicated to patient comfort and quality care. We provide the best and latest in dental technology and treatment options. We provide gentle, family-oriented dental care to the adults and children of this community. In addition to General Dentistry and Cosmetic Dentistry, we work with specialists in Periodontics (gum diseases), Implant Dentistry, and Endodontics (root canal treatments), as well as oral surgeons. Our team emphasizes comprehensive quality care and preventive dentistry.

We look forward to seeing you at our dental practice in Mount Laurel.
