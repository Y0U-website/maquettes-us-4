Meet Dr. Christine D. Laster…

Dr. Laster believes that the best dental outcomes are achieved when patients feel involved and informed. She focuses on empowering patients with the knowledge they need to make confident decisions about their oral health.

  • Explaining treatment options in clear, understandable terms
  • Offering gentle, comfort-oriented care
  • Encouraging questions and shared decision-making
  • Creating customized treatment plans that honor individual needs

Education and Professional Background

Dr. Laster earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from a respected dental program, where she developed a strong foundation in core dental disciplines, including preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Her education fostered a clinical mindset grounded in attention to detail, evidence-based decision-making, and lifelong learning.

Since graduating, Dr. Laster has continued to advance her skills through ongoing professional development, staying current with modern techniques and technologies that enhance patient care. Her dedication to continuing education reflects her commitment to providing the highest standard of dental treatment.

Comfortable, Collaborative Care

Dr. Laster understands that every patient has unique expectations and comfort levels when it comes to dental care. She creates a welcoming atmosphere where patients feel comfortable voicing their concerns and participating in their treatment decisions. Her approachable demeanor helps reduce dental anxiety, making it easier for individuals to move forward with care they may have postponed or avoided in the past.

With a focus on education and clear communication, Dr. Laster ensures patients understand what to expect from their treatment, why it is recommended, and how it will benefit their oral health in the long term.

Community Engagement and Continuing Education
