Meet Dr. Brandon D. Rensch, DDS

He holds active memberships in professional organizations that reflect his dedication to excellence: the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, American Dental Society of Anesthesiology, and the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. He holds fellowship status in both the International Dental Implant Association and the Academy of General Dentistry.

  • Comprehensive certification and experience in the full spectrum of in-office dental sedation including nitrous oxide (laughing gas), oral sedation, and IV sedation: ensuring relaxed, anxiety-and-pain-free care even for the most nervous or complex patients
  • Has completed thousands of hours of continuing education across the full scope of modern dentistry including root canal therapy, orthodontic alignment with clear aligners, TMJ dysfunction, full-mouth implant rehabilitation, cosmetic treatments, and simple and complex restorative care.

Clinical Focus

Why He Founded Connection Dentistry

After relocating from the midwest to North Carolina, Dr. Rensch fell in love with the “City of Oaks” and the welcoming spirit of Raleigh. He saw an opportunity to build a dental practice where patients are treated like family and friends — not just another appointment on the schedule. At Connection Dentistry, he and his team prioritize relationships, time-spent listening, and tailoring comprehensive holistic treatment uniquely to each patient.

Outside the Office

When he’s not transforming smiles, Dr. Rensch is likely to be found walking or playing with his dog, Potato, trying (sometimes succeeding!) to keep his golf ball on the fairway, or playing guitar. His approachable, fun-loving nature means you’ll never feel like you’re just “in for a cleaning” — you’re in for an experience with a competent dental team who genuinely enjoys getting to know you.

Your Smile Partner

Whether you’re just looking for a cleaning, have a painful tooth needing a root canal, or considering a full-mouth implant solution under sedation, Dr. Rensch offers expertise, compassion, and clarity. He believes each patient’s smile is a journey — and he’s honored to guide you every step of the way.
