Smile Society Promise

Smile Society was created to highlight that dentistry doesn’t have to be unpleasant. We want you to feel calm knowing that you will be coming to us every six months for a visit. Our goal is to make you feel like you are visiting your friend or going to your favorite coffee shop. Life is hard enough and if we can put a smile on your face, pun intended, we know that we are doing something right. We are here for you.

We understand what it’s like to be anxious about the unknown! Sharp tools, bright lights, gloved hands all aiming for your mouth! Come on, we know it can be scary. But we want you to know that though our tools are sharp and our light is bright, our hands are full of care and skill to help you lead a happy and healthier life.

Meet the Doctors

Dr. Munira Sulaiman

Dr. Sulaiman is an owner and General Dentist at Smile Society. She grew up in New Jersey and attended New York University College of Dentistry for her Doctorate in Dentistry. She is caring, sweet, and extremely goofy the perfect fit for Smile Society.

Dr. Mandeep Singh

Dr. Singh is an owner and General Dentist at Smile Society. He grew up in New Jersey and attended New York University College of Dentistry for his Doctorate in Dentistry. On a regular day you can find Dr. Singh bouncing around the office, making jokes and dancing. He believes that if you love what you do, and love where you are, work doesn’t really have to be “work”.

Dr. Inderpal (Sunny) Virdi

Dr. Virdi graduated from NYU College of Dentistry and completed his residency at Monmouth Medical Center. Dr. Virdi enjoys watching sports, spending quality time with friends, sneakers, and music. Dr. Virdi chose dentistry because he was extremely afraid of the dentist when he was younger and after overcoming his fears, he wanted to help others overcome their dental fears as well. We love having Dr. Virdi in the office and the calm, joyful presence he brings!
