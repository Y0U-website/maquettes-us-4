About Yost Family Dentistry

Family owned and operated, Yost Family Dentistry has been Delta County’s most reliable family dental practice since 1976. Founded by Lyle Yost and passed to his son, Morgan, in 2008, Yost Family Dentistry embodies the values, traditions, and commitment to service unique to our small town community.

We are dedicated to delivering a high value service, utilizing the newest technology, and educating you on how to best take care of your teeth and gums. Our rates are reasonable and have not increased in years. You also receive the benefit of seeing the same dentist each time you visit, so that your experience is consistent, thorough, and reliable.

Dr. Morgan Yost

Born and raised in Delta, Dr. Morgan Yost is proud to serve the community he’s called home for most of his life. He graduated from Delta High School in 1996, Brigham Young University in 2002 (with degrees in Zoology and Human Biology), and the University of Colorado School of Dentistry in 2006. Dr. Yost also served for two years on an LDS mission in the Philippines. He is happily married to his wife, Jennifer, and a proud father of three, Mia, Tyler, and Sam. Dr. Yost spends his free time coaching his children and actively supporting his community through Rotary Club.

The Yost Family Dentistry Team

Dr. Yost can’t do it alone; he is supported by a dedicated and hard-working team of professionals. Our team consists of four dental hygienists, two dental assistants, and two front desk attendants. We are committed to making your appointment with Yost Family Dentistry as comfortable as possible.
