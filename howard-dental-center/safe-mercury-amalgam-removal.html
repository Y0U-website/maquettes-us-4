Q&As: Safe Mercury Amalgam Removal

  • Why is it important to remove mercury fillings? Mercury-based fillings, known as amalgams, can release small amounts of mercury vapor over time, especially when chewing. Some patients choose to remove these fillings to reduce exposure, address sensitivity, or for peace of mind about their long-term health.
  • What safety measures are in place during SMART? The SMART protocol uses a combination of high-volume air filtration, specialized protective barriers, oxygen delivery, and precise removal techniques to minimize mercury exposure to both patients and the dental team.
  • Are there any side effects associated with SMART? When done with proper technique, SMART minimizes the risk of mercury exposure during the removal process. Some patients may experience temporary sensitivity or minor discomfort, but we take every step to support your comfort and recovery.
  • Can anyone undergo the SMART procedure? Most patients are eligible for mercury removal using SMART, though it’s important to discuss your medical history and personal health goals first. Our team will help you understand whether this is the right step for you.
