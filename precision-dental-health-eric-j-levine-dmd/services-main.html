Periodontial Care

  • Why Periodontal Care Periodontal care is essential for safeguarding your gum health and preventing tooth loss. At Precision Dental Health, we emphasize the significance of regular periodontal assessments as the cornerstone of lasting dental wellness. By taking a proactive stance, we help maintain your oral health, minimize inflammation, and reduce the risk of more serious health issues in the future.
  • Periodontal Disease Periodontal disease is an infection of the gums and the supporting structures of the teeth. It begins with plaque buildup on the teeth, leading to inflammation in the gums. If left untreated, this inflammation can advance to more severe stages, where the bone and tissues supporting your teeth are damaged.
  • Scaling & Root Planing Deep Cleaning for Healthier Gums--Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning treatment used to fight gum disease and restore your oral health when a traditional cleaning is no longer as impactful.

Restorative Care

  • Fillings A filling is a crucial solution for repairing teeth affected by decay or damage. By restoring the tooth’s shape and function, fillings not only alleviate discomfort but also protect against further decay, keeping your smile healthy and vibrant.
  • Crowns A crown is an effective way to restore a damaged or weakened tooth, providing strength and stability. By encasing the tooth in a durable cap, crowns enhance your smile’s appearance while protecting against further damage, ensuring long-lasting oral health.
  • Bridges A bridge is a reliable solution for replacing missing teeth, seamlessly filling the gap and restoring your smile’s integrity. By anchoring to adjacent teeth, bridges enhance your bite function and aesthetics, helping you enjoy a confident, complete smile once again.
  • Inlays & Onlays Inlays and onlays are custom-made restorations designed to repair larger cavities or damage while preserving more of your natural tooth structure. These durable solutions provide a perfect fit, enhancing both strength and aesthetics, ensuring your smile remains healthy and beautiful.Item description
  • Root Canals A root canal is a vital treatment for saving a tooth that has become infected or severely damaged. By removing the infected pulp and sealing the tooth, this procedure alleviates pain and prevents further complications, allowing you to maintain your natural smile and oral health.
  • Implants Item descriptionImplants are a transformative solution for replacing missing teeth, providing a sturdy foundation that mimics the function of natural roots. By securely anchoring artificial teeth, implants restore your smile’s appearance and bite, offering long-lasting durability and confidence in your oral health.
