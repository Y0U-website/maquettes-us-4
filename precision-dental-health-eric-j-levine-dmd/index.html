Periodontal care is essential for safeguarding your gum health and preventing tooth loss. At Precision Dental Health, we emphasize the significance of regular periodontal assessments as the cornerstone of lasting dental wellness. By taking a proactive stance, we help maintain your oral health, minimize inflammation, and reduce the risk of more serious health issues in the future.

Periodontal disease is an infection of the gums and the supporting structures of the teeth. It begins with plaque buildup on the teeth, leading to inflammation in the gums. If left untreated, this inflammation can advance to more severe stages, where the bone and tissues supporting your teeth are damaged.
