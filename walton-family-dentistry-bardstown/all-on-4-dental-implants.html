The Process for All-on-4 Dental Implants in Bardstown

At Walton Family Dentistry, we make receiving All-on-4 dental implants as simple and comfortable as possible. Our team uses state-of-the-art technology and a personalized approach to ensure the best results for each patient.

1. Initial Consultation and Evaluation

Your journey with All-on-4 implants begins with an initial consultation. Dr. Walton will thoroughly assess your oral health and review your medical history. During this evaluation, we may take digital X-rays, 3D scans, and CBCT imaging to evaluate the structure of your jawbone and determine if you are a good candidate for the procedure. This comprehensive assessment helps us create a customized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

2. Treatment Planning

Once we’ve completed the evaluation, we’ll use advanced technology such as 3D imaging and digital impressions to plan your treatment precisely. This allows us to determine the optimal placement of the implants and the most suitable design for your prosthetic teeth. During this phase, Dr. Walton will explain the entire process, including timelines, costs, and available financing options. We offer flexible payment plans, including CareCredit, cash, credit/debit cards, and our membership plan, making your treatment affordable and accessible.

4. Healing and Integration

After the implant placement, your jawbone will undergo a healing phase known as osseointegration, where the bone fuses with the implants. This process takes several months but is critical for ensuring the long-term stability of your implants. During this period, Dr. Walton will provide care instructions and monitor your progress through regular follow-up visits. Maintaining a soft diet and practicing good oral hygiene are essential during this stage.

5. Final Restoration

Once your implants fully integrate with your jawbone, we’ll replace your temporary prosthetic teeth with permanent, custom-made restorations. These look and feel like natural teeth, ensuring a comfortable fit and an aesthetic, functional smile. Dr. Walton will make any necessary adjustments to ensure your bite is perfect and you’re fully satisfied with your new smile.

6. Aftercare and Maintenance

Aftercare is vital to ensuring the longevity of your All-on-4 implants. We will provide personalized instructions for caring for your new teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and mouthwash keep your implants in excellent condition. Additionally, we recommend regular check-ups at Walton Family Dentistry to monitor the health of your implants and address any concerns.
