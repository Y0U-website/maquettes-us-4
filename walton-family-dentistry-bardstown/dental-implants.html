The Process for Getting Dental Implants in Bardstown

Getting dental implants at Walton Family Dentistry involves a few key steps, each supported by advanced technology, comfort-focused care, and flexible financial options.

Step 1: Personalized Consultation

Dr. Walton begins with a comprehensive evaluation using our CBCT and digital scanners to capture high-resolution images of your jaw. These tools allow us to plan your implant placement with precision. During this visit, we’ll review financing options such as CareCredit, our membership plan, or payment by card or cash to fit your budget. Feel free to relax with a blanket or hot beverage while we discuss your goals.

Step 2: Implant Placement

We place the titanium implant post into your jawbone using 3D imaging and CAD/CAM technology to guide the procedure. Our digital tools ensure accuracy and minimize healing time. If you’re nervous, nitrous oxide or sedation medicine is available to keep you calm and comfortable. The process is quick, and many patients enjoy same-day appointments.

Step 3: Healing and Integration

The implant integrates with your jawbone over several months. To support healing, we use soft tissue lasers and digital X-rays to monitor progress. You can stay connected with free Wi-Fi during follow-up visits, and our office is fully wheelchair accessible for easy access.

Step 4: Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we use digital impressions to design and place your permanent crown, bridge, or denture. Our intraoral camera helps us match the color and contour of your natural teeth for a flawless finish. Whether you’re using your membership plan or another payment method, our staff will make this final step smooth and stress-free.

Who Benefits Most from Dental Implants?

Patients who choose dental implants in Bardstown often report increased confidence, improved speech, and a youthful smile. Dental implants work well for people who:

  • Have lost one or more teeth.
  • Struggle with ill-fitting dentures.
  • Want a permanent, low-maintenance solution.
  • Seek to prevent jawbone loss and protect facial structure.

What to Expect After Getting Dental Implants

Taking care of your implants is easy and similar to caring for natural teeth:

Our team offers guidance and support every step of the way, from post-op care to long-term maintenance.

  • Brush and floss daily.
  • Visit us regularly for checkups and cleanings.
  • Avoid chewing on hard items like ice or pens.
  • Don’t smoke—healing and implant success rates improve without it.
