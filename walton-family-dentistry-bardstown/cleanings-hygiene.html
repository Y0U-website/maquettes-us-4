Dental Cleanings and Exams for Every Member of the Family

We know that dental needs change throughout life, so we offer dental cleanings and exams for everyone in the family. Whether your child is getting their first cleaning or you’re managing your oral health as an adult or senior, we’re here for you.

For Children

Starting healthy oral habits early is key to a lifetime of good dental health. Regular cleanings help detect early signs of tooth decay, and exams allow us to track the development of their teeth. Our pediatric care ensures that your child gets the right preventive care to avoid cavities and issues like alignment problems and overcrowding later on.

For Teenagers

Teenagers undergo rapid changes, and their dental needs evolve as they grow. Routine checkups help us address common concerns, such as the need for braces, wisdom teeth removal, or other orthodontic treatments. Since their risk for cavities and gum disease increases due to diet, hygiene habits, and hormones, regular visits are essential during this life phase.

For Adults

As an adult, it’s crucial to maintain regular dental cleanings and exams to prevent issues like gum disease, cavities, and tooth wear. Regular visits allow Dr. Walton to catch any potential problems early, providing the opportunity for more timely and affordable treatments. These checkups also contribute to your overall health, ensuring your teeth and gums remain in top condition.

For Seniors

Our dental needs change as we age, and regular visits become even more important. Seniors often experience dry mouth, gum recession, and tooth sensitivity. At Walton Family Dentistry, we help you maintain your natural teeth and gums while addressing issues like dentures, crowns, and other dental work. Regular visits prevent tooth loss and ensure your smile stays healthy.

How Often Should You Visit Us?

While we’d love to catch up weekly, we recommend scheduling a dental cleaning and exam every six months. Routine checkups help us catch potential problems early before they become more severe. By visiting us twice a year, you ensure that your teeth and gums remain in great condition and reduce the need for costly treatments later. Fortunately, most dental insurances cover semi-annual dental cleanings and exams. Additionally, we offer flexible financing options.
