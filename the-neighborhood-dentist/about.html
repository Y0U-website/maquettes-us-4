Get to Know Your Dentists

Dr. Christina Lundin

Growing up in West Dundee, Illinois, she was inspired by her father, a dentist, and her mother, a dental hygienist. From a young age, she admired her dad’s ability to blend science and artistry to help others, and she spent her teenage summers assisting at his office. Although she briefly considered becoming a surgeon, she ultimately chose dentistry for its hands-on creativity, rewarding patient relationships, and better work-life balance.

Dr. Lundin graduated Salutatorian from St. Edward’s High School in Elgin, Illinois, and went on to attend Marquette University as a Pre-Dental Scholar. This selective program allowed her to transition directly into Marquette University School of Dentistry after three years of undergraduate studies. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree summa cum laude and has called the Milwaukee area home ever since.

Her goal has always been to build a practice that’s dependable, compassionate, and centered around personalized care. She believes in listening closely, explaining thoroughly, and delivering high-quality dentistry in a space where patients feel truly cared for. Dr. Lundin has a particular passion for cosmetic and restorative dentistry, especially crown work and smile makeovers.

Outside the office, Dr. Lundin and her husband, Bill, stay busy keeping up with their two young children, Laine and Theo. They love family walks, visiting local parks and the zoo, trying new restaurants, and cheering on their favorite sports teams. Both Marquette grads, they have deep roots in the Milwaukee area and are proud to call it home.

What to Expect

Your Comfort Comes First from Day One

We want your first appointment at The Neighborhood Dentist to feel welcoming, upbeat, and easy. From the moment you walk in, our team will guide you through every part of your visit, answer your questions, and help you feel at ease.

Advanced Care with a Personal Touch

Why You&#x27;ll Love Our Amenities and Technology

We’ve invested in the latest equipment to help deliver quicker, safer, more comfortable care. From same-day crowns to digital X-rays, our technology helps us detect issues earlier and treat them more efficiently, so you can spend more time smiling.

Same-Day Crowns

No more multiple visits or temporary fixes. Our CEREC technology allows us to craft and place beautiful, custom crowns all in one visit, saving you time and restoring your smile fast.

Digital X-Rays

With reduced radiation and clearer images, our digital X-rays make it easier to diagnose problems and discuss treatment with you in real time—plus, they’re safer for your family.

suresmile & Custom Whitening Trays

Looking to upgrade your smile? We offer SureSmile clear aligners for straightening teeth comfortably and discreetly. For brighter smiles, we provide at-home whitening trays that fit your schedule and are gentler on teeth.
