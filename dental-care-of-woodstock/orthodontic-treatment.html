What to expect

  • 01 Assessment Digital x-rays and a bite assessment determine whether braces, aligners, or a night guard fit your situation, and roughly how long treatment will take.
  • 02 Fitting Braces are bonded to the teeth in one visit. Invisalign aligners are custom-made from a digital scan and swapped out every couple of weeks.
  • 03 Adjustments Periodic check-ins — every 4–8 weeks for braces — track progress and make small adjustments as teeth move.
  • 04 Retention After active treatment, a retainer holds teeth in their new position. Skipping this step is the most common reason teeth shift back.

Frequently asked

Braces or Invisalign — which is right for me?

It depends on the complexity of the correction needed and personal preference. Invisalign suits mild-to-moderate cases and is removable; braces handle more complex movements. We’ll recommend one honestly rather than upsell the pricier option.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Typically 12–24 months depending on the case, though minor corrections can be faster.

What is a night guard for?

It protects teeth from the wear of nighttime grinding (bruxism) and can ease jaw tension associated with TMJ issues.

Can adults get braces or Invisalign?

Yes — a meaningful share of orthodontic patients are adults. Teeth can be moved at any age; the main difference is that treatment can take slightly longer than in adolescents.
