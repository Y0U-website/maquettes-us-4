Caring for Partial Dentures

● Proper care is what makes partial dentures last and helps them retain their great condition.

● Clean your prosthetics daily by soft brushing and denture cleaner to avoid plaques and food accumulation.

● Do remove your prosthetics at night for gum rest to prevent discomfort.

● Avoid using hot water as it can warp the denture material.

● Minimize consumption of hard foods as well as sticky foodstuffs, which may either damage or dislodge your denture.

● Regular visits to your dentist’s office for checkups and professional cleaning will keep your partial denture well-fitting and your oral health maintained.
