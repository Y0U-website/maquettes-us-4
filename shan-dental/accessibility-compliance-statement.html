ACCESSIBILITY COMPLIANCE STATEMENT

AMERICANS WITH DISABILITIES ACT (ADA) COMPLIANCE

COMMITMENT TO ACCESSIBILITY

Shan Dental is committed to ensuring equal access to dental care for all patients, including individuals with disabilities, in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (ADA), Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, and applicable state accessibility laws.

PHYSICAL ACCESSIBILITY

● ADA-compliant parking spaces with proper signage

● Wheelchair-accessible entrances and operatories

● Appropriate door widths and corridor space

COMMUNICATION ACCESSIBILITY

We provide auxiliary aids and services, including:

● Qualified sign language interpreters (upon 48-hour advance notice)

● Written materials in large print or alternative formats

● Verbal description of procedures and policies

DIGITAL ACCESSIBILITY

Our website and digital platforms are designed to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards, ensuring compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies.

REASONABLE ACCOMMODATIONS

We will make reasonable modifications to policies, practices, and procedures to ensure equal access to dental services, unless such modifications would fundamentally alter the nature of our services.
