Get to Know

Dr. Ailey Blystone

Dr. Ailey Blystone’s journey to dentistry is as unique as her approach to patient care. Born in the Philippines and raised in Kentucky, Dr. Blystone’s early experiences with dental issues ignited her passion for the field. Her personal struggles with crooked teeth and other dental issues fueled her determination to become a dentist who could make a difference. After earning her Bachelor’s Degree in Human Nutrition from the University of Kentucky, she pursued her Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree at one of the top leading dental schools in the country, University of California San Francisco.

Your Tooth Café Experience

What to Expect

Your first visit to Tooth Café is designed to be refreshingly different. Start by unwinding in our cozy lounge with a complimentary coffee from our espresso machine. During your exam, Dr. Blystone will take the time to get to know you, understand your dental history, and discuss your dental goals. We’ll perform a comprehensive examination, take necessary X-rays, digitally scan your teeth, and create a personalized treatment plan. It’s all about your comfort and your smile.

What to Bring

For your first visit, please bring your ID, insurance information (if applicable), and a list of any medications you’re taking. Most importantly, bring your questions and concerns we’re here to address them all!

Spreading Smiles

How We Give Back

At Tooth Café, giving back to our community is at the heart of what we do. We are honored to partner with Up for Women and Children, a remarkable non-profit dedicated to empowering homeless women and children in Louisville.

For every new patient that joins our practice, we make a meaningful contribution to help provide essential services such as shelter, utilities, and access to basic necessities like hot showers. When you choose us for your dental care, you’re not just improving your smile — you’re becoming part of a larger mission to uplift those in need. Your support helps keep the lights on, warm showers running, and provides a safe space for women and children to rebuild their lives, giving them the dignity and hope they deserve.
