Instagram icon YouTube icon Google icon A Tradition of Excellence in Dental Care and Facial Aesthetics Dr. Andrew P. Stadler, a proud native of Kearney, Nebraska, is the heart and soul behind Stadler Dental Care in Columbus. With a distinguished career that began after obtaining his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1999 from the University of Nebraska College of Dentistry, Dr. Stadler has dedicated over two decades to perfecting the art of dentistry and facial aesthetics.

Founding Stadler Dental Care

In September 2000, Dr. Stadler’s vision of a patient-focused, innovative dental practice came to life when he founded Stadler Dental Care. This state-of-the-art facility, redesigned in May 2008 with the help of Unthank Design Group, reflects Dr. Stadler’s commitment to providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

A Comprehensive Approach to Dentistry

Dr. Stadler’s practice encompasses a broad spectrum of dental services, with a particular passion for restoring smiles and enhancing facial esthetics. He has mastered the art of smile makeovers utilizing advanced techniques in Crowns, Bridges, Veneers, and both removable and fixed Dentures. Furthermore, Dr. Stadler extends his expertise to the realms of Botox, Dermal Fillers, PDO threads, and Kybella, offering therapeutic relief from pain, cosmetic improvements, and facial feature enhancements.

Professional Affiliations

A lifelong learner and respected figure in the dental community, Dr. Stadler is an active member of the International Academy of Facial Aesthetics, the American Dental Association, and the Nebraska Dental Association. His contributions to the field were recognized in 2008 when he was named "Outstanding Young Professional" by the Nebraska Dental Association. Beyond his clinical excellence, Dr. Stadler has also served as North District president and on the Legislative Council for the Nebraska Dental Association, showcasing his commitment to advancing the profession.

A Life Rooted in Family and Community

Beyond his professional achievements, Dr. Stadler is a devoted family man, celebrating over 30 years of marriage with his wife, Cherie, a dedicated music educator. Their shared love for music and education has been a cornerstone of their life together, along with their deep commitment to family, demonstrated through their active involvement in their children and granddaughter’s lives.

As a man of faith, Dr. Stadler contributes to his community as an Elder, lay leader, and communion assistant at Peace Lutheran Church. He is also recognized for his compassionate work with Comfort Dog Eden, a Lutheran Church Charities comfort dog. Through this unique outreach ministry, Dr. Stadler and Eden provide mercy, compassion, and comfort to those in need, embodying the practice’s ethos of caring and support.
