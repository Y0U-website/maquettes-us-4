Learn About Today’s Dental

At Today’s Dental we care for your family like our own.

We get to know our patients on a personal level so we can help them establish and reach their dental goals. We want to celebrate your successes and encourage you to live a healthy life.

Personal Relationships We’re best known for the way we treat our patients — and that’s as if you’re family! We take the time to get to know you personally so we can deliver the highest quality of care for your individual needs. If you ever have questions or concerns, we listen carefully. We seek to understand your goals, values, obstacles, and commitment to your oral health. We truly care.

Welcoming Environment Everyone is welcome here. No matter your oral health history or teeth problems, we want you to come see us! We care about helping you improve from where you are at now more than we care about how you’ve taken care of your teeth in the past. Our team will welcome you with happy smiles and friendly attitudes. We are judgement-free and look forward to helping you understand your smile!

Exceptional Patient Experience We take customer service to the next level by paying attention to the ease and comfort of your visit. We offer many patient conveniences — such as online scheduling, new patient gifts, after-hours courtesy calls, warm scented towels, and Netflix streaming — to ensure each visit is as smooth and relaxing as possible. These amenities combined with our patient-focused approach to dental care are all aimed at exciting you about taking care of your smile and changing your perception of dentistry into something to look forward to!

Comprehensive Services At Today’s Dental, we don’t miss a detail in your health or treatments. With thorough exams, customized preventative treatments and a wide variety of services in general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, we are able to provide comprehensive care under one roof. We are also extremely skilled and qualified in treating obstructive sleep apnea, replacing missing teeth and improving smiles through cosmetic smile enhancement. Whatever your need, we are happy to help!

Advanced Technology We stay up to date on the most cutting-edge dental techniques and technologies to help us better serve you. Same-day crowns, digital impressions & x-rays, 3D printing, intraoral scanning and paperless charting include a few of the many technologies we utilize to ensure a safe, accurate, comfortable and convenient experience for you.

Patient Education Taking proper care of your smile goes beyond routine brushing, flossing and dental visits. It’s important to understand how your diet, nutrition, sleep patterns and whole-body health play a role in the health of your mouth and teeth. Our office is passionate about providing thorough patient education at every visit and will teach you the “whys” behind your oral health condition, treatment options and recommended at-home prevention plan. Doing so will aid us in putting you on the path to a lifetime of healthy smiles!

Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Joshua Eichelberger

Dr. Josh Eichelberger was born and raised in Illinois, where he attended Southern Illinois University, School of Dental Medicine where he graduated with honors. He moved to Chandler, AZ to enjoy the warmer weather and explore different parts of the country.

Dr. Josh thrives to provide top notch dentistry while connecting with his patients on a more personal level. He wants his patients to enjoy going to the dentist and feel confident they are receiving the best quality of care.

In his free time, Dr. Josh loves to exercise, hike, and try out new restaurants. His favorite movies are the Harry Potter series and he loves cinnamon rolls.

Dr. Tiffani Eichelberger

Originally from Illinois, Dr. Tiffani began her career in the dental field as a dental hygienist before pursuing her dream of becoming a dentist. With a strong foundation in patient care and a deep passion for dentistry, she is committed to helping patients feel comfortable, confident, and well-cared for at every visit.

Dr. Tiffani is recently married to Dr. Josh, and together they are excited to be growing their family. Outside of the office, they enjoy watching movies, exploring new restaurants, and spending quality time together.

Dr. Tiffani and Dr. Josh met during dental school at Southern Illinois University in Alton, IL, and are grateful for the opportunity to serve the Ahwatukee community together.
