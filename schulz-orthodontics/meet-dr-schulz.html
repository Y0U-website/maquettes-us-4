Meet Dr. Schulz

I am blessed to work in a profession that over the course of a couple years can change someone’s image of themselves so dramatically. Seeing their confidence improve and their personality shine—that’s what it is all about! We are all connected, and I hope that is some way, our patients experience with us can help them on their journey to success.

The best part of my day is hearing about all the fantastic activities and experiences our patients share with us. Being in small communities like Traverse City and Charlevoix, getting to know our patients and families is really getting to know our town. I love feeling connected to them and their journey. Orthodontics allows me to develop relationships while creating smiles, and that’s awesome.

Education and Continuing Education

I received my Bachelor’s Degree from Kalamazoo College in Biology. I attended the University of Michigan School of Dentistry for my Doctorate of Dental Surgery and Master of Science in Orthodontics.

I am passionate about getting better every day. I try to grow a little daily, to do things differently. Through continuing education, I get to grow professionally as well. I am always looking for ways to make our treatment results better in less time for maximum patient comfort and benefit. That includes making our orthodontic team the best they can be through education and using the latest clinically proven techniques for success.

I have been active in organized dentistry, both in the ADA and the American Association of Orthodontists, for the past 20 years. I am honored to serve as the President of the Great Lakes Association of Orthodontics for 2024-25. I attend continuing education seminars at annual orthodontic meetings at the University of Michigan and through the Resort District Dental Society to keep current with the latest developments in dentistry and orthodontics.

Professional Memberships

Outside the Office I grew up in Battle Creek, MI, the third of four children, the son of a High School Administrator and Christian Youth Minister. I have lived in Traverse City since 2003. I love living in Northern Michigan for the great community spirit, its natural beauty, and endless activities.

I am married to Kara, and we have two daughters and a son. We have three kids in college right now, and they keep us on our toes. We also have a dog and a cat. It’s a busy household, and spending time with my family is my number-one priority.

When I’m not in the office or at home, my favorite thing to do is downhill skiing with my family in winter. I also like to golf and spend time on the lake in the summer.
