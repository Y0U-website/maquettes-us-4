Mission

At PAS Smiles Spring TX, we are on a mission to provide you high quality dental care. We are setting up a family environment that is equipped with modern technology & techniques.

Our dentist – Dr. Romil Pasrija has been contributing generously back to society and is working towards her passion of opening a charitable dental clinic where she could serve the needy people.

Core Values

  • Predictable – We will never recommend something you don’t need
  • Affordable – We want to be the most affordable dental clinic yet provide you the highest quality dental care.
  • Spectacular- Smiles on us. We want a healthier oral health and a spectacular smile for everyone
  • Specialised Support Service

Why choose us

You are important to us and we mean it. Our mission is to provide you with exceptional dental care using the latest technological advancements. Every patient is an elite patient to us. We will treat you with utmost care, providing you with special attention to your comfort, time and your needs. Our goal is just not to meet your expectations but to exceed them.

Feel free to browse our website and get to know more about our services, our passionate Dentist, and our lovable team. Our belief is that every patient is like family and we will treat you the same.
