Dr. Romil Pasrija

Dr. Romil Pasrija obtained her Advanced Training and Doctorate of Dental Medicine DMD from Boston University Henry M. School of Dental Medicine.After practicing several years as an associate dentist, Dr. Romil founded Passmiles near her hometown of Houston, Texas. Becoming a Dentist and serving people and community was always her dream.

Dr. Romil is an active member of American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association and Greater Houston Dental Society. Our Doctor is passionate about dentistry and loves to work with all age groups, solving patient’s problem with her best professional knowledge and professional skills and years of experience. She encourages her patients to get educated and interested in oral health.

Dr. Romil is skilled with wide range of services including crown and bridges, Dentures, Root canals, Nitrous Sedation, cosmetic Dentistry. Patients appreciate her attention to detail and her thorough explanation through the course of their treatment.

She is committed to furthering her knowledge of skills of dental technology and procedures and often attends new CE courses.

Additionally, she worked as volunteer at Shock and Trauma ICU unit at Memorial Herman Head and Neck emergencies. She also participated in oral screening research program at UT school of Dentistry. She also volunteered at Breast Cancer Society. She also organized few oral health check up camps at New Jersey and Houston.

Besides Dentistry Dr. Romil enjoys music, Paintings and loves spending time with her loving and supportive husband Sanjay and her two musketeers Rakshan and Dev.
