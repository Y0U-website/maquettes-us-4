Dental Crowns

Dental Crown

Protect and Strengthen Damaged Teeth

Dental crowns are a dependable solution for restoring teeth that have been weakened by decay, fractures, or previous dental work. At East Bay Dental, we use crowns to reinforce and protect compromised teeth while restoring their natural shape and function. A well-crafted crown fits securely over the existing tooth, offering long-term strength and stability.

Crafted for a Natural Look and Fit

Our crowns are custom-designed to match the color, contour, and size of your surrounding teeth for a result that feels seamless. Using high-quality materials and precise imaging, we ensure your crown blends naturally into your smile. Whether you need a crown on a molar or a tooth in the front of your mouth, our focus is on creating a restoration that feels comfortable and looks beautifully authentic.

Versatile Treatment for Many Dental Concerns

Crowns can solve a wide range of dental issues—from protecting a tooth after a root canal to restoring one that is cracked or heavily worn. They can also improve the appearance of misshapen or severely discolored teeth. With their durability and versatility, crowns offer both functional and cosmetic benefits, helping you chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Expert Care from Start to Finish

Your crown treatment at East Bay Dental begins with a thorough evaluation to ensure a crown is the right choice for your needs. From preparing the tooth to placing the final restoration, we prioritize precision and patient comfort at every step. If you’re looking to restore strength, stability, and beauty to a damaged tooth, our team is here to provide a dependable and long-lasting solution.
