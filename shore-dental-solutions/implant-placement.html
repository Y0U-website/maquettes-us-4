Implant Placement

Our office is proud to offer comprehensive implant placement services to help restore your smile with confidence.

The process begins with a thorough consultation, during which Dr. King will evaluate your oral health, review your dental and medical history, and determine whether you are a candidate for dental implants.

Following the consultation, a cone beam CT scan is taken to provide a detailed, three-dimensional view of your bone structure, allowing Dr. King and her team of experienced oral surgeons to carefully analyze bone density, volume, and anatomical landmarks. ‍

Based on this in-depth assessment, her team will determine the most appropriate approach for implant placement and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your unique needs.
