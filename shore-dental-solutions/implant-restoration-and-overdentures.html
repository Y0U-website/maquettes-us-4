Dental Implants vs. Dentures and Bridges

Dental implants stand apart from traditional dentures and bridges in several ways:

  • Stability: Unlike removable dentures or bridges, implants are fixed directly to the bone, making them stable and secure.
  • Natural Function and Appearance: Implants look, feel, and function like natural teeth, with a strong biting force and no movement during daily activities like eating or speaking.
  • Preservation of Surrounding Teeth: Implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support, ensuring that the health of neighboring teeth remains uncompromised.
  • Longevity: While bridges generally last 7-10 years (less if a root canal is needed), implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.
