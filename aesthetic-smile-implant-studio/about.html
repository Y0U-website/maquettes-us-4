The People Behind Your Smile

Dr. Alena Barysenka is a caring and experienced dentist at Aesthetic Smile & Implant Studio , committed to providing personalized, high-quality dental care for patients of all ages. Her approach combines clinical experience with attention to each patient’s individual needs, comfort, and oral health goals.

Originally from Belarus, Dr. Barysenka began her dental career after earning her dentistry degree from Belarusian State Medical University in 2004. She practiced dentistry for five years before continuing her education in the United States. In 2014, she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from New York University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Barysenka has experience across multiple areas of dentistry, with a particular interest in family and cosmetic dental care. She believes that excellent dentistry involves not only treating dental concerns but also helping patients understand their oral health and feel confident about their smiles.

Committed to continued professional growth, Dr. Barysenka regularly expands her knowledge of modern dental techniques, technology, and advances in cosmetic and family dentistry. Her warm, attentive approach helps patients feel comfortable while receiving dental care tailored to their individual needs.

Dr. Rustam Zakiyev is a dedicated and compassionate dentist at Aesthetic Smile & Implant Studio , providing personalized dental care with an emphasis on comfort, quality, and long-term oral health.

Originally from the Republic of Azerbaijan, Dr. Zakiyev developed his passion for dentistry early in his career and graduated at the top of his class with a dental physician’s degree. After moving to the United States, he continued his professional education and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Zakiyev focuses on general and restorative dentistry, helping patients maintain healthy smiles while restoring the function and appearance of damaged or compromised teeth. He is committed to staying current with modern dental techniques, technology, and treatment approaches to provide each patient with thoughtful, individualized care.

What Dr. Zakiyev values most about dentistry is the opportunity to make a meaningful difference in his patients’ lives. His attentive and patient-centered approach helps create a comfortable dental experience while working toward healthy, confident smiles.

Dr. Won-Sang Lee is an experienced oral surgeon at Aesthetic Smile & Implant Studio , providing specialized surgical care with a strong focus on patient comfort, precision, and successful treatment outcomes.

Dr. Lee earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Columbia University and completed advanced training in oral and maxillofacial surgery at leading medical centers in New York City.

In addition to caring for patients at Aesthetic Smile & Implant Studio , Dr. Lee serves as an Oral Surgery Attending at Metropolitan Hospital Center, where he helps supervise and train oral surgery residents.

His clinical expertise includes tooth and surgical extractions, dental implant placement and repair, corrective jaw surgery, and other procedures involving the mouth, jaw, and facial structures. Dr. Lee takes an individualized approach to every case, carefully explaining treatment options and helping patients feel comfortable throughout their care.

Dr. Anya Rossinskaya is a general dentist who believes exceptional dentistry begins with trust, communication, and truly understanding each patient. She takes the time to listen, explain the why behind every recommendation, and make sure her patients feel informed and confident in their care.

Known for her thoughtful and precise approach, Dr. Rossinskaya strives to make every visit comfortable and personal. Whether she is restoring a tooth, enhancing a smile, or caring for a younger patient, she combines attention to detail with a gentle, compassionate touch.

For Dr. Rossinskaya , great dentistry is about more than treating teeth it’s about building lasting relationships and helping patients feel comfortable, cared for, and proud of their smiles.
