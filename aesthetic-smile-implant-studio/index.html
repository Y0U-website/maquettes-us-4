Experienced & Caring Team State-of-the-Art Technology Beautiful, Healthy Smiles Your Trusted Dentist in Dover, New Jersey Your smile is personal. Your dental care should be too.

At Aesthetic Smile & Implant Studio , we combine modern dentistry, personalized treatment planning, and an aesthetic approach to help patients throughout Dover and the surrounding Morris County community achieve healthier, more confident smiles.

Led by Dr. Alena Barysenka, DDS , our dental team provides comprehensive care ranging from preventive and restorative dentistry to cosmetic smile enhancements, dental implants, and oral surgery.

Together with Dr. Rustam Zakiyev, DMD , Dr. Won-Sang Lee, DDS , Oral Surgeon, and Dr. Anya Rossinskaya, DMD , Dr. Barysenka offers patients the convenience of receiving many different types of dental treatment in one practice.

Excellent dentistry isn’t simply about fixing teeth. It’s about understanding how your teeth, bite, gums, facial features, and smile work together.

At Aesthetic Smile & Implant Studio , we take a comprehensive approach to dental care. We begin by listening to your concerns and understanding what you would like to change or improve. After evaluating your oral health, we discuss appropriate treatment options and develop a personalized plan focused on health, function, and aesthetics.

Our multidisciplinary dental team allows patients to receive preventive, restorative, cosmetic, implant, and surgical dental care in one convenient Dover location.
