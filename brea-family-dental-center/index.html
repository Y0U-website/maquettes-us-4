The practice

Modern equipment and techniques mean more precise, comfortable treatments.

Emergency Dentistry Emergency dental care addresses oral health problems that need immediate attention, from sudden toothaches to traumatic injuries. At Brea Family Dental Center, we provide prompt, gentle treatment to relieve pain and protect your smile.

Preventative Dentistry Protect your smile with care designed to keep problems at bay. From thorough exams to professional cleanings, we help you maintain healthy teeth and gums for years to come.

Restorative Dentistry Restore your smile with solutions designed to bring back full function and comfort. From natural-looking crowns to durable fillings, we repair damage so you can eat, speak, and smile without hesitation.

Services

Our team utilizes a holistic approach in performing a thorough examination to not only address your immediate concerns but also focus on preventative oral healthcare.

Oral Surgery Receive expert care for procedures that require precision and skill. From extractions to bone grafting, we use advanced techniques to ensure your comfort and optimal healing.

Dental Implants Dental implants are a permanent tooth-replacement solution that mimics the look, feel, and function of natural teeth. We provide complete implant care — from initial planning through final restoration — right here at Brea Family Dental Center.

Cosmetic Dentistry Enhance your smile with treatments focused on improving both appearance and confidence. From professional teeth whitening to custom veneers that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, we help you achieve the look you want.
