0 Meet Our Dentists Dr. Kamaira “Dr. K” Philips Dr. Kamaira “Dr. K” Philips is an experienced general dentist and owner of Yadkin River Dental Spa, where she is passionate about changing the way people feel about going to the dentist. Her approach combines high-quality, comprehensive dental care with comfort-focused amenities and a calming, personalized experience designed to help patients feel relaxed, heard, and cared for.

Dr. Philips enjoys all aspects of general dentistry, with special interests in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, oral surgery, dentures, TMJ therapy, and helping anxious patients feel comfortable in the dental chair. She is committed to continuing education and advanced technology to provide modern, comprehensive care for patients in the Yadkin Valley community.

Outside the office, Dr. Philips enjoys singing classic rock with her local band, spending time with her husband and dogs, supporting small businesses, and serving her community through volunteer dental care and public health initiatives.

At Yadkin River Dental Spa, Dr. Philips and her team believe dental visits can feel different — more comfortable, more personal, and even something patients look forward to.

Dr. Brian “Dr. Brian” Delehanty

Dr. Brian Delehanty is a general dentist who is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident about their dental care. He believes in treating every patient with kindness, honesty, and respect while providing high-quality dentistry in a calm and welcoming environment.

Originally from Mount Holly, North Carolina, Dr. Brian earned his degree in Biomedical Engineering from the University of South Carolina before going on to earn his dental degree from the UNC Adams School of Dentistry.

Dr. Brian enjoys building relationships with patients and helping people improve both their oral health and their smiles. He is committed to continuing education and modern dental technology so he can provide excellent care tailored to each patient’s needs.
