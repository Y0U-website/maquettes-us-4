Your First Visit

Your first dental appointment at Rockport Family Dental will be about getting acquainted with each other. First, we will discuss your goals and what ultimately brought you to our practice, review and explain treatment options and learn about your medical history. After, a clinical evaluation will be performed and all questions answered.

Some routine dental services, if time permits, can be performed the same day as the consultation. In certain circumstance such as more complex services, or even if you have issues in your medical history, we will schedule a second appointment with you for treatment. At that appointment, we’ll thoroughly review our findings and present a detailed dental treatment plan that addresses your dental health needs and esthetic goals.
