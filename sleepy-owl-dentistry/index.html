The practice

Meet the Team At Sleepy Owl Dentistry, we strive to provide a safe and comfortable environment for your child while providing the highest quality of care. Our team of experienced professionals works hard to make sure that each child has the best experience possible and that all of their needs are met.

Dr. Eric Vieth Dr. Eric Vieth is a pediatric dentist, board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. He grew up in Buffalo, New York, received his undergraduate degree at the State University of New York at Geneseo, and attended dental school and completed his residency in Pediatric Dentistry at the State University of New York at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine.

Since finishing his education, Dr. Eric has focused on providing care for children with severe early childhood caries alongside an anesthesiologist with the use of IV sedation. Over the past several years, he has helped several thousand children receive necessary dental treatment in a stress-free environment. Dr. Dolan Frye Dr. Dolan Frye is a nationally board-certified pediatric dentist and a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Originally from Greenville, South Carolina, he earned his Bachelor of Science degree in Biological Sciences from Clemson University. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical University of South Carolina.

Following dental school, Dr. Dolan completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of Florida. He then began his career with the Indian Health Service in Taos, New Mexico, where his work with underserved children inspired him to pursue advanced training in pediatric dentistry.

We exclusively collaborate with the region’s leading board-certified anesthesia groups to provide hospital-grade care right here in our office.
