Dental Implants

0 Dental Implants Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the roots of missing teeth. They provide a stable foundation for a dental restoration, making them a durable and natural-feeling option for tooth replacement.

Our Frisco, Colorado team uses advanced imaging tools to ensure precise placement and optimal results. Throughout the process, you’ll have a clear understanding of each step and what to expect because transparency isn’t just our policy; it’s how we build trust.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Permanent, stable tooth replacement that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth

Preserves jawbone density and prevents the facial changes associated with tooth loss

They do not need support from other teeth, leaving your neighboring teeth strong and safe
