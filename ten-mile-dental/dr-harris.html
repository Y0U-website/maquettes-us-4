Dr. Colleen Harris

Dr. Harris is committed to lifelong learning in all areas of general dentistry, as she is continually studying advanced techniques and furthering her education to best care for her patients. While Dr. Harris enjoys comprehensive, patient-centered treatment planning, providing meticulous conservative and esthetic restorative dentistry is her passion. Dr. Harris believes educating patients about their oral health not only empowers informed decision-making regarding dental care, it creates a meaningful foundation to improve treatment outcomes and inspires self-confidence.

Aside from dentistry, Dr. Harris enjoys traveling and spending time with family and friends. As a Wisconsin native, water sports such as swimming, water skiing, and wake surfing are second nature to her. She grew up learning to ski at Breckenridge at the age of five. To this day, she enjoys skiing, hiking, mountain biking, and exploring all of Colorado’s mountain ranges. She and her husband, Ryan – a native of Colorado, are thrilled to work and live in Summit County.

“My goal is to provide sincerely gentle, honest, and thorough dental care precisely tailored to each patient.”
