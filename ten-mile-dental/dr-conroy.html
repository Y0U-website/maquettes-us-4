Dr. Conroy

Samantha Conroy, DDS, earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology with a minor in Chemistry from Missouri State University in Springfield, Missouri. She then completed her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Dentistry. Following graduation, Dr. Conroy practiced in a suburb of Kansas City before relocating to Summit County, where she has proudly served the community for the past several years.

Outside of the office, Dr. Conroy enjoys spending time with her husband, Mike, and their two Australian Shepherds, Briggs and Hoz, often exploring the outdoors together. Summit County has been an oasis for their adventurous lifestyle. She enjoys hiking, skiing, biking, running, and traveling, while also valuing quieter moments spent reading and practicing yoga. A devoted Jeopardy! fan, she can often be found watching the nightly episode.

Dr. Conroy recently completed her first marathon at the Chicago Marathon, fundraising for the Alzheimer’s Association in the process, and she frequently travels with her husband as he pursues his goal of running all the World Marathon Majors.

Dr. Conroy looks forward to partnering with you in achieving your dental health goals!
