Dental Services

At Smiles by Baltz, we offer comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages. From preventative cleanings to cosmetic enhancements and restorative treatments, our team is dedicated to providing high-quality, comfortable care to keep your smile healthy and confident.

Routine cleanings are essential to maintaining good oral health. Our general dentistry services include thorough cleanings, digital x-rays, cavity fillings, extractions, and preventative care to protect your teeth and gums. We also provide personalized oral health guidance, helping you build habits that support long-term dental wellness.

Missing or damaged teeth can affect both appearance and function. We offer custom crowns, bridges, partials and full dentures designed for a natural fit and feel. These restorative solutions help rebuild your smile’s strength, restore bite alignment and improve overall confidence—allowing you to chew, speak and smile with ease.

For patients seeking a metal-free option, we provide metal-free partials as well as metal-free crown restorations. These restorations are crafted for durability and aesthetic excellence, blending seamlessly with your natural teeth to restore strength and beauty without the gray lines associated with traditional crowns.

When fabricating these restorations we use digital impressions which provide us with a more precise fit as well as you the patient not having to deal with the gooey impressions.

If you’re looking to enhance your smile’s appearance, our cosmetic dentistry services can help. From professional teeth whitening to veneers and smile makeovers, our team offers solutions tailored to your goals. We focus on creating natural-looking results that complement your unique features.

For patients seeking a discreet way to straighten their teeth, Invisalign clear aligners are a trusted option. Invisalign offers the same effectiveness as traditional braces but with the comfort and convenience of removable, nearly invisible aligners. As a Preferred Provider, our team will create a customized treatment plan to help you achieve a straighter smile. Consultations for orthodontic services are complimentary.

Traditional braces remain a highly effective solution for correcting complex alignment issues. We provide orthodontic care for children, teens and adults, offering a range of options to meet your specific needs. Our team works closely with each patient to ensure the best possible outcome.

Brighten your smile in a single visit with Zoom! Professional Whitening. This in-office procedure can lighten teeth by several shades in under an hour, making it a perfect choice for removing stains caused by coffee, tea, tobacco and everyday wear. Zoom! Whitening offers fast, safe and noticeable results.

The Panthera Classic is a cutting-edge digital sleep apnea device designed for comfort, effectiveness, and adjustability. Manufactured in medical-grade nylon (polyamide 12), it’s lightweight, durable, and free of metal parts. Its low profile allows ease of speaking, drinking, and lip sealing. The device features a precise titration system with interchangeable rods (in 0.5 mm increments over a wide range), minimizes tooth movement by avoiding incisal contact, and aligns with the occlusal plane to reduce stress on the jaw. Plus, it now qualifies for PDAC-Medicare reimbursement under code E0486.
