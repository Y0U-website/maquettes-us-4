New Patients What to Expect Your first visit is an opportunity for us to get to know you and understand your dental health goals. During your appointment, we will: Review your dental and medical history
  • Perform a comprehensive exam, including digital x-rays if needed
  • Discuss your oral health and any recommended treatments
  • Answer any questions you may have about procedures, insurance or financial options

New Patient Forms

To save time during your first appointment, we encourage you to complete our new patient forms in advance. You can print and fill out the forms at home and bring them to your visit.

Insurance & Financial Options We accept a wide range of dental insurance plans and will assist you in filing your claims. While insurance coverage varies, our team will help you maximize your benefits and explain any out-of-pocket expenses upfront.

What is Cherry?

Cherry helps patients get the care they need now and pay over time with simple, flexible monthly payments. There’s no hard credit check, approvals are quick, and some plans even offer 0 % APR. It’s an easy way to make healthcare and wellness treatments more affordable and accessible.
