Dr. Britt Soden McCarthy

Dr. Brittany McCarthy grew up in Bexley and graduated from the Columbus School for Girls. She received a Bachelor of Science degree in Psychology from Duke University, with a minor in Chemistry. She graduated Cum Laude from Boston University with her Doctorate in Dental Medicine. While at Boston University she received awards for excellence in treatment planning and patient care, as well as the award for excellence in the field of Pediatric Dentistry.

In 2013 Dr. McCarthy was honored to receive the Ohio Dental Association’s Rising Star Award to be inducted as a Fellow in the International College of Dentists. She was inducted as a Fellow in the American College of Dentists in 2019. In 2015 (and every year since) Dr. McCarthy has been voted a topDentist by her peers.

Dr. McCarthy has been a member of the Junior Leagues of Cleveland, Washington DC and Columbus. She enjoys volunteering, cooking, being outdoors, cycling and spending time with her husband Michael and their two children.

We are dedicated to giving each of our patients the healthy smile they deserve!
