The practice

At Hi-Tech Family Dentistry, we’re a dental office where your smile and comfort matter most. Led by Dr. Gordon Dannelly, our team brings together experience, care, and cutting-edge technology to provide treatments for all your needs. We build trust and offer clear, honest advice, ensuring you feel confident and comfortable in every step of your dental journey.

Our dentists are driven by a real passion for helping you smile confidently, backed by years of hands-on experience.

We take a personalized approach to care, recognizing that every patient comes in with different goals and concerns. Our team focuses on creating a positive experience while making sure you feel informed and at ease throughout your visit.

At Hi-Tech Family Dentistry, modern technology plays an important role in how we deliver care. Our office is equipped with digital radiography, digital photography, and advanced computer imaging to improve accuracy and efficiency. We also offer CEREC® 3D one-visit crowns, allowing many restorations to be completed in a single appointment for added convenience.

Technology supports what we do, but how we treat our patients matters just as much. We take time to answer questions, explain options clearly, and provide care in a calm, supportive setting so you can move forward with confidence.
