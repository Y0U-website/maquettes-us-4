Dental Implants

Effective Teeth Replacement in Floral Park

Dental implants have revolutionized the field of dentistry, providing a highly effective and long-lasting solution for replacing missing teeth. Dental implants are titanium screws that are surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as artificial tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for dental crowns , bridges , or dentures to be securely attached, restoring the appearance, function, and confidence of patients’ smiles. Dental implants are known for their durability and can last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance.

At Woodbine Dental, Dr. Gill performs a thorough assessment to assess the health of your jawbone and determine the appropriate treatment plan. Bone grafting may be necessary in some cases. Our team will walk you through the process and care for you every step of the way.
