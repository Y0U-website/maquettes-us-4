About Sanam Wright D.D.S.

Born and raised in Coppell, Dr. Wright attended Lakeside Elementary, Valley Ranch Elementary, Coppell Middle School West, and graduated from Coppell High School. She obtained her undergraduate degree from the University of Texas at Dallas and received her dental degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center in Houston. She currently lives in Coppell with her husband Alex and their 3 children.

Dr. Wright has deep roots in Coppell and it has always been her mission give back to the community that she knows and loves. Dr. Wright has a true love of dentistry and is dedicated to providing you with the highest-quality dental care.

​ Memberships: American Dental Association, Texas Dental Association, Dallas County Dental Society, Irving Dental Study Club, Mommy Dentists in Business, and Coppell Chamber of Commerce
