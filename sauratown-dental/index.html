Preventative dental care includes brushing and flossing at home. But to maintain ideal oral health, bi-annual professional teeth cleanings and a thorough oral exam are recommended for the long-term condition of your teeth and gums.

Open communication and in-depth conversations about your needs, goals, and health history help us take better care of you. At every appointment, we update your record so we can better assess your dental needs and adjust our treatment approach.

The overall quality of care we provide is our top concern. We follow the recommended guidelines of the Occupational Safety & Health Administration (OSHA) and the American Dental Association (ADA) to ensure your safety and well-being.

Sauratown Dental is a locally-owned practice that values our patients. We are helpful, approachable, dependable, and every time you walk into the office, you’re greeted with a kind smile. We offer a variety of dental health services from the convenience of one office. Schedule your appointment today and receive quality dental care from a practice your neighbors know and trust.
