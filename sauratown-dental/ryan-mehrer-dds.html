Dr. Katherine Gamble

Dr. Gamble became fascinated with dentistry growing up and immediately made it her goal to join the dental field. Originally from Peoria, IL, she completed her dental degree at Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. Afterwards, she completed a two year Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. In her residency, she received extensive implant and full mouth reconstruction training. From there, she split her time for the next few years between private practice and non-profit care in the greater Oklahoma City area.

Working with each patient, Dr. Gamble plans what will be the best treatment option for the individual. Her passion for helping people is what drives her decisions every day. Whether it is a simple extraction or complex esthetic case, she takes the time to make sure the work is excellent and every patient is comfortable.

Dr. Gamble is excited to be a part of what Dr Mehrer has built at Sauratown Dental and take care of the dental needs of the community.
