What is a Dental Emergency?

A dental emergency occurs whenever some form of trauma causes your tooth to become damaged or dislodged. Severe tooth pain is also a major sign that you should seek emergency dental care. Some examples of dental emergencies include:

If you are experiencing a dental emergency it is often critical that you seek immediate care in order to alleviate pain and provide the best possible chance of tooth survival.

  • Severe Tooth Pain
  • Chipped Tooth
  • Tooth Decay
  • Cracked or Broken Teeth
  • Knocked Out Tooth
  • Lost Crowns & Veneers
  • Denture & Partial Denture adjustments

When Do You Need a Tooth Removed?

There are several possible reasons for why you might need to undergo a dental extraction procedure including:

  • Severe Pain
  • Dental Abscesses (Pus Around the Tooth)
  • Severe Decay
  • Broken or Cracked Beyond Repair
  • Advanced Periodontal Disease
  • Poor Positioning Causing Problems for Surrounding Teeth
  • In Preparation for Orthodontic Treatments

Our Tooth Extraction Services in King, NC

Tooth extraction can relieve pain and infection. We can usually offer same-day treatment and can treat most cases at our office in King, NC. We will refer some cases of wisdom tooth extraction if the teeth are impacted.

Before our dentist can determine if tooth extraction is necessary, an exam is needed to evaluate the tooth and determine if it can be restored or it needs to be removed. We will take an x-ray and examine the tooth clinically and radiographically. Only when our dentist has determined that there is no other way to safe your tooth will he recommend a tooth removal.

The actual dental extraction process can sound scarier than it actually is. Our team will do everything they can to make you as comfortable as possible. We will then numb the area with a local anesthetic to limit your pain during the procedure. Only after our team is sure the area has been thoroughly numbed will our experienced dentist begin the actual tooth removal. After the surgical tooth extraction, our dentist will fill the now empty socket with bone grafting material and suture the gums closed.

Is it Painful to Remove a Tooth?

It is not normally painful to have a tooth removed. We numb the tooth with local anesthetic so there shouldn’t be any pain during the procedure. Pressure is typical and it is normal to feel the work being done, but you shouldn’t feel anything sharp or painful.

Are There Any Potential Side Effects?

It is typical for patients to experience soreness, bleeding, and possible bruising or swelling during the days following the procedure. If these side effects persist, we recommend contacting our dentist for a follow-up evaluation.

How Much Do Dental Extractions Cost?

The cost of your dental extraction will depend on the complexity of the case. Whether you have insurance or not will also affect your out of pocket cost. We recommend you ask our team about to our affordable tooth extraction options before your procedure to find out what your individual cost will be.

After Your Tooth Extraction

The time it takes to heal will vary based on the type of extraction and where the tooth was removed, but generally you can expect it to heal within 7 to 10 days. Immediately after your tooth extraction procedure, our dentist will provide you with a customized plan for at-home care to allow for optimal healing.

Our dentist will also provide you with customized options for replacing the tooth if necessary. These options can include:

  • Dental implants
  • Fixed Bridges
  • Partial Dentures

When Can You Return to Work?

Most people are back to work the next day. The local anesthetic wears off in a couple hours and we usually recommend no heavy lifting or strenuous activity for 24 hours.

What is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic Dentistry is elective treatments patients chose to undergo to improve the look and aesthetics of their smile. While cosmetic dentistry is not a recognized specialty by the American Dental Association, some dentists, including our own dentist, are more knowledgeable and experienced to provide you with a more naturally-looking smile. These treatments can be as complex as a full smile makeover to as simple as a teeth-whitening. In the end though, the results are a more natural looking smile you are proud to show off.
