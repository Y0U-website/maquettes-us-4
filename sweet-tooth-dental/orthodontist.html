The Benefits of Straight Teeth

Besides the confidence that straight teeth and a beautiful smile bring, there are also other benefits of aligning your teeth.

Straight teeth make it easier to brush and floss effectively. Conversely, crooked or overlapped teeth can be harder to keep clean, increasing your risk of tooth decay or gum disease.

Correctly aligning your bite minimizes problems with your jaw joint (TMJ) and can protect you from a painful joint disorder known as TMD. When your bite is aligned, it also reduces wear and tear on your teeth

SureSmile® Frequently Asked Questions

How does Clear Aligners work?

Clear Aligners uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners that gradually shift your teeth into the desired position. You wear each set for one to two weeks before moving to the next, and at Sweet Tooth Dental we use digital scanning to plan and track your treatment from start to finish.

How long does Clear Aligners treatment take?

Most adult Clear Aligners cases are completed in 12 to 18 months, though simpler cases may finish in as little as six months. Wearing the aligners 20 to 22 hours per day is essential to staying on schedule.

SureSmile® at Sweet Tooth Dental typically falls in the range of comparable traditional braces, with the exact cost depending on the complexity and length of treatment. We offer in-house financing and accept most orthodontic insurance benefits to make treatment more affordable.

Can I eat and drink normally with Clear Aligners?

Yes, one of the biggest advantages of Clear Aligners is that you remove the aligners to eat and drink anything you want. Just remember to brush before putting them back in, and stick to water while the aligners are in your mouth.
