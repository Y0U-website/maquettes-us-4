Gentle care for children

Dr. Hanna takes extra time with young patients, creating a calm, supportive environment that helps children feel comfortable and builds positive dental experiences from an early age.

Restorative dentistry

Tooth-colored fillings

We use composite fillings that match the shade of your natural teeth, allowing us to repair cavities while keeping your smile strong and natural-looking.

Crowns & bridges

Crowns restore strength and function to damaged or heavily restored teeth, while bridges replace missing teeth by anchoring to neighboring teeth for a stable, natural-looking result.

Dentures & partials

We offer full and partial dentures that are custom-designed for comfort, helping restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Root canal treatment

Root canal therapy allows us to save infected or severely damaged teeth, relieve pain, and avoid extraction whenever possible so you can keep your natural teeth longer.

Cosmetic dentistry

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening can safely and effectively brighten your smile, helping reduce stains from coffee, tea, wine, and everyday life.

Veneers

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front of teeth to improve color, shape, and alignment, creating a more uniform and confident smile.

Bonding & minor reshaping

Tooth-colored bonding can repair chips, close small gaps, and smooth uneven edges, often in a single, comfortable visit.

Invisalign & alignment options

Invisalign clear aligners

Invisalign uses a series of nearly invisible aligners to gradually straighten your teeth, allowing you to improve your smile with minimal impact on daily life.

Minor tooth movement

For appropriate cases, we can address minor alignment concerns in our office or coordinate referrals for more complex orthodontic treatment when needed.

Emergency care

Emergency care

When urgent dental issues arise, we’re here to help relieve pain and restore your oral health quickly. We provide emergency exams and treatment, toothache and abscess relief, and repair of broken or knocked-out teeth.
