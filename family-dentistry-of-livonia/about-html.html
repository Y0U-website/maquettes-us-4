About Dr. Wasim Hanna

Dr. Wasim Hanna, DMD

Owner & Lead Dentist Family Dentistry of Livonia

Dr. Wasim Hanna is the owner and lead dentist of Family Dentistry of Livonia, where he provides comprehensive general and family dental care to patients in Livonia, Michigan, and the surrounding communities. He is committed to delivering high-quality dentistry in a professional, welcoming, and family-centered environment.

Education & Training

Dr. Hanna earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry in 2015. He remains dedicated to continuing education, with a strong clinical focus on implant dentistry and advanced restorative care. His goal is to provide reliable, long-term solutions that enhance both oral health and overall quality of life.

Clinical Expertise

Implant Dentistry & Advanced Restorative Care

With specialized training in implant and restorative dentistry, Dr. Hanna designs comprehensive treatment plans that restore function, comfort, and confidence. He focuses on providing reliable, long-term solutions for patients with damaged or missing teeth.

Invisalign® & Clear Aligner Treatment

As an Invisalign® provider, Dr. Hanna offers modern orthodontic solutions designed to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through customized and minimally invasive treatment plans.

Gentle Care for Children & Families

Dr. Hanna is especially known for his calm, gentle approach and his ability to connect with children. He takes extra care to ensure young patients feel comfortable and supported, helping families build positive dental experiences from an early age.

Philosophy of Care

Dr. Hanna believes in treating every patient as a member of his own family. He takes pride in listening carefully, clearly explaining treatment options, and creating a calm, respectful experience for patients of all ages.

His vision for Family Dentistry of Livonia is to elevate patient care through updated technology, modern clinical protocols, and an emphasis on trust, transparency, and patient comfort. While honoring the practice’s longstanding relationships within the community, he has implemented improvements focused on safety, quality, and personalized care.
