Insurance & Payment Options

Dental Insurance

We work with most major dental insurance providers. Our team will verify your benefits before treatment and help you understand your coverage, estimated costs, and any out-of-pocket expenses.

Payment Methods

We accept cash, checks, and all major credit cards. Payment is expected at the time of service unless other arrangements have been made in advance.

Questions about coverage or costs?

Our front desk team is happy to answer questions about insurance, payment plans, and financing options. Please call us at (734) 427‑2241 before your visit if you have specific concerns.

Your comfort is our priority

Dr. Hanna is known for his calm, gentle approach. Whether you’re nervous about dental visits or simply want clear communication and a relaxed environment, we’re here to make your experience as comfortable as possible.

If you have specific concerns, dental anxiety, or special needs, please let us know when you schedule your appointment so we can prepare accordingly.
