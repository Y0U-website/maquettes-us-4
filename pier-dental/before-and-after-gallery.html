Composite Veneer

Composite veneers are a type of dental veneer made chairside from tooth-colored composite resin material. Dr. Pierpont uses them to improve the appearance of teeth by correcting issues such as discoloration, chipping, misalignment, or uneven spacing.

Porcelain Veneer

Porcelain dental veneers are thin shells that are custom-made to fit perfectly over the front surface of a tooth. They are used to improve the appearance of teeth by addressing various cosmetic issues, such as discoloration, staining, chipping, misalignment, or gaps between teeth.

Partial Dentures

Partial dentures are removable dental appliances used to replace one or more missing teeth in a patient’s mouth. Unlike full dentures, which replace all the teeth in an arch, partial dentures are designed to fit around the remaining natural teeth and fill in the gaps where teeth are missing.

Partial dentures offer an effective and affordable solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring oral function and aesthetics. They provide a non-invasive option for patients who are not candidates for dental implants or fixed bridges. Here at Pier Dental, we offer various options for partial dentures from metal to all acrylic / tooth colored partial dentures.

Esthetic Anterior Composite Fillings

Esthetic composite fillings, also known as tooth-colored or white fillings, are dental restorations used to repair cavities or damage to teeth while maintaining a natural appearance. Esthetic composite fillings offer a conservative, natural-looking solution for restoring teeth and preserving smile aesthetics. They provide an excellent alternative to traditional metal fillings for patients who prefer a more discreet and natural-looking restoration.
