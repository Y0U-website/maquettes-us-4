Meet Dr. Brittany Pierpont

Dr. Brittany Pierpont leads our team with a passion for providing exceptional, compassionate dental care to families throughout the St Pete Beach area.

With extensive training in general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Pierpont combines clinical excellence with a gentle, patient-first approach. She stays current with the latest dental technologies and techniques to ensure our patients receive the most advanced care available.

Dr. Pierpont is committed to making dental care accessible and stress-free, especially for patients who may have avoided the dentist due to anxiety, cost concerns, or past negative experiences.

Advanced Technology for Better Care

TRIOS® 3D Scanner

No more messy impressions! Our advanced intraoral scanner creates precise 3D digital models for crowns, bridges, and aligners. Learn more →

Digital X-Rays

Low-radiation digital imaging provides instant, high-quality diagnostic images, reducing exposure by up to 90 % compared to traditional X-rays.

Precise Digital Crowns

We use our TRIOS scanner to capture fast, accurate digital impressions for your custom crown no messy molds. You will wear a temporary while your final crown is crafted, then return for a comfortable, precise fit.

Oral Sedation

For anxious patients, we offer oral sedation a prescribed medication that helps you feel calm and comfortable throughout your visit, so even longer appointments feel relaxed and stress-free.
