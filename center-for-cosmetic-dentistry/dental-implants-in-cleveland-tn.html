The Mini Dental Implant Advantage

Another significant advantage is their cost-effectiveness. The expedited process associated with mini dental implants saves time and makes them more affordable than traditional implants. Despite their compact size, these implants offer strong support for prosthetic teeth, ensuring durability and functionality.

Additionally, mini dental implants are incredibly versatile. They are an ideal solution for patients who may not have sufficient jawbone density to support traditional implants or those who require implants in smaller spaces within the mouth. This versatility broadens the scope of patients who can benefit from dental implants, making it a suitable option for a wider range of tooth replacement needs.

The Process Unveiled

The journey to getting dental implants typically involves the following steps:

Once we complete the process, you should care for your new smile the same way you’d care for natural teeth. While the implants themselves are stain-resistant, brushing, flossing, and attending regular checkups will keep the rest of your teeth radiant and the tissues surrounding your implants healthy.

  • Consultation: Our experienced team of dentists will assess your oral health, take X-rays, and develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs. While the procedure for mini implants is minimally invasive, at your consultation we can discuss whether sedation options are needed to put your mind at ease.
  • Implant placement: We ensure your comfort throughout the procedure by applying a local anesthetic to the treatment area. Then, one of our expert dentists will strategically place the titanium posts in your jawbone. We’ll ensure the implants are secure and stable before attaching a temporary restoration.
  • Healing and osseointegration: Traditional dental implants require a long healing period of 3-6 months as the implant fuses with the jawbone. Mini implants, on the other hand, are immediately sturdy because of their unique construction.
  • Customized restoration: We meticulously craft your artificial teeth to seamlessly integrate with your natural smile. When your final restoration is complete, we will remove the temporary restoration and firmly attach your custom teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Some patients with insufficient jawbone density to support a dental implant must undergo bone grafting or opt for a mini implant. During the bone grafting procedure, natural or artificial material is attached to existing bone, creating a more solid base for the implant. Because of the narrow size of mini implants, bone grafting is generally unnecessary.

Dental implants are most effective after the jawbone has fully developed, at approximately age 18. The procedure requires good oral health and sufficient jawbone density. However, mini implants are particularly suitable for older patients due to their less invasive nature.
