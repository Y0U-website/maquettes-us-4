  • Less invasive procedure: The streamlined design of mini dental implants means their insertion is less invasive. There’s no need for surgical cuts or stitches, leading to a more comfortable experience for the patient and a quicker recovery.
  • Immediate functionality: A key feature of mini dental implants is the rapid improvement in function they provide. Patients can quickly resume normal speaking and eating habits, bypassing the extended waiting periods needed for traditional implants.
  • Cost-effective: The faster placement and recovery associated with mini dental implants also make them more cost-effective than their traditional counterparts. Their affordability makes them accessible to a wider range of patients.
  • Enhanced comfort and confidence: Patients with mini dental implants often report increased comfort and confidence when speaking, eating, and engaging in social activities.

Understanding the Procedure

Placing mini dental implants involves a meticulous process designed for precision and patient comfort.

This comprehensive method ensures a smooth and effective integration of your new teeth, providing a reliable solution for enhancing your smile.

  • Consultation and assessment: At your free consultation, our dentists will thoroughly evaluate your oral health. This includes taking X-rays and assessing if you’re a suitable candidate for the procedure. We will then develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs and preferences.
  • Implant preparation and placement: To ensure a pain-free experience, we administer local anesthesia. Using specialized equipment, our skilled dentists then create a tiny pilot hole in the jawbone. We then gently and precisely insert the implant into the prepared site. Although this process takes mere minutes, our team takes the time to accurately place and align each implant.
  • Restoration placement: After the mini dental implant is securely positioned in the jawbone, we attach a custom-made restoration, like a crown or bridge, to complete the procedure. Depending on the situation, we may place a temporary restoration until your custom prosthetic is fabricated. However, our in-office CEREC machine makes same-day crowns possible.

Aftercare and Recovery

Post-procedure, you can immediately begin using your new teeth. Mild discomfort may occur during the first 24-48 hours, but over-the-counter pain relief can ease any soreness. Full integration of the implants with your jawbone takes a few weeks, but you can use your new teeth during this time. Thanks to their rapid fusion with the jawbone, mini implants allow you to enjoy all your favorite foods again without a prolonged healing period.

With diligent care, including brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups, these implants are designed to last a lifetime.
