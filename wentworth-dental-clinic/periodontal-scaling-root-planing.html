Signs You May Need Scaling & Root Planing

Gum disease can develop gradually, often without noticeable discomfort. Common signs include:

If left untreated, gum disease can progress and may require more advanced care within our restorative dentistry services .

  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Bleeding when brushing or flossing
  • Receding gums or longer-looking teeth
  • Persistent bad breath or unpleasant taste
  • Loose or shifting teeth

What to Expect During Treatment

Your periodontal treatment is carefully planned to ensure comfort and effectiveness:

  • Comprehensive Evaluation We assess your gum health using clinical measurements and imaging such as digital dental X-rays to determine the extent of the condition.
  • Local Anesthesia The treatment area is numbed to help keep you comfortable throughout the procedure.
  • Scaling and Root Planing Specialized instruments are used to remove buildup and smooth the root surfaces below the gumline.
  • Post-Treatment Guidance You’ll receive instructions for at-home care, which may include antimicrobial rinses and adjusted oral hygiene techniques.

Aftercare and Maintenance

Proper care after treatment helps maintain results and prevent recurrence:

Ongoing preventive care through our general dentistry services is essential for maintaining healthy gums.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is scaling and root planing painful?

The procedure is typically performed with local anesthesia to minimize discomfort. Some mild sensitivity may occur afterward.

How long does scaling and root planing take?

Treatment time varies depending on the extent of gum disease and may be completed in one or multiple visits.

How do I know if I need deep cleaning instead of a regular cleaning?

If gum pockets are deeper than normal or signs of infection are present, scaling and root planing may be recommended instead of a routine cleaning.

Will my gums fully heal after treatment?

Many patients see significant improvement with proper care and maintenance, though ongoing monitoring is important to manage gum health.
