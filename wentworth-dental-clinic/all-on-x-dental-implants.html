All-on-X vs. Traditional Dentures

When comparing All-on-X implants to traditional dentures, there are several key differences:

  • Stability : Traditional dentures rest on the gums and can slip out of place, while All-on-X implants are securely anchored to the jawbone.
  • Comfort : All-on-X implants do not cause the discomfort or irritation often associated with ill-fitting dentures.
  • Jawbone Health : Traditional dentures do not prevent bone loss in the jaw, while implants stimulate the bone and help maintain facial structure.
  • Maintenance : All-on-X implants are permanent and cared for like natural teeth, while dentures require removal and special cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants in Chicago

Am I a candidate for All-on-4 implants?

Most adults with significant tooth loss or failing teeth are candidates, though sufficient jawbone density is needed to anchor the implants. Patients who have had bone loss from long-term denture wear sometimes need a bone graft first. The best way to know is a consultation where Dr. Chan reviews your imaging and health history.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

The surgery itself typically takes three to five hours per arch. Most patients receive a temporary set of fixed teeth the same day, with the permanent prosthesis placed after the implants have fully integrated with the bone, usually three to six months later.

Does All-on-4 hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and most patients describe the discomfort in the days following surgery as manageable with the medication provided. The recovery is generally easier than patients expect, particularly compared to having multiple individual extractions.

How do you clean All-on-4 implants?

The prosthesis is cleaned like natural teeth, brushing twice daily and using a water flosser or special interdental brushes to clean under the arch. Dr. Chan will show you exactly how to care for your new teeth before you leave the office.

What does All-on-4 cost in Chicago?

The total cost depends on the number of arches being treated, whether extractions are needed, and whether bone grafting is required. All-on-4 is a significant investment but typically costs less than replacing each tooth individually with single implants. Wentworth Dental Clinic provides a detailed cost breakdown at your consultation.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

The titanium implant posts are designed to last a lifetime with proper care. The prosthetic arch may need adjustment or replacement after 15 to 20 years depending on wear.
