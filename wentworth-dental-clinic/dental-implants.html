Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer several advantages over other tooth replacement options:

Unlike dental bridges or full/partial dentures , implants provide a permanent solution that integrates into your jaw for maximum stability.

  • Look and feel like natural teeth
  • Prevent bone loss in the jaw
  • Restore proper chewing and speech
  • Do not rely on surrounding teeth for support
  • Easy to maintain with regular brushing and flossing
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a candidate for dental implants if you:

For patients with bone loss, we may recommend bone grafting or consider alternatives such as implant-supported dentures .

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Have healthy gums and adequate jawbone structure
  • Are free from conditions that could impair healing
  • Want a fixed solution rather than a removable one

Our Dental Implant Process

We take a precise, patient-centered approach to implant treatment:

Throughout the process, we’ll ensure your comfort and keep you fully informed. We also offer same-day CEREC crowns to expedite certain restoration phases.

  • Consultation & 3D imaging: We begin with a full exam and advanced imaging using 3D printing and digital scanning to evaluate bone density and plan implant placement.
  • Implant placement: The titanium post is surgically placed in your jawbone. Most patients report minimal discomfort.
  • Healing period: Over several months, the implant integrates with your bone.
  • Abutment & restoration: Once healed, we place an abutment and custom crown designed to match your natural teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last 25 years or more — often a lifetime.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Most patients experience only mild discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction. We use local anesthesia and offer options for enhanced comfort.

What is the recovery time?

Initial healing takes about 1–2 weeks. Full integration of the implant may take several months, but most patients return to work the next day.

Do implants require special maintenance?

No. Implants can be brushed and flossed just like natural teeth. Regular checkups and professional cleanings are essential for long-term success.

What if I don’t have enough bone for implants?

In such cases, we may recommend bone grafting or an All-On-X solution , which allows implants to be placed at strategic angles in areas with denser bone.
