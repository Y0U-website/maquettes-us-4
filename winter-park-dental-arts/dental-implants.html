What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible materials such as titanium that are surgically placed into the jawbone to support replacement teeth. They provide a strong foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth that are designed to match your natural teeth in both function and appearance.

The Benefits of Dental Implants:

1. Improved Oral Health: Dental implants preserve natural tooth tissue and stimulate bone growth, preventing bone loss and maintaining the integrity of your jawbone.

2. Enhanced Aesthetics: Implants look and feel like natural teeth, restoring your smile and boosting your confidence.

3. Durability and Longevity: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, providing a long-term solution for missing teeth.

4. Improved Functionality: Unlike traditional dentures, implants allow for comfortable chewing and speaking without the worry of slippage or discomfort.

The Dental Implant Process

At Winter Park Dental Arts, our skilled dental professionals will guide you through the dental implant process with care and expertise. The process typically involves the following steps:

1. Initial Consultation: We will assess your oral health, discuss your treatment goals, and create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

2. Implant Placement: Our experienced dentist will surgically place the implant into your jawbone, allowing it to fuse with the bone over several months.

3. Abutment Placement: Once the implant has integrated with the bone, an abutment is attached to the implant to support the replacement tooth or teeth.

4. Customized Restoration: Our team will create and place a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture onto the implant, completing your new smile.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a suitable option for many individuals, but a thorough evaluation by our dental team is necessary to determine your candidacy. Factors that may affect your eligibility for dental implants include:

- Sufficient Jawbone Density: A healthy jawbone is essential to support the implant, but bone grafting may be an option for those with insufficient bone density.

- Good Oral Health: Gum disease and other oral health issues may need to be addressed before proceeding with dental implants.

- Overall Health: Certain medical conditions or lifestyle habits such as smoking can impact the success of dental implant treatment.
