The Georgetown North Dental Group Smile Plan is a discount plan we offer to our patients to help with cost when there is no dental insurance.

We understand these financial times and we would like to help by offering special discounts to you and your family.

You will be offered this plan when you come in for an appointment or feel free to ask our receptionist if you have questions.

The Georgetown North Dental Group Smile Plan will provide an affordable alternative for individual and family dental care

There is an annual enrollment fee of $449 per adult family member (age 18+). There is an annual enrollment fee of $349 per child family member (under age 18).
