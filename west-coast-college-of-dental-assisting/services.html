OUR SERVICES

Great Jobs for Graduates

Graduates from West Coast College of Dental Assisting (known as WCCODA) are highly sought after by dentists, thanks to the advanced learning and one-on-one training you’ll receive at our college. We not only prepare you with the book learning and dental technology you’ll need, we highly teach you hands on through personal, detailed training in a dental office environment.

Flexible Class Schedules

At West Coast College of Dental Assisting, most students are able to keep up with their social life and their current job while they are in college. Class size is small. Only 10 students to each class. We offer a condensed class schedule to maximize your learning in the shortest amount of time. You will be coming to class 2 times a week. After your 18 weeks of training, you will be ready for your new career as a dental assistant, one of the fastest growing careers in the country.

Day or Evening Classes

You can select from either day classes or evening: twice a week on Tuesday/Wednesday, offered morning and evenings or once a week offered on Friday mornings.

Balance Work, School, and Fun

At West Coast College of Dental Assisting, we care about your education, so we provide one-on-one instruction to all students so that you will be prepared to succeed in your new career. The course is streamlined and detailed with a focus on hands-on training. That means less time in class for you, and more concentrated training methods so that you can learn while you keep up with your current job and your social life.

Latest in Technology

We are a green company. X-rays are processed digitally without the use of any dangerous chemicals. We are on the leading edge of the movement toward fully digital dentists’ offices.
