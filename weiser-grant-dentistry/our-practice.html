ABOUT DR. MARK T. WEISER

Fellow - American Academy of Ozone Therapy

Published author and frequent expert speaker

Vice President of the IAOH, the International Association of Ozone in Healthcare

Co-Author - ADVANCES OF OZONE THERAPY IN MEDICINE & DENTISTRY, 2016 . Premier reference book on Dental and Medical Ozone therapies.

After three decades in dentistry, Dr. Mark T. Weiser is an experienced, caring and forward-looking professional. An author, lecturer, and recognized leader in the field of cosmetic and laser and ozone dentistry, Dr. Weiser is a pioneer of innovative techniques in the uses of lasers, ozone and air abrasion for “drill-free” dentistry. His approach of healthy mouth/healthy body concept, sees the tie in between the health of your mouth and your overall body health.

Dr. Weiser offers a wide range of dental services, including: cosmetic, laser, biologically safe, snoring and sleep apnea therapies as well as, general preventive care.

An international expert in aesthetic and reconstructive dentistry, Dr. Mark T. Weiser graduated from the University of the Pacific. As a partner in the “Extreme Team” that provided cosmetic dentistry for the hit TV show ABC Extreme Makeover – Dr. Weiser has learned and perfected advanced techniques to improve anyone’s smile. Dr. Weiser has also completed filming for BBC’s "A Brand New You" where they changed the lives of English patients with amazing smile makeovers.

With state of the art technology and a highly trained staff, Dr. Weiser and his team are committed to giving you the best quality service for all your smile and dental needs.

Dr. Mark T. Weiser is part of the International Association of Mercury Safe Dentists Members and uses biologically compatible dental materials in all restorations.

ABOUT DR. LANCE L. GRANT

University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine

Dr. Grant is a Santa Ynez Valley Native with a deep-seated passion for creating beautiful smiles that dramatically impact the patient’s life. He is a highly skilled dentist that has been perfecting his craft and serving the Santa Barbara community for the last ten years. Dr. Grant has a gentle and conservative approach to treating his patients’ teeth that has earned him loyalty and trust across the country.

Dr. Grant brings a wide array of dental services to the practice. In addition to providing general and cosmetic dentistry, he is an expert of implant dentistry and oral surgery. Dr. Grant focuses on providing and replacing teeth using the latest in dental technology. Furthermore, Dr. Grant is certified in IV sedation dentistry enabling all his patients the highest level of care in a stress-free environment.

Dr. Grant began his education by studying Medical Biology at the University of Utah where he graduated Summa Cum Laude. He then went on to the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine where he received his Doctorate in Medical Dentistry. There, he served as the President of the American Student Dental Association of his school. As a leader in organized dentistry, he met with State and National Officials in Washington DC on an annual basis to discuss the latest dental issues.

Upon graduation, Dr. Grant came back to Santa Barbara and joined a group dental practice. During the past decade, he studied implant dentistry, oral surgery, Invisalign and endodontics. Becoming well-known for his implant successes, Dr. Grant has helped countless patients improve the functional and appearance of their teeth.

When not working on patients, Dr. Grant loves to spend time with his four children, cooking, and enjoying the Santa Barbara life.
