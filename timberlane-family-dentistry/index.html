The practice

Timberlane Family Dentistry is a privately-owned dental practice providing leading-edge technology and treatment services customized to your individual needs. Our team of dental professionals is dedicated to staying current with the newest techniques and tools in a warm and personable environment.

Our dental office is pleasant and relaxing, where patients receive the personal attention and quality care they deserve. Because we are a private, doctor-owned office, we can tailor your treatment to fit your particular needs and desires, and we will ensure you know how to care for your smile between dental visits.

Dr. Thomas Salinas and staff strive to develop a close relationship with our patients to emphasize how you can achieve good oral health through routine maintenance and early detection.

We offer the highest quality and most current dental technologies available today. We use digital imaging and x-ray technology, including cone-beam CT scans and intra-oral scanning, to produce 3-D images of your teeth, gums, nerves, and bone structures in and around your mouth. This detailed picture of your teeth supports hyper-accurate placement of dental restorations like crowns and implants.

To keep up with these advances, Dr. Salinas and staff constantly enroll in continuing education and training. As a result, Dr. Salinas has been granted the title of "Fellow of Academy of General Dentistry". Dr. Salinas also receives two to three times more continuing education hours than Florida requires and is an officer in the prestigious Woehler Research Group.

Your first exam will include a complete assessment of your oral health, including digital x-rays and a cancer screening. Next, Dr. Salinas will discuss your smile goals and treatment needs with you, answering all questions to understand your options.
