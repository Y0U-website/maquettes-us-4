What To Expect From Airway Orthodontics

If the evaluation reveals airway issues, we will discuss different treatment options to open up your airway and improve breathing difficulties. Common treatment measures include expanding the airway, correcting jaw misalignment, and using orthodontic appliances.

We customize each treatment to your unique needs. It works to widen your airway and address any blockages that may cause breathing pauses or sleep disturbance.
