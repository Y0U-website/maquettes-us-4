Who Needs Surgical Orthodontics

Our orthodontist usually recommends surgical orthodontics for teens and adults at our Tarzana dental practice whose jaws have stopped growing.

You might need jaw surgery if you experience:

  • Chronic jaw pain or headaches
  • Difficulty chewing, biting, or speaking
  • Breathing problems (like sleep apnea)
  • Protruding or recessed jaw
  • Severe overbite, underbite, or crossbite

Surgical Orthodontics Treatment Process at Our Tarzana Practice

Here’s what you can expect throughout your surgical orthodontics treatment at our Tarzana dental practice:

  • Pre-Surgical Braces (6-18 months) Your teeth are aligned with braces or clear aligners so that they fit correctly after surgery.
  • Jaw Surgery Our board-certified orthodontist carefully repositions your jaw under anesthesia.
  • Post-Surgical Recovery (two to six weeks) Swelling and soreness are normal but improve quickly
  • A soft-food diet is required for a few weeks
  • Follow-up visits allow proper healing
  • Final Orthodontic Adjustments (6-12 months) Braces fine-tune your bite for optimal results before removal.

Benefits of Surgical Orthodontics

The benefits of surgical orthodontics include:

  • Eating, speaking, and breathing more comfortably
  • Correcting asymmetry for a more harmonious profile
  • Reducing risks of tooth wear, gum disease, and TMJ pain

Is Surgical Orthodontics Safe

Yes, surgical orthodontics is generally very safe, especially when performed by experienced professionals using advanced techniques.

At our dental practice in Tarzana, surgical orthodontics is performed by Dr. Mona Derentz , a Board-Certified Orthodontist. This distinction means she has completed 2–3 years of advanced residency training in orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics, followed by rigorous board examinations. Only about 30 % of orthodontists in the U.S. achieve this level of certification, making her expertise especially valuable when it comes to complex surgical cases.

Dr. Derentz also uses the latest technology to plan and monitor each step of the treatment process. Every patient undergoes a thorough evaluation before surgery is ever recommended, so you can feel confident knowing that surgical orthodontics is only suggested when absolutely necessary and beneficial for your health and smile.
