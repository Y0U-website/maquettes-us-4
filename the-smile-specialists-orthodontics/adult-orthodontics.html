Treatments We Offer For Adult Orthodontics

At our practice, we offer comprehensive orthodontic solutions for adults that not only focus on cosmetic issues but also improve your overall oral health. During your consultation, our orthodontist will assess your oral cavity to determine your needs. Our treatment options include:

Braces

Traditional braces are one of the most effective orthodontic treatments. Using metal or ceramic brackets bonded to the teeth, we can address various concerns, from minor to complex alignment and bite issues.

Invisalign®

For those seeking a more discreet alternative to braces, Invisalign® aligners are a popular choice. These custom-made, transparent aligners gradually shift your teeth into place. This way, you don’t have to worry about hiding your beautiful smile!

Hidden Braces

Hidden braces are placed on the inner (lingual) surfaces of your teeth, making them virtually invisible. They work on the same principles as traditional braces while being more discreet. This is a perfect choice for adults who don’t want noticeable attachments on their teeth.

Retainers

Once your orthodontic treatment is complete, we provide fixed or removable retainers to preserve your new smile and prevent your teeth from shifting out of alignment. They are custom-designed to fit securely over your teeth.
