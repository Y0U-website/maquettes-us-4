Enjoy Straight Teeth Soon With Our Expert Treatment

At The DentaLink, we offer you two great orthodontic solutions for getting straight teeth. Dr. Link and our team will help you decide which works best for you.

For more than two decades, patients have loved orthodontic treatment with Invisalign. Its system of clear plastic teeth aligners has revolutionized the way people think about straightening their teeth.

The smooth aligners fit comfortably over your teeth to gently move them into their correct positions. All you have to do is wear the aligners for at least 22 hours a day (you can remove them for eating, brushing your teeth, or a special occasion). In as few as 12 months, you could have your beautiful, straight smile. And since the aligners are transparent, they offer you discreet treatment without a mouthful of metal!
