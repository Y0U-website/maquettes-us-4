Teeth Whitening Specialist

For patients who prefer the convenience of whitening their teeth at home, Dentist on Madison offers ZOOM! take-home kits. These kits include custom-fitted trays, whitening gel, and detailed instructions to help you achieve professional-grade results in the comfort of your own home. The at-home process typically takes two weeks, with noticeable results appearing within just a few days.

  • Preparation: Before the treatment, we’ll clean your teeth and place protective barriers on your gums and lips to prevent irritation from the whitening gel.
  • Application of Whitening Gel: We’ll apply the ZOOM! whitening gel to your teeth, which contains hydrogen peroxide as its active ingredient.
  • Activation: The ZOOM! LED light is used to activate the whitening gel, breaking down stains and discoloration on the tooth surface.
  • Rinsing and Repeating: The gel is removed, and the process is repeated up to three times for optimal results.
  • Post-Whitening Care: Once the desired shade is achieved, we’ll apply a post-treatment gel to help minimize sensitivity and protect your newly whitened teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do ZOOM! teeth whitening results last?

Results vary depending on individual factors such as your oral hygiene habits, diet, and lifestyle. On average, ZOOM! whitening results can last up to two years. To maintain your bright smile, we recommend regular dental cleanings, using whitening toothpaste, and limiting the consumption of staining foods and beverages.

Is ZOOM! teeth whitening safe?

Yes, ZOOM! teeth whitening is a safe and effective procedure when performed by a qualified dental professional. The in-office procedure is carefully monitored to minimize any potential risks, while the at-home kits are designed with your safety and comfort in mind.

Will teeth whitening cause sensitivity?

Some patients may experience temporary tooth sensitivity following a ZOOM! whitening treatment. This is generally mild and short-lived, often subsiding within 24-48 hours. Our team will provide you with post-treatment care instructions and recommend products to help minimize any discomfort.

Are there any side effects of ZOOM! teeth whitening?

Side effects from ZOOM! teeth whitening are generally minimal and short-lived. Some patients may experience temporary tooth sensitivity or gum irritation. These side effects usually subside within a few days after the treatment. Our dental professionals will take every precaution to minimize any discomfort during the procedure and provide you with guidance on post-treatment care.

Is ZOOM! teeth whitening suitable for everyone?

While ZOOM! teeth whitening is an effective solution for many patients, it may not be suitable for everyone. Pregnant or nursing women, children under 13, and individuals with gum disease, dental restorations, or extreme tooth sensitivity may not be ideal candidates for the procedure. During your initial consultation, our team will evaluate your oral health and discuss the best options for achieving a brighter smile.
