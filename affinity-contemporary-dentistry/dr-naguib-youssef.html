Meet Dr. Alana East

Dedicated | Compassionate | Adventurous

Dr. Alana East is a Washington native who was born and raised in Spokane. She attended undergraduate college at the University of Puget Sound, where she majored in Natural Science- Biology and spent four years running on both collegiate cross-country and track teams.

She attended the University of Washington School of Dentistry, where she first developed a deep appreciation for the art and science that intertwine in dental care. As a lifelong learner, Dr. East is committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered treatments. Beyond the practice, she is passionate about volunteerism and donating dentistry and time, believing that everyone deserves access to quality care.

In her spare time, Dr. East enjoys spending time on adventures with her husband and two kids, trail running, snow sports, and creating art.

Dr. East looks forward to joining Affinity Contemporary Dentistry and helping each patient achieve their best smile and optimum health.
