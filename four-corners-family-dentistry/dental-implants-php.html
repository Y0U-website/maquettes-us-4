What Are Dental Implants?

As the gold standard of tooth replacement, dental implants offer more than visible smile improvement; they also provide enhanced oral health. As the only prosthetic with a complete root-to-crown structure, they not only stimulate the jawbone to reduce the risk of future bone and tooth loss, but also keep you smiling confidently because of the lifelike teeth that look, feel, and act like the real thing.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Your dental implant process will look different than another patient’s, but you can expect to follow the same four steps to achieve lasting results:

  • Initial Consultation: You will meet with a member of our team to go over your smile goals and what you hope to accomplish with dental implants. We’ll evaluate your oral health, discuss your medical history, and determine your jawbone density.
  • Dental Implant Surgery: Once you are cleared for surgery, we’ll refer you to a local dental implant specialist who will complete the surgical placement of your implants.
  • Osseointegration/Abutments: After your procedure, you will return home to recover. The next 3-6 months will involve the fusing of your implants and jawbone (osseointegration). Once you are fully healed, you will have metal abutments put on top of your implants to create a secure attachment once your restoration is put in place.
  • Retrieval of the Final Restoration: Whether it’s a crown, bridge, or denture that you need, our team at Four Corners Family Dentistry will deliver a beautiful restoration. It will be affixed to your implant(s) to complete the process.
