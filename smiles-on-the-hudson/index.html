The practice

At Smiles on the Hudson, every patient receives personalized care tailored to their unique needs, goals, and comfort level. Our team is dedicated to creating a welcoming experience that prioritizes comfort, patient education, and exceptional treatment outcomes.

We believe that confident smiles begin with compassionate care, which is why we focus on supporting your overall well-being while helping you achieve lasting oral health and renewed confidence.

Our office is beautifully located on the banks of the Hudson River, overlooking New York City. We are proud to serve up breathtaking dentistry to all those in Edgewater, NJ and the surrounding areas.

Our team of experts are rich in skills from all corners of dentistry – whatever you need; we have you covered.
