Our Doctors

Dr. Fehresti devoted over 30 years of his life to caring for patients and building lasting relationships within the community. After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1994, he pursued excellence in all areas of dentistry, including cosmetic, restorative, oral surgery, orthodontics, and Invisalign, where he achieved Elite Preferred Provider status.

He held IV sedation certification from the University of Minnesota and served as a hospital-privileged dentist. Dr. Fehresti was also an Associate Fellow and Board Member of the Academy of GP Orthodontics, reflecting his deep passion for orthodontic care.

More than his accomplishments, Dr. Fehresti will be remembered for his kindness, dedication, and the way he made patients feel comfortable and cared for. He believed dentistry was both a science and an art, and he took great pride in helping people smile with confidence.

He loved spending time with his wife and two daughters, as well as fishing and working on his cars and motorcycles.

His impact on our office and our patients will never be forgotten, and we are proud to continue the care he was so passionate about.

Dr. Tomaides’s warm and infectious smile will put any patient at ease. He embraces a gentle philosophy with dentistry and provides the utmost care for all his patients. In his spare time, enjoys vacationing with his family and just spending his days with his son.

Born and raised in Minnesota, Dr. Styrbicki completed his dental school at Midwestern University in Downers Grove, Illinois. After dental school, Dr. Styrbicki went on to complete a heavily surgical-based general practice residency at the University of Iowa Hospitals and Clinics before returning to the Twin Cities to be close to family and friends. Dr. Styrbicki enjoys all aspects of dentistry, with a particular passion for oral surgery and implant dentistry.

In his spare time Dr. Styrbicki loves spending time with his wife, two daughters and their Bernese Mountain dog. He enjoys outdoor activities such as fishing, hunting, golfing and scuba diving. Dr Styrbicki loves to cook for his family and friends and is always up for trying new restaurants and food. Dr. Styrbicki is compassionate and prides himself on putting his patients’ best interests first. We are excited for you to meet the newest member of our team.

Dr. Traore is passionate about providing compassionate, high-quality dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. She began her career as a dental hygienist before earning her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry. She is committed to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through gentle, personalized care.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Traore enjoys spending time with her family. She is passionate about health and fitness, enjoys trying new restaurants and cuisines, and values continuous learning both professionally and personally. She looks forward to building lasting relationships with her patients and being a trusted part of their dental care.
