The practice

Orthodontic treatment results in straighter and healthier teeth for the future. Give your child the best possible chance of having a healthy mouth to support the overall health of their body and mind. Arrange a special orthodontic consultation with Dr. Bob.

Along with typical braces comes food restrictions like no gum, popcorn, or apples. With Invisalign®, you will never worry again about what foods you can eat. The answer is all of them!

Because Invisalign® is easily removable, your bedtime routine can continue with a simple addition of cleaning your aligners. No more struggling to floss and brush metal brackets and wires.

With up to 50 % faster treatment times, and less frequent office visits, your life can continue to move at the pace that it always has. Fewer visits correcting your smile means more time out there perfecting it.

Services

It’s never too late to achieve a beautiful straight smile. Whether you had braces in your teens and the teeth have shifted back, or you never had a chance to fix them, we offer options to suit your needs. Lean more about achieving the smile you want.
