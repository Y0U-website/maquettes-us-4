Answers to Common Questions about Dental Implants

Choosing a dental implant is a commitment and we want you to feel completely informed. Here are some of the questions we often receive from dental implant patients.

Ready for Your Dental Implant Consultation?

Are you considering getting a dental implant to replace a tooth? Simply fill out the form and we’ll connect to schedule a convenient time for a consultation.

  • How long does the process take? Placing the implant post usually takes about 40 minutes. Healing time takes 4 full months.
  • Will getting a dental implant hurt? We take every precaution to ensure you’re as comfortable as possible. The entire procedure is done with the use of anesthesia. Most patients agree that having a tooth removed is more uncomfortable than the implant procedure.
  • Will getting a dental implant be expensive? Total cost will depend on your insurance, but dental implants are a financial investment. While the procedure and implants themselves tend to be costlier than other services, we’re replacing your tooth with a permanent solution.
  • How long do dental implants last? If cared for properly a dental implant will last forever. Excellent gum health and regular dental visits are critical to the long-term success of dental implants.
