Implant Dentures Morgan Hill CA

Although the implant dentures procedure takes longer than traditional dentures, it provides results that are truly worthwhile!

  • Implant surgery. We will carefully place your implants in your mouth at their pre-planned locations.
  • Over a period of a few months, your implants will bond with the surrounding tissue. Then, you may receive abutments (small connector pieces) to go on top of your implants.
  • You will receive your beautiful new set of teeth!

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You may be a good candidate for implant dentures if:

If you are not sure if you qualify for implant dentures, visit us for a consultation. Most people can undergo this treatment, either right away or after some preparatory procedures.

  • You are missing all or most of your teeth.
  • Your jawbone is healthy enough to support dental implants.
  • You are free of active gum disease.
  • You are in fair overall health.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Some of the most outstanding benefits of implant dentures include:

  • You can enjoy a strong bite force that allows you to eat virtually any food, including items that are off-limits with traditional dentures.
  • Implant dentures tend to last much longer than their traditional counterparts.
  • We carefully design implant dentures so they look and feel as natural as possible.
  • Jawbone preservation. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent it from losing mass and density over time.
  • Convenient maintenance. In many ways, caring for implant dentures is similar to caring for natural teeth.
