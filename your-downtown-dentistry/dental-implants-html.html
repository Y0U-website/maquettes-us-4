What Are Dental Implants?

As the only tooth loss solution capable of restoring a tooth’s root and crown, dental implants are unlike dental bridges and dentures. Their surgical placement within the jawbone allows for greater stability over time so that you are free to eat the foods you love. Not to mention, the titanium that is used is not only safe, but capable of fusing with the surrounding jawbone. As a result, these prosthetics offer the support needed to hold up dental crowns, dental bridges, and implant dentures when put into place.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Dental Implant Consultation Your dentist will meet with you during a scheduled consultation to go over your current medical and oral health history. They’ll also look at your jawbone density and determine if your mouth needs additional treatment before receiving dental implants.
  • Dental Implant Surgery Instead of referring you to another dentist’s office for surgical placement, we can perform the surgery in-house so that you remain in one convenient location for the entire process.
  • Dental Implant Osseointegration/Abutment After returning home to spend the next 4-6 months recovering and allowing the implants to fuse with the bone (osseointegration), you will come back to receive your abutments that help to hold your restoration in place.
  • Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s) Once your new teeth are ready, you can expect that your final restoration will fit beautifully. Connected to your implants, you will see how seamless it looks, allowing you to say goodbye to the gaps in your smile.
