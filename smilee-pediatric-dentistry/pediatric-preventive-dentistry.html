It’s an essential part of pediatric dental care that supports not only oral health but also a child’s overall wellness. The Benefits of Preventive Dentistry

Healthier Smiles from the Start

Preventive dentistry helps protect young teeth from decay and damage, setting the foundation for strong, healthy adult teeth. Regular check-ups, cleanings, and preventive treatments make a significant difference in reducing cavities and promoting long-term dental health.

Builds Confidence and Positive Dental Habits

Routine preventive visits make dental care a positive experience for children. By creating a comfortable and engaging environment, we help children feel at ease, encouraging them to take pride in caring for their own smiles and building confidence in the dental chair.

Saves Time and Money in the Long Run

Early preventive care can reduce the risk of complex dental issues down the line, saving your family from potential discomfort and more costly treatments. Regular visits allow us to address small issues before they become larger, helping to protect both your child’s smile and your peace of mind.

Our Preventive Services At Smilee Pediatric Dentistry, we offer a comprehensive range of preventive services to protect and strengthen your child’s smile. From thorough exams to fluoride treatments, each service is designed to provide the best in pediatric dental care.

Exams Exams Our exams are tailored to your child’s age and needs. We offer infant exams to monitor early development and provide guidance to parents on proper care. For older children, routine exams allow us to check for signs of decay, growth abnormalities, and other issues, ensuring that their dental development is on track.

Digital X-Rays Digital X-Rays We use low-dose digital x-rays to get a clear view of your child’s teeth and jaw development while minimizing radiation exposure. Digital x-rays help us catch hidden issues that aren’t visible to the naked eye, allowing for early intervention and precise care.

Cleanings & Hygiene Cleanings & Hygiene Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that can lead to cavities and gum disease. Our hygienists use gentle techniques designed for children, and we offer tips for brushing and flossing at home to help kids keep their smiles clean and bright.

BPA-Free Dental Sealants BPA-Free Dental Sealants Our BPA-free sealants provide a safe, protective coating on the grooves of your child’s molars, preventing food particles and bacteria from getting trapped. Sealants are especially effective in preventing cavities in those hard-to-reach areas and can last for several years with proper care.

Interceptive Orthodontics Interceptive Orthodontics Interceptive orthodontics involves monitoring and guiding the growth of your child’s teeth and jaw. By catching potential alignment issues early, we can provide timely interventions that may reduce the need for extensive orthodontic treatment later on.

FAQS

Have questions about pediatric preventive dentistry?

At what age should my child start preventive dental visits?

Following AAPD guidelines, we recommend bringing your child for their first dental visit by their first birthday, or when their first tooth erupts, whichever comes first. Early visits help children get comfortable in the dental chair and allow us to monitor their development from the start.

How often should my child have a dental check-up?

For most children, a check-up every six months is ideal. However, depending on your child’s unique oral health needs, we may recommend a customized schedule to ensure their smile stays healthy.

Are dental sealants safe for children?

Yes! Our sealants are BPA-free and provide a safe, effective way to protect your child’s molars from cavities. Sealants create a barrier on the tooth surface, making it easier for young patients to keep their smiles healthy.
