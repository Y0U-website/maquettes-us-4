Meet Dr. Sol Han

Dr. Sol Han is a board-certified pediatric dentist who combines her passion for dentistry with a genuine love for working with children. She completed her dental degree from Boston University’s Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, where she graduated at the top of her class, earning a magna cum laude distinction. She continued her education with specialized pediatric training at the prestigious NYU College of Dentistry. As a diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Han brings extensive expertise, compassion, and dedication to every aspect of her care.

Dr. Han’s approach to pediatric dentistry is rooted in building trust and fostering a positive, enjoyable experience for children. She believes that dental visits should be both educational and fun, empowering kids to take pride in caring for their smiles. Her commitment to minimally invasive techniques and the use of high-quality, non-toxic materials reflects her dedication to providing the safest, most comfortable care for young patients.

In her practice, Dr. Han focuses on prevention and holistic care, integrating lifestyle education to help families make informed decisions about their child’s oral health. She goes beyond treating immediate concerns, helping children and their families develop lifelong habits for healthy smiles and overall wellness.

Outside the clinic, Dr. Han enjoys spending time with her family and friends, traveling with her husband, and learning something new every chance she gets. She’s an avid reader and an eternal student at heart, constantly seeking ways to grow both personally and professionally. Dr. Han feels incredibly fortunate to have found her calling in pediatric dentistry, where she can make a meaningful impact on children’s lives while doing what she loves every day.

Advanced Technology & carefully curated Materials

Your child’s safety and health are our top priorities. At Smilee, we’ve carefully selected advanced, bio-friendly materials and technologies to provide the best care. From BPA-free sealants to bioactive fillings that support natural tooth strength, every material we use is chosen with your child’s well-being in mind.

Activa BioACTIVE Restorative Materials Activa BioACTIVE Restorative Materials Our BioACTIVE materials actively support your child’s natural tooth structure by releasing beneficial minerals that help reinforce and protect their teeth. These fillings are designed to work with the body, aiding in natural remineralization for stronger, healthier smiles.

BPA-Free Sealants BPA-Free Sealants Our sealants are BPA-free, ensuring a safe protective layer on your child’s teeth. They create a barrier against cavities, especially in those hard-to-reach areas, while offering peace of mind.

Low-Dose Digital X-rays Low-Dose Digital X-rays We use low-dose digital x-rays, which minimizes radiation exposure and makes the experience much more comfortable for kids, while providing detailed images for accurate diagnosis. This technology allows us to keep a close eye on your child’s dental health, spotting potential issues early on in a safe, efficient manner.
