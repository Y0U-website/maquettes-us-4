What is Restorative Dentistry? Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and restoring teeth affected by decay, trauma, or developmental issues. For children, restorative treatments are essential not only for repairing damaged teeth but also for preserving oral health and ensuring proper dental development. At Smilee Pediatric Dentistry, we use safe, tooth-colored materials and gentle techniques to keep your child’s smile strong and healthy.

The Benefits of Restorative Dentistry

Preserves Natural Tooth Structure

Restorative treatments like fillings and crowns help to maintain the integrity of your child’s natural teeth. By addressing decay or damage early, we can prevent further deterioration and avoid the need for more invasive treatments later on.

Supports Overall Health and Development

Healthy teeth play a crucial role in your child’s overall health. Restoring damaged teeth can improve chewing and speech, promote proper jaw growth, and prevent potential infections that could affect your child’s well-being.

Boosts Confidence and Comfort

When dental issues are addressed promptly and effectively, children can feel more comfortable and confident in their smiles. Our tooth-colored, minimally invasive solutions help children maintain a natural-looking smile that they’re proud to show off.

Our Restorative Services At Smilee Pediatric Dentistry, we offer a variety of restorative treatments designed to protect, repair, and restore your child’s teeth. From minimally invasive fillings to emergency care, we use only the highest-quality, non-toxic materials and conservative techniques to maintain your child&#x27;s oral health.

Zirconia Crowns Zirconia Crowns For parents looking for a more natural, tooth-colored option, zirconia crowns offer strength and aesthetics. These biocompatible, metal-free crowns blend beautifully with your child’s smile while providing long-lasting protection. They’re a great choice for families prioritizing both wellness and appearance.

Dental Fillings Dental Fillings Our tooth-colored dental fillings are designed to blend seamlessly with your child’s natural teeth, restoring their appearance and function. We use bioactive materials that promote natural tooth remineralization and protect against further decay, ensuring a durable and aesthetic solution.

Stainless Steel Crowns (SSC) Stainless Steel Crowns (SSC) Tried and true, stainless steel crowns are a durable option often used for restoring baby molars after extensive decay. They’re strong, cost-effective, and ideal for back teeth that need extra protection until they naturally fall out. We use them when needed to ensure your child’s bite stays healthy and strong.

Resin Crowns Resin Crowns For front teeth that need extra protection, we offer resin crowns as a natural-looking, durable solution. These tooth-colored crowns are specifically designed for visible teeth, blending seamlessly with your child’s smile while restoring the tooth’s strength and function. Resin crowns are ideal for young patients who need a discreet, aesthetic option to protect their front teeth after decay or trauma.

Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) SDF is a gentle, non-invasive treatment that helps stop cavities from progressing, particularly in young children who may not tolerate traditional restorations. By applying a fluoride-based solution to the affected area, we can prevent decay from spreading without the need for drilling.

Pulpotomies Pulpotomies When decay affects the pulp (the nerve center of the tooth), a pulpotomy can save the tooth. This procedure, often called a “baby root canal,” removes the infected pulp tissue while preserving the rest of the tooth structure. It’s a safe, effective solution that relieves pain and prevents infection from spreading.

Extractions Extractions When a tooth is too damaged to save, or if it’s causing crowding issues, a gentle extraction may be necessary. Our team ensures that extractions are as comfortable as possible, offering sedation options to help keep your child relaxed during the procedure.

Dental Emergencies Dental Emergencies Dental accidents can happen at any time, and we’re here to provide urgent, compassionate care when you need it most. From broken teeth to severe toothaches, we offer same-day emergency appointments to relieve pain and protect your child’s smile.

FAQS

Have questions about pediatric restorative dentistry?

Do you offer whitening services?

Yes, but only for eligible candidates. If you’re interested in whitening for your child or teen, we’d love to talk with you about whether it’s the right fit. Just give us a call or ask us at your next appointment!

How will my child be able to sit through a dental procedure?

You’re not alone, this is one of the most common questions we get! Every child is different, and we tailor our approach based on their age, personality, and needs. We use gentle techniques, positive reinforcement, and a calm, kid-friendly environment to help them feel at ease. For children who need extra support, we also offer safe sedation options.

Are tooth-colored fillings safe for children?

Yes, our tooth-colored fillings are made from BPA-free, bioactive materials that are both safe and durable. They blend naturally with your child’s teeth and provide long-lasting protection against decay.

How do I know if my child needs a crown?

If a tooth is significantly damaged or has undergone a pulpotomy, a crown may be recommended to restore its function and prevent further issues. Our team will evaluate your child’s specific needs and discuss the best options with you.

What should I do if my child has a dental emergency?

If your child experiences a dental emergency, such as a broken tooth or severe toothache, contact us right away. We offer same-day appointments for urgent cases and are here to provide immediate, compassionate care.

Do you offer white zirconia crowns?

Yes! We offer both traditional stainless steel crowns and tooth-colored white zirconia crowns. Depending on your child&#x27;s oral health needs and other factors, like the location of the tooth, we will discuss the best option for getting their smile back into tip-top shape.
