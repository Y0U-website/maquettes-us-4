Dr. Michael Hansen

Dr. Michael Hansen, a graduate of Ohio State University, is committed to excellence and providing you with outstanding patient care. He wants to ensure that every patient has a great dental experience when they visit Santa Fe Dental. Dr. Hansen is the founder of the Oklahoma Center for Craniofacial Development.

Read More Dr. Michael Hansen, a graduate of Ohio State University, is committed to excellence and providing you with outstanding patient care. He wants to ensure that every patient has a great dental experience when they visit Santa Fe Dental. That’s why he consistently goes the extra mile in the care he provides and the training he receives. He shares a common goal with each patient ― to ensure you have great oral and systemic health provided through exceptional dental care.

Continuing education is the cornerstone to advanced and comprehensive patient service. Dr. Hansen seeks out and attends the best seminars and continuing education courses year after year to ensure he stays at the forefront of the dental industry. This allows Dr. Hansen to provide his patients with the best and most up–to–date dental care possible. He and his team offer a unique and positive dental experience where your health, comfort, function, and beauty are the top priority.

Dr. Hansen’s clinical emphasis is in the area of craniofacial orthopedic development, which lead him to found the Oklahoma Center for Craniofacial Development . He works closely with a multidisciplinary team that treats craniofacial underdevelopment of the jaws and the oral muscle dysfunctions that are associated with it. Through this treatment modality, he nonsurgically helps develop and balance the jaws and face to improve Sleep Disordered Breathing problems, TMJ disorders, and other craniofacial anomalies using oral orthopedic appliance therapy, and myofunctional therapy.

Clinical Advocate and Integrated Provider for Vivos Therapeutics

Graduate of a two-year certification program offered by the Clinical Foundation of Orthopedic, & TMD Therapy

Certified Invisalign® Provider, including completion of advanced courses

Dental Organization for Conscious Sedation (D.O.C.S.) Member,

Member of the American Academy of Physiological Medicine & Dentistry

Member of the American Dental Association

Member of the Oklahoma Dental Association

Dr. Hansen participates annually in the Oklahoma Mission of Mercy , and has since its inception. He considers it a great honor to serve the people of Oklahoma each year in this manner.

Dr. Hansen also participated in medical and dental missions in the Dominican Republic and Honduras.

Dr. Hansen, born in Scottsdale, Arizona, spent much of his childhood in various mining towns in the mountains of Colorado, California, and Utah . He enjoys the outdoors and is active in his church and community. He spent two years in Paraguay as a missionary and loves South American culture.

Dr. Hansen’s wife, Heather, was raised in Edmond, where she graduated from Deer Creek High School. Dr. Hansen and Heather met while attending Utah State University where he earned his Bachelor’s Degree in Biology. They have four beautiful children – Jacob, Lily, Leif, and Luke.

Dr. Nguyen

Dr. Nguyen was born and raised in Oklahoma City, OK. He is excited to have the opportunity to serve the community. Dr. Nguyen graduated Carl Albert High School and went on to attend the University of Oklahoma where he received his bachelor’s of science in Microbiology.

Read More Dr. Nguyen completed his training in dentistry at the Oklahoma College of Dentistry and has since received further training to provide the full spectrum of dental treatment which includes crowns, bridges, fillings, root canals, implants, extractions and etc. Dr. Nguyen is also a member of the American Dental Association and the Oklahoma Dental Association.

During Dr. Nguyen’s free time he enjoys spending time with family with an occasional round of golf. Dr. Nguyen and his wife have a lovely beagle that they adore. He is a huge sports fan and enjoys watching the OKC Thunder, Arizona Cardinals, St. Louis Cardinals, and the Sooners.

Dr. Nguyen is honored to work alongside great dentists and amazing staff. He is excited to meet new faces, make new friends and provide the best care to the community.

Dr. David Hoang

Dr. Hoang was born and raised in Oklahoma City, OK. He received his undergraduate degree at The University of Oklahoma and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at The University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. His vision is to create an enjoyable and comfortable environment to deliver treatment at the highest level to patients. Dr. Hoang believes in providing lifetime and comprehensive care by offering a wide range of procedures and treatment options to serve the individual needs of each patient.

Read More Dr. Hoang is committed to continuing education and has completed over 500 training hours with a focus on Invisalign, dental implants, advanced root canals, DOCs Oral Sedation, esthetics and smile makeovers.

Outside of the dental office, Dr. Hoang enjoys spending time with his wife, kids, and fur baby. He enjoys giving back to the community through volunteer events such as the Oklahoma Mission of Mercy and Open Wide Dental clinic. Dr. Hoang enjoys being active with exercise, sports, and being outdoors. In addition, he loves good food, game nights, and cherishing relationships.
