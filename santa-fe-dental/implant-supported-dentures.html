Services That Support Long-Term Denture Success

The process starts with a detailed exam and digital imaging. Our team checks bone density, oral health, and bite alignment before recommending treatment options.

After implant placement, the jawbone gradually bonds with the implants during healing. Once integration finishes, we attach the denture restoration securely to the implants. Some patients qualify for immediate temporary restorations, while others benefit from a staged approach that allows additional healing time.

Every mouth heals differently, so timelines vary. Still, patients often say the adjustment period feels easier than expected because implant-supported dentures stay more stable from the beginning.

The Treatment Process

The biggest changes often happen during ordinary moments. Patients eat crunchy foods again without hesitation, laugh without covering their mouths, and speak with fewer interruptions.

Implant-supported dentures also distribute pressure more evenly across the jaw. Traditional dentures place force directly on the gums, which can create sore spots over time. Implant support reduces that pressure and helps maintain a more balanced bite.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do implant-supported dentures last?

Dental implants can last for decades with proper care. The denture portion may require adjustments or replacement over time due to normal wear.

Are implant-supported dentures removable?

Some are removable, while others stay fixed in place. The best option depends on oral health, bone support, and personal preference.

Do implant-supported dentures hurt?

Most patients report mild soreness after implant placement, similar to other dental procedures. Our team provides clear aftercare instructions to help recovery stay manageable.

Can patients eat normally with implant-supported dentures?

Most patients regain much stronger chewing ability compared to traditional dentures. Firmer foods usually become easier to eat after healing finishes.
