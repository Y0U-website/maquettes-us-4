Why Choose Santa Fe Dental for Dental Implant Treatment?

The first step in dental implant treatment is a consultation with one of our experienced dentists. During this consultation, we will examine your teeth and discuss your goals for treatment. We will also take X-rays or 3D images of your mouth to determine the best placement for your implant.

If dental implants are the right option, we will schedule a surgical appointment to place the implant into your jawbone. The surgery is performed under local anesthesia, and you may experience some swelling and discomfort afterward. We will provide instructions on how to care for the area and manage any pain or discomfort.

Once the implant has fused with the bone and healed, we will attach a small connector called an abutment to the top of the implant. This abutment will hold the replacement tooth or bridge in place. We will take impressions of your mouth to create your custom restoration, which will be attached to the abutment once it is ready.

We will monitor your progress throughout the process and ensure your implant is healing properly. We will also guide you on how to care for your implant and maintain good oral hygiene.
