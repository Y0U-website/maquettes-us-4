Missing teeth affects more than appearance. Eating becomes frustrating, speech changes, and traditional dentures can shift at the wrong moment. At Santa Fe Dental, our team helps patients regain comfort and confidence with implant-supported dentures in Edmond that feel secure and function more naturally than removable options.

Unlike loose-fitting dentures, implant-supported restorations anchor directly to dental implants placed in the jawbone. That added stability changes everyday life in ways many patients notice immediately. Meals feel easier, conversations sound clearer, and the constant worry about slipping dentures fades into the background.

Implant-supported dentures combine dental implants with a custom denture appliance. Instead of resting only on the gums, the denture snaps or attaches to implants placed in the jaw. Those implants act like artificial tooth roots and create a stronger foundation for long-term support.

This treatment works well for patients missing several teeth or a full arch of teeth. Some patients choose removable overdentures, while others prefer fixed restorations that stay in place full-time. During consultations, our team explains each option clearly so patients understand how daily care, comfort, and cost may differ.

Patients can also meet our experienced providers, including Dr. David Allen, Dr. Tri Nguyen, Dr. Ryan Flake, and Dr. David Hoang , who guide treatment with a practical and patient-focused approach.

Traditional dentures often move during meals or while speaking. Over time, the jawbone also shrinks after tooth loss, which changes the fit even more. Implant-supported dentures solve many of those frustrations because the implants hold the restoration in place.
