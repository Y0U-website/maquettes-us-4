Dental Implants

  • Long-Term Solution: With proper care, dental implants can last decades—often a lifetime.
  • Jawbone Preservation: Implants stimulate the bone, helping prevent the bone loss and facial sagging associated with missing teeth.
  • Improved Comfort: Say goodbye to slipping dentures or gaps in your smile.

Traditional Dental Implants

  • Titanium Post : Surgically placed in the jawbone to act as the new tooth root.
  • Abutment : A connector piece that attaches to the post.
  • Crown : A custom-made restoration that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

All-on-4 / All-on-X Implants

  • Immediate Results : Walk out with a brand-new set of fixed teeth the very same day.
  • Cost-Effective : Requires fewer implants compared to traditional full-mouth restorations.
  • Stable & Permanent : No slipping or adhesives like traditional dentures.
  • Restores Confidence : Enjoy a natural-looking, fully functional smile again.
