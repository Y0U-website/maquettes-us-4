The practice

We believe that it is only when our neighbors have their basic needs met, including access to dental care, that our community can truly thrive and flourish.

Our mission is to create lasting oral health for the low-income children, adults, and seniors of Santa Cruz County and neighboring communities so that everyone has the dignity that comes from a healthy smile.

For over 30 years, Dientes has worked to ensure that cost, insurance, income, race, language, and transportation do not prevent people from visiting the dentist. We do this by providing affordable, high-quality, and comprehensive dental care for patients who are on public insurance or uninsured. Since 1992, we have treated 50,000 patients.
