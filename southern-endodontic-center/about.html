Dr. Jason McGovern, D.M.D.

Dr. McGovern graduated from Coffee County High School. He earned his associates degree from South Georgia College in Douglas, Georgia. He later transferred to Valdosta State University where he majored in Chemistry. He received a Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree from the Medical College of Georgia in 2006.

Following dental school, Dr. McGovern practice general dentistry for 14 years in Tifton, Georgia. He was accepted into Endodontics Residency Program at the University of Alabama in Birmingham, Alabama where he received a specialty certificate in Endodontics in 2022. Dr. McGovern is a member of the OKU Dental Honor Society, American Dentals Association, Georgia Dental Association, and the American Association of Endodontics.
