Endodontic treatment is typically performed using local anesthesia to help patients remain comfortable throughout the procedure. In many cases, patients are able to drive themselves home and return to their usual routine the same day, unless we recommend otherwise based on their individual needs.

Our team will review what to expect after treatment and provide clear recovery instructions before you leave the clinic. For patients with dental anxiety, nitrous oxide or a prescription for oral sedation is available.

Please continue taking medications for blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid conditions, or other medical concerns as directed by your physician. If you have any questions about your medications prior to your visit, we recommend contacting our clinic for guidance.
