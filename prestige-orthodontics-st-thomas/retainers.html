How to Care for Your Retainer

To keep your retainer in top condition and maintain its effectiveness, follow these care tips:

🚨 Remember : "If it s not in your face, it s in your case!"

  • Wear your Essix retainer full-time for the first 6 weeks after treatment, then switch to wearing it nightly while you sleep.
  • Clean your retainer daily using cold water, soap, and Q-tips .
  • Avoid toothpaste and hot water they can scratch or warp the plastic.
  • Always store your retainer in its protective case when not in use.
  • Keep it away from heat sources like hot cars or laundry pockets heat can distort the fit.
  • Keep it away from pets dogs love to chew on retainers!
  • Bring your retainer to every orthodontic check-up so we can ensure it fits properly and functions as it should.
