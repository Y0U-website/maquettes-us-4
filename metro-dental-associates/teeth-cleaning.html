What to Expect During a Teeth Cleaning Appointment

First, your dental hygienist will examine your mouth to check for signs of oral health issues. If any concerns are found, the hygienist will consult with the dentist.

Next, the hygienist will use special tools to remove plaque and tartar. This process is known as scaling. It’s thorough and can reach areas that regular brushing can’t.

After scaling, your hygienist will polish your teeth with a high-powered electric brush and gritty toothpaste to give your teeth a smooth, clean finish.

Finally, you may receive a fluoride treatment to protect your teeth until your next visit. The entire process is designed to keep your teeth healthy and your smile bright.

How Often Should You Get Your Teeth Cleaned?

The frequency of teeth cleaning depends on your oral health status. For most people, dentists recommend professional teeth cleaning twice a year. This frequency helps maintain oral health and catch potential issues early.

However, if you have certain risk factors, you may need more frequent cleanings. These factors include gum disease, frequent cavities, or a weak immune response to bacterial infection.
