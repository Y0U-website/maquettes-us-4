The practice

We provide your entire family with a different kind of dentistry, because you deserve nothing less. On your first visit, you’ll meet a gifted dental team that will help you achieve your goals for your smile through care individualized just for you. With advanced technology, such as digital radiographs, and a team dedicated to continuing education, you’ll see stunning results.

Call us today to schedule your initial evaluation. Dr. Gonzalez is excited to meet you and answer any questions you have about your dental health and your treatment options. Together, we will determine the care that is right for you… care that will leave you smiling!

When you arrive, we’ll ask you to complete a general and dental health history form. This form is also available online if you would like to complete it ahead of time. You’ll then visit with one of our dental hygienists who will assess your dental and oral health with a comprehensive oral evaluation. During this time, we’ll also take digital X-rays of your teeth to identify any areas of concern.

It is our office policy that any child under the age of 18 be accompanied by a parent or guardian. Upon arrival, you will be asked to complete a patient registration form for your child. For your convenience, this can be done ahead of time by accessing our online form.

Beginning with your first visit, we make it our priority to ensure each child’s dental experience at Union Family Dental is as pleasurable as possible.
