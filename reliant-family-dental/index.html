Dr. Chris Meunier attended Indiana University Southeast and is a graduate of Indiana University School of Dentistry. He also served as a lieutenant in the U.S. Navy, providing quality dentistry to the Marine Corps Recruit Depot at Parris Island in South Carolina where he received many commendations for clinical excellence and leadership.

Today, he is an active member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Indiana Dental Association, First District Dental Society, and the Select Study Club.

He enjoys continuing education and applying the latest technologies to patients at his Evansville, IN dental office. He is a gentle and compassionate dentist who enjoys building lasting relationships with families who visit his dental office.
