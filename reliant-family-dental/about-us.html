Meet the Team at Reliant Family Dental

From our comfortable and welcoming waiting room to our friendly and fun team members, we work hard to make sure every detail is perfect so your experience at Reliant Family Dental is fantastic each and every time.

We know going to the dentist usually doesn’t top the list of most people’s favorite activities, but we are trying to change that, one patient at a time!

We’d like to take a moment to introduce the members of our team. We look forward to getting to know you as well!

Dr. Chris Meunier, DDS

Upon the completion of his military service, Dr. Meunier and his family returned to the Evansville area. Dr. Meunier is an active member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Indiana Dental Association, First District Dental Society, and the Select Study Club. He devotes a lot of time to continuing education, ensuring his patients benefit from the latest technology and techniques.

Dr. Meunier and his wife, Cindy, have five wonderful children and reside in Newburgh. In his free time, he likes to play golf and help coach his kids’ sports teams.

Ashley

When enjoying her free time, you will find Ashley with her husband, kids, family, and friends.
