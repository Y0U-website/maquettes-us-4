How do I take care of my dental implants?

Caring for dental implants is as easy as caring for natural teeth. Brush and floss twice per day and visit your dentist in Evansville, IN regularly for exams and cleanings. It’s essential to maintain these practices because dental implants will not prevent dental infections and gum disease both of which can cause implant failure and additional tooth and bone loss.

It is also essential to treat any conditions that may compromise your dental implant restorations in Evansville, IN.
