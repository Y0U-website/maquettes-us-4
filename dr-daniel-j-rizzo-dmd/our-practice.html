Mission-Vision Statement

Our dental practice is dedicated to restoring and enhancing your smile using state-of-the-art and conservative procedures. It is our promise to every patient that we will help them achieve beautiful and long-lasting results that they will love.

High Standards

We uphold the highest of standards with every procedure that we undertake to ensure quality results. Dr. Daniel Rizzo, DMD provides advanced general dentistry treatments to improve your dental health. Our team also ensures that patients with dental emergency needs are seen and cared for as soon as possible.

Education and Prevention

We believe that a holistic approach to dental care and education is the best way to provide appropriate health care, not just disease care. Procedures such as checking your teeth and gum health, checking for oral cancer, and performing x-rays are done when necessary. Our practice also promotes routine maintenance such as cleaning and flossing.

In addition, we look at your medical history to see if you have any conditions that may be affecting your dental health. This way, we not only take care of your teeth, but also your overall well-being.

Uncompromising Safety

Our practice conforms to the standards recommended by the American Dental Association (ADA), the Occupational Safety and Health Administration, (OSHA), and the Center for Disease Control (CDC) when it comes to clinic safety. Infection control in our office involves strict sterilization and cross-contamination processes.

Training and Expertise

Our team is composed of highly trained and skilled clinicians who undergo continuous education and learning to ensure that we are following industry standards. We keep ourselves informed of the latest techniques, products, equipment, and developments in advanced general dentistry through lectures, conventions, and more.

We are also members of various professional dental associations which help us stay up to date with any changes and improvements for our profession. These efforts are our way of ensuring that we give the best possible services to all of our patients.

A Positive Experience

Dental visits can be scary for some patients, but we aim to help ease their worries by providing a comfortable experience during each appointment. We use a personal touch and incorporate a hometown feel in our clinic so they will always look forward in every visit.

We thank you for allowing us to take care of your dental needs and look forward to serving you.
