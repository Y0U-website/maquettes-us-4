  • Omaha Pediatric Dentistry Clinic at Children s Nebraska
  • Caring for Champions Dental Clinic at UNMC Munroe-Meyer Institute in Omaha

Why Choose our Clinics for Your Dental Care?

Quality Care

By helping individuals access quality dental services, we promote better oral health for all Nebraskans. At our Lincoln clinic and our Omaha pediatric clinic, we offer reduced rates compared to private practices, making high-quality dental care more accessible.

Compassionate Care

Patients benefit from a team approach that includes oral experts and students in the final phases of clinical training. Patients receive extensive personalized attention. Our team members are trained to care for patients with special needs, the elderly, non-English speakers, those with dental anxiety and other special groups.

Comprehensive Care

Our clinics provide for all of your dental needs, including exams, cleanings, braces, filings, crowns, bridges, root canals, oral surgery, dental implants, dentures, periodontal care, cosmetic dentistry, pediatric dentistry and oral pathology. We offer a breadth of technology unsurpassed among many dental schools. For example, with CAD/CAM technology, we even offer same-day crowns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to become a patient?

Anyone is eligible to become one of our valued patients. We accept patients of all ages and income levels.

What services are provided?

We offer comprehensive dental care year-round at our Lincoln clinic for adults and children; at our Omaha clinic for adults; and at our Omaha pediatric dentistry clinic for children. Comprehensive care for children and adults also is offered through the UNMC Munroe-Meyer Institute in Omaha in our Caring for Champions Clinic.

How much are fees for dental care?

We offer significantly reduced fees compared to regional dental fees at our Lincoln clinic for adults and children, and at our Omaha pediatric dentistry clinic. Rates at our adult Omaha clinic are comparable to regional dental fees.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept insurance. Find the types of insurance accepted at our Lincoln Clinics , our Omaha Pediatric Dentistry Clinic , our Omaha Clinic and the Caring for Champions Clinic at UNMC’s Munroe-Meyer Institute.

How long are appointments?

In our Lincoln clinics and our Omaha Pediatric Dentistry Clinic, where students provide some care, appointments are longer than typical appointments in private practice and is one reason why fees are lower in those clinics. New patient appointments with students typically are scheduled for four hours, though not all new patients will require that much time.

Do you offer services at federally qualified health centers?

  • Gering: Dental and dental hygiene students provide care at the Community Action Partnership of Western Nebraska .
  • Columbus – Dental and dental hygiene students provide care at the Good Neighbor Community Health Center.
