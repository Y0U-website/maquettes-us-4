Our team is dedicated to giving each patient a calming, carefree experience while providing them with the highest level of dental care. Making your dental health our top priority, we will partner with you to create a healthy and beautiful smile.

Dr. Tammy Roz has been practicing dentistry in the Five Towns since 2002. After graduating from Brooklyn College and NYU College of Dentistry, she went on to complete a General Practice Residency Program at Peninsula Hospital Center in Far Rockaway, NY. She won an award after completing her residency for her outstanding achievements in Prosthetics.

Our service is at the core of everything we do. Our team works together to provide a warm, comfortable, and relaxing environment.
