Your board-certified pediatric dentist

Dr. Kristen Cummings

Dr. Kristen spent over a decade practicing as a contracted pediatric dentist in Missouri before the opportunity arose to take over an established pediatric practice in Shiloh when the original doctor retired. She saw it as her chance, and she has never looked back. Since 2015, she has been building a practice rooted in community, trust, and a genuine love for the families she serves.

With over two decades of clinical experience, Dr. Kristen is known for her conservative, honest approach to treatment. She never puts unnecessary worry on parents, always making it clear that a child needing dental work is not a reflection of anything done wrong. Her ability to put both kids and parents at ease is one of the things her patients comment on most.

She is particularly passionate about preventive care and early education, believing that the habits and experiences children have in the dental chair shape their relationship with their oral health for life. She approaches every appointment with warmth, efficiency, and a whole lot of encouragement.

When she is not in the office, Dr. Kristen loves spending time with her husband and daughter, playing tennis, staying active, and traveling. She brings that same energy and enthusiasm to everything she does, inside the practice and out.

Your first visit

Here is exactly what to expect when you walk through our doors

We know that a child&#x27;s first dental visit, or any visit to a new practice, can feel like a big deal for parents and kids alike. At All Grins 4 Kids, we have designed every step of the experience to make it feel easy, comfortable, and even a little fun. From the moment you call us, we are already working to make you feel at home.

What to bring To help us get your child checked in and ready for their visit, please bring the following:

Insurance card(s) or TriCare information

Completed new patient forms (available online through our patient portal)

Photo ID for the accompanying parent or guardian

Care and comfort, every step of the way

We have thought about every detail of the experience at All Grins 4 Kids, from the technology we use to deliver precise, efficient care, to the little extras that make the office feel like a place your child actually wants to be.

Teddy, our comfort dog

Teddy is a beloved member of our team and one of the first things patients ask about when they come in. He has a way of calming nerves and bringing smiles to even the most anxious little visitors. Patients often say seeing Teddy is their favorite part of the visit.

Child-friendly communication

Language matters, especially with kids. Our entire team is trained to use age-appropriate, non-scary language during every visit, turning "x-rays" into "pictures" and "cleanings" into "counting teeth." This approach helps children feel safe and in control, and builds the kind of trust that keeps them coming back without anxiety.

Cloud-based records and patient protal

We use Oryx, a modern cloud-based practice management system, and offer a MyChart patient portal where all records can be accessed and shared with a single click. Whether you need to share records with a specialist or simply review your child&#x27;s treatment plan, everything is available at your fingertips.

Digital x-rays

Our digital x-ray technology allows us to capture clear, detailed images with significantly less radiation than traditional x-rays, and to share results with you immediately during the appointment.
