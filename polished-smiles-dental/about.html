0 “I’m Dr. Elizabeth Baumann, owner of Polished Smiles Dental, your local, small-town dental office in Watertown, WI. I knew that I wanted to be a dentist from a young age and I’m so happy to be practicing in a small community. I completed my undergraduate degree at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and graduated dental school in 2016, from Marquette University School of Dentistry. After graduating Marquette, I completed a General Practice Residency at the University of Washington Medical Center in Seattle, WA.

I worked the first part of my career as an associate dentist in Wauwatosa, WI for a large dental office. I valued my time working there and developed the skills needed to create long-lasting, trusting relationships with my team and more importantly patients.

In addition to owning Polished Smiles Dental, I am a Captain in the Wisconsin Army National Guard, proudly serving for over 4 years and I hope to continue my service to our country for many years to come.

I am a member of the American Dental Association, the Wisconsin Dental Association, and the Seattle Study Club. In my free time, I enjoy gardening, cooking, being outside and spending time with my husband, Drew and my daughter, Charlotte.”
