Meet the Staff

Dr. David Eldredge is originally from Utah but feels at home in Corvallis now. He is married to Katelin and they have three beautiful daughters. They both enjoy rock climbing, hiking, and generally being outside. He’s always reading something, so be sure to suggest a book or ask him what he’s reading some time.

Dr. Eldredge graduated in the top 10 % of his class in dental school and served as the president of his dental class as well as the Esthetic and Cosmetic Dentistry Club. He took electives in advanced surgery in school and has since taken courses to become proficient at removing wisdom teeth and placing dental implants. He loves learning so much he often takes 10x the number of required continuing education course work required of Oregon dentists each year.

Since he was 12, Dr. Theron Nebeker knew he wanted to be a dentist, and as a native of the Willamette Valley, he had no doubt he would stay and raise his own family here. After serving a 2 year mission trip in Paraguay, he attended Brigham Young University for his undergrad, where he met his wife. He then proceeded on to Oregon Health & Science University to complete his dental training. Dr. Nebeker’s favorite aspect of dentistry is seeing people feel confident in their smiles.

Dr. Nebeker has 4 kids and a black cat named Coconut. He enjoys fishing, watching and playing sports, reading (especially history), and anything to do with the outdoors, but his favorite hobby is spending time with his family.

Dr. Connor Brown is originally from Orange County, California, and now calls Albany, Oregon home. He’s married to his wife, Malia, and together they have three amazing kids: Lyla, Rowan, and Eleanor. When he’s not in the office, Dr. Brown enjoys snowboarding, dirt biking, playing board games, and spending time outdoors with his family. He also enjoys winding down with a good book when he gets the chance.

Dr. Brown completed his undergraduate studies at California State University, Long Beach before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of the Pacific in San Francisco.

What he loves most about dentistry is helping people feel confident not just in the way they smile, but in how they feel about themselves. He is passionate about building meaningful relationships with his patients and making sure each person feels comfortable, understood, and cared for.
