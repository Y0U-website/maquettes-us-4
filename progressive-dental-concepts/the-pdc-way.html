Less influence to negotiate favorable insurance contracts

Progressive Dental Concepts

Shared administrative resources lead to less risk for dental owners

Built-in salary and benefits, which includes your professional liability insurance, plus opportunities for higher income because of shared business efficiencies

Shared resources for marketing and facility up-keep

Access to mentors and continuing education for dentists to expand their clinical and business operations knowledge

Negotiates strategic insurance contracts

Has resources to build culture and recruit the best team to support clinical efforts

Flexible opportunities to meet your goals; options include maintaining an economic interest in a practice, investing in a practice, or an incentive-based compensation model

Dental Services Organization

Less control over clinical aspects while still having to meet business objectives

Access to clinical mentors and continuing education
