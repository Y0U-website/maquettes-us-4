Transform Your Smile with Our Services

Dental Implants

If you’ve lost a tooth, dental implants offer a reliable solution by replacing both the root and crown. Implants serve as anchors for replacement teeth, providing a natural appearance, enhanced comfort, and long-term convenience. The process involves fitting a titanium root into the jawbone, which requires a healing period of 6 weeks to 6 months before the replacement tooth is secured.

Crowns

When a tooth is severely damaged, a crown may be necessary to restore its structure. Crowns also help secure dental bridges, cover implants, or repair fractured teeth. Custom-made crowns, such as durable gold or natural-looking e-max, fit over the original tooth, providing strength and durability.

Bridges

Missing teeth can disrupt chewing and speaking abilities. A dental bridge fills the gap by attaching artificial teeth to the surrounding healthy teeth. This helps restore proper jaw alignment and improves functionality.

Veneers

If you have crooked, stained, or irregularly shaped teeth, veneers can transform your smile. These thin porcelain shells are custom-fitted over the front of your teeth, offering a natural appearance while enhancing color and shape.

Cosmetic Bonding

Bonding is a quick, affordable, and non-invasive way to repair imperfections such as chips, stains, or gaps. Composite resin is applied to your teeth, sculpted into shape, and hardened, providing immediate results.

Fillings

Dental fillings are used to treat cavities or repair broken teeth. Whether you choose composite, porcelain, or silver amalgam, fillings restore the tooth’s structure, ensuring better function and appearance.

Extractions

In some cases, tooth extraction is necessary due to severe decay or overcrowding. The procedure is done with local anesthesia for maximum comfort, and we ensure a smooth recovery process.

Dentures

If you’ve lost all your teeth, dentures offer a solution to restore your smile and functionality. After any remaining teeth are extracted, temporary dentures are fitted, and once the mouth heals, permanent custom dentures are created.

Nitrous Oxide

For anxious patients, Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas) helps create a relaxed and comfortable state during dental procedures. It is a safe and effective option for reducing anxiety and speeding up recovery time.

Invisalign Braces

Achieve a straighter smile with virtually invisible Invisalign braces. These clear aligners gradually shift your teeth without the discomfort of traditional braces. They are removable, making it easy to keep your teeth clean during treatment.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry includes services such as fillings, crowns, bridges, dental implants, and dentures. At Smile 1 Dental Group, we provide comprehensive care to restore your smile and improve oral function.

Periodontal Therapy

We emphasize preventive care to avoid gum disease, but if you have chronic periodontal issues, we offer advanced non-surgical treatments. If surgery is required, we focus on maximizing comfort and promoting natural healing.

Root Canal Therapy

A root canal can save a tooth with infected or damaged pulp. The procedure involves cleaning and sealing the root canal, followed by placing a crown to strengthen the tooth. Root canals are generally straightforward with minimal discomfort.

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile with professional teeth whitening. Whether caused by coffee, tea, or smoking, our whitening treatments effectively restore the natural shine of your teeth, boosting both appearance and confidence.

TMJ Treatment

If you suffer from jaw pain, headaches, or teeth sensitivity, you may have Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMJ). We offer a detailed examination and personalized treatment plan to address TMJ symptoms and alleviate discomfort.
