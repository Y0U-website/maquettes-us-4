What to Expect From Implant Preparation

Consultation and Digital Planning

Consultation and Digital Planning

Your journey begins with a detailed consultation focused on understanding your goals and evaluating your oral health. Advanced imaging, including 3D scans, allows your provider to assess bone quality, nerve location, and sinus anatomy. Digital impressions replace traditional molds, making the process more comfortable. This planning stage helps determine whether an implant can be placed immediately or if preparation such as bone grafting is needed to support the best outcome.

Tooth Removal and Bone Preparation

Tooth Removal and Bone Preparation

If a tooth needs to be removed, every effort is made to preserve the surrounding bone. In many cases, bone grafting is placed at the time of extraction to act as a scaffold for new bone growth. When conditions allow, an implant may be placed during the same visit, reducing the number of surgeries and shortening overall treatment time. If immediate placement isn’t possible, the graft is allowed to heal so the bone maintains proper shape and strength.

Healing and Readiness for Implant Placement

Healing and Readiness for Implant Placement

Healing is a key part of implant preparation. Bone grafts typically require several months to fully integrate and mature. During this time, you’ll receive guidance on diet, hygiene, and comfort to support recovery. Follow-up visits allow healing to be monitored and ensure the bone is ready. Once preparation is complete, implant placement can proceed with confidence, helping deliver stable, long-term results you can rely on.
