Mini Implants Overview

Replace a missing tooth in one day.

If you are missing one or more teeth but are apprehensive to the thought of getting a traditional implant, mini dental implants may be the option for you. These implants are less expensive, minimally invasive, and require less healing time where appropriate.

Typically, mini implants can be placed with a crown made and seated in a single day. Additionally, these implants can be used for the retention of dentures and partials.
