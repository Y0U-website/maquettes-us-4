Payment Plans

If you are worried about the cost, ask us about the different payment options we offer.

We participate in CareCredit which offers a variety of easy and flexible payment plans so you can have your dental and cosmetic wants and needs taken care of NOW.

You can apply at our office or online at www.carecredit.com . You can even be pre-approved before your first appointment. Even if you have dental insurance, this can be used to cover expenses not covered by your plan. You can call CareCredit at 1-800-365-8295.
