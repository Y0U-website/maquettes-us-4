cbct imagery

CBCT provides detailed 3D images of teeth, roots, and surrounding structures that cannot be seen on traditional 2D dental X-rays. This advanced imaging technology helps identify hidden canals, root fractures, resorption, and other conditions, allowing for more accurate diagnosis, precise treatment planning, and improved treatment outcomes.

Our CBCT system utilizes Planmeca Ultra Low Dose™ technology, an advanced 3D imaging protocol designed to provide exceptional diagnostic information while minimizing radiation exposure. In many cases, this technology can capture a three-dimensional CBCT scan with an effective radiation dose that is even lower than that of a conventional panoramic radiograph.

This innovative approach allows us to obtain highly detailed diagnostic images with significantly reduced patient exposure, without compromising the image quality needed for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning. The result is safer, more precise imaging that supports the highest standard of endodontic care.

Digital radiographs Digital radiography provides high-quality diagnostic images instantly while using significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays. Immediate image availability improves efficiency, enhances patient safety, and allows seamless electronic sharing of records with your dentist to help coordinate care and reduce treatment delays.
