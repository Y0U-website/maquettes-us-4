0 Meet Dr. Warner Dr. Brian Warner received his B.S. in Psychology from the University of Michigan. Following graduation, he relocated to Boston where he graduated magna cum laude and received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. At Tufts, Dr. Warner earned many accolades including honors for high performance on the National Board exams, merit scholarships for exceptional academic performance, and membership to the Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society.

Following 3 years of general practice in the Boston and New York City areas, Dr. Warner returned to school to earn his specialty Certificate in Endodontics from New York University College of Dentistry. While at NYU, he was selected by his peers to serve as Chief Resident. He also participated in dental outreach trips to provide treatment to under-served areas of Mexico and Nicaragua.

Dr. Warner is a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics (Board Certified), an achievement only 1 in 5 endodontists may claim.

MEET DR. CHAVEZ Dr. Robert Chavez brings more than 30 years of endodontic experience and a distinguished background in private practice, hospital dentistry, graduate dental education, public health, and military service. Throughout his career, he has established a reputation for clinical excellence, thoughtful patient care, and mentorship of the next generation of dentists and specialists.

Most recently, Dr. Chavez served as an Endodontist and General Practice Residency Attending at Denver Health Medical Center, where he directed the endodontic curriculum and helped train future dentists. Prior to that, he served as Dental Team Lead for one of Colorado’s largest public health dental clinics and owned a successful endodontic specialty practice in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

Dr. Chavez earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Colorado Health Sciences Center and completed both his Certificate in Endodontics and Master of Science degree at the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston. Beyond clinical practice, he has served as a Specialty Consultant to the Colorado State Board of Dental Examiners and currently serves as a Forensic Odontologist for the Arapahoe County Coroner’s Office.
