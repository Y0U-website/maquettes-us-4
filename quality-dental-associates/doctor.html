Meet Dr. Amer Azizogli, DDS

Amer Azizogli D.D.S. earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology. He then went on to New York University, where he graduated in 1993. Dr. Azizogli finished a 2-year training program in dental implantology.

Amer is a proud member of the Eastern Region of the Dental Practitioners Association, he had extensive training in Cosmetic Dentistry, and has received honors for his work with various charities.

He is a member of the International Association for Orthodontics.
