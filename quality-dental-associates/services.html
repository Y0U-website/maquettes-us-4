General Dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry

Professional-grade whitening treatments to brighten your smile by several shades in just one visit.

Restorative Dentistry

Repair and restore damaged teeth to full function, comfort, and natural beauty.

Dental Crowns

Custom-made caps that restore strength, function, and appearance to damaged or decayed teeth.

Dental Bridges

Fixed prosthetic devices that replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent natural teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

Pain-relieving treatment that saves infected teeth by removing damaged pulp and sealing the tooth.

Orthodontics

Straighten your teeth and achieve your ideal bite alignment for improved function and aesthetics.

Traditional Braces

Metal or ceramic brackets with wires that gradually move teeth into proper alignment.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Virtually invisible, removable aligners that straighten teeth without metal components.

Retainers

Custom-made devices that maintain teeth in their new positions after orthodontic treatment.

Dental Implants

Permanent solutions for missing teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Single Tooth Implants

Titanium posts that replace individual missing teeth roots, topped with natural-looking crowns.

Implant-Supported Bridges

Bridges anchored by dental implants rather than natural teeth, ideal for multiple missing teeth.

All-on-4 Implant Dentures

Full arch replacement secured by just four strategically placed implants for maximum stability.

Pediatric Dentistry

Children’s Dental Exams

Comprehensive evaluations to monitor growth and development of teeth and jaws.

Kids’ Cleanings

Gentle professional cleanings with age-appropriate oral hygiene instruction.

Dental Sealants

Protective coatings applied to chewing surfaces to prevent cavities in molars.
