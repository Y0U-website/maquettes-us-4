Checkup and Cleaning

A dental checkup cleaning is a routine visit to the Dentist that consists of an examination and a teeth cleaning, usually recommended every six months. Regular dental checkups and cleanings can help prevent cavities, gum disease, bad breath, tooth loss, and other oral health problems and treat any dental problems and to maintain good oral health.

During a dental checkup, the dentist will:

  • Perform a thorough examination of your mouth, teeth, and gums
  • Advise you on ways to improve your oral hygiene and general teeth-cleaning habits
  • Recommend treatment or other measures for dental problems, if necessary
  • Polish your teeth
  • Report their findings to the dentist
  • Take X-rays if ordered by the dentist

Dentures

A dental denture is a removable replacement for missing teeth and surrounding tissues. It can help you improve your appearance and your oral health. There are two types of dental dentures: complete and partial. A complete denture is used when all your natural teeth are gone, while a partial denture is used when some of your natural teeth remain.

Dental dentures can be customized to fit your mouth and match your natural teeth color. You may need some time to adjust to your new denture, but once you do, you will enjoy the benefits of having a beautiful smile and a functional bite.

Dentures can be removable or fixed, depending on your preference and dental condition.

  • Removable dentures can be taken out and cleaned easily.
  • Fixed dentures are attached to implants or crowns, and they are more stable and comfortable.

Inlays Onlays

Dental inlays and onlays are types of restorations that can repair teeth that have moderate damage or decay. They are different from fillings and crowns in terms of size, shape, and placement. Dental inlays are used to fill the space in the center of a tooth that has been affected by a cavity or injury. Dental inlays can strengthen the natural tooth. Dental inlays do not cover the cusps or points of the tooth.

Dental onlays are used to cover a larger area of a tooth that has more extensive damage or decay. They can restore the function and appearance of the tooth and prevent further problems. They are usually applied to molars and premolars that have a biting surface.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic procedure that can improve the appearance of your smile. It involves applying a bleaching agent to your teeth, which breaks down stains and discoloration. Teeth whitening can be done at home or by a dentist, depending on your preferences and budget. Some benefits of teeth whitening are:

  • It can boost your confidence and self-esteem.
  • It can make you look younger and healthier.
  • It can enhance your oral hygiene and prevent tooth decay.
