Fun with Ward + Campbell Orthodontics

we believe in family, friends and community.

We believe small contributions can make a big difference. We give back to the community through doing what we can to support our patient’s, our schools, and our local businesses.

Our Ice Cream Truck

Reserve our ice cream truck to raise money for your school or non-profit! We buy the ice cream and bring you the truck, you sell the ice cream and keep the money for your good cause!

How about some Popcorn?

Ward + Campbell Orthodontics has a snow cone machine and a popcorn machine. Reserve either machine and we will provide the supplies for your school or non-profit fundraiser! You provide the event, we provide the fun, you keep the profits!

Jae Foundation

Ward + Campbell Orthodontics is a proud sponsor of the Jae Foundation, a non-profit in the Magic Valley who works to raise mental health awareness in our community. For more information, visit their website: jaefoundation.com.

Local School Support

Ward + Campbell Orthodontics has always been a supporter of education in our community. We participate in sponsorship on many levels and love to see our patients thrive in their school activities.
