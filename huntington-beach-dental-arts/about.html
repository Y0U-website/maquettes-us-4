Dr. Freddie Hsiao

At Huntington Beach Dental Arts, Dr. Freddie Hsiao (“sh-ow”) believes exceptional dentistry is equal parts science, artistry, and genuine human connection. His philosophy is simple: every patient deserves thoughtful care, honest guidance, and treatment designed to improve not only their smile, but their long-term health and quality of life.

Dr. Hsiao is passionate about creating healthy, natural-looking smiles that stand the test of time. Whether helping a child feel comfortable during their first visit, restoring a worn smile with comprehensive treatment, or replacing missing teeth with dental implants, he approaches every case with careful planning, attention to detail, and a commitment to lasting results.

A graduate of the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, Dr. Hsiao graduated near the top of his class and has remained deeply committed to continuing education throughout his career. He regularly pursues advanced training in implant dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, full-mouth rehabilitation, occlusion, and digital dentistry to provide patients with the most modern, predictable, and conservative treatment options available.

Before establishing Huntington Beach Dental Arts, Dr. Hsiao proudly served as a dental officer in the United States Navy, where he provided comprehensive dental care in a highly disciplined clinical environment. That experience reinforced the values that continue to guide his practice today: integrity, precision, accountability, and putting patients first.

Outside the office, Dr. Hsiao enjoys spending time with family and friends, staying active, and continually refining his skills through advanced education. He is honored to continue the legacy of caring for generations of families in the Huntington Beach community while helping Huntington Beach Dental Arts become a destination for exceptional, comprehensive dental care.

A tradition of trusted care. A commitment to the future.

Our practice was originally established in 1987 and has served generations of Huntington Beach families for nearly 40 years.

In 2005, Dr. Christopher Chan continued the office&#x27;s tradition of compassionate, patient-focused care started by Dr. Meirovitz. Upon his retirement, Dr. Chan carefully selected Dr. Freddie Hsiao to carry that legacy forward.

Today, our team remains committed to the same values that have guided the practice for decades: honesty, integrity, quality dentistry, and genuine relationships with our patients.
