Get to Know Dr. Michelle Ostrovsky

Dr. Michelle Ostrovsky is a General Dentist with 19 years of experience in the dental field and a passion for building genuine relationships with her patients. She earned her undergraduate degree from UMass Amherst before attending Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. During her training, she completed an externship at Federal Medical Center Devens, where she gained experience treating medically complex patients, and has also pursued research in TMJ and sleep-related disorders.

Originally from Sharon, Massachusetts, Dr. Ostrovsky’s family immigrated to the United States from Moscow, Russia in 1990. Fluent in Russian, she discovered her love for dentistry at an early age through her passion for detailed, hands-on work like knitting, crocheting, and baking. Today, she lives in Mansfield with her husband, son, and two poodles. Outside the office, she enjoys hiking, camping, dancing, creative projects, and making home movies with her family.
