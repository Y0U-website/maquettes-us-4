About Atmore Family Dental

Our mission is to provide the best quality of care to each patient, while maintaining a relaxing and enjoyable environment.

The practice

For patients with a little anxiety about visiting the dentist, we offer nitrous sedation.

We offer a full array of services including general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry. We treat patients of all ages, including most children. Please call us today and let our courteous staff help you schedule an appointment.

Restorative Dentistry involves the “restoration” of teeth that have been damaged, decayed, or lost. We offer complete and partial dentures, same day crowns and bridges, restoration of dental implants, and extractions.

We are a Christian based practice that strives to serve our patients with the love and care that Christ embodies. We accept new patients and we accept many different types of insurances, people without insurance, and children. We truly want to provide dental care for the entire family. We enjoy serving and being apart of our community everyday.
