The Mission of Revive Dental

We are a community dental center focused on patients who need low-cost dental care, especially uninsured individuals and those enrolled in addiction recovery programs. We offer a wide range of treatment options for patients in need, including extractions, dentures, root canals, and even more sophisticated treatment such as crowns and implants.

Please drop by when we are open to schedule an appointment, or simply walk in to be seen.

The Values at Revive Dental

We are a faith-based, Christian non-profit community dental center. Our clinicians are volunteers dedicated to serving others. Our goal is to “love our neighbors” and to demonstrate the love of Christ in action. We fully believe we can impact the community in a positive way by providing dental care to folks. Many of our volunteers are leaders in their field and accomplished clinicians practicing in the Chattanooga area.
