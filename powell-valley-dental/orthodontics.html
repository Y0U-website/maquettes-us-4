Clear Aligners With Invisalign

For patients seeking a more discreet and removable option, Invisalign clear aligners offer an excellent alternative. These transparent trays are custom-made to gradually reposition your teeth without wires or brackets.

Aligners must be worn 20 to 22 hours per day and are replaced every few weeks to keep progress on track. Most patients complete treatment in about one year, though small adjustments may take only a few months.
