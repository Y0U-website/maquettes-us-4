Dental Implants

A Stable and Natural Way to Replace Missing Teeth

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium, surgically placed into the jaw to support a custom crown. This provides a permanent replacement for missing teeth, both restoring appearance and preventing bone loss.

Unlike bridges or dentures, implants offer unmatched stability, preserve the jawbone, and do not rely on neighboring teeth. At Powell Valley Dental, we recommend implants as a long-lasting, natural-feeling solution for eligible patients.

Single Tooth Implants to Complete Your Smile

When a single tooth is missing, an implant with a custom crown can fully restore your bite and appearance. The implant is placed into the jawbone, and after healing, a crown is attached that matches your natural teeth in color and shape.

This treatment prevents shifting of nearby teeth, supports the bone, and restores full function. It is ideal for patients seeking a conservative and lasting solution.

All-on-4 Full Arch Implants

For patients missing all upper or lower teeth, All-on-4 provides a complete and secure solution. This system uses four strategically placed implants to anchor a full set of permanent teeth.

All-on-4 offers superior comfort and function compared to traditional dentures. The prosthetic is fixed in place, does not need adhesives, and allows for normal eating and speaking. Patients benefit from improved stability, facial support, and jawbone preservation.

Timing, Healing, and Cost Considerations

Most implants are placed in a single visit, often in under one hour. Gum tissue typically heals within a few days, while the implant integrates with bone over three to six months. Placing implants soon after tooth loss can reduce the risk of bone loss and avoid the need for bone grafting.

Costs depend on the number of implants, type of restoration, required preparation, sedation option, and materials used. We will review every detail during your consultation and offer dental financing options to help make care affordable.
