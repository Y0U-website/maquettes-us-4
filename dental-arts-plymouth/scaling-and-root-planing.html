Long-Term Benefits of Periodontal Therapy

Scaling and root planing not only treats existing gum disease but also helps prevent its progression. Benefits include:

  • Reduced gum inflammation and bleeding
  • Improved gum attachment to teeth
  • Lower risk of tooth loss
  • Fresher breath and cleaner mouth
  • Enhanced overall oral and systemic health
