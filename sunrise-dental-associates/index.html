Discover our full range of dental services designed to keep your smile healthy, beautiful, and confident.

Services include routine cleanings to keep your smile bright and healthy, root canals to treat infected teeth, scaling and root planing to address gum disease, and extractions when a tooth cannot be saved. Our goal is to help you achieve and maintain strong, healthy teeth for life.

Restorative Dentisty Services include composite fillings, inlays and onlays, crowns, dental bridges, implant restorations, and dentures—all designed to strengthen your teeth and improve your bite. Our goal is to help you regain a natural-looking, confident smile that lasts.

Cosmetic Dentistry Services include Glow® Teeth Whitening for a brighter smile, Invisalign® for discreet teeth straightening, veneers for a flawless finish, cosmetic bonding to repair minor imperfections, and micro-abrasion to remove surface stains. These treatments are designed to help you achieve a confident, radiant smile you’ll love to share.

Dental Implants we offer advanced dental implant solutions designed to replace missing teeth, restore function, preserve bone health, and dramatically improve your smile’s appearance and comfort. Sleep Apnea & TMJ Treatment we provide personalized, non-invasive treatment options for sleep apnea and TMJ disorders that focus on long-term relief, improved sleep quality, and better overall health. Our approach combines advanced diagnostics with custom oral appliance therapy to address the root cause of symptoms while prioritizing patient comfort.

Les soins

Professional dental cleanings at Sunrise Dental remove plaque and tartar buildup to prevent cavities and gum disease while keeping your smile clean, healthy, and refreshed.

Scaling and root planing is a deep-cleaning periodontal treatment that removes harmful bacteria below the gumline to help reverse gum disease and restore healthy gum tissue.

For patients missing a single tooth, single tooth dental implants provide a stable and natural-looking solution with a custom crown attached to an implant post, restoring chewing ability and eliminating the need to alter healthy adjacent teeth.
