Our Services

Comprehensive General Dentistry You Can Trust

Professional Teeth Cleanings

Professional dental cleanings at Sunrise Dental remove plaque and tartar buildup to prevent cavities and gum disease while keeping your smile clean, healthy, and refreshed.

Oral Examinations & Dental Checkups

Our routine dental exams include a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw using advanced diagnostic tools to detect cavities, gum disease, and oral health concerns early.

Scaling & Root Planing (Periodontal Therapy)

Scaling and root planing is a deep-cleaning periodontal treatment that removes harmful bacteria below the gumline to help reverse gum disease and restore healthy gum tissue.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal therapy removes infected tooth pulp to relieve pain, eliminate infection, and save your natural tooth from extraction.

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth cannot be saved, we perform gentle tooth extractions using modern techniques to ensure patient comfort and discuss replacement options to restore your smile.

Custom Mouth Guards

We provide custom-fitted mouth guards to protect your teeth and jaw from injury during sports and high-impact activities while ensuring a comfortable, secure fit.
