Ala Din, DDS

Born and raised in Pakistan, Dr. Din went to dental school in Peshawar, Pakistan before migrating to San Jose, CA to fulfill his dreams and pursue excellence in dentistry. After completing rigorous testing procedures, he earned his license to practice dentistry in California and has been practicing in the Bay Area ever since.

Dr. Din is happily married to Abida Bibi and has six children. He is actively involved in the community and loves spending his spare time with his family.

Jae Yoo, DMD

Dr. Jae Yoo was born in South Korea and raised in Kentucky, where he developed a love for both science and community. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Kentucky before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree at the University of Louisville School of Dentistry. After graduation, Dr. Yoo moved to California to begin his career in dentistry.

Committed to providing the highest level of care, Dr. Yoo stays at the forefront of dentistry by incorporating the latest evidence-based techniques and technology into his practice. He dedicates many hours to continuing education, expanding his skills across all areas of dentistry. When needed, he works closely with trusted specialists to ensure the best possible treatment outcomes for his patients.
