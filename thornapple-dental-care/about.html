Get to know

Dr. Eryn Huber

Dr. Eryn Huber brings nearly two decades of experience, a record of excellence, and a genuine passion for patient relationships to Thornapple Dental Care.

Committed to lifelong learning and clinical excellence, Dr. Huber invests extensive time in advanced continuing education through the Kois Center and The Pankey Institute. Her advanced training focuses on occlusion, cosmetic dentistry, comprehensive treatment planning, and TMJ therapy, allowing her to provide highly personalized care for even the most complex dental concerns.

Dr. Huber offers a full range of general dentistry services and is experienced in treating both children and medically complex patients. She also places dental implants and is certified in Invisalign treatment.

Beyond clinical care, Dr. Huber served on the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry Alumni Board from 2021 to 2024 and remains actively involved in organized dentistry. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, West Michigan Dental Society, Kent County Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry. She is also a member of the local Pankey study club.

She chose general dentistry for the relationships it makes possible—with patients and their families over time. Dr. Huber believes oral health and overall health are deeply connected, and that the mouth is often where larger health issues first appear. She is committed to routine oral cancer screenings, preventive care, and early intervention, knowing that attentive dental care can make a meaningful difference in a patient&#x27;s long-term health and quality of life.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Huber enjoys spending time with her husband and their two young children. Her hobbies include cycling, camping, yoga, and working out. She values an active lifestyle and enjoys making memories with her family both at home and in the outdoors.

Designed with you in mind

Our office blends modern dental technology with a calm, welcoming atmosphere to help make care more efficient and comfortable. From digital systems to a peaceful setting filled with local artwork, every detail is meant to support a better patient experience.

Walk in, breathe easy

From the moment you walk in, Thornapple Dental Care feels different. The space is warm, grounded, and thoughtfully put together, with earthy tones and natural touches that make it easy to settle in and exhale. Local artwork from Grand Rapids lines the lobby, and pieces from the Upper Peninsula, Detroit, and the Leelanau Peninsula give each treatment room its own quiet character.

Kids play area For families visiting the practice, the kids play area helps make appointments easier and more convenient. It gives children a space of their own and helps create a more comfortable experience for both kids and parents.

Your comfort is our priority We believe dental visits should be as comfortable as possible. That&#x27;s why we provide complimentary water, earplugs to minimize procedural sounds, and chapstick to keep you comfortable throughout your appointment. These thoughtful touches are just one way we demonstrate our commitment to your care experience.

Digital tools that support efficient, high-quality care

Thornapple Dental Care uses technology to support a more streamlined experience and thoughtful treatment planning. These tools help improve efficiency while supporting clear communication and quality care.

Fully digital practice We built Thornapple Dental Care as a fully digital practice from the ground up. That means our workflow is smoother, our planning is more precise, and your experience is better for it.

Scanner for crown and bridge treatments Say goodbye to messy impressions. We use a digital scanner for crown and bridge treatment, making the process more precise, more comfortable, and a whole lot easier on you.
