About Us

Make Appointment Who is KidsKare P.C. Family Dentistry? Your Trusted Partner For Dental Health KidsKare P.C. First opened our doors in 1999 in Las Cruces , NM. Soon after that, we realized that there was a population in Las Cruces and especially in rural areas in New Mexico that because of simple geographical location and accessibility issues could not get to see a dentist to help prevent dental problems.

In many cases, the challenge was not the ability to pay or lack of insurance, it was simply the inability or inconvenience to drive long distances to see a dentist. KidsKare P.C. answered the need in 2000 and 2001 by opening up 7 additional state of the art dental facilities in rural areas of NM such as Alamogordo , Anthony , Carlsbad , Clovis , Farmington , Hobbs , Las Cruces , and Roswell New Mexico in an effort to reach those rural populations.

Each KidsKare P.C. Family Dentist location in New Mexico Operates in the highest standards of Dental care for the general public with state of the art equipment, experienced and friendly Dentists, helpful and informative Hygienists and Kids Friendly Staff. As of 2015, KidsKare P.C. Family Dentists serve 8 communities across New Mexico including Alamogordo , Anthony , Carlsbad , Clovis , Farmington , Hobbs , Las Cruces , and Roswell New Mexico.

Insurance?

KidsKare takes nearly all forms of insurance including Medicaid. We work hard on your behalf to maintain a wide variety of accepted insurance providers.

We know that not everyone has the same insurance company and we understand that. This is why our front desk staff are trained to help you get approved and into your appointment without any hassles. There are discounts for patients who wish to self-pay for services and we have coupons on our website to help keep costs down as well.

Our goal is to be your dental adviser and friend your whole life. We want to help you and your family maintain healthy teeth through every step of your life from baby teeth, to wisdom teeth to dentures. If you have not visited us in the past, we invite you to try our services and join the thousands of New Mexicans that love coming to KidsKare P.C. for all their families’ dental needs.
