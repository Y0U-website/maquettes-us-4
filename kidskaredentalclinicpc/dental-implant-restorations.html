Dental Implant Restorations

Dental implants are a stable, durable, and potentially permanent way to replace one or more missing teeth. A dental implant consists of three parts: a titanium screw that is inserted into the jaw bone, an abutment that attaches to the top of the screw, and a dental restoration that attaches to the abutment. The placing of the implant screw is performed by a dental specialist who has received specific and detailed training. These specialists are often periodontists or oral surgeons.

Once your implant has been placed, you will need a dental restoration to complete your treatment and fully restore your smile. At our practice, we can create your dental implant restoration that is customized to resemble your natural teeth in color, shape, size, and functionality. From a single dental crown to a set of dentures to replace an entire dental arch, you can count on our dentist to provide you with the natural-looking, beautiful restoration that you need!

After your dental implant restoration is created and attached to your implant abutment, you can go about your life as normal. You can eat and speak without worrying about your restoration coming loose or even falling out. You can also smile with confidence, knowing that the gap in your smile is filled, and that it is filled with a replacement tooth that blends in with your smile.
