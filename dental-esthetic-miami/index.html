Dental implants in Miami are a durable and secure solution to replace one or more missing teeth. They consist of small titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to act as the tooth’s root.

Crowns, bridges, or prosthetics are then attached to these implants, replicating the function and aesthetics of natural teeth. This treatment not only enhances the appearance of your smile but also prevents bone loss and improves chewing function.
