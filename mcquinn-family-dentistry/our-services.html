Family Dental Care

At McQuinn Family Dentistry in tipton you receive the latest, safest dental treatments. You also have the added benefit of four knowledgeable and skilled dentists who will work to give you the best experience to smile about.

Our top-quality dental care services include:

Cosmetic Whitening - Tooth whitening is a safe and fast solution for improving stained teeth.

Tooth-colored Fillings - If you have cavities we can restore the look of your teeth with tooth-colored fillings.

Crowns & Bridges – A crown or bridge can be used to repair a damaged tooth and restore the size and look of a tooth or tooth space.

Implant Crowns - We can replace missing teeth with a natural-looking implant crown so you can maintain your smile.

Implant Dentures & Partials - When multiple teeth have been lost, dentures or partials may be the best option. The benefit is that you’ll improve your quality of life and have a natural smile. Implants can help provide more retention for these appliances.

Veneers - Porcelain coverings (veneers) are placed on the front of the tooth and result in a natural look.

Preventative Sealants – Sealants act as a barrier to prevent cavities and protect your teeth. It’s particularly effective on teeth with lots of grooves.

Fluoride Treatments – Fluoride has been proven for decades to prevent tooth decay. Fluoride treatments are available to children and adults.

Dental Cleaning – Routine cleanings are the best way to keep your smile looking great. Our experienced hygienists provide a gentle and kind experience.

Comprehensive Exam & Oral Cancer Screening – We often are complimented on how thorough our dental exams are for our patients. We want to make dental visits helpful and effective.

Gum Disease Care – Periodontal disease (or gum disease) requires special treatment, so we can help return your teeth and supporting tissue back to better health.

Pain Management and Emergency Treatment – Unexpected dental emergencies can happen at any time. You can rely on us to help.

Tooth Removal – Sometimes tooth removal is the best option in certain cases of disease, decay, or trauma. We take steps to ensure that you’ll be comfortable during your procedure.

Root Canal – A tooth with a deep infection can be saved with a root canal. No tooth pulling necessary.

Our trust professionals use mild, safe sedation methods if requested during treatment.
