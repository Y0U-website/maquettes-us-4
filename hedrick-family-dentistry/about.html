Dr. Aubrey F. Hedrick, III

Dr. Aubrey Hedrick is a Heritage High School graduate. He did his undergraduate degree at Wake Forest University before entering dental school. After graduating with honors from Medical College of Georgia (MCG) in 2010, he completed a one-year hospital-based Advanced Education in General Dentistry Residency.

Dr. Aubrey Hedrick is highly trained in the placement and restoration of dental implants, as well as the care of medically complex patients and patients with special needs. He was a proud recipient of several awards and accolades when he was studying at MCG.

Dr. Hedrick is a member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

When he’s not practicing, he likes to spend time with his wife and two sons. He is also an avid fisherman. Dr. Aubrey is a Rockdale County native and is proud to be serving the community in which he grew up.

Dr. Lina Tran

Dr. Lina Tran received her bachelor’s degree in biology from Kennesaw State University in 2019 and her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Dental College of Georgia in 2024.

Dr. Tran’s goal is to ensure every patient in her care feels heard and comfortable receiving the treatment they need. She loves helping people not only achieve better oral health but also be proud of their smile.

Outside the office, she loves to travel and is working towards visiting the 7 wonders of the world with her parents. She enjoys hiking and seeing the mountains when possible, as well as spending time with family and friends.

Dr. Tran is proud to work in the dental field and help the community. She strives to stay current with new methodologies and techniques through continued education and is an active member of the ADA and GDA associations.

Dr. Aliyah Hill

After earning her bachelor’s degree from Georgia Tech and her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Dental College of Georgia, Dr. Aliyah Hill has officially returned home to Conyers, GA, to begin serving the community that raised her. She looks forward to welcoming new patients and helping every smile feel confident and comfortable through comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Passionate about serving diverse populations, Dr. Hill is known for her attention to detail, strong chair-side manner, and genuine love for people. She has been recognized for her extensive community service hours, has spoken on numerous dentistry panels, and has received various scholarships for her dedication to the field and the community.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Hill enjoys traveling, discovering new restaurants, vlogging, and making memories with her family. She is honored to serve Conyers with the same dedication that shaped her journey into dentistry.

Dr. A. Fred Hedrick, Jr

Dr. Fred Hedrick is a proud graduate of Georgia Tech and the Medical College of Georgia. He founded the practice in 1978. Dr Hedrick believed Hedrick Family Dentistry should provide top-quality care with the dignity and honesty that all patients deserve.

Dentistry is not only his profession; it is his passion. Though he retired on August 16, 2022, he can still be found filling in when needed and stopping by just to say hi to patients and staff. Hedrick Family Dentistry is in his DNA, and the practice is very grateful for the direction and precedent he set.
