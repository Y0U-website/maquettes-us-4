FAQ About Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a branch of dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and correcting misaligned teeth and bite problems. Orthodontic treatment can improve your smile, oral health, and the way your teeth function.

Orthodontic treatment can correct crowded teeth, crooked teeth, gaps between teeth, overbites, underbites, crossbites, open bites, and other alignment issues. Proper treatment can improve both the appearance and function of your smile.

Children should have an orthodontic evaluation by around age 7, but orthodontic treatment can benefit patients of almost any age. Many teens and adults choose orthodontic treatment to improve their smiles and oral health.

No. Adults can benefit from orthodontic treatment just as much as children and teens. Many adults choose braces or clear aligners to correct long-standing alignment issues and achieve a healthier, more confident smile.

Orthodontic treatment can improve your bite, make your teeth easier to clean, reduce wear on your teeth, improve speech in some cases, and enhance your overall smile and confidence.

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case. Most orthodontic treatments take between 12 and 24 months, although some minor corrections can be completed more quickly.

You may experience mild soreness for a few days after braces are placed or adjusted, or when switching to a new aligner. This discomfort is temporary and typically improves within a few days.

Brush after every meal, floss daily using orthodontic flossing tools if needed, avoid sticky or hard foods if you have braces, and attend regular dental cleanings and orthodontic checkups to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Some dental insurance plans include orthodontic benefits for children and, in certain cases, adults. Our team can review your insurance coverage and discuss financing options to help make treatment more affordable.
