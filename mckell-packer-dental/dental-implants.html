FAQ About Dental Implants

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacement solutions that consist of a titanium post placed into the jawbone, an abutment, and a custom dental crown. They look, feel, and function like natural teeth while helping preserve jawbone health.

Most adults with one or more missing teeth and healthy gums are good candidates for dental implants. You’ll also need enough jawbone to support the implant, although bone grafting may be an option if additional support is needed.

Dental implants restore your ability to chew and speak comfortably, prevent neighboring teeth from shifting, preserve jawbone density, improve your smile, and provide a long-lasting solution for missing teeth.

With proper care and regular dental checkups, dental implants can last for decades and often a lifetime. The dental crown attached to the implant may eventually need replacement due to normal wear.

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure is. Local anesthesia is used during implant placement, and any mild discomfort afterward can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medication and following your dentist’s post-operative instructions.

The complete dental implant process typically takes several months because the implant needs time to fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration. Your timeline will depend on your oral health and whether additional procedures, such as bone grafting, are needed.

Care for dental implants just like your natural teeth by brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and visiting your dentist for regular cleanings and exams. Good oral hygiene helps ensure the long-term success of your implants.

Dental implants offer several advantages because they replace the tooth root, help preserve jawbone, and don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support. However, the best tooth replacement option depends on your individual needs, oral health, and budget.

Insurance coverage varies by provider and plan. Some dental insurance plans cover portions of implant treatment, while others may cover related procedures such as crowns or extractions. Our team can help review your benefits and discuss financing options.
