Meet Dr. Kesha M. Andrews

Dr. Kesha McRae Andrews

As a general dentist, I have the opportunity to care for people of all ages and form long-lasting relationships.

I am grateful for the trust that each person places in our team to meet their dental needs. We take pride in delivering patient comfort and education while administering exceptional care.

I earned my bachelor’s degree in biology from The Fort Valley State University. To pursue my advanced education, I attended Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry for my Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

After graduating from Meharry, I had the opportunity to practice dentistry in Northeast Georgia with my father, this experience allowed me to gain comprehensive knowledge and build my expertise in dentistry. I am happy to now be in the Atlanta metro area with the skill set to offer my patients the highest standard of care.

Working in healthcare, I understand that learning never stops. I believe that being a part of organized dentistry is pivotal to staying current on the newest advancements in dentistry and evolving as a dental health expert. To that end, I am a member of several professional organizations that provide opportunities for continuing education, including: American Dental Association and the Georgia Dental Association.

When I am not at the office, I enjoy spending time with my husband, our four boys, family, and close friends. I also love to bake, exercise, travel to new places; as well as participate in community outreach, various programs, and service by being a member of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.
