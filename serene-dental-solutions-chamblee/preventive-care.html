What does Preventative Dentistry Look Like for My Child?

Allowing your child to become familiar with the dentist at an early age helps make them be less hesitant to visit later in life. When you bring your child to a dentist, they can receive fluoride treatments or dental sealants that help protect their teeth and build a foundation for a lifetime of superior dental health.
