Custom Orthodontic Appliances For Comprehensive Results

Orthodontics is about more than just tooth alignment. We provide a variety of custom appliances used throughout treatment to address structural issues and ensure stable outcomes. While braces are a vital tool, devices like expanders and retainers are equally important for guiding jaw development and maintaining progress under the expert care of Dr. Wigal and Dr. Kluemper.

Hyrax Expanders

A narrow upper jaw can lead to crowding, bite misalignment, and restricted airway health. The Hyrax Expander is a specialized device that attaches to the back teeth to gradually widen the palate, creating the necessary space for proper tooth alignment and improved breathing function. This expert-led intervention ensures a healthier foundation for long-term functional balance and facial harmony.

Retainers

A custom retainer is a specialized appliance designed to stabilize your teeth following active treatment. Fabricated from a precise impression, these devices are often reinforced with thin metal wires to ensure your teeth integrate securely into their new positions. By holding the alignment steady, the retainer protects the structural integrity of your results.
