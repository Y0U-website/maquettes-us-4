The practice

Dr. Timothy Wigal and Dr. Alex Kluemper are committed to delivering the highest standard of orthodontic treatment, carefully planning every case to support proper function, long-term oral health, and natural facial esthetics. Dr. Timothy Wigal and Dr. Alex Kluemper have several decades of combined experience in orthodontics and limitless passion for straightening smiles.

My passion as an orthodontist is to create beautiful, healthy smiles that bring confidence to patients for a lifetime! I evaluate each patient individually and develop a customized treatment plan to carefully position the teeth and jaws to optimize facial beauty and harmony!

My approach is centered on meeting patients where they are and planning treatment that respects the entire smile. By focusing on function, balance, and timing, we help patients at any age achieve confident, healthy results.
