Community Roots and Life Beyond the Practice

Dr. Wigal values building lasting relationships with the families he serves and is proud to contribute to the community through mentorship, volunteer work, and professional leadership. For him, the most rewarding part of being an orthodontist is helping patients achieve functional, confident, and beautiful smiles that last a lifetime.

Whether treating children, teens, or adults, my focus is on the whole face, not just the teeth. By aligning growth, structure, and smile aesthetics, we help patients achieve balanced, lasting results they can feel good about.
