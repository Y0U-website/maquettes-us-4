What are dental Implants?

A Permanent Tooth Replacement Solution

A dental implant is a small ceramic or titanium screw secured into the jawbone which serves as the anchor or tooth root of the replacement tooth.

Both the ceramic Metal Free and Titanium Implants Are biocompatible material that fuses with the jaw bone which helps to stabilize the implant and present facial changes that can occur with bone loss.
