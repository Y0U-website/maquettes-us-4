Meet Your Keller Dentist

Dr. Shweta Sinha

After Dr. Sinha completed her bachelor’s of Dental Surgery in India, she traveled to Southern California and obtained her dental degree from Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry in 2010. During her academic years, she was recognized on the Dean’s List for academic excellence.

Dr. Sinha resides in the Fort Worth area and deeply cherishes the professional, yet inclusive environment Dallas offers. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Sinha spends her time with her family, her two adorable daughters, and she loves reading when she has time.
