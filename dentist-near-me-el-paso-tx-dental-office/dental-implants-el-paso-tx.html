Are Dental Implants the Right Treatment for You?

So, is dental implant surgery worth all the trouble? Owing to their durability, appearance, and functionality, dental implants are probably the best option for replacing missing teeth, giving you a long-term option that can last for the rest of your life with proper care.

If you’re missing a tooth or about to have a tooth or teeth extracted, then you’ve probably been considering your options for tooth replacement. Dental implants in El Paso and elsewhere in Texas and New Mexico are steadily becoming one of the most popular treatments of tooth replacement, and they’re regarded as the most natural alternative to your original teeth.

Dental implants are the only form of tooth replacement that replaces the entire tooth, including the root, and since they’re inserted into the gum during a short surgical procedure, they’re also considered permanent. Basically, a titanium post is inserted into the jaw, which bonds with the surrounding bone, and once this has happened, it’s topped with a prosthetic crown. Implants can be used for single tooth replacement, fixed multiple teeth replacement, and for removable implant-supported dentures. So, however many teeth need replacing, there are options for all of our patients.

To be a suitable candidate for dental implants in El Paso, you will need to have the following:

  • A good level of oral hygiene with healthy teeth and gums
  • Healthy bone density in the jaw in which to support the implant
  • Good overall health and not suffering from any conditions that might prevent you from having surgery
  • A non-smoker or at least has quit smoking a few weeks before treatment and during the recovery period
  • A fully developed jaw, usually over the age of 18
