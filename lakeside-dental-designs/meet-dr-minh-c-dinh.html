Meet Dr. Minh C. Dinh

Upon graduation from residency, Dr. Dinh returned to his home state and worked as an associate general dentist in private practices. Dr. Dinh loves dentistry and he is skilled in multiple facets of general, cosmetic and surgical dentistry. He enjoys making his work beautiful, natural and life-like. He believes in conservative dentistry to preserve and strengthen his patients’ teeth, bone and gums.

When he is not performing dentistry, Dr. Dinh loves to spend time with his loved ones and performing music. Music is his second passion and he is a proficient pianist and composer in classical, jazz and contemporary music. Y ou can find him playing in church with his choirs on the weekends. He often spends his vacation at his second home in Montreal, Canada where he has traveled to hundreds of times in his life.

Dr. Dinh is a compassionate and caring individual who strongly believes in excellent communication and a patient’s full and complete understanding of his proposed care. He enjoys treating patients of all ages and he looks forward to meeting and working alongside with you. He is committed to excellent care in a warm and inviting environment.
