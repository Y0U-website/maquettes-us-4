Office Tour Rio Grande Valley Children s Dentistry

Office tour

Rio Grande Valley Children’s Dentistry, P.A. opened in 2003. In 2010, our office moved about a half-mile down the road to its current location at 413 W. Sam Houston Blvd in Pharr, TX.

Our office décor is bright, colorful, and designed to calm the most anxious child. We also use the latest in dental technology to help make their experience a positive one from start to finish. To accommodate all patients, every member of our staff speaks Spanish and our patient forms are available in Spanish too!

We offer sedation dentistry and laughing gas to help prevent a negative experience, to help eliminate the fear that some patients develop as children.

All of our operatories have windows, allowing for pleasant natural light and views of nature. Children love to point out the birds, squirrels, cats, and neighborhood dogs that run by.
