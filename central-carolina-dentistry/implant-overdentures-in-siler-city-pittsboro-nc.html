WHAT ARE IMPLANT OVERDENTURES?

Implant overdentures, sometimes called snap-in dentures, are removable prosthetics that attach securely to dental implants placed in your jawbone. Instead of relying on suction or adhesives, these dentures snap into implant-based connectors for a stable, comfortable fit.

Unlike traditional dentures that only rest on the gums, overdentures are anchored by 2–6 implants, providing stronger support, better chewing power, and improved long-term bone health.

ARE IMPLANT OVERDENTURES RIGHT FOR YOU?

You may be an ideal candidate for Implant Overdentures if you:

Currently wear loose or ill-fitting dentures

Have multiple missing teeth

Have sufficient bone for implants (or are open to bone grafting)

Want improved comfort and function without the cost of a full fixed restoration

Even if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate for implants elsewhere, Dr. David Baranowski’s advanced digital imaging and planning may make treatment possible.

HOW TO CARE FOR YOUR OVERDENTURES

Remove and clean your overdentures daily with a soft brush and cleanser

Clean around your implant attachments gently to remove plaque

Visit our office for regular checkups and to replace locator inserts every 12–18 months

With good care, your implants can last a lifetime, and your overdenture will provide years of confident, comfortable wear.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT IMPLANT OVERDENTURES

How Many Implants Do I Need for an Overdenture?

Most lower overdentures use two to four implants, while upper dentures need four to six for maximum stability.

Are Implant Overdentures Permanent?

The implants are a long-term solution, and the denture itself can be removed for cleaning and maintenance. This makes them a convenient, affordable hybrid option.

Can My Existing Denture be Converted to an Implant Overdenture?

In some cases, yes. If your denture is in good condition and compatible, Dr. David may be able to retrofit it with locator attachments.

Do Implant Overdentures Hurt?

Not at all. Most patients experience minimal discomfort thanks to gentle, guided surgery and local anesthesia. We also offer IV sedation. Any post-procedure tenderness typically subsides within a few days.

How Much Do Implant Overdentures Cost?

Cost depends on the number of implants and whether you need bone grafting. Dr. David will review your options and create an affordable, transparent treatment plan tailored to your needs.
