WHAT ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS?

Dental implants are titanium posts that replace the roots of missing teeth. Once placed in your jawbone, they fuse naturally through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Each implant is restored with a custom prosthetic tooth that looks and functions like the real thing so you can eat, speak, and smile with total confidence.

At Central Carolina Dentistry , implants are part of our digitally guided restorative philosophy, meaning every case is planned precisely for comfort, function, and long-term success.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT DENTAL IMPLANTS

Are Dental Implants Painful?

No. With local anesthesia and digitally guided placement, most patients experience minimal discomfort, often less than a tooth extraction.

How Long do Implants Last?

With proper care, implants can last 25+ years, many last a lifetime.

Am I a Good Candidate for Implants?

If you have healthy gums and sufficient bone, yes. We complete a thorough review of your complete medical history. If bone loss is present, Dr. David may recommend bone grafting or ridge augmentation procedures first. Smoking is one of the largest risk factors for implant failure.

What’s the Difference between Implants and Dentures?

Implants are fixed and permanent, while dentures are removable. Implants look, feel, and function more like natural teeth and prevent bone loss.

How Much do Implants Cost?

Pricing varies by case, but Dr. David provides clear, upfront estimates and multiple financing options before treatment begins.
