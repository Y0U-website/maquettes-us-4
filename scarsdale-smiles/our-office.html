EXCEPTIONAL, CARING DENTISTRY

OUR MISSION & APPROACH

We want every patient to know that dental care does not have to be intimidating or feel like a chore. Our goal is to ensure that you enjoy your experience in our office, leaving happier and healthier than when you came. We offer judgment-free care, we give you the tools and knowledge you need to maintain optimal dental health, and we explain every treatment in a way that you will understand and appreciate. At Scarsdale Smiles we feel that the best health outcomes happen when a patient feels at ease and comfortable, and when they participate in their own dental care.

Modern Technology

Enhancing Patient Experience

Our office is equipped with state-of-the-art technology to help us deliver the highest quality care. We have digital x-rays to ensure accuracy and comfort. Our intraoral scanners offer precise images for proper diagnosis and optimal treatment. We use diode lasers to maximize comfort and speed up healing times for many treatments. Ask us about our dental technology next time you’re in our office! We would love to tell you about the tools we use to keep our patients’ smiles happy and healthy.

Comforts & Amenities

Our Office Was Designed with You in Mind

Dental care should be comfortable, so we’ve designed our office to be a space where patients feel at ease. We have blankets, pillows, magazines, water, coffee, tea, headphones, music, WiFi, and sedation options to ensure a smooth visit. Visiting for your child? Let your little one choose a present at the end of their appointment! Our team goes above and beyond to deliver dentistry that makes every patient feel comfortable, so let us know if there is anything else we can do to make your visit more enjoyable!
