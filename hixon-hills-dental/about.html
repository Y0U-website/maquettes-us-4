Where You Can Find Us

Our Convenient Location

Hixon Hills Dental is nestled on Highway 16 between La Crosse and Onalaska. With our accessible location overlooking the La Crosse River, feel free to bike to our office and enjoy a cruise with views when you swing by and visit us. We’re looking forward to seeing you!

Elevating Patient Care

Best-in-Class Technology

Stellar patient comfort is what we’re all about. We want to empower our patients with seamless, stress-free visits, so we invest in industry-leading dental tools and technology to provide the most enjoyable appointments possible. Say goodbye to messy impressions and uncomfortable conventional x-rays. Our office is equipped with state-of-the-art digital panoramic x-rays, CEREC Primescan, and CEREC Omnicam 3D imaging technology, allowing us to take a comprehensive look at your entire smile, assess your oral health with unparalleled efficiency, and craft stunning results that are made to last.
