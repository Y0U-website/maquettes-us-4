About Us

Our doctors are here to provide you the best dental service

Making smiles in Columbus, Ohio wider and brighter since 2015

We aim to educate our patients in a safe environment while offering them necessary comprehensive care. We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we created a soothing environment and a scheduling system that never keeps you waiting long. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we give our patients the best care in the area.

Dr. Seung Hyun (Steven) Kim grew up in Cincinnati, Ohio and graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from The Ohio State University. He also completed his dental education at The Ohio State University, college of Dentistry, where he received a Doctorate in Dental Surgery.

Following his graduation, Dr. Kim began working as an associate dentist for three years before opening his new office. During this time, He had advanced training in Pediatric Dentistry, Endodontics and Implant dentistry. He continues his education and keeps up-to-date with changes in dentistry by attending various continuing education courses especially in implantology, cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

Dr. Kim is currently a member of the American Dental Association, The Academy of General Dentistry, Ohio Dental Association and Columbus Dental Association. Dr. Kim is here to treat every patient with compassion, professionalism, and a gentle touch. He is committed to providing comprehensive, quality dental care while making sure patients are informed and comfortable with their treatment plan.

Outside of Dentistry, Dr. Kim loves to spend his time with his lovely family Aimee, Caleb, Jesse, and Elias. He enjoys riding a bicycle with his sons and watching buckeye football.

Dr. Diana Park is a native Buckeye who grew up in Columbus. What drew her to dentistry was a combination of artistry and science that can be used to help others. She earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University. Following graduation, she completed a year of GPR residency where she had further training in treating patients with complex medical history as well as rotations in pediatric, OR, geriatric, and special needs.

Outside of the office, Dr. Park enjoys various hobbies such as watercolor painting, embroidery, playing pickleball, cooking/baking, and planning trips to different national parks with her husband and toddler son.

Dr. Lim is a compassionate and dedicated general dentist committed to delivering top-quality dental care. With a passion for helping patients achieve their best oral health, Dr. Lim combines expert clinical skills with a warm, personalized approach.

Dr. Lim earned a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from The Ohio State University and is committed to staying at the forefront of dental technology and techniques through continuous education and training. Whether it’s a routine check-up, cosmetic procedure, or complex dental treatment, Dr. Lim ensures each patient feels informed and comfortable throughout their visit.

Dr. Lim believes in building lasting relationships with patients, understanding their needs, and creating customized treatment plans to promote lifelong oral health. In addition to providing exceptional care, Dr. Lim is known for being a great listener and takes the time to explain every step of the treatment process.

Outside the office, Dr. Lim enjoys running, playing soccer, and actively participating in community outreach programs, sharing the importance of dental hygiene and preventive care. Most importantly he is God fearing man.
