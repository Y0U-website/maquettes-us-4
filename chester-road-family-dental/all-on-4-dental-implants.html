All-on-4 Dental Implants for a Complete Smile Restoration

All-on-4 dental implants are a modern solution for replacing a full arch of missing or severely damaged teeth. This treatment uses four strategically placed dental implants in the jaw to support a full set of replacement teeth. The implants act as artificial tooth roots, providing a stable foundation that allows you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. All-on-4 implants offer a durable and natural-looking option for restoring both the function and appearance of your smile.

What are the next steps?

Healing and Integration: Over the next few months, the implants integrate with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This helps ensure the implants remain stable and long-lasting. Follow-Up Appointments: Regular follow-up visits allow our team to monitor healing and ensure your implants and prosthetic teeth are functioning properly. Maintaining Your New Smile: Proper oral hygiene and routine dental checkups are essential for keeping your implants healthy and maintaining the longevity of your restored smile.

FAQ

  • How long do All-on-4 dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental visits, All-on-4 dental implants can last many years and are designed to be a long-term tooth replacement solution.
  • How many teeth do All-on-4 dental implants have? All-on-4 implants typically support a full arch of replacement teeth, restoring the appearance and function of an entire row of teeth.
  • How long does swelling last with All-on-4 dental implants? Swelling usually lasts a few days after surgery and gradually improves as the healing process progresses.
