Emergency Dental Care

There’s nothing worse than waiting for dental pain to go away on its own. In many cases, not seeing a dental professional for the pain could leave you with worse issues.

Don’t power through the pain, call our Lockhart dentist at 512-398-3429 instead. We keep appointment slots open each day so we can see patients as soon as possible for their emergencies.

Dental Implants

Caring properly for your teeth can help them last your entire life. However, sometimes life gets in the way and you might lose one or more teeth due to trauma, decay, or periodontal disease. Don’t worry, we can help.

Dental implants are the next best thing to natural teeth.

Invisalign ®

Invisalign ® is an orthodontic treatment system that corrects bite and alignment discrepancies using a series of clear aligners instead of conventional metal brackets and arch wires. This is an ideal method of creating a beautifully aligned smile without others noticing that you’re in orthodontic treatment.

Children’s Dentistry

At Premier Dental Center Lockhart, our children’s dentist provides comfortable services to give kids great smiles while putting parents at ease.

Kids of all ages are welcome and we like to see kids as young as three for their first appointment. This first visit lets us assess dental development and start your child on the path to lifelong oral health.

Tooth Extraction

In most cases, your dentist can handle a simple tooth extraction. However, sometimes a visit to an oral surgeon is needed for special cases.
