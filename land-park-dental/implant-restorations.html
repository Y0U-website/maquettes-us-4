A healthy, confident smile is one of the best gifts you can give yourself. It can impact your ability to chew and speak properly, and even lead to bone loss in your jaw over time. What are dental implant restorations?

Dental implant restorations are a modern, lasting solution for missing teeth. A dental implant itself serves as an artificial tooth root, securely placed in the jawbone. This titanium root provides a strong and stable foundation for a custom restoration that mimics the look, feel, and function of a natural tooth. Whether you are missing one tooth, multiple teeth, or an entire arch, dental implant restorations can be tailored to your specific needs.

At Land Park Dental, the restoration part of the process is where the magic happens. Once the dental implant integrates with your jawbone, a crown, bridge, or denture is custom-made and attached to the implant, completing your smile with seamless precision. These restorations are designed to look completely natural and improve your oral health.

Why should I consider dental implant restorations?

Dental implant restorations come with a variety of benefits. They provide unmatched stability, so you never have to worry about your new teeth shifting or falling out. Unlike more traditional dentures, implants allow you to eat your favorite foods without restrictions. Dental implants also stimulate bone growth in the jaw, preventing the bone loss that occurs when teeth are missing.

Another advantage of choosing dental implant restorations is their long-lasting nature. With proper care and regular dental examinations and checkups, implant restorations can last for many years, making them a highly effective and economical choice in the long term. Though they have higher upfront costs than other replacement options, their longevity makes them a solid selection for those seeking lasting repairs.
