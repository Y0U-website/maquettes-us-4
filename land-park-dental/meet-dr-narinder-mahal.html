Meet Dr. Narinder Mahal

Dr. Narinder Mahal

Dr. Mahal received his DDS from Columbia University in 2012, achieving Excellence in Restorative Dentistry. His area of concentration in dental school was oral surgery, and he spent numerous hours learning in oral surgical clinics. Prior, he had studied at Government Dental College in Amritsar, Punjab, India. To remain current with the latest techniques and technology, he regularly advances his knowledge through continuing education courses. He is also a member of the American Dental Association and the International Dental Implant Association .

In 2015, Dr. Mahal moved to Sacramento after serving as a dentist in Miami and Boston. His goal as a dentist is to take care of his patients with the best available and up-to-date knowledge. He wants to reduce dental pain and discomfort and help patients achieve great smiles that they are proud of. Dr. Mahal believes a great smile is the best dental referral. Land Park Dental is very compassionate and caring toward our patients’ needs; Dr. Mahal and our team do everything we can to keep patients comfortable and happy in the dental chair.

Dr. Mahal is married to Raman, a lawyer by profession, and they have two handsome sons, Ekam Singh Mahal and Zoravar Singh Mahal. Dr. Mahal enjoys hiking, water sports, and soccer. He has tried sitting in front of the TV and playing Xbox, but finds that he cannot stay indoors. If talking were a hobby, it would be his favorite.
