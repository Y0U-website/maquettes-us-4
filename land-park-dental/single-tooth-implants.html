What can I expect during my dental implant treatment?

At Land Park Dental, the implant process is made easy and stress-free. We prioritize patient comfort during every step of the treatment. The procedure typically involves the following steps:

  • Consultation and Planning During your first visit, you discuss your dental goals, and our team assesses your oral health using advanced imaging technology.
  • Placement of the Implant Post A titanium post will be gently placed into your jawbone under local anesthesia, ensuring a pain-free experience.
  • Healing Period Over the next few months, the implant bonds and connects with your bone through a process known as osseointegration.
  • Custom Crown Placement Once the area has healed, a beautifully crafted crown will be attached to the post, completing the restoration.
