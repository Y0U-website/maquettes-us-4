Dental Implants Financing: No…

  • How do I apply for a payment plan? You must apply through our payment plans page. Our fast and easy application process can be done in-store with an associate, through a link provided by email from us. If you wish, you can start your application now directly on our payment options page.
  • Is there a credit check? When applying, we perform a soft credit check, which helps us determine if you are eligible. A soft credit check does not impact your credit score. Your credit score may be affected when accepting a loan to complete a purchase or opening an account. This means you will have no impact on your score, thereby making your request risk-free.
  • Does financing carry interest? A 3-month 0 % APR option is available at participating locations only. For more accurate details about your purchase, please contact us through our payment options page.
  • Is there an up front payment? Yes, you are required to make a down payment when you are finalizing your treatment plan. This down payment goes toward your total purchase amount. If you apply and are pre-approved, you’ll be able to see the exact down payment amount for your purchase. Down payments can range depending on the finance offer. For accurate numbers, visit our payment plans page.
  • What if I have bad credit? If you have bad credit, we may be able to still approve you. Thanks to our partners, you will see no credit impact when you apply for financing for your dental implants treatment.
  • How do payments work? With your authorization, your payments are automatically debited or withdrawn on the due date you are assigned when completing your purchase and will be processed with the debit card you used for your down payment or with the account and routing number you provided. You can call Sunbit Customer Support at 855-678-6248 or manage your Sunbit payments online at my.sunbit.com to update or change your payment information. If you want to make an early or additional payment, you can call us or go online as well. There is no fee for making early or additional payments.
