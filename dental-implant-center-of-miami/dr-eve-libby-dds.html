About Dr. Eve Libby

Dr. Eve Libby is a graduate of New York University College of Dentistry and completed her general practice residency at New York Presbyterian in Queens. She attended University of Maryland, College Park.

Dr. Libby was born and raised in Great Neck, NY to parents who are both physicians, so the doctor-patient relationship was what drew her to the medical field from a very young age. Dr. Libby completed her rigorous implant training under the guidance of Dr. Sam Lee in San Diego, CA, who is a Harvard-trained periodontist, prosthodontist, and orthodontist.

She is constantly learning about the newest, most modern techniques and research findings so that she can provide the best, most well-rounded and customized treatment plans for each of her patients. Dr. Libby’s shrewd eye and judgment have enlisted the trust of many patients.
