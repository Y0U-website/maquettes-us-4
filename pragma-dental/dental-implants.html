How long does it take to recover from implant surgery?

Most patients experience mild soreness and swelling for a few days after implant placement, though recovery varies. While the initial healing period is often relatively short, the implant itself usually takes several months to integrate with the jawbone before the final restoration is placed.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Dental implants should be cared for much like your natural teeth: through regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups. Maintaining healthy gums and good oral hygiene is essential for protecting the long-term success of your implants.
