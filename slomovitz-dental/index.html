Home About Highly Trained Dental Professional Dr. Steven Slomovitz is a highly skilled and dedicated dentist. He grew up in Bucks County and comes from a family of dentists, which helped shape his career path. Dr. Slomovitz completed his undergraduate studies at the University of Maryland before earning his dental degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 2012.

With a passion for continuing education, he took to broadening his skills with implant training and is an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry.

Before establishing his own practice, Dr. Slomovitz served as the dental director of a large practice in New Jersey. He is also a proud father and husband who enjoys traveling in his free time. He currently resides in Bucks County, where he continues to serve his community with high-quality dental care.
