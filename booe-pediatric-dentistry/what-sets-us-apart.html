What Sets Us Apart

  • Every staff member works hard to ensure each patient and their family feels at home, starting the minute they walk in the door. Everyone is greeted warmly and made welcome, as our front desk staff check them in and assist with paperwork. Often the children head straight to our play area while their parent is still at the counter!
  • Our clinic staff is equally as friendly, providing a supportive and child-friendly atmosphere. They are gentle and patient, and excel at helping even the most anxious little one relax and feel comfortable during procedures.
  • While children are receiving care, our front office personnel update families on the progress of the treatment and how the child is doing. This ensures parents feel comfortable and aren’t worrying while their little one is in the treatment area.
  • We provide a lot of education, using kid-friendly terms, teaching patients and parents about oral health, nutrition, and proper home dental hygiene practices.
  • By equipping patients with knowledge and skills, and providing positive experiences, we ensure our patients graduate to a general dentist with a healthy smile and feeling at home in the dental environment!
