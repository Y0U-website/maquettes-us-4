Services

Routine Check-Ups and Cleaning

Nitrous Oxide

We offer an add-on of in-office nitrous oxide to help take the edge off any dental services that may cause some anxiety or discomfort.

Whitening

The dentist will evaluate your teeth for whitening procedures, and evaluate your candidacy for two options depending on the desired goal and condition of your teeth:

1) In-office bleaching will require an appointment at our office. With this technique the whitening agent is applied to the teeth by the dentist, and you will see rapid results. To achieve the desired color, multiple visits may be required.

2) Take-Home bleaching will require more applications to achieve a similar result to an in-office session. The kit will be provided to you in our office with specific application instructions. During the course of your whitening, you may have to schedule follow up visits for a supervised and safe process.

Extractions

Whether you need extraction for orthodontic purposes, or due to another misfortunate situation, we are here to help. We do offer Nitrous Oxide to create a more relaxing experience during the extraction procedure.

Traditional Braces or Invisalign

We offer traditional braces and invisible braces or Invisalign. Invisalign is plastic trays, similar to a mouth guard, that progressively move your teeth. Depending on the complexity of your orthodontic goals, the dentist will educate you on the best option for you.

Composite or Porcelain Veneers

If you are experiencing aesthetic problems with your front teeth (due to a history of trauma or natural anomalies) the dentist will offer aesthetic restorations such as composite or porcelain veneers.

Restoration

We offer many types of aesthetic restorations including white fillings, crowns, and bridges for anterior or posterior teeth. No more of a metallic look in your mouth!

Dentures

We offer partial and full dentures. If you are missing teeth, the dentist can evaluate the replacement of the missing teeth with a denture, and help you compare to other options.

Periodontal Treatment

Pediatric Dentistry

We still care for children and adolescents up to age 18 in our neighboring practice, Pediatric Dental Care. If your teenager is ready to graduate to our Family Dental Care practice prior to the age of 18, we are happy to evaluate an earlier transition on a case by case basis.

We know the dentist can be a stressful situation for little ones. Our board certified pediatric dentists provide a safe, comfortable environment for kids of all ages. They take their time with each patient, explaining all of our tools and processes to alleviate any fear! If needed, we also offer conscious in-office sedation, which can further assist in your child having a comfortable visit.

Sleep Medicine

We provide sleep medicine care for individuals who suffer from disturbed breathing during sleep.

Botox and Fillers

Botox is a safe and effective treatment for the reduction of lines in the face. It works by relaxing the muscles. Treatments last on average three to four months. Many of our patients report that Botox in the TMJ helps with clenching, grinding, and other TMJ related issues like headaches.

Fillers rejuvenate the face by restoring volume that has been lost with age. Fillers can also enhance or gently sculpt parts of the face that you’d like to change. We use fillers to refresh your face, so you still look like you-- just slightly better!
