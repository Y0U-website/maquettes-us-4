Bone grafting is a specialized surgical procedure that involves adding bone or a bone-like material to a deficient area of the jawbone. It is commonly performed in dentistry and oral surgery to restore and augment the bone structure in preparation for dental implant placement or to support natural teeth.

All-on-4 implants, also known as Teeth in a Day, is an innovative dental implant solution that provides a full arch of teeth with just four strategically placed implants. This procedure is designed for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth and desire a fixed and permanent tooth replacement option.
