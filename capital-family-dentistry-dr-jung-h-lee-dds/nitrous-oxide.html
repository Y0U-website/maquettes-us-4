Nitrous oxide

The best part about using Laughing gas is that it doesn’t stay in your system like more potent sedatives do. You will feel all normal right after the mask is removed. You don’t require a companion to attend your appointment, and there are no aftereffects or drowsiness.

Just like its name, Laughing Gas is best for making you feel calm and a little more cheerful while we take care of your smile.

  • Helps reduce anxiety and nervousness
  • Keeps you awake but deeply relaxed
  • No memory loss you’re still fully aware
  • Wears off almost immediately after treatment
  • Safe for both kids and adults
  • You can drive yourself home afterward
