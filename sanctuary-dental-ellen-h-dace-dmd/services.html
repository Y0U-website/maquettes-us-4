Our Services

Diagnostic & Preventative Care

Routine exams, digital imaging, and professional cleanings form the foundation of long-term oral health. We take a comprehensive, proactive approach to prevention, focusing on early detection and minimally invasive care. When appropriate, we offer treatments such as Curodont Repair Fluoride Plus to help restore early enamel changes without the need for traditional drilling.

Restorative & Cosmetic Dentistry

Whether repairing damage or enhancing your smile, our approach blends function and aesthetics to create results that are both durable and natural-looking. For areas affected by minor decay or damage, we offer tooth-colored fillings that seamlessly blend with your natural tooth structure. When a tooth is more significantly weakened, a custom crown may be recommended to restore strength, protect the tooth, and improve appearance.

For patients looking to improve the overall look of their smile, common treatment options include veneers, composite bonding, and crowns. Each option is selected based on your individual needs and goals, whether you are looking to refine shape, improve color, or restore worn or damaged teeth.

Cosmetic treatments are always thoughtfully planned and tailored to each patient. Whether subtle or more transformative, our goal is to enhance your smile in a way that looks balanced, natural, and in harmony with your features.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth and are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. An implant is made up of multiple components, including a titanium post that is designed to integrate with the jawbone, along with a custom restoration that completes the smile.

After the implant is placed, the body undergoes a healing process over several months, allowing the implant to fuse with the bone and create a stable, lasting foundation. We work closely with highly skilled specialists for the surgical phase of treatment, then complete the final restoration in-office once healing is complete. This collaborative approach ensures precision, longevity, and a seamless, natural result.

Clear Aligner Therapy

Clear aligner therapy offers a comfortable, discreet way to straighten your teeth and improve your smile without traditional braces. At Sanctuary Dental, we use the Orthobrain system to help guide treatment planning and provide efficient, personalized care. Clear aligners can address concerns such as crowding, spacing, and bite alignment while fitting seamlessly into your everyday life. Successful treatment depends on wearing your aligners as directed and maintaining excellent oral hygiene throughout the process.

Whitening

Professional whitening is a safe and effective way to brighten your smile, and we offer several options to fit your preferences and lifestyle using the Opalescence Whitening System.

For convenience, Opalescence Go pre-filled trays provide an easy, ready-to-use option with noticeable results. For a more customized approach, we offer custom-fitted whitening trays paired with professional-strength carbamide peroxide gel. These trays are designed specifically for your smile, allowing for even whitening, reduced sensitivity, and the ability to maintain your results over time.

We also offer in-office whitening for patients looking for faster, more immediate results in a single visit. During this treatment, a high-concentration whitening gel is carefully applied under supervision for safe and effective brightening.

Periodontal Therapy

Gum disease, also known as periodontal disease, is a common condition caused by bacteria and plaque buildup around the teeth and beneath the gumline. It often begins as gingivitis, which may present as red, swollen, or bleeding gums. Without treatment, it can progress to a more advanced stage where the supporting bone around the teeth is affected, potentially leading to gum recession, loose teeth, and even tooth loss.

Periodontal therapy focuses on gently removing bacteria and buildup from below the gumline, allowing the gums to heal and reducing inflammation. Treatment is tailored to each patient’s needs and may involve deeper cleanings and ongoing maintenance to keep the condition stable.

With early detection and consistent care, gum disease can often be effectively managed, helping to preserve both your oral health and overall well-being.

Pediatric Dentistry

We welcome children and strive to create a positive, comfortable experience from the very beginning. Early dental visits play an important role in building familiarity and confidence, helping children feel at ease in a dental setting as they grow.

Our approach emphasizes prevention, education, and gentle, attentive care. We take the time to guide both children and parents on proper oral hygiene habits, diet, and development, supporting healthy smiles at every stage.

By creating a calm, supportive environment and focusing on positive experiences, our goal is to help children build lifelong habits and a lasting sense of comfort with dental care. When a child would benefit from more specialized care, we work closely with trusted pediatric specialists to ensure they receive the most appropriate treatment.
