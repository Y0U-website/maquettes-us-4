Meet Dr. Dace

Dr. Ellen Dace is dedicated to providing high-quality, modern dentistry in a calm and inviting environment. She believes every patient deserves personalized care and is committed to creating an experience that feels comfortable, unhurried, and thoughtfully tailored to each individual.

Dr. Dace earned her undergraduate degree in biology from University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign before continuing her education at Midwestern University, where she completed a Master’s degree in Biomedical Sciences. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine–Illinois, where she developed a strong foundation in comprehensive, patient-centered care with an emphasis on modern techniques and attention to detail.

Outside of the office, Dr. Dace enjoys spending time with her husband and their two cats, Lottie and Minnie. She enjoys reading, baking, and cooking, and appreciates the balance that these creative outlets bring to her life.

Our Philosophy

At Sanctuary Dental, we believe exceptional dentistry is built on more than clinical skill. It is grounded in trust, attention to detail, and a deep respect for each patient’s individual needs.

We prioritize transparency, taking the time to clearly explain findings, recommendations, and options so patients can make confident, informed decisions about their care. We honor patient autonomy and believe that treatment should always align with personal goals, preferences, and comfort.

Our approach to care is comprehensive and intentional. Rather than focusing on isolated concerns, we consider the full picture to support long-term oral health, function, and aesthetics. Every recommendation is made with longevity and quality in mind.

We have thoughtfully created an environment that feels gentle, relaxed, and welcoming. We understand that dental visits can be stressful, and we strive to make each experience as comfortable and reassuring as possible.

Attention to detail is at the core of everything we do, from clinical care to the overall patient experience. We respect our patients’ time by maintaining an efficient, well-organized schedule while still allowing the time needed to provide attentive, personalized care.

Above all, we value relationships. We believe in building lasting connections with our patients, creating a space where they feel comfortable, supported, and confident in returning for their care.
