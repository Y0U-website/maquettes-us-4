Dr. Alisha Starbird, DMD

Dr. Alisha Starbird is proud to care for patients in Biddeford and the surrounding Southern Maine communities. She earned both her undergraduate and dental degrees from the University of New England and completed a one-year oral and maxillofacial internship at Tufts University, where she expanded her clinical skills and deepened her commitment to providing excellent surgical and restorative care.

Dr. Starbird is known for her friendly, down-to-earth approach and her dedication to helping patients feel confident in their smiles again. Whether she’s guiding someone through full-arch implants or helping restore daily comfort with dentures, she takes time to understand each patient’s needs and create a plan that truly fits.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Starbird loves exploring the Portland area with her wife, Allie, and their two dogs, Callie and Poppy. She enjoys relaxing at the beach, trying new local restaurants and coffee shops, and getting a good workout in at F45. At home, you’ll often find her working on landscaping projects or DIY upgrades around the house. She also loves spending time with friends—and now that her parents have moved into the neighborhood, they’re officially neighbors, too!
