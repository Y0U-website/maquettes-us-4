Not sure if it’s an emergency? Call us we’ll help you decide what to do next.

  • Swelling in the gums, face, or jaw
  • A cracked, broken, or chipped tooth
  • A lost filling or crown causing pain or sensitivity
  • Signs of infection (swelling, pus, fever, bad taste)
  • Injury or trauma to the mouth or teeth

What to Expect at Your Emergency Appointment

Our goal is to relieve pain, treat the cause, and help you avoid bigger problems later.

  • Listen to your symptoms and review your dental history
  • Perform a focused exam of the painful area
  • Take the appropriate X-ray (periapical or bitewing) if needed
  • Explain what’s causing the issue and your treatment options
  • Help you plan the best next step whether it’s same-day treatment or a follow-up
