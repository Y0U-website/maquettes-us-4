Meet Our Dentists

Serving Somerville, MA with exceptional family and cosmetic dental care.

At Max Dental Somerville, we’re proud to have a team of highly skilled and compassionate dentists dedicated to providing the best possible care for every patient. Our doctors combine advanced clinical training with a gentle, personalized approach that puts your comfort first. Whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning, cosmetic enhancement, or restorative treatment, you can trust our experienced team to help you achieve a healthy, confident smile.

Dr. Sharanjot Mann, D.M.D. With more than twenty years of experience, Dr. Sharanjot Mann, D.M.D. is a trusted Somerville dentist known for her compassionate care and commitment to excellence. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (D.M.D.) degree with high honors from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine. Before dental school, she completed a Master of Public Health (MPH) in Medical Epidemiology at Loma Linda University in Southern California, graduating Magna Cum Laude, and earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery from Government Dental College, Patiala in 1998.

Dr. Mann stays at the forefront of modern dentistry through ongoing continuing education and advanced training. She has completed professional courses at the Spear Institute in Arizona for dental implant restoration and root canal therapy, received nitrous oxide administration certification from Boston University, and trained in Invisalign and Pro-Monitoring aligner systems. Her background in epidemiology allows her to take a research-based approach to understanding the causes and prevention of oral diseases helping patients achieve healthier, long-lasting smiles.

Known for her warm, friendly personality and great sense of humor, Dr. Mann helps even the most anxious patients feel relaxed and comfortable. She enjoys working with patients of all ages, from young children to adults, and has a special talent for connecting with kids making every dental visit a positive experience.

Outside the office, Dr. Mann loves spending time outdoors with her family and their dog, Max. A devoted dog lover, she’s always happy to share a laugh (and a story or two) about her furry friends with her patients.
