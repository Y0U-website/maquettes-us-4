Mission

Rooted Dental delivers the highest level of dental care with expert insight and consideration for our patients.

Vision

Our office utilizes the most advanced technology and by creating awareness around the importance of oral health, we will provide uncompromising dental care with a focus on reducing the anxiety associated with dentistry through prevention and education.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Imtiaz is well-traveled and loves to continue to travel and learn about different cultures. During her downtime, she loves to run, explore Chicago on foot, and play video games.
