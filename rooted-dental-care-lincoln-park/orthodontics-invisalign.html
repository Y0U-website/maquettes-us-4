Benefits of Teeth Clear Aligners Over Traditional Braces

Choosing transparent teeth aligners over conventional braces offers significant lifestyle advantages:

  • Nearly Invisible: Invisalign aligners are made from smooth, BPA-free clear plastic; most people won’t even notice you’re wearing them.
  • Removable Design: Unlike fixed braces, you can remove your aligners from eating, drinking, and maintaining your oral hygiene routine without restriction.
  • Comfortable Fit: No metal wires or brackets means no irritation to your cheeks and gums. Each set of transparent aligners is precisely crafted for a smooth, snug fit.
  • Better Oral Hygiene: Because your aligners are removable, brushing and flossing remain as easy as ever. This reduces the risk of cavities and gum disease during treatment.
  • Predictable Results: The iTero scanner and Invisalign software let you visualize your final smile before treatment even starts.
  • Dual Function: Thin, clear aligner trays can also act as a nightguard for patients who grind or clench their teeth while sleeping.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign treatment plans at Rooted Dental Care take between 6 and 8 months. More complex cases may require up to 18 months. Your treatment timeline depends on the severity of your alignment issues and how consistently you wear your aligners.

Are transparent aligners as effective as traditional braces?

Yes. Invisalign clear aligners are clinically proven to correct a wide range of orthodontic issues, including crowding, spacing, overbites, and rotated teeth. For most mild to moderate cases, they deliver results comparable to traditional braces.

How many hours a day do I need to wear my invisible aligners?

For best results, you should wear your transparent teeth aligners 20 to 22 hours per day. Remove them only when eating, drinking (anything other than water), brushing, or flossing. Consistent wear is key to staying on schedule.

Will Invisalign aligners affect my speech?

Some patients notice a slight adjustment period during the first few days of wearing new aligners. Most people adapt quickly, and any minor lisp or speech change typically resolves within a week as your tongue adjusts to the clear aligners.

Can I eat and drink with my Invisalign aligners?

No, you should always remove your transparent aligners before eating or drinking anything other than plain water. This prevents staining and damage to your aligners and keeps your treatment timeline on track.
