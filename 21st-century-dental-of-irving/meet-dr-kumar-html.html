Where Did You Study Dentistry?

Dr. Kumar attended the University of Texas at Dallas for his undergraduate studies. He later went on to study at Louisiana State University to obtain his Doctor of Dental Science degree. He’s also undertaken advanced dental courses in various topics, including level 1 sedation dentistry, dental implant pathway, All of 4 Cadaver, sinus lift, dental emergencies, and dental XP.

Other than expanding his expertise and skills through continuing education, Dr. Kumar is also a proud and active member of professional organizations such as the:

  • American Dental Association
  • Texas Dental Association
  • Academy of General Dentistry (AGD)
  • Pride Institute

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

If you don’t find him treating pearly whites at the dental office, Dr. Kumar is spending much of his time creating amazing memories with his family and loved ones. He loves being with his partner, Angela, and their two dogs, Nola and Bunty. He also considers himself an adventure seeker and enjoys traveling as often as he can. His hobbies include hiking mountains, going to the beach, and checking out new coffee shops and restaurants. One interesting thing about him is that he enjoys woodworking and building furniture from scratch!
