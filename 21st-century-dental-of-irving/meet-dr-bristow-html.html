Giving Back

One of Dr. Bristow’s passions is volunteering her time and skills to help others. She has been on mission trips to Mexico and Guatemala, donating no-cost dental treatment to local communities in need. She has also participated in multiple Texas Mission of Mercy events, which provide free dental care to our veterans.

She spent time donating dental services through North Dallas Shared Ministries, which provides free dental care to residents in Dallas. After only a year, the program had grown into a five-day-per-week training course for dental students that could serve a much larger population of the community.

In 2012, she led the fundraiser for 21st Century Dental of Irving to raise a donation for the Special Olympics Texas and celebrated with the organization by rappelling down the side of a 15-story building.

Dr. Bristow volunteers with Dallas CASA and is an advocate for kids who are in foster care. Her role is to meet with the kids and work with CPS to help determine the best living situation for them and advocate to reunite families.

She is also a frequent volunteer with the Crisis Ministries food pantry in Irving.

Dr. Bristow is a known animal lover, with a special place in her heart for golden retrievers. In the past, she has volunteered as a foster with the Golden Retriever Rescue of North Texas. She now volunteers with the SPCA of Texas, advocating for adoption at local events and being of service at the Dallas SPCA location with day-to-day animal care.

Life Away from the Dental Office

Dr. Bristow enjoys spending time with her family, exercising (barre or yoga, anyone?), traveling, and volunteering. She loves to cook and is always excited to swap recipes and discuss healthy lifestyle choices in and out of the kitchen.

She lives in Colleyville with her husband, Michael, three-legged golden retriever, Blaze, and 1 eyed mixed breed lab, Pongo. Be ready, you’re likely to be asked to see photos of your fur babies when you’re in her chair.
