DENTAL IMPLANTS IN EXETER

With dental implants, we have an effective and permanent method of replacing the teeth our patients have lost. We can provide this entire procedure, including placements of both the titanium implant and the ceramic crown, in our office.

This procedure starts with the placement of the implant a slender titanium post. It is placed in the location of the missing tooth or in a strong area of bone so it can support a bridge or denture. Once it has bonded well with the bone, then an abutment, a connection between the implant and the crown to be placed later, is added. Finally, the crown is secured to the abutment.

Please talk to us if you are missing teeth to learn how dental implants can provide a highly satisfactory solution.

Implant Bridges and Dentures

When multiple teeth or an entire arch have been lost, implants provide a secure and permanent foundation. In some cases, dental implants can be placed and complete dentures can be attached in a single day. This is called All-on-4 because four implants are used to support the dentures.
