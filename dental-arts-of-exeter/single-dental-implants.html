SINGLE DENTAL IMPLANTS IN EXETER

Dental implants have revolutionized the science of tooth replacement in dentistry. A dental implant provides a replacement tooth that looks and feels natural. With our care for each patient’s appearance, we make sure each white ceramic crown placed matches our patient’s natural teeth for a perfect match.

With dental implants replacing any teeth you have lost, you can finally forget that you ever lost any teeth. Your new dental implants will be durable and can last for many years with the right maintenance care.

The Dental Implant Procedure

The first step is placing an implant a slender titanium post in the location of the lost tooth. The implant is given time to bond with the bone in the jaw. Then a connecting point called an abutment is added to the implant. Finally, the clean, ceramic crown is attached to the abutment.

At Dental Arts of Exeter, we can offer this entire procedure in our office so our patients do not need to be referred anywhere else.
