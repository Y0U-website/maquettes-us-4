Full Mouth Reconstruction Options

Fixed Implant-Supported

Most predictable and comfortable option. Provides maximum stability and function closest to natural teeth.

  • Individual implant crowns (optimal)
  • Multiple implant-supported bridges
  • Full arch fixed restorations
  • All-on- X solutions
  • Hybrid prostheses

Removable Implant-Supported

More affordable option with significantly improved retention and stability over conventional dentures.

  • Implant-supported overdentures
  • Bar-retained full dentures
  • Locator-retained prostheses
  • Magnetic retention systems
  • Precision attachment dentures
  • Combination fixed-removable

Conventional Fixed

Preserves existing tooth structure when sufficient healthy teeth remain. May require future maintenance.

  • Full mouth crown and bridge
  • Porcelain veneers with crowns
  • Extensive bridgework
  • Root canal and crown therapy
  • Periodontal regeneration
  • Orthodontic alignment first
