About

Following dental school, Dr. Miller completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at SUNY Buffalo, where he distinguished himself by setting the program record for the number of dental implants placed—demonstrating both technical excellence and an early commitment to advanced restorative care. He also completed formal training in Moderate Conscious IV Sedation through Augusta University, allowing him to provide a higher level of comfort and anxiety management for his patients.

Dr. Miller went on to own and operate a successful private practice in Charlotte, North Carolina, while continuing to expand his clinical expertise. He completed advanced restorative, cosmetic, and functional training at the prestigious Kois Center in Seattle, Washington, and earned his Fellowship in the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD)—an achievement attained by fewer than 6 % of general dentists nationwidethrough extensive continuing education and demonstrated clinical excellence.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Miller enjoys an active, outdoor lifestyle. His hobbies include hunting, fishing, SCUBA diving for megalodon teeth, backpacking through America’s National Parks, golfing, traveling, and photography. Many of the photographs displayed throughout the practice were taken by Dr. Miller during his travels and adventures in National Parks across the country—reflecting his appreciation for detail, craftsmanship, and natural beauty.

Dr. Miller brings the same dedication, precision, and enthusiasm from his personal pursuits into his dental practice, striving to provide care that is both technically exceptional and genuinely compassionate. For Dr. Miller, True Course Dentistry is more than just a dream realized, it’s a compass guiding patients toward lifelong oral wellness.

First Visit

A starting point built around understanding and trust

At your first visit, we don’t just check your teeth, we take the time to get to know you. Starting with a comprehensive exam and dental photography, Dr. Miller walks you through your entire mouth, tooth by tooth, so you leave with a clear understanding of your dental health. Expect a comfortable, personalized experience in a modern, friendly setting.

Patient Experience We believe that communication is everything. From the moment you walk in, you’ll be greeted with genuine hospitality and thoughtful amenities designed to make you feel at ease. During your appointment, we prioritize education and transparency, using visual aids and one-on-one discussion to explain everything clearly and compassionately.

At True Course Dentistry, we’ve thoughtfully designed every detail of our practice with your comfort and confidence in mind. From the latest clinical technology to creature comforts like warm towels and large-screen TVs, your visit is both high-tech and human-centered.

Serving our community

Dedicated to making a difference
