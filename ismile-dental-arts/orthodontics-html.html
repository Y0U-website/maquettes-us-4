Benefits of Straight Teeth

  • Reduced risk for dental damage that can occur when smile misalignment places excessive pressure on specific teeth, which can lead to chips, cracks, and excessive dental wear.
  • Relieved pressure on the temporomandibular joints (TMJ). When these small connective joints are strained, the resultant TMJ dysfunction can lead to jaw movement being difficult or painful, and TMJ dysfunction can even cause complete immobility or lockjaw.
  • Improved dental health due to easier to clean, properly aligned teeth. Most patients experience significantly fewer cavities following orthodontic treatment, especially those who struggled with crowded and/or overlapping teeth.
  • Renewed confidence to speak and smile in every personal and professional situation, showing off your beautiful grin.

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Invisalign is an excellent orthodontic treatment for patients who want to address alignment problems in their smiles. That said, Invisalign is not the perfect treatment for everyone. For example, it may not be a good option for kids and younger teenagers since they sometimes struggle to remember to wear their trays as often as needed. Ultimately, whether or not Invisalign is ideal will be a decision that you and Dr. Wael M. Kassem will make. In the meantime, you can read on to learn a few of the orthodontic problems clear aligners can correct.

Crowded Teeth

Not only can crowded teeth negatively impact your self-esteem, but they can also harbor food particles, plaque, and other debris. As a result, you may have a higher risk of problems like gum disease and tooth decay. There is some good news, though: traditional braces are no longer the only solution. In fact, Invisalign can correct mild to moderate cases, making it easier for patients to smile with confidence and keep their smiles clean and healthy.

Gaps Between Teeth

You might feel self-conscious about noticeable gaps between your teeth, but they’re more than just an aesthetic issue. Bits of food can get trapped in these spaces, attracting bacteria that cause bad breath, cavities, and more. For all of these reasons, we don’t recommend taking the wait and see approach. Instead, come to our office to explore your treatment options, like Invisalign. If we determine that you’re a candidate, then we can use clean, comfortable aligners to evenly space out your teeth.

Overbite

Wondering if Invisalign can fix overbites? The answer is yes! The easiest way to find out if you’re a good candidate for Invisalign is by scheduling a consultation with us. If your case isn’t too severe, then we can create a custom series of clear trays to align your teeth and your bite. Oftentimes, patients achieve the results they desire in just 12-18 months, too!

Underbite

Even mild underbites can place stress on your jaw joint, increasing the risk of chronic teeth grinding and jaw clenching. Eventually, this can wear down your tooth enamel, leaving you susceptible to sensitivity or even breakage. Don’t get discouraged, though Invisalign can fix mild to moderate cases without noticeable brackets and uncomfortable wires!

Crossbite

If the entire jaw needs to be moved, then traditional braces, orthodontic headgear, or other procedures may be necessary to correct your crossbite. If that’s not the case, then Invisalign may be an option! Usually, additional orthodontic accessories are still needed, like rubber bands, but they are still considerably more comfortable and discreet.

Open Bite

If your upper and lower teeth don’t meet together evenly due to a considerable space, you likely have an open bite. Since this bite problem, like the others on this list, can negatively impact everything from your oral health to your chewing patterns, we strongly recommend exploring your treatment options, like Invisalign.

Invisalign vs. Traditional Braces

No treatment is right for everyone, and many patients can still benefit from traditional orthodontic treatments. However, as you’re considering moving forward with Invisalign treatment, you should keep in mind the following advantages of Invisalign clear braces compared with bracket-and-wire orthodontic options:

  • Speed Patients will usually complete the entire treatment process in eight to 18 months. The average treatment is just 12 months. Most traditional braces treatment plans take 18 to 36 months. Additionally, Invisalign clear braces require fewer checkups throughout treatment than traditional braces, and the less-frequent appointments in our dental office are also shorter than bracket-and-wire adjustments. That makes Invisalign a great option for patients with busy schedules.
  • Cosmetic appearance Throughout your orthodontic treatment, you’ll look great. The aligners are completely clear, so all anyone will notice is your smile. You can also remove your aligners for short periods of time if you want to ensure a flawless look.
  • Allergies Many patients have metal allergies or sensitivities that make metal bracket-and-wire orthodontic treatment uncomfortable and even dangerous to health. Plastic alignment trays remove this potential concern completely.
  • Convenience Because you can simply remove your aligners during meals and while brushing and flossing, there’s no need to change your diet or oral hygiene routine during Invisalign orthodontics.

After Invisalign Treatment

In addition to using retainers to maintain your results, we’ll partner with you to ensure you’re caring for your smile properly at home and using the right oral hygiene products for your unique needs. During your six-month dental checkups, we’ll help you with all of your oral health care needs to keep your properly aligned smile looking great and functioning perfectly. Please don’t hesitate to call us if you have any concerns following your Invisalign treatment.

Living with Invisalign Aligners

Adjusting to Invisalign at iSmile Dental Arts is often easier than most patients expect. The trays are smooth, nearly invisible, and custom-fitted to your teeth. This means they’ll fade into the background quickly as you go about your daily routine. While there may be a short adjustment period, most people forget they’re even wearing their aligners after a week or two. With just a little care and consistency, you’ll be able to smile confidently and continue your normal lifestyle throughout treatment.

Wearing Your Trays

For the best results, Invisalign trays should be worn 20 22 hours a day. You’ll only take them out when eating, drinking anything besides water, or brushing your teeth.

At first, you might notice a slight lisp or a tight feeling in your teeth, but this usually disappears within a few days. Most patients take to the routine of taking their aligners out and putting them back in quickly, making Invisalign one of the most convenient orthodontic treatments available.

Cleaning Your Aligners

Keeping your trays clean is simple but important. Rinse them each time you remove them, and brush them gently with a soft toothbrush and clear, unscented soap. Avoid toothpaste, as it can scratch the plastic, making it easier for stains and bacteria to stick around.

For a deeper clean, consider using Invisalign cleaning crystals at least once every couple of days. A consistent cleaning routine prevents bacteria and plaque buildup, discoloration, and odors, keeping your aligners clear and your smile looking fresh.

Eating & Drinking

One of the best perks of Invisalign is flexibility with food. You’ll be able to eat anything you want as long as you remove your trays beforehand. Enjoy popcorn, crunchy apples, or your favorite pizza without a care.

Just be sure to brush or at least rinse your trays before snapping them back in to avoid trapping food particles. Drinking water is fine while wearing Invisalign, but sugary or hot drinks can stain or warp the aligners, so steer clear.

Losing or Damaging a Tray

Accidents happen and we have a plan in place for when things go wrong. So, don’t panic if you misplace or crack an aligner, just call our Williamstown office right away. Dr. Kassem will advise whether to move ahead to your next set or order a replacement. In the meantime, you might be able to wear your previous trays to prevent your teeth from shifting back. Taking action quickly keeps your treatment on track and avoids delays.

Routine Check-Ins

Invisalign doesn’t require as many visits as traditional braces, but you’ll still see Dr. Kassem every 6 to 8 weeks. These short appointments allow him to check your progress, answer questions, and provide your next set of aligners. Regular check-ins ensure your teeth are shifting correctly and help you stay motivated as you move closer to your finished smile.

Understanding the Cost of Invisalign

Planning an Invisalign treatment takes a lot of thought and care. One aspect that will definitely require careful consideration is the final cost of the treatment. There are a number of variables that can affect the amount you’ll end up paying for your clear aligners in the end, and we’ll only be able to provide a reliable estimate once we’ve carefully considered all of these factors. Use the short guide below to learn more about the basics of paying for Invisalign.

Factors that Affect the Cost of Invisalign

Invisalign is a process that involves multiple clear aligners. So naturally, the final cost will depend on how many aligners you need. In order to figure out that number, we’ll first need to determine:

  • How complex the treatment is . A single gap between the front teeth will be easier to correct than severe crowding.
  • How long the treatment will take . Longer Invisalign treatments require more aligners, which naturally means they cost more in the end.
  • Whether there are any complications during the procedure . If you don’t wear your aligners for the requisite amount of time each day, the treatment will take longer than expected, and the price will go up as a consequence.

Does Dental Insurance Cover Invisalign?

It depends on which insurance carrier you have. Some plans will include Invisalign with their orthodontic coverage while others will exclude it, and some won’t pay for orthodontic treatment at all. You should call your dental insurance company to see what their policy on Invisalign is. Our team can help explain anything that you don’t understand and file the necessary paperwork for you. An FSA account can also be used to cover the cost.
