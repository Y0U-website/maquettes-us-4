Implants

  • Preserve jawbone and prevent facial sagging
  • No slipping, clicking, or adhesives
  • Eat and speak with confidence
  • Long-lasting and easy to maintain

Our In-House Implant Process

We make the implant journey as smooth as possible by keeping everything in one place:

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging A comprehensive exam, digital scan, and CBCT imaging help us evaluate your bone structure and customize your treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement Our board-certified Oral Surgeon expertly places your implant with precision and care, using guided surgery techniques when needed.
  • Healing Phase Over the next few months, the implant integrates with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration.
  • Final Restoration Our restorative dentist places a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture on your implant for a natural and durable finish.

Same-Day and Full-Arch Implants

Looking for faster results or a full smile makeover?

  • Immediate Implants: In select cases, we can place an implant on the same day as extraction.
  • All-on-4 / Full Arch Implants: Restore an entire arch with just 4–6 implants and a custom fixed bridge.

Are Implants Right for You?

Most healthy adults are candidates for implants. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your gums, bone density, and overall health to determine the best approach. We’ll also discuss financing options, insurance coverage, and timeline.
