Restore Your Smile Permanently with Dental Implants

Losing a tooth or multiple teeth can be a traumatic experience. Luckily, with advancements in dental technology in the past few decades, replacing a missing tooth can be a good experience with our highly trained dentist and clinical staff at Pershing Family Dental. Dental implants can permanently restore the look and feel of your natural teeth in a comfortable El Paso, TX dental office.

Beauty & Benefits of Dental Implants

Along with restoring your smile and confidence to smile, dental implants also restore the ability to eat in comfort. While some patients may be able to get dental implants in one or two visits, more severe cases may take a few months to fully restore your smile.

Because dental implants are made of titanium (a bio-compatible material), they bond firmly to the jaw bone. This provides a sturdy foundation for a crown, bridge, or dentures to be placed on top of. While dental implants are more expensive than other treatment options, it is the most permanent solution to replacing missing teeth. With a 97 % success rate, dental implants provide the best solution for replacing missing teeth. With proper maintenance and care, your dental implants can last you for the rest of your life.

Dental Implant Maintenance

Dental implants provide you with a second chance to take care of your teeth. Since they are literally replacements for your former teeth, they have the same maintenance routine and schedule. If you brush and floss routinely every day along with seeing the dentist twice a year, your dental implants should last you a lifetime.
