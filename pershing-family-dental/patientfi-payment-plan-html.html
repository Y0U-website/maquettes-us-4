Pershing Family Dental: PatientFi Financing

At Pershing Family Dental, we understand that dental treatment can be expensive, which is why we offer our patients a range of options to help them finance their dental care. One such option is PatientFi Financing, a revolutionary platform that provides flexible payment options and affordable rates to make dental treatment accessible to everyone.

What is PatientFi Financing?

PatientFi Financing is a financing platform that allows patients to access affordable dental treatment by providing flexible payment options, low-interest rates, and easy online access. With PatientFi Financing, you can get the dental treatment you need and spread the cost of your care across multiple months, making it easier to manage your finances.

What are the benefits of using PatientFi Financing?

There are several benefits to using PatientFi Financing for your dental treatment:

Simple application process

The application process for PatientFi Financing is straightforward and can be completed in just a few minutes. With a few basic pieces of information, you can quickly receive a decision on your financing.

Flexible payment options

PatientFi Financing offers multiple payment options, allowing you to choose the plan that best fits your budget. Whether you need to spread out payments over several months or want to pay off your balance early, PatientFi Financing can accommodate your needs.

Low-interest rates

PatientFi Financing offers competitive interest rates, so you can rest assured that you’re getting a good deal on your financing.

No prepayment penalties

PatientFi Financing allows you to pay off your balance early without any penalty fees. This means you can save money on interest charges by paying off your balance sooner.

How does PatientFi Financing work?

Using PatientFi Financing is simple. After your consultation, our team can help you navigate the PatientFi Financing application process. Once your application is approved, PatientFi Financing will pay for your dental treatment up front. You will then repay the financing over a period of time according to the payment plan you chose.

What payment plans are available with PatientFi Financing?

PatientFi Financing offers a range of payment plans to suit your needs. Some of the most popular payment plans include:

6-month no-interest plan

This plan allows you to spread the cost of your dental care across six months with no interest charged.

12-month extended plan

This plan provides more time to pay off your balance, with payments spread out over 12 months.

24- to 60-month fixed-rate plans

For larger balances or more extended treatment plans, PatientFi Financing offers fixed-rate plans that allow you to spread out payments over two to five years.
