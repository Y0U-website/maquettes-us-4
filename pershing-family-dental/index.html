Dental implants are made from titanium, which is a bio-compatible material that bonds to the jaw bone. This bond strengthens the jaw by stimulating bone growth and prevents recession of the jawline which occurs from missing teeth. Dental implants also have a 98 % success rate and can last a lifetime with proper maintenance.

Missing teeth can cause your cheeks to look sunken in. Dental implants repair your natural bone structure and prevent you from looking old beyond your years. Dental implants not only restore your bright natural-looking smile, but they also restore your ability to chew hard or tough foods. Which in the long run will improve your overall health.
