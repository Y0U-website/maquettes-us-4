What Are All-on-4 and All-on-6 Dental Implants?

What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is a full-arch restoration technique that uses four dental implants to support a complete upper or lower arch of replacement teeth. By strategically angling the posterior implants, many patients can avoid additional procedures and enjoy a secure, long-lasting restoration.

  • Permanent, non-removable teeth
  • Improved chewing ability
  • Enhanced speech and comfort
  • Natural-looking results
  • Stable fixed-arch support
  • Preservation of jawbone structure

What Are All-on-6 Dental Implants?

All-on-6 uses six implants to support a full arch of teeth. The additional implants can provide greater support and distribute biting forces more evenly across the jaw.

All-on-6 may be recommended for patients who:

  • Have adequate bone volume
  • Generate higher bite forces
  • Desire maximum support and stability
  • Are candidates for additional implant placement

All-on-6 vs. Overdentures

The right option depends on your bone anatomy, oral health, restorative goals, and whether you prefer fixed or removable teeth.

A comprehensive examination and 3D CBCT scan will help determine whether fixed All-on-6 treatment or implant-supported overdentures are the better fit for your smile. Patients who prefer a fixed, non-removable restoration may be candidates for All-on-6, while patients who prefer a removable implant-retained option may consider implant-supported overdentures.

What to Expect With Full-Arch Implant Treatment

Your plan is based on your bone anatomy, bite, health history, and whether All-on-4 or All-on-6 support is the better fit.

  • Consultation and 3D Scan: review your goals, oral health, bone support, and full-arch options.
  • Digital Planning: map implant positions, restoration design, bite support, and bone grafting needs.
  • Implant Placement: place the implant posts and deliver a temporary restoration.
  • Final Arch: after healing, place the final teeth for comfort, function, and appearance.

Are You a Candidate?

You may be a candidate for All-on-4 or All-on-6 implants if you:

Even patients who have been told they are not candidates for implants may have treatment options available.

  • Are missing most or all of your teeth
  • Have failing or severely damaged teeth
  • Want a fixed full-arch solution
  • Are in good general health
  • Have sufficient bone or qualify for bone augmentation procedures

Why Patients Choose Full-Arch Dental Implants

These implant-supported restorations are fixed securely in place, so they are designed to feel stable during everyday function.

Many patients report that fixed implant-supported teeth feel stable, natural, and easier to trust when eating or speaking.

  • Greater confidence when eating and speaking
  • Improved comfort
  • Better chewing efficiency
  • Prevention of jawbone loss
  • A more youthful facial appearance
  • Long-lasting durability
  • No reliance on removable adhesive
