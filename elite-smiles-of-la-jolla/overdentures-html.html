  • Struggle with loose dentures
  • Have difficulty chewing
  • Want more retention than traditional dentures
  • Have sufficient bone or are open to grafting procedures
  • Want a removable implant-supported option

How Many Implants Are Needed?

The number of implants required depends on anatomy, bone volume, and treatment goals.

  • Lower overdentures often improve stability dramatically, may require fewer implants, and are often easier to adapt to.
  • Upper overdentures often require additional support because of differences in bone quality and anatomy, and palate coverage may vary depending on the design.
  • A CBCT scan and clinical evaluation help determine the appropriate treatment approach.

What to Expect With Overdenture Treatment

Your treatment is planned around denture fit, bone support, implant position, hygiene access, and the attachment style that best supports removable retention.

  • Consultation and CBCT Scan: review denture fit, bone support, and implant-supported options.
  • Implant Planning: decide implant number, attachment type, and whether grafting is needed.
  • Implant Placement: place the implant posts and allow healing.
  • Attachment Setup: add locator or bar attachments for removable retention.
  • Overdenture Delivery: fit, adjust, and review how to remove and clean the overdenture.
