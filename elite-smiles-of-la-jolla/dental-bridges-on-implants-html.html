Signs you might benefit from Implant-Supported Bridges

  • Several missing teeth in the same area
  • A traditional bridge is not ideal
  • You want fixed teeth instead of a removable appliance
  • Chewing or speaking feels difficult because of gaps
  • You want to avoid placing stress on nearby teeth
  • You want a stable, natural-looking restoration

Why consider Implant-Supported Bridges?

  • Strong support from implant posts
  • Helps preserve jawbone and facial structure
  • Restores chewing, speech, and smile appearance
  • Does not require reshaping nearby healthy teeth
  • Feels more stable than removable options
  • Designed for long-term comfort and confidence

Implant-Supported Bridges vs Traditional Bridges

Both options can replace missing teeth, but they differ in support, tooth preservation, bone support, and long-term planning.

Dr. Seraj can compare traditional bridges and implant-supported bridgework based on your bone support, bite, health, and goals.
