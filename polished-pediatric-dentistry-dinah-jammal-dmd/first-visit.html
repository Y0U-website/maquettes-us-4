The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends scheduling your child&#x27;s first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth appearing. At Polished Pediatric Dentistry, we offer free exams for children under 2 to help establish good oral health habits early!

What if my child is anxious or afraid of the dentist?

We get it – dental visits can be scary! That&#x27;s why we&#x27;ve created a warm, welcoming environment designed to put kids at ease. Dr. Dinah takes time to build trust with each child, explaining procedures in kid-friendly terms. We also offer amenities like in-chair entertainment and a prize station to make visits fun!

Can I accompany my child during the dental visit?

Absolutely! We encourage parents to be present during their child&#x27;s visit. It helps your child feel more comfortable and allows you to be directly involved in their dental care. We&#x27;ll show you how to support your child during the visit while allowing our team to build a positive relationship with them.
