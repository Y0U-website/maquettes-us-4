Meet Dr. Dinah Having spent much of her childhood living overseas, Dr. Dinah Jammal was immersed in a variety of cultures and experiences that broadened her understanding of people and the world around her. This early exposure sparked her deep interest in personal connections, and Dr. Dinah knew she wanted to continue fostering this passion by healing and helping others. It was only natural that during dental school she would combine these passions along with her upbeat, nurturing personality and dedicate her career to working with children and their families.

Outside of the office, you can often find Dr. Dinah spending time with her large and lively extended family. She loves playing the piano in her free time and is a dedicated Arsenal F.C. fan. As a self-proclaimed pop culture enthusiast, Dr. Dinah has a deep love for film and entertainment—Oscar and Grammy Night are serious events in her household!

Dr. Elisabeth Goldstein

Board Certified Pediatric Anesthesiologist

Dr. Elisabeth Goldstein is a board-certified physician anesthesiologist with 15 years of training and a specialization in pediatric anesthesia. She graduated from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and completed her anesthesia residency and her pediatric anesthesia fellowship at New York University. Dr. Goldstein is committed to providing safe, accessible, and high-quality sedation services for patients of all ages throughout the New Jersey area.

Supporting the Valerie Fund

We are proud to support The Valerie Fund, an organization dedicated to providing individualized care to children with cancer and blood disorders at medical centers close to home.

For every new patient who joins our practice, we will donate $5 to The Valerie Fund at the end of each month. Together, we can bring hope, support, and brighter futures to children in need.
