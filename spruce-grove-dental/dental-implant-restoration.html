Signs You May Need Implant Restoration or Repair

While dental implants are designed to be long-lasting, the visible restorations can wear out over time. You might need a restoration or repair if you experience:

At Spruce Grove Dental, we perform a full evaluation using digital imaging to determine the condition of your implant and restoration. We’ll explain your options and recommend the best course of action to restore both function and appearance.

  • Discomfort or bite issues around the implant
  • Visible wear, cracks, or damage to the crown or denture
  • Loosening of the restoration
  • Changes in how your teeth come together
  • Dissatisfaction with the aesthetics of your current restoration

Dental Implant Restoration FAQs

What’s the difference between implant placement and implant restoration?

Implant placement involves surgically inserting a titanium post into the jawbone. Implant restoration is the step that follows—attaching a custom crown, bridge, or denture to that post to complete your smile.

How long do implant restorations last?

With proper care and regular dental checkups, implant restorations can last 10–15 years or longer. The implant post itself can last a lifetime.

Can you replace a restoration from another dentist?

Yes. We frequently see patients who had implant work completed elsewhere and need adjustments or replacement restorations due to damage, wear, or dissatisfaction with aesthetics.

What does an implant crown feel like compared to a natural tooth?

Once placed, an implant-supported crown feels very similar to a natural tooth in both function and comfort. Most patients quickly adjust and forget it’s even there.

Will my restoration match the color of my other teeth?

Yes. We use shade-matching guides and digital imaging to create restorations that match your surrounding teeth for a seamless, natural appearance.
