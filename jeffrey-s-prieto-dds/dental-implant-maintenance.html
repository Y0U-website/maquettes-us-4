Professional Implant Cleanings: What to Expect

Specialized care for your implants.

Implants require gentle, customized cleaning techniques to avoid damaging the restoration. At Dr. Prieto’s practice, our hygienists are trained in advanced implant care and use non-abrasive tools for safe, effective cleanings. During an implant maintenance visit, we will:

  • Check for Plaque or Debris Removing buildup around and beneath the implant
  • Evaluate Gum and Bone Health Ensuring surrounding tissues stay supportive and healthy
  • Look for Early Warning Signs Identifying inflammation, bone changes, or exposed threads early

Long-Term Protection for Your Smile

With regular checkups and cleanings, dental implants can remain strong, comfortable, and natural-looking for decades. Our team is committed to protecting your investment so you can enjoy the benefits of your smile for life.
