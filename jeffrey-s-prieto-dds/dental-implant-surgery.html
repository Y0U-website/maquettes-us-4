The Dental Implant Surgery Process

A step-by-step approach to lasting results.

From consultation to final restoration, we make the implant journey straightforward and stress-free. Here’s what to expect:

  • Planning & Placement Your oral health is evaluated with advanced digital scans, and the titanium implant post is placed with precision under anesthesia for your comfort.
  • Healing & Integration Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with the bone (osseointegration), creating a stable foundation for your restoration.
  • Final Restoration A custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached, completing your smile with a natural look and feel.

Building Confidence, One Smile at a Time

Dental implant surgery not only restores function and appearance but also protects long-term oral health. With Dr. Prieto’s focus on patient-first care, you can feel confident knowing your treatment is designed around both comfort and success.
