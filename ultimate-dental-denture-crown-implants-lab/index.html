A dental laboratory is a facility dedicated to manufacturing custom dental prosthetics from dentist prescriptions and clinical impressions. Our laboratory team uses advanced materials and skilled craftsmanship to create restorations including crowns, bridges, dentures, implant abutments, and prosthetic devices that restore both function and appearance to patients’ teeth.

Ultimate Dental Laboratory was founded with the conviction that dental clinicians need laboratory partners evolving alongside the profession. We’ve developed a strong reputation as a resource where dentists access quality restorations and continuing education supporting their journey toward digital dentistry implementation.
