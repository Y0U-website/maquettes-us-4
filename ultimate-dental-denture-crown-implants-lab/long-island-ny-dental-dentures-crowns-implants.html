FAQ:

What does a dental laboratory actually do?

A dental laboratory is a specialized facility where custom dental prosthetics are fabricated. We work directly with dentists to create restorations based on their prescriptions and specifications. Our team of skilled technicians uses advanced materials and equipment to produce crowns, bridges, dentures, implant abutments, and other prosthetic devices that restore function and esthetics to patients’ teeth.

Ultimate Dental Laboratory was founded on the belief that clinicians need to advance along with the dental industry. We’ve become a trusted resource for dental professionals seeking high-quality restorations and continuing education that supports the digital transformation of dental practices.
