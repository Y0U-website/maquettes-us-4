The Different Dental Implant Restoration Options

1. Single Implant Restoration

This is a permanent tooth replacement option that is used when you only have one missing tooth that needs to be replaced.

2. Implant-Supported Bridge

This option is used if you have two or three missing teeth in a row. This is also a permanent tooth replacement option. The implants are what hold your new dental bridge in place.

3. Full and Partial Implant Dentures

This option is used for people who are missing multiple teeth or need to have most or all their teeth removed. For this option, your dentist will use four to eight dental implants. These are used to hold your full or partial sets of dentures in place, enabling you to talk and eat effortlessly without worrying about your teeth slipping.
