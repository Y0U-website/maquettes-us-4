Water Laser and Gum Treatments

Periodontal or gum disease is caused by plaque and tartar build-up on your teeth and gumline . This condition can damage the gums, and in severe cases, lead to tooth and bone loss. Treatment consists of removing tartar and plaque as well as any damaged tissue.

Laser-assisted gum therapy using the Waterlase greatly improves oral health by quickly removing tartar buildup. This technology offers treatments with less pain, fewer shots and faster recovery by directly targeting the source of inflammation without affecting healthy gum tissue.
