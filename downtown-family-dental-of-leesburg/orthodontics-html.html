How Does Invisalign Work?

Invisalign treatment uses a series of customized clear trays to gradually place pressure on your teeth to shift them into their ideal positions. The first part of the process involves scheduling a consultation. During your visit, Dr. Pham will examine your mouth to determine if Invisalign aligners are a good solution to fix your orthodontic problems. In some severe cases, traditional metal braces may be a better choice. If you’re a good candidate, our team will develop a customized treatment timeline for you, so you know how long you’ll need to wear the aligners for.

After sending off impressions to our lab, they’ll craft your customized trays and send them back to our office. Each of your aligners will be specially designed to comfortably fit over your teeth. Every two weeks or so, you’ll be tasked with switching to another set of trays so you can continue making progress.

What Problems Can Invisalign Fix?

Invisalign in Leesburg can fix a variety of issues that range from moderate to severe. In most cases, our team will suggest using aligners to remedy problems such as:

  • Crowded teeth that create small spaces in your mouth that are hard to clean.
  • Gaps between teeth that can make you feel unattractive and harm your self-esteem.
  • Bite misalignment that puts added stress on your jaw, causing teeth grinding and clenching.

The Benefits of Invisalign

When you visit our office, we’ll be sure to discuss all your options with you in detail so that you feel confident that you’re making the best decision for your smile. Many patients prefer using clear aligners to straighten their teeth because they offer a variety of advantages, including:

  • Discretion: Because your aligners will be made from clear plastic, you won’t have to worry about people noticing or pointing them out.
  • Easy Maintenance: To clean your aligners, all you need to do is brush them with denture cleaner and a soft-bristled brush daily and soak them in a 50/50 mixture of vinegar and water for 15 minutes weekly.
  • Removable Convenience: Instead of worrying about whether or not the foods you eat will break your orthodontic appliance, all you have to do is take it out of your mouth! That way, you won’t have to alter your regular diet or cut out some of your favorite snacks.
  • Improved Oral Health: Instead of having to worry about maneuvering between wires and brackets to clean your teeth, you just have to remove your aligners. That way, you can thoroughly clear plaque and food debris from your mouth and prevent oral health problems from developing over the course of your treatment.

Invisalign FAQs

Does Invisalign Treatment Hurt?

Invisalign works by exerting gentle pressure on one part of your smile at a time to shift it into the desired position. As a result, your teeth may feel a bit sore or sensitive when you first begin treatment or switch to a new set of aligners. However, this discomfort should dissipate after a couple of days. If it gets really bad, you can take an over-the-counter pain reliever in the meantime. You could also try switching to a new set of aligners right before bed so that you’ll sleep through the majority of the adjustment process.

Will Invisalign Affect the Way I Talk?

In the beginning, Invisalign might make you sound like you have a slight lisp. Many of the sounds you make are produced by placing your tongue against the space where the back of your front teeth meets the roof of your mouth. When the aligners reside in that spot, sometimes your tongue may get confused, resulting in a lisp. Any speech differences should fade away after a few days at most. You can help your tongue grow used to the aligners by counting from sixty to seventy out loud over and over again.

How Long Will Invisalign Treatment Take?

Your treatment time will vary based on several factors, like your age, your dedication to wearing your aligners, and the severity of your alignment issues. However, the vast majority of patients complete their treatment in 12-18 months. Although this might sound like a long time, keep in mind that traditional braces typically take 24-36 months to completely straighten your smile.

Will I Have to Wear a Retainer After I’m Done with Invisalign?

Much like with traditional braces, just because your orthodontic treatment is over doesn’t necessarily mean your teeth will remain aligned. In most cases, we will recommend that you wear a retainer after your treatment is finished. Otherwise, your teeth may shift back into their original crooked position, meaning your time spent wearing the aligners was all for nothing. Depending on your unique case, we may suggest wearing a retainer 24/7 for a few weeks or months after treatment. After that, you may be able to only wear a retainer at night.
