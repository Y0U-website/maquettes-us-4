What are Implant Dentures?

An implant denture is exactly what it sounds like: a denture anchored to dental implants. The exact number of implants used tends to vary, but it usually ranges from 4 to 8. The posts give your dentures better support and stability while also working to keep the jawbone healthy. Many patients report that their implant dentures feel and function almost exactly like their original teeth. Depending on your preferences, you can get a fixed implant denture or a removable implant denture.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

This type of denture remains in your mouth 24/7, only to be removed by Dr. Pham as needed. The big advantage of this type of implant is the ease of maintenance; you can just brush and floss like normal to keep your restoration clean. You need to have a certain level of jawbone density to support this type of denture.

Removable Implant Dentures

A removable implant denture snaps into place using clips or ball sockets. You’ll be able to take it out anytime, allowing you to clean the entire prosthetic from every angle. It usually takes fewer implants to support removable dentures, so it’s a better option for patients with less jawbone density.

The Implant Denture Procedure

  • The first step is a consultation with our team. We’ll check and evaluate your oral health and schedule preparatory treatments as needed. This might include bone grafts or gum disease treatment.
  • Next, a minor surgery has to be performed to place the dental implants in the jaw. Protective caps are placed on them afterward.
  • The bone and gum tissue will fuse with the implants after several months; you can then return to our office so that abutments can be attached.
  • Finally, a denture is made based on impressions on your mouth and attached to the implants.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

You can be considered for implant dentures if you have lost most or all of the teeth in one arch. The best candidate for dental implant surgery will have good oral and overall health; any health issues that might get in the way of the procedure need to be addressed first. Also, since getting implants is a lengthy process, you have to be willing to make a long-term commitment.

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

  • Improved oral health thanks to easy maintenance via good oral hygiene.
  • Prevention of bone loss due to implant simulation.
  • Greatly improved self-confidence.
  • A healthier diet thanks to increased chewing power that makes it easier to eat harder foods.
  • New teeth that can last 30 years or even a lifetime when cared for properly.

Do You Brush Implant Dentures?

Yes! In fact, if you want your implant dentures in Leesburg to last, it’s crucial that you brush them for two full minutes each morning and evening. You should also incorporate other healthy habits into your routine, like using a straw when drinking coffee, limiting your intake of sugary sweets, and scheduling a dental cleaning every six months.

What Can’t You Eat with Implant Dentures?

In an effort to prolong the lifespan of your dental implants in Leesburg, we do recommend steering clear of certain foods, starting with sticky sweets. You should also exercise caution with hard pretzels, thin pizza crust, popcorn, and other extremely crunchy foods. Instead, opt for more nutrient-dense foods that are softer in consistency, like plain yogurt, oatmeal, and bananas.
