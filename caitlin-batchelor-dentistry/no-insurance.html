Our Approach to Dentistry

At Batchelor Dentistry, our team not only wants to improve your long-term dental health, but build lasting relationships that go beyond just “fixing teeth.” That starts by helping you stay informed about the cost of your treatments. So if you’re uninsured, we invite you to let us know ahead of your appointment. That way we can discuss your options and help you avoid any surprises along the way

By offering this individualized approach to your care, we can determine the best care plan to fit your dental health care needs and your budget. By working together, we’ll help you take control of your dental health and achieve the dental care you need at a price you understand.
