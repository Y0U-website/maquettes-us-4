The practice

Caitlin Batchelor Dentistry is a general, cosmetic, and restorative dental practice at 1920 Medical Ave Ste J in Harrisonburg, Virginia. We treat adults, children, seniors, patients with special needs, and patients with dental anxiety.

Are you looking for a gentle, friendly Harrisonburg dentist for your entire family? You’re in the right place. We treat adults, kids, seniors, people with special needs, and people afraid of the dentist. We treat the whole family!

As a Harrisonburg dentist with a patient-centered focus, Dr. Batchelor can help you with all your dental needs or concerns. Whether you want whiter teeth, natural-looking fillings, beautiful options to replace a missing or damaged tooth, or an emergency appointment when your tooth hurts, you can count on us.

The entire team at Caitlin Batchelor Dentistry is dedicated to building a lasting relationship with you that goes beyond just “fixing teeth.” We look forward to meeting you!

We’re in-network with Delta Dental Premier and file claims for most other insurance plans, including out-of-network plans. We don’t participate in Medicaid or Medicare.

You’ll get a written treatment plan with your options and their costs before treatment begins, so nothing on the bill is a surprise.

New patients without dental insurance benefits can get $50 off a first visit that includes a comprehensive exam and X-rays. Call the office for details.

If dental visits make you anxious, say so when you call and we will plan around it. We offer sedation dentistry and NuCalm , a drug-free relaxation method with no recovery time. Patients using NuCalm can drive themselves to and from the appointment and return to their day immediately afterward.

Services

Regular dental preventive care is a great way to avoid serious dental and overall health problems. It’s also important for detecting small problems while they’re easy and inexpensive to treat.

However, if gum disease has already taken hold and caused periodontitis, we’ve got you covered. Not only do we offer preventive cleaning services, but therapeutic care as well, called scaling and root planing.

We’re here to create a smile that’s truly yours, with a process designed just for you.
