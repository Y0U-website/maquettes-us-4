The practice

We offer a wide range of treatments for all age groups, with an emphasis on prevention and optimal oral health.

Regular dental exams to help protect not just your oral health, but overall health. Restorative procedures are done with our SOLEA laser - which means no numbing in most cases!

We believe in the beauty of a natural smile. We can achieve that by several options such as veneers, crowns, dentures or orthodontic treatment.

We are passionate about your health. We truly believe that patients deserve the highest standard of dental care.

We submit to all insurance companies, give us to call to see if we are in your insurance network!

Les soins

We provide dental cleanings for all ages and strive to make our patients comfortable during the entire procedure.

We offer Invisalign - clear aligners that straighten your smile without the hassle of metal braces. Using our iTero scanner, we can show you a digital preview of your potential results in minutes!
