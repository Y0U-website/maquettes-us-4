Dental Implants in Weston, FL

Literally speaking, in order to hold a prosthetic tooth or bridge in place, dental implants are placed into your jaw. In most cases, however, when people use the term "dental implants," they’re referring to the combination of the implant and the prosthetic tooth. Patients who have lost one or more teeth due to periodontal disease, an injury, or some other reason and do not want to wear dentures may be a candidate for dental implants. First Weston Dental offers both single-tooth implants, as well as all-on-four dental implants in Weston, FL.

Single-Tooth Implants

Compared to other tooth replacement options, a dental implant offers several advantages. A dental implant looks and functions like a natural tooth while preserving the health of adjacent teeth. The bone is better preserved with a dental implant since it replaces the tooth root. Your jawbone integrates with dental implants, helping to keep the bone healthy and intact.

All-On-Four Implants

All-on-Four dental implants allow you to get a full mouth of new teeth with just a few implants. You don’t need to go through the process of having implants done for every single individual tooth. Eventually, the titanium dental implant fuses with your jawbone providing you with a strong, safe, and secure set of permanently fixed new teeth . And the maintenance is a breeze in comparison to traditional dentures. With just four implants on the top and four on the bottom, you will walk away with an entirely new smile.
