What Should I Do If I Have Dental Anxiety?

If you have dental anxiety, don’t worry - you’re not alone. Many people feel anxious or scared about going to the dentist, but there are ways to overcome those feelings. Here are some tips:

  • Talk to your dentist about your anxiety. We offer different sedation dentistry options to ease anxious feelings.
  • Distract yourself by focusing on your breathing or listening to music.
  • Bring along a friend or family member for support.
