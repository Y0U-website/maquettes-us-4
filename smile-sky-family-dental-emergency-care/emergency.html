What should I do if I knock out a tooth?

If a tooth is knocked out, act quickly — time is critical. Handle the tooth by the crown (the top portion), not the root. Rinse it gently with water without scrubbing, and attempt to place it back into the socket if possible. If re-implantation isn’t feasible, store the tooth in a container of milk or in between your cheek and gum to keep it moist. Call (678) 722-5212 immediately and come in as soon as possible. The sooner you are seen, the better the chance of saving the tooth.

How do I know if my toothache is a dental emergency?

Not all toothaches are dental emergencies, but certain symptoms indicate you should seek care right away. If your tooth pain is severe, throbbing, or persistent — or if it is accompanied by facial swelling, a fever, difficulty swallowing, or a bad taste in your mouth — you may have a dental abscess or infection that requires prompt treatment. Call our office at (678) 722-5212 and we will help you assess the urgency of your situation.

Can you treat a cracked or broken tooth the same day?

In most cases, yes. Depending on the severity of the fracture, same-day treatment options may include dental bonding, a tooth-colored filling, or placement of a temporary or permanent dental crown . If the crack has affected the nerve of the tooth, root canal treatment may also be performed to relieve pain and preserve the tooth. Our team will assess the damage and recommend the most appropriate treatment during your visit.

What happens if a dental infection is left untreated?

A dental infection or abscess should never be ignored. Without treatment, the infection can spread beyond the tooth to the surrounding bone, gum tissue, and even to other areas of the body — which can become a serious medical concern. If you notice swelling, pus, a persistent bad taste, or a fever alongside tooth pain, contact Smile Sky Family Dental immediately. Prompt treatment with antibiotics and appropriate dental procedures is essential to stopping the spread of infection.
