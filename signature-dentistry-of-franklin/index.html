The practice

When combining the latest dental techniques, hundreds of hours of continuing education, and cutting edge technology, we can deliver a dental result that truly excels. You can be sure we strive to do it right the first time.

From a cup of coffee when you check-in, to TVs on the ceiling to ease the fears of a nervous patient, to the warm scented towels after your appointment to wipe your face, to the heat and massage built into our dental chairs, we’ve created an experience to let you know we care.

We believe that a dental office can be so much more than just an office, that it can become your dental home, where the dental team and patient get to know each other over decades.

We believe dentistry can change a life, through a new smile to replace the one you’ve been hiding, to give you back the ability to chew and enjoy your food again, or even just the removal of pain that’s making it difficult to sleep. Dentistry matters!

There is nothing like seeing the joy (and often tears) of a patient who sees their new smile for the first time. Whether its clear aligners to straighten the teeth, porcelain veneers, or some simple bonding, there is no reason to hide your smile anymore.
