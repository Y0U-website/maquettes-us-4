About Dr. Woo Dr. John Woo was born and raised in Sunnyvale, California. He has been practicing orthodontics in Petaluma and Sebastopol since 1993 and continues to love what he does even after 27+ years! His knowledge of orthodontics and ability to achieve excellent treatment results have earned him much respect in the dental community and with his patients. He has been voted “Best Orthodontist” by the Argus-Courier every year since 2010 and was awarded Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics for his proficiency and excellence in the orthodontic field.

When he is not working, Dr. Woo can be found enjoying the Sonoma County lifestyle along with his wife and two children. A lover of sports, he can often be found playing tennis, basketball, golf, skiing or cycling. If Dr. Woo was to have any other job he would be in a band, which has partly inspired his music-themed office! He still enjoys playing the drums and guitar in his free time.

“ I enjoy practicing orthodontics because it is the artful application of science coupled with the subtleties of patient care. The field of orthodontics is continually evolving and every case is unique. Therefore, I am given the opportunity to be challenged and creative on an ongoing basis. The true reward of my work is delivering expert orthodontic care with an unparalleled experience that will leave you smiling on the inside and out!” – Dr. Woo

Education

Continuing Education

Dr. Woo is both a student and educator when it comes to continuing education. Dr. Woo attends continuing education courses to learn about new orthodontic technologies so that he can consistently provide the highest level of care available in the field. His expertise is also sought after within the greater dental community. He is able to share his knowledge by frequently providing continuing education courses for dentists, hygienists, and dental assistants on the different aspects of orthodontic treatment.

Complex Cases

Dr. Woo is the consulting orthodontist for a multi-disciplinary specialty group that focuses on complex cases. This group is composed of many top-rate dentists and select specialists to provide a team approach to creating a comprehensive and expert treatment plan for those patients that present with challenging or complex issues. Many in this group have years of experience in solving complex cases and know how to best approach specific needs. He has also worked with UCSF, Kaiser, and Stanford on surgical orthognathic cases.
