Accidents happen, but we’re here for your child no matter what tooth trauma occurs.

For patients who have difficulties tolerating dental treatment, we are able to treat them in the operating room setting at Cullman Regional Medical Center.

Sedation is another safe option for patients who have difficulties tolerating dental treatment or are very nervous.

Finding a dentist for your child with special needs can be challenging. As a parent it’s important to you to find the safest and best care for your child. Dr. Cat has had extensive training with children of all ability levels as well as ones with complex medical conditions, and is able to offer treatment to little ones with special needs.
