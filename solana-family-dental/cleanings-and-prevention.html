Dental Radiographs (X-Rays)

Solana Beach Family Dental offers dental X-Rays to help establish any unwanted dental structures, malignancies, or tooth decay. Below are some of the reasons why it’s important for you to have occasional dental radiographs:

  • Invaluable for detecting caries (decay) in between teeth and under existing restorations
  • May detect a number of otherwise invisible conditions, such as bone loss and abnormalities of tissues surrounding the teeth
  • Modern dental radiographs are extremely safe since digital radiographs emit extremely low doses of radiation
  • Dental radiograph frequency is determined individually based on the patient’s vulnerability and propensity to gum disease and/or tooth decay

Home Care

Most of the things that call for sophisticated dental procedures stem from a lack of proper dental home care. That’s why our dentistry team provides you with home care tips. Things like proper diet and cleaning of teeth are an indispensable part of maintaining a healthy smile. Here are some of the things you can do at home for improved oral health:

  • Floss teeth once a day
  • Brush teeth two to three times a day with fluoridated toothpaste
  • Rinse after meals if you can’t brush
