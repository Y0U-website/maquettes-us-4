Benefits of dental implants

Dental implants are the most preferred tooth replacement solution because of many reasons. The benefits of replacing missing teeth with dental implants include:

  • Support the integrity of bone structure even after tooth loss
  • Protect the health of adjacent teeth as there is no need for bridgework
  • Replace loose-fitting dentures
  • Distribute bite forces evenly.
  • Function like natural teeth
  • It helps improve facial appearance by enhancing bone health and preventing its deterioration.
  • Strong and long-lasting.
  • Restore oral functionality

What Is the Dental Implant Placement Process?

Planning stage: A carefully thought-out treatment plan is formulated. We may need specialized imaging to visualize the bone condition and 3D viewing to ensure proper placement.

Implant placement: The dental implant will be placed in the ideal spot to form the implant’s gum tissue.

Healing: The bone around the dental implant will grow and integrate with the new implant.

Temporary restoration: If the dental implant is in an easily visible area, a temporary restoration is placed for aesthetic purposes till the site heals.

Abutment placement: When the site is healed, the dental implant abutment is placed to attach the dental implant to the porcelain crown.

Porcelain crown: The new porcelain crown custom made to exact specifications is bonded to the abutment.
