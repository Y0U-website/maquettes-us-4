Why You Should Straighten Your Smile

When your teeth are not properly aligned, the way your upper and lower teeth meet could also be misaligned. This means excessive stress on parts of your teeth which are not designed for it. The misaligned bite can also place undue stress on the temporomandibular joint (located in the front of your ear) which can result in jaw, face, or neck pain, and other problems as well.

Poorly aligned teeth are also harder to keep clean. There are usually corners and narrow spaces that are impossible to reach with a brush and which might even prevent the use of dental floss. The risk of tooth decay and gum disease is higher when the teeth are crooked.

There is also the aesthetic aspect: most people prefer to have straight, white teeth. More attractive teeth can be a big confidence booster and turn a withdrawn person into someone who enjoys smiling with friends.
