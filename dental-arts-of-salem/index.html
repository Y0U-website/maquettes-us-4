Our restorative care in Salem NH consists of all those treatments that restore health to an individual tooth or restore completeness to a patient’s entire set of teeth. At the simpler end of this scale, our tooth-colored composite resin fillings are used to treat cavities and restore wholeness to the protective covering of a tooth while also preserving the tooth’s bright appearance.

At a more complex level, a dental implant is a way we can restore a full set of teeth for a patient. Dental implants in Salem NH are the closest thing to natural teeth that you can achieve. Using our surgical skills, we will place a titanium implant in the bone of the jaw and then attach a ceramic crown to the top of the implant.

To place this implant to the greatest degree of accuracy possible, we use a stent-guided system that maximizes our ability to place the implant in the very best location so it is surrounded by strong, solid bone. This replacement tooth can feel like you never lost a tooth at all.
