SureSmile® Clear Braces

Valley Oak Family Dentistry proudly offers state-of-the-art orthodontic care with Suresmile braces, a cutting-edge technology designed to revolutionize the teeth straightening experience. Our commitment to providing exceptional and personalized orthodontic services is reflected in the incorporation of Suresmile’s advanced 3D imaging, computer-aided design, and robotic wire-bending technology.

With Suresmile braces, our patients benefit from a customized and precise approach to orthodontic treatment. The process begins with a comprehensive evaluation, including detailed 3D imaging to create a digital model of the patient’s teeth. Our skilled orthodontic team utilizes this information to develop a personalized treatment plan, leveraging the power of computer-aided design to optimize the alignment of each tooth.

What sets Suresmile braces apart is the robotic wire-bending technology, which creates customized archwires for each patient. These archwires are tailored to the specific treatment plan, allowing for more efficient and targeted tooth movement. This level of customization often leads to shorter treatment times and fewer adjustments, contributing to a more comfortable and streamlined orthodontic experience.

At Valley Oak Family Dentistry, we understand the importance of providing not just effective orthodontic solutions but also ones that align with our patients’ preferences. Suresmile clear braces offer a sophisticated and modern option for those seeking a high-tech approach to achieving a beautifully aligned smile. For more information about our Suresmile braces services and to explore how this advanced technology can benefit you, please contact our office for a consultation.
