Traditional Orthodontics

Traditional metal braces and wires are made up of brackets and wires that apply pressure to the teeth to slowly move them towards new positions. There are many kinds of orthodontic treatments available, yet braces remain one of the most reliable options for addressing many complex issues. The average time for wearing braces is between 18 and 24 months, although some treatments may be shorter or longer depending on the kind of changes that need to be made.

Invisalign Clear Braces

Similar to traditional braces, Invisalign exerts pressure on the teeth to correct their positions. However, this form of treatment involves wearing a series of removable aligners that stay in the mouth for 20 to 22 hours every day. Invisalign aligners are transparent and are designed to sit right up against the teeth, so many people won’t notice them if they don’t know what to look for. Also, being removable means your aligners won’t stop you from eating all your favorite foods or brushing thoroughly.
