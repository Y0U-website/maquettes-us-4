Meet Our Newest Smile Expert!

Dr. Sarah Ditta

We’re excited to welcome Dr. Sarah Ditta to the Dental Serenity of Manhattan family!

Dr. Ditta brings a warm, gentle approach and a true passion for patient care. She focuses on orthodontics, jaw function, and cosmetic dentistry—helping patients achieve confident, natural-looking smiles in a way that feels comfortable and personal.

Dr.Ditta is passionate about sharing simple, practical tips to improve oral wellness and believes that small changes can make a big difference.

After completing her residency at Montefiore Medical Center, Dr. Ditta worked closely with orthodontic specialists to master Invisalign and braces. Her time practicing in Manhattan’s Upper East Side refined her love for minimal, natural smile design that enhances facial harmony while preserving the health of your teeth.

We’re thrilled to have her on board—and she’s now offering late weekday appointments to better fit your schedule!

Outside the office, Dr. Ditta enjoys being outdoors, writing, and participating in Bible study, and strolling the wide aisles of Costco.

Dr. Ditta looks forward to meeting you, hearing your goals, and helping you feel confident in your smile.
