What to include with a referral

Complete records let us schedule the right visit length and avoid repeat imaging.

  • Referral note The reason for referral, the teeth or sites involved, and any specific question you want answered.
  • Imaging, with dates Recent radiographs or CBCT volumes, labeled with acquisition dates, so we can judge whether new imaging is needed.
  • Relevant health history Medical conditions, current medications, anticoagulation status, and anything bearing on anesthesia planning.
  • Orthodontic plan, if applicable For impacted canines and surgical exposure cases: the treatment plan and desired eruption path from the orthodontist.

What you can expect from us

For you as the referrer

  • Acknowledgement that your referral was received and your patient was contacted. Turnaround expectation: we typically respond within one business day
  • A post-operative report after treatment, including findings, what was done, and any follow-up we recommend on your side.
  • A direct conversation with the treating surgeon whenever you want to discuss a case before or after referral.

For your patients

  • A calm, clearly explained consultation covering options, comfort and anesthesia choices, and costs before treatment.
  • Written pre- and post-operative instructions, with a direct line for questions during recovery.
  • A plan that respects your restorative or orthodontic treatment goals — we coordinate rather than redirect care.

Study club and referral liaison

How patient information is handled

Referral records are used only for evaluating and treating the referred patient and for coordinating care with your office. Until a secure, BAA-covered electronic channel is validated, we ask that records travel by phone or fax rather than email or web forms.

Website contact forms on this site are general-purpose and explicitly warn patients not to include health details; they are not a PHI channel. Questions about our privacy practices can be directed to the office at 908-520-2694 .

Ready to send a referral?

Call the referral line for urgent cases, or email the records checklist for routine care. We will take it from there and keep you in the loop.
