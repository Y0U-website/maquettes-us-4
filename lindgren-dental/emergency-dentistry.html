Emergency Dental

  • What is NOT a dental emergency? Minor toothaches, small chips, or slight discomfort without swelling or bleeding are typically not considered emergencies. However, it’s still important to contact your dentist promptly to avoid future problems.
  • What’s the best way to handle a dental emergency? For the best outcome, call our emergency dental clinic immediately. If you’ve knocked out a tooth, try to keep it moist and bring it with you. For severe pain or swelling, apply a cold compress and seek care as soon as possible.
  • What should I do if I have a toothache? Rinse your mouth with warm water and gently floss to remove any debris. If the pain persists, take an over-the-counter pain reliever and contact our same-day dentist for an evaluation.
  • What should I do if my dental filling or crown breaks? If a filling or crown breaks, keep the area clean and avoid chewing on the affected side. Contact us immediately to schedule an appointment so we can restore the damaged tooth.
  • Are you a same-day dentist? Yes! We offer same-day care for urgent needs — our team can see you quickly when you need an urgent care dentist to relieve pain, address dental emergencies, or get you out of discomfort the same day. If you’re experiencing a dental emergency, just give us a call and we’ll do our best to fit you in right away!
