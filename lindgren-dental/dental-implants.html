Frequently Asked Questions

  • Am I a candidate for a dental implant? Many adults with a missing or severely damaged tooth may be candidates for dental implants. Generally, implant patients should have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone to support the implant, and overall oral health that supports proper healing. The best way to know whether an implant is appropriate for you is through an evaluation. With our on-site 3D imaging, we can take a closer look at your oral anatomy and discuss the treatment options that make the most sense for your smile.
  • What type of dental implants does Lindgren Dental offer? We offer single-tooth dental implants at Lindgren Dental, including placement, restoration, and maintenance.
  • How often should I have my dental implants checked? We recommend regular dental checkups every six months to ensure your implants are functioning well and to address any issues early. During these visits, we’ll clean your implants and monitor the surrounding gum and bone health.
