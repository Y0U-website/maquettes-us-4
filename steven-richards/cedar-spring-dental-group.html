Cedar Spring Dental Group

Cedar Spring Family Dentistry believes that all patients deserve quality dental care whether they have insurance or not. For patients without dental insurance, we have developed the Cedar Spring Dental Group.

The Cedar Spring Dental Group is a health savings program that allows you to receive the same benefits that patients with insurance receive, plus some! For one flat annual fee, you receive a wide variety of dental benefits throughout the year all included.

The Cedar Spring Dental Group is not insurance it’s a dental health savings program.

How does the Dental Group save me money?

With the Cedar Spring Dental Group, you pay a yearly flat fee that covers several dental and periodontal procedures at our office instead of being charged separately for each one. You choose the plan that best covers your needs. The Cedar Spring Dental Group’s flat annual fee includes all of the following services:

The annual fee for the Cedar Spring Dental Group is only $489 (18 years & older) and $359 (17 years & younger), and the Periodontal Plan is $619. These plans offer a savings of more than 50 %.

The benefit period lasts for one (1) year from the date of the payment of your Cedar Spring Dental Group fee (e.g., Feb 10, 2024 Feb 10, 2025). During your year on the plan, you’ll have no maximum, no waiting period and no exclusions for pre-existing conditions.

The plan excludes services for which patients are referred to a specialist, or services for which the patient is unfit to have performed due to health reasons.

  • Routine dental exams
  • Oral cancer screenings
  • Diagnostic x-rays (including full mouth series)
  • Panoramic x-ray (recommended once every 3 years)
  • All emergency or limited exams
  • Fluoride treatment
  • 20 % discount off all other services
  • Routine periodontal cleanings (3x/year)
  • Routine dental exams

  • Diagnostic x-rays (as prescribed)
  • All emergency and limited exams

Why choose the Cedar Spring Dental Group?

Our dental and periodontal plan offers outstanding benefits for adults and children often better than standard dental insurance plans. With Cedar Spring Dental Group, you’ll benefit from:

  • No Maximums. Dental insurance programs typically have a maximum benefit that you can use during a calendar year. Most of these maximums are $1000-$2000, which means after $2000 worth of dental care during a 12-month period, you’re left paying the full difference. With Cedar Spring Dental Group, there is no maximum!
  • No Waiting Period. Many insurance programs require you to wait 3 6 months before you can use your benefits. With Cedar Spring Dental Group, you can enjoy the benefits of the plan from the moment you join!
  • No Exclusions. Insurance companies have many tactics to avoid covering your treatments. From denying pre-existing conditions to telling you when and where you can have your teeth fixed, the companies try to dictate your care. With Cedar Spring Dental Group, there are no exclusions. We provide the dental care you need, when you need it.

Can I combine the plan with my insurance?

Cedar Spring Dental Group is not dental insurance and cannot be combined with any dental insurance. Obtaining dental insurance will cause participant to be ineligible to continue in the Dental Group.

How do I sign up?

To sign up for the Cedar Spring Dental Group, call 864-583-4962 , or stop by our office. Our friendly staff will be happy to discuss the plan with you, help you enroll, and accept your payment by cash, check or credit card.
