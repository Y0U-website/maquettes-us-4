Walsh Reilly Dental Group Accessibility Statement

Walsh Reilly Dental Group is committed to facilitating the accessibility and usability of its website, wrdentalgroup.com , for everyone. Walsh Reilly Dental Group aims to comply with all applicable standards, including the World Wide Web Consortiums Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.0 up to Level AA (WCAG 2.0 AA). Walsh Reilly Dental Group is proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone.

We highly recommend using the userway accessibility widget linked in the footer, but should you experience any difficulty in accessing any part of this website, please feel free to call us at (610) 446-4210 or email us at frontdesk@wrdentalgroup.com and we will work with you to provide the information or service you seek through an alternate communication method that is accessible for you consistent with applicable law (for example, through telephone support).

Meet Dr. Rachel Walsh Reilly

Her Approach

Dr. Rachel Reilly focuses on providing quality dental care in a relaxed and gentle manner. Her main goal is to make her patients feel comfortable at their appointments and confident with their smile. Dr. Reilly has practiced primarily in Philadelphia and its surrounding suburbs since graduation from dental school, but is honored to be serving and creating relationships with the Havertown community.

Dr. Reilly’s Credentials

Dr. Reilly, formerly Dr. Walsh

Dr. Reilly recently was married on the top of a mountain in Hawaii: she loves to tell the story of the hike up and down! You may have previously known her as Dr. Rachel Walsh: she is still the same bubbly dentist whose laugh will help make you forget you’re sitting in a dental office. She and her husband, Patrick, have moved from the city and now are officially residents of Havertown! They look forward to becoming an integral part of the community and are always looking for recommendations for the best restaurants and food to try in the area! Dr. Reilly cannot wait to meet you!
