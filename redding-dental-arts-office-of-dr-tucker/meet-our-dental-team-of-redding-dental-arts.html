Meet the Team

Jennifer Registered Dental Hygienist (RDH), Bachelor of Science

Jennifer has been part of the Dental Team of Redding Dental Arts since the beginning. With over 15 years of dental hygiene experience she is very capable of answering any questions you may have and takes very good care of our patients. She was born and raised in Redding. She enjoys camping at the coast, hiking, snowboarding, fishing and spending time with her husband, sons and daughter. We are very fortunate to have her as one of our hygienists.

Katie is our Office Manager! She has a background in both medical and dental billing and can rattle off the answers to most questions you have about insurance. If not, she is quick to conduct research about how to best utilize the benefits you have. She’s also very good at handling most questions you have in other areas, so be sure to bring up any concerns you may have next time you see her.

Alicia Registered Dental Hygienist (RDH), Bachelor of Arts

Alicia is one of our hygienists has joined the dental team of Redding Dental Arts with years of experience. She worked as a supervising hygienist for a number of the area’s Shasta College hygienists in training. She is smart, clinically savvy and gentle. She truly has your overall oral health in mind as she helps with tips and tricks that will make your experience at home brushing and flossing manageable and more enjoyable.

Chelsea Registered Dental Assistant (RDA)

Chelsea is the daughter of the late Dr. Dague and we are so fortunate to have her as part of the Dental Team of Redding Dental Arts. She has a very sunny disposition and is very caring. She does her best to make sure your experience at Redding Dental Arts is one of the best around.

Halie’s mom was an RDA (Registered Dental Assistant) so you could say dentistry is in her blood. She moved to Redding in 2023 from the Bay Area and will never look back. We are happy to have her as our scheduling coordinator and first face you see when you enter the practice.

Megan joined us shortly after Chelsea came on board following the passing of Dr. Dague. She loves good music and often attends concerts. Dentistry is in her blood and she is very good at helping to make sure our patients have a comfortable experience. Feel free to ask her what her favorite band is the next time you are in!

Lesli is from Texas and started out as an RDA in an endo office. She likes to help our patients smile and get in for their appointments. She is the first face you see when you walk into our practice and will always greet you with a smile. We are grateful to have her as part of our team.

We are lucky to have Sarah in our office since she can pretty much fix anything and is always so cheery and peppy. If you see her, please feel free to say hello and start up a conversation. Also, Side note, she was a private eye in another life.

Elisia is our newest addition and an all around joy to be around. She has ambitious goals of becoming a Registered Dental Hygienist (RDH) in the future and we are excited she is part of our crew!
