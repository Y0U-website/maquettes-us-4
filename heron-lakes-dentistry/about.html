Elevating Your Dental Experience

We do dentistry a little differently here at Heron Lakes Dentistry. Say goodbye to sterile, noisy, and uncomfortable environments. Instead, prepare to enter a relaxing visit tailored to your comfort and care.

Technology

We don’t take your time lightly. That’s why we’ve invested in CEREC same-day restorations which allows us to create custom dental crowns, veneers, and bridges in a single visit. No more temporary restorations and multiple appointments, spend more time enjoying your free time.

Enjoy precise diagnosis, elevated treatments, and a high caliber of care. Our cutting-edge 3D imaging technology enables us to capture detailed, three-dimensional images of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This means we can get a good sense of dental issues and make precise treatment plans to get you back on track.

Gone are the days of messy, uncomfortable impressions. With digital impression technology, we capture highly accurate models of your teeth using a handheld scanner. Not only is this process more comfortable for you, but it also ensures precise and efficient treatment results.
