Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Volodymyr Tomyn

ALL. SMILES. ALL AGES. ALL CARE. Dr.Tomyn was born and raised in Ukraine. He received his first dental degree from dental school of IFNMU in 2008. Back in Europe he received his great clinical experience by working in federal and private practices. After coming to the US, he obtained his Doctorate of Medicine in Dentistry from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine.

Dr.Tomyn is an active member of American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry and American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry.

When treating his patients he is always striving to provide the highest quality care. He believes that all patients require individualized, unique approach in their needs. Our long term goal is to build an open, trusted relationship, so our patients can feel safe and comfortable every time they see us .

In his spare time he loves to be with his family. Also, he enjoys playing soccer (used to play professional soccer team in Ukraine), studying new languages and new technologies, and watching detective shows.

Why Choose Us
