Continuing Education

Dr. Brown enjoys participating in continuing education. The orthodontic profession is always evolving with new treatment options for patients and it is exciting to be involved in these advancements through continuing education. He is an active member of national and local orthodontic and dental societies, keeping him involved with other practitioners in the field. In addition to attending courses, he also volunteers at OHSU and LBCC dental education programs. He is committed to providing his patients with the best treatment options available.

Professional Memberships and Affiliations Dr. Brown not only belongs to many orthodontic associations, but he has assumed many leadership roles as a way to continue his education. Dr. Brown feels that his high level of involvement with his colleagues and professional associations, results in networking opportunities and a sharing of ideas that further advances his orthodontic knowledge. Dr. Brown is a member of the:

  • Southern Willamette Dental Society
  • Oregon Dental Association
  • American Dental Association
  • Oregon State Society of Orthodontists
  • Pacific Coast Society of Orthodontists
  • American Association of Orthodontists
