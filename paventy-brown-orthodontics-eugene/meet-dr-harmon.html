Passionate About Orthodontics

Dr. Harmon decided to become an orthodontist because of the impact a smile has on a person’s life. Looking back, he remembers how orthodontic treatment greatly improved his self confidence, and adds that giving people a beautiful smile is something that they can share with others daily! Dr. Harmon’s favorite part about orthodontics is watching people transform during their treatment, and not just with their braces; he loves watching patients grow up or reach certain milestones and celebrating life together while in treatment!
