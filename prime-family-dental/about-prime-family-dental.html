Our Team

Dr. Sabine Schtakleff

I’ve been involved in dentistry for over ten years, first in Dental Assisting, then in obtaining my Doctor of Dental surgery degree at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. My work doesn’t feel like work to me: I spend my days helping remarkable people improve their oral and overall health, along with their smiles. What could be better?

More about Dr. Schtakleff Dr. Adrienne Verga Associate DDS

I knew early in life, through experience with family members in the dental field, that dentistry would be my profession of choice. Not only does it so closely align with my meticulous and caring nature, but it also utilizes the perfect combination of art and science to treat patients’ individual dental needs.

I studied at Hofstra University in New York and graduated with honors in 2000, receiving a Bachelor’s of Science in Biology with minor concentrations in Chemistry and Fine Arts. I earned my Doctor of Dental Surgery license and graduated with honors from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill School of Dentistry in 2004.

During my time in Chapel Hill, I fell in love with the area and decided to make North Carolina my permanent home. My mother and three brothers now reside in North Carolina as well. I enjoy painting, cooking, hiking, working out, traveling and spending quality time with my family.

I have over 18 years of experience as a general dentist, certified in Invisalign, and an active member of the American Dental Association and North Carolina Dental Society. I am thrilled to be with Prime Family Dental and am available to address any dental concerns you may have!
