Enamel Microabrasion

Ever wonder if certain white or brown spots on your teeth can go away without having to do veneers or crowns? Enamel microabrasion is a conservative procedure we do in our office where we use a specially formulated gel to gently remove those superficial spots on your enamel. No anesthesia is required!

The gel buffs away the stains so they can blend in with the rest of the tooth. We usually use it along with at home whitening trays for improved esthetics.
