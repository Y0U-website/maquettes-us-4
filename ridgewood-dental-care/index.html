Ridgewood Dental Care is a dental office driven by Dr. Sang Y. Kim and his professional, friendly staff who make quality patient care and service their first priority. We offer high quality dental care at affordable prices to individuals of all ages and their families.

At our practice, we make it convenient for individuals and families throughout Queens/Brooklyn and neighboring areas to receive the dental care they need and deserve. We are located less than a block away from Myrtle-Wyckoff subway lines (L and M) and bus station (B13, B26, B52, B54, Q55, Q58).

Our experienced friendly staff and team of professionals, which include dental hygienists, general practitioners and dental specialists, all remain true to our mission, to provide high-quality dental care at affordable prices.
