Missing Teeth Can Impact More Than Just Your Smile!

Gaps in your smile don’t just affect appearance. They can make eating difficult, impact speech, cause surrounding teeth to shift, and gradually weaken your jawbone. Dental Implant Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Many patients qualify for implants, especially those with healthy gums and sufficient bone structure. During your consultation, we’ll assess your smile and discuss whether implants are the right choice for you.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime. They are one of the most durable and reliable tooth replacement solutions available.

Do dental implants look natural?

Absolutely. At OceanBreeze Family Dental, we design each implant crown to match the shape, color, and appearance of your natural teeth, giving you a seamless and natural-looking result.

Examination, imaging, and suitability

OceanBreeze’s implant evaluation includes an oral examination and digital images. Planning considers available bone, gum health, nearby teeth, and your medical history. Tell the team about smoking, medications, and conditions that could affect healing. Ask which imaging is needed for your case and how it informs the plan. The appropriate technology and any additional imaging depend on the clinical assessment.

How long does implant treatment take?

Implant care usually involves planning, any preparatory treatment, placement, healing, and the final tooth replacement. Healing can take several months or longer; grafting or other care may extend the process. Ask about temporary teeth during healing and the milestones needed before the final crown or bridge. A consultation cannot promise the same schedule for every patient. The FDA explains implant healing, benefits, and risks .

Compare replacement choices

Explore single dental implants for one missing tooth and full arch implant options for more extensive tooth loss. Depending on your situation, dental bridges or dentures may also be worth discussing. Compare surgery, maintenance, the teeth involved, expected visits, and the total estimate. Ask how each option would address your particular eating and appearance concerns.

Understand the complete estimate

Request an itemized estimate covering examination and imaging, extractions or grafting if needed, placement, the abutment, the final restoration, and follow-up care. Long-term care and patient experiences

Implants need daily cleaning and regular professional follow-up. Contact your dental provider if an implant feels loose or painful.
