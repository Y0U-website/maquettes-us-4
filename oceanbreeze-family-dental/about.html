Some Typical Questions

Do you accept dental insurance or offer financing options?

Yes! OceanBreeze Family Dental accepts a variety of insurance plans, including being in-network with Delta Dental among others. We also offer payment flexibility, including financing through providers such as Sunbit and CareCredit to help spread out costs for dental care.

What kinds of services do you provide, and is the practice suitable for all ages?

Our practice offers a wide range of dentistry services, including everything from routine check-ups and children’s dental care to cosmetic services (like veneers and teeth whitening), same-day crowns, implants, emergency dentistry, and more. We treat patients of all ages and strive to create a comfortable, family-friendly environment.

How do I schedule an appointment and what are your office hours?

To schedule an appointment, you can call us at 831-777-6465 or use our online appointment request form here. Our address is 1053 S Green Valley Rd, Watsonville, CA 95076, and our typical hours are Monday to Wednesday from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, and on Thursday & Friday we are open from 7:30 AM until 3:00 PM. We are closed on Saturday and Sunday.
