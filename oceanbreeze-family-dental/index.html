The practice

Dr. Jesse Halbleib welcomes children and adults to OceanBreeze Family Dental at 1053 S Green Valley Road in Watsonville. Explore family dental care , cleanings, crowns, dental implants , cosmetic treatments, and care for dental emergencies.

Get the care you need without the wait. Our advanced technology allows us to complete many treatments in a single visit, making your dental care faster, easier, and more convenient.

At our community-centered practice, your comfort, convenience, and long-term oral health are always our top priorities. We offer flexible scheduling, a welcoming team, and a gentle approach designed to make every visit stress-free.

Whether you’re here for preventive care, dental implants, or a complete smile makeover, we’re committed to providing exceptional results in a warm, friendly environment.

He and his wife, Shannon (BSN, RN), have been married for over 20 years. They have three children, Jesseathan, Braidan, and Shane, as well as two horses, two dogs, three cats, and two guinea pigs.

After graduating from Wilcox High School in Santa Clara, Dr. Halbleib completed his undergraduate degree in biology at the University of the Pacific (UOP) in 1982. He then earned his doctor of dental surgery (DDS) degree from Emory School of Dentistry in Atlanta, Georgia in 1986.
