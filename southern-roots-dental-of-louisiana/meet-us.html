MEET THE FAMILY

At Southern Roots Dental, we strive to provide comfortable and individualized dental care for natural-looking results.

We believe that a healthy smile is a happy smile and will provide the professional care you deserve.

Julie LeBell Simpson, D.D.S.

I was born and raised in the New Orleans area. After attending LSU in Baton Rouge for undergrad, I graduated from LSU School of Dentistry in New Orleans in 2007. Monroe became my home immediately following dental school and I recognized this community as a place I could establish my roots.

When we aren’t trying to keep up with my son John Aubrey, my husband James and I spend our time gardening, cooking, music, and traveling when possible.

Sarah Hummel Gregory, D.D.S.

Monroe has always been my home though I’ve had to relocate a couple of times to achieve my goals. I graduated from Louisiana State University with a Bachelor of Science degree in 2007. I completed my studies in New Orleans at LSU School of Dentistry in 2011. I have a passion for mission trips and have served in both Peru and Haiti where I was able to use my training to meet locals’ dental needs.

I am ecstatic to have the opportunity to start a practice in my hometown and serve this community. My dream has always been to create an atmosphere of comfort and compassion in a dental practice. I want my patients to see their dental experience in a different light. I feel that I have been called to this incredible career where I get to build relationships while also assist in healing and caring for my patients and their individual needs.

Dr. Nicholas Gregory and I were married in 2014 and we have three daughters, Lillian, Eliza and Hazel. I enjoy adventuring with my family, hiking, camping, and surfing the Ouachita River.

January “Jana” Franks, B.S. RDH

I am a native of Rayville Louisiana, but currently reside in Start Louisiana with my husband, our daughter, and our furbabies. I have been in the dental field serving the Monroe area since 2011, starting out as a Dental Assistant, fell in love with the field, and went back to school to receive a degree in Dental Hygiene with a B.S.

I graduated from the University of Louisiana at Monroe, ULM, May of 2019 and knew that I would continue to serve the Monroe area. I absolutely love what I do, being able to care for my patients, provide them with the care and compassion that they deserve, and being able to educate and bring awareness to oral health and disease.

When not working I enjoy spending time with my husband, our daughter, and family, anything outdoors whether it’s hunting or fishing or just sitting outside and reading a book, traveling - the beach is my favorite, concerts, and lazy days binge watching our favorite shows with my husband.
