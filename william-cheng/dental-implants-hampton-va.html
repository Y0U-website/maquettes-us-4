Dental Implants in Hampton, VA

Dental implants are replacements for missing teeth that look and feel natural. They are designed to provide a stable foundation for permanent replacement teeth that function just like your natural teeth.

Procedure

The implants themselves are tiny titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone where teeth are missing. An under-structure is placed on top of the implant, and a replacement tooth is attached to the fixture. Your restoration will look entirely natural and provide functionality that is very much like your natural teeth. It even helps to preserve facial structure by preventing bone deterioration that can occur when teeth are missing.

Following the placement of the implants, the bone begins to grow around the implants and bond with them. When healing is complete, small posts or abutments are attached to the implant. These posts provide stable anchors for the replacement teeth that will replace your missing teeth. When the procedure is over, you will have strong, functional new teeth that will allow you to eat your favorite foods again and smile with confidence.

Importance of Restoring Missing Teeth

It is important to replace missing teeth as soon as possible because the mouth is constantly changing shape over time. Teeth shifting out of place can lead to bite and/or jaw joint issues, which can make eating uncomfortable and cause headaches. It can also change your appearance because your face can start to appear sunken in areas where multiple teeth have been lost. Replacing missing teeth with dental implants is the best way to avoid these complications and enjoy a healthy, confident smile.

How Successful Are Dental Implants?

In short, they are very successful! When done properly by a skilled Dentist Hampton, VA , your dental implant should last a lifetime. The success rate of dental implants is extremely high, and most patients report a high level of satisfaction with their procedure. If you practice good oral hygiene habits, the chances of a successful outcome are even higher.

Dental implants have a very high success rate because of the materials used in their construction. Titanium is used because of its high strength-to-weight ratio and its compatibility with bone, allowing it to be integrated into the jaw without rejection or inflammation. Ceramic is used for the crown portion of the implant to create a strong tooth that looks and feels like real teeth.

Is Dental Implant Surgery Safe?

Dental implants are a safe and highly effective tooth replacement option. The treatment success rate is over 95 percent. A skilled implant dentist performs a comprehensive evaluation to determine if a patient is a good candidate for dental implants and to ensure that there are no underlying oral health issues that need to be addressed prior to surgery. Patients should visit their dentist twice a year for routine checkups and cleanings.

Typically, the surgical portion of the procedure is performed in the dentist’s office under local anesthesia or with intravenous sedation for greater patient comfort. Most patients do not experience pain following this procedure and can return to work the next day.
