About

0 DR. CELIN ARCE Originally from beautiful Costa Rica, Dr. Celin Arce brings a wealth of experience and expertise to the field of dentistry. He is a board-certified prosthodontist - highest achievement earned by only a small number of specialists nationwide- and a former professor at the University of Alabama at Birmingham and the University of Kentucky, where he spent many years teaching and mentoring hundreds of dental professionals.

Dr. Arce earned his dental degree from Universidad Latina of Costa Rica, followed by a residency in Advanced Prosthodontics and a Master of Science in Clinical Dentistry at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics and a Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists.

His passion for dental materials and digital technologies led him to become a Certified Dental Technician (CDT), a unique combination that allows him to bridge clinical practice with the most advanced dental laboratory techniques. This dual expertise enables him to work closely with the top dental laboratories and ceramists in the country. He also serves as a key opinion leader and consultant for several dental companies and lectures regularly both nationally and internationally on topics related to advanced prosthodontics and innovation in dental technology.

Dr. Arce enjoys life with his wife and their two greyhounds, Simon and Luna. In his spare time, he enjoys traveling, photography, and exploring new culinary experiences as a true foodie at heart.

His philosophy in dentistry is to deliver the highest level of care by combining art, science, and passion - all within a kindhearted and patient centered environment.
