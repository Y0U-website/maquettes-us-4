DR. EDWARD P. WYSOCKI

With 11 years of experience, Dr. Wysocki blends Midwestern friendliness with a meticulous eye for aesthetics and a gentle, detail-oriented approach. Originally from Chicago, he creates a dental experience that is both comfortable and elevated—tailored to patients who value clinical excellence and personalized care.

Dr. Wysocki graduated with honors from the University of Chicago, earning a double major in Biology and Comparative Human Development. He went on to complete a Master’s in Cell and Molecular Biology at Tulane University before returning north to attend Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he received both the Thomas B. Hunt and Tufts Merit Scholarships.

Since graduating, Dr. Wysocki has practiced in upscale dental offices across Boston’s South End and Financial District, and later became a partner at a boutique practice in Wayland, MA. Today, he continues to provide highly customized, cosmetic, and biologically sound care with an emphasis on patient comfort, education, and transparency in his private practice in Roxbury.

Dr. Wysocki is known for his calm demeanor, artistic eye, and ability to communicate complex treatment plans in a clear and compassionate way. Whether performing routine care or advanced procedures, his goal is always the same: beautiful, long-lasting results with minimal discomfort.
