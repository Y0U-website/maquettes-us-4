What are Dental Implants?

Beaverton dental implants have become the gold standard in implant dentistry for replacing one or more missing teeth. Unlike dentures or partial dentures, implants do more than fill empty spaces—they function like your natural teeth.

The titanium post acts as an artificial tooth root, fusing with your jawbone through oral surgery.

This creates a sturdy base for a custom crown that looks and feels like your real teeth.

No other dental solutions for missing teeth provide the same durability, function, and comfort. With dental implants, you can restore your smile, eat your favorite foods, and protect your oral health for years to come.

A dental implant consists of three important parts:

  • Post –This titanium-root replacement is lightweight and extremely strong.
  • Abutment –Securely attaches the post to the crown.
  • Crown –A natural-looking custom-crafted restoration.

FAQs About Beaverton Dental Implants

  • Who can get a dental implant? Almost anyone who has missing teeth. It’s an excellent option for adults who have healthy bone and gum tissue to support an implant. Dental implants are also a smart option for children who have lost permanent teeth from injury or accident. Once a child’s facial growth is complete, an implant can be placed, around age 16 for girls and age 18 for boys. Our Beaverton dentist, Dr. da Costa, will perform a thorough examination and let you know if implants are the right treatment for you.
  • Are dental implants safe? Yes. Unlike bridges or dentures , implants do not encourage bone loss. Instead, they stimulate bone regeneration. And unlike your real teeth, implants are made from materials that do not decay or deteriorate.
  • Do dental implants require any special care? Dental implants are cared for the same way you care for your natural teeth. While the material of your dental crown will not decay, it is essential to keep the gums surrounding your implant healthy. A good routine of brushing and flossing and seeing your dentist at scheduled visits will keep your implants healthy and functional for decades.
