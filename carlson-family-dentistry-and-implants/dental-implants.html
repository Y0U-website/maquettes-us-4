Dental Implants in Littleton

  • Natural look and feel: Implants look and function just like your natural teeth, boosting your confidence and improving your smile.
  • Improved oral health: Implants stimulate jawbone growth, preventing bone loss and maintaining a healthy facial structure.
  • Comfortable and secure: Unlike dentures, implants are permanently anchored, eliminating discomfort and the worry of them slipping.
  • Enhanced chewing ability: Dental implants restore full chewing power, allowing you to enjoy a wider variety of foods.

The Dental Implant Process at Carlson Family Dentistry & Implants

Our experienced dentists, Dr. Grant, Dr. Claire, and Dr. Coleman, will guide you through every step of the dental implant process at our Littleton, CO office. Here&#x27;s a general overview:

  • Consultation: We begin with a comprehensive consultation to discuss your goals, examine your oral health, and determine if implants are the right solution for you.
  • Implant Placement: During a surgical procedure, the implant is placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing Period: The jawbone fuses with the implant through a process called osseointegration. This ensures a strong foundation for the restoration.
  • Abutment and Crown Placement: Once healed, an abutment (connector) is placed on the implant. A custom-made crown is then attached to the abutment, perfectly matching your natural teeth.
