Are You a Candidate for a Single Implant?

Ideal candidates for a single tooth implant:

Before treatment, we’ll use advanced imaging like cone beam scans and digital scanning to evaluate your bone structure and create a precise treatment plan.

  • Are missing one tooth or need one extracted
  • Have sufficient jawbone density, or are candidates for bone grafting
  • Want a permanent, non-removable solution
  • Are in generally good oral and overall health

Frequently Asked Questions About Single Tooth Implants

  • How long does a single dental implant last?: With proper oral hygiene and routine dental cleanings , implants can last 20+ years, often a lifetime.
  • Is the procedure painful?: Implant placement is done under local anesthesia or IV sedation for comfort. Most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery.
  • How long does the process take?: It typically takes several months from implant placement to final crown placement, depending on healing time and bone integration.
  • Can I get a tooth implant immediately after extraction?: In some cases, yes. Immediate placement may be possible depending on your bone condition and oral health.
  • What if I have bone loss?: Minor bone loss can often be corrected with a bone graft prior to or during implant placement.
