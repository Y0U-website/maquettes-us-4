Signs You May Need an Implant Restoration

You might need implant restoration services if you notice any of the following:

We use advanced diagnostics like digital X-rays , intraoral cameras , and digital scanning to assess the condition of your implant and restoration. If necessary, we’ll coordinate with your implant surgeon to ensure a fully integrated treatment plan.

  • A loose or shifting implant crown
  • Chipping, cracks, or discoloration in the restoration
  • Pain or discomfort around the implant site
  • Difficulty chewing or biting
  • Gum irritation near the implant

Frequently Asked Questions About Implant Restoration

  • Can you restore implants placed at another office?: Yes. We regularly restore implants that were placed by outside providers and will assess the restoration and implant health before recommending treatment.
  • How long do implant crowns last?: Implant-supported crowns typically last 10–15 years or longer with proper care, though materials and bite forces can affect their lifespan.
  • Is implant restoration painful?: Restorative procedures are generally non-invasive and involve minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia is used when necessary.
  • What if the implant itself is damaged?: If the implant post is compromised, we’ll refer you to a specialist and work closely with them to coordinate your care and provide a new restoration afterward.
  • Can implant restorations be repaired or must they be replaced?: Minor damage may be repaired in-office, but full replacement is often required for long-term success and aesthetics.
