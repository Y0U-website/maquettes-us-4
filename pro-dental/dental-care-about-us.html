About Us

Our ultimate goal here at Pro-Dental is to save your smile. Oral health issues can have detrimental effects on your overall health. Apparently, poor oral care habits can lead to harmful bacteria entering your bloodstream. These harmful bacteria can then spread throughout your body and put you at risk for heart diseases, among other illnesses.

We don’t want that to happen to you. That is why we work to give you timely treatment to keep you feeling your best. And when you feel good, of course, you smile.

Our Mission and Vision Statement

Pro-Dental commits itself to deliver excellence in dental care in Central Texas and surrounding areas. We are dedicated to helping our clients improve their smiles. We work to help people in the community become more confident with their smiles. We want to foster a positive attitude towards oral health in the people we serve, especially children.
