The practice

Why choose a Pediatric Dentist? Get the best treatment for your child by putting them in the care of a pediatric dental specialist with extensive training in how to best serve your child and their dental needs.

Pediatric dentists are the pediatricians of dentistry. A pediatric dentist has two to three years specialty training following dental school. Pediatric dentists limit their practice to treating infants and children through adolescence, including those with special health needs. We Specialize In: Preventative Dentistry It is our goal to give our patients the best chance at having a healthy mouth. We are dedicated to teaching you about the preventive care you can do at home to keep your child’s mouth as healthy as possible.

Dental sealant is a plastic coating painted on your teeth. It attaches to your child’s teeth, smoothing the rough edges, depressions, and grooves that are typically found on teeth.

We are usually able to fill in broken teeth to make them look like normal teeth. We even use a cement that is the color of your child’s teeth. If that does not work, we offer veneers and caps to cover up any broken teeth.
