Preventive Treatments We Offer

Our team provides preventive treatments designed to strengthen your teeth and protect against decay. These treatments are quick, comfortable, and highly effective when combined with routine dental care.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride helps strengthen tooth enamel and makes it more resistant to acid attacks. Professional fluoride treatments provide added protection, especially for patients at higher risk of cavities.

Dental Sealants

Sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth where cavities are most likely to form. They act as a barrier against bacteria and food particles, helping prevent decay in hard-to-clean areas.

Daily Habits That Make a Difference

Preventing cavities does not stop at the dental office. Good daily habits play a major role in keeping your teeth healthy between visits.

  • Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth
  • Limit sugary snacks and drinks
  • Stay consistent with routine dental visits

Personalized Cavity Prevention

Every patient has different needs when it comes to cavity prevention. At Aurora Dental Group, we take the time to evaluate your oral health, risk factors, and lifestyle to recommend the right combination of treatments and habits.

By focusing on prevention, we help you avoid future dental issues and maintain a healthier, more confident smile.
