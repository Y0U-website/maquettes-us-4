Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement because they restore both function and appearance while supporting long-term oral health.

  • Look and feel like natural teeth
  • Restore full chewing ability
  • Help preserve jawbone structure
  • Do not rely on surrounding teeth for support
  • Designed to last for many years with proper care

The Dental Implant Process

The implant process is completed in stages to ensure proper healing and long-term success. We will guide you through each step and create a personalized treatment plan based on your needs.

Step 1: Consultation and Planning

We begin with a comprehensive evaluation, including imaging and a discussion of your goals. This allows us to determine if dental implants are the right option for you.

Step 2: Implant Placement

The implant is carefully placed into the jawbone, where it will serve as the foundation for your new tooth.

Step 3: Healing and Integration

Over time, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. This step is essential for long-term stability.

Step 4: Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, a custom crown, bridge, or restoration is placed on the implant to restore your smile.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many patients are good candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have healthy gums, sufficient bone structure, and are in overall good health. Even if you have been told you are not a candidate in the past, advancements in implant dentistry may provide additional options.

We will evaluate your individual situation and recommend the best approach for your long-term oral health.
