Web accessibility statement

  • A skip to content link is the first thing in the tab order on every page.
  • Photographs carry descriptive alternative text, and decorative shapes are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and button colors were checked for contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Buttons, menu items and links in the navigation are sized for a finger, not just a mouse pointer.
  • Headings run in order so a screen reader can move through a page by structure.

Known limitations

Being honest about the gaps matters more than a badge. As of today there has been no independent audit and no testing session with people who use assistive technology. Until that happens, the practice cannot list every barrier that exists on the site, only the ones people report.

How to report a barrier

Call (623) 320-1222 or email hello@romansorthodontics.com. Tell us the page you were on and what you were trying to do. Anything you can do on this site can also be done by phone, including asking for a consultation, so nothing has to wait for a fix.

If you use a telecommunications relay service, dial 711 and give the operator the office number above.

Reports come straight to the practice rather than to a web team, and are read by the office staff who answer the phone and the email above.

We respond at the earliest convenience. If something on this site is stopping you booking or finding what you need, calling the office is the fastest route and someone can take the information over the phone instead.
