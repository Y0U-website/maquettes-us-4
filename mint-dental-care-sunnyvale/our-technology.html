Technology

To ensure you get nothing but the highest quality of dental care, we’ve invested in state-of-the-art technology that pushes the boundaries of what dentistry can do. Never before has it been easier and more comfortable to get in-office treatments that save you time and money. For our dental team at Mint Dental Care, it’s not about getting you in and out as quick as we can. It’s about providing the kind of care we can proudly stand behind. With our technology, you can rest assured that your smile is in good hands. Leading-edge dentistry is within your grasp.

Some of our most popular technology includes:

  • Digital radiographs to show areas of concern, such as dental caries (cavities) and periodontitis (gum disease)
  • Intraoral photos so patients can see exactly what we see, including before, during, and after photos of treatment
  • Trios 5 scanner (intraoral scanner)
  • Straumann P20+ printer (3D printer)
  • BLU dental microlaser (laser)
