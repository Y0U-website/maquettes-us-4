Composite fillings are made from a resin material that bonds directly to your tooth. Unlike metal fillings, composite resin is tooth-colored, ensuring that your dental work is virtually invisible. It is available in multiple shades, allowing it to seamlessly match the natural color of your teeth. This makes composite fillings an excellent choice for repairing cavities or addressing minor cosmetic concerns.

The process of placing a composite filling is straightforward and typically completed in a single visit:

1. Preparation: The affected tooth is numbed to ensure your comfort during the procedure. Any decayed or damaged areas are carefully removed.

2. Bonding: To enhance the bond between the tooth and the filling, a special substance is applied to the prepared area. This creates a strong foundation for the composite material.

3. Filling Application: The composite resin is applied in thin layers, gradually building the filling to its full shape. Each layer is hardened using a special curing light.

4. Finishing Touches: Once the filling is fully formed, it is shaped, smoothed, and polished. This ensures a natural look and a comfortable fit for your bite.

Choosing composite fillings offers several advantages:

They blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

The bonding process strengthens your tooth structure.

Less healthy tooth material needs to be removed compared to amalgam fillings.

Root canal therapy is a vital procedure designed to save an infected tooth that has been damaged due to deep cavities, trauma, or cracks. Dr. Nicholas Withem, our trusted dentist, is committed to providing this essential treatment with precision and care, ensuring your oral health and comfort are our top priorities.

The purpose of root canal therapy is to remove the damaged pulp and nerves from the tooth’s inner chamber, effectively eliminating infection and pain. While the pulp is essential during the tooth’s development, it is not required for the tooth’s survival after maturity. This means the tooth can function perfectly well without it, thanks to the nourishment provided by surrounding tissues. Without timely treatment, however, the infection can spread, potentially leading to severe complications like swelling, jawbone damage, or even tooth loss.

Dr. Nicholas Withem will recommend root canal therapy if you experience symptoms such as:

- Severe tooth pain during chewing or biting.

- Persistent toothaches that wake you up at night.

- Sensitivity to hot or cold temperatures, with the sensitivity lingering for a prolonged period.

- Swollen or tender gums near the affected tooth.
