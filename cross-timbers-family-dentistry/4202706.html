Meet Dr. Rob and Dr. Crista Harrison

Dr. Crista was born in 1981 and grew up in Stephenville, Texas. She attended Texas A&M University, where she received her B.S. in Biology in 2003. Then she went on to Baylor College of Dentistry in Dallas, Texas, where she met Dr. Rob. After dental school, she and Dr. Rob returned to her hometown, Stephenville, to work in the well-established dental practice of her late father, Dr. Stephen Carroll, who had been serving the area as a dentist for 26 years.

They are blessed to have five fabulous kids: Rhett, Henry, Samuel, Jack, and Mabel. Dr. Crista enjoys reading with her kids, playing board games with her family, and cooking for all of them. She also enjoys sewing, scrapbooking, and running. Dr. Rob’s hobby interests include computers, astronomy, radio-controlled cars and aircraft, music (especially drumming), and board games. It’s no surprise his children like most of his hobbies too! Dr. Crista and Dr. Rob are both born-again Christians whose mission in life is to serve and love others as Jesus Christ did.
