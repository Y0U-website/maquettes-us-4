Meet Dr. Vincent Yang

Dr. Yang completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry, one of the most respected dental programs in the country, and went on to complete his General Practice Residency at the West Los Angeles Veterans Affairs Dental Services program. That experience sharpened his clinical skills across a wide range of complex cases and reinforced his commitment to treating every patient with the thoroughness and respect they deserve.

At Dental Duo & Co., Dr. Yang&#x27;s areas of focus include extractions, dental implants, oral surgery, and bone grafting. He brings a precise, engineering-minded approach to every procedure, always thinking about how today&#x27;s care sets the foundation for your long-term health. He uses advanced technology including CBCT imaging and intraoral scanners to ensure accurate diagnoses and thoughtful treatment planning from the start.

Outside of the office, Dr. Yang enjoys playing volleyball, gaming, and spending time with his family. He is a proud member of the California Dental Association (CDA), American Dental Association (ADA), the Orange County Dental Society (OCDS), and the San Gabriel Valley Dental Society (SGVDS).

The people behind your care

A team that feels like family

From the moment you call or walk through our door, you&#x27;ll be greeted by a team that genuinely cares about making your experience a great one. Our team is warm, attentive, and here to make sure every visit feels comfortable, clear, and taken care of. We speak English, Mandarin, and Spanish, so you can always feel at home.

From the moment you contact us to the moment you leave, our goal is to make sure you feel informed, comfortable, and genuinely cared for. Comfort and care, from the waiting room to the treatment chair

We&#x27;ve put thought into every corner of our office because we believe the experience of coming to the dentist should feel welcoming, not stressful. From the amenities in our reception area to the technology we use during your treatment, everything is here to make your visit more comfortable, more efficient, and more you.

Little comforts that make a big difference

We want you to feel at ease the moment you walk in. Our office is stocked with thoughtful touches designed to help you relax, settle in, and feel taken care of before your name is even called.

Coffee, tea & water station Help yourself to a warm cup of coffee, tea, or a glass of water when you arrive. We want your visit to feel welcoming from the very first moment, and a little hospitality goes a long way.

Earplugs For patients who prefer a quieter experience, we have earplugs available to help you tune out any background noise during your appointment. Sometimes a small thing makes all the difference.

Support pillows We offer support pillows to keep you comfortable during longer appointments. Your physical comfort matters to us just as much as your dental health.

Shades and eye protection Bright dental lights can be uncomfortable, so we provide shades and eye protection to shield your eyes during treatment. It&#x27;s a small detail that makes the whole experience more pleasant.

Lip balm Keeping your lips comfortable during treatment is something we&#x27;ve thought about too. We have lip balm on hand so you can stay comfortable from start to finish.

Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) For patients who experience dental anxiety, we offer nitrous oxide to help you relax during treatment. It&#x27;s a safe, effective, and fast-acting option that wears off quickly so you can get on with your day.

Technology that works as hard as we do

We believe that better tools lead to better care. The technology we use at Dental Duo & Co. allows us to diagnose with greater precision, plan more predictable treatment outcomes, and deliver results that are minimally invasive and built to last.

3D intraoral scanner We use one of the most advanced intraoral scanners available, allowing us to take highly accurate digital scans of your teeth without the mess or discomfort of traditional impressions. It is also used as a part of our Wellness Scans, giving us the ability to track dental health changes over time, allowing you an even deeper insight into your oral health. This technology helps us plan and deliver restorations, aligners, and other treatments with greater precision and efficiency.

Intraoral camera Our intraoral camera lets us show you exactly what we see during your exam, giving you a clear, real-time view of your oral health. Being able to see your own teeth up close helps you understand your treatment needs and feel confident in the recommendations we make.

CBCT imaging Cone Beam CT (CBCT) imaging gives us a detailed, three-dimensional view of your teeth, roots, jaw, and bone structure that traditional X-rays simply can&#x27;t provide. This level of detail is especially important for implant planning, surgical procedures, and complex diagnoses, allowing us to develop the most accurate and safe treatment plan possible.

Soft-tissue laser Our soft-tissue laser allows us to perform certain gum-related procedures with greater precision and less discomfort than traditional methods. Laser treatment typically means less bleeding, faster healing, and a more comfortable recovery, which is a win for everyone.
