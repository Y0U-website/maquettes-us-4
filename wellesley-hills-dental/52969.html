Who Is a Good Dental Implant Candidate?

Sadly, dental implants aren’t appropriate for every patient. Among the factors influencing implant eligibility are:

  • Jawbone health A recipient must have a fully developed and strong jawbone that is capable of supporting an implant.
  • Periodontal health For proper implant support, an individual’s gums must be healthy and gum disease-free.
  • Overall health Systemic health issues like diabetes can adversely affect implant success by hindering healing.

Understanding Dental Implant Placement

Getting dental implants involves a series of steps spanning multiple visits and several months. Here’s a detailed look at the complete process!

  • Consultation An assessment of your oral health, including full-mouth x-rays, to determine suitability and create a treatment plan.
  • Implant placement Surgical insertion of a tiny titanium peg (the implant) into the jawbone via a small incision in the gums, all under local anesthesia to ensure patient comfort.
  • Osseointegration A several-month-long healing period during which the implant fuses permanently with the jawbone.
  • Abutment and restoration After osseointegration, your dentist attaches a connector (abutment), which will support a permanent dental restoration like a dental crown.
