Our Process

First, we’ll apply Vitamin E to your gums and lips to keep them from drying out.

Next, we’ll insert a cheek retractor and examine your mouth for any problem areas as well as assess what shade your teeth are currently at.

Then, we’ll coat your teeth with a hydrogen peroxide gel and place you under an LED light for 40-60 minutes. We will rinse and reapply the gel halfway between the treatment.

After the treatment is complete, you will rinse your mouth and be ready to shine.

Who Should Whiten Their Teeth

Anyone who wants a brighter smile. The only contraindications to teeth whitening and those who should not whiten are anyone experiencing current teeth pain, cavities, exposed roots or are currently undergoing dental work.

If you are planning on having permanent dental work done including crowns, bonding, bridges or artificial teeth please make sure to whiten your teeth before you have these permanently cemented in. Your dentist will match your artificial tooth shade to your current tooth shade so you want them to be at their brightest shade possible. It is much harder to change the color of the artificial material
