Who Can Get Implants? Dental implants are suitable for individuals missing one or more teeth. Factors to consider include:

Health Status

General health is important for implant success. Share any health conditions with your provider.

Bone Density

Adequate jawbone density is required; if needed, a bone graft can prepare the area.

Smoking

Smoking can impact the healing process, so quitting is advised for better outcomes.

Oral Hygiene

Good oral hygiene practices are essential for implant longevity.
