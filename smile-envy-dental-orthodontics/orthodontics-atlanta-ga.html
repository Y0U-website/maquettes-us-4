Who Can Benefit from Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic care is available for both children and adults at Smile Envy Dental & Orthodontics.

Whether you need to improve the alignment of your teeth or correct jaw positioning, our team will create a personalized treatment plan to enhance your smile and improve your dental health.

Children

Treatment often begins around age 12, depending on individual dental needs.

Adults

Orthodontic treatment can start at any age, provided teeth and gums are healthy.

Orthodontic Services We Offer

At Smile Envy Dental Orthodontics, our orthodontist provides a variety of treatment options to suit your unique needs, including: Braces Traditional braces consist of brackets and wires that gradually move your teeth into the correct position.

Retainers

Clear, removable retainers are designed to maintain the position of your teeth after treatment.

Aligners

Custom-made plastic trays that fit over your teeth and gently shift them over time.

Mouthguards

Protective devices for athletes to prevent dental injuries during sports.

Orthodontic Surgery

Jaw correction surgery to address alignment issues affecting your bite, chewing, and speech.
