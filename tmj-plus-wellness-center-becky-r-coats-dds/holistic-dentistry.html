Holistic Dentistry FAQs

  • Are you SMART certified (or higher) by the IAOMT? Dr. Coats is SMART certified and a member of IAOMT.
  • Are you a mercury-safe dental office? Yes, we have several steps in place for the safe removal of mercury fillings, including dental dams and isolation, air quality control and HEPA filters, a total air purification system, and Dentivac.
  • Do you avoid using fluoride treatments? We do not offer fluoride treatments at our office.
  • Do you offer root canal alternatives? We do not perform root canals, but instead utilize other methods of treatment. Ozone plays a large part in this, depending on the diagnosis.
  • Do you offer non-metal options for implants? We use Z-Systems implants, which are metal-free.
  • Do you use ozone in your office? Every patient does a pre-rinse with ozonated water to kill viruses, fungi, and bacteria. We use ozone in almost every procedure, including fillings, oral surgery, dental cavitation removal, abscesses, whitening, ozone periodontal trays and more.
  • Is your filling material BPA free? Yes, we use BPA-free composite fillings.
  • Do you offer biocompatibility testing when placing a crown, implant, or filling material? Yes, we use an outside lab for biocompatibility testing. There is an additional lab cost if you would like to test materials.
  • Do you check oral galvanism of metal restorations? Dr. Coats does not perform this test, but we have another biological dentist and naturopath in the office who can when needed.
  • Do you use digital x-rays? Yes, we offer digital x-rays.
  • Do your CT scans include images of upper and lower arches? CBCT is important because it allows us to evaluate maxillary sinuses, TMJ, and the airway; both dental arches must be evaluated in order to give us a complete picture of your oral health.
  • Do you send the CT scan to a radiologist for reading? Yes, CT scans are sent to radiologists.
  • Do you recommend nutritional support or dietary changes? Nutrition is an important part of dental health, as well as overall health. Most of our referrals are from other professionals, so many patients already have a health coach, naturopath, or wellness provider such as a nutritionist that we will work together with as a team. If not, then we will refer you to a provider who can support the nutritional needs Dr. Coats identifies based on your oral health.
  • Do you work with other holistic practitioners? TMJ Plus is a wellness center, so we have many professionals in our network to support patients with a variety of issues and health concerns. Within the TMJ Plus Wellness building, we have access to a biological dentist, speech therapist, osteopath physician, naturopath, lymphatic specialist, and others, allowing us to work using a team approach when needed. On the other hand, a patient might already have a team of professionals in place, in which case TMJ Plus will work with their existing providers.
