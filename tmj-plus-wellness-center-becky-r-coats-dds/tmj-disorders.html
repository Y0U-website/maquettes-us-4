What type of solutions are available for TMJ disorders?

The first step in resolving your pain and other TMJ problems can include one or a combination of these methods for lasting relief:

A sphenopalatine ganglion (SPG) nerve block is a popular non-surgical treatment for migraines and acute head pain. The SPG is a collection of nerves in the pterygopalatine fossa, an inverted cone-shaped cavity located behind the nose, that carries sensory information such as pain to the brain.

The SPG block is a single treatment that will disrupt those pain messages and provide you with personalized care and immediate relief from headaches or TMJ pain.
