Dental implants are one of the most popular and proven ways to replace missing teeth. Unlike other types of tooth replacements that are removable or require the adjacent teeth to be cut down, implants are completely independent restorations that don’t rely on other teeth for support. When fully integrated, today’s implants are a permanent part of your smile.
