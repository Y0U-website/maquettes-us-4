New Patients at Tatum Dentistry in Charleston, SC

Two-hour first visits, no judgment, and a team that will make you feel like you have been coming here for years.

What to Expect at Your First Visit

Give Us a Call The easiest way to start is to pick up the phone. We will get to know you a little, find a time that works, and make sure you have everything you need before you walk in. Our schedule is built for real life, including select Saturday hours. Call us at 843-577-6453

Arrive and Settle In When you walk through the front door, you’ll be greeted by name. Help yourself to filtered water and settle into a comfortable chair in our calm, cozy reception area. We don’t keep people waiting long your visit begins shortly after you arrive, in a space designed to help you exhale before it even starts.

Be in the Driver’s Seat Your first appointment is two hours, because thorough care takes time and you deserve all of it. You’ll get a digital scan of your teeth, a 3D image so we can look at your airway and bite, and a real chance to see your own mouth on the screen. Then we’ll talk it through together you’ll see exactly what we see, share what is bothering you, and decide what to do and what can wait.
