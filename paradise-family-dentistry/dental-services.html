Family Dentistry in Rocklin, CA

Paradise Family Dentistry strives to provide excellent dental services with a focus on patient comfort and satisfaction. We offer a variety of treatments, from preventive care, such as cleanings and checkups, to restorative care, such as fillings and crowns.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is the marriage of art and science. All our cosmetic procedures are designed to help you achieve the beautiful smile you desire. We offer teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, porcelain dental crowns, dental bridges, bonding, shaping & contouring. Click on cosmetic treatments to see more information.

Laser Dentistry

Laser dentistry allows us to provide less invasive treatments that are more comfortable for our patients and produce superior results. Thanks to our Fotona LightWalker, we offer snoring/sleep apnea treatment, facial rejuvenation, lip laser treatment, photobiomodulation, and non-surgical gum treatment. Click on laser treatments to see more information.

Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry is critical to keep your mouth healthy and avoid common oral health issues like tooth decay and gum disease. We offer dental cleanings, fillings, fluoride treatments, children’s hygiene, and periodontal care. Click on preventive treatments to see more information.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry is any dental work needed to manage oral health problems and restore the function and look of teeth. We offer oral surgery/extractions, dental implants, complete and partial dentures, dental bridges, porcelain crowns, and root canals. Click on restorative treatments to see more information.
