Communication is Key!

It’s important to our doctors and staff that each patient feel comfortable during their visit. With a diverse team like ours, you’ll never have a problem communicating. We have staff members who are fluent in Japanese, Spanish, Russian and Tagalog. If you need someone in our office to translate, just give us a call and we’d be happy to accommodate you!

We’re Committed to Going Green!

At Carmel Mountain Ranch Dental Care, we pride ourselves on not only providing outstanding preventive dentistry services to our customers, but on being champions of the environment. At our office, we are committed to providing a brighter teeth whitening future for generations to come!

How we have continued to "go green" ‍ ‍

First, we became an amalgam-free office, in order to protect our employees and patients from mercury exposure.

Next, we became one of the first dental practices in San Diego to have digital x-rays, eliminating the need for toxic chemicals and reducing radiation exposure for our patients.

Then, we went paperless. We have all our patient charts digital. And now we are proud to announce we are the first dental office in the area to go solar!
