About BH Dental NYC

General and Cosmetic Dentistry located in Harlem, New York and Brooklyn, NY

BH Dental NY, with locations in the Morningside Heights neighborhood of Harlem and in Brooklyn, is a New York City-based dental practice offering patient-centered general and cosmetic dentistry to patients of all ages in Manhattan and Brooklyn. Highly trained dentist Leslie Azran, DDS, and the rest of the team are always pursuing advanced continuing education, and every dentist in the practice has at least 10 years of experience treating and repairing oral health issues.

Patients at BH Dental NY enjoy a welcoming environment where their providers truly care about their health. The team provides in-depth examinations to detect issues in need of treatments like root cana l therapy and emergency dentistry.

The BH Dental NY team is particularly well-versed in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, offering an array of options for effective treatment. Patients can get the repairs they need using dentures , crowns and bridges , veneers , and lifelong dental implants .

On the cosmetic side, patients at BH Dental NY can get effective teeth whitening no matter the level of stains or discoloration they have on their teeth. BH Dental NY also specializes in using Invisalign to address tooth alignment issues like overbite or underbite.

Dr. Azran and the team currently welcome new and returning patients for top-tier dental care in Harlem and Brooklyn, New York City. Book an appointment over the phone or online right away.
