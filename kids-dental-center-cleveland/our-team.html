Racheal joined Kids Dental Center in June 2023 and became Office Manager in October 2023. Outside of work, she enjoys family time, cheering on her son in sports, running 5Ks, and working out. After witnessing her mother experience a lack of dental education and support, Dr. Rad dedicated her career to helping children and families feel informed, comfortable, and confident about their dental care. She is passionate about creating a safe, fun, and welcoming environment where children can receive quality dental care while parents and children alike can learn about the importance of oral health.

Dr. Rad believes that dentistry should never be scary! Her goal is to help eliminate the fear often associated with visiting the dentist by making every appointment a positive, engaging, and enjoyable experience.

Outside of the office, Dr. Rad is an avid reader and can often be found finishing at least three books a week! She also loves the color pink and enjoys dressing up in fun, special outfits to bring extra excitement, smiles, and personality to the dental office.

Dr. Rad is excited to help every child feel comfortable, cared for, and proud of their smile !
