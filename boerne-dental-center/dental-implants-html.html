What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is actually a very small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone to recreate the root structure of a tooth. There, it firmly bonds to the surrounding bone (because titanium is very biocompatible with the human body), and it is topped with a new restoration like a crown or denture. Most other replacements only bring back the upper portion of a tooth, but implants are unique because they restore an entire tooth from bottom to top.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Dental implants involve a more extensive process compared to traditional restorations like bridges and dentures, as they’re the only tooth-replacement option that replaces the roots of missing teeth. In general, the entire process can take several months to a year to complete, which is still a short period to receive a reliable solution that can potentially last you the rest of your life. During your consultation, we’ll give you a more specific timeline of your treatment and what to expect. Until then, here are the four essential steps for the procedure and how dental implants work in Boerne.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Our team will first need to verify that you’re eligible for dental implant treatment. Fortunately, nearly any healthy adult who’s missing one or several teeth can be a good candidate for these titanium posts. That said, we’ll need to confirm that you don’t have any signs of oral health issues like dental decay, gingivitis, and insufficient jawbone density. If left untreated, these problems can cause your dental implants to fail later on. Our team will ensure to get you back on track with good oral health before moving on with your personalized treatment plan.

Dental Implant Surgery

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

One of the things that make dental implants so successful is the osseointegration process. This is when your dental implants will begin to bond with your jawbone tissue, forming a stable, natural-feeling foundation for your restorations. Although each patient is different, this portion of the treatment usually takes anywhere between four and six months.

Once you’ve properly recovered, you’ll come back to our office so that we can attach your abutments. These are small connector pieces that will link your new teeth to your implants. Afterward, we’ll take impressions of these areas and start fabricating your custom-designed restorations as your gum tissue heals over the following few weeks.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

You will return to our office for the final stage of your dental implant treatment. This appointment will involve anchoring your new restoration (crown, bridge, partial, or full denture) onto your titanium posts. Our team will take the proper time to ensure your bite is comfortable and that you’re satisfied with your results. You can also feel free to ask any questions you might have and we’ll be happy to answer. Then, you’ll be able to fully enjoy your newly restored pearly whites!

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Whether you’re missing a single tooth, multiple teeth, or all of them, dental implants are a viable option to bring back your smile. Our team can pair them with different types of restorations to meet the unique needs of each patient:

Missing Single Tooth

A dental implant root is placed between two healthy teeth and attached to an all-porcelain dental crown. Unlike a traditional bridge, this crown will be self-supporting and not put pressure on the nearby teeth, leading to a more comfortable fit and better longevity.

Missing Multiple Teeth

A small number of implants can be used to replace several teeth at once when attached to a partial denture or bridge. This is much more cost-effective versus using implants for each individual tooth, plus these prosthetics are much more stable compared to their removable counterparts.

Missing All Teeth

With just four to six implants placed along the jawbone, we can bring back an entire row of teeth using a full denture. Compared to regular dentures, implant-retained ones restore much more bite strength, plus they can be trusted to stay rock-solid and never shift unexpectedly.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are a wonderful investment with the proper care and maintenance, and our team at Boerne Dental wants to make sure that you’re able to get the most out of your new and improved smile. Before you leave our office, we’ll make sure that you have all of the information you need to make the best choices for your dental implants. We’ve also included some helpful tips below so you can continue enjoying your smile more than 10 or even 20 years down the road.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Just like with your natural teeth, dental hygiene is a top priority for dental implant care in Boerne. It’s crucial to brush twice a day and floss daily, making sure that plaque and tartar don’t have an opportunity to build-up and lead to gum disease or other common oral health issues that can compromise your replacement teeth.

Maintain a Balanced Diet

If you love to snack on foods that are processed and high in carbohydrates and sugars, it may be best to make some substitutions with your new dental implants. These foods can fuel harmful oral bacteria, increasing your chances of developing gum infections, chronic bad breath, and other symptoms.

Break Bad Habits

Do you smoke or chew tobacco, like to crunch on ice in your drinks, or use your mouth to open packages and other items? It’s best to be mindful of all the bad oral habits you do that could compromise the success of your rehabilitated smile and find ways to break them.

Protect Your Dental Implants

While participating in any sport that could cause damage to your mouth, it’s important to wear a mouthguard that’s custom-fitted to provide you with maximized protection. Similarly, if you grind and clench your teeth at night, invest in a customized nightguard to keep the constant pressure and force from causing one of your dental implants to become loose or even fall out.

Schedule Routine Checkups & Cleanings

Along the same lines as maintaining good oral hygiene at home, it’s still important to visit your implant dentist in Boerne every six months for routine checkups and cleanings . During these appointments, our team will have a chance to make sure that all of your replacement teeth are secure and healthy. If we do find any issues developing, these regular appointments allow us to treat them in their early stages, before they’ve had a chance to compromise your replacement teeth.

Dental Implant FAQs

Why Do I Need a Bone Graft?

When you’ve undergone tooth loss, especially for a prolonged period of time, your jawbone gradually begins to deteriorate in that area. This is because it’s no longer being stimulated by the root of your lost tooth through biting and chewing. As a result, your jawbone is no longer strong enough to support a dental implant. For this reason, we can graft artificial bone material onto your jawbone. After waiting a few months for your bone to become stronger, it should be fortified enough to provide support for as many dental implants as you need to rebuild your smile.

Does It Hurt to Get Dental Implants?

Believe it or not, the majority of our patients report very little discomfort during the implant placement process. This is because before we place the implants, we use plenty of local anesthetic to ensure you don’t feel a thing. Because it is a kind of oral surgery, you can expect to deal with some minor soreness and swelling for a few days following your procedure. Fortunately, this can be easily managed by taking your prescribed pain medication and applying a cold compress to your face for 10 minutes at a time.

Will I Have to See an Outside Specialist to Get My Implants?

Not at all! Dr. Luttrell , your trusted dentist in Boerne, is more than qualified to both place and restore dental implants, since he completed a General Practice Residency and Fellowship in Oral Surgery. He has more than 25 years of experience in oral surgery, which includes placing implants. You can rest easy knowing that your new smile is in his capable hands!

How Long Will It Take for My Dental Implants to Heal?

The answer to this question varies from person to person. It depends on whether you’ll need any preliminary procedures, like a bone graft or gum disease treatment. Those treatments might add a few months to your total healing time before you can receive your new teeth.

In general, it takes an average of three to six months for your implants to fully integrate with your jawbone and essentially become a part of your own anatomy. While your soft tissue may heal after just a couple of weeks, it takes longer for the bone to heal.

I Smoke Can I Still Get Dental Implants?

Yes, but there are some risks involved. Tobacco use severely slows down the oral healing process that are crucial to the implant integration process. Because of this, you are more likely to experience implant failure. If you currently smoke or chew tobacco and are considering dental implants, we strongly advise that you quit beforehand.
