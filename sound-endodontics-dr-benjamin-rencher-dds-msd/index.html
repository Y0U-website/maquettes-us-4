The practice

We are specialists members of the American Association of Endodontists and look forward to being of service to you.

Dr. Rencher received his DDS from the University of Washington where he excelled in restorative, preventative, and emergency dentistry. He continued his education by pursuing his passion for endodontics and received his Master of Science degree and Certificate in Endodontics from the University of Washington. He is highly trained in both non-surgical and surgical endodontic procedures.

Located in Poulsbo, our office boasts stunning views of Liberty Bay and the cascade mountains.

Our office uses advanced technology to aid in accurate diagnosis, gentle and effective treatment, while delivering an extraordinary experience for our patients.

Our mission as an endodontic practice is to provide exceptional and personalized care to each of our patients. We strive to deliver the highest quality root canal treatments in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Our goal is to ensure that every patient feels heard, understood, and informed about their treatment options.

We believe in using the latest technologies and techniques to provide conservative, effective, and long-lasting solutions for our patients’ dental needs. Our commitment to education and ongoing training allows us to stay at the forefront of the field and provide the best possible care to our patients. Ultimately, our mission is to help our patients maintain healthy, functional, and beautiful…
