What’s Included in Preventive Dental Care?

Preventive dentistry includes services designed to monitor oral health, identify concerns early, and support healthy teeth and gums. During your visit, we evaluate your dental needs and recommend preventive care based on your oral health and treatment goals.

Dental Exams

Regular dental exams allow your dentist to evaluate your teeth, gums, and overall oral health. Exams play an important role in identifying potential concerns before they become more complex and help guide future treatment recommendations.

Your care recommendations will depend on your exam findings and individual needs.

Professional Teeth Cleanings

Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar that cannot be eliminated through brushing and flossing alone. Routine cleanings help support healthy gums, maintain a healthy smile, and complement good oral hygiene habits at home.

Your cleaning schedule will depend on your oral health and clinical findings.

Digital Dental X-rays

Digital X-rays help detect concerns that may not be visible during a visual examination. They provide valuable information about tooth structure, bone levels, and other areas below the surface that contribute to comprehensive dental care.

The type and frequency of X-rays depend on your oral health history and current needs.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are thin protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of certain teeth to help reduce the risk of decay. Sealants are commonly used on molars because deep grooves can be difficult to clean thoroughly with daily brushing.

Your dentist will determine whether sealants are appropriate based on your risk factors and oral health.

Digital Scanning

Digital scanning creates detailed images of your teeth and oral structures without traditional impressions. This technology supports treatment planning, monitoring, and documentation while improving accuracy and patient comfort.

The use of digital scanning depends on your specific treatment needs and clinical evaluation.

When Should You Schedule Preventive Dental Care?

Preventive dentistry is an important part of maintaining oral health at every stage of life. Regular exams and cleanings help monitor changes in your teeth and gums, support prevention efforts, and provide opportunities to address concerns before they become more complex.

Many dental conditions develop gradually and may not cause noticeable symptoms in their early stages. Routine preventive visits allow your dentist to evaluate your oral health, discuss any findings, and recommend care based on your individual needs.

Preventive dental care may be especially important if you:

  • Are due for a routine dental exam or cleaning
  • Want to maintain healthy teeth and gums
  • Have a history of cavities or gum disease
  • Have not visited a dental office in several years
  • Want to support long-term oral health through prevention

What Happens During a Preventive Dental Office Visit?

Preventive dental visits are designed to evaluate your oral health, identify potential concerns, and help you maintain healthy teeth and gums. During your appointment, your dentist will review your dental history, examine your teeth and gums, and discuss any changes or concerns you may have noticed since your last visit.

Depending on your needs, your visit may include a professional cleaning, dental X-rays, digital scanning, or other preventive services. These tools help provide a more complete understanding of your oral health and support informed treatment recommendations.

Preventive care is personalized for every patient. Based on your examination findings, we may recommend a schedule for future cleanings, exams, or other preventive services designed to support your long-term oral health.
