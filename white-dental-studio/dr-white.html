Ongoing learning allows him to bring current approaches back to White Dental Studio and share them with his patients.

Experience and Approach to Care

Dr. White has spent over 17 years working in both private practice and larger clinical settings. That experience shaped how he built White Dental Studio and the kind of environment he wanted for his patients and team.

His approach is centered on clear communication, thoughtful planning, and making sure each patient feels comfortable during their visit. Every treatment plan is designed around the individual, not a one-size approach, with time taken to answer questions and address concerns directly.

He believes strong results come from trust, consistency, and a relationship between the patient and provider that develops over time.

Leadership and Community Involvement

Dr. White plays an active role in the local dental and business community. He has served in leadership positions with the Southern Oregon Dental Society, including President, and continues to stay involved in professional organizations and study groups.

Beyond dentistry, he values being part of the Ashland community and supporting local initiatives. His involvement reflects a broader commitment to service, connection, and contributing to the people and place he works in every day.

Life Outside the Practice

Outside of the clinic, Dr. White spends time with his family and enjoys being active outdoors. He especially enjoys hiking, visiting mountain lakes, and making the most of Southern Oregon throughout the year.

This balance between work and life is part of what shapes his approach inside the practice, creating an environment that feels relaxed, welcoming, and grounded for both patients and the team.
