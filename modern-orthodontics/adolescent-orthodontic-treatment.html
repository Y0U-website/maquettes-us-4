Braces for Teens in Bristow

  • Incognito/Lingual Braces Lingual braces are like traditional metal braces, so although they’re not invisible, they attach to the back of your teeth versus the front and are much less noticeable. Incognito Hidden Braces work behind the scenes – literally – so no one can see you’re wearing braces. The braces are hidden behind your teeth and practically invisible when you smile. And they’re 100 % customized for you, so you can be confident in the results.
  • Metal Traditional Braces Standard metal brackets that are placed on your teeth with an adhesive and connected by wire. They require periodic tightening so steady pressure can gradually straighten your teeth and align your jaw.
  • Ceramic Braces Ceramic braces are associated with being more discreet than metal braces. The way they are composed and colored makes them less noticeable than metal braces. Ceramic brackets are placed on your teeth with an adhesive and connected by wire. They require periodic tightening so steady pressure can gradually straighten your teeth and align your jaw.
  • Gold Braces Gold braces are actually made of stainless steel, just like standard braces. They just happen to be plated in gold! They are placed on the front of your teeth with an adhesive and connected by wire. They require periodic tightening so steady pressure can gradually straighten your teeth and align your jaw.

7 Benefits of Orthodontics

Orthodontic provides your child with benefits that go beyond aesthetics. Our corrective options are an excellent way to correct uneven bites that can interfere with speaking and chewing. Orthodontic treatment can also help to prevent future oral health issues.

Some of the benefits provided by our orthodontic treatments include:

  • Optimizing bite function
  • Increasing confidence
  • Improving oral hygiene
  • Preventing jaw pain
  • Reducing the risk of dental complications
  • Eliminating many costly future treatments
  • Minimizing the risk of crooked and protruding teeth
