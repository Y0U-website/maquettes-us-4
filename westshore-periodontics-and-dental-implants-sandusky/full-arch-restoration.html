Our Approach to Full Arch Restoration

The Full Arch Restoration Process

At your initial consultation, we’ll learn about your goals, your dental history, and how tooth loss has affected your daily life. Then we use advanced digital scans, X-rays, and CT imaging to study your bone structure and plan the number of implants needed for full arch reconstruction.

Once we understand your needs, we create a clear treatment plan. We walk you through your timeline, the cost of full mouth restoration, and what your new teeth will look like. You’ll never feel rushed, and all of your questions are welcome.

During treatment, several implants are placed along the jaw to support your new teeth. These implants act like roots and help your restoration feel steady and natural. The procedure is predictable and performed with safety in mind. Many patients receive temporary teeth soon after the procedure, so they can smile and function while the implants heal.

Recovery is steady and manageable. We give you a simple guide to help minimize discomfort and protect the implants as they integrate with the bone. Follow-up visits allow us to check your progress and make sure your healing stays on track.

Who Is a Candidate for Full Arch Restoration?

Full arch restoration works well for many people who have lost multiple teeth or feel frustrated with dentures. Good bone support helps, but bone grafting can sometimes make treatment possible when support is low. We also review your overall health to ensure the implants can heal properly. Our goal is to find an approach that gives you the strongest, longest-lasting result.
