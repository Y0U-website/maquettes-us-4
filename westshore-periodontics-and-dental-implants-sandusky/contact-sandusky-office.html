A Caring Environment Just for You

To combat any potential dental anxiety, we have created a supportive environment right here in Sandusky where your comfort always comes first. From your very first phone call to your final check-up, we treat you with the kindness and respect you deserve.

Our team takes the time to listen to your story. We want to know your goals, answer your questions, and make sure you feel completely at ease with your care.

Advanced Care Close to Home

You do not have to travel far to receive leading-edge periodontal and implant care. Our Sandusky office is equipped with innovative technology designed to make your treatment safer, faster, and more comfortable. We use precise 3D imaging to plan your procedure down to the smallest detail, ensuring the best possible outcome for your smile.

Whether you are looking to replace missing teeth with dental implants or need expert care for gum health, our skilled periodontists are here to help. We combine years of experience with a gentle approach, focusing on solutions that last a lifetime. Your oral health is a vital part of your overall well-being, and we are honored to help you protect it.

Meet the Team Guiding Your Transformation

Our doctors and staff look forward to taking the time to get to know you. Here’s a quick introduction to our board-certified periodontists.

Dr. Christopher Connors

A board-certified periodontist and former U.S. Navy dental officer, Dr. Connors brings over 15 years of advanced clinical experience to Westshore Periodontics & Dental Implants. He is known for his skillful, precise approach and a genuine commitment to helping patients achieve lasting confidence.

Dr. Arvind Adapalli

As a board-certified periodontist with over a decade of clinical experience, Dr. Adapalli is dedicated to elevating patient care using innovative technology. He has successfully performed hundreds of advanced periodontal and implant procedures, guiding patients to renewed confidence at every step.

Dr. Gian Schincaglia

A board-certified periodontist with over 25 years of experience, Dr. Schincaglia has improved thousands of smiles through advanced surgical care. He is recognized for his research leadership and for delivering outstanding, lasting results grounded in a patient-first approach.
