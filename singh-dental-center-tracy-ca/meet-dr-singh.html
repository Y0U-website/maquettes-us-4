Meet Dr. Singh

Dr. Shailaja Singh, DDS

Dentist located in Tracy, CA

The dedicated and experienced team at Singh Dental Center in Tracy, California, is led by Shailaja Singh, DDS. Dr. Singh has been in practice for more than 15 years and believes in empowering her patients with the knowledge that maintaining their oral health plays an essential role in their overall well-being.

In 2004, Dr. Singh graduated from the International Dental License Program at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, San Joaquin society and Tracy Chamber of Commerce.

Singh Dental Center maintains a welcoming, modern, and state-of-the-art facility, utilizing the most cutting-edge technologies and treatment protocols. Dr. Singh is passionate about the practice of dentistry and spends a significant amount of time continuing her education to stay up to date with advances in dental techniques and services. She possesses advanced skills in sedation dentistry, dental implants, and cosmetic dentistry.

From general dentistry and routine cleanings to cosmetic procedures like InvisalignⓇ and Zoom! laser teeth whitening, patients at Singh Dental Center receive only the highest quality dental care.
