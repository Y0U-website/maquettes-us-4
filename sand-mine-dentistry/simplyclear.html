Commonly Asked Questions

What is SimplyClear Treatment? SimplyClear clear aligners straighten teeth using a series of removable, nearly invisible aligners—no brackets, wires, or food restrictions like traditional braces.

Who is SimplyClear for? SimplyClear is ideal for adults and teens looking to improve their smiles, correcting a wide range of bite and alignment issues without disrupting daily life.

How is SimplyClear different from braces? Removable and virtually invisible No food restrictions—enjoy your favorites, even popcorn! Easy brushing and flossing + What can SimplyClear correct? Gaps Overbites & Underbites Crossbites & Open Bites Crowded & Crooked teeth + Does SimplyClear treatment hurt? Mild discomfort may occur with each new aligner but typically fades within a day.

How long is treatment? Most cases take 6–24 months. Aligners should be worn 20–22 hours daily but removed for eating and brushing.

What happens after treatment? Enjoy your new smile! A retainer may be recommended to maintain results, with specific wear instructions provided.
