Dr. David Sutton

When you choose us to be your dental home, it’s a massive privilege we never take lightly. We want to be such a pleasant retreat from daily stresses that you actually look forward to every visit. We definitely look forward to having you!

Changing Lives

Initially on a path to research cures for disease, Dr. Sutton found his true calling in dentistry when he saw the life-changing power of a transformed smile. As the first doctor in his family, he’s passionate about his calling and loves building lasting relationships and shaping smiles with style.

Dental Training

Dr. Sutton graduated with honors from UCF and completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Nova Southeastern University. After graduation he served alongside his mentor in South Florida where he learned to hone his clinical skills.

HOME LIFE

He loves spending time with his wife Maria and their two children, Maxwell and Claire. He also enjoys cartooning, working with his hands at home, and dabbling in stand-up comedy.

Dapper Dental

In 2019, Dr. Sutton launched Dapper Dental with the dream of creating a different kind of VIP dental experience, shaped by comfort, and uniquely tailored to each guest. His dream came to life and Dapper Dental has become an award-winning dental practice serving people and giving back to community organizations throughout Central Florida.

Dr. Adam Miller

"My passion for dentistry is reinforced every time a patient smiles and thanks me for helping them. Through techniques and materials, dentistry can provide comprehensive care and life changing results."

Dental Training

Born and raised in Orlando, Dr. Miller earned his undergraduate degree from Florida State University, where he also met his wife, Lauren. He went on to graduate with his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Florida College of Dentistry.

Areas of Interest

Although he provides a full range of comprehensive care for all ages, he has a specialized interest in cosmetic dentistry, root canals, and extractions.

HOME LIFE

He loves spending time with his wife, their son Aiden, and their identical twin girls Emma and Kate. He also enjoys golfing, fishing, and time with friends.
