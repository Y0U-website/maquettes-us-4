MEMBERSHIP GUIDELINES

  • Membership fee is due at time of enrollment, is valid for one year (12 months), and is non-refundable.
  • If you are not an established patient (if you are new to our office or have only been seen for emergencies) there is a one-time fee of $50 upon enrollment.
  • DapperVIP is NOT dental insurance.
  • Membership cannot be combined with dental insurance.
  • DapperVIP can only be used for services performed in our office.
  • If the individual is getting Invisalign, they must remain a member until treatment is complete.
  • Does not apply to costs covered by worker’s compensation or covered under auto or medical insurance.
