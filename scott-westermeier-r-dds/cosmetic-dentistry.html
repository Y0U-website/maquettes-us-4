  • Use Non-Abrasive Toothpaste: Apply non-abrasive toothpaste on a soft-bristled toothbrush to avoid scratching or damaging the surface of veneers.
  • Avoid Staining Substances: Minimize the consumption of coffee, tea, red wine, tobacco and other stain-causing substances to preserve the color of veneers.
  • Be Mindful of Hard Foods: Be cautious biting into hard foods or using teeth for tasks like opening packages to prevent unnecessary stress on veneers.
  • Reduce Teeth Grinding: If you grind your teeth, consider talking to your doctor about using a night guard to protect both natural teeth and veneers from potential damage.
  • Schedule Regular Dental Check-ups: Keep teeth healthy with dental check-ups. If you notice issues like chips or discoloration, consult your dentist for evaluation and adjustments.
