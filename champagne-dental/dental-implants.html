Dental Implants

Missing teeth can make everyday activities like eating and speaking feel challenging, but dental implants offer a reliable, long-term solution. With a success rate of over 90 %, they can restore your smile while preventing bone loss and supporting the structure of your jaw.

At Champagne Dental Care, we specialize in providing dental implants in Clearwater, FL. We combine advanced techniques with a patient-centered approach to deliver natural-looking and durable results.

Dental implants are small titanium posts that serve as artificial tooth roots. They’re surgically placed into your jawbone and provide a strong foundation for replacement teeth such as crowns, bridges , or even full-arch dentures. Titanium is used because it’s biocompatible, meaning it integrates with the bone in a process called osseointegration.

Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth for support, making them a healthier and more stable option.

If your jawbone has experienced shrinkage due to tooth loss, bone grafting may be recommended to create a strong foundation for the implants. Bone grafting involves adding bone material to the jaw to restore lost bone volume and improve implant success.

What Is the Process of Getting Dental Implants?

During your first visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health, review your medical history, and take X-rays to assess the condition of your jawbone.

The implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia for your comfort. A small incision is made in the gum to expose the jawbone, and the titanium implant is carefully inserted. The area is then stitched closed, and the healing process begins.

Healing, or osseointegration, typically takes 3-6 months. During this time, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a stable foundation for the replacement tooth.

After healing, a second minor procedure is performed to attach an abutment—a small connector piece that holds the final crown in place. In some cases, we add a temporary crown to maintain the appearance of your smile while the gum tissue heals.

Once your gums have healed, impressions are taken to create your custom crown, bridge, or denture. Depending on your needs, the prosthetic tooth can be attached to a small gold cylinder, which is screwed into the abutment, or it can be directly cemented onto the abutment itself.
