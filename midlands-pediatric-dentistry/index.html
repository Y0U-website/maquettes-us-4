Midlands Pediatric Dentistry is committed to providing the best possible care for your child during their growth and development. We continually pursue ways to minimize anxiety of dental visits and offer many techniques for treatment. We will treat your child as if they were our own.

Dr. Shoun is board certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. He is a member of the College of Diplomates of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, South Carolina Society of Pediatric Dentists, MUSC Alumni Association, and Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society.

Outside of his office, Dr. Shoun enjoys spending time with his wife, April, their two children, Forrest and Laurel as well as reading and doing yoga.
