Schrock Membership Plan

  • No annual maximums
  • No claims forms or denials
  • No pre-authorization forms or waiting periods
  • No limitations on existing conditions
  • Immediate eligibility

Plan Inclusions*

*Patients with periodontal conditions will get 2 periodontal maintenance cleanings per year and may have to pay additional fees for more cleanings.

  • All exams (including new patient, regular, emergency exams)
  • 2 Regular teeth cleanings
  • Any necessary x-rays
  • Fluoride treatments (2/year)

Additional Savings of 15 % Off

  • Fillings
  • Veneers,Crowns, and Bridges
  • Sealants
  • Extractions and Bone grafts
  • Dentures
  • Deep cleanings
  • Root canals

Total Annual Plan Costs and Options

  • Full payment – $525 to cover 12 months from the enrollment date
  • 6-months payment plan $100/month x 6 months = $600 to cover 12 months from the enrollment date

Guidelines For Savings Plan

  • Membership fees (either full or 6-months plan) are due at the time of enrollment.
  • For monthly recurring payment plan, a credit card information will be left in file and debited monthly on the 15 th of each month.
  • Patients are immediately eligible for care at that time.
  • Membership payment covers members for 12 months from the enrollment date.
  • Any additional charges on treatment are due at the time of service.
  • Membership fees are non-refundable.

Limitations For Savings Plan

  • This cannot be used as dental insurance or redeemed at other practices.

The discounts and coverages will not be applicable to the following:

  • Specialist referrals
  • Invisalign or any orthodontic treatments
  • Worker’s compensation related injuries
  • Procedures outside the treating doctor’s capabilities
  • Hospitalization charges of any kind
  • CareCredit or another financing program
