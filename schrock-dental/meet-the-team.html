Meet the Team

At Schrock Dental we believe skill and compassion go hand in hand when providing exceptional patient care. As dental professionals, we stay up to date on the latest advancements in dentistry in order to provide the most current treatment options for our patients. We recognize that our patients are unique individuals who have placed a great deal of trust in us and our team.

We appreciate the time you’ve taken to visit this site and find out more about our dental practice, and the many services we can provide to help you achieve optimum oral health.

Dr. Andy Suh, DMD, MS

Dr. Suh graduated from the University of California, Irvine, as a recipient of Excellence in Research Award. He went on to earn his master’s degree in Healthcare Ethics from Creighton University, and Doctor of Dental Medicine from Roseman University of Health Sciences.

Dr. Suh was born into the field of dentistry; his family had worked in the dental lab business for over 40 years in California. He got his first taste of dentistry at the early age of sixteen years old working in the dental lab, and multiple dental offices thereafter.

Recognizing the importance of having not just the hand skills but the highest ethics and moral principles as a doctor, Dr. Suh shared the knowledge from his advanced studies in Healthcare Ethics with first-year dental students and was later recognized by the American Dental Education Association with the Academic Dental Careers Fellowship.

With vast experience in the full scope of general dentistry, Dr. Suh completed the 4-year continuum at the world renowned dental continuing education facility, the Pankey Institute, and continuing to be involved in various Masters and Focus series in order to provide the most scientific and impactful treatments for his patients. When he’s not in the office, he enjoys playing on his guitar, building computers, and spending time with his family.

Andrea is our treatment coordinator and she is a Westerville native. After 10 years of chairside patient care as a CDA, Andrea found a new way to help her patients with the current position as treatment coordinator. With her prior chairside knowledge, Andrea takes pride in providing our patients with a thorough understanding of their treatment plan and insurance benefits. When Andrea is not spending time in the office, she enjoys reading books & spending time with her husband & two little boys!

Angela is our RDH and she grew up near Detroit, MI. She is caring, empathetic, & will explain your care in a way that makes you comfortable. Sharing her knowledge of dental care & the link to systemic health along with her gentle hand is part of why her patients love to see her!

When Angela is not caring for & educating her patients, she enjoys spending time with her husband & two children. She is a fan of stand-up comedy & loves to laugh! Ask her about the newest family member, Pickles!

Brigitte is our RDH and she grew up in Granville, OH. Her father was a dentist, so her interest in dentistry began at an early age. She has been a licensed dental hygienist for 41 years! Advertising her love for dentistry every where she goes, her license plate will remind you to “Go Floss!”

When Brigitte isn’t promoting healthy smiles, she enjoys spending time with her partner Joe, her pets Oliver & Dixie, shooting pool, & spending time with her nieces/nephews.

Sameen is our insurance coordinator who works remotely. Sameen attended Altamash Institute of Dental Medicine, where she obtained her Bachelors of Dental Surgery.

When she’s not working hard to answer your calls & verify your benefits/appointments, she enjoys experimenting with new hobbies! She is currently interested in crafting, cooking, and taking care of her stray dog/cats!
