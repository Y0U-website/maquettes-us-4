Dr. Jay Samuelson is not only a popular family and general dentist, but also a Best of Omaha award-winning cosmetic dentist . Dr. Samuelson attended the elite Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies (LVI) where he has combined his artistic ability, technical skills, caring demeanor and compassionate care to become one of Omaha’s preferred cosmetic dentists.

Dr. Samuelson is a Nebraska native who grew up in Kearney, Nebraska, and graduated from Kearney State. He went on to graduate from Creighton University School of Dentistry in 1994.

After working in an associate position, Dr. Samuelson saw the need for dental care in the newly expanding Northwest Omaha area and opened Hillsborough Family Dentistry in 1996. What started as a small, two-employee, neighborhood dental office has grown to the current multi-doctor, four-location practice.

While the practice has grown, it has still maintained the feel as the “neighborhood dentist” where the entire team still enjoys getting to know their patients. This is large in part due to Dr. Samuelson’s vision and excellent leadership.
