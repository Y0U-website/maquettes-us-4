Our Technology: Advanced Dental…

At Portland North Dental, we embrace the latest dental technologies to deliver exceptional care with precision, efficiency, and comfort. Our commitment to staying at the forefront of dentistry ensures that every patient receives state-of-the-art treatment in a modern, streamlined environment.

Our practice is fully digital, utilizing secure, chartless systems to store patient records, digital x-rays, treatment plans, and scheduling information. Digital charts and forms simplify your experience, allowing for faster check-ins, seamless communication, and easy access to your dental records. Our digital x-rays provide instant, high-quality images with significantly less radiation than traditional methods, enabling quicker diagnoses and a more environmentally friendly approach by eliminating chemical processing.

These technologies, combined with our teams’ dedication to ongoing education, allow us to provide efficient, effective, and patient-centered care. At Portland North Dental, we leverage innovation to make your visits more comfortable and your smile healthier, all while delivery results you can trust.
