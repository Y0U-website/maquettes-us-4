Dr. Stephanie Watson

Dr. Stephanie Watson was born and raised in Augusta, Maine. Her interest in dentistry began at an early age and continued to grow over the years. She attended the University of New England and graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science in Medical Biology. During her undergraduate career, she minored in Art and traveled abroad to Ecuador and Peru on medical mission trips.

Dr. Watson then attended the University of New England College of Dental Medicine. During this time, she was trained with a comprehensive and innovative curriculum focused on patient-centered care and grounded in evidence-based dentistry. Dr. Watson received the American College of Prosthodontics, 2020 Prosthodontic Achievement Award for her demonstration of excellence in the field. Dr. Watson is a member of the American Dental Association, Maine Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry and Salt Pine Study Club.

Dr. Watson believes that every patient deserves a dentist who will listen to their concerns and goals, provide them with quality dental care, and motivate them to maintain excellent oral health.

When Dr. Watson is not in the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband, daughter and their chocolate lab. Some of their favorite pastimes are gardening, cooking, boating, and camping.

Dr. Nathan Goff

Hi Everybody, I’m Dr Nathan Goff. I’m also known as Bear (my father gave me this name from day one), Ted (short for teddy bear, my high school and college pals decided that I resemble a stuffed animal more than a four legged beast), Nathan or Nate; Please call me any of these, inside or out of the office. As a native Mainer, I grew up in Fryeburg, and attended Fryeburg Academy (class of ’82), Bowdoin College Class of ’86 and UCONN dental class of ’91. I bought my dental practice from Dr. Henry Pollard in Portland in 1992.

I currently live in Scarborough with my wife Sheri and have 2 amazing boys (Cam and Liam) and 2 great dogs (Lexie and Brody). While not working, I enjoy most major sports, walks on the beach, and connecting with high school and college friends.
