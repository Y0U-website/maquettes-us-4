Full-Arch Restoration VS Dentures

Traditional dentures, while a common solution for tooth loss, often have limitations. They may slip, feel unstable, or lack the strength needed for normal chewing. Additionally, dentures do not stimulate the jawbone, which can lead to bone loss and changes in facial structure over time.

Full-arch restoration, by contrast, uses dental implants to securely anchor a custom prosthesis. This approach delivers a natural appearance and functionality similar to real teeth while helping to preserve jawbone health and prevent bone deterioration.

Benefits of Full-Arch Restoration

  • Complete Smile Transformation : Restores a full set of teeth for a confident smile.
  • Durable and Comfortable : Designed for long-term use with a secure fit.
  • Natural Appearance : Mimics the look and feel of natural teeth.

What to Expect During Full-Arch Restoration

During the procedure, we prioritize your comfort with appropriate anesthesia. Our dentist places the implants and attaches a custom prosthesis, creating a strong foundation for your new smile.

Who Is a Candidate for Full-Arch Restoration?

Dr. Shim will conduct a thorough evaluation of your condition during your consultation and provide personalized recommendations.

  • Individuals missing multiple teeth or entire arches.
  • Those expecting to lose multiple teeth due to extractions.
  • Patients in good overall health.
