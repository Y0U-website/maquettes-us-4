Why All-On-4 Dental Implants at Premier Dentistry?

Benefits of All-on-4® Dental Implants

All-on-4® dental implants are often the preferred choice for restoring an entire dental arch. Here’s why they stand out compared to traditional dentures or bridges:

  • Single-Visit Solution : Your entire smile can be restored in one appointment, with implants and permanent prosthetics placed together.
  • Natural Look and Feel : These implants function like natural teeth, staying securely in place without slipping.
  • Strong and Functional : Once healed, All-on-4® implants allow you to eat most of your favorite foods with confidence.
  • Seamless Integration : The implants blend naturally with any remaining teeth for a cohesive appearance.

Ideal Candidates for All-on-4® Dental Implants

The best candidates for All-on-4® dental implants:

  • Need to replace multiple teeth or expect to have several teeth extracted.
  • Are in good overall health, with no conditions that would complicate surgery.
  • Have adequate jawbone to support dental implants.

The All-on-4® Process

In some cases, preparatory procedures like tooth extractions or bone grafting may be needed to ensure your mouth is ready for All-on-4® implants. These may be performed during the same visit or scheduled separately, depending on your needs.

Recovery and Aftercare

Recovery from All-on-4® treatment is typically straightforward. Most patients can return to work or daily activities within 2–4 days. Any discomfort or swelling usually peaks around day three or four and can be managed with over-the-counter or prescribed medications.

During recovery, stick to a soft-food diet, avoiding spicy, hot, cold, or crumbly foods. Refrain from using straws for several days post-surgery. Once your implants have fully healed, your dentist will place custom-made prosthetics to complete your natural-looking, fully functional smile.
