Program exclusions & limitations

All plan premiums are non-refundable.

Premiums must be paid in full prior to receiving benefits. Annual premiums are due January 1st of each benefit year that you wish to participate in. Plan discounts are valid for the current calendar year only and expire after December 31st of the benefit year. There will be a finance charge associated with Care Credit services while covered under this plan. Patient portions are due in full on the day of services.
