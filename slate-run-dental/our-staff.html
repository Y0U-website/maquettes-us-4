Meet Our Team

Slate Run Dental has an amazing family of employees. Their dedication and hard work are the driving force behind our success. Everyday they do all that they can to make every patient have as pleasant an experience as possible. We can’t wait for you to meet them.

I grew up just around the corner from Slate Run Dental and know many of our patients personally. I have worked in dentistry for 20 years and was thrilled to get the opportunity to work with Dr. Daniel. Outside of work, I am busy with 4 children and enjoy going to their sporting events. I hope to have some spare time some day!

I grew up in Lancaster, and still live there with my daughter Brittnee and my son Brandon. I am very family oriented and love being with my children. I also enjoy spending time with my friends during my hours away from work.

I received my training in upstate New York and I have a background that’s heavy in periodontal therapy. Since joining Slate Run Dental in 1999, it has been my pleasure to help our patients achieve and maintain good oral health. Every patient is unique and I enjoy the interactions I have with each one. Dental hygiene is a preventive and healing profession, and I am proud to be a part of an office that emphasizes that.

I graduated magna cum laude from the Ohio State University in 2009. I received my Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene, with a minor in Spanish. Additionally, I specialized in Education within the Dental Hygiene Program and aspire to teach at the university level. For now, I enjoy talking with my patients about the "hows" and "whys" to preventative dental health and hope to shed light on questions and concerns each may have. I love our office because I am able to see young toddlers, to those who have been coming here for nearly 40 years and many in between!

I was raised in Northwest Ohio and now live in Lithopolis. I met my husband, Stephen, our freshman year at OSU. Together we have four children, Tucker, Stella, Roxie, and Selah the loves of our lives! I am blessed to work part-time in order to home school them. Our family can be found at church, exploring the outdoors, fixing our old house or at the library. We also enjoy museums, traveling and watching sports when we get a chance.

I am a graduate of The Ohio State University where I received my Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene with a specialization in Community Health Education. Since then, I have had the honor of serving patients in and around central Ohio until joining the team at Slate Run Dental in 2019. My passion is helping patients understand the connection between oral health and overall health. My focus is educating them so that they care about their health as much as I do. I am blessed to be able to do what I love with a wonderful group of patient oriented people.

I was born and raised in Lexington Ohio and have lived in this area since I graduated OSU. When I am not working, you’ll find me traveling with my husband Dan and daughter Delaney, tending to the operations of my small business or serving with the worship team at Tri-Village Christian Church in Pataskala.
