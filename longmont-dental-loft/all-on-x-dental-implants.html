Understanding the Risks of All-on-X Treatment

All-on-X is a predictable and well-established procedure when carefully planned with proper imaging and surgical experience.

As with any surgical treatment, success depends on factors such as bone health and medical history. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your situation thoroughly to ensure you’re a safe candidate and discuss any potential considerations in advance.

What All-on-X Implants Are Made From

All-on-X restorations are typically made from either a hybrid material or zirconia. Hybrid restorations use a metal framework with denture teeth layered on top, while zirconia is a solid, highly durable ceramic.

At Longmont Dental Loft, zirconia is our preferred material because it is strong, long-lasting, natural-looking, and easy to clean. Hybrid restorations may be considered in select cases.

How Long All-on-X Lasts

With proper care and regular checkups, All-on-X implants are designed to last decades and, for many patients, a lifetime.

How to Care for All-on-X Implants

Care is simple and similar to natural teeth. Brush daily and use a Waterpik with an implant or denture tip to clean around the prosthesis and along the gumline. You can learn more in our water flosser tips guide . Avoid traditional string floss.

You’ll also visit us once a year so we can remove the prosthesis, clean it thoroughly, and evaluate your implants to ensure long-term health and stability.

How Much Does It Cost?

All-on-X treatment typically ranges from $18,000 to $30,000 per arch, depending on bone quality, materials, and sedation needs.

We review all costs and financing options during your consultation so you can make a confident, informed decision.

Does Insurance Cover All-on-X?

Insurance coverage varies by plan. Some policies may help cover parts of treatment, such as extractions or the final prosthesis, while implant benefits are often limited.

We will review your coverage with you before treatment begins and discuss financing options to make your care more manageable.
