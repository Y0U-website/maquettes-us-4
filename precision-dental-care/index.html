The practice

Precision Dental Care is built around thoughtful treatment planning, patient education and care that makes sense for the person in the chair.

Rather than overwhelming patients with options, the practice focuses on understanding the concern, explaining what the dentist sees and discussing reasonable next steps. Preventive care is the foundation, with restorative, cosmetic and urgent treatment available when needed.

A native San Franciscan who grew up in the Glen Park area, Dr. Tanega founded Precision Dental Care at 44 Monterey Boulevard in 2011.

He has been in the dental profession since 2001, has taken courses at UCSF and postgraduate studies at University of the Pacific, and has practiced throughout the greater San Francisco Bay Area.

“Hi, I’m Dr. Tanega. I grew up here in Glen Park, and it means a lot to me to now care for the community that helped shape who I am. My goal has always been to make this office feel like a place where neighbors are known, listened to, and treated with the same care I’d want for my own family.”
