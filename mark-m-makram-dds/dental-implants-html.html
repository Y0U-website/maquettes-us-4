Benefits of Dental Implants

In recent years, dental implants have become the most popular solution for missing teeth. Because of their unique placement inside the jawbone, they’re able to provide a wide range of benefits that you simply can’t get with more traditional options like dentures and dental bridges. To learn more about the advantages of dental implants, contact our office and schedule a consultation today. We’ll gladly review your treatment options and help you find the tooth-replacement choice that will best suit your needs.

Day-to-Day Benefits

We use our teeth every day to speak, eat, and smile. For that reason, it only makes sense that you’d want a tooth-replacement solution that’ll allow you to live your day-to-day life with ease. Luckily, dental implants can help you do just that by giving the following benefits:

  • More biting strength. Since implants replace the entire structure of a missing tooth, they’re able to withstand the chewing of virtually any food.
  • Easier oral hygiene. With dental implants, you don’t have to worry about incorporating complex steps into your oral hygiene routine or purchasing special cleaning products. All you need to do is care for them as you would care for natural teeth!
  • Increased confidence. Unlike dentures, your implants will stay firmly in place once they’ve successfully fused with the surrounding bone tissue. They’ll allow you to eat, speak, and smile with much more confidence.

Health Benefits

Aside from appearance, dental implants offer multiple health advantages as well. Some of those include:

  • Jawbone preservation. Because dental implants stimulate the jawbone, they’re able to keep it strong, healthy, and functional for a lifetime.
  • Protection for natural teeth. It’s common for teeth to shift after tooth loss. Fortunately, dental implants can prevent that from happening by serving as a placeholder to ensure every tooth stays exactly where it belongs.
  • Better overall health. Studies show there’s a correlation between tooth loss and an increased risk of multiple diseases. Because implants are so similar to natural teeth, it’s reasonable to believe that they can help lower such risks.

Long-Term Benefits

By choosing to get dental implants now, they can help you out for years to come with the following benefits:

  • High success rate. Dental implants have an impressive success rate of about 95 % even 20 years after they’ve been placed.
  • Youthful appearance. Since implants stimulate blood flow in the jaw, they help prevent its deterioration and the sunken, aged look that often happens with tooth loss.
  • Long-lasting results. Unlike dentures and dental bridges, your implants can last decades without needing replacement.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

First and foremost, you’ll have a consultation with Dr. Makram and the rest of the team. We’ll review your medical history, assess the current state of your oral health, and inquire about your goals to determine whether or not dental implants are right for you. Some patients may need preliminary treatments like gum disease therapy, tooth extractions, or bone grafting before they can get implants. Once that’s taken care of, you can move along to the next step of the process dental implant surgery.

Dental Implant Surgery

Believe it or not, dental implant surgery in North Naples is a fairly simple procedure. Since Dr. Makram is able to perform the entire process from start to finish, you don’t have to worry about visiting an outside specialist for this step. During your procedure, Dr. Makram will make small incisions in your gum tissue to access the jawbone. After the implant posts are inserted, he’ll suture your gums shut, and place a protective cap over each implant to shield them throughout the healing process.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

Keep in mind that it’ll take about three to six months for osseointegration to take place. It’s the natural process that allows the implant to successfully integrate with the surrounding bone tissue, making this solution as reliable as possible. Once osseointegration is complete, you’ll return to the office for a second smaller surgery. You’ll receive your abutments, which are metal connector pieces that are placed on top of the implants. They’ll allow us to secure your new crown, bridge, or denture into place.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

While you recover, your replacement teeth will be created at a trusted dental laboratory using impressions taken of your natural smile. Your new restorations will be made using natural-looking materials, which means they’ll blend seamlessly with your remaining teeth. Once in place, you’ll be ready to flash your newly rebuilt grin with confidence!

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants have become one of the most popular tooth loss solutions because almost any healthy adult is eligible for them. However, there are three things you must have to be considered a good candidate for implants:

  • Good Overall Health The process requires oral surgery, which means you’ll need to be healthy enough to undergo the procedure.
  • Excellent Oral Health Preexisting issues like cavities and gum disease need to be addressed before getting dental implants because they can lead to unsuccessful treatment.
  • Sufficient Jawbone Density Your jawbone must be strong enough to support implants. If it’s not, you may need to undergo preliminary treatments like bone grafting.

Missing One Tooth

For one lost tooth, a dental implant can be attached to a dental crown. This will look and feel just like a natural tooth. It’s much sturdier and will last longer than a dental bridge. Best of all, this option doesn’t require the removal of healthy enamel from your natural teeth.

Missing Multiple Teeth

Depending on your needs, we can attach a bridge to two implants to replace three or more consecutive teeth. We can also affix a partial denture to implants to address teeth in different locations along the arch. Not only will it replace the gaps in your smile, but it’s a lifelike and stable solution that can last years to come.

Missing All Teeth

Even if you’ve suffered total tooth loss, dental implants can help! You don’t need to have a dental implant for each tooth. With just four to six implants, Dr. Makram can secure a full denture in place, giving you the confidence that your artificial teeth won’t slip or shift unexpectedly. Our team will use the minimum number of dental implants to give you the maximum amount of stability when using your pearly whites.

Dental Implant Technology

Since we handle the entire dental implant process in-office, we naturally need to make sure we’re using the right tools for the job. Advanced technology improves our ability to plan your dental implant surgery and place the implant posts more precisely. It also helps us create a prosthesis that is just right for your specific smile. Read on for a closer look at some of the technology we use to help our patients get the dental implant outcome that they deserve.

3D Cone Beam Imaging/3D CT Scanning

We need to gather a lot of information about your mouth and jaw in order to plan your dental implant surgery. However, a dental X-ray alone wouldn’t necessarily give us this information. That’s why we instead use a 3D cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) scanner to capture images of your mouth from various angles. All of these images can be combined into a comprehensive model that shows us the location of important oral structures.

By examining the model created with a CBCT scan, we can determine how much bone density is present in your jaw and whether bone grafting might be required before you can be considered eligible for dental implants. The model will also help us see exactly where the implant posts need to be inserted so that we can avoid damaging any blood vessels or nerve pathways.

Guided Dental Implant Surgery

It’s important for your implant posts to be inserted into exactly the right spot in your jaw. To make sure that we’re as accurate with our placement as possible, we can create a surgical guide based on a scan of your mouth. This gives us a physical representation of where the posts need to go, what angle they should be inserted in, and how deep they should be placed.

Guided dental implant surgery can help us minimize the chances of complications occurring during your procedure. Furthermore, it can sometimes make it possible to place dental implants with smaller incisions in the gums; as a result, your mouth won’t need as much time to heal afterward.

Digital Impression System

Before you can receive the final prosthesis for your dental implants, we’ll need to design it so that it’s suitable for your specific mouth. To do so, we can capture a digital impression of your smile by combining multiple oral scans. This lets us provide you with a prosthesis that fits properly and looks like a natural part of your mouth.

You may be familiar with traditional dental impressions that involve biting down on special putty. However, many patients find this approach uncomfortable, and it can be prone to errors that require the impressions to be redone. With digital impressions, there’s no putty to worry about, and we’re more likely to get accurate results the first time.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Proper dental implant care in North Naples is essential if you plan to keep your new smile for decades or even the rest of your life! It’s more than just seeing a dentist every six months for checkups and cleanings. Although these are vital, you should also be mindful of what you eat, how you clean your teeth, what type of mouth protection you use when playing sports, and which bad habits you should give up right now. Below, you will find a brief breakdown of what you can do to extend the longevity of your dental implants and enjoy a healthier smile.

Making Oral Hygiene a Priority

Do you regularly brush and floss? Are you using mouthwash? Depending on your answer, you may need to step up your oral hygiene game if you want your dental implants to last as long as possible. Just because these teeth are artificial doesn’t mean you can slack when it comes to your morning and nighttime routines. Brushing twice a day for two minutes, flossing at least once a day, and rinsing with an ADA-approved mouthwash can keep bacteria from forming around the implants and causing problems for your smile.

Eating a Healthy Diet

The foods you consume not only impact your waistline. They can also affect your teeth and gums. Should too much sugar and starch be incorporated into your diet, you’ll notice inflammation, possible bleeding, and tenderness of your soft oral tissues. You may even discover pockets of decay on your real teeth. By switching out these foods for healthier alternatives like leafy greens, lean proteins, fruits, vegetables, and dairy, you’ll discover your teeth become stronger, your gums become healthier, and your overall body functions at a higher level.

Breaking Bad Habits

Do you enjoy eating ice? Are you a nail-biter? Do you tend to smoke when surrounded by friends at a social gathering? No matter the bad habit, it’s time to give it up if you want to avoid impacting your dental implants in North Naples. These unhealthy habits can wreak havoc on your smile, resulting in dental implant failure. Should this happen, you can be left spending additional time and money trying to fix the problem. It is far better to simply give them up.

Protecting Your Dental Implants

Whether you enjoy playing sports or suffer from nighttime bruxism (teeth grinding), a customized mouthguard is a worthwhile investment. A sudden hit to the mouth can knock out a dental implant or lacerate soft oral tissues. Teeth grinding can cause your prosthetics to break and chip, resulting in an emergency trip to the dentist’s office for help. However, with a mouthguard, your smile is better protected and safe from any potential harm.

Scheduling Regular Dental Checkups

An implant dentist in North Naples is your friend. They not only want to see your new teeth thrive but also ensure the safety of your existing oral health. This is why it’s crucial to keep your regularly scheduled six-month checkups and cleanings. Not only will we be able to examine your real and artificial teeth for damage, but we can also check for any decay or infection that may try to negatively impact your smile and its future.

Dental Implant Post-Op Instructions

Want to make sure you heal successfully after receiving your new teeth? With dental implant post-op instructions in North Naples, Dr. Makram will provide the necessary details to give your smile the greatest chance of healing swiftly and effectively. From knowing what to eat to maintaining your smile and common side effects, these tips and tricks will help you on your road to recovery.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

Immediately following your dental implant surgery, you’ll find it in your best interest to go home and rest for at least 24 hours. The anesthesia and sedation will take time to wear off, and you don’t want to try driving or doing anything too strenuous following your procedure. You’ll need to have someone escort you home and monitor you for a short time to make sure you remain at ease. But one thing you should be sure to do is to take any prescribed medications or over-the-counter pain relievers before your anesthesia wears off. This will minimize any soreness that is likely to occur.

Common Side Effects

Will you be sore after your surgery? Most likely. After all, your gums and bone underwent serious work. Fortunately, this is a common side effect and will dissipate with the help of prescribed or OTC pain relievers. You should also expect some swelling and bruising as well, but don’t worry, as they will not last long.

Diet

Depending on your state, you may or may not crave food after your surgery. No matter your state, it’s best to stick to a liquid or soft food diet after receiving your dental implants in North Naples. This will prevent additional pressure from being placed on your surgical sites, allowing the gum tissue and bone to heal properly.

Some of the best foods you can eat during this time include:

  • Pasta
  • Scrambled Eggs
  • Yogurt
  • Ice Cream
  • Pudding
  • Warm Soups
  • Mashed Potatoes
