Dr. Kirsten Lee, DMD

Dr. K. Lee knew from a young age that dentistry was her passion, and was especially interested in the personal connection formed between patient and doctor. In addition to providing excellent dental care, Dr. Lee has also volunteered her services at the Give Kids a Smile Day, the American Academy of Women Dentists’ Free Clinic and the Huntridge Homeless Clinic at the University of Nevada, where she was the community service chair for the American Student Dental Association.

In her spare time, she enjoys being outdoors, traveling, playing tennis and trying new restaurants.
