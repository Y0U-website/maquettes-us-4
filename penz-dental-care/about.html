About Us

Penz Dental Care offers top tier general dentistry services in Rochester, Minn.

What Sets PDC Apart?

Personalized, Compassionate Care:

Founded by Dr. Matt Penz in 2016, our caring team provides an individualized, relationship-first approach to dentistry.

Comfort Focused Environment:

With modern decor, friendly staff, and relaxing amenities (think massaging dental chairs and TV/music during procedures!), our goal is to ease your dental anxiety.

Advanced, Tooth-Preserving Treatments:

Our dentists are trained in biomimetic restorative dentistry (BRD)—a minimally invasive method that preserves natural tooth structure and uses advanced bonding techniques proven to deliver restorations that last longer than traditional approaches.

Comprehensive Services:

We offer a full range of preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and pediatric dental services —from cleanings, digital x-rays, same-day crowns, dentures, implants, veneers, teeth whitening, sports mouthguards, and more.

Trusted by Patients:

Our reputation is built on exemplary patient reviews and word-of-mouth. See reverse for some of our five-star reviews! See what our patients are saying about us on Facebook and Google .

About Our Insurance and Financial Policy

We are able to send claims to most insurance plans, and we are in-network for Delta Dental Premier. Financing options and day-of-service payment reductions are available. Please view our financial policy for more information or contact us with any questions.

About Our Mission

Penz Dental Care is committed to providing you and your family with quality, honest care. Our mission is to build relationships with our patients and community through quality dentistry. Each day, we strive to be an inspirational leader in oral health care delivery to Rochester and southeastern Minnesota.
