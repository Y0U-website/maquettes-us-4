Dental Interests

Dr. Nicole Pauley

Growing up in Rochester, Minn., Dr. Nicole has been passionate about a career in healthcare from a young age. Dentistry blends her love for art and academics with her desire to make a difference in the community.

Education

Dr. Nicole is a proud graduate of Mayo High School in Rochester. She graduated magna cum laude from St. Olaf College in Northfield with a biology major before completing a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with distinction at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

Throughout her time at St. Olaf, Dr. Nicole was committed to both science and the arts. She was a dedicated member of the college’s choral ensembles and studied abroad in the Bahamas during an interim course that combined hands-on art (drawing, watercolor, and sculpture) with the study of local marine life.

Outreach

Following her undergraduate studies, Dr. Nicole served with AmeriCorps, where she helped elementary school students strengthen their reading skills—a role that reflected her dedication to service and education.

During dental school, Dr. Nicole was actively involved in community outreach and student support. She was a volunteer for UMN’s Give Kids a Smile and TeamSmiles at Target Field, both of which provide free dental care to underserved children. As a peer tutor, she helped fellow students deepen their understanding of coursework and apply it in clinical practice. She also participated in the University of Minnesota’s Health Initiative events, contributing to health education and service across the community.

Dental Interests

Dr. Nicole finds general dentistry to be very rewarding, but what she cherishes most is the chance to build meaningful relationships with her patients. She strives to ensure that each patient feels comfortable and relaxed during their visits. Dr. Nicole has a particular enthusiasm for working with children. Her goal is to make dental visits a positive and reassuring experience for her younger patients, fostering a sense of trust and ease from an early age.

Why Rochester?

“Returning to Rochester brings things full circle in the best way. This community shaped who I am, and I’m grateful for the chance to give back by serving the people who helped raise me. I’m excited to be close to family again and to reconnect with the city I’ve always called home. I am thrilled to join Penz Dental Care, where excellence in patient care, lifelong learning, and a supportive team culture are truly prioritized—making this opportunity all the more special.”

On a Personal Note…

Outside of her career, Dr. Nicole enjoys spending time with her family and friends, hiking with her collie dog named Harper, traveling, karaoke nights with her sisters, playing golf, and visiting her family’s cabin up north. She is excited to meet you!

Dr. David Wold

David Wold, DDS, grew up in Woodbury, Minn. After working as a general dentist in the Twin Cities and in Boise, Idaho, he is excited be back in Minnesota.

Education

Dental Interests

Dr. Wold is actively involved in the growing field of biomimetic restorative dentistry (BRD). He gravitated towards this philosophy of care as he was searching for ways to improve longevity and clinical results for his patients. With his demonstrated expertise of the material, Dr. Wold serves as a faculty mentor with the Alleman Center of Biomimetic Dentistry . In addition, Dr. Wold presents on this subject matter, most notably at the Idaho State Dental Association annual meeting.

Why Rochester

“Minnesota will always be ‘home.’ Rochester is a welcoming and growing community I am eager to be a part of. Dr. Penz and I attended dental school at the same time, and we reconnected through biomimetic dentistry. When an opportunity arose to work together, I couldn’t say no!”

On a Personal Note

In his spare time, Dr. Wold enjoys traveling, trying new restaurants with his wife Katie, and spending time with their children Lydia and Benjamin. He also enjoys hunting, fishing, and golf.
