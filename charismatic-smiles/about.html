Charismatic Smiles

We here at Charismatic Smiles provide a wide range of dental services, ranging from advanced prosthetics like fixed crowns, bridge work, removable dentures and partials, dental implant restorations and maxillofacial reconstruction to general dentistry services and dental hygiene.

Charismatic Smiles is both a relaxed and modern practice. We’ve taken the classic designs of Charlestonian architecture for a peaceful and beautiful building while utilizing some of the most advanced technology available to better serve our patients. Patients can fill out their forms before they even arrive on their own computers or in our waiting room with our iPads. We invite you to learn more about our office and the services we offer by Contacting Us .

Z. Vance Morgan IV, DMD, MS

Dr. Van Morgan is a board certified prosthodontist who has been in practice for over 18 years serving Columbia and the Greater Midlands. He and his team strive to provide patients the latest care in a modern and relaxed environment. He is a graduate of the College of Charleston and earned his DMD degree from MUSC College of Dental Medicine in Charleston. Dr. Morgan earned his specialty degree in Prosthodontics and a Masters in Science from UNC School of Dentistry in Chapel Hill, NC.

He currently serves on the SC State Board of Dentistry. In addition, he has been an Adjunct Clinical Professor at UNC School of Dentistry in the Graduate Prosthodontic Program. Dr. Morgan is a member of various dental organizations including the ADA, SCADA and American College of Prosthodontics, and he is a fellow of the Pierre Fauchard Academy International Honor Dental Society.

Dr. Morgan and his team work with ENTs, general dentists and other dental specialists to provide comprehensive care for patients. Prosthodontics focuses on four components of treatment, including fixed crown and bridge work, removable dentures and partials, dental implant restorations and maxillofacial reconstruction through dental appliances. In addition to these specific areas of dental care, both dental hygine and general dentistry services are available in the office.
