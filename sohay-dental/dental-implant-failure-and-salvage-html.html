Symptoms of a Failed Dental Implant

The main characteristic of a failed dental implant is discomfort. More specifically, you might experience:

  • Severe pain.
  • Bleeding, swelling, or pus.
  • Difficulty chewing.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

There are a couple of different treatments available for failed dental implants, so the first step to salvaging your restoration is identifying the cause of failure. Dr. Diep will visually examine your mouth and take detailed X-rays in order to diagnose the issue. Depending on his findings, we might be able to simply repair or tighten the implant, or we might have to remove it entirely in order to perform corrective treatments like bone grafting or gum disease therapy.

Either way, our team will keep you informed about your dental implant salvage.
