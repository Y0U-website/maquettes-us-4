Payment Options

We accept VISA, Amex, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover credit cards as well as cash. We also offer no-interest or low-interest financing through CareCredit. A 3 % fee will be charged for all card payments.

Insurance Policy

We participate with most insurance groups, including Aetna, Altius, Ameritas, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, Connection Dental, Delta Dental Direct, Dental Select Gold, Dental Select Platinum, DMBA, EMI, Guardian, Humana, Lincoln, MetLife, PEHP, Principal, TDA, and United Concordia. We will need all of your insurance information before treatment, and we will verify your coverage.

Please anticipate receiving statements during the time your insurance is pending. This will give you the opportunity to stay abreast of your balance. In the event that your insurance company pays more than expected and you have a credit on your account, you will be issued a refund or the credit can be used toward future treatment.

Please understand your insurance policy is a contract between you and the carrier and it is your responsibility to know your insurance maximum and benefits. If you have treatment at another office, it is your responsibility to know what insurance is being used at that office. It is important for you to be involved to help ensure timely payments on your account. To find out if we accept your plan, please call our office.
