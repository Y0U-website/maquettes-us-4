About Shila Yazdani, DDS, MBA

Meet Dr. Shila Yazdani

CEO & Lead Dentist at Pointer Ridge Dental Care

Dr. Shila Yazdani, DDS, MBA, is a skilled and compassionate general and cosmetic dentist who leads the team at Pointer Ridge Dental Care with a strong commitment to clinical excellence, personalized care, and advanced dental innovation. With a warm, approachable style and over a decade of experience, she proudly serves patients and families throughout Bowie, MD, and Prince George’s County.

A graduate of Howard University College of Dentistry , Dr. Yazdani received extensive training in oral surgery, orthodontics, and implant dentistry. She has gone on to expand her expertise through advanced certifications, including Invisalign and postgraduate studies at the prestigious Las Vegas Institute (LVI) , where she trained in cosmetic and neuromuscular dentistry .

Dr. Yazdani is especially passionate about integrating cutting-edge dental technologies into her patient care from digital scanners and 3D imaging to minimally invasive cosmetic and restorative treatments. Her goal is to provide the most comfortable, efficient, and precise dental experience possible.

Known for her friendly demeanor and personalized approach, Dr. Yazdani takes time to build trust with her patients, carefully explaining every diagnosis and treatment option. Whether you’re here for a routine cleaning or a complete smile transformation, she ensures your care is tailored to your goals and comfort level.

Outside the office, Dr. Yazdani enjoys spending time with her family, giving back to the community, and staying at the forefront of dental advancements.

If you’re looking for a dentist who combines advanced training with a truly patient-first approach, you’ll feel right at home with Dr. Yazdani.
