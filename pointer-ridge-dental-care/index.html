The practice

Pointer Ridge Dental Care has served residents of Prince George’s County for more than 35 years. The team strives to provide a friendly and compassionate environment. Rather than seeing as many patients as possible, the dentists, dental hygienists, and support staff ensure everyone feels listened to, understood, and well cared for.

These efforts are best illustrated by the team’s mission to apply the Golden Rule in everything they do. More specifically, the team treats patients how they want to be treated with kindness and integrity. To this end, the practice offers cutting-edge technology and employs only the most friendly, personable, and knowledgeable staff.

For individuals requiring specialized care, the team offers root canal therapy and treatment of temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders .

Whether you recently moved to the area and need to establish a dental home, or it has simply been more than six months since your last oral health checkup, Pointer Ridge Dental Care can help. Call the office today to schedule your appointment, or book online.

Dental implants are small metal posts inserted into your empty tooth sockets. After placement, they fuse with your upper or lower jaw and provide a foundation for crowns, bridges, or implant-supported dentures.

Since dental implants integrate with your bone, they function like your natural teeth. With optimal oral hygiene and routine preventive care, they typically last a lifetime.
