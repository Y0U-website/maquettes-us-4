Dr. Kieran Mullarney

Orthodontist

Dr. Mullarney earned his dental degree from Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry in Philadelphia, PA. He continued on to his residency in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. After his extensive training, he attained the distinguished Board Certification from the American Board of Orthodontics.

Dr. Mullarney grew up in New Jersey and currently resides in Austin, TX. He enjoys outdoor activities like hiking, biking, and climbing. His passion for travel has led him to explore new places around the world, immersing himself in local cultures and foods. When not traveling, he loves spending time with friends, family, and his awesome cat Jupiter.
