FAQs

How long does the full-arch implant process take?

The process involves evaluation, implant placement, and attachment of replacement teeth. Treatment typically spans several weeks to months, depending on healing and individual needs.

How do I care for full-arch dental implants?

Brush and floss daily, use antimicrobial rinses, and attend regular dental check-ups. Proper hygiene ensures implant longevity, prevents infection, and keeps the surrounding gums healthy.

Can full-arch implants replace traditional dentures?

Yes, full-arch implants offer a stable, permanent alternative to removable dentures, eliminating slippage and improving comfort, chewing efficiency, and confidence in your smile.

Are full-arch implants expensive?

While the initial cost may be higher than dentures, full-arch implants are cost-effective long-term because they reduce ongoing maintenance, repairs, and replacement costs, offering lasting value.
