Treatments All Ages Early Treatment The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children be seen for orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Not only can early orthodontic care help prevent dental issues, but it can also guide proper jaw growth, creating space and setting the foundation for a healthy, beautiful smile.

Teens

From traditional braces to clear aligners, we offer customized treatment options that fit your teen’s lifestyle, helping them smile with confidence during these important years.

Adults

It’s never too late to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted—our discreet and effective treatment options make orthodontic care convenient for adults of all ages.

Custom Treatment

At Centerton Orthodontics, we understand that no two smiles are the same, which is why we create custom treatment plans tailored to your unique needs. Whether it’s braces, clear aligners, or other orthodontic solutions, our personalized approach ensures the best possible results for every patient.

Using advanced technology and a thorough understanding of your goals, we’ll design a treatment plan that fits seamlessly into your lifestyle. With custom care and attention to detail, we’re here to help you achieve a confident, healthy smile you’ll love for years to come.

Braces

Traditional

Reliable and effective, traditional metal braces are a time-tested option for straightening your smile with precision and durability.

Ceramic

Designed to blend in with your teeth, ceramic braces offer a more discreet alternative while delivering the same great results.

Lingual

Custom-fitted to the back of your teeth, lingual braces are completely hidden from view, offering a discreet and effective way to straighten your smile.

Clear Aligners

Invisalign ®

Invisalign offers a clear, comfortable way to straighten your teeth without the look of traditional braces. Using a series of custom-made, virtually invisible aligners, Invisalign gently shifts your teeth into place while allowing you to eat, brush, and live your life without interruption.

Perfect for teens and adults, Invisalign is designed to fit seamlessly into your lifestyle. With fewer office visits and no metal wires or brackets, you can achieve the confident smile you’ve always wanted—discreetly and comfortably.

In-House Aligners

In-house aligners provide a cost-effective and convenient way to straighten teeth, and may be ideal for select cases. Designed and created right in our office, these clear aligners offer the same discreet and comfortable benefits as other aligner options.

For those seeking an affordable alternative, our in-house aligners can help you achieve a confident smile with fewer visits and a streamlined process tailored to your needs.

Retainers

After completing your orthodontic treatment, retainers are essential to maintaining your beautiful new smile. Designed to keep your teeth in their ideal positions, retainers help ensure your results last a lifetime. Whether you prefer a fixed or removable option, we’ll customize a retainer that suits your needs and lifestyle.

Even if you didn’t receive treatment with us, we’re here to help! If you’ve lost your retainer, need a replacement, or simply want a backup, we’d love to assist you. Let us help you take the next step in preserving your perfect smile.
