About Us Our Mission At Centerton Orthodontics, our mission is to deliver high-quality orthodontic care in a family-friendly environment to the families of Northwest Arkansas. We are committed to providing personalized treatment focusing on comfort, precision, and exceptional results. Every smile is unique, and we take pride in creating customized solutions to help you and your loved ones achieve healthy, confident smiles that last a lifetime.

Serving the community of Centerton, AR, and surrounding areas, we strive to be a trusted partner in your family’s oral health journey. Our dedicated team uses advanced technology and a caring approach to ensure each visit is a positive, stress-free experience. At Centerton Orthodontics, your smile is not just our profession—it’s our passion.

Meet Dr. Skylar Maxwell

Approach

Dr. Maxwell takes a personalized approach to orthodontics, tailoring each treatment plan to the unique needs and goals of his patients. While he values a conservative treatment philosophy, he is committed to staying at the forefront of his field by continually learning new techniques and integrating the latest technology into his practice. His innovative mindset ensures that patients receive exceptional care with modern, effective solutions.

Credentials

Dr. Maxwell earned his dental degree from Louisiana State University School of Dentistry, where he was a member of the C. Edmund Kells Honor Society and received both the C. Edmund Kells Student Scholarship Award and the Baldridge Scholarship for academic excellence. He completed his orthodontic residency at the University of Oklahoma, one of the nation’s top orthodontic programs, and has spent the past three years providing expert orthodontic care at a pediatric clinic in Northwest Arkansas.

Personal

As a native of Arkansas, Dr. Maxwell always knew he wanted to come back after residency. He and his wife, Cari, and their young son Jack, love getting to call Northwest Arkansas home. Outside of the office, he enjoys attending Fellowship Bible Church with his family, working on cars, playing golf, traveling, and cheering on those Razorbacks!
