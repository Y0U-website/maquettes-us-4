Samantha

Training Specialist & Patient Coordinator

Samantha joined our team in June 2020 and comes to us with years of dental experience. She started her career as a Certified Dental Assistant and although her role in the field has transitioned over the years she is still very passionate about providing quality dental care to patients. Samantha leads our Hanson office and is also responsible for training our new admin team members.

Outside of the office, Samantha spends her time with her husband Derrick, their 4 young children, and their dog Titan.

Amanda

Dental Assistant

Amanda brings over six years of experience as a Dental Assistant, including four years specializing in Oral Surgery and TMJ treatment. Her background also includes experience in General Dentistry, giving her a well-rounded understanding of patient care across multiple areas of dental health. In addition to her clinical experience, Amanda is a licensed esthetician with a strong passion for facial anatomy, muscle function, and overall wellness.

Her unique expertise in dental and aesthetics allows her to take a comprehensive approach to patient care, combining her clinical knowledge with her interest in facial health and function. Amanda is passionate about helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and supported throughout their treatment journey.

Outside of the office, Amanda enjoys spending quality time with family, exploring museums, reading, and cooking.
