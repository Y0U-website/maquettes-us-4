The practice

The TMJ & Sleep Therapy Centre of the South Shore offers our patients compassionate care for craniofacial pain and sleep-related breathing disorders.

We have always focused our dental treatment on whole body health, and a systemic approach to care. Our approach to the management of TMD, craniofacial pain and sleep disordered breathing is no different. We understand the challenges in diagnosing and addressing your pain, but we will partner with you to welcome you back to health.
