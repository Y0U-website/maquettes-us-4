A Vision for Kindness

Founded in 2019 by Dr. Johnna Thomas, Restore Dental was born from a vision to move beyond traditional dentistry and create a space where kindness and clinical excellence coexist. Our practice operates on a mission to lead with empathy, offering a calm and judgment-free environment where every patient feels genuinely valued rather than just treated. Driven by core values of integrity and empowerment, Dr. Thomas and her dedicated team are committed to fostering meaningful connections and providing high-caliber care that inspires confidence and a lifetime of healthy, happy smiles.

Johnna Thomas, D.D.S

Dr. Thomas is: Wife. Mom. Dog mom. Sister. Daughter. Aunt. Kansas Dentist. Volunteer. Dog rescuer. Entrepreneur. Lady boss. Fitness fanatic. Lover of learning & Rap music.

Dr. Johnna (John-na) Thomas grew up just outside of Kansas City on a farm. She is a first generation college graduate and dentist. Her love for learning began while achieving her first Bachelor’s degree in Biology & Psychology. A year later, she attended University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry and attained her Bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene.

She practiced as a dental hygienist in the Kansas City area for a few years, and although she felt it was a rewarding profession, she felt called to return to school in order to provide a wider variety of services for her community.

While in dental school, Dr. Thomas was a member of the coveted Senior Departmental advanced studies in restorative dentistry. She was also inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon National Dental Honor Society. She graduated from University of Missouri-Kansas City School of Dentistry Magna Cum Laude with distinction and received the Dentsply Sirona Merit Award in Removable Prosthodontics.

At Restore Dental, Dr. Thomas has created a judgment free atmosphere where patients feel comfortable and her teammates are excited to serve their community.
