Your First Visit

What to Expect

As you walk in, you’ll be greeted by the smiling faces of our welcoming team and the cozy amenities of our family-friendly office. We’ll answer any insurance-related questions you may have to make your child’s appointment as seamless as possible.

After a thorough yet gentle cleaning by one of our hygienists, you and your child will meet with Dr. Berger and get a comprehensive oral exam, complete with any necessary x-rays. We spend the extra time to address any concerns and discuss the state of your child’s dental health before outlining a unique treatment plan. Before leaving, we can also schedule any future visits.
