Teeth Cleanings

Sunset Dental performs regular cleanings and deep cleanings (scaling and root planing). Ultrasonic cleaning tools remove calcified tartar from above and below the gumline with less discomfort than manual scaling. Deep cleanings are recommended when gum pockets measure 4mm or deeper. Most patients need a professional cleaning every six months. Patients with active gum disease may need treatment every three to four months.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Composite resin fillings match your natural tooth color. Dr. Atanay removes decayed material, cleans the cavity, and places the composite directly in the tooth. The material bonds to the tooth structure and hardens in minutes. Composite fillings last 3 to 10 years depending on location and bite pressure. Sunset Dental does not place amalgam (silver) fillings.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

When decay or fracture damages a large portion of a tooth, a crown restores its shape and protects what remains. Dr. Atanay prepares the tooth, takes a digital impression, and places a temporary crown while your permanent porcelain restoration is fabricated in a dental lab. You return for the permanent placement appointment. Bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent natural teeth. Insured patients at Sunset Dental receive $100 off the crown and bridge copay.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment removes damaged or infected dental pulp from inside the tooth. Dr. Atanay cleans and shapes each canal, fills them with an inert material, seals the tooth, and typically restores it with a crown. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report it is no more uncomfortable than getting a filling. Avoiding root canal treatment when it is needed leads to infection and eventual tooth loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get a dental exam and cleaning in Ventura? Twice a year for most patients. Patients with active gum disease or high cavity risk benefit from visits every three to four months.

What is the difference between a regular cleaning and a deep cleaning? A regular cleaning removes plaque and tartar from above the gumline. A deep cleaning removes buildup from below the gumline and from the roots of the teeth. Deep cleanings treat early to moderate gum disease.

Does Sunset Dental take X-rays at every visit? No. Digital X-rays are taken as clinically needed. New patients receive a full set. Established patients receive bite-wing X-rays roughly every 12 to 18 months depending on their risk profile.
