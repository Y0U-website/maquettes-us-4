Frequently Asked Questions

How long does dental bonding last in Ventura? Bonding typically lasts one to ten years depending on location in the mouth, bite pressure, and how well it is maintained. Front teeth bonding lasts longer on average than bonding applied to chewing surfaces.

Does cosmetic dentistry near me require multiple appointments? Most bonding procedures are completed in one visit. More extensive smile design work involving multiple teeth or a combination of treatments may require two or more appointments. Dr. Atanay gives you a clear timeline before starting.

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by dental insurance? Most dental insurance plans do not cover elective cosmetic procedures. Bonding used to repair a damaged tooth may have partial coverage depending on your plan. Dr. Atanay’s team can help you check your benefits before treatment.
