Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know, All in one place

Does rootworks offer direct dental care for patients? We offer simple and comprehensive membership plans to all our patients that allows them to customize each plan that best fits their dental needs. Ask our care team about enrolling in our direct dental care.

How do I prepare for my first appointment? Before or upon your first visit, please complete our new patient forms and gather any relevant dental records or X-rays. If you have dental insurance, bring or email your insurance card and information. Arrive a few minutes early to ensure a smooth check-in process.

What are your sterilization and safety protocols? We adhere to the highest standards of sterilization and infection control, following strict guidelines set by the CDC and ADA. All instruments are thoroughly sterilized, and our team uses personal protective equipment to ensure a safe, hygienic environment for our patients.

How can I maintain my dental health between each visits? To maintain optimal oral health between visits, brush your teeth twice daily with a fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and use an alcohol free mouthwash. Maintain a balanced diet, limit sugary and acidic foods and drinks, and attend regular check-ups and cleanings every 3 or 6 months at rootworks dentistry.

Do you treat patients with dental anxiety? Yes we welcome patients with dental anxiety and do everything we can to help you feel more comfortable during your visit. Our calm, spa-like environment, gentle approach, and clear communication can make a big difference. We’ll listen to your concerns, explain each step, and move at a pace that feels manageable for you.

What services does rootworks dentistry offer? Rootworks Dentistry provides a comprehensive range of dental services, including routine check-ups and cleanings, surgical extraction, bone grafting, cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers), Invisalign® treatment, dental work and restorations (crowns, bridges, fillings), dental implants restorations, partial or complete dentures, and emergency dental services.

How can I book an appointment with rootworks dentistry? To schedule an appointment, simply call (718-849-5900 ) or email (hello@myrootworks.com) our office or use our convenient online booking system. We offer flexible scheduling options to accommodate your busy lifestyle.

What makes rootworks dentistry different from other dental practices? As an independently owned practice, rootworks dentistry stands out through our blend of cutting-edge technology, personalized care, and a calm, patient-centered environment. Our autonomy allows us to make decisions that prioritize our patients’ needs and experiences above all else.

Do you offer emergency dental services? Yes, we offer emergency dental services to address urgent issues such as severe pain, broken teeth, or lost fillings or crowns. If you experience a dental emergency, call our office immediately for prompt assistance.

What insurance plans does rootworks dentistry accept? We accept many major dental PPO insurance plans and will work with you to maximize your benefits. Our friendly staff is happy to assist you in understanding your coverage and filing claims on your behalf.
