What to expect from start to finish

Comprehensive Exam. We assess your bone density with imaging and a full exam, and walk you through exactly what your jaw needs — and why.

The Procedure. The area is fully numbed with local anesthetic. We make a small incision, gently access the bone, clean the site, and place the graft material — sometimes with a protective membrane over it to support healing. The gum is then repositioned and closed with stitches. Most patients are in and out within the hour.

Healing & Follow-Up. Initial healing — tenderness, swelling, the surgical site settling down — takes about a week. Full integration of the graft into your own bone takes longer: typically three to nine months, sometimes up to a year for larger grafts. Our dentists will see you for follow-ups along the way to confirm it’s healing exactly as it should, and let you know the moment you’re ready for the next step.

How do I care for my mouth after a bone graft? Post-operative care includes keeping the surgical site clean, avoiding strenuous activities, and following your dentist’s instructions for pain management and diet. Avoid smoking, drinking through a straw, and consuming hot or spicy foods. Attend follow-up appointments to monitor your healing progress.

Will my insurance cover it? Coverage for bone grafts varies depending on your insurance plan. Our billing team will work with you to maximize your benefits and explore financing options to make your treatment as affordable as possible.

Is a bone graft painful? Most people report little to no pain once the numbness wears off. The pain is similar when getting an tooth extraction and is manageable with local anesthesia and over-the-counter medicine for pain relief afterwards.

How long until I can get my implant? It depends on the graft type and the size of the area treated. It is typically three to nine months for full integration, occasionally up to a year for larger grafts. We’ll give you a realistic timeline at your consultation, not a generic estimate.
