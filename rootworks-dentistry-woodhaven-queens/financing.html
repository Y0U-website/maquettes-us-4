Financing

Your smile shouldn’t come with a side of financial anxiety.

At rootworks dentistry, we understand that financial concerns should not prevent you from receiving the dental care you need. Our team is committed to making high-quality dental care accessible to all patients, regardless of their insurance coverage. Our dentist & team accept most PPO insurance providers and offer flexible payment programs tailored to meet your individual needs, ensuring that you can achieve your best smile without the stress of financial barriers.

0 % Interest-Free Financing

We are pleased to offer Cherry, a financing option that provides pre-approval with a soft credit check and 0 % financing for patients.

Another flexible payment options we provide is CareCredit®, a financing solution specifically designed for healthcare and dental treatments. CareCredit allows you to finance your dental procedures with $0 down, 0 % financing and flexible payment plans. This option is ideal for patients with little or no dental insurance coverage, making it easier to receive the treatments you need. You can apply for CareCredit directly through our practice with an easy access link. For more details, visit their official website.
