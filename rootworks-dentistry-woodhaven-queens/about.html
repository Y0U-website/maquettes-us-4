About

Our goal is to deliver comprehensive care with advanced modern dentistry.

We practice dentistry like it actually matters. Because a healthy smile isn’t just cosmetic — it’s connected to how you sleep, how you feel, and how you show up in the world. We treat it that way.

Our mission

Every treatment plan we create is guided by a comprehensive view of your oral health, not just the symptoms in front of us. We take the time to understand where you are today and where you want to be — then we build a path to get you there, with no guesswork and no surprises.

Radical transparency isn’t a policy here — it’s how we operate. Before any treatment begins, you’ll know exactly what we’re recommending, why we’re recommending it, and what it will cost. Your trust is not something we take for granted.

Moreover, because we believe that excellent care and a genuinely beautiful environment aren’t mutually exclusive, our Woodhaven studio is designed to feel as good as it looks — calm, considered, and nothing like your average dental office.

The team behind your care

Behind every great patient experience is a team that genuinely cares — not just about the clinical outcome, but about how you feel when you leave.

At rootworks dentistry, our dentists and support staff share the same standard: uncompromising care. Our dentists bring deep clinical expertise and a collaborative approach to every case, staying current with the latest advancements in dental medicine so that your care reflects the best of what modern dentistry has to offer.

But what sets our team apart is the culture around that expertise. We communicate clearly, we listen carefully, and we treat every patient as a partner in their own care — not a number on a schedule. Whether you’re visiting us for a routine cleaning or a full-mouth restoration, you’ll always know what’s happening, why it matters, and what comes next.

This is dentistry done differently — in the heart of Queens, NY, by a team that holds itself to a higher standard.
