We believe in delivering a joyful dental experience

Your child will laugh with our team, enjoy a movie, choose their own flavor of toothpaste, pick fun prizes, celebrate their successes, and leave with a smile. Parents will enjoy the ease of our digital check-in system, caregiver seating in every treatment space, as well as comfortable reception, complimentary Wi-fi, and refreshments. (We even have a bathroom with a toddler toilet and a changing table.)

We believe in innovative treatment options

In addition to providing everything needed for pediatric oral health and wellness, we also offer white zirconia crowns, minimally invasive dentistry including silver diamine fluoride and SMART restorations, airway health solutions, X-ray alternatives, and more.

We believe in collaborative neighborhood care

We collaborate with parents by inviting them into the treatment rooms and involving them in the care of their children. We also work together with local businesses for oral health outreach in our community. And, we join with other pediatric wellness professionals to elevate the level of care for the children of Philadelphia.

We believe in building a foundation of wellness

Beginning with newborns, we are deeply committed to supporting nursing, childhood nutrition, cavity prevention, healthy airways, and special infant oral health needs.

We believe in the uniqueness of your child

One size does not fit all because every child is one of a kind. That’s why we listen closely, call them by name, learn their little personality, and tailor treatment to fit the distinctive needs and values of your family.

We believe children deserve state-of-the-art technology

Some of our leading dental technologies include digital radiography and X-ray alternatives, Lightscalpel (trademark) Co2 Laser, scanner impressions, and fully online registration and communication system. All this technology has a team member on the other side, giving you a balance of both worlds.
