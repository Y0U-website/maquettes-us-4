General Dentistry

Two dental visits a year go a long way in keeping you and your family’s teeth clean and healthy. Dr. Teng have invested the time, resources, and energy into advanced training and education in order to offer a wide range of services to our valued patients.

Preventative Dentistry

Preventative dentistry involves establishing habits that will keep your teeth health. Dr. Teng will point out any issues that you may be having with your oral hygiene. We will also evaluate the appearance of your teeth and recommend any aesthetic procedures that will enhance your smile.

Periodontal Care

Early stages of periodontal disease are best treated or prevented with nonsurgical therapy. Even in severe cases of periodontal disease, there are nonsurgical therapies that we recommend prior to surgery. Dr. Teng’s philosophy is to limit surgery in order to protect the overall tissue quality of a patient’s mouth.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Teng understands that your smile is part of who you are and your identity. Your smile is one of the first things that people notice about you. Patients seeking cosmetic dental treatments often comment to Dr. Teng that they hide their smile because they don’t feel comfortable or confident enough with their smile. They’re coming to Starry Dental for the best cosmetic solution that they can find.

Dental Implant

Dental implant surgery is a procedure that replaces tooth roots with metal, screwlike posts and replaces damaged or missing teeth with artificial teeth that look and function much like real ones. It is a welcome alternative solution to dentures or bridgework that doesn’t fit well and can offer an option when a lack of natural teeth roots doesn’t allow building denture or bridgework tooth replacements.
