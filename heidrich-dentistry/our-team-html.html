Meet the Doctor

Paul Daniel Heidrich III, Skip, was born and raised in Winter Park, FL. Skip graduated from Winter Park High Class of 2000. From there he went to the University of Florida and received his Bachelor of Science with a major in Zoology. After the University of Florida, Skip attended dental school in Ft. Lauderdale at Nova Southeastern University. He graduated in 2009 with a D.M.D and in the top 20 % of his class. He furthered his training at the Pankey Institute and has completed other post-graduate courses.

Skip enjoys the outdoors in his time off. He spends time hunting, fishing, and playing golf.

Meet Our Team

Piper - Chief Morale officer

Say hello to Piper, our sweet and friendly office pup! Piper is more than just a pretty face she’s an important part of our dental family. With her gentle nature and wagging tail, she helps create a calm and welcoming environment for patients of all ages.

Karen - Dental Assistant

With more than 40 years of experience in the dental field, Karen is a highly skilled and dedicated dental assistant who has taken pride in being a major part of the Heidrich Dentistry team for more than a decade. Known for her warm personality and meticulous attention to detail, Karen provides exceptional care to every patient she encounters. Karen is a native Floridian and on her days off she enjoys spending time at the beach with friends and family.

Andrea - Dental Hygienist

Andrea was born and raised in Ocala, Florida. She attended dental hygiene school at Santa Fe Community College in Gainesville, Florida. Andrea has been practicing dental hygiene since 1993. When Andrea is not working, she enjoys spending time with her family and two dogs.

Teri - Dental Hygienist

A registered hygienist for 21 years and almost 30 years in the dental field, Teri thoroughly enjoys what she does and cares tremendously about her patients dental health care. When she’s not in the office she enjoys meditation and spending time with her family.

Dina-Marie - Dental Hygienist

Dina-Marie was born in Winter Park, Florida, and has been a hygienist for 41 years. She went to the University of South Florida and St. Petersburg college for dental hygiene. She enjoys playing golf, working out, going to the movies, and traveling.

Ariana - Financial Coordinator

Ariana is a dedicated Financial Coordinator with a deep passion for helping others navigate their dental care journey. With 10 years of experience in the dental field, she enjoys building meaningful relationships with patients and ensuring they receive the best possible care at Heidrich Dentistry. On her days off she enjoys spending quality time with her family and puppy dog!
