Services

Implants Restoration

Fill Gaps in Your Teeth with Dental Implants & Restoration

Are you missing a tooth or a few? Are you looking for replacements for cracked and decaying teeth? Dental implants are the solution! Our dental specialists can restore your smile with long-lasting, natural-looking dental implants . New implants can help:

  • Prevent Bone Loss
  • Keep Adjacent Teeth Stable
  • Reduce the Risk of Gum Disease
  • Enable Natural Speech
  • Permanently Replace Teeth

Cleaning Check-Ups

Keep Your Mouth Healthy with Routine Teeth Cleaning & Check-Ups

Teeth cleanings are essential for preserving your teeth and protecting your smile. For children and adults, routine cleanings and check-ups allow dentists to check your teeth for cavities and prevent further decay. As such, from teeth cleaning, you can expect:

  • Gum Disease Prevention
  • Protection from Cavities
  • Good Smelling Breath
  • Removal of Plaque and Tartar Build-Up

Fillings

Keep Cavities in Check with Composite or Metal Tooth Fillings

Cavities can be a painful experience, leading to further tooth decay issues if left unchecked. Thankfully, River City Family Dentistry can supply you with the relief you need with tooth fillings . We remove any dental decay and fill your cavity with a composite or metal filling . Fillings are especially helpful for:

  • Stopping Pain
  • Strengthening Teeth
  • Preventing Further Decay
  • Restoring a Tooth’s Natural Look

Crowns Bridges

Restore Broken & Misshapen Teeth with Crowns & Bridges

No one’s teeth are perfect. However, teeth that are broken or misshapen look unattractive and can lead to further bone issues or tooth decay. Our dentists can form these problem teeth down and secure a crown to one tooth or a bridge to multiple teeth. Dental crowns and bridges resolve:

  • Uneven Smiles
  • Missing Teeth
  • Difficulty Chewing
  • Oral Health Issues
  • Tooth Pain

Dentures

Never Lose Your Smile with Dentures and Partials

Unfortunately, certain circumstances can lead to losing most or all of your teeth. If such is the case, River City Family Dentistry can help restore your smile with new dentures or partials . Dentures are easily removable teeth that are molded to precisely fit your mouth. With dentures or partials , you can look forward to:

  • Restoring Your Smile
  • Keeping Facial Muscles from Sagging
  • Proper Chewing
  • Gum Protection

Teeth Whitening

Brighten Up Your Teeth with Professional Teeth Whitening

Yellow and stained teeth can grow unsightly and unattractive after a long while. Thankfully, there are methods for restoring your teeth’s original color . Through take-home teeth whitening, our office can transform the appearance of your teeth via non-invasive procedures. Our patients have found teeth whitening to:

  • Restore Issues of Self-Esteem
  • Improve Oral Health
  • Affordably Improve Appearance
  • Show Results Relatively Quick

Invisalign

Straighten Your Teeth Using Invisalign® Clear Aligners

Unstraightened teeth are an issue often resolved in childhood with braces. However, if uneven teeth have become an issue in your adult life, and you wish to forgo the complication of braces, Invisalign® clear aligners offer a practical alternative . Unlike braces, clear aligners have the benefit of being:

  • Completely Transparent
  • Easy to Use
  • Comfortable
  • Custom Molded
  • An Effective Teeth Straightening Procedure
