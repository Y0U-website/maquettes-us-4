Meet The Doctor

Morgan Renner DMD, MS

Proud parent of two boys: Griffin and Hendrix

Enjoys being outdoors, traveling and cheering on Iowa State in all sports

Dr. Morgan Renner

Dr. Morgan Renner was born and raised in Rock Island, IL, a native of the Quad Cities! After completing her undergraduate degree at Iowa State University in Biology, she attended Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, where she obtained her Master of Science in Biological Sciences. Dr. Renner then continued on to the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine, where she earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD). It was while attending Iowa State that Dr. Renner met her husband, Nick. The happy couple now has two beautiful bright boys, Griffin and Hendrix (Hank).

With her dental practice in the Heart of Illinois, Dr. Renner is excited to call Peoria, IL home with her family! When she’s not busy being a dentist, Dr. Renner enjoys spending time with her husband and children, being outdoors, traveling, and cheering on Iowa State in all sports. In talking with Dr. Renner, she’ll tell you her husband and children inspired her to be a better person and have shaped the person she is today.

I’m committed to getting to know each of my patients and treating each with respect to their individual needs. I hope to provide an atmosphere of openness, with attention to your comfort, all while delivering great service. – Dr. Morgan Renner
