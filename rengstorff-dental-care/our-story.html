OUR STORY

Dr. Serena Hu graduated from the University of the Pacific dental school in 1996. After graduating, Dr. Hu worked at various clinics in the Palo Alto area for seven years before starting her own practice.

After one year of preparation, Rengstorff Dental Care opened its doors in February of 2003 as a brand new practice. Our practice has steadily grown since opening to include its first associate dentist in 2007. Almost all of our staff, dentists, hygienists, dental assistants, and receptionists in this office are proficient in English or another language (Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Vietnamese, and Spanish). All of our dentists speak English and either Cantonese or Mandarin.

Rengstorff Dental Care is located in the Rengstorff Shopping Center in Mountain View.

Since opening, Rengstorff Dental Care has served almost 10,000 patients from all around the Bay Area. Many of our patients come from Google, Stanford University, and Apple due to our central location in Mountain View and the technology boom of Silicon Valley.

As Rengstorff Dental Care celebrates 16 years of practice, Dr. Hu and her staff would like to thanks all their amazing patients and invite new patients to join the Rengstorff Dental Care family!

"Our entire team is dedicated to providing the personalized gentle care that you deserve! We are also committed to educating our patients with information that helps them make informed decisions about their oral health needs."
