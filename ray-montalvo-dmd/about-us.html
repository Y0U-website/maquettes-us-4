Meet Our

Team Of Professionals

For more than 30 years, Dr. Russell B. Rainey has been recognized for gentleness, dental excellence, professionalism, advanced dental education and his unwavering commitment to care for the uniqueness of each patient. Making a difference in the lives of others is Dr. Rainey’s goal.

Dr. Ray Montalvo has called Tallahassee home since graduating from dental school and has grown to love the community here. His passion lies in helping patients realize their own power in taking charge of their health. Holistic practices guide his treatment planning, and truly listening to patients’ needs and wants is essential in this process. He believes great outcomes involve being on the same page as his patients, whether the goal is to create the “dream smile,” or simply to maintain good oral health.

Cindy has been working with Dr. Rainey for over 28 years. He and Cindy met in Quincy, Florida, where Dr. Rainey bought a practice after he graduated from dental school. Cindy earned her certifications from Tallahassee Community College and the Las Vegas Institute. Cindy is highly skilled in cosmetic dentistry and truly enjoys her role in helping to make our patients feel great and confident about their smile.

Jane has been with Dr. Rainey since 2003, and she has over 24 years of experience as a dental assistant. She loves what she does every day. Making someone smile again is truly a wonderful feeling and seeing the impact our team can make on someone’s life is a profound experience.

Jacque has been in dentistry for over 36 years and with Dr. Rainey for 20 of those! Jacque is our clinical and treatment coordinator and works with the new patient experience. She appreciates that you have entrusted us with your dental needs.

Marie joined the practice in 2005 and wears many hats! In addition to being one of the first voices you might hear on the phone, or meet when you first arrive at our office, she also assists patients with their financial arrangements and helps them schedule treatment. Marie also handles many of the administrative and marketing responsibilities of the office. She thoroughly enjoys getting to know our patients and reassuring them that they have made the right choice in their dental care.
