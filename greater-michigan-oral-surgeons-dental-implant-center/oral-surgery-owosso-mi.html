All-on-4 ® Dental Implants

Our oral surgeons offer the All-on-4 ® treatment concept, a state-of-the-art approach to full mouth rehabilitation. They can restore entire rows of teeth using just a handful of strategically positioned implants.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

When left in place, wisdom teeth can contribute to several long-term oral health concerns, including persistent discomfort and crowding/spacing issues. Our doctors will keep you comfortable and safe through preemptive extraction.
