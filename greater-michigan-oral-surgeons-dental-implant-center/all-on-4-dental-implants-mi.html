What is the All-on-4® Treatment Concept?

The All-on-4® treatment concept is widely considered to be the gold standard for full mouth rehabilitation. This treatment method involves the use of as few as four dental implants. Each implant consists of a titanium post, which is inserted into the jaw to act as an artificial tooth root, as well as a connective piece called an abutment. Once the implant fuses with the existing jawbone, your oral surgeon and a dentist will carefully attach custom dental prostheses designed to look and behave just like your natural teeth.

This treatment concept can restore entire dental arches, allowing you to eat and chew as normal again and smile confidently.

Who Is the Best Candidate for Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Most people who are missing multiple teeth, or who anticipate the need to have multiple teeth extracted, can qualify for All-on-4® dental implants or full mouth dental implants. The best way to confirm your eligibility is to schedule a consultation at Greater Michigan Oral Surgeons & Dental Implant Center.

Most full mouth restoration candidates meet the following criteria:

Missing Teeth

All-on-4® dental implants are designed for anyone missing several teeth, including entire dental arches, or whose current teeth are no longer viable.

Healthy Enough for Surgery

To qualify for a safe and successful procedure, it is important to confirm that you do not have any underlying health problems that would impede your ability to heal.

No Gum Disease

Patients who have untreated periodontal disease are not eligible for oral surgery. If you have significant gum disease, the first step will be managing the condition and scheduling dental implant placement.

What Are the Benefits of All-on-4® Dental Implants?

The All-on-4® treatment concept is widely considered standard-of-care for full-mouth rehabilitation. Some of the primary benefits of this treatment method include:

  • All-on-4® dental implants replace teeth from root to crown, providing more stability and better overall health than other tooth replacement options.
  • The All-on-4® treatment concept replaces the maximum number of teeth with the minimum number of implants (about 4 implants per arch of the smile replaced). This means minimal dental implant surgery and potentially an abbreviated recovery time.
  • Unlike traditional dentures, the All-on-4® method provides ongoing stimulation to the jawbone, preventing bone reabsorption and unwanted changes to the facial structure.

Meet Your Doctors

Your All-on-4® Dental Implant Experience

All-on-4 treatment is designed to restore your full smile with strength and stability. Our team guides you through every stage with attentive care and clear communication, ensuring you feel confident in your treatment plan.

1. Your Personal Consultation

Your experience begins with a one-on-one consultation with one of our oral surgeons. During this visit, your surgeon will review your dental and medical history, take detailed 3D CBCT scans, and discuss your goals for full-arch restoration. You’ll learn what to expect and have time to ask any questions before moving forward.

2. Planning and Preparation

To ensure the best results, your surgeon will design a customized treatment plan based on your anatomy and desired outcome. Some patients may need preparatory procedures, such as a tooth extraction or strengthening the jaw with a bone graft . You’ll receive clear instructions to help you prepare for surgery and recovery.

3. Day of Your Procedure

When you arrive, our care team will make sure you feel comfortable and ready. Your surgeon will place four precisely positioned dental implants in the jawbone to support your new set of teeth. Advanced imaging and guided technology help ensure accuracy and long-term stability. The surgery is completed in one visit, allowing most patients to leave with a secure temporary restoration the same day.

4. Recovery and Healing

You’ll rest and recover at home for a few days while the implants begin to fuse naturally with your jawbone. During this time, you’ll follow a soft-food diet and gentle care routine to support healing. Minor soreness is common and can be managed with medication as recommended by your surgeon.

5. Your New Smile

After the healing phase, your final, custom-designed restoration will be securely attached. The result is a confident, natural-looking smile that feels strong and stable for years to come.

Recovery and Aftercare

Following your dental implant placement, you will want to rest and heal for a week or so. During this period, you can expect some swelling and discomfort. Prescribed and over-the-counter medications are available to help keep you as comfortable as possible.

We recommend following a few essential aftercare tips to achieve excellent results and expedite recovery:

  • Try to avoid chewing as much as possible during recovery. Stick to nutritious foods that require little or no chewing. Be especially careful to avoid chewing directly on the sites of implant placement.
  • Avoid food that is hot, spicy, or high in sugar.
  • Follow all your oral surgeon’s instructions to keep your smile healthy and clean while wearing the temporary prosthetic.
