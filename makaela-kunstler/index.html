Services

During your 6 month visits we take care in thoroughly examining your oral health including taking any necessary x-rays, an oral cancer screening and intraoral pictures so that you can see for yourself any problems or concerns.

We use only tooth-colored fillings and crowns and are often able to make repairs to chipped teeth with filling materials that look and feel natural. If a filling in a tooth gets too big the tooth gets weak and at this point we recommend a full coverage crown…

We offer a wide range of options to replace missing teeth such as implants, bridges, removable partial dentures and complete dentures. We will discuss each option and make a personalized treatment plan to suit your needs.

We offer a wide range of dental procedures that can improve your smile, from veneers to Invisalign. We can fabricate a "mock-up" to show you what the veneers will look like before we make them.
