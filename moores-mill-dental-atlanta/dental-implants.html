Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants come with many benefits that can improve your everyday life:

  • Natural Look and Feel : Dental implants are designed to blend in with your natural teeth, so your smile will look great.
  • Eat and Speak with Confidence : Unlike dentures, which can slip or feel uncomfortable, dental implants stay securely in place. This lets you eat your favorite foods and speak clearly.
  • Prevent Bone Loss : Missing teeth can cause your jawbone to shrink. Dental implants help preserve the bone and keep your facial shape the same.
  • Durable and Long-Lasting : Dental implants are made to last a long time. With proper care, they can serve you well for many years.

The Process of Getting Dental Implants

Getting dental implants usually involves a few important steps. First, our Atlanta dentist will check your oral health through exams and X-rays to see if you’re a good candidate for implants. If you are, the next step is placing a titanium post into your jawbone. This post acts as a replacement for the root of the missing tooth.

After the post is placed, you’ll need several months for the bone to heal and bond with the implant in a process called osseointegration. Once the implant is fully healed, our dentist will attach a custom-made crown that matches your natural teeth. Follow-up visits are important to make sure the implant is working properly and stays securely in place.
