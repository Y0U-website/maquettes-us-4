What to Expect from Orthodontic Treatment

The earliest people start treatment is around 12 or so, when the adult teeth have already developed. The most common exception would be a child with a cleft lip, which may require intervention before the adult teeth have the chance to come in.

To diagnose the problem the orthodontist will get a full oral health history from the patient, take X-rays of the teeth and jaw, and perform a full examination before developing a plan. In this field, treatment is always tailored to fit the individual’s needs.

If you’re interested in finding an orthodontist in St. Paul, MN , it’s crucial to find an expert who will structure a treatment that will fit both your lifestyle and preferences.
