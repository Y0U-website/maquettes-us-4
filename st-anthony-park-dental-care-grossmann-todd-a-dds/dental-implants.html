Dental Implants in St. Paul, MN

Dental implants are safe and have extremely high rates of success. You won’t have to worry about replacing them in five years, developing an infection after the procedure, or the cost of another pair of dentures.

  • Comfort: Remember what it was like to have a full set of teeth? Whether you’re eating, going for a bike ride, or just watching TV, people with dental implants get back many of the luxuries afforded to those with their natural teeth.
  • Better oral health: When your mouth is intact, it’s easier to take care of. Instead of putting all that extra pressure on the teeth you do have, you can let the dental implant take some of the burden off. Plus, implants prevent your other teeth from shifting or sliding to fill the gaps.

What Can I Expect During the Procedure?

Dental implants in St. Paul, MN are multi-step:

  • Custom treatment: The initial examination will evaluate your oral health, so the dentist can determine a personalized plan for you.
  • For a single-tooth implant, a small post will be placed into the bone socket of the missing tooth. As the jaw heals, it will grow around the post and secure it to the bones. This can take anywhere from 6 – 12 weeks, during which time you’ll have a temporary crown.
  • Once the implant has sufficiently attached itself, the abutment will be placed to hold the tooth. The dentist will create a mold of your teeth to ensure the abutment and new crown will match your other teeth.

Is Everyone Eligible for Dental Implants?

Unfortunately, not everyone will be eligible for dental implants. If the jawbone has begun to deteriorate, it may not be able to fully support the implant. There are also other factors, including health conditions or lifestyle choices (e.g., smoking, etc.) that may disqualify you from the procedure. Your dentist may be able to recommend other treatments if you’re not eligible for the procedure.
