What is the best way to replace missing teeth?

Dental implants are considered one of the most effective tooth replacement options. Because the dental implant is actually implanted in the jawbone, it is exceptionally secure and natural feeling, unlike removable partial or full dentures. Dental implants typically require no adjustment to the adjacent teeth (as dental bridges do), keeping the rest of your teeth in optimal health. A dental implant can enhance the appearance of your smile and improve your ability to chew, eat, and speak as normal.
