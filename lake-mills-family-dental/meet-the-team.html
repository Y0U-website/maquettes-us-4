Meet The Team

When you choose Dr. Boysen as your dentist, you can trust that you are in the hands of a skilled professional who not only values your oral health but also appreciates the importance of a warm and caring patient-dentist relationship. Join us at Lake Mills Family Dental for a dental experience focused on your well-being and delivered with expertise and compassion.
