Preventative Care, An Essential Step in Oral Care

At Lake Mills Family Dental, we prioritize preventive care, recognizing its crucial role in maintaining optimal oral health and minimizing the need for extensive dental treatments. As your trusted Lake Mills dentist, Dr. Joseph Boysen, DMD, emphasizes regular check-ups and cleanings as essential components of effective dental care.

For patients not requiring periodontal treatment, we recommend scheduling appointments every six months. During these visits, you’ll undergo a thorough cleaning, comprehensive examination, and an oral cancer screening. Our commitment to your well-being includes the use of digital bitewing x-rays at your yearly exam, especially if you’re prone to decay. These x-rays aid in early detection, enabling timely intervention to prevent further issues.

Additionally, periapical x-rays of your front and back teeth are taken as needed to check for cavities or the presence of any infection. This comprehensive approach ensures that potential dental concerns are identified and addressed promptly.

As part of our commitment to advanced dental care, a panorex of the upper and lower jaw is utilized for oral cancer screening and visualizing the roots and bone around the teeth. This panoramic x-ray, typically taken every 3-5 years, provides a comprehensive view that aids in early detection and thorough assessment of your oral health.

At Lake Mills Family Dental, our focus on preventive care, combined with cutting-edge dental technology, ensures that you receive personalized, comprehensive dental care that promotes a healthy and confident smile.
