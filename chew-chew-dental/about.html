Meet Dr. James Chiu

Dr. James Chiu has practiced dentistry since 2011. His approach begins with listening carefully and paying attention to the details. He takes time to examine your oral health, explain what he finds in clear language, and answer questions before treatment begins.

When treatment is needed, Dr. Chiu focuses on relieving discomfort, addressing the underlying problem, and helping prevent more serious complications. Patients appreciate his gentle approach, clear explanations, and commitment to quality work.
