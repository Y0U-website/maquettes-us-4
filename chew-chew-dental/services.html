Emergency Dentistry

If you are experiencing a toothache, swelling, a broken tooth, or another urgent dental concern, call our office. We’re here to help.

Emergency concerns may include: +

  • Severe or persistent tooth pain
  • Dental infections or swelling
  • Broken or cracked teeth
  • Lost fillings or crowns
  • Dental injuries

Restorative Dentistry

When a tooth is damaged, weakened, or affected by decay, restorative dentistry can help protect it and restore comfortable function.

Restorative services may include: +

  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Dental crowns
  • Dental bridges
  • Replacement of damaged restorations
  • Treatment for cracked or weakened teeth
  • Full-mouth restorative planning

Missing Teeth Solutions

Missing teeth can affect how you eat, speak, and feel about your smile. We offer several options for replacing missing teeth.

Treatment options may include: +

  • Dental implants
  • Implant-supported restorations
  • Dental bridges
  • Full dentures
  • Partial dentures
  • Replacement of older tooth restorations

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry can improve the color, shape, spacing, or overall appearance of your teeth. Dr. Chiu will recommend treatment options based on your oral health and goals.

Cosmetic services may include: +

  • Professional teeth whitening
  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Cosmetic bonding
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Dental crowns
  • Smile improvement planning

Oral Surgery

Sometimes a tooth cannot be restored or may be contributing to pain, infection, or other complications. Dr. Chiu provides careful, efficient surgical treatments.

Oral surgery services may include: +

  • Simple tooth extractions
  • Surgical extractions
  • Removal of damaged or infected teeth
  • Dental implant placement
  • Treatment planning after an extraction

Not Sure Which Service You Need?

You do not need to diagnose the problem before contacting us. Tell our team what you are experiencing, whether it is pain, sensitivity, a damaged tooth, missing teeth, or simply time for a checkup. We will help you schedule the appropriate type of visit.
