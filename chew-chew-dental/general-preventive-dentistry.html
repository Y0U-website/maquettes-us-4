What we offer

Preventive Care Built Around Your Oral Health

No two smiles are exactly alike. Your visit will be based on your dental history, current concerns, and what Dr. Chiu sees during the examination.

Dental Exams

Dr. Chiu carefully examines your teeth, gums, bite, and existing dental work to look for signs of decay, infection, wear, or other concerns.

Professional Teeth Cleanings

Professional cleanings remove plaque and hardened tartar from areas that can be difficult to reach at home, helping support healthier teeth and gums.

Gum Health Evaluations

We check for signs of gingivitis and gum disease, including bleeding, inflammation, recession, and changes in the bone supporting your teeth.

Periodontal Care

When gum disease is present, more focused treatment may be recommended to reduce bacteria and help protect the tissues and bone supporting your teeth.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride can help strengthen tooth enamel and provide added protection for patients who may be more prone to cavities.

Dental Sealants

Sealants create a protective barrier over the deep grooves of certain teeth, helping reduce the risk of decay in areas that are harder to clean.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Routine dental visits may include an examination of the mouth and surrounding tissues for unusual changes that may need further evaluation.

Nightguards and Protective Appliances

A custom appliance may help protect your teeth if you grind or clench, especially while sleeping.
