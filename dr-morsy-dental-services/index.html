Dr. Morsy Dental Services is a Riverside based family dentist practice that treats patients of all ages. We are committed to delivering the highest quality dental care and do so using advanced equipment. We are a cosmetic dentist that offers services like checkups, teeth cleaning, crowns and more.

Dr. Morsy Dental Services provides a wide variety of dental services and procedures to address all of our patients’ dental health needs. By combining quality patient care with up-to-date technology, we are able to treat patients of all ages in a comfortable and relaxing setting.

Dr. Nevine Morsy brings extensive experience to her practice, having graduated from Cairo University School of Oral Medicine and Dental Surgery in 1991. After relocating to California, she completed the UCLA dental program and began serving patients in 2001.

Dr. Morsy is passionate about helping patients achieve optimal oral health and aesthetics, utilizing modern technology and prioritizing patient comfort. She and her dedicated team are committed to creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere. We look forward to meeting you and your family.
