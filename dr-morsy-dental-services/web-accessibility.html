Web Accessibility

At Dr. Morsy Dental, we are committed to providing a website that is accessible for everyone and in compliance with the American with Disabilities Act. As part of this commitment, we are continually improving our website to make it more accessible.

Dr. Morsy Dental uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) published by Web Accessibility Initiative (WAI) of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) in order to stay up-to-date on guidelines for web accessibility. While we cannot guarantee that every page conforms to the WCAG Level AA 2.0 standards presented in these guidelines, we strive to make all of our webpages on drmorsydental.com accessible. Third-party platforms that link from drmorsydental.com may not conform to these same standards.

For more information on WCAG guidelines you can visit https://www.w3.org/WAI/intro/wcag.php .
