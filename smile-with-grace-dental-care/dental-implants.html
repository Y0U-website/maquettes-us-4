The Dental Implant Process

Receiving a full Ridgefield Park dental implant typically involves several stages, coordinated with a surgical partner for the placement phase:

Consultation and treatment planning. Dr. Grace evaluates your oral health, reviews digital x-rays, and discusses your goals. We’ll determine whether you’re a strong candidate for dental implants and create a personalized treatment plan.

Implant placement. The implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Smile with Grace coordinates this step with an experienced surgical partner as part of your overall treatment plan.

Healing and osseointegration. Over the following months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone. This is typically a three- to six-month process.

Custom restoration. Once your implant is fully integrated, we take a digital scan of your bite and design your custom crown, bridge, or denture. The final restoration is securely attached to the implant.

Follow-up care. Routine cleanings and exams help your implant last for decades. We’ll see you at regular intervals to keep everything healthy.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good general health with adequate jawbone density are candidates for dental implants. During your consultation, Dr. Grace will assess factors such as overall health, bone density, and oral hygiene habits to determine the best approach.

Patients with reduced bone density may still be candidates with the help of preparatory procedures such as bone grafting (which we offer using socket preservation techniques and bovine grafting material). If a graft is recommended, we’ll explain why and what to expect.
