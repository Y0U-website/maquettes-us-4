The Root-Planing and Scaling Procedure

The following are the steps involved in a proper scaling and root planing:

  • First, Dr. Grace takes a look at your gums and determines how much tartar and plaque have built up.
  • To make sure you don’t feel anything during the procedure, a local anesthetic will be used to numb the area.
  • Scaling is the process by which Dr. Grace uses instruments to scrape away tartar and plaque from the tooth’s surface and under the gums. Infection can be stopped in its tracks at this point.
  • Smoothing the root surfaces of a tooth to stop additional plaque and tartar accumulation is called root planing. The gums can then heal around the tooth.
  • An antibacterial rinse can be used to get rid of any lingering bacteria and jumpstart the healing process.
  • Appointments will be set up so that Dr. Grace may check on your gum health and make any required adjustments as time goes on.

Signs you need Scaling and Root Planning

  • Formation of deep pockets
  • Bleeding from the gums
  • Checking for bone loss
  • Shrinking gum line
  • Swelled and inflamed tissues
