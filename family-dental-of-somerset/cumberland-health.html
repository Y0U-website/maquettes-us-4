How On-Demand Virtual Care Works

The Cumberland Health app connects you with a trusted provider through a secure video visit. You can request on-demand care for common, non-emergency health concerns or schedule an appointment for select services.

Simply download the app, create your secure account, choose the type of care you need, and follow the prompts to begin your visit.

What health concerns can be treated virtually?

Virtual care may be appropriate for common, non-emergency health concerns, including cold and flu symptoms, allergies, sore throats, sinus concerns, minor skin conditions, nausea, and medication questions. The Cumberland Health app also provides convenient access to primary care, pediatrics, women’s health, behavioral health, substance use disorder services, follow-up appointments, and consultations to review lab and diagnostic results. Service availability may vary, and some services may require a scheduled appointment.
