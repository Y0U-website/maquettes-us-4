Dental Implants

  • Are Dental Implants Painful? Most patients experience minimal discomfort during the implant procedure, thanks to anesthesia and sedation options. Any post-procedure soreness can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relief.
  • What Are Full-Mouth Dental Implants? Full-mouth dental implants replace an entire arch of teeth using a few strategically placed implants. This option offers a stable, natural-looking solution for those needing a complete smile makeover.
