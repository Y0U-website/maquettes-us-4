Meet Dr. Shilpa Sojin

Dr. Shilpa Sojin is a compassionate dentist dedicated to creating healthy, confident smiles through gentle, patient-centered care. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, where she developed a strong commitment to providing high-quality dental care in a comfortable and positive environment.

At Perry Smiles, Dr. Sojin strives to make every visit feel welcoming, stress-free, and personalized to each patient’s needs. She believes dentistry is a blend of precision and compassion, and she is passionate about helping patients feel confident in their smiles while supporting their long-term oral health.

Outside the clinic, Dr. Sojin enjoys dancing, painting, and traveling with her family. She finds inspiration and joy in creativity, new experiences, and spending meaningful time with the people she loves.

Dr. Sojin is excited to serve the Perry Smiles community and help patients of all ages achieve healthy, beautiful smiles.
