Important to understand: It is your employer who chooses your dental plan coverage, and plans vary from employer to employer, sometimes drastically. It is the insurance company who will dictate what is and isn’t covered. It is your responsibility to make payment for any reimbursements not paid for by your insurance company.

  • Follow-up with the insurance company to ensure timely payment
  • Request dental insurance pre-authorizations (when needed)
  • Navigate the sometimes challenging dental insurance network
  • Inform us immediately if there is any change in your insurance coverage
  • Understand and pay your dental coverage co-payments at each visit
  • Understand that you are responsible for portions not covered by your dental insurance
  • Monitor your dental coverage limits and recognize when payment will need to be made out-of-pocket
