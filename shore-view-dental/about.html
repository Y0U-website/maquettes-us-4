About Shoreview Dental Pacifica

A Legacy Built on Trust & Innovation

When Dr. Ahani and Dr. Vafi opened Shoreview Dental in 1997, they had a simple vision: create a dental practice where cutting-edge technology enhances, not replaces, the human connection.

Our First Patient: Annette took a chance on us in 1997 when we didn’t even have an appointment book yet. She’s still with us today, all of these years later.

What started as a husband-and-wife team in a small Pacifica office has grown into a multi-generational practice serving thousands of families. Yet, we’ve never lost sight of what matters most: treating every patient like family.

Today, we’re proud to combine state-of-the-art technology with the warmth and personal attention that has defined us from day one.

Our Journey

Dr. Ahani and Dr. Vafi open Shoreview Dental, welcoming their first patient, Annette.

Expanded our practice into the space next door and upgraded to digital X-rays, paperless systems, and advanced technology.

Shoreview Dental opened its doors at its current location, moving into a beautiful state-of-the-art facility designed with patient comfort in mind.

Dr. Mahooti joins the team, bringing expertise in dental implants and restorative care.

Dr. Dezham joins, expanding our Invisalign and cosmetic dentistry services.

Celebrating almost 3 decades of serving Pacifica families with the latest technology and same personal care.
