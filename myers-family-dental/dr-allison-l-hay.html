0 Dr. Allison L. Hay Originally from Texas, Dr. Hay earned her Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Engineering from Mississippi State University before pursuing her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at The Dental College of Georgia.

Since moving to the Augusta area, Dr. Hay and her husband have built strong roots in the local community. They are active members of Central Church of Christ and are proud to call Augusta home. Outside of the office, Dr. Hay enjoys spending time with her husband and son, reading, and needlepointing.

Dr. Hay is passionate about helping each patient feel comfortable and confident in their smile. Her approach to dentistry centers on compassion, education, and individualized treatment plans designed to meet each patient’s unique needs. She truly enjoys building lasting relationships with her patients while helping them achieve healthier smiles.
