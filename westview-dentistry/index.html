The practice

Westview Dentistry has been part of the St. Cloud community for more than 20 years. In that time, we have built a team that genuinely enjoys coming to work, and you’ll see it in every interaction. They are caring, supportive, and excited to help you on your way to your healthiest smile.

We schedule one patient at a time, so you will never feel rushed or overlooked. When you are here, you have our full attention. Because we take the time to listen, you’ll always leave us feeling that your concerns have been heard and addressed.

Dr. Chase Liska has been practicing dentistry since 2018. He grew up in Buffalo, near St. Cloud, and wanted to stay close to the community he knew. When this practice became available, it felt like the perfect fit.

Dr. Liska chose dentistry because he wanted a healthcare career, working in an area requiring skill and precision. He is drawn to the hands-on, detail-oriented nature of dental work, and it shows in the care he brings to every appointment. He sees patients of all ages and takes pride in making every visit feel calm and unhurried.
