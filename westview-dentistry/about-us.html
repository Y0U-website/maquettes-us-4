Meet the Team at Westview Dentistry

Care that feels personal never happens by accident. It takes a team that communicates well, works together smoothly, and genuinely cares about the people walking through the door.

Where Teamwork Meets Personalized Care

At Westview Dentistry, that is exactly what we have built. Our team has worked together for years, and it shows in the way your visit flows from the moment you check in to the moment you leave. We ask about your day because we genuinely want to know how you re doing, and we remember what matters to you. We celebrate when your oral health improves, and we support you when it is a work in progress.

Dr. Liska and our team will always be honest with you about what we see and what we recommend. But we are also in your corner, cheering you on, answering your questions, and making sure you never feel judged for where you are starting from.

At the end of the day, we want you to feel like you’re visiting a place where you’re known, respected, and always welcome. We hope it feels that way every time you visit.

Dr. Chase Liska

Dr. Liska earned his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry degree from the Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine (LECOM) in Bradenton, Florida. Drawn to a field that blends science, problem-solving, and hands-on skill, he was further inspired by how profoundly oral health affects overall well-being and confidence. He has been practicing dentistry since 2018.

At the heart of Dr. Liska s practice is a commitment to creating a calm, trustworthy environment for both patients and his team. He takes pride in easing anxiety, listening carefully to concerns, and building the kind of trust that transforms apprehensive patients into confident ones. He values the same spirit of collaboration and mutual respect within his team, knowing that a well-supported staff translates directly into a better experience for every patient.

Outside of work, Dr. Liska and his family keep busy with two active young boys. They enjoy time on the lake, boating and fishing, and traveling when the opportunity arises. One of his favorite ways to recharge is heading out fishing with his son, having a chance to slow down and enjoy the outdoors together.
