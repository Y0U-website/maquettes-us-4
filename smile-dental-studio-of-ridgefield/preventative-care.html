Sealants

At Smile Dental Studio in Ridgefield, NJ , we offer dental sealants as a simple, effective way to protect teeth from decay. Dr. Jamie Hwang often recommends sealants for children and teens, but they’re also great for adults with deep grooves in their molars.

What Are Sealants?

Sealants are thin, protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth. They block out food particles and bacteria, helping to prevent cavities in hard-to-clean areas.

Quick & Painless Protection

Sealants are a smart investment in long-term oral health.

  • Applied in minutes
  • No drilling or numbing needed
  • Lasts several years with proper care
