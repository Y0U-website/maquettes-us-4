Crowns & Bridges

At Smile Dental Studio , Dr. Jamie Hwang provides custom dental crowns and bridges to repair damaged teeth and replace missing ones. Located in Ridgefield, NJ , our practice combines skill and precision to deliver long-lasting, natural-looking restorations.

Dental Crowns

Crowns (also known as caps) cover and protect a weakened or broken tooth, restoring its strength, shape, and appearance.

Dental Bridges

Bridges fill the gap left by one or more missing teeth, using adjacent teeth or implants for support.

Why Choose Crowns or Bridges?

Let us help you regain comfort and confidence with beautifully crafted dental restorations.

  • Improve chewing and speaking
  • Prevent teeth from shifting
  • Enhance the appearance of your smile
  • Durable, custom-made materials
