Dr. Miassar Abutabikh

Dr. Abutabikh is dedicated to providing comfortable, comprehensive dental care while helping patients maintain healthy and confident smiles.

Dr. Miassar Abutabikh earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2024 through the Advanced Standing Program for internationally trained dentists.

She provides comprehensive general dentistry with a gentle, patient-centered approach. Dr. Abutabikh has experience treating patients of all ages, including children, and has a strong interest in orthodontics. She is committed to delivering high-quality, personalized care while helping every patient achieve a healthy, confident smile.
