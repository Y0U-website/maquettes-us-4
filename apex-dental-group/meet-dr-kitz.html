Meet Dr. Kitz

  • Cosmetic Dentistry : For patients seeking to enhance the appearance of their smile, Apex Dental Group offers a full suite of cosmetic services. These include teeth whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers. Dr. Kitzmiller takes a personalized approach to cosmetic dentistry, ensuring that each treatment plan is tailored to the individual’s goals and needs.
  • Restorative Dentistry : Apex Dental Group specializes in restorative procedures such as dental implants , crowns , bridges , fillings , and dentures . For patients who need to replace missing teeth, dental implants are a popular and highly effective option. The practice also offers Hybrid All-on-4 Dentures , a type of implant-supported denture that provides a permanent solution for patients with missing teeth.
  • Orthodontics : For patients who need to address issues with tooth alignment, Apex Dental Group offers ClearCorrect™ orthodontics , a more discreet and comfortable alternative to traditional metal braces. ClearCorrect aligners gradually straighten teeth without the need for metal brackets and wires, making them an appealing option for both adults and teens.
  • Advanced Care for Airway Development and Sleep Apnea : Apex Dental Group goes beyond traditional dental care by focusing on airway development in young children. Dr.Kitzmiller is passionate about preventing airway problems that could lead to sleep apnea and other health issues in adulthood. The practice works closely with parents to monitor and support healthy oral and airway development from an early age.

Building a Stronger Community

Dr. Kitzmiller and his team at Apex Dental Group understand the importance of giving back to the community. Dr. Kitzmiller has been actively involved in organizing dental care initiatives for those in need throughout the Raleigh area. Through partnerships with local organizations such as the Lutheran Church Missouri Synod and events at the PNC Arena, Apex Dental Group helps provide much-needed dental care to underserved individuals in the community.

Beyond these initiatives, Dr. Kitzmiller is also dedicated to continuous education and professional growth. He regularly participates in workshops and training sessions to stay up-to-date with the latest dental techniques and advancements. This commitment to lifelong learning ensures that the team at Apex Dental Group remains at the forefront of modern dentistry.

A Patient-First Philosophy

At Apex Dental Group, the focus is always on the patient. The team believes that the key to exceptional care lies in building a strong partnership between the dentist and the patient. Dr.Kitzmiller and his team take the time to listen to each patient’s concerns, educating them about their treatment options and working together to develop a personalized care plan. This collaborative approach ensures that patients feel empowered to take an active role in their dental health.

Transparency is also a cornerstone of the practice’s philosophy. Apex Dental Group makes sure that patients are fully informed about their treatment options, the costs involved, and any potential financial concerns. The goal is to provide clear and honest communication every step of the way, creating a positive and stress-free experience for each patient.
