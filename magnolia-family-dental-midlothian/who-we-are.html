Introducing Your Midlothian Dental Experts

At Magnolia Family Dental, our team is the heart of everything we do. From our experienced dentists to our friendly, knowledgeable team, each member of our team looks forward to making your dental visits a comfortable and positive experience. We know that no one’s smile is the same, and we are proud to provide personalized dental care in a patient-focused and welcoming environment. Our Midlothian dental team is here to serve your family with healthy, strong and confident smiles. We can’t wait to meet you!

Meet Dr. Jessica Smith

Dr. Jessica Smith is the owner at Magnolia Family Dental and a native of Midlothian. With a passion for cosmetic dentistry and individualized care, she helps every patient feel proud of their smile. Her care philosophy involves building long-term relationships with patients and her local, beloved community through trust, compassion and completely personalized care.

Meet Dr. Scott Bittler

Dr. Scott Bittler is an Associate Dentist at Magnolia Family Dental with a special interest in restorations, specifically crowns. Known for his kind approach and commitment to patient comfort, he strives to make every dental visit both a memorable and positive experience. He enjoys creating functional, natural-looking results while helping patients navigate any dental anxiety.

Dental Hygienist

Brenna | Dental Hygienist

Brenna joined Magnolia Family Dental in the spring of 2025 as a Dental Hygienist. She holds an Associate in Applied Science In Dental Hygiene, is certified in the administration of local anesthesia and Nitrous and Basic Life Support and is a member of Phi Theta Kappa Honor Society . Within the dental field, she is particularly interested in oral hygiene assessment and education, gum disease prevention and treatment, periodontal screening, treatment and maintenance, and oral cancer screening.

The privilege of receiving high-quality, regular dental care was an important part of Brenna’s life growing up, and she wants to help others to have a healthy, happy smile. Patients can also always count on her to share a pun, joke or movie quote to make their visit a little more fun. Beyond caring for patients and hearing how she has helped them, her favorite part of being a Dental Hygienist is the great co-workers and coffee!

Brenna and her husband live in Chesterfield and have a son in the military and a daughter pursuing a degree in healthcare. Outside of work, you can find Brenna taking nature photography and listening to music.
