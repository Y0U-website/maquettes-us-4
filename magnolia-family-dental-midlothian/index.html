Dr. Jessica Smith is a General Dentist and the proud owner of Magnolia Family Dental. Born and raised here in Midlothian, Dr. Smith remained in the area for school, attending Virginia Commonwealth University , where she earned her Bachelor of Science in Chemistry, followed by her Doctor of Dental Surgery from VCU School of Dentistry .

Dr. Scott Bittler is an Associate Dentist at Magnolia Family Dental. He received his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Virginia Tech before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery from VCU School of Dentistry (formerly MCV), where he graduated magna cum laude. He has a special interest in restorations and crowns.
