Our restorative care treatments

Zirconia crowns

When a tooth is too damaged for a simple filling, a crown helps restore strength and protect it until the permanent tooth comes in. We offer zirconia crowns that look just like natural teeth with no metal or shine, just a clean, confident smile. These crowns are strong and durable, making them a great option for kids who need both protection and aesthetics.

Pulpotomies

If decay reaches the nerve of a baby tooth, a pulpotomy may be the best way to relieve pain and save the tooth. Sometimes called a “baby root canal,” this treatment helps preserve the tooth’s structure so your child can eat, speak, and grow with comfort. We make the experience as smooth and gentle as possible.

Laser frenectomies

If your child has a tongue- or lip-tie that’s affecting feeding, speech, or comfort, our CO₂ laser allows us to gently release the tissue with precision and minimal discomfort. Healing is faster, and there’s often no need for stitches. Many families come to us through referrals from lactation consultants, therapists, and pediatricians who trust our approach.

Tooth-colored fillings

Composite fillings are a natural-looking option for restoring small to moderate cavities. They blend in with the tooth, are durable, and allow us to treat decay conservatively. Your child will leave with a healthy smile that still looks and feels like their own.

Numbing and preparation

If numbing is required, we apply a topical gel first to keep the process comfortable. Then, we gently remove the decayed part of the tooth while keeping as much healthy structure as possible.

Filling and shaping

We apply a tooth-colored composite material in layers, shaping it to match the natural contour of your child’s tooth. Once it’s in place, we harden the material using a curing light.

Checking everything and celebrating

We make sure the bite feels natural, make any small adjustments needed, and then give your child high-fives for their bravery. We’ll walk you through home care and what to expect afterward.

Have questions? We&#x27;ve got answers.

Why fix cavities in baby teeth if they fall out anyway?

Baby teeth are important for chewing, speaking, and guiding adult teeth into place. If decay is left untreated, it can cause pain, infections, or damage to developing permanent teeth. Keeping baby teeth healthy supports your child’s overall growth and comfort.

Are zirconia crowns better than stainless steel ones?

Zirconia crowns are more natural in appearance and don’t contain metal. They’re especially popular for front teeth, where appearance matters most. Both options are strong and effective, but zirconia offers a more esthetic solution many parents prefer.

What if my child is nervous or won’t sit still?

We specialize in helping kids feel safe and supported during treatment. Our team uses a calm, friendly approach and offers options like nitrous oxide or IV sedation when needed. We’ll guide you in choosing what’s best for your child and help build positive experiences with each visit.
