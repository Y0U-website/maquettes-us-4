About Village Dental

Village Dental is a general and cosmetic dental practice located in Richmond Hill, GA

Dr. Anderson and his staff are dedicated to providing exceptional dental care in a relaxed environment.

Dr. Nils H Anderson earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Marquette University, graduating Magna Cum Laude. He has been in private practice since 1995 providing complete oral care to patients of all ages.

Dr. Anderson and his staff offer various general and cosmetic dental services to include preventative care, radiographs and dental cleanings. Other services provided are tooth extractions, composite filling restorations, at home whitening, dental implants for tooth replacment, veneers, crowns, bridges, partials and dentures

Dr. Anderson also offers clear aligner orthodontic treatment.

Dr. Anderson is Botox and Dermafil certified and offers injectable cosmetic procedures.

Dr. Anderson is a preferred provider for most PPO Dental insurace carriers. Please call with specific policy questions.

Outside of work Dr. Anderson enjoys spending time with his wife, Wendy, and their four children and grandchildren. He welcomes anyone seeking to improve oral health and create a beautiful smile.
