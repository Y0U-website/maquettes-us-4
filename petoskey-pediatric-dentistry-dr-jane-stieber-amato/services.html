Dental Pulpotomy

If your child is showing signs of discomfort due to a deep cavity or toothache, a dental pulpotomy can save the tooth and prevent infection from spreading. At Petoskey Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Stieber offers pulpotomy treatment for children. A pulpotomy removes the infected portion of the tooth pulp and spares the healthy part so your child may still enjoy the function of their tooth and feel comfortable.

If you’ve been looking for a pediatric dentist providing a dental pulpotomy near you, we provide efficient, in-office care that enables most kids to resume their day-to-day activities on the same day. If your child is experiencing sensitivity in a tooth, has a visible cavity, or is dealing with chewing pain, please contact us at Petoskey Pediatric Dentistry.
