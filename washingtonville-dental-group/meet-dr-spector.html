Meet Dr. Etan Spector DDS

Dr. Etan Spector is a dedicated dentist who graduated from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2018, followed by a General Practice Residency at New York Medical College, which he completed in 2019. Dr. Spector’s passion for dentistry stems from his love of working with his hands, transforming dental visits into positive experiences, alleviating pain, and bringing smiles to his patients.

When introducing Dr. Spector to new patients, it’s important to highlight his skill and efficiency in delivering high-quality care. For optimal appointment scheduling, it&#x27;s helpful to ensure that there is adequate time allotted for the necessary procedures.

An interesting anecdote from Dr. Spector’s past: during his dental school interview in Baltimore, he accidentally ripped his pants and, in a pinch, used a stapler from an Office Depot to make a temporary repair. Although the stapler job was less than perfect and left him waddling awkwardly, he successfully completed his interview. Unfortunately, his pants ripped again as soon as he sat in his car afterward!
