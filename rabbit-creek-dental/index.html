Les soins

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. Once the implant integrates with the bone — a process called osseointegration — it acts as a permanent anchor for a crown, bridge, or denture.

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you won’t feel pain during surgery. Afterward, most patients describe mild soreness for 2–4 days, manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the recovery is…
