About Dr. Patrick Shipp

Dr. Patrick Shipp is an associate dentist at Rabbit Creek Dental in Mobile, Alabama. He earned his DMD from the University of Mississippi Medical Center, completed an Advanced General Dentistry Residency there, and founded and ran Shipp Family Dentistry in Mississippi for 12 years before joining Rabbit Creek Dental in July 2025.

How Dr. Patrick Shipp practices

Dr. Shipp enjoys getting to know his patients personally and helping them build dental health that holds up over time. Having owned his own practice for twelve years, he is used to seeing patients through long arcs of care rather than single appointments.

Outside the office

Dr. Shipp has been married to his wife Morgan for over 12 years. They have three children and a beagle named Stella, and since moving to the Mobile Bay area the family has enjoyed exploring everything the bay has to offer.
