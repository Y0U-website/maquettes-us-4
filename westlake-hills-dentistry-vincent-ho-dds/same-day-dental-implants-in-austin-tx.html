Same-Day Dental Implants Options

Missing or damaged teeth do not always have to leave you hiding your smile for months. At Westlake Hills Dentistry, Dr. Vincent Ho offers same-day dental implants for qualified patients, allowing some patients to have a tooth removed, receive a dental implant, and leave with a natural-looking temporary crown in a single visit.

This treatment is designed to preserve your appearance while your implant heals and integrates with your jawbone. Instead of leaving with a visible gap or relying on a removable temporary option, qualified patients may be able to maintain their smile and confidence throughout the healing process.

Every patient is different. Dr. Ho will evaluate your gums, bone support, oral health, and 3D imaging to determine whether immediate implant placement is the right treatment option.

What is a Same-Day Implant?

A same-day implant combines tooth extraction, implant placement, and placement of a temporary crown into one streamlined procedure when conditions are ideal.

The temporary crown is designed to look natural while the implant heals beneath the gum tissue. In some cases, Dr. Ho may be able to use the patient’s existing tooth or crown as part of the temporary restoration. If the tooth is broken or cannot be used, a temporary crown may be fabricated.

After healing is complete, the temporary crown is replaced with a custom permanent crown designed for long-term strength, function, and appearance.

Why Patients Choose This Option

  • Helps avoid a visible gap during healing
  • Provides a natural-looking temporary restoration
  • May reduce the need for a removable temporary appliance
  • Preserves appearance while the implant integrates with the jawbone
  • Final restoration is customized after healing is complete

Who Is a Candidate for Same-Day Dental Implants?

Same-day dental implants are not right for every patient. Patients with healthy gums, adequate bone support, and good overall oral health are often the best candidates.

Dr. Ho will perform a comprehensive examination and use 3D imaging to evaluate the tooth, bone, bite, and surrounding tissues before recommending immediate implant placement.
