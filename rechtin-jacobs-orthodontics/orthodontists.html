Meet the Team at Rechtin Jacobs Orthodontics

At Rechtin Jacobs Orthodontics

our team is more than just a group of professionals—we’re your neighbors, your friends, and your partners in creating healthy, beautiful smiles. Led by Dr. Alli Jacobs and Dr. Paige Rechtin, our team is dedicated to making every patient feel at home. We believe that the best care comes from getting to know our patients and building relationships that last a lifetime. Here, you’re not just another patient—you’re part of our orthodontic family.

Meet

Alli Jacobs, DMD, MS

Professional Philosophy

Dr. Jacobs believes that the key to effective orthodontic care is individualizing treatment by truly connecting with each patient and understanding their specific needs. She is dedicated to obtaining ideal results using the latest technology and meticulous planning. As a mother herself, she understands the chaos of family life and strives to make orthodontic care as seamless and stress-free as possible for her patients and their families.

Why She Loves Orthodontics

For Dr. Jacobs, the most rewarding aspect of her profession is the ability to connect and grow with the families she serves. She cherishes the opportunity to change lives by changing smiles and finds immense joy in forming long-lasting relationships with her patients. Dr. Jacobs is passionate about inspiring confidence in her patients as they see their smiles—and their lives—transform.
