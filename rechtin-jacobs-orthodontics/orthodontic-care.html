Personalized Treatment Options for Every Smile

Explore Our Range of Orthodontic Solutions

Braces

Traditional metal braces are a reliable option for correcting misaligned teeth. At Rechtin Jacobs Orthodontics, we offer braces that are designed with modern technology to provide effective and comfortable treatment for children and adults alike.

Invisalign

Invisalign treatment provides a subtle, convenient solution for straightening your teeth without the need for metal braces. These clear, removable aligners are tailored to fit comfortably and work gradually to guide your teeth into proper alignment—so you can smile with confidence every step of the way.

Clear Aligners

Our clear aligners offer a discreet, flexible alternative to traditional braces. These custom-made aligners gently shift your teeth into the correct position, offering a nearly invisible way to achieve a beautiful smile.

Retainers

After your treatment is complete, retainers help maintain your new smile. We provide custom retainers that are comfortable and easy to wear, ensuring that your teeth stay in place.

Early Intervention (Phase 1)

Early orthodontic intervention can prevent more complex issues later in life. We offer Phase 1 treatments and screenings to guide the growth of your child’s smile, setting the stage for a healthy, beautiful future.

Invisalign First

Invisalign First is a gentle, effective solution for early orthodontic treatment. We use this clear aligner system to guide your child’s smile development, creating space for permanent teeth and addressing concerns like spacing and crowding early—supporting a confident, healthy smile as they grow.

Comprehensive Treatment

We provide comprehensive orthodontic care that includes full bite correction and surgical treatments. Whether you need minor adjustments or extensive correction, we tailor our services to meet your specific needs.

Limited Treatment

For those needing minor shifting or corrections, we offer limited treatment options that focus on achieving precise results with minimal intervention.

Digital Scans

Our intra-oral 3D scanners allow us to create highly accurate digital models of your teeth without the need for traditional impressions, making your treatment process more comfortable and efficient.

Surgical Treatment

For patients requiring more advanced care, we offer surgical orthodontic options to correct complex issues and achieve optimal results.
