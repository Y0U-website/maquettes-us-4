Dr. Shilpa Koneru, DDS

Dr. Koneru proudly continues the 25-year legacy established by Dr. Naran, serving families in Raleigh with integrity, compassion, and advanced dental expertise.

Originally from India, Dr. Koneru earned her Bachelor’s in Dental Surgery and later completed the International Dentist Program in Buffalo, New York. Since 2013, she has been committed to providing high-quality dental care and community outreach. Her philosophy is simple: every smile deserves time, attention, and personalized care.

She continues to pursue ongoing education to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry and looks forward to helping every patient achieve their healthiest, most confident smile.
