Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1. What are dental implants and how do they work? Dental implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone that act as artificial tooth roots. They support a crown, bridge, or denture and function just like natural teeth.
  • Q2. Am I a good candidate for dental implants? Most patients with healthy gums and adequate jawbone are good candidates. During your consultation at Wake Cross Family Dentistry, we evaluate your oral health and determine if additional treatments, such as bone grafting, are needed.
  • Q3. How long do dental implants last? With proper care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them one of the most durable tooth replacement options available.
  • Q4. Are dental implants better than dentures or bridges? Dental implants offer greater stability, comfort, and longevity. Unlike dentures, they won’t slip, and unlike bridges, they don’t require altering healthy neighboring teeth.
  • Q5. Is getting dental implants painful? The procedure is typically well-tolerated and performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report minimal discomfort during recovery, which can be managed with standard aftercare instructions.
