Our Team

Dr. Ann Khazzandra Uy Miranda was born and raised in Davao and Cebu, Philippines. She immigrated to the Bay Area in her teenage years and earned her bachelor’s degree in biology from California State University, East Bay (Hayward) before attending dental school at the University of Michigan.

Dr. Miranda’s philosophy is to provide a warm, inviting, fun, and educational experience for you and your family, always delivering exceptional care with a personal touch. She is passionate about preventive dentistry and believes in fostering trust and comfort by creating a friendly, welcoming environment for her patients.

Her dedication to giving back extends beyond her practice—she has participated in humanitarian missions abroad, volunteering her dental expertise on mission trips to Guatemala, the Fiji Islands, the Philippines, and Kenya.

One of the aspects she loves most about dentistry is the opportunity to educate parents, patients, and expectant mothers on maintaining a lifetime of healthy smiles. She believes that empowering families with knowledge leads to strong oral health habits and beautiful, confident smiles.

When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Miranda enjoys spending time outdoors—whether it’s snowboarding, scuba diving, hiking, or simply relaxing at the beach. She also loves traveling, dancing, volunteering, and cherishing quality time with her family and friends. As a busy mother of three teenage boys, she fully understands the joys and challenges of parenting and loves watching her children grow, especially when they’re engaged in sports.

At home, Dr. Miranda and her husband, Bernard Miranda enjoy family time together. They strive to create a fun, active, and supportive environment for their kids. She also shares her home with her beloved dog, Milo, who brings even more joy to their lives.

As a dentist, Dr. Miranda treats every child as if they were her own. She is dedicated to helping patients achieve the confident smiles they deserve because she believes a healthy, beautiful smile can truly impact a person’s self-esteem and happiness.

She looks forward to meeting you and your family and welcoming you to our practice. Dr. Miranda is excited to see how her patients grow and transform with their new smiles—and she can’t wait to be part of that journey!
