About Us

Meet Dr. Rohit Z. Patel, DDS

Graduating at the top of his class at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in New York, Dr. Patel continued at Columbia to complete his post graduate work in endodontics, the field of dentistry which addresses dental injuries and tooth infections to preserve your natural teeth.

Dr. Patel firmly believes each patient has their own unique dental issues which need to be addressed with personalized treatment. Each person is treated as an individual, not a number in an insurance system. Dr. Patel has assembled a staff of specialists that can help patients with any type of dental issue they are experiencing, providing them with the caring treatment they expect.

We provide the full line-up of general and restorative dental services to keep your teeth healthy and clean. We also specialize in dental procedures that improve your smile.

What Putnam Bright Smile Dentistry Offers You

Putnam Bright Smile Dentistry is here to help you with all your dental needs, whether it’s a routine checkup or restorative procedure, or one our specialty services like dental implants and smile makeovers. We gladly welcome new patients. Call us at (845) 279-7177 to schedule an appointment.

  • Invisalign: The Invisalign method uses clear aligner trays to straighten your teeth. These trays are virtually invisible while you’re wearing them, unlike the traditional metal braces which are very noticeable.
  • Veneers: We us dental veneers to repair a variety of problems, including uneven teeth, chipped or cracked teeth, and gaps between teeth. With veneers, we can quickly improve your smile.
  • Teeth Whitening: Yellowed or stained teeth make your smile less attractive. With our in-office teeth whitening system, you will see immediate results. You will leave our office with a whiter smile.
  • Dental Bonding: Used to repair damaged teeth, dental bonding can restore a tooth that has been broken or chipped.
  • Dental Implants: You can replace one or more missing teeth with dental implants. Implants are attached to your jawbone, making the replacement tooth permanent, just like your natural teeth.
  • All-on-4: For people who wear dentures, All-on-4 provides a way to give you replacement teeth that are permanently attached with dental implants. They don’t slip or shift on you while eating or speaking. The replacement teeth remain in your mouth and you clean them the same way as you clean natural teeth, by brushing and flossing.
