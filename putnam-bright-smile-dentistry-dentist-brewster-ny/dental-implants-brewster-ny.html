What Is a Dental Implant?

Dental implants are biocompatible artificial roots made of titanium. They are placed surgically into the jawbone, where they fuse together with the bone through a process known as osseointegration. Once the implant is integrated into the jawbone, it can be used as a foundation for a new tooth (crown), bridge, or even dentures.

At Putnam Bright Smile Dentistry, we offer customized dental implant solutions to meet your needs. Our team will customize your treatment plan for optimal results, whether you are missing one tooth or several teeth or need a full-mouth restoration.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants have several advantages over dentures and bridges as tooth replacement options.

  • Longevity: With the right care, dental implants can last for a lifetime.
  • Natural Appearance: Implants look and feel like natural teeth.
  • Bone Preserving : Dental implants help prevent bone loss when teeth are lost, maintaining the face’s natural shape.
  • Improved Function : Implants provide a more comfortable and stable solution than traditional dentures.
  • Implants Do Not Affect Adjacent Teeth : Contrary to bridges, implant placement does not alter healthy adjacent teeth.

Different Types of Dental Implants: Customized Solutions for Every Patient

We offer a variety of dental implant types to suit the needs of patients in Brewster. We will determine the best solution for you based on your goals and the extent of the tooth loss.

  • Single Tooth Implants Dental implants are the best solution for patients who have lost a single missing tooth. The implant replaces the crown and root of the tooth to restore full function and appearance without affecting the surrounding teeth. The implant is covered with a custom-designed crown, which provides a long-lasting and durable replacement that functions and looks like a real tooth.
  • Implant-Supported Bridges An implant-supported bridge is the best solution if you have multiple missing teeth in a line. Implant-supported bridges use dental implants to anchor them instead of traditional bridges, which rely on the adjacent teeth as support. This method preserves your existing teeth and provides a more durable and stable solution.
  • Full Arch Implants All-on-4(r), or All-on-6 (r) Full arch implants, also known as All on 4(r) and All on 6(r), offer a revolutionary solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth on one or both arches. Four or six implants strategically placed into the jawbone support a full arched of teeth. This treatment provides a set of fixed teeth that are not removable and look like natural teeth. Full arch implants offer a great alternative to dentures. They are more stable, comfortable, and have a natural look.

Maintenance of Dental Implants - Long-Term Care to Enjoy a Lifetime Smile

Dental implants can last a lifetime. Your implants can last many years with proper care and regular dental appointments. At Putnam Bright Smile Dentistry, we stress the importance of maintaining implants for long-term success.

  • Daily Oral Hygiene For the long-term health of your dental implant, it is important to maintain good oral hygiene. Brushing and flossing at least twice daily can help to prevent plaque from building up around the implant, and maintain healthy gums. Dental implants do not decay, but the gum tissue around them is susceptible to infection and swelling if not cared for properly. You may be advised to use special tools, such as water flossers or interdental brushes, to clean your implants better.
  • Regular Dental Check-Ups Regular dental checkups are essential for the monitoring of your dental implant’s health. Our team will check your implants, as well as the tissues surrounding them, to make sure everything is working correctly. Our team will perform professional cleanings in order to remove any tartar or plaque that has accumulated at the implant site. You can maintain the benefits of dental implants by maintaining regular dental appointments and adhering to a diligent at-home care routine.
