The practice

We pride ourselves on using advanced dental techniques and personalized care to promote optimal oral health for patients of all ages. Whether it’s routine check-ups, cosmetic enhancements, or specialized treatments , Charleston Dental Associates is your go-to destination for all your family dental needs.

Our practice utilizes state-of-the-art technology including panoramic and digital x-rays, along with intraoral cameras and laser cavity detection. Dr. Pennington strive to make every visit to our office a positive one. We want visiting the dentist to be an enjoyable experience for everyone, so our staff strives to provide the best care to both adults and children.

We offer preventive, restorative, and general dental services designed to help patients maintain excellent oral health at every stage of life. We also provide cosmetic services including botox and dermal fillers.

You’re always welcome. Our team provides compassionate, judgment-free care and will work with you to restore your oral health at a pace that feels comfortable.

Your first visit includes getting to know your dental history, evaluating your oral health, discussing any concerns, and creating a personalized treatment plan that fits your goals.
