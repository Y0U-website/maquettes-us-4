Meet Dr. Narang In Winter Springs

Dr. Parminder Narang is a caring and highly skilled dentist, passionate about providing exceptional dental care to patients of all ages. She believes in creating a welcoming atmosphere where families can achieve and maintain healthy, beautiful smiles for life.

Her strong educational background began in India, where she earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery, graduating in the top 10 percent of her class. Driven to expand her expertise, Dr. Narang came to the United States, where she received her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from New York University College of Dentistry. She also holds a Master’s in Dental Public Health from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey (now part of Rutgers University), giving her a broad perspective on dental wellness.

Dr. Narang has extensive experience as a general dentist, adept at a wide array of dental services. Her practice encompasses everything from pediatric to geriatric oral health. Patients trust her for routine care like restorations, cleanings, and preventive services, as well as more complex treatments. These include extractions, cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign, crown and bridge work, teeth whitening, and comprehensive treatment planning. Dr. Narang is also proficient in endodontic treatments, managing impactions and implant restorations.

A firm believer in patient education, Dr. Narang ensures that everyone she treats understands their dental health and treatment options. Her commitment to the field has also included contributions to dental research and community outreach, such as participating in a mobile dental van program in NYC.

Outside the dental office, Dr. Narang is a devoted wife and mother to two active teenage sons who are keen tennis players. She leads a balanced life, enjoying intense workouts to stay fit, expressing her creativity through cooking, and exploring new places through travel.

Dr. Parminder Narang combines her comprehensive clinical skills with a warm, personable approach, making her a trusted partner in her patients’ oral health journeys.
