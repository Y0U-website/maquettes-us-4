The implant retained denture treatment process 1 Consultation and planning Your journey begins with a caring conversation and a detailed 3D scan. We’ll listen to your goals, talk through your concerns, and walk you step by step through the options available. Our goal is to make you feel confident, supported, and ready before moving forward.

Tiny implant posts are gently placed in your jaw to act as anchors for your dentures. We use advanced imaging and a gentle touch to ensure everything is done with comfort in mind. Many patients are pleasantly surprised by how smooth this step feels.

Over the next few months, your implants naturally bond with your bone, creating a secure foundation. This stage takes patience, but we’ll be with you along the way, checking in and offering guidance so your healing feels steady and supported. By the end, you’ll be ready for your new smile.

Once healing is complete, we design custom dentures that look natural and feel comfortable. These are then attached securely to your implants, giving you a smile that stays in place and feels like your own. Patients often share how life-changing it feels to laugh, eat, and smile with confidence again.

Frequently asked questions How are implant retained dentures different from regular dentures? Traditional dentures rest on your gums and can sometimes slip or feel loose. Implant retained dentures are anchored to implants in your jaw, which makes them feel more secure and natural. The difference can mean more comfort, easier eating, and greater peace of mind in daily life.

The entire process usually takes a few months, since the implants need time to heal and bond with the bone. During this time, you won’t be left without teeth—you’ll have temporary options to keep you smiling and comfortable. Once healing is complete, we attach your custom dentures for a lasting solution.

Many people who struggle with loose or uncomfortable dentures find implant retained dentures to be a life-changing option. We’ll look at your oral health, bone structure, and personal goals to see if this treatment is the right fit. Our team will guide you with honesty and care, helping you feel confident in your choice.
