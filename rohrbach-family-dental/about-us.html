About

A practice built on compassion, community, and care

For over 50 years, Rohrbach Family Dental has been caring for families in the Pottstown area. Our mission is simple: to help people feel comfortable, confident, and cared for—every time they walk through our doors. We’re grounded in faith, guided by service, and committed to providing dental care that truly fits your life.

Advanced tools, better results

3D scanner Our 3D scanner helps us capture clear, detailed images of your teeth and jaw in a matter of seconds. It’s a comfortable, fast, and accurate way to support planning for implants and restorations.

CT scanning CT scans give us a precise look at your entire oral and airway structure, which is especially helpful when treating sleep apnea and preparing for complex dental procedures. This advanced imaging helps us deliver safer, more personalized care.

Laser therapy We use gentle laser technology for sleep apnea care and soft tissue procedures. It’s quiet, comfortable, and often speeds up healing.

Digital impressions Say goodbye to goopy molds. We use digital scanning to get a clear, accurate picture of your mouth—no mess required.

Digital paperwork Fill out your forms online at your convenience. It saves time and makes your first visit smoother and easier.

Secure Texting We make communication easy by texting with patients — and you can even send us images securely by text.
