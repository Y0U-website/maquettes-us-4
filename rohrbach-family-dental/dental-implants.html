The dental implant treatment process 1 Preparing your implant We begin by removing the tooth and placing bone and a protective membrane to support healing. Before moving forward, we confirm your dental health is ready — healthy bone and gums are essential for success. We ask that patients have had a dental cleaning within the past year and clearance from their general dentist.

Once the area has healed and is ready, we place the dental implant into the jawbone. This step allows the implant to fuse securely with your bone, creating a strong foundation. Healing after placement usually takes several months to ensure long-term stability.

After healing, we attach a small connector post called an abutment and take impressions. These impressions are used to design a crown that fits your bite perfectly. The crown is made to match your natural teeth in color and shape, blending seamlessly with your smile.

We schedule a follow-up visit to check your bite, comfort, and implant function. Our team ensures everything feels right and works well. We also recommend yearly checkups to monitor implant health and guide you in keeping your smile strong for the long term.

Dr. Briana has received over 300 hours of hands on training from the leading implant dentists in the country. We specialize in implant retained partials, dentures and bridges. We work with patients to get the best outcome. We can also restore implants if you have a surgeon place the implant.

The timeline varies, but the full process—from implant placement to crown delivery—typically takes several months. After the implant is placed, it heals for 6–9 months. Once the post is added, there’s a 3-week healing period before the crown is placed.

Not all patients need a bone graft. If your bone is thin or soft, we may recommend grafting to help support the implant. Your doctor will assess your bone during your visit and discuss the best path forward.

We recommend asking about costs, healing time, and treatment options. It’s helpful to compare fees with your oral surgeon before moving forward. At our office, we prioritize patient education so you can make confident, informed decisions.
