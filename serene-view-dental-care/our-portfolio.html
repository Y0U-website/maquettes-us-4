OUR WORK

This sweet patient came in because the veneer on her front tooth was broken. While she was talking to us about replacing it, she mentioned that she was never happy with her smile, since she felt her veneers did not look natural. She had a bad dental experience at her last office and said her last dentist did not seem to want to work with her to achieve a better esthetic, so she simply gave up on having the perfect smile. We offered to replace all her veneers and used her temporaries to find the perfect shape, contour, and color to give her the smile of her dreams!

Once the patient was ready, we milled full coverage crowns in our office. Our patient was thrilled with the results, and so are we!!
