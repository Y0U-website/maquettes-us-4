Our Approach

Ryon G. Schofield, DDS

Dr. Schofield is a 2002 graduate of the University of Oklahoma Dental School. He completed his undergraduate degree at Brigham Young University receiving a Bachelor’s Degree in Conservation Biology.

He is the father of five amazing children and enjoys spending time with them and his wife. He is an avid outdoorsman and enjoys bike riding, skiing, hiking, camping, fishing, hunting, motorcycle riding, or any other activity you can do in the outdoors of North Idaho.

Dr. Schofield enjoys helping and serving others, which is one of the main reasons he chose to go into dentistry. Since opening his practice in 2003, he has enjoyed working with and helping the wonderful people of North Idaho.

Skill and compassion go hand in hand when providing exceptional patient care. We stay up to date on the latest advancements in dentistry in order to provide the most current treatment options for our patients. We recognize that our patients are unique individuals who have placed a great deal of trust in our team. That is just one reason why we value each and every patient relationship.

We appreciate the time you’ve taken to visit our website and find out more about our dental practice, our team, and the many services we can provide to help you achieve optimum oral health.
