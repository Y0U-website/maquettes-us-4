Dental Services & Technology

The oral health of your smile is our paramount concern. Regular dental visits ensure that minor issues are detected before they become major problems. From regular cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments and cosmetic dentistry, we make sure that your teeth stay healthy, function well and look great!

Family and Preventive Dentistry

Professional Teeth Cleaning

Maintaining good oral health at home is important, but getting your teeth cleaned regularly by your dentist helps detect cavities and early signs of gum disease or oral cancer.

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric Dentistry helps maintain healthy teeth from the start. Services include teeth cleaning, oral exams, bite assessments, and diagnosis of oral health issues.

Dental Fillings

Composite restorations, commonly known as “fillings,” help restore and strengthen a damaged tooth. This is often used for teeth that have succumbed to decay or trauma or have worn down over time.

Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease is an infection of the gum tissue due to plaque buildup. Fortunately, there are treatments to prevent tooth loss and further damage to your gum tissue.

TMJ / TMD and Headache Treatment

We provide treatment to address Temporomandibular Disorders (TMD), which can cause pain in your jaw joint and in the muscles that control jaw movement.

Restorative Dentistry

The most common dental restoration provided by general dentists involves removing tooth decay and placing a filling in the cavity in the affected tooth. We also place crowns and bridges, fit patients for dentures, diagnose and treat gum disease (periodontitis), and treat dental traumas, such as cracked, broken, or knocked-out teeth. Dr. Schofield is also able to provide more advanced treatments, such as root canal therapy and dental implants.

Tooth Extractions

The removal of a tooth that is visible in your mouth due to excessive tooth decay, tooth infection, and crowding.

Root Canals

Inside the core enamel layer in your teeth, the pulp can sometimes become infected, inflamed, and cause severe pain. A root canal procedure is necessary to clean out the decay when this happens.

Bridges

Dental bridges bridge the gap created by one or more missing teeth and are made up of two or more crowns for the teeth on either side of the gap.

Crowns

Crowns are durable shields placed over a damaged tooth to protect it. Made to look like a normal tooth, you can fill in your smile and protect your teeth as much as possible.

Cosmetic Dentistry

While most people would like to have a perfect smile, few of us are born with one. Schofield Dental can help those who are dissatisfied with the appearance of their teeth through cosmetic dentistry. This method of professional oral care focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and mouth for a more esthetically pleasing smile. Patients with stained, worn, chipped, misshapen, misaligned, missing teeth or with a “gummy” smile can all benefit from cosmetic dentistry.

Teeth Whitening

A convenient and fast process of removing stains or discoloration from natural tooth enamel, leaving you with a brighter smile.

Veneers

Dental veneers can fix the appearance of cracked, chipped, stained, or misaligned teeth. Contact us today for a flawless smile. They are thin, porcelain wafers are placed over the surfaces of teeth to address a number of cosmetic issues.

Implants

Dental implant surgery replaces tooth roots with metal, screw-like posts and replaces damaged or missing teeth with artificial teeth that look and function much like real ones.

Emergency Dental Care

We are here to help you! When your dental health is at risk, we will do everything we can to make sure you are treated as soon as possible. While dental emergencies are rare, they can happen, and it’s important to know how to take care of your teeth.
