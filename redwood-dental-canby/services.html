FAQ

Frequently Ask Questions

Dental care often comes with questions, and we believe in providing clear, transparent answers. Below are some of the most commonly asked questions about our services, practices, and policies. If you don’t find the information you’re seeking, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us. We’re here to guide and support you every step of the way.

We accept most major dental insurance plans. However, as coverage details can vary, we recommend calling our office in advance to confirm your specific plan’s acceptance and to get an understanding of what treatments and procedures may be covered.

Preparing for a dental appointment with us is straightforward. We generally advise patients to brush and floss their teeth prior to the visit. If you are coming in for a specific treatment or procedure, there might be additional preparations or instructions. However, this largely depends on the nature of the treatment. It’s always a good idea to ask us when booking your appointment about any specific preparations needed. Lastly, always bring any relevant medical or dental records and a list of medications you are currently taking.

We strive to accommodate our patients’ needs as best as we can. While it largely depends on our schedule for the day, we do our best to provide back-to-back or same-day appointments for families. It’s always a good idea to mention this preference when you call to book so our front desk can assist you effectively.

Our practice stands out due to our holistic approach to dental care. We look at oral health as a part of the overall well-being of our patients. Additionally, we offer a wide array of services in-house, from basic cleanings to specialized treatments, which eliminates the need for external referrals and provides cohesive care. Our patient-centered philosophy, combined with our commitment to utilizing innovative dental technology, ensures that every visit contributes positively to your health journey.

We are committed to providing the best care possible, and that means investing in innovative dental technology. This allows us to offer more accurate diagnoses, efficient treatments, and comfortable experiences for our patients. Our team regularly undergoes training and continues education to stay abreast of the latest advancements in dental care.
