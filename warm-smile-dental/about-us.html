About Us

Our Story

Our mission is to uplift oral health through compassionate care, clear communication, and a welcoming space where everyone feels seen and supported. Because to us, you are not just a client, you are part of our story.

Our vision is to create a dental environment that transcends the ordinary, one where each treatment plan is meticulously crafted to suit the individual needs of our clients who are valued members of our community.

Here for You, in Every Way

We believe great dental care begins long before you sit in the chair. It begins the moment you walk through our doors and feel truly welcomed. At Warm Smile Dental, we take the time to listen, to understand, and to make sure you always feel comfortable and cared for. Your goals, your preferences, your peace of mind—they matter deeply to us. Whether you are here for a routine cleaning or a complete smile makeover, we are here for you in every way. Because we are not just caring for your teeth, we are caring for you.

What Makes Us Different

We believe the little things make a big difference. From the moment you call to schedule an appointment to the moment you leave with a brighter smile, you will notice that things feel warmer here. It is the way we create a space where you can truly feel welcome and genuinely cared for. We are not just in the business of dentistry. We are in the business of building trust, easing worries, and bringing a sense of calm and kindness into every interaction. Our team takes time to meet you where you are with respect and understanding.

Advanced tools help us deliver excellent care, but it is the human connection that makes all the difference. We show up with heart, we care about the details, and we genuinely love what we do. Because for us, it is never just about teeth. It is about people. And we are here for you.

Meet Our Team

Behind every warm smile is a team that genuinely loves what they do. We are more than dental professionals. We are your partners in lifelong oral health. From our front desk team to our dental assistant, hygienist, and doctor, we work together with kindness, skill, and heart. Every member of our team shares one mission: to make your experience easy, reassuring, and even joyful. We get to know you as a person, not just a chart, and celebrate every step of your smile journey together.

Dr. Toyin Abimbola, MDS, DDS

Dr. Abimbola believes that great dental care starts with good communication. She believes dentistry is most effective when patients are informed, empowered, and involved in their treatment decisions. Whether helping patients maintain their oral health through preventive care or enhancing smiles with restorative and cosmetic services like Invisalign® and Botox® therapy for TMJ, Dr. Abimbola is committed to delivering thoughtful, high-quality care tailored to each individual’s needs.

When she is not caring for patients, Dr. Abimbola loves spending time behind her camera, capturing special moments through photography. She is also currently learning Spanish to better connect with more members of our community – something she is really excited about! She is an avid reader who enjoys winding down with a good novel. Most of all, she treasures time with her husband and their two children, whether they are out exploring or just enjoying quiet days at home.
