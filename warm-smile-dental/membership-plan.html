Membership Exclusions & Guidelines

To keep our plans simple and transparent, membership benefits do not apply to:

  • Treatments performed by outside specialists if we refer you out.
  • Dental laboratory fees (when applicable for specific custom fabrications).
  • In-office retail products (like prescription toothpastes or electric toothbrushes).
  • Services already covered by traditional dental insurance or third-party discount plans.
