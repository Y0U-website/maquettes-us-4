Payment Options

We accept Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Discover credit cards as well as cash and personal checks. Our billing coordinator will be happy to discuss any insurance or billing questions you may have.

Insurance Plans

We will submit payment claims to all insurance providers, but our in-network agreements are currently limited to Cigna, Delta Dental Premiere and UnitedHealthcare plans. Patients are expected to pay their estimated portions at the time of their visit.
