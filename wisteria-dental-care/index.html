The practice

After witnessing firsthand how preventable dental problems can impact someone’s health, confidence, and quality of life, Dr. Hema Patel realized that dentists can do far more than simply fix teeth. They can help people lead healthier, happier lives.

That belief became the foundation of Wisteria Dental Care. Today, we empower families to take a more proactive role in their future health. Dr. Patel and her knowledgeable, attentive team offer preventive, minimally invasive dentistry focused on patient education, whole-body wellness and airway health, delivered with compassion and care.

Gain clearer sightlines into your personal risk factors for diseases so you can proactively support your long-term health.

Maintain as much of your natural teeth as possible, reverse and prevent cavities, and reduce pain with our gentle, minimally invasive approach.

Understand and treat the root cause of your family’s sleep and breathing challenges. We are passionate about giving you proactive, modern alternatives so you can make the best decisions for yourself and your family.

Enjoy the latest in laser dentistry, which can relieve your pain, speed up healing, treat sores, reduce snoring and more. All without needing surgery or unnecessary medication.

Transform your smile and feel more confident in your daily life. Using minimally invasive techniques, we’ll help you look and feel your best.

When restorative treatment is necessary, our goal is to preserve as much healthy tooth structure as possible while restoring comfort, function, and overall wellness.
