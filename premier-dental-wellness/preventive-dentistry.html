Our Preventive Services

Dental Cleanings

Professional cleanings every 6 months remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing can t reach, keeping your teeth and gums healthy.

Comprehensive Exams

Thorough examinations to detect early signs of decay, gum disease, oral cancer, and other conditions before they become serious problems.

Digital X-Rays

Advanced digital imaging allows us to see beneath the surface, detecting hidden cavities, bone loss, and other issues with minimal radiation exposure.

Fluoride Treatments

Professional fluoride applications strengthen tooth enamel and help prevent cavities, especially beneficial for children and patients at higher risk of decay.

Dental Sealants

Thin protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to prevent decay in the grooves and pits where bacteria tend to accumulate.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Regular screenings to detect early signs of oral cancer, significantly improving treatment outcomes when caught early.

What to Expect During a Preventive Dental Visit

Regular preventive visits are the cornerstone of lasting oral health. At Premier Dental, we recommend checkups and cleanings every 6 months for most patients, though some individuals with higher risk factors may benefit from more frequent visits. Here is what a typical preventive visit looks like.

Comprehensive Oral Examination

Your dentist performs a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, tongue, and soft tissues. We check for signs of cavities, gum disease, tooth wear, bite problems, and early indicators of oral cancer. Digital X-rays may be taken to reveal issues hidden beneath the surface, such as decay between teeth or bone loss around the roots.

Professional Cleaning (Prophylaxis)

Our dental hygienist carefully removes plaque and hardened tartar (calculus) from all surfaces of your teeth, including areas that are difficult to reach with regular brushing and flossing. After scaling, your teeth are polished to remove surface stains and create a smooth finish that resists future plaque buildup. Flossing is performed to ensure all interproximal areas are clean.

Fluoride Treatment and Sealants (If Recommended)

For patients who would benefit from extra protection, we apply professional fluoride treatments to strengthen enamel and reduce cavity risk. Dental sealants may be recommended for children and teens -- or adults with deep grooves in their molars -- to create a protective barrier against decay on the chewing surfaces of back teeth.

Personalized Care Plan and Education

Before you leave, your dentist reviews any findings and discusses a personalized care plan. If any issues were detected, we outline treatment options and timelines. We also provide tailored oral hygiene guidance, dietary recommendations, and answer any questions you may have. Your next preventive visit is scheduled so you can stay on track with your oral health goals.
