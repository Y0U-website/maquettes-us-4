Trust Your Smile to Dr. Paulina Rodrigues

Dr. Paulina Rodrigues has nearly a decade of experience providing comprehensive general dentistry and is known for delivering high-quality, compassionate care tailored to the unique needs of each patient.

Background & Education

Dr. Rodrigues earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, where she graduated with distinction. She also holds a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Ramapo College of New Jersey, graduating summa cum laude. Throughout her career, she has demonstrated a strong commitment to clinical excellence and lifelong learning, earning recognition from professional organizations including Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the Gorgas Odontological Society, and Gamma Pi Delta.

She has provided dental care in a wide range of clinical settings, including private practices and group dental offices across Maryland, New York, Pennsylvania, and New Hampshire. Dr. Rodrigues is well-versed in comprehensive restorative dentistry and has a particular interest in treating dental emergencies and alleviating pain. She is known for working seamlessly with patients of all ages from children to seniors delivering personalized care in both routine and urgent situations.

In addition to private practice, Dr. Rodrigues served as a Clinical Instructor at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, where she mentored and trained dental students in restorative dentistry and comprehensive care, further reinforcing her dedication to advancing the profession.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Rodrigues enjoys hiking, skiing, yoga, and cooking, and spending time with her family. As a parent herself, she understands the importance of compassionate, patient-centered care for individuals of all ages. Dr. Rodrigues’ experience, clinical skill, and compassionate approach make her a trusted resource for patients and a valued member of the community.
