Understanding Dental Implants

Dental implants are stable and lasting tooth replacements used to replace one missing tooth, several missing teeth, or arches of teeth. A screw made of titanium, the implant post is surgically placed in your jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. It replaces the missing tooth root and ensures that your dental restoration is solid. Unlike traditional dentures, dental implants in Huntington Beach, CA, integrate with your natural bone structure, providing a permanent and reliable foundation.

Even if certain conditions are present, such as bone loss, our team can explore additional procedures like bone grafting to enhance implant candidacy.
