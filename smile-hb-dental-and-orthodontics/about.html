About Our Practice

Everyone deserves a smile they feel great about, and the team at Smile HB Dental & Orthodontics in Huntington Beach, California, offers the chance to achieve that goal. Under the leadership of Subodh Swaroop, DDS, the practice is thriving and offers outstanding service and access to the very latest in general orthodontics and cosmetic dentistry.

Personalized care is a foundational principle embraced by each and every member of the Smile HB Dental & Orthodontics team. Patients feel warmly welcomed and cared for from the time they book an initial visit through treatment and follow-up care.

Preventive care begins with comprehensive dental exams and professional cleanings. Restorative treatment options span the full scope of modern dentistry, from fillings, crowns, bridges, and extractions to root canals and dental implants.

Cosmetic dentistry treatments help you move closer to your aesthetic ideals. Teeth whitening, dental veneers, and dental bonding are just some of the available options. Orthodontic services include traditional braces and Invisalign.

Every smile is unique, which is why each patient at Smile HB Dental & Orthodontics receives customized care. In addition to English, Dr Swaroop also communicates in Spanish and Hindi which helps extend top quality care to more members of the community.

The practice accepts all dental insurance plans, including PPO and HMO options. Uninsured patients have access to third-party financing services.
