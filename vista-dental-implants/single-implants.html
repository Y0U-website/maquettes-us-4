Missing a Single Tooth? Restore It Permanently in Vancouver, WA — Starting at $3,995

A single dental implant replaces one missing tooth, root included — so it looks, feels, and functions just like the tooth it&#x27;s replacing. No bridges, no altering neighboring teeth, just a permanent, natural fix.

Why Choose a Single Implant Over a Bridge or Denture?

A missing tooth doesn&#x27;t just affect your smile. It puts pressure on neighboring teeth, can accelerate bone loss in your jaw, and often shifts your bite over time. A single dental implant solves all of this by replacing the tooth and its root, so your jawbone stays healthy and your neighboring teeth are left untouched — unlike a bridge, which requires grinding down healthy teeth on either side. And unlike a partial denture, an implant never slips, clicks, or needs to be removed at night.

Implants

A zirconia or titanium post is placed in the jawbone and topped with a custom-made crown — a permanent, standalone replacement that looks and acts like a real tooth. Protects adjacent teeth: unlike bridges, they don&#x27;t require grinding down healthy neighboring teeth, and let you chew with normal chewing power.

Bridge

A false tooth is fused between two crowns, anchored to the healthy teeth on either side of the gap.

Snap-in Dentures

Secure, removable teeth that snap onto implant posts — no slipping, no adhesive.

What Does a Single Implant Actually Cost?

A single implant at Vista starts at $3,995 — including the implant post, abutment, and final zirconia crown, all in one transparent price. Here&#x27;s what goes into that number, and why some clinics quote less:

  • Implant Quality — Dr. Saleh uses only FDA-approved, premium-grade titanium implants backed by decades of clinical research. Discount clinics often use lesser-known off-brand implants — a gamble with your long-term health.
  • Who Performs It — Implant placement isn&#x27;t a recognized dental specialty, meaning any general dentist can legally place one. Dr. Saleh has placed 10,000+ implants personally over 25+ years — that experience directly affects how precisely your implant is placed and how long it lasts.
  • Restoration Material — Your crown is milled from premium zirconia, the gold standard for strength, aesthetics, and biocompatibility — not a lower-grade composite or porcelain-fused-metal crown.
  • 3D Imaging & Digital Planning — Every implant is planned using cone beam 3D CT scanning for sub-millimeter precision, reducing risk and improving fit versus freehand placement.
