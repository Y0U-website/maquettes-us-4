6 Factors That Affect Your Cost

These help you evaluate any quote you receive.

Surgical Complexity

Not every jaw is the same. Some patients need bone expansion or condensing. In advanced cases, pterygoid or transnasal implants may be needed. These techniques directly affect cost.

Bone Grafting

If you’ve had missing teeth for a while, bone loss may have occurred. Bone grafts rebuild jawbone for implant placement. Procedures range from bone replacement to sinus augmentation — the type and extent directly affect cost. Your CT scan tells us what’s needed.

Implant Quality

Dr. Saleh uses only FDA-approved, premium-grade implants backed by decades of research. Discount clinics use lesser-known brands — a gamble with your health.

Who Performs It

Implant placement isn’t a recognized ADA specialty — any dentist can legally perform it. Those who deliver consistent results have the deepest training, most experience, and leverage technology the right way. That expertise costs more — but less than fixing a failure.

3D Imaging & Technology

Cone beam CT scanning lets Dr. Saleh plan implant placement with sub-millimeter precision — performing the surgery digitally first, then replicating it precisely in your jaw. Combined with 3D teeth and implants scanning and photogrammetry, every phase from planning to final zirconia is entirely digital.

Restoration Material

Vista uses zirconia — the gold standard for strength, aesthetics, and biocompatibility. Our final restorations use top-grade zirconia, and same-day teeth use premium biocompatible printed resin. Top-tier materials at every stage separates results that last from those that don’t.

Why We Don’t Place Permanent Zirconia the Next Day

More clinics now promise permanent zirconia in 24–48 hours. Here’s why Dr. Saleh disagrees.

We use digitally designed, 3D-printed custom temporaries — not next-day permanent zirconia.

Dr. Saleh believes custom-designed temporaries are the key to a truly beautiful final restoration . With significant tooth loss, it’s nearly impossible to achieve perfect symmetry, precise midline alignment, and optimal lip support in a single attempt.

That’s why he works through 2–3 iterative redesigns — refining position, proportions, and aesthetics each time. The final design becomes a proven prototype replicated exactly in your permanent zirconia arch.

Real-world refinements are always needed. When you look at before-and-after photos from clinics delivering permanent teeth within 24 hours, look closely — you’ll often spot discrepancies in symmetry, midline positioning, and proportions.

You can do it fast, or you can do it right. We choose to do it right.

What Dr. Saleh Designs For

Each temporary is sculpted around these factors:

  • Smile line level — how high your lip rises when you smile naturally
  • Midline precision — the exact position where your two central front teeth meet
  • Horizontal alignment — ensuring your teeth are level with your upper lip line
  • Lip support — the amount of structural support your lips need from the arch
  • Teeth exposure — the ideal amount of tooth visible when you speak and smile
  • Facial contours — how your new teeth complement your overall facial structure
  • Functional spacing — proper clearance for cheeks and tongue for comfort and speech
