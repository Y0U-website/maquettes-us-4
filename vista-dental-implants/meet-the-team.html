Meet Dr. Mo Saleh, DDS

As a pioneer in implant dentistry, Dr. Mo Saleh has over 25 years of clinical practice experience and was among the first dentists to incorporate metal-free ceramic dental implants, also known as zirconia dental implants, into clinical practice in 2007.

Along with his amazing staff and dental care team, you’ll be in caring hands for your Vista dental treatments.
