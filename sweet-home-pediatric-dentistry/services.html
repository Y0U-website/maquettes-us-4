Restorations

Does your child have cavities? We believe in options and in working together with you and your child to build the best treatment plan for your child. Options mean having the latest and most up-to-date services:

  • Tooth colored filling
  • Tooth colored Zirconia crowns
  • Preventive resin Infiltration
  • Silver Diamine Fluoride

Behavior Guidance

Sweet Home Pediatric Dentistry believes that it is possible to love going to the dentist. The doctors and staff will focus on trying to make each visit a positive experience using various behavior guidance techniques:

  • Visual aids and TVs as distractions
  • Tell-show-do technique
  • Positive modeling
  • Desensitization
  • Positive reinforcement

Sedation

If however, your child needs more than just tender loving care, the doctors are especially trained and qualified to deliver various levels of sedation to facilitate safe delivery of treatment.

  • Nitrous oxide/oxygen (Laughing gas)
  • Oral conscious sedation
  • IV sedation
