Our Services

RESTORATIVE DENTISTRY

Your ability to chew significantly impacts your health and your well being. If you feel like your oral health is impacting your overall health, we can help! A bridge replaces missing teeth with artificial teeth, looks great, and literally bridges the gap where one or more teeth may have been. A crown can covered weakened or broken teeth and restore functionality. Finally, when you are missing multiple teeth, a partial or full denture is an option worth exploring.

EMERGENCY CARE & ORAL SURGERY All teeth are different and accidents happen. Whether your teeth are crooked, chipped, damaged or if you’ve had mouth trauma, our team can treat you quickly using the latest techniques and the finest materials. We are here to help you at any time, on any day. When your dental health is at risk, we will do everything we can to make sure you’re treated as soon as possible. If you experience a dental emergency, be sure to call our practice as soon as possible.

PEDIATRIC DENTISTRY We know the dentist can be a stressful situation for little ones. Our dentists provide a safe, comfortable environment for kids of all ages. They take their time with each patient, explaining all of our tools and processes to alleviate any fear! Children should visit the dentist by their first birthday. It is important that your little one’s newly emerged teeth (which appear between six and 12 months) receive proper dental care and benefit from proper oral hygiene habits right from the beginning.
