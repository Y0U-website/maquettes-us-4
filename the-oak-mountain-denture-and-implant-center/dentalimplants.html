FAQs About Dental Implants:

How long do dental implants last?

Long-term studies show that dental implants are an extremely reliable form of treatment. When patients maintain good overall health and oral hygiene, around 97 % of implants are still in function ten years after placement . Outcomes are strongly influenced by patient-controlled factors — stable blood sugar levels, avoiding nicotine, and getting enough Vitamin D all play a role in implant longevity, while also benefiting overall health.
