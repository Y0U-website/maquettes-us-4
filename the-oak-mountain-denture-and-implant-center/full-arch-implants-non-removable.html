What Is a Fixed Implant Arch?

A fixed implant arch is the very best treatment to replace a full set of damaged or missing teeth. With this procedure, Dr. Kwolek will remove any remaining teeth at once, place dental implants around the jaw, and give you a new, non-removable smile, often in the same day.

Your new set of teeth does not come in or out , and you will care for them with gentle brushing and gum hygiene just like natural teeth.

Life After Healing

For a few months, you will be put on a semi-soft diet. We usually say anything you can cut with a plastic knife . However, once you are fully healed, you’ll be able to eat anything you like, from apples to steak, with complete confidence. Patients often say they forget they even have implants because they feel so natural.

FAQs:

How long do full arch implants last?

Fixed full-arch implants have an excellent long-term track record when properly planned and maintained. Research shows that implant systems supporting full-arch restorations demonstrate around a 97 % survival rate at ten years , particularly when patients follow recommended maintenance and health guidelines. Because full-arch implants support an entire set of teeth, factors like smoking status, blood sugar control, and nutritional health are especially important in protecting both the implants and your bone over time.
