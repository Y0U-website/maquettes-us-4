How to Care for Dental Implants

Dental implants are the best way to restore missing teeth but just like the real thing, they require consistent home care to stay healthy and strong.

Whether you have a single implant, an implant bridge, or a full arch, here’s how to keep everything clean and functioning beautifully.

Caring for Implant Teeth

For individual implant crowns, implant bridges, or All-on-4 arches, brush just as you would natural teeth — using a soft toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste. Pay special attention to the gum line around each implant.

For bridges and All-on-4 restorations, use a water flosser daily to clean under the bridged portion of the prosthetic. You can also use floss threaders or proxy brushes to access areas that are difficult to reach with other methods.

Consistent daily cleaning will lower risk of peri-implantitis (bone infection around the implant) and keep the surfaces of your teeth in beautiful shape.

Implant Care FAQ

Do I still need to floss if I have dental implants?

Yes — cleaning between implants is essential. A Waterpik is often the easiest and most effective way, especially under bridges or fixed prosthetics.

Can I use whitening or charcoal toothpaste on implants?

No – these pastes are too abrasive. Use a gentle, non-abrasive toothpaste to avoid scratching your restoration.

How often should implants be professionally cleaned?

We recommend at least twice a year. In-office cleanings reach areas that home care tools can’t – especially for fixed bridges.
