Athletic dentistry focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of sports-related dental injuries. This includes: Preventive education and screening for athletes
  • Fabrication of custom sports mouthguards
  • Emergency care for knocked-out or broken teeth
  • Monitoring of dental wear from bruxism and jaw clenching
  • Restorative solutions for chipped, cracked, or missing teeth

Why Athletes Need Specialized Dental Care

Athletes are more prone to dental trauma than non-athletes. Impact sports like football, basketball, soccer, hockey, and martial arts carry a high risk for:

Our goal is to prevent these issues before they occur and offer fast, expert care if an injury happens.

  • Tooth fractures and chips
  • Avulsed (knocked-out) teeth
  • Jaw misalignment or TMJ pain
  • Mouth injuries (lips, gums, cheeks)
  • Excessive grinding during training or competition

Custom Sports Mouthguards: Your First Line of Defense

One of the most important services we offer athletes is the creation of custom sports guards . Compared to store-bought options, custom guards offer:

Our guards are molded from a digital scan of your mouth and can be customized for specific sports and ages — including youth athletes in our pediatric dentistry program.

  • Superior comfort and fit
  • Easier breathing and communication
  • Higher durability and shock absorption
  • Better compliance, especially for younger athletes

Emergency Dental Care for Sports Injuries

Accidents can happen, even with the best precautions. That’s why we offer same-day emergency dental services to treat:

Prompt treatment can save a tooth — and your season. Call us immediately if an injury occurs during play.

  • Chipped or cracked teeth
  • Tooth dislodgement or trauma
  • Damage to existing crowns or veneers
  • Soft tissue lacerations inside the mouth
