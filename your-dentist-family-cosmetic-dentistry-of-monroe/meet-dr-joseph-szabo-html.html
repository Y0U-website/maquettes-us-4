Meet Dr. Joseph Szabo

Each year Dr. Szabo completes many hours of continuing dental education. He’s committed to providing his patients with leading-edge care, using current technology and techniques that are comfortable and deliver the best results. In addition to attending conferences, seminars, and lectures, Dr. Szabo is a member of several study clubs. He’s an active member of the Horse Creek Study Club, the Spear Study Club, and takes courses at the Spear campus in Arizona, too.

Personal Life

Dr. Szabo grew up and lived in West Seattle until he moved to Ann Arbor, MI, for dental school. He then lived in Perrysburg, OH, before returning to the Pacific Northwest. He now lives in Bothell, WA, with his wife – and best part of his life – Dr. Cindy Du. They have two young sons, Leo, who loves to eat and occasionally scream at the top of his lungs, and Landon who is growing quickly! The family is rounded out by their two Cavalier King Charles Spaniels, Cocoa and Mocha.

When he’s not in the office and seeing patients, Dr. Szabo is working at home: studying, learning, and taking continuing education courses. Once that’s done, it’s family time. He enjoys taking Cindy, Leo, and Landon around town, trying new restaurants, and on various adventures. They love spending time at the park and traveling.

In what free time remains, Dr. Szabo can be found running, cycling, practicing yoga, and losing himself in a good book. He’s also working on improving his Mandarin, his wife’s native tongue.
