Why

Should I Get Invisalign?

Discover A Beautiful, Straight Smile

Invisalign discreetly straightens your teeth, allowing you to enjoy the smile you&#x27;ve always wanted, without any bulky metal wires and brackets. You can confidently smile, socialize, and express yourself throughout your treatment.

Comfort and Convenience

Invisalign aligners are custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth, which ensures a comfortable and irritation-free experience. They are also removable, so you can continue to enjoy your favorite foods and maintain your oral hygiene routine with ease.

Improved Oral Health

Invisalign aligners are designed to gradually shift your teeth into their proper positions, improving your bite alignment and reducing the risk of dental issues down the line, such as tooth decay. It&#x27;s a treatment that not only enhances your smile but also promotes long-term oral health.

Here’s How It

Works

Your journey to a transformed smile begins with a personalized consultation led by Dr. Ullery. Together, we&#x27;ll explore your smile goals and map out a tailored treatment plan to create your dream smile.

We&#x27;ll capture detailed digital impressions of your teeth using our advanced iTero scanner. These impressions will serve as the foundation for crafting a series of custom-made clear aligners designed exclusively for your smile.

You will wear each set of aligners for about two weeks, removing them only to eat, drink, brush, and floss. As you progress through the series, your teeth will gradually shift into alignment. Regular check-ups will allow us to monitor your progress and provide any necessary adjustments.

Once your treatment is complete, we will provide you with a retainer to help maintain your beautiful new smile. It&#x27;s important to wear your retainer as directed to ensure the long-term stability and success of your Invisalign treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I a good candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign is a fantastic option for many people looking to improve their smile. Whether you have mild or moderate alignment issues, our skilled team can assess your needs during a consultation and determine if Invisalign is the perfect fit for you

Does Invisalign hurt?

Most patients find Invisalign to be much more comfortable than traditional braces. While you may experience some initial pressure or slight discomfort as you adjust to wearing the aligners, it&#x27;s only temporary. Soon enough, you&#x27;ll be happily on your way to a straighter, more confident smile.

How do I care for my aligners?

Simply remove your Invisalign aligners before enjoying a delicious meal or a refreshing drink (water is always okay), and remember to give them a gentle brush with a soft toothbrush. Don&#x27;t forget to keep up with your regular oral hygiene routine of brushing and flossing your teeth. With a little love and attention, your aligners will stay clean and keep your smile on the right track!
