Why

Should My Baby Get A Lip Or Tongue Tie?

Improved Feeding

Addressing lip or tongue ties can help your baby latch more easily and feed comfortably, allowing for a more enjoyable experience for both baby and mom.

Better Overall Health

Treating lip or tongue ties early on can prevent potential dental issues, such as tooth decay and misalignment. This ensures your child&#x27;s oral health is set on the right track from the start.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are LightScalpel laser procedures safe?

Absolutely! LightScalpel laser procedures are safe and highly effective. The advanced technology requires no numbing and allows for precise and controlled treatment, minimizing any potential risks or complications.

What are common signs that my child may need a lip or tongue tie?

Common signs include difficulty with breastfeeding, poor weight gain, clicking sounds during feeding, speech delays, and dental issues. If you notice any of these signs, we recommend scheduling a consultation to evaluate your child&#x27;s condition.

What is recovery and aftercare like for lip and tongue ties?

After the procedure, your baby may experience some mild discomfort or swelling, but it typically goes away quickly. We&#x27;ll provide you with specific instructions on how to care for the treatment area. Regular follow-up visits will allow us to monitor your baby&#x27;s progress and ensure proper healing.
