Top-Notch Tools & Treatments

3D Printing Technology

3D printing with cutting-edge SprintRay technology allows us to create high-quality, custom dental solutions with precision and speed. With 3D printing, we are able to offer same-day production of retainers, custom-fit night-guards to protect teeth from grinding and clenching, and whitening trays that provide a perfect mold for effective at-home teeth whitening. We also offer partial dentures, designed for comfort and a natural appearance, and same-day veneers that enhance your smile without the long waiting periods.
