How We Use Advanced Technology at West Chester Dental Arts

Cone Beam CT Scanner

This advanced imaging tool provides 3D views of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. It’s perfect for planning treatments like dental implants and root canals, ensuring optimal precision.

Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays offer clear, detailed images with lower radiation exposure compared to traditional X-rays. These images help us diagnose issues quickly and accurately.

Intraoral Scanner

With our intraoral scanner, we can create 3D models of your teeth without messy impressions. It’s a faster, more comfortable way to plan treatments like crowns and aligners.

Laser Dentistry

Laser technology allows for minimally invasive procedures with less discomfort and faster healing times. We use lasers for treatments without the need for sutures, this can cause minimal bleeding.
