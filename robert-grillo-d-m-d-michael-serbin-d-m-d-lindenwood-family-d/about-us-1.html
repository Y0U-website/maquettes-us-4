About Us

In his free time, Dr.Grillo enjoys gardening, cooking , and time with his family.

Michael Serbin D.M.D. A Long Island native, Dr.Serbin has been a member of the Grillo Family Dentistry team since 2020. Dr Serbin received his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Nova Southeastern University in 2019. He then completed his General Practice Residency training at Jamaica Hospital and Medical Center. He also holds a Bachelors of Science degree in Psychology, graduating Cum Laude from SUNY Stony Brook in 2013.

Experienced in all facets of general dentistry, Dr Serbin strives to make the patient experience an honest, comfortable, and transparent one, while maintaining the highest standard of care. As a matter of professional development and personal curiosity, he continues to expand his expertise in the field. In addition to his full time position at Grillo Family Dentistry, Dr Serbin currently trains at New York University’s Post-Doctoral Dental Implantology program.

Dr Serbin speaks English, Russian, and a modicum of Spanish.

Outside of his dental care Dr Serbin loves to play tennis, cook up various exotic recipes, gallivant with his friends, and pine for his next trip to Japan.
