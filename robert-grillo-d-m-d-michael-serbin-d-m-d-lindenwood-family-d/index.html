Here you will find a compassionate team of professionals that are interested in making your experience in our office as comfortable, efficient and successful as possible.

We are not a large corporate office. We are a work family, with many of us working together 15 years or more! You will notice the difference. We welcome you to join our practice and become part of that family.

We are proud to have a technologically advanced dental office. Digital radiographs improve our diagnostic ability and decrease radiation. Lasers used for soft tissue treatments improve patient comfort and outcomes. 3D imaging and printing with our Cerec system allows for permanent crowns, onlays and veneers to be made in as little as an hour.
