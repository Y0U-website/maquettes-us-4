Your First Visit Questions Answered

We’re here to help

How long will my first appointment take?

Most new patient appointments at Dallas Cosmetic Dental take about 1 hour and 15 minutes. This gives us enough time for a full evaluation, X-rays, and a personal consultation.

Will I get a cleaning at my first visit?

In many cases, yes. If scaling and root planing are needed, we may schedule that for a second visit to allow enough time for your gums to heal.
