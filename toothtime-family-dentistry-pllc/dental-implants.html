How Dental Implants Work

Dental implants are titanium or ceramic posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Over time, the bone integrates with the implant (a process called osseointegration), creating a strong foundation for the restoration.

The dental implant process includes:

In cases where bone loss is present, bone grafting or regenerative medicine may be used to prepare the site.

  • Initial Consultation : We assess bone health, scan your jaw, and create a treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement : A biocompatible post is placed into the bone.
  • Healing Period : Osseointegration occurs over 3–6 months.
  • Abutment & Crown Placement : A custom cosmetic crown is placed on top for a natural look and function.

FAQs About Dental Implants

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of up to 98 % when placed by experienced professionals and properly maintained.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, implants can last 15 years or more , and in many cases, a lifetime.

Is the procedure painful?

Most patients report only mild discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction. Sedation options are available to ensure comfort.

How much do dental implants cost?

Costs vary based on the number of implants, restoration type, and need for additional procedures like bone grafting. A detailed estimate is provided at your consultation.

Do I need a bone graft?

If there is insufficient jawbone volume, bone grafting or regenerative therapy may be recommended to support implant placement.
