Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

What age should my child have an orthodontic evaluation?

Many children benefit from an orthodontic evaluation around age 7. Early evaluations allow our team to monitor growth and identify bite or spacing concerns as the mouth develops.

Are clear aligners or braces better?

The right treatment depends on the complexity of your orthodontic needs, lifestyle preferences, and oral health goals. Some patients are better candidates for braces, while others may qualify for clear aligners.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length varies by patient and type of treatment. Some clear aligner cases may take several months, while more comprehensive orthodontic treatment can take longer.

Will I need to wear a retainer after treatment?

Yes. Retainers help maintain tooth alignment after orthodontic treatment is complete. Our team provides customized retainer recommendations based on your treatment plan.

Do you provide orthodontics for adults?

Yes. We regularly provide orthodontic treatment for adult patients from New Braunfels, Seguin, and surrounding TX communities.
