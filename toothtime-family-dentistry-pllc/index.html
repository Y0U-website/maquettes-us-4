The practice

With convenient offices serving patients throughout New Braunfels, Seguin, Marion, Canyon Lake, McQueeney, Santa Clara, Geronimo, Gruene, and nearby TX communities, we make it easier for families to access orthodontic care close to home.

Whether you are considering braces for your child or exploring clear aligner options as an adult, our team offers comprehensive care in a comfortable, family-focused setting.

Our experienced dental team provides customized orthodontic treatment plans using modern technology and proven techniques. Every orthodontic consultation begins with a thorough exam, digital imaging, and a discussion of your oral health goals.

Patients seeking comprehensive dental care can also explore our general dental services and cosmetic dentistry options to support long-term oral health and smile function.

Orthodontic treatment can benefit patients at many stages of life. At ToothTime Family Dentistry, we provide individualized care plans based on age, oral development, and treatment goals.

Early orthodontic evaluations can help identify developing bite or spacing issues before they progress. Our team may recommend monitoring, preventive guidance, or early treatment when appropriate.

Families from New Braunfels, Seguin, Marion, and Canyon Lake often schedule orthodontic evaluations alongside routine preventive dental visits.

Teen patients may benefit from traditional braces or clear aligners depending on their alignment needs and lifestyle preferences. Our team works closely with families to create treatment plans that support both function and appearance.
