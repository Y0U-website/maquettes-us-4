An Orthodontic Experience Designed for Adults

Portland is an evolving, blooming city with a lively, cosmopolitan feel. You’ll sense that fresh energy at smile. , an orthodontic office offering a boutique clear aligner orthodontic experience on Fore Street, in the heart of downtown Portland, ME.

From your first consultation with trained aligner specialists, through your teeth straightening treatment using the 3M Clarity Aligner system, the board-certified orthodontists and technicians at smile. are dedicated to delivering stellar results, in a convenient, comfortable and relaxing environment. We’ve honored our seaside city through the use of local materials and colors, with ample windows to capture the beautiful natural light. We also created a streamlined atmosphere that reflects a chicness and modernity that appeals to our urban patients .

New office. Same attentive care.

It is important to us that we offer you a balanced experience throughout your teeth aligning treatment . smile. is not your average orthodontist’s office! Make some coffee from the Nespresso machine and continue your work day with strong Wi-Fi and convenient high-top work stations. Enjoy some tea on the rooftop garden and watch the boats cruise along Casco Bay. Or, simply relax and listen to music in our gorgeous plant-filled waiting area.

We love our new convenient location: mere minutes of walking from anywhere in downtown Portland, right across from the AC Marriott Hotel. And because we live in Maine, and the weather can change in a breath, we happily offer free covered parking for the duration of your appointment.

Dr. Bruce J. Podhouser, DMD

Bruce is a board-certified orthodontist and a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. For more than two decades, Bruce has been a prominent member of the Maine and New Hampshire orthodontic community, providing both professional leadership and cutting-edge care. Bruce is one of Maine’s leading clear aligner providers , and has pioneered the procedure since its introduction in 1999.

Dr. Jameson L. Hardy, DMD

Jameson started his orthodontic career in Bangor and Ellsworth, Maine. A second-generation orthodontist, he guided his father’s practice to attain distinction as a premier clear aligner provider in Northern Maine. In 2016, Jameson partnered with Bruce to expand the availability of clear aligner and orthodontic treatment in Southern Maine and New Hampshire.
