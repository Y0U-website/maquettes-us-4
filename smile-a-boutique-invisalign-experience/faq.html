Yes! 3M Clarity Aligner orthodontic treatments with smile. can be funded using a qualifying Health Savings Account (HSA) or Flexible Savings Account (FSA) plan.

Will dental insurance cover my 3M Clarity Aligner treatment? Does smile. accept dental insurance?

We accept most major insurance at smile. (Delta, Cigna, Metlife, etc.). At this time, we do not accept MaineCare. smile. is considered in-network or a participating provider with Delta Dental.
