Pediatric Dental First Visit

Welcome!

Our team will greet your kiddo with warm smiles and a welcoming atmosphere so you both feel at home. From our relaxing waiting area filled with engaging activities to our gentle, patient-centered approach, we&#x27;ve designed every aspect of our practice to put children (and parents!) at ease.

Comprehensive Exam

After checking you in and a quick office tour, our friendly team will take you and your little one back to one of our treatment rooms. Your dentist will perform a thorough examination, including x-rays if needed, to assess your child’s oral health and discuss any concerns.

Treatment Discussion

Depending on your child’s exam, we’ll perform a thorough cleaning, discussing oral habits in a kid-friendly and age appropriate way. If anything needs extra attention, we&#x27;ll explain our findings and work with you to create a personalized treatment plan.

Questions Answered

We encourage you to ask questions! Our team is here to help you understand all aspects of your child’s dental care. If you’re curious about orthodontic treatment, for you or your child, let us know!
