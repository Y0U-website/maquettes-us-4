The Toothpillow program for kids

Helping kids breathe, sleep, and grow the way they were meant to.

What is the Toothpillow program?

A program built around naturally guiding jaw and airway growth to support healthy breathing, sleep, and development.

The Toothpillow Program is a 24-month journey designed for children ages 3–12. It helps them breathe, sleep, and grow well by guiding how the jaws, tongue, and airway develop during their most important years of growth.

Three core elements work together:

Oral appliances

Soft, comfortable devices that help the tongue rest in the right place. This gentle guidance encourages the jaws to widen and grow forward, creating space for teeth and supporting healthy nasal breathing.

Myofunctional therapy

Short, kid-friendly exercises that strengthen the tongue, lips, and face. These movements help build the muscle patterns needed for nasal breathing, healthy swallowing, and balanced facial growth.

Nasal hygiene routines

Simple daily habits that keep the nose clear and the airway open. Regular nasal care supports deep sleep, steady oxygen flow, and the breathing patterns that guide healthy growth.

Guidance at Every Step

Parents receive clear instructions, regular check-ins, and progress tracking through the Toothpillow app.

Kids build independence through daily steps that fit naturally into family life. Most families complete the program 100 % virtually, with the option of local partner offices when in-person support is helpful.

Why early guidance matters

Waiting comes at a cost

By the time most kids are offered braces, nearly 90 % of jaw growth is already finished. That means the chance to influence how their face develops, how much room their airway has, and how well they can breathe at night is slipping away.

Toothpillow steps in during the right years

Rather than focusing on straightening teeth at the end of growth, the program supports airway health and jaw development while those structures are still forming. Acting early creates more space for teeth to come in naturally, while also improving sleep, breathing, and overall development.

The impact shows up in daily life

Mouth breathing, low tongue posture, and narrow jaws can affect sleep quality, attention span, energy, and even behavior. These struggles are often treated as separate issues, but many trace back to the airway. Guiding growth early helps protect against these challenges and reduces the need for extractions or complex treatment later on.

The four pillars of the Toothpillow program

Pillar 1: Guided growth appliances

Appliances that guide, not force

Instead of waiting for braces to straighten teeth at the end of growth, we use soft, comfortable Vivos and Myobrace appliances to direct how the jaws grow during the years when their jaws are growing most rapidly. These guides encourage proper tongue posture, which in turn guides the jaws to widen and grow forward naturally.

Night and day support

The appliances are worn each night while your child sleeps, plus short periods during the day. Nighttime wear passively directs growth, while daytime wear takes advantage of natural swallowing and muscle use to reinforce healthy posture. Over the course of the program, your child may progress through 2–3 different appliances as they grow.

Guiding growth in real time not only reduces crowding and the need for extractions later, it also opens the airway, which helps to support better breathing and sleep.

Pillar 2: Myofunctional therapy

Stronger, better-coordinated muscles mean more stable growth and long-term results that braces alone can’t achieve.

Training the muscles that shape the face and airway

The tongue, lips, and cheeks are powerful growth drivers. If they’re weak, imbalanced, or misplaced, the jaws can grow narrow, teeth crowd, and the airway stays small. Myofunctional therapy retrains these muscles so function and structure work together.

Simple, child-friendly routines

Families receive app-based exercises that take just minutes a day. These short routines teach the tongue to rest against the palate, train proper swallowing, and reinforce nasal breathing. For children who need more support, one-on-one sessions with licensed myofunctional therapists are available to customize plans and track progress.

Pillar 3: Nasal hygiene

Protecting the airway every night.

Clear nasal breathing is essential for healthy sleep. Even with good growth and muscle training, a congested nose can force a child to mouth-breathe, undoing progress. That’s why Toothpillow includes simple, child-safe nasal hygiene routines.

Daily habit, lasting impact.

Families are provided with nasal spray and shown how to make nose-clearing part of the bedtime routine. This small step reduces congestion, helps kids breathe consistently through their nose at night, and improves oxygen delivery while they sleep.

Nighttime nasal breathing supports better sleep quality, protects against snoring, and maximizes the impact of growth guidance and myofunctional therapy.

Pillar 4: Ongoing monitoring and support

The real difference is ongoing support. Toothpillow is a two-year partnership with a care team that tracks your child’s progress, adjusts the plan as they grow, and celebrates meaningful milestones along the way.

You’re never doing this alone.

Growth guidance only works if families feel supported. That’s why Toothpillow pairs every family with a dedicated care coordinator and uses the app to keep progress on track.

Regular reviews, real adjustments.

You’ll submit photos, videos, and quick check-ins that our clinical team reviews regularly. The plan is adjusted as your child grows, ensuring no one falls through the cracks. Dentists check in every six months, coordinators respond to questions quickly, and families always know their child’s growth is being followed.
