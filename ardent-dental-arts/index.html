The practice

Dr. Ravneet Nanda is the doctor and owner of Ardent Dental Arts. She received her dental degree from NYU College of Dentistry in 2009.

Dr. Nanda received numerous accolades and recognitions for her excellence in practicing dentistry and warm chairside manners.

She is highly regarded and respected. She is the mother of 2 boys and resides in Monroe Township, NJ.

Dr. Nanda is proficient in all areas of dentistry, including restorative procedures, extractions, scaling and root planning, crowns, bridges, root canal treatments, uncomplicated periodontal surgeries, cosmetic dentistry, Zoom! teeth whitening, bleaching, and Invisalign

Our practice is dedicated to creating a welcoming, family-friendly environment where your comfort and health come first. From preventive care to advanced treatments, we combine modern dentistry with a personalized approach.

Our office is honored to care for patients across Manalapan Township, Englishtown, Monroe Township, Old Bridge, Millstone, and Freehold Township. We are committed to providing trusted dental care close to home.

We accept most PPO insurance plans as well as payment via cash, card, or check. Our team is happy to help you understand your coverage and ensure you receive the care you need without added stress.
