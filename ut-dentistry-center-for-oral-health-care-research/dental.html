A dental care home you can trust

General Dentistry

Our General Dentistry Clinic offers private practice, evidence-based care from expert faculty providers in our state-of-the-art facilities.

Specialty Care

Our specialty care clinics provide collaborative multidisciplinary care from renowned faculty specialists and residents across all dental specialties under one roof.

Student Dental Clinic

Our Student Dental Clinic offers reduced-cost dental care services from student dentists in exchange for an extra investment of a patient’s time.

Referring Providers

Our expert specialists ensure that every patient and referring dentist receives unparalleled care and attention. Learn how to refer patients to our specialists.

Payments & Insurance

Our providers are in-network with a range of dental insurance carriers and offer service options based on a patient’s care needs, time constraints and price point.

Services

We offer the region’s most comprehensive dental services in one location with faculty, resident and student providers working together to deliver patient-centered care.

We re how. You re why.

Finding the right dentist and specialist is key to establishing a dental care home you can trust. Find a dental provider that best suits your care needs.
