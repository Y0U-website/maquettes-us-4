Orthodontics

Appointments

Request an appointment online or call us at 210-450-3500 .

Our Staff

Care in the orthodontics clinic is provided by our expert board-certified orthodontists and orthodontics residents under the close supervision of our faculty specialists, depending on the patient’s needs and requirements.

Payments and Insurance

We accept most dental insurance plans, but check with your provider to confirm coverage or ask our staff about coverage when you make an appointment. We also accept cash, checks, credit cards and CareCredit.
