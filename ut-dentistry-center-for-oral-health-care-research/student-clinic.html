Student Dental Clinic

UT Dentistry’s Student Dental Clinic offers patients a reduced-cost alternative for dental care they can trust. Under the close supervision of expert School of Dentistry faculty, predoctoral dental students provide affordable dental services for patients 18 years and over in exchange for an extra investment of a patient’s time.

A screening appointment is required to become a patient in the Student Dental Clinic. During your screening, a faculty dentist will evaluate your health history and dental care needs to ensure that a student dentist can meet your treatment requirements.

Appointments

To schedule an appointment in our Student Dental Clinic, please call 210-450-3700 during business hours or complete the online appointment request form .

Prior to dental care with a student dentist, patients must first have a screening appointment with our faculty dentists. Screening appointments require a $50 payment at the time of the appointment booking.

Dental care in the Student Dental Clinic costs about 60 percent less than private practice care, but appointments can take two to three times longer than those with a private practice dentist.

Location

UT Dentistry Center for Oral Health Care & Research 3rd and 4th floors 8210 Floyd Curl Drive San Antonio, Texas 78229

Please park in the garage behind the clinical building, enter through the main lobby and proceed to the 3rd or 4th floor for your appointment.

Payments and Insurance

Screening appointments require a $50 payment at the time of the appointment booking . This payment covers the cost of the screening and associated x-rays.

Fees at the Student Dental Clinic are about 60 percent lower than private practice but vary by procedure.

The Student Dental Clinic does not accept dental insurance, and payment is expected at the time of service. Fees can be paid by cash, check or credit card. We also offer online payments.

Our Staff

Dental care in our Student Dental Clinic is provided by School of Dentistry students who are gaining practical experience performing procedures. Student dentists are closely supervised by our School of Dentistry faculty experts while providing care.
