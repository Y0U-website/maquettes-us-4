Advanced Education in General Dentistry

We offer comprehensive routine, cosmetic and select specialty dental services for patients 17 years and older, provided by dental residents who have received professional dental degrees and are completing advanced training under the supervision of our renowned dental faculty. Our fees are, on average, 60 % less than private practice with slightly longer appointment times.

Our services include fillings, crowns (caps), wisdom tooth removal, root canals, implants, cosmetic options (teeth whitening, veneers), extractions and preventative care (routine cleanings and deep cleanings). We offer IV and oral sedation and are capable of treating patients with complex medical histories, including radiation and chemotherapy and patients with special needs.

Appointments

Patients do not require a referral to be seen in our clinic. We also accept provider referrals.

Patients:

Request an appointment online or call us at 210-450-3270 . No referral is needed.

For the Student Dental Clinic, please call 210-450-3700 .

Referring Providers:

Providers can refer patients through our secure online referral portal .

Location

Center for Oral Health Care & Research, 1st Floor 8210 Floyd Curl Drive San Antonio, Texas 78229

Payments and Insurance

We accept cash, checks, credit cards and CareCredit. Ask if your dental insurance is accepted when you call to make an appointment.

Our Staff

Our practice is staffed by dental residents who have already completed a dental degree and are pursuing advanced training in general dentistry. The dental residents are closely supervised by School of Dentistry faculty.
