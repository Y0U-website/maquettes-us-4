Contact Us

We offer several types of dental practices:

Teaching Practice : Treatment by our School of Dentistry students under the direct supervision of faculty members. To call for a screening appointment, call 210-567-3217 .

Resident Practice : Treatment by dentists who are receiving advanced general dentistry or specialty training. All care is provided under the direct supervision of faculty members. To make an appointment at the resident practice, call 210-450-3270 . Specialty practice numbers are listed below.

Faculty Practice : A practice made up of School of Dentistry faculty members, who include general dentists and specialists. To make an appointment, call 210-450-3245 . To call a specialty practice staffed by faculty members, see the list below.

Our practices may require referrals or special evaluations before service. Contact each practice directly to determine if it will meet your health care needs.

Want to pay online? Patients may now pay online through our secure payment system.
