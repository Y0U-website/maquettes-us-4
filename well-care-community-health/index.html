The practice

Our mission is to care for the members of our community by removing the barriers of access to exceptional medical, dental, and behavioral health care.

Well Care was awarded a perfect score as a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC). This means that we are here to help our community receive quality health care by removing obstacles that underserved groups sometimes experience.
