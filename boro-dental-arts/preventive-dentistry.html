Teeth Cleanings, Exams & X-Rays

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments can be administered as part of a standard teeth cleaning. The doctor will simply apply a fluoride-rich paste or gel to your teeth, leave it in place for a few minutes, then rinse it away. Fluoride has the unique ability to remineralize and strengthen teeth that have been weakened by minor decay. With regular fluoride treatments, your teeth will remain strong and cavity-free.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants consist of a special, liquid resin that is applied to your rear teeth after a cleaning. After it’s been applied, Dr. Lau will use an LED light to harden it into a clear, strong barrier. This barrier prevents cavities, since it stops food, bacteria, and acid from touching your enamel directly. When properly applied and maintained, this protection can last for up to 10 years.
