What Are Single-Tooth Implants?

Single-tooth implants are the most common type of implant. They use one surgically-implanted “post” made of titanium, which is designed to bond permanently with your jaw bone and gum tissue. This post is then “capped” with a dental crown (false tooth). ‍ This two-part design mimics the natural structure of a tooth. The implant functions as a false root, while the crown functions as a false tooth. Because of this, single-tooth implants look very natural, never move, and can last more than 30+ years with proper care, making them the best way to replace a missing tooth in Long Island City.

Aftercare For Dental Implants

Once your single-tooth implant has been completed, all you need to do to keep it healthy is brush twice a day and floss once a day. You can treat it just like a natural tooth! Of course, you should also come to BORO Dental Arts for six-month appointments with Dr. Lau, who will clean your teeth and your implant to ensure you maintain great oral health.
