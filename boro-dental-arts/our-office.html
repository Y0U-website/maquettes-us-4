Artist&#x27;s Series

Our office is proud to feature the works of local Queens-based artists. Their art is displayed all throughout the space and highlights varying styles, themes and media. Be sure to inquire if interested!

Helen Quinn

Helen Quinn is a stylist, designer, and artist living in New York City. She has been an editor and prop stylist for television, magazines, and catalogs. Her experience with color, texture, and interiors has led her to design textiles as well as paint lines. She has taught Color Lab and has led workshops at Parsons/The New School for the Aftertaste series and for Michelle Obama&#x27;s "Reach Higher" initiative at the White House. Silkscreening, drawing with gouache and more recently, ceramics are her media of choice.

Will Kaplan

Writing artist Will Kaplan combines different mediums, techniques, and text to probe boundaries. This New Jersey native grew up exploring highway hemmed nature preserves; tensions between the organic and the human-made manifest in his work. After graduating Skidmore College in 2017, Kaplan has made a new home in Queens, a rich setting in which to explore these themes. He currently serves as a board member at the Manhattan Graphics Center, and works for the art-handling company, Maquette Fine Art Services.

Giving Back to Long Island City

At BORO Dental Arts, it is our goal to go beyond providing top-notch dental care. We want to make sure we are encouraging our community to thrive and flourish. As an avid supporter of the arts in her community, Dr. Lau is proud to be working with the local art district to feature local artists’ work in the office. Next time you’re in the office, be sure to support your local artists by taking a look at their showcased art! Or, to learn more about our involvement, give us a call today.
