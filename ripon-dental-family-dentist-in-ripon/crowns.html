Types of Dental Crowns We Offer in Ripon, CA

At Ripon Dental, we provide a range of crown options, including:

  • Porcelain and ceramic crowns for a natural look that blends with enamel
  • Zirconia dental crowns that are strong, stain-resistant, and ideal for front or back teeth
  • Metal or porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) crowns that provide durability for molars

What to Expect During Your Dental Crown Procedure in Ripon

  • Consultation and Exam – We evaluate your tooth and discuss the best crown type for your needs
  • Tooth Preparation – A small amount of enamel is reshaped for a precise fit
  • Impression and Temporary Crown – A digital or traditional impression is taken, and a temporary crown is placed
  • Permanent Crown Placement – Once your custom crown is ready, we bond it securely to restore both function and appearance
