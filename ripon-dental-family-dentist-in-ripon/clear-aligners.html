Invisalign Treatment Process in Ripon, CA

Our Invisalign treatment begins with a consultation, where we use digital scans to design your custom aligners. You’ll receive a series of trays, each worn for about two weeks, gradually moving your teeth into their ideal positions. Most patients wear their Invisalign aligners 20–22 hours per day, removing them only for eating and cleaning.
