The practice

ToothTown Pediatric Dentistry is Woodstock’s go-to dental clinic for children of all ages. The concept is simple: we provide the highest quality care with a positive and fun environment. Our goal is to help you build a foundation of health for your family. We can’t wait to meet you!

Like most parents, we know just how excruciating it can be to sit in a lobby, seemingly forever, just to check off one more thing off your ever growing to-do list. Our incredibly efficient staff makes every appointment a priority while giving each child the attention they deserve.

So just sit back, relax, let your little munchkins play in our Kids Zone, and know that you’re in good hands.

Dr. Tom has a unique ability to engage children of all ages and backgrounds. To him, the best part about working with kids is seeing their endless potential and helping guide their growth. He is an energetic kid at heart who takes pride in his work and is thrilled to have found a profession in which he looks forward to coming to work every day.

Dr. Tom is a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Georgia Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Georgia Dental Association.
