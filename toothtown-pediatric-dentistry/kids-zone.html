Kids Zone

At Tooth Town Pediatric Dentistry, we offer a variety of amenities for the whole family to enjoy.

Our facility boasts the brand new BEAM Interactive VR game room. BEAM is an interactive projector game system that makes entertainment lively, hygienic, educational, and above all, a ton of fun. BEAM is the first device of its kind that is as beneficial for a child’s development as it is fun. BEAM’s revolutionary technology gives children the opportunity to explore, discover, and interact. BEAM enhances not only brain development, but also gross and fine motor skills.

There are various arcade games and movies on display for the kids to keep them occupied before their appointment time or while they’re waiting for their siblings!

We also offer a beverage station for the parents, along with complimentary Wi-Fi while their children are being treated.
