FAQs

Does Dr. Steven Crump still work in your office?

No, he does not. Dr. Crump retired in 2019 after nearly 40 years of providing exceptional dental care to the City of Longview. He is now enjoying his retirement living closer to his family.

What insurances do you accept?

Although we are only in-network with MetLife, we are still able to file on all other insurances except for Medicaid. Instead of going towards your in-network benefits, we would reflect your out-of-network benefits. If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to call our office!
