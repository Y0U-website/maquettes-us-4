The Answer To Your Dental Fears

Administered orally or via a small mask placed over the mouth and nose, sedation usually lasts between one and four hours depending on your needs. During this time you will be in a deeply relaxed state and feel almost no pain. You will remain awake and be able to communicate with your dentist throughout your procedure. We control exactly how much sedation you receive and our trained sedation dentist is always with you. You are never alone.

When you ‘awake’ from the procedure you will feel completely calm and refreshed. Best of all, sleep dentistry allows us to complete multiple treatments in just one or two appointments. In just a few hours, you can start enjoying the confidence that comes with a bright new smile and exceptional dental health.
