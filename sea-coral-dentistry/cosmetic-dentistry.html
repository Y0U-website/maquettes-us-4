Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile with professional-grade whitening that removes stains and discoloration caused by food, drinks, or aging. In-office or take-home options available for fast, noticeable results.

Dental Bonding

Dental Bonding

An affordable and quick fix for minor flaws, bonding uses tooth-colored resin to repair chips, cracks, or small gaps. It’s a single-visit solution that enhances your smile effortlessly.

Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are ultra-thin shells that cover the front of your teeth to correct chips, gaps, stains, and misalignments. Custom-made to blend seamlessly, they deliver dramatic transformations with minimal prep.

Tooth Contouring & Reshaping

Smile Makeovers

If you have uneven or slightly misshaped teeth, contouring gently reshapes the enamel to create a smoother, more balanced smile—no discomfort, no downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your smile. It includes treatments like teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and smile makeovers to enhance the color, shape, and alignment of your teeth.

While cosmetic treatments are designed to improve aesthetics, many also enhance function. For example, veneers can strengthen worn teeth, and straightening misaligned teeth can improve your bite and oral hygiene.

If you’re unhappy with the look of your teeth due to discoloration, chips, gaps, or misalignment, you may be a great candidate. At Sea Coral Dentistry, we offer personalized consultations to help you explore the best options for your smile goals.
