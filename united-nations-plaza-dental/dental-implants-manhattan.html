The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process is a precise and personalized treatment that ensures long lasting results. Here’s what you can expect during your journey to a new smile

During your initial visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health and use advanced imaging technology to create a customized treatment plan.

The implant, a titanium post, is carefully placed into your jawbone. This acts as the foundation for your new tooth.

This minimally invasive procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation for your comfort.

Over the next 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration, ensuring a stable and durable foundation. During this time, you’ll have a temporary crown to maintain your appearance.

Once the implant has fully integrated, we’ll attach a custom-designed crown, bridge, or denture to complete your new smile. The restoration is crafted to match the color and shape of your natural teeth.
