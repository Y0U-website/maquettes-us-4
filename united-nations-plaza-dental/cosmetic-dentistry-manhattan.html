What others are saying

How long do cosmetic dental results last?

Porcelain veneers typically last 10 to 15 years with proper care. Dental bonding lasts 5 to 7 years. Invisalign results are permanent with consistent retainer use. Professional whitening results vary by lifestyle and typically last 1 to 3 years.

Does cosmetic dentistry hurt?

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive and performed under local anesthesia where needed. Invisalign involves no anesthesia at all. Patients are typically surprised by how comfortable the process is from start to finish.
