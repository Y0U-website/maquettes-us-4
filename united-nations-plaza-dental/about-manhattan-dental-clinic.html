GET TO KNOW US

Over Four Decades of Excellence in Dental Care

For more than 40 years, we’ve proudly served patients from all walks of life, including United Nations staff, diplomats, and local families. Our practice began with a simple mission: to deliver compassionate, high-quality dental care in a welcoming environment. Today, we continue that legacy, combining experience and innovation to meet the evolving needs of our patients.

Our vision is to create healthier, brighter smiles through exceptional dental care tailored to each individual. We strive to provide a welcoming and comfortable environment where our patients feel confident and cared for at every visit.

Who we serve Caring for Diplomats, Professionals & Families from 100+ Countries Trusted by the U.N. and Embassy Communities Located just steps from the United Nations, we’ve been the preferred dental care provider for international staff, diplomats, and dignitaries for over 45 years.

Multilingual, Culturally Sensitive Care From scheduling to treatment, we understand the nuances of working with patients from diverse cultures offering a personalized, respectful, and confidential experience.

MEET THE FOUNDER & CEO Meet Dr. Nolan Krinick leading dental expert Dr. Nolan Krinick is an honors graduate of the New York University School of Dentistry, where he received first-rate training in general and implant dentistry. Since founding his multi-specialty practice in 1979, he has combined state-of-the-art technology, sensible treatment plans, and genuine devotion to meet the unique needs of each patient.

With a dedication to lifelong learning, Dr. Krinick stays ahead of the latest innovations in dentistry to deliver minimally invasive, effective treatments. Over the years, he has improved the smiles of thousands of patients by blending artistic skill with technical expertise.

Dr. Krinick is licensed by the New York State Department of Education and holds memberships in the American Dental Association, the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, and the First District Dental Society. Together with his multilingual staff, fluent in 11 languages he ensures every patient feels welcome and understood.
