Is gum disease reversible?

Gingivitis, the earliest stage, is fully reversible with professional treatment and improved home care. Periodontitis, the more advanced stage, is not reversible but is highly manageable with proper treatment and consistent maintenance visits every 3 to 4 months.

Does gum disease affect my overall health?

Yes. Research has established links between untreated gum disease and an increased risk of heart disease, stroke, diabetes complications, and other systemic conditions. Treating gum disease has shown measurable improvement in several of these conditions.

Bone Grafting Advanced gum disease can lead to bone loss around the teeth. Bone grafting can rebuild this foundation, either to support remaining natural teeth or to prepare the jaw for implants.

Gum Contouring For patients who want cosmetic improvement to the gumline after periodontal treatment, gum contouring refines the shape and symmetry of the gums for a more balanced smile.
