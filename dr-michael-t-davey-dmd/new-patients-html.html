Insurance Accepted

If you have dental insurance, our team will happily accept your plan, and we’ll even handle the entire claims process from beginning to end to make sure you’re maximizing your benefits. We should point out, however, that our treatment decisions will be based on what is best for your oral health and not just on your coverage. If you would like to learn how we can use your particular plan to help you save at your next appointment, contact us today.

Financing Available

Do you currently NOT have dental insurance or require a treatment that isn’t covered by your plan? No problem! We can help you break up the cost of your care into smaller monthly payments thanks to CareCredit. They have a variety of plans that can work for any budget, and you can explore your options simply by clicking on the link below.
