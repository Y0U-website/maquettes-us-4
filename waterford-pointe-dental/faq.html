0 FAQs What’s the difference between a deep cleaning and a regular cleaning? A regular cleaning is a routine cleaning that focuses on your gum line and the exterior surface of your teeth. A deep cleaning is a more extensive dental procedure that involves cleaning the teeth in between the gums below the gum line. The deep cleaning goes beyond the tooth’s surface to remove debris that accumulates in the roots of the teeth.

Do I really need to floss?

Yes, flossing is an essential part of maintaining good oral health and should be done at least once a day. While brushing your teeth is important for removing plaque and food debris from the surface of your teeth, flossing is necessary to clean between the teeth and along the gum line where a toothbrush cannot reach.

Why do I need dental exams?

Regular dental exams are important for maintaining good oral health, preventing dental problems, and detecting any issues early on. It’s recommended that adults and children visit the dentist for a comprehensive exam and professional cleaning at least once every six months.

Do teeth need flouride?

Fluoride has been shown to be effective in preventing tooth decay and strengthening tooth enamel, which is the hard outer layer of teeth that protects against damage.

Many dental professionals recommend that children and adults receive regular fluoride treatments to help prevent cavities and protect the teeth.

How do fillings work?

Fillings are a dental restoration method used to repair teeth that have been damaged by decay or trauma. Before a filling can be placed, the damaged or decayed areas of the tooth must be removed.

Once the decayed portion of the tooth has been removed, the dentist will clean the area and prepare it for the filling material. The filling material, which can be made of composite resin, amalgam, gold, or porcelain, is then placed in the prepared cavity and shaped to fit the tooth. If the filling material is composite resin, it will be bonded to the tooth using a special curing light. After the filling material has been placed, the dentist will shape and polish it to match the natural contours of the tooth.

Fillings are a common and effective way to repair damaged or decayed teeth, and can help to restore the function and appearance of your smile. With proper care and maintenance, fillings can last for many years.

What’s the best way to whiten my teeth?

There are several ways to whiten teeth, and the best method for you will depend on your personal preferences, the extent of staining or discoloration, and any underlying dental conditions you may have. Some common methods for whitening teeth include: Professional teeth whitening, at-home whitening kits, whitening toothpaste and natural remedies.

How long does Botox last?

On average Botox can last 3-6 months depending on the individual’s metabolism, and the amount and location of the injection.

What happens if I stop using Botox?

If you were to stop receiving Botox injections, your facial muscles return to their natural strong state, and your wrinkles and fine lines will return.

What are Dermal fillers?

Dermal fillers are injectable substances that are used to enhance the appearance of features such as the lips, cheeks, and chin and treat wrinkles, fine lines and deep creases.

What’s the difference between Dermal fillers and Botox?

The main difference between dermal fillers and Botox is that dermal fillers add volume to the skin to smooth out wrinkles and fine lines, while Botox paralyzes the muscles that cause wrinkles to reduce their appearance. The choice between these treatments depends on the individual’s specific needs and goals, and should be discussed with a qualified medical professional.
