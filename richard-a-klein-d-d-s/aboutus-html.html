About Us

Dr. Richard A. Klein DDS is a graduate of the prestigious Loma Linda University, School of Dentistry, located in Southern California. He is a talented and accomplished professional; recognized as a gifted artisan, craftsman, innovator, and leader in the specialized fields of restorative, cosmetic, and bio-esthetic dentistry.

Dr. Klein is passionate about the science of oral health, dental function, and smile esthetics. He thrives on restoring the most difficult and complex cases; particularly full mouth reconstruction and extensive cosmetic restorations; yet is delighted to work with patients seeking only minor restorations or smile enhancements.

His communication style and chairside manner are straightforward, honest, direct, and compassionate. He enjoys teaching, and openly shares his wealth of knowledge with medical professionals and patients alike. He believes in an informed consumer, and encourages patients to actively participate in the development of their treatment plan and continued oral health regiment.

Dr. Klein accepts new patients who have decided their oral health and esthetics are important to them, and seek to optimize the relationship between good oral health, good physical health, and attractive esthetics. Dr. Klein serves his patient as their advocate, artisan, and compassionate friend.
