Implant Solutions for Every Level of Tooth Loss

Whether you&#x27;re missing one tooth or all of them, Hallmark Dentistry has an implant solution for you.

Single Tooth Implant

Replaces one missing tooth — root and crown — without altering adjacent healthy teeth.

Ideal for: One missing tooth anywhere in the mouth

  • Preserves adjacent healthy teeth
  • Matches your natural tooth color and shape
  • Prevents bone loss at the extraction site
  • Easy to clean — brush and floss normally

Implant-Supported Bridge

Ideal for: Two or more consecutive missing teeth

  • No need to crown healthy adjacent teeth
  • Fixed — does not come out
  • Maintains jawbone density beneath the bridge
  • Easier to clean than traditional bridges

Implant-Supported Denture

Ideal for: Many missing teeth or full edentulism (denture wearers)

  • Dramatically better chewing ability than conventional dentures
  • Eliminates denture adhesive
  • Snap-on versions can still be removed for cleaning
  • Fixed versions function like natural teeth

All-on-4 Full-Arch Restoration

Ideal for: Full arch replacement, failing teeth, or heavy denture wearers

  • Same-day temporary teeth in most cases
  • Only four implants per arch — efficient and cost-effective
  • Works with reduced bone volume in many cases
  • Permanent fixed teeth — no removal required

Your Dental Implant Journey at Hallmark Dentistry

From first call to permanent crown — here&#x27;s what to expect at Hallmark Dentistry in Cullman.

Consultation & Imaging

Dr. Ward takes a 3D cone beam CT scan to evaluate bone volume and angles, then presents a complete treatment plan with upfront pricing.

Bone Grafting (If Needed)

If bone density is insufficient, a graft is placed and allowed to integrate over 3–6 months — your CT scan determines whether this step is required.

Implant Placement

Under local anesthesia, Dr. Ward places the titanium post; a healing cap or temporary restoration is placed the same day.

Osseointegration

Over 3–6 months the bone fuses with the post — Dr. Ward confirms integration before proceeding to the crown.

Crown Placement

Dr. Hallmark designs and places your permanent crown, custom-shaded to match your natural teeth exactly.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth qualify — bone grafting addresses bone loss in many cases. A CT scan consultation with Dr. Ward in Cullman is the only reliable way to confirm.

Ideal Implant Candidates

  • Healthy adults with one or more missing teeth
  • Patients with adequate jawbone density (or who can grow it with grafting)
  • Non-smokers or patients willing to quit during healing
  • Patients with controlled diabetes or other systemic conditions
  • Denture wearers seeking a permanent, stable alternative
  • Patients with failing or severely damaged teeth

Factors That Require Additional Evaluation

Dr. Ward reviews your CT scan and gives you an honest assessment — no obligation.

  • — Significant bone loss — may require bone grafting before implant placement
  • — Active gum disease — must be treated and resolved first
  • — Uncontrolled diabetes — implant healing requires controlled blood sugar
  • — Certain medications (bisphosphonates) — require specialist evaluation
  • — Heavy smoking — substantially increases implant failure risk
  • — Growing adolescents — jawbone must be fully developed first
