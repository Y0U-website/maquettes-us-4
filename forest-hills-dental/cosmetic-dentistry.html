How Dr. Gregory Mark Designs Your Smile

Every cosmetic case starts with understanding the whole picture. Dr. Mark’s process is calm, collaborative, and designed to help you feel confident in every step. His goal is to create a smile that feels natural, fits your face, and supports your long-term comfort and health.

Your visit begins with a very detailed evaluation that goes far beyond a surface-level exam. You may find that you’ve never been asked some of the questions by any doctor before, let alone dentist.

Dr. Mark reviews full-mouth photos, digital scans, and an occlusal analysis to understand how your teeth fit and function together . When appropriate, he may use CBCT imaging to study bone structure, airway space, and the deeper elements of your oral health.

These details shape how your teeth wear over time and what your finished smile will need in order to stay comfortable and stable.

Drawing on his Kois and bio-functional training, Dr. Mark blends advanced science with an intuitive understanding of aesthetics . The result is a treatment plan that supports both beauty and long-term function, tailored to the unique way your smile works.

Once Dr. Mark understands your goals and the way your teeth function, he uses digital tools to preview potential changes. This may include scanned models, digital smile design, or physical mock-ups that let you see how updated shapes, lengths, or proportions could look before making any commitments.

This phase is collaborative. You’re able to try in shapes, compare shades, and give feedback on what feels most “you.” And it’s never about adding unnecessary work.
