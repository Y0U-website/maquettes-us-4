At Smile Now STL, we pride ourselves on providing expert dental care tailored to the needs of our patients. Our team, led by Dr. Thomas E. O’Connor, is dedicated to ensuring that every visit is comfortable and informative, addressing all your dental concerns with precision and care.

With a focus on advanced technology and patient-centered practices, we offer a wide range of services from routine check-ups to complex implant procedures. Our commitment to excellence in dental care ensures that you receive the best treatment available, making us a trusted choice for residents of St. Louis and surrounding areas.

Smile Now STL utilizes the latest advancements in dental technology to enhance the patient experience and treatment outcomes. From digital imaging to laser dentistry, our state-of-the-art tools allow for more accurate diagnoses and minimally invasive procedures.

For instance, our digitally guided implant surgeries not only improve precision but also significantly reduce recovery times for our patients. By integrating cutting-edge technology into our practice, we ensure that you receive the highest standard of care in a comfortable environment.
