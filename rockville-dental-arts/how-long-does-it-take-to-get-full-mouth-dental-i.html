Frequently Asked Questions

Can I really get full mouth dental implants in one day?

Yes. With All-on-4, you receive a fixed temporary set of teeth the same day your implants are placed.

How long does healing take after full mouth implants?

Most patients heal in 3–6 months before the final zirconia prosthesis is delivered.

Yes, but you’ll follow a soft diet during the healing phase to protect your implants.
