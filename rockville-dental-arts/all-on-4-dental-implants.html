What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants are a full-arch restoration technique that uses four dental implants to support a fixed bridge of teeth.

The implants are angled strategically to maximize existing bone and reduce the need for bone grafting in many cases.

This approach allows many patients in Rockville, MD to leave with functional teeth the same day.

Teeth in a Day: How the Procedure Works

One of the most powerful benefits of All-on-4 dental implants is that you can often receive teeth the same day.

Here’s how the process works at Rockville Dental Arts:

Step 1: Consultation & 3D Imaging

We use advanced CBCT technology to evaluate bone density and create a precise surgical plan.

Step 2: Digital Smile Planning

Your new smile is digitally designed for aesthetics and function.

Step 3: Implant Placement

Four implants are placed strategically in the jaw.

Step 4: Same-Day Temporary Bridge

A fixed provisional bridge is attached the same day in most cases.

Step 5: Healing Phase

Over several months, implants integrate with your bone.

Step 6: Final Zirconia or Hybrid Bridge

Your permanent restoration is placed after healing.
