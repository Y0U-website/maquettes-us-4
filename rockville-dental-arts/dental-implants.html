Dental Implant Options We Offer in Rockville

Single Tooth Dental Implants

Replace one missing tooth with an implant and a natural-looking crown . This option restores function without affecting neighboring teeth.

Multiple Tooth Replacement (Implant Bridges)

When multiple teeth are missing, implants can support a bridge for stable function and a clean, natural appearance.

Snap-In Dentures (Implant-Retained Dentures)

If you want more stability than traditional dentures, snap-in dentures attach to implants for a secure fit while remaining removable for cleaning.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

All-on-4 Dental Implants (Teeth in a Day Options)

All-on-4 is a full-arch solution designed to replace an entire arch of teeth using strategically placed implants—often allowing patients to leave with fixed teeth the same day.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental visits, implants can last decades.

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia is used and post-procedure soreness is typically manageable.

How long does the implant process take?

Timeline depends on the case, but many patients complete treatment in a few months. Some full-arch cases may allow same-day temporary teeth.

Are implants better than dentures?

For many patients, yes. Implants can offer improved stability, comfort, and bone preservation.
