What to Expect

Consultation

At Hoyos & Pinto Dentistry, we are dedicated to providing exceptional dental care for you and your loved ones. Contact us today to schedule an appointment.

Implant Placement

Once you&#x27;re cleared and ready, the titanium implant post is placed in your jawbone during a simple in-office procedure. Local anesthesia is used — most patients are surprised by how manageable the experience is.

Healing (Osseo-integration)

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your jawbone. This is what makes implants so stable and durable. During this time, a temporary restoration keeps things looking and functioning normally.

Final Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, your custom crown is attached. It&#x27;s matched to the shape and shade of your surrounding teeth — and that&#x27;s it. You&#x27;re done.
