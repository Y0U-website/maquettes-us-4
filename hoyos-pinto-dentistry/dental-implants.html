Implant-Supported Bridge

When several adjacent teeth are missing, an implant-supported bridge can restore the entire area without replacing every tooth with its own implant. Instead, two strategically placed implants anchor a bridge that spans the gap, replacing two to four teeth at once.

This option is significantly more stable than a traditional tooth-supported bridge, because the implants go directly into the bone — there&#x27;s no reliance on neighboring teeth for support. The bridge is fixed in place and does not come in and out.

An implant-supported bridge may be right for you if:

  • You are missing two or more adjacent teeth
  • You want a fixed solution that doesn&#x27;t require an implant for every missing tooth
  • Your surrounding teeth are healthy
  • You have sufficient bone in the area to support implants

Full Arch Implants — Replacing All Teeth

When most or all of the teeth in an arch are missing or failing, full-arch implant solutions offer a permanent, fixed replacement for every tooth — all supported by just four to six implants. At Hoyos & Pinto Dentistry, we offer two distinct full-arch options: Teeth-on-6 and All-on-X. The right choice depends on your bone volume, your anatomy, and the final result you&#x27;re looking for.

Teeth-on-6 — Full Arch Zirconia Bridge on 6 implants

Teeth-on-6 (TO6) is our premium full-arch implant option for patients with good bone volume who want the most natural-looking result possible. At least six implants support an FP1 style full zirconia bridge — a solid, tooth-colored restoration that mimics the exact appearance of natural teeth with no pink gum component. There&#x27;s no artificial gum tissue involved; instead, the bridge sits right at the gumline, just like your natural teeth would.

Because Teeth-on-6 requires less bone reduction during placement and uses zirconia — the strongest dental ceramic available — it offers exceptional durability and an incredibly lifelike aesthetic. For patients who are good candidates, Teeth-on-6 represents the closest thing to having your natural teeth back. ‍

  • You are have many missing or failing teeth in an arch but have good bone volume
  • You want the most natural-looking result with no visible pink gum
  • You value a strong, durable, all-zirconia restoration

All-on-X — Full Arch Implants with Natural Looking Gum

All-on-X — Full Arch Implants with Natural Looking Gum

‍All-on-X (AOX) is our full-arch implant option for patients who have experienced some bone loss or gum recession — which is very common in people who have been missing teeth for a period of time. Using at least four strategically placed implants, AOX supports a FP3 style fixed prosthesis that replaces your entire arch of teeth, including a pink prosthetic section that recreates the appearance of natural gum tissue.

The pink gum component isn&#x27;t just cosmetic — it&#x27;s functional. It fills in the space left by missing bone and tissue, providing lip and facial support that contributes to a natural, youthful appearance. The prosthesis is fixed and does not come in and out like a denture. ‍

  • You are missing most or all teeth in an arch
  • You have experienced some bone or gum tissue loss
  • You want a fixed, stable solution that functions like natural teeth
  • You want improved facial support and a full smile

This thorough evaluation allows us to recommend the solution that will serve you best — not just today, but for years to come. Whether you need a single implant crown or a full zirconia arch, you can trust that our recommendation is based on what&#x27;s truly right for you.

  • The health and volume of your existing bone
