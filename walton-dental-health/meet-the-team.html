Dr. Shawn R. Walton

Dr. Shawn R. Walton is from Lime Springs, Iowa. Shawn graduated from Crestwood High School in Cresco, Iowa in 1984 before attending college at the University of Iowa. He graduated from college in 1988 with a degree in Microbiology and a Chemistry minor. Walton was accepted into the University of Iowa College of Dentistry and graduated in 1992 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) Degree. Upon graduation from dental school, Dr. Walton was accepted to the University of Iowa and the VA Hospital for a one year General Practice Residency in Hospital Dentistry.

In 1993 Walton moved to Sioux City where he has practiced dentistry ever since. Dr. Walton and his wife, Tricia, are raising five children: Alex, Blake, Hannah, Emily and Elizabeth. Shawn spends his free time with his family and riding his motorcycle. Dr. Walton is proud to have worked in Siouxland for over twenty years and looks forward to continuing his practice and serving both new and long-time patients.
