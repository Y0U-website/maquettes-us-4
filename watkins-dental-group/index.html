Whether you choose individual dental implants or you replace an arch using overdentures, dental implants are the ideal way to restore your smile. Dental implants are the safe, attractive and low-maintenance way to get the smile you’ve been dreaming about.

Dental implants are surgically-placed individual prosthetic replacements for missing teeth. Unlike dentures, dental implants are not removable. Rather, dental implants are permanent replacements that look and feel just like your natural teeth.

Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, dental implants are surgically inserted into the jawbone. Known as the fixture, the implant itself is made of titanium, a highly durable metal that is also biocompatible. Ultimately, this allows the dental implant to form a remarkably secure bond with your jawbone thanks to a process known as osseointegration.

Ultimately, this naturally-occurring process allows the implant to become a part of the jaw bone and creates a strong foundation for the rest of the dental implant.
