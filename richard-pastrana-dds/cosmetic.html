Cosmetic Dentistry Questions Answered

Answers to what Capital District patients ask us most about whitening, veneers, and improving the look of their smile.

  • What does cosmetic dentistry include? Cosmetic dentistry covers any treatment that improves the look of your teeth and smile. That includes teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and reshaping, along with replacing old or discolored fillings. We’ll talk through your goals and recommend the options that fit your smile and budget.
  • How long does teeth whitening last? Professional whitening results typically last several months to a few years, depending on your habits. Coffee, tea, red wine, and smoking can fade the results faster. We can recommend ways to keep your smile bright longer, including occasional touch-ups.
  • Are veneers permanent? Veneers are a long-term option. Because a small amount of enamel is usually prepared to place them, the process isn’t reversible, and veneers will eventually need to be replaced after many years of wear. They’re a long lasting way to improve the shape, color, and alignment of your smile. We’ll go over what to expect before moving forward.
  • Will cosmetic treatments look natural? Yes. The goal of cosmetic dentistry is a smile that looks like a better version of your own, not an obvious one. We match color, shape, and proportion to your face and your other teeth, so the results blend in and look natural.
  • Is professional whitening better than store-bought kits? Professional whitening is stronger and more even than most over-the-counter kits, and it’s done under our supervision to protect your gums and enamel. Store-bought options can help with mild staining, but for noticeable, longer-lasting results, in-office or professional take-home whitening is the better choice.
