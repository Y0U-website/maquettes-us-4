The Dental Implant Process

At Glauser Family Dentistry, our skilled team, led by Dr. Grant Glauser and Dr. Kathleen McNeil , uses advanced technology to ensure a smooth and comfortable dental implant process.

  • Consultation and Examination : During your initial visit, we will assess your oral health and determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants.
  • Implant Placement : The titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone, providing a stable foundation for the replacement tooth.
  • Crown Placement : Once the implant has fully integrated, a custom-made crown is attached, completing the restoration.
