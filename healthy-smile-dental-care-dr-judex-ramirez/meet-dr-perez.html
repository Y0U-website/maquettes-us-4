Dr. Carlos Perez

Dr. Perez graduated from UF College of Dentistry. He is very passionate and loves dentistry. His interest in different dental procedures such as surgery, prosthodontics, and endodontics has led him to expand his knowledge by taking continuing education courses. He has completed courses on dental implants, bone grafts and PRF, and plasma membranes. He’s currently a fellow implantologist and focuses his practice on giving patients the chance to be functional again by substituting their missing teeth with state-of-the-art restorations through implant dentistry.
