DR. CAROLINE SHEPHERD

My goal as a dentist is to work with patients to develop a complete treatment plan, addressing all of their needs and concerns. I want my patients to feel comfortable with decisions involving their dental care and know that I take pride in all aspects of my dental work.

I was born and raised in Waukegan, IL and attended Carmel Catholic High School in Mundelein, IL. I went on to earn my Bachelor of Science Degree from the University of Iowa 2002 and my Doctor of Dental Surgery Degree from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry in 2006.

I am a member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society and Chicago Dental Society and regularly attend their continuing education courses in all areas of dentistry.

I have two daughters, Charlotte and Whitney, and two German shorthaired pointers, Greta and Henrietta. Outside of work and family, I have been a part of the Men’s and Women’s Volleyball coaching staff at Carmel Catholic High School. I currently coach youth volleyball at St. Patrick School in Wadsworth.
