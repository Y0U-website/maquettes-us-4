Meet Dr. Robert Remmick

Dr. Remmick is a long-time member of the Devils Lake community. He graduated from Devils Lake High School and began his college education at Lake Region State College before completing his undergraduate degree at the University of North Dakota. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Minnesota.

In 2011, Dr. Remmick returned to Devils Lake with his family to serve the community he calls home. When he’s not at the dental clinic, you can often find him enjoying the outdoors hunting or fishing, or staying involved in the community by coaching and cheering on his kids in their athletic activities.
