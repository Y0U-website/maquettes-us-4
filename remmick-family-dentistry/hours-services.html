Dental Services in Devils Lake, ND

Our two service areas preventive and restorative dentistry cover the vast majority of what most families ever need. Below is exactly what each visit looks like, so you know what to expect before you arrive.

Preventive & General Dentistry

Regular preventive care is the single best investment you can make in your oral health. Most cavities, gum disease, and tooth loss are preventable with cleanings every six months.

Routine cleanings : plaque and tartar removal, polish, fluoride treatment, oral cancer screening (every 6 months).

Comprehensive exams : full evaluation of teeth, gums, bite, jaw joint, and soft tissues.

Digital X-rays: low-radiation digital images to catch decay and bone loss early.

Fluoride treatments: topical fluoride to strengthen enamel and prevent cavities.

Dental sealants: thin protective coating bonded to molar chewing surfaces (ages 6 14).

Periodontal care: deep cleanings (scaling and root planing) for early gum disease.

Custom mouthguards: athletic guards and night guards for grinding (bruxism).

Restorative Dentistry

When teeth are damaged, decayed, or missing, restorative dentistry brings them back to full function. We use modern materials such as tooth-colored composite, porcelain, and zirconia so repairs blend in naturally.

Tooth-colored fillings: composite resin fillings that match your natural tooth shade.

Dental crowns: custom porcelain or zirconia caps that fully cover a damaged tooth.

Dental bridges: fixed prosthetic to replace one or more missing teeth.

Full & partial dentures: removable replacements for several or all teeth, fitted for comfort.

Root canal therapy: removes infected pulp and seals the tooth, saving the natural tooth.

Tooth extractions: simple and surgical extractions, including wisdom tooth removal.

Same-day repairs: many fillings and crown prep visits completed in one appointment.

Absolutely. We treat children from their first tooth onward, most kids start with us between ages 1 and 3. We focus on positive, low-stress visits to build a lifetime of good dental habits.

What should I do for a dental emergency?

Call our office at ( 701) 662-8980 as soon as possible we save time in the daily schedule for emergencies. For knocked-out adult teeth, gently rinse the tooth (don’t scrub it), place it back in the socket if you can, or store it in milk and come in immediately.

How often should I come in for a cleaning?

Most adults and children should have a cleaning and exam every six months. Patients with a history of gum disease, heavy tartar buildup, or certain medical conditions may benefit from visits every three to four months.
