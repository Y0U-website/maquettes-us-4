Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments are extremely different than when you were a kid! Now fluoride varnish is applied easily with a small brush. Fluoride is very beneficial for preventing cavities. It is a naturally occurring mineral that can make teeth more resistant to tooth decay and can harden, or remineralize, areas where cavities are starting. Fluoride treatments have a higher concentration of fluoride than regular toothpaste and must be applied by a professional. For optimal cavity prevention, application twice per year is typically recommended.

Why Children Like Fluoride Varnish

  • Children get to select their favorite flavor
  • Fewer cavities!
  • Applied with a small brush and hardens upon contact with saliva
  • Children can eat and drink immediately after application

What are Dental Sealants?

A dental sealant is a preventative protective coating that is placed on children who have deep pits and grooves on their six- and/or twelve-year molars.

Benefits of Sealants:

The application of dental sealants is a very simple and easy process. Your pediatric dentist will clean off any debris on the chewing surface of the tooth, apply a special tooth gel (we let children know it’s like shampoo!) and apply the clear dental sealant. After we shine the LED light, the material is hardened and all deep grooves are now smooth.
