Dr. Monica B. Swope, DDS

Monica Swope graduated with a health science degree from Western Michigan University. She earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. She completed the course at Hornbrook Center for Cosmetic Dentistry and Nash Institute of Dental Learning.

Dr. Swope has spent a lot of time throughout the community with career day speeches and numerous health fair events associated with several churches in the area. She volunteers regularly with Leave No Vet Behind Program. Dr. Swope enjoys donating her time with the annual University of Cincinnati Community Dental Day in Blue Ash serving the under-privileged.

Dr. Swope continues her commitment to providing the most up to date care by attending courses throughout the year. Despite her many civic and professional obligations, Dr. Swope has enjoyed extensive travel with close friends and family. She has also completed many cycling events, mini-triathlons, golf scrambles and challenging hikes.

Dr. Swope is married to Rodney and they have 3 children: Kristin, Taylor and Caleb who she loves spending time with. Dr. Swope&#x27;s mission is to provide dental care to the Forest Park community and neighboring areas. She is committed to serving her patients through prayer and letting His light shine so that others may follow Him.

Dr. Rebecca B. Yoxthimer, DDS

Dr. Bekah Yoxthimer is a soccer mom, triathlete, general dentist, motivational speaker and a woman of faith. She is president of the Loveland Speakeasy Toastmasters club, a public speaking and leadership organization. In 2021 she became the International Speech Champion for District 40.

Dr. Yoxthimer&#x27;s passion for community service led her to start a volunteer group named Strong Women Helping Others. Her organization of over 100 volunteers sponsors charity events, such as The Studious Studs Fashion Show raising money for a homeless shelter for women and children. She also created Kicking Out Poverty, a kickball tournament that benefits providing meals for the homeless living underneath bridges in Cincinnati.

Her desire to give back led her to be a part of the "No Vet Left Behind Program" where she provides free dental work to veterans. This summer she and her fourteen-year-old went on a dental missions trip to Nigeria, providing extractions and cleanings to children at three different orphanages. Dr. Yoxthimer believes that being a dentist is great, but the most fulfilling part is being able to help those in need with dentistry.
