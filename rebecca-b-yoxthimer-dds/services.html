Technologies We Offer

These new technologies allow us to treat dental cases with extreme precision, detect dental concerns at early stages, and provide stunning and customized restorations. When you visit our office you can be confident that you will receive the finest and most advanced dental care.

Intraoral Camera

Allows us to show you exactly what we see inside your mouth — making diagnosis and treatment planning clear and collaborative.

Laser Whitening

Advanced laser technology delivers dramatically brighter results in a single visit, safely and effectively.

Biolase Laser

State-of-the-art dental laser for soft tissue procedures, reducing discomfort and speeding healing.

BOTOX®

Therapeutic Botox for TMJ/bruxism relief and cosmetic facial esthetics, administered by our certified providers.

Telescopic Loupes

High-magnification loupes give our doctors exceptional precision during every procedure.

Paperless Charting

Fully digital patient records for accuracy, efficiency, and easy access to your dental history.

E-Claims

Electronic insurance claims processing for faster reimbursements and less paperwork for you.

Digital X-Rays

Up to 90 % less radiation than traditional X-rays, with instant high-resolution images for precise diagnosis.

Diagnodent

Laser cavity detection that finds decay at its earliest stage — before it becomes a bigger problem.
