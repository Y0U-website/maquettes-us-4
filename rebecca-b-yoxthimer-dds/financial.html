Financial Information

We Do Not Want Finances to Be an Obstacle

We do not want finances to be an obstacle in obtaining your dental health needs. We have developed a number of options to help with the payment of your treatment.

We accept cash, check, money order, credit cards, CareCredit and most insurance plans.

Financial Policy We make every effort to keep the cost of dental care affordable and to provide the highest quality of care available. We feel that all patients should know what dental work they need before beginning treatment and to have the opportunity to discuss the cost of the treatment plan ahead of time.

Insurance

We are pleased to be able to process most insurance claims and preauthorize treatments as needed. It is the patient&#x27;s responsibility to provide us with current information the day of treatment. If that information is not available, payment is due on that day.

Payment If you have insurance, you will need to pay the co-payment at the time of service. If we have over or underestimated the co-payment, you will be given a refund or a statement for additional fees due after insurance has paid.

We Accept

CareCredit Financing

CareCredit is a healthcare credit card that allows you to pay for treatment over time with low or no-interest financing options. Ask our team about applying — it only takes a few minutes.
