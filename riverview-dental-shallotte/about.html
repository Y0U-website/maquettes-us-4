Our Tools and Technology

We use CBCT, digital x-rays, and intraoral cameras so that we can provide you with the best quality of care. Dental CBCT images provide three-dimensional information that takes any guess-work out of your treatment. With digital x-rays and intraoral cameras, we’re able to get a clear picture of your teeth that can be viewed on-screen. These tools offer clearer diagnostics, which allow us to provide more effective treatment options. These images also make it easier to show you what we see, so you’ll always know exactly what’s going on with your smile.
