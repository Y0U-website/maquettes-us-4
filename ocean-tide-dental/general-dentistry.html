General Dentist

  • Fillings can help repair a decayed tooth with composite fillings to prevent further bacterial damage.
  • Dental bonding is a cosmetic dental procedure that uses a tooth-colored resin material to repair or improve the appearance of chipped, broken, or discolored teeth.
  • Root canals can save severely infected teeth by removing the pulp and nerve tissue, cleaning the canals, and sealing them.
  • Dental crowns and bridges can help protect severely fractured teeth or fill gaps in between teeth by placing a permanent restoration over the top of existing teeth.
  • Inlays and onlays. Inlays and onlays replace decayed or damaged portions of your teeth. These aesthetic restorations help protect the remaining healthy tooth structure.
