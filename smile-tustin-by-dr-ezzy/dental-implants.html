Signs You May Need Dental Implants

  • ✦ Missing Teeth — You are missing one or more teeth and want a permanent replacement rather than something removable each night that has to come out at night.
  • ✦ Daily Impact — A missing tooth has begun to affect your biting, your speech, your eating choices, or your self-confidence a little more with each passing year.
  • ✦ Adequate Bone — You have adequate bone in the jaw, or extraction and grafting could prepare the site for implant placement well ahead of the implant surgery.
  • ✦ Permanent Solution — You want a solution that will look and function like a real tooth rather than one that only looks the part and feels like one when you chew.
