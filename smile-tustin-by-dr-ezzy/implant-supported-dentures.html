Signs You May Need Implant-Supported Dentures

  • ✦ Loose Dentures — Your current dentures slip or shift while you talk and eat, and adhesives no longer solve that problem for you reliably the way they once did.
  • ✦ Sore Spots — Sore spots and discomfort from a removable denture have made wearing it unpleasant throughout the day from first thing until you take it out.
  • ✦ Bone Loss — Bone loss from your missing teeth has started to change your facial structure and the overall shape of your jaw steadily over the past several years.
  • ✦ Lasting Solution — You want a genuinely lasting solution rather than an appliance that you expect to have to replace again in only a few short years down the line.
