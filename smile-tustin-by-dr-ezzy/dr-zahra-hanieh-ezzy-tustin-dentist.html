Her Path to Dentistry

Driven by her newfound passion, Dr. Ezzy pursued her dental education at USC s prestigious Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, graduating with her Doctor of Dental Surgery in 2013. Her academic excellence was evident throughout her educational journey, earning her spots on the Dean s List at USC and UC Irvine.

Expertise and Continuous Learning

Her continuous pursuit of knowledge reflects Dr. Ezzy s commitment to providing the best possible care for her patients. She has completed advanced training in aesthetic dentistry through the UCLA School of Dentistry Aesthetic Continuum and holds a fellowship from the International Dental Implants Association. Dr. Ezzy s dedication to staying at the forefront of dental advancements is evident in her impressive commitment to continuing education, completing at least 100 CE credits annually.

A Personal Touch

What truly sets Dr. Ezzy apart is her warm, personable approach to dentistry. She believes in treating patients like family, taking the time to get to know each individual on a personal level. Getting to know you allows her to offer personalized care tailored to each patient s needs and preferences.

When she s not crafting beautiful smiles, Dr. Ezzy enjoys spending time at the beach, baking sourdough bread, and cherishing moments with her son and dogs. Dr. Ezzy s varied interests and experiences shape her holistic patient care approach, ensuring she treats each person with empathy and respect.
