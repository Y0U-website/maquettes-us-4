Michael Wright

Michael Wright, DDS, MS, is a highly recognized prosthodontist specialist at Wright Dentistry in Montgomery Village, Maryland. Dr. Michael Wright takes pride in offering his patients the best in dental care.

Dr. Wright earned his bachelor’s degree from the University of Virginia in Charlottesville before relocating to the University of Maryland to earn his master’s in oral biology and certificate in prosthodontics. Since graduating, Dr. Wright has practiced dental medicine for over two decades, becoming a member of the American Academy of Fixed Prosthodontics and a key opinion leader (KOL) for the globally recognized implant company, Nobel Biocare. Dr. Wright lectures nationally and internationally to his peers with a focus on full-mouth reconstruction and advanced implant dentistry.

Contact Wright Dentistry today to book a consultation appointment with Dr. Wright.

Maria Wright

Maria Wright, DDS, is a leading general dentist at Wright Dentistry in Gaithersburg, Maryland. She is passionate about cosmetic dentistry and educating patients on optimal oral care.

Dr. Wright‘s success as a dentist began with her dental training. After earning her bachelor’s degree from the University of Virginia in Charlottesville, Dr. Wright pursued the latest in dental education by earning her master’s at the University of Maryland. Before entering private practice, she completed a general practice residency at the Veteran Affairs Medical Center in Washington, DC, where she was able to further her studies in dentistry and discover her talent in cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Wright completes numerous hours of continuing education yearly to achieve her mission to provide optimal patient care, including training in Invisalign™, laser dentistry, and sleep apnea. She also attended the prestigious Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies.

She remains active, particularly in the local school systems and churches, volunteering to promote oral health in the community. In addition, she advocates for the use of mouthguards for athletes. She had the opportunity to accomplish this professionally with athletes of the National Basketball Association and the National Football League.

Outside the office, Dr. Wright loves traveling and golf. To book an appointment with Dr. Wright, contact the practice today.
