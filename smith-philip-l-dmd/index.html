The practice

Dr. Smith has been practicing excellent general dentistry since graduating from the prestigious University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine in 1996. After completing residency at Montefiore Medical Center, he entered private practice and has been at his Mahopac, NY location since 2003.

Dr. Smith has acquired many hours of advanced training in all aspects of dentistry including cosmetics and implant procedures.

Dr. Smith has also spent several years teaching clinical dentistry at the Columbia University College of Dental Medicine to many future dentists.

Our office is a participating provider for most of the major PPO/PDP insurance company plans.

We accept many forms of payment including all major credit cards as well as Care Credit ,and are always willing to arrange financing options when necessary.

Preventative Care Proper preventative care is the key to having a healthy smile. Regularly scheduled checkups will allow our team to diagnose oral cancer, gum disease, and tooth decay before they spread and become larger problems
