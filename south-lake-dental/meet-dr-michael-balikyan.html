Patient Care and Philosophy

In the stressful world that we live in, more and more people are grinding and/or clenching their teeth, which damages the teeth by fracturing them, breaks down bone, and for some, it even causes headaches and muscle pain. With additional Certification Education, I have learned that we can use Night guards , and in some cases, use Botulinum Toxin to relax the muscles and help specific trigger points. This way the intensity of the parafunction is reduced, increasing the quality of life for many patients.

We also can make dental appliances that help with snoring, and more importantly Sleep Apnea, which research has shown to be linked with heart disease, Alzheimer’s, etc.
