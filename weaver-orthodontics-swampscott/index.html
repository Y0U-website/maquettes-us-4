The practice

We do that by crafting beautiful smiles for patients of all ages.

We provide our patients with the best possible care through the latest orthodontic advancements. This includes Angel Aligners, Lightforce braces, Dental Monitoring, digital X-rays, 3D scans, airway obstruction treatment & more.

Located in Vinnin Square, Swampscott, we’re only a short drive from Marblehead, Salem, Lynn, Beverly, Peabody, Saugus, and Danvers. We offer flexible appointments to accommodate busy schedules.

At Weaver Orthodontics, we know you’re busy. Convenience and efficiency matter and with Dental Monitoring, clear aligner treatment is now easier and more convenient than ever!

Dr. Carlin Weaver, owner of Weaver Orthodontics, was born and raised in Swampscott, Massachusetts, where she grew up proudly watching her father, the beloved local orthodontist, transform smiles across their community. Hearing classmates say, “Your dad is my orthodontist!” was a constant source of joy and inspiration.

Dr. Weaver joined her father, Dr. Buck, in practice in 2015. Since Dr. Buck’s retirement in 2019 (he’s now saving caterpillars and raising butterflies in sunny Key West!), Dr. Weaver has continued his legacy of creating confident smiles and making patients feel truly cared for. One of her greatest joys is meeting new patients whose parents once sat in the same chair, saying, “Your dad did my…

Dr. Weaver is deeply passionate about helping people feel their best, enhancing lives through beautiful, healthy smiles, and delivering the latest advancements in orthodontic care — all in a warm, welcoming environment. She takes great pride in her team and the genuine dedication they bring to every patient experience, creating a practice rooted in compassion, excellence, and personalized care.

Weaver Orthodontics is proud to be an active part of the local community, regularly supporting schools, sports teams, Girl Scout troops, and various community fundraisers. From visiting local kindergartens to teach kids how to brush their teeth to donating holiday gifts to children in need, the team is passionate about giving back and making a positive impact beyond the office.
