The Dental Implant Process

A consultation is the first step in your journey towards a new smile at Mount Laurel Dental & Implant Center. During this appointment, Dr.Suh will evaluate if implants are recommended for you with imaging including a 3D x-ray. Bone grafting is recommended if you do not have enough bone density to place implants.

The next step after you are cleared to receive dental implants is surgical placement. The procedure is done under local anesthesia for your comfort.

The healing process starts after the implant has been placed. The implant will gradually fuse with your jawbone over the next few months through osseointegration. This creates a solid foundation for your new teeth.

After the implant has integrated fully with the jawbone Dr. Suh will attach a flange to the top of the implant post. The abutment is the connector that connects the implant to the restoration. This step is usually performed after the initial healing process.

The placement of the customized restoration is the final step of the dental implant procedure. Dr. Suh can provide you with a single implant-supported crown, bridge, or full denture set. Dr. Suh will create a restoration to perfectly match your natural teeth in terms of color, size, and shape. Your smile will be restored once the restoration has been attached to the abutment.

Types of Dental Implants

We offer a variety of dental implant options to meet our patients’ needs.

1. Single tooth implants

A single dental implant is a great option for replacing a missing tooth. It looks natural and lasts a lifetime. The implant post is implanted into the jawbone, and a custom-made crown is attached. This blends seamlessly with the surrounding teeth.

2. Implant-Supported Bridges

Implant-supported bridges can be a great option for patients who are missing several teeth in a line. This type of bridge does not rely on the adjacent teeth to support it. Instead, two or more implants anchor this bridge, which is stable and durable and restores function and appearance.

3. All-on-4 Implants

The All-on-4 system is designed for patients who have lost all or most of their teeth. It restores a full arch using only four strategically placed implants. This method is more cost-effective and efficient than traditional methods for replacing a full set of teeth. The results are also long-lasting.

4. Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures are a great alternative to traditional dentures if you have lost all your teeth. They’re more comfortable and secure. These dentures are attached directly to dental implants. This offers improved stability and eliminates the need for adhesives. It also prevents slippage.

Infection Prevention with Platelet-Rich Fibrin

At Mount Laurel Dental & Implant Center, we are pleased to offer our patients infection prevention with platelet-rich fibrin. This innovative treatment both improves healing time and helps fight infection after dental implant placement.

Platelet-rich fibrin comes from your own blood. While you’re being prepared for a dental implant or extraction, we will take a vial of your blood. This vial is placed in a centrifuge (medical spinning machine) where it is spun for several minutes. Your blood will separate into three layers: a layer of dark red cells, an almost-clear layer of plasma and a small jelly-like layer of platelet-rich fibrin.

This fibrin contains a high proportion of proteins that improve the healing of surgical sites such as when teeth are pulled or dental implants are placed. Placing fibrin in these areas that have experienced surgery helps them form a protective clot and begin healing more quickly. There is a reduced chance of a dry socket that occurs when the blood clot is lost. A dry socket lengthens healing time and can be quite painful.

Our implant dentistry in Mount Laurel NJ began offering treatment with platelet-rich fibrin because we want the very best results for each one of our patients.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Durable and Long-Lasting Durability is one of the biggest advantages of dental implants. Dental implants can last for a lifetime with proper care. The titanium post resists decay, and because it is integrated with the jawbone, it creates a secure and stable foundation that will not shift or become loose over time.
