Meet Dr. Olinto

Dr. Maggie Olinto brings a passion for compassionate, high-quality dental care to Loveland Dental of Fort Mill, SC. Originally from Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Dr. Olinto moved to Jacksonville, Florida during high school and went on to graduate with honors from Florida State University with a degree in Chemical Science.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Roseman University College of Dental Medicine, where she graduated with honors and was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the prestigious national dental honor society. Following dental school, Dr. Olinto gained valuable clinical experience while practicing in Utah before relocating to South Carolina to be closer to family.

Dr. Olinto is deeply committed to creating a welcoming and comfortable environment for all her patients. She understands that visiting the dentist can be intimidating for many, and she strives to ensure that every patient feels safe, heard, and cared for throughout their dental journey.

Outside of the office, Dr. Olinto enjoys reading, baking homemade sourdough bread, and spending time outdoors. Whether hiking, camping, or enjoying a picnic at the park with her husband and their dog, she finds balance and joy in nature.

Dr. Olinto is excited to serve the Fort Mill community and looks forward to building lasting relationships with her patients through excellent care and genuine connection.
