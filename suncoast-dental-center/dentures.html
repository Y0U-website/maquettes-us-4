FAQs

What are dentures?

Dentures are removable dental appliances designed to replace missing teeth and surrounding tissues. Complete dentures replace all teeth in either the upper or lower arch, while partial dentures fill in gaps after some natural teeth remain. The base is designed to match your natural gum color, while the teeth are carefully selected to complement your facial features and skin tone. They’re custom-made to fit your mouth precisely, restoring both function and appearance after tooth loss.

How long do dentures last?

Dentures typically last between five to ten years with proper care and maintenance, though this varies based on individual factors. Your jawbone and gums naturally change shape over time, which can affect how your dentures fit. Regular visits to Dr. Lowell allow us to reline or rebase your dentures as needed to maintain optimal comfort and function. Daily cleaning, careful handling, and professional adjustments significantly extend the lifespan of your dentures.

How to clean dentures?

Remove your dentures and rinse them under running water to eliminate loose food particles after each meal. Brush them daily using a soft-bristled denture brush and non-abrasive denture cleanser, as regular toothpaste can scratch the surface. Soak dentures overnight in a denture-cleaning solution or plain water to keep them moist and maintain their shape, and brush your gums, tongue, and any remaining teeth before reinserting your dentures.

How to care for dentures?

Handle your dentures carefully over a folded towel or basin of water to prevent breakage if you drop them. Remove and rinse them after eating, and clean them thoroughly at least once daily with appropriate denture products. Soak your dentures overnight in a denture-cleaning solution or plain water to keep them moist and maintain their shape. Brush your gums, tongue, and any remaining teeth before reinserting your dentures to promote healthy oral tissue and fresh breath.

Can you eat with dentures?

You can absolutely eat with dentures once you complete the initial adjustment period. Start with soft foods and gradually introduce firmer textures as you become more comfortable. Properly fitted dentures allow you to chew most foods effectively, though some patients find certain sticky or very hard items challenging. Dr. Lowell ensures your dentures fit securely so you can enjoy a varied, nutritious diet without significant restrictions or discomfort during meals.
