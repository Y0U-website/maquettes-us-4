Dental Implants

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last 20 years or longer. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential to ensure their longevity.
  • What do dental implants look like? Dental implants are designed to mimic the appearance of natural teeth. They consist of a titanium post that integrates with the jawbone, topped with a custom crown that matches the size, shape and color of your surrounding teeth.
