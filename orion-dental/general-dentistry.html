Diagnostic Imaging

3D imaging is safe, simple to administer and provides your dentist with a digital image that can be manipulated on the screen to zoom in and gain more specific information. Not only of great benefit but we are confident this innovative technology will improve your dental experience.

For more information read on Five reasons digital x-rays are better than traditional radiography .

Routine Cleaning

Routine Cleaning is Your Best Value Dental Care.

The truth is that many factors impact your oral health, including lifestyle habits, diet, genetics and oral hygiene. Dental problems that have gone undetected for lengthy periods, may develop more severe disease, become more difficult and expensive to treat.

When evidence of disease is detected at a routine appointment, treatment should be simple, and relatively inexpensive to treat.

Periodontal Maintenance

When evidence of mild to moderate periodontal disease is present, deep cleaning can prevent worsening of the disease. The use of laser therapy treats early gum disease with no discomfort and prevents symptoms from worsening.

Clinical Examinations

Dr. Escudero is known for his detailed clinical examinations. He is on the lookout for early warning signs of oral health issues. Undetected, common issues can become serious health problems whereas early treatment is associated with best outcomes.

Dr. Escudero is passionate about advanced scientific research and education that preserves your natural teeth, bones, lips, jaw and all the structures of your face to keep you looking youthful and healthy.

With careful observation of your tongue, throat, face, head and neck, he is checking for any signs of trouble. Simple, effective treatments can maintain optimal function and help to avoid future health problems.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are proven to protect teeth from decay. Find out more about sealants: Sealants

Cavity Arresting Treatment

Cavity arresting treatment (SDF) is a medicament approved by the FDA in 2012 for use in the United States. When painted onto teeth it will help prevent a cavity from becoming worse.

Desensitizing Treatment

Desensitizing medications can be applied with a fine brush, to seal the exposed microscopic pores in the dentin layer of teeth that causes the sensitivity you feel.

Tooth-strengthening Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride helps to strengthen the enamel on your teeth, so that cavities are less likely to develop.

At routine dental appointments Dr. Escudero applies the gel or foam to your teeth, and rinses it off after a few minutes.
