Symptoms of Failed Dental Implants

Dental implant failure can happen at any time, whether it is weeks, months, or even decades after the initial placement surgery. That is why you should always monitor your mouth and take action at the first sign of a problem.

Some potential indications of dental implant failure include:

  • Unusual pain. Once you heal from dental implant surgery, you should enjoy pain-free function from your new teeth. Severe or prolonged discomfort is a big red flag.
  • Difficulty chewing. It should be easy for you to eat with your implants. Challenges may indicate the possibility of failure.
  • An implant feels loose. This could be a problem with the implant itself or the restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) that is attached to it.
  • Signs of infection. Redness, swelling, gum recession, and pus could all point to the presence of peri-implantitis.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

It is important that you visit us as soon as possible after you notice that something is amiss with your dental implants. Early intervention might help you to avoid invasive, extensive, and expensive procedures.

When you come to our office, we will thoroughly examine your mouth and use advanced imaging equipment to look beneath your gumline. After we gather adequate information, we can suggest your next steps. In some cases, something simple such as antibiotics or a new restoration is all that is needed. If the situation is more severe, we might have to surgically remove the implant. Later, after you heal and undergo any necessary restorative care, you might be able to get a new one.
