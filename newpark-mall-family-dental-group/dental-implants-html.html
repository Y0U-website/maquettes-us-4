What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small, thin posts that resemble screws. More often than not, they are made out of titanium, which is a biocompatible material that can fuse with the jawbone. Since dental implants can act as new tooth roots, they offer better stability than traditional bridges and dentures, and they also provide enhanced chewing power that allows you to handle all kinds of foods. Dental implants can support a crown, bridge, or denture depending on how many teeth are missing. An abutment helps keep the chosen restoration firmly anchored to the post(s).

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

Getting dental implants is a specialized multi-step process that takes several months. While this may seem like a big commitment, these remarkable appliances deliver a range of considerable benefits that make them more than worth the effort. At Newpark Mall Family Dental Group, we perform the entire dental implant process in-house so you won’t have to worry about turning to an outside specialist. Here’s a step-by-step guide to how dental implants are placed at our office.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

The process begins with a consultation at our office. Then, we’ll thoroughly examine your mouth to determine the severity of your tooth loss and verify that you are a suitable candidate for implants. We’ll also discuss the timeframe for your treatment and give you a cost estimate. Some patients may need preliminary procedures like periodontal therapy and bone grafts before they can be ready to receive dental implants. Once you have been approved for the treatment, we can schedule you for surgery.

Dental Implant Surgery

We perform dental implant surgery in-house, which can simplify the process by eliminating the need to deal with an outside surgeon who uses a different pricing structure. Dr. Lei will begin the procedure by administering a local anesthetic to ensure your comfort, and you may receive sedation if needed. Then, he will place titanium posts in the jawbone and close the gums around them. He’ll then place caps on your implants to protect them from damage.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment

After surgery, you’ll need several months for your dental implants to fuse with your jawbones through the natural process of osseointegration. This gives your implants the sturdy hold they need to exert maximum chewing power and last for decades. When your gums have healed adequately, you’ll come back to our office so Dr. Lei can place abutment pieces on your implants. Your final restoration will be crafted while you are recovering from this procedure over the following several weeks.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restoration(s)

A skilled technician at a trusted dental laboratory will craft your final restorations based on detailed impressions taken of your mouth. Once your crown, dental bridge, or denture is ready, you’ll come back to our office to have it mounted on your implants. We’ll then check to make sure that your new teeth are positioned properly and comfortably, make any final adjustments, and send you on your way so you can enjoy your newly restored smile.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Since patients receive more than half a million dental implants annually, these amazing prosthetic devices are quickly becoming one of the most popular ways to replace lost teeth. While this treatment is a significant commitment of time, effort, and finances, its benefits are well worth the investment. Here are only a few of the incredible advantages that you can enjoy thanks to this modern tooth replacement solution from Newpark Mall Family Dental Group.

Day-to-Day Benefits

Dental implants can deliver several considerable benefits that you will be able to notice in your everyday life such as:

  • Boosted confidence: You won’t have to feel self-conscious about empty spaces in your smile anymore, which can make it easier to socialize and make excellent first impressions at work.
  • Improved chewing power: Dental implants exert almost all of the same chewing power as real teeth, which means that you’ll be ready to dig into tough but tasty fare like juicy steaks, smoky corn-on-the-cob, and crunchy nuts and seeds
  • Secure hold: Since they are anchored directly to the jaw, your replacement teeth won’t slip when you’re trying to eat or speak, which can save you from much embarrassment in social situations.
  • Easy oral hygiene: Implants only require the same hygiene measures as real teeth so you won’t have to worry about the elaborate nightly cleaning and soaking rituals needed by traditional dentures.

Health Benefits

Dental implant patients also enjoy a range of benefits for their oral and overall health, including:

  • Varied diet: Dental implants have enough chewing power to handle most foods, which is good news for patients concerned about nutrition. Replacing your lost teeth can also reduce your risk of malnutrition and indigestion.
  • Stronger jaw: Unlike traditional bridges and dentures, dental implant posts will actively stimulate the jaw to help prevent bone loss. This can help you maintain a strong and youthful profile and avoid issues like facial sagging.
  • No irritation: Since dental implants are securely anchored in the jaw, the restorations mounted on them will not slide about on the gums, which can prevent injuries such as soft tissue irritation and chips in your remaining teeth.
  • Prevent infections: Simple oral hygiene requirements make it easy to avoid infections like gum disease and tooth decay.

Long-Term Benefits

Dental implants can also provide a few astounding benefits over the long term such as:

  • Amazing success rate: More than 95 % of dental implants are still successful ten years after they are placed.
  • Long lifespan: Dental implants that receive excellent care can last for decades or possibly a lifetime.
  • Saving money: Restorations supported by dental implants can last several times longer than traditional dentures and bridges, which can possibly save a patient thousands of dollars over the years by reducing the need for replacement appliances.

Who Dental Implants Can Help

You may not be sure whether dental implants are truly the best choice for you, but fortunately, our team is here to help you figure that out. We can check to see whether you meet the criteria for getting dental implants, such as having good oral health. Note that dental implants are very versatile, and they can be an excellent choice regardless of how many teeth need to be replaced; below are some situations that dental implants can help with.

Missing One Tooth

Regardless of whether the empty space left by a missing tooth is visible when you smile, you should still have it filled promptly if you want to avoid long-term oral health complications. A dental implant post can be placed in the gap. Once it has formed a bond with the jaw, it can be restored with a crown that has been carefully personalized to blend in with your natural teeth as closely as possible.

Missing Multiple Teeth

You don’t necessarily need to get a separate dental implant post for every individual tooth that you have lost. When three or more teeth in a row are missing, the space that they left behind can be completely filled with a beautiful, long-lasting dental bridge anchored to a pair of sturdy dental implant posts. Unlike traditional bridges, an implant bridge can be placed without the need to make permanent alterations to any of the teeth that are still in your mouth.

Missing All of Your Teeth

The idea of not having any natural teeth can be a frightening one, but even patients who have suffered from extensive tooth loss can still enjoy complete smiles again. You can get full dentures supported by dental implants in order to replace an entire arch of teeth. Implant dentures can be removable, but they can also be permanently fixed in place; we’ll help you figure out which kind you should get based on your needs and preferences.

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

We can only give you a reliable estimate of how much you will need to pay for dental implants once we have examined your mouth and learned more about the specifics of your unique situation. When comparing the prices of your available tooth replacement options, it is worth remembering that dental implants can potentially last for decades as long as they receive the right maintenance, meaning you won’t need to pay to have them replaced as often compared to traditional bridges and dentures.

Dental Implant Failure & Salvage

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime, and indeed, they provide most patients with decades of reliable function. However, there is always a very small chance that they will fail. This can happen due to various issues, such as an infection, facial trauma, or underlying medical problems. If you ever believe that is happening to you, contact our team right away. We will evaluate the situation and design a treatment plan to help you. Our goal is to help you get back to enjoying a complete, healthy, and confident smile ASAP!

Can I take dental implants out?

Since dental implants are surgically fixed in the jaw and fused with their surrounding bone structure through the natural process of osseointegration, they should not be removable by anyone but a qualified dental professional. Never attempt to remove a dental implant on your own, as this can lead to serious oral injuries and appliance failure. However, some types of implant dentures are designed to disconnect from the implants supporting them for cleaning.
