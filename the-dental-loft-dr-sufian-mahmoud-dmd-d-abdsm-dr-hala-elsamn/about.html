Bringing precision to every smile

Dr. Hala Elsamna abo - Certified Orthodontist

Dr. Hala Elsamna is a board certified orthodontist through the American Board of Orthodontics ABO, a distinction reflecting the highest standards of clinical excellence, precision, and continued mastery.

She was selected for a highly competitive 7 year accelerated dental program between the New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT) and Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, graduating summa cum laude. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Rutgers, completed a General Practice Residency at Hackensack Meridian Health, and pursued advanced orthodontic training through a fellowship at Rutgers and her specialty residency at the Center for Translational Orthodontic Research (CTOR) in Hoboken, New Jersey.

Dr. Elsamna’s clinical approach is centered on early orthodontic intervention and growth guidance, helping shape proper development at the most critical stages. She combines this with comprehensive, aesthetically driven care for patients of all ages, including sophisticated clear aligner therapy as an Invisalign certified provider.

Known for her calm, attentive presence, Dr. Elsamna creates a refined, personalized experience where patients feel comfortable, understood, and confident throughout their treatment journey. As both an orthodontist and a mother, she brings a thoughtful, family centered perspective to every interaction.

Dr. Elsamna is the co-founder of the Global Academy of Dentistry (GAD) and an active member of the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), New Jersey Dental Association (NJDA), American Dental Association (ADA), and the Charles H. Tweed International Foundation. She remains deeply committed to continued excellence, innovation, and meaningful community impact.

Beyond the practice, Dr. Elsamna treasures time with her husband, daughter, and son, an essential part of the balance that shapes her thoughtful, family centered approach to care.

New patients

Here’s what you can expect

Your first visit at The Dental Loft sets the tone for everything that follows. We take dentistry seriously, and we take your time seriously too. You’ll receive a comprehensive exam, digital imaging, and a real conversation about your goals. Not just “how are your teeth,” but what you want your smile to look and feel like long term. Whether you’re here for cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, sleep appliances, or routine care, we build a plan around you.

The Dental Loft experience We believe great dentistry starts with precision and communication. That means clear explanations, transparent recommendations, and treatment plans that are not dictated by insurance. With in-house specialists and experts, your care stays streamlined and collaborative. Everything is coordinated. Everything is intentional.

What to bring with you To keep your visit smooth and efficient, please bring:

Designed for comfort. Built for performance.

Technology and comfort go hand in hand in how we deliver care. From digital scanning to biomimetic dentistry, our tools and techniques allow for precise, personalized treatment, while thoughtful touches and small comforts help you feel relaxed and at ease throughout your visit.

Digital scanning and treatment planning

We use iTero and 3Shape digital scanners to capture highly detailed images without traditional impressions. This allows for more accurate diagnoses, clearer communication, and treatment plans built with precision from the start.

Advanced orthodontic care

Orthodontic treatment is carefully planned before it begins. With customized bracket placement and Invisalign clear aligners, we guide tooth movement with intention, creating efficient treatment timelines and balanced, natural-looking results.

A thoughtfully designed office

Our space reflects the level of care we provide. Upgraded operatories, dual monitors, and a welcoming environment help you feel informed, comfortable, and confident throughout your visit.

Minimally Invasive approach

We practice biomimetic dentistry, an advanced, minimally invasive approach focused on preserving as much of your natural tooth structure as possible. This conservative philosophy often means less drilling, fewer aggressive procedures, and restorations designed to last.
