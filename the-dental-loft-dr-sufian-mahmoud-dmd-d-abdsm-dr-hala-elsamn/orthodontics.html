Braces

Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. They also function better when you chew, preventing uneven wear and reducing the risk of jaw pain. At The Dental Loft, we use the latest in orthodontic technology, including DIBS AI, a digital system that allows our team to plan your treatment with precision, ensuring optimal results. Whether you choose traditional braces or clear aligners, the journey to a healthier, more confident smile is easier and more comfortable than ever.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces are clear, wire-based appliances that orthodontists use to correct crooked, gapped, or crowded teeth and create an aligned smile. Similar to traditional braces, ceramic braces gradually apply pressure over time to straighten teeth but use clear or colored brackets and wires that match the shade of your own teeth. Due to their translucent appearance, ceramic braces are an attractive alignment option for patients looking for a less noticeable alternative to metal braces.

Invisalign

Our practice is proud to offer Invisalign, a modern and discreet way to straighten your teeth without the look or feel of traditional braces. Using a series of custom-made, clear aligners, Invisalign gently shifts your teeth into place while allowing you to eat, brush, and smile with confidence. Each aligner is tailored to your unique treatment plan, ensuring comfort and effective results. Whether you&#x27;re looking to correct crowding, spacing, or bite issues, Invisalign provides a convenient, nearly invisible solution that fits seamlessly into your lifestyle.
