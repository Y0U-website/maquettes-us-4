Rather than relying on aggressive treatments, this philosophy uses advanced materials and techniques to mimic the natural strength, function, and appearance of your teeth. The result is a more conservative, durable solution designed to support long-term oral health.

Dr. Sufian Mahmoud has completed extensive training in biomimetic dentistry, including working alongside pioneers of the technique. He applies these principles to create restorations that look natural, feel comfortable, and perform like your own teeth. By prioritizing both aesthetics and function, this approach helps deliver healthier, longer-lasting smiles with less invasive treatment.

Dental Fillings

Cavities are one of the most common oral diseases, so it&#x27;s imperative we tackle them as soon as they begin to compromise the health and wellness of your teeth. When left untreated, they can cause serious discomfort and may require more invasive procedures to alleviate. Thanks to fillings, your dentist can halt the progression of tooth decay in a single visit. By stopping by for your regular 6-month cleaning and checkup, you can prevent new cavities from forming in the first place.

Dental Crowns

Dental crowns restore damaged teeth by covering and protecting what remains of your natural tooth. When you have a cracked or broken tooth, these custom caps not only shield the root but also improve how your teeth work together when you bite. If you&#x27;ve had a root canal or dental implant, your dentist will typically recommend a crown to strengthen the treated tooth. With several material options available, crowns help both your dental health and appearance, giving you back a smile you can feel good about.

Extractions

Extractions are always a last resort because preserving your smile is our top priority. However, sometimes a problematic tooth causes more harm than good. In these cases, your dentist may recommend an extraction to restore your oral health. Using gentle techniques and local anesthesia, decayed or damaged teeth can be safely removed to relieve discomfort or make space for dental implants, a bridge, or another type of tooth replacement.
