Away from the office Dr. Trujillo enjoys spending time with her husband and two boys skiing, hiking, biking and other outdoor adventures.

Renee - Registered Dental Hygienist Renee was born and raised in Las Cruces New Mexico where she worked as a dental receptionist for 11 years. She later received her degrees in Public Health and Applied Science of Dental Hygiene from New Mexico State University/Dona Ana Community College in 2019. Renee is extremely devoted to educating her patients on their individual dental needs while creating a comfortable and friendly experience. In her spare time she enjoys spending time with family, reading, and crocheting.

New to dentistry, she appreciates the opportunity it gives to meet and communicate with a variety of wonderful people every day. Jules enjoys enhancing her learning with dentistry, giving her an aspect of how oral health is important. In her free time, Jules loves going on new adventures with her son, trying new foods, and spending time outdoors.
