Parth U. Patel, DDS

Math and Science were always my favorite subjects in school, but I always knew that I wanted to be in the healthcare field because of my desire to help people. Dr. Patel says. My dream job includes aspects of working with my hands and having an opportunity to meet and build relationships with new people, thus being able to have a positive impact on other people’s lives.

Dr. Parth Patel considers Joplin, Missouri his hometown. He has been lucky enough to call the states of New Jersey, Mississippi, and Georgia his home over the years. He considers Joplin his hometown because this was the place where he found his aspirations of being a dentist, and because this is where his parents have resided over the last 12 years.

In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his 5-month-old golden retriever puppy, Mia, his parents, and his friends. He also enjoys hiking, going to watch the Kansas City Chiefs play, and traveling. Through the years he has dedicated his time through volunteering to better his communities that he has resided in such as KC pet project, Smile Academy, and Harvesters. He hopes to continue this and use his specific training as apart of his volunteering to better the community of Joplin!
