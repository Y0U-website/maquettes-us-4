A child mouth is a big deal

Each children is unique and that calls for exceptional and personalize care. In some cases the first experience with a dentist happens at an office focused on adult care or family care. Dental care in this environment is often intimidating and frightening for kids. Kids need patience and information about what will happens. This first impression can create anxiety and avoidance of the dentist that will last a lifetime.

Education Matters

We designed Under the Sea Children’s Dentistry to make your child feel safe, calm and carefree. The office has been created with your child in mind. From a child atmosphere starting in the waiting room, and then every area in our office we have strived to create and leaves a lasting, positive memory.

We have handpicked a staff that is passionate about your child’s comfort and care. Our Pediatric Dentist has been educated at premier pediatric dental schools and has committed her careers to the treatment of children.

Under The Sea Children’s Dentist will treat your child from infancy through late adolescence, as well as those with special needs. For the comfort of your child the offices offer Nitrous Oxide, Conscious Sedation, as well as General Anesthesia delivered in a hospital setting which is recommended for apprehensive children, very young children, and children with special needs that would not work well under Conscious Sedation.
