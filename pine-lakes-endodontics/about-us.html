Meet Our Endodontists

Dr. Brock Droll

Dr. Droll graduated first in his class from the Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine in 2009, gaining membership into the prestigious Omicron Kappa Upsilon dental honor society. Following dental school, Dr. Droll attended the Ohio State University School of Dentistry’s program in Graduate Endodontics. He graduated with a specialty certificate in Endodontics and a Masters of Science from Ohio State in 2011, having been published in the Journal of Endodontics.

Following graduation, Dr. Droll and his wife made a home in central Minnesota and are proud to raise their family in our beautiful area. Having dedicated himself and this practice to professional excellence, Dr. Droll is proud to be a board-certified endodontist and has worked tirelessly to make Pine Lakes Endodontics a world-class endodontic clinic, bringing state of the art care to the good people of Northern Minnesota.

Welcome to Our Endodontic Practice

We warmly welcome you to our dedicated endodontic practice, where your comfort and dental health are our top priorities. Our exclusive focus on endodontic care (root canal treatment) means you receive expert attention for your tooth and root-related needs, delivered in a welcoming, supportive environment. We’re the toothache specialists!

Why Choose an Endodontist?

Endodontists are dental specialists who receive advanced training beyond dental school, focusing exclusively on diagnosing and treating issues within the tooth’s interior. By concentrating solely on endodontic care, we bring a higher level of expertise and skill to every patient, ensuring you benefit from the most effective and precise treatments available.

Expertise in Challenging Cases

As endodontic specialists, we routinely handle complex cases that require additional skill and experience. Whether you’re dealing with a difficult root canal, re-treatment, or trauma to a tooth, our team is equipped to manage even the most challenging situations, ensuring optimal outcomes for your dental health.
