Crowded Teeth

Do your teeth crowd together, overlapping in the process? If so, then you already know the negative impact that overcrowding can have on your self-esteem and oral health. Gaps Between Teeth

Overbite

Oftentimes, patients assume that Invisalign is only an option for patients who are struggling with mild orthodontic problems, like a small gap between their two front teeth. If you’re struggling to chew properly, enunciate clearly, and smile confidently because your upper teeth jut out considerably, then it’s time to take the next step: schedule an appointment with one of our talented cosmetic dentists .

Underbite

Do your lower teeth sit in front of your upper ones when you bite down? The good news is that Invisalign clear aligners can be used in tandem with other orthodontic accessories, like rubber bands, to correct mild to moderate cases. As a result, aligning your teeth and your bite may not involve any metal!

Crossbite

Benefits of Invisalign

When you look at all the amazing benefits Invisalign can provide, it’s easy to see why this treatment is an incredibly popular way to address mild to moderate dental misalignments. Here’s a quick look at how Invisalign can help you from the friendly team at DeMartino Dental Group .

Improved Comfort

Invisalign trays are constructed from smooth plastic that eliminates the possibility of pointy metal wires and brackets poking into the insides of your lips and cheeks. If you experience any discomfort during your Invisalign treatment, it will probably be when you start wearing a new set of aligners, but this will be mild and temporary. Many patients find that they can sleep through the worst of it by switching to a new set of aligners and taking an over-the-counter pain reliever before bed.

Discreet Trays

Invisalign aligners are designed to be virtually invisible when worn so patients won’t have to worry about them showing up in photographs or being noticed by casual observers. This makes the treatment especially great for those who are anticipating a special event such as a wedding, graduation, or job interview.

Better Dental Hygiene

Invisalign clear aligners are designed to be easily removed by the patient. This allows them to take them out before brushing and flossing so they won’t have to worry about cleaning around their orthodontic appliances during oral hygiene sessions, which can help them prevent stains and painful oral infections that can cause treatment delays. These simple hygiene requirements also eliminate the need for specialized dental cleaning equipment.

Quick Follow-Ups

Invisalign check-in appointments typically take only fifteen to thirty minutes and mostly serve to allow our dentist to monitor the progress of your treatment and provide you with new aligners as needed. These checkups do not involve uncomfortable appliance adjustments and are excellent for those with busy schedules.

Boosted Confidence

Since Invisalign trays are virtually invisible when worn, it’s incredibly unlikely that other people will notice you wearing them. This can help you maintain a stylish appearance around your friends and a professional look at work, and it can make the treatment incredibly suitable for image-conscious teens.

Noninvasive Treatment

Invisalign is a minimally invasive orthodontic treatment that requires fewer changes to a patient’s lifestyle than traditional braces. You’ll be able to continue eating and drinking whatever you like and brushing and flossing your teeth normally since the devices are easy to remove. The brief check-in appointments may also allow you to avoid taking time off work or school.

Life with Invisalign

Living with Invisalign isn’t much different than life without braces once you get used to it. To start, there’s no need to modify your diet or worry about any special cleaning techniques. Just wear your aligners for 20 to 22 hours each day, and you can remove them as needed.

You’ll need to make sure you brush your teeth and rinse your aligners before putting them back in. Beginning with clean teeth and trays is crucial for your dental and oral health. The aligners fit very closely to the surface of your teeth; this means that any bacteria or food particles will become trapped, accelerating tooth decay. With good oral hygiene practices, you will gradually advance through your treatment and be on your way to straighter, more beautiful teeth!

Invisalign FAQs

Can you get Invisalign after braces?

It is possible, yes. So, don’t let the fact that you’ve had traditional braces in the past stop you from scheduling an Invisalign consultation! During this appointment, one of our dedicated cosmetic dentists in Phillipsburg will review your dental history, complete an oral exam, and discuss the responsibilities that come with clear aligners in order to determine if you’re a good candidate.

Who is not eligible for Invisalign ?

Although Invisalign in Phillipsburg is quite versatile, it isn’t for everyone. For example, it isn’t ideal for patients who are struggling with severe misalignment, gum disease, or tooth decay. That’s why the initial consultation is so important! After completing a comprehensive oral exam, we can determine if Invisalign is right for you and, if it isn’t, which treatment (or combination of treatments) we recommend instead.

What should I do if my Invisalign tray breaks?

First, try to pinpoint the last place you know you had your aligners. Then, carefully retrace your steps. Since there’s a chance that they ended up somewhere unexpected, it’s important to look in the not-so-obvious places, like the center console of your car and your laundry basket.

If you’ve looked everywhere you can think of, but your aligners are nowhere to be found, then call our Phillipsburg dental office. Over the phone, we can let you know if it’s safe to progress to the next set in the series or if you should revert to the previous set while we have a replacement made.

How do I clean my Invisalign trays?

Fortunately, you don’t need a laundry list of special cleaning products. Quite the opposite: you likely already have everything you need at home!

First, use clean, cool water to rinse your aligners each time you take them off. Before you put them back on, brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash. Then, use a damp, soft-bristle toothbrush and clear, mild dishwashing liquid to gently remove saliva, food particles, plaque, and other debris from the surface of your trays. If they need a deeper clean, use the cleaning crystals that came in your welcome kit or a dentist-approved clear aligner solution.

Will I need to wear a retainer after Invisalign treatment?

The short answer is yes. Don’t get discouraged, though. After all, most patients only need to wear theirs at night while they sleep. Plus, they make retainers that look almost identical to clear aligners. So, if you do need to wear them a bit more often, you don’t have to worry about unwanted attention being drawn to your smile.

Can I drink coffee with Invisalign ?

Although you may have heard otherwise, you cannot drink coffee while wearing your aligners. There are a few reasons for this, including that hot coffee can warp the plastic and iced coffee can cause a noticeable, yellow hue to surface. So, if you want to stay on track with your treatment plan and maintain the discreet appearance of your trays, you need to take them off beforehand.
