What Should I Expect When Getting Dental Implants?

Most people know that implants are a considerable investment, but they’re also a time investment. At Colony Dental, we work hard to minimize the overwhelm of your dental care. Typically, dental implants is a several months-long journey that can be broken down into three steps:

Post placement and osteointegration

When Dr. Hassan places your post, or posts, they typically require 3 to 6 months to fuse to your jawbone through the process of osteointegration.

Abutment placement

Once the post is fused to your jawbone, your Sugar Land dentist will place the abutment that serves as the attachment for your crown. There is typically 1-2 weeks of soft-tissue healing required after the placement of the abutment.

Placement of crown

This is the final step. The crown or restoration is attached to the abutment(s), and you leave with a beautifully restored smile!

Every patient is different, meaning you will have your own unique timeframe. Your dentist at Colony Dental will work with you to set realistic expectations. Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements used to restore one or more missing teeth. They usually include three parts: a titanium post placed in the jawbone, an abutment that connects to the post, and a natural-looking crown that replaces the visible part of the tooth.

Why should I replace missing teeth?

Replacing missing teeth is important for both your smile and your long-term oral health. Missing teeth can lead to bone loss, shifting teeth, bite problems, changes in facial shape, and a higher risk of tooth decay or gum disease.

What types of dental implants are available at Colony Dental?

Colony Dental offers implant solutions for different needs, including single-tooth replacement, replacement of an entire arch, and full-mouth tooth replacement with All-on-4 dental implants.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Many patients with missing teeth may be good candidates for dental implants. Dental implants work best when there is enough bone density to support the implant post. If you have been told you do not have enough bone, Colony Dental may still be able to help with bone grafting.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The dental implant process usually takes several months. After the implant post is placed, it typically needs about 3 to 6 months to fuse with the jawbone. After that, the abutment is placed, followed by the final crown or restoration.
