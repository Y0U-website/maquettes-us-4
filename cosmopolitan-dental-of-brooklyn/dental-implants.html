Dental Implants Explained

In everyday use, dental implants often refer to a false tooth attached to a metal abutment that is anchored in the jaw. Technically, however, “dental implant” refers to the anchor device that is planted into your jaw. The dental implant itself is essentially the foundation of the false tooth.

An abutment will connect to the dental implant, and a crown can be placed on top of that abutment. The false tooth itself is actually called a crown. Dental Implants and crowns typically go together, so sometimes folks will refer to them both as implants or crowns.

Dental terminology can be a bit intimidating at first. That’s why we work closely with our patients in Brooklyn and across New York to understand the whole process of selecting, installing, and maintaining dental implants and crowns.

Dental Implants Frequently Asked Questions

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of titanium or other biocompatible materials that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They provide a strong and stable foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Contact Cosmopolitan Dental of Brooklyn to schedule a consultation. If you’re suffering from gingivitis or another condition that could complicate the implant procedure, we can work with you to clear that condition up.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last for many years, even a lifetime, if properly cared for. Factors such as oral hygiene, overall health, and maintenance play a significant role in their longevity. If you live in or near Brooklyn and have dental implants, schedule regular cleanings at Cosmopolitan Dental of Brooklyn.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes! Dental implants have a high success rate and are considered safe when performed by an experienced dental professional. As with any surgery, there are risks, such as infection, damage to surrounding structures, and implant failure. While these risks are rare, they make it crucial to work with the best dental professionals, such as those found at Cosmopolitan Dental of Brooklyn.

How much do dental implants cost in NYC?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on factors such as the number of implants needed, the materials used, and the complexity of the procedure. It is best to consult with a dental professional for a detailed cost estimate. If you live in Brooklyn or greater-NYC, feel free to reach out for specifics.

Will dental insurance cover the cost of dental implants?

Dental insurance coverage for implants varies widely between policies. Some plans may cover a large portion of the cost, while others may not cover implants at all. It is essential to review your specific insurance plan to determine your coverage.

What is the dental implant procedure like?

The dental implant procedure typically involves several steps, including consultation and planning, tooth extraction (if needed), bone grafting (if necessary), implant placement, healing and integration, and finally, placement of the artificial tooth or teeth.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The entire dental implant process can take anywhere from a few months to over a year, depending on factors such as the need for bone grafting, healing time, and the complexity of the case.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the dental implant procedure, as local anesthesia is used to numb the area. Post-operative pain can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers or prescription medication, as recommended by your dentist.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for dental implants involves maintaining good oral hygiene, including regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups. Your dental professional will provide specific instructions on how to care for your implants to ensure their longevity.
