Meet the Team at SJ Dental

At SJ Dental, we are always delighted when individuals and families consider us for their new dental home. We believe that building long-term, trusting relationships is crucial when providing personalized dentistry. Our dentists know that there are factors in everyone’s life that impact their decisions about dental treatment. When we learn about the things outside of dentistry that matter to you, we can create personalized treatment plans to meet your needs.

Dr. Jing and Dr. Steve are committed to providing quality dental care for all ages and educating our patients to stay informed about their oral health. So if you’re a parent who needs to schedule your toddler’s first dental visit or a senior looking for tooth replacement, we’d love to help you. And for all the ages and stages of life in between, we’ll be there for your family, providing individualized dental care with compassion and honesty.

Our dental team looks forward to welcoming you to SJ Dental in Twinsburg, OH. Until then, please take a few moments to read the biographies below to learn more about us!

Dr. Jing Wang

Dr. Jing Wang received her bachelor s degree from Case Western Reserve University in 2004, then went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Case School of Dental Medicine in 2010. After graduation, Dr. Wang completed a one-year residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at CWRU and University Hospital. She loved the family-friendly atmosphere of the east side of Cleveland when growing up and decided to stay and raise her family here as well.

Dr. Wang loves working with patients of all ages and is very experienced in helping anxious patients overcome their fears of dentistry. With a passion for sharing her knowledge with others, Dr. Wang is also a clinical instructor in the Advanced Education in General Dentistry Department at Case School of Dental Medicine.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Wang enjoys spending time with family and friends, as well as traveling.

Dr. Steve Wang

Dr. Steve Wang earned his Bachelor s and Master s of Dental Surgery degrees at West China School of Stomatology in China. In 2004, he graduated from Case Western Reserve University with a bachelor’s degree in chemistry. Dr. Wang subsequently attended Case School of Dental Medicine, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree. He also completed a one-year general practice residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at Case School of Dental Medicine.

With a desire to inspire the next generation of dentists, Dr. Wang serves as a clinical instructor at Case School of Dental Medicine AEGD. He has also won several awards and had numerous articles published. In addition, with a desire to give back to our community, Dr. Wang volunteers his time providing dental services to a free clinic, the Care Alliance Health Center, and the Medworks Dental Clinic. Dr. Wang is a member of the American Dental Association, the Ohio Dental Association, and the Great Lakes Dental Association.

In his free time, Dr. Wang enjoys reading, basketball, and traveling. His favorite quote is, The only way to do great work is to love what you do.
