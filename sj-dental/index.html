The practice

Dr. Jing and Dr. Steve are committed to providing a high standard of dental care for all ages. We’re dedicated to patient education because being informed about your family’s dental needs allows you to make confident, informed decisions about their care.

So, if you’re a parent who needs to schedule your toddler’s first dental visit or a senior looking for tooth replacement, we’d love to help you. And for all the ages and stages of life in between, we’ll be there for your family, providing individualized dental care with compassion and honesty.

Our goal? We want to be the kind of dental office you want to visit, not have to visit, and our five-star Google rating is proof that we deliver.

At SJ Dental, we simplify your family’s life by providing quality dentistry for all ages, from 2 to 102. Everyone is welcome, and our age-appropriate dental care ensures healthy smiles at every stage of life.

Your dental journey with us might begin with your toddler s first gentle cleaning and cavity prevention, setting the foundation for a lifetime of healthy habits. As your children grow, we re here for everything from protective sealants to wisdom teeth management.

From routine family dentistry and teeth whitening to complex procedures like root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, and full mouth rehabilitation with veneers, we ve designed our practice to grow with your family s changing needs.

We love the human side of dentistry and interacting with our patients, and that will never change. However, we value the role that technology plays in improving your experience and the level of care we can provide.
