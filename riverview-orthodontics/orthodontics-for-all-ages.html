Orthodontics Designed for Kids

As a parent in Tuscaloosa, we know you want nothing but the best for your child, and that absolutely includes a healthy smile.

Here at RiverView Orthodontics, we totally get your concerns, and we re here to provide outstanding orthodontic care for your little ones.

Dr. Mike Upton and the American Association of Orthodontists recommend that children in Tuscaloosa receive their first orthodontic evaluation around the age of seven. However, there s no minimum age for orthodontic treatment. Relax and rest assured we ll treat your children like our own, offering them the exceptional care they deserve.

Finding the Perfect Time for Treatment

We know there s no one-size-fits-all for orthodontic treatment for kids. That s why we typically begin orthodontic treatment for Tuscaloosa children when they’re between the ages of 9 and 14.

Starting early can make corrections easier and smoother. Dr. Upton will take a good look at your child s unique needs and provide expert guidance on the best time to begin treatment. It s a strategic approach that helps us to:

  • Guide jaw growth
  • Minimize the risk of front teeth trauma
  • Correct any not-so-healthy oral habits
  • Boost your child s appearance and self-esteem
  • Nudge permanent teeth into their ideal positions
  • Create a harmonious smile that fits their face perfectly

Schedule Your Child s Orthodontic Check-Up by Age 7

By the time your child reaches the age of seven, their teeth have developed enough for Dr. Upton to spot any subtle issues with jaw growth and emerging teeth, even if some baby teeth are still hanging around. During this check-up, we might find that your child s bite is totally ok and your child doesn’t need braces at the moment.

On the other hand, Dr. Upton might spot a developing problem and recommend keeping an eye on your child s growth and development.

We ll start treatment at the right time for your little one if treatment is necessary. Early intervention can often tackle those potential problems much better at this stage.

Early Treatment for a Brighter Future

Early orthodontic treatment can be a game-changer. It has the potential to prevent or intercept more serious issues from rearing up, which means that future treatment will be shorter and less complicated.

Dr. Upton can work wonders during this early stage, and trust us, once the face and jaws have finished growing, it becomes a bit trickier.

Dr. Upton, our experienced orthodontist, has a keen eye for spotting spacing issues, crowding, bite problems, and alignment concerns in teenagers. Plus, your teen s bone and jaw structure have developed enough to kickstart orthodontic treatment. It s a great time to take action.
