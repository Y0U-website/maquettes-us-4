What does orthodontic treatment cost in Tustin?

Typical Orange County ranges: Invisalign runs about $3,500 to $7,000 and braces about $3,000 to $7,500 , depending on how far your teeth need to move and how long treatment takes.

Many PPO dental plans include an orthodontic benefit, often covering a portion for patients under 18 and sometimes for adults.

At your consultation you get one itemized number for your case, your insurance verified, and monthly payment options laid out, so the decision gets made with real figures.
