Your First Visit

Your First Visit at Brentwood Dental Center

Take a few minutes to walk through your first visit with Dr. Nelson and see exactly what to expect before, during, and after your appointment.

New Patient Appointment Overview

Your first visit is designed to be comprehensive and comfortable, typically lasting about 90 minutes for a full exam and cleaning. During this time, our team evaluates your overall oral health and goals while using advanced digital imaging and technology to assess your condition and plan your care.

If you have an urgent dental concern, same-day appointments are often available to provide prompt care.

Before Your Appointment

New Patient Paperwork

If you’re unable to complete the forms in advance, plan to arrive about 15 minutes early so the team can assist you. Previous Records X-rays or records from prior dental offices can be emailed to info@brentwooddentaltn.com

Sending these records ahead of time helps ensure a smooth first visit.

Appointment Reminders

You’ll receive text reminders starting two weeks before your appointment. Our team is happy to answer questions or make scheduling adjustments via text or phone.

01. Arrival and Check in

From the moment you arrive, our front office team works to make you feel welcomed and comfortable.

Greeting and Check-In

Patients are welcomed at the front desk and guided through check-in.

Medical & Dental History Review

Our clinical team reviews your health history, medications, and past dental experiences.

Patient Priorities

We want to hear about your goals, concerns, or any dental sensitivities so we can customize your care.

02. Records & Digital Imaging

We use the latest technology to provide precise, effective care.

Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays give us a clear view of your teeth and surrounding bone, allowing for precise evaluation and thoughtful care planning.

Intraoral Scan

We’ll use an intraoral scanner to create a digital model of your teeth, helping you see what we see and better understand your treatment options.

03. Gum Health & Cleaning

Gum health is the foundation of a healthy smile. Your hygienist will perform a detailed evaluation to check for gingivitis or more advanced periodontal disease.

Cleaning

Whenever possible, the cleaning is completed during your visit. If additional or advanced care is needed, the team will guide you through the next steps and schedule follow-up visits as necessary.

Home-Care Instructions

Personalized guidance on brushing, flossing, and tools supports long-term oral health and confidence.

04. Comprehensive Exam & Treatment Planning

Dr. Nelson carefully reviews your findings with you and outlines the next steps for your care.

Thorough Evaluation

Healthy areas and any concerns are reviewed in detail, so you understand exactly what’s happening and why.

Personalized Treatment Options

Recommendations are thoughtfully presented based on your health needs, goals, and comfort level.

Collaborative Care Planning

Questions are encouraged, and treatment is prioritized together to ensure you feel informed and confident in every decision.

05. Financial Review

We want patients to feel confident and informed about costs:

Insurance Verification

Benefits are confirmed and out-of-pocket estimates are provided.

Payment Options

Monthly financing is available through third-party providers, along with structured payment plans for treatments such as Invisalign.

In-Office Membership

Offers savings and flexibility for patients without insurance.

06. Next Steps

Before you leave, you’ll have a clear understanding of what comes next and how we’ll support you moving forward.

Follow-Up Guidance:

Our team will help schedule future visits and review any next steps needed to maintain your oral health.

Ongoing Support

Following your visit, our team is here to support you through every step of your treatment, answering questions and guiding you so you feel comfortable and confident.

Scheduling and Cancellations

Cancellation Policy

If you need to reschedule, we kindly request at least 48 hours’ notice whenever possible, except in the case of illness or emergency. This allows us to offer that time to another patient in need of care.

Call List

If your preferred day or time isn’t available, you’re welcome to join our call list. We review it daily and often move patients up when openings become available.
