Meet Dr. Huff

Kevin D. Huff, D.D.S.

Dr. Huff was born in Massillon, Ohio, and was the Valedictorian of his class at Tuslaw High School. Upon graduating high school, Dr. Huff attended Miami University in Oxford, Ohio, where he received a Bachelor of Arts degree in Zoology, graduating Cum Laude . In 1996, Dr. Huff earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry.

Dr. Huff has completely rigorous training required to become a Diplomate of the American Board of Orofacial Pain and now can be referred to as an Orofacial Pain Specialist and General Dentist.

Dr. Huff has also been awarded the designations of Fellow and Master from the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD) . Continuing on his journey for excellence and proven dedication to the service of his profession and to the community, Dr. Huff was one of the youngest dentists in the world to be awarded the highest honor bestowed by the Academy of General Dentistry: the Lifetime Learning and Service Recognition award. Mastership in the AGD reflects a dentist’s ongoing commitment to providing quality care through continuing education.

Dr. Huff is married and has one daughter. The Huffs are active in their community and give back by actively participating in their local church, various fund-raising athletic events, and Dr. Huff provides reduced-fee services for financially challenged people through the Ohio OPTIONS Program.

My Personal Philosophy

My goal is to educate my patients and colleagues about current concepts in dental medicine and to provide the best ethical advice and care possible for their individual situations. I intend to uphold honesty, integrity, and professionalism as key components of my overall persona and service to humanity.

The ability to reach this goal comes from a solid and caring family and wife who support countless hours of out-of-house labor and continuing education. Understanding that my skills as an educator, as a scientist, and as a clinician are God-given gifts, I believe that it is my mission to uphold the ethical ideals of the profession of dentistry and to benefit the patients who it serves.
