Preventative Oral Health

Gum Disease Treatment

We can help treat gum disease in patients through a variety of deep cleanings, called scaling and root planning, as well as per protect trays that help minimize the amount of time spent in an office visit.

Restorative Services

In the event that you should need any restoration of one or more of your teeth, Simply Good Dentistry offers fillings, crowns and bridges, partial dental repairs, dentures, root canals, and extractions to ease any pain and as preventative care.

Specialty Services

We can provide and fit you with sleep apnea appliances, orthodontic treatments, TMJ treatments, and even tooth bleaching.
