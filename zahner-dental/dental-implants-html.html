What Are Dental Implants?

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are the only replacement teeth that are embedded directly into your jawbone, providing unique benefits over alternative restorations, such as dentures or dental bridges. As a result, many experts consider them the ideal way to rebuild your smile’s appearance and functionality after tooth loss.

Continue reading to learn more about the many advantages of choosing this treatment, and please don’t hesitate to contact us for additional information!

Day-to-Day Benefits

Gaps in your grin can negatively impact your daily quality of life by altering your ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently. Getting dental implants can help by:

  • Increasing your chewing power . The support rod of your implant eventually fuses with your jawbone, providing added strength and stability that can return up to 90 % of your original bite force, in some cases.
  • Improving enunciation. Spaces in your smile can cause a lisp, slur, or other changes to your speech patterns that can be overcome once these prosthetics are placed.
  • Boosting your confidence. We provide fully customized restorations designed to match your natural grin to help you look and feel like the most attractive version of yourself.

Health Benefits

In addition to enhancing your daily experience, getting dental implants can also be advantageous to your oral and overall health in the following ways:

  • Preserving jawbone density. The roots of your teeth strengthen your jawbone by triggering new bone growth with each bite down, so it begins to thin when they go missing. Implants are the only replacement teeth that include placing support rods to fulfill this function.
  • Maintaining your oral health. You’re less likely to develop oral issues, like gum disease, because it’s easier to brush and floss these sturdy standalone structures than other options, like dentures and bridges.
  • Prevent dental drifting. Any remaining natural teeth are prone to shifting out of position to fill empty spaces, damaging your enamel and contributing to TMJ disorders and jaw pain, which can be avoided by placing implants.

Long-Term Benefits

It can take several months to recover after dental implant surgery in Ellington because that’s how long it takes your jawbone to fuse with the support poles of your new teeth. Once this process is complete, your implants are firmly anchored, so you can enjoy additional long-term benefits, such as:

  • High success rate. This procedure has an incredibly high success rate of more than 90 %, meaning you’re not likely to experience failure if you follow our team’s post-op directions carefully.
  • Resilience . Implants include a support pole made from biocompatible materials that fuse with your jawbone for added strength and stability, and can last 20+ years if cared for correctly.
  • Reduced expenses. Although this procedure may cost more initially than other treatments, there’s no need to invest in additional accessories like special cleaning solutions, pastes, or adhesives. Plus, implants are less likely to require repairs or replacement down the road, saving you money over time.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Do you have good oral and overall health? If so, there’s a good chance that you’re a good candidate for dental implants! During your consultation, we will take the necessary X-rays, complete a visual exam, and learn about your daily routine to determine if that’s the case. If it is, then we will move on to the next step in the process: creating your custom treatment plan.

Important reminder: If you aren’t a candidate due to an active infection in your gums, a severely decayed tooth, or insufficient jawbone density, don’t get discouraged. After all, we can often provide the necessary preliminary care at our office, like gum disease treatment. So, having a complete smile, a strong bite, and renewed confidence won’t be out of reach for long.

Missing One Tooth

If you are missing only one tooth, you’ll be happy to hear that a traditional bridge is no longer the only option. In fact, we’ve helped countless patients in the same position restore their smiles with dental implants! The first step is placing a biocompatible implant post into your jawbone. Then, after your jaw has healed, a custom-made dental crown is secured in place with an abutment, effectively restoring the entire structure of your missing tooth .

Missing Multiple Teeth

For years, patients who were missing multiple teeth consecutively had one choice: a dental bridge. Although effective, this restorative solution requires the teeth surrounding the gap to be altered, even if they are healthy. The good news is that isn’t the case with implant bridges! If we decide together that this is the best tooth-replacement solution for you, then two implant posts are inserted into the jaw to anchor your dental bridge. Since it is carefully crafted to match the appearance of your remaining teeth, it will blend in seamlessly when you smile.

Missing All Teeth

Are you interested in upgrading from dentures to dental implants? If so, let us know! If we determine that you’re a good candidate for this state-of-the-art tooth-replacement solution, then you never need to worry about any slipping or irritation again. We can say this with confidence because 4 to 6 posts are used to secure the restoration in place, replacing the need for suction or an adhesive to restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Are Dental Implants Worth the Investment?

Patients who research the cost of various tooth replacement options may believe that dental implants in Ellington are not worth the investment given their higher upfront cost compared to bridges or dentures.

However, over the long-term, you may actually end up saving money by choosing dental implants over traditional dental prosthetics. With proper care, dental implants can restore your smile for decades or even the rest of your life! In contrast, you will likely have to replace a bridge or denture once or more over the years.

Of course, dental implants also offer superior stability and appearance over more traditional dental prosthetics by replacing both the missing tooth and tooth root structure.

So, if you are considering replacing missing teeth and wondering if dental implants are worth the investment, the answer is a resounding “yes!”

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

It’s no secret that rebuilding your smile with dental implants requires an investment of your valuable time and money. However, it’s well worth it because they are lifelike, can prevent bone loss, and are long-lasting. To keep yours in pristine condition throughout the years, we recommend:

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

All too often, patients let their oral hygiene regimen take a back seat when they find out that dental implants cannot decay. However, peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease) is one of the leading causes of dental implant failure. So, if you want yours to last, you need to incorporate healthy habits into your daily routine, like brushing, flossing, and rinsing with mouthwash.

Tip: Replace your toothbrush (or toothbrush head) every 3-4 months!

Eat a Healthy Diet

One of the many benefits of dental implants is that they can restore the strength of your bite, allowing you to enjoy a wide variety of foods again. However, it’s important that you are still selective about what you eat. After all, hard pretzels, tortilla chips, and other crunchy foods can cause your restoration to break. Not only that, but sticky sweets, like caramel-filled candies, can pull them out of place. So, it’s best to eat mostly healthy, well-balanced meals that are filled with more smile-friendly foods (i.e., steamed carrots, plain yogurt, fresh bananas).

Break Bad Habits

Of course, it’s almost important to avoid harmful habits that jeopardize the condition of your dental implant, like smoking and chewing on ice cubes. You should also avoid using your teeth as tools, whether it’s to open plastic wrapping or trim your nails. This is important because, although it may seem harmless, the pressure that’s exerted can cause your restoration to chip or crack.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Avoiding the harmful habits that we mentioned above is a great first step. To further protect your dental implants, we also recommend wearing a mouthguard when you’re active. That way, your teeth, gums, and restorations are protected from a bad fall.

Important reminder: If you grind or clench your teeth at night, then you should schedule an appointment at our office so we can make a custom nightguard for you.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Whether you’re only missing one tooth or your smile is now comprised entirely of dental implants, you need to schedule an appointment at our office every six months. This is an important part of dental implant care in Ellington because routine visits ensure that oral cancer, gum disease, and other common health conditions are detected (and treated!) in the early stages. Plus, the cleaning portion of these appointments allows us to remove plaque and tartar before they have a chance to wreak havoc on your smile.

Can I take dental implants out?

Your jawbone fuses to your implant posts through a process called osseointegration. Therefore, you can’t take dental implants out on your own. Only a trained and qualified dental professional can remove them, which is only done if they fail. Some dentures can be taken out for cleaning; however, the implants will remain in place.
