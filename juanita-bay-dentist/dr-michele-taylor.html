Dr. Michele Taylor

  • She continued her education at Pacific Lutheran University and graduated with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology and a double minor in Chemistry and Chinese studies.
  • From 2000 to 2004, she attended Creighton University School of Dentistry in Omaha, Nebraska, and graduated with special recognition in general dentistry and periodontology.
  • Dr. Taylor practices comprehensive and evidence- based dentistry.
  • She is passionate about staying current with the ever-changing field of dentistry.
  • She is also a graduate of the prestigious Kois Center.
  • Dr. Taylor enjoys continuing education to advance her skills and services to meet all of her patients’ needs.

In the PNW
