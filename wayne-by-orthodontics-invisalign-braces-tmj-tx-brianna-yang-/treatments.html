Services We Offer for TMD Patients:

At BY Orthodontics, we take a comprehensive, evidence-based approach to diagnosing and treating Temporomandibular Joint Disorder (TMD). Dr. Brianna Yang and Dr. Normand Boucher combines advanced technology with collaborative care to address the root causes of your symptoms and support long-term relief.

  • Comprehensive TMD Evaluations: We perform detailed assessments of your jaw joint using advanced Cone Beam CT (CBCT) imaging. This 3D technology provides high-resolution views of the TMJ and surrounding structures, allowing for accurate diagnosis and informed treatment planning.
  • Customized Splint Therapy: Our custom-made bite splints are designed to promote jaw relaxation, reduce clenching or grinding, and redistribute pressure on the TMJ. These appliances are tailored to your unique anatomy and serve as an effective, non-invasive option for managing TMD symptoms.
  • Orthodontic Treatment for TMD Relief: Bite misalignment and jaw imbalance often contribute to TMD. Through precise orthodontic treatment, we can correct these issues to reduce joint stress, relieve pain, and restore healthy jaw function.
  • Botox Therapy for TMJ Disorders: For patients with muscle-related TMD, Botox injections may be used to relax overactive jaw muscles, ease tension, and reduce chronic pain. This minimally invasive treatment can offer effective relief as part of a broader care strategy.
  • Collaborative Care with Trusted Specialists: TMD treatment often benefits from a team-based approach. We coordinate with experienced professionals—including physical therapists, ENTs, and oral surgeons—to ensure every aspect of your condition is addressed.
  • Research-Based, Modern Care: Dr. Yang and Dr. Boucher are actively involved in ongoing TMD research and incorporate the latest clinical advancements into your treatment. You can feel confident knowing your care is guided by the most current science and standards in the field.

AIRWAY/SLEEP APNEA

Orthodontics with a Focus on Airway Health

At BY Orthodontics, we prioritize not only the aesthetics and function of your smile but also your airway health. Dr. Brianna Yang and Dr. Normand Boucher use advanced Cone Beam CT (CBCT) imaging to assess your airway in 3D, identifying potential obstructions or issues that may affect breathing, sleep, and overall wellness.

We offer airway-friendly treatments, such as palatal expansion, to improve airflow and support long-term health. If needed, we collaborate with specialists like ENTs and sleep medicine doctors to ensure personalized care.

Breathe better with a healthy foundation— if you or your child experiences mouth breathing, snoring, poor sleep, or has a history of narrow jaw structure, schedule a consultation with Dr. Yang or Dr. Boucher. We’re here to provide expert orthodontic care that puts your airway health first.

The Connection Between Oral Structure and Airway Health

Airway-Focused Treatment Options

  • Palatal Expansion for Children and Adults: A narrow palate can restrict nasal airflow. Palatal expanders gently widen the upper jaw, improving nasal breathing and creating more room for proper tooth alignment. While expansion is most commonly done in children, adults can also benefit from carefully planned expansion treatments.
  • CBCT-Based Airway Analysis: Our use of CBCT imaging provides a more complete view of the airway space, allowing for more accurate diagnosis and better-informed treatment planning.
  • Collaborative, Multi-Specialist Care: When needed, we work closely with ENTs, myofunctional therapists, sleep specialists, and other medical professionals to ensure every patient receives fully integrated care that addresses both orthodontic and airway-related concerns.
  • Treatment That Supports Long-Term Health: By focusing on airway-friendly treatments—rather than simply straightening teeth—we help support better breathing, healthier sleep, and improved long-term outcomes.
