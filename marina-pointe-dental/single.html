Replace Your Missing Tooth With a Permanent Solution

A single dental implant is the only tooth replacement that restores function, preserves jawbone, and stands on its own without altering the teeth on either side. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone where the tooth was lost, and a custom crown attaches on top. The result looks and functions like a natural tooth. At Marina Pointe Dental, we place single-tooth implants for Panama City, FL patients who have lost a tooth to decay, injury, or extraction.

According to the CDC’s National Center for Health Statistics , more than 13 % of adults 65 and older have lost all their permanent teeth, underscoring how critical early intervention is when a single tooth is lost. Replacing a missing tooth before bone loss progresses gives the best long-term outcome. Our team reviews your bone density with digital X-rays and walks you through your options and the full treatment timeline before anything is scheduled.

Benefits of Single Tooth Dental Implants

Stands Alone Without Altering Adjacent Teeth Unlike a dental bridge, a single implant doesn’t require reshaping the healthy teeth on either side. Each neighboring tooth stays completely intact throughout the process.

Preserves Jawbone Where the Tooth Was Lost The titanium post acts as a tooth root, stimulating the bone and preventing the density loss that begins as soon as a tooth goes missing.

Looks, Feels, and Functions Like a Natural Tooth The custom crown is matched to your surrounding teeth in shape, size, and color. Most patients find no one can tell which tooth was replaced.

Your Single Tooth Implant Process, Step by Step

Every dental implant treatment begins with a thorough evaluation and discussion of your goals. Our step-by-step approach ensures you feel informed and confident throughout the entire process at our Panama City dental office.

Initial Consultation and Evaluation

During your first visit, we examine the area where your tooth is missing, take digital X-rays to evaluate bone density, and discuss your treatment options. This consultation allows us to determine if you’re a good candidate for a dental implant and explain what results you can expect.

Implant Placement Surgery

The titanium implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone, where it will serve as the root for your new tooth. We use local anesthesia to ensure your comfort during the procedure. Over the following months, the implant fuses with your bone through a process called osseointegration.

Healing and Osseointegration

During the healing period, which typically lasts three to six months, the implant bonds securely with your jawbone. We may provide a temporary restoration so you won’t have a gap in your smile. We monitor your progress with follow-up appointments to ensure proper healing.

Crown Placement

Once your implant has fully integrated, we attach an abutment and place your custom-made porcelain crown. The crown is designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size, creating a seamless, beautiful result. We make precise adjustments to ensure a comfortable bite.

Why Choose a Dental Implant Over Other Options

While bridges and partial dentures can replace missing teeth, single tooth implants offer unique advantages that make them the preferred choice for many patients. During your consultation, we’ll help you understand why an implant may be the best solution for your situation.

Single tooth implants are ideal for patients with good overall health and sufficient jawbone density to support the implant. If you’ve experienced bone loss, we may recommend bone grafting before implant placement to ensure a successful outcome.
