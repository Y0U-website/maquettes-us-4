Benefits of Implant Overdentures

Stable, Secure Foundation Implant overdentures snap onto titanium implants anchored in your jawbone, eliminating the slip and instability of traditional dentures. Your bite force is supported by the implants, not your gums.

Preserved Jawbone Health Dental implants stimulate your jawbone the same way natural tooth roots do, preventing the bone loss that occurs under traditional dentures and helping maintain your facial structure over time.

Eat, Speak, and Live Freely Patients with overdentures report significant improvements in their ability to eat a full range of foods, speak clearly, and engage socially — without the anxiety of dentures that shift or fall out at the wrong moment.

Your Overdenture Treatment Journey

Every overdenture treatment begins with understanding your goals and evaluating your oral health. Our step-by-step approach ensures you feel informed and confident throughout the entire process at our Panama City dental office.

Initial Consultation and Evaluation

During your first visit, we discuss your concerns with your current dentures, examine your jawbone and gum tissue, and take digital X-rays to evaluate bone density. This consultation allows us to determine if you’re a candidate for overdentures and explain what results you can expect.

Implant Placement Planning

We create a detailed treatment plan outlining how many implants you’ll need, where they’ll be placed, and the timeline for your procedure. Most overdentures require two to four implants per arch. We discuss all costs upfront and review financing options to make treatment accessible.

Implant Surgery and Healing

The dental implants are surgically placed into your jawbone, where they will fuse with the bone over the following months. During the healing period, you may wear a temporary denture. We monitor your progress closely and ensure you remain comfortable throughout the process.

Final Overdenture Attachment

Once your implants have fully integrated with your jawbone, we attach special connectors and fit your custom overdenture as a part of our restorative treatment . We make precise adjustments to ensure a secure, comfortable fit and provide detailed instructions for caring for your new implant-supported dentures.
