Meet Dr. Bhavya Paranthaman

Dr. Bhavya Paranthaman, who goes by Dr. P in the office, brings warmth and clinical excellence to every patient interaction. Together with her husband, Dr. Alejandro Nieves, she purchased Marina Pointe Dental with a vision to create a practice where patients feel like family. Her approach to dentistry combines technical skill with genuine compassion, creating a calm and relaxing environment where even the most anxious patients feel at ease.

Outside the office, Dr. Paranthaman and Dr. Nieves have embraced life in the Florida Panhandle and are committed to serving the Panama City community for years to come. To schedule an appointment with Dr. Paranthaman , contact our office — or meet the full team to learn more about who you’ll be working with.

We’re proud to share that Dr. Paranthaman is a participating provider in the Reserve Health Readiness Program (RHRP), administered through QTC: a program designed to keep our Reserve and National Guard service members healthy and ready to serve.

Through this partnership with the government, our office helps provide essential dental care to eligible military personnel. This includes comprehensive exams, digital X-rays, fillings, extractions, root canals, and other treatment needed to ensure each service member meets dental readiness requirements. Simply put, we help take care of dental issues before they become bigger problems so our Reserve members can stay focused, prepared, and ready if they are called to duty.

It’s truly an honor for Dr. Paranthaman and our team to support the men and women who serve our country. Providing care through RHRP is just one of the ways we give back to those who give so much.
