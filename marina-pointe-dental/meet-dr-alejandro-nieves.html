Meet Dr. Alejandro Nieves

Dr. Alejandro Nieves is a dedicated dentist who brings both clinical excellence and genuine compassion to every patient interaction. Together with his wife, Dr. Bhavya Paranthaman, he purchased Marina Pointe Dental to build a practice centered on patient relationships, modern techniques, and personalized care. As a bilingual provider fluent in Spanish, Dr. Nieves serves the diverse Panama City community with comfort and clarity, ensuring every patient feels heard and understood.

Outside the office, Dr. Nieves enjoys the Florida Panhandle lifestyle and is committed to making Panama City his long-term home. His practice philosophy reflects a simple truth: dentistry is about people, not just teeth. He understands that behind every smile is a person with unique concerns, goals, and circumstances — and he tailors his care accordingly.
