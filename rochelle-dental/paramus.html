OUR SERVICES

Preventive Dentistry

Our preventive dentistry services focus on ensuring optimal dental health for our patients, young and old. Some of our preventive dental services include dental cleaning, X-rays, sealants, oral cancer screening, and custom mouthguards. Our dental hygienist can handle your dental treatment for the whole family.

Invisalign

We also offer Invisalign clear aligners for patients who prefer a discreet, less bulky alternative to traditional braces. The dentist will take impressions of your teeth before sending them to a lab to manufacture your aligners. Invisalign aligners are super comfortable, highly effective, and barely noticeable.

Veneers

Patients with tooth imperfections like chips, cracks, and discoloration will greatly benefit from veneers. We attach these wafer-thin, tooth-colored shells to the front surface of your teeth to hide these imperfections. The dental procedure is simple, non-invasive, and only takes a few minutes to attach veneers to your teeth. We also provide most cosmetic dentistry treatments such as teeth whitening or dental makeovers.
