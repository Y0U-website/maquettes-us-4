Orthodontic - Clear Aligner

At Southview Dental Care, Dr. Caitlin Spacek offers SureSmile orthodontics as a discreet alternative to metal braces! SureSmile allows our dentist to help patients align their teeth for healthier, more confident smiles. With this trusted clear aligner system, our dentist provides customized treatment plans.

How Does SureSmile Work? Using advanced digital imaging technology, Dr. Caitlin will take precise scans of your mouth to create a series of custom clear aligners. Each set of aligners is designed to be worn for about two weeks before switching to the next, gradually guiding your teeth into proper alignment. Throughout your treatment, you’ll visit our Moorhead, MN dental office for periodic check-ins, allowing our SureSmile dentist to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments—ensuring your smile transforms exactly as planned.
