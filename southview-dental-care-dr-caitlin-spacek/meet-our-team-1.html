Meet Dr. Caitlin Spacek

When it came time to decide where to establish our roots, my husband and I knew that the Fargo-Moorhead area was the perfect place to call home. We are excited to serve the community where my husband grew up and that I fell in love with during my time spent at NDSU.

Speaking of family, we have one fur baby named Fenrir, who brings us endless joy and laughter. We hope to expand our family in the near future and share our love with more little ones. At Southview Dental Care, my mission is to provide exceptional dental care that goes beyond just treating teeth. I believe in building long-lasting relationships with my patients, based on trust, compassion, and open communication. I strive to create a comfortable and welcoming environment where you can feel at ease during your dental visits.

I am committed to staying at the forefront of dental advancements and techniques, ensuring that you receive the highest quality of care. Whether you need routine check-ups, cosmetic dentistry, or more complex procedures, I am dedicated to tailoring treatments to meet your unique needs and goals.

Thank you for considering Southview Dental Care as your dental home. I look forward to meeting you, getting to know you, and being a part of your journey towards optimal oral health.

Please don’t hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or would like to schedule an appointment. Let’s embark on this dental journey together!
