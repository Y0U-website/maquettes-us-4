IMPLANT & DENTURE

At Southview Dental Care , Dr. Caitlin Spacek restores confidence and function with beautiful, long-lasting dental implants . Whether you’re missing a single tooth or a full set, we provide personalized implant treatments.

Single Dental Implant Looking for a permanent fix for missing teeth? Dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function just like the real thing! Each implant uses a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to hold a custom-made crown securely in place. After healing, your new tooth feels completely natural—so you can eat your favorite foods, brush and floss normally, and smile with confidence.

Overdentures Overdentures offer a revolutionary solution for those seeking an improvement from traditional dentures. These are supported by dental implants or natural teeth, ensuring a secure fit that enhances your ability to chew and speak confidently. Designed to be removable, they provide easy cleaning while promoting better oral health.

Implant-Retained Dentures Implant-retained dentures are removable full or partial dentures that attach securely to permanent dental implants. This advanced design provides a stable, comfortable fit and helps preserve jawbone health making them a superior alternative to traditional dentures. Like conventional dentures, they are removable and require daily cleaning and care.

Partial Dentures We offer partial dentures to help fill in gaps in your smile and support your remaining natural teeth. Custom-fitted and removable, they make eating, speaking, and smiling easier and more comfortable. With simple daily care, partial dentures can help keep your mouth healthy and your smile confident.
