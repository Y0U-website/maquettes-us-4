Compatibility with browsers and assistive technology

Our goal is to ensure that our website is compatible with browsers and assistive technologies to be widely accessible to all users.

Technical specifications

The accessibility of Malibu Dentistry’s website relies on the following technologies to work with the particular combination of web browser and any assistive technologies or plugins installed on your computer:

These technologies are relied upon for conformance with the accessibility standards used.

  • HTML
  • JavaScript

Third Party Applications

Our website may use third-party add-ons or “plugins” for certain functions, such as Google Maps, social media feeds, etc. These may not work or may not work the same for every user and/or every type of disability. We do not have control over the structure of these plugins, and are unable to modify them at all or to the extent that would accommodate every user of our website, and are not responsible for those elements that we do not control.

Limitations and alternatives

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility of Malibu Dentistry’s website, there may be some limitations. Please contact us if you observe an issue.

Assessment approach

Malibu Dentistry assesses the accessibility of its website through self-evaluation and external auditing.

We are here for you

If you are experiencing difficulties with any content on our website because of a disability, or if you require assistance with any part of our site because of your particular disability, please contact us and let us know. We will be happy to assist.

By committing to this policy, Malibu Dentistry demonstrates our dedication to making our website accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We strive to adhere to the accepted guidelines and standards for accessibility and usability but acknowledge that it is not always possible to do so in all areas of the website. We continue to seek out solutions that will bring all areas of the site up to the same level of overall web accessibility.

At Malibu Dentistry, we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to ensure we provide equal access to all users.
