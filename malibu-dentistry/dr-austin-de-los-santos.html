Meet Dr. Austin

Dr. Austin is not just an expert dentist in Malibu, CA; he’s a Southern California native with a rich dental heritage. As a third-generation dentist, Dr. Austin brings a unique blend of traditional values and cutting-edge expertise to Malibu Dentistry. His journey in dentistry began long before his formal education, shaping him into the experienced Malibu dentist he is today.

Education and Early Passion

Dr. Austin graduated from the prestigious University of Southern California Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry, where he honed his skills in cosmetic, biomimetic, and implant dentistry. His passion for dentistry sparked early in eighth grade, he studied implant dentistry and placed his first dental implants in pig jaws, winning second place in his school’s annual science fair. This early success fueled his continued pursuit of the latest technologies in implant and stem cell dentistry.

Commitment to Service and Holistic Health

Prior to dental school, Dr. Austin immersed himself in dentistry through his family’s non-profit, Global Changers. This organization, run by medical and dental professionals, provides treatment to underserved people in Boracay, Philippines. This experience shaped Dr. Austin’s commitment to service and holistic health approach.

A Holistic Approach to Dentistry

As a dentist in Malibu, CA, Dr. Austin’ passion for holistic health, keen eye for detail, and commitment to service make him a practitioner who treats beyond just teeth. He understands how oral health profoundly affects systemic health, happiness, and overall well-being. At Malibu Dentistry, our dentist believes in treating all patients with the highest level of care and love.

Beyond the Dental Chair

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Austin enjoys spending time with family and pushing his limits in fitness, breathwork, running, and cycling. His commitment to personal growth mirrors his dedication to advancing in his professional field.
