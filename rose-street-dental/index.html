The practice

Dr. Tim Wentzloff opened Rose Street Dental in Traverse City the summer of 2012, and has been welcoming patients to the longtime dental office at the corner of Rose and Front Street where Dr. Daniel Cote and Dr. Robert H. Chase formerly practiced.

Dr. Tim Wentzloff is an active member of the American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association and the Resort District Dental Society. He practices modern dentistry with a small-town family focus. He is a second-generation dentist following in the footsteps of his dad, Dr. Dale Wentzloff who practiced in Frankfort, Michigan for almost four decades.

Dr. Tim and his wife Karly live in Acme along with their daughter Hazel and son Alden. Karly Wentzloff is a Realtor with Real Estate One.

At Rose Street Dental in Traverse City, we accept new dental patients of any age. We can provide dental services for everyone in your family from infant to adult.

Starting dental care at a young age is important for the overall health of your children. Regular dental cleanings can help prevent health issues, or find challenges before they become serious. Starting a lifelong regimen of regular dental care here at Rose Street Dental will put your children on the path to a healthy lifestyle.

At Rose Street Dental, we can help the whole family with dental services. You will be able to eliminate special trips to multiple offices, and consolidate your appointments in one easy location. Our caring staff will ensure that your children receive the special attention they deserve, so you can feel comfortable here.
