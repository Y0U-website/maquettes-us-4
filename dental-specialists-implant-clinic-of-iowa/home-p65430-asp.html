Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (OMS) is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on the diagnosis and surgical treatment of a wide range of conditions affecting the mouth, jaws, face, and neck. Oral and maxillofacial surgeons are highly trained dental professionals who undergo extensive education and clinical training to perform various surgical procedures related to these areas.

Endodontics

Endodontics

Endodontics specializes in the treatment of dental pulp and the tissues surrounding the roots of teeth. The most common procedure associated with endodontics is root canal therapy. This treatment becomes necessary when the dental pulp becomes infected or inflamed, often due to deep decay, dental trauma, or other dental issues.
