Will I be able to eat normally after implant-supported dentures?

Yes. Once your implants heal fully, you’ll regain your optimal chewing strength. Patients can enjoy foods they previously avoided, including steak, nuts, apples, and salads. While you may begin with softer foods during the healing phase, long-term results allow for a far more natural eating experience.

Are implant-supported dentures removable?

Implant-based dentures can be either removable or fixed. Implant-retained dentures (overdentures) “snap” into dental implants, and you can remove them for cleaning. Implant-supported dentures are permanently secured in the mouth and only removed by a dentist. The right option depends on your preferences, bone structure, and clinical needs.

Does age make me ineligible?

Age alone does not disqualify you. Implant-supported dentures are often ideal for older adults seeking better stability than traditional dentures provide. What matters most is overall health and sufficient bone support. A comprehensive evaluation with our experienced dentist will help determine your candidacy.
