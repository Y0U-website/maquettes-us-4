I am 70—do I qualify for dental implants?

Yes, you can still qualify for dental implants at age 70. Unlike some treatments with an upper age limit, dental implants do not. Many patients in their 70s, 80s, and even 90s successfully receive them.

The key factors are overall health, bone density, and gum condition, not age alone. While there may be a lower age limit (implants aren’t recommended for younger patients whose jaws are still developing), older adults are often excellent candidates. A consultation and exam will determine if implants are right for you.
