Can smokers get dental implants?

Tobacco contains harmful chemicals that compromise your immunity and healing ability. Smoking increases the risk of implant failure, but it doesn’t make you ineligible for the procedure. To become eligible, your dentist will recommend that you stop smoking before and after surgery to improve your implant’s success rate.

What happens if I have periodontal disease?

Active gum disease must be treated before placing an implant. Periodontal disease damages the bone and supporting tissues needed for implant stability. At Wes Park Family Dentistry, we evaluate and manage gum health first to create a solid foundation for long-term success.

Can I get an implant if I have bone loss?

Your dental implant requires a healthy jawbone to provide the stability it needs to thrive. But what if you have lost your jawbone because of gum disease or untreated tooth loss? You can become eligible for implants with bone grafting. This procedure creates a solid foundation for healthy, long-lasting implants.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last decades, and often a lifetime. However, the lifespan of your implants depends on your maintenance and grinding habits. To maximize the longevity of your implants, brush your teeth diligently and maintain routine dental visits. If you grind your teeth at night, consider a custom nightguard to keep your implants healthy and strong.

Can dental implants be placed immediately after extraction?

In select cases, yes. Immediate implant placement may be possible if the extraction site is infection-free and has sufficient bone. Not every situation qualifies, but when conditions are favorable, this approach can shorten overall treatment time.

Will dental insurance cover dental implants?

It depends on your plan. Some procedures cover a part of the implant cost, while others cover parts of the procedure, such as the crown or extraction. We are happy to verify your coverage before your treatment begins.
