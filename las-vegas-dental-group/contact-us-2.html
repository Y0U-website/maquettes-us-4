Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need to visit a dentist?

We typically recommend patients schedule a visit every 6 months for a check and cleaning. If you experiencing any dental pain or discomfort we recommend scheduling a visit to Las Vegas Dental Group today so we can to to have that evaluated?

Why do I need dental exams?

Scheduling regular dental exams and cleanings as recommended by your Las Vegas Dental Group dentist is essential to maintaining optimal dental health. Regular checkups help your dentist identify early signs of dental decay and or periodontial disease and recommend treatment top prevent unnecessary pain and expense.

How often should I visit my dentist?

For our patients in good dental health we typically recommend scheduling every six month. This can vary from person to person depending on your dental health and the treatment prescribed by your Las vegas Dental Group dentist.
