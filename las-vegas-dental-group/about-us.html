Meet The Las Vegas Dental Group Dentists and Hygienists

The LVDG team of highly educated, experienced, and caring dental professionals is dedicated to providing you with personalized care and an exceptional dental experience in Las Vegas.

Nicholas Maffeo, DMD

Dr. Nicholas Maffeo is originally from Boston, Massachusetts, but grew up in Las Vegas, Nevada graduating from Ed W. Bishop Clark High School in 1990. He completed his undergraduate degree in Kinesiology at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas in 2003 with minors in Chemistry and Biology. Dr. Maffeo received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine in 2007 from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine. He went on to complete his general practice residency at the Malcom Randall VA Medical Center in Gainesville, Florida in 2008.

Steven Morris, DDS

Dr. Steven Morris was born and raised near Detroit, MI. After graduating from North Farmington High School, he initially moved to Raleigh, NC. Seeking a more fulfilling career path, he returned to Michigan to complete his pre-dental requirements at Oakland University while working as a dental assistant and pizzeria manager.

He attended the University of Detroit Mercy Dental School, graduating in 1995. Dr. Morris then spent a decade practicing as a dentist for the family friend who first inspired his career. He eventually relocated to Las Vegas to be close to his family.

Dr. Morris’s philosophy centers on patient care, believing in treating every patient “with kindness, courtesy and respect.” He strives to connect with patients on a personal level, explaining treatment options and answering questions, stating, “I think of them as my big, extended family. I feel honored and privileged to be able to help people in many ways and to be a part of Las Vegas Dental Group!”

In his spare time, Dr. Morris is an avid traveler, sports enthusiast (especially hockey), and enjoys spending time with his family. As a native of Detroit, he maintains a fondness for classic cars; he owns an old Corvette and serves as Vice President of the local C3 Corvette club. He is also a passionate collector of gas, oil, and automobile memorabilia.
