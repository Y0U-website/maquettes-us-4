Our Dental Implant Process at ViVE Dental Care

1. Personalized Consultation

Dr. Linda Sah will perform a detailed evaluation, including digital imaging, to determine if you’re a good candidate for implants.

2. Bone Grafting (if needed)

If bone loss is present, we may recommend bone grafting to build a strong foundation for the implant.

3. Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed in the jawbone during a minimally invasive procedure. Healing (osseointegration) takes a few months.

4. Restoration

Once the implant is secure, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached for a natural look and feel.

Need more support? Ask about dentures or crowns and bridges as part of your treatment plan.

Full-Mouth Restoration and All-on-4 Implants

Are you missing all or most of your teeth? Full-mouth reconstruction is possible with fewer implants using the All-on-4 technique — a life-changing solution that provides a fixed, full-arch prosthesis supported by just four implants.

This is ideal for patients considering full mouth reconstruction in Redlands, CA or seeking alternatives to traditional dentures.
