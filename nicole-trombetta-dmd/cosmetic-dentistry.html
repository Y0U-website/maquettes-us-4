Cosmetic Dentistry in Tucson, AZ

Contact Marana Dental Care to schedule a consultation to discover your options for a healthier, more beautiful smile today.

  • Veneers are thin pieces of porcelain that are applied to the front of a tooth often on the front teeth to perfect your smile and conceal chipped, worn or aging teeth, and fill in gaps between teeth. A thin layer of tooth enamel must be removed to make space for the veneer which is attached by an adhesive to the tooth. The adhesive is hardened or cured with a special curing light.
  • Cosmetic bonding fills or restores teeth with tooth colored material to improve the appearance of the tooth. Bonding uses a composite material to adjust size, shape or color of the tooth.
  • Microabrasion is a safe and conservative procedure to minimize the appearance of stains, yellowing and spots on the tooth enamel that often occurred in childhood. It is done by removing a very thin layer of tooth enamel. It is not a restoration. It is conservative treatment targeting deeper stains. If needed, it may be followed by bleaching to brighten and transform the smile.
  • Crowns cover the entire tooth to protect it and to improve the appearance of the tooth. They are made of ceramic or porcelain. Bridges are used to fill in missing teeth with artificial teeth. Implants are crowns that are implanted into your bone to restore lost teeth with a permanent replacement. They look and function just like your natural teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Over time teeth naturally lose their whiteness and brightness. Coffee, tea, tobacco and some childhood medications can damage darken or discolor the teeth. Bleaching brightens and whitens the teeth. It can be done in office or at home.

Adult Orthodontics

Adult orthodontics with invisible custom-made aligners gently straighten the teeth. It is a good option to improve oral health as well as giving you nice, straight teeth.
