The practice

Dr. Tang enjoyed supporting patient care for the general dentists. She understands how to be part of the team from her own experience as a general dentist in Massachusetts for three years.

With her knowledge in multiple disciplines, Dr. Tang employs evidence-based clinical studies to guide her clinical practice and keeps updated with new knowledge and technology.

While not working or volunteering at local dental society and in the community, Dr. Tang loves spending time with her family. She enjoys yoga, hiking, kayaking, traveling, learning new languages and watching movies, while dreaming of returning to her previous hobbies including painting and fencing.
