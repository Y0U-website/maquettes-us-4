How the Same-Day Crown Process Works

Thanks to modern dental technology, what used to take multiple weeks can now be done in a single appointment. Here’s what to expect during your visit:

  • Digital imaging and preparation Instead of using traditional impressions with messy putty, we take a digital scan of your tooth, creating a precise 3D model.
  • Custom crown design Using CAD/CAM technology, we design your crown with exact specifications to match your natural tooth in shape, size, and color.
  • Milling and fabrication Your custom crown is crafted right in our office using a state-of-the-art milling unit, eliminating the need to wait for a dental lab.
  • Final placement and bonding Once the crown is ready, it is securely bonded to your tooth, allowing you to leave with a fully restored smile the same day.

When Is a Traditional Crown Necessary?

While same-day crowns are an excellent solution for most patients, there are some situations where a traditional lab-made crown may still be required.

If a same-day crown isn’t the best fit for your situation, we will discuss the best alternative to ensure you receive the highest quality care.

  • Front teeth with specific aesthetic concerns Some patients prefer an extra level of customization, particularly for highly visible teeth. In these cases, a lab-created crown may provide additional shade-matching options.
  • Extensive damage or unique structural needs If a tooth is severely compromised, a traditional crown may be the better option for long-term durability.
