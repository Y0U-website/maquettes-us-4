How Do Dental Crowns Work?

When a tooth cannot support a filling, a crown is custom-made to fit over the entire portion of the tooth, showing above the gum line. Here s how it s done:

Our dental crowns in Hickory, NC service ensures a precise fit and a natural look.

  • Impressions or digital scans of your tooth are taken.
  • Skilled dental technicians craft a crown custom-made to match your natural teeth, bone, and jaw.
  • The crown is securely bonded or cemented onto your tooth.
  • Typically, crowns take two appointments, each lasting 60 to 90 minutes.

What Are Crowns Made Of?

Unlike fillings, which are placed directly in the tooth, crowns are made in a dental lab. They can be made of various materials, including:

  • Porcelain for a natural appearance
  • Zirconia, a strong dental ceramic
  • Lithium disilicate, another dental ceramic
