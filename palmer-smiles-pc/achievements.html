Dr. Divya Mudumba

For Dr. Divya Mudumba, dentistry is more than just a profession—it’s a way to connect with people, ease their worries, and help them smile with confidence. More than just a dentist, she is a dedicated healthcare professional, educator, and community advocate who believes in making dental care accessible, comfortable, and fear-free for all her patients. With over a decade of experience, she brings a global perspective and a commitment to excellence in dentistry.

Her dedication has earned her the prestigious 40 Under Forty award, recognizing her impact in both clinical care and community outreach. As a 2025 Invisalign Gold Provider and 2026 Invisalign Platinum Provider, Dr. Mudumba continues to transform smiles with advanced, patient-centered treatments that reflect her passion for innovation and compassionate care.

A Journey of Dedication and Expertise

Dr. Mudumba earned her dental degree in 2010 from the Army College of Dental Sciences in India, where she also completed a residency in Pediatric Dentistry. Driven by a passion for learning and innovation, she relocated to the U.S. and graduated from the International Dentist Program at Boston University. Today, she proudly owns and manages two successful dental practices in Massachusetts, where she provides exceptional care to patients of all ages.

Leadership in Dentistry

Beyond her clinical work, Dr. Mudumba plays an active role in shaping the future of dentistry. She serves on the boards of esteemed organizations, including the Massachusetts Dental Society, the Academy of General Dentistry, and the PACE Council. Her dedication to advancing the profession has earned her prestigious fellowships with the Pierre Fauchard Academy (USA), the International College of Dentists (India), and the International Association of Pediatric Dentistry (UK).

Committed to Community and Patient Education

Dr. Mudumba believes that dental care extends beyond the chair. She is passionate about educating patients and reducing dental anxiety, ensuring that every visit is a comfortable and empowering experience. She volunteers with Smiles for Success, helping women affected by abuse regain their confidence through dental care, and serves as a board member of the local YWCA, advocating for vulnerable populations. She also conducts oral health education programs at community centers, daycares, and preschools to promote early preventive care.

Dr. Mudumba has made notable academic contributions, publishing articles in respected journals and co-authoring a major dental textbook. Her impact on the field was celebrated when she was named a 40 Under 40 honoree by Incisal Edge, recognizing her as one of the most innovative young dental professionals in the country.

Experience Compassionate, Expert Care

Dr. Divya Mudumba is committed to delivering exceptional, patient-focused dental care while continuing to lead, educate, and inspire.
