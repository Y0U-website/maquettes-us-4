OUR TEAM

Dr. Benjamin Stein

Dr. Benjamin Stein began his dental training at the University of Minnesota in 2012. After graduating near the top of his class, he decided to continue his education by completing a General Practice Residency program (GPR) at Loyola University Medical Center, just outside of Chicago, IL. After residency, Dr. Stein began working in downtown Chicago, while continuing to teach dental residents at Loyola as a part-time faculty member. In 2017, Dr. Stein was awarded "Part-time faculty member of the year" as voted by his residents.

In 2020, Dr. Stein moved back home to Minnesota and began working in both a private dental practice as well as a sleep apnea specialty clinic. It became evident to him that treating sleep apnea could truly change the quality of life for many of his patients, and he has since made this a focus. Dr. Stein became a diplomate of the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine in 2022.

Dr. Stein takes pride in treating the patient, not just their teeth. He takes time to listen to, and truly understand their needs, often coming up with creative solutions to solve dental problems within their budget.

Dr. Evanthia Shook

Dr. Shook’s passion for dentistry is not solely clinical, as every day she aspires to be a general dentist who is focused on balancing the body, mind, and spirit. In the office, this may be coaching patient breathing, prioritizing overall health, and educating patients on their dental health and wellbeing. Outside of the office, you will find her training for another triathlon on the trail systems, trying new adventurous restaurants, and attending local wellness events.
