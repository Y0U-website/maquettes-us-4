SERVICES

We are a family-friendly, professional, full-service dental practice providing dental services to people of all ages. We offer full-service dental solutions, including preventive, cosmetic, restorative, pediatric, limited orthodontic, periodontic, and reconstructive dentistry. Our team is compassionate and caring, treating each patient according to their own personalized dental requirements. We observe the highest possible professional standards to ensure that each patient receives the individual care that he or she deserves.

Preventative Care

Avoid cavities, gum disease, enamel wear, and more with our comprehensive preventive care. We take great pride in keeping your mouth healthy and giving you the tools to do it on your own between each visit.

Snoring/Sleep Apnea Treatment

Did you know? 1 in 5 adults have mild sleep apnea, and 75 % of severe cases go undiagnosed. If you’ve been diagnosed with sleep apnea, we offer effective treatment options for mild to moderate cases using custom oral sleep appliances. These devices can help you breathe easier and sleep more restfully so you wake up feeling refreshed. Learn more about how we can help improve your sleep quality.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic Dentistry includes all dental work that improves the appearance of your teeth, gums, and alignment of your bite. We are committed to helping you maintain a smile that makes you feel your best.

Zoom Whitening

Zoom Whitening is a cosmetic dentistry procedure that involves an in-office bleaching process. This process is unique in that it enables significant removal of stains and discoloration in the shortest period of time.

Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry is the treatment and management of existing teeth and gum conditions in order to bring your teeth and gums back to their optimal health. We work hard to restore your tooth and gum health through thorough diagnosis and treatment.

Pediatric Dentistry

We provide both primary and comprehensive preventive care for your little ones in a comfortable environment. We are dedicated to the oral health of your child’s mouth throughout all stages of childhood.

Periodontal Dentistry

Periodontal dentistry focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal disease and the placement of dental implants. We work hard to ensure your optimal periodontal health.
