Meet The Team

Dr. Krishna Amin is a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist. She earned her Bachelor of Science and Dental Medicine Degree through a 7-year accelerated program at Stevens Institute of Technology and Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. Thereafter, she completed a 2-year specialty training program in Pediatric Dentistry at NYU Langone Hospitals Center in Brooklyn, NY, where she served as chief resident. She is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Dental Association.

Dr. Krishna Amin has a wealth of knowledge and experience in the field of pediatric dentistry providing the highest quality of care to her patients. Dr. Krishna Amin treats every patient as she would her own family, ensuring that each child is comfortable and leaves with a smile on their face. As a mother to a young child herself, Dr. Krishna Amin values the importance of compassion when treating pediatric patients and tailors her treatment approach to meet the specific needs and concerns of each patient.

In her spare time, Dr. Krishna Amin enjoys traveling, cooking and spending time with her family and friends. She is a native of New Jersey, currently residing locally with her husband and daughter.

Dr. Vrinda Tarneja is a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist who completed her Pediatric Dentistry training at Boston Children’s Hospital at Harvard University. She graduated summa cum laude with a Bachelor of Science from the University at Buffalo. She subsequently earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery from University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine where she received the Pediatric and Community Dentistry Award on graduation. Dr. T continued to work at a pediatric dental office in Boston for two years post residency and made the move to New Jersey after her wedding!

Dr. Tarneja has a demonstrated passion for working with kids, from tutoring middle-school students to volunteering with organizations including Give Kids a Smile Day and Special Olympics. She is an active member of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She believes in educating parents and kids in oral health care to aid in implementing changes at home. She understands each kid is unique and benefits from specialized care and attention.

Dr. Tarneja loves to travel, dance, spend time with her family and try new restaurants in her free time. She is excited to serve and connect with the Sparkles community!

Dr. Sidd spent most of her summers growing up as a youth camp counselor. She went on to apply her love of working with kids to dentistry, leading her to specialize in pediatric dentistry--the best decision she’s ever made! Dr. Sidd strives to provide the highest quality of care possible and create positive, early dental experiences for her little patients! Outside the office, you can find Dr. Sidd cooking new vegetarian recipes, trying out workout classes, and exploring running trails! She is so excited to help bring smiles and laughs to the Sparkles community!!
