Located on SLU’s medical campus in Midtown St. Louis, we house four state-of-the-art treatment clinics, as well as an imaging center that includes cone beam 3D imaging. We prepare dentists to become astute and multidisciplinary clinicians in the fields of orthodontics, endodontics, periodontics and pediatric dentistry. Our clinics, Dental Specialties of Saint Louis University, provide services for both adults and children. We offer advanced care at a lower cost than traditional practices. Services include braces, root canals, gum disease treatment and implants, and general children’s dentistry.
