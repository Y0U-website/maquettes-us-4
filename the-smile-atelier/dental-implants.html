With proper care, dental implants can last decades — in many cases, a lifetime. The titanium implant post itself, once fully integrated with the jawbone, is extremely durable and rarely needs replacement. The crown restoration on top typically lasts 10 to 15 years or longer before it may need to be replaced due to normal wear. Staying consistent with daily oral hygiene and routine checkups at The Smile Atelier is the most important factor in maximizing the longevity of both the implant and the restoration.

The surgical placement is performed under local anesthesia by a specialist, so the procedure itself should not be painful. Most patients report that post-surgical discomfort is mild to moderate — comparable to having a tooth extracted — and responds well to over-the-counter pain relief within a few days. Some swelling or minor bruising near the site is normal and typically resolves within a week. The restorative phase with Dr. Arif involves no surgery and no significant discomfort.
