Meet Dr. Rania Arif, DMD

Dr. Rania Arif

Dr. Rania Arif founded The Smile Atelier with a simple vision: to thoughtfully reimagine the dental experience from the ground up.

She created the kind of dental practice she had always wished existed, one where every detail is designed around the patient experience. After nearly a decade of practicing comprehensive and cosmetic dentistry, she set out to build a practice where exceptional care extends beyond clinical treatment. From the calming environment and modern technology to the way patients are welcomed, educated, and cared for, every element was intentionally designed to help people feel comfortable, informed, and confident.

Whether you’re visiting for a routine cleaning, restorative treatment, or a smile transformation, her goal is simple: to provide modern dentistry that’s thoughtfully delivered and centered entirely around you.

Meet Dr. Rania Arif, DMD

Education, Training & Professional Memberships

Dr. Arif is a proud member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the Massachusetts Dental Society (MDS). She has completed advanced continuing education through respected programs and organizations, including Spear Education, Aesthetic Advantage, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the American Academy of Facial Esthetics, Kois, RipeGlobal, Phelan Dental Seminars, Stribe, and additional clinical symposiums focused on aesthetic and restorative dentistry.

Her commitment to continuing education reflects her dedication to clinical excellence and to providing patients with thoughtful, comprehensive, and up-to-date dental care.

A Focus on Cosmetic & Comprehensive Dentistry

Dr. Arif practices the full spectrum of general and family dentistry, with a deepening focus on cosmetic and restorative cases. She is experienced in porcelain veneers, Invisalign, teeth whitening, full-mouth rehabilitation, and implant restoration. Her clinical approach is built around digital workflows and modern technology. From digital scanning and precision treatment planning to a fully integrated practice model that prioritizes accuracy, efficiency, and patient comfort at every step.

A Dentist Who Knows This Community Well

As a mom and a neighbor to this community, Dr. Arif designed The Smile Atelier with Newton families and professionals specifically in mind. She understands the pace of life here, the value residents place on quality, and the kind of experience busy professionals and families are looking for. That connection to the community informs everything about how she has designed her practice: from the boutique aesthetic of the space to the way every appointment is customized around the individual patient.
