The practice

Our primary goal is to save your natural teeth whenever possible. Our practice specializes in endodontics, commonly referred to as root canal therapy. Endodontics is the treatment of the pulp and surrounding tissues of a tooth.

The information provided here should help answer many of your questions about endodontic treatment. If you would like additional information, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us!

Dr. McCunniff is a native Iowan who was born and raised in Waterloo. He is a board-eligible endodontist and active member of the American Dental Association, American Association of Endodontics, and Des Moines District Dental Society.

Working in close partnership with our referring doctors is an important aspect of the care we provide. We value each and every referral and take great pride in providing a warm, comfortable and technologically advanced environment. Our friendly staff is here to assist you when you need us.
