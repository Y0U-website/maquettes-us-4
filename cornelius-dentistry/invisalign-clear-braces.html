How does Invisalign work?

Following your Invisalign exam, the next step in the process is taking impressions of your teeth. We send these models off to the Invisalign lab, and they are used to create your custom invisible braces in Cornelius, NC.

You change the aligner every few weeks, and this method gradually straightens your smile. Your Cornelius dentist will tell you how long to wear your new aligners and how often.

What are the advantages of Invisalign?

The most significant advantage is that Invisalign is transparent and does not affect your daily life. No one will know you are wearing them unless you tell them. Although there may be a slight lisp when you first start wearing them, you will be able to laugh, smile, and eat without restrictions.

Invisalign is an excellent alternative to traditional braces because you don’t have to deal with metal brackets. You won’t have to go to the dentist every two weeks to get your brackets tightened. And you never have to worry about food getting lodged in your wires or brackets.

Metal brackets aren’t just noticeable and often unsightly; they can tear your cheeks and gums. Getting them tightened is also painful, and frequent trips to the dentist can hamper your schedule.

Invisalign in Cornelius, NC can be removed to eat, drink, or brush your teeth. However, tooth repositioning will only be effective if you wear the aligners full-time. You must wear them for around 22 hours per day, or the process may slow down. If you break or misplace your clear aligners, contact our office immediately for replacement instructions.

Once you and your Cornelius, NC, doctor agree that Invisalign is the best orthodontic procedure for you, our dentists will take photos, x-rays, and impressions of your teeth. These impressions will be used to create your unique, personalized treatment plan and custom-made Invisalign braces.

Your smile treatment plan will also display your ideal final tooth position. Your Invisalign aligners will then be manufactured and received by your doctor. Our dentists will give you several sets of aligners, which you replace every two weeks until you reach the end of your aligner set.

Invisalign works by gradually and gently positioning your teeth into the right places, based on your smile treatment plan.

Straight teeth help prevent the enamel wear caused by a misaligned bite. Enamel where can lead to tooth breakage and cavities. Straight teeth are also simpler to brush and floss.

What types of issues does Invisalign correct?

It’s commonly believed that Invisalign clear aligners in Cornelius, NC can only benefit very mild or minor tooth and alignment issues. While some problems need metal braces, clear plastic braces can still treat minor, moderate, and even severe teeth alignment issues. Invisalign can help with gaps, crowding, overlapping, and twisted teeth.

To learn whether you are compatible with this treatment, our dentists will scan your dental arches and malocclusion to determine whether aligners fit into your smile treatment plan.

Will I have to wear a retainer after Invisalign?

Yes, a retainer is an integral part of your Invisalign therapy. A retainer helps remind your teeth that straight is their new permanent position, keeping teeth from reverting to their previous overlapping or contorted positions.

Your retainer is made of smooth, comfortable plastic. And your Cornelius, NC dentist will tell you how long you have to wear your retainer for best results.

Is Invisalign noticeable or painful?

Invisalign clear braces in Cornelius, NC are designed to be discreet, and most of the time, no one will notice that you’re wearing Invisalign.

Additionally, Invisalign in Cornelius is designed to feel more comfortable inside your mouth compared with traditional metal braces. With Invisalign, there are no tightening appointments and no metal to weigh down your mouth, scratch your cheeks, or rub against your gums.
