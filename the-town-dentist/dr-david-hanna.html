Dr. David Hanna, DMD

Born and raised in Bergen County, Dr. David Hanna brings a hometown warmth and cutting-edge expertise to every patient he sees. After earning his undergraduate degree from Fairleigh Dickinson University, he went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. He then completed advanced clinical training at NYU Langone’s prestigious residency in General Dentistry.

Dr. Hanna is known for blending compassionate care with advanced, modern techniques. He goes far beyond the standard continuing education requirements, diving deep into implantology, prosthodontics, periodontics, orthodontics, and oral surgery. Patients appreciate his attention to detail, gentle approach, and dedication to using the latest digital dentistry technology — all of which help ensure the most accurate, comfortable, and long-lasting results.

Whether he’s restoring a single tooth or rebuilding an entire smile, Dr. Hanna’s calm demeanor and commitment to excellence make patients feel confident and cared for. He’s a proud member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, and Bergen County Dental Society.

When he’s not creating beautiful, healthy smiles, Dr. Hanna loves spending time with his family and exploring the tri-state area — from weekend hikes to discovering new local spots. His genuine connection to the community, combined with his clinical skill, is what keeps patients coming back and referring friends and family.
