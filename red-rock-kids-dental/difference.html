Our Kids Dentistry Special Offers

Dr. Charlene Mo’s journey to becoming a pediatric dentistry specialist began in her hometown of Los Angeles. After completing her Bachelor of Science in Psychobiology at UCLA , she embarked on a path dedicated to children’s oral health.

She earned her DMD (Doctor of Dental Medicine) from Roseman University of Health Sciences , setting the foundation for her dental expertise. But Dr. Charlene’s commitment to pediatric dentistry didn’t stop there. She pursued an additional two-year residency at UNLV , specializing in pediatric dentistry.

Dr. Charlene brings her passion for creating healthier smiles to every patient she treats. Her goal is to make dental visits fun and comfortable, empowering you to make informed decisions about your child’s oral health. When she’s not in the office, Dr. Charlene enjoys playing golf, traveling with friends and even indulges in the art of making her own fresh pasta.​

Dr. Karen Kang’s fascinating journey from South Korea to Georgia infuses her care with a vibrant cultural perspective. From day one, her heart was set on pediatric dentistry, and she carries this diverse background into every patient interaction. With a rich tapestry of experiences and a mastery of sedation dentistry, she’s dedicated to making every child’s dental experience extraordinary.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Medical College of Georgia , laying the foundation for her dental expertise.

Driven by her passion for pediatric dentistry, she went on to complete specialized training at Temple University Hospital , becoming proficient in treating diverse pediatric patients and sedation dentistry techniques. Dr. Karen is a proud board-certified member of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry .

With a gentle and conservative approach, Dr. Kang is committed to providing the highest quality dental care, ensuring a positive and comfortable experience for your child. Her years of dental experience and unwavering compassion for pediatric dentistry make her a trusted partner in your child’s oral health journey.

Beyond her role as a dedicated pediatric dentist, Dr. Kang enjoys exploring her culinary talents, embarking on exciting travel adventures, and savoring precious moments with her cherished family.

Dr. Jennifer Lapsker grew up in the suburbs of Chicago, deeply influenced by her father, a dentist. This early exposure to dentistry fostered a passion that led her to pursue a career in the same field. After completing dental school, she relocated to Albuquerque, New Mexico, where she practiced for 14 years in the “Land of Enchantment.” Recently, Dr. Jennifer and her family decided to make a change and moved to Las Vegas, excited about the city’s vibrant food scene and entertainment options.

Dr. Jennifer finds immense joy and satisfaction in dentistry, especially in treating children. She loves the creative aspects of her work and the opportunity to build meaningful relationships with her patients and their families. Interacting with children every day is one of the highlights of her job, and she takes pride in helping kids overcome dental anxiety and fostering a positive attitude toward dental care.

As a parent and a lifelong “child at heart,” Dr. Jennifer brings a unique perspective to her practice. She strives to create a fun and relaxed environment where kids look forward to their dental visits. Her dedication to providing exceptional care and her ability to connect with her young patients make her a valuable asset to the team at Red Rock Kids Dental .

Outside of the office, Dr. Jennifer loves exploring Las Vegas with her son and toy poodle. She enjoys sunset walks, playing video games, and discovering new recipes. Dr. Jennifer also has a passion for crafts such as sewing, embroidery, and building miniatures. When she wants to unwind, she enjoys working out with the Peloton community. Fun fact: Dr. Jennifer is a former Starbucks barista, loves to tap dance, and used to work with Disney to help guests plan vacations.
