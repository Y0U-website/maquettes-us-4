Orthodontic Retainers

Retainers, designed to protect your results.

Because a great smile isn’t just created — it’s maintained.

Orthodontic treatment doesn’t end when your aligners come off. That moment — the final reveal, the smile you worked toward — is the beginning of the retention phase. And the retention phase is what makes the result permanent.

Retainers are what hold everything in place. Without one, teeth don’t stay where treatment left them. They shift — gradually, quietly, and sometimes significantly — back toward where they started. A retainer isn’t optional aftercare. It’s the last step of treatment.

Whether you’re finishing treatment at ClearCut, need a replacement, or haven’t worn your retainer in a while — we’re here to help you protect what you’ve already built. No lectures. No judgment. Just a plan to keep your smile exactly where it belongs.

Your teeth have a memory. Retainers are how you override it.

Teeth are held in place by periodontal ligaments — elastic tissues that, after orthodontic treatment, still retain some memory of where your teeth used to be. Without consistent retainer wear, those ligaments gradually pull teeth back toward their original position. It’s not a flaw in your treatment. It’s biology.

The shift can start within weeks of stopping retainer wear. In the first year after treatment, it’s most significant. Over time, the risk decreases, but it never fully disappears — which is why orthodontists recommend nightly retainer wear indefinitely.

The good news: retention is simple. A retainer worn consistently — even just at night — is enough to hold your smile in place for life. The investment you made in your teeth is worth protecting. Retainers are how you protect it.

Clear Vivera® Retainers

Custom-fabricated from a precise digital scan of your teeth, your Vivera® retainers are made from a thicker, stronger material than your aligners — engineered specifically for long-term retention. Nearly invisible, removable, and designed for comfortable nightly wear, they’re the standard choice for protecting your results after clear aligner treatment.

Replacement Retainers

Lost, broken, or worn out? We create a new Vivera® retainer quickly — typically from a new digital scan to ensure the most accurate possible fit. The sooner you replace it, the less likely your teeth are to have shifted in the interim.
