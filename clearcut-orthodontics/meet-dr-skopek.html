How he thinks about treatment.

Exclusivity is a clinical decision, not a marketing one.

Dr. Skopek chose to focus exclusively on clear aligners using the Invisalign® brand because he believes focus produces better outcomes than division. When every case you see is an Invisalign® clear aligner case, your pattern recognition improves, your planning precision deepens, and your ability to handle complexity increases. ClearCut doesn’t offer traditional braces because Dr. Skopek decided — after years of practicing both — that doing one thing exceptionally well is worth more than doing many things adequately.

The plan matters as much as the treatment.

Skopek Remote is a clinical standard, not a convenience feature.

Dr. Skopek built Skopek Remote — powered by Dental Monitoring technology — into ClearCut treatment because remote scan review catches issues that in-office visits alone would miss. Aligner fit problems, tracking deviations, and compliance drops are identified and addressed between appointments — not just at them. For Dr. Skopek, remote monitoring isn’t about reducing office visits. It’s about improving the quality of oversight between them.

The patient experience is part of the clinical outcome.

Dr. Skopek designed the ROX® System — a trademarked six-pillar patient experience framework — because he believes how a patient feels throughout treatment affects their engagement with it. A patient who feels informed is more compliant. A patient who feels heard is more honest about concerns. A patient who feels calm in the chair is easier to treat accurately. The ROX® System is, at its core, a clinical tool.

The doctor your patients will actually meet.

Dr. Skopek has been part of the Barrington community for over thirty years — long enough to have treated the children of patients he treated in his first years of practice, and to consider that continuity one of the most meaningful things about his work.

Beyond the office, Dr. Skopek is a familiar face in the communities he serves. He supports WINGS and Barrington Giving Day, sponsors local schools and activities, and stays actively involved in the Chamber of Commerce at both locations. He has never separated the practice from the community — in his mind, they’ve always been the same thing.
