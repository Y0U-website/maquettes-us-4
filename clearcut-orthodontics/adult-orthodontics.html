Step 1 — Your Complimentary Consultation

Step 2 — Your Treatment Plan, Designed by a Doctor

Your treatment plan is designed by Dr. Skopek, supported by technology. Digital planning tools allow him to map every tooth movement with precision — but the clinical decisions behind those movements are his. The sequencing, the forces, the biological response of your bone and tissue, and the long-term stability of your result all reflect thirty years of orthodontic expertise. The technology makes that expertise more precise. It doesn’t replace it.

Step 3 — Your Custom Clear Aligners

A series of clear, removable aligners guides your teeth into position on a carefully controlled schedule. You wear each set for the prescribed period, then move to the next.

Step 4 — Skopek Remote Between Visits

Using Skopek Remote — guided at-home scans — Dr. Skopek’s team tracks your progress between visits. Fewer trips to the office. The same level of expert oversight throughout. It’s a standard part of your treatment, not an optional upgrade at additional cost.
