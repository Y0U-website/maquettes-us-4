Triangle Street Dental is proud to serve Worcester, MA and surrounding areas. We are dedicated to providing the highest level of dental medicine along with friendly, compassionate service.

MEET THE DENTIST

Dr. Deepti Kalra

Dr. Deepti Kalra graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine (DMD) in May of 2010 with honors. Prior to that, she completed her training with a Bachelor’s degree in Dental Surgery (BDS) at the SGRD Dental School in India in 2005. Dr. Kalra was awarded a university gold medal on graduation.

Since completing her formal dental training, Dr. Kalra has worked in private practice in Central Massachusetts and Rhode Island before transitioning to her own practice. She is enthusiastic about dental health and enjoys educating patients about preventative techniques (nutrition and homecare) and possesses a vast knowledge of clinical procedures. While her primary focus is prevention, Dr. Kalra is competent in end-to-end patient management with a strong background in Fixed and Removable prosthesis, Crown and Bridge, Root Canal Therapy, and Complex Restorations.

Dr. Kalra is a Member of the American Dental Association, Massachusetts Dental Society, as well as the Worcester Dental Society.

Dr. Kalra is constantly seeking additional training and continuing education opportunities to perfect her skillset and provide her patients the very best care available. In 2024, she began a mini-residency in TMJ and Orofacial Pain at Tufts Dental School, further expanding her expertise in these specialized areas. Additionally, in 2025, she completed her Invisalign certification, enhancing her ability to offer comprehensive orthodontic care to her patients.

Dr. Kalra and her husband (Rohan) have two children, ages three and eight. They are raising their beautiful family in Grafton, MA. Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kalra spends her time volunteering and supporting school activities, traveling, and playing badminton and tennis.

Dr. Kalra is fluent in English, Hindi, and Punjabi.
