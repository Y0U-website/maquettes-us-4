Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance the appearance of your smile with treatments like teeth whitening, veneers, and bonding, customized to give you a brighter and more confident smile.

Dental Implant crowns

Replace missing teeth with durable and natural-looking implant crowns that function like real teeth, restoring your smile and improving oral health.

Crowns & Bridges

Protect damaged teeth or replace missing teeth with custom crowns and bridges that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

Root Canal Therapy

A procedure to save an infected or severely decayed tooth by removing the damaged pulp and sealing the tooth to prevent further infection.

Invisalign

Straighten your teeth with clear aligners, improving both the appearance and function of your smile.

Tooth Extractions

Safe removal of problematic or impacted teeth, including wisdom teeth, to alleviate pain and prevent future dental issues.

Pediatric Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care for children, focusing on preventive care, education, and creating a positive experience for young patients.

Emergency Dental Care

Urgent treatment for dental emergencies such as toothaches, broken teeth, or injuries, helping you manage pain and avoid further complications.

Gum Disease Treatment

Specialized care to treat and manage periodontal disease through deep cleanings, scaling, root planing, and ongoing maintenance.

Dentures & Partial Dentures

Custom-made full or partial dentures to restore function and appearance for patients who have lost multiple teeth.

Oral Cancer Screenings

Early detection of oral cancer through routine screenings during dental exams, ensuring peace of mind and timely intervention if needed.

Union Smiles Family Dental is a trusted family dentist in Union City, NJ, led by Dr. Marina Isaac. We provide personalized preventive, cosmetic, and restorative dental care to patients throughout Hudson County.
