Jeremie Sauve, DMD

Dedication to Care

Dr. Jeremie Sauve is committed to fostering genuine relationships and serving patients well. He loves being the choice dentist for Madison families, helping people find their smile and share their story. Rooted in compassion and respect, his care is centered around delivering comfortable, personalized solutions for healthy smiles.

Education & Training

Dr. Sauve graduated from the University of Wisconsin-Madison with his bachelor’s degree in biology. Attending Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, he was granted a Merit Scholarship, received Deans list honors for three years, and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine Degree.

home life

Born in Saint-Hyacinthe, Canada, Dr. Sauve moved to the United States when he was nine years old. His family lived in Paris, Texas and then moved to Appleton, WI where they put down roots. Dr. Sauve loves spending time with his family and friends. He also enjoys staying active weightlifting, playing squash, and hiking.

Kevin Greene, DDS

Exceptional Care

Dr. Kevin Greene is passionate about delivering consistent, high-quality dental care while treating every patient like family. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) from Marquette University and is proud to serve patients at Excellence in Dentistry – Cottage Grove.

Dr. Greene has a special interest in CEREC® same-day dentistry, allowing patients to receive beautiful, custom restorations in a single visit. He is also committed to continuing his education in sleep apnea treatment, TMJ/TMD therapy, dental implants, and the business of dentistry so he can provide exceptional care for years to come.

home life

Outside the office, Dr. Greene enjoys spending time with his wife, their new daughter, and their three cats.
