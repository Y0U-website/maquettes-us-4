Our Values

Relationship

Genuine connections lead to lasting relationships that create a comfortable, supportive dental experience. You’ll feel like you belong and know we truly care.

Integrity

Rooted in trust and respect, we empower you with clear and honest conversations about your oral health. You’ll know what to expect every step of the way as we do only what’s best for you and your smile.

Your Dentists

Expertise

Dr. Batchelor, Dr. Sauve, and Dr. Greene combine years of expertise with leading-edge solutions to deliver exceptional results. They’re passionate about serving each patient with respect and excellence.

Compassion

No judgment. No pressure. With understanding and compassion, we listen to your needs, wants, and concerns while making you feel at home.

Service

We are dedicated to serving our Madison and Cottage Grove communities by helping every patient find their smile and share their story. With exceptional care, we strive to make a positive impact in every life and each community.
