Technology & Services

General & Family Dentistry

Prophylaxis (Teeth Cleaning)

A prophylaxis dental appointment provides preventative treatment to thoroughly clean the teeth. Attending your regular prophylaxis appointment will keep your mouth healthy.

Fluoride Treatment

Oral treatment with fluoride helps strengthen enamel, reduce the risk of tooth decay, and provide advanced cavity protection.

Oral Cancer Screening

During checkups, you’ll receive an oral cancer screening to investigate the inside of your mouth and your throat and neck to check for lumps or other abnormalities.

Pediatric Dentistry

We provide comprehensive dental care for children from infancy through the teen years.

Tooth Colored Fillings

Composite fillings fill in cavities with a resin designed to blend in naturally with the rest of your smile.

Dental Crowns

Custom designed, a dental crown permanently covers a decayed, damaged, or cracked tooth.

Dental Bridges

If you are missing teeth, we can custom fit a dental bridge to fill a space that your tooth previously occupied.

Tooth Extractions

A simple tooth extraction involves the removal of a tooth due to decay, disease, or trauma.

Emergency Care

If you think you may have a dental emergency, give us a call and we’ll help guide you. Dr. Batchelor and Dr. Sauve have extensive experience treating and managing dental emergencies.

Sedation Dentistry

We offer safe and comfortable relaxation with oral conscious sedation to ensure you can relax and rest easy during your visit.

TMD Treatment

We offer options to relieve and resolve TMD concerns ranging from relaxation techniques and therapies to helpful oral devices or medications.

Cosmetic & Restorative Solutions

Professional Teeth Whitening

Our professional whitening treatment is more effective than over-the-counter options. Your fresher, brighter smile awaits.

Dentures

Whether you need a partial set of dentures or a complete set, our team will assess your situation, make recommendations, answer all your questions, and deliver the option you choose.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Before dental implants, the only solution for missing teeth was the instability of traditional dentures. Now we can create an implant-retained denture that snaps securely into place to create stability and restore natural function.

Porcelain Veneers

The quickest way to a five-star smile, porcelain veneers are perfectly crafted to fit your smile. In as little as two visits you could be showing off your stunning new smile.

Invisalign®

Invisalign’s clear aligners are a modern orthodontic solution—virtually invisible and removable—allowing you to achieve a beautifully straight smile without the challenges of traditional braces.

Gum Disease Treatment

To restore healthy gums, periodontal treatment is primarily utilized to clean the pockets around the teeth to prevent bone damage.

Root Canal Therapy

Designed to remove nerves from the infected pulp of a tooth, root canal therapy eliminates infection and pain while also reducing the chance of future infection.

Complimentary Consultations

Let us help you discover the best solution for you and your smile.

Cone Beam Imaging
