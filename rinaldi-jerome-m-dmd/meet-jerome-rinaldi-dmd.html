Meet jerome rinaldi dmd

Since completing dental school, in 2006, Dr. Rinaldi has actively sought out continuing education opportunities. He takes a range of courses through the Lehigh Valley Health Network continuing education program and is an active member in the Spear Education Study Club.

Through these activities, Dr. Rinaldi reinforces the quality dentistry he provides patients and allows him to utilize the best proven techniques and materials.

Beyond the Practice

Dr. Rinaldi grew up in a small town outside of Scranton. He now lives in the Nazareth area with his wife and their two daughters. When not at the office, Dr. Rinaldi most values spending time with his wife and kids. Whether they’re planting a garden, riding bikes, snow tubing, or just watching a movie together, it’s always eventful and always treasured. Dr. Rinaldi also enjoys woodworking projects and most anything related to cars.
