Dr. Michelle Siegelwax is originally from Long Island, New York. She completed her undergraduate studies at the State University of New York at Oneonta, where she graduated magna cum laude with a B.S. in Biology. She then attended the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, achieving her DMD while receiving multiple merit awards. Following graduation, Dr. Siegelwax completed a one- year general practice residency at St. Luke s in Bethlehem, PA.

She prides herself on getting to know her patients on a personal level. She firmly believes that a strong doctor-patient relationship is critical to achieving the best dental outcomes.

Dr. Siegelwax enjoys spending time with family, friends and her tabby cat named Leo. She is an avid hiker and loves finding new trails. Some of her other passions include working out, cooking, baking and playing volleyball.

Please join us in welcoming her to our family!
