About

Caring for the Community I Call Home

I grew up in Idaho but found my way to Rome, GA through my wife, Carol, who is originally from Chattanooga. We’ve put down roots here, raising our five amazing kids and becoming a part of this wonderful community. Caring for neighbors and friends feels like more than a career. It’s a privilege.

My Approach to Dentistry

I understand that going to the dentist isn’t always easy, so I do everything I can to create a calm and comfortable experience for every patient. I enjoy a wide range of dental care, including cosmetic bonding, crowns and bridges, root canals, and extractions. Continuing education is a big part of my life, and I’m proud to offer many treatments right here in our office, so patients don’t have to travel elsewhere for care.

Beyond the Office

When I’m not working with patients, you’ll probably find me in my workshop. Woodworking is a favorite hobby of mine and gives me a chance to slow down and focus on detail, something I also bring to my dentistry. Most of all, I love spending time with Carol and our kids, whether we’re outside exploring or just relaxing at home.

Here to Help You Smile with Confidence

Whether you’re coming in for a routine checkup, a smile makeover, or to fix a dental concern, I want you to feel comfortable, cared for, and confident every step of the way.

Dr. Thomas Shepherd

I’m Dr. Shepherd, a proud Georgia native who discovered my love for dentistry while studying at Berry College. After earning my undergraduate degree, I went on to graduate cum laude with distinction in research from the Dental College of Georgia, where I received my Doctorate of Medicine in Dentistry. I then completed a General Practice Residency in Augusta, where I gained advanced training in IV sedation, dental implants, extractions, bone grafting, and caring for patients with complex medical needs.

Making Every Patient Feel at Ease

I believe dentistry is about so much more than teeth—it’s about people. My goal is always to make patients feel comfortable, cared for, and confident about their dental health. I especially enjoy performing dental implants, wisdom tooth extractions, sedation dentistry, dentures, crowns and bridges, and supporting patients with special needs.

Committed to Lifelong Learning

I’m passionate about staying on the cutting edge of dentistry, constantly expanding my skills so I can provide the best care possible. Oral surgery has always been an area I love, and I take pride in helping patients feel at ease during even the most complex treatments.

Life Outside the Office

My wife, Olivia, and I met at Berry College, and we chose Rome as our home because we love its welcoming community and small-town charm. Outside of dentistry, I enjoy camping, cooking, traveling, and playing basketball. Most of all, I love spending time with Olivia and exploring new adventures together.
