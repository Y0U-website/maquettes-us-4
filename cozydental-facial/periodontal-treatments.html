Gum Grafts

Gum grafting helps restore receding gums, rebuilding tissue that protects the roots of your teeth.

By covering exposed roots, gum grafts reduce sensitivity while improving both oral health and the appearance of your smile.

Benefits

  • Protect exposed roots
  • Reduce tooth sensitivity
  • Improve gum health
  • Enhance smile appearance

Bone Grafts

Bone grafting rebuilds areas of bone loss caused by periodontal disease or tooth loss, restoring a healthy foundation.

This helps preserve jaw structure and can prepare the area for future restorative treatment, such as dental implants, when needed.

Benefits

  • Restore bone support
  • Preserve jaw structure
  • Support future implants
  • Improve long-term stability

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the signs of gum disease?

Common signs include bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, swelling or tenderness, and teeth that feel loose. A dental exam can confirm what&#x27;s going on.

Can gum disease be reversed?

In its earliest stage (gingivitis), gum disease is often reversible with professional cleaning and good home care. More advanced cases can be effectively managed with treatment.

Does periodontal treatment hurt?

Modern periodontal treatments, including laser therapy, are designed to minimize discomfort. Most patients find the process far more comfortable than they expect.

How often should I receive periodontal maintenance?

Most patients benefit from periodontal maintenance every three to four months, though your dentist will recommend a schedule based on your individual needs.
