Choosing the Right Approach

The best technique for you depends on how much bone height is available and your overall treatment plan — we&#x27;ll walk through the right option together during your consultation.

Lateral Window Sinus Lift

  • Recommended when significant bone height is required.
  • Allows greater bone augmentation.
  • Ideal for advanced implant cases.

Crestal (Osteotome) Sinus Lift

  • Less invasive.
  • Suitable when adequate existing bone remains.
  • Often performed together with implant placement.

What to Expect After Your Procedure

The grafted bone typically needs several months to fully integrate before an implant is placed, ensuring a solid, lasting foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a sinus lift hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so patients typically feel little to no discomfort. Mild swelling afterward is normal and manageable.

How long is recovery?

Initial healing generally takes about one to two weeks, while the grafted bone typically needs several months to fully integrate before implant placement.

Can my implant be placed at the same time as the sinus lift?

In some cases, yes — particularly with the crestal technique when enough existing bone remains. We&#x27;ll determine the best sequence for your case during your consultation.

Which technique will I need?

It depends on your available bone height, evaluated with detailed 3D imaging. We&#x27;ll recommend the lateral window or crestal approach based on what&#x27;s best for your anatomy.
