Orthodontics

Orthodontics at Woodard Family Dentistry, is the field of dentistry that deals with the alignment of teeth and jaws to improve a person’s smile and oral health. Orthodontic treatments are performed to correct issues such as crooked or crowded teeth, overbites, underbites, crossbites, and other malocclusions (bite issues). While many people associate orthodontics with teenagers wearing braces, treatments are available for patients of all ages, including children, adolescents, and adults.

The Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontic treatment in Spokane Valley, WA, can provide numerous benefits beyond just a beautiful smile. Here are some of the key benefits:

  • Improved oral health : Straight teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities, gum disease, and other oral health issues.
  • Enhanced aesthetics : Orthodontic treatment can significantly improve the appearance of your smile, boosting your self-esteem and confidence.
  • Better functionality : Correcting bite issues can improve chewing, speaking, and overall dental function.
  • Preventing future problems : Addressing misaligned teeth and jaws early can prevent more severe dental issues and complications later in life.
  • Jaw alignment : Orthodontics can help align the jaws properly, reducing the risk of temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders and associated pain.

Orthodontic Treatments in Spokane Valley, WA

At Woodard Family Dentistry in Spokane Valley, WA, we offer various orthodontic treatments tailored to meet the unique needs of each patient. Here are some of the most common orthodontic treatments available:

Clear Aligners

At Woodard Family Dentistry, we are proud to offer SureSmile and Clear aligners as part of our comprehensive orthodontic services. These advanced aligner systems are designed to provide a discreet and effective solution for straightening teeth and correcting bite issues without the need for traditional metal braces.

SureSmile aligners utilize cutting-edge technology to create a precise and customized treatment plan, ensuring optimal results and a comfortable fit. Similarly, Clear aligners offer a virtually invisible way to achieve a beautifully aligned smile, making them an excellent choice for adults and teens who want to straighten their teeth without the noticeable appearance of braces.

Retainers

Retainers are custom-made appliances used to maintain the results of orthodontic treatment. After braces or aligners have moved the teeth into their new positions, retainers help ensure that the teeth do not shift back to their original positions. They can be removable or fixed, depending on the patient’s needs. Contact us to learn more.

The Orthodontic Treatment Process in Spokane Valley, WA

The orthodontic treatment process at Woodard Family Dentistry involves several key steps to ensure the best possible results:

Initial Consultation

The first step in the orthodontic journey is the initial consultation with Dr. Woodard. During this appointment, he will evaluate your dental and facial structure, discuss your treatment goals, and determine if orthodontic treatment is right for you. This visit may include X-rays, photographs, and impressions of your teeth to help create a customized treatment plan.

Treatment Planning

Based on the information gathered during the initial consultation, Dr. Woodard will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs. He will explain the recommended treatment options, the estimated duration of treatment, and any associated costs. This is an excellent opportunity to ask any questions you may have about the process.

Starting Treatment

Once you decide to proceed with orthodontic treatment, the next step is to begin the process. This may involve the placement of braces, fitting of aligners, or other orthodontic appliances. Dr. Woodard and his team will provide detailed instructions on how to care for your braces or aligners and maintain good oral hygiene throughout the treatment.

Regular Adjustments and Monitoring

Throughout your orthodontic treatment, you will have regular appointments with Dr. Woodard to monitor your progress and make any necessary adjustments. These appointments are typically scheduled every four to six weeks. Regular checkups ensure that your treatment is progressing as planned and allow Dr. Woodard to address any concerns or issues that may arise.

Completing Treatment

Once your teeth have moved into their desired positions and your bite has been corrected, it’s time to complete your orthodontic treatment. Dr. Woodard will remove your braces or provide you with your final set of aligners. You will receive retainers to help maintain your new smile and prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original positions.

Post-Treatment Care

After completing your orthodontic treatment, it’s important to follow Dr. Woodard’s instructions for wearing retainers and maintaining good oral hygiene . Regular dental checkups and cleanings are essential to keep your teeth and gums healthy and ensure your new smile lasts a lifetime.
