Dental implants are the premier replacement choice for single tooth loss and multiple tooth loss. Unlike other tooth replacement options such as dentures and dental bridges, dental implants preserve the jawbone. Without something to support, whether an artificial tooth root or real, the jawbone will begin to deteriorate and cause facial sagging. Facial sagging causes pre-mature aging and changes the overall shape of ones face. Additionally, dental bridges require the neighboring teeth to be ground down in order to accept the bridge. With dental implants, healthy adjacent teeth remain intact.
