The practice

Dr. Saxon is a native of Oklahoma City and a graduate of Putnam City North High School. After finishing high school, he received degrees in Zoology and Chemistry from University of Oklahoma. He earned his doctorate from the University of Oklahoma - College of Dentistry.

Dr. Saxon takes between 100-150 hours of continuing education every year. He travels extensively to stay ahead of the latest technological advances in cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

When teeth are damaged, missing, or worn down, restorative dentistry can rebuild both function and aesthetics. We offer bridges, crowns, implants, and dentures to restore strength, stability, and comfort to your bite. Our goal is to create results that look natural while allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence again.

From growing smiles to lifelong maintenance, we provide comprehensive care for every stage of life. Our family dentistry services include pediatric care, routine cleanings, fillings, sealants, sleep apnea treatment, oral cancer screenings, and gum disease therapy . We focus on prevention, early detection, and comfortable treatment to keep your entire family’s smiles healthy for years to come.
