We hope this letter finds you and your family in good health at this very extraordinary time dealing with the Coronavirus Pandemic. These last few months have been and continue to be very challenging for everyone, and all of us are looking forward to resuming our normal habits and routines. While many things have changed, one thing has remained the same, our commitment to your health and safety.

Our office follows recommendations and guidelines made by the American Dental Association (ADA), Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), Pennsylvania Department of Health, and the State, to protect you and our staff. You may notice the following changes when it is time for your next appointment.

We will be charging $25 for surgical appointments and $15 for non-surgical appointments for the increased cost of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) that is required to keep you and ourselves safe.

You will be hearing from us regarding scheduling your next appointment when the office reopens. We will begin a slow reopening with scheduling patients based on the date last seen. We feel that this is the most efficient method of adequately getting all patients back on track. We look forward to seeing you.
