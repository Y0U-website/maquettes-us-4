About the Doctor

Farshid Sanavi, DMD, PhD

Dr. Sanavi is the wise and calming voice of the office. He received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) and Certificate in Periodontics from the University of Pennsylvania. He continued his post-doctoral training as a Research Fellow at the University of Rochester and completed his PhD thesis at University of Pennsylvania, Department of Pathology studying immunopathology and mechanisms of bone destruction in the presence of infection.

When away from the office and school, Dr. Sanavi spends time with his lovely wife, son, and little Yorkie.
