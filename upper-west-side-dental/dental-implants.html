Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults missing one or more teeth are candidates. Key factors: enough bone volume, healthy gums, controlled systemic conditions like diabetes, and no tobacco habits that impair healing. Thin bone can often be grafted. Most patients also ask whether healing means a gap in their smile — not esthetically in most cases, because temporary coverage is planned into the sequence. Cleaning visits happen around the implant schedule rather than conflicting with it; that flow is planned at consult.

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Healthy gums and adequate bone (or graftable)
  • Controlled diabetes, no heavy smoking
  • Long-term mindset over quick-fix pricing

Implants vs. Bridges vs. Dentures

A bridge is faster and often cheaper but crowns the adjacent teeth; dentures cost least and remain removable. Implants stand alone and protect bone. The right answer depends on your mouth and priorities — compared side by side at consult, including implant-retained dentures for stability at lower cost than full fixed implant work.
