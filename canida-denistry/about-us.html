Dr. Robert Canida, D.D.S .

Dr. Bob was introduced to dentistry at a very early age through his father’s dental practice in Madison, and now has been joined in the practice by his son, Ben. He is a graduate of Taylor University and the Indiana University School of Dentistry. Bob received postgraduate training at the L D Pankey Institute and also a yearlong Mini Residency in Oral Facial Pain under Dr. Jeff Okeson at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry. He routinely uses Osteopathic style Dentocranial treatment.

He is an active member of Calvary Baptist Church in Madison. He has participated in several missionary trips to foreign locations including Sierra Leone, West Africa, Honduras, Panama, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Haiti, Ecuador, and the Amazon in Brazil. Bob enjoys athletic activity. He has completed several bicycle trips across America as a dedicated participant with the Wandering Wheels, where he met his wife Charlotte. These days they enjoy riding their tandem bicycles and doing bike tours. Besides cycling Bob also loves the river and waterskiing, rowing, racquetball, and pickle ball.

Aside from his dental and athletic endeavors, Bob placed great importance on community involvement. He has served on the Human Relations Commission, Historic Madison Board, Girls Inc., Youth Shelter, MCS Education, La Casa Amiga, Jane’s Kids, and the Salvation Army. He enjoys planting and watching trees mature. He is proud to be part of a group of dentists and hygienists who have implemented a free community dental clinic at the Salvation Army to serve those in need in the Madison area.

Dr. Benjamin Canida, D.D.S.

Dr. Ben Canida grew up in Madison and attended Eggleston Elementary School and is a graduate of Madison Consolidated High School. He went on to earn his bachelor’s degree from Taylor University and his professional degree from the Indiana University School of Dentistry. Dr. Ben has also received postgraduate training at the L D Pankey Institute and the Dawson Academy, both recognized worldwide for their excellence in advanced technical reconstructive dentistry focusing on severe wear problems, TMJ, and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Ben joined his father in the practice in 2005 carrying on the tradition of his grandfather as he became the third generation in the Canida family to serve the community of Madison in the practice of dentistry. Dr. Ben is a member of the American Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. He also serves on the board of directors for the Heritage Trail Conservancy and volunteers as a swim coach for the Hoosier Hills Otters.

Dr. Ben and his lovely wife, Becky, have three beautiful daughters, Lily, Ava, and Kate. Becky is molding young lives and minds as a preschool teacher at Madison Preschool. In his time away from the office Dr. Ben especially enjoys the river and waterskiing, Crossfit, running, and biking. He and his family are actively involved with Calvary Baptist

Christy Facemire

Office Policies

Patients are seen by appointment only. Your scheduled appointment time has been reserved specifically for you. We request 24 hours notice if you need to cancel your appointment. We are aware that unforeseen events sometimes require missing an appointment. After missing your second appointment without notifying us 24 hours in advance, you may be charged an additional fee.

If you are a new patient, you can save time on your trip to the dentist by downloading and filling out these forms below before your first dentist appointment.

Despite careful scheduling, emergencies can cause delays. We try our very best to stay on time. If your appointment time is affected due to an unforeseen emergency, we’ll try to notify you. We know that your time is valuable, too. You will receive the same quality care no matter how our schedule is running.

Your treatment plan will include a breakdown of all applicable fees, and we will inform you of all costs before treatment is administered. We accept Visa, Mastercard, Personal Checks and currency for your payments. Please ask a staff member and we will review our payment options to accommodate your financial needs. If special arrangements are needed, please talk to our office manager prior to receiving service.

We believe that important decisions about your dental care should be made by you and your dentist, not your insurance company. Once you have decided on the course of treatment that you want, we will submit your claim and help you maximize your insurance benefits. Because insurance benefits vary greatly, we can only estimate your coverage in good faith, but cannot guarantee coverage due to the complexities of insurance contracts. Your estimated patient portion must be paid

at the time of service. As a service to our patients, we will bill insurance companies for services and allow them 45 days to render payment. After 60 days, you are responsible for the entire balance, paid in full. If you have any questions, our courteous staff is always available to answer them.
