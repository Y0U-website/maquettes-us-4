Expert Care for Complex Cases

After graduating from the UCLA School of Dentistry, Dr. Razmik pursued three additional years of rigorous training at Temple University to become a specialist in Periodontology and Oral Implantology. His expertise allows Calabasas Dental Institute to treat even the most complex cases—such as gum grafting, bone regeneration, and full-mouth implant reconstruction—right here in our office, without needing to refer you to an outside surgeon.

Philosophy & Personal Life

Dr. Razmik believes that a healthy body starts with a healthy mouth. He is passionate about educating patients on the vital link between gum health and systemic conditions like heart disease. When he is not performing surgery or teaching at USC, he is an avid traveler and outdoorsman who enjoys hiking, biking, and playing flamenco guitar.
