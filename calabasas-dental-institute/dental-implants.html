Solutions for Every Case

Single Implants

This replaces one missing tooth without affecting the neighbors. A titanium post replaces the root, and a custom porcelain crown is placed on top, looking and functioning exactly like the tooth you lost.

Implant Bridges

If you are missing three or four teeth in a row, we can use two implants to support a bridge. This restores a large section of your bite without the need for a removable partial denture.

Implant Dentures

We can use implants to lock a denture in place. This eliminates the slipping, clicking, and messy adhesives associated with traditional dentures, giving you the bite force to enjoy steak, apples, and corn on the cob.

Surgical Precision

We utilize CT scanning technology to map your nerves and bone density before surgery. This digital guidance ensures the implant is placed in the optimal position for quick healing and long-term stability.
