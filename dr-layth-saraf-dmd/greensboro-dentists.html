Meet Our Doctor

Dr. Layth Saraf

A graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Saraf has over 15 years of experience providing dental services to communities in the United States and across the globe. Dr. Saraf has completed an advanced dental surgical degree from the Royal College of Surgeons of Edinburgh in the United Kingdom. He is a member and Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. He continues to advance his knowledge and expertise by attending numerous continuing education courses and training annually.
