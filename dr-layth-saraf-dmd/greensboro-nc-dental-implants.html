The Dental Implant Process at Dr. Layth Saraf, DMD

Receiving dental implants in Greensboro, NC, is a structured, multi-step process engineered to prioritize clinical safety, patient comfort, and long-term success.

Step 1: Consultation & Digital Evaluation

Your journey begins with an in-depth evaluation . Dr. Saraf reviews your medical history, performs a comprehensive oral exam, and utilizes high-definition digital imaging. This allows us to accurately evaluate your bone density, map nerve pathways, and determine whether you are an ideal candidate for implant placement.

Step 2: Precise Implant Placement

During the secondary phase, the titanium implant post is carefully positioned into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia to ensure the patient&#x27;s absolute comfort. Most patients report minimal, easily manageable discomfort following the procedure.

Step 3: Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next few months, your body naturally fuses with the implant post. This healing window is crucial, as osseointegration builds the immovable foundation necessary to support the pressure of everyday chewing.

Step 4: Abutment & Final Custom Restoration

Once the implant has completely integrated with the bone, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached. A custom-fabricated crown, bridge, or denture is then securely fastened to the abutment, completing your smile restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants in Greensboro, NC

Who is an ideal candidate for dental implants in Greensboro?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth and sufficient jawbone density are excellent candidates for implants. Patients with active gum disease, managed diabetes, or bone loss may simply require routine preparatory procedures—such as periodontal therapy or a bone graft—prior to their implant placement.

Does the dental implant procedure hurt?

No. The actual surgical placement of the titanium post is done under localized anesthesia, meaning the area is completely numbed, and you will feel no pain. Mild, temporary soreness or swelling after the anesthesia wears off is completely normal and can be easily managed with over-the-counter ibuprofen or ice packs.

How long do dental implants last?

When properly cared for, the titanium implant post itself can last a lifetime. The visible custom crown or restoration attached to it undergoes normal daily wear and tear and typically lasts 10 to 15 years before needing an adjustment or replacement.

How do I clean and care for my dental implants?

You care for dental implants just as you do your natural teeth. Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss daily, and maintain regular professional cleanings and examinations at our Greensboro office.

Can dental implants stabilize my slipping dentures?

Yes! If you suffer from loose or shifting dentures, we can design an implant-supported denture. This locks your existing appliance firmly in place using strategically placed implant posts, instantly eliminating shifting, sore spots, and messy dental adhesives.
