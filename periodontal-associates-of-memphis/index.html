Dr. Godat joined Periodontal Associates of Memphis in 2000. Together with his partners, they have placed over 29,000 dental implants. He is also the co-founder of the Mid-South Interdisciplinary Dento-Facial Study Club and Mid-South Spear Study Club. These organizations help coordinate treatment between a patient’s general dentist and dental specialists to give them the best treatment possible.

As a dental professional, adventurer, and wellness enthusiast, Dr. Olga Byakina seamlessly blends expertise with warmth in her practice. Her philosophy extends beyond the dental chair, creating an environment where each patient is met with not just dental care but a genuine commitment to holistic well-being.

Following the completion of her residency, Dr. Olga made the move to Memphis to be with her fiancé, embracing a new chapter both personally and professionally.
