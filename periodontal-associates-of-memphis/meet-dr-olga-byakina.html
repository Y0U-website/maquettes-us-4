Dr. Olga Byakina

Dr. Byakina is a compassionate and dedicated dentist with a diverse background that spans across continents. Born in the charming town of Yelets, Russia, her journey took an exciting turn when she and her family traded the cobblestone streets for the vibrant hills of San Francisco when she was just 12.

Her academic adventure began at San Francisco State University, where she earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology with Magna Cum Laude distinction. Her commitment to excellence continued at the University of California School of Dentistry, where she completed her dental education.

Eager to expand her expertise, Dr. Byakina pursued a post-doctoral training in Periodontics and Implantology at Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine. Alongside her residency, she earned a Master of Science in Oral Biology and Pathology, showcasing her commitment to advancing her dental knowledge.

Following the completion of her residency, Dr. Byakina made the move to Memphis to be with her fiancé, embracing a new chapter both personally and professionally. Beyond the dental office, she embraces a rich and active lifestyle, finding joy in traveling, exploring the depths of the ocean through scuba diving, hitting the pavement with her running shoes, and grounding herself through the practice of yoga.

As a dental professional, adventurer, and wellness enthusiast, Dr. Byakina seamlessly blends expertise with warmth in her practice. Her philosophy extends beyond the dental chair, creating an environment where each patient is met with not just dental care but a genuine commitment to holistic well-being.
