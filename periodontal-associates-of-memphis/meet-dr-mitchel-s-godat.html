Miscellaneous

The Mid-South Mission of Mercy (MidMOM) is a free, two-day dental clinic for under-served and under-insured people living in Memphis and the surrounding Mid-South area. Since 2016, we have provided over 8.6 million dollars worth of dentistry to more than 13,000 patients in the greater Memphis area with the help of nearly 10,000 volunteers.
