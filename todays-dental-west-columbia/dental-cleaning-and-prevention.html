The Importance of Cleanings & Prevention for Your Oral Health

Routine cleanings are essential for maintaining your overall oral health. While brushing and flossing daily can help, only a professional cleaning can effectively remove calculus below the gum line, which often cannot be removed through regular brushing. Teeth decaying at the gum line can lead to gum disease and tooth loss if not treated early. At Todays Dental West Columbia, our team of skilled professionals performs thorough exams to assess and address any oral health issues, from mineral buildup on teeth to heavy calculus buildup on teeth.

What Happens During a Professional Cleaning?

During your exam and cleaning, our dental hygienists will carefully assess your oral health. If there s moderate tartar buildup, we ll remove it and clean all areas of your teeth, including the spaces below the gum line. This deep cleaning will help eliminate heavy calculus buildup on teeth that contributes to plaque accumulation and teeth decaying at the gum line.

Our goal is to prevent oral health issues before they start. Shiny teeth are often associated with good oral hygiene, but it s essential to remember that shiny teeth don t always indicate healthy teeth. Regular cleanings help remove harmful minerals that can cause decay and gum disease.

What to Expect After Your Cleaning

After your cleaning, you may feel some mild discomfort if you had significant calculus buildup below the gum line. This is normal and should resolve within a day or two. It s important to follow up with consistent at-home care, including brushing twice a day, flossing, and using mouthwash to maintain your oral health. Regular cleanings, along with proper oral hygiene habits, help prevent teeth decaying at the gum line and the buildup of harmful minerals that could lead to cavities.
