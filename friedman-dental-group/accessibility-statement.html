Accessibility Statement

Goal of being Accessible

Friedman Dental Group is committed to providing a positive and practicable website experience to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We continuously work to improve the accessibility of content on our website and our goal is to permit you to successfully gather information and transact business through our website whether you are using assistive technologies like a screen reader, a magnifier, voice recognition software, or captions for videos.

Accessibility Assistance

If you have difficulty using or accessing any element of this website, please feel free to call us at 877-421-0584 or email us at info@friedmandentalgroup.com , and we will work with you to provide the information you seek through a communication method that is accessible and best for you (for example, through telephone support).

Actions

Friedman Dental Group is currently taking a variety of steps and devoting resources to further enhance the accessibility of our website.

Ongoing Effort

At Friedman Dental Group we are proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone. We view accessibility as an ongoing effort and are continually seeking solutions that will bring all areas of our website to the same level of overall web accessibility.

Feedback

Please contact us at info@friedmandentalgroup.com if you have any feedback or suggestions as to how we could improve the accessibility of this website.
