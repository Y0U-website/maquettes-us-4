Our Office’s Dental Services

Is it time to refresh your smile? Consider cosmetic dentistry options like braces, porcelain veneers, custom dental crowns or dental bridges.

With the right mix of cosmetic treatments, you could completely change the way you look when you smile. Cosmetic dentistry can even make it easier for you to speak, chew or comfortably bite down.

Although most dentists agree with saving teeth whenever they can, some teeth have to be extracted. At our Tamarac office, we can handle both simple and complex tooth extractions onsite. This allows you to remove a cracked, decayed, broken or otherwise unwanted tooth in a familiar surrounding.

All oral surgery patients are given appropriate anesthesia and numbing agents to ensure a comfortable experience.

Though people often talk about root canals being uncomfortable, the highest discomfort comes before a specialist takes care of the tooth and its infected pulp. Patients who undergo our endodontic care at Friedman Dental Group in Tamarac are routinely surprised at how much better they feel.

Tooth pain is not normal. If you have sudden pressure or pain in or around a tooth, please get in touch with us for an X-ray and evaluation.

Our family dentistry treatments start at age eight and go up from there, allowing us to take care of everyone under your roof.

Having a family dentist in Tamarac allows you to stack appointments, saving you time. Additionally, all of your family’s dental health records will be located in one place.

If you need periodontic care, such as treatment for periodontal disease (advanced gum disease) or placement of dental implants , we can help.

Periodontal disease requires proper deep cleaning treatments, such as scaling and foot planing, to manage the infection. When gum disease has progressed into moderate to severe stages, tooth extraction may sometimes be necessary. However, with routine care, patients with gum disease can reverse to a healthy state allowing proper restorative and cosmetic solutions.

Every mouth has its unique challenges, which is why we offer full mouth reconstruction consultations at our Tamarac location. Get advice from knowledgeable dental professionals on your candidacy for full mouth reconstruction procedures such as extractions, placement of implants, crowns, bridges and many other services

Do you have tooth loss but not enough bone density to support dental implants? Give us a call to set up a consultation to see whether dental crowns and bridges could give you the smile you deserve. We have solutions for you.

Our in-house custom-crafted crowns and bridges mimic the look and function of natural teeth, restoring your appearance and confidence.

Are you tired of dealing with the challenges of traditional removable dentures? Full mouth dental implants are a permanent treatment that allow you to eat and chew comfortably..
