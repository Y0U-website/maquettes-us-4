Frequently Asked Questions

Restorative dentistry repairs or replaces teeth damaged by decay, disease, genetics, or accidents. At Friedman Dental, treatments such as crowns, bridges, inlays, and onlays rebuild your smile, restoring comfortable chewing, natural appearance, and long-term oral health.

You may need restorative treatment if you have cracked, chipped, decayed, or missing teeth, large or worn fillings, or discomfort when chewing. A thorough examination helps our team identify the problem and recommend the most suitable restoration.

The right treatment depends on how much of the tooth is damaged and your oral health goals. Inlays and onlays repair moderate damage, crowns cover and protect weakened teeth, and bridges replace missing teeth. Our specialists tailor each plan to your needs.
