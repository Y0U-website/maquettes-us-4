Dental Crowns

Many times teeth with large fillings break down over time under chewing force. Many times there is not enough tooth structure to hold another filling or the tooth is cracked. In these cases, a crown (or cap) made of enamel- like material is used to restore a tooth back to a great appearance and proper biting function.

What is Cerec? Cerec is a Chairside Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics made of enamel-like material. Translated, it means that Dr. Shuffield can restore damaged teeth in a single appointment using a high-quality ceramic material that matches the natural color of other teeth.
