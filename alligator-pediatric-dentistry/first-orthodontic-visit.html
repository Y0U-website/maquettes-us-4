Your First Orthondtic Visit

Your time is valuable, and so is your oral health. Our specialists will treat your appointments and mouth with the care and attention they deserve.

For your first time visit, we’ll show you around our facility and discuss any questions or concerns you may have. To better treat each patient, we take digital panoramic radiographs for a comprehensive and accurate model of your oral structure.

Once we’ve covered the basics, Dr. Mongillo will sit down with each patient and family to determine the best course of action for your orthodontic treatment. We’ll cover everything from the type of treatment, treatment duration, and scheduling to prepare you and your family for the most streamlined process as possible.

Mongillo Price Lock

If you or your child aren’t quite ready to undergo orthodontic treatment, the Mongillo Price Lock will keep the price of treatment to what you were quoted during your first exam. Regardless of what prices may be in the future, you are guaranteed the cost of your first quote.
