The practice

Contact us to schedule an appointment with an Idaho Falls pediatric dentist. We invite you and your child to experience the Alligator Difference and come away with a smile.

Our services include pediatric dental care, starting with infants through those 18 years old. We also offer adult orthodontics. From general cleanings to emergency care, learn what Alligator has to offer.

Infant Dental Exams Checking infants’ teeth is a preventative approach, as we value keeping ahead of potential dental problems. Let us help encourage good dental hygiene and routines through our age-appropriate infant dental exams.

Pediatric Sedation Dentistry Sedation options are available to support kids as they receive important dental care. We provide oral/conscious sedation, IV sedation, and general anesthesia. Our dentists can discuss when sedation dentistry is advisable, safety measures, and more.

Pediatric Laser Dentistry Laser dentistry allows for efficiency and precision as a minimally invasive approach to dental care. Laser dentistry can be used for the detection of cavities, in the application of fillings, in treating gum issues, and more.

Check out resources on brushing, flossing, fluoride, and more!

A particular aspect of dentistry is orthodontics, which focuses on correcting issues that primarily involve the teeth and jaw. Multiple orthodontic services are available at our office.

Early Intervention Early intervention —which applies to kids ages 7-11—can help eliminate the need for more invasive dental care when children get older. Early intervention can be important for resolving and detecting issues such as bite misalignment, overbite, and abnormal airway development.
