Dr. Chad J Barney DMD

Dr. Chad J Barney DMD , a Lewiston dentist, grew up in Richland, WA and went to Dental School in Portland, Oregon. He graduated from Oregon Health Sciences University in 1993 with a DMD degree. He practiced in Libby, Montana for a few years before moving to Lewiston Idaho and acquiring the practice of Roscoe Thoen, DDS in 1995. Dr. Chad J Barney is primarily engaged in the practice of preventative dentistry , restorative dentistry , cosmetic dentistry , periodontics as well as family dentistry .

You and your family are in good hands with Dr. Chad J Barney. He has more than two and a half decades of experience in dentistry, including 22 years as a dentist. His passion for providing comfortable wellness-focused dentistry has led him to provide a wide range of services, including dental cleanings, fluoride treatment, sealants, dental implants, composite fillings, inlays/onlays, crowns, bridges, teeth whitening, veneers, orthodontics, and periodontics. In addition, Dr. Chad J Barney is committed to keeping his practice up to date with the latest technologies to better serve you.

Dr. Chad J Barney is a member of the ADA and ISDA . He volunteers at the Snake River Community Clinic to help ensure individuals in need have proper health care. Dr. Barney also coaches little league fast pitch softball.

Dr. Chad J Barney and his wife Karrie have three children, Taryn, Loryn and Jenna. They all enjoy camping, fishing, and skiing in the winter. Dr. Chad J Barney loves the Lewiston-Clarkston Valley and cannot imagine living anywhere else.
