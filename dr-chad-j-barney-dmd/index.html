Crooked, unevenly spaced, or misaligned teeth are harder to keep clean, more prone to tooth decay and periodontal disease. Properly aligned teeth are easier to keep clean and provide a more attractive smile. A straight smile helps to boost self-esteem, self-confidence, and your self-image.
