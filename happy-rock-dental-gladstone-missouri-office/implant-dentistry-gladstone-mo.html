Procedure Steps

We perform the implant procedure in a series of steps, starting with consultation appointments, exams, and impressions and finishing with the implant and crown placement. The steps, in more detail, include the following:

The procedure is worth it! Call our staff to learn more about our tooth replacement services. For more information on our other restorative services, visit our page on CEREC dentistry- the most advanced way to get same-day dental restorations.

  • Before the procedure, we will numb the area with an anesthetic to prevent any uncomfortable sensitivity during the insertion of the implant.
  • A prosthetic root made of titanium is installed into the bone socket area where the old tooth root once resided. The implant will provide a “root” to which the prosthetic tooth will attach. Our dentists use the advanced Galileos digital implant planning to map out the placement of the implant before actual insertion. This technology means that the placement of the implant will always be successful and prevents unnecessary pain throughout the remainder of the procedure.
  • After some time, the gum and tissue around the new root will fuse together and become one solid base.
  • Once the root fuses with the jawbone, a piece called an abutment is attached on top of the implant. The abutment works like a connective piece between the implant and the crown and ensures a sturdy connection site.
  • The last step is to attach the prosthetic tooth to the abutment and adjust into place. Your new tooth will fit perfectly into the area where your old tooth resided, creating the most natural appearance. After the procedure, you should be able to resume normal activities. Over the counter pain medications can be taken to alleviate any discomfort or pain. You will most likely only experience very mild discomfort.
