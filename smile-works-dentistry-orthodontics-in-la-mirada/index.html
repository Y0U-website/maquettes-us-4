Smile Works Dentistry & Orthodontics is a family dental clinic serving La Mirada and the surrounding communities. Our office provides advanced care including General Dentistry , Orthodontics , Dental Implants , Periodontics , Oral Surgery , Clear Aligners , Traditional Braces , Sleep Apnea Treatment , and Smile Gallery .

Dentist - Oral Surgery and Implantology Dr. Mohammad Shiraz Haider has been practicing dentistry since 2012. He obtained his Doctorate in Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Southern California (USC) and went for further specialty training in oral surgery at Case Western Reserve University. He received his MD from Avalon University School of Medicine.

Dr. Haider has also received specialty training in Sleep Apnea and completed a residency in Sleep Medicine at the UCLA School of Dentistry. He is well versed in all aspects of cosmetic dentistry, implants, oral surgery, and sleep apnea. He has received numerous accolades for his clinical skills from USC, and won the outstanding student of the year award in the field of Oral Surgery in 2009.

Dr. Haider is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. Dr Haider is also a Diplomate of the American Board of Dental Sleep Medicine.

Prior to becoming a dentist, Dr. Haider worked as a software engineer and has his Masters in Computer Science from Cal State Fullerton University. He received his Bachelors degree in Computer Science from the University of California, Irvine.
