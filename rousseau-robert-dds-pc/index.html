Dr. Huy Nguyen knew he wanted to pursue dentistry from a young age. Growing up in Vietnam, he experienced the hardships of wartime and his father’s imprisonment as a military officer after the Fall of Saigon. In 1990, he and his family immigrated to the United States, where he realized his dream of becoming a dentist was within reach through hard work, passion, and dedication.

Dr. Nguyen received his DDS in 2002 from the Meharry Medical College School of Dentistry in Nashville, TN. After graduating, he served in the Air Force in Minot, ND, a life-shaping experience that deepened his passion for serving others.

With nearly 20 years of dental experience, Dr. Nguyen is dedicated to serving his community through a comprehensive biological and holistic approach to oral healthcare. He recognizes the critical connection between oral health and systemic wellness, focusing on treatments that support the body’s natural healing processes.
