Our Cosmetic Services in Yakima

Dr. Immel has been practicing dentistry since 1994 and brings decades of hands-on experience to every cosmetic case. He takes time to understand what each patient actually wants, not just what looks good on paper. That personal attention is what sets this small practice apart from larger dental offices.

The cosmetic treatments we offer include:

  • Teeth whitening takes years of staining from coffee, wine, and everyday wear and gives you a noticeably brighter smile in a single visit. It’s one of the fastest, most affordable ways to refresh your appearance.
  • Composite dental bonding is one of the most-used treatments in our practice. Using tooth-colored resin, Dr. Immel can repair chips, close small gaps, and reshape teeth, often in one appointment with no drilling required.
  • Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth to completely change their shape or color. Dr. Immel offers veneers for patients who are good candidates, though he focuses on recommending the right treatment rather than the most expensive one.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cosmetic Dentistry

  • What is the difference between composite bonding and porcelain veneers? Bonding uses tooth-colored resin applied directly to the tooth and is typically faster and more affordable. Veneers are custom-made porcelain shells that offer longer-lasting results for more significant changes.
  • How much does cosmetic dentistry cost in Yakima, WA? Costs vary depending on the treatment, with whitening being the most affordable and veneers carrying a higher price point. We encourage you to call our office at (509) 457-5387 so we can discuss options that fit your goals and budget.
  • Is cosmetic dentistry covered by dental insurance? Most cosmetic procedures are considered elective and are not covered by insurance, though some treatments like bonding may qualify if they also serve a restorative function. We can help you review your options during your consultation.
  • How long do cosmetic dental results last? Longevity depends on the treatment and how well you care for your teeth at home. Bonding typically lasts several years, while veneers can last ten to fifteen years or longer with proper maintenance.
