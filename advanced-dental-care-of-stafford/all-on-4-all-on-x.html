Overview

All-on-4 and All-on-X are advanced full-arch implant solutions that replace an entire set of missing or failing teeth with a secure, fixed restoration. Instead of placing an implant for every tooth, four to six implants are used to anchor a full arch of teeth, designed to restore strength, comfort, and confidence in fewer visits.
