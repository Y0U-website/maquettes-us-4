Traditional Metal Braces

Metal braces are made up of sleek and thin medical-grade stainless steel.

A common misconception is that metal braces are bulky and uncomfortable while this may have been true with the metal braces of yesteryear, modern metal braces are much more comfortable and attractive.

Today’s braces are much smaller and more discreet than those of the past, although they’re still much more noticeable than clear alternatives.

Braces can also be customized by color, which is perfect for children wishing to express themselves. New innovations in wire technology mean braces move your teeth much more quickly now than they did in the past.

Traditional metal braces are extremely durable, making them an ideal treatment option for extensive cases, or for individuals who regularly engage in contact sports.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces were introduced to provide a more discrete and aesthetically pleasing alternative to traditional metal braces.

Rather than being made up of stainless steel, ceramic braces are made up of polycrystalline alumina and are commonly tooth-colored or completely clear. The wires are typically available in clear or tooth-colored options to provide the most discrete overall look possible.

While ceramic braces are an excellent option for those who are looking for less-visible orthodontic treatment, they may not be an ideal option for patients with extensive cases, or for those who engage in contact sports, as they are not as durable as traditional metal braces.
