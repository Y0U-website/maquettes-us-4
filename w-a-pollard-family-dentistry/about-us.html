Our Dentistry Philosophy

Healthy Mouth, Healthy Body

At Pollard Family Dentistry, we focus on dental health to improve overall wellness to reduce the risk of diabetes, heart disease, stroke, and other serious illnesses that can cause life-threatening health issues.

Dr. William Pollard focuses his Conway dental practice on helping patients improve their oral health and overall wellbeing with preventative, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Our goal to help our patients have a beautiful smile and live a happy, healthy life.

Dr. Pollard has been helping patients achieve healthy smiles in Conway since 1978, building a practice that is focused on conservative, practical treatment to help improve oral health and overall wellness. He is a graduate of Hendrix College and the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry, and is a lifetime member of the Arkansas State Dental Association and the American Dental Association. He and his wife live in Conway and have one daughter, who is also a dentist. Outside the office he enjoys hiking, biking, photography and researching Arkansas railroad history.

Relaxed Enviroment

We provide treatments in a relaxed, comfortable environment. We also offer nitrous oxide gas and anesthesia-free cosmetic procedures.

Better Health

All of our instruments are autoclaved (heat sterilized) according to the latest recommendations of the American Dental Association and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention.

Quality Treatment

We focus on conservative treatment methods focused on keeping teeth as naturally healthy as possible while restoring and treating any issues as they arise.

Certified Dentists

Our staff are certified dental professionals with decades of experience serving patients in the Central Arkansas area. We believe that you’re part of the team when it comes to caring your health.

Stressed about visiting the dentist?

Our relaxed and friendly Conway dentist office offers a stress-free experience for our patients. We offer nitrous oxide gas if necessary to make your visit more comfortable.

Insurance & Payment

We accept most traditional dental insurance programs, and can save you paperwork by submitting claims directly to your carrier. We are a participating provider for Delta Dental, Blue Cross, Aetna, Metlife and other insurance plans. Our staff will be happy to answer any questions you may have concerning coverage, your deductible, or other insurance details. We also offer CareCredit financing for patients who prefer to spread payments over a longer time period.
