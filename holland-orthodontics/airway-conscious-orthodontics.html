Airway Conscious Orthodontics

By considering these factors, we can design treatment plans that support both a healthy smile and a healthy airway.

For children, early orthodontic evaluations allow us to guide jaw growth and create space for proper airway development. When orthodontic expansion is indicated, the best time to expand is between 8-10 years of age. These ensure the best orthopoedic outcome and can help support better breathing, improved sleep, and overall development.

Airway-conscious orthodontics isn’t just for children. Teens and adults can also benefit from treatments that improve alignment while considering airway function. In some cases, we collaborate with other healthcare providers to ensure a comprehensive, multidisciplinary approach.

We are committed to treating the whole patient—not just the teeth. By integrating airway-focused principles into orthodontic care, we aim to help you achieve a smile that looks great, feels great, and supports your long-term health.

If you’re interested in learning more about how airway-conscious orthodontics can benefit you or your child, we invite you to schedule a consultation with our team.
