White Crowns for Children

Because pediatric zirconia crowns are prefabricated, placement requires precise tooth preparation and careful technique. When placed correctly by a pediatric dentist, white crowns offer excellent durability and aesthetics for primary teeth that must remain functional until they naturally fall out.

  • Restore proper chewing and function
  • Resist wear, staining, and chipping
  • Provide a natural, white appearance

White Zirconia Crowns vs. Stainless Steel Crowns

We offer both white zirconia crowns and stainless steel crowns , and each has appropriate indications depending on the tooth and clinical situation. Also, important note Ozone is used on all teeth that are prepared for crowns with the intention to decrease bacterial load and increase success of treatment!

White Zirconia Crowns (White Crowns)

In our professional experience, white zirconia crowns are often the preferred option , particularly when aesthetics, gum health, and a metal-free restoration are priorities.

  • Tooth-colored and highly aesthetic
  • Strong and durable
  • Resistant to staining and plaque accumulation
  • Well tolerated by surrounding gum tissue
  • Require precise placement and moisture control
  • Less forgiving during placement
  • Typically higher cost than stainless steel crowns

Stainless Steel Crowns

Stainless steel crowns remain a valuable and effective option in pediatric dentistry, especially in cases where durability, efficiency, and a positive patient experience are the primary considerations.

  • Extremely durable and time-tested
  • More forgiving during placement
  • Often the most economical crown option
  • Highly reliable in certain clinical situations
  • Silver in color and visible when smiling
  • Not tooth-colored
  • Some families prefer a metal-free alternative when appropriate
