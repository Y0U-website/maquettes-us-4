Island Dental Associates – Full-Mouth All-on-X Dental Implants FAQ

All-on-X (including All-on-4) is a full-arch tooth replacement system that uses four or more strategically placed dental implants to support a fixed, natural-looking set of teeth. This solution is ideal for patients who are missing most or all of their teeth. To learn whether you qualify, call Franklin Square at (516) 565-6565 or Levittown at (516) 865-6565 to schedule an evaluation.

All-on-X provides a permanent, stable full-arch restoration that doesn’t shift or require adhesive. Unlike dentures, All-on-X implants help preserve jawbone and restore stronger chewing ability. If you’re comparing options, our implant team can guide you — call (516) 565-6565 or (516) 865-6565 to book a consultation.

Most patients missing several teeth or currently wearing dentures may be candidates. Even those with bone loss often qualify thanks to angled implant placement. A full exam and 3D scan at our office will determine suitability. To schedule your assessment, contact Franklin Square or Levittown today.

Many patients receive a temporary fixed arch of teeth on the same day implants are placed. After healing, a durable final prosthesis — often zirconia — is delivered. If you want to know whether “teeth-in-a-day” is possible for your case, call (516) 565-6565 or (516) 865-6565 .

The implants themselves are designed to last decades with proper care. Costs vary based on the number of implants, materials used, and whether additional procedures are needed. Island Dental Associates offers transparent pricing, 0 % financing , and flexible payment plans. Call either location for a personalized cost estimate.

If you’re considering full-mouth dental implants or want a long-term alternative to dentures, Island Dental Associates can help. Call Franklin Square (516) 565-6565 or Levittown (516) 865-6565 to schedule your free All-on-X consultation and review your treatment options.
