Island Dental Associates – Orthodontist FAQ

An orthodontist specializes in diagnosing and correcting misaligned teeth and bite issues using braces, Clear Aligners, and other corrective treatments. While general dentists focus on overall oral health, orthodontists receive additional training to manage alignment problems, facial growth patterns, and complex bite adjustments.

Common signs include crooked or crowded teeth, bite problems (overbite, underbite, crossbite), gaps, early tooth loss in children, difficulty chewing, or shifting teeth as an adult. A consultation at Island Dental Associates can determine whether orthodontic treatment is recommended.

Island Dental Associates provides a full range of orthodontic services, including traditional metal braces, ceramic (clear) braces, Invisalign®, teen orthodontics, adult orthodontics, and early intervention treatment for children. We customize every plan based on age, alignment needs, and lifestyle preferences.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a screening by age 7. Early evaluation allows us to identify developing issues and determine whether early treatment may help guide jaw growth or prevent more complex problems later.

Treatment duration varies depending on alignment needs. Most orthodontic cases take 12–24 months , although mild alignment issues may require less time. After your evaluation, we provide a detailed, personalized timeline.
