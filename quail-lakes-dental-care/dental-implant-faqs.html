Dental Implant FAQs

Answers to Your Most Common Dental Implant Questions

At Quail Lakes Dental Care, we understand that patients have many questions when considering dental implants. Below, we’ve answered some of the most frequently asked questions to help you make an informed decision about this life-changing procedure.

Dental implants are a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. They consist of a titanium post that is surgically placed in the jawbone, acting as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant fuses with the bone, a custom crown is attached, restoring the appearance and function of a natural tooth.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. It’s essential to maintain good oral hygiene and schedule regular check-ups to ensure the longevity of your implants.

The implant procedure itself is typically performed under local anesthesia, ensuring you feel little to no discomfort during surgery. Some mild soreness or swelling may occur afterward, but this can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain medications.

The dental implant process varies depending on your individual case. Generally, it can take several months from the initial consultation to the final placement of the crown. This timeline allows the implant to fully integrate with the jawbone, providing a stable foundation for your new tooth.

Most patients with healthy gums and adequate bone density are good candidates for dental implants. However, certain health conditions, such as gum disease or insufficient bone density, may require additional treatments like bone grafting to prepare for implants. During your consultation at Quail Lakes Dental Care, we will thoroughly evaluate your oral health to determine if implants are the right solution for you.

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential to maintaining the health of your implants and surrounding teeth. Avoiding smoking and following your dentist’s aftercare instructions will also help ensure the longevity of your implants.

Coverage for dental implants varies by insurance plan. Some plans may cover part of the procedure, such as the crown or related treatments, while others may not. Our team at Quail Lakes Dental Care will work with you to review your insurance benefits and provide a detailed cost breakdown.

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 %. The key to success is choosing an experienced dental team and following proper care guidelines after the procedure. At Quail Lakes Dental Care, we use advanced techniques to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients.
