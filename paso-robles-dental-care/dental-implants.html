The Process of Getting Dental Implants

Schedule a visit to our office to discuss your dental history and determine if implants are the right solution for you. Dr. Holbrook will perform a comprehensive examination, including X-rays or 3D scans.

Based on your specific needs, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan outlining the steps and timeline for your dental implant procedure.

Dr. Holbrook will surgically place the implant into your jawbone. This is typically done under local anesthesia, however, we also have sedation options available for your comfort.

After the surgery, you will have to wait a few months for the implant to heal. This healing process is known as osseointegration and is an essential part of the dental implant process as it allows the implant to fuse with the jawbone to provide a stable foundation for the replacement tooth.

Once the implant has fully integrated, an abutment (a connector piece) is attached to hold the replacement tooth.

After we place your abutment, we will place a custom-made crown. The crown is the final step of the dental implant process and gives your smile the official natural look and function of an actual tooth.

Dental Implants Aftercare

Taking care of your dental implants is crucial for their longevity and success. Here are some tips for effective aftercare:

  • Maintain Good Oral Hygiene: Brush and floss your teeth regularly, paying special attention to the implant area to prevent plaque buildup.
  • Regular Dental Checkups: Schedule routine checkups with Dr. Holbrook to monitor the health and stability of your implants.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: Refrain from chewing on hard foods or objects that could damage your implants or surrounding teeth.
  • Quit Smoking: Smoking can hinder the healing process and increase the risk of implant failure.
