Dental Implants at Point Clear…

  • Personalized Treatment Plan: Your dentist designs a custom plan tailored to your oral health, bone structure, and smile needs.
  • Implant Placement: A small titanium post is gently placed into the jawbone, acting as a secure foundation for your new tooth.
  • Healing & Integration: Over several months, the implant naturally fuses with the bone, creating long-term stability.
  • Abutment Placement: Once healed, a connector piece is added to support the final restoration.
  • Custom Crown: A natural-looking crown is attached, restoring function, comfort, and confidence.
  • What types of implants are available? The most common type, endosteal implants, are small titanium posts placed directly into the jawbone to support a single crown, bridge, or denture. For patients missing many or all teeth, All-on-4 implants provide a full-arch restoration using just four strategically placed implants, often with faster healing and fewer surgeries. Your dentist will evaluate your oral health, bone structure, and goals to recommend the implant option that restores comfort, function, and confidence for a smile that feels secure and looks beautifully natural.
  • How long do dental implants last? Dental implants are designed for longevity. With good oral hygiene and regular dental visits, the implant post itself can often last 20 years or more, and in many cases, a lifetime. The visible crown attached to the implant may need to be replaced after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear, just like natural teeth. Factors such as smoking, gum health, grinding, and certain medical conditions can affect longevity. Our team will help you protect your investment with personalized care and maintenance tips.
