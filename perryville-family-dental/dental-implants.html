Dental Implants

Just because you lose a tooth doesn’t mean you’re doomed forever to an embarrassing smile or difficulty chewing. Dental implants are a practical and permanent solution that our team can provide by surgically placing an anchor for a replacement tooth.

At Perryville Family Dental , implants happen in three phases:

1. A skilled dentist places the post into the jawbone 2. The area heals and undergoes osseointegration, which means it combines with the bone to create a solid foundation. 3. An artificial tooth is placed securely onto the implant.

At times, implant dentures are often the best solution for our patients. Implant dentures are a permanent, effective, attractive, and secure way to fill gaps in your smile— all while preserving your existing teeth . In addition to eliminating the need for paste or adhesives to stay in place, implant dentures can also help prevent bone loss in your jaw and are capable of fully restoring your ability to chew and speak.
