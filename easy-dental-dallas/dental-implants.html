The Dental Implant Process at Our Clinic in Dallas, TX

While the dental implant process typically takes a few months from start to finish, the lasting results—a strong, natural-looking smile—are well worth the time.

Your transformation begins with a thorough consultation where Dr. Sandhu, our dental implant dentist in Dallas, evaluates your oral health using advanced digital scans to create your personalized roadmap. Together, you’ll explore all your options, whether you need a single tooth replacement or a comprehensive solution like All-on-4® implants.

If your jawbone isn’t thick enough, Dr. Sandhu may recommend a bone graft before placing the implant.

The next step involves a minor surgical procedure to place the titanium implant post in your jawbone. Over the following months, a remarkable biological process called osseointegration occurs as your bone gradually bonds with the implant, creating an incredibly stable foundation, just like natural tooth roots.

Once fully healed, we’ll complete your smile by attaching a custom-designed crown that matches your natural teeth perfectly in both form and function.

Every step of your dental implant journey at our Dallas practice is carefully planned to deliver comfort, precision, and ultimately—a smile that looks, feels, and functions exactly how it should.
