How to Schedule To request an appointment, please text the appropriate keyword to 650-922-8005:

​Please include your name, email address, and a brief description of your symptoms.

TMJ, Chronic Headache, and Orofacial Pain consultations are scheduled on a rolling three-month basis and are often fully booked in advance. Patients who text FACIAL PAIN will be added to our waitlist and notified when new appointments become available or if an earlier appointment opens.

Limited emergency and after-hours appointments may be available for an additional fee. Please text URGENT to 650-922-8005 with a brief description of your symptoms, and we will determine whether an expedited appointment may be appropriate.
