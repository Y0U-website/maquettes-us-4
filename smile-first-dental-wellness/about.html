About Smile First

A name with a story. A practice with a soul.

it came from Dr. Cristian watching his father Dr. Pavel Sr. — who emigrated from Romania in 1986 with a medical degree and a dream. He grew up sweeping the floors and cleaning out alginate trays at his practice. When Dr. Cristian took it over, he named it Smile First because that’s what his father always did.

Now he’s a new father. What Dr. Ilie passed down to him isn’t just a practice. It’s a way of showing up. A set of values. Dental practices across this country are being bought up by private equity, patients treated like appointments on a conveyor belt. We don’t have investors to answer to. We have three dentists across two generations, a mother who has ran this office before Dr. Cristian, and a commitment to knowing your name before we know your insurance number. Staying independent isn’t just a values decision — it’s an operational one!

Meet Our Professionals

Meet our dedicated professionals, committed to your smile’s wellbeing. From preventive care to advanced treatments, we’re here for you.

From nutrition to intuition, our team is well endowed in the most successful ways of obtaining optimal oral health and overall health.

Dr. Cristian and Dr. Danielle are widely known as The Dental Yogis in the dental community. They lecture on health and wellness throughout the country and they also host semi-annual dental-aid yoga retreats in the Dominican Republic with the intention of helping dentists improve their personal health/wellness while also providing free dentistry to underserved villagers of the DR.
