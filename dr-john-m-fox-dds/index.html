Nathan Littauer Hospital & Nursing Home offers regular tours to the general public and to the community every Thursday at 10 a.m. and one Thursday evening at 5 p.m. Customized times may be requested as well. Nathan Littauer cordially invites anyone to come and visit the hospital campus for a tour.

Tour requests must be made one week prior to your desired time of tour. Please understand there may be times due to hospital events, infection control protocol, etc., when tour requests cannot be accommodated. Due to patient privacy laws, photography is not allowed during the tour.
