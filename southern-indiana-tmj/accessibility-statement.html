Accessibility Statement

  • Continuous Improvement: We are continually reviewing and updating our website to enhance accessibility and address any issues that arise.
  • Training and Awareness: Our team is trained in accessibility best practices to ensure that all new features and content are as accessible as possible.

Measures to Support Accessibility

  • Regular accessibility audits and testing with both automated tools and human evaluations.
  • Implementing feedback from users to resolve accessibility issues.
  • Ensuring that our content is structured and coded to support assistive technologies.
  • Providing alternative text for images, transcripts for audio/video content, and ensuring keyboard navigability throughout our site.

Known Limitations

While we work to ensure a fully accessible experience, some parts of the website may not yet be fully optimized. We are aware of these issues and are actively working on improvements. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website, please let us know.

Feedback and Contact Information

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at office@southernindianatmj.com . We will make every effort to respond to your concerns and suggestions as quickly as possible.

Updates to This Statement

This statement was last updated on July 30, 2025. We may update this statement periodically to reflect changes to our website or improvements in our accessibility practices.

Thank you for visiting Southern Indiana TMJ . We are dedicated to ensuring that our website remains accessible and user-friendly for everyone.
