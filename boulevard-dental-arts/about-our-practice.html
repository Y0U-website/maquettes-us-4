Our Services Include

Cosmetic Dentistry and Bonding

Dental bonding is used to repair various types of tooth damage, such as cracks, gaps, and breaks. The procedure adheres a tooth-colored resin (durable plastic material) to the natural tooth. The resin is cured using a visible blue light that “bonds” the material to the tooth. The result is an improvement in the overall appearance of the teeth. Dental bonding is a relatively simple cosmetic dental procedure that preserves the most amount of the tooth’s enamel.

Crown & Bridges

A dental crown, is a type of restoration that completely caps or encases a damaged tooth. The crown can be made from a variety of materials, including metals and porcelain. The crown is fabricated from an impression of the tooth, using strong materials. The crown is bonded to the tooth using dental cement. A bridge is a type of restoration that is fixed (permanently attached). It is used to replace one or more missing teeth with an artificial tooth by permanently joining the artificial tooth to the adjacent teeth.

Laminates / Veneers

Dental veneers (also known as porcelain veneers or dental porcelain laminates) are a thin, custom-made shell designed to cover the front of the teeth to improve your appearance. Veneers are used for a variety of cosmetic corrections, ranging from teeth whitening to orthodontic adjustments. The veneers are bonded to the teeth, changing their color, shape, and size.

Dental Implants, Dentures, & Partials

Dentures are prosthetic devices used to replace missing teeth. Conventional dentures are removable (partial dentures or complete dentures); partial dentures are used when only some teeth are missing on a particular arch, and complete dentures are used when all the teeth are missing on an arch. Other denture designs rely on bonding or clasping replacement teeth onto teeth (crown and bridge work) or dental implants (over dentures).

Bleaching/Teeth Whitening

Your teeth can get brighter more quickly with professional teeth whitening. Generally speaking, the bleaching solution is far stronger than what comes in at-home kits. Additionally, your dentist may accelerate and enhance the teeth-whitening procedure by using heat, light, or a combination of the two.
