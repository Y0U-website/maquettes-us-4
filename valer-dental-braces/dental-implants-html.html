What Are Dental Implants?

Designed to mimic natural tooth roots, dental implants are small titanium posts that look like screws but fit into the jawbone, fusing over the course of 4-6 months. Their ability to restore function and last 30+ years make them far superior to other forms of tooth replacement, especially since they are versatile in treating all types of tooth loss.

Every patient’s dental implant journey is unique, but the steps we follow are carefully designed to ensure the best possible success. Initial Dental Implant Consultation

Your journey begins with a one-on-one consultation with our experienced implant dentists. We’ll thoroughly examine your oral health, overall health, and the condition of your jawbone to determine if you’re ready for implants.

If needed, we may recommend treatments such as gum disease treatment or bone grafting to prepare your mouth for surgery. Every detail of your personalized treatment plan will be carefully explained, including the steps ahead and financial options.

Dental Implant Surgery

Unlike many practices that refer you out, we perform your dental implant surgery right here in our office. To ensure you stay comfortable, we’ll begin by administering local anesthesia. Then, we’ll make small incisions in your gums and precisely place the implant posts into the best areas of your jawbone for stability.

Once in place, your gums will be closed, and healing caps will be added to protect your implants during the recovery phase. With everything done under one roof, you’ll save time and enjoy continuity of care.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

Next comes a crucial healing period of about 4 to 6 months. During this time, your implants will undergo osseointegration a natural process where they fuse with your jawbone to create a lasting foundation.

After healing, you’ll return to our office for a second minor procedure to place abutments. These small metal connectors will attach your new teeth to the implant posts. We’ll also take precise impressions to design your custom restoration, ensuring a seamless fit and natural look.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Finally, after you’re fully healed, it’s time for the big reveal! Depending on how many teeth you’re replacing, we’ll provide a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture that’s securely attached to your implants. We’ll make sure your bite feels right and your smile looks fantastic. Once everything is perfect, you’ll leave our office ready to enjoy the full benefits of your restored teeth.

Day-to-Day Benefits

If you struggle to accept how your grin looks in the mirror after tooth loss or have difficulty with basic tasks, like speaking and eating, you’re in luck! Dental implants can enhance your day-to-day experience by:

  • Restoring bite force. Unlike dentures and dental bridges, this treatment can return an estimated 80 % to 100 % of your original bite force, so you can enjoy all your favorite meals.
  • Improving enunciation. They fill in the spaces left by missing teeth so you can pronounce different sounds and words clearly and be understood easily.
  • Boosting confidence. The crown (or other appropriate restoration) capping your implant is made from tooth-colored materials that can be customized to look lifelike to raise your self-esteem.

Health Benefits

These prosthetic teeth also provide additional benefits to your oral and overall well-being that may be less obvious, but just as significant, such as:

  • Preserving your jawbone. Your jaw thins after tooth loss without roots to stimulate new bone growth to keep it healthy, but the titanium rod of your implant fulfills this function to sustain a strong foundation for your teeth and gums, preventing additional tooth loss.
  • Maintaining your facial structure. Supporting your jawbone keeps your facial muscles and ligaments from growing slack, which causes sunken, hollow cheeks, a jutting jawline, and additional facial wrinkles.
  • Preventing oral issues. It’s easy to brush and floss them to avoid common dental concerns, like gum disease or tooth decay, which often occur with alternatives like dentures or dental bridges.

Long-Term Benefits

One of the reasons that many experts in Albuquerque consider dental implants the ideal way to rebuild smiles is because of their incredible strength and resilience. Because they’re firmly embedded in your jawbone, you can enjoy the following long-term benefits:

  • Extended lifespan. Depending on how well you care for them, these tend to last anywhere from 10 to 25+ years, longer than the 5 to 10+ years you can expect with dentures and dental bridges.
  • High success rate. This procedure has an incredibly high success rate of more than 90 % even years after its initial placement, meaning you’re unlikely to experience failure as long as you follow our team’s post-op instructions.
  • Save money over time. There’s no need to invest in additional accessories, like adhesives or special cleaning solutions, that can add up over the years. Plus, implants are less likely to sustain chips, cracks, and other damage than the alternatives, so you won’t have to pay for costly repairs or replacements.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

As we mentioned above, dental implants can help patients at all different stages of tooth loss. In order to determine if you’re a good candidate, we will need to take X-rays, conduct a comprehensive oral exam, review your medical history, and learn about your lifestyle. With this information, we can determine several important factors, including the density of your jawbone, the health of your gums, and your willingness to commit to good habits, like brushing and flossing consistently.

If we determine that dental implants are the best tooth-replacement solution for you, then we will get to work on the next step: creating your custom treatment plan.

Missing One Tooth

If you are only missing one tooth, then we will likely recommend a single dental implant. That starts with inserting the post directly into your jawbone and ends with attaching a custom crown on top. Not only does this effectively replace both the root and crown of your missing tooth, but it also restores some of the strength of your bite that you lost. Plus, with the right maintenance, your dental implant can last for 30+ years!

Missing Multiple Teeth

Are you missing several teeth in a row? If so, you can opt for an implant bridge, which anchors the replacement teeth securely in position with two dental implants. If you are missing several teeth throughout your mouth, then we will either replace each gap with a single dental implant or create a partial implant denture. Both can effectively replace a large number of missing teeth in a way that looks and feels completely natural.

Missing All Teeth

The loss of all your teeth can make everything from smiling confidently to eating a healthy, well-balanced diet very difficult. Fortunately, that’s where implant dentures come in. In this case, your restoration will be secured in place with several strategically placed dental implants. One of the biggest perks of this tooth-replacement solution is that it can last for several decades with proper care. Plus, the entire restoration is custom-made with your face shape, dental needs, and smile goals in mind, ensuring that it doesn’t feel bulky or look unnatural.

Dental Implant Post-Op Instructions

The recovery process that follows your dental implant placement surgery allows your prosthetic device to form a strong bond with your jawbone, establishing the sturdy foundation it needs to exert maximum chewing power and last for decades. We’ll be sure to set you up with detailed aftercare instructions so you know how to keep your healing process smooth and complication-free. Here’s a quick look at the best ways to make sure your recovery stays on track from the friendly team at Valer Dental.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

It is essential to make sure the blood clot that forms over the surgical site remains undisturbed during the healing process. This scab being broken or dislodged will expose the healing tissue to the germs and irritants in the air and oral environment, resulting in a severely painful condition called dry socket. To prevent this, stick to the following rules:

  • Refrain from spitting or drinking through a straw
  • Abstain from tobacco use for at least the first day
  • Avoid touching the surgical area with your fingers or tongue
  • Be careful when brushing and flossing near the surgical sites

Common Side Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

You can expect to experience some mild side effects for at least the first few days after receiving dental implants. These may include:

  • Intermittent bleeding that can be reduced by applying light pressure through gauze.
  • Swelling that may last up to a week. You may be able to reduce it with a cold or warm compress.
  • General discomfort that can be relieved with prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

You’ll want to stick to soft, mild foods for several days after receiving dental implants. A few great options include:

  • Pasta
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Pudding
  • Soup that isn’t hot
  • Ice Cream
  • Yogurt
  • Mashed potatoes

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

You can resume brushing your teeth the day after your surgery, but it’s important to be careful when cleaning near the surgical area. It’s best to rinse your mouth with salt water several times a day, preferably after meals, and you may also receive prescription mouthwash. Refrain from using mouthwash products that are high in alcohol such as Scope or Listerine.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

You’ll be able to begin using your smile normally as soon as you are fitted with your restoration. You may experience some mild and temporary sensitivity after you receive your denture, bridge, or dental crown, but this will be easy to control with pain medication and should fade away on its own after several days. You should experience no swelling or bleeding, but please contact our office if you develop symptoms like these or if your discomfort doesn’t begin to fade after several days.

Dental Implant FAQs

What can cause dental implants to fail?

Since dental implants have a success rate of over 95 %, failure is pretty rare. When it does happen, it’s usually due to peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease) or failed osseointegration (when the jawbone and dental implant don’t fuse properly). There are several steps we will take to prevent either from happening, starting with assessing the density of your jawbone during the initial exam and extending to recommending that you quit smoking and other harmful habits.

Is there anything I shouldn’t eat with dental implants?

We will provide you with a few dietary restrictions following the procedure, such as sticking to plain yogurt, applesauce, and other extremely soft foods. After that, however, you’ll be able to enjoy all of your favorite meals and snacks again! All we ask is that you steer clear of extremely crunchy items, like raw almonds, peanut brittle, and ice cubes, since, like your natural teeth, they can break with enough pressure.

Am I too old to get dental implants?

Sometimes, patients in their 70s, 80s, and 90s assume that they are too old to rebuild their smiles with dental implants in Albuquerque. However, candidacy isn’t determined by age. Rather, we need to consider the strength of your jawbone, the health of your gums, and your commitment to taking good care of your smile. So, if you are ready to face tooth loss head-on and are interested in this state-of-the-art solution, don’t let your age stop you from scheduling a consultation with our team at Valer Dental!

Will people be able to tell that I have dental implants?

Not only are dental implants secured to your jawbone, but they are topped with restorations that are custom-made to suit your face shape, dental needs, and smile goals. As a result, they look and feel extremely natural! All that to say, you don’t have to worry no unwanted attention will be drawn to your smile.

Can dental implants replace molars?

They can. In fact, they can replace all of your molars!
