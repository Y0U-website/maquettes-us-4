Is an implant right for you?

Most adults with a missing or failing tooth are candidates — the consultation and 3D scan confirm gum health and bone support before you commit to anything. A consultation is required to confirm candidacy and your treatment plan.

Good candidates

A missing tooth, a cracked tooth or one that can t be saved, a failing crown or an old bridge that s giving out — and healthy gums. Bone volume is checked on the scan.

A tooth lost years ago

There are still options. Bone thins where a tooth has been missing a while; the scan measures it, and grafting is how it is rebuilt when a case needs it.

An implant placed elsewhere

If an implant from another office never felt right, bring it to the consultation. Dr. Chon examines it on the 3D scan and tells you plainly what it needs.

How long it lasts

Kept clean, the titanium post can last for decades; the crown on top is the part to expect to replace once along the way. A bridge or denture, by contrast, usually gets redone over a lifetime.

Aftercare is simple

Brush, floss, and keep your six-month checkups. An implant is cared for like a natural tooth.

4-year care policy

We stand behind our implant work with a four-year care policy, kept active with routine six-month maintenance.

Replacing two or three teeth

Most of the implant work at In Line is one to three teeth — a single gap, or a small run of teeth lost together. Each implant is placed and priced the same way, so a two- or three-tooth plan is the same treatment repeated, not a different procedure with a different rulebook. Where several teeth in a row are missing, the plan often needs fewer implants than there are missing teeth, and it is priced as a plan rather than by counting teeth. Dr. Chon maps the whole sequence at the consultation so you know the order, the timeline, and the total before anything starts.

One tooth

A single implant in the gap. The teeth either side are left completely untouched — no reshaping, no crowns on healthy teeth.

Two or three teeth

Each gap gets its own implant, placed in one surgical visit where the spacing allows. One recovery instead of several.

A tooth that is failing

Cracked or badly decayed teeth can sometimes be removed and the implant placed the same day. The 3D scan is what decides it.

A full arch or dentures

Start with a full-arch and denture consultation with Dr. Chon. He explains snap-on and fixed options and tells you plainly whether your case is treated here or by a specialist he refers you to.

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. Once healed, it holds an abutment and a porcelain crown — so the finished tooth looks, feels, and works like a natural one.

Typically three to six months start to finish: placement is a single visit, the implant then integrates with the bone over a few months, and the final crown is placed once it s solid. Dr. Chon maps your own timeline at the consultation, and you re never left without a plan for the gap in between.

Yes. We stand behind our implant work with a four-year care policy, kept active with routine six-month maintenance visits.

The placement itself is done under local anesthetic to numb the area. If dental work makes you anxious, In Line offers nitrous oxide sedation — tell Dr. Chon at the consultation and it is arranged before the day. Most patients describe the recovery as easier than a tooth extraction, and manage it with over-the-counter pain relief.
