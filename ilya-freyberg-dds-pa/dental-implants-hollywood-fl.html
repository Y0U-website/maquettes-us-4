Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Discreet Unlike removable dentures, dental implants are completely indistinguishable from your natural teeth. Dental implants don’t slip and they don’t need to be removed. They’re just like your real teeth!
  • Permanent Solution Getting dental implants done at our practice in Hollywood FL means you’re opting for a permanent solution. This is the most convenient and effective way to restore your smile.
  • Bone Loss Prevention Tooth loss causes jawbone loss. Jawbone loss can cause facial collapse, making you look older. Dental implants are the only method of teeth restoration that stimulates the jaw just like your natural teeth, dental implants prevent bone loss and increase the overall quality of your face structure.
  • Restored Bite Force Dental implants allow you to use just as much bite force as your natural teeth. This isn’t possible with dentures, which require you to be careful with the food you eat and how you chew. Implants are the worry-free option for teeth restoration.
  • Prevent Face Shape Changes Your teeth help support your facial structure. Tooth loss eventually changes the appearance of your face, making it look less youthful. Dental implants provide structural support like your natural teeth. They successfully prevent the changes in your facial structure that would occur otherwise.
  • Easy To Care For There’s no need to buy special products or devices for cleaning your implants. A standard routine of correctly brushing and flossing will maintain the great appearance of your implants.

How It Works

  • Step 1: Examination The first step is an examination. You’ll come to our Hollywood, FL practice for a check-up. We will examine your overall oral health and check for gum disease. We will also examine the part of your jaw, which will receive the implant to see if the bone is strong enough. In some cases, a bone graft might be needed before the implant.
  • Step 2: Post Insertion The process of dental implants begins by inserting titanium posts in the bone of your jaw. They act as support for your dental implant. After this procedure, we’ll wait for this area to heal. During the healing process, the bone in your jaw will bond with the titanium posts.
  • Step 3: Abutment The next step is essential for connecting the titanium post that is now healed with the custom made porcelain tooth . During this phase, the abutment (the connecting piece) is inserted. Then the area is once again left to heal completely.
  • Step 4: Artificial Tooth Once the abutment has healed completely you’re ready to have your replacement tooth fixed securely on top. This tooth will be indistinguishable from your natural teeth and will act the same as your natural teeth. You will care for it exactly the same as for the rest of your teeth too
