Choose the Dayton-area dental practice that’s always dedicated to you, your time and your smile. Learn more about us and schedule an appointment for you or a loved one today!

Dedicated to You

We like to maximize quality over quantity at Today’s Dental, and so we always dedicate plenty of time for you and your loved ones. We’ll never rush you in and out so we can get someone else in the chair when you are in our office, you are our one and only focus. Maybe that’s why Dr. Allport and our team have won the local Best of the Best award every year running.

We Value Your Time

When you’re at the dentist’s office, we know there are approximately 1,001 other places you could be instead but we’re always glad you’re here. To enhance your experience and keep you coming back for routine care, we promise to always run right on time, for every single appointment. Life is far too short to be spent waiting in the dentist’s office. We promise to get you in, out, and on your way smiling better than before.

Same-Day Emergency Dental Care

While it would be much easier if you could, you can’t always plan for when you’ll need dental care. If something goes wrong with the oral health of you or someone you love, stay calm knowing that Dr. Allport and our team can usually see dental emergencies on the same day. Call our office as soon as possible we’ll plan on a time for you to come in as soon as possible given your situation, and provide advice for what you can do in the meantime.

Complimentary Sedation

If you’re known to be a little jittery in the dental chair, we offer complimentary sedation to help you relax. Nitrous oxide, more commonly known as laughing gas, helps you float through any dental appointment freeing you from your worry related to the sights and sounds of what’s going on around you. And the effects wear off as soon as the mask is removed, so you can go on about your day without any lasting grogginess!

Modern Communication

Remember the days when you HAD to call a doctor’s office to get in touch? That often meant leaving a message and waiting for a callback. Today’s Dental offers a modern alternative if you prefer to text, email, or send a Facebook message, please feel free to take advantage of our easy-to-use communication software Rhinogram. It’s completely encrypted for your total privacy. Getting in touch with us has never been easier!

We Stand by Our Work

From quality general dental care to complex restorative dentistry and same-day emergency services, we will always stand by our award-winning work. And we feel like our proven results speak for themselves.
