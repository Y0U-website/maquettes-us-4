Our Practice Select Dental

Making smiles in Pittsburgh, PA wider and brighter.

Welcome to Select Dental, conveniently located just outside downtown Pittsburgh in Millvale. Here, our professionals know that dental health is not just about white smiles: it’s about having strong teeth and healthy gums. It’s about prolonging the simple joy of eating delicious things! Our practice prides itself on being an independently women-owned business in the steel city.

Our patient-centered practice offers comprehensive dental care with an equal commitment to preventative, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry. Our patients are our top priority. We value personalized attention and long-term relationships with our clients.

Dr. Liao and her team at Select Dental perform a full range of dental services, from simple fillings, crowns and teeth whitening to full smile makeovers and dental implants - all performed in a warm and caring environment. We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we created a soothing environment and a scheduling system that never keeps you waiting long. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we give our patients the best care in the Burgh.
