A dentist in Silver Spring and Riverdale, MD , who specializes in dental implants, can guide you through the process, ensuring you regain both the functionality and appearance of your smile. Let’s explore dental implants, how they work, and why they may be your ideal option.

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials like titanium. These implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they are fused with the bone through a process called osseointegration. Once the implant has been integrated with the bone, it can serve as a strong foundation for a replacement tooth, such as a crown , bridge , or denture .

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution, unlike traditional dentures or bridges. They provide superior stability and durability, making them an excellent choice for individuals looking to replace missing teeth.
