What Are FASTBRACES®?

FASTBRACES® is a cutting-edge orthodontic system developed to move both the crown and the root of the teeth simultaneously. This is what sets it apart from traditional braces that focus on adjusting the crown first, followed by the roots. By addressing both aspects of tooth movement at the same time, FASTBRACES® can achieve straighter teeth in a much shorter period, typically between three and 12 months.

This system uses a unique triangular bracket and a heat-activated wire that works together to shift teeth into the desired position more efficiently. The technology behind FASTBRACES® is the product of research and development that took about 20 years, ensuring its safety and effectiveness.

How FASTBRACES® Work

In traditional braces, the movement of teeth occurs in two stages: the first stage shifts the crown (the visible part of the tooth), and the second stage moves the root. This process often takes up to two years or more. FASTBRACES® in Silver Spring and Riverdale, MD, utilizes advanced bracket design and specially engineered wires to simultaneously move both the root and crown. The triangular brackets allow for a greater range of movement, while the flexible wire provides the necessary pressure to straighten the teeth from the roots up.

By reducing the stages of tooth movement, FASTBRACES® can deliver faster results without compromising the quality of treatment. Patients can achieve a beautifully aligned smile in significantly less time than traditional orthodontic treatments.

What to Expect During FASTBRACES® Treatment

Getting FASTBRACES® is similar to traditional braces but with fewer visits and a faster overall timeline. Here’s what you can expect during your treatment:

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a consultation with your dentist in Silver Spring and Riverdale, MD. During this appointment, your dentist will take X-rays, photos, and teeth impressions to evaluate your orthodontic needs. Based on this assessment, they will create a customized treatment plan using FASTBRACES®.

Placement of FASTBRACES®

Once your treatment plan is established, your FASTBRACES® will be placed. The brackets are attached to each tooth, and a wire is threaded through the brackets to apply gentle pressure to move the teeth into alignment. This procedure typically takes about an hour, and you can resume normal activities immediately afterward.

Progress Monitoring

During your treatment, you’ll need to visit your dentist in Silver Spring and Riverdale, MD, periodically to check your progress and make any necessary adjustments. Because FASTBRACES® works more quickly than traditional braces, these appointments will be less frequent.

Completion and Retainer

Once your teeth have reached their desired position, your FASTBRACES® will be removed. You’ll likely need to wear a retainer to maintain your new smile, which helps prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original position.
