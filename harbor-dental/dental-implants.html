Frequently asked questions

How long do dental implants last, and how do I take care of them?

The average lifespan of dental implants is trending up—most sources cite 7–9 years, but with proper care and modern advancements, we aim for implants to last a lifetime. At Harbor Dental, we place implants with the intention that they’ll serve you long-term. Success depends on a combination of precision placement and post-care maintenance. That’s why we focus on patient education around brushing, flossing, and routine cleanings to protect your investment and preserve your oral health.

What makes dental implants a good investment?

Dental implants aren’t just about replacing a tooth—they’re about stopping the domino effect that happens when one goes missing. When left untreated, gaps can lead to shifting teeth, jawbone deterioration, bite issues, and more invasive procedures down the road. Implants solve the problem at its source and help you avoid bigger issues and costs later. They’re also durable, lifelike, and comfortable, making them a smart, long-term investment in your overall well-being.

I’m nervous about surgery. What can I expect?

You’re not alone—many people feel anxious about the idea of oral surgery. That’s why we’ve intentionally designed a process that’s calm, efficient, and patient-centered. Our digital workflows allow us to place implants quickly and precisely—usually in 30 minutes or less for a single implant. We also offer nitrous oxide and mild sedation to help you feel at ease. And above all, we take the time to talk you through every step so you know exactly what to expect and never feel rushed or overwhelmed.
