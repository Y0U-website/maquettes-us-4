The practice

Red Hat Neighborhood Dentist was founded on the conventional, small-town model that a healthy dentist-patient relationship built on trust and communication provides the best outcomes for patients. We understand unique patient needs and goals are the result of a meeting point of personal values related to dental esthetics, function, and finance.

Our clinical dentistry is rooted in the latest available scientific research to provide our patients with the safest, longest-lasting, and most natural-looking dentistry possible.

From the front desk to clinical assistants, our highly experienced staff partners with our patients to achieve their dental goals. We stay ahead of continuing education and licensing requirements to ensure patients get our best.

It’s no secret that the general recommendation is to brush at least twice a day. Still, many of us continue to neglect brushing our teeth at night. But brushing before bed gets rid of the germs and plaque that accumulate throughout the day.

Plaque can also build up on your tongue. Not only can this lead to bad mouth odor, but it can lead to other oral health problems. Gently brush your tongue every time you brush your teeth.

Flossing can be difficult, especially for young children and older adults with arthritis. Rather than give up, look for tools that can help you floss your teeth. Ready-to-use dental flossers from your local pharmacy can make a difference.
