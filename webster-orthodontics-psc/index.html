Braces correct problems with your teeth, including crooked, crowded or overlapping teeth.

The practice

Invisalign® straightens your teeth without wires and brackets, using a series of clear, customized, removable appliances called aligners.

A retainer is an appliance used after orthodontic treatment to protect the results of the intervention.

Phase I and II early interceptive orthodontics typically occurs between 6 and 9 years of age when patients begin to lose their baby teeth.
