Located in Brigham City, we service the Tremonton, Bear River City, Perry, Willard, Mantua, and North Ogden areas with patients ranging all the way to New York City.

Dr. Olson was born and raised in Brigham City and is a 3rd-generation graduate of Box Elder High School. After completing his undergraduate studies at the University of Utah, he attended The Ohio State College of Dentistry.

Upon completion of his Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree and graduating Magna Cum Laude, Dr. Olson attended a one-year general dentistry residency at the Lutheran Medical Center located in Mesa, Arizona.

During his one year of post-doctoral education, Dr. Olson studied advanced procedures in general dentistry with an emphasis on complex comprehensive treatment and dental implant therapy.

Dr. Olson resides near Box Elder County with his wife Angie and their four children, Kayson, Kyler, Liam, and Scarlett.
