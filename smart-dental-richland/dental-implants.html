Snap-in Dentures

Experience the security and confidence of implant-supported dentures. Unlike traditional dentures, snap-in dentures are anchored by dental implants, eliminating slipping and allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence. This solution combines the affordability of dentures with the stability of implants for optimal comfort and function.

Service FAQs:

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown attached to the implant typically lasts 15-20 years before needing replacement.

Is dental implant surgery painful?

Most patients report minimal discomfort. We use local anesthesia and offer sedation options to ensure a comfortable experience during and after the procedure.

How long is the implant process?

The typical process takes 3-6 months from implant placement to final restoration. Some patients may qualify for same-day temporary teeth.

Can I get implants with bone loss?

Yes! We offer bone grafting procedures to rebuild adequate bone support for successful implant placement. Each case is evaluated individually.

What’s the success rate of implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % when placed by an experienced provider like Dr. Petersen, who focuses extensively on implant dentistry.

Are implants covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans provide partial coverage. We offer various financing options and will help maximize your benefits for affordable implant treatment.
