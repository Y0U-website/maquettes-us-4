About of Richland, WA

Advanced dentistry. Genuine care. A practice that feels like family.

Smart Dental is built around a simple idea: excellent dentistry feels better when patients feel supported, understood, and completely at ease.

Care that begins with listening.

Led by Dr. Julian Petersen, a Richland dentist since 2009, our team brings clinical expertise and genuine compassion to every conversation. Your visit is about more than dental health—it is about feeling safe, supported, and confident in your care.

Little comforts can change the whole visit.

TVs

Catch up on a favorite show or relax with a movie.

Blankets

A cozy layer to help the treatment room feel warmer.

Water

Bottled water before or after your appointment.

Chapstick

A thoughtful touch for dry or sensitive lips.

Stress balls

Something simple to hold when nervous energy rises.

A journey inspired by compassion.

Dr. Petersen’s path to dentistry began in Cambodia, where volunteering with Operation Smile showed him the life-changing impact of dental care. He earned a bachelor’s degree in human biology from Brigham Young University and his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry in 2009.

He continues to strengthen his skills through ongoing education and is a member of the American Dental Association and the Benton-Franklin County Dental Society.

More than a dentist

Away from the practice, Dr. Petersen values laughter, church and community service, coaching soccer, and exploring Washington outdoors. His greatest joy is time with his wife, Sheri, and their four children.
