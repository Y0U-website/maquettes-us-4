Our Services

At U Street Family Dentistry, our goal is to ensure a confident and beautiful smile that lasts a lifetime. Your smile is powerful and you deserve the best! Come see us in person, and let’s talk about what’s next for your oral health. We offer many services from dental checkups to Invisalign.

Routine Checkups

Prevention is the key! At U Street Family Dentistry our goal is to avoid dental problems from occurring. We offer many services to our Washington DC patients such as sealants, prophylaxis, flouride varnish to maintain your teeth and gums.

KöR whitening

Have a special event? Make your smile sparkle with our best whitening product: KöR. KöR is widely recognized as the world’s most effective whitening system—with no need for lights or lasers. KöR provides brilliant results with take home or in office whitening treatment. It has been proven to remove tetracycline staining— and everything in-between.

Invisalign

Invisalign is a virtually invisible solution, so you can smile confidently both during and after treatment. Using a series of clear, removable aligners, Invisalign gradually moves your teeth toward the smile you’ve always wanted.

Restorative Dentistry

Got a sweet tooth? No worries we are here to help. We understand what it is like to love sweets! U Street Family Dentistry will do everything possible to help you restore your teeth. No side eye allowed. Our team will meet with you to set up an initial exam and treatment plan.

Cosmetic Dentistry

We offer cosmetic and restorative solutions to help you achieve your dream smile. Stop by to learn about our cosmetic procedures such as Teeth Whitening, Crowns, Bridges, Dentures, Veneers and Bonding

Modern Oral Health Care

We are proud of our office in Washington DC, which utilizes state-of-the-art dental equipment, that will make your time at U Street Family Dentistry faster and more effective. Our friendly and competent staff is dedicated to providing quality oral care for the entire family.
