Dentistry Runs In The Melkers Family

Before becoming a dentist in Hanover , Dr. Melkers and his family lived in Washington state. Because of their love for the New England area, Dr. Melkers and his wife decided to upend their life and moved to Hanover and joined Lyme Road Dental. Why? Because of the people who work there and the patients they serve. “I have never seen anything like it. I was drawn to becoming a part of a team that was so committed to each other and their patients’ well-being.”

  • Dad was a dentist
  • Mother was a dental hygienist
  • Uncle was an orthodontist
  • Aunt was a laboratory technician
  • His sister Julia escaped the dental gene, but she is a Ph.D. Professor at Georgia Tech

Why Dentistry?
