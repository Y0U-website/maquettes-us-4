Cedar Hills Dental Center

We opened our family dental office November of 1986. It began with one patient chair, and a telephone. We have steadily grown as our patients refer their friends and family. We pride ourselves on making our practice not just a place where you will feel comfortable - our patients often say they enjoy coming to the dentist - but also a place where you know your health and the health of your loved ones is our top priority. We enjoy keeping your smiles beautiful!

All five of our patient treatment areas have the latest in dental technology, which helps our team provide you with the best possible dental care.

Digital x-rays and the intraoral camera allow us a more detailed look at your teeth. The patient viewing monitor allows Dr. Wagnild to show you a clear picture of your mouth and the status of your treatment. Pairing that with our expertise, attention to detail and caring concern allows us to provide you with the utmost in quality dental care for your oral and general health.

We view our patient referrals to their friends and family as compliments. Besides a happy and healthy smile which our patients proudly share, those recommendations to new patients from someone they trust is an honor. It is also an honor to be part of a caring community. Part of our commitment to our community has always been involvement. We annually participate in Give Kids A Smile Day, where we provide free dental care to students in need. We have coached various athletic teams and continue to be proud sponsors of community youth activities and sports.

We are a family dental practice

and are able to provide treatment for all your concerns and needs.

These services might include routine hygiene, bleaching, fillings, relieving pain, or just some cosmetic changes you have in mind.

We sincerely hope to see you soon!

We participate in most insurance plans as out-of-network providers. We are a Delta Dental Premier, United Health Group, and Premier Classic Plus Network provider. We accept VISA, MasterCard, Discover and American Express credit cards and Care Credit. We will assist you in processing all insurance claims. At your appointment, we will discuss your treatment as well as your payment. A payment will be requested at each appointment to help assist you with your dental care costs.

We work hard to schedule appointments that meet your schedules. We request a 48 hour notice when an appointment is changed. Under certain circumstances, a same day and or no-show appointment could carry a $65 charge. We understand emergencies occur and will review each case.

We are excited about the new features and access we have with our web site and hope you browse the various pages. We will be updating it regularly, so watch for new and interesting articles on our FAQ page. We will add new links to help you keep up-to-date on oral and general health issues. Remember to bring us your questions as well as your smiles!
