The Benefits of All-on-X Implants

Choosing All-on-4 dental implants over other tooth replacement options offers many benefits.

Improved Function

All-on-X replacement teeth function like natural teeth, and you can eat the foods you love without discomfort.

Stability

Appearance

Because All-on-4 implants support your facial features, they help maintain more youthful contours and prevent the sunken appearance common with missing teeth.

What’s recovery like after All-on-4 implant surgery?

Our patients who have had All-on-4 implant surgery are relieved to find how smooth their recovery goes. In general, recovery takes about 2-6 days, and it’s common to experience tenderness, swelling, and bruising. What foods can I eat after All-on-4 implant surgery?

All-on-4 implant surgery is a procedure that involves placing four dental implants in the jawbone to support a full arch of replacement teeth. After surgery at our Elk Grove, CA, dental office, it is crucial to follow our diet recommendations to ensure proper implant healing.

Foods to Avoid after Implant Surgery

  • Hard or crunchy foods (nuts, popcorn, chips)
  • Sticky or chewy foods (caramel, taffy, gum)
  • Hard candies
  • Tough meats
