Our Dental Practice

Pecan Park Dental

Pecan Park Dental offers various services that we tailor to meet your individual needs, and our office is equipped with the latest technology to provide you with the best possible care. Our primary goal is to help you achieve and maintain healthy teeth for a lifetime, and we want everyone to walk away from our office feeling confident with their smile.

Our Charming Office

A state-of-the-art facility in a warm, cozy setting. Located in the heart of DFW.

Pecan Park Dental is a state-of-the-art facility in north-central Arlington that feels more like home than a dental office. From the moment you arrive, you’ll be surrounded by the charm of our 4,000 square-foot flagstone building that sits beneath majestic 70-year-old pecan trees.

We hope to create a warm and cozy environment rather than the clinical feeling you might normally find in a dental office. You will find this same feeling from Dr. Bangs, Dr. Fritz, and Dr. Nguyen during your visit with us!
