First Visit

Our goal is for you to leave your visit knowing exactly where things stand and feeling confident about what comes next.

  • A Thoughtful Review: We’ll review your medical and dental history and take time to listen to any concerns or goals you’d like to share.
  • Your Exam: A full exam and any necessary X-rays help us get a clear picture of your oral health.
  • A Professional Cleaning: Your visit includes a thorough teeth cleaning to help you start off feeling refreshed.
  • Your Questions, Answered: Before you leave, you’ll meet with one of our doctors to review what we found, talk through next steps if needed, and ask any questions.

Getting Ready for Your Visit

A little preparation can help your visit feel relaxed and unhurried. Here’s how to get ready for your appointment:

If you’re feeling nervous or have questions, that’s completely okay. Our team here to listen, talk things through, and make sure you feel comfortable every step of the way.

If you are experiencing pain or a dental emergency , please contact us right away, and we will make it our priority to see you as soon as possible.

  • Schedule your visit online or call call our office and we’ll help you find a time that fits your schedule.
  • Complete your patient forms ahead of time, if possible, to save time when you arrive
  • Bring a valid ID and insurance card, if applicable
  • Plan to arrive a few minutes early so we can review any details together
