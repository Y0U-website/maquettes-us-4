Wellness Dental Care: Trusted Dental Services in Ravenswood, Chicago

Finding a dentist you can trust is the most important step in maintaining healthy teeth and gums. At Wellness Dental Care, our skilled team ensures you receive quality services every time you visit our office. Patient satisfaction is our highest priority, which is why we dedicate the time and attention you need when visiting our office.

Not only do we offer modern technology solutions to improve your time in the dental chair, but we work hard to give you a safe and relaxing experience. Whether you need a routine exam or dental restorations, we deliver top-notch results to create beautiful, healthy smiles.

Rest assured knowing that we custom-design the treatment plan based on the unique needs of each patient. In addition, our team continually educates patients about available services, so you feel empowered to choose the treatment plan that matches your preferences.

Meet Dr. Jennifer J. Lee

Dentist

Dr. Lee feels so fortunate to have found a career that she enjoys full-heartedly while serving her patients and the community. If you have any questions about anything dental-related, please do not hesitate to ask! Dr. Lee believes in helping patients understand the why’s and the how’s of oral conditions and dental treatments. Let’s work together toward healthy smiles!

Dr. Lee graduated from Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, where she became a Steelers fan while majoring in Biology and Psychology with a minor in Chemistry. She then attended UIC College of Dentistry where she received Paffenbarger Award to conduct research on strengthening dentin. Dr. Lee stays up to date with the latest treatment with a passion for learning and is always challenging herself.

In her spare time, Dr. Lee enjoys yoga, playing with her dog Hazel, streaming movies, and making trips to local pastries and restaurants. Let her know which pastries and restaurants are your favorites!
