Removable Prosthodontics (Dentures and Partials)

Govani Dental also helps patients who have, or need, removable tooth replacement options . We provide both complete and partial dentures.

Complete dentures

Can be used when all of a patient’s natural teeth are missing or as a temporary solution before fixed treatment options.

Partial dentures

An option used to fill in missing teeth as needed. Whatever situation you may be in, we can help your mouth feel better and help you feel confident in your smile.

Bruxism (Clenching & Grinding)

We want to educate our patients and help aid them with bruxing concerns, learn more here . Dr. Govani and her trusted team will evaluate the temporomandibular joints, or TMJ. Temporomandibular disorders (TMD) can often cause discomfort and myofacial pain. Myofacial pain happens when muscles in the head, neck or body contract and tense, often caused by stress or clenching or grinding teeth. It can be characterized by face and jaw pain. Dr. Govani works with patients to find the most conservative solutions.

We are committed to ensuring all patients are comfortable and will work to find the solution that is best for you.

  • A custom fit occlusal guard to help prevent bruxing or grinding
  • Soft food
  • Avoiding gum and hard candies
  • Jaw, head and neck stretches
  • Pain relief medications
  • Massage therapy
  • Referral to a jaw and pain management specialist
