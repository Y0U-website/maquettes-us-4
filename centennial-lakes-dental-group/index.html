Dr. Chad Brettingen grew up in Mora. He received his Bachelor of Science and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Minnesota . Dr. Brettingen joined Centennial Lakes Dental Group in 2005 and practices in our Edina office. He has a particular interest in laser and computer dentistry, and makes patient comfort and convenience his top priorities.

Dr. Brettingen has three children. The family enjoys outings, cooking, and traveling. He relaxes by golfing and gardening.
