Dr. Lill earned both her undergraduate and dental degree from the University of Minnesota , where she developed a strong foundation in biology and dental medicine. Following her education, she completed a general practice residency at Oregon Health & Science University (OHSU) . She then completed two additional oral surgery internships at both OHSU and the University of Minnesota, further enhancing her expertise in diagnostics and dentoalveolar surgery.

In addition to her residency and internships, Dr. Lill gained valuable experience working part-time at a community clinic in Minneapolis. This role allowed her to connect with diverse patient populations and provide accessible dental care to those in extreme need.

With a heavy emphasis on overdentures, Dr. Lill has honed her skills in dental implantology and prosthodontics, ensuring patients receive the highest quality of care. With a commitment to excellence and a passion for improving oral health, Dr. Lill is dedicated to providing high-quality dental care to her patients.

In her free time, Dr. Lill enjoys spending time outdoors with her family and two dogs, fishing, golfing as well as traveling and dining out in the local Minneapolis area.
