About Us

Meet the Team at Gorrell Smiles

What’s the value of a beautiful, healthy smile? We know that smiling conveys love, warmth, and friendliness. Sharing a smile has also been proven to release endorphins, so it also fills you and those around you with a sense of happiness and well-being.

For over 40 years, Dr. Brent Gorrell has been helping patients in Santa Clara County achieve optimal oral health and beautiful, confident smiles. He believes that your smile should allow you to express your individuality openly and comfortably. Dr. Gorrell and his dental team help promote, enhance, and maintain smiles for every patient in our community who desires a healthy, natural-looking smile.

Our skilled and dedicated dental team works together to ensure that you have a pleasant and enjoyable experience. We believe you’ll appreciate the combination of warm, personalized care and cutting-edge technology that allows us to provide the highest standard of dental care in a friendly, stress-free environment. We re a multilingual dental practice with team members who speak Spanish, Russian, Portuguese, Mandarin Chinese, Taiwanese, and Georgian in addition to English.

Are you ready to get started? Please call our Mountain View, CA dental office to arrange a dental appointment at your convenience. Until we meet you in person, please take a few minutes to get to know our team.

Jonathan Low, DDS

Dr. Jonathan Low was born and raised in Stockton, California. He received a bachelor’s degree at The College of Idaho. He then went on to pursue his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of the Pacific, Dugoni School of Dentistry.

Dr. Low is a third generation dentist within his family. His grandpa, father, uncles, siblings, and cousins all became dentists with Dr. Low being the eleventh member of thirteen dentists in the family. He found he wanted to become a dentist at the age of six and pursued dentistry his whole life.

Dr. Low enjoys doing oral surgery, root canals, implant restorations, bridges, and biomimetic restorative dentistry. He enjoys dentistry so much that you can even find him catching up on dental videos or literature while on vacation.

Outside the office, Dr. Low enjoys playing pickleball, bowling, shooting (former trapshooting competitor), attending Kpop concerts, and watching Korean dramas.
