The practice

Our team strives to provides an enjoyable dental experience in a comfortable, educational, and fun environment for our patients. We provide all of the services your infant, child, or adolescent needs to achieve and maintain a healthy smile in a friendly setting they will enjoy!

0 Dr. Eric Originally from Lexington, Kentucky, Dr. Eric Jacobson attended the University of Kentucky (UK), where he earned his bachelor’s degree before completing his dental degree and pediatric residency at the UK College of Dentistry. Before deciding to specialize in pediatrics, he spent 2 years as an adult dentist.

Dr. Eric’s father is a dentist and helped influence his decision to become a dentist. Growing up around dentistry, he has a long-standing passion for providing exceptional treatment for the pediatric and special needs communities. Dr. Eric loves getting to know his patients and their families. His ability to empathize with them is what truly makes him unique.

Dr. Eric and his wife, Natalie, have 3 young boys, Owen, Jude and Tate and a dog named Bruce. In his spare time, he enjoys park days with the boys, jogging, building things, lake days, cooking Japanese food with his wife and spending time with his friends and family.
