About us

Welcome to Little Smiles of Brooklyn! We are a dental office that specializes in treating infants, children, teenagers, and children with special needs. We are dedicated to providing the best oral health care possible in a fun and caring setting. We practice pediatric dentistry with the goal of maintaining happy and healthy mouths while teaching children how to take control and maintain their oral health as they grow. We always take our time with each of our patients to ensure that they feel comfortable and safe. We can’t wait to meet you!
