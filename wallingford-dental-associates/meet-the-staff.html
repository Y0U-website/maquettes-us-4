Dr. Victoria D’Agostino

Dr. Victoria D’Agostino earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Providence College and her Master of Science in Oral Health Sciences from Boston University. She went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine in 2025. Following dental school, she completed advanced training through a General Practice Residency at Hartford Hospital.

Dr. D’Agostino is proud to practice in her home state of Connecticut. She is committed to providing comprehensive dental care and helping patients achieve and maintain healthy smiles. Outside the office, she enjoys crafting, baking, hiking and spending time with family and friends. She is excited to be part of the team at Wallingford Dental Associates and looks forward to caring for her patients.
