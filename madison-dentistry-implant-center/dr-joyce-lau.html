Meet Dr. Joyce Lau

Dr. Joyce Lau is known for her compassionate nature and warm disposition, which are embodied in her gentle approach to dentistry. Her goal is to make her patients feel relaxed and at ease under her care as she strives to educate and encourage them to achieve their optimal oral health and their best possible smile.

Dr. Joyce earned her DMD from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine after completing a Masters degree in Biomedical Sciences at UMDNJ. In addition to having served as chief dental resident at New York Presbyterian Queens Hospital, she is a proud member of the American Dental Association, the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, the New York State Dental Association, and the Queens County Dental Society.
