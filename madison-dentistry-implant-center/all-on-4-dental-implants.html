All-on-4 Dental Implants Procedure At Madison Dentistry & Implant Center

Dr. Boukheir, a specialist in full mouth reconstruction and dental implant surgery with over 1,000 full arches and All-On-4 treatments performed, will carefully measure your oral profile to plan the precise placement of your implants. Bite impressions of your upper and lower jaws will be taken to create molds for custom prosthesis.

Once you’re sedated, the surgery begins with the extraction of existing teeth and any remnants of previously lost ones. Damaged gum tissue is removed, and the upper and lower jawbones are exposed to prepare for implant placement. Dr. Boukheir will then insert and secure the implants at strategic angles to ensure maximum stability and durability. After restoring the gum tissue, the temporary prosthesis will be placed on the abutments. Any gaps in the temporary prosthesis will be sealed, and the permanent prosthesis will be attached once healing is complete.
