I am insured—can I join the membership plan?

An in-house membership plan is a game-changer for individuals and families seeking affordable dental care. They eliminate the hassles of traditional insurance, but you need to note a few things to get the most from your membership.

  • It’s not for insured patients: In-house membership plans are designed for patients without dental insurance. If you already have coverage, the plan won’t apply.
  • No rollover benefits: Unused services don’t carry over to the next year, so be sure to use your benefits before they expire.
  • Non-transferable: Memberships are for individual use only and can’t be transferred to another person, even within the same family.
