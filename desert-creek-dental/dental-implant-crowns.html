The transformation is life-changing when Dr. Ismail Pardazi pairs a crown and implant. You enjoy a stronger bite, restored confidence, and a healthier, more complete smile.

What is the procedure for getting an implant crown in Queen Creek? Nothing in life is guaranteed, but consistency and careful planning often lead to the best results. At our office, implant crowns reflect that philosophy. Each restoration is placed with precision, ensuring long-term success and patient satisfaction.

  • Severely damaged tooth: An implant crown can replace a non-repairable, extensively damaged, decayed, or worn-down tooth.
  • Failed root canal: An implant crown can replace a tooth that fails to heal properly after a root canal.
  • Initial examination: We’ll assess your oral health needs to determine whether an implant crown is the best solution. Often, this involves using digital photos, X-rays, and scans to get a complete picture of your smile.
  • Implant placement: During a minor surgical procedure, the doctor carefully places a titanium post into a specific area of the jawbone. We exercise precision to ensure your implant is optimally placed for maximum alignment, comfort, and function.
  • Healing: Your titanium fixture needs at least 4-6 months to heal and fuse with the jawbone. This process forms a rock-solid foundation to support your crown restoration.
  • Crown placement: Dr. Pardazi will take precise scans of your implant to ensure your crown is made to measure. Your crown matches your tooth’s exact color, shape, and size for a perfect fit and natural appearance.

Are dental implant crowns worth it?

Restore your smile with a dental implant crown and enjoy:

  • Renewed confidence: Speak, smile, and eat without hesitation. Many patients say they finally feel like themselves again.
  • Improved oral function: Talk clearly and eat your favorite foods without discomfort or embarrassment.
  • Durable fix: Our restorations can withstand the everyday demands of biting and chewing. With good upkeep, they can last several years or even decades.
  • Stabilize remaining teeth: Implant crowns prevent adjacent teeth from shifting to keep your bite and teeth aligned.
