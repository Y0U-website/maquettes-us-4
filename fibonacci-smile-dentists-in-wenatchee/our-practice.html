Why Choose Us Attentive and Compassionate Care: We prioritize your preferences and emotions, taking the time to truly understand your needs, which brings an added layer of reassurance to your experience.

Comfort-First Amenities: Elevating your comfort, we offer a range of amenities such as movies, music DVDs, paraffin hand dip, cozy blankets, and specialized head and leg pillows. Our sedation dentistry in Wenatchee options, including laughing gas and Valium, further ensure your ease during treatments.

Innovative Pain Relief: Experience dental injections like never before with our state-of-the-art Novocain delivery system, The Wand. This technology minimizes discomfort by carefully controlling fluid pressure, resulting in gentle injections that often go unnoticed by our clients.

Seamless Insurance and Financing: We make your financial journey hassle-free by accepting insurance and offering flexible financing options through Care Credit, allowing you to focus on your oral health without worry.

Precision with Soft Tissue Laser: Our advanced soft tissue laser technology enhances the precision and comfort of various procedures, promoting faster healing and minimizing discomfort.

CEREC Same Day Crowns: Enjoy the convenience of CEREC technology, which allows us to create and place crowns in a single visit, saving you time and reducing the need for multiple appointments.
