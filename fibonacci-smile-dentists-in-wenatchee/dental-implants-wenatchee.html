What Do Dental Implants Cost in Wenatchee?

Single-tooth implant costs in Washington State typically range from $3,000 to $6,000 per tooth. That estimate generally includes the implant post, abutment, and crown. Preparatory procedures like bone grafting are priced separately. Multi-tooth and full-arch cases involve different pricing based on the number of implants and materials required.

These figures give you a ballpark. The only way to get an accurate number for your specific situation is to come in for a consultation. Dr. Milner will provide an itemized breakdown before any treatment begins, so there are no surprises.

Insurance Coverage in Washington

Dental insurance coverage for implants varies significantly by plan. Some Washington plans cover part of the crown or preparatory procedures. Most do not cover the implant post itself. Fibonacci Smile accepts insurance as a courtesy, files claims on your behalf, and collects an estimated co-pay at the time of service. Call your insurer before your appointment to confirm what your specific plan covers for implants.
