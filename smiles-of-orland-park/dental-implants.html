Signs You Might Benefit from Dental Implants

Dental implants can significantly improve life for many patients. At Smiles of Orland Park, we’ve helped many people regain their smiles and self-assurance. Consider dental implants if you have:

If any of these issues sound familiar, dental implants might be right for you. Our team at Smiles of Orland Park can guide you through the process and help you make an informed decision about your oral health.

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Trouble chewing or speaking
  • Loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Sunken facial appearance
  • Jawbone deterioration
  • Shifting of remaining teeth
  • Low self-confidence due to gaps in your smile
