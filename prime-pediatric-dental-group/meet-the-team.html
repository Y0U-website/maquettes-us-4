Dr. Yenisey Montero, DDS

Dr. Yenisey Montero is a devoted general dentist with a strong passion for helping children maintain healthy , confident smiles . Her journey in dentistry began in July 2007 at the Superior Institute of Medical Sciences in Cuba . She later continued her education in the United States , completing the Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at Larkin Community Hospital in Miami from February 2019 to July 2020 .

In addition to her clinical training , Dr. Montero has consistently demonstrated a deep commitment to serving her community . She volunteered her time at Larkin Community Hospital in 2019 and participated in the Community Health Fair at Borinquen Medical Center in Miami from 2015 to 2017 . Dr. Montero is known for her gentle touch , caring nature , and dedication to creating a positive dental experience for every child she treats . Her goal is always to keep young smiles healthy , happy , and bright .

Dr. Christina Puig

is a dedicated pediatric dentist who earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine . She completed her residency at Nicklaus Children’s Hospital , where she gained extensive experience in treating children with diverse dental needs .

Dr. Puig has a strong commitment to serving special populations , having completed an extracurricular course in special needs dentistry . Her passion for outreach is also reflected in her participation in international dental mission trips to Jamaica and the Dominican Republic , where she provided care through simple and surgical extractions to combat oral infections in underserved communities .

In recognition of her academic excellence and community service , Dr. Puig was honored with the Hispanic Dental Association Foundation Scholarship in June 2014. She is authorized to practice at both Nicklaus Children’s Hospital and Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital , where she continues to provide compassionate , high quality care to her young patients .

Dr. Juan Garrastazu, D.M.D.

Dr. Juan Garrastazu is a highly trained dental professional with extensive experience in general and oral surgery . His journey in dentistry began in 1989 at Florida International University , where he pursued a Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences . He continued his academic path at the University of Florida , further solidifying his foundation in dental care .

Following his education , Dr. Garrastazu completed a General Practice Residency at the Medical Center Hospital of Vermont from June 1993 to August 1994. His passion for surgical care led him to pursue an Oral Surgery Residency at Jackson Memorial Hospital , which he completed between July 1994 and August 1995 .

Dr. Garrastazu holds hospital privileges at Memorial Regional Hospital and Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital , where he continues to provide expert care with a focus on safety , precision , and compassion . His dedication to patient well being and clinical excellence has earned him the trust of both his peers . and his community .

Dr. Valeria Kresevic, DDS

Dr. Valeria Kresevic discovered her passion for dentistry in 1996 when she began her studies at Cayetano Heredia Peruvian University, School of Dentistry, where she earned her degree in General Dentistry. Her dedication to children’s oral health inspired her to continue her education in the United States, where she attended the University of Rochester in 2004 and went on to complete her Pediatric Dentistry Residency at Bronx Lebanon Hospital Center.

Throughout her academic journey, Dr. Kresevic actively contributed to dental research. In 2003, she conducted studies on orthodontic treatment needs among Peruvian university students, children’s dental visit patterns, and overall dental health status. She also published research on Dental Esthetic Self-Perception in Young Adults With or Without Previous Orthodontic Treatment.

Known for her gentle and compassionate approach, Dr. Kresevic creates a fun, positive dental experience for every child she sees. Her patients love visiting her because she takes the time to make them feel comfortable and cared for—helping them build lifelong healthy smiles
