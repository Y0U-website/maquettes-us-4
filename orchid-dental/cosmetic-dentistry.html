FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT COSMETIC DENTISTRY

  • What is cosmetic dentistry? Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and smile. Treatments can address discoloration, misalignment, chips, gaps, and other aesthetic concerns.
  • What procedures are considered cosmetic dentistry? Common cosmetic dentistry procedures include teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, tooth-colored fillings, orthodontics, crowns, dental implants, and cosmetic periodontal surgery.
  • Does cosmetic dentistry improve oral health? While the primary goal of cosmetic dentistry is to enhance appearance, many procedures like veneers, crowns, and orthodontics also improve functionality and oral health.
  • Is cosmetic dentistry painful? Most cosmetic dental procedures are minimally invasive and cause little to no discomfort. For more extensive treatments, we use local anesthesia to ensure your comfort.
  • How long do cosmetic dentistry treatments take? The timeline depends on the procedure. Teeth whitening can often be completed in one visit, while veneers or orthodontics may take multiple appointments over weeks or months.
  • How much does cosmetic dentistry cost? Costs vary widely depending on the procedure and the complexity of your case. We offer affordable options and financing through third parties such as Care Credit and Cherry to help fit treatments into your budget.
  • Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance? Most insurance plans do not cover cosmetic dentistry since it is not considered medically necessary. However, some restorative procedures with cosmetic benefits (like crowns) may be partially covered.
  • Am I a good candidate for cosmetic dentistry? If you’re unhappy with the appearance of your teeth or smile, you’re likely a candidate. We’ll assess your oral health and recommend treatments based on your personal goals and needs.
  • How long do cosmetic dentistry results last? The longevity of your results depends on the procedure and how well you care for your teeth. For example, veneers can last 5-15 years or more, while teeth whitening results may need touch-ups every 6-12 months.
  • What are the risks of cosmetic dentistry? Cosmetic dentistry is generally very safe. Potential risks, like sensitivity or minor discomfort, are rare and typically temporary.
  • Can cosmetic dentistry fix crooked teeth without braces? Yes, veneers or cosmetic bonding can address minor misalignment or gaps without the need for braces. However, for more significant alignment issues, orthodontics may be necessary.
  • How do I maintain my results after cosmetic dentistry? Maintain results with good oral hygiene, regular dental check-ups, and avoiding habits like smoking or consuming excessive staining foods and drinks. We’ll provide specific care instructions based on your procedure.
  • Will my teeth look natural after cosmetic dentistry? Absolutely. At Orchid Dental, we use advanced techniques and materials to ensure that your results are natural-looking and customized to your unique smile.
