WHAT TO EXPECT DURING TREATMENT

We prioritize your comfort and make the experience as easy and stress-free as possible. Deep cleaning is usually completed in two separate visits each focused on one side of the mouth to ensure thorough care.

A local anesthetic will be applied to numb the treatment area, so you can relax without discomfort. The scaling and root planing procedure may take a little longer than a routine cleaning, but it’s well worth the investment in your oral health.

After treatment, it’s normal to feel some tenderness or sensitivity for a few days. We’ll provide detailed aftercare instructions and may recommend a follow-up cleaning or periodontal maintenance visit to track your progress and keep your gums healthy.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT DEEP CLEANING

  • Is scaling and root planing painful? Most patients feel little to no pain during the procedure thanks to local anesthesia. You might experience some soreness or sensitivity afterward, but this typically subsides within a few days.
  • How long does a deep cleaning take? Deep cleanings are usually split into two appointments, treating one side of the mouth at a time. Each visit typically lasts about 45 minutes to an hour.
  • Will my gums heal after a deep cleaning? Yes. Scaling and root planing allow your gums to heal by removing bacteria and giving tissue a chance to reattach to your teeth. You’ll notice reduced bleeding, inflammation, and improved gum health over time.
  • How do I care for my mouth after a deep cleaning? We recommend gentle brushing and flossing, avoiding hard or crunchy foods for a day or two, and rinsing with warm salt water. We’ll provide specific post-treatment instructions tailored to your needs.
  • How often do I need a deep cleaning? Some patients only need one deep cleaning if their gum health improves and they maintain good oral hygiene. Others may require ongoing periodontal maintenance every 3 4 months to manage chronic gum disease.
  • Does insurance cover scaling and root planing? Many dental insurance plans do cover scaling and root planing if it’s deemed medically necessary. Our team can help check your benefits and explain your coverage before starting treatment.
