WHAT TO EXPECT DURING THE DENTAL CROWN PROCESS

Getting a dental crown is a simple and effective treatment, completed in three steps:

During your first visit, we’ll examine your tooth and discuss your treatment needs. Using advanced digital scanning technology, we’ll take precise impressions to design a custom crown that blends perfectly with your natural teeth.

We’ll gently remove any damaged or decayed areas and reshape the tooth to fit the crown. A temporary crown will be placed to protect your tooth while your permanent crown is crafted.

Once your custom crown is ready, you’ll return for placement. We’ll ensure the crown fits comfortably, make any needed adjustments, and securely bond it in place.

The result? A restored tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like your own. With proper care, your dental crown can last for many years, helping maintain a healthy and confident smile.

UNDERSTANDING THE BENEFITS OF DENTAL CROWNS

Dental crowns offer both restorative and cosmetic benefits, making them one of the most versatile solutions in modern dentistry. They help preserve the natural tooth structure while restoring strength, function, and appearance.

Some of the key benefits of dental crowns include:

  • Protection for Damaged Teeth : Crowns shield weakened or cracked teeth from further damage or decay.
  • Restored Function : You’ll be able to bite and chew comfortably again without pain or sensitivity.
  • Natural Appearance : Crowns are custom-made to match the shape, size, and color of your surrounding teeth.
  • Durability : Made from strong, long-lasting materials, crowns can last 10 15 years or more with proper care.
  • Support for Other Treatments : Crowns can anchor dental bridges or cover dental implants for a complete restoration.
  • Improved Confidence : A well-crafted crown can enhance your smile and boost your self-esteem.

DENTAL CROWN FAQS

  • What materials are dental crowns made from? Crowns can be made from porcelain, ceramic, metal alloys, or zirconia. We often recommend tooth-colored materials for front teeth and stronger options for molars.
  • How long do dental crowns last? With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental crowns can last 10 15 years or longer.
  • Is getting a crown painful? The procedure is not painful. We use local anesthesia to ensure you are comfortable, and most patients report minimal discomfort.
  • Will the crown match my natural teeth? Yes, we use color-matching technology to ensure your crown blends seamlessly with your existing smile.
  • Can a crown fix cosmetic issues? Absolutely. Crowns can be used to cover discolored, misshapen, or uneven teeth, offering a significant cosmetic improvement.
  • How do I care for my dental crown? Brush and floss as you usually would. Avoid hard foods that could chip the crown, and visit us regularly for checkups.
  • What’s the difference between a crown and a veneer? A crown covers the entire tooth, while a veneer only covers the front. Crowns are used for structural damage, while veneers are more cosmetic.
  • Does insurance cover the cost of a dental crown? Insurance coverage for dental crowns varies depending on your provider and plan. Many insurance plans cover a portion of the cost if the crown is deemed medically necessary, such as for restoring a damaged or decayed tooth.
