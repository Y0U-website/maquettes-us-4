At Vail Dental Design, Dr. Alexandra Urbana provides beautifully designed implant restorations that look, feel, and function like your real teeth. Whether you’re restoring a single tooth or a full arch, we’ll ensure your smile is strong, natural-looking, and built to last.

The Benefits of Dental Implant Restorations

Natural Look and Feel

Our implant restorations are made from high-quality materials that blend seamlessly with your existing teeth. They mimic the color, shape, and translucency of natural enamel—so no one will know it’s not the real thing.

Durable and Long-Lasting

When paired with a properly placed dental implant, these restorations offer a stable, long-term solution for missing teeth. With good care, they can last many years without needing replacement.

Fully Restored Function

Dental implant restorations allow you to chew, speak, and smile confidently—without worrying about slippage or discomfort. You’ll enjoy improved oral health and everyday ease.

Types of Dental Implant Restorations We Offer

Implant Bridges

Implant Bridges

If you’re missing two or more teeth in a row, an implant bridge can fill the gap without relying on surrounding natural teeth. These bridges are anchored securely to dental implants for lasting stability.

Implant Crowns

Implant Crowns

Implant-supported crowns are used to restore a single missing tooth. Each one is custom-designed to match the color and shape of your adjacent teeth, blending in beautifully with your natural smile.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing all or most of their teeth, implant-supported dentures provide a secure and comfortable alternative to traditional dentures. They stay firmly in place while offering full arch function and aesthetics.

The Dental Implant Restoration Process

Detailed Consultation

We’ll begin with a consultation to assess your implant(s) and determine the best type of restoration for your needs. Dr. Urbana will review your dental records, take digital scans, and walk you through your options.

Custom Design & Fabrication

Your restoration is carefully crafted to ensure a perfect fit and natural look. We’ll work closely with our dental lab partners to match color, size, and shape to your surrounding teeth.

Final Placement

Once your restoration is ready, we’ll attach it to your dental implant. Whether it’s a crown, bridge, or denture, the result will feel secure, comfortable, and functional from day one.
