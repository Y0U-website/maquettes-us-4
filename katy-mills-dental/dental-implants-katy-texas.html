What are dental implants?

If you have missing teeth and are looking for a solution, Katy, Texas dental implants might be right for you. We can find an option that works best for your situation and you could have your smile back in no time.

A dental implant is a restoration tool that replaces a lost or extracted tooth. An implant is surgically placed into your jawbone, like a screw, to provide a secure base. Then a supporting tooth, called an abutment, is connected to the implant. Finally, an artificial tooth, called a crown, is placed on top to make the new tooth look natural.

Dentists use different types of dental implants in Katy, Texas, depending on the patient’s unique circumstances. For one missing tooth, your dentist may recommend a single-tooth implant. Patients who have multiple missing teeth may receive a type of implant-supported bridge. In these cases, implants may be placed in the outer spaces of each row of missing teeth. Then the implants are connected by a row of crowns that are fixed in the middle, giving the appearance of a full set of natural teeth.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

When a tooth has been lost or extracted, bacteria can accumulate and quickly degrade your jaw bone and remaining teeth. Dental implants protect your jaw and teeth from such degradation.

Unlike natural teeth, dental implants are made of materials that aren’t susceptible to damage from bacteria, making them resistant to cavities. They don’t require any special cleaning and can be brushed like the rest of your teeth.

Other types of artificial teeth might make speaking more difficult, but dental implants feel more natural, making it easier to speak and effectively pronounce your words.
