Cosmetic Dentistry

Here at Cornell Family Dental, we offer a variety of cosmetic treatments using modern technology and high-quality materials. We’ll work with you to understand your goals and recommend personalized options to help you achieve the beautiful, natural-looking smile you’ve always wanted. Even when your oral health is in great shape, stains, discoloration, gaps, chips, or cracks can affect how you feel about your smile. Cosmetic dentistry can help refresh and enhance your smile while giving you the confidence to show it off.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings, also known as composite fillings, offer a natural-looking alternative to traditional metal fillings. We use durable composite material designed to blend seamlessly with the natural color of your teeth.

With modern techniques and technology, we can precisely remove decay and restore the tooth while preserving as much healthy tooth structure as possible. Tooth-colored fillings are metal-free, aesthetically pleasing, and provide a beautiful, natural-looking result.

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

Dr. Lindsay uses advanced digital technology to design and create custom CEREC crowns right here in our office, often in just one visit. Each crown is carefully designed to match the natural shape, size, and color of your smile for a beautiful, seamless result.

This technology combines a precise, natural-looking restoration with the convenience of same-day care. Our team will guide you through the process and help make your visit comfortable and efficient.

Dental Veneers

Custom dental veneers offer a personalized way to enhance the appearance of your smile. These thin, custom-made porcelain restorations are bonded to the front of your teeth. They can help improve the look of chipped, cracked, discolored, uneven, or slightly crooked teeth, as well as close small gaps or change the shape and size of your teeth.

Dr. Lindsay carefully designs each veneer to complement your natural smile, creating a beautiful, seamless result. With proper care and good oral hygiene, veneers can provide a long-lasting enhancement to your smile.

Dental Implants

At Cornell Family Dental, we offer custom implant restorations to replace missing teeth with natural-looking, functional results. Dental implants act as artificial tooth roots, providing a strong foundation for custom dental crowns designed to blend seamlessly with your smile.

A consultation gives us the opportunity to evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine whether dental implants are a suitable option for you.

Implant Placement & Restoration

While dental implant placement is performed by a trusted specialist, our team can help coordinate your care and facilitate the placement process. Once the implant has healed and is ready for restoration, Dr. Lindsay can complete the final crown here in our office, restoring the appearance and function of your smile.

Full Dentures

For patients who are missing all their teeth in the upper or lower arch, we offer custom full dentures tailored to their needs. Each denture is designed to provide a comfortable fit, natural appearance, and improved function, helping you feel confident in your smile.

Partial Dentures

If you’re missing several teeth, custom partial dentures can help fill the gaps and restore your smile. These removable restorations are designed to blend naturally with your existing teeth while improving both appearance and function. Our team will help you understand your options and find the right fit for your needs.
