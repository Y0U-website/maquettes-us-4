Comfort-Focused Treatment in a Relaxed Environment

The practice is being purchased by Dr. Eric De Boef, who is passionate about preserving private practice in dentistry. Dr. De Boef started a consulting company to oversee the growth of his vision and entering into Washington became a part of that vision. Dr. Kerby is a local Iowa dentist who also shares the same passion for dentistry and patient care. Dr. Kerby will be working as the full time dentist and associate at Restoration Dental & Implant Center .

Both Dr. De Boef and Dr. Kerby feel strongly about supporting Iowa communities and want to help maintain a small town feel. This includes reliable care that can be found by having a dentist living in their community who can understand and relate to their needs. Dr. Kerby s goal is to grow the practice and the team.
