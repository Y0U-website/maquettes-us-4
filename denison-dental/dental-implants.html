What are dental implants, and how do they replace missing teeth in Denison, Texas?

Dental implants are small titanium posts placed in the jaw that act as artificial tooth roots, topped with a natural looking crown, bridge, or full arch of teeth. At Denison Dental in Denison, Texas, implants replace one tooth, several teeth, or an entire arch, restoring your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants or All on X full arch implants in Denison, Texas?

Most adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates for dental implants, including full arch All on X implants. Healthy gums and enough jawbone help, and bone grafting can rebuild areas that need support. Dr. Damilola Johnson, DDS will review your oral health and goals at a consultation to confirm the best option for you.

What is the difference between dental implants, bridges, and dentures in Denison, Texas?

Dental implants are the most natural looking and longest lasting choice because they replace the tooth root and do not rely on neighboring teeth. Bridges fill a gap using the adjacent teeth for support, while dentures are a removable, budget friendly option. We will help you compare all three in Denison, Texas.

Does getting dental implants hurt, and how long do they last?

Implant placement is performed with local anesthetic and sedation options, so most patients feel little to no discomfort during treatment. With good care, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. Our team guides you from your first consultation through every follow up visit.
