Meet Dr. Joe D&#x27;Alesio

Dr. D&#x27;Alesio graduated from West Virginia University School of Dentistry and has been caring for patients in the Munhall community since 1999. His focus has always been on one-to-one relationships — taking time to explain options, listen to concerns, and plan treatment that fits each patient&#x27;s goals.

He brought Digital Smile Design to the practice to give patients something most dentists don&#x27;t: the ability to see their result before committing. Every cosmetic case starts with a digital preview — photos, scans, and a plan you approve first.
