The Mini Implant Procedure

Dr. Shatkin has placed over 45,000 mini dental implants using his patented technique, which he has taught to over 40,000 dental professionals worldwide. The process includes:

Comprehensive Examination – X-rays help determine bone health and the best treatment plan.

Consultation – Discuss your options, including conventional implants if appropriate.

Preparation – Address any dental health issues before surgery.

Placement – Under local anesthesia, the implant is placed with a small incision. The replacement teeth are mounted the same day.

Aftercare – Minor discomfort may occur, but most patients return to normal life quickly.

Caring for Your Mini Implants

Mini dental implants are easy to maintain. For single crowns, regular brushing and flossing are all that’s needed. For dentures secured by mini implants, simply remove them for cleaning and snap them back into place afterward. Routine dental checkups ensure the implants and crowns remain in excellent condition.

Who Can Benefit?

Mini dental implants are versatile and can:

  • Replace single teeth or multiple teeth
  • Anchor dentures securely
  • Fit smaller teeth like incisors or premolars
  • Work for patients with narrow jaws or bone loss where traditional implants aren’t suitable
