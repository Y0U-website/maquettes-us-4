Proudly Serving Northeast Ohio since 1993 .

Since 1993, Village Dental Hudson has been a proud cornerstone of the local community—providing compassionate, high-quality dental care with a personal touch. Founded by Drs. Azam & Betty Qadri, the practice was established with a vision: to offer advanced, patient-focused dentistry in a welcoming environment where comfort and trust come first.

For over three decades, Village Dental Hudson has grown with the community, embracing the latest in dental technology to deliver thorough, comfortable, and efficient care. From routine cleanings and comprehensive exams to advanced cosmetic procedures like whitening and gap closures, our experienced team is here to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

We utilize state-of-the-art equipment including digital impressions, panoramic CT scans, and laser technology—ensuring our patients benefit from the safest and most effective treatments available. Every service we provide is done with one goal in mind: making your experience as stress-free and positive as possible.

Whether you’re due for a checkup or are seeking a new dental home, we invite you to experience the difference at Village Dental Hudson—where your comfort is our priority, and your smile is our passion.

“Located in one of Hudson’s most historical buildings” Our office is uniquely situated in one of Hudson’s most historic landmarks. Originally built in 1841 by Edgar B. Ellsworth, the building once served as the Ellsworth Store. Located at the corner of Division and East Main Street, it is among the oldest commercial buildings still standing in Hudson today. Its blend of charm and character provides a distinctive setting where tradition meets modern care.
