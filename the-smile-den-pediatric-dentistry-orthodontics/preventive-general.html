Pediatric Dental Cleanings

A healthy smile starts early, and routine pediatric dental cleanings are a crucial step in ensuring your child’s dental health stays on track. At The Smile Den in Florham Park, NJ, our team focuses on making dental cleanings fun, stress-free, and effective for all ages, from infants and toddlers experiencing their very first visit to older children establishing solid oral care habits.

Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital dental x-rays are a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child&#x27;s teeth and are taken only when deemed necessary by the dentist. X-rays are taken for both diagnostic and preventive purposes. They are essential to help find dental decay or diseases that cannot be seen upon visual examination. They also help us evaluate the teeth for any potential dental anomaly’s like extra teeth, missing teeth, abnormal anatomy, etc. They are essential to help us monitor growth and developmental as your child’s dentition is constantly changing.

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to strengthen enamel and help prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride varnish is applied, which initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals. Fluoride treatments are both safe and effective in decreasing the prevalence of cavities in children.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors and are one of the most common preventive treatments performed today! We paint a clear, safe resin on your child&#x27;s teeth that acts like a shield to keep away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Oral Habits

Non-nutritive sucking habits like finger-sucking and pacifiers are considered normal in infants and young children. Long-term continuation of these habits past the age of 3 can lead to dental developmental changes like an anterior open bite or a crossbite leading to negative effects on jaw growth. Our team can help guide you with cessation protocols and educate you on the appropriate ways to address the habit if it continues long-term.
