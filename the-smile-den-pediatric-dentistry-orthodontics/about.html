Monthly Raffles & No-Cavity Club!

Join the excitement at The Smile Den by participating in our monthly raffle, where every visit could be your ticket to winning fantastic prizes as a token of our appreciation. Additionally, young smiles can aim for the prestigious No-Cavity Club, earning rewards and recognition for maintaining excellent oral hygiene and making dental visits a positive and cavity-free experience.

Kid & Parent-Approved Amenities

We’re passionate about showing children and adults alike that going to the dentist can and should be an enjoyable experience! Don’t believe us? Come see for yourself!

Kid’s Play Area

At The Smile Den, young patients will feel right at home. Our play area offers a fun and engaging space where they can relax and enjoy themselves before or after their dental appointments.

Beverage Station

Enjoy our complimentary beverage station where you can relax and get a cup of coffee or tea during your child’s appointment.

TVs with Disney+

Your kiddos have the option to enjoy their favorite shows and movies during their visit at The Smile Den with our TVs featuring Disney+, ensuring a positive, relaxing experience.

Prize Wall

Your child can explore and celebrate their dental milestones at The Smile Den&#x27;s prize wall, where they can choose exciting rewards for their commitment to oral health.

Noise Canceling Headphones

Sometimes the sounds in the dental office can be overwhelming and stressful, especially for little ones. Using our noise canceling headphones, we’re able to minimize stimulation so your child can have a positive experience!

Fidget Toys, Stress Balls, & Stuffed Animals

We know that young patients process their environment in many different ways, which is why we’ve equipped The Smile Den with all kinds of fidget toys and tools to help keep them regulated and calm.

Brushing Station

The best way to instill healthy oral habits is to make them fun! At our brushing station, we teach our young patients fun and engaging ways to maintain dental hygiene.

iTero Intraoral Scanner

Our iTero intraoral scanner ensures a seamless process for enhanced diagnostics and treatment planning. With our digital impression technology, your child won’t have to deal with the discomfort of goopy impressions.
