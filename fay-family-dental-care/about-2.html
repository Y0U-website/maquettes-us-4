ABOUT Bringing Healthy Smiles to the Upper Sandusky and Wyandot County Community

Fay Family Dental Care delivers high quality dental services to help patients towards optimal dental health and bright smiles. In 2019, Dr. Stephen Hoover DDS and his staff welcomed a new Dentist to lead the practice, Dr. Morgan Fay.

Dr. Morgan Fay grew up in the Kirby area and graduated from Riverdale High School. Dr. Morgan Fay then continued her education and graduated from Bowling Green State University with a Bachelor of Business Administration, specializing in Accounting in 2014, and DDS from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry in 2019.

Dr. Fay enjoys all aspects of dentistry and loves working with families. She enjoys watching them grow together and building relationships with her patients.

Dr. Fay provides preventative and restorative dental procedures, root canals, extractions, and removable dentures, among other dental services. Dr. Fay is also excited to learn new clinical skills and the new technology that the dental field has to offer in hopes to provide patients with the best possible care.

Dr. Fay is accepting patients of all ages and is excited to bring healthy smiles to the community. Call 419-294-2436 to schedule your appointment.
