What to Expect

Your initial oral examination includes a visual examination, charting, periodontal probing, diagnose and treatment recommendations. We will also take x-rays, which includes the panoramic x-ray for proper diagnosis of the anterior (front) and posterior (back) teeth as well as the bite-wing x-ray series for proper diagnosis of decay in between teeth.
