Meet Dr. Abdelrazek

Dr. Abdelrazek is a recognized expert with extensive training in cosmetic , laser , implants, reconstructive and sedation dentistry with over 20 years of practical experience in various dental specialties.

Dr. Abdelrazek is a Faculty Club Member of the renowned Spears Institute in Scottsdale, Arizona. She is a preferred provider with invisalign and is certified in the treatment of Sleep Apnea (Appliances).

Dr. Abdelrazek has an associate Fellowship with the World Clinical Laser Institute, in which she is considered one of less than 3 % of dentists practicing Laser Dentistry, worldwide.

Her skill to providing excellent care to patients is paired with her warm personality.

Dr.Abdelrazek received her doctor of dental surgery degree (DDS) with honors in 1994 from the University of Alexandria Faculty of Dentistry, Egypt. She then spent a year of internship in general dentistry at the University of Alexandria.faculty of dentistry completed 1995. She subsequently attended a two year residency at the University of Maryland at Baltimore (advanced education in general dentistry program ) where she received extensive postdoctoral training in advanced procedures of general dentistry in 2000.

In 2003 Dr. Abdelrazek established her private practice and has been practicing there to the present date. She recently celebrated the office’s 14th anniversary. She is an active member with the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Northern Virginia Dental Society, American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, International Congress of Oral Implantology, The Dental Organization of Conscious Sedation, and World Congress of Minimally Invasive Dentistry.​
