What Is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical periodontal treatment used to remove plaque, tartar, and bacteria from below the gumline .

The procedure consists of two important steps:

Scaling

Scaling removes plaque and hardened tartar from the surfaces of teeth and beneath the gumline.

Root Planing

Root planing smooths the tooth roots to help the gums reattach to the teeth and reduce bacterial buildup.

This deep cleaning procedure helps stop the progression of gum disease and promotes healthier gums.

Scaling and Root Planing FAQ

Is scaling and root planing painful?

Local anesthesia is often used during the procedure to ensure patient comfort.

How long does scaling and root planing take?

Treatment time depends on the severity of gum disease but is typically completed in one or two appointments.

Is scaling and root planing the same as a regular cleaning?

No. Scaling and root planing is a deep cleaning procedure that removes bacteria below the gumline, while regular cleanings focus on the tooth surfaces above the gums.

How long does it take for gums to heal after treatment?

Gum healing typically occurs over several weeks as inflammation decreases and the gums reattach to the teeth.

Can scaling and root planing cure gum disease?

While it may not completely cure advanced periodontal disease, scaling and root planing can significantly control the infection and prevent further damage.
