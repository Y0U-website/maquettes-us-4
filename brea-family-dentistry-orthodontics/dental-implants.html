Dental Implants Replace More Than the Visible Tooth

A dental implant is designed to replace the missing tooth from the foundation up. The implant is placed in the jawbone to create a stable base, and after healing, a custom restoration is attached to complete the tooth.

Because the implant works independently, it does not rely on neighboring teeth for support in the same way as a traditional bridge. This can help preserve the surrounding teeth while restoring chewing function and stability.

Treatment usually begins with detailed imaging and an evaluation of bone support, gum health, bite, and overall oral health. After placement, the implant is given time to integrate with the jawbone before the final crown or restoration is completed.

For patients replacing several missing teeth, options such as implant-supported dentures may also be considered depending on the condition of the mouth and treatment goals.
