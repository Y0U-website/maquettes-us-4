Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Avendano is originally from Newport Beach, CA, and he moved to Reno with his fiancé, who is originally from the area. Since settling here, they along with their dachshund, Jaxx have loved getting to know the Spanish Springs community. An interesting fact about Dr. Avendano is that he enjoys collecting vinyl and going to live music events. He played several instruments many years ago, but now his passion has shifted to discovering and supporting upcoming artists. In his free time, he enjoys walking the dog, grilling, cooking, traveling, reading, and watching or playing sports.
