Insurance Information

We are proud to offer high quality dentistry that fits within your budget.

Quality dental care is important for you and your family and we don’t want you to worry about how you’re going to pay for it. That’s why we’ve developed several payment options for you.

We accept most dental insurance plans. If your insurance plan doesn’t offer dental coverage or you’re unable to afford dental insurance, become a member of our Smile Care Dental Savings Plan to receive reduced rates for you or your family.

Additionally, we accept all major credit cards and bank debit cards. You can also finance your treatment through a variety of third-party companies such as Compassionate Finance.
