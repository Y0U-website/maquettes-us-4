Dr. Taylor Cook

Dr. Taylor Cook completed his dental training at the University of Iowa. Developing a special interest in endodontics, he continued with his studies at Boston University, training extensively on the latest in endodontic therapies and earning his Certificate in Advanced Graduate Study. Dr. Cook continues to attend educational courses on a regular basis, keeping up with the latest advances in endodontics including root canal therapy, retreatment, pulpotomies, and apicoectomies.

Dr. Cook’s experiences travelling broadly and living in many different areas offers him the gift of appreciating and developing meaningful relationships with patients of different backgrounds. His empathy, gentle touch and compassionate demeanor are appreciated by both patients and team.

When he’s not taking care of patients and their smiles, he enjoys skiing, biking, disc golf and cheering on his favorite Penguins.

A member of the American Association of Endodontists, the American Dental Association as well as the Pennsylvania Dental Association, Dr. Cook sees patients at the Fox Chapel and Lower Burrell (PA) offices.

Dr. Maria Jun

Dr. Maria Jun received her Dental Degree from the University of Connecticut in 2019 and continued on to receive her Certificate in Endodontics at Temple University in 2021. Prior to her dental education, Dr. Jun earned her Bachelor of Arts degree at Wellesley College studying Chemistry. Dr. Jun’s goal is to create a comfortable and relaxing environment for patients needing endodontic therapy while providing quality and optimal treatment. Outside of the dental clinic, Dr. Jun is an active member of her church community, and loves to watch movies, play music, and spend time with her husband.

Dr. Teresa Capristo

Dr. Capristo is a board-certified endodontist. She is native of Titusville, PA and graduated from Titusville High School in 2004. She graduated Summa Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from Gannon University in 2008. She earned a Doctor of Dental Surgery from West Virginia University in 2012 and was directly commissioned into the United States Navy.

In 2013, she completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at the James A. Lovell Federal Health Care Center while stationed at Great Lakes Naval Station. She served aboard the USS GERMANTOWN homeported in Sasebo, Japan, and at Naval Health Clinics in San Diego, Bahrain, and Washington Navy Yard during her active-duty service in the Navy.

Dr. Capristo transferred to the Navy Reserves during endodontics residency. She graduated from University of Pittsburgh Dental School of Medicine with a Certificate in Endodontics in 2024.

Currently, Dr. Capristo is practicing as an endodontist for Precision Endodontics in Pittsburgh and is a Major in the US Air Force Reserves. She is a Diplomate of the American Board of Endodontics and a Fellow in the Academy of General Dentistry. She is also an active member of the Pennsylvania Dental Association and American Association of Endodontics. Dr. Capristo enjoys spending time with her husband, Michael, and goldendoodle, Liberty. Dr. Capristo enjoys the outdoors and has interests in playing pickleball, down-hill skiing and traveling.
