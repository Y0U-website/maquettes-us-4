What are All on 4 Implants?

This method of dental treatment involves the placement of dentures that are supported by implants within the mouth. It can be frustrating to have traditional dentures that slip out of place, so All on Four Implants were created to give you the benefit of dentures without these worries.

This top-of-the-line dental technology is designed to improve your smile and restore your confidence. We want to be sure that you are completely happy with your smile, which is why we offer different types of dental restorations for you to choose from.

The Benefits of All-on-4 Implants

All-on-4 dental implants offer a secure, long lasting alternative to traditional removable dentures. A full arch of replacement teeth is supported by just four implants, so your smile stays comfortably in place while you eat, speak, and laugh. Without adhesives or slipping, you can enjoy more confidence in everyday life. Because implants act similarly to natural tooth roots, they can also help stimulate the jawbone and support facial structure over time. In many cases, All on 4 treatment provides a streamlined path to a fixed, natural looking smile that is designed to last for years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are All-on-4 dental implants the same as All-on-4 dentures?

They work together: All-on-4 uses four dental implants to permanently anchor a full arch of teeth (a fixed implant denture), so it stays in place and won’t slip like a traditional removable denture.

Do you offer All-on-4 dental implants near me in Arlington Heights?

Yes. New Family Dental provides All-on-4 (full-arch) dental implants in Arlington Heights, replacing a full set of teeth with a secure, natural-looking result.

Am I a candidate for All-on-4?

Many people who’ve lost most or all of their teeth, or whose teeth are failing, are good candidates. As an all-on-four provider, we’ll evaluate your jawbone and health and walk you through your options.
