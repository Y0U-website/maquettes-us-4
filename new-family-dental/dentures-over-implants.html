Overdentures in Arlington Heights, IL

Missing teeth can lead to a number of dental problems, so it is important that you work closely with an experienced dentist to learn more about the dental restoration treatments that are available. Here at New Family Dental, we offer state-of-the-art technology to treat all of your dental needs , including Dentures Over Implants in Arlington Heights, IL.

Basic, traditional dentures can be used to restore your smile, but many patients find that these basic dentures are a hassle to wear. It can be frustrating to have the dentures move out of place. Also, some people find that it is difficult to maintain regular daily functions such as talking or eating.

Instead of being frustrated with traditional dentures, consider the benefits of new technology that is available: Dentures Over Implants . This treatment method combines the use of dental implants to hold your dentures in place. These implants are secured into your mouth, and it is easy to hook the dentures on. As a result, you can remove the dentures as needed, and still maintain a semi-permanent placement for the dentures when they are attached within your mouth.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Overdentures?

Overdentures can be a great option if you are missing most or all of your teeth and would like more stability than traditional dentures provide. With an implant supported overdenture, a removable denture attaches to a small number of carefully placed dental implants for a more secure and comfortable fit.

Overdentures may be used for the upper, or maxillary, arch and the lower, or mandibular, arch. They can be especially helpful for lower dentures, which often have less natural stability. At New Family Dental, we provide overdentures in Arlington Heights, IL and will evaluate your jawbone, gums, and smile goals to recommend the implant option that fits your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between overdentures and traditional dentures?

A: Overdentures snap onto dental implants, so they’re far more stable than traditional dentures. No slipping, better chewing, and no messy adhesives. You still get the affordability of a denture with the security of implants.

Do you offer upper and lower overdentures?

Yes, we provide both upper (maxillary) and lower (mandibular) overdentures, and we’ll recommend how many implants each needs for a secure, comfortable fit.
