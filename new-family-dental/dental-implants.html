If you are missing a tooth, then you don’t need to dread your appointment to see the dentist. Dental Implants in our Arlington Heights, IL office are an effective way to replace the missing tooth, and the new dental implant will blend in naturally with the rest of your teeth.

Benefits of a Tooth Implant

The greatest benefit to having a dental implant placed within your mouth is that the implant will function like your original tooth. The implant is designed with a post that is first placed within the jaw bone , then the bone needs time to heal and assimilate the post. After a few months, the post will be held securely in place, allowing a dental crown to be placed on top of the post to mimic your original tooth.

Once this dental implant is in place, you will love the way you smile looks. By offering the best dental technology, we make your life easier so that you can experience these dental treatments with minimal interruption to your busy schedule.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

One of the biggest benefits of dental implants is that they are easy to care for. You can brush and floss them much like natural teeth, and with consistent home care and regular dental visits, they can provide a long lasting solution for missing teeth. Because the titanium implant post bonds with the jawbone, it can also help support the bone and maintain the natural shape of your smile.

Whether you are replacing one tooth or several, the team at New Family Dental will show you how to care for your dental implants in Arlington Heights, IL and keep your gums healthy for the long term.
