Single Tooth Implant Replace one missing tooth with a standalone implant and crown. Preserves adjacent teeth
  • Prevents bone loss
  • Natural appearance
  • Easy maintenance

Implant Bridge

Replace multiple adjacent missing teeth with an implant-supported bridge.

  • Replaces 3-4 teeth with 2 implants
  • Cost-effective solution
  • Fixed, non-removable
  • Natural chewing function

Implant-Supported Denture

Secure a denture with implants for improved stability and comfort.

  • Snap-on or fixed options
  • No adhesives needed
  • Improved chewing ability
  • Prevents denture slippage

Full Arch Restoration

Replace an entire arch of teeth with a permanent implant solution.

  • All-on-4 or All-on-6 options
  • Permanent, fixed teeth
  • Life-changing transformation
  • Long-lasting results

Which Option Is Right for You?

Every patient is unique. During your consultation, Dr. Allen will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and recommend the implant solution that best fits your needs and budget.
