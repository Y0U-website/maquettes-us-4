The practice

We are your leading pediatric dentists who love the quality of your child’s smile. Our commitment is to give a safe, fun and educational dental experience that incorporates cleanings, white fillings, braces to dental emergencies.

Ranging from orthodontics and emergency care, your child always comes first. With numerous years of education and expertise, your child’s dental health will always be in good hands.

Each and every location has a unique theme catered to your child’s interests. We are proud to have four convenient locations ranging from under-the-sea adventures to the jungles of the rainforest. Learn more about each location and the same quality dentistry your child will experience.

The exam/procedure room is designed with your child in mind. Your child is in a horizontal position with a tv monitor overhead to distract them. Hygienists and dentists alike take their time explaining to both parents and the child what is going on during the cleaning/procedure. When your child looks forward to going to the dentist, you know you’ve hit the jackpot!
