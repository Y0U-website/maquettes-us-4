WE WANT YOU COMFORTABLE !

Wouldn’t it be wonderful if you could look forward to going to the dentist ? Dr Gruell and Dr Creese are highly trained in giving comfortable injections and in techniques for minimizing pain. Learn more about how we look after you below.

We all want a comfortable dental experience that does not make us nervous. So at our dental office in Alameda we are VERY FOCUSED on keeping you comfortable and want to provide every patient with the most comfortable dental work they will ever receive. This means not making one rule for everyone. We have perfected our comfort dentistry with thousands of patients which makes Dr Gruell and Dr Creese two of the most experienced dentists in the area. No wonder people travel for miles to see them!

Comfort dentistry is just as it sounds – dental care that is more comfortable, less painful and so less scary! To minimize the trauma of dental care requires a combination of dental skill and the latest dental technology. Here is how we combine the very best equipment with the skilled hands of the dentist to make visits more comfortable and less painful.

LESS PAINFUL INJECTIONS

  • We use the smallest needles (= less ow’) and skill to give the gentlest injections
  • Doctor is watching for comfort signs all the time including eye blinks, body movement and behavior
  • Doctors score 100 % on UCLA manual dexterity scores for dentists
  • Our Dentists have The Hands’ as they say in the dental profession and are highly experienced Comfort Dentists’
