TEETH CLEANING

Routine dental cleanings are important for dental health and hygiene.

As well as regular cleanings it’s important to brush and floss every day. Water picking can also be very helpful. Maintaining good dental routines will help minimize the likelihood of problems with teeth or gums.

  • Every 6 months or more frequently
  • Take about an hour
  • Trained hygienists remove plaque and tartar

INVISALIGN

Invisalign is a great way to move your teeth into a big welcoming smile.

Once the Doctor ensures that Invisalign would be a good fit for you, aligners are ordered and the process begins. Your teeth move slowly and comfortably and you will start to see results within a few weeks.

  • A custom fit invisible tray for your teeth
  • Remove trays when eating
  • That’s it!

Cosmetic Dentistry

Every day wear and tear on the teeth can cause lots of damage over time

Cosmetic dentistry improves the appearance of your smiles. It includes whitening discolored teeth and fixing mild or severely damaged teeth (such as cracks and breaks) with customized cosmetic procedures.

  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Teeth Whitening

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a great way to address missing teeth or replace teeth that are not save-able.

A dental implant is an artificial tooth that is securely placed into your jaw for where there is one or more teeth missing. They look and feel like your own teeth and are cared for like normal teeth.

  • Very natural looking
  • Durable and long-term
  • Improve ability to chew and speak

GENERAL DENTISTRY

General dentistry is the prevention and treatment of everyday dental problems.

Regular cleanings and home routines are the best defense against cavities, tooth decay and gum disease. When these are present we treat these issues with the latest techniques.

  • Teeth cleaning
  • Fillings
  • Extractions

CONSCIOUS SEDATION

No matter how gentle we are, we know people still get anxious and nervous.

With oral conscious sedation, you are given a pill prior to your appointment and your heart rate and oxygen are closely monitored. Anxious patients find it incredibly helpful.

  • Safe and convenient
  • Combats fear and anxiety
  • No lasting effects

VENEERS

Dental veneers are ultra-thin, custom-crafted shells designed to cover the front surface of your teeth, enhancing their appearance.

The veneer application process is typically minimally invasive, involving a few visits. Your dentist will carefully prepare the teeth, take impressions, and bond the veneers securely.

  • Address various cosmetic concerns
  • Blend with your natural teeth
  • No healing time required
