Braces

Metal

Classic and reliable, metal braces are the go-to choice for a reason! They’re super durable and great for all kinds of orthodontic needs, making them a popular pick for kids, teens, and adults alike. Plus, with fun-colored bands, you can customize your look at every visit!

Clear

Want the power of braces without the metal look? Clear braces are a fantastic option! They blend in with your teeth, making them a more subtle choice, but still deliver the same amazing results. It’s all about getting that dream smile while keeping things low-key!

Clear Aligners

Invisalign ®

Say hello to straight teeth without the wires! As a Platinum Invisalign® Provider, TW Orthodontics delivers expert-level Invisalign® care you can trust. Our aligners are nearly invisible, super comfortable, and perfect for anyone who wants to straighten their smile on the sly. Just pop them in and go about your day — it’s that easy!

Whitening

Ready to brighten up that smile? Our professional whitening treatments give you dazzling results quickly and safely, perfect for when you want to make your smile sparkle a little extra. Whether it’s for a big event or just because, we’ve got you covered!

A brighter smile can boost your confidence in a big way! Our team will help you find the perfect shade for a natural, radiant look. Get ready to show off your newly brightened smile with pride!

Retainers

Retainers are your smile’s best friend after braces or Invisalign®! As your Platinum Invisalign® Provider, we ensure your teeth stay in their new, perfect positions so your hard-earned results last a lifetime. Just wear them as directed, and you’re good to go!

We have a variety of retainer options to fit your lifestyle, from clear retainers to traditional wire ones. No matter which you choose, keeping your smile in tip-top shape has never been easier!

Technology

3D Scanner

Our high-tech 3D scanner is like having a magic wand for your smile! It creates a detailed, digital map of your teeth with precision and ease, making the whole process smooth and super accurate. No more messy impressions— just a fast, fun way to get the perfect fit!

3D Printing

Embrace the future with 3D printing! We use this technology to craft custom braces and aligners that fit your smile perfectly. It’s all about creating the best possible results with cutting-edge tech that makes your orthodontic journey a breeze!
