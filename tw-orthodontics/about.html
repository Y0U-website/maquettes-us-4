About Us

About Us Our Mission At TW Orthodontics, we’re all about going the extra mile to make sure you get the smile you’ve always wanted, with a variety of affordable treatment options to choose from.

Whether it’s Invisalign® or braces, our expert team — and proud Platinum Invisalign® Provider — is ready to help you find the best way to achieve your perfect smile. When you step into our office, you’re not just a patient—you’re part of our family!

Dr. Nick Turner, DMD, MS

Dr. Turner, a Birmingham native, graduated magna cum laude with a Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Sciences from Auburn University in 2005. Following in the footsteps of his father, uncle, and grandfather, he attended the University of Alabama School of Dentistry and earned his Doctorate of Dental Medicine in 2009. He then completed his Orthodontic Specialty residency at UAB, finishing in April 2012.

Dr. Turner and his wife, Penny, are Enterprise born and raised -- and now they’re raising their own crew of awesome kids right back where it all started. Reese (11), Will (9), and Ben (6) are keeping life full of energy, laughs, and snack crumbs. When he’s not crafting confident smiles, you’ll find Dr. T hanging out with family and friends, working on his golf swing (watch out, PGA), and cheering on his Auburn Tigers -- War Eagle! The Turner family are active members of First Baptist Church in Enterprise, where community and faith are at the heart of it all.

Dr. Brett Wood, DMD, MS

Dr. Wood earned his Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Sciences from Auburn University and his Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry. After completing his residency at the Veterans Administration Hospital in Birmingham, he went on to receive his Master of Clinical Science in Orthodontics from UAB.

Dr. Wood and his wife, Kate, have been married since 2013 and now call Selma, AL -- Dr. Wood’s hometown -- home. They have three wonderful children: Aimee (11), George (9), and Charlie (6). The Wood family is deeply rooted in their community and are actively involved in their church (Blue Jean Church), their children’s school, and various local projects. When he’s not busy perfecting smiles, Dr. Wood enjoys time with family and friends and can usually be found outdoors -- boating, golfing, hunting, gardening, or cruising around on his tractor.

Dr. Jeremy Scarpate, DMD, MS

Dr. Jeremy Scarpate is a board-certified orthodontist who graduated magna cum laude from Auburn University in Biomedical Sciences before earning his dental degree at the University of Florida College of Dentistry. He completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at Eglin Air Force Base, then specialized in orthodontics at UT Health Houston, where he also taught in the military’s residency program. A retired US Air Force officer, he now brings precision, energy, and a love for creating custom smiles to TW Orthodontics.

Outside of the office, he’s a proud dad of four (including triplets), an adventure seeker who’s just as comfortable biking trails and tackling whitewater rapids as he is cheering for his Auburn Tigers and Arsenal Gunners—all while trying to prove to his kids he’s still got it!
