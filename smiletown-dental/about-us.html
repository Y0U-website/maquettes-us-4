Meet Dr.

Karan Doomra, DDS

Dr. Karan Doomra is a dedicated and passionate dentist who graduated from the NYU College of Dentistry in 2016. As a committed professional, he is a proud member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Central Florida Dental Association (CFDA), and the International Congress of Implantologists, staying actively engaged in the latest advancements in dental care.

Dr. Doomra has always envisioned himself as a “smile architect,” devoted to enhancing smiles, improving oral health, and educating patients about the importance of dental care. His mission is to provide compassionate, high-quality treatment that not only restores dental function but also boosts confidence and well-being.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Doomra leads a fulfilling personal life with his beautiful wife, two daughters, two dogs, and three birds. He has an adventurous spirit and enjoys thrilling sports like skydiving. After work, he cherishes spending time with his family, creating lasting memories with them. Fluent in both English and Hindi, Dr. Doomra connects with a diverse range of patients, ensuring they feel comfortable and well-informed about their dental health.
