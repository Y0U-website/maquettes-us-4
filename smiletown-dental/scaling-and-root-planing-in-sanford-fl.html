Understanding Scaling and Root Planing

Scaling and root planing is a non-surgical deep cleaning treatment designed to combat periodontal disease. This procedure removes harmful plaque and tartar deposits from above and beneath the gumline. The root planing phase smooths tooth root surfaces, allowing your gums to reattach naturally and heal properly.

Smile Town Dental delivers professional scaling and root planing services to restore your gum health and protect against serious oral complications.
