Types of Dental Implants We offer a variety of dental implant options to suit different needs: Full Mouth Implants : Ideal for patients needing a complete teeth replacement.
  • Mini Implants : Smaller in size, they are often used for stabilizing dentures.
  • All-on-4 : A revolutionary technique that replaces all teeth in either the upper or lower jaw with just four implants.
  • Single Tooth Implants : Perfect for replacing individual missing teeth.

Dental Implants Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Durability : Implants can last many years, even a lifetime with proper care.
  • Functionality : They function like natural teeth, allowing for comfortable eating and speaking.
  • Aesthetic Appeal : Implants look and feel like your own teeth.
  • Jawbone Health : They stimulate and preserve jawbone, preventing bone loss.

Cons

  • Surgical Procedure : Implants require surgery, which involves a recovery period.
  • Cost : The initial investment is higher compared to other tooth replacement options.
  • Time : The process from start to finish can take several months.

Are Implants Worth It?

Considering the long-term benefits of dental implants, many patients find them to be a worthwhile investment. They offer a permanent solution to tooth loss and can significantly improve quality of life.

The Dental Implant Process The process for getting dental implants generally includes:

  • Consultation : Dr. Kostrzewski will assess your oral health and discuss implant options.
  • Preparation : If necessary, preparatory procedures like extractions or bone grafting are done.
  • Implant Placement : The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing : A period of healing is required for the implant to integrate with the bone.
  • Abutment Placement : A small connector is placed on top of the implant once it has bonded with the jawbone.
  • Final Restoration : The custom-made replacement tooth (crown) is then attached to the abutment. This tooth will be shaped and painted to seamlessly blend with your smile.
