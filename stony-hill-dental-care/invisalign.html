Alternatives to Invisalign®

Other orthodontic treatment options include:

  • Traditional Braces: Effective for a wide range of dental alignment issues.
  • Clear Braces: Similar to traditional braces but with clear or tooth-colored brackets.
  • Lingual Braces: Placed on the back of your teeth, hidden from view.

What Is the Process for Getting Invisalign®?

The journey to a straighter smile with Invisalign® involves several steps:

  • Consultation: Dr. Kostrzewski will assess your teeth and discuss your orthodontic goals.
  • Custom Treatment Plan: If Invisalign® is right for you, a personalized treatment plan will be created using 3D imaging technology.
  • Receiving Your Aligners: You’ll receive a series of custom aligners to wear, changing them every 1-2 weeks.
  • Regular Check-Ups: Periodic appointments will be necessary to monitor progress and make adjustments as needed.

How Long Do I Need to Wear Invisalign® Retainers?

After completing your Invisalign® treatment, you’ll need to wear retainers to maintain your new smile. Initially, you might need to wear them full-time, but over time this will decrease to only wearing them at night.

Invisalign® FAQs

How often do I need to wear my Invisalign® aligners?

For optimal results, wear your aligners for 20-22 hours per day. A good rule is to only remove your aligners for meals and the occasional special event.

Will Invisalign® affect my speech?

Some people might notice a slight lisp initially, but this typically goes away as you get used to the aligners.

Can I remove my aligners for special occasions?

Yes, one of the benefits of Invisalign ® is that you can remove your aligners for brief periods. Just remember to put them back on as soon as possible for optimal results.

How do I clean my Invisalign® aligners?

Clean them daily with a soft toothbrush and lukewarm water, avoiding hot water that can warp the plastic.

How long is an Invisalign® treatment?

Treatment times for Invisalign ® will vary depending on the complexity of your case. Some treatments can be as short as nine months, but others can be 15 months or longer. Dr. Kostrzewski will be able to give you a specific timeline as he assesses your Invisalign ® journey.

Can I move to my next set of aligners early?

While we understand your eagerness to reach your dream smile, we don’t recommend changing your aligners before instructed. Trying to move your teeth too quickly with clear aligners runs the risk of causing permanent damage to your smile, like tooth shrinkage, instability in the teeth, or teeth falling out.
