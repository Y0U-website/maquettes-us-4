The All-on-4 Procedure

The process of getting All-on-4 dental implants is straightforward:

  • Consultation and Planning: Dr. Kostrzewski evaluates your oral health and discusses the procedure.
  • Implant Placement: Four implants are precisely placed in areas with the highest bone density.
  • Temporary Restoration: A temporary set of teeth is attached, allowing you to eat and speak normally during the healing process.
  • Final Restoration: Once healed, your custom-made, permanent teeth are affixed, completing your new smile.

All-on-4 FAQs

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

With proper maintenance, the implant posts can last many years, often a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth can also last 20 decades or more when well cared for!

Is the procedure painful?

Discomfort is minimal, and we ensure you’re as comfortable as possible during the procedure. Common over-the-counter pain medications are often enough to manage any pain or discomfort following the surgery.

How do I care for my All-on-4 implants?

Care involves regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental check-ups, just like natural teeth. The better your oral health, the longer your implants and prosthetics will last.

Can anyone get All-on-4 implants?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates, though some conditions like severe bone loss may need to be addressed first.

Can I get All-on-4 when I’ve had bone loss?

If you’ve already lost some bone density in your jaw, you may still qualify for All-on-4 implants. Thanks to their innovative angled installation, these implants can work in a variety of jaws. If your bone loss is too severe for safe implantation, we’ll let you know.
