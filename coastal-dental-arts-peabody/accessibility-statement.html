Here are some recommendations to enhance your browsing experience: General Accessibility Tips If you have difficulty seeing web pages, the US Social Security Administration offers advice on optimizing your computer and browser for a better online experience: Use your computer to read web pages aloud : Many operating systems and browsers offer text-to-speech functionality.
  • Navigate with your keyboard : Most web pages can be navigated using keyboard shortcuts.
  • Increase text size : Adjust the text size in your browser settings for easier reading.
  • Magnify your screen : Use screen magnification tools available in your operating system.
  • Change background and text colors : Customize color settings to reduce eye strain.

  • Closed Captioning : Closed captions provide a transcript for the audio track of videos, synchronized with the video and audio.   • Volume Controls : Adjust the volume using your device’s controls and the media player’s controls to optimize audio levels. Each device and media player has its own volume settings that can be modified for the best listening experience.
