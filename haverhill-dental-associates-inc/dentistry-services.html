Preventative Dentistry

A healthy bright smile starts with regular dental visits. Young children should visit the dentist by the age of 3 to start healthy dental habits and to track development. But no matter your age, there’s no time like the present to start again.

Our dentists can evaluate your teeth, gums, tongue and palate for signs of poor oral health. X-rays reveal cavities, cracks in your teeth and periodontal issues and design a treatment plan that can positively impact your smile.

Our friendly licensed hygienists will remove gingivitis-causing plaque and calculus from your teeth and around the gum line. They will prescribe periodontal treatments as necessary to treat or prevent gum disease.

Restorative Dentistry

When a treatment plan is in place to restore your smile, Haverhill Dental Associates takes the impact of the available treatments and your life into consideration. Our foremost agenda is to stop pain and continued deterioration.

The deterioration infects the nerves in the roots of your teeth and causes excruciating pain. Remember this: it causes you more pain to forego a root canal when you need it than to actually have the root canal. We offer nitrous and local anesthesia so you won’t feel a thing.

As a last recourse, sometimes it’s best to remove a tooth. With proper healing, Haverhill Dental Associates offers several cosmetic options to restore your confidence.

Cosmetic Dentistry

When you’re looking to flash a beautiful white smile, Haverhill Dental Associates is the dentist for you. We have solutions that will whiten your teeth, fix your bite and replace missing. Consult with Dr. Palmer Lewis Jr. for the solutions that work best for you.

Close the gap between healthy teeth and a beautiful smile literally with bridges and crowns. Our dental staff provides natural looking solutions for a more confident smile.

Haverhill Dental Associates offers custom made bleaching trays and take-home whitening kits for a mega-watt smile.
