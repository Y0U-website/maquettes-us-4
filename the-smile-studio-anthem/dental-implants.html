What are Dental Implants & ‍ How Do They Work?

Dental implants are an incredible solution for those who have lost one or more teeth. Made from biocompatible titanium, each implant serves as a secure, durable “root” for a custom tooth replacement that looks and functions like a natural tooth. Our dental implant process at The Smile Studio involves expertly anchoring these implants to the jawbone, ensuring a stable, permanent foundation. Patients enjoy a tooth replacement that feels, looks, and behaves just like their original teeth, allowing them to speak, chew, and smile with confidence.

What are the Different Types of Dental Implants?

Every smile is unique, and so are dental implant options. At The Smile Studio, we offer a range of solutions to suit individual needs, including:

Our team will work with you to determine the ideal implant solution, whether you need one tooth replaced or a full set of mouth implants to transform your smile.

  • Single Tooth Implants – Ideal for replacing one missing tooth
  • Implant Bridge – Ideal for replacing 3 or more adjacent teeth
  • All-on-4 Dental Implants – Perfect for full-arch restoration with just four implants
  • Full-Mouth Dental Implants – Comprehensive option for replacing an entire set of teeth

What are the Steps Involved in Getting a Dental Implant?

Getting dental implants is a multi-step process, and our Phoenix practice is here to support you at every stage. Here’s what you can expect during the implant journey at The Smile Studio:

Our implant dental experts guide you through the entire journey, making sure you feel informed, comfortable, and confident.

  • Consultation and Evaluation – We’ll begin with a detailed consultation and imaging to assess your unique needs and ensure the best implant plan.
  • Treatment Planning and 3D Imaging – Using advanced 3D imaging technology, we map out precise implant placement for optimal stability and function.
  • Surgical Placement of Implants – Implants are anchored into the jawbone in a safe, comfortable procedure. With our team’s skill, the process is smooth and efficient.
  • Healing and Osseointegration – Over the next few months, your implants fuse naturally with the jawbone, creating a solid foundation.
  • Placement of Custom Crowns – Once healed, we attach custom-made crowns that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth for a beautiful, functional smile.
