What is a Single-Tooth Implant?

A single-tooth implant is a permanent solution that replaces one missing tooth using a small titanium post anchored in the jawbone and topped with a custom dental crown. The implant acts as an artificial tooth root, preserving bone health and preventing the shifting of nearby teeth.

Unlike removable bridges or dentures, a single-tooth implant is fixed, durable, and designed to blend seamlessly with your smile. It restores full chewing function and maintains the natural contours of your jawline, ensuring both beauty and long-term stability.

What Are the Steps Involved in Getting a Dental Implant?

Getting a dental implant is a multi-step process, and our team supports you at every stage.

Step 1 – Consultation & Evaluation

We begin with a detailed exam and imaging to evaluate your jawbone, bite, and gum health, ensuring you are a good candidate.

Step 2 – 3D Treatment Planning

Using advanced 3D digital technology, we plan precise implant placement for optimal stability and esthetics.

Step 3 – Surgical Implant Placement

The implant post is gently placed into the bone in a comfortable, efficient procedure with sedation and relaxation options available.

Step 4 – Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with the jawbone, creating a strong and stable foundation.

Step 5 – Custom Crown Placement

Once healed, a custom-crafted crown is attached to complete your smile. The restoration is color-matched and shaped to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

How Long Will My Single-Tooth Implant Last?

With proper care, a single-tooth implant can last 15 to 25 years or longer. Durability depends on regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings.
