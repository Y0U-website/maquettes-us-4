What is an Implant-Supported Bridge?

An implant-supported bridge is a custom restoration that replaces multiple missing teeth using two or more tooth implants as anchors. Instead of relying on adjacent teeth for support, the bridge is securely attached to titanium implants placed in the jawbone, creating a stable and natural foundation.

This treatment restores chewing ability, speech, and aesthetics while preventing bone loss in the area where teeth are missing. Every case at The Smile Studio is digitally planned for precision and tailored to the unique needs of each patient, ensuring your new smile blends seamlessly with your natural teeth.

How Does an Implant-Supported Bridge Differ from a Traditional Bridge?

While both options replace missing teeth, the approach and long-term benefits differ:

Patients often find implant bridges feel more natural, require less maintenance, and offer a more permanent solution for tooth loss.

  • Traditional bridges rely on neighboring teeth that must be reshaped to hold the restoration.
  • Implant-supported bridges are anchored directly into the jawbone with dental implants.
  • Implants preserve bone structure and do not affect nearby healthy teeth.
  • The result is greater stability, longevity, and comfort.

How Long Does the Procedure Take for an Implant-Supported Bridge?

The timeline for implant bridge treatment typically includes several stages:

At The Smile Studio, our advanced technology and meticulous approach help streamline this process, providing predictable results and a comfortable experience.

How Long Will an Implant-Supported Bridge Last?

With proper care, an implant-supported bridge can last 15 to 25 years or more. The longevity of your restoration depends on maintaining healthy gums, practicing good oral hygiene, and attending regular dental visits.

Our team provides personalized maintenance tips and professional cleanings to help you care for your implants. Tools like floss threaders or water flossers make it easy to clean around your bridge, helping preserve your investment for decades.
