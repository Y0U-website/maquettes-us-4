Dr. Riki Kreitman leads a warm, long-tenured team that is excited to meet you and serve your dental needs in a friendly and welcoming environment.

A dentist, mentor, and Highland Park neighbor.

Dr. Riki Kreitman grew up in the Highland Park and Edison area and built her practice in the community she knows best. Patients often describe her as calm, direct, and reassuring: the kind of dentist who explains carefully, listens closely, and gives people room to make thoughtful decisions about their care.

She earned her BA in Biology from Douglass College at Rutgers University , her DMD from UMDNJ – New Jersey Dental School , and completed a General Practice Residency at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital . She later served there for more than a decade as an attending dentist and became the hospital’s Residency Program Director, mentoring younger dentists and treating medically complex patients.

In private practice, Dr. Kreitman focuses on cosmetic and restorative dentistry , dental sleep medicine , and Invisalign® orthodontics . She brings the same teaching mindset to patient care that she brought to hospital dentistry: make the diagnosis clear, explain the options plainly, and choose treatment that fits the person in front of her.
