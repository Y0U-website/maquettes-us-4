Safety, technology, and comfort.

Infection control

We offer the highest level of sterilization possible. Our team follows the infection control protocol set by OSHA, CDC and EPA. Most products are disposable and used only once. All reusable instruments are sterilized in our autoclave per standard of care. Operatory surfaces are disinfected between every patient.

Digital x-rays

Our office uses state-of-the-art Schick by Sirona digital radiography. The highly innovative sensor optimizes images and improves visibility of anatomical structures while keeping radiation exposure minimal — a fraction of traditional film.

Canker sores

Although the cause of canker sores is unknown, they are a painful nuisance that can last up to 14 days. If you’re experiencing one, we can treat it in our office and provide immediate relief — limiting the duration of your symptoms.
