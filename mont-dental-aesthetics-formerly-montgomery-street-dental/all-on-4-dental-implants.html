We will utilize local anesthesia, sedation, or a combination of both to ensure that you experience minimal discomfort or pain during the procedure. After the treatment, any post-operative discomfort can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.
  • 4. How do I care for my All-on-4® dental implants? Caring for your All-on-4 dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth. You will need to brush and floss regularly, as well as visit our office for routine check-ups and professional cleanings. Our team will provide you with detailed instructions and guidance on proper oral hygiene practices to maintain the longevity and appearance of your implants.
  • 5. Can I eat normally with All-on-4® dental implants? Yes, one of the significant advantages of All-on-4 dental implants is that they restore your ability to eat a wide range of foods. Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 implants allow you to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions, providing you with the freedom to savor a varied and nutritious diet.
