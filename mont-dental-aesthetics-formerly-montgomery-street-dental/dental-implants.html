The Tooth Implant Process: What to Expect

The journey to a restored smile involves several precise steps designed to ensure a lasting, successful result. Our team at Mont Dental Aesthetics guides you through every stage to ensure you remain comfortable and informed.

  • Consultation and Planning We begin with a comprehensive exam, including X-rays and 3D scans to evaluate your jawbone health and plan the ideal placement for your implant.
  • Implant Placement Surgery The titanium post is carefully placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure using local anesthesia for your comfort.
  • Healing and Osseointegration Over a few months, your jawbone heals and bonds with the implant, creating a permanent foundation for your new tooth.
  • Abutment Placement Once integrated, a small connector piece (the abutment) is attached to the implant to support the final restoration.
  • Final Restoration We take digital impressions to create a custom crown that matches your natural teeth, which is then securely attached to the abutment.

Types of Tooth Implant Solutions

Depending on your specific needs and oral health goals, Dr. Khoshvaghti offers several specialized implant options:

  • Single Tooth Implants : A stand-alone solution for one missing tooth that preserves the integrity of neighboring healthy teeth.
  • Multiple Tooth Implants : Used to support a dental bridge when several teeth are missing in a row, eliminating the need for removable partial dentures .
  • Full Arch Restoration ( All-on-4 ) : An efficient method using four to six implants to support a full set of new teeth for an entire upper or lower arch.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most individuals missing one or more teeth are eligible for implants. Key requirements include good general health and sufficient jawbone density to support the post. If bone loss has occurred, treatments like bone grafting can often create the necessary foundation.

Certain factors, such as uncontrolled diabetes or smoking, can affect the healing process. During your consultation, we will review your medical history and follow guidelines from organizations such as the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) to ensure your treatment is safe and effective.

Caring for Your New Smile

Maintaining dental implants is very similar to caring for natural teeth. With proper hygiene, they can last a lifetime. We recommend the following routine:

  • Brush twice daily using a soft-bristle toothbrush.
  • Floss daily around your implants and natural teeth.
  • Schedule regular dental check-ups and cleanings at Mont Dental Aesthetics.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are dental implants? Dental implants are posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They provide a stable foundation for fixed or removable replacement teeth that look and function like natural teeth. The implants fuse with the jawbone, creating a strong, permanent base for crowns, bridges or dentures. This integration ensures stability and durability, making dental implants an effective solution for restoring both the function and appearance of your smile.
  • Why choose dental implants over other tooth replacement options? Dental implants are a preferred choice because they provide a permanent, durable solution that mimics natural teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants prevent bone loss, maintain facial structure and offer superior comfort and functionality. They integrate with the jawbone, ensuring a stable and secure fit, and do not require alteration of adjacent teeth. Dental implants provide a long-term investment in your oral health, enhancing your quality of life and preserving your natural smile.
  • How long do dental implants last? With excellent oral hygiene and professional care, implants can last several decades, often a lifetime. The titanium post integrates with the bone to provide an exceptionally durable foundation.
  • Is the dental implant procedure painful? Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure. We use local anesthesia to numb the area, and any post-operative soreness is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain medication.
  • What is the cost of dental implants? The cost varies based on the number of implants, the need for bone grafting, and the type of final restoration. We provide a detailed cost estimate following your initial examination.
  • How do I care for my dental implants? Proper maintenance includes: Brushing twice daily with a soft brush.
  • Flossing daily around the implant site.
  • Visiting Mont Dental Aesthetics for regular cleanings.
  • What happens if I don&#x27;t replace a missing tooth? Leaving a gap can lead to shifting teeth, bite misalignment, and jawbone resorption, which may eventually alter your facial structure.
