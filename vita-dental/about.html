About

In an era where dental offices are increasingly corporate-owned, Drs. Shaftel and Jordan sought to create an independent, doctor-owned practice that places patient care above all else. Vita Dental is the result of that vision.

Vita Dental is comprised of a talented team of skilled professionals who collectively strive to make visiting the dentist a positive experience for each patient. Our doctors are highly selective when it comes to choosing the materials, equipment, and methods used in the practice and we only use those state-of-the-art products that have been proven through research to be industry leaders. Every decision made at Vita Dental is done so with the intention of giving our patients the highest quality, most comprehensive and personalized care.

The health of your mouth is directly linked to the health of your body, and a healthy body means a healthy life. We welcome you and your family to visit our conveniently located Mason office to experience the Vita Dental difference and to see for yourself how our personalized approach creates a healthier mouth and, in turn, a healthier life!
