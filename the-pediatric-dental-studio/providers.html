Biography

Jerrick W. Rose, DMD

Dr. Rose has extensive training in pediatric oral conscious sedation, operating room treatment under general anesthesia, treatment of dental emergencies and trauma, and treatment of special needs patients. Dr. Rose is fully dedicated to his home state and has made an unyielding commitment to serve Mississippi as a resident and quality health provider. He has a beautiful princess, Riley-Paige Rose, who loves sitting and talking with daddy, eating sushi, dancing, painting, and playing with her cousins!
