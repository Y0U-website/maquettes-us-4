At Trailside Dental, we provide comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages. We offer all the treatments you’d expect from a general dentist plus same-day emergency care and oral sedation options to reduce anxiety. Not sure what your smile needs? Start with an exam and cleaning, and we’ll go from there!

Routine radiographs, cleaning, and exam to ensure optimal dental health

Removing and repairing small cavities to prevent them from becoming larger and more problematic

When large cavities or fractures are present, restoring a tooth to its natural size and strength

Therapeutic bacterial removal to restore your gums natural health

Disinfecting a tooth from the inside-out to avoid further pain or discomfort

Fabricating custom appliances to combat grinding, snoring, and mild to moderate sleep apnea
