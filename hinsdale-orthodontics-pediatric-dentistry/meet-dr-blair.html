Outside the Office

Growing up in Oklahoma City around friendly people with strong family values helped shape me into the person I am today, so my family and friends are very important to me.

I absolutely love to travel and am fortunate to have been to many places all around the world. I continue to frequent museums and learn about history and art. I am passionate about animal rescue and have always been involved with local humane societies. I also enjoy running, particularly training for half-marathons.

My husband, Dr. Nick Riccio, is a Glen Ellyn native. He often talks about the benefits of growing up in a community with a small town feel outside of Chicago. I have grown to appreciate the charm and sense of community in the western suburbs of Chicago as well.

We recently welcomed our baby boy, Massimo, into the world and we re bursting with love! We are very excited to call Hinsdale home and raise our family here!
