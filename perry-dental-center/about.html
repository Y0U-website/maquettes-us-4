About

Proud to be Perry&#x27;s dentist.

Perry Dental Center is a brand new family and restorative dental practice right on Iowa Street in downtown Perry. We are locally owned, personally run, and built around the simple idea that good dental care should feel like being looked after by a neighbor. As a dentist in Perry, Iowa, we care for the whole family, from a toddler&#x27;s first checkup to a grandparent&#x27;s new crown. No pressure, no jargon, and no judgment. Just steady, high-quality care you can count on for years to come.

The people who will look after you.

Dr. Jack Thomas

Hello, my name is Jack Thomas, and I am very excited to be practicing dentistry in Perry. I grew up nearby in Dallas Center before moving to Osceola. I graduated from Interstate 35 High School and have always enjoyed being part of a small community. I received my bachelor&#x27;s degree from Simpson College, majoring in Biochemistry with a minor in Business Management. I then attended the University of Iowa, where I earned my Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Outside of dentistry, I enjoy spending time with my fiance Erin, our dog Brux, and hunting whenever I get the chance. I enjoy getting to know patients and helping them feel comfortable while providing high-quality dental care. It means so much to be a part of the Perry community, and I look forward to working with you all alongside the amazing staff at Perry Dental Center.

Erin

Erin grew up in Ankeny, Iowa and attended school at DMACC. She received her registered dental assistant certification in 2022 and earned her expanded functions certification in dental assisting in 2023. Since then, Erin has worked in general dentistry and periodontics. She plans to obtain her license in dental hygiene through DMACC in 2027.

In her free time, she enjoys house projects with her fiancé, walks with their dog, reading a good thriller, and spending time with family and friends. Erin always wants her patients to feel happy, comfortable, and safe when visiting our office.
