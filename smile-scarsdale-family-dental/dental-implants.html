How many days does it take for a dental implant to heal?

Initial healing after a dental implant placement typically takes about 7 to 10 days, during which swelling and discomfort usually subside. The full integration of the implant with the jawbone, known as osseointegration, generally takes 3 to 6 months. During this period, the implant gradually bonds with the bone.

What are the do s and don ts after dental implants?

  • Follow your dentist’s instructions carefully for oral hygiene and aftercare.
  • Eat soft foods for the first few days to avoid irritating the implant site.
  • Keep the area clean with gentle brushing and saltwater rinses as advised.
  • Avoid hard, sticky, or chewy foods that could dislodge the implant or cause discomfort.
  • Do not smoke or use tobacco products, as these can impair healing and increase the risk of complications.
  • Avoid vigorous physical activity for the first few days to prevent undue stress on the implant site.

What are the restrictions after dental implants?

After dental implants, you should avoid:

  • Eating hard or sticky foods that may disrupt the implant site.
  • Smoking and alcohol consumption during the initial healing phase, as they can interfere with healing.
  • Intense physical activities or heavy lifting for a few days post-surgery.

How long before dental implants feel normal?

Most patients start feeling more normal within a few weeks as the initial healing progresses. However, it can take a few months for the implant to fully integrate with the bone and for the final prosthetic to feel completely natural. The implant should feel normal once the healing is complete and the final restoration is in place.

Can I walk after a tooth implant?

Yes, you can walk after a tooth implant procedure. In fact, light walking is encouraged to maintain overall health and circulation. However, it’s advisable to avoid strenuous exercise or activities that might impact the implant site for the first few days to ensure proper healing.
