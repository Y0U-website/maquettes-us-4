Dental Sealants

  • Long-lasting Protection: Sealants can last for many years, providing long-term protection against decay and cavities.
  • Easy and Painless Application: The application process is simple, painless, and non-invasive, making it a stress-free experience for patients.
  • Cost-Effective: Dental sealants are a cost-effective solution, preventing the need for more extensive and expensive treatments like fillings and crowns.
  • Reduced Risk of Tooth Decay: Sealants significantly reduce the risk of tooth decay, promoting healthy teeth and a beautiful smile.
  • Maintenance of Dental Health: Sealants help maintain good dental health by preventing decay and promoting regular oral hygiene practices.
  • Non-Invasive: The procedure is non-invasive, meaning no drilling or painful injections are required.
  • Improved Oral Hygiene: Sealants create a smooth surface that is easier to clean and brush, making it easier to maintain good oral hygiene.

Why Are Dental Sealants So Effective?

Dental sealants act as a physical barrier, preventing bacteria and food particles from entering the pits and fissures on the chewing surfaces of the back teeth. Their smooth surface makes it difficult for bacteria to accumulate and cause decay. With a high success rate in preventing tooth decay, dental sealants are a reliable and cost-effective solution for maintaining healthy teeth.

How Dental Sealants Are Applied at Schaefer Dental Okemos

Our experienced team follows a precise procedure to apply dental sealants, ensuring a safe and effective experience for our patients:

  • Assessment: Our dentist conducts a thorough examination to identify teeth that are eligible for sealants, assessing their shape and structure.
  • Cleaning: The teeth are thoroughly cleaned and dried to prepare them for the sealant application.
  • Application: A thin layer of dental sealant is applied to the chewing surfaces of the teeth, filling in the pits and fissures.
  • Curing: A special light is used to harden the sealant, creating a strong and durable barrier against decay.
  • Final Check: Our dentist performs a final check to ensure the sealant has been applied successfully and that the teeth are healthy and strong.

When Should Your Child Get Dental Sealants?

Dental sealants are most effective when applied to a child’s teeth at the optimal time. Typically, children should get dental sealants on their permanent molars and premolars as soon as these teeth come in, usually between the ages of 6 and 12.

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends the following:

  • First permanent molars (typically around age 6)
  • Second permanent molars (typically around age 12)
  • Premolars (typically around age 10)
