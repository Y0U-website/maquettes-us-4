Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that anchor natural-looking crowns, restoring oral function and aesthetics.

Can dental implants be done in one day?

While some procedures can be done in one day, most dental implant treatments require multiple appointments.

Are teeth implants suitable for everyone?

Teeth implants are suitable for most adults with good oral health and sufficient jawbone density.

How many teeth is it possible to replace?

Dental implants can replace single or multiple teeth, depending on individual needs.

How do I care for teeth after the procedure?

Gentle brushing, flossing, and regular check-ups ensure the longevity of your dental implants.

Are dental implants considered costly?

While initial costs may seem high, it offer long-term benefits and cost-effectiveness compared to other tooth replacement options.

How should one go about caring for dental implants?

Regular oral hygiene, avoiding harmful habits, and scheduling regular check-ups ensure success and longevity.

Is dental implant treatment painful?

Our experienced team minimizes pain with numbing agents and gentle techniques, ensuring a comfortable experience. We also offer pillows and blankets for patients.
