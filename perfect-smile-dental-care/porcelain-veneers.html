The Porcelain Veneer Process

  • Consultation & Smile Design: We assess your goals, evaluate your teeth, and determine whether veneers are the best solution or if another orthodontic treatment may be recommended.
  • Tooth Preparation: A small amount of enamel is gently removed from the front surface of each tooth to make room for the veneer.
  • Impressions: Digital scans or impressions are taken to ensure your veneers are precisely crafted for fit and appearance.
  • Temporary Veneers: While your permanent veneers are being made, temporary ones may be placed for comfort and aesthetics.
  • Final Placement: The custom veneers are bonded securely to your teeth, with adjustments made for comfort and symmetry.

Advantages of Porcelain Veneers

  • Highly durable and stain-resistant

  • Can correct multiple aesthetic concerns at once
  • Long-lasting results with proper care
  • Customizable in shape, size, and color to suit your smile

Caring for Your Veneers

  • Brush and floss daily with non-abrasive toothpaste
  • Avoid biting directly into very hard foods
  • Wear a night guard if you grind your teeth
  • Schedule regular dental exams to keep your veneers and surrounding teeth healthy

FAQs: Porcelain Veneers

How long do porcelain veneers last?

With proper care, veneers can last 10–15 years or more. Do veneers require special cleaning? No. They should be cared for like natural teeth, with daily brushing, flossing, and regular checkups. Are veneers reversible? Because a small amount of enamel is removed, the procedure is not reversible, but veneers can be replaced if needed. Will veneers look fake? When properly designed and placed, veneers blend naturally with surrounding teeth for a realistic appearance.

Can veneers chip or crack?

Yes, though they are strong, avoiding hard impacts and wearing protection during sports can help prevent damage.
