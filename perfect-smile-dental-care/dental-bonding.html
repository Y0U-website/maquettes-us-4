The Dental Bonding Process

  • Evaluation & Shade Selection: We choose a resin color that closely matches your existing tooth shade.
  • Surface Preparation: The tooth surface is gently etched to help the bonding material adhere.
  • Application: The resin is applied and carefully shaped to restore or enhance the tooth’s appearance.
  • Curing: A special light hardens the resin in place.
  • Polishing: The bonded tooth is polished for a smooth, natural finish.

Benefits of Dental Bonding

  • Quick, typically completed in a single appointment
  • Minimally invasive, often requiring no anesthesia
  • Cost-effective compared to some other cosmetic treatments
  • Immediate results with little to no downtime
  • Can be combined with other other services for a complete smile makeover

Caring for Bonded Teeth

  • Maintain daily brushing and flossing to prevent staining or decay around the bonding
  • Avoid biting directly into hard objects like ice or pens
  • Schedule regular dental cleanings and exams to ensure your bonding remains in excellent condition
  • Consider a night guard if you grind your teeth

FAQs:Dental Bonding

How long does dental bonding last?

With good care, bonding can last 3–10 years before needing touch-ups or replacement.

Does bonding stain over time?

Yes, bonding material can stain, so limiting coffee, tea, and red wine can help maintain its appearance.

Is bonding painful?

No. The process is generally comfortable and does not require anesthesia unless used to fill a cavity.

Can dental bonding fix large gaps?

Bonding works best for small gaps; larger spacing issues may benefit from Invisalign or braces .

Will my insurance cover bonding?

If it’s done for restorative purposes, such as repairing decay, it may be covered. Cosmetic bonding is often considered elective.
