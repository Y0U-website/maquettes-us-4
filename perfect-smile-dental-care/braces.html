Types of Braces We Offer

  • Traditional Metal Braces: A reliable and cost-effective option for patients of all ages, offering precise control over tooth movement.
  • Ceramic Braces: Tooth-colored brackets that blend in more naturally, providing a discreet alternative to metal braces.

When to Start Braces

Orthodontic evaluations can begin earlier than many parents realize. By age 7 or 8 , children typically have a mix of baby and permanent teeth, allowing our doctors to identify skeletal or developmental issues that may benefit from early intervention. Early assessment helps guide jaw growth, manage crowding, and reduce the need for more complex treatment later on.

However, it’s never too late to achieve a straighter smile. Many of our orthodontic patients are adults who choose braces to enhance their confidence, correct bite problems, or improve long-term dental health.

At Perfect Smile Dental Care, we welcome patients of all ages—from children needing early evaluation to adults seeking modern, discreet orthodontic options.

The Braces Treatment Process

  • Comprehensive Orthodontic Evaluation: We assess your bite, alignment, and facial symmetry, often using digital X-rays or 3D imaging .
  • Treatment Planning: Based on your goals and diagnostic findings, we recommend the best type of braces for your case.
  • Placement: Brackets and wires are attached, and adjustments begin to gently guide teeth into their ideal positions.
  • Aligner Therapy: For patients who prefer a discreet option, clear aligners offer a removable and virtually invisible way to straighten teeth. They work by gently and consistently shifting teeth into proper alignment without the brackets and wires associated with traditional braces. Many patients choose aligner therapy for its comfort, convenience, and subtle appearance.
  • Ongoing Adjustments: Regular visits allow us to make precise changes to keep your treatment progressing smoothly.
  • Retention Phase: After braces are removed, a custom retainer helps maintain your new alignment.

FAQs: Braces

How long will I need to wear braces?

Treatment time varies, but most patients complete their braces journey in 12–24 months.

Are braces uncomfortable?

Some mild discomfort is common after adjustments, but it usually subsides within a few days.

Can adults get braces?

Yes. Braces are effective at any age, provided your teeth and gums are healthy.

Will I need to change my diet?

You may need to avoid hard, sticky, or overly crunchy foods to prevent damage to brackets and wires.

What happens after braces come off?

You’ll wear a custom retainer to maintain your results and prevent teeth from shifting back.
