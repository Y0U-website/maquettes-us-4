The Invisalign Treatment Process

  • Consultation & Evaluation: We review your alignment concerns, discuss goals, and determine if Invisalign is right for you.
  • Digital Impressions: Using advanced imaging technology, we create a precise 3D model of your teeth for accurate aligner fabrication.
  • Custom Aligner Series: You’ll receive a set of aligners, each worn for about 1–2 weeks, that gently guide your teeth into place.
  • Progress Check-ins: Regular visits ensure your treatment is on track and adjustments are made if necessary.
  • Retention Phase: After completing treatment, a custom retainer keeps your new smile stable.

Conditions Invisalign Can Address

For more complex orthodontic needs, we may recommend braces or a combination approach to achieve optimal results.

  • Mild to moderate crowding
  • Spacing or gaps between teeth
  • Overbites and underbites
  • Crossbites and open bites
  • Relapse after previous orthodontic treatment

Caring for Your Aligners

  • Remove aligners when eating or drinking anything other than water
  • Brush and rinse aligners daily to keep them clear and odor-free
  • Wear them for 20–22 hours per day for best results
  • Store in a protective case when not in use

FAQs: Invisalign

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

For many mild to moderate cases, Invisalign can be just as effective as braces when worn as directed.

How long does treatment take?

Treatment time varies but typically ranges from 6 to 18 months, depending on case complexity.

Will Invisalign affect my speech?

Most patients adjust within a few days and experience little to no long-term change in speech.

Can teens get Invisalign?

Yes. Invisalign Teen is designed specifically for younger patients with growing mouths.

What happens if I lose an aligner?

Contact our office immediately so we can advise you on the next steps and keep your treatment on track.
