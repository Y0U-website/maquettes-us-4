FAQs: Teeth Whitening

How long do whitening results last?

With proper oral hygiene and healthy habits, KöR Whitening results typically last about one year. Depending on your lifestyle and diet—such as coffee, tea, or wine consumption—periodic touch-ups may be recommended to maintain your brightest shade.

Will whitening make my teeth sensitive?

KöR Whitening is designed to significantly reduce sensitivity compared to other systems.

Can I whiten my teeth if I have fillings or crowns?

Restorations will not whiten, but we can discuss replacement to match your new shade.

How white will my teeth get?

Results vary based on your starting shade and type of staining, but most patients see several shades of improvement.
