ABOUT DR. Q DENTAL

At an early age, Dr. Quy Lam Nguyen has found his passion in dentistry from spending quality time in his father’s dental office. Dr. Quy received his doctoral degree in dentistry from the prestigious University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California. Dr. Quy then embarked on a comprehensive practice of general, cosmetic and implant dentistry.

Understanding that dental technologies and treatment techniques are constantly advancing, Dr. Quy has been pursuing continuing education in advanced dental fields to ensure you will receive excellent quality dental care. Dr. Quy is an active member of many respected dental institutions, including the American Dental Association, California Dental Association and the International Association of Orthodontics.

Dr. Quy and his dental team are committed to providing high quality patient care to you and your family, while making your experience as comfortable as possible. As a San Diego native, Dr. Quy is excited for the opportunity to give back to the community. Dr. Quy’s goal is to provide you a dental care with Quality, Honesty and Affordability. During his spare time, Dr. Quy enjoys trying new restaurants and traveling with family and friends.
