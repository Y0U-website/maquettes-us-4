Dr. Jennifer Schaumberg

Board Certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Jennifer Schaumberg is a board certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon who is also certified in anesthesia. Born and raised in West Bloomfield, Michigan, Dr. Schaumberg completed her undergraduate studies at the University of Michigan, and went on to obtain her dental degree from the University of Michigan Dental School. She pursued an internship and a residency program at the University of Miami/Jackson Memorial Hospital, a globally-recognized center for research and education in the fields of craniofacial trauma, tumor, and reconstructive surgery.

In addition to her education in dentoalveolar, orthognathic surgery, and dental implants, Dr. Schaumberg has advanced training in facial cosmetic, tumor, reconstructive, and pre-prosthetic surgery, as well as craniofacial trauma. Dr. Schaumberg is licensed and trained to perform all levels of anesthesia, and holds privileges to operate and covers emergency call at Holy Cross Hospital. Well versed in non-surgical cosmetic enhancement procedures, Dr. Schaumberg also provides a full range of cosmetic services including BOTOX /Dysport , and injectable fillers.
