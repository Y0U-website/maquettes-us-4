What Are All-on-6 Dental Implants?

All-on-6 dental implants are a type of dental implant that is used to support an entire set of false teeth. Unlike traditional dental implants, which are placed individually, all-on-6 implants are placed in a single surgery. This means that you can get your new teeth faster and with less pain.

How Do All-on-6 Dental Implants Work?

All-on-6 dental implants work by using six titanium posts that are surgically placed into your jawbone. Once the implants have healed, they will provide a strong and stable foundation for your new teeth. You will not have to worry about your teeth slipping or falling out, and you will be able to eat and speak with confidence.

Single Tooth Replacements: Dental implants are a great option for single tooth replacements. This is often the most common option for our patients.

Multiple Teeth Replacement: Dental Implants can also be a great option for several missing teeth. As a permanent solution, you will be able to chew comfortably once again.

Full Teeth Replacement: for serious cases, Dental Implants can be a great option for full teeth replacement. Dental implants can be as natural as the normal teeth most people were born with.

Implant Failure Treatment: if you have had an implant treatment and it did not go well, our dentists at Oral Surgery of Lighthouse Point can help you get back on track. After all, it is what we specialize in!

What Are the Benefits of All-on-6 Dental Implants?

There are many benefits to all-on-6 dental implants, including:

Improved Appearance: All-on-6 implants look and feel like your natural teeth. They are also designed to match the color and shape of your existing teeth.

Increased Confidence: With all-on-6 implants, you will not have to worry about your teeth slipping or falling out. This can help you feel more confident when you are speaking or eating.

Better Oral Health: All-on-6 implants do not require the same care as natural teeth. There is no need to worry about cavities or gum disease.

Durability: All-on-6 implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care.

The All-on-6 Dental Implants Process

If you are missing all of your teeth, or if you are considering getting rid of your dentures, all-on-6 implants may be the right choice for you. The first step is to schedule a consultation with the Oral Surgery Center of Lighthouse Point . During your consultation, we will examine your mouth and jawbone to determine if you are a good candidate for all-on-6 implants. We will also discuss your medical history and answer any questions you may have about the procedure. If you decide to move forward with all-on-6 implants, the next step is the surgery. The rest of the steps are outlined below:

Surgery will be performed under general anesthesia and will take about two hours. During the surgery, six titanium posts will be placed into your jawbone.

Once the posts are in place, they will need to heal for a few months before the next step can be performed.

After the implants have healed, you will come back to our office to have your new teeth placed. The teeth will be made to match the color and shape of your existing teeth.
