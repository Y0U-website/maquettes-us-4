What Is Surgical Orthodontics?

Surgical orthodontics is a type of dental surgery that is used to correct a wide variety of dental problems. These problems can include anything from misaligned teeth to jaw deformities. In most cases, surgical orthodontics is used in conjunction with traditional braces or other orthodontic treatments. However, in some cases, surgery may be the only option.

What Are the Benefits of Surgical Orthodontics?

There are many benefits to surgical orthodontics. First, it can help to improve the overall appearance of your smile. If you have misaligned teeth or a jaw deformity, correcting these problems can give you a much more polished look. In addition, surgical orthodontics can also help to improve your bite. This is because your teeth will be properly aligned, allowing you to chew food more effectively. Finally, surgical orthodontics can also help to reduce the risk of future dental problems. By correcting dental problems now, you can avoid more serious problems down the road.

Types of Surgical Orthodontics

There are several different types of surgical orthodontics, each designed to correct a specific dental problem. Some of the most common types of surgery include:

Jaw realignment surgery: This type of surgery is used to correct misaligned jaws. In some cases, the upper and lower jaws may be misaligned. In other cases, only the lower jaw may be misaligned. Jaw realignment surgery can help to improve your appearance and your bite.

Tooth extraction: In some cases, one or more teeth may need to be extracted in order to improve the alignment of your teeth. This is typically only done if other methods, such as braces, have not been successful.

Jaw reduction: In some cases, the jaws may be too large. This can cause problems with chewing and speaking. Jaw reduction surgery can help to correct this problem.
