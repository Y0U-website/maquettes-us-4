Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Generally speaking, candidates for implant dentures:

If you aren’t sure if you qualify for implant dentures, please schedule a consultation with our practice. Most adults do qualify, either immediately or after some preliminary treatments.

  • Have adequate bone density to support dental implants
  • Are in fair overall health
  • Are free of gum disease and other oral health conditions that might interfere with the implants
  • Stick to a good oral hygiene routine

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Although implant dentures require more time and money upfront than traditional dentures, they are a worthwhile investment. Some of their most outstanding benefits include:

  • Jawbone preservation. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the deterioration that normally occurs after tooth loss.
  • Dental implants may last a lifetime, and implant dentures last much longer than traditional ones (which usually need to be replaced every 5 – 7 years).
  • Implant dentures are so sturdy that they can handle virtually any food, including tough and chewy items.
  • Natural appearance. Mueller takes care to design implant dentures so they are virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth.
