General Dentistry

You do not need to wait for pain to see a dentist. Many problems grow quietly for months. Dr. Goodman catches them early, when care is simple. A quick visit now can save you a lot later.

  • Bleeding, swollen, or receding gums.
  • A chipped, cracked, or broken tooth.
  • A loose or lost filling or crown.
  • Persistent bad breath or a bad taste.
  • Jaw pain or signs of grinding.

Why does prevention matter?

Prevention is the cheapest, easiest dental care you will ever get. A cleaning and exam cost far less than a crown or root canal. Dr. Goodman catches small problems before they grow. He also protects the cosmetic and implant work you have invested in. Steady care keeps your whole smile stable for years.

Your mouth is also connected to the rest of your body. Healthy gums support your overall health. Dr. Goodman watches for early signs at every visit. He treats prevention as the smartest care, not an upsell.

What’s Our approach?

Dr. Goodman treats general dentistry as the foundation, not an afterthought. He starts with a careful exam and your full history before he recommends anything. He suggests only what you truly need, and tells you plainly when something can wait and watch instead of being treated now. He has practiced this way in Beverly Hills since 2003. Honest, conservative care is the standard here.
