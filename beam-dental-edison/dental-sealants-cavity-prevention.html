Why Choose Beam Dental

Maintaining a cavity-free smile involves more than just brushing; it requires proactive protection. At our Edison office, we offer specialized preventative treatments like fluoride applications and dental sealants to reinforce your tooth enamel. Fluoride is a natural mineral that strengthens the outer layer of your teeth, making them more resistant to decay. This treatment is highly beneficial for both children and adults and can be applied quickly during a routine visit.

Dental sealants provide an additional layer of defense, particularly for the back teeth where deep grooves can trap food and bacteria. These thin, protective plastic coatings are applied to the chewing surfaces of the molars and cured to create a smooth, durable barrier. By sealing off these hard-to-reach areas, we can significantly reduce the risk of plaque development and tooth decay. When combined with healthy at-home habits, these preventative measures are an excellent investment in the long-term health of your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can sealants and fluoride be used together?

Absolutely. Your dentist may recommend a fluoride treatment to strengthen the enamel followed by a sealant coating to provide a secondary layer of protection. This combination offers the maximum defense against cavities.

How many years do dental sealants typically last? With proper care, sealants can last between 5 and 10 years. To help them last as long as possible, it is best to avoid very sticky or hard foods that could potentially crack or dislodge the coating.

Is fluoride treatment a safe option for my child? Yes, fluoride is safe and highly beneficial for patients of all ages. It works by strengthening the enamel, and while it is often found in public drinking water, professional topical treatments provide a more concentrated boost of protection for developing teeth.

How do sealants help in preventing cavities? Sealants create a physical barrier that prevents food from directly contacting the enamel in the deep ridges of your teeth. Since food debris eventually turns into plaque and tartar, this shield stops the decay process before it can start.

What exactly are dental sealants? Sealants are protective plastic coatings applied to the surfaces of the teeth to block out food debris and bacteria. They are safe, quick to apply, and act as a shield to prevent the onset of decay and cavities.
