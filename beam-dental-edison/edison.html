Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

A dental emergency includes severe tooth pain, a knocked-out or broken tooth, a dental abscess with swelling, a lost crown or filling causing significant pain, or bleeding that will not stop. When in doubt, call Beam Dental Edison - we would rather help you assess the situation than have you wait in unnecessary pain.

Can I get a same-day dental appointment at Beam Dental Edison? Beam Dental Edison does its best to accommodate same-day emergency visits. Call as early as possible - our Edison office opens at 9am Monday through Friday and at 8am Saturday - to check availability and get the earliest slot.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency near Edison? Call Beam Dental Edison right away. We serve patients from across Middlesex County including Woodbridge, Metuchen, and New Brunswick, and most patients can reach us quickly by car. For a life-threatening emergency or severe swelling affecting your airway, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Does Beam Dental Edison offer emergency dental appointments? Yes. Beam Dental Edison offers emergency dental care and prioritizes urgent cases. Call our Edison office as soon as possible and we will work to get you in the same day or at the earliest available time.

What are ceramic veneers and how are they different from composite bonding? Ceramic veneers are thin, custom-made porcelain shells bonded permanently to the front surface of your teeth. They offer a highly natural appearance, excellent durability, and stain resistance. Composite bonding uses tooth-colored resin applied and shaped directly on your tooth in a single visit - it is faster and more affordable, but may need touch-ups over time. Your Edison dentist can help you weigh the options based on your smile goals and budget.

How long does a cavity filling take? Most cavity fillings at Beam Dental Edison are completed in a single appointment lasting about 30 to 60 minutes, depending on the size and location of the cavity. We use white resin composite fillings that match your natural tooth color and blend seamlessly with your smile.

How long does Invisalign treatment take? Invisalign treatment time at Beam Dental Edison depends on the complexity of your case. Mild to moderate alignment corrections typically take between 6 and 18 months. Your Edison provider will assess your bite and teeth during a consultation and give you a realistic timeline tailored to your situation.

How much do dental implants cost at Beam Dental Edison? Dental implant costs at Beam Dental Edison depend on your specific treatment plan - the number of implants, the type of restoration, and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are needed. Beam Dental is known for offering implants at more affordable prices than many other practices, and our transparent pricing means you will know the full cost before treatment begins. Schedule a consultation at our Edison office to get a detailed estimate.

How do I switch to the Beam Dental Edison office from another Beam location? Switching to Beam Dental Edison is easy - your records transfer seamlessly within the Beam system. Just contact our Edison team, let us know you want to transfer, and we will take care of the rest. No paperwork headaches.

What should I bring to my first appointment at Beam Dental Edison? Bring a photo ID, your dental insurance card if applicable, and a list of any current medications. If you have recent dental X-rays from a previous provider, those are helpful to bring along so our Edison team can get a full picture without extra imaging.

The 8am Saturday slot is a popular choice for patients who want to take care of their dental health without taking time off work during the week.
