Fixed Full Arch Dental Implants in Edison, NJ

Why Choose Beam Dental

Fixed full arch dental implants represent a modern innovation in restorative dentistry, designed to replace an entire set of teeth on the upper or lower jaw—or both. Unlike traditional dentures that require removal and repositioning, these implants use a series of titanium screws to anchor a permanent, natural-looking bridge directly to the jawbone. This provides a stable and long-term solution that eliminates the stress and aesthetics of missing teeth.

At our Edison office, full arch dental implants offer the look and functionality of natural teeth, allowing you to speak and eat with total confidence. This procedure provides lasting stability and durability, serving as a lifetime investment in your oral health. Our dedicated team at Beam Dental is here to help you decide if fixed implant dentures are the right choice for your lifestyle and goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the required upkeep for fixed implants?

Maintenance involves practicing good daily oral hygiene, attending your scheduled follow-up appointments, and following the specific care recommendations provided by your dentist.

What exactly are full arch dental implants? They are a system of titanium screws secured into the jawbone that act as "roots" to anchor custom-made, natural-looking prosthetic arches permanently in place. These can be used for partial replacement on one jaw or a full replacement for both.

How long does the surgical procedure take? The implant surgery itself can take up to eight hours, potentially longer if extractions of remaining teeth are required. Following the surgery, the healing process for the bone to fuse with the implants usually takes between three and six months.

What is the difference between full arch implants and All-on-4? While both provide a stable set of replacement teeth, full arch implants typically use a higher number of implants to provide even greater stability than the All-on-4 system, which relies on four strategically placed posts.
