ABOUT

0 We are a family-owned private practice focused on creating a comfortable, empathetic experience for our community. We are Cape Codders serving Cape Codders.

Dr. Richard Weiler founded our office in 1986, and built a practice based on combining his clinical experience with listening to his patients’ needs. Dr. George Brady joined the practice nearly 30 years ago.

In 2022, Dr. Sarah Gibbons, who grew up in Harwich, and Dr. Bennett Olmsted bought the practice from Dr. Weiler and have maintained it as a community-focused private practice. Dr. Gary Campagna joined the practice in 2023, after practicing in his own dental office in Harwich for 37 years.

Along with our staff, we hope to foster an environment based on respect, kindness and empathy and strive to implement clinical excellence for all of the patients who trust us with their wellbeing.
