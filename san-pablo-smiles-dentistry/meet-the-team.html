Meet Our Team

San Pablo Smiles Dentistry is a dental practice focused on care for the entire family. Our mantra is very simple: to provide exceptional dental care, individualized for each patient in a warm and caring environment. We strive to develop long-lasting, trusting relationships with each person in our care, relationships based on respect and compassion. Each of us firmly believes that caring is an essential part of dental treatment.

Ana was born and raised in San Pablo/Richmond area. She graduated from Richmond High School and attended Contra Costa College. She has been in the dental field and as a team member of the practice for over 15 years. One of the most rewarding parts of her job is helping others and making them feel comfortable. She is happily married and has two wonderful kids. She loves spending time with her family, exercising, going on hikes and traveling.

Teresa has been in dentistry for more than 18 years. She attended Contra Costa College Dental Assisting program where she obtained her Registered Dental Assistant license. She always assures patients are fully informed and feel comfortable with all of their dental needs. Teresa will encourage you to ask questions and make sure that all of your concerns are addressed before treatment. As a RDA she will be there to make your dental experience pleasant. When she’s not at work, she loves being with her family and cooking.

Sonia was born in El Salvador and came to the United States when she was 12 years old. She attended Jefferson High School in Daly City and graduated from Bryman College in San Francisco to become a Registered Dental Assistant. Sonia has been in the dental field for 19 years and really enjoys helping and getting to know her patients. She also enjoys spending quality time with her husband and son Jonathan.

Natividad – Dental Assistant Natividad is originally from Guatemala and moved to the United States when she was 13 years old. She graduated from the JVS Dental Assisting Program in 2023 and is excited to continue growing her career in the dental field. She truly enjoys working with patients and loves helping everyone feel welcome and comfortable in our office. It’s especially meaningful to her when she can support her family and friends with their dental care. Outside of work, she enjoys going to the gym, spending quality time with her family, and hanging out with her dog, Mochi.

Outside the office, Edwin enjoys hitting the batting cages, hiking, playing board games with his family, and enjoying time with his dog, Dior.

Born and raised in the Bay Area, Isabel completed her Bachelor’s in Dental Hygiene at West Coast University in Orange County. Passionate about healthcare and her local community, she has actively engaged in volunteer and outreach programs with the goal of impacting people’s lives positively. Outside the office, she enjoys spending quality time with her family and her dog and trying new coffee and boba places.

Isabelle is a registered dental hygienist who graduated from Fresno City College in 2024. Growing up in a dental office, she was exposed to a variety of patients from a young age, inspiring her passion for patient education and preventative care. Isabelle loves helping patients take control of their oral health by creating personalized treatment plans. Outside the dental office, Isabelle enjoys playing pickleball with family, making matcha lattes, and collecting cute knick-knacks.
