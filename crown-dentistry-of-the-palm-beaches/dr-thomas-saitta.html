Welcome to Crown Dentistry of the Palm Beaches

Dr. Saitta earned his DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) degree from Indiana University Dental School, renowned as one of the best in the country. He has dedicated 35 years to serving the dental health needs of this community and continually enhances his expertise with post-graduate courses in various fields of dentistry.

His focus on patient comfort, coupled with staying abreast of the latest advancements in dentistry, enables him to offer the highest quality of care in the field. Dr. Saitta’s experience spans various areas of dentistry, including cosmetic and restorative dentistry, surgical treatments, and root canal therapies. He is a leading practitioner in CPT (Conservative Periodontal Therapy) the early detection and non-surgical treatment of periodontal (gum) disease. As a highly trained dentist, he also administers Restylane, a lip enhancer.

Dr. Saitta takes pride in serving a diverse patient base, including other discerning dentists, medical doctors, and individuals like you. His approach to treatment begins with patiently listening to you. Following a comprehensive examination, he reviews and discusses the diagnosis and treatment options. In Dr. Saitta’s own words, "I am proud to provide my patients with the best in dental technology, treatment options, and patient comfort. It is my pleasure to address all your questions and concerns."
