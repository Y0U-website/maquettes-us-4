Dr freyda b cohen

Dr. Freyda B. Cohen, a magna cum laude graduate from Stern College for Women with a BA in Biology, received her DMD in 2007 from UMDNJ - New Jersey Dental School. During her academic tenure, she was part of the Gamma Phi Delta Prosthodontic Honor Society and earned the New Jersey Academy of General Dentistry Award upon graduation.

Following this, Dr. Cohen completed a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center, affiliated with Albert Einstein College of Medicine. Prior to relocating to Florida, she practiced general dentistry in New York and New Jersey. A member of the American Dental Association, the Florida Dental Association, and the Atlantic Coast District Dental Society, Dr. Cohen is committed to staying abreast of the latest dental techniques and advancements through regular lectures and continuing education courses.

Outside the practice, she cherishes time with her husband and four children, engaging in cooking, exercise, and volunteering in her children’s schools and community. Renowned for her compassionate, caring chairside manner, as well as her thoroughness and meticulous attention to detail, Dr. Cohen is dedicated to ensuring a positive dental experience for all her patients.
