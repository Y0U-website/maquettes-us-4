Who is a good candidate for same-day implants?

There are lots of scenarios where we recommend same-day implant treatment. The treatment is best for patients who have not experienced any bone loss as a result of their missing or damaged tooth.

  • Healthy or strong jaw bone with no significant bone loss
  • Want fast results and a more convenient treatment experience
  • No underlying medical issues preventing bone from healing properly
  • Recent tooth loss or damage with intact bone structure
