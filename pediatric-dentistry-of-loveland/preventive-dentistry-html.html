Dental Sealants

Molars have deep grooves that love to trap food and bacteria. Thankfully, sealants are a game-changer. These clear, protective coatings act like tiny shields, covering teeth and keeping debris out of those tough-to-reach places. They’re easy to apply, BPA-free, and especially effective for kids who are at a higher risk of decay.

Cavity-Fighting Medicines

We want to support your child’s health with personalized, science-backed care that also fits your values. Dr. Katie and Dr. Matt may recommend these medicine-based options for in-office or home use.

  • Traditional fluoride treatment that defends teeth against bacteria
  • Silver diamine fluoride (SDF) to stop early decay non-invasively (ideal for anxious kids!)
  • Fluoride-free remineralizing options for parents who want alternatives
  • Prescription toothpastes for patients who are considered high-risk for cavities

Athletic Mouthguards/ Sportsguards

Active kids need active protection. Contact sports can lead to collisions, falls, and dental injuries. Our custom-fit mouthguards help shield teeth and jaws from impacts while also fitting comfortably. We’ve got their athletic smiles covered!

Bruxism/Grinding Guards

Does your child complain of jaw soreness or struggle with grinding teeth at night? Bruxism is the fancy term for teeth grinding. We can help identify signs of this condition early and provide custom guards that fit well, are easy to clean, and offer great protection.

Pediatric Airway Health

A clear airway is the anchor of a child’s overall wellness. When kids breathe well day and night their bodies rest better, their faces grow properly, and their smiles develop the way nature intended. Dr. Katie and Dr. Matt take a gentle, whole-child approach to airway health, looking beyond the teeth to understand how your child sleeps, breathes, and grows.
