What to do in an Emergency

If you experience tooth pain or loose and broken brackets, bands, or wires, call our office to find out if you can wait until your next scheduled appointment for the repair or if you should come in sooner. While actual emergencies are rare, if you think that you have an emergency, don’t hesitate to contact either of our offices to schedule an emergency appointment. You will receive superb professional care regardless of your need.
