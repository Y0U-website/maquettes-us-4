ABOUT US

Our Values

Sandhills Orthodontics strives to create a family atmosphere where patients feel fully comfortable when receiving care. We love learning about you as a patient and as a person, and we aim to have every person we treat leaving happier and healthier than when they arrived. We are also an eco-friendly practice that cares just as much about protecting our planet as we do about protecting your dental health. We make visiting the orthodontist fun!

Dr. Glynda R. McConville

Dr. Glynda R. McConville earned her dental degree from the University of North Carolina – Chapel Hill before receiving her orthodontic training at New York University. A Board Certified orthodontist, she has been practicing dentistry since 1999. Dr. McConville stays abreast of the latest technological advances in her field with continuing education courses and active memberships in the American Dental Association, American Association of Orthodontists, Southern Association of Orthodontists, and the North Carolina Dental Society.

Fun Facts

In her spare time, Dr. McConville enjoys training for half marathons, hanging out with her french bulldog, Titan, and teaching classes at Sandhills Crossfit, which she owns.

Her boxer, Sampson, had an extreme underbite and became the office mascot and a source of our tagline: Need a Better Bite?

Dr. McConville has a sweet tooth and is a “foodie”. She likes to try new restaurants when she travels.

Meet Our Team

Our staff makes up the heart of who we are and what we value as a practice. We care about our patient interactions from the moment you schedule your first appointment, to when you meet with Dr. McConville, through when you leave our office! Our team is experienced and confident in the level of care they provide, and we enjoy seeing the change that occurs in each smile. We love getting to know each patient, and we want you to get to know us!
