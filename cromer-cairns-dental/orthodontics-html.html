How Invisalign Works

Adults and teenagers alike can benefit from Invisalign, an aesthetically pleasing alternative to metal braces. Instead of cumbersome brackets and wires, Invisalign gradually repositions your teeth using a series of clear plastic trays called aligners. Each pair will take you one step closer to your ideal smile and is worn for a few weeks before moving on to the next. While you’re wearing these aligners, they should be unnoticeable to the people around you.

Benefits of Invisalign

Of course, the main benefit of Invisalign is right there in the name; the aligners are practically invisible, allowing you to smile confidently throughout your treatment. Of course, that isn’t the only reason more than 17 million patients have chosen to straighten their teeth with this innovative orthodontic solution. Below, you will find a few additional perks that come with Invisalign clear braces!

Convenience

Unlike traditional braces, which are bonded to your teeth, clear aligners can be removed at any time. Not only does this mean you can take them off before a big date or an important presentation at work, but it also means you don’t need to buy any special dental care products. All you need to do is remove your aligners, rinse them with clean, cool water, and proceed with your usual oral hygiene regimen.

Comfort

Another considerable perk: Invisalign aligners don’t have any metal components. They are crafted from smooth plastic, which makes them considerably more comfortable. Plus, they are custom-made to fit the contours of your teeth, which helps prevent sores or irritation in your mouth’s soft tissues as well. So, your journey to a straighter smile will be considerably more comfortable.

Speed

Did you know that the average treatment timeline with Invisalign is only 12-18 months? Traditional braces often take two or three times that long! So, if you want a straighter smile for a special event, like your wedding, consider clear aligners. Not only will they deliver the results you desire faster, but they also won’t draw unwanted attention to your smile in the process.

Freedom

If you or someone you know had traditional braces in the past, then you might remember the long list of dietary restrictions. While they are there for a good reason (to help prevent an orthodontic emergency), they can be a bit of a nuisance. Fortunately, Invisalign doesn’t require you to change your diet. Since the aligners are removable, you can continue to eat all of your favorites throughout the teeth-straightening process.

Versatility

Do your teeth overlap? Are there noticeable spaces between them? Maybe you’re struggling with a common bite problem, like an overbite, crossbite, or underbite. Whatever the case may be, Invisalign might be the solution you’ve been looking for! Living with Invisalign Aligners

Invisalign offers a more comfortable and convenient way to straighten teeth than traditional braces, but it still requires no small amount of discipline on the patient’s part. During your consultation at our Vero Beach office, we’ll be sure to explain how the process works and what you’ll have to do to keep your treatment on track. Here’s a brief guide to what life is like when you’re wearing Invisalign clear aligners, but please contact our staff if you have any questions.

Wearing Your Trays

You’ll need to wear each set of aligners for twenty to twenty-two hours each day to give them enough time to shift your teeth properly. After about two weeks, you’ll switch to the next set in your sequence, but the precise timeframe will be determined by our dentist when they are designing your treatment. Neglecting to wear your trays sufficiently can lead to treatment delays, and it’s ideal to only remove your aligners for oral hygiene sessions or when you are eating or drinking anything but water.

Cleaning Your Aligners

Hot or cold water can warp your Invisalign clear aligners and compromise their ability to straighten your teeth, so it’s important to only clean them with cool or lukewarm water. You’ll need to soak them in Invisalign cleaning crystals or treat them with a soft-bristled toothbrush and hand soap at least once a day. You’ll also need to thoroughly rinse your aligners off each time before putting them back in your mouth. Since food trapped between the aligners and the teeth can lead to infections and stains, you must also carefully brush and floss each time before putting your aligners back in.

Eating & Drinking

Since Invisalign clear aligners are easy to remove, you can eat whatever you like during treatment as long as you clean your teeth and appliances properly afterward. It’s crucial to always remove your aligners before eating or drinking anything but water since they are not built to withstand the pressure of chewing and can shelter food debris and residues from the mouth’s natural cleaning process, which can lead to infections and stains. When you are not wearing your aligners, it’s important to place them in a clean protective case to prevent them from being damaged or thrown away.

Invisalign vs. Mail-In Aligners: Which Costs More?

Mail-in aligner companies center most of their marketing around the lower upfront cost. While it’s true that these alternatives are typically cheaper, they don’t tell you why that is. Invisalign FAQs

What are Invisalign attachments?

Many people aren’t aware that they may need Invisalign attachments during their treatment, which can make hearing that you do a surprise! This added feature is most helpful for patients whose teeth aren’t tracking during treatment, or they aren’t moving as they’re supposed to. The attachments themselves are small, bonded dots placed on the surface of your teeth to help guide their movement in a certain direction.

Can you get Invisalign after you’ve already had braces?

Yes! In fact, many of our patients that we see in Vero Beach want to invest in Invisalign treatment after receiving braces as a teenager but not wearing their retainer as they were instructed to. This way, they can address any shifting that may have occurred without having to worry about looking younger than they actually are in professional settings due to visible braces. In some cases, Invisalign aligners may even be able to make these slight adjustments in a shorter timeframe than it would take if you got traditional braces again!

Are there extra costs involved with Invisalign treatment?

Invisalign treatment is designed to be all-inclusive, meaning you pay a set cost based on your consultation with one of our dentists at Cromer & Cairns Dental. However, you may find that you need some services after treatment to make sure your smile looks as great as it feels!

To help keep your newly aligned teeth in their proper places, you may need to purchase a separate custom retainer. Our team will discuss whether or not your retainer is included in your treatment with you before you begin the process, and we’ll let you know how long you’ll need to wear it for after your treatment. Many patients also invest in teeth whitening after their treatment, because attachments can lead to tooth discoloration. Sometimes, this can be bundled in your treatment, but if not, it will incur a separate fee.

Do you eat with Invisalign trays in your mouth?

You should never eat or drink anything besides water with your clear aligners in your mouth because it could cause them to become damaged or warped, ultimately setting back your treatment timeline. Every time you have a snack, drink coffee or tea, or sit down to eat a meal, you’ll need to remove your trays. Once you do, you can eat any food!

How should I clean my Invisalign trays?

You should clean your Invisalign trays several times a day after you eat or snack. You’ll want to rinse and scrub them with clear hand soap each time before putting them back in your mouth and soak them in Invisalign cleaning crystal solution for 15 minutes once a week. Never clean your aligners with hot water, as this could warp them!
