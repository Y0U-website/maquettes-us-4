What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that provide a permanent base for fixed, replacement teeth. They&#x27;re a popular and effective long-term solution for people who suffer from missing teeth, failing teeth or chronic dental problems. Made to match your natural teeth, dental implants look, feel and function like your real teeth.

How do dental implants work?

Dental implants are surgically placed in your jawbone, where they serve as the roots of your missing teeth. Because they&#x27;re made of titanium, which is a metal that is naturally compatible with your body, they eventually fuse with your bone. Once the implant is firmly in place, a crown, which is a replacement tooth, is attached to the implant. This gives you the functionality and appearance of a natural tooth.

Are dental implants right for me?

Most people are good candidates for dental implants. If you&#x27;re missing one or more teeth, or if you&#x27;re unhappy with your dentures, you may be a good candidate for dental implants. The key requirement is that you must have enough bone in your jaw, and the bone has to be strong enough to hold and support the implant. If you don&#x27;t have enough bone, there are many safe and effective ways to correct bone deficiency. Dr. Barakat will assess this and suggest the best treatment plan for you.

How long does the dental implant procedure take?

The length of the dental implant procedure varies depending on several factors. These include the number of teeth being replaced and the location of the replacement teeth. In some cases, if additional procedures such as bone grafting are required, the process may take longer. Generally, the procedure involves several steps that take place over a time period of a few months. However, with the advancements in dental technology, Dr. Barakat can now offer same-day dental implants to eligible patients.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a high success rate, with over 95 % of patients experiencing successful outcomes. This success rate is attributed to advancements in dental technology, the expertise of dental professionals like Dr. Barakat, and proper patient selection. With good oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for your dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth. Brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and regular dental check-ups are essential for maintaining the health of your implants and preventing gum disease. It&#x27;s also important to avoid habits that can damage your implants, such as chewing on hard objects or grinding your teeth. Dr. Barakat will provide you with specific instructions on how to care for your implants to ensure their longevity.
