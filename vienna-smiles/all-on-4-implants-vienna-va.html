How Does the All-on-4 Procedure Work?

At Vienna Smiles, your treatment begins with a detailed consultation and evaluation. Dr. Barakat carefully examines your oral health, discusses your goals, and determines whether All-on-4 implants are the right solution for you.

Step 1: Consultation and 3D Imaging

Your appointment includes advanced 3D imaging to evaluate bone structure, gum health, and potential implant sites. This technology allows Dr. Barakat to create a personalized treatment plan with precision.

Patients who have been told they are not candidates elsewhere due to bone loss may still qualify. Vienna Smiles performs bone grafting and sinus lift procedures in-office, helping many patients avoid outside referrals.

Step 2: Implant Placement

During surgery, four titanium implants are strategically placed into the jawbone. The implants are angled to maximize available bone support and stability.

For eligible patients, temporary same-day teeth may be attached during this visit. This “teeth in a day” approach allows many patients to leave with a functional smile immediately after surgery.

Step 3: Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next several months, the implants gradually fuse with the jawbone through osseointegration. During this healing period, patients wear temporary teeth while the implants stabilize.

Step 4: Final Prosthesis Placement

Once healing is complete, your final restoration is placed. The permanent prosthesis is custom-crafted for appearance, comfort, and durability.

The result is a stable full-arch restoration that looks and functions like natural teeth.

All-on-4 vs. All-on-6 Implants

Some patients may benefit from All-on-6 implants instead of All-on-4. Both treatments support a full arch of replacement teeth, but the number of implants differs.

All-on-4 uses four implants to anchor the prosthesis, while All-on-6 uses six implants for additional support and bite distribution.

All-on-6 may be recommended for patients who:

  • Want additional long-term stability

Recovery and What to Expect After Surgery

Recovery varies from patient to patient, but most individuals return to normal daily activities within a few days.

Most swelling subsides within the first week. Patients are typically advised to rest for two to three days following surgery.

During the osseointegration phase, which usually lasts 4 to 6 months, softer foods help protect the implants as healing occurs. After the final prosthesis is placed, patients can gradually return to a more normal diet.

Long-term care is similar to caring for natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, regular cleanings, and routine dental visits remain important for maintaining oral health and implant longevity.

  • Mild swelling and soreness for several days
  • A soft-food diet during healing
  • Follow-up visits to monitor progress
  • Gradual improvement in comfort and function

How Long Do All-on-4 Dental Implants Last?

The titanium implants themselves can last a lifetime with proper care. The prosthetic arch may eventually need maintenance or replacement after approximately 10 to 15 years, depending on wear patterns and oral habits.

Why Patients Choose All-on-4 Over Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures can restore appearance, but they often come with limitations. Patients commonly experience slipping, discomfort, gum irritation, and difficulty eating certain foods. Over time, dentures may also contribute to jawbone shrinkage because they do not stimulate the bone beneath the gums.
