The Full Story

​ Quinn Family Dental has been in Chicopee since 1967, when Dr. Thomas Quinn bought the practice originally housed on Britton Street.

He was joined in 1968 by his brother Dr. Robert Quinn.

Together they treated patients for generations from the City of Chicopee and surrounding communities.

In 1989 Dr. Tom s daughter, Dr. Maureen Quinn joined the practice.

After a few years Dr. Maureen returned to school to pursue a specialty in Pediatric Dentistry.

She rejoined the practice in 1994 as a Pediatric Dentist.

Many patients will remember our previous location in Chicopee on Britton Street.

As our practice grew, our need to accommodate more patients was evident,

In 2001 the practice was relocated to our current location on Memorial Drive,

where Dr. David Vanderboom joined in 2009.

Dr. Tom retired in 1998 and Dr. Robert in 2010.

​Dr. Andrea Boraski joined the practice in 2025.

Their legacy lives on with the care and compassion they always gave their patients,

that Dr. Maureen, Dr. Dave and Dr. Andrea continue to give their patients today. ​
