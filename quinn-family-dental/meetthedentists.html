Our Doctors

Maureen Quinn, D.D.S.

Dr. Maureen Quinn followed in her dads footsteps by attending Georgetown University School of Dentistry. She returned to Western Mass and joined her dad and uncle at Quinn Family Dental. After a few years, it became clear that Dr. Maureen had a passion for working with children, so she attended a Residency at Boston Children s Hospital/ Harvard School of Dental Medicine, Graduating in 1994. She returned to Quinn Family Dental as a Pediatric Dentist, limiting her practice to children and patients with special needs.

Dr. Quinn is a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and the Massachusetts Dental Society. More recently she became a teacher, giving her time to train future Pediatric Dentists as an associate Professor of NYU Langone. She enjoys sharing her passion for Pediatric Dentistry with the next generation of students.

Dr. Maureen and her husband have 2 children. They enjoy summers at The Cape with friends and family.

David Vanderboom, D.M.D.

Dr. David Vanderboom has been a provider at Quinn Family Dental since 2006. Following his undergraduate studies at Calvin College in Grand Rapids, MI, he pursued and completed his D.M.D degree from Boston University.

In his spare time, Dr. David loves to spend time with his wife and four kids, traveling, skiing, and working on home projects.

Andrea Boraski, D.M.D.

Dr. Andrea Boraski completed her D.M.D. from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. After completion of her degree, she moved forward specializing in Pediatrics and completed her specialty at NYU Langone Holyoke Hospital site.

As a native of Western Mass, Dr. Andrea is excited to return and provide services to the children and families of her community.

Dr. Andrea is a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, and the Massachusetts Dental Society.

Dr. Andrea loves her profession and is committed to continuing her education to better serve her patients.

In her spare time, Dr. Andrea enjoys playing tennis, singing in the Springfield Symphony Chorus, skiing, traveling, and spending time with her friends, family, and dog.
