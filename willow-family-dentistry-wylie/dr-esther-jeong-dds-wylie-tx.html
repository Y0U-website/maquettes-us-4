Who is the dentist at Willow Family Dentistry in Wylie?

Dr. Esther B. Jeong is the owner and lead dentist at Willow Family Dentistry in Wylie, TX. She graduated from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry and is a member of the American Dental Association. She is known for her gentle approach with anxious patients and children, and has volunteered on dental missions in four countries.

Dr. Esther Jeong, DDS, Founder and Lead Dentist

Dr. Esther B. Jeong, DDS

Esther Jeong grew up moving every few years. Her father served in the U.S. Army, so home was wherever the family landed. She has lived in more states than most people visit. That constant change taught her something: people everywhere want the same thing. They want to be seen, heard, and treated with kindness.

She found dentistry through a winding path. Biology at Andrews University, dental school at Loma Linda, and eventually Texas, where she and her husband Tony are raising five kids: Dawn, Jade, Hope, Drew, and Soul. Yes, five. Yes, it is busy.

Dr. Jeong did not just want to fix teeth. She wanted to serve people. That is why she has spent time on dental missions in Belize, Honduras, Korea, and Bangladesh. It is why she volunteers at community health events. And it is why she runs her practice the way she does, with patience, honesty, and a genuine interest in every person who sits in her chair.

When she is not at the office, she is probably at one of her kids&#x27; games, attempting to keep up with her two Shih Tzus (Max and Ruby), or picking up her guitar.
