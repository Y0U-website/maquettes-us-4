The practice

Orthodontists, Dr. Bruce M. Ulrich and Dr. Beau M. Ulrich , along with our highly trained professional staff , understand that choosing to receive orthodontic care is an important decision. Our practice is committed to providing excellent customer service and an extraordinary treatment experience.

To schedule a complimentary consultation, please contact our office.

Our patients see only our doctors as we have one location, giving our patients the time and service they deserve. This includes the initial consultation with our doctors and all appointments.

We have built a reputation for providing quality orthodontics treatment and excellent customer service.

Both our doctors live in and support the community where they practice.

Our office uses the highest quality appliances and materials. This minimizes treatment time, broken appliances and allergic reactions to materials.

Our office has its own in-house x-ray and imaging facility. All records are taken in our office by our imaging certified staff with the exception of 3-Dimensional Cone Beam images.

Contemporary Metal Braces ​ Contemporary metal braces are the most common type of braces and are more comfortable today than ever before. Made of high-grade stainless steel, they are the fastest and least cost prohibitive option. The majority of our patients select this kind of treatment.

Les soins

Should you experience a problem or unexpected pain with your braces, we recommend you contact our office. One of our doctors is always on call to schedule a time to resolves the issue.

When you get your braces on, we ll talk with you about the right kind of brush to use, and how to properly care for your braces.
