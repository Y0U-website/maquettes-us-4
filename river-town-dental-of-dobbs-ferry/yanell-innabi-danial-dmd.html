Yanell Innabi-Danial, DMD

Coming from a family of dentists, Dr. Innabi-Danial has always been influenced by the leaders in her field. She is 1 of 11 dentists in her family! Her husband is an emergency medicine physician at Bronx and Westchester hospitals. As a mother of three boys, she is very social when not practicing dentistry. She is also trilingual - proficient in both Spanish and Arabic.
