The Fine Print

  • Wellness plan cannot be used in conjunction with dental insurance.
  • Benefits of the plan are only available in our office - this plan is not part of any other insurance or discount plan.
  • Benefits are not available at specialists&#x27; offices if a referral is needed.
  • Services cannot be filed to dental insurance.
  • Plan benefits not available with any other discount offer and not transferable.
  • Not valid on Invisalign, surgical guides, cosmetic veneers, bleaching, night guards, DTR, and in office products.
  • Participants must remain in the plan for 12 months. Enrollment fees are not refundable.
  • Plan is good for 12 months from the time of initial purchase and may be renewed annually. We will NOT auto-renew without your consent.
