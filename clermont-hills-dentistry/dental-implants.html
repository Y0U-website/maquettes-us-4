Dental Implants

At Clermont Hills Dentistry, every implant is placed with precision using advanced digital imaging and guided technology for the most accurate, comfortable results possible.

  • Strong & Stable – Restores full chewing power.
  • Protects Jawbone – Prevents bone loss and gum recession.
  • Long-Lasting – With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Versatile – Supports single crowns, bridges, or full dentures.

Our Dental Implant Process

We make the implant journey simple, predictable, and patient-centered:

Consultation & 3D Evaluation

Our team will assess your bone health using digital imaging and discuss your goals and treatment options.

Implant Placement

Healing & Integration

Over the next few months, the implant naturally fuses with the bone creating a stable foundation for your new restoration.

Custom Restoration

Once healed, we attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture designed to match your natural teeth perfectly.

From start to finish, our team ensures every step is clearly explained, comfortable, and tailored to you.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be an excellent candidate if you:

Even if you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone, we can often perform bone grafting or sinus lift procedures to prepare the area for implant placement.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Want a long-term, fixed alternative to dentures
  • Have healthy gums and sufficient bone density

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are easy to maintain just treat them like natural teeth:

With proper care, your implant can last decades even a lifetime.

  • Brush and floss daily
  • Use a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • Schedule regular cleanings and exams
  • Avoid smoking and excessive grinding
