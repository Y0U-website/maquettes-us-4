What to Expect During Your Visit

We believe preventive care should be simple, comfortable, and transparent.

Here’s what a typical visit includes:

Whether it’s your first visit or your fiftieth, we take our time, explain each step, and make sure you feel completely comfortable.

  • Comprehensive Exam – Our clinicians will evaluate your teeth, gums, bite, and overall oral health.
  • Digital X-Rays – Clear images with minimal radiation for your comfort and peace of mind.
  • Gentle Cleaning – Our hygienist gently removes plaque and leaves your teeth feeling clean, smooth, and refreshed.
  • Personalized Guidance – You’ll receive honest recommendations and tips to maintain your smile between visits.

How Often Should You Schedule Cleanings?

For most patients, we recommend exams and cleanings every six months.

If you have a history of gum disease, are prone to cavities, or wear dental restorations, we may suggest more frequent visits to keep your mouth in optimal health.

Our goal is simple to help you maintain a clean, healthy smile for life.

Keep Your Smile Bright Between Visits

Between appointments, daily habits make all the difference. Our team at Clermont Hills Dentistry recommend:

Little steps add up to a lifetime of healthy smiles.

  • Brushing twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
  • Flossing once a day
  • Staying hydrated and limiting sugary drinks
  • Replacing your toothbrush every 3 4 months
