ABOUT US

Our Story

Caring for Clermont, One Smile at a Time

Clermont Hills Dentistry was founded with one simple goal to make going to the dentist feel comfortable, transparent, and genuinely personal.

What began as a vision from Dr. Luis Veloz, a lifelong Clermont resident, has grown into a trusted practice known for its compassionate team, advanced technology, and commitment to doing what’s right for every patient.

We believe great dentistry starts with listening. That’s why we take time to understand your goals, explain your options clearly, and deliver care that fits your comfort level and your lifestyle. Whether you’re here for preventive visits, advanced implants, or a complete smile transformation you’ll always feel informed, valued, and cared for.

Meet Dr. Luis Veloz, DMD

A native of Clermont, Dr. Luis Veloz built his practice on community, connection, and care that goes beyond the chair. Patients describe him as approachable, patient, and dedicated qualities that guide every visit.

Dr. Veloz takes pride in blending modern dental techniques with a gentle, down-to-earth style. His goal is to help patients overcome fear, rebuild trust, and enjoy lifelong oral health through clear communication and individualized treatment.

When he’s not in the office, you’ll likely find Dr. Veloz spending time with his family, supporting local schools, or cheering on his kids at community sports events. He’s proud to serve the same town he grew up in caring for neighbors, friends, and families across Clermont.
