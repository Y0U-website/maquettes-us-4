Invisalign® for Children

Invisalign First: Invisalign for Growing Children

Invisalign First is a great option for some children who need help with overcrowding, spacing, and narrow dental arches. With Invisalign, children can remove their trays when eating, drinking, brushing their teeth, playing sports or musical instruments. Other great benefits include no broken brackets or poking wires, which in turn keeps busy parents from having to make special trips to our office.

Invisalign® FAQs

Invisalign uses a series of virtually invisible, removable, and comfortable aligners made of special plastic. Each tray is created specifically for you, to move your teeth exactly the way your PDGO orthodontist prescribed.

Most patients only report mild soreness during the first few days of each Invisalign aligner. The rate of tooth movement can be slowed down to just 0.25 millimeters per aligner so your teeth experience the lightest amount of pressure as possible. Since the aligners are made from comfortable plastic, you won’t experience the same abrasiveness as metal brackets and wires.

Nope, none! The aligners are removable so you can take them out to eat popcorn, corn on the cob, and all of your favorite foods!

You can achieve the same result with Invisalign as you can with braces in the majority of cases. While a lot of people think that Invisalign takes longer than braces to straighten teeth, you can actually achieve your optimal smile in the same amount of time, or even less.

Every case has unique differences, but at our office the average length of treatment for our Invisalign patients is 9-15 months. You play an important role in staying on track with your timeline - the better you wear your aligners, the quicker your treatment completes.

Most patients can get the same results with Invisalign as braces. Come see us for a complimentary consultation to assess your specific desires and we will tell you whether or not you are a good candidate for Invisalign.

It is important that an orthodontic specialist oversee your tooth movement and we advise caution with DIY aligner companies. Invisalign requires in depth knowledge and an experienced professional’s watchful eye.
