About SOLAY DENTAL

Solay Dental is an exceptional general and cosmetic dental practice in Oldsmar and New Port Richey, Florida, where Shiva Soleimani, DDS and her team, provides preventive dental procedures, general dentistry services, implant dentistry, and aesthetic treatments that enhance patients’ smiles.

The Solay Dental experts take a holistic approach to care and offer top-quality dental treatments using skill, artistry, and attention to detail. The superior specialists ensure every patient experience is stress-free and comfortable in a state-of-the-art facility.

The outstanding dental providers keep patients’ smiles healthy, beautiful, and free from disease. Solay Dental offers dental cleanings, oral cancer screenings, virtual consults, fillings, dental implants, full and partial dentures, extractions, and other oral surgeries.

Solay Dental provides crowns, bridges, teeth whitening, veneers, dental bonding, Invisalign , and numerous other cosmetic dentistry options. The practice also offers dermal fillers and additional aesthetic injectables.

The Solay Dental professionals treat patients with utmost compassion, care, and transparency. The specialists have patients’ best interests at heart, always giving them a voice in their treatment plans.

Solay Dental sees emergency patients in a timely manner, and the staff informs patients about the costs involved before treating them and even files insurance claims.

They warmly welcome existing and new patients to the practice and look forward to perfecting their gorgeous smiles.
