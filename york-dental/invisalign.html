How Does Invisalign Work?

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that straightens teeth using a series of custom-made clear aligners. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign does not require metal brackets or wires, making it a discreet and comfortable solution for patients seeking a more confident smile.

Each set of aligners is carefully designed to apply gentle, controlled pressure to your teeth, gradually moving them into proper alignment. The aligners are made from smooth, transparent material that fits comfortably over your teeth and remains virtually invisible during treatment.

Because Invisalign aligners are removable, you can continue enjoying your favorite foods and maintain your normal brushing and flossing routine with ease.

How Long Does Invisalign Take?

Treatment time varies depending on the complexity of your case and your individual smile goals. Many patients begin noticing improvements within several weeks, while most Invisalign treatments are completed within 6 to 18 months .

For the best results, aligners should be worn 22–23 hours per day , removing them only when eating, drinking anything other than water, and performing your oral hygiene routine. Throughout treatment, you will switch to a new set of aligners every one to two weeks as your teeth gradually move into their ideal positions.
