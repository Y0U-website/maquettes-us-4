Whether you re here for a routine cleaning or more advanced treatment, we’ll take the time to understand your needs and make sure your experience is as easy and stress-free as possible.

Our warm, modern office was thoughtfully designed with your comfort in mind. From soothing décor and relaxing amenities like massage chairs and TVs in treatment rooms, to personalized care and flexible scheduling, everything we do is centered around you. Dr. Kim’s gentle approach and years of experience ensure that every patient receives individualized care in a supportive, judgment-free environment. We value our relationships with our patients and strive to create a dental home where you feel truly cared for.

Dr. Gloria Kim grew up in Centreville, Virginia and went on to graduate from the University of Virginia with bachelor’s degrees in chemistry and Spanish. Dr. Kim received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Virginia Commonwealth University School of Dentistry in 2009 and completed general practice residency at the Washington D.C. Veterans Affairs Medical Center.
