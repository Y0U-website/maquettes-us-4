The All-on-X Procedure

The process begins with a comprehensive consultation, where we conduct a thorough examination, including 3D imaging, to create a treatment plan tailored specifically for you. During the implant placement, the implants are precisely positioned into the jawbone. After the surgery, you’ll leave with a beautiful set of temporary teeth, providing you with an immediate improvement. Over the next 4-6 months, the implants bond with your bone, creating a stable foundation. After healing, we replace the temporary set with your final, custom-made prosthetics for a perfect, long-lasting smile.

Who Is a Good Candidate for All-on-X?

All-on-X is an excellent choice for patients who are missing multiple teeth or an entire arch, desire a fixed, permanent alternative to dentures, and have adequate bone density or are interested in bone grafting to support implants. If you’re unsure whether All-on-X is right for you, schedule a consultation at Prime Dental to discuss your goals and receive expert guidance on your options.
