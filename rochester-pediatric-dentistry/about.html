Our Passion Is Pediatric Dentistry

The vision of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) is optimal health and dental care for infants and children through adolescence, including those with special healthcare needs. At Rochester Pediatric Dentistry, our goal is to uphold this vision and promote healthy smiles for all of our patients. Our goal is to provide the highest quality dental care possible, while paying close attention to all of your child’s needs and well-being.

Our office staff has over 70 years of combined experience serving kids and we strive to make every visit a positive experience. At Rochester Pediatric Dentistry, we are specialists in the treatment of children and foster an environment designed specifically for pediatric patients.

The dental home is an ongoing relationship between our dental office and our patients to provide comprehensive and coordinated care. Dental caries, periodontal diseases, and other oral conditions, if left untreated, can lead to pain, infection, emotional trauma and loss of function. This being the case, we follow the recommendation of the AAPD to establish a dental home for all children no later than the age of 12 months.

Dr. Kelly strives to maintain the optimal dental home for all Rochester Pediatric Dentistry patients and thus, subscribes to the vigorous standards necessary to maintain Diplomate status and board-certification with AAPD.

To learn more about the AAPD and up to date information about current dental recommendations, visit their site at aapd.org .
