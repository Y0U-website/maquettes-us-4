Our Team

Dr. Kelly Jones is a board certified pediatric dentist who grew up near Madison, Wisconsin. She received her bachelor’s degree from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Marquette University School of Dentistry where she graduated summa cum laude. Her education continued at the University of Iowa College of Dentistry where she earned her Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry. Dr. Kelly has earned Diplomate status with the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Kelly strives to provide comprehensive dental care for all children and adolescents. She truly enjoys the time spent working one-on-one with young patients as a pediatric dentist. Through her calm and encouraging demeanor, Dr. Kelly aims to produce good dental experiences for all of her patients.

Outside of her busy practice, Dr. Kelly enjoys spending time with her family and friends. Dr. Kelly and her husband have twin daughters and love exploring Rochester as a family.

Dr. Kelly also is an avid horsewoman, having grown up on a small farm and showing horses much of her life.

Nicole Youngers is board certified pediatric dentist who has a strong passion for helping children. She is an Iowa native, who earned a bachelor’s degree from Marquette University in Milwaukee, WI. She continued on to receive her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry. Following graduation, she practiced as a general dentist for eight years in Medford and Sisters, Oregon. Her love of children brought her back to school where she completed her Certificate in Pediatric Dentistry at University of Iowa College of Dentistry.

Dr. Youngers is passionate about providing quality dental care for children and those with special health care needs. She strives to make every dental visit a comfortable experience. She hopes to help promote a lifetime of good oral and dental care to her patients while establishing a trusting relationship with her patients and their families.

Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband and their two children. Together they enjoy spending time outside, biking, boating, camping, and skiing.
