Deron Davenport, DDS

A Tucson native and fourth-generation Wildcat, Dr. Deron Davenport has been proudly serving patients in his hometown since 2001. After earning his undergraduate degree in Exercise and Sport Science at the University of Arizona—where he was a student manager on the 1997 NCAA National Championship basketball team—he went on to complete his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Southern California.

A lifelong Wildcat, Dr. Davenport served as a manager with the University of Arizona basketball program while he was in college — the same program that made the Final Four in 2026, further cementing his deep ties to the university and the Tucson community he loves.

Dr. Davenport is an active member of the American Academy of Restorative Dentistry, the American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry, the Academy of Private Dental Practice, the American Dental Association, and the Arizona Dental Association (SADS). Deeply committed to his community, he is also a proud member of The Centurions, a charitable organization dedicated to supporting local causes.

Whether he&#x27;s rebuilding a smile from the ground up or preserving a single tooth, Dr. Davenport is passionate about providing high-quality care that improves both oral health and confidence—helping every patient feel their best.

Kirsten Linaker, DDS

Dr. Kirsten Linaker combines clinical skill with a down-to-earth approach that helps patients feel at ease. She believes great dentistry begins with genuine connection—and she takes the time to truly listen, explain, and tailor care to each person she sees.

Originally from California, Dr. Linaker earned her undergraduate degree in psychology from the University of San Diego before completing her Doctor of Dental Medicine at A.T. Still University’s Arizona School of Dentistry and Oral Health. During dental school, she provided care in community health centers, where she developed a lasting commitment to compassionate, service-oriented dentistry.

At Davenport & Davenport Dental Practice, Dr. Linaker treats patients of all ages with a strong emphasis on prevention, education, and long-term oral health. With a growing interest in cosmetic dentistry, she continues to expand her skills—most recently by pursuing advanced training in Botox therapy.

Approachable, attentive, and always striving for clinical excellence, Dr. Linaker is proud to serve the Tucson community she now calls home.

Honoring Dr. David Davenport

For nearly five decades, Dr. David Davenport was a cornerstone of Tucson dentistry, founding Davenport & Davenport Dental Practice on a commitment to integrity, excellence, and treating patients like family. His vision and high standards built the foundation for the practice&#x27;s trusted reputation.

Dr. Davenport retired in 2018 after a long and rewarding career, and today his legacy lives on through the continued work of his son, Dr. Deron Davenport, and the entire team. His dedication to patients and the community remains a guiding inspiration, as Davenport & Davenport celebrates nearly 50 years of caring for Tucson families.
