The benefits of preventive dental care

Protect your oral and overall health Preventive visits catch issues early and help lower your risk of cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer.

Save time and money Regular care helps you avoid costly, complex procedures by stopping problems before they worsen.

Maintain a confident smile Cleanings and checkups keep your teeth looking and feeling their best, supporting both health and self-esteem.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I visit the dentist for preventive care?

Most patients benefit from a visit every six months. However, depending on your oral health needs, we may recommend more frequent visits to monitor gum health, treat buildup, or address specific concerns.

Do preventive visits include x-rays?

Yes. X-rays are an important part of your exam because they allow us to spot issues that aren’t visible to the eye, like cavities between teeth or bone loss. We take them only as needed, using digital technology for your safety.

Why is preventive care important if I brush and floss at home?

Even with great home care, plaque and tartar can build up in hard-to-reach areas. Preventive visits also allow us to catch problems early, before they cause pain or require more complex treatment.
