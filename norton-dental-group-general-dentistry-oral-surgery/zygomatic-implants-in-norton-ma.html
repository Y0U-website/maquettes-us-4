Who Benefits from Zygomatic Implants in Norton, MA?

Zygomatic implants are specifically designed for patients facing complex upper jaw reconstruction challenges:

Severe Bone Loss Patients

Individuals who have lost significant bone volume in the upper jaw due to long-term tooth loss, failed previous implants, or trauma can benefit from zygomatic implants when traditional implants aren’t feasible.

Failed Bone Graft Cases

Patients who have undergone unsuccessful bone grafting procedures or those who want to avoid the lengthy bone grafting process can achieve immediate results with zygomatic implants.

Sinus Issues

Patients with enlarged sinuses or sinus problems that complicate traditional implant placement often find zygomatic implants to be the ideal solution.

Immediate Full-Mouth Restoration

Those seeking full upper jaw restoration without waiting months or years for bone grafting procedures to heal can achieve their goals with zygomatic implants combined with immediate loading protocols. Patients also benefit from the ease of recovery, as the process is straightforward and allows a quick return to normal activities.

Treatment Benefits: Why Choose Zygomatic Implants?

Avoid Extensive Bone Grafting

Traditional approaches to severe upper jaw bone loss often require multiple bone grafting procedures that can take 6-18 months to heal before implant placement is possible. Zygomatic implants eliminate this lengthy process, allowing immediate implant placement and often same-day tooth delivery.

Immediate Results

Many patients receiving zygomatic implants in Norton, MA can have temporary teeth attached on the same day as implant placement. This means you can walk out of our office with a functional, attractive smile immediately following surgery.

High Success Rates

When properly planned and executed by experienced practitioners, zygomatic implants have success rates comparable to traditional implants, typically exceeding 95 % in appropriate cases.

Restored Function

Zygomatic implants provide excellent stability for chewing and speaking, allowing patients to enjoy a normal diet and speak clearly without the limitations associated with traditional dentures.

Preserve Facial Structure

By providing immediate stimulation to the bone, zygomatic implants help preserve facial height and prevent the sunken appearance that develops with prolonged tooth loss and bone resorption.
