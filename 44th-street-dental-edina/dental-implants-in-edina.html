What is a Dental Implant?

Surgically placed into the jawbone a dental implant is made of titanium. It can be called an artificial tooth that either supports the fixed bridges or replaces the entirety of the tooth.

They are a popular choice of dental solutions as they can perfectly replicate your natural teeth’s appearance and function. Preserving the bone structure, they effectively improve your speech, overall aesthetic, and facial structure.

If you want the freedom to speak freely and be confident while socializing with your friends get dental implants near you. You will surely observe the difference they make in your daily life and routine.

What to Expect During the Implant Procedure

We will divide the implant procedure into three parts to get a brief overview: consultation, implant and restoration. Whether it’s an endosteal implant or all-on-4 procedures for dental implants depending on their types

  • Consultation: When you visit a dentist in Edina to get a dental implant they will assess your problems. This is done through X-rays to examine your facial structure, specifically teeth, head, and jawbone.
  • Implant: In the jawbone beneath the gum tissue a metal, screw-like post made from platinum is placed. Don’t worry the implant will integrate with our bone and act as a support for the gum tissue.
  • Restoration: The healing of the jawbone after the implant takes around 3-9 months. But once the jawbone is healed your dentist will place an artificial tooth to complete the restoration.

How Long Does the Implant Take?

There are various types of dental implant procedures. It depends on the problem you are facing. Generally, a dental implant process takes around 1-3 hours. However, the healing and restoration phases take 3-9 months.

During this time your dentist might recommend visits that help to track your implant’s progress. It also ensures that the implants support the overall dental health for your well-being.

Care Tips for Implant

When you visit a dentist near you, they will advise these tips for proper care and best results.

  • Brushing twice a day
  • Don’t miss out on flossing to remove food particles and plaque build-up.
  • Avoid tobacco consumption; it slows down the healing process.
  • No hard foods for a short period during the healing phase.
  • Lastly, make sure you are consistent with your regular checkups.
