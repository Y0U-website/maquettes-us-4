Meet Dr. Kurt Tran

Dr. Kurt Tran is a USC-trained dentist and the founder of The Smile Lounge in Los Alamitos, California. With a General Practice Residency behind him and a focus on implants, oral surgery, and comprehensive care, he brings a higher level of skill and attentiveness to every patient he sees.

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Kurt Tran

Education & Training

Dr. Tran earned his dental degree from the University of Southern California, one of the most respected dental programs in the country. Following graduation, he completed a General Practice Residency, a year of advanced post-doctoral training that deepened his clinical skills across a broad range of procedures. That foundation is what allows him to confidently offer dental implants, oral surgery, and cosmetic care all under one roof. He continues to invest in his education, currently completing coursework in Invisalign clear aligner therapy to bring even more treatment options to his patients.

Philosophy of Care

Dr. Tran’s philosophy is rooted in the belief that every patient deserves honest, high-quality dentistry delivered in a comfortable, welcoming environment. He approaches each appointment with attentiveness and transparency, taking the time to explain treatment options and ensure you feel confident in your care. At The Smile Lounge, you are never rushed, you are a priority.

Patient-Centered Dentistry

Dr. Tran’s approach to dentistry goes beyond treating individual teeth; he takes a whole-patient perspective that considers your oral health in the context of your overall well-being. From cosmetic dentistry like porcelain veneers and professional teeth whitening to surgical services including bone grafting and implant placement, every service is delivered with the same level of care and precision.

Serving Los Alamitos and the Surrounding Community

The Smile Lounge is proud to serve the communities of Los Alamitos, Seal Beach, Rossmoor, and Cypress. We offer flexible scheduling, extended Friday hours, and financing options through Cherry, CareCredit, and Alphaeon to make quality dental care accessible for everyone. Dr. Tran and the team look forward to welcoming you. Contact our office to schedule your visit today.
