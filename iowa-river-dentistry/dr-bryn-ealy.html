MEET DR. BRYn!

As a dental professional, Dr. Bryn is a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Iowa Dental Association (IDA), Academy of General Dentists (AGD), and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI), in which she holds fellowship status. Dr. Bryn has a special interest in geriatrics and special needs populations, as well as placing and restoring implants, and advancing her skills and procedures in her office through in-classroom and hands-on continuing education.

She is a member of Rotary International Club and is on the committee for the Pat Clark Art Foundation.

Dr. Bryn and her husband, Reece, were married in November 2018, and welcomed their son, Silas, in December of 2022. They are expecting their second child in fall of 2024. And don t forget their beloved corgi, Crouton. In her free time she enjoys reading and cheering for the Iowa Hawkeyes!
