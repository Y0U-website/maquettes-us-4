About

Dr. Dan Javaheri

Dr. Daniel Javaheri brings years of experience and second-to-none patient care to the state-of-the-art practice in Vista, CA. Under his care, thousands of patients have been given the gift of a healthy, beautiful smile.

Since graduating from the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry , Dr. Javaheri has embarked on a very comprehensive (and busy) practice of general, cosmetic, and implant dentistry, both in the state of New York, and for the past several years, in Southern California where he settled in North San Diego County .

Dr. Javaheri has participated in extensive research and has been published by the National Institute of Health (NIH) and the UC Davis Medical Center. Additionally, he has pursued thousands of hours of continuing education in “Lumineers” cosmetic veneers, Invisalign , progressive orthodontics, and is also certified by the California Academy of Implant Dentistry . His participation in both research and continued clinical dentistry education, together demonstrate Dr. Javaheri’s commitment to his profession and more importantly, to the full-service treatment of his patients.
