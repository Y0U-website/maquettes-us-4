  • Predetermination of benefits are often requested for dental treatment. If it is not mentioned, be sure to request one so that you know what your responsibility will be. Predetermination benefits are not a guarantee of insurance payment.
  • Become an educated consumer of your dental insurance plan understanding your benefits and what is covered. Health insurance companies often are not allies in your health and they do not have any health care responsibility to you in any way.
  • It is your responsibility to provide accurate insurance information in a timely manner.
  • Accept responsibility for your dental insurance, benefits and for your balance regardless of insurance payments. It is expected and the patient’s responsibility to pay your balance at the time of service.
