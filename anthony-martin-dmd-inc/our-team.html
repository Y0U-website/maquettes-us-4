Dr. Anthony Martin, D.M.D

meet Dr. martin

Dr. Anthony Martin grew up in Knoxville, TN and attended East Tennessee State University and then Temple Dental School. Dr. Martin has attained Mastership Status in the Academy of General Dentistry and is currently serving as the Past President of the Tennessee Academy of General Dentistry . He is a member of the Second District Dental Society and active with the ADA. In 2018 Dr. Martin was selected as Dentist of the Year for Tennessee. Has been awarded Lifetime Service award in 2015 for work with the Knoxville Interfaith Clinic and VMC Dental Clinic.

Dr. Martin is married to Duy-Thu Martin who is an OBGyn in Knoxville. They have two sons and two daughters. As a family, they enjoy boating in the summer, hiking, and are involved in scouts.

Anthony A. Martin, DMD is a general dentist in both Crossville and Knoxville, TN whose focus is in family and cosmetic dentistry.

We offer a modern dentistry experience, including a comprehensive list of general , restorative and cosmetic dental services to meet the needs of your whole family. Our goal is to assist each patient in achieving and maintaining long term dental health and a beautiful smile.
