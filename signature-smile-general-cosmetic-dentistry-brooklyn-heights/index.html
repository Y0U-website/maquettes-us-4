The practice

Inna Trogan, DDS, specializes in cosmetic and general dentistry at Signature Smile General & Cosmetic Dentistry. She has been proudly providing dental care to residents throughout Brooklyn Heights in New York City for over 20 years.

Dr. Trogan graduated Summa Cum Laude from SUNY Stony Brook with a Bachelor of Science in biochemistry and received her DDS degree from the SUNY Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine. She furthered her education through a General Practice Residency at the New York Hospital of Queens in Fresh Meadows, New York.
