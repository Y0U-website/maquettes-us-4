Meet Your Dental Care Expert

Inna Trogan, DDS, specializes in cosmetic and general dentistry at Signature Smile General & Cosmetic Dentistry. She has been proudly providing dental care to residents throughout Brooklyn Heights in New York City for over a decade.

Dr. Trogan graduated Summa Cum Laude from SUNY Stony Brook with a Bachelor of Science in biochemistry and received her DDS degree from the SUNY Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine. She furthered her education through a General Practice Residency at the New York Hospital of Queens in Fresh Meadows, New York.

Dr. Trogan’s clinical interests include cosmetic dentistry, periodontics, and general dentistry. At Signature Smile General & Cosmetic Dentistry, she and her team offer a comprehensive range of dental services, including root canals , dental implant restorations , and dental crowns and bridges . She also helps patients achieve a smile they’re proud of through cosmetic services such as Invisalign® , teeth whitening , and veneers .

An active member of the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Dental Association, Dr. Trogan continuously stays up-to-date on the latest state-of-the-art technologies and advances in dentistry. She takes personal pride in providing courteous and high-quality professional treatment for all of her patients’ dental care needs.

Dr. Trogan and her husband have made Brooklyn their home for over 3 decades. Dr. Trogan has 3 kids who all attend schools in the Brooklyn/NYC area. She and her family are active members of the Brooklyn community.
