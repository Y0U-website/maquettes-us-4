Welcome to Newcastle Dentistry

Conveniently located in Newcastle, WA, our team of dentists and staff care for families from Newcastle and the communities of Bellevue, Renton, Issaquah, and Renton Highlands.

The practice

We are a family owned dental office, able to manage everything from your children’s checkups to more complex restorations, root canals, wisdom teeth extraction, tooth-colored fillings and cosmetic treatment such as tooth whitening and veneers.

Our highly skilled doctors and staff genuinely care for their patients and treat them like family.

With our three general dentists, Dr. Bishai, Dr. Strange and Dr. Schwarz, and our Pediatric Dentist, Dr. Michael Lee, your whole family can be seen in one office. We believe in modern dentistry by professionals who care.

While excellent oral health is a partnership between you and our team, we depend on you to make sure your teeth and gums stay strong and healthy between appointments. Together we can create an individual plan with the latest tools and knowledge to help protect your smile.
