Our Team

Annemarie

Annemarie began her dental career over 50 years ago after graduating from Midwestchester Center for Occupational Education in White Plains, NY as a trained dental assistant. Her current role is that of front office manager. She enjoys helping and interacting with patients within this practice for 28 years after starting as a dental assistant.

Annemarie values quality time with her five grown children and 3 grandchildren (and expecting 3 more this year!). She also loves working out in the gym and happy hour with her family when she is not at work.

Jackie

Jackie is a certified dental assistant with a passion for helping people achieve healthy and confident smiles. At 26 years old, she loves what she does and takes pride in making patients feel comfortable and cared for.

When she’s not in the office, you’d probably find her traveling and exploring new places. She loves experiencing different cultures, trying new foods, and making unforgettable memories along the way.

Lillian

Lillian is a Registered Dental Hygienist that grew up in San Diego spending a lot of time in the dental office. She had three rounds of braces, nine teeth extracted, and a whole lot of silver fillings and metal crowns as a child. Suffice it to say: she has spent a lot of her life as a patient in the dental chair, and wanted to be just like her dental friends when she grew up.

Now, she is passionate about educating her patients on improving their oral habits at home while providing gentle and thorough dental cleanings.

When she is not at the office, Lillian can be found cuddled up with her dachshund, Sailor, reading a book. She is always on the hunt for a cool coffee shop and record store, and enjoys off-roading, camping, hiking, and seeing live music with her husband, Jason. She can’t wait to get to know you better!
