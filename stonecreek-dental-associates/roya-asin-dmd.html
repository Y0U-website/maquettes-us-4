Meet Dr. Roya Asin

At Stonecreek Dental Associates, Dr. Roya Asin brings together skilled artistry, advanced clinical training, and a heartfelt commitment to patient-centered care. Inspired by her father’s legacy as a physician in private practice, Dr. Asin knew from a young age that she wanted to impact people’s lives through healthcare. Dentistry offered her the perfect opportunity: a hands-on, creative profession where she could foster meaningful relationships and deliver life-changing care.

A Journey Rooted in Passion and Precision

Dr. Asin earned her dental degree from Midwestern University College of Dental Medicine, where she served as class president and played a key role in student leadership initiatives. Her time at Midwestern not only sharpened her clinical skills but also reinforced her passion for mentorship and excellence—foundations that continue to guide her career today.

After years of private practice experience, Dr. Asin fulfilled her dream of opening Stonecreek Dental Associates: a boutique practice where personalized care, trust, and uncompromising quality are the heart of everything. Having her own practice allows her to deliver dentistry the way it should be—thoughtful, precise, and centered around the patient experience.

A Commitment to Lifelong Learning and Excellence

Dedicated to continual growth, Dr. Asin actively participates in advanced continuing education, including the Kois curriculum and the Pascal Magne Biomimetics series, and has completed extensive training in Invisalign therapy. She remains deeply involved with the American Dental Association, the Arizona Dental Association, and the Central Arizona Dental Society, where she previously served on the board.

At Stonecreek Dental, technology enhances the exceptional care already being delivered. From digital scanning to GBT hygiene technology, every advancement is selected with a simple goal in mind: to create a more comfortable, precise, and lasting experience for each patient.

A Philosophy Centered on Relationships, Trust, and Personal Care

For Dr. Asin, dentistry has never been just about teeth—it’s about people. Her approach is centered on building trust, fostering genuine connections, and ensuring that every patient feels truly seen and heard. She is passionate about empowering patients with education and creating a calm, unrushed environment where thoughtful, evidence-based care is the standard.

Every treatment plan at Stonecreek Dental is designed with integrity, meticulous attention to detail, and the long-term well-being of the patient in mind. Dr. Asin’s ultimate goal is to create lasting relationships—where patients feel like family and know they are cared for at every step.

A Practice with Deep Community Roots

For some dentists, an office address is just a location. For Dr. Roya Asin, it’s a part of her story.

Dr. Asin grew up less than a mile from Stonecreek Dental Associates, at the crossroads of Tatum and Shea—an area that has always felt like home. Her roots here run so deep that she gave her son the middle name of "Shea," a heartfelt tribute to the community that helped shape her childhood and dreams.

Today, Dr. Asin finds great joy in caring for many familiar faces—neighbors, former classmates’ families, and lifelong friends. For her, it’s about more than dentistry; it’s about giving back to the community that has given her so much.

Life Beyond the Practice

Outside the office, Dr. Asin treasures time with her husband, their two young children, and their two beloved dogs. While much of life right now is delightfully full and busy, she looks forward to future adventures—traveling, discovering new cuisines, and maybe even stealing a few peaceful moments with a good book.

Experience a Different Kind of Dental Care

At Stonecreek Dental Associates, Dr. Roya Asin combines clinical excellence with compassion, artistry, and an unwavering dedication to her patients’ well-being. Whether you’re visiting for preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, or a complete smile transformation, you’ll experience a level of personal attention and quality that’s simply different.

"At the end of the day, my greatest reward is knowing that my patients feel heard, valued, and genuinely cared for. That’s the kind of dentistry—and the kind of community—we’re building here." – Dr. Roya Asin
