All-on-4 vs. Dentures and Traditional Implants

Removable dentures rest on the gums and can slip, cause sore spots, and lose their fit as the jawbone shrinks over time. All-on-4, by contrast, locks a full arch of teeth onto implants for a stable, worry-free bite. Compared with implant-supported dentures that may use more implants, All-on-4 achieves a fixed full-arch result with only four — reducing surgical time, healing, and cost. If you currently wear dentures , All-on-4 is often the ideal upgrade to a permanent solution.

Am I a Candidate for All-on-4 in Sarasota?

You may be an excellent candidate for All-on-4 if you are missing most or all of your teeth in an arch, have several failing or hopeless teeth, or want a permanent alternative to dentures. Thanks to the angled implant design, many patients qualify even if they’ve been told they don’t have enough bone for conventional implants. The best way to find out is a personal evaluation and 3D scan with Dr. Michael, who will review your bone, gum health, and goals to build a plan around you.

The Cost of All-on-4 Dental Implants

Every smile is different, so the cost of All-on-4 depends on the arch being treated, the material of your final bridge, any needed extractions, and your specific case. Because All-on-4 restores a complete arch on just four implants, it is frequently more economical than replacing each tooth individually.
