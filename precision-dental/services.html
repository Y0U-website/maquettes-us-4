Custom Porcelain Veneers

Striking the perfect balance between dental art and science, veneers are hand-crafted, paper-thin pieces of porcelain bond to the outside of your natural teeth. There’s an extensive amount of detailed diagnostic work that goes into a treatment plan that includes veneers. From models to photographs to digital x-rays, there are many steps needed for us to create an ideal plan for your treatment. Once the veneers are thoughtfully crafted, they will be precisely bonded to the surface of your teeth.

Crowns

If your tooth has been weakened by multiple fillings or having a root canal treatment, then it may need a crown placed to protect it and build it back to the correct shape and color. Dental crowns are caps placed on top of damaged teeth which can be made out of metals, porcelain, resin, and ceramics. We make a wide variety of crowns, both tooth-colored and metallic to suit each situation in different parts of your mouth.

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding is a procedure in which a tooth-colored resin material is bonded to the teeth. This procedure is used to repair teeth, change their shape, or close gaps between them. From repairing chipped teeth to closing space between teeth, there are countless applications of this procedure. Although it has so many use cases, it generally only takes 30 to 60 minutes and is also one least expensive cosmetic (“appearance-changing”) dental procedures.
