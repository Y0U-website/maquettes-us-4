The practice

Avoid discomfort and high future dental costs by choosing Pediatric Dental Center! Our dentists focus on preventative education, teaching your child how to properly care for his or her teeth and gums. We offer preventive services like cleanings, X-rays, and examinations to help prevent problems before they begin.

At Pediatric Dental Center, we understand that a life full of healthy smiles starts early—as early as a child’s first birthday! Bring your child to see our team of compassionate professionals. We can determine if your child’s teeth and gums are developing properly, helping them set up a foundation of oral health to last a lifetime.

We work hard to make our practice stand out. Here are some things you can expect from Pediatric Dental Center:

Your kids will feel right at home when they walk through our doors. Pediatric dentistry is about having fun. It’s about learning to trust our phenomenal dentists and reaping the benefits of a healthy smile. We make kids feel excited about pediatric dentistry by providing an office with tons of kid-friendly amenities.

We understand the importance of education, and we always choose prevention over treatment. Preventing cavities and other oral health issues will keep your child comfortable and healthy while keeping your dental costs low. We teach our patients and parents how to maintain healthy teeth and gums.

Our board-certified pediatric dentist in Northern Kentucky never uses potentially harmful materials or tools. This is a mercury-free dental practice; we only use composite fillings and stainless steel crowns. We stay educated on the latest discoveries and we know what is safe for your child.

We don’t settle for average pediatric dentistry. Dentistry for children should be a unique experience for each patient, depending on their needs and preferences.

You live a busy life. We offer dentistry for children in multiple locations for your convenience, and we strive to make scheduling pleasant and simple.
