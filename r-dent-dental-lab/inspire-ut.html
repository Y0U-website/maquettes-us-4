INspire UT R

INspire UT restorations can be used for almost any C & B or screw-retained implant case when anterior or posterior crowns, veneers and 3-unit bridges are prescribed. Also it can be used as an esthetic alternative to a PFM with metal occlusion due to limited occlusal or lingual space.

INspire HS restorations can be used for any C & B or screw-retained implant case when anterior or posterior crowns and long-span bridges are prescribed. Also it can be used as an esthetic alternative to a PFM with metal occlusion due to limited occlusal or lingual space.
