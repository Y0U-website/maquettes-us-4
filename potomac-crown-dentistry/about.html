Meet Dr. Simranjit Bawa

Dr. Bawa didn’t have to look far for his purpose. He grew up right here in Potomac, Maryland. In the same neighborhoods, on the same streets as many of the patients he now serves. That connection to this community isn’t incidental. It’s the reason he came back, and the reason he built something worth coming back to.

That sense of purpose is rooted in something deeper than geography. As a US-born Sikh, Dr. Bawa was raised with the principles of selfless service to others, woven into his everyday life. For him, dentistry was never simply a career path. It was a natural extension of that commitment: a way to show up for the people around him in a way that’s meaningful and lasting. That philosophy quietly shapes everything at Potomac Crown, from the way the office feels the moment you walk in, to the way his team answers the phone.

Academically, Dr. Bawa’s foundation is as strong as his reputation. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from American University in Washington, DC, before going on to receive his DDS from Howard University College of Dentistry in 2013 — one of the most respected dental programs in the country. He further refined his clinical skills through an Advanced Education in General Dentistry Residency at East Carolina School of Dental Medicine in North Carolina. It was during these years that his passion for cosmetic dentistry and clear aligner therapy began to take real shape.

Outside the office, Dr. Bawa is a family man through and through. He loves spending time with his loved ones, exploring new restaurants and cuisines, traveling, and singing. He brings that same enthusiasm and warmth into every appointment, and patients feel it the moment they meet him.

A different level of dentistry

We’ve thought carefully about what it feels like to visit a dental office for the first time — and we’ve worked just as carefully to make sure Potomac Crown feels nothing like what you might be dreading. Your first visit is designed to be thorough, relaxed, and completely on your terms. A practice and philosophy designed around you

iTero 3D scanners

Say goodbye to messy traditional impressions. Our iTero 3D scanners capture highly accurate digital impressions of your teeth quickly and comfortably. These scanners are central to our Invisalign treatment planning and ensure a precise fit for crowns, restorations, and more. You’ll actually be able to see your own teeth in three dimensions on screen, which makes understanding your treatment that much easier.

Digital X-rays

Our digital X-ray technology produces high-resolution images with significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays, so we can see more while exposing you to less. Sharper images mean more accurate diagnoses, earlier detection of potential issues, and better-informed treatment decisions. It’s one of those behind-the-scenes upgrades that makes a meaningful difference in the quality of your care.
