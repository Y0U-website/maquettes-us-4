Dr. Jackson

Dr. Sanjie Jackson grew up here in Dunedin Florida. However she was born on a small tropical island in the Caribbean called Dominica. A fun fact about Dominica, the Nature Island, is that it has 365 rivers. When she was eight years old her family moved to Saint Thomas U.S. VI.. and then at the age of 12 Dr. Jackson and her family moved to Dunedin Florida. She attended Dunedin Highland Middle School as well as Dunedin High School where she was heavily involved in Show Choir, Chamber Choir, Madrigals, and Drama (thespian troop 2177).

Dr. Jacksons practice philosophy is to help restore and maintain good oral health for all her patients by providing excellent dental care. She does this by being involved in her local dental study club. It is important to her that patients are as comfortable as possible while receiving personal dental care.

Its a privilege to serve the people of my community and I look forward to practicing here for many years to come.

Dr. Jacksons Affiliations: American Dental Association Upper Pinellas Dental Association Florida Dental Association West Coast Central Florida Dental Association Pinnacle Study Club Missionary Ventures
