The practice

Dr. Souza prides herself with offering her patients the best quality orthodontic care in a comfortable and fun environment. Dr. Souza is not only an Orthodontist but was a General Dentist for 2 years prior to her orthodontic training. This experience gives her an advantage when formulating individualized treatment plans for her patients of all ages.

Orthodontics is constantly evolving to provide patients with faster, safer, and more comfortable treatments. At Souza Family Orthodontics, we keep up with the latest technology to provide our patients with the very best.

At Souza Family Orthodontics, we offer patient-centered care every step of the way. Our treatments are curated to serve the needs of each of our patients.

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that an orthodontist evaluate all children by age 7. Although most children will not need early treatment, Dr. Souza will do a thorough exam and still monitor their growth and dental development to ensure a future healthy smile absolutely free of charge!

We are always here for you! One our highly skilled orthodontic assistants is always on call to handle any type of emergency over the phone.
