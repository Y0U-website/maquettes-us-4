The practice

Dr. Chatfield and his team are excited to help you live a healthier & happier life by opening your airway thereby improving your breathing, sleeping and overall health and wellness. The treatments used by Dr. Chatfield have improved conditions such as ADD/ADHD, hypertension, obesity, migraines, anxiety, depression, fibromyalgia, and many others.

Dr. Chatfield has practiced dentistry since graduating from Marquette University School of Dentistry in 2003. Although trained in all aspects of dentistry, he has a passion for treating patients suffering from sleep breathing disorders. He has gone through extensive training and educational courses specifically focused on treating the root causes of these disorders.

Dr. Chatfield loves helping people live healthier & happier lives with a patient-first philosophy. He provides only the highest level of care⁠. First, by identifying the root cause of your ailments, and then by thoroughly explaining the diagnosis and treatment options available.
