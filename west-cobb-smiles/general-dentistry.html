Signs You Need General Dentistry

Regular dental visits are crucial for maintaining optimal oral health. West Cobb Smiles recommends watching for these indicators that it’s time to schedule an appointment:

  • Persistent bad breath or strange taste in your mouth
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold foods and drinks
  • Bleeding or swollen gums when brushing or flossing
  • Visible discoloration or staining on teeth
  • Pain or discomfort when chewing
  • Been over 6 months since your last check-up

Importance of Consistent General Dentistry

Consistent general dentistry is essential for preventing serious oral health issues and maintaining overall wellbeing. Here’s why regular visits to West Cobb Smiles are so important:
