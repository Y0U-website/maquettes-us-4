What is a CEREC Crown?

CEREC crowns are created in office. CEREC crowns come as a solid block of ceramic or resin material. The type of crown needed is made on site within an hour. We prefer using CEREC crowns because it allows patients to leave our office with their permanent crown instead of a temporary crown. The other positive element for patients is that they only need a single appointment for the complete crown seating instead of multiple visits.

CEREC crowns are designed using computer technology to take a picture of the tooth that will receive a crown. The software works with the picture and Dr. Jenson to design your new tooth on the screen. Dr. Jenson will review the color to make sure it matches up to the rest of your teeth. Our goal is to make sure the new crown matches properly and that it helps to create a uniformed smile.
