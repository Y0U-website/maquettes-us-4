Services

In addition to routine cleanings, we offer protective treatments like dental sealants and fluoride applications to reduce your risk of decay—especially for children and patients with a history of cavities.

Scaling and root planing is the most effective non-surgical treatment for gum disease. At Hinsdale Dental, we use this deep cleaning procedure to eliminate harmful bacteria and restore gum health, helping you avoid more serious complications down the road.

An implant is a small titanium post that acts like a tooth root. Once placed in the jawbone, it creates a strong base for a custom crown, bridge, or denture. Because implants integrate with bone, they feel secure and function like natural teeth.
