A dentist whose approach begins with you

Meet Dr. Geetha Srinivasan, DMD

Dr. Geetha Srinivasan brings over 20 years of dental experience, and a lifelong passion for healthcare, to her Pennsylvania community. Born in India, she earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 1996 before continuing her education at Penn Dental Medicine in Philadelphia. Her mission has always been clear: to provide high-quality dental care that’s not only precise but deeply compassionate.

Dr. Geetha’s advanced qualifications reflect her commitment to treating the whole patient. She is a member of the International Association for Orthodontics and holds an Anesthesia Permit - Restricted II. Her advanced training includes fellowships in Implant Dentistry and Medically Complex Patient Care, as well as certifications in Periodontics and Myobrace therapy. This broad scope of knowledge enables her to offer truly comprehensive treatment options, so patients can receive well-informed, individualized care under one roof.

What makes Dr. Geetha particularly special is her belief in connecting all areas of dentistry to educate and empower patients. She excels in interdisciplinary care that helps her patients understand the "why" behind their treatment plans. From implants to sedation dentistry, she’s dedicated to ensuring that patients not only receive treatment but also peace of mind, especially those who deal with dental anxiety.

Dr. Geetha is known not just for her technical expertise, but for the warm relationships she builds with patients and their families. Her approach is especially appreciated by the generational families who continue to return for care, decade after decade, because they trust her to treat them with empathy, clarity, and the gentle touch that defines the practice.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Geetha enjoys hiking, traveling, and spending quality time with her daughters and beloved pets. These personal passions reflect the calming and approachable energy she brings into her practice every day. While she loves providing comprehensive care, she’ll be the first to admit, she’s not a fan of fillings!
