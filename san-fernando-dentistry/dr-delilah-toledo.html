Career and Philosophy

Dr. Toledo has been working as a general dentist in the San Fernando Valley since 2006. In 2011, she opened her own dental practice to better serve her community. Inspired by her childhood dentist and encouraged by Dr. Sera, a successful female dentist, she combines her skills with a passion for helping patients understand their care and feel confident. To stay up-to-date with the latest dental tools and techniques, she completes over 50 hours of continuing education every two years.

Outside the office, Dr. Toledo enjoys gardening, crocheting, quilting, and reading. She loves gaming with her sons, hiking, and traveling with her family.
