Are Implant-Supported Dentures Right for You?

Implant-Supported Dentures are ideal for those seeking a stable alternative to removable dentures. Consider this option if you:

  • Are frustrated with loose or uncomfortable traditional dentures
  • Want a more stable option without needing a full set of implants
  • Are looking to improve chewing ability and speak with confidence
  • Seek to maintain your facial structure and prevent jawbone loss

Implant Overdenture FAQs

If you have any questions about Implant Overdenture, we’ve got answers.

Most lower overdentures are secured with two to four implants, while upper overdentures typically require four to six for optimal stability.

While implant overdentures may not feel exactly like natural teeth, they offer a major upgrade in comfort, stability, and function compared to traditional dentures. Most patients find them to be a life-changing improvement.

To keep your implant-supported overdenture in great shape, clean it daily, gently brush around the implants, and don’t skip your regular dental check-ups.

Overdentures are also known as implant-supported dentures, implant-retained dentures, or snap-on dentures—each name highlighting their secure fit and stable connection to dental implants.
