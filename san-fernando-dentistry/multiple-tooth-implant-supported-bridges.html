Is an Implant-Supported Bridge Right for You?

You may be a great candidate for multiple-tooth implant-supported bridges if you:

  • Have several missing teeth in a row
  • Want a strong, lasting solution that looks and feels like natural teeth
  • Prefer to preserve your remaining natural teeth
  • Are looking for an alternative to removable partial dentures
  • Have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone to support implants

Implant-Supported Bridge Dental Implants FAQs

If you have any questions about Implant-supported bridges, we’ve got answers.

The full process for multiple-tooth implant-supported bridges usually takes about 4 to 6 months, depending on how quickly your jawbone heals and integrates with the implants.

We customize each implant-supported bridge to match the shape, color, and alignment of your natural teeth, so your new smile looks seamless and feels completely your own.

Caring for your implant-supported bridge is simple and similar to caring for natural teeth. Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss daily—especially around the implant area—and consider using a water flosser for extra support. Regular check-ups and cleanings are also important to keep your gums healthy and ensure your bridge stays secure.
