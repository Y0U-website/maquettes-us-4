Are Single-Tooth Implants Right for You?

Single-tooth implants are a great option if you’re looking for a lasting solution that doesn’t involve altering nearby teeth. You may be a good candidate if you have:

  • One missing tooth that needs replacement
  • Failed root canals requiring tooth replacement
  • Long-term missing teeth ready for permanent solutions
  • A desire for a secure, natural-looking solution that feels just like your own tooth
  • Healthy gums and enough bone to support an implant
