What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is a full-arch technique that replaces an entire upper or lower set of teeth with a fixed bridge anchored on as few as four dental implants . The two rear implants are placed at an angle to use the strongest available bone which is why many patients qualify without bone grafting , even after years of wearing dentures .

Unlike a removable denture, an All-on-4 restoration stays fixed in place. You brush it, chew with it, and speak with it like natural teeth no adhesives, no slipping, no covered palate. For patients missing most or all of their teeth , it is the closest thing modern dentistry has to growing a new set. And when some teeth are still worth saving, Dr. Cannady may recommend full mouth reconstruction instead rebuilding with crowns, veneers, and targeted implants rather than replacing everything.

Teeth in a Day: How Same-Day Dental Implants Work

All-on-4 is often called teeth in a day or same-day dental implants and the name is earned, with one honest caveat. When your implants achieve enough stability at placement, Dr. Cannady attaches a fixed temporary bridge the same day as surgery, so you never walk out of the office without teeth. You eat (carefully), smile, and speak with a full arch while your implants heal.

Your Path to a Fixed Full-Arch Smile

1. Consultation & 3D imaging. Digital X-rays and 3D imaging show exactly what your bone supports. You leave with a written, itemized plan and transparent pricing no surprises, no pressure.

2. Implant placement. Four or more implants are placed per arch under local anesthesia, with sedation options for anxious patients. In select cases a fixed temporary bridge goes on the same day.

3. Healing & final teeth. Over the following months the implants fuse with your jawbone. Then your final custom full-arch restoration is attached designed for your face, your bite, and your smile.

All-on-4 Dental Implants Cost in Clearwater

What determines All-on-4 dental implants cost? Four things: how many implants your arch needs (four is the minimum, not always the answer), whether any bone grafting is required, the materials in your final restoration (acrylic hybrid vs. zirconia, for example), and the sedation you choose for surgery. Nationally, full-arch implant-supported restorations typically range from about $12,000 to $30,000 per arch depending on whether the final restoration is removable or fixed.
