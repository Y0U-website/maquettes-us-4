The Implant Process

From your first visit to your final restoration, here’s what to expect when you choose dental implants at Cannady Dental.

Consultation & Planning

Dr. Cannady performs a thorough examination including digital X-rays and 3D imaging to evaluate your jawbone, gum health, and overall candidacy. He’ll create a detailed, personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is precisely placed into the jawbone during a minor surgical procedure. Local anesthesia and sedation options ensure your complete comfort throughout the process. A temporary restoration may be placed the same day.

Healing Period

Over the next 3 to 6 months, the implant undergoes osseointegration fusing naturally with your jawbone to create an incredibly strong foundation. During this time, you’ll have periodic check-ups to monitor your healing progress.

Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, the abutment is attached and a custom-crafted ceramic crown is permanently secured to the implant. The result is a beautiful, fully functional tooth that blends seamlessly with your natural smile.

Wondering about same-day dental implants? In select cases when your bone quality and implant stability allow Dr. Cannady can place a temporary tooth the same day your implant is placed, so you never walk out with a visible gap. Your 3D imaging at the consultation determines whether same-day teeth are an option for you.

The Most Natural-Looking Permanent Solution

Dental implants are the closest thing to natural teeth. Unlike dentures or bridges, implants are permanently anchored into your jawbone, providing unmatched stability, function, and aesthetics. They look, feel, and work just like your natural teeth.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them the most cost-effective long-term solution for missing teeth.

Dental Implant Cost & Financing in Clearwater

Dental implant cost depends on how many teeth you are replacing and how you replace them: a single tooth implant, an implant-supported bridge for several adjacent teeth, and a full-arch restoration are each planned and priced differently which is why questions like how much do full mouth dental implants cost? have no one-size answer. The number of implants, whether bone grafting is needed, the materials used, and any sedation you choose all affect the total.

As a starting point: nationally, a single dental implant with its abutment and crown generally runs between $3,000 and $5,000, while full-arch implant-supported options typically range from about $12,000 to $30,000 depending on whether the restoration is removable or fixed.
