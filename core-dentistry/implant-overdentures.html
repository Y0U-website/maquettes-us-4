FAQs About Implant Overdentures

How many implants are needed for an overdenture?

Most implant overdentures are supported by two to four implants, depending on the arch being treated and your bone structure.

Are implant overdentures removable?

Yes. Implant overdentures are designed to be removed for cleaning while remaining secure during daily activities.

Do implant overdentures feel natural?

Yes. Patients often report improved comfort and confidence compared to traditional dentures due to reduced movement and improved fit.

How long do implant overdentures last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades. The overdenture itself may require maintenance or replacement over time due to normal wear.

How do I maintain implant overdentures?

Daily cleaning of the denture and attachments is essential, along with regular dental cleanings and exams to keep the implants and surrounding tissue healthy.
