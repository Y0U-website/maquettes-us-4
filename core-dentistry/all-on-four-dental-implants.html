Our All-on-4 Process at Core Dentistry

At Core Dentistry, your All-on-4 journey is carefully planned and customized to your unique needs. Here&#x27;s what to expect:

  • Initial Consultation We begin with a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, and 3D imaging using our Cone Beam CT scanner to assess bone structure and confirm your candidacy.
  • Treatment Planning If necessary, we’ll address any preliminary issues such as tooth extractions or bone grafting to prepare for implant placement.
  • Implant Placement Four implants are placed into the jaw—two vertically and two at angles to maximize bone support and avoid anatomical structures. A temporary full-arch prosthetic is typically attached the same day.
  • Final Restoration After a healing period of several months, your custom permanent prosthetic is placed. It’s designed for a precise fit, optimal comfort, and natural aesthetics.

FAQs About All-on-4 Dental Implants

How long do All-on-4 dental implants last?

With proper care, the implants themselves can last a lifetime. The prosthetic teeth may need replacement after 10–15 years due to wear but can often be repaired or updated.

Am I a good candidate for All-on-4?

Most adults in good general and oral health are candidates. Even patients with some bone loss may still qualify thanks to the angled implant placement. We’ll confirm your eligibility during your initial exam.

Will I leave with teeth the same day?

In many cases, yes. A temporary set of teeth can often be placed on the same day as your surgery, allowing you to leave with a complete smile.

Is the All-on-4 procedure painful?

The procedure is performed with local anesthesia or sedation if needed, and most patients report only minor discomfort afterward. Recovery is typically faster than with traditional implant procedures.

How do I care for my All-on-4 prosthetic?

Daily brushing, flossing with special tools, and regular dental cleanings and exams are essential to maintain the health of your implants and gums.
