Single Tooth Dental Implants

For patients missing only one tooth, a single dental implant offers a permanent solution without affecting the surrounding teeth. A custom dental crown is attached to the implant, providing the same strength, appearance, and function as a natural tooth.

  • Prevents bone loss in the jaw
  • Does not require filing down neighboring teeth (as a bridge would)
  • Easy to care for with regular brushing and flossing
  • Lasts decades with proper care

Benefits of Dental Implants

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care. The crown or restoration placed on top may need replacement after 10–15 years due to wear.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

The procedure is typically done under local anesthesia and is well-tolerated by most patients. Mild soreness afterward is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain medication.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults are good candidates for implants. You&#x27;ll need healthy gums and sufficient jawbone volume. We offer bone grafting if bone support is lacking.

What if I&#x27;m missing several teeth?

We can use multiple implants to support a dental bridge or even full-arch solutions like implant overdentures for comprehensive smile restoration.

How do I care for my implants?

Maintain good oral hygiene, attend routine dental cleanings and exams , and avoid habits like smoking to protect your implant investment.
