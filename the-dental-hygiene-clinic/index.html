The Dental Hygiene Clinic at Macomb Community College

We provide a variety of preventative services at reduced costs to the general public with additional discounts for senior citizens, military veterans and Macomb students.

The practice

All services are performed by dental hygiene students under the supervision of licensed dental professionals.

Tuesday & Thursday: 8:00 am - 12:00 pm | 1:00 pm - 5:00 pm | 5:30 pm - 9:30 pm

Macomb’s Strategic Plan defines the college’s goals and the strategies for achieving them, guiding institutional priorities through continuous improvement.

These foundational components were developed through a collaborative process that included the work of the Strategic Planning Committee and Work Team , with input from stakeholders across the college and approval of the College’s Board of Trustees.

The Strategic Plan now enters the next phase, centered around five revised Strategies to further drive continuous improvement through 2025 and beyond. This milestone reflects the dedication of numerous strategic planning teams committed to advancing student success, community engagement and institutional excellence.

To support the college’s strategic planning process, Macomb partnered with CampusWorks, a consulting firm with more than 25 years of experience providing educational leadership and advisory services to higher education institutions. CampusWorks was selected for its proven success in guiding strategic planning efforts with other community colleges nationwide, as well as its history with Macomb…

Leading the project for CampusWorks is Kevin David, who has more than 20 years of experience in higher education. He holds a Ph.D. in Developmental Psychology from the University of Oklahoma and previously served as provost and associate vice president for Institutional Effectiveness at Tulsa Community College.
