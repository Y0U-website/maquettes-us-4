The practice

Removal of hard and soft deposits with included guided biofilm therapy

Is a non-invasive dental cleaning method used to remove biofilm from your teeth and gums. Using advanced dental tools, GBT eliminates this sticky bacterial layer which can be overlooked in traditional cleanings. When completed, your mouth will feel fresher, your teeth and gums cleaner and will leave you smiling!

Effective and thorough cleaning without the traditional scraping tools for better oral health.

Removing biofilm lowers the chance of developing cavities and gum disease.

Guided Biofilm Therapy can be customized for those with sensitive mouths, braces or other needs.
