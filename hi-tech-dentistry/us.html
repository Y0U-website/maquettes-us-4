Dr. Tatyana Brayman D.D.S. F.A.G.D.

Dr. Tatyana Brayman graduated with honors from Ukrainian Medical Academy in 1983. Upon graduation from the dental school, she attended the Ukrainian State Institute of Art, receiving her degree in management of ballroom dancing.

After moving to the United States Dr. Brayman continued teaching ballroom dancing, and in 1997 she was admitted to the University of Detroit School of Dentistry. During her time at the University of Detroit, Dr. Brayman was on the Deans List for nine of the nine semesters, graduating second in her class in May of 2000. She was awarded the American Academy of Oral Medicine Certificate of Merit, Russell Bunting Study Club Award, Chester Coccia Pediatric Dentistry Award.

Dr. Brayman is the current owner of the 30 year well established practice, and excels at attendance of continuing education courses, as well as investing in the latest technology. Along with her husband and two sons, she enjoys dancing, playing guitar and piano, nature and collecting minerals.
