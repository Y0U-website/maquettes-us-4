Who Is a Good Candidate for a Single Implant?

Most patients with one missing tooth can benefit from an implant, especially if they want a durable and natural-looking restoration.

If bone support is limited, additional treatment options (such as bone grafting) may be discussed to ensure the best results.

  • Have healthy gums
  • Have adequate jawbone support
  • Want a permanent, fixed replacement
  • Prefer a strong and natural-feeling option
  • Are missing a single tooth due to injury, decay, or extraction

FAQs About Single Dental Implants

How long does a dental implant last? With proper care, most implants last a lifetime. It’s one of the most reliable dental treatments available.

Is the procedure painful? Most patients report minimal discomfort. We use gentle techniques and local anesthesia to keep you comfortable.

Will the implant match my natural teeth? Yes — your custom crown is color-matched and shaped to blend seamlessly with your smile.

How long does the process take? The entire process typically spans a few months, allowing time for the implant to bond securely with the bone.

Can anyone get a dental implant? Most healthy adults qualify, but a consultation helps us determine if this is the right solution for you.
