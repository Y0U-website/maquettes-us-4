Why Deep Cleaning Matters

Treating gum disease early protects your smile in powerful ways:

Untreated gum disease doesn’t just affect your mouth — it’s associated with heart disease, diabetes complications, and more. Deep Cleaning plays a critical role in oral and overall wellness.

  • Stops active gum infection
  • Reduces inflammation and bleeding
  • Protects bone and gum support
  • Prevents tooth mobility or loss
  • Improves breath and overall oral health
  • Lowers risk of systemic conditions linked to gum disease

Deep Cleaning FAQs

Will the treatment hurt? Most patients feel little to no discomfort thanks to effective numbing and gentle techniques.

How long does the appointment take? Typically 1–2 visits, depending on the severity of gum inflammation.

Do gums grow back after treatment? Gums don’t “grow back,” but they can reattach and tighten around the tooth once inflammation is controlled.

Is Deep Cleaning covered by insurance? Many plans do offer coverage. We’ll help verify your benefits before treatment.

What happens if I don’t treat gum disease? Untreated disease can lead to bone loss, tooth mobility, tooth loss, and systemic health complications.
