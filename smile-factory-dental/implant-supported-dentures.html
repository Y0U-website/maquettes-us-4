How Implant-Supported Dentures Work

The treatment is designed around strategic implant placement to support the full arch of teeth. Typically, 2–6 implants may be used depending on the type of restoration and jawbone health.

This creates a stable, long-lasting foundation that feels more like natural teeth compared to conventional dentures.

  • Implants integrate with the jawbone , reinforcing bone strength and preventing bone loss.
  • The denture attaches securely , reducing movement and improving bite performance.
  • Force is distributed through the implants instead of the gums, allowing improved functional efficiency.

Clinical Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Implants lock the denture into place, significantly reducing or eliminating denture movement.

Patients often experience a major increase in bite strength, allowing a more varied and comfortable diet.

Implants stimulate the bone, helping slow or prevent bone resorption following tooth loss.

Reduced gum pressure means fewer sore spots, less irritation, and a more natural feel.

By maintaining bone volume and supporting proper function, implants contribute to better long-term oral health outcomes.

  • Enhanced Stability & Retention
  • Improved Chewing Capacity
  • Preserves Jawbone Structure
  • Greater Comfort
  • Long-Term Oral Health Support

Our Clinical Process for Implant-Supported Dentures

At Smile Factory Dental, treatment planning is precise and comprehensive. Here is an overview of the clinical workflow:

1. Consultation & Diagnostic Imaging

We use 3D CBCT scans and digital impressions to assess bone health, prosthetic needs, and implant positioning.

2. Treatment Planning

Bone quality, bite alignment, and facial structure are evaluated to determine the number and placement of implants. We discuss various prosthetic designs (snap-in vs. fixed hybrid) based on your goals.

3. Implant Placement

Implants are positioned in areas with optimal bone support. Many patients only require 4–6 implants per arch.

4. Healing & Osseointegration

Over several weeks to months, the bone integrates with the implants, forming a stable foundation.

5. Attachment of the Denture

A custom-designed denture is created to attach securely to the implants, restoring function and appearance.

6. Follow-Up & Maintenance

We provide guidance on hygiene routines, periodic evaluations, and long-term care.

This systematic approach ensures predictable, durable, and comfortable outcomes.
