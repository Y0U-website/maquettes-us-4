Financing & membership options

Everyone deserves a healthy, confident smile, and finances should not be a barrier to getting the care you need. Smile Factory Dental offers several flexible payment and financing options so you can proceed with treatment now and pay over time.

For patients without insurance—or for services not fully covered—we offer an in‑office membership program that includes routine preventive care plus discounts on additional treatments for a simple annual or monthly fee. This can be an excellent alternative to traditional dental insurance for many individuals and families.

We also partner with reputable third‑party financing providers and accept major credit cards, allowing you to divide larger treatment costs into manageable monthly payments. Our team will walk you through all available options and help you choose the solution that best fits your budget.

One of the options that we provide for financing is CareCredit. CareCredit is a credit card exclusively used toward your healthcare needs. If our patients can’t pay their bills right now, then CareCredit is there for them to have the option to pay with credit. This is a successful strategy because it means they get the care that they need. This is one of the best ways to assist in handling healthcare bills.
