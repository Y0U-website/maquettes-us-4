Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment are accepted? We accept cash, checks, and credit cards and have a convenient online payment option.
  • When can I start using my membership? Your can start using your membership immediately—no waiting periods! Call today to schedule an appointment or schedule online. Schedule Online Call Smoky Mountain Dental
  • How can I use my member exclusive discount for treatment? When scheduling an appointment, simply tell us you’re a member. When you come in, we’ll make sure your membership is current and you will be eligible to save on your treatment.
  • Can I add members to my account? Absolutely! You can sign up other family members.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Your membership is only with Smoky Mountain Dental and is non-transferrable to another dental office.
