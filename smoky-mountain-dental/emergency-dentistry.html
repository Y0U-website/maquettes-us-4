Emergency Dentistry in Maryville, TN

While inconvenient and stressful, dental emergencies do happen, unfortunately. No need to worry though, the team at Smoky Mountain Dental is here to help. Whether you have an unexpected accident or intense pain that comes suddenly, our emergency dentistry services will help restore comfort and function when you need it most.

If you experience a dental emergency, please call us during our office hours for assistance.

What to Do if You Need Emergency Dentistry

Call us immediately if you believe you have a dental emergency. If needed, we will give instructions on treating your injury until you can reach our office. This may include managing bleeding, preserving a knocked-out tooth, or applying cold compresses.

Once you arrive, our dental team will quickly evaluate your issue. We will also address any pain and begin the necessary emergency treatment.

Following Your Emergency Dentistry

If needed, we will schedule a follow-up visit to check your condition and determine if further treatment would be best.

Depending on your emergency and the treatment provided, you may also be given aftercare instructions for ideal healing.

FAQ

  • What should I do if I knock out a tooth? If you knock out a tooth, act quickly: Pick up the tooth by the crown (top), not the root.
  • Rinse it gently with water—don’t scrub or remove tissue.
  • Try to place it back in the socket if possible.
  • If that’s not possible, store it in milk or saliva (or inside your cheek).
  • Call us immediately so we can provide further instructions and get you into our office.
  • Can I take painkillers before coming to your office? Yes, if you are experiencing pain, taking over-the-counter relievers like acetaminophen or ibuprofen is fine. But you should avoid aspirin, as it may increase bleeding.
  • Are all same-day visits considered emergencies? No, not all same-day appointments are considered emergency dentistry services. However, we prioritize cases based on the severity of the issue and possible issues that can result from delayed treatment.
