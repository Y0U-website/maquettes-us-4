Accessibility Statement

  • Use the keyboard to navigate screens
  • Increase text size
  • Magnify your screen
  • Change background and text colors
  • Make your mouse pointer more visible (Windows only)

Alternate Input Methods

If you need alternatives to traditional input methods, such as a mouse and keyboard, some speech recognition applications may help you navigate the web. Dragon Naturally Speaking is one such option. This software may assist with navigating a website with voice controls.

Contact Us

If the recommendations above do not meet your needs, we invite you to contact us for assistance.
