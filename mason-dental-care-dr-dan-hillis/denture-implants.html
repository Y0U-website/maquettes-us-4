What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are a modern, advanced solution for replacing multiple missing teeth. Instead of resting on the gums like traditional dentures, these dentures are securely attached to dental implants—small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone.

The implants act as artificial tooth roots, providing a stable foundation for the dentures. Depending on your needs, implant-supported dentures can be either removable or fixed (permanent). Both options offer significant advantages over traditional dentures, including improved comfort, functionality, and aesthetics.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Patients love implant-supported dentures for a variety of reasons:

No more slipping, clicking, or discomfort. Implant-supported dentures stay securely in place.

Enjoy eating, speaking, and smiling with confidence.

Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.

With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last for decades.

Custom-crafted dentures look just like natural teeth.

The Implant-Supported Denture Process

At Mason Dental Care, we’ve streamlined the process of getting implant-supported dentures to make it as smooth and stress-free as possible:

Consultation and Diagnosis

During your initial visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health, take digital images, and discuss your goals. If implant-supported dentures are the right solution for you, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement

Using our custom surgical guide, we’ll place the titanium implant posts into your jawbone with precision. These implants will serve as the foundation for your dentures.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implants will fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a secure and permanent bond.

Denture Fabrication and Placement

Once the implants have fully integrated, we’ll craft and place your custom dentures. Whether you choose removable or fixed dentures, your new teeth will look and feel completely natural.

Removable vs. Fixed Implant-Supported Dentures

When choosing implant-supported dentures, you have two main options:

Which option is right for you? Our team will help you decide based on your lifestyle, preferences, and oral health needs.

  • Removable Implant-Supported Dentures: These snap onto the implants and can be taken out for cleaning. They offer the convenience of traditional dentures with the added stability of implants.
  • Fixed Implant-Supported Dentures: These are permanently attached to the implants and cannot be removed without a dentist’s assistance. They feel and function like natural teeth, offering the ultimate in stability and convenience.
