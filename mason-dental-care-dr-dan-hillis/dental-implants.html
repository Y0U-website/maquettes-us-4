Our Approach to Dental Implants

Unlike many practices that refer patients to multiple specialists for different stages of the implant process, Mason Dental Care performs the entire dental implant procedure in-house. From diagnosis and surgical placement to crafting and placing your custom restoration, we handle every step right here in our office. This means you’ll enjoy a seamless, efficient experience with a team you know and trust.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants are widely regarded as the gold standard for tooth replacement. Here’s why:

Implants function just like natural teeth, allowing you to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Unlike other tooth replacement options, implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.

With custom-crafted restorations, dental implants look and feel like your natural teeth.

Caring for implants is as simple as brushing, flossing, and regular dental checkups.

Implants are designed to last for decades with proper care, making them a long-term investment in your oral health.

The Dental Implant Process

At Mason Dental Care, we’ve streamlined the dental implant process to make it as smooth and stress-free as possible:

Consultation and Diagnosis

During your initial visit, we’ll evaluate your oral health, take digital images, and discuss your goals. If implants are the right solution for you, we’ll create a personalized treatment plan.

Surgical Placement

Using our custom surgical guide, we’ll place the titanium implant post into your jawbone with precision. This post acts as a synthetic tooth root, providing a stable foundation for your restoration.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a secure and permanent bond.

Restoration Placement

Once the implant has fully integrated, we’ll craft and place your custom restoration. Whether you need a single crown, a bridge, or implant-supported dentures, your new teeth will look and feel completely natural.
