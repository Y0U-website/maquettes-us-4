Dr. Dan Hillis

Dr. Dan Hillis has been practicing advanced dentistry, including restorative, surgical, and cosmetic care since 2001. He is regarded as one of the most accomplished dentists in the Cincinnati area.

He holds both a B.S. and a Doctorate of Dental Medicine from the University of Kentucky and is certified in both Intravenous and Conscious Sedation. His advanced techniques in reconstruction attract patients from around the country and have resulted in a network of referring specialists.

Certifications and Publications

Dr Hillis is as passionate about his personal pursuits as he is about his professional work. Dr Hillis is an avid outdoorsman exploring all outdoor pursuits including skiing, mountain biking, mountaineering, rock and ice climbing and hunting. He has taken his clinical skills to the mountains of Mexico in the Sierra Madre Occidental. Dr Hillis also has a passion for the sport of lacrosse, creating a youth and middle school lacrosse program for a local school. He has coached in that program for 8 years and now works with individual lacrosse athletes wanting to play at the college level.
