The practice

At your first appointment, we will spend time understanding your oral history. Dr. Hillis will perform a TMJ screening, oral cancer exam, a bite analysis, and review your mouth including the teeth, gums, and bone.

The hygiene appointment is customized to your level of oral health, providing at-home hygiene protocols and product recommendations. This is followed by a consultation that reviews any additional treatment recommendations.

We believe in empowering our clients to make decisions for their oral health without restrictions. Performing necessary dentistry often reduces emergencies and gives you the smile you want, and the oral health you deserve!
