Robotic precision with a personal touch

Dental implants are small titanium posts that replace missing tooth roots and support crowns, bridges, or full arches that look and work like natural teeth. If missing teeth have changed the way you eat, speak, or smile, you have options close to home. We plan and place Santa Rosa dental implants right here at our office, without sending you somewhere else for surgery.

Dr. Michael Rice pairs advanced implantology training with a patient-first approach, using the Yomi robotic system and CBCT 3D imaging to place implants accurately and keep recovery time short. The first step is a free one-hour consultation at our Santa Rosa office.

Implant options for every smile

Single-tooth implants

When one tooth is missing or can’t be saved, a single implant topped with a custom crown fills the gap without touching the healthy teeth beside it. If a failing tooth needs to come out first, we handle extractions and bone grafting here as well, so your care stays under one roof.

All-on-4 and full-arch implants

For patients missing most or all of their teeth, a full arch of replacement teeth can be secured on as few as four implants. These cases are often part of a larger full mouth reconstruction plan, and we design each one around your bite, your bone, and your features.

Same-day implants

How does robotic-guided implant placement work?

Robotic-guided implant placement pairs the Yomi robotic system with CBCT 3D imaging to plan your implant in advance and guide it into position with precision a freehand approach can’t match. The CBCT scan gives Dr. Rice a detailed 3D view of your teeth, jaw, and nerves, and the Yomi system keeps the surgical plan steady during placement. Smaller incisions and faster healing follow from that accuracy, and the implant lands where the plan says it should.

Dr. Michael Rice completed advanced implantology training through Loma Linda University and has earned a Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) along with an Associate Fellowship with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID). Robotics and 3D imaging are part of the same dental technology we use across the practice.

FAQs

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post placed in the jaw where a tooth root used to be. Once it heals, it anchors a crown or bridge , or a full set of replacement teeth, that looks and functions like the tooth you lost.

Do you place implants in your office?

Yes, every implant is placed on-site at the Smile Rejuvenation Implant Center inside Santa Rosa Dental. Dr. Michael Rice leads each case with robotic guidance and 3D imaging, so you never need a referral to an outside surgeon.

Are your doctors specially trained in dental implants?

Dr. Rice completed advanced implantology training through Loma Linda University and holds a Fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) as well as an Associate Fellowship with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID). His training also covers CEREC digital dentistry, CBCT 3D imaging, and laser dentistry.

How does robotic technology improve implant placement?

The Yomi® robotic guidance system holds the surgical plan steady while Dr. Rice places your implant, which helps ensure the post sits precisely where it was planned. The approach also keeps surgery less invasive than freehand placement.

Am I a candidate for Santa Rosa dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates, as long as their gums are healthy and there’s enough bone to anchor the implant. If bone loss is a concern, bone grafting can rebuild a stable foundation first. The 3D imaging we take as part of your consultation process gives us a clear answer, and it’s the starting point for the dental implants Santa Rosa residents ask us about most.

How long does the implant process take?

Plan on a few months from placement to final restoration. After placement, the implant needs time to fuse with your jawbone before the permanent crown, bridge, or arch is attached. The exact timeline depends on your healing and on whether grafting comes first, and we map it out step by step at your consultation.

What is recovery like after implant placement?

Because robotic guidance allows smaller incisions, recovery tends to be smoother and faster than with traditional freehand surgery. We go over aftercare with you before you leave, so you know what to expect while the implant heals.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Some dental plans contribute toward implant treatment and others don’t, so the honest answer depends on your coverage. We help patients file out-of-network claims, and our Healthy Smile Plan offers exclusive savings on most treatments for those without insurance.

Do you charge for implant consultations?

No. Your first implant visit is a full hour with Dr. Rice and our implant coordinator, free of charge. You’ll leave with a clear picture of your options, whatever you decide.

Do you offer full-arch or All-on-4 implants?

Yes. Full-arch and All-on-4 cases are a regular part of our implant work, alongside single-tooth and same-day implants. We’ll walk through which approach makes sense for you at that first visit.

Are dental implants permanent?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades or even a lifetime. The post fuses securely with your jawbone, which makes implants one of the most reliable and long-lasting tooth replacement options available.
