Financing options that fit you

We believe high-quality care should be accessible to all, regardless of their insurance status.

At Santa Rosa Dental, we’ve chosen not to join insurance networks, so we can focus on delivering the highest quality of dentistry. If you are insured, our team will file claims on your behalf and help you make the most of your benefits.

Patients without insurance can join one of our in-house membership plans for preventive care and savings on other treatments. For added flexibility, we partner with CareCredit and LendingClub to offer financing options that fit different budgets.
