What are the Benefits of CEREC Same-Day Crowns?

You can go into a dentist’s office and walk out with your new CEREC crown the same day, rather than waiting up to two weeks. The dentist in Salem, OR, will use computer-aided design (CAD)/computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) technology to capture digital images of your tooth, design a crown, and then fabricate the crown in the office for placement on the same day.

Your friends may never notice that you have a crown on your tooth. A CEREC crown looks more natural and closely resembles the surrounding teeth because it does not have a metal core. The lack of a black core to break light reflection improves the aesthetic look.

The 3D scanner is extremely precise, and the resulting scan closely resembles your mouth in real life, ensuring that the crown created using the technology will fit properly. Same-day crowns are made of porcelain, a high-quality dental material. It is a strong material that, once bonded, provides long-term stability.
