A Permanent Solution for Missing Teeth

Missing teeth affect more than appearance. They can change the way a person chews, speaks, and smiles. Dental implants offer a permanent, stable replacement that looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth because the implant integrates directly with the jawbone.

Dr. Patricia Chao provides dental implant consultations and restorations at Chao Dental Studio in Santa Clara, helping patients regain confidence and full oral function.

How Dental Implants Work

The Implant Post

A dental implant consists of a small titanium post that is placed into the jawbone. Titanium is used because the body accepts it as a natural material, allowing the bone to fuse around the post in a process called osseointegration. This fusion creates a stable foundation for the replacement tooth.

The Abutment and Crown

Once the implant post has fully integrated with the jawbone, an abutment is attached to the top of the post. A custom-made dental crown is then secured to the abutment. The crown is designed to match the color, shape, and size of the surrounding teeth, creating a seamless result.

Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

Preserve Jawbone Health

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate because it no longer receives stimulation from the tooth root. Dental implants prevent this bone loss because they replace the root and continue to stimulate the bone. This preservation of bone structure helps maintain facial contours over time.

Long-Term Durability

Dental implants are designed to last for decades with proper care. Unlike bridges or dentures that may need replacement every 5 to 15 years, implants provide a long-term solution that does not require adhesives, clasps, or modification of adjacent teeth.

Natural Look and Feel

Patients often report that dental implants feel indistinguishable from their natural teeth because the crown is custom-crafted and the implant is anchored securely in the jaw. There is no slipping, no clicking, and no dietary restrictions.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults with good general health are candidates for dental implants. Dr. Chao evaluates each patient individually, assessing jawbone density, gum health, and overall medical history. Patients who have experienced bone loss may still qualify because bone grafting procedures can rebuild the foundation needed for implant placement.

Factors that may affect candidacy include uncontrolled diabetes, heavy smoking, and certain medications that affect bone healing. A thorough consultation at Chao Dental Studio will determine the best approach for each patient.

The Dental Implant Process at Chao Dental Studio

Initial Consultation

The process begins with a comprehensive examination that includes digital imaging and a review of dental and medical history. Dr. Chao discusses the findings, explains the available options, and develops a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement and Healing

The implant post is placed during a minimally invasive procedure. Most patients report less discomfort than expected because modern techniques and local anesthesia ensure a comfortable experience. The healing period typically lasts three to six months as the bone integrates with the implant.

Crown Placement

After healing is complete, the custom crown is fabricated and attached to the implant. Dr. Chao verifies the bite, appearance, and fit before finalizing the restoration. The result is a tooth that blends naturally with the rest of the smile.

Caring for Dental Implants

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth. Brushing twice daily, flossing, and attending regular dental checkups at Chao Dental Studio are essential for maintaining the health of the implant and surrounding tissues. With proper maintenance, implants can last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent solution. The implant post can last a lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits. The crown may need replacement after 15 to 20 years due to normal wear.

Is the implant procedure painful?

Most patients report that the procedure is more comfortable than expected because local anesthesia is used throughout. Post-procedure discomfort is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on the number of implants, the need for bone grafting, and the type of restoration. Chao Dental Studio provides detailed cost estimates during the consultation and offers financing through CareCredit and the in-house membership plan.

Can dental implants replace multiple teeth?

Dental implants can replace a single tooth, several teeth, or an entire arch. Implant-supported bridges and dentures provide stable, permanent alternatives to traditional removable prosthetics.
