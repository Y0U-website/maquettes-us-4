A Career Dedicated to Gentle, Personalized Care

Dr. Patricia Chao earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The University of Texas School of Dentistry. After beginning her career in Chicago, she relocated to Santa Clara and established Chao Dental Studio, where she has served the community for over 10 years.

Dr. Chao believes that every patient deserves honest, unhurried dental care. She takes time to listen, explain findings in plain language, and collaborate on a treatment plan that respects both clinical needs and personal preferences. This philosophy has earned the trust of generations of families throughout the Santa Clara Valley.

As a Gold Invisalign Provider, certified BioClear Doctor, and chair of the AACA San Jose Clear Aligners Study Club, Dr. Chao remains at the forefront of orthodontic and restorative techniques. Her practice also features CEREC same-day crown technology, laser dentistry, and digital imaging, allowing patients to benefit from the latest advances in dental science.

Dentistry That Puts Patients First

Listen First Every appointment starts with a conversation because understanding a patient’s concerns and goals is the foundation of good care.

Educate Clearly Patients make the best decisions when they understand their options. Dr. Chao explains every finding and recommendation in plain language.

Treat Honestly Only necessary treatments are recommended. There is no corporate pressure, no upselling, and no rushing. The patient is always in control.
