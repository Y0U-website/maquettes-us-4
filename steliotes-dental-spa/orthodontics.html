Advantages of Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are a series of clear, lightweight plastic trays that correct misaligned teeth in patients with permanent dentition. With continuous gentle forces, clear aligners move teeth gradually to bring them into alignment. The trays are custom-made for each patient based on the mold of his or her teeth.
