National Health Center Week

National Health Center Week (NHCW) is an annual nationwide celebration designed to raise awareness about the critical role that Community Health Centers like Venice Family Clinic play in providing comprehensive, innovative and cost-effective care to people in underserved communities.

Each year we host a series of free and family-friendly events to reach people who need it most, including neighbors who are living unsheltered.
