The practice

At Venice Family Clinic, we believe everyone deserves quality health care that goes beyond just treating symptoms of illness. We go beyond traditional medical care and see each person as a whole so we can support their overall well-being. Our comprehensive care addresses not just physical health, but also the mental and social aspects that empower patients to thrive.

Venice Family Clinic values the diversity of the community and welcomes people from all backgrounds. Everyone is treated with respect and dignity, regardless of their ability to pay.

We do not discriminate based on age, race, national origin, disability, gender, housing status, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

We are dedicated to keeping patient information safe and private. All personal and health information is protected in compliance with HIPAA laws.
