A periodontist is a dentist who specializes in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of periodontal disease (a chronic inflammatory disease that affects the gums and bone supporting the teeth also known as gum disease), and in the placement of dental implants. Periodontists receive extensive training in both of these areas and more, including additional years of education beyond dental school.

While some general dentists treat less serious periodontal disease, many will refer their patients to periodontists for the more challenging periodontal cases. Similarly, while some general dentists perform dental implant procedures, many will refer their patients to periodontists for the more challenging implant cases as well.

In addition to handling challenging periodontal and implant cases, periodontists also provide a variety of other specialized services. These include scaling and root planing, oral inflammation treatment, and cosmetic periodontal procedures like tissue grafts.

Single Tooth Dental Implants – If you’re missing a single tooth, one dental implant can replace it.

Multiple Tooth Dental Implants – If you’re missing several teeth, they can be replaced by multiple dental implants.
