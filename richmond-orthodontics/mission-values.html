About Our Practice

Richmond Orthodontics opened in December 2012. After months of planning and construction, we opened a new office on National Road West. This office provides a convenient location for patients throughout Wayne and surrounding counties.

Our Mission The mission of Richmond Orthodontics is to give our patients the beautiful, happy, and healthy smile they deserve. At Richmond Orthodontics, your smile is our specialty.

To accomplish our mission, we will provide excellent orthodontic treatment, using the latest knowledge and technologies in a fun, cheerful, and comfortable environment. We believe in achieving your treatment goal in the fastest, least invasive way possible. We are committed to excellence.
