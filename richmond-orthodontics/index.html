Personalized orthodontics for a smile that is healthy confident beautiful

At Richmond Orthodontics, we are passionate about helping children, teens, and adults create a smile that will last a lifetime .

The practice

Treatment aims to modify jaw growth, align teeth for improved dental health, and optimize bite to reduce wear and maintain jaw joint health. During your consultation appointment, you’ll go through three easy steps to get your personalized plan.

The first thing we do is take photos of your teeth and an x-ray.

We examine your teeth and talk to you about any concerns you may have.

We put together a treatment plan based on your needs and discuss it with you.

Being an orthodontist is rewarding because I have the chance to maintain a relationship with my patients while solving a complex problem, and the end result is a self-confident smile.

Getting patients involved in their treatment is my goal. I enjoy teaching patients about their treatment and how orthodontics works. As my patients know… I’ll talk about teeth all day long if they let me!
