About Our Pediatric Dentists

A Place for Smiles

Expert Pediatric Dental Care in Lafayette

At Pediatric Dental Specialists of Lafayette, we believe that great dental care starts with education, prevention, and a fun, stress-free experience. Our mission is to create a welcoming environment where children feel safe and excited about their oral health. With a strong emphasis on preventive care, gentle techniques, and a commitment to individualized treatment, we help every child build the foundation for a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist Get To Know Dr. Seth DeJean Dr. Seth DeJean is a native of Opelousas, Louisiana. He brings not only top-tier training but also a deep-rooted love for his community and a passion for helping children thrive.

Before finding his passion in pediatric dentistry, Dr. DeJean earned a degree in Civil Engineering from LSU. His path took a turn toward healthcare, and he returned to school, where he earned a doctorate of dental surgery from LSU School of Dentistry. He then completed a prestigious Pediatric Dentistry Residency earning dual certificates from the Harvard School of Dental Medicine and Boston Children’s Hospital – the top pediatric hospital in the nation.

Dr. DeJean remains actively involved in organized dentistry. He is a proud member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Louisiana Dental Association (LDA), Southwestern Society of Pediatric Dentistry (SWPD), and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD). He also currently serves as the President of the Louisiana Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. DeJean is a proud father of four, and enjoys an active lifestyle. He loves to exercise, practice jiu-jitsu, and travel with his family, always looking for new adventures and ways to stay connected.

Dr. DeJean combines Louisiana warmth with world-class training to create a comfortable, fun, and compassionate dental experience for kids and families.
