Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I park at the Atascocita office?

We’re located inside the Shops at Breakwater off W. Lake Houston Parkway. There’s plenty of free parking available directly in front of the office for your convenience.

What should I expect at my first appointment?

When you arrive, you’ll be warmly greeted by our reception team. If you haven’t completed your paperwork online, we’ll help you take care of it and get checked in quickly (no long waits!). During your visit, we’ll review your health history with you, take digital x-rays and a full iTero® scan, complete your cleaning (if a deep cleaning isn’t needed), and perform a comprehensive exam—including oral cancer screening, airway assessment, sleep questionnaire, and dental evaluation.

You’ll have plenty of time to ask questions and talk with your doctor—no rush, no pressure. Before you leave, you’ll receive a personalized treatment plan and we’ll even start your treatment on the same day, when possible.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major dental PPOs, including Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, MetLife, and United Healthcare. If you’re unsure about your coverage, our Atascocita care coordinators are happy to check your benefits and walk you through next steps.

Is your Atascocita office kid-friendly?

Absolutely! We welcome patients of all ages, and our team is especially skilled at making dental visits positive and low-stress for children.

If you’re bringing a little one along, feel free to call ahead—our team is happy to help make your visit as smooth as possible.

What makes airway-focused dentistry different?

Airway-focused dentistry looks at the bigger picture—how breathing, jaw development, and sleep health are deeply connected to dental health. We treat beyond the symptoms, helping to build healthier foundations for breathing, sleeping, and living well.
