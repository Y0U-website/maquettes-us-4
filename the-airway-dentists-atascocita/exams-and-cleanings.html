What Happens During Your Visit

Health & Sleep Screening

We begin by reviewing your health history and asking about symptoms like snoring, energy levels, and headaches. These patterns can sometimes be connected to oral health, breathing, or sleep.

Low-Radiation 3D Imaging

When appropriate, we use CBCT imaging to get a more complete view of the teeth, jaws, and airway. This can help us spot issues that may not be as clear on traditional 2D X-rays.

Gentle Hygiene Care

Your hygienist performs a thorough cleaning to remove plaque and tartar. We also talk through preventive product options, including fluoride-free alternatives when that fits your preferences.

Clear Next Steps

If we find something that needs attention, we explain what we’re seeing, what it may mean, and what options make sense moving forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do you ask about my sleep during a dental exam?

Because the shape of your mouth determines how well you sleep. If your jaw is too narrow or your tongue falls back, your airway collapses. As Airway Dentists, we are often the first healthcare providers to spot the physical signs of Sleep Apnea.

How often should I come in?

For most healthy patients, we recommend a cleaning and exam every 6 months. However, if you are undergoing airway treatment or managing gum disease, we may suggest more frequent visits to monitor your progress.

Do you see children for regular cleanings?

Absolutely. We love seeing kids! Our pediatric visits are designed to be fun and educational. We teach them healthy habits early and screen their growth at every single visit to ensure they are on the right track.
