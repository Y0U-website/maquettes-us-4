Frequently Asked Questions

Do you do metal braces?

No. We are a wire-free practice. We believe modern clear aligners (Invisalign®) and custom appliances (expanders) can achieve superior results without the pain, hygiene issues, and “metal mouth” look of traditional braces.

Can adults fix their teeth later in life?

Absolutely. It is never too late. In fact, many of our airway patients are adults who are finally fixing bite issues that have caused them headaches or sleep apnea for years.

How long does treatment usually take?

It varies by case, but most Invisalign® cases take between 6 and 18 months. Because we use digital planning, we can often achieve results faster than traditional methods.

I was previously told I wasn t a candidate for Invisalign®. Is that true?

In most cases, no. Invisalign® can do far more than many people realize. In the right hands, clear aligners can correct complex bite issues, crowding, spacing, and even cases often labeled as “braces only.” As Invisalign® Diamond Providers with experience planning over 10,000 cases, we routinely treat patients who were told they needed metal braces. The key isn’t the tool — it’s the expertise behind the treatment plan.
