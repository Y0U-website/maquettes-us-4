0 Dedicated to improving social and environmental impact. We’ve met the highest verified levels of social and environmental performance. That’s a pretty big deal for our small but forward thinking practice. Certified B Corporations are part of a growing movement of people using business as a force for good. Across a variety of industries and around the world, these companies are putting people and the planet before profits and redefining what it means to be successful in business.

This diverse community is constantly evolving and often surprising. Though B Corp Certification originated in the U.S., there are now more B Corps operating outside the U.S. And while many people are most familiar with B Corps who sell consumer product goods, the majority of the community is actually made up of B2B companies.

We strive to improve the lives of our patients and the world around us, which is why we have committed to donating a portion our revenue each year to charitable partners.
