0 Let us make you smile. Dr. Rebekah Browder Dr. Browder completed her Bachelors and Doctorate at New York University, with Honors programs in both Aesthetic and Public Health Dentistry. She has a passion for continuing education, is a member of the renowned Spear Study Club and is pursuing her Mastership from the Academy of General Dentistry, something accomplished by less than 2 % of General Dentists.

After receiving her Doctorate, Dr. Browder completed the highly regarded General Practice Residency Program at New York Methodist Hospital, pursuing advanced training in oral surgery, pedodontics and prosthodontics. Dr. Browder taught at USC Ostrow School of Dentistry in Los Angeles, while providing pediatric care as part of a school-based public health program. She was also a member of the California State Dental Association’s Ethics Committee.
