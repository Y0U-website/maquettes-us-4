The practice

Innovative and highly skilled approaches are coupled with care and consideration to treat a variety of conditions from gum disease to dental implants. Periodontal care is our passion, and your smile is our promise.

We believe in the latest technology to help restore your confident smile. We are constantly perfecting minimally-invasive techniques to deliver more precise and targeted treatments to our patients.

In most cases, we are the only practice in the state to offer cutting edge technology that helps ensure a gentle approach to most periodontal issues and a faster recovery time.

Dr. Randal Comeaux is among the top 100 periodontists in the United States, and he is the only periodontist in Louisiana trained to combine operating microscopes and periodontal micro-endoscopes during treatment.

Dr. Randal Comeaux is among the top 100 periodontists in the United States, and he is the only periodontist in Louisiana trained to combine operating microscopes and periodontal micro-endoscopes during treatment.

He graduated from Louisiana State University in 1974 and received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from LSU School of Dentistry in 1978. He completed a General Practice Residency at Charity Hospital, New Orleans and LSU School of Dentistry, then went on a Periodontics Residency at the University of Iowa. Shortly after he began private practice in Lafayette, Louisiana in the Summer of 1982.

Dr. Comeaux is from Mamou, Louisiana and is active in the community.
