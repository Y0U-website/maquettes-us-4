Overbite

Overbite is a vertical misalignment of teeth that occurs when your upper teeth overlap your lower teeth more than they should.

Depending on your case’s severity, Invisalign® generally works for most overbite cases.

During your initial consultation, Dr. Matthew Biermann will assess your overbite to determine whether it is too severe for Invisalign®. We always try to develop a treatment plan that aligns with our patients’ preferences, but when that is not possible, we strive to find solutions that will best address their treatment needs, such as braces.

Tooth Shape or Position

As we age, our teeth tend to move around and reposition themselves. Whether you’re an adult or teenager who has previously had braces but find that only your top teeth have become mispositioned over the years, Invisalign® is an excellent option for crooked, overlapped, or rotated teeth.

However, if your smile is impacted by teeth that are pegged, sharp, rotated, or too short, you may need additional treatment beyond Invisalign®. We would love to have you in for a consultation to evaluate your smile and create a personalized treatment plan that fulfills your goals promptly and effectively.

Answering the Most Frequently Asked Questions About Invisalign® for Top Teeth Only Can I Get Invisalign® for Just My Top Teeth? Yes! For mild to moderate cases, Invisalign® can correct top teeth issues just as well as braces.

How Long Does Invisalign® Take for Top Teeth?

The length of your Invisalign® treatment may depend on the complexity of your case. Generally, it may take three to 12 months for your smile to straighten.

Do You Have To Straighten the Entire Top Row of Teeth?

One of the greatest features of Invisalign® is its ability to provide customizable treatment. Clear aligners are designed to correct the whole row of teeth or the just alignment of several teeth, a particular zone, or even a single crooked tooth. If you’re someone with a mostly straight smile, except for a few teeth on your top row, then it’s completely possible to have aligners created that will only straighten those few teeth.
