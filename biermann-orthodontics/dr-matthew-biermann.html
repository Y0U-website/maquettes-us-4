About Dr. Biermann &

Dr. Matthew Biermann

I love being able to improve peoples’ lives by giving them a nicer smile, greater confidence, and a healthier mouth. Each person I meet is as unique as the smile we create for them, and I find great satisfaction in building meaningful relationships that last, even after treatment is completed. My team and I enjoy our work and appreciate the opportunity to meet and help people. We treat all our patients like we would treat a member of our own family.

Working with patients to improve their smile and bite is extremely rewarding, especially when we see them become more confident and happy. I enjoy the whole process involved with braces, but love when we finally get to remove the braces and reveal the beautiful results of all the work we and our patients have done.

Education Continuing Education

Bachelor of Science in Biology – University of Oregon Doctorate of Dental Medicine – Oregon Health Sciences University School of Dentistry Master of Science in Orthodontics – Marquette University School of Dentistry

I participate in numerous hours of continuing education every year to stay at the cutting edge of orthodontics, and to ensure that we’re using the most efficient, evidence-based treatment modalities and technology. I want to be sure our patients get great results which will be stable and completed in the least amount of time possible.

Outside the Office

I am a proud third-generation native of Portland, OR, and come from a family of dental health professionals. My father, Michael Biermann, is a pediatric dentist in north Portland, and my uncle, Bruce Biermann, is a general dentist in Sandy, OR.

When I’m not in the office, I can most often be found playing racquetball, running, hiking, or golfing. I enjoy reading, cooking, good wine, and eating at any of the many great restaurants Portland has to offer.
