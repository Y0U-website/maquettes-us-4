The Dental Implant Process

The dental implant procedure is typically completed in several steps over a few months. Here’s what you can expect:

Our team ensures a comfortable experience throughout each step, providing detailed aftercare instructions to promote a speedy recovery.

  • Consultation : During your initial consultation, our team will evaluate your oral health, review X-rays, and discuss whether dental implants are the right choice for you.
  • Implant Placement : The titanium implant is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. This implant will act as a stable foundation for your new tooth.
  • Healing Period : Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. During this time, a temporary crown may be placed for aesthetics.
  • Crown Placement : Once the implant has successfully integrated with the bone, a custom dental crown is attached to complete the restoration.
