What to Expect During Treatment

Throughout the process, we offer both oral sedation and nitrous oxide to ensure your comfort during every phase of treatment.

  • Initial Consultation and Planning: Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam, including digital imaging and iTero digital impressions for accurate 3D modeling. We may also use 3D printing to create surgical guides for optimal implant placement.
  • Preparatory Procedures: Depending on your jawbone density, you may need procedures such as bone grafting or oral surgery prior to implant placement.
  • Implant Surgery: Four to six dental implants are placed per arch. In many cases, a temporary prosthesis is attached on the same day so you can leave our office with a full, functional smile.
  • Healing and Osseointegration: Over the next few months, your implants integrate with the bone. Follow-ups and Digital X-rays are performed to monitor healing.
  • Final Restoration: Once integration is complete, your permanent full arch prosthesis is placed. It will look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

FAQs About Full Arch Implants

  • How long do Full Arch Implants last?: The implants themselves can last a lifetime with proper care. The attached prosthesis typically lasts 10–15 years and can be replaced when needed.
  • Are Full Arch Implants better than dentures?: Yes. Implants are fixed, do not shift or slip, and help maintain jawbone health, unlike removable dentures which can cause bone loss over time.
  • Is the procedure painful?: The surgery is performed under local anesthesia and sedation, so discomfort is minimal. Most patients report manageable soreness post-procedure, similar to a tooth extraction.
  • What if I don’t have enough bone for implants?: We offer bone grafting to rebuild jaw structure and make implants possible even for those with bone loss.
  • Do you offer financing options?: Yes. We accept various payment methods and offer financing options to help make your implant treatment more affordable. Online payments are also available through our Patient Info page.
