  • Improved Oral Health: Implant-supported dentures help preserve the jawbone’s integrity by stimulating bone growth. This helps to prevent bone loss, which can lead to further dental problems down the line.
  • Improved Aesthetics: Implant-supported dentures are designed to look and feel like natural teeth, providing a more aesthetic and natural-looking smile.

The Implant Supported Denture Process

At Precision Dental Care And Sleep Solutions , we work closely with our patients to ensure a smooth and stress-free implant-supported denture process. The process typically involves the following steps:

Step 1: Initial Consultation During the initial consultation, one of our experienced dentists will assess the patient’s oral health and determine if implant-supported dentures are a suitable treatment option.

Step 2: Dental Implant Placement The next step involves placing the dental implants into the jawbone. This is typically done using local anesthesia to ensure the patient is comfortable throughout the procedure.

Step 3: Healing Period After the dental implants are placed, there is a healing period of several months during which the implants fuse with the jawbone in a process known as osseointegration.

Step 4: Denture Placement Once the dental implants have fully fused with the jawbone, the dentures can be attached to the implants using either the bar-retained or ball-retained method.
