Dental Implants in Imperial, MO

  • Improved Appearance: Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth, restoring the appearance of your smile and boosting your confidence.
  • Improved Comfort: Unlike dentures, which can slip and cause discomfort, dental implants are permanently anchored into the jawbone, providing a stable and comfortable foundation for replacement teeth.
  • Improved Speech: Missing teeth can make speaking difficult, but dental implants can restore your ability to communicate confidently.
  • Improved Function: Dental implants function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat your favorite foods without restrictions.

Our Dental Implant Services

At Precision Dental Care And Sleep Solutions, we offer a range of dental implant services to meet the needs of our patients. Our services include:

  • Single Tooth Implants: We can replace a missing tooth with a dental implant and a custom-made replacement tooth.
  • Multiple Tooth Implants: We can replace several missing teeth with various dental implants and a custom-made bridge.
  • Full Mouth Implants: We can replace all your missing teeth with a complete set of dental implants and a custom-made denture.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: We can provide dentures anchored to dental implants for a more stable and comfortable fit.
  • Implant Restorations: If you already have dental implants but need them restored, we can provide implant restoration services to ensure they function correctly.
