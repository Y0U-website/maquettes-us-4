Braces And Orthodontic Care Is More Than Just Cosmetics And A Straight Smile

When It Comes To Braces & Orthodontics, We Specialize In The Following:

Traditional Braces

At Precision Dental Care and Sleep Solutions, we offer traditional braces to give you a beautiful smile. Traditional braces are composed of metal brackets glued onto the teeth and connected with an archwire, which your dentist adjusts every two weeks. These brackets push and pull your teeth into proper alignment over time to create a balanced bite in both the front and back of your mouth. We understand that having metal on your teeth can be uncomfortable or embarrassing for some patients; therefore, our office provides many options, such as different colored elastics, to make it more fun!

Clear Braces

Our dental office offers clear braces for those who don’t want their orthodontic treatment visible. Clear braces use ceramic materials instead of traditional metals for brackets, giving them an almost invisible appearance when worn. They work just like regular braces but without drawing attention from others due to their subtle coloring scheme. Our staff will help guide you through each step of the process so that you get results quickly and easily in minimal time frames.

Invisalign

Self-Ligating Braces

At Precision Dental Care, we utilize self-ligating braces as well, which helps reduce patient discomfort as there aren’t any rubber bands needing tightness adjustments during every visit meaning faster results too! In addition, self-ligating brace technology uses sliding door mechanisms built inside each bracket. This allows us here at PDC to reduce friction between wires & brackets, creating more efficient movement than ever before – perfect for complex cases requiring more precise movements & optimal force distribution during the treatment duration.

Lingual Braces

At Precision Dental Care and Sleep Solutions, we offer lingual braces to our patients looking for an orthodontic treatment that is less visible than traditional options. Lingual braces differ from other braces in that they are placed on the inside of the teeth and not on the outside. This unique placement makes them virtually invisible compared to metal or ceramic brackets and wires. In addition, the technology used in lingual braces has significantly improved, making them more comfortable and effective.
