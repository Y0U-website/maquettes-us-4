Dr. Sandeep Kumar and Dr. Jyoti Sagar are pleased to present our wonderful dental team! Sandeep Kumar Dentist

Jyoti Sagar Dentist

Dr Jyoti Sagar graduated from Boston University in 2005. She worked in Upper Michigan for two year before moving to Greater Cleveland area in 2006. Working as dentist for more than 20 yrs, she loves all aspects of dentistry. Dr Sagar is certified from AAFE for Botox and Fillers, and also for INVISALIGN. Dr Sagar loves to spend time with her family, reading books, painting, hiking in her free time.
