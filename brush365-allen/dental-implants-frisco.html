More than cosmetic dentistry—real solutions for real smiles.

Dental implants are designed to integrate with your jawbone and support a custom-crafted restoration. The result is a tooth replacement that looks natural and functions reliably in everyday life.

Because implants replace the root of the missing tooth, they help prevent bone resorption and reduce stress on neighboring teeth.

Protecting Your Dental Implants: Long-Term Care Guidelines Dental implants are designed to be durable and long-lasting, but their success depends heavily on daily care and professional monitoring. Just like natural teeth, implants rely on healthy gums and consistent oral hygiene to remain stable over time.

Even though implants cannot decay like natural teeth, the surrounding gum tissue can still develop inflammation or infection if plaque accumulates. Protecting your implants means protecting the foundation around them.

Brush and floss daily

Brush at least twice a day using a soft-bristled toothbrush to remove plaque along the gumline. Floss carefully around the implant to prevent bacteria buildup that can lead to peri-implant inflammation.

Choose the right products

Use non-abrasive toothpaste to protect the restoration surface. Interdental brushes or water flossers can help clean hard-to-reach areas, especially around implant-supported bridges.

Schedule regular dental checkups

Routine visits allow our Frisco dental team to evaluate implant stability, assess gum health, and perform professional cleanings. Early detection of minor issues can prevent more serious complications.

Avoid hard or sticky foods

Although dental implants are strong, biting into extremely hard objects such as ice or hard candy can create unnecessary stress on restorations and surrounding teeth.

Quit smoking

Smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure and slows healing. Patients who avoid tobacco tend to experience better long-term outcomes.

With consistent home care and routine professional maintenance, your dental implants in Frisco can remain secure, functional, and aesthetically pleasing for many years.
