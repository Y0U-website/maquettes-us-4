Dental Implants Brentwood, CA

  • Implant surgery: Before this outpatient surgery, we precisely numb your gums with a local anesthetic to minimize discomfort. Once comfortable, we use fully guided techniques to place your implant precisely in the bone for a successful outcome.
  • Healing: Over the next 4-6 months, we’ll leave your implant to heal and integrate with the jawbone through osseointegration. After this process, the implant becomes part of your dentition to give you a lifetime of function and aesthetics.
  • Abutment placement: The dentist will make a small incision on the gums to expose the implant. Then, we’ll attach an abutment that protrudes through the gum line to link the implant to the dental prosthetic.
  • Tooth restoration: We usually install a dental crown to replace a single tooth, but a denture or bridge is preferable when replacing multiple teeth. Our tooth replacement options are life-looking and made to last, just like the natural tooth you lost.

Does dental insurance cover dental implants?

Insurance coverage depends on your specific plan and benefits. Some policies may contribute toward certain portions of implant treatment, while others may have exclusions or limitations. Because implant treatment involves several stages, patients should verify which services are covered. Brentwood Dental Care can help patients understand costs and applicable insurance benefits.

What are the benefits of dental implants compared with other tooth replacement options?

Dental implants replace missing teeth with support anchored in the jawbone. They can provide stability for biting and chewing while supporting natural-looking restorations. Implants are also versatile because they can be used with a crown for one missing tooth or incorporated into bridges and dentures when multiple teeth require replacement.

Can I get a dental implant if I am missing only one tooth?

Yes. A single missing tooth can often be replaced with an individual implant and dental crown. This creates a replacement that extends from the implant within the jaw to the visible restoration above the gums. Dr. Mungcal can determine whether this approach is appropriate after evaluating the missing-tooth area and your oral health.

Can dental implants replace several or all of my missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants are not exclusively used for single-tooth replacement. When several teeth are absent, implants can provide support for a bridge or denture. The number of implants and type of prosthetic restoration needed will depend on the extent of tooth loss and findings from your examination.

Can I still get dental implants if I have experienced bone loss?

Possibly. Adequate bone is important for supporting an implant, but patients with insufficient bone may have additional options. Brentwood Dental Care evaluates the supporting structures using digital imaging during treatment planning. In some situations, bone grafting may be recommended to prepare the jaw for future implant placement.

How long does the dental implant process take?

Dental implant treatment occurs over several stages. After placement, Brentwood Dental Care generally allows approximately 4-6 months for the implant to heal and integrate with the jawbone. Once sufficient healing has occurred, treatment can progress toward the abutment and final restoration. Additional procedures may extend the overall timeline.

Are dental implants designed to look like natural teeth?

The final restoration is designed to recreate the visible portion of a missing tooth.
