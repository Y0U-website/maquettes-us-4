Meet our St. Petersburg Dental Team

Hilary Arvin, Dental Hygienist

Hilary was born and raised in Dunedin, FL. She moved to St. Pete in 2007 and shortly after began her dental hygiene career. She graduated dental hygiene school from St. Pete College and has been practicing as a hygienist for over 6 years. She loves dentistry as it enables her to meet and bond with so many different people. She has a number of hobbies including sewing and card making, but most of her free time she spends with her grand-babies! She is a source of positivity in our office and as a hygienist she provides outstanding oral hygiene care and instruction to our patients.

Katie Hartline, Dental Assistant

Zhenya Beloshapkina, Dental Hygienist

From Barnaul, a city in Siberia, Zhenya is now enjoying warm Florida. Nevertheless, she still enjoys winter sports, one of her favorites being skiing. Zhenya loves traveling, dancing and has many other hobbies. When she has free time, she spends it with her husband and adorable daughter. She has been in the dental field for 20 years, serving as a hygienist since 2006. With all her experience and knowledge, her main priority is making her patients happy and doing everything she can to benefit their oral health.

Jamaiya Gainer, Business Assistant

Jamaiya is one of the few who were born and raised in St. Petersburg! She loves spending time in the sunny outdoors. She enjoys relaxing by the pool with her three kids and loves to read. Jamaiya has been in the dental field for over 5 years and what keeps her motivated is the joy that comes from helping people. She is a valuable part of our team and often communicates with patients while answering phones, greeting at the front desk, scheduling, and much more.

Maria Hough, Dental Hygienist

Originally from Russia, Maria came to the United States and graduated from St. Petersburg College in St. Petersburg, Florida. She has worked in dentistry since 2001. As a hygienist, Maria prides herself on great patient relationships and the resulting smiles she is able to produce on a daily basis. Maria enjoys kayaking, yoga, and running.

Michelle Bower, Treatment Coordinator

Michelle is a native Midwesterner. She moved to St. Petersburg in 2022 with her husband and has been in dentistry for over 30 years. Her hobbies include going to the beach, fishing, and making TikToks. She has four adult children including twins, which graduated from Purdue. Creating a positive environment for our patients and staff is her priority.

Natalia Moukhov, Dental Assistant

A native of Russia, Natalia moved to the United States with her husband and twin daughters in 1998. She has worked in the dental field in St. Petersburg, Florida, since 2000. As a dental assistant, she loves making a positive difference in our patients’ lives. Her goal is to assist the doctor in creating beautiful and healthy smiles in a comfortable and trustworthy setting. Natalia fills her time away from the office with family and friends. She enjoys the beautiful Sunshine State through biking, crafting, photography, kayaking and spending time at the beach.
