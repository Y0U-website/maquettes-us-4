Philosophy

  • Friendliness is a main focus for our practice, you will be welcomed from the first step in the door.
  • Trustworthiness is our keystone to providing you with the best care around.
  • Comfort is our main priority, while receiving treatment from us, you should be at ease.

What are the things that make us different?

A Relaxing Environment - We understand that going to the dentist can be difficult. Our kind team is very sensitive to your needs and strives to create a welcoming environment at our practice where you can feel at ease. We are dedicated to helping you have the most comfortable dental experience possible.

Reduced Anxiety - Dr. Gutu and Dr. De Jesus have a special gift for helping patients with dental anxiety. Between a gentle touch and great chairside manner, you will quickly see they care about you as a person and will do everything possible to eliminate pain and discomfort. It is their goal to put you at ease.

Quality Care - We stand behind all of the work we do because we never settle for anything but our very best. Our Doctors are skilled professionals in providing excellent services for patients of all ages. We work with the latest dental technologies, including digital x-rays, intraoral cameras and lasers, to deliver better accuracy, safety and comfort for you. Additionally, our costs are reasonable so you can receive quality care at a price you can afford.

Proper Patient Education - We know that the more educated you are during your visit, the more confident you will feel. Our caring team spends significant time with you explaining your exam results and going over your treatment options. We will give our honest recommendations and answer any questions you might have. We want you to have full confidence in your dental health. If you value these qualities in a dentist, call us today! Come to a practice where you can finally relax and feel at ease!
