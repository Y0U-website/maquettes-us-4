How to Find Out What Your Insurance Covers

The best way to understand your implant coverage is to contact your insurance provider directly. Ask about specific procedures using the correct billing codes, and find out about annual maximums, deductibles, waiting periods, or any exclusions that might apply. Your dental provider’s office can also assist with a pre-authorization or insurance verification to help you get a clearer picture of what’s covered before treatment begins.

What If Insurance Doesn’t Cover It?

If your insurance offers little or no coverage for implants, don’t panic. Many dental practices offer financing options or payment plans to make implants more affordable. There are also third-party financing companies that specialize in healthcare lending. While the upfront cost might seem high, it’s worth considering the long-term value of dental implants they can last decades with proper care and often outperform bridges or dentures in comfort and durability.
