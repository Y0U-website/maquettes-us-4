  • Natural Appearance: The crown or bridge placed on top of the implant is custom-made to match your natural teeth, giving you a seamless and aesthetic result.
  • Improved Functionality: Dental implants restore full chewing function, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods without difficulty. Unlike dentures, which can slip or cause discomfort, implants stay securely in place.
  • Preserve Jawbone Health: When a tooth is missing, the jawbone in that area can deteriorate over time. Dental implants help preserve bone structure by stimulating the bone just like a natural tooth root.
  • Enhanced Confidence: Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth, so you can smile confidently without worrying about gaps or ill-fitting dentures.
  • Consultation & Evaluation: Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we assess your oral health and discuss your goals. We’ll take digital X-rays and 3D scans to determine the best location for your implants and ensure you have enough bone density to support them.
  • Implant Placement: The next step is the surgical placement of the implant into your jawbone. This is a minimally invasive procedure performed under local anesthesia, ensuring that you’re comfortable throughout the process.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: After the implant is placed, your body will undergo a healing process called osseointegration, where the implant fuses with the jawbone. This process usually takes several months but is crucial for the stability of the implant.
  • Placement of Restoration: Once the implant has fully integrated with the bone, a custom-designed crown, bridge, or denture is placed on top of the implant, completing the restoration process and restoring your smile.
  • Sufficient jawbone density to support the implant
  • Healthy gums free from periodontal disease
  • No severe health conditions that could affect healing (e.g., uncontrolled diabetes)
  • A commitment to maintaining good oral hygiene

  • What is a dental crown, and when do I need one? A dental crown is a custom-made cap that covers a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, size, strength, and appearance. Crowns are often used for teeth that have been severely decayed, cracked, or treated with a root canal. At Perfect Smiles California, we offer tooth-colored crowns for a natural look that blends seamlessly with your smile.
  • What is the difference between a dental filling and a dental crown? A dental filling is used to restore a tooth that has a cavity, whereas a dental crown is typically used for teeth that are more severely damaged or weakened. While fillings restore smaller areas of decay, crowns are used when a tooth requires more extensive repair or protection, such as after a root canal.
  • What is a dental implant, and how does it work? A dental implant is a permanent solution for replacing a missing tooth. It consists of a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, acting as the tooth’s root. Once the implant has fused with the bone, a custom-made crown is placed on top to restore the tooth’s function and appearance. Perfect Smiles California specializes in implant dentistry to give you a strong, natural-looking smile.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Implants are highly durable and resistant to decay, making them a long-term solution for missing teeth. Regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene, and avoiding habits like smoking will help maximize the longevity of your implants.
  • What is a root canal, and is it painful? A root canal is a procedure used to treat a tooth that is infected or severely decayed, removing the damaged tissue from the tooth’s pulp. The tooth is then sealed to prevent further infection. Contrary to popular belief, root canal therapy is not painful, as the procedure is done under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. After the treatment, your tooth will be restored with a crown or filling.
  • What is a full-mouth restoration, and when do I need one? A full-mouth restoration is a comprehensive treatment plan that combines multiple restorative procedures to improve the function, health, and appearance of your teeth. It may include a combination of crowns, implants, veneers, and fillings. This option is ideal for patients who have multiple dental issues or severe damage to their teeth. Our team at Perfect Smiles California will develop a personalized plan tailored to your needs.
  • Does insurance cover restorative dentistry procedures? Many restorative dentistry procedures are covered by dental insurance, though coverage may vary depending on the procedure and plan.
