Dental Implant Options

Single-Tooth Dental Implants

A titanium implant replaces the root of the missing tooth, and a custom ceramic crown completes it — shaded and shaped to match the teeth around it.

Unlike a traditional bridge, neighboring healthy teeth generally do not need to be reshaped to carry the restoration, and the implant helps preserve bone in the area of the missing tooth.

  • Titanium implant replaces the missing tooth root
  • Custom ceramic crown completes the tooth
  • Healthy neighboring teeth usually stay untouched
  • Designed to look and function naturally

Multiple Missing Teeth

The number and position of implants is planned around bone availability and how your bite loads that part of the mouth, so the restoration stays supported over the long term.

  • Fixed restoration supported by implants, not by natural teeth
  • Fewer implants than missing teeth in many cases
  • Planned digitally before treatment begins

Full-Arch Dental Implants

Treatment is digitally planned with 3D imaging so implant position, bite, and smile design are confirmed in advance. Many qualifying patients can leave with a fixed provisional smile on treatment day, with the final restoration delivered once healing is complete.

  • Typically four to six implants per arch
  • Fixed restoration — no removable plate or denture adhesive
  • Digitally planned using 3D imaging
  • Designed to restore chewing function and confidence

Which Type of Dental Implant Treatment Do I Need?

Best when one individual tooth is missing and the surrounding teeth remain healthy.

Best when several teeth are missing and a fixed bridge on implants can replace them together.

The correct solution depends on bone health, remaining teeth, bite, medical history and long-term goals. A 3D evaluation allows the team to determine which approach is appropriate.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many adults with one or more missing teeth, failing teeth, or uncomfortable dentures are candidates for some form of implant treatment. The evaluation looks at your general oral health, gum condition, available bone, medical history, medications, and smoking, along with what you want the result to achieve.

Gum health matters in particular, because untreated periodontal disease is one of the more common reasons implants fail. Where bone is limited, grafting may be appropriate. None of this can be determined from a photograph — a professional evaluation with 3D imaging is what tells us whether implants are the right option for you.

What If I Don&#x27;t Have Enough Bone?

Bone can resorb in the years after a tooth is lost, which is why some patients are told elsewhere that they are not implant candidates. In many cases that conclusion is premature.

A 3D scan shows exactly how much bone is present and where. Depending on the findings, bone grafting may build the site up, or implants may be positioned to engage areas of denser bone. Patients with bone loss are not automatically disqualified from dental implant treatment.

Full-Arch Dental Implants and All-on-X in Destin, FL

Full mouth dental implants replace an entire arch with a fixed restoration rather than a removable plate. A conventional denture rests on the gums, moves during eating, and often depends on adhesive. Implant-supported teeth are anchored in the jawbone, so they stay put and are cleaned in place.

All-on-X treatment positions four to six implants per arch where the bone is strongest, then attaches one fixed prosthesis across them. Because chewing forces transfer through the implants into the bone, patients typically regain far more function than a denture allows, and the bone in the treated arch is stimulated rather than left unloaded.

Every case is planned digitally before surgery day, and provisional teeth may be delivered the same day when clinically appropriate. You can read a fuller comparison in our article on single-tooth versus full-arch dental implants in Destin .

Same-Day Dental Implants and Fixed Provisional Teeth

Patients searching for same-day dental implants in Destin are usually asking whether they will leave the office with teeth. In many qualifying full-arch cases the answer is yes: a fixed provisional arch can be attached to the implants on treatment day, so you are not without teeth while healing takes place.

Same-day teeth are not appropriate for everyone. It depends on bone quality and how stable the implants are once placed, along with your medical history and the condition of the arch being treated. When immediate loading is not clinically appropriate, a short healing period comes first. We tell you which situation applies to you before treatment day rather than on it.

What Affects the Cost of Dental Implants in Destin?

No two implant cases cost the same, and any figure quoted before an evaluation is guesswork. These are the factors that move the number:

  • How many implants are required and in which arch
  • Whether extractions, grafting or a sinus procedure are needed first
  • The restoration attached to the implants — crown, bridge or full arch
  • Materials selected for the final prosthesis
  • Sedation preferences and the complexity of the surgical plan
