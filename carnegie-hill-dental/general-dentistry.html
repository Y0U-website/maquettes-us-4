Routine dental visits allow us to monitor changes in your oral health and recommend treatment only when it is clinically necessary.

  • Professional teeth cleanings
  • Digital X-rays and oral cancer screenings
  • Gum disease evaluation and treatment
  • Tooth-colored, mercury-free fillings
  • Preventive guidance for at-home oral care

Patient-Focused Preventive Care

Preventive dentistry helps protect natural teeth and supports overall wellness. During your visit, we take time to explain findings, answer questions, and outline practical steps you can take to maintain your oral health between appointments.

Our conservative approach prioritizes preservation of natural tooth structure whenever possible.

Comfortable Care in a Modern Setting

We use digital imaging and modern tools to improve diagnostic accuracy while enhancing patient comfort. Our Upper East Side practice emphasizes efficiency, cleanliness, and a calm clinical experience.

When to Schedule a General Dentistry Visit

You should schedule a dental visit if you:

  • Are you due for a routine cleaning or exam
  • Experience tooth sensitivity or discomfort
  • Notice bleeding gums or persistent bad breath
  • Want to maintain long-term oral health
