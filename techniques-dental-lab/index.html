Techniques Dental Lab integrates modern digital dentistry to streamline the workflow between dentist and laboratory. Our Columbus dental lab offers a full range of restorative solutions, including:

Zirconia crowns and layered Emax restorations, Porcelain to metal ceramic crowns Custom implant abutments Gold crowns and specialty restorations Digital dental workflows and CAD design

Every restoration is proudly made in the USA using premium materials and precise laboratory processes to ensure exceptional fit, strength, and esthetics.
