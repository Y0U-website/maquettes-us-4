Getting Used to Your New Teeth

Adapting to full mouth implants is a process that requires patience and time. Feel free to contact our office if you have concerns about the fit or function of your new teeth, and we can address any issues promptly. Here are some tips to help you get used to your full mouth implants:

  • Follow post-operative instructions
  • Allow time for healing
  • Gradually transition from soft food to hard food
  • Practice speaking
  • Maintain good oral hygiene
  • Schedule regular dental checkups
