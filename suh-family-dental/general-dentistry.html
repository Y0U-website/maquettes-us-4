General Dental Care

The experienced team at Suh Family Dental understands that a number of factors must come together for an individual to achieve and maintain great oral health. They are:

  • Quality preventative care. Our highly-trained hygienists and assistants recognize the positive impact that thorough and regular professional cleanings can have on maintaining a great smile. We also take time to properly educate our patients about the best ways to care for their teeth and gums in between check-ups, and also share with them valuable information about the latest tools available to help with hygiene.
  • Early identification of potential problems. We use the latest technology, including digital x-rays, along with thorough examinations by Dr. Bleicken to identify issues before they become significant problems. This reduces the amount of time our patients spend in our office, associated discomfort, and the costs they incur over time.
  • Excellent communication. We recognize that sharing a clear understanding of goals, and the processes to achieve them, is every bit as important as quality work. We want all of our patients to understand exactly what their situation entails, all of the options available, and our best recommendation for moving forward.

Comprehensive Dentistry in a Comfortable Environment

Suh Family Dental provides the full range of dental care to individuals of all ages, and we do so in a clean, state-of-the-art facility. Every patient is provided an individualized treatment plan, starting with the most effective procedures delivered in a way that seeks to make the dental care process an enjoyable experience. Some of our comprehensive dental treatments include:

  • Cleanings
  • Visual examinations
  • Cancer screenings
  • Digital x-rays, including full mouth series and traditional bitewings
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Sealants
