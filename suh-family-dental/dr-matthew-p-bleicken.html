Dr. Matthew P. Bleicken, DMD

General Dentist

A native of Metro-Atlanta, Dr. Matthew Bleicken has been proudly serving families in Duluth and the surrounding communities for over 25 years. He earned his Bachelor of Science from the University of Georgia and his Doctor of Medical Dentistry from the Medical College of Georgia in Augusta. Since receiving his Georgia dental license in 1999, Dr. Bleicken has built a practice grounded in trust, compassion, and the belief that every patient deserves to be treated like family.

Dr. Bleicken is an active member of the American Dental Association, the Georgia Dental Association, and the Northern District Dental Society, and he regularly participates in continuing education to stay current with advances in dental care.
