Dr. Ethan Suh, DDS

General Dentist

Dr. Ethan Suh is originally from Dallas, Texas, and is excited to now call the Atlanta area home. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Southern Methodist University, his Doctor of Dental Surgery from Texas A&M College of Dentistry, and completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at the Olin E. Teague Veterans’ Center in Temple, Texas, where he had the privilege of caring for veterans alongside a multidisciplinary medical team.

Dr. Suh brings seven years of experience treating patients of all ages and backgrounds across Texas and New York. His clinical skills include dental implants, molar root canals, multi-unit restorations, Invisalign, and cosmetic dentistry. He is also certified in Invisalign clear aligner therapy.

Dr. Suh believes dentistry is about more than teeth — it is about building trust, helping people feel at ease, and caring for each patient as an individual. He takes time to listen, explain treatment options clearly, and make sure every patient feels confident in their care.

Dr. Suh is married and lives close to the office. Outside of work, he enjoys playing pickleball, exploring Atlanta’s neighborhoods with his wife, digital photography, and a deep appreciation for good coffee.
