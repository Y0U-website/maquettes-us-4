Restorative Dentistry: Endodontics, Periodontics Prosthodontics

Restorative Dentistry is a term used to describe many procedures that are designed to improve overall oral health. Patients who choose restorative procedures are generally looking to address an existing or potential problem, or accomplish another goal related to functionality or appearance. The experienced team at Suh Family Dental routinely performs procedures that correct the following issues:

  • Cracked or chipped teeth
  • Improper bite alignment
  • Speech problems
  • Injury to the teeth, gums or oral tissue
  • Tooth decay and replacement
  • Gum disease
  • Infections
  • Improper spacing
  • Excessively worn or uneven teeth
  • Tooth sensitivity
  • Mouth ulcers and canker sores
