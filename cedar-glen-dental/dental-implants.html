Dental Implants

A long-lasting choice, implant-supported dentistry brings you as close to growing new teeth as possible.

The Number One Replacement Solution

Expertly placed in the same bone that once supported teeth, our modern implant solutions will help you enjoy the strength and function of a natural tooth once again.

Dr. Caldwell uses the latest technology (digital x-rays, 3D mapping, models, and photographs) to collect all the data necessary to help plan your restorative case.

He also uses highly regarded specialists to bring all components of your case together. But at all times, he serves as the quarterback of the entire plan.

Our modern porcelains produce a result so life-like, you may forget they aren’t your natural teeth. The stability and sense of confidence you develop with implants will leave you smiling.

Surgical

We first numb the site using a modern dental anesthetic. Then, the sterile implant piece is placed into an exact location, and a smooth cover, or healing cap, protects the implant during the healing period.

In some cases, a temporary or permanent crown is placed to protect the implant until it has fully fused with your bone. This period usually lasts three to six months as the bone grows around the implant.

Dr. Caldwell will help you decide what will work best during the initial healing period, ensuring you’re able to function while still protecting your implants.

Restorative

A well-thought-out treatment plan that includes implants creates the opportunity for different restoration options.

After the healing period, crowns or bridges are custom-made and attached to your implants with small screws or dental cement.

In the end, you’ll be able to enjoy a fully functional and natural-looking smile for life!
