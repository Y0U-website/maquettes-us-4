Implants

0 Implants more services Dental implants are a predictable and safe was to replace single or multiple missing teeth. Implants allow us to restore your smile and function and look just like natural teeth without having to drill on the neighboring teeth.

A dental implant is a titanium post that is placed in the bone where the root of the missing tooth was located. After the bone has adequately healed around the implant, a crown is placed on top of the post.

Implants require great bone and great oral hygiene for long term success. This type of tooth replacement can be a little more expensive than traditional partials or bridges but provide superior benefits for long term oral health.
