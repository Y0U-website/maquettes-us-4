About Us Who We Are

Valley Wide Dentistry serves the Huntsville Area’s families in all their dental needs by providing friendly and professional services at an affordable rate.

Valley Wide Dental has provided patients top quality dentistry in the local area for over 30 years. Originally established as a general practice for adult patients, it expanded to have Huntsville offer pediatric dentists in 2001 and now operates as a comprehensive family dental practice.

Our comprehensive approach may include a toddler’s first visit, sealant fillings for teenagers, cosmetic smile makeovers, emergency care, dentures, and even implants. With over 8 Huntsville dentists on staff and an experienced support staff, our goal is to have a solution for every patient that walks through our door.

Valley Wide is well known throughout North Alabama for our Medicaid and Medicare dental services. Two reasons Valley Wide is popular with parents are our board certified Huntsville pediatric dentists that are specifically trained to work with young children and general dentists that are trained to work with older children, all at the same practice.

Many other teenage and adult patients are referred to Valley Wide for our filling, root canal, crown, and tooth extraction services. In summary, siblings of all ages, parents and grandparents can be seen at the same location.
