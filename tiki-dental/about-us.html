A positive attitude toward at-home oral hygiene is the best way to prepare your child for a great dental visit! Turn their daily routine into a fun adventure by playing their favorite tunes and brushing together—it’s a party for their pearly whites!

A legal guardian must be present for your child’s first visit. After the initial visit, if they are under 18, their legal guardian must complete the consent form (linked below) and have the patient bring it to their appointment or send it to us by email.

https://docs.google.com/document/d/1A1FxeFAy4ZayVpjL2LLLKICm8WT31zDLF38PJq3bGig/edit?usp=sharing

Our top priority is getting to know you and your little one, and empowering your family with the tools and resources your child needs for smiling success! We’ll take your family on a tour around our space, introduce your child to our welcoming team, and learn their unique needs with a gentle, informative exam.

For a smooth, stress-free visit, please remember to bring your driver’s license, dental insurance card, and any questions you may be wondering for Dr. Adelson. We also invite your child to take along their favorite toy, snuggly stuffed animal,their favorite toothpaste, or cozy blanket for that added boost of comfort!
