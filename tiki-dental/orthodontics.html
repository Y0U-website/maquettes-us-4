Invisalign

Invisalign is a popular orthodontic treatment option that utilizes clear aligners to straighten teeth. The aligners are custom-made from a transparent plastic material, making them virtually invisible when worn. Invisalign offers several advantages over traditional braces including aesthetics, comfort, and convenience. They are also removable, allowing for easier maintenance and the freedom to eat without restrictions.
