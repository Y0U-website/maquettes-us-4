Pediatric Dental Care

Holistic Approach

At Tiki Dental we prioritize gentle, non-invasive treatments that support the body’s natural ability to heal. Through remineralization techniques, we help strengthen enamel and prevent cavities before they start. By using fluoride alternatives, calcium-rich treatments, and diet-based recommendations, we encourage natural tooth repair and long-term health.

Pediatric Digital X- rays

Some problems are invisible to the naked eye. An x-ray, however, can tell a different story. Digital x-rays are a quick and efficient method for capturing images of your child’s dental health. Because we don’t have x-ray vision, they help us capture in-depth images that allow us to see between teeth.

Pediatric Oral Cancer Screenings

Yes, even kids need oral cancer screenings! This quick and safe check can be done without your child even noticing. Our Doctor will take a good look at your child’s mouth, gums, and oral tissues for any signs of trouble. If we spot something out of the ordinary, we’ll guide you through the next steps. Regular dental visits can help us keep your child’s mouth healthy and happy!

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Are you a parent looking for ways to protect your child’s teeth from cavities? Consider Pediatric Dental Sealants in Hallandale Beach. This is a safe and painless preventative dental treatment that involves the application of a thin, protective coating (sealant) to the chewing surfaces of your child’s back teeth (molars and premolars).All of our sealants are BPA free !
