Dental implant restorations in Stow offer several advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. Unlike removable dentures or bridges that rely on neighboring teeth, implant restorations:

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant consists of a titanium root that is surgically placed into the jawbone by a dental specialist to replace a missing tooth root. Over time, the bone fuses with the implant, creating a strong foundation for a custom-made crown that resembles your natural teeth. At Stow Acres Family Dental, we focus on restoring dental implants once they have been placed, completing both the function and aesthetics of your smile.

Types of Dental Implant Restorations Stow, Massachusetts Residents Can Choose

At Stow Acres Family Dental, our Stow implant restoration team provides custom solutions for dental implants that have already been placed by a specialist:

Single Tooth Implant Restorations

For patients missing one tooth, a dental implant crown restores a previously placed implant with a natural-looking replacement. This final step involves attaching a custom crown designed to match your existing teeth.

Multiple Tooth Implant Restorations

When several teeth are missing, multiple existing dental implants can support a bridge or partial denture. Restoring implants in this way helps replace missing teeth without altering adjacent healthy teeth.
