The practice

Welcome to our practice, where your dental health and well-being are our top priorities. We are proud to introduce you to the heart and soul of our team, Dr. Minerva Patel, DDS. With a commitment to excellence and a passion for providing exceptional dental care, Dr. Patel is dedicated to helping you achieve your brightest and healthiest smile.

Dr. Patel currently serves on the board of the American Dental Association as a Delegate to the New York Dental Association and also as vice-chair of the Membership Committee.

Dr. Patel is an active member of the American Dental Association and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Dr. Patel, a committed pediatric dental specialist, provides an extensive array of services encompassing sedation, preventive care, diagnostics, and restoration, all supported by the cutting-edge technology available at her practice.

State-of-the-art technology like low-radiation digital X-rays helps us examine your child’s teeth and gums.

Our in-office sedation is a safe, integral part of dentistry that helps patients cope with various dental procedures.

Our office resides in the heart of White Plains, just a short 10-minute stroll away from the train station.
