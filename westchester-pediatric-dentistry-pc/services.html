About Our Services

At Westchester Pediatric Dentistry

We specialize in providing comprehensive dental care tailored specifically for children, from infancy through adolescence. Our primary objective is to promote proper oral hygiene, prevent dental issues, and ensure a positive dental experience for young patients.

Our services encompass a range of treatments and techniques designed to address the unique needs of children, making visits to the dentist enjoyable and fostering a lifetime of healthy smiles.

Prevention

Prevention is a vital part of healthy teeth, including regular cleanings, fluoride and sealant applications and mouth guards.

Diagnostic

State-of-the-art technology like low-radiation digital X-rays helps us examine your child’s teeth and gums.

Sedation Dentistry

Our in-office sedation is a safe, integral part of dentistry that helps patients cope with various dental procedures.

Restorative

Restorative dentistry helps to improve teeth in both health an appearance after tooth damage or loss.

Pediatric Dental Trauma

If you face a dental emergency, give us a call immediately.

Silver Diamine Fluoride

Silver Diamine Fluoride is an FDA approved chemotherapy clinically applied to control active dental caries.

Myofunctional Appliances / Interceptive Orthodontics

Children with Disabilities

Laser Frenectomy

Ankyloglossia or tongue-tie is the restriction of oral tissues such as the lingual or labial frenulum.
