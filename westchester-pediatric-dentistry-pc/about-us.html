OUR DOCTOR

Minerva Patel, DDS

Board Certified Pediatric Dentist

25+ years of experience

Patient-first approach

A Board Certified Pediatric Dentist in New York. Born and raised in Harrison, New York, Dr. Patel has a degree in dentistry from New York University and is an active member of the American Dental Association and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry

With an experience of over 25 years, Dr. Patel has extensive training in pediatric facial growth and development, as well as interceptive orthodontics. Besides being a winner of several research awards, Dr. Patel also organizes and presents continuing education classes for dentists.

Dr. Patel has served on multiple committees of the American Dental Association, the New York Dental Association, and is also the past President of the 9th District Dental Association.

At Westchester&#x27;s Pediatric Dentistry, we have been dedicated to delivering top-notch pediatric dental care in the White Plains area since 2001. Our Mission

Our mission is to create a joyful and nurturing dental experience.

With a commitment to excellence and compassion, we strive to provide exceptional pediatric dental care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. Our team is dedicated to partnering with parents and guardians to educate, empower, and instill positive oral hygiene habits from a young age. Through innovation, education, and personalized care, we aspire to be a trusted pillar of support in the journey to optimal oral health for every child we serve.
