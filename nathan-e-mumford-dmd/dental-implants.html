Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

If you are missing one or more teeth and are free of active gum disease, you are likely an excellent candidate for dental implants. To confirm eligibility, schedule a dental implant consultation today by calling 860-677-8666 .

Do you offer Saturday appointments?

We do. We know that it can be challenging to fit a dental appointment in during the week, so we are open on Saturday from 8:00 am 1:00 pm. For your convenience, we also begin seeing patients at 7:15 am Monday-Friday.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Some dental insurance plans may offer partial coverage for dental implants, including implant-supported dentures. Other policies may not provide any coverage. Check your insurance policy for details. To make dental implants more affordable, Dental Associates accepts CareCredit financing.

How long does it take to get dental implants?

Each patient is unique. If you’ve recently lost a tooth or had a tooth extracted, the jaw bone is often strong enough to support dental implants immediately. In other cases, additional treatments such as bone grafts may be necessary to fortify the jaw to support implants. Our dentists can provide an accurate timeline based on your unique needs.
