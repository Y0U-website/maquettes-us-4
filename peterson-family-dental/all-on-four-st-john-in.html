Advanced Dental Implants in St. John IN for Long-Term Results

Extensive tooth loss can significantly impact on oral function, health, and overall quality of life. At Peterson Family Dental, we offer a reliable, long-lasting solution: all-on-four dental implants. This innovative technique replaces a full arch of missing teeth using just four precisely placed implants, creating a stable foundation for a fixed, natural-looking prosthesis.

Why Choose All-On-Four Implants in St. John, IN?

The all-on-four system is an excellent choice for patients who need full-arch restoration but want to avoid the inconvenience of removable dentures or multiple individual implants.

Here are key benefits of choosing all-on-four:

Fixed Solution: The prosthesis is securely anchored, providing excellent stability.

Efficient Treatment: Fewer implants are required, reducing treatment time and surgical complexity.

Natural Appearance: Custom-designed prosthetics closely replicate the look and function of natural teeth.

Durable Results: With proper care, all-on-four implants can last 10 years or more.

Improved Oral Function: Restore full chewing capability and speech clarity.
