What Are Veneers and How Do They Work?

Dental veneers are ultra-thin, custom-crafted shells designed to cover the front surface of teeth. At Peterson Family Dental, we use both porcelain veneers and composite resin veneers, tailored to meet the unique cosmetic and functional needs of each patient.

Whether your teeth are discolored from years of coffee, chipped from accidents, or naturally uneven, veneers in St. John IN can transform your appearance with minimal tooth reduction. The results? A brighter, more symmetrical, and youthful smile that boosts your self-confidence.

The Veneers Procedure: What to Expect

Getting veneers in St. John IN is a straightforward, transformative process:

Step 1 Personalized Consultation

We begin with a thorough examination and consultation to understand your goals and determine whether veneers are the right choice. We’ll discuss options, preview your smile design, and answer all your questions.

Step 2 Tooth Preparation & Impressions

For porcelain veneers, we gently prepare the teeth by removing a minimal amount of enamel. Next, we take digital impressions to ensure precise, custom-fitted veneers. Temporary veneers may be placed while your permanent ones are crafted.

Step 3 Final Placement

Once your veneers are ready, we carefully bond them to your teeth using advanced adhesive techniques. We make adjustments for fit and color, ensuring your new smile looks natural and feels great.

Maintaining your veneers is easy. Just follow good oral hygiene practices brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and visiting Peterson Family Dental for routine cleanings and exams. Avoid biting hard objects or using your teeth as tools, and your veneers in St. John IN will remain radiant for years to come.
