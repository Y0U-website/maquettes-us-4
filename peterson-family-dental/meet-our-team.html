Meet Our Team

Dr. Rachel grew up in Hammond, IN. After receiving her B.S. in General Biology from University of Michigan and her M.S. in Biology from Purdue University, she went on to earn her DDS degree from New York University College of Dentistry. Following dental school, Dr. Rachel returned to Northwest Indiana to join her mother’s dental practice, Peterson Family Dental.

Dr. Rachel provides general dentistry services to patients of all ages. She is a member of the American Dental Association, the Indiana Dental Association, the Northwest Indiana Dental Society, and the Chicago Dental Society. As a lifelong learner, Dr. Rachel enjoys participating in a local dental study club and attending numerous continuing education events each year.

Dr. Rachel lives in Dyer with her husband and two young children. When she isn’t practicing dentistry, she enjoys working on home-improvement projects, reading, and spending time with her family.

In his spare time, he enjoys playing the piano, producing music, exploring new restaurants, and taking long walks in the park with his wife.
