Ideal Candidates for Dental Implants Dyer, IN

Dental implants are best suited for patients with healthy gums and adequate bone density. If you’ve experienced bone loss, we may recommend a bone graft to prepare the area for implant placement. Our prosthodontic services include using advanced imaging and guided techniques to ensure precise, comfortable treatment and long-term success for both implants and dentures.

Comfortable and Affordable Dentures

Dentures are a time-tested and cost-effective option for replacing multiple or all missing teeth. At Peterson Family Dental in Dyer, IN, we offer both full and partial dentures, custom-designed to fit your mouth comfortably and restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Comprehensive Denture Options

Full dentures are used when all teeth in the upper or lower arch are missing, while partial dentures are used when some natural teeth remain. We also offer implant-supported dentures, which combine the affordability of dentures with the added stability of dental implants.

The Benefits of Dentures

Dentures may be the right choice if you’re looking for a non-surgical solution, or if you’re not a candidate for implants due to bone loss or other health concerns. They are also a great option for patients who want a more budget-friendly alternative to implants.

Our team in Dyer, IN, will work closely with you to ensure your dentures fit securely and look natural. We’ll also provide guidance on how to care for both implants and dentures and when to return for adjustments.

  • Improved ability to chew and speak
  • Enhanced facial appearance and support
  • Custom fit for comfort and aesthetics
  • Easy removal for cleaning and maintenance
  • Lower upfront cost compared to implants

Making the Right Choice for Your Smile

Choosing between dental implants and dentures depends on several factors, including your oral health, lifestyle, and budget. Implants offer a long-term, stable solution that closely mimics natural teeth, while dentures provide a more affordable and non-invasive option. At Peterson Family Dental, we take the time to understand your goals and help you make an informed decision that supports your long-term health and confidence.
