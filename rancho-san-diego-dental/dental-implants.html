Book Appointment What to Expect During the Procedure The placement of dental implants is a carefully planned surgical procedure performed with precision and comfort in mind.

If necessary, tooth extractions or bone grafting may be completed before implant placement to create a healthy foundation.

The gum is gently opened, and a small channel is prepared in the jawbone. The implant posts are then positioned securely.

Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. During this healing time, temporary teeth may be provided so you can maintain appearance and function.

Once fully healed, a custom crown, bridge, or implant-supported dentures are attached to the implant, completing your smile restoration.

Each step is designed to support long-term success and comfort.

Book Appointment Full Arch Replacement Solutions For patients missing most or all teeth, full-arch dental implants offer a secure alternative to removable options. These restorations attach firmly to implant posts, improving stability and chewing strength.
