Meet Dr. Bachrach

I love helping my patients achieve the smile they have always wanted. Whether it is the entire

dentition or just one tooth, we work together to create a plan that provides treatment with

optimal oral health, function, and aesthetics at the forefront.

I received my B.S. from The Honors College at Adelphi University, as a Biology Major in the Pre-

Dental Program, and my D.M.D. from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. I completed a

General Practice Residency at New York’s Lutheran Medical Center, and practiced in midtown

Manhattan and Long Island for several years prior to moving to New Jersey.

I am a member of the American Dental Association, New Jersey Dental Association, and

Bergen County Dental Association. I am an Invisalign and Clear Correct Provider. I

frequently attend continuing education courses in order to stay abreast with the latest

technology and provide the most advanced care to my patients.

I look forward to getting to know your smile and the person surrounding it!
