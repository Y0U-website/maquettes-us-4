As one of only a couple female owned and operated endodontic practices in Pittsburgh, Dr. Sara O’Brien values staying at the forefront of training and education to ensure her patients receive the best care possible. Dr. O’Brien brings an extensive medical background and years of clinical training to Soft Touch Endodontics. She is a pharmacist as well as an endodontist.

When it comes to endodontic pain, prompt diagnosis and treatment is crucial. Our practice specializes in all aspects of endodontics including initial root canals and retreatments, apical microsurgery, cracked teeth, traumatic injuries, internal bleaching, and repairing root resorptive defects.
