All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Stability and Comfort : Implants fuse with the jawbone, providing a stable and comfortable fit.
  • Enhanced Appearance : Enjoy a natural-looking smile.
  • Improved Oral Health : Reduce bone loss and preserve facial structure.

The All-on-4 Procedure

  • Consultation and Evaluation : During your initial visit, our team will evaluate your oral health and determine if All-on-4 implants are right for you.
  • Customized Treatment Plan : We create a personalized plan tailored to your unique needs.
  • Implant Placement : On the day of the procedure, the implants are surgically placed in your jawbone.
  • Temporary Teeth : You will receive temporary teeth on the same day, allowing you to leave our office with a new smile.
  • Healing and Final Restoration : After a healing period, your permanent teeth will be placed, completing your transformation.
