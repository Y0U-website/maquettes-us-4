Meet Dr. Reynolds

Family Dentistry with a Smile.

Dr. Reynolds was born in Havre De Grace, Maryland. She was raised in Edgewood, Maryland and graduated from Edgewood High School. She attended the Community College of Baltimore and received her Associate of Arts degree in Dental Hygiene. Dr. Reynolds attended the University of Maryland Dental School earning her doctorate in dental surgery. She was a honorary member of Prosthetics Society Gamma Pi Delta and Gorgas Odontological Society.

Dr. Reynolds belongs to the Harford/Cecil Dental Society, the Maryland State Dental Association and the American Dental Association. Dr. Reynolds attends many of the Chesapeake Health Education Programs which keeps her up to date with the latest dental technology.

On a personal note, Dr. Reynolds enjoys spending time with her grandson Jack, named after her father, and her Cavalier King Charles, Abby, along with baking, gardening, movies and travel.
