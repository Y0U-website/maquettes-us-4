Accessibility Statement

  • Accessibility Statement | Firefly Family Dentistry Accessibility Statement At Firefly Family Dentistry , we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities. We strive to adhere to the best practices and standards defined by Section 508 of the U.S. Rehabilitation Act and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as established by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) . Our Accessibility Efforts: Text Resizing : We support text resizing in browsers to help users with visual impairments read content more easily.
  • Alt Text for Images : All images include alternative text descriptions, ensuring users with screen readers can understand visual content.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Our websites are designed to be fully navigable using a keyboard, making it accessible for users with motor disabilities.
  • Consistent Layouts : We use consistent layouts across our websites to help users easily navigate and understand content.
  • ARIA Landmarks : We implement ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) landmarks to provide better navigation for screen reader users.
  • Accessible Forms : Our forms are designed to be accessible, with clear labels and instructions.
  • Video and Audio Transcripts : We provide transcripts for all video and audio content, available upon request, to support users with hearing impairments.
  • Contrast and Color : We ensure sufficient color contrast between text and background, and avoid using color alone to convey information.
  • Ongoing Testing : We regularly test our websites for accessibility and continuously work to improve our digital experience.

Continuous Improvement:

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing process and are committed to continually improving our digital offerings. We regularly review our websites and update our practices to enhance accessibility.

Feedback and Assistance:

If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our website or need assistance, please contact us at info@gumlinemarketing.com with the URL of the page you are trying to access. We will do our best to provide you with the information you need and resolve any issues.

Thank you for visiting Firefly Family Dentistry . Your feedback is valuable in helping us make our websites more accessible to everyone.
