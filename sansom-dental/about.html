A Longstanding Dental Home in Millcreek

Sansom Dental is a family-owned practice built around steady, personal care for patients of every age.

Sansom Dental has served Millcreek and the Salt Lake Valley since 1952. That history matters to us because dentistry is personal: people want to know who is caring for them, where to go when something hurts, and whether the team will explain choices without pressure.

Today the practice is led by Dr. Kyle Sansom, DDS, who brings broad training in general dentistry, dental implants, IV sedation, esthetic dentistry, wisdom teeth surgery, and root canals. The office welcomes children, adults, and seniors, and many families choose Sansom Dental because they can keep preventive, restorative, cosmetic, and emergency care in one familiar place.

Our approach is simple: listen first, be clear about what we see, and help each patient understand practical next steps. Whether you are coming in for a routine cleaning, replacing a missing tooth, improving your smile, or calling because of a toothache, the goal is careful care that feels grounded and human.

The office is located at 2505 East 3300 South, Suite 101, in Millcreek, Utah. We are convenient to nearby Salt Lake City, Holladay, Sugar House, and surrounding Wasatch Front neighborhoods.
