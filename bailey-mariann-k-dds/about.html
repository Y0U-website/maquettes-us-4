About

Our Commitment “La Red” means “the network” in Spanish—a name that reflects our deep connection to the community and our collaborative approach to care. We work closely with hospitals, schools, nonprofits, and local agencies to address social determinants of health and advance health equity for all.

  • Women’s Health & Prenatal Services
  • Behavioral & Mental Health Services
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Medication Assistance & Care Coordination
  • Mobile Health Services
  • Insurance Enrollment & Social Support Services

Our History

  • Pre-2001 : Operated as a 24/7 telephone hotline connecting individuals with limited English proficiency to bilingual physicians offering discounted care.
  • 2001 : LRHC officially launched by a network of Sussex County physicians in partnership with a local hospital, community center, faith-based organization, and local government.
  • 2003 : Became an independent, federally and privately supported ambulatory care center.
  • 2004 : Designated as a Federally Qualified Health Center (FQHC) Look-Alike.
  • 2005-2006 : Received full FQHC status and began receiving federal funding.
  • 2009 : Introduced mental health services for patients over age 50.

Partners At La Red Health Center, we’re proud to work with many local partners to improve our services.
