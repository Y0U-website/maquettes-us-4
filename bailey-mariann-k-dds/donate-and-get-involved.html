Donate and Get Involved

Join La Red’s 2001 Club Honoring Our Roots. Investing in Our Future. In 2001, La Red Health Center was founded with a bold vision: to provide high-quality, compassionate, and accessible health care to everyone in Sussex County—no matter their income, background, or insurance status.

To honor our founding year and ensure continued access to care for years to come, we are proud to introduce the 2001 Club , a special giving society for La Red’s most dedicated supporters.

  • Sponsor a La Red Event Show your support and gain visibility in the community by sponsoring one of our annual fundraising or outreach events.
  • Host a Fundraiser Get creative! Whether it’s a birthday fundraiser, benefit concert, or workplace giving campaign, your efforts can make a real difference. We partner with the United Way and the Annual State Employee Charitable Giving Campaign to help make workplace giving even easier.
  • Leave a Legacy with a Planned Gift Talk to your attorney or financial adviser about including La Red Health Center in your will, trust, or other estate plans to create a lasting impact.
  • Donate Your Vehicle Have an old car you no longer need? Donate it through our vehicle donation program to support our mission. La Red partners with the nonprofit CARS (Charitable Adult Rides and Services) to make donating your vehicle simple and easy. Donate your vehicle today .
  • Recommend a Grant If you have a donor-advised fund, consider recommending La Red Health Center as a grantee to support accessible health care. Give directly from your donor-advised fund.
  • Shop for a Cause Purchase La Red Health Center merchandise through our Bonfire Shop — show your support and spread awareness wherever you go!

What Is the 2001 Club?

The 2001 Club recognizes donors who make an annual contribution of $2,001 or more to support our mission. Your commitment helps La Red expand access to critical services and improve health outcomes across our community.

Member Benefits

  • Recognition on our website, social media, and newsletter.
  • A photo and thank you mention in our Annual Report.
  • A press release announcing your inclusion in the 2001 Club.
  • A featured photo with La Red’s CEO and leadership team.
  • A personalized tour of La Red Health Center. See how your generosity is changing lives—up close and personal.

Why Join?

When you join the 2001 Club, you’re not just donating. You’re joining a movement for community health and healing that started in 2001 and continues to grow today.

Corporate Sponsorship Opportunities

Why your sponsorship matters

La Red Health Center is the place where Sussex County’s workforce goes to get well. We care for the essential workers who take care of all of us. They work in the hospitality industry, construction, agriculture, poultry factories, real estate, education and nearly every part of our economy. Without La Red’s patients’ contributions, Sussex County would not be one of the most sought-after destinations in the nation by residents or businesses.

They are the fabric of Sussex County. Your corporate support says that you value La Red’s patients but most of all you value Sussex County and the unique community we all have built here.

Partner with us to improve community health while elevating your brand.
