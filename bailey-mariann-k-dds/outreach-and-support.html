Outreach and Support

Connecting our community to care, wherever you are.

Everyone deserves access to quality health care, regardless of where they live or their circumstances. Our outreach and support programs extend beyond our clinic walls to meet people where they are—in their workplaces , communities , and neighborhoods. From our Mobile Health Unit that brings care directly to you, to our bilingual promotoras who break down language barriers, we&#x27;re committed to ensuring that distance, language, or life circumstances never stand in the way of getting the care you need.

Outreach and Support Programs

Promotora Program

Bilingual Community Health Workers and promotoras help patients access care and navigate language barriers.

Supporting individuals transitioning back into the community with health care access and resources.

Partnering with local businesses to provide health services and wellness programs for employees.

Workplace wellness programs designed to improve employee health and productivity.

Homeless Connection to Care

Connecting individuals experiencing homelessness with essential health services and resources.

Delaware WIC On-Site On-site WIC services providing nutrition education and support for women, infants, and children.

Produce RX Program

Partnering with local farms to provide patients in need with free, fresh fruits and vegetables.

Mobile Health Unit

Bringing accessible, affordable health care directly to Sussex County residents wherever they are.
