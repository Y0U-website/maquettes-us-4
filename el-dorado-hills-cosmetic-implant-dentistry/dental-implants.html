Are Implants Safe?

Absolutely! Dental implants have been in the game for decades and the technology has only improved. With the right care and a knowledgeable team like ours, they’re not only safe but can be a long-lasting solution to missing teeth.

Dental Implants Pros and Cons

Every option has its highs and lows. Here’s a quick look at implants:

Pros

  • Natural Look & Feel : They’re the next best thing to natural teeth.
  • Longevity : With proper care, they can last a lifetime.
  • Easy Maintenance : Brush and floss just like with natural teeth! No fancy cleaners or techniques are necessary.
  • Bone Health : Implants help prevent bone loss, which can occur with missing teeth.

Cons

  • Initial Cost : While upfront costs might be higher than other periodontic options, the long-term benefits can outweigh these initial expenses.
  • Surgery : Yes, it’s a minor procedure, but it’s surgery nonetheless.
  • Time : The full process, from implantation to crown placement, can span several months.

Are Implants Worth It?

Given the reviews and countless success stories, many find dental implants a game-changer. Think about it: a permanent solution that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth. While there are more affordable options, implants often stand out for their longevity and overall benefits to oral health.

The Implant Process

Curious about the process of getting one or more dental implants? Here’s a quick, generic overview:

  • Consultation : Your initial appointment with Dr. Jonah Tabrizi will focus on determining if dental implants are right for you. Let him know of your dental goals and you can explore your options together!
  • Procedure : On the day of the surgery, the implant will be placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing : Over the next several months, the implant will fuse with the jaw bone and create a stable foundation for your crown. You’ll be wearing a temporary crown during the healing process.
  • Final Crown : Once Dr. Tabrizi has determined your implants have properly fused, you’ll receive a permanent, customized crown that is shaped and painted to blend seamlessly with the rest of your teeth.
