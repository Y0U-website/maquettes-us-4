Meet the Team

Learn More About Dr. Zegeer

Dr. John Edward Zegeer grew up in Charleston’s East End and graduated from Charleston High School. He then obtained his undergraduate degree in Biology at West Virginia University in 1977. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the West Virginia University School of Dentistry in 1981.

With over 40 years of dental experience, Dr. Zegeer was first an associate dentist for 16 years until he established his practice in Kanawha City in 1998. Since that time, he has devotedly provided the community with quality dentistry that he so passionately enjoys.

Away from the office, Dr. Zegeer loves sports, hunting, fishing, camping, and most of all, his wife Sharon and children Drew, Devon, and Josh. He is a loving grandpa to three amazing children, twins Mac and David, and baby girl Della.

Learn More About Dr. Devon

Dr. Devon Nicole Zegeer is the daughter of Dr. John Zegeer. She graduated from the West Virginia University School of Dentistry in 2019.

Dr. Devon grew up in Charleston, and was delighted to come back home after being in Morgantown for nine years to do what she loves while supporting her local community. She has always been interested in the dental field and works diligently to be able to follow in her father’s footsteps. She plans to learn a great deal from Dr. Zegeer while teaching him a few new tricks along the way.

Dr. Devon hopes that she can one day earn the respect and loyalty that the practice’s amazing patients have imparted to her dad.

Dr. Devon is a die-hard WVU fan, music and movie enthusiast, and an avid puppy lover. She is a self-proclaimed foodie who loves trying new things and seeing new places. Becoming an aunt has been the most exciting aspect of her life in recent years.

Our Team

Meagan (Front Desk Coordinator)

Meagan began her dental career in 2008 when she graduated as a dental assistant from Putnam Career and Technical Center. From assisting to the front office, she has worn many hats in the dental field and has loved every one of them. She joined the Zegeer Dental team in 2020 as Front Desk Coordinator. Meagan is an expert in all things insurance and billing and is always willing to take time for her patients, whether it’s explaining an EOB or standing up to insurance companies. Getting to know her patients and making them smile is her favorite part of the job.

Meagan is a Putnam County native that has become a transplant resident of Rand, WV. She lives with her husband Micheal and three children, daughter Meme and sons Marley and Brandon. In her free time, you’ll find Meagan enjoying the mom life, spending time with her huge family (she is the oldest of four children), and traveling when she can. Meagan has an affinity for shoes and has more pairs than she cares to admit. She loves shopping and has a serious weakness for Starbucks, Target, and spaghetti.

Madi (Front Desk)

Madi graduated from Ross Medical Center’s Dental Assisting Program in March of 2021. Though she’s new to the front desk game, Madi is an exceptionally fast learner and is implementing her new knowledge each and every day. Starting as an assistant in the back, Madi has made her way to the front desk and has the general know-how to help with any questions you may have regarding anything in the office, from types of treatment to insurance info and more.

This Racine native can make any patient feel like she’s known them for a lifetime and her at-ease personality calms patients during their dental visits and her understanding nature is sure to make for a pleasant experience. You’ll be hard-pressed to find someone who Madi doesn’t know, and that, in turn, makes her feel like family.

Madi likes to spend her time at home with her two girls, brand new baby boy, and fur babies. She can binge-watch Netflix shows with the best of them and is a hardcore stan of the Croc.

Stephanie (Registered Dental Hygienist)

Stephanie is a graduate of West Liberty State College with an Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene. She joined Zegeer Dental in 2001 and is dedicated to providing excellent patient care and education. After over 20 years, she is now a part-time hygienist at our office, working on Fridays.

Stephanie is quick to make patients feel at ease by building confidence, trust, and relationships. She spends time getting to know every one of her patients to make their dental visit more like a sit-down with a friend. She knows Dr. Zegeer like the back of her hand, and has built a family with her patients here at Zegeer Dental.

Stephanie resides in Ripley with her husband John and two beautiful daughters Abigail and Jillian. In her free time, Stephanie enjoys traveling, concerts with her friends, trying new foods, and the Fourth of July.

Zoey (Registered Dental Hygienist)

Zoey grew up in Leewood, WV but now resides in Chesapeake with her dog, Twinkie, and cat, Snowstorm. She graduated from BridgeValley Community and Technical Center in 2020 with an Associate’s Degree in Dental Hygiene. While waiting to take her National Board Examination, Zoey worked as a dental assistant where she gained real-life experience in dentistry and more knowledge of the dental field, outside of just hygiene. This quality has made her a well-rounded asset to our practice.

Zoey always loved going to the dentist as a child, and that is when she realized she wanted to help people by educating and providing quality dental services to the people of her home state. She loves when patients leave the office with confidence in their smiles!

When not cleaning teeth, you’ll find Zoey out shopping, spending time with friends and family, hanging out at the local Mexican restaurant, and watching re-runs of The Golden Girls.
