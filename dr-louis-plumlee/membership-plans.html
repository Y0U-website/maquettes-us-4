Other Benefits

Members will receive a 12 % discount off any dental treatment when paying with cash or check or a 10 % discount when paying with credit or debit. Third-party financing may be used utilizing financing options available through Plumlee Dentistry. Due to outside financing merchant fees, this membership discount is 5 %.

This includes, but is not limited to, additional diagnostic X-rays or emergency exams, endodontics (root canals), fillings, tooth extractions, crowns and bridges, dentures, partials, implants, and periodontics (gum therapy). The discount for orthodontic treatment will be 10 % if paid in full with cash or check prior to starting the case.

The membership plan remains in effect for exactly one year after sign-up. It is non-refundable, and any unused services cannot be transferred to a future year, even upon renewal of the program.
