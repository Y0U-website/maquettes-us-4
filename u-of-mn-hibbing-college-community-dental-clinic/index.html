Minnesota North College has six campuses located in northeastern Minnesota. We are a fully accredited, award-winning college. We offer a variety of Associate Degrees, transfer pathways, and certificate programs to help you start your career in two years or less.

Our six campuses are located in Hibbing, Grand Rapids, Eveleth, Virginia, Ely, and International Falls. Want to learn remotely? We have that too.
