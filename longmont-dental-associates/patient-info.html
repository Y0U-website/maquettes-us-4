Patient Info

Our goal is to make sure you feel informed, comfortable, and confident in your care from the very first visit.

  • Review of your dental and medical history
  • Full set of digital X-rays (if needed)
  • Comprehensive oral exam with our caring dentists
  • Time to discuss your concerns, goals, and questions
  • Personalized treatment plan, if necessary
  • Clear communication about any next steps

Financing Options

If you have questions about coverage or payment options, our team is always happy to help.

  • We are in-network with Delta Dental Premier and Medicare Advantage Plus
  • We will bill to any insurance that allows out of network coverage
  • We’re happy to help verify your benefits before your appointment
  • We offer a flexible in-house savings plan for uninsured patients
  • CareCredit financing is accepted for patients who prefer to make monthly payments
  • Transparent pricing and no surprise fees
