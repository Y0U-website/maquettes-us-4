Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept dental insurance for implant restoration?

Yes, we work with most major insurance providers and help maximize your benefits. Our team handles claim submissions and provides detailed treatment estimates so you understand your coverage before starting treatment.

How long does the complete implant restoration process take?

The timeline varies based on individual healing and specific needs. Generally, the entire process from implant placement to final restoration takes four to eight months. We’ll provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Will my dental implant restoration require special care?

Your restored implant requires the same care as natural teeth: regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings. We’ll teach you proper maintenance techniques and monitor your oral health during routine visits to ensure long-term success. Watch for symptoms of damaged implants and contact us immediately if you experience any concerns.
