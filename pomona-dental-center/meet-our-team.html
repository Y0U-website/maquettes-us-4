Razmik Galustian, DDS

Periodontist & Implant Specialist

Dr. Razmik Galustian received his DDS degree from University of California Los Angeles, UCLA. He continued his education to achieve his specialty certificate in Periodontics and Dental Implants as well as his Master’s in Oral Biology from Temple University.

Dr. Galustian places a tremendous value in a courteous and gentle chair side manner in addressing all aspects of Periodontics including but not limited to surgical and non-surgical treatment of Periodontal diseases, surgically placement of Dental Implants, Root coverage and Gum grafting, Bone regeneration including Sinus augmentation, vertical and horizontal Ridge augmentation, Esthetic Periodontal surgeries and minimally invasive tooth Extractions.

Dr. Galustian is a Diplomat of International Congress of Oral Implantologists. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and San Fernando Valley Dental Society.

Dr. Galustian goal is to provide his patients with the highest quality, thorough and state of art Periodontal and Dental Implant therapy. We feel very fortunate to have him part of our team.

Rashid Kamdar, DDS

Orthodontist

Dr. Rashid Kamdar Graduated from UCLA Dental School at the top of his class with a degree of dental surgery (DDS). Then he moved to New York to attend a general practice residency at St. Barnabas Hospital in the Bronx. He then completed his Orthodontic specialty training at the UNLV School of Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics.

Dr. Kamdar is a Premier Invisalign Provider. He is also an active member of American Association of Orthodontics, American Dental Association and all of their constituent Societies. Speaking both Spanish and Farsi, we feel very fortunate that Dr. Kamdar joined our team.

Dr. Kamdar shares with Dr. Zahedi the same Cosmetic principles and techniques needed to achieve a great esthetic and functional orthodontic results. “I am very proud to work with Dr. Zahedi, helping to enhance the smiles and self esteem of so many people on a daily basis.” He loves meeting new patients and seeing the result of a beautiful smile.
