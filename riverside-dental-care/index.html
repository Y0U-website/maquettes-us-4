Dental Implants are a wonderful gift that research and technology has given our patients. Restoring your smile to its natural beauty is our goal. It allows us to replace lost and missing teeth in a way never seen by past generations.

If you have been diagnosed with obstructive sleep apnea and have trouble with your CPAP machine, we may have a solution. We make oral sleep apnea appliances that are very effective in treating most mild to moderate cases of OSA. They can also be used in conjunction with your CPAP to lower the air pressure and make the CPAP more comfortable.

Porcelain Veneers allow us to fix, reshape and change the color of broken, chipped or crooked teeth in 2 relatively close together visits. By hearing what your needs are and our experience of achieving smile makeovers, we can give you the gorgeous, straight, white smile you desire.

A root canal procedure is performed to remove decayed pulp from the central part of the tooth. We then reshape the canals and place a strengthening filler inside, preserving the tooth and retaining its original integrity; thereby, saving a tooth that in the past would have to have been pulled.
