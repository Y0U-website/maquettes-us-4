What to Expect During Invisalign Treatment

Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation where we take digital impressions and photographs of your teeth. We’ll discuss your concerns, smile goals, and answer any questions about the process. Once we create your custom treatment plan, you’ll receive your first sets of aligners along with detailed instructions.

You’ll wear your aligners for 20 to 22 hours each day, removing them only for eating, drinking anything other than water, and cleaning your teeth. Every one to two weeks, you’ll switch to the next set in your series. We’ll schedule periodic check-ins every six to eight weeks to monitor your progress and make sure everything is tracking as planned.

The experience is straightforward and fits easily into most lifestyles. Many patients appreciate that they can continue their normal routines without the adjustments and maintenance appointments that traditional braces require.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most patients complete treatment in 12 to 18 months, though your timeline depends on the complexity of your case. Some minor adjustments can be finished in as little as six months, while more involved cases may take up to two years. We’ll give you a personalized estimate during your initial consultation.

Is Invisalign painful?

You may feel mild pressure or discomfort when switching to a new set of aligners, which is actually a sign the treatment is working. Most patients describe this as temporary tightness rather than pain. The smooth plastic material is much gentler on your mouth than metal brackets and wires.

Do you accept insurance for Invisalign?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Many policies cover Invisalign similarly to traditional braces, typically providing partial coverage. We’ll verify your benefits and provide a detailed cost breakdown before you begin treatment.
