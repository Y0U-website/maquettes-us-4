Dr. Feliciano is known for his welcoming personality and making his patients feel at home. He takes great pride in his work and puts his best foot forward for each patient he encounters.

Ysabel

Ysabel loves connecting with patients and making sure everyone feels comfortable, welcomed, and taken care of from the moment they arrive. Whether She’s helping with scheduling, answering questions, or just sharing a smile, her goal is to make your visit as easy and stress-free as possible. Inez

Inez’s journey into dentistry began with her background in cosmetology. Her passion for beauty naturally led her to the cosmetic side of the dental field where She found the perfect blend of both beauty & health care.

She loves being a part of the process from start to finish. Helping transform smiles, and seeing the confidence that comes with the final results, is the most rewarding part of what she does.

jacqueline

Registered Dental Assistant - Extended Functions

Outside of her thriving professional life, Jacqueline balances a rewarding family life. She has been happily married for eight years and is the proud mother of three children.
