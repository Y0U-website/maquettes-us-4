Restorative Dentistry

At Justice Family Dentistry in Statesville, NC, Restorative Dentistry is a cornerstone of our practice, aimed at repairing and rebuilding damaged or missing teeth to restore both function and aesthetics. Our skilled dental team utilizes advanced techniques and materials to address a wide range of dental issues, including cavities, fractures, infections, and tooth loss. Whether you need a simple filling, dental crown, bridge, or implant, we tailor each treatment to meet your individual needs and goals.

Fillings

At Justice Family Dentistry in Statesville, NC, we offer expert fillings to repair and restore teeth affected by cavities or minor damage. Using durable and tooth-colored composite materials, our skilled dental team ensures seamless integration with your natural teeth for a flawless smile. Whether it’s a small cavity or a larger area of decay, our precise and gentle approach ensures optimal comfort and long-lasting results.

Partial Dentures

At Justice Family Dentistry in Statesville, NC, Partial Dentures offer a reliable and comfortable solution for replacing multiple missing teeth. These removable dental appliances are custom-made to fit snugly in your mouth, restoring function and aesthetics while filling in gaps caused by missing teeth. Crafted from durable materials and designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, Partial Dentures provide stability and comfort for everyday activities like eating and speaking.

Full Dentures

At Justice Family Dentistry in Statesville, NC, Full Dentures offer a comprehensive solution for replacing a full arch of missing teeth. These custom-made dental prosthetics are meticulously crafted to fit comfortably and securely in your mouth, restoring both function and aesthetics. Full Dentures not only enable you to chew, speak, and smile with confidence but also help support facial muscles and prevent bone loss in the jaw.

Endodontics

Endodontics focuses on saving natural teeth by treating the innermost layer, known as the dental pulp. Our skilled endodontists perform procedures such as root canal therapy to alleviate pain, remove infected tissue, and preserve the tooth’s structure. Using advanced techniques and technologies, we ensure a comfortable and effective treatment experience. Whether it’s relieving toothaches or addressing dental trauma, you can trust Justice Family Dentistry for expert Endodontic care that prioritizes your comfort and oral health.

Oral Surgery

Oral Surgery encompasses a range of procedures aimed at addressing complex dental issues with precision and expertise. Our skilled oral surgeons perform procedures such as tooth extractions, dental implant placements, and bone grafting to restore oral health and function. With advanced techniques and compassionate care, we prioritize patient comfort and safety throughout every procedure. Whether you require wisdom teeth removal or corrective jaw surgery, you can trust Justice Family Dentistry for expert Oral Surgery that ensures optimal outcomes and peace of mind.
