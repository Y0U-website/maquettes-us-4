Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers offer a transformative solution for enhancing your smile. These ultra-thin, custom-crafted shells are bonded to the front surface of your teeth, instantly correcting imperfections such as discoloration, chips, cracks, and minor misalignments. Our skilled dental team meticulously designs and applies each veneer to ensure a natural-looking and durable result that complements your facial features and smile aesthetics. With porcelain veneers from Justice Family Dentistry, you can achieve a radiant and confident smile that you’ll be proud to show off.

Minimal-Prep Veneers

At Justice Family Dentistry in Statesville, NC, we offer Minimal-Prep Veneers as a conservative and effective solution for enhancing your smile. These ultra-thin porcelain veneers require minimal to no alteration of your natural tooth structure, making them an ideal choice for patients seeking a less invasive cosmetic option. With Minimal-Prep Veneers, you can achieve a beautiful, symmetrical smile while preserving the integrity of your teeth. Our experienced dental team customizes each veneer to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth, providing a natural and radiant appearance.

Composite Bonding

At Justice Family Dentistry in Statesville, NC, Composite Bonding is a versatile and minimally invasive cosmetic dental procedure that can transform your smile in just one visit. Using a tooth-colored composite resin material, our skilled dental team can repair chipped, cracked, or discolored teeth, fill in gaps between teeth, and even reshape irregularly shaped teeth. The composite resin is carefully applied, sculpted, and hardened to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, creating a beautifully restored smile.

Teeth Recontouring

Teeth Recontouring is a cosmetic dental procedure designed to reshape and refine the appearance of your teeth. Using advanced techniques, our skilled dental team gently removes small amounts of enamel to correct imperfections such as uneven tooth lengths, minor chips, or rough edges. This minimally invasive procedure can create a more harmonious and balanced smile, enhancing your overall facial aesthetics. With our precision and expertise, you can trust Justice Family Dentistry to deliver Teeth Recontouring that results in a confident and radiant smile you’ll love to share.
