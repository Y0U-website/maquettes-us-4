Dr. Greg Werner

Orthodontist

Dr. Werner grew up in Greenfield and graduated from Mount Vernon High School and DePauw University. After college, he played football professionally with the New York Jets and the Philadelphia Eagles. Following a brief career in the pharmaceutical industry, he followed the advice of his father-in-law, Dr. Richard Cowan, and enrolled at Indiana University School of Dentistry, where he graduated with high distinction and received many academic honors. Following two years of post-doctoral study, he received a Certificate in Orthodontics and a Master of Science in Dentistry.

Dr. Werner and his wife Nancy live in Hamilton County and have four children and a son-in-law — Ellen, Dan, Pete, Tom and Zak.

Dr. Werner is a member of the following professional organizations:

Indiana Association of Orthodontists (Board Member)

Indiana University School of Dentistry Alumni Association (Board Member)

Indiana University Orthodontic Alumni Association (President 2004-2005)

American and Indiana Dental Associations

Dr. Michael Myers

Orthodontist

Dr. Michael Myers is a Hoosier through and through. He was born and raised in Fishers, Indiana and attended Indiana University in Bloomington before he continued his education at Indiana University Dental School, where he graduated with High Distinction Honors. He continued his professional education at Indiana University’s Department of Orthodontics and Oral Facial Genetics, graduating in July of 2024. When he’s not at work, Dr. Myers and his wife, Kennedy, enjoy traveling, attending Indy sporting events, and, of course, golfing!

Fun fact: Dr. Myer’s wife had her orthodontic care completed at Werner Orthodontics 15 years ago!
