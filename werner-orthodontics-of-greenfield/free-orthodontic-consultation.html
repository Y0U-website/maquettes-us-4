What To Expect During Your Orthodontic Consultation

During your consultation we will discuss treatment options, time frames, and financial arrangements. We promise that you will leave the office with a clear understanding of you or your child’s needs, what the braces or Invisalign® treatment will consist of, and how long it will take.
