Our Practice

Serving Orrville with Honest, Family Focused Dentistry

We aim to educate our patients in a safe environment while offering them necessary comprehensive care. We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we created a calm environment and a scheduling system that serves you in a timely fashion. By combining our expertise with honest service, we give our patients the best care possible.

Daniel Phillips, D.D.S.

Dr. Daniel Phillips is a native of Wadsworth, OH. After school, he attended The University of Akron and Wayne College, where he received his Bachelors of Science in Biology. He then proceeded to obtain his Doctorate in Dental Surgery at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where he graduated Magna Cum Laude. He and his wife currently live in Doylestown, OH with their two children. During his spare time, he enjoys playing music and serving at his local church.

Melissa Phillips , Dental Assistant

Growing up in Millersburg, Melissa started dental assisting in 2018. After obtaining her radiograph certification from the Dale Foundation, she has continued to excel in the field. Between scheduling patients and assisting the doctor, she loves her job. On her days off she enjoys walks outside and playing worship music at her church.

Tammy Lyons, RDH

A native of Massillon, Ohio, Tammy earned her Bachelors Degree in Dental Hygiene from West Liberty State College in 1985. Married to Dr. Lyons since 1987, they share three wonderful adult children. In her spare time, she enjoys coaching the swim teams for Orrville High School and the YMCA of Wayne County. She also works at Ramseyer Farms in the fall. Her latest passion is playing pickleball.

Cari Stamey, Front Desk

Cari is the first face you will be greeted by in the office. She loves people, animals, and all things outdoors. Her family farm has goats, chickens, ducks, and a squirrel. Her husband and children live together in Doylestown Ohio.

Emily Trivette , RDH

Emily has worked in dentistry her whole life, starting as a dental assistant, becoming a lab technician, and finally a dental hygienist. She pursued her Associates Degree in Dental Hygiene at Stark State. Outside of working , she enjoys spending time with her two boys.

Amanda Fuller , Dental Assistant

Having grown up in Dalton, Amanda wanted to continue to serve the people of her community. In 1999, she started as a dental assistant for Dr. Lyons. Her love for families has allowed her to serve the community of Orrville since then. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her husband, son, and daughter.
