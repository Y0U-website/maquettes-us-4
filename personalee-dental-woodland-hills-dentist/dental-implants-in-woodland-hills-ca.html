Comprehensive Evaluation and Imaging

Dr. James Lee, DDS, begins your dental implant consultation with thorough dental examinations, including advanced 3D imaging and X-rays to assess your bone density and oral health. This detailed evaluation helps determine whether you’re a candidate for All-On-4 dental implants or traditional single-tooth replacement options. Your dental professional will discuss your medical history, current medications, and any concerns you have about the procedure to ensure patient comfort and optimal outcomes.

Personalized Surgical Planning

Based on your diagnostic results, Dr. Lee creates a customized treatment plan that maps the precise angle and position for each titanium post. For full arch restoration cases, he strategically plans the placement of four implants per arch to maximize bone contact and stability. Your dental team explains every detail of the upcoming dental treatments, including the timeline, sedation options, and what to expect during recovery, so you feel fully prepared.

Same-Day Implant Surgery

On your procedure day, Dr. Lee prioritizes patient comfort by administering local anesthesia and sedation appropriate for your needs. He carefully places the four titanium posts at specific angles—two straight in the front and two angled in the back—to create a stable foundation without requiring bone grafting. After securing the implants, Dr. Lee often attaches a temporary set of teeth the same day, allowing you to leave our dental clinic with a functional smile while your implants integrate with your jawbone.

Healing and Integration Period

Over the next several months, your titanium posts bond with your jawbone through osseointegration, creating a permanent foundation for your final restoration. During this crucial phase, you’ll maintain excellent dental hygiene by following Dr. Lee’s specific care instructions and attending regular dental visits for progress monitoring. Your dental team remains available to address any questions and ensure your tissue health is progressing properly throughout the healing process.

Permanent Restoration Placement

Once Dr. James Lee, DDS, confirms complete integration through dental examinations, you’ll return to PersonaLee Dental for your final restoration. He removes the temporary teeth and attaches your custom-crafted permanent crowns or bridge, ensuring a perfect fit, function, and natural appearance. Your dental professional provides detailed guidance on maintaining your new smile through proper dental care and schedules regular follow-up dental visits to protect your long-term investment in optimal oral health.

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lifts

Bone grafting and sinus lifts are essential components of our comprehensive dental implant services. The purpose of bone grafting is to strengthen the jawbone by adding bone material in areas where it may be lacking, creating a stable foundation for dental implants.

On the other hand, sinus lifts are performed when there is insufficient space between the upper jaw and sinuses for implants to be placed. By lifting the sinus membrane and placing bone material underneath, we can create enough space for successful implant placement.

These procedures ensure long-lasting and secure dental implants that improve oral health and restore smiles.

FAQs

What makes All-On-4 different from traditional dental implants?

All-On-4 dental implants use only four strategically angled titanium posts per arch to support a full set of replacement teeth, while traditional methods may require six to eight implants. This streamlined approach often allows for same-day tooth replacement and eliminates the need for bone grafting in many cases. The angled placement of the posterior implants maximizes your existing bone structure, making full arch restoration more accessible and efficient.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth and adequate bone density make excellent candidates for dental implants, though your dental professional will evaluate your specific situation during a consultation. Good oral health, non-smoking status, and commitment to proper dental hygiene improve your chances for successful implant placement and long-term results. Even patients who’ve been told they lack sufficient bone may qualify for All-On-4 treatment due to its unique angled implant design.

How soon can I eat normally after getting dental implants?

With All-On-4 dental implants, you’ll receive temporary teeth on the same day as your surgery, allowing you to eat soft foods almost immediately. During the healing period, your dental team will provide specific dietary guidelines to protect your implants while they integrate with your bone. Once your permanent restoration is placed and fully healed, you can enjoy all your favorite foods with the same confidence as natural teeth.

Will my dental implants look natural?

Your replacement teeth are custom-designed to match the shape, size, and color of natural teeth, creating a seamless appearance that blends beautifully with your smile. Dr. Lee works closely with skilled dental technicians to ensure your restoration complements your facial features and looks authentically your own. Most people won’t be able to tell you have dental implants unless you choose to share that information.

How do I maintain my dental implants after treatment?

Maintaining dental implants requires the same dental care routine as natural teeth—brushing twice daily, flossing regularly, and attending scheduled dental visits for professional cleanings. Your dental practice will provide specific instructions for cleaning around your implant sites to ensure optimal tissue health. Regular dental examinations allow your dental professional to monitor your implants and address any concerns early, helping your investment last for decades.
