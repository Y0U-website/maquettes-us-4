Why Choose All-On-4 Dental Implants?

Traditional dental implants can be invasive and time-consuming, requiring multiple implants for each arch. All-On-4 dental implants offer a streamlined process with unique benefits:

  • Efficiency: Dr. Lee and his expert team tailor each implant to your needs, minimizing installation time.
  • Comfort: With just four implants per arch, discomfort and downtime are significantly reduced.
  • Same-Day Procedure: Dr. Lee often completes your implant procedure in a single day, ensuring a swift and convenient experience.

The All-On-4 Dental Implant Journey at PersonaLee Dental

Step 1: Consultation Your journey starts with a comprehensive consultation. Dr. Lee and his team conduct X-rays and an oral exam to determine your eligibility for All-On-4 implants.

Step 2: Surgery Upon confirmation, an appointment for surgery is scheduled. Dr. Lee ensures your comfort by administering local anesthesia and sedation. With precise incisions, he strategically places the implants required for your restoration.

Step 3: Healing After surgery, your mouth requires time to heal and integrate with the implants. This healing phase typically lasts several months.

Step 4: Final Restoration Once your implants have fully healed, you’ll return to Personalee Dental for the attachment of your permanent crowns. Your beautiful, functional smile is then revealed!
