Caring for Your Implant Crown

Once your implant crown is in place, caring for it is straightforward. Treat it the same way you would a real tooth: brush twice a day, floss daily, and keep up with your regular professional visits. Try not to chew on hard objects or use your teeth as tools, which helps protect the restoration over time.

One of the advantages of modern implant crowns is how naturally they integrate into your daily routine. There are no adhesives, no removal at night, and no dietary restrictions beyond those you would follow for your natural teeth.
