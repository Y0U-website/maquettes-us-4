What Should I Expect at My Implant-Supported Dentures Consultation?

At your initial implant-supported denture consultation at Newton Smile Centre , we will take X-rays , make impressions of your mouth, and perform other diagnostic tests to determine your jawbone health and overall oral health. All of our certif i ed dentists’ findings will determine your treatment plan.

What Does an Implant-Supported Denture Process Look Like?

Here are the 5 steps Newton Smile Centre takes with implant-supported dentures:

1. Treatment plan acceptance

As your dentist in Newton Centre, we will discuss our findings of your initial appointment and the options we recommend for treatment. All of your questions will be answered. A treatment plan will be developed and agreed upon. At Newton Smile Centre, you always have a voice and we will listen.

2. Preparation

If you have teeth that are to be removed before your implant-supported dentures are placed, we will book a convenient time for you. Your comfort is always our number-one focus.

3. Placement of Dental Implants

We will schedule an appointment to place the implants that will support your dentures. Meanwhile, your implants are being made in a laboratory by specialists who are technicians and artists – your new teeth will be gorgeous! Talk to us about wearing temporary dentures until your implant-supported dentures are ready for placement.

4. Healing Process

This is the period of time where the implants fuse to the jawbone, which may take several months before we can move forward with the implant-supported denture placement.

5. Placement of your implant-supported dentures

Your beautiful, new, fully functioning denture will be affixed to the implants. Our dentists will adjust the dentures accordingly for comfort.

How long does it take to get implant-supported dentures?

From the initial consultation to the end of the procedure, it can be roughly a 3-month process.

How many implant-supported denture appointments will I need?

Since implant-supported dentures are a permanent solution for missing teeth, they become a custom-fitted part of your mouth. To ensure a perfect prosthetic, we take several intricate steps. Depending on your treatment plan, the time required may span several appointments over the course of several months.

Are implant-supported dentures painful?

You may experience discomfort and swelling for a few days after the procedure, but the implants themselves are not painful to have once you are used to them.

How Long Do Implant-Supported Dentures Last?

With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last as long as 20 years. The denture part may need to be replaced within 10 to 15 years.

Does insurance cover implant-supported dentures?

Most insurances do not cover implant-supported denture procedures.
