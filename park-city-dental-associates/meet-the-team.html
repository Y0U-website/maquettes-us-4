Dr. Brandon McKee

Dr. Nykamp

I was born and raised in Idaho Falls, Idaho. At the young age of four, I made up my mind that I wanted to be a dentist and never lost sight of that goal. After finishing high school, I served a two-year mission for my church in New Mexico and then subsequently graduated from BYU-Idaho with a degree in Exercise Physiology. My dental training was completed at Midwestern University-Arizona, where I graduated with honors at the top of my class (top 10 % in a class of 147 students).

Only a week after graduating from dental school, I married my sweetheart Kate and together we moved to Rio Rancho, New Mexico to start both my career… and a family! We have a little girl and all love spending time together regardless of the activity, whether fishing, hiking, or taking a spontaneous trip to Disneyland.

In Rio Rancho over the last few years I was able to gain valuable experience in all facets of dentistry, including fillings, crown & bridge, partials, dentures, Invisalign, and implants. I’m proud to bring that experience to the people of Park City and am committed to nothing less than excellence.

I truly love what I do as a dentist. It’s incredibly rewarding to restore broken, damaged, or decayed teeth and help people enjoy a beautiful smile. I have a lot of passion for my profession and am always seeking to hone my skills and perfect my craft. Perhaps the coolest thing about my work, however, is getting to know so many amazing patients with stories of their own. I am truly fortunate to have that opportunity every day!
