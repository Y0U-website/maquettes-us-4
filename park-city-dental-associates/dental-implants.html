Dental Implants

  • You can brush your teeth as normal—no need to take them out to clean them or soak them overnight.
  • When you care well for your implants, they can last the rest of your life.
  • You can pick if your new tooth or teeth are fixed or removable.
  • The new teeth are not anchored to your existing teeth, which is better for their long-term health and resiliency.

Bone Grafting

Depending on your particular case and the condition of your jaw, the dentist may recommend bone grafting. We will be able to determine this after removing your damaged tooth or teeth and examining your jaw. If it is needed, we will take bone from another part of your body and graft it into your existing jawbone. Over time, the bone will fuse together and become one solid foundation for the implant.

We invite you to reach out today to our professional team in our Park City, UT, office. They can set up a consultation appointment that is at a time that is convenient for you. Together, you can determine what choice is best for you and examine what the dental implant process could look like for you.
