Orthodontics is commonly thought to be only for children. This is a misconception. Orthodontic treatment can be successful at any age and adults especially appreciate the benefits…

The practice

One of every five patients in orthodontic treatment is an adult patient.

Crooked Teeth: Crooked or misaligned teeth can make it harder to brush and floss adequately, which can lead to tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health issues. Straightening your teeth with adult braces can also improve your smile’s appearance and boost your confidence.

Bite Problems: Bite problems can lead to jaw pain, chewing difficulties, speech problems, and other serious dental issues. Adult orthodontic treatment can correct these problems and help your jaw function properly.

Tooth Loss: Tooth loss can cause the remaining teeth to shift out of position. Orthodontic treatment can help move them back into their correct places and improve chewing function.

Overcrowding: Overcrowding refers to having too many teeth in too little space. This can make it harder to clean your teeth and increase the risk of tooth decay and gum disease. Orthodontic treatment can create more space by gradually moving the teeth into their correct positions.

Gum Disease: Gum disease can cause your teeth to become loose, which can lead to tooth loss. Orthodontic treatment can help realign your teeth and make brushing and flossing easier, reducing the risk of gum disease and other oral health problems.
