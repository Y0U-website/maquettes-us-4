Limited treatment or appliance only Various orthodontic appliances could include, but are not limited to, removable retainers, fixed retainers, Hawley retainers with bite plates, space maintainers, traditional expanders, or Invisalign expanders.

Touch-up treatment involves mild tooth movement for our previous patients who were treated by Dr. Liu, Dr. Wallen, or Dr. Peyton. This very simple orthodontic care treatment is designed to restore alignment after treatment if patients did not wear their retainers regularly. The treatment fee will depend on the duration of treatment with traditional braces or the number of aligners required.

Phase I or early treatment For kids with severe bite issues or crowding, we recommend addressing these concerns sooner to prevent worsening conditions. This phase I treatment, often referred to as early orthodontic care, may include options such as an expander, partial braces, elastics, a bite correction appliance, or Invisalign with or without bite blocks. An additional fee may apply for Invisalign treatment.

Simple treatment For patients who have undergone orthodontic care with braces or Invisalign treatment, or for those needing single arch treatment, we offer significant discounts to help restore their perfect smile. Please note that this treatment does not involve bite correction, and the back teeth are usually in good positions. Both traditional braces and Invisalign can be utilized for this treatment, with Invisalign typically recommended in most cases. Additionally, an extra fee may apply for Invisalign treatment.

Comprehensive Treatment Comprehensive orthodontic care for teens or adults includes straightening teeth, correcting bite issues, and improving facial profile. These treatment strategies can involve traditional braces, Invisalign, expanders, bite correctors, elastics, closing space after tooth extraction, or opening space for dental implants. The primary goal is to establish a perfect smile with an ideal relationship. An additional fee will apply when using Invisalign.

Complicated Treatment Complicated cases in orthodontic care encompass all the conditions outlined in comprehensive treatment, including extensive treatment time, the insertion of mini-screws, or jaw correction surgery. Additionally, an extra fee will apply when using Invisalign.
