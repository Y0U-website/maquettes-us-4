Children’s Dental Care

Children’s Dentistry at Kraner Family Dentistry

Gentle, Fun, and Focused on Healthy Smiles for Life

At Kraner Family Dentistry, we love caring for our youngest patients! Our team understands that a child’s early dental experiences can shape how they feel about the dentist for years to come. That’s why we go the extra mile to make visits comfortable, educational, and even fun.

We provide comprehensive children’s dental care, from first visits and cleanings to fluoride treatments and cavity prevention, we provide personalized care designed for growing smiles. We use age-appropriate language and a calm, encouraging approach to help kids feel safe and confident in the dental chair.

  • First dental visits (recommended by age 2 or sooner if you have a concern)
  • Gentle cleanings and exams
  • Preventive sealants and fluoride treatments
  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Growth and development monitoring
  • Guidance for parents on brushing, flossing, diet, and habits like thumb-sucking
