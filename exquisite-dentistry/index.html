The practice

Dr. Sanjay Talluri built Exquisite Dentistry around a simple belief: cavities are not an inevitable part of life. Early in his career, he noticed patients returning again and again for the same recurring issues, an experience that shaped the prevention-first philosophy guiding his practice today.

At Exquisite Dentistry, we believe the best dental visit is the one where there is nothing to fix. Dr. Talluri takes a conservative approach built on identifying problems before they require procedures, and helping patients understand not just what happened to their teeth, but why, so it does not happen again.
