The practice

Here at Your Caring Dentist, we know a healthy and happy lifestyle involves total body healthcare. Oral health affects the entire body, and without proper treatment, it will have a ripple effect. Your Caring Dentist is a holistic dental office. We work with patients and, sometimes, their primary care doctors to improve their overall health alongside their oral health.

We also believe in being on the cutting edge of dentistry. That’s why you will find the latest and greatest technology in our office. Additionally, our team is always looking for advanced training classes and additional opportunities to learn. The more we learn and adapt to technology, the better experience we can give our patients. Keeping up with technology means we can save our patients time…

As a complete health dental office, we recognize that oral health is part of your overall wellbeing. We work to improve your smile as well as your overall health. Our team takes a holistic and preventative approach and we use biocompatible and non-toxic materials. These give you all-natural long-lasting results.

We make sure our office meets the highest levels of sterilization. We do this for the protection of both our patients and our team. We also use UV air disinfection, HEPA NASA-grade filtration, and a state-of-the-art extraoral dental suction system

Our team is genuinely compassionate and empathetic, especially towards those with dental fear. You are more than just a number here; you are family. You will never feel like just another patient at Your Caring Dentist.

We stay ahead of the curve by using the latest technology with our patients. We use technologies like lasers, iTero 3D scanner, Extra-oral Dental Suction, CariVu, VELscope and more to provide our patients with an earlier and more accurate diagnosis of dental issues like cavities and oral cancer. An earlier diagnosis leads to less invasive procedures which save you time and money.

Great dental care doesn’t have to be unaffordable. To make our high level of care accessible, we offer a variety of flexible payment options such as Credit Card, Care Credit, Denefits, Lending Point, and Green Sky. These options help make our high-quality dentistry affordable to all patients

Are you hesitant about making that first dental appointment? Whether it’s your child’s introduction to dentistry, or you put off going to the dentist because you’re nervous, you probably know it’s time.
