Common Questions Answered Directly

Where did Dr. Ford go to dental school?

Dr. Ford earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. Before that, he completed his undergraduate studies at Hope College in Michigan, where he was also a member of the football team.

What advanced training does Dr. Ford have?

Dr. Ford has completed extensive postdoctoral training through Spear Education, The Pankey Institute, and the Kois Center, along with hands-on programs in no-preparation veneer techniques with Dr. Dennis Wells. His aesthetic treatment planning is influenced by his completion of the DSD Residency at Digital Smile Design. He continues to pursue advanced education with leading clinicians worldwide across cosmetic, restorative, and implant dentistry.
