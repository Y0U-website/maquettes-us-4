Benefits of Dental Implants in Gresham, OR

The Benefits of Dental Implants

A long-term solution for tooth replacement.

While more traditional options, such as removable dentures and bridges, must be replaced every 5 years, a dental implant can last a lifetime if properly cared for. Patients with dental implants can expect a high long-term success rate for their new teeth.

Protects healthy teeth and bone.

With dental implants, there is no need to grind down healthy teeth to support a bridge. Implants keep adjacent teeth from shifting, a common occurrence when a tooth is missing for an extended period of time. Dental implants also help to prevent jaw bone deterioration that commonly occurs when teeth are lost.

Most similar prosthesis to healthy, natural teeth.

An implant fits into your mouth similar to a natural tooth. Unlike other restorative solutions that merely substitute for missing teeth, dental implants are true tooth replacements, allowing your replacement tooth to function just like a natural one.

Easier speaking, eating, and maintenance.

You can enjoy chewing and biting into your favorite foods while maintaining the health of your surrounding teeth. Brush, floss, and care for them just like the rest of the teeth in your mouth.

Retain natural facial aesthetics.

Removable dentures or tooth-supported bridges may fall out or loosen during your day-to-day activities. Meanwhile, dental implant patients can smile confidently, knowing that their prosthetic teeth look and function like their healthy natural teeth would have. Dental implants also prevent facial features to sag as a result of tooth loss.

Why Replace Missing Teeth?

Adult teeth are meant to last a lifetime, but when periodontal disease, trauma, or other diseases strike, they may need to be removed or fall out on their own. The oral surgeons at Beacon Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeons offer solutions for patients with one or more missing teeth, so that our patients can return to a healthy and confident smile.

A tooth’s root stimulates the jaw bone, keeping it strong and healthy. Without a tooth, the jaw bone does not get stimulated in that area and weakens over time. This may cause nearby teeth to shift into the empty spot or cause facial features to sag. Dental implants recreate the entire structure of a natural tooth, including the root, and provide the proper stimulation to keep the jaw bone healthy.

Types of Dental Implants in Gresham, OR

  • Single Implants. One dental implant can replace one missing tooth.
  • Immediate Implants. Your surgeon can place an implant post on the same day as a tooth extraction, reducing the number of appointments needed to replace the missing tooth.

Types of Anesthesia

We offer several options for anesthesia to make your experience as comfortable as possible.

Local Anesthesia

A local anesthetic is administered to the surgical site to numb the area and can be combined with other anesthetic options.

Nitrous Oxide ✕ Nitrous Oxide Also known as laughing gas, this is inhaled through a mask and makes the patient feel relaxed. It also provides mild pain relief.

General ✕ General Anesthesia When you undergo general anesthesia, you will fall asleep quickly and wake up quickly without the extended drowsiness or nausea commonly experienced with general anesthesia in the past. We match your anesthetic duration to your procedure so that you can have a quick recovery in the office and return to the comfort of your home.

Pediatric ✕ Pediatric Anesthesia Dr. Lieblick and Dr. Rehrer are extensively trained in pediatric anesthesia and are Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) certified for your child’s safety. For children who are afraid of undergoing surgery, we incorporate techniques for anesthesia induction that require no needles. After your child is comfortably asleep, we insert an IV that your child will never see or feel. A positive experience in this way will help develop your child’s confidence in healthcare procedures that can last a lifetime.
