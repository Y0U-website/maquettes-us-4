How do I take exceptional care of my dental implants?

Dental implants should be cared for like the rest of your smile. Your teeth still need to be brushed and flossed on a daily basis, so don’t think you can skip your important dental routine just because of your new smile. Don’t be afraid to ask us about the best kind of toothbrush and toothpaste for your teeth. There are certain brushes and floss that can help clean hard-to-reach areas of your mouth.

We also highly suggest quitting any unhealthy habits including tobacco, especially if that was one of the major reasons to getting dental implants. Smoking will weaken your bone structure and gums, as well as your natural teeth surrounding any of your implants, which can contribute to its failure. No matter that these new teeth aren’t natural – you still need to take care of them as if they are!

While your diet can go back to normal, you still want to make sure you’re not going out of your way to damage your implants. Try and avoid chewing on any hard foods, like candies and ice, since they can still break your crown and damage the way your smile looks. Your crown still needs to be created just like your natural teeth, so be aware of anything that can seriously damage both the implant and the rest of your smile.

And, as always, make sure you’re still visiting our office for check-ups! Your dental implants, while strong, are not invulnerable, so make sure you’re still scheduling your visits with us to check on them. During these times, we can also perform your cleanings and exams so your smile is in tip-top shape!
