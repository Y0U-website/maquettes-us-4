Mini Implant Benefits

Mini dental implants have many benefits, making them an excellent first-choice treatment for many individuals.

Small size and simple design: A key benefit of mini dental implants is their small size. Mini implants are less than 3 millimeters in diameter, making them half the size of bulky traditional implants. Unlike conventional implants, which have multiple components, mini implants boast a streamlined design. The single post contains the screw and a built-in connector piece that attaches directly to the artificial tooth. Conversely, a traditional implant has a separate connector piece, complicating the placement procedure.

Minimally invasive placement: Mini dental implants can be placed in one minimally invasive procedure. For those with dental anxiety, this is a significant benefit. With his expert technique, Dr. Apel can place a mini implant through the gums without making an incision. This minimizes discomfort after the procedure and allows you to recover quickly.

Instant results: Because of their size and design, conventional implants require a multi-stage placement process that extends between 6 months and a year, depending on whether bone grafting is needed. The single-stage placement procedure of mini implants is a significant advantage for those who want timely results. With mini implants, we can place the implants and mount a prosthesis in just one visit. You will walk into the office with missing teeth and walk out with a functional smile.

Quick healing: Because their size necessitates only minimally invasive surgery, you can expect a short healing period. Patients can often resume their normal daily activities in as little as 24-48 hours. Many working adults like that they don’t have to take several days off from work when they get mini dental implants.

Long-lasting: Made from durable titanium, mini implants will last for years and years. Titanium resists corrosion and can handle even the strongest bite forces. This biocompatible material is also well tolerated by the tissues in your body, which allows your implant to integrate seamlessly with your jawbone, providing exceptional stability. With proper care, your mini implants can last a lifetime.

Budget-friendly: Compared to traditional implants, mini implants are a more cost-effective solution for replacing missing teeth. Because of their small size, simple design, and efficient placement, mini implants cut the cost of traditional implants by half. However, if you need financial assistance, we partner with multiple third-party lenders to leverage the best possible rates for your dental care. With different payment plans available, you can make monthly payments that fit your budget.

The Mini Dental Implant Process

On the day of your placement procedure, we will first numb your gums with a local anesthetic to make you comfortable. To place the implant, Dr. Apel will drill a pin-sized pilot hole through the gum. He will then screw the implant into your jaw. No incisions or sutures are needed with mini implants.

After the implant has been secured and tightened, Dr. Apel will attach the artificial crown (or another prosthetic) with a rubber O-ring. Finally, he will take impressions so your custom crown can be fabricated in a dental lab. Ensuring the crown is made exactly to your unique specifications takes approximately 2-3 weeks. In the meantime, we will place a temporary crown to revitalize your smile.

What to Expect After a Mini Implant Is Placed

Though you won’t need to make any dietary changes once your permanent crown has been placed, we recommend a soft food diet as you recover. Eating foods like soup, applesauce, mashed potatoes, eggs, and pasta will ensure the implant heals properly. You should also avoid hard or chewy foods until your implants have healed so that you don’t accidentally loosen them.

If you experience discomfort after your procedure, ice or other cold compresses can help. While pain should diminish quickly in a day or two, taking over-the-counter pain relievers like ibuprofen can help in the interim. Additional aftercare instructions will be given to you before your procedure so that you understand exactly what you need to do to ensure a successful outcome.

Then, once your custom prosthetic is ready, we will place your permanent restoration, allowing you to regain full function of your bite so you can eat all your favorite foods.

Caring for Mini Dental Implants

Maintaining the integrity of your mini dental implants is simple.

Proper care for mini dental implants means following standard oral hygiene practices. Be sure to:

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristle toothbrush.
  • Use non-abrasive fluoride toothpaste. Whitening toothpastes are typically too abrasive.
  • Floss daily to remove plaque-causing bacteria and food debris. A water flosser is especially effective for those who have had gum disease.
  • Use an alcohol-free anti-microbial mouthwash to prevent bacteria buildup without irritating the implant site.
  • Wear a night guard if you grind your teeth or clench your jaw at night. These extreme forces can damage your mini implants.
  • Maintain routine six-month visits to Designing Smiles. At your routine checkups, we will evaluate the condition of your implants and replace your rubber O-ring as needed.
