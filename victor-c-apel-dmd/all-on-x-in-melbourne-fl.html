Who Is a Good Candidate?

With so many benefits of All on X, you may be wondering if you’re a good candidate. We’re happy to share that most patients are! Because we use mini and one-piece dental implants, many patients who don’t have enough bone for traditional two-piece implants can still qualify.

During your consultation, Dr. Apel will evaluate several factors that can impact your eligibility and long-term success:

No matter what, if you’re not happy with your smile, we can find a way to help. Even if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate for traditional implants, All on X may still be an option!

  • Bone volume and density: Even with moderate bone loss, you may qualify, as mini implants are smaller and can often be placed in the available bone.
  • Gum and oral health: Active gum disease or untreated infections should be managed before implant placement to reduce the risk of complications.
  • Overall health and healing ability: Conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes, autoimmune disorders, or certain medications can affect how well your body heals after surgery.
  • Lifestyle habits: Smoking significantly increases the risk of implant failure by impairing blood flow and bone healing, so we strongly recommend quitting before and after treatment.
  • Bite forces and habits: Heavy teeth grinding (bruxism) or a very strong bite may require additional planning or protective measures to protect your implants.
  • Commitment to aftercare: Long-term success depends on proper oral hygiene and routine professional cleanings.
