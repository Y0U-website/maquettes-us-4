Meet Dr. Jit Chan, DDS

More Topics

At the age of 26, Dr. Chan moved from China to Chicago, where he attended Northwestern University. He graduated as a Doctor of Dental Surgery in 1993, and in 1996, he opened his own dental office in the neighborhood of Little Village. Serving the Hispanic and Chinese communities.

For more than three decades, he has treated generations of families and gained his patients respect through his impeccable work technique and warm, individualized, patient attention. With many years of experience under his belt, Dr. Chan continues to keep up to date by actively being a part of the dental community and attending lectures and seminars in order to adapt to the newest technology in our office.
