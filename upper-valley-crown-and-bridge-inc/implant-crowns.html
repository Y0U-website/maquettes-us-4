What Is an Implant Crown?

An implant crown is the final restoration placed on top of a dental implant after the implant has integrated with the jawbone. Unlike traditional crowns that attach to a prepared natural tooth, implant crowns are secured to an implant abutment, which connects the prosthetic crown to the implant fixture placed in the bone. This structure allows the restoration to function like a natural tooth while preserving surrounding teeth and supporting long-term oral health.

Our Implant Crown Process

Upper Valley Dental Laboratory uses advanced digital technology to produce quality implant restorations.

Dentists send a traditional impression or digital scan along with case details, shade selection, and implant system information.

Our technicians convert the case into a digital 3D model using CAD software. This allows us to design the crown with ideal contours, occlusion, and emergence profile for the implant restoration.

The restoration is milled using high-precision equipment to ensure accurate margins and reliable structural integrity.

Our technicians refine the crown with detailed finishing, staining, and glazing so it blends naturally with the patient’s existing teeth.

Each crown undergoes a final inspection for fit, esthetics, and durability before being prepared for delivery.
