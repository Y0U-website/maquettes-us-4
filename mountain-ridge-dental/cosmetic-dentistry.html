Emergencies of many kinds happen to everyone, and dental injuries are not an exception. With any emergency, you can become stressed and worried. Call or text our dental office as soon as possible so we can help you get the needed treatment.

What to do in an emergency?

Dental emergencies are typically sudden and overwhelming, and we know it can be stressful figuring out where or when you will get in. Should you or a loved one experience dental pain, a broken tooth, or any other worrisome symptoms in your smile, here’s what you should do:

Call or text us right away. Our team will give you first-aid instructions for handling your situation and schedule an emergency appointment as soon as possible, often within the same day.

Get an emergency exam. As soon as you arrive, we will work quickly to evaluate your emergency and ease any severe pain you may be feeling.

Go over the findings and make a treatment plan. We will review our findings with you and fully explain the treatment options including the estimated length and price.

Get the professional dental care you need. Once a treatment plan is made, our team will get to work right away. Whether you need a filling, crown, root canal, or something else entirely, your health and comfort will be our top priority.
