Dental Services

Below are just some of the services we offer. We accept all insurances and want to work with you to help get the treatment you need. Before any treatment is performed, we give you a complete price and estimate of how much insurance should cover. We also have promotions going on all year that change frequently.

Accidents happen. If you are in pain or have a broken tooth, give us a call and 9/10 times we will see you same day. We understand dental emergencies happen and we want to be able to take care of your needs as soon as possible and get you out of pain or fixing cosmetic concerns.

We are passionate about helping people feel confident in their smile. This includes tooth shape, color, size, and overall appearance. Everyone deserves the smile of their dreams. Ask us about botox/laser therapy for gummy smiles, diastema closures (gaps between front teeth), veneers, crowns, and ortho options. We also do in office whitening and full mouth rehabs which involve changing the bite with severe grinding.

We are happy to help with any dental work that you need including fillings, crowns, root canals, nightguards/splints, deep cleanings, and bridges. If you don t see something on the list, please don t hesitate to call and ask.

We are happy to announce that we are certified to provide IV sedation to our patients. This makes extractions and implants easier and creates an amnesia effect. The benefits of IV vs Oral is the level of sedation and safety of patients. IV Sedation can also be great for patients who are anxious and just want to get their dental work done being completely relaxed.

We love to see all ages! We strive to build a relationship with our patients. We are accepting new patients. Right now we offer same day cleaning with a new patient exam.

Our office is comfortable with extractions, bone remodeling to help with fit of dentures, bone grafts to preserve bone for implants, and placement of implants. If you are scared of dentures and losing your teeth, we completely understand. We encourage you to come in so we can talk about different options and help make you feel comfortable regarding any treatment.
