Expert Dental Implants in Burleson, TX

At Dental Visions, our experienced dental implant dentist provides advanced solutions for replacing missing teeth, including single implants, implant-supported bridges, overdentures and full-mouth restorations. Whether you’re missing one tooth or several, we tailor your treatment for comfort, function and long-term success.

Implants preserve jawbone health, restore full chewing ability, and look and feel like natural teeth. If needed, we offer bone grafting to support implant placement. Sedation options—including oral, nitrous and IV—are available for a stress-free experience. Schedule your consultation today to explore the right implant option for your smile.

Process

Initial Consultation: We begin with a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays and a one-on-one conversation to understand your goals and map out a tailored implant plan.

Implant Placement: A biocompatible titanium post is precisely placed into the jawbone, serving as a stable foundation for your restoration.

Healing & Osseointegration: Over the next few months, the implant integrates naturally with your bone to ensure a secure, long-lasting fit.

Custom Restoration: A custom-made crown, bridge or denture is attached to your implant. Temporary options may be used during the fabrication process for uninterrupted function and aesthetics.

Next Steps

Follow-up Care: Scheduled visits allow us to monitor your healing progress, verify implant stability and ensure that surrounding tissues stay healthy.

Maintenance: You’ll receive clear guidance on daily care—like proper brushing, flossing and routine checkups—to help protect your implant and keep your smile in top shape.

FAQ

  • Are dental implants permanent? Dental implants are designed to be a permanent tooth replacement solution. With proper care and maintenance, they can last 25 years or more, often for a lifetime.
  • How long is the dental implant process? The dental implant process typically takes several months, ranging from 3 to 6 months or longer. This includes time for healing, implant integration with the jawbone (osseointegration), and placement of the final crown, bridge or denture.
  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? You may be a candidate for dental implants if you have one or more missing teeth, healthy gums and enough bone density to support an implant. A consultation with your dentist—including X-rays or scans—will help determine your eligibility and the best treatment approach.
