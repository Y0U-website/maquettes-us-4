The practice

Your first visit is an exciting time! It’s a chance for us to get acquainted and discuss the best treatment approach.

Say “goodbye” to the goop and relax while we take a digital scan of your teeth! Not only is a digital impression more comfortable, it’s also more accurate. In less than 5 minutes our scanner takes an incredibly detailed impression of your teeth and gums. Once the scan is complete, the 3D model of your mouth is used to diagnose and plan your individualized orthodontic treatment.

Upon completion of dental school, Dr. Smith pursued further training at the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston where she earned her Master of Science degree and specialty Certificate in Orthodontics. Immediately following residency, Dr. Smith gained board certification and became a diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics.

Dr. Smith’s passion is in helping others and truly caring about the patients that she is blessed to treat. Dr. Smith is also passionate about teaching and maintaining the standards of her profession.
