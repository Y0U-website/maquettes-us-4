Meet Dr. Heather Willis

Dr. Heather Willis .

While the practice keeps me and my husband busy (he’s the Business Manager), we are also part-time kid expeditors for our two active kids, Hannah and Quinn. When we’re not at a soccer, hockey or volleyball event, I try to squeeze in my activities, both personal and volunteer. I picked up cross-country skiing when I moved back to Alaska to keep me going during the long winter days and I jam in running, hiking and gardening in the summer when I’m not out on the pitch watching my kids.

I believe strongly in my profession and am heavily involved in the American Dental Association (ADA) . Having served in my local dental society as President and have made my way through nearly all of the officer positions in the Alaska Dental Society (ADS) , serving proudly as the ADS President 2013-2014 and currently I represent Alaska as a delegate to the ADA.

I continue to grow in my clinical knowledge an am active in a local Spear Education Study Club as well as attend an average of 50 hours of yearly continuing education. And finally, I feel that one of the blessing I have in this profession is to give back to my community and provide dental services that others cannot. My husband and I have chaired and organized two Alaska Mission of Mercy’s (AKMOM) which have provided nearly $1.5 million of dentistry to the Interior of Alaska.

Thanks for taking the time to get to know who you are trusting your and/or your family’s dental health with.

Hello, I’m Dr. Cristina Mondelli, and I’m excited to join the wonderful team at Spruce Roots Family Dentistry! I grew up in Fairbanks, Alaska, as part of a military family and have always felt a deep connection to this community. After earning my degree from the University of Alaska Fairbanks, I continued my education at Oregon Health and Science University (OHSU) in Portland, where I graduated with my Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) in 2024.

During my time at OHSU, I gained invaluable experience working in public health clinics in Roseburg and Enterprise, Oregon. I had the opportunity to focus on providing care to children and helping patients with dental anxiety feel more comfortable. I also volunteered at the Bridges Collaborative Care Clinic, where I provided free dental care to the homeless population of Multnomah County, further reinforcing my passion for preventive dental care and oral health for all.

As a member of the American Dental Association, I’m committed to helping my patients achieve and maintain excellent oral health. I believe that a healthy smile is an essential part of overall well-being, and I strive to make each patient feel comfortable and confident throughout their care.

Outside of the office, you’ll find me exploring the Alaskan wilderness, capturing its beauty through photography, or trying out new recipes in the kitchen. I also enjoy coaching youth soccer and spending time on the field with young athletes.

I’m looking forward to meeting you and being a part of your journey toward optimal dental health!

Warm regards, Dr. Cristina Mondelli Spruce Roots Family Dentistry
