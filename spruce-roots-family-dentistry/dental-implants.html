  • Do not hinder your ability to speak
  • Increase comfort
  • Do not hinder your ability to eat
  • Increase dental health
  • Are very durable and permanent
  • Can be cleaned just like your natural teeth
  • Are matched to the shade of your other teeth

Can Anyone Get Dental Implants ?

When you express an interest in the dental implants offered at Spruce Roots Family Dentistry, our dentists will have a consultation with you to determine whether or not you will be able to get the implants. In order to be able to get a dental implant, you need to have healthy gums and enough bone so the implant will fuse properly.

The success rate for those patients who are eligible to have dental implants in Fairbanks is very high, at 98 percent. As long as you follow the direction of our dentists and take proper care of your teeth, your dental implant will be everything you wanted it to be.
