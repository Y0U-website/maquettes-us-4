Dental Implant FAQs

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a permanent and highly durable solution for replacing a missing tooth. It consists of a titanium screw placed into the jawbone, followed by an abutment and a crown. The completed restoration looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth, making it one of the most advanced options available for tooth replacement.

How are dental implants different from dentures?

Dentures are removable appliances used to replace multiple teeth. They require special care, may shift during eating or speaking, and often take time to adjust to. Dental implants, however, are fixed in place and do not move. Because they are integrated into the jawbone, they feel stable and natural. Implants can also be cared for just like natural teeth, with daily brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits.

How much do implants cost?

The cost of dental implant treatment varies based on several factors, including the number of implants needed, the complexity of the procedure, and your insurance coverage. Every patient has unique needs, so we encourage scheduling a consultation for a personalized treatment plan and a detailed cost estimate. We can also discuss available financing options to help make treatment more accessible.

Do dental implants hurt?

During the procedure, we use a local anesthetic to ensure your comfort, so you should not feel pain while the implant is being placed. Some mild soreness afterward is normal and can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication. If you experience anxiety about dental procedures, we can also review sedation options to ensure a calm and comfortable experience.

How long do implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting solution, and with proper care, they can last a lifetime. The key to longevity is maintaining good oral hygiene and attending regular dental checkups. While the crown may eventually need replacement due to normal wear, the implant itself can remain strong and stable for decades.
