Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal therapy is a term that describes a wide range of services that focus on treating and preventing advanced gum disease . Proper periodontal care and treatment are important in maintaining the strength and proper function of your teeth because weak and diseased gums can lead to loss of teeth and bone structure.

Common periodontal therapies often involve deep cleanings, antimicrobial treatments, special prescriptions, and occasionally a referral to specialty care.

Evaluation begins with the identification and measurement of any recession of the gum line. Based on the level of periodontal disease, Dr. Coleman will develop and discuss with you a plan, both long and short to prevent further complications.

Think of your gums like the ground and soil beneath a tree. Just like a tree cannot thrive in bad soil, your teeth will not survive in toxic and diseased gums. Here at Coleman Dental, our dental hygienists specialize in periodontal therapy and deep cleanings so that your gums and bone stay healthy, ensuring long-lasting overall oral health and a beautiful, functioning smile.
