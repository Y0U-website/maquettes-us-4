Oral Exams Azle

Any medical checkup is vital for your health as it’s the doctor’s way of reviewing your current health status. The same thing is considered with an oral exam. It helps our dentist check for any dental problems you might have for the past few years or after your last visit and take specific measures to maintain your oral health.

Regular oral exams help protect not just your dental health but also your entire body’s health. We suggest oral exams to detect signs and symptoms of some systemic diseases like mouth cancer, lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, and diabetes, which might show up in your mouth first. Once our dentist finds indications of such disease, they will suggest you see a doctor timely.

Also, our oral exams offer an opportunity to provide suitable tips on taking care of your teeth, gums and detect dental health issues early, which they’re most treatable.

When it comes to your oral health, we believe a dentist is your ultimate best friend. You can clean your teeth and mouth easily at home, but it doesn’t mean that you have thoroughly cleaned and polished them. For extensive teeth cleaning and oral examination, schedule an oral exam with our reliable dentists. Allows us to help you keep your mouth in top shape with our across-the-board dental examination and checkups.

  • We may utilize an ultrasonic vibrating tool for shaking loose plaque and tartar. Later, it will be rinsed off with flowing water.
  • We polish your teeth using a lightly abrasive paste and wrap up with flossing.
  • Our dentist will next examine your teeth and mouth to detect any severe oral condition after the dental cleaning.
  • To check signs of decay, our dentist employs a metal probe, including a tiny mirror that comes with an angled handle.
  • To check signs of gum diseases, we check gums for swelling, redness, and depth of the gingival pockets.
  • We also find any signs of bruxism (teeth grinding) and TMJ issues.
