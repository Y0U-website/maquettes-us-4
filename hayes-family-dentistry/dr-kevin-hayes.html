About Dr. Kevin Hayes

Dr. Kevin Hayes is certified in oral and IV sedation dentistry. He has practiced in Cookeville, Tennessee, for over 40 years and is actively involved in the community through various public service initiatives. He is also a passionate sports enthusiast, particularly enjoying college basketball.

Background

Dr. Hayes grew up in a small town in southern Kentucky, where his parents still reside. He graduated from the University of Kentucky in Lexington, earning his Doctor of Medical Dentistry (DMD) degree in 1986.

Shortly after finishing dental school, Dr. Hayes moved to Cookeville and has been in private practice ever since. He is deeply committed to the community, where he lives with his two sons. He is actively involved in many local organizations and is the founder and coach of the Highland Knights, a boys’ basketball organization.
