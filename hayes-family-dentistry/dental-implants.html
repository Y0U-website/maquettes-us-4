What Are Dental Implants And How Do They Work?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone, acting as artificial roots for replacement teeth. Once the implant integrates with the bone, a custom crown is attached, providing a natural-looking, durable solution for missing teeth.

Who Is A Suitable Candidate For Dental Implants?

Ideal candidates have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone density to support the implants. Patients should also be committed to maintaining good oral hygiene and regular dental visits. A consultation at Hayes Family Dentistry will help determine whether implants are the best option for you.

How Long Does The Dental Implant Process Take?

The process varies depending on individual cases but generally involves a few months. It starts with an evaluation and implant placement, followed by a healing period to allow for osseointegration (the integration of the implant with the jawbone). After healing, the crown placement completes the restoration.

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. This involves maintaining excellent oral hygiene, attending regular dental check-ups, and potentially making lifestyle adjustments to support oral health.

What Are The Benefits Of Dental Implants Compared To Other Tooth Replacement Options?

Dental implants offer several advantages, including improved appearance and comfort, enhanced oral health as they do not affect adjacent teeth, and the prevention of bone loss. They also provide a permanent, stable solution that mimics the function of natural teeth better than dentures or bridges.
