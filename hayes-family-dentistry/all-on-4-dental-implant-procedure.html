What Is The All-On-4 Dental Implant Procedure?

The All-On-4 procedure is an innovative dental implant technique that uses four implants per arch to support a full set of replacement teeth. This method provides a secure, permanent solution for individuals with significant tooth loss, offering both functional and aesthetic restoration.

Who Is A Suitable Candidate For The All-On-4 Procedure?

Candidates typically include those with multiple missing teeth or those currently using dentures who seek a more permanent solution. A consultation at Hayes Family Dentistry will involve a detailed assessment of your oral health and bone structure to determine if All-On-4 is right for you.

What Are The Benefits Of Choosing All-On-4 Implants?

Benefits include immediate results with same-day teeth, enhanced stability for eating and speaking, improved aesthetics with natural-looking teeth, and preservation of jawbone health, which helps maintain facial structure.

How Long Does The All-On-4 Treatment Process Take?

The surgical placement of implants and fitting of temporary teeth usually occurs in one day. However, a comprehensive treatment plan may involve several appointments for evaluation, preparation, surgery and final placement of the permanent prosthesis once healing is complete.

How Should I Care For My All-On-4 Implants?

Caring for All-On-4 implants involves maintaining excellent oral hygiene by brushing twice daily, flossing with special instruments designed for implants, and attending regular dental check-ups for monitoring and professional cleaning. This care helps ensure the longevity and success of your implants.
