What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is more than just a new tooth it’s also a new tooth root . A small post (made of a biocompatible material such as titanium, zirconia, or ceramic) is placed into the jaw, and this serves as the support for a crown, bridge, or denture. The implant actually fuses with the bone like a real tooth, making it incredibly strong and stable.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation: Are dental implants a good fit for your situation? We’ll give you an exam and answer all your questions so taking the next step is easy.
  • Placement: A minor surgical procedure is performed in-house (no referral necessary) to position the implants within your jaw.
  • Healing: Over the next three to four months, your implant will bond with your jaw.
  • Restoration: Once your mouth is ready, you’ll return to our office to have your new teeth connected to your implants.
