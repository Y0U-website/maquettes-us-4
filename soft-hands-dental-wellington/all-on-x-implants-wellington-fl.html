How the All-on-X Process Works

Comprehensive Consultation: We will assess your bone health and treatment objectives with an exam and 3D imaging.

Implant Placement: During the procedure, four or more implants are placed in the jawbone. Temporary teeth are attached the same day so that you can leave the office with a complete smile.

Healing and Final Restoration: Once the implants integrate with the bone, your permanent bridge is placed, giving you a strong, natural-looking smile.
