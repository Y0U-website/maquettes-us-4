Our Approach to Implant Dentistry

At Soft Hands Dentistry, you will have a consultation, digital X-rays, and 3D imaging so that we can ensure your comfort and precision as we move through the three stages of your implant:

Evaluation and Planning: We assess your oral health, bone density, and overall needs to design a custom treatment plan.

Implant Placement: A titanium post is gently placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation for your comfort.

Restoration: Once healed, a lifelike crown or bridge is attached to complete your smile.
