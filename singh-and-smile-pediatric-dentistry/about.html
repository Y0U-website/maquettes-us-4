Meet Our Pediatric Dentists

Meet the Team Behind Every Smile

Here’s something most dental offices can’t say: whatever your child needs, we have a doctor who specializes in exactly that.

We’re a woman-led practice with three board-certified pediatric dentists, each bringing a different set of expertise but sharing the same belief: that trust is built through consistency, communication, and time. We move at your child’s pace, from their very first visit onward, because kids who trust their dentist grow into adults who take care of their health. Whether your little one is nervous, has special healthcare needs, or you’d simply feel more at home speaking Spanish, you’ll find your person here.

THE DOCTORS: Meet Dr. Singh If your child gets anxious at the dentist, Dr. Singh is who you want in their corner. Before she ever picked up a dental tool, she earned a degree in psychology, and she puts it to work every single day. She knows how to read a child, ease their fears before they take hold, and turn a scared first-timer into a kid who can’t wait to come back.

Dr. Singh is both a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist and a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. She completed her Doctorate of Dental Medicine in 2010, followed by a General Practice Residency at Montefiore Medical Center, an Oral Oncology fellowship at the renowned Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, and two years of specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry.

She’s a member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the College of Diplomates. Outside the office, she loves to travel, practice yoga, spend time with family and friends, and give back through community outreach, including volunteer work in Ecuador.

Meet Dr. Tamarinda ("Dr. T")

Dr. T believes every dental visit should be something children look forward to. She transforms routine appointments into engaging, collaborative experiences through games, songs, storytelling, and plenty of smiles. Fluent in both English and Spanish, and with extensive experience caring for children and individuals with disabilities, she strives to make every patient and family feel respected, comfortable, and right at home.

Outside the office, you’ll likely find Dr. T tending to her garden, conducting "highly scientific" research on New Jersey’s best ice cream shops, or singing five-part harmonies with her husband and their three young children.

Why we’re called Singh and Smile Pediatric Dentistry We take dentistry seriously. Ourselves? Not so much.

Our doctors have been known to sing to their patients just to see them smile. (Hence the name.)

Because here’s what we believe: when kids and parents feel relaxed, everything goes better.

We know some children come to us nervous, especially if they’ve had a rough experience somewhere else. So we’ve built our entire approach around keeping things calm and positive. You won’t hear words like shot, needle, or drill in our office. We don’t believe in creating fear when we can create comfort instead.

Our goal is simple: we want every child and every parent to walk out feeling good, and to actually look forward to coming back.

Take a look around Our office was designed with one audience in mind: kids. From the moment your family walks in, everything is built to feel welcoming, fun, and warm.
