Life Beyond the Dental Chair

Outside of the office, Dr. Henderson finds her joy in family, nature, and sports. She loves spending quality time with her husband and two children, often outdoors, playing tennis, skiing, or horseback riding. The family shares their home with a lovable Cavaliere Spaniel, a charming Basset Hound, a bearded dragon, and a chinchilla — all part of what they lovingly call their “mini farm.”

This balance of professional passion and personal fulfillment is what allows Dr. Henderson to show up for her patients with genuine presence and energy.
