Our Dental Implant Restoration Process

We make the restoration experience smooth, collaborative, and tailored to your needs. Here’s what to expect:

Step 1: Consultation & Digital Imaging

We begin with a comprehensive exam and use iTero® digital scanning to capture high-resolution images of your bite and implant site.

Step 2: Coordination with Your Specialist

If your implant surgery has not yet occurred, we coordinate with trusted local oral surgeons and periodontists to ensure a smooth surgical experience. If your implant is already placed, we’ll evaluate healing and readiness for restoration.

Step 3: Custom Restoration Design

Using digital impressions (no messy molds), we design a precise, natural-looking restoration that fits your bite and blends seamlessly with your smile.

Step 4: Final Placement

Once your custom crown, bridge, or denture is ready, we securely attach it to your implant post and check for bite comfort, esthetics, and function.

Throughout the process, Dr. Henderson works closely with your surgical provider to ensure complete care and long-term stability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you place dental implants in your office?

Dr. Henderson focuses on what she does best: designing and placing the crown, bridge, or denture that completes your implant with a natural, long-lasting result. For the surgical placement, we coordinate directly with trusted Palo Alto oral surgeons and periodontists, so your entire implant journey, from surgery to final restoration, is managed as one seamless experience.

I already had an implant placed, can you restore it?

Absolutely. Whether you recently had surgery or need a replacement crown for an older implant, we can evaluate your case and design a custom restoration.

How long does the restoration process take?

After implant placement and healing (usually 3–6 months), your restoration typically takes 2 visits over a few weeks. We’ll provide a timeline based on your case.

Is a dental implant restoration permanent?

With proper care, implant-supported restorations can last 15+ years or more. Regular checkups and good hygiene are key to longevity.
