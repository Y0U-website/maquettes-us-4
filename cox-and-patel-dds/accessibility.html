Accessibility Statement

  • All images include descriptive alternative text
  • Sufficient color contrast throughout (WCAG AA minimum)
  • All interactive elements are keyboard-navigable with visible focus indicators
  • Touch targets are at least 44 × 44 pixels
  • A skip-to-content link is provided at the top of every page
  • Motion is reduced when the user’s operating system preference is set to reduce motion
  • Forms include clearly associated labels and error messages

Known limitations

We are actively working to identify and resolve any remaining accessibility barriers. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this site, please let us know and we will work to address it promptly.
