What to Expect

Depending on the case, implants can require several visits.

Your dentist will implement a local anesthetic before inserting the implant and then sowing the treatment area. The healing process can take anywhere from two to four months depending on the extent of the procedure. After your gums have had time to heal (usually after three months) you will come in for an examination in which we will determine how well the implant has fused to the bone and if the treatment is going smoothly, you will receive a healing cap.

After a couple of weeks, you will be asked to come in for a final visit in which the treatment area is disinfected and the healing cap is removed and replaced with the final implant crown.
