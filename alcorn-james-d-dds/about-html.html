Meet Dr. Alcorn

Why is Dr. Alcorn the right choice to be your family dentist?

Our Philosophy

If you value these qualities in a dentist, call Dr. James Alcorn today! Come to a practice where you can build trusted relationships and receive lasting oral health.

  • Maintaining Your Natural Smile Dr. Alcorn not only believes that everyone deserves lasting oral health but that everyone deserves a natural smile. We take a conservative approach, working hard to maintain your natural smile and deliver only the highest standard of care. We want to save your natural teeth and help you enjoy a beautiful smile throughout your entire life.
  • Advanced Technology We understand the pivotal role that technology plays in saving teeth and maintaining a natural smile. Because of this, we utilize advanced technologies, including digital x-rays and lasers, in order to offer a wider range of services and deliver better results for you.
  • Meaningful Relationships We care about you, and we care about helping you keep your natural smile. We also enjoy getting to know you personally. We truly love having the opportunity to serve you and your family and build a lasting relationship with you. We enjoy hearing about your life, and we take the time to listen to your needs so we can customize your care and help you feel at ease. We look forward to getting to know you through the years.
  • Family-Friendly Environment Our patients love the family-friendly feel of our practice. We construct a welcoming environment with a gentle and caring team constantly available to comfort and assist patients of all ages. Dr. Alcorn also has an excellent chairside manner and a gift for helping you feel at ease. Your experience here will be an enjoyable one.
  • Honest Recommendations Having a dentist you can trust is important to keeping a healthy, natural smile. At our practice, you will be working with honest and caring professionals. We provide reliable recommendations and clear explanations so you can make informed, confident decisions. We promise to always keep your best interests in mind.
