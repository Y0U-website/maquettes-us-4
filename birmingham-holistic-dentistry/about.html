Guided Biofilm Therapy

Cleanings are the foundation of a healthy smile, and nothing cleans better than Guided Biofilm Therapy with the EMS Airflow. This advanced Swiss technology allows us to target and remove harmful biofilm, plaque, calculus, and stains using combination of air, warm water, and an ultrafine powder.

Digital Imaging

Digital Imaging

To get a complete view of your oral health, we will take low-radiation digital x-rays, 3D CBCT scans, and intraoral scans. With these images we can more accurately and efficiently plan treatment for optimal outcomes.

Saliva Testing

Saliva Testing

We take first visits to the next level with salivary testing that tests for 5 types of destructive bacteria and a microscopic oral microbiome exam. Advanced Technology For You

Fotona All-Tissue Laser The Fotona laser sits squarely at the top of it’s class–it’s versatility allows for precise, minimally invasive methods for a variety of treatments, including periodontal therapy, root canals, and much more.

Ozone Therapy Ozone (O3) is a naturally occurring compound that significantly reduces the presence of oral bacteria. This not only limits the risk of infection after a procedure such as an extraction, but can also completely halt the progression of tooth decay by eliminating the bacteria that cause the decay in the first place.

iTero Intraoral Scanner Digital intraoral scans allow us to create virtual impressions of your teeth and gums for increased patient comfort, more effective treatment planning, and improved outcomes.

CBCT Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scans can show us what lies beneath the surface of your smile. With these 3D images, we can create a complete view of your oral cavity, including blood vessels and nerve pathways

EMS Airflow EMS Airflow is a dental technology that uses a combination of air, water, and a gentle abrasive powder to efficiently remove dental plaque, stains, and biofilm from teeth and gums, providing an improved patient experience with minimal discomfort and reduced risk of enamel damage.

SMART Technology The technology used in SMART Amalgam Removal typically includes high-volume suction systems, rubber dams, and protective equipment to capture and contain mercury vapor and particles during the removal process, ensuring a safer environment for both you and our team.
