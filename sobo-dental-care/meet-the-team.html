Rachel Blum, DDS Education Dr. Rachel grew up in upstate New York and completed her undergraduate studies at SUNY Binghamton before earning her dental degree from Touro University. Inspired by her father, who is also a dentist, she developed an early appreciation for the positive impact dentistry can have on people’s lives.

Dentistry

After many years in New York, Dr. Rachel moved to Colorado in 2023 to complete a two-year General Practice Residency at the University of Colorado, serving as chief resident during her second year. Her advanced training in prosthodontics, surgery, and comprehensive, patient-centered care equips her to address a wide range of dental needs with precision and compassion. Dr. Rachel is thrilled to be joining Dr. Moore and looks forward to providing exceptional, personalized care to the community.

Home Life

Outside the office, Dr. Rachel enjoys hiking with friends and family, soaking up the Colorado sunshine, reading nonfiction, and usually sipping a cup of tea. She has a genuine passion for learning and dedicates much of her free time to continuing education.

Dr. Alan Belenski

Honoring the legacy

Esteemed founder of Alan D. Belenski, D.D.S. (now SoBo Dental Care), Dr. Alan Belenski has left his mark on the Louisville community over the past 40 years. Delivering exceptional dental care and compassionate dedication to his patients, he has created a legacy worth continuing.

He is overjoyed to be passing the practice and his legacy to his trusted partner, Dr. Kevin Moore. Knowing everything he’s created will live on and his patients will be well cared for, Dr. Belenski looks forward to spending more time with his wife, children, and grandchildren.

He will also enjoy more golfing, traveling, and Buell Theatre productions, Avalanche hockey games, and CU Buff football games.

Thank you Dr. Belenski for your many years of heart, dedication, and expertise as you served the Louisville community so well.
