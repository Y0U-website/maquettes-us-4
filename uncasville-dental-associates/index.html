Thank you for trusting us with your smile. We look forward to supporting your dental health with expertise and a gentle touch!

At Uncasville Dental Associates, our mission is to provide dedicated and compassionate dental care to the communities of Uncasville, Montville, New London, Norwich, and beyond. We offer various services, from emergency dentistry to comprehensive cosmetic treatments, including porcelain veneers, chairside veneers, crowns, and bridges.

Our commitment is to deliver outstanding care in a gentle, relaxing environment where our patients feel welcomed and comfortable. We strive to offer treatment options that meet each patient’s unique needs and budget. We take pride in building meaningful relationships with our patients, supporting them in achieving optimal dental health and fulfilling their personal goals for their smiles.
