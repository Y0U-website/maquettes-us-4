Our Office

As your Family Dentist, we always strive to provide “dental health care” as opposed to “disease care” because we understand that the key to optimal dental health is preventive care. Hence we focus on thorough exams and also checking the overall health of your teeth and gums as we perform oral cancer exams and taking x-rays when necessary. We make sure you and your family are safe from dental diseases. We care for the whole family, from children, to parents, and grandparents.

We can stay informed of your overall health after reviewing your medical history. Any new medications and any illnesses that may impact your dental health should always be reported to us. We are also aware that routine cleanings and flossing are all helpful in preventing dental disease.

Tolland Family Dentistry is not a participating provider with any insurance companies.

Co-pays are due at time of service. Your co-pay is an estimate only, so there is a chance you will be billed for additional co-pay that the insurance company won’t allow payment for. The maximum amount of time we will process a claim without receiving payment from your insurance carrier is 3 months. You are responsible for payment in full if we do not receive payment in that time frame. We will provide you at that time the necessary documentation for you to submit to your insurance carrier.

  • - We do not participate in any DMO or HMO plans.
  • - If you choose to see us as an out of network provider, we will gladly submit your insurance claim. However, this is a courtesy to our patients.
  • - Patients are responsible for knowing the benefits of their individual plans including co-pays, deductibles and maximums. There should be an 800 number on the back of your card that you can call and get all this information. If you do not have a card, your human resources department should be able give you information so that you may contact your insurance carrier.
  • - We ask that you have all of the above insurance information available upon arrival at your first visit.
  • -Here at Tolland Family Dentistry, we know that your time is as valuable as ours, therefore we ask that you give a minimum of 24 hours notice if you need to cancel any appointments.
  • -There will be a fee for any appointment missed without proper notification.
  • -Patients that are more than 10 minutes late for their appointment will not be seen as we need to be fair to those patients who are on time.

Training and Expertise

We want you to be completely confident in your dental office as your dental health care professionals knowing that we are a team of highly trained and skilled clinicians.

It is our pride to give you and your family the kind of gentle care that you need to keep your smile healthy. We are also committed to continued education to deliver you the best possible service and results.

We stay informed of all the new techniques, the latest products, and the newest equipment of a modern dental studio by attending many dental lectures, meetings, and conventions.

We are one of only a handful of offices that possess the latest technology know as CEREC , which allows us to offer crowns in one visit. Find out more information here.

With an uncompromised standard of excellence in personalized dental care and with our professional staff, we assure you with the best quality dental services that you deserve.

Our staff is trained to work with patients who suffer from dental anxiety. We make sure all our patients are treated not only with quality service but also with deep understanding of their emotional well-being. Although we are not a pediatric office, the professional staff is well-trained to handle children.

Comprehensive treatment plan and use of the finest restorative and cosmetic dentistry technology available to achieve your optimal dental health and appearance will be provided to you!
