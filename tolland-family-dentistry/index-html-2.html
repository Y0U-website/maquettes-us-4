Leading Cosmetic and General Dentist Niloo Gorji, DDS, is to be Recognized as a 2021 Top Doctor in Tolland, Connecticut

Renowned as one of Connecticut’s leading experts in cosmetic and general dentistry, Dr. Gorji and her dedicated team provide a comprehensive range of dental solutions at Tolland Family Dentistry in Tolland, Connecticut. They always strive to provide dental health care as opposed to disease care because they understand that the key to optimal dental health is preventive care.

Being a very experienced and respected dentist, Dr. Gorji is one of the few general practitioners who has a Cone Beam CT-scan for detailed and thorough diagnostic purposes and a CEREC machine to fabricate and deliver same-day crowns. Expert procedures undertaken by her include oral cancer screening, ceramic veneers, implant restorations, crowns, root canals, sleep apnea appliances, whitening procedure, geriatric and pediatric care.

After earning her Bachelor of Arts degree in Economics from Syracuse University in 1993, Dr. Gorji went on to obtain her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Case Western Reserve School of Dental Medicine in 1998. Following this, she completed her residency and fellowship at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine.

She keeps up to date with all the latest advances in dentistry through her active membership of many professional organizations, including the American Dental Association, the Connecticut State Dental Association, the Tolland County Dental Society and the Spear Education Dental Group.

Dr. Priya John

University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine

Dr. John comes from a lineage of dentists. Her grandmother, aunt, and father all practiced dentistry, and she began her career working as an assistant in her father’s office before enrolling in dental school. She was inspired by the relationships he built with his patients and loved that dentistry combined science and art. The decision to follow her family’s tradition was an easy one.

Dr. John attended Dickinson College in Carlisle, Pennsylvania for her undergraduate degree before entering the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine. Since graduating with her DMD, she has completed several hours of continuing education on subjects such as CEREC restorations, dental implant surgery, Invisalign, 3D CBCT imaging, and more. She is also active in organized dentistry and is a member of the American Dental Association, Connecticut State Dental Association, and Spear Education Study Clubs.

Dr. John enjoys spending time with her friends and family. She and her husband, Adam, met in school and were married in 2022. She loves to cook as well as discover new restaurants and cuisines in the area. Whenever she has the opportunity, Dr. John enjoys traveling to new places, whether they be close to home or overseas.
