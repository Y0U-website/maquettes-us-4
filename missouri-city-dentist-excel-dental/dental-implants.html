The Basics of Dental Implant Surgery

Compared to other tooth-replacement options like bridges or dentures, dental implants Missouri City are designed to mimic the structure, function closely, and feel of natural teeth. During dental implant surgery, we place a biocompatible post into your jawbone, which acts like an artificial tooth root. On top of that base, we connect the replacement tooth that matches the appearance of your other teeth.
