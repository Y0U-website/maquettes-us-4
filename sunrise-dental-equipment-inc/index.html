Located in Central Florida, Sunrise Dental Equipment, Inc. is a locally owned and operated company. Our certified team members, with 75+ years of combined experience in the dental industry, have provided quality refurbished equipment to dental and medical offices across America, Canada, & the Caribbean Islands since 2001.

We purchase quality used dental equipment, restore the items to factory specifications, and resell the equipment in Like New cosmetic condition at a fraction of the original price. All our refurbished items include a 1-year parts / 90-day labor warranty.

We offer a large selection of quality dental equipment. From Dental Chair Operatory Packages, Dental Cabinets, Compressors, Vacuums, X-Ray Systems, Lab Equipment to a Single Handpiece, we have your equipment needs covered.

We have a great selection of complete office packages. We can accommodate 1 12 Operatory setups. We can also create a package around these listings tailored to your specific needs!

Our selection of fully Operatory Packages includes top brands known for comfort, durability, and functionality.

Designed for organization and efficiency, our refurbished dental cabinets provide ample storage and a professional look for any dental practice.

We offer a variety of Dental X-Ray Systems, Sensors, Viewboxes, and Processors for your various imaging needs
