Meet Dr. Alonzo M. Bell

Dr. Bell has been very active in the leadership of the ADA serving in their House of Delegates and on their Council on Communications.

Whether performing general dentistry services or complicated cosmetic procedures, Dr. Bell delivers the absolute best in patient care. His attention to detail keeps his loyal group of patients coming back while also attracting a new crowd over the years.

Dr. Bell strives to make his dental practice accommodating and inclusive by offering advanced, low-radiation X-ray technology, same-day emergency appointments, and a staff that speaks English and Spanish.

Outside of the office and away from Dentistry, Dr. Bell enjoys attending area wide sporting events, gardening, and exploring new restaurants in and around the Alexandria and DC communities. He also enjoys traveling with his wife and two daughters.

Rachel Kogan, D.M.D

Dr. Kogan was born and raised on Long Island, New York, where she developed an early appreciation for great bagels and pizza. She graduated Magna Cum Laude from Stony Brook University with a Bachelor of Science in Biology, concentrating in Neuroscience, and a minor in Anthropology.

She went on to earn her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Pennsylvania in Philadelphia, PA. Following dental school, Dr. Kogan completed a General Practice Residency at the Cleveland Clinic in Cleveland, Ohio, where she received advanced training in comprehensive dental care and gained extensive experience treating medically complex patients in both hospital and emergency settings.

Dr. Kogan’s residency experience greatly shaped her patient-centered philosophy, emphasizing comprehensive care and individualized treatment plans. While she valued her time in Ohio, she ultimately relocated to Northern Virginia to be closer to extended family and her fiancé.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Kogan enjoys hiking, traveling—especially to national parks—cooking, knitting, and spending time with her fiancé and her Bernese Mountain Dog (puppy), Amelia.
