The practice

Regular dental cleanings include a thorough assessment and gentle cleaning to maintain oral health, with recommendations for biannual visits. For those with gum disease, scaling and root planing may be necessary to ensure gum health.

A crown is a dental restoration used to protect and restore damaged teeth. After preparing the tooth and taking a digital impression, we craft a custom crown that can be made of porcelain, porcelain fused to metal, or all metal before permanently cementing it in place.

Make a statement with custom dental grillz designed and fitted by a licensed dentist. Our grillz are crafted using precise dental impressions or digital scans for a comfortable, custom fit. We partner with professional dental laboratories to create high-quality, dental-grade grillz that blend luxury style with oral health awareness. Removable options available. Cosmetic service.

Dentures are a great option for individuals missing most or all teeth. The process involves taking digital impressions, several fitting appointments, and selecting tooth color to create custom dentures that restore functionality and aesthetics.

Tooth-colored fillings are used to treat small cavities by removing decayed areas and applying a composite resin that matches your natural tooth color, restoring both appearance and function.

Nitrous oxide is a safe and effective sedative agent that is mixed with oxygen and inhaled through a small mask that fits over your nose to help you relax.

We offer clear aligners and traditional braces for orthodontic treatment, helping patients achieve straight teeth under professional care.

Partial dentures are removable appliances for replacing multiple missing teeth, custom-fitted through a series of appointments for a natural look and comfortable fit.

Les soins

A dental bridge fills the gaps created by missing teeth by anchoring crowns on adjacent teeth. After preparing these teeth and taking a digital impression, we will fit and permanently secure a custom bridge in place, enhancing both function and appearance.

Tooth extraction is required when a tooth is damaged beyond repair or infected. Our gentle techniques ensure comfort during the procedure, and we will provide detailed aftercare instructions for a smooth recovery.

Implant restoration offers a permanent solution for missing teeth through the use of a titanium post, followed by custom crowns or bridges that enhance your smile and support oral health.
