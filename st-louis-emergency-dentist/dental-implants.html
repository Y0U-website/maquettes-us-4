What are dental implants?

Dental implants are your best long-term solution for missing teeth because they bond with your healthy bone, providing a sturdy base for a dental crown. Together, the implant, connector and crown function just like your natural tooth.

What are dental implants?

A dental implant (sometimes called tooth implants) is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a foundation for a replacement tooth or teeth. Placing an implant is a common surgical procedure performed by your highly trained clinician at Austin Emergency Dental. Explore types of dental implants for you and learn more about our full range of affordable, high-quality options.

Do dental implants hurt?

A common concern about dental implants is pain, but at Austin Emergency Dental, our caring teams prioritize your comfort every step of the way. How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are made to last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene habits can help ensure the longevity of your implant. Your Austin Emergency Dental team can provide help and guidance along the way.

Are dental implants expensive?

Dental implant costs can vary depending on the number of implants needed, the location of the implants, and if there are any additional procedures required. Here at Austin Emergency Dental, we offer a range of dental financing options to help make dental implants fit your unique budget.

How long does the implant process take?

The implant process can vary depending on your personal needs, but it typically takes a few months to complete, given the bone can take up to 6 months to heal. This includes your initial consultation, implant placement surgery and then the placement of the final restoration.

Are there any downsides to dental implants?

Generally, dental implants offer the best long-term value for tooth replacement needs. Completing your implant journey may take several months to ensure your bone has enough time to heal after your implants are placed. Additionally, they cost more upfront—but our implants are protected by a 25-year warranty so you can enjoy them for years to come.

What is the aftercare process for dental implants?

After the implant process is complete, it is important to maintain good oral health habits to ensure the longevity of your implants. This includes brushing and flossing regularly, as well as scheduling regular checkups with your Austin Emergency Dental dentist.

What Are the 2 Types of Dental Implants Available in St. Louis?

There are two main types of dental implants offered at St. Louis Emergency Dentist:

Our St. Louis dental team recommends the most appropriate implant option based on your oral health, bone structure, and treatment goals to ensure long lasting results.

  • Endosteal Implants These are the most common type of dental implants, placed directly into the jawbone. They act as strong, stable foundations for replacement teeth, similar to natural tooth roots.
  • Zygomatic Implants: These are used when there is significant bone loss in the upper jaw and traditional implants are not suitable. They are anchored in the cheekbone to provide a secure base for restorations.
