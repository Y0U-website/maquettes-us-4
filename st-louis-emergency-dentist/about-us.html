What to do in a dental emergency:

In a dental emergency, please remain calm so you can think clearly and determine the best course of action. Depending on the type of emergency, you will either contact an emergency dentist or go directly to the hospital. If you’re experiencing a serious or life threatening issues, like a jaw fracture, severe facial cuts, or an infection affecting breathing or swallowing, go to the hospital immediately. If your dental emergency isn’t life-threatening, it’s best to contact an emergency dentist. The previous examples like a toothache or cracked tooth, would fall under this type of emergency.

Your visit in 5 steps

Here’s what you can expect when working with your personal care team.

Examine

To identify the cause of your emergency, your St. Louis Emergency Dentist professional will conduct a thorough examination. To locate the root cause and determine the extent of the damage, we will need to use diagnostic tools like an X-ray.

Explain

After your dental emergency dentist finds the underlying problem, they will explain what caused your pain. Then, they will discuss treatment options and what to do next.

Recommend

Depending on the diagnosis, you’ll receive a recommended treatment plan to alleviate your pain. From there, your team will set up a follow-up visit to continue the care recommended in your treatment plan.

Treat

Once you accept treatment, your dental office team will get right to work on pain relief. Other procedures may involve removing a tooth, filling a cavity, or repairing a cracked tooth.

Follow-up

After your visit, your team will provide post-procedure care instructions like medication, rest and avoiding certain activities. If they need to see you again, they’ll schedule your follow-up so you go home prepared and ready to focus on healing.
