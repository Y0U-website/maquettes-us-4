What Are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are custom-made, removable trays designed to gradually move teeth into improved positions. Instead of brackets and wires, treatment uses a planned series of aligners that apply controlled pressure as your smile changes. The trays are transparent and fit closely over your teeth, making them a discreet alternative to traditional braces.

Frequently Asked Questions: Clear Aligners in Palmetto Bay, FL

How do clear aligners work?

Clear aligners use a planned series of custom trays to apply gentle, controlled pressure to your teeth. As you progress through the series, your teeth gradually move toward their planned positions.

Are clear aligners as effective as braces?

Clear aligners can effectively treat many mild to moderate alignment problems, but traditional braces or other orthodontic approaches may be more appropriate for certain complex cases. An evaluation is necessary to determine the right treatment.

How many hours a day should I wear clear aligners?

Clear aligners generally need to be worn for most of the day and night, often around 20–22 hours daily depending on the system and treatment plan. They are typically removed for eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, and flossing.

Can I eat normally with clear aligners?

Yes. Because the aligners are removable, you can take them out during meals and generally continue enjoying your normal diet. Teeth should be cleaned before putting the aligners back in whenever possible.

Do clear aligners hurt?

You may experience temporary pressure or tenderness when beginning a new set of aligners. This usually reflects the gentle forces being used to move your teeth and commonly improves as you adjust to each tray.

How long does the clear aligner treatment take?

Treatment time depends on the amount and complexity of tooth movement required. Some relatively simple cases can be completed in several months, while others may take a year or longer.

Can teenagers use clear aligners?

Clear aligners may be appropriate for responsible teens who can consistently follow their prescribed wear schedule. Candidacy depends on dental development, orthodontic needs, and the individual’s ability to comply with treatment.

Does dental insurance cover clear aligners?

Some dental insurance plans include orthodontic benefits that may contribute toward clear aligner treatment. Coverage varies significantly, so our team can help review your available benefits.
