What Happens During the Aligner Process?

Cambridge Dentist Committed to Your Smile

The aligner process begins with a consultation and evaluation of your teeth, bite, and orthodontic concerns. Dr. Jeffrey Greenberg will discuss your goals and determine whether aligners are the right treatment option for your smile. Digital scans or impressions are then taken to create a customized treatment plan that maps out the gradual movement of your teeth.

Patients receive a series of custom aligners that are worn throughout treatment and changed regularly as the teeth begin shifting into their new positions. Routine visits allow our team to monitor progress, make adjustments if needed, and ensure treatment stays on track. Because aligners are removable, patients can continue brushing, flossing, and eating more comfortably during treatment.

Who Is a Candidate for Aligners in Cambridge?

Aligners may be an option for teens and adults with mild to moderate orthodontic concerns such as crowding, spacing, or bite irregularities. Patients looking for a more discreet alternative to traditional braces may especially benefit from aligner treatment.

Candidates should have healthy teeth and gums before beginning orthodontic care. During your consultation, our team will evaluate your oral health and discuss whether aligners are the most appropriate treatment option for your goals and lifestyle.
