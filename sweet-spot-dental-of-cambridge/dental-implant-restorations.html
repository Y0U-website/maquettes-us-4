What Happens During the Dental Implant Restoration Process?

Cambridge Dentist Committed to Your Smile

The dental implant restoration process begins with a consultation and evaluation of your oral health, existing dental implants, and treatment goals. If dental implants have already been placed and properly healed, Dr. Jeffrey Greenberg will take impressions or digital scans to design a custom restoration that fits comfortably and blends naturally with your smile.

Depending on your needs, the final restoration may include a crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture. Once the restoration is ready, it will be carefully attached to the implant and adjusted as needed to ensure proper fit, appearance, and function. Our team will also provide guidance on caring for your implant restoration to help maintain long-term oral health.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implant Restorations in Cambridge?

Dental implant restorations may be an option for patients who are missing one or more teeth and have dental implants ready to support a custom restoration. Candidates should have healthy gums and sufficient oral health to maintain long-term implant stability.

Patients looking for a durable and natural-looking tooth replacement option may benefit from implant restorations. During your consultation, our team will evaluate your oral health, discuss your goals, and determine the most appropriate restoration option for your smile.
