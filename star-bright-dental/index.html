Dr. Cuong (Alex) Nguyen is a proud graduate of the University of Columbia, College of Dental Medicine. He is a long-standing member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the California Dental Association (CDA) and the Orange County Dental Society (OCDS). An avid learner committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Nguyen has been deeply involved in continuing education courses and dental study clubs…

For over twenty years, Dr. Nguyen has combined skill, compassion, and precision to help patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. He specializes in minimally invasive and aesthetic dentistry, using advanced techniques such as air-abrasion sandblasting and meticulous bonding methods to ensure lasting, natural-looking results.

Believing that every smile is unique, Dr. Nguyen creates personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient’s needs and goals. At Star Bright Dental, he treats every patient like family. He takes time to listen, explain, and provide care that’s as thoughtful as it is thorough.
