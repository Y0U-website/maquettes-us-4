Dental Implants in Los Angeles, CA

A dental implant is a small titanium post that acts as a tooth root under the gum line that can support crowns, bridges, and dentures to replace missing teeth. At Smileywood Dental Office, we place the implant into the jawbone through a minor surgical procedure and then the jawbone is allowed to heal for several months. During this time, the jawbone will fuse to the implant in a process known as “osseointegration.” Once this has occurred, the implant can support restorations such as a crown or bridge.

Dental implants look natural, allowing you to chew and speak normally. Dental Implants also help keep the surrounding healthy teeth from shifting out of place and causing further dental problems.

The Benefits Of Dental Implants

Dental implants are one of the most permanent ways to replace missing teeth, and they can last a lifetime with proper care. They are also the most natural-looking way of replacing missing teeth because they look and function like your natural teeth! When cared for properly, dental implants can completely restore your smile and enable you to eat the foods you love again without having to deal with messy adhesives or loose dentures.
