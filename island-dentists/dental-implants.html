Dental Implants in Palatka, FL

We’ll go over each step and the expected timeline for your specific situation at your consultation — implant timelines vary enough from patient to patient that we don’t want to quote a one-size number here.

  • Planning. If bone grafting or other preparation is needed, we’ll walk you through that step before the implant itself is placed.
  • Placement. The implant post can be placed into the jawbone with a local anesthetic injection, and the procedure can be performed with oral sedation in addition if you’d like extra help relaxing.
  • Healing. The bone needs time to fuse with the implant — this varies by patient, so we’ll give you a realistic timeline for your case.
  • Restoration. Once healed, we attach your custom crown, bridge, or denture, completing the repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do dental implants cost in Palatka?

Implant cost depends on how many teeth are being replaced, whether bone grafting is needed, and what type of restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) is used. Because every case is different, the most accurate number comes from an in-person consultation. We accept dental insurance and offer CareCredit financing to help spread out the cost.

How long do dental implants take?

Timelines vary by patient — factors like bone healing time and whether any preparatory work is needed both play a role. At your consultation, we’ll walk you through a realistic timeline based on your specific case rather than a generic estimate.

Do you offer implant-supported dentures?

Yes. For patients missing most or all of their teeth in an arch, implants can be used to anchor a full denture, giving it more stability and a more secure fit than a removable denture alone. We’ll discuss whether this is a good option for you at your consultation.

Do dental implants hurt?

Implant placement can be performed with a local anesthetic injection to numb the area, and with oral sedation in addition if you’d like extra help relaxing. Most patients report the recovery is more manageable than they expected — similar to what you’d feel after a tooth extraction. We’ll go over what to expect for your specific procedure beforehand.

Will my insurance cover a dental implant?

Coverage varies by plan — some dental insurance plans cover a portion of implant treatment, especially the restoration (crown) piece. Our front desk can help you understand your specific benefits, and CareCredit financing is available for costs insurance doesn’t cover.
