Insurance and Billing

We accept most traditional insurance plans, contact our office to verify acceptance of your plan. We also have an In House Dental Plan if you do not have traditional dental insurance. We accept checks, cash or credit cards . Please see our Financial Coordinator for details. We are happy to file insurance for your reimbursement as long as you are free to choose your own dentist.

We are IN NETWORK with the following plans:

United Concordia (Tricare and Fee for Service)

Additonally, a 20 % discount will be applied to all treatments with no annual limits.

***Always check with your insurance prior to your visit to make sure that we are still in network with your plan! (if you have any questions don’t hesitate to contact our office) The contract is between you are your insurance.
