About us

Welcome to the Super Smiles Headquarters!

Super Smiles 4 Kids Portsmouth, VA

From your very first interaction with Super Smiles 4 Kids VA, our team is dedicated to providing personalized dental care tailored to your child’s unique needs and goals. We strive to create a comfortable, stress-free environment where children feel at ease and confident in their care. Your child’s health and comfort are our highest priorities, and we take the time to help every patient and parent understand symptoms and treatment options in a fun, relaxed setting.

Our mission is to educate and support families while delivering exceptional pediatric dental care. To support that commitment, we’ve designed our website as a helpful resource, offering the information you need to make informed decisions about your child’s oral health. We encourage you to explore our office page, review our services, and visit our patient education library whenever questions arise.

At Super Smiles 4 Kids VA, we believe achieving quality dental care should be simple and accessible. Whether you’re seeking pediatric dentistry, preventive care, or emergency treatment, our services page provides clear guidance on all available options. For your convenience, you can also request appointments online and complete patient forms directly through our website.

If you have any questions, please don’t hesitate to contact us. We look forward to making every visit a positive and enjoyable experience for your child.
