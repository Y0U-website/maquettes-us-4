Dr. Eric Nelson, DDS

Education, Credentials, and Clinical Training

Dr. Nelson’s path to dentistry started with a strong science foundation and continues today through ongoing training in newer techniques.

B.S. in Molecular Biology, Brigham Young University

Before dental school, Dr. Nelson earned a Bachelor of Science in Molecular Biology from Brigham Young University. That background gave him a deep understanding of how the body works, which still shapes how he explains treatment to patients today.

Doctor of Dental Surgery, University of Utah School of Dentistry

Dr. Nelson went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Utah School of Dentistry. His training there covered the full range of general dentistry, from routine cleanings to more complex restorative work.

Continuing Education and Modern Treatment Techniques

Dentistry changes, and Dr. Nelson keeps learning. He stays current on modern treatment techniques and equipment, including digital X-rays and cone beam CT imaging, so patients benefit from more accurate diagnosis and more comfortable visits.

Dr. Nelson s Patient-First Philosophy

A Single Provider Who Knows You by Name As a general dentist in Kaysville, Utah, Dr. Nelson believes patients do better when they see the same doctor every time. There’s no rotating cast of providers here. Dr. Nelson gets to know your history, your concerns, and your smile, visit after visit. Comprehensive General Dentistry at One Kaysville Practice

From Preventive Care to Implants and Cosmetic Treatment Dr. Nelson provides comprehensive care close to home, so patients rarely need to be referred out for common procedures. From routine general dentistry like cleanings, fillings, and exams to implants and cosmetic treatment, most of what a family needs can be handled in one Kaysville office by one familiar doctor.

Modern Technology, Personal Touch Modern tools like digital imaging and 3D scanning help Dr. Nelson diagnose issues earlier and plan treatment more precisely, which often means less discomfort and fewer surprises. But the technology never replaces the personal touch. Every plan is built around what makes sense for you, not a one-size-fits-all checklist.
