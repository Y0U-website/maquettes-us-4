What to Expect: The Implant Process Step by Step

Every implant case starts with a Cone Beam CT scan, a 3D X-ray that lets Dr. Nelson see the exact shape, density, and depth of your jawbone before any drilling begins. This kind of detailed planning helps make placement more precise and the outcome more predictable, so there are fewer surprises along the way.

From there, the process generally follows these steps:

1. Consultation and imaging. We review your health history and take a Cone Beam CT scan to map your jaw. 2. Implant placement. The titanium post is placed into the jawbone during a short procedure, usually done with local anesthesia. 3. Healing time. Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration. 4. Crown placement. Once healing is complete, a custom crown is attached, matched to the color and shape of your natural teeth.

Most patients describe the process as far more manageable than they expected, especially with clear communication from Dr. Nelson at every step.

What Does a Dental Implant Cost in Kaysville, Utah?

The cost of a dental implant depends on a few things: how many teeth you’re replacing, whether you need a bone graft first, and the type of crown used. A single implant is usually less expensive than implant-supported dentures or a full-arch solution, simply because there’s less involved.

We know cost is often the biggest question for patients considering implants, and we’d rather be upfront about it than leave you guessing. During your consultation, Dr. Nelson will walk through the exact plan for your case and give you a clear estimate before any treatment begins.
