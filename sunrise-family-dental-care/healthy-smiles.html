Sunrise Smiles Membership Plan

No Insurance, No Problem!! Enroll in the Sunrise Smiles Membership Plan

*Annual membership fees apply. Discounts are applied to our usual, customary and reasonable (“UCR”) fees.

Membership Plan Details

  • Pediatric Plan (14 Years and Younger) $255/benefit year/member.
  • Adult Plan (15 Years and Older) $295/benefit year/member.
  • UNLIMITED benefit maximums
  • No waiting periods , exclusions , pre-determinations/ authorizations or deductibles (start ANY treatment at ANYTIME)
  • Includes two (2) regular cleanings; two (2) exams (initial and/or routine exam); One (1) Emergency exam; One (1) set of four (4) bitewing x-rays;
  • Two (2) Fluoride Applications (CHILD PLAN ONLY)
  • Periodontal Cleanings ( ADULT PLAN) $33.00 co-pay assessed for each first and second periodontal cleanings in a benefit year, Additional periodontal cleanings (third or fourth cleaning in a benefit year) have a $110 co-pay per cleaning.
  • Debridement and/or Root Planing and Scaling (Deep Cleaning) are discounted 25 % from Office Fees (UCR) ADULT PLAN ONLY
  • Receive TWENTY-FIVE PERCENT (25 %) discount off most other fees for any additional and necessary treatment other than the services listed above

Additional Terms and COnditions, Limitations and Exclusions

  • Memberships are NON-TRANSFERABLE and NON-REFUNDABLE;
  • Membership Plan discount applies to dental services rendered at Sunrise Family Dental Care’s ( Sunrise ) offices ONLY. Eligible services MUST BE provided by a Sunrise dentist or hygienist. Services, including but not limited to Specialists, provided outside of Sunrise or by an Independent Contractor. Specialists are not eligible for discounts; e.g. Endodontists, Orthodontists, Periodontists, etc.;
  • lnvisalign procedures and dental products (including, but not limited to, Sonicare, Waterpics, Whitening Gel, etc.) shall not be discounted;
  • Members’ discounts are contingent on payment for services at completion of treatment/time of service. Failure to pay at time of service/completion shall result in forfeiture of the associated discount for performed services. Member(s) cost shall be based on Sunrise’s non-discounted fees; and
  • Members paying for services with cash, check, credit card or debit card are entitled to the 25 % Membership plan discount.
  • Members using CareCredit for payment shall have their discount reduced by 5.0 %; i.e., Twenty Percent (20 %)
