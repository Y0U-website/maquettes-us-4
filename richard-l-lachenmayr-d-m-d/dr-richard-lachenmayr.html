Meet Dr. Richard Lachenmayr

Dr. Richard Lachenmayr

Dr. Richard Lachenmayr is an esteemed dentist in Alpha, NJ, with a wealth of experience and a strong commitment to providing top-quality dental care to his patients. He obtained his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Fairleigh Dickenson University School of Dentistry in Hackensack, New Jersey, graduating in 1986. Prior to that, he completed his Bachelor of Science in Biology at Fairleigh Dickenson University in Madison, New Jersey, from 1979 to 1982.

After completing his formal education, Dr. Richard Lachenmayr pursued a General Practice Residency at the East Orange VA Medical Center from 1986 to 1987. This residency further honed his skills and allowed him to gain valuable experience in various aspects of dentistry.

Since 1987, Dr. Richard Lachenmayr has been dedicated to serving his patients through his private practice in general dentistry. With a passion for dental health and a commitment to staying up-to-date with the latest advancements in dentistry, he ensures that his patients receive the best possible care for their oral health needs.

As a highly respected dentist in Alpha, NJ, Dr. Richard Lachenmayr is known for his compassionate and patient-centered approach to dental care. He takes the time to understand each patient’s unique needs and provides personalized treatment plans to achieve healthy and beautiful smiles. With a focus on building lasting relationships with his patients, Dr. Lachenmayr is committed to ensuring their comfort and satisfaction with every visit.

Dr. Richard Lachenmayr is a proud member of several esteemed dental organizations, including the American Dental Association (ADA), the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the New Jersey Dental Association (NJDA). Through his continued involvement in these associations, he remains at the forefront of the latest trends and best practices in dentistry.
