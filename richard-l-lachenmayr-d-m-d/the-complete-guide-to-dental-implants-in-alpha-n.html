The Dental Implant Process at Lachenmayr Dental

Every implant treatment is customized to your needs, but the general process includes several key steps.

Comprehensive Consultation

During your exam, Dr. Lachenmayr evaluates your teeth, gums, and jawbone using digital X-rays and a thorough clinical evaluation. This visit allows us to determine whether implants are right for you and discuss your goals for your smile.

Personalized Treatment Plan

We explain timelines, the number of implants needed, and whether any additional procedures, such as bone grafting, are recommended. If your treatment plan includes crowns or bridges, we may also review your options as part of your broader restorative dentistry plan.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant post is placed into your jawbone during a comfortable, minimally invasive procedure. Local anesthesia is used to help you feel at ease throughout the visit.

Healing and Integration

Over several weeks, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong, stable foundation for your future crown, bridge, or denture.

Abutment Placement

Once healing is complete, a small connector called an abutment is attached to the implant. This piece will support your final restoration.

Placement of Your Custom Crown

A beautifully crafted crown or other restoration is secured to the abutment to complete your new, fully functional tooth or teeth. Your restoration is designed to match the color and shape of your natural teeth for a seamless result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do dental implants hurt?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia ensures a comfortable experience during implant placement, and any post-procedure soreness is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers.

How long does the implant process take?

The full process typically takes 3-6 months, depending on healing time and whether any additional procedures like bone grafting are needed.

Are dental implants safe?

Yes. Dental implants have a very high success rate and are considered a safe, well-established treatment when placed by an experienced dentist.

How much do dental implants cost?

Pricing varies depending on how many implants are needed and whether any preparatory treatments are required. During your consultation, we will provide a clear estimate and review your options.

Are implants better than dentures?

For many patients, yes. Implants provide superior comfort, stability, and chewing power compared to removable dentures, and they help preserve jawbone health over time.
