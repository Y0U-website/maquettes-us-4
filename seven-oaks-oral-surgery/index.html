Millions of Americans lose teeth every year. Bridges and dentures have been the only options for replacing missing teeth for a long time. However, with advances in dental technology, dental implants have become a more effective option for many people available.

Dental implants are metal posts screwed into the jawbone to serve as new tooth roots for prosthetic teeth. After application, they look, work, and feel like natural teeth. While bridgework or dentures may slip, make noise, or damage your jawbone, implants fuse with your jawbone, eliminating these issues. Implants are an ideal treatment option for adult patients with good oral health.
