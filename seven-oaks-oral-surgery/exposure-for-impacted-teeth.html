What to Expect During Treatment

Exposure surgery for impacted teeth is a carefully planned and precise procedure designed to improve oral health. Here’s what you can expect during treatment:

Consultation and Planning : Before the exposure surgery, we will assess your oral health, take X-rays if necessary, and discuss the treatment plan with you. This planning phase ensures that the procedure is tailored to your specific needs.

Anesthesia and Sedation : Depending on your individual preferences and the complexity of the case, you may receive local anesthesia to numb the surgical area or intravenous (IV) sedation to help you relax during the surgery. Your oral surgeon will determine the most suitable option for you.

Surgical Exposure : During the procedure, your oral surgeon will carefully expose the impacted tooth by making a small incision in the gum tissue. If necessary, they may also remove any obstructions, such as bone or tissue, to allow the tooth to emerge properly.

Stitching and Recovery : After the tooth is exposed, the incision will be sutured closed. You’ll receive post-operative instructions on how to care for the surgical site during your recovery. It’s essential to follow these instructions closely to minimize the risk of complications and promote healing.

Recovery and Post-Treatment Care : You may experience some discomfort and swelling in the days following the surgery, but these symptoms can be managed with pain medication and cold compresses. Your oral surgeon will schedule follow-up appointments to monitor your progress and ensure the exposed tooth erupts correctly.
