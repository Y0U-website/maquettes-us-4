All-on-4 Treatment Process

  • Initial Consultation and Assessment The first step in the All-on-4 treatment process is an initial consultation. During this visit, our oral surgeons will carefully evaluate your oral health, discuss your treatment goals, and address any questions or concerns you may have.
  • Treatment Planning and Customization Using advanced digital technology, our team will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. We will take detailed impressions and images of your teeth and jaw, allowing us to design the ideal placement of the implants and the customized prosthetic teeth attached to them.
  • Implant Placement and Temporary Restoration Our skilled oral surgeons will surgically place the four implants into the predetermined locations in your jawbone. This is typically done under local anesthesia or sedation to ensure your comfort. Once the implants are securely in place, a temporary restoration will be attached, allowing you to leave our office with a functional set of teeth while your implants heal and integrate with the bone.
  • Healing Process and Final Restoration Placement Over the next few months, your implants will undergo osseointegration , fusing with the surrounding bone, providing a strong and stable foundation. Once your implants have fully healed, we will proceed with the final step of the process. Our team will carefully craft and attach your permanent, custom-designed replacement teeth.
