The practice

Thousands of people have come to trust the friendly and honest approach of Childrens Dentistry and Orthodontics at Pahrump along with the easy appointment scheduling, flexible payment plans and convenient office location.

Frenectomies A frenectomy removes the connective tissue membrane between the two front teeth and is a very simple surgery. We provide this surgery at the Sun Valley Surgery Center.

Special Needs Dentistry We provide comprehensive preventive and therapeutic dental care to patients who have special healthcare needs.

Sedation Dentistry Sedation is most commonly used during extensive procedures or for patients with dental phobia or who find it difficult to sit still.

Orthodontics An orthodontist is a doctor who specializes in realigning the jaw, teeth or bite patterns of a patient.

Early Treatment for Children Early dental treatment can help prevent problems from impacting a child’s health, well-being, self-image and overall achievement.

Adolescent Treatment Pediatric dentistry treats the oral health needs of adolescents. Adolescence is often the time when orthodontics should be considered.

Digital X-Rays Digital X-rays can reduce your child’s exposure to radiation by up to 80 % compared with traditional X-rays!
