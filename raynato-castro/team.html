Our Team

Dr. van Dyk is a graduate of the University of the Pacific School of Dentistry in San Francisco. He participated in a general dentistry residency at Madigan Army Medical Center at Ft. Lewis, Washington, before opening a practice in San Pablo in 1976. He has taught continuously at the University since returning to the Bay Area and is a noted lecturer traveling around the US sharing information related to the successful practice of dentistry.

He participates in many community organizations including the Contra Costa College Foundation, the San Pablo Rotary, the San Pablo Chamber of Commerce, and Brookside Foundation.

He lives in Pt. Richmond with his wife, Margi, and enjoys travel and regular exercise, often together, including climbing to the top of Mt. Whitney and Mt. Kilimanjaro, and walking across England.

Originally born in Southern California, Dr. Jill Alderfer grew up on the North Carolina coast. She received her bachelor’s degree from North Carolina State University and immediately moved to Mongolia for two years as a member of the Peace Corps. Following this, Dr. Alderfer volunteered at an orphanage in Uganda before coming back to the United States and pursuing her dental dreams in California.

Dr. Alderfer earned her doctorate from the University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry in 2017. While in dental school, Dr. Alderfer excelled in many areas and received the George Hughes Award for excellence in Prosthodontics. She completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at UCSF where she treated patients with complex medical and dental needs.

In her free time, she enjoys exploring the many vegetarian restaurants around the bay and hiking with her dogs and husband. She is also an active member of the Pinole Rotary Club.

Wendy joined our office in 2002 while at the Middle College High School as an administrative assistant. While continuing to work for us she earned an Associate Degree from Contra Costa College and went on to Western Career College for her dental hygiene degree and license. We’ve enjoyed watching her progress in her career and at the same time meet the needs of the practice and our patients. Outside of the office, Wendy enjoys spending time with her husband Luis, two girls Isabella and Julianna, as well as her new dog Neno. By the way, she is a huge Giants fan!

Gina graduated from the Diablo Valley College Dental Hygiene Program in 2012 after earning a bachelor’s degree at Sacramento State. She started with our office after graduation and immediately demonstrated the compassion and excellence which we strive to achieve. She lives in Vallejo with her husband, Taylor. Her other career is as a singer and actor in local productions.

Serena is an El Sobrante native who has been a valued member of the practice since 2018. As a Registered Dental Assistant, her strong technical skills and attention to detail help ensure patients feel comfortable and well cared for during their visit. Known for her friendly personality and dedication, Serena is an important part of the team and takes pride in supporting both patients and colleagues. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her three beloved dogs, going out with friends, and finding balance between her career and personal life.

Eli has over 20 years of experience in the dental field and is proud to be a member of Dr. Castro’s office since 2022. She truly enjoys being a part of a supportive and dedicated work family. Outside of the office, she loves spending time outdoors, especially hiking and going on trips with her family.

Born and raised in the Bay Area, Isabel completed her Bachelor’s in Dental Hygiene at West Coast University in Orange County. Passionate about heathcare and her local community, she has actively engaged in volunteer and outreach programs with the goal of impacting people’s lives positively. Outside of the office, she enjoys spending quality time with her family and her dog as well as trying new coffe and boba places.
