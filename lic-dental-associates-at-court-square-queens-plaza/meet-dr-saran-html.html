Get to Know Dr. Ishwinder Saran

Why Did You Want to Become a Dentist?

Dr. Ishwinder Saran believes strongly in establishing trusted patient-doctor relationships, valuable patient education, and high-quality dentistry that not only restores the patient’s oral health, but also contributes to the enhancement of their overall health. His philosophy is to cater to each patient s dental needs by formulating a multi-disciplinary treatment plan that will best suit their individual requirements.

Where did you Study Dentistry?

Dr. Saran received his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine, which is one of the top dental schools in the nation. He trained for an additional year in general dentistry (Advanced Education in General Dentistry) at the Stratton VA Medical Center in Albany, NY, treating healthy and medically compromised veterans for their dental needs.

More recently, Dr. Saran has completed the Core Workshop series at Spear Education, studying advanced topics like restorative design, occlusion, the treatment of worn dentition, treatment for TMD, and much more. He also leads a local Spear Study Club that consists of a general dentist and multiple specialists, where the group collaborates on real-world cases and share valuable clinical insights.

His professional affiliations include memberships in the American Dental Association, the NY State Dental Association, and the MA Dental Society.

Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Saran grew up in Cleveland, Ohio and spent many years living in Boston before moving to LIC in 2014. He loves the dynamic and diverse nature of this vibrant neighborhood. He especially loves living and working near the water and taking long strolls along the LIC waterfront. When he is not in the dental office, Dr. Saran enjoys traveling, reading, cooking, and discovering all that NYC has to offer.
