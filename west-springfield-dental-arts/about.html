Melissa Smiley, DMD.

Born and raised in Saint Petersburg, Florida, Dr. Melissa Smiley attended the Center for Advanced Technologies at Lakewood High School. She then graduated from Florida State University with a B.S. in Exercise Science. While volunteering at the Leon County Dental Clinic in Tallahassee, she decided to become a dentist. She served as a board member of the FSU Pre-Dental Society. She graduated from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

Before moving to Virginia, Dr. Smiley was practicing dentistry at a not-for-profit clinic in Tampa, Florida. While working with an underserved population, she also took on the role of training assistants in their clinical practice. She enjoys treating patients of all ages with restorative and cosmetic dentistry..

Outside the Office and In the Community: In her spare time, Dr. Smiley enjoys spending time with her friends, family and two dogs. She also enjoys going to the beach, reading, and working out at a neighborhood gym. She’s an avid runner, having run multiple half marathons and will run her first Army Ten Miler this Fall.

Yeliz Swayne, DDS.

Dr. Yeliz Swayne was born and raised in Burke, Virginia, and attended Lake Braddock High School. She is very excited to join West Springfield Dental Arts, where she can practice and serve the community in which she grew up. Dr. Swayne received her B.S. in Biology from the University of Virginia and her M.S. in Physiology from Georgetown University. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at Virginia Commonwealth University.

Following dental school, Dr. Swayne completed a General Practice Residency program at the Baltimore VA Medical Center, where she received additional training in advanced dental procedures, including implant dentistry, endodontics, oral surgery, and hospital dentistry. Since then, Dr. Swayne has worked in private practice in Virginia.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Swayne enjoys spending time with her husband and family, traveling, playing soccer, and exploring restaurants in DC. Dr. Swayne is also an avid sports fan, and loves cheering on the DC and UVA sports teams!

Maryam Malik, DMD.

Dr. Maryam Malik grew up in Georgia and earned her Bachelor of Science in Microbiology from the University of Georgia before receiving her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) from the University of Louisville, graduating with honors from both institutions.

From a young age, Dr. Malik knew she wanted to become a dentist, and she has built her career around helping patients feel comfortable, cared for, and confident in their smiles. She is passionate about providing comprehensive, patient-centered dentistry, with a focus on cosmetic and restorative care, Invisalign®, and implant placement and restoration.

Dr. Malik is grateful for the mentors who have helped shape her journey and remains committed to lifelong learning. She has pursued advanced training in Invisalign®, implantology, oral surgery, and laser dentistry, while staying current with the latest technology to provide high-quality, modern care for her patients.

Outside the office, Dr. Malik enjoys spending time with family and friends and exploring creative hobbies. Most recently, she has enjoyed building miniature 3D wooden models and is always excited to discover new creative pursuits.
