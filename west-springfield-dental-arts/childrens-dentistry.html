Children s Dentistry

0 For ALL Members of the Family. We LOVE treating little smiles! One of the highlights of our days is when we see a child have a great dental experience. As a family dentist, our doctors are trained to care for patients of ALL ages. You child doesn’t have to see a specialist for general dental needs. Your family can be seen at one practice, saving time, and likely money as well by not seeing a specialist.

We prefer to see children by the age of three and every six months afterwards. This helps establish good oral care routines and will allow us to treat any concerns while they are minor and can be easily treated. We take our time and explain everything we do in language that they can comprehend.

To make things fun for children, in addition to our "Treasure Box", we have our "No Cavity Club" to reward children for good oral hygiene. It is our goal to create good dental patients for life!

No Cavity Club

We care about ALL of our patients dental hygiene but pay special attention to starting our younger ones with good habits. Our No Cavity Club is designed to inform and reward these patients for maintaining excellent dental health!
