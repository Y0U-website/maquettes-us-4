What Are Dental Implants?

Dental Implants are a permanent tooth loss solution for those in need of a stunning new smile. Although there are other restorative options, dental implants are superior because they’re attached directly to your jawbone, providing full and strong restoration. This natural solution renews your oral health and completely restores the function of your teeth from root to crown.

Our Dental Implant Options offered at Smile Creator

Single-Tooth Implants

Single-tooth implants are typically recommended for patients missing one or more teeth. This solution is highly recommended because it doesn’t cause any damage to the natural neighboring teeth.

Multi-Tooth Implants

Multi-tooth implants are a great choice to replace a traditional dental bridge . Unlike conventional bridges that rely on natural teeth to stay in place, implant-supported bridges don’t rely on adjacent natural teeth, and can even strengthen them by providing support.

Full Arch Replacements

Full arch replacements consist of replacing an entire arch of teeth with implant supported dentures . Unlike traditional dentures that are removable, this implant solution stays in your mouth and restores the overall health and attractive appearance of your smile.
