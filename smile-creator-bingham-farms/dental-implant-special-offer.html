AI-Assisted Implant Special Offer

Michigan Dentist and his Revolutionary Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Technology for Dental Implants

Thanks to the innovative work of Dr. Timothy Kosinski of Bingham Farms, Michigan AI-assisted dental implants are within reach of nearly everyone. Dr. Timothy F. Kosinski, a Bingham Farms dentist, is using artificial intelligence to create dental implants . Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is a branch of computer science that analyzes, assesses, and learns from data in order to improve accuracy and make predictions, going beyond human-created results.

In dentistry, AI can be used to create crowns and implants that are precise, functional, and accurate. Learning from a database of dental records, AI recognizes patterns, shapes, and anomalies in teeth in order to create 3D models that will fit a specific patient’s teeth. For patients, this means their implants will be customized to their teeth in both quality and functionality.

“AI algorithms assess the variations available in the database, producing realistic esthetics for crowns that blend in with existing dentition,” Dr. Kosinski says. “The collection of this vast information is leveraged to produce a high-quality, functional and esthetic crown design for the given clinical situation.”

Dr. Kosinski is looking for up to 16 individuals who are willing to be evaluated to see if they are eligible for AI-assisted dental implants. This procedure will be a part of Dr. Kosinski’s teaching program for doctors learning his techniques in implant dentistry.

Candidates must be missing upper or lower back teeth and have no uncontrolled medical problems. A no-fee evaluation will be completed to determine if they are candidates for this teaching program. Once selected, the individuals will receive a dental implant for $799 – an incredible $1,000 savings . After 3-4 months of healing, an abutment and crown over the implant will be created by Dr. Kosinski, also for a significantly reduced fee. All patients accepted in this program will require a CT scan, or three-dimensional X-ray to idealize their situation.

About Dr. Timothy F. Kosinski, DDS, MAGD

Dr. Timothy Kosinski is an Affiliated Adjunct Clinical Professor at the University Of Detroit Mercy School Of Dentistry and serves on the editorial review board of Reality, the information source for esthetic dentistry, the Michigan Dental Association Journal and is the Associate Editor of the Academy of General Dentistry journals.

He is a Past President of the Michigan Academy of General Dentistry and served as editor of its journal. Dr. Kosinski received his DDS from the University of Detroit Mercy Dental School and his Mastership in Biochemistry from Wayne State University School of Medicine. He is a Diplomat of the American Board of Oral Implantology/Implant Dentistry, the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and the American Society of Osseointegration. He is a Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry and received his Mastership in the Academy of General Dentistry.

Dr. Kosinski has received many honors including Fellowship in the American and International Colleges of Dentists and the Academy of Dentistry International. He is a member of OKU and the Pierre Fauchard Academy. Dr. Kosinski was the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry Alumni Association’s Alumnus of the Year. In 2009 and 2014 he received the Academy of General Dentistry’s Lifelong Learning and Service Recognition.

Dr. Kosinski has placed more than 18,000 dental implants, published over 240 articles on the surgical and prosthetic phases of implant dentistry and was a contributor to the textbooks, Principles and Practices of Implant Dentistry, and 2010’s Dental Implantation and Technology.
