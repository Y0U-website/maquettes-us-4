We Can Help Ease Your Anxiety

We offer intravenous sedation in this office if you prefer to doze off during the procedure.

A small butterfly needle is placed in your arm, and small amounts of Valium and Demerol are administered slowly throughout your surgery. You’ll feel ultimate relaxation, but won’t be completely asleep. If you are asked to open your eyes or turn your head you’ll be capable of doing so.

Instead of feeling apprehensive about what’s going on around you, you may just drift off into a light daze with no worries. Most patients lose track of time and are a little surprised to find we are finished.

During your free implant consultation, we’ll provide you with all the necessary information to help you make the right decision for your smile.

What happens during my implant consultation?

During your dental implant consultation, Dr. Kosinski will discuss your smile goals with you, go over your medical history, and map out the process for you to better understand.

After that, he will need to perform an oral exam and take dental x-rays of your mouth so he can see if you’re a viable candidate for dental implants. He will check the health of your gums and surrounding teeth, plus look to see if you obtain sufficient bone density.

Once he examines your mouth, he will create a more personalized treatment plan for your smile. This provides you with exactly how many implants you need and whether or not a supplemental procedure is necessary.

Do I need to bring anything to my implant consultation?

When you visit for your dental implant consultation, generally we recommend that patients bring a record of their medical history and insurance. This way we can provide you with a better treatment plan and a more accurate estimate of your entire costs.

However, if you forget these items, don’t worry at all. If you decide to choose us for your dental implant procedure, we can evaluate these documents at your first appointment.
