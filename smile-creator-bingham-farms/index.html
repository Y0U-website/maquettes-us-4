If you want to maintain a healthy smile, make sure to visit your dentist at least twice a year for a professional cleaning and exam. Dr. Kosinski may recommend that some patients come in more often, such as those who have crowded teeth or are especially prone to cavities. But for most patients, coming in every six months is enough to keep their smile healthy.

Many patients suffer from dental anxiety and avoid getting the care they need as a result. Thankfully, Dr. Kosinski offers IV and oral sedation to help you feel relaxed during treatment. No matter which option you choose, Dr. Kosinski will monitor you closely and ensure your safety. With the help of sedation, you never have to feel anxious about visiting the dentist again.

Our practice is a family-friendly dentistry and Dr. Kosinski is happy to treat patients of all ages. If you just want to bring your child with you to your own appointment, that’s perfectly fine too. In fact, we highly recommend bringing your child to the dentist from an early age, as this will help prevent them from developing dental anxiety in the future.
