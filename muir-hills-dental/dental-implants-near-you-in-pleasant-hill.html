What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts that are placed at the roots of missing teeth. They create a strong, stable base for tooth replacements that look, feel, and function like natural teeth.

Dental implants stay fixed in place and do not move, which helps you eat, speak, and smile with confidence every day.

Unlike removable dentures, implants become part of your jaw. They support long-term dental health and protect your bone structure. When teeth are missing, your jawbone can weaken over time. Dental implants help prevent this bone loss and support your natural facial shape.

Teeth implants do not decay like natural teeth, but maintaining healthy gums is still essential. Daily brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits help protect your investment and keep your implants strong for years. With proper care, dental implants can last for life.

Before teeth implant dental placement, detailed X-rays and impressions are taken to carefully evaluate your jawbone and surrounding teeth. This step ensures that we customize your dental implant treatment according to your needs.

Do I Need Bone Grafting Before Getting Teeth Implants?

Some patients need bone grafting before receiving dental implants. This is because their jawbone has weakened and lost bone volume after tooth loss. A strong jawbone is essential to support an implant and keep it secure in the long term.

Bone grafting helps rebuild and strengthen your jaw so teeth implants can stay stable and function like natural teeth. This step provides long-term success for implant dental treatment. Not every patient needs bone grafting, but it plays a key role when bone loss is present.
