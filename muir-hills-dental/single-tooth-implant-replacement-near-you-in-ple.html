How Single Tooth Dental Implants Work at Our Dental Office?

FREE Initial Consultation and Planning

During your initial visit, our dentist will conduct a comprehensive dental examination, including X-rays or 3D scans, to assess your jawbone and determine your suitability for single dental implants.

The dentist will also treat any underlying issues, such as decay or gum disease, before proceeding with implant placement.

Preparation and Bone Grafting (If Needed)

If your jawbone requires reinforcement, bone grafting may be recommended to provide a strong foundation for your implant.

Implant Placement Surgery

Once ready, your tooth implant is placed into the jawbone during a surgical procedure under local anesthesia, serving as the root for your new tooth.

Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, your jawbone will heal and naturally integrate with the implant, creating a stable base.

Abutment and Crown Placement

After healing, an abutment is added to your implant, followed by a custom crown designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth. In some cases, immediate restoration with lifelike crowns may be available.

Follow-Up and Care

Regular check-ups and proper oral hygiene ensure the longevity and success of your single dental implant.
