Dental Implant Restorations

These restorations are securely anchored to the dental implants placed by your surgeon, providing the strength and stability needed for everyday chewing and speaking.

  • Implant-supported bridges
  • Implant-retained dentures
  • Full-mouth dental implants (All-on-X restorations)

How Dental Implants Work

Dental implants replace missing teeth with a titanium post that acts like an artificial root. It bonds with the bone during the healing period, creating a strong foundation for your dental restoration.

We receive a detailed report from your surgeon so we can begin planning your restoration as soon as healing allows.

  • Consultation and planning: Your surgeon evaluates your bone health and determines whether a bone graft or tooth extractions are needed.
  • Implant placement: A titanium implant post is placed into the jaw during a brief surgical procedure.
  • Healing period: The bone integrates with the implant. A healing abutment may be placed to shape the gums.
  • Final restoration: Once the implant is stable, we design the custom-made crown, bridge, or denture that attaches to it.

Our Approach to Implant Restorations

Both offices use digital scanners, modern materials, and trusted local dental labs to create long-lasting implant restorations. Your restoration is designed for strength, comfort, and a lifelike appearance.

Because every case is unique, we take time to review your goals, bite, and oral health before finalizing any restorative options.

Full-Arch and All-on-X Restorations

If you are replacing all or most of your teeth, your surgeon may recommend an All-on-4 or All-on-X procedure. This approach uses four or more strategically placed implants to support a full set of replacement teeth.

We provide the final full-arch restoration once the implants have healed. These restorations are:

Patients enjoy improved stability compared to traditional dentures and greater confidence when eating and speaking.

  • Secure and comfortable
  • Custom-designed for a natural look
  • A long-term solution for advanced tooth loss

What to Expect When Receiving Your Implant Restoration

Your visit is designed to be comfortable and efficient. Here’s what you can expect:

Most patients adjust quickly as their new restoration feels much like a natural tooth.

  • Digital scan: We take precise digital impressions.
  • Custom design: We work with skilled dental labs for detailed, natural-looking results.
  • Fitting: For multi-tooth and full-arch cases, a trial fitting may be done to check comfort and bite.
  • Final placement: Your implant crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the support post.

Caring for Your Implant Restoration

A dental implant can last many years with proper home care and regular dental visits. To protect your restoration:

We will review personalized care tips based on the type of restoration you receive.

  • Brush twice daily
  • Floss or use interdental brushes around the implant
  • Avoid using your teeth to open packages
  • Keep up with routine dental exams and teeth cleanings
  • Treat gum disease early to prevent damage to the implant site

How long does a dental implant take?

The full process, from implant placement to final restoration, typically takes 3-6 months or more. Healing time varies based on bone health and whether a bone graft is needed.

What are dental implants made of?

Most dental implant posts are made of titanium or titanium alloys. These materials bond well with bone and are safe for long-term use.

Are dental implants painful?

Implant placement is performed by an oral surgeon using local anesthesia and comfort-focused techniques. Most patients report mild soreness afterward, often managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Are dental implants safe?

Dental implants have a long track record of safety and predictable results when placed by trained professionals. Your surgeon will review your medical history to see if you are a good candidate.
