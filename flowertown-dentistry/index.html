The practice

Flowertown Dentistry is situated next to the Dorchester County Building in a growing part of downtown Summerville with a mix of local services and shopping just steps away. This charming stretch of North Cedar Street places you just blocks from Summerville’s historic Main Street, with its cafes, shops, and community attractions.

Our office is fully accessible and offers free on-site parking for all patients. A wheelchair-accessible entrance is located on the side of the building. If you let us know in advance, we’ll be ready to ensure a smooth and comfortable arrival. A doorbell at this entrance alerts our team when you’ve arrived.

We are conveniently located for patients from Ladson, Goose Creek, Lincolnville, Knightsville, Sangaree, Nexton, Carnes Crossroads, Cane Bay, Wescott, Summers Corner, Legend Oaks, The Ponds, Ridgeville, St. George and many other nearby communities.

At Flowertown Dentistry, patients receive personalized, patient-centered care built on compassion, communication, and trust. The practice has been part of the Summerville community for many years, with a continued focus on prevention, comfort, and thoughtful dental care.

Dr. Tiffany Deaton leads the practice with an emphasis on preventive care, minimally invasive treatment, and clear communication. Her gentle, relationship-based approach helps patients feel informed, confident, and comfortable throughout every visit.

Services

Cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing cannot reach. They also support fresher breath and healthier gums.

Deep cleanings treat active gum disease by removing bacteria beneath the gumline and supporting gum healing.

Our team restores single implants, implant bridges, and full-arch cases with custom-made crowns and prosthetics designed for a natural look and stable fit.

Bonding repairs chips, cracks, and minor cosmetic concerns using tooth-colored composite that blends naturally with your smile.
