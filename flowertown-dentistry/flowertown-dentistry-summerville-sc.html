Parking & Accessibility

Our office is fully accessible and offers free on-site parking for all patients. A wheelchair-accessible entrance is located on the side of the building. If you let us know in advance, we’ll be ready to ensure a smooth and comfortable arrival. A doorbell at this entrance alerts our team when you’ve arrived.

We are conveniently located for patients from Ladson, Goose Creek, Lincolnville, Knightsville, Sangaree, Nexton, Carnes Crossroads, Cane Bay, Wescott, Summers Corner, Legend Oaks, The Ponds, Ridgeville, St. George and many other nearby communities.

About Us

At Flowertown Dentistry, patients receive personalized, patient-centered care built on compassion, communication, and trust. The practice has been part of the Summerville community for many years, with a continued focus on prevention, comfort, and thoughtful dental care.

Dr. Tiffany Deaton leads the practice with an emphasis on preventive care, minimally invasive treatment, and clear communication. Her gentle, relationship-based approach helps patients feel informed, confident, and comfortable throughout every visit.

Services We Provide

Drs. Rhett and Tiffany Deaton offer comprehensive dental care for children and adults at at Deaton Dental Associates and Flowertown Dentistry, including:

Dental Exams

Routine dental exams allow us to monitor your oral health, evaluate changes over time, and identify concerns early.

Professional Teeth Cleaning

Cleanings remove plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing cannot reach. They also support fresher breath and healthier gums.

Scaling and Root Planing

Deep cleanings treat active gum disease by removing bacteria beneath the gumline and supporting gum healing.

Dental Sealants

Sealants create a smooth protective layer over the grooves of back teeth, helping to prevent cavities

Dental Bonding

Bonding repairs chips, cracks, and minor cosmetic concerns using tooth-colored composite that blends naturally with your smile.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy treats infections within the tooth to relieve discomfort, preserve the natural tooth structure, and prevent the need for extraction.

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth cannot be restored, gentle extraction helps protect oral health.

Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings restore areas affected by decay while supporting long-term durability.

Dental Crowns

Crowns protect weakened or fractured teeth using strong, natural-looking materials designed to restore proper shape and bite.

Partial and Full Dentures

We offer complete and partial dentures, as well as implant-supported options, to restore comfort, appearance, and chewing strength.

Dental Bridges

Bridges replace missing teeth and improve everyday chewing and speaking. Both traditional and implant-supported bridge options are available.

Dental Implant Restorations

Our team restores single implants, implant bridges, and full-arch cases with custom-made crowns and prosthetics designed for a natural look and stable fit.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening brightens your smile using safe ingredients that lift stains and create a more uniform tooth shade.

Veneers

Composite and ceramic veneers improve shape, color, and symmetry using durable materials designed for natural aesthetics.

Custom Mouthguards

We offer custom night guards, athletic guards, and snore guards designed for a precise, comfortable fit.

Emergency Dental Care

Urgent dental issues can happen unexpectedly. Both locations offer same-day emergency care for concerns such as pain, swelling, dental injuries, and broken teeth.

Dental Membership Plan

Our Dental Membership Plan provides an easy, affordable alternative to traditional insurance, offering preventive care, exclusive savings on additional treatment, no deductibles or waiting periods, and simple monthly or annual payment options. Joining is quick, and your benefits begin right away.

What Our Patients Are Saying
