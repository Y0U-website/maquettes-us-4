Meet Our Dentists

Dr. Thanh-Xuan Nguyen Dr. Nguyen, a Louisiana native, grew up in Opelousas and is the second child out of three siblings. She attended Louisiana State University in Baton Rouge where she received her undergraduate degree in biology. She then received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from LSU School of Dentistry in 2011. She also completed postgraduate training as an IMPLANT RESTORATIVE FELLOWSHIP under the Periodontics department at LSUSD in 2012.

Gently, patient, and caring, Dr. Nguyen takes the time to provide you with the best care possible. She believes in conservative, preventative dentistry and her goal is to increase the longevity of her patient’s teeth.

Dr. Nguyen is dedicated to stay up-to-date with the latest dental materials and technology through continuing education courses. Her hobbies include baking and crafts. She also loves working with children.

Dr. Doan Bui

Dr. Bui was born and raised right here in Fort Worth, TX. He went to high school down the road at Fossil Ridge High School and then attended The University of Texas at Austin. After, he received his dental education at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry and decided to moved back to his hometown in Fort Worth.

With the utmost care and attention to detail, Dr. Bui believes in treating every patient as he would a family member. Dr. Bui stays up to date in today’s ever changing world in dentistry taking continuing education in Invisalign, restoring implants, and occlusion.

He is the youngest of nine siblings. He enjoys cooking(and eating!), playing the guitar, attending sports and music shows, reading, and watching TV and movies. He is also a Game of Thrones fanatic, so he won’t stop talking about it if you bring it up, you have been warned!

Dr. Linda Dinh

Dr. Dinh was born and raised in Fort Worth, TX. She attended Birdville High School and graduated top 10 in her class. Dr. Dinh received her Bachelor of Business Administration with Honors and Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Oklahoma. After being away for eight years for education, she decided to move back to the DFW area to establish deep roots and be closer to her family.

Volunteering is very important to Dr. Dinh. She has previously volunteered with Mission Arlington, MobileSmiles, Good Shepherd, Catholic Charities, Oklahoma Mission of Mercy, and many more. She is also an active member of Texas Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, and American Dental Association.

Dr. Dinh strives to provide the highest quality of dental care tailored specifically to you. Everyone will be treated with the utmost respect and care, just like family. She will always put your needs before her own and make your visit a positive experience.

In her spare time, Dr. Dinh loves to spend time with her family, friends, and 90-pound Labrador Retriever.

Dr. Melody Basta

Dr. Basta was born and raised in Southeast Texas and later moved to Austin to attend the University of Texas. She received her Bachelor of Science degree in Human Biology, concentrating in Cellular, Molecular, and Developmental Biology. As an added bonus, it was there she met her husband, Chase, a Montana transplant who tolerates the hot Texas weather surprisingly well.

Following her time in Austin, she headed to Dallas to attend Texas A&M College of Dentistry, remaining active in community and volunteer opportunities in the DFW area and graduating with honors in 2019.

Her passion for dentistry stems from her desire not only to improve the health of her patients, but to give them the confidence they deserve with a smile they can be proud of. She strives to provide comprehensive and comfortable care, tailoring every experience to the needs of her patients.

Outside of work, Dr. Basta stays very busy at home with her husband, three young children, and rescue dog Marilo. Together, they enjoy all of the wonderful things Texas has to offer- lots of sunshine, great BBQ, and Dallas Cowboys football.
