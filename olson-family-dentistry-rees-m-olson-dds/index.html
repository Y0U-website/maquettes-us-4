Olson Family Dentistry, Placentia CA

For nearly 50 years, Olson Family Dentistry has been providing caring, premium dental care for families in the Placentia-Yorba Linda area.

The practice

Working together, the father-and-son team of Dr. Craig T. Olson and Dr. Rees M. Olson created beautiful, healthy smiles for their patients. Dr. Craig is now retired but Dr. Rees continues the practice his father started, reflecting his deep commitment to the oral health of the residents of North Orange County.

Excellence in dentistry doesn’t just happen. It’s the result of extensive training, years of experience, and a commitment to North Orange County to bring you the latest, most effective dental care and advanced technology available.
