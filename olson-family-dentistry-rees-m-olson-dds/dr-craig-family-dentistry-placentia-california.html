Dr. Craig & History Of The Practice

For nearly 50 years, Olson Family Dentistry has been providing caring, premium dental care for families in the Placentia-Yorba Linda area. Working together, the father-and-son team of Dr. Craig T. Olson and Dr. Rees M. Olson created beautiful, healthy smiles for their patients.

Retired, Dr. Craig has passed the torch to his son Dr. Rees, who is the owner and lead dentist at Olson Family Dentistry.

Craig T. Olson, DDS, FAGD (Emeritus) 1946 - 2022

DDS: UCLA School of Dentistry, Los Angeles, CA

Diplomate: American Board of Orofacial Pain

Lecturer: University of California School of Dentistry, Los Angeles, CA (1980-2005)

“An essential element in providing quality dental care through the years has been a commitment to ongoing continuing education. The decision to offer excellent treatment to the entire family requires a continuous dedication to learning. I am pleased that Dr. Rees has made that commitment to excellence. I know our patients and friends in North Orange County are in good hands.”
