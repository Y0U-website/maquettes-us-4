Dental Office History Moorestown, NJ

In 1989 , Dr. Mark Walker took over Dr. Reid Porter’s dental practice. His goal was to put the word family in family practice. He practiced several months in Dr. Porter’s house…

The practice

In 1993 , Dr. Frank Truskoloski heard how Dr. Walker was doing things and asked him if he would take over his practice, which he did. With the practice flourishing and the staff growing (some have been with him for almost 30 years now!), he moved to the present location in 1996. Dr. Land joined the practice a few years later. Today , with the dental office being how he envisioned, Dr. Walker has been able to step aside with confidence that his patients are in great hands. He now has more time to spend with his family, play even more golf and caddy in some PGA events.

Dr. Walker is happily married to his wife Colette, and they have three grown children. Their youngest, Chelsea, was actually on season 39 of Survivor! They also have three amazing grandchildren, who will see a lot more of him now that he’s retired.

For Dr. Land, there’s nothing better than creating lasting relationships with her patients and their families. The nature of her job allows Dr. Land to meet and interact with people. Getting to foster their oral health and unlock their smile’s true potential brings her an unparalleled amount of joy. She hopes you’ll entrust your and your family’s smiles to her and looks forward to meeting you!…

Dr. Land has always felt that rather than stumbling upon dentistry, the career actually found her! When she isn’t assisting dental patients with their oral health, you can find Dr. Land leading an active and exciting lifestyle! She has a passion for seeing new places and enjoys traveling to various beaches, exercising, playing golf, and cooking all sorts of unique cuisines.
