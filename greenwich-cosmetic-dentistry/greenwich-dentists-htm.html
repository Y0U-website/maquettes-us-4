Monika Vermani, DMD, AEGD

Dr. Monkia Vermani graduated from Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 1998 and completed her residency with a concentration in advanced crown and bridge applications and full-mouth rehabilitation. She also taught in the pre-doctoral clinic at Boston University before relocating to Stamford, CT.

With more than 18 years of experience in Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry, Dr. Vermani is continuously evolving her skills while maintaining a patient-first philosophy. As an active member of the American Dental Association, the Connecticut State Dental Association and the Stamford Dental Society, she remains current with the latest advancements in technology and modern dentistry.

Passionate and dedicated to the art of dentistry, Dr. Vermani prides herself in helping patients make informed decisions through customized treatment plans along with clear guidance, education and support. From routine check-ups to emergency care to cosmetic work and replacement of missing teeth, Dr. Vermani provides a complete suite of dental services to her patients and their entire family.

Dr. Vermani’s primary focus is patient satisfaction. Referrals from clients are the greatest compliment to her business!

Dr. Vermani offers convenient extended business hours and financing options to ensure all her patients’ individual needs are met. She is also a certified Invisalign® provider and practitioner of proven relaxation techniques.

Oliver Viera, DDS, MMSc

Dr. Viera believes the right course of action is not always the simplest or the most extensive—it is the one that best serves the patient. He wants patients to feel heard and understood, comfortable asking questions, and confident in the decisions they make. Whether caring for a single tooth or planning a comprehensive rehabilitation, his guiding principle remains the same: to deliver care that is as thoughtful as it is personalized, balancing each patient’s goals with their long-term oral health.

Dr. Viera earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Touro College of Dental Medicine before completing specialty training in Prosthodontics and earning a Master of Medical Sciences at Harvard School of Dental Medicine. His advanced specialty training encompasses surgical, esthetic, and restorative care for patients with complex dental needs.

Outside the office, Dr. Viera enjoys spending time with his wife and son, furniture restoration, fine art, and music. That commitment to craftsmanship and lifelong learning continues to influence the thoughtful, personalized care he provides.

Dr. Viera is a member of the following professional societies:

  • Board Eligible, American Board of Prosthodontics
  • Member, Academy of Osseointegration
