Frequently Asked Questions

How do I care for my implants?

Just like natural teeth — regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups.

Is getting a dental implant painful?

Most patients are pleasantly surprised by how comfortable the procedure is. We use local anesthesia — and sedation options when needed — to keep you relaxed throughout. Some mild soreness or swelling is normal for a few days afterward and is typically managed well with over-the-counter pain relievers and our post-op care instructions.

Will I be without teeth during treatment?

Timelines vary by patient, but after the implant post is placed, healing typically takes a few months to allow proper osseointegration. In most cases, we can provide a temporary restoration during this period so your smile and daily function aren&#x27;t affected.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most healthy adults with adequate jawbone density are good candidates. Ideal candidates are non-smokers, free of uncontrolled chronic conditions, and committed to good oral hygiene. If bone loss is a concern, procedures like bone grafting can often create the foundation needed. Dr. Berrada will review your dental and medical history to determine the best plan for you.
