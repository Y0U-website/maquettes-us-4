About Dental Arts of Eagle

Dr. Shainholtz was married to his long time girlfriend, Anne, in the summer of 1983 in a wonderful rose garden service. Todd and Anne will celebrate their 33rd wedding anniversary this year. They have 30 year old son who is an architect in Frisco, Colorado. Also they have 27 year old daughter who works for the school district in speech and hearing, after receiving a bachelor’s degree in that field.

Dr. Todd likes to ski in the winter, as well as vacationing in various tropical locations involving both surfing and scuba diving. In the summertime he likes hiking, biking and hitting the mountain roads in his ’66 Bronco. Also he likes stand-up paddle boarding in the Colorado River and local lakes. He plays trombone in local musicals and even played with a rock band. He plays great music in his office, usually classic rock and the blues, but he takes requests!
