Frequently Asked Questions

When you need to replace one or multiple missing teeth, consider implants, or full dental implants, and other restorations. Our nearby team will help you choose the ideal treatment for your unique needs.

This treatment is a common and effective way to replace missing teeth. For most teeth implants, the success rate is 90 – 95 %.

The exact price depends on the type of restorative treatment used with the implant (crown, dentures, or bridge). It’s a good idea to talk to your insurance provider in advance to check available coverage. We also welcome you to call our dental office in your area for more information.

When you are proactive with good oral health habits, an implant can last for the rest of your life. Additionally, maintain consistent dental checkups to monitor the placement and strength of your implant.
