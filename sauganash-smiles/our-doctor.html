Our Doctor

Dr. Dalia Marouf

Dr. Dalia is a local Sauganash resident who is thrilled to invite you to her practice!

She is a Chicago native, who received her DMD degree from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 2013. With over 11 years experience in private practice, she has opened her office in the neighborhood she calls home.

Dr. Dalia is always continuing her education and leveraging technology to be able to offer her patients the latest in dentistry. She has taken additional training in Invisalign, implant dentistry, cosmetics, and botox for TMJ in order to provide her patients comprehensive care.

She is also an active member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Association and Chicago Dental Society. Locally, Dr. Dalia is a member of the Edgebrook-Sauganash Chamber of Commerce and Sauganash Community Association. She is also an active board member of the Sauganash Woman’s Club and Everyday Edgebrook.

Dr. Dalia is excited to be able to treat those in her community and surrounding areas. She loves getting to know her patients and creating a fun, stress free and judgement free environment.

Outside of the office, you can find her spending time with her husband and two young kids. She joined forces with a group of supermoms in the neighborhood, who have formed North Branch Market. They volunteer their time in bringing events to the community, including the Edgebrook Farmers Market, and Fests throughout the year. In the rare moments of free time, she enjoys a group fitness class or relaxing with a good Bravo show.
