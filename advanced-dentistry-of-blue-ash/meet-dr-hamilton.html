Dr. Sydney K. Hamilton, DDS

Dr. Sydney Hamilton joined the team at Advanced Dentistry of Blue Ash in July 2025, shortly after graduating from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. She chose a career in dentistry because it allows her to combine her love of both art and science, while making a real impact on people’s lives. Dr. Hamilton is passionate about helping patients feel confident in their smiles and strives to create a welcoming, comfortable experience for everyone she treats.

Outside of the office, Dr. Hamilton enjoys staying active and spending time with her family and friends. She loves exploring new restaurants and local coffee shops throughout the city. A sports fan at heart, you’ll often find her watching football. She also has a creative side and recently picked up watercolor painting as a relaxing artistic outlet. She’s also a proud dog mom to Reggie, her adorable cavapoo.

Dr. Hamilton is excited to be part of the ADBA team and is committed to providing compassionate, high-quality care to every patient she meets.
