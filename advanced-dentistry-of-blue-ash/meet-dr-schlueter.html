Dr. David J. Schlueter, DDS

Dr. Schlueter is a native Cincinnatian who grew up in Montgomery and attended Moeller High School. Upon graduation, Dr. Schlueter attended the University of Dayton and received a bachelor’s degree in biology. Dr. Schlueter earned his degree of Doctor of Dental Surgery from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry and he completed a General Practice Residency at Miami Valley Hospital. At that hospital, he refined his dental surgical skills and was licensed in IV sedation.

Dr. Schlueter is thrilled to be back in the Cincinnati area and to be able to offer high-quality dental treatment. He and his wife, Ashley, have a son named Andrew and a daughter named Annabelle. Dr. Schlueter is a member of the American Dental Association ®, the Ohio Dental Association and the Cincinnati Dental Society . Dr. Schlueter volunteers at University of Cincinnati Blue Ash Hygiene School with their UC Smiles and Dental Community Day donating dental services to the public and underserved kids. In his free time, Dr. Schlueter spends time with his family and is an avid Ohio State fan.
