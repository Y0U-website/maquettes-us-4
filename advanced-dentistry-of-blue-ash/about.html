Advanced Dentistry of Blue Ash

Advanced Dentistry of Blue Ash provides patients with high-quality dental care and a wide range of dental services. We pride ourselves on utilizing the latest dental technology and techniques to provide efficient and effective dental care.

Our team of experienced and friendly dental professionals are dedicated to ensuring your comfort and satisfaction during each visit. From routine cleanings and check-ups to more complex procedures such as dental implants and orthodontics, we strive to provide personalized and comprehensive care to meet your unique dental needs.

At Advanced Dentistry of Blue Ash, we also understand the importance of patient education. We take the time to explain your treatment options and answer any questions you may have, so that you can make informed decisions about your dental health.

Whether you’re looking for a basic cleaning or a complete smile makeover, Advanced Dentistry of Blue Ash is here to help you achieve your dental goals. Contact us today to schedule your appointment and experience the difference in our approach to dental care.

Meet the Dentist

Dr. David Schlueter was born and raised Cincinnati and is happy to be back in our area providing great dentistry to our patients. In addition to taking care of our clients, Dr. Schlueter volunteers at University of Cincinnati Blue Ash Hygiene School with their UC Smiles and Dental Community Day donating dental services to the public and underserved kids Dr. Schlueter attended the University of Dayton and received a bachelor’s degree in biology before earning his DDS from Ohio State University. In his free time, Dr. Schlueter spends time with his family and is an avid Ohio State fan.
