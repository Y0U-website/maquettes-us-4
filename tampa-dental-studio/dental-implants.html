Replacing a Full Arch

When you need to replace many teeth or a complete arch, implant-anchored restorations deliver stability that removable options can t match.

  • Implant-Supported Dentures — a few implants secure a full denture for a far more stable and secure alternative to regular dentures.
  • All-On-4 Implants — four strategically placed implants support a full arch of teeth, ideal for patients with insufficient bone density for traditional implants.

The Dental Implant Process

From your first consultation to the final restoration, our team guides you through every step of a proven, comfortable process.

  • Initial Consultation and Evaluation — we assess your oral health, take digital X-rays, and evaluate your jaw s bone density.
  • Implant Placement Surgery — titanium posts are surgically placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia for your comfort.
  • Healing and Osseointegration — over a few months, the implants fuse with the jawbone to form a stable foundation.
  • Attachment of the Restoration — a custom crown, bridge, or denture is placed to match your natural teeth.
  • Follow-Up Care — regular check-ups ensure your implants heal properly and last for years to come.
