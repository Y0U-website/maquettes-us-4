At National Dental, care extends beyond the chair. Smyles for Heroes provides full-arch dental implant restorations to deserving veterans and first responders, while Doshi Smiles delivers free reconstructive and craniofacial care to children and underserved communities in need.

As part of this mission, every National Dental patient who com- pletes a full-arch dental implant treatment receives $500 off their care, and National Dental donates $500 toward another patient in need through these charitable programs—allowing each restored smile to help create another.
