Why Patients Choose Same-Day Dental Implants

Teeth in a Day is a strong option for patients seeking a fast, efficient, and long-lasting solution to missing teeth. Common reasons include:

This procedure is especially ideal for patients with adequate bone density and minimal surgical risks.

  • Wanting to avoid multiple surgeries
  • Time-sensitive schedules (e.g., professional or personal events)
  • Difficulty wearing temporary dentures
  • Frustration with traditional removable appliances
  • Desire for quicker return to normal eating and speaking

Who Is a Good Candidate?

Not everyone is eligible for same-day implant placement. Candidates for Teeth in a Day typically meet the following criteria:

Patients who are not candidates may benefit from traditional implants or implant-supported overdentures , depending on bone quality and treatment goals.

  • Missing one or more teeth with stable oral health
  • Sufficient bone volume and density (verified by a 3D scan )
  • No uncontrolled chronic health conditions (e.g., diabetes, smoking-related concerns)
  • Not currently undergoing major dental or gum treatment
  • Have realistic expectations for function and aesthetics

How the Procedure Works

Teeth in a Day relies on digital planning and precision execution, made possible by our 3D imaging technology . Here’s what to expect:

Initial Consultation & Digital Imaging: Your jaw is scanned using a CBCT (cone beam CT) system to assess bone structure and determine implant positions.

Virtual Treatment Planning: Using specialized software, we design your new teeth and plan the exact angle, depth, and location of each implant.

Surgical Guide Fabrication: A custom surgical guide is made based on your anatomy to allow for accurate, flapless placement.

Implant Placement & Immediate Restoration: On the day of surgery, implants are placed using the guide, and a custom prosthesis is secured — all in about a day.

Follow-Up Care: You’ll return for monitoring and adjustments, ensuring the prosthetic remains comfortable and stable.

To enhance comfort, patients may choose from multiple sedation dentistry options, including IV sedation.

Recovery and Care Guidelines

Recovery is generally shorter than traditional implant surgeries, thanks to minimally invasive techniques. Here’s what to expect:

Comprehensive surgical instructions are provided before and after your visit to support healing.

  • Mild swelling or tenderness for 1–3 days
  • Return to normal activities within 1–2 days
  • Soft diet recommended for the first week
  • Daily oral hygiene and gentle cleaning of the prosthesis
  • Routine checkups to monitor healing and fit

FAQs: Teeth in a Day Is Teeth in a Day really completed in one visit? Yes, the surgical placement and prosthetic attachment occur in a single visit. However, planning, imaging, and design are done in advance.

What if I don’t have enough bone for same-day implants?

In that case, you may need bone grafting before implant placement or may be better suited for a staged procedure.

Will I need to wear a temporary denture during healing?

No — one of the main benefits of Teeth in a Day is that you leave the office with fixed, functional teeth.

Is the procedure painful?

It is typically well-tolerated under local anesthesia or sedation. Most patients report minimal discomfort afterward.

How long do the implants and prosthesis last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades. The prosthesis may require replacement after 10–15 years depending on wear.
