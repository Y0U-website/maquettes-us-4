Comparing the Long-Term Cost

While bridges and dentures typically involve a lower up-front cost, they require frequent maintenance and replacement — often every 5 to 10 years. These ongoing expenses can add up significantly over time.

When factoring in long-term performance, implants frequently offer better value and stability than other tooth replacement options. Learn more about long-term implant alternatives such as implant-supported overdentures or Teeth in a Day .

  • Are designed to last decades, often a lifetime with proper care
  • Preserve jawbone health, reducing future complications
  • Avoid the need for adhesives or relining, common with dentures
  • Provide greater day-to-day comfort and functionality

Implants and Jaw Bone Preservation

What many patients don’t realize is that traditional dentures and bridges do not prevent bone loss in the jaw. Over time, this bone deterioration can change the structure of the face — creating sunken cheeks, premature aging, and bite issues.
