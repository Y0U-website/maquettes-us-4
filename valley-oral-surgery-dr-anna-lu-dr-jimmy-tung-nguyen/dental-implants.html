Why Choose Implants Over Bridges or Dentures?

  • Preserve bone and facial structure: Implants prevent jawbone deterioration, which occurs after tooth loss.
  • Independent restoration: Unlike bridges, implants don’t rely on adjacent teeth for support.
  • Superior function and comfort: Implants feel and function like natural teeth — no shifting or adhesives.
  • Long-term cost efficiency: While the initial investment may be higher, implants typically last decades, reducing the need for replacements.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

If you’re unsure, we’ll evaluate your case and review all treatment options during your consultation.

  • Missing teeth: One or more teeth are absent.
  • Good health: Both general and oral health are stable.
  • Adequate bone volume: Sufficient bone to support implants (or willingness to undergo grafting).
  • Low risk factors: Free of uncontrolled diabetes, gum disease, or smoking-related complications.

FAQs About Dental Implants How long do dental implants last? With proper care, implants can last 20 years or more — often a lifetime. The crown or prosthesis may eventually require replacement due to normal wear.

Does the implant procedure hurt?

No. The area is fully numbed during surgery, and sedation is available if needed. Mild post-operative discomfort is usually managed with over-the-counter medication.

Can I get implants if I’ve worn dentures for years?

Yes. Many patients who have worn dentures for years are candidates, though some may require ridge augmentation or a sinus lift to rebuild bone before implant placement.

What is the success rate of implants?

Dental implants have a success rate of over 95 % in healthy patients who maintain good oral care and regular checkups.

Are dental implants covered by insurance?

Some insurance plans offer partial coverage. Our team will help verify your benefits and provide a detailed cost breakdown before treatment begins.
