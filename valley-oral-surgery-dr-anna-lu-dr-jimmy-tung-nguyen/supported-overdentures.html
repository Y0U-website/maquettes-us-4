Why Consider Implant-Supported Dentures?

Many patients choose implant-supported overdentures when traditional dentures no longer meet their functional or aesthetic needs. Common reasons include:

Implant-supported dentures provide a more natural-feeling solution, improving quality of life while reducing many of the frustrations associated with conventional dentures.

  • Loose fit: Dentures that shift or feel unstable
  • Difficulty eating/speaking: Problems with function in daily life
  • Chronic discomfort: Sore spots or pressure from traditional dentures
  • Bone loss: Jawbone shrinkage affecting facial appearance
  • Long-term stability: Desire for a secure, lasting solution

Who Is a Candidate?

You may be a strong candidate for implant-supported overdentures if you:

In some cases, bone grafting may be recommended before implant placement. To determine candidacy, we perform advanced diagnostics using our in-house 3D imaging system to assess bone quality and structure.

  • Missing teeth: All or most of the upper or lower teeth
  • Adequate bone: Sufficient jawbone volume to support implants
  • Dissatisfied with dentures: Poor fit or limited function with current dentures
  • Good health: Free from conditions that impair healing

How the Procedure Works

The process of placing implant-supported overdentures typically involves:

Note: Some patients may qualify for Teeth in a Day if immediate placement is appropriate.

  • Initial Consultation & Imaging: Clinical exam, medical history review, and 3D scan to plan treatment.
  • Implant Placement Surgery: Dental implants placed surgically into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation .
  • Healing Phase (3–6 months): Implants fuse with bone through osseointegration.
  • Overdenture Fabrication & Attachment: Custom denture designed and securely fitted once healing is complete.

What to Expect: Timeline, Recovery, and Care

We’ll review detailed surgical instructions and post-op care before your procedure.

  • Timeline: From start to finish, treatment typically takes 3 to 6 months, depending on bone health and healing time.
  • Recovery: Discomfort is usually mild and managed with over-the-counter medication. Most patients resume daily activities within a few days.
  • Care: Removable overdentures should be cleaned daily. Fixed overdentures require brushing and flossing similar to natural teeth. Regular checkups ensure implant health.

FAQs: Implant-Supported Overdentures How many implants are needed to support an overdenture? Typically, 2 to 4 implants are used for a lower overdenture, and 4 to 6 for an upper one. The exact number depends on bone density and prosthetic design.

Are implant overdentures removable?

Yes, most are removable by the patient for cleaning. However, fixed options are also available for those preferring a permanent solution.

Will I be without teeth during the healing phase?

No — a temporary denture is usually provided while the implants heal and integrate with the jawbone.

Is the procedure painful?

The placement is done under local anesthesia or sedation. Mild swelling and discomfort afterward are expected but manageable with standard pain relief.

How long do implant-supported overdentures last?

With good care, the implants can last decades. The denture portion may need replacement or adjustment every 7–10 years depending on wear.
