Dr. Grover began his dental career in New York City, where he gained valuable clinical and administrative experience working in multi-specialty dental practices. Drawing on that foundation, he established Simply Dental of Stamford in 2016 with a vision of providing comprehensive, patient-centered care under one roof.

For the past decade, Dr. Grover has proudly served the Stamford community. Together with his team of doctors, he is committed to delivering high-quality dental care to patients of all ages in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

A graduate of the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Grover earned his dental degree with clinical honors. He has completed both the Mini Residency and Advanced Implant Residency at Smile USA in Elizabeth, New Jersey, under the mentorship of world-renowned implant dentist Dr. Shankar Iyer. Committed to lifelong learning, Dr. Grover regularly attends advanced seminars and workshops to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry. He is an active member of the American Dental Association and the Connecticut State Dental Association.

Dr. Grover has also enjoyed working closely with Children’s Learning Centers of Fairfield County (CLC), providing dental education to students, parents, and staff to help promote lifelong oral health.

Outside the office, Dr. Grover enjoys spending time with his wife and their beautiful five-year-old daughter. Being a dad is one of his greatest joys, and he loves making the most of weekends with his family.

Madhumietha Arumugam, DMD General & Cosmetic And Implant Dentist

Fellow - American Academy of Implant Dentistry

https://aaid-implant.org/dentist/arumugam-madhumietha/

Implant Mini Residency trained - American Academy of Implant Dentistry recognized mini residency- Smile USA, New Jersey

Dr. Madhumietha Arumugam is committed to providing comprehensive, evidence-based dental care with a personalized approach focused on helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. A graduate with honors from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine’s Advanced Standing Program, she brings more than 16 years of combined experience as a periodontist and general dentist in India and as a general, cosmetic, and implant dentist in Connecticut. She has proudly served the Greater Stamford community at Simply Dental of Stamford for the past five years.

Dr. Arumugam is an Associate Fellow of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) and has completed an AAID-recognized Implant Mini Residency and Advanced Implant residency through Smile USA in New Jersey. She is Invisalign® and laser certified and maintains active memberships in the American Dental Association (ADA), Connecticut State Dental Association (CSDA), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID).

Dr. Arumugam’s extensive training encompasses restorative and cosmetic dentistry, periodontics, implant dentistry, endodontics, oral surgery, fixed and removable prosthodontics, laser dentistry, Invisalign®, and CAD-CAM restorations. By integrating her expertise in periodontal health with her passion for restorative dentistry, she delivers comprehensive care founded on the two essential pillars of long-term oral health: healthy gums and functional, beautiful teeth.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Arumugam enjoys reading fiction and non-fiction, blogging, learning new languages, exploring diverse cuisines, and spending quality time with her husband, daughter, and family. She is actively involved in organized dentistry and currently serves as Secretary of the Stamford Dental Society, reflecting her commitment to professional excellence and community service.

About Us

Simply Dental of Stamford opened in May 2016. Founded by Dr. Janmeet Grover, his goal is to care for families from Stamford, CT, and its surrounding communities like members of his own family. By combining skilled care and the latest technology with compassion, Simply Dental of Stamford has quickly become a precious asset in their community.
