Why Early Orthodontic Treatment Helps Children

Many parents wonder when their child should first see an orthodontist. The truth is that early evaluation and treatment can prevent bigger problems down the road.

Between ages 5 and 10, some permanent teeth have already come in, and the bones and muscles are growing quickly. This window gives us a unique chance to guide healthy development and shape the best possible bite. Some things we can accomplish during this stage simply can’t be done as well later.

Early orthodontic treatment offers several important benefits:

Early treatment often lasts a year or more. During this phase, your child may wear braces or appliances that act as anchors to influence bone and muscle growth. In most cases, a second phase of full braces follows once all the permanent teeth have arrived, moving them into their final positions.

  • Boosts self-esteem by improving your child’s smile and jaw alignment
  • Creates more stable, lasting results, especially with severe bite problems
  • Shortens or simplifies any future full-braces treatment
  • Takes advantage of cooperative ages, when children adapt well to appliances
  • Protects protruding teeth that are more likely to chip during falls or sports
  • Reduces the need for future jaw surgery or permanent tooth removal

Frequently Asked Questions

1. At what age should my child first see an orthodontist in West Covina?

Most children benefit from an orthodontic evaluation by age 7. By then, enough permanent teeth have come in for Dr. Khavaran to spot developing bite or alignment issues. Early evaluation doesn’t always mean early treatment, but it helps us plan the right timing for your child’s care.

2. Is Invisalign as effective as traditional braces?

For many patients, yes. Invisalign works wonderfully for mild to moderate alignment and spacing issues, and adults and teens love how discreet it is. More complex cases may respond better to traditional braces. During your consultation at Covina Smile, we’ll recommend the option best suited to your smile.

3. How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment time varies based on your specific needs. Simple cases may take under a year, while more involved corrections can last two years or more. Early-phase treatment for children often runs about a year, sometimes followed by a second phase later. Dr. Khavaran will give you a clear timeline at your evaluation.

4. Can adults get orthodontic treatment too?

Absolutely. It’s never too late for a straighter, healthier smile. We treat patients of all ages at Covina Smile, and many adults choose Invisalign for its discreet, convenient design. Whatever your age, we’ll help you reach your smile goals.
