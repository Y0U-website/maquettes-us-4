Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does a dentist in West Covina, CA provide?

4. What is cosmetic dentistry, and how can it improve my smile?

Cosmetic dentistry includes treatments that improve the appearance of teeth, gums, and the overall smile. A cosmetic dentist may recommend professional teeth whitening for discoloration, veneers for chipped or uneven front teeth, Invisalign for spacing or alignment concerns, or natural-looking restorations that improve both appearance and dental function.

5. Is professional teeth whitening better than over-the-counter whitening products?

Professional teeth whitening is personalized by a dentist after reviewing your tooth sensitivity, gum health, existing dental work, and the cause of discoloration. This evaluation helps determine whether whitening is appropriate and which option may help create more even-looking results than many store-bought whitening products.

6. What are dental veneers, and who may be a candidate?

Veneers are thin, custom-designed coverings bonded to the visible front surfaces of teeth. They may be appropriate for patients with stubborn stains, chipped teeth, minor gaps, worn edges, or uneven tooth shape. A cosmetic dentistry consultation helps determine whether veneers, whitening, bonding, or another treatment is the best fit for your tooth health and smile goals.

7. Can I get Invisalign treatment from a dentist in West Covina?

Many dentists provide Invisalign or similar clear-aligner treatment for qualified teens and adults. Invisalign uses a series of removable, nearly invisible aligners that gradually move teeth and may help correct mild to moderate crowding, spacing, or certain bite concerns. A dentist can evaluate your teeth and bite to determine whether clear aligners are a suitable option.

8. Why are routine dental exams and cleanings important?

Routine dental exams and cleanings allow a dentist to remove plaque and tartar, monitor gum health, screen for early signs of cavities and other oral-health concerns, and identify problems before they become more complex. Your family dentist can recommend a visit schedule based on your oral-health history, habits, and current needs.
