The Dental Implant Process at Covina Smile

Getting dental implants is a step-by-step journey, and we guide you through every stage. For most patients, the process involves two phases and takes about six to eight months from start to finish. Most people carry on with daily life with little to no disruption.

First, Dr. Khavaran places the titanium post into your jawbone. Healing time varies from person to person and depends on factors like bone density. In some cases, we can restore an implant right away.

Over the next three to four months, the post sits just beneath your gums and gradually bonds with the bone. During this time, you can usually wear a temporary denture and enjoy a soft diet.

Once the implant has fully bonded, we begin phase two. Our team uncovers the implant and attaches a small healing collar. After about four weeks, we take an impression and connect the abutment posts. Then we craft and place your custom crown, bridge, or denture over the top.

When it’s all done, you walk out with a strong, beautiful smile that looks and feels completely natural.
