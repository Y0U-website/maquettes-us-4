Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Connor Zehak, DMD

Dr. Connor Zehak is deeply committed to providing every patient with the highest quality dental care while ensuring their experience is as comfortable & efficient as possible. He takes the time to listen, explain treatment options clearly & tailor each plan to meet individual needs. His favorite part of the job is that it allows him the opportunity to not only improve his patients’ oral health but also transform their confidence.

Dr. Zehak obtained his dental degree from the Midwestern University of Arizona & has now been practicing for two years. The learning never stops, as he strives to hone & expand his skills through continuing education courses in Spear education, implant dentistry, endodontic therapy & biomimetic dentistry. He is also a member of Spear & the Academy of General Dentistry.

Though he lives in Mooresville now, Dr. Zehak actually grew up in Oak Brook, a suburb in Chicago. He has been around the dental field his entire life—his dad is a dentist & from a young age, he was able to see firsthand the impact that a great dentist could have on a person’s life. To him, dentistry is more than just a career—it’s a calling that has allowed him to combine science, artistry & compassion in a way that helps others feel confident, healthy & heard.

In addition to his work in the office, Dr. Zehak enjoys volunteering & giving back to the community. He participates in Free Dental Day every year & has also participated in Give Kids a Smile & Mission of Mercy. He loves to connect with people from all walks of life & believes that it is important to give back to the community & those less fortunate.

Outside of the office, Dr. Zehak can be found hanging out with his animals or at the lake. He is passionate about being outdoors & enjoying nature. He lives with his wife, who is also a dentist that he met in dental school, & their five birds & two dogs.
