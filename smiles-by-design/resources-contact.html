Frequently Asked Questions

Are you accepting new patients?

We are accepting new patients! Please contact the office and reception will help you with your dental needs or concerns.

Do you see children?

Our office loves to treat the whole family! The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry and the American Dental Association both recommend establishing a dental home within six months of seeing a primary or baby tooth or by age one. This seems early to most parents, and a lot of patients this age won’t get through a dental cleaning, but bringing them in helps them become familiar before they develop dental issues. It also allows the dental provider to give instruction and guidance on homecare, and also apply fluoride varnish, which protects their teeth from cavities.

Are you in network?

We are not in network with any dental insurance companies. As a courtesy to our patients, we file all insurance claims as some insurances provide an out-of-network benefit. Our receptionists are happy to assist with any insurance questions or concerns.
