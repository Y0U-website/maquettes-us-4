The Dental Implant Process

Initial Consultation

You’ll undergo an initial consultation with one of our dentists at our Rockford, IL office. Your dentist will thoroughly examine your mouth, review your medical history, and discuss sedation options as well as your smile goals with you. This will help us decide if dental implants are the right treatment for you.

In certain cases, the tooth has gone unreplaced for too long and has caused gum recession or jawbone resorption to occur. If this has happened, your dentist may recommend a supplemental procedure to increase your chances of implant candidacy.

Your dentists will also let you know about our other restorative options, so you can make an informed decision that best suits your oral health needs. If you both decide to go forward with dental implants, we’ll design your implants and schedule your next appointment.

Designing Your Dental Implants

To design your dental implants so they match your natural smile, we’ll take images and impressions of your smile. We’ll send these images and impressions to a dental laboratory, where your custom-made restorations will be created. This will typically take about two weeks .

The Implant Procedure

To prepare your mouth for implants, we’ll clean your teeth and apply a local anesthetic. We’ll also apply your chosen sedation method, or ensure any sedatives you took at home before your procedure are working. Then, your dentist will make small incisions in your gums and surgically place the implant posts into your jawbone.

You’ll return to have your restorations placed once your implants have fully fused to your jawbone in a natural process called osseointegration . Osseointegration usually takes three to six months , but this time frame can vary based on your unique physiology and your post-procedure behaviors.

Placing Your Restoration

When you return to our Rockford office, your dentists will place your restoration by attaching it to the implant post with a connector piece called an abutment . Once your restorations are attached, your implants will be complete and you can leave our office with a new, stunning smile.
