Dental Implants Procedure

The implant procedure is carefully planned and carried out in stages to ensure comfort and long-lasting success. At Encino Smile Makeover, we make the process as smooth and stress free as possible.

  • Initial Consultation : During your first visit, we will evaluate your mouth, discuss your health history, and determine if dental implant treatment is right for you. We may use X-rays or digital scans to assess your jawbone and create a customized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement : In this stage, the implant is surgically inserted into your jawbone. Our team uses advanced technology to perform the implant placement procedure with precision, minimizing discomfort and healing time.
  • Healing and Bone Integration : Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. This step is essential to provide a stable foundation for your future replacement teeth.
  • Abutment and Crown Attachment : Once healing is complete, a small connector called an abutment is placed on top of the implant. Then we attach a custom-made crown or other prosthetic teeth to complete your new smile.
  • Follow-Up Care : After the procedure, we’ll schedule regular checkups to monitor your healing and ensure long-term success. We will also give you guidance on how to care for your implants, gums, and overall oral health.

FAQ

What is the average cost of a set of dental implants?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on how many teeth you are replacing, the type of implant used, and whether additional procedures like bone grafting are needed. On average, a single implant with a crown can cost between $3,000 and $5,000. Full mouth implant supported dentures can cost more but are often a better value in the long run. We will go over all costs during your consultation and provide a personalized estimate. Payment plans are also available to help make your treatment more affordable.

What is the 3/2 rule for dental implants?

The 3/2 rule is a guideline that helps determine how many implants are needed to support a given number of replacement teeth. It suggests using at least two implants for every three missing teeth. For example, six missing teeth might be supported by four implants. This rule ensures the structure is strong and secure. Our team will assess your situation to find the best and most efficient solution.

How much does most dental insurance pay for implants?

Most dental insurance plans offer limited or partial coverage for dental implant treatment. Some may cover parts of the procedure, such as the crown or consultation, while others may not include implants at all. We will review your insurance details and help you understand what benefits may apply. Our office staff is experienced in handling insurance claims and paperwork. Even if your plan doesn’t cover implants fully, we will help you explore cost effective alternatives.
