What We Check for During Your Dental Health Examination?

  • Signs of Gum Disease We check for gum disease, inflammation, and warning signs of infection.
  • Tooth Decay Our expert team inspects for cavities, chips, worn enamel, and previous dental work that show signs of wear.
  • Bite & Jaw Alignment We ensure your teeth fit together properly and assess any tension in your jaw, including grinding.
  • Oral Cancer Screening We examine your lips, cheeks, tongue, chin, neck and throat for any abnormalities.
  • Dental X-Rays These provide a detailed look at decay, infections, and bone loss below the surface.
  • Intraoral Cameras We use 3D or 2D cameras to make a model of your mouth and help you see your teeth in a new way. See your teeth as your dentist sees them on our TV monitors.
  • Buildup of Plaque We evaluate plaque and tartar accumulation, which can lead to cavities and gum disease.

The Role of Dental X-Rays in Your Initial Visit

Dental x-rays are an essential part of assessing your dental health, allowing us to detect dental issues before and if there are major problems. Most patients receive digital x-rays as little as one to 18+ (considered a full-mouth series of x-rays) during their first visit, which highlight cavities and bone health. If necessary, we may take a panoramic x-ray or a CT scan in some offices for a broader or 3D view of your entire mouth while you’re comfortably sitting on the dental chair.

What to Expect During Your Teeth Cleaning

  • Detailed Cleaning Hand scalers refine and target specific areas.
  • Plaque & Tartar Removal Ultrasonic scalers gently remove hardened buildup. We also perform traditional hand scaling.
  • Polishing A smooth, refreshing finish keeps your teeth feeling clean. We offer prophypaste different flavors, fluoride or no fluoride, specific to your preferences.
  • Fluoride Treatment or Hydroxyapetite Strengthens enamel and protects against future decay.
  • Plaque & Tartar Removal Depending on your needs, we will also recommend different treatment modalities for at-home use. These include: type of toothbrush, waterpick, floss, lozenges/chewing gums, mouth rinses, and oral probiotics.
