What Is a Dental Membership Plan?

Our dental care membership is an in-house option created for patients who value transparency and personal relationships. It is not dental insurance. It is a direct agreement between you and Firm Foundation Dental, giving you access to consistent care and predictable pricing from a local dental team you can trust.

How Does a Dental Membership Plan Work Compared to Dental Insurance?

Traditional dental insurance often comes with deductibles, waiting periods, annual maximums, and treatment approvals. A dentist payment plan through our membership program removes those obstacles.

As a dentist with no insurance requirements, we are able to focus fully on your care. You receive the services you need when you need them, with clear expectations and predictable costs.

  • No deductibles
  • No annual maximums
  • No waiting periods
  • No prior authorizations
  • No claim forms

What Services Are Included in Our Dental Membership Plans?

Our dental office membership plan is built around the services patients rely on most to protect their oral health, catch problems early, and reduce the need for more complex treatment later. We offer two plan options based on your gum health, and both prioritize preventive care, diagnostic imaging, and consistent monitoring so your care stays on track.

Prevention Plan (For Patients With Healthy Gums)

This plan centers on routine preventive services that keep your teeth and gums stable, while giving us regular opportunities to identify concerns early.

  • 2 regular cleanings per year to remove plaque and tartar buildup and help prevent cavities and gum inflammation
  • 2 periodic examinations per year to evaluate tooth health, bite, restorations, and signs of early decay
  • 2 oral cancer screenings per year as part of a thorough preventive evaluation
  • 1 full periodontal evaluation per year to measure gum health and monitor for changes over time
  • 2 fluoride applications per year to strengthen enamel and support cavity prevention
  • 1 set of bitewing X-rays per year to detect decay between teeth and evaluate bone levels
  • 1 full mouth set of C-rays and panoramic X-ray every 5 years to support comprehensive diagnostics and long-term treatment planning
  • 15 % off ALL dental services for treatment needs that come up outside routine preventive care

Periodontal Plan (For Patients With Periodontal Disease)

This plan is designed around the services that help manage periodontal disease with consistent maintenance, closer monitoring, and targeted periodontal treatment savings.

If you are looking for a consistent approach to preventive and periodontal maintenance, these plans keep the focus where it belongs, on the services that protect your health and the relationship that supports it.

  • 4 periodontal maintenance cleanings per year to help control gum infection, reduce inflammation, and stabilize periodontal health
  • 2 periodic examinations per year to monitor overall oral health and evaluate how your gums are responding to care
  • 2 oral cancer screenings per year as part of ongoing preventive monitoring
  • 1 full periodontal evaluation per year to track gum measurements and identify changes that may need treatment
  • 2 fluoride applications per year to support enamel strength, especially for patients at higher risk of decay
  • 1 set of bitewing X-rays per year to check for decay and monitor bone levels associated with gum disease
  • 1 full mouth set of C-rays and panoramic X-ray every 5 years for comprehensive imaging and long-term diagnostics
  • 30 % off scaling and root planing when deep cleaning is needed to treat active periodontal infection
  • 15 % off ALL dental services for additional dental care beyond the included visits
