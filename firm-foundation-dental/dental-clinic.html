Rooted in Faith, Built on Craftsmanship

Firm Foundation Dental isn’t just a name—it’s a calling.

Inspired by the Christian hymn, our practice was founded as a ministry through dentistry, where every appointment is a chance to serve with grace, honesty, and excellence. We don’t believe in pushing procedures or playing the numbers game. Instead, we foster relationships, earn trust, and treat each person with the same care we’d give our own families.

A Midwest work ethic meets Texas hospitality, welcoming patients from Prosper and Celina.

We believe in building from the ground up—whether it’s restoring a smile or helping a young family establish lifelong oral health. And with the booming growth in Prosper and Celina, we’re committed to being a steady, faith-led presence in these fast-growing communities.
