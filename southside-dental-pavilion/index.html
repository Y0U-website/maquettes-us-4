Were you aware that the bone in your jaw tends to diminish in areas with missing teeth? It’s a fact! Dental implants play a crucial role in maintaining bone integrity by engaging the bone and promoting its strength.

If you’ve experienced tooth loss or anticipate it in the future, consulting your dentist about opting for a dental implant is highly recommended. This will not only safeguard your jawbone but also serve as a replacement for your lost tooth.

In the event of a prolonged absence of a tooth, the dentist will require an X-ray to assess the condition of the jaw bone in that particular region.
