Dental Crowns and Bridges

Smile Restoration With Dental Crowns and Bridges

When you have a broken or damaged tooth, it can be hard to find a reason to smile. That’s why you need to talk to Southside Dental Pavilion about how crowns and bridges can restore your smile and your confidence.

A crown is like a cap, and it’s placed on top of an existing tooth. It may be placed after a root canal to protect the tooth underneath, or it could be used to strengthen a cracked tooth. It can also serve a cosmetic purpose.

A bridge, on the other hand, is used to replace missing teeth. If there are healthy teeth around the gap, a bridge can be used. The bridge is anchored on the healthy teeth, and prosthetic teeth are made to match your smile in between.

Crowns can be made from many materials, but they are typically crafted from metal, porcelain, and ceramic. At Southside Dental Pavilion, we use the material that best matches your smile and your budget.

Don’t let problematic teeth dampen your smile. Instead, talk to the dental experts at Southside Dental Pavilion about crowns and bridges. We’ll work with you to find the best options for a complete smile makeover.

What to Expect With Crowns and Bridges

First, we’ll evaluate your teeth. We have to look at your mouth to see if you’re a good fit for crowns or bridges. We’re proud to use digital scans to create the most accurate impressions of your mouth.

Then, we’ll figure out the best path forward based on your needs and budget. To do this, we’ll use digital imaging to accurately plan your crown or bridge procedure. If you need a temporary fix, we’ll provide a provisional restoration until we can get you in for the real procedure.

Finally, we’ll perform the actual procedure. We’ll place the crown or the bridge to see how it fits and feels. If you like the way it looks, we’ll cement it into place for a long-term restoration for your smile.
