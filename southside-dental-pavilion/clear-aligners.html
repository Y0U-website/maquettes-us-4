Dental Clear Aligners

Straighten Your Teeth With Clear Aligners

Straightening your teeth doesn’t have to be an uncomfortable or embarrassing prospect. In the past, braces were the only way to get straighter teeth. However, when you visit Southside Dental Pavilion, we can use clear aligners for real results.

Clear aligners do the work of braces without the mess or fuss. Instead of using metal brackets, clear aligners are clear mouth guards that are custom fitted to support your teeth. They’re a great option for mild to moderate orthodontic problems, and many people like that they’re virtually invisible.

Are you a good match for clear aligners? Call Southside Dental Pavilion for an appointment. We’ll assess your teeth and discuss your concerns. If clear aligners are a good choice for you, we’ll get you set up and ready to go.

What to Expect With Clear Teeth Aligners

Clear aligners may be invisible, but they still require a lot of time and preparation. At Southside Dental Pavilion, we’ll create 3D images of your mouth. We’ll use the images to create the custom aligner. You’ll wear the aligner as much as possible, removing it for eating, drinking, and normal dental care.

After several weeks, you’ll get a new custom aligner. As we advance in the treatment, the aligners will slowly change shape to move your teeth into position. In this way, you won’t just get one aligner. You’ll get a series of aligners until your teeth are completely in the correct position for perfect alignment.

In general, clear aligners are more discreet and more comfortable. Braces can be painful, but most patients have no problems with their aligners. Aligners also do not stain your teeth, and dental care is easy and stress-free.

Clear aligners may be covered by your insurance if you have orthodontic care. However, not all insurance providers have the same provisions. Be sure to check your policy before pursuing clear aligners to see if you’re covered.

Most patients see results in a few months after using clear aligners. However, the length of your treatment will vary depending on your alignment needs.
