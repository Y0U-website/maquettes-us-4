Dr. Koby Sarubin

Dr. Koby focuses on comprehensive dental services, including general dentistry, teeth whitening, Invisalign, crowns, bridges, dentures, root canals, extractions, and implants. Dr. Koby spent four years working for Health Care for the Homeless in Baltimore City providing comprehensive and emergency dental care to the underserved populations in our community. Committed to providing the highest quality of care, Dr. Koby furthers his education by regularly attending continuing education courses. Dr. Koby strives to create a comfortable environment and positive experience for his patients.

Dr. Koby has been nominated multiple times for the American Dental Associations 10 Under 10 Award honoring dentistry’s rising stars. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Maryland State Dental Association, Maryland Academy of General Dentistry, and Baltimore County Dental Association.

A Baltimore native, Dr. Koby is a third-generation dentist, following in the footsteps of both his father and grandfather. He currently resides in Pikesville, MD with his wife, Kelsey, children Lake and Palmer, and their adorable cats, Frankie and Olive. Beyond dentistry, Dr. Koby enjoys spending time with friends and family, trying new restaurants, playing golf, and rooting on his hometown teams - the Ravens, Orioles, and Terps.
