The practice

Children have unique dental needs that require specialized care to ensure the health of their teeth and gums as they grow. Regular visits to a pediatric dentist can help prevent dental problems and ensure that any issues are addressed promptly.

You can prepare your child for their first dental visit by explaining what to expect and the importance of taking care of their teeth. You can also bring their favorite toy or book to help keep them calm and relaxed during their appointment.

We offer a range of services including routine cleanings, fluoride treatments, dental exams, cavity fillings, extractions, and orthodontic evaluations. We also provide guidance on oral hygiene practices and nutrition to help promote healthy teeth and gums.

If your child has a dental emergency, such as a knocked-out tooth or severe toothache, call our office immediately. We offer emergency dental services and can provide guidance on what to do next.

Yes, dental x-rays are safe for children when done properly and with the appropriate precautions. We use digital x-rays, which emit less radiation than traditional film x-rays and are safer for children. We also only take x-rays when necessary to diagnose and treat dental problems.
