We want you to feel confident not only with your smile but confident in knowing that when you visit us, there will be no lectures. Instead you will find a calm environment with welcoming staff. We understand that many issues can keep you from visiting the dentist regularly. Our dedicated team will provide advice and options that will suit your schedule and health needs as well as your budget. Dr. Bielkie is humbly honored to have earned a reputation for resolving difficult cases that patients have been previously told cannot be fixed. The skill of our staff combined with compassion and a calm environment makes Stoneridge Dental the best choice for you!

Preventative Dentistry

Routine dental care can give you and your family a healthy smile for a lifetime. Cleanings and check ups with digital x-ray, laser cavity detection, oral cancer screening, fluoride treatments & gum treatments are all provided for you.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Restore your smile and boost your confidence. We offer bonding, veneers, ceramic fillings, dental implants, teeth whitening, braces and Invisalign. Broken, discolored, crooked and missing teeth can be a thing of the past with little expense.

Restorative Dentistry

Dental implants, partials, crowns and dentures are all available. We offer full denture services and have an in-house lab for denture creation, repair and adjustments. We are on the first floor and handicap accessible for convenience.

Emergency Services

In the event of a dental emergency, call (586) 739-6400 for Shelby Twp. or (586) 771-3440 for St. Clair Shores. Most emergencies can be seen the same day for extractions, root canals, denture repairs, bonding, veneers & crowns.

We Accept Most Insurance

Stoneridge Dental accepts most insurance plans and we have an insurance specialist on staff. We accept VISA, MasterCard and Discover cards and we accept CareCredit which can be an interest-free option for extended payment. You can apply online at www.CareCredit.com .

Stoneridge Dental in cooperation with the state of Michigan offers dentistry at no cost for children under the age of 21 with Medicaid. Call our office at (586) 739-6400 for details. The Healthy Kids Dental program information can be found at http://www.deltadentalmi.com/Individuals/Special-Programs/Healthy-Kids-Dental.aspx .
