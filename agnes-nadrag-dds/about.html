Dr. Agnes Nadrag Specializing in general and cosmetic dentistry, Dr. Agnes Nadrag is among the highest-rated dentists in New York, serving the Manhattan and Queens neighborhood and surrounding areas.

Dr. Nadrag received her doctor of dental surgery degree from New York University’s acclaimed school of dentistry. She’s now a dental authority in New York and a member of professional organizations such as the American Dental Association, the American Association of Dental Examiners, and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.

As a certified dental practitioner, Dr. Nadrag’s focus is on her patients. She strives to provide the best standard of care and the highest quality dental options available. Her five-star patient-review ratings are a testament to the dedication she puts into her patient-first mission.

Offering a full range of dental services, Dr. Nadrag covers everything from preventive cleanings and tooth repair to complete implant tooth replacement and custom smile design. Dr. Nadrag places particular importance on using cutting-edge dental technology and procedures to correct facial pain and dangerous conditions such as sleep apnea.

As a resident of New York and a graduate of NYU, Dr. Nadrag is a local dentist who proudly serves her neighborhood with a personalized, one-on-one approach.
