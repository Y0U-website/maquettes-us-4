Dr. Neha Patel

DDS

Dr. Neha Patel was born in New Jersey and grew up in upstate New York. In 2014, she graduated from Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine and then continued with her dental training at NYPH- Weill Cornell in Manhattan as a general practice resident and then as a chief resident. While in the office, Dr. Patel strives to provide our patients with the best and most compassionate care. In her free time she loves spending time with her family and friends, traveling, hiking and painting.
