The practice

Our dental team takes a conservative approach to dentistry and encourages preventive treatments to avoid costly procedures in the future, such as dental cleanings, sealants, and fluoride. If you do need restorative care, we create customized treatment plans to help you get back to optimal oral health.

Our staff members are devoted to ensuring each of our patients has a pleasant dental experience. We understand that many patients struggle with dental anxiety and we never want fear to prevent patients from receiving important dental procedures. Our team always does their best to make patients comfortable and approaches each treatment with a gentle touch.

Jayshree Sawhney, DDS, is an experienced dentist and the owner of Cranford Dental Arts in Cranford, New Jersey.

In 2001, Dr. Sawhney graduated from Howard University College of Dentistry in Washington, DC. After graduating from dental school, she opened a private practice in nearby Maryland, where she worked for nine years.

At Cranford, West Caldwell, and Westfield Dental Arts, Dr. Sawhney listens to each patient and provides comprehensive care that focuses on not only oral health, but overall quality of life. As a mother of three daughters, she enjoys teaching children the importance of taking care of their teeth. It’s her goal for patients of all ages to enjoy going to the dentist.

Being a mother of three girls, Dr. Sawhney’s friendly and warm personality helps patients feel relaxed throughout their visits. She values building lifelong relationships with each and every patient, tailoring care towards their individual needs.
