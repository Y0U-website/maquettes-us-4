Featured SERVICES

Zoom Whitening

To achieve the smile you want, Zoom teeth whitening offers you a single appointment smile transformation utilizing professional teeth whitening technology. Brightening your teeth with Philips ZOOM is a safe and sustainable option used by over 10 million people to achieve immediate results in just one visit. Bring it up during your next appointment to see if you are a candidate.

night Guards

Night guards are a patient specific device designed to address teeth grinding, clenching, and jaw discomfort. If you wake up in the morning with tooth pain or a sore jaw, or if you have noticed chipping/ wear on your teeth, you might be a candidate for a night guard. A custom night guard can be a simple, life-changing addition that is designed to protect your teeth and improve your symptoms. Ask us about our custom made guards at your next visit.

invisalign

Invisalign is a treatment to gradually straighten teeth using a series of clear, removeable aligners without the hassle of metal braces, brackets, and wires. Using SmartTrack proprietary material and custom patient retainers, Invisalign offers more predictable tooth movements when compared to other teeth straightening options. Invisalign prioritizes optimal patient comfort, function, and aesthetics to achieve the smile you have always dreamed of. Find out if you are a candidate!
