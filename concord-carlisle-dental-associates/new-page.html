New Patients Welcome!

We look forward to meeting you

Welcome to our practice, we look for to seeing you! Before you come in, we thought you might appreciate knowing a little bit about us and getting an idea about what you can expect during your visit.

We may be a little different than dentists you have visited in the past. We believe that achieving and maintaining healthy teeth and gums takes a team effort that you play as big a role as we do in reaching that mutual goal. So, you can expect to be extensively involved in your treatment program.

During you first visit, we’ll ask you to fill out a medical history form which is vital in developing your treatment plan. Next, we’ll examine your teeth, gums, jaw alignment, and soft tissues. We’ll take x-rays, examine your bite, check for oral cancer and clean your teeth.

Later, using our findings from the exam, we will determine a treatment plan and go over it together. In Addition to treatment, we can discuss a financial plan at that time. If you have insurance, we ask that you pay your estimated portion on the day treatment is complete. If you do not have insurance, we ask that you pay for services on that day in full (we do accept VISA, MasterCard and Discover). If this poses a hardship, we will work with you to develop an alternative financial agreement.

If you have any questions about our practice, please call us at any time. Additionally, Please find New Patient Forms available for you to download as a .PDF and bring with you to your first appointment.

We welcome you to Bjornson Family Dentistry and look forward to meeting soon!
