Meet Dr. Maya A proud Trenton, NJ native, Dr. Maya Thompson is thrilled to return home as a board-certified pediatric dentist dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality, and truly fun dental care to the children of her community.

Dr. Maya’s path to dentistry is far from traditional. After graduating from Villanova University with a Bachelor of Science in Chemical Engineering, she spent her first career solving complex projects in the oil and gas industry. While she enjoyed the challenge, she felt called to a profession that would allow her to directly impact people’s lives, tap into her creativity, and embrace her natural…

She went on to earn both her Doctorate of Dental Medicine and Master of Public Health degrees from the University of Pennsylvania, followed by specialty training in pediatric dentistry at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, OH.

As a pediatric dentist, Dr. Maya is passionate about ensuring that kids and families feel seen, welcomed, and confident that their smiles are in the best hands. She founded Duck Duck Tooth with the vision of creating a practice that is not just in the community but truly for the community. Her goal is to build a space where families feel valued, supported, and celebrated.

Dr. Maya brings a calm confidence, a love of teaching, and a commitment to making every dental visit enjoyable, educational, and culturally inclusive. She strives to create an environment where children and parents know they are genuinely cared for—not just as patients, but as people.

Good preventive dentistry is about more than cleaning teeth. At Duck Duck Tooth, each visit is designed to help Dr. Maya understand what your child needs now while helping your family feel prepared for what comes next.

A pediatric dental crown covers and protects a tooth that has lost too much structure for a filling to provide enough support. Crowns may also be used after certain pulp treatments or when a tooth has been significantly weakened by decay.
