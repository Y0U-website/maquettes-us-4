Learn more about our pediatric dentistry:

Summit Pediatric Dentistry proudly accepts Medicaid so all children can get the dental care they need, no matter their insurance. We make it easy for families with Medicaid to get important services like cleanings , fillings , and regular checkups without worrying about the cost.

We also offer care in a hospital dentist setting. Dr. Moncy Matthew provides safe dental treatment under sedation for kids who have high anxiety, special needs, or need more complex procedures. Our kind and caring team works hard to make every visit as calm and comfortable as possible. Families can trust that their children are in good hands and getting the best care available.

  • Trained for Kids’ Unique Needs: Pediatric dentists have additional education and training to handle children’s dental needs. They understand that kids are growing and developing at different rates, and their teeth require special care. Pediatric dentists also have training in managing children’s behavior, making dental visits stress-free and enjoyable.
  • Prevention and Early Treatment: Pediatric dentists strongly emphasize prevention. We teach both you and your child how to take care of their teeth at home. By using preventive measures like fluoride treatments, sealants, and early orthodontic evaluations, we can help avoid problems before they even start. If problems do arise, we can treat them early before they become more serious or painful.
  • Education and Support for Parents: At Summit Pediatric Dentistry, we understand that being a parent means wanting the best for your child. We’re here to help you understand your child’s dental health needs and guide you in supporting their oral health. Our team is happy to answer all of your questions, from teething to orthodontics, and provide tips for maintaining a healthy smile at home.
  • Prevent Dental Anxiety: Many adults have dental anxiety, but starting dental visits early and making them positive experiences can prevent this. Dr. Mathew knows how to speak to children in ways that make them feel at ease. We use techniques that help children feel relaxed. Over time, your child will view dental visits as an important and fun part of their health routine.

Why Should Children Visit A Pediatric Dentist?

Starting dental visits early helps to set your child on the right path to a lifetime of good oral health. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children have their first visit by their first birthday or within six months after their first tooth erupts. Early dental visits are important for many reasons, and our team emphasizes the benefit of routine care for minimizing the risk of common problems.

Your child’s teeth and mouth constantly change as they grow, and as a pediatric dentist in Lee’s Summit, Dr. Mathew is trained to spot any developing issues early. Even babies can develop cavities, and early visits allow us to catch problems before they turn into bigger concerns. Catching oral development-related issues early also reduces the need for more complex treatments later.

We’ll monitor how the teeth come in and make sure everything is progressing as it should. We can also talk about things like thumb sucking, pacifiers, and other habits that might affect your child’s dental health.

Regular visits help establish healthy habits early. During visits, our pediatric dental team can show your child the right way to brush and floss, talk about healthy eating habits, and discuss the importance of regular check-ups. Establishing these habits early on can reduce the chances of your child developing cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues. Regular visits to our office will allow your child to become familiar with the dentist, the staff, and the equipment in a friendly, fun environment.

Dental health is also tied to overall health. Poor oral health can lead to problems with eating, speaking, and self-confidence. It can even affect your child’s general well-being. Regular pediatric dental care visits help your child maintain healthy teeth and gums, which can also help them feel confident and happy as they grow.
