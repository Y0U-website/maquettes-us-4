Accessibility Statement

  • Descriptive alt text on meaningful imagery and decorative alt="" on ornamental images.
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Sufficient color contrast that meets or exceeds WCAG AA ratios.
  • Forms with associated labels and clear error messaging.
  • Responsive layouts that work from 320px up to large desktop displays.
  • Keyboard support for menus, dialogs, and carousels via Radix UI primitives.
  • Reduced-motion preferences respected for animations and transitions.

In-office accessibility

Please call before your visit to discuss any access or communication accommodations you need.

Assistive technology compatibility

The Mount Prospect Dental Care website is designed to be compatible with recent versions of:

  • Screen readers including NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver (macOS / iOS), and TalkBack (Android).
  • Browser zoom up to 200 % without loss of content or function.
  • Speech-recognition tools and on-screen keyboards.
  • Operating-system high-contrast and dark-mode settings.

Known limitations

Despite our best efforts, some content on the site may not yet be fully accessible. We are actively working to address:

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please let us know, we want to hear from you and will work to provide the information or service through an alternative communication method.

  • Older blog images that lack descriptive alt text, being updated.
  • Embedded third-party widgets (e.g., maps, reviews) whose accessibility we do not directly control.
  • PDF documents predating this statement, replacements are in progress.
