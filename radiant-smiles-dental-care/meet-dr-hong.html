About Bin Vinh Hoang, D.D.S.

About Us

Bin Hoang, DDS, is a caring aesthetic and implant dentist at Radiant Smiles Dental Care, located in San Juan Capistrano, California. His emphasis on patient comfort as well as keeping up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry enable him to provide patients with the best care in the field. Dr. Bin Hoang has a wide range of experience in different areas of dentistry, from cosmetic and restorative dentistry to surgical treatments such as dental implants and root canal treatments.

The approach of Dr. Hoang and the team at Radiant Smiles Dental Care starts with patiently listening to you. Then, after a complete and thorough examination, they review and discuss the diagnosis and treatment options.

They know there are numerous options for dental providers in the Orange County area, but they also know that they provide unsurpassed patient care and amazing results no matter what treatment a patient may need. The modern facility produces a relaxing vibe that anyone can be comfortable in, and the friendly team only adds to the tranquility. To schedule an appointment, call the office or book online today.
