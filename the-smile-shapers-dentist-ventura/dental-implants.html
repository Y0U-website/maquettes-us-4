Who Makes a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

If you have one or more missing teeth and desire a permanent solution, implants can be the way to go. Ideal candidates typically:

  • Have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone structure
  • Don’t have active gum disease or undiagnosed decay
  • Don’t smoke or are willing to quit to ensure healing success
  • Want a natural, stable substitute for missing teeth

Our Personalized Dental Implant Process

Every smile is different—your dental implant treatment in Ventura, CA, should be as well. Here’s how we tailor your oral care:

  • Initial Consultation : We’ll examine your oral health, check your medical history, and utilize imaging technology to discover your bone structure and tooth position.
  • Custom Treatment Plan : We create a customized plan to fit your timeline and goals. No matter if you require one implant or a full arch restoration, we walk you through each process.
  • Implant Placement : With tender care, your implant is carefully fitted into the jawbone. We give healing time to establish a firm integration.
  • Crown or Restoration Attachment: After the implant has healed, we attach a custom-made crown or prosthetic. We fine-tune the fit for comfort, bite, and appearance.
  • Ongoing Support: We don’t leave you hanging. We remain with you during healing and thereafter, providing ongoing support to maintain your implant and your smile healthy.
