Emergency Care & Oral Surgery

Lakeville Dental purposely allows time in it’s schedule for emergency patients because we know that accidents or sudden pain can happen. Dr. Pham or Dr. Coscia will do their best to see an emergency patient the same day to provide treatment or develop a treatment plan. Most procedures are performed in our office with great care and expertise. However, if it is determined that the patient would benefit from a specialist, they will be referred to one of the best in the area.

Dr. Pham’s and Dr. Coscia’s main priority is to ensure you receive exceptional dental care for your particular situation.
