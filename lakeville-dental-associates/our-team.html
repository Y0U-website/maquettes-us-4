Chuong Pham, DMD

Our Dedicated Staff

Our Dedicated Staff

Dr. Pham received his D.M.D from Temple University in 2003. Prior to that, he received his B.S. in Biology in 1997 and his M.S. in Biology in 1999 from Northeastern University.

Dr. Pham opened Lakeville Dental in 2005 and has enjoyed growing his practice in the close knit community of Lakeville, Massachusetts. In order to better serve his growing patient base, he opened another practice, Sippican Dental, in Marion, providing his patients with a choice of locations.

Dr. Pham is well known for his dental expertise, bright conversation and the ability to help patients feel at ease in the dental chair. He also enjoys mentoring and helping others. Outside of the office, Dr. Pham enjoys spending time with his wife, their 5 children, their dog Wilson and cat Chloe. He is an avid golfer, enjoys playing and collecting guitars, and is known to have a fun and interesting sense of humor.

Christa Cosca, DMD

Dr. Coscia received her D.M.D from Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine in 2017, Magna Cum Laude.

Prior to that she received her B.S in Biology and Biotechnology from WPI, where she played softball for 4 years and served as captain her senior year.

During her years at BU, Dr. Coscia participated in a mission program, providing dental care in underdeveloped areas of Honduras.

After graduation Dr. Coscia began working with Dr. Pham at Lakeville Dental, in the town where she grew up.

She happily credits her career to Dr. Pham, who was her dentist growing up, and ultimately inspired her to pursue a career in dentistry.

Now, years later, she is working side by side with her mentor and has become a part of the Lakeville Dental Family.

When she is not working with patients she enjoys spending time with her family, her husband, their 2 children and 2 rescue dogs.
