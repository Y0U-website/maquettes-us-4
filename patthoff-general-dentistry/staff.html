Erica Patthoff, DDS

Dr. Erica graduated from the Honors College at West Virginia University with an undergraduate degree in chemistry, and is a 2004 graduate of the West Virginia University School of Dentistry. Upon graduation, she joined her father’s practice as an associate, and assumed ownership of the practice in the summer of 2018. Dr. Patthoff is a member of the Academy of General Dentistry and the West Virginia Chapter of the American Dental Association. She lives in Gerrardstown with her husband and three children.
