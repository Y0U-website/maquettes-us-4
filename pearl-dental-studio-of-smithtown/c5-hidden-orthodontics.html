How Does C5 Hidden Orthodontics Work?

Very thin wires (about the width of a hair) are super flexible and customized to your unique dental anatomy. When strategically placed behind your teeth, they’ll move into their ideal position. You’ll come in every 7-8 weeks for adjustment appointments, where we’ll reposition the wires for the next phase of tooth movements. Throughout your treatment, we’ll track your progress before each appointment with easy-to-use remote monitoring technology.
