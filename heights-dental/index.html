Dr. Tracz administers anesthesia and makes an incision in your gums before drilling small holes in your jawbone and placing titanium implants in the holes.

Dr. Tracz stitches your gums closed to give your new implants time to fuse with your jaw. This fusion process is called osseointegration. For your implants to successfully fuse to your jawbone, it usually takes around three to six months once your implants are placed. You can wear a temporary denture in the interim.
