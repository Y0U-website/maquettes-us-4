About Us

We like to think we’re Group Practice Done Right ® Why Plum? Our group was founded by a family of proud third generation dentists who wanted to build a group the right way—by staying true to their family roots and the firm belief that the best clinical decisions are those made by dentists in their own practices.

You’ll never see the Plum logo or name on any of our practices. The patient relationship is clinical and personal, and the business side should never get in the way of that.

At Plum Dental, we have a top-notch support team, a seamless and simple affiliation process, and excellent partnership and equity opportunities for both selling and new dentists.

Let’s start a conversation to help you build on your success, enjoy more free time, and practice on your terms.
