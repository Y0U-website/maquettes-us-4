Comprehensive Dental Services

Dr. Yosi P. Behroozan offers comprehensive dental services for the entire family. We invite you to preview a selection of the many services we provide.

OUR SERVICES

Bridges

Teeth can be lost for various reasons. Periodontal disease, tooth decay, and trauma are three major causes of tooth loss. There are three primary methods for replacing missing teeth: implants, bridges, and removable dentures.

Cosmetic Fillings

There are several restorative materials available for treating dental decay. Dr. Behroozan offers various types of fillings, including composite, porcelain, gold, and amalgam.

Crowns and Veneers

Our teeth endure significant stress throughout our lives, making them susceptible to chipping, fractures, discoloration, and decay—leading to both functional and aesthetic concerns. Crowns and veneers help strengthen and restore your smile to its original, natural brilliance.

Dental Implants

In most cases, dental implants are considered the leading clinical solution for replacing missing teeth.

Dentures

If you’ve lost some or all of your natural teeth due to periodontal disease, tooth decay, or injury, partial and complete dentures can restore both your smile and oral function.

Tooth Extraction

Tooth extraction is considered a last resort when a tooth is severely fractured or decayed beyond repair. We offer every available relaxation technique to ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.

Invisalign

An increasing number of cosmetically conscious patients are turning to the Invisalign system to correct specific alignment issues. For some, combining Instant Orthodontics with Invisalign offers an ideal and efficient solution for straightening teeth—often reducing or even eliminating the need for long-term treatment.

Kid’s Dentistry

Dr. Behroozan offers comprehensive family dentistry and is committed to making each child’s dental visit a positive and enjoyable experience. Our goal is to encourage and establish good oral hygiene habits from an early age.

Night Guards and Snoring Devices

Night guards can help relieve jaw tension for patients who grind their teeth. Dr. Behroozan primarily prescribes them to alleviate pain in the jaw muscles and temporomandibular joint (TMJ).

Pain Free Computerized Injections

We understand that some patients feel nervous at the sight of needles and syringes. That’s why Dr. Behroozan uses THE WAND, a small, pen-like handpiece designed to provide a more comfortable and less intimidating injection experience.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment is used to treat a tooth with a diseased or infected nerve. When this procedure is necessary, we utilize our in-house, board-certified endodontist to ensure expert care and effective results.

Ultrasonic Cleaning with Jet Whitening

All our cleanings include a jet whitening that helps remove stains and plaque, significantly whitening the teeth. This technique is unique because it uses no peroxides, bleach, or chemicals.

Whitening

Dr. Behroozan is now offering several different state-of-the-art whitening techniques that can help whiten your teeth. After evaluating your smile, we can recommend the one that suits you best.

Ultrasonic Scalers, Prophy Jets and Polishes

Having access to modern dental technology is essential for maintaining healthy, strong teeth over time. Tools such as ultrasonic scalers, prophy jets, and polishing systems play a vital role in delivering comprehensive dental care that supports long-term oral health.
