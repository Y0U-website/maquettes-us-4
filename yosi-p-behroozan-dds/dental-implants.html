Dental Implants

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants? Most healthy adults who have lost one or more teeth are potential candidates for dental implants. A good candidate will have healthy gums, adequate bone to support the implant, and be committed to good oral hygiene. During your consultation, Dr. Behroozan will evaluate your overall health, medical history, and perform digital imaging to determine if implants are right for you. In some cases, bone grafting or other preparatory procedures may be recommended to ensure a strong foundation.

Complete Implant Treatment – Start to Finish At our Santa Monica office, we offer full-scope implant services — all under one roof. Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive consultation and digital imaging to create a precise treatment plan. The implant post is then placed with expert precision by our experienced surgical team. Once healing and integration are complete, Dr. Behroozan will restore your implant with a custom-made crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture that fits seamlessly with your natural smile.

If you’re ready to restore your smile with a permanent and reliable solution, contact our office today to schedule your implant consultation with Dr. Behroozan. Let us help you regain the function, confidence, and comfort you deserve.

  • A natural look and feel that mimics your real teeth
  • Independent function without affecting neighboring teeth
  • Enhanced chewing ability and speech
  • Long-term durability with routine dental care
