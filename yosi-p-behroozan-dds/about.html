Dr. Yosi P. Behroozan

Meet Dr. Yosi P. Behroozan – Santa Monica’s Trusted Family Dentist

Dr. Yosi P. Behroozan is a second-generation dentist who has built his Santa Monica dental practice on the foundation of comprehensive, personalized care for patients of all ages. He is passionate about providing both oral health and needs-based dentistry—carefully tailored to the unique needs of each individual.

Dr. Behroozan earned his DDS degree from the UCLA School of Dentistry , where he completed his entire dental education and clinical training. Upon graduating in 2002, he was invited to join the esteemed UCLA clinical faculty. To this day, he remains actively involved in dental education, spending part of each week as a Continuing Lecturer and Clinical Instructor at UCLA.

Committed to delivering the highest standard of care, Dr. Behroozan stays current with the latest innovations in dental materials and technology. His dedication to continuing education ensures that patients benefit from cutting-edge, minimally invasive, and long-lasting dental treatments .

To provide a truly comprehensive dental experience, his office also features in-house specialists—offering seamless care for everything from general dentistry to implants, oral surgery, and orthodontics—all in one convenient location.

We welcome you to reach out with any questions and look forward to helping you achieve your healthiest, most confident smile.
