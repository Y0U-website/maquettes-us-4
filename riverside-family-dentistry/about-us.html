About us Riverside Family Dentistry

Over 100 years of combined experience

Dr. Al-Jerdi grew up in Southeast Michigan. Attended University of Detroit Mercy for his undergraduate studies and for dental school. It was always a goal to stay close to home and serve the communities that shaped him into the health care professional that he is today. Dr. Al-Jerdi feels that it is very important to have good work-life balance in order to be fully recharged to see patients and give them quality care. In his free time he enjoys cooking, playing competitive soccer, hiking, and being around friends and family.
