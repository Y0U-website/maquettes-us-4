Compare Your Full-Arch Tooth Replacement Options

Replacing most or all teeth in an upper or lower arch is a major decision. A consultation should compare function, daily cleaning, maintenance, treatment stages, and total cost before you choose a plan.

Fixed teeth or a removable denture?

A fixed implant restoration stays attached in your mouth; a conventional denture is removable. Implant-supported removable designs may also be worth discussing. Ask the dentist which designs are appropriate and available for your case, how each is cleaned, and what future maintenance could involve.

What should a full-arch estimate include?

Ask whether the estimate covers one arch or both, extractions if needed, implant placement, temporary teeth, the final restoration, and follow-up care. Clarify the restoration material, any separate laboratory charges, and which future repairs are outside the quoted treatment.

Review the practice’s financing resources alongside the written treatment plan. Confirm total repayment and current terms with the financing provider before applying.

Will I have teeth during healing?

Ask specifically about the temporary-tooth plan. The timing of implant placement and attachment of replacement teeth depends on the examination, bone support, and healing needs. A temporary restoration and a final restoration are different stages; a same-day final smile should not be assumed.

Prepare for a full-arch consultation

Bring a medication list, previous dental records if available, and the questions that matter most to you: chewing, comfort, speech, appearance, and budget. Read our dental implant overview and Dr. Weber’s profile before your visit.

Ask which existing teeth can be maintained, whether other procedures or specialist care are needed, and what home care and maintenance visits will be required.
