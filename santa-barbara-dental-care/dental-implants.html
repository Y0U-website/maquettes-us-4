Planning Dental Implants in Santa Barbara

A useful implant consultation should leave you knowing which teeth need replacement, the available alternatives, and what treatment would involve. Bring your questions and any previous treatment estimates to our Bath Street office.

What affects dental implant cost?

The number of teeth being replaced is only part of the estimate. Ask which examinations, imaging, implant components, replacement teeth, and follow-up visits are included. Extractions, bone preparation, temporary teeth, or sedation may be separate parts of a recommended plan.

Compare the complete treatment plan when considering prices. Our dental financing information explains the Cherry and CareCredit resources linked by the practice. Ask about current terms and eligibility; a monthly payment alone does not describe the total cost.

Is an implant the right option?

An examination considers your remaining teeth, gums, bone, health history, and ability to maintain the restoration. Tell the dentist about medications, medical conditions, and tobacco use. Implant placement involves surgery, and healing or long-term results cannot be guaranteed.

Depending on your needs, the conversation may include dental bridges , removable dentures , or full-arch implant treatment . Ask which options preserve useful teeth and suit your priorities.

From planning to the final restoration

Implant treatment generally involves placement, a healing period, and fitting the replacement tooth or teeth. Some cases need additional preparation. Ask whether a temporary replacement is appropriate, how eating may change during healing, and which visits are needed before the final restoration.

Your dentist will provide an individual timeline and care instructions. Tell the office promptly about a loose or painful implant, and keep recommended maintenance visits.
