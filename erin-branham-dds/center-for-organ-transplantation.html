Who We Are

At WVU Medicine Center for Organ Transplantation, you’re more than just a number— you are the center of our care.

Whether you or a loved one has struggled with advanced heart failure or chronic kidney disease, WVU Medicine Center for Organ Transplantation helps patients find hope and healing on their journey to better health.

What We Do

We’re here to help those in need of life-saving heart and/or kidney transplants.

Those living with advanced heart failure or chronic kidney disease (CKD) no longer need to leave the state for life-saving care – your team is right here.

Our care team provides more than transplants – we offer unmatched support from referral to post-transplant recovery and beyond. At every step, you can count on compassionate care delivered by some of the country’s top surgeons, physicians, and transplant professionals who work closely with a broader team, including pharmacists, social workers, dietitians, and financial coordinators.

We are passionate about working to improve the field of transplantation care, research, and education. Our team is your team.

Heart Transplant

Heart transplant is one of the many treatments available to patients diagnosed with advanced heart failure. If you’re referred for a heart transplant evaluation, you don’t have to navigate the journey alone. We’re here every step of the way.

Kidney Transplant

Kidney transplant is one option for treating end-stage kidney disease and chronic kidney disease. Our board-certified nephrologists and surgeons are trained to care for patients with the most complex kidney conditions using the latest treatments and advances in organ transplantation.
