The practice

Endodontic Studio | Oakton is a specialty endodontic practice in Oakton, Virginia focused on accurate diagnosis, precise treatment, and preserving natural teeth. Dr. Abasin Safi provides root canal therapy, root canal retreatment, endodontic microsurgery, cracked-tooth evaluation, root resorption treatment, and advanced 3D imaging in a calm, personalized setting.

We welcome patients from Oakton, Vienna, Fairfax, and surrounding Northern Virginia communities, and work closely with referring dentists to provide clear communication and thoughtful specialty care.

Why choose an endodontist? Endodontists are dental specialists focused on diagnosing tooth pain and treating conditions involving the dental pulp and tissues surrounding the roots of teeth. Our practice is limited to endodontics, allowing us to focus on complex diagnosis, root canal treatment, retreatment, microsurgery, and other procedures designed to help preserve natural teeth.

Our mission is to help patients preserve their natural teeth through thoughtful, precise endodontic care. We are committed to creating a calm and supportive experience built on trust, clarity, and comfort. We strive to be a dependable partner to patients and referring doctors through excellent care and clear communication.

Dr. Safi is a board-eligible endodontist with experience as both a general dentist and an endodontic specialist. This background provides a restorative-minded approach to specialty endodontic care.
