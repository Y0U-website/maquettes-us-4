What to Expect During Treatment

Your comfort is our priority during scaling and root planing at Cameron Park Family Dentistry. The procedure typically occurs over one or two visits, with Dr. Nav focusing on different quadrants of your mouth during each session. We use local anesthesia to ensure you’re comfortable throughout the treatment. Our team takes time to thoroughly clean each area, using specialized instruments and techniques to achieve optimal results. Following treatment, you may experience mild sensitivity for a few days, but this typically resolves quickly as your gums begin to heal.

Is Scaling and Root Planing Right For You?

Dr. Nav may recommend scaling and root planing if you show signs of early to moderate gum disease. Common indicators include:

  • Bleeding gums during brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath
  • Red, swollen, or tender gums
  • Deeper periodontal pockets (measured during your exam)
