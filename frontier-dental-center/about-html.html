Meet Your Apex, NC Dentist

About Us

Dedicated to Your Dental Health & Your Family

Frontier Dental Center was founded with one simple mission: make high-quality dental care accessible, comfortable, and affordable for every family in the Apex, Cary, and the greater Triangle area, NC community.

Led by Dr. Priyadarshini (Priya) Raju, our team brings decades of clinical expertise and a genuine passion for patient well-being. We believe a healthy smile is the foundation of confidence and quality of life.

From routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic and restorative treatments, we offer comprehensive services tailored to each patient’s unique needs — all in a warm, welcoming environment where you’ll always feel at home.

Dr. Priya Raju didn’t just build a dental practice — she built a place where patients feel genuinely cared for. As the founder and lead dentist of Frontier Dental Center, she brings more than 15 years of experience in general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry, along with an unwavering belief that exceptional dentistry is equal parts science, art, and human connection.

A Foundation Built Across Two Continents

Originally from Southern India — a region steeped in culture, art, and academic tradition — Dr. Raju earned her Bachelor of Dental Surgery with Distinction from Dr. M.G.R. Medical University in Chennai, where she trained under accomplished dental professionals and developed a deep foundation in comprehensive patient care.

Her drive for excellence brought her to the United States, where she completed the Advanced Standing Program for International Dentists and earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree with High Honors from the Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine at Boston University — one of the most respected dental institutions in the country.

Dr. Raju approaches every case — whether a routine cleaning or a complete smile transformation — with the same level of precision, creativity, and personalized attention. She believes that restoring a smile requires more than clinical skill; it requires an artistic eye, a thoughtful plan, and a dentist who truly listens.

Patients consistently describe her as warm, unhurried, and easy to talk to. She has a gift for making even the most apprehensive patients feel comfortable, and she finds deep fulfillment not just in creating beautiful smiles, but in relieving pain and improving the overall well-being of the people she serves. Her guiding philosophy is straightforward: treat every patient the way she would want her own family treated — with honesty, compassion, and exceptional care.

Dr. Raju is an Invisalign-certified provider and is actively enrolled in continuing dental education, ensuring that her patients benefit from the latest evidence-based treatments and modern techniques the field has to offer. Staying current isn’t just a professional obligation for her — it’s a reflection of the respect she has for her patients.

Dr. Raju lives in Cary with her husband, two children, and a full, joyful life outside of dentistry. She is an avid painter who works across multiple mediums, loves experimenting in the kitchen with new recipes, and treasures every moment spent with family and friends. The same creativity and care she brings to her art is evident in every smile she creates.
