Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, creating a permanent root for a natural-looking crown that blends seamlessly with your smile.

Unlike dentures, implants are fixed, function exactly like natural teeth, and help preserve your jawbone for a lasting solution that can serve you for decades.

  • Permanent, natural-looking tooth replacement
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure
  • No slipping, adhesives, or special cleaning
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care
  • Improves chewing function and speech

Root Canal Therapy

When a tooth’s pulp becomes infected due to deep decay, trauma, or cracks, a root canal removes the infection, cleans the canal, and seals the tooth to prevent further damage.

  • Relieves severe toothache and infection pain
  • Saves your natural tooth
  • Prevents infection from spreading
  • Comfortable with modern technique
  • Completed with a protective crown

Porcelain Veneers

Veneers are ultra-thin porcelain shells bonded to the front of your teeth to transform their color, shape, size, and alignment. One of the most powerful cosmetic dental treatments available.

Whether you want to correct chips, discoloration, minor misalignment, or gaps, veneers deliver a dramatic, long-lasting smile transformation that looks completely natural.

  • Corrects chips, cracks, and deep stains
  • Closes gaps and reshapes uneven teeth
  • Stain-resistant porcelain material
  • Results look completely natural
  • Lasts 10–15 years with proper care
