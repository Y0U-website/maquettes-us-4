What is a dental implant? A reason to smile

Regain confidence, function, and long-term oral health with custom-designed Louisville dental implants!

A dental implant is a permanent tooth replacement that mimics a natural tooth root. Surgically placed into the jawbone, the implant fuses with the bone to create a strong, stable foundation for a custom crown, bridge, or denture — restoring the look, feel, and function of your natural teeth.

Answers to the most common questions of Dental Implants

What are dental implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a foundation for a replacement tooth or teeth.

What are the benefits of dental implants?

Dental implants offer incredible benefits. When compared to traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants offer improved oral health, increased confidence, and better functionality and comfort.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

If you have one or more missing teeth or are experiencing problems with your dentures, our dental implants may be a great option. We don’t take a one-size-fits-all approach and would love to see you for a consultation and give you a personalized evaluation and recommendation.

How many teeth can be replaced with dental implants?

Dental implants can replace a single tooth or the entire arch of missing teeth.

Will I need a bone graft?

You may need a bone graft if your jawbone has deteriorated or is too thin to support a dental implant. A bone graft helps strengthen your jawbone and provides a solid foundation for the implant.

How long does the process take?

Each case is unique, but with our advanced technology and in-house lab, some patients can receive implants in just one day!
