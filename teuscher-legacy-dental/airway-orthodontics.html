Guiding Growth, Not Just Straightening Teeth

Every plan is built around your child’s unique anatomy, habits, and stage of growth — never a cookie-cutter protocol.

Palatal Expansion

Gently widening the upper jaw broadens the smile — and because the palate is the floor of the nose, it opens the nasal airway at the same time.

Growth Guidance Appliances

Age-appropriate appliances that encourage forward, balanced jaw development while the facial bones are still shapeable.

Tongue & Lip Tie Evaluation

Restricted tongues keep the palate from developing properly. We assess ties in the context of the whole child — and never rush a release decision.

Nasal Breathing Restoration

Habit correction and myofunctional therapy partnerships help retrain tongue posture and re-establish nasal breathing day and night.

Sleep-Focused Outcomes

Better breathing means deeper sleep — and parents often report calmer mornings, better focus, and fewer behavior struggles.

Growth Monitoring

We track development through the growing years, intervening only when it truly benefits your child — and watching patiently when it doesn’t.
