The words that make it easier .

What to say the night before

  • "You’ll get to meet Mr. Thirsty and the tooth counter." Names the tools without naming a threat. Little kids love Mr. Thirsty.
  • "They’ll count your teeth and take pictures." Accurate, and it’s genuinely most of the visit.
  • "I’ll be right there with you." The single most reassuring thing you can say.
  • "You get to pick something from the treasure box after." A reward for going, not for enduring.
  • "It won’t hurt." They weren’t thinking about pain until you said the word.
  • "Don’t be scared." Same problem — it introduces the idea.
  • "You might need a shot / a drill." We don’t use those words in the office. Neither should the car ride.
  • "Be brave and we’ll get ice cream." Frames the visit as an ordeal to survive.
