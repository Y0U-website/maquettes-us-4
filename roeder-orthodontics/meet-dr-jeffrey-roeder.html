Meet Dr. Jeffrey Roeder

Being a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics allows me to help people of all ages, from many walks of life, and make significant improvements in their oral health and facial and dental aesthetics. For more than 30 years, it’s been my pleasure to help others through a profession that boosted their health and emotional well-being.

In our practice, I am the only orthodontist. This allows me to get to know each patient and also to be able to follow each patient’s treatment closely allowing for the most predictable and efficient outcomes.

In the years I have been seeing patients, I have had the honor to meet and care for many amazing people. I appreciate having the time to get to know each patient on a personal level during the treatment experience. Our conversations, whether they’re telling me about their latest after-school activity, sharing a joke, or talking about their weekend plans, always delight me.

My work is fascinating, as well as professionally and personally fulfilling. I look forward to work each day; everyone should be so lucky!

BEYOND THE OFFICE

Originally from Philadelphia, I’ve lived in Fletcher for 25 years. When I’m not seeing patients, I’m usually spending time with my wife, four children, daughter-in-law, and 3 grandchildren. Family means everything to me and I treasure our time together.

I also enjoy exploring the outdoors and enjoying all that our state offers, especially plentiful fishing excursions and hiking opportunities.
