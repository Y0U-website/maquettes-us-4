Frequently Asked Questions

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

Treatment length depends on your specific needs but typically ranges from 12–24 months.

Is Invisalign as effective as braces?

Yes. For many patients, Invisalign is just as effective as braces when worn as directed.

What is the best age to see an orthodontist?

Age 7 is ideal for an initial evaluation, but teens and adults can begin treatment at any time.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — we provide flexible payment plans and accept most insurance providers.

Are braces painful?

Modern braces and Invisalign cause minimal discomfort, usually limited to the first few days after adjustments.
