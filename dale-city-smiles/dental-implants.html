The Dental Implant Process

Your visit includes digital imaging, X-rays, a comprehensive oral exam, and treatment planning with your dentist.

Using local anesthesia, the implant is carefully placed into the jawbone. Sedation options are available to help keep the procedure comfortable.

Over several months, the implant heals and integrates with the bone. Once healed, a custom crown is attached to complete the restoration and support normal function.
