Dental Care

  • Radiographs: Do I really need x-rays? Yes! At least periodically. Dental radiographs have very low radiation and are the only way to detect certain conditions. To see is to know and not to see is to guess. We don’t take chances with your health!
  • Topical fluoride varnish: If you have numerous cavities, we may recommend fluoride to be "painted" on your teeth every 6 months. Fluoride can help strengthen the outer portion of your tooth (enamel) and make it more resistant to getting cavities.

Restorative

These procedures help to restore and revive your smile to proper function, health and esthetics by repairing anything from a small cavity to tooth loss.

  • Fillings: We use tooth colored fillings (composite) to repair tooth defects such as cavities and chipping.
  • Crowns: These are restorations that cover an entire tooth that would be prone to breaking or is too damaged to be repaired with a traditional filling. We perfectly blend the color and function of these teeth to match your natural smile.
  • Bridges and Implant Restoration: If you are missing a tooth, these options will help you avoid having a removable appliance. We work with top notch dental laboratories and dental surgeons to help achieve a beautiful and natural result when replacing a natural tooth.
  • Removable dentures: "False" teeth get a bad rap. A properly designed and executed removable denture can fit well and look beautiful if implant or bridge restorations are unsuitable.

Cosmetic

This is all about simply enhancing your existing beauty and letting your smile do the talking.

The following procedures can be implemented to help give you the smile of your dreams.

  • Whitening: A simple 1-hour in office whitening procedure will give you that instant boost in confidence and sparkle. This is non-invasive and can be easily "touched up" at home with complimentary custom whitening trays.
  • Porcelain veneers: these provide a new "facing" to your teeth. With porcelain veneers you can change the size, shape and color of your teeth for a total smile transformation.
