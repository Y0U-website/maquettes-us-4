Learn more about Dr. Whitney Lamb

Dr. Whitney Lamb was born and raised in Altus, OK. She attended Altus High School, East Central University and Southwestern Oklahoma State University. She continued her education at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry earning her Doctorate of Dental Surgery in June 2007. Dr. Lamb returned to Altus to practice in her hometown. In April 2008, Dr. Lamb opened her own private practice.

Dr. Whitney Lamb is a member of the American Dental Association, Oklahoma Dental Association, American Association of Women Dentist, American Academy of Facial Esthetics. She is also certified in CAD/Cam technology, hard and soft laser and specializes in Invisalign.

Dr. Lamb is dedicated to providing State of the Art Dentistry in a family environment. She brings a gentle and compassionate approach to dentistry. She strives to treat patients as she would her own family.

Dr. Whitney Lamb and her husband, Jonathon, have 4 boys. They treasure any time they can get to spend time camping with their family. Dr. Lamb’s husband is a coach for the high school football team and baseball team, so they stay very busy supporting him and the teams. Sports are a big part of their lives and they enjoy attending all of the games.
