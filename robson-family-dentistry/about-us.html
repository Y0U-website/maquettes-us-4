About Us

Since 1979, Robson Family Dentistry has been the dental practice families in Bellefontaine, OH and the surrounding areas have trusted for quality, affordable dental care. We proudly treat patients of all ages, as we also offer pediatric and geriatric dentistry. Our dentists offer a wide range of treatments , including cleanings, exams, restorations, teeth whitening and more. We strive to create a comfortable, patient-centered environment where you and your family’s dental needs always come first!

Our History

Robson Family Dentistry was founded in Bellefontaine by Dr. Jason E. Robson D.D.S. His son, Dr. Jason A. Robson D.D.S., joined in 2014 when they renovated a local church to be the new office. They both love the science of healthcare and are driven to serve and better the Bellefontaine community.
