Replace missing, damaged or severely decayed teeth with dental implants in Tampa, FL

Dental implants are the most advanced method for replacing missing teeth. A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone to act as a new root, topped with a custom crown, bridge, or denture for a seamless and stable fit. Implants look and feel like real teeth, helping you chew, smile, and speak with confidence—without worrying about slipping or shifting.

Process of Dental Implants

Consultation and 3D Imaging: We assess your oral health, take digital scans or X-rays, and determine if you’re a good candidate for implants. Bone grafting may be recommended if additional support is needed.

Implant Placement: A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone where the missing tooth was. The area is then allowed to heal and integrate with your bone over several months.

Next Steps After Getting Dental Implants

Abutment and Restoration: Once healed, we attach a small connector (abutment) and place your custom crown, bridge, or denture—designed to match your natural teeth in color and shape.

Final Fit and Function: We make any necessary adjustments and ensure your bite feels comfortable and natural. You’ll leave with a fully restored, functional smile.

FAQ

  • Does insurance cover dental implants? Insurance may partially cover implants if they’re medically necessary. We’ll help verify your benefits and offer flexible financing options if needed.
  • How do I care for dental implants? Treat your implant like a natural tooth—brush twice a day, floss daily, and visit us regularly to keep your gums and implant healthy.
  • How long do dental implants last? With good oral hygiene and regular dental care, implants can last 20 years or more.

Related Services

Dental Crowns and Bridges

Dental crowns strengthen damaged or decayed teeth with a natural-looking cap, while bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to surrounding teeth. When there’s not enough exposed tooth for a crown to fit properly, crown lengthening gently reshapes the gum and bone to reveal more of the tooth structure, allowing for a secure and lasting restoration.

Dentures

Dentures are a reliable, natural-looking solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence. We offer full and partial dentures that are custom-fit for comfort and function. With ongoing support, we ensure your dentures look great and feel secure every day.
