Retainers

At Connection Dental, we understand that retention is just as critical as active treatment. That is why we are dedicated to guiding you through the final step of your orthodontic journey: retention.

After you have worn braces or aligners for the appropriate length of time, you will enter the retention phase. This phase helps your teeth settle into their new positions and ensures your smile remains aligned for years to come. Without proper retention, your teeth may begin to shift back, undoing all the progress you have made.

Invisalign Aligners

One of the most popular orthodontic options for patients of all ages is Invisalign. These custom-made, clear aligners are nearly invisible, providing a discreet way to straighten teeth without brackets or wires.

Invisalign is ideal for adults and teens who prefer a more modern approach to orthodontics. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before moving to the next set in the series. The trays apply gentle pressure to guide your teeth into their ideal positions over time.

Benefits of Invisalign include:

Patients appreciate the control and flexibility Invisalign offers especially those in professional settings or with active lifestyles. And because the trays are removable, it is easier to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.

  • Discreet appearance virtually invisible to others
  • Removable trays for easy eating, brushing, and flossing
  • Fewer office visits compared to traditional braces
  • Smooth plastic trays that are more comfortable than metal

Braces

For patients who need more comprehensive correction, traditional braces remain a reliable and time-tested option. Braces are incredibly effective at treating complex orthodontic issues such as significant crowding, bite misalignment, and jaw irregularities.

We offer both traditional metal braces and more subtle ceramic options to cater to your aesthetic preferences and clinical needs. Today’s braces are sleeker, more comfortable, and more efficient than ever before.

While they are more visible than aligners, braces are still an excellent choice for many. Teens, children, and adults alike benefit from the thorough treatment that braces provide, predominantly when guided by our experienced dental team.

During your consultation, we will explain how braces can be incorporated into your treatment plan and what to expect throughout your orthodontic journey

  • Read More Braces offer: Complete control over tooth movement
  • Excellent results for even the most complex alignment issues
  • Durable, long-lasting materials
  • Suitable for patients of all ages

Invisalign vs. Braces: Pros and Cons

Choosing between Invisalign and braces depends on several factors, including your lifestyle, treatment goals, budget, and oral health needs. Here is a side-by-side comparison to help you make an informed choice:

  • Invisalign Pros Discreet and virtually invisible
  • Removable for meals and oral hygiene
  • Smooth and comfortable
  • Ideal for mild to moderate alignment issues
  • Fewer dietary restrictions
  • Preferred by many patients of all ages
  • Invisalign Cons Must be worn 20 22 hours per day for effectiveness
  • It may not be suitable for severe alignment issues.
  • Requires discipline to avoid losing or forgetting trays
  • It can be more expensive in some instances.
  • Braces Pros Effective for complex and severe alignment issues
  • No risk of losing them always on
  • Often faster for significant tooth movement
  • Available in multiple types (metal, ceramic)
  • Reliable option for children and teens
  • Braces Cons Visible on the teeth
  • Dietary restrictions (avoid hard, sticky foods)
  • Requires extra effort for oral hygiene
  • May cause initial discomfort from brackets and wires

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • Who Is a Good Candidate for Orthodontic Treatment? Orthodontic treatment is suitable for patients of all ages, including children, teenagers , and adults. Whether you are dealing with crooked teeth, bite issues, or spacing concerns, we can design a treatment plan tailored to your needs and lifestyle.
  • Is Invisalign as Effective as Braces? Yes, Invisalign is effective for many mild to moderate alignment issues. However, traditional braces are often the better choice for more complex cases. Our team will help you weigh the pros and cons based on your goals. Both options can deliver an attractive smile, but they differ in their working mechanisms and the level of control they offer.
  • How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Usually Take? Treatment duration varies depending on the method used and the complexity of your case. Invisalign and clear aligners may take 6 to 18 months, while braces can range from 12 to 24 months or more. Regardless of which option you choose, the goal is always a healthy, attractive smile that lasts.
  • Will My Orthodontic Treatment Be Noticeable? Not necessarily! If you are concerned about visibility, options like Invisalign and clear aligners are designed to be discreet, allowing you to straighten your teeth without drawing attention to yourself. Even our modern braces are smaller and more subtle than traditional metal brackets.
  • Are Orthodontic Treatments Painful? You may experience some mild pressure or discomfort when starting treatment or switching to a new aligner, but this is normal and temporary. Our caring staff is here to make sure your experience is as smooth and pain-free as possible.
  • Do I Need to Wear a Retainer After Treatment? Yes, retention is essential to maintaining your results. After completing your treatment with aligners or braces, you will be fitted with a custom retainer to help keep your teeth in place. Wearing your retainer as directed ensures your attractive smile lasts a lifetime.
  • Can Adults Get Braces or Invisalign? Absolutely. We treat patients of all ages, and adult orthodontics is becoming increasingly common. Whether you are preparing for a significant life event or just want to feel more confident, it is never too late to start your smile journey.
  • What Is the Difference Between Clear Aligners and Invisalign? Invisalign is a specific brand of clear aligners recognized for its advanced technology, comfort, and customized treatment plans. We also offer other discreet aligner solutions, like Candid, depending on your needs and preferences. Our team will explain all your options during your consultation.
