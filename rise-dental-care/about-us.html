About Our Practice

Comfortable, Modern Dental Care Designed Around You

For more than five decades, our practice has served Brevard County with a commitment to clinical excellence and lasting patient relationships. Established in 1974, the practice continues to build on decades of experience, offering comprehensive general dentistry supported by advanced technology and a highly trained team. Every visit is designed to be efficient, comfortable, and personalized—delivering modern care in a welcoming, stress-free environment.

Advanced Care

Technology That Elevates Your Care

We believe that investing in advanced dental technology is essential to delivering the highest level of care. From same-day restorations to detailed digital imaging, our technology allows us to diagnose more accurately, treat more efficiently, and provide a better experience for every patient.

3D Imaging & Digital X-Rays

Advanced diagnostic precision Our advanced imaging technology combines digital X-rays with detailed 3D imaging to provide highly accurate views of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding structures. This technology allows our team to diagnose conditions earlier, plan treatments more precisely, and ensure safer, more predictable results.

Intraoral Cameras

See what we see Intraoral cameras allow us to capture high-resolution images inside your mouth in real time. This technology helps patients clearly understand their oral health and allows our team to detect issues such as decay, cracks, and gum disease earlier.

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF)

Natural healing and regeneration PRF therapy uses a small sample of your own blood to concentrate growth factors that accelerate healing and tissue regeneration. This natural treatment is commonly used in dental implants, extractions, bone grafting, and facial aesthetic procedures to improve healing and support better long-term results.

BIOLASE Laser Dentistry

Minimally invasive laser treatment BIOLASE laser technology allows many dental procedures to be performed with greater precision, less discomfort, and faster healing. Laser dentistry often reduces the need for traditional tools like drills or scalpels, providing a more comfortable and minimally invasive experience.

DEXIS Digital Scanning

Comfortable digital impressions The DEXIS IS 3800 intraoral scanner captures highly accurate 3D images of your teeth and gums in seconds. This digital scanning technology replaces traditional impressions, making the process faster, cleaner, and much more comfortable while improving treatment precision.

Advanced 3D Printing

Precision and customization Our advanced 3D printing technology allows us to create highly precise dental models, surgical guides, and custom appliances. By combining digital scanning and 3D printing, we can plan treatments more accurately and deliver faster, more efficient care.
