Dr. Jonah P. Spaeth 🎬

Dr. Jonah Spaeth grew up in Riverhead, New York. He received his B.A. in Biological Sciences in 2017 from Binghamton University. He then completed his dental education at Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine and earned his D.D.S. degree in 2021. Dr. Spaeth then pursued his two-year Pediatric Dentistry specialty training at One Brooklyn Health-Interfaith Medical Center where he worked with pediatric and special needs patients to make coming to the dentist an enjoyable experience.

In his free time Dr. Spaeth loves going to the gym, cooking, and spending time with his family and his two french bulldogs, Lola and Maple.
