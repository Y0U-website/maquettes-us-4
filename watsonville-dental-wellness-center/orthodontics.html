Orthodontics in Watsonville

When you’re thinking of straightening your teeth with braces or Invisalign, you’re thinking of orthodontics. It’s a field of dentistry that focuses on correcting the position and alignment of your teeth and jaws.

Besides creating a straighter smile, effective orthodontic treatment can prevent facial muscles and bone from deteriorating. It also preserves the normal growth of your jaw, which can be stunted by misalignment.

Performing quality orthodontics requires extensive training and expertise, which our staff at Watsonville Dental Wellness Center happens to have. We offer several orthodontic treatment options that can work best for you.

Do You Need an Orthodontist?

Orthodontists focus on irregularities in the jaw and teeth. Children and teenagers may need orthodontic treatment to guide proper dental development, but adults can also benefit from orthodontic care to correct long-standing misalignments or bite problems.

Orthodontic treatment goes beyond just improving the appearance of your smile. Orthodontists can help improve your bite, your ability to chew and speak properly, and even your breathing.

If you’re suffering from malocclusions (bad bites), or pain and discomfort from jaw misalignment or teeth grinding, you might want to talk to an orthodontist.

Here are some other issues that orthodontic treatment can fix:

Overbites

An overbite occurs when the upper front teeth overlap the lower front teeth excessively, which can lead to issues with chewing, jaw pain, and uneven tooth wear.

Underbites

An underbite is when the lower teeth extend beyond the upper teeth, often causing difficulty with chewing, speech issues, and an imbalanced facial appearance.

Cross Bites

A crossbite happens when some of the upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth when biting down, which can lead to uneven wear, gum issues, and jaw discomfort.

Teeth Crowding

Crowding occurs when there isn’t enough space in the mouth for all the teeth to align properly, often resulting in overlapping or twisted teeth that are harder to clean and maintain.

Crooked Teeth

Misaligned or crooked teeth not only affect the appearance of your smile but can also cause difficulties with oral hygiene and increase the risk of gum disease and tooth decay.

Frequently Asked Questions about Orthodontics

What Is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialized branch of dentistry that focuses on diagnosing, preventing, and treating irregularities in the teeth and jaw, such as misaligned teeth and bite issues.

What Age Is Best for Orthodontic Treatment?

While orthodontic treatment can be done at any age, it’s typically recommended for children around the age of 7, when early issues can be detected. However, adults can also benefit from orthodontic care.

How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take?

The length of treatment varies depending on the individual case, but most orthodontic treatments last between 18 months and 3 years.

Are Braces My Only Option?

No, there are various options available, including traditional metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear aligners like Invisalign, which offer more discreet treatment options.

Will Orthodontic Treatment Hurt?

There may be some discomfort or soreness after braces are applied or adjusted, but it is typically mild and temporary. Over-the-counter pain relievers can help ease any discomfort.

How Do I Care for My Teeth With Braces?

It’s important to maintain excellent oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly, avoiding sticky or hard foods that could damage your braces, and visiting your dentist for routine cleanings.

Can Orthodontics Fix Jaw Problems?

Yes, orthodontic treatment can address issues related to jaw misalignment, such as overbites, underbites, and crossbites, improving both function and appearance.

Do I Need a Retainer After Treatment?

Yes, wearing a retainer after braces or aligner treatment is essential to keep your teeth in their new positions and prevent them from shifting back.
