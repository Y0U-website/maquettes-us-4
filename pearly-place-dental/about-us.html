Dr. Anudeep Grewal

Dr. Grewal brings a delicate and gentle touch to Pearly Place Dental. Her genuine passion for cosmetic and restorative dentistry as well as clinical training create a personal and patient-focused atmosphere. She is an avid believer in thoroughly discussing all available treatment options in order to create an individualized plan that addresses both the clinical needs and concerns of each patient. Dr. Grewal is committed to providing comfortable and quality comprehensive dental care to all of her patients.

In addition to Dr. Grewal’s essential role at Pearly Place Dental, she is also an active member of organized dentistry through her affiliations with the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, and various state dental associations in New York, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania. When she isn’t helping patients, Dr. Grewal enjoys trying new recipes, yoga, gardening, and learning new things.

Kaela

Kaela attended Farmingdale State College to become a registered dental hygienist because she is a people person who loves helping patients maintain their smiles and prevent oral health issues. She also thrives as part of the collaborative team at Pearly Place Dental.

When she isn’t busy helping patients and working with Dr. Grewal, Kaela enjoys walking her Rottweiler puppy named Raider, going to the driving range to hit golf balls, and watching documentaries. She also likes to play Candy Crush.

Wendy

Wendy has been a dental assistant since 1996; she started out working in podiatry, but when an opportunity came up in the dental field, she grabbed it and never looked back. Wendy’s responsibilities at Pearly Place Dental include making patients feel comfortable, sterilizing treatment rooms, pouring models, and assisting Dr. Grewal and the dental team with anything they need.

Wendy loves interacting with our patients and her fellow team members, and she enjoys everything the dental field offers.

When she isn’t working, Wendy enjoys taking walks, reading, attending comedy clubs, watching movies, attending concerts, and just relaxing at home. You might find her shopping for angel figurines and statues to add to her collection.
