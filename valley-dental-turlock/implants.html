Caring for Your Implants

After your procedure is complete, it is important to care for your implants just as you would your natural teeth, including daily brushing and professional dental cleanings at least every six months. A follow-up appointment is necessary so we can make sure your gums are healthy and the implant is healing well. With good hygiene and regular checkups, implants can last for decades, often a lifetime.

Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots. Once placed in your jawbone, they support a crown, bridge, or denture, providing a permanent solution for missing teeth that looks and feels natural.

Implants are a long-term solution that helps preserve your jawbone and prevent shifting of other teeth. While dentures and bridges remain viable options, implants offer exceptional stability, comfort, and durability for many patients.

If you are missing one or more teeth and have healthy gums and bone, you may be a good candidate. Dr. Ahmadi will perform a thorough evaluation to determine if implants are right for you or if bone grafting or other treatments are needed first.

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or more, often a lifetime. Regular checkups and good oral hygiene are key to long-term success.
