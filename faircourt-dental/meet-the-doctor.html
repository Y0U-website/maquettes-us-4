Meet Dr. Sabrina R. Salim

Cosmetic Dentistry Restorative Dentistry Sedation Dentistry Cosmetic Dentistry Restorative Dentistry Sedation Dentistry patient resources success stories contact Call us Today meet the doctor Meet Dr Sabrina Salim For Dr. Salim, dentistry is both a profession and a passion. She earned her dental degree from the University of Detroit Mercy and completed a dental residency at the University of Michigan. After more than a decade in practice, she became the owner of Faircourt Dental, where she continues to focus on thoughtful, patient-centered care.

Dr. Salim is deeply committed to the future of dentistry. She enjoys teaching and mentoring emerging dental professionals and has served as a clinical professor at the University of Michigan School of Dentistry, working closely with younger colleagues as they develop their skills and confidence.

She is also actively involved in advancing the profession. Over the years, Dr. Salim has held leadership roles with the American Dental Association, the Michigan Dental Association, and the Oakland County Dental Society, where she served as president. She currently serves on the Michigan Dental Association’s Endorse Committee, evaluating dental products and services to support high standards of care.

At the heart of her work is a belief that everyone deserves access to quality dental care. Dr. Salim regularly volunteers her time in underserved communities locally and abroad, including service trips to Honduras, Lebanon, and Peru, as well as outreach efforts throughout Michigan. She also participates in the Give Kids A Smile program.
