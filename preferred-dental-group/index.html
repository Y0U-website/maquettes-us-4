The practice

Dr. Chet Regula DMD, founded our practice in 1987. He earned his DMD from the University of Pennsylvania in 1975. Dr. Chet Regula DMD, traveled with the Detroit Redwings Hockey Team for 27 years of his career and since 2002 he had been the only Team Dentist for the Detroit Lions Organization. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys spending time with family, staying fit, and golfing.

Dr. Susannah Lyddon was raised in Plymouth, Michigan with her Mom, Dad, and two younger sisters. Dr. Lyddon graduated from Michigan State University with a Bachelor of Science degree in Nutritional Science.

Dr. Kosta Patsarikas attended Oakland University for undergrad and then attended the University of Michigan Dental School. Outside of work, Kosta enjoys being active, playing golf, basketball, and soccer; he also enjoys spending time with his friends and family.

Dr. Meghil is board-certified in periodontology and dental implant surgery by the American Board of Periodontology. He earned his dental degree from the University of Benghaz and practiced as a general dentist before moving the United States.
