What to Do in a Dental Emergency

  • Knocked-out tooth: Keep the tooth moist in milk or saline, and handle it only by the crown—avoid touching the root.
  • Swelling: Apply a cold compress to the affected area to reduce inflammation.
  • Bleeding: Apply firm, gentle pressure with clean gauze until it stops.
  • Lost filling or crown: Use temporary dental cement (available at most pharmacies) to protect the area until you’re seen.
  • Toothache: Rinse with warm salt water and avoid placing aspirin directly on the gums.
