FAQs

What makes dental implants different from dentures or bridges?

Unlike removable dentures, dental implants permanently anchor into your jawbone with a titanium implant post, providing stability that feels just like your natural teeth. Bridges require altering healthy adjacent teeth, while implants preserve your existing tooth structure and maintain better oral hygiene access. This makes implants an excellent solution that protects your long-term oral health while delivering superior chewing efficiency.

How do I know if I’m a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with missing teeth and adequate jawbone density make ideal candidates for this modern dentistry solution. Your overall health, oral hygiene habits, and commitment to proper care play important roles in determining candidacy during your consultation. We’ll review your medical history and examine your specific situation to ensure dental implants are the right choice for your needs.

Will dental implants look natural with my existing teeth?

Our skilled dentist carefully matches your implant crown to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. The result is a complete, natural-looking smile that no one will be able to distinguish from your original teeth. You’ll regain confidence in social situations and daily life without worrying about your restoration being noticeable.

Can I eat all my favorite foods after getting dental implants?

Once fully healed, dental implants restore your ability to enjoy a wide range of foods that may have been difficult with missing teeth. The titanium implant post provides the same biting and chewing strength as natural tooth roots, allowing you to eat comfortably and confidently. You’ll no longer need to avoid certain textures or worry about your teeth shifting while eating.

How long does recovery take after implant placement?

Initial healing typically takes a few days to a week, during which you’ll follow specific aftercare guidelines to ensure proper healing. The complete integration process takes several months as your jawbone naturally fuses with the implant, but most patients return to normal activities within days. Regular dental appointments during this period help monitor your progress and address any concerns throughout your dental experience.
