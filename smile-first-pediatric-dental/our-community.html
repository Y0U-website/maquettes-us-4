Giving Back to Durham

Since we began our practice in 2015, the team at Smile First Dentist has donated more than 10 % of our profits yearly. We genuinely believe that our services should reach beyond the doors of our office, and it’s important for us to give back to our community.

We have donated to many local elementary schools, daycares, families in need, local businesses, and partner with Nourish.org who guide students with the skills, confidence, and self awareness needed to become social impact leaders.
