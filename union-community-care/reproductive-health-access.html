Reproductive Health Access

Why women choose IUDs: highly effective, discreet, and fertility returns quickly after removal.

  • Copper IUDs (e.g., Paragard) contain no hormones and last up to 10 years.

Birth Control Implants

Birth Control Implants A thin rod placed under the skin of the upper arm.

Why women choose implants: over 99 % effective, nearly invisible, and ideal for those who don’t want to manage daily birth control.

  • The implant (e.g., Nexplanon) releases progestin and lasts up to 3 years.
