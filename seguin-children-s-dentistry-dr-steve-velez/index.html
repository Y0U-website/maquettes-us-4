The practice

We look forward to teaching your child the importance of forming healthy dental habits early in life to maintain a healthy smile that will last a lifetime.

Seguin Children’s Dentistry specializes in treating infants, school age children, toddlers, and teens. Our goal is to help each patient develop good oral hygiene practices from a young age and maintain their dental health throughout their lives.

​Personalized Dental Care We provide routine exams, cleanings, comprehensive dental treatment, and immediate dental appointments when dental emergencies occur. We offer longer appointment times for patients with special needs or healthcare concerns.

During exams, we check a patient’s teeth and gums, conduct oral cancer screenings, and complete necessary imaging such as X-rays. Most dental issues can be prevented with proper oral care, that includes daily flossing, brushing, and the application of dental sealants .

Although your smile might seem like our primary concern, we also focus on your overall health. Medication or illnesses you have can affect your teeth and gums, that in turn can impact the rest of your body.

​Sedation Dentistry for Children Dental sedation for young patients can make long or intensive dental procedures much easier, or simply calm anxious patients who require some help feeling at ease in a dental office.

​Uncompromising Safety Because of the importance of infection control, our office goes to great lengths to protect ourselves and our patients.
