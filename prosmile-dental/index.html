Dr. Wang received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of the Pacific Dugoni School of Dentistry. She graduated with the highest honors and was inducted into the top dental honor societies of Omicron Kappa Upsilon and Tau Kappa Omega. She was also a recipient of the Dean’s Valedictorian award for her top academy achievement. Among other awards and accolades, she is most proud of…

Before coming to the United States, Dr. Wang was a licensed dentist in China. She graduated from Peking University and completed a three-year residency in endodontics at Peking University Stomatological Hospital. This training expanded her clinical experience and improved her treatment efficiency.

Dr. Jing Xu has been practicing dentistry since 2007. After graduated with honors from dental school in China, she went to Japan and received her PhD degree and specialty in Restorative and Preventive Dentistry. After that, she came to US with her family and went on to receive her DDS degree from USC.

Dentistry is one of her passion in life. She believes that open communication between patient and doctor, and patient-centered comprehensive care are critical for providing good dental service to patients. She takes the time to listen to patient’s needs and explained the reasoning behind every procedure she plans. Her goal is providing quality dental care to her patients.
