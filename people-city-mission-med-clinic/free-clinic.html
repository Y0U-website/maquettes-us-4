A CULTURE OF CARING

NOTICE: We are open for mental health, dental, chiropractic, and podiatry. Clinic hours vary based on provider availability. Call to make an appointment at 402-817-0980.

Located just north of People’s City Mission, the Free Medical Clinic provides dental, chiropractic, and mental wellness care to uninsured residents of Lancaster County, filling gaps in the existing medical network where it is difficult to meet the needs of low-income uninsured residents as quickly as necessary.

Medical services are provided through a network of practicing and retired volunteer doctors and other medical professionals. At the Free Medical Clinic, volunteer doctors and medical professionals can focus on providing care while minimizing their administrative requirements - i.e. they can treat people without worrying about insurance coding, managing staff, paying rent, etc.

Services

The PCM Free Medical Clinic exists to offer physical, emotional, and spiritual healing to hurting people in Lancaster County, Nebraska, who lack medical insurance or the ability to pay for health care.
