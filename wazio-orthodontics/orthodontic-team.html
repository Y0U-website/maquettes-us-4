The Wazio Orthodontic Team

Our Mission

Wazio Orthodontics is committed to contributing to the overall good health of the patient, transforming their smile, and enhancing self-confidence.

About the Staff

Our team work to attain an unequaled level of professional service that exceeds our patients’ highest expectations; a level that secures Wazio Orthodontics as the leader in orthodontic care in our community. We will strive to motivate and educate our patients in a friendly, caring manner so that they understand the full benefits of orthodontics and realize the importance of a shared commitment between the patients, parents, and the orthodontic staff.

Together with Dr. Wazio, we are confident that you will be completely satisfied with the level of care you receive in our office.
