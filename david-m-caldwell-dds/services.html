Dental implants

For patients seeking a permanent solution to missing teeth, dental implants offer a durable and natural-looking option. Dr. Caldwell provides comprehensive dental implant placement and restoration services, helping patients restore both function and confidence in their smiles. Whether you’re missing a single tooth or require full arch replacement, implants provide long-term support for crowns, bridges, or dentures. During your consultation, we will evaluate bone density and discuss a treatment plan customized to your individual needs.

Gentle tooth extractions

While preserving natural teeth is always our goal, extractions may be necessary in cases of severe decay, infection, or crowding. Dr. Caldwell performs tooth extractions with precision and care, ensuring a comfortable experience and thorough follow-up instructions for healing. We also discuss tooth replacement options when needed, including bridges, partials, or implants, to maintain your bite and smile aesthetics.
