Our Philosophy

More than three decades of dental devotion have created results that improve patients’ lives. It has been fascinating to be part of the history to see dentistry changes in the last ten years and be able to bring them to the practice and allow patients to benefit from new technologies.

Personal Life

I am a native of Houston, TX, and have called Beaumont my home since 1981. My wife and I have one son, of whom we are very proud. My leisure time is family-focused, but I also enjoy swimming and studying.

Community involvement is important to me. I participate in programs such as Boys’ Haven of America, Foster Care Awareness Month, Kid Fest, Fall Fest, Muscular Dystrophy Lock-Up, Chamber of Commerce, and the Better Business Bureau. I also sponsor local high school basketball teams and local middle school volleyball.
