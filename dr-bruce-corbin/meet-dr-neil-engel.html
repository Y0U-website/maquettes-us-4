Meet Dr. Neil Engel

Dr. Neil Engel

Your Dentist In Oyster Bay-East Norwich, New York

Dr. Neil Engel is a 1979 graduate of Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, where he honed his skills and passion for providing exceptional dental care. Prior to dental school, he earned his undergraduate degree from Brooklyn College, graduating Cum Laude. Dr. Engel is deeply committed to staying at the forefront of dentistry, having completed over 900 hours of advanced training in implants, cosmetic dentistry, and periodontics.

He is an active member of the American Dental Association, New York State Dental Society, Nassau County Dental Society, and Glen Cove Study Club. Since 1981, Dr. Engel has been proudly serving the Oyster Bay-East Norwich community, practicing family dentistry with a focus on high-quality care, patient comfort, and creating a welcoming environment.

Dr. Neil Engel takes pride in helping his patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized treatment plans and a commitment to excellence. He is excited to continue this long-standing tradition of high-quality family dentistry as part of the Corbin Dental at Oyster Bay team.
