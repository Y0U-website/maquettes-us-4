The practice

Knowledge is everything to Dr. Bruce Corbin. He has passion and tireless drive to ensure that he provides the latest dental techniques. Dr. Corbin graduated from The University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 1992. He then went on to complete advanced training in full mouth reconstructive and prosthetic dentistry from New York University Dental School in 1999.

Most recently, World-renowned doctor Arun Garg personally chose Dr. Bruce Corbin to join the limited faculty of highly specialized implant doctors. We travel the country and educate fellow dentists on refining and developing skills and techniques to enrich and enhance the lives of patients.

Dr. Garg is the world’s preeminent authority on bone biology, bone harvesting, and bone grafting for implant surgery. He has created lecture and hands-on educational courses in those specialities. Dr. Garg is also the founder of Implant Seminars, the nation’s largest dental implant continuing education provider. He is the author and publisher of nine textbooks and hundreds of articles.

Dr. Bruce Corbin practices all phases of dentistry and has over 2000 hours of education and specialized training in a wide range of dental issues and technology. He has certifications in Pinhole Gum Rejuvenation, Laser-Assisted New Attachment Procedure (LANAP), and is an Invisalign Preferred Provider.

Continually recognized for his Humanitarian Medical Work as well as an active member of the American Dental Association, The New York State Dental Society, The Queens County Dental Society, and the Nassau County Dental Society, Dr. Bruce Corbin is a very well respected member of the community.

Dr. Bruce Corbin grew up in Bayside, Queens, and is deeply connected to it. He continuously strives to expand his knowledge in the field of Dentistry to ensure that he is providing his patients with the safest and most advanced dental care.

Dr. Richard S Corbin is a 1989 graduate of Columbia University’s School of Dental and Oral Surgery. After graduation from dental school, he completed a general practice residency program at St. Lukes-Roosevelt Hospital in Manhattan. Dr. Corbin performs all phases of general dentistry with an emphasis on cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Richard Corbin is certified in Laser-Assisted New Attachment Procedure (LANAP), Invisalign, Oral Conscious Sedation and Sleep Dentistry. Dr. Richard Corbin has created thousands of smile makeovers enabling individuals to feel more confident about themselves.
