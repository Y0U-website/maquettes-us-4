Meet Dr. Kevin Barajas

Dr. Kevin Barajas

Your Dentist In Bayside & Oyster Bay, New York

Dr. Kevin Barajas holds a dental degree from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine. During his postgraduate training at Mount Sinai Hospital, he discovered a passion for family and cosmetic dentistry. His second year was marked by a commitment to improving the lives of those affected by sleep-related breathing disorders, with a particular focus on dental sleep medicine.

Dr. Barajas has a background in general dentistry and emphasizes personalized treatment for patients with sleep disorders. He collaborates with sleep physicians and healthcare professionals to offer comprehensive care, which includes the use of Oral Appliance Therapy.

Patient-centric care is at the core of Dr. Barajas’s approach. He places great importance on educating patients about oral health and continually incorporates the latest advancements in dentistry into his practice. He also remains dedicated to enhancing his skills through ongoing education and digital resources.

Dr. Barajas is an active member of professional organizations such as the Academy of General Dentistry and the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine. He is in the process of achieving fellowship status with the AGD.

In his leisure time, Dr. Barajas enjoys exploring state parks in the tri-state area and participating in a local soccer league in Stamford. He also relishes hiking, running trails, and quality family time during visits to his California home.
