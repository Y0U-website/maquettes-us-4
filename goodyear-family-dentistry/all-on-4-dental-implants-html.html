How Do All-On-4 Dental Implants Work?

All-On-4 dental implants in Goodyear are exactly what you would imagine they would be an entire, customized denture attached to four dental implants. The implants are surgically placed within the most dense and supportive areas of the jawbone for maximum support. Because of this, it’s rarely if ever the case that extensive preparatory treatments like bone grafting are needed, making them an ideal solution for patients who have had traditional dentures for a while.

Am I a Candidate for All-On-4 Dental Implants?

The best candidates for All-On-4 dental implants are those who:

  • Have good general health, allowing them to heal after surgery.
  • Have good oral health, ensuring their replacement teeth last for as long as possible.
  • Have adequate jawbone volume, or at least enough to support four dental implants.
  • Aren’t a smoker or are willing to quit before and following their treatment to reduce their risk of implant failure.

Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants

All-On-4 dental implants allow you to enjoy virtually all of the benefits that you would with traditional implant dentures, including:

  • Dramatic results more quickly.
  • Enhanced chewing ability.
  • Better prosthetic stability.
  • Overall enhanced comfort.
  • Boosted confidence.
  • Improved overall quality of life.
  • Better oral health and hygiene.
  • Easy maintenance and a long lifespan.

All-On-4 vs. Traditional Dental Implants

Traditional implant dentures require the placement of anywhere from four to eight dental implants to be placed, largely depending on the volume of the jawbone. However, All-On-4 is designed to maximize the support of the area of the jawbone that is thicker, drastically minimizing the need for preliminary treatments like bone grafts. This not only allows patients to enjoy their results much faster, as it eliminates the recovery time of an entire surgery, but it also takes less of a toll on the body.
