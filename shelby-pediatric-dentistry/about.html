Our Team

Richard Baxter DMD, MS

Dr. Baxter loves working with children and is dedicated to helping them have positive dental experiences. He attended Vanderbilt University for his undergraduate studies where he met his wife, Tara.

Dr. Baxter then attended the University of Alabama at Birmingham School of Dentistry where he graduated at the top of his class. In order to receive a specialty certification in pediatric dentistry, Dr. Baxter attended the residency program at Nationwide Children’s Hospital in Columbus, OH, one of the nation’s top-ranked pediatric hospitals and busiest pediatric dentistry clinics.

Dr. Baxter is a board-certified pediatric dentist, Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, member of the Alabama Dental Association, and the American Dental Association.

He is a nationally-recognized speaker on tongue-ties, author of the bestselling book Tongue-Tied: How a Tiny String Under the Tongue Impacts Nursing, Speech, Feeding, and More, and creator of Tongue-Tied Academy.

Dr. Baxter enjoys spending time with his wife, who is a nurse practitioner, and their three girls, Hannah, Noelle, and Molly. Dr. Baxter serves as a small group leader and BH Kids Faith Trainer at The Church at Brook Hills and is on the board of Reach The Rest, a global missions organization.

Our Care

Along with providing your children a fun and friendly dental home, we also do all that we can to ensure that the dental visit is safe for their health and the environment. We have done a ton of research, consulted with experts and implemented a plan to maintain a green office and provide you and your family with pure, honest dental care.

We’ve also invested in state-of-the-art dental equipment that helps us catch problems like tooth decay and improper tooth eruption earlier so we can treat your child’s teeth in the best way possible. This technology often saves you time and money and your child from more extensive dental treatments in the future.

Safe for Your Kids and for the Environment

Low-Radiation Digital X-rays

Digital X-rays offer many benefits. Studies show that digital X-rays emit up to 90 % less radiation than traditional X-rays and do not require any lengthy chemical processing. Your child’s X-ray scans are available on our computer screen within seconds, so we can review them with you and discuss a treatment plan without having to schedule another appointment. And, if you ever need to take your child to a different specialist, we can easily email your scans with no unnecessary paper waste.

BPA-Free Dental Materials

According to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, bisphenol A (BPA) has been present in many consumer plastic products and food packaging since the 1960s. Some studies suggest that BPA has adverse health effects. We are committed to using BPA-free dental materials to ensure the safety of your child’s oral and overall health.

Mercury-Free Dental Materials

Silver (amalgam) fillings are made of mercury and can be a source of direct mercury exposure for your little ones. Instead of silver fillings, we treat your child with tooth-colored composite fillings that are durable, natural-looking, and safe.

Latex-Free Dental Practice

We take your child’s health seriously, and since many people suffer from an allergy to latex, we use only safe, synthetic substitutes.

Safe Root Canals (...only if they re absolutely needed!)

Formocresol is a toxic substance used by many dentists for baby tooth root canals (pulpotomies). We use only non-toxic materials that are proven safe for children. Whenever possible, we try to avoid a root canal to both save you money and minimize stress for your child.

Non-Toxic Cleaning Products Used Daily

We exceed the ADA and OSHA standards for a clean and safe dental practice by using non-toxic cleaning products to disinfect and sterilize our treatment rooms and dental equipment.

Advanced Technology

Digital X-rays

We use state-of-the-art digital X-ray sensors and a digital panoramic X-ray machine so your child receives the lowest dose of radiation possible. We also employ the newest handheld NOMAD X-ray unit to make getting X-rays even easier.

Intraoral Camera

With our intraoral camera, we take pictures of all your child’s teeth and any problem areas. We then put the images up on our computer screen so that we can walk you and your child through the exam. This diagnostic and teaching tool helps us explain your child’s unique oral health and treatment options. Now you can see what we see when we are examining the mouth.

LightScalpel CO2 Laser

With our state-of-the-art LightScalpel CO2 Laser, we can release tongue-ties and lip-ties with minimal discomfort and improved healing, remove pieces of gum tissue without bleeding or stitches, and even perform baby tooth root canals.

DryShield

DryShield is a unique mouthpiece that makes your child’s time in the dental chair more comfortable. This mouthpiece isolates sections of the mouth, allowing us to perform higher quality dentistry while comfortably protecting the gums, cheeks, and tongue. Now your child can relax, rest his or her mouth, and enjoy the time at the dentist while watching a new movie.

Highest Quality Dental Materials

We only use the highest quality dental materials that are designed to be long-lasting and esthetic. We want you to be happy with the results and we only want to treat a baby tooth one time.
