Maintaining Results After Your Visit

After your bridges or dentures procedure at John S. Ledakis, DDS, PA, follow these steps to care for your restoration:

With consistent care, your prosthetic solution will provide lasting support and comfort.

  • Brush and floss daily, including under pontics or around prosthetic edges
  • Use nonabrasive toothpaste to prevent wear
  • Visit twice a year for checkups and maintenance
  • Avoid extremely hard or sticky foods that could stress connectors
