Conscious Sedation

Conscious sedation in West Palm Beach is ideal for easing dental visits and ensuring comfort.

Conscious sedation is administered as a prescribed medication before treatment, offering a calm experience while keeping patients aware and responsive. Unlike general anesthesia, it allows for faster recovery and a return to normal activities sooner. It’s especially helpful for patients with strong gag reflexes, low pain tolerance, or those needing extensive dental work. At John S. Ledakis, DDS, PA, our compassionate team evaluates your health and dental needs to determine if sedation is right for you.
