Maintaining Results After Your Visit

After your orthodontic treatment at John S. Ledakis, DDS, PA of West Palm Beach, follow these steps to protect your new alignment:

With consistent care and retention, your straightened smile can last for years.

  • Wear your retainers as instructed
  • Brush and floss daily to avoid relapse
  • Attend follow-up visits to monitor progress
  • Avoid hard or sticky foods that could shift alignment
