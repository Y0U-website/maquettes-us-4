Maintaining Results After Your Visit

After your dental implant care at John S. Ledakis, DDS, PA in West Palm Beach, follow these steps to maintain implant health:

With attentive care, your implants will continue to support your smile for many years.

  • Brush gently at least twice daily around implants
  • Floss or use interdental brushes daily
  • Avoid biting hard objects and chewing ice
  • Come in for regular checkups and maintenance
