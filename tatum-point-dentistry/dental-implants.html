Dental implants are the most advanced and long-lasting solution for missing teeth. At Tatum Point Dentistry, we use implants to replace the root of the missing tooth, topped with a crown that looks and feels natural.

Implants prevent bone loss in the jaw, provide unmatched stability, and function just like real teeth. With proper care, they can last a lifetime, making them an investment in both health and confidence.

Unlike dentures or bridges, implants don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support, preserving your natural tooth structure.

Send A Message The Implant Process The dental implant process typically involves several stages. First, a titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it serves as an artificial tooth root.

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a strong, permanent foundation. Once healed, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant.

Throughout the process, our team ensures your comfort with sedation options and provides detailed guidance for each stage of healing.

Send A Message Benefits of Dental Implants Dental implants offer numerous advantages over other tooth replacement options. They restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence.

Because they’re anchored directly in the jawbone, implants provide stability that dentures and bridges can’t match. They also stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone deterioration that often follows tooth loss.

Implants are easy to care for—simply brush and floss as you would your natural teeth. No special adhesives, no removal at night, no dietary restrictions.

Send A Message Are You a Candidate? Most adults with good overall health are candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates have sufficient jawbone density to support the implant and healthy gums free of periodontal disease.

Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, bone grafting procedures can often create the foundation needed for successful implant placement.
