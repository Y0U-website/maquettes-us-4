The practice

Why Patients Choose Tatum Point Dentistry Choosing a dentist is about more than finding a convenient location, it’s about finding a team you can trust with your long-term oral health. At Tatum Point Dentistry, we combine decades of experience, modern technology, and personalized care to help patients throughout North Phoenix feel confident about their smiles and their treatment decisions.

Dr. Christine Gonzalez has spent more than 30 years helping patients improve and maintain their oral health. Her gentle approach, attention to detail, and commitment to patient comfort have helped generations of Phoenix-area families feel at ease in the dental chair.

We place a strong emphasis on restorative dentistry, helping patients rebuild healthy, functional smiles with treatments such as crowns, bridges, fillings, dentures, dental implants, and tooth replacement solutions. Our goal is to provide treatment options that improve both comfort and long-term oral health.

We understand that visiting the dentist can feel stressful for some patients. That’s why we focus on creating a welcoming environment where patients feel heard, respected, and informed. Sedation options are available for patients who may benefit from additional comfort during treatment.

Our office utilizes advanced dental technology, including digital imaging and modern diagnostic tools, to support efficient treatment planning and a more comfortable patient experience.

We proudly care for patients from North Phoenix and surrounding communities. Dr. Gonzalez and members of our team speak Spanish, helping us provide a comfortable and accessible experience for Spanish-speaking patients and families.
