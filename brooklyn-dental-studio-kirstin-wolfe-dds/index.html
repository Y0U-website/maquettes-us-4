Many TMJ problems, such as pain, clicking or headaches can be solved non-invasively or non-surgically. At Brooklyn Dental Studio we use a number of non-surgical techniques, such as night guards or Botox to relieve and reverse joint pain.

Traditionally, TMJ treatment options have included dental appliances, physical therapy, acupuncture or medications. While these methods can be effective, some patients are not candidates or these procedures are insufficient. Botox offers a minimally invasive, non-surgical approach to alleviating TMJ-related discomfort and tooth wear.

Dr. Kirstin Wolfe grew up around dentistry. She is a fourth-generation dentist and the tenth dentist in her family. Her earliest exposure to the profession was accompanying her father, Dr. Robert Wolfe, to NYU’s Dental School, where he was a professor in the Prosthodontics department. She was drawn to the profession because dentistry, and especially Prosthodontics, is a combination of art…

She has been in private practice since 2012 and founded Brooklyn Dental Studio in 2016. While she and her team provide a range of services, her specialty is veneers, implants, and cosmetic dentistry. She prides herself on treating her patients as a whole, using the best materials to achieve beautiful and functional results, unique to each person. Her patients would describe her as friendly…

Dr. Wolfe is an active member of her local, state, and national dental society. She sits on the board of trustees for the Second District Dental Society (representing Brooklyn and Staten Island) and is a delegate to the American Dental Association as well as the New York State Dental Association. She is also a member of the American College of Prosthodontics.
