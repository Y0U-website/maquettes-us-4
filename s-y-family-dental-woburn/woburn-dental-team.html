Meet Dr. Y

Danielle Lacet DMD

Dr. Danielle Lacet, DMD, is a Massachusetts-licensed dentist with dental training and experience in both Brazil and the United States. Known for her warm, attentive approach, she is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable, heard, and confident throughout their dental care.

Dr. Lacet began her dental career in Brazil, where she was trained and licensed as a dentist and completed advanced training in Periodontics. She later earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine.

She enjoys providing comprehensive, personalized dental care and values building long-lasting relationships with her patients. Her approach combines evidence-based dentistry with thoughtful communication and attention to each patient’s individual needs.

Outside the office, Dr. Lacet enjoys traveling, yoga, running, staying active, and spending quality time with family and friends.
