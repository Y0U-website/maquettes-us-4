Services

Professional Whitening

With our professional whitening we can enhance your smile by whitening teeth that have become discolored or stained.

Implant Dentistry

Dental implants are the best way to replace missing teeth. We offer many implant options including single implants, implant bridges, and implant retained dentures.

Cosmetic Dentistry

We want you to love your smile. We offer many cosmetic options including cost friendly composite fillings/veneers, porcelain veneers, crowns and bridges.

Dentures and Partials

We offer many removable denture options to replace missing teeth. From full dentures that replace an entire arch of missing teeth to partial dentures that replace multiple teeth

Periodontal Treatment and Regular Cleanings

Good home care and regular dental cleanings help prevent gum and bone disease. With our caring hygiene team we can reverse gum disease and halt periodontal disease and bone loss from progressing. It is our goal to keep your gums disease free and maintain healthy teeth that last a lifetime.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy is necessary when the root or nerve part of a tooth has an infection. We are able to save the tooth by cleaning out the infected tissue and placing a filling like material in its place, called gutta percha.

Invisalign/Clear Aligners

Invisalign clear aligners are used to straighten your teeth and give you the smile you’ve always wanted. With Invisalign you can achieve straight teeth with clear aligners and avoid the metal of traditional braces. We would love to talk about if Invisalign is right for you.

Emergency Dental Treatment

Some dental infections can lead to severe pain and swelling. Whenever possible we offer same day dental emergency treatment to get you out of pain and prevent the infection from spreading.

Pediatric Dentistry

Our goal is to make every dental visit enjoyable. We work to create a calming environment and set a foundation for a lifetime of good oral health.
