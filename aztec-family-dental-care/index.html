Dr. James Durfey graduated from Brigham Young in 1999 and earned his Doctorate from the University of Missouri Kansas City School of Dentistry in 2006.

Dr. Durfey grew up on a ranch in south-central Utah and worked in construction and as a Social Services Director for a nursing home prior to dental school. He is a proud Eagle Scout a tradition carried down from his grandfather, his father, and that he has passed on to his two sons.

Dr. Durfey enjoys mountain biking, mountaineering, ice-climbing, and horseback riding and writes poetry in his free time.
