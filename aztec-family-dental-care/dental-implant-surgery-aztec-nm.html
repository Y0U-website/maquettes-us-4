What Is Dental Implant Surgery?

Dental implant surgery is a procedure that involves replacing missing or damaged teeth with artificial ones anchored directly into the jawbone. The implant is a small titanium post that replaces the tooth’s root. Over time, the bone around the implant fuses with the titanium, creating a strong and secure foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

The surgery is typically performed in multiple stages, including implant placement and a healing period. Once the implant has integrated with the bone, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant, completing the process and restoring your smile.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implant Surgery?

Most adults with missing teeth can be good candidates for dental implant surgery, but certain factors can influence eligibility. Ideal candidates are those who:

  • Have sufficient bone density: A strong jawbone is crucial for supporting the implant. If you have experienced bone loss, additional procedures like bone grafting might be necessary before placing the implant.
  • Are in good oral health: Healthy gums are one of the biggest requirements for the success of dental implants. Candidates should be free of gum disease and maintain good oral hygiene practices.
  • Avoid smoking: Smoking can hinder the healing process and raise the risk of implant failure. Non-smokers or those committed to quitting are more likely to have successful outcomes
  • Are committed to the process: Dental implant surgery requires time and patience. To ensure the best results, following your dentist’s instructions closely and attending all follow-up appointments is important.

What Are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

  • Longevity: Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care. Unlike dentures or bridges, which may need to be replaced after several years, implants are a permanent solution.
  • Natural Appearance: Dental implants have a tooth-like appearance and feel. They are custom-made to match your surrounding teeth’ color, shape, and size, ensuring a seamless blend with your smile.
  • Improved Functionality: Implants fully restore your ability to chew so you can enjoy your favorite foods confidently. They also help preserve the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur with missing teeth.
  • Enhanced Confidence: A complete, natural-looking smile will make you feel more confident in social and professional settings. Dental implants can also improve speech, eliminating the slipping or shifting that can happen with dentures.
