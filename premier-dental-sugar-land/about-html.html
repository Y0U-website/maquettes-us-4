About Dr. Badri Ismaeel, DMD

Dr. Badri Ismaeel, DMD

A dentist who listens first, explains everything, and treats every patient like family.

Dr. Badri Ismaeel earned his Bachelor of Dental Surgery in Cairo in 2012 and practiced for four years before continuing his training in the United States, where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree in 2016. That dual perspective, international foundations refined by American clinical standards, shapes a style of care that is both thorough and warmly human.

Patients know him for unhurried appointments, plain-language explanations, and a firm belief that no one should feel talked into treatment. He is committed to continuing education across implantology, clear aligner therapy, and cosmetic dentistry, so his patients benefit from techniques that are both proven and current.

Away from the office, Dr. Ismaeel is a devoted family man who loves calling Sugar Land home.

A practice with deep roots in Sugar Land

Premier Dental has been part of the same Sugar Land community for more than 25 years, in the same welcoming corner of New Territory. Generations of families have grown up in these chairs, and several members of our team have been caring for neighbors here for over 15 years. Some patients have been greeted by the same familiar faces since their very first visit.

In recent years, Dr. Ismaeel took the helm of the practice, honoring everything that made it a neighborhood institution while thoughtfully bringing in modern tools like 3D imaging and digital scanning. The names on the door may evolve, but the promise hasn’t changed in a quarter century: honest care, gentle hands, and neighbors treating neighbors well.
