The practice

Whether you’re seeking relief from sleep apnea, TMJ pain, or chronic jaw-related tension, you’ll find a supportive team committed to helping you achieve lasting results and a better quality of life.

At Regenerative Sleep & TMJ, patients receive far more than symptom relief; they receive a fully supportive experience built around comfort, clarity, and long-term wellness. Every treatment plan begins with a thoughtful understanding of the patient’s goals, medical history, and daily challenges, ensuring care is tailored to the individual.

We look at how airway health, jaw function, and sleep quality interact to influence overall well-being.

Each therapy is designed around the patient’s unique anatomy, symptoms, and comfort goals.

Our care emphasizes sustainable improvements, not temporary fixes.

We take time to listen, educate, and guide patients through every process of their journey.

We collaborate closely with medical teams to ensure all recommendations align seamlessly with the patient’s diagnosis and treatment plan.
