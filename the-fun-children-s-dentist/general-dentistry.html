Specialists in Children’s Dentistry from Infants to 15-Years Old

At The Fun Children’s Dentist, we provide dentistry for children under the age of 3 to kids all the way through the age of 15. Dental Service Worth Smiling About

From routine cleanings to cavities, crowns, and more, The Fun Children’s Dentist provides complete pediatric care, including:

For children 3 and under, we recommend scheduling routine cleanings every 3-months. For children over the age of 3, we recommend cleanings every 6-months. Maintaining regular teeth cleanings and an exam performed by our dental staff, we can be on the lookout for any issues early-on.

Composite Dental Fillings

Sometimes cavities happen! Our practice offers composite dental fillings to quickly and seamlessly restore your child’s tooth. Composite fillings provide a more natural appearance once completed.

Teeth Extraction

From overcrowding to removal of a baby tooth that just won’t budge, The Fun Children’s Dentist offers teeth removal and extraction, when necessary. Most extractions can be performed using local anesthesia to numb the tooth and surrounding area. In cases that require it, our office can provide additional sedation/anesthesia measures.

Sedation Dentistry

Is your child afraid of the dentist? Do they have trouble sitting still during their appointment? Sedation dentistry has grown in popularity for its ability to make visits for comfortable. Our office offers Nitrous Oxide, aka Laughing Gas , Conscious Sedation, and Outpatient General Anesthesia. Click here to learn more about our Sedation Dentistry.
