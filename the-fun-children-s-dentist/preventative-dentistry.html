Building Healthy Dental Habits for Your Child

When it comes to caring for the health of your child’s smile, prevention is key. At The Fun Children’s Dentist in Corpus Christi, TX, we strive to set our patient’s smiles up for success by taking a proactive approach to their dental care. By keeping up with regularly scheduled dental cleanings, exams, combined with healthy habits at home, we help to avoid uncomfortable or costly dental issues later down the line.

Our pediatric dental practice is here to educate children and their parents about how to properly care for their teeth, gums, and mouth health at home. We offer demonstrations for proper brushing and flossing techniques that help ensure your child’s teeth are cared for in between professional cleanings at our office. As a practice that specializes working with children, we are experienced in making dental care fun for our patients. At-Home Dental Care

Your child spends most of their time at home, not at the dentist, making this a critical piece of the puzzle to caring for their smiles. Establishing healthy routines at home for brushing and flossing will keep your child’s teeth and gums healthy in between their visits to our office. This can prevent issues, such as cavities, periodontal problems, and more.

While at-home care plays a significant role in your child’s dental health, keeping up with regularly scheduled dental cleanings and exams performed by our highly trained staff will allow our team to identify issues as early as possible. This can include initial at-risk factors which may provide enough time for our dentist to deliver the necessary treatment to avoid more severe issues. Our preventative services include Teeth Cleanings, Dental Examinations, X-Rays and Diagnostics, Dental Sealants, and more.
