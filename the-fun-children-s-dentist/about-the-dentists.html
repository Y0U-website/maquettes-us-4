Meet Our Friendly Pediatric Dentists

Kare Opaneye, D.D.S, MPH

Dr. Opaneye , is certified by the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and is a part of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD). He received his DDS at the University of Lagos-Lagos , Nigeria and Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard School of Dental Medicine in Boston, MA. His pediatric dentistry residency was completed at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine Boston, MA. Dr. Opaneye received his MPH (Master of Public Health) at Emory University Rollins School of Public Health Atlanta, GA. Dr. Opaneye has cultivated a dentist office that focuses on children’s oral health.

Henry Vasquez, D.D.S

Our dentist Dr. Vasquez , is a board-certified general dentist who graduated from the University of Texas of Houston in 2015. Dr Vasquez focuses his practice solely on children. He joined The Fun Childern’s Dentist in 2016 and has been a tremendous asset in pediatric dentistry.
