What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jaw to serve as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant has fully integrated with the bone, a custom-crafted crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture is attached, completing the restoration and fully restoring the tooth. Since the restoration is anchored securely in the jaw rather than sitting on top of the gums, it’s remarkably close to your natural teeth in terms of look, feel, and function.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • Initial Consultation The first step of the process is meeting with Dr. Byars at our office. She’ll assess your oral health and jawbone density to help you decide if dental implants are a good fit for your situation.
  • Dental Implant Surgery We’re partnered with trusted local dental implant experts to ensure that you receive the high-quality tooth replacement you deserve.
  • Osseointegration/Abutment The implant post(s) will need several months to fuse with your jaw to form a permanent bond that mimics your natural tooth roots. Abutments, or connectors, can be attached once healed.
  • Delivery of Final Restorations Dental implants can restore any number of missing teeth in one or both arches. Once your posts and abutments are ready, your custom-made, fully functional restoration designed by Dr. Byars can be placed.
