Accelerated Recovery with Growth Factors

To help your mouth heal faster and feel better after certain dental procedures, Dr. Ghodsi uses a technique that taps into your body’s own healing power. It starts with a small sample of your blood. That blood is spun in a special machine, which pulls out a part rich in natural healing cells—this part is called PRF, short for platelet-rich fibrin.

PRF is like a healing booster. When placed where bone has been added to support dental implants, it helps the area heal more quickly, keeps swelling down, and strengthens the jaw.

PRF isn’t just for implants, though. Dr. Ghodsi can also use it after a tooth is pulled. For patients who take blood thinners, a quick injection of PRF into the gums can help stop bleeding safely and naturally.

Same-Day Dental Implants

One-Day Dental Implant Solutions

Same-day dental implants offer a fast, long-lasting solution for missing teeth. Whether you need a single implant or several, this procedure allows you to regain a beautiful, fully functional smile in a single visit, saving you time and hassle.

Traditional implant treatments often require multiple appointments spread over several months. This includes placing a titanium post into the jawbone, followed by a healing period to allow the post to fuse with the bone. While effective, this process can take up to six months, causing discomfort and taking precious time out of your schedule.

At Lasting Smiles , we streamline the process, giving you a permanent, fully functional tooth replacement in just one day. We use state-of-the-art technology like 3D CT scans and digital planning software to ensure your implants are placed accurately for a natural, comfortable fit. Every implant is custom-designed for the best possible outcome, improving both the aesthetics and health of your smile.

Benefits of Same-Day Implants

Same-day dental implants come with many advantages:

  • Comparable longevity to traditional implants, but in a fraction of the time
  • Protection of gum and bone tissue from infection and damage
  • Shorter recovery times
  • Last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance

Am I a Candidate for Same-Day Implants?

Dental implants replace missing tooth roots through osseointegration, where the implant fuses with the jawbone to create a secure foundation for a replacement tooth. For same-day implants, you’ll need a healthy and sufficient amount of jawbone to support the implant.

During your consultation, we’ll assess the health of your jawbone to determine if you’re a good candidate for same-day implants. We can discuss options like bone grafting to prepare your jaw for the implants if necessary.

If you’re looking for a quick, permanent solution to restore your smile, same-day dental implants could be the answer. Contact us today to schedule your consultation and see how we can help you get the smile you’ve always wanted in just one visit.
