Meet Your West Covina Dentist

Specializing in gENERAL, Dental Implants & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Sharam Ghodsi, DDS

FOUNDER AND LEAD DENTIST

Dr Sharam Ghodsi has over 36 years of experience. He is highly trained and experienced in cosmetic, implant and sedation dentistry. He has completed more than 10,000 successful implant cases making him a leader in implant dentistry.

Dr. Ghodsi has studied with other leaders in implant dentistry, including Dr. Paulo Malo, the inventor of the original All-on-4 full-mouth implant procedure. He has put in state-of-the-technology, procedures and protocols in each of the Lasting Smiles practices. His experienced team has been trained in these cutting-edge protocols, ensuring all our patients receive uniformly excellent treatment.

Dr. Ghodsi is actively involved in promoting quality dental care in dental study clubs and local organizations. He also completes numerous continuing education courses with an emphasis on cosmetic, implant and reconstructive dentistry.

  • Doctor of Dental Surgery from USC in 1988
  • Former Faculty of USC Dental School, Restorative Department
  • Member of American Dental Association
  • Member of American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry
  • Member of International Congress of Dental Implantology

  • Member of Academy of Laser Dentistry
  • LVI Graduate

Dr. Justin Nguyen

ORAL & MAXILLOFACIAL SURGEON

Dr. Justin earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Harvard University, where he served as class president and held national and international leadership roles. His work included an internship at the WHO/PAHO headquarters in Washington, D.C., and serving as a site director for Oral Health Across Nations.

He later earned his medical degree and oral and maxillofacial surgery certificate at Case Western Reserve University. His hospital training included the Cleveland Clinic, University Hospitals Cleveland Medical Center, and MetroHealth Medical Center, including experience at Level I trauma centers. He also completed a general surgery internship and rotations in head and neck oncology, plastic and reconstructive surgery, trauma surgery, and pediatric surgery.

Dr. Justin provides full-scope oral and maxillofacial surgery, including infected and wisdom tooth removal, dental implants, bone grafting, sinus lifts, and full-mouth dental rehabilitation.

Outside of dentistry, he enjoys traveling, hiking, watching movies, and spending time with family and friends in Placentia.
