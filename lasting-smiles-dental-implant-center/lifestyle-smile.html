Fast Healing with ATP38 Light Therapy

We use ATP38 Light Therapy , a non-invasive treatment that uses specific wavelengths of light to stimulate your cells and increase natural energy (ATP) production. This helps your body repair tissue more efficiently so you heal faster and more comfortably after procedures.

After dental treatments, your body needs time and energy to heal. ATP38 helps speed up that process naturally, reducing downtime and improving your overall recovery experience.
