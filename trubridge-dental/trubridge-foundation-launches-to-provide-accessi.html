TruBridge Foundation Launches to…

Dr. Helena Soomer-Lincoln, the innovator behind TruBridge™, has always been driven by a desire to make quality dental care available to everyone. That vision has now taken a significant step forward with the creation of the TruBridge Foundation, a non-profit organization designed to remove financial obstacles to TruBridge™ restorations.

The Foundation’s mission is to transform lives by providing access to permanent, confident smiles. Discover the inspiring story behind the TruBridge Foundation and how it’s making a difference in the lives of deserving individuals.

Read the full article in the Bainbridge Island Review to learn more and find out how you can support this vital cause.
