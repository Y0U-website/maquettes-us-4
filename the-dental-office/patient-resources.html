We accept most dental insurances, including participation in the Delta Dental PPO, Delta Dental Premier, Medicare Advantage, Unum/Colonial Life, United Health Care (UHC), and Principal.

Maximizing your benefits potential is our top priority, and we will file all dental insurance claims for you.

Financing Options

Cherry

Cherry offers convenient, flexible payment plans that allow you to break down the cost of your treatment into manageable monthly payments. With a quick application process and instant approval, you can get the care you need without delay.

CareCredit

CareCredit is a healthcare credit card designed to help you pay for the treatment you need, when you need it. With flexible financing options and a simple application process, CareCredit makes it easy to manage the cost of your care over time, so you can focus on your health instead of your budget.
