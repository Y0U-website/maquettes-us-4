About The Dental Office, LLC

Committed to Quality Care You Can Trust

Welcome to The Dental Office, LLC!

At The Dental Office, our mission is simple: to provide exceptional dental care that is both accessible and affordable . With locations in Lancaster and Prairie du Chien , we proudly serve individuals and families throughout the surrounding communities with a consistent standard of clinical excellence and personalized attention.

We believe patients should never have to choose between quality and cost . That’s why we focus on delivering advanced, high-level dentistry in a way that remains practical and approachable for every patient we serve.

Dr. Sean Engel

We are excited to welcome Dr. Sean Engel, DDS, as the newest member of The Dental Office team. Dr. Engel was born and raised in Southeast Minnesota. After graduating from Stewartville High School in 2013, he attended Wartburg College in Waverly Iowa until 2017 when he graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree while studying Biology and Religion. Dr. Engel then studied at the renowned University of Iowa College of Dentistry, graduating in 2021 with his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS).

Dr. Engel currently lives in Cassville Wisconsin, where his wife, Payton Engel (Brinkman) was born and raised. When he isn’t in the clinic, Dr. Engel enjoys to spend his time socializing with friends and family, hunting, or fishing. We are excited to bring Dr. Engel’s experience and knowledge to the Lancaster Community.

Accessible, Affordable Care

We are committed to making dentistry accessible for individuals and families in our communities. By combining efficient workflows, modern technology, and a patient-first philosophy, we’re able to provide high-quality care while keeping treatment affordable.

Our team is always available to discuss your options and help you plan care in a way that fits your needs and budget.

Patient-Centered. Honest. Results-Driven.

Our approach to dentistry is grounded in three core principles: personalized care, transparency, and long-term results. Every patient is unique, and we take the time to understand your needs, clearly explain your options, and develop treatment plans tailored to your long-term oral health. We are committed to honest communication and straightforward guidance, ensuring you feel informed and confident in every decision. Beyond addressing immediate concerns, our focus is on supporting your oral health for years to come through thoughtful, preventive-focused care.
