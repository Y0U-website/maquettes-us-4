About

Dr. Osama Odeh

The founder of Spotsy Dental Care – Kids and Family.

Dr. Osama Odeh is the founder of Spotsy Dental Care – Kids and Family. He obtained his dental degree from Jordan University of Science and Technology (JUST) in Jordan. He then completed 2 years of Post-Graduate training in General Practice Residency at the University of Texas-Houston.

He has been practicing dentistry for over 20 years and has been practicing in the Fredericksburg area since 2013. Dr. Odeh enjoys treating children and adults and has great experience in caring for both. He believes in providing honest, high quality and comfortable care to all patients. Dr. Odeh is committed to life-long learning through continuing dental education.

Dr. Odeh lives in Stafford, VA with his wife and two children.
