Implant Crowns vs. Bridges: Which Is Right for You?

Implant crowns are ideal when you’re missing a single tooth, anchored by one implant and one crown. In contrast, implant bridges restore several missing teeth in a row and are typically supported by two or more implants. During your consultation, we’ll walk through your options and help you decide whether a single tooth implant or a dental implant bridge best suits your smile goals.

How Are Implant Restorations Different Than Dentures?

There are key differences between implant restorations and traditional dentures:

For many patients, implant-supported tooth replacements often feel more like real teeth and are easier to maintain.

  • Implants are secured to the jawbone, providing a stable, natural fit
  • Dentures sit on the gums and may require adhesives
  • Dental implants help prevent bone loss; dentures do not
  • Implants typically offer greater comfort, chewing ability, and longevity
