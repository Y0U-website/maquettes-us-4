Dr. James Pacheco DDS

Passionate about alleviating dental anxiety , Dr. Pacheco has become a pioneer in sedation dentistry, helping countless individuals overcome their fears and receive the care they need in a comfortable, stress-free environment.

What inspired you to pursue a career in dentistry?

From an early age, Dr. Pacheco was drawn to professions that help others and create positive change . Dentistry, with its unique blend of artistry and science , provided the perfect opportunity to restore not just smiles, but also confidence. What continues to inspire him is the ability to build lasting relationships with his patients and community , ensuring they feel valued, heard, and comfortable at every step of their dental journey.

How do you prioritize patient comfort and care?

Dr. Pacheco understands that dental visits can be daunting, which is why he focuses on creating a welcoming and reassuring environment . He prioritizes open communication , thoroughly explaining procedures, and offering personalized sedation options to help patients feel at ease. His empathetic approach ensures that every patient receives not just clinical treatment, but also emotional and mental support throughout their care.

What is your approach to building relationships with patients?

Beyond the dental chair, Dr. Pacheco enjoys getting to know his patients on a personal level. He takes the time to chat with families, share stories, and create meaningful connections —whether it’s discussing hobbies, travel experiences, or life milestones. By fostering genuine relationships, he ensures that every visit feels more like catching up with a trusted friend than a routine appointment.

How do you stay updated with advancements in dental technology?

Dr. Pacheco is committed to providing the most advanced care available by staying at the forefront of dental innovations. His practice utilizes CBCT imaging for precise diagnostics, intraoral scanners for digital impressions, and GentleWave technology for more effective, minimally invasive root canal procedures. By continuously integrating cutting-edge techniques, he enhances both efficiency and patient comfort .

Outside of the office, Dr. Pacheco is an avid outdoorsman and athlete . He enjoys skiing with his kids, snowboarding with his wife, hiking, and mountain biking . He also coaches his children’s sports teams and is currently training for an Ironman triathlon .

Travel is a big part of his life , and before starting a family, he and his wife spent summers backpacking across the world , immersing themselves in diverse cultures. One of their most cherished experiences was traveling through Southeast Asia , where they explored Thailand’s beaches, jungles, and street food markets , even spending time caring for elephants in remote mountain villages. It’s an adventure they hope to one day share with their children .

His team often jokes that there’s nothing he hasn’t tried —and they’re not wrong. From his early days working in various industries to finding his true passion in dentistry, Dr. Pacheco thrives on learning, connecting with people, and pushing his limits . He is naturally outgoing and deeply values the genuine relationships he builds with his patients. When you sit in his chair, you’re not just receiving dental care—you’re gaining a lifelong dental partner who truly cares .

A respected member of top dental organizations, Dr. James is committed to ongoing education in diverse dental areas and represents Sonendo’s Gentlewave technology, bringing innovative and comfortable solutions to root canal therapies. Professional Background Personal Philosophy & Approach Personal Interests Innovation and Continuing Learning

Dr. Pacheco earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry , where he demonstrated leadership as class president and played a key role in advancing dental education initiatives. His professional journey began as a dental assistant , where he gained hands-on experience in pediatrics, orthodontics, and general dentistry —a foundation that fueled his passion for patient care and innovation.

Dr. Pacheco holds IV Sedation Certification and has advanced training in Endodontics, Orthodontics, Surgery, and Implant Dentistry . As an educator for Sonendo , he specializes in GentleWave technology , ensuring minimally invasive, highly effective root canal treatments.
