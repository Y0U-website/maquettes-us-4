Dental Implants in Castle Rock…

Unlike dentures or bridges, dental implants are fixed in place and do not rely on neighboring teeth for support, making them a more stable and durable option.

Benefits of Implant Restorations Dental implants offer numerous benefits, including:

  • Abutment : A connector is placed on the implant post to hold and support the crown.
  • Crown : The visible part of the tooth is made to match your natural teeth in color and shape.
  • Enhanced Appearance : They look and feel like natural teeth, improving your smile and boosting your confidence.
  • Improved Oral Function : Dental implants allow you to chew and speak more effectively than traditional dentures.
  • Long-term Durability : With proper care, dental implants can last many years, providing a reliable solution for tooth replacement.
  • Jawbone Preservation : Implants help maintain jawbone density and prevent bone loss that occurs with missing teeth.
  • Prevent Adjacent Teeth Shifting : They keep adjacent teeth in place, maintaining the alignment of your natural teeth.
