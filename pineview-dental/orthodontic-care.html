  • Underbite : When the lower front teeth protrude past the upper front teeth.
  • Crossbite : A situation where some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth rather than outside.
  • Open Bite : Occurs when there is a gap between the upper and lower teeth when the mouth is closed.
  • Crowded Teeth : Insufficient space in the mouth causes teeth to overlap and twist.
  • Spacing Issues : Gaps or spaces between teeth due to missing teeth or other factors.
  • Jaw Misalignment : Problems with the alignment of the jaws can lead to functional issues and discomfort.

Why is Proper Teeth Alignment Important?

  • Health Benefits : Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of plaque buildup, tooth decay, and gum disease. Additionally, well-aligned teeth help in preventing abnormal wear of tooth surfaces and minimize the risk of developing jaw disorders.
  • Functional Benefits : Straight teeth improve the ability to chew food properly, enhancing digestion and nutrient absorption. Proper alignment also aids in clearer speech and reduces the strain on jaw muscles and joints, preventing issues such as temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders.
  • Aesthetic Benefits : A well-aligned smile is aesthetically pleasing, boosting confidence and self-esteem. Proper teeth alignment enhances facial symmetry and contributes to an attractive appearance. A beautiful smile can positively impact social interactions and overall quality of life.

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Invisalign clear aligners offer a discreet and comfortable way to straighten teeth without the need for traditional metal braces. These custom-made aligners are virtually invisible and can be easily removed for eating, brushing, and flossing. Invisalign aligners are designed to gradually shift teeth into their desired positions, providing an effective solution for mild to moderate orthodontic issues while allowing patients to maintain their normal lifestyle.

Orthodontic Retention

Orthodontic retention is a critical phase following active orthodontic treatment to ensure that teeth remain in their new positions. Pineview Dental provides custom-made retainers designed to fit perfectly and comfortably. These retainers help prevent teeth from shifting back to their original positions, ensuring long-lasting results. Regular use of retainers is essential to maintain the benefits achieved through orthodontic treatment, preserving the health, function, and aesthetics of the smile.

What is The Right Age for Orthodontic Care?

The ideal age for an initial orthodontic evaluation is around 7 years old. Early assessment allows for timely detection of potential issues and the opportunity to guide the growth of the jaw and teeth. However, orthodontic care isn’t limited to children and teenagers; many adults also seek treatment to improve their oral health and aesthetics. Advances in orthodontic technology make it easier for adults to achieve their desired smile discreetly and effectively. At Pineview Dental, we offer tailored orthodontic solutions for patients of all ages, ensuring optimal results and long-term benefits.
