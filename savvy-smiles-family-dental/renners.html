Renners

0 Savana Renners, DDS Dr. Savana Renners, formerly Driller, grew up in Cincinnati, OH, and brings a unique blend of expertise and personality to Savvy Smiles. A proud graduate of Miami University, she earned both her BS and Master’s in Accountancy in 2018 and became a Certified Public Accountant before pursuing her true passion—dentistry. Dr. Renners attended The Ohio State University College of Dentistry, where she graduated in the top 20 % of her class in May 2022 with honors. She was recognized as “Best of the Best in Prosthodontics” and received the prestigious Student Leadership Award.

While her maiden name might have given patients a chuckle, Dr. Renners’ dedication to comfort and care has always been no joke. Patients can count on her for a positive, personalized dental experience.

Outside the office, Dr. Renners enjoys spending time with her husband, Tyler, her family, and their dog, Ted. She loves exploring new recipes, getting lost in a good book, and discovering new destinations. With her approachable nature and strong commitment to patient well-being, Dr. Renners is a trusted and cherished member of the Savvy Smiles team.
