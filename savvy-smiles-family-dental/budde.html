Our Doctors

0 Savannah Budde, DDS A proud graduate of New Richmond High School, Dr. Savannah Budde was born and raised in Cincinnati, OH. She earned her degree in Biology from the University of Cincinnati before pursuing her passion for dentistry at The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. Dr. Budde graduated in 2021 with honors and a deep commitment to providing exceptional care for her patients. She is an active member of the Ohio Dental Association, the Cincinnati Dental Association, and the Cincinnati Dental Society.

Dr. Budde combines meticulous skill with genuine compassion, ensuring every patient feels comfortable and cared for. Her cheerful disposition and ability to connect with patients of all ages make every visit an enjoyable experience. With her warm approach, Dr. Budde takes pride in leaving her patients not just with healthy smiles but with a sense of confidence and trust in their care.

Outside of work, Dr. Budde is a devoted wife and mom who loves spending time with her husband Nate and their three children, Will, Walt, and Winnie. Whether she’s playing outside with her kids or simply keeping up with the joyful chaos of family life, she brings the same energy and passion to her home as she does to her practice.
