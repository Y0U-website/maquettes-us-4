How Do I Maintain Teeth Implants?

While implants require a little more care and maintenance than something like dentures, they’re still relatively low-maintenance. All you need to do is pay close attention to your tooth and gum health, as usual. Some special considerations for your implants include:

The healthier your gums, the higher the likelihood of success for your implants. A lot of behaviors go into creating a healthy environment for your teeth, so we’d be happy to give you more information on risky choices and positive habits.

  • Using implant-safe toothbrushes and toothpaste
  • Flossing around the tooth close to the gums to prevent peri-implantitis
  • Regular maintenance for your remaining natural teeth, including twice-daily brushing and once-daily flossing
  • Routine visits to your dentist to assess the health of your implants
  • Avoiding cigarettes and vapes
