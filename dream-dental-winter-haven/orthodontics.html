How Long Does Invisalign Take?

How long your treatment takes will depend on the severity of your spacing and alignment issues and your malocclusion. In the most ideal circumstances, you could be in your new smile within six months!

While your teeth may be done moving in as few as six months, you will have to continue wearing retainers. The amount of time that you spend in your retainers will slowly decrease while your teeth are retrained to stay in position. For some people, this can be a bummer. Let us assure you, however, that wearing your retainers is the best way to keep your teeth straight for a lifetime.
