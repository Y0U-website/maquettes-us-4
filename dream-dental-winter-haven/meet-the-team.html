Meet Katiuska

Hello, my name is Katiuska Suarez, and I am Registered Dental Hygienist in the state of Florida. I am passionate about helping patients achieve and maintain excellent oral health while making every visit as comfortable and stress free as possible. I believe that patient education is one of the most important parts of dental care, and I enjoy taking the time to explain oral health in a way that is easy to understand and personalized to each patient’s needs.

My goal is to create a welcoming, calm environment where every patient feels heard, respected, and confident in their care. I am committed to continuing my education and staying up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry so I can provide the highest quality care!

Originally from Venezuela, I earned my dental degree there and practiced as a dentist for five years before continuing my professional journey in the United States.

When I’m not in the office, I enjoy spending quality time with my family and friends, whether we’re relaxing at home or exploring new places together.
