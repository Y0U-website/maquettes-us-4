Get To Know Dr. Sunghyeok Hong

Dr. Sunghyeok Hong brings a wealth of experience and a passion for transformative dentistry to Crown Dental. With a philosophy centered on prevention and early intervention, Dr. Hong is dedicated to helping patients achieve and maintain optimal oral health. His approach is simple yet powerful: address issues early, but always be prepared to offer comprehensive solutions when needed.

Dr. Hong&#x27;s journey in dentistry is marked by a continuous pursuit of knowledge and skills. He is an active member of the American Academy of Clear Aligners (AACA), showcasing his expertise in orthodontic treatments like Invisalign. Additionally, his membership with the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID) underscores his advanced skills in implant dentistry.

Dr. Hong&#x27;s commitment to his patients extends beyond clinical excellence. He understands that visiting the dentist can be anxiety-inducing for many, which is why he prioritizes creating a comfortable, stress-free environment. His gentle approach, combined with a willingness to listen and explain procedures thoroughly, helps put even the most nervous patients at ease.

When not at the office, Dr. Hong enjoys staying active in the Elkridge community and continuously exploring ways to improve patient care through advanced dental technologies and techniques. He is passionate about education, both for himself and his patients, believing that informed patients are empowered to make the best decisions for their oral health.

Your First Visit Experience

Personalized Care From Day One

Your first visit to Crown Dental is designed to be comprehensive, comfortable, and informative. Upon arrival, you&#x27;ll be warmly greeted by our friendly staff. Dr. Hong will conduct a thorough examination, including digital X-rays and intraoral scans if necessary. We&#x27;ll discuss your dental history, concerns, and goals for your smile. Together, we&#x27;ll create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs. Our goal is to ensure you leave feeling informed, cared for, and confident in your dental health journey. ‍ ‍

What to Bring

To make your first visit as smooth as possible, there are a few items you can bring. Please bring your ID, insurance information (if applicable), and a list of current medications. If you have recent dental X-rays or records from a previous dentist, these can be helpful too.
