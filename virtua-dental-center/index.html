The practice

Virtua Dental Center in Camden, NJ, also known as the Noble-Evans-Wisniewski Dental Center, is a fully-accredited general dentistry practice specializing in affordable dental care for adults and children ages 3 years and older.

Our expert team includes a staff of attending general dentists, post-graduate resident dentists, dental specialists, and dental assistants.

A Personal Health Navigator can help you find a doctor, schedule appointments or classes, and help you find a service or location.
