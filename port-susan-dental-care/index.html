Dr. Joe Eidsness’s journey into dentistry, and to his place at Port Susan Dental Care, is a unique one. After finding his calling by working with his hands while building houses during summers, he realized he could combine this and his desire to make a difference in the medical field with dentistry—creating the perfect fit.

That’s not all that makes his story special. Dr. Joe was also a patient at Port Susan Dental Care! So, when Dr. Smith offered him the opportunity to take over the practice, it felt like the stars had aligned. Today, Dr. Joe is honored to continue the compassionate care you’ve always known, alongside the familiar faces on our dedicated team.

From wisdom tooth extractions to preparing for dental implants, Dr. Joe can provide the specialized surgical expertise these procedures require.
