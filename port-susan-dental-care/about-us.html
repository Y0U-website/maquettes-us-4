About Our Legacy

Port Susan Dental Care started its legacy over a century ago as the very first dental practice to serve our town’s smiles. For generations after, we’ve been a place of comfort and care. Today, this dental legacy continues under the leadership of Dr. Eidsness.

Back To The Beginning

In 1927, a young Dr. Ezra G. Wheeler, having just graduated from Portland Dental School, saw that his community was in need of dedicated dental care. This led him to establish the town’s first formal dental clinic, which was located in the historic First National Bank building on the corner of Main and Augusta.

But Dr. Wheeler was more than just a dentist; he was a pillar of the community, even serving as President of the Washington Dental Association! His series of decisions laid a foundation of trust and community for the practice we know today.

Passing The Torch

In 1965, Dr. Wheeler passed the torch to Dr. Robert C. Peterson. As a Minnesota native and a lieutenant in the U.S. Navy, he brought a steady hand and a commitment to his care, which he continued for 34 years!

Into the 21st century

As Stanwood prepared to enter a new millennium, the practice also entered its next chapter. Dr. Russel J. Smith took the helm in 1989 and, with an eye toward the future, built our current facility in 1997.

Dr. Smith became a stalwart of the community, blending time-honored patient care with new, innovative techniques. For over three decades, he upheld this legacy, continuing service to the smiles of local families until his retirement in early 2026.

Coming Full Circle

Today, our practice is led by Dr. Joseph Eidsness, the fourth owner in our nearly 100-year history. In a turn of events that speaks to our deep community roots, Dr. Smith has known Dr. Joe since he was a boy in grade school! Dr. Eidsness grew up in this community and, after earning his DDS from the prestigious Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, he returned to the town and the practice that shaped him.

More About Dr. Eidsness

Dr. Joseph Eidsness—known to most as Dr. Joe—is proud to serve the very community where he grew up… and even prouder to now lead the practice where he was once a patient himself! After earning his dental degree from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry, Dr. Joe returned home to carry on a tradition of compassionate, high-quality care at Port Susan Dental Care.

With advanced training and a commitment to staying at the forefront of dentistry, Dr. Joe is especially excited to offer in-house dental implants, providing patients with long-lasting, comfortable solutions—without the need to go elsewhere.

Friendly, down-to-earth, and always focused on what’s best for his patients, Dr. Joe is here to help you feel confident in your smile and cared for at every visit.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Joe enjoys spending time with his wife and two boys, getting outdoors to hike, ski, and run. He’s also an avid dog lover and loves adventuring with his two dogs by his side.
