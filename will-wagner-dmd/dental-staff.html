Dental Staff

All professionals at Will Wagner DMD maintain the highest levels of accreditation and pursue ongoing education to stay abreast of the latest trends in the Dental field. Read on to learn more about our staff’s experience and training.

When she is not caring for her patients, she enjoys spending time with her grandchildren, exploring the outdoors—especially the mountains—and visiting antique shops.

Mackenzie enjoys watching her son’s baseball games, shopping, and watching Netflix.
