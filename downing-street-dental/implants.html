Single Implants With Long-Term Strength

Not all patients require a full-arch restoration. For those missing one or a few teeth, single dental implants provide a targeted and dependable solution. A single implant is composed of a titanium post, an abutment, and a custom crown. It stands independently without affecting surrounding teeth.

Choosing this route offers durability and functionality for years to come. Downing Street Dental takes pride in designing implant crowns that match surrounding teeth in shape and color. For patients in the early stages of tooth loss, this is often the first step toward considering broader solutions like four on four dental implants in Denver, CO down the road if future needs arise.

Precision Matters: Why We Use Digital Scanning

Implant success depends heavily on accuracy. That’s why Downing Street Dental uses digital CT scans to map each patient’s jaw structure before any implant placement begins. These scans allow us to plan exact implant positions, measure bone density, and minimize the risk of complications.

For procedures such as four on four dental implants in Denver, CO, precision is especially important. We use the scan data to create a detailed surgical guide that informs every step of the placement process. Patients benefit from a smoother surgery, quicker recovery, and better long-term stability. It’s one more way we work to provide reliable outcomes.

What to Expect From Your Implant Timeline

Implant procedures take time, and that’s a good thing. Healing properly is essential to long-term success. At Downing Street Dental, the average timeline from consultation to final restoration is about 6 to 9 months. This gives the jawbone time to bond with the implants, a process called osseointegration.

For patients receiving four on four dental implants in Denver, CO, we provide a detailed treatment plan that outlines each step from initial imaging and surgery through to the final restoration. Temporary teeth may be provided during healing so you never go without a smile. Our goal is to keep you informed, involved, and comfortable throughout the process.

Protect Bone Health While Restoring Your Smile

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area begins to deteriorate. Over time, this can lead to noticeable facial changes and make future dental treatments more difficult. Implants help prevent this process by stimulating the bone just like a natural tooth root would. This is especially important for patients receiving full-arch solutions.

Those who choose four on four dental implants in Denver, CO often notice that their facial structure appears more natural and supported compared to when they wore traditional dentures . Implants keep the bone healthy and help preserve the shape of your face while giving you a strong foundation for chewing and speaking.
