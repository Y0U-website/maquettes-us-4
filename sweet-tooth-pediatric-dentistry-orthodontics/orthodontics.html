Orthodontic Services We Offer

Braces are a reliable and effective way to correct alignment and bite issues. Modern designs are more comfortable and efficient than ever. They help create a healthy, confident smile that lasts a lifetime.

Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten teeth. It’s a discreet and convenient option for teens and adults. Treatment is customized for comfort, flexibility, and great results.

Retainers help maintain your child’s smile after orthodontic treatment is complete. They keep teeth in their new position and prevent shifting over time. Consistent wear helps protect long-term results.

Palatal expanders gently widen the upper jaw to create space for proper tooth alignment. They are often used during growth to guide healthy development. Early use can reduce the need for more complex treatment later.

An orthodontic consultation helps determine if and when treatment is needed. We evaluate your child’s teeth, bite, and development. You’ll leave with clear recommendations and next steps.

Orthodontic emergencies can include broken brackets, poking wires, or discomfort. Our team is here to provide quick guidance and care when needed. We’ll help keep treatment on track and your child comfortable.

Early orthodontic care helps guide proper growth and development. We monitor changes and address concerns at the right time. This proactive approach can prevent more complex issues later.

The teen years are an ideal time for orthodontic treatment. We offer options that fit busy schedules and growing confidence. Our goal is a healthy, great-looking smile that lasts.

It’s never too late to improve your smile. We offer comfortable, modern options designed for adult lifestyles. Treatment is customized to help you achieve confident, lasting results.

Comprehensive Orthodontic Care

At Sweet Tooth Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics, we provide personalized orthodontic care for children, teens, and even adults guiding healthy smiles at every stage of development.

For younger children, early dental visits help create a strong foundation for lifelong oral health. As your child grows, regular checkups allow us to monitor development and identify potential orthodontic concerns early often before they become more complex.

The teen years are typically the ideal time for orthodontic treatment, as most permanent teeth have come in and the jaw is still developing. Addressing alignment and bite issues during this stage can lead to more efficient treatment, improved function, and a boost in confidence during an important time in your child’s life.

For older patients, today’s orthodontic options are more comfortable and discreet than ever. Whether using modern braces or other customized solutions, we tailor treatment to fit each patient’s goals and lifestyle.
