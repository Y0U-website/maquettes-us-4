Dr. Leila Khamsei

A Lifelong Passion for Dentistry:

Dr. Khamsei’s fascination with dentistry began in her teenage years. Curious about the intricacies of the mouth, she always wanted to know what her dentist was doing during her appointments. A high school co-op at an oral surgeon’s office solidified her passion, leading her to pursue a career in dentistry.

I believe in building strong relationships with my patients based on trust and open communication. My goal is to provide personalized care that helps each patient achieve a healthy, confident smile. Dr. Khamsei

Advanced Training & Continuing Education:

Committed to providing the best possible care, Dr. Leila pursues continuing education to stay at the forefront of dental advancements. She is certified in Botox and fillers by the AAFE and has advanced training in implant dentistry.

Beyond the Dental Chair:

When she’s not brightening smiles, you’ll likely find Dr. Leila exploring the beautiful trails around San Juan Capistrano with her dog, immersed in a captivating fiction novel, or trying out new restaurants (she’s a self-proclaimed foodie!). She also loves to travel and express herself through dance.
