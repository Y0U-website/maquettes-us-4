Scaling and Root Planing FAQs

If you have any questions about scaling and root planing, we’ve got answers. View all FAQs .

Is scaling and root planing painful?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort thanks to local anesthesia and our advanced laser technology. Any mild sensitivity after treatment typically resolves within a few days.

How much does scaling and root planing cost?

The cost varies depending on the extent of treatment needed, but most dental insurance plans cover a significant portion of this essential procedure. We also offer CareCredit financing to make treatment affordable with convenient monthly payment options.

Does insurance cover scaling and root planing?

Most dental insurance plans cover scaling and root planing as a necessary periodontal treatment, typically at 50-80 % after your deductible. Our team will verify your specific coverage and benefits before treatment.

How long does the procedure take?

Depending on the severity of gum disease, treatment may be completed in one visit or divided into multiple appointments, typically taking 45 minutes to an hour per quadrant of your mouth.

What can I expect after the procedure?

You may experience mild tenderness and sensitivity for a few days after treatment, but our laser technology significantly reduces recovery time. Most patients return to normal activities immediately with proper aftercare instructions.
