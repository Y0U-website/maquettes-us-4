Advanced Tools, Amazing Outcomes

Digital CBCT Imaging

iTero Digital Scanner

Say goodbye to uncomfortable traditional impressions! Our iTero scanner creates highly accurate digital models of your teeth in minutes without the mess and discomfort of conventional impression materials. Perfect for crowns, bridges, clear aligners, and more.

Digital X-Rays

Our digital X-ray system produces high-quality images instantly while using less radiation than traditional film x-rays. These images can be enhanced and enlarged for better diagnosis, allowing us to catch potential issues earlier.

Intraoral Camera

We use an intraoral camera to capture detailed images of your teeth and gums, allowing us to show you exactly what we see during your examination. This technology helps us better explain your oral health condition and treatment options, making it easier for you to understand and participate in decisions about your dental care.

Diode Laser

We use diode laser technology to provide gentler, more precise dental care—perfect for soft tissue treatments like gum contouring or reducing inflammation. This minimally invasive tool promotes faster healing and greater comfort during and after your visit.

Creating Smiles Throughout Our Community

Daniel Dental Studio is proud to partner with local community health centers to provide dental care to under and uninsured patients through a sliding fee scale. We believe that quality dental care should be accessible to everyone in our community, regardless of financial circumstances. This partnership reflects Dr. Daniel&#x27;s deep commitment to oral health equity and her belief that everyone deserves a healthy smile they can be proud of.
