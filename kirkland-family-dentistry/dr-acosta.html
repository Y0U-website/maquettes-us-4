Meet Dr. Olivia Acosta

Dr. Olivia Acosta studied biology at Western Washington University before receiving her Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Washington. Her highest priority is to have relationships with her patients that are built on trust, communication, and comfort. As a member of the American Dental Association and the Washington State Dental Association, Dr. Acosta enjoys participating in continuing education courses and stays up to date with current research, as she strives to offer the most evidence-based, progressive treatment options available.

Dr. Acosta grew up in South Dakota, but discovered the beautiful Pacific Northwest after her family’s relocation to Western Washington, and has never looked back! Outside of dentistry, she loves to play piano, travel, and experiment in the kitchen.
