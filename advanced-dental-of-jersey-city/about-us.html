Explore the Benefits of Visiting a Proven Family Dentist

Family dentistry focuses on addressing oral health at every stage of life, with a particular emphasis on children and teenagers. When you choose a family dentist, you’re choosing a practice that can addresses the needs of every member of your family. There are many benefits to working with this type of dentist:

Our family dentist serves patients from throughout the Jersey City, NJ area. Call today to schedule an appointment!

  • Eliminate Anxiety: Dental anxiety and phobia is a serious issue that anyone can experience. It can cause you to delay or avoid care or treatment, which can lead to serious issues down the line. Visiting one inviting dentist’s office helps children overcome this anxiety, leading to better oral health throughout their lives.
  • Convenience: We can’t overstate the convenience of having one family dentist for everyone in your household. Visiting one office allows you to streamline the experience; you can make multiple appointments for children of different ages and get all of their cleanings accomplished in one visit, making it easier than ever to maintain their oral health.
  • Familiarity: One of the most significant advantages to visiting a family dentist is the fact that we are familiar with your entire family’s dental history. Not only will we be able to recognize changes in your own situation, but we’ll be able to watch for those same issues to surface in your children. Our familiarity with your situation can help provide more comprehensive preventive care.
  • Consistency: Regular and consistent maintenance is necessary to properly manage your oral health. By having the same dentist attend to your needs every appointment, you’ll have consistent treatments.
  • Build a Relationship: Having a family dentist allows you and your children to build a lasting relationship with our office. We encourage open and honest communication, which allows us to properly diagnose and treat any issues. Since it’s easier to communicate this way with someone you know and trust, it’s beneficial to work with a professional you’ve been seeing for years.

Learn a Little More About Our Friendly Staff in Jersey City

Advanced Dental of Jersey City is owned and operated by Dr. Peter Tsambazis. He performs all phases of general dentistry as well as periodontics and oral surgery. He graduated from the University of Medicine and Dentistry in 1992 and graduated from their Periodontics Specialty Program in 1996. He has a Periodontic specialty license from UMDNJ issued in 1996.

After graduating, Dr. Tsambazis has worked in dental offices throughout Manhattan, Brooklyn and New Jersey, from North Bergen to Toms River.
