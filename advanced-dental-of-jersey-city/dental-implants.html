What is a Dental Implant?

Dental implants are man-made tooth roots that are made from biologically safe titanium and implanted in the bone of the jaw or skull to help support a crown, bridge, or another dental prosthesis. They are available in various shapes and sizes to fit your specific needs.

What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

When you are thinking about dental implants, you’ll be amazed by the benefits that come along with getting them. Here are a few benefits:

  • Improved comfort - Getting dental implants will naturally make you feel more comfortable because they will make it easier to eat certain foods.
  • Appearance & self-esteem - A perk of dental implants is it will improve your smile and make you more comfortable smiling with teeth. As a result, your self-esteem is likely to increase knowing that people won’t see a gap every time you smile.
  • Enhancement in the ability to chew your food - Dental implants are implanted into your gums and act as a replacement for the tooth that was once in your mouth.
  • Improved speech - When you are missing teeth it can affect your speech, but getting dental implants can restore the clarity of your speech.
