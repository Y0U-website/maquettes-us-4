The practice

When he was a kid, Dr. Shelley often spent time at his uncle’s dental practice. Seeing the impact the practice had on his uncle’s patients, as well as the community, inspired Dr. Shelley to join the orthodontic field. He is now a board-certified orthodontist with the knowledge and expertise to impact the lives of those in his own community!
