Meet Our Team

The Terry Dental Aesthetics team comprises expert individuals passionate about providing superior service to our dental patients and community. We are vibrant, diverse, and wholeheartedly committed to you and your flourishing smile. Most of all, we’re a family, and we’re excited to welcome you.

What to expect

PATIENT EXPERIENCE

When you first walk through our doors, your senses will be greeted by pleasant, relaxing sounds and smells that are reminiscent of your favorite high-end spa. Our professional, compassionate team will check you in and get you prepared for your appointment. Whether you’re interested in taking your appearance to the next level with our excellent cosmetic services like teeth whitening or Botox, or you’re here for a routine exam, we’re committed to providing you with the best care in New Orleans.
