Comprehensive Exam

We want you to relax as our skilled registered dental hygienists provide a thorough dental cleaning to help get your mouth healthy, and your smile beautiful.

Frequently Asked Questions

What payment options do you accept?

We accept cash, check, and most dental insurances. We don&#x27;t want finances to be an obstacle to getting high quality dental care, and so if you need financial assistance, we offer Care Credit which provides no interest payment options up to 12 months.

Do you accept my insurance?

Dr. Visscher and his team are in-network for Delta Dental Premier. However, we will work with any PPO insurance plan, please call us to discuss your plan in detail. *Please note, we do not accept Medicaid/Dentaquest or Health First.

Why do I need x-rays as a new patient?

Dental x-rays are a safe way to diagnose disease and injury of the teeth and gums, as well as help in treatment planning. As a new patient, a full series of X-rays will allow us to assess your current oral health and establish a baseline going forward. X-rays allow us to maintain your dental health through prevention.
