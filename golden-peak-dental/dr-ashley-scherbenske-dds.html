Dr. Ashley Scherbenske, DDS

Comprehensive Dentistry — Restorative, Surgical, Pediatric & Implant Care

Dr. Ashley earned her DDS from Virginia Commonwealth University and then deepened her clinical foundation with a two‑year General Practice Residency at the University of Virginia. During that residency, she gained advanced experience across restorative, surgical, pediatric, and implant dentistry.

She’s passionate about delivering full‑scope dental care and meeting the diverse needs of her patients—whether it’s a filling or a more complex restoration.

Care that feels personal, comfortable, and truly patient-centered is at the heart of her approach. Dr. Ashley builds strong relationships through listening closely, communicating clearly, and working with each patient to create a treatment plan that fits their unique needs. She believes everyone deserves to feel confident, informed, and genuinely cared for—at every visit.

Before joining Golden Peak, Dr. Ashley volunteered at free dental clinics in Virginia and helped coordinate Mission of Mercy outreach events, offering dental care for underserved populations. She sees these as opportunities to give back and connect with communities in meaningful ways.

When she isn’t caring for smiles, Dr. Ashley enjoys staying active—running, hiking, skiing, and paddleboarding are regular parts of her routine. She also finds joy in reading, working with clay (pottery), and spending time with friends and family. A proud Virginia Tech Hokies and Washington Commanders fan, she’s always ready to cheer on her teams!
