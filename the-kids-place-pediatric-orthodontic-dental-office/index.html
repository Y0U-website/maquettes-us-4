At The Kid’s Place, we believe in proactive pediatric dental care . Because no child wants to undergo a tooth extraction, Dr. Wilkerson specializes in moving teeth instead of removing them.

With our early interceptive orthodontic treatment , we address tooth crowding and monitor facial development during the most critical growth years. Identifying orthodontic concerns early helps ensure your child develops a healthy, beautiful smile — and can often reduce or even eliminate the need for braces down the road!

The Kid’s Place is exactly what our name says — a dental office designed specifically for kids! Our pediatric dental practice in Boerne, TX is committed to providing exceptional care in an environment children actually enjoy.

Let’s be honest: our team are all big kids at heart! We’re always smiling, laughing, and making sure every child feels comfortable and excited about their dental visit. Our goal is to create a fun, educational, family-friendly experience where kids leave happy and can’t wait to come back!

Services

At The Kids Place, our primary goal as a pediatric dental team is to provide quality prevention services for a lifetime of happy, healthy smiles. Prevention is the best way to try to maintain a healthy smile and avoid costly dental procedures in the future.
