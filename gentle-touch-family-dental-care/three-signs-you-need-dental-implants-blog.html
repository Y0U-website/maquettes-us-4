So, when is the best time to get a dental implant ?

Usually you should consider replacing a tooth soon after it has been lost — this will prevent bone loss or the shifting of teeth or bite imbalance. Dental implants will allow you to eat, smile and talk until your heart’s content — while giving you a higher quality of life with your restored and healthy mouth.

So, don’t wait any longer. If you have missing teeth and want to regain the quality of life you miss, talk to your dental implant dentist about dental implants .
