Before you begin.

How do I choose between clear aligners and braces?

How often will I come in for visits?

Most orthodontic progress visits are scheduled every 4–6 weeks. The exact interval may vary according to your appliance, tooth movement, and stage of treatment.

How many hours a day should I wear clear aligners?

Most patients are instructed to wear aligners for 20–22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink anything other than water, brush, and floss.

Are braces only for children and teenagers?

No. Traditional braces can move teeth effectively at many ages, provided the teeth and supporting tissues are healthy. Adults may choose braces when they offer the best control for their case.

Will I need retainers afterward?

Yes. Teeth naturally tend to shift over time. Retainers are an essential part of protecting your orthodontic result.
