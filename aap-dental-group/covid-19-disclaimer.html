Covid-19 Disclaimer

AAP DENTAL GROUP will reopen to provide emergency and scheduled dental care for our established and new patients beginning May 6th.

In coordination with the CDC, State of Illinois, American Dental Association, we will operate under the following directives to minimize the risk of infection:

All patients and staff will be prescreened when they enter the facility.

Patients will be limited in the office. Family members should remain at home or in their vehicles. Patients should call the office from their car before entering the building in order to maintain the physical distancing.

All doctors, staff, and patients will wear masks throughout the exam or while giving or receiving care. Additional PPE will be used by dentists and staff (face shields).

Social distancing guidelines will be maintained and all surfaces, exam rooms, equipment will be disinfected.

Our central air system is equipped with a UV-C light in an effort to kill 99 % of bacteria and viruses during operation. Additionally, portable air cleaners with HEPA filters and UV lights were added in order to completely clean and disinfect the air in the office within one hour.

If you have been recently ill, have a fever or any symptoms, please ask our patient coordinator to reschedule your appointment.

We appreciate your assistance in keeping everyone safe and healthy and we look forward to seeing you soon!
