Invisalign ® is designed as a less conspicuous replacements to traditional metal braces. Using Invisalign ® I create a series of custom clear aligners. These aligners are made of clear plastic, similar to a bleach tray, and are worn day and night and gradually straighten your teeth.

In order to prevent periodontal disease, it is recommended to have a dental cleaning at least (at least in italics) twice per year. Regular exams will allow us to find and repair cavities more easily.

Endodontic therapy is a sequence of treatment for the pulp of a tooth which results in the elimination of infection and protection of the decontaminated tooth from future microbial invasion.

A veneer is a thin layer of restorative material placed over a tooth surface, either to improve the esthetics or to protect a damaged tooth surface.

Services

We restore teeth using the latest resin-based composite filling materials, which allow an unprecedented level of shade matching and ability to polish and blend.

A crown is a type of dental restoration which completely encircles a tooth or dental implant.

A dental implant is a “root” device, usually made of titanium, used in to support restorations that resemble a tooth or group of teeth to replace missing teeth.

We work closely with competent, qualified orthodontists to provide orthodontic solutions. We also offer a progressive series of removable trays as a solution for simple cases.

As a person ages their teeth often become darker, partly due to pigments found in everyday food, drinks, or tobacco products. With modern bleaching products we can restore your teeth to their natural shade and beyond!
