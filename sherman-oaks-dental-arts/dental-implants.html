How Dental Implants Work

A dental implant in Sherman Oaks, CA, includes a small titanium post that is surgically inserted into the jawbone in place of the missing tooth root. Titanium is a biocompatible material, meaning it can safely bond with the bone in a natural process called osseointegration. This fusion creates a solid anchor for the replacement tooth and helps prevent bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss. Following the healing period, which usually lasts several months, a custom-made dental crown, bridge or denture is placed over the post to complete the restoration.

Every implant restoration is crafted to fit your mouth precisely, ensuring a comfortable bite and a seamless appearance. Whether you need to replace a single tooth, multiple teeth or a full arch, dental implants offer the versatility to meet your specific dental needs.

Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

Dental implants in Sherman Oaks, CA, offer several advantages over other restorative options. Because they are anchored in the jawbone, they provide excellent chewing strength and stability. They also help preserve the natural shape of your face and jaw by stimulating the bone and preventing deterioration. Unlike removable dentures, implants do not shift and they require no adhesives or special cleaning solutions. You can brush and floss them just like natural teeth.

Another key benefit is their aesthetic value. Implant restorations in Sherman Oaks, CA, are made to closely match your surrounding teeth in color, shape and size, contributing to a natural-looking smile. Patients often report a renewed sense of confidence after treatment, knowing that their smile looks complete and feels secure.
