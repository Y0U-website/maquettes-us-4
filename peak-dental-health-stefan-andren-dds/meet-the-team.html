Dr. Stefan Andren, Dentist

Dr. Stefan Andren is one of Peak Dental’s dentists. In addition to earning his DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, Dr. Andren brings a wide variety of experience to the Peak team, and he is a Tongue Tied Academy graduate, an elite member of Airway Health Solutions, an ambassador for The Breathe Institute, and more.

Each day Dr. Andren performs a variety of patient care procedures, including tongue-tie release (infants through adults), expansive orthodontics treatment from early childhood through adulthood, and performs treatment for TMJ diseases and facial pain. Dr. Andren also works with the Peak team to fabricate oral appliances for sleep-disordered breathing, performs Solea laser restorative dentistry and cosmetic dentistry procedures, and directs his team of highly skilled and passionate caregivers.

His favorite part of working at Peak is the satisfaction he gets from creating an environment where patients can share their goals and concerns without fear of judgment or pressure. He also loves that the Peak team brings the best and latest research and technologies to Peak patients to help them achieve comfort, esthetics, and health that extends well beyond the mouth. His top passion? Working with children’s growth and development to assist them in becoming the best version of themselves.

When he’s not caring for Peak patients or guiding his team, Dr. Andren enjoys playing with his four children and being on the water, as well as watching and performing live music! He joined his first band, Dancing Pilgrims, in high school, and today he is the bass and saxophone player for the Dovetail Joints.

Dr. Patrick Randall, Dentist

Dr. Patrick Randall joined the Peak Dental Health team in March of 2023. Born and raised in Saco, Dr. Randall stayed local and earned his DMD (Doctor of Dental Medicine) from The University of New England Dental School. Dr. Randall loves meeting new patients and helping to improve and maintain their oral health. He is especially passionate about helping people restore their smile through the use of dental implants.

Prior to attending dental school, Dr. Randall was an epidemiologist for the State of Massachusetts. He finds that dentistry allows him to combine his love of science with his passion for helping people. Patients with dental anxiety will find that Dr. Randall has a very calm presence and ensures patient comfort through every step of an appointment.

When he’s not working, Dr. Randall enjoys the outdoors like most Mainers, hunting at camp up north or fishing and lobstering on his boat in the Saco Bay.
