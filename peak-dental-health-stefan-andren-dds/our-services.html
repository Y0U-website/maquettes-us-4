Schedule Today Comprehensive Dental Services No matter what your dental care needs are, we promise to be your partner throughout your journey to peak dental health, an amazing smile, and beyond! We work with you to find out what your goals are and make a plan to achieve them that is tailored to your specific needs.

Below you’ll find a few of the many services and procedures we provide, all of which are performed with our signature holistic approach and exceptional level of care. We always present patients with proactive options now in hopes of helping you avoid reactive decisions later. If you have any questions or would like to schedule an appointment, please contact us today!

Cleanings Our team of skilled dental hygienists partner with you to ensure the health of your teeth and gums, and work with you to craft a strategy for future care.

Gum Disease Facing periodontal disease? Our team will work with you to determine the cause of the infection, solve the problem, and give you the tools you need to maintain healthy teeth and gums.

Restorative Dentistry No matter what caused damage to your teeth, repairing your smile is our priority! Our dentists use minimally invasive techniques to return the function and esthetics of your natural teeth.

Dental Implants Whether you have a tooth that was lost or never formed, we can repair your smile with strong replacements that seamlessly fit in with your natural teeth.

Esthetic Dentistry Unsatisfied with your smile? No matter what changes you want to make, our team will work with you to achieve your ideal smile in the most comfortable, efficient way possible.

Orthodontics Orthodontics has the power to both resolve and prevent tooth crowding and bite problems. We use a holistic approach to orthodontics to give you the smile you’ve been dreaming of!

Tongue Tie Release It is relatively common to have a too tight connection under the tongue that restricts mobility and function. When this impacts quality of life, our practice can revise the area with a quick and minimally invasive procedure.

Myofunctional Therapy Through myofunctional therapy , an exercise-based therapy that focuses on the muscles of the mouth, tongue, and face, our team will teach you how to correct your mouth’s alignment.

TMJ TMJ disorders can cause pain or dysfunction in the joints of your lower jaw. Our team will partner with you to address and relieve discomfort caused by your TMJ disorder.

Free Tooth Whitening for Life!

We are excited to provide our patients with the benefit of free tooth whitening for life!
