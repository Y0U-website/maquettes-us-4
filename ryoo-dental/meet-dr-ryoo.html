Meet Dr. Ryoo

Dr. Victor K. Ryoo, DDS

Dr. Ryoo strives for excellence in dentistry by delivering state-of-the-art care and unparalleled patient service, which is noted in all aspects of his dental office.

A graduate of the University of Southern California School of Dentistry, class of 2001, Dr. Ryoo has been practicing in the area since opening his office in 2006. In addition to his doctorate, he completed advanced education in implant care at UCLA in 2005 and has achieved Level 2 Certification with Invisalign®.

Today, Dr. Ryoo is a noted doctor of dental surgery and cosmetic dentistry serving Fullerton, Orange County, Los Angeles and surrounding areas.

Dr. Ryoo is a committed dental professional, which is evident in his research interests, memberships with respected organizations in the dental field, and his involvement in the local community. Dr. Ryoo takes pride in providing excellent dental care and approaching treatment with the latest technology and optimal treatment options in order to provide the highest level of care, economically.

Previous Official Dental Provider for The Rinks.

Official Training Facility of Anaheim Ducks.
