Dental Implants in Tonawanda

  • Permanent solution — With proper care, implants can last a lifetime
  • Preserve bone health — Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss that occurs after tooth loss
  • No damage to adjacent teeth — Unlike bridges, implants don’t require grinding down neighboring teeth
  • Eat and speak with confidence — Implants are stable and secure—no slipping or clicking
  • Easy care — Care for implants like natural teeth with brushing and flossing

Implant Solutions

Single Tooth Replacement

A single implant topped with a custom crown replaces one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth.

Multiple Teeth Replacement

Multiple implants can support a bridge to replace several adjacent missing teeth, or strategically placed implants can support a full arch of teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing all teeth, implant-supported dentures provide far greater stability and comfort than traditional dentures. They can be fixed (permanently attached) or removable (snap-on).

The Implant Process

  • Consultation — We evaluate your oral health, jaw bone, and overall health to determine if you’re a good candidate for implants.
  • Surgical placement — A trusted oral surgeon or periodontist places the implant post in your jawbone.
  • Healing (osseointegration) — Over several months, the implant fuses with your bone, creating a stable foundation.
  • Restoration — Once healed, we attach a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture to complete your smile.

Are Implants Right for You?

Most adults with good general health are candidates for dental implants. Factors we consider include bone density, gum health, and overall health conditions. During your consultation, we’ll assess your situation and discuss whether implants are the best option for you.
