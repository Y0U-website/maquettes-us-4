Get to Know Rome Dental

Who We Are

Rome Dental is a small, independent practice built on the idea that dental care should be honest, accessible, and actually pleasant.

We opened our doors because too many people in Central New York were driving an hour just to find a dentist they trusted, especially for complex work like implants. That didn’t sit right with us.

So Dr. Candido set up our dental office right here in Rome, NY and hired a team that genuinely cares about people. We’ve been hard at work building a dental practice where patients feel like family.

You get world-class dental expertise right from the comfort of Rome.

Our Philosophy

We Treat Patients Like Family

You’ll never leave our office feeling like you were pushed into an expensive treatment you didn’t understand. We explain your options, answer your questions, and let you decide what’s right for you. If a simple filling solves the problem, that’s what we recommend, not a crown that costs five times more.

We Take Our Time

Dental work shouldn’t feel like an assembly line. We schedule appointments with enough time to do the job right, answer your questions, and make sure you’re comfortable. You won’t feel rushed out the door here.

We Follow Up

After any surgical procedure, Dr. Candido personally calls every patient that evening to check in. That’s not standard practice in dentistry but it is here.
