The Process

At your consultation appointment, we will assess your your complete medical and oral health and explain all your treatment options, requirements and concerns so you can arrive at an informed decision.

The next step is to conduct a CT Scan (Cone Beam). Clinical examination combined with the CT, is necessary and prudent for establishing the best possible treatment plan for dental implants. Then we will book a 90 minute appointment where we surgically place the dental implants.

During the procedure, implants are placed in the jaw. This procedure is done under local anesthesia. Your existing/new denture is modified to snap on to the implant abutments. The denture will be fully secure but can easily be removed for cleaning. Discomfort from the procedure is usually minor.
