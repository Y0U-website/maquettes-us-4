Technology

Our Advanced Technology

We provide only the best for our patients.

Digital X-Ray

Digital imaging provides instant digital images of teeth, bones and tissues. The digital sensor detects images and sends them electronically to a computer where it can be immediately processed and displayed. This delivers the highest quality and the lowest dose of radiation possible.

Intra-Oral Camera

Digital intra-oral camera provides instant digital images of teeth, soft tissue, tongue, palate and more. This is sent to a computer where it can be immediately displayed. Our digital technology delivers the highest quality. Digital pictures help our team and our patients in proper diagnosis and treatment planning.

Cone Beam CT-Scan

When the regular dental x-rays are not sufficient, this advanced 3D x-ray technology produces three dimensional images of the teeth, jawbone and other hard tissues anatomy. 3D imaging is used in diagnosis and procedures such as tooth infection, cracked tooth, root canal treatment, and implant surgery. Our office is one of the few dental offices in southern New Hampshire to have this technology. This imaging can be done in different fields of views and exposure levels.

Digital Scanner

This fast digital scanning technology allows us to design and directly order the appliance to the dental laboratory to fabricate night guards, ortho retainer, Invisalign and more. This would eliminate the old-fashioned impressions mess and issues such as gagging.

CEREC - Same Day Crowns

CEREC stands for Chairside Economical Restoration of Esthetic Ceramics and is a computer-aided design and manufacturing system for dentists. This advanced German technology combines a camera, computer, milling machine, and an oven into one machine allowing tooth restorations to be created in the dentist office, all within a single visit. Through this technology our team is able to design and manufacture crowns and bridges for our patients all in a single visit. This would eliminate the old-fashioned impressions mess and issues such as gagging.

Zoom whitening

This is an in-office immediate result teeth whitening technology. Drinking tea, coffee, wine, and soda could darken and yellow the teeth as we age. But luckily, Zoom Teeth Whitening is an excellent solution. The process combines a special lamp, referred to as a Zoom light with a hydrogen peroxide-based whitening gel. Using the system, our team will bleach each tooth and produce a brighter smile in only 60 minutes.

3D Printing

We have relationships with couple of best digital labs in the U.S. to 3D print and manufacture our oral appliances. After we digitally scan the planned treatment, the digital file is securely sent to the lab where they 3D print them to the highest accuracy. These include, occlusal guard, orthodontic retainer, Invisalign , and surgical guides. Surgical guides are used to place dental implants in the highest level of accuracy.

Nitrus Oxide Sedation

Nitrus oxide is a colorless and odorless gas, commonly known as laughing gas. This mild sedative agent is a safe and effective way to help reduce the patient’s pain and anxiety during dental treatment. The gas is given through a nose piece.

Computerized Novocain Injection (70 % less pain and 50 % less Novocain)

In an advanced dental injection system, the flow rate of local anesthesia is controlled by a computer. The technology provides a slow, steady, and comfortable injection along with a classical music. This means less pain and less amount of Novocain.

Laser Dentistry

Lasers have revolutionized the way dentists provide care. A safer and healthier approach to soft tissue management and reduction in post-op discomfort. This technology has multiple applications such as recontouring gum for a better aesthetic, removal of excess soft tissue, hemostasis, frenectomy, implant dentistry, and bacterial reduction on deep cleaning procedure and pocket reduction.
