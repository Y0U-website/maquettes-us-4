Frequently Asked Questions

General dentistry focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of common dental issues. It includes routine checkups, cleanings, fillings, exams, and ongoing oral health care for patients of all ages.

Most patients should visit the dentist every six months for a routine exam and professional cleaning. However, patients with gum disease or specific dental concerns may need more frequent visits.

A routine visit typically includes a comprehensive oral exam, professional teeth cleaning, digital X-rays (if needed), and an evaluation of your gums, teeth, and bite to detect potential issues early.

Tooth pain or sensitivity can indicate cavities, gum issues, or other dental problems. We recommend scheduling an appointment as soon as possible so the issue can be diagnosed and treated before it worsens.
