A dental crown is a method of restorative dentistry that covers and protects a tooth that has experienced damage or decay. Typically made of porcelain or ceramic, the dental cap is placed over the existing tooth to restore its shape, size, and strength. When Do You Need a Tooth Crown?

There are many situations where the team at Southwest Family Dental in Las Cruces, NM can offer a dental crown to strengthen your smile. If you have a damaged tooth, then the crown will protect from further damage, and can even restore a broken or cracked tooth. A crown is also a great option for covering discolored or misshapen teeth to improve the appearance of your smile, and is also used following a root canal to restore the shape of your tooth.
