As a General Dentist Dr Yadav is very passionate about providing comprehensive and gentle dental care to all ages. Her approach is focused on building long-term relationships with our patients, ensuring they feel comfortable and informed about their oral health.

Dr Yadav graduated with her Bachelor in Dental Surgery (BDS) in 2015 from India. Following that she completed her Master’s in Public Health (MPH) at City University of New York. She then graduated from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. Dr Yadav has been practicing in the US since 2022 and has gained experience in a variety of dental procedures like esthetic composite bondings, fillings, surgical extractions, crowns, fixed and removable prosthesis etc. She is committed to staying up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry through continuing education courses.

Outside of the office she is an avid reader, enjoys spending time with her family, and hiking.

Dr Yadav believes in making dentistry accessible and enjoyable for everyone. She is dedicated to creating a positive and comfortable experience for each patient.
