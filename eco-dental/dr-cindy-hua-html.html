Get to Know Cindy Hua, DMD

Dr. Cindy Hua is a native Houstonian who has been practicing dentistry for over 10 years. She earned her undergraduate degree from The University of Texas at Austin, where she also met her husband, and went on to receive her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale.

Dr. Hua is married and a proud mother of two boys. She also has a beloved poodle mix and enjoys spending time with her family traveling and creating meaningful memories together.

She has a genuine passion for dentistry, particularly for building connections with her patients and helping them achieve their dental goals. Her clinical interests include cosmetic and implant dentistry, though her practice is not limited to these areas. Dr. Hua is committed to lifelong learning and has pursued extensive continuing education in cosmetic dentistry, implant dentistry, and orthodontics to provide the highest level of care for her patients.
