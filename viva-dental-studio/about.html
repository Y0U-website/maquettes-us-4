About ViVa Dental Studio

Your smile is a vital aspect of your identity, deserving of the best care and protection. At ViVa Dental Studio, we offer dependable dental services designed to help you maintain a confident and radiant smile. Our experienced and compassionate team is dedicated to guiding you towards achieving your smile goals. We focus not only on aesthetic improvements but also on boosting your overall oral health, ensuring that your teeth and gums remain healthy for years to come. Let us be your partners in dental care, where your smile is our top priority.

Meet Dr. Juliana Correal Daughter’s

"Valentina" and "Victoria"

My Daughters, Valentina and Victoria, have changed the manner in which I view this world. They are the light of my life and the reason why I chose to open my own dental studio, after practicing with a privately-owned group practice for 13 years. They motivated me to create a space where I can not only continue to create beautiful smiles but also be a leader in my community and profession.

I am trying to instill in my girls that they need to work hard for the things they want and to never allow anyone or anything to limit their capabilities or goals. They need to fill their lives with things that bring them happiness and be the light in all this world’s darkness! That is how the name ViVa Dental Studio came to pass. It is a combination of the first letters of their names (ViVa) and a reminder of how we need to live life to the fullest. I should add as a footnote that the girls also want me to let everyone know that they came up with the name!!!

Community Partners

ViVa Dental Studio was recently recognized by the local Chamber of Commerce for its exceptional dental care and community involvement. The article highlighted the studio’s personalized approach, advanced treatments, and dedication to patient comfort. The Chamber also praised ViVa Dental’s efforts in giving back to the community by honoring Luca Arnaldo’s memory. This recognition reflects the practice’s commitment to both excellence in dentistry and its positive impact on the community.

ViVa Dental Studio Partners with Kids Beating Cancer

ViVa Dental Studio is proud to partner with Kids Beating Cancer to support their incredible mission. In honor of our beloved neighbor, Luca Arnaldo, who recently passed away, we are donating a portion of the proceeds from all new patient exams to KBC. This partnership reflects our commitment to making a positive impact in our community and helping children in need.

Discover Dr. Correal’s Inspiring Story

At ViVa Dental Studio, we’re proud to have Dr. Juliana Correal, DMD, as the heart and soul of our practice. Her journey, from her roots in Colombia to becoming one of Central Florida’s most trusted dentists, is one of resilience, dedication, and passion for creating confident smiles.

Dr. Correal’s story has been featured in Orlando Style Magazine, highlighting her extensive education, professional achievements, and community involvement.
