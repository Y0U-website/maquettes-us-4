Digital X-rays

At ViVa Dental, we utilize advanced digital X-rays to provide a thorough and precise assessment of your oral health. Digital X-rays offer a clearer, more detailed view of your teeth, bones, and surrounding structures, ensuring accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning.

Experience the benefits of cutting-edge technology with our Digital X-rays at ViVa Dental, where your health and safety are our top priorities.

  • Reduced Radiation: Digital X-rays emit significantly less radiation compared to traditional X-rays, making them safer for you.
  • Enhanced Image Quality: High-resolution images allow for better detection of dental issues such as cavities, bone loss, and infections.
  • Immediate Results: Digital images are available instantly, allowing for quicker diagnosis and treatment.
  • Eco-Friendly: Digital X-rays eliminate the need for film and chemical processing, making them more environmentally friendly.

Digital Impressions

At ViVa Dental, we embrace the latest technology to enhance your dental experience, including the use of digital impressions. This innovative technique allows us to create precise, 3D representations of your teeth and gums without the need for traditional molds.

Experience the future of dentistry with Digital Impressions at ViVa Dental, where precision and patient comfort are our top priorities.

  • Comfort and Convenience: Say goodbye to messy, uncomfortable putty impressions. Digital scanning is quick, clean, and much more comfortable.
  • High Accuracy: Digital impressions provide highly accurate and detailed images, leading to better-fitting crowns, bridges, aligners, and other dental appliances.
  • Fast Turnaround: With digital impressions, we can streamline the process, reducing the time it takes to create and fit your dental restorations.
  • Enhanced Communication: Digital files can be easily shared with dental labs and specialists, ensuring a collaborative approach to your dental care.
