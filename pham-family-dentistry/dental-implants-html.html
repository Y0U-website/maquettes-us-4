Dental Implants North Bend, WA | Tooth Replacement

  • Improved Chewing Ability: Since they’re securely anchored, implants restore nearly all of your natural chewing power, allowing you to enjoy a wide variety of foods without discomfort or worry.
  • Preserves Jawbone Health: When a tooth is lost, the jawbone beneath it can start to deteriorate. Dental implants stimulate the bone, preventing this bone loss and preserving your facial structure.
  • Long-Lasting Solution: With proper care and good oral hygiene, dental implants can last a lifetime, making them a wise long-term investment in your oral health.
  • Prevents Shifting of Adjacent Teeth: A gap from a missing tooth can cause surrounding teeth to shift. Implants fill this space, keeping your remaining teeth in their proper positions.
  • No Damage to Adjacent Teeth: Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants don’t require altering healthy neighboring teeth for support.
  • Enhanced Confidence: A complete, beautiful smile can significantly boost your self-esteem and allow you to speak, laugh, and eat without hesitation.

Versatile Solutions for Every Need

  • Single Tooth Replacement: One implant and one crown to replace a single missing tooth without affecting neighbors.
  • Multiple Tooth Replacement: Implant-supported bridges can replace several teeth in a row.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: Secure your full or partial dentures with implants to eliminate the need for messy adhesives and improve biting power significantly.

The Process

  • Consultation & Planning: We assess your bone health and design your treatment.
  • Placement: The implant post is gently placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing (Osseointegration): Over a few months, the bone fuses with the implant, creating a strong foundation.
  • Restoration: Your custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached, completing your new smile.
