What to expect

PATIENT EXPERIENCE

We strive to give our patients an elevated experience at every visit.

You will be seen at your reserved appointment time. Select complimentary amenities from our personalized comfort menu to enhance your experience. Ample time is reserved to provide comprehensive care and education. You will never feel rushed.

SCHEDULING POLICY

During the initial phone call, we will collect any dental insurance you would like to use towards your visit. We do not accept Medicare or Medicaid. Our dedicated insurance coordinator will verify and help you utilize your benefits. We will call you with the details of your policy as well as any patient portion. We will collect your portion as a non-refundable deposit to reserve your appointment. We require 2 business days (Monday-Thursday) notice for any changes to your appointment.

To help make your visit more efficient, we are paperless! We ask that you fill out all patient forms electronically ahead of your visit.
