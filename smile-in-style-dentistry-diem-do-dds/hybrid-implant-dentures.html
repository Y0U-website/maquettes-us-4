Why Should I Get Hybrid Implant Dentures?

Choosing hybrid implant dentures is a decision that can significantly enhance your daily life. If you’ve been struggling with uncomfortable dentures or missing teeth, this treatment offers a stable and durable solution. Hybrid implant dentures provide the security of implants, which are fused to your jawbone, ensuring that your dentures remain firmly in place. This stability allows for natural chewing and speaking, giving you the freedom to enjoy meals and conversations without the fear of slippage.

The Hybrid Implant Dentures Treatment Process

Initial Consultation and Assessment

Your journey to a restored smile begins with a comprehensive consultation at Smile in Style. Dr. Diem Do will conduct a thorough examination of your oral health, including X-rays and 3D imaging, to assess your jawbone density and determine the best implant placement. This personalized approach ensures that your treatment plan is tailored to your specific needs and goals.

Surgical Implant Placement

Once your treatment plan is finalized, the next step is the surgical placement of the dental implants. This procedure is performed with precision and care, ensuring that the implants are securely positioned in your jawbone. Thanks to modern techniques and anesthesia options, the surgery is typically comfortable, and the recovery period is minimal. At Smile in Style, you’ll receive a provisional denture within 24 hours, allowing you to leave the office with a functional smile.

Final Denture Placement

After a healing period, during which your implants integrate with the jawbone, you’ll return to Smile in Style for the final step: the placement of your custom-crafted hybrid implant dentures. These dentures are designed to match your natural teeth in appearance and function, providing a seamless and aesthetically pleasing result. Dr. Diem Do will ensure that your new dentures fit perfectly, allowing you to enjoy the benefits of a stable, beautiful smile for years to come.
