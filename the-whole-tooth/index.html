The practice

Our patient s health and happiness are our top priority here at The Whole Tooth. We treat people, NOT a set of teeth! We care for every person that walks through our doors and understand that each has a unique history. Dentistry is not one-size-fits-all, and we will work together to meet your dental goals.

Dr. Bongiovi is a board-certified pediatric dentist with years of additional specialized training beyond dental school. Before opening The Whole Tooth, Dr. Bongiovi had parents asking all the time if they could see him as well!

By establishing a new practice with Dr. Starnes as our general dentist, we can offer comprehensive dentistry for patients of all ages in one comfortable location. Whether you have baby teeth, permanent teeth or no teeth, everyone can be treated here. Our pediatric rooms were designed for families, and we have ample space for parents and children to have their teeth cleaned at the same time!

We love being part of the Dunwoody community and also serve patients from Sandy Springs, Brookhaven, Roswell, and all surrounding communities.
