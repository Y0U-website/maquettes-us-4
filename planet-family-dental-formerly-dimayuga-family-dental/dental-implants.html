What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots that replace missing teeth. At Planet Family Dental in Lynnwood, WA, we use titanium posts that fuse with the jawbone to support crowns , bridges , or dentures . With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.

Why choose dental implants in Lynnwood, WA?

How does the dental implant procedure work?

The implant process has four main steps. First, you receive an exam and treatment plan. Second, the dentist places the implant post into your jawbone. Third, the implant heals and fuses with the bone over several weeks. Finally, an abutment and crown are attached to complete your new tooth.

Consultation and Qualification — The first step is a comprehensive consultation and examination. This includes thoroughly reviewing your medical history, an oral examination , and imaging studies such as digital X-rays or a CT scan. Dr. Sheth uses this information to assess your jawbone density, gum health, and overall candidacy for implants.

Implant Placement — Once Dr. Sheth has determined you’re a good candidate, the next step is to surgically place the implant. This involves inserting a titanium post into the jawbone to serve as the new tooth’s root. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation to ensure your comfort. Most patients are surprised by how manageable the process is.

Healing and Integration — After placement, you’ll need a healing period. This allows for osseointegration — when the implant fuses with the bone. Depending on individual healing rates and bone quality, this can take several weeks to a few months. During this time, a temporary restoration may be placed so you’re never without a tooth.

Abutment and Crown Placement — Once the implant fully integrates with the bone, Dr. Sheth will install the abutment, which holds the prosthetic tooth. Finally, a custom-made crown is attached to the abutment, completing the restoration. The crown is designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth for a seamless result. Dr. Sheth will work closely with you throughout the process to ensure you’re comfortable and informed, providing detailed instructions for care and recovery at every stage.

How do you care for dental implants?

Caring for implants is simple — treat them just like your natural teeth. With good daily habits and regular visits to Planet Family Dental in Lynnwood, WA, your implants can last for decades.

Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush

Floss daily, including around the implant and under the crown

Visit Planet Family Dental for routine cleanings every six months

Avoid smoking, which can weaken the bone around the implant

Avoid chewing hard items like ice, hard candy, or pen caps that could damage the restoration

Consider a night guard if you grind or clench your teeth

If you notice any changes around your implant — such as swelling, discomfort, or looseness — contact our Lynnwood office right away. Early attention helps protect your investment and keep your smile healthy.
