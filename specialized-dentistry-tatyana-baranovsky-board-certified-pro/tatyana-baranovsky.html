Tatyana Baranovsky, BDS, FACP

Board Certified Prosthodontist

BIOGRAPHY:

Dr. Tatyana Baranovsky graduated from dental school in 1999 in her homeland of Ukraine.

Dr. Tatyana Baranovsky defended and passed her four-part Prosthodontics Board exam immediately after graduating with her Certificate in Prosthodontics in 2016 and distinguished herself by becoming a Diplomate of the American Board of Prosthodontics. She subsequently held the position of Assistant Professor and was the Director of Prosthodontic Courses at the University of New England in Portland, Maine between 2016-2019.

After 20 years of Dental Clinical experience, Dr. Baranovsky has come to Syracuse with her family to fulfill her dream of owning her own Prosthodontic practice
