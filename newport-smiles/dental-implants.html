What is the most expensive part of a dental implant?

The most expensive part of a dental implant in Newport Beach is usually the crown, which is custom-made to match your natural teeth. Dr. Aarti Puri will ensure that the crown blends seamlessly with your existing smile.

How many teeth do you get with 4 implants?

With 4 dental implants, patients in Newport Beach can support a full arch of teeth, typically 10 to 12 teeth. Dr. Aarti Puri offers this advanced solution for a stable and long-lasting replacement of multiple missing teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants placed by Dr. Aarti Puri in Newport Beach can last a lifetime with proper care. The crown may need replacement after 10-15 years, but the implant itself is designed to be permanent.

How many teeth can you fix at once?
