Why Missing Teeth Cause Problems

Your teeth have roots that extend deep into your gums and jawbone. When you chew, pressure travels down through your teeth and into the roots, stimulating your jawbone. This regular stimulation helps maintain a dense, strong, and healthy jawbone.

When a tooth is missing, there is no root to deliver that pressure. Without stimulation, the bone tissue in the jaw begins to deteriorate. Over time, this process can weaken the surrounding teeth, make chewing more difficult, and even alter the shape of your face by causing a sunken or prematurely aged appearance.

What Exactly Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post designed to serve as an artificial tooth root. During the procedure, an oral surgeon places the implant into the jawbone at the site of the missing tooth. Over time, the bone heals around the implant in a natural process called osseointegration. This bond ensures that the implant becomes a permanent and stable part of your jaw.

Once healing is complete, Dr. Yves Gattereau , your trusted Stoughton dentist, attaches a custom-made crown to the top of the implant. The crown is crafted to match your surrounding teeth in terms of color, shape, and size, ensuring a seamless smile. The result is a restoration that feels completely natural, allowing you to chew, speak, and smile without worry. Unlike removable dentures or bridges, implants provide stability that does not shift or slip. With the proper care, they can last for decades, providing patients with peace of mind and a permanent solution to tooth loss.

Who Can Get Dental Implants?

Not every patient is immediately ready for implant placement , but many people are excellent candidates. During your consultation at Healthy Smiles Studio, Dr. Gattereau will perform a complete examination and take detailed images of your teeth and jaw. Good candidates for implants are generally in overall good health, have healthy gums free of infection, and possess enough bone density to support the implant.

Types of Dental Implants

At Healthy Smiles Studio, we recognize that no two smiles are alike. That is why we offer several types of implant-supported restorations tailored to the needs of each patient:

  • Single Dental Implants A single implant, topped with a custom crown, replaces a single missing tooth. It blends seamlessly with your natural teeth, restoring both aesthetics and function.
  • Implant Bridges When two or more consecutive teeth are missing, an implant bridge provides a stable solution. Instead of relying on neighboring teeth for support, the bridge attaches directly to implants, preserving your natural tooth structure.
  • Fixed (Implant-Supported) Dentures For patients who have lost all or most of their teeth, implant-supported dentures such as All-on-X solutions provide unparalleled stability. These dentures are securely anchored to implants, eliminating the slipping and discomfort commonly associated with removable dentures. They allow you to eat, smile, and speak with confidence.

The Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are widely regarded as the standard of care for tooth replacement because they offer unique advantages over traditional restorations. Unlike bridges or dentures, implants actively protect and preserve the jawbone. This stimulation helps maintain facial structure and prevents the bone loss that often follows tooth loss.

Another significant benefit is the natural appearance and feel of implants. Once your crown is attached, it is nearly impossible to distinguish from a natural tooth. With proper hygiene and routine care from your dentist, implants can last decades , making them a cost-effective and reliable long-term solution.

Since they are firmly anchored to the jaw, implant-supported crowns, bridges, and dentures will remain in place while eating or speaking. This gives patients freedom to enjoy foods they might otherwise avoid and confidence in social settings. Ultimately, implants play a crucial role in maintaining overall oral health. Unlike traditional bridges, they do not require neighboring teeth to be filed down for support. This means your natural teeth remain strong and intact, further protecting your smile for the future.

The Dental Implant Process

Restoring your smile with implants is a step-by-step process designed for lasting results.

Throughout each stage, our team is here to answer questions, manage comfort, and support patients every step of the way.

  • Initial Consultation At your first visit, Dr. Gattereau reviews your dental and medical history, takes advanced images, and evaluates whether you are a good candidate for implant placement .
  • Surgical Placement A skilled oral surgeon carefully places the implant post into your jawbone. The precision of this step is vital to ensure long-term stability.
  • Healing and Osseointegration Over the next several months, your jawbone naturally fuses with the implant. This healing phase is what makes implants such a reliable restoration.
  • Final Restoration Once healing is complete, your Stoughton dentist attaches an abutment and a custom crown, restoring the whole look and function of your smile.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

  • How Long Do Implants Last? With proper oral care and regular visits to your dentist, dental implants can last 20 years or longer many remain successful for a lifetime.
  • Is the Procedure Painful? Thanks to modern techniques and local anesthesia, most patients experience minimal discomfort during and after surgery.
  • What if I Do Not Have Enough Bone for Implants? In such cases, bone grafting may be recommended before implant placement to establish a strong foundation.
  • Are Implants Safe for Older Adults? Yes. Health and bone density matter more than age. Many older patients opt for implants to replace dentures or restore their dental function.
  • Can Implants Replace Dentures Entirely? Yes. Fixed implant-supported dentures are a reliable option for those seeking an alternative to removable dentures.
