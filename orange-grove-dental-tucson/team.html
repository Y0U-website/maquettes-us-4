Dr. Ola Gloger, DDS

Originally from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, Dr. Gloger is proud to call Tucson her new home. While Arizona wasn’t always part of the plan, it didn’t take long for Tucson’s welcoming community, beautiful desert landscape, and year-round sunshine to win her over; she knew she had found the place where she wanted to build her career and life.

Dr. Gloger earned her Bachelor of Science degree majoring in Biology and Psychology from the University of New Brunswick. She was consistently named to the Dean’s List and ultimately inducted into the Golden Key International Honour Society in recognition of her academic achievements. She went on to earn her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Dalhousie University in Halifax, Nova Scotia, where she received the Pierre Fauchard Academy Award and the Canadian Academy of Periodontology Award in recognition of her dedication to clinical excellence and patient care.

Drawn to dentistry by its unique blend of art, science, and engineering, Dr. Gloger loves that no two days are ever the same. She especially values the opportunity to make a meaningful difference in the lives of her patients by improving their oral health and confidence. Whether relieving pain, or creating a beautiful smile, she finds it incredibly rewarding to see the direct impact her care has on her patients.

After dental school, Dr. Gloger completed the AEGD residency in Tucson, where she gained advanced clinical experience and developed particular interests in oral surgery, dental implants, and restorative dentistry. She is committed to lifelong learning, providing personalized care to each patient, while helping them feel comfortable, informed and at ease throughout every step of their treatment.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Gloger enjoys getting lost in a good book or movie, watercolor painting, Polish folk dancing, camping, hiking and biking Southern Arizona’s scenic trails, travelling and hitting the ski slopes whenever she has the opportunity. Fluent in English, French, and Polish, she values connecting with patients from diverse backgrounds, learning new things and making everyone feel welcomed and appreciated.

Dr. Gloger is grateful to have found a home in Tucson and looks forward to building lasting relationships with new friends and colleagues while helping members of the Orange Grove Dental community achieve healthy, confident smiles for years to come.

Dr. Sean Ham, DDS

Dr. Sean Ham is a Tucson native. He attended Ironwood Ridge High School and did his undergraduate studies at the University of Arizona. At the UofA, Dr. Ham completed his Bachelor of Science in Biochemistry and graduated Magna Cum Laude. While in undergrad, Dr. Ham also played for the UofA’s Club Ultimate Frisbee team, Sunburn.

Upon graduation he was accepted to the prestigious 3-year program at the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, California. Dr. Ham graduated with honors and was awarded the Henry Sutro Award for Clinical Excellence. Both during and after dental school, Dr. Ham competed in the American Ultimate Disc League (AUDL) as a professional frisbee player. He played for the San Jose Spiders and San Diego Growlers and in 2015 won the National Championship.

Dr. Ham attended dental school on a Health Services Collegiate Program Scholarship for the U.S. Navy and upon graduation commissioned as a United States Naval Dental Officer. He did additional training in the Navy’s “credentialing program” while serving at the Marine Corps Recruit Depot San Diego, addressing the dental needs of marine recruits in Bootcamp. Dr. Ham finished out his time in the Navy as the Dental Department Head Afloat USS Tripoli LHA 7, where he served as the ship’s dentist for a nearly 1200-person crew.

Sean is married to his high school sweetheart, Kirsten, and has two beautiful daughters, London and Madeline. They enjoy hiking together and love exploring the various mountain ranges in Tucson.
