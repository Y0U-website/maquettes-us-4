A bit more about Dr. Lavigne

Dr. Lavigne is a board-certified orthodontist with a background in esthetic dentistry, creating beautiful smiles for even the most complex cases. She has extensive training in airway & breathing health and growth & development, and a passion for early intervention to help children who show signs of sleep or breathing disorders, working closely with pediatricians, sleep physicians, ENTs, speech pathologists, and myofunctional therapists to aid in improved overall health. She is happy to offer virtual consultations for patients referred for care from outside of the state.
