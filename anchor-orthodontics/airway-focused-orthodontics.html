Airway Orthodontist for Kids

Every child at Anchor Orthodontics is evaluated through an airway lens. Both of our offices use iCAT CBCT 3D imaging, which shows the size and structure of the airway alongside the teeth and jaws, at a fraction of the radiation of traditional x-rays. When treatment is needed, it is usually simpler than parents expect: a custom 3D-printed expander, gentle growth guidance through early intervention , and coordination with your pediatrician, ENT, or myofunctional therapist when it helps.

Dr. Courtney Lavigne lectures nationally on airway orthodontics. This is not a service we added to a list. It is the way we look at every child who sits in our chair.

What signs should parents watch for?

Mouth breathing during the day or night, snoring, teeth grinding, restless sleep, chronic congestion, and even bedwetting or daytime attention struggles can all point to an airway issue. If any of these sound familiar, an evaluation is worth it.

Do you treat sleep apnea?

No. Sleep apnea is a medical diagnosis that belongs with a physician. What we do is evaluate and treat the structural side, like a narrow palate that crowds the airway, and we coordinate with pediatric sleep physicians and ENTs whenever a medical workup is needed.

Is airway-focused treatment only for children?

No. Children benefit most because we can guide growth, but adults with narrow palates can often be treated too. Dr. Lavigne and Dr. Cardarelli both offer non-surgical adult palate expansion (MSE/MARPE), which was not an option for most adults until recently.
