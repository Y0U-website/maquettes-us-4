Dr. Arpan Patel, DMD

Dr. Arpan Nalin Patel: owner dentist in Langhorne, PA

Owner dentist at Alpha Dental Excellence. DMD, University of Pennsylvania, 2004. More than 22 years in dental implantology, full-mouth rehabilitation, cosmetic, restorative and surgical dentistry, and the dentist who brought the Yomi robotic implant system to the practice.

Immediately after graduating, he joined his father’s practice, where he received extensive one-on-one mentorship that accelerated his clinical growth far beyond the norm. From day one, he was performing advanced cosmetic, restorative, and surgical procedures that many dentists do not attempt until seven to ten years into their careers.

This early mastery pushed him to the forefront of full-mouth rehabilitation and cosmetic dentistry, well ahead of his peers, and established the foundation for the high-level expertise he is known for today.

In 2008, Dr. Patel completed a rigorous Dental Implantology program, expanding his capabilities in implant surgery and complex restorative care.

Throughout his career, his philosophy has remained consistent: technology is a tool that elevates patient care and enhances outcomes. He has continuously invested in the newest, most advanced systems to ensure patients receive the safest, most precise, and most comfortable experience possible.
