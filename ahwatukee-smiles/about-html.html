About Dr. Kyler Campbell & Team

We want to give you the smile you deserve.

At Ahwatukee Smiles, we believe in the transformative power of a smile. Your smile is our passion, and we are dedicated to creating happy, healthy smiles for every patient that visits our office.

Dr. Kyler Campbell

Dr. Campbell was born and raised in Ogden, Utah. He attended Weber State University for his undergraduate studies before earning his dental degree from the University of Utah. He met his wife during college while she was studying to become a registered dental hygienist. They have been married for 8 years and have two wonderful daughters, Rose and Ruth.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Campbell enjoys all things sports and is always happy to talk about your favorite teams. He also loves playing golf and pickleball, as well as watching movies and trying new restaurants. Dr. Campbell and his family are new to the Ahwatukee area and would love to hear your favorite local spots and things to do.

Dr. Campbell’s passion for dentistry comes from his love of helping his patients. He is committed to providing high-quality dental care while making sure each patient feels heard, comfortable, and cared for. He takes the time to understand each patient’s concerns and goals and looks forward to helping you achieve a healthier, happier smile!
