New Patients

New here? Here’s what to expect.

Your first visit at Ahwatukee Smiles is about getting to know you, your smile, and your goals. You’ll spend time with your hygienist for a thorough exam and cleaning, with plenty of time to ask questions and share any concerns. Dr. Campbell will then meet with you to review his findings and discuss any treatment options that may be right for you.

Our Core Values

Happy

We believe a happy smile begins with a team that genuinely cares. We’re committed to creating an outstanding patient experience through compassionate care, clear communication, positivity, and integrity. Our goal is to bring happiness to our community one smile at a time.

Healthy

We believe that a healthy smile is the foundation for our patients overall well-being. Our expert team is dedicated to providing strong and vibrant smiles through disease prevention, education, skilled practitioners, and modern technologies. At Ahwatukee Smiles, we promote health by providing a safe and clean environment.

Family

We believe in treating every patient with the same quality, passion, and commitment that we would provide our own family. We’re dedicated to creating a caring, supportive environment where you feel comfortable, valued, and well taken care of.

Insurance & payment.

We accept all Dental PPO plans and also welcome patients without insurance. Before your appointment, we verify your insurance benefits so you know what to expect. Before you leave, we’ll provide you with a clear treatment estimate outlining your expected out of pocket costs, so there are no surprises.
