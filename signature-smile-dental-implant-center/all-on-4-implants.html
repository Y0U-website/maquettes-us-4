Procedure Of Denture Implants

The procedure can vary based on the particular brand of entire arch implant-supported dentures you choose. However, to give an idea of the process, we will review the step-by-step process for All-on-4s.

Step1: The first step is to schedule an initial consultation so your dentist can assess whether you’re a suitable candidate for implant-supported dentures. Your dentist will use 3D imaging to get a picture of your jaw bone structure and decide the aesthetic and functional properties of your new teeth, such as dental crown length and bite placement.

Step 3: This step involves the surgery of inserting the four implants. This step also includes any additional necessary treatments, such as tooth extraction and bone shaping. Then the implants are capped with abutments and temporary teeth. At the same time, the jaw bone recovers and slowly begins to fuse with the implants. Diet during recovery must be carefully managed with soft foods to avoid any damage to the osseointegration process.
