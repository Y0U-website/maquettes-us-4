About Our Practice

Superior Dental, located at 3705 Nostrand Ave., Brooklyn, NY 11235, has been a trusted provider of dental care for over 20 years, serving the Sheepshead Bay community and surrounding areas. The practice specializes in a comprehensive range of dental services, including general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry, tailored to meet the needs of patients of all ages.

The clinic is known for its commitment to patient care, emphasizing individualized treatment and a gentle approach to dentistry. Superior Dental aims to help patients achieve and maintain long-term dental health while providing a welcoming environment. The team at Superior Dental is dedicated to educating patients about their oral health, enabling informed decisions regarding treatments and procedures.

With a focus on quality service, the practice has garnered positive reviews for its skilled dentists and friendly staff, making it a preferred choice for dental care in the area. Superior Dental operates Monday through Saturday, offering flexible appointment options to accommodate busy schedules.

Building a foundation of trust by treating our patients as special individuals is vital to our success. We understand how uneasy some patients may feel about their dental visits, and how we can make a difference in providing a relaxing and positive experience. Our entire team is dedicated to providing you with excellent, personalized care and service to make your visits as comfortable and pleasant as possible.

We thank you for allowing us to take care of your dental needs and look forward to serving you.
