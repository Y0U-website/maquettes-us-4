Meet Dr. Rohit Z. Patel, DDS

Dr. Patel attended Columbia University College of Dental Medicine in New York where he graduated at the top of his class. He followed up by getting his postgraduate degree in endodontics, the specialty field of dentistry which includes root canal treatment and addresses dental injuries to preserve your natural teeth.

Dr. Patel is committed to seeing that each patient receives individual, personalized attention and service. In each of his practices, the guiding principle is service to the patient, taking care of that patient’s specific needs. Dr. Patel has assembled a staff of dental specialists so that his patients can get any dental procedure they need at his practice.

What We Can Do for You

At Broadway Cosmetic Dentistry PC, we specialize in creating nice smiles. But the foundation of a good smile is healthy teeth and gums, so we also provide all the necessary dental specialties to fix any problems you may have with your teeth and gums.

At Broadway Cosmetic Dentistry PC, all our services are geared toward providing you with healthy teeth and a good-looking smile. We want you to have a smile that you’re happy to show to people. If you’ve not been to our office, we want you to know we’re always happy to welcome new patients. Call us at (914) 631-5222 to schedule an appointment.

  • Teeth Whitening: One of the more popular methods of improving your smile, teeth whitening can be done in one office visit. Our teeth whitening system works faster and better than any of the over-the-counter products you may use at home.
  • Veneers: Dental veneers are a versatile way of improving your smile and correcting a variety of teeth problems. Veneers can improve the appearance of teeth that are chipped, cracked, stained or uneven. They can also correct gaps between your teeth.
  • Invisalign: Straight teeth make your smile look nicer. With Invisalign clear braces, you can have straighter teeth in about half the time of metal braces.
  • Dental Bonding: Dental bonding improves your smile by restoring a tooth that has been broken or chipped.
  • Dental Implants: Missing teeth diminish your smile. With dental implants, you can have missing teeth replaced with firmly attached, permanent teeth that look just like your natural teeth.
  • All-on-4: This dental implant method allows denture wearers to replace loose dentures with implant-anchored dentures which don’t move or slip during eating or speaking.
