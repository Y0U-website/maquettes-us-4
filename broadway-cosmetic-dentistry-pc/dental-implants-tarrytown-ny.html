Dental Implants

Replacing your missing teeth will greatly improve your smile. Dental implants are the latest advance in dentistry for replacing missing teeth. Implants are firmly attached to your jaw bone, allowing you to eat and smile with confidence. Implants work great whether you need a single tooth replaced or several. You can even replace dentures with implants.

What Are Denta l Implants?

The actual implant is a small, titanium post that we attach directly to your jaw bone. An artificial tooth, called a crown, is permanently fixed to the implant, giving you a tooth that is just as firmly fixed in your mouth as your natural teeth.
