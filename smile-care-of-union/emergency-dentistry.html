What to do during a dental emergency

Call us immediately at (908) 851-9100 — The sooner you reach out, the better we can help.

Even if the pain subsides, don’t ignore it! Hidden infections or damage can worsen over time. Call us right away and our Union emergency dentist can help you fast.

  • For a knocked-out tooth: Handle it by the crown (not the root) and store it in milk, saline, or saliva until you can get to our office.
  • For a broken or chipped tooth: Keep the area clean and use a cold compress to reduce swelling.
  • For a severe toothache: Rinse your mouth with warm salt water and gently floss to remove any debris. Avoid putting aspirin directly on your gums or tooth.
