What makes an implant supported dental bridge different from traditional dental bridges?

Unlike traditional bridges that rely on adjacent teeth for support, implant supported bridges are anchored directly to the jawbone with dental implants. This offers a more stable, durable solution that promotes jawbone health and doesn’t require altering neighboring healthy teeth.

How long does the process for implant supported bridges take?

The entire process, from initial consultation to the final placement of the bridge, can vary depending on individual cases. Generally, it involves several appointments over a few months to allow for healing and proper integration of the implants into the bone.

How do I care for my implant supported bridge?

Caring for an implant supported bridge is similar to caring for your natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups are essential. Although these bridges are highly durable, maintaining good oral hygiene will ensure their longevity and the health of your surrounding teeth and gums.

Are implant supported bridges covered by insurance?

Coverage can vary by insurance provider and plan. Our team at Mountain Bay Dental will work with you to understand your benefits and maximize your insurance reimbursement for covered procedures. For non-covered portions, we offer flexible payment plans to fit your budget.

What’s the difference between implant-fixed bridges and implant-supported bridges?

Implant-fixed bridges replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring directly to dental implants, offering a stable solution without involving adjacent teeth. Implant-supported bridges, however, use implants to support a bridge that spans a gap of multiple missing teeth, requiring fewer implants for more extensive restoration. The choice between the two depends on the number of missing teeth, jawbone health, and individual needs, with both options providing secure, aesthetically pleasing solutions to tooth loss.
