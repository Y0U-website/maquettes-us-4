How do All-on-4 Dental Implants differ from traditional dental implants?

While traditional dental implants replace individual teeth, All-on-4 implants support an entire arch of teeth with just four implants. This method often requires less bone grafting and can be completed more quickly than replacing each tooth individually.

Are All-on-4 Dental Implants painful?

Patient comfort is a priority at Mountain Bay Dental. The All-on-4 implant procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation to minimize discomfort. Post-procedure, patients may experience some soreness, which can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain medication.

How long does the All-on-4 procedure take?

The surgical component of the All-on-4 procedure can often be completed in a single day. However, the entire process, including consultations, pre-surgical preparation, and final placement of the permanent prosthetic teeth, will span several appointments.

How do I care for my All-on-4 Dental Implants?

Caring for All-on-4 dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Daily brushing, flossing, and regular dental check-ups are essential. Dr. Cheema and the team at Mountain Bay Dental will provide detailed aftercare instructions and support to ensure the longevity of your new smile.
