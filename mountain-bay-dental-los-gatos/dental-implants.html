What is the best age for dental implants?

The ideal age for dental implants is when jaw growth is complete, usually around 18 for men and 16 for women. However, age alone is not the only factor to consider when it comes to dental implants. The overall health and bone density are important considerations taken by your dentist in Los Gatos for successful tooth implantation.

Who is not a candidate for implants?

Patients with certain medical conditions such as diabetes, complications due to heavy smoking, and patients with inadequate jawbone density may not be considered for dental implants by our Los Gatos dental clinic. A thorough examination by a dentist will be needed to determine whether a tooth implant procedure is appropriate.

How long do tooth implants last?

Tooth implants can last a lifetime if taken care of properly. Their longevity is influenced by factors such as:

  • Proper oral hygiene
  • Maintaining a good overall health
  • Regular dental check-ups to ensure proper maintenance and address any potential issues promptly.

Can dental implants get cavities?

The straight answer is no, dental implants are not susceptible to cavities. They are made of materials like titanium and porcelain that are resistant to decay. However, it is important to note that maintaining good oral hygiene is essential to prevent issues like gum disease around the implant.

Are dental implants easy to maintain?

Yes. Regular dental check-ups and the maintenance of good oral hygiene (i.e. brushing and flossing twice a day) help ensure the longevity and health of the dental implant.

Can you get a tooth implant years after extraction?

Yes, you can get a tooth implant years after tooth extraction. However, to prevent bone loss and maintain proper jaw structure for successful implantation, it is preferable to have a dental implant procedure sooner rather than later.

Are implants as strong as real teeth?

The strength and stability of dental implants make them a reliable solution for missing teeth. Dental implants are remarkably strong and can withstand normal biting forces comparable to natural teeth, allowing them to improve functionality and comfort for patients.

Do implants look the same as real teeth?

Yes, dental implants look the same as real teeth. Your dentist in Los Gatos will closely match your dental implant to the appearance of your natural teeth. Our experts in cosmetic dentistry in Los Gatos will work to customize the dental implant’s colour, shape, and contour and allow it to blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth, providing a natural and aesthetically pleasing result.

Are dental implants worth it?

Dental implants are worth the investment as they are durable, have a natural appearance, and have the ability to restore oral function. The long-term benefits often outweigh the initial cost for those seeking a permanent and reliable tooth replacement solution.
