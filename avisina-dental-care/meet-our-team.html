Kamyar Yazdi, D.D.S. south berwick, me dentist

Dr. Yazdi has dedicated his life to excelling in the dental profession. Graduating with high honors from the UT Health San Antonio School of Dentistry in 2011, Dr. Yazdi has ever since gained extensive experience treating patients in multiple dental settings in states of Oregon, Texas and New York. For the past six years, he owned an extremely successful private dental practice in Austin, TX. He believes in importance of dental continuing education and dedicates large part of his time beyond dental practice to gaining new knowledge on advancements in the dental field.

He chose South Berwick as his home in 2022 as he loved the nature and breathtaking beauty of Maine.

In his spare time, he loves to spend time with his family at the ocean or exploring a new coffee shop.
