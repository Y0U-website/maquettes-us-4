Services offered, dentist south berwick, me

DENTAL EXAMS AND CLEANING

Initial dental examinations include a thorough understanding of your dental history and main concerns. As well as a comprehensive clinical, radiographic and periodontal evaluation. This exam reveals any dental disease that you might have, as well as a comprehensive treatment plan to treat those diseases. Once the examination is completed, a thorough dental cleaning is done.

DENAL CROWNS AND FIXED BRIDGES

Dental crowns are protective coverings that are used to cap a cracked or damages tooth. Crowns are also indicated after a Root Canal procedure is done and also after an implant is placed. These caps are made out of durable and strong porcelain that esthetically match the size and shape of your tooth. If multiple teeth are missing, a dental bridge is some times indicated.

COMPOSITE Tooth-colored FILLINGS

Composite fillings are durable, tooth-colored, esthetically pleasing restorations that are used to restore the shape and function of decayed, broken or chipped teeth.

ROOT CANAL THERAPY

Root Canal Therapy is a procedure used to salvage a tooth with infection or extensive decay.

TEETH WHITENING

At AviSina Dental Care in South Berwick, ME, we offer the latest technology for teeth whitening. Using the Zoom whitening technology, we can achieve maximum results in one in-office whitening session.

INVISALIGN

Invisalign is the modern way to straighten your teeth. For many patients, it is possible to avoid the metal braces and achieve the optimal orthodontic results with custom-made clear aligners

DENTAL IMPLANTS

Dental Implants are the modern and the most advanced way to restore missing teeth. There are many advantages to use a dental implant to restore a missing teeth, including restoring chewing, speaking capabilities. As well as not losing the supporting bone after a tooth extraction.

Scaling and Root Planning

Scaling and Root planning or professional dental deep cleaning is a procedure used to perform a more advanced cleaning of teeth in order to treat the gingival and priodontal disease. Usually patients diagnosed with periodontal disease are placed on a 3-4 months recall in order to have more frequent cleanings done instead of every 6 months.

Tooth Extractions

When a tooth is non-restorable and can not be saved, an extraction is performed to remove the tooth.

Oral Cancer Screening

Performed at every recall appointment to diagnose the possible existence of oral cancer.

Sealants

Sealants are placed to protect Children and, in some cases, adult teeth from cavities.
