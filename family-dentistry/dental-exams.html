What Happens During a Dental Exam

Many patients avoid dental visits because they feel unsure about the process. However, dental exams are simple and designed to help protect long-term oral health.

The office explains every step clearly so patients understand their dental condition and treatment options.

  • Teeth cleaning
  • Dental X-rays
  • Oral cancer screening
  • Gum health evaluation
  • Cavity detection
  • Examination of existing dental work

Dental Exams for the Whole Family

Children and adults both benefit from regular dental checkups. Preventive care helps children develop healthy habits early while helping adults maintain strong teeth over time.
