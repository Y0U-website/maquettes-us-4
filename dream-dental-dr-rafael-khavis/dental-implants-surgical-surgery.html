Dental Implants & Oral Surgery

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a durable solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring your smile. A dental implant uses a titanium post placed in the jawbone to support a custom dental crown, bridge, or other restoration. Designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth, dental implants can restore chewing ability, improve comfort, and help preserve your oral health.

Guided Implant Surgery

Guided implant surgery uses advanced 3D imaging and digital treatment planning to precisely place dental implants. This technology helps your dentist plan the implant position, improve surgical precision, and create a more predictable treatment experience. Guided implant placement can help restore missing teeth with a natural-looking and functional result.

Tooth Extractions

Tooth extractions are performed when a tooth is severely damaged, decayed, infected, or cannot be restored. Depending on your needs, an extraction may be simple or require a surgical approach. Our team focuses on patient comfort and careful treatment while providing instructions to support proper healing and recovery.

Dental Bone Grafting

Dental bone grafting helps rebuild and strengthen areas of the jawbone affected by bone loss or tooth extraction. Bone grafting may be recommended to create a stronger foundation for a future dental implant. By restoring lost bone structure, this procedure can support successful implant placement and long-term oral health.
