What to Expect at Your First Visit

At your first appointment, we take time to learn your history, your goals, and any current concerns with your teeth or gums. Most new-patient visits include a comprehensive exam, diagnostic imaging as needed, and a conversation about what we find and what comes next.

Maintaining Good Dental Health Between Visits

Daily habits can make a big difference in how your teeth look and feel between cleanings. Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss or use interdental cleaners, and limit frequent snacking or sugary drinks that feed cavity-causing bacteria. If you grind your teeth at night, tell your dentist—protective options may help prevent future tooth wear and reduce the need for restorative treatment.

Staying consistent with preventative dentistry, keeping up with home care, and scheduling routine checkups helps patients protect their smile for the long run.
