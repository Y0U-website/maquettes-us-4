Implant vs. Crown

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. Once surgically placed, it bonds with the bone to create a stable foundation for a restoration, which is the visible part of the tooth—usually a crown, bridge, or full-arch replacement. Implant placement is the first step, with the restoration often done by your regular dentist.

Single vs. Multiple Dental Implants

A single implant supports one restoration. Even if multiple implants are placed, each supporting one tooth is considered a single implant, unlike All-on-4 or full-arch procedures that use multiple implants to support a complete set of teeth.

Are Single Dental Implants Right for You?

Single implants are ideal for replacing one or a few missing teeth. They offer the most natural look, preserve surrounding teeth and bone, and provide a stable, long-lasting solution compared to bridges.
