Welcome to Taylor Dental Associates.

Our mission is to provide personalized caring and professional dentistry using the same values and principles that we use to provide for our own families.

At Taylor Dental Associates you’ll receive complete care dentistry for the whole family. We understand your life is busy enough without having to travel between several different offices to obtain treatments or seek the services of a specialty dentist. Our office is conveniently located in Taylor.

Becoming a new patient is easy – just give us a call and we’ll answer any questions you have. We offer all aspects of general, cosmetic and orthodontic dental care. We take most insurance plans and even offer financing, subject to approved credit. So give us a call and get started.
