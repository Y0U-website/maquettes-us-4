The practice

Dr. Porter continued his education at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry where he obtained his Doctorate of Dental Surgery. He served as class president all four years, a position he was elected to by his colleagues each year.

Since graduation Dr. Porter has been serving patients in the Oklahoma City metro, furthering his clinical skills and continuing to learn new and exciting procedures in implant and cosmetic dentistry. Occasionally Dr. Porter fills in as the Thunder team dentist! Dr. Porter is an active member of the American Dental Association, Oklahoma Dental Association and the Oklahoma County Dental Society.

Dr. Porter is happily married to his wife Cara, who also serves as a healthcare provider. They have a son and daughter that are a part of the Oakdale community. As always, trips are made in the fall to attend Sooner football games.

Dr. Porter is excited to meet new patients and offer high quality dental care in the Edmond community.
