Symptoms of Failed Dental Implants

There are two categories of dental implant failure. Early failure occurs within the first few months after the implant is placed, while late failure happens after osseointegration is complete. In fact, it could occur years or even decades after the placement surgery.

Both early and late dental implant failure display similar symptoms, which include:

If you experience any of these symptoms, we urge you to request an appointment with us as soon as possible so that we can devise a plan to save your dental implants in Crown Point, IN.

  • Intense or unusual pain While some soreness is to be expected after dental implant surgery, your new teeth should feel comfortable and natural after you’ve finished healing.
  • An implant that feels loose There’s a chance that your restoration is loose, but in other cases, a loose feeling could point to a failed dental implant.
  • Swelling, bleeding, or pus These signs all indicate an infection that needs immediate attention.
  • Difficulty chewing After your restorations have been placed, chewing should be as easy as it was before tooth loss. If you’re having trouble chewing, that’s a major red flag.

How Dental Implant Salvage Works

If you suspect there’s something wrong with one of your dental implants, reach out to us as soon as you can. Dr. Joe Ornelas will examine your mouth, you’ll explain your symptoms, and together, you’ll discuss your treatment options. In some cases, we might be able to save the implant with conservative measures like a deep cleaning or antibiotics. The sooner you seek treatment, the higher the chances are that we can save your smile with inexpensive and simple procedures.

However, if the implant or surrounding tissue is too damaged, removing the implant altogether may be the best solution. Once you’ve recovered, you may be able to receive a new implant after bone grafting, gum disease therapy, and other treatments that are designed to get your oral health back on track.
