How Do All-on-X Dental Implants Work?

Am I a Candidate for All-on-X Implants?

Most people who have lost all of their teeth in one or both arches are good candidates for all-on-X. Even if you have experienced tooth loss-related bone loss, you may still be a candidate because of the strategic angle placement used for this procedure. A patient who would normally need bone grafting for implant dentures might not need it to get all-on-X. Just keep in mind that with any type of tooth replacement, it is crucial that you maintain excellent oral hygiene. This includes healthy lifestyle practices and avoiding bad habits, like smoking and chewing tobacco.

Benefits of All-on-X Implants

All-on-X dental implants offer various benefits. Here are some of the most prominent ones.

  • Fixed: All-on-X is fixed to the mouth, so your replacement teeth can be brushed just like natural teeth. You don’t need to take them out to clean your smile.
  • Comfortable: All-on-X dental implants feel more natural than traditional dentures. After the adjustment period, they shouldn’t feel much different from your natural teeth.
  • Bite Force: All-on-X restores more chewing power than traditional dentures. This allows patients to enjoy a full, nutritious diet without restriction.
  • Healthy Jawbone: Unlike traditional dentures, all-on-X dental implants prevent jawbone resorption.

All-on-X vs. Traditional Implants

All-on-X has a lot in common with implant dentures. Here are a few of the things that separate them from each other.

  • Number of Dental Implants: Implants require up to eight dental implants for a stable smile, increasing their cost.
  • Bone Grafting: To get implant dentures, a higher percentage of patients require bone grafting.
  • Quicker: It takes many months to get implant dentures. All-on-X sometimes allows patients to show off their new smile on the same day.
