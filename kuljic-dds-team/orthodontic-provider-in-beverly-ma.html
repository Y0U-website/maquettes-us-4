Orthodontic Care FAQs

Is orthodontic treatment worth it as an adult?

Will braces or aligners be obvious?

Not the way they used to be. Ceramic braces blend close to your natural tooth color, and clear aligners are nearly invisible up close. Most adults in treatment find that people around them do not notice unless they mention it.

How long does treatment take?

It depends on how far teeth need to move and which approach you use. Minor corrections can take a few months. A full case with braces or aligners usually runs somewhere between a year and two. Functional treatment in a growing child follows the child’s growth, so its timeline is different again. We give you a realistic estimate at your consultation, not a number that sounds good.

Do you treat children as well as adults?

Yes. For children, the most valuable orthodontic work often happens early, while the jaw is still growing and we can guide how it develops rather than correcting problems later. This is the heart of our functional orthodontics, and it is worth an evaluation well before all the adult teeth are in.

Whether you are an adult finally straightening your teeth or a parent wondering if your child should be seen early, the first step is the same: a conversation about what your smile needs and how we would get there.
