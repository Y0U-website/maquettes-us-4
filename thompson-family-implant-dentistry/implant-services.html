DENTAL IMPLANT & FULL MOUTH RECONSTRUCTION

At Thompson Family & Implant Dentistry, we offer state of the art dental implants! So, what are dental implants? Implants replace one or many missing teeth, rebuilding your smile to gain your confidence and lifestyle back. Tooth loss is mainly caused from tooth decay, periodontal (gum) disease, or injury. There are many benefits to getting implants, some of the biggest advantages are improved speech and comfort, easier eating, durability and better overall health. When an implant is placed, it restores your bite to natural function, leaving you feeling confident about your smile!

Dr. Thompson and the rest of our team will ensure comfort and ease from your first consultation until you have your beautiful and new confident smile! Our team offers the highest quality dental implant services in Georgetown and the surrounding areas.

HOW DENTAL IMPLANTS WORK

Implants are inserted directly into the jawbone in the space that was vacated by one or many missing teeth. While the implant is healing, it will fuse to the bone surrounding it making it very durable and long lasting! The surgical procedure to place an implant is minor and routine, usually only requiring local anesthesia. Implants by far have a much higher success rate (over 95 %) than any other tooth-replacement option. Implants replicate the appearance and feel of natural teeth, and can be as strong or stronger than natural teeth.

Single Tooth Replacement

For a lone gap in your smile, a single dental implant root and crown can be placed between two healthy teeth. This treatment choice has the highest success rate (compared to other tooth replacement options), making it the best long-term investment for replacing a single tooth. Implants never decay or need root canal treatment; it feels and looks just like a natural tooth. Plus, this option is a much less invasive approach than other procedures like a traditional bridge which require neighboring teeth to be filed down.

REPLACING MISSING TEETH

When replacing multiple missing teeth, implants are the ideal replacement option. If you are missing multiple teeth in a row, the good news is you do not have to have an implant placed for every tooth! Implants can act as support on either side of a gap, and a crown is placed between the two. The benefit of this process is we do not have to use any of your remaining natural teeth as bridge supports, which could make them more susceptible to decay.

FULL MOUTH DENTAL IMPLANTS

If you are missing an entire arch of upper or lower teeth, a full, permanent arch can be placed into the mouth. Just like if you are replacing one or a few missing teeth, this option is never removed, acting as a full set of natural teeth. The benefit of this option is we can replace 10-12 teeth with only 5-6 implants. Implant replacement teeth protect your jawbone, never move and should last a lifetime!

DENTAL IMPLANT FAQs

HOW DO I CARE FOR MY IMPLANTS?

It is imperative that a patient who receives dental implants brushes and flosses twice a day and schedules regular, professional cleanings. Poor oral hygiene can lead to implants becoming loose and ultimately failing.

ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS RIGHT FOR ME?

Most adults are candidates for implants. If you are having trouble with speech, confidence, eating or other daily routines then implants may be a great option for you!

ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS SAFE?

Yes! Dentists have been using implants for years and the technology is the best it’s ever been. Today’s implants are the best and most durable option for tooth replacement.

ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS PAINFUL?

Many patients ask “how much pain will I be in after the procedure?” and the answer is not much at all! Many patients come back and say it’s not nearly as uncomfortable as originally anticipated. Dr. Thompson will ensure comfort and ease during the procedure and walks patients through gentle techniques to become more and more comfortable with your beautiful, new smile!

ARE DENTAL IMPLANTS COVERED BY INSURANCE?

Each plan has different benefits, we are happy to contact your insurance provider to see what your benefits include.

HOW MUCH DO DENTAL IMPLANTS COST?

Every patient is different and has a customized treatment plan, which varies the cost for each patient. We will always walk through the cost with you. Our ultimate goal is to ensure you are confident in your smile and maintain good oral health, we are happy to discuss payment plans with you.
