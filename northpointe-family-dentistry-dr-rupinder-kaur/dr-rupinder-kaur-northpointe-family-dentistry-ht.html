Rupinder Kaur, DDS

Dr. Rupinder Kaur received her DDS (Doctor of Dental Surgery) Degree from New York University College of Dentistry, NY in 2002. After finishing school, she worked as an associate in Michigan for three years and then moved to Ohio in 2006. She lives in Dublin with her husband and three children. She started her Practice in 2010 and remains committed to learning all the new advancements in Dentistry. She has completed hundreds of hours of continuing education in different phases of dentistry.

Dr. Kaur has great passion for her children, and for the field of Dentistry. Her goal is to educate her patients and provide excellent dental care. She believes that by using modern equipment and techniques, dental procedures are not painful anymore. She has over twenty years of experience in General Dentistry, Cosmetic and Surgical procedures. She listens to the problems and concerns of patients about their oral health and then provides an optimal treatment plan for them. She believes in relationship and trust the most .
