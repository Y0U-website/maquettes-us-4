About Brady Dental Group

Brady Dental Group is a family dental practice proudly serving Amarillo, Texas , and the surrounding Texas Panhandle. Founded by Dr. Chris Brady and his wife, Ronnie , on July 5, 1977 , the practice has grown over the decades while staying true to its core value: “Our patients are family.”

Dr. Brady has been practicing dentistry since 1977. In 2007, his daughter, Dr. Kimberly Brady Gilbert , along with Dr. Laura Turner joined the practice, and more recently, Dr. Jordan Short joined in 2022, followed by Dr. Shealee Allen in 2023. The entire team is welcoming, knowledgeable, and passionate about making every patient feel comfortable and cared for.

Our Story

With over 90 years of combined experience , Brady Dental Group has been providing high-quality dental care to Amarillo and the surrounding Texas Panhandle for generations.

When the practice first opened, Dr. Brady provided dental care while Ronnie managed all other aspects of the office. They often worked Fridays, weekends, and evenings at Dr. Carter Karr’s office. As the practice grew, Dr. Brady moved to 2815 S. Georgia St. , offering full-time care Monday through Friday.

In 1985, Dr. Brady partnered with Dr. Dan Bentley , moving the practice to its current location at 2915 S. Georgia St. Their partnership was unique for its time—sharing office space and equipment while maintaining two separate practices—built entirely on trust, mutual respect, and a shared faith in the Lord .

In 2015, Dr. Brady decided to scale back and sold the practice to Dr. Gilbert and Dr. Turner , remaining on as an associate. Today, he enjoys joking with patients that his daughter is his boss while continuing to provide outstanding dental care to the community he has served for decades.

Our Philosophy

At Brady Dental Group, every patient is treated like family. The team is dedicated to delivering the highest quality dental care while fostering meaningful, lasting relationships. With deep roots in the Texas Panhandle, Brady Dental Group remains a family-owned practice , committed to serving generations of patients with compassion and expertise.
