Meet The Team

With 34 years of experience as a dental hygienist, my career began at Indiana State University before continuing my dental education at Indiana University Northwest. Originally from Crown Point near Lakes of the Four Seasons, I have called Kouts home for the past 24 years. My husband and I have been married for 36 years, and we have two grown children and a golden retriever.

Outside the office, I enjoy hiking, traveling, reading, and spending time with family and friends. The most rewarding part of dental hygiene is getting to know patients and helping improve both their oral health and confidence in their smiles. My interest in dentistry began in third grade after getting braces, which I wore for five years. I decided to become a hygienist after seeing mine arrive at work in a bright red sports car! I’m still waiting on the sports car, but the wonderful coworkers and patients I’ve met have more than made up for it!

As a lifelong resident of Jasper County, I love small-town life and look forward to raising a family here one day. I grew up in DeMotte and graduated from Kankakee Valley High School in 2015. After graduation, I attended Indiana University Northwest in Gary, earning my bachelor’s degree in dental hygiene in 2019.

My high school sweetheart and I were married on September 20, 2025, at The Highland Barn in Rensselaer, and we now live in our first home in Wheatfield. While we don’t have children yet, we share our home with three much-loved cats: Sylvester, Simon, and Smoki. In my free time, I enjoy being outdoors and spending time with friends and family. Working in dentistry is incredibly rewarding because it allows me to meet new people every day, build meaningful relationships with patients, and make a positive difference in their oral and overall health.
