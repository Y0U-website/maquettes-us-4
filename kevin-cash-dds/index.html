Dr. Kevin Cash is proud to call Indiana home. A Crown Point native, Dr. Cash completed his undergraduate studies at Wabash College before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Marquette University School of Dentistry.

To fund his dental education, Dr. Cash honorably served in the United States Military for four years, including a nine-month deployment to the Middle East, with postings in Georgia and El Paso, Texas. His time in the military instilled in him a deep commitment to service, which is a value he carries into his dental practice every day. It was also during his service that he met his wife, Dessaray.

Dr. Cash is excited to serve the Rensselaer community, where he is dedicated to providing exceptional, patient-centered care. He believes every patient deserves to feel comfortable, well-informed, and confident in their smile.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Cash enjoys being an active member of the community he is grateful to call home.
