Do mini dental implants last?

Mini dental implants, like conventional implants, are an excellent way to restore missing teeth. The titanium posts inserted into the jaw can last for a lifetime. With adequate care and maintenance, the abutment crown can survive 15 to 25 years. Dental implants are manufactured to last a long time with Dr. Manali Shah DDS ’s expertise and knowledge.

Are mini dental implants as good as regular implants?

Mini dental implants work just like traditional implants. Although these types of implants are smaller (around 2.5mm in diameter) and it is excellent for areas in the mouth where there is not enough room for traditional dental implants or it can cause severe bone loss. At Summit Smiles you can choose between conventional and mini dental implants.

Do mini implants break?

Although conventional implants are designed to be as sturdy as natural teeth, they are composed of multiple components. The replacement tooth is made of titanium and inserted into the jawbone. When correctly integrated into the jawbone for over numerous months, it may even be more durable than the original tooth. But the abutment screw can be broken or bent if there is trauma in the mouth. The dental crown can be strong but it can break if it has applied the same force needed to break a natural tooth.

How long after extraction can I get mini implants?

In most situations, a recovery period is required following tooth extraction, as the hole created by the tooth extraction is bigger in diameter than the diameter of the mini dental implant.
