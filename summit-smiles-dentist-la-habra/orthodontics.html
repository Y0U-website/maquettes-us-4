When is the best time to begin orthodontic treatment?

Most orthodontic problems will occur at ages nine to fourteen years old. At this stage of your child’s growth, the majority of permanent teeth have erupted and the primary teeth have been lost. However, due to the developing mouth of the kid, Dr. Manali Shah, DDS thinks that treating severe issues like as an severe crowding, crossbite, overbite, underbite, and protruded teeth at this time is more comfortable.

How long will the procedure take?

The duration of treatment will be determined by a number of criteria, including the severity of the condition and any prior treatment required. Orthodontic therapy typically lasts between 12 and 36 months. To determine the duration of your treatment, please contact our Summit Smiles office .

How frequently will I need to make an appointment?

Appointments are scheduled according to the patient’s specific requirements. Patients who are wearing braces, for instance, should visit their dentist every six to eight weeks. Certain orthodontists will demand more frequent visits, particularly if the type of therapy requires frequent monitoring.

Will I be able to continue participating in sports or playing my instrument?

Yes. You can still play with your musical treatment and it won’t affect your performance. However, there will be an adjustment period after getting your braces. It is possible to participate in sports as well, however, we suggest wearing a mouth guard to shield your orthodontic device from possible outside impact.

Orthodontics Near Me

Finding an orthodontist who is close to your home, work, or school is necessary if you want to save time and money. You should anticipate a few follow-up appointments in addition to your usual visits in the future. Having a dental practice nearby simplifies matters. Furthermore, look for dental specialists who have high orthodontic evaluations and with whom you feel comfortable working.
