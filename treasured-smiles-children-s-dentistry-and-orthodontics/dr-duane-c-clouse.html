Dr. Duane C. Clouse, DDS - Your Child’s Dental Hero

At the helm of Treasured Smiles Children’s Dentistry is one of the top pediatric dentists in the field, Dr. Duane Clouse. Keep reading to learn about his journey to becoming a pediatric dental specialist and why he’s the perfect fit for your child’s dental care.

Early Roots in Dentistry and Education

Born in Chicago, Illinois to a father finishing dental school, Dr. Clouse was raised in Mesa, Arizona and attended Arizona State University. Following in his father’s footsteps, he pursued dentistry and graduated from Creighton University School of Dentistry in Omaha, Nebraska in 1991, earning the Preventive Dentistry Award.

A Passion for Pediatric Dentistry

After practicing general dentistry for 12 years, Dr. Clouse realized his true passion was working with children and decided to specialize in pediatric dentistry. He was accepted as a resident at Temple University Children’s Medical Center in Philadelphia, a top program for sedation dentistry, where he earned the Clinician Award for his exceptional dental skills. He earned his certification in pediatric dentistry in 2005 and has been board certified since 2009.

Professional Memberships and Personal Interests

Dr. Clouse is an active member of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Arizona Dental Association. He enjoys outdoor activities, spending time at the cabin, and supporting Arizona State University football. With roots in Arizona dating back five generations, Dr. Clouse provides exceptional dental care to children in Maricopa and the surrounding areas.
