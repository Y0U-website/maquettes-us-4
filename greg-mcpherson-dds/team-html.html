Meet the Doctor

Dr. Greg McPherson, DDS - Your New Town St. Charles Dentist

After bringing excellent dental care to the people of eastern Montana for 8 years, Dr. Greg McPherson recently opened his practice in New Town at St. Charles, just 35 minutes from downtown St. Louis. Greg lives in New Town with his wife, Bridget, and two children, Nathaniel and Nora.

Greg received his dental training at the University of Michigan, graduating in 2001. Continuing his education is very important to him. He typically takes several times the required amount of continuing education hours every year. He has had extensive post-graduate training at the prestigious Las Vegas Institute in the areas of Neuromuscular Dentistry , Complex Restorative Dentistry , and practice management.

DDS : University of Michigan, School of Dentistry, 2001

BS : Biology, University of Michigan, 1997

Member : Missouri Dental Association, Montana Dental Association, American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry
