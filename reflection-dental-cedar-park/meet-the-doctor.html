Meet Dr. Ki Chung

Dr. Ki Chung grew up in Los Angeles, where he received his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Southern California (USC). During dental school, Dr. Chung discovered his passion for dentistry while participating in humanitarian trips to Honduras and Colombia, as well as providing care to the homeless population in downtown Los Angeles. Being able to help someone in need and resolve their dental concerns is one of his greatest joys.

Dr. Chung has been practicing dentistry since 2013. He is extremely detail-oriented and committed to ensuring every patient leaves happy with their experience. His friendly nature and sense of humor help put patients at ease and leave a smile on their face. Outside the office, he enjoys running, hiking, snowboarding, traveling, exploring new cuisines, and playing basketball.

Meet Dr. Kevin Min

Dr. Kevin Min was born in South Korea and comes from a multicultural, multilingual background. He has lived in Asia, New Zealand, and Canada, which has shaped his love for exploring new travel destinations and different cuisines. Dr. Min earned his dental degree in Cleveland and lived near Chicago before moving down to Austin. He has been practicing dentistry since 2016 and is enjoying the slower-paced, country-style living while always looking for new outdoor activities to try.
