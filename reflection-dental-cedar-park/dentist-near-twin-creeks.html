Single Tooth Implant Placement

Lost a tooth? One implant is a long-term solution that appears, feels, and functions the same as your natural tooth. It also saves bone loss and moving nearby teeth, maintaining your oral health.

Crowns

Crowns work well for fixing teeth that are weakened, decayed, or cracked after a root canal. Crowns sit on top of the tooth’s remaining structure to give it structure and yet have it still fit in your smile.
