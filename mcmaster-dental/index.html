Combining His Love of Science & People Dr. Todd McMaster, DDS, is the owner and family dentist at McMaster Dental in Escondido, CA. With more than 25 years of experience, Dr. McMaster is committed to providing honest and ethical treatment, taking ample time with each patient to explain every treatment in detail.

His goal is to alleviate his patients’ pain or discomfort, allowing everyone to leave the practice happy and smiling.

Dr. Todd has been married for 25 years to his wife, Deon, and they have four children, Jessica, Zach (who is also a dentist with us!), Jordyn, and Jaycee, and eight grandchildren. They reside in San Marcos. Dr. McMaster enjoys weightlifting, skiing, and snowboarding and can frequently be found on his mountain bike!
