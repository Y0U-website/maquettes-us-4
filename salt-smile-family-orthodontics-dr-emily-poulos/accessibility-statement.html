Salt + Smile Family Orthodontics Accessibility Statement

At Salt + Smile Family Orthodontics, we are committed to providing a website experience that is accessible and inclusive for all visitors. We believe everyone deserves equal access to information about our practice and services, regardless of ability or the technology they use.

We strive to design and maintain our website in accordance with recognized accessibility best practices and are continually working to improve the usability of our digital content for all users.

Our Commitment

  • Provide content that is clear and easy to navigate.
  • Support keyboard navigation where possible.
  • Use readable fonts, sufficient color contrast, and descriptive headings.
  • Include alternative text for meaningful images when appropriate.
  • Continuously evaluate and improve our website s accessibility.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website or have suggestions for improving accessibility, we encourage you to contact us. We value your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or assistance you need in an accessible format.

Additionally, reserved handicap parking spaces will be available. At the office we strive to provide a fully ADA compliant healthcare experience from the moment you enter, to the front desk, restroom, facilities, x-ray equipment and clinical areas.

If you find any of our practice does not meet your needs, please reach out so we can make every effort to accommodate your needs.
