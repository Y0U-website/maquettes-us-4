Could Dental Implants Be Right for You?

Dental implants may be an option if you are missing one or more teeth, have a tooth that cannot be predictably saved, or are frustrated with a loose or uncomfortable denture.

You may benefit from an implant evaluation if you:

Have a missing tooth and want a fixed replacement Have a broken, infected or severely damaged tooth Are considering an alternative to a traditional bridge Have several missing teeth Have difficulty chewing because of tooth loss Wear a denture that moves, rubs or feels unstable Have been told you may need a bone graft before receiving an implant Want to understand your options before making a decision

Not every patient needs the same treatment. Your recommendation will depend on factors such as your oral health, bone support, medical history, the number and location of missing teeth, and your personal goals.

At Seneca Dental Care, we begin with a thorough evaluation and use 3D imaging when appropriate to assess the area more closely. We will explain what we find, discuss the available options and help you understand the expected treatment sequence before you decide how to proceed.

You Do Not Have to Know Which Implant You Need

Many patients contact us without knowing whether they need a single implant, an implant-supported bridge, a bone graft or help stabilizing a denture. That is completely normal.

The purpose of the consultation is to determine which options may be appropriate for your situation and answer your questions about treatment, timing and cost.

Eat the Foods You Enjoy Again

Dental implants provide stable support for replacement teeth, helping many patients chew more comfortably than they can with a missing tooth or a loose denture. Whether you’re enjoying crunchy vegetables, steak or your favorite snacks, implants can help restore confidence at mealtime.

Replace Missing Teeth Without Relying on Neighboring Teeth

Unlike a traditional dental bridge, a single dental implant replaces the missing tooth without requiring healthy neighboring teeth to be reshaped for support. This conservative approach helps preserve your natural teeth while restoring the appearance and function of your smile.

Improve Denture Stability

If your denture slips, shifts or makes eating and speaking difficult, implant-supported dentures may provide a more secure fit. Dental implants can help stabilize certain dentures, allowing many patients to smile, eat and speak with greater confidence.

Which Dental Implant Option Is Right for Me?

Every patient has different needs depending on the number of missing teeth, the health of the surrounding bone and gums, and their long-term goals. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your smile and discuss which treatment options may be appropriate for your situation.

What is a Single Tooth Implant?

A single tooth implant may be recommended for patients who have lost one tooth due to decay, injury, gum disease or a failed root canal. It can replace a missing tooth without affecting the healthy teeth on either side.

Benefits

A single implant is designed to look, feel and function like a natural tooth. It helps restore your smile, improves chewing ability and avoids the need to reshape neighboring teeth as is often required for a traditional bridge.

When are Single Tooth Implants Recommended?

A single tooth implant may be appropriate when the surrounding teeth are healthy and there is adequate bone to support the implant. During your consultation, we’ll determine whether an implant or another treatment option is the best choice for your needs.

What should I do if I have Multiple Missing Teeth?

If you are missing several teeth, dental implants can often provide a more secure and comfortable solution than removable appliances alone. Depending on your situation, one or more implants may be used to support a bridge or other type of restoration.

What is an Implant-Supported Bridge?

Instead of replacing every missing tooth with an individual implant, an implant-supported bridge uses strategically placed implants to support several replacement teeth. This approach may reduce the number of implants needed while restoring function and appearance.

What is an Implant-Supported Denture?

Many patients become frustrated with dentures that slip while eating or speaking. Implant-supported dentures use dental implants to help hold the denture more securely in place.

Snap-In Dentures

Snap-in dentures attach to dental implants using special connectors, allowing the denture to "snap" into position. They remain removable for cleaning while providing improved retention compared with many traditional dentures.

Greater Stability

Implant-supported dentures may reduce unwanted movement during daily activities such as eating, laughing and speaking. Many patients find they feel more confident because their denture is more secure.

What is Full Arch Implant Treatment?

For patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one arch, full-arch implant treatment may provide an alternative to a traditional removable denture. Several dental implants are placed to support a full arch of replacement teeth.

When would I benefit from full arch dental implants?

This treatment may be appropriate for patients with extensive tooth loss, failing teeth or those who want a more secure alternative to conventional dentures. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your oral health, available bone and treatment goals to determine whether full-arch implant treatment is an appropriate option.

What should I expect During my implant consultation?

Your dental implant consultation is designed to help you understand your oral health, determine whether dental implants may be an appropriate treatment option, and answer your questions before making any decisions. Our goal is to provide clear recommendations and help you feel informed throughout the process.

Comprehensive Examination

Your visit begins with a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, bite and overall oral health. We’ll discuss your dental history, any concerns you have and your goals for replacing missing teeth.

Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays allow us to evaluate the health of your teeth and the surrounding bone while using significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays. These images help us identify conditions that may affect your treatment plan.

3D CBCT Imaging When Appropriate

For many implant cases, we use advanced 3D cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) imaging to evaluate the quality and quantity of available bone and visualize important anatomical structures. This detailed information helps us determine whether additional procedures, such as bone grafting, may be recommended and assists with treatment planning.

Personalized Treatment Discussion

After reviewing your examination and imaging, we’ll explain our findings in easy-to-understand language. We’ll discuss your treatment options, answer your questions and review the advantages and considerations of each option so you can make an informed decision.

Customized Treatment Plan

If dental implants are an appropriate option, we’ll develop a personalized treatment plan based on your oral health, the number of missing teeth and your treatment goals. We’ll explain each phase of treatment, the expected timeline and any procedures that may be recommended before implant placement.

Cost, Insurance and Financing Options

Before treatment begins, we’ll review the estimated cost of your care, explain any applicable insurance benefits and discuss available financing options, including CareCredit and Cherry , for qualified patients. Our team is happy to answer your questions so you can make a decision that fits your needs and budget.
