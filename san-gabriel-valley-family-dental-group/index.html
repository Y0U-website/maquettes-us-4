If you are missing a tooth and are looking for a long-lasting, natural-looking replacement, dental implants are a fantastic option. Implants are used to replace teeth to restore function as well as aesthetics. Implants are beneficial in preventing a sunken look to the face which can happen when there are missing teeth. They typically last 10 years and can last much longer with proper care.
