About Adult Orthodontics

Not at all! Healthy teeth can be moved at any age. In fact, more adults than ever are choosing orthodontic treatment to improve their smiles and oral health. Whether you’re in your 30s, 50s, or beyond — it’s never too late to get started.

The main difference is growth. Teens are still developing, which can affect treatment timing. Adults have fully developed jaws, so treatment focuses solely on moving the teeth. You may also have more complex dental needs — like previous dental work or mild gum issues — which we always consider in your custom treatment plan.

Not if you don’t want to! We offer several discreet options for adult orthodontics , including clear ceramic braces , champagne-colored braces , and clear aligners like Invisalign® . We’ll help you choose what fits your goals and lifestyle best.

Treatment times vary depending on your unique case, but most adult patients wear braces or aligners for 12 to 24 months . Some simpler cases may take less time, and we’ll give you an accurate estimate during your consultation.

Our adult patients are often surprised by how easy treatment fits into their routine. Clear braces and aligners are subtle, and appointments are quick and spaced out every few weeks. Clear aligners are especially convenient — you can remove them for important meetings or events.
