Digital X-Rays

We use low-dose digital X-rays to get detailed images of your teeth, roots, and jaw structure. These images help us diagnose alignment issues, monitor growth, and plan treatment safely and effectively.

  • Up to 90 % less radiation than traditional film
  • Instant imaging — no waiting or processing
  • Clearer results for more accurate planning
  • Easily shareable with your general dentist if needed
