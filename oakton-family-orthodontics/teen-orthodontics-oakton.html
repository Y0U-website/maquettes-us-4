About Braces & Clear Aligners for Teens

Most teens begin treatment between ages 11 and 15 , once most or all of their permanent teeth have come in. This is a great time to address alignment and bite issues while your teen is still growing, which often leads to faster and more effective results.

Braces are fixed to the teeth and work 24/7, which is great for teens who may need more guidance. Clear aligners (like Invisalign®) are removable and nearly invisible — perfect for responsible teens who want a discreet option. We’ll help you choose the right fit during your consultation.
