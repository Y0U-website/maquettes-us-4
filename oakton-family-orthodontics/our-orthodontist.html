Dr. Helena Kilic is Our Wonderful…

After dental school, Dr. Kilic practiced general dentistry for 2 years prior to pursuing her passion in orthodontics at Seton Hill Center for Orthodontics, where she earned both a Master of Science and a Master in Business Administration in 2016 .

Dr. Kilic had the privilege to have worked alongside the renowned orthodontist Dr. Neal Kravitz and Dr. Kevin McGrath, with whom she collaborated on patient care for many years. Dr. Kilic brings all of that expertise, patient focused care and compassion to her patients at Oakton Family Orthodontics.

Dr. Kilic is a board-certified orthodontist since 2018 and a proud member of: More Than a Smile: Meet the Heart Behind Oakton’s Trusted Orthodontist Dr. Kilic is passionate about helping patients restore their confidence and self-esteem through a new, beautiful, and healthy smile. Getting to know her patients and their families — and being part of the Oakton community — is one of her favorite parts of being an orthodontist.

She has a genuine passion for orthodontics and strives to educate her patients on available treatment options , working together to get the very best outcome. She enjoys serving and supporting her Northern Virginia communities, schools and businesses.

Dr. Kilic has been living and practicing in Northern Virginia with her husband, Dan and their son Theo. Together, they enjoy exploring D.C.’s museums, trying new restaurants, and spending quality time with their family. When she’s not creating beautiful smiles, you’ll likely find Dr. Kilic in a spin class, traveling, or enjoying time with family and friends.

Personalized Care from a Doctor Who Truly Cares

Dr. Kilic believes that every smile is unique — and so is every patient. That’s why she takes time to really listen, explain, and build a treatment plan that feels right for each individual and their family. Whether you’re exploring early orthodontic care for your child or you’re an adult finally ready to prioritize your smile, you can trust Dr. Kilic to guide you with honesty, compassion, and expert care.

She combines her scientific background, clinical excellence, and warm personality to make sure every patient feels comfortable and confident — from the first visit to the final smile reveal.
