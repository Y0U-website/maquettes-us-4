Invisalign takes a modern approach to straightening teeth, using a custom-made series of aligners created for you and only you. These aligner trays are made of smooth, comfortable, and virtually invisible plastic that you simply wear over your teeth. Wearing the aligners will gradually and gently shift your teeth into place, based on the exact movements determined in your treatment plan .

What Makes a Good Invisalign Candidate?

Many patients will wonder which choice is right for them: traditional braces or Invisalign? While traditional braces are known to have the ability to align all teeth, no matter how severe the case is, Invisalign is more for moderate cases. Specifically, Invisalign is good for patients with the following dental issues:

Any patients with mild to moderate malocclusions
