We’re More Than Just a Dentist, We’re Family.

Dental services are an important part in helping to maintain good oral hygiene. Without dental services, many of us would have different smiles than we have today. Dental services cover everything from replacing a missing tooth to removing decaying teeth and replacing them with implants that function and look the same as normal teeth.

Dental Services We Offer

When seeking out a professional dental office, you want to ensure you choose a facility with advanced technology and educated staff members. In addition, you want a place that can offer a full range of services, so that no matter what particular dental service you need, they will be able to help. At Warrenton Dental Center, we offer a wide range of dental services, including:

Full Mouth Reconstruction: If you have experienced lots of tooth decay, been in an accident or trauma, or have had oral cancer, you may need full mouth reconstruction. This involves replacing any missing teeth, and ensuring all your teeth are functioning as they should while looking physically attractive and natural.

General Dentistry: From checkups every six months, to checking for cavities and screening for oral cancer, these services help to ensure patients are progressing well. General dentistry services also help you to maintain a healthy smile by educating you on what you can do better to minimize cavities and disease.

Cosmetic Dentistry: These services help to enhance your smile and make it more aesthetically pleasing. This typically involves whitening, replacing missing or chipped teeth with veneers, and more.

Porcelain Veneers: If you are looking for a way to cover a chipped tooth, look no further. Porcelain veneers ensure that your tooth’s original state is restored, chipped-free.

Nite-Guide: Children from 5-7 years of age can use this to help minimize the chances of needing braces or Invisalign in the future. This dental intervention aids in shaping a child’s teeth and correcting their bite, before it gets worse with age.

Oral Surgery: There are a number of reasons a patient may need oral surgery. From tooth extractions to bone grafting, oral surgery involves a procedure where a patient undergoes anesthesia in order to get the dental service they need.

Tooth Extractions: Tooth extractions are extremely common. No matter if you have an overcrowded mouth or a decayed/diseased tooth, tooth extractions can get the job done so you only have room for healthy teeth.

Bone Grafting: Dental interventions like implants often require a strong jawbone that can usually withstand daily functions. Sometimes, patients have a weakened jawbone and need a bone grafting procedure in order to restore that healthy, strong jawbone.

Sleep Apnea: This is a common sleep disorder that involves a cessation of breathing during sleep. Fortunately, there are a number of interventions that can help.

TMJ: This is a painful disorder that involves comprised movement within the jawbone. If you are experiencing TMJ disorder, you can rest easy knowing that Warrenton Dental Center can help find a solution right for you.
