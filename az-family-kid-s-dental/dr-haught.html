Dr Haught

He is fluent in Portuguese and speaks some spanish. He married his beautiful wife Heather in 2002 and currently have 2 boys and 2 girls. He graduated from Arizona State University with a B.S. in Nutrition. Soon after he moved to Fort Lauderdale, FL to attend dental school at Nova Southeastern University. His first couple of years of practicing dentistry he worked in family practices in Arizona and Texas. For the past several years he has focused his career to working solely with children.

Dr. Haught has a true passion and talent working with kids, including children with special needs. His twins both have autism and his youngest has ADHD. He loves helping and educating kids with their oral health. Dr Haught and his family enjoy the outdoors including hiking, hunting, fishing, camping, and skiing.
