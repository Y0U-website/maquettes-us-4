The Benefits of Full-Arch Implant Rehabilitation

Long-Lasting Results Full-arch implant restorations offer a long-lasting solution for patients with extensive tooth loss. With proper oral hygiene and routine dental care, dental implants can remain healthy and functional for numerous years or more—and in many cases, they last a lifetime.

Better Oral Health Full-arch implant restorations do more than replace missing teeth—they also help preserve your jawbone and facial structure. When teeth are lost, the jawbone can begin to shrink over time, leading to a sunken or aged appearance. Unlike dentures, dental implants are anchored in the bone, providing natural stimulation that helps prevent bone loss. This not only supports long-term oral health but also helps maintain your appearance and confidence.

Improved Fit, Comfort, And Functionality Dentures can begin to shift or slip over time as the shape of your jaw changes, which can make it harder to eat, speak, or smile with full confidence. Full-arch implant restorations offer a more stable, long-term solution. Because they’re securely anchored to dental implants, they stay firmly in place and won’t move or loosen with time. This means a more natural appearance, better function, and greater comfort in your everyday life.

The Full-Arch Implant Rehabilitation Process Tooth Extractions & Implant Placement Your journey begins with a comprehensive consultation and 3D imaging. We’ll evaluate your bone structure, review any remaining teeth, and create a personalized surgical plan. If full-arch rehabilitation is right for you, we’ll coordinate with your dentist to ensure seamless care.

Tooth Removal & Implant Placement

In a single surgical visit, we’ll remove any remaining teeth in the arch and place 4 to 6 dental implants with precision. In many cases, a temporary prosthesis is secured the same day, giving you immediate function and a confident smile during healing.

Healing & Final Smile Restoration

As your implants integrate with your jawbone over the next few months, we will monitor your healing closely. Once healing is complete, your general dentist will place your final restoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

Am I a good candidate for a full-arch implant rehabilitation?

Full-arch implant rehabilitation is an excellent treatment option for patients who have lost all or most of their natural teeth due to periodontal disease, extensive cavities, or dental trauma. However, successful outcomes depend on having a healthy oral environment and sufficient jawbone density to support the implants. Get in touch with our team today to schedule a consultation.

Do full-arch dental implants look and feel like natural teeth?

Yes, full-arch implant restorations are made from strong, high-quality materials and are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They allow you to speak, smile, and eat comfortably—without the worry of shifting like you might experience with other types of prosthetics. With good daily oral hygiene and regular checkups with your general dentist, your restoration can remain functional for many years.

Can full-arch implant restorations be done in one day?

In some cases, yes. Depending on the extent of the extent of any pre-treatment care, some patients may be eligible to have implants placed and a temporary restoration delivered in a single appointment. However, for more complex cases, additional healing time between surgical stages may be required to ensure long-term treatment success.
