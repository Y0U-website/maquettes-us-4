The practice

Piedmont Implant and Oral Surgery isn’t just another surgical center — it’s a practice built on innovation, community trust, and patient-centered excellence. We partnered with local Bay Area small businesses to thoughtfully design our space crafted with the patient experience in mind.

Dental implants are made from biocompatible materials and are surgically placed into the jawbone, where they integrate to provide a stable foundation for a custom crown. Dental implants and full-arch dental implants are an excellent solution for tooth loss, providing a secure, lifelike replacement that looks and feels just like your natural teeth.

Implants Can Last A Lifetime With consistent care—including daily brushing and flossing, along with regular visits to your general dentist every six months for routine cleanings—dental implants can last for many years. In fact, many patients are able to keep their implants for the rest of their lives.

Dental Implants Look & Feel Completely Natural Your dental implant is designed to function like a natural tooth. It doesn&#x27;t require any special care beyond regular brushing, flossing, and maintaining your routine dental cleanings every six months with your general dentist.

Your Implant Protects Your Oral Health Dental implants prevent common issues after tooth loss, such as teeth shifting. Uniquely, they also protect your jaw bone. They stimulate the bone and prevent it from weakening over time. Other treatments like bridges and dentures don’t do this, so your jaw bone could continue to weaken over time.

Dr. Han has successfully restored hundreds of full arches using advanced techniques like zygomatic and pterygoid implants—offering life-changing results even in the most complex cases.

We use Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) and Bone Morphogenetic Proteins (BMP) to support faster, natural bone regeneration—improving both surgical outcomes and healing timelines for grafting and implant procedures.

X-Guide navigation offers real-time, 3D surgical guidance for extraordinary accuracy in implant placement. With less invasive incisions and greater predictability, patients experience quicker healing and long-term functionality.

Services

Dentures rest on the gums and can shift during eating and speaking. All-on-4 implants are fixed in place and function like real teeth. This means better comfort, stronger biting, and greater confidence. There’s no need for adhesive or removal at night.

We manage impacted, infected, or complex wisdom tooth extractions with skill and gentle care, keeping your comfort and post-op recovery top of mind every step of the way.

From single-tooth implants to full arch restorations, we offer the most advanced solutions for missing teeth — including zygomatic, pterygoid, and trans-sinus implant options for complex cases.
