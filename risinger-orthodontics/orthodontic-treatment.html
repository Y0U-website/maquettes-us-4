Types of Orthodontic Treatment & Appliances Getting the best orthodontic treatment means individualized plans that are customized to your lifestyle. At Risinger Orthodontics, we want your treatment to make sense for your life .

We offer many types of orthodontic treatment options, including:

  • Crooked teeth
  • Gapped teeth
  • Misaligned teeth
  • Missing teeth
  • Persistent headaches

Invisalign

As an Invisalign Diamond Provider, we know how to use Invisalign to give you superior results. Invisalign’s invisible, removable, and comfortable aligners are designed with your freedom in mind. We offer the power of Invisalign for adults, teens , and children.

Braces

Braces are a time-honored method of smile correction. Contemporary braces have come a long way from the clunky and unattractive braces you remember from growing up. Our stylish metal and clear braces are available for adults , teens, and children.

Early Treatment

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children start seeing an orthodontist for check-ins by the time they are seven. At Risinger Orthodontics, we offer complimentary appointments to catch minor concerns before they become major issues.

Retainers

You will need to wear a retainer after you complete your initial treatment. A retainer will make sure that your mouth keeps its proper alignment and that your teeth don’t drift from their new location. We offer permanent and removable retainers.
