At What Age Can Children Get Braces?

Much like the question of what is the best age to get braces , this question is a little tricky to answer. There is no correct age for seeking orthodontic treatment. That said, it’s a good idea to have your child taken in for an orthodontic evaluation by the time they are seven .

Treatment for most children typically begins around ages 10-14. However, depending on the child’s needs, they may start as soon as seven-eight years old. While most children won’t need treatment at this age, yearly check-ins allow you to develop a relationship with an orthodontist you trust . That way, if the time comes to start treatment, you’ll already have a working relationship with an orthodontist.

How To Care For Your Children’s Braces

Once your child has started braces treatment, it is important that you properly care for their appliance. Here are some of our tips for taking care of a child’s braces:

  • Make sure your child is brushing their teeth after every time they eat. Brushing with fluoride-rich toothpaste and a soft-bristled brush helps loosen food and debris that braces may trap.
  • Remind your child to floss every day. Tools like a floss threader may help access hard-to-clean areas under the archwire.
  • Avoiding foods that are sugary or starchy. These types of foods can damage the appliance. Even if your child is wearing aligners, indulging in these types of snacks can affect their outcome.
  • Follow any other guidelines presented to you by your orthodontist.
