When Should Your Child See an Orthodontist?

Children don’t need to wait until all permanent teeth come in to benefit from orthodontic care. In fact, early evaluations can identify concerns before they become more serious. Here are common signs your child may benefit from pediatric orthodontic treatment:

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an evaluation by age 7, as this is an ideal time to identify developing concerns. Early attention allows us to guide growth, reduce complications, and often shorten future treatment.

  • Crowded, crooked, or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps or spacing issues between teeth
  • Bite problems (overbite, underbite, crossbite)
  • Jaw imbalance or shifting
  • Difficulty chewing or speaking clearly
  • Mouth breathing or improper oral posture
  • Early or delayed loss of baby teeth
  • Permanent teeth erupting out of position
  • Thumb sucking or oral habits beyond early childhood
  • Family history of orthodontic issues
