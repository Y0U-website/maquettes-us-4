Straight Teeth Are Healthier Teeth

Orthodontic treatment is about more than a confident smile. Properly aligned teeth contribute to better oral health at any age. For adults, correcting misalignments can alleviate stress on jaw joints, relieve overcrowding for easier oral hygiene, and prevent abnormal tooth wear.

For children, addressing dental alignment early is crucial for their oral development, speech, and the ability to maintain proper dental hygiene. At Sugar Creek Smiles, we agree with the American Association of Orthodontists, which recommends an orthodontic evaluation by age seven to assess the need for future treatment.

SureSmile Clear Aligners for Discreet Treatment

SureSmile clear aligners are similar to Invisalign and designed for those who prefer a less noticeable orthodontic treatment. Ideal for teens and adults, these aligners correct overcrowding, gaps, and bite issues with a series of custom-made, virtually invisible trays. Aside from perfecting your smile discreetly, clear aligner trays are comfortable and removable for eating and oral hygiene.

The process for getting clear aligners at our Crawfordsville dental office includes the following steps:

  • Consultation We begin with a detailed assessment of your dental alignment needs.
  • Imaging Advanced imaging technology helps us create your personalized treatment plan.
  • Custom Aligners Several sets of custom-fit aligners are crafted to shift your teeth into the desired position gradually.
  • Regular Check-Ins We ll monitor your progress and make adjustments as necessary.

Traditional Braces: Time-Tested and Effective

Traditional braces are a powerful tool for correcting more complex dental issues and are suitable for patients of all ages. Using a system of brackets and wires, traditional braces apply consistent pressure to teeth, guiding them into alignment over time. They are highly effective for a wide range of corrections, from simple adjustments to more significant transformations.

Frequently Asked Questions

How effective are clear aligners compared to traditional braces?

Clear aligners are as effective as traditional braces for many orthodontic issues. At Sugar Creek Smiles, we provide both options to suit diverse needs and lifestyles.

Clear Aligner Advantages

Clear aligners have revolutionized orthodontic treatment because they are discreet and removable. They are designed to treat a variety of dental misalignments effectively, such as overcrowded teeth, uneven spacing and gaps, and specific bite issues. Aligners are custom-fitted to your teeth, making them comfortable and less noticeable than metal braces.

Comparative Effectiveness with Traditional Braces

Traditional braces are a time-tested solution for correcting complex orthodontic problems. They can address more severe misalignments and malocclusions that clear aligners may be unable to fix. However, clear aligners offer convenience and discretion that appeal to adults and teens.

Aligning Expectations with Treatment Goals

The effectiveness of clear aligners depends on your specific orthodontic needs and commitment to wearing the aligners as prescribed. At Sugar Creek Smiles, our experienced team ensures that you receive the most appropriate treatment for your situation, whether clear aligners or traditional braces.
