How Long Does the Implant Process Take?

Getting a dental implant begins with an evaluation using state-of-the-art CBCT technology for precise planning. Implant placement is performed comfortably under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort.

Next, a process called osseointegration occurs. As the jawbone heals around the implant post, they fuse so the implant is as stable as a natural tooth root, maybe more so. Healing times vary but typically take 3 6 months. The final step is the placement of your custom tooth restoration to restore function and appearance.

Factors Affecting Dental Implant Longevity

A dental implant’s lifespan is influenced by several factors, including oral hygiene practices, lifestyle choices, and the expertise of the dental professional overseeing their placement. Implants require diligent at-home care and regular dental checkups to ensure they remain in good condition.

Poor gum health, smoking, or uncontrolled diabetes can compromise implant longevity and emphasize the importance of overall health in maintaining your implants.
