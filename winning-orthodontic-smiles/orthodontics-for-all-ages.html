Signs to Look for in Your Child s Smile

Here are a few key indicators to look for to know if your child needs orthodontic treatment:

  • Crowded or missing teeth
  • Premature or delayed loss of baby teeth
  • Challenges in biting or chewing
  • Speech difficulties
  • Prolonged pacifier use
  • Narrow palate
  • Underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite

The Importance of Treatment

Empowering Your Teenager

A healthy smile can have a positive impact on every aspect of a teenager s life. From school presentations and social events to sports and extracurricular activities, confidence matters.

Orthodontic treatment helps teens feel great about their smiles while building self-esteem during important developmental years.

Ageless Solutions for Straighter Smiles

Think orthodontic treatment is only for kids and teens? Think again.

Today, more adults than ever are choosing orthodontic treatment to improve their smiles, confidence, and oral health. Whether you ve experienced shifting teeth over time or never had the opportunity to receive treatment earlier in life, we can help.

Reasons for Adult Orthodontics

Look Good & Feel Great

Many adults seek treatment to address concerns they ve had for years.

Straightening crooked teeth, closing gaps, and improving smile aesthetics can boost confidence both professionally and personally.

Teeth Continue to Shift

Even if your teeth were once straight, they can move over time.

Changes in alignment may lead to crowding, bite problems, or difficulty maintaining proper oral hygiene. Orthodontic treatment can restore balance and improve comfort.

Better Oral Health

Properly aligned teeth are easier to brush and floss effectively.

By correcting misalignment, orthodontic treatment may reduce the risk of gum disease, tooth decay, and uneven tooth wear while supporting long-term dental health.

Benefits of Adult Treatment

Discreet Treatment Options

Today s orthodontic solutions are more flexible and discreet than ever before.

Adults can choose from clear aligners, clear braces, or modern self-ligating braces depending on their goals and lifestyle preferences.

Personalized Care

No two adult patients have the same smile goals.

Whether you re correcting relapse from previous treatment or starting orthodontics for the first time, our doctors create customized plans tailored to your unique needs.
