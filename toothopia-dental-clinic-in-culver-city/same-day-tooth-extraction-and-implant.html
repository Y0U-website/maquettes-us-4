Our Same-Day Implant Process at Toothopia Dental

Same-day dental implants follow a clear, planned sequence that we map out before any surgery begins. Here is how a typical visit unfolds:

Full fusion of the implant with your bone, called osseointegration, usually takes a few months, but the visible part of your smile is restored on day one.

Ready to see your timeline? Book a consultation, and our team will walk you through each step before anything begins.

What Patients Ask About Same-Day Tooth Replacement

Most single same-day implant appointments take about one to two hours, depending on the tooth and whether a temporary crown is placed. More involved cases, such as a full-arch restoration, can run longer. Our team gives you a realistic time estimate at your consultation so you can plan your day.

Mild swelling, tenderness, and minor bleeding are normal in the first day or two and usually ease with rest and simple care. Most patients feel back to normal within one to two weeks, while the implant fuses with the bone over the following months. We provide clear aftercare instructions and check your progress at follow-up visits.

Most patients feel little to no pain during the procedure thanks to local anesthesia and sedation options. After the procedure, mild soreness or swelling is normal and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and the aftercare instructions provided by Toothopia Dental.

You can usually eat soft foods within a day, then gradually return to a normal diet over the following weeks as the implant settles. We ask you to avoid hard, crunchy, or sticky foods near the implant site while it heals. Your dentist confirms when full chewing on that side is safe.

Good candidates have enough healthy jawbone, no active infection at the site, and overall health that supports healing. A CBCT scan and exam confirm whether immediate placement is right for you, since this same-day tooth replacement solution is not suitable for every case. If you are not a candidate yet, we will explain the staged options that can get you there.
