Benefits of Implants

Dental implants offer many benefits that go beyond the traditional options for tooth replacement. Braddock Family Dental believes in educating patients about the benefits of dental implants so they can make an informed decision about their oral care. Here are some of the key benefits of dental implants.

Braddock Family Dental is committed to helping patients achieve beautiful, healthy smiles with advanced implant dentistry. Schedule a consultation with Dr. Cho if you are considering dental implants to replace missing teeth. You will learn how implants can improve your smile and your quality of life.

  • NATURAL APPEARANCE Dental Implants closely resemble natural teeth both in appearance and function. Implants are made from materials of high quality, such as porcelain and titanium, which blend seamlessly with existing teeth. This gives you a natural-looking smile, restoring your confidence.
  • STABILITY AND FUNCTION ENHANCED Dental implants, unlike removable dentures and bridges, are securely embedded in your jawbone. They provide unparalleled stability and support when chewing or speaking. You can enjoy all your favorite foods with implants without any discomfort or worry about slippage.
  • PRESERVING JAWBONE HEALTH The jawbone can deteriorate due to a lack of stimulation when a tooth is missing. Dental implants stimulate the bone around them and help to maintain its strength and density.
  • DURABILITY AND LONGEVITY Dental implants are a long-term, cost-effective solution to tooth replacement. Implants are more durable and long-lasting than traditional dentures or bridges.
  • BETTER ORAL HEALTH Implants do not alter adjacent teeth as traditional options often require. Implants promote better dental health by preserving the integrity and strength of adjacent teeth.
  • COMFORT AND CONVENIENCE Implants eliminate the inconvenience and discomfort associated with removable dentures. This includes messy adhesives, sore spots, and uncomfortable fittings. Implants feel and work like natural teeth once they are integrated into the jawbone. This allows you to smile with ease and comfort.
  • STIMULATED FACE STRUCTURE Tooth Loss can cause facial sagging, premature aging, and a prematurely aged look. Dental implants help to maintain facial contours by supporting the bone structure. They also prevent the sunken appearance often associated with missing teeth.
  • IMPROVED SPEECH Missing teeth or dentures that don’t fit well can impact the clarity of your voice and pronunciation. Dental implants give you a solid foundation to speak clearly and confidently.

How to Care for Your Implants

It is important to maintain your dental implants properly to ensure their long-term durability and success. We at Braddock Family Dental are committed to giving you comprehensive advice on how to care for your dental implant to maintain your oral health and protect your investment. Here are some important tips to care for your implant.

We thank the many families that have given us positive reviews!

  • MAINTAIN GOOD ORAL HYGIENE Dental implants need to be cleaned twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste. Use a non-abrasive toothbrush and soft-bristled toothpaste to gently clean around the implant. Using an antimicrobial mouthwash and flossing around the implants can also help prevent gum diseases and implant-related complications.
  • REGULAR DENTAL CHECKUPS Routine dental cleanings and check-ups are important to monitor the health of your implants and detect any potential problems early. Visit our office regularly for comprehensive examinations and professional cleanings. We will assess the stability of your implant, and make personalized recommendations to maintain your oral health.
  • MONITOR CHANGES AND SEEK TREATMENT Pay close attention to any discomfort or changes around your implant. These include pain, swelling, or mobility. These symptoms could indicate implant-related issues or underlying problems that need attention. Do not hesitate to call our office if you have any concerns or symptoms. We will provide an assessment and the appropriate treatment.
  • AVOID BAD HABITS Certain bad habits can cause your implant to fail. Avoid chewing hard objects like ice, pens, or fingernails as they can put excessive pressure on your implant and the surrounding bone. Smoking or using tobacco products can also cause complications and slow down healing.
  • PROTECT AGAINST TEETH GRINDING It’s important to protect your implants if you grind your teeth or clench them, especially when sleeping. Dr. Cho might recommend wearing a night guard made to fit your mouth, which will cushion the implant from the damaging effects of bruxism. This can prevent damage to the implants and ensure their long-term stability.
  • FOLLOW POST-OPERATIVE INSTRUCTIONS After undergoing implant surgery, you should carefully follow Dr. Cho’s post-operative instructions to ensure proper healing and integration with the surrounding bone. It may be necessary to take prescribed medication, adhere to a mild diet during the first healing period, or avoid strenuous activity that can disrupt the healing process.
