Boasting an impressive 95 percent success rate ten years after placement, this treatment is ideal for anyone who wants to restore their complete biteforce without any of the hassle of removable or temporary restorations.

The Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

There are many incredible benefits of All-on-4 over traditional dentures, including:

  • The ability to permanently fix your dentures in place so they can be brushed just like real teeth without needing to be removed.
  • A natural looking appearance.
  • Realistic feeling.
  • Increased biteforce over traditional dentures, allowing you to enjoy all your favorite foods again.
  • The prevention of jawbone deterioration so you stay looking youthful and vibrant longer.

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dental Implants

While traditional dental implants and implant dentures offer a variety of unique perks over old-fashioned dentures, All-on-4 goes a step further, with their own special set of benefits that can’t be found with any other treatment:

  • Although traditional implant dentures may require up to eight posts to stay in place, All-on-4 only requires four, meaning they’re not only more cost-effective, but they also require less surgery.
  • In many cases, All-on-4 will not require an invasive bone graft procedure, or the need to wait for several months while your graft heals to have your implants placed.
  • All-on-4 dentures can often be placed on the same day as your dental implant surgery, which means you’ll be able to enjoy your new smile right away instead of waiting for months to complete healing!
