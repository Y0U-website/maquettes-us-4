Traditional Orthodontics

Regular metal braces have been around for decades. Consisting of a bracket and wire system, Dr. Omrani will gently apply the components to your teeth to allow for shifting to begin. Designed to move teeth in the appropriate direction for better alignment, they can address even the most complex dental problems (i.e., bite misalignment, gaps, overcrowding) and are a great option for both children and adults!

Invisalign Clear Braces

If you prefer a more subtle method of treatment that doesn’t shine a spotlight on your smile, we can discuss your candidacy for Invisalign! Made of clear, plastic trays, these aligners are customized to fit snugly over your teeth and apply gentle pressure to ensure proper realignment. When worn 20-22 hours a day for 1-2 weeks at a time, you can potentially achieve desired results much quicker.
