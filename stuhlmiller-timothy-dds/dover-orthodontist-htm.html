Orthodontist, Dr. Timothy Stuhlmiller

Dr. Tim Stuhlmiller grew up in Ohio on a farm in Portage County. He completed his pre-dental studies at the University of Toledo with a degree in Pharmacy. Dr. Stuhlmiller attended dental school at Ohio State University on a Navy Scholarship. Following graduation, he participated in a one year Advanced Education in General Dentistry program at Naval Dental Center, Great Lakes, IL. He also completed an operational tour at Naval Air Station Keflavik, Iceland before beginning his career in private practice.

In 2006, Dr. Stuhlmiller returned to school to complete a residency in orthodontics earning his Certificate of Advanced Graduate Studies from Jacksonville University, School of Orthodontics where he was honored with the Theodore Jerrold Memorial Award as well as the Excellence in Teaching Award. In 2008, Dr. Stuhlmiller returned to Ohio to be near family and friends.

Some of Dr. Stuhlmiller’s professional memberships include American Dental Association, Ohio Dental Association, and Tuscarawas County Dental Association.
