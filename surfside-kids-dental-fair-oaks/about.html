Our office policies

What if I need to reschedule my appointment?

Since appointments are reserved exclusively for each patient, we ask that you please notify our office at least 48 hours in advance of your scheduled appointment time if you are unable to keep your appointment.

Missed appointments are unused times that another patient who needs our care could have scheduled their treatment. We do understand that unexpected events can happen, and ask for your help in minimizing missed appointments.

How are appointments made?

Surfside Kids Dental does our best to schedule appointments which are most convenient for you and when the time is available. Our younger patients should be seen in the morning because they are more refreshed and our team can take as much time as needed for your child’s comfort. School children with a lot of work to complete should be seen also in the morning for the same reasons.

Dental appointments are an excused absence and Surfside Kids Dental will be more than happy to send a letter with you for either work or school needs.
