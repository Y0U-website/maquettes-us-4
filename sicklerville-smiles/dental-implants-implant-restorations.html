Who Qualifies for Implant Restoration?

If you’re missing one or more teeth and looking for a long-lasting replacement, implant restoration might be the perfect option for you. Dental implants can offer a natural-looking, functional solution that feels just like real teeth. But who’s a good candidate for this type of restoration?

Healthy Gums and Jawbone Strength

A healthy foundation is essential for successful implant restoration. Implants fuse with the jawbone, so having enough bone density is important for holding the implant securely. If you’ve experienced bone loss due to missing teeth, your dentist may recommend a bone graft to create a stronger base. Healthy gums are also a must, as they support the implant and help maintain long-term success.

Commitment to Oral Health

Good oral hygiene practices are essential for anyone considering implant restoration. This treatment requires committing to regular brushing, flossing, and dental visits to keep both your natural teeth and implants in great condition. Patients who value their oral health and are motivated to maintain it are ideal candidates for implant restorations.

Non-Smokers or Those Willing to Quit

Smoking can negatively impact the healing process and long-term success of dental implants. Smokers are at a higher risk of implant failure, so if you’re a smoker, your dentist might discuss quitting to increase your chances of a successful implant. Patients who don’t smoke or are ready to stop often have better outcomes with implants.

Ready for a Lasting Solution

Implant restoration is for those looking for a permanent solution to tooth loss. If you’re tired of dentures or other temporary fixes, implants can provide a stable, reliable, and natural-looking option. Talk to your dentist to see if this transformative solution aligns with your goals for a complete, healthy smile.
