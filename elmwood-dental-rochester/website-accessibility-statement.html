Website Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment ‍ Elmwood Dental is committed to ensuring that this website is accessible to people with disabilities. We believe everyone has the right to use the web with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence, and we work to make elmwooddental.com usable for the widest possible audience regardless of ability or technology.

Conformance Target ‍ We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). We treat WCAG 2.1 AA as an ongoing goal and regularly review this website against it as we add and update content.

Measures We Take ‍ We work to provide text alternatives for meaningful images, maintain sufficient color contrast, support keyboard navigation, use clear headings and structure, and design forms and interactive elements that assistive technologies can interpret. We also make available a supplementary accessibility tool that lets visitors adjust aspects of the display. This tool is one part of our broader effort and is not a substitute for accessible design.

Third-Party Content ‍ Some content is provided by third parties, such as embedded videos or maps. While we encourage these providers to deliver accessible content, we may not be able to control or correct their accessibility. Where third-party content presents a barrier, please contact us and we will provide the information through an alternative method.

Ongoing Effort and Limitations ‍ Despite our efforts, some content may not yet be fully adapted to the most current accessibility standards. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we are continually working to improve the experience for all visitors.

We Are Here to Help ‍ If you have difficulty using any part of this website, need information in an alternative format, or want to complete a task that a page does not let you complete, please contact us and we will work with you to provide the information, service, or transaction you need through an accessible method.

Contact Us ‍ Questions can be directed to accessibility@elmwooddental.com , or by phone at (585) 461-4350 , or by mail to Elmwood Dental, 1950 S. Clinton Ave., Rochester, NY 14618 .
