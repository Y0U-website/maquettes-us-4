3-D Cone Beam Computed Tomograhy (CBCT)

What is a CBCT scan, and why is it used for urgent dental care?

At Urgent Dental Care, we use 3-D Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) to create high-definition, three-dimensional images of your teeth, jawbone, and soft tissues in seconds. Unlike traditional 2D x-rays, CBCT imaging allows our team to see the full structure of your mouth, which is critical for providing same-day relief for complex dental pain.

  • Faster Emergency Diagnosis : We can pinpoint the exact source of an infection or fracture immediately, reducing your time in the chair.
  • Precision Root Canals and Implants : By mapping your anatomy in 3-D, we increase the success rate of restorative procedures and minimize the risk of complications.
  • Enhanced Safety : Our advanced sensors provide a high-resolution view with significantly lower radiation exposure than traditional medical CT scans.
  • Preventative Foundation : We also utilize CBCT during preventative consultations to detect hidden issues, like bone loss or impacted teeth, before they turn into painful dental emergencies.

iTero Digital Scanner

What is the iTero scanner, and why does it replace traditional impressions?

At Urgent Dental Care, we use the iTero digital scanner to capture precise, 3-D images of your teeth and bite in minutes, eliminating the need for the thick, uncomfortable putty trays used in traditional dental impressions. This wand-like device creates a highly accurate digital model of your mouth, which is essential for planning crowns, implants, and dentures with confidence.

  • No More Messy Impressions : We ve eliminated the gooey, gag-inducing putty trays of the past, replacing them with a comfortable, open-mouth scanning wand.
  • Faster, More Accurate Restorations : Because the digital scan captures every detail of your bite and tooth structure, we can design crowns, implants, and dentures with a level of precision that reduces the need for adjustments or remakes.
  • Real-Time Visual Co-Diagnosis : Just like our intraoral cameras, the iTero scan displays on our in-room screens, so you can see your own teeth and bite in 3-D as we walk you through your treatment options.
  • Streamlined Same-Day Planning : For patients catching up on years of missed care, digital scans allow us to move from diagnosis to treatment planning faster, which is especially valuable for urgent restorative cases.

Dental Implants vs. Dental Bridges: Understanding Your Options

When it comes to replacing a missing tooth, most patients choose between a dental bridge and a dental implant. While both restorations look natural and restore your ability to chew, they differ significantly in how they affect your long-term oral health.

The Dental Implant: A Permanent, Standalone Solution

Many of our patients in West Allis and Milwaukee choose dental implants because they function exactly like a natural tooth.

  • Preserves Natural Teeth : Unlike a bridge, an implant is a standalone solution that does not require the neighboring teeth to be filed down or altered.
  • Bone Health : An implant is the only replacement option that simulates a natural tooth root, which prevents the jawbone from shrinking (atrophy) over time.
  • Long-Term Success : With a success rate of 95 % and proper care, implants are designed to be a lifelong investment in your smile.
  • Functionality : Because the implant fuses to the bone through osseointegration, you can eat and speak with total confidence.

The Dental Bridge: An Efficient Alternative

A bridge is a fixed prosthetic that "bridges the gap" by using the healthy teeth on either side of the space for support.

  • Faster Results : A bridge can typically be completed in just two visits, making it a faster way to restore your smile compared to the implant healing process.
  • Non-Surgical : For patients who prefer to avoid minor surgery or who may not have enough bone density for an implant, a bridge is a highly effective alternative.
  • Custom Aesthetics : We use premium materials like zirconia and porcelain to ensure your bridge matches your surrounding teeth seamlessly.
