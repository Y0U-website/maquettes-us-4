Introducing All-on-4: A Same-Day Smile Transformation

All-on-4, also known as the all-on-x, revolutionizes how we restore complete smiles. Using this technique, we can place as few as four dental implants along your jaw to support an entire arch of replacement teeth .

Even better, we can complete the process in one day, so you never have to go without teeth. All-on-4 is tailored for those facing full tooth extraction or denture wearers desiring a more stable, permanent solution.

The term "all-on-x" refers to all teeth being supported "on X number of implants" the X representing the number of implants placed in the jaw, which is determined based on your unique needs.

What Are the Advantages of All-on-4® Implants?

Choosing implant dentures for your smile offers several advantages over traditional dentures. Here’s what you should know.

Jawbone Health

Dental implants protect the health of your jawbone by keeping it stimulated for regrowth. This helps you avoid the bone loss caused by wearing traditional dentures.

Quality of Life

You’ll enjoy an improved quality of life when you can chew and eat confidently and efficiently since you don’t have to endure the movement and slippage accompanying traditional denture wear.

Better Longevity

When you don’t experience bone loss due to wearing traditional dentures, it ensures you enjoy better longevity from your All-on-4 denture.

All-on-4® Implants vs. Traditional Dentures

If you are currently wearing dentures or facing the loss of an entire arch of teeth, you have a big decision to make regarding your quality of life. While traditional dentures have been a standard solution for years, All-on-4 implants represent a modern revolution in dental technology.

At Urgent Dental Care, we find that most patients prefer the "All-on-x" method because it provides a permanent, fixed solution that traditional dentures simply cannot match. Unlike dentures that rest on the gums and can slip or cause discomfort, All-on-4 implants are anchored securely into the jawbone, acting just like natural tooth roots. This not only gives you a "same-day smile" but also protects your long-term facial structure by preventing the bone loss commonly associated with denture wear.
