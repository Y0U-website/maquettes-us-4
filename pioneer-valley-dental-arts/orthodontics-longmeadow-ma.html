Orthodontics

Treating a jaw alignment problem with orthodontics at an early age may alleviate the need for jaw surgery later in life to address an overbite or protrusion of the top teeth. It can also play a role in enabling normal speech development.

  • Tongue thrusting
  • Snoring problems or mouth breathing
  • Enlarged adenoids or tonsils which restrict the airway
  • Bone development problems in and around the jaw
  • Congenital missing teeth

Adult Orthodontic Care Longmeadow

Although we often consider braces to be for the young, many adult patients can benefit from orthodontics. Patients with bite problems, cosmetic and dental health concerns can restore a healthy, comfortable bite with orthodontic treatment. The development of effective clear braces has dramatically increased the number of adult patients using orthodontics to achieve the smile they desire.

Pioneer Valley Dental Arts offers invisible braces, including Invisalign® and Six Month Smiles to correct a wide range of problems with discreet and convenient treatment. Dr. Evans or Dr. Ziemba can evaluate your dental health and cosmetic concerns to determine the best solution for your needs and goals. Invisible braces can offer patients comfortable treatment with removable aligners or clear brackets. Treatment time is typically about a year and can be maintained for years to come with daily use of a retainer.
