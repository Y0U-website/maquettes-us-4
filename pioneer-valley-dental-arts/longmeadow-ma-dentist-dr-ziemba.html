Longmeadow MA Dentist Dr. Ziemba

He is also on the advisory committee at Porter and Chester Institute, Dental Assisting Department, the committee helps with the design of the students’ curriculum. Pioneer Valley Dental Arts is also one of the externship sites for dental assisting students to shadow the staff and get some real-life experience.

Each year Dr. Ziemba attends numerous hours of Dental continuing education to keep up with the newest technology, techniques and procedures. At this time he is participating in advanced training at the Spear Center of Excellence. He is certified in Straumann, 3i, and Noble-Biocare Implant Systems.

Dr. Ziemba is married to his wife Christine, a Pioneer Valley native. They have two children.

In his spare time, he enjoys playing and coaching ice hockey, golfing, fishing, and spending quality time with his family and friends.
