Meet Longmeadow, MA Dentist Dr. Julia Reilly

Dr. Julia Reilly grew up in southeastern Connecticut and completed her B.A. in biology at Swarthmore College in 2006. She earned her D.M.D. at the University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine in 2011, followed by a one-year advanced training program. Dr. Reilly has worked in private practice dentistry and raised her family in western Massachusetts for the past 14 years.

Dr. Reilly has a passion for continuing education and lifelong learning. She has completed advanced training in occlusion, cosmetic dentistry, and dental implant placement. She recently completed a postgraduate fellowship in forensic dentistry at Lincoln Memorial University in Knoxville, Tennessee. She serves as the forensic odontology consultant for the Vermont Office of the Chief Medical Examiner, performing dental autopsies and completing forensic dental identifications. She also serves as an expert witness.

Outside the office, Dr. Reilly enjoys being outside whenever possible, hiking, birding, gardening, and running. She is married to her husband Steve, a Springfield native, and they have two children. Dr. Reilly is always excited to serve her patients with skill, humor, and compassion.
