FAQs About Broken Tooth Treatment

What causes a broken tooth?

Broken teeth result from trauma, biting hard objects, teeth grinding, and large cavity weakening. Sports injuries, car accidents, and falls cause sudden fractures. Chewing ice, popcorn kernels, or hard candy cracks enamel. Bruxism gradually weakens tooth structure until breakage occurs.

When should I see a dentist for a broken tooth?

How does a dentist fix a broken tooth?

Dentists repair broken teeth using bonding, crowns, veneers, or root canal therapy. Small chips receive composite resin application. Moderate fractures need porcelain or ceramic crowns. Severe breaks involving pulp require root canal treatment followed by crown placement.

Can a broken tooth heal on its own?

Broken teeth cannot regenerate or heal naturally. Enamel lacks living cells capable of repair. The fracture will remain or worsen without professional treatment. Bacteria will eventually infect exposed inner tooth layers.

Does dental insurance cover broken tooth repair?

Most dental insurance plans cover broken tooth treatment as a basic or major service. Coverage percentages vary by plan type and provider. Our Pasadena office verifies your benefits before treatment begins. We provide cost estimates and payment options for any remaining balance.
