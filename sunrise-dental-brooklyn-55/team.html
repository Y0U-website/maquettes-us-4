Meet our skilled Dental Professionals

At Sunrise Dental Brooklyn, our team of experienced dentists, hygienists, and support staff are here to ensure you receive the highest quality of care. Learn more about the people dedicated to your dental health.

Dr. Trish Wing-Sze Wong

Dr. Trish Wing S. Wong, DDS, brings a wealth of expertise and a compassionate approach to her dental practice. A proud alumna of Columbia College of Dental Medicine, she earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree in 2007, solidifying her commitment to advanced dental care. Dr. Wong further refined her clinical skills and broad medical knowledge during her residency at the Manhattan VA Hospital, a rigorous program that exposed her to diverse patient needs and complex cases.

Beyond her professional dedication, Dr. Wong leads a rich and balanced life. She is an avid reader, constantly expanding her knowledge and perspective. To maintain her physical and mental well-being, she enjoys playing tennis, practicing yoga, and engaging in meditation. Dr. Wong also cherishes her time traveling with her family, embracing new experiences and creating lasting memories.

Dr. Jacqueline Ng

Dr. Jacqueline Ng, DDS, is a highly respected and experienced dentist devoted to providing exceptional oral healthcare. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the prestigious New York University (NYU), building a strong foundation in comprehensive dental science and patient care. After graduating, Dr. Ng further honed her clinical skills and expertise during her residency at Hackensack University Medical Center, a renowned institution known for its rigorous training programs.

Outside of her professional life, Dr. Ng enjoys a fulfilling personal life. She has a passion for travel, exploring new cultures and experiences. In her leisure time, she cherishes the opportunity to spend quality moments with her family and friends, valuing these connections as much as her commitment to her patients’ well-being.

Dr. Shirley Hu

Dr. Shirley Hu, DDS, is a highly experienced and compassionate dentist dedicated to providing comprehensive oral healthcare. A proud graduate of SUNY Buffalo, Dr. Hu further honed her skills during her residency at the Brooklyn VA Hospital. With over 14 years in practice, she offers a wide range of services including preventive dentistry, fillings, root canals, crowns and bridges, partial and complete dentures, extractions (including wisdom teeth), implants, and teeth whitening.

Beyond her commitment to her patients’ smiles, Dr. Hu enjoys a vibrant life outside the office. She finds relaxation and joy in swimming, baking, and creating beautiful latte art. Recently, she has embraced the challenge and benefits of Pilates and is an avid outdoor enthusiast, regularly engaging in hiking, kayaking, and paddleboarding.

Dr. Jennifer Tran

Dr. Jennifer Tran, DMD, is a highly skilled and compassionate dentist dedicated to providing excellent oral healthcare. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the prestigious University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 2009, building a strong foundation in comprehensive dental practices. Dr. Tran further refined her clinical expertise by completing her General Practice Residency in 2010 at Montefiore Hospital, gaining invaluable experience across a wide range of dental procedures.

Dr. Tran’s area of practice encompasses essential aspects of general dentistry, focusing on both the preservation and restoration of healthy smiles. She is proficient in preventive dentistry, emphasizing proactive measures to maintain optimal oral health. Her restorative services include precise fillings, durable crowns and bridges, and effective root canal treatments to address decay and preserve natural teeth. Dr. Tran also provides prosthetic solutions, offering partial and complete dentures to restore function and aesthetics, and performs simple extractions when necessary.

Outside of her professional commitments, Dr. Tran cherishes her free time. She enjoys traveling and is happily dedicated to keeping up with her three active boys, balancing her passion for dentistry with a vibrant family life.
