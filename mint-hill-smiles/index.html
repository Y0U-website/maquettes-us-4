Mild pain after implant placement is normal. Try to take pain medication before the numbness from the procedure wears off, rather than waiting until it starts to hurt.

Our doctors recommend 600mg of ibuprofen (3 over-the-counter tablets) every 6 hours for the first 3 days. Ibuprofen (Advil) and naproxen (Aleve) both help control pain and minimize swelling.

Vitamin D3 has been shown to support healthy bone formation around a new implant. Take 1000 IU, especially if you know you run low on Vitamin D.

Swelling is your body’s normal response to surgery and part of healing. An ice pack, or an unopened bag of frozen peas or corn, applied to the outside of your face keeps swelling to a minimum.
