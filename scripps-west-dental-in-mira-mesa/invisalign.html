What is Invisalign®?

Invisalign® is a virtually invisible solution for straightening teeth that allows you to smile confidently both during and after treatment. Using a series of clear, removable aligners, Invisalign® gently and gradually moves your teeth toward the smile you’ve always wanted. The clear aligner trays are so discreet that no one will even know you’re wearing them!

How Does Invisalign® Work?

Your Invisalign® dentist will evaluate your smile, mapping out a precise, customized digital treatment plan that showcases the step-by-step transformation of your smile. Once your plan is approved, your unique aligners will be created. You’ll wear each set of aligners for 20 to 22 hours a day and will change to a new set every 1 to 2 weeks.

Is Invisalign® Convenient?

Invisalign® in San Diego is comfortable to wear and easy to live with. Because the aligners are removable, you can continue to brush and floss normally to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

What Kinds of Conditions Can Invisalign® Treat?

Invisalign treatment can be an excellent solution for adults and teens alike! This innovative, technologically advanced system makes it possible to fix almost all common teeth-straightening and bite issues, from simple to complex — without interrupting your busy life. The Scripps West Dental team has treated patients with crowding, spacing, underbites, overbites, and even deep bites with Invisalign®.

Why Should I Choose Invisalign® Treatment?

Over 9 million people have used Invisalign clear aligners to transform their smiles. That includes more than 1.4 million teens! All Invisalign aligner materials are made from medical-grade thermoplastic polymers that were selected for use based on their safety. These innovative materials have a long history of FDA approvals for long term use inside the human body. Tests have shown that they are biocompatible and pass all regulatory requirements for human wear in the mouth.
