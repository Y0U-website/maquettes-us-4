Built-In Accessibility Toolbar

Every page on our website includes a built-in accessibility toolbar — the gold icon in the bottom-left corner. It allows you to adjust the experience to your needs without any additional software or plugins.

Your preferences are saved automatically and will be remembered on your next visit.

  • Increase or decrease text size
  • Enable high contrast mode
  • Invert colors for light sensitivity
  • Switch to a dyslexia-friendly font
  • Increase line spacing for easier reading
  • Enable a large cursor
  • Underline all links for better visibility

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some content may not yet fully conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We are actively working to identify and address any accessibility barriers. If you encounter any issues, please contact us so we can address them promptly.

Physical Accessibility

Our dental office is designed to be accessible to patients with physical disabilities.

If you have specific accessibility needs, please contact us before your visit so we can make appropriate accommodations.

  • Accessible parking spaces near the entrance
  • Wheelchair-accessible entrance and treatment rooms
  • Accessible restrooms
  • Staff trained to assist patients with disabilities
