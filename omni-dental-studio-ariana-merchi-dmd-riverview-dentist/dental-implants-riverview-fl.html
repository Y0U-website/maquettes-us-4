Permanent tooth replacement in Riverview — built to last a lifetime.

Dental implants are the closest thing to a natural tooth that modern dentistry can offer. A titanium post is placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone over several months — a process called osseointegration. Once healed, a custom porcelain crown is attached on top, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions just like the real thing.

How much do dental implants cost in Riverview, FL?

A single dental implant at Omni Dental Studio typically ranges from $3,000 to $5,500, which includes the implant post, abutment, and porcelain crown. The total cost depends on whether bone grafting is needed, the location of the implant in the mouth, and the type of crown selected.

Most dental insurance plans do not cover implants, though some plans offer partial coverage for the crown portion. We will verify your benefits before your consultation. If your plan includes major restorative coverage, a portion of the crown cost may be covered.

CareCredit financing is available with 0 % APR promotional periods, making it possible to spread the cost of implants over 12, 18, or 24 months. Many patients find the monthly payment is comparable to what they would spend maintaining a bridge or denture over the same period.

Frequently Asked Questions

The full implant process typically takes 3 to 6 months from placement to final crown. After the implant post is placed, it needs 3 to 4 months to fuse with the jawbone (osseointegration). Once healed, the abutment and crown are placed in one or two additional appointments. If bone grafting is needed, the timeline may be longer.

The implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you should feel pressure but not pain during the procedure. Most patients report that the recovery is easier than they expected — mild soreness and swelling for a few days, manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers. Dr. Merchi will provide detailed post-operative instructions.

With proper care, dental implants can last 20 years or more — and many last a lifetime. The implant post itself is titanium and highly durable. The porcelain crown on top may need to be replaced after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear. Good oral hygiene and regular dental visits are key to implant longevity.

If you do not have sufficient bone density or volume, a bone graft may be needed before implant placement. Bone grafting adds bone material to the jaw to create a stable foundation for the implant. This adds time to the process but makes implants possible for many patients who would otherwise not qualify.
