Dental Services in Plymouth, MI

From preventive care to advanced treatments, Smiles on Main proudly serves patients in Plymouth, Canton, Livonia, Northville, and Westland with compassionate, high-quality dental services. Whatever your needs, our experienced team is here to help you achieve and maintain your best smile. Schedule your next dental visit today and discover the difference local, personalized care can make.

  • Home » Dental Services Dental Services Bridges: A custom-made dental prosthetic is a great way to fill in the gaps when you are missing one or more teeth. This false tooth anchors to healthy neighboring teeth, or it can be secured using a dental implant. The result: your bite and smile look and function normally.
  • Cleanings & Check-Ups: The best way to prevent cavities and gum disease is by maintaining consistency with regular cleanings and dental exams. We’ll evaluate your oral health and catch potential issues in the earliest stages.
  • Crowns: When a tooth is severely damaged, then a crown offers an effective solution to preserve the remaining structure of the tooth. Not only does it protect the weakened tooth, but a crown will also restore the shape and appearance to optimize your smile.
  • Dentures: Multiple missing teeth can be replaced using dentures. Modern dentures are comfortable and effective, allowing you to eat, talk, and smile normally. Talk to us about traditional dentures or implant-supported dentures.
  • Dental Implants: One of the most secure dental restorations is to replace a tooth with an implant. The post is secured into your jawbone, then the new crown, bridge, or denture is placed on top. Dental implants look and feel like natural teeth.
  • Emergency Services: Timing is critical when a dental emergency occurs. It’s important that you treat broken teeth, toothaches, and other injuries without delay. The faster your treatment, the lower the risk of bigger complications.
  • Fillings: Tooth-colored fillings restore the function of your tooth after decay and cavities are removed. The goal is to seal the tooth and offer strong protection to avoid further damage in the future.
  • Invisalign: Enjoy improved aesthetics with clear aligners, instead of the discomfort of wearing traditional wire-and-bracket braces. This orthodontic treatment is discreet and comfortable – an ideal fit for your lifestyle.
  • Root Canals: Severe decay and infection within a tooth can be removed using modern root canals. This treatment cleans out the inner area of the tooth while preserving the outer structure of the tooth, helping to reduce the need for extraction.
  • Tooth Extractions: Some teeth that are severely infected or damaged need to be removed in order to preserve your overall oral health. We use care when completing this procedure and offer a variety of restorations to fill in the gaps after extraction.
  • Veneers: A fast and easy way to upgrade your smile is by using porcelain veneers over the visible surfaces of your teeth. We can cover up common cosmetic concerns, including chips, discoloration, gaps, and more.
