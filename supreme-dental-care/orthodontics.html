Why Is Orthodontic Treatment Important?

Orthodontic treatment may be necessary for both cosmetic and health-related reasons. Crooked or crowded teeth can affect your self-confidence, making you hesitant to smile or speak freely. Beyond appearance, misaligned teeth can also make it difficult to brush and floss effectively. This can lead to a higher risk of tooth decay, gingivitis, and even more serious gum disease over time.

FAQs

  • Who can benefit from orthodontic treatment? Orthodontic treatment can help at any age. Kids should have their first check-up around ages 7–9, and adults can start anytime. We’ll assess your smile to find the right time for treatment.
  • How long does orthodontic treatment usually take? We start with an exam and x-rays, then discuss your treatment options. You’ll choose the best plan for your needs and goals. Regular visits are needed for adjustments or new aligners. Treatment time varies, usually from a few months up to two or three years.
  • Am I a good candidate for Invisalign? If you have crooked or crowded teeth that affect your confidence or oral health, Invisalign might be the right choice for you. To determine if clear aligners are suitable for your needs, schedule a consultation with an Invisalign-trained dentist who can evaluate your smile and discuss your treatment options.
  • What can I expect during my Invisalign treatment? You’ll need to wear your aligners almost all day, removing them only for about two hours each day. Treatment times vary depending on the individual, but most patients achieve their desired smile within one to two years. Throughout your treatment, you’ll have periodic dental appointments so your dentist can track your progress and make any necessary adjustments.
  • Will I need any care after my Invisalign treatment? Once your treatment is complete, you won’t need to wear Invisalign trays anymore. However, to maintain your new smile, you’ll need to wear a retainer daily. It’s also important to keep up with regular dental check-ups and professional cleanings twice a year to ensure your oral health stays in great shape.
