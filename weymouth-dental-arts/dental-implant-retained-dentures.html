Dental Implant-Retained Dentures FAQ

Here are a few commonly asked questions about dental implant-retained dentures:

How long do implant-retained dentures last?

Dental implant-retained dentures should make a permanent solution for your missing teeth. Assuming you care for them properly, they should last for many years to come.

Does insurance cover implant-retained dentures?

Although it varies from company to company, PPO insurance plans normally cover the costs of implants and implant-retained dentures. The implant part of the procedure is typically provided with coverage at 50 % up until your annual maximum, while the denture part is often covered at up to 60 % of the cost.
