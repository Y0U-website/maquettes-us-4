Dental implants are a permanent investment in your smile, confidence, and oral health.

  • Preservation of Jawbone Health Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that naturally occurs after tooth loss.
  • Improved Aesthetics Implants look, feel, and function just like natural teeth.

Our Dental Implant Process

At Bay Area Dental Specialists, we prioritize personalized care at every step of your implant journey.

  • Consultation and Planning We start with a thorough consultation to evaluate your dental health, review your medical history, and take 3D images of your mouth. This allows us to create a precise, customized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement The implant post is surgically placed into your jawbone during a minimally invasive procedure. We offer sedation options to make the experience as stress-free and comfortable as possible.
  • Healing and Osseointegration Over the next few months, the implant naturally integrates with your bone. During this time, we may place a temporary restoration if needed.
  • Abutment and Crown Placement Once healed, we attach an abutment (connector) to the implant, followed by your custom-crafted dental crown or restoration. The result? A strong, beautiful tooth replacement that blends perfectly with your natural smile.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults are excellent candidates for dental implants.

During your consultation, our team will carefully assess your unique situation and discuss your best options.

  • Have one or more missing teeth
  • Have sufficient jawbone density (or are willing to undergo bone grafting if needed)
  • Are in good general health (certain conditions like uncontrolled diabetes may require extra precautions)
  • Are a non-smoker or willing to quit (smoking can affect healing)

  • How Long Do Dental Implants Last? With proper care, including regular dental visits, good oral hygiene, and avoiding smoking, dental implants can last 20 years or longer. Many patients enjoy lifetime results. Crowns or bridges attached to implants may eventually need replacement due to normal wear, but the implants themselves are designed for long-term success. Routine checkups allow us to monitor their condition and ensure your smile stays strong and healthy.
  • Is Getting Dental Implants Painful? Most patients report only mild discomfort after implant surgery, similar to what you might feel after a simple tooth extraction. We offer local anesthesia and sedation options to keep you comfortable during the procedure. Post-surgical soreness usually subsides within a few days and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team will guide you through recovery with personalized tips to help you heal quickly and comfortably.
  • How Much Do Dental Implants Cost? The cost of dental implants varies depending on the number of teeth being replaced, the need for preparatory procedures (like bone grafting), and the type of restoration. During your consultation, we’ll provide a transparent breakdown of costs and financing options to make implants affordable. We also work with most major PPO dental insurance plans and can help maximize any available benefits.
  • What Happens if I Don’t Replace a Missing Tooth? When a tooth is lost, the surrounding bone begins to shrink due to a lack of stimulation. This can cause shifting of nearby teeth, bite problems, facial sagging, and increased risk of additional tooth loss. Dental implants prevent these issues by preserving jawbone health and maintaining proper alignment. Replacing missing teeth also supports speech, chewing function, and your overall confidence in daily life.
  • Are Dental Implants Better Than Bridges or Dentures? While bridges and dentures are still useful in certain cases, dental implants offer the most natural, stable, and long-lasting solution. They don’t rely on neighboring teeth for support and provide the closest function and appearance to your natural teeth. Implants also help prevent bone loss, improve comfort, and allow you to eat your favorite foods without slipping or irritation.
