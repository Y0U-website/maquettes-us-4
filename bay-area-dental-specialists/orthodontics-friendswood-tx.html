Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • How Do I Know if I Need an Orthodontist in Friendswood, TX? If you have crooked, crowded, gapped, or protruding teeth, or if your bite feels uncomfortable, you could benefit from an orthodontic evaluation. Some signs are obvious, but others, like subtle bite misalignment or jaw discomfort, are less noticeable. A consultation with our team at Bay Area Dental Specialists can reveal underlying issues that, if addressed early, could prevent bigger dental problems later. We offer thorough exams to explain your options clearly and help you decide whether orthodontic treatment is right for you.
  • What Is the Best Age to Start Orthodontic Treatment? Ideally, a child’s first orthodontic evaluation should happen around age 7, even if treatment doesn’t start right away. Early exams let us monitor jaw development, spacing, and eruption patterns. Many kids begin treatment between 9 and 14 when their bones are still growing. That said, adults of any age can still greatly benefit from orthodontic care! We offer braces and clear aligners for teens, young adults, and mature adults who want to improve their smile and bite alignment.
  • How Long Does Orthodontic Treatment Take? Treatment times vary depending on the complexity of tooth movement required. Minor crowding corrected with clear aligners may take as little as 6 to 12 months. Moderate cases handled with traditional braces often take 18 to 24 months. Severe bite issues may require slightly longer. During your consultation, our Friendswood orthodontist will create a customized timeline based on your smile goals and how quickly we can achieve healthy, beautiful alignment.
  • Are Clear Aligners as Effective as Braces? For many mild to moderate cases, absolutely. Clear aligners can straighten teeth, close small gaps, and correct minor bite problems when worn 20 22 hours a day. However, traditional braces may be the better option for patients who need major bite corrections, significant rotation, or vertical tooth movement. We’ll carefully assess your case and recommend the best, most effective solution for your unique smile.
