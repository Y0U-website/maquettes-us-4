Meet the Team at Willow Pediatric Dentistry of Tecumseh

We are honored that you chose our practice to be a part of your child’s dental health. Our mission is to provide excellent oral care in a relaxing, comfortable, and safe environment for kids, ensuring a positive dental experience that best fits each individual’s needs.

It is important to establish positive oral health at a young age. The American Dental Association and the American Association of Pediatric Dentistry recommends that children are seen between the arrival of their first tooth and their first birthday. Help protect your child’s teeth and their future adult teeth by starting dental check-ups by their first birthday.

Our staff are well trained to treat children of all ages and special needs. Every patient will be treated as if they were our own. Establishing communication and trust with you and your child is our top priority. We provide positive reinforcement to educate your child while completing the necessary treatment. Our modern office was designed to be kid-friendly and inviting.

It is our vision is to provide the best pediatric dental care and establish a long lasting relationship with the patient s, families, the city of Tecumseh and the rest of the communities of Lenawee County. Feel free to call us anytime with any questions or concerns. We look forward to working with you and your child!

Dr. Jody Atzmon, DDS

Dr. Jody brings a thoughtful, compassionate, and child-centered approach to caring for our community’s children. Dr. Jody is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and a member of the Michigan Academy of Pediatric Dentistry. She is a proud graduate of the University of Michigan School of Dentistry and completed two years of specialized pediatric residency training in her home state of Alaska at the Alaska Native Medical Center.

Prior to dentistry, Dr. Jody owned and operated a daycare, where she learned firsthand how children grow, learn, and respond to encouragement. That early experience shaped her belief that helping kids succeed especially in the dental chair comes through patience and kindness.

When she isn’t at the office, you might find Dr. Jody making pottery (especially during Michigan’s snowy winters, which she truly loves), spending time outdoors, or enjoying family time at home. She feels deeply honored to serve the families of Lenawee County and is committed to creating a practice where every child feels safe, confident, and cared for.

Dr. Chad Zillich, DDS, MS

With over 15 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Chad brings a seasoned, practical approach to caring for our community s children.
