ADA Accessibility Statement

Conformance Status

Our website is in a state of partial conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Partial conformance means that some parts of the content do not yet fully meet the accessibility standard. We regularly test the site — including manual testing with keyboard navigation and screen-reader-relevant checks, not only automated scanners — and prioritize fixes based on the real impact to visitors using assistive technology.

Accessibility measures we have implemented include: descriptive alternative text on images, including detailed descriptions for clinical before-and-after photos; properly labeled and associated form fields; a visible keyboard-focus indicator throughout our main navigation; captioned video content; accessible names for embedded maps and media; and a logical heading structure across our pages.

We are aware of, and actively working on, a small number of remaining gaps, including keyboard focus visibility in certain expandable FAQ sections and full autocomplete support in our contact form. These are tracked and prioritized for correction.

Here For You

If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on our site, encounter an accessibility barrier, or require assistance with any part of our site, please contact us during normal business hours as detailed below and we will be happy to assist — either by working around the barrier with you directly or by escalating it for a fix.

Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you wish to report an accessibility issue, have any questions, or need assistance, please contact us by calling us or by emailing info@jonaridental.com. We aim to respond to accessibility-related requests promptly.
