Dental Implants in Cooper City, FL

  • Improved Chewing and Speech: Function like natural teeth, allowing you to eat and speak with confidence.
  • Long-Lasting Solution: With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Preserve Jawbone Health: Prevents bone loss that occurs after tooth loss.
  • Protect Adjacent Teeth: Unlike dental bridges, implants don’t require grinding down healthy teeth.

Who is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

You may be a good candidate for implants if you:

  • Are missing one or more teeth
  • Have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone support
  • Maintain good oral hygiene habits
  • Do not smoke heavily or have uncontrolled health conditions

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation & Exam: We evaluate your oral health, take digital X-rays, and create a customized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium post is surgically placed in the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation.
  • Healing & Osseointegration: Over a few months, the implant fuses with the bone to create a stable foundation.
  • Abutment & Restoration: A connector (abutment) is placed, and a crown, bridge, or denture is attached
