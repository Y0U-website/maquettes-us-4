At our dental office in Jonari Dental of Cooper City, we offer dental implants as the gold standard for tooth replacement. Whether you’re missing one tooth, several teeth, or all of your teeth, implants provide a permanent, natural-looking solution that restores both function and confidence.

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible titanium. They are surgically placed into the jawbone and act as a strong foundation for replacement teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Because implants fuse with your bone, they provide stability and durability unmatched by other tooth replacement options.
