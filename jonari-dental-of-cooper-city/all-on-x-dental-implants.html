Recovery and Aftercare of All-on-X Implants

‍ After your All-on-X procedure, most patients experience only mild discomfort and swelling, which typically subsides within a few days. A soft-food diet is recommended during the initial healing phase to allow your implants to integrate properly with the jawbone.

Our team will provide detailed instructions and schedule follow-up visits to monitor your progress and ensure optimal healing. With proper care, including good oral hygiene and regular dental checkups, your All-on-X implants can provide lasting comfort, function, and confidence.
