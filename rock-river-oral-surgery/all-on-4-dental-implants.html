All-on-4 Procedure

Once the optimal locations are marked, the procedure will begin with administration of a local anesthetic and/or sedation. Any loose or unhealthy teeth are extracted at this point. Next, four titanium posts are strategically placed in your gums. Once these posts are properly positioned, it will take some time for them to fully integrate with jawbone (osseointegration). During this period, temporary teeth are placed on the implants to reduce your risk of developing an infection and allow you to preserve a full, healthy smile.

Once the jawbone has fully fused with the dental implants, permanent teeth will be placed. Following placement, an evaluation of the overall fit and aesthetic appearance is made. Finally, the All-on-4 process is complete and you can begin to enjoy your bright, new, full healthy smile.

Cost of All-on-4 Dental Implants?

The cost of having All-on-4 Dental Implants placed will depend on several factors, such as whether sedation is used, additional procedures that need to be performed, how many visits are required, and your dental insurance plan.

At Rock River Oral Surgery & Dental Implant Center, we are committed to providing you with high-quality, dental & oral surgery services, which is why we offer flexible payment options to ensure that you receive the care you require.

If you are covered under a dental insurance plan, we do ask that you bring your insurance cards and any necessary forms with you to your appointment. We are happy to provide a pre-estimate of dental benefits prior to treatment , and will submit any receipts, forms, or other information required by your insurance company. Once insurance payment has been received, the remaining balance will be mailed to you.
