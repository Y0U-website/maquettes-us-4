Meet Dr. Apata

I have a lot of experience providing care to families from all walks of life and I’m very excited to start my own practice in my own community. I belong to a number of organizations: the Maryland Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Maryland State Dental Association, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, and the American Dental Association.

I have devoted my professional career to all aspects of pediatric dentistry practice with special interests in prevention, evidence-based treatment, and disease management. To keep abreast of the latest developments in my specialty, I have always exceeded the continuing education requirements for Maryland dentists.

Aside from dentistry, I have many hobbies that include listening to music; singing and songwriting; traveling; watching movies, shows, and sports; and spending time with family and friends. Fun fact – I sang the national anthem at the Wizards/Bulls game on New Years Day in 2022. I’ve been married to my husband, Joseph, for 11 years. We have been blessed with three amazing children: Alexander, Christian, and Joy.

Dr. Apata is Dedicated to Exceptional, Family-friendy Pediatric Dental Care
