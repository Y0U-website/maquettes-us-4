0 Who We Are Behind The Masks Deborah P. Poole, DDS Practice Owner

Dr. Poole is a proud member of a worldwide selective group, Seattle Study Club, which is made of less than thirty dentists from Maui. Driven by her mentors and a deep passion for oral health, she has been apart of the teaching faculty at the University of Hawaii Maui College’s Dental Hygiene Program. She met her husband on island and they currently have two children and expecting their third baby November 2025. Dr. Deb is excited to continue growing deeper roots in Maui by opening her own dental practice and looking forward to long-lasting relationships with those she serves.

Destiny

Destiny began her dental career on the Big Island, where she spent three years as a front office assistant. What initially started as part-time role quickly grew into a true passion as she discovered her love for dentistry and patient care. After relocating to Maui in 2023, she transitioned into the clinical side of dentistry to gain a more hands-on experience and broaden her knowledge. Regardless of her role in the office, Destiny is dedicated to making every patient feel comfortable, cared for, and valued. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time outdoors and caring for her animals.

Maile

Maile was born and raised on the island of Maui and graduated from Maui High School. She earned her Associate in Science degree in Dental Hygiene from Miami Dade College in 2008. When Maile is not at work, she keeps busy with her 2 kids’ school and sport activities. She also loves Latin dancing, traveling, spending time with her family and all beach activities.
