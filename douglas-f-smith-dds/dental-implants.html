DENTAL IMPLANT FAQS

Whether you need a single tooth replaced or an entire mouthful, Dr. Smith is here to help. We go above and beyond to ensure the comfort of our patients. Call us to discuss dental implants in our Logan, UT office.

Dental implants are teeth substitutes that need to be taken care of like regular teeth. To give your dental implants the best care, be sure to brush and floss like you would with a natural tooth. You might also want to use an antibacterial mouthwash and a soft-bristle toothbrush.

A single dental implant can cost between $1,500 and $4,500. Dental implant prices typically include the implant, the support post, and the crown. The exact price of your dental implant depends on where you need the implant, how many implants you need, and if you will have to have a bone graft.

Dental implants can be a possibility for people of all ages. However, older adults often have one or more missing permanent teeth, making them excellent candidates for implants. If you are over the age of 65 and have a missing tooth, dental implants could be the best option for you.
