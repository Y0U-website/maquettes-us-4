Out-of-Network Benefits

Don’t see your insurance listed? We can still help! We file claims for out-of-network patients when there are assignment of benefits available for out-of-network providers. Contact our office to discuss your specific coverage and options.

Medicaid, CHIP, and State Insurance

Families who qualify for Medicaid or the Children’s Health Insurance Program (CHIP) can access pediatric dental services at our offices. Pediatric dental coverage is considered an essential health benefit under the Affordable Care Act, meaning most children’s plans must include dental care.

We work with state insurance programs to ensure your child receives the care they need. Our staff handles pre-authorizations and coordinates directly with your provider so the process stays simple on your end. Whether your child needs a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, we will work with your plan to get the necessary approvals before treatment begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

We accept most major insurance plans, including Medicaid and CHIP. Because plan networks can vary by location and provider, the best way to confirm your child’s coverage is to call the office where you plan to schedule. Our staff will verify your benefits before the appointment so you know exactly what to expect. If you are unsure which plan your child is enrolled in, check your insurance card or contact your provider directly, and we can take it from there.

Some insurance plans require approval before certain treatments can begin. This is called a pre-authorization, and it is the insurance company’s way of confirming they will cover the procedure. For services like extractions, root canals, or hospital dentistry, our team submits the pre-authorization request on your behalf and waits for confirmation before scheduling. This means you will have a clear picture of your coverage and any estimated costs before your child’s treatment begins.

Please bring your child’s insurance card, a valid photo ID for the parent or guardian, and any completed patient forms . If your child has dental records from a previous provider, those are helpful but not required. Having your insurance information ready when you arrive allows our front desk team to verify benefits quickly and keep your appointment on schedule.

There are times when your child’s dentist may recommend care your plan does not fully cover. When this happens, we explain your options and estimated costs before any work begins. We can also help you explore payment alternatives through our financial information options so your child’s care is not delayed.

For children who need sedation dentistry or treatment in a surgical center, we work with your insurance provider to get as much of the procedure covered as possible. Our team handles the pre-authorization process and will walk you through your estimated costs before anything is scheduled. If there is a remaining balance your plan does not cover, we will discuss payment options with you so you can make the best decision for your child without any surprises.

If your child’s insurance plan has changed for any reason, whether from a new job, a Medicaid renewal, or a change in coverage, please let us know before your next appointment. Calling ahead gives our team time to verify your new benefits and make sure everything is in order before your child is seen. This helps avoid billing confusion and ensures your child’s visit goes smoothly from the moment you walk in.
