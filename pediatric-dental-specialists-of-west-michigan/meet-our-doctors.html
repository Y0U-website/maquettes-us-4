Meet Our Doctors

We are proud that our doctors are trained in all aspects of child development. Both Dr. Stephanie and Dr. Katie are Board Certified Pediatric Dentists, Diplomates of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and Laser Certified through the American Board of Laser Surgery. They have received specialized and extensive training in child psychology, sedation and laser dentistry. Our team will work with parents and caregivers to identify those treatment approaches that are best for each individual child.
