0 Our Story For over 35 years, our conveniently located Madison, Connecticut dental office has provided exceptional dental care services to the Connecticut Shoreline community.

Dr. Tony D’Occhio, a Connecticut native, joined the practice as Partner in 2013, following an extensive professional education at UConn School of Dental Medicine, a dental residency program in Chicago, and several years of private practice experience in the Boston area. In addition, one of our original founders, Dr. Brian Sawchuk , continues to care for patients dental needs at our office today.

Our team has expanded on this mission by modernizing our dental treatment rooms, embracing and incorporating the latest dental technology and techniques into patient care, and hiring expertly trained, professional dental staff.

Each patient’s dental visit centers around prevention of dental issues through comprehensive evaluation and treatment, along with education of proper oral hygiene. Since our founding and today, we take great pride in consistently delivering a superior dental health care experience for each patient.
