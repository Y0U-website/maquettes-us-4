The practice

We offer an array of oral health services for adults, children and seniors. Our modern equipment, digital x rays, and easily accessible off-street parking make us an easy stop.

We’re always welcoming new patients to our family-oriented dental practice located on the West Side of Cincinnati, Ohio.

Our hours are tailored to ensure that you can fit in a visit with your busy schedule. Limited weekend hours are available.
