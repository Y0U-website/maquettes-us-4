The Academy of General Dentistry suggests checkups twice per year for people of all ages. Regular dental exams help you maintain healthy teeth and gums while also allowing our team to identify concerns early.

We offer veneers as a perfect, natural-looking option for patients wanting to make minor adjustments to the look and feel of their smile.

Invisalign ® is a straightening system that uses a personalized series of invisible, removable, and comfortable aligners that no one can tell you’re wearing.

We offer crowns to strengthen or improve the shape of a tooth that is broken, worn, or partially destroyed by tooth decay. Let us help restore your smile!

It’s our goal to help your child’s visit be positive! Getting to know your young one, using pleasant, non-frightening words, and having fun together is how we will keep you, and your child, wanting to come back again!

If you have pain while chewing or a severe toothache, the cause may be an infection. Our expert team may be able to save your tooth and relieve your pain with a root canal.

Services

We offer dental fillings to treat cavities and to repair cracked or broken teeth. Ask us about tooth-colored fillings that can protect the brightness of your smile.

Also known as periodontal disease, gum disease is an infection of the gums surrounding your teeth and is one of the top reasons for tooth loss in adults. We can help you prevent and/or treat this condition to avoid damage to your smile.

Dental implants are a popular and effective way to replace missing teeth. When our work is through, no one will be able to notice them and you can smile with confidence again.

At Trinity Oaks Family Dental & Orthodontics, we have three options to help you achieve a smile you love: Traditional Braces, Clear Braces, and Invisalign.
