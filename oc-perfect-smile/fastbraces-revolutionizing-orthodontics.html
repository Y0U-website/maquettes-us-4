Choosing Fastbraces offers several compelling benefits:

If you’re considering orthodontic treatment but are hesitant about the long duration of traditional braces, Fastbraces might be an ideal solution. They are suitable for both children and adults looking for a quicker path to a healthier, more beautiful smile.

  • Flexible Wires: The wires used in Fastbraces are more adaptable, which means they can exert the optimal amount of pressure needed to align teeth and roots simultaneously.
  • Gentle Forces: The system applies gentle forces that are effective yet comfortable, reducing discomfort often associated with traditional braces.
  • Faster Results: Since both the crown and root of the tooth are targeted at the same time, patients usually see visible results much quicker.
  • Reduced Treatment Time: The most appealing feature is the significantly shorter duration of treatment compared to traditional braces.
  • No Teeth Extraction Needed: One of the most significant benefits of Fastbraces is that they typically eliminate the need for tooth extractions. This is particularly advantageous for patients with crowded teeth, as Fastbraces are designed to create more space in the mouth without the need for removing any teeth. Instead, they focus on moving the roots of the teeth into their proper positions right from the beginning of the treatment.
  • Comfort: Many patients report less discomfort due to the gentle forces applied throughout the treatment.
  • Fewer Appointments: With faster progress, patients typically require fewer visits to the orthodontist, saving time and effort.
  • Improved Oral Health: Correctly aligned teeth can lead to better oral hygiene and reduce the risk of dental issues in the future.
