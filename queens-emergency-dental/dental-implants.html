What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into your jawbone to replace missing tooth roots.

They serve as stable foundations for artificial teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. The result is a natural-looking and functioning tooth that seamlessly blends with your existing teeth.

Advantages of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer numerous advantages over traditional tooth replacement options. They provide superior stability, durability, and aesthetics. Since they are anchored directly into the jawbone, they prevent bone loss and maintain the structural integrity of your face.

How Do Dental Implants Work?

The dental implant procedure involves several steps to ensure a successful and lasting result. It begins with a comprehensive evaluation of your oral health to determine your eligibility for dental implants. Once deemed suitable, the implant placement surgery takes place, where the titanium posts are positioned into the jawbone.

Healing and Osseointegration

After the implant placement, a crucial process called osseointegration occurs.

This is when the titanium post fuses with the surrounding bone, creating a strong and stable foundation for the artificial tooth. Healing time varies depending on individual cases, but it is essential to ensure the long-term success of the dental implant.

Placing the Dental Crown

Once the osseointegration process is complete, the final step involves placing the dental crown onto the implant. The crown is custom-made to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, resulting in a seamless and aesthetically pleasing smile.

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime, providing you with a beautiful and functional smile for years to come.
