Website Accessibility Statement

  • Skip to content Lake Pointe Dental Group is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people of all abilities. We believe everyone deserves a welcoming experience, online and in our office. Our commitment We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, and cognitive differences. Ongoing efforts Using clear structure, readable text, and sufficient color contrast.
  • Providing text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Supporting keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.
  • Reviewing new pages and features for accessibility as our site evolves.

Reporting an issue
