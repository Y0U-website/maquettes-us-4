Robert E. Anderson, D.D.S.

Dr. Robert E. Anderson is a graduate of Loma Linda University, where he received his DDS in 1989. Prior to attending dental school, Dr. Anderson received a Bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry. Following his dental studies, Dr. Anderson established his Placerville, CA dental practice in 1994. For nearly 20 years, he has been helping families in our community through his excellent dental care.

Dr. Anderson is married to his lovely wife, Marilyn, a fifth-grade teacher. He has two step-sons, Nate and Taylor, who are both currently attending college. When he has a moment to himself, Dr. Anderson enjoys traveling, SCUBA diving, and fly-fishing.
