FAQs about Emergency Dentists in Puyallup, WA

What constitutes a dental emergency?

A dental emergency is any situation that requires immediate dental care to alleviate pain, prevent further damage, and preserve oral health. Common dental emergencies include severe toothaches, broken or knocked-out teeth, dental abscesses, and uncontrolled bleeding.

What should I do for a knocked-out tooth?

If you have a knocked-out tooth, it’s essential to immediately seek dental care. Before seeing the emergency dentist, gently rinse the tooth with water and try to re-insert it into the socket. If that’s not possible, store the tooth in a container of milk and bring it with you to the emergency dental appointment.

Should I see a dentist for tooth pain in Puyallup, WA?

Yes. If you have tooth pain in Puyallup, it is important to see a dentist promptly. At SK Family Dental, we offer same-day emergency appointments to assess the cause, such as decay, infection, broken tooth, or abscess, and provide appropriate treatment, such as fillings, root canals, or extraction,s to relieve your discomfort.

Should I see a dentist or a doctor for emergency dental issues?

You should see a dentist when the issue involves your teeth, gums, jaw pain, swelling, toothache, or oral infections. At SK Family Dental in Puyallup, we can diagnose and treat these issues directly. You should see a medical doctor if you have symptoms such as fever, facial or neck swelling, trouble breathing, or signs of systemic infection, and we can help you determine if a doctor’s care is needed as well.
