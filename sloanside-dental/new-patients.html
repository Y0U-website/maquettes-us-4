  • All of our new patient exams begin with one of our hygienists. The exam includes a full set of xrays and a thorough evaluation of your teeth and gums.
  • If you have a healthy mouth and have kept up with your routine dental appointments, you should expect to have your teeth cleaned as well.
  • If your dental health requires more attention and effort, you will be given a treatment plan and further care will be scheduled at a later date.
  • If you have recent xrays from another office and would like for us to reference those at your exam, please make arrangements to send those over before your appointment. We are happy to assist with that. A high quality set of xrays are key to providing excellent care to you.
