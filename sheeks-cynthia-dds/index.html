The practice

Led by Dr. Leanne Nichols, our team combines advanced dentistry with a friendly, familiar atmosphere that makes you feel right at home. You’ll find us on the corner of Eagle Way and Main Street, just next to Broomfield High School.

We don’t just care for teeth; we look at the whole person. Every treatment plan is thoughtfully personalized around your lifestyle, medical history, medications, and overall health. By understanding how your oral health connects to your total well-being, we’re able to provide care that supports long-term comfort, balance, and confidence.

Our comprehensive, patient-centered, and family-focused philosophy means we aim to prevent problems before they start, so you can spend more time smiling and less time in the dental chair. Ultimately, we want to help you enjoy a healthy smile for life!

When you visit Main Street Dental Studio, you’ll see familiar faces. Many of our team members have worked together for over 20 years. This sense of continuity and genuine connection shows in everything we do. We take the time to listen, to explain, and to make sure every patient feels comfortable and well cared for. Our team dynamic is one of warmth, laughter, and trust the kind that turns…

We combine compassionate care with the latest dental technology. Our Broomfield dental office features digital x-rays, advanced sensors, and OralID screenings for early, accurate detection of oral health issues. These tools make your visits more precise, efficient, and comfortable. But what truly sets us apart is the personal attention behind every treatment. Our patients trust us not just for…

We understand that going to the dentist can feel intimidating for some people, which is why we go out of our way to make every visit calm and comfortable. You’ll find pillows, blankets, and a friendly team that truly listens. For those who need a little extra help relaxing, we offer nitrous oxide and oral sedation options.

From your very first visit, you’ll notice the difference. Patients are welcomed with an in-depth conversation with one of our dentists to get to know you, discuss your goals, and create a personalized plan for your smile.

At Main Street Dental Studio, we believe great dentistry comes from a blend of knowledge, compassion, and connection. We’d love to welcome you to a practice where healthy smiles lead to happier lives. We invite you to experience the difference of care that’s comprehensive, refreshingly personal, and centered on you.
