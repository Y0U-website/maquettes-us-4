Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Dr. Romack and her husband have been married since 1999 and have two daughters and six grandchildren. In her words, Being a Grandma is awesome! Their oldest, Melissa, graduated from Texas A&M Law School and lives in Fort Worth. Their youngest daughter, Sara, is a combat veteran and served as a Naval Aircraft Mechanic for five years. She graduated from Midwestern State University with a B.S. degree in Radiological Sciences.

When not at the office, Dr. Romack and her husband both enjoy golfing, fly fishing, playing cards, and spending time with their grandbabies and friends.
