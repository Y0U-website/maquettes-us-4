Why is professional care necessary despite good home hygiene?

Even the most diligent brushers and flossers can’t remove all plaque. Plaque is a sticky substance that continuously builds up on teeth and can only be effectively removed with professional tools. Without regular professional cleanings, plaque can lead to cavities, gum disease, and other issues that might not be immediately apparent.

What are the benefits of early detection and treatment?

Routine exams allow Dr. Childers to detect and address oral health issues in their early stages. Early treatment is generally more effective, less invasive, and more affordable. Identifying problems early helps prevent more severe conditions and ensures that your oral health remains in top shape.
