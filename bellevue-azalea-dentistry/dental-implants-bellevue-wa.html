What to Expect: The Implant Process Step by Step

We believe that understanding the process helps patients feel more confident and at ease. Here’s a general overview:

  • Consultation & Digital Imaging Dr. Sharma will assess your oral health and use advanced imaging to create a precise treatment plan.
  • Tooth Extraction (if needed) Any damaged or decayed teeth are gently removed.
  • Bone Grafting (if necessary) If your jawbone lacks density, we may recommend a bone graft to strengthen the area.
  • Implant Placement A titanium post is placed into your jawbone in a minimally invasive procedure.
  • Healing Period (Osseointegration) Over the next 3–6 months, the implant fuses naturally with your bone.
  • Abutment & Crown Placement Once healing is complete, we place a custom-made crown that blends perfectly with your smile.

Dental implants are considered a safe tooth replacement procedure. They are made from titanium and other materials which are compatible with the human body. For the majority of patients in good health, implants are successful when performed by a suitably qualified professional by undertaking detailed pre and post surgery examination/investigation. However, dental implants do pose a small degree of risk but you do need to meet certain eligibility criteria before your dentist recommends implants as a course of treatment.

Are there any side effects of dental implants?

The recovery time may vary depending on how the procedure went, how well you take care of yourself post surgery and how your body reacts to the implant. If you underwent a simple or moderate procedure, you can typically resume your regular diet within seven to 30 days. Usually, within 5-7 days of the surgery all symptoms tends to subside. However, some may experience longer recovery time due to different factors. So, depending on whether you get a single implant, multiple implants, or All-On-4 dental implants, your recovery time can vary.

Dental Implant cost vary based on number of teeth to be replaced and type of material to be used for making of artificial teeth: Individual cemented crowns, screw-retained crowns or fixed bridges, removable (snap on) bridges or complete dentures stabilized by implants. A single implant crown may cost between $1,000 and $3,000. Implant bridges replacing several or many teeth range from $3,500 to $25,000.

How to get dental implants covered by insurance? Generally, dental implants in Bellevue are not covered by dental insurance. However, more companies are covering dental implants today than they did several years ago. In many cases, your insurance can help pay for the teeth that are placed on the dental implants, if not the implants themselves. You can call your dental insurance provider and ask if dental implants are a covered procedure.
