Prevent. Restore. Maintain.

Inside our modern dental home you’ll find a friendly and experienced team dedicated to helping each patient prevent, restore and maintain a healthy mouth for life. Our goal is that you always leave feeling better than you came!

We’re a friendly dental home for the whole family. We love our patients, and we love welcoming new ones to our dental family! If you’re looking for a dental home that treats patients right and offers a wide range of services for all ages, you’ve come to the right place. We are warm, caring, qualified and nonjudgmental. Whether it’s been 5, 10 or 25 years since you’ve been to the dentist, we want to see you! We are proud to be the local practice in the area that patients come from near and far to visit!

We seek to prevent, restore & maintain optimal oral health. Good dental care goes beyond simply fixing smiles. It takes into consideration your overall well-being and finds long-term solutions that fit your individual needs. We proudly offer this type of dental experience. We help patients prevent problems before they start, we restore smiles back to optimal health, and we put a plan in place to maintain the results for life! Through Dr. Patel’s philosophy of Prevent, Restore & Maintain, we point our patients toward a lifetime of optimal oral health!

Our patients leave feeling better than they came. If you can’t tell, your health & happiness are of the utmost importance to us, which is why we go out of our way to help our patients have the best dental experience possible. If you are experiencing dental pain or discomfort, we will help bring relief. If you feel nervous or anxious, we will put you at ease. If you are already having a great day, we hope to make it even greater! No matter how you walk through or doors, we will do everything we can to ensure you leave our practice feeling better than you came!

Our environment is comfortable & patient-focused. We know that a large portion of your satisfaction comes from how you feel during your visits. Because of this, your comfort is a top priority. Inside our treatment rooms, you’ll find TVs for you to enjoy your favorite show as well as sunlit windows overlooking scenic mountains to help you relax. Our office is also large and spacious for your comfort, safety and privacy. Additionally, our patient-focused team will remain attentive to your needs and always work with a gentle touch. We are confident your experience will be an enjoyable one.

We put patients before profit. We are proud to be a locally owned practice that doesn’t have to listen to big dental corporations who care little about the individual patient. Dr. Patel truly has your best interests in mind. You matter to us, and you’ll see this in the relationships we build here. We get to know our patients personally and will customize your care to fit your unique needs. We don’t oversell. Because you mean much more to us than a paycheck, we will be open, honest and always do what’s best for you.

We maintain a clean, state-of-the-art dental facility. We’re excited to show off our brand new, state-of-the-art dental facility full of the latest high-end equipment! We have all new low-dose radiographs & 3D imaging for your safety, a Cone Beam CT scanner for improved accuracy and speed of treatment, and a new state-of-the-art sterilization center for enhanced infection control and proper cleaning of the workplace. We take our investment in the best technologies seriously and will continue to update our practice to better serve you.

About US / Meet Our Team

Meet Dr. Ankit K. Patel

Dr. Ankit K. Patel grew up in Birmingham, AL and graduated from Homewood High School. Following high school, he graduated Summa Cum Laude with University Honors from the University of Alabama at Birmingham (UAB) in Biology. During his time in college, he was active in various leadership roles including the UAB Ambassadors and Leadership Service Council and received the Mr. UAB award in 2005.

Dr. Patel completed dental school at UAB and his Advanced General Dentistry Residency at the Veterans Affairs Hospitals in Birmingham and Tuscaloosa. He then practiced dentistry in Madison, AL where he diligently expanded his dental education and clinical skills while working at a well-structured and caring office. In 2020, he moved to Georgia to pursue the next level in his career and felt at home in Cartersville.

Dr. Patel is described as kind, compassionate, attentive, and more than willing to go the extra mile. He is excited to deliver the high quality that patients expect and want as he guides them towards lasting oral health through his philosophy of Prevent, Restore, and Maintain.

Dr. Patel is a current member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Georgia Dental Association, and the local district dental society.

During his free time, Dr. Patel enjoys spending time with his wife and son, being close with his family, attending continuing education classes, enjoying outdoor activities, exploring new restaurants, and traveling.

Meet Dr. Matthew A. Dimassi

Dr. Matthew A. Dimassi brings a well-rounded background in family and cosmetic dental care to our practice. He received his Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the prestigious University of Alabama School of Dentistry. He then completed a 1 year Advanced Education in General Dentistry providing treatment to our nation’s veterans at the VA Hospital in Birmingham, Alabama. Additionally he serves as an adjunct faculty member at the Ben Massell Dental Clinic, where he treats patients of limited means and also helps dental students improve their skills through instruction and mentoring.

When not treating patients, Matthew enjoys spending time with his wife, son and two dogs. Additionally, he enjoys outdoor activities including hiking, camping and mountain biking.
