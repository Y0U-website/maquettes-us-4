At Highlands Point Dental, we believe in the power of community and giving back. That’s why we are proud to support Lasagna Love, a remarkable grassroots organization dedicated to making a positive impact on the lives of individuals and families facing food insecurity and challenging times.

What is Lasagna Love?

Lasagna Love is a heartwarming community-based initiative that began in 2020, responding to the unique challenges brought about by the COVID-19 pandemic. Its mission is simple yet incredibly powerful: to connect caring volunteers with families in need, all while sharing the comfort of homemade lasagna.

How Can You Get Involved?

There are several ways you can join us in supporting Lasagna Love:

Spread the Word: Help us raise awareness by sharing information about Lasagna Love with your friends, family, and social networks. You can start by sharing this message or visiting their official website at https://lasagnalove.org to learn more.

Become a Volunteer: Consider becoming a Lasagna Love volunteer in your local community. By preparing and delivering homemade lasagna meals to those who could use some extra support, you can make a meaningful di erence in someone’s life.

Donate: If you’re unable to volunteer your time, you can still contribute by making a donation directly to Lasagna Love. Every contribution, no matter how big or small, helps in their mission to provide meals and support to those in need.

Request a Meal: If you or someone you know is facing challenges and could benefit from a comforting meal, don’t hesitate to reach out to Lasagna Love and request a meal.

Join Us in Making a Difference At Highlands Point Dental, we believe that small acts of kindness can have a big impact. By supporting Lasagna Love, we hope to inspire our community to come together, share the love, and make a positive di erence in the lives of those who need it most.

Let’s create a world where everyone has access to a warm meal and a helping hand during challenging times. Together, we can make that vision a reality.
