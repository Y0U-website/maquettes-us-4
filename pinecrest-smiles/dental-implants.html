Dental Implants

  • Natural Look & Feel : Indistinguishable from your natural teeth
  • Full Functionality : Eat, speak, and smile with complete confidence
  • No Impact on Adjacent Teeth : Unlike bridges, healthy teeth remain untouched

The Implant Process

Initial Consultation & Planning

We conduct a comprehensive examination including 3D cone-beam CT scans to assess bone density and structure. Digital planning allows us to map the exact implant placement before surgery begins.

Implant Placement Surgery

Using local anesthesia or sedation, we place the titanium implant post into your jawbone. This minimally invasive procedure typically takes 1-2 hours per implant. Most patients return to work the next day.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates the strong foundation needed for your permanent tooth.

Abutment & Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, we attach an abutment (connector) and custom-crafted crown. Your new tooth is color-matched to blend seamlessly with your natural smile.

Candidates for Dental Implants

Even if you’ve been told you’re not a candidate due to bone loss, modern techniques like bone grafting and sinus lifts can often make implants possible.

  • Adequate bone density (or willingness to undergo bone grafting)
  • Healthy gums free from periodontal disease
  • Good overall health and oral hygiene habits
  • Realistic expectations about the process and timeline

Single Tooth vs. Multiple Teeth

Single Tooth Implants Replace one missing tooth without affecting adjacent teeth. The most conservative and effective solution.

Multiple Implants Replace several missing teeth with individual implants or implant-supported bridges.

Full Arch Restoration Replace an entire upper or lower arch with as few as 4-6 implants using All-on-4 or similar techniques.

Implant Success Rate

Dental implants have a success rate exceeding 95 % when placed by experienced professionals. Factors that contribute to success include:

  • Non-smoking status (or willingness to quit)
  • Good oral hygiene practices
  • Regular dental check-ups
  • Adequate bone density
  • Proper healing compliance

Caring for Your Implants

Implants require the same care as natural teeth:

"My implants changed my life. I can eat anything I want and smile without embarrassment. Best investment I ever made." David R.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Floss daily, including around the implant crown
  • Use antibacterial mouthwash if recommended
  • Attend regular dental cleanings and check-ups
  • Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice or pens
