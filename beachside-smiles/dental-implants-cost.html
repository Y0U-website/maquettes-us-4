Average Dental Implants Cost

Dental implants are classified as oral surgery and all the complex characteristics of this procedure make it an advanced restorative dentistry treatment. Associated costs reflect the nature of the work, but it is important to remember that implants are a permanent tooth loss solution. The restorations reduce the need for certain types of future dental care by improving overall oral health. Smiles fortified with dental implants are stronger and healthier and can protect you from the undesirable aspects of dentures.

Keep these facts in mind when considering out-of-pocket costs before dental insurance is applied to the full treatment. All costs listed here are general estimates and do not reflect the final cost of your particular procedure:

Costs multiply, of course, the more dental implants you receive or if you need implant-supported dentures for an entire arch of teeth, but this worthwhile investment is possible so you can have a beautiful, fully functional, complete smile.

  • One dental implant costs between $1,000 and $3,000.
  • The abutment, crown, or implant denture costs between $500 and $3,000.
  • The surgery costs between $1,500 and $6,000 per tooth, a price that typically includes all consultations and follow-ups.
  • Gum grafts and oral sedation may incur additional costs or be rolled into the surgery price.

Paying for Dental Implants Costs

Your Manhattan Beach dentist wants you to have excellent oral health and is prepared to make all the costs as manageable as possible, no matter what type of restorative dentistry you need. The following options are often combined to create a complete payment plan that blends with your budget and oral health needs:

  • Dental insurance: Because dental implants are categorized as restorative dentistry, some of the cost is often covered by insurance.
  • Financing: Affordable monthly payment plans and low fixed interest rates are available through your dentist or an affiliate to make it easier to spread out your investment over months or years as necessary.
  • Credit cards: Low- or no-interest credit cards may work to your advantage for dental implants costs, as well as balance transfer offers.
  • Cash or check: If you have resources at your fingertips, or even partial resources, cash or check is a great way to make a dent in overall dental implants costs.
