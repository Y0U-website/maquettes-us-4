Preventive Tools Beyond the Exam

Prevention is always our first priority, and we offer specialized tools that protect your child’s smile before problems have a chance to develop. These preventive measures are customized to your child’s specific activities and needs, providing protection that goes beyond regular oral hygiene.

Custom Sports Mouthguards for Kids

Children who participate in contact sports face a significant risk of dental injuries, from chipped teeth to completely knocked-out teeth. Custom sports mouthguards provide far superior protection compared to store-bought boil-and-bite options, offering a comfortable fit that won’t interfere with breathing or communication during play.

We recommend custom mouthguards for children participating in football and soccer, which are high-impact sports with frequent contact and collision risks. Basketball and hockey are fast-paced games where accidental contact with elbows, sticks, or balls is common. Wrestling and martial arts involve direct physical contact that can impact facial areas, while baseball and softball activities expose children to fast-moving balls that can cause sudden dental trauma.

Our custom mouthguards are made from safe, biocompatible materials that fit your child’s mouth perfectly. This ensures maximum protection while allowing for comfortable wear during practices and games. Investing in a custom mouthguard now can prevent costly and painful dental emergencies later.

Kids Nightguards for Teeth Grinding

Many children grind their teeth at night, a condition that can cause significant damage to developing teeth and create jaw discomfort. Signs of teeth grinding include worn teeth surfaces, jaw soreness in the morning, or complaints of headaches upon waking. If left untreated, grinding can affect your child’s bite development and cause long-term dental problems.

Dr. Grace evaluates signs of teeth grinding during every routine visit, looking for wear patterns on teeth and asking about symptoms like morning jaw pain or restless sleep. When we identify grinding concerns, we may recommend a custom nightguard designed specifically for your child’s growing mouth.

Our kids’ nightguards protect developing teeth from the excessive forces of grinding while supporting proper jaw alignment. Unlike adult nightguards, these are designed to accommodate your child’s changing mouth and growing teeth, providing protection without interfering with normal development.

Ongoing Care and Risk-Based Recommendations

At The Smile Place, we understand that every child has different oral health needs based on their diet, hygiene habits, genetics, and medical history. That’s why we develop personalized care recommendations based on your child’s individual risk level. This collaborative approach ensures that your child receives exactly the right level of preventive care.

Low-Risk Patients

Children who demonstrate excellent oral hygiene, maintain a tooth-friendly diet, and show no signs of decay or gum problems typically continue with our standard six-month checkup schedule. For these patients, we focus on maintaining their strong hygiene routines and encouraging continued excellent habits.

We celebrate these children’s success while continuing to monitor their development and oral health. Even low-risk patients benefit from regular professional cleanings and examinations to ensure they stay on the right track as they grow and their oral health needs evolve.

Moderate-Risk Patients

Children who show early signs of gum irritation, have dietary challenges, or need improvement in their brushing technique receive additional support and guidance. We provide personalized dietary counseling and work closely with both parent and child to review and improve oral hygiene techniques.

For moderate-risk patients, we might recommend more frequent hygiene coaching, specific dietary modifications, or additional monitoring between regular visits. Our goal is to help these children move into the low-risk category through education and improved habits rather than waiting for problems to develop.

High-Risk Patients

Children with a history of cavities, high sugar intake, medical conditions affecting oral health, or significant hygiene challenges receive our most intensive preventive support. We may recommend dental sealants on permanent molars, more frequent prophy visits, and comprehensive habit change coaching.

Our approach for high-risk patients includes detailed nutritional guidance, intensive oral hygiene education, and closer monitoring of developing issues. We work as partners with families to identify practical solutions that fit your lifestyle while significantly improving your child’s oral health outcomes.

FAQ – Routine Dental Hygiene for Children

How often should my child have a dental cleaning?

We recommend visiting every 6 months for a prophy and exam to catch issues early and reinforce healthy habits. Some high-risk children may benefit from more frequent visits, while most children do well with the standard six-month schedule.

What’s the difference between a prophy and a cleaning?

A prophy is a professional dental cleaning performed by a hygienist to remove plaque and tartar. It’s the clinical term we use in children’s care and refers to the preventive nature of the treatment, focusing on maintaining health rather than treating disease.

Are mouthguards really necessary for kids?

Yes — if your child plays contact sports, a mouthguard helps prevent dental injuries like chipped or knocked-out teeth. Custom mouthguards provide the best protection and comfort, making them a worthwhile investment in your child’s dental safety.

What if my child grinds their teeth at night?

We may recommend a nightguard if we see signs of grinding, which can protect developing teeth and ease jaw stress. Many children outgrow grinding, but protecting their teeth during this phase prevents permanent damage from occurring.

How do you determine if my child is high risk for cavities?

We assess diet, hygiene habits, medical history, and past decay experience. Based on risk level, we create a personalized hygiene plan that provides the right level of preventive care for your child’s specific needs.
