Meet Dr. Chand

“I’m extremely passionate about making people feel better about their smile. I love how my patients feel when they leave the dentist happy. I get excited when people see how much improvement to their daily life a visit to the dentist can make!

The thing I appreciate the most of my patients is the level of trust they have in me. When you work with patients of all ages, sometimes dentistry can get challenging. It’s when I hear feedback about how amazing a visit went that my work seems truly valuable.”

Education and Continuing Education

A native of Great Falls, Dr. Uppasna Chand attended Langley High School in McLean, Virginia. Her undergraduate education was earned at Virginia Tech , from which she graduated in 2002. Dr. Chand then continued on to Virginia Commonwealth University for dental school, graduating in 2009 with her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Continuing her education both in-person and online, Dr. Chand has attended courses on occlusion and Invisalign®, in addition to all aspects of general and cosmetic dentistry. She is committed to staying up-to-date on the newest trends in dental technology.

Outside the Office

Currently residing in Vienna, Virginia with her husband, Samir, Dr. Chand loves spending time with her family. Her other interests include reading a good book, being outdoors, bike riding, watching movies, and trying new restaurants in the area.

Meet Dr. Evelyn Chou

“I initially chose dentistry as my profession because it was the perfect marriage of science and art. In my 10+ years of practicing dentistry, I have learned that there is so much more to dentistry than just teeth. I value the relationships we form with our patients and the lasting impression we, as dentists, are able to leave with them.”

Education and Continuing Education

Dr. Chou grew up outside Baltimore, Maryland. She majored in Bioengineering at the University of Pennsylvania, where she graduated cum laude with a Bachelor of Science in Engineering degree. She then returned to Maryland where she studied at the University of Maryland Baltimore College of Dental Surgery and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Dr. Chou continues to take continuing education courses in various aspects of dentistry including Invisalign and dental sleep medicine. She has an interest in airway dentistry and has experience with oral appliance therapy (OAT) to treat sleep-disordered breathing, which includes obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and snoring.

Following graduation, Dr. Chou practiced general dentistry in Southern Maryland for over 10 years. She is thrilled to have joined the team at McLean Family Dentistry and be much closer to home! With the advanced technology that McLean Family Dentistry has to offer, Dr. Chou is able to diagnose and provide treatment plans for patients by taking a holistic approach to comprehensive care and prevention.

Outside the Office

Dr. Chou resides in nearby Vienna, Virginia with her husband and two daughters. When not at the office, you can probably find her attending classes at Pure Barre. She loves food, and is always looking for great restaurant recommendations.

Meet Dr. Rania Abich

I am dedicated to understanding your individual health goals, and creating a personalized plan that empowers and equips my patients with the knowledge they need to make impactful change happen with their oral health. For over a decade, I have been committed to delivering a comprehensive and transparent approach to dentistry. It truly moves me to the core to create confident, healthy smiles! I get to help my patients grow in understanding the connection between functional health and aesthetic dentistry, and that’s really awesome!

Education and Continuing Education

Dr. Rania Abich earned her undergraduate degree in Neurobiology and Physiology from the University of Maryland, College Park, before completing her Doctorates of Dental Surgery at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2013. Following dental school, Dr. Rania continued her training through a General Practice Residency at the VA Medical Center in Palo Alto, California. There, she honed her expertise in advanced comprehensive care, particularly in treating medically complex patients in collaboration with the Stanford University Oral Medicine and Maxillofacial Surgery Clinic.

Dedicated to clinical excellence, Dr. Rania continues to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry through numerous continuing education events and organizations. She is a certified Invisalign provider, an ambassador of The Breathe Institute, and a proud member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, and the Northern Virginia Dental Society.

Dr. Rania brings a passion for precision, compassion, and comprehensive care to every patient she sees. Known for her thoughtful communication and attention to detail, she guides her patients to make informed decisions and take pride in their smiles. Above all, she values building trust through meaningful relationships and making a genuine difference in the lives of those she serves.

Outside the Office

You can usually find Dr. Rania tucked in a cozy coffee shop with a good book or chasing after her two adorable boys at a nearby park. She also enjoys interior design, traveling to explore new cultures, and is always on the hunt for the richest European style hot chocolate come the holidays. She brings the same energy and care to her personal life as she does to her practice—always curious, always engaged, and always ready to connect.
