Can a dental bridge replace one or more missing teeth?

Yes. A dental bridge can replace one or more missing teeth by filling the gap with artificial teeth called poetics and anchoring them to your natural teeth or dental implants on either side. Most traditional bridges replace one to three teeth in a row while implant supported options can support four or more.

Is professional teeth whitening worth it?

Yes. Professional teeth whitening is a safe and effective way to reduce stains and brighten discoloured teeth without damaging the tooth surface. Teeth can become stained by coffee, tea, wine, smoking or certain medications. That’s why we use ADA-recommended whitening procedures and products to deliver a noticeably brighter and healthier looking smile.

What are the benefits of choosing Invisalign over traditional braces?

Invisalign offers a comfortable and discreet way to straighten your teeth using clear, custom-made aligners instead of metal brackets and wires. The removable aligners make it easier to eat, brush and floss allowing you to maintain your daily routine while achieving a healthier and more confident smile.

What is the difference between complete and partial dentures?

Complete dentures are recommended when all teeth in the upper or lower jaw are missing while partial dentures are designed for patients who still have healthy natural teeth. At Shrijana Shakya DDS Inc., we will evaluate your oral health and recommend the option that best restores your smile, comfort and function.

Is nitrous oxide safe for dental procedures?

Yes. It is commonly known as ‘laughing gas’ and is a safe and effective method to help patients relax during dental procedures. It reduces dental anxiety, takes effect quickly, is carefully monitored and wears off within minutes allowing most patients to resume their normal activities soon afterward.
