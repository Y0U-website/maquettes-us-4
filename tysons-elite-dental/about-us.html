About Us

Tysons Elite Dental is a state-of-the-art dental office located in the heart of Vienna, Virginia. The practice caters to patients of all ages and backgrounds from all over Fairfax County and surrounding areas.

The warm, friendly staff, which is led by Bahar Bahrami, DDS, makes patients feel at home when they walk through the door. Even those who deal with dental anxiety and fear will be at ease under the team’s calm, compassionate care.

At Tysons Elite Dental, the team uses cutting edge technology to ensure patients receive the absolute best oral health care treatment available today. Techniques the staff uses, such as sedation dentistry and digital scanning technology, make treatments less invasive and more convenient.

The team proudly offers a wide variety of services, including Invisalign® and tooth-colored fillings. It doesn’t matter if you’re coming in for a routine check-up, a professional teeth whitening, or for a veneer fitting, the team always adopts a patient-centered approach. They work with you to understand your needs and preferences, routinely developing treatment plans that work for you and deliver optimal results. That’s why patients keep coming back and trust the Tysons Elite Dental team with their most precious commodity: their smiles.

To join the Tysons Elite Dental family today, just call the office or schedule your next visit online.
