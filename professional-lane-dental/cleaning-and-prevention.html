CHECKUPS AND CLEANINGS

Patients of all ages should receive a checkup and cleaning at least every six months. Following through with this regular appointment is the best way to keep your smile healthy for life. While you are here, you will receive a thorough examination and teeth cleaning. Then, we will assess your smile, recommend therapies to treat or prevent dental problems, and provide tips on effective homecare. DENTAL SEALANTS

Kids are at a high risk of tooth decay between the ages of 6 and 14. During this period, they can benefit greatly from dental sealants. These thin protective layers can be painted directly onto the irregular surfaces of premolars and molars, literally sealing out acids and bacteria to prevent tooth decay. It only takes a few minutes to apply dental sealants and they can provide years of protection. Cavity-prone adults can benefit from dental sealants as well.

PERIODONTAL THERAPY

The majority of adults over the age of 35 have gum disease to some extent, and if left untreated, this condition gradually destroys oral health and can result in complete tooth loss. If your gums show signs of periodontal (gum) disease during your routine checkup and cleaning, Dothan Dentist Drs. Joseph Dougherty and Trenton Dunford can provide treatment to ease discomfort and renew periodontal health.

TMJ THERAPY

Whether due to stress or dental misalignment, many adults experience jaw pain as they age. This chronic pain is due to strain on the temporomandibular joint, or TMJ. We alleviate TMJ-related distress with custom-fitted mouthguards that promote proper alignment and restore healthy function. Treating TMJ prevents wear and tear on the teeth, reducing the risk of sensitivity and tooth loss that can occur due to teeth grinding and jaw clenching.
