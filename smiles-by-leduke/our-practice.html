Our Team

Dr. Randall LeDuke is a graduate of University of Memphis and received his dental degree from the University Of Tennessee College Of Dentistry. He is a past president of the Tennessee component of the Academy of General Dentistry, ( www.AGD.org ) and has achieved AGD Master status, one of only 41 AGD Master dentists in Tennessee.

Dr. LeDuke is certified to administer Limited Conscious Sedation. He received training in this area through the Dental Organization of Conscious Sedation ( DOCS ) program.

Aesthetic or cosmetic dentistry is a passion with Dr. LeDuke. He has logged hundreds of hours of Continuing Education in the study of esthetic dentistry technique and is a member of the Tennessee Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry. Please view our Smile Gallery for examples of our work.

Dr. LeDuke is not an Orthodontic Specialist. However, he has treated hundreds of children and adults within his dental practice over the past twenty-five years. Conventional braces, clear ceramic braces and clear aligner orthodontics, ( ClearCorrect.com ) are available at our office. Dr. LeDuke has a special interest in interceptive therapies for achieving better development and alignment of the jaws of young, growing patients. See our Smile Gallery for some examples.

Dr. LeDuke and his wife enjoy the beauty of Obion County and have lived here since 1981.
