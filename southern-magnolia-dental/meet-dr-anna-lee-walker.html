Meet Dr. Anna Lee Walker

A Foundation of Compassionate Care

Dr. Anna Lee Walker grew up in Climax, Georgia, and graduated from Bainbridge High School. She earned a Bachelor of Science in Biological Sciences from the University of Georgia before completing a Master of Science in Microbiology at the University of Florida. She then received her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Dental College of Georgia.

Dr. Walker is passionate about providing comprehensive, patient centered dental care and helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. She is committed to building lasting relationships with her patients and their families while creating a welcoming and supportive dental experience.

Compassionate Care with a Personal Touch

Dr. Walker is honored to serve her community and looks forward to caring for her patients for years to come. She enjoys helping patients feel comfortable while supporting their long term oral health through personalized dental care.

In February 2026, Dr. Walker and her husband, Tanner, welcomed their daughter, Essie, into the world. When she is not caring for patients, she enjoys antiquing, reading, needlepoint, cooking, spending time outdoors with her family, and making memories with their two Labrador retrievers, Crew and Argos.
