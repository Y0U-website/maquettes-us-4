Prevention first, always

Dr. Raju built Tender Dental Care on a simple idea: an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. She listens carefully, explains what she sees in plain language, and recommends the most conservative treatment that will genuinely work, which is why so many of her patients have stayed with her for twenty years and now bring their children.

Her special interest in sleep apnea comes from personal experience, and she has seen first-hand how the right oral appliance can change a patient&#x27;s days as well as their nights. She is also trained in conscious sedation through DOCS, so even the most anxious patients can get the care they need comfortably.
