What information do we collect, and how can you access it?

Sweet Smiles Dentistry learns certain information about you from your visits to the Websites and what we do with this information depends on what you do when you are visiting the Websites. This Policy explains the uses we currently make of this information. This Policy lets you know what choices, access and security you have with respect to information Sweet Smiles Dentistry collects.

How is this information used?

If you do not consent to permit us to share your personal information with third parties, we will use that information only to track your visits to our web site, which enables us to learn more about what you, specifically, and our customers, in general, are expecting from our service.

If you do consent to allow us to share your personal information with third parties, we will only use that information according to the consent you give. We may use aggregate data (information about our base of users in general, from which all personally-identifying information has been removed) for any purpose.

Note that certain links on our web pages will take you off the Websites to web pages operated by third parties. Sweet Smiles Dentistry will have no control over the information you enter or the cookies your browser accepts while on third parties’ sites. You should examine the terms of use and privacy policies of other web pages whenever you leave one of the Websites.

When we use personal information gathered through the Websites we will do so pursuant to this Policy. Children
