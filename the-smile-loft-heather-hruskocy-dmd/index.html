A Lake Forest native, Dr. Heather Hruskocy is thrilled to have opened The Smile Loft in her hometown. The Smile Loft is a modern private dental practice serving patients of all ages conveniently located on the west side of Lake Forest. Dr. Hruskocy is devoted to educating her patients and tailoring their treatment options to best fit their unique needs. She is committed to providing her patients…

In 2014, Dr. Hruskocy graduated from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine and earned her Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD). She practiced in the city of Chicago for 9 years before opening The Smile Loft in Lake Forest in 2023.

Growing up in Lake Forest, Dr. Hruskocy attended the School of St. Mary and graduated from Lake Forest High School. While at LFHS, she was on the varsity dance team for four years. Dr. Hruskocy attended the University of Wisconsin in Madison (Go Badgers!), where she received her Bachelor of Science degree in Biology in 2009.
