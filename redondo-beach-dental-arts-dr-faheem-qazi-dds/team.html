Meet Our Team

modern dentistry in a comfortable environment

Providing compassionate, gentle dentistry is the heart of our practice. By offering accessible insurance and financing options, we are able to lower barriers to superior dental care, which allows us to make real differences in our patients’ lives. Everything we do at Redondo Beach Dental Arts is with you in mind. We love to have fun with our patients and hope that we will be able to form lasting friendships as we help maintain your smile. We always provide honest, straightforward advice and strive to keep you informed every step of the way.

Doctors

Dr. Faheem Qazi, DDS

Dr. Qazi is a South Bay native, raised in Torrance. He received his undergraduate degree in 3 years from University of the Pacific’s accelerated dental program, and graduated from University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, considered by many to be the best dental school in the nation.

After practicing in Northern California, Dr. Qazi returned home to pursue his dream of caring for his local community. He looks forward to providing one-on-one, individualized care and helping his patients achieve their dental needs and goals.

Dr. Qazi is caring and takes pride in delivering high-quality and long-lasting dentistry with ethics. He is up-to-date on the latest technology, training, and techniques to ensure that his patients receive the best care. His top priority is to make sure patients feel at ease each time they are in the office. Dr. Qazi is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, the West Los Angeles Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry.

Outside of the office, Dr. Qazi loves to spend time with his large extended family, and friends that he has known for decades. He loves reading, exercising, playing basketball, and searching for new Mediterranean restaurants (lamb dishes are his weakness). There is a good chance you may see Dr. Qazi around the community, so don’t hesitate to stop him and say hello!

dr. tandis nami, associate dentist

Dr. Tandis Nami grew up in Southern California and attended the University of California, Los Angeles, where she received a Bachelor’s degree in Biology. She pursued a Doctoral degree in Dentistry from the prestigious University of California, San Francisco School of Dentistry in 2002. After finishing at UCSF Dental School, she moved to Los Angeles and has been practicing here with over 20 years of experience in general, family, and cosmetic dentistry.

Dr. Nami is a renowned, board-certified dentist using state-of-the-art technology to help her patients restore their smiles and maintain an optimal level of dental health. She believes healthy teeth and gums are not only a powerful source of self-confidence, but they are also the driving force behind overall body health and she prides herself on being able to create a healthy smile for her patients.

Dr. Nami stays current with cutting edge developments in the field of dentistry by continuously educating herself through various seminars and continuing education courses. Additionally, she is an active member of American Dental Association, the California Dental Society, and the West LA Dental Society. Dr. Nami provides a personalized and comprehensive treatment approach to all of her new and existing patients for long-term health. High standard of quality and patient care is of utmost importance to her.

When she is out of the office, she can be found with her husband and beautiful dog Ginger.
