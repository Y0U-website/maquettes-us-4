Tad Suzuki, DDS

My Practice

My dental specialty practice is dedicated to surgical and non surgical endodontic treatment. I am committed to provide you with the highest standard of patient care in a friendly and relaxed environment. Dr. Suzuki and his professional staff are dedicated to provide you with comprehensive endodontic care utilizing the latest advancements in technology. We work closely with your general dentist and strive to make sure you have a positive experience and we exceed your expectations.

My advanced diagnostic equipment includes surgical operating microscopes, Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) and digital X-rays. Universal infection control guidelines and OSHA protocols are followed to insure the safety of our patients and staff.

Endodontics or “Root Canal Therapy” is a dental specialty of the American Dental Association. We are specialists members of the American Association of Endodontists. Our goal is to help you preserve your healthy teeth for your lifetime. We look forward to be of service to you.
