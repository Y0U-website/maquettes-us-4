Dental Assistant

Rose was born and raised in the Philippines and has lived in Washington for over 20 years. After graduating from dental assisting school in 2015, she began a career focused on helping patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident in the care they receive.

She understands that visiting the dentist can be stressful, so her goal is to make each appointment as comfortable and positive as possible by listening carefully and providing compassionate, dependable support.

Outside of work, Rose enjoys spending time with her husband, children, and her two dogs, as well as hiking and exploring the outdoors.
