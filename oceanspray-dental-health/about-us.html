Oceanspray Dental Health is located in beautiful Gig Harbor, Washington, where we provide gentle, quality dentistry in a comfortable, relaxing environment.

  • Our story

Care that treats you as a Whole Person

Oceanspray Dental Health is committed to providing gentle, quality dentistry to meet your needs, and we’re excited to be given the opportunity to share that with you. We offer many phases of dental care, from dental hygiene and routine care through to full-mouth rehabilitation.

Our team is committed to treating you with the personalized, gentle care that you deserve, in a comfortable and relaxing environment.

At Oceanspray Dental Health, you can expect the best in oral care. Our goal is to determine the dental treatment you need and want, and to do our very best to provide it.

  • Our philosophy

What guides every visit

Our philosophy of treatment at Oceanspray Dental Health consists of:

  • 1 Treating you as a whole person, not only focusing on one tooth at a time.
  • 2 Trying to find the "why" behind what is happening, to help avoid future problems.
  • 3 Providing dental work that lasts a long time with proper care and maintenance.
  • 4 Providing dental work that feels and looks as if it belongs there.
  • 5 Ensuring you are comfortable before, during, and after any procedure, both physically and emotionally.
  • 6 You, the patient, are the driver and absolutely involved in all treatment decisions.
  • Questions Welcome

The more you know, The Better Your Outcome

Please feel free to call Oceanspray Dental Health anytime to ask questions or discuss treatment options. We believe that the more education you have about dentistry, the better your treatment outcomes will be.
