In the Community

There are incredible organizations and people that have been serving Southwest Atlanta long before Westside Dental arrived. We are committed to joining with these partner organizations and individuals to support broader community health in addition to our patient’s oral health.

Blueprint 58 is a community based mentoring organization that believes in restoring communities through relationships. Dr Cole and his family have long supported Blueprint 58’s work in the Adair Park and Pittsburgh neighborhoods in Southwest Atlanta. Westside Dental has been a sponsor and volunteer at their community events and youth sports programs. Westside Dental has also hosted interns and hired from Blueprint 58’s job training program.

Located in the historic Pittsburgh neighborhood, the Atlanta College and Career Academy (ACCA) seeks to provide pathways to careers to APS high school students. Westside Dental partners with ACCA’s dental science pathway program by providing hands-on, work-based learning for students interested in a career in the dental field.
