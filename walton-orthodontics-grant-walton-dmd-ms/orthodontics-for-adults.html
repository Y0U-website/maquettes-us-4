Reasons You May Need Adult Braces

As an adult, getting braces is a personal decision. You may feel that you would have more confidence with a straighter smile, or maybe you are tired of feeling discomfort when eating, speaking, or just in your day-to-day life. Whatever the reason, we are here for you! There are many great options for adults to straighten their teeth affordably and discreetly (if that is a concern). Here are some reasons you may need/want braces as an adult:

There are many reasons to get braces as an adult, and the best way to decide whether or not you need them is by discussing with your dentist and orthodontist. You may want to boost your confidence and get braces for cosmetic reasons and that is absolutely acceptable.

  • Misalignment
  • Improper Bites (including crossbite, open bite, underbite, and overbite)
  • Discomfort or Pain of the Jaw or Teeth
  • Spacing or Crowding
  • Frequent Headaches
  • Eating or Speech Difficulty

Invisalign®

The most popular option for older teens and adults when it comes to braces is Invisalign . A clear alignment system that allows patients to discreetly straighten their teeth within 12-15 months, Invisalign might just be the solution you have been waiting for!

How it Works

Using the latest in orthodontic technology, we use 3D imaging to get a detailed blueprint’ of your teeth. The clear aligners are then custom-made for the unique size and shape of your mouth and teeth. Patients will wear the aligners for at least 22 hours every day removing to eat, speak, or for special occasions. Gradually, the aligners will move the teeth into proper alignment. Patients will see the orthodontist every 4-6 weeks to get the next series of aligners until success has been reached.

Benefits

There are many benefits of Invisalign. Here are just a few:

  • Discreet a perfect solution for professionals or adults that don’t want to draw attention to their mouths during treatment.
  • Flexible it is recommended that the aligners are worn for at least 22 hours every day, but patients have the flexibility to remove them during meals or for special instances such as speaking or photographs.
  • Comfort because the aligners are custom-made, patients love the comfort that Invisalign gives them over traditional braces.
  • Simple No special tools are required for cleaning the aligners, and adjustments don’t need to be made. Simply wear them, clean them by rinsing them with water or an approved denture cleanser, and watch your beautiful smile appear.
  • Fast most patients see results within weeks and complete their treatment within 12-15 months.
