Become A Patient

Eligibility

  • Do not qualify for assistance through Medicaid
  • Do not qualify for Veterans Administration
  • Currently do not have an existing health care coverage
  • Located in Brooke, Ohio, Marshall, Wetzel and Tyler Counties in West Virginia or Belmont County, Ohio.

Necessary Forms

To receive health care services, patients must provide proof of identity:

and proof of income, including one or more of the following, depending on the patient’s situation:

  • Photo ID
  • Form 1040 or 1040A (Tax Return)
  • Form 4506-T (if you don’t file incomes taxes)
  • 1099 Benefits Statement (if you are on Social Security)
  • W-2 from Employer
  • Pay Stubs for One Month
