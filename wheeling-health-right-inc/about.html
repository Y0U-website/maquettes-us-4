Free Health & Wellness Clinic

Equal access to the most basic medical services is still only a dream for many who cannot afford or whose employers do not provide health insurance. With changing economic times, the number of people faced with layoffs, minimum wage jobs, and decreasing benefits, find themselves without proper health care coverage.

Wheeling Health Right believes that every person, no matter their economic status should have access to quality and compassionate health care services.

Our staff and volunteer healthcare professionals, who offer their services at no cost to the clinic or patients, make it possible for us to serve the growing number of individuals that are in need treatment.

Wheeling Health Right now serves 27,000 medically underserved, uninsured or under-insured people in 6 surrounding counties of Wheeling, WV.

Our clinic provides free comprehensive medical care and pharmaceuticals to Ohio Valley patients in need. Wheeling Health Right promotes wellness by offering educational coaching on Disease Prevention and Behavior Modifications that can lead to living a healthier life.

To service the ever-growing need for Mental Healthcare in our region, we are now offering comprehensive Social Service programs on site through a licensed therapist.
