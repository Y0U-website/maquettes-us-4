Welcome to Brigham Dental Care, the Morrisville dental practice of Matt Ebert, DMD!

The practice

We provide outstanding, personalized dental care to the residents of Morrisville, Hyde Park, Stowe, Johnson, Elmore, and other surrounding communities.

One of our most fundamental values is the belief that you are in the driver’s seat when it comes to your dental care. We aren’t here to tell you what to do. Instead, we’re here to provide you with the information you need to make educated choices about your dental health and dental treatment.

We don’t treat you like a cog in an assembly line. You are a unique individual with personal beliefs and preferences, and we’ll treat you with the respect you deserve. Dr. Ebert makes a point of sitting down face to face with his patients. In fact, part of every treatment plan is a Review of Findings. At Brigham Dental Care, Dr. Ebert performs your dental treatments, not the assistants. While we accept all major forms of dental insurance, we also believe that decisions about dentistry should be made by you and your dentist, not your insurance company! We’ll always be up front with you about any out-of-pocket costs you can expect because we know that surprise expenses aren’t any fun.

We’ve designed our office to have a calm and relaxing atmosphere, and we love it when patients let us know that they actually enjoy visiting the dentist. Our reception area features natural wood toys and coloring books for your children as well as fun crossword puzzles and other types of games for the adults. No outdated magazines at our practice!

During your appointment, you can get cozy with blankets and pillows. Free WiFi allows you to listen to music or watch a favorite show. We also provide plenty of free toothpaste and dental floss. In fact, as a patient of record, you’re welcome to stop by and pick some up any time!

One of the things our patients often tell us they love about your practice is its stability. Many of our team members have been here for more than 30 years, which means you’ll get to see the same friendly faces at each visit. We love building relationships with our patients, and we welcome you like you’re one of the family.

We offer a full range of dental services, including everything from preventive dental cleanings to advanced treatments like dental implant restorations. We also know that dental emergencies can be an unfortunate fact of life, and we do our very best to offer patients of record same-day appointments for emergency treatment.
