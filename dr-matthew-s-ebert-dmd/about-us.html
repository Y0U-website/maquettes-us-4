About Us

Brigham Dental Care Staff

At Brigham Dental Care, we hold very dear two values in particular – relationships and education. We listen carefully to understand and embrace your needs and desires for your dental care, and this enables us to provide customized, goal-oriented treatment with the dedication and respect that you deserve. Like all our patients, you are a unique individual, and our goal is to help you retain the function and esthetics of your teeth for a lifetime.

We care deeply about our patients, and we want you to feel comfortable and confident with every member of our team. We’d like to take a moment to introduce ourselves so you can get to know us a bit better. We’re looking forward to meeting you and getting to know you, as well!

Dr. Matt Ebert DMD

Dr. Ebert received his Bachelor of Science with distinction from Brock University in St. Catharines, Ontario. In 1997, he was awarded his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Temple University in Philadelphia.

Dr. Ebert has been practicing in Morrisville, VT since 1998. He is a member of the Vermont State Dental Society and the American Dental Association . His commitment to excellence is emphasized by his active participation in continuing education courses and seminars. He shares with his staff a great enthusiasm for the continued study of dentistry. He loves the way art and science blend in dentistry and the fact that he spends his days helping people.

Dr. Ebert and his wife, Kelly, are residents of St. Johnsbury, VT and are extremely proud of their three children, Matthew, Owen, and Anna. Outside of the office, Dr. Ebert is an automobile enthusiast. He also enjoys getting outside with activities like snowmobiling, mountain biking, skiing, camping, and fishing.

Kelly Ebert RDH

Registered Dental Hygienist with Local Anesthesia Privileges

Kelly serves multiple roles at Brigham Dental Care. She is a Practice Coordinator, a registered dental hygienist, a dental assistant.

Kelly graduated from Vermont Technical College in May of 2014. She has her local anesthesia certification and is CPR and AED certified. While at VTC, Kelly was recognized multiple times, earning her the Vermont Dental Hygienist Association Spirit Award, the Colgate Oral Health Advisor Star Award, the Dental Hygiene Peer Recognition Award, and the Sigma Phi Alpha Honor Society Award.

A part of Brigham Dental Care since 2004, Kelly is passionate about educating patients about their oral health and how it relates to their overall well-being.

Kelly and her husband, Dr. Matt Ebert, have three children, Matthew, Owen, and Anna. Kelly’s joy is watching her children live their lives to the fullest.
