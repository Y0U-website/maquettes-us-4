Meet The Doctor

Ed Hurst DDS

USU & USC Graduate

Dr. Ed Hurst received his bachelor’s degree from Utah State University, graduating with a degree in Biology and Chemistry. Dr. Hurst completed his dental training at the University of Southern California (USC), where he graduated at the top of his class while also receiving the graduating class anesthesia award. Dr. Hurst was the first dental student at USC to receive I.V. anesthesia certification.

After dental school, Dr. Hurst returned to Logan to practice with his father and continue a legacy of high quality dental care. Dr. Hurst has continued his education by logging thousands of hours of continuing education. His advanced training has focused on placing and restoring dental implants and the art of cosmetic dentistry. His favorite aspect of dentistry is developing lasting relationships with his patients.

Dr. Hurst and his wife Leslie are the proud parents of four daughters. Dr. Hurst enjoys skiing, hunting, fishing, and visiting new places with his family.
