Dental Implants

When teeth have been lost to periodontal disease or any other reason, dental implants provide the solution to replace them. Dr. Hurst is the leading credentialed implant dentist in Cache Valley, Utah, and will help you determine if implants are right for you.

Dr. Hurst is the only dentist in Cache Valley that is credentialed by the Academy of Implant Dentistry, the foremost organization in implantology in the United States. This was accomplished by written and oral examination and many hundreds of hours of continuing education. He has also been performing implant procedures longer than any other dentist in the Cache Valley area. Dr. Hurst will provide you with the most up-to-date and successful implant placement and restoration, using computer-guided technologies.

How Dental Implants Work

Implant

A dental implant is placed in the bone and given time to create a strong bond.

Abutment

A small connector is attached to the implant for the new tooth.

Crown

A prosthetic tooth is attached to the abutment to complete the procedure.

Implant Bridges

There are many ways to use implants. You can even hold down dentures with two, four or six implants, depending on the situation.
