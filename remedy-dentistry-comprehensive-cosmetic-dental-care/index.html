The practice

Tooth length, width, texture, translucency, lip movement, gum levels, facial proportions, and bite all influence how a finished smile looks and functions.

Our approach to cosmetic dentistry prioritizes careful diagnosis, patient communication, enamel preservation, and individualized design. You remain involved throughout the planning process so the final result reflects your preferences rather than a predetermined template.

At Remedy Dentistry, Dr. Gregory Balaes combines clinical precision, digital technology, and an artistic approach to create personalized treatment plans for patients seeking a healthier, brighter, and more balanced smile.

Every cosmetic dentistry treatment plan is based on the patient’s starting point, facial features, goals, and dental health.

Individual results vary. A consultation and clinical examination are required to determine appropriate treatment.

We discuss what you like, what you would like to change, and what type of result feels natural to you.
