Meet Our Team

Dr. David Brooks

Dr. David Brooks was born and raised in Houston, Texas. He received his undergraduate degrees from Utah Valley University in Biology and Biotechnology.

He attended dental school at Roseman University where he studied dentistry. Soon after graduating dental school, he was trained under Dr. Randy Roberts on specializing in full-mouth restorations and dental implants. Dr. Brooks is the only licensed 3 on 6™ doctor in Utah Valley.

When not involved in his professions, Dr. Brooks enjoys camping, sports, enjoying game nights with friends, and most importantly, spending time with his wife and four sons.
