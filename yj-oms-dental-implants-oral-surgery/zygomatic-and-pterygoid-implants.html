Zygomatic and Pterygoid Implants

This streamlined process, combined with advanced technology and our expertise, ensures optimal results and a lifetime of confident smiles.

  • Precise Implant Placement: Using minimally invasive techniques and our detailed surgical plan, we ll strategically anchor the zygomatic implants into your cheekbone (zygomatic arch). In many cases, we can attach a fixed bridge on the same day, providing you with immediate tooth replacement.
  • Immediate Function & Aesthetics: You ll leave our office with a restored smile and the ability to eat and speak comfortably. These new teeth are designed to look, feel, and function just like natural teeth, enhancing both your appearance and confidence.
  • Maintaining Your Implants: To ensure the long-term success of your zygomatic implants, we ll provide guidance on maintaining excellent oral hygiene. This includes regular brushing, the use of tools like Waterpiks and interdental brushes, and routine checkups at our office for professional cleaning and monitoring.

What are the Benefits of Zygomatic and Pterygoid Implants?

  • High Success Rates: Zygomatic and pterygoid implants have demonstrated high success rates, comparable to traditional implants.
  • Reduced Treatment Time: By eliminating the need for extensive bone grafting, these techniques often shorten overall treatment time.
  • Immediate Function: In many cases, you can receive temporary teeth on the same day as surgery, allowing you to enjoy the benefits of a restored smile immediately.
  • Improved Quality of Life: Regain the ability to eat your favorite foods, speak clearly, and smile confidently, improving your overall quality of life.
  • Long-Term Solution: With proper care, zygomatic and pterygoid implants can last a lifetime, providing a permanent solution for missing teeth.
