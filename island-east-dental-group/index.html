The practice

We use the latest diagnostic procedures, including “digital radiography” – offering patients as much as 80 percent less exposure than conventional x-rays. If you are referred to a specialist, or a consultation is required, your radiographs can be sent instantaneously, via email.

Visiting our office is convenient. We take a late lunch, so you can be seen on your lunch hour. During the summer, we start at 8am on Saturdays, so you can have your dental concerns attended to and still make the beach. As a patient, you will be given Dr. Fluger’s cell phone number, so even after hours, on weekends or on holidays, you’ll never be out of touch!

You’ll always hear a familiar voice when you call and see a familiar face when you arrive for your appointment. Chances are that you know somebody who is a patient here too. Interested in making an appointment?

Call our office, (631) 399 4470. If it’s after hours, leave a number where you can be reached the next business day and a convenient time for us to return your call.
