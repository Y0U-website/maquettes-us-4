Testimonials

My husband is a “special needs patient”. When we moved out to this area a few years back, he tried out a couple of dentists. The experience was not very good to put it mildly. They had the attitude of “get ‘em in, get ‘em out and get paid”.

Now I am aware that my hubs can be a cranky patient. But he does have special needs as a quadriplegic in a wheelchair, along with the many other internal issues that are affected. He is bed-bound and house-bound, getting out only on the rare occasion to see a specialist or for another of his frequent hospital stays. So when we set up an appointment with Dr. Fluger, I made sure they had some understanding of what is involved.

These days, it is rare to find quality customer service. Even rarer is it to find a health professional that really has the best interest and concern for his patient at the top of the list. I have been fortunate to have met a few and Dr. Fluger is one the best in his field.

Thank you for all you have done for us, Doc. You will be blessed!
