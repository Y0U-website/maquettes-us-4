The Benefits Of All-On-4® Implants

Patients who choose this treatment often find that it improves both their confidence and their quality of life. With the stability of implants, you can enjoy meals more comfortably and speak with ease. It’s a long-lasting option that provides peace of mind.

  • Restores self-esteem : Helps patients smile freely without worrying about gaps or loose dentures.
  • Enhances function : Restores chewing ability so you can enjoy a wider variety of foods.
  • Supports bone health : Stimulates the jawbone to slow or prevent the bone loss that comes with missing teeth.
  • Convenient solution : Uses fewer implants, which makes treatment and recovery simpler than traditional methods.

The All-On-4® Procedure

The treatment starts with a personalized consultation where your goals and oral health are carefully reviewed. Four implants are placed in precise locations within your jaw to provide the right support. In many cases, a temporary set of teeth can be attached until healing is complete.

  • Personalized planning : Involves x-rays, impressions, and a detailed plan designed just for your needs.
  • Implant placement : Four implants inserted strategically to maximize strength and support.
  • Provisional teeth : A temporary set of teeth may be placed, letting you leave the office with a complete smile.
  • Final restoration : Custom-made teeth are attached after healing, providing long-term stability and function.

Caring For Your All-On-4® Implants

Caring for your new implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Brushing, flossing, and keeping up with regular dental visits help protect both your implants and your gums. Following these steps helps ensure long-lasting results.

  • Brush daily : Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste to protect your implants.
  • Floss carefully : Special floss or water flossers can help clean around the bridge and gumline.
  • Routine check-ups : Regular visits allow your dentist to monitor implant health and address small issues early.
  • Healthy choices : Avoid smoking and maintain a balanced diet to support healing and long-term success.

Are You A Candidate For All-On-4®?

This treatment is often ideal for adults who want a permanent alternative to dentures or bridges. It’s especially helpful for those who are missing most or all of their teeth, or for people who feel limited by slipping or uncomfortable dentures. Even if you have experienced some bone loss, advanced techniques may make this option possible when paired with a commitment to long-term oral care.

  • Individuals missing many teeth : Adults who have lost most or all teeth in one arch and want a full replacement.
  • People frustrated with dentures : Those dealing with sore spots, slippage, or adhesive use who want more stability.
  • Adults with bone concerns : Patients who may not qualify for traditional implants but could benefit from this approach.
  • Patients dedicated to care : Individuals willing to maintain oral hygiene and regular check-ups to keep results successful.
