About Us

Welcome To Daniels Parkway Dental

Welcome to our Fort Myers dental practice. The staff at Daniels Parkway Dental is a group of dedicated individuals who work together to provide a stress free, positive dental experience. We take pride in providing an unmatched level of customer service centered around patient-focused dental care to those we serve in Southwest Florida.

Dedicated To Our Patients

We take pride in exceeding patient expectations. As one of the last remaining family owned dental practices in Fort Myers, we look forward to establishing lasting relationships with those we serve!

Treatment Centered Around Your Goals

First and foremost, Dr. King places his focus on the health and safety of you, his team, and our community by adhering to strict disinfection and sterilization protocols.

Different patients have different goals when it comes to their dental health – from getting out of pain, to improving chewing, to smiling more confidently. Dr. King wants to understand your goals, make recommendations for the health of your mouth, and then provide a plan that reaches your goals and takes care of your teeth. We work together with you to preserve your smile and let you enjoy your life.

As Dr. King continually studies to learn newer and better ways to help solve your smile concerns, he also works hand-in-hand with some of the most trusted local specialists to ensure that you receive quality care, while guiding you towards the results and service you desire.

Fort Myers Florida Residents

Dr. Bryant King was born and raised on a small farm here in Fort Myers. He met his wife, Shelly, at Florida Southwestern College. He and his family reside in Buckingham on the Orange River, upstream from where his great-grandparents homesteaded back in 1920.

Education

After graduating from Riverdale High School, Dr. King earned his bachelor’s degree in Biology with a minor in Mathematics from the University of Central Florida. From there, he attended the University of Florida College of Dentistry, where he earned his doctorate in 2003. Since taking over his private practice in 2004, Dr. King remains dedicated to keeping Daniels Parkway Dental family owned and operated so he can give you the personalized care you deserve. Continuing education is a critical part of how Dr. King stays current with the most comfortable and convenient options for your care.

Since 2017, Dr. King has focused on learning more and more dental skills by taking numerous classes. In recent years, he has averaged over 100 hours per year of continuing education in order to offer you the most current procedures and technologies for your dental needs.

Family Life

A dedicated family man, Dr. King and his wife Dr. Shelly King, have been married since 1997 and have 5 kids – 4 of them adopted. After being foster parents for years, Drs. King brought one after another of these deserving kids into their forever home and are proud of the young adults they will become. Rounding out the family are two great Danes, a Jack Russell Terrier, and several turtles.
