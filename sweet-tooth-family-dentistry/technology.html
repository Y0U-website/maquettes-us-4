Our Technology

Digital X-Rays

Digital X-rays capture detailed images of your teeth and jaw using a fraction of the radiation of traditional film. Because the images appear instantly on-screen, Dr. Nish and Dr. Mahek can review them with you in real time, point out areas of concern, and explain a treatment plan you can actually follow along with.

3D X-Rays

A 3D X-ray builds a complete, three-dimensional model of your teeth, jaw, and surrounding bone in a single scan. This depth of detail allows our doctors to plan procedures like implants and extractions with far greater precision than a flat image ever could, catching details a traditional X-ray would miss entirely.

iTero Digital Health Scan

The iTero scanner replaces the old impression tray and its mouthful of goop with a quick, wand-based scan of your teeth. In minutes, it produces a precise digital model of your smile that we use to track changes over time, plan orthodontic treatment, and show you exactly what&#x27;s possible for your smile before any work begins.
