Eric Hoffman dds

dentist

Dr. Eric Hoffman brings a thoughtful blend of advanced training, clinical experience, and an appreciation for the artistry of modern dentistry to every patient he treats.

Originally from the suburbs of St. Louis, Missouri, Dr. Hoffman graduated magna cum laude from Creighton University before earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Missouri–Kansas City. He further refined his clinical expertise through a year-long dental residency at Loyola University Medical Center, where he gained extensive experience caring for medically complex patients and individuals with dental anxiety.

Following residency, Dr. Hoffman practiced general dentistry on Chicago’s North Side, where he developed a particular focus in restorative and cosmetic dentistry. His approach is centered on personalized care, attention to detail, and creating treatment plans that thoughtfully balance oral health, function, and aesthetics.

Dr. Hoffman is a member of the Chicago Dental Society, Illinois State Dental Society, and American Dental Association, and remains committed to advancing his knowledge and staying at the forefront of modern dental care.

Outside the office, Dr. Hoffman enjoys traveling, playing sand volleyball, and spending time with his dog, Berkley. He believes that exceptional dentistry begins with a genuine relationship between doctor and patient and takes pride in creating an experience that is both comfortable and personalized.

devaansh bawa

board certified orthodontist

Dr. Bawa is a board-certified orthodontist dedicated to creating healthy, confident smiles for patients of all ages. With extensive experience treating children, teens, and adults, he takes a personalized and collaborative approach to orthodontic care, making sure every treatment plan reflects each patient’s unique goals, needs, and lifestyle.

Dr. Bawa earned his Bachelor of Arts from the University of Virginia and his Doctorate of Dental Surgery from Virginia Commonwealth University. He went on to complete his specialty training in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics at Indiana University. He is a Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, a distinction that reflects his commitment to advanced orthodontic education and excellence in patient care.

Having practiced in multiple states, Dr. Bawa has had the opportunity to care for patients from a wide range of backgrounds and at every stage of life. Whether a patient is considering traditional braces, clear braces, or Invisalign, he believes the best orthodontic treatment begins with listening. He takes the time to understand what each patient hopes to achieve and works together with them to create a treatment plan that is both effective and personalized.

With a keen eye for aesthetics and attention to detail, Dr. Bawa enjoys the challenge of creating beautifully aligned, natural-looking smiles while maintaining a strong focus on oral health and long-term results. His goal is for every patient to feel comfortable, informed, and confident throughout their orthodontic journey.

Outside of the office, Dr. Bawa is a proud Washington, D.C. native and a passionate Baltimore Ravens and University of Virginia sports fan. When he isn’t creating smiles, he enjoys playing pickleball and tennis and recently earned his sailing license.

Dr. Bawa looks forward to helping you or your family achieve a healthier, more confident smile.
