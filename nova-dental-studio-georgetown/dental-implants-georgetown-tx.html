What Happens During the Implant Process?

Getting a dental implant takes a few steps, but we’ll guide you through each one:

  • Check-Up and Planning – Our dental office near you will examine your mouth, take X-rays, and decide if implants are right for you.
  • Implant Placement – The metal post is placed into your jawbone. You’ll be numb, so it won’t hurt.
  • Healing Time – Your bone needs time to grow around the implant. This usually takes a few months.
  • Crown Placement – Once healed, we attach a custom-made crown that matches your other teeth.
