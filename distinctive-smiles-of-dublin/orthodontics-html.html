ClearCorrect & Invisalign Invisible Aligner Trays

The ClearCorrect and Invisalign systems consist of a series of custom-fabricated clear aligner trays. These aligners are designed to apply steady, gentle pressure, gradually shifting teeth into proper alignment. Typically, patients will wear each aligner for two weeks before starting a new one. Treatment time is unique to each patient but generally ranges from about 6 to 9 months.

Benefits of Invisible Aligner Trays:

Straight teeth create beautiful smiles and correcting crowded, crooked, or open spaces between teeth can be completed faster than you might think. No matter what stage of life you’re in, straighter teeth can enhance your smile and improve your self-confidence! Ask Dr. Buck whether ClearCorrect or Invisalign is right for you.

  • Clear aligners do not affect your appearance.
  • Aligner trays fit your schedule and your lifestyle. They are easy to remove in fact, Dr. Buck will instruct you to take them out when you eat, brush, and floss. You can even go without the clear braces if you would rather not wear them on special occasions.
  • Clear aligners are smooth, so they won’t irritate sensitive oral tissue the way metal braces can. Because you can remove the aligners, you can keep your teeth much cleaner than with traditional metal braces.

Six Month Smiles

What if you could have a straighter, more attractive smile in just half a year or less? With Six Month Smiles, you can. This special system uses brackets and wires that match the color of your teeth. Over just a matter of months, they’ll use low but continuous force to move your teeth into their correct position, giving you great results in less time than is possible with traditional braces.
