The Foundation of a Healthy Smile

The foundation of a healthy smile is not a single procedure or a one-time fix — it is consistent, honest, preventive care delivered by a team that genuinely pays attention. At 207 Dental, general dentistry is where lasting oral health begins.

We do not recommend treatment based on what is easiest to schedule or what insurance covers most conveniently. We recommend what your teeth actually need, explain it clearly, and make sure you understand what we find at every visit. That means some visits end with nothing more than a clean bill of health — and we think that is a very good outcome.

General Dentistry Services at 207 Dental

Professional Teeth Cleanings

No matter how thoroughly you brush and floss, tartar accumulates in areas home care can&#x27;t reach. Professional cleanings remove that buildup before it leads to gum disease or decay — and establish the baseline your smile needs to stay healthy.

Comprehensive Exams & Digital X-Rays

A thorough exam at 207 Dental goes beyond a quick visual check. We take high-resolution digital X-rays, screen for oral cancer, check bite and jaw function, and document the full picture of your oral health so we can catch problems before they become expensive ones.

Dental Sealants

The deep grooves on back teeth are prime locations for cavities — a toothbrush simply can&#x27;t reach them reliably. Sealants fill those grooves with a protective coating, eliminating the shelter bacteria need to start decay.

Fluoride Treatment

Professional fluoride strengthens enamel and helps teeth resist acid attacks between visits. It takes a few minutes and adds a meaningful layer of protection for patients at higher cavity risk.

Nightguards & Sports Mouthguards

Grinding and clenching during sleep causes progressive enamel wear, jaw pain, and cracked teeth. A custom nightguard stops that damage before it requires restorative treatment. Sports mouthguards protect against impact injuries during athletic activity.

Oral Cancer Screening

Every comprehensive exam at 207 Dental includes an oral cancer screening. Early detection is the single most important factor in successful treatment outcomes — and a visual check takes less than two minutes.

What To Expect At A General Dentistry Appointment

A routine visit at 207 Dental typically includes a professional cleaning by your hygienist, a comprehensive exam by the doctor, updated digital X-rays when clinically indicated, and an oral cancer screening. If we find anything that needs attention, we explain it clearly — what it is, why it matters, and what your options are — before any decision is made.

New patient visits include a full health history review, a thorough baseline exam with comprehensive X-rays, and an honest conversation about where your oral health stands and what, if anything, needs to be addressed. We take the time to listen first — to understand what has and hasn’t worked for you in the past and what matters most to you going forward.

For patients who haven’t been in for a while: no judgment. We’ve heard it all, and our job is to help you move forward from wherever you are — not to make you feel bad about the past. Come in, let us see what’s going on, and we’ll take it from there.

How Often Should You Come In?

The standard recommendation of “every six months” is a starting point, not a universal prescription. The right frequency for preventive visits depends on your individual cavity risk, history of gum disease, how quickly you build tartar, whether you smoke, and other systemic health factors.

Some patients genuinely do well with annual visits. Others benefit from three or four appointments per year. At 207 Dental, we evaluate your actual risk profile and recommend a schedule that makes sense for you — not a blanket interval applied to every patient the same way.

What is consistent across all patients is this: the longer the gap between visits, the more time small problems have to become larger and more expensive ones. Regular care catches issues early, when the solution is still straightforward.
