The practice

In our practice we are dedicated to reducing your costs. Prevention is the key and we will work hard to provide you with the information and skills we all need to protect your teeth and your investments in treatment. The least expensive treatment you will ever need is what you don’t have to have!

We offer emergency services on a priority basis. If you call early, we can possibly see you that day. Your questions are always respected. Call us!

My staff and I are happy you have visited our site. For over thirty years my general dental practice has served the needs of the people of Mebane and Alamance County with a focus on quality, responsible care and friendly personal service. Together, we provide a full range of dental services from basic maintenance and restorative treatment to cosmetic procedures.
