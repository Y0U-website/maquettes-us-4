FAQs About Dental Implant Restoration in Saginaw

  • What is dental implant restoration?: Dental implant restoration refers to the process of repairing or replacing the visible part of a dental implant—usually a crown, bridge, or denture—without removing the titanium implant post that’s anchored in the jawbone.
  • How do I know if my implant needs to be restored?: Common signs include pain or discomfort around the implant, a loose crown or bridge, visible cracks or chips, or difficulty chewing. If you&#x27;re unsure, schedule a consultation for a professional evaluation.
  • Can a damaged implant crown be replaced without removing the implant?: Yes. If the implant post is stable and healthy, only the damaged restoration needs to be replaced. We use digital impressions to ensure a perfect fit for the new crown or bridge.
  • Is dental implant restoration painful?: Most patients find the procedure very comfortable. Since the implant post is not being removed, treatment usually involves minimal discomfort and is similar to having a new crown or bridge placed.
  • Do you offer repairs for implant-supported dentures?: Yes. We restore both single-tooth implants and full-arch solutions like implant-supported dentures. We can evaluate the condition of your prosthetic and make necessary adjustments or replacements.
