Dr. Anna Lam received her Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Eriselda Bano

Dr. Eriselda Bano is a dedicated pediatric dentist committed to providing every child with a positive, supportive, and confidence-building dental experience. She earned her first dental degree from the University of Tirana, Faculty of Dental Medicine, and continued her advanced education at the Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, graduating with high honors and induction into Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU), the National Dental Honor Society. Dr. Bano completed her specialty training in Pediatric Dentistry at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Bano is known for her gentle, child-centered approach, emphasizing calm communication, behavior guidance, and trust-building techniques that help children feel safe and proud of their smiles. She values strong partnerships with parents and strives to ensure every family feels informed, supported, and confident throughout their dental care.

Outside the office, Dr. Bano enjoys traveling, hiking, and most of all spending time with family and friends. She is a proud mother of a little girl who inspires her daily to treat every child with the same care and compassion she would want for her own family.

Dr. Parham Shahkar

Dr. Shahkar received his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Boston University in 2014.

With a strong academic background and a special interest in orthodontics, Dr. Shahkar is dedicated to providing high-quality, personalized dental care in a calm and welcoming environment. He is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA) and the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), staying up to date with the latest advances in dentistry.

Dr. Shahkar is known for his patient-centered approach. He takes time to sit one-on-one with each patient, maintaining eye contact and explaining every step of treatment in friendly, easy-to-understand language. He also uses dental models and online videos to help both parents and children feel informed, comfortable, and confident throughout their visits.

He is committed to making every patient feel safe, heard, and supported, creating a positive dental experience for the entire family.

In his free time, Dr. Shahkar enjoys cooking, playing tennis, working out at the gym, exploring new restaurants, and learning about artificial intelligence and robotics.

Dr. Andrew Moawad

Dr. Andrew Moawad is a board-certified pediatricdentist dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care in a warm, child-friendly environment. A native of Weymouth, Massachusetts, he is passionate about helping children build healthy smiles and positive dental experiences from an early age.

With a focus on preventive dentistry, early intervention, and patient education, Dr. Moawad works closely with families to promote lifelong oral health. Dr. Moawad is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and completed his advanced pediatric dental training and DMD degree at Boston University School of Dental Medicine. Outside of the office, he enjoys staying active and participating in various sports.
