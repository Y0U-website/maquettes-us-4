Ask the Doctor

Special treatment for all children

Our team is specially trained to care for children, including those with special needs, with compassion, patience, and respect for each child’s unique personality and pace. At Smileland, your child receives focused attention and care tailored to every stage of growth.

Can I Stay With My Child?

For children over age 4, we recommend that parents remain in the waiting area to help keep the space calm and focused. We’ll fully update you after your child’s visit.

Appointments & Scheduling

Dental visits count as excused absences for school.

Please give 24+ hour notice for rescheduling.

We work to schedule at your convenience.

Insurance & Office Policy

We accept most major dental insurance plans and are in-network with Delta Dental Premier, BCBS, and MetLife.

As a courtesy, our office submits claims on your behalf.

Please note that you are responsible for any balance not covered by your insurance.

If an overpayment occurs, refunds will be issued after the claim has been processed.

At Smileland, pediatric dental care is gentle, informative and built on trust, giving your child a confident and positive start to lifelong oral health.
