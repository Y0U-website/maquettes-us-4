Dental Implants in Hillsborough, New Jersey

If You’re Looking for Tooth Replacement Options, Consider Dental Implants from Dubitsky Family Dental

For most patients, dental implants are the best possible solution when it comes to missing teeth. Unlike removable dentures which can be unstable, and fixed bridges which don’t support the bone, dental implants actually fit directly into the jaw, fusing with the bone and offering both stability and support. At Dubitsky Family Dental in Hillsborough, NJ, we can provide beautiful dental implant restorations to complete the process and make you one hundred percent happy with your smile.

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is anchored to the jawbone and then topped with a natural looking replacement tooth. If multiple teeth are missing in a row, a bridge can be secured to two or more implants anchored along the jawbone. If all teeth are missing in an entire arch, dental implants can be used under dentures for a more comfortable and stable fit, as well as to preserve the underlying jaw bone.
