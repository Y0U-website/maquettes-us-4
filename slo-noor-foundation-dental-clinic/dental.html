  • Preliminary Eligibility Determination Our team will review your application and determine if you are eligible to receive our services. We will inform you if you do not meet our requirements. Please see Eligibility Requirements below
  • Eligibility Interview Every month our staff will select applications for eligibility verification. You will be contacted to set up an appointment with us. On your scheduled appointment, you will be asked to bring in verification of your eligibility (see What you must bring to your interview below).
  • Dental Appointment If you meet all eligibility requirements and have provided all necessary verifications, we will contact you for an appointment as openings become available in the coming months. Please see our policy on cancellations and no shows below.

Dental Services Offered

What to bring to your eligibility interview

  • General Dentistry
  • Dental Exams
  • Digital Dental X-ray
  • Dental Hygiene
  • Dentures, Partials, Bridges and Crowns
  • Oral Surgery
  • Periodontist
  • Endodontist
  • You must not have any type of dental insurance including: private dental insurance, government provided dental coverage, MediCal, CenCal or any other type of dental insurance.
  • You must have been denied MediCal coverage each year you receive SLO Noor dental services.
  • Household income must not be more than 200 % of the current year’s Federal Poverty Level for all household members.
  • You must be vaccinated against COVID-19. Please visit our COVID-19 policy page for more information.
  • You must be 18 years old or older.
  • Proof of Identity : You will need to provide photo identification at your Eligibility interview such as a California Driver’s license, state-issued I.D. or U.S. Passport.
  • Proof of Income : Each working member of the household must provide proof of income, including: Federal tax return for the last year, PLUS an additional form of proof of income such as one of the following: Copy of the last three pay stubs from every person who works in the household
  • Unemployment benefits statement and proof of current payment history
  • Proof of social security/disability income or retirement/pension benefits. If you have direct deposit for these checks, you must provide a bank statement
  • Child support/alimony support documentation
  • If you are paid in cash, your employer must provide a letter verifying your income with contact information
  • If self-employed, bring a Profit and Loss statement or a record of state tax sales revenue for past year
  • If living on savings, you must provide three of your most recent bank statements
  • If no income, we require a letter of support from the person who provides food and shelter for you.
  • No-show for 2 appointments. A cancellation less than 24 hours ahead of your appointment will beconsidered a no-show.
  • Cancel or are late for more than 2 appointments.
