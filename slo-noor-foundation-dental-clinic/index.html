The practice

We are a volunteer-based, community supported organization committed to advancing diversity and inclusion by helping ensure that all people across our communities feel valued and have equal access to resources, services, and opportunities provided by SLO Noor Foundation.

At the Noor Dental Clinic, volunteers work together to provide free dental care for uninsured, low-income individuals who qualify for the clinic’s services. To qualify, you must meet our eligibility requirements and provide the required documents.

At the SLO Noor Vision Clinic, services designed to meet the essential needs of our community. We offer free, comprehensive eye exams and diagnosis of eye diseases. A full selection of high-quality glasses and frames are also available. The Vision Care Clinic is located inside the Medical Clinic on Phillips Lane.

Cal Poly Center for Health Research and the SLO Noor Foundation are partnering to offer the Women’s Mobile Health Unit. Our mission is to provide medical and preventive health care to uninsured women who lack access to care in California’s Central Coast.

This exciting addition to SLO Noor Foundation introduces mobile primary care to "under-resourced" areas throughout all of San Luis Obispo County. Our goal: to overcome transportation barriers and provide patient exams and primary care in residential and commercial areas where our patients reside, work, and play.

The SLO NOOR Free Clinics are the ONLY truly free medical, vision and dental clinics in San Luis Obispo County.

Thank you for joining us in the quest to bring healthcare to the uninsured! Your generosity makes a profound difference in so many lives.
