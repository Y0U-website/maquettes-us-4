Meet Your Dentist Dr Ryan Implant & Restorative Dentist

Dr. Yang is a dedicated general dentist committed to providing compassionate, high-quality dental care with a patient-first approach. He earned his undergraduate degree from Indiana University Bloomington before pursuing his dental education at University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry in Detroit, Michigan, where he received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Following graduation, Dr. Yang continued to advance his clinical training by completing a rigorous two-year Implant and Prosthodontics program at NYU College of Dentistry. His advanced education allows him to provide comprehensive restorative and implant dentistry with a strong focus on both function and aesthetics.

Dr. Yang believes that great dentistry starts with trust, compassion, and attention to detail. His goal is to make every patient feel comfortable and cared for while providing treatment tailored to each individual’s needs. He approaches every patient with the mindset of treating them like family and always strives to deliver honest, gentle, and exceptional care.
