Essential information about this procedure

Dental implants work differently. The titanium post is placed directly into the jawbone, where it bonds with living bone tissue over three to six months. That process — called osseointegration — means your implant becomes a true part of your anatomy, capable of withstanding the full force of biting and chewing.

At Brilliant Smiles of North Brunswick, Dr. Sonal Deshpande begins every implant case with a thorough consultation that includes digital X-rays and a CT scan. That imaging gives her a precise, three-dimensional picture of your bone density and anatomy before any treatment begins — so your plan is built around your specific situation, not a one-size-fits-all template.

If a bone graft is needed to prepare the site, Dr. Deshpande discusses that as part of your initial consultation — no surprises on cost, no unexpected procedures. You&#x27;ll know exactly what to expect at every stage before you commit to anything.

Your Path to a Complete Smile From your first visit to a fully restored tooth, here&#x27;s exactly what the implant process looks like.

Consultation & Imaging

Dr. Deshpande performs a full oral exam with X-rays and a CT scan to assess your bone density and confirm you&#x27;re a good candidate for implants. You&#x27;ll leave with a clear treatment plan and transparent cost breakdown.

Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed into the jawbone during a surgical appointment — most patients are surprised by how manageable the experience is. If a bone graft is needed, this step may happen slightly earlier to allow healing time.

Osseointegration

Your body bonds with the titanium post over the next several months, creating a stable, permanent anchor. During this time, a temporary restoration keeps your smile intact and functional.

Final Restoration Placed

Once integration is confirmed, your custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached. The result looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth — and with routine care, it can stay that way for decades.
