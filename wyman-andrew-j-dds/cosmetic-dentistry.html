Cosmetic Treatments We Offer

Dental bonding uses tooth-colored composite resin to repair chips, cracks, and minor gaps. It’s applied directly to the tooth surface and shaped by hand for a seamless finish. Most bonding appointments take under an hour per tooth and require no anesthesia.

For a more durable long-term solution, porcelain veneers cover the front surface of a tooth to change its color, shape, or size. They’re thin, strong, and look like natural enamel. Veneers work well for teeth that are discolored, misshapen, or slightly crowded.

Tooth-colored composite fillings replace old silver amalgam restorations or treat new cavities with material that blends into your smile. If you have old metal fillings that you’ve always wanted updated, this is a straightforward option.

Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

How long does teeth whitening last?

With custom trays, most patients see results that last one to three years depending on diet and habits. Coffee, red wine, and tobacco stain teeth faster. Occasional touch-up sessions with the same trays keep things looking fresh without starting over from scratch.

Is dental bonding permanent?

Bonding typically lasts three to ten years before it may need touching up or replacing, depending on wear. It’s not as durable as a veneer, but it’s a fraction of the cost and doesn’t require removing any tooth structure. For minor corrections, it’s often the right first step.

Will my insurance cover cosmetic dentistry?

Most insurance plans don’t cover purely cosmetic procedures like whitening or veneers. However, tooth-colored fillings and bonding used to repair damaged teeth are often partially covered. We verify your benefits before treatment so you know what to expect.

Can I whiten my teeth if I have crowns or veneers?

Whitening gel doesn’t affect crowns, veneers, or bonding. If you have existing restorations and whiten your natural teeth, there may be a noticeable color difference. We’ll assess your specific situation before recommending whitening to make sure the results will look consistent.

How do I know which cosmetic option is right for me?

That’s exactly what the consultation is for. There’s no single answer because it depends on your teeth, your goals, and your budget. Some people want a fast, affordable improvement. Others want something that lasts 15 years. We’ll recommend what makes sense for your situation, not the most expensive option on the menu.
