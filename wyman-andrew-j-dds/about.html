Common Questions Answers to Your Most Asked Dental Questions We want every patient to feel informed and confident before their visit. Here are the answers to some of the questions we hear most often at our Oak Harbor dental office.

  • Warm, Experienced Team Dr. Wyman and his staff have built a reputation for making every patient feel at ease. From greeting to checkout, expect genuine care and a friendly face at every visit.
  • Flexible Scheduling & Emergency Care Life doesn’t follow a schedule. That’s why we offer flexible appointment times Monday through Thursday, and provide 24-hour emergency dental care for patients of record.
  • New Patient Benefits Verification We make it easy to get started. As a new patient, we’ll help verify your insurance benefits so you understand your coverage before your very first appointment.
  • Are you accepting new patients? Yes! We are proudly accepting new patients of all ages. Our team will even help verify your insurance benefits before your first visit so you know exactly what to expect. Book online or give us a call to get started.
  • What insurance plans do you accept? We work with most major dental insurance plans. Not sure if yours is covered? Contact our office and we’ll be happy to verify your benefits before your appointment so there are no surprises.
  • How much will it cost? We’re committed to transparent, affordable pricing. For patients without insurance, we offer a 5 % discount on treatment. We’re also happy to provide estimates before any procedure so you can make informed decisions about your care.
  • Do you offer emergency dental services? Yes. We provide 24-hour emergency dental care for patients of record. If you’re experiencing a dental emergency, call our office right away and we’ll do everything we can to see you as quickly as possible.
  • Do you accept CareCredit or offer payment plans? Yes, we accept CareCredit to help make your dental care more manageable. CareCredit allows you to finance treatment and pay over time with convenient monthly payments. Ask our front desk about applying before your visit.
  • What is your 3D scanner used for? Our state-of-the-art 3D scanning technology allows us to create highly precise digital impressions of your teeth — no messy molds required. We use it to design and deliver custom crowns, bridges, and dentures in-house, resulting in a faster turnaround and a superior fit.
