0 Orthodontic Services Lingual Braces (Invisible Braces) Lingual Braces are adhered to the back of the teeth making them truly invisible. Each bracket is uniquely positioned with infrared light to fit each tooth, maximizing efficiency. In addition, this is the only method that allows for tooth whitening during treatment.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic Braces serve as a cosmetic alternative and blend naturally with teeth color. The translucent clear material will provide a low-profile appearance.

Traditional Braces

Traditional Braces are the most common type of braces and have been recognized for versatility, value, and easy oral cleaning. The stainless steel brackets are smaller, which means less discomfort inside the mouth and a more discreet look.

Invisalign

Invisalign is the clear alternative to metal braces. By using a series of customized aligners, teeth can be straightened without wires and brackets.
