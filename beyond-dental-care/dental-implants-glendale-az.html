Dental Implants

A dental implant can replace a tooth and restore your mouth to optimal function. Implants stabilize the surrounding bone, tissue, and teeth by replacing the missing tooth. This helps prevent further serious complications that can arise from missing teeth like bone loss, teeth drifting, and impaired chewing ability.

First, the site may be prepared for placing a dental implant with bone. Second, the implant body is placed into the bone. After a period of healing, a dental crown is placed onto the implant body.

With your implant complete , your mouth is restored to optimal shape and function while stabilizing, protecting, and preserving the surrounding bone and tissue.

Why Seniors Choose Implants Over Dentures

For many older adults, the conversation starts with dentures — but it often ends with implants. The reason is simple: implants do something dentures cannot. By anchoring directly into the jawbone, they preserve the bone that would otherwise shrink after tooth loss, protecting your facial structure for the long term. There’s no slipping, no adhesive, and no nightly removal. You eat what you want, speak clearly, and go about your day without thinking about your teeth.

For seniors managing conditions like well-controlled diabetes or stable heart disease, implants remain a viable option. Dr. Lazore works closely with your primary care physician when needed to ensure your treatment plan accounts for your complete health picture — not just your dental history.
