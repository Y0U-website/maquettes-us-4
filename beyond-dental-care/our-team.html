Dariene Lazore, DMD

Office Manager: Savannah

Team Member: Mr. Woodford

Mr. Woodford is the star of the show at Beyond Dental Care ! His calm demeanor and loving personality will put a smile on your face. He enjoys getting attention from everyone, and can often be found snoozing in Dr Lazore’s office.

Mr. Woodford comes to us from Stealing Hearts Rescue in Phoenix, AZ. He was adopted by Dr Lazore a few years ago. Sadly, he had been shot with a pellet gun and left to fend for himself in the Arizona heat. He has several missing teeth so it is thought that he chewed his way out of a fence or cage. But now his days are filled with treats, air conditioning, comfy beds, and tons of love!
