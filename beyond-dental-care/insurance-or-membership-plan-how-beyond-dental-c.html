Your Questions, Answered

Choosing how to pay for your dental care is a big decision, and it s normal to have questions. Here are a few common ones we hear from our patients.

Can I Use My HSA or FSA for the Membership Plan?

Yes, absolutely! Many of our patients use their Health Savings Account (HSA) or Flexible Spending Account (FSA) to pay for their membership fees. It’s a great way to use pre-tax dollars to pay for your dental care.

How Does the 15 % Discount Compare to Insurance for Major Work?

This is a great question. An insurance plan might say it covers 50 % of a major treatment, but that’s often after you’ve paid a deductible. Plus, coverage stops once you hit your yearly limit, which is often around $1,500.

Our membership plan’s 15 % discount is simple. It applies right away to the full cost. There are no deductibles and no annual maximums to worry about. For many patients, this provides more predictable savings.

What Is the Perio Maintenance Plan For?

The Perio Maintenance Plan is designed for patients who have had periodontal (gum) disease. While a standard cleaning is great for polishing teeth, perio maintenance is a more targeted cleaning that goes below the gumline. This helps stop gum disease from returning and keeps your gums healthy for the long term.
