About Main Street Dental of Cartersville, GA

Main Street Dental offers quality family dental care in the heart of Cartersville, Georgia. Drs. Denny, Kimberly and Tony Lim provide dental services for adults and children, using the latest technology along with the personal service and care you deserve. Main Street Dental offers comprehensive dental services such as preventative care, cosmetic dentistry and restorative dentistry.

Every patient is important to us at Main Street Dental. We strive to make your visit as pleasant and comfortable as possible by treating you as family. Every room is equipped with cable TV and headsets. We offer nitrous oxide sedation for dental procedures such as tooth extractions. Main Street Dental is committed to providing family dentistry services with your care and comfort in mind. Flexible financing plans to fit your budget are available.

Contact Main Street Dental today and let us help you achieve your best smile!

Meet the Dentists

Denny Y. Lim, DDS Dr. Denny Lim graduated from the University of Tennessee Dental College in 2002. After graduation, he practiced in Cartersville, Georgia, until 2009. Today, Dr. Lim currently has a practice in Acworth, Georgia, serving communities in Cobb County and Paulding County, GA. Dr. Lim makes continuing education a priority to provide state-of-the art dentistry. Every patient is important to Dr. Lim, and he strives to make your dental experience convenient and comfortable.

Kimberly Lim, DDS Dr. Kimberly Lim also graduated from the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry. After graduation, she joined a private practice in Kennesaw, Georgia, as an associate. In 2005, Dr. Lim opened her own practice. She regularly attends continuing education to remain current on the ever-changing world of dentistry. It is her pleasure to serve her Acworth, Kennesaw, Woodstock, Marietta, and Dallas GA neighbors.

Tony U. Lim, DDS Dr. Lim has been in private practice in Northwest Georgia since 1999. He attended the University of Tennessee College of Dentistry in Memphis where he received a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Dr. Lim strives to remain current in the rapidly changing world of dentistry by attending numerous hours of continuing education. Dr. Lim looks forward to providing you and your family the highest quality dental care through experience, education, and commitment.
