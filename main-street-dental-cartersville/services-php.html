Services

You can trust the dentists and staff at Main Street Dental to provide the best in preventative dental care, cosmetic dentistry and restorative dentistry. Whether you come in for your regular teeth cleaning appointment or a major dental procedure, we provide personalized service that meets your individual dental needs. We also handle unforeseen dental emergencies, so you can get back to your normal routine as quickly as possible. Main Street Dental is your hometown dentist for:

  • Family Dentistry for Adults and Children
  • Teeth Cleaning and Periodontal (Gum) Therapy
  • Teeth Whitening /Bleaching
  • Composite Fillings (Tooth Colored)
  • Dental Crowns and Dental Bridges
  • Dental Veneers
  • Full and Partial Dentures
  • Implant Restorations
  • Root Canal Therapy
  • Tooth Extractions and Oral Surgery
  • Nitrous (Relaxation) Gas
