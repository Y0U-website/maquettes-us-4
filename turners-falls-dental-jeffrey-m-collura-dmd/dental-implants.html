What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone to act as an artificial tooth root. Once the implant integrates with the surrounding bone — a process called osseointegration — it provides a stable, long-lasting foundation for a custom-crafted crown, bridge, or denture. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants do not rely on adjacent teeth for support and do not require removal for cleaning.

Because implants are anchored in the jawbone, they stimulate bone growth and prevent the bone loss that commonly occurs after tooth extraction. This makes them the only tooth replacement option that preserves long-term jaw structure.

Dental Implant Options at Turners Falls Dental

We offer different dental implant solutions depending on your specific needs:

Single Tooth Implant

For patients missing one tooth, a single implant topped with a custom ceramic crown provides a seamless, permanent replacement that looks and functions exactly like a natural tooth. No adjacent teeth are altered in the process.

Implant-Supported Bridge

For patients missing several consecutive teeth, two implants can anchor a fixed bridge — eliminating the need for a removable partial denture and providing a stable, natural-feeling restoration.

Implant-Retained Dentures

For patients who have lost all or most of their teeth, implant-retained dentures provide dramatically improved stability compared to traditional dentures — eliminating slipping, clicking, and the need for messy adhesives.

The Dental Implant Process

The dental implant process at Turners Falls Dental typically unfolds over several months to allow for proper healing:

  • Consultation & Planning: Dr. Collura evaluates your oral health, reviews your medical history, and uses 3D Cone Beam CT imaging to assess bone volume and plan precise implant placement.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. In some cases, Dr. Collura places the implant; in others, he coordinates with a trusted oral surgeon or periodontist.
  • Osseointegration: Over three to six months, the implant fuses with the surrounding bone, creating a solid foundation.
  • Abutment & Crown: Once healed, an abutment (connector piece) is attached to the implant, and your custom crown — often fabricated with our CEREC technology — is placed to complete the restoration.

Dental Implants vs. Bridges vs. Dentures

All three options can effectively replace missing teeth, but they differ significantly in longevity, maintenance, and impact on surrounding teeth and bone. Dental bridges require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth and don’t prevent bone loss. Traditional dentures can slip, affect speech, and need periodic relining as the jawbone changes shape. Dental implants avoid these limitations — they are self-supporting, preserve bone, and with proper care can last a lifetime.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

Are dental implants permanent?

Dental implants are designed to be a permanent tooth replacement solution. The titanium implant post can last a lifetime when properly cared for. The crown placed on top typically lasts 10–15 years or longer before it may need to be replaced due to normal wear. Regular dental exams at Turners Falls Dental will help ensure the longevity of your implant.

Do dental implants hurt?

The implant placement procedure is performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during surgery. Afterward, mild soreness and swelling are normal for a few days and are typically managed with over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients report that the procedure was more comfortable than they expected.

How long does the dental implant process take?

From start to finish, the full dental implant process typically takes three to nine months, depending on whether bone grafting is needed, the time required for osseointegration, and your overall healing rate. Dr. Collura will give you a realistic timeline at your consultation at Turners Falls Dental.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most adults in good general health with sufficient bone density are candidates for implants. Dr. Collura will evaluate your bone volume using our Cone Beam CT imaging , assess your gum health, and review your medical history to determine whether implants are appropriate for your situation. Call (413) 774-6553 to schedule a consultation.

How do I care for dental implants?

Caring for dental implants is essentially the same as caring for natural teeth — brush twice daily, floss around the implant, and visit Turners Falls Dental regularly for professional cleanings and exams . While implants cannot get cavities, the surrounding gum tissue and bone still need to be kept healthy.

How much do dental implants cost?

The cost of dental implants varies depending on how many teeth are being replaced and whether bone grafting or other preparatory procedures are needed. Implants are often covered partially by dental insurance when tooth loss is due to injury or disease. Turners Falls Dental offers financial options to help make implant treatment accessible. Call (413) 774-6553 for details.

What happens if I don’t replace a missing tooth?

When a tooth is lost and not replaced, the jawbone in that area begins to shrink because it no longer receives the stimulation it needs. Adjacent teeth can shift into the gap, altering your bite and increasing the risk of decay and gum disease. Replacing missing teeth promptly — ideally with a dental implant or bridge — protects your long-term oral health.
