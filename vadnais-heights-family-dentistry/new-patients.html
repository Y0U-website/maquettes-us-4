What to Bring to Your First Visit

Being prepared helps us serve you better. On your first visit, please bring the following:

  • A valid photo ID
  • Your dental insurance card, if applicable
  • A list of any current medications
  • Any previous dental records or X-rays, if available
  • A completed new patient form, if provided in advance

Frequently Asked Questions for New Patients

  • What happens during my first visit? We review your medical and dental history, take X-rays if needed, and perform a full exam. Then we talk through any concerns and build a care plan together.
  • Do you accept dental insurance? Please call our office at 651-482-0180 to discuss your insurance plan. We are happy to help you understand your coverage.
  • How early should I arrive for my first appointment? We recommend arriving 10 to 15 minutes early. This gives you time to fill out any paperwork and get settled before your appointment.
  • Do you see children as new patients? Yes. We welcome patients of all ages. Our team is experienced in making young patients feel safe and comfortable.
  • How do I schedule my first appointment? Just give us a call at 651-482-0180 . Our friendly team will find a time that works for you.
