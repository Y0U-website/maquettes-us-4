Our Dental Services in Vadnais Heights

To support your holistic health journey, we provide a wide range of services:

Sleep Apnea Appliances

Struggling to get a good night’s sleep? You are not alone. Sleep apnea affects millions of people. Our custom oral appliances gently reposition your jaw while you sleep. This keeps your airway open and helps you breathe easier. No mask. No machine. Just a simple, comfortable device that works.

TMJ Appliances (Night Guards)

Jaw pain, headaches, and grinding teeth are common signs of TMJ disorder. Our tailored night guards protect your teeth from damage. They also reduce muscle tension and ease discomfort. If you wake up with a sore jaw, a night guard can make a real difference.

Physical Therapy Appliances

Some dental issues are connected to how your body moves and functions. Our specialized appliances are designed to work alongside physical therapy treatment. This combined approach helps patients find lasting relief from chronic pain and tension.

Extractions

Sometimes a tooth needs to come out. Whether it is wisdom teeth or a damaged tooth, our team handles extractions with care and precision. We make sure you are comfortable throughout the process. Healing instructions and follow-up support are always included.

Dentures

Missing teeth affect more than your smile. They can change how you eat, speak, and feel about yourself. Our high-quality, custom-fitted dentures restore both function and appearance. We work closely with each patient to ensure a proper, comfortable fit.

Root Canals

Root canals have a bad reputation, but they should not. The procedure relieves pain, not causes it. When a tooth is infected, root canal therapy removes the source of infection and saves the tooth. Our team uses modern techniques to keep the process smooth and as comfortable as possible.

Teeth Whitening

Years of coffee, tea, and everyday wear can dull your smile. Our professional whitening treatments are safe, effective, and fast. Results are far stronger than anything you can buy at a store. A brighter smile can boost your confidence in ways you might not expect.

Same-Day Crowns and Bridges

Nobody wants to make two trips to the dentist for one procedure. With our advanced in-office technology, we design and place crowns and bridges in a single visit. You walk in with a problem and walk out with a finished restoration. It is that simple.

Dental Implants

Implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. They look, feel, and function just like natural teeth. Our team handles the full implant process from start to finish. Whether you need one tooth or several, we build a plan that fits your needs and your life.

Invisalign

Straight teeth do not have to mean metal braces. Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gently shift your teeth into place. They are nearly invisible and easy to wear. Most patients see results in months, not years.

Digital and 3D X-Rays (CT Scans)

Accurate diagnosis starts with clear imaging. Our digital and 3D X-ray technology gives our team a detailed view of your teeth, jaw, and airway. It is faster, safer, and far more precise than traditional X-rays. Better imaging means better care.

Aesthetic Fillings

Old silver fillings stand out every time you laugh or speak. Our tooth-colored fillings blend naturally with your smile. They are durable, safe, and completely discreet. Most patients are amazed at how natural they look after treatment.

Botox and Fillers

Botox is not just for cosmetic use. We use it to relieve muscular tension linked to jaw clenching, migraines, and TMJ pain. We also offer facial fillers to enhance your appearance and complement your smile. It is a modern, effective option that more patients are choosing every year.

Teeth Cleanings

Regular cleanings are the foundation of good oral health. Our hygienists go beyond a basic polish. They check for early signs of gum disease, cavities, and other issues. Catching problems early saves time, money, and discomfort down the road.
