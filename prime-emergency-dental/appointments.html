Best Way To Get Seen Based On Your Situation

Not every emergency feels the same. Use this quick guide to decide whether to call, book online, walk in, or go directly to the ER.

😖 Severe Tooth Pain

🦠 Swelling or Abscess

If you have gum swelling, facial swelling, pus, fever, or a bad taste, you should be evaluated as soon as possible.

💥 Broken or Cracked Tooth

If a tooth broke, cracked, or feels sharp, book online or come in before the tooth worsens or becomes more painful.

👑 Lost Crown or Filling

If a crown or filling came out, bring it with you if you still have it. We will evaluate whether it can be repaired, replaced, or stabilized.

🦷 Wisdom Tooth Pain

If the gum behind your molar is swollen, painful, or difficult to bite on, you may need same-day evaluation and treatment.

🚨 Trouble Breathing or Swallowing

If swelling is spreading toward the neck or eye, or you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, this may be a medical emergency.

Your Visit, Step by Step

From arrival to diagnosis to treatment, this is what actually happens during an emergency visit.

Call, Book Online, or Walk In

Calling ahead is fastest for urgent pain. Online booking works well if you already know the time you want. Walk-ins are welcome during business hours.

Quick Check-In

We confirm your chief complaint, medical history, allergies, medications, and basic information so the doctor can focus on the emergency.

Emergency Exam & Digital X-Rays

The doctor examines the problem area, reviews X-rays, identifies the source of pain, and explains what is happening in plain language.

Treatment Options & Upfront Cost Review

Before treatment begins, we review your options, the recommended treatment, the fee, and what to expect after treatment.

Same-Day Treatment When Appropriate

If treatment is clinically appropriate and you choose to proceed, we treat the source of the problem the same day whenever possible.

What To Bring And What To Know

These small details help us move faster and reduce delays once you arrive.

🆔 Bring Photo ID

Bring a driver’s license, state ID, passport, or another valid photo ID so we can confirm your identity.

💊 Medication List

Bring a list of current medications, allergies, medical conditions, and any blood thinners or heart-related medications.

💳 Payment Method

We review costs before treatment begins. Bring a payment card or ask us about available financing options.

📄 Insurance Card If Available

No insurance is required. If you have insurance, bring your card so we can help with out-of-network reimbursement information when applicable.

🦷 Bring Broken Pieces

If a crown, filling, denture tooth, or broken tooth fragment came out, bring it with you in a small bag or container.

⏱️ Do Not Wait Too Long

Dental infections and severe tooth pain usually do not resolve on their own. Earlier treatment often means simpler treatment.
