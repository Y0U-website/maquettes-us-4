The practice

Start with a $99 exam & X-ray. We show you exactly what treatment costs before we begin — no surprises, no pressure.

Dental pain usually does not become easier by waiting. If the tooth is throbbing, swollen, broken, infected, or keeping you awake, get a same-day dental evaluation and a real treatment plan.

Every patient who walks through our door has an urgent dental problem. We do not fit painful visits between routine cleanings. Our team, schedule, and workflow are built around getting you seen quickly, giving you clear answers, reviewing the cost, and helping you move toward treatment today when appropriate.

If you are nervous, embarrassed, or worried treatment will hurt, tell us before treatment begins. Nitrous oxide/laughing gas is available for anxious or nervous patients when clinically appropriate.

If you are in pain right now, chances are we treat it. These are the urgent dental problems we see every day.

Throbbing pain, nerve pain, pressure, or pain that keeps you awake needs a real diagnosis — not another day of guessing.

Swelling, pus, gum bumps, fever, or a bad taste can signal an infection that should be evaluated promptly.

A fractured tooth causing pain, sharp edges, swelling, or sensitivity can worsen quickly. We assess and treat when possible.

Les soins

Severely broken, infected, loose, or non-restorable tooth? Same-day extraction is available when clinically appropriate after diagnosis and consent.

Go to the ER or call 911 immediately if you have trouble breathing, trouble swallowing, swelling spreading into your neck or throat, serious facial trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, confusion, faintness, rapidly worsening swelling, or you feel severely ill.
