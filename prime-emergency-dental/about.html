Care that travels.

Our commitment to serving others doesn’t stop at the edge of Edmonds. We regularly participate in dental outreach efforts - both locally and internationally - bringing care to communities with limited access to treatment.

Our mission work has taken us to communities in need across the Philippines, Vietnam, Thailand, Nicaragua, Mexico City, Cambodia, and beyond - providing hands-on emergency dental care to people who otherwise wouldn’t have access to it.
