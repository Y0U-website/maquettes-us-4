What Are Veneers & Prepless Veneers?

Veneers are thin coverings placed over the front surface of your teeth. They are used to improve your smile’s shape, colour, size, or alignment. Veneers are made from strong, tooth-coloured materials that mimic the look and feel of natural enamel.

Traditional veneers involve preparing the teeth by removing a small amount of enamel. This ensures the veneers fit properly and look natural once bonded in place.

Prepless veneers are a more conservative option. These veneers are ultra-thin and often require little to no enamel removal. They’re bonded directly to the tooth surface. They are ideal for patients who want to enhance their smile without altering the underlying tooth structure.

Types of Veneers

Two main types of veneers are offered at Bella Dental: traditional veneers and prepless veneers. Each option has its advantages, and your dentist will help you choose the most suitable one based on your teeth, your desired outcome, and how much preparation you’re comfortable with.

Traditional Veneers

These are typically made from porcelain and are known for their strength, durability, and natural appearance. A small amount of enamel is removed from the tooth before placing the veneer to ensure the final result is smooth and properly aligned.

Prepless Veneers

Prepless veneers are very thin and often do not require enamel removal. They are bonded directly to the tooth surface and are best suited for patients with smaller or less prominent teeth. Because little to no tooth structure is altered, prepless veneers are considered a more conservative option.

We will guide you through the benefits of each and help you decide which option will best enhance your smile.

What Happens if I Do Not Get Veneers When Needed?

Veneers are often used for cosmetic purposes but can also protect teeth that are slightly worn or chipped. If damaged teeth are left untreated, the wear can worsen over time and lead to further issues such as deeper cracks or uneven bite pressure.

Discolouration, alignment problems, and chips can also affect confidence and make patients self-conscious in social settings. Addressing these concerns early helps prevent future damage and supports long-term oral health and comfort.

The Veneers Process

If you are having traditional veneers, we begin by preparing the teeth. A thin layer of enamel is gently removed to make space for the veneers. Impressions are taken and sent to the dental lab, where your custom veneers are made. Temporary veneers may be placed in the meantime.

Once your veneers are ready, we’ll check their shape and shade and make sure you’re happy with the appearance. The veneers are then carefully bonded to your teeth and polished for a natural finish.

For prepless veneers, the process is similar but usually quicker. There is little to no enamel removal, and the ultra-thin veneers are bonded directly to your existing tooth surface. You may not need temporary veneers in these cases.

Both types of veneers are colour-matched to your natural teeth and shaped to blend in seamlessly.

Veneers Aftercare

Caring for your veneers is simple. Good hygiene and regular dental visits can help them last for many years.

It’s also a good idea to avoid smoking and limit staining foods and drinks to keep your smile looking bright.

  • Brush and floss gently but thoroughly each day
  • Avoid biting into very hard foods with veneered teeth
  • Use a non-abrasive toothpaste to maintain the surface polish
  • Wear a nightguard if you grind your teeth while sleeping
  • Schedule routine check-ups to monitor the condition of your veneers

Your Veneers & Prepless Veneers Questions

Are veneers permanent?

Veneers are long-lasting but may need to be replaced after many years. Traditional veneers usually require removing enamel, so they are not reversible.

Do veneers look natural?

Yes. Veneers are made to match the colour, shape, and texture of natural teeth. Most people cannot tell the difference once they are placed.

What is the difference between traditional and prepless veneers?

Traditional veneers require light enamel removal for proper fit. Prepless veneers are thinner and usually placed without altering the natural tooth.

Will my teeth be sensitive after getting veneers?

You may initially feel some sensitivity, especially with traditional veneers, but this usually fades within a few days.

How long do veneers last?

With proper care, veneers can last 10 to 15 years or more. Regular check-ups help monitor their condition.

Can veneers fix gaps or crooked teeth?

Yes. Veneers can close small gaps and improve the appearance of slightly misaligned teeth.

Do veneers stain?

Veneers are stain-resistant, especially those made of porcelain. Limiting foods and drinks that stain teeth is still best to keep them looking bright.

Can I get veneers on just one tooth?

Yes. Depending on your needs, veneers can be used to improve a single tooth or a full set.

Is the veneers process painful?

No. The procedure is minimally invasive and performed under local anaesthesia. Most patients are comfortable throughout.

Will my speech change with veneers?

There may be a short adjustment period, but speech returns to normal quickly once your mouth adapts.
