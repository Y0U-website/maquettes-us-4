Dental Implants

Implants are designed to replace missing teeth and restore your bite. They also help prevent other issues that can arise when teeth are lost, such as shifting nearby teeth or bone loss in the jaw.

  • Have a damaged tooth that cannot be saved
  • Are unhappy with removable dentures
  • Want a permanent solution for tooth loss
  • Have healthy gums and enough bone to support an implant

Types of Dental Implants

There are several types of implants, and your Bella Dental dentist will help you choose the one that best fits your needs:

Single-tooth implants: These are used to replace one missing tooth with an individual implant and crown.

Implant-supported bridges: Ideal for multiple missing teeth in a row. Two or more implants support a bridge of connected teeth.

Implant-supported dentures: A secure option for those missing most or all teeth. Several implants anchor a full denture in place.

Mini implants: Smaller size, often used in areas with less bone or to stabilise a lower denture.

We will carefully assess your mouth and jaw before recommending the most suitable option.

What Happens if I Do Not Have Dental Implants When Needed?

Missing teeth can lead to more than just a gap in your smile. Over time, the jawbone where the tooth once was can begin to shrink. This process, known as bone loss, can change the shape of your face and affect the strength of nearby teeth.

Other teeth may begin to shift, leading to misalignment and bite problems. Eating may become more difficult, and you may find yourself avoiding certain foods or favouring one side of your mouth.

Delaying treatment can also make implant placement more complex later, especially if bone loss becomes severe. Taking action early helps preserve the health of your mouth and the structure of your jaw.

Your Dental Consultation

Your first visit includes a full dental exam, digital X-rays, and possibly a 3D scan to assess the condition of your jawbone. Your Bella Dental dentist will review your health history and talk with you about your treatment goals.

We’ll explain whether implants are the right choice for you and discuss what the process will involve. You’ll be able to ask questions, review the timeline, and understand what to expect before moving forward.

The Dental Implants Process

Implant treatment usually happens in stages.

Stage 1

First, the implant post is placed into the jawbone during a short surgical procedure. This is done under local anaesthetic, and we’ll ensure you’re as comfortable as possible throughout.

Stage 2

The implant is then left to heal for several months, during which time it fuses with the bone. This creates a stable foundation for the final restoration.

Stage 3

Once healing is complete, we place an abutment. This is a small connector that links the implant to your replacement tooth. Finally, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached to restore function and appearance.

In some cases, additional procedures such as bone grafting may be needed before implant placement. Your Bella Dental dentist will discuss this with you if required.

Dental Implants Aftercare

Taking care of your implant is much like caring for your natural teeth. After the procedure, you may experience some mild swelling or discomfort. This usually improves within a few days.

To help your implant heal and stay healthy, we recommend:

Good oral hygiene and professional care will help ensure your implant lasts as long as possible.

  • Brushing and flossing daily
  • Using a soft-bristled toothbrush
  • Avoiding smoking and excessive alcohol, especially during healing
  • Attending regular checkups to monitor your implant

Your Dental Implants Questions

Are dental implants safe?

Yes. Implants are a widely used and well-tested treatment for tooth loss. They are made from biocompatible materials and are very safe when placed by a trained professional.

Does getting an implant hurt?

The area is numbed with local anaesthetic so that you won’t feel pain during the procedure. Some soreness afterward is normal and usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief.

How long does the implant process take?

It typically takes several months from start to finish, allowing time for healing between stages. Your dentist will give you a detailed timeline based on your treatment plan.

Can anyone get dental implants?

Most adults in good health are candidates. We’ll check your bone density and oral health to confirm that implants suit you.

How do I care for my dental implant?

Brush and floss daily, just like with your natural teeth. Regular dental checkups are important to keep the surrounding gum and bone healthy.

Will my implant look like a natural tooth?

Yes. Your replacement tooth is custom-made to match the colour and shape of your surrounding teeth.

What if I’ve already lost bone in my jaw?

A bone graft may be needed to prepare the area for an implant. We’ll assess this during your consultation and explain your options.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care, dental implants can last many years, often a lifetime.

Are implants better than dentures?

Implants offer more stability and comfort than removable dentures. They also help preserve bone and support natural facial structure.

Is the cost of dental implants covered by insurance?

Some insurance plans offer partial coverage. Our team will help you understand your benefits and provide a clear breakdown of any costs.
