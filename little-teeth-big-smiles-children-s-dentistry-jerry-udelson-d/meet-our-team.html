Michelle

Dental Assistant – Michelle’s journey in dentistry began with a role at the front desk of a general dental office, but it wasn’t long before she discovered her true passion for direct patient care. She pursued her dental assistant certification at Triton College, where she gained two years of experience in general dentistry. What she finds most fulfilling in her role is connecting with her patients and knowing that she is making a positive impact.

When she’s not working, Michelle enjoys spending quality time with her son, cooking, and being outdoors—whether it’s running or simply enjoying nature. Music plays a central role in keeping her grounded, and her favorite color is pink!
