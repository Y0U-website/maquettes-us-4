Jerry Udelson, DDS, PC

Throughout his career Dr. Jerry has spoken at various dental meetings and institutions, both nationally and internationally. In 2003, Dr. Jerry was invited by the People to People Ambassador Program to visit Cuba. There, he observed and learned about the state of health care in Cuba. He also participated in dialogue with Cuban dentists sharing ideas about how their pediatric dental care differs from that offered in the United States.

Dr. Jerry has the expertise, experience, and heart to treat children with a variety of special needs within his practice. His desire to work with special needs patient began years ago. While caring for his brother Mark who was severely disabled with cerebral palsy, he found that his capacity to care for him was natural and the results rewarding.

For many years, Dr. Jerry has offered his time and service to the community. His commitment to the special needs population is evident in his continued volunteer efforts, which has included treatment of individuals from The Infant Welfare Society.

Dr. Jerry was born and raised in the Oak Park Area and graduated from OPRFHS in 1975. He currently resides in the area with his wife Michelle and their four children. In his spare time, Dr. Jerry enjoys traveling with his family, golfing, and glass blowing.

Risa Hurwich, DDS, MPH

Dr. Risa Hurwich is so excited to be a part of the Little Teeth Big Smiles community! A true Midwesterner, Dr. Risa was born in Chicago, IL, and was raised across the border in Munster, IN. She graduated magna cum laude from Indiana University in 2012 with a Bachelor of Arts. She majored in Spanish while at Indiana, and she spent a semester studying abroad in Seville, Spain. Dr. Risa still loves any opportunity to practice her Spanish-speaking skills with her patients and their parents!

Dr. Risa continued her education at Indiana University School of Dentistry and quickly became interested in pediatrics. She served as the co-president of the Pediatric Dentistry Outreach Club and received a community service scholarship, as well as the Special Interest in Pediatric Dentistry award from the Indiana Society of Pediatric Dentists.

When not working as a pediatric dentist, Dr. Risa loves to do yoga and spend time with her family and friends around the city. She also enjoys podcasts, cooking and trying new bites on the Chicago restaurant scene.

Josh Woodruff, DMD

Dr. Josh Woodruff is a Board Certified pediatric dentist. He was born and raised in Huntington, West Virginia. Dr. Josh attended college at the University of Florida where he received a bachelor’s degree in Psychology. Following college, Dr. Josh attended dental school at Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine where he earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree.

Upon graduation from dental school Dr. Josh headed north to New York City where he completed a residency in General Practice Dentistry at New York-Presbyterian Hospital/Cornell Medical Center. Dr. Josh then worked as an adult dentist before deciding to head back and further his education in pediatric dentistry. Dr. Josh continued his training in New York and completed his residency in Pediatric Dentistry at The Mt. Sinai Hospital in Manhattan.

Through his years in dental school, residency, and practice, Dr. Josh has obtained extensive training in treating children of all ages. His residency in pediatric dentistry emphasized greatly behavior management, helping children to feel comfortable and confident in visiting the dental office.

Dr. Josh loves having the opportunity to shape the experiences children have at the dentist and he strives to foster a relationship with every patient so that they feel comfortable and confident at all dental visits. Working with children and young adults on a day to day basis is an exciting journey, and Dr. Josh is happy to be a part of the team.

When he’s not at the office, Dr. Josh can be found spending his free time with his family and his dogs, Gordon & Bruce.

Ines Guerra, DMD, MS

Dr. Ines Guerra began her dental career in Portugal, where she trained and practiced as a dentist before moving to New York City to complete the International Advanced Program in Orthodontics at New York University (NYU) College of Dentistry. She then returned to Portugal, where she worked as an orthodontist, before later relocating to the United States with her family. Her orthodontic background gave her a deeper understanding of growth and development in children, which naturally led her to pursue a career in pediatric dentistry.

At the University of Illinois at Chicago (UIC) College of Dentistry, Dr. Guerra earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree summa cum laude, as well as a Master of Science in Oral Sciences, and completed her residency in Pediatric Dentistry. While at UIC, her research focused on early childhood caries and the impact of maternal and perinatal health on children’s oral health.

Dr. Guerra is passionate about prevention and believes every child deserves a positive, comfortable dental experience. She enjoys caring for children of all ages, particularly those who may feel anxious, and is dedicated to helping families establish strong, lifelong oral health habits. Being fluent in English, Spanish, Portuguese, and French, she is able to connect easily with families from diverse backgrounds and help them feel at home.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Guerra enjoys traveling, hiking, and art, and treasures time spent with her husband and daughter. She is excited to be part of the Little Teeth Big Smiles team and looks forward to serving families in the community.

Bilyana Daskalova, DMD

She is passionate about helping children develop healthy smiles while creating positive dental experiences that help them feel comfortable and confident at the dentist. Dr. Bilyana is excited to be part of the Little Teeth Big Smiles team and looks forward to building relationships with her patients and their families. She loves working with children and helping them feel at ease while providing compassionate, high-quality pediatric dental care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Bilyana enjoys staying active by running, reading novels on her Kindle, and traveling whenever she gets the chance. Most of all, she loves spending time with her two sons and making memories with her family.
