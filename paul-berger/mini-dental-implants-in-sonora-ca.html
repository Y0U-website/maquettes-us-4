Mini Dental Implants

  • Broader eligibility: They work well for patients with less bone volume, making advanced tooth replacement solutions accessible to more people than ever. If you were ever told you weren’t eligible for traditional implants, you’re likely a good candidate for mini implants.
  • Minimally invasive placement: Thanks to their slim design, mini implants usually don’t require bone grafting. The placement is very gentle, and most patients don’t feel any pain during the procedure.
  • Faster treatment timeline: Dr. Berger can place your implants and attach a temporary restoration in a single visit. And, unlike traditional implants, the full treatment with mini implants takes just weeks instead of months.
  • Lower risk of infection: The one-piece design reduces gaps where bacteria can collect, reducing the risk of complications like peri-implantitis (an inflammatory condition that can cause implant failure).
  • Reduced treatment costs: With fewer components and a simpler process, mini implants are more budget-friendly than traditional implants.
  • Quicker recovery time: Thanks to their less invasive placement technique, most patients can return to normal routines within a day or two after the procedure.

What To Expect From the Procedure

The mini dental implant process is straightforward and surprisingly quick. Every part of the process is carefully planned to keep you comfortable, ease anxiety, and help you heal quickly.

Consultation and Planning

The process begins with a free personalized consultation. Dr. Berger will discuss your concerns, evaluate your smile, take images, and confirm that mini implants are right for you. If you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone for traditional implants, don’t worry. You could still be an ideal candidate for mini implants. Because the implants are so small, patients rarely need bone grafting to strengthen the bone.

At this initial visit, Dr. Berger will design your custom treatment plan, which includes how many implants and what type of restoration you’ll need to restore your smile. He’ll also ask you about your preferences and help you choose details like the material for your final restoration. As part of the treatment planning process, our team will help you understand expected costs and explain the insurance and financing options available to you.

Placement and Temporary Restoration

On the day of your treatment, we’ll administer a local anesthetic to keep you comfortably numb throughout the procedure. This step means you shouldn’t feel any pain at all!

Dr. Berger places the implants using a pinhole technique, so no incisions or stitches are needed. Instead, he makes a small pilot hole directly through the gums and into the bone beneath. He’ll then screw the implant into the bone and check its stability.

Mini implants are stable enough to support a restoration immediately, so Dr. Berger can attach a temporary tooth right away. You’ll leave your appointment with a functional, natural-looking smile. And because the procedure is so gentle and efficient, recovery is usually quick and easy.

Final Restoration

After a brief healing period, Dr. Berger will connect your custom-made final restoration. This could be a crown, bridge, or denture, depending on your needs. The entire process takes just weeks rather than months with traditional implants. It’s a faster way to enjoy lasting results.

Aftercare and Maintenance

Caring for your mini implants is simple. Just brush, floss, and visit Dr. Berger regularly for routine checkups. You won’t need any special tools or products, but we do typically recommend a water flosser for more thorough cleaning. With proper care, your mini implants can last as long as you need them to!

How Much Do Mini Implants Cost?

Mini dental implants are often about half the cost of traditional implants, making them a more accessible option for many patients. And because they’re designed to last for many years, they can help you avoid the added expense of future dental work. Unlike bridges or removable dentures, mini implants support long-term function, comfort, and appearance.

In some cases, dental insurance may help cover part of the treatment, especially the restoration. Since every plan differs, we’re happy to guide you through your coverage and answer any questions. We also offer financing options to make care more manageable and affordable. With flexible payment plans, you can spread the cost of treatment over time. Many of our patients find that this makes it easier to move forward with care without putting off their health.

No matter your situation, we’ll work with you to find a plan that fits your budget and needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most people who are missing one or more teeth are great candidates for mini dental implants, even those previously told traditional implants weren’t an option. Because mini implants are smaller and less invasive, they work well for patients with lower bone density and those who want to avoid complex surgeries or lengthy recovery times.

However, they may not be right for everyone. People with uncontrolled diabetes, active gum disease, or certain health conditions that affect healing may need to address those issues first.

The best way to find out if you qualify for mini implants is to book a free, no-obligation consultation with Dr. Berger.

Despite their slim size, mini implants are remarkably strong and stable, so once your final restoration is in place, you can enjoy a full, satisfying diet. That means all your favorite foods are back on the menu. Think crunchy veggies, chewy bagels, juicy steaks, corn-on-the-cob, and more!

Because your teeth are anchored securely in your jaw, you won’t have to worry about slipping or discomfort like you might with other tooth replacement solutions. Get ready for more enjoyable mealtimes!

Mini dental implants are intended to last as long as you need them to. With proper care, they can serve you well for decades. Their one-piece design helps reduce complications, and because they’re made from high-quality titanium, they’re both durable and biocompatible.

Many patients find that mini implants are a reliable, low-maintenance option that holds up beautifully over time. It’s a long-term investment in your smile and your quality of life.
