Same-Day Dental Implants

  • Faster healing: The minimally invasive procedure promotes quicker recovery. Patients are often surprised at how quickly they can return to their normal routines.
  • Bone protection: Like traditional implants, mini implants help prevent the bone loss that starts soon after a tooth is lost. They stimulate the jawbone, preserving its strength and shape. This long-term support for your jawbone is one of the reasons we recommend implants over bridges or dentures .
  • Proven performance: Mini implants are highly effective at supporting crowns , bridges, and implant dentures . In fact, while dental implants have a 95 % success rate, mini dental implants have exceeded the longevity of standard implants in many cases.
  • Greater affordability: Because they are smaller and simpler to place, mini implants cost significantly less than traditional options. This affordability makes them a practical choice for many patients.

Who Is Eligible for Same-Day Dental Implants?

While mini dental implants are the only FDA-approved implants designed for same-day placement, not every patient is a candidate for immediate loading (when the new tooth is immediately attached). In most cases, even small-diameter implants need time to heal and stabilize. This healing process, called osseointegration , allows the bone to grow around the implant and anchor it securely, much like the root of a natural tooth.

Because of this, we generally recommend allowing time for the implant to heal before placing pressure on it. However, in certain situations where appearance is a priority, such as emergencies or special events, we can place a temporary crown that stays out of the bite. This means the implant won’t be used for chewing but will still restore your smile while the bone heals.

Determining if you’re a good candidate for same-day implants depends on several factors, including your oral health, jawbone condition, and personal goals. At your free implant consultation, Dr. Berger will review your medical and dental history, perform a full exam, and take 3D images to assess your jawbone. This information helps him decide if same-day dental implants are right for you.

The Same-Day Procedure

If you qualify for same-day implants, we’ll create a customized treatment plan, walk you through the next steps, and explain the costs and financing options. In some cases, patients even decide to move forward with a mini implant the very same day as their consultation.

Before Dr. Berger places your implant, we’ll use a local anesthetic to numb the area around your gums and jaw. This will keep you comfortable throughout your procedure. However, if you’re anxious about dental work, we also offer oral conscious sedation to keep you at ease. This is something we can discuss at your consultation.

If you have a failing tooth that needs to be extracted, Dr. Berger will gently remove it. Then, Dr. Berger will make a small pilot hole in your jawbone by going directly through the gums. He’ll secure the implant into this pre-made hole and ensure it is stable. Once secured, he can attach a temporary crown to the implant post.

Thanks to the implant’s simple design and Dr. Berger’s gentle technique, the entire process typically takes less than an hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Same-day dental implants are a cost-effective option for those looking for a secure, long-lasting tooth replacement. Mini implants are significantly more affordable than traditional implants, often cutting the overall cost in half. They’re not only budget-friendly but also built to last, offering reliable results for years to come.

To help manage costs, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Coverage varies, so we’ll work with you to verify what your plan includes and guide you through the process.

For any out-of-pocket expenses, we offer flexible financing through trusted third-party lending companies. These options make it easier to fit your treatment into your budget without compromising on care!

Although the mini implant procedure takes only a short time, the results are long-lasting. With consistent oral hygiene, these implants can last a lifetime!

In the first few days after your procedure, it’s important to avoid hard, sticky, or chewy foods. These can put pressure on the implant before it’s fully stable. Once healing is complete, you can return to enjoying your favorite foods without restrictions.

To keep your implants in top condition, maintaining a strong oral hygiene routine is essential. Brush twice a day with a soft-bristled toothbrush, floss daily, and use an alcohol-free mouthwash. If you grind your teeth at night, wearing a nightguard can protect your implants and help extend their lifespan. With the right care, your new smile can stay healthy and strong for many years to come.
