Dental Implant Placement

  • Healthy gum tissue free of periodontal disease
  • Non-smoker or willingness to quit before and after surgery
  • Commitment to good oral hygiene and regular dental visits

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Look, feel and function like natural teeth
  • Preserve jawbone and prevent bone loss
  • Do not require alteration of adjacent healthy teeth
  • Permanent solution that can last a lifetime with proper care
  • Restore full chewing ability and natural speech
