Implant-Supported Dentures

  • Improved chewing ability — eat the foods you love with confidence
  • Preserve jawbone density by stimulating bone through the implants
  • More natural look and feel
  • No denture adhesives needed
  • Long-lasting and durable
  • Improved speech clarity

The Process

The treatment typically begins with a comprehensive evaluation and 3D imaging to determine the optimal number and placement of implants. Usually, four to six implants are placed per arch. After a healing period to allow the implants to integrate with the bone, your custom denture is fabricated and securely attached. Some patients may qualify for same-day temporary teeth while their permanent denture is being made.
