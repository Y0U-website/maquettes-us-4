Frequently Asked Questions about Root Canal Therapy

  • What are the signs of a tooth that needs a root canal? Jaw and facial pain.
  • Sensitivity to hot and cold.
  • Difficulty chewing or biting.
  • Severe, throbbing toothache.
  • Swelling of the gums, face, and jaw.
  • Is a root canal safe? Yes. Root canals safely stop infection and preserve your natural teeth. Please contact our dentists at Summer Dental Orthodontics to learn more about root canals in Las Vegas, NV.
  • Is a root canal painful? Root canals are about as painful as a large filling. You will be numb during the procedure. If you experience dental anxiety, ask about our dental sedation options.
  • How long does a root canal take? A root canal can take 30-90 minutes, depending on the condition and location of your tooth.
  • What should I do to protect my tooth after a root canal? Root canal therapy makes your tooth weaker and more vulnerable. You must cover your tooth with a crown to protect it; otherwise, you risk losing it.
