Frequently Asked Questions about Dental Emergencies

  • Will my tooth need to be removed? Not necessarily. Depending on the level of decay, we may be able to save your tooth with a filling , a dental crown , or root canal therapy. If you’re suffering from tooth pain, please contact our Las Vegas emergency dentist for an exam at Summer Dental Orthodontics .
  • Will my toothache go away on its own? While your pain may occasionally lessen, that does not mean the problem went away. Often, the pain comes back worse than before. Acting quickly will prevent more damage to your tooth and treat the cause of your toothache.
  • What are the signs of an infected tooth? Jaw and facial pain.
  • Sensitivity to hot and cold.
  • Difficulty chewing or biting.
  • Severe, throbbing toothache.
  • Swelling of the gums, face, and jaw.
  • What should I do if I knock out a tooth? Pick up the tooth by the crown (not the root) and rinse it off briefly. If possible, place it back into the socket. If not, put the tooth in your mouth close to your cheek or in a small container of milk. Call us immediately or walk in Monday through Sunday. The sooner you can get to our office, the better the chance we can save your tooth.
  • Can the emergency room treat my dental emergency? While hospital emergency rooms may give you medication to temporarily ease your pain, you will probably need to see a dentist to treat your dental emergency.
