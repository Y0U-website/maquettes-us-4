Frequently Asked Questions about Orthodontics

  • Why do I need to straighten my teeth? Gapped, crooked, or misaligned teeth may trap bacteria, leading to cavities, tooth decay, and gum disease. In addition, misaligned teeth often cause increased tension in the jaw, neck, and head. You may grind or clench your teeth, making them more prone to chipping and/or cracking. You may also experience migraines and TMJ issues. Please contact our orthodontists for a braces consultation in Las Vegas, NV. Our team at Summer Dental Orthodontics works with kids, teens, and adults.
  • Can I straighten my teeth at home? At-home aligner kits lack a dentist’s supervision, making them a risky option for something as important as tooth alignment. Misaligned teeth can cause teeth grinding, headaches, jaw pain, tooth decay, and gum disease.
  • How long will my treatment take? The average treatment time for braces is two years, though you may achieve your desired results more quickly. The most important factor in your treatment is you! The more compliant you are with your treatment, the faster you’ll see results.
  • What should I avoid while in braces? Avoid hard and sticky foods like jolly ranchers, lollipops, nuts, popcorn, gum, taffy, etc.
  • How do I care for my teeth after braces? Once your teeth are in the desired position, you’ll wear a clear retainer at night to prevent your teeth from shifting back.
