Website Accessibility Statement

Our commitment

We continually work to improve every visitor’s experience and aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA wherever reasonably possible.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As our website evolves, we review its content, navigation, forms, color contrast, and keyboard experience to identify and address barriers.

Need assistance?

If you have difficulty accessing any part of this website or need information about our dental services, appointments, office hours, or location in another accessible format, please contact us.

We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service you need through an accessible communication method.

Accessibility feedback

When contacting us, please tell us which page or feature caused difficulty. If you are comfortable sharing the browser or assistive technology you were using, that information can help us investigate and improve the experience.

We are here to help

Contact our Oakland Park office and our team will help you access the information or service you need.
