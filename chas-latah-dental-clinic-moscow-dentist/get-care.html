Medical Care Team

At CHAS Health, your health care is important to us. CHAS Health has a care team of various healthcare professionals to ensure you are seen, heard, and cared for.

CHAS Health’s care team includes providers, medical assistants, registered nurses, clinical pharmacists, dieticians, and other professionals who support you. You might not see your provider at your appointment, but your provider will remain your provider. We work as a team at CHAS Health to provide you with the best care possible.

When it comes to whole-person care of you as the patient, CHAS Health wants to make it as easy as possible to access the care you need in a timely manner.

Schedule a Visit

The first step to getting care at one of our many CHAS Health clinic locations is to schedule a visit. Call our main scheduling number to choose a convenient location, find the provider you need, and get started. Currently, care is available through video visits and office visits. Your scheduler will help you determine what kind of visit is right for your needs.

Urgent Care

CHAS Health offers walk-in urgent care in extended hours at two locations, North Spokane and Spokane Valley, so you can get immediate care for urgent medical needs that aren’t serious enough for the emergency room.

After Hours Care

When CHAS Health urgent care clinics aren’t open, you can access our 24/7 Nurse Advice Hotline, which is available for medical emergencies after hours with on-call medical, dental, and OB providers available if it’s an after-hours emergency, after-hours access care through our Nurse Advice Hotline.

Medical

At CHAS Health, our providers offer comprehensive care from primary and urgent care to a wide range of specialties to meet all medical needs for you and your family. Our medical providers are based in Spokane, Spokane Valley, the Latah community, and the Lewis-Clark Valley.

Dental

Our dental clinics are in Cheney, Moscow, Clarkston, Deer Park, and Spokane. Visit our dental clinics for your next exam, cleaning, or x-ray, and discover how our friendly staff can make your next root canal, crown, or filling can be a positive experience.

Pharmacy

We have convenient, in-clinic pharmacies at our primary care sites for easy prescription pick-up. We also offer prescription auto-refill and mail delivery, check with your pharmacy for eligibility. Additionally, our expert pharmacy staff offers anticoagulation services and diabetes education.

Behavioral Health

We offer psychiatry, medication management, counseling for adults, and specialized therapies for children and adolescents. Additionally, chemical dependency professionals offer substance abuse services, including assessment and counseling. We also have a team dedicated to reaching out to and caring for those without a home.
