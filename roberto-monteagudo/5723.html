Restorations

West Allis dentist Dr. Monteagudo offers many restoration options. If you need a filling, we ve got you covered with beautiful, tooth-colored composite fillings. We also offer CEREC crowns , the state-of-the-art system that offers you same day permanent crowns. No more fussing with temporary crowns while the lab crafts the crown. With this system you ll be in and out and on your way with a strong, healthy-looking restoration.

If you re missing teeth, we also offer dental implants. Implants are the closest dental science has yet to get to replacing your natural tooth. These biocompatible titanium implants not only restore your smile to its natural beauty, but also help keep your bite properly aligned and your jawbone healthy and strong.
