The practice

The mission at Kappa Dental Group is to pursue excellence, embrace innovation and keep a high ethical standard in patient care. We treat our patients according to their individual needs while keeping the highest level in the quality of our services. It is our goal to work in partnership with our patients so they can make informed decisions that are best suited to their needs.

What makes Kappa Dental Group unique is the dedication of the providers to their profession and their commitment to progress. Our specialists devote part of their schedules to training the next generation of dentists, staying current with best practices and taking an interdisciplinary approach to dentistry.

Kappa Dental Group is composed of accomplished and recognized specialists in Endodontics, Periodontics, Orthodontics, experienced Restorative dentists and dedicated hygienists. We work in synergy in order to provide the best comprehensive dental care to our patients.
