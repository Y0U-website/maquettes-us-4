ABOUT T HE CENTER

The Wisdom Tooth Center was created by nationally-recognized Oklahoma oral surgeons for the express purpose of simplifying the process of wisdom tooth extraction surgery and dental implants for all Oklahomans. We offer oral surgery procedures with flat package pricing that is easy to understand and plan for, with no mysterious, unexpected fees.

Our team is highly-skilled, all board-certified surgeons with a combined 70+ years of experience in oral surgery. Dr. Sullivan and Dr. Smith have trained over 65 surgeons in their leadership roles at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry. Dr. Tiwana has trained over 35 surgeons while affiliated with the University of Louisville and Parkland Hospital in Dallas. Doctors Sullivan, Smith and Tiwana enjoy national and international reputations for their contributions to the specialty of oral and maxillofacial surgery.

Expert Precision

Doctors affiliated with Wisdom Tooth Center are Oklahomans focused on precision and expert execution that lead to results for you.

REDEFINING THE PROCESS

Wisdom tooth removal patients can enjoy the combination of experts with experience, focused services and dedicated teams, clear pricing with or without insurance, and the best-in-class technology. We offer flat package pricing for our uninsured patients that is easy to understand, plan for, and with no mysterious, unexpected fees.

We are redefining the process of wisdom tooth care, dental implants and oral surgery, because our goals are your goals:

Faster recovery Quicker, perfected processes Expert-focused surgeons Dedicated, highly-trained team Simplified pricing
