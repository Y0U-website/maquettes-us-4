What Should I Expect During the All-On-4 ® Procedure?

Here are the three basic restorative treatments for which All-On-4 ® or All-On-6 procedures can often be an effective option:

Upper Arch Replacement

For patients who are missing all teeth on the upper row, All-On-4 ® can beautifully rejuvenate smile aesthetics while improving both speaking and chewing abilities.

Lower Arch Replacement

An All-On-4 ® implant denture to replace all teeth on the lower dental arch can help enhance the look of the smile and improve chewing and speaking. Lower arch replacement can also address changes in jaw and facial symmetry caused by the loss of multiple teeth on the bottom row.

Full Mouth Restoration

For individuals who are missing all of their teeth on the upper and lower dental arches, All-On-4 ® implant dentures on both rows may be an ideal treatment. This can provide a full mouth restoration that renews both function and appearance, ultimately resulting in significant, comprehensive improvement.
