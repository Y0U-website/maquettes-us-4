RiverStone Dental is a father-son dental practice specializing in biocompatible dentistry. Our doctors believe in treating each patient as an individual, improving not only their dental health but their overall systemic health as well. We specialize in the removal of mercury fillings by following specific protocols outlined by IAOMT and replacing those fillings with biocompatible materials.
