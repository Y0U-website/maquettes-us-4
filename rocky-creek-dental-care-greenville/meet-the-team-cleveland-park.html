Building Lasting Relationships

As a practice, we are very grateful to be a part of this community and love to be involved. While we support multiple swim teams and sports teams, we also partner up with our patients to make a real impact together, such as raising money for local charities like McCall Hospice House and collecting hundreds of pounds of canned food for Harvest Hope. We participate in team charity runs, visit schools during dental health month, and provide free dental services through Dental Life Line.

One of our favorite projects was collecting scarves and mittens with our generous patients and leaving them around areas of need with an encouraging note one cold winter day. We appreciate our patients and love being a part of our community!
