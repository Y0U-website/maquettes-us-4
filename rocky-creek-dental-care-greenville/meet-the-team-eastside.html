Dr. Myles Elliott

Dr. Myles Elliott joined the Rocky Creek family in January 2021. As a Greenville native, he’s proud to serve the community where he grew up. Dr. Elliott graduated from Riverside High School, Clemson University, and the Medical University of South Carolina. While at MUSC, he enjoyed serving as a legislative liaison for the ADA and SCDA in Washington, D.C. After earning his dental degree, he spent a few years practicing in Columbia before he and his wife, Blair, made their way back home to the Upstate.

Dr. Elliott is a member of the SCDA, ADA, and AGD. He’s passionate about getting to know his patients, building lasting relationships, and helping each one feel confident about their smile and oral health.

Outside the office, Dr. Elliott loves spending time with his family. He and Blair have two wonderful children—a daughter and a son—and a Boykin Spaniel named Teal. The Elliott family are active members at Mitchell Road Presbyterian Church. When he’s not with patients, you can find him traveling, enjoying time on the lake, hunting, or cheering on his beloved Clemson Tigers.

Dr. John R. Piccione

Dr. John R. Piccione received a Bachelor’s degree from Illinois Benedictine College and graduated in 1983 from the University of Illinois Dental School. After practicing dentistry in the Chicago area for 10 years, Dr. Piccione began practicing in Greenville in 1993 (his wife’s hometown). He is a member of the American Dental Association , South Carolina Dental Association , Piedmont Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry . He and his wife Kathy have three children and one grandchild.
