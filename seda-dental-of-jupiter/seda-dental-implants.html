Our Dental Implant Process: From Consultation to Aftercare

  • Consultation and treatment planning: A dental implant specialist will assess your oral health, review your medical history, and determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants.
  • Tooth extraction (if necessary): If the damaged or decayed tooth hasn’t been removed yet, it will be extracted to make room for the implant.
  • Bone grafting (if necessary): If your jawbone lacks the required density or quality to support the implant, a bone grafting procedure may be needed to create a stronger foundation.
  • Implant placement: A dental implant specialist will surgically insert the dental implant (a small, screw-like post) into your jawbone. This procedure is typically done under local anesthesia.
  • Osseointegration: Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with the surrounding bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable and secure base for the prosthetic tooth.
  • Abutment placement: After osseointegration is complete, a small connector called an abutment is attached to the implant.
  • Prosthetic tooth placement: Finally, a custom-made dental crown, bridge, or denture is fabricated to match the color and shape of your natural teeth and is attached to the abutment.
