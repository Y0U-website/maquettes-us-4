What Is All-on-4?

All-on-4 replaces an entire upper or lower arch of missing or failing teeth using just four strategically placed dental implants. The new teeth are fixed in place, so they do not slip or come out like dentures. Most patients leave surgery with a functional temporary bridge the same day, then receive their final set once healed.

Is All-on-4 Right for You?

All-on-4 is built for people missing most or all teeth in an arch, or whose remaining teeth are failing from decay or gum disease. If dentures are wearing you down, or you are facing the loss of many teeth at once, this is the modern alternative.

Because the rear implants are angled into denser bone, many patients who were told they lack the bone for implants still qualify. Where grafting genuinely is needed, our in-house bone grafting and sinus lift expertise handles it. For single missing teeth, see dental implants .

Life After All-on-4

The first weeks favor a soft-food diet while your implants integrate with the bone. From there, function climbs steadily until your final teeth arrive, at which point you simply live your life: brush, use the water flosser we recommend, and keep your checkups.

Implant-supported teeth do not decay, but the gums around them still need care. Routine professional cleanings protect your investment for decades.

Why choose SEDA for All-on-4

How painful is the recovery?

Most patients describe it as far easier than expected: a few days of swelling and soreness managed with medication. Many are back to normal routines within a week.

How long do All-on-4 implants last?

The implants themselves can last decades, often a lifetime, with good hygiene. The tooth bridge may be refreshed after many years of wear.

What if I was told I do not have enough bone?

Get a second opinion with a 3D scan. The angled All-on-4 technique uses bone most people still have, and grafting can rebuild the rest when needed.

Can All-on-4 replace just my upper or lower teeth?

Yes. Each arch is treated independently. Some patients do one arch now and the other later.

How do I clean fixed implant teeth?

Brush normally, clean under the bridge with a water flosser or special floss, and keep regular hygiene visits. We walk you through the exact routine.
