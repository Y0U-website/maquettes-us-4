Clear Aligner Therapy

Clear Aligner Therapy is a type of orthodontic treatment that consists of a series of clear trays. Expect to receive a number of trays every few months, each slightly different than the last, to be worn 7-10 days at a time.

Patients prefer clear aligner therapy as opposed to traditional braces because they are virtually invisible! Orthodontics are sometimes recommended in certain esthetic cases prior to veneers in order to achieve a stable functional alignment. Results after clear aligner therapy are often the cosmetic result some patients are looking for, no need for restorative work!

Whitening

Enamel, the outermost layer of our teeth, contain micro-pores that can lock in stains over time. There are two major types of stains: Extrinsic and Intrinsic. Extrinsic stains refer to external environmental factors such as coffee, tea, wine, smoking etc. Intrinsic stains (ie dead/necrotic teeth or teeth stained from the use of tetracyclines) refer to internal stains that are more permanent and resistant to whitening procedures.

First teeth are polished in order to remove any superficial debris. Next, a barrier is carefully placed on your gums, lips, and cheeks (think everything but your teeth!) in order to isolate and protect the soft tissues from any damage from the whitening agents. A whitening gel is applied to your teeth until the desired shade is achieved. A post-operative sensitivity kit is handed to each patient to manage any post operative issues. It is important to remember that some sensitivity is completely normal and temporary!

ZOOM!

ZOOM! Whitening is a very effective whitening therapy that consists a single, ONE hour appointment! Four 15 minute sessions are completed with a light activated whitening gel. A mirror is offered between gel applications and patients can stop at any time. Expect to walk out with brighter and whiter teeth the same day!

KOR

KOR Whitening is another whitening therapy that is recommended for patients with either deeper staining or patients that are looking for a more dramatic result. KOR Max (the most commonly recommended system) consists of 2 weeks of at home whitening therapy with their signature KOR trays, followed by a 1 hour in office whitening procedure.

Internal Bleaching

Teeth that have greyed after a root canal (due to a history of trauma or necrosis), the doctor may recommend a procedure known as internal bleaching. A peroxide gel is placed into the already empty chamber for two weeks and sealed with a temporary material. This process is repeated until the desired shade is achieved. Afterward, the material is removed and the tooth is sealed with a filling.
