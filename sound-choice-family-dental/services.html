We can help you with

We offer a wide range of services including the following:

Examinations and Prophylaxis

Keeping up with regular cleanings and exams is the best way to reduce the risk of dental issues. We often find and prevent problems early enough to prevent pain and breakage in the future at these appointments. We also provide intraoral cancer screenings, treatment plan consultations, and a variety of cosmetic, smile improving options!

Invisalign

Invisalign uses clear, custom trays to orthodontically straighten teeth. Invisalign can be used for patients of all ages who would like to improve their smile without the use of traditional wires and brackets orthodontics.

Extractions

If you need a tooth pulled, we will perform the procedure as gently as possible. We can also do some wisdom tooth extractions eliminating the need to book an appointment with an oral surgeon.

Restoring Implants

Implant restoration can be performed to replace missing teeth improving speaking and chewing while leaving you with a beautiful smile.

Whitening

We offer a wide range of treatments designed to whiten and brighten your teeth. Schedule a consultation so we can help you find the solutions best suited to your needs.

Fillings

Fillings remove tooth decay to prevent further damage and improve your both your dental and overall health.

Routine Dental Cleanings

Should be performed at least twice a year to maintain dental wellness.

Root Canals

Root canals are performed to treat infections and nerve damage that can occur due to cracks in the tooth, tooth decay, or infection. While many people think root canals are painful, they can often be simple, routine procedures.

Crowns

Crowns are used to reinforce damaged teeth. They are made from materials like ceramic, gold silver, or composites.

Bridges

A dental bridge is a non-removable cemented tooth replacement option that is designed to replace 1-3 missing teeth.

Veneers

Veneers adhere to the fronts of teeth to give them a more uniform yet natural appearance that will take your smile to the next level.

Botox Treatment for TMD

Botox treatment may offer significant reduction in pain, matched with improved functionality and mouth opening capacity. By relaxing the muscles that contribute to jaw movement, Botox can help alleviate headaches and discomfort, reducing jaw tension and preventing unwanted pain.
