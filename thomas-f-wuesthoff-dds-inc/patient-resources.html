  • What Is CareCredit? For patients without dental insurance, CareCredit can be a convenient and versatile option. Under CareCredit, patients can make small payments over time, rather than all at once. CareCredit has no up-front costs, no pre-payment penalties and no annual fees. It also offers no-interest financing if paid within 12 months (additional plans may be available). Your CareCredit card can be used again and again for additional treatment for yourself or your family in any CareCredit practice, and members can sign up for it online.
  • Do I Need A Dental X-Ray? Dental X-rays are an important part of the diagnostic process at the dentist and should be used only when necessary. At Westlake Dental Associates, our team uses digital dentistry and 3D Cone Beam imaging to create highly comprehensive virtual roadmaps of the mouth, bone, joints, gums and teeth, with up to 90 % less exposure to radiation than traditional dental film. These images reveal changes in dental health in cutting edge detail, offering dentists the ability to provide the best forms of treatments, treatment planning and advice.
