General Dentistry

Regular cleaning and checkups remove the plaque and prevent tartar formation and gum diseases. A regular check-up every six months helps prevent serious oral damage.

Emergency Services

When oral pain can’t wait for the scheduled appointment, we offer emergency services for your comfort. We treat toothaches, chipped teeth, lost fillings and more.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Dentistry is much more than just your state of oral health. Our various dental-cosmetic procedures enhance the appearance of your teeth and empower you to smile confidently.

Dental Crowns

If you want to preserve the integrity and health of your teeth, our experienced staff can help you, by providing detailed information about dental crowns, bridges with implants and their benefits.

Periodontal/Gum Disease Treatment

We ensure you know the difficulties come with periodontal or gum disease and their prevention. We provide treatment for gum diseases.

Root Canals

Root Canal prevents future infections and other related problems. We execute the process with perfection and extreme care.

Conscious Sedation

As promised, we treat patients with love and care. We make your visits as comfortable as possible by offering nitrous oxide analgesia (laughing gas). We also provide sedation using a small pill with nitrous oxide, for kids or adults, who are not very comfortable with the process.

Dental Hygiene

Our Dental hygienists clean your teeth, examine our patients for signs of oral diseases such as gingivitis, and provide other preventive dental care. They also educate our patients on ways to improve and maintain good oral health.

We, at Cascio Family Dental Care, treat emergencies as a top priority. We understand what tooth pain feels like and the importance of relieving it. Our same-day emergency team takes the case as a priority and initiates the treatment at the earliest. We ensure fast, safe, and best solutions.
