Dr. Tram Le, DDS

Dr. Tram Le is a skilled and compassionate dentist who combines clinical precision with a refined artistic eye to create beautiful, lasting smiles. Known for her attention to detail and warm, approachable manner, she is dedicated to delivering care that is as personalized as it is high-quality.

Dr. Le earned her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry before moving to New York to complete her residency at Wyckoff Heights Medical Center, where she was selected to serve as chief resident — a distinction reflecting both her clinical excellence and leadership.

With a thoughtful, patient-centered approach, Dr. Le focuses on achieving optimal oral health while enhancing each patient&#x27;s natural smile. She prioritizes comfort, clear communication, and individualized treatment planning, ensuring that every patient feels informed, at ease, and confident in their care.

Outside of the office, Dr. Le is an avid surfer who finds both inspiration and balance in the ocean — an energy she brings into her practice through her calm, focused, and attentive approach to dentistry.

Dr. Aaron Tadayon, DMD

Dr. Aaron Tadayon is dedicated to helping patients preserve their natural teeth through advanced, compassionate endodontic care. With a strong foundation in both general dentistry and specialty training, he brings precision, expertise, and a calm, reassuring presence to every treatment.

Originally from Baltimore, Maryland, Dr. Tadayon earned his B.A. from the University of Miami before obtaining his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry in 2019. He went on to complete a General Practice Residency at NYU Langone, where he further strengthened his skills in comprehensive patient care.

He then pursued advanced specialty training in endodontics at Montefiore Medical Center and NYU Langone, developing extensive expertise in root canal therapy, microsurgery, and laser-assisted endodontic techniques.

Dr. Tadayon is known for his gentle approach and clear, thoughtful communication. He takes time to ensure patients feel comfortable, informed, and confident at every stage of treatment. Outside the practice, he enjoys basketball, traveling, and exploring his neighborhood of Greenpoint, Brooklyn.
