Meet the Team

Dr. Steven F. Puffer, DMD

Dr. Puffer relocated to Nacogdoches, Texas in June of 2015. He is always educating himself and his team by finding high-quality continuing education programs which help them provide the utmost and quality care for his patients.

Dr. Puffer studied implant surgery under Dr. Hilt Tatum Jr. from 2016-2017, completing the Puerto Rico American Academy of Implant Dentistry MaxiCourse program and clinical residency. Upon graduation, he and his team of fellow dentists collaborated on a case that required a year-long series of surgeries. Their work was honored by the Academy of Implant Dentistry for "Treatment of the Atrophic Maxilla: A Case Study Utilizing the NIRISAB Approach", with the case placing second among the many reviewed by the Academy of Implant Dentistry.

In 2023, Dr. Puffer earned his Masters from the Academy of General Dentistry, a distinction achieved by less than 5 % of dentists that requires hundreds of hours of advanced training and the successful completion of a rigorous examination. Most recently, in January 2026, he received his master’s degree from the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology (ADSA), further expanding his expertise in sedation and anesthesia to ensure the highest level of patient comfort and safety.

He is a diplomate of the American Board of Oral Implantology/Implant Dentistry. He is a member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology, the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association , the Texas Dental Association, the Total Patient Service Institute, and The Crown Council. *
