The practice

At Puffer Dental , we believe great dentistry starts with genuine care. Our team is committed to creating a welcoming, comfortable environment where patients of all ages feel heard, respected, and confident in their care.

Led by Dr. Steven F. Puffer, our practice combines advanced dental technology with a patient-first approach. From routine cleanings to cosmetic and restorative treatments, we focus on personalized solutions designed to support long-term oral health and overall well-being.

As a proud part of the Nacogdoches community , we’re honored to serve generations of families with honest recommendations, gentle care, and results you can trust all in a space designed to feel modern, calm, and inviting.

Your smile is our priority, and we’re here to care for it every step of the way.

We proudly offer a full range of dental services to meet the needs of individuals and families, helping patients maintain healthy smiles for life.
