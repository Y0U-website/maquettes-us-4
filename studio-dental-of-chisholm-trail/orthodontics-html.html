How Does Invisalign Work?

Who Can Invisalign Help?

Many patients aren’t aware that Invisalign in Fort Worth can treat a variety of orthodontic issues, not just crowded teeth. Our team will evaluate your smile during your initial consultation to determine how best to address your bite misalignment issues. Until you’ve come to visit us, here are several common problems that clear aligners can help fix.

Crowded Teeth

Teeth that are overlapping and too close together can be difficult to clean and efficient at trapping harmful substances such as food debris and bacteria. Invisalign aligners can act to gradually move these teeth side-by-side to reduce the risk of developing oral health problems down the road.

Gaps Between Teeth

Having spaces between teeth can make you feel embarrassed by your smile. It can also create hard-to-reach nooks and crannies for food particles to become trapped in. Invisalign aligners can help shift these teeth closer together, creating a fuller-looking and more even smile.

Overbite

A subtle overbite is typically natural and healthy for chewing food effectively and showing off a nice smile. However, if your upper teeth are protruding too far away from your bottom set, this can result in orthodontic complications. Not only can this make eating more difficult, but it can also increase the risk of uneven wear and tear on your smile as well as increase the risk of jaw discomfort, speech problems, and more. With aid from specialized accessories, Invisalign can help correct this misalignment issue.

Underbite

An underbite is when the lower arch of teeth protrudes further out from the upper arch. This can be due to various factors such as oral injuries and genetics. Whatever the reason, leaving this orthodontic problem untreated can potentially lead to other issues like mouth breathing, trouble with chewing or talking, and even a higher risk of sleep apnea. Invisalign can straighten your teeth as well as help gradually move your jaw into the proper position.

Crossbite

Sometimes both the upper and lower teeth can end up resting in front of each other, a condition known as a crossbite. Due to the lack of uniformity across the smile, you can end up having trouble chewing efficiently or experiencing an increased risk of gum disease because of the difficulty in cleaning your teeth. Other issues like headaches and jaw pain can also arise without orthodontic treatment. Invisalign can be the solution you need to gradually shift both upper and lower teeth into a healthier alignment.

Open Bite

An open bite is when the upper and lower arch of teeth refuse to touch each other, creating a gap in your smile that won’t close even when biting down. Not only can this impact your facial appearance but it can also interfere with proper oral function, such as eating, talking, and smiling comfortably. By using Invisalign along with attachments and elastic bands, our team can help improve your orthodontic situation.

Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign is a modern orthodontic treatment that allows patients to achieve a straightened and aligned smile without having to deal with inconvenient and intrusive oral appliances, such as metal brackets and wires. This procedure offers a variety of unique benefits compared to traditional braces, including:

Faster Average Treatment Timeline

The average Invisalign treatment plan lasts between 12 and 18 months. Traditional braces, on the other hand, often require 24 to 36 months to achieve optimal results. So, even though factors like the complexity of your case need to be taken into account, there is a good chance that you’ll have straight teeth sooner if you choose clear aligner treatment!

Custom-Made, Comfortable Aligners

One reason that patients don’t love traditional braces is because the brackets and wires are known to scratch and irritate the soft tissue in your mouth. Fortunately, Invisalign aligners are made from a smooth, plastic material. Plus, they are custom-made to fit your unique dental structure, reducing the risk of cuts to your tongue, lips, and gums even further.

Virtually Undetectable Trays

Aligners are made from a virtually unnoticeable material that doesn’t stick out like shiny metal. Instead, they seamlessly blend into your smile, preventing you from feeling the need to hide your braces throughout your teeth-straightening journey.

Note: The trays are also removable, which allows you to have an even clearer view of the progress you’re making in your treatment plan!

Easy Maintenance

Because your Invisalign aligners will be removable, you won’t need to worry about maneuvering around bulky brackets and wires in order to clean your teeth and gums properly. All you have to do to brush and floss is take out your aligners. That means that you’ll be at a decreased risk of developing oral health problems during your treatment, including tooth decay and gum disease.

No Dietary Restrictions

Do you love snacking on whole apples, raw nuts, and crunchy vegetables, like celery? If so, you’ll be happy to hear that (unlike traditional braces) Invisalign doesn’t come with any dietary restrictions! The only guideline you need to keep in mind is not over-indulging in foods and drinks with added sugar. That way, there’s a lesser chance of oral health problems developing and delaying your treatment plan.

Quick Follow-Up Appointments

Lastly, Invisalign follow-up appointments are usually pretty quick. In fact, they usually only last between 15 and 30 minutes! That combined with the fact that they often take place every six to eight weeks, and you can see why patients with busy schedules prefer this orthodontic treatment.

Living with Invisalign Aligners

At this point, you might be wondering, What will my day-to-day life look like with Invisalign? Since the aligners are removable and won’t draw any unwanted attention to your smile, you don’t need to completely overhaul your routine. That said, there are a few things you need to keep in mind to keep your smile healthy and stay on-track with your treatment plan.

Wearing Your Trays

There’s basically one rule: you need to wear your aligners for a minimum of 20 hours a day. At first, this may seem impossible, but there are several habits that make it significantly easier, starting with only removing your trays when it’s absolutely necessary. It’s also important to put them back on as soon as you can.

Tip: Multi-task where you can! For example, instead of drinking your morning cup of coffee on its own, drink it with your breakfast.

Cleaning Your Aligners

Naturally, you don’t want your aligners to develop stubborn stains or an unpleasant odor. Good news: you don’t need any special oral hygiene products to keep them clean! Just use clean, cool water to rinse them every time you take them off, and clean them with a damp, soft-bristled toothbrush before you put them back on. Many patients use the cleaning crystals as well, especially after 10+ days of wear.

Eating & Drinking

To prevent your aligners from discoloring, breaking, or developing an unpleasant odor, you need to take them off before eating or drinking anything other than water. When you take them off, place them in their designated case to reduce the chances of them getting lost or misplaced. Then, when you’re done enjoying your meal, brush your teeth, clean your aligners, and put them back on ASAP.

Losing or Damaging a Tray

Losing or damaging a tray may not seem like a big deal, but it can lead to significant treatment plan delays. So, we strongly recommend doing what you can to avoid finding yourself in either of these situations from cleaning your aligners over a soft towel to placing them in their designated case anytime you aren’t wearing them. With all of that said, accidents sometimes happen. In that case, call us ASAP.

Tip: Always put your clear aligner case in the same place! If you can, choose a brightly-colored one so it’s easy to find too.

Invisalign FAQs

Do you still have questions about getting Invisalign in Fort Worth? Our team has answered some common questions that we receive from patients below to help you decide whether this advanced orthodontic treatment is the right solution for you.

Does it hurt to wear Invisalign aligners?

Unlike traditional metal braces that can rub against cheeks and lips, causing irritation and wounds, Invisalign clear aligners are made from a smooth plastic that’s more comfortable. With Invisalign treatment, you won’t have to worry about constantly carrying around orthodontic wax with you. Plus, since each set of trays is custom fitted to your mouth, they won’t protrude or snag on your oral tissues.

Many patients do report slight discomfort when they advance to a new set of aligners because they place a different amount of pressure on the teeth. However, this typically goes away within a day and can be avoided if you switch your aligners at night. That way, you sleep through a majority of the adjustment period. You can also take pain medication such as ibuprofen to help make you more comfortable.

How do I clean my Invisalign trays?

Maintaining your trays during your treatment is an important part of your oral hygiene. Without regular cleanings, food debris and plaque could become trapped between your appliance and teeth, increasing your risk of developing oral health problems. Here are some basic tips to help keep your trays clean:

If you need any extra help and tips to maintain your aligners, don’t hesitate to contact our office . Our team can answer any questions you may have and provide you with some helpful guidance.

  • Soak your aligners in a 50/50 mixture of water and vinegar for 15 minutes each day.
  • Brush your aligners using a soft-bristled toothbrush and clear antibacterial soap.
  • Rinse your aligners before you put them in your mouth.
  • Brush your teeth after each time you eat.

What should I do if I break or lose an aligner?

If you damage or lose one of your trays, contact our office immediately so we can solve the problem without pushing back your treatment. Depending on how far you were through your two week time period on that certain set of trays, we may recommend moving to your previous ones, advancing ahead, or getting you a replacement. The longer you wait to contact us, the further back your treatment timeline will be pushed, so let us help!

How much does Invisalign treatment cost?

The cost of Invisalign treatment depends on each unique patient’s situation. Typically, the more severe your orthodontic problems are, the more aligners you’ll need in your treatment, the longer it will take, and the higher the cost. However, we’ll let you know during your initial consultation how much your treatment price will be.

How long does Invisalign take?

While traditional braces often take 2+ years, the average treatment timeline with Invisalign is only 12-18 months. Not only that, but some patients with mild spacing and crowding issues achieve the results they desire in just 4-8 months! During your consultation, we will review your dental history, determine if any preliminary treatments are needed, and assess the complexity of your case. Can I drink coffee with Invisalign?

You can drink coffee with Invisalign in Fort Worth! Just make sure that you remove your aligners first. After all, hot coffee can cause the aligners to warp, and iced lattes can leave noticeable, yellow stains on the surface.

How often do I need to wear my Invisalign aligners?

In order to stay on track with your treatment plan, you need to keep your aligners on for a minimum of 20 hours a day, every day. Towards the beginning of your orthodontic treatment, you may find it helpful to use the stopwatch on your phone so you can see how close or far you are from the mark. Once you find a routine that works, wearing your trays for the recommended amount of time will become second nature.
