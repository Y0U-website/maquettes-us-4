Digital X-rays

All of our x-rays taken in the office are completely digital, and expose you to 90 % less radiation than regular film x-rays. Digital x-ray sensors act Just like a snap and shoot digital camera, your x-rays are available almost instantaneously. With digital x-rays, we are able to catch early cavities, signs of dental nerve infection, cysts, etc. Our Patient education TV monitors will show all of your x-rays so your Pingree Grove dentist will be able to discuss your oral health and educate you on recommended treatment options.

Laser Dentistry

One of the latest advances in dentistry is the soft tissue laser. Soft Tissue Laser uses laser energy to perform a variety of soft tissue dental procedures. Laser technology allows us to perform variety of soft tissue procedures with better precision, no use of scalpels or blades, causes less discomfort, decreased pain, less bleeding and faster healing times. Numerous research studies have shown that laser dentistry can be far less traumatic than other instruments, especially for anxious patients.
