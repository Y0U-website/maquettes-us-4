How do you determine the total cost?

Dr. Shoffet evaluates your mouth. Factors such as the number and health of the remaining teeth contribute to the nature and type of treatment/appliance that is recommended. Treatments and appliances can vary considerably in cost. For instance, full arch implants to replace all of the teeth in the upper or lower jaw generally cost more than implants or restorations to replace one tooth or a few teeth.

Do some full arch options cost more than others?

Yes. Appliances can be designed in a variety of different ways. There are varied systems and materials. Removable options that stabilize full-arch restorations or dentures largely cost less than their counterparts that are fixed in the jawbone. This latter alternative can only be removed by a dentist.

How much do dental implants cost per tooth?

A single-tooth implant costs an average of $4,000 to $6,000. Implants to replace a single tooth generally include the artificial tooth root itself (the implant), the abutment or connector piece that links the implant and the new tooth, and the restoration (dental crown). Costs may also be based on things like the location of the tooth, materials used, and additional procedures that are necessary for the success of your implant and to complete treatment.
