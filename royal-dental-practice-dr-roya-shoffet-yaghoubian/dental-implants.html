Dental Implants FAQs

What is a dental implant?

A dental implant is a small, surgically inserted titanium post that acts as an artificial tooth root, anchored securely into your jawbone and topped with a custom-made crown. Unlike removable options, implants fuse directly with the bone to provide stability for chewing, speaking, and smiling with confidence. At Royal Dental Practice, implants are planned and placed with precision and care to restore both function and aesthetics.

What advantages do dental implants offer over other tooth-replacement options?

Implants provide several long-term benefits. They feel and function like natural teeth, prevent bone loss by stimulating the jaw, and avoid altering nearby healthy teeth (unlike bridges ). They also eliminate the hassles of removable dentures , like adhesives or slippage, and support lasting oral health. At our practice, we prioritize these benefits, helping patients achieve comfortable, permanent, and natural-looking results.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Local anesthesia ensures minimal discomfort during implant placement. Most patients feel pressure, not pain, during the procedure. It’s common to experience mild swelling or soreness afterward, which typically subsides in just a few days and is manageable with over-the-counter pain relief. At Royal Dental Practice, your comfort and well-being throughout the implant process are top priorities.

What does the dental implant procedure involve?

Treatment generally unfolds in several phases. First, the implant post is surgically placed into the jaw and allowed to integrate over a few months. Once healed, an abutment is secured on top, followed by the placement of a custom crown. Depending on case complexity, stage timing may vary. Our team oversees each stage carefully to ensure healing, precise fit, and natural-looking results.

How much does a dental implant cost?

Implant costs depend on factors like the number of implants, bone grafting needs, and restoration type (single crown vs. multi-unit prosthesis). On average, a single-tooth implant costs $3,000 $6,000, depending on each individual’s available bone. Multiple implants or full-arch options may cost more. During your consultation, we provide a personalized treatment plan with clear breakdowns of pricing and payment options to fit your needs.

Is a dental implant covered by insurance?

Insurance coverage for implants varies widely. Some plans may cover a portion if implants are medically necessary, but many view them as elective or cosmetic. If coverage is available, it may be limited to a percentage of cost or subject to annual maximums. At Royal Dental Practice, we help you understand your plan, secure any available benefits, and offer flexible financial solutions when needed.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene, routine dental checkups , and healthy habits, dental implants can last a lifetime. Unlike other restorations, implants won’t decay and are structurally resilient. We use premium materials and precise placement techniques to maximize long-term success, supported by ongoing care for lasting oral health.

What should I do after my implant is completed?

Post-implant care is straightforward: maintain daily brushing and flossing, and consider a water flosser to clean around the implant. Avoid hard or sticky foods during healing. Stay consistent with appointments so we can monitor the implant’s health and your overall oral condition. Choosing Royal Dental Practice ensures you receive reliable support from implant placement through years of confident, healthy function.

Are implants right for you?

Most people who are healthy enough for a very minor oral surgery are potential implant candidates. However, a CT scan or X-ray is necessary to ensure that you have enough bone tissue to support them. Occasionally patients with severe bone loss may need a grafting procedure before implants can be placed.
