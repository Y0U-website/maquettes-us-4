Treatment options

In rare cases, severe orthodontic problems may require measures such as tooth extraction or oral surgery . However, most cases can be corrected by wearing braces. There are three choices, which are highly effective:

Invisalign – Clear, removable aligners.

Invisalign® is an orthodontic procedure that uses a series of removable transparent plastic aligner trays. It is a quick solution for improving bite alignment and correcting gaps, voids, and misaligned teeth.

Traditional – Metal brackets and wires.

Traditional braces are made out of regular metal brackets that are attached to your teeth and connected by wires. They must be tightened every 4-6 weeks so that the consistent pressure may eventually straighten your teeth and realign your jaw.

Candid clear aligner

Candid clear aligners are efficient, effective, and inexpensive. Candid is great for those who wish to obtain the straight smile of their dreams in as few consultations as possible.

Does orthodontic treatment hurt?

Feeling discomfort when wearing new aligners is normal, as gentle pressure moves your teeth into place. This soreness usually lasts only a few days and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relief and soft foods. Many patients describe the sensation as pressure rather than pain.
