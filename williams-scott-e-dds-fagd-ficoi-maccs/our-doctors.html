Dr. Andy Fuhriman & Staff

Dr. Andy Fuhriman grew up near the Rocky Mountains in Idaho. After graduating from high school in Sandpoint Idaho, Dr. Fuhriman served for two years as a missionary for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the South American country of Ecuador, where he learned to speak spanish.

After his mission, Dr. Fuhriman attended Boise State University. After graduating from Boise State, Dr. Fuhriman and his family moved to Ohio to attend the College of Dentistry at The Ohio State University. It was after graduating from Ohio State that Dr. Fuhriman and his family moved to Zanesville.

Dr. Fuhriman enjoys being outdoors, whether it is camping, backpacking, fishing, or anything else that involves being outdoors. Dr. Fuhriman and his wife Rachel have eight kids, who keep them very busy. Dr. Fuhriman spends a lot of time coaching local youth sports teams that his kids play on.

Dr. Fuhriman’s emphasis on patient comfort as well as keeping up-to-date with the latest advancements in dentistry enable him to provide patients with the best quality of care in the field. Dr. Andy Fuhriman has a wide range of experience in different areas of dentistry, from cosmetic and restorative dentistry to orthodontics.

His approach to your treatment starts with patiently listening to you. Then, after a complete and thorough examination, he reviews and discusses the diagnosis and treatment options with you.

I am proud to provide my patients with the best in dental technology, treatment options and patient comfort. We welcome you to our advanced dental office in Zanesville. It is my pleasure to address all your questions and concerns.

After graduating from Reynoldsburg High School in 1980, Dr. Williams went on to attend Ohio State University, Newark Campus. After his freshman year, Dr. Williams served a two-year mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Brisbane, Australia.

Once home, he attended Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Economics with a minor in Chemistry. It was then that he married his childhood sweetheart, Sarah, with whom he has had three wonderful children: Melissa, Nikki and Alex.

OSU College of Dentistry

Dr. Williams went on to attend the Ohio State University College of Dentistry, graduating in 1990 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. Throughout his studies at OSU, he earned numerous awards and honors. Dr. Williams was one of only two senior dental students placed on the training staff of the Ohio State University Athletic Department.

While on the training staff, his duties included caring for any trauma sustained during an athlete’s play, constructing custom mouth guards and performing oral surgery (mainly wisdom teeth removal). His treatment enabled athletes to perform in their chosen sports.

His experience serving as a team dentist and with an office in the Woody Hayes Practice Facility created a die-hard Buckeye fan. Dr. Williams will be glad to take a trip down memory lane with you, showing you his letter jacket and OSU flag which was presented to him by the OSU training staff for his 89-90 season of service.

Dr. Williams also earned a Certificate of Recognition from the American Dental Society of Anesthesiology for outstanding achievements in the field of dental anesthesiology.

The partnership formed by the joining of two progressive dental practices was begun in January 2010 when Drs. Williams and Fuhriman merged their individual practices. They feel this allows their patients a much wider range of services without having to go outside the expertise of these two practitioners.

Staying Up-to-date with the Latest Techniques

Dentistry is a dynamic field, constantly changing. Techniques and materials continuously evolve. Dr. Williams believes in keeping up with the latest. With a great deal of emphasis on continuing education, Dr. Williams serves both as a lecturer and as a student.

While he’s deeply dedicated to his practice, patients and staff, Dr. Williams feels most at home when surrounded by his family. His daughter, Melissa, graduated in April 2008 with a degree in dental hygiene from the Utah College of Dental Hygiene and returned to Zanesville to practice with her dad. She was married in June 2006 to Ben Heemeyer of Troutdale, Oregon who also graduated from Brigham Young University and is now a supervisor for Spectrum. They are the proud parents of Dr. Williams’ grandsons, Logan, Ian, Dallin and a grandaughter, Leah.

Nikki and her husband, Gerry, have been married since December of 2009. They both graduated from Brigham Young University and have moved to back to Zanesville, where her husband works as an assistant prosecutor for Muskingum County. Nikki uses her psychology degree as she works at the most challenging and valuable of all professions, that of motherhood. Gerry and Nikki are the parents of 4 beautiful girls named Alice and Colette (who are twins), and then Gwen and Gemma.

If you want to see pictures of any of Dr. Williams’ family, just ask! He is very proud of his kids and 11 grandkids.

Dr. Alex Williams grew up in Nashport, Ohio and graduated from Tri-Valley High School. After high school, he studied Exercise Science at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah. Between his freshman and sophomore years at BYU, Dr. Alex served a full-time mission for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints in San Pedro Sula, Honduras for two years speaking Spanish. He is still excited when he gets to practice his Spanish with patients!

While finishing his studies at BYU, he married his high school sweetheart Chelsea, who is from Westerville, Ohio. They now have two beautiful little girls, Adaline and Elise, and an active toddler boy names Charles. Dr. Alex is always happy to show you a picture or tell you a story about them.
