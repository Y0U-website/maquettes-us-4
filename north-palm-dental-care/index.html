Restorative Dentistry Restorative dentistry focuses on repairing and restoring damaged or missing teeth. We provide solutions such as dentures and partials for tooth replacement, dental bridges and crowns to restore function and aesthetics, and full mouth restorations for comprehensive rehabilitation. Our goal is to bring back the natural look and function of your teeth.

General dentistry forms the foundation of good oral health. Our services include regular dental exams and cleanings to prevent cavities and gum disease, as well as tooth extractions and emergency dentistry to address urgent dental issues. By maintaining regular visits, we can catch problems early and keep your smile healthy.

We understand that dental visits can be stressful for some patients. Our sedation dentistry options help you relax and have a more comfortable experience. Oral sedation and nitrous oxide sedation are available to reduce anxiety and make your treatment as pleasant as possible.

Our specialized services address more complex dental needs. We offer periodontics for the treatment of gum disease, endodontics for root canal therapy, laser dentistry for minimally invasive treatments, and CT scanner technology for precise diagnostics and planning. These services ensure comprehensive care for every aspect of your oral health.
