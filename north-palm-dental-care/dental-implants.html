Who Can Benefit from Dental Implants?

Dental implants are suitable for a wide range of patients, particularly those who:

  • ‍ Have One or More Missing Teeth : Whether you’re missing a single tooth or multiple teeth, implants can provide a permanent replacement. ‍
  • Seek an Alternative to Dentures : For those who find traditional dentures uncomfortable or inconvenient, implants offer a superior option. ‍
  • Require a Strong Bite : Implants restore full chewing ability, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. ‍
  • Want a Long-Lasting Solution : Unlike bridges or dentures that may need replacement over time, implants offer a more durable solution.

The Dental Implant Procedure

Our dental implant process is thorough and designed to ensure optimal results:

Initial Consultation

Dr. Chokshi will conduct a comprehensive examination of your oral health, including X-rays and possibly a CT scan, to assess the condition of your jawbone and determine the best implant strategy. Learn more about our CT scanner technology that enhances diagnostic precision.

Implant Placement

The implant placement involves surgically embedding the titanium post into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation, ensuring a comfortable experience. Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with the bone through a process called osseointegration.

Abutment Placement

Once the implant has integrated with the jawbone, an abutment is attached to the implant. This connector will hold the replacement tooth or teeth.

Custom Restoration

Finally, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the abutment. These prosthetics are designed to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, ensuring a natural appearance and comfortable fit.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Maintaining your dental implants is straightforward and similar to caring for natural teeth:

  • Good Oral Hygiene : Brush and floss daily to keep your implants and surrounding gums healthy.
  • Regular Dental Check-Ups : Schedule routine visits for professional cleanings and examinations. Our dental exams and cleanings help ensure the longevity of your implants.
  • Healthy Lifestyle Choices : Avoid smoking and excessive consumption of hard or sticky foods that could damage your implants.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants can last many years and may last a lifetime with good oral hygiene and regular dental care. The attached restoration may need replacement over time.

At What Age Should You Not Consider Dental Implants?

There is usually no upper age limit. Candidacy depends more on overall health, jawbone development, bone quality, and oral health.

Who Should Not Get Dental Implants?

Dental implants may not be suitable for patients with uncontrolled gum disease, severe bone loss, poor oral hygiene, or certain health conditions that affect healing.

Can People With Autoimmune Disease Get Dental Implants?

Some can. Eligibility depends on the condition, medications, overall health, and healing ability.

How Painful Is It to Get Dental Implants?

Most patients experience some soreness or swelling after treatment, but discomfort is usually manageable and varies by person.

How Long Does It Take to Put in a Dental Implant?

Placement time varies by case. The full process usually takes longer because the implant needs time to heal before the final restoration is completed.
