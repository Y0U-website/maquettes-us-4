Meet Dr. Brent Jarrett

Experience & Training

For over 20 years, Dr. Brent Jarrett has helped patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. After earning his dental degree from West Virginia University, he completed advanced training at the University of Florida in endodontics, oral surgery, and dental implants.

This training allows him to provide a wide range of dental care under one roof, from routine visits to more complex treatments.

Patient-Centered Care

From 2000 to 2024, Dr. Jarrett owned and operated his own practice in Margate, Florida. During that time, he built long-standing relationships with patients and earned a strong reputation for full-mouth restorations, crown and bridge work, and implant dentistry.

Patients appreciate his gentle chairside manner and clear communication. He takes the time to explain treatment options and help patients feel confident about their care.

Community & Personal Life

Dr. Jarrett is fluent in Spanish, helping make quality dental care more accessible to more members of the community.

Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with his twin son and daughter. He also enjoys long beach walks, fishing, and traveling to new places.

Meet Dr. Sarah McCourt

My Mission

Education

Dr. McCourt obtained her Bachelor of Science Degree in Biology and a Minor in Chemistry at Nova Southeastern University, where she worked as a math and science tutor, and held numerous leadership positions in various pre-professional and community service groups.

Dr. McCourt continued her education at Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine, where she obtained her Doctorate in Dental Medicine, centering her knowledge and skills in comprehensive treatment planning and advanced restorative and surgical procedures.

Continuing Education and Professional Memberships

Dr. McCourt is a firm believer in providing the highest standard of care to her patients, with the most up to date and technologically advanced treatments available. She does so through continuing education, and she is also a member of the American Dental Association.

Life and Hobbies

There is nothing Dr. McCourt enjoys more than being in the presence of family and friends, but above all things, she most loves being a mom, sharing in the joys of parenting with her husband, Keith. At the end of the day, all that we have is our family and our health.
