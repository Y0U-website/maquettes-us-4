Accessibility

  • Magnify your screen

Alternate Input Methods

If you need alternatives to traditional input methods such as a mouse and keyboard, there are some speech recognition applications that may help you navigate the web. Dragon Naturally Speaking is one such option. This software may assist with navigating a website with voice controls.
