  • You have an underbite, overbite, crossbite, or open bite.

Types of Orthodontics

Orthodontic treatment can be split up into fixed appliances and removable appliances. An example of the first would be traditional braces while an example of the second would be clear aligners. Both have their own approaches and advantages when it comes to straightening teeth.

Traditional Metal Braces

Braces use a combination of stainless-steel wires, brackets, and bands that slowly add pressure to teeth to move them into a specified direction. Modern braces are sleeker than they used to be, and they’re also more comfortable for patients to wear. Even though traditional metal braces are more noticeable than alternative treatments, they offer several advantages. For example, they are:

  • Capable of correcting even severe misalignment
  • More affordable compared to other alternatives
  • Customizable for a unique personal touch

Ceramic Braces

Patients who want the reliability of traditional metal braces with a more discreet look can opt for ceramic braces. These appliances work the same way as their conventional counterparts, but the brackets and wires are tooth-colored, so they blend in with your smile more seamlessly.

Invisalign

Aligner therapy is a treatment process involving a series of virtually invisible aligners, which look similar to a mouthguard or retainers. Each Invisalign® aligner is molded to fit your teeth specifically and designed to get you closer and closer to your end goal.

Invisalign offers a range of advantages for eligible patients. For example, clear aligners are:

  • Barely noticeable for an attractive smile throughout treatment
  • Completely removable for easy eating and brushing
  • Made from smooth plastic for increased comfort
