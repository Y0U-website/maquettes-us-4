The practice

Orthodontics encompasses a suite of treatments designed to correct the position of teeth. Many of us aren’t born with naturally perfect teeth, but orthodontic treatment can be used to fix alignment issues and achieve beautiful, straight smiles.

At Cinco Ranch Smiles & Braces, our dentists provide two main types of orthodontics: conventional braces and clear aligner therapy. If you are interested in straightening your smile, we invite you to schedule a consultation at our practice so we can help determine which one is best suited to your dental needs.

Orthodontics are a way for adults and teens to achieve smiles that are not only more attractive, but also healthier too. Straighter teeth can help boost your self-confidence and are easier to keep clean, since there are fewer places for bacteria to accumulate.
