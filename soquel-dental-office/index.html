The practice

Welcome to Soquel Dental Office and the practice of Dr. Justin Shyba. Our team is happy to continue in the footsteps of Dr. Steve Mann as we move into the future of Dentistry.

Our state-of-the-art dental office is equipped with the latest technology to improve both comfort and results. At our Soquel practice, you can receive all-ceramic quality crowns and bridges in one day as well as inlays, onlays, and porcelain veneers.

At Soquel Dental Office, we understand the link between ideal oral health, a strong body, and a confident self-image. Dr. Shyba finds great reward in helping his patients achieve a beautiful smile and ensures that their teeth and gums are in good condition before performing any cosmetic procedures.

Because a clear link has been established between oral health and systemic illness, Dr. Shyba educates patients of all ages on the proper way to preserve healthy and appealing smiles. He conducts thorough exams and listens carefully to your concerns and goals.

Services

Preventive, cosmetic, restorative, and Invisalign care all under one roof. Now welcoming new patients.

When you have missing teeth, an alternative solution to dentures is dental implants. While dentures are removable, dental implants provide permanent new teeth to restore your mouth’s function and appearance.

Invisalign® is a modern alternative to straighten your teeth without wires or metal. Invisalign® involves a series of custom trays designed specifically for you and the plan your dentist or orthodontist creates to move your teeth.
