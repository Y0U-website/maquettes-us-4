Why Dental Studio

Improved Oral Health:

At Dental Studio, our braces help straighten your teeth, making them easier to clean and reducing the risk of cavities, gum disease, and other dental issues.

Enhanced Aesthetic Appeal

Braces at Dental Studio can significantly improve the appearance of your smile, boosting your confidence and self-esteem.

Better Bite and Jaw Alignment

Dental Studio’s braces correct bite issues such as overbite, underbite, and crossbite, leading to better jaw alignment and reducing strain on the jaw muscles and joints.

Improved Speech

Misaligned teeth can cause speech difficulties. Braces from Dental Studio help align your teeth properly, leading to clearer and more effective speech.

Prevention of Dental Problems

Correcting alignment and bite issues with braces from Dental Studio can prevent future dental problems, such as excessive wear on certain teeth, jaw pain, and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders.

Long-Term Cost Savings

Investing in braces at Dental Studio can save you money in the long run by preventing more serious dental issues that may require costly treatments or surgeries later in life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We got answers, browse through some of the most asked questions. Not seeing your question? Call us, our friendly staff is here to help.

At Dental Studio, the duration of your orthodontic treatment with braces typically ranges from 18 to 36 months, depending on the complexity of your orthodontic issues and your body’s response to treatment. During your initial consultation, our expert orthodontists will provide a personalized treatment plan outlining the estimated duration based on your specific needs​.

While you may experience some initial discomfort or soreness, braces are generally not painful. Any discomfort is usually mild and temporary as your mouth adjusts to the braces. At Dental Studio, we provide guidance on managing any discomfort, including recommending over-the-counter pain relievers and orthodontic wax to ensure a smooth and comfortable experience​.

At Dental Studio, we schedule adjustments every 4 to 6 weeks to ensure your teeth are moving according to the treatment plan. During these appointments, our orthodontists will make necessary adjustments to the wires and brackets to continue guiding your teeth into alignment. Regular visits are crucial for monitoring progress and making any necessary modifications to your treatment plan​.

While some dietary adjustments are necessary to protect your braces and prevent damage, you can still enjoy most of your favorite foods during your orthodontic treatment. It’s essential to avoid sticky, hard, or chewy foods that can damage your braces or bend wires. Dental Studio provides detailed dietary guidelines and recommendations to help you navigate your orthodontic journey without sacrificing your favorite treats.

Yes, wearing a retainer after braces is crucial to maintain the results of your orthodontic treatment and prevent your teeth from shifting back to their original position. At Dental Studio, we design custom retainers for you to wear after braces and provide instructions on how often to wear them and how to care for them properly. Consistent retainer wear is essential for preserving your newly aligned smile for years to come​.
