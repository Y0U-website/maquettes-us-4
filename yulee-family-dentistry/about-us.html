Meet Our Doctors Dr. Jonathan Gordon Dr. Cole Washington ✕ Dr. Jonathan Gordon A Florida native, Dr. Gordon earned his Bachelor of Science in Exercise Physiology from the University of Florida before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at the University of Florida College of Dentistry in 2008. He has called the Yulee and Fernandina Beach community home since 2013.

Dedicated to growth and innovation, Dr. Gordon is CEREC and Invisalign certified and stays at the forefront of dentistry through continuing education. He is an active member of the American Dental Association, Academy of General Dentistry, Florida Dental Association, American Academy of Implant Dentistry, American Academy of Facial Esthetics, and the Journal of Implantology. Beyond the office, he volunteers with the Baptist Dental Bus to serve those in need.

For Dr. Gordon, dentistry is the perfect blend of art and science. He’s passionate about using his skills to transform smiles and believes improving someone’s oral health can truly change their life. His philosophy is simple: treat every patient with the respect, care, and attention they deserve.

Dr. Gordon stays busy enjoying the outdoors, spending time with his wife, three sons, and of course their two dogs. He also likes to stay fit and travel. Dr. Gordon enjoys competing in obstacle course races all over the U.S. throughout the year, along with visiting national parks for hiking.

Dr. Cole Washington

Dr. Cole Washington provides comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages, with a focus on comfort, precision, and long-term health. He earned his bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Central Florida before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry. Following dental school, he expanded his expertise through a General Practice Residency at Newark Beth Israel Medical Center, where he trained extensively across multiple specialties.

Dr. Washington’s advanced training allows him to serve patients in preventive and restorative dentistry, oral surgery, and endodontics. He is also certified in IV sedation, offering a safe and supportive option for patients with dental anxiety. His goal is always the same: to help patients feel at ease while achieving their healthiest, most confident smile.

Born and raised in Jacksonville, FL, Dr. Washington comes from a family deeply rooted in community service through the Jacksonville Fire and Rescue Department. He met his wife, Beth, during dental school, and together they enjoy life with their golden doodle, Bee, who loves swimming and boat rides. In January 2024, they welcomed their first son, Benjamin. Outside the office, Dr. Washington enjoys spending time outdoors, hunting, and fishing.

Now back in North Florida, Dr. Washington is proud to care for patients in the community he calls home and is currently welcoming new patients.
