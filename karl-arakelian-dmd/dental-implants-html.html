What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small cylindrical post, usually made out of titanium, that we surgically place underneath the gumline and directly in the jaw. Over the next several months, the implant forms a strong bond with the bone surrounding it. As a result, it can perform all of the functions that natural tooth roots do and secure a custom dental crown, bridge, or denture into place depending on the number of teeth a patient is missing. Better yet, dental implants are capable of lasting for 30+ years, which is more than can be said for traditional tooth replacements like dentures and bridges.

Initial Dental Implant Consultation

It’s important to make sure we fully understand your treatment expectations and needs. During your consultation, our implant dentist will ask you questions about your tooth loss and what you’re hoping to get out of your dental implants. Then, we’ll perform an oral exam, review our findings with you, and help you build the perfect treatment plan and schedule.

Dental Implant Surgery

For dental implant surgery, we’ll numb your mouth with an anesthetic and make sure you’re comfortable in the patient’s chair. Your implant dentist will make a small incision in your gumline to access your jawbone, carefully place the biocompatible implant posts, and suture your gums closed. We’ll also provide you with strict recovery instructions, so please don’t hesitate to voice any questions or concerns.

Dental Implant Osseointegration & Abutment Placement

It’s important not to disturb your implant site during recovery (which could take between three and six months). This is because the biocompatible posts and your jawbone will begin osseointegration a natural process where compatible materials fuse together. Rest assured, this is quite safe, and is a big reason why dental implants are so strong and durable.

Once osseointegration is complete, we’ll have you return to the office so your implant dentist can fix the abutment to your surgically placed posts. The abutment is basically a small connector piece that will allow us to attach your final restoration later.

Delivery of Dental Implant Restorations

Dental implant restorations are highly customized. It will likely take some time for your crown, bridge, or denture to be ready for placement. But as soon as it is, we’ll get you scheduled for the fourth and final step: delivering your restoration and completing your smile. It should only take a few minutes, and after your implant dentist makes any necessary adjustments, you’ll be free to enjoy your dental implants in Haverhill.

Benefits of Dental Implants

It’s not hard to see why dental implants continue to be the preferred tooth replacement solution among dentists and patients alike. With millions of people living with these prosthetics and experiencing what others cannot provide, it’s no surprise that more individuals are beginning to understand why they’re worth the financial investment. Whether you’re still on the fence or you’re just starting your research, here are several benefits of dental implants in Haverhill you simply cannot overlook.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • No Food Restrictions : Dental implants are known to work just like natural teeth, so you are free to eat the foods you love. While you should be cautious about biting down on hard, crunchy items for fear of cracking or chipping a tooth, enjoying your favorite meals is no longer an issue.
  • Teeth Stay in Place : Instead of worrying about whether your teeth will shift or slip while eating or speaking, you can do both with greater peace of mind. The reason is that dental implants in Haverhill are permanently embedded into the jawbone, so they will not move, unlike traditional dentures.
  • Greater Confidence : Being able to wake up each morning knowing that your smile is permanently set in place can increase your confidence in a new and exciting way. Although it requires daily maintenance and care on your part, your teeth will continue to look and feel their best, which will positively impact various areas of your life.

Health Benefits

  • Jawbone Preservation : Jawbone deterioration is a real concern following the loss of natural teeth because the root, which provided vital stimulation, is gone. Dental implants successfully prevent this gradual process by creating a new, seamlessly lifelike root in its place that offers essential stability and support.
  • Natural Appearance : Once fully restored, implant-supported teeth are virtually indistinguishable from natural ones when it comes to aesthetics, overall feel, and function. After some time has passed, a patient may not even be able to successfully point it out in the mirror.
  • Maintain Natural Teeth : Dental implants don’t require the remaining natural dental structure to be significantly reduced in any way for accommodation, which is a much better outcome for the patient’s overall oral health and well-being.

Long-Term Benefits

  • Longer-Lasting Smile: The key to maintaining longer-lasting dental implants is maintenance. As long as you take proper care of them, you should never need to worry about having to replace your titanium posts. While the restoration on top will likely need to be swapped out with a new one over time, your implants can remain where they are for a lifetime.
  • Cost - Effectiveness: Other tooth replacement methods require the use of specialized products as well as investing in replacements. This cost can increase over the years. However, dental implants do not. Not only do they not require denture adhesives or specific brushes for more thorough cleaning, but they don’t have to be replaced as long as you make sure they stay healthy and damage-free.
  • High Success Rate : Dental implants have an average success rate of over 98 %, and their longevity is truly unmatched. In fact, many patients can enjoy them for the rest of their lives without issue.

Missing Multiple Teeth

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

Estimating the cost of dental implants is complicated because the procedure itself has several components to it. Until then, here are some things to keep in mind.

Preliminary Treatments & Dental Implant Surgery

Any preliminary treatments that you require before getting implants, like gum disease therapy, tooth extractions, or bone grafting, will contribute to the overall cost. For many patients, these treatments are necessary to ensure that the dental implants are successful. Sometimes, preliminary treatments are covered by dental insurance, so this is something you should check with your insurance provider. The Parts of Your Dental Implant

There are a few main factors that will affect the cost of your dental implants:

  • Number of dental implants : Depending on the number of teeth you are needing to have replaced and where they are in the mouth, you will need more or less implants. The more that you need, the higher the overall cost will be.
  • Type of restoration : Crowns, bridges, and dentures all have different costs. Small restorations are generally more affordable.
  • Size or material : Implants can be made out of zirconia or titanium. These materials are at different price points.
  • Brand of dental implant: Dental implants come from numerous different manufacturers. This can influence their cost.

How Dental Implants Can Save You Money

Dental Implants Post-Op Instructions

Once your dental implant surgery is over, the recovery process comes next. However, there is no need to worry! What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

The best step you can take after your surgery is to leave the forming blood clot untouched to promote healing. In order to do this, make sure you’re following these simple rules:

If you ever have any questions, call our implant dentist in Haverhill! We are happy to help clarify any information and guide you through this process.

  • Do not spit. Use tissues or swallow saliva.
  • Do not use drinking straws.
  • Do not smoke for at least the first day.
  • Do your best to keep fingers/tongue away from the surgical site(s).

Common Side Effects

Especially during the first few days after your surgery, some minor side effects are completely normal. You may notice symptoms like:

All of these symptoms are typical and should gradually subside over time. If you notice any of these side effects getting worse, contact us right away for possible treatment.

  • Intermittent bleeding: This can be lessened with gauze and light pressure. Expect some bleeding for multiple days.
  • Swelling: This can last for 72 hours up to over a week. You can manage swelling by using a cold compress or ice pack.
  • Discomfort: Some general discomfort is normal, which can be managed with the pain medication provided to you.

Diet

In the first few days after your implant surgery, we recommend eating soft and non-abrasive foods to prevent damage. Some ideas include yogurt, ice cream, pudding, soup (not extremely hot), scrambled eggs, mashed potatoes, and pasta. You can return to your regular diet as soon as you feel comfortable, however do your best to avoid chewing directly on the implant site.

Health & Oral Hygiene

You can start brushing your teeth the day after your implant procedure but be careful around the surgical site. Rinse your mouth 2 to 3 times a day with salt water. If you have it, you can use prescription mouth rinse. Avoid mouthwashes that have alcohol (like Listerine or Scope) because they dry out the mouth and slow down healing.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

Once you get your restoration, the hard part is over! All that’s left to do is smile with a full set of teeth thanks to your implant supported crown, bridge, or denture. You may feel temporary gum sensitivity, however there will be no swelling, bleeding, or extensive recovery. You can control sensitivity with pain medication and you’ll be smiling on the same day.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Rebuilding your smile with dental implants requires an investment of both your valuable time and money. So, it’s only natural for you to want them to last. Fortunately, they are known for having a long lifespan (30+ years), and you can make yours last with the following habits:

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Although your dental implants cannot decay because they are artificial, the gums supporting them can develop an infection. So, it’s important that you prioritize healthy habits at home. That starts with the basics, like brushing your teeth twice a day, and extends to wearing a nightguard if you have Bruxism, cleaning your tongue, and replacing your toothbrush (or toothbrush head) every 3-4 months.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Like your natural teeth, your dental crown, bridge, or denture can break with enough force. So, it’s important that you steer clear of hard pretzels, croutons, and other crunchy foods. Instead, focus on filling your plate with foods that benefit your oral health and are easier to chew, like scrambled eggs, oatmeal, cooked carrots, and cottage cheese.

Break Bad Habits

Oftentimes, patients immediately think of smoking when they hear bad dental habit. While that’s certainly a noteworthy one, there are others. In an effort to prolong the lifespan of your dental implants, we also advise against biting your nails, using your teeth as tools to open things, and chewing on ice cubes.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Do you like to stay active by taking exercise classes with friends? Maybe you prefer a solo activity, like riding your bike, lifting weights, or jogging. Whatever the case may be, it’s important that you wear a mouthguard so your teeth, your gums, and your dental implants are protected from unforeseen impact.

Tip: If you don’t have a mouthguard already, let us know! That way, we can take impressions of your mouth and have a custom one made for you.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Remember, even if you don’t have any of your natural teeth left, you need to schedule an appointment with our team at Associates in Dental Health of Haverhill every six months. That way, we can examine the condition of your dental implants, remove built-up plaque and tartar, and catch common oral health problems, like gum disease, in the early stages.

Important reminder: If you notice anything unusual, like pain stemming from the implant site, call us right away. The sooner you do, the higher our chances are of preventing dental implant failure.

Dental Implant Technology

Dental techniques and technology become more advanced every year. Today, we use a wide range of techniques to make dental implant placement go as smoothly and successfully as possible. Read on to learn about some of the advanced pieces of technology we use here at Associates in Dental Health of Haverhill.

CBCT/Cone Beam CT Scanner

Dental X-rays are used to help dental professionals see the structures of the mouth that aren’t visible to the naked eye. This includes the roots of the teeth and jawbone. However, dental X-rays aren’t able to detect everything that we need, so we use a cone beam scanner. This piece of technology shows us important details that aren’t visible with digital X-rays. This includes the nerve pathways, blood vessels, and the thickness of the jawbone. With our CT scanner, we can plan exactly where the dental implants should be placed for the highest probability of success.

Digital Impression System

If you have a dental crown or have received orthodontic treatment in the past, you are likely to remember the gooey putty that you had to bite down on to make impressions. The good news is that this is a practice of the past. Today, we use digital impressions. They are easier to take and produce highly accurate and reliable images. This simplifies the process of providing patients with high-quality, perfectly fitted restorations.
