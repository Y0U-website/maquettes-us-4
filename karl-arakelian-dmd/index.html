The practice

I have been fortunate for 33 years to be part of a second-generation dental practice that my dad started in 1960. He was a one man show doing fillings and billings, all with no gloves or masks. Some of you remember me when I was just a twinkle in my mom’s eye. Back then both my family and my dad’s practice fit in the same house. Eventually the practice grew, and my family moved out.

Today, 64 years later, we have grown our practice to the point where we have outgrown our space. This had led to reduced flexibility with scheduling, and patients having to wait for appointments longer than we’d like. We still love doing dentistry, treating old and new patients alike.
