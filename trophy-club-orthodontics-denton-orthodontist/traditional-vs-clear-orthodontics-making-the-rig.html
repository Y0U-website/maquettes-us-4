  • Visible Appearance : Traditional braces are made of metal brackets and wires, making them noticeable when you smile. Some patients embrace this as a fashion statement, while others may feel self-conscious about the appearance.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners, like Invisalign , have gained popularity in recent years for their discreet and convenient nature. Here’s what you should know about clear aligners:

  • Invisibility : Clear aligners are virtually invisible when worn, making them an attractive option for those who want to keep their treatment discreet.
  • Comfort : Clear aligners are made of smooth, comfortable plastic, reducing the risk of irritation to the cheeks and gums compared to metal braces.
  • Removability : Clear aligners are removable, allowing you to eat, drink, brush, and floss without any restrictions. This makes them a convenient choice for many patients.
  • Treatment Duration : In some cases, treatment with clear aligners may take slightly longer than traditional braces, depending on the complexity of your orthodontic issues.
