Welcome to Cherry Street Dental, where we proudly continue the trusted legacy of Dr. Straley and his father in serving the Flushing, Michigan community. Located in the heart of downtown Flushing, we provide compassionate, comprehensive dental care for patients throughout Genesee County, including Grand Blanc, Swartz Creek, Clio, and Mount Morris

Dr. Joseph Thayil pours his heart into every aspect of his dentistry practice. Originally from Florida, he is known for his unwavering dedication to putting patients first and striving for excellence in everything he does. Graduating with distinction from Nova Southeastern University in 2008 marked the beginning of a career centered on patient care, innovation, and clinical proficiency.

With more than 16 years of experience in general dentistry, Dr. Thayil has become a trusted figure in the field. He is committed to staying at the forefront of dental advancements, ensuring that every patient receives personalized and cutting-edge care. From IV dental sedation to the smallest details of treatment, he approaches every procedure with compassion and meticulous attention.

After relocating to Michigan in 2019 to prioritize family, Dr. Thayil’s dedication to providing exceptional dental care has remained unwavering. He is deeply committed to serving his community with integrity and professionalism, standing as a beacon of excellence in the dental profession.

Driven by a lifelong passion for dentistry and a relentless pursuit of knowledge, Dr. Thayil is more than a dentist—he is a trusted partner in each patient’s journey toward optimal oral health and overall wellness. He believes every smile matters and is honored to be an essential part of his patients’ dental care team.
