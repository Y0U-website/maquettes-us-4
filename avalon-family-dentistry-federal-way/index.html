The practice

In addition to providing a host of general dental services using the latest technology and techniques, our staff offers tender pediatric care to our youngest patients and restorative care for adults and senior citizens. At our Federal Way, WA, dental office, you will receive personalized treatment plans that provide you with the greatest level of care and compassion possible.

Beyond immediate relief for tooth pain and dental problems, we strive to help each patient develop an oral hygiene plan that keeps their smiles healthy and happy for years to come. Every patient matters to us, and we’ll always go above and beyond to make sure you feel heard and truly cared for.

Dr. Hannah N. Doan was born, raised, and educated in Vietnam, where she practiced dentistry for several years. She harbored a strong dream to travel overseas and become an international dentist. This pursuit required her to study English and re-study to requalify her dental credentials in the United States. After successfully completing the University of Colorado International Dentist Program…

Her own heritage and unique experiences living and learning abroad have given her an appreciation and understanding of cultural diversity that she uses to provide personalized care to every patient.

General procedures include comprehensive dental exams for babies, children, and adults, regular cleanings, fillings, and periodontal care. Dr. Doan also performs cosmetic and restorative procedures, such as professional-grade teeth whitening, Invisalign, veneers, crowns, bridges, and dental implants.

We see patients with dental insurance, as well as without dental insurance. We work with CareCredit to offer the most flexible payment plans possible.
