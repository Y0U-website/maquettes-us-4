About Us

Hannah Doan, DDS I grew up on my mom’s dental chair—napping, playing with denture teeth, and shaping wax. Dentistry found me in the most natural way possible. My mom was a dentist in our home country, Vietnam, and during my years of dental school there, I was lucky to work alongside her in the evenings as her assistant.

Before I could graduate, my family immigrated to the U.S. I still remember walking across the stage to receive my diploma and silently promising myself that one day, I’d earn another degree—this time in the U.S., with my family there to celebrate. That dream came true in December 2018, when I graduated (again!) from the University of Colorado School of Dental Medicine .

My mom continues to inspire how I practice today. From her, I learned to truly listen—to understand each patient’s unique story, lifestyle, and priorities. I believe dentistry should never be one-size-fits-all. Every smile deserves care that’s personal, thoughtful, and centered around what matters most to each individual.

When I’m not in the office, you can usually find me outdoors with my family—hiking under golden larches in North Cascades National Park or taking peaceful walks among the trees. Some of my favorite memories are from exploring the Maple Pass Trail Loop, standing beneath the Delicate Arch in Arches National Park, watching the Milky Way light up the sky at Bryce Canyon, and driving the breathtaking route from Banff to Jasper.

I also love creating things with my hands and my imagination—from DIY projects to digital art. I’ve designed Avalon’s T-shirts, water bottles, and tote bags for our team and patients, and lately, I’ve been learning to film and edit videos with my Insta360 camera to capture those little moments and memories from our adventures.

Josephine Lee, DDS As a Federal Way dentist, Dr. Josephine Lee enjoys caring for the families in her area and has been in private practice as a family, restorative, and cosmetic dentist since 1997. She continues to educate herself and hone her skills by regularly attending lectures and seminars.

Dr. Lee is known for her gentle and kind manner. Her deep feelings of commitment and compassion for her patients are evident in the way she treats them like members of her own family.

She tries to model herself on her heroes: Mother Theresa and her mother. Mother Theresa because of her sacrificial service to others and her mom for her sacrificial love for her family.
