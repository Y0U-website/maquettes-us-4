Why Choose a Pediatric Dentist?

Pediatric dentists receive an extra 2 years of specialized training to work with children. They have the necessary tools and resources to combat the challenges that come along with treating kids. This includes behavior management techniques, a child-friendly office space, and staff geared toward child care.

Our Dental Services:

The first dental visit should be before your child’s first birthday. This not only gives pediatric dentists a chance to examine and evaluate your child’s oral health, but it gives us an opportunity to provide education to prevent dental problems before they arise.

Preventive dentistry is on the forefront of modern dentistry. We utilize a focus on trying to prevent dental problems from arising to begin. Preventive dentistry includes oral hygiene education, dietary education, prophylactic cleaning and fluoride application. Preventive dentistry should be specialized and unique to each individual dependent on their risk of cavities or gum disease.

When treating dental decay, often treatment can be difficult for a child that is unable to cooperate in the chair. Fluoride goes a long way in strengthening the tooth to slow the progression of cavities. Silver diamine fluoride (SDF) is a medication that can stop the progression of cavities by simply painting the medicine on the tooth. More advanced techniques such as Atraumatic Restorative Treatment (ART), SDF-Modified Atraumatic Restorative Treatment (SMART), or Hall Crowns can prove to be very useful in reducing the need for a sedation while providing restorative dental treatment.

Fillings utilize a white composite resin material to replace missing tooth structure destroyed by dental decay. There are limitations with white fillings as they need to mechanically stick to the tooth itself, which requires enough healthy tooth structure to present. In smaller baby teeth, often cavities progress very quickly, making it difficult for white fillings to have enough healthy tooth structure to hold onto.

Stainless steel crowns (SSC) completely cover the entire tooth, restoring the entire tooth’s shape and function. SSCs are composed of iron, chromium, and nickel, and are glued onto a tooth using a resin-based cement. The silver metal of these crowns are ideal for baby teeth, because we are able to bend and shape the metal to fit perfectly for each individual tooth.

A zirconia crown is a pre-fabricated white crown that can be used instead of the silver stainless steel crown (SSC). The benefits of these crowns are the improved esthetics and ideal compatibility with the surrounding gum tissues. The limitations with placing these crowns are they are much bulkier than an SSC, and can not be placed in a child with too much crowding.

A dental pulpotomy is a nerve treatment used to fix teeth where cavities have now entered the nerve of the tooth. A pulpotomy is not as simple as explaining it as a “baby root canal”. A root canal is when the entire nerve root is infected and entirely cleaned out. As baby teeth tend to have very long and slender roots, it is almost impossible to clean the entire root. A pulpotomy will remove as much of the “sick” portion of the nerve as possible, then place a medication to help the “healthy” portion of the nerve heal.

When a cavity progresses through the entirety of a tooth, the bacteria causing the cavity will then form an infection under the tooth and into the bone. When there is an infection present, the only option in baby teeth is to extract a tooth. Extractions include the complete removal of a tooth from the bone, allowing the source of the infection to be removed so the body can heal on its own.
