About us

Fishtown Pediatric Dentistry focuses on the treatment of its community.

Utilizing evidence-based dentistry, we create an individualized plan for every child that comes to our office. We strive to create accessible care for every family that steps through our doors.

Our goal is to return to an idea of being a part of the community we are treating. We are local providers giving back to our community. We shop at the same stores, eat at the same restaurants, and our kids go to the same schools. This helps us identify with our patients and create a unique experience that is personalized for your child’s care.

D r . A l e c R o b i n

Continuing his studies, Dr. Robin received his residency training as the chief resident at both the Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia and the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Robin emphasized his training by working with children with medical complexities and children with disabilities.

Upon finishing training, Dr. Robin has been working as a Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist in the Philadelphia area. Dr. Robin’s main areas of expertise include preventive dentistry, minimally invasive dentistry, treatment of children with dental anxiety, treatment of medically complex children, and treatment of patients with disabilities. Dr. Robin also gives pediatric dental lectures to the pre-doctoral dental students at UPenn, as well as the pediatrician residents and fellows at CHOP.

Outside of dentistry Dr. Robin loves trying new restaurants around Philadelphia. In addition, Dr. Robin is involved with a lot of community theater around the Philadelphia area. Hobbies include rock climbing, pickleball, volleyball and improv. Dr. Robin currently lives in Fishtown with his phenomenal wife, Amanda, and their adorable cat, Aggie.

We always recommend taking public transportation to get to us. We are easily accessible by the Market-Frankford Line at the Girard stop. We are also near multiple bus and trolley stops including the G Route 15 trolley at the Girard & Frankford stop, Route 5 at the Girard & Frankford stop, and Route 25 at the Girard & Frankford stop.

Street parking is available along Frankford avenue and in the neighboring side streets. We recommend closely reading parking signs as there are often time limitations. There is also a parking structure associated with the Rivers Casino that is available.
