Sleep Apnea & Oral Appliance Therapy

Oral sleep appliance therapy is an effective treatment option for both obstructive sleep apnea and snoring. These devices are worn during sleep and are custom fit to your teeth and jaw. By positioning the jaw in a forward position, the device helps to maintain an open air way while you sleep. Research has show this is an effective treatment alternative to the traditional CPAP machine for obstructive sleep apnea and most patients prefer an oral device to a CPAP machine due to improved comfort and lack of a noisy machine.

Advantages of an oral sleep apnea appliance vs a CPAP machine: •Improved comfort •Portable - easy to travel with •Quiet •Easier to clean and take care of

If you are unsure if you have sleep apnea, it is recommended you have a sleep study performed and reviewed by a qualified physician. If you have been diagnosed with mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea and are interested in learning more about an oral sleep appliance, give our office a call for a consultation.
