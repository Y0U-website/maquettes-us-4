Dr. Smits

Kyle A. Smits DDS

Dr. Kyle A. Smits is committed to providing quality, professional dental care to the West Seattle neighborhood. After attending undergraduate, graduate and dental school on the east coast, Dr. Kyle returned to the Northwest in 2010 to work with his father, Dr. Paul K. Smits DDS, who served as a dentist in this exact location for over forty years.

Dr. Kyle graduated from the University of Maryland Baltimore College of Dental Surgery where he was a member of several honor societies and received the International Congress of Oral Implantologists’ award for implant dentistry as a pre-doctoral student.

Prior to attending dental school, he received his Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees from Georgetown University in Washington, DC where he was also a member of Georgetown’s NCAA Division I Cross Country and Track & Field teams. Dr. Smits lives in Seattle Washington with his wife and two children and in his spare time enjoys running, skiing, and all things outdoors.

Dr. Smits is a member of the following organizations: Seattle-King County Dental Society Washington State Dental Association American Dental Association Academy of General Dentistry Premier Dental Study Club West Seattle Endodontics Study Club
