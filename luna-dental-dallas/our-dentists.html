Meet Our Dentists at Luna Dental and Orthodontics

At Luna Dental and Orthodontics, we are proud to introduce our team of highly skilled and compassionate dentists. Each member of our dental team brings a wealth of experience and a passion for dental health to our practice in Dallas, TX. Our dentists are not only experts in their fields, ranging from general dentistry to specialized areas such as orthodontics, pediatric dentistry, and oral surgery, but they also share a commitment to providing personalized, patient-focused care.

Our practice is built on the foundation of trust, expertise, and unwavering dedication to patient satisfaction. We believe in treating each patient as a unique individual, tailoring our treatments to meet your specific needs and goals. Whether you’re coming in for a routine check-up, a cosmetic procedure, or more complex dental work, our team is here to ensure you receive the highest standard of care in a warm and welcoming environment.

Dr. Antonio Rivera

Dr. Antonio Rivera grew up in small towns in North Fort Worth and graduated from Northwest High School in just three years. He proudly obtained his Bachelor of Science in Biology from The University of Texas at Austin in 2003, "Hook’em Horns!" Subsequently, Dr. Rivera pursued his dental education at Baylor College of Dentistry, now Texas A&M College of Dentistry, where he graduated in 2007 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree.

Following the completion of his studies, Dr. Rivera commenced his professional journey in the Dallas area, initially serving as an associate dentist. In 2010, he took a courageous step forward by founding Luna Dental. Over the past decade, Luna Dental has flourished into a cherished dental home for thousands in the neighboring community, propelled by Dr. Rivera’s genuine care and respect for his patients.

Dr. Rivera’s visionary leadership has molded Luna Dental into a practice that instills pride among its staff and continues to serve the community with unwavering gratitude and dedication. Luna has emerged as one of the flagship brands of the Lucent Dental Group.

Dr. Steve Garcia

Dr. Steve Garcia, a 2017 graduate of UNLV Dental School in Las Vegas, has been making waves in the dental profession since the beginning of his career. Working diligently in South and West Texas, Steve has earned a stellar reputation as a skilled practitioner.

Proficient in root canals and adept at pediatric dentistry, Steve continuously updates his skills, staying abreast of the latest advancements in the field. His commitment to excellence is reflected in his ability to provide top-notch care to a diverse patient base, enhanced by his fluency in both English and Spanish.

Beyond the dental clinic, Steve is a passionate traveler, exploring the world and embracing new cultures. An avid skier, he finds joy in the winter slopes. While his professional life thrives, Steve values his downtime spent reconnecting with his Las Vegas roots and family.

Dr. Steven D Peters DDS

Oral Surgeon

Dr. Steven D Peters was born and raised in the DFW area. He later attended HPHS in Dallas, Texas. Dr. Peters attended Baylor University in Waco, Texas. While attending Baylor, he walked - on the Baylor track team as a pole vaulter. He was a member of the Phi Eta Sigma National Honor Society. He also pledged Phi Delta Theta fraternity. He graduated from Baylor University in 1982 with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology.

In 1982, Dr. Peters attended The University of Texas Health Science Center Dental Branch in Houston, Texas. Later in that year, Dr. Peters made the Dean’s Academic Honor List. Dr. Peters graduated in 1986 with a Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center Dental Branch in Houston.

He then attended The University of Texas Health Science Center at Dallas, Parkland Memorial Hospital , Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Residency Training Program from 1986 through graduation in 1990.

Dr. Peters worked in the Metroplex for a few years before establishing a private practice in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery located in Paris, Texas. After practicing in Paris, Texas for 24 years, Dr. Peters moved back to the DFW Metroplex to be closer to family and has served the DFW area as an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon. Dr. Peters is a member of The American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons and The American Dental Society of Anesthesiology.

Outside of his professional life , Dr. Peters loves seeing his family, friends, colleagues and enjoys worshiping The Lord with his church family and supports local missions in the DFW area. He also enjoys hunting, fishing, walking, working out, playing guitar, and watching all sports and The Jimmy Houston Catch a Better Life Channel, on YouTube. He has been attending Bible Study Fellowship over the past five years and highly recommends it to anyone.
