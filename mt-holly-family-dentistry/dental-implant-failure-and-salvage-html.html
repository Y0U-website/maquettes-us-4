How Dental Implant Salvage Works

If you suspect any issues with your dental implants, give us a call right away. We’ll conduct a thorough examination, discuss your symptoms, and outline potential treatments. In some cases, we can rescue implants through a comprehensive cleaning, antibiotics, and conservative infection control. Seeking swift intervention boosts the possibility of restoring your smile’s health affordably and seamlessly.

For more extensive damage, we may need to remove the implant. After we restore your oral health (i.e., through procedures like bone grafts and gum disease treatment), you might be able to receive a new dental implant.
