The practice

What makes us different is that we exclusively serve patients who are insured through Minnesota Health Care Plans (MHCP) or are uninsured. We want to be your dental home and a place where you can participate in regular preventative and routine dental care.

Call us directly to speak with a member of our service team or insurance expert.
