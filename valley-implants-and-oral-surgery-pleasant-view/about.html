Board-Certified Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon | Owner & Operator

Dr. Travis Sorensen is a board certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon with over 11 years of experience helping patients feel comfortable and confident with their care. He is the owner of Valley Implants and Oral Surgery in Bountiful and is excited to announce the opening of a second location in Pleasant View in November 2025.

Dr. Sorensen specializes in dental implants, All-on-4’s, wisdom teeth removal, IV sedation, bone grafting, biopsies/pathology and orthodontic exposure of impacted teeth. Patients know him for his gentle approach, advanced techniques and dedication to making every visit as stress-free as possible.

A graduate of BYU and dental school at the University of Oklahoma, Dr. Sorensen completed his surgical residency at the University of Mississippi. He and his family live in Plain City, where they enjoy skiing, camping, fishing, music and life on their small hobby farm with their miniature highland cow.
