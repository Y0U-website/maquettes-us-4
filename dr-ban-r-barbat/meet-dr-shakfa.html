Dental Implants and Surgical Care

Dental implant treatment requires careful evaluation of the bone, surrounding teeth, bite, and long-term restorative plan. Dr. Shakfa focuses on the surgical phase of implant care, including evaluation, grafting when needed, and implant placement.

Because Dr. Barbat and Dr. Shakfa work within the same office, patients benefit from coordinated planning between the surgical and restorative phases of treatment. This is especially helpful when an implant is part of a larger plan to restore function, comfort, or appearance.

IV Sedation and Patient Comfort

Many patients delay dental care because of anxiety, past dental experiences, or fear of surgery. Dr. Shakfa provides IV sedation for appropriate patients so longer or more involved procedures can often be completed with greater comfort.

Before sedation is recommended, the patient’s health history, treatment needs, and safety considerations are reviewed. Throughout the appointment, the patient is monitored carefully. The goal is not simply to complete treatment, but to help the patient feel supported and safe throughout the process.

Communication and Care Philosophy

Patients appreciate Dr. Shakfa’s calm manner, clear explanations, and gentle approach. He takes time to explain the reason for treatment in plain language and helps patients understand their options before moving forward.

His approach fits closely with Dr. Barbat’s philosophy: dental care should be careful, honest, comfortable, and designed for long-term health. Whether he is placing an implant, removing a tooth, performing a root canal, or helping an anxious patient through treatment with sedation, Dr. Shakfa’s focus remains on doing the procedure thoughtfully and helping the patient feel at ease.

Patient Experiences

Patients often come to Dr. Shakfa feeling nervous about surgery, implants, or sedation. Many later describe feeling relieved by how clearly everything was explained and how comfortable the experience was.

FAQs

Does Dr. Shakfa provide IV sedation?

Yes. Dr. Shakfa provides IV sedation for appropriate patients, including those with dental anxiety, a strong gag reflex, or more complex treatment needs. A patient’s health history and treatment plan are reviewed before sedation is recommended.

What procedures does Dr. Shakfa perform?

Dr. Shakfa provides dental implant placement, bone grafting, wisdom tooth removal, extractions, root canal therapy, and other surgical procedures. He also provides IV sedation for appropriate patients.

Is IV sedation safe?

IV sedation can be safe and effective when properly administered and monitored. Before IV sedation is recommended, the patient’s medical history, medications, and treatment needs are reviewed. During the appointment, the patient is monitored carefully.

Does Dr. Shakfa place dental implants?

Yes. Dr. Shakfa places dental implants and performs related surgical procedures, including bone grafting when needed. Implant treatment is coordinated with the restorative plan so the final result is designed for function, comfort, and appearance.

Can anxious patients receive sedation dentistry?

Yes. Sedation may be an option for patients who feel anxious about dental treatment, have had difficult past experiences, or need longer procedures completed more comfortably. Dr. Shakfa will review whether IV sedation is appropriate based on the patient’s health history and treatment needs.
