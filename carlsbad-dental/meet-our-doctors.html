Dr. Bruna

Dr. Bruna is a passionate and experienced dentist committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered care. She began her dental journey in Brazil, graduating from UNOPAR – University of Northern Paraná – in 2011. Driven by a desire to deepen her expertise, she completed a postgraduate specialization in Prosthodontics from 2012 to 2014.

After several years of serving patients in both private practice and public health settings in Brazil, Dr. Bruna relocated to the United States. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Colorado in 2020 and has been practicing in both public and private practice ever since.

With hundreds of hours of continuing education across various areas of dentistry, Dr. Bruna stays at the forefront of the latest advancements to ensure the best outcomes for her patients. She is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the American Academy of Implant Dentistry (AAID).

Outside the office, she enjoys spending time outdoors, hiking, exercising and taking long walks along the Pecos River with her husband Dr. Mendoza and their dogs.

Dr. John Mendoza

Welcome to Carlsbad Dental! I’m Dr. John Mendoza, and I’m dedicated to providing high-quality, compassionate dental care to patients of all ages here in Carlsbad, NM. My goal is to help you achieve not only a healthy smile but also a confident one that you can be proud of.

I graduated from the University of the Pacific in San Francisco in 2015 with my Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree. Since then, I have pursued extensive postgraduate training in several key areas of dentistry, including Invisalign, dental implants, braces, bone grafting, and sleep apnea treatment. This additional training enables me to offer a wide range of treatments tailored to meet your unique needs.

I am an active member of several prestigious dental organizations, including the American Academy of Implant Dentistry, the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association (ADA), and the Academy of GP Orthodontics. These memberships allow me to stay at the forefront of advancements in the field, ensuring that my patients receive the best possible care.

At Carlsbad Dental, I strive to create a comfortable and friendly environment where every patient feels like family. Whether you’re here for routine preventive care or more complex treatments, I take the time to listen to your concerns and craft a personalized treatment plan just for you.

Outside of the office, I enjoy staying active with hobbies like working out, hiking, hunting, shooting, and spending time with my two playful doodles. I also have a passion for stand-up comedy, both watching and performing, which adds a fun and creative outlet to my life. In my college years, I swam for UNLV and even tried out for the 2008 Olympic Swim Team, which taught me valuable lessons in discipline and determination.

I’m also proud to share that I’m married to Dr. Bruna, who is also a dentist. Together, we share a passion for helping others and are committed to serving our community through our dental practice.

I look forward to meeting you at Carlsbad Dental and helping you achieve a smile that reflects your overall health and well-being!

Dr. William T. McCauley III

Dr. William T. McCauley III, DDS, is a highly respected dentist who has been serving the Carlsbad, New Mexico community for over four decades. After earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Baylor University in 1978, he established his dental practice at 612 N Canyon Street, where he has remained ever since.

Throughout his extensive career, Dr. McCauley has been dedicated to providing comprehensive dental care to patients of all ages.

Patients consistently commend Dr. McCauley for his attentive and compassionate approach to dental care. His commitment to patient education and preventive care has earned him a loyal following within the community.
