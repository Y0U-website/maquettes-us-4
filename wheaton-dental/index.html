Originally from Mansfield, Ohio, Dr. Ryan Wheaton attended Ohio Northern University for his degree in biology and then The Ohio State University for dental school. He practiced in eastern Ohio for several years after graduation before opening his general dentist office in Grove City, Ohio.

Outside of working hours, Dr. Wheaton enjoys exploring the outdoors with his family and working around their house in Ashville.

Wheaton Dental was founded in Grove City in July 2018. As a dentist, Dr. Wheaton places patient needs and education first. In his office, he uses a small intraoral camera and digital x-rays to bring images of problem teeth to the TV screen where they can be explained and discussed, incorporating the patients desires and thoughts into each treatment plan.
