Choose your plan

Simply choose the plan that aligns with your oral care requirements, whether it’s the Preventive or Periodontal Maintenance option. Complete a swift enrollment form, and you’r re ready to enjoy the benefits of maintaining a healthy smile without the hassle of traditional insurance constraints.

The Adult Preventive Plan is designed to maintain optimal oral health through regular preventive care. This plan helps prevent common dental issues, such as cavities and gum disease, by promoting routine dental visits. By staying ahead of potential problems, patients can enjoy healthier teeth and gums, reducing the need for more extensive and costly treatments in the future. Additionally, this plan supports overall health, as regular dental care is linked to better general well-being.

The Child Preventive Plan focuses on establishing a foundation of good oral health habits from a young age. Regular preventive care helps prevent tooth decay and other dental issues, ensuring children develop strong and healthy teeth. Early and consistent dental visits foster a positive relationship with dental care, reducing anxiety and promoting lifelong oral hygiene habits. This plan also supports the child’s overall health and development by maintaining a healthy smile.

The Periodontal Prevention Plan is tailored for patients with a history of periodontal (gum) disease, offering intensive care to manage and prevent further progression. Regular periodontal maintenance helps control bacterial growth, reducing inflammation and preventing bone loss. This plan significantly lowers the risk of tooth loss and supports overall oral health by keeping gum disease in check. Enhanced preventive care through this plan also contributes to better overall health, as periodontal health is closely linked to systemic conditions like heart disease and diabetes.
