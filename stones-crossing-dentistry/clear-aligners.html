The Clear Aligner Process

  • Consultation and digital scan to assess alignment and plan tooth movement.
  • Review of a digital preview showing projected results stage by stage.
  • Custom trays fabricated and delivered in sequenced sets.
  • Progress checks at scheduled intervals to confirm tracking.
  • Retainer phase after active treatment to hold teeth in their new positions.

What to Expect

At your initial visit, we take a digital scan of your teeth and discuss your alignment goals. You will see a digital preview of projected results before committing to treatment. Once your trays arrive, you wear each set for one to two weeks, visiting our office periodically for progress checks. After active treatment, a retainer keeps teeth stable in their new positions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Treatment time varies by case, but most patients complete their plan in 6 to 18 months depending on the complexity of alignment needed.

You may feel mild pressure when switching to a new set of aligners, but this is a sign they are working. Discomfort is typically minor and temporary.

Remove aligners before eating or drinking anything other than water. This prevents staining and damage to the trays.

For best results, aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them only for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing.

For mild to moderate alignment issues, clear aligners deliver comparable results. Severe cases may still require traditional orthodontics.
