Overcome Dental Anxiety

At Filling Station Family Dental Center we know that fear of the dentist or dental work is a common problem that 75 % of Americans suffer from. Many people let that fear keep them from the oral care they need. We want our patients to feel comfortable the entire time they visit us and we have solutions for overcoming your dental anxiety.

Communication is Key

Our Pain Policy

Tell us what you are most afraid of. For most people it is pain. We will do everything we can to ensure you are not in pain for any procedure. If needed we can provide types of anesthesia to keep you comfortable. We also can take a break during your treatment if you start to get nervous. Just signal us by lifting up a hand to tell us know you need a break.

Fear FREE

Fear of the dental office in general is another common issue. This usually has to do with fear of the unknown. We will walk you through every step and tell you what is coming next, so there are never any surprises. We also can help you to practice breathing techniques to keep you calm as well as things like listening to music. Most treatments allow for you to listen to music to help distract your mind.

Don’t let fear prevent you from dental care! Come see Dr. Arrington and his staff and let us help you to see there is nothing to fear.
