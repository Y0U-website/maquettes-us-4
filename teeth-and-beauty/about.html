Advanced Tools,

Amazing Results

CBCT

Our CBCT scanner creates detailed 3D images of your teeth and jaw in seconds, helping us plan your treatment with incredible precision. It&#x27;s faster, more comfortable, and gives us the insights we need to provide the best care.

Intraoral Scanner

No more messy putty! Our intraoral scanner creates perfect digital impressions and scans of your teeth in minutes. Not only is it more comfortable than traditional molds, but it also means more accurate crowns, veneers, and Invisalign treatments. We use these digital scans to show things in your mouth that you often can&#x27;t see. It&#x27;s truly eye opening for some patients!
