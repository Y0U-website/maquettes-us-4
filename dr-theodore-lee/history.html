Our history

Sun River Health was founded in 1975 when four African American mothers led efforts to open our first health center in Peekskill, New York with the promise of delivering high-quality, affordable health care to all.

Today, after 50 years of service, Sun River Health is still delivering on that promise, serving more than 250,000 patients at 50 locations throughout the Hudson Valley, New York City, and Long Island.
