Meet The Doctors at Mira Mesa…

In his General Practice Residency, Dr. Faris received extensive training, experience and speed in treating emergencies and providing comprehensive care treatment for his patients. Dr. Faris also has a Doctorate degree from MSA British university. Awards and honors received The Clinic Award from Loma Linda Dental school Reach Scholarship from Loma Linda Dental school Family and Cycling.

When not practicing dentistry, Dr. Faris enjoys spending time with his wife and two children doing outdoor family activities. His hobbies include cycling and swimming, and he is the founder of the ‘’Mira Mesa Dental Cycling Club’’ so that he and his patients who are interested can enjoy some time cycling on the road away from the dental world. Professional Associations Dr. Faris is a proud member of the: American Dental Association California Dental Association American Academy of Implant Dentistry San Diego County Dental Society.
