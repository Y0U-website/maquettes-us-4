Services

Preventative Dental Care

Your oral health is one of the most important aspects of overall body health. While you may think visiting a geriatric Portland dentist like Dr. Thomas Cardwell is the only way to protect your teeth and gums, scientific research shows that there a strong connection exists between our oral and overall health. At Prime Dental Care, Dr. Cardwell and his team will work to develop a custom treatment plan designed to maintain and improve your oral health.

  • Dental Exams & Cleanings
  • Dental Sealants
  • Mouthguards

Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Cardwell provides the latest methods in professional dental care to enhance the appearance of your mouth, teeth, and smile. Whether you are looking to get a straighter looking smile to repairing chipped, cracked, discolored, or fractured teeth, or just want a brighter, whiter smile, the Prime Dental Care team in Milwaukie is here to help you reach your very best smile.

  • Teeth Whitening
  • Dental Bonding
  • Crowns

Restorative Dentistry

Prime Dental Care in SE Portland is proud to provide custom treatment plans and dental procedures to keep your mouth healthy and functional. Common restorative dental procedures that we provide include: filling cavities to prevent further decay; replacing any missing teeth with implants, bridges, or dentures; and strengthening and protecting broken teeth with crowns or caps.

  • Full and Partial Dentures
  • Dental Implants
  • TMJ Treatment
