Meet Your Alpharetta, GA Dentist: Dr. Humam

Dr. Humam Salahieh is an accomplished general dentist with a passion for providing exceptional oral healthcare to his patients. With almost a decade of experience in the field, he brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to Main Street Dentistry.

Dr. Salahieh earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the prestigious University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. During his time there, he demonstrated a remarkable dedication to his studies and received recognition for his outstanding clinical skills and academic achievements. Armed with a solid foundation, he embarked on his journey to make a meaningful impact on the lives of his patients.

Read More Throughout his years in practice, Dr. Salahieh has demonstrated a genuine commitment to his patients’ wellbeing. He believes that excellent oral healthcare is more than just treating dental issues; it’s about building trusting relationships with patients and understanding their unique needs and concerns. Driven by compassion and empathy, he ensures that every patient feels comfortable and heard during their visit.

As a general dentist, Dr. Salahieh is proficient in a wide array of dental treatments, including preventive care, restorative procedures, and cosmetic dentistry. His comprehensive approach to dental care ensures that each patient receives personalized treatment plans that cater to their individual dental health goals.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Salahieh nurtures two distinct passions: piano and film making. He has been an avid pianist for many years, finding solace and creativity in the melodies he plays. Dr. Salahieh’s love for music complements his precision and attention to detail in dentistry, enhancing his abilities as a dentist.

Moreover, Dr. Salahieh is an aspiring filmmaker, using his creativity to craft compelling stories and visual experiences. His passion for film making has not only provided him with an artistic outlet but has also honed his communication skills, which he applies in educating patients about their dental health in clear and engaging ways.

Dr. Salahieh firmly believes in giving back to the community. He actively participates in dental outreach programs and initiatives aimed at promoting oral health awareness in underserved areas. By sharing his expertise and knowledge, he strives to improve the overall dental health of those in need.

With Dr. Humam Salahieh’s blend of skill, passion for dentistry, and creative spirit, he is an invaluable member of our dental team. We invite you to experience his compassionate care and dedication to excellent dental health. Book an appointment today and become a part of our dental family.
