What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are a more secure and stable alternative to traditional dentures. They are anchored to dental implants, which are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone. This solution provides greater stability and functionality, closely mimicking the feel of natural teeth.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures

Stability: Implant-supported dentures are securely anchored to implants, preventing slipping and shifting.

Bone Preservation: Dental implants help preserve the jawbone, preventing bone loss that can occur with missing teeth.

Improved Functionality: These dentures offer better chewing and speaking abilities compared to traditional dentures.

Long-Term Solution: Implant-supported dentures are durable and can last many years with proper care.

The Implant-Supported Denture Process

  • Initial Consultation: Our dentists will assess your oral health and determine if you are a candidate for dental implants.
  • Implant Placement: Titanium implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. Healing time is required for the implants to integrate with the bone.
  • Scanning/Impressions: Once the implants have healed, we will take precise digital scans or impressions to create custom prostheses that fit perfectly.
  • Denture Attachment: The custom dentures are securely attached to the implants, ensuring a stable and comfortable fit.
  • Care Instructions: We will provide guidance on how to care for your implant-supported dentures to ensure their longevity and effectiveness.

What Are Fixed Implant Prostheses?

Fixed implant prostheses, also known as fixed dentures or implant bridges, are permanent dental restorations anchored to dental implants. Unlike removable dentures, fixed prostheses are securely attached. The patient cannot remove them, offering the highest level of stability and functionality.

Benefits of Fixed Implant Prostheses

Permanent Solution: Fixed prostheses are securely attached and do not need to be removed for cleaning.

Natural Appearance: They closely mimic the look and feel of natural teeth.

Enhanced Stability: Fixed prostheses offer superior stability and functionality compared to removable options.

Improved Oral Health: They help maintain jawbone density and support facial structure.

The Fixed Implant Prosthesis Process

  • Initial Consultation: Our dentists will evaluate your oral health and discuss your goals to determine if fixed implant prostheses are right for you.
  • Implant Placement: Titanium implants are surgically placed into the jawbone. Healing time is required for the implants to integrate with the bone.
  • Scanning/Impressions: Once the implants have healed, we will take precise digital scans or impressions to create custom prostheses that fit perfectly.
  • Prosthesis Attachment: The custom prostheses are securely attached to the implants, providing a stable and comfortable fit.
  • Care Instructions: We will provide guidance on how to care for your fixed implant prostheses to ensure their longevity and effectiveness.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dentures last?

With proper care, dentures can last between 5 to 10 years. Regular check-ups are essential for maintaining their fit and function.

Are implant-supported dentures painful?

The implant procedure involves minor surgery, which may cause discomfort but is generally well-tolerated. Pain management options are available to ensure your comfort.

How do I care for my dentures?

Clean your dentures daily with a soft-bristle brush and denture cleaner. Soak them overnight in a denture solution and avoid using hot water, which can warp them.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

It may take some time to adjust, but you should be able to eat most foods. Implant-supported dentures will enhance your chewing ability and stability.

Are fixed implant prostheses removable?

No, they are permanently attached to the implants and can only be removed by a dentist.
