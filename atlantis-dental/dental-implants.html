When a Single Dental Implant May Be the Right Fit

A single implant can replace a missing tooth from root to crown. These common treatment examples help explain when a focused implant solution may be considered.

Missing Front Tooth

Restore a visible gap with a carefully planned implant and custom crown.

Missing Molar

Rebuild chewing support in the back of the mouth without a removable appliance.

Failing Tooth

When a tooth cannot be predictably saved, an implant may be discussed as a replacement option.

Existing Gap

Replace a long-standing single-tooth space after evaluating bone and neighboring teeth.

Is a single implant appropriate for you?

A single implant is generally considered when one tooth is missing or cannot be saved and the surrounding teeth are otherwise healthy. Bone support, gum health, bite forces, and medical history must be evaluated.

Single Dental Implant Questions

What does a single implant replace?

It replaces one missing tooth with an implant in the jaw and a custom restoration above it.

Will it look like a natural tooth?

The restoration is planned to coordinate with nearby teeth in shape and shade. Results vary with anatomy and treatment needs.

How long does treatment take?

Timing depends on healing, bone support, whether grafting is needed, and when the final restoration can be placed.

Is a bridge another option?

Sometimes. Your consultation compares an implant, bridge, or other appropriate option based on the condition of surrounding teeth.
