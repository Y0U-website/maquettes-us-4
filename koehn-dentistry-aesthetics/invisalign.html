FOR TEENS

Teenagers aren’t always good at adhering to the strict rules braces require, but with Invisalign, they get a lot more freedom. Because Invisalign is removable, you won’t have to worry about the extra expense of broken brackets because your teen wanted some popcorn or sticky candy.

Plus, teens are in the process of discovering their self identity. If you to improve your teenager’s self confidence without the worry of metal brackets, invisible braces are the way to go. With Invisalign, these removal braces can help your teen stay on top of their cleaning and avoid future white spots on their teeth. Clear and fast-working, this option will straighten teeth faster than traditional braces and without the potential heartache caused by less discreet methods.

For Adults

Whether you had braces when you were younger, and your teeth just moved out of place, or this will be your first foray into teeth straightening, Invisalign is the perfect option for you. From getting your teeth in perfect alignment for better protection to preventing expensive dental procedures later in life, Invisalign is a great way to protect your teeth.

Invisalign is clear and fits your teeth perfectly, so most people won’t even notice as your teeth begin to straighten. You don’t have to worry about being uncomfortable as you speak, laugh, and smile with customers and colleagues.
