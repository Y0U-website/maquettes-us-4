Why Do We Offer Spark Clear Aligners?

Spark clear aligners are newer to the clear aligner scene, and may not be as well-known as some other brands. However, these aligners are quickly and quietly making a name for themselves as a premium aligner choice.

Spark aligners are made with TruGEN , a material that stains less, is clearer, and allows for better teeth movement than many more well-known aligner brands. In addition, this material is BPA, mercury, latex, and phthalate-free, making it an ideal choice for those with allergies and sensitivities.
