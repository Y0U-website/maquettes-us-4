Our Services

Proudly providing an expansive catalog of services to keep your families smiles happy & healthy! Let us take care of you.

Invisalign

The easy way to straighten your teeth. The Invisalign system uses clear, removable aligners. Great for adults and teens.

Teeth Whitening

Brighten your smile a shade or two with professional teeth whitening treatments.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells made of porcelain or resin that fit around your teeth to improve their appearance.

Cosmetic Bonding

Made from natural tooth-colored materials, bonding covers up and eliminates imperfections like chips, cracks, stains, and gaps.

Bone Graft

Restore the dental bone loss for the structure, strength, and appearance of your smile.

Implant Overdenture

Restore all the teeth in your upper or lower jaw with as few as 4 dental implants.

Implants

The most effective tooth replacement option available today. Restores the look, function, and health of your teeth.

Wisdom Teeth

If your wisdom teeth are causing problems, then removing them may be necessary to ensure long-term dental health.

Extraction

Removing a tooth is always a last resort, but may be necessary when dealing with severe decay, infection, or tooth with poor prognosis.

Night/Mouth Guards

Stop grinding your teeth at night and protect your teeth during sports with a custom-made mouthguard.

Denture

Replace missing teeth! Full and partial dentures restore your ability to chew, speak, and smile.

Root Canal

Root canal therapy is a safe, simple, effective way to stop decay and infection and save your tooth from extraction.

Dental Crown

Cover up and protect a damaged tooth. Often the final step of an implant and root canal treatment.

White Cavity Filling

With early detection technology, advanced imaging, and safe filling materials, addressing tooth decay is easier than ever.

Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease is the top cause of tooth loss. Early detection and personalized treatment options will keep your mouth healthy.

Emergency Care

Same day appointments available to help heal toothaches, chipping, & other dental emergencies.

High Tech Dentistry

Comfortable digital/3D x-rays done in seconds! Provides better diagnosis for more personalized care.

Exam & Cleaning

Visit us every 6 months to keep your smile healthy and to receive gentle and thorough cleaning and exam.
