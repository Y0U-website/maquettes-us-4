Q: What happens during a general dentistry appointment?

A: During your general dentistry appointment with Dr. Patel , the visit typically begins with a professional dental cleaning by one of our experienced hygienists. This process involves removing plaque and tartar buildup, polishing your teeth, and flossing. Dr. Patel will then conduct a thorough examination, checking your teeth, gums, and mouth for signs of cavities, gum disease, or other dental issues. In some cases, X-rays may be taken to get a closer look at areas not visible during the visual exam. Dr. Patel will discuss any findings with you and recommend treatment if needed.

Q: What are the signs that I may need a dental filling?

A: Common signs that you may need a dental filling include:

  • Tooth sensitivity, especially to hot or cold temperatures
  • Pain when chewing or biting
  • Visible holes or dark spots on your teeth
  • Chipped or fractured teeth If you experience any of these symptoms, it’s important to schedule an appointment with Dr. Patel . Early intervention with a tooth-colored filling can prevent more extensive damage and restore your tooth’s structure and function.

Q: What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

A: If you experience a dental emergency, such as a severe toothache, broken or knocked-out tooth, or injury to your mouth, contact Dr. Patel at Alpine Dental Care immediately at (908) 387-9170 . Our office provides emergency dental care and will do everything possible to accommodate same-day appointments. In the meantime, avoid putting pressure on the affected area and use over-the-counter pain relief if needed. For a knocked-out tooth, try to place it back in the socket or store it in milk and bring it with you to the office as soon as possible.

Q: Does Dr. Patel provide treatment for gum disease?

A: Yes, Dr. Patel offers comprehensive treatment for gum disease (periodontal disease). Early-stage gum disease, known as gingivitis, can often be treated with improved oral hygiene and professional cleanings. For more advanced cases (periodontitis), Dr. Patel may recommend deep cleaning procedures such as scaling and root planing. This treatment removes plaque and tartar from below the gumline and smooths the tooth roots to help the gums reattach to the teeth. Dr. Patel will work closely with you to develop a personalized treatment plan to manage and prevent the progression of gum disease.

Q: Is fluoride treatment necessary for adults?

A: While fluoride treatments are commonly associated with pediatric dentistry, adults can also benefit from fluoride. Dr. Patel recommends fluoride treatments for patients of all ages to strengthen enamel and protect against tooth decay. Fluoride is especially beneficial if you are prone to cavities, have exposed tooth roots, or experience dry mouth, as these conditions can increase the risk of decay.

Q: Can Dr. Patel help with bad breath (halitosis)?

A: Yes, Dr. Patel can help diagnose and treat the causes of chronic bad breath (halitosis). Bad breath can be caused by various factors, including poor oral hygiene, gum disease, dry mouth, or underlying medical conditions. During your visit, Dr. Patel will assess your oral health and identify any potential issues that may contribute to bad breath. Proper oral hygiene, regular dental cleanings, and addressing underlying issues such as gum disease are effective ways to combat bad breath.

Q: What should I expect after a dental extraction?

A: After a dental extraction performed by Dr. Patel , it’s important to follow post-operative care instructions to promote healing and prevent complications. You may experience some swelling and mild discomfort, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication. Avoid eating hard or chewy foods and do not use a straw for the first few days, as this can disrupt the blood clot and lead to dry socket. Dr. Patel will provide you with detailed care instructions and schedule a follow-up appointment to ensure proper healing.

Q: How do I know if I need a root canal?

A: You may need a root canal if you are experiencing symptoms such as:

  • Persistent tooth pain, especially when biting or chewing
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold that lingers after the stimulus is removed
  • Swollen, tender gums near the affected tooth
  • A darkened tooth
  • A small pimple-like bump on the gums If you’re experiencing any of these signs, contact Dr. Patel immediately. A root canal can save your tooth by removing infected pulp tissue and sealing the tooth to prevent further damage.
