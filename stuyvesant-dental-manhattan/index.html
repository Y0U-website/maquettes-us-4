Dr. Jasjit Basra earned his Bachelor of Science degree from Siena College, followed by a Master of Science in Biomedical Sciences from the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. He went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from NYU College of Dentistry, where he refined his clinical expertise in comprehensive and restorative dentistry.

With a strong foundation in biomedical sciences and advanced dental training, Dr. Basra approaches patient care with both precision and compassion. He believes exceptional dentistry lies at the intersection of science, artistry, and trust. His clinical philosophy emphasizes minimally invasive treatment, long-term oral health, and aesthetic excellence tailored to each individual.
