Dental Implants in Manhattan, New…

  • Natural Look & Feel: Implants are designed to match the color and shape of your natural teeth, blending seamlessly into your smile.
  • Preserved Oral Health: Implants don’t require altering surrounding teeth like bridges do, helping to maintain the integrity of your healthy teeth and gums.
  • Improved Functionality: With implants, you can chew, speak, and smile with confidence, without worrying about slipping or discomfort.
  • Jawbone Preservation: Implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent bone loss, which can occur when a tooth is missing.

The Dental Implant Process

Consultation and Evaluation: We’ll examine your oral health and discuss your specific needs to determine if dental implants are the right solution for you.

Implant Placement: The titanium post is placed into the jawbone during a minimally invasive surgical procedure.

Osseointegration: Over several months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a stable foundation for your new tooth.

Final Restoration: Once the implant is securely in place, we will attach a custom crown, bridge, or denture that looks and functions like your natural teeth.
