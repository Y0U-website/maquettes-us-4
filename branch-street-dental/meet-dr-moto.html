Meet Dr. Moto

Dr. Mota was born and raised in Lawrence, Massachusetts, however for the past 12 years has resided in the City of Boston. He earned a B.S. degree in Dental Hygiene from the Massachusetts College of Pharmacy and Health Sciences and a Master’s in Biomedical Science from Tufts University School of Medicine. Dr. Mota’s passion for dentistry motivated him to continue his education and obtain his Doctorate of Dental Medicinedegree from the Tufts University School of Dental Medicine.

He is dedicated to dentistry and strives to stay up to datewith the latest techniques and procedures. Dr. Mota enjoys working with the adult and pediatric population and values building meaningful relationships, working hard to meet patients’ dental needs.

Outside of work, Dr. Mota enjoys hiking, cooking and spending time with family.
