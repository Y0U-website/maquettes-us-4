Dental Implant Questions

Most patients with good general health are candidates for implants. Key factors include adequate jawbone density and healthy gums. During your consultation, we’ll evaluate your specific situation and discuss all options.

Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after implant placement. We use local anesthesia during the procedure, and many patients find that implant placement involves less pain than a tooth extraction.

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The restorations attached to implants may need replacement over time, but the implants themselves are designed to be permanent.

Dental implants require the same care as natural teeth: regular brushing, flossing, and professional cleanings. With proper maintenance and regular check-ups, your implants can provide decades of reliable service.
