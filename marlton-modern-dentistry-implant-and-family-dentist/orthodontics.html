Orthodontics Questions

Most teens and adults with common orthodontic issues are candidates for clear aligner therapy. These systems can effectively treat crowding, spacing, mild to moderate bite issues, and more. During your consultation, Dr. Rockwell will evaluate your specific situation and recommend the most appropriate treatment approach.

Clear aligner therapy is typically less uncomfortable than traditional braces. You may experience mild pressure or discomfort for a day or two after switching to a new set of aligners, which indicates the treatment is working to move your teeth. This temporary sensation is easily managed with over-the-counter pain relievers if needed.

Clean your aligners daily with a soft toothbrush and lukewarm water or special aligner cleaning solution. Avoid hot water, which can warp the plastic. Always remove aligners when eating or drinking anything besides water, and brush your teeth before reinserting them to prevent staining and decay.
