Meet Our Dentist

THE PATHWAY TO DENTISTRY

Dr. Robinson’s journey into dentistry began at Ricks College before he attended the University of Hawaii, where he played quarterback for the Rainbow Warriors. He earned his dental degree from the University of Louisville School of Dentistry and spent two years practicing at Remmers Dental Group. In 2008, he returned to his home state and founded Robinson Dental & Implants.

BEYOND THE DENTAL CHAIR

Outside the office, Dr. Robinson is a family man through and through. He married his high school sweetheart after serving a mission in Hong Kong, and together they are proud parents of six children and one grandchild. When he is not caring for patients, you can find him outdoors, traveling with his family, farming with his team of Percherons, or riding his horses.
