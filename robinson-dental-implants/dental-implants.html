Finding the Right Option for Your Smile

No two smiles are the same, so the right solution depends on how many teeth are missing and what you want your smile to feel like again.

If you are missing a single tooth, a dental implant can replace it with a custom crown that blends naturally with the surrounding teeth. If several teeth are missing in a row, implant supported bridges can fill the space by using implants for support instead of relying on healthy neighboring teeth. For patients missing most or all of their teeth, All-on-X dental implants offer a way to restore a full smile with a small number of strategically placed implants.

Dr. Robinson has provided implant care throughout Eastern Utah since 2008 and will help you determine which approach fits your needs and long-term goals.

How long do dental implants last?

With good home care and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for many years. The crown or restoration may eventually need replacement due to normal wear, but the implant itself is designed for long term stability.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Many people with missing teeth are candidates. Dr. Robinson will evaluate your gums, bone levels, and overall oral health to determine whether implants are right for you.

Does getting a dental implant hurt?

Most patients are surprised by how manageable the process feels. The area is numbed during treatment, and any soreness afterward is usually mild and temporary.

How long does the process take?

Treatment time varies depending on healing and individual needs. In many cases, the process takes a few months to allow the implant to fully integrate with the bone before the final restoration is placed.

Will my implant look natural?

Yes. Your final restoration is custom designed to match your surrounding teeth so it blends naturally with your smile.
