Who is Suitable for Dental Implants?

Most people are suitable for implant treatment. You do need to be over 18, as your jaw and teeth must have finished growing and developing. Other important factors include:

We always carefully screen individuals for suitability, based on their oral and general health. It is worth coming to see us even if you are unsure if you are suitable for treatment. Being older is not usually a problem, and we routinely place dental implants in patients in their eighties and older.

  • Missing one or more teeth or having teeth that are failing and need to be removed.
  • Good dental health and no signs of active diseases like periodontitis (gum disease).
  • Reasonable general health, so issues such as diabetes need to be well-controlled.
  • Being a non-smoker, or willing to quit during treatment, and we would recommend quitting for good.
  • Sufficient jawbone to support dental implants, or are willing and able to have dental bone grafting to restore missing bone.
  • Able to maintain a good oral hygiene routine, as otherwise the tissues around the implants can become infected.

What to Expect During Dental Implant Treatment

The process for getting teeth implants is straightforward and is completed over several months. Your oral health is unique, which is why your treatment is fully customized. However, you can gain an idea of what to expect in the overview below.

Consultation, Dental Exam, and Diagnostics

Your treatment journey begins with an in-depth consultation at Dental Pearl . We discuss your treatment goals and any concerns you may have.

We review your dental and medical history and conduct a thorough dental examination. This is to ensure that dental implants are the best and most appropriate solution for your tooth loss.

Diagnostic imaging includes a cone beam CT scan. We may also take digital X-rays, photos, and digital dental impressions.

These diagnostics enable our implant dentist to complete comprehensive treatment planning and identify the optimal location for each implant.

Discussion of Your Treatment Plan

Our dental implant dentist will discuss your treatment plan with you. It will outline which implant system we believe will best meet your goals, as well as any additional treatments required, such as removing failing teeth and performing bone grafting.

These treatments may need to be completed separately before implant placement. In certain situations, they can be completed simultaneously with implant placement during a single procedure.

There may be more than one solution; in such cases, we will discuss all possible options and the dental implant cost. We can also discuss temporary teeth to wear during implant healing and until we make and fit your permanent teeth.

Implant Placement

Your implants will be placed using computer-guided surgery, using your custom treatment plan. We use your cone beam CT scan to plan each implant placement. Once complete, a computer-generated stent is created.

This is a template used during your implant surgery, which fits over your gums and guides our dentist. It ensures we only need small incisions and that every implant is inserted accurately, aiding faster healing afterward. Stitches close these incisions, and healing begins.

If required, we will fit your temporary teeth at this stage.

Healing and Osseointegration

Before you leave Dental Pearl, we will provide clear instructions on aftercare that are tailored to your needs. The information includes how to keep your mouth clean during healing, and suitable foods to eat.

We will also provide advice on managing any discomfort. Usually, over-the-counter pain relief is adequate. Please remember that we are always here to help with any further questions or concerns, so don’t hesitate to reach out.

It typically takes 3 to 6 months for dental implants to heal fully. This involves a process called osseointegration, and it cannot be rushed.

Osseointegration is essential for implant success, and is when new bone is deposited on and around the implant, gradually fusing it in place. During osseointegration, implants must remain undisturbed.
