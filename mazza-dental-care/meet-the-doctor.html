Mazza Dental Care

Meet the doctor

On April 11, 1994, he opened Mazza Dental Care in Winters providing general dentistry to the City of Winters and the surrounding community.

He is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), California Dental Association (CDA), the Sacramento District Dental Society (SDDS), the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), where he holds the Level of Master, and The Wisconsin Dental Association. He is also a Graduate of the Kois Center in Seattle, WA.

Dr. Mazza maintains the license to practice dentistry in the states of California, Wisconsin, and New York.

He maintains an Oral Conscious Sedation Permit with the State of California.

He continues to expand his base of dental knowledge and skill by completing hundreds of hours of continuing education at prestigious institutions across the country.

Dr. Mazza maintains membership with the AARP, the United States Fencing Association and has Life membership with the Eagle Scouts of America, and the Reserve Officers Association.
