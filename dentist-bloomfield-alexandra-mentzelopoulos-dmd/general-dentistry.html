What Is General Dentistry?

General dentistry is the foundation of oral health care, focusing on preventive measures to maintain the health of teeth, gums, and mouth. It includes routine dental exams, professional cleanings, X-rays, and oral cancer screenings to identify any potential issues early on. Through regular dental visits, we can prevent tooth decay, gum disease, and other oral health problems before they require more intensive treatment. General dentistry also involves education on proper oral hygiene habits and diet to support long-term dental health.

Benefits of General Dentistry

General dentistry offers numerous benefits, making it an essential part of your overall healthcare routine. Regular check-ups help to catch dental issues such as cavities and gum disease in their early stages, saving you time and money by avoiding complex procedures. Professional cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing cannot address, ensuring your teeth and gums remain healthy.

Additionally, routine X-rays and exams allow for a deeper understanding of your dental condition, providing insights into underlying concerns like impacted teeth or bone loss. By prioritizing general dentistry, you can maintain a healthy smile and enjoy a lifetime of good oral health.
