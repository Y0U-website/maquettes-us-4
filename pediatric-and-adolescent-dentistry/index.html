Welcome to the office of Dr. Nitsa Gilbert, where helping children achieve optimal oral health is our passion. Located in Warner Robins, Georgia, we are committed to providing comprehensive dental care for infants, children and adolescents and strive to create a fun-filled environment where children feel at home!

We believe early dental care can promote a lifetime of healthy smiles for your child. Our skilled and nurturing staff will provide a positive dental experience and help equip your child with the knowledge necessary for good oral hygiene. To ensure that your child grows up with a healthy smile, we offer sealants, fluoride treatments and regular check-ups.

We love children, and we have the knowledge and experience needed to provide your family with child-friendly dental care. You can rest assured that we will work hard to make sure your family’s dental experiences with us are positive ones!

We want your dental experience to be comfortable and relaxing, which is why we specialize in gentle sedation dentistry procedures. With our advanced equipment and techniques, you’ll be amazed how comfortable a dental procedure can be!

Finding new ways to provide excellent pediatric dental care is our priority. We look forward to keeping your child’s smile bright and cavity-free
