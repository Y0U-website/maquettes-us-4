About Us

Schedule Online ABOUT US MISSION STATEMENT: At Gem State Smiles, we are dedicated to providing a warm and welcoming environment where everyone feels comfortable, respected, and genuinely cared for. Our values of professionalism, respect, and friendliness guide us in delivering patient-centered care that is both compassionate and high-quality. We believe in educating our patients about their oral health, collaborating with them to make informed decisions, and offering affordable options for their care.

We believe that investing in your oral health supports your overall health, confidence, and quality of life. Our goal is to promote lifelong oral wellness through evidence-based practices and constant learning, ensuring that our patients receive the best, most up-to-date care possible. Dental visits can be daunting for some, which is why we strive to create a calm atmosphere and offer supportive options to help overcome dental fears.

Guided by our commitment to service and compassion, we are dedicated to giving back to our community by making a positive impact beyond the dental chair. We don’t just treat smiles—we nurture them with care, compassion, and expertise, helping every patient achieve the healthy smile they deserve and can enjoy for a lifetime.

DR. MADSEN

Dr. Madsen’s journey began by earning a degree in Business Administration from Utah State University. His passion for patient-centered care led him to Dalhousie University in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada, an esteemed institution known for its highly selective dental program. During his time in Canada, Dr. Madsen not only excelled academically and clinically, earning multiple awards for his strong personal character, humanitarian efforts, and dedication to the ethical practice of dentistry, but also developed a deep appreciation for the country.

To further his expertise, Dr. Madsen completed a one-year general practice residency program at the University of Utah. This additional training equipped him with advanced skills in various areas of general dentistry, ensuring that he delivers the highest standard of care to his patients.

Dr. Madsen is dedicated to continuous learning and stays updated with the latest advancements in dental techniques and technology. He believes in creating a warm, welcoming, and calming environment where his patients’ needs are always prioritized. Whether you’re due for a routine check-up or require specialized treatment, Dr. Madsen is committed to providing compassionate care and achieving optimal oral health outcomes for every patient.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Madsen finds joy spending time with his wife, Jessica, and their three children. They share a love for travel and exploring new places. Dr. Madsen loves supporting his children in their various activities.
