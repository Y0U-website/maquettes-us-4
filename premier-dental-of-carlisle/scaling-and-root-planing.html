Scaling and Root Planing

Our skilled dental professionals will carefully remove plaque and tartar down to the bottom of each periodontal pocket. The root surfaces are then smoothed to promote healing and to help prevent future buildup.

If you are experiencing signs of gum disease, such as bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, or loose teeth, contact Premier Dental of Carlisle to schedule an evaluation. Early intervention with scaling and root planing can save your gums, your teeth, and improve your oral and overall health. Our team is committed to providing you with compassionate, effective care.

  • Prevents Disease Progression: This treatment helps stop the progression of periodontal disease, protecting your teeth and the supporting bone.
  • Improves Overall Health: Treating gum disease can improve your overall health. Gum disease is linked to other health complications, such as heart disease and diabetes.
