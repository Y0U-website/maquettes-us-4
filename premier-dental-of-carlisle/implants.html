Dental Implants

The process of getting implants involves several steps:

If you’re considering dental implants, contact Premier Dental of Carlisle today to schedule your consultation. Let us help you take the first step toward a confident, permanent smile.

  • Longevity: With proper care, implants are very durable and can last many years, often a lifetime.
  • Bone Preservation: Unlike other replacement options, implants stimulate and preserve natural bone growth, which can help maintain the structure of your face.
  • Improved Oral Health: Implants do not require reducing other teeth, as a tooth-supported bridge does. Because nearby teeth are not altered to support the implant, more of your own teeth are left intact, improving long-term oral health.
  • Consultation and Planning: We conduct a detailed assessment of your oral health and use imaging technologies to plan the precise placement of the implants.
  • Implant Placement: The implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. As the jaw heals, it grows around the implanted metal post, anchoring it securely in the jaw.
  • Healing Process: The healing process can take several weeks to months, during which a temporary tooth replacement option can be worn over the implant site.
  • Abutment and Crown Placement: Once the implant has bonded to the jawbone, a small connector post — the abutment — is attached to hold the new tooth. The new tooth (a crown) is then attached to the abutment.
