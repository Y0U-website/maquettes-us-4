How Dental Implants Work at Complete Smiles Dental

1. One-on-One Consultation with Dr. Albert Your implant journey at Complete Smiles starts with a thoughtful conversation and detailed evaluation by Dr. Albert Ilyayev. Using digital X-rays and precise imaging, we’ll assess your bone health, review your options, and design a plan that fits your smile goals — no pressure, just clear guidance.

2. Precise Implant Placement in Our Forest Hills Office All implant surgeries are performed in-house at our Forest Hills practice using modern, minimally invasive techniques. Dr. Albert places the titanium post with surgical precision, ensuring maximum comfort and stability. Most patients are surprised by how quick and smooth the process feels.

3. Custom Restoration for a Natural Look Once the implant has fully integrated with your jawbone (usually a few months later), we create a custom crown or bridge right here at Complete Smiles. Each restoration is hand-matched to your existing teeth in shape, color, and bite — so your new tooth feels just like your own.

In-House Oral Surgery & Tooth Replacement Some implant cases require minor oral surgery, which our experienced implant dentist can perform right here in our Forest Hills office. We take a careful, patient-first approach to ensure every step is safe, precise, and comfortable for you. If you’re unsure about coverage, we’re happy to walk you through your insurance before starting treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions Am I a candidate for dental implants at Complete Smiles? If you’re missing one or more teeth and have healthy gums, there’s a good chance you qualify. Dr. Albert or Dr. Danny will walk you through a full evaluation and give honest feedback on whether implants are the best fit for your situation.

How long will my implant last?

Our patients in Forest Hills can expect their dental implants to last 15+ years, and sometimes much longer with routine checkups and good home care. We’ll show you exactly how to keep your implant healthy long-term.

Does Complete Smiles Dental place implants in-house?

Yes. We handle the entire dental implant process right here in our Forest Hills office — from the initial consultation to the surgical placement and final crown. There’s no need to visit a separate specialist.

Can I use insurance for dental implants?

We accept most PPO plans and will check your coverage up front. Some plans help with parts of the procedure. And if you’re uninsured, we offer payment options to make it manageable.
