DEXIS - Digital Radiography Dexis digital x-rays are an extremely progressive addition to our dental office. These images require up to 75 % less radiation than with convention film type x-rays. X-ray projections appear on computer monitors within a few seconds in full view.

Axeos (3-D Dental Imaging System) The newest equipment and the latest in dental technology to offer the best care to our patients in our office is Axeos. Axeos is a dental CT scan which provides us with 3-D data to help with improved patient diagnosis. You are seated in an open environment and the procedure can be done in our office.
