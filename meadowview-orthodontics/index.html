The practice

Since Dr. Hamdan moved to Minnesota in 2002, he attended the University of Minnesota for orthodontic residency and 2 residencies in advanced general dentistry, totaling 4 years of schooling after dental school. His academic achievements include graduating summa cum laude from dental school, a Master’s degree in orthodontics, and advanced training certificates from 3 residency programs. He…

Prior to establishing Meadowview Orthodontics, Dr. Hamdan worked in Lakeville, MN for 10 years as a general dentist. He loves photography, computers, and nature. He enjoys taking his wife and two children hiking and canoeing.

Dr. Hamdan’s practicing style is to be approachable and directly within reach for his patients whether by phone, email, or on social media. He prides himself with research-based decision making and hopes to make Meadowview Orthodontics a hub for creating outstanding smiles!
