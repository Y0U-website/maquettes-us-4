Why Meadow?

Convenient hours We offer evening hours until 7pm and are open two Saturdays a month. That way our patients don’t have to miss school for their braces appointments!

Easy payments Downpayments are optional. Monthly payments are run with zero interest, no credit check required. We offer downpayment and family discounts.

Digital records, 3D X-rays and digital impressions offer a superior patient experience while enabling better treatment planning and reducing environmental waste. No more gooey stuff for impressions!

Strictly research-based Sometimes orthodontic treatment is good for breathing, but this is the exception, not the rule. Sometimes orthodontic treatment will help with TMJ issues, but this is rarely the case. Sometimes very minor discrepancies add character and do not require any treatment. We never make claims to try to "sell" orthodontics or orthodontic devices. If we do not have strong evidence that orthodontics will help you, we will be sure to let you know. Honesty in healthcare is a fundamental expectation.

Zero pressure policy A healthcare clinic is no place for pressure tactics to sign up for treatment. Consults are always free with no strings attached. Ever.

A mission to support local causes

Whether in schools, local venues, local businesses, or local charities, Meadowview Orthodontics is always striving to make an impact. Part of the Farmington Business Association, MvO has supported organizations such as the Farmington Special Olympics, the Farmington Police Department, Caring Hearts in Action, and others. Follow us on Facebook and Instagram to stay up to date on our local activities.
