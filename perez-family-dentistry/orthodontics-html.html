How Does ClearCorrect Work?

ClearCorrect is able to address most of the same orthodontic issues as traditional braces, including misalignment, crowding, gaps, and bite problems that range from mild to moderate severity. However, instead of utilizing metal brackets and wires, they use clear trays that place a strategic amount of pressure on teeth to gradually shift them into their ideal positions. Every two weeks, you’ll graduate to the next set of aligners in your treatment until reaching your final results.
