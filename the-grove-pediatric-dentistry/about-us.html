About Us

Pediatric Dental Office in Grove City, OH

At The Grove Pediatric Dentistry, our goal is to ensure a relaxed experience for both you and your child. Our kid-focused office is wise enough to walk parents through challenging dental situations, while eager to get down on your child’s level to make a new friend.

When you’re in our office, we promise to be more than dental providers. We’ll make you and your child feel at home while providing the most current and latest treatments. Come join our family at The Grove Pediatric Dentistry to see why we’re kid experts!

A father of three, Dr. Kyle understands the successes and struggles of raising children, especially when it comes to oral care. His favorite aspect of pediatric dentistry is getting to see all the different personalities children can possess and having an excuse to watch the latest Disney movie. He takes pride in his compassion, patience, and empathy when working with young children, and is excited to serve the families of Columbus and the surround area.

Meet Dr. Martinez

Dr. Eileen Martinez says she became a pediatric dentist because she loves making kids smile. She has a very nurturing and compassionate approach to children. “I love helping them get past their fears, and seeing how proud they are of themselves when they get through treatment!”

A Mentor, OH native, Dr. Martinez earned her BS in Electrical Engineering at Ohio University in Athens, OH, and completed dental school at The Ohio State University. Her pediatric dental residency was completed at the University of Toledo. In her spare time, Dr. Martinez can be found spending time with her husband and young son working on their home in Grove City. She loves cooking, trying new restaurants, and riding her peloton. Dr. Martinez is also passionate about giving back to the community and participates yearly in ‘Give Kids a Smile’ and ‘Magical Moments’ fundraisers.
