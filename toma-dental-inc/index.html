Dr. Silvia Sanchez Toma studied at Florida International University in Miami, Florida where she obtained a B.S. in Biology. She completed her dental school training at the Tufts University School of Dental Medicine and received her DMD degree in 1993. She also was awarded the Hanau "Best of the Best" Prosthodontics award in 1993 by her professors.

She is currently licensed in California and Florida. She has taken numerous continuing education courses in Cosmetic Dentistry and Orthodontics. Dr. Silvia Sanchez Toma is a member of the American Dental Association, the California Dental Association, and the San Diego County Dental Society.

Her hobbies include participating in triathlons, yoga, cooking, and spending time with family and friends.
