IMPLANT SUPPORTED

Implants

At Remedy Dental Studio we use the finest quality dental implants to ensure optimum results, every time. Our Implantology department has been developed over a long period of time and is founded on the perfect combination of our experience and expertise and the latest technology. We constantly update and review the technology and equipment we use to ensure that our clients receive only the best service and products and we strive to remain at the forefront of the dental implant industry.

Benefits of ordering custom abutments through Remedy Dental Studio include:

  • CAD/CAM designed by expert implant dental technicians.
  • Cost effective, high end solution.
  • Available in full titanium or hybrid (zirconia top with titanium interface).
  • Fabricated from precision turned blanks for an intimate fit every time.
  • We can send you a video preview of your case so you can approve it before it is fabricated.
