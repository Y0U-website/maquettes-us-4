Early Orthodontic Treatment for Children

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends children have their first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early treatment allows us to identify potential issues before they become severe, preventing more complex problems later in life. Our gentle, child-friendly approach makes orthodontic care a positive experience for young patients.

It’s never too late to achieve the smile you deserve. Our adult orthodontic services include a range of discreet and efficient treatment options, from clear aligners to metal braces .
