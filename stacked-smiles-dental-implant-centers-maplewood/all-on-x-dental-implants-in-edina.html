What EXACTLY is Teeth in a Day?

Also known as All-on-4®, Teeth in a Day is a specific dental implant procedure that replaces an entire arch of teeth with as few as four implants.

Patients who should consider Teeth in a Day typically fall into one of the following categories.

1. Someone currently suffering with a full, conventional, removable denture.

2. Someone who has been told that they may need to remove all or most of their teeth and will be headed toward a removable denture.

3. Anyone missing more than a few teeth in one arch and suffering from a bacterial infection (periodontitis) or decay.

Teeth in a Day is a revolutionizing treatment concept that expedites and simplifies your treatment cost and treatment time. At Stacked Smiles we believe it is often the best option for patients looking for a fast, effective, and hassle-free treatment.

To be successful, this treatment must follow specific treatment fundamentals. This is not simply putting in 4 implants and a bridge. It should not be trusted to just any dentist. You should research the experience level of the providers who you may choose.

If you have been told that you are not a candidate for dental implants, then do not despair. Dr. Sauza and Stacked Smiles offer hope for many who were previously told they can not have any treatment other than a denture. Even those who have worn dentures for a long time and whose bone might be on the thin side. Teeth in a Day has made it possible for even these patients to have permanent teeth again.

What this means for you is a minimally invasive, shorter surgery. Teeth in a Day is more cost effective than when dentists had to use multiple, individual implants for a full-arch of teeth.

“All on 4” and “Teeth in a Day” are terms used to describe a procedure that allows patients to receive replacement teeth in just one day. You may hear of other versions of this procedure such as “Teeth in an Hour,“ “Dental Implants in One Day,“ “Teeth In One Day,“ “Teeth In One Visit.” While they go by many names, all refer to a one-day implant treatment, which restores a full arch of replacement teeth.

Dentures vs. Teeth in a Day Dental Implants​

Traditional Dentures

  • Dentures are bulky. Traditional dentures cover the roof of your mouth, where many of our taste buds are, interfering with your ability to fully taste foods.
  • They tend to slip out of place. Dentures are only held in place by your mouth’s natural suction, so often messy adhesives are necessary to keep them in place.
  • Partially restore chewing ability.
  • Dentures sit on top of your gums. This can lead to rubbing and irritation. Worse, it can increase the speed at which your jawbone erodes.
  • Dentures do nothing to promote healthy bone growth. Only a tooth root or something inserted into the jawbone can keep your jawbone ample and healthy.

Teeth in a Day Dental Implants

  • All-on-4 hybrids are small and lightweight. Dentures cover the roof of your mouth, a dental implant hybrid is just the teeth and gums .
  • Dental implants are the longest lasting, most comfortable treatment option. Implants have the best long-term prognosis of any prosthetic lasting 20+ years 98 % of the time.
  • They essentially become new teeth and you clean them exactly the same way. No hassle with taking them in and out.
  • 95 % chewing ability restored. Enjoy all your favorite foods again!
  • Dental implants are implanted into your jawbone. Implants become a part of your body and fit in. your mouth as comfortably as natural teeth.
  • Dentures do nothing to promote healthy bone growth. The implant replaces your tooth root so that your body continues sending signals to keep growing bone there. This keeps your facial structure intact and more youthful looking.
