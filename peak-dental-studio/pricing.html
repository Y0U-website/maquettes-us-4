Pricing — FAQs

Peak operates on pay-per-case pricing. No subscription. No monthly minimum. No long-term contract. You receive a fee schedule on request and pay only for the cases you submit.

Subscription labs like Dandy require a $1,000–$2,500 monthly minimum spend regardless of case volume. Peak charges per case only. Practices submitting variable volume typically pay less with Peak. See our Dandy review for the full comparison.

Standard cases include prepaid UPS 2nd Day Air return shipping. Rush turnaround and overnight shipping are available on request.
