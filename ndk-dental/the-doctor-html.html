Meet the Doctors

Dr. Nic has dedicated her entire career to learning about teeth! Upon graduation from high school, Dr. Nic attended Luzerne County Community College in Pennsylvania to become a dental hygienist. Prior to attending dental school, she worked for 6 years as a hygienist while pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene Education from Bloomsburg University in Pennsylvania. She now resides in North Kingstown with her husband Gary and her 2 children.

A Personal Letter to My Future Patients:

A native of Upstate New York, she earned a Bachelor’s degree from the University at Buffalo and a Master’s in Public Health from the University at Albany.
