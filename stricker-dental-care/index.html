The practice

Our doctors are committed to delivering the very best modern dentistry has to offer for you and your family. As graduates of the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry, they prioritize continuing education and consistently integrate the latest advancements in techniques and technology into the practice.

Our philosophy is simple: provide advanced, biocompatible, and comfortable dental care tailored to each individual — all within a welcoming and thoughtfully designed environment.

We are proud to be a mercury-free practice, offering beautiful, natural-looking white restorations as a safe and aesthetic alternative to traditional silver fillings.

For your convenience, we offer evening and Saturday appointments to accommodate your schedule.

We invite you to experience dentistry delivered with precision, integrity, and genuine care — and to take the next step toward a healthier, more confident smile.

Your oral health is an essential part of your overall wellness. A healthy, confident smile enhances the way you eat, speak, and live each day. Through thoughtful preventive care and personalized treatment, we help you protect your smile — and support your long-term well-being.

We are dedicated to delivering exceptional dental care with a focus on your overall health and well-being. Our holistic approach emphasizes treatments that support both your oral and whole-body health, favoring natural, minimally invasive solutions whenever possible. At our practice, we believe that thoughtful, patient-centered care is always the better way.

At our practice, your health and safety are our highest priorities. We carefully review all materials used in your mouth and use safe, precise methods for the removal of old mercury-based fillings.
