Wisdom Teeth

Wisdom teeth, the last molars that usually come in during late teens or early twenties, can be different for everyone. Some people’s wisdom teeth come in fine, while others might face problems as they push through the gums at an angle. These issues can damage the jaw and nearby teeth and create spots for bacteria to gather, which are hard to clean. To avoid bigger problems, it’s sometimes best to take out these troublesome wisdom teeth. Your dentist can use x-rays to figure out if removal is needed.
