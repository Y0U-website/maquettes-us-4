Meet Dr. Jordan Whatcott

Dr. Jordan Whatcott is an Arizona native. He furthered his education at the University of Utah where he played baseball and discovered his passion for Dentistry.

After completing his Bachelor of Psychology, he continued his baseball career playing professionally with the Philidelphia Phillies! Dr. Jordan attended Roseman University of Health Sciences where he received multiple awards including The Quintessence Award for Achievement in Restorative Dentistry.

Dr. Jordan is dedicated to providing his patients with the most comprehensive and advanced dental care, including Basic Restorative Services excelling in Cosmetic Services, Endodontic, Oral Surgery including Wisdom Teeth, Implant Services, and SureSmile Orthodontic Aligners.

On the weekends you will find Dr. Jordan enjoying all things sports related and spending time with his Wife and 5 lovely children.
