About Trident Smiles

At Trident Smiles, our team is the heart of everything we do. From our skilled dental hygienists, Kristi and Sara, to our dedicated assistants, Tamesha and Maria, and our welcoming administrative team, Gina and Lisa, every member is committed to delivering exceptional care. Together, we create a friendly, supportive environment where patients feel comfortable, valued, and confident in their smile.

Comprehensive Care, Modern Comfort

At Trident Smiles, we’ve redefined what a dental visit should be. Under the leadership of Dr. Shivam Patel, our practice combines state-of-the-art technology with genuine, patient-focused care to create a dental home where every member of your family feels welcomed and understood.

We understand that finding a dentist you trust can be challenging. That’s why we’re committed to offering comprehensive services under one roof, from routine cleanings to advanced cosmetic procedures, clear aligners, and dental implants. Our approach centers on building long-term relationships with our patients, eliminating the stress of being referred between multiple practices.

What sets us apart is our integration of advanced technology like intraoral scanners, which replace uncomfortable impression methods with precise digital imaging. Whether you’re considering Invisalign clear aligners, exploring implant-supported dentures, or simply maintaining your family’s preventive care, we provide high-quality treatment in a warm, welcoming environment where anxiety-free dentistry is our priority.

A Dental Home Built Around You

Finding a dental practice you can count on for every stage of life should not be complicated. At Trident Smiles, we have built our practice around the idea that patients deserve consistent, comprehensive care, without the frustration of being sent elsewhere every time their needs change. From your first visit, we are equipped to handle it all in one comfortable, familiar setting on Statesville Blvd in Salisbury.

We also recognize that cost and convenience are real factors in the decisions patients make about their oral health. That is why we work with Delta Dental and offer flexible financing options through Cherry, CareCredit, and Alpheon, along with in-house payment plans designed to fit a range of budgets. Our goal is to make sure financial concerns never stand in the way of you getting the care you need and deserve.

Our practice proudly serves patients throughout Salisbury and the surrounding communities, including families, working adults, and long-time residents who have called this area home for years. Whether you are new to the area, searching for a new dental home, or simply ready to take a more proactive approach to your oral health, we welcome you. Trident Smiles is here to be the practice you rely on, not just for today, but for years to come.

His commitment to advanced education has continued well past graduation. Dr. Patel recently completed an intensive, hands-on implant training program in Colombia, working alongside oral surgeons and implantologists on complex cases over the course of an entire week. That experience led to a fellowship award from the American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics, and he is currently pursuing fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI). He is also certified in Invisalign clear aligner therapy and is a member of the American Dental Association.
