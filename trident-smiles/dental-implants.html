FAQs

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

With proper care, dental implants can last decades and often function for a patient’s lifetime, making them one of the most durable tooth replacement options available. The titanium post itself rarely needs replacement once it has fused with the jawbone, though the crown on top may eventually need updating after many years of normal wear. Routine dental visits allow us to monitor your implant and address any concerns early.

Does the Procedure Hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort during implant placement since the area is fully numbed with local anesthesia beforehand. Any soreness after the procedure is typically mild and manageable with over-the-counter pain relievers, similar to recovering from a tooth extraction. We discuss what to expect in detail during your consultation so there are no surprises on treatment day.
