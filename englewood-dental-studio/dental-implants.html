Evaluation for Dental Implants

Dental implant placement is a team effort between a periodontist and a restorative dentist. Dr. Goldweit performs the actual implant surgery, initial tooth extractions, and bone grafting if necessary. The restorative dentist (your dentist) fits and makes the permanent prosthesis. Your dentist will also make any temporary prosthesis needed during the implant process.

Dental Implants Procedures

Phase 1: Implant Placement

  • Implants are placed within the jaw bone
  • During the first 3–6 months, implants remain beneath the gum surface to gradually bond with the jaw bone
  • Patients can wear temporary dentures and eat a soft diet during this period
  • The restorative dentist designs the final bridgework or denture during this time

Phase 2: Attaching the Replacement Teeth

  • Once bonded, the surgeon uncovers the implants and attaches a small healing collar
  • An impression is taken for the new teeth
  • Posts or attachments are connected to the implants
  • Replacement teeth are made and fitted over the posts or attachments

Duration & Impact

  • The entire procedure typically takes 6–8 months
  • Most patients experience no disruption to their daily life

FAQs

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

If you have lost one or more teeth, dental implants may be an option. Englewood Dental Studio recommends a dental and radiographic examination along with a health history to determine your specific needs and whether implant treatment is appropriate.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are small titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as replacement tooth roots. Once the bone bonds with the implant, it provides a stable foundation for replacement teeth.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The entire dental implant procedure typically takes 6–8 months. During the first 3–6 months, the implants remain beneath the gum while they gradually bond with the jawbone.

Is dental implant placement painful?

The dental implant process involves surgical placement of the implants into the jawbone. Your treatment plan and specific needs are evaluated during your consultation so the dental team can help you understand the procedure and address your concerns.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Your dentist will provide guidance based on your individual implant treatment and replacement teeth. Maintaining good oral hygiene and following your dental team’s recommendations are important for protecting your oral health.

Can dental implants replace missing teeth?

Yes. Dental implants are designed to provide a foundation for replacement teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They can help restore your ability to eat and smile with confidence.

Can dental implants help preserve my facial structure?

Yes. Dental implants can help preserve facial structure by helping prevent the bone deterioration that can occur when teeth are missing.

What happens during a dental implant evaluation?

The evaluation includes a dental and radiographic examination and a review of your health history. Your specific needs and concerns will be discussed with Dr. Goldweit or an oral and maxillofacial surgeon referred by the practice.

What happens after the dental implant is placed?

After the implant has bonded with the jawbone, the surgeon uncovers it and attaches a healing collar. An impression is then taken, posts or attachments are connected, and the replacement teeth are made and fitted over the implants.
