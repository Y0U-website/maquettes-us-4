Why do I need Scaling and Root Planing?

When plaque builds up on your teeth, it can harden into tartar, leading to gum disease if not treated promptly. Scaling and Root Planing effectively removes this hardened plaque, cleaning below the gum line where regular brushing cannot reach. Dr. Keith Wolfenden, our experienced dentist, will determine whether this procedure is right for you during your comprehensive oral examination at Berthoud Dental Care.

How is Scaling and Root Planing performed at Berthoud Dental Care?

The Scaling and Root Planing procedure at our Berthoud location involves two main steps. First, we scale your teeth, meaning we scrape off the tartar from above and below your gum line. Next, we perform root planing to smooth out your teeth roots, helping your gums reattach to your teeth. At Berthoud Dental Care, Dr. Keith Wolfenden uses advanced techniques and tools to ensure your comfort during the procedure.
