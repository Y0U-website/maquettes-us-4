Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants

Choosing All-on-4 implants over traditional dentures or individual implants comes with several advantages:

  • Permanent Solution – No slipping or adhesives like traditional dentures.
  • Restores Full Functionality – Eat, speak, and smile with confidence.
  • Immediate Results – Many patients receive same-day temporary teeth , reducing downtime.
  • Preserves Jawbone Health – Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss.
  • Natural Look & Feel – Designed to mimic natural teeth for a seamless appearance.

The All-on-4 Dental Implant Procedure

Consultation & Treatment Planning

During your initial visit, Dr. Wolfenden will perform a detailed oral exam and take digital X-rays to assess your bone structure. He will discuss your treatment plan, including the design and customization of your new teeth.

Implant Placement Surgery

  • Four implants are placed into the jawbone—two in the front and two at an angle in the back for maximum support.
  • If possible, a temporary prosthetic arch is attached so you can leave with a full smile the same day.

Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next 3 to 6 months , the implants fuse with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation.

Final Restoration Placement

Once healing is complete, your permanent custom prosthetic is placed, providing a strong, long-lasting new smile.

All-on-4 vs. Traditional Dentures

If you&#x27;re considering All-on-4, here’s how it compares to traditional dentures:

If you currently wear dentures but want a more secure, natural-feeling solution , All-on-4 implants may be the perfect upgrade.

  • Stability – All-on-4 implants are securely fixed, while dentures can slip or move.
  • Jawbone Health – Implants help prevent bone loss, whereas dentures can contribute to bone resorption over time.
  • Comfort – All-on-4 feels more natural, while dentures may cause irritation or sore spots.
  • Lifespan – All-on-4 implants can last 20+ years , while dentures typically need replacement every 5-10 years .
  • Chewing Ability – All-on-4 allows for a stronger bite, while dentures limit chewing force.

How to Care for All-on-4 Dental Implants

  • Brush twice daily and floss carefully around the implants.
  • Schedule routine dental cleanings to keep your implants and gums healthy.
  • Avoid smoking, which can impact healing and long-term success.
  • Use a water flosser or special implant floss for thorough cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-4 Dental Implants

How long do All-on-4 dental implants last?

‍ With proper care, All-on-4 implants can last 20 years or more . The prosthetic arch may need replacement over time, but the implants themselves are designed for long-term use.

Is the procedure painful?

‍ The surgery is performed under local anesthesia or sedation, ensuring comfort. Most patients experience only mild soreness for a few days, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain medication. ‍

How soon can I eat after getting All-on-4 implants?

‍ Most patients can eat soft foods the same day but should follow a modified diet for several weeks as the implants heal. Once fully integrated, you can enjoy all your favorite foods. ‍

How do All-on-4 implants compare to traditional dental implants?

‍ Traditional implants require 6-8 implants per arch , while All-on-4 uses just four implants , reducing surgery time and cost. All-on-4 is also ideal for patients with bone loss , as it often eliminates the need for additional bone grafting.
