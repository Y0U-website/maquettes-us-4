Dental Implant Solutions for Every Level of Tooth Loss

Your treatment plan is based on your oral health, bone support, smile goals, preferred restoration and financial priorities.

Single-Tooth Implant

Replace one missing tooth with an implant, abutment and custom crown without relying on neighboring teeth for support.

Multiple Dental Implants

Replace several missing teeth with individual implants or an implant-supported bridge designed for strength and appearance.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

Use a strategic number of implants to support a full-arch fixed restoration for patients missing most or all teeth.

All-on-X Dental Implants

A personalized full-arch approach using the number and position of implants best suited to your anatomy and treatment plan.

Implant-Retained Dentures

Improve denture stability with implants that help reduce movement while eating and speaking.

Full-Mouth Reconstruction

Comprehensive care for patients with extensive tooth loss, failing dental work, severe wear or multiple oral health concerns.

What to Expect During Dental Implant Treatment

Consultation and Evaluation

We review your concerns, medical history, existing teeth, gums, bite and goals.

Imaging and Personalized Plan

Digital imaging helps evaluate bone, anatomy and possible implant positions. You receive treatment choices and estimated costs.

Implant Placement

Implants are placed according to the surgical plan. Sedation and comfort options may be discussed when appropriate.

Temporary Teeth When Appropriate

Some qualified patients may receive a temporary tooth or fixed provisional restoration on the same day.

Healing and Final Restoration

After healing and implant integration, the final crown, bridge, denture or full-arch restoration is delivered and adjusted.

Can You Get Teeth in One Day?

Some patients may qualify for same-day implant placement with temporary teeth. This is often called immediate loading or “teeth in a day.”

The same-day teeth are usually temporary. A final restoration is typically made after healing.
