What does getting dental implants involve?

At Green Dental, we begin dental implant procedures with a thorough examination and consultation . These examinations are done considering patients’ goals & expectations and oral health to make sure that dental implants are the right solution for you.

After defining the treatment plan, the next step then is the placement of the dental implant . This involves surgically inserting a titanium post into the jawbone , which serves as a stable foundation for the replacement tooth or teeth. Our dental team utilizes advanced techniques and technology to ensure precision and minimize discomfort during this procedure.

After the implant is placed, a healing period of several months is typically required. During this time, the implant fuses with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong and durable foundation for the replacement tooth or teeth.

Once the healing period is complete, the final restoration is created and attached to the dental implant. This can be a crown, bridge, or denture, depending on your specific needs. Our team takes great care in crafting restorations that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, ensuring a beautiful and functional smile.

Throughout the entire dental implant process, our team at Green Dental prioritizes your comfort and satisfaction. We provide detailed instructions for aftercare and are always available to address any questions or concerns you may have.

Few facts to know about Dental Implants:

A dental implant is the best solution for tooth replacement

Dental implants are the best solution for tooth replacement because they provide a strong foundation for permanent or removable replacement teeth that are made to match your natural teeth.

Unlike other options, such as dentures, dental implants don’t require special care or adhesives and they’re also less likely to cause irritation to your gums.

A dental implant is a painless procedure

Dental implants are a permanent solution

A dental implant is considered a permanent solution for your missing tooth replacement. The dental implant procedure involves placing a titanium screw in your jawbone and they can last a lifetime with proper care.

A dental implant is a precisely planned procedure

Dental implants are a precisely planned procedure, so you don’t have to worry about it. The first step is to consult with your dentist to see if you are a candidate for dental implants. Once it is determined that you are a good candidate, the next step is to have a consultation with an oral surgeon.

The oral surgeon will develop a treatment plan for you during this consultation. This treatment plan will take into account your overall health, the condition of your teeth and gums, and your personal preferences.

Once the treatment plan is developed, the next step is to have the implant surgery. This surgery is usually done under local anesthesia in an outpatient setting.

There is minimal discomfort after the dental implant procedure

There is minimal discomfort after the dental implant procedure. Most patients report that the procedure is much less painful than they had anticipated. Recovery is typically quick and easy, with patients being able to return to their normal activities within a few days.

Dental implants can be performed with local anesthesia

Dental implants are a popular and effective way to replace missing teeth. They can be performed with local anesthesia, which is a big advantage for many patients. The procedure is relatively simple and has a high success rate.

Dental implants are covered in most medical insurance policies

You may have heard that dental implant surgery may cost you a lot. That may be true because of the process involved in dental implant surgery.

However, the good news is that most medical insurance companies cover dental implant costs. So you don’t have to worry about the high cost of dental implants.

Talk to your nearby dentist to check if your medical plan includes dental implant costs or not.

A dental implant is a safe procedure

A dental implant is a safe procedure because it is a titanium screw that is placed in the jawbone and fuses with the bone over time.

The implant acts as a replacement for the root of a tooth and can be used to support a crown, bridge, or denture.

Reasons why Dental Implants are better than Dentures:

Dental implants have become one of the most popular and preferred dental treatments in recent years. They are used to replace missing or damaged teeth and provide a range of benefits that make them superior to traditional dentures.

In this blog, we’ll discuss the top 14 reasons why dental implants are better than dentures.

#1 Longevity

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with proper care and maintenance, while dentures may need to be replaced every few years.

#2 Comfort

Dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone, providing a stable and secure foundation for replacement teeth. This means that they are more comfortable to wear than dentures, which can slip and cause discomfort.

#3 Improved speech

Because dental implants are anchored to the jawbone, they allow for clearer speech than dentures, which can shift and cause slurring or mumbling.

#4 Natural look and feel

Dental implants are made to look and feel like natural teeth, whereas dentures can look and feel artificial.

#5 Improved chewing ability

Dental implants function like natural teeth, allowing for improved chewing ability and better digestion.

#6 No dietary restrictions

With dental implants, there are no dietary restrictions, unlike dentures, which can limit what foods you can eat.

#7 Bone preservation

Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss and preserving the facial structure, while dentures can accelerate bone loss.

#8 Easy maintenance

Dental implants can be cared for just like natural teeth, with regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups. Dentures, on the other hand, require special cleaning solutions and adhesives.

#9 Increased self-esteem

Dental implants can restore your smile, giving you more confidence and improved self-esteem. Dentures, on the other hand, can be embarrassing and cause social anxiety.

#10 Better oral health

Dental implants do not require any alteration of adjacent teeth, which can compromise their integrity. This means that they help to maintain better overall oral health, unlike dentures.

#11 Improved facial aesthetics

Dental implants can improve facial aesthetics by filling out sunken cheeks and preventing sagging skin. Dentures, on the other hand, can cause a “sunken-in” appearance.

How do I take care of my dental implants?

  • Brush and floss your implants just like you would your natural teeth. Be sure to use a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste to avoid damaging the implants.
  • Visit your dentist regularly for checkups and cleanings.
  • Avoid chewing hard foods or using your teeth as tools, as this can damage the implants.
  • If you play contact sports, wear a mouthguard to protect your implants.
