What are printed veneers?

A printed veneer is a thin cover made just for your tooth and placed right over it. Because it is designed and printed for you, it fits perfectly and looks natural.

PERL vs. traditional veneers

Traditional veneers usually mean grinding healthy teeth away for good, weeks of waiting, and $25,000 to $60,000. PERL goes right over your teeth, in days, at about half the price.

PERL vs. composite bonding

Hand applied bonding stains, chips, and is shaped by hand. PERL is more stain resistant and made as one matched set, so your whole smile looks uniform.

Who PERL is built for

PERL printed veneers are ultra-thin and minimally invasive, which makes them a beautiful fit for many smiles and the wrong tool for a few. Here is an honest look, so your consultation can confirm what is right for you.

The consultation exists to tell you honestly whether PERL is the right fit, and what your options are if it is not.

  • Want a thin, natural-looking enhancement rather than a dramatic, bulky makeover
  • Have minor cosmetic concerns: mild discoloration, small gaps, or slightly uneven edges
  • Have healthy natural teeth and gums, with no active decay or failing fillings
  • Have reasonably straight teeth and a stable bite, or wear a night guard if you grind
  • Want to refresh older composite veneers or bonding
  • Prefer a conservative option that preserves your natural enamel
  • Have dark or severely discolored teeth that ultra-thin veneers may not fully mask
  • Have large fillings, crowns, or significant existing work on the teeth in question
  • Have severe misalignment or large gaps that are better aligned first
  • Grind or clench heavily without protection
  • Have weak or damaged teeth that may need a crown rather than a veneer
  • Expect a complete change in tooth shape and color, like traditional cut-down veneers
