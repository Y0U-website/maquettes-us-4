Porcelain is the time-tested choice, layered by hand and bonded to lightly prepared teeth.

Each veneer is crafted one at a time. Most cases take two to three weeks across a few visits.

Bonding is fast and affordable for one tooth. For a full smile that stays consistent, PERL printed veneers are designed as one matched set.

Porcelain is the classic standard for a full smile. PERL printed veneers keep more of your real teeth and finish in days, not weeks.

Dr. Jeff Trembley is a Nashville cosmetic dentist and educator, emphasizing non-invasive smile makeovers, Printed PERL Veneers, and digital veneer workflows.
