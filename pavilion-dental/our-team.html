Meet Our Doctors

A mother-daughter duo dedicated to exceptional, heartfelt dental care

Dr. Amal Hanna, DDS

Dr. Amal Hanna is the heart and soul of Pavilion Dental. A highly accomplished dentist with over three decades of clinical experience, Dr. Hanna founded this practice with a vision to bring family-centered, comprehensive dental care to the Westminster community — and she has never stopped delivering on that promise.

Dr. Hanna’s approach to dentistry is both artful and deeply personal. She takes the time to truly understand each patient’s concerns, goals, and anxieties before recommending any treatment. Her patients consistently praise her calming chairside manner, meticulous attention to detail, and extraordinary ability to create beautiful, natural-looking results.

Beyond her technical excellence, Dr. Hanna is a lifelong learner. She regularly attends continuing education courses and professional seminars to stay at the forefront of dental innovation — ensuring her patients always have access to the very best techniques and technologies available. When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Hanna enjoys quality time with her family, traveling, and giving back to the local community through volunteer dental outreach programs.

Dr. Nadine Tawfik, RDH DDS

Dr. Nadine Tawfik brings a rare and immensely valuable dual perspective to Pavilion Dental — that of both a seasoned Registered Dental Hygienist (RDH) and a skilled Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS). This unique combination allows her to approach patient care with an exceptionally thorough understanding of oral health at every level.

Inspired by her mother’s lifelong dedication to the field, Nadine pursued her own path in dentistry with the same passion and unwavering commitment to excellence. Her training as a hygienist first gave her an intimate understanding of what it truly means to prioritize patient comfort and preventive care — values she carries into every appointment she conducts as a dentist.

Nadine is particularly beloved by patients who experience dental anxiety. Her gentle technique, unhurried pace, and thorough explanations help even the most nervous patients feel completely relaxed and confident in the dental chair. She has a special affinity for pediatric patients and is widely known for making children’s dental visits a positive, even enjoyable experience. Outside the office, Nadine is passionate about her hobbies including art, community health outreach, and spending meaningful time with family — values she and her mother have always shared.

Two Doctors. One Heart.

What makes Pavilion Dental truly special is the bond between Dr. Hanna and Nadine — a mother and daughter who not only share a profession, but share a calling. Their complementary expertise, deep mutual respect, and profound personal connection create a practice culture that patients feel the moment they walk through our door. At Pavilion Dental, you are never just a patient. You are family.
