About Pavilion Dental

A family-founded practice built on compassion, excellence, and genuine care

A Practice Built on Family Values .

Pavilion Dental was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that every patient deserves to be treated like family. Dr. Amal Hanna established this practice with a vision to bring compassionate, comprehensive dental care to the Westminster community — and that vision has guided every decision we’ve made since opening our doors.

What began as one dentist’s commitment to her community has grown into a thriving family practice, now shared with her daughter, Dr. Nadine Tawfik, RDH DDS. Together, this mother-daughter team brings decades of combined experience, a shared passion for excellence, and an unshakeable dedication to the patients who trust them with their smiles.

At Pavilion Dental, we take the time to listen. We explain your options clearly, involve you in every decision, and never rush your care. When you walk through our doors, you are not just another appointment on the schedule — you are a member of our dental family.

Our Core Values .

Six commitments that guide every visit, every treatment plan, and every conversation at Pavilion Dental.

Patient-First Care

Every decision we make is guided by what’s best for you. We listen carefully, answer every question, and never rush your care.

Clinical Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of dental practice, staying current with the latest techniques and technology.

Gentle & Compassionate

We understand dental anxiety is real. Our entire team is trained to make every visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Honest Communication

We believe in clear, transparent communication about your treatment options and costs — always, with no surprises.

Community Roots

We are proud to be part of the Westminster community and to serve families across generations with genuine warmth.

Lifelong Learning

Our doctors regularly attend continuing education courses to ensure you always have access to the very best care available.
