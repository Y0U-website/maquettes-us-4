Orthodontics

A straight smile is a healthy smile — and it’s never too late to achieve one. We offer modern orthodontic solutions for teens and adults alike. Our team will evaluate your bite and tooth alignment and recommend the most effective, comfortable treatment path for your unique needs.

Whether you’re looking for traditional braces or discreet clear aligners, we’ll guide you through the entire process from start to finish — including retainers and follow-up care to make sure your results last a lifetime.

Emergency Dental Care

Dental emergencies don’t wait for a convenient moment. Whether you’re dealing with a sudden toothache, a broken tooth, a lost crown, or a dental injury, Pavilion Dental is here to help you quickly and compassionately. Please call us right away — we do our best to accommodate same-day emergency appointments.

Our team is trained to handle dental emergencies with calm, efficiency, and genuine care. We’ll assess your situation, relieve your pain, and develop a clear plan for restoring your smile — all as quickly as possible so you can get back to your life.

  • Severe Toothache Relief
  • Cracked or Chipped Teeth
  • Knocked-Out Tooth
  • Lost Fillings or Crowns
  • Dental Abscess
  • Broken Dentures
  • Soft Tissue Injuries
  • Same-Day Appointments
  • Emergency Extractions
