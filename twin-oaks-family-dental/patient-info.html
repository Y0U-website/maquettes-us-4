Understanding Your Health

To provide safe and personalized dental care, we’ll take a few minutes to review your medical history. This helps us tailor your treatment and ensure your overall health is supported.

  • Current medications and dosages
  • Vitamins or supplements
  • Relevant health conditions
  • Allergies or sensitivities

Your Comprehensive Dental Exam

  • Full-mouth digital X-rays
  • A detailed look at your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw
  • Digital intraoral photos (so you can see what we see)
  • A gum health screening
  • A simple, non-invasive oral cancer screening

Creating Your Personalized Treatment Plan

Once your exam is complete, we’ll sit down and go over everything with you in plain, straightforward language. We’ll explain what we see, answer your questions, and talk through any treatment options.

Together, we’ll put together a custom treatment plan that fits:

  • Your oral health needs
  • Your goals
  • Your timeline
  • Your budget

Your Comfort Matters to Us

  • Cozy treatment rooms
  • Blankets, pillows, and headphones
  • Nitrous oxide (laughing gas)
  • Gentle, patient-first techniques
  • Modern technology for faster, easier care
  • A welcoming, judgment-free atmosphere

What to Bring to Your First Visit

  • Photo ID
  • Dental insurance card (if applicable)
  • List of medications
  • Any past dental X-rays or records (optional)
