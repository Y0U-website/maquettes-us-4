General Family Dentistry

Dr. Lin and his team want to make all your visits with us pleasant. When you’re with us, you are part of our extended family. Comprehensive Family Dental Treatments

We want to make it easy for you and your family to receive the dental treatments you need from a caring professional team you know and trust. Dr Lin and his team are able to provide you a wide range of general, preventative, cosmetic, and restorative services.

Cosmetic Dentistry for a Smile You Can’t Stop Smiling about

Not everyone was born with a celebrity-caliber smile—in fact, most celebrities weren’t either!—but with today’s modern cosmetic dentistry, everyone can have a stunning smile. Talk to Dr. Lin about options such as porcelain veneers and teeth whitening to get the smile of your dreams.

Emergency Dental Services When You Need Us

Although taking good care of your teeth on a regular basis minimizes the chance of emergencies, we know that they can still happen. If you find yourself with a tooth that has been chipped or knocked out or you are suffering from excruciating toothache pain, give us a call and we will get you in as soon as possible!
