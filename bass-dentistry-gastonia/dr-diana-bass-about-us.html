Our Promise​

Who We Are

We are a family dentist in Gastonia, providing gentle, compassionate care for patients of all ages. Led by Dr. Diana Bass, a second-generation dentist, our dedicated team prioritizes your smile and overall dental health.

What we do​

Our services include teeth cleanings, cavity fillings, dental evaluations, x-rays, tooth extractions, cosmetic restorations, teeth whitening, and Invisalign. We cater to the dental needs of your entire family, regardless of age.

what we believe​

Dental visits should be positive and result-oriented. We strive to create a comfortable environment while providing comprehensive care and education on the latest dental practices.

Family And Cosmetic Dentist​ Dr. Diana​ Bass Dr. Diana Bass, a Gastonia native, returned to her hometown to pursue her dental career closer to family. As the daughter of a dentist, she learned the importance of a healthy smile early on. Dr. Bass is married and has two young daughters.

She holds degrees in Biology and Psychology from Duke University and earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery with honors from UNC Chapel Hill. After completing a two-year General Practice Residency at UNC Hospital, where she served as Chief Resident, Dr. Bass worked as a general dentist in the Research Triangle. There, she held full surgical privileges at WakeMed Raleigh, providing care for patients with severe anxiety, special needs, and medical issues.

Dr. Bass is experienced in aesthetic and functional restorative dentistry, crown and bridge restorations, oral surgery, and root canals. She values patient education and collaborates with patients to restore their smiles. Passionate about community service, Dr. Bass participates in dental projects like the Missions of Mercy Dental Clinics through the North Carolina Dental Society. She is a member of the First United Methodist Church and loves serving as a dentist in her hometown community.

Let’s get to the Root What Patients Ask Us Most Who is Dr. Diana Bass, and what is her approach to dentistry? Dr. Diana Bass is a Gastonia native who returned home to practice dentistry and serve the community she knows and loves. With a strong family background in dentistry and advanced training from Duke University and UNC Chapel Hill, she brings both skill and compassion to her work. Dr. Bass takes a patient-centered approach focused on education, collaboration, and gentle, comprehensive care, helping patients of all ages feel comfortable while restoring healthy, confident smiles.

Bass Dentistry is built around comfort, trust, and personalized attention. From the friendly, experienced team to a welcoming environment designed to reduce anxiety , every aspect of care is patient-focused. The practice combines modern technology with a gentle, family-oriented approach to make dental visits easy and positive.

Bass Dentistry is both a family and general dental practice . We provide comprehensive care for patients of all ages, from children’s checkups to adult preventive, restorative, and cosmetic treatments. Our team focuses on personalized, gentle care, making it easy for the whole family to maintain healthy, confident smiles in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

We provide comprehensive care for patients of all ages. Services include preventive cleanings and exams, restorative treatments such as fillings and crowns, and cosmetic dentistry, including teeth whitening, veneers, and Invisalign.
