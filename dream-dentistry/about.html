Jenny - Dental Assistant

Jenny is a wonderful new addition to the Dream Dentistry team! She grew up in Kelso and graduated from Kelso High School in 2001. She began her career in dental assisting at age 21 and has been passionate about it ever since. What she loves most about her work is calming nervous patients and helping transform their smiles. Jenny and her husband Ian are kept busy by their three daughters, Kallie, Peyton, and Katelynn. In her free time, she enjoys camping and spending time outdoors with her family -- and in the summer, you can almost always find her out on the water.

Jaylee - Registered Dental Hygienist

Jaylee graduated from the dental hygiene program at Clark College in 2022 and has been an integral part of the Dream Dentistry team for the past 4 years. She brings a gentle touch and genuine enthusiasm to every appointment, and her patients’ comfort and confidence are always her top priority. Jaylee lives in Longview with her partner and their two dogs, Margo and Charlie, who keep life fun and full outside of the office. She is known for building real connections with her patients and has a gift for making even the most anxious visitors feel right at home.
