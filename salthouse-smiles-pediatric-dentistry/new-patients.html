At Salthouse Smiles, we are excited to welcome new families to our practice. We are dedicated to providing exceptional pediatric dental care in a warm, nurturing environment. From your first visit, you’ll see why families in Des Peres and the St. Louis area trust us with their children’s smiles.

What To Expect

Your First Visit

We understand that a child’s first visit to the dentist can be exciting but also a little daunting—for both child and parent! We strive to make this experience as welcoming and stress-free as possible. When you arrive, you’ll be greeted by our friendly staff who love working with kids and are trained in making dental visits fun. We’ll give you a brief tour of our office, explain the procedures in kid-friendly language, and answer any questions you may have.

Comprehensive Dental Care

Our initial exams are thorough and tailored to the needs of children and adolescents, including patients with special needs. We use the latest dental technologies to ensure the best care, including preventive treatments like cleanings and sealants, and advanced procedures when necessary.

Financial Information

We accept a wide range of dental insurance plans and are committed to helping you understand and maximize your benefits. For our uninsured patients or those seeking additional financial assistance, we offer flexible payment options and our Salthouse Smiles Kids Club and will guide you through the available solutions to ensure your child receives the best care without financial stress.

Specialized Care for Patients with Special Needs

Personalized Care for Every Patient

At Salthouse Smiles in St. Louis, MO, we recognize our pediatric patients with special needs require individualized dental care tailored to their specific conditions. We are committed to providing this personalized care to patients of all ages, whether they are children, adolescents, or adults with special healthcare needs.

Facility Accommodations

Our office is designed to ensure accessibility and comfort for all patients. Features include wheelchair-friendly spaces and special care suites that are fully equipped with the necessary tools to ensure a safe and pleasant visit for everyone.

Our Commitment

We uphold the principle that insurance coverage should not dictate our preventative and treatment recommendations, which are always based on the latest evidence and literature. We ensure that families are fully informed of all available options and empower them to make the best decisions for their loved ones’ dental health.

Ready to Join Our Family?

If you live in the St. Louis area and you’re ready to experience a different kind of pediatric dentistry, we invite you to contact us to schedule your child’s first visit. We look forward to welcoming you to the Salthouse Smiles family and being a part of your child’s journey to a healthy, beautiful smile!
