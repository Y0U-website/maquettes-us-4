DENTAL SERVICES

Cosmetic Dentistry

For those concerned about the state of their smiles, our team at DOCS Dental offers an array of services ranging from partial and complete implants, veneers, and teeth-whitening treatment to dramatically correct discoloration and staining.

General Dentistry

At every checkup, your hygienist (or dentist) will check each of your teeth, plus your gums, tongue, and cheeks. During cleanings, we’ll work along and below the gumline to remove built-up plaque and tartar. The dentist will check X-rays to identify any potential problems, like infections, cavities between teeth, impacted wisdom teeth, and more.

Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies come up, and some require immediate treatment. It’s important that you contact DOCS Dental after-hours line for consultation. For unbearable pain and profuse bleeding, go to the ER.

Pediatric Dentistry

Our team will treat your child’s teeth and provide procedures ranging from routine to specialized treatment. Our team of experienced doctors regularly perform children’s dental procedures, such as cleanings, braces, and various other specialized procedures.

Dentures

DOCS Dental is here to bring your smile back from broken, chipped, or missing teeth. We offer beautiful dentures to restore the appearance of your smile and maintain the functionality of your teeth.

Teeth Whitening

For those concerned about the state of their smiles, our team at DOCS Dental offers teeth-whitening treatments to dramatically correct discoloration and staining.

Invisalign ®

Our team will identify pre-existing characters of your mouth and plan a treatment leading to a great smile. We offer a discreet way to straighten teeth through Invisalign ® , without having to deal with the traditional metal braces and their accompanying treatments.

Dental Implants

DOCS Dental is here to bring your smile back from broken, chipped, or missing teeth. We offer beautiful implants to restore the appearance of your smile and maintain the functionality of your teeth.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

DOCS Dental is here to bring your smile back from broken, chipped, or missing teeth. With All-on-4 Dental Implants, restore the appearance of your smile and maintain the functionality of your teeth.

Orthodontics

Our team will identify pre-existing characters of your mouth and plan a treatment leading to a great smile. We also offer a discreet way to straighten teeth through Invisalign ® , without having to deal with the traditional metal braces and their accompanying treatments.

Braces

Our team will identify pre-existing characters of your mouth and plan a treatment leading to a great smile. We offer teeth straightening with traditional braces.

Wisdom Tooth Extractions

Wisdom teeth are often removed because they can cause a variety of dental problems, including pain, infection, swelling, and damage to neighboring teeth. Our team will remove any wisdom teeth that may need to be taken out.

TMJ/TMD Treatment

DOCS Dental includes diagnosis and treatment of TMJ/TMD, a common jaw disorder that can result in symptoms ranging from headaches to tooth damage. Neuromuscular dentistry can be used for treatment as well as other functional disorders of the jaw.

Oral Surgery

Our procedures will help fix your dental pain. These services include extraction (moderate to difficult, including wisdom teeth), reconstructive dental oral surgery, and placing dental implants.
