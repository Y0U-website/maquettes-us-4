How to find our suite:

All appointments take place at our location on Broadway.

Our entrance is located at 1881 Broadway, between West 62nd and West 63rd Streets on the Upper West Side in Manhattan. Please note that the corner entrance is for the AAA office and is not our entrance.

To enter the building, use the 1881 Broadway door and buzz “CPW Dentistry” using the bottom buzzer. When the door unlocks, the small door icon on the buzzer will turn green.

Once inside, take the elevator to the 4th floor. Central Park West Dentistry occupies the entire floor, so you’ll know you’re in the right place as soon as you arrive.
