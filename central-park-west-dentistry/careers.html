  • We go above and beyond. People who go above and beyond for patients and fellow team members. They are driven, and never give up, putting their best foot forward every day they come to work. Dreaming big-then making it happen, and always delivering more than what’s expected. They focus on what’s best for the team, and eliminate personal agendas.
  • We celebrate. Those who celebrate find joy in their work, and in their coworkers. They thank and praise others for a job well done. They contribute to a positive environment, and uplift others in times of frustration and challenge. They believe in the value of what they do, and celebrate all victories, both big and small.
  • We are accountable. A person who is accountable is reliable, honest, and keeps their promises. They use good judgment when making decisions. They take pride in their work—taking the win when they succeed and learning from mistakes when they happen. Accountable people are open and receptive to feedback, are slow to criticize others, and would never throw a teammate under the bus. They’re humbly confident, good listeners, and passionate about delivering excellent work and achieving goals.
  • We embrace growth. People who are passionate about growth believe that big things are possible. They are not satisfied with the status quo. They are excited about education, and are always looking for ways to improve themselves personally and professionally. People who grow believe we are in a constant state of progress; they take chances and continue to set the bar higher. They are not resistant to change or new ideas because they find excitement in a challenge, and fulfillment in becoming stronger, wiser, and more efficient.
  • We enrich lives. Each team member must connect to a greater sense of purpose in the work that they do. Enriching the lives of the people we interact with each day is at the foundation of our business, and those who are successful in our organization find ways to practice this model on a regular basis. Those who are committed to a spirit of enrichment are happy for the success of their coworkers, are good communicators, and treat others with kindness and respect. They listen well and have a caring, compassionate heart.

Careers FAQ

  • What roles does CPW Dentistry typically hire for? We regularly hire for clinical and front-office roles including dental hygienists, dental assistants, treatment coordinators, and dentists. Check our Open Roles section above for current openings, or check back. We’re a growing practice!
  • What’s the interview and hiring process like? After you apply, if you meet the requirements, you’ll chat with our hiring managers for an initial interview. Select candidates are then invited to the office for a paid working interview, where you’ll meet the team and get a firsthand feel for what it’s like to work at CPW Dentistry. We love this step because it helps make sure the fit is right for everyone, you included.
  • What are CPW Dentistry’s hours, and will I need to work weekends? We’re open Monday through Friday from 8am to 8pm and Saturday through Sunday from 9am to 6pm. Typically people do work a weekend day, but this can vary depending on candidate preferences and the needs of the practice. Weekend shifts come with additional compensation.
  • Since the office is open from 8am to 8pm, will I be expected to work a 12-hour shift? No. Our hours reflect the practice’s full span of operation, not any one person’s schedule. Shifts are staggered across the team so no one is working the entire 8am–8pm window. You’ll have a defined shift within those hours.
  • What benefits do full-time employees receive? Full-time team members receive subsidized health, vision, and life insurance, paid time off, covered dental care, and additional perks like a 401(k) with employer contribution, commuter benefits, and an Employee Assistance Program. Full details are listed on each role’s job posting.
  • How do I apply? Click Apply Now on the job posting that matches your interest, or reach out to us directly at careers@cpwdentistry.com if you don’t see an open role that fits. We’re always happy to hear from strong candidates.
