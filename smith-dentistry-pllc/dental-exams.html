Dental Exam Questions & Answers

The general recommendation is to schedule dental exams every six months. However, the frequency may vary based on your individual oral health needs. If you have a history of dental issues, such as gum disease, cavities, or other risk factors like smoking or diabetes, Dr. Grant may recommend more frequent visits. Conversely, if your oral health is consistently excellent, Dr. Grant may suggest an annual exam. It’s important to follow your Smith Dentistry teams’s advice to ensure your oral health is properly monitored and maintained.

Dental exams are generally not painful. The examination itself involves visual inspection and gentle probing of the teeth and gums, which should cause minimal discomfort. If X-rays are taken, you may feel slight pressure from the bitewing or sensor, but it is usually brief and not painful. The professional cleaning may involve some discomfort, especially if there is significant tartar buildup or if your gums are sensitive. However, any discomfort is typically mild and can be managed with local anesthesia or numbing gels if necessary.

Most dental insurance plans cover routine dental exams and cleanings, usually every six months. This coverage often includes the cost of X-rays, which are typically taken once a year or as needed. However, the extent of coverage can vary depending on your specific insurance plan. It’s advisable to check with your insurance provider to understand what is covered and if there are any out-of-pocket costs. Your dental office can also help you verify coverage and understand any co-pays or deductibles that may apply.
