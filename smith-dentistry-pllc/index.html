Dental Cleanings Regular dental cleanings are a vital part of maintaining oral health and it’s important to understand the procedure and the benefits. Dental cleanings, also known as prophylaxis, are more than just a routine; they are essential for preventing dental issues like cavities, gum disease, and bad breath, ensuring your smile remains healthy and bright.

The next step is scaling , where the hygienist uses specialized tools to remove plaque and tartar (hardened plaque) from the surfaces of your teeth and below the gumline. Plaque is a sticky film of bacteria that constantly forms on your teeth, and if not removed, it can harden into tartar, which can only be removed by a dental professional.
