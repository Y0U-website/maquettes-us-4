Welcome to AZ Dental Office Laguna Hills — a modern, patient-first practice where every smile matters. Whether you’re coming in for a routine cleaning or a full smile transformation, you’ll always leave knowing exactly…

We opened AZ Dental Office Laguna Hills because we believe every patient deserves honest care, advanced technology, and a team that genuinely cares about your outcome.

AZ Dental Office Laguna Hills offers everything your family needs to maintain a healthy, confident smile — all in one convenient Laguna Hills location.

Emergency Dental Care When something unexpected happens, we work hard to get you seen quickly and out of discomfort fast.

I earned my dental degree from the prestigious Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry at USC — one of the nation’s top dental schools. But my journey into dentistry was always about more than a degree.

Dentistry sits at the intersection of medicine and art, and that combination has always fascinated me. More than anything, I love seeing a patient walk out of my office with a restored smile and renewed confidence. That transformation — from hiding your smile to showing it off — never gets old.

Outside the office, you’ll find me at the gym or on the soccer field. I bring that same energy and commitment to every patient I treat.

From your first phone call to the moment you leave our office, we make every step of your visit as smooth and comfortable as possible.
