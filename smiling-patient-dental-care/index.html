Hi there! I’m Rizina Hakimiyan - but most of my patients call me Dr. Rizina. I truly believe a healthy smile can change how you feel about yourself and I’m here to make that journey as comfortable and empowering as possible.

My path into dentistry began with shadowing, then working as a dental assistant for my longtime family dentist in Skokie, Illinois. I quickly fell in love when I realized dentistry combines many of my passions - art, science, using my hands to create something both functional and beautiful, all while caring for others.

After earning my bachelor’s degree in Biological Sciences, I went on to complete my Doctorate of Dental Medicine at the University of Illinois at Chicago College of Dentistry.

From the beginning, caring for patients has been central to my "why" and the foundation of my practice. I know firsthand how intimidating the dental chair can feel, so I strive to make every visit as gentle and stress-free as possible. My goal is for you to leave each appointment feeling a little more confident and a lot more at ease.

I’m an active member of the American Dental Association, Illinois State Dental Society, and Chicago Dental Society, and I stay up to date on the latest in dental care through continuing education. In addition to general dentistry, I’m certified by the American Academy of Facial Esthetics to provide Botox and dermal fillers—not only for aesthetics, but also for TMD and headache relief.

My husband and I recently bought our first house so our dog, SoDi, could finally have a yard! When I’m not in the office, you’ll likely find me on a family walk, trying out new recipes, or teaching a hot yoga class.
