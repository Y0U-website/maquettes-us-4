When Should You See A General Dentist?

Most patients should visit a general dentist every six months for a routine exam and cleaning. Don’t wait for your next scheduled visit, though, if you notice tooth pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, bleeding or swollen gums, persistent bad breath, or visible changes to your teeth — early care keeps small problems from becoming bigger ones.

General & Cosmetic Dentistry FAQs

What’s the difference between general and cosmetic dentistry? General dentistry focuses on the health of your teeth and gums through exams, cleanings, and restorative treatments such as fillings and crowns. Cosmetic dentistry improves how your smile looks with treatments like whitening, bonding, and veneers. Many West Covina patients combine both for a smile that’s healthy and beautiful.

How often should I see a general dentist? Most patients benefit from a cleaning and exam every six months. If you have gum disease or other ongoing concerns, Dr. Motamedi may recommend more frequent visits based on your individual oral health.

Are cosmetic dental treatments safe? Yes. Cosmetic treatments such as bonding, whitening, and veneers are safe when performed by a trained dentist. During your consultation, we review your dental history and goals to recommend the safest, most effective option for you.

Does insurance cover general or cosmetic dentistry? Most PPO dental insurance plans cover some or all preventive and restorative treatments, such as cleanings, exams, and fillings. Cosmetic treatments are typically not covered by insurance, but our team can help you understand your benefits and payment options.

Can I combine general and cosmetic treatments in one visit? In many cases, yes. During your exam, Dr. Motamedi can discuss combining restorative and cosmetic treatments into a single, efficient plan that fits your schedule and goals.
