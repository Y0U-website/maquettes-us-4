The practice

Visit our office for a consultation where our specially trained pediatric dentists will examine your child and provide a recommendation. Please allow 60 minutes for the initial consultation, as we treat every patient like a member of our family, and this time ensures that we will be able to formulate a plan that is right for your child.

Your next visit is the treatment visit, where our doctors perform the tie release. There will be an opportunity to ask any final questions, and our doctors will fully cover post-treatment care with you. You can take your child home safely immediately after the treatment.

The final step is your child’s post-treatment follow-up visits to our office, where our doctors will ensure that everything is healing well and everything is functioning properly. Our team is here to support your family with 1 and 2-week follow-up care visits and is always available for any questions or concerns.

As “Boy Moms” and board-certified pediatric dentists, Dr. Jackie and Dr. Katie both have had personal experiences with tethered oral tissues in their own families. These experiences at home have made them uniquely aware of how ties can impact feeding, speech, facial and dental growth, and airway development.

Dr. Jackie and Dr. Katie founded Tied together because they believe in a collaborative and functional approach to treating tongue and lip ties. They wish to provide a safe space for families to ask questions and receive information on the diagnosis, management, and treatment of tethered oral tissues. They are so thankful for this opportunity to be part of your child’s care.
