Meet Dr. Alicia Yim

Experience. Integrity. Excellence.

Dr. Alicia Yim was born in Alexandria Hospital and grew up in Northern Virginia . She attended Virginia Commonwealth University where she earned her degree in Fashion Merchandising and continued her post-baccalaureate studies at George Mason University . Later she attended the Maurice H. Kornberg School of Dentistry at Temple University in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. She has practiced dentistry in Pennsylvania, Maryland, the District of Columbia, and Virginia .

Dr. Yim enjoys advancing her experience and knowledge of dentistry through continuing education courses, volunteer events, and mission trips . She especially enjoys challenging cosmetic smile transformations as well as full-mouth rehabilitation and reconstruction .

Dr. Yim places a strong emphasis on the essential role that dental and oral health plays in the overall health of the patient and spends her time educating her patients on the importance of preventive dentistry.

Dr. Yim currently resides in Arlington and outside of work she can be found with her rescue dog, Rita, exploring hiking trails and frequenting local dog parks . She has a deep appreciation for all things artistic and feels blessed to live in such a culturally rich area.
