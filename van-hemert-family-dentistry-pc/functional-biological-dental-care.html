Functional + Biological Dental Treatments

At Van Hemert Family Dentistry, we take a more thoughtful, whole-body approach to dental care. That means evaluating the materials, methods, and impact of every treatment—reducing toxins, supporting healing, and considering how oral health connects to overall wellness. From safe materials to airway assessments and pediatric development, our care is always intentional.

Biocompatible Materials

We offer materials that are safe, non-toxic, and compatible with your body—including the option for biocompatibility testing—while avoiding materials and chemicals that may disrupt healing or health.

Safe Amalgam Removal

Following SMART-inspired protocols, we remove mercury fillings using protective techniques to minimize exposure for both patients and our team—creating a cleaner, safer foundation for future restorations.

Airway Assessments

Breathing impacts everything—from sleep to focus to heart health. We evaluate airway structure in kids and adults to catch restrictions early and guide treatment that supports proper function and overall wellbeing.

Pediatric Mouth & Facial Development

Early intervention matters. We support healthy jaw growth, facial balance, and oral function through gentle, airway-focused pediatric care—helping children develop strong foundations for lifelong health and confident smiles.

Whole-Body Root Cause Approach

We don’t just fix symptoms—we look deeper. Whether it’s chronic inflammation, clenching, decay, or imbalances revealed through saliva testing, we help uncover and address the root cause through personalized care and functional support.

Ozone & Natural Therapies

We integrate natural therapies—like ozone —for their antimicrobial, healing-supporting benefits. These options align with your body’s rhythms, reduce the need for chemicals, and promote faster, more comfortable healing.
