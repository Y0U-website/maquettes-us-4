About Us

Our practice is conveniently located at 1827 Memorial Drive . Our doors opened for business in May of 1979 with Dr. Leon Stanislav. Dr. Stanislav practiced with his daughter Dr. Kristy until the end of 2022 when he retired. Since then, we have a new name (The Dental Cottage, PLC), but continue to provide the same exceptional care you’ve seen for over 40 years.

Dr. Kristy and her team remain committed to continuing education and staying abreast of dental advancements in materials and techniques. Also, all of our clinical team members are certified in handling emergencies. We emphasize prevention because we know it is more important to prevent a cavity than fill it. When treatment is indicated, we always use the least invasive technique that will also optimize success.

We will meet you where you are at and we pride ourselves in the comfort and confidence you will experience inside our Cottage. Our team is dedicated to all your dental needs and concerns. We understand that every person and every mouth requires special attention and we would love to have you at our practice. Please stop in and see us anytime or contact our front office. We will be very happy to answer any questions you may have and accommodate you with a convenient appointment time. We even offer 7:00 AM appointments and we see patients of all ages.

Thanks for taking the time to check out our website!
