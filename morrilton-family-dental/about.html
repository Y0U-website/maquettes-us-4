About

Dr. Chloe Andrews joined Morrilton Family Dental in July of 2024. She grew up in Morrilton with her parents, Allen and Natalie, and her sister, Claire. Her family is a member of Sacred Heart Church where she also attended school. Upon graduating from Sacred Heart, Dr. Andrews attended college at the University of Central Arkansas where she obtained a bachelor’s degree in Biology and a minor in Spanish. After college, she chose to attend the University of Missouri-Kansas City. There, she received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Recently, Dr. Andrews has moved back to Morrilton with her sweet cat, Olive, whom she treats more like her child. Sometimes, you can even find her taking Olive on walks in a stroller. If you do, be sure to say “Hi!” Dr. Andrews is happy to be back in Morrilton and excited to be a part of the MFD team.
