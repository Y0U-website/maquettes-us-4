The practice

0 Who we are Our practice began in 1966 when Dr. David Whittemore opened a small dental office in his home. A graduate of the University of Iowa College of Dentistry, he built the practice around honest, compassionate dental care. In 1972, the office moved to its current location, where it continues to serve patients today. Dr. David retired in 2023 after 57 years in dentistry.

After graduating from the University of Iowa College of Dentistry in 1989, Dr. Keith Whittemore served as a dentist in the U.S. Army from 1990 to 1994. He later returned to Waterloo and purchased the practice in 2004.

Dr. Dylan Whittemore came home to practice in 2023 after graduating from Midwestern University CDMI. Having grown up in Waterloo, he is glad to care for patients in the community he has always called home.

Today, our office remains focused on high-quality dental care in a welcoming, comfortable setting. We are proud to serve Waterloo and the surrounding communities, and we are grateful for the trust our patients place in us.
