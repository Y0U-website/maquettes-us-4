Benefits of All-On-4 Dental Implants

Choosing all-on-4 dental implants offers several advantages over traditional dentures or individual implants:

Patients seeking affordable All-On-4 dental implants often find this solution more cost-effective than replacing each tooth with an individual implant.

  • Full-arch restoration with fewer implants
  • Fixed, non-removable teeth for better comfort and confidence
  • Improved chewing and speech
  • Preserves jawbone and facial structure
  • Faster results, often with teeth placed the same day

All-On-4 Dental Implants: Step-by-Step Process

At Manteca Smiles, the All-On-4 dental implants step-by-step process is carefully planned for safety and long-term success:

1. Comprehensive Consultation

We assess your oral health, bone density, and smile goals using digital imaging and advanced diagnostics.

2. Treatment Planning

A customised plan is created for your upper jaw, lower jaw, or both, following the proven All-On-4 treatment concept .

3. Implant Placement

Four implants are placed precisely into the jawbone under local anaesthesia for comfort.

4. Temporary Teeth Placement

In many cases, a temporary fixed set of teeth is placed on the same day, allowing you to leave with a functional smile.

5. Final Restoration

After healing, a permanent custom prosthesis is secured, completing your All-On-4 dental implants treatment.

Who Is a Good Candidate for All-On-4?

You may be a suitable candidate for dental implants All-On-4 if you:

  • Are missing most or all teeth in an arch
  • Struggle with loose or uncomfortable dentures
  • Want a fixed, long-lasting solution
  • Have sufficient bone structure or wish to avoid bone grafting.
