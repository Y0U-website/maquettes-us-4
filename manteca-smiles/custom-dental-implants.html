The Custom Abutment Process at Manteca Smiles

When you visit Manteca Smiles for your restoration, we utilize advanced digital technology to ensure a perfect fit.

  • Digital Impressions: Instead of messy putty, we use intraoral scanners to create a 3D map of your mouth and the position of your implant.
  • CAD/CAM Design: Your dental implant abutment is designed using specialized software. We can adjust the height, width, and angle to a fraction of a millimeter.
  • Precision Milling: The design is sent to a high-tech milling center where your custom dental implant abutment is carved from a solid block of titanium or zirconia.
  • Final Placement: Once the abutment is ready, we secure it to your implant and transition to the final crown placement, completing your Manteca Smiles transformation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dental implant abutment placement painful?

The process of placing a dental implant abutment is typically very straightforward. Since the implant is already integrated into the bone, there are usually no active nerves in that specific spot. Most patients at Manteca Smiles report only minor pressure during the procedure and require nothing more than local anesthesia.

How long does a custom dental implant abutment last?

When properly cared for, a custom dental implant abutment can last a lifetime. Because it is designed to fit your unique anatomy, it experiences less mechanical stress than a stock part. Regular checkups at Manteca Smiles and good oral hygiene are the keys to longevity.

Does insurance cover the dental implant abutment cost?

Many dental insurance plans do provide coverage for the abutment as part of the overall implant restoration. However, coverage percentages vary. Our team at Manteca Smiles is happy to help you navigate your insurance benefits to determine your out-of-pocket dental implant abutment cost.
