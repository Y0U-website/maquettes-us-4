Benefits of Single Tooth Implants

Choosing single tooth implants offers several long-term advantages:

  • Preserves surrounding teeth – No need to grind down adjacent teeth
  • Prevents bone loss – Stimulates the jawbone like a natural tooth root
  • Natural appearance – Custom crown matches your smile
  • Long-lasting solution – Designed to last for many years with proper care
  • Improved comfort & function – No slipping or discomfort

Single Tooth Implant Procedure: What to Expect

The single tooth implant procedure is performed in carefully planned stages:

1. Consultation & Planning

We assess your oral health, jawbone density, and suitability for single tooth dental implants using digital imaging.

2. Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia for comfort.

3. Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the bone. This is a critical part of the single tooth implant process.

4. Crown Placement

Once healed, a custom crown is attached, completing your smile.

Does a Single Tooth Implant Hurt?

Concerns about single tooth implant pain are common. Most patients report minimal discomfort during and after the procedure. Any soreness is typically mild and manageable with medication, subsiding within a few days.
