General Dentistry Practice

All care at A Touch of Art is accompanied by an array of comfort options, such as pillows, noise-canceling headphones, and ceiling-mounted televisions. We combine methods of relaxation with our modern, state-of-the-art dental equipment to provide you dental excellence at every appointment.

  • Restorative Dentistry. If one or more of your teeth is damaged, decaying, or missing, restorative procedures fix the problem and give you back the function of your teeth.
  • Cosmetic Dentistry . Cosmetic procedures are all about giving you a brighter, more beautiful smile. Procedures include teeth whitening, cosmetic bonding, and other ways to improve the overall look of your teeth.
  • Emergency Dental Care. If you need immediate dental care, we’ll get you in the dentist’s office as soon as possible.

How Much Does it Cost to See a General Dentist?

Your dental treatment is tailored to your unique situation. Therefore, the cost will vary from person to person. The staff at our general dentistry practice will always inform you of treatments and costs beforehand, so you will know what to expect. Many preventative services and restorative procedures are covered entirely or in part by most dental insurances.
