The Benefits of Clear Aligner Therapy

There are a countless number of benefits to clear aligners as an orthodontic treatment option, here are a few of those benefits:

Discreet

Transparent aligners offer a discreet and efficient solution for misaligned teeth. Rather than using traditional metal brackets, aligners are clear and removable. Patients love the ease of wearing aligners compared to more traditional orthodontic options.

Shorter Treatment Time

The average time needed to straighten your teeth is about 12 months. Some patients may see results quicker than this. At your consultation, Dr. Velez will give you an estimated timeline time so you know how long you could be in treatment.

Eat Whatever You Want

If you&#x27;ve ever worn traditional braces, you may remember sacrificing some foods because you didn&#x27;t want to break a bracket. With clear aligners, you can remove the aligner, eat your favorite food, and not worry about broken brackets.
