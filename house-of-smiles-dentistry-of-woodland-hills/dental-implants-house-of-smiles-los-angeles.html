The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant process at House of Smiles is designed to be as smooth and comfortable as possible. Initially, our team conducts a thorough evaluation to determine the suitability of implants for your specific case.

Once deemed suitable, the procedure involves placing a titanium post into the jawbone, which serves as a sturdy foundation for the replacement tooth. This is followed by the attachment of a custom-made crown, designed to blend seamlessly with your existing teeth.

  • Initial consultation and assessment
  • Surgical placement of the implant post
  • Attachment of a custom crown
