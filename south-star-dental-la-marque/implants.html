Reasons for Choosing Dental Implants

Many of our patients in La Marque, TX, prefer dental implants for tooth replacement, as they offer various benefits, including:

  • Natural look and feel: An implant replaces the full tooth (including root and crown), offering realistic results.
  • Enhanced smile: They fill the missing tooth gaps in a natural-looking way, so you can smile more confidently.
  • Better oral functionality: You can bite, chew, and speak much more easily with implants.
  • Reduced bone loss: They merge with the surrounding jawbone, lowering the risk of future bone loss.
  • Durable and long-lasting: The results of this treatment can last for many years with routine oral hygiene maintenance.

What To Expect From the Process of Dental Implants at Our La Marque Clinic?

The process starts with a comprehensive exam, during which our dentist assesses your oral cavity and takes dental X-rays to determine your eligibility for dental implants.

We then create a unique treatment plan to replace your missing teeth.

The core step of the procedure is implant surgery, during which we insert implant posts in the jawbone under local anesthesia.

Once the posts are secure, we allow them to heal for about three to six months.

After complete healing, we place abutments and connect the posts with replacement teeth. They are made to fit your aesthetic needs, restoring a flawless appearance.
