Types of Implant-Supported Dentures We Offer in La Marque, TX

Our dental office offers two main types of implant-supported dentures, including:

Fixed Dentures

Fixed dentures are securely attached to implant posts and can only be removed by a dental professional. Typically, 2 to 6 implants are inserted per arch. They are ideal for patients seeking a stable, long-term solution that functions like natural teeth.

Removable Dentures

If you are looking for a more durable alternative to conventional dentures, these dentures may be the right option. They attach to implants with specialized connectors that make it easy to snap the denture on and off as needed. This way, you can clean the denture and maintain your oral health easily.

What Happens During the Process of Implant-Supported Dentures?

At the time of your initial visit, our La Marque team examines your jawbone, gums, and remaining teeth to determine the most suitable type of implant-supported dentures. During the process, we carefully place implant posts into the jawbone, which help create a strong foundation for your denture.

After the healing period (about 3 to 6 months), we take impressions to create a custom-fit denture for your oral cavity. The denture is then securely attached to the implants to restore your fuller smile. We also provide instructions on how to clean and maintain the denture in the long run.

You can enjoy a wide range of foods with implant-supported dentures. However, it is best to avoid too hard or chewy foods as they put excess strain on the implants.
