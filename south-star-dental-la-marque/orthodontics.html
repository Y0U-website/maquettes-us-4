Orthodontic Treatments We Offer at Our Practice

After performing a detailed assessment, our orthodontist in La Marque will create a customized treatment plan to address your specific concerns. Our orthodontic treatment options include:

Traditional Metal Braces

Traditional metal braces remain one of the most reliable ways to correct misalignment and bite issues. At our clinic, we provide modern metal braces with smaller brackets and advanced archwires that offer greater comfort. They apply a steady, gentle pressure on your teeth, guiding them into their ideal positions.

Invisalign®

For those who prefer a more discreet, metal-free option, Invisalign® may be the perfect fit. This innovative system uses clear, removable aligners that are custom-made to fit over your teeth. As they work, they gradually shift your teeth into a perfect alignment. The best part is that you can easily take them off before eating your favorite foods or cleaning your teeth.

Retainers

After your orthodontic treatment is complete, wearing retainers is the final step to ensure your teeth remain secure in their new alignment. Wearing retainers is essential for maintaining long-term alignment. Most patients need long-term nighttime wear.

We offer different types of retainers, including fixed and removable, based on your needs. Our team will guide you on proper wear and care to help maintain lasting results.

Palatal Expanders

Palatal expanders gradually widen the upper jaw to create more space and improve bite alignment. They are often recommended for children and pre-teens whose jaws are still developing to prevent the risk of more complex orthodontic issues later on.

Space Maintainers

If your child loses a baby tooth too early, it may cause orthodontic issues later on. In this case, our dentists may recommend space maintainers to preserve the space for the proper eruption of the permanent tooth and prevent the adjacent teeth from shifting away.
