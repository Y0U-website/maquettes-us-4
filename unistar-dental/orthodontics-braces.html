ORTHODONTICS BRACES

Orthodontic braces Orthodontic braces (also known as braces, orthodontic braces) are tools used in the orthodontic department at Unistar Dental.

To straighten crooked teeth, bring them into the correct position in the bite, while improving oral health.

This technology can correct gaps in teeth, underbites, protruding teeth while minimizing dental diseases such as tooth decay caused by food stuck in crooked teeth.

Treatment time with braces usually lasts from 18 to 24 months. Depending on the level, it may take longer or faster depending on the case.

WHO SHOULD GET ORTHODONTICS?

• These include people with overbite, underbite, gap teeth, missing teeth, deep or open bite, crossbite of one tooth, crowded teeth, permanent teeth growing in the wrong position and some cases of combined orthodontics. With surgical treatment,

• Patients have difficulty chewing, lose their appetite, and have poor appetite, greatly affect-ing the functioning of the digestive system.

• Patients have many difficulties in daily hygiene and oral care.

• If the patient’s malocclusion persists without treatment, it can cause very serious effects on the musculoskeletal system.

• This disease can greatly affect the patient’s face and smile.

• More worryingly, prolonged malocclusion can also lead to SADAM syndrome, also known as temporomandibular joint syndrome.
