Born and raised in San Francisco, Dr. Kevin Ho attended the University of California, Irvine where he graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Biology. He then attended the Western University of Health Sciences, College of Dental Medicine in Pomona, CA and received his doctorate degree in 2018. Dr. Ho is a member of the American Dental Association and California Dental Association.

Dr. Ho has taken numerous courses to expand his skill sets and keep his clinical knowledge up to date. These courses include molar endodontic treatment, surgical third molar extractions, and implant placement and restoration. He is always eager to incorporate new technologies and renovations into his treatment in order to better serve his patients.

When he is not in the office, Dr. Ho enjoys spending time with his wife and son, traveling, and cheering on his favorite SF sports teams.
