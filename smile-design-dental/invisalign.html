How Invisalign Works

1. Digital Scan & Smile Preview

We capture a 3D scan and map the exact tooth movements needed. You’ll see a simulation of your future smile before we begin.

2. Series of Custom Aligners

You’ll wear each set of aligners for about 1–2 weeks, 20–22 hours per day, removing them only to eat, drink anything other than water, and to brush and floss.

3. Progress Checks & Refinements

Quick visits every 6–8 weeks keep treatment on track. When alignment is complete, we finalize with retainers to maintain your results

Who’s a Candidate?

Most teens and adults with crowding, gaps, mild to moderate bite issues (overbite, underbite, crossbite), or relapse after braces can be great candidates. At your consultation, our doctors confirm if Invisalign is the best path to your goals.

Smile Design Dental Reviews

How long does treatment take?

Average treatment ranges from 6–18 months , depending on complexity and compliance.

How many hours per day do I wear aligners?

Aim for 20–22 hours daily . Consistent wear is the key to staying on schedule.

Will it affect how I speak?

Most patients adjust within a day or two with little to no impact on speech.

What about retainers?

Retainers help maintain your new smile. We’ll review the best retainer plan for long-term stability.
