Who’s a Candidate?

Most healthy adults missing one tooth, several teeth, or a full arch may qualify. Candidacy is based on gum health, bone volume, and overall medical status. If additional care (such as bone grafting) is recommended, we’ll explain timing and options.

What’s Included

Diagnostic imaging may be recommended as part of determining candidacy. Any additional diagnostics or treatment beyond the consultation will be discussed with you beforehand.

Smile Design Dental Reviews

Am I guaranteed to be a candidate?

Not always. Candidacy depends on bone volume, gum health, and medical factors. If additional procedures are needed, we’ll outline them.

How long does the consultation take?

Most visits take 30–45 minutes , depending on your needs and questions.

Will I get a cost estimate the same day?

Yes. After we confirm an appropriate plan, you’ll receive an itemized estimate and payment/financing options.

Do implants hurt?

Most patients report minimal discomfort that’s well-managed with prescribed care. We review comfort options before treatment.
