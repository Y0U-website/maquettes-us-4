Dr. Jouliana Davoudi

Dr. Jouliana Davoudi, is a graduate of USC’s Orofacial Pain and Medicine center. Her passion for Orofacial Pain and Medicine led her to pursue further education in this area. She has taken various courses, a mini-residency, and a full conventional residency at USC to deepen her expertise. Dr Davoudi is Clinical Assistant Professor, Division of Periodontology, Diagnostic Sciences & Dental Hygiene at USC.

Driven by her interest in this field, she created a tool that can help clinicians treat patients more effectively. She is thrilled that her research won the Most Disruptive Award from the USC Stevens Center for Innovation and was accepted for oral presentation and publication in the Oral Surgery, Oral Medicine, Oral Pathology, and Oral Radiology paper. She is committed to advancing the field of Orofacial Pain and Medicine and providing patients with the best possible care.
