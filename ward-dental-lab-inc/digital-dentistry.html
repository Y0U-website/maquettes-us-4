Digital Dentistry

As dentistry continues to move towards more digital workflows, so must the laboratory. We are committed to staying on the cutting edge of technology and combining new information with all we have learned over the decades. Our goal is to ensure that you and your patients receive the best final outcome.

CAD/CAM Capabilities

MicroDental’s state-of-the-art CAD/CAM capabilities enable our most skilled technicians to enhance form, fit, and function and optimize the seating time of our restorations. Contact us for digital support and a seamless workflow for your practice!

Computer-Aided Manufacturing Robots with self-calibrating features enhance precision, consistency, and predictability.

Compatibility with Cerec®, iTero™, 3M True Definition™, Carestream Dental, 3Shape®, Planmeca PlanScan®(formerly E4D), and other intraoral scanning devices.

Computer-Aided Design Software makes it easier for technicians to design, communicate, and collaborate.

Case Workflow Software promotes on-time delivery, case management, and the communication of preferences.
