0 Meet Our Team SCOTT WARD FOUNDER Founder Scott Ward, CDT, has overseen Ward Dental Laboratory for over 25 years. Scott built Ward Lab into a high-quality, full-service laboratory servicing primarily Southeastern Michigan. Scott enjoys working with doctors in treatment planning for patients. He believes the lab is an integral part of the dental team.

KAREN MASON LAB MANAGER Ward’s lab Manager, Karen Mason, is an expert in all aspects of dental laboratory fabrication and has hands-on experience in every department of the dental laboratory. Karen believes that the business’s success is built on the foundation of excellence in customer communications, consistent quality, and attention to detail in every restoration that leaves Ward.

Nemer Hussein works with current and new doctors on all of Ward’s All-on-X type surgeries and consultations for treatment planning of larger removable and fixed cases. Before joining Ward Dental Lab, Nemer worked with Dr. Carl Misch for 30 years as his in-house technician, specializing in Removable Implant Prosthetics. Nemer is currently a Fellow of the International Prosthetic Society.
