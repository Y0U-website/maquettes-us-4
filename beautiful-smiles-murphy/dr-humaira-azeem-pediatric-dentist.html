Dr. Humaira Azeem BDS, DDS

Family Dentist for Pediatric

Dr. Humaira Azeem, BDS, DDS, is a compassionate family dentist for pediatric patients at Beautiful Smiles Clinic in Murphy, TX. She has been providing comprehensive dental care to children and adolescents in the DFW area for 13+ years. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the prestigious University of Illinois College of Dentistry, followed by completing hundreds of continuing education hours to stay current with the latest advances in dental techniques and technology, especially for pediatric patients.

Dr. Azeem is dedicated to providing high-quality dental care for children of all ages. With her background in general dentistry, she found her true calling in treating young patients. She specializes in creating a comfortable, child-friendly environment where every visit feels safe and positive.

Known for her gentle approach and ability to connect with children, Dr. Azeem works closely with families to build healthy dental habits and support long-term oral health. She is fluent in English, Hindi, Punjabi, and Urdu. New patients are always welcome, and most major insurance plans are accepted.
