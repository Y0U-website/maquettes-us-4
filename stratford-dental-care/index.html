The practice

Dr. Leonardo Pena-Abosaid built Stratford Dental Care on the belief that great dental care should feel comfortable, not only clinical. Whether you’re a nervous first-timer, bringing in your kids, or ready for a full smile transformation — our trilingual team meets you with warmth, patience, and genuine expertise.

Drag the slider on each photo to see the difference — real cases from our own patients, not stock photography.

The equipment we’ve invested in isn’t about looking high-tech — it’s about making your appointment faster, quieter, and far more comfortable than the dentistry you remember.

Remember the tray full of putty you had to hold in your mouth while trying not to gag? We don’t use it. Our 3D scanner takes a precise digital picture of your teeth in a few minutes — nothing to bite down on, nothing to swallow, no gagging. It’s more accurate than the old molds, which means crowns, aligners, and night guards that fit right the first time. You can even watch your own scan build on…

For many gum and soft-tissue procedures, our dental laser replaces the scalpel and the stitches. That usually means little to no bleeding, less need for anesthetic, less swelling afterward, and healing measured in days instead of weeks. Most patients are surprised the procedure is over before they expected it to start.

Our diverse, trilingual team has one goal — making sure every patient leaves feeling better than when they arrived.
