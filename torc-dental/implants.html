How the Treatment Works

A tooth replacement solution uses titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots, surgically placed into your jawbone where teeth are missing. Once positioned, these posts integrate with your bone tissue through a natural process called osseointegration. This typically takes several weeks as your bone grows around the post, creating a stable foundation. After this healing period, a custom-made crown is attached, completing your restoration.

There are two primary types of implants available. Endosteal implants are placed directly into the jawbone and are the most common type used today. Subperiosteal implants sit under the gum tissue but rest on top of the jawbone, making them suitable for patients who may not have sufficient bone height for traditional implants.

Benefits of Permanent Tooth Replacement

Protects Adjacent Teeth from Damage

Traditional bridges require grinding down healthy neighboring teeth to serve as anchors, permanently altering their structure. This treatment option stands independently without relying on surrounding teeth for support. This preservation approach keeps your natural teeth intact and reduces the risk of future dental problems in those areas.

Eliminates Speech Difficulties

Missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures can cause slurring, whistling sounds, or mumbling that affects how others understand you. This treatment option remains fixed in place and fills gaps completely, allowing your tongue to move naturally against stable surfaces. You’ll speak clearly and confidently without worrying about dentures shifting or gaps altering your pronunciation.

Provides Superior Comfort

Unlike removable dentures that can create sore spots, rub against your gums, or require messy adhesives, this permanent tooth replacement solution becomes part of your mouth. You won’t experience the irritation that comes from taking dentures in and out multiple times daily. The permanent nature of this treatment means no awkward adjustments during meals or conversations.

Maintains Proper Tooth Alignment

When a tooth is missing, neighboring teeth gradually drift into the empty space, creating bite problems and increasing your risk of decay and gum disease. This tooth replacement solution fills these gaps immediately, acting as a placeholder that helps keep your remaining teeth in their correct positions. This stability protects your overall dental health and helps prevent costly orthodontic issues down the road.

Supports Overall Health and Nutrition

The ability to chew thoroughly affects how well your body digests food and absorbs essential nutrients. This tooth replacement solution restores full chewing power, enabling you to eat fibrous vegetables, lean proteins, and other nutritious foods that benefit your overall health. You won’t need to limit yourself to soft, processed options that may lack the vitamins and minerals your body needs.

Personalized Implant Strategy

Based on your examination results, Dr. Hijazi creates a detailed treatment plan that addresses your unique dental needs and goals. For patients missing multiple teeth or a full arch, she’ll explain how the All-on-4 technique can replace all your teeth using just four strategically positioned implants.

Precise Implant Placement

The surgical procedure is performed using local anesthesia to keep you comfortable while Dr. Hijazi positions each titanium post into your jawbone with exact precision. These implants serve as artificial tooth roots that will bond with your bone tissue over the following weeks. If you qualify for our Teeth in a Day service, you’ll receive temporary replacement teeth during this same appointment, eliminating any period without teeth.

Healing Period and Bone Integration

Following your implant placement, your body begins the natural process of osseointegration where bone grows around the titanium posts. This typically takes several weeks to a few months, during which you’ll follow specific care instructions including eating soft foods and avoiding dairy products initially. Dr. Hijazi monitors your progress to ensure the implants are bonding properly with your jawbone before moving to the next phase.

Final Restoration and Ongoing Care

Once osseointegration is complete, Dr. Hijazi attaches your permanent crowns, bridges, or dentures to the fully integrated implants. These custom-made restorations are designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and function. Regular check-ups every six months help maintain your implants and ensure they continue performing optimally for years to come.

FAQs

How much does a permanent tooth replacement cost?

The cost of this tooth replacement solution depends on how many restorations you need, the complexity of your case, and the type of restoration used. A single restoration typically ranges from $3,000 to $5,000, while full-arch solutions cost more. Many dental practices offer financing options to make treatment more accessible. Schedule a consultation to receive an accurate estimate based on your specific situation and learn about payment plans that fit your budget.

What is a permanent tooth replacement solution?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into your jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. These posts bond with your bone through a natural healing process, creating a permanent foundation for artificial teeth. Once healed, crowns, bridges, or dentures attach to the implants, restoring your ability to chew and smile naturally. They’re the closest replacement option to natural teeth in both function and appearance.

How long does this tooth replacement solution last?

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits. The titanium post rarely needs replacement once it integrates with your jawbone. The crown or prosthetic attached to the implant may require replacement after 10 to 15 years due to normal wear. This longevity makes implants more cost-effective long-term compared to bridges or dentures that need frequent adjustments or replacements.

Does the procedure hurt?

You won’t feel pain during implant placement because the procedure uses local anesthesia. Most patients describe post-surgery discomfort as similar to or less than a tooth extraction. Any soreness typically fades within a few days and responds well to over-the-counter pain relievers. Following your dentist’s aftercare instructions helps minimize discomfort and promotes faster healing, making the recovery process manageable for most people.

Why should I avoid dairy after the procedure?

Dairy products can promote bacterial growth in your mouth immediately after implant surgery, increasing infection risk at the surgical site. Avoiding dairy for the first few days helps protect the area while it begins healing. Your dentist will let you know when it’s safe to resume eating dairy based on how well you’re recovering. This temporary restriction is a simple precaution that supports successful implant integration.
