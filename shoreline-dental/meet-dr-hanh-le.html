Hanh Le, DDS

More Topics

I enjoy spending time with my patients and gaining their trust, while educating them about prevention and oral health. It is rewarding to see patients develop better dental health and more beautiful smiles.

Dr. Hanh Le grew up in the Bay Area and is proud to serve her community. Providing leading-edge dental solutions, in a pleasant and supportive environment, she helps patients develop and maintain functional and beautiful smiles.

Known for her clinical skills and detailed, individualized treatment planning, Dr. Le places her patient’s wellbeing as the top priority. Dedicating time to speak with and listen to her patients is core to how she practices; the more she understands who her patient is, along with their expectations and concerns, the better she can identify the best approach to solving the individual’s dental needs.

As she designs her patient’s treatment, Dr. Le discusses the situation with her patient. Explaining the diagnostic findings, she walks the individual through treatment options, including what she feels is best and why, and encourages questions. This discussion ensures her patient is able to make informed decisions about the health of their smile.

Background and Education

Dr. Le chose to join the oral healthcare field from a desire to educate and promote good oral hygiene while also delivering confident and healthy smiles.

Her undergraduate studies were at the University of California - Davis, where she received a degree in Biochemistry. Next came dental school, at Creighton University School of Dentistry, where Dr. Le was awarded her Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Dr. Le began practicing dentistry in 2003 and swiftly gained a reputation for gentle, effective care that improved her patients’ oral and overall health. Her focus remains trained on each patient, their comfort, education, and well-being, and building a trusting relationship as she delivers beautifully healthy smiles.

Continuing Education

Throughout her career, Dr. Le has sought opportunities to further develop her knowledge and skills. An enthusiastic learner, she regularly completes continuing education courses, as well as attends meetings, seminars, and lectures.

Dr. Le is a member of the American and California Dental Associations, and the Mid-Peninsula Dental Society. These professional organizations provide numerous opportunities for Dr. Le to learn from leading researchers and practitioners. By staying at the forefront of the latest developments in oral healthcare, Dr. Le is able to utilize the latest technologies and techniques to improve patient care and outcome.

Beyond the Office

Among her many interests outside of oral healthcare, Dr. Le is an active gardener with a special love for orchids.
