About Us

What Makes Us Unique

Dr. Rebecca Testa and her incredible team strive to make each dental experience positive. Our office is designed to create a safe and nurturing environment for children of all ages. We practice conservative treatment options and we work towards educating your whole family so that we can work together to help prevent dental problems. Parents are welcome to be with their children during their appointments.

This is a place where families are comfortable and everyone looks forward to visiting. Each and every child who walks into our practice is important to us, and we want to make sure they have a stress-free, relaxing dental experience.

Meet Dr. Rebecca Testa

Dr. Rebecca J. Testa graduated undergraduate with honors from Valparaiso University. She then completed her 4 year dental training, 2 years postdoctoral training in pediatric dentistry, as well as MS in Oral Health Sciences at the University of Illinois at Chicago (UIC) college of Dentistry. Her masters work has been published in Pediatric Dentistry, the official publication of the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

Meet Rocco & Lucky, Our Therapy Dogs!

Rocco & Lucky are Rebecca Testa Pediatric Dentistry’s Official Therapy Dogs. If your child suffers from anxiety when visiting the dentist, Rocco and Lucky may be able to help! Both dogs love to be with kids and are two of the sweetest dogs you’ll ever meet. If your child has anxiety when going to the dentist, a therapy dog can help turn a scary visit into a comfortable experience.

Therapy animals have been shown to reduce nervousness, lower blood pressure, and increase endorphin levels – the natural substance produced by the body, which works by reducing feelings of pain or stress.

Of course, we only introduce Rocco and Lucky to our patients who are comfortable with dogs, and every care is taken to ensure a positive experience for everyone. Our therapy dogs are everyone’s favorite team members, they have very calm and friendly bedside manners, and they can’t wait to meet you (and have you scratch behind their ears…they love that!)
