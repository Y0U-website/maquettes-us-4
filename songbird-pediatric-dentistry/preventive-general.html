Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Pediatric dental digital X-rays help us see hidden areas of your child’s teeth, roots, and jaw with quick, low-radiation imaging. At Songbird Pediatric Dentistry, we take X-rays only when needed to check growth, find cavities early, and plan safe, accurate care for little smiles.

Pediatric Dental Sealant

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We paint a clear, safe resin on your child&#x27;s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, keeping away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles. This treatment is especially great for back teeth, where cavities are most likely to form.

Non-Invasive Remineralization Treatments

The early stages of cavities often present as white spot lesions on the surface of affected teeth, and can still be reversed with our innovative remineralization treatments. Both ICON Resin Infiltration and Curodont Repair Fluoride Plus are non-invasive solutions that remineralize teeth, without staining them, resulting in a healthy, strengthened smile.

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to prevent dental decay. After your child&#x27;s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish is applied, followed by a short period of no eating or drinking. Fluoride initiates a process called “remineralization,” strengthening teeth with essential minerals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Check out these frequently asked questions, or call us to speak with our team.

What Is Pediatric Preventive Dentistry?

Pediatric preventive dentistry focuses on protecting your child’s teeth before problems become bigger. It includes cleanings, exams, fluoride, sealants, X-rays when needed, and parent guidance to support healthier smiles at home.

How Often Should My Child Visit the Dentist?

Most children should visit every six months for pediatric cleanings and exams. These visits help remove buildup, check tooth growth, and catch small concerns before they become painful.

How Do Sealants And Fluoride Prevent Cavities?

Dental sealants protect hard-to-clean back teeth, while fluoride helps strengthen the tooth surface. Together, they give children extra cavity protection during important growth years.

Are Digital X-Rays Needed For Prevention?

Digital X-rays are only recommended when needed. Pediatric digital X-rays help the dentist see hidden cavities, tooth growth concerns, or problems that may not be visible during a regular exam.

What If My Child Has Early Signs Of Cavities?

If early cavity signs appear, the dentist may suggest gentle options like silver diamine fluoride or non-invasive remineralization care to help protect the tooth before more treatment is needed.
