Education and Prevention

As a practice, we are true believers that preventative care and education are the keys to optimal dental health. We strive to provide dental health care’ focusing on prevention versus disease or emergency care. That’s why we focus on thorough exams checking the overall health of your teeth including an oral cancer check and taking x-rays only when necessary.

Uncompromising Safety

Infection control in our office is at the top of our priority list. To protect our patients and ourselves, we maintain strict sterilization and cross contamination processes, using standards recommended by the American Dental Association, OSHA and the CDC.

Training and Expertise

We are a team of highly trained and skilled clinicians. We take pride in providing the care you need to keep your smile healthy. To give you the best possible service and results, we are committed to continued education and learning. We attend dental lectures, meetings, classes and dental conventions to stay informed of new techniques, the latest products, and the newest equipment that can be used to provide state-of-the-art dental care.
