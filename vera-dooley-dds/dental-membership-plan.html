Each Year You Will Receive:

  • Two Routine Exams (first exam is comprehensive for new patients)
  • One Emergency Exam (conditions apply)
  • Two Standard Cleanings (in the absence of active periodontal disease)
  • 1 Set of Bitewing X-rays
  • One Fluoride Varnish Treatment
  • $50 in Bankable Treatment Credit, Available Immediately

Additional Benefits:

  • Panorex or Full Mouth Series X-rays (every 3 years)
  • 15 % off All Fees for Dental Services at Vera Dooley, DDS

Advantages of Joining our Dental Membership Plan

  • No yearly maximums
  • No deductibles
  • No claim forms
  • No pre-authorizations or waiting for coverage decisions
  • No pre-existing condition limitations
  • No one will be denied coverage
  • No waiting periods to start treatment (immediate eligibility)
