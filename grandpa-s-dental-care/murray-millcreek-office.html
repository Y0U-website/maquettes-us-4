Welcome to Grandpa’s Dental Center in Millcreek, UT!

Grandpa, a second-generation dentist, strongly believed in the direct relationship between oral health and overall wellness. Grandpa had a simple vision for his practice: high-quality dental care should be accessible and affordable for all.

Today, we proudly carry on his inspiring legacy at Grandpa’s Dental Center. Our practice is about more than just dentistry; it is about each person, their family, and friends. It is about you.

Our Murray office features cutting-edge dental technology, including digital X-rays, Cone Beam CT imaging, and in-house lab services.
