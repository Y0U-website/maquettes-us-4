Emergency Dental Care in Tallahassee, FL

Dental emergencies can happen without warning. Whether you have severe tooth pain, a damaged tooth, swelling, or another urgent dental concern, you can count on Apalachee Bay Family Dental for prompt care. Our expert team can help relieve discomfort, address the cause of the issue, and restore your oral health as quickly as possible.

Process

Contact our office as soon as your dental emergency occurs so your visit can be scheduled promptly. The dentist will examine your mouth, identify the source of the problem, and determine the appropriate treatment. Immediate care may focus on relieving pain, controlling bleeding, treating infection, or protecting a damaged tooth. If additional treatment is needed, you’ll receive a personalized plan to restore your smile and support long-term oral health.

Next Steps

Follow your dentist’s aftercare instructions carefully to support healing. Take any recommended medications as directed. Attend any follow-up appointments to monitor healing and complete additional treatment if necessary. Continue practicing good oral hygiene and schedule regular dental checkups to help maintain your oral health.

FAQ

  • What dental problems require emergency treatment? Severe toothaches, knocked-out teeth, oral bleeding, facial swelling, jaw injuries, and severe infections require prompt emergency dental care.
  • What should I do if I have a knocked-out or damaged tooth? Call your dentist immediately. If a tooth is knocked out, place it in a glass of milk and seek care as soon as possible.
  • Can I visit an emergency dentist for a severe tooth infection? Yes. Severe dental infections are considered emergencies because the infection can cause lasting damage and spread to different parts of your body.

Related Services

Gum Disease Treatment

Healthy gums are the foundation of a healthy smile. At Apalachee Bay Family Dental, we provide personalized periodontal care to control infection, restore gum health, and help protect your teeth for years to come.

Fillings and Sealants

Treating cavities early with fillings helps preserve your natural teeth and prevent more extensive dental treatment later. Dental sealants provide an additional layer of protection by shielding the chewing surfaces of your back teeth from bacteria and plaque, making them an excellent preventative option for both children and many adults.
