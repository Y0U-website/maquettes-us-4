Meet Our Doctors Drs. Tiffany and Trisha Vo are sisters & Fountain Valley, CA natives

Drs. Tiffany and Trisha Vo are graduates of the accelerated dental program at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco. While in dental school, Dr. Trisha was selected for a dental oncology and hospital dentistry externship at Stanford Hospital for medically complex and special care patients. Dr. Trisha’s postdoctoral training includes the Rancho Los Amigos Rehabilitation Center General Practice Residency, which is solely dedicated to treating patients with complex medical histories as well as physical and developmental disabilities.

Both Drs. Tiffany and Trisha spent their early careers working for pediatric dental practices and non-profits with Denti-Cal in order to serve special care patients. However, Dr. Trisha’s additional training fueled her passion for addressing the lack of dental providers available for special care patients after they age out of traditional pediatric practices.

This led to the sisters founding of Elements Dentistry as a dental practice focused on providing comprehensive and emergency dental care for special care and medically compromised patients and their families. Drs. Tiffany and Trisha envision Elements Dentistry as a safe place for all patients to receive humanistic and quality dental care.

Patients of Elements Dentistry can rest assured that their doctors have prioritized continuing to learn years after graduating from dental school: both Drs. Tiffany and Trisha are graduates of the Kois Center, a prestigious evidence-based post-doctoral dental education center in Seattle, WA in addition to working toward fellowship with the International Congress of Oral Implantologists.
