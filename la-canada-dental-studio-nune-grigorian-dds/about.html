A Commitment to Service and Compassion

Get To Know Dr. Nune Grigorian

Raised in Glendale, California, Dr. Nune Grigorian has had a passion for dentistry and helping others since her childhood. Her dedication to service and hard work along with her compassion for friends, family, and her community have led her to her current path.

Dr. Grigorian received her undergraduate degree in evolutionary biology with Honors from University of California, Los Angeles. She has attended USC Herman Ostrow School of Dentistry. She also lived and practiced in Houston, Texas for eight years, broadening her exposure to dental practices nationwide.

When she’s not in the office, Dr. Grigorian enjoys spending time with her husband and three children, hiking, hosting gatherings for her friends and family, and tending to her rose garden and large plant collection.

Prioritizing the Patient Experience

Your First Visit

First, you’ll be greeted by our friendly and welcoming staff, and get to sit back and relax in our comfortable office as you enjoy our beverage station and TVs in our waiting room.

You’ll meet with Dr. Nune Grigorian and receive a comprehensive oral exam, which includes intraoral photos, FMX, oral cancer check, periodontal exam, and adult prophylaxis, along with any necessary x-rays. Then we&#x27;ll perform a thorough cleaning depending on your needs. We always spend time addressing your concerns and discussing the state of your dental health with you before outlining a unique treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Before leaving, we can also schedule any future visits and sign you up for our annual membership plan.
