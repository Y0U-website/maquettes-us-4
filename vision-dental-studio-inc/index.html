Often referred to as hybrid prostheses or hybrid implant restorations, are a type of dental restoration that combines elements of both fixed dental bridges and removable dentures.

Depending on specific needs and preferences our lab creates many types of dentures with exceptional quality for dental practices.

The process of creating crowns and bridges in our dental lab involves precision, attention to detail, and collaboration between the dentist and dental technician to ensure that the restorations function properly, fit comfortably, and look natural within the patient’s mouth.

Our work begins by creating a mold. These impressions are typically made using dental putty or digital scanning technology. The impressions provide an accurate replica of the patient’s oral structures. Based on the impressions and the dentist’s treatment plan, the dental technician creates a detailed design for the crown or bridge.

Les soins

All-on-4 or-6 involves the placement of a fixed denture on four or six dental implants in a single arch (upper or lower). This provides superior stability and function and eliminates the need for a removable prosthesis.

Vision Dental Studio works closely with dental professionals to create customized implant-supported prostheses and components that are crucial for restoring patients’ oral health and function after tooth loss.
