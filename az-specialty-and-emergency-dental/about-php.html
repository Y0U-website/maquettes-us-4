Dr.Adam Zaher

Dr. Adam Zaher has been practicing Dentistry for 20 years. He graduated from University of Illinois at Chicago School of Dental Medicine. He was one of the top students in his class. The Lone Star State has always been his favorite state. He moved with his wife to Dallas,TX and started his own Dental Practice.

Dr. Zaher believes in providing state of the art, relationship-based, comprehensive care that is tailored to fit into the lives and budgets of his patients. He is very ambitious, compassionate and provides the best care possible. He believes that honesty is the key to success!

Our doctor decided to provide dental care for his patients at late hours . With our busy life, Dr. Adam Zaher believes that patients should access dental care at their convenience.

By pursuing advanced degrees and training, Dr. Adam’s practice spans from essential care to full smile makeovers. His advanced education is an essential tool that can be used for treating complex and restorative cases.
