How Functional Is Tooth Implant?

Tooth implants are made to provide the complete function that a regular tooth has in our mouth. We use the most effective materials that completely resemble the look, functionality, and longevity of the real teeth.

A tooth implant is made with metal parts that are acting like tooth roots. These metal materials are completely safe and provide the structure that lasts for a lifetime. We use safe dental implants that are providing functionality and exceptional quality. With the right installation of tooth implants, you can be sure that your new implants will have the same look, feel, and power of the real, healthy teeth.

In crafting of tooth implants, we use the technology that applies to your specific case. Each patient is different, and each requires special attention. Our goal is to ensure that your tooth implant is made in perfect size so we can install it into your jaw with huge precision. After the procedure, you can use temporary installments until your dental implant is accepted by the jaw. After the probation period, we install a dental crown that completely replaces your original tooth.

The whole procedure is safe and effective. After the installation of the tooth implant, you can be sure that your implant will last for a long period. There is no need for additional installation of other tooth implants in the near future.

What Is Dental Implant Cost?

Dental implant cost depends on many factors. One of the most important element is how prepared your jaw is for dental implants. If you need a lot of work to prepare the jaw and gums for dental implant installation, the cost of the procedure will be more expensive. On the other hand, if you are ready for instant installation, you will pay much less. It all depends on your specific case and the health of your gums.
