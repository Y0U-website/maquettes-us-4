0 Meet your dentist Su Ping Mai, DMD, MPH Dr. Mai is a highly skilled dentist with a diverse background in private dentistry, academia, public health, and military service. A San Francisco native, she earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine and Master of Public Health degrees from A.T. Still University, graduating 2nd in her class. Dr. Mai served as a Major in the U.S. Army, completing her Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AGED) residency and gaining expertise in various dental procedures.

With experience in private practice, community health clinics, and as a former hygiene school professor, Dr. Mai is committed to providing comprehensive oral care for patients of all ages. Her unique background combines military precision with a passion for community health, allowing her to create a welcoming environment for even the most anxious patients.

Dr. Mai regularly attends continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of dental advancements. Outside of dentistry, she enjoys spending time with her family, hiking, and experimenting with new cooking recipes.
