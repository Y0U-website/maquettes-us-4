Meet Dr. Michael McCafferty

Dr. Michael McCafferty

Michael McCafferty has spent his entire life in the Western Chicago suburbs. He received his Dental degree from the Loyola School of Dentistry in 1986 and has spent 35 years practicing General & Family Dentistry in the Oak Park, River Forest area. His focus has always been on providing the highest quality dentistry available while meeting his patient’s needs and building relationships. Dr. McCafferty has extensive experience in the restorative, prosthetic, cosmetic and endodontic aspects of dentistry.
