Clear Solutions for Straighter Teeth

We specialize in clear orthodontic aligners , which are subtle, comfortable, and fit easily into daily routines. No wires, no brackets just results.

Aligners Clear aligners are a modern alternative to braces. Using a series of custom-fit, removable trays, aligners gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment. Most people won’t even notice you’re wearing them.

At Renwick Family Dental, we work with trusted aligner systems and provide hands-on monitoring throughout your treatment. You’ll switch trays every 1 2 weeks and visit us periodically for progress checks.

Invisalign We’re proud to offer Invisalign in Plainfield, IL the gold standard in clear aligner technology. Invisalign provides exceptional precision and comfort, with smart tracking features that improve efficiency.

Invisalign can treat everything from minor crowding to more complex bite issues. Many adult patients choose Invisalign because it fits their professional lifestyle while delivering long-term results.

Retainers After orthodontic treatment, retainers play a vital role in maintaining your new smile. Without them, teeth can slowly shift back to their original positions a process known as relapse.

We’ll provide a custom-made retainer and guide you through how and when to wear it. For some patients, nightly wear is enough; others may need to wear retainers more often depending on their case.
