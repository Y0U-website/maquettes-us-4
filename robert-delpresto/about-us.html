Our Story And Philosophy

Dr. Robert Del Presto has been serving patients throughout the Jersey Shore area since 1992. After graduating from UMDNJ-NJDS, he immediately entered private practice in Beachwood before establishing his long-standing Brick, NJ office. With over three decades of experience, Dr. Del Presto is deeply committed to continuing education, allowing him to integrate the latest dental advancements into gentle, high-quality patient care. He stands proudly behind his work, always prioritizing doing what is best for his family of patients and bringing a true passion for dentistry to every appointment.

Our Story And Philosophy

Robert N. Del Presto D.M.D. is a family-friendly dental practice in Brick Township, offering general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry with an emphasis on comfort, clear explanations, and long-term relationships built over decades of local care.

A Small, Steady, Familiar Team

Patients are cared for by the same close-knit team at each visit, which creates a calm, family-like atmosphere. Michelle helps with treatment and finances, Deanna focuses on cleanings and gum health, and Krista supports you chairside. Together we listen, answer questions in plain language, and work at a pace that feels comfortable.

Care On Your Terms

Honesty and practicality guide every recommendation. We explain findings, review options, and outline costs before any treatment begins, then help you use insurance and payment choices wisely. Whether you need a single filling or a full plan over time, we partner with you so that healthy, confident teeth feel achievable.

Meet Our Staff

Michelle

Michelle has been working side-by-side with Dr. Del Presto since the beginning at Arrowhead Family Dental. Serving as Dr. Del Presto’s right arm, she oversees all day-to-day operations at the practice. As our primary financial coordinator and treatment plan presenter, Michelle helps guide patients through their care options with clarity and ease. She also serves as our primary KöR Whitening consultant, helping patients achieve their goals.

Deanna

Deanna brings immense compassion and exceptional clinical care to our hygiene team. Utilizing our state-of-the-art equipment and technology, she provides comprehensive, non-surgical periodontal therapy with a emphasis on maximum comfort—often without the need for local anesthesia. Deanna is passionate about patient education and works interactively with each individual to help them master personalized home-care routines.

Krista

Certified & Registered Dental Assistant (CDA, RDA)

Krista is our skilled chairside dental assistant who performs expanded clinical functions while assisting Dr. Del Presto during procedures. Her kind, compassionate nature instantly puts apprehensive patients at ease, making every visit as comfortable as possible. Krista takes the time to thoroughly guide patients through their treatment plans and provides clear, helpful instructions for aftercare and at-home maintenance.
