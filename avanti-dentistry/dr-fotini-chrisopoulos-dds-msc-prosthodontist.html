Advanced Education and Specialty Training

Dr. Chrisopoulos has built her career around a lifelong commitment to excellence in dentistry.

She graduated at the top of her class from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki in Greece, earning recognition for academic achievement and clinical excellence. Following dental school, she served as a dentist in the Greek Military National Guard before pursuing advanced specialty training in the United States.

Dr. Chrisopoulos completed her prosthodontic residency at Columbia University, where she graduated with honors and earned a Master of Science degree in Prosthodontics. She further advanced her expertise as Chief Fellow at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, where she completed extensive surgical implant training.

This unique combination of restorative, prosthodontic, and surgical education allows her to provide comprehensive solutions for even the most complex dental conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a prosthodontist?

A prosthodontist is a dental specialist with advanced training in restoring and replacing missing or damaged teeth. Prosthodontists are experts in dental implants, full-mouth rehabilitation, dentures, crowns, bridges, and complex restorative treatment.

Is Dr. Chrisopoulos board-certified?

Dr. Chrisopoulos is a board-eligible prosthodontist who has completed advanced specialty training in prosthodontics and surgical implant dentistry.

Does Dr. Chrisopoulos provide dental implant treatment?

Yes. She specializes in implant-supported restorations, including single implants, implant-supported dentures, full-mouth implant solutions, and Teeth-in-a-Day procedures.

Can prosthodontic treatment improve both function and appearance?

Absolutely. Prosthodontic care is designed to restore chewing function, comfort, oral health, and smile aesthetics through customized treatment solutions.

Does Dr. Chrisopoulos treat patients with complex dental needs?

Yes. She frequently provides treatment for patients requiring full-mouth rehabilitation, extensive restorative care, implant reconstruction, and other complex dental procedures.

Avanti Dentistry offers a unique model where all specialists are available in-house. This eliminates the need for referrals and allows for more efficient, coordinated care.
