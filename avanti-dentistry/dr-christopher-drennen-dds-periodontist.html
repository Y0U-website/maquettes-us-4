Frequently Asked Questions

What does a periodontist treat?

A periodontist specializes in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease and conditions affecting the supporting structures of the teeth.

Does Dr. Drennen place dental implants?

Yes, Dr. Drennen provides advanced implant treatment as part of comprehensive tooth replacement care.

What is LANAP treatment?

LANAP is a minimally invasive laser treatment for gum disease that promotes healing while preserving healthy tissue.

Can gum recession be treated without surgery?

Yes, procedures like pinhole gum treatment provide minimally invasive options for correcting gum recession.

Do I need a referral to see a periodontist?

No, patients can schedule directly with Avanti Dentistry for periodontal evaluations and treatment.
