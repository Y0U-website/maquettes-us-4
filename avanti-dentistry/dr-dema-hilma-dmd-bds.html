Education and Professional Background

After receiving her Bachelor’s degree in Dental Surgery, Dr. Hilmi earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Rutgers University, formerly known as the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. She also completed a postdoctoral program in Advanced Education in General Dentistry from NYU College of Dentistry.

While her education began in dentistry, Dr. Hilmi’s work at Avanti Dentistry is focused on facial esthetics and rejuvenation treatments.
