Education and Specialty Training

Dr. Lopez began her academic journey at the George Washington University, where she majored in Public Health. She then earned her dental degree from Howard University College of Dentistry, continuing her education in the Washington, DC area.

After eight years in the DC region, Dr. Lopez moved to Boston to complete her orthodontic specialty training at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. Her advanced training allows her to diagnose and treat a wide range of orthodontic concerns with precision and care.

Now back in Virginia, Dr. Lopez is excited to bring her experience and specialty expertise to patients throughout Tysons and the surrounding communities.
