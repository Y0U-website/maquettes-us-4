Extensive Training and Experience

Dr. Rowe brings a unique combination of advanced education, military service, and teaching experience to his role as an endodontist.

Originally from the Chicago area, Dr. Rowe earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Illinois Chicago College of Dentistry. He completed a post-graduate Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency at Fort Benning, Georgia, before serving as a general dentist in the United States Army.

He later earned his Endodontics Certificate from the University of Iowa and has spent more than 15 years practicing as an endodontist. Throughout his distinguished military career spanning more than 20 years, Dr. Rowe has treated patients across the country and served in both Iraq and Germany.

His extensive clinical background allows him to manage a wide range of complex endodontic cases with confidence and precision.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an endodontist do?

An endodontist is a dental specialist who diagnoses and treats conditions affecting the inside of the tooth, including infections, inflammation, and dental pain related to the root canal system.

Does Dr. Rowe perform root canals?

Yes. Dr. Rowe provides root canal therapy and other advanced endodontic treatments designed to save natural teeth and relieve discomfort.

What is endodontic retreatment?

Endodontic retreatment is performed when a previously treated tooth requires additional care due to persistent infection, healing concerns, or new complications.

Is root canal treatment painful?

Modern endodontic treatment is designed to be comfortable. Advanced techniques and anesthesia options help minimize discomfort during treatment.

Can I complete my treatment in one office?

Yes. Avanti Dentistry offers multi-specialty care under one roof, allowing patients to receive coordinated treatment in a single location whenever possible.
