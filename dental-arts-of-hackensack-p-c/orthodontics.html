Orthodontics

Braces

By applying constant and gentle pressure, braces can help to correct crooked teeth or a collapsed bite, improve your ability to bite, chew and speak, and enhance your overall appearance. They can also prevent cavities, gum disease, and problems related to mouth movement.

Clear Aligners

Removable clear aligners gradually move teeth without using metal braces or wires. They are an appropriate treatment when the teeth are fully erupted and there is not a large degree of crowding, spacing or malocclusion.

Retainers

It can take at least four to six months for the new position of your teeth to become permanent after the orthodontic treatment. That’s why we offer retainers to keep your teeth from moving after they’ve been straightened with braces.
