Dental Implants

Dental implants have three key elements: the implant , the abutment , and the crown . The dental implant, a metal rod that is inserted into the jawbone, acts as the tooth’s ‘root.’ The abutment is a connective fixture that will eventually attach to the crown, which looks and behaves like a natural tooth.

The Advantages of Dental Implants

Individuals who are missing multiple teeth sometimes opt for full or partial dentures. Patients lose track of removable appliances more often than you might think, and replacements can become costly!

Unlike other restorative solutions, dental implants allow you to once again enjoy all of your favorite foods. This includes even tough-to-chew foods like steak, corn on the cob, and apples.

Missing teeth have been linked to many different oral and general health concerns, including periodontitis, dental infections, and digestive issues.

Because dental implants and crowns are made from strong, synthetic materials, they rival the strength of your natural teeth and cannot become affected by tooth decay.

Looks aren’t everything, but if you find yourself covering your mouth when you smile, dental implants can help!

  • Permanent results.
  • Restored functionality.
  • Reduce certain health risks.
  • Impervious to tooth decay.
  • Speak, laugh, and smile confidently.

The Dental Implant Process

Our implant dentist in Woodland Hills, Dr. Yury Bershadsky, treats every patient based on their unique dental needs. The steps involved in placing a dental implant may vary depending on your one-of-a-kind smile.

Consultation & Examination

Before moving forward with a dental implant, our implant dentist in Woodland Hills will need to conduct a thorough examination of your mouth. This will include a visual inspection of your mouth, dental x-rays, and a consultation with you about your dental health.

Who is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

In order to be a good candidate for dental implants, you must already be in good oral and general health. Those who have certain ongoing health concerns, such as periodontitis or cavities, may need to seek treatment prior to undergoing a dental implant procedure.

Dental Impressions

Once you have been cleared for a dental implant, we will schedule you to return to our office so that impressions can be taken of your mouth. These impressions will provide our implant dentist in Woodland Hills with an accurate representation of the features of your smile so that the perfect dental crown can be made.

Getting Your Implant

Your implant will then be surgically placed into your jaw. It will take weeks to months before the dental implant is solidly fused to your bone. When our implant dentist in Woodland Hills is confident that your implant is fully fused, the crown will be securely attached at the gumline for a natural-looking result.
