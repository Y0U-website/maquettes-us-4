Crafting beautiful smiles every day

Cosmetic dentistry at Smith & Wetterhus Dentistry is about more than just aesthetics; it’s about giving you the confidence to smile without hesitation.

Our Puyallup cosmetic dentists understand that imperfections like stained, chipped, or misaligned teeth can affect how you feel about your smile. Our treatments, from professional whitening to complete smile makeovers, address these issues with precision and care. Our goal is to help you achieve the smile you’ve always wanted with results that look natural and feel great.

Perfect your smile with veneers

Veneers offer a versatile solution to many smile imperfections, including discoloration, chips, and gaps between teeth. By applying a thin porcelain veneer to the front of your teeth, we can create a bright, even, and attractive smile. Veneers are durable, long-lasting, and crafted to match the natural appearance of your teeth.
