How Invisalign Works

Instead of relying on brackets and wires, Invisalign patients wear a progressive series of clear plastic trays that slowly move their teeth into the desired positions. They just wear one set of trays for about 20-22 hours a day before progressing to the next set. Treatment can typically be completed in as little as 12-18 months, and patients will make regular progress checks with their dentist to ensure their teeth are on the right path.

The Benefits of Invisalign

  • Discreet: Invisalign trays slide onto the teeth and practically disappear, meaning patients don’t have to worry about friends or coworkers noticing their treatment.
  • Comfortable: Made of smooth plastic that is shaped to fit your mouth, Invisalign doesn’t cause the gum and cheek irritation common with metal braces.
  • Flexible: You can take Invisalign out whenever you eat or clean your teeth, meaning you don’t have to change up your daily routines.
