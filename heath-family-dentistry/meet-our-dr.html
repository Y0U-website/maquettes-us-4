Meet Dr. Winston Heath

With over a decade of experience in various dental practices, Dr. Heath brings a unique perspective to his own practice, Heath Family Dentistry. He recognizes that dental visits can be stressful, which is why he emphasizes compassion and kindness in patient care. The team at Heath Family Dentistry is dedicated to making dental experiences enjoyable.

Outside of work, Dr. Heath cherishes time with his wife, Julie, and their three young sons: Court (7), Connor (5), and Colton (2). The family loves fishing on weekends and exploring Texas’s charming small towns. You can often find them enjoying dinner at Gringo’s, followed by a cozy family movie night. They are also avid supporters of Aggie sports, frequently attending baseball, football, and basketball games together.
