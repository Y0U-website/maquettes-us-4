Adults over 35 lose more teeth to gum diseases (periodontal disease) than from cavities. Three out of four adults are affected at some time in their life. The best way to prevent cavities and periodontal disease is by good tooth brushing and flossing techniques, performed daily.

Periodontal disease and decay are both caused by bacterial plaque. Plaque is a colorless film, which sticks to your teeth at the gumline. Plaque constantly forms on your teeth. By thorough daily brushing and flossing you can remove these germs and help prevent periodontal disease. Learn more on our Oral Hygiene page.

What should I do immediately after my dental procedure? After your appointment, follow all instructions provided by your dentist and avoid eating until any numbness has completely worn off. Stick to soft foods for the first several hours, avoid extremely hot beverages, and be gentle around the treated area. If you experience any intense pain or bleeding please call our office .

Unless directed otherwise by your dentist, yes you should go about your normal dental routine. Maintaining oral hygiene is important. Just avoid brushing or flossing directly on the treated area for the first 24 hours, especially if you’ve had a surgical procedure or deep filling.
