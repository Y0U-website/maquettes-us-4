What Is Customized Orthodontic Treatment?

Customized orthodontic treatment is a personalized approach to straightening teeth and correcting misalignment. It involves creating a unique plan for each patient, considering their needs and goals. This type of treatment is designed to provide the best possible outcome in the shortest amount of time. At Atlanta Smiles, our experienced dental team will work with you to create a customized treatment plan tailored to you.

What Are the Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic treatment can provide many benefits, including improved oral health, self-confidence, and a more attractive smile. Straightening teeth can also help reduce the risk of tooth decay and gum disease. Additionally, orthodontic treatment can help correct jaw misalignment, leading to improved chewing and speaking.

Types of Orthodontic Treatment

Traditional Braces

We offer traditional braces with either metal or clear brackets. We use these to straighten limited cases in the aesthetic zone. This works by placing six to eight brackets on only the front teeth to straighten the teeth quickly. Patients can achieve their aesthetic goals without the hassle of keeping up with trays.

Clear Aligners

We offer a selection of clear aligner products for our patient’s convenience. Our primary clear aligner product is Reveal Aligners.

Reveal Aligners are removable, custom-made plastic trays that fit over your existing teeth. The benefit of Reveal Aligners, compared to other brands, is they do not require buttons or attachments to be placed on the teeth.

As with other types of clear aligner treatments, Reveal works by slowly moving the teeth into the desired position through a progression of trays worn over time to correct gaps and misaligned teeth. As an alternative to metal braces, clear aligners offer orthodontic correction without the bulk or pain of braces.

Frequent Questions

Can I Order Clear Aligners without a Dentist?

Technically yes. Some companies will provide clear aligners without a dental professional managing the process. However, moving teeth into proper alignment is an intricate process that can impact more than how teeth look. The roots of your teeth, facial bones, gum tissue, and jaw structure are all affected when you use orthodontics to move the teeth. To achieve a beautiful and healthy smile, your dental professional considers a range of factors, including your facial profile, bone for your teeth to anchor, and bite stability after treatment.

You need a trusted dental team to guide you through your options and monitor your treatment. Your oral health is too important to compromise. We recommend working with Atlanta Smiles to ensure proper treatment and outstanding results.

How Does the Process Work with Reveal Aligners?

First, we’ll schedule an orthodontic consultation to discuss your options. If we recommend Reveal Aligners and you choose to move forward with treatment, we’ll develop your customized treatment plan.

We’ll take scans of your teeth to provide to the manufacturer to produce your custom-fit, ultra-clear aligner trays to match your treatment plan. During your treatment process, we will regularly monitor your progress. We’ll address and adjust your plan if any issues arise.

How Much Do Reveal Aligners Cost?

The cost of clear aligners varies based on various factors. Reveal Aligners are comparable in price with other aligner brands. The treatment cost primarily depends on the number of trays needed and the length of treatment.
