Dr. Taylor McElwee

Dr. Taylor was born and raised in the suburbs of Philadelphia, PA, and in 2022, she made the Fox Valley her home when she married Jack, a native of Appleton. She is dedicated to providing excellent dental care and thrives on close, personal relationships with her patients.

Dr. Taylor knew early on that she wanted to be a dentist. In high school, she was accepted into Marquette University’s Pre-Dental Scholars program, an accelerated program during which she completed both her undergraduate and dental studies in just seven years. She graduated dental school with honors and was the recipient of two awards: the AAE Student Achievement Award, which recognized her exceptional skill level in the treatment of root canal therapy, and the Brian D. Stone Award, which awarded her efforts in helping patients with special needs.

Following dental school, she completed a one-year general practice residency at Lehigh Valley Hospital in Allentown, PA. She spent the year delivering advanced and comprehensive dental care in a hospital environment, focusing on surgery, trauma, and treating serious dental infections at the level 1 trauma center. She has been in private practice since and is excited to treat the patients of Little Chute and the surrounding area! Dr. Taylor is also a member of the American Dental Association (ADA), Wisconsin Dental Association (WDA), and the Fox Valley Dental Society.

Beyond dentistry, Dr. Taylor enjoys traveling with her husband and spending time on the golf course during the warmer months. She is an avid runner and has completed two full marathons. She loves to root for her Philadelphia Eagles and her Marquette Golden Eagles. Dr. Taylor and Jack recently welcomed their beautiful baby girl, Nellie, at the end of January!
