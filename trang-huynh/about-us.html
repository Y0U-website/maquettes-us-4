Haven Health Management is a Behavioral Health Operating Company

The Haven Detox Group is one of Haven Health Management’s three sub-organizations. The Haven Detox Group operates the eight Haven Detox treatment centers and is the brand under which those centers are organized.

Three Brands, 23 Treatment Locations, One Parent Operating Company

Haven Health Management operates three behavioral health treatment brands. Each brand has its own clinical leadership, branding, and patient population. All three operate under shared Joint Commission accreditation and Haven Health Management’s clinical governance.

The Behavioral Health Brands of Haven Health Management

The Haven Detox Group

Indiana Center for Recovery

Behavioral health treatment in Indiana, including residential and outpatient programs for substance use and co-occurring mental health conditions. Joint Commission accredited.

The Recovery Team

Recovery treatment programs in Florida, New Jersey, Arkansas, and Massachusetts — providing continuity-of-care options for patients moving between treatment phases. Operates in shared geographies with The Haven Detox Group to support ongoing aftercare and step-down support.

Shared Standards Across All 23 Locations

Haven Health Management treatment locations are individually licensed in their specific state, but all share a focus on patient safety, clinical quality, and evidence-based behavioral health care.

Across the network, locations maintain Joint Commission accreditation. The Joint Commission Gold Seal of Approval is held by fewer than 10 % of behavioral health treatment facilities and requires recurring third-party evaluation against patient safety and quality standards.

All Haven Health Management treatment locations are LegitScript certified. LegitScript certification helps verify that a treatment center meets standards for transparency, compliance, and responsible online healthcare advertising.

Individual locations hold additional credentials based on their state, programs, and levels of care. These include AHCA in Florida, BSAS in Massachusetts, CCSP in Arkansas, and Veteran Ready certifications at select centers.

Haven Health Management Leadership

Haven Health Management is led by a team with deep experience in behavioral health care, clinical operations, and organizational growth.

Brian Thorn was appointed CEO of Haven Health Management in April 2026 following the acquisition by MKH Capital Partners. He brings decades of behavioral health leadership experience, including tenures as Chief Operating Officer of Pinnacle Treatment Centers and Foundations Recovery Network.

Bruce Shear serves as Chairman of the Board of Haven Health Management. A behavioral health industry veteran, he previously served as Executive Vice Chairman of Acadia Healthcare and as a member of the board of directors for Newport Academy.

MKH Capital Partners is a private equity firm headquartered in Miami, founded by entrepreneurial families and professional investors. MKH acquired Haven Health Management in a nine-figure transaction that closed on March 31, 2026.
