Dental Cleaning

At our office, we have the latest and best technology to brush, floss, and clean your teeth. Our dentists and hygienists use specialized tools to remove buildup that may lead to decay and cavities. We offer tips and advice on how you can maintain the best dental hygiene on a daily basis.

Dental Exams

We will complete a whole examination of your teeth, gums, tongue and cheeks as well as your head and neck. We look for all sorts of dental issues which may include cavities, damaged teeth, gingivitis and most importantly oral cancer. We will also provide x-ray results to give a whole glimpse of your teeth.
