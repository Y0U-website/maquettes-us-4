Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

Orthodontics is about more than just appearance. Properly aligned teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of cavities and gum disease. Additionally, a balanced bite helps prevent excessive wear on your teeth and relieves stress on the jaw, which can mitigate symptoms of TMJ disorder. A straight, healthy smile also enhances your confidence, allowing you to feel comfortable in social and professional settings.
