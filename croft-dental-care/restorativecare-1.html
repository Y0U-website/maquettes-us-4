Preventative Care

CLEANINGS/HYGIENE

A healthy smile is in the preventive care. Dr. Croft and his staff provide regular oral check-ups and cleanings to detect gum disease and treat any symptoms. Scheduling regular check-ups with Dr. Croft will enhance the health and appearance of your gums and smile.

WHAT IS GUM DISEASE?

Gum disease is a buildup of plaque and bacteria around the teeth and into the gums. The more common symptoms include red or swollen gums, bad breath, and separation between teeth and gums. It can be difficult to remove this buildup by yourself. This is why it is important to schedule regular checkups and cleaning to remove and prevent gum disease. Dr. Croft’s office provides non-surgical periodontal care.

SCALING AND ROOT CLEANING

Dr. Croft and his team perform routine cleanings and, if necessary, deep scaling and root cleaning to remove substantial buildup and reduce the effects of gum disease.

SEALANTS

A sealing is a clear coat that goes over cleaned and polished teeth. This sealant provides a barrier against decay. This procedure is quick and does not involve any drilling. Just a quick polish, a couple coats and your set!
