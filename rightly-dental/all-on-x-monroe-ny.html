What is All-On-X , Full-Arch implants? All-on-X uses a small number of strategically placed dental implants to support a full set of fixed teeth . Unlike dentures, these teeth do not come out . They are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth . You may be a candidate if you;

• Are missing most or all of your teeth • Have loose or uncomfortable dentures • Have been told you need multiple extractions • Have gum disease or failing dental work • Want a permanent alternative to dentures
