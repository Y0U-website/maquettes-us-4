Dr. Ryan Jeong earned both his B.A. in Biology and Doctorate in Dental Surgery from NYU through an accelerated 7-year program. He then completed his residency at Jersey City Medical Center, where he received extensive training in emergency dental care, with a focus on safe and efficient extractions.

To further his surgical expertise, he completed Columbia University’s Implant Curriculum, specializing in the predictable restoration of missing teeth with dental implants.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Jeong enjoys spending time with his wife, Maria, and their daughter, Rina. He has also been an avid practitioner of jiu-jitsu since 2017, splitting his free time between family and training on the mat.
