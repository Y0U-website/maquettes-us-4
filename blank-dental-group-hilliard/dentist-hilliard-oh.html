Emergency Dentistry

When you have a dental emergency , you can count on Blank Dental to provide you with prompt, effective care to relieve your pain and improve your well-being.

TMJ Treatment

If you suffer from jaw pain, headaches, or difficulty chewing due to TMJ disorder, we offer customized TMJ treatment options to relieve discomfort and restore function. From oral appliances to bite adjustments, our solutions help reduce strain on the temporomandibular joint, providing lasting relief.

Sleep Apnea

We treat sleep apnea with custom oral appliances that gently reposition your jaw to keep the airway open—a quiet, travel-friendly CPAP alternative. Reduce snoring, improve sleep quality, and boost daytime energy.

Oral Surgery

Oral surgery treats issues like extractions and implants with precision and care. We use advanced techniques to ensure comfort, faster healing, and long-term results.

Restorative Procedures

Fillings

We use tooth-colored composite fillings to fill cavities and minor damage and blend them with your natural teeth.

Root Canals

Root canal therapy removes infection and relieves pain while preserving your natural tooth. With modern, gentle techniques, we clean and seal the canal. Many patients complete treatment with a custom crown for long-term strength.

Dental Implants

If you have missing teeth, dental implants are a permanent solution that looks and functions like natural teeth. Our implant services restore your confidence and function to your smile.

Dentures

Replace missing teeth and restore your smile with our custom dentures . Designed for comfort and a natural look, our dentures help you eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Periodontics

Protect your gums and preserve your smile with expert periodontal care . Our treatments target gum disease early to prevent bone loss and support long-term oral health. With personalized care and gentle techniques, we help restore comfort, stability, and confidence in your smile.

Full Mouth Reconstruction

Whether due to trauma, decay, or multiple missing teeth, we offer full mouth reconstruction by tailoring a combination of restorative and cosmetic procedures—such as dental implants, crowns, veneers, and bridges—to rebuild your smile.

Cosmetic Treatments

Veneers

Veneers are custom-made shells that cover the front of your teeth to improve color, shape, and overall appearance.

Bonding

Bonding is a minimally invasive procedure that uses tooth-colored resin to repair minor chips, cracks, or gaps in teeth.

Botox

Our cosmetic services include Botox, which can be used to treat TMJ issues or just for facial aesthetics.

Bridges and Crowns

Bridges and dental crowns restore function and appearance after tooth loss or damage. Crowns cover damaged teeth, and bridges fill gaps from missing teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Whiten your smile with our professional teeth whitening , which can whiten your teeth in several shades in one visit.

Invisalign

Invisalign is a discreet and convenient alternative to traditional braces. These clear aligners straighten your teeth without metal brackets or wires and are suitable for both teens and adults.
