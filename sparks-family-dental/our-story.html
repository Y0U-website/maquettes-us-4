OUR STORY

At the heart of Sparks Family Dental is a team of compassionate and dedicated dental professionals, united by the mission to deliver the highest quality care to our patients and their families. Your well-being is our top priority, and we are confident in our team’s ability to help you achieve and maintain your brightest, healthiest smile.​

Since Dr. McGrath laid the foundation of our practice in 1952, our legacy of quality dental care has been upheld and enriched by remarkable dentists who joined the practice over the years Dr. Galvin in 1972, Dr. Round in 1986, and Dr. Herrick in 2019 each contributing to our esteemed reputation in the Glastonbury and Greater Hartford communities. In 2022, Dr. Sparks became the latest to continue this proud tradition, bringing a fresh perspective on patient-centered care while honoring the values established by his predecessors.​

We derive immense satisfaction from assisting our patients in maintaining optimal oral health. Our practice is steadfast in its commitment to comprehensive and preventive care for every patient.​ We eagerly anticipate the opportunity to serve you and your family, ensuring a future filled with healthy smiles!
