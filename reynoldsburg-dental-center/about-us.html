Show More Dr. Naylor was born and raised in Iowa. He completed his undergraduate degree majoring in biology at Brigham Young University in Utah. During that time, he also lived in Mozambique, Africa for two years serving as a Christian missionary for his church.

He came to The Ohio State University for dental school and has loved Ohio since. He loves dentistry for the chance to help improve people’s health, working with his hands, and meeting and connecting with people. He is a member of the ADA (American Dental Association), the ODA (Ohio Dental Association), and CDS (Columbus Dental Society), and is passionate about how oral health is connected to whole body health. He also regularly attends a local study club of dentists and specialists called Advanced Dental Concepts to further his education and connections with dental colleagues.

Dr. Naylor lives in Canal Winchester and has a lovely wife, Liesen, who grew up in Chile before moving to Ohio. They both speak Spanish. They have two kids, Adelina and Cristiano. They love art and music, cooking, and traveling. Dr. Naylor also loves reading and writing.

Show More A lifetime resident of Ohio, Dr. Lenz attended Ohio State University’s Dental Hygiene program and received a Bachelor of Science in 1996. He continued his education at O.S.U.’s College of Dentistry and graduated in 2001. While going through dental school Dr. Lenz was also an instructor at Columbus State’s Dental Hygiene Program where he taught one of our hygienists.

Dr. Lenz began in private practice over twenty years ago and purchased Reynoldsburg Dental Center in 2003. He dedicated much of his personal time to renovating and remodeling the practice at our current location on 6504 E Main St.

To give patients the best care possible, Dr. Lenz stays up to date with continuing education classes and state-of-the-art dental technology. He is also a member of the ADA (American Dental Association), ODA (Ohio Dental Association), CDS (Columbus Dental Society), and GCSCD (Greater Columbus Society of Contemporary Dentistry).

Dr. Lenz has three wonderful boys, Maxwell, Wyatt and Andrew. He is a very dedicated family man who coaches his sons’ Little League football and baseball teams and is active in Cub Scouts. Dr. Lenz loves to be outdoors and is an avid fisherman.
