Your First Visit

What to expect

Because we know how important a healthy mouth is for our overall wellbeing and quality of life, your first visit is normally focused on where your health and smile are now, and where you would like to be. We’ll examine everything in and around the mouth--an exam that includes an oral cancer screening and airway assessment, as well as checking your tooth and gum health--and spend time educating you about what you can do at home to improve your health, explaining any concerns we might find, and the services we offer that can help address your needs.

We reserve significant time for this opportunity to get to know you, to hear your questions, and educate and empower you on your health journey. If any need to change your appointment arises, we greatly appreciate your advance notice.

We can’t wait to meet you and serve you!

- Dr. Ryan Naylor and the Reynoldsburg Dental Center team
