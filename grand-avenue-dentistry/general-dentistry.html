Regular dental visits are the foundation of a healthy smile. Preventive care helps you avoid pain, expensive treatments, and keeps your smile looking its best.

Why Are Oral Cancer Screenings Important?

Oral cancer screening are an essential part of comprehensive dental care. During routine visits, we evaluate your oral tissues for signs of abnormalities that could indicate oral cancer. Like many forms of cancer, oral cancer is often more successfully treated when discovered through early detection.

What Are the Risk Factors for Oral Cancer?

Several factors may increase a person’s risk, including:

Individuals with one or more risk factors may be considered high risk and benefit from routine screenings.

  • Tobacco use
  • Heavy alcohol consumption
  • Human papillomavirus (HPV)
  • Excessive sun exposure to the lips
  • Family history of cancer

Why Does Early Detection Matter?

Finding oral cancer during its early stage can significantly improve treatment outcomes. Regular oral cancer screenings allow dentists to identify concerning changes before symptoms become severe.

How Do Fluoride Treatments and Dental Sealants Help?

Two of the most effective preventive dentistry tools are fluoride treatments and dental sealants. Together, fluoride treatments and dental sealants play an important role in preventing tooth decay and preserving long-term oral health.

Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride helps strengthen tooth enamel and reduce the risk of cavities. Professional fluoride treatments can be particularly beneficial for children, teenagers, and adults who are more susceptible to tooth decay.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants create a protective barrier over the chewing surfaces of teeth, helping prevent bacteria and food particles from becoming trapped in deep grooves.
