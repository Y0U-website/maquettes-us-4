What Dental Services are Available in Diamond Bar?

Grand Avenue Dentistry provides dental care for patients at different stages of their oral health journey. Whether you are focused on prevention or need treatment for an existing problem, we can help you understand your options.

Preventive Dental Care

Routine dental care can help identify problems before they become more serious. Regular exams, cleanings, X-rays, and preventive treatments allow your dentist to monitor your oral health and address concerns early.

Preventive services include:

  • Routine dental exams
  • Professional teeth cleanings
  • Digital X-rays
  • Fluoride treatments
  • Oral health evaluations

Restorative Dentistry

If a tooth has been damaged by decay, injury, or normal wear, restorative treatment can help restore its function and appearance.

Restorative treatments include:

  • Tooth-colored fillings
  • Dental crowns
  • Root canal therapy
  • Tooth extractions

Emergency Dental Care

Grand Avenue Dentistry offers same-day treatment for urgent dental needs when possible. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, contact the office as soon as possible so we can determine the appropriate next step.

Dental emergencies could include:

  • Jaw pain
  • Broken tooth
  • Severe toothache
  • Swelling
  • Infection

Sedation Dentistry

Dental anxiety can make it difficult to schedule or complete necessary dental care. Grand Avenue Dentistry offers nitrous oxide sedation to help eligible patients feel more relaxed during their appointments.

Procedures that can include sedation are:

  • Dental exams
  • Teeth cleanings
  • Dental fillings
  • Dental Crowns
  • X-rays

How Often Should I Visit A Dentist?

Most patients benefit from routine dental examinations and professional cleanings based on their individual oral health needs. Your dentist can recommend an appropriate schedule after evaluating your teeth, gums, hygiene habits, and risk factors.

Regular appointments are important because some dental problems may not cause noticeable symptoms in their early stages.

  • Check for cavities and tooth damage
  • Evaluate your gums
  • Monitor changes in your oral health
  • Identify potential problems early
  • Provide professional cleaning
  • Recommend appropriate preventive care
