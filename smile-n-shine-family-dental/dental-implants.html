What Is A Dental Implant?

Your Guide To The Basics

Dental implants are small, screw-shaped titanium rods that are surgically implanted into your gums and jaw bone. Once implanted, they permanently bond with the jaw bone, and function as an artificial “root.” ‍ Depending on whether you get one or more implants, a variety of dental implant restorations can be attached to them, such as a crown, a dental bridge, or an implant-supported denture. These dental prostheses replace your natural teeth, restoring your smile.

The Benefits

Dental Implants

Dental implants have a wide variety of benefits. First, they can last 30+ years with proper care, which is much longer than alternatives like dentures and dental bridges. In addition, implants help strengthen and support your jaw bone, preventing bone resorption after tooth loss. ‍ Implants also look and feel completely natural and will never shift or move when you chew or speak. They’re easy to care for, too. Single-tooth implants, for example, can be treated just like a natural tooth. All you have to do is brush and floss regularly and see Dr. Gandhi for a checkup every six months.
