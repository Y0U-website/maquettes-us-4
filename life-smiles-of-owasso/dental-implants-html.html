What Are Dental Implants?

By itself, a dental implant post could be mistaken for a screw. It is made out of titanium, a metal known for being biocompatible. When dental implants are inserted into your jaw, they can form a lasting bond with the surrounding bone thanks to osseointegration. By combining the right number of dental implants with a crown, bridge, or denture, our team is able to replace any number of teeth in your smile from the roots up.

What is Facial Collapse?

Your teeth give your smile its height, and even contribute to the functioning of your jaw and facial muscles. Losing some, most, or all of your teeth will cause your nose and chin to meet closer together when you bite down. Wrinkles and sagging skin will also quickly form around your mouth when there is no framework of teeth to support your facial tissues. This is the first stage of facial collapse.

The bone resorption process shrinks the area where your tooth used to be, creating a cratered appearance. If you are missing all or many of the teeth on the lower half of your mouth, then, over a period of 10 or 20 years, your entire jawbone will gradually shrink in height. As your smile height collapses, facial wrinkles will deepen, and your profile will look aged. You may experience this even if you’ve replaced your teeth with dentures.

While dentures are a great option for many people, not everyone can wear them long-term due to the impact on their facial profile. Dentures concentrate the bite pressure on your gums and the top of your jawbone when your bite pressure is supposed to be concentrated in your jawbone. This excess pressure actually speeds up the process of facial collapse, causing many people to ditch the dentures and replace them with dental implants. You can regain some of the facial height you lost by doing so!

When you replace lost teeth with dental implants, the implant posts act like natural tooth roots and stimulate your jawbone to prevent shrinking. This makes dental implants the superior tooth replacement option compared to removable dentures, which merely rest on top of your gums.

The 5-Step Dental Implant Process

Here is what you can expect from your dental implant treatment at Life Smiles of Owasso.

Step 1. Evaluation

Dr. Lim will perform a thorough examination of your dental health and bite to determine whether dental implants are right for you. You will have X-rays, photographs, and even a CT scan taken in order to capture a comprehensive picture of the structures in and around your mouth. Dr. Lim will let you know if he recommends dental implants in your case, then review with you a suggested treatment plan that has been customized to your needs and preferences.

Step 2. Implant Placement

In a brief procedure, Dr. Lim will administer local anesthetic to the treatment site and then carefully create an opening in your gums and bone. He will place the implant screw in the site and then close up the gum tissue around it to allow the area to heal undisturbed. If you have a tooth extracted prior to getting a dental implant, Dr. Lim may recommend building up the bone tissue in the socket to ensure a strong foundation for the post. If you are nervous about the procedure, conscious sedation is available for your comfort.

Step 3. Healing

It takes several months for a dental implant to create a tight bond with the surrounding bone tissue in a process called osseointegration. A dental implant can only bear the forces of biting and chewing once it has integrated with the bone. It may take three to six months for the area around your implant to fully heal. In the meantime, your implant dentist in Owasso will carefully monitor your progress. But don’t worry, we’ll give you a temporary restoration while you heal.

Step 4. Abutment Placement

The abutment is a small piece that connects your restoration to your dental implant screw. In most cases, the abutment is placed after the implant has successfully integrated with the bone tissue.

Step 5. Restoration

Your implant dentist will begin designing your restoration at the start of your dental implant journey. You will be fitted for your restoration once the area around your implant has healed. We make dental implant restorations out of zirconia, a ceramic material that looks like natural tooth enamel but is as strong as metal.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants are the most beautiful and lifelike way to replace lost teeth, but they also offer a host of incredible oral and overall health benefits that can make them a smart long-term investment in your wellbeing. Here’s a quick guide to how receiving implants at our dentist’s office in Owasso can improve your life.

Day-to-Day Benefits

  • Boost your confidence Dental implants can help you feel better about the appearance of your smile because they fill in unsightly gaps and allow you to present a lifelike grin.
  • Natural look and feel When implant placement and restoration are done well, no one will ever be able to tell that your dental implant is not a real tooth. Implants also feel and function like natural teeth so that you can speak and eat comfortably.
  • Confidence and Security Traditional restorations like bridges and dentures may slip out of place when speaking or chewing and lead to embarrassing situations. Since dental implants are firmly anchored in your jawbone, you won’t have to worry about the prosthetic devices mounted on them moving in ways healthy real teeth would not.
  • Tastier Diet Dental implants can restore up to 80 % of your chewing ability, which means you’ll be able to enjoy all sorts of interesting foods like crunchy snacks, raw veggies, tender steaks, and corn-on-the-cob.

Health Benefits

  • Enjoy improved nutrition Dental implants are just as strong as natural tooth roots. You can chew all kinds of foods easily and comfortably with dental implants. This opens up more possibilities when it comes to choosing a healthy diet.
  • Preserve the health of the jaw The jaw loses the exercise it needs to stay healthy and strong after tooth loss, which can cause it to atrophy. Dental implants address this problem by stimulating growth in the jawbone during the chewing process.
  • Preventing dental damage Traditional restorations can slide about on the gums if they don’t fit right, which can lead to soft tissue irritation and enamel damage. Since dental implants are rooted in the jawbone just like real teeth, they won’t harm your gum tissue or other parts of your smile.

Long-Term Benefits

  • Avoid facial collapse Dental implants prevent bone resorption and preserve the height of your smile. A traditional denture can temporarily improve your profile, but it cannot stop the gradual loss of bone in your jaw.
  • Long-lasting Unlike dentures and other tooth replacements, dental implants have the potential to last you a lifetime if you take excellent care of them.
  • Cost-effective Since restorations mounted upon dental implants can last much longer than traditional devices, they can save a patient vast sums of money by negating the need to receive costly replacement appliances every few years.

Missing One Tooth

Missing Multiple Teeth

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Like many other patients, you may have chosen dental implants over the other tooth-replacement solutions that are available because they can last for 30+ years. The key to prolonging the lifespan of yours is incorporating the right habits into your routine and breaking the harmful ones. More specifically, our team at Life Smiles of Owasso recommends:

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Taking good care of your smile at home is crucial. After all, even though your dental implants cannot decay, your gums can develop an infection if they aren’t taken care of. So, if you want to have a smile that’s as healthy as it is beautiful, you need to brush twice a day, floss and rinse with mouthwash daily, and replace your toothbrush every 3-4 months.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Of course, dental implant care in Owasso goes beyond just brushing and flossing your teeth. You also need to eat healthy, well-balanced meals to ensure your body gets the nutrients, vitamins, and minerals that it needs. You should also exercise caution with crunchy foods, like tortilla chips, hard pretzels, and raw almonds since your restoration can break with enough pressure.

Break Bad Habits

As we mentioned above, taking care of your smile is just as much about what you should NOT do. We don’t recommend biting your nails, chewing on ice cubes, or using your teeth as tools since the immense pressure can cause the restoration that’s secured on top of your dental implant to break. We also advise against smoking since doing so increases the risk of dental implant failure.

Protect Your Dental Implants

Do you like to play pickleball or tennis? Maybe you prefer to lift weights, ride your bike, or run on your own. Whatever the case may be, it’s important to protect your dental implants from unexpected impact, like a rogue ball. That’s why you need to wear a mouthguard, even during non-contact sports. If you don’t have one already, don’t hesitate to contact our team at Life Smiles of Owasso.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

As we mentioned above, dental implants cannot decay. However, that doesn’t mean you can skip your biannual visits. In fact, these routine appointments are still extremely important because they allow us to screen for oral cancer, examine the condition of your dental implants, look for warning signs of gum disease, and remove built-up plaque and tartar.

Dental Implant FAQs

How Long Do Dental Implants Last?

Dental implants can often last for 30 or more years, but their lifespan greatly depends on your individual health and lifestyle.

To make sure your dental implants last as long as possible, be sure to brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash daily. Also, don’t forget to attend your regular, preventive dental exams and cleanings.

Never chew on anything that is too hard or sticky, or open packaging with your teeth.

Smoking and using tobacco or nicotine products has also been linked to dental implant failure.

Can I Take Dental Implants Out?

No, you cannot remove dental implants yourself. They are fixed to the bone and can only be removed by a dental professional. Never attempt to remove your dental implants yourself.

There are some types of implant dentures that can be removed by the patient for cleaning.

Does Getting Dental Implants Hurt?

The good news is that your dentist will numb your mouth with local anesthetic before your dental implant procedure begins, so you should not feel any pain.

The jawbone doesn’t have many nerve endings to begin with; however, while the surgery itself shouldn’t hurt, your mouth may be sore for the next few days after the procedure.
