At Sol Dental, our mission is to transform lives by restoring confidence, function, and aesthetics through personalized and expert prosthodontic care. We are dedicated to providing exceptional dental solutions that enhance the quality of life for our patients.

During these appointments, our dental professionals conduct a thorough examination of your teeth, gums, and oral structures to detect potential issues such as cavities, gum disease, or oral cancer. Additionally, we perform teeth cleaning to eliminate plaque and tartar buildup, addressing these issues promptly to prevent tooth decay and gum disease.

If cavities are discovered during your check-up, we will perform dental fillings to repair the affected tooth, preventing further decay. We offer both traditional metal fillings and tooth-colored composite fillings that seamlessly blend with the natural color of your teeth.

In instances where a tooth is extensively damaged or decayed beyond repair, we may perform extractions with utmost care to ensure minimal discomfort.

Prosthodontics stands as a specialized field within dentistry, dedicated to the restoration and replacement of missing or damaged teeth. Within our dental practice, we provide an extensive array of prosthodontic services, all aimed at assisting our patients in attaining not only optimal oral health but also a radiant and beautiful smile.

Les soins

If a tooth cannot be effectively repaired with a filling, we may suggest a crown or bridge. A crown acts as a cap, restoring the damaged tooth’s shape and function, while a bridge is employed to replace one or more missing teeth.
