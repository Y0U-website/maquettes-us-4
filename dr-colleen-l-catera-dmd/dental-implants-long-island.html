Health Benefits of Dental Implants

Beyond restoring your appearance, dental implants offer real health advantages:

Whether you’re missing one tooth or multiple, dental implants are a long-term solution that supports your overall wellness.

  • Prevent Bone Loss: When you lose a tooth, the jawbone in that area begins to shrink. Implants keep your jawbone strong.
  • Improve Stability: Unlike removable dentures, implants don’t slip or shift when eating or speaking.
  • Enhance Speech: Restoring missing teeth improves clarity and pronunciation.
  • Boost Confidence: You’ll feel comfortable smiling, laughing, and enjoying meals again.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants What are dental implants, and how do they work? Dental implants are small titanium posts that act as artificial tooth roots. They’re surgically placed into the jawbone, where they fuse with the bone over time. Once healed, a custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached creating a long-lasting, natural-looking replacement for missing teeth.

  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients report minimal discomfort. The implant placement is typically done under local anesthesia, and any post-procedure soreness can be managed with over-the-counter medication. We’ll make sure you’re completely comfortable before, during, and after your procedure.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last 15 years or longer often a lifetime. Good oral hygiene, regular dental checkups, and avoiding smoking can help extend the life of your implant.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The process typically takes a few months, as the implant needs time to integrate with your jawbone. After the healing phase, your custom restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) is placed. The result is worth the wait strong, secure, and natural.
  • Am I a candidate for dental implants? Most healthy adults with one or more missing teeth are candidates. You’ll need enough jawbone to support the implant, and we’ll assess that during your consultation. Even if you’ve been missing a tooth for a while, we can often restore your smile with additional treatments, such as bone grafting.
  • What’s the difference between implants, bridges, and dentures? Implants replace the entire tooth, including the root, offering unmatched stability and bone support. Bridges rely on adjacent teeth for support, and dentures rest on the gums. Implants are considered the most natural and long-term solution for missing teeth.
  • Will my dental implant look like a real tooth? Yes! The restoration placed on top of the implant is custom-crafted to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth. Most patients say they can’t tell the difference and neither can anyone else!
