The practice

The office of Dr. Colleen L. Catera, DMD is a family dental practice in Babylon Village, providing expert restorative, preventive, and cosmetic dentistry with a calm, judgment-free approach. We are located at 1 John St, Suite 206 one block from Main Street, with parking on site.

Serving Babylon Village and the South Shore: patients visit us from West Babylon and Lindenhurst (about 5 minutes away), North Babylon (about 8 minutes via Deer Park Avenue), and Deer Park, Bay Shore, Copiague, and Amityville (10 12 minutes).

Compassionate, Gentle Dentistry We pride on the compassion, professionalism, and care we deliver to each and every patient.

The cornerstone of Dr. Catera’s practice is establishing honest and open relationships with her patients. She is a caring practitioner, treating her patients with respect, kindness and compassion.

Over the years, Dr. Catera has treated patients with dental anxiety, and she has been able to help many of these patients conquer their fear of the dentist. She provides patients with an exceptional level of restorative, cosmetic, and preventive dental care.

We strive to educate our patients regarding any dental health conditions, as well as any recommended treatment. We feel it is imperative for our patients to be part of their dental health.

Therefore, we spend time to ensure that our patients understand their dental treatment, along with any recommendations to maintain their dental health.

Our office is accepting new patients and would love to have you and your family become part of ours.
