Our Cosmetic Dentistry Services

At Dr. Colleen L. Catera DMD , we offer a range of cosmetic dental treatments designed to enhance your smile.

Composite Restorations

Natural-Looking Tooth Repairs

Composite restorations, also known as tooth-colored fillings , blend perfectly with your natural teeth, making them aesthetic and functional .

  • Restore chipped , cracked , or decayed teeth seamlessly
  • Tooth-colored material for a completely natural look
  • Durable and long-lasting and minimally invasive

Porcelain Veneers

The Ultimate Smile Makeover

If you’re looking for a dramatic transformation , porcelain veneers are an excellent choice. These thin, custom-made shells are bonded to the front of your teeth for a flawless, natural look.

  • Instantly correct stains, gaps, misalignment, and chips
  • Crafted to match your desired shape, size, and color
  • Long-lasting and resistant to stains and discoloration

ZOOM! Whitening

Instantly Brighter Teeth

Get instant, long-lasting results with ZOOM! Whitening, a professional treatment that can enhance your smile’s brightness in just one session.

  • Whiten teeth up to 8 shades lighter
  • Available as an in-office service or take home kits
  • Safe, fast, and professionally monitored
  • Removes stains from coffee, wine, smoking, and more
