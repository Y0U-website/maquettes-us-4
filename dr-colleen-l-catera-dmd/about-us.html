Dr. Colleen L. Catera DMD

Dr. Colleen L. Catera earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the University of Pennsylvania School of Dental Medicine in 1996. After practicing in both New York and Connecticut, she returned to her native Long Island in 2011 to be closer to family. That same year, she purchased a dental practice in West Babylon, which she relocated to a newly renovated, state-of-the-art facility in Babylon Village in May 2018. Patients have described this new location as peaceful and calming.

Dr. Catera emphasizes building honest and open relationships with her patients, treating them with respect, kindness, and compassion. She has extensive experience assisting patients with dental anxiety, helping many overcome their fears. Her practice offers advanced technologies, including digital x-rays, intraoral cameras, and Zoom in-office whitening.

In 2013, Dr. Catera became a certified Invisalign provider and has since achieved Silver Provider status. She engages in ongoing education, attending the Greater New York Dental Meeting annually since 1997 and participating in study clubs with local oral surgeons and periodontists. In 2015, she trained at the Zimmer Institute in Carlsbad, CA, to become certified in restoring "All on 4" implant cases.

Dr. Catera is a member of the American Dental Association, the Suffolk County Dental Society, and the New York State Dental Association. She is committed to community service, volunteering with organizations such as the Connecticut Mission of Mercy and participating in events like "Dentistry from the Heart." In 2016, she hosted a Children’s Free Dental Day at her office and has delivered oral health presentations at various elementary schools in New York and Connecticut.

In her personal life, Dr. Catera is married to her husband, Hayden, and they have a Chihuahua named Luna. She enjoys traveling, cooking, yoga, weight training, and taking dance classes.

Alexandra: Dental Hygienist

With over 15 years of experience, Alexandra is a highly skilled dedicated dental hygienist who creates a welcoming, trusting environment where patients feel heard and cared for. Helping patients overcome dental anxieties is especially gratifying, along with the dental endoscope she is focused on helping patients achieve optimal oral health and preserve their natural teeth.

A key part of her approach is patient education. She emphasizes practical oral hygiene techniques and provides realistic, effective strategies patients can easily integrate into their daily routines. By combining this education with advanced technology she ensures every patient receives the highest level of care, customized to their needs.

Certified in using the dental endoscope, a cutting-edge tool that provides a nonsurgical solution for treating periodontal disease, she offers an advanced alternative to traditional cut and stitch surgery. Unlike conventional periodontal treatments, the dental endoscope allows for precise treatment, targeting the problem at its source while keeping the procedure minimally invasive.

This innovative approach leads to improved periodontal health, faster recovery times, and greater comfort, all without the need for painful surgery

Alexandra earned her Associate of Applied Science in Dental Hygiene from Briarcliffe College, where she was recognized on the Dean’s List , President’s List and received the Compassionate Caregiver Award . Throughout her career, she has worked alongside general dentists and specialists across Long Island, honing her expertise in comprehensive patient care.

Today, she combines her experience with state-of-the-art technology to deliver exceptional results for her patients.
