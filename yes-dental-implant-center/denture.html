Benefits of Dentures

  • Improved Chewing Ability: Restores the ability to eat a variety of foods.
  • Enhanced Speech: Helps eliminate speech difficulties caused by missing teeth.
  • Natural Appearance: Custom-designed to match your natural gums and facial structure.
  • Facial Support: Prevents facial sagging by providing proper structural support.
  • Affordable Solution: Cost-effective compared to other tooth replacement options.

Denture Process at YES Dental Implant Center

  • Initial Consultation – Our dentists will assess your oral health and determine the best denture solution for you.
  • Impressions & Measurements – We take precise measurements and impressions to create custom dentures that fit comfortably.
  • Fitting & Adjustments – Once your dentures are ready, we ensure they fit perfectly and make any necessary adjustments for maximum comfort.
  • Final Placement & Care Guidance – We provide instructions on proper denture care to help maintain their longevity and function.

Caring for Your Dentures

To ensure durability and comfort, follow these denture care tips:

  • Clean your dentures daily using a soft-bristled brush and mild cleanser.
  • Soak dentures overnight in a denture-cleaning solution.
  • Handle them with care to prevent damage.
  • Schedule regular dental check-ups for adjustments and maintenance.
