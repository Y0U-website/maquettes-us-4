The Professional Side

His philosophy is to treat every patient the way he would want someone he cares about to be treated. That means being thorough, being honest about what he sees, and recommending treatment because it’s the right thing for the patient—not simply because something can be done.

From the first phone call to the way someone is greeted, how clearly he explains a diagnosis, how smoothly treatment is delivered, and how he follows up afterward, Dr. Arthur believes every interaction should reinforce that the patient is in good hands. He makes treatment as efficient, comfortable, and seamless as possible, while ensuring patients always feel informed and cared for. If a patient feels comfortable sending a friend or family member to him, that’s one of the greatest compliments he can have.
