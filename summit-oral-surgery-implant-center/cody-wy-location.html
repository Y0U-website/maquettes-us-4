Dental Implants & Oral Surgery in Cody, Wyoming

Summit Oral Surgery provides oral and maxillofacial surgery in Cody, WY, with care that supports long-term oral health and function. From dental implants to surgical extractions, our team works closely with referring dentists and orthodontists when surgery is part of an overall treatment plan.

Procedures are performed by oral surgeons with advanced specialty training. Sedation options may be available when appropriate to support comfort and peace of mind during treatment.

Dental Implants

Dental implants can replace one or more missing teeth with a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or full-arch restoration. Implant care is planned around your goals, your health history, and the anatomy of your jaw for a result that looks natural and functions well.

If your jawbone needs additional support, bone grafting may be recommended to provide a strong foundation for implant placement. We can also coordinate timing with your restorative dentist so the surgical phase and final restoration stay aligned.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth do not always erupt the way they should. When they become impacted or hard to keep clean, they can lead to pain, infection, crowding, or damage to nearby teeth.

Our team evaluates your situation with an exam and imaging, then explains recommended timing, what to expect during recovery, and whether sedation may be appropriate for your needs.

Explore how evaluation and wisdom teeth removal work

Jaw Surgery

Jaw surgery (orthognathic surgery) may be recommended when jaw alignment affects bite function, comfort, or overall oral health. In these cases, surgery can help the jaws fit together more effectively and support long-term stability.

Your consultation focuses on clear guidance: what the evaluation shows, what options exist, and how surgical timing may coordinate with other care when appropriate.

Bone Grafting

Bone grafting can help rebuild or preserve jawbone structure after tooth loss, infection, or trauma. It is often used to support future dental implant placement and promote a healthier foundation for long-term function.

If bone grafting is recommended, we’ll explain why it applies to your case, what healing typically involves, and how it supports the next step in your plan.

Tooth Extractions

A surgical tooth extraction may be recommended when a tooth is severely damaged, infected, impacted, or cannot be removed safely through routine care. Extractions are planned carefully to protect surrounding teeth and bone and support predictable healing.

When appropriate, sedation options may be available based on procedure complexity and patient needs.

All-on-4® / Hybrid / Teeth-in-a-Day

For patients missing many teeth, All-on-4® (sometimes called hybrid implants or Teeth-in-a-Day) can be an option for replacing a full arch with strategically placed implants. This approach may allow for a secure, functional smile with a treatment plan designed around stability and comfort.
