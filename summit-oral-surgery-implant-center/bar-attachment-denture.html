Bar Attachment Dentures

When it comes to full-mouth tooth replacement, the Teeth in a Day procedure—also known as All-on-Four or a fixed hybrid implant bridge—has revolutionized how we restore an entire arch of missing or failing teeth. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on the gums and may slip or limit food choices, this solution is permanently secured in place. The result is a confident, natural-looking smile with the strength and stability to function like real teeth.

This procedure replaces a full arch of teeth using as few as four strategically placed dental implants. These implants act as artificial tooth roots, anchoring a custom-designed, fixed bridge that does not need to be removed. Because the implants are precisely positioned to maximize available bone, many patients can avoid extensive grafting—reducing overall treatment time and cost while maintaining exceptional strength and long-term durability.

In most cases, temporary fixed teeth are placed the same day as surgery, allowing you to leave our office with a functional, beautiful smile. During the healing phase, the implants integrate with the jawbone to create a solid foundation. Once healing is complete, your final custom bridge is delivered—designed for optimal aesthetics, comfort, and long-term performance.

With a fixed implant bridge, you can enjoy the freedom to eat the foods you love, speak clearly, and smile confidently—without the inconvenience or limitations of traditional dentures.
