Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term solution for replacing missing teeth and restoring oral function. At Summit Oral Surgery in Miles City, implant placement is performed on-site using advanced surgical techniques. When needed, procedures such as bone grafting may be recommended to support proper implant placement and long-term success. Our oral surgeons coordinate care with your restorative dentist or orthodontist.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Wisdom teeth may cause pain, infection, or damage to nearby teeth when they do not erupt properly. Our surgeons evaluate each patient to determine whether removal is recommended and the appropriate timing for treatment. Sedation options may be used when appropriate to support comfort during the procedure.

Jaw Surgery

Jaw surgery may be recommended when jaw alignment or function affects oral health, comfort, or orthodontic treatment. Our oral surgeons in Miles City evaluate each case using clinical exams and imaging and coordinate recommendations with the referring provider before proceeding.

Tooth Extractions

Tooth extractions may be needed when a tooth is damaged, infected, or causing oral health issues. At Summit Oral Surgery in Miles City, extractions are performed with a focus on comfort, safety, and proper healing. Sedation options are available when appropriate, and additional planning may support future treatment such as dental implants.

Learn more about Tooth Extraction services.

All-on-4® / Hybrid / Teeth-in-a-Day

All-on-4®, also known as hybrid implants or Teeth-in-a-Day, is an advanced option for replacing a full arch of missing teeth. This approach uses strategically placed implants to support a complete set of teeth, often with a temporary restoration placed the same day as surgery. At Summit Oral Surgery in Miles City, our surgeons perform the surgical phase with careful planning and offer sedation when appropriate.
