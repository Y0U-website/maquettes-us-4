Accessibility Statement

Updated: July 31, 2025

At Forever Smiles Dental & Braces, we are committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We believe all patients, including those with diverse needs, should be able to access the information and services provided through our website, https://www.foreversmilestx.com, without barriers.

Our Commitment

We actively work to ensure our website aligns with:

Our goal is to offer an accessible and user-friendly experience for all individuals, including those using screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice recognition software, or other assistive technologies.

  • The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
  • The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA
  • Relevant federal and Texas state accessibility standards

Accessibility Features

We have taken the following steps to enhance accessibility:

  • Structured, semantic HTML for better screen reader compatibility
  • Alt text for images and meaningful link descriptions
  • Logical heading structure and navigable menus
  • Keyboard accessibility throughout the website
  • Accessible forms with proper field instructions
  • Color contrast that supports visual clarity
  • Responsive design optimized for mobile and tablet devices

Help us get better

If you experience difficulty accessing any content on our website or require assistance, please let us know. We will do our best to ensure you receive the information or service you need in a format that works for you.

Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We continue to improve our digital accessibility and welcome feedback from all users to help guide future enhancements.
