Orthodontics

  • Invisalign involves a series of clear aligners instead of metal braces.
  • Digital impressions of your teeth are taken to customize the aligners.
  • You’ll receive a set of plastic aligners, changing them approximately every 2 weeks.
  • It’s recommended to wear the aligners for 22 hours daily, removing them only for eating and drinking (except water).

Traditional Braces

Metal braces use consistent, gentle pressure to gradually shift teeth into proper alignment.

If you’re ready to achieve the smile of your dreams, schedule a consultation with Bishop Family Dentistry Orthodontics today. We look forward to helping you achieve a lifetime of healthy, beautiful smiles!

  • Metal brackets are affixed to your teeth using adhesive.
  • An archwire connects the brackets, applying the necessary pressure to move teeth.
  • Braces effectively address issues like crooked teeth, crowding, misaligned bites, and orthodontic challenges affecting eating and speech.
  • Periodic adjustments are required to maintain adequate pressure.
  • The duration of wearing metal braces varies depending on individual correction needs and specific orthodontic issues being addressed.
