Dental Implants

  • Versatile Applications: Suitable for single-tooth replacement, multiple teeth, or anchoring complete upper and lower dentures, dental implants offer a comprehensive solution.

Integration and Crown Placement

The dental implant process involves:

  • Placement: The implant is surgically placed in the missing tooth area.
  • Integration: Allowance for 4-6 months for the implant to integrate seamlessly into the bone.
  • Crown Placement: Following successful integration, a crown is expertly placed on the implant, restoring both function and aesthetics.

Benefits of Dental Implants:

  • Natural Look and Feel: Implants provide a natural-looking and functional solution, mimicking the appearance and performance of natural teeth.
  • Bone Health: Implants stimulate bone growth, promoting long-term oral health and preventing bone loss.
  • Stability and Durability: Enjoy the stability and durability of dental implants, ensuring a confident and long-lasting smile.
