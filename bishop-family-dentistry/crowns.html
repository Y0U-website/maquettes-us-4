Restore Your Smile with Fixed Partial Dentures (FPD) - The Bridge Solution

Fixed Partial Dentures, commonly known as a "bridge," offer a reliable solution to replace a missing tooth with a secure prosthesis. This procedure involves preparing the adjacent teeth for crowns, and these crowns are then connected to form a stable structure that seamlessly replaces the missing tooth.

Procedure Overview

The teeth on both sides of the gap left by the missing tooth are prepared for crowns, forming the foundation for the bridge. The crown attached to these prepared teeth supports the prosthetic tooth, creating a fixed restoration that does not come in and out of the mouth.

Benefits of Bridges:

  • Prevention of Tooth Shifting: Bridges play a crucial role in preventing the shifting of adjacent teeth, which can lead to bite (occlusion) issues.
  • TMJ and Periodontal Health: By maintaining the alignment of teeth, bridges contribute to preventing TMJ problems and periodontal issues associated with tooth movement.
  • Improved Chewing Function: Fixed bridges ensure stability during mastication, enhancing the ability to chew comfortably.
