Partial Dentures

Partial dentures, akin to complete dentures, offer a comprehensive solution for replacing multiple teeth with a single prosthesis. This versatile dental restoration is designed to address the unique needs of individuals with multiple missing teeth.

Key Features of Partial Dentures:

  • Comprehensive Tooth Replacement: Much like complete dentures, partial dentures provide an effective way to replace a significant number of missing teeth with one prosthesis.
  • Metal Framework: Typically, a partial denture includes a metal framework that extends to the opposite side of the arch, providing stability and support.
  • Metal-Free Options: Under certain circumstances, partial dentures can be crafted without using metal, offering a discreet and aesthetically pleasing alternative.

Integration with Remaining Teeth

Unlike complete dentures, partial dentures work in harmony with the remaining natural teeth. The prosthesis is strategically designed to utilize the remaining teeth as anchor points, enhancing retention and stability.

Implant Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures mark a groundbreaking advancement in modern dentistry, offering a revolutionary solution for complete upper and lower dentures. This technique involves strategically placing two to four implants in the remaining bone, creating a secure foundation with small attachments that allow the denture to effortlessly "snap" onto the implants.

Key Advancements:

  • Complete Denture Stability: Implant-supported dentures provide an unparalleled level of stability, addressing common challenges associated with traditional dentures.
  • Strategic Implant Placement: The placement of implants is carefully planned to optimize support and retention, ensuring a comfortable and secure fit.
  • Retentive Mechanism: Small attachments on the implants create a retentive mechanism, allowing the denture to snap securely into place.

Benefits of Implant-Supported Dentures:

  • Enhanced Comfort: Say goodbye to concerns about denture movement. Implant-supported dentures offer a stable and comfortable fit.
  • Improved Chewing Efficiency: Enjoy improved chewing efficiency and confidence in your ability to enjoy a variety of foods.
  • Natural Look and Feel: The result is a natural-looking smile that feels secure and enhances your overall quality of life.
