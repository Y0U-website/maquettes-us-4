Dental Implant Restorations in…

Restorations are shaped and shaded to blend with your smile. Materials often include porcelain or zirconia for strength and lifelike appearance.

  • Implant-supported bridge replaces several teeth using two or more implants for support.
  • Implant-retained overdenture improves stability for a removable denture that snaps onto implants.
  • Full-arch fixed bridge replaces a complete upper or lower arch with a non-removable solution.

Benefits of Dental Implant Restorations

  • Natural chewing function allows you to enjoy a wide range of foods with confidence.
  • Preserves neighboring teeth by avoiding the need to grind down healthy enamel for a traditional bridge.
  • Supports jawbone health by transferring bite forces to the bone, which helps limit bone loss.
  • Stable speech and comfort reduce slipping or clicking often associated with removable dentures.
  • Long-term durability offers high success rates when supported by good oral hygiene and checkups.
  • Predictable esthetics provide a custom shape and shade designed to match your smile.

How Dental Implant Restorations Work

The process begins with a thorough exam, X-rays, and often 3D imaging to plan implant placement. If bone is thin or missing, grafting may be recommended before or during placement.

After the implant is placed, the bone heals around it in a process called osseointegration , which typically takes several months. Healing caps or temporary restorations may be used during this phase.

Once integration is confirmed, the abutment is connected and a final impression or scan captures the precise position. The restoration is then fabricated and placed.

Your Implant Restoration Experience

Expect numbness during procedures and mild soreness afterward, which is usually manageable with over-the-counter pain relief. Most patients return to daily activities within a day or two. A soft diet may be recommended right after abutment placement and restoration delivery. Your dentist will provide home care instructions tailored to your case.

Maintenance for dental implants focuses on keeping the gums and implant surfaces clean. Daily care includes brushing twice a day with a soft brush and low-abrasive toothpaste. Interdental brushes, floss threaders, or water flossers help clean under implant bridges and around abutments. Regular checkups allow professional cleanings and monitoring for signs of inflammation.
