Dr. Jessica Rhodes

Dr. Jessica Rhodes leads Juniper Dentistry with a patient-first approach rooted in gentle care, honest communication, and clinical precision. She is committed to creating a welcoming environment where every patient feels heard, comfortable, and confident in their treatment plan.

With a focus on modern techniques and continuing education, Dr. Rhodes provides comprehensive general, cosmetic, and restorative dentistry for patients of all ages. Her philosophy centers on conservative care — preserving natural tooth structure whenever possible and empowering patients with the knowledge to maintain long-term oral health.

Sydney

Sydney began her dental assisting career in 2001 after completing a CDA program at Illinois Dental Careers. She joined our team in 2022 and has been a valued part of our clinical staff ever since. Sydney enjoys being part of a close-knit team and appreciates the supportive and welcoming environment of the office.

As a dental assistant, Sydney plays a vital role in supporting both patients and the clinical team. She is always open to lending a hand wherever needed, helping ensure that each day runs smoothly and efficiently. Her positive attitude and team spirit help create a comfortable and caring atmosphere for everyone who walks through our doors.

Outside of the office, Sydney enjoys spending her free time gaming, crafting and relaxing with her cats. These hobbies give her a fun and creative outlet while allowing her to recharge and enjoy the things she loves.

Chiane

Chiane graduated from Aurora University with a Bachelors of Art degree in psychology. She joined our team in June 2024, and enjoys being part of a practice that is in the heart of a friendly, close-knit community that truly makes you feel at home and where patients are warm and welcoming.

As a front desk team member, Chiane sets the tone for a positive patient experience by providing a warm welcoming, keeping schedules organized and ensuring clear communication. She supports both our patients and our clinical team to help Juniper Dentistry run smoothly and efficiently.

In her free time, she enjoys building LEGO sets as a creative and relaxing outlet. She is also a big fan of puzzles — especially Sudoku! She even uses an app to time herself just to see how fast she can finish them. Chiane also has an interest in crime documentaries, finding the investigation process fascinating and thought-provoking.
