Meet Your Dentist in Chicago, IL

Jessica Rhodes, DMD

Dr. Rhodes completed her bachelor&#x27;s degree in biology from Augustana University before going onto receive her doctorate in dental medicine from Southern Illinois University. She is a proud member of the Academy of General Dentistry, the American Dental Association, the Illinois Dental Society and the Chicago Dental Society.

Juniper Dentistry opened in October 2020 and has quickly grown into a vibrant, trusted practice serving a fantastic community. Thanks to word of mouth and a strong reputation for compassionate care, our patient family continues to grow. Dr. Rhodes is always excited to welcome new patients and help them feel right at home while achieving their healthiest, most confident smiles.

Dr. Rhodes became a dentist to help patients enhance their smiles, boost their confidence and improve their overall quality of life. Her approach at Juniper Dentistry reflects the calming and reassuring qualities of the juniper plant, creating a soothing environment for all patients. She has a special passion for caring for those who experience anxiety about dental visits — taking time to walk them through their treatment options, explain what to expect and find ways to help them feel as comfortable as possible throughout their care.
