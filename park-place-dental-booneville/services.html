Questions we hear most often Questions we hear most often

  • How often should I visit the dentist? Most patients should visit every six months for cleanings and exams. However, some patients may need more frequent visits depending on their oral health. Regular checkups help prevent problems and catch issues early.
  • What if I have not been to the dentist in years? You are not alone, and there is no judgment here. Our team focuses on helping you move forward with a clear, comfortable plan. We will assess your current needs and guide you step by step.
  • Do you accept dental insurance? Yes, we accept most major insurance plans and will help you understand your benefits. Our team works with you to maximize coverage and make the process as simple as possible.
  • Do you offer payment plans? We offer flexible financing through CareCredit, which allows you to break treatment costs into manageable monthly payments. This is especially helpful for larger procedures like implants or cosmetic work.
  • Are dental procedures painful? Modern dentistry is more comfortable than ever. We use gentle techniques and prioritize your comfort throughout your visit. Many patients are surprised at how easy and stress-free their experience is.
  • What are my options for replacing missing teeth? We offer several solutions, including dental implants, bridges, and dentures. Implants are often the most long-term solution, providing a natural look and feel while restoring function.
