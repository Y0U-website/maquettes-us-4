Implants vs Bridges vs Partial…

  • Tooth-supported bridge A traditional bridge uses crowns on teeth beside the gap to support the replacement tooth. Preparing those supporting teeth is part of the decision. Other bridge designs exist, so ask which design is being proposed.
  • Removable partial denture A partial denture carries replacement teeth in a removable appliance. Its design depends on the remaining teeth and support available. Your dentist will explain how to insert, remove and care for it.

Ask about ongoing care before choosing

A bridge still needs cleaning around the supporting teeth and under the replacement. Removable appliances need their own handling and cleaning routine. Ask the team to demonstrate the routine for the design being discussed; a name on an estimate does not tell you how it will fit into everyday life.

Also ask what follow-up visits are expected and whom to contact if the replacement feels different later. Put those answers beside the initial estimate so the conversation includes ongoing care.
