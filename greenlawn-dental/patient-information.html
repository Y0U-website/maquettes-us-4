Is there anything I need to do regarding insurance before coming in for my appointment?

When you call to schedule your appointment, please have your insurance information available so that we can verify your insurance coverage prior to your appointment. For your appointment, please bring your driver’s license and insurance card.

Don’t have insurance?

Not a problem. We offer In House Insurance (IHI)!

  • Adult Prophylaxis IHI for those with healthy gums $279/year Routine examinations and dental x-rays
  • 2 professional dental cleanings/year
  • 20 % off of all non-cosmetic treatment
  • Periodontal Maintenance IHI for those with a history of periodontal disease (gum disease) $389/year Routine examinations and dental x-rays
  • 3 periodontal maintenance dental cleanings/year
  • 20 % off all non-cosmetic treatment

What if I can’t afford to pay for all of my treatment at once?

We offer alternative options to finance your dental care. A third party company known as Care Credit offers flexible financing to help ensure you get the care that you need. Find out more information below.
