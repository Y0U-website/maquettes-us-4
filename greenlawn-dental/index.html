The practice

Dr. Heiman prides himself on patient-centered care. He strives to connect with his patients and deliver a personal approach to your North Austin dental needs. The goal at Greenlawn Dental is to mitigate any dental anxiety and be as transparent as possible with all of your visits so that you feel comfortable at every appointment!

Welcome to Greenlawn Dental, where our top priority is to inspire our patients to achieve optimal oral health. We accomplish this through personalized care, cutting-edge technology, and transparent communication. At our North Austin dental office, we also believe that a healthy smile goes beyond aesthetics — it has the power to transform lives.

Our commitment to your well-being extends beyond the dental chair. We aim to make a positive difference in your everyday life. We strive to ensure that you leave our practice with more than just a healthy smile.

What sets Greenlawn Dental apart is our dedication to personalized care. We take the time to understand your unique needs, concerns, and goals, tailoring our treatments to provide the best possible outcome for you.

Communication is at the heart of our practice, because we believe in transparency and involving our patients in their treatment plans. Our team takes the time to explain procedures, discuss options, and address any questions or concerns you may have. We want you to feel empowered and informed throughout your dental journey with us.

When you choose Greenlawn Dental, you’re choosing more than just a dental practice — you’re choosing a North Austin dental partner in your oral health. We invite you to experience the difference that personalized care, advanced technology, and transparent communication can make.

We take a patient-centered approach to your treatment. By understanding your health care needs and desires, we will customize your treatment to address your oral health as well as any other concerns.
