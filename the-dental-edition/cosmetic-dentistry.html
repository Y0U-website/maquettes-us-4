What is Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the aesthetic appearance of your smile through treatments that address color, shape, size, alignment, and overall harmony. These procedures range from professional whitening that brightens stained teeth to porcelain veneers that completely transform your smile’s appearance. Unlike restorative treatments that primarily address function and health, cosmetic procedures emphasize the artistic aspects of dentistry—creating beautiful smiles that complement your facial features and reflect your personality.
