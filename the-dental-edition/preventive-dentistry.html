What Is Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry encompasses all proactive measures designed to maintain optimal oral health and prevent dental problems before they develop. This includes regular professional cleanings, comprehensive examinations, diagnostic X-rays, fluoride treatments, dental sealants, and oral cancer screenings. The philosophy is powerful yet simple: catching problems early—or preventing them entirely—saves time, money, discomfort, and preserves your natural teeth for life. Prevention is always more effective, less invasive, and more affordable than treating advanced disease.

Most patients benefit from cleanings and exams every six months. This schedule allows us to remove accumulated plaque, monitor oral health, and catch problems early. Patients with gum disease history, frequent cavities, diabetes, or other risk factors may benefit from visits every three to four months. We’ll recommend a schedule based on your individual needs.

Children should visit by their first birthday or within six months of their first tooth erupting.
