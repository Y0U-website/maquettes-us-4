The Dental Implant Process

Your treatment is carefully planned and executed to ensure predictable, high-quality outcomes.

1. Consultation and Evaluation

A comprehensive exam, including digital imaging, is performed to assess bone structure and overall oral health.

2. Implant Placement

The implant is placed into the jawbone using precise, minimally invasive techniques to promote optimal healing.

3. Healing and Integration

Over several weeks to months, the implant integrates with the bone, creating a stable base.

4. Final Restoration

A custom-designed restoration is securely attached, completing your smile.

Throughout the process, patient comfort and safety are prioritized, and you will receive detailed guidance at every stage.

FAQs About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, dental implants can last for decades and, in many cases, a lifetime.

Are dental implants painful?

The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia. Most patients report mild discomfort during recovery, which can be managed with standard post-operative care.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The full timeline varies but generally ranges from a few months to allow for proper healing and integration.

How do I care for dental implants?

Daily brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups are essential to maintaining implant health.

Can smokers receive dental implants?

Smoking can impact healing and success rates. A consultation is necessary to evaluate individual risk factors.

What is recovery like after implant placement?

Most patients return to normal activities within a few days. Mild swelling or discomfort is common but temporary.
