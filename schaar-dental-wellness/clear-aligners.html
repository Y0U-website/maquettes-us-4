Are Clear Aligners Right for You?

Clear aligners are best suited for teens and adults with mild to moderate orthodontic issues, such as minor crowding, spacing, or bite irregularities. Dr. Taylor Schaar will evaluate your smile and determine whether clear aligners or another treatment option is the best fit for your goals. For more complex alignment cases, traditional braces may be recommended instead, as outlined in our Invisalign vs. braces comparison . Many patients also pair aligner treatment with teeth whitening for a complete smile refresh once treatment is finished.

The Clear Aligner Treatment Process

  • Consultation and 3D Imaging: We evaluate your smile and goals, then use advanced 3D imaging to create a precise treatment plan.
  • Custom Aligner Design: Your personalized series of aligners is designed to gradually move your teeth into their ideal position.
  • Wearing and Changing Aligners: You will wear each set for 20 to 22 hours a day, switching to a new set every one to two weeks, with regular check-ins to monitor progress.
  • Final Retainers: Once your smile has reached its final position, you will receive a set of retainers to maintain your results.

FAQs About Clear Orthodontic Aligners

How much do clear aligners cost in Victoria, TX?

Cost depends on the complexity of your case and the length of treatment needed. Our clear aligner cost guide breaks down the factors that affect pricing in more detail.

How long does clear aligner treatment take?

Most patients complete treatment in 6 to 18 months, though timelines vary depending on the extent of correction needed.

Are clear aligners as effective as traditional braces?

Clear aligners are highly effective for mild to moderate alignment issues. For more complex cases, traditional braces may achieve better results. A consultation can help determine the right option for your smile.

Can I eat and drink with clear aligners in?

Aligners should be removed before eating or drinking anything other than water to avoid damage or staining, then brushed and reinserted afterward.

Will clear aligners work for teens?

Yes. Clear aligners are a popular option for teens with mild to moderate alignment issues who want a more discreet treatment than traditional braces.
