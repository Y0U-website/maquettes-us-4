Types of Dentures

Every case is different — we’ll recommend the right category during your consultation, then fabricate it on site.

  • Conventional Partial Dentures A removable appliance that restores form and function by replacing one or several missing teeth.
  • Conventional Complete Dentures A removable appliance that restores form and function by replacing one or several missing teeth.
  • Immediate Dentures Fabricated prior to final tooth removal and delivered the same day teeth come out — you’re never without teeth.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures Two or more titanium screws anchor the denture in the jaw — the most stable, natural-looking, and highest-functioning option available.
