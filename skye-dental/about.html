Michelle

Director of Patient Care

Michelle is also a Bay Area native who graduated from Notre Dame High School in San Jose. She continued her education at Santa Clara University, earning a Bachelor of Science degree in Combined Sciences.

What makes Michelle most famous is her Olympian status! She was the youngest woman in history to represent the United States Women’s Table Tennis National Team in the 2000 Sydney Olympics. She continues to coach table tennis in her spare time.

But, these days, most of her time is taken by her kids, Jordan, Evie, & Asher. She and her husband, Barney, love taking their kids and their dog, Odie, on various mini trips down to San Diego and LA. They visit family in San Diego, and Michelle’s second home at Hogwarts in Universal Studios, Hollywood. She’s a huge Harry Potter fan.

In her free time, she enjoys walking her dog, reading, crafting, and singing with multiple church choirs, including the Faith Choir with Dr Do!

Nikki, RDH

Before moving to San Jose in 2007, Nikki grew up in Peterborough, New Hampshire. Following high school, she earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Health Promotion and Fitness, and went on to assist many people in reaching their fitness and health goals. Upon replanting in the Bay Area, Nikki continued her studies and became a Registered Dental Hygienist, a profession she always wanted to pursue.

In 2011, Nikki married a fellow Granite Stater and have their hands full with their daughter, Evyn. Nikki has also convinced her parents to move to the Sunshine State, so she feels so blessed to have their help while she’s at work.

Nikki pursued education in myofunctional therapy to deepen her understanding of the function of the muscles of the face and mouth. She works with patients to improve their oral health and overall wellness.

Coincidentally, Peterborough is the same tiny little town that Dr. Do lived in when her family first immigrated here from Vietnam. So, when Dr. Do met Nikki at her previous office they formed an instant bond. They have been working together since 2007 and share the same treatment philosophy.
