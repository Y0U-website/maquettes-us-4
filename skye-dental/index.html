The practice

Our Goal Our goal is to inform, educate, and have an open discussion to help you make informed decisions regarding your care. Welcome to Skye! Your smile makes us smile!

Creating Smiles We create beautiful and healthy smiles by providing care that ranges from the basic dental cleanings and exams, to teeth whitening, clear aligner therapy, and veneers.

Your Skye dentists understand that dental health is more than just functional teeth. Acknowledging the oral-systemic (mouth-body) connection can help connect the dots in understanding your overall health. Inflammation in your mouth can result in inflammation in your body as bacteria enters your blood stream through ulcerations in your mouth. Gum disease has been linked to heart disease…

Cleanings and Exams Ultrasonic scalers, combined with traditional hand scaling, makes cleanings more efficient and more comfortable for you. Combined with intra-oral cameras, you will be amazed at your before and after photos!

Porcelain Veneers For patients with stained, chipped, cracked, injured, or misaligned teeth, our office offers the option of natural-looking porcelain veneers.

CLEAR ALIGNER THERAPY Now there is an alternative to traditional metal braces to create your beautiful smile. Clear aligner therapy is a series of clear, removable, plastic aligners that are custom-made for your teeth.

Bonding Bonding is a form of smile sculpture accomplished with a composite resin which enables patients with imperfections in their teeth to have more attractive smiles.

Dental Implants Dental implants have revolutionized how we replace missing teeth. In the past, we replaced teeth with bridges or dentures.
