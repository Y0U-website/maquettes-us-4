About Our Weekend Dental Team

The dental practice that opens when you actually need it.

We’re a small, locally owned office in Riverton, Utah — focused on giving people in the south Salt Lake valley a better way to take care of their teeth.

Most people don’t have a free Tuesday.

Open Now Dental started with a simple observation: the people who need dentists most — working parents, shift workers, students, families — almost never have weekday afternoons free. So they put it off. And small problems become big ones.

We flipped the schedule. Instead of asking patients to fit us in, we built our hours around the times most people are actually free: Friday afternoons, Saturday and Sunday. The result is a calmer practice, shorter waits, and a lot fewer patients walking in already in pain.

If you’ve been putting off the dentist because the timing never works — that’s exactly the problem we’re here to solve.

Three things we won’t compromise on

Genuine Kindness No lectures. No shame. We treat anxious patients and long-overdue ones with the same warmth.

Time That Respects Yours We start on time, communicate clearly, and don’t pad appointments. Your weekend is yours.

Honest, Conservative Care We recommend what you actually need — not what generates the biggest invoice. Period.
