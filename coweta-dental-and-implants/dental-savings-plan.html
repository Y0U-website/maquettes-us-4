Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment are accepted? For your convenience, we accept cash, checks, major credit cards, ApplePay, Proceed Finance, CareCredit ® and Sunbit.
  • When can I start using my membership? Your membership becomes active immediately after signing up, so you can start saving right away.
  • How do I schedule an appointment for preventative care? Scheduling is simple—use our online scheduling tool or call our office, and we’ll reserve a time that works for you. Schedule Online
  • How can I use my member-exclusive discount for treatment? Just let us know you’re a member when you schedule your appointment, and we’ll apply your discount during your visit.
  • Can I add members to my account? Yes, your entire family can join the plan and enjoy the same benefits.
  • Can I transfer my membership to another dentist? Our Dental Savings Plan is exclusive to our practice and cannot be transferred to another dentist.
