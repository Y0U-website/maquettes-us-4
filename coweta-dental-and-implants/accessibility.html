Online Accessibility Statement

These tips were provided by the US Social Security Administration .

If you are looking for mouse and keyboard alternatives:

Speech recognition software such as Dragon allows users to navigate around a website through voice commands.

Please contact us for assistance if these recommendations do not meet your needs.

  • Skip to Content Accessibility Statement Accessibility Statement Please review the following resources to help make your experience on our website more accessible. If you have trouble seeing web pages: Use your computer to read web pages out loud
  • Use the keyboard to navigate screens
  • Increase text size
  • Magnify your screen
  • Change background and text colors
  • Make your mouse pointer more visible
  • Closed Captioning: Closed captioning on videos allows all users to experience the presentation by overlaying real-time visual transcripts of the audio track over the video. Learn how to turn captioning on and off on YouTube .
  • Volume Controls: Every device — such as a computer, tablet or mobile device — has the ability to adjust the volume. Videos and audio presentations have volume controls within their media players, and adjusting both your device’s and your media player’s volume can make the listening experience more enjoyable.
