The practice

I took an oath to take care of my patients and strive to do that every single day, with every single person I have the opportunity to care for. By really listening to my patients, and putting their well-being at the forefront. I help people achieve their best smiles and am at their side as they enjoy optimal oral health, for life!

A person’s smile is the first thing anyone notices; it breaks my heart to see someone cover their smile when they laugh. I want people to walk into a room and be able to smile from ear to ear and feel confident.

Being able to give someone that confidence, to get someone out of pain, to educate and motivate people to care for their oral health — these opportunities are the best!

Dr. Austin Dodge is a specialist Periodontist from Marquette University. A “green and gold blooded” Wisconsinite, he was born in the Fox Valley, and grew up in Stevens Point, Wisconsin. He earned his Bachelor’s of Science in Biomedical Sciences, Doctorate of Dental Surgery, and Master’s of Science in Periodontology at Marquette University in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

Dr. Dodge was trained on the motto of Cura Personalis (care for the whole person) and always strives to “do what is right” for his patients. His top priority in patient care is to establish a partnership with his patients in order to work together to achieve their goals and establish exceptional oral health.
