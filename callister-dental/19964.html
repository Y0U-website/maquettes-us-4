The Benefits of Dental Implants

Aside from a beautiful new smile, dental implants promote better oral and systemic health in ways you may not have considered.

When teeth are missing, your jawbone no longer stimulated by tooth roots begins to shrink away. This can leave you with a sunken or aged appearance in your face. Dental implants stimulate your jawbone to keep it viable and present.

Additionally, when teeth are missing, your remaining teeth tend to shift toward these gaps. The combination of jawbone instability and shifting teeth may lead to erosion, further bite complications, and future tooth loss.

Dental implants also restore your dietary choices you’re able to return to your favorite foods and enjoy a more balanced diet.
