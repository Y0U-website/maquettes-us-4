Braden Callister, DMD

Dr. Braden Callister grew up in Merced, CA. He graduated with a Bachelor’s Degree in Exercise Science from Brigham Young University and received his Doctor of Medical Dentistry (DMD) from A.T. Still University Missouri School of Dentistry and Oral Health. Dr. Callister spent two years working as an associate dentist in Tulsa, Oklahoma before moving back to the Central Valley to be closer to family and the warm California weather.

Dr. Callister has received awards and recognition from the American Academy of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS) and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) for excellence in both implants and oral surgery. He is a member of the American Dental Association and Fresno Madera Dental Society. He is Invisalign certified and has also received additional training in implants.

Dr. Callister lived in South Africa for two years while serving a church service mission. He and his wife, Alyssa, have four children. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his family, visiting the beach, gold panning, and camping. He LOVES ice cream and always has a stock full in the freezer! As founder and CEO of Callister Dental, Dr. Callister offers prestigious care with the latest technology in dentistry and is dedicated to the care and wellbeing of the patients he serves.

Dr. Zachary Jaeckel, DDS

Dr. Zachary Jaeckel grew up right here in Clovis, CA. He attended Clovis Community College and graduated with a degree in Biology from Fresno State University. He received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Southern California and is excited to be able to utilize his education to help patients here in his hometown. While attending USC, Dr. Jaeckel had the opportunity to provide dentistry to underserved communities during his rotations in elementary schools and with the homeless populations in Los Angeles.

When Dr. Jaeckel isn’t doing dentistry, he enjoys playing basketball, watching football, working out, playing video games, and going to concerts. He is the oldest of six children, including five adopted younger siblings. Dr. Jaeckel is a Cowboys fan, so be sure to give him a hard time if you are a fellow 49er’s fan!
