The practice

Our doctors provide the highest level of skilled patient care within a caring and family friendly atmosphere. Using the latest in technology we ensure you receive the quality care you expect and the gentle treatment you deserve from a professional dentist office.

We offer comprehensive general dentistry services that include regular checkups and routine and advanced procedures to maintain your overall oral health. Our dentists provide individualized patient care to meet each person’s specific dental needs. We also offer the latest, proven quality advice on the most effective healthy home dental practices.

Our restorative dentistry services can make the whole complexion of your teeth and mouth look brand new. We perform several types of restorative procedures including crowns, bridges, dentures, partial dentures and implants.

I am writing to share that, after almost 45 years in dentistry, I will be retiring at the end of September.

It has been one of the great privileges of my life to care for generations of patients and families at our practice. My goal has always been to see that my patients were well cared for and respected. I hope that I was able to provide you with the best treatment possible and to make you feel relaxed and have a good laugh.

I am doing my best to complete outstanding treatment in the few remaining weeks, but availability is becoming very limited. Unfortunately, it is not possible for me to begin any new work that I will not be able to complete.

As I look ahead to retirement, I do so with tremendous gratitude and complete confidence in the future of the practice. Drs. Rosen, Cannon, Trivedi and Sandau, as well as the rest of our team, are fully committed to providing the same thoughtful, compassionate care that you have come to expect. Many of you know them already, and I am confident that you will continue to be in excellent hands.

I want to thank everyone who has already shared kind words, memories, and support. I have been deeply touched by the response. Thank you for your trust, your loyalty, and the many years of shared smiles. It has been an honor to serve you.
