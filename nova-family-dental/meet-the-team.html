The NOVA Family

Compassionate. Exceptional. Dedicated.

New name, same family. Dr. Kris and her family-orientated team have been providing our community with exceptional care and services for over 20 years. At NOVA Family Dental, we are dedicated to continuing to provide compassionate, exceptional dental care for you and your family.

Dr. Kris

Dr. Kris started out her career as a dental hygienist. She received her B.S. from Oregon Health Science University School of Dentistry and her Doctor of Dental Medicine from Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine. Dr. Kris Pham speaks fluent English and Vietnamese. When she is not busy being a dentist, she is busy being a mom to a teenage daughter, spending time with family, and exploring the outdoors.

Lisa - Financial Coordinator

Lisa has over 16 years of experience working in the dental field. She enjoys helping our patients navigate through their treatment, offering kindness, and compassion. She and her husband are longtime Vancouver residents. They enjoy spending time with their three grown children and their families. Their greatest joy is spending time with their four grandsons.

Jen - Hygienist

Jennifer graduated from Clark College in 1997 and has been serving Clark County for 25 years. She prides herself on providing thorough, gentle care for her patients. She and her husband of 30 years have two adult children and two grandchildren whom they love to spoil as much as possible. Outside of the office, they enjoy camping, fishing, and hiking in the Pacific Northwest. They enjoy traveling, dining locally, and the Seahawks!
