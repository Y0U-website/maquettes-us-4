1. Comprehensive Consultation & Treatment Planning

The process begins with an in-depth consultation with Dr. M. Ihsan Kayali, an experienced periodontist specializing in dental implants. During this visit, X-rays and digital scans are used to assess your oral health and determine if you are a good candidate for dental implants.

Under local anesthesia or IV sedation for enhanced comfort, the titanium implant posts are surgically inserted into the jawbone. This precise placement is crucial for long-term success and stability.

3. Osseointegration Healing & Bone Integration

Over the next few months, the implants undergo a process called osseointegration, where the surrounding bone fuses with the titanium. This ensures a strong foundation for the new tooth or teeth.

Once the implants have fully integrated with the jawbone, small connectors known as abutments are placed. These serve as the link between the implant and the final restoration.

A dental crown, bridge, or denture is custom-fabricated to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size. This final step completes your transformation, restoring function and aesthetics.
