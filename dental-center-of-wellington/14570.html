Why would my dentist say I need a sinus lift?

If you’ve been told you need a sinus lift before getting dental implants, there’s no need for concern. A sinus lift is a common preparatory procedure that helps create a strong, healthy foundation for implants. Because the upper back jaw naturally has less bone and is located near the sinus cavity, many patients in Royal Palm Beach need a little additional support before moving forward with implant placement.

Why a Sinus Lift Helps Dental Implants Succeed

A sinus lift increases bone height in the upper jaw by gently elevating the sinus membrane and placing bone grafting material beneath it. Over time, this graft integrates with your natural bone, creating a stable structure capable of supporting long-lasting dental implants. While the term may sound intimidating, sinus lift procedures are predictable, safe, and designed to improve the success and comfort of implant placement.

Your dentist may recommend a sinus lift if:

  • You’ve had tooth loss for several years, leading to bone shrinkage.
  • Your sinus cavity is positioned low, limiting available bone.
