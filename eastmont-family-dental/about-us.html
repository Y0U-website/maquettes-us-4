About Our Doctors

Brian Anantatmula, DDS

Dr. Anantatmula grew up in the Tri-Cities area. After high school, he served for two years at a church mission in India. He enjoys volunteering his time in community church services. He and his wife, Melinda, have four children. He is excited about being part of the Wenatchee Valley — his wife grew up here and just happens to be the daughter of Dr. Shurtz.

Dr. Anantatmula values long-term personal relationships with his patients while guiding them in their oral health decisions to ensure the best quality care possible.

Kevin Clive, DDS

Dr. Clive grew up locally in Wenatchee and enjoyed participating in basketball, football, and piano. After graduating from WHS, he served as a missionary for his church for one year in Salt Lake City and one year in Sao Paulo, Brazil. As a result, he is fluent in Portuguese, with a few Spanish phrases here and there. Following his mission, he graduated from Brigham Young University.

At BYU, Dr. Clive met his wife, Jen and they got married after his first year of dental school at the University of Maryland. They now have a little boy!

During Dr. Clive’s time at Dental School, he was awarded recognition after completing the President’s Student Leadership Institute with a concentration in Effective Leadership. He was also one of few students in his class selected to be an Oral Surgery Clerk.

Dr. Clive knew that he wanted to go into dentistry ever since he completed the dentistry merit badge in boy scouts as an 11-year-old in this very office! He is now excited to come back and be a part of the fun and caring atmosphere created by Dr. Anantatmula and his colleagues. He loves getting to know his patients and looks forward to taking care of the dental needs of anyone that walks into the office.

Our Office

We have been in our Eastmont Avenue location for 30 years. It’s convenient and easy-to-find. We share a building with Merrill Orthodontics, so our clients can receive many dental services in one location.

Our staff is made up of the best people in the business. Their longevity with us reflects our commitment to providing the best for our patients.

Rest assured that our office uses a modern sterilization system that surpasses OSHA requirements. We use bottled, distilled water for our dental units to prevent contamination that can occur when using tap water. When you’re at Eastmont Family Dental, you’re receiving the best possible care.
