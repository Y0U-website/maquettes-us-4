What Happens in a Dental Cleaning?

Dental cleaning is ideal for adults but also children, even if they don’t yet have all their permanent teeth yet. Some people are nervous when they go to the dentist, and so we want to help you better prepare yourself for what to exact at your dental cleaning.

During a dental cleaning, you will sit in a chair and lie back so the dentist or dental hygienist can better access your teeth. There are several techniques and tools that a hygienist or dentist might use during a dental cleaning.

The dental tools your hygienist might use during a dental cleaning include:

  • A dental mirror to see
  • Scaler
  • Perioprobes
  • Curettes
  • Polishing devices
  • Saliva Ejectors
  • Ultrasonic scalers

Dental Exam for Early Dental Diagnosis and Prevention

Combined with dental cleaning procedures, dental exams are one of the best types of oral prevention you can do. At regular intervals, it’s important to inspect your teeth for signs of cavities, plaque build-up, gum recession, and more.

Dental examinations are often combined with your dental cleaning, but at times can be scheduled seperately. A typical dental exam in our Kansas City office will include a cleaning by a dental hygienist, x-rays on certain visits, and a checkup of your mouth by the dentist.

What’s Included in a Full Dental Exam?

Every full dental exam may differ depending on the patient’s situation. Most full dental examination services include:

  • Dental X-Ray
  • Checking for gum recession
  • Bitewing X-Rays
  • Some cleaning and scraping
  • Checking for cavities, gum disease, loose fittings, and oral cancer
  • Questionnaire of the patient’s oral health and history
  • Teeth photographs

Schedule Your Dental Cleaning & Dental Exam in Kansas City

Many people wonder what the difference is between a dental cleaning and a dental exam. The simple answer is that dental examinations don’t always treat the teeth, or if they do, it is a minor treatment, and primarily exists to assess the state of mouth and teeth health.

Dental examinations are recommended whenever you go to a new dentist or at least once per year. This gives your dental clinic accurate information about your teeth, including the overall health of the teeth, changes in your dental health, and the status of any fillings.

A dental exam is vital for healthy teeth and gums and shouldn’t be avoided for too long. Your Kansas City dentist will use the information found in a dental exam during a dental cleaning and as part of your overall treatment plan.

Dental cleanings involve the actual cleaning and removal of plaque and tartar from the teeth.

Dental cleaning is an important preventative dental treatment that helps reduce the chance of oral decay and oral disease.
