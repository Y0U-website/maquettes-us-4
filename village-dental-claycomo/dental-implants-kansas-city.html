What are Dental Implants? Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing lost teeth and restoring our smile and mouth functionality. Dental implants involve a metal titanium post carefully inserted into the jawbone. Don’t worry, this doesn’t hurt as you are numbed for this process.

After a few months, the post fuses with the bone and begins to function as an artificial tooth root, to which the dentist can attach a replacement tooth.

There is more than one type of dental implant. You can replace a single tooth with an implant-supported dental crown or place an implant-supported bridge to replace multiple missing teeth next to each other.

A full implant-supported denture can replace teeth all along the upper and lower dental arch, which requires numerous implants and attachment pieces called abutments and other restorations.

What are the Benefits of Dental Implants?

Dental implants allow people with missing permanent teeth to go back to living their life with all of their teeth again. With super sturdy and reliable materials, you most likely won’t need another dental implant again!

Studies show that with the proper upkeep and oral hygiene, dental implants have a high success rate and can last a lifetime.

Once the dental implant has properly healed, you’ll have a beautiful smile again. Your replacement teeth or restorations will look and behave like your natural ones.

Dental implants are relatively permanent, so one of their biggest benefits, other than looking entirely natural, is that you will never have to worry about them or wear a removable denture again.

Dental implant restorations replace a missing tooth or section of teeth.

What to Expect With Dental Implants?

If you’re considering getting a dental implant with Village Dental in Kansas City, then you’ve made the right choice. Dental implants are the preferred treatment for tooth replacement.

Unlike other options, like bridges or dentures, you don’t need to shave down adjacent teeth.

Dental implants require a procedure to attach the post to your jawbone. A few types of procedures can be used, so you’ll have to ask your dentist or surgeon which one they will perform to understand how dental implants will be applied.

However, you can expect a quick outpatient surgery. This is done in a few stages, with healing time between each stage. The stages of adding a dental implant include:

  • Removing the damaged tooth
  • Jawbone preparation and grafting if your jawbone is not thick enough
  • Dental implant placement
  • Bone growth and healing
  • Abutment placement
  • Artificial tooth placement
