What are Dental Implants?

Dental implants themselves are small titanium posts that are surgically placed below the gumline and into the jawbone to provide a strong and secure foundation for dental restorations to be attached to. Because titanium is a biocompatible material, they’re able to chemically fuse with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, allowing them to function just like your natural tooth roots do.

The 4-Step Dental Implant Process

  • The Initial Consultation. During the initial consultation, Dr. Geller will capture X-rays and a cone beam CT scan of your mouth and facial structure to determine whether you’re a good candidate and create a treatment plan. After presenting you with a plan customized to address your unique needs, our team will schedule your surgery.
  • Dental Implant Surgery. In most cases, Dr. Geller can perform the dental implant surgery himself, meaning you won’t have to worry about traveling to another provider in the middle of the process.
  • Osseointegration/Abutment Placement. Once the implants are placed, they’ll undergo a process called osseointegration, where they fuse with the jawbone. After this is complete, we’ll attach small metal connector pieces to the top of the implants, called abutments.
  • Attachment of Restorations. After your restoration has been crafted, we’ll secure them to the abutments that are connected to the dental implants, completing the process.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Whether you’re missing all of your teeth or you are only missing one or two, it’s likely that you’ll have more than one option to choose from when it comes to rebuilding your smile. So, why choose dental implants? In short, they offer a variety of benefits that set them apart from traditional tooth replacement procedures , including:

Day-to-Day Benefits

Below, you’ll find a few of the many day-to-day benefits you’ll enjoy with dental implants:

  • Easier Maintenance: Unlike some of the other tooth-replacement solutions out there, dental implants don’t require special oral hygiene products. In fact, you can use all of the same best practices you use for your natural teeth, like brushing with fluoridated toothpaste and a soft-bristled toothbrush consistently.
  • Increased Confidence: One of the biggest perks of dental implants is that they look and feel identical to natural teeth, which can boost your confidence significantly. They also function identically to natural teeth since they get their support from your jawbone, not your gums.
  • Ability to Eat Most Foods : Since dental implants are inserted directly into your jawbone and crafted from durable materials, there are little-to-no limitations on what you can eat with your new replacement teeth.

Health Benefits

In addition to benefiting your self-esteem and diet, dental implants provide the following health benefits:

  • Jawbone Preservation: We mentioned a few times above that dental implants are unique because they are inserted directly into your jawbone. As a result, it’s stimulated every time you chew, preventing bone loss in the process.
  • Protection for Natural Teeth: One downside of dental bridges is that they require the teeth surrounding the gap to be altered. That’s not the case for dental implants. They are self-supporting, so none of your healthy teeth need to be treated in the process of rebuilding your smile.
  • Better Overall Health: Tooth loss has been linked to malnutrition, indigestion, and several other health issues. The good news is that dental implants can restore the strength of your bite, helping prevent these problems from surfacing.

Long-Term Benefits

Lastly, dental implants offer patients several long-term benefits, including:

  • High Success Rate: Simply put, dental implants have an extremely high success rate (around 95 %). It’s actually even higher among non-smoking patients!
  • Youthful Appearance: In addition to helping prevent facial structure change over time, dental implants fill the gaps in your smile seamlessly so you look more youthful.
  • Lifespan of 30+ Years : With proper care, dental implants can last for several decades. In fact, some patients enjoy theirs for a lifetime!

Who Can Dental Implants Help?

While dental implants aren’t the ideal solution for every patient dealing with tooth loss, this versatile treatment may be a great option for you. The best way to find out is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Geller and discuss all of your options. We may recommend getting dental implants in Novi if you’re in any of the following situations and have adequate jawbone support, good oral hygiene, and no untreated autoimmune conditions that could impact your body’s healing ability after surgery.

Missing Single Tooth

To replace a single tooth, we’ll secure one dental implant below the gumline and into the jawbone and attach a dental crown to it to fill in the gap between teeth.

Missing Multiple Teeth

To replace one or more consecutive teeth, instead of needing to alter your natural teeth to secure a bridge, we can place two dental implants on either side of the gap in your smile. These will act as the foundation of your restoration.

Missing All of Your Teeth

To replace an entire arch of your teeth, we can secure anywhere from four to eight dental implants along your gumline. These will hold a full denture in place, eliminating the need for denture adhesive and any chance of slipping or shifting restorations.

Different Factors That Impact the Cost of Dental Implants

There are several additional factors that can impact the cost of dental implants in Novi, including the following:

  • The number of dental implants you need will play a large role in your overall treatment price.
  • What type of restoration you require will also determine cost, as smaller ones, like dental crowns, tend to be less costly than dentures and bridges.

Maintaining & Caring for Your Dental Implants

Like many other patients, you may have heard that dental implants have the potential to last a lifetime and were sold. That’s certainly true; this state-of-the-art tooth-replacement solution can last for 30 years or more with the right best practices in place. More specifically, we recommend adopting the following habits into your routine to maintain both your smile and your restorations.

Make Oral Hygiene a Priority

Remember, just because your dental implants are decay-resistant doesn’t mean you no longer need to commit to good oral hygiene habits. Quite the opposite considering peri-implantitis (a form of gum disease) is one of the leading causes of dental implant failure. In short, if you want your dental implants to last, you need to take good care of your smile by brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and rinsing with mouthwash consistently.

Eat a Healthy Diet

Eating healthy, well-balanced meals won’t just benefit your overall health; it will benefit your oral health. So, do your best to fill your plate with nutrient-dense foods, like cooked carrots, plain yogurt, and scrambled eggs. Simultaneously, avoid anything that has the potential to cause your restoration to break, like chewing on hard pretzels or ice cubes.

Break Bad Habits

Speaking of chewing on ice cubes It’s important that you break bad habits that dramatically increase your risk of dental damage. A few other examples include biting your nails, smoking, and using your teeth as tools to open things. Although some of these may seem harmless, they can have a serious impact on the look, health, and function of your smile. So, make quitting a priority.

Protect Your Dental Implants

It goes without saying that you need to wear a mouthguard if you play football, you wrestle, or you participate in any kind of contact sport. We also recommend wearing one if you run, hike, ride your bike, or even lift weights. That way, your smile is protected from unforeseen impact whether it’s a bad fall or consistent grinding.

Schedule Regular Dental Checkups

Lastly, make your biannual visits with our dentist in Novi a priority. This is crucial for several reasons, including that Dr. Geller uses these appointments to examine the condition of your dental implant, assess the health of your gums, and screen for oral cancer. Plus, your dental hygienist will remove built-up plaque and tartar, helping to keep your smile healthy between visits as well.

Dental Implant Post-Op Instructions

Now that you’ve had your dental implants surgically placed, it’s time for the recovery process. Thankfully, this stage is nothing to worry about! Below, we’ll share what you can expect, which symptoms are normal, and how you can help your smile heal. Should you experience intense, abnormal pain or other strange side-effects that aren’t mentioned here, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us for guidance.

What to Do Directly After Dental Implant Surgery

Immediately after your dental implant surgery, your biggest priority should be to leave the forming blood clot untouched so that your healing happens smoothly. To do this, please be sure to follow these rules:

  • Do not spit. Use tissues or swallow your saliva.
  • Don’t use drinking straws.
  • Do not smoke, vape, or use tobacco products for at least the first day after surgery.
  • Do your best to keep your fingers and tongue away from the surgical site or sites.

Common Side-Effects When Recovering from Dental Implant Placement

Following dental implant surgery, some mild side-effects are completely normal, especially in the first few days after placement. These may include:

The good news is that each of these effects should be temporary and will gradually subside as time goes by. However, if this is not the case, please let us know as soon as possible.

  • Intermittent bleeding. To lessen this, you can gently bite or press down on gauze with light pressure. This may continue for several days.
  • Swelling may occur over the first 72 hours and could last for over a week. You can apply cold or warm compresses to help.
  • General discomfort is common but can be treated with prescribed or OTC pain medication.

Your Diet After Dental Implant Surgery

In the first few days following your dental implant surgery, we recommend sticking to a softer diet with non-abrasive foods such as:

You can return to a normal diet as soon as you feel up to it; however, we recommend limiting your consumption of any overly crunchy or tough foods and avoiding any direct chewing on your dental implant sites.

  • Mashed potatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Ice cream
  • Oatmeal
  • Pudding
  • Soup (not extremely hot)
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Pasta

Post-Op Health & Oral Hygiene

On the day after your dental implant surgery, it is okay to brush your teeth as usual, though be sure to be very careful near your surgical sites.

Rinse your mouth two or three times a day (ideally after each meal) with salt water and use prescription mouthwash if you have it.

Avoid using any mouthwash that contains high levels of alcohol, such as Listerine or Scope.

What to Do After Your New Teeth Are Attached

The hardest part of your dental implant treatment is behind you. When your new crown, bridge, or denture is attached to your dental implant or implants, some minor, temporary sensitivity in your gum tissue may occur. Thankfully, this is easy to control with pain medication. There should be no swelling, bleeding, or extensive recovery at this point. In fact, you should be ready to show off your new smile that very same day!

Dental Implant Technology

One of the biggest factors in the success of your dental implants is the technology used to place them. Not only do better tools make the experience more comfortable for you, but more advanced technology also ensures that implant surgery is more precise. This translates to more stable outcomes and longer-lasting results.

3D Cone-Beam Imaging/ 3D CT Scanning

We use the Carestream CBCT Cone Beam Imaging System to carefully plan your implant treatment. Unlike traditional X-rays, 3D cone-beam scans create a detailed, three-dimensional image of your teeth, jawbone, nerves, and sinuses in a single pass. This gives Dr. Geller a complete picture of your oral anatomy before surgery ever begins.

With this information, he can choose the ideal implant size, angle, and position while avoiding sensitive structures. That means more predictable results, a lower risk of complications, and a smoother overall experience. Best of all, the scan itself is quick, comfortable, and uses a focused beam to minimize exposure while maximizing diagnostic detail.

Digital Impression System

Messy impression material is quickly becoming a thing of the past. For implant restorations and other advanced treatments, Dr. Geller uses Trios 3Shape and iTero Element 2 digital impression systems to capture incredibly accurate 3D models of your teeth and gums. Instead of biting into trays filled with cold putty, you simply sit back while a small handheld scanner glides over your teeth.

These digital impressions are more precise and comfortable than their traditional counterparts, which leads to better-fitting implant restorations like crowns and bridges. The scans can be reviewed instantly on-screen, allowing Dr. Geller to fine-tune your treatment plan in real time. Digital files also streamline the process of making restorations, either with our in-house technology or at a trusted external dental lab. This shortens turnaround times and improves the overall quality of your final results.

3D Printer

To bring your implant plan from the digital world into reality, our practice uses a SprintRay 3D printer. Using data from your CBCT scan and digital impressions, we can fabricate super-accurate surgical guides and other custom components right here in our Novi office. These guides help Dr. Geller place each implant at the exact depth and angle planned during your consultation.

Not only does this technology improve surgical precision, but it can also speed up your treatment and reduce the need for multiple visits. It allows us to maintain greater control over the quality of your surgical guides and mock-ups, ensuring that everything is tailored specifically to your smile. The result is a smoother, more efficient implant experience from start to finish.
