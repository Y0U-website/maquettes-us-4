What Types of Dental Implants Are There?

Our dental team helps you choose the right kind of dental implants in Wyoming, OH , based on your situation. A few types of these replacement options include the following.

Single-Tooth Implants

These replace one missing tooth. You can get just one or a few or more, depending on how many teeth you’ve lost.

Implant-Supported Bridges

These are used to hold a bridge in place rather than using dental crowns on the neighboring teeth.

Implant-Supported Dentures

These posts are placed in certain areas to hold dentures in place rather than using removable dentures.

All-on-X Implants

These implants involve placing a post in a specific area on the upper or lower arch to replace a few or more missing teeth in a row.

Can Anyone Get Dental Implants?

Not everyone is a suitable candidate. You’ll need to have adequate jawbone and healthy gums while also meeting other criteria, such as not being a smoker or having medical conditions that affect healing.

How Long Does the Implant Process Take?

You’re looking at several months to complete this procedure — with a few or more months needed for your jaw to heal between steps.
