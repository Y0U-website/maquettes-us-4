Trust Your Smile to Dr. Pucci

Dr. James E. Pucci is a board-certified periodontist dedicated to delivering advanced, patient-centered periodontal and implant care. Known for his gentle approach and commitment to excellence, he focuses on creating healthy, confident smiles through precision, compassion, and individualized treatment.

Background & Education

Dr. Pucci earned his Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Yale University before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine. He then pursued advanced specialty training in Periodontology and Implantology at Boston University School of Graduate Dentistry, where he received his Certificate of Advanced Graduate Studies in Periodontology.
