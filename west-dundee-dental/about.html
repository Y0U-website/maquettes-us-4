Simply put, we care about our patients and it shows.

From the first time you enter our beautifully restored 1870’s building, resplendent with magnificent gardens that can be viewed from treatment chairs to the time you are warmly beckoned in to meet with the dentist, you will feel the extraordinary difference when choosing us as your family dentist.

You are never just a number with us. We schedule a full hour and a half with each new patient, evaluating each tooth individually, viewing x-rays and performing oral cancer and periodontal screenings. Then each patient recieves a customized treatment plan as unique as their smile.

No wonder then, that West Dundee Dental has been the family dentist of choice for generations. With an array of services, modern technology, financing plans, and most importantly, true quality care that focuses on the patient, West Dundee Dental offers many reasons to smile.
