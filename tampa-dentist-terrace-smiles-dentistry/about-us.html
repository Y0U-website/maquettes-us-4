About Dr. Brian Witt

Temple Terrace’s Dentist

Brian Witt, DMD, a life-long resident of Tampa, received his dental degree from the University of Florida in 1995. After receiving his dental degree, he joined an existing dental practice that he made his own in 1999. Since that time, Terrace Smiles has been known for superior, state-of-the-art dentistry with an emphasis on aesthetic.

Always striving for perfection, Dr. Witt has completed extensive hours of postgraduate training at the prestigious Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies. Las Vegas Institute is a world-renowned facility for the study of Aesthetic and Neuromuscular Dentistry. Dr. Witt has also completed hundreds of hours of advanced Implant Training and is a Diplomate of the International Dental Implant Association with the purpose of giving people back their missing teeth.

“My purpose, as a dentist, is to heal my patients’ bodies through their teeth and gums.”
