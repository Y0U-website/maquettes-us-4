Routine Dental Services

Check-Ups and Cleaning

We recommend visits at least every 6 months so Dr. Chaudhry and the team at Somerset Dentistry can conduct a thorough exam of your teeth and gums. During your exam our hygienists gently clean and polish your teeth. They will take diagnostic x-rays if they are due, which Dr. Chaudhry will review with you as she conducts these routine services:

  • Oral cancer screening
  • Periodontal screening (for gum disease)
  • Dental caries (cavities) screening
  • Examination of temporomandibular joints (TMJ) and jaw position

Dental Implants

If you’ve lost a tooth, your best option for replacing it is a dental implant. Implants are the next closest thing to your natural tooth. It preserves the jaw bone just as your natural teeth would. Implants can replace as few as one tooth or as many as all your teeth. Dr. Chaudhry can help you determine if implants are the right option for you.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth Straightening

Teeth straightening isn’t just for teenagers. Today, about 1 in 5 orthodontic patients are adults. Ask Dr. Chaudhry about our clear aligners. The removable aligners consist of a series of clear plastic trays that fit over your teeth. You use each tray for a specific length of time before moving to the next. Each tray moves your teeth just a little bit until you are happy with your smile.

Whitening Services

Costly tooth whitening systems fill the shelves at every pharmacy. But did you know these can damage your mouth? Ill-fitting trays can damage your gums. Some of the products contain abrasives to remove superficial stains. Instead of taking the risk, ask Dr. Chaudhry about our safe, effective, and affordable tooth whitening system. We can help you find the best and safest way to begin improving your smile today.

Emergency Services

When to Seek Emergency Care

  • Toothache: Call (248) 781-8900 if discomfort or pain is intense.
  • Broken, chipped or cracked tooth: Rinse your mouth with warm water and apply a cold compress to your face and call (248) 781-8900 . If the tooth was broken or chipped, bring the tooth fragment wrapped in wet gauze or a wet towel.
  • Loosened or knocked-out tooth: If your tooth becomes loose due to trauma, call (248) 781-8900 immediately. For a knocked out tooth, gently insert the lost tooth back in its socket if possible, holding the tooth by the crown using a clean washcloth. If the tooth is dirty, first rinse the root, but do not scrub it or remove any attached tissues. If reinsertion is not possible, hold the tooth under the tongue and go to the dentist immediately.
  • Bitten Tongue or Lip: Clean the area with a wet cloth and place a cold compress on the area to reduce swelling. If the bleeding persists or if it is excessive, call (248) 781-8900 or go to an emergency center.
