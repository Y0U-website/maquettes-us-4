Your Health Always Comes First

Before recommending implants, Dr. Dedov performs a thorough evaluation of your teeth, gums, and jawbone. Your overall health always comes first.

Both titanium and zirconia (ceramic) implants are foreign objects placed into the body, much like other implants such as knees or hips. For patients with a compromised immune system or specific health concerns, consulting your healthcare provider before treatment is recommended.

If implants aren’t ideal, Dr. Dedov will discuss other biocompatible alternatives such as bridges or dentures. If your jawbone isn’t strong enough to support implants, a bone grafting procedure may be recommended to create a stable foundation for your new teeth.

Why Choose Ceramic Dental Implants? Dental implants are small fixtures surgically placed beneath your gums that act as artificial tooth roots, providing a secure base for natural-looking replacement teeth. Here’s why many patients choose ceramic implants:

  • Preserves Jawbone Integrity: Ceramic implants stimulate the jawbone like natural tooth roots, helping prevent bone loss and maintaining the structure of your face.
  • Convenient Placement: In some cases Implants can be placed immediately after tooth extraction, saving time and reducing the number of procedures.
  • Protects Adjacent Teeth: Unlike bridges, implants don’t require reshaping or altering nearby healthy teeth.
  • Durable and Reliable: Once the crown is placed on your ceramic implant, it provides a strong, stable tooth replacement, allowing you to eat your favorite foods comfortably and confidently.
  • Confidence in Speaking and Smiling: Unlike removable flippers, dental implants stay securely in place, so you can speak, smile, and laugh without fear of slipping or detaching.
  • Biocompatible and Hypoallergenic: Made from zirconia, ceramic implants are metal-free, safe for living tissue, and ideal for patients seeking a natural, biologically compatible dental solution.

Ceramic vs. Titanium Implants

Titanium implants have been widely used for decades, but for patients seeking a metal-free and biologically harmonious option, ceramic implants are often the preferred choice at Tranquility Dental Care.

Key Advantages of Ceramic (Zirconia) Implants:

For many biological dentists and health-conscious patients — including those with autoimmune conditions or allergies — ceramic implants represent the most natural and biocompatible tooth-replacement option available today.

  • 100 % Metal-Free and Hypoallergenic: Zirconia implants contain no metal components, making them an excellent choice for patients with metal sensitivities or allergies.
  • Natural Aesthetics: Their white, tooth-colored appearance blends seamlessly with surrounding teeth and gums — unlike traditional grey titanium.
  • Highly Biocompatible: Zirconia integrates naturally with bone and soft tissue without triggering inflammatory or immune responses that can sometimes occur with metals.
  • Bioinert and Non-Reactive: Ceramic implants do not conduct electricity or heat, reducing the risk of galvanic effects or electromagnetic interference.
  • Corrosion-Resistant: Unlike metal, zirconia does not oxidize or corrode over time, helping maintain long-term stability and tissue health.
  • Gentle on Gums and Surrounding Tissue: Clinical studies have shown lower plaque accumulation and inflammation around ceramic implants compared to metal ones.
  • Proven Long-Term Success: Zirconia implants have been FDA-approved in the U.S. since 2008, and research demonstrates over 96 % success rates at 10 years.

Implant-supported Denture vs. Traditional Denture

Traditional removable dentures have been known to cause severe irritation of the gums, difficulty eating and speaking due to slipping, and an increase risk of infection if they don’t properly fit.

Denture-wearing patients may experience difficulties in chewing and tasting foods, which can compromise their digestion and enjoyment of meals, as well as proper wholesome nutrition.

Advancements in modern technology offer options to improve dentures fit and appearance. One such option involves securing dentures more firmly to the jaw bone using dental implants. By placing implants in the jawbone, dentures can be anchored securely in place, providing greater retention and a more natural look.

Implant-supported dentures offer several advantages over traditional dentures. Not only do they provide enhanced stability and aesthetics, but they also tend to last longer. This option provides patients with increased comfort, confidence, and overall satisfaction with their dental prosthetic.

Implant-supported denture offer a significant improvement by functioning more like natural teeth, it enables individuals to eat a wider variety of wholesome foods without discomfort. This not only enhances their nutritional intake but also improves taste perception and promotes proper digestion. As a result, implant-supported dentures can have a significant impact on overall health and well-being.
