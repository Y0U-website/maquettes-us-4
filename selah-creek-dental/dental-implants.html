Process of Dental Implants

Your consultation includes a comprehensive examination, digital imaging, and a discussion of your smile goals. Your dentist determines whether you’re a candidate for dental implants and creates a personalized treatment plan. The implant is precisely placed into your jawbone, where it gradually fuses with the surrounding bone during healing. After healing is complete, your custom restoration is attached to the implant to restore the appearance and function of your smile.

Next Steps After Getting Dental Implants

Follow all post-operative instructions to support proper healing after implant placement. Maintain excellent oral hygiene by brushing and flossing around your implant every day. Attend your scheduled follow-up visits so your healing can be monitored. Continue routine dental checkups to help keep your implant and surrounding tissues healthy for years to come.

FAQ

  • How long do dental implants last? With proper oral hygiene and regular dental visits, dental implants can provide a long-lasting solution for missing teeth.
  • How many dental implants can you have? The number of dental implants you may need depends on how many teeth you’re replacing and your personalized treatment plan.
  • How often do dental implants fail? Dental implants have a high success rate when properly placed and cared for, but maintaining good oral hygiene is essential for long-term success.
