Rosenberg Orthodontics: Our Treatment Options

When it comes to Rosenberg orthodontics, patients have different needs, requirements, and desires. Luckily, our dental office has the expertise and skill required to provide personalized care in a comfortable environment.

Once your Rosenberg orthodontist diagnoses your condition, we work swiftly and effectively to ensure you enjoy a smooth treatment process. Below are two of the most common orthodontic treatments our patients use to gain a better smile.

INVISALIGN – THE INVISIBLE WAY TO STRAIGHTEN TEETH

As an orthodontics treatment, many adult patients prefer Invisalign. Invisalign aligner trays are virtually unnoticeable, allowing you to smile with confidence throughout the treatment process. As you undergo treatment, virtually no one knows you’re wearing them unless you tell them yourself.

With Invisalign , your Rosenberg orthodontist uses a series of clear, removable aligners to straighten your teeth over time. Every two weeks, you change to a new set of aligners. Little by little, the aligners adjust the position of your teeth and help you work toward the smile you’ve always wanted!

TRADITIONAL & CLEAR BRACKETS – SHORTER TREATMENT TIME

Traditional braces offer your Rosenberg orthodontist a time-tested option to adjust your smile. With our advanced self-ligating system and high-tech wires, this Rosenberg orthodontics treatment moves your teeth more efficiently. Coupled with precision care, this results in faster tooth alignment and shorter treatment time.

Oftentimes, people associate traditional braces with a great deal of discomfort due to wire tightening and elastic changes. However, modern improvements allow for faster tooth movement with much less discomfort. Moreover, it results in a shorter treatment period.

Early Treatment

While your child might still have their primary teeth, that doesn’t mean they are too young to see their Rosenberg orthodontist. In fact, the American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have an orthodontic evaluation at the age of seven.

Typically, our orthodontists simply check and keep a record of how your child’s teeth and jaw develop over time. With a proper record, we know precisely when it’s the right time to begin treatment.
