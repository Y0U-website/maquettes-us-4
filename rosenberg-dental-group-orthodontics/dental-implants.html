HOW DENTAL IMPLANTS WORK

A dental implant replaces the missing tooth root. A natural root stimulates the healthy growth of bone in your jaw. However, when you lose a tooth and its root, the process of regeneration stops. Then, the bone begins to deteriorate.

Due to this, it is essential to replace missing teeth. While bridges and dentures help you chew, they do not replace the root structure. This structure is essential for the bone to grow. A dental implant is a small titanium post that we set in your jaw and allow to heal. Once your jaw heals, the implant helps the process of healthy bone growth resume.

Titanium is a biocompatible material that forms a secure bond with surrounding bone and tissues during the healing process. After healing is complete, the implant provides a stable and secure foundation for the restoration – a crown, bridge, or denture – that will be attached to it to restore function and appearance.

Following the treatment, you may need to eat soft foods and take pain medication for a short time. However, the healing process does not take long. Although implant placement at Rosenberg Dental and Orthodontics is highly advanced, the results are basic.
