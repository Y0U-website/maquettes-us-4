The practice

Sleep solutions from a dentist often include custom oral appliances that help keep the airway open during sleep, reducing snoring and symptoms of sleep apnea. By addressing oral and jaw alignment, dentists can improve breathing patterns and support better quality sleep.

Dr. Sorenson specializes in personalized treatments for patients diagnosed with Obstructive Sleep Apnea and other sleep-related breathing disorders, using customized oral appliances designed to help keep the airway open during sleep.
