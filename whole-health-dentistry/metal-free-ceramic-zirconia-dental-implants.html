Benefits of Choosing Zirconia

Zirconia earns its place for reasons that go beyond avoiding metal:

  • Truly metal-free. Nothing corrodes, and nothing conducts electricity or hot-and-cold sensation the way a metal post can.
  • Natural-looking results. Because the ceramic is tooth-colored all the way through, your gumline stays bright rather than shadowed by a gray edge, which matters most for front teeth. This is a natural fit with our biological, whole-body approach to dentistry .
  • Biocompatible by design. The material is chosen to sit peacefully in your body, a real consideration if you’re managing sensitivities or an autoimmune condition. Dr. Copeland places these implants using the Swiss BioHealth zirconia implant protocol.
  • Strong and stable. Zirconia is a dense, hard-wearing ceramic that stands up to daily chewing and biting.
  • Restores full function. A well-integrated implant lets you eat, speak, and smile without working around a gap.
  • Supports the bone. Like a natural root, an implant helps preserve the jawbone that otherwise shrinks where a tooth is missing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are zirconia implants different from titanium?

Zirconia is a ceramic, so no metal is placed in your body. For anyone sensitive to metals, or who simply prefers the most biocompatible route, that distinction is the whole point.

Are they as strong and durable as titanium?

Zirconia is a dense, hard-wearing ceramic that handles the demands of daily chewing and biting. It’s been used in dentistry for years and is a clinically sound choice for implant restorations.

Will a zirconia implant look natural?

Yes. Because the ceramic is tooth-colored throughout, there’s no dark metal edge appearing at the gumline over time, and most people find the result blends seamlessly with the teeth around it.

How do I care for them long-term?

You brush and floss it like any other tooth, plus keep up with regular checkups. For the full routine, including cleaning around the implant site during healing, see our care and preparation guidance above.

Can I still get zirconia if I’ve had implants before?

Prior implant experience doesn’t automatically rule you out. A careful look at your bone health, gum tissue, and overall oral environment will clarify what’s possible for your situation.

How does the Swiss BioHealth protocol fit in?

It’s the biocompatible, ceramic-focused approach we use for placement, developed specifically around zirconia implants and whole-body healing. It guides how the implant is placed and how we support your recovery.
