The practice

Dr. Caitlin has always had a love for science and medicine. Growing up in her mom s organic chemistry lab gave her an early opportunity do genetic research and advance studies that would later culminate in her graduating top 10 in her undergraduate class at Hillsdale College and 1st in her dental school class at the University of Detroit Mercy.

Dr. Caitlin also played volleyball as a collegiate athlete with her team making the Division II playoffs tournament every year. Dr. Caitlin is a happy mom of 3 and an active member of her church community here in West Michigan. She enjoys baking, gardening, and sharing her love of reading and learning with families visiting the practice.

Staci Best is our office manager. She knows all our patients by name, and with many years experience, she makes scheduling appointments and working with your insurance company easy.
