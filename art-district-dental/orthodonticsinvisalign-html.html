Orthodontics & Invisalign in Fort Worth

Patients of all ages have relied on traditional metallic braces to straighten out their smiles, but for some, the length of treatment time, frequent visits to the orthodontist, hassle involved in wearing bracket and wire braces, and the appearance of traditional braces all deter patients from seeking out orthodontic treatment. At Art District Dental, we offer patients two alternative orthodontic treatment methods, Invisalign and Six Month Smiles, that allow patients to correct irregular spaced or crooked teeth without the complications associated with bracket and wire braces.
