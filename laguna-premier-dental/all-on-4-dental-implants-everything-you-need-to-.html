How Does the All-on-4 Procedure Work?

The process for getting All-on-4 implants is streamlined and efficient, often completed in just one day:

  • Consultation and Planning During your initial visit, the team at Laguna Premier Dental will assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and take detailed scans to create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement On the day of the procedure, four implants are strategically placed into the jawbone. Two implants are positioned vertically at the front, while two are placed at an angle at the back to maximize support and bone contact.
  • Attachment of Temporary Teeth A set of temporary prosthetic teeth is secured to the implants, allowing you to leave the office with a fully functional smile.
  • Final Restoration After a healing period of 3-6 months, during which the implants integrate with your jawbone, your permanent teeth are attached, providing a seamless and durable finish.

All-on-4 is an excellent solution for individuals who have lost most or all of their teeth or are tired of dealing with the discomfort and inconvenience of traditional dentures.

Why Choose All-on-4 Over Traditional Dentures?

While traditional dentures are a common option for replacing missing teeth, they often come with drawbacks such as slipping, discomfort, and a lack of stability. All-on-4 offers several advantages:

  • No messy adhesives.
  • A more natural look and feel.
  • Preservation of jawbone health, as implants stimulate bone growth.
  • Easier maintenance—no need to remove for cleaning.
