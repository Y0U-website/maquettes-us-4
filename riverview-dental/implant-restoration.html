When Do You Need an Implant Restoration?

You may need a new or replacement restoration if:

We’ll assess the condition of your implant and create a tailored plan to restore your smile quickly and comfortably.

  • You’ve had a dental implant placed and need the crown added
  • Your current implant crown or bridge is cracked, chipped, or loose
  • You’re experiencing discomfort or changes in bite alignment

Caring for Your Implant Restoration

Maintaining your dental implant restoration is simple. Brush and floss daily, use a soft-bristled toothbrush, and visit us for regular dental cleanings and exams. We’ll monitor the health of your gums and surrounding teeth to ensure long-term success.
