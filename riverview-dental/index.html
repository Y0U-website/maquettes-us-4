At Riverview Dental, we understand that visiting the dentist can be stressful. That is why we work hard to make our office a safe, supportive space.

The practice

Dr. Synthia Chowdhury believes that fear often comes from the unknown. We help reduce anxiety by removing the mystery. We move at your pace and explain every step before we do it, ensuring you are comfortable. We focus on conservative care, saving your natural teeth and keeping treatments simple. No surprises, no rushing, just gentle care on your terms.

Honest Guidance We promise transparency. We show you exactly what we see on the X-rays and recommend only what is truly necessary for your health.​

Supportive Care Whether you are fearful or just haven’t been in a while, you will be met with kindness. We use gentle techniques to make your visit as comfortable as possible.

Your Choice We lay out your treatment options clearly so you understand the plan. You decide what happens next. No pressure, ever.

As the owner of Riverview Dental, Dr. Synthia Chowdhury is personally invested in the well-being of every patient who walks through the door. She is here to build long-term relationships, not just fix teeth.

Known for her gentle touch and honest guidance, she treats you like family - focusing on conservative care and saving your natural teeth, rather than upselling treatments you don t need.
