Dr. Molina&#x27;s Endodontic Training & Credentials

Specialty Focus: Endodontics at San Jose Dental Clinic

Endodontics is the branch of dentistry focused on the inner structures of the tooth — the pulp, nerves, and surrounding tissue. Dr. Molina&#x27;s specialty training allows her to diagnose and treat complex cases that go beyond what a general dentist typically handles. As an in-house specialist at San Jose Dental Clinic, she provides care that would otherwise require a separate referral to an outside practice.

Clinical Technology She Uses — Operating Microscope, Apex Locator & More

Dr. Molina uses advanced tools to achieve precision in every procedure. An operating microscope lets her see inside the tooth with exceptional detail, revealing tiny fractures or canals that could be missed with the naked eye. She also uses an electronic apex locator to measure the root canal precisely, and an Obtura system for efficient, thorough filling of the canal space. These technologies help make treatment more predictable and often more comfortable.
