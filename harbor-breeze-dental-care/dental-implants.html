Dental Implants- Jupiter, Florida

When you have gaps in your smile from tooth loss, dental implants are the closest possible substitute for your own natural teeth. Implants are long-lasting, comfortable, and with the use of tooth-colored crowns and bridges, blend seamlessly with existing teeth. If you’re missing one tooth, or even all of your teeth, dental implants provide a solution to tooth loss that does not cause damage to your oral health. Harbor Breeze Dental offers patients the benefits of implant dentistry in Jupiter, FL.

In-house Dental Implants

Implants are a durable way to restore missing teeth as they are surgically placed and integrate with surrounding tissue, acting like a real tooth root. Because placing dental implants requires minor oral surgery, visiting a certified oral surgeon or periodontist is typically required. At Harbor Breeze Dental, we understand how much of a hassle it can be to visit a secondary office for care, which is why we bring specialists to you.

We strive to keep all parts of your treatment plan in our Jupiter dental office. The convenience of in-house implant dentistry means that all of your records and treatment planning information is easily accessible by both Dr. Mark Kubiliun and any visiting specialists. We use the advanced technology of 3D panoramic imaging to determine if you’re a candidate for treatment and pinpoint the most appropriate angle for implant placement, so your new teeth are as strong as possible.

Options for Implant Dentistry

Implants are versatile and work well to replace any number of missing teeth. Dental implants themselves look like a small screw, which is placed into bone in the jaw at the site of a missing tooth, and is then topped with fake tooth or set of teeth. Think of implants as new tooth roots – they nurture bone health and act as anchors for replacement teeth.

Our Jupiter implant dentist provides patients with multiple options for treatment, which include:

Implant-Supported Crowns – If you have just one missing tooth, or sporadic tooth loss in several places in your smile, implants can be added individually and topped with single dental crowns.

Fixed Implant Bridges – A traditional dental bridge is a multi-unit piece that is used to fill in the gap caused by the loss of one or more teeth in specific location. Unlike a standard bridge, which uses existing teeth as anchors, implant bridges are independent prosthetics that are secured by dental implant posts.

Permanent Over-Dentures and Hybrid Dentures – For patients who are missing all of their teeth, these fixed or permanent dentures are significantly more comfortable and functional than traditional dentures. Depending on your treatment goals and oral health, Dr. Kubiliun can use between 2-6 dental implants to hold a full arch of teeth in place.

Mini Dental Implants – The best candidates for dental implants are those who have healthy jaw bone quality so implants have enough tissue to embed into. Patients with thin bone, however, can still benefit from implant dentistry with the use of mini dental implants. As these implants are smaller in diameter, they do not require dense bone volume to integrate. If you need a bone graft before an implant can be placed, Dr. Kubiliun can perform the graft in his office to ensure you receive the most comprehensive care possible.
