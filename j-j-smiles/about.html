What to Expect at Your Visit

Patient Experience

When you first walk through our doors, you’ll be greeted by our comfortable environment and our friendly office staff. Then, you’ll be invited to sit down and relax as you fill out any new patient forms prior to your appointment. Once those are complete and your hygienist is ready for you, we’ll invite you back into one of our operatories.

You’ll receive a cleaning, comprehensive exam, and X-rays. Next, your dentist will sit down with you to discuss the results. If everything looks good, you’ll be able to schedule your next appointment in the front office. If anything needs extra attention, your dentist will explain the results, take time to address any questions or concerns, and describe any further treatment recommendations.

What to Bring

In order to help make your visit more efficient, we ask that you bring your ID, your insurance information, and any patient forms you’ve filled out prior to your appointment.
