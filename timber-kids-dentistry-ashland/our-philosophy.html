The Importance of Building Relationships Early

We love kids!

Here’s how we ensure every child feels comfortable and safe at the dentist!

Communication is imperative. Your child&#x27;s doctor will get to know you and your family with one-on-one communication.

Did your child have a bad experience in the past? We drop down to their level and discuss those past experiences with them to gain trust.

We won’t lean that chair back right away. Instead, we let your child feel empowered to love the dentist and give them control in their dental care.

Every appointment is tailored to your child. From finding the perfect Netflix movie and using the same team every time, your child will love the dentist.

If these options aren’t enough to ease your child’s fears or anxiety, we offer in-office sedation and general anesthesia.

What Makes Our Team Different

Our team works hard to create positive dental experiences for kiddos. The best way to do that is to be energetic, upbeat, and to speak fluent kid. We know how to communicate with children of all ages in ways that make them feel respected. When they understand what’s going on with their oral health, they’re more likely to take care of their smiles. By instilling a little responsibility, we open up the door for more adventure!

What Makes Our Dentists Different

Our team of expert, fun-loving dentists understands the importance of enthusiastic dental care. We focus on skillful dental work while developing real friendships with our patients throughout the years. Nothing makes us happier than to help maintain your child’s oral health and catch up on all of your adventures during every visit!
