Evan Lynn, DDS

Dr. Evan Lynn graduated from SUNY Stony Brook School of Dental Medicine in 2000. He completed his post-graduate endodontic residency at NYU School of Dental Medicine in 2005 and thereafter joined the faculty as an Assistant Clinical Professor in the Department of Endodontics. Subsequently, he completed a two-year fellowship in implant dentistry at the school’s Department of Periodontics and Implant Dentistry. As a dual-trained endodontist and implantologist, Dr. Lynn became one of the leaders in treatment planning for the compromised tooth.

Dr. Lynn has lectured at over fifteen post-graduate endodontics programs throughout the country. He has given an all-day lecture at the American Association of Endodontics annual meeting in San Diego. He has an accredited continuing education online presentation available to general dentists. He has lectured internationally on the factors involved in selecting whether to save a compromised tooth or to replace it with an implant.
