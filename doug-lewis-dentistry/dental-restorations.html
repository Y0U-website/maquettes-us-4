Damaged or Missing Teeth? We Can Help

Chipped, decayed, or missing teeth don’t just affect your appearance—they impact your confidence, comfort, and oral health.

While regular dental cleanings can identify problems early, untreated damage can worsen over time, leading to pain or tooth loss. Missing teeth can shift your bite and cause neighboring teeth to drift out of place. DIY or temporary fixes don’t provide the protection and durability your teeth need.

Composite Fillings

Composite fillings are tooth-colored and natural-looking, seamlessly matching your existing teeth for a beautiful finish. They are durable and designed to withstand daily chewing for many years. This versatile solution is ideal for addressing chipped, cracked, decayed, or worn teeth and can also be used to close small gaps. Composite fillings restore teeth to full function while preserving a natural appearance.

Porcelain Fixed Bridges

Porcelain fixed bridges are a non-removable solution that replaces missing teeth with a durable and long-lasting structure. They prevent neighboring teeth from drifting out of place while restoring function and appearance, improving chewing, speech, and smile aesthetics. The process involves two visits: during the first, anchor teeth are prepared, impressions are taken, and a temporary bridge is placed. At the second visit, the custom-made bridge is adjusted, checked for fit, and securely cemented.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy is a highly effective treatment that saves a tooth by addressing infection and decay without requiring extraction. It eliminates pain, swelling, and sensitivity while providing long-term success. Signs that you may need a root canal include severe toothache pain, sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling or tenderness, and the presence of an abscess. The procedure involves removing the damaged pulp and decay, thoroughly cleaning the tooth, and sealing it to prevent reinfection. To ensure long-term protection, a crown is placed on the treated tooth.

FAQ

Here are answers to some common questions about dental restorations.

What are dental restorations?

Dental restorations repair or replace damaged, decayed, or missing teeth to restore full function, strength, and appearance.

How do I know if I need a crown or a filling?

Fillings are used for smaller areas of decay, while crowns are needed to protect teeth with significant damage or after a root canal.

What is a dental implant restoration?

It’s a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture placed on a dental implant post to replace missing teeth.

Is a root canal painful?

No. Modern root canal treatments are comfortable, and we ensure the area is completely numbed during the procedure.

How long do dental restorations last?

With proper care including regular dental cleanings , fillings, crowns, and bridges can last many years. Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime.

Will my dental restoration look natural?

Yes. We use high-quality, tooth-colored materials like porcelain and composite resin to blend seamlessly with your smile.
