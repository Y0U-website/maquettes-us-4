FAQ

Here are answers to some common questions about our dental cleanings and exams.

How often should I schedule a dental cleaning?

We recommend a cleaning every six months to maintain healthy teeth and gums, but your dentist may suggest more frequent visits based on your needs.

What happens during a dental cleaning?

Our cleaning includes tartar and plaque removal, teeth polishing, and an exam to check for any dental issues or gum inflammation.

Are dental x-rays necessary during a check-up?

Yes, x-rays help detect hidden issues like decay, bone loss, or cysts that aren’t visible during a visual exam.

What are dental sealants, and who should get them?

Sealants are a protective coating applied to the back teeth to prevent decay. They’re especially beneficial for younger patients when permanent teeth first emerge.

Can regular cleanings prevent gum disease?

Absolutely! Regular cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup, which are key factors in preventing gum disease

Is a dental cleaning uncomfortable?

Not at all! Our team uses gentle techniques to ensure your cleaning is thorough and comfortable.

What’s included in a dental exam?

A dental exam includes x-rays, oral cancer screening, gum disease evaluation, and checking for decay or issues with existing fillings or crowns.
