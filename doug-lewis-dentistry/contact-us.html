Driving directions:

From UAB — We’re right here. From the UAB Hospital and campus area, it’s a two-minute drive (or an easy walk) south to 17th Street South near 10th–11th Avenue South.

From Vulcan Park & Museum — Head down Red Mountain on 20th Street South and continue north into the Five Points South / Southside area. We’re just a few blocks west on 17th Street South — about a 5-minute drive.

From the Birmingham Zoo & Botanical Gardens — Take Cahaba Road onto Highland Avenue South and follow Highland through to Five Points South, then continue a few blocks to 17th Street South. Roughly 10 minutes.

From The Summit (Hwy 280) — Take US-280 West, which feeds onto the Red Mountain Expressway heading down into the city. Exit toward UAB/Southside (University Boulevard) and you’ll reach 17th Street South in about 12–15 minutes, depending on 280 traffic.
