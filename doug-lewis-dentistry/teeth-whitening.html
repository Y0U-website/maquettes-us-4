How long does in-office teeth whitening take?

Our Sensational Smile in-office treatment takes just 30 minutes, making it perfect for busy schedules.

Are the at-home whitening kits effective?

Yes! Both our custom-fitted trays and pre-made kits provide consistent, gradual whitening for noticeable results over time.

Is professional teeth whitening safe?

Absolutely. Our whitening treatments are designed to be safe for your enamel and minimize sensitivity when used correctly.

How long will the whitening results last?

Results vary depending on your habits. Avoiding staining foods and drinks and maintaining good oral hygiene can extend the results for months to years.

Will teeth whitening work on all types of stains?

Our treatments work best on yellow-brown stains caused by food, drinks, or aging. We’ll discuss your options during your consultation.

We’ll help you choose between in-office whitening or an at-home kit based on your schedule, goals, and preferences.
