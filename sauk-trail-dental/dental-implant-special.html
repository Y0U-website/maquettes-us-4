Benefits of Dental Implants

Before dental implants, patients who suffered from tooth loss only had the option to replace missing teeth with dentures or a bridge. With dental implants, a patient can completely restore the function and aesthetics of their smile with a natural-looking prosthetic that will last a lifetime. An implant acts as an artificial tooth root, supports a beautiful restoration, and minimizes common issues associated with tooth loss.

How long will my implant last?

With proper care, a dental implant can last 30+ years, and they often last for the rest of a patient’s life. We strategically and precisely place your dental implant to ensure that it bonds with the rest of your jaw and gum tissue and gives you a strong artificial tooth root. The materials we use to create dental implants are the highest quality, and you can trust that the results of your treatment will be long-lasting.

How do I care for dental implants?

After your first visit, we’ll set you up with a personalized treatment plan. It could be as simple as maintaining your regular cleanings every six months, or a plan to address more complex issues. Whatever it is, we’ll work together to get your smile into shape. You should care for your dental implant the same way you care for your natural teeth— brush, floss, and come to Sauk Trail Dental every six months for an oral exam and cleaning.
