Our Dental Services

Family Dentistry

We offer dental care for your entire family including children, teenagers, adults, and seniors.

Children’s Dentistry

Our office is child and family friendly and we look forward to caring for your children for a lifetime.

Dental Emergency Care

Whether it’s a tooth ache or a chipped tooth, we are ready to help you alleviate your pain and save your smile.

Pediatric Extractions

Digital X-Rays

Digital systems use 70 to 90 % less radiation than conventional x-rays, which is more accurate and safer for the patient.

Children Restorations

Education on oral hygiene is something we take very seriously and view as an important part in creating a lifelong smile for your child.

Nerve Treatments

These procedures allow us to save a baby tooth if possible while the adult tooth matures and has the time to erupt naturally.

Space Maintainers

When a baby tooth is lost prematurely it may be necessary to place a space maintainer to correct tooth alignment.

Special Needs

We are aware of the challenges associated with oral hygiene and instruction of special needs and can assist to make it easier.

Cavity Free at Three

We are happy to start dental exams as early as 6 months of age. Our goal is to work with you and your child to help your child grow cavity free.

Baby Teeth and Infant Teething

Baby teeth normally start to come in at 6 months of age, but the teething process can start as early as 3 months.

Dental Restorations

Dental restorations refer to the practice of repairing injured tooth structure with a dental material of some type.

Dental Cleanings

We believe strongly in the importance of oral hygiene education. Our dental professionals can review areas where you are doing well or where you may need improvement.

Dental Exams

Dental exams and cleanings are recommended every 6 months, this gives us the opportunity to monitor your oral health and spot any issues as they arise.

Dentures

If you are suffering from missing teeth or feel like your teeth are beyond saving a denture might be a good option for you. A full denture is meant to replace a full arch of teeth.

Partial Dentures

Missing teeth can affect how we look and how we eat. Partial dentures can be a great cost-effective option if you are missing teeth and are looking to restore function.

Root Canal Therapy

This is a procedure where the inside of your tooth that houses the nerve and the pulp is removed and cleaned. After the inside of the roots is cleaned it is filled with a dental material.

Dental Crowns

Many tooth problems can be solved with simple filling restorations, but sometimes due to extent of tooth loss or for cosmetic concerns, it may be necessary to crown the tooth.

Tooth Extractions

Adult teeth are meant to last a lifetime, but sometimes due to crowding, decay, or trauma teeth may need to be extracted. Tooth Extractions are what many refer to as pulling a tooth.

Deep Cleaning

The main treatment for periodontal disease is what many commonly call a deep cleaning or what we refer to as scaling a root planning.
