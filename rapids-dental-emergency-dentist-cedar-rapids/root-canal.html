Signs You Need a Root Canal

The symptoms of needing a root canal are rarely subtle. If you notice any of the following, it may be time to give Rapids Dental a call to get an appointment for a root canal in Cedar Rapids today. Routine dental checkups are also helpful in spotting any tooth decay before it can progress into a full, painful infection.

  • Severe and sharp pain in and around your tooth
  • Discoloration of your gums or teeth (for example: dark purple gums or a darkening of the tooth)
  • Bleeding from gums
  • Increased sensitivity to hot or cold foods and beverages
  • A tooth is noticeably loose
  • Swelling and tenderness around a tooth
  • Damage to your tooth (such as cracks)
