When should I take my child to the dentist for their first check-up?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends your children see a pediatric dentist when their first tooth appears or no later than their first birthday.

Most children benefit from professional dental cleanings every six months. Some children at higher risk for cavities may need more frequent visits.
