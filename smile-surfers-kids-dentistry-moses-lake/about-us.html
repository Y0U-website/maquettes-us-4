About Us

At Smile Surfers, we’re passionate about making a difference in the lives of Moses Lake families and fostering a community of bright, healthy smiles.

  • Local Schools: We offer educational presentations at preschools and early childhood programs throughout the Moses Lake area.
  • Community Support: We proudly sponsor local school sports teams and fundraisers to support our community’s youth.
  • Holiday Giving: Every Christmas, we adopt families in need. We learn about what they need or enjoy, shop for gifts, wrap them, and personally deliver the presents. The joy on their faces is our greatest reward!
