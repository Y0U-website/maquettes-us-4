What Counts as a Dental Emergency?

  • Throbbing or severe toothache
  • Cracked or broken tooth
  • Knocked-out tooth
  • Gum or jaw swelling
  • Dental abscess or infection
  • Bleeding that won’t stop
  • Pain after dental procedure
  • Lost crown or filling
  • Facial trauma
