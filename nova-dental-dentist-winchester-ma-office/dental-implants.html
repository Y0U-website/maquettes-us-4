What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for tooth replacement because they restore both the visible tooth and the root beneath it. Each implant has three components:

The Implant Post

A small biocompatible titanium post is placed into the jawbone. Over time, it naturally fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation.

The Abutment

This connector attaches to the implant post and supports the final restoration.

The Custom Crown

The visible portion of the tooth is custom-designed to match the color, shape, and size of your surrounding teeth. Once complete, your implant looks natural, feels secure, and allows you to eat and speak confidently. If you are searching for permanent tooth replacement in Winchester MA , dental implants provide unmatched durability and stability.

Why Choose Dental Implants Instead of Dentures or Bridges?

While traditional dentures and bridges remain viable options, implants offer significant advantages.

They Preserve Your Jawbone

When a tooth is lost, the underlying bone begins to shrink. Implants stimulate the bone like a natural tooth root, helping prevent bone loss and facial changes.

They Do Not Slip or Shift

No adhesives. No movement. No discomfort. Implants remain fixed in place.

They Protect Adjacent Teeth

Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require altering neighboring healthy teeth.

They Are Built to Last

With proper care, implants can last 15 to 25 years or longer. For patients comparing dental implants vs dentures in Winchester MA , implants often provide the most natural experience. ‍

You may be a strong candidate if you: ● Have healthy gums ● Maintain good oral hygiene ● Have adequate jawbone support ● Are a non-smoker or willing to quit ● Do not have uncontrolled medical conditions that impair healing Even if you have experienced bone loss, you may still qualify. The Dental Implant Process

Step 1: Consultation and Digital Imaging

Step 2: Implant Placement

The titanium implant is gently placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia or sedation for comfort.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long does the dental implant process take?

Typically three to six months, depending on healing and whether additional procedures such as bone grafting are needed.

Do dental implants hurt?

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation. Most patients report mild discomfort afterward.

Are implants better than dentures?

For many patients, yes. Implants provide superior stability, comfort, and bone preservation compared to removable dentures.

Can I eat normally with dental implants?

Yes. Once healed, implants allow you to chew normally without restrictions.

What happens if I do not replace a missing tooth?

Untreated gaps can lead to shifting teeth, bite issues, bone loss, and facial changes over time.
