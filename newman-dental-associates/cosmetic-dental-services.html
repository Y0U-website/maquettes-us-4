Why Choose Newman Dental Associates

Cosmetic Dentistry Our wide range of cosmetic dentistry services cater to various aesthetic concerns. We offer solutions for minor adjustments and complete smile changes that can significantly improve how your teeth and gums look. We customize our treatments to your unique needs, ensuring results that appear natural.

Porcelain Veneers Porcelain veneers are slim, custom-made covers that match your tooth color and fit over the front of your teeth. They’re great for fixing stained, chipped, decayed, or crooked teeth. Our veneers are made from quality porcelain that looks like natural teeth, giving you a beautiful smile that lasts.

Smile Makeovers A smile makeover is a full treatment plan that uses several cosmetic procedures to achieve your desired look. We’ll work with you to create a custom plan that might include teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, or other treatments.

Teeth Whitening Our professional teeth whitening treatments can make your smile much brighter, removing stains and discoloration that have built up over years. We offer both in-office and take-home whitening options to fit your needs and lifestyle.
