Accessibility Statement

Smile Orthodontics Stillwater LLC is committed to facilitating the accessibility and usability of its website, https://smileortho.net , for everyone. Smile Orthodontics Stillwater LLC aims to comply with all applicable standards, including the World Wide Web Consortium’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.0 up to Level AA (WCAG 2.0 AA).

Smile Orthodontics Stillwater LLC is proud of the efforts that we have completed and that are in-progress to ensure that our website is accessible to everyone. Should you experience any difficulty in accessing any part of this website , please feel free to call us at 651.351.7777 or email us at info@smileortho.net and we will work with you to provide the information or service you seek through an alternate communication method that is accessible for you consistent with applicable law (for example, through telephone support).
