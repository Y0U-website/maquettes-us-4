Meet your Doctors

Dr. Doug Wolff

Inspired by a college friend’s career choice, Dr. Wolff sought advice from his father, a dentist, sparking his own passion for dentistry. With a commitment to providing top-notch dental care, Dr. Wolff has built a reputation for excellence at Smile Orthodontics.

In his personal life, Dr. Wolff resides in St. Paul with his family—Sara, Maverick, Olympia, and their rescue dog, Millie.

Outside of work, Dr. Wolff finds joy in exploring local coffee shops and reading old-fashioned newspapers.

Dr. Tsitsi Hungwe

Originally from Zimbabwe, Dr. Tsitsi immigrated to the US in 2003 with her family to pursue higher education opportunities. As a middle child with four siblings, she values family and currently resides in Brooklyn Park.

Beyond her professional life, Dr. Hungwe enjoys laughter, believing it is good for the soul! She also has a passion for trying new foods and exploring different cultures.

Dr. Jennifer DeChant

A city lover since 2002, Dr. DeChant calls South Minneapolis home, indulging in her love for biking along Minnehaha Parkway, exploring the chain of lakes, and enjoying the urban lifestyle. Beyond her professional pursuits, she is an avid reader, baker, and a dog lover, particularly to her Puggle, Winnie.

Dr. DeChant finds happiness out on the water, as she dreams of someday living on a boat in the middle of the ocean.

Dr. Steve Clifford

He decided to specialize in orthodontics because he enjoys working with children and teenagers, and likes the challenge of improving someone’s smile. He gets satisfaction from seeing the changes orthodontic treatment can make in a Patient’s life — not just in appearance, but also in their confidence and self-image..

In his free time, Dr. Clifford enjoys going for bike rides, listening to podcasts about biking, and relaxing with his family.

Dr. Mike Mrosak

Residing in the Twin Cities, Dr. Mrosak has deep roots in the community where he has lived his entire life. Beyond his professional achievements, he values family, sharing his life with a loving wife, three accomplished adult daughters, and two grandsons.

Dr. Mrosak has a talent for strategy, as he was among the top five grade school chess players in Minnesota, Leading his grade school chess team to consecutive state championships in 5th and 6th grade.

Dr. Sean O’Dell

Originally drawn to pharmaceutical research, Dr. O’Dell’s desire to work directly with people led him to dentistry. His patient-centered approach reflects his belief in the importance of personalized care. Outside the office, Dr. O’Dell enjoys family life with his wife, whom he met in college, and their daughter.

Dr. O’Dell enjoys scary movies and is a former boxer.
