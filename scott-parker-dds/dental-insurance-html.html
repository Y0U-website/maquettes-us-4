How Does Dental Insurance Work?

Dental insurance aims to reduce overall costs of your dental care by heavily discounting or completely covering the preventive dentistry that help you avoid cavities and other oral health hazards. You receive the benefits in exchange for a premium, or a set amount of money you or your employer pay each month. Schedule these treatments before the calendar year is complete to avoid wasting the benefits you are owed.

You will pay any amount that is not fully covered under your plan out-of-pocket. Your dentist receives the full amount from your insurance provider after we file and process the claim.

Making the Most of Your Dental Insurance Plan

Dental insurance is a wonderful investment in your oral and overall health. Unfortunately, many patients allow their benefits to go to waste because they are unsure of how to maximize on their dental insurance plan. Don’t let that happen to you! Remember that you can get in touch with our office at any time to discuss what benefits you can receive with your dental insurance plan. Most patients can receive a checkup and cleaning for free every six months. Necessary restorative care, like tooth-colored fillings, crowns, bridges, and other treatments, are usually significantly discounted.

You probably won’t meet your deductible, or the amount you have to pay out-of-pocket before insurance kicks in, every year. But when you do, you will want to ensure that you also reach your annual maximum -- the amount of money up to which your dental insurance must cover. We can talk to you about your options and help you decide which treatments you could benefit from most.
