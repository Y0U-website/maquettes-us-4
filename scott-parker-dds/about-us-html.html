About our Calimesa, CA Dental Office

What Sets us Apart

Professional & Caring

You’re never rushed through an informal treatment experience at our practice. Dr. Parker takes his time to get to know each patient from the very first appointment, and building relationships with you and your loved ones is important to him. More than anything, our staff wants to be your partners as you work towards achieving and maintaining excellent oral health.

Compassionate Team

Here in Calimesa, patients can trust in our team members in fact, some of them have been with the practice for well over 10 years! We understand the realities of daily life and how oral health may fall by the wayside from time to time; that’s why we’re here to help with kind attention, helpful oral hygiene education, and plenty of support.

Convenient Location

Having to drive around in circles to find your chosen dental practice is a good way to get started on the wrong foot. Thankfully, you won’t have to worry about that with our Calimesa office! We’re conveniently located just off Christopher Columbus Transcontinental Highway and right by the Calimesa post office for easy access that benefits you and your family’s busy schedules.

Personalized Experiences

Seeing the same patients time and time again is such a joy for Dr. Parker and the rest of our team, and we really enjoy getting to know about not just your oral health, but your hobbies, your school or work life, and the goals you have in mind for your new and improved smile. Being able to create an atmosphere where you feel welcomed and right at home is important to us!

High-Quality Dentistry

We’re proud to offer a wide range of services that can fit any smile’s needs, from routine check-ups and cleanings to more advanced services like dental implant restorations, sleep apnea treatment, and children’s dentistry. We even offer high-quality, tooth-colored ceramic materials for your new dental work, ensuring that your teeth keep their natural and confident appearance.

Insurance & Financing

Paying for quality dental care shouldn’t have to cause a headache. We strive to make the process as simple and convenient as possible here in Calimesa! Our team accepts insurance and is an in-network provider for both Cigna and Delta PPO plans. We also welcome manageable, low-interest financing through CareCredit.
