Education and Certifications

Dr. Jess studied Kinesiology at the University of British Columbia, earning both her bachelor’s and master’s degrees before heading south to sunny Los Angeles to pursue dentistry. She later completed her pediatric dental residency at USC and Miller Children’s & Women’s Hospital in Long Beach.

Dr. Niki attended the University of California, Berkeley, majoring in Integrative Biology—a foundational step in her ongoing study of integrative health. After completing her undergraduate studies, she earned her dental degree at the Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco. Driven by her love for working with children and families to establish healthy, lifelong habits, she then headed to the University of Michigan. There, she completed her Pediatric Dentistry Residency and simultaneously earned a Master’s in Pediatric Dentistry.

Orthodontist

Dr. Ohanesian’s treatment philosophy integrates facial esthetics, functional harmony, as well as healthy growth and development to create smiles that look great and function well for a lifetime. He has extensive training from both UCLA and Loma Linda University. Dr. Ohanesian is passionate about creating smiles that are as healthy as they are beautiful — and he believes those two things are deeply connected.

At Milk Tooth, Dr. Ohanesian brings a whole-body approach to orthodontic care. A straight, confident smile matters, but so does how it gets there — how a child’s jaw grows, how their airway develops, and how their bite affects breathing, sleep, and posture over a lifetime. He has extensive experience in airway-focused orthodontics, including treatment for mouth breathing, narrow palates, and sleep apnea, and he considers these foundational to long-term wellness, not just add-ons to a straighter smile.

Dr. Ohanesian treats patients of all ages, combining traditional techniques with cutting-edge options including fixed braces, Invisalign clear aligners, functional appliances, myofunctional therapy, mini-implants, and laser-assisted procedures. His expertise spans early growth modification in children all the way through complex orthognathic surgery for adults.

What sets him apart is his individualized approach. He takes the time to understand each patient’s goals and comfort level, so treatment feels efficient, effective, and — yes — even enjoyable. As a father of three young children himself, he understands firsthand what it takes to guide a young patient through the ups and downs of orthodontic treatment, and he uses that perspective to choose methods that deliver incredible results while keeping kids (and their parents) comfortable every step of the way.

Dr. Ohanesian is committed to helping every patient feel confident, supported, and excited about their smile journey — inside and out.
