What are mini dental implants?

Dental implants are small but mighty tools that can help solve all sorts of dental concerns. Mini dental implants are a great option for people who need to replace one or a few missing teeth but don’t want to deal with traditional, bulky implants. They’re quick to place, low maintenance, and can help you get your smile and confidence back in no time.
