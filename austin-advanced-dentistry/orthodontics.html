What is orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a specialty within dentistry that realigns and reshapes your tooth and jaw placement. The way your teeth and nearby structures interact with one another can significantly impact your health. With Austin Advanced Dentistry orthodontics, they can create personalized treatment options to ensure your teeth, jaw, and other structures function properly.

Why is orthodontics important?

Proper teeth and jaw alignment can significantly impact your breathing and general oral health. When teeth sit correctly in the mouth, there is a much lower risk of developing health concerns, such as cavities, gum disease, and excessive wear. Additionally, when your jaw correctly aligns, you may experience fewer headaches and temporomandibular disorder (TMD) symptoms.

What kind of orthodontic solutions are there?

Austin Advanced Dentistry offers three main orthodontic treatments to support your oral health needs and fit into your daily life. The most common form of orthodontics is traditional braces that include a wire frame that surrounds the teeth, supported by wires and brackets. Traditional braces prove to be one of the most effective solutions to this day.

Austin Advanced Dentistry understands that the appearance of traditional braces may look unappealing to some. Adults, in particular, love clear alignment systems, like Invisalign , that don’t interfere with responsibilities and appear invisible to friends and colleagues alike.

The specialists can also provide a two-phase treatment to target orthodontic concerns early for children starting at age six. Two-phase treatment solves development issues by removing early signs of crowding. This is followed by traditional orthodontic treatment once all of the permanent teeth have grown in. These changes help growth during the teenage years.
