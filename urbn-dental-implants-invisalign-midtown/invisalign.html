Benefits of Invisalign Treatment

Invisalign treatment is not only an aesthetic choice but also a lifestyle-friendly decision.

Virtually Invisible

The clear aligners are discreet. It is a wonderful option for adults, students, and professionals looking to straighten their teeth without wearing braces.

Comfort and Convenience

No wires. No brackets. Invisalign aligners are made of smooth plastic to perfectly fit your teeth and won’t irritate your cheeks or gums like metal braces.

Removable Aligners

With these aligners, there are generally no restrictions on eating. You may take out your aligners when you eat, brush, floss, and drink, except for water.

Because the aligners are removable, brushing and flossing are more manageable. You are less at risk of plaque buildup, tooth decay, and even gum disease.

Invisalign Treatment in Houston: What to Expect

Step 1: Consultation

Your Invisalign experience begins with an initial consultation. Our dentist will start with a check-up, discuss your goals, and determine if you’re a good candidate.

Step 2: 3D Imaging & Smile Preview

Our dentist will capture a detailed 3D image of your teeth with digital scanners. After treatment, you’ll even see a preview of how your smile will look.

Step 3: Custom Aligner Creation

After considering your digital scan, Invisalign will develop a set of clear aligners for your treatment plan.

Step 4: Wear & Monitor

You must wear your aligners 20–22 hours a day and switch to a new set every 1–2 weeks, based on your condition. Our dentist will schedule regular check-ups to monitor your progress.

Step 5: Retainers Post-Treatment

After the final aligners, you’ll receive retainers to keep your teeth in place and protect your new smile.
