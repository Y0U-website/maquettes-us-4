What is the purpose of dental X-rays? Dental X-rays help detect issues that may not be visible during a routine exam, such as cavities between teeth, impacted teeth, infections, or bone loss. Digital X-rays are faster, use less radiation, and provide clearer results compared to traditional film.

Why are gum health and periodontal care important? Healthy gums provide essential support for teeth. If untreated, gum disease can lead to tooth loss, bone deterioration, and even affect overall health. Periodontal treatments like scaling, root planing, and tissue grafts help restore and protect gum health.

When is a root canal necessary? A root canal is needed when the pulp inside a tooth becomes infected due to deep decay, cracks, or trauma. The procedure removes the infection, relieves pain, and preserves the natural tooth instead of extracting it.

What are the advantages of dental implants? Dental implants replace missing teeth by anchoring artificial teeth into the jawbone. They provide stability, improve chewing ability, prevent bone loss, and look natural. Unlike dentures, implants are permanent and can last many years with proper care.

How does Invisalign work compared to braces? Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners to gradually move teeth into alignment. They are removable, comfortable, and nearly invisible, unlike traditional metal braces. Treatment time varies but often ranges between 12–18 months.
