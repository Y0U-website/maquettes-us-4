What Are Implant Dentures?

An implant denture combines the advantages of dental implants with traditional dentures to give patients the best looking, feeling, and functioning smile. Dental implants are small posts that are surgically placed in the jawbone to act like natural tooth roots. We can place an average of four dental implants into the jawbone, which we can then use to support a custom-made denture. The result is a prosthesis that is more fortified, comfortable, and natural-looking than traditional dentures.

During your initial consultation with a member of our team, we will discuss the two types of implant dentures you can choose from: fixed or removable.

Fixed/Permanent Implant Dentures

A fixed implant denture is also considered a hybrid prosthesis. Designed to remain permanently in place, it cannot be removed by anyone other than a skilled implant dentist in Arlington Heights. These dentures are held in place using between four and eight implant posts, which means you must have adequate jawbone density.

Removable Implant Dentures

A removable denture, also referred to as a Snap-On denture, requires between two and five implant posts. This means you do not need as much jawbone density because our team will identify the densest areas for placement. Because you can easily remove your denture at night to clean, it is held into place using a bar- or ball-retained device.

The Implant Denture Process

The process of receiving dental implants requires time and patience. Yes, it takes much longer to achieve the desired results, but the wait is worth it once you see your finalized smile. While all patients receiving implant dentures will have a different experience, it is necessary for everyone to begin with an initial consultation to determine if any preliminary treatments are needed before moving forward. These might include bone grafting or even periodontal therapy.

Once you are cleared for dental implant surgery, our team will administer local anesthesia before making a few small incisions in your gums. Exposing the bone, we will carefully place each implant post before closing the tissue and placing a protective healing cap over the surgical sites. Returning home to spend the next 3-6 months recovering, osseointegration will occur that allows the bone and implant to fuse. Once you are healed, you’ll return to have your metal abutments attached then finally receive your customized denture.

Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

The best way to know with certainty whether or not implant dentures near Rolling Meadows are right for you is to visit our dental office for a consultation. One of our experienced dentists will carefully examine your smile, the number of teeth you’re missing, the strength of your jawbone, and the condition of your oral health. Afterward, we will be able to develop a personalized plan with the proper treatment recommendation.

Patients who are candidates for implant dentures will typically be missing most or all of their teeth, but they should also commit to a good oral healthcare routine once they receive their replacement. Typically, candidates will also avoid heavy consumption of alcohol and the use of tobacco products.

Implant Dentures FAQs

Will People Be Able to Tell I Have Implant Dentures?

One of the unique benefits of implant dentures in Arlington Heights is their natural-looking appearance. Once put into place, they blend in with any existing teeth to create a cohesive and lifelike aesthetic. Even if these prosthetics are being used to replace both arches, the results will produce an eye-catching response in all the right ways. The fabrication of your implant dentures will be based on the specifications our team provides, so the shape, size, and color of your new teeth will mimic natural tooth enamel.

Is There Anything I Shouldn’t Eat with Implant Dentures?

Implant dentures allow you to consume highly nutritious foods that offer many great benefits to your overall health. There are virtually no restrictions when it comes to enjoying a delectable and lavish meal. Of course, while you are recovering, it’s best to stick to soft foods to allow your mouth to fully heal; however, once your new teeth are securely in place, you are free to expand your diet. You should be mindful, though, about eating hard and sticky foods, as these can damage your artificial and real teeth.

What Can Cause Implant Dentures to Fail?

The success rate for any kind of dental implant placement is around 95 %. After 10 years, this percentage remains the same. However, if you fail to take proper care of your teeth during this time, you put your smile at risk. Peri-implantitis is the most common reason for implant failure, as it affects the gums and bone surrounding your implants. Other potential causes include teeth grinding, smoking or tobacco use, facial trauma, and certain medications.
