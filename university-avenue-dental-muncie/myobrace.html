What to Expect During Treatment

Treatment options are tailored to the specific condition of each patient to ensure the most effective care.

Step 1: Schedule a consultation

Your journey begins with a visit to University Avenue Dental in Indiana, where Dr. Pyle will carefully determine whether Myobrace® is right for your child. During this stage, you’ll receive clear information, product brochures, and helpful content to feel fully aware of the treatment features.

Step 2: Receive your child’s device

If Myobrace® is a good fit, your child will be fitted with a comfortable, removable device. This simple appliance is worn for short daily sessions and while your child is asleep, supporting natural development without disrupting daily life or eating routines.

Step 3: Protect healthy habits

Throughout treatment, Dr. Pyle and our caring team provide additional information, coaching, and fun exercises to set your child up for success. We work with parents, kids, and even teens to build strong habits that improve long-term airway health, jaw function, and confident smiles.
