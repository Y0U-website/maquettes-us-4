FAQs About Invisalign®

Answers to common patient questions.

  • How long does Invisalign® treatment take? Treatment length varies, but many patients complete Invisalign® treatment in months, not years. The timeline depends on orthodontic issues, wear habits, and how teeth respond to treatment.
  • How often do I wear aligners? You wear aligners most of the day and night. Removing them only to eat, brush, and floss helps keep treatment on track and results predictable.
  • Do Invisalign® aligners affect speech? Some patients notice a short adjustment period. Most adapt quickly, and speech returns to normal within weeks.
  • Is Invisalign® painful? Invisalign® treatment feels gentle. You may feel mild pressure when starting a new set, which means the aligners are working to straighten teeth.
  • How do I care for my aligners? Clean aligners daily and maintain oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly. This supports oral health and keeps aligners clear and fresh.
