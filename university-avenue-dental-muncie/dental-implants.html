Placing Your Dental Implant

How does it work?

To begin the process, a small titanium post is inserted that replaces your missing tooth root. This is the implant that fuses with the jawbone over time and provides support for the new tooth.

Once your implant and jawbone fuse and heal, our Muncie restorative dentist attaches a connector onto the base. This connection (aka abutment) allows us to securely attach your new restoration.

The custom-crafted tooth replacement is created using precise mouth impressions and advanced technology so that your smile looks and feels natural. The most common are crowns, bridges, and implanted-supported dentures.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are dental implants, and what do they replace? A dental implant replaces a missing tooth root with a small titanium post that integrates with the jawbone. It supports a natural-looking restoration so you can chew, speak, and smile with more confidence.
  • What are the main steps of getting a dental implant? Implant treatment usually includes placing the titanium post, allowing it to heal and fuse, attaching an abutment, and placing the final restoration. Your timeline depends on healing and the specifics of your case.
  • What restorations can go on top of an implant? Common implant restorations include a single implant crown, an implant-supported bridge, or implant-supported dentures. The best option depends on how many teeth you’re replacing and your bite and bone support.
  • Can implants replace one tooth or multiple teeth? Yes. Implants can replace a single missing tooth or serve as stable support for multiple missing teeth. Your dentist will design a plan that matches your smile, function, and long-term stability needs.
  • How do I know if I’m a candidate for dental implants in Muncie? Start with a consultation. Your dentist will evaluate your oral health, jawbone support, and goals, then explain implant options and whether implants are the best solution for your situation.
