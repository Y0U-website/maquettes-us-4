Dentist Dr. Aliza Dashiff

Meet Dr. Dashiff

Dr. Dashiff joined Miami Beach Dental Solutions from NYC. She brings years of extensive experience in diverse patient care of cosmetic, restorative, prosthetic, and emergency treatment. Dr. Dashiff is committed to improving and enhancing oral health by focusing on preventive measures and minimally invasive procedures.

Dr. Dashiff earned her DMD from the Rutgers School of Dental Medicine and continued her training by completing a residency at Montefiore Medical Center in NYC. In addition to her extensive clinical experience, Dr. Dashiff is also a well-published author at the forefront of dental science, clinical innovations, research, and emerging trends in oral health.

Dr. Dashiff is known by her patients to provide exceptional care, enhance long-term improvements in oral health and facial aesthetics, and create a compassionate and well- informed environment.
