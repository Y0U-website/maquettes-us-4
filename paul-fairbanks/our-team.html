0 Our Team Paul Fairbanks Dr. Paul Fairbanks has been the owner of Prineville Dental since early 2017, taking over for Dr. Bill McClure who started the practice and served the people of Prineville since 1978.

Prior to working here, Dr. Fairbanks worked in Bend Oregon, but enjoyed the smaller, more tight knit community of Prineville. Dr. Fairbanks has a constant drive to always bring the best care possible to his patients; delivering comprehensive dental solutions to the needs of all of crook county. He spends many of his weekends taking hours of continuing education courses to bring our community the latest in dental advancements.

When not in the office, Dr. Fairbanks spends his time with his wife and children, writing and playing music, swimming, biking, and hiking all over Central Oregon. He is very proud to serve this community.
