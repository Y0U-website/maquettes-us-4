MEET OUR DENTIST AT GENTLE FAMILY DENTAL CARE

DR. BRETT T. SKINNER DOS

Dr. Skinner grew up in Cardson Alberta Canada. He moved to Cache Valley to get his bachelors in science degree in biology. While at Utah State University he fell in love with all that Logan has to offer. He then attended Virginia Commonwealth University’s School of Dentistry.

He and his family returned to Logan, Utah where he has spent the past several years building his own dental practice and deepening ties to the community.

Dr. Skinner prides himself on building a relationship of trust with his patients and making every effort to make his patients feel as comfortable as possible from the first time they sit in the chair.

Dr. Skinner is consistently attending various continuing educating courses with topics ranging from restorative and cosmetic dentistry to full mouth reconstruction and implant dentistry. Dr. Skinner’s primary goal is to offer the highest level of patient care with the latest equipment so that you can maintain the health of your teeth and gums and the beauty of your smile.

Dr. Skinner has many interests outside of dentistry. His favorite is spending time with his family.

DR. CADEN SKINNER DMD

Dr. Caden Skinner is a proud Cache Valley native dedicated to providing high-quality, patient-centered dental care. He earned his bachelor’s degree in Human Biology from Utah State University, along with minors in Chemistry and Spanish.

Dr. Skinner went on to receive his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from Midwestern University in Glendale, Arizona, where he developed a strong clinical foundation and a passion for comprehensive dentistry.

He and his wife—his high school sweetheart—are thrilled to be back in Cache Valley, raising their two children and serving the community they love.

Dr. Skinner is committed to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through a personalized and compassionate approach. He regularly pursues continuing education to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry and provide the highest standard of care.

Outside the office, Dr. Skinner enjoys skiing, biking, golfing, and spending quality time with his family.
