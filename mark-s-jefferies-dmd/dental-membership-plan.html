Terms & Conditions

Our plan is not insurance or qualified under the Affordable Care Act. Once signed up, you are committed for the entire year. You may cancel at any time but will not be refunded. Payment for dental treatment(s) is/are not included in the plan and must be paid at the time of service. Routine X-rays do not include Pano/FMX/CT imaging (3-5 years). Membership does not include products or whitening services, and does not cover procedures completed by specialists outside our practice. Membership plan cannot be combined with insurance, discounts or other offers.

Membership FAQs

  • What forms of payment are accepted? We accept cash, check, and credit cards for payment.
  • When can I begin using my dental membership plan? You can begin using it right away. The plan takes effect immediately after your first annual payment is made.
  • Can I add members to my account? No, each patient requires their own dental membership plan.
  • Can I use the dental membership plan even if I have insurance? You cannot use the dental membership plan in combination with insurance, but in some cases it may be cheaper than insurance, so you can use it congruently if you wish.
  • Can I transfer my dental memership plan to another dentist? No, the Mark S. Jefferies, DMD, PLC Family and Cosmetic Dentistry Dental Savings Plan is only good at this practice.
