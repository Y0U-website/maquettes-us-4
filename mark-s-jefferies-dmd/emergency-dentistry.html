Am I Experiencing a Dental Emergency?

It can be difficult to determine what constitutes as a dental emergency. If you are unsure if you are experiencing a dental emergency, please contact our dental office. Our team can determine if you need to visit our Herndon, VA dentist office for an emergency appointment. We offer convenient office hours so that we can get you to our dental office as soon as possible.

To determine if you are experiencing a dental emergency that should be addressed promptly, consider this list of common dental emergencies:

  • Trauma to the teeth, gums, jaw, or lips
  • A cracked, broken, or chipped tooth
  • Dental infection
  • Dental abscess
  • A knocked-out tooth
  • Broken or loose restorations
  • Severe tooth sensitivity

FAQ

  • Can I prevent a dental emergency? A good way to reduce your risk of a dental emergency is to schedule biannual visits to our office. Routine dental visits allow our team to spot any early signs of dental issues like tooth decay, gum disease, and tooth grinding. With early intervention, we can build a treatment plan that is right for you and possibly reduce your risk of experiencing a dental emergency.
  • Do you need to be a patient of the practice to be seen for a dental emergency? No, you do not need to be a patient of Mark S. Jefferies, DMD, PLC to be treated for a dental emergency. We accept walk-ins and will do our best to get you treated the same day and as soon as possible.
  • What should I do if I knock a tooth out? If you knock out a tooth, you should gently rinse it, avoid touching the root, place it in milk or saliva if possible, and seek emergency dental care immediately to increase the chance of saving it.
  • How do I know if I need emergency dental care right away? You should seek emergency dental care if you’re experiencing severe tooth pain, swelling, bleeding, a cracked or knocked-out tooth, or signs of infection. These issues can worsen quickly without treatment and should be evaluated as soon as possible.
  • Do I need to be an existing patient to receive emergency dental care? No. Dr. Mark Jefferies welcomes both new and existing patients for emergency dental visits. If you’re in pain or dealing with a dental emergency, the office will work to get you seen promptly.

Related Services

Tooth Extractions

A tooth extraction is the removal of a tooth from its socket in the jawbone. This is usually recommended when a tooth is damaged beyond repair due to decay, infection, trauma or crowding.

Dental Crowns and Bridges

If you have weak, damaged or broken teeth, a dental crown is used to repair the tooth and restore optimal function. In turn, a dental bridge closes the gap left behind by one or more missing teeth.
