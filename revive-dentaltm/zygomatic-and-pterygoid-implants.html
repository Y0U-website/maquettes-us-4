Who Can Benefit from These Implants

Zygomatic and pterygoid implants are often recommended for patients who:

These solutions open the door to advanced care for patients who thought they had no path forward.

  • Have severe bone loss in the upper jaw
  • Have worn dentures for many years and lost significant bone volume
  • Want to avoid or are not ideal candidates for bone grafting or sinus lifts
  • Need full-arch restoration but have been told implants are not possible

Step-by-Step: How Treatment Works

Comprehensive Consultation

We begin with a detailed exam and 3D imaging to evaluate bone structure and determine whether zygomatic or pterygoid implants are the best option.

Custom Treatment Plan

Dr. Briskie designs a plan tailored to your needs, often combining these specialized implants with traditional implants for maximum stability.

Precise Implant Placement

Using computer-guided technology, implants are placed strategically into the cheekbone or pterygoid bone.

Temporary Teeth

In many cases, patients leave with a set of provisional teeth on the same day as surgery.

Healing and Final Restoration

Once integration is complete, a permanent full-arch prosthetic is attached, giving you a strong and natural-looking smile.

Advanced Technology for Precision and Safety

Zygomatic and pterygoid implants require advanced skill and planning. At Revive Dental, we use cone beam CT scans, digital planning software, and guided surgical techniques to ensure accuracy. These tools allow us to visualize the anatomy in detail, position implants safely, and achieve predictable long-term results. High-quality restorative materials are used for your final prosthetic, ensuring durability and a natural look.

Common Questions About Zygomatic and Pterygoid Implants

  • Are these implants safe? Yes. With advanced imaging and precise planning, zygomatic and pterygoid implants are safe, predictable, and highly successful solutions for patients with severe bone loss.
  • Do I still need bone grafting with these implants? In most cases, no. These treatments are specifically designed to avoid the need for grafting or sinus lift procedures.
  • How long do they last? When properly cared for, zygomatic and pterygoid implants are designed to last decades and often for a lifetime.
  • Is recovery more difficult than with regular implants? Recovery is similar. Some patients may experience mild soreness, but with modern surgical techniques and proper aftercare, healing is usually smooth and manageable.
