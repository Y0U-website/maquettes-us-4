All-on-6 Dental Implants

All-on-6 is about more than replacing teeth — it’s about restoring the freedom to live comfortably and fully.

  • Improved Chewing Power – Eat a wider variety of foods without worry.
  • Enhanced Bone Preservation – Stimulates more of the jawbone to prevent bone loss.
  • Natural Appearance – Custom restorations are designed to look and feel like real teeth.
  • Long-Term Reliability – Added support reduces stress on each implant, promoting durability.
  • Same-Day Options – In many cases, patients can leave with temporary teeth the same day implants are placed.

Step-by-Step: The All-on-6 Process

Consultation and 3D Imaging

We start with a thorough exam and cone beam CT scan to evaluate bone health and plan implant placement.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Dr. Briskie designs a plan based on your anatomy and goals, explaining each step clearly.

Implant Placement

Six titanium implants are placed strategically in the jawbone for maximum stability.

Temporary Teeth

In many cases, you’ll leave with a provisional set of teeth immediately after surgery.

Healing and Integration

Over the next several months, implants fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration.

Final Restoration

A custom full-arch prosthetic is attached, completing your smile with strength and beauty.

This carefully planned process ensures predictable, lasting results.

Common Questions About All-on-6

  • How is All-on-6 different from All-on-4? All-on-6 uses two additional implants per arch, offering greater stability and improved bone stimulation for long-term oral health.
  • Is All-on-6 right for me? If you are missing most or all teeth and have adequate bone density, All-on-6 may be an excellent option. We’ll evaluate it with 3D imaging to confirm.
  • Will I be without teeth during treatment? No. Most patients receive temporary teeth on the same day implants are placed.
  • How long will All-on-6 implants last? With proper care, implants can last decades — often a lifetime.
