Educator and Mentor

In addition to treating patients at Revive Dental, Dr. Briskie is deeply involved in dental education. He is the owner of the Colorado Surgical Institute, where he trains dentists from across the country in full-arch implant surgery, prosthetics, and digital dentistry. His institute has become a hub for advanced learning, equipping general dentists with the knowledge and skills to expand their practices and serve their own patients more effectively.

He also works as an instructor for the leading implant company in the United States, helping shape the future of implant dentistry by mentoring colleagues in the latest techniques. Through this teaching role, Dr. Briskie is influencing dentistry well beyond his own practice, ensuring patients everywhere benefit from improved care.
