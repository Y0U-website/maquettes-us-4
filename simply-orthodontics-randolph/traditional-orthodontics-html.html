Traditional Braces FAQs

Do Traditional Braces Hurt?

While every orthodontic treatment comes with some soreness, patients are often surprised by how little discomfort comes with modern braces. Immediately following the placement of the brackets and wires, you might feel some soreness as your teeth and gums adjust. Fortunately, this sensation can be minimized with a cold compress and an over-the-counter pain reliever. Plus, if your braces are ever irritating your gums, lips or tongue, you can use a piece of dental wax to immediately alleviate the discomfort!

What Can You Not Eat with Traditional Braces?

The dietary restrictions that come with braces serve an important purpose: to help prevent an orthodontic emergency. As a result, you will be asked to refrain from eating hard, sticky and chewy foods, such as popcorn, nuts, potato chips and whole apples. Instead, you should fill your plate with soft foods that are tooth-friendly too, including yogurt, oatmeal, soft fruit and cooked vegetables.

Can You Drink Coffee with Traditional Braces?

Technically, yes! However, while braces won’t stop you from enjoying a cup of coffee, this dark beverage can stain your teeth. Since the brackets are covering a portion of them, this can result in uneven discoloration when it comes time to remove your braces. That’s why we recommend using a straw when you do indulge, and sipping water simultaneously.

Can I Get Traditional Braces on Just My Top or Bottom Teeth?

Single-arch treatment is possible. However, it’s more rare than you might think. After all, moving one row of teeth and not the other can lead to several issues, including trouble chewing properly. As a result, it’s usually only recommended in cases of minor misalignment, like a single gap between the two front teeth.

Am I Too Old to Get Braces?

Oftentimes, patients assume that braces are only for kids. In reality, this orthodontic treatment can help patients of all ages, including those in their 50s, 60s, and 70s. So, if you are interested in adult orthodontics , you shouldn’t let your age stop you from scheduling a consultation with us! During this visit, we will review your dental history, assess the severity of your misalignment, and determine if you’re a good candidate for traditional braces.

Can I Chew Gum with Traditional Braces?

Chewing gum with traditional braces is not recommended. The reason for this is simple: the sticky consistency can cause a bracket or wire to come loose, potentially derailing your treatment in the process. So, if you want to freshen your breath, it’s best to brush your teeth or, if that’s inconvenient, opt for a sugar-free mint.

How Do I Know If My Child Needs Braces?

If your child is struggling to chew properly, enunciate clearly, or even smile confidently due to the alignment of their teeth, you shouldn’t hesitate to bring them to our New England dental office. During this visit, we can learn about your concerns, take a look at the alignment of their teeth and bite, and determine if they would benefit from undergoing orthodontic treatment .

Can Traditional Braces Fix Overbites?

Traditional braces can fix several common bite problems, including overbites. The best way to find out if you’re a candidate is by scheduling a consultation with one of our talented orthodontists in New England. From there, they can complete a comprehensive oral exam and determine if traditional braces are the ideal solution. If the answer is yes, then they will get to work creating your custom treatment plan!

How Long Do Traditional Braces Take?

Usually, traditional braces take between one and three years to achieve the desired results. The length of your treatment plan will depend on a few factors, including the severity of your case and how well you follow the guidelines we provide. The good news is that we can provide you with an estimate of your timeline during your consultation. We will also share what you can do to avoid delays, like prioritizing your monthly adjustment appointments.

Can You Whiten Your Teeth While Wearing Braces?

Typically, it’s not recommended because a portion of the surface of your teeth is covered with brackets. The good news is that there are several steps you can take to help prevent stubborn stains from developing, starting with using a straw when you drink coffee. Limiting your intake of dark-colored sauces, like BBQ sauce, mustard, and marinara sauce, is important as well.
