How Long Does Pediatric Orthodontics Take?

Pediatric orthodontics typically lasts between 9 and 12 months or longer on average, though the exact timeline depends on your child s specific needs and how much guidance their teeth require. Following treatment, your child might also wear a retainer to hold the improvements in place while their remaining permanent teeth continue to come in. It s worth noting that pediatric orthodontic treatment is not meant to be a complete solution, but rather a small, focused intervention that makes any future treatment shorter, simpler, and more effective.
