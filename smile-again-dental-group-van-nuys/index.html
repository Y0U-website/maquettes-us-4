The practice

We, Smile Again Dental Group, offer comprehensive dental services using state-of-the-art technology to provide high-quality service and customer satisfaction. We have been providing top-quality dental and oral care to the people of Los Angeles and San Fernando Valley.

Dental implants are made from surgical-grade titanium and, in most cases, offer a permanent solution that is one of the best for one or more missing teeth. Our Los Angeles Dentists Group helps patients with the best knowledge, techniques, and recovery methods.

Dr. Nathan Lee and Dr. Christopher Chung hold Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degrees from the prestigious Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in New Jersey. Our Los Angeles Dentists Group engages in continuing education to stay current on the latest advances in modern dental care.

Smile Again utilizes 3D CBCT to approach complicated treatments safely. We also utilize an iTero scanner for our Invisalign orthodontic treatments and restorative cases.

Our doctors at Smile Again perform guided surgery for the most accurate and safe result.
