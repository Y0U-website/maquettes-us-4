Our Mission

Little smiles are our specialty

Tulsa Children’s Dentistry is the ideal place to establish a positive relationship between your child and dentistry. Our team loves working with children and genuinely care for the families they serve. We use innovative techniques and modern technology to ensure every child has a comfortable dental experience.

Your child will feel right at home at our office. With fun wall art, video games, and other kid-friendly entertainment, your child is sure to leave our office with a smile on their face. Call or stop by today to start your child’s best dental experience at Tulsa Children’s Dentistry.

Dr. Lindsay Flynn

Dr. Lindsay Flynn graduated from the university of Oklahoma college of dentistry. She became a dentist in order to exercise her love for people, science, and working with her hands. Before attending dental school, she was an environmental science researcher and spent time studying the great smoky mountains of North Carolina.

Dr. Flynn loves to try new things and explore new places. In her spare time, she enjoys yoga, cooking, and being outdoors.

Dr. Matthew Enns

Dr. Matthew Enns graduated from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry and has dedicated his career to the field of dentistry with a special focus on pediatric care. As a member of the American Dental Association, Oklahoma Dental Association and American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, Dr. Enns is committed to providing exceptional care and promoting oral health education. He is also passionate about local and international community service, reflecting his commitment to giving back.

Outside the office, Dr. Matt enjoys exploring Tulsa and beyond with his wife, Brooke, and their three daughters, cherishing the time spent on family adventures.
