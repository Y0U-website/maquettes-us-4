About Dr. Ung

Dr. Lady Ung

Dr. Lady Ung started her holistic journey at Georgetown University with a masters degree in complementary and alternative medicine. She went on to further pursue a degree in Dentistry at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry. Graduating in 2013, she emerged not only as a skilled dentist but as an advocate for the harmonious integration of oral health with overall wellness.

Drs Adams and Ung have partnered since 2020. They both share a love for holistic and biological dentistry. Using ozone, PRF, non-metal implants and restorations and safe amalgam removal protocols by the IAOMT.org are commonalities of both Drs practice.

For over 10 years, Dr. Ung has journeyed with patients, embracing the nuances of their unique oral health needs. Beyond dental procedures, she has woven a tapestry of care that considers the interconnectedness of mind, body, and spirit. Each interaction is an opportunity to cultivate not only a healthy smile but also a sense of overall well-being.

Dr. Ung’s expertise spans a wide range of dental services, including but not limited to preventive care, cosmetic dentistry, restorative procedures, oral surgery and IV Sedation. She has advanced training in Implant dentistry and is IAOMT certified as a SMART dentist. She takes pride in staying at the forefront of the latest dental techniques and technologies, ensuring that patients receive the most advanced and effective treatments available.

Dr. Ung’’s practice embodies a holistic approach to dentistry, offering a spectrum of services that extend beyond conventional treatments. From preventive care rooted in patient education to the artistry of cosmetic dentistry and the restorative touch of oral surgery, every service is delivered with a mindful awareness of its impact on the whole person.

At the heart of Dr. Ung’’s practice is the belief that patients are partners in their own wellness journey. Through empathetic communication and a genuine understanding of individual needs, she co-create treatment plans that resonate with the patient’s values and aspirations, fostering a sense of trust and collaboration.

Dr. Ung invites you to experience dentistry as a holistic journey toward well-being. Schedule an appointment and discover a compassionate approach that nurtures not only your smile but the harmony of your entire being. Your path to a healthier, more vibrant life begins here.
