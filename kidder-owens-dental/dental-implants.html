What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Over time, they integrate with the bone through a process called osseointegration, creating a secure foundation for a dental crown, bridge, or implant-supported denture.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Long-Lasting Solution – With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Natural Look & Feel – Designed to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Improved Oral Health – Prevents bone loss and maintains facial structure.
  • No More Slipping or Discomfort – Unlike dentures, implants stay securely in place.
  • Restored Chewing Ability – Enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.

Our Dental Implant Services

At Kidder Owens Dental, we offer customized implant solutions tailored to your needs.

Single Tooth Implants

A single dental implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting neighbouring teeth. A custom crown is placed on top for a seamless, natural appearance.

Implant-Supported Bridges

For patients missing multiple teeth in a row, an implant-supported bridge provides a secure alternative to traditional bridges without relying on adjacent teeth for support.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

An innovative solution for full-arch tooth replacement, the All-on-4 technique uses just four strategically placed implants to support an entire upper or lower denture, offering stability, comfort, and a natural look.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Unlike traditional removable dentures, implant-supported dentures snap onto implants for a more secure and comfortable fit—no adhesives needed.

Full Mouth Reconstruction

For patients with extensive tooth loss or damage, a full mouth reconstruction combines implants, crowns, and other restorative treatments to completely rebuild their smile.

Bone Grafting for Implants

If you have insufficient jawbone density, a bone graft can strengthen the area to ensure successful implant placement.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults are good candidates for dental implants, but a consultation is necessary to determine if this is the right option for you.

If you have gum disease, severe bone loss, or certain health conditions, Dr. Kidder may recommend pre-treatment options to improve your candidacy.

  • You have one or more missing teeth
  • You have adequate jawbone density (or are willing to undergo bone grafting)
  • You maintain good oral hygiene and overall health
  • You’re tired of traditional dentures or bridges

What to Expect: The Dental Implant Process

1. Personalized Consultation

Your journey begins with a comprehensive exam, including 3D imaging to assess your jawbone and plan the best treatment.

2. Implant Placement

A titanium implant post is surgically placed in your jawbone. Over three to six months, the implant integrates with the bone for a solid foundation.

3. Abutment Placement

Once healed, an abutment (connector piece) is attached to the implant to support the final restoration.

4. Custom Crown, Bridge, or Denture Placement

A custom-designed tooth replacement is securely attached, restoring your smile’s function and aesthetics.

5. Enjoy Your New Smile

With proper care, your implants can last a lifetime—just brush, floss, and schedule regular checkups.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental visits and good oral hygiene are essential for long-term success.

How much do dental implants cost? The cost varies depending on the number of implants and additional procedures like bone grafting. We offer financing options to make treatment more affordable.

What is the recovery time for dental implants? Most patients resume normal activities within a few days. However, full healing and implant integration take three to six months.

Can smokers get dental implants? Smoking can affect healing and implant success rates. If you smoke, we will discuss strategies to improve your chances of a successful outcome.
