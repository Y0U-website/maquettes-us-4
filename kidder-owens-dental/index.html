The practice

Our advanced dental treatments are designed to enhance your oral health and transform your smile with precision and care.

Porcelain Veneers are custom-designed ultra-thin shells bonded to the front of your teeth, instantly transforming your smile into a brighter, more symmetrical appearance.

Over time, teeth can become stained from coffee, wine, smoking, and aging. Our teeth whitening treatments provide safe, effective, and long-lasting results, lightening your teeth by several shades in just one visit.

Services

We listen first. Whether you’re here for preventive care or a full-mouth transformation, every visit is tailored to your goals and comfort level. ‍

If you have gum disease, severe bone loss, or certain health conditions, Dr. Kidder may recommend pre-treatment options to improve your candidacy.

Implant-supported dentures are custom full or partial dentures attached to dental implants, providing a more secure fit than traditional removable dentures. The implants fuse with the jawbone, ensuring stability and preventing bone loss.

Crooked teeth can affect both appearance and function. Our Invisalign treatment provides a discreet and comfortable way to straighten teeth using clear, removable aligners.

Botox is a non-surgical treatment that smooths wrinkles and fine lines around the mouth, forehead, and eyes, enhancing your smile and facial appearance.
