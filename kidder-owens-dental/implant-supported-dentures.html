What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are custom full or partial dentures attached to dental implants, providing a more secure fit than traditional removable dentures. The implants fuse with the jawbone, ensuring stability and preventing bone loss.

Our team will evaluate your oral health to determine the best option for you. If additional bone support is needed, we also offer bone grafting to strengthen the jawbone before implant placement.

  • Fixed implant-supported dentures, which remain in place permanently
  • Removable implant-supported dentures, which snap onto implants but can be removed for cleaning

The Implant-Supported Denture Procedure

1. Consultation and Treatment Planning

Your journey begins with a detailed exam, including digital imaging, to evaluate your jawbone and overall oral health.

2. Implant Placement

Titanium implants are surgically placed in the jawbone to serve as anchors for the dentures. Depending on your needs, two to six implants per arch may be used.

3. Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next three to six months, the implants integrate with the jawbone, ensuring a strong foundation. For patients needing additional healing support, we may recommend PRP therapy to accelerate recovery.

4. Custom Denture Attachment

Once healing is complete, a custom-designed denture is securely attached to the implants, restoring function and aesthetics.

5. Enjoy Your New Smile

With implant-supported dentures, you can eat, speak, and smile with confidence. Routine cleanings and exams will help keep your implants and dentures in excellent condition.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do implant-supported dentures last? With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime, while the denture portion may need replacement after ten to fifteen years. Regular cleanings and exams help maintain their longevity.

Are implant-supported dentures better than traditional dentures? Many patients find implant-supported dentures more comfortable and functional than digital dentures since they do not slip or require adhesives.

Is the procedure painful? The implant placement is performed under anesthesia, and sedation dentistry options are available for a stress-free experience.

Can I remove my implant-supported dentures? If you choose removable implant dentures, they can be taken out for cleaning. Fixed implant dentures remain permanently in place.
