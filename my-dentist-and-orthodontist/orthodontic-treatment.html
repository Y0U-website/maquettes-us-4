Is Orthodontic Treatment Right for You?

Many people assume orthodontic treatment is only for children or teens, but that is far from the truth. Adults make up a growing share of orthodontic patients, often seeking treatment to correct issues that were never addressed earlier in life or to fix shifting that has occurred over time. If you have crowded, gapped, or slightly misaligned front teeth and want a faster, more discreet option, treatments like Six Month Smiles may be worth discussing with our team. A consultation is the best way to determine which orthodontic treatment approach fits your goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does orthodontic treatment typically take?

It depends on the treatment and the complexity of your case. Six Month Smiles is designed to be completed in about six months, while more comprehensive orthodontic treatment can take longer.

What should I do if I have an orthodontic emergency?

Contact our office right away. Do not try to fix broken brackets or wires yourself, as this can cause further irritation or damage.

Is Six Month Smiles noticeable?

Six Month Smiles uses clear brackets and tooth-colored wires, making it significantly less noticeable than traditional metal braces.

Do I need a referral to start orthodontic treatment?

No referral is needed. You can schedule a consultation directly with our office to discuss your orthodontic treatment options.
