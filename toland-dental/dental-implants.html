Why Choose Dental Implants for Missing Teeth?

Unlike removable dentures or traditional bridges that rely on adjacent teeth for support, dental implants replace the entire tooth structure, including the root. This approach preserves your healthy teeth while providing the stability and strength needed for comfortable chewing and confident speaking.

Understanding Dental Implant Treatment

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants consist of three essential components working together to replace your missing teeth:

The implant post integrates with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration, creating a stable foundation that can last decades with proper care. This biological bonding process sets implants apart from other tooth replacement options, providing unmatched stability and preserving your facial structure.

  • Titanium implant post : Acts as an artificial tooth root, surgically placed in your jawbone
  • Abutment : Connects the implant to your restoration
  • Crown, bridge, or denture : The visible tooth replacement that matches your natural teeth

Types of Implant Restorations We Provide

Single Tooth Implant Restorations

For replacing individual missing teeth, single implant restorations offer the most conservative and effective solution. Rather than preparing adjacent healthy teeth for a bridge, a single implant preserves your natural tooth structure while providing permanent replacement.

Benefits of Single Tooth Implants:

  • Preserves adjacent healthy teeth
  • Maintains natural bone structure
  • Easy to clean and maintain
  • Looks and feels like your natural tooth
  • Can last a lifetime with proper care

Implant- Supported Bridges

When you’re missing multiple adjacent teeth, implant-supported bridges provide an excellent alternative to traditional bridges or partial dentures. Typically requiring two implants to support three or four replacement teeth, this solution offers superior stability without compromising healthy teeth.

Advantages Over Traditional Bridges:

  • No preparation of healthy adjacent teeth required
  • Superior long-term stability and function
  • Easier maintenance than removable appliances
  • Preserves jawbone in the area of missing teeth
  • More cost-effective than individual implants for each missing tooth

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing most or all teeth, implant-supported dentures represent a life-changing improvement over conventional dentures. Using a minimum of four implants per arch, these restorations eliminate the slipping, clicking, and dietary restrictions associated with traditional dentures.

Life-Changing Benefits:

Many dental insurance plans provide partial coverage for implant restorations. Our team assists with pre-authorization and maximizing your benefits. We also offer flexible payment options to make implant treatment accessible.

  • Eat with confidence : Chew your favorite foods without worry
  • Speak clearly : No slipping or whistling sounds
  • Secure fit : No adhesives or daily removal required
  • Preserve facial structure : Prevent the sunken appearance from bone loss
  • Improved comfort : No sore spots or gum irritation

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implant

With proper care and regular maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. The crown or bridge attached to the implant may need replacement after 10-15 years due to normal wear, but the implant itself typically remains stable indefinitely.

Good candidates have adequate bone density, healthy gums, and realistic expectations about treatment. Factors like smoking, uncontrolled diabetes, and certain medications can affect success rates. Dr. Toland provides honest evaluation of your candidacy during consultation

Results vary by treatment type and individual care habits. Professional whitening typically lasts 2-3 years with proper maintenance, while porcelain veneers can last 10-15 years or longer with excellent care. Dental bonding usually requires touch-ups every 3-5 years. Dr. Toland provides specific longevity expectations based on your chosen treatments and lifestyle factors.

Implant costs vary based on the number of implants needed and the type of restoration. During your consultation, we provide detailed cost estimates and discuss insurance coverage and payment options. While implants require a higher initial investment, they often prove more economical long-term than alternatives requiring frequent replacement.

While implant failure is rare (success rates exceed 95 %), it can occur due to infection, inadequate bone integration, or other factors. Dr. Toland stands behind his surgical work and will address any complications promptly. Most failed implants can be replaced after appropriate healing time.
