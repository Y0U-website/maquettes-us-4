Meet Dr. Laif Toland – Your Trusted Dentist in Melbourne, FL

Hi, I’m Dr. Laif Toland. I grew up here in Melbourne, FL, and graduated from Holy Trinity Episcopal Academy. I attended the University of Central Florida, where I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Biology, and then completed my dental degree at the University of Kentucky in 2018. After gaining experience as an associate dentist in New Smyrna Beach, FL, I returned to my hometown in 2020, where I’m thrilled to serve the Melbourne community.

Quality Family Dental Care for All Ages at Toland Dental At Toland Dental, I see patients of all ages, from children to adults. Whether you need routine dental care, cosmetic dentistry, or sleep dentistry, I am committed to offering quality, personalized service for the entire family. In my free time, I enjoy surfing, golfing, weightlifting, and traveling, all of which keep me energized and inspired to bring my best to my patients.
