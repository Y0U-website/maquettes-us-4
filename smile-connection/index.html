The practice

At Smile Connection Ontario, we are proud to offer dental implants as a premier solution for replacing missing or damaged teeth. Dental implants provide a permanent, durable, and aesthetically pleasing way to restore your smile and improve your oral health. Our experienced team is dedicated to delivering implants that not only enhance your appearance but also restore the function of your teeth…

Dental implants are titanium posts that are surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as a stable foundation for replacement teeth. Once the implant is securely in place, a custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is attached to the post, completing your smile. Unlike traditional dentures or bridges, dental implants mimic the look, feel, and function of natural teeth, making them an ideal option…

Most individuals who are missing one or more teeth and have healthy gums and sufficient bone density are candidates for dental implants. If you have lost bone in your jaw, bone grafting procedures may be necessary to create a stable foundation for the implants.

During your consultation, our dentist will assess your oral health, take X-rays or 3D images, and discuss your treatment goals. This comprehensive evaluation helps us create a personalized treatment plan that meets your specific needs.

The dental implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation for your new tooth.

Once the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, an abutment is attached to the post. The abutment serves as a connector between the implant and the replacement tooth.

We provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure your implant heals properly. Regular checkups are also essential to monitor the health of your implant and surrounding tissues.

Maintaining good oral hygiene is crucial to the longevity of your dental implants. Brush and floss daily, and visit our office regularly for professional cleanings and checkups. With proper care, your dental implants can last a lifetime, providing you with a healthy, functional, and beautiful smile.
