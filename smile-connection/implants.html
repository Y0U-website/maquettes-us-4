Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most individuals who are missing one or more teeth and have healthy gums and sufficient bone density are candidates for dental implants. If you have lost bone in your jaw, bone grafting procedures may be necessary to create a stable foundation for the implants.

Our Dental Implant Procedure

1. Initial Consultation and Planning

During your consultation, our dentist will assess your oral health, take X-rays or 3D images, and discuss your treatment goals. This comprehensive evaluation helps us create a personalized treatment plan that meets your specific needs.

2. Implant Placement

The dental implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, the implant will fuse with the bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation for your new tooth.

3. Abutment Placement

Once the implant has fully integrated with the jawbone, an abutment is attached to the post. The abutment serves as a connector between the implant and the replacement tooth.

4. Crown, Bridge, or Denture Placement

A custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is then attached to the abutment. This final restoration is designed to match the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, completing your smile.

5. Follow-Up and Aftercare

We provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure your implant heals properly. Regular checkups are also essential to monitor the health of your implant and surrounding tissues.

FAQs About Dental Implants

Q: How long do dental implants last? A: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular dental checkups and good oral hygiene are essential for maintaining their longevity.

Q: Is the dental implant procedure painful? A: Most patients report minimal discomfort during the implant procedure. Local anesthesia and sedation options are available to ensure your comfort.

Q: How do I care for my dental implants? A: Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Brush and floss daily, and visit your dentist regularly for cleanings and checkups.
