What to Expect During the Dental Implant Procedure

The process is designed to be comfortable and predictable, with clear steps from start to finish. At Sepe Dental, Dr. Christopher Sepe focuses on thorough planning and follow-up so you understand every stage of care.

Consultation and Evaluation

Your visit includes a detailed exam and necessary imaging to confirm candidacy and plan your treatment.

Implant Placement

The implant post is placed under local anesthesia for comfort. Most patients describe pressure, not pain, during the appointment.

Healing Period

Over several weeks, the implant integrates with your bone to form a stable anchor.

Abutment and Impression

An attachment piece is secured, and precise impressions are taken for your final restoration.

Custom Crown or Restoration

A natural-looking crown, bridge, or denture is crafted to match your smile and securely connected to the implant.

Care and Maintenance

Routine checkups and home care help protect your investment and support long-term success.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer advantages that make them a leading choice for replacing missing teeth. With careful planning and ongoing care, they are known for long-term reliability and everyday comfort.

Many patients searching for Delray Beach dental implants also appreciate the everyday convenience and confidence that come with a fixed, low-maintenance solution.

  • ✓ Stable function: Implants do not slip, so you can enjoy a wider range of foods and clearer speech.
  • ✓ Jawbone support: Implants help stimulate and preserve bone, supporting facial shape over time.
  • ✓ No impact on neighboring teeth: Adjacent teeth are not reduced for support.
  • ✓ Proven longevity: With good hygiene and regular visits, implants can last many years.

Pros and Cons of Dental Implants

Pros

  • Natural look, feel, and function for comfortable eating and speaking.
  • Long-term solution that can last for many years with proper care.
  • Preserves bone and supports facial structure over time.
  • Protects neighboring teeth by avoiding the need to reshape them.
  • High patient satisfaction as a stable alternative to removable options.

Cons

  • Multiple steps and healing time are required before final restoration.
  • Surgical placement may not be ideal for certain medical conditions.
  • Upfront investment can be higher than other options, though long-term value is strong.
  • Good oral hygiene and regular dental visits are essential for success.
