Meet Your Dentist in Delray Beach, FL

Dr. Christopher Ryan Sepe, DDS

Dr. Christopher Ryan Sepe is passionate about changing the way people experience dentistry. His goal is to move dentistry away from the stressful or intimidating experiences many patients remember from childhood and create an environment where dental visits feel like a positive part of a patient&#x27;s self-care routine, something they can actually look forward to.

Dr. Sepe attended Florida State University and later earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the NYU College of Dentistry. Following dental school, he completed a one-year hospital-based residency at Staten Island University Hospital, where he received advanced training in oral surgery and emergency dental care. He continues his education throughout the year by taking courses and attending conferences, allowing him to stay current with the latest dental techniques and technology.

A member of the American Dental Association and Florida Dental Association, Dr. Sepe has also been featured as a Top Doctor in Boca Magazine since 2024.

Outside the office, Dr. Sepe and his wife live in Delray Beach and enjoy staying active in their community. He volunteers each month at Mission Medical Center in West Delray, providing dental care to underserved patients. In his free time, he enjoys boating, golfing, spending time with family, cheering on Florida State football, and taking his classic car to shows.

For Dr. Sepe, great dentistry is about more than treating teeth. It is about creating an experience that helps patients feel comfortable, cared for, and confident about returning.
