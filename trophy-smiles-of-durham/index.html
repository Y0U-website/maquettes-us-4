Most dental professionals recommend routine checkups twice a year. The regular cleanings are necessary to clean areas of your teeth that your toothbrush and floss won’t reach. Sugar and acid in your mouth create an environment where mouth germs and bacteria can start to eat their way into the surfaces of our teeth like worms into an apple. These cavities start out small but if not treated can become larger over time. Once cavities grow and spread the center of the tooth, toothaches can occur. The larger the cavity, the more complicated and costly in can be to fix it.

For cosmetic enhancement and/or restoring extensively damaged teeth the best treatment may be a full or partial coverage (Veneer) crown. Crowns are ceramic or metal encasements that are placed on top of the teeth to protect them when there excessively weak or damaged. Veneers are partial encasements that cover the front face of the tooth.
