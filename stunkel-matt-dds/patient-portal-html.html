Granting Portal Access to Others

By granting proxy access, you are voluntarily allowing others complete access to the contents of your Patient Portal record. Access may include a problem list, allergies, medications, lab and radiology results, and other clinical documentation. Proxy access will remain in effect until revoked in writing and submitted to our Health Information Management department.

Have questions about proxy access? Contact technical support at 417-328-4774 or portal@citizensmemorial.com .

Grant Access Online

To grant proxy access online, log in to your portal and go to your profile. Click on "Share My Portal," then "Create Invitation" and enter the requested information. Our system will send an email invitation to the person you want to have access to your account.

Complete a Paper Form

Return a completed form to the CMH Health Information Management (HIM) department. Download the Proxy Access Form for use by patients older than 18. If the person granting proxy access is between the ages of 13 and 18, download the CMH Patient Portal Request Form .

Patient Portal Frequently Asked Questions

The Patient Portal is a secure, convenient way to view your personal health information and communicate with health care providers.

You can view lab and test results, visit history, medications, reports and messages from your health care provider.

Create an account online. Our Health Information Management staff will verify your information and send you a welcome email with login instructions.

All you need is a computer or smartphone with access to the internet. Simply open the app or log in to the website. You will find your personal health information conveniently organized and available to you.

If you forget your password, resetting it is both secure and convenient. Just click on "Forgot Password?" under the password field and the on-screen instructions will direct you in the process.

Where does my health information come from?

All of your personal health information on the Patient Portal comes directly from your electronic health record at Citizens Memorial. This helps us provide the most accurate, up-to-date information for your health care.

It’s free! We don’t think you should have to pay for your personal health information or communicate with your provider.

Can my family access my health information?

You can grant proxy access to family members online through the Patient Portal. To grant proxy access online, go to your Profile. Click on "Share My Portal" and then "Create Invitation" and enter the requested information. Our system will send an email invitation to the person you want to have access to your account.

You also can return a completed form to the CMH Health Information Management (HIM) department. Download the Proxy Access Form for use by patients older than 18. If the person granting proxy access is between the ages of 13 and 18, download the CMH Patient Portal Request Form .

By granting proxy access, you are voluntarily allowing them complete access to the contents of your Patient Portal record. Access may include a problem list, allergies, medications, lab and radiology results, and other clinical documentation. Proxy access will remain in effect until revoked in writing and submitted to our HIM department.

Who do I contact if I find errors in my health record?

If you find errors in your personal health information on the Patient Portal, please contact your physician’s office. They will consult with you to resolve the error. It is a priority of ours to maintain an accurate record of your health care.

What if I have technical problems with the patient portal?

After logging in, click on "Contact Technical Support" to send a message to our technical support team. They will resolve your problem as quickly as possible.

Can I submit my own information to the patient portal?

To submit information to the patient portal, send a secure message to your provider through the portal.

Can I request an appointment through the patient portal?

Yes! After logging into the Patient Portal, select "Appointments." Then select, "Request Appointment" and follow the prompts.

Can I access my child’s health records from my account?

In accordance with Missouri law, all requests for proxy access to minors between 13 and 18 will automatically be denied. Minors between the age of 13 and 18 can grant their parents proxy access to their health record by completing this form.

For children under the age of 13, contact our Health Information Management department at 417-328-4774 . Existing proxy access will automatically be revoked on their 13th birthday. Patients may revoke proxy access at any time.
