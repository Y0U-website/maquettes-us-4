Soirée Presented by SGC Foodservice

September

The annual Citizens Memorial Health Care Foundation Soirée is presented by SGC Foodservice. The event is a vibrant evening at the home of John and Maria Kallenbach near Bolivar.

It’s an evening of fancy dress, community and generosity under the stars!

Sporting Clay Fundraiser Presented by SGC Foodservice

October

The Sporting Clay Fundraiser, presented by SGC Foodservice, made its debut in 2023. Teams of four compete to raise money for the Foundation.

At the end of each round, teams are placed into flights. The top team in each flight receives trophies.

For more information about the sporting clay fundraiser, contact CMH Community Relations & Development at 417-328-6318 or development@citizensmemorial.com .

Hospice Lights of Remembrance

November

The purpose of the Lights of Remembrance Cheer-ity Open House and Fundraiser is to:

Individuals can donate $10 to $500 per light in honor of family and friends or in memory of loved ones.

The lights decorating the tree are dedicated to those people who are being honored or memorialized. Your giving ensures that terminally ill patients and their families in our area receive physical, emotional, spiritual and bereavement care.

The fundraiser benefits CMH Hospice. Caregivers help patients remain as comfortable as possible at home, or in a homelike setting, with family and friends . A bereavement program also provides support to the family after the death of their loved one.

  • Honor the life of a loved one.
  • Support the Hospice philosophy of living life fully , comfortably and dying with dignity.
  • Involve the community in supporting Hospice care .
