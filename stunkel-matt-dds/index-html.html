Careers at CMH

Citizens Memorial Hospital/Health Care Foundation (CMH) is a fully integrated health system. Citizens Memorial contributes to the health of communities by offering compassionate care in eight counties in southwest Missouri. Community involvement and engagement are at the core of CMH culture. We are committed to creating and sustaining programs that support wellness, disease prevention and charity.

CMH invites you to Step Up for Success with up to $20,000 to help you pay for college for healthcare-related degrees. From scholarships for high school students to employee loan forgiveness, CMH will support you in your educational journey.

Find your Career at CMH

Our employees want to make a difference in people’s lives. It is the perfect place to have a meaningful career in a supportive environment. We’re passionate about helping people. Our medical staff and support teams are making a difference in the lives of our patients and residents every day.

Physician and Provider Opportunities

Physician satisfaction at CMH is in the TOP QUARTILE NATIONWIDE , and physician turnover is far below the national average. CMH offers competitive salaries and benefits for physicians and advanced practice providers. Learn more about working as a physician or provider at CMH.

Nursing Opportunities

Being a nurse at Citizens Memorial is like being part of an amazing family. You’ll have a sense of community with co-workers and leaders who really care about you. What are some of the perks of working at CMH? Opportunities for growth, safe patient ratios, career development and supportive life/work balance.

CMH is working toward becoming a Pathway to Excellence organization. We are committed to empowering nurses and other professionals to provide exceptional care and improve patient outcomes. Learn more about working as a nurse at CMH.

Student Experiences

From high school and college students to recent graduates, CMH has programs to support training for future health care providers. Find information about scholarships, certification classes, apprenticeships, internships, residency and job shadowing programs.

Why Work at CMH

"We’re like family"

People ask me why I’m still at CMH all these years later. I tell them it’s like walking into an episode of Cheers. Everyone knows your name, and they’re happy to see you. We’re like family. Cody Allison, RN Med/Surg Director

"Community is very important to me"

I have felt so welcomed by everyone at Lake Stockton Healthcare Facility! Everyone has been helpful, kind and patient as I learn my new role. I chose CMH because it is present in all the smaller communities, and community is very important to me. Jennifer Weinstein Secretary at Lake Stockton Healthcare Facility

"I learn something new every single day"

Med Surg is not for the weak. Not one day is the same, and you always have to use your critical-thinking skills. You get interaction with the doctors and know why they are doing the things they are doing. If you don’t know, you ask. I learn something new every single day, and I challenge myself to teach what I learn. Cyndi Zerbey, LPN Med/Surg Nurse

"The atmosphere is friendly and caring"

The atmosphere at CMH is so friendly and caring. I love that people you don’t even know will acknowledge you when passing in the halls. I love the fact that you can have a normal conversation with the CEO or HR Director without feeling inferior. Patricia Schill Assistant Director of Environmental Services

Life at CMH

Our Organization

Citizens Memorial Hospital is an award-winning 86-bed acute care hospital serving an eight-county area in southwest Missouri.

  • Level III Trauma Center
  • Level II STEMI Center
  • Level III Stroke Center
  • 34 physician offices, including 12 certified Rural Health Clinics
  • Senior living facilities: Independent living, residential living and long-term care
  • Home care services
  • Retail pharmacies

Employee Benefits

A robust benefits package from CMH enhances your health, career, life and future.

  • Zero-premium health insurance option for employees
  • Employee wellness program
  • Retirement plan with employer matching
  • Profit-sharing plan
  • Life insurance
  • Professional development opportunities
  • And more

Life in Southwest Missouri

There’s always something to do in the Ozarks of southwest Missouri. From lakes to golf and shopping to tennis, we’ve got it all.

The communities we serve embody small-town charm where you easily can become an active part of your local community.

Larger cities such as Springfield, Branson and Kansas City are conveniently located nearby.
