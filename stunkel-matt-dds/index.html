The practice

Hosted at the beautiful John and Maria Kallenbach Estate east of Bolivar, the CMH Foundation Soirée brings together community leaders, local businesses, philanthropists, families and friends for an elegant evening of fine dining, entertainment and philanthropy.

The CMH Foundation Soirée is the Foundation’s premier annual fundraising event. This year, proceeds will benefit the CMH Dialysis Center and CMH Nephrology Clinic Patient Assistance Fund, helping patients overcome financial barriers to treatment.

The accreditation recognizes CMH Dialysis Center for meeting rigorous national standards for patient safety, quality outcomes and excellence in dialysis care.
