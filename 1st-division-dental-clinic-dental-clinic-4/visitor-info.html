Visitor Access

REAL ID Act licenses/identification cards marked " Limited Term " or " Temporary Visitor " are issued to individuals who are in a ’temporary’ lawful status as defined in the Real ID law. Unless a permanent resident card is provided, these visitors are considered foreign and must also provide documentation showing their lawful status (i.e. l-94, work visa, Refugee Travel Document (l-571) etc.). These visitors will be required to have a DoD Sponsor with a completed Fort Riley Form 103.

Pre-planned visits from family and friends can be easy with advance coordination. Check out these tips for Installation Access for Pre-Planned Family Visits .

Fort Riley Online Pass Application

Visitors requiring a pass for access to Fort Riley can now apply for passes online. The online pass application is available at: https://pass.aie.army.mil . The online pass process can only be utilized by personnel who are US citizens, have a valid state issued REAL ID-compliant driver’s license or Identification card, and have a mobile phone.

After vetting, visitors will be sent a text informing them if their visit has been approved or denied. Approved visitors with a REAL ID-compliant driver’s license or identification card may proceed to any of Fort Riley’s access control points to be scanned for access. Passes will NOT be sent via email to the visitor.

Passes may be requested no more than 30 days in advance and are only valid for the purpose of the visit (not to exceed a maximum of 7 days). Online pass applications are only processed during Visitor Control Center operating hours. It is recommended that visitors submit pass applications 24 hours in advance of their planned visit.

Gate Locations and Hours

Henry Gate

Exit 301, U.S. Interstate Highway 70 is Fort Riley’s main gate, is open 24 hours daily and also the location of the Visitor Control Center.

Closing Tab Ogden Gate On the east side of Fort Riley from Manhattan via Kansas Highway 18 is open 24/7 daily. Traffic patterns change during non-peak hours so use caution when approaching.

Closing Tab Trooper Gate On the southwest side of Fort Riley from Junction City via Washington St. Trooper Access Control Point is currently closed.

Grant Gate

On the south side of Fort Riley from Junction City via Grant Avenue - Grant ACP is open to inbound and outbound traffic. Currently open 24/7 daily. Traffic patterns change during non-peak hours so use caution when approaching.

Closing Tab Parker Gate Located on the west side of Fort Riley from U.S. Highway 77 via Old Highway 77. Currently operating 24/7 while 4-Corners ACP is closed due to repairs on Rubio Road.

Normal Hours of operation are Monday through Friday 5 a.m. - 7 p.m. Closed on weekends, federal holidays and training holidays .

Closing Tab 12th Street Gate Located on the east side of Fort Riley off of Kansas Highway 18 at Camp Funston. The gate is open from 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday thru Friday (closed on federal holidays) for commercial traffic only. Outbound traffic is not authorized.

Closing Tab Four Corners Gate Located on the north side of Fort Riley, Four Corners currently closed while repairs are being made to Rubio Road.

Closing Tab Merli Gate Merli Gate (formerly Rifle Range) is closed.

NOTICE: Located just prior to the Henry Gate. (Exit 301, U.S. Interstate Highway 70.) Federal holiday hours 8 a.m. - 4 p.m.

Personally Owned Firearms Registration Process

Motorcycles

In accordance with the Army Safety Program, AR385-10, CH 11-9, all riders entering Fort Riley must be properly licensed and wear the required Personal Protective Equipment to operate a motorcycle on Fort Riley. PPE consists of Department of Transportation approved Helmet, eye protection, foot protection, long sleeve shirt or jacket, long pants, full fingered gloves/mittens.

Soldiers assigned to Fort Riley are required to possess an ATV Safety Institute or Motorcycle Safety Foundation certification.

Commercial and Oversized Vehicle Access

When accessing Fort Riley, the term commercial vehicle may also apply to conveyance not traditionally considered commercial. In addition to the information below, check our reference chart to ensure you are using the right gate.

Commercial and Oversized vehicles such as construction / job trucks, windowless vans able to carry equipment or supplies without rear seating, moving/transportation vehicles, semi-trucks, FEDEX, agricultural vehicles (hay lease), etc. are required to use 12th Street gate for additional vehicle screening. Commercial and oversized vehicles may enter through Henry gate on Saturdays, Sundays, Federal Holidays, or during hours the commercial vehicle gate is closed.

Vehicles that are designed to transport passengers, including: passenger vans, commercial busses, etc., can access Fort Riley through any of the gates with proper identification.

When accessing Fort Riley, an oversized vehicle is not tied to weight or dimensions. Vehicles or combinations of vehicles that may have a problem coming through an ordinary gate would be required to go through a commercial gate.

All commercial and oversized vehicles will be inspected and logged. Bill of lading or invoice must contain an address on the installation. All occupants must be vetted or have a DoD ID card. Retirees or dependents operating a commercial vehicle must possess an installation pass or badge.

For more information on what qualifies as a commercial vehicle and what is a passenger vehicle, please view the "Vehicle Types" document listed under Forms and Information.

Personal Firearms

Anyone entering Fort Riley in possession of a firearm must declare the firearm(s) at the access control point prior to entering Fort Riley. All firearms brought onto the installation must be registered with Fort Riley IAW AR 190-11 and FR Reg 190-1.

For firearms registered on Fort Riley a person needs only present proof of registration along with proper identification to enter post.

For firearms not registered on Fort Riley, the owner will be directed to the appropriate application link to initiate the process and obtain a Temporary Registration prior to accessing the installation.
