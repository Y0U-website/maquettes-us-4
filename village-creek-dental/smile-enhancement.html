Inlays, Onlays, and Crowns

Tooth-colored ceramic inlays, onlays, and crowns help restore large areas of your tooth lost to a cavity or fracture. These provide more support and strength than a filling, which is necessary when restoring the form and most importantly, function, of the natural tooth.

Our office works with Smiles by Rego to ensure your ceramic restoration fits seamlessly with your smile.

Smile Makeover

A smile makeover is a comprehensive approach to improving the aesthetics of your smile through one or more cosmetic dental procedure such as teeth whitening, teeth alignment, veneers, or dental restorations.

The process is tailored to your specific needs and desires, addressing issues like tooth discoloration, gaps, chips, misalignments, or missing teeth.

Come in for a consultation with Dr. Ulep and Dr. Hernandez to see how your smile can make your confidence shine through.
