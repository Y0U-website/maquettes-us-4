The practice

About Us By serving clients within 125 % of the poverty line with no access to insurance, volunteer dentists and hygienists provide life-changing services to those who need it most.

Our Mission The Ben Massell Dental Clinic, a program of Jewish Family & Career Services, provides the most advanced dental care available to its patients. We are the only resource for comprehensive, quality dental care available at no cost to Atlantans who cannot afford it. Volunteer dentists offer their time to help people who would not have access to care.

We rely on volunteer dentists and hygienists to provide essential free services to our clients. Make a difference and volunteer once a month to help change lives.

Compassionate care and confidentiality are the hallmarks of the clinic.

The Ben Massell Dental Clinic is the only dental clinic in Georgia to be fully staffed by volunteer dentists.
