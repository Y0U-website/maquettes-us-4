First Visit

During your first visit, we want to get to know you and fully understand your dental health needs. Try to arrive a few minutes early so that you can relax and enjoy coffee or herbal tea while you review your medical and insurance information with our office team. You can complete your paperless registration forms when you arrive for your appointment.

By spending time with you and listening to what you need, Dr. Kay and our team can deliver quality dentistry. We allow about an hour and one-half for your first visit, which will include digital X-rays and an in-depth look inside your mouth. After a complete assessment, Dr. Kay will explain your current oral health and make treatment recommendations. Our team will provide information, answer questions, and support your decisions about your dental health.
