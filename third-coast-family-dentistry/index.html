The practice

In March 2025, Third Coast Family Dentistry merged with Dr. Hubbard’s office, allowing us to expand our team, resources, and ability to serve our community while maintaining the personalized, family-focused care our patients expect.

Whether you are a long-time patient or visiting us for the first time, we look forward to caring for your dental needs and serving generations of families for years to come.

Excellent care begins with proper education and training and is enhanced by hands-on experience. Our staff is dedicated to lifelong learning and fully committed to adapting to the ever changing dental science and advanced technologies.

At our office, your oral health is of paramount concern to our entire staff. From routine cleanings and exams to advanced restorative treatments, Third Coast Family Dentistry can meet all of your dental needs. We strive to make each visit safe and your comfort during procedures our primary focus.

Dr. Jared is the owner of Third Coast Family Dentistry. He is a native of Traverse City, MI. He also lived in Belize and spent his high school years in Indiana. It was in Indiana where his passion for dentistry blossomed when a dentist took him under his wing. Jared chose this path because he was able to see the compassion and care that can be provided to help improve the lives of so many people…

Dr. Jared completed his undergraduate studies at Grand Valley State University . It was there that he earned a bachelor of science degree in Biomedical Science. He then went on to graduate school at University of Michigan’s School of Dentistry earning his Doctorate of Dental Surgery in 2016. Upon graduation, he received the P.C. Lowery Award as well as the Comprehensive Care Award.
