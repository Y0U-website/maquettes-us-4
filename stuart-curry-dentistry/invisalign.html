Invisalign® Vs. Traditional Braces

This form of braces has several advantages over traditional metal braces. The main advantage is that you can take the aligners out yourself anytime you want a break from wearing them. Having the ability to take out the aligners to floss, brush your teeth, eat all the foods you love, and receive normal periodic dental cleanings, is a major plus compared to metal braces that are cemented onto your teeth.

Another side-effect of regular braces is that a small white circle of stained enamel will appear due to wearing the braces for long periods of time. You might even know someone who has these circles on their teeth from the cement of regular braces. Invisalign will not cause this type of discoloration because the aligners are removable.

Is Invisalign Recommended for Everyone?

Invisalign is not recommended for younger teens or children, since their teeth are still moving and growing. Invisalign also isn’t recommended for patients that have bite alignment issues or teeth that are severely rotated or crooked.

For some patients, Invisalign cannot provide the appropriate level of pressure that’s required to make adjustments. In these cases, traditional braces will be more effective and will have more of an impact on the growth and the direction of teeth. In other cases, veneers or surgery may be a better option.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is Invisalign different from traditional braces?

Invisalign uses clear, removable aligners instead of metal brackets and wires. You can take them out to eat, brush, and floss normally, there are no food restrictions, and most people won’t notice you’re wearing them. They don’t cause the discomfort or the white enamel spots that cemented braces can leave behind.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Invisalign treatment length depends on your teeth, but the average is about 12 months, with most cases ranging from six months to two years. Traditional braces, by comparison, average 18 to 24 months. Dr. Curry estimates your specific timeline after examining your teeth and reviewing your digital treatment plan.

How many hours a day do I have to wear the aligners?

Invisalign aligners should be worn about 22 hours a day for the best results. You take them out only to eat and to brush and floss, then put them right back in, including while you sleep. Staying consistent with wear time keeps your treatment on schedule and avoids delays.

Am I a good candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign works well for teens and adults with slightly crooked or overlapping teeth or gaps. It’s not ideal for young children, severely rotated teeth, or significant bite problems, where traditional braces may work better.
