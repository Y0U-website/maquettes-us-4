Dr. Jordan Buhr

Meet Dr. Jordan Buhr of Westside Dental.

Dr. Jordan Buhr became a dentist out of a genuine desire to help people feel healthy, confident, and cared for. He views dentistry as a combination of technical skill, problem-solving, and human connection. For him, great dental care means understanding each patient’s goals, earning their trust, and delivering treatment with precision and compassion.

Originally from Bismarck, North Dakota, Dr. Buhr earned his Bachelor of Science degree from the University of North Dakota, majoring in Health Sciences and Political Science. He went on to complete his Doctor of Dental Medicine at the Oregon Health & Science University School of Dentistry in Portland, Oregon.

Dr. Buhr and his wife, Cassandra, are grateful to be putting down roots in Olympia. Outside the office, he enjoys spending time with their two young daughters, Kathryn and Julia, exploring the outdoors, hiking, and playing or watching nearly any sport. Dr. Buhr looks forward to welcoming patients of all ages and providing care that supports lasting health and confidence.
