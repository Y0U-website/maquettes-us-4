Dr. Kyle Winter

Meet Dr. Kyle Winter of Westside Dental.

Growing up in Kent, Washington, Dr. Winter first became interested in dentistry through his mom who was a dental assistant. He was drawn to the artistry and science of the profession, and the opportunity to meaningfully help people.

Dr. Winter values developing relationships of trust with his patients and helping them achieve healthy and beautiful smiles. He is committed to providing the highest level of dentistry delivered with care and kindness.

Attending Western Washington University for his undergraduate studies, Dr. Winter earned a Bachelor of Arts in Economics. Following his studies at Western, he attended The University of Washington School of Dentistry where he earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS), graduating at the top of his class. After finishing dental school, Dr. Winter moved to Olympia and began practicing at Westside Dental. He remains an active learner completing advanced courses in implants , endodontics, esthetic dentistry , and comprehensive dental care .

Dr. Winter is married to his high school sweetheart and is the father of four beautiful girls, Phoebe, Tabitha, Lydia, and Magdalene. In his free time he enjoys snow skiing, hiking, playing the guitar, songwriting, bread baking and reading. He is active in the community, volunteering at the Olympia Union Gospel Mission, and can be seen singing in the annual OUGM concert in support of the No-Fee Dental Clinic. He also enjoys serving at his church, Reality Church Olympia.
