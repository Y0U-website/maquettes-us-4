What to Expect from the Implant Process

We will walk you through each step so you know what to expect and feel comfortable with your care.

  • Comprehensive exam and 3D imaging to review your bone health, gums, and overall oral condition.
  • Personalized treatment planning to decide how many implants you need and what type of restoration will work best.
  • Comfort-focused implant placement using gentle anesthesia and clear aftercare instructions for smooth healing.
  • Healing period to allow the implant to fuse with your jawbone for a strong, stable foundation.
  • Placement of a custom crown, bridge, or implant denture designed to fit your bite and blend with your smile.

Am I a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Many adults with one or more missing teeth are good candidates for dental implants. Healthy gums, adequate bone support, and good overall health are important factors. Even if you have worn dentures for years or have been told you have bone loss, you may still qualify with the help of grafting or other supportive procedures. During your consultation, we will review your medical history, take detailed images, and discuss all of your options.

Our team will also review your insurance benefits and go over the expected costs before treatment begins. We can discuss payment plans or financing options to make care more manageable. If you are interested in dental implants, contact Port Road Dental to schedule a personalized consultation and learn whether this treatment is right for you.
