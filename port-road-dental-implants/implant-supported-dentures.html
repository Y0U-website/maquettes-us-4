What to Expect From Treatment

Your journey to a more secure smile starts with a thorough exam and a conversation about what you would like to change about your teeth and dentures. We use detailed imaging and diagnostics to plan implant placement, then coordinate each step of your care so you always know what comes next.

  • Comprehensive consultation and 3D imaging to assess bone health and plan implant positions.
  • Comfort-focused implant placement with local anesthetic and optional additional comfort options when appropriate.
  • A healing phase to allow the implants to fuse with the bone for long-term stability.
  • Design and fitting of your new custom denture for a natural appearance and comfortable bite.
  • Ongoing checkups and adjustments to keep your denture and implants working their best.

Is an Implant Supported Denture Right for You?

Implant supported dentures are often a great option if you are missing most or all of your teeth, dislike the fit of your current denture, or want a more secure and natural-feeling solution. Good candidates generally have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support implants, or may benefit from preparatory treatments to build a stronger foundation. The team at Port Road Dental will review your health history, take necessary images, and talk through your goals to help you decide.

If you are curious whether implants could improve your denture, we encourage you to schedule a consultation. We will review expected costs, timelines, and any available insurance or financing options in clear language, so you can make a confident decision.
