Dr. Grant is a top-rated pediatric dentist, known for his friendly and light-hearted personality. He specializes in creating fun dental experiences for children, compassionately guiding them through their appointments, inspiring them to achieve great oral health, and love going to the dentist!

Dr. Grant’s unique approach to pediatric dentistry is conservative and preventative. He understands that every child is unique and prioritizes your child’s needs.

​Dr. Grant and his wife, Alison, grew up in the South Bay, where they met in high school. They love being outdoors, hiking and skiing in Mammoth, and walking their dog, Snowy, around the South Bay. Dr. Grant and Alison are proud parents of their baby boy, Clayton, and are enjoying the journey of parenthood, filled with love, joy, and endless precious moments.

Education Dr. Grant completed his bachelor’s degree at the University of Southern California and earned his dental degree at the USC School of Dentistry, where he was honored with the Floyd E. Hogeboom award for excellence in pediatric dentistry. He went on to complete his Pediatric Dental Residency at USC, Long Beach Memorial/Miller Children’s Hospital.
