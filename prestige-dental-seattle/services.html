Welcome to Prestige

Periodontal Evaluation A periodontal evaluation is a comprehensive assessment of the health of your gums and the supporting structures of your teeth. It typically involves several steps including a medical history review, a clinical examination to assess the condition of the gum tissue and tooth mobility, and x-rays to evaluate bone density. Based on the findings of the evaluation, your dentist will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to address any gum disease or periodontal issues that were detected.

Laser Dentistry Laser dentistry offers a safe and minimally invasive alternative to scalpels and other traditional instruments. We may recommend using a laser for various dental treatments, combining precision, reduced discomfort, and faster recovery times. Some examples of laser-based procedures you may expect to encounter at Prestige Dental include removing inflamed tissue prior to scaling and root planing to treat gum disease by removing plaque and tartar from below the gum line; preparing cavities for fillings ; and cosmetic treatments such as gum reshaping and teeth whitening.

TMJ Botox Injection A TMJ (temporomandibular joint) Botox Injection is a medical procedure used to treat jaw pain , muscle tension, and dysfunction associated with dysfunction of the joint. During the procedure, a member of the Prestige team will inject a small amount of Botox into the jaw muscles. Botox temporarily relaxes these muscles, providing relief from pain and improving jaw function.

Digital Imaging X-ray and CT Scan Digital imaging , including X-rays and CT scans, plays a crucial role in diagnosing various conditions. X-rays are fast and accessible, ideal for spotting bone-related issues. CT scans (Computed Tomography) provide detailed cross-sectional images of bones, blood vessels, and soft tissues, useful for diagnosing tumors and internal bleeding. We use digital imaging to generate the highest possible resolution radiography while minimizing radiation exposure to infinitesimal levels.

Restorative Services

Crown & Bridge A dental bridge replaces missing teeth using abutments (supporting teeth) and artificial teeth (pontics). Abutments can be natural teeth or implants. Bridges maintain bite alignment and aesthetics. A dental crown , on the other hand, is a tooth-shaped cap placed over a damaged tooth to restore its strength and appearance. It’s used for cracked teeth, worn-down teeth, and securing bridges.

Dental Implants A dental implant procedure involves surgically placing artificial tooth roots (implants) into the jawbone. These implants bond with the bone, allowing replacement teeth (crowns) to be attached. The process includes evaluation, implant placement, healing, abutment attachment, and crown restoration. Dental implants offer distinct advantages over dentures and bridges which your treating professional will discuss with you at the time of treatment.

Nitrous Oxide Sedation Nitrous oxide, commonly known as laughing gas, is a mild sedative agent used in dentistry to manage pain and anxiety during dental treatments. This colorless and odorless gas is mixed with oxygen and inhaled through a small mask placed over the patient’s nose. Dentists have been using nitrous oxide for nearly a century, and it remains one of the most common inhaled sedatives today. Nitrous oxide provides a safe and effective way to enhance patient comfort during dental procedures.

3D Digital Scanning (No Squishy Impressions) Digital dental scanning , also known as intraoral scanning , revolutionizes the way we capture impressions in dentistry. Unlike traditional alginate impressions, which involve gooey materials, digital impressions use advanced technology to create a 3D digital model of a your teeth and gums. Systems like our Cadent iTero take precise digital “impressions” without discomfort or mess. These scans result in better-fitting restorations, faster appointments, and less time spent in the dental chair.

In-Office Zoom Whitening

Porcelain Veneers Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells crafted from tooth-colored materials that are designed to cover the front surface of teeth. They are used to improve the appearance of teeth that are discolored, chipped, worn down, unevenly spaced, or irregularly shaped. Veneers are known for their natural appearance, durability, and resistance to staining. They can effectively transform the smile, providing a more uniform, brighter, and aesthetically pleasing look.
