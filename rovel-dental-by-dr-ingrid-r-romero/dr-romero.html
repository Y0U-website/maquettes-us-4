The meaning behind Rovel

Rovel Dental holds a special meaning to me, the name "Rovel" is a tribute to my parents, created from a combination of their names. Their unwavering support, values, and dedication have shaped who I am, both as a person and as a dentist. This practice is not just a reflection of my passion for dentistry, but also a way to honor them and the foundation they built for me.

Dr. Romero with a patient at Rovel Dental, Palm Springs My approach to care At Rovel Dental, I believe in combining artistry, science, and compassion to create smiles that are both healthy and beautiful. My approach focuses on education, trust, and patient comfort, ensuring that each person who walks through our doors feels heard, valued, and confident in their care.

Whether it’s preventive care, cosmetic treatments, or full smile transformations, my goal is to provide modern, minimally invasive treatments that prioritize both function and aesthetics. Staying active in leading dental organizations keeps me at the forefront of new advancements, so I can offer the most effective and comfortable care available.

That commitment includes advanced training at the Kois Center in Seattle, one of the most rigorous continuing-education programs in dentistry. As a Kois trained dentist, I bring its evidence-based approach to treatment planning, restorative care and esthetics to every patient I see.

Beyond the office

Beyond the office, I love traveling, trying new cuisines, and spending time with my family. My dog, Riley, and my cat, Sammy, bring so much joy and balance to my life. But most importantly, I’m incredibly grateful for my husband, Chris, who has been my biggest supporter in this journey. His love, encouragement, and belief in my vision for Rovel Dental have made this dream a reality.

I truly believe that every smile tells a story, and I’m honored to be part of that journey with my patients. I look forward to welcoming you to Rovel Dental and helping you achieve a smile you’re proud to share.
