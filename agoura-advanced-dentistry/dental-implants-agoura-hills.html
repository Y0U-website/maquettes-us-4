Dental Implant Solutions: Your Complete Guide

Not all implant cases are the same. The right solution depends on how many teeth are missing, the condition of your jawbone, your budget, and your long-term goals. This page is the hub — below, we’ll send you to the specific service page that fits your situation.

Single-Tooth Implants in Agoura Hills

The most common implant we place. A titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone where a missing tooth was. After 3–6 months of healing (osseointegration), a custom crown is bonded to it. The result is indistinguishable from a natural tooth — and far better than the alternative of leaving the gap (which causes adjacent teeth to drift and bone to recede).

  • Best for: Replacing a single missing tooth without affecting neighbors. Avoids the need to grind down adjacent teeth for a bridge.
  • Lifespan: 20+ years with care, often a lifetime.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When 2–4 adjacent teeth are missing, an implant-supported bridge spans the gap with two implants anchoring a multi-tooth prosthesis. Stronger and more -effective than placing 3–4 individual implants, and protects the neighboring teeth from the stress of a traditional bridge.

  • Best for: Patients with several adjacent missing teeth in a row.

All-on-4® Full-Arch Implants

The gold-standard solution for replacing an entire upper or lower arch — using just four strategically placed implants. Same-day teeth, no removable dentures, no covering the palate. Designed specifically for patients with significant bone loss because the angled posterior implants engage denser bone.

  • Best for: Patients missing all teeth in an arch, current denture wearers, or anyone with multiple failing teeth and reasonable bone density.
  • Read more: All-on-4® Dental Implants in Agoura Hills →

All-on-6® Full-Arch Implants

The same full-arch concept as All-on-4 but with six implants per arch instead of four. The two extra implants distribute bite force more evenly, reducing per-implant load and providing a stronger long-term margin. Recommended when patients have heavier bite forces, parafunctional habits (clenching, grinding), or significant bone loss that makes All-on-4’s posterior angled implants borderline.

  • Best for: Heavy-bite patients, patients with significant bone loss, anyone wanting maximum long-term stability.
  • Read more: All-on-6® Dental Implants in Agoura Hills →

Mini Dental Implants

About half the diameter of a standard implant. Used when bone density is too limited for standard implants and grafting isn’t an option, or to stabilize a lower denture. Less invasive procedure, faster healing, but not as durable as full-size implants for chewing-load applications.

  • Best for: Denture stabilization, very limited bone density, patients medically unable to have standard implant surgery.

Bone Grafting (When Needed)

If your jawbone has resorbed too much to support implants directly, a bone graft adds bone mass to the site so an implant can be placed once the graft heals. About 30 % of our implant cases require some grafting, especially in posterior upper jaw (sinus lift) or in patients who lost teeth more than 5 years ago without replacement.

  • Healing time: 3–6 months before implant placement

How to Know Which Implant Solution Is Right for You

Quick decision framework — though every case needs a 3D consultation to confirm:

  • 1 missing tooth → Single-tooth implant
  • 2–4 adjacent missing teeth → Implant-supported bridge
  • 5+ missing teeth in a single arch, or full denture replacement → All-on-4® (most patients) or All-on-6® (heavy bite, significant bone loss)
  • Any missing teeth + insufficient bone → Bone grafting first, then implants
  • Insufficient bone + medically can’t have standard implants → Mini implants or denture stabilization

Single Tooth Implants

A single dental implant is a permanent replacement for a missing tooth. The implant acts as an artificial tooth root, providing a stable foundation for a custom crown that blends seamlessly with your natural teeth. This solution restores full function, improves your bite, and prevents bone loss in the jaw — all while maintaining the beauty of your smile.

Full Mouth

For patients missing multiple or all of their teeth, full mouth dental implants offer a long-lasting, natural-looking restoration. This treatment uses strategically placed implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, providing superior comfort, stability, and appearance compared to traditional dentures. If you’re searching for full-mouth dental implants near you , our Agoura Hills clinic offers customized solutions designed for your lifestyle and oral health goals.

Implant-Supported Bridges

When multiple adjacent teeth are missing, implant-supported bridges can effectively restore your smile without compromising nearby healthy teeth. These bridges are anchored by dental implants for added strength, function, and aesthetic appeal. They prevent bone deterioration and provide a long-term, durable alternative to removable partial dentures.

All-on-4

The All-on-4® technique allows for a complete set of upper or lower teeth to be replaced using only four implants. This approach offers a faster, more -effective way to achieve a full-arch restoration. Many patients walk out with a new smile the very same day, making this one of our most popular options for affordable dental implants .

Mini Dental Implants

For patients with limited bone density or those seeking a quicker and less invasive solution, mini dental implants are an excellent alternative. These smaller implants stabilize dentures and bridges securely without requiring extensive surgery or lengthy healing periods. They are a practical and -effective dental implant option that still delivers lasting benefits.
