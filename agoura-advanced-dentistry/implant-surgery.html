Single Tooth Implant

If you’re missing one tooth, a single dental implant surgery is the most effective way to restore your smile without affecting neighboring teeth. The implant acts as a replacement tooth root, topped with a custom crown that matches your natural teeth. This permanent solution provides exceptional strength, function, and appearance, helping you regain confidence in your smile.

Multiple Teeth Implants

For patients missing several teeth, multiple implant surgery offers a stable and long-term solution compared to traditional bridges or dentures. Each implant is carefully placed to support crowns or bridges, ensuring excellent bite function and seamless aesthetics. With our advanced technology, the procedure is precise, comfortable, and delivers beautiful, lasting results.

Full Mouth Dental Implants

If you’ve lost most or all of your teeth, full-mouth dental implants —often known as All-on-4® or All-on-6® implant systems —can restore your entire smile in one procedure. This innovative approach replaces an entire arch of teeth with just a few strategically placed implants, supporting a full, fixed bridge. The result is a strong, natural-looking smile that feels completely real.

Implant Restoration

At Agoura Advanced Dentistry , we also offer implant restoration services for patients with damaged or aging implants. Whether your crown, bridge, or abutment requires repair or replacement, our experts ensure your dental implants remain functional, comfortable, and visually flawless. We use high-quality materials designed for longevity and aesthetic appeal.

Bone Grafting and Sinus Lift

Some patients may require additional bone support before teeth implant surgery . We provide bone grafting and sinus lift procedures to strengthen the jawbone, ensuring a secure foundation for implant placement. These treatments are performed with precision and care, using advanced techniques to promote quick healing and long-term success.

Make An Appoinment The Dental Implant Surgery Process Step 1: Consultation and 3D Imaging Your journey begins with a detailed consultation. Using digital X-rays and 3D imaging, Dr. Kamangar evaluates your oral structure and bone density to create a customized implant treatment plan .

Step 2: Implant Placement

During your implant surgery, a small titanium post is placed into your jawbone to act as an artificial root. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, with sedation options available for maximum comfort.

Step 3: Healing and Osseointegration

Over the next few months, your implant naturally fuses with your bone in a process called osseointegration, creating a stable base for your restoration.

Step 4: Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, a custom-made dental crown is securely attached to your implant. The final result looks and functions like a natural tooth, restoring your full smile.
