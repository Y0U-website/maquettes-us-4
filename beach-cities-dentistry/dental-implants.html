The dental implant process is specific for every patient, based on the number and type of implants required and whether any preparatory procedures are necessary. Generally, the process is further divided into these steps :

  • Consultation : During the first consultation, the dentist will conduct an oral health review with the patient regarding their treatment goals for dental implants before any procedures are performed. If so, the dentist will assess the patient to determine if they are a suitable candidate for dental implants, which may include x-rays or 3D scans to examine the density of the jawbone and any associated problems.
  • Treatment planning: Based on this evaluation and examination, our team would formulate a personalized treatment plan consisting of which type of implant, how many implants would be needed, and an outline of the time required.
  • Implant placement: An outpatient procedure for surgically placing the implant in the jaw bone. Local anesthesia will be used to make your procedure comfortable.
  • Healing and osseointegration: In the coming months, the implant integrates into the bone, and osseointegration helps provide a stable base for an artificial tooth. For now, a temporary prosthesis will serve to maintain your looks.
  • Abutment placement and restoration fabrication: After the implant is integrated with its surrounding bone, it will be attached to the implant. Impressions or scans will then be taken to create a custom dental restoration that mimics your natural teeth.
  • Prosthesis fitting : The abutment will be connected to attach the dental prosthesis, restoring your smile and oral functionality.

Benefits Of Dental Implants

There are countless benefits of having dental implants compared to more commonly accepted alternatives for replacement, such as:

Adjacent teeth protection

Dental implants don’t alter adjacent teeth, preserving their natural structure and overall oral health.

Preservation of jawbone

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care, making them a very cost-effective solution.

Preservation of jawbone

Implants stimulate the bone, thereby preventing bone loss following tooth loss.

Enhanced functionality

More abilities Implants provide a secure base for restoring confidence in eating, speaking, and smiling.

Improved appearance

Dental implants feel and appear like natural teeth, integrating with your current smile.
