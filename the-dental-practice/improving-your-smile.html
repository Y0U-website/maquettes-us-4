Our Cosmetic Restorative Dental Services

ClearCorrect®

Are you feeling self-conscious about your crooked teeth or misaligned smile? ClearCorrect® aligning trays make it possible to get a straighter and more beautiful smile without the need for metal braces.

Every few weeks, we’ll adjust your ClearCorrect® trays and customize them to your changing teeth. Once you’ve completed treatment, your teeth will look straighter, radiant, and more uniform.

Teeth Whitening

Have years of smoking or drinking coffee caused your teeth to lose their natural luster? Tooth discoloration is a natural part of the aging process, but your lifestyle can also cause staining.

Rehabilitation case 1 SQ

This case used a combination of crowns, bridges, implants and injection molding. The goal was to restore chewing function and improve appearance and comfort. 5 teeth were replaced. One tooth was not in function and was left alone. All the others were restored.

Rehabilitation case 2 MD

This patient had severe wear and erosion. Several teeth had to be removed and replaced. We restored the teeth to normal size and shape. That gave the patient their smile back and full chewing comfort and function. All done with ceramic crowns and bridges.

Rehabilitation case 3 TD

This patient had lost back teeth and was functioning only with front teeth. The teeth had become loose and in poor repair. To restore comfort, appearance and some function, we used ceramics to lock all the upper teeth together. That gave the strength and stability needed.
