Dental Implants Missing teeth can impact more than just your smile. They can affect your ability to chew, speak clearly, and even influence the structure of your jaw. That’s why Dublin Unique Dentistry presents state-of-the-art dental implant solutions that restore both function and aesthetics to your smile.

With advanced technology and a skilled team, we help you regain the confidence and comfort that come with having a full, functional set of teeth.

For patients missing a single tooth, dental implants are an excellent choice. A single implant includes a titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone. Over time, the post fuses with the bone. This process creates a strong foundation for a custom-made crown.

It’s an ideal solution for replacing a single tooth without affecting the surrounding healthy teeth. A single tooth implant looks, feels, and functions like a natural tooth.

For patients who are missing multiple teeth or an entire arch, full arch implants offer a life-changing solution. This procedure involves the placement of several titanium posts into the jawbone. These are then used to support a full set of replacement teeth.

Full arch implants are more stable and comfortable than traditional dentures. They allow patients to enjoy improved chewing ability, better speech, and increased confidence. No matter if you need to replace the top, bottom, or both arches, our team will create a personalized solution that restores your smile’s function and aesthetics.

Dental implants are designed to last for many years. They offer a more permanent solution than other methods like dentures or bridges.

Implants are custom-designed to match the appearance of your natural teeth, providing a seamless and attractive smile.

Implants restore the ability to chew, speak, and smile with ease. They can improve your overall quality of life.

Unlike dentures, implants stimulate the jawbone, prevent bone loss, and maintain facial structure.

Implants are securely anchored to the jawbone. They offer a more stable and comfortable alternative to removable dentures.
