Treatment time varies based on your needs. Some cases take 12 to 18 months. More complex alignment issues may take longer. At your visit, we will give you a clear timeline that matches your specific plan.

A check-up is often recommended by age 7. Early visits help us spot jaw growth issues or spacing concerns before they become bigger problems. Not all children need treatment right away, but early checks allow for better planning.

Braces use brackets and wires fixed to the teeth. They stay on during treatment. Invisalign uses clear, removable trays you switch out every week or two. Braces handle a wider range of bite problems. Invisalign offers more freedom with eating and cleaning.

You may feel some pressure or soreness when braces are first placed or when aligners are changed. This is normal and means your teeth are shifting. It usually fades in a few days. Our team works gently to keep you comfortable throughout care.
