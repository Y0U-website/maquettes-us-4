Understanding Full Arch Implants Full arch implants replace a row of missing teeth using four or more dental implants . These implants serve as anchors for a full set of artificial teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, full arch implants do not rest on the gums. The implants fuse with the jawbone and create a secure foundation that prevents slipping or shifting.

This treatment benefits individuals who have lost most or all teeth in one or both arches. The procedure involves placing the implants in strategic positions to maximize support.

Once the implants integrate with the bone, a custom prosthetic is attached. Patients receive a strong, stable set of teeth that functions like a natural smile.

Benefits of Quality Full Arch Implants Full arch implants offer tooth replacement. They restore oral function, protect jaw structure, and enhance appearance.
