Dr. Dong-Phuong Ly, D.M.D.

Dr. Ly is our General Dentist. She has been providing gentle dental care for patients in the NoVa, DC and MD areas since 2000. She forms trusting bonds with her patients who return year after year even when they have moved overseas. Dr. Ly cares for patients as young as 3 years old and as mature as 96+. Dr. Ly received her D.M.D. from Boston University Goldman School of Dental Medicine. She loves all aspects of dentistry but is extremely passionate about giving everyone a beautiful smile.
