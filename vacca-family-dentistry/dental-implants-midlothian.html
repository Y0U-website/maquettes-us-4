Determining the Right Dental Implant Procedure

How dental implants are performed depends on the type of dental implants and the condition of your jawbone. Tooth extraction replacement may involve a few or several procedures. The goal with each dental implant procedure is to ensure solid support for your new teeth a process that requires the bone to heal tightly around the implant.

The first step in determining the right treatment option for you is scheduling a consultation. You’ll meet with either Dr. William Vacca or Dr. Richard Vacca at our dental office in Midlothian to discuss your unique situation and develop a treatment plan that works best for you.

It’s About Restoring Healthy Beautiful Smiles

At Vacca Family Dentistry, our dental implant process focuses on developing a long-lasting, natural-looking solution. Implants are designed to restore both the function and the aesthetics of your smile allowing you to eat, speak, and live with comfort and confidence. Because they closely mimic the structure of your natural teeth, implants provide exceptional durability, stability, and natural results.

Another valuable benefit of choosing dental implants is the support of long-term oral health. Dental implants help to protect and preserve your jawbone, helping to maintain your facial structure without compromise.

If you are currently wearing traditional dentures, dental implants may be a more comfortable and secure alternative for you. No more slipping, clicking, or irritation just a confident smile and improved quality of life.
