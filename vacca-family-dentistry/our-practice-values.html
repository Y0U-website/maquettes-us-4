Our Core Values

Trust

Trust is the basis of every relationship. We will strive to earn the trust of our patients and co-workers-and always make eye contact when speaking with each other. When we commit to telling the truth and in turn, are open to hearing the truth, trust grows stronger every day. We Care.

Honesty

The only way to instill trust is to always-always-be honest. We will always keep our word both to patients and fellow team members, never being afraid to speak up when something did not go as we expected. We will always try to do the best for our patients and fellow team members. We Care.

Integrity

We always try be considerate and fair to our patients and fellow team members. We will always conduct ourselves with honesty while upholding strong moral principles. This will allow us to maintain a whole and undivided team. Our personal and our Practice’s reputation depends on it. We Care.

Fun

We try to make work "fun." We try to make dental care "fun." We strive to do this through our attitudes and the way we interact with each other. Smiling... laughing... greeting each other daily... saying please and thank you. We all have to work and we all want to provide quality care for others. Let’s have some fun along the way! We Care.
