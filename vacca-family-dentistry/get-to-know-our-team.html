William R. Vacca, DDS | Family & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. William Vacca graduated Magna Cum Laude from Virginia Tech with a major in Biological Sciences and a minor in Spanish. He went on to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the VCU School of Dentistry and completed a hospital-based residency at Newark Beth Israel Medical Center in New Jersey, where he served as Chief Resident. Since joining the practice alongside his father, Dr. Vacca and the team have consistently been recognized among the Top 5 Dental Practices in Richmond by the Richmond Times-Dispatch’s annual reader poll for three years running and counting!
