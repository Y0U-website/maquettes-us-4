Comparing Implant-Supported Dentures and Traditional Dentures

Implant-supported dentures eliminate the need for adhesives and provide unmatched stability. Dr. Gaff carefully evaluates each patient’s bone structure and oral health to ensure ideal results.

The Process of Getting Implant-Supported Dentures

Dr. Gaff uses advanced imaging and guided implant placement techniques to ensure precision and comfort. The process is typically completed in several steps, with each phase designed for safety and long-term success.

Once complete, you’ll have a durable, natural-looking restoration that enhances both appearance and function.

  • Consultation and evaluation: Dr. Gaff assesses your oral health and bone structure using 3D imaging.
  • Implant placement: Titanium implants are surgically placed into the jawbone.
  • Healing period: Implants fuse naturally with bone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Denture attachment: A custom denture is secured to the implants, completing your new smile.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Implant-Supported Dentures

Most adults with multiple missing teeth are good candidates for this treatment. Even patients who have worn traditional dentures for years may qualify with minimal preparation.

Patients from neighborhoods like Rancho Niguel, Crest De Ville, and Monarch Point trust Dr. Gaff for implant-supported dentures that restore confidence and improve daily life.

  • Have healthy gums and sufficient jawbone.
  • Are missing most or all teeth.
  • Want a more stable alternative to removable dentures.
  • Practice good oral hygiene.

Caring for Your Implant-Supported Dentures

Maintenance is simple and similar to caring for natural teeth. Dr. Gaff provides guidance on daily care and long-term maintenance to keep your implants and dentures healthy.

Proper care ensures the longevity of your implants and the continued health of your gums and jawbone.

  • Brush twice daily with a soft toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste.
  • Clean around the implant attachments carefully.
  • Avoid smoking, which can affect implant success.
  • Schedule regular dental visits for checkups and professional cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do implant-supported dentures last?

With proper care, implant-supported dentures can last 15–20 years or longer. (Source: ADA, https://www.ada.org ; Cleveland Clinic, https://my.clevelandclinic.org )

How much do implant-supported dentures cost?

Costs vary depending on the number of implants and materials used but typically range from $10,000 to $25,000 per arch. Dr. Gaff provides detailed estimates and financing options during your consultation. (Source: WebMD, https://www.webmd.com)

Are implant-supported dentures painful?

No. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia or sedation for comfort. Most patients report mild soreness that subsides quickly.

Can I convert my existing dentures to implant-supported ones?

In many cases, yes. Dr. Gaff can modify or replace your current dentures for implant support after evaluating your bone health. (Source: ADA, https://www.ada.org )
