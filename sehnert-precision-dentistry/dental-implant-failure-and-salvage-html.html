How Dental Implant Salvage Works

If you think that something may be wrong with your dental implant, give us a call right away. The earlier we are able to take a look, the more likely that we can save it! After we examine the area and discuss your symptoms, we can put together a treatment plan that is ideal for you. There are a variety of different treatments that could be necessary, like deep cleanings, antibiotic treatment, and other infection control methods. By seeking help as soon as possible, you can keep your dental implants strong and healthy!
