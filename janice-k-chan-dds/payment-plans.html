Payment FAQs

What does it mean to be out of network?

Out of network means the provider has not signed a contract with a specific insurance company. You may still receive partial reimbursement under your plan. Our team will submit your claim and help you understand what your insurance will cover.

What if my insurance doesn’t cover everything?

We understand insurance often doesn’t cover the full cost of treatment. We partner with Cherry to offer flexible third-party financing so you can get the care you need and pay over time. Contact our office to learn more.

When is payment due?

For patients without a payment plan, payment is typically due at the time of service. If you have questions about your estimated costs before your visit, our team is happy to provide a pre-treatment estimate based on your insurance.

Can I get a cost estimate before treatment?

Absolutely. We can submit a pre-authorization request to your insurance company to get an estimate of your out-of-pocket costs before you commit to treatment. Just ask our team at your appointment.
