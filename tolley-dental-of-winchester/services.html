Our Comprehensive Dental Services

Routine Care

At Tolley Dental, we believe in the importance of preventative dental care for maintaining excellent oral health and thwarting potential dental conditions. Our routine care services encompass thorough teeth cleanings, vigilant dental examinations, and comprehensive X-rays.

Our dedicated team performs these services and emphasizes individual care and personal attention. By checking your mouth’s health conditions routinely, we help ensure that our current patients’ natural teeth remain free from decay and disease.

Emergency Dentistry

Unexpected dental emergencies, which may trigger severe toothaches or intense pain, can occur unpredictably. Recognizing the urgency of these situations, we provide comprehensive emergency dentistry , backed by our experienced team, to aid our community during such critical moments.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Tolley Dental offers an array of cosmetic dentistry services to enhance your smile aesthetically and elevate your self-confidence. Our treatments, including dental veneers, teeth whitening, bonding, and tooth-colored fillings, aim to eliminate dental conditions impacting your smile. Through these treatments, we can help you achieve a visually pleasing, straighter smile, bringing you closer to your dental health goals.

Whether you want to transform your crooked teeth into a beautiful, confident smile or replace lost teeth with artificial ones for a complete smile, our dedicated, compassionate team at Tolley Dental can make your dental dream a reality.

Advance Care

Our advanced care service spectrum encompasses comprehensive treatments for complex dental conditions demanding specialized expertise. This suite of services ranges from complex root canal treatment to efficient TMJ treatment and alternative sleep apnea treatment options. Our knowledgeable and skilled team executes these dental treatments with an unwavering commitment to uphold the highest standard of care and patient safety.

Our dental experience, knitted with honest, patient-focused care and conscious sedation options such as nitrous oxide, ensures we treat complex dental conditions like severe toothache, dental pain, and bone loss, thereby sourcing comfort for our patients.

Restorative Care

Experiencing tooth loss or decay can undoubtedly affect one’s oral health and daily life. Nonetheless, our restorative care services can lend a helping hand. Our dedicated team utilizes advanced dental materials to provide tooth-colored fillings, dental crowns, and bridges that mimic the natural teeth and tooth structure. We also offer custom dentures , which are designed to fit comfortably and match the appearance of your natural teeth.

All these exquisite services have forged Tolley Dental into an expert restorative and family dental practice committed to rebuilding your beautiful smile and enhancing your overall oral health.

Invisalign

We offer Invisalign treatment for those seeking a discreet method to straighten teeth. This advanced care solution is a popular alternative to traditional metal orthodontic appliances. We provide complete information about Invisalign’s cost and how it compares to traditional braces, giving you a wide range of treatment options. Our experienced team is ready to guide you through the entire Invisalign experience, from initial consultations to the final stunning result.

This approach ensures that our patients make sound decisions anchored in comprehensive knowledge, ultimately helping them achieve the confident, straighter smile they’ve always desired.

Tooth Extraction

Extraction becomes an unavoidable necessity in specific circumstances where teeth have decayed severely. At Tolley Dental, our experienced staff utilizes nitrous oxide sedation during such dental procedures, ensuring our patients feel as comfortable and relaxed as possible. Tooth extraction not only relieves the discomfort of a decayed tooth but also paves the way for restorative procedures to maintain the integrity of natural teeth structure and overall dental health.
