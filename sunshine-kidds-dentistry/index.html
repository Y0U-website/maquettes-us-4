The practice

At Sunshine KiDDS Dentistry, Dr. Sheryl George and our team provide thoughtful, age-appropriate dental care for infants, children and teens in Parkland.

Our practice is dedicated to pediatric dentistry, and every part of the experience is designed around the needs of children and families from the way we explain treatment to the pace at which we work.

Whether your child arrives excited, curious or a little nervous, we take the time to help them feel comfortable, understood and proud of their visit.

Every child is different. Our approach is built around your child’s age, comfort level, dental needs and personality.

For children who need additional support feeling calm during treatment, carefully monitored nitrous oxide may help create a more comfortable experience. Dr. George will determine whether nitrous oxide is appropriate based on your child’s needs and planned treatment.

We help fearful kids feel safe, build trust, and have positive dental experiences. Using gentle communication, a calm environment and a patient, step-by-step approach, our team helps children move past dental anxiety so future visits feel easier and less stressful.

Some children benefit from additional time, flexibility or accommodations during a dental visit. Our team takes an individualized approach and works closely with parents to understand each child’s communication style, comfort needs and previous dental experiences.

Meet Dr. Sheryl George Dr. Sheryl George is a pediatric dentist dedicated to creating thoughtful, positive dental experiences for children and families.
