Are You a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most adults in good general health who are missing one or more teeth can be candidates for implants. The most important factors are adequate jawbone density to support the implant and healthy gum tissue. A thorough evaluation at Wenatchee Dental Arts will tell you exactly where you stand.

Advanced Technology for Precise Implant Placement

Wenatchee Dental Arts uses Cone Beam Computerized Tomography (CBCT) — the same type of 3D imaging technology used by dental surgery practices — to plan every implant procedure. The i-CAT 3D imaging system produces detailed images of your teeth, jaw, nerves, blood vessels, and sinuses with far less radiation than a medical CT scan.

Before any implant is placed, Dr. Divis uses this digital data to map the ideal position and angle for each implant, perform a virtual mock placement, and create a surgical guide for use during the actual procedure. This level of pre-surgical planning makes the process more predictable, reduces chair time, and significantly lowers the risk of complications. The results are more precise and the recovery is more straightforward.

What the Implant Process Looks Like

Implant treatment happens in stages over several months. The exact timeline depends on whether preparatory work (such as an extraction or bone graft) is needed, and how quickly osseointegration — the process of the implant fusing with your jawbone — takes place in your case.
