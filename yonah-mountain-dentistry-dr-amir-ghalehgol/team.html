Dr. Amir Ghalehgol

Dr. Amir Ghalehgol (or Dr. G) is originally from Oxford, Mississippi but has lived in Georgia since the first grade and considers himself to be a Georgia native. He is a graduate of the Medical College of Georgia, School of Dentistry. During his time at MCG, he received awards of excellence in general dentistry. He continued his studies in a General Practice Residency at the University of Alabama in Birmingham and while there, he learned advanced techniques in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, as well as received extensive training in working with medically compromised patients.

Dr. Ghalehgol also enjoys facial rejuvenation and smile enhancements in the form of Botox and dermal fillers. Dr. G has trained with Allergan (the company that produces Botox), Restalyne, The Consultant Clinic in England, and highly acclaimed injectors across the US.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Ghalehgol enjoys many activities, including hiking in Georgia and the neighboring states (with Yonah mountain being his favorite), photography, and traveling. In his spare time, he also likes to volunteer at animal shelters and participate in movies and TV shows being filmed in the Atlanta area. Some of his favorite books include The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo trilogy, The Hunger Games trilogy, and The Alchemist.
