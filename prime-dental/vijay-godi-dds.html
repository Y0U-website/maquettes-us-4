Vijay Godi, DDS

Dentist located in Fort Wayne, IN

Dr. Vijay Godi has been practicing dentistry for over 8 years. He earned his MBA in Healthcare Management prior to completing his Doctorate of Dental Surgery (DDS) from the University of Southern California. Specializing in general dentistry, Dr, Godi offers a wide range of services, including preventative care, restorative treatments, and cosmetic procedures. He stays current with the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques by regularly attending continuing education courses.

Dr. Godi is an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Indiana Dental Association (IDA), and the Isaac Knapp Dental Society (IKDS). When not in the office, he enjoys traveling and spending time with family and friends.
