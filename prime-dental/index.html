The practice

Everyone deserves access to top-quality dental care. Prime Dental aims to meet patient needs with personalized care and state-of-the-art tools and treatments. We welcome patients of all ages and can support optimal dental health for children and adults.

Dr. Godi brings a wealth of experience to his role at Prime Dental. He earned his Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the University of Southern California in Los Angeles in California and also holds a Master of Business Administration in Healthcare Management.

Preventive dental care begins with comprehensive dental exams and professional cleanings. Advanced imaging provides insight into the structural integrity and health of tooth and bone tissue, guiding care planning at all stages. When restoration is needed, patients can rely on our team for fillings, crowns, root canals , and much more.

Cosmetic dentistry is also an important focus within the practice. We offer teeth whitening , veneers, bridges, and full or partial dentures so you can have full confidence in your smile. Since communication is key, Prime Dental starts with an in-depth consultation process to ensure you receive customized care aligned with your needs and goals.

If you’re searching for a new dental care team for you or your family, we would love to sit down with you and explore how Prime Dental can meet your needs and exceed your expectations. Booking a visit is as easy as a quick phone call or a few moments on the website, so schedule your appointment with us today.

Prime Dental is a full-service dental practice proudly meeting the dental health needs of the community of Fort Wayne, Indiana. Under the leadership of Vijay Godi, DDS, Prime Dental offers the full scope of preventive, restorative, and cosmetic dental services, all in a warm and welcoming environment.
