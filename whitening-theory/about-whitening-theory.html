About Whitening Theory

At Whitening Theory, every session is tailored to you and your desired results while taking into consideration your current shade, sensitivity level, and daily habits. You’ll see noticeable results after just one session, and we’ll provide a simple plan to help you maintain your bright smile for months. Your comfort and safety are always part of the process while helping you to reach the exact look you want.

At Whitening Theory, we re helping clients in Northeast Ohio achieve a brighter, healthier smile without the high cost or discomfort of a dental office visit. Whitening Theory s services are safe, effective, and personalized to each smile.

To maintain and enhance your results, you get your personal take-home products to maintain your new, beautiful smile. Each take-home packet includes a re-mineralizing gel sample to strengthen teeth and manage sensitivity, with the option of a full-size and a whitening pen for touch-ups in between sessions.

Sessions take place in a comfortable, salon-style setting, giving you professional-level results without the high cost or discomfort of a dental office. Whitening Theory s approach is gentler than in-office dental whitening and far more effective than typical DIY products. We believe in keeping your enamel safe while minimizing sensitivity.

Can t come to us? Whitening Theory will come to you. We are the first mobile whitening service in the Northeast Ohio area, ready to host you for private parties and events. Whitening Theory brings everything to you with the same safe, effective whitening experience you d have in the studio, but where you decide.

We re here to answer any questions and ensure every client feels informed and confident before booking. Your comfort, safety, and satisfaction are a top priority, and Whitening Theory will strive to deliver results that make your smile shine. One session is all it takes for a brighter smile, with easy maintenance tips to keep it shining for months with very little upkeep and no unnecessary visits.
