What Is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics focuses on aligning teeth and correcting bite issues. Whether you’re dealing with crowding, gaps, overbites, or underbites, orthodontic care improves both function and aesthetics. At Mantena Dental, we offer modern options that are effective, comfortable, and personalized.

What to Expect During Treatment

1. Comprehensive Exam & Smile Assessment

  • Dr. Mantena evaluates your teeth, bite, and jaw using digital imaging to determine the best treatment plan.

2. Customized Treatment Plan

  • We’ll review all options with you—braces, Invisalign, or retainers—based on your smile goals and clinical needs.

3. Active Treatment Phase

  • You’ll begin wearing your braces or aligners, with regular check-ins for adjustments and progress tracking.

4. Post-Treatment Maintenance

  • Once alignment is complete, we fit you with a retainer to keep your results in place long-term.
