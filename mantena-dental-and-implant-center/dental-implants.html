What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. They support crowns, bridges, or dentures, creating a permanent solution that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth. Implants prevent bone loss and restore full bite function—making them the gold standard for tooth replacement.

What to Expect During Dental Implant Treatment

1. Initial Consultation

  • Dr. Mantena will examine your mouth, review digital images, and determine if you’re a candidate for dental implants.

2. Personalized Treatment Plan

  • Based on your oral health and goals, we’ll create a custom plan—whether you need a single implant, implant bridge, or full arch restoration.

3. Implant Placement

  • The implant post is placed into the jawbone. Over time, it fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration for a stable foundation.

4. Healing & Restoration

  • Once healed, we’ll attach a natural-looking crown or denture to the implant, restoring your full smile and bite function.

5. Ongoing Care

  • With proper hygiene and checkups, your implants can last a lifetime. We’ll guide you every step of the way.

Who Can Benefit from Dental Implants?

  • Adults with one or more missing teeth
  • People tired of loose dentures or bridges
  • Patients with healthy gums and sufficient jawbone
  • Those looking for a long-lasting, natural-feeling solution
  • Individuals who want to restore full confidence and function
