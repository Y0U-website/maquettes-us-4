What Are Full Mouth Dental Implants?

Full mouth dental implants are a permanent solution for patients missing most or all of their teeth. This treatment uses a series of strategically placed implants to support a full arch of teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, implant-supported restorations stay firmly in place and look, feel, and function like real teeth.

What to Expect During Full Mouth Implant Treatment

1. Comprehensive Consultation

  • Dr. Mantena evaluates your oral health, bone levels, and smile goals using advanced imaging and diagnostics.

2. Personalized Treatment Plan

  • We design a custom plan tailored to your needs, including implant placement and your ideal restoration type—fixed or removable.

3. Implant Placement Surgery

  • Multiple dental implants are placed in the jawbone. These posts act as anchors for your full arch of replacement teeth.

4. Healing Phase & Temporary Teeth

  • You may receive temporary teeth while your implants heal and bond with your jawbone, typically over a few months.

5. Final Smile Restoration

  • Once healing is complete, we secure your permanent prosthetic—crafted to match your bite, facial shape, and desired smile.

Who Can Benefit from Full Mouth Implants?

  • People missing most or all of their teeth
  • Denture wearers seeking a more stable solution
  • Patients with sufficient jawbone or willingness to graft
  • Individuals who want to restore natural biting and chewing
  • Anyone ready for a long-term investment in their smile and health
