We aim to educate our patients in a safe environment while offering them necessary comprehensive care. We understand that many people have anxiety about dental treatment, so we created a soothing environment and a scheduling system that never keeps you waiting long. By combining the latest technology with traditional techniques, we strive to give our patients the best care.

Dr Sugimori is proficient in all areas of General Dentistry, including specialized treatment such as root canal treatment and root repair, gum surgery, osseous surgery, and bone replacement grafts, surgical extractions and dental implant surgery, and dentures, crowns, and bridges.

Dr. Sugimori is an avid LA Dodgers and Kings fan and regularly attends their games in his spare time.
