Rapid Palatal Expander (RPE)

An RPE is used to widen the upper jaw, most often in children and teens. It creates more space in the mouth and can help correct crossbites, crowding, and breathing issues.

  • Uses gentle pressure to expand the upper arch
  • Custom-made and attached to molars or premolars
  • Typically worn for several months to stabilize results

Lower Lingual Arch (LLA)

The LLA is a fixed appliance placed in the lower jaw to maintain space, stabilize molars, and support proper arch development. It can also be adapted for light tooth movement when needed.

  • Helps prevent crowding and misalignment
  • Provides anchorage during orthodontic treatment
  • Custom fit to allow natural growth and tooth eruption

Space Maintainers

Space maintainers are used to hold open space after a primary tooth is lost early, ensuring permanent teeth can erupt in the correct position. They can be fixed or removable, depending on the treatment plan.

  • Prevents shifting of nearby teeth
  • Protects arch length and alignment
  • Supports healthy bite and jaw development

Essix Retainers

Essix retainers are clear, removable appliances designed to maintain alignment after orthodontic treatment.

  • Transparent for discreet wear
  • Custom fit for patient comfort
  • Durable materials for long-lasting use
