The practice

Services Comprehensive care under one roof. From preventative care to restorative and specialty treatment, we care for patients with the same careful attention at every stage of life.

Dentistry has changed considerably over the life of our practice. We have changed with it adopting advanced diagnostics and treatment technology while preserving the judgement, continuity, and personal attention that have always mattered most.

Meet Our Doctors Experienced care. Friendly faces. A dental practice is ultimately defined by the judgement of the people who practice there. Our doctors bring advanced training, broad clinical expereince, and a shared belief that good dentistry begins with sound judgement.

You’ll find us on the second floor of the green-glass building beside McCaffrey’s in the Princeton Shopping Center a familiar setting for generations of our patients.

Our approach is straightforward: preserve what is healthy, treat what requires attention, and do the work carefully. From routine preventive care to complex restorative and specialty treatment, our doctors consider the whole picture — your health, your history, your priorities, and the long-term consequences of every recommendation.

Whenever possible, care is coordinated within our practice, allowing patients and families to return to people they know and trust.
