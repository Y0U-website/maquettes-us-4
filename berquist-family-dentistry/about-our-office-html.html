About Us Crown Point, IN

At Berquist Family Dentistry, we’re dedicated to giving families from Crown Point, Whiting, and beyond exceptional care in a clean, contemporary office. But serving patients of all ages isn’t all we’re talking about when we use the term family practice. The emphasis on family expands to the father-son team of Dr. Robert Berquist and Dr. Daniel Berquist . When you come to us for care, you can expect gentle, compassionate service from a team that not only has years of experience and training but also values what you value as well.

One-on-One, Personalized Patient Care

Even though we’re all vulnerable to the same kinds of oral health problems, we don’t all have the same treatment needs. During your time with us, Dr. Robert Berquist and Dr. Daniel Berquist will carefully assess your situation and review your history, so they can get an accurate picture of exactly how they can help you and the rest of your family. They’ll also talk to you about your own goals. Then, once they have a clear picture of your situation, they can talk to you about your treatment options. Our practice offers a wide range of today’s best general, cosmetic, and restorative services.

Catered to Your Needs

If at any time you have a question or concern about a service we have recommended, please let us know. We want our patients from Crown Point, Whiting, and beyond to feel comfortable with the treatment plan we recommend. We also believe that the more you know about your needs and the different options available to you, the more equipped you are to make a good decision about your own care, as well as your family’s.

Flexible Scheduling for the Whole Family

As we just said, we understand that patients have different needs, and sometimes those needs go beyond the treatment stage. For instance, some patients might have difficulty scheduling an appointment for a weekday. If that’s your situation, we want to be flexible for you. Our office has convenient Saturday hours to make care more accessible to those with busy schedules. We believe staying open during these hours caters to hard-working families who may not otherwise have the time to schedule a visit.

Emergency Care a Priority

For patients who might be experiencing a dental emergency, we try our hardest to provide same-day care so you can enjoy optimal oral health and wellbeing again. Just give us a call and we would be happy to talk to you. By calling our office instead of putting off treatment, you can save money on overall dental care costs and help yourself or a family member get out of dental discomfort.
