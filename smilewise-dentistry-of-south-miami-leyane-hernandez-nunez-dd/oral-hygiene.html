Frequently Asked Questions

  • How often should I get a professional dental cleaning? Most patients benefit from a professional cleaning every six months to remove plaque, tartar, and surface stains. However, your dentist or hygienist may recommend more frequent visits if you have gum disease, braces, or specific oral health concerns.
  • Why is oral hygiene so important? Good oral hygiene prevents tooth decay, gum disease, and bad breath, while also supporting your overall health. A clean mouth reduces the risk of infections and contributes to a brighter, healthier smile that lasts a lifetime.
  • What can I expect during a professional cleaning? During your visit, our dental hygienist will remove plaque and tartar buildup, polish your teeth, and evaluate your gums for signs of disease. You’ll also receive personalized advice on brushing and flossing to help you maintain great results at home.
  • How can I improve my oral hygiene routine at home? Brush your teeth at least twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and use an antibacterial mouthwash. Eating a balanced diet, limiting sugary snacks, and scheduling regular dental check-ups will also help keep your mouth healthy.
  • Can poor oral hygiene affect my overall health? Yes. Research shows that poor oral hygiene can contribute to serious conditions like heart disease, diabetes, and respiratory infections. Maintaining good oral health not only keeps your smile beautiful but also supports your overall well-being.
