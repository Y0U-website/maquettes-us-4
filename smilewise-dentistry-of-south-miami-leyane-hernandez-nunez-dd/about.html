Meet the Doctor

Dr. Leyane Hernandez Nuñez DDS brings more than 20 years of dedicated dental experience to her patients, delivering care that is both compassionate and clinically advanced. She believes every smile tells a story, and her mission is to help each patient feel confident, understood, and genuinely cared for from the moment they walk through the door.

Her dental career began at the University of Dentistry of La Habana, Cuba, where she graduated in 2003. After moving to the United States, she continued her professional journey at the University of Miami, completing her residency in 2015. This blend of international education and U.S. clinical training has shaped her into a dentist who combines artistry, precision, and a broad perspective on oral health.

As a family dentist, she provides compassionate, high-quality care for patients of all ages from children to adults. She warmly welcomes you to join the practice and looks forward to helping you achieve a healthy, radiant smile you’ll be proud to share.
