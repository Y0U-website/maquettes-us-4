Frequently Asked Questions

  • What are dental implants made of? Dental implants are typically made from medical-grade titanium, a biocompatible material that fuses naturally with your jawbone, creating a strong and lasting foundation for replacement teeth.
  • How long do dental implants last? With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups help maintain both the implant and surrounding gum health.
  • Are dental implants painful? Most patients report little to no pain during the procedure. Thanks to modern anesthesia and gentle techniques, the process is comfortable, and any mild post-surgery soreness typically fades within a few days.
  • Am I a good candidate for dental implants? If you have healthy gums, sufficient bone density, and good overall health, you’re likely a great candidate. Our team will perform a full evaluation and discuss the best treatment options for your smile.
  • How long does the dental implant process take? The timeline varies depending on healing time and restoration needs. In most cases, the complete process from implant placement to crown attachment takes three to six months.
