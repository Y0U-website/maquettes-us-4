Privacy Policy

The Dental Way respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, protect, and manage information submitted through our website.

1. Information We May Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through this website, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Appointment request details
  • Message or reason for contacting us
  • Insurance-related information if submitted
  • Any other information you choose to provide We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, referring website, IP address, and general website usage data.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through this website to:

  • Respond to your questions or appointment requests
  • Contact you about scheduling
  • Provide information about our dental services
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Maintain website security
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, or administrative requirements

3. Appointment Forms and Health Information

Please do not submit sensitive medical or dental information through website forms unless the form specifically requests it and you are comfortable doing so.

Information submitted through appointment or contact forms may be used by our team to respond to your request. If submitted information becomes part of your dental record, it may be handled according to applicable health privacy laws and The Dental Way’s Notice of Privacy Practices.

4. HIPAA and Protected Health Information

The Dental Way may be subject to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, commonly known as HIPAA.

HIPAA protects certain health information and gives patients rights regarding their protected health information. For details about how protected health information may be used and disclosed, please review The Dental Way’s Notice of Privacy Practices or contact our office.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website may use cookies, analytics tools, pixels, or similar technologies to understand website traffic, improve performance, and support marketing or advertising efforts.

You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being used. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

6. Analytics and Advertising

We may use third-party tools such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta/Facebook tools, or similar services to understand website usage and improve marketing.

These third-party providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers who help operate our website, manage communications, support marketing, process appointment requests, or provide technical services.

We may also share information when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our patients, team, website, or practice.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to help protect information submitted through this website.

However, no website, email, or online transmission method is completely secure. Please use caution when submitting sensitive information online.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request that we update, correct, or delete certain personal information collected through this website, subject to legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping requirements.

You may also unsubscribe from marketing communications where an unsubscribe option is provided.
