The practice

Our office proudly serves Shakopee and the surrounding communities of Savage, Prior Lake, Jordan, Burnsville, New Prague, and Belle Plaine, Minnesota with excellent dental care.

Our team includes both English- and Spanish-speaking doctors and staff, ensuring a welcoming and accessible experience for all.

At Shakopee Dental we’re proud to provide comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages—all in one convenient, welcoming location. From preventive check-ups and cleanings to advanced restorative and cosmetic treatments, our approach is centered on helping you and your family achieve long-term oral health and confident smiles.

Our experienced team of dentists— Dr. Matthew Jelinek , Dr. Eduardo Silva , Dr. Sydney Schuberg , Dr. Ishwpriya Singh , and Dr. Ines de Llano —bring a wide range of expertise and a shared commitment to gentle, high-quality care. By working collaboratively, we’re able to offer thoughtful, personalized treatment solutions tailored to each patient’s needs.

We offer excellent dental care at an affordable price and are in network with a number of insurance providers. If you have questions about your dental insurance benefits please call our office.

We are excited that you have chosen Shakopee Dental for your dental care. Your comfort and convenience are our priority, and we strive to make every visit to our office a positive experience.

From preventive care and restorative treatments to cosmetic enhancements, each service is thoughtfully tailored to support your individual needs and long-term oral health goals.

Your comfort and health are our top priorities at Shakopee Dental. That’s why we use the latest dental technology—including Waterlase® dental lasers and iTero® scanning—to make your visits more comfortable, efficient, and precise.
