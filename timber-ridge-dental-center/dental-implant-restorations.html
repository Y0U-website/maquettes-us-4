Types of Implant Restorations We Offer

Single Crown Restorations

If you are missing just one tooth, a single implant crown is often the best solution. This restoration replaces the missing tooth without affecting the neighboring teeth. Crafted from durable, tooth-colored ceramic, the crown is color-matched to your natural smile and allows you to chew and speak comfortably.

Implant Bridges

For patients missing multiple teeth in a row, a dental implant bridge offers strength and stability that traditional bridges cannot. Instead of relying on surrounding teeth for support, the bridge is anchored by implants placed securely in the jaw. This option not only restores your bite but also helps maintain bone health in the affected area.

How Long Does the Implant Restoration Process Take?

The timeline for a dental implant restoration varies from patient to patient. Some restorations, such as replacing a crown, can be completed within weeks. More complex cases that involve bone grafting or multiple implants may take several months to complete as the implant post integrates with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration.

During your initial visit, we create a personalized treatment plan and outline the expected timeline. Our goal is to restore your smile efficiently without compromising quality or comfort. By setting clear expectations, you will always know what step comes next in your journey to a stronger, healthier smile.

Longevity: How Long Do Implant Restorations Last?

With proper care, dental implant restorations can last many years, often decades. Factors that affect longevity include the quality of the material, the skill of placement, oral hygiene habits, and overall health.

Single crowns and bridges typically last 10–15 years or more, while the implant posts themselves often last a lifetime when cared for correctly. At Timber Ridge Dental Center, we design your restorations with durability in mind, ensuring that they can withstand the demands of daily use while preserving your smile.
