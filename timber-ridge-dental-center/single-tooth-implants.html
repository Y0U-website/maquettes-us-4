Can a Single Tooth Implant Help Prevent Bone Loss?

Yes. When a tooth is lost, the jawbone in that area can begin to deteriorate over time. A single tooth implant helps stimulate the jawbone, similar to a natural tooth root.

This stimulation can help slow bone loss and preserve facial structure, supporting long-term oral health.
