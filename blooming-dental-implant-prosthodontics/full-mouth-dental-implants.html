Which Full-Arch Option Is Right for You?

We place both — so our recommendation is based on your 3D scan and your goals, never on what a practice happens to offer.

All-on-4 Dental Implants

The treatment that made teeth in a day famous. Four implants are placed at strategic angles — the back two tilted to anchor into the strongest available bone — with a full, fixed arch attached on top. An excellent fit for long-term denture wearers and patients with failing or loose teeth.

  • Often avoids bone grafting — angled implants use your strongest bone
  • Fixed temporary teeth typically placed the same day as surgery
  • Restores natural chewing power, speech and facial support

All-on-6 Dental Implants

Not every practice offers All-on-6 — and for the right patient, those two extra implants make a real difference. Six implants share biting force more evenly, put less stress on each fixture, and stimulate more of your jawbone. When your scan shows healthy bone volume, it’s often the strongest long-term investment.

Only missing one or two teeth? A single dental implant may be all you need — we always recommend the least treatment that gets you a healthy, lasting result. Prefer a removable option at a friendlier price? Compare snap-on dentures .

  • Bite force shared across six implants — less stress per implant
  • Broader bone stimulation helps protect long-term bone volume
  • Can allow a slimmer, more natural-feeling prosthetic arch
