Dental Implant Options at Blooming Dental

Implant solutions vary based on how many teeth you’re replacing, your bone condition and your goals. Your 3D scan makes the recommendation honest and exact.

Single-Tooth Implants

One missing tooth replaced with an implant and a custom crown without cutting into the healthy teeth beside it.

  • Best if: one tooth is missing or failing

Same-Day Implants

The implant is placed and a temporary crown or denture attached the same day you leave with functional teeth. Good bone density and healthy gums required; Dr. Yoon evaluates each case individually.

  • Best if: you qualify and don’t want to wait months

Guided Implant Surgery

A CBCT scan and digital plan produce a surgical guide, so the implant is placed exactly where it was planned on screen safer, more accurate, more predictable.

  • Best if: you want maximum precision (we use it routinely)

Full-Arch: All-on-4 & All-on-6

An entire upper or lower arch of fixed teeth on just 4 6 strategically placed implants, often placed and restored the same day. See the full All-on-4 guide .

  • Best if: you’re missing most or all teeth in an arch

Snap-On Dentures & Partials

Dentures that click onto 2 4 implants for stability without full-arch pricing removable for cleaning, secure in use. Explore snap-on dentures .

  • Best if: you want denture stability at a friendlier cost

Bicon Short Implants

Wider, shorter implants designed to succeed in limited bone often eliminating the bone graft other offices said you needed. Less invasive, faster healing.

  • Best if: you’ve been told you don’t have enough bone

Advanced Digital Planning & PRF Healing

3D imaging, computer-guided placement and platelet-rich fibrin make outcomes predictable and recovery easier.

When bone grafting or a sinus lift is needed first, PRF accelerates graft integration and makes recovery noticeably smoother.

  • CBCT 3D imaging bone density, nerve locations and sinus anatomy mapped to the millimeter
  • Computer-guided placement the implant goes exactly where it was planned, no freehand guesswork
  • Digital impressions an intraoral scanner instead of goopy molds; no gagging
  • PRF therapy healing cells from your own blood placed at the site: faster healing, less swelling, lower risk of complications
