Full-Arch Implant Options (All-on-4/6)

All-on-4 and All-on-6 replace an entire arch of teeth using just four or six strategically positioned implants. This approach provides stability, renewed bite function, and a confident smile for patients with multiple missing teeth.

Fixed restoration that doesn’t come out like dentures—eat, speak, and smile with confidence.

Same-Day & Immediate-Load Implant Options

Same-day implants mean the implant and temporary restoration are placed in one appointment when bone density supports it. Digital planning through CBCT imaging and computer-guided placement create predictable outcomes with reduced downtime.

Fewer appointments, immediate function, and prosthodontist expertise ensure successful immediate-load cases.

Bicon Short Implants for Difficult Cases

Many patients are told they need extensive bone grafting before implants. Bicon short implants are designed wider and shorter to succeed in limited bone without major grafting procedures.

Less invasive surgery, faster recovery, excellent stability—ideal for patients with bone loss who want to avoid sinus lifts or ridge augmentation.

Veneers & Bonding for Natural, Beautiful Results

Veneers are thin porcelain shells bonded to front teeth to correct shape, color, spacing, or minor alignment. Minimal-prep veneers preserve more natural tooth structure while delivering dramatic aesthetic improvement.

Composite bonding repairs chips, closes gaps, and reshapes teeth in a single visit—conservative, reversible, and natural-looking results.

Professional Whitening Options

Zoom in-office whitening: Dramatic results in about an hour—ideal for events or quick smile transformation.

Opalescence take-home whitening: Gradual whitening over 1-2 weeks using custom trays—gentler for sensitive teeth.

Both deliver professional-grade results far beyond drugstore whitening strips.
