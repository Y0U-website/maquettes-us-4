Dental Implants Cape May Court…

If you decide to replace a single missing tooth or teeth with implant restorations, the procedure may take 6 months or longer. Our dentists will partner with an experienced periodontist or oral surgeon to provide collaborative care.

Treatment will begin with an oral evaluation based on high-resolution images and scans of your mouth. Then, other advanced technology and sophisticated software will be used to precisely implant the biocompatible screws. The screws will be covered with a healing cap, which will remain on the screws for several months to allow the implant to fuse with the bone and soft tissues in a process called osseointegration.

You’ll return to our practice once osseointegration is complete. Your implanted screws will be fitted with an abutment. We will then attach the artificial permanent teeth (dental crowns) to the implants.

The final result are dental implants that look and function like natural teeth, and prevent further loss of jaw bone.

Benefits of the Dental Implant Procedure

Dental implants offer far more benefits than other restorations to replace missing teeth. Benefits of dental implants include:

  • Restored bite force
  • Improved speech
  • Better chewing capabilities
  • Self-esteem boost
  • Stopping further jawbone loss (implants may even promote new growth of the jawbone)
  • Natural appearance
  • Long-lasting (at least 20 years, but often last a lifetime)
  • Reduced risk of losing additional teeth
  • Reduced risk of infections developing in empty pockets
  • Preventing adjacent teeth from drifting into the space left by missing teeth
  • No special tools, adhesives, or soaks are required to care for dental implants
  • Crowns will not develop cavities
  • Prevent an altered facial appearance (sagging/sinking around the mouth)
  • More comfortable than dentures
