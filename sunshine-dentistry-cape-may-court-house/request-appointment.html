Why Choose Sunshine Dentistry?

Sunshine Dentistry is an award-winning, all-age dental practice serving the Jersey Shore. We’re proud to have been named a 2022 Best of Jersey Shore provider in the categories of Dentistry, Cosmetic Dentistry, and Pediatric Dentistry.

Each day our experienced, efficient, and skillful team works hard to provide patients with an exceptional experience. At our state-of-the-art dental facility, we’ve invested in all of the latest dental technologies to ensure enhanced comfort, successful results, and expedited recovery.

Our dentists are some of New Jersey’s most respected dental experts. They are actively involved in professional organizations on the national, state, and local levels that raise the bar of excellence in dentistry. They are passionate about educating patients about the vital role that good oral health plays in maintaining good health and longevity.

From your child’s very first dental visit to your teen’s orthodontic treatment with Invisalign clear aligners to problems that arise with breathing, such as sleep apnea, it is our honor to provide complete oral health and dental care for your whole family.

  • Restorative Dentistry for Teens & Adults . Dental crowns, dental bridges, and dental implants.
  • Dental Implant Restorations . Single tooth implants, implant-supported bridges, and full-arch restorations.
