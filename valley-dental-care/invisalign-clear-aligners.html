Invisalign Clear Aligners

We’ll start with a comprehensive exam and digital scan to determine if Invisalign is the right solution for your case. The American Association of Orthodontists also outlines how clear aligners like Invisalign can address a wide range of bite issues effectively.

  • Small gaps or spacing issues

  • Relapse after previous orthodontic treatment

How the Treatment Works

Step-by-Step Straightening

During your first visit, we’ll take a digital impression of your teeth and develop a personalized treatment plan. You’ll even get to preview your expected results. Once your aligners arrive, you’ll wear them for 20–22 hours per day and switch to a new set every couple of weeks. We’ll check your progress during routine visits to make sure everything stays on track.

Why Patients Love Invisalign

Subtle, Convenient, and Lifestyle-Friendly

Invisalign clear aligners are a popular choice for many reasons:

They’re an excellent option for busy adults, teens, or anyone who prefers a less noticeable alternative to braces.

  • Nearly invisible appearance
  • No dietary restrictions
  • Easy removal for brushing and flossing
  • More comfortable than metal brackets and wires
  • Fewer visits than traditional braces

Caring for Your Aligners

Keep Them Clean and Effective

Proper care keeps your aligners clear, odor-free, and working as intended.

  • Remove aligners before eating or drinking (except water)
  • Rinse and brush them daily with a soft toothbrush
  • Store them in their case to prevent damage or loss
  • Brush and floss after meals before putting them back in
