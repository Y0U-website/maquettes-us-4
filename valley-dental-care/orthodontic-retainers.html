Orthodontic Retainers

We’ll help you decide which option is best based on your treatment and daily routine.

  • Wire retainers (Hawley-style): These use a thin metal wire and acrylic base. They’re durable and adjustable but more visible than clear options.
  • Fixed retainers: A small wire bonded to the back of your teeth, typically on the lower front teeth. These are not removable but work continuously without patient effort.

Why Are Retainers Important?

Preserving Your Orthodontic Investment

Your teeth are supported by soft tissue and bone, both of which need time to adapt after shifting. Retainers ensure that new bone forms around the roots in their new positions. Without one, relapse is common — especially after clear aligner therapy.

Wearing a retainer as directed is the best way to avoid additional treatment down the line.

How Long Do You Need to Wear a Retainer?

Short-Term Healing, Long-Term Habits

Most patients wear their retainer full-time for the first few months after completing orthodontic care. After that, wear typically transitions to nighttime only. In many cases, nighttime wear is recommended indefinitely to maintain results.

We’ll provide clear instructions based on your case and help you build a routine that works for you.

How to Care for Your Retainer

Simple Tips to Keep It Clean and Effective

If your retainer no longer fits or gets damaged, we can remake it using a new digital impression.

  • Clean removable retainers daily with a soft toothbrush and water (avoid toothpaste or hot water)
  • Store them in their case when not in use to prevent damage or loss
  • Avoid eating or drinking (other than water) while wearing a removable retainer
  • Bring your retainer to checkups so we can inspect for wear

Retainers After Invisalign

Protecting Your Clear Aligner Results

If you’ve completed treatment with Invisalign or clear aligners , wearing your retainer is especially important. Since aligners are removable, your teeth may be more prone to relapse if retainer wear is inconsistent. Our retainers are designed to match your final alignment for a seamless transition from trays to maintenance.
