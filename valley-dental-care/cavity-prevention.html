Fluoride Treatments

How Fluoride Protects Your Teeth

Fluoride is a naturally occurring mineral that helps strengthen enamel and reverse early signs of tooth decay. While it’s found in many kinds of toothpaste and drinking water, in-office fluoride treatments offer a higher concentration for added protection.

Who Benefits Most?

We may recommend fluoride treatments for:

  • Children with developing teeth
  • Patients with frequent cavities or sensitivity
  • Individuals wearing braces or appliances
  • Anyone with dry mouth or reduced saliva flow

Dental Sealants

What Sealants Do

Molars have deep grooves where food particles and bacteria can hide — even with good brushing habits. Sealants are thin coatings applied to these surfaces to prevent cavities from forming in hard-to-clean areas.

Are Sealants Just for Kids?

Sealants are most commonly recommended for children and teens, but adults with cavity-prone molars can benefit too. The application is fast, non-invasive, and provides protection that can last for years.

Gum Disease and Its Role in Cavity Prevention

The Link Between Gums and Decay

Healthy gums play a vital role in protecting your teeth. When gums are inflamed or receding due to gingivitis or periodontitis, it exposes more of the tooth surface — making it easier for bacteria to cause decay. That’s why cavity prevention also includes gum disease screening and education.

Preventive Care at Every Visit

At Valley Dental Care, your checkups include:

  • Gum pocket measurements to track inflammation
  • Deep cleaning and removal of plaque and tartar
  • Advice on brushing, flossing, and antibacterial rinses if needed

Education and At-Home Habits

We believe the best results happen when patients feel empowered to care for their teeth every day. During your visit, we’ll share tips tailored to your age, health, and lifestyle — including nutrition guidance and brushing techniques to help you stay cavity-free between appointments.
