Ana

Sleep Coordinator and Front Desk Manager

As our Sleep Coordinator and Front Desk Manager, I am dedicated to ensuring every patient has a positive, seamless experience from the moment they contact our office. I coordinate our dental sleep medicine program, guiding patients through each step of treatment while providing education, support, and personalized care.

In addition to overseeing our front office operations, I strive to create a welcoming environment where patients feel comfortable, informed, and valued. Whether I’m helping schedule appointments, answering questions, or assisting patients on their journey to better sleep and improved dental health, my goal is to make every visit as smooth and enjoyable as possible.

I look forward to welcoming you to our practice and helping you achieve a healthier smile and a better night’s sleep.

The initial exam includes a review of your general health status and medical treatment history, a complete set of x-rays with reduced radiation by digital imaging, evaluation of the tissues and bone inside and surrounding your mouth, an oral cancer screening, a recording and evaluation of the teeth and present dental work, oral health education, and if time permits, aid with your home care by a professional cleaning. Together with Dr. Shah, we will inform you of your oral health status and make appropriate treatment recommendations.

Annually, I participate in continuing education coursework. I am certified to provide CPR and AED (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation and Automatic External Defibrillation) by the American Heart Association (AHA).

Dr. Shah’s office has a very relaxed and friendly atmosphere that promotes patient comfort. We have fun while doing our jobs and providing exceptional quality of care. I hope you find our office just as enjoyable. I look forward to meeting you!
