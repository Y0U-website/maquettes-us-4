Benefits of Dental Implants in Gurnee, IL

Dental implants are securely anchored into your jawbone, allowing you to eat comfortably, just like natural teeth. Say goodbye to dietary restrictions and hello to enjoying your meals.

Unlike traditional dentures, implants support the jawbone, preventing deterioration. They also maintain the position of your natural teeth for long-term oral health.

Implants provide a natural-looking solution that restores your smile and helps regain self-confidence for personal and professional interactions.

With proper care and regular visits to the dentist, dental implants offer a long-lasting solution, saving you from the hassle of replacements.

Your complete solution for replacing missing teeth.

Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth that look, feel, and function like natural teeth. Implants consist of a titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone to replace the tooth’s root.

This post is topped with a custom-made prosthetic tooth that restores your smile and chewing function.

If you are looking for professional dental implants, look no further!

If you are looking for professional dental implants in Gurnee, IL, look no further! Dr. Bradley and his restorative dentistry team use the latest implant technology to ensure you enjoy a better experience, faster recovery times, and the most natural results.

  • 1st APPOINTMENT Our dental implant team will create an impression of your mouth. Based on this impression, Dr. Bradley or Dr. Scott will construct an implant that fits your jaw perfectly.
  • 2nd APPOINTMENT The implant is inserted into your jawbone. Over the next three to six months, your gums will grow around the dental implant while it becomes securely attached to your jawbone.
  • 3rd APPOINTMENT Once the area has fully recovered, Dr. Bradley will place the restoration over the implant.
