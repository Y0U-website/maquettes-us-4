Dr. Landon Blatter

Dentist In Centennial, CO

Dr. Landon Blatter, DMD , is a highly respected expert in restorative, TMJ, cosmetic, Invisalign, and implant dentistry. He provides comprehensive dental care for patients of all ages at his two practices: Aspen Springs Dental and Aurora Family Dentistry. Known for his commitment to clinical excellence, Dr. Blatter leverages advanced digital dental technologies to enhance both the oral health and overall experience of his patients.

Dr. Blatter earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the Harvard School of Dental Medicine and holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Management from Brigham Young University. He has pursued extensive postdoctoral education in areas including comprehensive dentistry, TMJ therapy, implant dentistry, smile design, endodontics, periodontal therapy, occlusal disease, and advanced restorative techniques.

He is a graduate of the prestigious Dawson Academy for Comprehensive Dentistry, where he also served as associate faculty. He continues his advanced training in implant dentistry through the Denver Implant Academy. Dr. Blatter is an active member of several professional organizations, including the American Dental Association (ADA), Colorado Dental Association (CDA), Metropolitan Denver Dental Society, and the Denver Implant Academy.

Outside of his professional work, Dr. Blatter is deeply engaged in community service through his church and other local organizations. He and his wife are the proud parents of five children. In his free time, he enjoys spending time outdoors, hunting, and playing or watching baseball.
