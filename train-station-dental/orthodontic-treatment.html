Why Straight Teeth Matter

Beyond aesthetics, properly aligned teeth contribute to better oral health overall. Straighter teeth are easier to clean, reducing your risk of cavities and gum disease. A well-aligned bite reduces strain on your jaw, which can prevent headaches and TMJ issues down the road. And yes, there’s the confidence factor too. Research from the American Association of Orthodontists shows that straight teeth matter for both health and self-esteem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance for orthodontic treatment? Yes, we work with most dental insurance plans and will help you maximize your benefits. Our team verifies coverage before treatment begins so you know what to expect. We also offer flexible payment plans to make orthodontic care accessible.

How long does orthodontic treatment take? Treatment timelines vary based on your individual needs, but most patients complete treatment within 12 to 24 months. During your consultation, we’ll give you a personalized estimate based on your specific case.

Is orthodontic treatment painful? Most patients experience mild discomfort when treatment begins or after adjustments, but this typically fades within a few days. We provide guidance on managing any soreness and are always available if you have concerns.
