Dr. John Savon is a graduate of Shawnee High School in Medford, NJ. He attended St. Joseph’s University in Philadelphia as a biology major and was frequently on the Dean’s List. He then attended the University of Medicine and Dentistry in Newark.

Dr. Savon graduated at the top of his dental class and received numerous academic awards. After dental school, he completed a general practice residency at Virtua Hospital in Camden, NJ. It was at this residency that he met Dr. William Judd.

Dr. Savon began working with Dr. Judd in 1993. They later formed a partnership, which is now Train Station Dental. Dr. Savon is on staff at Virtua Hospital and has received “Teacher of the Year” honors while volunteering his time at the dental residency program. He is also certified by Invisalign Orthodontics to perform invisible braces.

Dr. Savon is married with two beautiful children. He currently resides in Thornton, PA.
