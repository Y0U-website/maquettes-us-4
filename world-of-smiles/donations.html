World of Smiles, Inc is a non-profit pediatric AND ADULT dental provider for the Maryland Healthy Smiles program

Our mission is to help Children battle childhood dental disease and educate parents to prevent illness and possibly even death.

World of Smiles, Inc is a non-profit 501(c)3 pediatric and adult dental provider for the Maryland Healthy Smiles program, providing dental care for patients age 0-20 years old. We provide our patients with healthy gums and beautiful smiles.

When you visit our office, there are no out-of-pocket fees and we can offer evening appointments!

We are super thankful to all our patrons for contributing to our mission in helping our patients have optimal dental health, regardless of socioeconomic status, through some of the most formative years of their lives. Thank you!
