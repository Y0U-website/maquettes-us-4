About Us

We bill medical insurance directly, coordinate with your healthcare providers, and make the entire experience feel clear and low-stress. If you’ve struggled with CPAP, chronic pain, or unanswered health concerns, we’re here to help you move forward.

Dr. Alissa Edwards

Dr. Alissa Edwards is passionate about helping patients live healthier lives through better sleep and pain relief. She earned both her undergraduate and dental degrees from Marquette University and has spent over 15 years building trusted relationships with patients while developing a specialized focus in sleep dentistry, TMJ therapy, and chronic pain care.

Her approach is thoughtful and personalized. Whether you’re dealing with sleep apnea or ongoing jaw pain, she takes the time to understand your story and tailor treatment to your needs.

Dr. Edwards is a Qualified Dentist with the American Academy of Dental Sleep Medicine and a member of the ADA and WDA. She believes in simplifying the process for patients by keeping care in-house as much as possible. From diagnostics to treatment and follow-up, it’s all handled here.

Outside the office, Dr. Edwards loves traveling, camping, and cheering on her kids in their many activities. That same energy and commitment carries over to her care philosophy, as she meets every patient where they are and helps them thrive.

Your journey to relief starts here

Consultation and candidacy We begin with a one-on-one consultation to review your symptoms, health history, and any existing diagnosis. Whether you’ve tried CPAP or are brand new to sleep and airway care, we’ll assess if you’re a good candidate for treatment here in our office.

Sleep test or workup If you haven’t been diagnosed, we’ll provide a take-home sleep test that’s easy to use and interpreted by a board-certified pulmonologist. If you’re moving forward with oral appliance therapy, we schedule a comprehensive in-office workup to collect all the data needed for your treatment and insurance approval.

Delivery and follow-up Once your appliance is ready, we’ll fit it using a digital scanner to ensure comfort and accuracy. No impressions required. You’ll leave with clear next steps and ongoing support from our team.

Digital tools that make your care more effective

Sleep apnea therapy

Skip the CPAP and discover an easier way to sleep. Our oral appliances are custom-fit, effective, and even covered by most medical insurance.

CBCT cone beam imaging

Our 3D cone beam scanner allows us to evaluate your airway, jaw joints, and bone structure with precision, leading to more accurate diagnoses and better outcomes.

Intraoral scanner

We use digital scanning instead of traditional impressions to create custom oral appliances. This means a faster, cleaner, and more comfortable experience.

A calming environment that supports your care

We understand that dealing with sleep or pain issues can feel overwhelming. That’s why we’ve created a calm, supportive space where you can feel comfortable. Our office includes cozy blankets, stress balls, and a warm team that puts you at ease. Every detail is designed to make your visit more relaxing and your care more approachable.
