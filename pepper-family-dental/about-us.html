About Us

A Dental Team Focused on Your Comfort and Care

At Dr. Pepper Family Dental, we focus on creating a space where patients feel comfortable from the moment they walk in. Visiting the dentist doesn’t have to feel stressful or rushed. Our goal is to make every experience feel calm, clear, and easy to navigate.

As a trusted dentist in Arcadia, we take pride in building long-term relationships with the families we serve. Whether you’re coming in for a routine visit or something more involved, you’ll be treated with care, respect, and attention to detail.

Care That Feels Personal

At Dr. Pepper Family Dental, we believe dental care should feel approachable, not overwhelming. Our team takes the time to listen, explain your options, and make sure you feel comfortable with every decision.

We understand that every patient is different. Some visits are quick and simple, while others may require a bit more time and attention. No matter the situation, we’re here to support you in a way that feels right for you.

How We Approach Every Visit

Patient-First Care

Your comfort and understanding come first. We take the time to answer questions and move at a pace that works for you.

Honest Recommendations

We believe in being clear and straightforward about your treatment options. You’ll always know what’s needed and why.

Comfortable Experience

From the way we communicate to the environment we create, everything is designed to help you feel at ease during your visit.

Team

Dr. Seth Pepper

Dr. Seth Pepper was born and raised in Arcadia, FL. He attended Florida State University on multiple academic scholarships where he majored in Biology with a minor in chemistry. While attending Florida State University, he was inducted into Phi Beta Kappa academic society, was in the honors program all four years and graduated magna cum laude.

Upon Graduation from FSU, he was awarded a scholarship to attend University of Kentucky College of Dentistry in Lexington, Kentucky. It was here where he met his wife, Dr. Christina Pepper, and completed his Doctorate of Dental Medicine.

After graduation, Dr. Pepper practiced dentistry in Birmingham, Alabama while his wife attended her residency in Orthodontics. After 3 years in Alabama, they moved to Punta Gorda, FL to be closer to his family. After being part of an award winning office in Port Charlotte for five years, he decided it was time to return to his hometown to provide quality dental care.

Dr. Pepper has spent numerous hours on continuing education and prides himself on being a well-rounded dentist, who keeps up with the latest in material and technique science. When he is not practicing dentistry, Dr. Pepper enjoys spending time with his family, including his two basset hounds. He also enjoys tarpon fishing, bird hunting and watching football.

Dr. Christina Pepper

Dr. Christina Pepper, a native of Lexington, KY, is excited to join the Arcadia community as the new orthodontist in town! She offers one-of-a-kind smile designs with an emphasis on esthetics, as well as proper function. She and her team strive for excellent customer service and to provide a family-centered atmosphere in which to receive your traditional braces or certified Invisalign® treatment.

Dr. Pepper completed her undergraduate studies at Transylvania University in Kentucky with a major in biology and a minor in psychology. She received honors in biology and graduated magna cum laude. In dental school, she graduated with high honors from the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry and received multiple merit-based scholarships, including a national P.E.O. woman’s educational scholarship. She was also inducted into the Omicron Kappa Upsilon national dental honor society.

Continuing her educational path, Dr. Pepper received her certificate in Orthodontics at University of Alabama at Birmingham, as well as a master’s degree in Clinical Dentistry. During this time, she taught dental students as a teaching assistant and performed orthodontics on veterans at the local VA hospital. Also while in Alabama, Dr. Pepper was a dentist for children at a non-profit clinic, offering treatment for underprivileged kids.

While not in the office, Dr. Pepper enjoys spending time with her family, taking walks with their basset hounds Daisy and Loki, and enjoying time outside, fishing, and boating. Being a mom to her daughter Kenzie is her greatest accomplishment and she loves watching her learn and explore the world. She loves to read and travel, especially to places where she can hike and enjoy nature.
