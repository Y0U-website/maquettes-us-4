What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant consists of three main parts: the post, the abutment, and the restoration. The dental implant post is small and screw-shaped, and it is often crafted out of titanium. Titanium is well-known for being a biocompatible material, and it’s used in other medical procedures like hip and shoulder replacements. Once the titanium post is inserted into the jaw, the surrounding bone tissue will heal around it, holding it in place and providing a reliably strong, long-lasting foundation just like the roots of a natural tooth.

On top of the implant post is the abutment, which is a small connector piece. This attaches (and is covered by) the final restoration. Depending on how many teeth you’re missing, one or more implants can be used to support a custom-made restoration like a crown, bridge, partial denture, or full denture. The final result is an incredibly strong and lifelike replacement that looks, feels, and functions just like the real thing!

Benefits of Dental Implants

Year after year, dental implants continue to be one of the most sought-after tooth-replacement solutions. There are several reasons for this, which we’re covering in the next section. That said, you shouldn’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with our implant dentist in Chesterton if you want to learn more about the perks that come with this restorative treatment .

Day-to-Day Benefits

Oftentimes, patients are focused on the long-term benefits of dental implants. While those are certainly noteworthy, you won’t have to wait long to experience the advantages. In fact, once you’ve healed, you’ll enjoy that dental implants:

  • Look and Feel Natural Since every aspect of your restoration will be customized with your unique face shape and aesthetic goals in mind, even those closest to you will find it extremely difficult to tell which teeth are dental implants and which are not.
  • Are Easy to Maintain Simply put, you won’t need to buy a bunch of expensive products to maintain your new smile. All you need to do is brush, floss, and rinse with mouthwash consistently as well as come to our office at least once every six months for a checkup and cleaning.
  • Restore the Strength of Your Bite Oftentimes, patients who are struggling with tooth loss are stuck eating applesauce, yogurt, and other extremely soft foods on repeat. Since dental implants can restore some of the strength of your bite, eating your favorite foods is not only easy but enjoyable!

Health Benefits

Another reason dental implants are so widely loved is that they can have a positive impact on both your oral and overall health. For example, they:

  • Prevent Dental Drift In the months and years following tooth loss, it’s very normal for the teeth surrounding the gap to move. This can quickly impact the alignment of your bite, increasing your risk of chronic jaw pain, premature wear on your teeth, and more.
  • Help You Maintain a Diverse Diet It’s hard to get all of the nutrients your body needs if you’re limited to a soft foods diet. Fortunately, dental implants can restore some of the strength of your bite, which makes eating a healthy, well-balanced diet considerably easier.
  • Preserve Your Jawbone While other tooth-replacement solutions rest against your gums or the surrounding teeth, dental implants are inserted into your jawbone. Not only does this provide unmatched stability, but it also helps prevent bone loss.

Long-Term Benefits

Last, but definitely not least, dental implants offer several long-term benefits, including:

  • Unmatched Stability Thanks to surgical placement, you won’t have to worry about your dental implants shifting or slipping unexpectedly; they will stay in place whether you’re talking to a coworker, laughing with a friend, or enjoying a meal with a loved one.
  • Preserve Your Youthful Face Shape Following tooth loss, many patients notice that their cheeks look sunken in. Not only will dental implants fill the gaps in your smile, but they will also preserve your jawbone, which helps you maintain a youthful appearance.
  • Long Lifespan Unlike other tooth-replacement solutions, which typically last 5-7 years, dental implants can last for several decades. In fact, if you practice regular oral care and stay committed to routine visits, they may last for the rest of your life!

Who Dental Implants Can Help

We offer several types of dental implants based on your pattern of tooth loss. For a single missing tooth, we can seamlessly complete your smile with a single implant crown. If you have multiple consecutive lost teeth, we may recommend an implant bridge to fill the gap instead of individual single dental implants. For those missing all or most of their teeth, an implant denture will provide the most stable and lifelike full smile replacement available.

Understanding the Cost of Dental Implants

Dental implants typically cost more than traditional restorations like crowns, bridges, and dentures upfront. However, patients often find that they’re actually the most cost-effective option because they can last for life, meaning they won’t need to pay for a replacement every five to fifteen years. The overall cost of the procedure can vary, but at Advanced Dental Concepts, we can provide an estimate during your FREE implant consultation and discuss your financial options, which might include dental insurance or CareCredit.

Are Dental Implants Worth the Cost?

The benefits of dental implants make them well worth the investment. Primarily, dental implants can last for a lifetime with proper care, which may even save you money in the long run from avoided replacements. They also allow you to eat, speak, and chew with the confidence of having a smile that looks, feels, and functions like your natural one.
