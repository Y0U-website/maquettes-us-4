Frequently Asked Questions

Which Lasts Longer: Dental Implant vs Bridge? Both can provide long-term service. Longevity depends on planning, bite forces, gum health, hygiene, decay risk, and regular professional care.

Is a Bridge Better When Nearby Teeth Have Crowns? It may be. If those teeth already need crowns and have strong roots and gums, a bridge may use restorations that are clinically necessary.

Is an Implant Better for a Front or Back Tooth? Either may work. Front teeth require careful gum and appearance planning; back teeth must tolerate stronger chewing forces.
