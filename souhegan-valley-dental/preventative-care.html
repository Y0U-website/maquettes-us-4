General Dental Services

Souhegan Valley Dental provides comprehensive general dental services to keep your smile at its best. Our team of skilled dentist and hygienists are dedicated to providing personalized care and creating a comfortable environment for all our patients. Our general services include:

Regular Dental Cleanings and Exams

Regular dental cleanings and exams are essential for preventing cavities and gum disease. Our skilled hygienists will thoroughly clean your teeth, removing plaque and tartar buildup, while our dentists will conduct a thorough exam to catch any early signs of dental issues.

Root Canal Therapy

Infected teeth no longer have to be extracted thanks to our advanced root canal therapy. Our skilled dentists will carefully remove the infection, clean the root canal, and seal it to save your natural tooth. This procedure helps preserve your oral health and prevents the need for tooth extraction.

Treatment of Gum Disease

Removal of Wisdom Teeth

Removal of wisdom teeth is a common preventative procedure to prevent future complications. Our oral surgeons perform this procedure safely and efficiently to avoid any discomfort or damage. Removing wisdom teeth can prevent issues like misalignment, crowding, and infections that may arise later on.

Same Day Crowns

We understand the importance of convenience and efficiency when it comes to dental procedures. With our state-of-the-art equipment and skilled dentists , you no longer have to wait weeks for your crown to be fabricated at an off-site lab.
