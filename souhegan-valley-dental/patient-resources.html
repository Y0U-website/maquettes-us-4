Your First Visit

We prioritize your oral health and want to provide you with the best care possible. During your first visit to our dental office, we will conduct a comprehensive oral evaluation to assess your specific dental needs. O ur experienced dental team will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to meet your oral health goals.

We kindly request that you bring your dental insurance card, a list of medications you are currently taking, and take any necessary antibiotics as prescribed. This will help us provide you with a seamless experience.

O ur team encourages you to ask questions and express any concerns you may have regarding your treatment options. We offer a wide range of services, such as cleanings, fillings, and cosmetic dental procedures to address your unique dental needs.

Your Child’s First Visit

The dental visit will include:

  • Thorough head and neck examination
  • Evaluation of teeth and gums
  • Possible x-rays if necessary
  • Teeth cleaning
  • Fluoride treatment if no further treatment is required

Important things to remember:

  • Bring your child’s dental insurance card
  • Complete any initial paperwork required for their first visit
