Sleep Apnea Oral Appliances

Types of Sleep Apnea Oral Appliances:

These appliances are an alternative to Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) machines, especially for those with mild to moderate OSA .

  • Mandibular Advancement Devices (MADs): These devices move the lower jaw forward to create more space in the airway .
  • Tongue-Stabilizing Devices (TSDs): These use suction to hold the tongue in a forward position, preventing it from blocking the airway .

We can make appliances for mild to moderate sleep apnea to help work with your CPAP machine or eliminate the use of it all together. We can also work with severe sleep apnea patients to help drop the pressure from the CPAP and make the mask more comfortable. Most of the time eliminating a lot of the headgear associated with a CPAP mask.
