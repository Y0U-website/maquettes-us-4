Dental Filling

What does dental filling treatment involve?

Dental fillings are a widely performed procedure aimed at repairing teeth that have been compromised due to decay or injury. During the process, the dentist eliminates the affected area and replaces it with a sturdy material, effectively restoring both the tooth’s function and appearance while also preventing additional decay. The choice of materials, which can include composite resin, porcelain, or gold, depends on the severity of the decay and the preferences of the patient. Fillings play a crucial role in maintaining oral health and safeguarding the integrity of the natural tooth structure.

Signs You Might Need A Dental filling treatment:

Experiencing tooth pain, sensitivity, or visible holes? Consider a dental filling at Nova Dental. Signs like decay, discoloration, or rough edges indicate treatment is needed. Prompt care prevents further damage and preserves oral health. Trust our experienced team for personalized care to restore your smile.
