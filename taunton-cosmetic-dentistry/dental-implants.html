The Dental Implant Procedure Explained

Caring for Your Dental ImplantsCaring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. To ensure the longevity of your implants, follow these guidelines:

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Shabana Chaudhry evaluates your oral health to tailor a treatment plan specifically for your needs.
  • Implant Placement: The procedure begins with the placement of a titanium post under local anesthesia directly into the jawbone where the tooth is missing.
  • Healing Process: The implant undergoes osseointegration, where it fuses with the natural bone over a period of weeks, creating a strong foundation.
  • Abutment Placement: After healing, an abutment is attached to the implant. This piece serves as the link between the implant and the replacement tooth.
  • Custom Crown Creation: A dental impression is made to ensure the new crown perfectly matches the shape and color of your natural teeth.
  • Crown Fitting: The final step involves securely attaching the custom-made crown to the abutment, fully restoring your smile.
  • Regular Cleaning: Brush twice a day and floss daily. Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and non-abrasive toothpaste.
  • Routine Dental Visits: Schedule regular dental check-ups and cleanings every six months.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: Protect your implants by avoiding hard and sticky foods that can cause damage.
  • No Smoking: Smoking can hinder the healing process and negatively affect the durability of your dental implants.
