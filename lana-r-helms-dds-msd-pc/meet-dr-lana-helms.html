Meet dr lana helms

  • Meet Dr. Lana Helms | Dr. Lana R. Helms, DDS, MSD, PC As a young girl, I looked up to my orthodontist, a kind man who seemed to enjoy his profession. Years later I understand the privilege of interacting with a family of patients that is appreciative. Orthodontic treatment is life-changing. I am honored to provide orthodontic care that requires precision and skill, yet improves the patient’s overall well being. Personal

My husband and I are long-time residents of Daviess County and love spending time with our children and grandkids. We have enjoyed serving in our church and community for the past 25 years, and hope to continue to be a part of it for years to come.
