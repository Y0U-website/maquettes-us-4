Dr. Jarrett M. Burr, DDS

Dr. Jarrett Burr focuses on blending function with aesthetics, helping patients achieve beautiful, natural-looking results with advanced orthodontic and cosmetic solutions. He is certified in Invisalign® and ClearCorrect , and offers specialty services including Powerprox 6-Month Braces and Lingual Braces placed behind the teeth. Dr. Burr brings an artistic eye to every case — whether he’s closing gaps, correcting bite alignment, or performing smile makeovers. He ensures each treatment not only looks great but also supports long-term function and stability.
