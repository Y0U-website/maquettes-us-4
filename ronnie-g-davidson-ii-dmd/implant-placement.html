When Are Dental Implants Recommended?

Dental implants are ideal when you’ve lost one or more teeth due to:

We often recommend implants as part of full-mouth solutions such as All-on-4®, full mouth reconstruction, or to support implant-supported dentures.

  • Injury or trauma
  • Gum disease or decay
  • Failed root canals
  • Congenital absence (missing adult teeth)
  • Extraction due to fracture or infection

Our Complete Implant Placement Process

Step 1: Comprehensive Consultation

We start with a thorough exam, digital X-rays, and 3D CBCT imaging to assess bone density, gum health, and implant site suitability.

Step 2: Treatment Planning

We’ll map out a custom treatment plan — including the number of implants needed, sedation options, and whether bone grafting or tooth extraction is required prior to placement.

Step 3: Surgical Implant Placement

Using a minimally invasive technique, we place the titanium implant post(s) into the jawbone. We offer IV sedation or oral conscious sedation for patients who prefer a more relaxed experience.

Step 4: Healing & Osseointegration

Over the next 3–6 months, your jawbone will fuse with the implant post in a process called osseointegration. Temporary restorations may be used to maintain comfort and function during this phase.

Step 5: Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we attach your permanent crown , bridge , or denture , restoring full function and a beautifully natural look.
