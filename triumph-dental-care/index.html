The practice

At Triumph Dental Care, we believe a healthy smile is about more than teeth — it’s about confidence, comfort, and feeling genuinely heard. Founded by Dr. Roshni Gehlot, a board-trained Prosthodontist with over a decade of experience across two continents, our practice was built on one simple promise: exceptional dentistry without the anxiety.

We take the time to understand your unique goals before recommending any treatment. You’ll always receive honest, transparent guidance — no surprises, no pressure, just results you’ll love for years to come.

Dr. Roshni Gehlot’s prosthodontic training means you get specialist care for everything from a filling to a full smile rebuild.

No rushed visits, no copy-paste treatment plans. We listen first, then act — always with your goals in mind.

Our welcoming office uses up-to-date technology to make every visit as easy and effective as possible.

We’re part of the Marin and Bay Area community — and we treat every patient like a neighbor we’re proud to serve.
