Dr. Kirk Larson

Started in Dentistry: 1988 | Joined Smile With Style: 1988

Continuing a 60-year family tradition, Dr. Kirk Larson brings both expertise and heart to his role as a second-generation dentist at Smile with Style. Raised in Fullerton, he takes pride in caring for his community and building lifelong relationships with his patients. Dr. Larson’s approach to dentistry combines a commitment to clinical excellence with a focus on patient comfort, which is evident in his state-of-the-art practice designed to offer a warm and relaxed environment.

Known for his patient-first philosophy, Dr. Larson goes beyond standard requirements in continuing education, consistently expanding his skills to provide the latest in general and cosmetic dentistry. His practice is defined by the personalized attention and high standard of care he brings to every patient interaction, whether he’s helping a young child feel at ease or crafting a transformative smile makeover.

When he’s not in the office, Dr. Larson enjoys an active lifestyle in Fullerton with his wife, three adult children and grandson. You might find him mountain biking, at the gym, or immersed in his passion for music, composing and recording songs in his free time. He and his wife are also active at Antioch Fullerton Church.

Dr. Timothy Hedrick

Started in Dentistry: 1993 | Joined Smile With Style: 2020

Dr. Tim Hedrick has a passion for helping families maintain healthy smiles across generations. For him, dentistry is about creating real relationships and witnessing the growth of patients from young children into parents themselves. With an eye for esthetics, Dr. Hedrick loves helping patients achieve smiles that boost their confidence and enhance their quality of life, specializing in smile transformations that make a lasting impact.

A lifelong learner and community-oriented dentist, he’s also actively involved in providing dental care abroad, having volunteered in various countries to bring smiles to underserved communities.

Outside of the office, Dr. Hedrick enjoys life in Fullerton with his two sons and attends Eastside Christian Church. He is an avid traveler who has visited 44 states and hopes to explore as many national parks as possible. When he’s not at work or on an adventure, he can be found paddle boarding, playing tennis, or trying out new recipes as a devoted foodie.
