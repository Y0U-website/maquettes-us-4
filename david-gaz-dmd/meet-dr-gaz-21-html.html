Meet Dr. Gaz

Dr. David Gaz

Dr. Gaz graduated from The University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine after completing his undergraduate studies in the College of Liberal Arts and Sciences. He then went on to complete his residency in General Dentistry at the University of Maryland Dental School.

Dr. Gaz is a member of the American Dental Association, the Dental Society of Delaware, and the Delta Sigma Delta Fraternity. In addition to his professional affiliations, Dr. Gaz has received the Kohrman Award for Excellence in Full Denture Prosthesis as well as the award for Outstanding Achievement in Operative Dentistry from the Academy of Operative Dentistry. Dr. Gaz is committed to staying current on the latest technologies which will ensure the best possible dental care for his patients.
