The Orthognathic Surgery Treatment Process Initial Consultation and Planning The first step of surgical orthodontics is a comprehensive evaluation by Dr. Phan, which includes X-rays, photographs, digital scans, and a bite analysis to assess your teeth and jaw alignment. If surgery is needed, a customized treatment plan is created in collaboration with an oral surgeon. Typically, braces are required for 1-2 years prior to the surgery and remain on during and after the surgery for approximately 6 months to complete the treatment.

Pre-Surgical Orthodontics

Jaw surgery is done in a hospital under general anesthesia, so you will be completely unconscious. Once you’ve been sedated, your oral surgeon will make cuts into your jaw bone, move them into the correct position, then use plates and screws to hold the jaw bone together as it heals. Everything is done inside the mouth so there are no scars. The process may vary depending on the type of surgery you need.

The Surgery and Recovery

You will usually spend one night in the hospital and go home the next day. However, there will be some food and activity restrictions to ensure your jaw heals properly. Your oral surgeon will send you home with instructions on how to care for your mouth, and you’ll need to come back for follow-ups to check on your healing. It usually takes 6-12 weeks to fully recover. You will then return to our office at Trident Orthodontics to finish the last part of your treatment prior to complete removal of your appliances.
