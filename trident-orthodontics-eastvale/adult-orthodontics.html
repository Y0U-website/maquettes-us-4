Ceramic Braces

Many adult patients choose ceramic braces as a less-noticeable alternative to metal braces. With proper care and attention, ceramic braces guide your teeth to its coveted alignment for a healthy, straight smile and bite.

The benefits of adult orthodontics

Improving Your Airway Orthodontic care can do so much more for you than just aligning your teeth. Airway orthodontics can reshape your mouth to open up your airway to its ideal state for better breathing and more restful sleep. By repositioning your jaw and to promote nasal breathing, airway friendly orthodontics can reduce the risk of obstructive sleep apnea so you can live your life to the fullest.

Speak and Eat with Ease By fixing dental misalignment, adult orthodontics can make it much easier for you to bite, chew, and talk. Many patients find relief from chronic jaw pain and notice their overall oral health improves.

Prevents Further Oral Health Problems Straight teeth are easier to brush and floss effectively, which helps prevent the buildup of plaque and bacteria that cause cavities, gum disease, and bad breath. When teeth are crowded or overlapping, it creates hard-to-clean areas that can lead to long-term oral health issues. Orthodontic treatment aligns your teeth properly, making daily hygiene more effective and reducing the risk of more serious dental problems in the future.

How it works: The adult orthodontics process Consultation To begin your consultation, Dr. Phan will conduct a thorough examination of your jaw, teeth, and gums to address any misalignment or bite problems. Our practice offers various smile alignment solutions, so we can carefully select a custom treatment outline that best fits your unique needs.

Impressions and Digital Imaging

X-rays, photographs, mouth measurements, and bite impressions or models will be taken so that Dr. Phan can properly diagnose the problem and create a tailored treatment plan just for you. After the data has been gathered and assessed, Dr. Phan will then design a custom orthodontic appliance for your smile.

Placement and Fitting

After the appliance is created, Dr. Phan will place it in your mouth and make necessary adjustments to ensure proper fitting and comfort. The Trident Orthodontics team will then provide you with care and maintenance instructions to keep your appliance and smile in tip-top shape.

Next Steps

Visit our orthodontic practice every 4-8 weeks to track progress, adjust your appliance, and follow up with any questions or concerns. While every adult orthodontic journey is unique, treatment can last anywhere from 12 months to 3 years.
