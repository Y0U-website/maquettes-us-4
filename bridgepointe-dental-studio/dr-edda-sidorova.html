Why I Chose Dentistry

Dentistry allows me to combine science, precision, and artistry with meaningful human connection. I’ve always been drawn to healthcare because it gives me the opportunity to improve lives in a very real, everyday way. Many patients come to us feeling anxious or unsure about dental care. Helping them feel calm, comfortable, and confident and seeing that transformation is the most rewarding part of what I do.

About Dr. Edda Sidorova

A proud graduate of Montclair State University, Dr. Edda earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology in 2016. She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the prestigious Rutgers School of Dental Medicine in Newark, NJ.

Fluent in both English and Spanish, Dr. Edda is committed to creating a welcoming and inclusive environment for families in the community. As an active member of the American Dental Association (ADA), the Academy of General Dentistry (AGD), and the New Jersey Dental Association (NJDA), she stays at the forefront of dental advancements to deliver the best possible care.

Specializing in minimally invasive dentistry, Dr. Edda prioritizes preserving natural teeth while providing gentle, effective treatments tailored to each patient’s unique needs. At Bridgepointe Dental Studio, patients can expect a warm, friendly atmosphere and a team that is dedicated to making every visit comfortable and stress-free.

Dr. Edda takes pride in building lasting relationships with patients and helping them achieve healthy, confident smiles. Whether you’re visiting for a routine check-up, a cosmetic procedure, or specialized care, you can trust Dr. Edda and the team to provide personalized, compassionate dental care for the whole family.
