Meet Dr. Yazan Alkhawam

Dr. Yazan Alkhawam

Dr. Yaz is a Jersey native who was raised in Kearny, NJ. He currently resides in South Amboy, NJ with his wife and newborn son. He has a passion for cooking that he gets from his father, who is a chef and he loves to play soccer at a competitive level. One of the many things he loves about dentistry is “building a trustworthy relationship with a patient who has a dental phobia by exceeding their expectations and increasing their desire to return to the dental chair by creating an environment that is safe and fun.”
