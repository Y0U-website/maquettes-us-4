Caring for Your Dental Crowns

Maintaining your dental crowns involves simple yet effective care:

  • Practice good oral hygiene, including regular brushing and flossing.
  • Avoid chewing hard foods or ice to prevent damage to the crown.
  • Regular dental check-ups at Wolski Dental Group are essential to ensure the longevity of your crown.

FAQs About Dental Crowns

How long do dental crowns last?

With proper care, dental crowns can last for many years, often over a decade.

Will my dental crown look natural?

Yes, each crown is customized to match the appearance of your natural teeth for a seamless look.

Is the procedure for getting a crown painful?

The procedure is typically performed with local anesthesia, making it a comfortable experience. Some sensitivity is normal after the procedure, but it typically subsides quickly.
