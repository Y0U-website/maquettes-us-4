The Dental Bonding Procedure

The dental bonding procedure at Wolski Dental Group is straightforward and typically completed in one visit:

  • Shade Selection: We begin by selecting a composite resin color that closely matches the natural color of your teeth.
  • Tooth Preparation: The tooth surface is lightly etched to roughen it, and a conditioning liquid is applied to help the bonding material adhere.
  • Application of the Bonding Material: The tooth-colored resin is then applied, molded, and smoothed to the desired shape.
  • Hardening and Polishing: The material is hardened with a curing light, and then the bonded tooth is trimmed, shaped, and polished to match the sheen of the rest of the tooth surface.

Dental bonding can significantly enhance the look of your teeth and smile.

Quick and Convenient

Most bonding procedures are completed in one office visit.

Minimally Invasive

The procedure typically requires minimal alteration to the natural tooth structure.

Cost-Effective

Dental bonding is a more affordable option compared to other cosmetic dental procedures like veneers or crowns.

Caring for Your Bonded Teeth

To maintain the results of your dental bonding:

  • Practice good oral hygiene, including regular brushing and flossing.
  • Avoid habits that can chip the bonding material, such as biting nails or chewing on hard objects.
  • Schedule regular dental check-ups for professional cleanings and to monitor the condition of the bonding.
