Dental Implants

Dental implants in Royal Oak, MI, are artificial tooth roots made from biocompatible materials, such as titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone. They serve as a sturdy foundation for supporting one or more replacement teeth, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures. Over time, the implant integrates with the jawbone through a process called osseointegration, providing stability and durability that closely mimic natural teeth.

Dental implants consist of three main components:

  • The implant : A screw-like post that is surgically inserted into the jawbone and acts as the root of the missing tooth.
  • The abutment : A connector piece that attaches to the implant and supports the replacement tooth.
  • The crown : The visible part of the tooth, custom made to match the shape, size, and color of your natural teeth.

Types of Dental Implants

There are several types of dental implants available, depending on your specific needs:

  • Endosteal implants : These are the most common type of dental implants. They are placed directly into the jawbone and are shaped like small screws, cylinders, or blades. Endosteal implants are typically used for patients with sufficient bone density to support the implant.
  • Subperiosteal implants : These implants are placed under the gum but on top of the jawbone rather than inside it. They are used for patients who have insufficient bone height or cannot undergo bone grafting. Subperiosteal implants are less common than endosteal implants.
  • Zygomatic Implants: These implants are used in cases where the upper jawbone has deteriorated significantly, making it difficult to place traditional implants. Zygomatic implants are longer and anchored in the cheekbone (zygomatic bone) rather than the jawbone.
  • All-on-4 ® Implants: This technique involves placing four implants in the jaw to support a full arch of replacement teeth. It is an efficient solution for patients who need to replace all of their upper or lower teeth and offers quicker recovery times compared to traditional methods.

The Benefits of Dental Implants in Royal Oak, MI

Dental implants can provide a range of benefits, making them the preferred choice for tooth replacement at Royal Oak Dental:

  • Natural appearance and functionality : Dental implants look and function like natural teeth. They are custom designed to match the color, shape, and size of your existing teeth, providing a seamless and aesthetically pleasing result.
  • Durability and longevity : With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. They are made from durable materials like titanium and porcelain, which are resistant to decay and wear.
  • Improved oral health : Unlike traditional bridges, dental implants do not require the alteration of adjacent teeth. This preserves the integrity of your natural teeth and promotes better long-term oral health.
  • Bone preservation : Dental implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that often occurs with missing teeth. This helps maintain the natural contours of your face and prevents the sunken appearance that can result from bone resorption.
  • Enhanced comfort and stability : Unlike removable dentures, dental implants are fixed in place, eliminating the discomfort and inconvenience of slipping or shifting. This provides greater confidence when eating, speaking, and smiling.
  • Ease of care : Caring for dental implants is as simple as caring for your natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups are all that are needed to maintain their health and appearance.
  • Improved speech : Missing teeth or ill-fitting dentures can cause speech problems, such as slurring or mumbling. Dental implants provide a stable foundation, allowing you to speak clearly and confidently.
  • Restored bite force : Dental implants restore the full bite force of your natural teeth, allowing you to eat your favorite foods without restriction.

The Dental Implant Process in Royal Oak, MI

At Royal Oak Dental, we take a personalized approach to dental implant treatment, ensuring that each patient receives the best possible care. The dental implant process typically involves the following steps:

Initial Consultation

Your journey to a restored smile begins with an initial consultation at our office. During this visit, we will conduct a thorough examination of your oral health, including the use of X-rays or 3D imaging, to assess the condition of your jawbone and determine if you are a suitable candidate for dental implants. We will also discuss your treatment goals, medical history, and any concerns you may have.

Treatment Planning

Based on the findings from your consultation, we will develop a customized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. This plan will outline the number of implants required, the type of restoration needed (crown, bridge, or denture), and any additional procedures, such as bone grafting or sinus lifts, that may be necessary to ensure the success of the implants.

Bone Grafting (if needed)

If your jawbone lacks the necessary density or volume to support a dental implant, a bone graft may be recommended. This procedure involves adding bone material to the deficient area to create a stable foundation for the implant. The bone graft may take several months to heal and integrate with the existing bone before implant placement can proceed.

Implant Placement

Once your jawbone is ready, the next step is the surgical placement of the dental implant. This is typically done under local anesthesia, though sedation options are available for patients with dental anxiety. During the procedure, a small incision is made in the gum to expose the bone, and the implant is carefully inserted into the jawbone. The gum tissue is then sutured closed, and a temporary restoration may be placed to protect the implant during the healing process.

Osseointegration

After the implant is placed, a process called osseointegration occurs. This is when the jawbone fuses with the implant as the former heals over several months. This process creates a strong and stable foundation for the replacement tooth. During this time, it’s important to follow your dentist’s instructions for care and avoid putting excessive pressure on the implant site.

Abutment Placement

Once osseointegration is complete, a small connector piece called an abutment is attached to the implant. The abutment serves as the attachment point for the final restoration. In some cases, the abutment may be placed at the same time as the implant, depending on the treatment plan.

Restoration

The final step in the dental implant process is the placement of the custom-made crown, bridge , or denture. This restoration is designed to match your natural teeth in color, shape, and size, providing a seamless and functional result. Once the restoration is securely attached to the abutment, your dental implant treatment is complete, and you can enjoy your new smile with confidence.

Follow-up Care

After your dental implant is placed, regular follow-up appointments at Royal Oak Dental are essential to monitor the health of the implant and surrounding tissues. We will provide you with detailed instructions on how to care for your implant at home, including proper brushing and flossing techniques, and recommend routine dental checkups to ensure long-term success. Contact us to learn more.

Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are an excellent option for most patients who have lost one or more teeth. However, certain factors must be considered to determine if you are a suitable candidate:

  • Jawbone health : Sufficient bone density and volume are required to support a dental implant. If your jawbone has deteriorated due to tooth loss, periodontal disease, or other factors, a bone graft may be necessary to create a stable foundation.
  • Gum health : Healthy gums are crucial for the success of dental implants. Patients with untreated gum disease may need to undergo periodontal treatment before proceeding with implants.
  • Overall health : Good overall health is important for a smooth recovery and successful osseointegration. Patients with chronic conditions, such as diabetes, or those who smoke may have a higher risk of complications and should discuss these factors with their dentist in Royal Oak, MI .
  • Commitment to oral hygiene : Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential for the long-term success of dental implants. Patients must be committed to regular brushing, flossing, and dental checkups.
  • Age: While dental implants are suitable for adults of all ages, younger patients whose jawbones are still developing may need to wait until their bone growth is complete before receiving implants.
