Our Services

At Hill Family Dentistry we are committed to providing access to optimal oral health. We feel that this is done in partnership between our staff and our patients. Our patients can expect that they will not only receive treatment of their dental concerns, but will also receive the education and training they need to maintain their health at home. We feel that your smile is important and we want to work with you to meet all your dental goals.

We Provide the Following:

Digital x-rays including CT scan Oral Hygiene Instruction to maintain healthy smiles Routine cleanings Deep cleanings Sealants Night Guards Space Maintainers Retainers Fillings Crown and Bridge Bleach Trays Veneers Root Canal Therapy Extractions including wisdom teeth Dentures & Partials Nitrous Oxide Implants Laser procedures for Dental & Hygiene Treatment

Insurance and Payment

At Hill Family Dentistry, we do all that we can to make your treatment accessible. We accept the following:

Most PPO insurance plans Select HMO Policies. Some AHCCCS Plans for patients under the age of 21. For our cash patients, we offer a discount plan for individuals and families that reduce the cost of treatment and provide exams, x-rays and regular cleanings at reduced cost.
