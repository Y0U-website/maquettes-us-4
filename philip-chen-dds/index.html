Dr. Philip Chen has practiced general dentistry in Modesto since 2000. His practice provides routine and restorative dental care, with an emphasis on preserving natural teeth when…

The practice

Crowns and similar restorations are designed and made in the office using CEREC, allowing many to be completed in a single appointment without a temporary crown or return visit. Cone beam CT imaging, a three-dimensional X-ray, is used when a more detailed view of a tooth, its roots, or the surrounding bone is helpful. When a condition is better treated by a specialist, the patient is referred.

Dr. Chen earned a BS in Electrical Engineering from UCLA in 1987, an MS in Electrical Engineering from UC Irvine in 1989, and his DDS from the University of the Pacific in 2000.
