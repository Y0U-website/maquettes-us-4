The practice

Dr. Alan Mead, D.D.S., was born and raised in Midland. He graduated from Miami University with a Bachelor’s Degree in Zoology and the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry.

Prior to starting his dental practice in Saginaw, Dr. Mead practiced dentistry with his dad in Midland. In 1998, he began practicing general dentistry in Saginaw.

Dr. Mead and his wife Debra live on a boarding horse farm west of Midland with their two sons, Sean and Jacob. Most mornings and evenings you’ll find Dr. Mead feeding 17 horses, several barn cats, two dogs, and one miniature donkey named Floyd.
