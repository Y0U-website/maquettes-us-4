Get Started

Get Started First Visit We’re excited to welcome you and your family to Zamora Orthodontics! Your first visit is all about getting to know each other. We’ll take the time to review your goals, explain your treatment options, and walk you through what to expect. Our friendly team will make sure you feel comfortable and informed every step of the way.

During this visit, we’ll also take some necessary records, like digital impressions and X-rays, to create a personalized treatment plan just for you. In many cases, we can even get started with treatment the same day—saving you time and getting you one step closer to your new smile. (Some cases may require additional planning before treatment can begin.) Whether you’re here for braces, clear aligners, or a consultation, we’re here to make your orthodontic journey as smooth and enjoyable as possible.

Insurance & Financing

We work with most insurance plans to help make your treatment affordable. During your first visit, we’ll provide a complimentary insurance benefits check to let you know what’s covered and help you plan accordingly.

For added flexibility, we offer financing options through trusted partners to fit your monthly budget. Whether you’re opting for braces or clear aligners, we’ll work with you to find a payment plan that works for your family, ensuring that cost isn’t a barrier to achieving your dream smile.

Patient Forms At Zamora Orthodontics, we value your time and strive to make your experience as convenient as possible. Once your consultation is scheduled, you’ll be provided with all the required patient forms so you can complete them online in advance. This helps ensure a seamless and efficient first visit.
