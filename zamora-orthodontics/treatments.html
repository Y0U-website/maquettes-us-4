Orthodontic Treatments

Treatments All Ages Children Early orthodontic treatment helps create the best foundation for a child’s smile. The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children see an orthodontist by age 7 to check for any potential issues. By starting early, we can address concerns before they become more complex, giving your child the healthiest smile possible.

Teens are at the perfect age to start their smile journey, and we offer treatment options like braces and clear aligners that fit their busy lifestyles. We understand the importance of self-confidence during the teenage years, and we work closely with each teen to ensure their treatment plan is both effective and convenient.

It’s never too late for a beautiful smile! Many adults are discovering the benefits of orthodontics, from clear aligners to discreet braces, and we’re here to guide you through a treatment plan that fits your goals and lifestyle. Whether you’re looking to fix alignment issues, improve bite, or simply enhance your smile, we tailor treatment specifically for your needs.

Braces

Traditional metal braces are still one of the most effective ways to align your teeth. With modern advancements, they are more comfortable and discreet than ever, offering precision and lasting results for a wide range of dental issues. They are also one of the most affordable options, making them a great choice for a variety of patients.

Ceramic braces work similarly to traditional metal braces but use tooth-colored brackets that blend in with your natural teeth. They’re a great option for those who want a more discreet treatment while still benefiting from the proven effectiveness of traditional braces. Ceramic braces are ideal for those seeking a balance between aesthetics and reliable results.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are a discreet, comfortable alternative to traditional braces. These custom-made trays gradually shift your teeth into place and can be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing—making them a convenient choice for busy teens and adults. With no metal brackets or wires, they offer a more subtle and lifestyle-friendly way to achieve a straighter smile.

Retainers

You’ve worked hard for your confident smile, and now it’s time to keep it that way! Retainers are key to maintaining the results of your treatment, ensuring your teeth stay in their perfect position after braces or aligners.

Custom-made to fit your mouth, retainers are designed to be comfortable and easy to wear. We offer removable and permanent retainer options so you can choose what fits your lifestyle and treatment goals best.

Orthognathic Surgery For patients with jaw alignment issues that can’t be corrected with braces alone, orthognathic surgery—also known as corrective jaw surgery—may be recommended. This procedure helps improve chewing, speaking, and breathing while enhancing facial balance and overall function.

We work closely with skilled oral surgeons to create a treatment plan tailored to your needs, combining surgery with orthodontics to achieve lasting, life-changing results.

Our Technology

Digital scanning offers a clean, comfortable alternative to traditional dental impressions. Using advanced imaging technology, we capture precise 3D models of your teeth—eliminating the need for messy molds. This allows us to plan your treatment with greater accuracy, efficiency, and a more comfortable overall experience.

Our digital X-ray system provides high-quality images with minimal radiation exposure. This technology helps us quickly identify any issues, plan your treatment accurately, and track your progress. Plus, the process is faster, with results available immediately.

With digital photography, we capture high-resolution images of your teeth and smile. These images help us monitor your progress and ensure your treatment is on track. It also allows us to explain your treatment plan in greater detail so you feel fully informed.
