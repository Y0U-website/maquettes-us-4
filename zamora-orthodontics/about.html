About Us

About Us Our Mission At Zamora Orthodontics, we’re committed to providing personalized care that transforms smiles and boosts confidence. Our goal is to offer a positive, supportive environment where every patient feels heard, valued, and well taken care of.

With a focus on cutting-edge technology and tailored treatment plans, we aim to help our patients achieve healthy, beautiful smiles that last a lifetime. We’re here to guide you through every step of your orthodontic journey, making it as comfortable and seamless as possible.

Meet Dr. Zamora

Meet the smile behind Zamora Orthodontics—Dr. Kristopher Lee Zamora, a dedicated orthodontist committed to transforming smiles and boosting confidence.

Dr. Zamora takes a patient-centered approach, tailoring every treatment plan to meet individual needs. He focuses on delivering the most effective results while keeping the process comfortable and approachable for all ages. He emphasizes open communication and education, ensuring patients understand every step of their treatment.

Dr. Kris Zamora earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Portland State University and his Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD) from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 2016. After dental school, he served nearly five years as a general dentist in the United States Navy Dental Corps, providing care to Marines and Sailors in South Carolina, California, and Okinawa, Japan. Dr. Zamora then returned to Tufts University School of Dental Medicine for his Certificate in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopedics, before moving to North Carolina to pursue his career in orthodontics.

When he’s not perfecting smiles, Dr. Zamora likes to stay active, play guitar, and travel whenever he gets the chance. He’s originally from Idaho and loves spending quality time with family and friends. Whether it’s helping out at local events or just getting to know people around town, he really enjoys staying connected to the community.

Supporting Belmont and Beyond At Zamora Orthodontics, we’re committed to giving back to Belmont and the surrounding areas, including Mount Holly, Cramerton, and Lake Wylie. We believe in supporting local schools, sports teams, and community events, and we’re always looking for new ways to make a positive impact.

If you have an event or initiative that could use a sponsor or partner, please reach out to our office - We’d love to get involved and help make a difference in the community!
