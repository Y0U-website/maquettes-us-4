Birch Family Dentistry proudly provides comprehensive dental care to patients throughout Bedford, NH and surrounding communities. Our experienced team offers personalized, family-focused treatment in a comfortable environment, making quality dental care convenient and accessible close to home.

Our Dental Services for Families in Bedford, NH & Surrounding Areas

Routine Care

Regular exams, cleanings, and preventive care help maintain healthy teeth and gums—catching issues early and keeping your smile strong and confident for years to come.

Pediatric Dentistry Gentle, kid-friendly care for children of all ages—focusing on prevention, education, and comfort to build a lifetime of healthy dental habits in a fun, safe environment.

Restorative We repair damaged or decayed teeth using fillings, crowns, and more—restoring both function and appearance so you can eat, speak, and smile comfortably again.

Periodontal Therapy We provide periodontal therapy to treat gum disease using thorough cleanings and deep scaling to protect your gums and preserve a healthy smile.

Dentures We provide custom-made full and partial dentures designed for comfort, function, and a natural-looking smile.

Dental Emergencies Dental emergency services offer fast, compassionate care for toothaches, injuries, and urgent dental issues to relieve pain and restore oral health.

Teeth Whitening Our professional teeth whitening treatments remove stains and discoloration, giving you a brighter, more confident smile quickly and safely.

Dental Mouth Guards We provide custom-fitted dental mouth guards to protect your teeth from sports injuries and nighttime grinding while ensuring comfort and lasting protection.

Oral Surgery We provide gentle tooth extractions and minor oral surgery using modern techniques to maximize comfort and support a quick, comfortable recovery.

Endodontics Our endodontic care helps save infected or damaged teeth through gentle root canal therapy, easing pain, removing infection, and protecting your natural smile.
