The Dental Implant Procedure

The dental implant procedure requires a dental surgeon to implant a metal post, usually made of titanium, through the empty tooth socket and into your jawbone below. Once the post has bonded, or osseointegrated, to the bone, your dentist can place an abutment and then a crown atop the post. The crown is made of a prosthetic material that looks just like your natural tooth. Therefore, nobody will ever know it’s not real unless you tell them.

The process to complete a dental implant is lengthy, often 6 to 12 months, because the post needs time to bond with the bone. But once it’s completed, you’re left with a beautiful new addition to your smile. Biting, chewing, and even speaking may become easier as a result.
