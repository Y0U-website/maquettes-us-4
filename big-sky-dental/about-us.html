Jordan - Dental Hygienist Jordan is a Registered Dental Hygienist here at Big Sky Dental. She went to school for Dental Assisting, graduating in 2015. After a few years, Jordan returned to school and graduated from Dental Hygiene in 2022. She practiced as a dental assistant for 6 years, and has 8 years of experience in the dental field. Jordan has a great passion for dentistry and loves all of her patients.

Her hometown is Centerville, a small town southeast of Great Falls. In 2021, Jordan moved to Great Falls to start new adventures with her soon-to-be-husband. In her free time, she loves to hunt, soak up the sun, cuddle her kitties, read, and watch Netflix.
