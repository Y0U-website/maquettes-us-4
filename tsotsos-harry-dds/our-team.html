Dr. Harry Tsotsos is a Long Island native and graduated from John F. Kennedy high school in Bellmore in 1982. Dr. Harry has been a member of the Wantagh community his entire life, as his parents own the Circle M Diner on Wantagh Avenue. Dr. Harry received his Bachelor of Science in biology from Stony Brook University in 1986 and shortly after completed his dental education at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in 1990. Dr. Harry was ranked in the top 10 of his graduating class and was inducted into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, the National Dental Honor Society.

Dr. Harry married Dr. Deborah Messina Tsotsos, a pediatrician in Huntington, NY, in 1993. They have three children, John, Kylie, and Luke. While not in the office. Dr. Harry plays hockey in a men s league, enjoys boating, and grows the best tomatoes on Long Island!
