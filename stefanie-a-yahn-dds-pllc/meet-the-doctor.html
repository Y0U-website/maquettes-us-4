Meet the Doctor

Log In Dr. Stefanie Yahn Dr. Stefanie Yahn was born and raised in Triadelphia, West Virginia. She earned her Bachelors of Science degree in Biology in 2011 from West Virginia University. Dr. Yahn then received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from West Virginia University in 2015.

Dr. Yahn’s focus is on comprehensive dentistry while educating her patients on the importance of oral health and its correlation to total body health. Dr. Yahn is a member of the American Dental Association, the West Virginia Dental Association, the Wheeling District Dental Society, and the Academy of General Dentistry. She also enjoys furthering her knowledge in the ever changing world of dentistry with continuing education classes to better treat her patients. Dr. Yahn serves as the Supervising Dentist for the Ohio County Schools Dental Clinic.

Dr. Stefanie and her husband, Andrew married in 2016 and currently reside in St. Clairsville, OH with their sons, Brooks and Wesley and dogs, Zibby and Oakley. Outside the office you will find Dr. Stefanie walking on trails, shopping at Target, or drinking coffee at a local coffee shop!
