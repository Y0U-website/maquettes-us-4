The practice

At the office of Dr. Stefanie Yahn, we offer complete general

and preventive dentistry to patients of all ages. Dr. Stefanie Yahn

and her team will always provide you with excellent dental care

Dental fillings are used to repair damaged or decayed teeth back to their original state. Dental fillings are important because they prevent further breakdown of the teeth.

Crowns are a full coverage restoration that restore severely damaged teeth. Bridges are another option to replace missing teeth.

Partials are one of the many options available to our patients wanting to replace missing teeth and restore function.

Our office works with dental specialists on restoring dental implants for single tooth and full-mouth restorations.

We offer an in-office whitening and multiple take-home whitening options that can be done in the comfort of your home!
