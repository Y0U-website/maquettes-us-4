Non-Contracted Insurance Plans

Non-Contracted or Out-of-network (OON) means providers and facilities that have not signed a contract with a health plan to provide services. Out-of-network providers may be allowed to bill for the difference between what the plan pays and the full amount charged for a service. This is called balance billing . Surprise billing is an unexpected balance bill. Surprise medical bills could cost thousands of dollars depending on the procedure or service.

*Please note - this is not a complete list of non-contracted insurance plans, these are the most common plans.

Other Non Contracted/ OON Plans not subject to NSA:

Reference Based Plans-- These plans typically have NO NETWORK even possible and have Acceptance Language on the card that says we would accept a low percentage of Medicare (e.g. 120 % of Medicare) for the services. These are not recognized by SWG as Contracted Plans and therefore do not accept or recognize a discount, unless the medical plan can access a contracted wrap PPO network specific for Facilities, (common examples: Cigna, PHCS, or First Health).
