Pre-Placement Treatment Under One Roof

Because we offer comprehensive implant care in-house, you can complete every step of your treatment with the team you trust. Common pre-placement procedures may include:

Tooth Extractions

If any damaged or unsalvageable teeth remain, we can remove them comfortably, often during the same visit, to prepare the area for implants.

Bone Grafting

If jawbone strength has weakened due to tooth loss or gum disease, we may recommend bone grafting to rebuild the foundation needed for implants. This process places a small amount of bone material in the area and allows it to fuse and strengthen naturally.

Guided Tissue Regeneration

In certain cases, we use this advanced technique to promote healthy healing after grafting.

At every stage, you can expect thorough, compassionate care from our highly trained and certified implant team.
