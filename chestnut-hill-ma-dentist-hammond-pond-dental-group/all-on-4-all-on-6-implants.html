How Do All-on-4 and All-on-6 Implants Work?

In the past, replacing a full arch required multiple surgeries and many months of healing. With today’s advanced techniques, All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants make full-mouth restoration more efficient and predictable.

After your implants are placed and healed, our skilled restorative team will attach high-quality crowns , bridges , or dentures , restoring full function and aesthetics to your smile.

Which Option Is Right for You?

The key difference between All-on-4 and All-on-6 implants is simple:

  • All-on-4 uses four implants to support a full arch
  • All-on-6 uses six implants for added strength and stability

Both options provide excellent long-term results. Your dentist will recommend the best choice based on:

You’ll receive a thorough evaluation and customized treatment plan tailored to your goals.

  • Your jawbone density
  • Number of missing teeth
  • Bite alignment
  • Overall oral health
