Experience the epitome of discreet orthodontic treatment with clear aligners, a marvel of modern dentistry. Embrace the confidence of a straighter smile with minimal impact on your daily life.

Retainers

Once your active orthodontic appliances are removed, we will provide a set of upper and lower retainers to stabilize the dental correction. The bone and soft tissues surrounding the teeth require time to stabilize and it is imperative that the retainers are worn as instructed. Failure to wear the retainers may result in undesirable movement of the teeth and could necessitate re-treatment.

Jaw Pain

Our practice is dedicated to addressing jaw pain with a personalized touch, incorporating a range of sophisticated treatments, including:

  • Splint/Nightguard: Beyond traditional approaches, our customized splints and nightguards offer a protective barrier against teeth grinding, providing relief from temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders. Tailored to your unique anatomy, these appliances enhance comfort and contribute to your overall well-being.
  • Therapeutic Botox for Jaw Pain: Dr. Hirsch harnesses the power of therapeutic Botox injections to precisely target and relax specific facial muscles, alleviating tension and discomfort associated with jaw pain. This nuanced approach provides relief while preserving natural facial expressions.
