Advantages of dental implants over dentures or bridges

Improved Comfort Because dental implants are supported by the jawbone, they can provide a stable, secure replacement for missing teeth. Many patients find that implant-supported teeth feel more comfortable and natural than removable dentures.

Enhanced Appearance Because Dental implants are permanent, they look and feel like your own teeth.

Easier Chewing & Eating Unlike sliding dentures that can make eating difficult, dental implants feel & function like your own teeth; therefore, they allow you to eat your favorite foods confidently and without pain.

Improved Speech & Self-Confidence Poor fitting dentures or bridges can sometimes slide or slip within the mouth causing you to mumble or slur your words. Dental implants are permanently positioned like your natural teeth, you will not have to worry about speech problems.

Improved Oral health A tooth-supported bridge usually requires the reduction of other teeth. Since an implant does not require support from nearby teeth, more of your teeth are left intact, improving long-term oral health. Implants also enhance oral hygiene because individual implants allow easier access between teeth.

Longevity Implants are very durable and with proper care, many implants last a lifetime.

Convenience Dental implants eliminate the embarrassing inconvenience of removing dentures, as well as the need for messy adhesives to keep them in place.
