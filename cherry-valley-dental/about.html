Meet the Dentists

Dr. Pooja Bhardwaj

Dr. Pooja Bhardwaj

Dr. Pooja is especially known for her gentle approach and her ability to put even the most anxious patients at ease. She takes the time to listen to her patients’ concerns, explain each step of their treatment with care, and ensure they feel comfortable and informed. Her warm demeanor and genuine compassion help transform dental visits into positive and reassuring experiences.

With latest advances in the field of dentistry, Dr. Pooja is committed to providing top-quality care tailored to each individual’s needs. She stays current with the latest advancements in dentistry through ongoing education and is an active member of Washington State Dental Association and American Dental Association. Dr. Pooja’s goal is to help her patients achieve optimal oral health while being genuinely cared for every step of the way.

Outside of her dental practice, Dr. Pooja loves spending time with her family, watching movies and travel.

Dr. Dipti Soni

Dr. Dipti Soni

Dr. Dipti Soni earned her DDS degree with cum laude honors from the International Dental Program at the University of Nevada. In addition, Dr. Soni completed her undergraduate degree in Dental Surgery and postgraduate degree in Orthodontics from India. Dr. Soni provides patient-focused, high-quality dental care, with an emphasis on ethical practices, building a rapport with patients, and continuous learning.

Outside of her work life, Dr. Soni enjoys spending time with her family and exploring the natural beauty of the Pacific Northwest with her husband and two daughters.
