We assess the accessibility of the Main Street Dental website through ongoing research and external evaluations and implement accessibility improvements accordingly. We have employed experts proficient in accessible web design and development practices to conduct automated and manual testing methods.

Our automated accessibility scan was performed using the ARC Analytics Platform and issues were resolved by 3/20/2025. Updates to the content management system, its plugins, and/or website content after this date have the potential to affect the level of compliance reached.
