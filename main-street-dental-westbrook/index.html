Main Street Dental is committed to providing a website that is accessible to everyone. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website and in doing so adhere to Universal Design principles and version 2.1 of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. Main Street Dental is conformant with WCAG 2.1 Level A.

WCAG is developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) , an international community that develops open standards to ensure the long-term growth of the Web. WCAG 2.1 applies broadly to various current and future Web technologies, and is testable with a combination of automated testing and human evaluation.

Services

Whether you’re due for a routine cleaning or need complete dental care, Main Street Dental is here for you. We combine modern dentistry with friendly, compassionate care to help you feel comfortable and confident in your smile.

Dentures are removable replacements for missing teeth. If you’ve lost all or most of your teeth due to gum disease, tooth decay or injury, dentures can help restore your appearance and preserve your health by allowing you to eat normally.

Implants are an effective way to replace missing teeth because they are permanent and designed to blend in with your other teeth.

Invisalign ® uses a series of custom-made, removable plastic aligners to gradually shift teeth into alignment, functioning as a comfortable alternative to traditional braces.

Dental veneers and tooth bonding are two cosmetic dentistry procedures that can help patients improve the appearance of their smiles.
