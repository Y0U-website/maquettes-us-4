Website Features

Our website has many features intended to make the experience of interacting with our website perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust to those accessing the information contained herein. Addition of accessibility content is required under California Technology Letter 15-05 and can be found on the accessibility section of the CA.gov Webtools website .

Browsers

We design our website to work with the latest version of your browser. This ensures you have the current security technology to keep your information safe.

For the best experience on our website, we recommend you:

  • Use the latest version of your browser
  • Enable cookies and JavaScript
  • Use the latest version of your PDF reader to view, download, and print our forms
  • Delete any personal data your browser may have collected (on a public computer)
