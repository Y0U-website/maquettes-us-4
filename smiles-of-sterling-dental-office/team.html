Meet our dentist…

Dr. Bomi Kim

As a highly experienced general dentist, Dr. Bomi Kim is dedicated to providing exceptional dental care to her patients. Dr. Kim is committed to maintaining the highest standards of excellence in her field and staying up-to-date with the latest advances in dental technology and techniques.

With a strong background in preventive and corrective dental services, crown and bridge works, dentures, endodontic treatments, and oral surgeries, Dr. Kim is skilled in a wide range of procedures. Her core skills include emergency dental work, comprehensive dental treatment planning, cosmetic procedures such as direct composite bonding and porcelain veneers, and pediatric/geriatric dentistry.

Dr. Bomi Kim is passionate about learning new skills and strives to provide her patients with the highest standard of care. She takes great pride in developing comprehensive treatment plans that are tailored to each patient’s unique needs and goals. Dr. Kim is committed to helping her patients achieve optimal oral health and a beautiful smile.

Dr. Bomi Kim earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from West Virginia University School of Dentistry and a Bachelor of Science in Biology from George Mason University. Her education and training have equipped her with the knowledge and skills necessary to provide top-quality dental care.

As an owner dentist at Smiles of Sterling, Dr. Bomi Kim is excited to continue her career as a general dentist and looks forward to serving her patients and community with compassion and skill.
