Frequently asked questions about dental implants

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots made of biocompatible materials placed into the jawbone. They serve as a foundation for dental restorations, such as crowns, bridges, or dentures, to replace missing teeth.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

A comprehensive evaluation is necessary first to determine your eligibility. Pre-existing medical conditions and lifestyle factors may also be considered. If both your oral health and jawbone density are adequate, you might be suitable for dental implants.

Are dental implant procedures painful?

Dental implant procedures are performed under local anesthesia, ensuring minimal discomfort during the surgery. Some post-operative soreness and swelling may occur, but this can be managed with pain medications prescribed by us.
