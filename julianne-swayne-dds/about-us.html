Meet Dr. Julie

One interaction with Dr. Julie Swayne and you will understand why patients come to our practice and stay with our practice for decades. Dr. Julie knows that every patient is someone’s loved one—and she treats them with the kindness and respect she shows to her own family and children.

Dr. Julie is more than just a warm smile. She inspires our team to remain on the cutting edge of dental care and technology, and we travel extensively to learn from the best leaders in the industry. Our team accrues more than 200 hours in training each year, and Dr. Julie has completed continuums at the L.D. Pankey Institute of Advanced Dental Education , Dawson Center for Advanced Training , and Spear Education .

The best reflection of ourselves can be seen in how we treat the people around us. Dr. Julie goes out of her way to ensure that patients who have fears or anxieties about dental care feel calm and comfortable during each treatment. Outside of our practice, she has organized and led the local Give Kids A Smile Program , a national ADA sponsored event donating dental services to under-served children.

A Cincinnati native, Dr. Julie and her husband, Chuck, have two daughters. Along with sharing her time and passion between her practice and family, she also enjoys traveling, playing piano, golfing and gardening.

  • The International Congress of Oral Implantologists Achievement Award recipient
  • Member: American Dental Association

  • Proud community volunteer, wife and mother
