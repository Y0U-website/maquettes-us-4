Protect your child’s teeth with our custom-designed mouth guards.

Mouth guards are recommended to protect your child’s jaw, tongue, cheek lining and teeth during physical activity and participation in sports such as football, soccer and basketball. Mouth guards are made of durable plastic and molded to fit the shape of your teeth. Creating a custom mouth guard is a simple process of taking impressions and fitting the guard.

Please contact us to create a custom-fit mouth guard product for your child’s specific needs.
