Meet Our Team

Dr. Joon Hee Lee

Dr. Lee takes a unique approach to dental care — he genuinely wants the best for your kids and is dedicated to their lasting oral health. You’ll see this in the kind and caring way he interacts with each little patient and through the high-quality, detailed services he provides.

Dr. Lee received his Bachelor of Arts in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at Boston University, graduating Cum Laude and on the Dean’s Honor List. He then attended Tufts University of Dental Medicine in Boston. Following his dental degree, Dr. Lee continued his education in Pediatric Oral Sedation, became a Pediatric Advanced Life Support Provider and is enrolled with Denti-Cal. He is also a member of the ADA, CDA and Harbor Dental Society.

Outside of the office, Dr. Lee enjoys traveling, swimming, diving, soccer and baseball.
