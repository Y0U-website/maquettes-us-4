The practice

You see what’s happening from the start. Using digital scans and detailed imaging, we can identify issues early and guide the right next steps, so your decisions feel informed and straightforward.

You can take care of most of your dental needs in one place with us. If you ever need a specialist, we’ll connect you with trusted partners and help guide your next steps so nothing feels confusing or disconnected.

You want to understand what’s going on and make the right decision for your health, not just for today, but for the long term.

Dr. Clark takes the time to listen and look deeper, focusing on the root of the issue so you can avoid more involved treatment down the road. His approach is shaped by a commitment to continuous learning. He completes advanced training throughout the year to stay current with the latest techniques and technology.

He enjoys what he does, and it shows in how he cares for people. You’ll get clear explanations, modern tools that help you see what’s happening, and a plan that’s built to work for you now and hold up over time.

If your teeth feel crowded, uneven, or your bite doesn’t seem to fit together comfortably, it can affect more than just your appearance. Using detailed digital planning, Dr. Clark guides clear aligner treatment to improve alignment and support better long-term results.

Losing a tooth can change how you chew and place pressure across your bite, often causing nearby teeth to shift over time. We use precise digital planning to place dental implants that restore stability and help your bite function as a complete system again.

When several dental concerns start building up over time, it can feel like everything is connected and harder to manage. Dr. Clark takes a step back, evaluates the full picture with modern diagnostics, and creates a plan focused on restoring long-term balance and function.
