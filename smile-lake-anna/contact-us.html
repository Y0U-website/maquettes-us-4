Frequently Asked Questions

Dental bonding and porcelain veneers are similar cosmetic procedures in that they both improve minor imperfections in your teeth, but there are a few differences. Dental bonding is often a more affordable cosmetic alternative to veneers. They are also applied differently. Bonding uses a special tooth-colored resin material to improve the look of your teeth, while veneers use a porcelain material.

Depending on the type of bonding material used, the length of time that the restoration lasts varies greatly. Most restorations made with composite resin will last anywhere from five to ten years.

If you have minor imperfections such as chips, cracks, or gaps between teeth, dental bonding may be a cost-effective solution to restore your teeth without having to undergo major surgery.

Avoid eating sticky foods such as gum or candy & chewing on hard objects such as ice, which could result in the composite resin breaking down prematurely. It is important to take proper care of your bonded teeth. You should continue with your normal oral health care routine, including daily brushing and flossing. Make sure to schedule regular dental visits and maintain good oral hygiene to keep your healthy smile looking good for many years.
