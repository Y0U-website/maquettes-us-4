How Invisalign Works

Your treatment plan is customized to guide your teeth into alignment step by step.

  • Digital impressions to design your custom aligners
  • Series of aligners worn in sequence
  • Each set worn for about 1–2 weeks
  • Gradual movement toward your ideal smile

Benefits of Invisalign

Invisalign offers more than just aesthetic improvements—it also supports better oral health and daily comfort.

  • Improved Oral Health Easier brushing and flossing compared to braces.
  • No Food Restrictions Remove aligners when eating.
  • Faster Treatment Often completes sooner than traditional braces.
  • Enhanced Confidence Straighten teeth discreetly. Great for: adults and teens

What Can Invisalign Treat?

Invisalign is effective for a wide range of orthodontic concerns, from mild to moderate cases.

Crooked teeth, gaps, overbites, underbites, and crowding can all be improved with Invisalign treatment, helping you achieve a healthier and more balanced smile.

Is Invisalign Right for You?

If you want a discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth, Invisalign may be an excellent option. A consultation will determine if this treatment is right for your smile and goals.
