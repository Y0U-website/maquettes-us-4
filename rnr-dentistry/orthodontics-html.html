How ClearCorrect Works

Instead of guiding your teeth into proper alignment with metal brackets and wires, ClearCorrect achieves the same outcome with clear, custom-made trays. Each one is designed to fit you perfectly and apply just the right amount of pressure to move your teeth a step closer to their ideal positions. Then, the process begins again when you transition to the next set in the series. Since the aligners are basically invisible, you’ll have an unobstructed view of the progress along the way too!

Who Can ClearCorrect Help?

Good news: ClearCorrect has helped patients with crooked teeth, noticeable gaps, and just about everything in between! So, if you are interested in straightening your teeth with clear aligners, don’t hesitate to get in touch with us; there’s a good chance that you’re a candidate. You can also read on to learn about the common problems this discreet orthodontic treatment can correct.

Crowded Teeth

There was once a time when overcrowding could only be addressed with metal braces. That’s no longer the case. ClearCorrect can move your teeth into their properly aligned positions discreetly and comfortably. Plus, the average treatment timeline is only 12-18 months. So, if you’ve been waiting for the day when you could straighten your teeth discreetly (and in a fraction of the time), it’s here!

Gaps Between Teeth

If you have a considerable gap between your two front teeth or several smaller spaces throughout your smile, don’t wait to explore your orthodontic treatment options. After all, even minor gaps can trap food particles and plaque, negatively affecting your oral health in the process. Fortunately, there’s a good chance that ClearCorrect is an option. If we determine that you’re a candidate, then we will have a custom series of aligners made to gently and gradually close the gaps.

Overbite

Do your upper teeth jut out considerably? If so, you might have an overbite, which is an orthodontic problem that can lead to everything from chronic jaw pain to trouble enunciating clearly and chewing properly. The good news is that clear aligners are up for the challenge! If we determine that you’re a candidate, we will use a custom series to gently move your teeth and bite into proper alignment.

Underbite

Maybe you have the opposite problem: your lower teeth sit in front of your upper ones. Rest assured, ClearCorrect may be an option. The best way to find out is by scheduling a consultation with us so we can assess the severity of your case. If we determine that you’re a candidate for clear aligners, then we will create your custom treatment plan and before you know it you’ll be a big step closer to your dream smile!

Crossbite

If you have a crossbite, then you know first-hand that it can lead to chronic jaw pain, chips in your enamel, and trouble chewing properly. Since misalignment usually gets worse with time instead of better, we strongly recommend coming to our office for a consultation. From there, we can determine if you’re a candidate for ClearCorrect and, if you are, take impressions so custom aligners can be made!

Open Bite

Is there a space between your upper and lower teeth that’s preventing you from biting down all the way? If so, you might have an open bite. Unfortunately, like the other bite problems on this list, it can negatively impact your self-esteem, oral health, and overall quality of life if left untreated. So, don’t take the wait and see approach.

Benefits of ClearCorrect

It’s very common for patients to be drawn to clear aligner treatment since it’s so much more discreet than traditional braces. However, that’s not the only reason to consider this orthodontic solution! Below, you will find a few of the many reasons ClearCorrect continues to be so popular.

Faster Average Treatment Timeline

Do you want a straight smile for an upcoming event, like your wedding? If so, you may not have 2-3 years to wait to see results. Fortunately, ClearCorrect has a much faster average treatment time compared to traditional braces: 12-18 months.

If you are interested in getting an estimate of your teeth-straightening journey, schedule a consultation with us! The sooner you do, the sooner we can learn all about your unique dental needs and provide you with an estimate.

Hassle-Free Dental Care

If you had traditional braces when you were younger, you may remember how difficult it was to brush and floss around the brackets and wires. Don’t worry you won’t experience the same difficulties with ClearCorrect . All you have to do is take them off first. Then, you can proceed with your usual routine, clean your trays, and put them back on. No lengthy oral hygiene regimen or special products are required.

No Dietary Restrictions

Another considerable perk of ClearCorrect : there aren’t any dietary restrictions. As your dental team, all we ask is that you do your best to eat mostly healthy, well-balanced meals so your teeth don’t decay and your gums don’t develop an infection. It’s also important to drink lots of water throughout the day to stay hydrated, avoid dry mouth, and prevent stubborn stains from developing.

Improved Comfort

One drawback of traditional braces is that the metal brackets and wires aren’t very comfortable. In fact, soft tissue irritation and mouth sores are both normal and expected. Fortunately, ClearCorrect aligners are metal-free and don’t have bulky components. Rather, they are crafted from smooth materials and custom-made to fit the contours of your teeth perfectly, ensuring your journey to a straighter smile is significantly more comfortable.

Basically Invisible Aligners

To put it simply, ClearCorrect uses a series of see-through plastic aligner trays that gradually and discreetly bring your teeth into perfect alignment. No metal components. No covering your teeth when you laugh. No feeling self-conscious about your smile. In fact, once the aligners are on your teeth, most of the people around you shouldn’t even be able to notice unless they know what to look for.

Living with ClearCorrect Aligners

One of the biggest perks of ClearCorrect is that the aligners are basically invisible. Of course, that’s not the only benefit; they are also removable! As a result, they don’t come with the same rules and restrictions as traditional braces. To stay on-track with your treatment plan, you simply need to:

Wear Your Trays

Naturally, an important part of life with Invisalign is wearing the aligners. More specifically, wearing the aligners for a minimum of 20 hours a day, every day. If you’re not quite sure how close or far you are from the mark, use the stopwatch on your phone as a guide. It’s also important to incorporate healthy habits into your routine, like putting your aligners back on immediately after brushing your teeth.

Clean Your Aligners

Speaking of brushing your teeth you need to take good care of your teeth and your trays throughout your orthodontic treatment! Fortunately, you don’t need any special cleaning products; clean, room-temperature water, a soft-bristled toothbrush, and clear, mild dishwashing liquid should do the trick. If needed, you can also soak your aligners in a mixture of water and the cleaning crystals that came in your welcome kit.

Be Mindful of What You Eat and Drink

Although there aren’t any dietary restrictions with Invisalign, there are certain drinks that increase your risk of tooth decay and certain foods that can break an attachment. That’s why we encourage our patients not to over-indulge on soda, ice cream, hard pretzels, caramel-filled candies, and the like. Instead, fill your plate with healthy, well-balanced meals that will benefit your oral health and keep you full until your next meal.

Avoid Losing or Damaging a Tray

Losing or damaging a tray may not seem like a big deal, but it can quickly cause you to fall off-track with your treatment plan. For this reason, it’s of the utmost importance that you do what you can to avoid finding yourself in this situation, like placing your aligners in their case instead of a napkin. If you do accidentally misplace or break one, call us ASAP so we can determine the best way to proceed.

Attend Routine Check-Ins

Last, but certainly not least, you need to attend progress visits with Dr. Ramona Rivera every 6-8 weeks. These appointments are crucial because they allow her to monitor the movement of your teeth and make sure they are tracking. If they aren’t, then she can make the necessary adjustments to your treatment plan, helping prevent significant delays in the process.

Understanding the Cost of ClearCorrect

ClearCorrect aligners are clear trays that fit over your teeth to gradually improve their alignment, much like traditional braces can. But unlike traditional braces, treatment with ClearCorrect is often much faster and more discreet. If you’re interested in learning more about ClearCorrect to determine if it’s a good value for you or your family, please don’t hesitate to reach out to our friendly office team, or schedule a consultation for more customized information.

Factors that Affect the Cost of ClearCorrect

The cost of ClearCorrect can vary from patient to patient based on several important factors, including:

  • Whether you’re straightening one arch or both. Typically, it is best to straighten both at the same time; however, there are exceptions.
  • The overall complexity of the tooth movements necessary (for example, if you are only correcting a minor gap, you will likely pay less than someone with severely crooked teeth).
  • The proposed length of your treatment (the longer your treatment, the more aligners you will need, and the higher your cost will be).
  • Your compliance with treatment, such as wearing the aligners as prescribed to finish treatment on schedule.

ClearCorrect vs. Mail-In Aligners: Which Costs More?

If all you’re searching for is a quick fix at a low cost, mail in aligners are the clear winner. However, it’s important to remember that mail-in aligners are typically very do-it-yourself and have almost no professional oversight. This means that not only can a lot go wrong during treatment, but you also may not realize there’s a problem until it’s too late. At that point, you’d need to see a professional, adding even more to your total cost and wiping out any savings over ClearCorrect.

With ClearCorrect, you have the benefit of oversight by Dr. Rivera every step of the way, as well as professionally made, highly accurate impressions, and a smoother, more effective treatment process overall. If peace of mind is important, the cost of ClearCorrect is well worth it.

ClearCorrect FAQs

How Long Will I Need to Wear My Aligners Per Day?

You should wear your aligners for a minimum of 20 hours a day. Any less than that and your chances of falling off-track with your treatment plan increase immensely. That’s why we recommend only taking your trays off when it’s absolutely necessary, like when it’s time to eat, and that you put them back on as soon as you’re done brushing your teeth.

Tip: Having trouble wearing your aligners for the recommended amount of time? Try creating a schedule with the help of the stopwatch on your phone. That way, keeping them on for 20 hours a day eventually becomes second nature.

Can You Get ClearCorrect After Braces?

It’s possible, yes! So, if you experienced orthodontic relapse in the months or years after your time with braces, and you want to have a straight smile again, don’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with our dedicated dentist, Dr. Ramona Rivera ! Once she has reviewed your dental history, ensured your teeth and gums are healthy, and assessed the severity of your case, she can determine if you’re a good candidate for ClearCorrect.

What Should I Do if My ClearCorrect Tray Broke?

Let’s start with what you shouldn’t do: you shouldn’t use superglue to piece your aligners back together or move onto the next set in the series unless instructed to do so. Instead, you should call our Fair Oaks dental office right away to share what happened. Once we have all the details and have a chance to see where you are in the teeth-straightening process, we can determine the best way to get you back on-track with your treatment plan.

Will I Need to Wear a Retainer After ClearCorrect Treatment?

Yes! In fact, wearing a retainer is a crucial part of the retention phase. Without it, your teeth could easily move back into their previously misaligned positions, wasting the time and money you invested in ClearCorrect in the process. To prevent that from happening, we will have a custom retainer made and provide you with detailed instructions on how often to wear it and how to take care of it so it lasts.

How Long Does ClearCorrect Take?

Since no two treatment plans are exactly the same, we can’t provide you with an accurate estimate here. We can, however, share that the average treatment timeline with clear aligners is only 12-18 months. Traditional braces, on the other hand, can take well over two years. So, if you have an event coming up that you want to look and feel your best for whether it’s your wedding or a dream vacation don’t hesitate to schedule a consultation with us to see if you’re a candidate for ClearCorrect.
