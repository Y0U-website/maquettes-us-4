Preventative Care

Preventative dentistry can protect your smile from cavities, gum disease, and oral cancer. We educate our patients on the best oral hygiene habits. Our regular check-ups involve a teeth cleaning, digital x-ray examinations, and screenings for tooth infections, gum disease, oral cancer, and temporomandibular joint disorders (TMJ).

Teeth Cleaning

LEARN MORE

Teeth cleanings are recommended every six months to remove hardened plaque and prevent gum disease. During routine teeth cleaning, our team will remove stains, plaque, and tartar before polishing your teeth.

Scaling

LEARN MORE

Scaling removes bacteria and tartar from underneath the gum line. It can prevent further infection and periodontitis.

Root Canals

LEARN MORE

During a root canal, we’ll remove the infected tissue and clean out the inner layer of a tooth, known as the pulp. Once we’ve eliminated bacteria from the canal, we’ll fill and seal the area and bond a dental crown onto the tooth to preserve its strength.
