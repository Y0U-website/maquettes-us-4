Welcome to Pellegrini Dental, a family-owned dental practice providing comprehensive general, cosmetic , and restorative dentistry to Duxbury and the South Shore.

The practice

For more than 30 years, our family has believed that exceptional dentistry is about more than treating teeth. It’s about understanding each patient, taking the time to listen, and creating a personalized approach to long-term oral health.

Led by Dr. Peter Pellegrini and Dr. David Pellegrini , our practice combines advanced dental technology, modern treatment techniques, and a personalized approach to deliver an exceptional experience from your first visit forward.

From preventive care and routine dentistry to cosmetic treatments, dental implants, and complex restorative care , we’re here to help you achieve a healthy, confident smile that lasts.

At Pellegrini Dental, we believe exceptional dentistry starts with getting to know the person behind the smile. For nearly four decades, our family-owned dental practice has served patients in Duxbury and throughout the South Shore with personalized, comprehensive care.

Founded by Dr. David Pellegrini in 1987, Pellegrini Dental has been part of the Duxbury community for generations. Today, Dr. Peter Pellegrini and his father, Dr. David Pellegrini, carry that tradition forward while combining decades of experience with a modern approach to dentistry.

No two patients-or smiles-are alike. We take the time to listen to your concerns, understand your goals, and develop an individualized treatment plan that puts your health, comfort, and priorities first. Our goal is to build lasting relationships with our patients and make every visit a positive experience.

We combine modern dental technology with advanced treatment techniques to provide precise, comfortable, and high-quality care. From digital dentistry and biometric bonding to cosmetic treatments and dental implants, we continually pursue education and technology that can improve the patient experience and treatment outcomes.

Whether you need routine preventative care, cosmetic dentistry, restortative treatment, dental implants, or complex care such as full mouth reconstruction, our team offers a comprehensive range of services to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.
