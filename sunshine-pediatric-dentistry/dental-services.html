Preventive Care

Preventive Care At Sunshine Pediatric Dentistry, we focus on preventive care because it is the foundation of a lifetime of healthy smiles. Starting early and maintaining regular checkups helps children avoid cavities, catch small problems before they become big ones, and build lasting positive habits. The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) also emphasizes that preventive visits should begin with the eruption of the first tooth and continue throughout childhood to promote lifelong oral health.

Fluoride

Fluoride strengthens tooth enamel and makes teeth more resistant to the breakdown that causes cavities. Regular fluoride applications provide an added layer of protection, especially for children who are more prone to decay. The AAPD recognizes professionally applied topical fluoride as a safe and effective way to prevent cavities, and we recommend treatment intervals based on their guidelines and your child’s individual needs.

Dental Sealants

Molars naturally have deep grooves on their chewing surfaces where food and bacteria can get trapped. Even with careful brushing, these areas can be hard to keep clean. Dental sealants provide a simple solution. A thin protective coating is applied to these grooves, sealing them off from repeated exposure to sugars and acids, thereby reducing the risk of cavities. The AAPD recommends sealants for permanent molars because they are proven to significantly lower cavity rates in children.

Radiographs (X-rays)

X-rays allow us to see what the eye cannot, such as cavities between teeth, bone health, eruption patterns, or possible trauma. They are an important tool for ensuring your child’s teeth and jaws are developing properly. We use the latest digital X-ray technology with extremely low exposure. Following AAPD guidelines, we only recommend X-rays when they are necessary for diagnosis or monitoring your child’s growth.

Guidelines referenced are based on the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry (AAPD) Policy on Preventive Pediatric Oral Health Care, 2023.

Fluoride

Teeth are composed of many tiny pieces called molecules that attach to each other. These tiny pieces are constantly breaking apart throughout the day and reforming depending on our diet. Fluoride can be thought of as a glue for these pieces by keeping the molecules more tightly bound together and less prone to breaking apart. Topical fluoride treatment is effective in protecting children’s teeth and reducing cavities.

Dental Sealants

Think of a tooth’s top (chewing) surface as mountains with many grooves. When your child eats, food gets caught in those grooves. Although daily brushing is essential, applying a dental sealant will enhance the ability to keep those hard-to-reach areas clean. A dental sealant flows into the grooves, sealing the tooth and reducing the probability of cavities.

Radiographs (X-rays)

Radiographs (or X-Rays) are an essential part of our diagnostic protocol. Without them, dental conditions may go undetected. Dental X-Rays detect much more than dental cavities, and they allow the dentist to evaluate growth and development, bone health, pathology, and trauma. At Sunshine Pediatric Dentistry, we use the latest dental X-ray technology with very minimal exposure.

Restorative Care Restorative dental care focuses on repairing and preserving the health and function of your child’s teeth. These treatments address dental decay, injuries, or developmental issues, helping children regain their smiles and maintain optimal oral health.

Tooth Colored Fillings

Should your child have a cavity, we use a resin tooth-colored filling to restore function and comfort to your child’s tooth. These fillings restore teeth to an attractive, healthy state!

Pulpotomy / Nerve treatment

A pulpotomy is performed when decay reaches the pulp, the soft tissue inside a baby tooth. The diseased pulp in the crown is removed, a medicament is placed to protect the remaining healthy root pulp, and the tooth is restored with a crown. This stops bacterial progression, preserves the tooth’s function, and maintains space for the permanent tooth.

Extractions

Extractions may be needed due to infection, decay, trauma, or for orthodontic purposes. Our doctors review each child’s situation carefully and discuss all treatment options with parents before proceeding.

Space Maintenance

Baby teeth act as placeholders for permanent teeth. Losing a baby tooth too early can cause neighboring teeth to drift into the vacant space, potentially leading to orthodontic issues later on. A space maintainer holds the space open, giving the adult tooth room to erupt in the correct position. We offer different types of space maintainers based on your child’s dental development and the specifics of their case, ensuring the best solution for each child.

Restorative dental care for children focuses on repairing and preserving the health and function of their teeth. These treatments aim to treat dental decay, injuries, or developmental issues, allowing children to regain their beautiful smiles and maintain optimal oral health.

Crowns

If your child’s cavity is too large to restore with a tooth-colored filling, a crown may be recommended. Crowns help protect the baby teeth, providing structure for the tooth. Crowning a tooth also maintains the space, allowing the eventual permanent tooth to erupt properly. If you are concerned about a silver crown, talk to our doctors about the possibility of a tooth-colored crown!

Baby Root Canals

Baby root canals are often needed when the cavity approaches the tooth’s nerve. This procedure stops the progress of the cavity and minimizes the chances of an infection or abscess. The procedure is completed by cleaning out the center of the tooth where the nerve lives and placing soothing medicine. The tooth is then protected by placing a crown.

Space Maintenance

The baby teeth serve as space holders for the permanent teeth. With the loss of a baby tooth, neighboring teeth start migrating into that vacant space, potentially causing problems as your child grows. This loss may lead to the need for extensive orthodontic treatment at a later age. A space maintainer is placed in that area to hold the space open so that the adult tooth can erupt in a more appropriate position.

Extractions

Your child may need to have a tooth extracted. Most often, extractions are indicated due to infection or for orthodontic purposes. Our doctors will review your child’s needs and discuss treatment options with you.
