MORE ABOUT DENTAL IMPLANTS Frequently Asked Questions What are dental implants made of? They are made of titanium, and the bone cells are able to grow and fuse to them.

  • What types of teeth can be replaced using dental implants? They can be used to replace any of your natural teeth, whether it is one or several that are missing.
  • What are the advantages of dental implants? They are not only aesthetically pleasing, they: • Replace the tooth and fill the hole the tooth root occupied • Stimulate the bone and stop bone loss • Prevent the necessity of having other teeth cut down to build bridges or crowns • Are biocompatible
  • What kinds of dental implants are there? The most common are ’root form’ implants that are screwed into your jawbone. These now have a proven track record for tooth replacement and have been used predictably and successfully for decades.
  • How long do dental implants last? This will depend on the specific placement and design of the implant, the physiology of your mouth, gums and jaw, and how well you maintain them. Some implants have lasted up to thirty years.
  • How old do you have to be to consider dental implants? Patients need all of their permanent teeth to have come in before implants can be used.
