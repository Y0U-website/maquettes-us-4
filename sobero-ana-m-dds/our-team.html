Our Team Dr. Ana Sobero

Dr. Ana Sobero is a member of the California Dental Association, she is also a member of the American Dental Association and the Tri-County Dental Association which includes the Los Angeles, San Bernardino, and Riverside counties. Dr. Sobero has been practicing Dentistry in the Coachella Valley for more than eleven years, this is also her place of residency in Southern California. Desert Family Dental Office is located in Cathedral City, California

Dr. Ana Sobero has an excellent background and reputation in the management of patients with complex medical histories, complex dental infections, and emergency treatment. She has also received many certifications related to this area. Many of the patients she works with in her private practice in Cathedral City, come from Palm Springs, Palm Desert, La Quinta, Rancho Mirage, Indio, Desert Hot Springs, Bermuda Dunes, Thousand Palms as well as from the Riverside, Los Angeles, and San Bernardino counties.
