Dental Implants FAQs

Yes, dental implants are considered a permanent solution for replacing missing teeth. They are designed to integrate with your bone, providing a stable and durable foundation that can last for many years with proper care.

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime with the appropriate care and maintenance. This includes regular dental check-ups, good oral hygiene practices such as brushing and flossing and avoiding habits that could damage the implants.

The implant procedure is typically associated with minimal discomfort, as local anesthesia is used to numb the area during the surgery. Post-operative pain can be managed with prescribed medications, and most patients report that any discomfort is comparable to that of a routine tooth extraction.

Yes, dental implants can be used to replace multiple teeth, whether they are adjacent to each other or scattered across the mouth. Dentists can use implants to support bridges or dentures, offering a versatile solution for those in need of multiple replacements.

Caring for dental implants involves maintaining good oral hygiene by brushing twice a day, flossing daily, and using an antibacterial mouthwash. Regular dental check-ups and cleanings are also crucial to monitor the health of your implants and surrounding tissues. Avoiding habits like smoking and using teeth for non-eating activities will also help ensure the longevity of your implants.
