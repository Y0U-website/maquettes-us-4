Do you accept patients without dental insurance? Yes, we absolutely welcome patients without dental insurance. At Vibrant Smiles of Holland, we believe everyone deserves access to high-quality dentistry - whether you have dental insurance or not.

We are an out-of-network dental practice, which means we are not contracted with insurance companies. However, we gladly accept insurance benefits and will submit claims on your behalf so you can receive any available reimbursement directly from your plan.

Many patients choose our office because they value personalized care, advanced treatment options, and a team that prioritizes health over insurance restrictions.
