What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants involve the placement of a titanium post into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. These teeth implants are beneficial for long-term results because they integrate with the jawbone and can support a permanent restoration such as a crown , bridge , or implant-supported dentures.

Each implant is custom-designed based on the patient’s unique needs. We’ll evaluate the teeth that need to be replaced, then recommend an ideal treatment plan for your smile goals. At St. John Premier Dental, our experienced implant dentist creates permanent, stable restorations that you can rely on for many years.

Single Tooth Implants

We can complete a single tooth replacement without damaging the nearby teeth. The dental implant post holds a crown on top for the tooth restoration.

Multiple Teeth

A few teeth can be replaced using a dental implant and a bridge. Or several dental implants with crowns can be used if the missing teeth are in different parts of the mouth.

Full Dental Implants

Multiple implants can be placed to hold a full smile restoration. For example, 4 or 6 dental implants can hold permanent dentures in place.

When Are Dental Implants Recommended?

Dental implants are often recommended after tooth extraction for patients who want long-lasting restorations. In order to be a good candidate for a tooth implant, you must have sufficient jawbone density to support the implant post.

Talk to our dental implant doctors to see if dental implants are right for you. We will schedule a consultation to evaluate your bone health, gum conditions, and overall oral health. Some of the most common reasons we recommend implants include:

A single tooth needs to be extracted and replaced.

Multiple teeth are missing in the same area of the mouth, and an implant is needed to secure a bridge.

Dental restorations are failing, so implants can be a secure alternative to repeated repairs in the future.

The bone density needs to be preserved after tooth loss.

A patient wants more permanent restorations instead of wearing traditional dentures every day.

Benefits of Dental Implants

The benefits of permanent dental implants are undeniable, especially when you compare other types of restorative treatments that are available. Here are some of the benefits that you can expect if you choose implants for your dental reconstruction treatment:

Dental implants are secured into the bone directly, which offers a strong foundation for your dental restorations. This reduces the movement of dental restorations throughout the day.

The titanium post stimulates the bone and reduces the risk of bone density loss. When bone density is preserved, it can help to maintain the shape of the face and jaw.

Dental implants fill in the gaps to improve the overall appearance of your smile. Custom dental restorations can be designed to match the color and shape of the remaining natural teeth.

With the right oral health habits, it’s possible for dental implants to last for the rest of your life.

Unlike removable prosthetics (such as traditional dentures), dental implants are secured in place and don’t need to be removed at night for cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants can last for decades and even a lifetime. They must be maintained with consistent oral hygiene and regular dental visits.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

No, you won’t feel any pain during the dental implant procedure because we use local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Most patients experience mild discomfort for a few days after the procedure.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

In order to be a good candidate for dental implants, you must have healthy gums and sufficient bone support.

Can dental implants replace all missing teeth?

Yes, dental implants can be custom-designed to replace as many teeth as needed. Full dental implants can support dentures for a complete smile restoration.
