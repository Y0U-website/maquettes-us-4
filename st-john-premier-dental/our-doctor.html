Meet Our Dentist

Dr. Peter Straka

Dr. Straka is a seasoned dentist with over half a decade worth of experience, training and rigorous coursework focusing on multiple areas of dentistry from pediatrics to geriatrics. He has earned his Doctor of Medicine in Dentistry from Case Western Reserve University School of Dental Medicine in Cleveland, Ohio and completed his General Practice Residency at the Richard L. Roudebush VA Medical Center in Indianapolis, Indiana.

Coming from a large family history of dentists and medical providers, Dr. Straka considers it a calling to serve others. He prides himself on an experience to deliver both quality and care while catering to each patients’ fears, anxieties, and needs. Thus, no expense has been spared to develop and build a contemporary environment where patients’ worries are left at the door.

In addition to his professional experience, Dr. Straka is a current member of the United States Army National Guard Wisconsin with 7 years of service and 1 deployment to Kuwait in 2023. He currently holds the rank of O3 Captain. It is his goal to meet the needs of veterans by giving back to charitable organizations and pro bono work through his practice.

When out of the office, Dr. Straka’s spends time enjoying many of his hobbies: photography, soccer, golf, fitness, traveling, culinary arts, music events, and educational classes. He cherishes his time spent with family, his 5 God children and growing deeper in his faith through the Catholic archdioceses of Chicago.

Do you accept new patients?

Yes, we are currently accepting new patients at St. John Premier Dental. We welcome patients of all ages and look forward to serving you and your family.

What services do you provide?

We offer a comprehensive range of dental services including preventive care, cleanings, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, restorations, Invisalign, oral sedation, and more. Dr. Straka and our team are equipped to handle both routine and advanced dental needs.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend scheduling a checkup and professional cleaning every six months. Patients with specific oral health conditions may benefit from more frequent visits based on their individual needs.
