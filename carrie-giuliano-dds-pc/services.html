Cosmetic Dentistry . This can be anything done to correct imperfections in the appearance of the mouth. Anyone who is unhappy with their smile can have it fixed. The color, alignment, spacing as well as regularity of the teeth are the characteristics that give the overall appearance. Any of these can be repaired to give a stunning look to the mouth.

We offer a wide variety of cosmetic options. Some of these options are whitening, smile design, recontouring of the shape of the teeth, veneers, bonding, all ceramic/porcelain crowns, and two orthodontic options to straighten your teeth.

Whitening .

This is the procedure of making teeth whiter, and therefore more attractive. Our office uses in office whitening, at home tray whitening, and professional at home whitening srips.

ZOOM! is a revolutionary tooth whitening procedure. It’s safe, effective and in just over an hour, your teeth will be dramatically whiter. The convenience of ZOOM! in comparison to days of using strips or wearing trays makes it the perfect choice for the busy individual. The ZOOM! whitening procedure is simple. It begins with a short preparation to isolate your lips and gums. The clinician then applies the whitening gel that is left on your teeth for fifteen minutes.

The clinician will then remove the gel and apply fresh gel for another fifteen minute interval. Patients usually require 3 intervals. Teeth typically become at least six to ten shades whiter, sometimes more. A fluoride treatment completes the procedure. You’ll be amazed with the results. In most cases, teeth get even whiter the first few days after the procedure. Trays with gel are recommended afterward for maximum whitening and maintenance.

The tray method involves having impressions taken from which laboratory fabricated custom vinyl trays are made. A hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide gel is placed in these trays and the trays with gel are worn for about 30-45 minutes. The entire process takes approximately 2 weeks of daily use. The tray method is recommended for badly stained teeth, such as tetracycline staining, and it may take several months of use to see significant color improvement. For maintenance of whitened teeth the trays should be used every several months.

Veneers . Veneers are a dental procedure in which a covering is placed over the outside (visible area) of the tooth. Veneers are usually only done to the part of the teeth that are visible when talking or smiling. The procedure can be direct or indirect.

The direct technique usually involves placing composite resin on the outside of the tooth using bonding. This method is usually referred to as bonding.

The indirect technique usually involves two appointments because the veneers will be fabricated at a dental laboratory.

At the first appointment the teeth are prepared, impressions taken, and the teeth are given a temporary covering. In two to three weeks the veneers are back from the laboratory, the temporaries are removed and the veneers are bonded to the teeth. The laboratory fabricated veneers are usually made using porcelain or pressed ceramic, and are very esthetic.

The advantage of veneers versus crowns is that much less tooth material is removed, and the procedure is generally less uncomfortable. Veneers are recommended for teeth that have large fillings or little tooth structure.

CBCT and iTero scanners . Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) scanners provide three-dimensional imaging, allowing dental professionals to capture detailed views of the teeth, bones, and surrounding structures. This level of detail is crucial for accurate diagnosis and treatment planning, particularly in complex cases involving implants, orthodontics, and endodontics.

iTero scanners, on the other hand, are digital impression systems that replace traditional, messy dental molds with precise digital scans. The iTero scanner quickly captures detailed 3D images of the patient’s oral cavity, enabling dentists to create accurate digital impressions for crowns, bridges, aligners, and other restorations.
