Our Mission

At Overhill Dental, we believe that dentistry should be accessible to everyone. Our mission is to help our patients and others in our community understand that regular dental care is just as important as a physical at your doctor’s office. Dr. Alexaundrea Smith has dedicated her life to caring for patients and bringing awareness of dental health issues to communities throughout the globe through local and global outreach programs. All of us at Overhill Dental hope to inspire and encourage others to achieve their dreams and to be a source of hope and optimism to every patient we meet.

Giving Back is Our Passion

Dr. Smith has made it her mission to continue to help others develop their skills and reach their goals by serving as an example of excellence in the community. She provides mentorship to local high school students, future doctors studying at USC, and other leaders in the field of dentistry so she can help equip the next generation with the tools and resources they need to succeed. Helping support and lift those around her strengthens the community and paves the way for future generations to achieve their dreams of success. ‍ ‍ ‍ Contact us to learn more about mentoring with Dr. Smith.
