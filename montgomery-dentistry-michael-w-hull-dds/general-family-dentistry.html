General & Family Dentistry

Dental Bridges

If you have lost a tooth because of injury, decay, or periodontal disease, then Dr. Hull can restore it with a dental bridge. This restoration attaches over the teeth adjacent to a gap in order to stay firmly in place. Schedule an appointment with our dentist at Montgomery Dentistry if you want to receive a dental bridge in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Dental Crowns

We can cover weak, misshapen, or broken teeth when you come to Montgomery Dentistry for a dental crown. This restoration protects the living tissues inside of a tooth and renews structure that was lost due to decay or injury. Call Dr. Hull if you think that you may need to receive a dental crown in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Restorative Dentistry

If you have cracked, broken, or missing teeth, then come to Montgomery Dentistry for our restorative dentistry. Dr. Hull provides several dental restorations that renew the strength and structure of your smile. Call us today if you want to learn more about what we can do with our restorative dentistry in Cincinnati, Ohio.

TMJ Treatment

If you suffer from the headaches and pain of a popping jaw, then see us for TMJ treatment. Dr. Hull is trained to help the temporomandibular joints (TMJ) function smoothly so you can have relief. Call Montgomery Dentistry if you want to learn more about how we provide TMJ treatment in Cincinnati, Ohio.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

When a cavity develops in your smile, visit Dr. Hull to have it repaired with a dental filling. We use materials that match the color of your teeth so your family and friends won’t realize that you have had dental work completed. Call Montgomery Dentistry today if you want us to repair a cavity with a tooth-colored dental filling in Cincinnati, Ohio.
