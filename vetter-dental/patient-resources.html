What to Expect at Your First Visit

When you Arrive

Upon parking in our spacious car lot, our front-desk team warmly checks you in and ensures you feel right at home. Relax in our comfortable waiting area as we assist you with any remaining paperwork and provide an overview of what to expect during your first visit.

New Patient Exam

Dr. Vetter and our hygienist prioritize understanding your primary concerns, engaging in a thorough discussion before examining your teeth and gums to identify any issues. For those who haven’t visited the dentist recently, we may conduct x-rays and include an oral cancer screening for comprehensive care.

Treatment Plan

Following the exam, Dr. Vetter personally reviews our findings and discusses ideal options for your dental care. He provides recommendations, cost estimates, and collaborates with you to create a tailored treatment plan, ensuring all your questions are addressed.

Financing & Insurance

At Vetter Dental, we understand that financial considerations play a role in your dental care decisions. We offer flexible financing options to ensure you receive the treatment you need without compromising your budget. Our team is dedicated to helping you navigate the insurance process, maximizing your benefits and providing transparency throughout your dental journey. Feel free to contact us to discuss your financing and insurance options, and let us work together to achieve your optimal oral health.

We have third party financing options through Proceed Finance, CareCredit, and Cherry. If you would like to discuss financial arrangements with one of our team members prior to treatment, please call 701.235.7322.
