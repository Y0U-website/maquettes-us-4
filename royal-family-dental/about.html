Committed To Dental Excellence

From the first minute you walk into Royal Family Dental in Cañon City, Colorado, you can be sure to expect genuine, compassionate, and respectful treatment from the entire staff. Even if it’s been a while since your last dental cleaning, we are a no-judgment zone. We build our patient relationships on integrity and kindness while looking forward to providing you with dental care throughout your life.

Our exceptional team loves making a difference in patients’ lives. Our goal is to put a smile in your life – one that looks and feels great. At our modern and comfortable facility, you’ll find all the comprehensive smile care you need, from dental cleanings to full-mouth restoration with dental implants.

The team at Royal Family Dental loves helping their patients get back on track with their oral health, as well as working in a fun and engaging environment with patients of all ages. When you visit our friendly office, our caring and experienced dentists take the time to get to know you and thoroughly understand your oral health needs and smile goals before providing you with personalized treatment.

We work hard to maintain a friendly, positive atmosphere in which you can feel at ease, and we utilize the latest dental technologies to enhance your comfort. Dr. Michael Darais, DDS and his team of dentists, assistants, and office personnel work hard to provide you a welcoming, calm and enjoyable dental experience. Dr. Darais, DDS has hand-picked a phenomenal team whose top priority is to give you your best dental experience.

Michael Darais

Doctor of Dental Surgery

Dr. Michael Darais was born in Provo, Utah spending most of his time outdoors, hiking, biking or fishing up Provo Canyon. During high school, Dr. Darais was awarded multiple times in art contests where he excelled in architectural designs and renderings, pottery and woodworking, which would later spark his interest in dentistry.

Dr. Darais is passionate about dentistry and is very detail-oriented, caring and loves his patients. He enjoys a profession that allows him to grow and constantly improve in patient care. Dr. Darais prides himself in knowing that our office provides only the best treatment to all of our patients, and is constantly seeking ways in which to improve our office and our care for our patients.

Aside from dentistry, Dr. Darais enjoys mountain biking, camping, skiing, dirt biking, spending time with his family and working on building his custom motorcycle. Dr. Darais looks forward to meeting you and hopes to serve you and your family with all of your dental care needs.
