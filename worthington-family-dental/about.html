Our Story

Worthington Family Dental has established itself as a cornerstone of dental care in our community. Built on a foundation of trust, clinical excellence, and genuine patient relationships, our practice has evolved to meet the changing needs of families throughout the Worthington area.

Our dedicated team brings years of collective experience and an unwavering commitment to evidence-based care. We’ve cultivated a reputation for combining advanced dental techniques with the personal attention that makes every patient feel valued and comfortable.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to the principles that have guided us from the beginning: providing exceptional care, building lasting relationships, and serving as trusted partners in our patients’ oral health journeys.

Please note: Our practice was previously known as Worthington Dental Associates, lead by Dr. Howard Spector. Same trusted location and team, same commitment to excellence, with a renewed focus on comprehensive family care.
