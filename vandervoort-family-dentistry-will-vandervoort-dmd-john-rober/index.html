A lasting, natural-feeling replacement for missing teeth — planned with precision, placed with care.

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone, where they fuse with the bone over time and act as a stable foundation for a custom crown. Unlike a bridge, an implant doesn’t rely on neighboring teeth for support — the replaced tooth stands on its own, looks completely natural, and functions like the real thing.

Implants can replace a single missing tooth, several teeth, or serve as anchors for a full arch restoration. We use cone beam CT imaging and digital intraoral scanning on every case to plan placement with accuracy — so we know exactly what we’re working with before the day of treatment.

Dr. Will is credentialed to provide IV sedation in-house, which means most implant procedures can be completed comfortably at our Tuscaloosa office without outside referrals. From your first consultation through the final crown, you stay with a team that knows your case.

A dental implant consists of a titanium post, an abutment, and a custom crown — all working together as a complete tooth replacement.
