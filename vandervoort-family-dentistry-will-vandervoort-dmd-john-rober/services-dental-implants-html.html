What to Expect with Dental Implant Treatment

Implant treatment takes place over several months. Here’s a clear picture of the general timeline and what each stage involves.

Consultation & Planning

We take a cone beam CT scan to evaluate your jawbone from every angle and confirm you’re a good candidate. If bone grafting or a sinus lift is needed, we’ll discuss it here. You’ll see the same images and understand the full plan before we schedule anything.

Implant Placement & Healing

The titanium post is placed into your jawbone. Over the next four to six months, it fuses with the bone — a process called osseointegration. Sutures, if necessary, are removed about two weeks after placement, and we’ll check in to confirm integration before moving to the next stage.

Final Crown Placement

Once integration is verified, we take digital scans to fabricate your crown. About three weeks later, your custom crown is placed — and you leave with a fully functional, natural-looking tooth. Total treatment typically runs five to six months from implant placement to final restoration.
