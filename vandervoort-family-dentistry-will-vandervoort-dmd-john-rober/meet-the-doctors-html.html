Meet the Doctors

Three dentists. One standard. Dr. Will Vandervoort, Dr. John Roberson, and Dr. William Maitre share a commitment to honest, skilled care and the kind of relationships that make patients want to come back.

Three dentists. One standard.

Dr. William M. Vandervoort

Dr. William M. Vandervoort was born and raised in Anniston, Alabama. He completed his undergraduate studies with honors at The University of Alabama in 2010 with a Bachelor of Science in Biology and was inducted into the Phi Beta Kappa Honor Society. In 2014, he graduated from The University of Alabama School of Dentistry, receiving his Doctorate of Dental Medicine.

After dental school, Dr. Will completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency program at The Foundry Dental Center in Bessemer, Alabama. Through the residency program, he received advanced training in IV sedation, oral surgery, implant placement and restoration, prosthodontics, endodontics, and restorative and preventive dentistry.

Dr. Will is an active member of the American and Alabama Dental Associations, the Academy of General Dentistry, and Tuscaloosa Spear Dental Study Club, and was a graduate of the Chamber of Commerce of West Alabama Leadership Tuscaloosa 2016 2017 class.

The best part about being a dentist for Dr. Will is the relationship aspect of working with both his patients and team members. He is a people person and enjoys the interaction. He also enjoys the life-changing aspect of dentistry when he gets to help restore patients to health.

Dr. Will and his wife, Nancy, have three daughters, Virginia, Julia, and Elizabeth. When he’s not caring for patients, he enjoys spending time with family and friends, hunting, fishing, exercising, sports, and most anything outdoors.

Dr. John Roberson

Dr. John S. Roberson was born and raised in Birmingham, Alabama. He attended the University of Alabama where he received a Bachelor of Science degree in Biology in 2017 with cum laude honors. He then went to the University of Alabama School of Dentistry at UAB and graduated in 2021 with a Doctorate of Dental Medicine (DMD).

Dr. John enjoys dentistry because he loves for his work to genuinely benefit people and values the everyday interaction he gets to have with his patients. He loves the opportunity to consistently learn and improve in both knowledge and skill as he grows in his profession. Dr. John recognizes dentistry is incredibly rewarding in that he gets to help restore health, function, and esthetics for a patient, and he always cares to make the patient feel at ease and motivated about their oral health.

Beyond the dental office, Dr. John most enjoys spending time with family and friends. Dr. John also enjoys house projects, exercising, playing sports, watching movies, cooking, and anything Alabama sports.

Dr. William H. Maitre

Dr. William H. Maitre was born and raised in Mobile, Alabama. He attended the University of Alabama for his undergraduate education and obtained his dental degree from The University of Alabama School of Dentistry. Dr. Maitre went on to complete an Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) residency program at The Foundry Dental Center in Bessemer, AL. During his residency program, he developed a strong foundation in comprehensive restorative care and dental implants.

Dr. Maitre is passionate about delivering high-quality dental care to his patients and stays current through continuing education in areas including implant prosthetics and advanced restorative techniques.

Outside of the office, Dr. Maitre is an avid outdoorsman with a particular passion for turkey hunting, having chased birds across the Southeast and well beyond Alabama’s borders. He also enjoys fishing and spending time in God’s creation. Grounded in his faith, he lives in Tuscaloosa with his wife, AnnaMarie, and the two are eagerly expecting their first child.
