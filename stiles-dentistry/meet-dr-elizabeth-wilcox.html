Elizabeth Wilcox DDS

Dr. Wilcox is excited to return to the dental practice where she first began her journey in dentistry. Having previously interned at our office for one year before attending dental school, she is excited to reconnect with the team and contribute her expertise to the care of our patients.

Dr. Elizabeth earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Maryland Dental School, followed by a residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry at Roseman College of Dentistry in Salt Lake City. She then joined a practice in Utah, which allowed her to gain valuable experience and further hone her skills.

We are thrilled to have Dr. Wilcox back in Maryland, where she’s eager to reconnect with our community. She is known for her compassionate care and dedication to providing excellent patient care, and we are confident she will continue to build strong relationships with our patients and their families.

We would like all of you to help us welcome back Dr. Elizabeth Wilcox to our dental family as she joins in the tradition of providing excellent dental care to the people we are privileged to serve.
