Implant Dentistry

Dental implants are small titanium or ceramic posts that act as artificial tooth roots . Once placed into the jawbone, they fuse naturally with the bone over time, providing a strong foundation for a crown, bridge, or even full denture. This integration helps restore both the look and function of your teeth — as if they were never missing at all.

Whether you’re replacing a single tooth or stabilizing a full arch, implants are the gold standard in modern restorative dentistry.

The Dental Implant Process at Roseroot Dental

Comprehensive Consultation

We’ll evaluate your oral health, review your goals, and determine if you’re a good candidate. If bone loss or gum disease is present, we may recommend preparatory treatments like bone grafting or periodontal care.

Precision Planning

Using our in-office CT scanner, we take a full 3D image of your mouth and jaw. This allows us to plan your implant placement with millimeter-level accuracy for optimal comfort and longevity.

Implant Placement

The implant post is gently placed into the jawbone. Over the next few months, it fuses with your bone in a natural process called osseointegration.

Custom Restoration

Once healing is complete, a lifelike crown (or bridge/denture) is attached to the implant — restoring both function and appearance.
