Invisalign offers a modern, nearly invisible solution for aligning your teeth and correcting bite issues. Unlike traditional metal braces, Invisalign uses a series of clear, custom-made aligners crafted from smooth, flexible, BPA-free plastic. These aligners gently and gradually shift your teeth into their ideal positions, giving you a confident, radiant smile without the discomfort or appearance of metal brackets and wires.

Invisalign effectively treats a wide range of orthodontic concerns, including:

• Crooked or crowded teeth • Widely spaced teeth • Overbite, underbite, crossbite, and open bite

Many patients also choose Invisalign for cosmetic reasons to achieve a generally straighter, healthier smile.

• Discreet Appearance Clear aligners are virtually invisible, letting you straighten your teeth without drawing attention. • Unmatched Comfort Smooth, flexible plastic fits snugly over your teeth, avoiding the irritation often caused by metal braces. • Convenience and Freedom Invisalign aligners are removable, so you can enjoy your favorite foods, maintain your regular oral hygiene routine with ease, and never have to worry about dietary restrictions or special cleaning tools.

At Roseroot Dental, we begin by using advanced digital scanning technology to create a precise 3D model of your teeth—no uncomfortable molds needed. This model helps us design a personalized treatment plan that maps out the gradual movements needed to perfect your smile.

You’ll receive a series of aligners, each worn for about two weeks , applying gentle, consistent pressure to shift your teeth step-by-step. Most patients wear their aligners 22 hours a day , removing them only to eat, brush, and floss.

As you progress through the series, your teeth move closer to ideal alignment until your treatment is complete — resulting in a beautiful, natural-looking smile you’ll love to show off.

Space Maintainers: Protecting Your Child’s Smile as They Grow

When a child loses a baby tooth earlier than expected — whether from decay, trauma, or extraction — the surrounding teeth can begin to shift into the empty space. This may block the path for the adult tooth that’s still developing underneath, potentially leading to crowding, misalignment, or more complex orthodontic issues later on. That’s where space maintainers come in.

Space maintainers are small custom-made dental devices designed to hold the space open after the premature loss of a baby tooth. They ensure that the permanent tooth has enough room to erupt naturally into its proper place.

• Guide Proper Tooth Eruption Without a space maintainer, neighboring teeth may drift into the empty space, causing problems for the erupting adult tooth. • Prevent Future Orthodontic Problems By maintaining proper spacing, these devices help minimize the need for braces or extractions later on. • Support Healthy Oral Development They help ensure the jaw, bite, and alignment develop as intended during a child’s critical growth years.

We offer different types of space maintainers based on your child’s needs: • Fixed (non-removable) Cemented to adjacent teeth — ideal for younger children who may misplace or forget removable devices. • Removable Similar to a retainer, and usually recommended for older children who can manage their care. All our space maintainers are custom-made to ensure comfort, proper fit, and effectiveness.

At Roseroot Dental, we understand how important it is to set the foundation for a healthy adult smile early on. If your child loses a tooth early, Dr. Roseroot and our caring team will assess their smile and determine whether a space maintainer is necessary. We’ll design and place the device quickly and gently, and monitor it over time to ensure it’s doing its job — and that your child is comfortable and confident.
