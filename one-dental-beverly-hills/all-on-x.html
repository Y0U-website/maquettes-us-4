All-on-X Full Arch Solutions: A Revolutionary Approach to Complete Smile Restoration

At One Dental, we are excited to offer All-on-X dental implant solutions, a groundbreaking treatment designed to restore full arches of teeth with fewer implants. This innovative technique uses four to six strategically placed implants to support a complete arch of fixed, natural-looking teeth. Unlike traditional dental implants, which often require an implant for every missing tooth, All-on-X minimizes surgical procedures, reduces recovery time, and provides a permanent, secure alternative to dentures.

If you’re struggling with extensive tooth loss, ill-fitting dentures, or significant jawbone deterioration, All-on-X may be the perfect solution for you. While a single dental implant is suitable for replacing one or a few teeth, All-on-X is tailored for individuals who need a full-arch restoration. The best way to determine whether this is the right option is through a thorough consultation with our expert team at One Dental, where we will assess your oral health and customize a treatment plan to meet your needs.
