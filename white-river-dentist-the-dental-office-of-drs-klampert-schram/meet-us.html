Dentists

Richard G. Schramm, DMD Originally from Worcester, MA, Rick graduated from Assumption College with a BA in Natural Science, and later from Boston University Dental School with a DMD degree. He moved to Vermont in 1987, the same year he married his wife Maria. He worked in Woodstock, VT and White River Junction, VT for ten years before partnering with Mark in 1998.

Rick and Maria have two children, Alex and Mikayla. Rick enjoys spending time with his wife and children, and also likes camping, fishing, and being outdoors. He is happy to serve the community one smile at a time!
