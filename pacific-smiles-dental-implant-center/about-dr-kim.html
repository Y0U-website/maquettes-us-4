About Dr. Kim

Meet The Expert

Fred J. Kim, DDS, FAAID

Dr. Fred J. Kim received his Doctorate in Dental Surgery from Loma Linda University School of Dentistry in 2003. Being a long term native of the South Bay area along with his desire to be closer to family and friends, it only seemed natural that he return to the area for private practice.

Striving to provide his patients the highest quality of dental care Dr. Kim has received over 1000 hours of additional training in all phases of comprehensive and cosmetic dentistry including advanced smile design and adult cosmetic orthodontics.

Desiring to provide his patients the highest level of Implant Dentistry, Dr. Kim has had extensive advanced training in the surgical placement and restoration of dental implants. He is one of only 400 Dentists Board Certified in Implant Dentistry and has been designated a Diplomate by the American Board of Oral Implantology/Implant Dentistry . This designation symbolizes the highest level of competency in Implant Dentistry.

Mission and Values
