Meet The Expert

Marc J. Tully, DDS

Marc J. Tully, DMD is passionate about patient education and comfort while using the latest technology and conservative treatments to restore and maintain his patients’ beautiful smiles.

Dr. Tully attended the University of Massachusetts Amherst receiving an undergraduate degree in psychology. From here, he received his Doctor of Dental Medicine at Tufts University School of Dental Medicine in Boston, Massachusetts. After earning his dental degree, Dr. Tully completed a comprehensive residency program at the University Of Maryland School Of Dentistry.

Dr. Tully is affiliated with numerous professional associations, including the American Dental Association, California Dental Association and the Academy of General Dentistry. He is constantly attending continuing education courses to keep him up to date with the most cuttingedge techniques to deliver the best results for his patients.

If Dr. Tully is not improving his patient’s smiles or continuing his education to advance his techniques, you’ll find him spending time with his family, immersing himself in music, and enjoying the outdoors.

Meet The Expert

Fred J. Kim, DDS, FAAID
