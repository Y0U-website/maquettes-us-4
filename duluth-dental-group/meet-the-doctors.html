Meet the Doctors

Ken Cheng, DDS, MS, FAGD

Dr. Ken Cheng is one of the skilled cosmetic and general dentists on the team at Duluth Dental Group. Dr. Cheng is passionate about helping patients improve their oral health and achieve beautiful smiles. He loves to build personal relationships with his patients and treats the person, not just the tooth. Dr. Cheng works hard to address the concerns of each of his patients at the office in Duluth, Georgia.

Dr. Cheng is a Gwinnett County native who graduated from Norcross High School. In 2005, he received his undergraduate degree in biomedical engineering at Georgia Tech, currently ranked No. 1 in the country for that program at that time. He then went on to receive his master&#x27;s degree two years later.

With a passion for helping people and serving his community, Dr. Cheng moved forward with his education, receiving his dental degree at Columbia University in New York City, one of only three Ivy League dental schools in the country. He has extensive training in virtually all aspects of general dentistry, including endodontics, oral surgery and implant and cosmetic dentistry.

As a lifelong learner, Dr. Cheng has continued to stay up to date in all fields of dentistry, notably implant dentistry and endodontics. Most importantly, he enjoys spending time with his family — Dr. Chan Chan, his wife, who also practices dentistry, their two beautiful daughters, Zoey and Bailey, and fur baby, Mojo!

Chan Chan, DDS

Dr. Chan Chan grew up in Macon, Georgia. In 2010, she graduated from the prestigious New York University, where she received her dental degree. Dr. Chan is diligent in her continual pursuit of personal and professional growth. She maintains active memberships in the American Dental Association®, Georgia Dental Association and Academy of General Dentistry.

In the rare moments they are away from the office, they love to travel! Most importantly, they enjoy spending time with their two beautiful daughters, Zoey and Bailey, and fur baby, Mojo!
