ClearCorrect

  • Removable trays allow you to eat, brush and floss without restriction
  • Smooth, comfortable plastic with no metal brackets or wires
  • Fewer office visits compared to traditional braces
  • Predictable results with a customized digital treatment plan

Conditions ClearCorrect Can Treat

ClearCorrect aligners can address a wide range of orthodontic concerns, including:

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbite, underbite and crossbite
  • Generally crooked or misaligned teeth

The ClearCorrect Process

Your ClearCorrect journey begins with a consultation at our office. Dr. Ken Cheng or Dr. Chan Chan will evaluate your teeth and determine whether clear aligners are a good fit for your needs. If ClearCorrect is right for you, we will take digital impressions of your teeth to create a precise 3D treatment plan. You will be able to see a preview of your expected results before treatment even begins.

Once your custom aligners are fabricated, you will wear each set for about two weeks, removing them only to eat, drink, brush and floss. You will visit our office periodically so we can monitor your progress and provide your next sets of aligners. Most treatment plans are completed within 12 to 18 months, though your timeline may vary depending on the complexity of your case.

Is ClearCorrect Right for You?

ClearCorrect is an excellent option for patients who want to straighten their teeth without the look and feel of traditional metal braces.
