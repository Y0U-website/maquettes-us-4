The practice

Founded in 1976 by Dr. Alan Barton, Oakhill Dentistry provides comprehensive dental services in Petaluma, CA. Since our beginning, our dental practice has been dedicated to helping patients of all ages achieve healthy, bright smiles. As a family owned practice, we understand the importance of receiving reliable, trustworthy care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Together with our dental healthcare team, Dr. Scott Jereb provides patients with a wide range of treatment options including dental cleanings, composite fillings, dental implants, root canals, veneers, dentures, and more. Our focus is on providing quality dental care for every member of your family. We work closely with our patients to develop individualized treatment plans, that not only improve…

Oakhill Dentistry serves Petaluma, CA and the surrounding areas including, Cotati, Rohnert Park, Penngrove, Sebastopol, Santa Rosa, Windsor, Healdsburg, Sonoma, Glen Ellen, Novato, San Rafael, and many other communities.
