Dental Implants

Dental implants are modern and highly effective solutions for replacing missing teeth. They are considered the gold standard in tooth replacement because they offer a permanent, durable, and natural-looking solution that mimics the structure and function of natural teeth.

A dental implant is a small, screw-shaped titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone to serve as a replacement root for a missing tooth.

What To Expect During Treatment

The dental implant procedure typically involves several steps. First, a thorough examination and assessment are conducted to determine the suitability of the patient for implant placement.

After the implant is placed, a healing period of several months is usually required to allow osseointegration to occur. During this time, the jawbone integrates with the implant, providing a solid foundation for the restoration. In certain cases, a temporary crown or bridge may be placed to maintain aesthetics and function during the healing process.

Once the healing period is complete, an abutment is attached to the implant. The abutment serves as a connector between the implant and the final restoration. Impressions of the abutment and surrounding teeth are taken to create a customized dental crown or bridge that will match the color, shape, and size of the patient s natural teeth. The final restoration is then securely attached to the abutment, resulting in a fully functional and aesthetically pleasing replacement tooth.
