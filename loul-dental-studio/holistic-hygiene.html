Your hygiene visit, step by step

Pre-Visit Medical Review

We start by reviewing what’s changed since your last visit — health updates, new medications, life changes that might affect your oral health. Small details often matter more than patients expect.

Comprehensive Evaluation

A thorough look at gums, teeth, soft tissues, bite, and airway — not just a quick scan. Digital wellness mapping documents your mouth’s state for your records and ongoing care planning.

Personalised Cleaning

The cleaning itself is paced for thoroughness, not speed. We use biocompatible products calibrated to your specific needs — adjusting for sensitivity, previous restorations, and your home care patterns.

Ozone Final Rinse

An ozonated water rinse provides antimicrobial closure without harsh chemicals — reducing bacterial load while supporting the healthy oral microbiome you want to maintain.

Plan for Next Visit

We discuss frequency (some patients benefit from quarterly visits, others from semi-annual), specific areas to focus on at home, and any developments we want to monitor. Care is personalised and ongoing.
