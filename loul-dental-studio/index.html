Services

The cleaning itself is paced for thoroughness, not speed. We use biocompatible products calibrated to your specific needs — adjusting for sensitivity, previous restorations, and your home care patterns.

Once integration is confirmed, your custom ceramic crown is attached — milled in our studio and carefully matched to your surrounding teeth. The result is a permanent, natural-looking tooth that is indistinguishable from the real thing.

For patients seeking a metal-free alternative, we offer ceramic (zirconia) dental implants — delivering the same lifetime durability as titanium with none of the metal.
