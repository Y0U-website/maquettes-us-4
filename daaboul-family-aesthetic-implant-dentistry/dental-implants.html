(817) 488-7885 DENTAL IMPLANTS FOR A LASTING SMILE What are Dental Implants? A dental implant is a modern tooth replacement solution that uses a small titanium post placed in the jawbone to act as a replacement tooth root. Once the implant integrates with the surrounding bone over a few months, a custom crown is attached to fully restore the function and appearance of a natural tooth. Unlike bridges or removable dentures, implants are anchored directly to the jaw, providing unmatched stability and a feel that closely mimics natural teeth.

Depending on your needs, implants can replace a single missing tooth, support a multi tooth bridge, or anchor a full denture for added stability. They are often recommended for patients who want a permanent, secure alternative to traditional options. With proper care, dental implants can last for decades and protect oral health by preventing bone loss.

Who Needs Dental Implants?

Dental implants are an excellent option for patients missing one, several, or all of their teeth who want a permanent, secure, and natural feeling replacement. They are especially beneficial if you have lost teeth to injury, decay, or extraction, or if you want to upgrade from a removable denture or bridge to a more stable, long lasting solution.

You may also be a strong candidate if you have noticed bone loss in your jaw, if your current dentures slip or cause discomfort, or if you are frustrated with the limits of removable options. Unlike other tooth replacements, implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent further bone loss. For many patients, this upgrade delivers lasting comfort, restored function, and a smile that feels and acts like the real thing.
