Welcome to Quarry Hill Family Dental

When building her professional team at Quarry Hill Family Dental, Dr. Jarrett’s focus was to find individuals who share both her passion for providing quality dentistry and enjoy the rewards of building a trusting relationship with each patient.

The entire team understands the vision Dr. Jarrett has for her practice and works together to provide a seamless, stress-free visit for you every time you visit. We look forward to meeting you and welcoming you to our dental practice in person. Until then, please take a moment to get to know our dedicated team.

Dr. Molly Jarrett

Dr. Molly Jarrett was born and raised in Rochester, just minutes from the office. As a child, she spent many hours in our very building, which was once home to the veterinary practice run by her father, Dr. Gary Noser.

After earning her undergraduate degree from Concordia University, St. Paul, Dr. Jarrett completed her dental training at The University of Iowa College of Dentistry, where she learned from top specialists in the field. During her time there, she developed a strong passion for patient-centered care, particularly helping patients feel at ease and building trust through compassionate dentistry.

Dr. Jarrett continually advances her clinical skills through professional development, including a pediatric mini-residency at the University of Minnesota, Spear study clubs and hand-on workshops, and many other ongoing continuing education classes.. She is a member of the American Dental Association, Minnesota Dental Association, and Zumbro Valley Dental Association.

Outside of the office, she enjoys spending time with her husband, Nick, and their five young children. They love hiking through Quarry Hill Park and exploring Minnesota’s state parks. In the summer, you might catch her sneaking in a quick nine holes of golf in the evening.

McKenzie , RDH

McKenzie joined Quarry Hill Family Dental in January 2020 as our first full-time dental hygienist, bringing over 13 years of experience most of it in a small, single-doctor practice like Quarry Hill Family Dental. She shares Dr. Jarrett’s passion for delivering exceptional, patient-centered care in a comfortable setting.

Originally from Wisconsin, McKenzie earned her Bachelor of Science degree in Dental Hygiene from Minnesota State University, Mankato. After spending 11 years in Colorado, she and her husband returned to Minnesota to raise their two daughters closer to family.

Brittany , RDH
