Meet the Team

Meet our experienced team of dental professionals! Each Restoration Dental Studio team member brings a unique blend of skill, compassion, and expertise to ensure that you receive the highest level of care. Our team is passionate about not only improving your oral health, but also building lasting relationships with every patient and crafting unique, positive experiences.

Julie

Julie

Dental Hygienist

Julie has served Restoration Dental Studio as a Dental Hygienist since our doors first opened in 2020. She has been a hygienist for 30 years and holds a variety of certifications, including her EFDA, anesthesia permit, nitrous oxide certification, and many others. Julie originally earned her Dental Hygiene degree from Indiana University Northwest.

Julie says that her favorite parts of her job include making her patients comfortable and being with loved ones each day. She says, “I love helping people overcome their fear of dentistry. I also love working with the kids and creating a fun and happy environment for them! And of course I love getting to come to work every day and be with my daughter, Kallie, and my daughter-in-law, Allison.”

Outside of work, you can find Julie enjoying the great outdoors! She enjoys going out on her boat, and also loves spending time with her granddaughter.

Taylor

Taylor

Dental Hygienist

Taylor has served Restoration Dental Studio as a Dental Hygienist since 2023. She joined our team shortly after earning her Dental Hygiene degree from Indiana University Northwest, where she graduated at the top of her class with highest distinction after serving as class president. She has earned many additional certifications during her time with RDS, including her EFDA, allowing her to expand her scope of care to better serve patients.

Taylor says that her favorite part of her job as a hygienist is forming authentic connections. She says, “Unfortunately, it’s well known that many find trips to the dentist intimidating due to negative past experiences. Because of this, I value our welcoming environment, especially our comfort amenities. I strive for all of my patients to leave feeling well cared for and listened to, and my greatest hope is to have my patients look forward to their dental cleanings and prioritize their health.”

Outside of work, you can find Taylor spending quality time with her cats, her mini goldendoodles, and her husband. She enjoys undertaking home improvement and DIY projects, as well as decorating.

Allison

Allison

Expanded Functions Dental Assistant

Allison has served Restoration Dental Studio as a Dental Assistant since 2021. During her time at our office, she has earned her EFDA certification after originally starting her journey at the front desk and growing into a clinical role. In addition to her many dental certifications, Allison holds a Health Studies degree from Purdue Northwest.

Allison’s favorite parts of her job include placing fillings, picking shades for crowns, and getting to see patients’ reactions to their new smiles. She says, “I like to make people laugh and feel comfortable with us!”

Outside of work, you can find Allison working out, shopping for home decor, spending time with friends and family, and relaxing with her golden retriever, Theo.

Kallie

Kallie

Treatment Coordinator

Kallie has served Restoration Dental Studio as our Treatment Coordinator since 2021, joining the team shortly after RDS opened its doors. With over 5 years of experience in the dental field, Kallie has supported the office in several positions from assisting to administrative support. She holds a degree in Community Health Science from Indiana University.

Kallie says that her favorite part of her job is helping the general public learn about their own dental health in a positive light. She says, “What I value most is the opportunity to educate patients on their dental health I find it fulfilling to empower individuals with knowledge, helping them make informed decisions about their individual care. I also enjoy being able to learn about things our patients are looking forward to when they leave our office, whether it’s a vacation, wedding, or graduation, I just love to build those personal connections!”

Outside of work, Kallie loves relaxing, enjoying the great outdoors, and spending time with her German Shepherd, Zara.
