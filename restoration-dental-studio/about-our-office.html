Office Features

Our current office layout features eight private treatment suites, including three added during our 2023 expansion. Each room is designed for privacy and comfort, with sliding pocket doors, dual TVs placed on the wall and the ceiling above treatment chairs, and noise-canceling headphones available for use during treatment. Patients can also enjoy their choice of music or podcasts through speakers in every room.

What Sets us Apart from Other Dental Offices?

At Restoration Dental Studio, we take pride in offering unique dental experiences tailored to our patients. Here’s what sets us apart:

State-of-the-Art Technology:

We stay at the forefront of digital dentistry with tools like the CEREC® machine for same-day restorations, the Primeprint® 3D printing system for custom nightguards and dental models, and Primescan® for advanced imaging. Our Guided Biofilm Therapy® cleanings provide a precise, minimally invasive way to keep teeth healthy and comfortable.

Comprehensive Preventative Care for All Ages:

Preventative care is the foundation of our practice, and we happily provide care for patients of all ages. We offer services like routine dental check-ups, advanced oral cancer screenings, low-radiation x-rays, and fluoride treatments to help patients maintain long-term oral health.

Personalized, Compassionate Care:

Every patient is unique, and we focus on creating customized treatment plans in a welcoming environment.
