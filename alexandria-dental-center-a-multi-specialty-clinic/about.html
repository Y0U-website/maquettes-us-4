Laser-Assisted Gum Therapy & Dental Procedures At Alexandria Dental Center , we utilize advanced laser technology to support precise, minimally invasive dental and periodontal care. Laser-assisted treatments allow our clinicians to address gum disease and soft-tissue concerns with improved comfort, accuracy, and healing compared to traditional methods in appropriate cases.

Laser dentistry may be used as part of comprehensive periodontal therapy, gum treatments, and select soft-tissue procedures. This technology enables targeted treatment of diseased tissue while preserving healthy surrounding structures.

Benefits of laser-assisted dental care may include:

Reduced bleeding and swelling Enhanced patient comfort Minimized need for sutures Faster healing and recovery Improved precision during treatment

Laser-assisted therapy is not a replacement for all traditional procedures, but when clinically appropriate, it can be an effective adjunct to scaling and root planning, periodontal maintenance, and minor soft-tissue surgical procedures.

Our team carefully evaluates each patient to determine whether laser-assisted treatment is suitable as part of their personalized care plan.

Laser dentistry is performed based on individual diagnosis and clinical indication and is integrated into evidence-based treatment planning.

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) Therapy At Alexandria Dental Center , we incorporate Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) therapy as part of select surgical and regenerative dental procedures. PRF is a natural, autologous biomaterial derived from a small sample of the patient’s own blood and processed without additives.

PRF contains a concentrated matrix of platelets, white blood cells, and growth factors that may support the body’s natural healing response. When clinically appropriate, PRF can be used to enhance soft-tissue healing and support bone regeneration in procedures such as extractions, implant placement, bone grafting, and periodontal surgery.

Potential benefits of PRF therapy may include:

Enhanced healing and tissue regeneration Reduced post-operative discomfort and swelling Improved wound stability Support for bone and soft-tissue integration Use of the patient’s own natural biologic material

PRF therapy is not required for every procedure and is recommended based on individual clinical needs. Our providers carefully evaluate each case to determine whether PRF may be a beneficial adjunct to treatment.

PRF therapy is used as an adjunctive treatment and outcomes may vary based on individual patient factors and clinical conditions.

Dental Implant Surgery & Guided Implant Placement At Alexandria Dental Center , dental implant surgery is performed to replace missing teeth with stable, long-term restorations that support function, comfort, and oral health. Dental implants are titanium posts placed into the jawbone to serve as foundations for crowns, bridges, or implant-supported dentures.

When clinically appropriate, guided implant placement may be used as part of the surgical process. This approach utilizes digital imaging and treatment planning to help determine optimal implant position based on anatomy, bone structure, and restorative goals. In select cases, a custom surgical guide may be used to enhance accuracy and predictability during implant placement.

Dental implant surgery is carefully planned on an individual basis and may be coordinated with periodontal care, bone grafting, PRF therapy, or laser-assisted procedures when indicated.

Dental implant treatment at our practice may include:

Single-tooth implant replacement Multiple-tooth implant restorations Implant-supported bridges Implant-supported dentures Digitally guided implant placement Implant maintenance and follow-up care

Dental implant surgery and guided placement are not appropriate for every patient. Our providers conduct comprehensive evaluations to determine candidacy and develop personalized treatment plans based on clinical needs.

Advanced technologies such as guided implant planning, PRF therapy, and laser-assisted care may be incorporated to support precision and healing when clinically appropriate.
