Dental Implants in Fort Lee, NJ

We use digital radiography to carefully plan for the optimal size of implants. The implants are then surgically placed into the bone. A collagen membrane and healing cap is placed on the top of the implant for approximately three months. This is known as the healing period, and it allows the implants to osseointegrate (fuse to your existing bone) and the gum tissue to heal.

The next portion of the procedure is the restorative phase, at which point the implant is exposed and a post called an abutment is screwed into the implant. At 201 Dental Care, we only place custom abutments that are fabricated to precise specs, ensuring the prosthetic’s precise fit. An impression is then taken and sent to our team, who will fabricate a custom crown or bridge that will be attached to the abutment. Once completed, your mouth will be restored to natural form with strong, beautiful teeth!

Implant Dentures

In circumstances where many replacement teeth are needed, we offer implant dentures. Implant dentures are very similar in form to traditional dentures; however, they are stabilized by dental implants. Very often, denture wearers complain that dentures become loose and “float” around the mouth, limiting one’s ability to eat and speak comfortably. Implant-supported dentures are a cost-effective solution, and in many cases, we can actually retrofit an existing denture with implants.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dental Implants

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are permanent tooth replacement solutions that mimic the structure of natural teeth. They consist of a titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone, a connector called an abutment and an implant restoration. This restoration could be a single crown, a bridge or a complete or partial denture, depending on your needs. Implants are durable, look natural and restore full function for chewing, speaking and maintaining jaw health.

Who is a good candidate for dental implants?

Ideal candidates for dental implants are individuals with healthy gums, sufficient jawbone density and no untreated oral health issues. Non-smokers and those committed to good oral hygiene typically achieve the best outcomes. Our dentist will evaluate your oral health and medical history to determine if implants are the right choice.

How long does the dental implant process take?

The implant process can take several months. After placing the implant post, the jawbone needs time to heal and integrate with the post, which may take three to six months. Once healed, the abutment and crown are attached, completing the restoration. Our experienced dentist will guide you through each step.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for dental implants is similar to caring for natural teeth. Brush twice daily, floss regularly and attend routine dental checkups . Avoid chewing on hard objects and maintain a balanced diet. Proper oral hygiene and professional cleanings will ensure your implants stay healthy and last for many years.
