Nitrous Oxide

Our team understands that you would probably rather be anywhere else than in a dental office receiving treatment. Millions of people feel the same way, but we have found that gentle nitrous oxide is usually all that’s needed to help our patients relax and get the care they need.

Also called inhalation sedation, we deliver nitrous oxide through a small nasal mask. Just breathe normally, and in a few moments, you will feel very relaxed, dreamy, and slightly detached from all the activity around you. However, you will remain fully conscious and able to respond to questions from your team.

Nitrous oxide has no lingering effects, and when we finish treatment, we’ll switch to pure oxygen for a few minutes that will leave you feeling alert and refreshed. You don’t need someone to drive you to and from the office, and you will be able to go on with your usual routine when you leave our office.

Electric Handpieces

Receiving dental care is a challenge for many people, so at Schultz Center for Dentistry, we’re always looking for new ways to streamline your visits and improve your comfort while still delivering world-class dental care. Our electric handpieces are stronger than air-driven handpieces because a motor is driving the bur instead of air.

This technology allows Dr. Schultz to remove decay quickly and efficiently and minimize the time you have to spend in the dental chair. Electric handpieces are quieter and also provide a level of precision that’s not possible with air-driven tools.

Rotary Endodontics

If you experience anxiety when you think about root canal treatment, you’re not alone! But at Schultz Center for Dentistry, we do everything possible to provide you with a comfortable, stress-free procedure, and rotary endodontics help us do that.

Using rotary endodontics, Dr. Schultz performs efficient root canal treatment, and that means you have to spend less time in the dental chair. We can also achieve more desirable outcomes for our patients because rotary endodontic technology cleans the root canals more thoroughly than traditional endodontic tools.
