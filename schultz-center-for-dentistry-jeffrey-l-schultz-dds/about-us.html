Meet the Team at Schultz Center For Dentistry

Today, more and more people are concerned about how their smile affects their appearance. Using the most current techniques and dental technology, our cosmetic dentist Dr. Jeffrey Schultz treats patients who have a wide range of dental needs and helps them achieve the smile they deserve.

The mission statement for our practice is as follows: Dr. Schultz and his staff are committed to building a lasting dental care relationship you can trust. They take this statement to heart and are dedicated to giving their patients first-rate service throughout their lives.

From initial contact through continued treatment, Dr. Schultz and his team will work to educate you, to accommodate you, and to exceed your expectations. They understand the value of a patient’s trust and intend to earn that trust through attentive, personalized service.

Please take a look at our website and then contact us for an appointment. We would love to hear from you. We are a referral-based practice and appreciate meeting your friends and family.

Dr. Jeffrey Schultz

Dr. Schultz is committed to an ethical, conservative approach to dentistry. He firmly believes in patient education, personalized service, and long-term patient relationships. He strives to stay up-to-date with all the changes occurring in dentistry and has taken several hundred hours of continuing education courses. This dedication has earned Dr. Schultz the prestigious Fellowship Award from the Academy of General Dentistry, one of the most rigorous continuing education awards offered today.

When Dr. Schultz is not working, he enjoys spending time with his wife Shelly and daughter Haley. He enjoys jogging, golfing, biking, tennis, hunting, and fishing.
