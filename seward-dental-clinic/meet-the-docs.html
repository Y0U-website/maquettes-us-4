Meet The Docs

Matt Kunasek, D.D.S.

Dr. Matt Kunasek became interested in dentistry through his positive experiences with his dentist, Dr. Larry Smith, and his orthodontist, Dr. Robert Schoettger. In fact, Dr. Smith recruited him in to the practice, noting his own plans to retire coincided with Kunasek’s graduation from dental school. Growing up on a farm between Bee and Dwight, Nebraska, he also knew he wanted to settle in a small town close to family, thus making the Seward Dental Clinic the perfect fit.

Dr. Kunasek graduated from East Butler High School and was accepted in to the Rural Health Opportunities Program (RHOP) through Wayne State College where he earned a degree in Health Science in 2001. He graduated from the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry in 2004 and immediately joined the Seward Dental Clinic. Dr. Kunasek is a member of the American Dental Association and the Nebraska Dental Association.

According to Dr. Kunasek the Seward Dental Clinic is special because they treat patients like family, while offering customized dental care and treatments.

“We also have a great staff that really cares and tries to provide a comfortable experience for our patients,” said Kunasek.

Dr. Kunasek’s wife, Kate, is a hygienist who previously worked at the clinic until 2013 when she became a stay-at-home mom to their children – Michael, Jonathan, Audrey and James. In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with family as well as being outdoors hunting, fishing and helping on his parent’s farm.

Andrew Hibbert, D.D.S.

Dr. Andrew Hibbert grew up in nearby Beatrice, Nebraska, where he developed an appreciation for small-town values and the importance of building lasting relationships. He knew he wanted to raise his own family in a close-knit community, and Seward has been the perfect place to do just that.

After earning his bachelor’s degree from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in 2009, Dr. Hibbert received his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry in 2013. He joined Seward Dental Clinic shortly after graduation and is now a partner at the practice, where he is proud to serve generations of families in the Seward community. He is a member of both the American Dental Association and the Nebraska Dental Association.

Dr. Hibbert believes exceptional dentistry is about more than healthy smiles—it’s about helping patients feel confident, comfortable, and cared for every step of the way. He enjoys continuing to learn and invest in modern techniques and technology, allowing Seward Dental Clinic to offer advanced treatment options—including dental implants, same-day crowns, and clear aligner therapy—while maintaining the personalized care of a hometown practice.

Outside the office, Andrew enjoys spending time with his wife, Natasha, who serves as the Marketing & Community Relations Coordinator for Seward Dental Clinic. Together they have three children—Braelyn, Broderick, and Brielle. As a family, they enjoy traveling, making memories together, cheering on the Nebraska Cornhuskers, and spending countless hours at the gym and ball fields. Whether he’s coaching a youth team or cheering from the sidelines, Andrew loves being involved in his children’s activities and the Seward community.

Andrew is grateful for the opportunity to care for the families of Seward and the surrounding communities and looks forward to welcoming new patients for years to come.

Jake Wiesen, D.D.S.

Dr. Jake Wiesen is a proud Seward High School graduate and is excited to return to his hometown to care for the community he grew up in. He values the close-knit relationships that come with practicing in a small community and is passionate about providing personalized, hometown care to every patient.

Dr. Wiesen earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree with honors from the University of Nebraska Medical Center College of Dentistry. He enjoys combining modern dentistry and the latest advancements in dental care with a comfortable, patient-centered approach. His goal is to help every patient feel confident, comfortable, and well cared for throughout their dental experience.

Outside the office, Jake enjoys spending time with his wife, Kenzie, and their son, Myles. As a family, they enjoy spending time outdoors and making memories together.
