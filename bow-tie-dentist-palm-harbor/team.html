Holly

Originally from New York, Holly has called the Palm Harbor area home since middle school. After graduating from St. Petersburg College, she began her rewarding career as a dental hygienist and has enjoyed every minute of helping others lead healthier lives through preventive dental care.

Holly worked alongside Dr. Wunderlich for many years and has been a valued member of the practice for more than 36 years. Over the decades, she has built lasting relationships with patients and their families, many of whom she has cared for across multiple generations. She enjoys educating patients on good oral health habits and is passionate about helping them maintain healthy teeth and gums for a lifetime.

When she is not at work, Holly enjoys playing tennis, cycling, boating, traveling, and spending time with her husband and family.

Mary

Originally from Stamford, Connecticut, Mary has worked in the dental field for more than 30 years, serving patients throughout Pasco and Pinellas counties. One of the major influences on her decision to become a dental hygienist was a family member who owned a dental laboratory in her hometown, sparking an early appreciation for the dental profession.

Mary worked alongside Dr. Wunderlich for many years and has been a valued member of the practice for more than 25 years. Fluent in Greek, she is known for her warm personality, strong work ethic, and compassionate approach to patient care. She is dedicated to helping patients feel comfortable and ensuring they have a positive experience at every visit.

A proud mother of two and stepmother of seven, Mary treasures time spent with her family. Outside the office, she enjoys exercising, gardening, and staying active outdoors.
