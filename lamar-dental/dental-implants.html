How Does It Work?

The dental implant process is different for everyone based on individual needs and the number of implants required. For example, the approach our team takes varies based on whether you need a single tooth implant or a full set of implants.

Here’s a typical overview of how the procedure works:

  • The journey starts with an initial evaluation of your oral health, where the dentist discusses your goals and may take X-rays or 3D images to plan your treatment.
  • Then, we create a personalized plan based on your evaluation, determining how many implants you need and the condition of your jawbone. At times, an extraction may be needed to prepare for the implant.
  • An appointment is then made for the initial implant placement with an oral surgeon.
  • Next, the oral surgeon makes an incision in the gum to expose the jawbone, inserting the titanium post deep into the bone as the new tooth root.
  • After placement, your jawbone grows around the implant over the next few months, securing it firmly in place.
  • Once healed, an abutment is attached to the implant to hold the crown, with the gum tissue closed around it for further healing.
  • Impressions of your mouth are then taken to craft a custom crown that matches your natural teeth.
  • Finally, the crown is attached to the abutment, completing your restoration.
