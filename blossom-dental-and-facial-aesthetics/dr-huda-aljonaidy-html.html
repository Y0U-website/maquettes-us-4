Dr. Huda Aljonaidy

In 2023, she received certification of completion of training program in the use of YOMI, a robot-assisted guidance system for dental implants. She also became the only CARE esthetics provider on Ormond Beach which allowed her to offer exclusive natural esthetic services like microneedling, bio-fillers and bio-lift treatments as well as utilizing platelet rich fibrin technology to get better healing results with surgery patients.

Dr. Aljonaidy has been practicing dentistry in Volusia County since 2011, during which she has treated more than 15,000 patients and educated them about their oral health. Her scope of practice ranges from limited emergency to complex oral rehabilitation cases.
