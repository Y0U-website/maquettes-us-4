Cosmetic Dentistry Whether your teeth are the wrong shape, crooked, stained, or chipped, Dr. Mackey has extensive training to treat your teeth with conservative methods using the latest techniques and the finest materials. Preventative Care Efficient preventative care is key to having a healthy smile. Consistent checkups will allow our team to diagnose things like tooth decay, cracking teeth and gum disease early, before they spread and become a problem. We even do a thorough oral cancer screening for all patients at every hygiene visit. We can’t be too thorough when it comes to your health!

Individualized dental care Our mission is to listen to your needs and provide you with personalized care. We are a team dedicated to improving and maintaining your oral health. Whether you’re looking for preventative care, cosmetic dentistry, or want to maintain your health, we are here for you.

The cadillac treatment for replacing missing teeth, an implant restoration is made to look and function like a natural tooth. Often you can’t feel or see the difference.

Crowns are synthetic "caps", usually made of strong porcelain. Crowns are typically used to restore a tooth’s structure and function when decay or fracture is more advanced. A bridge is multiple crowns fused together to replace a missing tooth.
