Gum disease is known to trigger a wide range of oral and systemic health concerns. The bacteria that cause gum disease spread and form deep pockets in the gums. Gums become inflamed and sore, often bleed, and may begin to recede away from the surface of the teeth.

We offer periodontal therapy services and preventative dental care to help control the spread and minimize the damage caused by gum disease.

TMJ disorders and appliances

For many patients, regularly occurring headaches and persistent jaw pain are symptoms of complex bite disorder known as TMD. TMD affects the health and function of the temporomandibular joint (tmj, or jaw joint). Patients with TMD often have an underlying imbalance of the jaw and musculature that can be managed with appliance therapy.
