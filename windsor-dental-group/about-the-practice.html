Windsor Dental Group is proud to provide dental health care to families throughout the North Bay area. We provide comprehensive care in Windsor, CA , at a comfortable, state-of-the-art office.

We believe a team approach is ideal, and we use our complementary skills to deliver outstanding care. We specialize in adult restorative dentistry, dental implant treatments, and provide complimentary second opinions. To schedule an appointment, please contact our office today.

About Windsor Dental Group

We make patient convenience and comfort a priority. Simply put, we respect your busy schedule, value your comfort, and are passionate about helping you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

If we diagnose a need for treatment, or if you request a cosmetic procedure, many times we can try to complete or begin the treatment during the same appointment.

In terms of comfort, we aim to ensure that you and your family receive the treatment you need in a calm and relaxing environment. We understand that many feel nervous about visiting the dentist’s office. To minimize that stress, we work closely with patients to ease their anxiety and provide prompt care. We offer several types of pain management as needed.

We are one of only a few dental offices in the North Bay that offer state of the art advanced 3D imaging in-house. Our advanced 3D imaging technology and partner lab help streamline the path from diagnosis to treatment. All necessary procedures can be completed under one roof, so there is no need for referrals.

Smile restoration using dental implants is one of our specialties. We offer traditional dental implants, as well as special configurations for supporting full dentures .

We provide Invisalign , dental bridges , dental crowns , veneers , and implant-supported dentures crafted to provide durability and a completely natural appearance.
