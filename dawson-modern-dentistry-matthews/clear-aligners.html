Clear Aligners in Matthews, NC

  • Aesthetics
  • Speed

Comfort

Patients often experience little or no discomfort during clear aligner treatment. Because there are no metal brackets and wires, there’s nothing poking and prodding the inside of your mouth. Instead, you’ll wear clear plastic aligners that are smooth to the touch.

Convenience

Invisalign clear aligners offer convenience and flexibility compared to traditional braces. Wearing clear aligners doesn’t require you to change your lifestyle or food habits. Unlike traditional metal braces, you can remove your aligners before having your food or drinks and before brushing and flossing your teeth.

Aesthetics

If you are not happy with having easily noticeable metal braces on your teeth, clear aligners can be the best option to align your teeth. Clear aligners are clear and transparent; hence they are hardly noticeable. Most people won’t even notice you are wearing them. So, you can talk or smile with confidence.

Speed

For some patients, clear aligners are an appealing option because they offer the fastest possible treatment. Unlike traditional braces that can take anywhere from 2 to 3 years to straighten teeth, clear aligner orthodontics offers patients a much shorter treatment time. On average, clear aligners can take anywhere between six months to two years to align and straighten teeth, depending on individual cases.
