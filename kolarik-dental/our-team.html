Meet our exceptional team

Paul Kolarik, DDS

Dr. Paul Kolarik has been in practice since graduating from the University of Missouri - School of Dentistry in 2012. He maintains memberships with the American Dental Association, Kansas Dental Association, and Fifth District of Kansas. Dr. Paul Kolarik has pursued additional training in Clear Correct invisible braces. With this technology, he is able to overcome smile discrepancies and deliver a straighter, healthier smile. Dr. Paul enjoys giving back to his community by volunteering for programs like Kansas Mission of Mercy, a program that donates dental care to the underserved.

In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his wife Jamie, and three kids, Tenley, Noah & Levi. His hobbies include golfing, pickleball and would never say no to a game of ping pong. He stays involved in his church community and has recently partnered with an organization named Start a Second Story which has started a Christian school in Pakistan.

Matt Kolarik, DDS

Dr. Matt Kolarik was born and raised in Hutchinson, KS, and attended the University of Kansas where he received his B.S. in Biology. After KU, he received his D.D.S. degree from UMKC School of Dentistry in 2017 and has been providing dental services in the Johnson County community since then. He completed a Dental Implant residency with the International Dental Implant Association and is well versed in dental implantology. He is also certified in Therapeutic Dental Botox applications.

When he’s away from the office he enjoys spending time with his wife, Michaela, his two sons, Otis & Harvey and daughter Stella. He is an avid golfer and lifelong Chiefs & Royals fan!
