What Are Porcelain Veneers?

Veneers are wafer-thin porcelain facings bonded to the front surface of your teeth. They require minimal tooth reduction and produce dramatic cosmetic improvements — correcting chips, stains, gaps, and uneven teeth in as few as two appointments.

The Design Process

Your veneers are custom-designed to complement your facial features, skin tone, and lip line for a completely natural look. We start with a digital preview so you can see your new smile before any work begins. Our in-house dental lab gives us full control over shade, shape, and translucency.

Longevity and Care

With proper care, porcelain veneers last 10–20 years. They resist staining better than natural enamel and maintain their appearance over time. Normal brushing, flossing, and regular checkups are all that&#x27;s needed to keep them looking great.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many teeth need veneers?

Most patients get 6–8 veneers on the upper front teeth for a balanced smile, but you can veneer as few as one tooth or as many as needed.

Can veneers fix crooked teeth?

Veneers can correct the appearance of mildly crooked or overlapping teeth. For more significant misalignment, we may recommend clear aligners first.
