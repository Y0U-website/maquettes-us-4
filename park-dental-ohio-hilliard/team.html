Dr. Siddhi Patel

Born in the small town of Lancaster, Ohio, Dr. Siddhi Patel remembers her dental visits as positive experiences. She would shadow her childhood dentist and decided early on that she wanted to pursue dentistry. After graduating from the Ohio State University with a degree in psychology, she went on to attend the prestigious New York University College of Dentistry . It was there this small town girl enjoyed the culture of the city that never sleeps.

Dr. Molly Joseph

Dr. Molly Joseph attended dental school at The Ohio State University, where she also received her undergraduate degree. Her passion for dentistry stemmed from her father, who is a dentist himself. She grew up in the town of Zanesville, Ohio, where she was surrounded by the dental profession. We recommend asking her how many people in her family are dentists.

Dr. Abigail Simmons

Dr. Abigail Simmons grew up Worthington, Ohio. She completed her undergraduate degree at the The Ohio State University in and proceeded to graduate cum laude from The Ohio State University College of Dentistry. Dr. Simmons is committed to providing comprehensive dental care through continuing education. She finds passion in getting to know patient’s personally and believes it is crucial to meet both short and long term oral health goals. When she’s not in the dental office.
