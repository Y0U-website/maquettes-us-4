What Is a Dental Implant Consultation?

Dentist 30214

A dental implant consultation is a comprehensive evaluation designed to determine whether dental implants are an appropriate solution for replacing missing teeth. During this visit, Dr. Pamela Morton reviews your dental history, examines your oral health, and discusses the condition of your jawbone and surrounding tissues. These factors help determine whether the bone structure can properly support a dental implant.

Did You Know?

Dental implants are designed to function like natural tooth roots. Once integrated with the jawbone, they provide a stable foundation for crowns and other restorations that can last for many years with proper care.
