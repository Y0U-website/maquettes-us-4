What Are Multiple Teeth Implants?

Dentist 30214

Multiple teeth implants are used to replace several missing teeth using two or more dental implants to support a restoration. Instead of placing an implant for every missing tooth, strategically placed implants can support a bridge or other prosthetic that replaces multiple teeth at once. This approach provides stability while reducing the number of implants required.

Dr. Pamela Morton carefully evaluates the spacing and structure of the jawbone before recommending this treatment. By placing implants within the jawbone, the restoration receives strong support that allows patients to chew comfortably and maintain natural bite alignment. Implant-supported restorations also help prevent bone loss in areas where teeth are missing. With proper care and maintenance, multiple teeth implants provide a durable and long-lasting solution for restoring a healthy, functional smile.

Did You Know?

Dental implants can often support multiple replacement teeth with fewer implants than the number of missing teeth. Strategic placement allows dentists to restore several teeth while maintaining strong support.
