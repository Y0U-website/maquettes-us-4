What Is a Single-Tooth Implant?

Dentist 30214

A single-tooth implant is a dental restoration designed to replace one missing tooth using a small titanium post that is placed within the jawbone. This post functions as an artificial tooth root and gradually integrates with the bone through a process called osseointegration. Once the implant has fully fused with the bone, a custom crown is attached to the top of the implant to restore the appearance of the tooth.

This approach provides a strong and stable foundation that closely mimics the function of a natural tooth. Dr. Pamela Morton carefully evaluates the health of the surrounding bone and gum tissue before recommending implant placement. Because implants replace both the root and visible portion of the tooth, they help preserve bone structure and prevent nearby teeth from shifting. With proper care and regular dental visits, a single-tooth implant can provide many years of reliable function while maintaining a natural-looking smile.

Did You Know?

Dental implants are designed to integrate directly with the jawbone, creating a stable foundation similar to a natural tooth root. This stability allows implants to support restorations that look and function like natural teeth.

Benefits of Single-Tooth Implants

Dental Implant Services in 30214

Restores Natural Function

Dental implants function similarly to natural teeth, allowing patients to chew comfortably and speak clearly without the movement often associated with removable restorations.

Preserves Jawbone Health

Because implants replace the tooth root, they help stimulate the jawbone and prevent the bone loss that often occurs after tooth loss.

Maintains Natural Tooth Structure

Unlike dental bridges, implants do not require reshaping neighboring teeth, allowing surrounding healthy teeth to remain intact.
