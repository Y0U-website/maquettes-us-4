Dental Implant Framingham

A dental implant is a titanium tooth root that is inserted into the jawbone for teeth replacement. They are an alternative to bridges and dentures. Moreover, they preserve the jawbone which deteriorates from missing teeth.

The procedure is simple. A screw is fashioned to the jaw bone for a sturdy foundation. The implant heals in 3 to 6 months. Afterwards, an abutment and dental crown are attached for final restoration. Patients prefer implants due to the natural-appearing results and longevity. In fact, an implant last up to 15 years and longer.
