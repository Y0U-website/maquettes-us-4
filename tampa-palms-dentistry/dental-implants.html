  • Dental implants deter future tooth loss as they do not rely on other teeth for support. Therefore, your other teeth are not impacted by the procedure.

Types of Dental Implants: Single Tooth Restoration

A single-tooth implant provides the most natural-looking and functioning replacement for a missing tooth. These advanced restorations include:

  • Titanium implant post (artificial root)
  • Custom-crafted crown
  • Natural-looking appearance
  • Permanent solution
  • Preserves jawbone health
  • Protects surrounding teeth

Dental Implant Process

If you’re considering getting dental implants, here’s a look at what to expect from your appointments and procedures.

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a thorough oral examination where Dr. Raffoul will assess your oral health, bone density, and gum condition using advanced digital imaging technology. During this visit, we’ll discuss your goals, answer your questions, and determine if implants are the right choice for you.

After evaluating your specific needs, we’ll create a customized treatment plan that outlines every step of your implant process, including any preliminary procedures that might be necessary. We’ll also discuss timeline expectations and ensure you understand all aspects of the treatment.

Preparation Phase

If needed, we’ll address any underlying oral health issues to ensure the best possible outcome for your implant. This might include treating gum disease, performing bone grafting, or removing damaged teeth. We’ll carefully coordinate each aspect of your treatment plan, ensuring optimal timing between procedures and allowing appropriate healing periods when necessary.

Implant Placement

Using state-of-the-art technology and precise surgical techniques, Dr. Raffoul will place the titanium implant post into your jawbone. This procedure is performed with appropriate anesthesia to ensure your comfort throughout the process.

Healing Period

Over the next few months, your implant will undergo a natural process called osseointegration, where it fuses with your jawbone to create a stable foundation for your new tooth. During this time, we’ll monitor your progress through regular check-ups.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, we’ll create your custom-designed crown, carefully matching it to your natural teeth in both color and shape. This crown will be crafted to provide optimal function and aesthetics. We’ll attach your permanent crown to the implant, make any necessary adjustments to ensure a perfect bite, and provide detailed instructions for long-term care of your new tooth.
