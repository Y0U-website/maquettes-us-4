Am I a Good Candidate for a Dental Implant Bridge in Medford?

Most healthy adults with missing teeth are potential candidates for a fixed implant bridge . You may be a good match if you:

  • Are missing several consecutive teeth
  • Have sufficient bone density (or are open to bone grafting )
  • Do not smoke or have uncontrolled health conditions
  • Are seeking care with a local Medford implant specialist

How Long Do Implant-Supported Bridges Last?

With excellent care, your implant bridge can last 10–20 years or more. The implant posts themselves often last a lifetime, while the bridge may eventually need replacement due to wear. Regular cleanings and exams will help maximize the lifespan of your implant restoration .

Is Bone Grafting Required Before Implant Bridge Placement?

Not all patients require bone grafting, but it may be recommended if your jawbone has deteriorated due to long-term tooth loss. We use panoramic imaging to assess your bone structure and determine whether grafting is needed for optimal implant stability.

What Is the Recovery Like After Getting an Implant-Supported Bridge?

Most patients experience mild discomfort for a few days after the procedure. Swelling and tenderness are common but manageable with over-the-counter pain relief. While the healing phase may take 3–6 months for the implants to integrate fully with the bone, we provide clear post-op instructions and ongoing support for a smooth recovery.

Can Implant Bridges Replace Multiple Missing Teeth or Full Arches?

Yes. Implant bridges are ideal for replacing multiple missing teeth—especially when teeth are missing in a row. We also offer full-arch restorations such as the All-on-4 implant bridge, a fixed solution that can restore a complete upper or lower arch with just four implant posts.

Our team can customize your implant bridge to suit the location and function of your missing teeth, whether you’re missing molars, front teeth, or a full row.

How Do I Care for and Maintain My Implant-Supported Bridge?

Maintaining your implant bridge is simple and similar to caring for natural teeth. We recommend:

With proper care, your implant-supported bridge can serve you for decades.

  • Brushing twice a day with a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • Using a water flosser or interdental brush to clean between the bridge and gums
  • Attending regular dental cleanings at our Medford office
  • Avoiding sticky or hard foods during the healing phase
