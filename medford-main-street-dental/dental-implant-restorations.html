How Do Dental Implant Restorations Work?

A dental implant restoration begins with a stable foundation—your dental implant. Once the titanium post has fully integrated with your jawbone, we attach an abutment, which serves as the connection point for your restoration. The restoration itself is a custom-designed crown, bridge, or denture that completes your smile. This process ensures your replacement teeth are secure, functional, and natural-looking.

Are You a Candidate for Implant Dentistry ?

If you already have dental implants or are considering them, you are likely a candidate for implant restorations. Patients with damaged or worn restorations can benefit from replacements that improve function and aesthetics. For new implant patients, our team ensures that the restorations are tailored to match the size, shape, and color of your natural teeth, creating a seamless look.
