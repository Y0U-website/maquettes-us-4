Benefits of Choosing Implant Overdentures:

More Stability Than Traditional Dentures

Implant-supported dentures stay in place during eating, speaking, and laughing. You won’t need adhesives or worry about slipping.

Fewer Implants, Faster Results

Most implant overdentures only require 2–4 implants per arch, reducing healing time and cost compared to fixed full-arch bridges.

Improved Chewing and Speaking Function

You can eat more of the foods you love and speak clearly without denture movement interfering.

Bone Preservation and Facial Support

Implants stimulate your jawbone, helping prevent the sunken facial appearance that often comes with missing teeth.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Implant Overdentures?

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for overdentures with implants , especially those looking for a more stable alternative to removable dentures. Even if you’ve experienced bone loss, you may still qualify. Systems like locator implant dentures often require less bone volume than other implant options, and additional procedures like bone grafting may be discussed during your consultation.

How Many Implants Are Needed for Overdentures?

Typically, 2 to 4 implants are placed per arch to support an overdenture. The exact number depends on your jaw’s shape, bone density, and the retention system used. Upper arches sometimes require more support due to softer bone, while lower arches often succeed with fewer implants.

Are Implant Overdentures Removable or Fixed?

Most implant overdentures are removable, allowing for easy cleaning and at-home maintenance. However, fixed options may be considered for patients seeking a non-removable solution. During your consultation, we’ll review the pros and cons of each and determine the best option for you.

What Is the Healing Time for Implant Overdentures?

After implant placement, healing usually takes 3 to 6 months. In some cases, we may provide a temporary denture to wear during this time. Once healing is complete, your custom implant-supported denture is securely attached, completing the process. How Long Do Implant Overdentures Last?

With proper care, the implants themselves can last 15 to 25 years or more. The overdenture portion may need adjustments or replacement every 5–10 years depending on wear, fit, and maintenance. We provide ongoing care and check-ups to help extend the life of your restoration.

How Do I Care for Implant Overdentures?

Caring for your implant overdentures is simple:

Proper maintenance helps protect both the implants and your soft tissues.

  • Remove them daily for cleaning
  • Use a soft brush and non-abrasive cleaner
  • Clean around the implant abutments with floss or specialized brushes
  • Visit us regularly for check-ups and professional cleanings
