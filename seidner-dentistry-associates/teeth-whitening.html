Our Teeth Whitening Services

The doctors at Seidner Dentistry & Associates offer a revolutionary, award-winning whitening technology: Zoom WhiteSpeed in-office treatment. According to studies, 99 % of patients experience little or no sensitivity following this procedure. Zoom teeth whitening has revolutionized the whitening experience, and only your dentist can offer these professional-strength treatments.

Take-Home Nitewhite Kit

Seidner Dentistry & Associates patients can also choose take-home whitening treatments with custom trays. This option delivers results in as little as three nights. The high-potency gel works best for yellow, brown, or gray tooth color. Our Zoom take-home kit is the only whitening system that uses the Power of Three formula, which strengthens tooth enamel while it whitens your teeth. Optimum results most often can be seen in two weeks. Your kit comes with 18 days’ worth of treatment.

Whitening Maintenance

Our office stocks professional-strength NiteWhite refill kits for nine at-home touch-up treatments that are used with your custom trays.
