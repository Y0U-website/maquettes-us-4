Dentist

Dr. Bradley earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from the University of Georgia before completing his Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) at the University of Kentucky College of Dentistry. After graduation, he worked for a hospital-based GPR program to expand his clinical skills and learn more complex dentistry to better serve his patients. He continues to stay active in the dental community through various Atlanta, Georgia, and National dental study groups.

He is originally from the Atlanta area and came back home. He has worked at this location for over a year, and officially bought the practice in August of 2025. Dr. Bradley loves the Dunwoody area, lives nearby, and will be in the area for a very long time! If you want a reliable, consistent dentist that will be serving the Dunwoody area for the next 30+ years look no further!
