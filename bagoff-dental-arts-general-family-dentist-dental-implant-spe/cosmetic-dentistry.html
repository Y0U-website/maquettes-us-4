Cosmetic Fillings

If you have metal fillings, they can be replaced with cosmetically beautiful tooth-colored restorations. This lets people notice your smile more than your fillings while prioritizing the health and comfort of your mouth and bite. Science has come a long way in developing safer and more aesthetically pleasing materials we can use as fillings, and you can take advantage of these advancements.

Teeth Whitening

These treatments can whiten teeth by several shades. You can choose from an hour-long in-office procedure, or a take-home kit that you can use almost anywhere. Some people utilize teeth whitening to minimize staining from food or drinks, after braces, or after another treatment.

Composite Bonding

Bonding uses a tooth-colored resin which is sculpted to match your tooth and hardened with a safe ultraviolet light. Composite bonding procedures can help close small gaps between teeth, change your teeth color, and cover small chips and cracks.

Contouring and Reshaping

This procedure can straighten or correct overlapped, cracked, chipped, or misaligned teeth. Contouring and reshaping can be performed as a singular procedure or in combination with composite bonding to achieve the look you want.

Porcelain Veneers

Thin sheaths of porcelain are placed on your teeth to improve overall appearance. Porcelain veneers can be used to correct teeth that are:

  • Heavily stained or discolored

  • Spaced apart or slightly overlapped

Invisalign

Invisalign is a state-of-the-art, nearly invisible way to straighten your teeth. Invisalign treatment uses comfortable, clear plastic trays, called aligners, to reposition your teeth over time. There are no metal wires or brackets involved like there are for conventional braces. The entire Invisalign process takes about 9 to 15 months.

These are an excellent alternative to braces or other brackets, especially if you want to avoid the look and feel of having metal in your mouth. Many dental insurance policies offer coverage for Invisalign and similar invisible teeth straightening options, as they can be used to correct bite and alignment.
