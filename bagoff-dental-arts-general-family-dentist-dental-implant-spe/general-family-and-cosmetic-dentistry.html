General Family Dentistry

Many individuals only visit the dentist when an issue arises, but preventive dental care is key to avoiding discomfort and expense in the long run. Regular dental visits are also essential for detecting and treating oral health problems promptly. While some may need more frequent visits to address specific concerns like plaque buildup or gum disease, a minimum of one annual visit is recommended for everyone.

During your regular dental appointments, a thorough oral exam will be conducted to assess the health of your teeth and gums. Professional cleanings will remove stubborn deposits, leaving your teeth looking and feeling great.

  • Cosmetic Bonding: Cosmetic bonding is a versatile procedure that repairs small chips or cracks, enhancing the overall appearance of your smile.
  • Crowns & Bridgework: Used to replace large amounts of lost tooth structure or missing teeth, crowns and bridgework restore both function and aesthetics.
  • Dental Implants: Dental implants provide a durable and natural-looking solution for the longest-lasting tooth replacement available today.
  • Extractions: When unhealthy teeth cannot be saved, extractions become necessary to maintain overall oral health.
  • Fillings: Fillings restore decayed teeth, preventing further damage and preserving natural tooth structure.
  • Oral Cancer Screenings: Routine screenings are crucial to detecting oral cancer in its early stages, offering a higher chance of successful treatment.
  • Orthodontic Treatment: Modern orthodontic options help move teeth into the right position, improving both aesthetics and functionality.
  • Root Canal Treatment: Root canal treatment is employed to rescue diseased teeth and prevent the need for extraction.
  • Sealants: Sealants are particularly beneficial for protecting children’s teeth and are applied to help prevent cavities.
  • Teeth Whitening: A popular cosmetic procedure, teeth whitening brightens faded or discolored smiles for a more youthful appearance.
  • TMD Treatment: TMD treatment ensures proper biting and chewing and targets jaw pain and discomfort.

Your Smile Makeover: Communication is Key

If you’re considering a smile makeover, effective communication with your dental team is essential. Before your consultation, take a moment to reflect on the following questions:

Your input is invaluable in crafting a customized plan to achieve your desired smile. Bringing in pictures of smiles you admire or photos of your own previous smile can further aid in conveying your aspirations.

  • Color, Size, Shape, and Spacing: What do you like or dislike about the color, size, shape, and spacing of your teeth?
  • Teeth Show: Are you pleased with how much your teeth show, both when you smile and when your lips are relaxed?
  • Alignment and Shade: Do you desire perfectly aligned and bright “Hollywood White” teeth or a more natural look with slight color, shape, and shade variations?
  • Gum Appearance: Would you like more or less of your gums to show when you smile?
