What happens during the Implant Bridges process?

The Implant Bridges process involves several steps over a few months. Here’s an overview:

  • Initial Consultation: Dr. Samir Gupta, Dr. Som Gupta will conduct a thorough assessment, including imaging, to evaluate your jawbone and plan implant placement.
  • Implant Placement: Small titanium posts are surgically inserted into the jawbone, where they will fuse with the bone over several months in a process called osseointegration.
  • Healing Period: After the implants integrate, they create a secure foundation for the bridge.
  • Bridge Placement: A custom-made bridge, designed to match your natural teeth, is securely attached to the implants, completing your smile restoration.

How do Implant Bridges compare to traditional dental bridges?

Traditional bridges depend on adjacent teeth for support, which can lead to additional wear and tear. Implant Bridges, on the other hand, are supported by implants in the jawbone, which acts like natural tooth roots, offering greater stability, longevity, and protection for your remaining teeth.

Are Implant Bridges easy to care for?

Caring for Implant Bridges is similar to caring for natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine check-ups will help maintain the integrity of your implant-supported bridge. With good oral hygiene, Implant Bridges can last for many years, giving you a confident, lasting smile.
