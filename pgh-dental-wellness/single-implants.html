Single Implants in Pittsburgh, PA

Single Implants are an exceptional choice for individuals looking to replace a missing tooth with a solution that feels natural, looks great, and offers permanent stability. With a personalized approach and high standards in implant technology, Dr. Samir Gupta, Dr. Som Gupta are dedicated to helping you achieve a fully restored, healthy smile at PGH Dental Wellness in Pittsburgh.

  • Implant Placement : The next step is the placement of the titanium implant post into the jawbone. This outpatient procedure is performed under local anesthesia, ensuring a comfortable experience. Over the following months, the implant will undergo a process called osseointegration, where it fuses with the jawbone to provide stability comparable to a natural tooth.
  • Crown Placement : Once the implant has fully bonded with the jawbone, a custom-made crown is attached to the implant post. The crown is crafted to blend with your natural teeth, both in color and shape, for a seamless look.
  • Follow-Up Care : Our team at PGH Dental Wellness will provide guidance on caring for your new implant to ensure long-lasting results. Regular checkups help monitor the implant’s stability, allowing you to enjoy your restored smile for years to come.
