Our Team

Local & Family Owned

When it comes to finding a dentist, there’s something special about supporting a locally owned, family-operated practice. At Streetman Family Dental, we’re not just here to fix teeth—we’re here to serve our neighbors, our friends, and our community with the same care and respect we’d show our own families. Rooted in West Texas values, we believe that great dental care is about more than just cleanings and checkups. It’s about building relationships, earning trust, and providing the kind of personal, thoughtful treatment that only a family business can offer.

We’re not just your dentists—we’re your neighbors!

Dr. Nicole Streetman, DDS

Nicole has 15 years of Dental experience and an advanced degree in Prosthodontics after completing her residency in 2013 at Nova Southeastern University in South Florida. Upon completion, Nicole returned to Lubbock with her husband Ryan to continue her career and start her family. Nicole and Ryan have a deep love for West Texas and have called it home since they married in 2008. Nicole’s passion for Dentistry and helping her community led to her working for a Lubbock community health center for 5 years and she continues that passion today as a volunteer.

West Texas values and compassion for her patients are the foundation of her ethic. She works with her patients to make a plan for healing and for prevention. Visit her at Streetman Family Dental and start down the road to a healthier smile.
