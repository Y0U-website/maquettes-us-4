The Benefits

Invisalign® offers a modern, flexible approach to achieving the smile you’ve always wanted. Here are the key advantages:

  • Virtually Invisible : The clear aligners are nearly invisible, making it a discreet option for straightening your teeth. You can smile confidently throughout your treatment without the visual impact of traditional braces.
  • Removable for Convenience : Invisalign® aligners can be easily removed for eating, brushing, and flossing. This not only allows you to enjoy your favorite foods without worry but also makes maintaining oral hygiene a breeze.
  • Fewer Dental Visits : With no wires or brackets that need adjustments, Invisalign® typically requires fewer check-ups and adjustments than traditional braces. This convenience is perfect for anyone with a busy schedule, allowing more time for the things you love.
  • Customized Treatment Plan : Using advanced 3D imaging technology, Invisalign® provides a customized treatment plan tailored specifically to your dental needs and goals. This personalized approach ensures effective and efficient results.
  • Comfortable Wear : The aligners are made of smooth, flexible plastic, offering a comfortable fit without the irritation often caused by the brackets and wires of traditional braces.

What To Expect

Here’s a breakdown of what to expect during your Invisalign® journey at Preferred Dental Partners:

  • Initial Consultation : Your journey starts with a detailed assessment of your dental needs and goals.
  • Personalized Treatment Plan : Utilizing 3D imaging technology, a tailored treatment plan is crafted specifically for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I clean my Invisalign® aligners? Keeping your Invisalign® aligners clean is crucial for oral hygiene and preventing any unwanted odors. Rinse them under lukewarm water every night and brush them gently with a toothbrush to remove any lingering plaque or food particles. Avoid using hot water, as it can warp the plastic. Additionally, soaking your aligners in a cleaning solution specifically designed for Invisalign® can help keep them clear and hygienic.
  • Are Invisalign® aligners better than braces? Both Invisalign® aligners and traditional braces have their advantages, depending on your specific needs and lifestyle preferences. Invisalign® offers a more aesthetic and less intrusive way to straighten teeth, with the convenience of being removable for eating and cleaning. However, traditional braces might be more effective for more complex orthodontic issues. During your consultation at Preferred Dental Partners, our team will help determine the best treatment option for you.
  • Can I still get aligners if I’ve already had braces? Absolutely! Many patients choose Invisalign® as a discreet solution to adjust teeth that may have shifted after previously having braces. It’s an excellent option for refining your smile without returning to traditional metal braces. A consultation with Dr. Borja, Dr. Minnema, or Dr. Oldridge will help determine the best approach for your specific situation.
  • Will Invisalign impact my daily life? Invisalign® is designed to fit seamlessly into your life. You can speak, work, and engage in social activities without feeling self-conscious. The aligners are removable, so you can continue to enjoy all your favorite foods and maintain your oral hygiene routine without hassle. Most patients quickly adapt to wearing the aligners and notice minimal impact on their daily routine.
  • Are clear aligners painful? While you may experience some pressure and discomfort each time you begin wearing a new set of aligners, this sensation is typically mild and temporary. It’s a sign that your Invisalign® is working, as the aligners gently move your teeth to their new positions. Any discomfort can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers and typically subsides within a few days.
  • How do I maintain my results? Maintaining your beautiful new smile after completing Invisalign® treatment involves wearing a retainer as directed by Dr. Borja, Dr. Minnema, or Dr. Oldridge. This is crucial for preventing your teeth from shifting back to their original positions. Initially, you might need to wear your retainer full time, but eventually, you may only need to wear it at night. Regular dental check-ups and a good oral hygiene routine are also key to keeping your smile looking its best.
