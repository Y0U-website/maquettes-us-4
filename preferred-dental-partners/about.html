About Our Dental Team

FAQs Are you accepting new patients? As of now, YES! We are currently accepting new patients of any age. You can call our New Patient Concierge Cassandra in Horicon at 920.485.4831 or our New Patient Concierge Linda in Beaver Dam at 920.887.1292 to find out more.

Your first visit with us will be a comprehensive oral and radiographic examination with one of our doctors. After your customized care plan is determined, then we will schedule you with one of our amazing hygienists.

  • Always Be Growing We are never satisfied with the status quo, always seeking to expand and refine our skills. We see growth as a journey and enjoy the process of learning and self-improvement.
  • The WOW! Experience We transcend typical customer service by personalizing the experience, showing genuine passion, and creating a lasting impression. Our approach is unexpected, delightful, and leaves patients feeling valued and inspired.
  • All-In Attitude We embrace every situation with energy and a positive mindset, always striving for excellence. We give our wholehearted best in every aspect, regardless of the outcome.
  • Can I be seen at either location? Yes! You do not have to choose one location. You are free to set appointments at either Horicon or Beaver Dam depending on what works for you. All of our doctors and care teams rotate between the two locations.
  • Do you have payment plans? Yes! We partner with CareCredit in order to provide both interest-free payment plans and longer-term payment plans. Call our Patient Care Coordinators at Horicon 920.485.4831 or Beaver Dam 920.887.1292 to find out more. Or visit CareCredit to apply or find out more.
  • At what age should my child begin seeing a dentist? Does your child have teeth? If so, bring them in! Our doctors are trained to do early childhood exams. These exams are usually quick, easy, and fun to help gain your child’s trust. Studies show that children who see a dentist at a young age typically have less dental problems and are more comfortable receiving care in a dental office.
  • Do you offer sedation? Yes! We offer nitrous oxide sedation for children and adults to help with anxiety. This type of “sedation” allows the patient to drive to and from the appointment and not disrupt their day. In addition, we can provide other oral medications if needed to aid in patient comfort. However, this may require a driver to and from the appointment. We also have iPads for children (or adults!) that need a little extra distraction.
  • Will I need to be referred out for any treatment? Probably not! We provide a broad range of treatments in-house, including oral surgery, dental implants, Invisalign, Botox, TMJ care, pediatrics, root canal therapy, and more! If your treatment does require something we cannot provide at PDP, we will refer you to one of our trusted specialist partners throughout the area.
