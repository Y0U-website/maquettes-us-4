Get to Know

Dr. Delia Cotera

A gifted clinician, Dr. Cotera is passionate about creating and maintaining beautiful and healthy smiles. She is committed to providing the highest quality of dental care available in a warm and caring ambience. Dr. Cotera enjoys working with children; her gentle approach can ease even the most anxious patient. She is also passionate about giving back to the community. Dr. Cotera has worked with underprivileged children in rural areas from as far away as the Andes, and looks forward to offering dental assistance to disadvantaged people in Sarasota County.

She and her husband moved to Sarasota from the Washington, D.C., area in 2005. They fell in love with Sarasota’s rich cultural offerings (avid cinephiles, they are now fans of the Sarasota Film Festival and Sarasota Film Society) and its lush natural beauty. “We didn’t think twice after spending a few days here. We knew this was home.” Their daughters, Camille and Evangeline, were born in Sarasota in 2008 and 2013 respectively. Fluent in English and Spanish, Dr. Cotera enjoys traveling to new places, exercising, movies, music and spending time with her family.

Your First Visit Experience

Your first visit to Osprey Dental sets the foundation for your oral health journey. During your new patient exam, you&#x27;ll experience our thorough yet gentle approach. We&#x27;ll start with a comprehensive oral examination, including digital X-rays and intraoral photos. Dr. Cotera or Dr. Kesling will personally discuss your dental history, concerns, and goals. Together, we&#x27;ll create a customized treatment plan that aligns with your needs and lifestyle.

What to Bring

For your first visit, please bring your ID, insurance information (if applicable), a list of current medications, and any recent dental X-rays. You can also review our patient resources before your appointment. Don’t forget to share your dental concerns and smile goals with us!
