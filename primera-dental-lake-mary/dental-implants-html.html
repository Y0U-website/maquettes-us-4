Dental Implants in Longwood, FL

Rebuild Your Smile with Long-Lasting Dental Implants

Missing teeth can affect more than just your appearance. Gaps in your smile may make it harder to eat comfortably, speak clearly, and feel confident in social situations. At Primera Dental, we offer high-quality dental implants in Longwood designed to restore both the function and beauty of your smile.

Dental implants are one of the most durable and natural-looking tooth replacement options available today. Whether you are missing a single tooth or multiple teeth, our team can help you regain a strong, healthy smile with personalized implant treatment.

What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that replaces the root of a missing tooth. The implant is surgically placed into the jawbone, where it naturally bonds with the bone over time. Once healed, a custom dental crown , bridge , or denture is attached to complete your smile.

Unlike traditional tooth replacement options, dental implants are designed to look, feel, and function like natural teeth. They stay securely in place without slipping or shifting, helping you speak and chew with confidence.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer both cosmetic and long-term oral health benefits.

Natural Appearance and Feel

Implants are custom-made to blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth for a beautiful, natural-looking smile.

Prevent Bone Loss

When a tooth is lost, the jawbone underneath can begin to shrink over time. Dental implants stimulate the jawbone and help preserve your facial structure.

Improved Comfort and Stability

Unlike removable dentures, implants stay securely in place. You can eat, laugh, and speak without worrying about movement or discomfort.

Long-Lasting Solution

With proper care, dental implants can last for many years and may even last a lifetime.

Better Confidence

Restoring your smile can improve your self-esteem and help you feel more comfortable in everyday interactions.

Dental Implant Options

At Primera Dental, we offer several implant solutions based on your needs and goals.

Single Tooth Implants

A single dental implant replaces one missing tooth without affecting neighboring teeth. The implant is topped with a custom crown that matches your natural smile.

Multiple Tooth Implants

If you are missing several teeth, implants can support a dental bridge or multiple replacement teeth for a stable, comfortable restoration.

Implant-Supported Dentures

Implant-supported dentures provide a more secure alternative to traditional removable dentures. They help improve comfort, chewing ability, and overall stability.

If you are exploring alternatives, we also offer restorative options such as dentures and dental bridges.

What to Expect During the Dental Implant Process

We understand that many patients feel nervous about oral surgery. Our team focuses on making your experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible.

Consultation and Planning

Your treatment begins with a comprehensive evaluation to determine whether dental implants are right for you. We will review your oral health, discuss your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan.

Implant Placement

The implant is carefully placed into the jawbone using local anesthesia for comfort. Sedation options may also be available for anxious patients.

Healing and Integration

Over the next few months, the implant fuses with the jawbone through a natural process called osseointegration. This creates a strong foundation for your replacement tooth.

Final Restoration

Once healing is complete, your custom crown, bridge, or denture is securely attached to the implant.

If damaged or severely decayed teeth need to be removed before treatment, we may recommend a tooth extraction before implant placement.

Are Dental Implants Painful?

Most patients are surprised by how comfortable the procedure feels. Local anesthesia helps numb the treatment area during surgery, and many patients report only mild soreness afterward. Recovery is typically manageable with over-the-counter pain medication and proper aftercare.

Our team will provide detailed instructions to help you heal comfortably and reduce the risk of complications.

Are Dental Implants Worth It?

Dental implants are considered one of the best investments in long-term oral health because they replace both the visible tooth and the root underneath. Unlike other restorations, implants help maintain jawbone health while restoring full chewing function and appearance.

Many patients choose implants because they provide greater comfort, stability, and confidence compared to removable tooth replacement options.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Dental implants are easy to maintain with regular oral hygiene habits. Brushing, flossing, and routine dental visits can help keep your implants and surrounding gums healthy for years to come.

We may also recommend additional cosmetic or restorative treatments, including white fillings , to improve the overall health and appearance of your smile.
