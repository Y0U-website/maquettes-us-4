Meet Our Doctors

Dr. Amal Hamdi, Dentist in Lake Mary, Florida.

Dr. Hamdi completed her degree in Microbiology from the University of Central Florida and received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from The Indiana University School of Dentistry.

Dr. Hamdi practices by the principle of minimally invasive dentistry and patient comfort. Her areas of expertise include Veneers, Bonded esthetic restorations, Root Canal Therapy and Dental Implants. In her free time, Dr. Hamdi enjoys spending time with her family, traveling, reading and she is very passionate about volunteering.
