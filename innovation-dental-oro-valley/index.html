The practice

Your smile is our top priority. We strive to help you achieve lasting oral health while feeling at ease every step of the way. With a genuine commitment to patient education, state-of-the-art tools, and compassionate service, we partner with you to create a tailored treatment plan for your unique needs.

For multiple missing teeth, we provide custom dentures crafted to fit comfortably and restore confidence. From partial to full dentures or even implant-supported solutions our goal is to help you speak, eat, and smile with ease.

We consider your initial appointment the start of a long-term relationship. During your first visit:

Our friendly front-desk team will assist with any paperwork and answer questions.

We assess your oral health using advanced diagnostic tools and techniques.

We’ll recommend treatments tailored to your specific goals and discuss any concerns you may have.

Whether you’re new to the area or seeking a more personalized approach, our team is ready to help you feel at ease from day one.

At Innovation Dental, we want to make quality dental care accessible for everyone. We accept a variety of insurance plans and offer flexible financing options, including CareCredit, so you can focus on your health rather than the cost of treatment. Our dedicated Insurance & Billing Specialist is here to help you navigate coverage and payment plans.
