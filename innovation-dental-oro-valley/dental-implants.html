Our Implant Services

Whether you need a single tooth replaced or are seeking a full smile transformation, Innovation Dental has a range of dental implant solutions designed to fit your needs.

Single Dental Implants

If you’re missing a single tooth, a dental implant is the gold standard for replacement. Unlike a bridge which requires altering adjacent teeth a single implant stands independently, preserving your natural tooth structure.

  • Long-Term Durability: With proper care, implants can last for decades.
  • Natural Appearance: Custom crowns blend seamlessly with your existing teeth.
  • Bone Health: Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing bone loss.

Implant-Supported Dentures

For patients missing multiple teeth or entire arches implant-supported dentures offer stability that traditional removable dentures simply can’t match. By anchoring the denture to two or more implants, you’ll experience:

  • Improved Chewing and Speaking: No more slipping or adhesive worries.
  • Increased Comfort: Less irritation to the gums and more confidence in everyday activities.
  • Easier Maintenance: Snap-in designs can be removed for cleaning, maintaining hygiene with ease.

Full Arch Fixed Restorations

Also known as All-on-4 or All-on-X, this solution uses strategically placed implants to support a permanent, fixed bridge across an entire arch of missing teeth.

  • Immediate Transformation: Often, you’ll leave with a temporary prosthesis the same day as implant placement.
  • Stable, Secure Fit: No slipping or removing your teeth each night these restorations feel and function like natural teeth.
  • Life-Changing Results: Regain a confident smile and the ability to eat, speak, and laugh without worry.

Zirconia Bridges

For patients seeking top-tier aesthetics and robust durability, zirconia bridges are an excellent choice. Zirconia is an advanced ceramic known for its:

We often pair zirconia with guided implant surgery to ensure precise placement, resulting in a long-lasting, picture-perfect smile.

  • High Strength: Resistant to cracks, chips, and wear.
  • Natural Translucency: Mimics the look of real teeth.
  • Biocompatibility: Gentle on the gums, reducing irritation or allergic reactions.

Immediate Load Implants

Traditionally, implants require a healing period of several months before attaching a restoration. However, immediate load implants (sometimes referred to as teeth in a day ) allow for a temporary crown or bridge to be placed right after your implant surgery.

  • Reduced Treatment Time: Gain functional teeth much sooner.
  • Fewer Appointments: Ideal for busy lifestyles.
  • Careful Selection: Our team will determine if you’re a suitable candidate, considering factors like bone quality and overall health.

The Implant Process: What to Expect

  • Consultation & Evaluation: -We begin with a thorough exam, including X-rays or 3D scans, to assess bone structure and oral health. -Our doctors will discuss your smile goals and outline a custom treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: -Under local anesthesia (and sedation options if desired), we precisely place your implant(s) using guided surgery or a conventional method, depending on your case. -A healing period follows, during which the implant integrates with your jawbone.
  • Abutment & Restoration: -Once healed, we attach an abutment a connector piece to the implant. -A custom crown, zirconia bridge, or full arch prosthesis is then crafted to blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.
  • Ongoing Care & Maintenance: -With regular checkups and good oral hygiene, your new implants can last a lifetime. -Our team provides ongoing support to ensure you continue smiling confidently for years to come.
