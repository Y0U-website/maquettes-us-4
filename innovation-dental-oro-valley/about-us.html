Welcome to Innovation Dental

At Innovation Dental, we believe in transforming lives one smile at a time. For over 30 years, our team has proudly served the Oro Valley community with patient-focused, advanced dental care. Building upon the legacy started by Dr. John Haymore, Oro Valley’s first dentist, we continue to combine modern technology with a family-oriented approach ensuring every visit is as comfortable and personalized as possible.

Our Philosophy

Your smile is our top priority. We strive to help you achieve lasting oral health while feeling at ease every step of the way. With a genuine commitment to patient education, state-of-the-art tools, and compassionate service, we partner with you to create a tailored treatment plan for your unique needs.

Meet Our Doctors

Jess Haymore, DDS

Dr. Jess Haymore is continuing the tradition of providing trusted, quality dental care set by his father, Dr. John Haymore, who was the first dentist in Oro Valley more than 30 years ago. For Dr. Jess, dentistry is an extension of family and community, and he is dedicated to ensuring that each patient receives the finest care possible.

Dr. Haymore obtained his DDS degree from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2002. Since then, he has established himself as a leading dental provider in Oro Valley. His extensive skills in restorative, cosmetic, and implant dentistry, combined with his pleasant chairside manner and sense of humor, make him a favorite with patients of all ages.

Both Dr. Jess and his wife, Tania, are Arizona natives. They have five children who stay busy with sports, dance, music, and a variety of other activities. He is actively involved in the Oro Valley community and church-based services, from organizing community projects to helping run youth dances and supporting El Tour de Tucson aid stations. As an avid athlete, he enjoys playing soccer and participating in running and cycling events.

Samuel D. Peterson, DMD

Dr. Sam Peterson joined Innovation Dental in July 2018. He graduated from Temple University’s Kornberg School of Dentistry in 2013. Early in his career, Dr. Peterson worked at Fine Arts Dental Lab and served at a community health center, gaining valuable experience in both dental aesthetics and function.

His commitment to professional excellence is evident daily both to our team and the patients he treats. Dr. Peterson ensures each patient is greeted warmly, feels heard, and understands the recommended treatment plan along with any available alternatives.

Outside the office, Dr. Peterson enjoys spending time with his wife and five children. The family is highly involved in their church, and the children participate in various sports and activities. His personal interests include reading, cooking, and rock climbing.

Brian Adams, DMD

Dr. Brian Adams is a welcome addition to the Innovation Dental team. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Midwestern College of Dental Medicine AZ, where he excelled both academically and in hands-on clinical practice. Known for his compassionate approach and patient-focused philosophy, Dr. Adams strives to create a reassuring environment for every patient.

Believing in open communication, Dr. Adams collaborates closely with patients to achieve optimal oral health outcomes. He is excited to build lasting relationships in the Oro Valley community and continue the legacy of outstanding care at Innovation Dental.

Outside the office, Dr. Adams enjoys spending time with his wife, Brooke, and their two daughters. Relatively new to Tucson, they have quickly embraced everything the city has to offer. He also enjoys an active lifestyle, sports with his girls, and trying new restaurants in the area. Dr. Adams looks forward to making a positive impact on patient care at Innovation Dental.
