Mouth Guards

When it comes to protecting your family’s smiles, a custom mouth guard is an essential piece of gear especially for young athletes or those who grind their teeth at night (bruxism). We offer:

By investing in a high-quality, custom mouth guard, you can safeguard your teeth and gums from preventable damage and discomfort.

  • Sports Mouth Guards: Specially fitted to each patient’s mouth, reducing the risk of dental injuries during contact sports or other physical activities.
  • Night Guards: Designed for those who clench or grind their teeth at night, helping to prevent wear, fractures, and jaw discomfort.

Retainers

After you’ve completed orthodontic treatment whether with clear aligners, braces, or another method retainers help keep your newly aligned teeth in their ideal positions. At Innovation Dental, we provide:

We’ll work closely with you to determine the best retainer type based on your lifestyle, ensuring the longevity of your straightened smile.

  • Fixed Retainers: A small, discreet wire bonded behind the teeth for continuous support.
  • Removable Retainers: Custom-fitted trays or wire-acrylic retainers you’ll wear part-time according to your treatment plan.

Pediatric and Adult Orthodontics

Pediatric Orthodontics

For children and teens, early orthodontic intervention can guide proper jaw growth and address any issues before they become more complex. Our team takes a gentle approach, focusing on education and prevention to keep kids excited about caring for their smiles. We’ll monitor growth patterns and recommend treatments like spacers, early-phase aligners, or partial braces if and when they’re truly needed.

Adult Orthodontics

It’s never too late to achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. Adult orthodontic treatment is more popular than ever, especially with discreet and convenient clear aligner systems. SureSmile is designed with these considerations in mind, offering efficient, comfortable treatment that minimally impacts your day-to-day life.
