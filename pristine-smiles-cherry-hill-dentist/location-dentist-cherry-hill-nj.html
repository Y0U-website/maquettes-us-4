FAQ’s

  • 1. What services does a dentist in Cherry Hill, NJ provide? A Cherry Hill dentist may provide preventive care such as exams, cleanings, digital X-rays, and gum-health evaluations, as well as family dentistry, restorative treatment, and cosmetic dentistry. Depending on a patient’s needs, treatment may include tooth-colored fillings, dental crowns, bridges, implants, teeth whitening, veneers, Invisalign, and urgent dental care.
  • 2. How do I choose a family dentist in Cherry Hill? Choose a family dentist who provides care for different stages of life, listens to your concerns, and explains treatment choices clearly. It can also help to find a Cherry Hill dental office that emphasizes preventive care, uses modern techniques, provides a comfortable environment, and creates personalized treatment plans instead of taking a one-size-fits-all approach.
  • 3. What makes Pristine Smiles different from other dentists in Cherry Hill? Pristine Smiles emphasizes gentle precision, modern comfort, and individualized patient care. The practice gives patients time and attention, listens closely to their needs, and focuses on building trusted relationships that support healthy habits and confident smiles over time.pristinesmilesnj
  • 4. What is cosmetic dentistry, and which treatments can improve my smile? Cosmetic dentistry includes treatments that improve the appearance of teeth, gums, and the overall smile. A cosmetic dentist may recommend professional teeth whitening for discoloration, veneers for chips or unevenly shaped front teeth, Invisalign for alignment concerns, or tooth-colored restorations that improve both smile appearance and dental function.
  • 5. Is professional teeth whitening worth it? Professional teeth whitening can be a good option for patients who want a brighter smile with treatment tailored to their oral health. Before whitening, a dentist can evaluate tooth sensitivity, existing crowns or fillings, gum health, and the source of stains to determine whether whitening is appropriate and which approach may produce the most even result.
  • 6. What are dental veneers, and who is a candidate? Veneers are thin, custom-made coverings bonded to the front surfaces of visible teeth. They may help improve the appearance of chipped, worn, discolored, slightly uneven, or mildly spaced teeth. A cosmetic dentistry consultation helps determine whether veneers, bonding, teeth whitening, or another solution is the best choice for your goals and tooth health.
  • 7. Can I get Invisalign treatment from a Cherry Hill dentist? Many Cherry Hill dentists offer Invisalign or similar clear-aligner treatment for qualified teens and adults. Invisalign uses removable, clear aligners that gradually move teeth and may be appropriate for mild to moderate crowding, spacing, or bite concerns. A dentist can evaluate your smile and bite to confirm whether clear aligners are suitable.
  • 8. Why are routine dental checkups important for families? Routine dental exams and cleanings help a dentist identify cavities, gum disease, enamel wear, bite issues, and other concerns early—often before symptoms become painful or require more extensive treatment. Regular family dentistry visits also provide preventive guidance tailored to each patient’s oral-health history and daily habits.
