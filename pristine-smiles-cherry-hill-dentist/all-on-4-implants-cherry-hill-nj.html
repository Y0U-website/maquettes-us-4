Understanding Tooth Loss and Its Impact

Tooth loss is more common than many realize, and isn’t just a part of aging—it can result from accidents, sports injuries, car collisions, severe decay, or gum disease. Over time, teeth weakened by decay or disease may need to be extracted, leaving noticeable gaps. Beyond the physical challenges, missing teeth can also affect your confidence, nutrition, and overall well-being.

Some common effects of tooth loss include:

Thankfully, innovative treatments like All-On-4 dental implants can address these concerns and help you reclaim your quality of life.

  • Sensitivity to temperature: Tooth loss and decay can result in pain or discomfort when eating hot or cold foods.
  • Gum and jaw discomfort: Edentulous (toothless) patients may experience sore gums and difficulty with speech or chewing. Full dentures, while helpful, are not always comfortable or stable.
  • Social anxiety: People missing teeth often feel self-conscious about their smile, leading to avoidance of social gatherings or photographs.

How the All-On-4 Implant Process Works

Traditional dental implants often require placing an individual implant for every missing tooth, which can be invasive, lengthy, and expensive. All-On-4 streamlines the process, using four carefully positioned implants to anchor a full arch of replacement teeth. This method works even for patients with significant tooth loss and minimal bone, thanks to the angled placement of certain implants for enhanced strength and stability—typically eliminating the need for bone grafts.

The All-On-4 approach is designed for quick recovery and immediate improvement in quality of life.

  • Comprehensive Evaluation: We begin with digital X-rays and impressions to plan your custom treatment.
  • Preparation: Any remaining unhealthy or damaged teeth are gently removed.
  • Implant Placement: Four titanium implants are strategically placed into your upper and/or lower jaws—two placed vertically in the front and two at an angle in the back to maximize contact with bone.
  • Attachment of Temporary Teeth: A beautiful set of temporary teeth is secured to your implants on the very same day, allowing you to leave our office with a functional, confident smile.
  • Final Restoration: After the implants fully integrate with your bone over a few months, your final custom bridge will be attached, providing permanent results.
