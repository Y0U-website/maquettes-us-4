The practice

Don t wait any longer to get the smile you deserve. Contact us today and book your appointment with Sunahara Smiles.

Thorough exams with the doctor to address your concerns and evaluate your teeth for cavities, gum disease, and any other oral health issues. Includes a full discussion afterwards of any findings. We take digital x-rays, which are more comfortable and accurate.

Using our state-of-the-art Itero Scanners, we can track oral health issues over time with our complementary wellness scans. And the best part is no goopy impressions for nearly all dental procedures!

We use high-quality esthetic materials to help fillings last as long as possible. Porcelain crowns and bridges are done with digital scans and skilled local laboratories.

Dr. Kimi Sunahara ha s been an Invisalign provider for 15 years. We only use official I nvisalign products for orthodontic treatment and clear tray retainers. All treatment plans are carefully designed by Dr. Kimi Sunahara to resolve your orthodontic goals.

We have three experienced and very kind hygienists to help our patients maintain their teeth and gum health. They are often praised for their compassion and skill, especially with patients who have sensitive teeth.

Our new whitening system is able to whiten your teeth in less time and with less sensitivity. We have in-office and at-home options, and it only takes 8 minutes per session! Much easier to fit into your day.

If you are missing one or even all of your teeth, we can fabricate removable appliances to help you smile and chew again. We truly understand how this can affect your quality of life and will do our absolute best to help.
