During your visit, our dental experts conduct a detailed examination of your teeth, gums, and jaw, identifying any potential dental issues. Together, we’ll create a personalized preventive care plan tailored to your specific needs and risk factors.

Gum Disease Treatment/Scaling and Root Planing

Periodontal Disease is an infection and inflammation that affects the tissues and bone that support your teeth. It’s also called gum disease. When your gums are healthy, your gum tissues tightly hug each of your teeth. When you have gum disease, the tissues and bone that support your teeth are damaged. Treating gum disease early is very important in preventing tooth loss. Periodontal Scaling and Root Planning is a type of deep cleaning, effective in treating early gum disease.

Veneers

Veneers are thin, porcelain shells custom made to fit precisely over the front surface of teeth. Each veneer is custom made to look and feel like natural teeth. They’re one of the most effective and gratifying remedies for teeth that are chipped, cracked, spaced, stained, mis-shaped or mis-aligned. Even a subtle change in your smile can make a dramatic difference in the way you look and feel. Veneers are one of the most common cosmetic procedures in Dentistry.

Bonding

Dental bonding is used to fix a defect or imperfection within a tooth. A tooth colored resin is applied to the surface of the tooth to correct the shape or size, repair chips, camouflage discoloration and close spaces between your teeth. Bonding is minimally invasive and unlike veneers, bonding is reversible.

Teeth Whitening

Dark, stained and discolored teeth have many causes. Coffee, wine, sodas and smoking are most common. Injury and childhood use of certain medications are also responsible for dulling otherwise bright smiles. Fortunately, there are many ways to restore the luster of a faded smile.

Our team are the experts in the wide variety of teeth whitening techniques, and we will work with you and your unique conditions to find the best way to bring back your bright and confident smile. Some require a series of in-office visits, while others simply apply treatments at home. Either way, we hope you take advantage of your opportunity to regain a brighter, whiter smile with teeth whitening—perhaps the easiest, most popular of cosmetic dental procedures.

Dental Implants

Dental Implants are the permanent way to replace missing teeth with a look and feel that’s very close to your natural teeth. Implants are metal posts surgically placed in the bone beneath your gums. The implants fuse with the jawbone providing stable support to artificial teeth, preventing individual teeth, bridges, or dentures from shifting in your mouth. They are a high-tech way to replace missing teeth, and offer more stability than traditional bridgework since they do not rely on neighboring teeth for support.

Snore Guard Sleep Apnea

During sleep, muscles and soft tissues in the throat and mouth relax, shrinking the airway. This causes noisy breathing or snoring. At certain levels of severity, complete blockage of the airway space by the soft tissues and the tongue can occur. Snoring doesn’t merely interrupt your sleep cycle. The struggle for breath can result in future systemic health problems. For the majority of snorers, the most affordable, non invasive, comfortable and effective snoring solution remains a snore prevention device. This custom fabricated device treats snoring and mild to moderate sleep apnea.

Orthodontics Braces / Invisalign

Orthodontics is the practice of straightening misaligned teeth and correcting malocclusions (bite problems). This can be achieved by fixed devices (braces) or removable devices (Invisalign). The benefits of braces go far beyond straighter teeth and a beautiful smile. Properly aligned teeth make it easier to brush and floss which helps to prevent tooth decay and gum disease.

Bridges

A missing tooth is a serious matter. Teeth are made to work together. When you loose a tooth, the nearby teeth may tilt or drift into the empty space. This may affect your bite and change the way the jawbone supports the lips and cheeks. A bridge is a replacement tooth or teeth that fill the space where one or more teeth are missing. The bridge restores your bite and keeps the natural shape of your face.

Full and Partial Dentures

Whether your are missing some or all of your natural teeth, you can derive many benefits from replacing them with full or partial dentures. Replacement teeth help fill out your smile by giving support to the cheeks and lips. In many cases dental implants can be placed to provide support , preventing partial or full dentures from shifting in your mouth. Dental implants can give you denture stability, confidence and your natural bite.

Dentures aren’t what they used to be. New advances in dentistry enable us to improve the fit, comfort and appearance of dentures.

Root Canals

Root canal treatment (Endodontics) treats disorders of the nerve of a tooth. It used to be that a tooth with a diseased or infected nerve had to be removed. In 95 percent of the cases today, however, this is no longer true. We believe in saving teeth (instead of removing them). We make every effort to help you keep your natural teeth for a lifetime!

TMJ - Temporomandibular Joint Dysfunction

Do you wake up with a tired, stiff or painful jaw? Are your teeth sensitive to cold drinks? If you answered yes to either of these questions, you may be grinding or clenching your teeth during sleep. Grinding of the teeth is a medical condition called bruxism. Over time, bruxism will result in the wearing down of your natural tooth enamel. Treatment aims to relieve severe tooth, jaw or facial muscle pain and protect your existing teeth against the effects of bruxism.
