Our CBCT Imaging Services in San Francisco

Your Appointment at Reveal Diagnostics San Francisco Opening hours: Monday – Friday, 8:00am to 5:00pm

Your comfort is our primary concern. Please let us know in advance if you have any special needs and we will gladly accommodate you. Most visits to our dental imaging centers take less than 30 minutes. If we are also scanning your denture, please allow 45 minutes. We suggest that, if you are able to have someone drive you, you ask them to circle the block a few times as the appointment will not take long.

Otherwise the can use Union Square Garage 333 Post Street San Francisco (415) 397-0631 (between Powell & Stockton - entrance on Geary)

Please remember:

  • Bring the referral form your doctor gave you
  • Be ready to remove jewelry, piercings and hair pins
  • Notify the technician if you think you are pregnant
  • Expect to pay at the time of service
