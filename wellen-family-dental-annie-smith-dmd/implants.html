Implants Wellen Family Dental

A dental implant is a permanent long term solution to replace teeth. They are made out of titanium and are surgically placed into your jaw bone. Depending on your bone structure sometimes a buildup is required. During the buildup phase, bone membrane is placed into the missing tooth cavity to increase surface area and improve implant integration rates.
