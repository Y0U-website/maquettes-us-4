A large portion of what we do here at Symmetry is focused on guiding the growing face in the right direction. An overwhelming number of children in today’s population develop habits.

Several common habits or issues can contribute to aberrant or irregular growth in your child’s jaws. For example, behaviors such as thumb sucking, tongue thrusting, and mouth breathing often play a role. In addition, irregular swallowing, grinding, and various other factors may also impact proper jaw development.
