Clinical Background & Dental Education: Dr. Bouzoghlanian

Dr. Vasag Bouzoghlanian was born in Los Angeles and has lived in Southern California his entire life. It has been his greatest privilege to establish a private practice in his hometown of Pasadena, and serve his community.

Dr. Bouzoghlanian attended prestigious UCLA and earned a Bachelors degree in Molecular, Cell, Developmental Biology in 2003. He graduated top honors with a 4.0GPA, summa cum laude honors, completed the College Honors program, and was on the Dean’s List during his entire education.

Because of outstanding academic records and personal achievement, Dr. Bouzoghlanian was awarded the honorary UC Regents Scholarship, the MOST PRESTIGIOUS award given to a student by the UC.

Dr. Bouzoghlanian continued his academic excellence at the world-renowned UCLA School of Dentistry, where he earned his degree in 2007 with cum laude top honors. He remained on the Dean’s List and continued his UC Regents status throughout dental school, and was recognized with many awards and honors (see below), including acceptance into Omicron Kappa Upsilon, a national dental honor society where membership is reserved for graduates at the top 10 % of their class.

Following the UCLA School of Dentistry, Dr. Bouzoghlanian completed a residency at the Department of Veterans Affairs in Long Beach to obtain advanced training in surgery, dental implants , complex reconstruction, and managing patients with complex medical problems.
