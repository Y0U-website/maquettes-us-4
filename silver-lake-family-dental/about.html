About Us

At Silver Lake Family Dental, our goal is simple: to provide quality dental care that’s stress free. By using the industry’s latest tools and providing simple comforts like blankets, WiFi, and televisions in each room, our office aims to create personalized care that meets your top priorities.

Busy schedule? No problem! We want to make your appointment easy and convenient, so we offer evening and Saturday openings. It’s the small things that sets Silver Lake Family Dental head and shoulders above the rest.

Members of our highly qualified team consistently adhere to the latest standards in the dental industry, and we are members in the American Dental Association and the California Dental Association.

What Sets Us Apart From Everyone Else

We are conservative, preventive dentists, and have implemented state-of-the-art dental technology into our practice. We offer low-radiation, digital X-rays and provide each patient with an intraoral photography tour of their mouth, viewed on a large television screen.

Our main goal is to be sure that each patient leaves our practice knowing what the problem is, what it looks like, what can be done to fix it, and how to prevent it in the future.
