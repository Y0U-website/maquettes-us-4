Dr. Nicholas Politis

Dr. Nicholas Politis strives to provide the highest quality care and personalized patient experiences, guided by compassion and education. He enjoys working closely with patients to promote lifelong oral health.

Born and raised in River Forest, IL, Dr. Politis believes in a team approach to oral care, aligning patient goals with best practices to provide dependable outcomes. Dentistry has also been a meaningful part of his family for generations. Dr. Politis is a third-generation dentist, being the grandson of a general dentist and son of a periodontist.

After graduating from Marquette University School of Dentistry, Dr. Politis completed the General Practice Residency program at Advocate Illinois Masonic Hospital. His residency provided more comprehensive training in treating medically compromised, pediatric, and patients with special needs.

Dr. Politis takes pride in giving back to his hometown, helping Oak Park and the surrounding communities continue to smile for years to come. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys exploring new foods around the Chicagoland area, spending time with his family, and continuing his childhood hobbies of drawing and model building.
