Dr. Brandon Murphy

Office: 100 S Kenilworth Ave Oak Park, IL 60302

Dr. Brandon Murphy is a proud native of Illinois, raised in the southern suburbs of Chicago. With a deep commitment to providing exceptional dental care, Dr. Murphy brings both expertise and a compassionate touch to every patient interaction. Before founding Murphy Dental Group, PLLC, he gained invaluable experience serving as an associate dentist for several years.

Dr. Murphy earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree from the University of Illinois at Chicago and pursued advanced training through a General Practice Residency at the Advocate Illinois Masonic Medical Center. During his residency, he received specialized training in managing medically compromised patients, sedation dentistry, and advanced dental implant procedures. His dedication to continuous learning has allowed him to expand his scope of practice and refine his skills to meet the diverse needs of his patients.

In addition to his clinical work, Dr. Murphy holds a faculty appointment at Advocate Illinois Masonic Medical Center, where he shares his knowledge and mentors future dentists. With over 1,200 hours of continuing education, he remains at the forefront of advancements in dentistry, particularly in CADCAM dentistry and clear aligner therapy.

Dr. Murphy takes great pride in helping his patients achieve beautiful, healthy smiles and strives to create a gentle, comfortable experience for every individual. His approach to dentistry is rooted in trust, expertise, and personalized care.

In his personal life, Dr. Murphy is an avid film enthusiast with a knack for recommending must-watch movies. A resident of Chicago, he enjoys exploring the city’s vibrant restaurant scene, cooking at home, and staying active to maintain a healthy lifestyle. For Dr. Murphy, a great smile isn’t just a professional goal—it’s a way of life.
