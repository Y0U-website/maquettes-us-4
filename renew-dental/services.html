A Healthier Smile Begins Here

At Renew Dental Care, in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, we provide comprehensive dental services for your entire family. Proper oral care is important to maintain your overall physical health. As your local dentist office, our goal is to ensure you get the care you deserve. Our knowledgeable and experienced dental professionals can anticipate your oral health needs and ensure you have a bright, healthy smile. We provide general, restorative, and cosmetic dentistry services for adults, teens, and children. Contact us today to schedule your next appointment.

General Dentistry

Renew Dental Care provides complete general dentistry services for our patients. Dental cleanings and exams should be performed at least every six months to monitor your oral health. Our general dentistry services allow us to uncover any potential issues with your teeth or gums, including decay, gingivitis, as well as certain cancers. We also offer emergency dental services in the event of dental trauma. Learn more about our general dentistry services.

Restorative Dentistry

With restorative dentistry, our team can restore form and function back to your teeth, which will allow you to speak, eat, and digest your food better. We utilize the latest in dental technology to provide you with natural-looking results that will last. Our dental prosthetics are custom designed to fit your mouth to maximize comfort. Schedule your restorative dentistry consultation today and discover how we can assist you.

Cosmetic Dentistry

A smile that you love is crucial for your self-confidence, and the dental professionals at Renew Dental Care can help you achieve it with our cosmetic dentistry services. We combine effective treatment options carried out by knowledgeable and experienced dentists to produce exceptional results for your teeth. Whether you’d like to correct a crooked tooth, brighten your smile, or fill gaps between your teeth, we have the perfect solutions for you. Our dentists have access to cutting-edge dental technology to ensure you get natural-looking results that you’ll love.

Clear Aligner

Clear Aligner is the solution our dentists’ trust for patients who want straighter teeth. With thousands of satisfied patients, Clear Aligner has been leading the way in invisible aligners. We utilize 3D technology to create a custom plan to shift your teeth to a new, straighter position gradually. Clear Aligner can be worn discretely throughout the day without the need for wires and brackets.
