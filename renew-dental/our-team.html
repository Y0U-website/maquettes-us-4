Meet the Team

Our Mission: Your Health & Comfort

At Renew Dental, our team shares a common mission: to support our patients’ oral health while building a relationship of trust and respect. We are here for you at every step, whether you’re scheduling routine cleanings, exploring treatment options, or simply asking questions about your care.

Schedule your visit today, and experience a team that’s truly invested in your smile and overall health. We look forward to welcoming you to our Renew Dental family!

Dr. Jade Koch

Lead Dentist

With a passion for refreshing smiles and building lasting relationships, Dr. Jade Koch is at the heart of Renew Dental. Dr. Koch is committed to providing personalized, gentle dental care for patients of all ages, from young children to adults. Her patient-first approach ensures that every treatment plan is tailored to each individual’s needs, and she takes the time to listen, understand, and support her patients’ dental health goals.

Dr. Koch is a wife and proud mother of two young children, which inspires her focus on creating a family-friendly environment at Renew Dental. She understands the importance of trust in a dental relationship and works to ensure that every patient, from children to adults, feels valued and at ease.

Education and Experience: Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from University of Nebraska Medical Center-College of Dentistry

Committed to continuing education to stay at the forefront of dental innovations and techniques, Dr. Koch integrates the latest advancements into her practice for the benefit of her patients.

Hobbies and Interests: When she’s not caring for patients, Dr. Koch enjoys spending time with her family, exploring the beautiful outdoors of Sioux Falls, and supporting her children’s activities.
