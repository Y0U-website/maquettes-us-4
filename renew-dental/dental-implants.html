Dental Implant Procedure

Our team at Priority Dental Care has decades of combined experience with the dental implant process. A dental implant consists of two parts: the tooth and the abutment. The titanium abutment is surgically placed in your jawbone. Not only does it help secure the abutment, but the titanium piece will also help stimulate jawbone growth. Once the abutment has been placed, we will permanently affix an artificial tooth specially crafted to match the rest of your teeth. Once the tooth has been healed, you will notice an improvement in your ability to speak, chew, and digest food.
