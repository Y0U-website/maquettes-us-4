Treatment

Today, orthodontic care has made many advanced strides from traditional methods to the most inventive in the last decade. Traditional metal wire braces gradually straighten teeth with the aid of brackets and metal wires to keep teeth from shifting. The treatment period can last for many years to get you a picture-perfect smile.

Clear braces are a nearly undetectable set of aligners for teeth. They are conveniently removable and only needs to be in the mouth up to 22 hours per day!
