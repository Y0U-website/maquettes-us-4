How it Works

An impression of your teeth will be taken, so the titanium post can match your natural teeth and bite. The titanium post will be anchored in your jawbone and you will be provided with anesthesia to alleviate the pain.

After the post heals properly with your jawbone, a dental crown can be affixed on top. This makes your smile appear seamless and naturally beautiful.
