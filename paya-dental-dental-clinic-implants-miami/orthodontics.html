Frequently Asked Questions About Orthodontics

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

The length of treatment varies based on your needs. Most Invisalign or braces cases take between 12 to 24 months, but some may be shorter or longer depending on complexity.

What is the best age to start orthodontic treatment?

While orthodontics can be done at any age, early intervention typically starts between ages 7 and 10. However, adults can also see excellent results with Invisalign or braces.

Does Invisalign work as well as braces?

In many cases, yes. Invisalign is highly effective for mild to moderate misalignment. For more complex cases, braces may be recommended.

Will orthodontic treatment hurt?

You may experience temporary discomfort during adjustments or when switching aligners, but it&#x27;s generally mild and manageable.

Can I eat normally with braces?

You’ll need to avoid sticky or hard foods with braces. Invisalign offers more dietary freedom since the trays are removable when eating.
