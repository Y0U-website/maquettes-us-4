Implant Overdentures in Miami & Hialeah

Overdentures are especially beneficial for patients who cannot tolerate full removable dentures or those who have lost significant bone but still qualify for dental implants.

  • Better preservation of the jawbone compared to traditional dentures
  • Enhanced bite force for improved function
  • Long-lasting results with proper care
  • No messy adhesives required

Our Step-by-Step Implant Overdenture Process

At Paya Dental, our experienced clinicians—Dr. Anabel Paya and Dr. Adeleydis Quintana—work closely with each patient to create a customized treatment plan. Here&#x27;s what to expect during the process:

  • Consultation & Imaging: We perform a full oral evaluation with digital scans to determine implant candidacy.
  • Implant Placement: A series of small implants (typically 2–4 per arch) are surgically placed in the jaw.
  • Healing Period: A healing phase allows the implants to fuse with the bone (osseointegration).
  • Overdenture Fitting: Once the implants are stable, a custom overdenture is crafted to attach securely to the implants.
  • Final Adjustments: We ensure proper alignment, comfort, and esthetics before finalizing.

FAQs About Implant Overdentures

How long do implant overdentures last? With regular maintenance, the implants can last decades, and the overdenture itself often lasts 5–10 years or more.

Are implant overdentures removable? Yes. They can be taken out for cleaning, but they lock securely in place when worn, offering the feel of natural teeth.

Will insurance cover the procedure? Coverage varies. Many plans cover part of the cost for dentures or implants. Visit our patient information page for help verifying your benefits.

How do overdentures compare to All-on-Fours? All-on-Fours offer a permanent fixed prosthesis, while overdentures are removable and typically require fewer implants—making them a more budget-friendly option.

Is the procedure painful? Most patients report mild discomfort after implant surgery, which resolves within days. We provide detailed aftercare instructions and follow-up to ensure healing.
