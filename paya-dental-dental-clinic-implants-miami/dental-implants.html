Our Dental Implant Solutions

At Paya Dental, we offer a range of implant-based solutions tailored to your needs.

1. Single Tooth Implants

A single dental implant with a customized porcelain crown restores one missing tooth seamlessly. This is an excellent alternative to a dental bridge, as it does not require modifying adjacent teeth.

2. All-on-Four Dental Implants

Ideal for patients missing all teeth in an arch, All-on-Four implants replace a full set of teeth using only four implants. This minimally invasive procedure provides permanent, stable dentures that look and feel natural.

3. Implant-Supported Dentures

Unlike traditional dentures, implant-supported dentures snap securely onto implants, preventing slipping, discomfort, and difficulty eating.

4. Full-Mouth Reconstruction with Dental Implants

For patients needing a complete smile makeover, we create a personalized treatment plan that may include implants, crowns, and other restorative treatments.

The Dental Implant Process at Paya Dental

Same-day implants may be available for qualifying patients.

  • Consultation & 3D Imaging – We assess your oral health and use advanced imaging technology to plan precise implant placement.
  • Implant Placement Surgery – A biocompatible titanium implant is placed in your jawbone.
  • Healing & Osseointegration – The implant fuses with the bone over a few months, creating a strong foundation.
  • Abutment & Crown Placement – A custom porcelain crown, bridge, or denture is attached to complete your new smile.
