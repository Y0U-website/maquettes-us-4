Les soins

A single dental implant with a customized porcelain crown restores one missing tooth seamlessly. This is an excellent alternative to a dental bridge, as it does not require modifying adjacent teeth.

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed in the jawbone to replace a missing tooth&#x27;s root. Once healed, it provides a stable foundation for a custom dental crown, bridge, or denture, restoring the appearance and function of your teeth.
