Meet Dr. Renee Kirkpatrick

I was born and raised in Southeastern Michigan. I completed my undergraduate degree in Health Sciences from Kalamazoo College in 1998 and graduated from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Dentistry. I have worked in family practice dental offices since

graduation. I enjoy all aspects of general dentistry including cosmetic, restorative, crown and bridge, pediatric dentistry, dentures and extractions.

Of the interests I have outside of the office, the biggest is family. I am married and the proud mom of 3 boys. We call Fenton our home. I’m also very involved with animal rescue. We have a small farm and love to be surrounded by animals. We have dogs,

cats, guinea pigs, chinchillas, chickens and more. If you like to talk about animals, I’m your gal.

Thank you for taking the time to find out about me and our office. I hope to meet you soon.
