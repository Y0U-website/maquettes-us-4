Destin Dental Cleanings and Services

Regular dental cleanings help to avoid future dental problems and are essential to maintaining good oral hygiene. Even with regular brushing and flossing, mineralized plaque can still form. Professional Destin Dental cleanings by one of our dental hygienists can eliminate it, especially from hard-to-reach places. Every six months, we advise getting your teeth professionally cleaned.

☑ Removal of Tartar and Plaque: Destin Dental cleanings remove tartar and plaque that has accumulated over time and may not be entirely removed by routine brushing and flossing. This aids in preventing gum disease and tooth decay.

☑ Preventing Gum Disease: By eliminating bacteria and treating early signs of inflammation, routine cleanings can aid in the prevention or management of gum disease (gingivitis and periodontitis).

☑ Fresher Breath: By getting rid of the germs that cause mouth odor, Destin dental cleanings can greatly reduce bad breath, or halitosis.

☑ Stain Removal and Whiter Teeth: Dental cleanings can get rid of surface stains and brighten your teeth, which will improve the appearance of your smile.

☑ Early Problem Detection: During cleanings, dental hygienists and dentists can spot possible problems including cavities, oral cancer, and misaligned teeth, allowing for prompt treatment.

☑ Benefits to Overall Health: Maintaining good dental hygiene is associated with general health, and routine cleanings can lower the risk of systemic conditions including diabetes, heart disease, and respiratory disorders.
