☑ What are Dental Implants: Destin dental implants are prosthetic tooth roots surgically inserted into the mandible from biocompatible materials like titanium. They provide a long-term solution for people who have lost one or more teeth as a result of trauma, disease, or other oral health problems by acting as a strong foundation for replacement teeth.

☑ The Process of Dental Implants: Usually, the dental implant procedure involves several steps:

▪ Evaluation: A thorough dental examination is the first step on your path to a restored smile. Your oral health and bone density will be evaluated by our skilled staff at Lazenby Dental to see if you are a good candidate for dental implants.

▪ Surgical Placement: A small surgical incision is made to insert the dental implant into the jawbone. The fake tooth or teeth that will be attached in the following step are anchored by this implant.

▪ Osseointegration: The jawbone and implant require time to fuse together following implant implantation. This guarantees that the replacement teeth will have a solid and sturdy basis.

▪ Abutment Placement: An abutment is affixed to the implant following the completion of osseointegration. The prosthetic tooth and the implant are connected by the abutment.

▪ Restoration: Lastly, the abutment is fitted with a specially manufactured dental crown, bridge, or denture. Both in terms of appearance and functioning, this replacement closely mimics real teeth.

Destin Dental Implant Advantages

Why should you think about getting dental implants?

▪ Enhanced Aesthetics: Destin dental implants are made to resemble real teeth in both appearance and functionality. They create a stunning and harmonious smile by blending in smoothly with your natural teeth.

▪ Restored Oral Function: It can be difficult to chew, speak, and eat correctly when you are missing teeth. Because Destin dental implants offer a strong foundation, you can eat the things you love and talk with assurance.

▪ Durability and Longevity: Dental implants have a lifetime of potential with proper upkeep. They are therefore among the most durable options for replacing lost teeth.

▪ Prevents Bone Loss: By stimulating the jawbone, dental implants in Destin help to stop the normal bone loss that happens when teeth are lost. This preserves the general health of your teeth and the structure of your face.

▪ Simple Maintenance: Taking care of dental implants is just like taking care of your natural teeth. It only takes routine dental exams, brushing, and flossing to keep them in optimal condition.
