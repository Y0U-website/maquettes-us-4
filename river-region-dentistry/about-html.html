Meet The Team

Dr. Harrell Bullard DMD, MAGD

Dr. Bullard has been practicing cosmetic and general dentistry in Montgomery, AL since 1987 and is dedicated to delivering professional, customized care for those in his community. He is a member of the American Dental Association, the Academy of General Dentistry, the Alabama Dental Association, the Seattle Study Club, and the International Congress of Oral Implantologists. Comfortable in a number of dental disciplines, Dr. Bullard served as Vice President of the Alabama Academy of General Dentistry in 2023. He is serving as President Elect of the Academy in 2024.

Dr Bullard is an Eagle Scout and has enjoyed volunteering with the Boy Scouts of America in several capacities over the years including several years service as a Commissioner. He is an active member of the First United Methodist Church and has gone on three Amazon Health Care Mission trips to provide care to those in need. In addition to his trips to the Amazon, he has also been to Ecuador and Haiti three times. His most recent trip was to Bolivia. In his free time, Dr. Bullard enjoys spending time with his wife, Kim, whom he married in 1992, and their two daughters.

Our Dental Philosophy

Personalized Care

We know that excellent care goes beyond your dental condition. Our friendly team takes the time to listen to your needs and desires so we can get to know you personally. This allows us to provide a high-quality treatment plan customized to your individual needs. Our ultimate goal is to help you achieve and maintain your maximum oral health.

Compassion

Excellence

At Dr. Bullard’s practice, we aim for excellence in all aspects of our care for you. We maintain an on-time schedule and act in a courteous and professional manner. Our organized and efficient approach to dentistry allows us to provide exceptional care for you. In our office, we utilize advanced technology, such as digital x-rays, intraoral cameras and nitrous oxide for your comfort and convenience. We strive to deliver the highest quality of care around.

Education

We are passionate about education for our patients and our team. We explain dental procedures and all treatment options in a clear manner that allows you to make informed, confident decisions regarding your care. We are also committed to maintaining the highest level of training for ourselves so we can stay up-to-date on the latest dental techniques and methods, all to better serve you. We know that proper education is key to good overall health.

If you value these qualities in a dentist, call us today! We’re passionate about your smile and passionate about helping you achieve optimal health!
