How Many Appointments Are Needed?

Visit 1: Surgery & Temporary Teeth

  • Tooth extractions (if needed)
  • Implant placement (usually 4 6 per arch)
  • Bone grafting (if required)
  • Placement of temporary acrylic dentures/bridges
  • Tomography

Healing Time (Back Home)

  • Osseointegration (implants fuse with the jawbone)
  • You wear temporary teeth during this period

Visit 2

  • Digital Impressions for final bridge
  • Additional adjustments
  • Try-ins
  • Personal preference for more detailed customization

Visit 3

  • Fitting and placement of permanent teeth (zirconia or acrylic prosthesis with titanium)
  • Bite adjustment, esthetics, and polish
