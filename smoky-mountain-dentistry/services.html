Crowns & Bridges

A crown, also known as a cap, is a cover that encases a damaged or decayed tooth to restore its shape, size, and strength. On the other hand, a bridge is an option for replacing one or more missing teeth, and it’s made up of two or more crowns for the teeth on either side of the gap (abutment teeth) and false teeth in between.
