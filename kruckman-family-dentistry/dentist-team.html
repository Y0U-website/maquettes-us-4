Dentist Team

Dr. Jalissa Kruckman was born and raised in Glencoe, Minnesota, and graduated from Glencoe-Silver Lake High School. She attended The College of Saint Benedict for her undergraduate degree in Biology. She then attended the University of Minnesota School Of Dentistry and graduated in 2010. Since then she has been able to fulfill her passion of helping others obtain their brightest, healthiest smiles as a practitioner of dentistry, periodontics , and endodontics serving the Carver, Waconia, and Victoria area.

"I am continually blown away by the ever-changing and advancing dental techniques and technology in my field, and I am proud to be able to share with my patients the benefits of my continuing education.”

Dr. Kruckman and her husband, Chad, have two children, Kinsley and Brayden, who keep them busy and happy. In addition to spending time with her family and friends, Dr. Kruckman enjoys golfing and spending time at her family cabin.

Dr. Taylor Sawyer grew up in Waconia, MN and graduated from Waconia High School. She then attended the University of Minnesota and received a degree in Biology and a minor in Public Health before being accepted into dental school. Choosing to continue her education at the University of Minnesota School of Dentistry, she received her Doctorate of Dental Surgery. She has also been actively involved with the American Student Dental Association as well as events such as Give Kids a Smile Day and Special Smiles.

“I am thrilled to be a family dentist for a community and practicing in an office where I can provide dentistry for the whole family. I take the time to listen to my patients, lay out treatment options to meet each person’s needs, and provide care with a strong attention to detail. Like Dr. Kruckman, I see the immense value in continuing education and being able to provide the most up to date care for you and your family.”

In her free time, Dr. Sawyer enjoys spending time with her family and cooking with her and her husband Dalton, daughter Molly and son Tate!

“I cannot imagine a career I would love more than dentistry and I am proud to offer exceptional dental care for the whole family. I believe dentistry is an ever changing and developing field and it is very important to stay up to date, so I may offer the latest treatment options.”

Dr. Klinkenberg and his wife, Erin, have two children. In their free time they enjoy biking, being outside, and exploring their city.
