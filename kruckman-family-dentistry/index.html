Dental implants are a popular and effective tooth replacement option for patients who are missing one or more teeth. A dental implant is a small titanium post that is inserted into the jawbone to act as a new “root” for a replacement tooth. The implants are then topped with a dental crown, bridge, or denture, depending on the number of teeth that need to be replaced.

Dental implants are a popular choice for replacing missing teeth because they provide a strong, stable foundation for your dental prosthetic. That’s because they provide advantages over other tooth replacement options, such as dental bridges and dentures. They are permanent, durable, and look and feel more like natural teeth.
