Dr. Laura Rammer - Dentist and Owner

Dr. Laura Rammer graduated from Marquette University’s School of Dentistry in 2007. She worked as an associate with her Marquette School of Dentistry mentor, Dr. Paul Gruber for three years prior to purchasing the dental practice in July, 2010.

In 2011, Dr. Laura received the esteemed "New Dentist Leadership Award" from The Wisconsin Dental Association. On November 1, 2013, Dr. Laura was inducted into the International College of Dentists . Dr. Laura was named Sheboygan County’s 2013 Entrepreneur of the Year and a Top-10 Young Professional in both 2011 and 2013 by Coastal Connections’ Young Professionals Network of Sheboygan County. She was named 2013 Outstanding Young Wisconsinite at the mid-September WI Jaycee Convention. In 2014, Dr. Laura was inducted into the Pierre Fauchard Academy.

In 2019, Dr. Laura was awarded the prestigious Young Alumna of the Year Award by Marquette University’s School of Dentistry. This award recognizes significant professional achievement, personal responsibility, and service to Marquette and community before the age of 40.

Dr. Laura Rammer is an active member of the Sheboygan County Dental Association and has held leadership positions in the organization including President and Programming Chair. As an active member of the Wisconsin Dental Association , Dr. Laura is a member of the WDA’s Mission of Mercy committee (MOM) and the WDA’s Membership Committee. Dr. Laura also serves on the Sheboygan County Oral Health Partnership committee. Dr. Laura is a member of the American Equilibration Society.

In order to serve her patients at the highest level, Dr. Laura eagerly and regularly attends continuing education courses at county, state and national levels.

Outside of the dental practice, Dr. Laura is a Maywood Advisory Board member, Sheboygan County Holstein Association Member and Sheboygan Economic Club Member.

Dr. Laura is married to Josh DePagter, a master electrician who owns his own business, DePagter Electric. Together, they enjoy cheering for the Milwaukee Brewers. Dr. Laura also enjoys raising and exhibiting show-quality Holstein dairy cattle, attending cattle shows and sales, bike riding, playing piano, watching sports, letter-writing, reading, and visiting with friends and family. Together, Laura and Josh are active members within their First Presbyterian-Oostburg congregation..
