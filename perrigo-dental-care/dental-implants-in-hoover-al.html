Sometimes, a dental bridge isn’t a viable option for replacing a missing tooth. If you are missing too many teeth for a dental bridge, or if your existing teeth aren’t healthy enough to be used for support, we may recommend dental implants to restore your smile.

What Is a Dental Implant?

Are you missing teeth and looking for a permanent solution that feels and looks natural? With a dental implant, your new artificial tooth will be fixed in place with a steel root. The gum tissue and jaw will accept this new root as if it were your natural tooth. Dental implants are a great solution for tooth replacement, as they act, look, and feel like your natural teeth. They also offer unparalleled benefits, including improved oral health, enhanced confidence, and long-lasting results. Say goodbye to gaps and hello to a perfect smile.

Teeth implants are a wonderful choice because the titanium root of an implant is biocompatible. This means you won’t need to worry about your body rejecting the implant. Your bone will grow around it and adhere to the artificial root in a natural way. This prevents bone loss as well, which can be a huge problem for people who have lost teeth.
