Dental Hygiene and Periodontal Health Cottleville, MO

All dental restorative options must have a good foundation. Even the best dental work will fail if it is not well maintained. At Brown Family Dental Care, we have our greatest success with patients that understand this commitment. Routine hygiene visits are an important way to ensure your teeth are healthy and will remain healthy for decades to come. At these visits, we’ll check your teeth for any potential problems, and we’ll perform a routine cleaning to promote ongoing oral health. In some instances, a deeper cleaning may be necessary that would take place at a separate appointment.

Many dental problems can be easily avoided by personal commitment to home care and bi-annual visits to see your dental hygienist. We can talk to you about establishing better habits that keep your teeth healthy and white.

Oral health is not just about your teeth. Dental hygiene also impacts your periodontal health. Periodontal issues are related to your gums, and poor dental hygiene can lead to gum damage and disease. We’ll help you maintain your gums with cleaning, treatment, and ongoing care recommendations.

This is all part of how we care for our patients at Brown Family Dental Care. Your dental hygiene matters to us, and we want to partner with you for a lifetime of smiles. Don’t let the dentist be a place only for emergencies.
