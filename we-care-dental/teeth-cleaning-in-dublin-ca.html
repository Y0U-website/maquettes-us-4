The Hidden Risks of Skipping Routine Visits

Nearly 1 in 4 adults skips regular dental visits, often due to anxiety, cost concerns, or busy schedules. However, the implications go far beyond a little plaque buildup:

  • Gum Disease: Gingivitis, the earliest stage of gum disease, is reversible with professional care. Left unchecked, it progresses to periodontitis, which destroys bone and is linked to heart disease and diabetes ( National Institute of Dental Research ).
  • Tooth Loss: The American College of Prosthodontists reports that 178 million Americans are missing at least one tooth—often due to untreated decay or gum disease.
  • Oral Cancer: Dr. Lardizabal screens for lesions or discoloration during exams. Early detection improves survival rates from 60 % to 90 % ( Oral Cancer Foundation ).

Debunking Myths: “My Teeth Feel Fine—Do I Really Need a Checkup?”

Many patients assume no pain equals no problems. Unfortunately, dental issues like cavities or gum disease are often silent until they reach advanced stages. For example:

Routine visits allow us to intervene before symptoms arise, saving you time, discomfort, and expense.

  • A small cavity might not hurt until it reaches the nerve.
  • Gum recession can occur without noticeable bleeding.
  • Oral cancer lesions may look like harmless canker sores.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should I get my teeth cleaned? Most people should have professional teeth cleanings every six months. However, Dr. Lardizabal may recommend more frequent cleanings based on your individual oral health needs.

2. What happens during a teeth cleaning? During a cleaning, our hygienist will remove plaque and tartar, polish your teeth, and floss. We may also perform a fluoride treatment to strengthen your enamel

3. Are dental cleanings painful? Dental cleanings shouldn’t be painful. If you experience discomfort, let us know, and we can use numbing gels or other techniques to ensure your comfort

4. How long does a dental cleaning take? A typical dental cleaning usually takes between 30 minutes to an hour

5. Do you offer deep cleanings? Yes, we offer deep cleanings (scaling and root planing) for patients with more advanced gum disease.

6. Will my insurance cover teeth cleanings? Most dental insurance plans cover preventive care like regular cleanings. We can help you understand your coverage.

7. Can I get my teeth cleaned while pregnant? Yes, it’s safe and important to maintain your oral health during pregnancy. Let us know if you’re expecting so we can provide appropriate care.

8. Do dental cleanings whiten teeth? While cleanings can remove surface stains, they don’t actually whiten teeth. We can discuss professional whitening options if you’re interested.

9. How should I prepare for my dental cleaning? Continue your regular oral hygiene routine and brush before your appointment. Be ready to share any concerns or changes in your oral health.

10. What if I have dental anxiety? We understand dental anxiety and offer a gentle approach. Dr. Lardizabal can discuss sedation options to help you feel more comfortable during your visit.
