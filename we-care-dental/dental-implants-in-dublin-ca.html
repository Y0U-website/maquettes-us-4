FAQs About Dental Implants

1. What are dental implants, and how do they work? Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed in your jawbone to replace missing teeth roots. They fuse with the bone (osseointegration) and support crowns, bridges, or dentures. Unlike dentures, they prevent bone loss and mimic natural teeth in function and appearance.

2. How long does the dental implant process take in Dublin? The full process takes 3–6 months on average. This includes healing time for osseointegration. Complex cases (e.g., bone grafting) may extend to 12–18 months.

3. Are dental implants painful? Most patients report less discomfort than a tooth extraction. Local anesthesia minimizes pain during surgery, and over-the-counter pain relievers manage post-op soreness.

4. Am I a candidate if I have bone loss? Yes! Dublin clinics like We Care Dental offer bone grafting to rebuild jawbone density, ensuring implant stability. A CT scan during your consultation assesses bone health.

5. What’s the cost of implants in Dublin, and does insurance cover them? Single implant: $4,000–$6,000 Full arch: $15,000–$25,000 Local insurers like Delta Dental and Aetna often cover 25–50 % if medically necessary. Flexible financing (e.g., CareCredit) is available.

6. How long do implants last? With proper care, implants last 25+ years—often a lifetime. Crowns may need replacement every 10–15 years.

7. Are there age restrictions for implants? No! Seniors in Dublin/San Ramon are common candidates. Eligibility depends on bone health, not age. Teens must wait until jaw growth completes.

9. How painful is the procedure? With modern pain management techniques, most patients report mild discomfort, similar to a tooth extraction.

10. Can implants fail? While rare, factors like poor oral hygiene or smoking increase risks. Regular check-ups at our office mitigate this.
