10 Essential FAQs About Dental Deep Cleaning

1. What exactly is dental deep cleaning? Deep cleaning (scaling & root planing) removes plaque/tartar below gumlines using specialized tools, targeting early gum disease (gingivitis/periodontitis).

2. How does it differ from regular cleaning? How does it differ from regular cleaning?Regular cleanings focus on tooth surfaces above gums, while deep cleaning addresses sub-gum bacterial colonies causing gum disease.

3. Does the procedure hurt? Most patients feel only pressure due to topical/local anesthesia. Post-procedure tenderness typically resolves within 72 hours.

4. How long does a deep cleaning take? 1-2 visits of 45-90 minutes each, depending on gum disease severity. Half-mouth treatments per visit are common.

5. Why might I need this treatment? Indications include bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, gum recession, pockets 4mm, or visible tartar below gums.

6. What’s the cost range? $180–$450 per quadrant (mouth section). Most PPOs cover 80–100 % if gum disease is diagnosed.

7. Will my insurance cover it? Yes, if periodontal disease is confirmed via pocket measurements/X-rays. Payment plans/CareCredit are often available.

8. What’s recovery like? Mild sensitivity/discomfort for 1-3 days. Avoid hard/spicy foods initially. Maintain gentle brushing/flossing.

9. How often is deep cleaning needed? How often is deep cleaning needed?Typically once, followed by 3-4 month maintenance cleanings to prevent recurrence.

10. Are there risks? Rare complications include infection or temporary gum sensitivity. Proper aftercare minimizes risks .
