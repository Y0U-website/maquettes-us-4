What the process looks like

  • Consultation. Dr. Hamad reviews your history, examines the site, and captures a low-dose 3D scan to evaluate bone volume and nerve position.
  • Planning. We digitally plan the implant position for the best long-term restorative result — not just the easiest surgical path.
  • Placement. The implant is placed under local anesthesia in an appointment most patients describe as easier than a routine extraction.
  • Healing. Over roughly three to six months, bone integrates with the implant. You wear a temporary during this period so you&#x27;re never without a tooth.
  • Restoration. A final crown, bridge, or full-arch prosthesis is fitted and adjusted. Many cases benefit from our in-house lab for faster, more precise fits.

Same-day and in-house lab support

For qualifying cases, our on-site lab lets us design and fabricate restorations without sending impressions off site. That can mean fewer visits, shorter timelines, and closer collaboration between Dr. Hamad and the technician shaping your restoration.

Frequently asked questions

Most single-implant cases span three to six months from placement to final crown, allowing bone to integrate with the implant. Timelines vary by patient health, bone density, and whether grafting is needed.

The procedure is performed under local anesthesia; most patients report the recovery is easier than they expected. We provide detailed after-care instructions and are always reachable if you need us.

A brief clinical evaluation and 3D scan tell us whether you have the bone volume and gum health for a predictable result. Some patients need grafting first; others are ready right away.

Fees depend on the number of implants, whether grafting is required, and the restoration type. We provide a clear written estimate at your consultation and can help you plan through insurance and financing.

With good hygiene and regular checkups, implants routinely last decades. The crown or bridge on top may need refreshing sooner than the implant itself.
