Combining aligners with cosmetic work

Straight teeth are often the foundation for whitening, bonding, or veneers to look their best. When it fits your goals, Dr. Hamad will sequence aligners first so any cosmetic work afterward is minimal and conservative.

Individual results vary. A clinical evaluation is required to confirm candidacy and expected timeline.

Frequently asked questions

Most adult cases finish in six to eighteen months. Minor corrections may finish sooner; complex cases can take longer. Dr. Hamad will give you an estimated timeline after reviewing your scan.

Fees depend on case complexity and the number of aligners. We provide a written estimate at your consultation and can typically bill dental insurance orthodontic benefits when they apply.

For the majority of mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues, aligners work as well as fixed braces — and they&#x27;re removable for eating and brushing. Some cases still call for braces; Dr. Hamad will tell you honestly which fits you best.

Aligners work best worn 20 to 22 hours per day, removed only for meals, drinks other than water, and brushing. Consistency is the single biggest predictor of on-time results.

You&#x27;ll wear a retainer — usually a clear one that looks like an aligner — to hold your new alignment. We&#x27;ll build a retainer plan tailored to your case so results last.
