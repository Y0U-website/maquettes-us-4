All-on-4 Dental Implants

If you’re missing all or most of your teeth in one arch and are seeking a fixed, long-lasting solution, All-on-4 dental implants may be the ideal option. This advanced treatment, often referred to as fixed full-arch dental implants, replaces a complete row of teeth using just four strategically placed implants.

Many consider all-on-4 to be the next best thing to natural teeth, delivering stability, functionality, and a natural appearance without the need for removable dentures.

What Is All-on-4?

This advanced technique allows patients to walk away with a fixed, complete smile that looks, feels, and functions like natural teeth, often in just one day.

  • All remaining teeth in an arch are extracted (if needed).
  • We place four or more implants with precision into the jawbone to form a stable foundation.
  • We then attach a custom-crafted full arch of prosthetic teeth securely to the implants.

Key Benefits of All-on-4 Implants

  • Fixed and Secure. Unlike removable dentures, All-on-4 implants stay in place. No slipping, clicking, or adhesives are required.
  • Natural Look and Feel. Custom-designed for your mouth, the complete arch restoration offers lifelike aesthetics and excellent comfort.
  • Improved Chewing & Speaking. Eat the foods you love and speak with ease, without slipping, shifting, or hesitation.
  • Bone Preservation. Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss commonly associated with missing teeth.
  • Same-Day Results. Many patients can receive extractions, implants, and a new set of teeth all in one appointment.
