Cosmetic Dentistry

From porcelain veneers to subtle bonding, Dr. Salha designs smiles that look entirely natural — balanced, bright, and unmistakably yours. Every case begins with a careful conversation about the result you want.

General Dentistry

Thorough cleanings, gentle exams, and tooth-colored fillings form the foundation of lifelong oral health. We focus on prevention first — catching small issues before they become big ones.

Dental Implants

A permanent, natural-feeling solution for missing teeth — and the entire process happens here. Dr. Salha performs implant surgery in our own office: no referrals, no outside specialists. Placement, healing, and your final restoration, all in one familiar place.

Teeth Whitening

Professional, enamel-safe whitening that lifts years of stains in a single visit. Far beyond what over-the-counter kits can achieve — with results customized to look bright, never artificial.

Crowns Bridges

Custom-crafted restorations that rebuild damaged or missing teeth with strength and seamless beauty. Each crown and bridge is shaded and shaped to blend perfectly with your natural smile.

Root Canal Therapy

Modern root canal treatment is comfortable and remarkably gentle — relieving pain quickly while saving your natural tooth. Most patients are surprised by how easy the experience truly is.

Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies don’t wait, and neither do we. From sudden pain to a broken tooth, we offer same-day appointments at both locations so you can get relief fast — with compassionate, immediate care.

Gingivitis Treatment

Bleeding or tender gums are your mouth’s early warning sign. With gentle, thorough cleanings we treat gingivitis before it advances — protecting your gums, your teeth, and your overall health.

Periodontics

Healthy teeth need healthy foundations. Our periodontal care treats gum disease at every stage — from scaling and root planing to ongoing maintenance — protecting the gums and bone that support your smile, all in our own office.
