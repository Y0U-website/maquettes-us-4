What Does an Oral Surgeon Do?

An oral surgeon is a dental specialist trained in performing surgical procedures involving the teeth, gums, jaw, and surrounding structures.

With advanced training beyond dental school, our oral surgeon is equipped to handle both routine and complex cases with precision, safety, and patient comfort in mind.

Oral Surgery Services We Offer

Our in-house oral surgeon provides a wide range of procedures, including:

Tooth Extractions

From simple extractions to more complex cases, we ensure the procedure is as comfortable and efficient as possible.

Wisdom Teeth Removal

Impacted or problematic wisdom teeth can cause pain, infection, and crowding. Our oral surgeon safely removes them while minimizing discomfort and recovery time.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are a long-term solution for missing teeth. Our oral surgeon works closely with our restorative team to place implants precisely, ensuring strong, natural-looking results.

Bone Grafting

When there is insufficient bone to support implants, bone grafting procedures help rebuild the foundation needed for successful treatment.

Surgical Treatment for Oral Conditions

We diagnose and treat a variety of oral conditions, including infections, cysts, and other concerns affecting the jaw and surrounding tissues.
