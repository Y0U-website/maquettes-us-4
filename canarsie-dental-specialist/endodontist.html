What Does an Endodontist Do?

An endodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on the inner structure of the tooth, including the pulp, nerves, and root canals.

With advanced training beyond dental school, an endodontist is highly skilled in:

Their primary goal is simple: to preserve your natural tooth whenever possible.

  • Diagnosing complex tooth pain
  • Performing root canal treatments
  • Treating infections inside the tooth
  • Saving teeth that might otherwise need extraction

Signs You May Need Endodontic Care

You may benefit from seeing our in-house endodontist if you experience:

Prompt treatment can prevent further complications and help you avoid more extensive procedures.

  • Persistent or severe tooth pain
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold that lingers
  • Pain when biting or chewing
  • Swelling or tenderness in the gums
  • A darkened or discolored tooth
  • A previously treated tooth that still causes discomfort

Endodontic Treatments We Offer

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed pulp tissue, cleans the inside of the tooth, and seals it to prevent reinfection. Contrary to common belief, modern root canal therapy is comfortable and designed to relieve pain, not cause it.

Endodontic Retreatment

If a previously treated tooth has not healed properly, retreatment may be needed. Our endodontist carefully reopens the tooth, removes old material, and treats the area again to restore health.

Apicoectomy (Root-End Surgery)

For persistent infections near the root tip, a minor surgical procedure may be performed to remove the affected area and seal the tooth.

Why Saving Your Natural Tooth Matters?

Whenever possible, preserving your natural tooth is the best option for your oral health.

Our in-house endodontist focuses on treatments that protect and extend the life of your natural teeth.

  • Maintaining natural bite and chewing function
  • Preventing bone loss in the jaw
  • Avoiding more complex and costly replacements
  • Preserving the natural look and feel of your smile
