What Does an Orthodontist Do?

An orthodontist is a dental specialist trained to diagnose and correct misaligned teeth and jaws. With advanced training beyond dental school, they understand how teeth move, how jaw development affects alignment, and how to achieve long-lasting, stable results.

While general dentists may offer basic orthodontic solutions, an orthodontist brings a higher level of expertise, especially for complex alignment and bite issues.

Who Can Benefit from Orthodontic Treatment?

Orthodontic care is not just for children; patients of all ages can benefit from treatment.

You or your child may need an orthodontic evaluation if you notice:

Addressing these issues early can prevent more complex dental problems in the future.

  • Crooked, crowded, or overlapping teeth
  • Gaps or spaces between teeth
  • Overbite, underbite, crossbite, or open bite
  • Difficulty chewing or jaw discomfort
  • Teeth that are hard to clean due to positioning

Orthodontic Treatment Options

We offer a range of modern orthodontic solutions tailored to your needs and lifestyle:

Traditional Braces

Metal braces remain one of the most effective options for correcting complex alignment and bite issues. Today’s braces are smaller, more comfortable, and highly efficient.

Ceramic Braces

Ceramic braces function like traditional braces but use tooth-coloured brackets for a more discreet appearance, making them a popular choice for adults and older teens.

Clear Aligner Therapy (Invisalign)

Clear aligners are removable, nearly invisible trays that gradually shift your teeth into place. They are ideal for patients seeking a convenient, subtle treatment option, especially for mild-to-moderate cases.

Retainers & Post-Treatment Care

After active treatment, retainers are essential to maintain your results. Our orthodontist provides personalized guidance and long-term monitoring to ensure your smile stays aligned.
