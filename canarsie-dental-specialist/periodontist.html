What Does a Periodontist Do?

A periodontist is a dental specialist with advanced training in the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease and conditions affecting the supporting structures of the teeth.

Their expertise ensures that your smile has a strong, healthy foundation for the long term.

  • Gum health and disease management
  • Bone support around teeth
  • Soft tissue procedures
  • Dental implant support and maintenance

Why Gum Health Matters?

Gum disease is one of the most common dental conditions and often one of the most overlooked.

If left untreated, it can progress to more serious conditions, leading to:

Healthy gums are essential not only for your smile but also for your overall health.

  • Red, swollen, or bleeding gums
  • Bad breath
  • Gum sensitivity
  • Gum recession
  • Bone loss
  • Loose or shifting teeth
  • Tooth loss

Conditions We Treat

Our in-house periodontist evaluates and treats a wide range of gum and soft tissue concerns, including:

Early diagnosis and treatment are key to preventing long-term damage.

  • Gingivitis – Early-stage, reversible gum inflammation
  • Periodontitis – Advanced gum disease affecting bone and tissue
  • Gum recession – Exposure of tooth roots due to tissue loss
  • Gum infections and abscesses
  • Bone loss around teeth
  • Implant-related gum issues (peri-implantitis)

Periodontal Treatments We Offer

Scaling and Root Planing (Deep Cleaning)

A non-surgical treatment that removes plaque and bacteria from below the gumline, allowing gums to heal and reattach to the teeth.

Periodontal Maintenance

Ongoing care following gum disease treatment to prevent recurrence and maintain gum health over time.

Gum Grafting

A procedure that restores lost gum tissue, protects exposed roots, and improves both function and appearance.

Osseous Surgery

For advanced cases, this procedure reshapes the bone around teeth to eliminate areas where bacteria can accumulate.
