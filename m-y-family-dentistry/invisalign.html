What is Invisalign and How Does It Work?

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear aligners to gradually move your teeth into their ideal position. Each set of aligners is worn for about one to two weeks before being replaced with the next set in your treatment plan. For the best results, aligners should be worn for 20 to 22 hours a day and removed only when eating, drinking anything other than water, brushing, or flossing.

Your Invisalign journey begins with a consultation and digital scan of your teeth. Using this information, your dentist creates a personalized treatment plan that maps out each stage of your smile transformation. Throughout treatment, you will receive new aligners as your teeth gradually shift into place and attend regular checkups to ensure everything is progressing as planned.

Treatment Duration for Invisalign
