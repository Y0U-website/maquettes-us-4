Dental hygiene

  • Replace toothbrushes every three or four months, or sooner if the bristles become frayed.
  • Use dental floss daily to clean between the teeth and under the gum line.
  • Use mouth rinses with fluoride once a day.
  • Tongue scrapers can help improve your bad breath.
  • Visit Smiles by JB every six months to have your teeth cleaned.
