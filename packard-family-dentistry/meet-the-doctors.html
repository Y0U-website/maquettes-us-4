Meet the Doctors

Proudly Providing Holistic Dentistry to Our Community

Our skilled dentists in Ypsilanti, MI, Dr. Alsamerai, Dr. Rashid, and the entire team at Packard Family Dentistry are pleased to be able to offer beautiful smiles to their neighbors in Ypsilanti and the surrounding communities.

Laith Alsamerai, DDS

Dr. Alsamerai graduated from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry in 2013, after he graduated from the University of Baghdad School of Dentistry in 1999. He has practiced dentistry in Iraq, Jordan, and the United States for over 17 years; he’s been practicing dentistry in the Jackson area for over 8 years. Dr. A, as many of his patients call him, believes in providing the best treatment for his patients. Treating patients from different cultures provides him with a deeper understanding of his patients’ dental concerns and allows him to offer a range of creative solutions.

Dr. A belongs to a family of dentists and doctors. His siblings practice in Canada, Australia, and the United States. He moved to the US in 2008 with his wife Teba, who’s also a dentist, seeking personal freedom and safety with hope for a brighter future for them and their kids. They have lived in Michigan since 2011. He is a member of American Dental Association, Michigan Dental Association, and Washtenaw District Dental Society.

He and his wife have two sons (Hasan and Mustafa). In his free time, he enjoys traveling and playing sports with his boys.

Teba Rashid, DDS

When not wearing her white coat, she can be found cooking or traveling with her husband and her two boys.

We look forward to providing you with excellent service!
