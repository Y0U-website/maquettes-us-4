Kathleen Torres, DDS

Kathleen Torres, DDS, is a skilled and compassionate family dentist practicing at Reality Bites Dental in San Antonio, Texas. She provides general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, and orthodontic services to patients of just about every age.

Dr. Torres is originally from Alice, Texas. She received her degree in microbiology from the University of Texas in Austin and then completed her dental training at the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio.

More Dr. Torres is an active member of the Academy of Orthodontics and a certified Six Month Smiles provider. She is a lifelong learner, dedicated to staying on top of advancements in dental health in an effort to provide the quality care that her patients deserve.

Outside of the office, she has three beautiful children that she loves to spend time with.

To schedule an appointment with Dr. Torres, call Reality Bites Dental or request an appointment online today. She looks forward to meeting you and your family.

Kateryna Nechay, DDS, FAGD

Originally from Kyiv, Ukraine, Dr. Nechay moved to Minnesota with her family at the age of 10. She earned her bachelor’s degree in Physiology and her dental degree from the University of Minnesota before relocating to San Antonio in 2020.

She is proud to be a Fellow of the Academy of General Dentistry (FAGD), a distinction earned by only about 5 % of general dentists in the United States. To achieve this honor, she completed more than 500 hours of advanced continuing education, demonstrated expertise across all areas of general dentistry, and passed a rigorous comprehensive examination.

This accomplishment reflects Dr. Nechay’s dedication to lifelong learning and her commitment to providing the highest standard of modern, evidence-based dental care.
