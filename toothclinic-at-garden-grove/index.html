During your first dental appointment, the dentist will typically perform a comprehensive examination of your teeth, gums, and oral tissues. This may involve X-rays, checking for cavities, assessing your bite, and screening for any signs of oral diseases. The dentist will discuss your dental history and address any concerns or questions you may have.
