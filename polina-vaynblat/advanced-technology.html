CBCT 3D Imaging

Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) provides comprehensive 3D imaging for a complete view of your mouth and jaw. This technology is especially valuable for planning dental implants, orthodontics, or oral surgery. It helps ensure treatments are precisely tailored to your anatomy and long-term comfort.

  • Comprehensive visualization: Displays bone structure, nerve pathways, and sinus cavities in incredible detail.
  • Improved treatment accuracy: Allows precise implant placement and safer surgical planning.
  • Minimal radiation exposure: Uses a narrow, focused beam that’s safe and effective for diagnostic use.
  • Personalized care: Gives your dentist a complete picture to design treatment that fits your unique smile.

Diagnodent® Early Cavity Detection

The Diagnodent system uses laser fluorescence technology to detect early signs of decay—often before they’re visible to the eye or show up on x-rays. This gentle, non-invasive device helps preserve natural tooth structure by catching decay when it’s easiest to treat. It’s an ideal tool for proactive, preventive dental care.

  • Laser-based detection: Uses safe laser light to find hidden decay inside grooves or between teeth.
  • Painless procedure: Simply scans the tooth surface without scraping or drilling.
  • Early intervention: Prevents minor spots of decay from becoming larger restorations later.
  • Better preservation: Helps you maintain more of your healthy, natural enamel over time.

Velscope Oral Cancer Screening

Early detection can make all the difference in oral health, and the Velscope system enhances that process with advanced fluorescent lighting. This quick, non-invasive scan allows your dentist to detect abnormal tissue changes that might otherwise go unnoticed. The entire process takes just a few minutes and could be life-saving.

  • Enhanced visibility: Blue light highlights subtle tissue irregularities invisible to the naked eye.
  • Fast and painless exam: Performed during your routine check-up without any special preparation.
  • Early detection benefits: Allows prompt referral for further evaluation if irregularities appear.
  • Routine integration: Adds a valuable layer of screening for patients at every stage of life.

Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays are a safer, faster, and more detailed way to see what’s happening beneath the surface of your smile. Unlike traditional film x-rays, they reduce radiation exposure while producing clearer, high-resolution images. These images allow our dental care providers to detect issues early and plan treatments with precision.

  • Lower radiation exposure: Up to 90 % less radiation than traditional x-rays for peace of mind during every visit.
  • Instant image viewing: See your x-rays on-screen within seconds as your dentist explains what they reveal.
  • Enhanced image quality: Identifies small cavities or bone changes before they cause noticeable discomfort.
  • Eco-friendly process: Reduces environmental waste by eliminating film and chemical processing.
