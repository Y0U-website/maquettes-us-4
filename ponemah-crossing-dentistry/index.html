The practice

Our team at Ponemah Crossing Dentistry, believes in the power of a healthy, beautiful smile. We offer a range of services designed to address every aspect of dental health, from routine checkups and cleanings to advanced procedures like root canal therapy, TMJ treatments, extractions, and more. Our approach is always gentle and focused on ensuring your comfort and satisfaction.

Proudly serving as your local Amherst dentist, we are deeply rooted in the community of Hillsborough County and the wider Souhegan Valley area. Our practice continues to grow and evolve, driven by our unwavering commitment to doing the best for our patients.
