How does Invisalign® work?

Invisalign is a unique orthodontic system that straightens your teeth with clear plastic aligner trays instead of the traditional wires and brackets (as seen in normal braces). These aligners would be custom-made for your smile, based on a treatment plan created by an orthodontist.

Over the course of treatment, you would be provided with a number of clear trays, each tray making small changes to your tooth and jaw alignment. Over time, the aligner trays are designed to move your teeth into the desired position. One of the biggest benefit to clear aligners is how discrete they are in day-to-day life!
