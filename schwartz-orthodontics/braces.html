Metal Braces

What are Metal Braces?

When we think about orthodontic treatment, the majority of us have metal braces in mind since they are the most common form of them. They have undergone significant development over the years, and as a result, they are today much more comfortable, lighter, and smaller than in the past. In addition, you have the option of customizing your unique brackets and with colored bands.

These are a great choice for children, teens, and adults aiming for a beautiful, healthy smile in the shortest time frame. During your treatment, you’ll visit us every 6 to 8 weeks for a check-in and we will make any necessary adjustments to your bands or wires.

Clear Ceramic Braces

What are Ceramic Braces?

Many people worry about wearing braces because they dislike the idea of having a flashy silver smile. If you are among those who don’t like the look of the traditional metal version, clear braces could be the right solution for you.

Made from transparent or tooth-colored materials, ceramic braces blend naturally with the shade of your teeth, making them both discreet and inconspicuous. This makes them an excellent option for those who prefer a more subtle approach to correcting misalignments.

Ceramic clear braces work similarly to the traditional metal version. The main difference is ceramic braces have clear brackets! This feature helps the brackets blend in with the natural color of the teeth for a less noticeable appearance.
