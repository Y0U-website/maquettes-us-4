Introducing your Vancouver dentist

Dr. Sean McCombs

General Dentist

Dr. Sean McCombs, DDS, is dedicated to providing exceptional dental experiences through safe, comfortable, and high-quality care. His primary goal is to help patients maintain lifelong oral health while feeling confident in a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dr. McCombs earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry and brings years of experience delivering compassionate, patient-centered care. He is known for his thoughtful approach, attention to detail, and commitment to helping patients feel at ease.

Outside the office, Dr. McCombs and his wife, Marissa, have been married for 10 years and are proud parents of three children—Ivy, Jackson, and Gwen. An avid outdoor enthusiast, he enjoys hiking, camping, skiing, rock climbing, backpacking, and surfing. Dr. McCombs is passionate about making a positive impact in his community, both professionally and personally.

Dr. Chris Epperson

DMD

Dr. Chris Epperson earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from A.T. Still University’s Arizona School of Dentistry & Oral Health in Mesa, Arizona. He first discovered his passion for dentistry while participating in humanitarian trips to Mexico and the Dominican Republic.

With an extensive background in implant dentistry, including full-mouth implant solutions, Dr. Epperson is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists and a member of the American Academy of Implant Dentistry. He has also served as a faculty member at Implant Pathway, a Phoenix-based continuing education facility dedicated to providing high-quality implant training to dental professionals across the U.S. Dr. Epperson is committed to lifelong learning to ensure he delivers the highest level of care to his patients.

Dr. Epperson and his wife, who have been together for more than 20 years, are proud parents of three children. Originally from Arizona, their family has embraced life in the Pacific Northwest. Outside of dentistry, he enjoys outdoor adventures, perfecting Texas-style BBQ, and tinkering with his old truck and motorcycle.
