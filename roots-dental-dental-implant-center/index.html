At Roots Dental, our mission is to make your dental experience truly exceptional. Our friendly and knowledgeable Vancouver team is excited to serve our local community with compassionate, high-quality dentistry.

When you visit our office, you’ll experience all the fantastic things modern dentistry has to offer. We’ve invested in the latest technology to better care for our patients. Whether you’re after a check-up and cleaning, dental implants, cosmetic dentistry to improve your smile, or need a complete dental reconstruction, we’re ready to take care of you

Dr. Sean McCombs, DDS, is dedicated to providing exceptional dental experiences through safe, comfortable, and high-quality care. His primary goal is to help patients maintain lifelong oral health while feeling confident in a healthy, beautiful smile.

Dr. McCombs earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry and brings years of experience delivering compassionate, patient-centered care. He is known for his thoughtful approach, attention to detail, and commitment to helping patients feel at ease.

Outside the office, Dr. McCombs and his wife, Marissa, have been married for 10 years and are proud parents of three children—Ivy, Jackson, and Gwen. An avid outdoor enthusiast, he enjoys hiking, camping, skiing, rock climbing, backpacking, and surfing. Dr. McCombs is passionate about making a positive impact in his community, both professionally and personally.
