At Indigo Dentistry, we believe everyone deserves a confident, healthy smile they can enjoy for life.

We offer multiple services that are especially beneficial to seniors, including traditional dental cleanings as well as comprehensive periodontal care. We also provide options to repair teeth affected by decay and wear, and offer several reliable solutions for replacing missing teeth. Some of those options include, crown and bridge, partial and complete dentures. Check out more about this under our services.

We are in-network with most insurance carriers, including Medicare supplement plans, such as Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, and Humana.
