Dental implants have an extremely high success rate – over 95 % but like natural teeth, they can need repairs and maintenance. Regular exams and prompt repairs can reduce the risk of dental implant failure. If you have signs of damage to your dental implant, contact our team at Radiant Smiles. Call our office in Arlington, VA, to schedule a dental implant checkup or repair.

  • Implant-Supported Bridges: Bridges can replace multiple missing teeth by anchoring to two or more dental implants. They restore both function and aesthetics. If any of the crowns, pontics, abutments or implant posts are damaged, we can repair or restore the bridge.
  • Loose Implants: Implants that become loose may need procedures to increase bone growth to secure the implant. Our dental team provides routine checkups to examine implants to check for any problems.
