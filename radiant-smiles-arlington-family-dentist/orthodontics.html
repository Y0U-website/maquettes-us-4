Benefits of Orthodontic Treatment

When teeth are not aligned properly, it can impact more than just smile aesthetics. While many people seek orthodontic treatment to improve the appearance of crooked teeth, it can have significant impacts on your oral and overall health. Straight teeth are easier to clean and keep free from decay and accumulation of plaque and tartar. This can reduce the risk of gum disease and associated health concerns. Plus, an aligned bite can reduce the chance of bruxism (teeth grinding) and TMJ disorders, which can affect your teeth and mouth function.
