Arlington VA Dental Implants

Single dental implants offer several advantages. The prosthetic crown closely mimics the appearance and function of a natural tooth, blending seamlessly with your smile. Unlike traditional bridges, which require adjacent teeth to be altered, single implants do not affect neighboring teeth for a more conservative approach to tooth replacement. Another advantage is that implants provide a stable and long-lasting solution, allowing you to enjoy the benefits of a fully functional tooth.

  • Abutment: The abutment serves as the connector between the implant and the prosthetic crown, securely holding the crown in place.
  • Crown: The crown is the custom-made, artificial tooth that is attached to the abutment. It is designed to resemble the surrounding natural teeth in both appearance and function.
