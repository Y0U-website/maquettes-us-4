Services

  • 0 Preventative Dental Services Teeth Cleanings Southern Oaks Family Dentistry welcomes both adults and children for their ADA recommended semi annual professional teeth cleaning or prophylaxis.
  • Exams & Consultations Dr. David Phelps performs thorough exams to regularly screen for oral cancer, check periodontal/ gum health, and catch tooth decay/ cavities among various other oral conditions.
  • Digital X-Rays Southern Oaks Family Dentistry uses digital radiography to diagnose a multitude of problems and conditions. Early detection is key in avoiding costly and painful dental problems.
  • Sealants Sealants are often recommended to protect the occlusal/ chewing surface of teeth from cavities.
  • Fluoride The application of fluoride is an excellent/ proven preventative measure to strengthen enamel and protect teeth from cavities.

Restorative & Surgical Dental Services

Fillings Teeth that have been damaged with decay, cracks, or other problems often times need to have the damaged part removed. Frequently the damaged part can be replaced by filling the void with one of many restorative materials, most commonly a tooth colored, composite filling.

  • Crowns & Onlays When a tooth needs more reinforcement than a filling can offer, a crown or onlay may be recommended. These cover the chewing surfaces of the teeth for strength, and can be made from different materials, depending on the requirements of the situation.
  • Root Canal Treatment Root Canal Treatment involves removing the nerve from a painful tooth before cleaning and sealing the pulp chamber to provide relief.
  • Tooth Extraction Sometimes a tooth is non-restorable and needs to be removed. Dr. Phelps ensures patient comfort with anesthetic prior to removing the tooth.

Tooth Replacement/ Prosthetic Dental Services

Bridges A bridge is similar to a crown in that it is fixed or “cemented” in place. It is usually supported by two teeth on either side of a missing tooth.

  • Implants Implant supported prosthetics are a great way to replace one or more teeth. The implant body is surgically placed in the site where the natural tooth was, and then after a period of healing, we can attach crowns, partials or even dentures securely.
  • Partials & Dentures Different types of removable prosthetics, commonly called partial dentures and full dentures can be made to replace one or more teeth depending on the patient’s needs.

Periodontal Dental Services

Non-Surgical Periodontal Treatment Scaling and root planing, often referred to as a deep cleaning, removes subgingival (below the gum line) plaque and tartar that if not treated can call irreversible boneless.

  • Periodontal Maintenance For patients requiring periodontal treatment a maintenance program may be recommended to ensure lasting results

Cosmetic Dental Services

Home Whitening Dr. David Phelps and his team offer many teeth whitening options. A home whitening kit includes custom fabricated clear trays to be worn with a whitening solution at home on your schedule.

  • Veneers Veneers are typically made of porcelain, and can be placed to achieve a desired aesthetic by closing spaces, changing shape, size, and shade of one’s teeth.
  • Bonding Bonding uses a composite tooth colored filling material to improve shade, shape, or even close spaces.
