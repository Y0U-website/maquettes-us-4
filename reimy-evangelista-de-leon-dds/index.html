The practice

The American Dental Association (ADA) and American Dental Education Association (ADEA) have defined Orofacial Pain (OFP) as One of the most recent developments in dental education.

OFP associated disorders include, but are not limited to, temporomandibular muscle and joint (TMJ) disorders, jaw movement disorders, neuropathic and neurovascular pain disorders, headache and sleep disorders.

The doctors and supporting staff at Vermont Dental Medicine are committed to improving the quality of life for patients who suffer with Temporomandibular Joint Disorders (TMD), Orofacial Pain (OFP) and Dental Sleep Medicine.

We believe that an evidence-based diagnosis of the root causes of TMD, OFP and Sleep disorders is central to providing a successful treatment plan to suit the needs of each individual.
