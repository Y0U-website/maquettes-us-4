Meet Dr. Draper

Dr. Eric Draper, DDS, MS

Diplomate American Board of Periodontology

Dr. Eric Draper is an enthusiastic and caring periodontist. He enjoys the opportunity to develop a trusting relationship with his patients. He grew up in Louisville, KY and earned his Bachelor of Science in Biology from Brigham Young University. He then attended the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill to earn his Doctor of Dental Surgery, where he graduated top of his class.

Dr. Draper commissioned as an officer in the United States Navy. He attended the Naval Postgraduate Dental School to earn his Periodontal Certificate and Masters of Science in Oral Biology. He has since become board certified by the American Board of Periodontology. He is grateful the Navy brought him to Whidbey Island, where he and his family immediately knew they wanted to make their permanent home.

In his free time, Dr. Draper loves enjoying the sites and nature of Whidbey Island with his wife Natasha, and three children Ethan, Ivy, and Sam.
