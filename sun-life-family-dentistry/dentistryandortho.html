WHY YOU SHOULD USE SUN LIFE HEALTH DENTISTRY AND ORTHODONTICS

Open Communication

Our team of dentistry experts believe in open communication, so you know all your options, and then together can best choose the right treatment plan for you.

Modern Equipment

By using state of the art, modern equipment, we are able to provide many services, including invisalign.

Fear Friendly

We understand for many people there can be any number of reasons going to the dentist is a scary place. Our compassion and friendly team are here to make you feel safe and comfortable during your visit.

Comfort Care

We offer services to help make sure your dental and orthodontics experience is one that puts our patients at ease. With in-room TVs and nitrous options available to help provide comfort during your treatment.

Orthodontics

Orthodontics is a dental specialty focused on aligning your bite and straightening your teeth. You might need to see an orthodontist if you have crooked, overlapped, twisted or gapped teeth.

  • Preventative Dentistry Annual checkups, comprehensive exams, x-rays, fluoride & sealants and gum disease care.
