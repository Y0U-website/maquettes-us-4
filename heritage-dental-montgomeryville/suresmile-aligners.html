SureSmile Aligners FAQs

SureSmile aligners in Montgomeryville, Pennsylvania, are an aesthetically pleasing alternative to metal braces. This revolutionary orthodontic system uses bespoke plastic aligners to align your teeth and bite without metal brackets and wires.

How does SureSmile work?

This orthodontic system uses customized clear aligners to apply controlled pressure on your teeth. Gradually, the clear aligners move your teeth into perfect alignment, usually after 9-18 months. SureSmile aligners are created from advanced 3D technology with an incredible ability to predict your treatment from start to finish. Your treatment involves several aligners (probably 18-30) worn in progression until you achieve your orthodontic goals. Also, you should wear your aligners 20-22 hours daily, only removing them during meals or when flossing and brushing your teeth.

Is the SureSmile treatment painful?

Since SureSmile aligners move your teeth from one position to another, you can expect some discomfort at the start of each new stage of treatment. Don’t worry! This is entirely expected and is a sure sign that your aligners are working. This discomfort—mild jaw pain and tenderness—disappears a few days after inserting your new aligner. The beauty of SureSmile aligners is that they are made from personalized, high-quality plastic material. Unlike metal braces, clear aligners fit snugly over your teeth and don’t have wires or rough edges that can irritate your soft tissues.

What are the benefits of SureSmile aligners?

Unlike traditional braces that use conspicuous metal wires and brackets, SureSmile aligners are clear and virtually invisible, quietly aligning your smile without announcing it to the world. The other benefits of SureSmile aligners include:

  • Fast treatment
  • Fewer dental appointments
  • Removable convenience
  • No dietary restrictions
  • Metal-free orthodontic system
  • Undisrupted oral hygiene
  • Comfortable treatment

Can I eat anything I want while undergoing treatment?

Yes! Unlike traditional braces, you’ll eat and drink what you want because your aligners are removable. This way, you can eat chewy and sticky foods like popcorn, steak, apples, etc., that are off-the-limit for conventional braces. However, it’s essential to brush your teeth before wearing your aligners. Good oral hygiene prevents food debris and harmful bacteria from compromising your aligners.

Will my SureSmile aligners affect my speech?

Like all orthodontic treatments, your SureSmile aligners will temporarily affect your speech as your tongue adjusts to having an appliance in the mouth. However, your natural speech will be restored after a few days, and any lisp caused by your aligners will disappear.

What’s the best way to clean my SureSmile aligners?

You must maintain your aligners to protect them from stains and harmful bacteria. Good news! Caring for your aligners isn’t cumbersome. Brush and rinse your aligners in lukewarm water. Never use hot water, as it can warp your aligners. Lastly, store your aligners in a dry container when not in use.

What happens after I finish my SureSmile treatment?

After you complete your treatment with SureSmile, the journey doesn’t end there. We’ll give you a retainer to maintain your teeth in their newly aligned positions.
