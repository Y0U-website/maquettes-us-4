Am I a Candidate for Implant Dentures?

Generally speaking, a candidate for implant dentures should:

  • Have fair overall health
  • Be free of gum disease and other serious oral health conditions
  • Have adequate bone density in the jaw to support dental implants
  • Stick to good habits that will help their implant dentures to stand the test of time

Benefits of Combining Dentures & Dental Implants

Implant dentures are one of the best ways to replace missing teeth because:

  • Dental implants preserve the jawbone , helping it to stay strong and whole.
  • Implant dentures can handle virtually any food , including ones that pose a challenge to people who wear traditional prosthetics.
  • Implant dentures can last for much longer than their traditional counterparts (which typically need to be replaced every 5 7 years).
  • We carefully design implant dentures so they feel and look as natural as possible .
  • Implant dentures are much more comfortable than traditional ones and take less time to adapt to.
