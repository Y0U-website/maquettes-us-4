Why Do Dental Implants Fail?

Although they can provide superior stability for your smile, certain things can result in dental implant failure, including:

  • Peri-implantitis : This infection in the gums surrounding the dental implants is the most common reason for potential failure. It typically occurs due to poor oral hygiene and can eventually weaken the jawbone and ruin the connective tissues holding the implants in position.
  • Failed osseointegration : After your implant is inserted in the jawbone, it should fuse with it via osseointegration. However, if this process is hampered by bad oral habits, poor oral hygiene, or an infection, then the bond between the implant and bone tissue may not form.
  • Accidental trauma : If you experience a physical injury to the jawbone that injures the bone tissue or damages the implant, the metal post can end up failing.
  • Certain medical conditions : Dental implants can fail due to other medical issues that affect your oral health, such as osteoporosis and diabetes.

Symptoms of Failed Dental Implants

Here are some of the most common indications that your dental implant may be failing:

  • A loose or wobbly sensation in your dental implant
  • Discomfort or difficulty when chewing
  • Intense, persistent, or worsening pain surrounding your implant
  • Swelling, bleeding, or discharge around the implant site
