Often referred to as laughing gas, is used by dentists to help your child relax during treatment. It is a safe sedative that is combined with oxygen and inhaled through a small mask during treatment. Once treatment is complete and the mask is removed, the effects of nitrous oxide fade quickly as fresh air is breathed into the lungs.

Your child’s experience is designed to be as positive as possible, and sometimes nitrous oxide isn’t enough to keep your child comfortable. Our providers are qualified to provide oral sedation medications that can keep your children relaxed, comfortable. For more anxious patients, oral sedation can be a helpful and calming technique help get the job done!

For some children, dental treatment under general anesthesia is necessary to provide the best possible experience while still completing all necessary treatment. Anesthesia is recommended for several reasons including: previous traumatic experiences, extensive needs, special healthcare needs, or generally high anxiety or fear of the dentist.
