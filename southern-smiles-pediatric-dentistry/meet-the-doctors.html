Community

Southern Smiles has been serving the families of Cameron, Fayetteville, and Ft. Liberty for over a decade. Our goal is to make our office a place where you can feel at home for your oral health.

Transparency

We emphasize patient and caregiver education and engagement to assure that you can make the most informed and educated decision regarding your child’s oral health.

Patient-Centered

Children..and teeth…come in all shapes, sizes, and dispositions. We’ll fit our approach per your child to keep them at ease and to provide the best experience.

Zev Orentlikher, DDS Dr. Zev Orentlikher is a board-certified pediatric dentist who is proficient in providing comprehensive therapeutic oral health care for infants and children through adolescence, including those with special healthcare needs.

Post-Education Following graduation, Dr. Zev completed a residency in Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at the Eastman Institute of Oral Health-Strong Memorial Hospital University of Rochester, NY. Dr. Zev was granted a position in a pediatric dental specialty hospital program in New York City at Maimonides Medical Center, the sole Level 1 trauma facility of pediatric care in Brooklyn.

Fun Facts After many harsh winters, he decided to relocate to a sunnier climate, settling in Pinehurst, North Carolina with his family. He describes himself as a “typical dad” who enjoys the company of his wife and two children. Parents and children know him as a go-to compassionate pediatric dentist who treats anyone in his chair like family.
