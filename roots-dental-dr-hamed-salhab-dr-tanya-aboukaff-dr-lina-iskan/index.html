The practice

Sedation dentistry helps patients feel relaxed and comfortable during dental treatment. This option is helpful for adult and pediatric patients with dental anxiety, sensitive teeth, or procedures that require longer appointments. At Roots Dental, sedation options allow patients to complete important dental care while feeling calm and supported.

Restorative dentistry repairs teeth that are damaged, decayed, or missing. Treatments may include fillings, crowns, bridges, and other tooth restorations that restore strength and normal chewing function. At Roots Dental, restorative care is designed to preserve natural teeth whenever possible while rebuilding healthy, functional smiles.

Oral surgery treats dental conditions that require surgical care. Procedures may include tooth extractions, wisdom teeth removal, and surgical preparation for dental implants . At Roots Dental, oral surgery is carefully coordinated with restorative treatment to support long-term oral health.

Roots Dental provides family dentistry delivered by a family of dentists working together. Patients receive coordinated care for children, adults, and older adults in one trusted practice. Our team combines pediatric dentistry, restorative care, sedation dentistry, and dental implants so treatment can evolve as your needs change. Hamed Salhab, DMD provides dental implant and oral surgery care focused on restoring healthy, functional smiles. Implant treatment helps patients replace missing or damaged teeth with long-term solutions that restore comfort and confidence. Dr. Salhab emphasizes thoughtful treatment planning and clear patient education so patients understand their options and feel confident in their care.

Tanya Aboukaff, DMD provides board-certified pediatric dentistry for infants, children, and teens. Pediatric dental care helps children prevent cavities, protect developing teeth, and build healthy oral habits early in life. Dr. Aboukaff emphasizes gentle care, clear communication with parents, and positive dental experiences that help children feel comfortable at every visit.
