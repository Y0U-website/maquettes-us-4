The practice

We recognize the mouth as part of the larger whole, the human body, and our approach to dental care is a holistic one. Everything we do in the mouth can have an effect elsewhere in the body, and as a biological dentist, Dr. Ulm takes your overall health into consideration when recommending treatment.

The mouth is the gateway to the body, and any materials used in dental treatment should not only be safe and non-toxic, but biocompatible. From anesthetic to implants, we take every precaution in researching our materials and ensuring they are safe for you and your family

Your natural enamel is the very best protection for your teeth, which is why Dr. Ulm makes every effort to maintain as much natural structure intact as possible, from recommending remineralization whenever possible, conservative preparation of simple fillings, to using a biomimetic alternative to crowns.
