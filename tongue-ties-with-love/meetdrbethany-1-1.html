About TTWL

At TTWL, Dr. Bethany Kum takes a conservative, holistic, and collaborative care approach to frenectomies (aka lip and tongue tie releases) providing traditional and alternative methods to baby’s functional oral health. You aren’t pushed into unnecessary procedures and you’ll never be rushed to make a decision.

Dr. Bethany is here to listen to your concerns and goals so together, a game plan will be created with your family and team of providers that’s personalized to you and your baby’s needs.

Being a Mom herself and having gone through her own tongue tie journey with her daughter, she knows how vital it is to have support, empowerment, love, and care on your feeding journey with your baby.

TTWL provides customized 1:1 care to baby & parents to make tongue tie release as calm, nurturing, and loving as possible.

Benefits of a frenectomy (tongue tie release)

After tongue tie release, babies have more range of motion of their tongue that will give the opportunity to develop a positive functional foundation for feeding. Over the course of this leads to functional health benefits like:

  • Breathing
  • Airway development
  • Nervous system
  • Posture
  • Alignment
  • Sleep
  • Sensory
  • Developmental milestones
  • Solid food feeding
  • Speech development
  • Oral growth and development
  • And so much more!
