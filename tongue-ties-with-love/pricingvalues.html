CAN A FRENECTOMY PROCEDURE BE DONE ON THE SAME DAY AS THE CONSULTATION? If Dr. Bethany determines that a restriction is present and there are no other factors that need to be addressed beforehand to ensure the best surgical and functional outcome, the procedure can be performed on the same day as the consultation. However, if families have many questions or need additional support before proceeding, we may recommend scheduling the procedure for a later date.
