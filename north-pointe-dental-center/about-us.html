Your Trusted Dental Home in North Fort Myers

North Pointe Dental Center proudly serves North Fort Myers, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, and nearby communities with preventive, restorative, periodontal, and cosmetic dental care in one welcoming office.

Dr. David J. Pfent, DMD leads the practice with more than 30 years of experience, a patient-first approach to treatment planning, and a commitment to clear communication at every visit. As an ADA member, he focuses on helping patients feel informed, comfortable, and confident in their care.
