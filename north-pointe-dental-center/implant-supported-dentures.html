  • Implant Placement — We work with a surgeon that will evaluate and place the necessary titanium implants into your jawbone. The number of implants depends on the type of denture and whether it’s for your upper or lower jaw. You’ll receive a temporary denture to wear while your implants heal.
  • Healing & Osseointegration — Over the next three to six months, your implants fuse with your jawbone through a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong, permanent foundation for your dentures.
  • Denture Fabrication & Attachment — Once your implants have fully integrated, we take impressions and work with our dental lab to create your custom denture. At your final appointment, we attach your new implant supported denture and make any necessary adjustments for a comfortable, secure fit.

Benefits of Implant Supported Dentures

Superior Stability

Dental implants anchor your dentures firmly in place. No more worrying about slipping, clicking, or embarrassing moments while eating or speaking.

Eat What You Love

Traditional denture wearers often avoid certain foods. Implant supported dentures restore nearly full chewing power, letting you enjoy steak, apples, corn on the cob, and other foods you may have been missing.

Preserve Your Jawbone

When teeth are missing, the jawbone begins to deteriorate. Dental implants stimulate the bone just like natural tooth roots, preventing bone loss and maintaining your facial structure.

Improved Comfort

Because implant supported dentures don’t press directly on your gums, they eliminate the sore spots and irritation often caused by traditional dentures.

Enhanced Confidence

Speak, laugh, and smile without worrying about your dentures shifting. Implant supported dentures stay securely in place so you can focus on enjoying life.

Long-Lasting Investment

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The denture attachment may need replacement every 10 to 15 years, but the implant foundation remains stable for decades.

No Messy Adhesives

Say goodbye to denture creams and adhesives. Your implant supported dentures stay in place on their own.

Most patients who are healthy enough for routine dental procedures are candidates for implant supported dentures. You’ll need adequate jawbone density to support the implants, though bone grafting can often help patients who have experienced bone loss. Conditions like uncontrolled diabetes or heavy smoking may affect healing and implant success. Our team will evaluate your health history and oral condition to determine if implant supported dentures are right for you.

The complete process typically takes four to eight months from implant placement to final denture attachment. This timeline allows for proper healing and osseointegration. Some patients may qualify for immediate load implants, which allow a temporary denture to be attached the same day as surgery. We’ll discuss your timeline during your consultation.

Most patients report less discomfort than expected. A local anesthesia is used during the procedure, and sedation options are available for anxious patients. After surgery, you may experience some swelling and mild discomfort for a few days, which can be managed with prescribed or over-the-counter pain medication. Most patients return to normal activities within a day or two.

Care depends on whether you have removable or fixed implant dentures. Removable snap-on dentures should be taken out daily for cleaning. Brush the denture and clean around the implant attachments. Fixed implant dentures are cleaned like natural teeth—brush twice daily and use a water flosser or interdental brushes to clean around the implants. Regular checkups at North Pointe Dental Center are essential for maintaining your implants and dentures.

The cost varies based on the number of implants needed, the type of denture, and any additional procedures like bone grafting. While implant supported dentures have a higher upfront cost than traditional dentures, they’re a long-term investment that can save money over time by eliminating the need for adhesives, frequent relines, and replacements. Our team will provide a detailed estimate and discuss financing options during your consultation.

For removable snap-on dentures, we typically recommend removing them at night to give your gums and implants a rest. Fixed implant dentures stay in place 24/7, just like natural teeth. Our dentists will provide specific care instructions based on the type of denture you receive.

One of the biggest advantages of implant supported dentures is the ability to eat almost anything. Once fully healed, you can enjoy foods that traditional denture wearers often avoid, including steak, raw vegetables, nuts, and crunchy fruits. We recommend starting with softer foods immediately after implant placement and gradually returning to your regular diet as you heal.
