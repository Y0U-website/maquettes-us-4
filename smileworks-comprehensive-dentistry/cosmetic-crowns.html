The Problems a Quality Crown Can Solve

Porcelain crowns are often recommended when a tooth is damaged, weakened, or cosmetically compromised and needs support beyond what a filling or bonding treatment can provide. If you’re struggling with sensitivity, sharp pain when chewing, visible fractures, or an older crown that feels unstable or unattractive, a new cosmetic crown can protect your tooth and restore full function so you can eat and smile without hesitation.
