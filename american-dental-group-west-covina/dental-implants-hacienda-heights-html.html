Hacienda Heights patient FAQ

How far is your office from Hacienda Heights?

About 6.3 miles, about 15 minutes without traffic. Head north on Hacienda Boulevard and continue to Amar Road, then west to Sunset Avenue. By freeway, take the 60 west to the 57 north to the 10 west and exit at Sunset Avenue. There is free parking on site, which matters for implant appointments because you will be here longer than for a checkup.

Do I have to come back to Hacienda Heights’s area for every step?

No, and that is the point of having a periodontist place the implant. The consultation, 3D scan, any grafting, the surgery and the final crown all happen in this one office, so there is no second practice to coordinate with and no repeated travel between two providers.

How many visits will I need?

For a straightforward single implant, expect a consultation with a 3D scan, the placement appointment, one or two short check-ins during healing, and two appointments for the crown. Grafting adds one visit and several months of healing. We schedule the longer appointments to suit the drive.
