Meet Our Team

Dr. Michael Davis

Dr. Davis originally hails from Utah, where he completed his undergraduate degree at Brigham Young University before making the cross-country move to attend LECOM School of Dental Medicine in Bradenton, Florida. During his training, he built a strong foundation in both restorative and preventive dentistry, always keeping patient comfort and education at the heart of his approach.

After graduation, Dr. Davis and his family had grown to love the Sunshine State so much that they decided to make Clermont their permanent home. Today, he is proud to serve the people of Clermont and the surrounding communities.

At Smile 4 Me Dental, Dr. Davis is committed to providing high-quality, compassionate care in a welcoming environment. He values long-term relationships with his patients and believes dentistry goes beyond fixing teeth — it’s about restoring confidence, improving overall well-being, and helping people feel good about their smiles.

Outside the office, Dr. Davis enjoys life with his wife and their three sons. He loves traveling, going to the beach, golfing, and playing basketball.

Dr. Seth Calish

Dr. Calish holds a B.S. in Biological Sciences from Florida State University and a Master’s in Medical Sciences from the University of South Florida. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from Lake Erie College of Medicine, where he received the prestigious Surgeon of the Year Award. He is board-certified in Florida and actively teaches and lectures in the dental field, contributing to curriculum design and mentoring future dentists.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Calish enjoys life with his wife Lori and their Shih-Tzu Daisy, and he pursues hobbies like bodybuilding, surfing, fishing, and home improvement.

His professional affiliations include leadership roles and fellowships in top dental organizations, including President of the Academy of General Dentistry Central Florida.

Dr. Adam Miller

Dr. Miller’s passion for dentistry is reinforced every time a patient smiles and thanks him for helping them. He believes taht through the use of modern techniques and materials, dentistry can provide comprehensive care and life changing results.

Outside of Dentistry, Dr. Miller enjoys spending time with his wife, son, and family. His hobbies include golfing, fishing, and cooking.
