Meet Your Treatment Coordinator

Nancy is one of the warm, smiling faces you see when you walk in to our office. She is the key point of contact for you as a patient. She has over 20 years of experience in dental and orthodontic care, and holds a degree in Pharmacy from the University of Rhode Island.
