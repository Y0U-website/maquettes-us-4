Our mission is to provide the best dental healthcare available, preserving the health and longevity of your teeth.

We believe that our group practice is unparalleled in the Washington Metropolitan area with guarantees on a wide range of the treatments we offer. With a commitment to excellence, our goal is to provide superior services for all our patients, through careful, individualized attention to their dental needs.

We continually strive to broaden our knowledge and training through continuing education courses to ensure that we keep abreast of the latest materials and most modern techniques available to create a brighter and healthier smile for our patients.

As you entrust your oral healthcare to us, we, in turn, will strive to provide you the best care possible, using the most up-to-date techniques and materials available. Each and every one of us would like to make sure that your visits here are pleasant and comfortable, and that you are completely satisfied.

Watergate Dental Associates (WDA) is an unique group dental practice that has been serving the needs of the Washington, D.C. community for over 40 years. WDA is comprised of a team of highly trained and experienced general practitioners, advanced specialists, outstanding hygienists and support staff, offering a comprehensive range of dental services.

In addition to general dentistry, our practice offers the highest quality in cosmetic dentistry. Whether it’s veneers, crowns, bridges or front tooth implants, our wide range of services can transform your smile in life-changing ways.
