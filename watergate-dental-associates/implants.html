Watergate Dental offers implant-supported prosthetics

Tired of your removable dentures? Get back to enjoying life with an implant-supported fixed prosthesis. Implants allow you to permanently fix a long-span bridge to replace many or all of your teeth -- which means a beautiful smile with less hassle.

Implants can also be used to support and retain removable dentures. Ask our dentists about converting your traditional denture into an implant-supported prosthesis, and see the benefits of better retention, support and function.
