Savannah Smiles Dental Center is a family-focused dental practice located off Highway 3009 on Savannah Drive in Schertz, TX. Dr. Carolyn B. Walker and associates specialize in general, restorative, cosmetic, and advanced dentistry, as well as endodontics, periodontics, and oral surgery.

Whether you are in pain or unhappy with the way your teeth look, we can get to the root of your oral health issues and give you the smile you’ve been longing for.

We offer an extensive range of dental services—from routine cleanings and exams to advanced restorative, cosmetic and surgical treatments—so that every smile in your family is healthy and beautiful.

Our highly skilled dentists and dental team bring years of experience and expertise to every treatment plan.

Our friendly team strives to create a warm, welcoming environment from the moment you walk through our doors

That’s why we emphasize comfort, offering options like sedation dentistry when appropriate and ensuring that each visit is as relaxing as possible.
