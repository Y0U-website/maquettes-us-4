Meet Dr. Kolluri

NITHYA KOLLURI

Dr. Kolluri is happy to be back in her hometown, serving the community that she loves. Currently, she is an adjunct professor at Harrisburg Area Community College (HACC) and teaches students in the Dental Hygiene Clinic. In her free time, Dr. Kolluri enjoys traveling the world with her husband, spending time with her family, reading, and rooting for the Eagles. Some of her favorite trips have been visiting the Amazon Rainforest in Ecuador and hiking in Switzerland.
