If you have missing teeth or teeth that need to be extracted and replaced, a dental implant might be the best choice for you. There is no other tooth replacement option that creates a better, longer-lasting solution than dental implants. Dental implants have a 98 % success rate which makes them the most successful dental treatment.

Implants are typically small, screw-shaped titanium posts that replace the roots of missing teeth and are covered by porcelain crowns that look and feel just like your original tooth. Implants help to preserve tooth-supporting bone that naturally deteriorates when a tooth is lost, which prevents further problems in the future.
