Dental Implants

  • Improved Functionality: Next, they offer exceptional stability and functionality, unlike traditional removable dentures or bridges. However, since implants are securely anchored in the jawbone, they provide a strong foundation for biting and chewing, allowing you to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions. Plus, this enhanced chewing ability contributes to better digestion and overall oral health.
  • Long-lasting Solution: Their durability and longevity make them well-known. However, with proper care and regular dental check-ups, implants can last a lifetime. Therefore, this makes them a cost-effective investment compared to other tooth replacement options that may require periodic repairs or replacements.
  • Preservation of Jawbone Health: Losing a tooth reduces stimulation, which causes the jawbone to deteriorate over time. Plus, they stimulate the jawbone through osseointegration, which helps maintain its strength and density. Therefore, by preserving the bone structure, implants prevent the collapse of facial features and the associated “sunken” look that can occur with tooth loss.
  • Improved Speech: Missing teeth can affect speech clarity, causing difficulty pronouncing certain words or sounds. Implants restore the natural function of the teeth, allowing for clear and confident speech. With implants, you can easily communicate and regain your ability to pronounce words accurately.
  • Convenient and Easy Maintenance: Lastly, dental implants do not require any special maintenance or extensive daily care. You can care for them like natural teeth by brushing, flossing, and visiting the dentist regularly. However, this simplicity and convenience make implants a hassle-free option for tooth replacement.

All-on-4s, All-on-5s, All-on-6s, and Implant-Supported Dentures

We understand that each patient has unique dental needs and preferences. We offer various implant options to cater to your specific situation. Plus, our practice specializes in placing and restoring All-on-4s, All-on-5s, and All-on-6s, as well as implant-supported dentures.

First, All-on-4s, All-on-5s, and All-on-6s are innovative implant-supported full arch restorations that provide a fixed, permanent solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth. These procedures involve the strategic placement of a minimal number of implants to support a complete arch of prosthetic teeth. The result is a secure, stable, and natural-looking smile that allows you to eat, speak, and smile confidently.

Plus, implant-supported dentures offer an alternative for patients who desire the stability of dental implants combined with the convenience of removable dentures. With this solution, a series of implants are strategically placed to anchor and stabilize a custom-made denture, providing a secure and comfortable fit.
