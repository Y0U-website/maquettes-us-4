A thoughtful first-visit experience.

Every appointment is different, but these four steps help you know what the process generally looks like.

A warm welcome

Our bilingual team will confirm your information, concerns, and goals for the visit.

A complete evaluation

Your doctor will review your health history, examine your smile, and recommend the appropriate digital images.

A clear conversation

We explain findings in everyday language and answer questions before discussing treatment.

A plan that fits

You receive practical next steps organized around urgency, timing, comfort, and budget.

Know what comes next.

Our team can help verify available insurance information and explain estimated patient responsibilities. Coverage is determined by your individual plan.

In pain or unsure what to do?

Call the office and describe what you are experiencing. We will help determine the most appropriate next step and appointment availability.
