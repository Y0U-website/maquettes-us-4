Dental Implants

We use 3D CBCT imaging and Sidexis planning to evaluate bone, anatomy, and restorative needs before treatment. Your plan is explained clearly and coordinated from the surgical phase through the final restoration.

Strength, function, and a natural appearance—designed digitally.

Crowns & Fixed Bridges

Primescan digital impressions and Sirona design technology help us create precise restorations without traditional impression material. Crowns protect weakened teeth, while bridges can replace one or more missing teeth.

Personalized options for restoring your smile and confidence.

Dentures

We provide full and partial dentures and help patients understand the adjustment process, fit, maintenance, and future reline needs. Each treatment plan is based on your anatomy and functional goals.

Clear next steps when dental pain cannot wait.

Emergency Dental Care

Call us for tooth pain, swelling, broken teeth, lost restorations, or other urgent concerns. We focus first on identifying the source of the problem, relieving discomfort, and explaining the appropriate treatment options.

Treatment designed to relieve pain and preserve your natural tooth.

Root Canal Treatment

When the nerve inside a tooth becomes inflamed or infected, root canal treatment may help save it. We evaluate symptoms and imaging carefully and coordinate specialty care when a referral is the best option.

A discreet, digitally planned approach to a straighter smile.

SureSmile Clear Aligners

SureSmile clear aligners use digital records and customized treatment planning to guide tooth movement. We evaluate your bite, discuss expected treatment time, and monitor progress throughout care.

Professional whitening for a brighter, more confident smile.

Philips Zoom Teeth Whitening

Philips Zoom whitening is performed in a controlled clinical setting. Before treatment, we evaluate your teeth and existing restorations, discuss sensitivity, and help set realistic expectations.

Consistent care that protects your smile over time.

Preventive & General Dentistry

Routine exams, professional cleanings, X-rays, tooth-colored fillings, and personalized home-care recommendations help identify concerns early and support long-term oral health.
