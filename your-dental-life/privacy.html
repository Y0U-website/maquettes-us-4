Website Privacy & HIPAA

This website provides general information about our dental practice. It does not currently include online intake, appointment, payment, or patient-portal forms.

Please do not send medical, dental, insurance, or other sensitive information through ordinary email.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how Your Dental Life handles information associated with visits to this website. It is separate from the clinic&#x27;s HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which describes how protected health information may be used and disclosed.

2. Information processed when you visit

Our hosting and security providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. This information is used to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the site.

3. Cookies and tracking

We do not currently use advertising pixels or optional behavioral analytics on this website. The hosting platform may use technologies that are strictly necessary for security, access control, and basic operation. If our practices change, we will update this policy and add appropriate choices where required.

4. How information may be used or shared

Technical information may be used to maintain security, prevent misuse, understand errors, and comply with legal obligations. It may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf or disclosed when required by law. We do not sell personal information collected through this website.

Notice of Privacy Practices

This website policy is not a substitute for the clinic&#x27;s formal HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices. You may read the current notice online or request a paper copy by calling (305) 223-2828 .

5. Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the nature of this informational website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Technical logs are kept only as reasonably necessary for operations, security, and legal obligations.

6. Children and external services

This website is intended for general audiences and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites linked from this site.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the website or applicable requirements change. Questions may be directed to Your Dental Life, 13323 SW 42nd St, Miami, FL 33175, by telephone at (305) 223-2828, or by email at management@yourdentallife.com .
