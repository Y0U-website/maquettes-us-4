FAQs

How much are dental implants?

The cost of dental implants typically ranges from $3,000 to $6,000 per tooth, though this varies based on your specific situation and geographic location. Factors like bone grafting requirements, the number of teeth being replaced, and the type of restoration all influence the final investment. At our practice, we provide transparent pricing during your consultation and offer financing solutions to help make this life-changing treatment accessible and manageable for your budget.

What are dental implants?

Dental implants are titanium posts that function as artificial tooth roots, surgically placed into your jawbone to support replacement teeth. The implant fuses with your bone tissue over several months, creating a stable foundation for crowns, bridges, or dentures. This tooth replacement method is considered the gold standard because it preserves bone health, doesn’t require altering neighboring teeth, and provides a permanent solution that closely replicates the look and function of natural teeth.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are designed to be a lifetime solution when properly maintained through good oral hygiene and regular dental visits. The titanium post itself can last indefinitely once it integrates with your jawbone. The crown or restoration attached to the implant typically lasts 10 to 15 years before normal wear may require replacement. Factors like teeth grinding, smoking, and poor oral care can affect longevity, but most patients enjoy decades of reliable function from their implants.

Do dental implants hurt?

You won’t feel pain during the implant procedure itself because local anesthesia numbs the treatment area completely. Most patients experience mild discomfort, swelling, or tenderness for a few days after surgery, which is easily managed with over-the-counter or prescribed pain medication. Many people report that the discomfort is less intense than they expected and comparable to a tooth extraction. The healing process is typically smooth, and any discomfort diminishes significantly within the first week as your body begins to heal.

Why dairy isn’t allowed after getting a dental implant?

Dentists often recommend avoiding dairy products for the first few days following implant surgery because they can promote bacterial growth in your mouth and potentially increase infection risk. Dairy may also stimulate mucus production, which can be uncomfortable after oral surgery and interfere with the healing process. Additionally, some proteins in dairy products might affect blood clotting at the surgical site. Your dentist will let you know when it’s safe to reintroduce dairy, usually within three to five days once initial healing has progressed.
