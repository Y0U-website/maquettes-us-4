Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about dental care, treatment options, and what to expect before your appointment.

How often should I visit the dentist for a checkup?

We recommend a checkup and cleaning every six months. Regular visits allow Dr. Jensen to catch small issues early before they become bigger, more costly problems. Some patients with gum disease or other conditions may benefit from more frequent visits.

What happens during a routine dental exam?

Dr. Jensen performs a thorough evaluation including digital X-rays, an oral cancer screening, and a full assessment of your teeth, gums, and jaw. We use advanced imaging when needed to get the most accurate diagnosis possible.

What age should I bring my child in for their first visit?

We recommend scheduling your child’s first appointment by age one or when their first tooth comes in. Early visits help build comfort with the dentist and allow us to monitor development from the start.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency?

Call us right away at (816) 605-0034 during office hours. We accommodate same-day emergency appointments for toothaches, broken teeth, and dental trauma. For after-hours emergencies, you can text us at 816-495-4073 .

How can I prevent cavities between visits?

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and limit sugary snacks and drinks. Regular professional cleanings remove buildup that brushing alone can’t reach. Dr. Jensen can also recommend sealants for children and adults at higher risk.

Do you offer cosmetic services in addition to general dentistry?

Yes! We provide a full range of cosmetic options including Invisalign, veneers, bonding, and multiple whitening treatments. Dr. Jensen can discuss cosmetic goals during any routine visit so you can explore your options without a separate consultation.
