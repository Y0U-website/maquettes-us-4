Ready to Schedule Your Appointment?

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a checkup and professional cleaning every six months. Regular visits allow Dr. Jensen to catch small issues early before they turn into bigger, more costly problems. Patients with gum disease or other conditions may benefit from more frequent visits.

What happens during a routine dental exam?

Dr. Jensen performs a thorough evaluation including digital X-rays, an oral cancer screening, and a full assessment of your teeth, gums, and jaw. We use advanced 3D imaging when needed to get the most accurate diagnosis possible.

Do I really need X-rays at every visit?

Not necessarily at every visit, but digital X-rays are an important tool that lets us see cavities, bone loss, and other issues that aren’t visible during a visual exam. Our digital X-rays use significantly less radiation than traditional film.

How can I prevent cavities between visits?

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and limit sugary snacks and drinks. Professional cleanings remove buildup that brushing alone can’t reach. Dr. Jensen can also recommend sealants for patients at higher risk.

What’s the difference between a regular cleaning and a deep cleaning?

A regular cleaning removes plaque and tartar from above the gumline. A deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, goes below the gumline to treat early gum disease by removing bacteria and smoothing root surfaces so your gums can heal.
