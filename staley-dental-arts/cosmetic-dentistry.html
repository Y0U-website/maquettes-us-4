Is cosmetic dentistry painful?

Most cosmetic treatments involve little to no discomfort. For procedures like veneers or bonding, we use local anesthetic to keep you comfortable throughout. Many patients are surprised at how easy the process actually is.

How long do veneers last?

Porcelain veneers typically last 10 to 15 years or longer with proper care. That means brushing, flossing, and keeping up with regular checkups. Avoiding habits like chewing ice or opening packages with your teeth helps too.

How long does teeth whitening take?

Our in-office Boost whitening treatment takes about an hour and delivers immediate results. We also offer KöR whitening and take-home kits for patients who prefer a more gradual approach on their own schedule.

Am I a good candidate for Invisalign?

Invisalign works well for mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues. Dr. Jensen can evaluate your teeth during a complimentary consultation and let you know if clear aligners are the right fit for your goals.

Does insurance cover cosmetic procedures?

Most cosmetic treatments are not covered by insurance, but some procedures like crowns may qualify depending on your plan. We offer financing through CareCredit and Cherry financing with 0 % interest options to help make treatment affordable.

Can I combine multiple cosmetic treatments?

Absolutely. Many patients combine treatments like whitening and bonding or veneers and Invisalign as part of a full smile makeover. Dr. Jensen will help you prioritize and sequence treatments for the best results.
