What is Pediatric Dentistry?

Beyond treatment, a big part of pediatric dentistry is education. We teach kids how to brush and floss properly, talk to parents about diet and habits that affect oral health, and make sure every child leaves our office feeling good about taking care of their teeth. Our goal is to raise kids who actually want to go to the dentist, not ones who dread it.

When should my child first see the dentist?

We recommend scheduling their first visit by age one or when their first tooth comes in. These early appointments are quick, gentle, and focused on getting your child comfortable with the dental environment while we check that everything is developing properly.

What if my child is scared of the dentist?

That’s completely normal and we’re prepared for it. Our team takes a patient, gentle approach and lets your child set the pace. For kids who need a little extra help relaxing, we offer nitrous oxide, which is safe, mild, and wears off within minutes after the visit.

Do cavities in baby teeth really need to be filled?

Yes. Baby teeth hold space for permanent teeth and help with chewing and speech development. Untreated cavities can cause pain, infection, and damage to the adult teeth forming underneath. Catching them early keeps treatment quick and simple.

Can I stay in the room during my child’s appointment?

Absolutely. Parents are always welcome in the treatment room. We find that younger children especially feel more comfortable with a parent nearby, and it gives you a chance to hear directly from Dr. Jensen about your child’s oral health.

What is the Healthy Start program?

Healthy Start is an early intervention system that addresses airway and breathing issues, crooked teeth, and sleep-disordered breathing in children. It uses a series of gentle oral appliances to guide jaw growth and tooth development while your child is still growing.
