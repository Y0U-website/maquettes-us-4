Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe tooth pain, a cracked or knocked-out tooth, a lost filling or crown, swelling, bleeding that won’t stop, or any injury to your mouth or jaw. When in doubt, call us. We’d rather see you and rule something out than have you wait and let it get worse.

Can you see me the same day?

Yes. We reserve time in our schedule specifically for emergency patients. Call us at (816) 605-0034 as soon as possible and we’ll get you in the same day.

What should I do if a tooth gets knocked out?

Pick it up by the crown, not the root. Rinse it gently with water if it’s dirty but don’t scrub it. Try to place it back in the socket if you can. If not, keep it in a glass of milk and get to our office as fast as possible. The sooner you get here, the better the chance of saving it.

What can I do for pain while I wait to be seen?

Take over-the-counter ibuprofen as directed and apply a cold compress to the outside of your cheek to help with swelling. Avoid placing aspirin directly on the gums as it can burn the tissue. Call us and we’ll talk you through what to do on your way in.

What if my emergency happens after hours?

Text us at 816-495-4073 and someone will get back to you as soon as possible. We’ll help you manage the situation and get you scheduled first thing when the office opens.

Will my insurance cover emergency treatment?

Most dental insurance plans cover emergency exams and common treatments like fillings, crowns, and extractions. Our team will verify your benefits and let you know what to expect. We also offer financing through CareCredit and Cherry financing if needed.
