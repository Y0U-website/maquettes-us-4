Les soins

We recommend a checkup and cleaning every six months. Regular visits allow Dr. Jensen to catch small issues early before they become bigger, more costly problems. Some patients with gum disease or other conditions may benefit from more frequent visits.

A regular cleaning removes plaque and tartar from above the gumline. A deep cleaning, also called scaling and root planing, goes below the gumline to treat early gum disease by removing bacteria and smoothing root surfaces so your gums can heal.

Implant Crowns Permanent replacement for missing teeth

Invisalign works well for mild to moderate crowding, spacing, and bite issues. Dr. Jensen can evaluate your teeth during a complimentary consultation and let you know if clear aligners are the right fit for your goals.

We offer Invisalign, bonding and veneers, implant crowns, short-term adult braces, and multiple whitening options including Boost in-office whitening, KöR whitening, and take-home whitening kits.
