Originally from Poland, Dr. Dominik immigrated to Parma Heights with his family as a teen in search of the American dream’. He went on to graduate from The Ohio State University with a Bachelor of Science in Microbiology and later The Ohio State University College of Dentistry.

After graduation, Dr. Dominik honed his skills through a residency in Youngstown, OH, establishing a solid foundation for his career. With seven years as an associate dentist under his belt, he took the next step by founding Pleasant Dental, a testament to his commitment to providing top-notch dental care.

In his relentless pursuit of knowledge, Dr. Dominik consistently exceeds the required continuing education credits to maintain his dental license. He takes pride in staying abreast of the latest developments and technologies in dentistry, showcasing his commitment to providing cutting-edge care.

Beyond the dental office, you may find Dr. Dominik tackling projects around the house, running, and spending time with his wife, family, and friends.
