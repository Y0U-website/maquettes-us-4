Dr. Leonid Nashtatik

Dr. Leonid Nashtatik, or "Dr. Lenny", graduated from Stony Brook University School of Dental Medicine in New York with his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree. He continued his training at Northport Veteran Affairs Medical Center General Practice Residency with a focus on dental implants. Shortly thereafter, Dr. Lenny went on to be the Chief General Practice Resident at UCLA/ West Los Angeles Veteran Affairs Medical Center.

Dr. Lenny is passionate about dentistry and his patients. He provides comprehensive dental care to patients and families of all ages from babies to seniors. Dr. Lenny’s clinical expertise includes cosmetic dentistry, veneers, implant surgery, Invisalign, root canals, and wisdom teeth extraction. He is very gentle and is adept at making even the most anxious patient feel comfortable and at ease. Dr. Lenny’s philosophy on patient care is to facilitate good oral health and to build a lasting doctor-patient relationship while creating a stress-free environment.

Eco-Friendly Dentistry

Did you know the health of humans and the health of the environment are connected? Because of this, we are an eco-friendly dental practice that focuses on planetary wellness. Our goal is to reduce waste and pollution in the environment while providing exceptional high-quality care that focuses on our patients’ overall well-being. We do things like use digital dentistry to minimize environmental waste, use environment-friendly white composite fillings, and minimize disposables — just to name a few. We truly care about our environment, and it shows.

Comprehensive, Quality Services

We strive to offer the highest quality dentistry in the area and always aspire to keep improving. Our team is knowledgeable, skilled and committed to excellence. In addition to delivering each service at only the highest standard of care, we are also comprehensive. We can meet the majority of your family’s needs right here under one roof with results that last. When it comes to quality dentistry, you can count on us.

Patient-Focused Care

One of our biggest strengths is how much we care about our patients. Our friendly team members take the time to get to know you as an individual. We know that you have unique needs and desires, and we do everything we can to fulfill them. We truly value our relationship with you and look forward to serving you through the years.

Total Body Approach

We look at the big picture. What does that mean? When diagnosing and treating, we seek solutions that are focused on the entire body, not just the teeth and gums. We keep your overall health and well-being in mind during every treatment. Doing so not only allows you to enjoy a healthier smile, but a healthier life.

Modern, Comfortable Environment

Your comfort during your visit is important to us. We maintain a modern, up-to-date facility so we can provide high-quality, environmental-friendly treatment solutions but we do so in a calm, relaxing and intimate setting for patients. We have a great team that is kind, friendly and patient-focused. We will remain attentive to your needs throughout your care and always work with a gentle touch. You will find our friendly personalities and great chairside manners enjoyable to be around.

Accommodating Appointments

We recognize that a trip to the dentist isn’t always easy. Not only do our comprehensive services make it convenient for you to get most of your dental needs addressed in one location, we also keep our doors open on weekends and offer flexible hours. We strive to make ourselves available for even the busiest of schedules!

Our team is skilled, knowledgeable and detailed.
