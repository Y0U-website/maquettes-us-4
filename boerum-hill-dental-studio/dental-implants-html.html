Dental Implants

Dental implants are the most cutting-edge therapy available for the replacement of missing teeth. Dr. Lenny is a dedicated doctor who pays extraordinary attention to detail. Because of his passion for perfection, he goes the extra mile for you. He has completed advanced training in prosthetic implants to ensure that he will provide only the best results for you. Dr. Lenny also implements today’s best implant technologies in our office, solidifying our place as dental implant experts.

Implants provide a strong foundation to replace missing teeth. Because they are designed to fuse with bone, they are a permanent solution. There are many advantages to dental implant therapy.

Dental implants may have a higher initial cost, but when you add up all the ongoing expenses of other treatment options available—dental implants are more than worth the investment! Consider all the benefits in comfort, time, esthetics and health, and dental implants become an easy choice. Let us restore the function and beauty of your smile. Set up a consultation today!

  • Look, act and feel like your own teeth
  • Improve functionality—both eating and speaking
  • Minimize oral bone loss
  • Reduce compromise to adjacent healthy teeth
  • Eliminate the discomfort of removable dentures
  • Improve oral health and hygiene
  • Give you back your smile and help you feel better about yourself!
