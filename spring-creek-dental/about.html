0 OUR PHILOSOPHY We believe that a patient’s dental experience should be as comfortable as possible while achieving a positive, long-lasting outcome. When it comes to dental procedures every patient responds differently. Some may fear the dentist, while others are at ease with visits. This is why we treat each patient separately so that we can create a dental experience that best fits their emotional mood and expectations.

Patients are welcome to communicate with our staff and request individualized sedation or comfort methods. We also believe that through a regular dental care program, many dental issues can be avoided or minimized, which in the long run saves our patients both time and money.

As an environmentally conscious dental practice we choose to use the safest, highest quality and most environmentally responsible dental materials. Because of our commitment to the environment, we use Digital X-Rays which enable us to produce precise images without the use of chemicals. We also believe in running our office in an environmentally efficient manner by using proper recycling techniques and energy saving measures.

We are a certified environmentally responsible dental practice and our safety and sterilization techniques exceed OSHA, the ADA and CDC recommendations.

Meet Dr. Molly Francis

Dr. Molly Francis was born in Spokane, Washington and attended college at Creighton University in Omaha Nebraska, studying English. She then decided to forgo another midwest winter and chose dental school in Mesa, Arizona, after which she moved to the Oregon coast to work for the Confederated Tribes of Siletz Indians. Molly went into dentistry because she finds in it the opportunity to have a rewarding career in medicine and also be able to be present and active in her family life as well; a unique balance.

In 2010, Dr. Francis began working with Kaiser Permanente Dental and was there until recently, moving into a private practice. Her philosophy is focusing on individualized care of her patients, and strongly believes in the integration of medicine and dentistry towards achieving her patients’ total health.

Dr. Francis has four children and enjoys time with them as well as cooking, gardening, travel and all sports.
