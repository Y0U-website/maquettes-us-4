What Are Dentures?

Dentures are removable prosthetic devices designed to replace missing teeth. They come in two main types:

  • Full dentures : Replace all teeth in the upper or lower jaw.
  • Partial dentures : Replace several missing teeth and are attached to your natural teeth using clasps.

Pros of Dentures:

  • More affordable upfront compared to implants
  • Non-invasive—no surgery required
  • Can be fabricated relatively quickly
  • Ideal for patients with weaker jawbone structure

Cons of Dentures:

  • Can shift or slip while eating or speaking
  • Require adhesives and frequent adjustments
  • May cause gum irritation or sore spots
  • Do not prevent jawbone loss over time

What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are permanent replacements that mimic natural teeth. A titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone to act as a root, and a crown is attached to restore the visible part of the tooth.

Pros of Dental Implants:

  • Look, feel, and function like natural teeth
  • Permanently fixed—no slipping or adhesives
  • Prevent bone loss by stimulating the jawbone
  • Long-lasting solution (can last a lifetime with proper care)
  • Enhance bite strength, comfort, and speech clarity

Cons of Dental Implants:

  • Higher upfront cost
  • Requires oral surgery and healing time
  • May need bone grafting if the jawbone is weak
  • Not suitable for patients with certain medical conditions or poor oral hygiene

Who Is a Good Candidate for Each?

At Platinum Touch Oral and Facial Surgery, we perform comprehensive evaluations to determine the best solution for your smile.

You may be a good candidate for dentures if:

  • You need a quicker or more affordable tooth replacement
  • You have significant bone loss in the jaw
  • You prefer a non-surgical option
  • You have medical conditions that prevent surgery

You may be a good candidate for dental implants if:

  • You want a permanent solution with a natural look and feel
  • You have adequate jawbone or are willing to undergo bone grafting
  • You’re in good overall health
  • You’re committed to long-term oral hygiene

Our Approach at Platinum Touch

We believe that you deserve a solution that’s not only functional—but empowering. Whether you choose dentures or dental implants, our goal is to help you restore confidence in your smile and enjoy the benefits of better oral health.

Conclusion: Which Is Better for You?

Ultimately, the decision between dentures and dental implants depends on your personal goals, health condition, and budget. Implants offer unmatched stability, longevity, and bone support, while dentures may be more suitable for those looking for an affordable, non-surgical solution.
