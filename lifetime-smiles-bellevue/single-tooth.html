  • Aesthetic integration: The custom crown is matched to the color and contour of surrounding teeth, providing a result that is virtually undetectable.

Are You a Candidate for a Single-Tooth Implant?

Most adults who are in good general health and have sufficient bone density at the treatment site are potential candidates for a single-tooth implant. Certain conditions, such as uncontrolled diabetes or active gum disease, may need to be addressed before proceeding.

Maintaining preventive dental care and good oral hygiene before and after implant placement also plays a meaningful role in the long-term success of the restoration.
