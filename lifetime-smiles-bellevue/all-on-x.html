Permanent Full-Arch Restoration with All-on-X Implants

Same-Day Teeth with Immediate Function

All-on-X treatment allows you to walk into our office with failing or missing teeth and leave the same day with a beautiful, functional smile. Unlike traditional implant approaches that require months of healing before receiving your final teeth, All-on-X provides immediate restoration. Dr. Beke places four to six dental implants at strategic angles to maximize bone support, then attaches a temporary full-arch prosthesis the same day, so you never have to go without teeth.

Advanced Stability Without Bone Grafting

One of the most significant advantages of All-on-X is that it often eliminates the need for bone grafting procedures . By angling the implants to engage the strongest areas of your jawbone, Dr. Beke can achieve excellent stability even in patients with moderate bone loss. This approach reduces treatment time, lowers costs, and allows more patients to benefit from permanent tooth replacement without complex preliminary surgeries.

Why Choose All-on-X Over Traditional Dentures in Bellevue, WA

Traditional dentures sit on your gums and rely on adhesives or suction to stay in place, which often leads to slipping, clicking, and discomfort while eating or speaking. All-on-X implants are permanently fixed in your mouth, anchored directly to your jawbone through titanium implants that integrate with your bone tissue. This creates a stable foundation that withstands normal chewing forces, allowing you to eat your favorite foods without worry and speak naturally without the fear of denture movement.

The All-on-X Treatment Process

Understanding what to expect during All-on-X treatment helps you approach the procedure with confidence. Your transformation begins with a comprehensive consultation where Dr. Beke evaluates your oral health, takes detailed 3D imaging of your jawbone, and discusses your aesthetic goals. This thorough planning phase ensures precise implant placement and optimal results.

On the day of your procedure, Dr. Beke places four to six titanium implants into your jawbone at carefully calculated angles that maximize support and stability. The posterior implants are typically angled to avoid sensitive structures and engage stronger bone, while the anterior implants are placed vertically for additional support. Once the implants are secured, a custom-fabricated temporary prosthesis is attached, giving you immediate function and aesthetics. You’ll leave our office with a complete set of teeth the same day.

Over the following months, your implants undergo osseointegration, a biological process where your bone tissue fuses with the titanium implants. During this healing period, you’ll continue using your temporary prosthesis while Dr. Beke monitors your progress. After complete healing, typically three to six months, we’ll replace the temporary teeth with your final custom-crafted prosthesis, designed to match your facial structure, gum tissue, and personal preferences for a natural, beautiful result.

Frequently Asked Questions About All-on-X Dental Implants

Understanding All-on-X treatment helps you make informed decisions about your oral health. Here are answers to the most common questions our Bellevue patients ask about full-arch implant restoration.

The surgical procedure typically takes three to four hours per arch, depending on whether you’re replacing your upper teeth, lower teeth, or both. Dr. Beke places the implants and attaches your temporary prosthesis during this single appointment, so you leave with functional teeth the same day. The total treatment timeline from consultation to final prosthesis spans approximately four to six months, allowing time for complete healing and osseointegration before your permanent teeth are fabricated and placed.

Most patients report minimal discomfort during the procedure itself, especially when oral conscious sedation is used. After surgery, you’ll experience some swelling and soreness for several days, similar to other oral surgery procedures. Dr. Beke provides detailed pain management instructions and prescribes appropriate medications to keep you comfortable during the initial healing phase. Most patients return to normal activities within a few days and find any discomfort is well worth the life-changing results.

Caring for your All-on-X prosthesis is simpler than you might expect. You’ll brush your teeth twice daily using a soft-bristle toothbrush, paying special attention to where the prosthesis meets your gum tissue. Special floss threaders or water flossers help clean beneath the prosthesis to remove food particles and bacteria. Regular professional cleanings every three to six months allow Dr. Beke to examine your implants, check for any issues, and professionally clean areas you can’t reach at home. With proper maintenance, your All-on-X implants can last for decades.
