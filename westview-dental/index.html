Services

Dental crowns are placed over damaged or decayed teeth to restore their shape, size, strength, and appearance. They are used to protect weakened teeth or cover dental implants.

Dental implants are surgically placed into the jawbone to support a replacement tooth or bridge. They provide a long-lasting, stable solution for missing teeth and help preserve jawbone structure.

Clear aligners are transparent, removable trays designed to straighten teeth over time as an alternative to traditional metal braces. They gradually shift teeth into alignment through a series of custom-made, snug-fitting trays worn daily.

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of a person’s teeth, gums, and smile. It includes procedures like teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and orthodontics to enhance dental aesthetics.
