Frequently Asked Questions

Can I receive sedation during restorative dental treatment?

Yes. If you feel anxious or nervous about restorative dental treatment, we offer sedation options to help you feel more comfortable throughout your visit. Our team will discuss your needs and recommend the best option for you. Learn more about our Sedation Dentistry and Nitrous Oxide Sedation services.

What if I&#x27;m nervous about getting restorative dental treatment?

It&#x27;s completely normal to feel anxious about dental treatment. Our team is committed to creating a comfortable, stress-free experience and offers sedation options for patients who need extra support. We&#x27;ll work with you to make your visit as relaxing as possible.

Will I be comfortable during my restorative procedure?

Your comfort is one of our top priorities. We use gentle techniques and, when appropriate, offer sedation options to help you feel relaxed during your restorative treatment. If you have concerns about discomfort or dental anxiety, let us know before your appointment.
