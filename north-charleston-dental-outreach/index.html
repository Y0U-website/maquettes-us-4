The practice

We operate to the glory of God in response to a call to help and love those among us in need. In addition, by mentoring dental students and residents, we seek to train the next generation of dental volunteers.

Different countries of origin are represented by our patients. 47 % of our patients are from the 29403 and 29405 zip codes in the closest proximity to our clinic. 78 % are from the Greater Charleston Tri-County Region.
