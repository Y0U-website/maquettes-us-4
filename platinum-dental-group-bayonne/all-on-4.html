Things to Consider Before Choosing All-on-4 Dental Implants

Although all-on-4 implants offer many benefits, there are also some potential disadvantages.

  • All-on-4 can have a significant upfront cost. They are also typically more expensive than traditional dental implants.
  • In addition, all-on-4 requires a higher level of care and maintenance than conventional implants. Patients must take special care to clean and floss around the implant sites.
  • Patients may need to avoid certain foods and drinks that can damage the implants.
  • All-on-4 is also not a suitable treatment option for all patients. Sufficient bone tissue in the jaw is required for placement, so patients with osteoporosis or other conditions that affect bone density may not be candidates for all-on-4. Gum disease can also disqualify you as a candidate for all-on-4.
  • There is a risk of failure with any dental implant surgery, but this risk is increased with all-on-4 because there are four implants instead of just one.
