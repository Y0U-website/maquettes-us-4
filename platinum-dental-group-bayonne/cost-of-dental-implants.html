Dental Implants Cost in New Jersey

Cost of Dental Implants

Dental implants are a popular way of replacing lost teeth. They are made of titanium, which is compatible with the human body, and they fuse to the bone over time. This makes dental implants permanent and much more stable than other options, such as dentures. However, dental implants can be expensive, costing anywhere from $3,500 to $4,000 per tooth. Overall, dental implant surgery is an excellent option for those who are missing teeth and are willing to invest in their oral health.

Factors that Affect Dental Implants Cost

Dental implants are an effective solution for replacing lost teeth. However, they can be quite costly, and the price can vary depending on several factors.

Type of Dental Implant

One important consideration is the type and quality of the dental implant. Different manufacturers use different materials, and even subtle differences in design or construction can affect how well the implant performs and how long it lasts.

Dental Insurance Coverage

Another critical factor is dental insurance coverage. Many dental insurance plans will cover a portion of the dental implant procedure, although some may limit how much they pay per tooth or for certain types of implants. It also will depend on the reason you are getting the implant because they typically will not cover anything that isn’t considered a medical necessity.

Number of Implants

The number of implants needed can also affect the cost. If you are only missing one tooth, a single tooth implant may suffice, while full mouth implants may be required if you are missing many teeth or if they will be used to hold bridges or dentures in place. The single-tooth implant cost is far less expensive than a full mouth.

Bone Grafting

Bone grafting may also be required before implant placement can happen. This can further increase the overall dental implant cost. This is a procedure in which healthy bone tissue is added to the jawbone to provide enough support for an implant. Again, the type of bone graft and the extent of the procedure will impact the overall cost of dental implants.

Does insurance cover dental implants?

The answer to this question will depend on your specific insurance plan. Some insurance companies offer full coverage for dental implants, while others only cover a portion of the cost. In some situations, you may be able to get insurance to cover the entire cost of dental implants if they are medically necessary. However, if you’re looking to get dental implants purely for cosmetic reasons, it’s unlikely that your insurance will cover the cost.

Paying for Your Dental Implants

Considering dental implants, it’s essential to factor in cost. There are several ways to finance your procedure and make it more affordable.

Dental Insurance

Dental insurance can be a great way to offset the cost of dental implants. If your insurance plan covers implants, check with your provider to see how much they will cover.

Payment Plans

CareCredit is a popular option for those looking to finance their dental implants. CareCredit is a credit card used for medical and dental procedures. It offers low-interest financing options, and there is no annual fee.

Flexible Spending Accounts

Many employees are offered flexible spending accounts, or FSAs, which allow you to set aside a portion of your paycheck before taxes. These funds can be used for medical and dental expenses throughout the year.
