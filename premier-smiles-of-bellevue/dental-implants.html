  • Implant-Supported Bridges Designed to replace several missing teeth in a row, these bridges are anchored to implants rather than natural teeth, providing enhanced stability and durability.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures Ideal for patients missing most or all teeth, these dentures attach to a few strategically placed implants for a secure, comfortable fit without the need for adhesives.
  • All-on-4 Implants This technique restores an entire arch of teeth using just four implants, offering a faster, more efficient solution for full-mouth restoration with fewer implants and quicker healing.

Cost, Insurance And Payment Plans

We understand that choosing dental implants is a meaningful financial investment. While the upfront cost may seem significant, implants are often the most cost-effective solution over the long term.

The total investment depends on several factors, including the number of implants needed, whether bone grafting is required. Before any treatment begins, you’ll receive a detailed, easy-to-understand breakdown of all associated costs—no hidden fees and no unexpected charges.

We proudly work with major insurance providers and offer flexible financing options through partners such as Sunbit in case you do not have insurance. Our goal is to make advanced dental care accessible to every family in Bellevue, because everyone deserves the benefits of modern, high-quality dental technology.
