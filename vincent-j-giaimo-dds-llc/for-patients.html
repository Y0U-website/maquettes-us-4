Accepted Payment Methods

At the office of Vincent J. Giaimo, D.D.S., we understand that financial considerations can impact your dental care decisions. That’s why we offer a variety of payment options to accommodate your needs. Whether you prefer to pay with cash, check, or credit, we have you covered!

We accept all major credit cards and CareCredit, making it easier for you to manage your dental expenses. If you have any questions about our financial options or would like to schedule your appointment, please reach out to us at our Amsterdam location at (518) 843-9177 or our Malta location at (518) 289-5707. Our friendly dental team is here to assist you!
