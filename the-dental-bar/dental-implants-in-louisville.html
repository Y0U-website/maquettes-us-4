What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a modern solution for replacing missing teeth by restoring both the root and the visible crown. A small titanium post is placed into the jawbone, where it eventually fuses with the bone. Think of a dental implant as a sturdy anchor for your tooth, much like a foundation supports a beautiful Louisville home.

Single Tooth Implants

Candidacy for Dental Implant Surgery

Many adults are excellent candidates for dental implants, though a thorough evaluation is necessary. Ideal candidates typically have:

  • Healthy gums free of active infection
  • Sufficient bone density to support the implant post
  • Good overall health to facilitate proper healing
  • A commitment to consistent oral hygiene

Who Might Need Additional Preparation?

  • Significant bone loss that requires a bone graft
  • Uncontrolled medical conditions like diabetes
  • Active gum disease that needs treatment first
  • Tobacco use, which can hinder the bone fusion process

The Dental Implant Surgical Procedure at The Dental Bar

The dental implant process at our Louisville dental office happens in several stages to ensure the best possible results. Your journey starts with a detailed consultation where we review your history and perform advanced digital scans.

  • Consultation and Planning: We use radiographic scans to get a clear picture of your bone structure, helping us plan the precise placement of your implant.
  • Implant Placement: During the surgical phase, a small titanium screw is inserted into the jawbone. We use local anesthesia to ensure you remain comfortable throughout the procedure.
  • Osseointegration (Healing): Over the next three to six months, the implant fuses with the bone, creating a solid foundation. This healing period is crucial for long-term success.
  • Abutment and Final Crown: Once healing is complete, an abutment is placed to secure a permanent, custom-made crown that matches your natural teeth.

Caring for Dental Implants

By following these simple steps, you can enjoy a healthy and beautiful smile for decades.

  • Brush twice daily using a soft-bristle toothbrush
  • Floss regularly to keep the area around the implant clean
  • Avoid biting on extremely hard objects that could damage the crown
  • Maintain routine professional cleanings and checkups
