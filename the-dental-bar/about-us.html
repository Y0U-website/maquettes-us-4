Meet Dr. Sharma

Expert Care for Your Perfect Smile

Dr. Rohini Sharma was born in London, England, and raised in Dallas, Texas. She spent a few years in India, where she graduated with distinctions and honors from Rohilkhand University and earned her Bachelor s in Dental Surgery. She moved to Louisville and completed her Doctorate in Dental Medicine in 2015.

She is fluent in three languages: English, Hindi, and Punjabi . Dr. Sharma is passionate about serving and caring for her patients in a judgment-free manner. Her goal is to help patients feel at ease by providing high-quality care, demonstrating kindness and empathy—and sharing a few laughs along the way. She also offers Botox treatments for both aesthetics and pain relief.

My goal is simple: no judgment, no pressure just great care with a little laughter along the way. Dr. Rohini Sharma
