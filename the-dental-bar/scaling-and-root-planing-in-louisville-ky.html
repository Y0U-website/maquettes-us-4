What is Scaling and Root Planing?

Scaling and root planing (SRP) is a deep cleaning procedure performed to treat gum disease (periodontal disease). It involves removing plaque and tartar buildup from both above and below the gumline. Root planing smooths the root surfaces to help the gums reattach and heal.

At The Dental Bar, we provide effective scaling and root planing in Louisville, KY, to improve gum health and prevent serious oral health issues.
