The practice

At LoVerdi Dental, we offer a full range of treatments designed around your family’s needs.

We use cutting-edge diagnostics to catch concerns early before they grow into bigger problems. This allows for early intervention, minimizing any dental drama down the track.

We provide high-quality treatments to help to improve and transform your smile. Each smile is unique, and we’ll ensure your treatment is matched to your needs and goals.

When damage strikes, we rebuild smiles to look and feel like new. From dental crowns to dental implants, our restorations will have you smiling proudly in no time.

We warmly accept new patients and look forward to welcoming you and your family. Learn what to expect at your first visit to LoVerdi Dental with our step-by-step guide.

We provide a range of preventive treatments that protect your teeth from decay and safeguard your gums.

We treat gum issues and periodontal disease with gentle deep cleanings and targeted therapies.

We design and fabricate custom dental crowns that restore strength and match your smile’s color perfectly, including CEREC same day crowns for your convenience.
