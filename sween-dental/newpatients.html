Children’s Appointment (Ages 3-11)

We try to make going to the dentist a fun experience! Your child will be scheduled with one of our friendly hygienists who will perform a prophylaxis (cleaning), which will include a fun segment of counting teeth to see how many they have or if they have any loose ones (mainly for the younger ones). An exam by Dr. Mark is filled with fun analogies to teach young minds. We will then assess them for any necessary X-rays and fluoride treatments at that time.
