Dental Implant Procedure

At Silicon Valley Dentistry, we make the dental implant procedure as smooth and comfortable as possible. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

  • Consultation and Planning: During your initial visit, we evaluate your oral health and create a personalized treatment plan.
  • Implant Placement: The titanium implant is surgically inserted into your jawbone.
  • Healing Period: Over a few months, the implant fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration.
  • Abutment Placement: After healing, the abutment is attached to the implant.
  • Crown Placement: Finally, a custom-made crown is placed to complete the restoration.

Addressing Common Concerns

Patients often worry about discomfort during and after the procedure. At Silicon Valley Dentistry, we prioritize patient comfort, and many patients report minimal discomfort during the dental implant process. Recovery typically involves minor swelling and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Benefits of Dental Implants

Dental implants offer numerous long-term benefits, making them an ideal solution for tooth replacement:

  • Improved Chewing and Eating: Implants allow you to eat your favorite foods without discomfort.
  • Enhanced Speech Clarity: With dental implants, there’s no risk of slippage like with dentures, leading to clearer speech.
  • Boosted Confidence: A complete, natural-looking smile can significantly improve your self-esteem.
  • Long-Term Durability: With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime.
  • Natural Appearance: Dental implants are designed to match your natural teeth, offering a seamless look.

Dental Implant Candidates

Dental implants near Milpitas are a great option for patients with the following characteristics:

  • Good Oral Health: Healthy gums and no untreated tooth decay or infections.
  • Adequate Jawbone Density: Sufficient bone to support the implant.
  • Commitment to Oral Hygiene: Maintaining a routine of good oral care is essential for the long-term success of dental implants.

Factors to Consider

Certain factors, such as gum disease, smoking, and certain medical conditions (e.g., diabetes), may affect your eligibility for dental implants. We’ll assess these factors during your consultation.
