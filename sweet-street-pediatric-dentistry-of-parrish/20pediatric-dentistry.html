Pediatric Preventive Dentistry In Parrish, FL

Help your little one get started on the path to a lifetime of healthy smiles with pediatric preventive dental care at our office. At Sweet Street Pediatric Dentistry , we take a conservative, prevention-focused approach that prioritizes education, early detection, and minimally invasive treatments whenever possible. Our experienced team works closely with families to ensure your child’s mouth is developing properly while helping reduce the need for more complex treatment later on. Reach out and schedule your visit today!

Pediatric Cleanings & Exams

Along with giving your child a brighter smile, these routine checkups allow Dr. Kristan Ingraham-McQueen and our team to catch potential issues early, provide personalized oral health guidance, and ensure your child feels confident about their dental care. With our kid-friendly office and compassionate approach, we make every visit a positive step toward your child’s healthy future.

Infant Exams

Infant dental exams help ensure your child’s teeth and oral development are on the right track from the very beginning. These introductory visits allow our team to check for early concerns, provide guidance on teething and home care, and help your baby become comfortable at the dentist.

Monitoring Dental & Jaw Growth

We carefully monitor your child’s dental and jaw growth to ensure their teeth and bite are developing properly. During routine visits, we also check for early signs of growth disturbances or enamel defects so we can address any concerns as early and conservatively as possible, and determine if there&#x27;s any reason your child should be seen by a pediatric restorative dentist .

Pediatric Digital X-Rays

Digital x-rays are a quick and efficient way to capture detailed images of your child’s dental health and are used only when deemed necessary by the dentist. These images help us detect cavities, identify missing or extra primary and permanent teeth, and evaluate the jaw for any bony pathology in kids.

Pediatric Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are like invisible tooth protectors! We apply a safe, tooth-colored resin to your child’s teeth that hardens under a special light. It acts like a shield, helping to keep away cavity-causing bacteria and food particles in the deep grooves of the back teeth. Sealants are typically placed on the deep grooves of the adult first and second molars. Our sealants are free of Bis-GMA and are always carefully placed by the dentist or a dental hygienist.

Nutritional Counseling

We believe in a "whole health" approach, which includes yummy, healthy food choices. It&#x27;s not always easy to change eating habits, but it&#x27;s definitely worth it. Eating well benefits not only your child&#x27;s smile but their overall wellbeing. Let us help guide you toward tooth-friendly food and snack choices, along with healthy habits around when and how often to eat and drink.

In-Depth Home Dental Hygiene Guidance

We provide personalized, in-depth guidance to help families build strong home dental hygiene habits for their children. Our team teaches proper brushing and flossing techniques, discusses nutrition and cavity prevention, and offers age-appropriate tips so parents and kids can confidently care for their smiles at home.

Oral Habits

Building a strong foundation for healthy oral habits helps set your child up for a lifetime of confident, healthy smiles. Through educational and compassionate care, we guide children and families in developing great hygiene routines while also supporting them in breaking non-nutritive oral habits like thumb sucking, finger sucking, and prolonged pacifier use, which can impact growing jaws and facial development

Pediatric Fluoride Treatments

Fluoride treatments offer a simple, non-invasive way to help prevent dental decay. After your child’s cleaning, a fluoride-rich gel or varnish can be applied to support remineralization, a natural process that strengthens teeth with essential minerals. We believe in a conservative, parent-centered approach and do not push fluoride treatments; our team is always happy to discuss the benefits and allow families to decide what feels right for their child.
