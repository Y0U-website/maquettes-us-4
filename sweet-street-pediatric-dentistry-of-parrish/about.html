Little Extras That Make a Big Difference

We believe the dental office should be a place that sparks joy! That’s why we’ve outfitted Sweet Street Pediatric Dentistry of Parrish with kid-friendly features, state-of-the-art technology, and little comforts that make a big difference.

TVs

Every operatory and waiting area is equipped with TVs to help make your child’s visit more comfortable and enjoyable. Kids can watch their favorite shows or movies for a fun distraction that helps ease nerves and keep them relaxed before and during their appointment.

Custom Prize Station

Your child’s bravery deserves a reward! After their appointment, they’ll get to visit our custom prize station and choose a special treat to celebrate a job well done.

Kids Play Area

Our kid-friendly play area helps children feel comfortable and excited about visiting the dentist, featuring fun activities and iPads with games and educational apps. It’s a welcoming space that keeps kids entertained and relaxed before their pediatric dental appointment.
